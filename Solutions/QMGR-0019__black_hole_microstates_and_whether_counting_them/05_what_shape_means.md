## 1. A shape is a constraint, not a filler

The canonical figures README states the limit: the framework returns *"what a missing thing would have to look like (a shape, not a name)."* Fig 9 :32: *"you get a kind of thing, never the answer itself."* Fig 13 :134-135: it *"can report that it lacks something rather than producing an answer anyway"* and *"cannot say which primitive is missing, because naming it would mean already holding it."*

From *The Library of Babel* (carried): *"You can cut your search short if the set of books returned is small and descriptive enough that it provides a general shape of the answer that satisfies your question."*

So a **shape** is the narrowed form of an answer you can state before you can name it. It constrains; it does not fill. For this question, the shape is:

> A successful account must enumerate distinguishable states with symmetric relabelings quotiented out, must return area scaling without a parameter chosen to force area scaling, must carry a derivation record that distinguishes convergence from adoption, and must be stated per black-hole class.

The name of the states, D-branes or spin-network punctures or fuzzballs, is not part of the shape.

## 2. How the shape is obtained

- *Library of Babel* (carried): use the vocabulary already closest to the thing, look for recurring new terms that overlap with it, recurse; *"With each new term gained, you narrow the space and deepen your vocabulary."*
- Fig 6 :30: *"The description travels. Fails by being sharpened into terms only we can read. Cast it in effect terms."* Fig 9 :30-31: *"your words are borrowed too, so describe the effect rather than the cause."* Here the effect is: a count returns `A/4` in a protected sector and has not been characterized outside it. The cause, whether branes or punctures, is exactly what is not settled.
- *Decoding Tacit Knowledge* (carried): *"When contact isn't possible, the best you can send is a plain description of what you'd expect to see, because anyone can check that against the world."* This is why `02_outreach_email.md` sends the expected failure signatures rather than the architecture.
- Fig 8 :53: *"Residual first, frame second."* The residual here is the provenance gap between two readings of one number. Choosing string theory first and then finding a count would be naming the frame before describing the residual.

## 3. What licenses a name

Two things, both outside the shape itself:

1. **The ground settles it.** `THE_FORMULA.md` step 5: *"the ground settles it. Verified only by what pays. A gate is not the ground."* The canonical README: the framework *"stops at the limit of what the available instruments can distinguish, and records the lineage so the stop is honest."* For QMGR-0019 no ground reading exists at the unprotected scale, so no microstate name is licensed by settlement.
2. **A provenanced import from a closure that differs** (Fig 6 :23, Fig 8 :50-52), with a source gate and a shadow test. Naming D-branes is a binding from the string-theory frame onto atoms already held (horizon area, distinguishability, count). It is licensed as a binding with a record, not as a derivation.

*They're Scanning Books* (carried) sharpens the distinction: *"The real import is not a new term in the existing space. It is a new dimension of the space."* And: *"Declaring the primitives does not make them neutral. It makes the choice visible."* The six-term set is a visible guess, not nature's own list. Turning a visible choice into an invisible one is the failure mode when a binding is presented as a derivation.

## 4. Why the comparison matters for source discrimination

*The Hard Problem is About Source* (carried) gives the mechanism. A spectrometer reads green but *"cannot comment on the source"*: one green light, or yellow plus blue, or RGB, or CMYK. *"If several paths create the same behavior, then behavior is a projection with lossy information."* And *"An instrument that can't distinguish these cases demonstrates its own inadequacy."* It was the spectrometer, not an argument, that resolved it.

The `S_stat = S_BH` equality is a reading of one number from two instruments. It does not comment on the source. Many different microstate ensembles could give the same logarithm, which is the metamerism case. Fig 8 :50-52 says the same thing structurally: *"Two frames can hold the same atom by convergence, reaching it separately, which is corroboration at maximum independence"* and *"Convergent derivation and adopted import look identical in the contents. Only the record of where each came from separates them."* What is owed is a discriminator, not a name. The opened Wikipedia *Black hole thermodynamics* page supplies the concrete limit: the Schwarzschild micro/macro relation *"has not been characterized."*

*The Recursive Self-Improvement Trap* (opened) adds: *"the output is only as good as the validator. The agent, or the tool, or the frame, or the theory, cannot validate itself. An external validator, or ground, is always needed to tether the veracity of the output to reality."* A count that is checked only by comparing it to the number it was built to match is a frame validating itself.

## 5. Q answered

**What does the framework say about whether counting reproduces the entropy?** It says two things, and both are shapes:

1. **Per class, not globally.** The residual is indexed by black-hole class; aggregation hides the live slot (Fig 1 :19, :22). The shape of the answer is a vector of per-class verdicts, not a single yes.
2. **By provenance, not by equality of numbers.** The equality is a comparison (Fig 12 :87, *"a threshold relation between two quantities"*), and the comparison is evidence only when the derivation records are independent. The shape includes the record, not just the number.

**What does the framework refuse?** To name the states by derivation. Fig 9 :32 and the canonical README's shape-not-a-name limit govern here, with the narrowing in section 5b below.

## 5b. Correction to my own section 3 (kept visible)

I first wrote that "shape, not a name" forbids naming D-branes or spin-network punctures in this solution. That overstates the rule. The README's phrase is *"what a **missing** thing would have to look like (a shape, not a name)."* Its subject is a primitive not held in Γ. Distinguishability, area and count are already held or bindable onto held atoms, so naming a particular realization is a **binding from the physics frame**, not the invention of a seventh primitive and not an import that moves the wall (Fig 6 :22: *"NOVEL means novel relative to atoms, not to the world. IMPORT adds an atom, and the added atom is what expands the closure."*).

What remains wrong after the correction is claiming the binding is *derived*, or claiming the match settles the identification. Those failures are the metamerism point of section 4 and the provenance point of Fig 8 :50-52. Both the overstatement and its correction are recorded so neither is quietly lost. This matches the correction the exemplar records in its own `05` section 5b.

## 6. Two consequences for outreach

**Prestige is not action.** *The Recursive Self-Improvement Trap* (opened): *"Prestige must never be allowed to validate an action directly... Prestige can fund an action. It can give an action a budget to be tried. But it cannot settle an action. Only the ground can settle it. The Ground Maintainer must be blind to prestige."* A list of famous names who agree that string theory succeeded is the named collapse, not corroboration. The legitimate ask is a budget to try the refuter and a public record of the result.

**My own audit is a simulation, not a ground reading.** Same essay: *"LLMs can quickly simulate an approximate answer/output with the formula found in the architecture post... However, the truly testable output would need to be derived from the real architecture or working the formula yourself."* So `01_figure_grounded_audit.md` is a simulated derivation, checked for internal consistency against the figures. It is not a test of the claim and must be labelled as such. It cannot settle anything it reports on.
