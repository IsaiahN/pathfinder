## 4. Distribution rules: offer, do not push

Figure-grounded reasons:

- **Fig 4 :22-23** *"Only methods cross up. Only head starts come down."* Sending the full framework before a recipient has described a residual sends a recording, not a generator. Lead with the shape claim and the refuter; offer links after.
- **Fig 10 :42** *"A verifier that can reconstruct the claim cannot verify it."* A recipient who has absorbed the framework before the verdict has joined the frame being checked.
- **Fig 9 :27-28** *"describe it before you go looking, or any frame you pick will seem to fit."* Ask what they would expect to see if the shape claim were true before giving them the articles.
- **Fig 8 :50-52** *"Convergent derivation and adopted import look identical in the contents. Only the record of where each came from separates them."* The record is the useful thing to send, not a list of agreeing papers.
- **Fig 7 :13** *"same closure, handed along; agreement compounds, evidence does not"* and Fig 2 :40 *"agreement about the world is worthless when the agreeing frames share an evidence pool."* Many papers citing one 1996 result are one witness. No mass send selected for likely agreement.
- **Fig 2 :42** *"Buy independence in proportion to the cost of being wrong."* One-to-one contact through public opt-in venues buys independence; a list of believers does not.

## 5. The two-stage offer

1. **Stage 1.** Send the two conditions, the shape claim, and the five pre-registered refuters. Ask what the recipient would count as a failure. Record the verdict before showing any source.
2. **Stage 2, opt-in only.** Only if curious, offer the three links in `02_outreach_email.md`, with the note that the figure text, not the summary, is the authority. Later agreement is never pooled as corroboration (Fig 2 :40).
3. **Stage 3.** Offer the formula as a method to reuse. Each user writes their own refuter and states their own mode. From `THE_FORMULA.md` "Direction": a frame cannot check the direction of its own question. The *Recursive Self-Improvement Trap* adds that *"LLMs can quickly simulate an approximate answer/output"* but the testable output must be derived properly.

**Hard precondition.** Stages 1-3 only work if stage 1 survives. If the fitted-count refuter (refuter 1 in `01` section 3) cannot be answered, the reproduction verdict is withdrawn before any email is sent.
