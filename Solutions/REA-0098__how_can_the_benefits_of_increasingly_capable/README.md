## What is deliberately not here

- No personal emails of named individuals.
- No ranking of people by how likely they are to agree.
- No bulk-send plan, no receptiveness-as-evidence column.
- No concrete universal allocation percentage, UBI figure, tax rate, or named policy presented as a figure result. The figures return a shape, not a policy.

Three figure reasons, quoted verbatim in `01` and `04`:

1. **The anchor decides, not agreement.** Fig 2 :35: "Alignment is triangulation, not negotiation. A mirror cannot correct you." A list selected for likely agreement manufactures the correlation that would make its agreement worthless.
2. **A verifier must not hold the interiors.** Fig 10 :42: "A verifier that can reconstruct the claim cannot verify it." The first contact carries the residual and its refuter, not the framework.
3. **Distribution must be read per slot.** Fig 1 :19: "The residual is indexed by object slot, not aggregated." An aggregate benefit total hides the very slot where concentration occurs.

Legally, public opt-in channels avoid the GDPR and CAN-SPAM style failure that unsolicited bulk email to harvested addresses creates. That is a practical failure on top of the structural one.
