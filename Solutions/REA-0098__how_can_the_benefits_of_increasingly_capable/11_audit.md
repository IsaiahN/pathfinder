## Round trip gap R_T

**R_T is nonzero.** Four things the shorthand holds do not survive the trip back down:

- the **two-currency non-additivity** is easily re-fused into one "distribution score" once a program has one KPI (Fig 12 :125);

- the **per-slot rule** is replaced by an aggregate that hides the live slot (Fig 1 :22).

One thing survives cleanly: a benefit unit is not a capability, so the two must be reported separately. That is the residue worth sending. R_T was **not** measured numerically, because no ground reading of a distribution program was taken; it is recorded qualitatively as the enumerated set of dropped constraints.
