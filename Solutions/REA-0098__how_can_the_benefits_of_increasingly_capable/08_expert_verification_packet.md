# Expert verification packet — the Tether on distributing AI benefits

**What this is:** a claim in a form a researcher in AI benefit distribution, political economy, or policy can check. Mode, derivation, and refuters are stated before the conclusion, so a reader knows what to look for and what would settle it.

**Mode: `specified` on the sub-claims, `general` on the structural shape, `gap` on the specific policy. Not `grounded`.** No economy, policy, or institution was measured. This is a simulated derivation checked for internal consistency against the figures, not a ground reading. It cannot settle the claim it reports on.

## 1. The claim, in one paragraph

Benefits and power are two currencies and they do not add (Fig 12 :125), so the question is a union of conditions with separate anchors: producing the surplus, allocating benefit units per slot, keeping reach from concentrating, and maintaining the habitat in which capability is expressed. Capability is agent-and-habitat (Fig 11 :39), so distributing an isolated model changes no contact and no capability; what can travel is a method or a head start (Fig 4 :22), not a recording of one occasion. Concentrated power is a decision process in which frames draw on one evidence pool and the anchor updates on their reports (Fig 2 :35, :40), so distribution without concentration requires independent frames, a non-updating ground, and a seat able to refuse (Fig 10 :47). Effects must be read per slot, never as an aggregate (Fig 1 :19, :22). This is maintained work (Fig 11 :63), and the framework returns a shape, not a policy (Fig 9 :32).

## 2. Three checkable sub-claims

### C1 — framework-internal; checkable by reading Figures 12, 1, and 3
- **Statement:** benefits (a transfer currency) and reach (an ordered `⋛` comparison) are two currencies that do not add, and reading them as one aggregate hides the live slot where access disappears. The dependency chain breaks at the objective, because no instrument for reach-per-slot is held.
- **Source, verbatim:** Fig 12 :125 "A composition costs in two currencies, and they do not add. Bits price the description. Actions price finding out whether it holds." Fig 1 :19 "The residual is indexed by object slot, not aggregated." Fig 1 :22 "Aggregating across slots is how a live signal disappears into an average." Fig 3 :27 "A link with no instrument is not a link that works. It is a link nobody has looked at."
- **Refuter:** a measure in which moving a benefit unit raises that slot's reachable composition set one-for-one against a non-updating anchor for all slots, or a proof that aggregate and per-slot reach move together for all residuals with no hidden live slot.

### C2 — capability and habitat; checkable by an AI-policy or economics researcher
- **Statement:** distributing an isolated model does not distribute capability. The transferable objects are methods and head starts, plus the habitat conditions (instruments, data access, skills, the ability to check outputs). Benefit-unit transfer alone leaves reach concentration standing.
- **Source, verbatim:** Fig 11 :39 "So the answerable question is not what intelligence is, but what makes a system capable. And capability is a property of agent-and-habitat, never of the agent alone." Fig 11 :40 "A system cannot be made more capable by improving the agent in isolation. An improvement that does not change contact changes nothing, however much it improves." Fig 4 :22 "Only methods cross up. Only head starts come down." Fig 4 :25 "A recording carried upward looks like knowledge and is a description of one occasion."
- **What to check:** in a case you know (a compute-sharing program, an open-weight release, a cash transfer, a skills program), did the receiving slots gain real reach against a ground that did not update on the program's own reports, or only a benefit unit / model artifact? Does the program introduce a method or replay a recording?
- **Refuter:** an isolated model transfer with no habitat or contact change that measurably increases receiving slots' capability against the ground.

### C3 — the concentration condition; checkable by a governance or measurement researcher
- **Statement:** "without concentrating power" is re-grounded as a structural condition on the decision process. Independent frames range against an anchor that does not update on their reports, and a seat exists that can refuse a verdict. Agreement from a shared evidence pool is not a verdict, and a convention that fixes an outcome has stopped being a convention.
- **Source, verbatim:** Fig 2 :35 "Alignment is triangulation, not negotiation. A mirror cannot correct you." Fig 2 :40 "disqualifying for a verdict: agreement about the world is worthless when the agreeing frames share an evidence pool". Fig 7 :35 "A room's agreement is variation, not a verdict. Selection between rooms is where the ground votes." Fig 10 :47 "a convention that decides an outcome has stopped being one". Fig 2 :42 "Buy independence in proportion to the cost of being wrong."
- **Refuter:** a distribution procedure whose frames share one evidence pool and whose anchor updates on their reports produces a verdict that survives independent re-testing, or a demonstration that later agreement after full disclosure of the framework carries independent evidential weight.

## 3. What is explicitly not claimed

- Not a tax rate, UBI amount, bound, allocation percentage, or named institution as a figure result. That is a `gap` (Fig 9 :32, Fig 13 :134).
- Not that redistribution is useless or predistribution sufficient. They are isomers or a functional group over a shared atom set (`06` Q1), and the conditions form a union, so one lever leaves intersections standing (Fig 9 :55 to :57).
- Not that any existing institution is permanently pro-distribution. Fig 11 :51: "No standing list of good actors survives a change of residual."
- Not that endorsement by any economist, institute, or expert makes the claim true. Prestige can fund a trial, not settle one.
- Not `grounded`. Nothing here was measured, and the second seat that audited it shares the same evidence pool.

## 5. What would settle it

A source-discriminating instrument at the slot, not a further argument. From *The Hard Problem is About Source*: an instrument that reads green *"cannot comment on the source"*; the spectrometer resolved it. Here:

1. Does a benefit transfer to a slot raise that slot's reachable composition set one-for-one against a non-updating anchor? (C1)
2. Does an isolated model transfer with no habitat change increase receiving-slot capability against the ground? (C2)
3. Can a verdict from one evidence pool with an updating anchor survive independent re-testing? (C3)
4. The six pre-registered failure conditions in `01` section 4. Any one fires a withdrawal.

## 6. What an expert can check in twenty minutes

1. Read Fig 12 :125, Fig 1 :19 and :22, and Fig 11 :39 to :40. Four short passages.
2. Check C2 against one distribution program you know: did it change reach against an unmoved ground, or only deliver a benefit unit or artifact?
3. Name one instrument that would read reach per slot without updating on the program's own reports, or say why none is possible.
4. Say which of C1, C2, C3 you would refute first and on what evidence. Send the refutation. A failure is more useful than an endorsement.
