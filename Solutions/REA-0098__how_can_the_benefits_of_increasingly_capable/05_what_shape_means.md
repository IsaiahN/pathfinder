## 1. A shape is a constraint the answer must have, not a filler or a policy

The canonical README states the limit: the framework returns *"what a missing thing would have to look like (a shape, not a name)."* Fig 9 :32: "you get a kind of thing, never the answer itself". Fig 13 :134: "Unreachable is a verdict here, not a silence".

From *How Intelligence Actually Works* (read directly): the framework *"cuts down to the shape of a thing, problem or question based on the given information available at this point in time."* And: *"The recursion converges toward indiscernibility, and indiscernibility is not identity."*

For REA-0098, the **shape** is the set of constraints any acceptable distribution must satisfy: per-slot reading, two currencies kept apart, an unmoved anchor, independent frames, methods and head starts rather than recordings, a maintained habitat, and a seat able to refuse. A **name** would be a specific tax rate, UBI amount, bound, or named institution. The framework does not return those. The concrete policy is a binding from the economics or policy frame, provenanced as such.

So the honest form of the answer is: *"an acceptable distribution has shape S"*, not *"adopt policy P."*

## 2. How the shape is obtained here

- Fig 9 :27: "describe it before you go looking, or any frame you pick will seem to fit." The shape term was written down in `01` section 0 before any figure line was quoted or any world source was chosen.
- Fig 6 :30: "The description travels. Fails by being sharpened into terms only we can read. Cast it in effect terms." Fig 9 :30: "your words are borrowed too, so describe the effect rather than the cause." The effect description is: benefits read per slot; reach kept from concentrating; capability carried by habitat; the decision anchor unmoved.
- From *Decoding Tacit Knowledge* (carried): *"When contact isn't possible, the best you can send is a plain description of what you'd expect to see, because anyone can check that against the world."* That is exactly stage 1 of the two-stage offer.
- From *The Library of Babel* (carried): *"You can cut your search short if the set of books returned is small and descriptive enough that it provides a general shape of the answer that satisfies your question."* The search stops at the shape, not at a policy.

## 3. What licenses a concrete policy

Only two things, both outside the shape:

1. **The ground settles it.** `THE_FORMULA.md` step 5: *"the ground settles it. Verified only by what pays. A gate is not the ground."* For distribution, that means a preregistered policy experiment with a per-slot anchor that does not update on the program's own reports.
2. **A provenanced import from a frame whose closure differs** (Fig 6 :23, Fig 8 :50), with a source gate and a shadow test against the described residual. The policy briefs and reports in `10_source_ledger.csv` are imports of this kind, not derivations.

Until one of those, the policy is the searcher's own contribution and must be recorded as such.

From *They're Scanning Books* (carried): *"The real import is not a new term in the existing space. It is a new dimension of the space."* And: *"Declaring the primitives does not make them neutral. It makes the choice visible."* A distribution agenda that declares "benefits" and "power" to be one axis has made a choice; the shape requires keeping them as two axes (F1 of `01`).

## 4. Why an aggregate distribution score is the specific forbidden error

*The Hard Problem is About Source* (carried) gives the mechanism. An instrument reads green and *"cannot comment on the source"*: one green light, or yellow plus blue, or RGB, or CMYK, or a scheme not yet discovered. The reading does not determine the source. *"If several paths create the same behavior, then behavior is a projection with lossy information."* It was the spectrometer, not an argument, that resolved it.

Here the aggregate distribution index reads "green" (a single number) while the source could be broad benefit delivery with concentrated reach, or narrow benefit delivery with broad reach, or several other arrangements. Fig 1 :22 is the figure version of the same point: "Aggregating across slots is how a live signal disappears into an average." What is owed is a source-discriminating instrument at the slot, not a better aggregate.

From *The Recursive Self-Improvement Trap* (read directly): *"the output is only as good as the validator. The agent, or the tool, or the frame, or the theory, cannot validate itself."*

## 5. Correction to my own §3 (kept visible)

I initially wrote that the shape-not-a-name rule forbids naming any concrete mechanism. That overstates it. The canonical README's phrase governs a **missing** primitive, one not held in the library. Naming an already-available binding, such as "independent readings against an unmoved anchor" or "predistribution rather than redistribution", is provenance from another frame, not the invention of a new primitive. Fig 6 :26: "An import adds no atom to the world."

What remains forbidden: claiming that a figure line selects the universal policy, or presenting a binding as if the framework named it. So this solution may name predistribution, benefit-sharing routes, and independent oversight as **candidate bindings**, with provenance, but never as the framework's answer. Both the correction and the original constraint are recorded so neither is quietly lost. Triangulated (canonical README + Fig 6 :26 + Fig 9 :32).

## 6. Two consequences for this question

**Prestige is not action.** From *The Recursive Self-Improvement Trap* (read directly): *"Prestige must never be allowed to validate an action directly... Prestige can fund an action. It can give an action a budget to be tried. But it cannot settle an action. Only the ground can settle it. The Ground Maintainer must be blind to prestige."* A distribution claim endorsed by famous economists is a budget to be tried, not a verdict. The same essay's Ouroboros passage is directly on point for concentration: *"If prestige could buy actions, the system would collapse into a monoculture. The highest-prestige agent would monopolize compute and repeat its past success, and the system would stop exploring."* That is a simulated multi-agent description of Fig 2's mutual-update collapse, in a different vocabulary.

**This audit is a simulation, not a ground reading.** Same essay: *"LLMs can quickly simulate an approximate answer/output with the formula found in the architecture post... However, the truly testable output would need to be derived from the real architecture or working the formula yourself."* So `01` is a simulated derivation checked for internal consistency against the figures. It is **not** a test of any distribution policy and must be labelled as such. It cannot settle anything it reports on.

## 7. What would move the shape toward a name

A discriminator at the slot: a measurement that distinguishes broad benefit delivery with concentrated reach from broad reach, using an anchor that does not update. *How Intelligence Actually Works* gives the general condition: *"The ground must be immovable by persuasion and not bi-directional like the frames are. The ground doesn't update on anything that the frames do and only outputs data that will show if something is the right answer or not for a problem."* Until such a discriminator exists, the answer stops at the shape and records the lineage so the stop is checkable.
