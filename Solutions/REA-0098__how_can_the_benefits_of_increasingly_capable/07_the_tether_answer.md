# The Tether's answer on distributing AI benefits

**Mode: `specified` on the per-domain claims, `general` on the structural shape, `gap` on the concrete allocation rule. Not `grounded`.** This is a shape and a route, not a policy. Simulated derivation checked for internal consistency against the figures (`05_what_shape_means.md` §6), not a ground reading.

## 1. The residual it answers

Increasingly capable AI produces a benefit surplus. The observed residual is that the surplus can spread while the control of capability, and therefore reach and opportunity, concentrates. The default prediction, that benefit distribution trades off against concentration, does not resolve because these are two currencies with different anchors (Fig 12 :125, Fig 1 :19). The question is a union of at least four overlapping conditions with separate verdicts, not one dial with a right setting (Fig 9 :54 to :57).

## 2. The shape

1. **Benefits and reach do not add.** Fig 12 :125: "A composition costs in two currencies, and they do not add. Bits price the description. Actions price finding out whether it holds." A transferred benefit unit is not a transferred capability. The verdict per currency stays separate and is never averaged into one distribution score.
2. **The capability is agent-and-habitat.** Fig 11 :39: "So the answerable question is not what intelligence is, but what makes a system capable. And capability is a property of agent-and-habitat, never of the agent alone." Distributing an isolated model changes no contact, so it changes no capability (Fig 11 :40). What can travel is a method or a head start (Fig 4 :22: "Only methods cross up. Only head starts come down."), not a recording of one occasion.
3. **Concentration is a structural failure of the decision process.** Fig 2 :35: "Alignment is triangulation, not negotiation. A mirror cannot correct you." When the frames deciding an allocation all draw on one evidence pool, their agreement is the pool talking (Fig 2 :40), and if the anchor moves when they push on it, everything drifts together coherently. "Without concentrating power" is re-grounded as independent frames ranging against a ground that does not update, plus a seat able to refuse (Fig 10 :47: "a convention that decides an outcome has stopped being one").
4. **Read per slot, never as an aggregate.** Fig 1 :19: "The residual is indexed by object slot, not aggregated." Fig 1 :22: "Aggregating across slots is how a live signal disappears into an average." A falling national inequality index can hide a slot losing all real access. The broken link is the objective: there is no settled instrument for "not concentrating" at the slot (Fig 3 :27).
5. **It is maintained work, not a terminal allocation.** Fig 10 :72: "When the question changes, the room should change. A room that stays fixed while the question moves is the failure; the correlation is only its symptom." Fig 11 :63: "Which is why a managed ecosystem is permanent work rather than a solved state." No standing institution is permanently pro-distribution, because valence is relative to the residual (Fig 11 :49, :51).
6. **No policy is returned.** Fig 9 :32: "you get a kind of thing, never the answer itself". The specific tax, bound, income floor, or institutional design is a `gap`, a verdict and not a silence (Fig 13 :134). Policies are provenanced bindings from other frames with a shadow test (Fig 6 :24, Fig 8 :50), not figure outputs.

## 4. What it explicitly does not say

- Not a distribution percentage, UBI amount, tax rate, bound, or named institution as a figure result.
- Not that redistribution is useless or that predistribution is sufficient. They are isomers or a functional group (`06` Q1), and the union means one lever leaves intersections standing.
- Not that any existing institution is permanently pro-distribution. Valence follows the residual.
- Not that agreement among stakeholders, experts, or institutes settles the question. Agreement from one evidence pool is one witness (Fig 2 :40).
- Not that this analysis is `grounded`. No economy, policy, or institution was measured here.

## 5. What would settle it — the ground

A source-discriminating instrument at the slot, not a further argument. From *The Hard Problem is About Source* (carried in `05`): an instrument that reads green *"cannot comment on the source"*; the spectrometer resolved it. Here the questions are:

- Does a benefit transfer to a slot raise that slot's reachable composition set one-for-one against an anchor that does not update? If yes, F1 collapses.
- Does an isolated model transfer with no habitat change measurably increase receiving slots' capability against the ground? If yes, F2 collapses.
- Can a distribution verdict from frames sharing one evidence pool, with an updating anchor, survive independent re-testing? If yes, F3 collapses.
- Standing refuters from `01` section 4: the six pre-registered failure conditions. Any one fires a withdrawal.

## 6. The honest stop

**Unreached, not unreachable in principle.** The per-slot reach instrument does not yet exist, so the specific allocation rule is out of reach at this budget (Fig 8 :39: "So a search that finds nothing is never a proof of absence."). This is a `gap` about a missing binding from the policy frame, not a claim that no distribution can ever be settled. The six terms and the meta-rule set are a visible guess, not nature's own list; the analysis stops at the limit of the available instruments and records the lineage so the stop is checkable. *"Indiscernibility is not identity."*

## 7. One-paragraph version for outreach

> The Tether does not pick a tax rate or a universal basic income. It says the question bundles two different currencies: benefit units and real reach, which do not add, so spreading one does not spread the other. It says capability belongs to an AI-and-its-habitat, so handing out an isolated model changes no one's capability; what can travel is a method or a head start, not the recording of one success. It says concentrated power is a decision process in which everyone reads the same evidence and the reference moves when they push on it, so distribution without concentration needs independent readings against a ground that does not update, and a seat that can refuse a verdict. It says read every effect per person or slot, because an aggregate hides exactly the live slot where access disappears. And it says this is maintained work, not a one-off settlement, because the question keeps moving as capability rises. What it offers is that shape, six refuters written in advance, and the instrument still owed. The specific policy stays open until a ground settles it.
