# Outreach draft — check the output first, then decide about the theory

**Use:** one to one, or as a cover letter attached to a submission through the public venues in `03_public_contact_points.csv`. **Not** for bulk send.

**Hard precondition:** do not send until the shape claim and the refuter in `08_expert_verification_packet.md` survive the reader's own check. The two-stage offer collapses on contact if stage 1 fails.

---

**Subject:** Check one distribution claim before you decide whether the framework is worth reading

> Hi [name],
>
> I am working on a figure-grounded derivation of this question: how can the benefits of increasingly capable AI be distributed without concentrating power and opportunity? I have one specific output I would like you to check against what you already know. I am not asking you to read the theory first, and I am not asking you to agree with it. If the output does not survive, there is nothing else to look at.
>
> **The output (`specified`, not `grounded`):** benefits and power are two different currencies and they do not add. Fig 12 of the source set says, verbatim, "A composition costs in two currencies, and they do not add. Bits price the description. Actions price finding out whether it holds." A transfer of a benefit unit to a person does not by itself change that person's reach, that is, what they can actually compose or access. So the question is a union of at least four conditions with different anchors: producing the surplus, allocating benefit units per person, keeping reach from concentrating, and maintaining the habitat in which capability is expressed. One lever clears the place where one condition holds and leaves the intersections standing.
>
> Three things follow, all stated before the conclusion:
>
> 1. **The capability is not the model.** Fig 11: "capability is a property of agent-and-habitat, never of the agent alone." Handing out an isolated model changes no contact, so it changes no capability. What can travel is a method or a head start (Fig 4: "Only methods cross up. Only head starts come down."), not a recording of one occasion.
> 2. **Concentration is a measurement failure as much as a distribution failure.** When the frames that decide an allocation all read the same evidence, their agreement is one pool talking, and when the anchor moves when they push on it, everything drifts together coherently. Fig 2: "Alignment is triangulation, not negotiation. A mirror cannot correct you." Distribution without concentration therefore requires independent readings against a ground that does not update on the program's own reports, plus a seat able to refuse a verdict.
> 3. **Read per slot, never as an aggregate.** Fig 1: "The residual is indexed by object slot, not aggregated" and "Aggregating across slots is how a live signal disappears into an average." A falling national inequality number can hide one slot losing all real access.
>
> **What the framework does not return:** a tax rate, a universal basic income amount, a bound, or a named institution. That part is a declared `gap`, a verdict rather than a silence. The framework returns a shape any acceptable distribution must have, not the policy that fills it. Any concrete policy is a binding from another frame and has to be recorded with where it came from and what gap it explains.
>
> **What would refute the shape claim.** I wrote these down before sending this:
> 1. Show that moving a benefit unit raises the receiving slot's reachable composition set one-for-one against an anchor that does not update, for all slots.
> 2. Show that an isolated model transfer with no habitat change measurably increases receiving slots' capability against the ground.
> 3. Show that a distribution verdict produced by frames sharing one evidence pool, with an anchor that updates on their reports, survives independent re-testing.
>
> If any one holds, the corresponding claim is wrong and I will retract it. A one-line "this fails because..." is more useful to me than agreement. If you would rather not engage, no reply is needed.
>
> **If it survives, and only then:** the figures are the authority, not my summary of them.
> - Verbatim figure text with line numbers (local extract): `FRAMEWORK/reference/03-FIGURES_TEXT.md`
> - Canonical usage rule: `FRAMEWORK/reference/FIGURES_README_canonical.md`
> - Governing world reading on the two-currency split: Malhotra and Vogel, "The Predistribution Solution," Roosevelt Institute, 2025: https://rooseveltinstitute.org/publications/the-predistribution-solution
>
> **If you want to use the machinery:** the formula is in `FRAMEWORK/figures/THE_FORMULA.md`. Run it on a problem you know well and can check against a ground. Write down what would refute your result *before* you run it, and state the mode of what you get. A reported failure is more useful to me than a reported success.
>
> — [name]

## Why this version is ordered this way

| the move | figure-grounded reason |
|---|---|
| Output, residual, and refuter first; theory withheld | Fig 10 :52 "Bring a characterised residual, never the source." Fig 10 :42 "A verifier that can reconstruct the claim cannot verify it." |
| Do not ask for agreement | Fig 2 :35 "Alignment is triangulation, not negotiation. A mirror cannot correct you." |
| No request for trust or prestige | *The Recursive Self-Improvement Trap*: "Prestige must never be allowed to validate an action directly." Prestige can fund a trial; it cannot settle one. |
| Pre-registered refuter before the claim is accepted | Seat map rule carried from the exemplar: a falsifier written before the run is a design instrument. |
| Mode stated on the output | `THE_FORMULA.md`: "Mode. general · specified · grounded. State which." |
| No policy named | Fig 9 :32 "you get a kind of thing, never the answer itself"; canonical README: a shape, not a name. |
| Two currencies, not one score | Fig 12 :125 "A composition costs in two currencies, and they do not add." |
| Per slot, not aggregate | Fig 1 :19 and :22. |
| Habitat rather than model handout | Fig 11 :39-40, Fig 4 :22. |
| Links after the verdict | Fig 4 :25 "A recording carried upward looks like knowledge and is a description of one occasion." |
| Verdict recorded before theory is shown | Fig 2 :40 "agreement about the world is worthless when the agreeing frames share an evidence pool." |
| Offer the formula as a method to reuse | Fig 4 :22 "Only methods cross up. Only head starts come down." |

## One-to-one only

No bulk send, no purchased list, no ranked recipient list, no personal emails harvested from private posts. A public institutional submission route or an opt-in channel is used.
