# Isomers, order and analogues for the empathy question

**Mode: `specified` on the structural claims, `gap` on the habitat-specific atom list.** This is a simulated derivation checked against the figures, not a ground reading (see `05_what_shape_means.md` §8).

## RESIDUAL

The opening sentence bundles two questions. Three supporting derivations are owed: (1) the structure of "empathy" as a composed term, (2) the order in which E and N must be run, and (3) the analogues that make the shape recognizable in a low-overlap field. Each has its own falsifier.

## DOMAIN CENSUS

Atoms held in the relevant frames: observation `o'`, prediction `Γ(b,a)`, residual `R` per slot, and the bonds of sequence (→), comparison (⋛), production (⇒) and negation (¬). The human-cognition frame contributes perspective taking and affective resonance as already-available predicates; the machine-empathy frame contributes detection of a signal and prediction of a reaction (Wikipedia, *Artificial empathy*). No new primitive is imported. Bonds are the issue, not atoms.

## DERIVATION

### Q1. Structure: is "empathy" one atom, a molecule, or a functional group?

- Wikipedia's *Empathy* entry records that *"Affective and cognitive empathy are also independent from one another; someone who strongly empathizes emotionally may not necessarily be good in understanding another's perspective."* So the single word covers at least two independently varying components plus a behavioral output.
- Fig 12 :5: *"A set of ingredients is not one. The bond is the rest of the information, and it is the half nothing was recording."* A system that predicts a person's state and a system that resonates affectively share ingredients and differ in bond.
- Fig 12 :111: *"A vocabulary without bonds can name a state and cannot name a route to one."* A rating scale names a state (high empathy); it cannot say which route produced it.
- Fig 12 :117: *"isomer. Same atoms, different bond, different substance. The difference is invisible in the formula and decisive in the behaviour."* Over a fixed atom set, detection-plus-prediction and detection-plus-resonance are isomers: the rating can be identical, the substance is not.
- Fig 12 :123: *"functional group. A substructure whose presence fixes the outcome. The group says what kind; the qualifier says which member."* Affective resonance may be a functional group for a subset of residuals (comforting distress) and absent from others (impartial triage).
- **Falsifier, written before the run:** if a controlled ablation shows the same valence for perspective taking and affective resonance across every residual tested, the isomer and functional-group readings fail and "empathy" may be a single atom after all.

### Q2. Order: why the discriminator comes before the necessity verdict

- Fig 3 :19: *"Optimising a link that already works cannot rescue one that does not."*
- Fig 3 :27: *"A link with no instrument is not a link that works. It is a link nobody has looked at."*
- The necessity question presupposes a settled term "empathy." If that term is only a fluent reading with no settled source, then the necessity question is a comparison over an ungrounded quantity. Formula step 5: *"the ground settles it. Verified only by what pays. A gate is not the ground."*
- Therefore: describe R (union, per slot), settle the source discriminator (E), then run the ablation (N). Running N first and asking "does the machine need empathy to be social?" before the term is settled is a reading below the break. Fig 3 :22-23: it is a reading of nothing, not a weak reading.
- **Falsifier:** a source-discriminating instrument that also returns the necessity relation in the same measurement would collapse the order. Until then the dependency stands.

### Q3. Analogue 1: metamerism (perception and instrumentation)

The color case is the clean analogue. An instrument reads green; the source could be one green light, yellow plus blue, RGB, CMYK, or a scheme not yet discovered. The reading does not determine the source. The framework essay records that the case was settled *"not by argument, but by the arrival of a better instrument"* (the spectrometer). Wikipedia's *Affective computing* gives the same shape inside the machine-empathy field: emotion labels *"may or may not correspond to what the person is actually feeling,"* and genuine versus posed smiles differ in dynamics. The second fact is the edge an instrument can be improved from (Fig 6 :32: *"An instrument is not built from a description. It is improved from a worse instrument already returning something"*).

**Limit of the analogue:** a spectrometer discriminates physical sources in a controlled spectrum; a social situation has no comparably closed source set. The essay itself notes the full set of ways to produce a reading *"is not known to be closed or determined."* So the analogue licenses the search for a discriminator, not the assumption that one is nearby.

### Q4. Analogue 2: the intentional stance (philosophy of mind)

Wikipedia's *Theory of mind* records Dennett's intentional stance: treating a thing as having intentions because that predicts its behavior, and that this is *"a functional relationship, describing the use of a theory due to its practical utility, rather than the accuracy of its representation of the world."* This is a low-overlap, non-framework corroboration of the reading/source split. Taking the intentional stance toward an empathic chatbot is rational for prediction and silent about source. The same entry's term *"noosemia"* for users attributing mental states to generative AI shows the stance is cheaply triggered, which is exactly why an unregistered rater's agreement cannot be a witness.

**Limit of the analogue:** functional utility does not prove absence of source. It only shows the stance cannot decide the question. This is why the E verdict is `gap`, not "no."

### Q5. Analogue 3: the mask of sanity (clinical psychology)

Wikipedia's *Psychopathy* entry describes traits *"masked by superficial charm and immunity to stress, which create an outward appearance of normality."* The outward social reading can be produced while the affective atom is absent or different. This is a natural case of the same isomer structure as Q1: same outward formula, different bonds. It also weakens the universal necessity claim without settling any habitat-specific one. The entry's criticism of the construct (subjective, weakly predictive on some axes) is a warning to use it as a mechanism-shape analogue only, not as a diagnostic claim about any machine.

**Falsifier:** if the clinical literature showed that the outward appearance of normality never occurs without affective resonance, this analogue would fail and would be withdrawn.

### Q6. What order is the union?

- Fig 9 :57: *"What remains is smaller and it is also categorically different: a case in two conditions is not a harder case of either."* After E is split off, N is not "the same question but harder"; it is a different question with a different anchor.
- Fig 12 :104: *"A settled molecule becomes an atom for whatever composes over it. So irreducible is a fact about a level and never about the world, and there is no bottom."* Once a source-settled "empathy" term exists, it can become an operand in the N composition. Before that, it cannot.
- Fig 13 :121: *"Different routes can arrive at the same substance."* So N is answered by enumerating routes and testing which atom is shared, not by asserting one route is the route.

## CONCLUSION

Empathy in this question is a molecule with at least two independently varying components and a behavioral output, not an atom and not a seventh primitive. Over a fixed atom set, different ways of producing the same outward reading are isomers; for some residuals one component behaves as a functional group. The order is fixed by dependency: settle the discriminator before running the necessity ablation. The three analogues (metamerism, intentional stance, mask of sanity) are low-overlap corroborations of the reading/source split and of the fallback statement that different bonds can give the same outward substance; each has a stated limit. No figure line returns a specific instrument or a habitat-specific necessary atom, and neither is invented here.
