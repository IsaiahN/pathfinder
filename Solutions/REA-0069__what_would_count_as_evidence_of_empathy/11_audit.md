## Independent domain census (from the residual line alone)

I redid this census before reading the primary's conclusions, using the WATSON order: R by arity, symmetry and scale; one line per domain with actors / currency / anchor; partition or union; order by dependency.

- **R (arity / symmetry / scale).** The residual is the question as asked: evidence of machine empathy, and necessity for social competence. Arity: two, and they are different kinds of question (one asks for an instrument, one for a composition relation), so a **union**, not a hard question with two halves. The construct "empathy" inside the first part is itself a bundle of independently varying components, so there is a second union underneath. Symmetry: the outward reading is many to one across possible sources, so the inference from reading to source is ordered and lossy, not symmetric or identity. The necessity claim, "must be present in every route," is also ordered. Scale: first order within a single interaction, second order across a trajectory of interactions where a settled molecule can serve as an atom.
- **Domain 1, machine-internal frame.** Actors: library, belief, action, observation, residual per slot, bonds. Currency: description length and closure. Anchor: none internal; the ground is external. Not a judging domain without an external anchor.
- **Domain 2, observer and measurement frame.** Actors: raters, scales, coders, benchmarks. Currency: readings. Anchor: human judgment legitimate for a question constitutively about people, instrument only for a question about mechanism. This boundary is where the metamerism lives.
- **Domain 3, social competence and habitat frame.** Actors: machine, people, relations, conditions. Currency: outcomes. Anchor: an outcome that does not move when a frame thinks it did. Question type: a union of overlapping routes with residual-relative valence.
- **Order by dependency.** Describe R in effect terms; settle the source discriminator in D2; then run the necessity ablation in D3. Refuse the unproven inference before minting an affirmative (Formula step 2).

My census matches the primary's three domains and its union reading. Where the primary derived the union from Fig 9 :55 and the reading/source split from Fig 9 :32 plus the metamerism essay, I derived the union from the anchor mismatch between the two halves (an instrument anchor versus an outcome anchor) and the reading/source split from Fig 2 :29 (a non-updating anchor) and Fig 6 :32 (an instrument improved from a worse one). ## Round trip gap R_T

The Symbols table defines R_T as the gap between what was sent up and what came back down, `gap(x, (T_E ∘ T_A)(x))`, measuring what the coarser description cannot hold. Fig 4 :30 requires it to be measured rather than assumed. I ran the round trip on the condensation this folder actually uses.

1. **Sent up (x):** the verbatim figure set, chiefly Fig 9 :32 (shape, not the answer), Fig 6 :32 and :34 (instrument from an instrument already returning something), Fig 11 :49 and :51 (valence, no standing list), Fig 12 :117 (isomer) and Fig 13 :134 (unreachable is a verdict).
2. **Abstraction (T_A):** the shorthand carried in `07` and `09`: a reading is not a source; what would count is a discriminator with a non-updating anchor; empathy is not a standing necessary actor; both missing items are `gap`.
3. **Concretisation (T_E):** the question's ordinary phrasing pushed back toward an answer: "chatbots are rated as more empathetic than clinicians, so machines have empathy," and "socially competent behavior requires empathy."

**R_T is nonzero.** Three things the shorthand holds do not survive the trip back down:

- the **instrument condition**, which the ordinary reading replaces with a rating (the useful fact that a rating exists becomes a source claim);
- the **per-slot condition**, which the ordinary reading collapses into a single empathy score (Fig 1 :22);
- the **habitat and valence condition**, which the ordinary reading turns into a universal standing list (Fig 11 :51), losing the fact that the same actor changes valence with the residual.

Two things survive cleanly: a source discriminator is owed rather than a behavior list, and no standing necessity claim is available without an ablation. That is the residue worth sending. R_T was **not** measured numerically, because no ground reading exists; it is recorded qualitatively as the enumerated set of dropped constraints. Treating "the formula is the condensed version of the metalaws" as identity would set R_T to zero and would be the dead-reckoning error Fig 4 :30 names. The honest form is a lossy condensation with a measured gap, which is what `09_watson_answer.md` states.
