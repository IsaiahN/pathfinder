# The Tether's answer on machine empathy and necessity

**Mode: `specified` on the per-domain verdicts, `general` on the structural shape, `gap` on the specific instrument and the habitat-specific atom list. Not `grounded`.** This is a shape and a route, not a result. It is a simulated derivation checked for internal consistency against the figures (`05_what_shape_means.md` §8), not a ground reading.

## 1. The residual it answers

The question bundles two residuals with different anchors. Part E asks what observation would discriminate the sources of an empathic reading. Part N asks whether an actor called empathy is required in every composition that produces social competence. Fig 9 :55: *"A bundled problem is usually a union of overlapping conditions, not a list of separate ones."* Verdicts are kept separate and never averaged.

## 2. Part E: the shape of evidence

1. **A reading is not a source.** An empathic response is an observation that many internal sources can produce. *The Hard Problem is About Source*: *"If several paths create the same behavior, then behavior is a projection with lossy information."* Fig 9 :32: *"you get a kind of thing, never the answer itself."* So an outward reading is evidence of a reading, not yet evidence of empathy.
2. **What would count is a discriminator, not more readings.** Fig 6 :32: *"An instrument is not built from a description. It is improved from a worse instrument already returning something."* Fig 6 :34: *"Where nothing is, there is no edge to extend from, and the search for a proto-instrument is a search for one that is already there."* The edge exists: Wikipedia's *Affective computing* records that genuine and posed smiles differ in dynamics, so something already returns a reading that fails to resolve in some cases.
3. **The anchor must not move.** Fig 2 :29: *"An anchor is whatever settles the question and does not change its answer because of what any frame thinks."* Human judgment is the legitimate anchor for "was this good for this person," because the question is constitutively about people (Fig 2 :36-37); it is only an instrument for "what produced this," where it can be wrong on axes it does not measure. Conflating the two is the coherent-drift failure of Fig 2 :33-34.
4. **Per slot, not an average.** Fig 1 :19: *"The residual is indexed by object slot, not aggregated."* Fig 1 :22: *"Aggregating across slots is how a live signal disappears into an average."* An overall empathy score hides the person and situation where the live signal sits.
5. **Independence decides whether two raters are two frames.** Fig 2 :31: *"Independence, between two frames, is how little they draw on the same evidence."* Two scales on the same transcripts with raters trained on the same corpus are one witness (Fig 2 :40).
6. **A benchmark is a filter, not a witness.** Fig 9 :60: *"Use filters for the budget and witnesses for the verdict. Never let a filter hand you a verdict."* Fig 11 :70: *"A test harness is a substituted habitat. Which is why a synthetic solve proves wiring and never capability."* The fluency/accuracy distinction in *Decoding Tacit Knowledge* says the same thing: *"Sounding right isn't the same as being checked against the world."*

**E verdict (`specified` shape, `gap` on the instrument):** evidence of empathy would be a source-discriminating measurement with a non-updating anchor, independent frames, per-slot readings and a written failure condition. No figure names such an instrument for machines, and none was found in the world sources opened. That is `unreached`, not "impossible." Fig 13 :134: *"Unreachable is a verdict here, not a silence,"* and Fig 8 :39: *"So a search that finds nothing is never a proof of absence."*

## 3. Part N: the answer on necessity

1. **"Empathetic" is a label applied afterwards.** Fig 11 :36: *"Where nothing maintains anything, intelligence is a label applied afterwards to a lineage that met its ground. Nothing was intelligent; selection ran. The word does no work there."* By the same move, a socially competent trajectory that met a social ground does not need the label "empathetic" to explain it. Fig 11 :41: *"Which is the same move as asking what generates lift rather than whether a thing is a bird."*
2. **No standing list.** Fig 11 :51: *"No standing list of good actors survives a change of residual."* Fig 11 :49: *"An actor's valence is relative to the residual, not a property it carries."* Empathy helps in some tasks, is neutral in others, and can be exploited in others (*Empathy* entry: selective empathy, manipulative use, compassion fatigue).
3. **Different routes to the same substance.** Fig 13 :121: *"Different routes can arrive at the same substance."* Fig 12 :117: *"isomer. Same atoms, different bond, different substance."* Perspective taking without affective resonance, explicit norms and learned habit can compose to the same outcome; over a fixed atom set they are isomers or functional-group variants (Fig 12 :123).
4. **The clinical analogue is a demonstration, not a proof of a universal.** Wikipedia's *Psychopathy* entry shows an *"outward appearance of normality"* alongside impaired affective empathy, and notes possible links to *"interpersonally successful outcomes."* That is a metamerism case: the social reading is produced without the affective atom. It weakens a universal necessity claim; it does not enumerate which atoms do compose competence in any named habitat.

**N verdict (`specified` per domain, `gap` on the atom list):** empathy is not a universal necessary actor for socially competent behavior. Its necessity is a habitat-specific, residual-relative question, answerable only by an ablation that removes only the affective-resonance atom while holding prediction, norms, habitat and ground contact fixed, measured against an outcome neither side can move. No figure returns that list and no such ablation was run. Recorded as `gap`, not filled.

## 4. What it explicitly does not say

- Not that any particular machine is or is not empathetic. No source measurement was taken.
- Not that empathy is irrelevant. Its valence is residual-relative; in some habitats it may be the functional group that fixes the outcome.
- Not that a rating scale, benchmark or chatbot comparison is a source. All are filters for the budget.
- Not that the six-term set has a seventh member called empathy. Fig 13 :115: *"The set is closed. The arrangements are not."*
- Not a standing list of necessary social behaviors, and not a percentage.
- Not `grounded`. Nothing here is asserted as settled by an instrument.

## 5. What would settle it

- **For E:** a source-discriminating instrument that returns distinct, ground-checked outputs for two different internal sources that yield the same outward empathic behavior, with a non-updating anchor and an independence audit. That would narrow or close the `gap`.
- **For N:** a controlled ablation in a named habitat, removing only affective resonance, with a non-updating social outcome. That would return a per-habitat necessity verdict.
- **Standing refuters from `01` §4:** the re-census firing (a seventh primitive), the independence audit firing against our own witnesses, and an anchor that moves when the machine's own report changes. Any of these re-opens the relevant domain.

## 6. The honest stop

**Unreached, not unreachable.** The edge for a better instrument already exists in the literature on genuine versus posed expression; there is something returning a reading that fails to resolve. The habitat for N is enumerated from the world, never composed (Fig 11 :43-48), and no enumeration was performed here. The answer stops at the limit of the available instruments and records the lineage so the stop is checkable. The canonical README's rule governs: *"Indiscernibility is not identity."*

## 7. One-paragraph version for outreach

> The question bundles two questions with different anchors. On evidence, an empathic response is a reading that several internal sources can produce, so more readings of the same kind cannot settle the source. What would count is a shape of instrument: one that discriminates sources, ranges against an anchor that does not move when the system's own report changes, keeps the reading per person and situation, and is built from a proto-instrument already returning something. No such instrument is named by the figures or found in the sources opened, so that part is recorded as `unreached`, not "no evidence possible." On necessity, the figures refuse a standing list: the value of empathy depends on the residual, and different routes (perspective taking, explicit norms, habit, resonance) can arrive at the same socially competent outcome. Empathy is therefore not a universal necessary actor; which atom is required in which habitat is a question for an ablation against an outcome neither side can move, and that list is recorded as `gap`. Both verdicts carry their modes and their refuters, and neither claims to settle whether any particular machine is empathetic.

## 8. Cost, undoability and reversal

- **Cost if wrong:** if the necessity verdict is wrong in an affirmative direction, we exclude capable systems and design evaluation around an unnecessary trait; if wrong in a negative direction, we may permit systems that harm through a missing relational capacity. The E cost is spent independence: sending a recording instead of a generator converts outside checkers into believers, which Fig 2 :42 prices as *"Buy independence in proportion to the cost of being wrong."*
- **Undoable:** yes. Both verdicts are per domain and the gaps are recorded rather than filled; a source instrument or an ablation updates one domain without withdrawing the other.
- **Reversed by:** a ground reading, not agreement. See §5. Prestige, popularity, and a larger survey of raters are not such readings.
