## QUESTION

Decide, in effect terms, what the Tether says about the bundled question: what would count as evidence of empathy in a machine, and is empathy necessary for socially competent behavior? Part E is a measurement question, part N is a composition question. Option A: treat the empathic reading as evidence of empathy and assert that empathy is a standing requirement for social competence. Option B: split the union, return the shape of a source-discriminating instrument for E and a residual-relative, habitat-specific verdict for N, and record both gaps. What each leads to: A counts fluency as accuracy, manufactures a disqualifying correlation among raters, and closes a question the figures leave open; B sends a checkable shape, written refuters and an honest stop at the limit of the available instruments.

## DOMAIN CENSUS

R by arity, symmetry and scale, then one line per domain with actors / currency / anchor, partition or union, and order by dependency.

- **R (the residual).** Arity: at least two and a union. E and N have different anchors; inside E the construct "empathy" is itself a bundle of cognitive perspective taking, affective resonance, empathic concern and behavioral output, which vary independently (Wikipedia, *Empathy*, 2026). Symmetry: the reading is many to one, so inference from reading to source is lossy and ordered, not identity; the "must be present in every route" claim is also ordered, not a symmetric correlation. Scale: first order inside one interaction, second order across a social trajectory where a settled molecule becomes an atom for the next level (Fig 12 :104). Fig 9 :55 governs the union: a bundled problem is usually a union of overlapping conditions, not a list of separate ones.
- **Domain 1, machine-internal frame.** Actors: library Γ, belief b, action a, observation o', residual R per slot, bonds. Currency: description length and composition inside closure(Γ). Anchor: none internal; the ground is external. Without a nameable anchor this is not a judging domain but the object judged. No verdict on empathy is available from this domain alone.
- **Domain 2, observer and measurement frame.** Actors: human raters, self-report and clinical scales, behavioral coders, benchmark tests. Currency: ratings and readings of behavior. Anchor: human judgment is legitimately the anchor where the question is constitutively about people, and only an instrument where the question is about mechanism (Fig 2 :36-37). Question type: partition boundary between reading and source. Verdict: `specified` shape of evidence, `gap` on the specific discriminator.
- **Domain 3, social competence and habitat frame.** Actors: machine, people, relations (help, trust, coordination), conditions (context, culture, history). Currency: outcomes in the world. Anchor: an outcome that does not change because a frame thinks it did (Fig 2 :29). Question type: a union of overlapping routes with residual-relative valence. Verdict: `specified` that no standing universal necessity holds, `gap` on the atoms required for a named habitat.
- **Order by dependency.** Describe R (union, per slot), settle the source discriminator (D2), then run the necessity ablation (D3). A reading below the break is a reading of nothing (Fig 3 :22-23).

Strain test applied: Fig 2 :36-37 makes human judgment an anchor, while F3 says it only reads behavior. Re-census shows two different questions: "is this good for a person" is about people, "what produced this" is about mechanism. Different objects, so no strain. Fig 11 :51 (no standing list) versus the intuition that empathy is required is re-census into a habitat-specific claim in D3, also no strain.

## VERDICT (from the figures)

The question is a union and is unbundled. On E, an empathic reading is a projection; what would count as evidence is the shape of a source-discriminating instrument with a non-updating anchor, independent frames, per-slot readings and a written failure condition. No such instrument is named by the figures or found in the sources opened, so E is `unreached`, not impossible, and not silence. On N, empathy is not a standing universal necessary actor: its valence is residual-relative, no standing list survives a change of residual, and different bonds can arrive at the same socially competent substance. Which atom is required in which habitat is a `gap` settled only by a controlled ablation against a non-updating outcome. All verdicts are per domain, never averaged. Nothing here is `grounded`, and no claim is made about any particular machine.

- **cost if wrong:** bounded and locatable for the structure, because modes and refuters are stated before the conclusion. The real cost is spending the independence of outside readers by sending a recording instead of a generator, and, on N, either excluding capable systems by asserting an unnecessary requirement or permitting harm by denying a real one. Fig 2 :42 prices the first: "Buy independence in proportion to the cost of being wrong."
- **undoable:** yes. Both verdicts are per domain; the E and N gaps are recorded rather than filled. A source instrument updates E, an ablation updates one habitat in N, and neither withdrawal touches the other domain or the figure set.
- **reversed by:** a ground reading, not agreement. A source-discriminating instrument that distinguishes internal sources behind the same outward behavior would narrow or close E. A controlled ablation in a named habitat removing only affective resonance would return a per-habitat necessity verdict. A re-census requiring a seventh primitive, or showing the two components cannot compose from held atoms, would break the structure. An independence audit showing our corroborators draw on one pool would withdraw world support without flipping the figure verdict. Prestige, popularity and larger rater surveys are not such readings.
