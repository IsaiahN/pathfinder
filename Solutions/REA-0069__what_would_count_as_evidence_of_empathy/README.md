## What this question is, in effect terms

It is a bundled question with two parts that have different anchors, so it is a union, not one question with two halves (Fig 9 :54-55).

1. **The evidence part** asks what observation would license the word "empathy" for a machine. In effect: what instrument would discriminate among the several underlying sources that can produce the same outward reading? This is a measurement question. Its anchor is a source-discriminating instrument plus human judgment where the question is constitutively about people (Fig 2 :29, :36).
2. **The necessity part** asks whether an actor called empathy must be present in every composition that arrives at social competence. In effect: is empathy a required atom in every route to the substance, or one actor among several whose valence depends on the residual (Fig 11 :49-51)? This is a composition question. Its anchor is an outcome in the world that does not update on the system's own report.

What each option leads to: treating the outward reading as evidence of the source leads to counting fluency as accuracy, to a verdict that cannot tell simulation from resonance, and to manufacturing the very correlation that disqualifies a verdict (Fig 2 :40). Requiring a source-discriminating instrument and keeping the necessity verdict per habitat leads to a checkable shape, written refuters, and an honest stop where the instrument does not yet exist (Fig 13 :134).

**Direction bound as an ordinary term before the run:** an answer would look like (a) a stated shape of the instrument that could settle source, with an anchor that does not move, and (b) a per-domain ruling on whether empathy is a standing necessary actor or a residual-relative one, with the conditions under which it would be necessary written down. Both are bound and refusable. This is the formula's "Direction" discipline, not a preference carried unbound.

## What is deliberately not here

- No personal emails of named individuals.
- No ranking of people by how likely they are to agree that machines can be empathetic.
- No bulk-send plan.
- No claim that this derivation settles whether any particular machine is empathetic.

Three reasons in the figures' own words:

1. **Agreement is not an anchor.** Fig 2 :35: *"Alignment is triangulation, not negotiation. A mirror cannot correct you."* A list selected for people already inclined to attribute empathy to machines is a list selected to agree.
2. **Shared evidence is one witness.** Fig 2 :40: *"agreement about the world is worthless when the agreeing frames share an evidence pool."* Two empathy rating scales applied to the same transcripts by raters trained on the same corpus are one instrument wearing two names.
3. **Do not show the interior before the verdict.** Fig 10 :42: *"A verifier that can reconstruct the claim cannot verify it."* The first contact carries the residual and the refuter, never the framework.

Legally: scraping personal emails for unsolicited bulk outreach breaches GDPR and CAN-SPAM style rules in many jurisdictions. That is a practical failure on top of the structural one. Public opt-in institutional venues in `03` avoid both.
