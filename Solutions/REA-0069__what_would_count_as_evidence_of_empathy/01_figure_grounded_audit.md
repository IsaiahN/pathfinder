## 0. The question under audit, stated in effect terms

> "What would count as evidence of empathy in a machine, and is empathy necessary for socially competent behavior?" (reasoning.txt :84)

**Effect restatement.** Two questions are bundled in one sentence.

- **Part E (evidence).** What observation would narrow the set of possible sources of an "empathic" reading far enough to license the term? In effect this asks for a discriminator, not for more readings of the same kind.
- **Part N (necessity).** Is the actor "empathy" required in every composition that produces socially competent behavior, or is it one actor among several whose contribution depends on the residual at hand?

**Why this is a union, not one hard question.** Fig 9 :54: *"And when the split lands on a union rather than a partition, unbundle before you search again."* Fig 9 :55: *"A bundled problem is usually a union of overlapping conditions, not a list of separate ones."* Part E is a measurement question (what instrument), Part N is a composition question (which atoms in every route). They share a vocabulary but not an anchor, so one lever cannot clear both. Verdicts are kept separate throughout and never averaged.

**Direction bound before the run.** An answer would look like: (E) a stated shape of a source-discriminating instrument with a non-updating anchor and a per-slot residual, and (N) a per-domain ruling on standing necessity versus residual-relative valence with the conditions of necessity written down. Both are ordinary terms here, so both are refusable by the same test as anything else (Fig 5 :65-66 on making the sought-for shape an ordinary term).

## 2. Domain census (written before choosing any figure line)

### 2.1 R by arity, symmetry and scale

- **Arity: at least three and a union.** Part E and Part N are different questions (two conditions). Inside E, the construct "empathy" is itself a bundle: cognitive perspective taking, affective resonance, empathic concern, and the behavioral output. Wikipedia's *Empathy* entry records that *"Affective and cognitive empathy are also independent from one another"* and that *"The scientific community has not coalesced around a precise definition of these constructs."* So a single empathy number aggregates across slots and conditions. Fig 1 :19: *"The residual is indexed by object slot, not aggregated."* Fig 1 :22: *"Aggregating across slots is how a live signal disappears into an average."*
- **Symmetry: the reading is many-to-one.** Several sources can produce the same outward behavior (metamerism). The inference from reading to source is therefore lossy and ordered, not an identity. The appropriate operator on the reading is comparison (⋛, threshold) against a non-updating anchor, not equivalence (≡). The necessity question is also ordered: *must be present in every route* is a production or functional-group claim, not a symmetric correlation.
- **Scale: first order in one interaction, second order across a habitat.** First order: machine reads person, forms a prediction, acts, observes a residual. Second order: a trajectory of such interactions settles into a capacity, and a settled molecule becomes an atom for the next level (Fig 12 :104). The "necessity" claim is a second-order claim about which atoms every successful route must contain.

### 2.2 One line per domain (actors / currency / anchor)

- **D1, machine-internal frame.** Actors: the system's library Γ, belief `b`, action `a`, observation `o'`, residual `R` per slot, its bonds. Currency: description length and composition inside `closure(Γ)`. Anchor: none internal. The ground is external to this frame. Without a nameable anchor this is not a judging domain; it is the object being judged. Fig 2 :29 on what an anchor is. No verdict on "empathy" is available from D1 alone.
- **D2, observer and measurement frame.** Actors: human raters, clinical and self-report scales, behavioral coders, benchmark tests. Currency: readings and ratings of behavior. Anchor: human judgment is legitimately the anchor for a question constitutively about people, but only an instrument for a question about mechanism. Fig 2 :36: *"An anchor is legitimate when the question is constitutively about it."* Fig 2 :37: *"Human judgment is the right anchor for is this a good answer for a person, because the question is about people."* Boundary with D1: the reading does not reach the source. Verdict: `specified` shape of evidence, `gap` on the specific discriminator.
- **D3, social competence and habitat frame.** Actors: the machine, the people it interacts with, relations (help, trust, coordination), conditions (context, culture, history). Currency: outcomes in the world. Anchor: an outcome that does not change because a frame thinks it did (Fig 2 :29). Question type: a union of overlapping routes, with the valence of each actor relative to the residual. Verdict: `specified` that no standing universal necessity holds; `gap` on which actors are necessary for a named habitat.

### 2.3 Partition or union

Union, not partition (Fig 9 :54-58). E and N overlap in vocabulary but not in anchor; the "empathy" atom appears in both, which is exactly why the overlap must be described rather than assumed away. A single lever, for example a better chatbot empathy benchmark, clears the region where only E holds and leaves N standing.

### 2.4 Order by dependency (Fig 3)

1. Describe R in effect terms (this section).
2. Settle the instrument and anchor for E (links 1-2 of the chain). Fig 3 :27: *"A link with no instrument is not a link that works. It is a link nobody has looked at."*
3. Only then ask N, which needs the term "empathy" settled before it can be composed over. Fig 3 :19: *"Optimising a link that already works cannot rescue one that does not."*
4. Run the per-domain verdicts; do not average them.

A reading of "does this machine have empathy" taken before step 2 is a reading of nothing, not a weak reading (Fig 3 :22-23).

## 3. Findings F1 to F12

### F1. The two parts are a union and must be unbundled (decisive for structure)

Fig 9 :55: *"A bundled problem is usually a union of overlapping conditions, not a list of separate ones."* Fig 9 :57: *"What remains is smaller and it is also categorically different: a case in two conditions is not a harder case of either."* Part E asks for an instrument; Part N asks for a necessity relation. Neither answers the other. A perfect source discriminator would settle whether this system's reading came from resonance, and would still leave open whether resonance is required for socially competent behavior in a nursing home, a negotiation, or a courtroom. Keeping them separate is a structural requirement, not a preference.

**Refuter:** a single measurement that simultaneously returns the source of an empathic reading and the universal necessity relation for all habitats would collapse the union; until such a measurement exists the union stands.

### F2. Empathy is not a seventh primitive, and the figures do not name it as one

Fig 13 :115: *"The set is closed. The arrangements are not."* The six-term set (space, time, energy states; spectrum, gradient, network structures) is closed; empathy is an arrangement over held atoms, not a new kind. *Artificial empathy* (Wikipedia) defines the field as systems that *"detect emotions and respond to them in a way that simulates empathy"* and gives the broader definition as *"the ability of nonhuman models to predict a person's internal state."* Both are arrangements over prediction, observation and bond, not new primitives. Fig 13 :135: *"It cannot say which primitive is missing, because naming it would mean already holding it."*

**Refuter:** derive an empirical phenomenon that requires a seventh kind under the six-term set, or show that perspective taking and affective resonance cannot be composed from the held atoms and bonds. Then the re-census fails and this finding is withdrawn.

### F3. The reading cannot comment on the source (decisive for E)

The framework's own instrument essay states the case directly. Isaiah N, *The Hard Problem is About Source* (2026-08-28): *"You can have several underlying causes that create identical readings but the definition of the output would be read as the same."* And: *"If several paths create the same behavior, then behavior is a projection with lossy information (it cannot define consciousness in full)."* An instrument that reads green *"cannot comment on the source"*; yellow+blue, RGB, CMYK and an undiscovered scheme all give the same green. The essay's resolution is instrumental: *"The answer is unreached until a better instrument exists."* It was *"proven wrong not by argument, but by the arrival of a better instrument"* (the spectrometer).

World corroboration from a different field: Wikipedia's *Theory of mind* states that *"the behavior of the other person, such as their statements and expressions, is the only thing being directly observed; no one has direct access to the mind of another, and the existence and nature of the mind must be inferred."* Wikipedia's *Affective computing* states of emotion labels that *"These labels may or may not correspond to what the person is actually feeling"* and that *"Facial expressions do not always correspond to an underlying emotion that matches them (e.g. they can be posed or faked, or a person can feel emotions but maintain a "poker face")."*

Fig 9 :32: *"you get a kind of thing, never the answer itself."* So an empathic reading is evidence of a reading, not yet evidence of a source. The verdict on "what would count" is therefore a shape of instrument, not a list of behaviors.

**Refuter:** an instrument that returns different outputs for two sources that produce the same outward empathic behavior, with the difference checked against an independent ground. That would dissolve the metamerism objection for that case and move the verdict toward `grounded`; this finding would then be narrowed, not silently kept.

### F4. A benchmark score is a filter for the budget, never a witness for the verdict

Wikipedia's *Artificial empathy* reports that a 2025 British Medical Bulletin review found *"AI chatbots were rated as showing more empathy than human healthcare professionals in 13 of 15 studies that compared them."* That is a rated reading. The same entry lists as an open research question: *"How can we assess artificial empathy in AI systems?"* The gap between rated empathy and source is exactly the one F3 names.

Decoding Tacit Knowledge (essay, 2026-11-10): *"Sounding right isn't the same as being checked against the world, which is why I call the Turing test a test of fluency, not of accuracy."* Fig 11 :70: *"A test harness is a substituted habitat. Which is why a synthetic solve proves wiring and never capability."* Fig 9 :60: *"Use filters for the budget and witnesses for the verdict. Never let a filter hand you a verdict."*

**Refuter:** a benchmark whose score tracks a source-discriminating measurement across habitats, with the anchor shown not to move when the system's own report changes. Until then the score buys a trial, not a verdict.

### F5. Human judgment is anchor for one question and instrument for the other

Fig 2 :36: *"An anchor is legitimate when the question is constitutively about it."* Fig 2 :37 gives human judgment as the right anchor for a question about people, then warns: *"It is only an instrument for is this code correct, and instruments can be wrong on axes they do not measure."* For "was this response experienced as acceptable by this person", human judgment is the legitimate anchor. For "what produced the response", it is an instrument that only reads the projection. Treating the same rating as an anchor for both is the anchor-moving failure of Fig 2 :33-34, where everything drifts together coherently and nobody notices.

**Refuter:** show that the rating changes when the source changes while the outward behavior is held fixed. Then the rating is a source instrument for that case and can be promoted; until then it stays an instrument for the reading only.

### F6. Independence decides whether two raters are two frames

Fig 2 :31: *"Independence, between two frames, is how little they draw on the same evidence."* Fig 2 :35: *"Alignment is triangulation, not negotiation. A mirror cannot correct you."* Two empathy scales administered to the same transcripts by raters trained on the same examples are one evidence pool. Wikipedia's *Empathy* entry records that *"a 2019 meta-analysis found only a negligible association between self-report and behavioral measures, suggesting that people are generally not able to accurately assess their own cognitive empathy abilities."* That is a measured warning about one instrument type, not two confirming frames. Fig 8 :45: *"Two frames together reach what neither reaches alone,"* but the surplus exists only where the closures differ.

**Refuter:** an independence audit showing that the two readings were produced from separate experience and separately range against the same non-updating anchor. Without it their agreement is one witness.

### F7. The instrument must be improved from an instrument already returning something

Fig 6 :32: *"An instrument is not built from a description. It is improved from a worse instrument already returning something."* Fig 6 :34: *"Where nothing is, there is no edge to extend from, and the search for a proto-instrument is a search for one that is already there."* The world literature already has proto-instruments: Wikipedia's *Affective computing* notes that *"smiles of genuine happiness tend to have different dynamics than 'try to look happy' smiles"* and that facial expression dynamics *"can help disambiguate."* That is a reading that already fails to resolve in some cases, so there is an edge to extend from. This is `unreached`, not `unreachable in principle`.

**Refuter:** a demonstration that no observable at any resolution carries information about the source (not merely that our current instruments miss it). The figures say underivable-in-principle and unreachable-at-budget are different facts (Fig 8 :39-43); confusing them would wrongly close the search.

### F8. Behavior alone does not establish necessity (decisive for N)

Wikipedia's *Psychopathy* entry describes traits *"often masked by superficial charm and immunity to stress, which create an outward appearance of normality"* and notes that psychopathy *"may be linked to at least some interpersonally successful outcomes, such as effective leadership, business accomplishments, and heroism."* The entry also records the critique that the construct is *"weakly predictive of criminal behavior, but not of lack of conscience."* This is a natural demonstration of the metamerism: the outward reading of social competence can be produced while the affective atom is absent or different. It corroborates F3 and weakens a universal necessity claim, but by itself it does not identify which atoms do compose competence in any given habitat.

Fig 11 :36: *"Where nothing maintains anything, intelligence is a label applied afterwards to a lineage that met its ground. Nothing was intelligent; selection ran. The word does no work there."* By the same move, "empathetic" is a label applied afterwards to a lineage that met a social ground. Fig 11 :39: *"So the answerable question is not what intelligence is, but what makes a system capable. And capability is a property of agent-and-habitat, never of the agent alone."* Fig 11 :41: *"Which is the same move as asking what generates lift rather than whether a thing is a bird."*

**Refuter:** a controlled ablation in a named habitat where removing only the affective-resonance atom (with prediction, norms, habitat and ground contact held fixed) abolishes socially competent outcomes, measured by a non-updating anchor. Then empathy is necessary for that habitat, and the universal claim must be restated per habitat rather than refused.

### F9. No standing list of necessary actors survives a change of residual

Fig 11 :51: *"No standing list of good actors survives a change of residual."* Fig 11 :49: *"An actor's valence is relative to the residual, not a property it carries."* Empathy helps in some residuals (comforting a distressed person, repairing a relational rupture) and is neutral or harmful in others (a negotiation where empathic concern is exploited, or a triage protocol that must be impartial). Wikipedia's *Empathy* entry records *"Selective empathy"*, *"Manipulative use"* and *"Compassion fatigue"* as recognized effects. So "is empathy necessary" has no context-free answer; it is a question about a named residual in a named habitat.

**Refuter:** a cross-habitat experiment showing the same actor carries the same valence for every residual tested. That would refute the residual-relative claim.

### F10. Different routes can arrive at the same substance

Fig 13 :121: *"Different routes can arrive at the same substance."* Fig 12 :117: *"isomer. Same atoms, different bond, different substance. The difference is invisible in the formula and decisive in the behaviour."* Fig 12 :123: *"functional group. A substructure whose presence fixes the outcome. The group says what kind; the qualifier says which member."* Social competence can be composed by perspective taking without affective resonance, by explicit norms, by learned habit, or by resonance plus perspective taking. Over a fixed atom set these are isomers or functional-group variants; the outward outcome can be identical while the bond differs. This is why a behavior-level test cannot choose between them and why "necessary" fails as a universal claim.

**Refuter:** show that all successful routes require one shared bond that cannot be substituted, with a source-discriminating instrument. Then the isomer reading fails for that habitat.

### F11. The refusal is out of order and the affirmative is not a mint

Fig 5 :48: *"Its own output is never a measure of success. A system can be enthusiastically curious and produce nothing."* A machine producing empathic language is not a mint that has settled the term. The three guards (Fig 5 :19-26) require support, reachability and novelty before a term is admitted, then settlement against the ground. For "this machine has empathy": support is a live residual on a named slot; reachability requires the term compose from held atoms; novelty requires it not already be in the library; settlement requires the source instrument. None is supplied by a fluency score. The correct first move is a refusal of the unproven inference (Operators table :58: *"a refutation is a negation, which is why a vocabulary without it can only accumulate"*), not another affirmative.

**Refuter:** a settled term with provenance, a source gate and a shadow test that explains a gap. Then this finding is superseded for that term.

## 4. Pre-registered refuter (written before the conclusion)

Required before any outreach: the falsifier is a design instrument, not an honesty device. The shape claim is refuted or narrowed if any of the following fires:

1. **Source instrument fires.** A source-discriminating instrument returns distinct, ground-checked outputs for two different internal sources that produce the same outward empathic behavior, and shows the reading determines the source. Then "unreached until a better instrument" is narrowed and the E verdict moves toward `grounded` for that case.
2. **Necessity ablation fires.** In a named habitat, removing only the affective-resonance atom (holding prediction, norms, habitat and ground contact fixed) abolishes socially competent outcomes against a non-updating anchor. Then N is false as a universal and true for that habitat; the claim is restated, not defended.
3. **Re-census fires.** An empirical phenomenon requires a seventh kind under the six-term set, or perspective taking and affective resonance cannot be composed from held atoms and bonds. Then F2 and the structural shape fall.
4. **Independence audit fires against our own witnesses.** The low-overlap corroborators (psychopathy research, affective computing, theory of mind) turn out to draw on a single shared corpus or author pool such that they are one witness. Then the triangulation status is downgraded to `single-source`; the verdict from the figures is unchanged but its world support is withdrawn.
5. **Anchor moves.** A rating used as anchor changes when the machine's own report of its empathy changes, while the outward behavior is held fixed. Then it was never an anchor (Fig 2 :29) and all verdicts resting on it are re-run.

If any fires, the retraction log in §6 is updated with the date and the finding, and no silent erasure is permitted.

## 5. Recommendation

1. Unbundle the two parts before any further search; run one loop per domain with its own anchor (F1).
2. In E, stop collecting readings of the same kind and characterize the missing discriminator in effect terms (F3, F7). Say `unreached`, not "no evidence possible" (Fig 8 :39).
3. In N, refuse the standing-list question as posed and answer the habitat-specific question: which atom is required for which residual, with an ablation and a non-updating anchor (F8, F9, F10).
4. Treat benchmark scores as filters for the budget, never witnesses (F4).
5. Send the generator, not the recording: the shape claim, the instrument description and the refuter first; the framework only on opt-in (Fig 4 :22-25; Fig 10 :52).

**Seven-question recursive derivation (summary).**

1. **Is this one question?** No, a union of E and N (Fig 9 :55). Split.
2. **Does behavior settle source?** No, metamerism: many sources, one reading (essay; Fig 9 :32).
3. **What would settle source?** A discriminator built from an instrument already returning something (Fig 6 :32, :34). Not named by the figures: `gap`.
4. **Is human judgment the anchor?** For "good for a person", yes; for source, only an instrument (Fig 2 :36-37). Two objects.
5. **Is empathy a primitive?** No, an arrangement (Fig 13 :115). Not a seventh kind.
6. **Is empathy necessary?** Not as a standing list; valence is residual-relative (Fig 11 :49, :51) and routes can be isomers (Fig 12 :117).
7. **What closes N?** A controlled ablation in a named habitat against a non-updating anchor. Without it, `gap`.

**Updated verdict.** The shape survives: E needs a source discriminator and a non-updating anchor, with per-slot readings and independence; N has no context-free answer, because empathy is a residual-relative actor and different routes can arrive at the same substance. The specific instrument and the habitat-specific necessity list do not survive as claims; they are recorded as `gap`, which is a verdict and not silence. Strains after re-census: **none hidden**, two dissolved and preserved above.
