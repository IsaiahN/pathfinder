# Expert verification packet — machine empathy and necessity

**What this is:** a claim in a form a researcher in machine empathy, affective computing, theory of mind or social robotics can check. Mode, derivation and refuters are stated before the conclusion, so a reader knows what to look for and what would settle it.

**Mode: `specified` on the two per-domain verdicts, `general` on structural shape, `gap` on the instrument and the atom list. Not `grounded`.** This is a simulated derivation checked for internal consistency against the figures, not a ground reading. No measurement was taken.

## 1. The claim, in one paragraph

The question bundles a measurement problem (E) and a composition problem (N), with different anchors. On E: an empathic response is a reading that several internal sources can produce, so more of the same readings cannot settle the source; what would count is a shape of instrument, discriminating among sources, ranging against a non-updating anchor, indexed per person and situation, and improved from a proto-instrument already returning something. No such instrument is named by the figures or found in the sources opened, so E is `unreached`, not impossible. On N: empathy is not a universal necessary actor, because its valence is relative to the residual and different bonds can arrive at the same socially competent substance; which atom is required in which habitat is a `gap` settled only by a controlled ablation against an outcome neither side can move. Both verdicts carry written refuters, and neither claims to settle whether any particular machine is empathetic.

## 2. Three checkable sub-claims

### C1. Reading versus source (framework-internal corroborated by measurement theory)
- **Statement:** an empathic reading does not by itself determine its source; the E verdict is a `gap` on a discriminator, not an assertion that evidence is impossible.
- **Verbatim source:** Fig 9 :32 *"you get a kind of thing, never the answer itself."* Fig 6 :32 *"An instrument is not built from a description. It is improved from a worse instrument already returning something."* Fig 8 :39 *"So a search that finds nothing is never a proof of absence."*
- **World corroboration:** Isaiah N, *The Hard Problem is About Source* (adventuresinml, 2026-08-28): *"If several paths create the same behavior, then behavior is a projection with lossy information."* Wikipedia, *Theory of mind*: *"the behavior of the other person... is the only thing being directly observed; no one has direct access to the mind of another."*
- **Refuter:** an instrument that returns distinct, ground-checked outputs for two internal sources that yield the same outward empathic behavior, and shows the reading determines the source. Then this sub-claim is narrowed and E moves toward `grounded` for that case.
- **Status:** `specified`, triangulated across two figures with low overlap (a composition limit and an instrument rule) plus a high-overlap framework essay. The world corroborators span philosophy of mind and cognitive science; the essay shares the author, so it is one pool.

### C2. No standing necessary actor; routes can be isomers (framework-internal)
- **Statement:** empathy is not required in every route to social competence; its valence is residual-relative, and over a fixed atom set different routes are isomers or functional-group variants. The habitat-specific necessity list is a `gap`.
- **Verbatim source:** Fig 11 :51 *"No standing list of good actors survives a change of residual."* Fig 11 :49 *"An actor's valence is relative to the residual, not a property it carries."* Fig 13 :121 *"Different routes can arrive at the same substance."* Fig 12 :117 *"isomer. Same atoms, different bond, different substance. The difference is invisible in the formula and decisive in the behaviour."*
- **What to check:** is the isomorphism claim faithful to the figure definition? Is the clinical analogue (outward normality without affective empathy) a metamerism case rather than a claim about all social competence?
- **Refuter:** a controlled ablation in a named habitat, removing only the affective-resonance atom while holding prediction, norms, habitat and ground contact fixed, that abolishes socially competent outcomes against a non-updating anchor. Then necessity is real for that habitat and this sub-claim is restated, not defended.
- **Status:** `specified` per domain, triangulated across Fig 11, Fig 12 and Fig 13 (low overlap between valence, bond and route). World corroboration from clinical psychology and cognitive science is mechanism-shape only.

### C3. The anchor and the benchmark boundary (framework-internal)
- **Statement:** human judgment is a legitimate anchor for "good for a person" but only an instrument for "what produced this"; a benchmark or rating score is a filter for the budget, never a witness for the verdict.
- **Verbatim source:** Fig 2 :29 *"An anchor is whatever settles the question and does not change its answer because of what any frame thinks."* Fig 2 :37 *"Human judgment is the right anchor for is this a good answer for a person, because the question is about people."* Fig 9 :60 *"Use filters for the budget and witnesses for the verdict. Never let a filter hand you a verdict."* Fig 11 :70 *"A test harness is a substituted habitat. Which is why a synthetic solve proves wiring and never capability."*
- **What to check:** does the rating used in a given study move when the machine's own report of its empathy changes while behavior is held fixed? If so it was never an anchor (Fig 2 :29). Do two rating instruments draw on separate experience or one corpus?
- **Refuter:** a rating that tracks a source-discriminating measurement across habitats, with the anchor shown not to move when the system's own report changes. Then it can be promoted from filter to witness for that case.
- **Status:** `specified`, triangulated across Fig 2 and Fig 9/Fig 11 (low overlap between anchor theory and budget/seat rules).

## 3. What is explicitly not claimed

- Not that any particular machine is or is not empathetic; no source measurement was taken.
- Not that empathy is a seventh primitive. Fig 13 :115 *"The set is closed. The arrangements are not."*
- Not that a high empathy benchmark score, a chatbot comparison, or a fluent reply is evidence of a source. The 2025 British Medical Bulletin review found chatbots *"rated as showing more empathy than human healthcare professionals in 13 of 15 studies"*; that is a rating, not a source.
- Not a standing list of socially competent behaviors, and not a percentage.
- Not that empathy is irrelevant. Its valence is residual-relative and it may be the functional group in a specific habitat.
- Not `grounded`. Nothing here is asserted as settled by an instrument.
- Not a claim that this derivation settles its own question. *The Recursive Self-Improvement Trap*: *"the output is only as good as the validator. The agent, or the tool, or the frame, or the theory, cannot validate itself."*

## 5. What would settle it

1. A source-discriminating instrument with a non-updating anchor and an independence audit (settles E).
2. A controlled ablation in a named habitat removing only affective resonance (settles N for that habitat).
3. A re-census firing: a seventh primitive, or evidence that perspective taking and affective resonance cannot compose from held atoms (settles the structure).
4. An independence audit against our own corroborators, or an anchor that moves (withdraws world support or re-opens the verdict).
5. Prestige or agreement is not a settlement. Fig 2 :42 *"Buy independence in proportion to the cost of being wrong."*

## 6. What an expert can check in twenty minutes

1. Read Fig 9 :32, Fig 6 :32, Fig 8 :39, Fig 11 :49, :51 and Fig 12 :117. Six short passages.
2. Check C2 against your own field: is the isomer reading faithful, and is the clinical analogue only a metamerism demonstration?
3. Name one observable that would distinguish two internal sources of the same empathic behavior, or say why none is available. That is the E discriminator, and naming a candidate is not the same as settling it.
4. Name one habitat where removing affective resonance would abolish competent outcomes, and one where it would not. That is the N ablation design.
5. Send the refutation. A failure is more useful than an endorsement.
