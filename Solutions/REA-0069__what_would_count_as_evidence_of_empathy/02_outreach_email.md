# Outreach draft: check the separator before you decide about the claim

**Use:** one-to-one, or as a cover note attached to a submission through a public venue in `03_public_contact_points.csv`. **Not** for bulk send.

**Hard precondition:** do not send until the direction term and the refuter in `01_figure_grounded_audit.md` §4 are written down and dated. The offer collapses on contact if the refuter is improvised after the fact.

---

**Subject:** One check on the empathy-in-machines claim, before theory

> Hi [name],
>
> I have one specific output I would like you to check against what you already know. I am not asking you to read the framework first, and I am not asking you to agree with a position on machine empathy. If the output does not survive, there is nothing else to look at.
>
> **The question, split into two.** "What would count as evidence of empathy in a machine, and is empathy necessary for socially competent behavior?" bundles a measurement question and a composition question. They have different anchors, so I run them separately.
>
> **The output, part E (`specified`, not `grounded`).** An outward empathic reading is a projection, not a source. More of the same readings does not settle what produced them. What would count as evidence is a *shape* of instrument: it must discriminate among the different internal sources that can yield the same reading, range against an anchor that does not move when the system's own report changes, keep the reading indexed per person and per situation rather than collapsing it into one score, and be built from a proto-instrument that is already returning something that fails to resolve. I am not claiming such an instrument exists. The figures do not name one. I record that as `unreached`, not as "no evidence is possible."
>
> **The output, part N (`specified` per domain).** Empathy is not a standing universal necessary actor. Its value depends on the residual at hand: it helps in some tasks, is neutral in others, and can be exploited in others. Different routes, such as perspective taking without affective resonance, explicit norms, or learned habit, can arrive at the same socially competent outcome. Which atom is required for which task is a question for an ablation in a named habitat against an outcome neither side can move. I do not have that list and the figures do not return one. That is a `gap`, recorded rather than filled.
>
> **What I am not claiming.** That the metric of a benchmark score is evidence of a source. That a fluent empathic reply is the same as a checked one. That empathy is a seventh primitive or a standing list item. That this derivation settles whether any particular machine is empathetic. The figures return a shape, not a name.
>
> **What would refute the shape claim.** I wrote these down before sending this:
> 1. A source-discriminating instrument returns distinct, ground-checked outputs for two different internal sources that produce the same outward empathic behavior, and the reading then determines the source. That narrows the E claim and moves it toward `grounded`.
> 2. In a named habitat, removing only the affective-resonance atom (holding prediction, norms, habitat and ground contact fixed) abolishes socially competent outcomes against a non-updating anchor. Then necessity is real for that habitat and my universal refusal is wrong.
> 3. A re-census shows the phenomenon requires a seventh primitive, or that perspective taking and affective resonance cannot be composed from the held atoms and bonds.
> 4. An independence audit shows my low-overlap corroborators draw on one shared corpus, in which case the world support collapses to one witness even though the figure verdict stands.
>
> A one-line "this fails because..." is more useful to me than agreement. If you would rather not engage, no reply is needed.
>
> **If it survives, and only then:** the sources are below. The figures are the authority, not my summary of them.
> - The figures and formula for direct download (branch `general-agent`): https://github.com/IsaiahN/tether/tree/general-agent/figures
> - The canonical usage rule: https://github.com/IsaiahN/tether/blob/general-agent/figures/README.md
> - The instrument essay, "The Hard Problem is About Source": https://adventuresinml.substack.com/p/the-hard-problem-is-about-source
>
> **If you want to use the machinery:** the formula is in the figures link. Run it on a problem you know well and can check against a ground. Write down what would refute your result *before* you run it, and state the mode of what you get. A reported failure is more useful to me than a reported success.
>
> — [sender]

## Why this version is ordered this way

| the move | figure-grounded reason |
|---|---|
| Split the two questions before asserting anything | Fig 9 :55 *"A bundled problem is usually a union of overlapping conditions, not a list of separate ones."* One lever cannot clear the intersections. |
| Output and refuter first, theory second | Fig 10 :52 *"Bring a characterised residual, never the source."* Frames range against the ground separately (Fig 2 :45). |
| Do not ask for agreement or trust | Fig 2 :35 *"Alignment is triangulation, not negotiation. A mirror cannot correct you."* |
| Pre-registered refuter | The seat rule: a falsifier written before the run is a design instrument, not only an honesty device. |
| Mode on every substantive claim | `THE_FORMULA.md`: *"Mode. general · specified · grounded. State which."* |
| No claim that a benchmark score is a source | Fig 9 :60 *"Use filters for the budget and witnesses for the verdict. Never let a filter hand you a verdict."* Fig 11 :70 on a synthetic solve proving wiring and never capability. |
| Shape, not a name, for the missing instrument | Fig 9 :32 *"you get a kind of thing, never the answer itself."* |
| `gap` recorded, not filled | Fig 13 :134 *"Unreachable is a verdict here, not a silence,"* and Fig 8 :39 on a search that finds nothing not proving absence. |
| No "the machine really feels" framing, no prestige ask | *The Recursive Self-Improvement Trap*: prestige *"can give an action a budget to be tried. But it cannot settle an action. Only the ground can settle it."* |
| Offer the formula as a method only after the verdict | Fig 4 :22 *"Only methods cross up. Only head starts come down."* A generator can be given away without giving away an answer. |
| Ask each user for their own ground and refuter | `THE_FORMULA.md` "Direction": a frame cannot check the direction of its own question. |
