## Wider literature that bears on the question

1. **Instrument discrimination (low overlap with the framework).** The metamerism argument in *The Hard Problem is About Source* is a general measurement claim: a reading that cannot distinguish sources demonstrates its own inadequacy, and the color case was settled by a spectrometer, not an argument. Applied here, it converts "what counts as evidence" from a debate about behavior into a search for a discriminator already returning something (Fig 6 :32, :34). This is the strongest corroboration of the E verdict, but its author is the framework author, so it is high-overlap and carries no independent witness weight on its own.
2. **Human cognition and the construct (medium overlap).** Wikipedia's *Empathy* and *Theory of mind* entries show that the construct is a bundle, that cognitive and affective components are independent, that self-report tracks poorly against behavior, and that other minds are never directly observed. These are ordinary scientific sources, not framework sources, so they are genuinely lower-overlap corroboration of the union and reading/source split.
3. **Clinical demonstration (low overlap).** Wikipedia's *Psychopathy* entry shows an outward appearance of normality produced alongside impaired affective empathy, and records that the clinical construct itself is contested and weakly predictive on some axes. It corroborates the metamerism shape without settling which atoms compose social competence.
4. **Machine-empathy field (directly on question).** Wikipedia's *Artificial empathy* and *Affective computing* entries show that the field currently measures ratings and emotion labels, that labels may not match feelings, and that genuine and posed expressions differ in dynamics. The second fact is the proto-instrument edge: there is something already returning a reading that fails to resolve, so this is `unreached`, not underivable.
5. **Evaluation critique (high overlap).** The fluency/accuracy distinction in *Decoding Tacit Knowledge* and the validator rule in *The Recursive Self-Improvement Trap* are framework essays. They corroborate the refusal to count a benchmark as a source, but they share the author's evidence pool with the figures.

**Overlap statement.** The four framework essays and the canonical README are one evidence pool with the figures. The five Wikipedia entries span medicine, clinical psychology, cognitive science and computer science but are encyclopedic syntheses and partly cite the same underlying studies, so they are corroboration of mechanism shape, not independent experimental witnesses. No world source in this folder conflicts with the figure verdict; the conflict rule was not triggered. If a conflict appeared, the procedure is to return to the domain census, not to flip the verdict.

## Distribution: offer, do not push

- **Fig 4 :22-25** - *"Only methods cross up. Only head starts come down. A recording carried upward looks like knowledge and is a description of one occasion."* Sending the full framework before anyone has described a residual sends a recording. Lead with the split, the shape and the refuter; offer links after.
- **Fig 10 :42** - *"A verifier that can reconstruct the claim cannot verify it."* A recipient who has absorbed the framework before giving a verdict has joined the frame being checked.
- **Fig 9 :27-28** - *"describe it before you go looking, or any frame you pick will seem to fit."* Ask what they would expect to see if the source claim were true before showing the essay.
- **Fig 2 :40** - *"agreement about the world is worthless when the agreeing frames share an evidence pool."* Do not select recipients for likely agreement and do not pool later agreement as corroboration.
- **Fig 9 :60** - *"Use filters for the budget and witnesses for the verdict."* Venue prestige is a filter for where to try, never a reason to accept a claim.

**Recommended order in a reply:** (1) the two-part residual and the shape claim; (2) ask what they would count as a failure, and record the answer; (3) only then the three links, with the note that the figures, not the summary, are the authority; (4) if they are curious, the formula as a method with the instruction to write a fresh refuter.

## Two-stage offer

1. **Stage 1:** the residual and its four refuters. The recipient checks the shape without holding the framework interior. This is opt-in by construction: no follow-up is sent unless they ask.
2. **Stage 2:** if and only if they want it, the three source links. Their verdict on stage 1 is already recorded and is never pooled with later agreement (Fig 2 :40).
3. **Stage 3:** the formula as a method to reuse, with each user writing their own ground and refuter and stating their own mode (`THE_FORMULA.md` "Direction").

**Hard precondition:** stages 1 to 3 only work if the stage-1 shape survives. It does so here only with the modes and gaps stated. If a reader supplies refuter 1 or 2, the stage-1 claim is narrowed immediately and the retraction log in `01` is updated with the date.

## Hosting

Nothing is hosted or sent by this agent. The packet in `08_expert_verification_packet.md` is the file to host. Prefer an opt-in download page that lets a recipient decline contact over an attachment pushed to a list. A pushed attachment both breaches the structural rule and creates a legal problem in jurisdictions with unsolicited-email rules.
