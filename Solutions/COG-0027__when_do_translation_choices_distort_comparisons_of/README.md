## Restatement as an effect

Two concepts, one held in language-culture A and one in language-culture B, are compared after one has been carried into the other's terms by a translation choice. The residual R is the gap between that comparison and what the comparison would be if each concept were first settled against its own cultural ground. A translation choice distorts when the gap is contributed by the transform rather than by a real difference between the concepts. What an answer would look like: a list of conditions, each falsifiable, under which the transform contributes mass to R. Those conditions are bound here as an ordinary term so they can accrue a residual (Formula, "Direction").
