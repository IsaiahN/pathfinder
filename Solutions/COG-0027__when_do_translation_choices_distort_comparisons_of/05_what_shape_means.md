## 1. A shape is a constraint, not a filler

From *The Library of Babel*, fetched this session: "This means that you can cut your search short if the set of books returned is small and descriptive enough that it provides a general shape of the answer that satisfies your question." And: "With each new term gained, you narrow the space by definition and deepen your vocabulary."

For COG-0027 a shape is therefore the narrowed set of conditions a translation must satisfy before a comparison can be trusted. It is not the selection of a particular equivalent, and it is not a verdict that culture A differs from culture B.

- Shape: "the transform must have a measured semantic round trip; the frames must be independent; the relation must survive substitution."
- Name: "translate X as Y" or "culture A is more individualistic than B."

## 2. How the shape is obtained

- *Library of Babel*: use the vocabulary you already have that is closest, look for recurring new terms that overlap or correlate with it, recurse. "With each new term gained, you narrow the space by definition and deepen your vocabulary."
- Fig 6 :30 "outward: does another frame already hold it? The description travels. Fails by being sharpened into terms only we can read. Cast it in effect terms."
- Fig 9 :30-31 "your words are borrowed too, so describe the effect rather than the cause."
- *Decoding Tacit Knowledge* (as recorded in the exemplar mapping): "When contact isn't possible, the best you can send is a plain description of what you'd expect to see, because anyone can check that against the world."
- *Library of Babel* on stopping: "You will know that you are done because the finish line will also be the starting place, as there is nowhere else to search and nothing else that can describe the answer better."

Applied here: describe the failure (which item, which term, which direction of change) before choosing a theory of equivalence. That is Fig 8 :53 "Residual first, frame second."

## 3. What licenses a name

Only two things, both outside the shape:

1. **The ground settles it.** Formula step 5: "the ground settles it. Verified only by what pays. A gate is not the ground." Here: a ground-anchored task on which the translated term behaves as the source term does.
2. **A provenanced import from a frame whose closure differs** (Fig 6 :23), with a source gate and shadow test. Here: an equivalent borrowed from another culture's practice, recorded as borrowed, with an account of which gap it explains.

*They're Scanning Books When They Should* (as recorded in the exemplar mapping): "The real import is not a new term in the existing space. It is a new dimension of the space." And: "Declaring the primitives does not make them neutral. It makes the choice visible." A declared dictionary equivalence is a visible choice; the failure is treating it as invisible.

## 4. Why a name from a reading is the characteristic error

*The Hard Problem is About Source* (as recorded in the exemplar mapping) gives the mechanism. Metamerism: an instrument reads green and "cannot comment on the source"; several sources give one reading; "If several paths create the same behavior, then behavior is a projection with lossy information." It was the spectrometer, not an argument, that resolved it.

In COG-0027 the reading is the same score on a translated scale. Several conceptual arrangements can produce it: genuine equivalence, a shared frame, or a translation that changed the relation between terms. Naming the source ("they have the same concept") from the reading is the metamerism error. What is owed is a discriminator: the round trip, an instrument check, an independent frame.

## 5. Correction to the naive reading of "shape, not a name"

The canonical phrase governs a **missing** primitive, one not held in Γ. The translated term is usually a binding onto atoms the target frame already holds, not a new primitive. So:

- It is **not** forbidden to say which equivalent was chosen. That is a binding and must be provenanced (Fig 8 :50-52 "Convergent derivation and adopted import look identical in the contents. Only the record of where each came from separates them").
- It **is** forbidden to present that binding as what the framework derived, or as an import that moves the wall (Fig 6 :22 "NOVEL means novel relative to atoms, not to the world").
- What the framework returns is the shape the equivalent must satisfy (a measured R_T, an independent frame, a surviving relation), not the equivalent.

This correction is kept visible rather than silently substituted. The earlier, stronger reading would have made the solution say nothing about any concrete translation; that was an overstatement.

## 6. Prestige and simulation, the two standing limits

**Prestige is not action.** From *The Recursive Self-Improvement Trap*, fetched this session: "Prestige can fund an action. It can give an action a budget to be tried. But it cannot settle an action. Only the ground can settle it. The Ground Maintainer must be blind to prestige." A list of eminent translation scholars who agree with the condition list is the named collapse, not corroboration.

**This audit is a simulation.** Same essay, fetched this session: "LLMs can quickly simulate an approximate answer/output with the formula found in the architecture post" and "the truly testable output would need to be derived from the real architecture or working the formula yourself." So `01_figure_grounded_audit.md` is a simulated derivation checked for internal consistency against the figures. It is not a test of the conditions and must be labelled as such. It cannot settle anything it reports on.
