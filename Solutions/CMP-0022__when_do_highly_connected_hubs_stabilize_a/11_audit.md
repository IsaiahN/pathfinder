I redid the census before relying on the primary's conclusions, using the WATSON order: R by arity, symmetry and scale; one line per domain with actors / currency / anchor; partition or union; order by dependency.

Symmetry: asymmetric; targeted removal is ordered against random removal, and degree is a threshold reading, not a symmetric join. Scale: one node and one removal locally; giant component, cascade or prevalence globally.
- **D1 Structural frame.** Actors: nodes, edges, degree, component, spatial position. Currency: adjacency and ordering. Anchor: a measured connectivity statistic. This frame contains two independent sub-frames, network and spectrum; fig 13 says they are not readings of one quantity.
- **D2 Threat frame.** Actors: random removal, targeted removal, cascade, epidemic. Currency: nodes removed, load shifted, infection injected. Anchor: fragmentation, die-out or prevalence.
- **D3 Dynamics frame.** Actors: loads, flows, infection. Currency: influence or bandwidth. Anchor: an experiment or dataset on the propagating quantity.
- **D4 Verification frame.** Actors: readers and instruments. Currency: agreement versus residual. Anchor: the ground, never agreement. Its independence in this run is zero.

- **X2 universal scale-free cause.** Absorbed into D1 as an empirical condition after Broido and Clauset 2019 and Doyle et al 2005.

Order by dependency: perception of the graph, vocabulary for arrangements, anchor (objective), perturbation and instrument (planning), carry. One thing survives cleanly: the hub's sign is a function of the residual, so the single-verdict answer is the wrong shape. That is the residue worth sending.
