## Restatement as an effect

The same structural feature, a high-degree hub, is reported to have opposite network-level consequences. Under random loss of nodes the network often stays connected. Under removal of a few high-degree nodes the same network fragments. The residual is the flip in the valence of the hub as the perturbation and the measuring instrument change. The package asks what must be named before the flip can be answered, and it derives the answer that the valence is relational rather than intrinsic, so the question is a union of at least two threat conditions plus a dynamics condition, not one threshold on degree.

An answer would look like a conditional: for a given anchor and a given threat model, hubs have this sign and this mechanism. That sought-for shape is bound as an ordinary term in `01` and allowed to accrue a residual like anything else (formula step "Direction"), so it can be refused.

## What is deliberately not here

- No personal emails of named individuals.
- No ranking of people by how likely they are to agree with this derivation.
- No bulk-send plan.
- No claim that a figure line settles an empirical network question by itself. The figures decide the shape of the answer. The ground, meaning measurement, decides the sign for a particular network.

Three reasons, in the figures' own words. Fig 7 :35: *"A room's agreement is variation, not a verdict. Selection between rooms is where the ground votes."* Fig 2 :35: *"Alignment is triangulation, not negotiation. A mirror cannot correct you."* Fig 10 :42: *"A verifier that can reconstruct the claim cannot verify it."*

Legally: unsolicited bulk outreach to harvested personal addresses breaches GDPR and CAN-SPAM style rules in many jurisdictions and gets the sending domain blacklisted. Public opt-in channels avoid the practical failure as well as the structural one.
