## 0. The residual in effect terms

> Early gas is nearly smooth. Observed stars and galaxies are clumpy, bound, luminous and metal-bearing. What composes the second from the first, and in what order?

Describe the effect, not the cause (Fig 9 :30-31: "your words are borrowed too, so describe the effect rather than the cause"), or the question itself will pick a favourite frame before the search starts (Fig 9 :27-28: "describe it before you go looking, or any frame you pick will seem to fit").

- **Arity:** more than one condition binds at once. A halo can be massive enough to collapse and still fail to cool if its coolant has been dissociated. This is a union compound, not one hard question. Fig 9 :55-58: "a case in two conditions is not a harder case of either" and "a bundled problem is usually a union of overlapping conditions, not a list of separate ones."
- **Symmetry:** ordered, not symmetric. Gas precedes stars; metals precede low-mass stars; reionization precedes the observability of the faintest galaxies. Swapping the operands changes the thing. Operators table: "A then B; B needs A to have happened" and "A produces B; B did not exist before."
- **Scale:** from Mpc (the halo and the cosmic web) down to pc and AU (the protostellar disk); from recombination to the first spectroscopically confirmed galaxies. The same loop must be read at both scales, with a lossy transform between (Fig 4 :21: "The membrane is the boundary between the sizes, and the rule about what may cross it. Not a wall: a filter with a direction.").

The sought-for shape was bound before the run (README, "Direction"). It is a cross-kind composition chain with an imported cast and an observational ground. It is now refusable by the same test as everything else (Fig 5 :67: "make the sought-for shape an ORDINARY TERM").

## 3. Findings

### F1 - The six-term grammar governs the shape, not the physical cast (decisive on the shape)

Fig 13 :74: "Everything else is a composition across the two kinds, never within one."
Fig 13 :75: "Two states never bond directly. It is not space-time but space, a structure, time; and two structures join only through a state."

A formation answer is a chain of crossings. A halo is a network structure over space; a density or temperature gradient is a gradient structure; the ordering of cooling thresholds (H2 below about 10^4 K, atomic hydrogen above) is a spectrum structure. These sit between states. The chain cannot be written as a bare state to state bond. The grammar constrains the shape; it does not select which physical actor occupies which slot.

**Refuter:** derive one of the six from the other five; or exhibit a first-order formation step that the framework must admit as a direct state-state or structure-structure composition and cannot refuse. If either fires, the grammar finding fails. **Status:** `general` on the grammar, `specified` on its application here. Triangulated (Fig 13 plus Fig 4 plus the derived Rules 1 and 2 in *How Intelligence Actually Works*, low overlap between the six-term census and the derivation narrative).

### F2 - The physical cast is a habitat, enumerated, not composed (decisive on method)

Fig 11 :43: "The habitat is enumerated, never composed."
Fig 11 :44: "List everything in contact with the residual, then what is in contact with those, and outward until the cascade stops mattering."
Fig 11 :52-54: "And contact cascades. Scoping the habitat to direct contact is how DDT reached the eagles: first-order enumeration, second-order effect, and the residual that mattered was three steps out."

Enumerated habitat for this residual:

- **Actors:** baryons (H, He), dark matter minihalos, residual free electrons, H2, UV photons from the first stars, metals, dust, supernovae.
- **Conditions:** virial temperature, gas density, redshift, residual electron fraction, Lyman-Werner flux.
- **Relations:** gravitational collapse, H2 formation and cooling, atomic-line cooling, photo-dissociation, enrichment, hierarchical merging, reionization.

First-order enumeration would stop at gas plus halo. The cascade forces the second order: the first stars' UV flux dissociates H2 in neighbouring halos, raising the minimum mass for later star formation (Barkana and Loeb 2001). The residual that matters is three steps out. Valence is relative to R: the same UV photon field is a benefactor to reionization and a malfactor to H2 cooling (Fig 11 :49-51).

**Refuter:** name an actor in contact with R that is absent from the list above and whose addition changes the outcome. **Status:** triangulated (Fig 11 plus Seat Map :215) for the enumeration rule; single-source and ground-settled for the specific cascade claim, which comes from the world sources, not the figures.

### F5 - Bonds carry the arrow of time; production is not sequence

Operators table: "A then B; B needs A to have happened" for `→`, and "A produces B; B did not exist before" for `⇒`.
Fig 12 :117: "isomer. Same atoms, different bond, different substance. The difference is invisible in the formula and decisive in the behaviour."

The transition from primordial gas to the first stars is production: metals, photons and enriched dust did not exist before the first stars fired. The transition from one halo generation to the next is sequence: B needs A to have happened. Collapsing these into a symmetric "and" loses the arrow (Fig 12 :5: "A set of ingredients is not one. The bond is the rest of the information, and it is the half nothing was recording"). Writing the whole chain as `→` overcharges the sequence where `⇒` is owed; writing it as `+` undercharges the direction.

**Refuter:** show the formation transition can be written as a symmetric conjunction `+` with no loss of meaning, or that `⇒` and `→` are not distinguished by the figures. **Status:** triangulated for the bond definitions (Fig 12 plus Operators table); `specified` and single-source for the binding onto cosmology, which is an import from the physics frame, not a figure derivation.

### F6 - Feedback is a cascade and a cycle; settled products become atoms

Fig 12 :104: "A settled molecule becomes an atom for whatever composes over it. So irreducible is a fact about a level and never about the world, and there is no bottom"
Fig 12 :121: "cycle. The output re-enters as input. The sign of the loop decides whether it closes, and a chain cannot close by construction."
Fig 11 :61-62: "The seat's power is asymmetric. Introduction is available; exclusion is not. You can add an actor and you cannot subtract the habitat, because contact is a fact about the world and does not consult you."

Each stellar generation settles against the ground and becomes an atom for the next: Pop III products are operands for Pop II formation, which is why the chain has levels rather than one long sequence. Feedback is a cycle, because the output (UV flux, supernova ejecta) re-enters as input to the next halo's cooling. It is not a seat's introduction: nobody can subtract the habitat, so enrichment is permanent work.

**Refuter:** show that feedback cannot re-enter as input (no cycle), or that a settled first-generation product cannot serve as an atom for the next generation. **Status:** triangulated (Fig 12 plus Fig 11 plus Seat Map). `specified`.
