## Two things in the astronomy literature that bear directly on this question

1. **The anchor is a spectrum, not a consensus.** Bromm and Larson report that simulations converge on a characteristic gas state but leave the primordial initial mass function undetermined. Barkana and Loeb show that the coolant itself changes with feedback. These are two different frames with low overlap, and their agreement on the broad sequence is corroboration at maximum independence (Fig 8 :50-52). Their disagreement on masses and slopes is a residual, not a defect to average away (Fig 1 :19: "The residual is indexed by object slot, not aggregated").
2. **A channel can fail silently.** The Nature Astronomy editorial reports that JWST photometric redshifts came down about 20 percent after the pipeline was updated. Fig 10 :24 says "the ground does not decay, the channel does." A beautiful image is not a settled reading. Photometry alone left open the possibility of low-redshift interlopers; spectroscopy settled it. This is the single best ground example for the outreach pitch.

## Distribution: offer, do not push

- **Fig 4 :22-25:** "Only methods cross up. Only head starts come down. A recording carried upward looks like knowledge and is a description of one occasion." Sending a full cosmology before anyone has described a residual sends a recording, not a generator. Lead with the shape claim and its refuter.
- **Fig 10 :42:** "A verifier that can reconstruct the claim cannot verify it." Seat 4 "must not hold the interiors". A recipient who has absorbed the whole framework before being asked for a verdict has joined the frame being checked.
- **Fig 9 :27-28:** "describe it before you go looking, or any frame you pick will seem to fit." Ask what they would expect to see in a formation story before giving them the architecture.
- **Seat Map :163:** "High correlation is disqualifying for a verdict and efficient for a convention." A mass send selected for people who already agree produces a convention, not corroboration.

**Recommended order in a reply:** (1) the shape claim and its refuter; (2) ask what they would count as a failure, and record the answer before any theory is shown; (3) only then the links, with the note that the figures, not the summary, are the authority.

## The two-stage offer

1. **Stage 1 before stage 2.** Fig 10 :52, "Bring a characterised residual, never the source"; Fig 2 :45, "Frames range against the ground. They do not negotiate with it." The recipient checks the shape without holding the framework's interior.
2. **Stage 2 is opt-in.** Once a recipient has absorbed the theory, their agreement stops being evidence. Their verdict on stage 1 must be recorded first and never pooled as corroboration later (Fig 2 :40).
3. **Stage 3 sends a method, which is permitted.** Fig 4 :22, "Only methods cross up. Only head starts come down." The formula is a generator, so giving it away does not give away an answer.
4. **Stage 3 still needs a ground per problem.** `THE_FORMULA.md` "Direction": a frame "cannot check the direction of its own question, because the shape a frame is searching for is not a term it holds." Each user must write down what would refute their result before running it and label the mode of what they get.

**Hard precondition:** stages 1 to 3 only work if the stage-1 output survives. It is a shape claim, not a cosmology. If a reader thinks the figures have derived H2 cooling or a formation redshift, the output has been overstated and must be corrected before the links are sent.

## Hosting and sending

The four working files in this folder can be archived locally. Nothing here is sent by this agent. Hosting the archive and sending it is the user's action. Prefer an opt-in download page with a decline route over an attachment pushed to a list: that keeps the recipient outside the frame until they choose to enter it.
