# Bonds, order and isomers in the first-star story

**Mode: `specified` on the bond readings, `gap` on the physical names.** This is a simulated derivation checked against the figures, not a ground reading (see `05_what_shape_means.md` §6).

## RESIDUAL

The formation story is told as a list of ingredients: gas, dark matter, cooling, collapse, stars, metals, galaxies. A list cannot carry the order or the arrow of time. Two questions are owed: (1) what bonds actually join these items under Fig 12's definition, and (2) are two rival formation channels isomers over a shared atom set?

## DERIVATION

### Q1 - What bond is a formation step?

- Operators table: `→` sequence means "A then B; B needs A to have happened"; `⇒` production means "A produces B; B did not exist before". The test for `⇒` is "is there a B before A fires? No."
- Halo collapse precedes star ignition: sequence, `→`. Metals, UV photons and enriched dust did not exist before the first stars fired: production, `⇒`. Fig 12 :41: "same two atoms, three substances / and the bond is charged for what it could have been".
- A flat formula `gas + halo + cooling + stars + metals` uses `+`, whose test is "swap the operands. Does the meaning change? No." That is false here: swapping stars and metals gives a different world. The flat formula records ingredients and not arrangement (Fig 12 :14-16: "the ingredients are recorded and the arrangement is not / nothing downstream can tell them apart").
- **Correction kept visible:** a first draft of `01` wrote the whole chain as `→`. Sequence is right for ordering but too weak for production. Both bonds are now retained. This is a correction, not an erasure.
- **Falsifier:** if the transition from gas to metals can be written as a symmetric conjunction with no loss of meaning, the bond reading fails.

### Q2 - What is the cycle in the story?

- Fig 12 :121: **"cycle. The output re-enters as input. The sign of the loop decides whether it closes, and a chain cannot close by construction."**
- The first stars emit UV photons which dissociate H2 in neighbouring halos, raising the mass threshold for later star formation. Output re-enters as input. A chain cannot express that; a cycle can.
- Metals from the first supernovae become coolant and dust for the next generation. Fig 12 :104: **"A settled molecule becomes an atom for whatever composes over it. So irreducible is a fact about a level and never about the world, and there is no bottom"** gives the level structure: each generation, settled, is an operand for the next.
- **Falsifier:** show that feedback cannot re-enter as input, or that a settled first generation cannot serve as an atom for the next.

### Q3 - Are the rival formation channels isomers?

- Fig 12 :117: **"isomer. Same atoms, different bond, different substance. The difference is invisible in the formula and decisive in the behaviour."**
- Fig 12 :124: **"functional group. A substructure whose presence fixes the outcome. The group says what kind; the qualifier says which member."**
- Two channels are relevant to early structure: direct collapse into a massive black-hole seed, and formation through a massive Pop III star that later leaves a smaller seed. Written over the same habitat atom set (gas, halo, cooling agent, angular momentum, feedback), they differ in which bond carries the collapse and in which actor mediates the cooling. They are candidates for Fig 12 isomers: same atoms, different arrangement, different observable substance (seed mass, enrichment pattern, ionising output).
- As usually written in the literature they share only "gas in a halo", so the weaker claim is a **functional group** (`state → structure → state`) with different qualifiers, not an isomer. The stronger claim needs both channels written over the full shared atom set.
- **Falsifier, written before the run:** if one channel requires an atom the other does not, for example a coolant unavailable to direct collapse, the isomer claim fails and the functional-group reading stands instead.
- **Anchor:** this is figure-internal on the definition only. The physics comparison is ground-settled by astronomy and is not asserted here as `grounded`.

### Q4 - What order is the union?

- Fig 3 :20-21: "The chain is ordered by dependency, not by importance. Each link consumes what the one above produces, so effort spent above the break arrives at a step that cannot use it."
- The dependency order for this residual is: perceive the smooth to clumpy residual; acquire terms for what gas affords (cool, collapse, fragment, ionize); say what counts as progress (a bound luminous object); plan a route under incomplete information; name what worked and carry it to the next problem.
- The likely break is between vocabulary and objective (Fig 3 :25-27: "The link between them, having terms for what a situation offers and being able to say what would count as progress, has neither, so it attracts the least attention and the fewest instruments"). Fig 3 :27: "A link with no instrument is not a link that works. It is a link nobody has looked at."
- **Falsifier:** an instrument that directly settles what counts as progress at the vocabulary link, so the break moves elsewhere.

### Q5 - What does the round trip lose?

- Fig 4 :30: **"So take the round trip and measure the gap. Without it you are navigating by dead reckoning: confident about a position nobody checked."**
- Sent up: the bond set and the six-term grammar. Abstracted: "formation is a sequence of cross-kind compositions with production and feedback." Brought back down: the concrete minihalo and H2 narrative.
- **R_T is nonzero.** Three things in the concrete story are not held by the abstraction: the actual coolant species, the actual halo mass and redshift, and the discriminating instrument that separates a Pop III signature from a metal-poor Pop II spectrum. Conversely, one thing survives cleanly: a structure must sit between two states and the arrow of time is carried by typed bonds.
- R_T is not measured numerically here; no figure settles the physical constants. It is recorded qualitatively as the enumerated set of dropped constraints. Treating the formula as identical to the figure set would set R_T to zero and would be exactly the dead-reckoning error Fig 4 :30 names (see `11_audit.md`).

## CONCLUSION

The formation story is not a flat list. It is a typed chain: sequence for ordering, production for the appearance of metals and photons, cycle for feedback, comparison for thresholds such as the atomic-cooling line, and settled products becoming atoms for the next stellar generation. Rival channels are isomers only under the stronger writing over the full shared atom set; as ordinarily written they share a functional group. The bond definitions are figure-derived; the mapping onto cosmology is an import and is recorded as such. Specific masses, redshifts and coolants are `gap` at the figure level.
