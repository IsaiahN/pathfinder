# Expert verification packet: the Tether on first stars and galaxies

**What this is:** a claim in a form an astronomer or cosmologist can check. Mode, derivation and refuters are stated before the conclusion, so a reader knows what to look for and what would settle it.

**Mode:** `general` on the grammar, `specified` on the shape claim, `gap` on the physical mechanism. Not `grounded`. No measurement is claimed by this derivation.

## 1. The claim, in one paragraph

At recombination the gas is nearly smooth; today it is clumpy, luminous and metal-bearing. The framework does not derive the physical formation story. It constrains the shape any such story must have. Composition runs across two kinds and never within one, so a structure sits between two states and a flat list of ingredients cannot carry the arrows. The physical cast is a habitat that must be enumerated, including coolants and feedback, not composed from the six-term set. Gravity and cooling physics enter as imports with provenance and a shadow test. Time's arrow is carried by production and sequence bonds, with feedback closing a cycle. Only an instrument settles which actor fills which slot. The framework returns a shape, not a name; specific halo masses, redshifts and coolants are `gap` at the figure level.

## 5. What would settle it

1. A source-discriminating stellar spectrum separating a Pop III signature from a metal-poor Pop II star.
2. Spectroscopic confirmation of high-redshift candidates, not photometry alone. The JADES team used NIRSpec to confirm four galaxies at 10.3 <= z <= 13.2.
3. A threshold test at the atomic-cooling line, Tvir about 10^4 K, where H2 cooling yields to atomic line cooling.
4. A habitat test: a missing actor in contact with the residual that changes the outcome.
