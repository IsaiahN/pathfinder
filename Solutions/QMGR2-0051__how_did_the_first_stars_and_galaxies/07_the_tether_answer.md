# The Tether's answer: how the first stars and galaxies formed from gas

**Mode:** `general` on the six-term grammar, `specified` on the shape claim, `gap` on the specific physical mechanism. Not `grounded`. Simulated derivation checked against the figures (`05_what_shape_means.md` §6), not a ground reading.

## 1. The residual it answers

At recombination the gas is nearly smooth (CMB fluctuations of order 10^-5). Today it is clumpy, luminous and metal-bearing. The residual is the gap between those two states: what composes localized, bound, luminous objects out of a nearly uniform gas, and in what order? It is a **union compound**, not one hard question (Fig 9 :55-58: "a case in two conditions is not a harder case of either"). Collapse, cooling and feedback bind at once and need separate loops.

## 3. What it explicitly does not say

## 4. What would settle it, the ground

The ground is an instrument, not an argument. From *The Hard Problem is About Source*, an instrument that reads green "it cannot comment on the source"; the spectrometer resolved which source it was. Here:

- A source-discriminating stellar spectrum that separates a Pop III signature from an unusually metal-poor Pop II star would name one slot.
- Spectroscopy rather than photometry for high-redshift candidates. The JADES team needed NIRSpec to confirm z = 10.3 to 13.2 and to rule out low-redshift interlopers. That is the anchor working.
- **Threshold test:** does a comparison bond hold at the atomic-cooling line, Tvir about 10^4 K, where H2 cooling gives way to atomic line cooling, or demonstrably not?
- **Habitat test:** an actor in contact with the residual that the enumeration misses and that changes the outcome, for example a feedback channel that raises the minimum halo mass at a given redshift.
- Standing falsifiers from `01`: derive one of the six from the other five; admit a direct state-state or structure-structure composition; derive gravity or H2 cooling from the six-term closure with no import; settle a formation claim by agreement alone; derive a specific mass or redshift from the figures.

## 5. The honest stop

The answer stops where the available instruments stop distinguishing and the lineage is recorded so the stop is checkable. "Indiscernibility is not identity" (canonical README).

## 6. One-paragraph version for outreach

> The Tether does not derive a cosmology of first light, and it does not claim the final answer. What it says is narrower and checkable: any formation story must be a cross-kind composition chain, because a structure has to sit between two states; the physical cast cannot be composed from the six-term set, so it has to be enumerated as a habitat, including coolants and feedback, not just gas and a halo; gravity and cooling physics are imports that need provenance and a shadow test; the arrow of time is carried by production and sequence bonds, with feedback closing a cycle; and only an instrument, not agreement among models, settles which actor fills which slot. The framework returns a shape, not a name. It offers that shape, the habitat method, and refuters written in advance. Specific halo masses, redshifts and coolants remain a `gap` at the figure level, to be settled by telescopes and spectra.
