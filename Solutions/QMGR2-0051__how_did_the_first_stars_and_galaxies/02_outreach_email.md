# Outreach draft: check the output before deciding whether the theory is worth reading

**Use:** one-to-one, or as a cover letter attached to a submission through the public venues in `03_public_contact_points.csv`. **Not** for bulk send.

**Hard precondition:** this draft states a shape claim, not an astrophysical result. Do not let a recipient leave stage 1 believing the figures have derived a halo mass, a cooling channel or a redshift. If that happens, the two-stage offer collapses on contact.

---

**Subject:** Check one shape claim before you decide whether the framework is worth reading

> Hi [name],
>
> I am working on a figure-grounded framework and have one narrow output I would like you to check against what you already know. I am not asking you to read the theory first, and I am not asking you to agree with it. If the output does not survive, there is nothing else to look at.
>
> **The question, in effect terms:** the early gas is nearly smooth, with CMB fluctuations of order 10^-5. Today it is clumpy, luminous and metal-bearing. What composes the second from the first, and in what order?
>
> **The output (`specified`, not `grounded`):** the framework's minimum set has six terms in two kinds, states (space, time, energy) and structures (spectrum, gradient, network), with the rule that composition runs across kinds and never within one. From that rule it follows that there is no direct state to state bond: if a structure sits between two states, that slot stays open. The rule is `GAS(???)OBJECT`, not a named cosmology. The framework also says that a physical cast cannot be composed from the six. It has to be enumerated as a habitat: everything in contact with the residual, then what contacts those, outward until the cascade stops mattering. For this question that brings in coolants, feedback from the first stars and enrichment, not just gas and a halo.
>
> **What I am not claiming:** that the figures derive minihalos of 10^6 solar masses, H2 cooling below 10^4 K, Population III masses, reionization by z about 6, or the JADES galaxies at z = 10.3 to 13.2. Those are ground readings made by astronomers. The framework returns a shape, not a name. Importing physics from another frame is allowed, but it needs provenance and a shadow test: where did the atom come from, and what gap does it explain? Without that record, convergence and adopted import look identical and carry no evidentiary weight.
>
> **Refuter, written before this email:**
> 1. derive any one of the six terms from the other five;
> 2. show the framework must admit a state-state or structure-structure composition with no intervening opposite-kind term;
> 3. name an actor in contact with this residual that the habitat enumeration misses and that changes the outcome;
> 4. derive gravity, H2 cooling or nuclear fusion from the six-term closure with no import;
> 5. settle a formation claim by model agreement alone with no instrument reading that could have refused it;
> 6. derive a specific first-star mass, halo mass or formation redshift from the figures.
>
> If any one holds, the corresponding claim is withdrawn. A one-line "this fails because..." is more useful to me than agreement. If you would rather not engage, no reply is needed.
>
> **If it survives, and only then:** the sources are below. The figures are the authority, not my summary of them.
> - Canonical figures README and citation rule: https://github.com/IsaiahN/tether/tree/general-agent/figures
> - Full architecture and the eight-step formula (19 min): https://medium.com/@IsaiahNwukor/arrival-of-the-fittest-the-tether-architecture-1de7edf641ec
> - Worked narrative, the six-term derivation and Rules 1 to 5: https://adventuresinml.substack.com/p/how-intelligence-actually-works
>
> **If you want to use the machinery:** the formula is in the figures link. Run it on a problem you know well and can check against a ground. Write down what would refute your result before you run it. The framework's own rule is that a loop cannot check the direction of its own question, and the architecture page warns that outputs "could never be verified if the solutions presented were not contrived to fit the formula." A reported failure is more useful to me than a reported success.
>
> - Isaiah
