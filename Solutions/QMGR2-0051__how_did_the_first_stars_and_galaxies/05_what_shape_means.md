## 1. A shape is a constraint, not a filler

The canonical README states the limit: the framework returns "what a missing thing would have to look like (a shape, not a name)". Fig 9 :32: "you get a kind of thing, never the answer itself". Fig 13 :135: "It cannot say which primitive is missing, because naming it would mean already holding it."

From *The Library of Babel*: "You can cut your search short if the set of books returned is small and descriptive enough that it provides a general shape of the answer that satisfies your question."

So a **shape** is the narrowed form of an answer you can state before you can name it. It constrains; it does not fill. `GAS(???)OBJECT` is a shape. `GAS(H2 COOLING IN MINIHALOS)OBJECT` is a name owed from the astronomy frame.

## 2. How a shape is obtained

- *Library of Babel*: use the vocabulary you already have that is closest to the thing, look for recurring new terms that overlap or correlate with it, recurse. "With each new term gained, you are able to narrow the space by definition and deepen your vocabulary."
- Fig 6 :30: "The description travels. Fails by being sharpened into terms only we can read. Cast it in effect terms." Fig 9 :30-31: "your words are borrowed too, so describe the effect rather than the cause."
- *Decoding Tacit Knowledge*: "When contact isn't possible, the best you can send is a plain description of what you'd expect to see, because anyone can check that against the world."
- *Library of Babel* on stopping: "you will know that you are done because the finish line will also be the starting place, as there is nowhere else to search and nothing else that can describe the answer better." The paradox is that by then you have "composed the answer through the process of locating it."
- *How Intelligence Actually Works* gives the astrophysicist's own library explicitly: "Library for an astrophysicist (data on galaxies, stars, planets, laws, theories and so forth)". The shape search runs against that library, and the physicist supplies the measurements. The framework does not.

## 3. What licenses a name

Only two things, both outside the shape itself:

1. **The ground settles it.** `THE_FORMULA.md` step 5: "the ground settles it. Verified only by what pays. A gate is not the ground." The canonical README: the framework "stops at the limit of what the available instruments can distinguish, and records the lineage so the stop is honest." For first stars that means spectra, not arguments.
2. **A provenanced import from a frame whose closure differs** (Fig 6 :23, Fig 8 :50-52), with a source gate and a shadow test. The astronomy papers are exactly such imports: they explain a gap and they name where the atom came from.

*They're Scanning Books When They Should Be Studying Nature* sharpens this: "The real import is not a new term in the existing space. It is a new dimension of the space." And: "A minimum viable set of six terms is a human's careful guess at reality's compression axes; another sensorium's minimum set need not be those six." Also: "Declaring the primitives does not make them neutral. It makes the choice visible."

The six are a visible guess, not a discovery of nature's own list. Naming H2 cooling as *the* answer to the formation slot turns a visible choice into an invisible one.

## 4. Why reading a source off a projection is the specific forbidden error

*The Hard Problem is About Source* gives the mechanism. Metamerism: an instrument reads green and "it cannot comment on the source" (one green light, or yellow plus blue, or RGB, or CMYK, or a scheme not yet discovered). The reading does not determine the source. "If several paths create the same behavior, then behavior is a projection with lossy information". "An instrument that can't distinguish these cases demonstrates its own inadequacy." It was the spectrometer, not an argument, that resolved it.

The same shape appears in cosmology. A metal-poor stellar spectrum can be read as a Pop III signature or as an unusually metal-poor Pop II star, and the reading alone does not name the source. What is owed is a discriminator, not a name. The JADES team used NIRSpec spectra, not photometry, to settle the redshifts of the four earliest confirmed galaxies. That is the instrument doing the work the figure demands.

*The Recursive Self-Improvement Trap*: "the output is only as good as the validator. The agent, or the tool, or the frame, or the theory, cannot validate itself." The Medium architecture page is blunter: LLM outputs "must be seen as simulacra" and "could never be verified if the solutions presented were not contrived to fit the formula."

## 5. Q: what licenses a particular physical actor in the formation chain?

**Answer:** nothing in the framework. The framework returns a shape. A particular actor is licensed only by the ground settling a minted term, or by a provenanced import with a shadow test. Until one of those, the name is the searcher's own contribution and must be recorded as such.

**Does this solution commit the forbidden error?** No, provided it keeps the F7 `gap` verdict visible. The figure-derived claims are the grammar (F1), the habitat method (F2), the import rule (F3), the anchor rule (F4), the bond distinction (F5), the cascade (F6) and the budget/witness rule (F8). Halo masses, redshifts, coolants and stellar masses are recorded as world-frame readings, never as figure outputs. If a later edit ever states "the figures show that H2 cooled the first gas", that is the metamerism error and must be retracted.

## 5b. Correction to my own §1 and §3

I initially wrote "shape, not a name" as a blanket ban on naming any actor. That overstates it. The canonical README's phrase is "what a **missing** thing would have to look like (a shape, not a name)." It governs a primitive not held in the library. The astrophysical cast is not a seventh primitive; it is a habitat of actors enumerated under Fig 11 :43. Naming them is allowed as an import from the astronomy frame, provided provenance and shadow test are shown. What remains wrong is claiming the figures derive them. Both the narrower rule and the original overstatement are kept here so neither is quietly lost.

## 6. Two consequences for outreach

**Prestige is not action.** From *The Recursive Self-Improvement Trap*: "Prestige must never be allowed to validate an action directly... Prestige can fund an action. It can give an action a budget to be tried. But it cannot settle an action. Only the ground can settle it. The Ground Maintainer must be blind to prestige." A campaign that gathers famous astrophysicists to agree with the shape claim is the named collapse, not corroboration. The legitimate ask gives the claim a budget to be tried and lets the ground settle it.

**My own audit is a simulation, not a ground reading.** Same essay: "LLMs can quickly simulate an approximate answer/output with the formula found in the architecture post... However, the truly testable output would need to be derived from the real architecture or working the formula yourself." So `01` is a simulated derivation, checked for internal consistency against the figures. It is not a test of the claim and must be labelled as such. It cannot settle anything it reports on.

## 7. What this means for the first-stars question specifically

*How Intelligence Actually Works* uses the phrase "the cosmic background mistaken for antenna noise" as an example of discovery under consequence: a random walk produced an unpredicted sample, and a recognizer refused to discard it as noise. That is the honest posture for this question too. The formation story is not something the framework reads out of a diagram. It is something astronomers assembled by pointing instruments at residuals the models could not absorb. The figure-grounded contribution is the shape of that assembly, not the assembly itself.
