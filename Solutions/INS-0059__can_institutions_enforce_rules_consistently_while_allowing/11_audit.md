## Round trip gap R_T

The Symbols table defines R_T as `gap(x, (T_E ∘ T_A)(x))`. Fig 4 :30 requires it to be measured rather than assumed. I ran the round trip on the condensation this folder actually uses.

1. **Sent up (x):** the verbatim figure set, chiefly Fig 10 :54 and :57-60 (idealisation and self-correction), Fig 2 :29-33 (anchor), Operators :57 (negation), Fig 10 :62-66 (provenance), Fig 10 :73-81 (unauthored and rotated pool), Fig 1 :19 (per slot), Fig 9 :55-58 (union).
2. **Abstraction (T_A):** the shorthand in `07_the_tether_answer.md`: four parts, anchor, written refusal, dated record, unauthored reviewer, plus per-case residual.
3. **Concretisation (T_E):** the everyday phrasing of the question, "be consistent but allow discretion", and the two tempting institutional readings: uniformity with no exceptions, and discretion as an unwritten favour.

**R_T is nonzero.** Three things the shorthand holds do not survive the trip back down:

- the **self-correction at Fig 10 :57-60**, which the everyday phrasing drops. Without it, F1 reads as a blanket ban on absolute rules rather than as a rule about satisfiability by real occupants. This is the most dangerous drop, because it is the part that makes the answer constructive rather than merely negative.
- the **arity of negation**, which the everyday phrase "allow discretion" renders as a permission rather than as an operator. A permission can be granted and withdrawn by the same official; an operator in the rule vocabulary changes what the rule set can say.
- the **union reading of the unusual case**, which the everyday phrase renders as a hard case requiring a gentle exception. Fig 9 :57 says it is a different compound, which needs a different loop, not a softer application of the same one.

Conversely, one thing survives cleanly: a rule whose answer moves when someone pushes is not an anchor, so the consistency half of the question cannot be uniformity. That residue is worth sending. R_T was **not** measured numerically, because no ground reading exists; it is recorded qualitatively as the enumerated set of dropped constraints. Treating the question's everyday phrasing as identical to the shape would set R_T to zero and would be the dead-reckoning error Fig 4 :30 names.
