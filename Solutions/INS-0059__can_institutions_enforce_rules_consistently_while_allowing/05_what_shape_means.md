## 1. A shape is a constraint, not a filler

The figures README states the limit: the framework returns "what a missing thing would have to look like (a shape, not a name)". Fig 9 :32: "you get a kind of thing, never the answer itself". Fig 13 :138 repeats it. Fig 13 :134: "Unreachable is a verdict here, not a silence".

From *The Library of Babel*: "you can cut your search short if the set of books returned is small and descriptive enough that it provides a general shape of the answer that satisfies your question."

So a **shape** is the narrowed form of an answer you can state before you can name it. It constrains; it does not fill. For this question, "consistency needs a non-updating anchor and discretion needs a written refusal" is a shape. "This country's administrative law is the answer" is a name. The shape can be checked against the figures; the name cannot be derived from them.

## 2. How a shape is obtained

- *Library of Babel*: use the vocabulary you already have that is closest to the thing, then look for recurring new terms that overlap with it. "With each new term gained, you narrow the space and deepen your vocabulary."
- Fig 9 :30-31: "your words are borrowed too, so describe the effect rather than the cause". Residual first, frame second (Fig 8 :53: "Residual first, frame second.").
- Fig 6 :30: "The description travels. Fails by being sharpened into terms only we can read. Cast it in effect terms." This is why the residual here is stated as "a case in two conditions with a rule that cannot refuse itself", not as a legal doctrine's name.
- *Library of Babel* on stopping: "you can cut your search short if the set of books returned is small and descriptive enough". The paradox is that by the time you have the exact terms, "you have already composed the answer through the process of locating it."

For this question the bootstrapping went: consistency → not uniformity but a non-updating anchor; discretion → not a favour but a negation; unusual case → not a hard ordinary case but a union compound with its own loop. Each step narrowed the shape.

## 3. What licenses a name

Only two things, both outside the shape itself:

1. **The ground settles it.** `THE_FORMULA.md` step 5: "the ground settles it. Verified only by what pays. A gate is not the ground." The figures README: the framework "stops at the limit of what the available instruments can distinguish, and records the lineage so the stop is honest."
2. **A provenanced import from a frame whose closure differs** (Fig 6 :23, Fig 8 :50-52), with a source gate and a shadow test.

Applied here: naming a specific institution, statute or review board as *the* solution is not licensed. The three world sources read in `04_links_and_distribution.md` describe what real institutions do; they do not derive from the figures and they cannot name the answer. Their agreement with the shape is secondary corroboration from different fields (SEP Non-monotonic Logic especially), not a decision.

## 4. Why "describe the case, then pick the frame" is the load-bearing move

*The Hard Problem is About Source* gives the mechanism. An instrument reads green but "it cannot comment on the source". "If several paths create the same behavior, then behavior is a projection with lossy information". And: "An instrument that can't distinguish these cases demonstrates its own inadequacy". It was the spectrometer, not an argument, that resolved it.

For institutions: an aggregate compliance reading is a projection. It cannot comment on the source (a justified refusal, a captured reviewer, an unread channel). Naming the source from the reading is the metamerism error. What is owed is a discriminator, not a name: a per-case record with the residual, the reasoning, the date and an outside checker. This is the essay's point applied to a new residual, and it is why F5 and F7 of `01_figure_grounded_audit.md` are not stylistic preferences.

## 5. Q: does "shape, not a name" forbid naming a mechanism here?

**Answered: no, with a boundary.** The README's phrase governs a *missing* primitive, one not held in Γ. Here the mechanisms named (negation, provenance, unauthored pool) are already in the framework's library: negation is the eighth operator (Operators table :53-58), provenance is Fig 10 :62-66, the unauthored pool is Fig 10 :73-79. Naming them is selecting held atoms, not inventing a seventh term. What remains forbidden is claiming that a particular real institution is *the* answer to this question. That would name a source from a reading without a discriminator.

**Correction to my own §3.** My first draft of §3 said "naming one is not licensed" without the missing-primitive qualifier. That was too broad. It would forbid citing the operator table at all. Narrowed: held atoms may be named; missing primitives may only be described in effect terms. Both readings are kept here so the correction is visible.

## 6. Two consequences for this solution

**Prestige is not action.** From *The Recursive Self-Improvement Trap*: "Prestige can fund an action. It can give an action a budget to be tried. But it cannot settle an action. Only the ground can settle it. The Ground Maintainer must be blind to prestige." A list of well-known administrative-law scholars who agree with the shape is the named collapse, not corroboration. The legitimate ask is a budget to try the refuter against a real case.

**This audit is a simulation, not a ground reading.** Same essay: "LLMs can quickly simulate an approximate answer/output with the formula found in the architecture post." But "the truly testable output would need to be derived from the real architecture or working the formula yourself." And: "Metatheories cannot validate themselves." So `01_figure_grounded_audit.md` and this file are simulated derivations, checked for internal consistency against the figures. They cannot settle whether any actual institution achieves both consistency and discretion. That needs a measurement with a real ground.
