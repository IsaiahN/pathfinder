## Distribution: offer, do not push

- **Fig 10 :52:** "Bring a characterised residual, never the source." Send the residual and the refuter first. Sending the full architecture before anyone has described the unusual case sends a recording, not a generator.
- **Fig 10 :42:** "A verifier that can reconstruct the claim cannot verify it." A recipient who has absorbed the framework before being asked for a verdict has joined the frame being checked.
- **Fig 9 :27-28:** "describe it before you go looking, or any frame you pick will seem to fit." Ask what they would expect to see if the shape claim were true before giving them the links.
- **Fig 7 :35:** "A room's agreement is variation, not a verdict. Selection between rooms is where the ground votes." A panel of agreeable readers does not upgrade the label.
- **Fig 2 :40:** agreement from a shared evidence pool is the pool talking. Record a stage-1 verdict before showing any theory; never pool later agreement as corroboration.

**Recommended order in a reply:** (1) the residual and the four-part shape; (2) the pre-registered refuter and the question "what would you count as a failure?"; (3) only if they are curious, the three framework links, with the note that the figures, not the summary, are the authority; (4) the formula as a method, with the instruction that each user write their own refuter and state their own mode.
