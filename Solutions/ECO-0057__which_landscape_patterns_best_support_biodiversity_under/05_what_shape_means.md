## 1. A shape is a constraint, not a filler

The figures README states the limit: the framework returns *"what a missing thing would have to look like (a shape, not a name)."* Fig 9 :32: *"you get a kind of thing, never the answer itself."* Fig 13 :134: *"Unreachable is a verdict here, not a silence,"*

From *The Library of Babel*: *"you can cut your search short if the set of books returned is small and descriptive enough that it provides a general shape of the answer that satisfies your question."*

So a **shape** is the narrowed form of an answer you can state before you can name it. It constrains; it does not fill. For this question:

- A shape: "any ranked answer must be enumerated from per-species contact, must treat valence as residual-relative, must be maintained, and must be settled by measured persistence."
- A name: "connect 30% of the landscape with 100 m riparian buffers and keep core patches above 1000 ha." That is a prescription the figures did not derive.

The distinction is the whole answer to the question as asked. The question wants a name. The derivation returns a shape and declares the name a `gap`.

## 2. How a shape is obtained

- *Library of Babel*: use the vocabulary you already have that is closest to the thing, look for recurring new terms that overlap or correlate with it, recurse. *"With each new term gained, you narrow the space and deepen your vocabulary."*
- Fig 6 :30: *"The description travels. Fails by being sharpened into terms only we can read. Cast it in effect terms."* Fig 9 :30–31: *"your words are borrowed too, so describe the effect / rather than the cause."*
- *Decoding Tacit Knowledge*: *"When contact isn't possible, the best you can send is a plain description of what you'd expect to see, because anyone can check that against the world."*
- *Library of Babel* on stopping: *"the finish line will also be the starting place"*, because by then you have composed the answer through the process of locating it.

Applied here, the effect description is: "a pattern is good to the extent that it keeps a named species in contact with what it needs, across a named climate shift, with effects enumerated outward." That description can be checked by anyone with a population survey. "A well-connected, heterogeneous mosaic" cannot be checked in the same way until its terms are tied to a residual.

## 3. What licenses a name

Only two things, both outside the shape itself:

1. **The ground settles it.** Formula step 5: *"the ground settles it. Verified only by what pays."* The README: the framework *"stops at the limit of what the available instruments can distinguish, and records the lineage so the stop is honest."*
2. **A provenanced import from a frame whose closure differs** (Fig 6 :23, Fig 8 :50–52), with a source gate and a shadow test.

*They're Scanning Books* sharpens the second: *"The real import is not a new term in the existing space. It is a new dimension of the space."* And: *"A minimum viable set of six terms is a human's careful guess at reality's compression axes; another sensorium's minimum set need not be those six."* Also: *"Declaring the primitives does not make them neutral. It makes the choice visible."*

For this question, "corridor", "core", "matrix", "refugium", and "heterogeneity" are bindings from the landscape-ecology frame onto a held vocabulary. They are legitimate as bindings, provided their provenance is recorded. They are not licenses for a figure-derived ranking.

## 3b. A declined mapping that matters here

The canonical article has a section titled "Three mappings offered and declined", and one of the three is exactly the mapping a landscape ecologist would reach for. It says:

> *"The habitat as an ecological niche. A niche is defined by an organism's relation to its environment. A habitat here is defined by contact with a residual, and the residual moves so the same actors constitute a different habitat when the question changes. The resemblance is real and the definitions do not align, which is why the figure says enumerated, never composed rather than borrowing the ecological term outright."*

This is recorded rather than hidden. Fig 11 is **not** Figure 11 of habitat ecology, and "the habitat" in this derivation is not a synonym for "the physical landscape". It is the set of actors, conditions, and relations in contact with a specific residual. When the residual changes, the habitat changes even if the map is unchanged. That is the precise reason a single "best landscape pattern" cannot be transferred from one species or one climate state to another. This mapping refusal strengthens F1 rather than weakening it.

## 4. The ground and the source problem

*The Hard Problem is About Source* gives the mechanism. A color instrument reads green and *"cannot comment on the source"*: one green light, or yellow plus blue, or RGB, or CMYK, or a scheme not yet discovered. *"If several paths create the same behavior, then behavior is a projection with lossy information."* And: *"The answer is unreached until a better instrument exists."* It was the **spectrometer**, not an argument, that resolved it.

For this question, a biodiversity index is a reading of green. Many landscape configurations can produce the same index. Naming the source (which pattern caused it) from the reading is the metamerism error. What is owed is a source-discriminating instrument at the per-species level, not a better ranking argument.

*How Intelligence Actually Works* adds the provenance side: *"the answer doesn't exist until the question is contacted"* and *"could never be verified if the solutions presented were not contrived to fit the formula."* The lineage is what makes a claim checkable, not its fluency.

## 5. Answer to the shape question

**What licenses a particular landscape pattern as "best"?** No figure line. The figures return a shape. A particular pattern is licensed only by a ground settlement for a stated species and a stated climate state, or by a provenanced import with a shadow test. Until one of those, the name is the searcher's own contribution and must be recorded as such.

**Does the question commit the error the README forbids?** Yes. "Which patterns best..." asks the framework to name a ranking where it returns a shape. Worse, it asks for a ranking that holds *while the residual changes*, which Fig 11 :51 refuses directly.

### 5b. Correction to my own §3

I initially wrote that "shape, not a name" forbids naming any landscape element. That overstates it. The README's phrase governs a *missing* primitive, one not held in Γ. Corridors, cores, matrix, heterogeneity, and refugia are already in the ecology frame's library. Naming one is a binding, not the invention of a new primitive. What remains forbidden is asserting a figure-derived ranking among them, and claiming a binding moves the wall (Fig 6: *"IMPORT adds an atom"*; this one adds none). Both readings are recorded so neither is quietly lost.

## 6. Consequences for contact

**Prestige is not action.** From *The Recursive Self-Improvement Trap*: *"Prestige must never be allowed to validate an action directly."* And: *"Prestige can fund an action. It can give an action a budget to be tried. But it cannot settle an action. Only the ground can settle it. The Ground Maintainer must be blind to prestige."* A list of highly cited reviews that agree on a pattern is the named collapse. The legitimate ask is a budget to try the claim against measured persistence.

**A group is only worth having while its members stay different.** From *How a Society of Agents Would Work*: *"Ten copies watching the same thing are one opinion said ten times."* A panel of landscape ecologists trained from the same textbooks is one frame wearing many names. Independence must be bought from low-overlap fields (animal behaviour, cognition, mathematics) in proportion to the cost of a wrong recommendation.

**My own audit is a simulation, not a ground reading.** Same essay as entry 2: *"the truly testable output would need to be derived from the real architecture or working the formula yourself."* And the architecture article is explicit: *"the outputs generated must be seen as simulacra."* So `01` is a simulated derivation, checked for internal consistency against the figures. It is **not** a test of any landscape pattern and must be labelled as such. It cannot settle anything it reports on.
