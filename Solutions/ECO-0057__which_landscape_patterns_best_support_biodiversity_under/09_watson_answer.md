## QUESTION

Decide, in effect terms, what the Tether says about the claim that there is a ranking of landscape patterns which best supports biodiversity while the climate changes. Option A: name such a ranking. Option B: keep the figure-derived constraints and declare the ranking a `gap`. ## DOMAIN CENSUS

R by arity, symmetry and scale, then one line per domain with actors / currency / anchor, partition or union, and order by dependency.

- **R (the residual).** Arity: many-to-many, a landscape arrangement indexed per species slot and per climate state. Symmetry: "best" is a threshold relation, not a symmetric join, and the sign of any element is relative to the residual (Fig 12 :87, Fig 11 :49). Scale: local patch, landscape mosaic, region, time. Fig 1 :19 bars aggregation across slots.
- **D1 Species-population loop.** Actors: populations, their Γ, dispersal. Currency: survival and per-slot residual reduction. Anchor: measured persistence of that population. Admitted.
- **D2 Landscape habitat.** Actors: patches, corridors, matrix, conditions (light, water, temperature, season, terrain), relations (pollination, predation, competition, dependency). Currency: contact between a species and what its goal requires. Anchor: the ground through measured persistence. Admitted.
- **D3 Climate as changing condition.** Actors: temperature, precipitation, disturbance regime. Currency: the conditions the outcome is read against. Anchor: none on its own. It is a habitat condition that updates (Fig 11 :73), not the ground. Not a standalone domain; absorbed into D2.
- **D4 Verification frame.** Actors: ecologists, indicators, monitoring instruments. Currency: agreement and residual. Anchor: measured persistence, never agreement. Admitted.
- **D5 Cross-scale and temporal channel.** Actors: local patches, regional networks, successive climate states. Currency: contact carried across scales. Anchor: the same measured persistence. Admitted.
- **X1 "The optimal pattern" as a landscape property alone.** No nameable anchor. Not a domain yet.
- **X2 "Connectivity" as an intrinsic good.** Absorbed into D2 relations; its anchor is D2's anchor.

Union, not partition. D1, D2, D4 and D5 form overlapping conditions; the whole is a compound of contact-now, contact-under-shift and cross-scale contact. Dependency order: perception, vocabulary, objective, planning, learn and carry (Fig 3). The vocabulary/objective link has no instrument. Strain test applied: a naive reading makes climate the ground; Fig 11 :73 sends it back to D2, after which no line strains.

## VERDICT (from the figures)

The figure-derived verdict is a shape with a declared `gap`, not a ranking. Score per species and per climate state, never as one index. Enumerate habitat contact outward, including second- and third-order effects. Treat the value of corridors, cores, stepping stones and refugia as relative to the species residual, so a change of climate changes the ranking and no standing "best" list survives. Treat the problem as a union of contact-now, contact-under-shift and cross-scale contact, which one lever cannot clear. Treat implementation as permanent work. Let the ground be measured persistence, not a suitability map or a count of reviews. No figure line names a winner, so the ranking stays open. Mode `specified`, ungrounded, candidate. World sources corroborate the conditional shape from low-overlap fields and are one witness each, not a panel.

- **Cost if wrong:** if a single best pattern is asserted, management is steered by a name whose sign changes with the residual, and the outside people asked to check it lose the independence that makes their check worth having. Fig 2 :42 prices that: "Buy independence in proportion to the cost of being wrong." The cost is real, bounded and avoidable by leading with the shape and its refuter.
- **Undoable:** yes. No term is deleted if the claim fails. A refutation is added and the reach falls because something was added rather than because anything was struck out (Fig 6 :42-43). The shape, the five refuters and the source ledger carry provenance so a later reader can locate the error.
- **Reversed by:** a ground reading, not agreement. A fixed pattern that keeps per-species persistence low and non-trending across two or more documented climate states would break Fig 11 :51. An agent-only improvement that moved measured persistence without changing contact would break Fig 11 :40. A single lever clearing all three conditions with no categorically different residue would break Fig 9 :57. A source-discriminating instrument naming a pattern for a given residual would start closing the `gap`. Agreement among reviews sharing one evidence pool is not such an instrument.
