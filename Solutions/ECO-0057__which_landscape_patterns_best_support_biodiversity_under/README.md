## Restatement as an effect

The question asks for a name: a ranking of landscape arrangements by how well they keep biodiversity small in residual under a climate that moves. Stated as an effect, `R = |Γ(b,a) − o'|` is indexed per species slot and per time (Fig 1 :19), and the question wants an arrangement whose `R` stays low across a change in the conditions that `o'` is read against. What an answer would look like, bound as an ordinary term before the run: a pattern `P*` such that for every species slot `s` and every climate state `c` in a stated range, `R_s(c | P*)` is low and not trending up. Binding that shape first is what lets the figures refuse it rather than let the frame chase a preference it cannot see (THE_FORMULA.md, "Direction").

## Headline result

The figures return a **shape, not a name** (Fig 9 :32), and the strongest refusal is Fig 11 :51: *"No standing list of good actors survives a change of residual."* Climate change is a change of the residual, so a "best pattern" list does not survive it. What survives is a set of constraints any answer must satisfy: enumerate contact rather than compose a recipe (Fig 11 :43–44), keep valence relative to the residual (Fig 11 :49–51), treat isolation as substitution (Fig 11 :66–69), keep the ground as measured persistence and not the climate or agreement (Fig 11 :73, Fig 2 :29/:35), and expect a union rather than a partition (Fig 9 :54–57). The specific ranking is declared `gap`.

## What is deliberately not here

- No personal emails of named researchers, and no scraped contact list.
- No ranking of people by how likely they are to agree. Selecting for agreement manufactures the correlation that disqualifies a verdict (Fig 2 :40).
- No bulk-send plan, no automation, no misleading subject line.

Three figure-grounded reasons, in the figures' own words:

1. **Agreement is not a ground.** *"Alignment is triangulation, not negotiation. A mirror cannot correct you."* (Fig 2 :35). A list of receptive ecologists is a mirror.
2. **A shared pool is one witness.** *"disqualifying for a verdict: agreement about the world is worthless when the agreeing frames share an evidence pool"* (Fig 2 :40). The 74 reviews synthesised by Ranius et al. are one pool, not seventy-four checks.
3. **Do not hand over the interiors before the verdict.** *"A verifier that can reconstruct the claim cannot verify it."* (Fig 10 :42). The first contact carries the residual and its refuter, never the framework.

Legally: unsolicited bulk email breaches GDPR and CAN-SPAM style rules in many jurisdictions. Public opt-in and submission venues avoid that failure as well as the structural one.
