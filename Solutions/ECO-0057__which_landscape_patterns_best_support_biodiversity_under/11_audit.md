## 1. Independent domain census

I redid the census from the residual line alone before reading the primary's conclusions, using the WATSON order: R by arity, symmetry and scale; one line per domain with actors / currency / anchor; partition or union; order by dependency.

- **R (arity / symmetry / scale).** The residual is the failure of a landscape ranking to keep species persistence stable while the conditions change. Arity: many-to-many, indexed per species and per climate state. Symmetry: "best" is a threshold against a ground, and the sign of a landscape element is not carried by the element (Fig 12 :87, Fig 11 :49). Scale: patch, landscape, region, time. This is not one hard question with an aggregate score; an aggregate is the move Fig 1 :22 disqualifies.
- **D1 Species-population loop.** Actors: populations and their Γ. Currency: per-slot residual reduction. Anchor: measured persistence. Admitted.
- **D2 Landscape habitat.** Actors: patches, corridors, matrix, conditions, relations. Currency: contact. Anchor: measured persistence via the ground. Admitted.
- **D3 Climate as condition.** No standalone anchor. It updates. Absorbed into D2.
- **D4 Verification frame.** Actors: ecologists and instruments. Currency: agreement and residual. Anchor: measured persistence, never agreement. Admitted.
- **D5 Cross-scale channel.** Actors: patches, networks, successive climate states. Currency: contact carried across scales. Anchor: measured persistence. Admitted.
- **X1 A best pattern as a landscape property.** No nameable anchor. Not a domain yet.
- **X2 Connectivity as an intrinsic good.** No anchor of its own; absorbed into D2.

Union, not partition: contact-now, contact-under-shift and cross-scale contact overlap. Dependency order: perception, vocabulary, objective, planning, learn and carry (Fig 3). The vocabulary/objective link is uninstrumented. My census matches the primary's four admitted domains plus the absorbed condition, and its union reading. I derived it from Fig 9's union split and Fig 1's per-slot rule; the primary derived it from Fig 11 and Fig 3. Different starting figures, same boundaries. Low overlap between routes within one evidence pool, so the agreement is expected and carries no independent weight.

## 4. Round trip gap R_T

The formula defines `R_T = gap(x, (T_E ∘ T_A)(x))`, and Fig 4 :30 requires it to be measured rather than assumed. I ran the round trip on the condensation the folder actually uses.

1. **Sent up (x):** the verbatim figure set, chiefly Fig 11 :43-51 and :66-73 (enumeration, valence, substitution, habitat versus ground), Fig 9 :55-57 (union), Fig 1 :19 (per-slot).
2. **Abstraction (T_A):** the shorthand in `07_the_tether_answer.md`: score per species, enumerate contact, valence is residual-relative, the problem is a union, the ground is measured persistence, the name is a `gap`.
3. **Concretisation (T_E):** a field recommendation: connect X percent of the landscape with corridors of width W and keep core patches above area A, evaluated by a single biodiversity index.

**R_T is nonzero.** Four constraints in the shorthand do not survive the trip back down:

- the **per-slot requirement**, replaced in the concretisation by a single aggregate index (Fig 1 :19/:22);
- the **residual-relative valence rule**, replaced by a standing prescription whose sign is fixed (Fig 11 :51);
- the **union structure**, replaced by one lever (Fig 9 :57);
- the **ground distinction**, replaced by a suitability projection treated as the anchor (Fig 11 :73).

Two things survive cleanly: contact must be enumerated rather than composed, and a name requires a ground settlement. That is the residue worth sending. R_T was **not** measured numerically, because no field reading exists; it is recorded qualitatively as the enumerated set of dropped constraints. Treating "the formula is a condensed version of the metalaws" as identity would set R_T to zero and would be the dead-reckoning error Fig 4 :30 names.
