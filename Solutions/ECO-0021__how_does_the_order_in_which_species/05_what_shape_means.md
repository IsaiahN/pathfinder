## 1. A shape is a constraint, not a filler

The canonical figures README states the limit: the framework returns *"what a missing thing would have to look like (a shape, not a name)"*. Fig 9 :32: *"you get a kind of thing, never the answer itself."*

From *The Library of Babel*: *"you can cut your search short if the set of books returned is small and descriptive enough that it provides a general shape of the answer that satisfies your question."*

For this question a **shape** is the separator we can state before we can rank any particular order:

- order dependence is a **bond-type** question (ordered versus symmetric), not a list of species;
- the final species list is the **artifact**, and the removal sequence is the **lineage**;
- the answer is indexed per function, not pooled into one stability number.

That is a shape. A ranked list of the k! worst orders would be a name, and the figures do not return it.

## 2. How a shape is obtained

- *Library of Babel*: *"With each new term gained, you narrow the space and deepen your vocabulary."* Use the vocabulary closest to the thing, look for recurring terms that overlap with it, recurse.
- Fig 6 :30: *"The description travels. Fails by being sharpened into terms only we can read. Cast it in effect terms."* Fig 9 :30-31: *"your words are borrowed too, so describe the effect rather than the cause."* So we describe the residual as a symmetry test, not as "which species matter".
- *Decoding Tacit Knowledge*: *"When contact isn't possible, the best you can send is a plain description of what you'd expect to see, because anyone can check that against the world."* This is why `02_outreach_email.md` asks the recipient what they would expect under an ordered bond before showing them anything.
- *Library of Babel* on stopping: *"the finish line will also be the starting place"*, and the paradox that by then you have *"composed the answer through the process of locating it."*

## 3. Route and lineage are the whole issue for this question

This is the essay that most directly governs ECO-0021. From *How Intelligence Actually Works*:

> *"often, very little about a composed term tells you, from looking at it alone, which route produced it."*

And on the same question of order:

> *"we cannot say which state composed first, and we don't know if the order mattered, because at this level of the set order is only introduced as a concept when it comes to composition"*

The author is speaking about the six-term minimum set, not about ecosystems. The structural claim transfers because both are route questions about a composition: the artifact does not carry the order, the lineage does. Fig 13 :129 is the figure-level statement of the same thing: *"the order is not recoverable from the artefact. It is recoverable from the lineage."*

From *The Recursive Self-Improvement Trap*: *"You cannot recreate humanity. The exact lineage of events is lost. You would need to know every natural event and interaction with every other species, all the way back, and even then you would need the exact sequence."* The author writes "you would not get the same result without the exact events playing out". This is a direct statement of order dependence, and it is the strongest essay-level corroboration of the figure reading here.

## 4. What licenses a name

Only two things, both outside the shape itself:

1. **The ground settles it.** `THE_FORMULA.md` step 5: *"the ground settles it. Verified only by what pays. A gate is not the ground."* A sequence-resolved experiment at fixed final species set would settle whether order matters in that system and for that function.
2. **A provenanced import from a frame whose closure differs**, with a source gate and a shadow test (Fig 6 :23, Fig 8 :50-52). A result from network science on targeted versus random node removal is such an import; it is not the same measurement as ecosystem function.

*They're Scanning Books* sharpens the limit: *"The real import is not a new term in the existing space. It is a new dimension of the space."* And: *"A minimum viable set of six terms is a human's careful guess at reality's compression axes; another sensorium's minimum set need not be those six."* Also: *"Declaring the primitives does not make them neutral. It makes the choice visible."*

So naming "sequence" and "subtraction" as the bonds at work is a binding from the figure vocabulary onto the ecological case. It is a visible choice, provenanced as such, not a discovery of nature's own list.

## 5. The source problem: why the species list cannot decide

*The Hard Problem is About Source* gives the mechanism. An instrument reads green and *"cannot comment on the source"*; one green light, or yellow plus blue, or RGB, or CMYK, or a scheme not yet discovered can all give the same reading. *"If several paths create the same behavior, then behavior is a projection with lossy information (it cannot define consciousness in full)."* It was the **spectrometer**, not an argument, that resolved it. *"An instrument that can't distinguish these cases demonstrates its own inadequacy."*

Applied here: two communities with the same final species list give the same "reading". The reading cannot comment on which route produced it. Four loss orders, four cascade histories and four distinct functional states can all present as the same species list. What is owed is a source-discriminating instrument, meaning a measurement that resolves the route, not another argument about which species are important.

## 6. Correction to my own reading of "shape, not a name"

I initially read the phrase as forbidding the naming of bonds and functions in this question. That was an overstatement.

- The canonical phrase is about a **missing** thing: *"what a missing thing would have to look like (a shape, not a name)."*
- Sequence, subtraction, symmetry and the functional group are all already held in the figure library (Fig 12, Operators table). Naming them is a **binding** from the figure frame onto the ecological case, not the invention of a new primitive.
- Fig 6 :21: *"MINT composes inside the closure and can never add an atom."* Binding an already held bond adds no atom. Claiming that "extinction order" is a new primitive would be the error.
- What remains forbidden is ranking the orders. That is a name the framework does not hold, and it is declared as a `gap` in `01` F9 and in `07`.

Both the earlier overstatement and this correction are recorded so neither is quietly lost.

## 7. Two consequences for this solution

**Prestige is not action.** From *The Recursive Self-Improvement Trap*: *"Prestige must never be allowed to validate an action directly... Prestige can fund an action. It can give an action a budget to be tried. But it cannot settle an action. Only the ground can settle it. The Ground Maintainer must be blind to prestige."* A list of famous ecologists who agree that order matters is the named collapse, not corroboration. Fig 2 :40: *"disqualifying for a verdict: agreement about the world is worthless when the agreeing frames share an evidence pool"*.

**This audit is a simulation, not a ground reading.** Same essay: *"LLMs can quickly simulate an approximate answer/output with the formula found in the architecture post... However, the truly testable output would need to be derived from the real architecture or working the formula yourself."* So `01`, `06`, `07`, `08`, `09` and `11` are simulated derivations checked for internal consistency against the figures. They are not a test of the ecological claim and must be labelled as such. They cannot settle anything they report on.

## 8. Falsifier for this canonical reading

The reading in sections 1 to 5 is falsified if a figure line or an opened essay supplies a rule that ranks k! extinction orders from the bond set alone, or demonstrates that the final species list carries the route with no loss. In either case the "shape, not a name" limit would be narrower than stated here and `01` F9 would be withdrawn. Until then, the honest output is the separator and the lineage requirement, not a ranking.
