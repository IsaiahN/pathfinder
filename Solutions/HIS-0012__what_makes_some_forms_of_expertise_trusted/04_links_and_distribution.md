## Wider literature that bears on the claim, and its overlap

1. **Sociology of expertise, high conceptual overlap but no figure overlap.** Collins and Evans 2002 argue that expertise must be treated `as real` and developed into a normative theory, because `Trust scientists because they have special access to the truth` no longer holds. This is the closest discipline to the question and therefore the least independent witness. It corroborates F2 and F5 at the level of shape.
2. **History and philosophy of science, genuinely low overlap.** The SEP entry on incommensurability reports that revolutions change the list of problems and the standards of admissibility, and that Newton's theory was initially rejected under the older standards. This is a direct corroborator of F3 (the room follows the question). It does not conflict with Fig 2's non-updating anchor, because the standards it describes are values inside a frame while the anchor is what settles a question from outside the frame.
3. **Engineering and organizational sociology, genuinely low overlap.** The CAIB report shows a risk classification drifting downward through repeated `in-family` rulings while the physical hazard stayed fixed. This is F7 (the ground does not decay, the channel does) observed outside the humanities.
4. **Animal behaviour, low overlap, analogue only.** Chimento et al. show that social information use changes with spatial variability and payoff structure. This supports the *shape* of F4 (valence relative to residual and habitat), not any claim about historians.
5. **Human cognition, low overlap.** Kumkale and Albarracin show the source and the message part company over time. This corroborates the separation of prestige from content in Fig 1 :26-27; it does not make credibility an anchor.
6. **History of science case material, the question's own domain.** Thompson's phrenology entry shows a specialty rising through elite institutions, spreading to popular practice, and losing scientific standing while remaining culturally alive. Kept as case material, not as an independent witness, because it is the same field the question asks about.

No source conflicts with a figure verdict. The conflict rule was not triggered. Where a tension was visible (Kuhn's changing standards versus the non-updating anchor; the sleeper effect's decaying credibility versus Fig 1's disqualification of credibility), it was returned to the census and recorded in `01_figure_grounded_audit.md` section 8.

## Distribution: offer, do not push

- **Fig 4 :22-25:** `Only methods cross up. Only head starts come down. A recording carried upward looks like knowledge and is a description of one occasion.` Sending a list of which experts were trusted in which century is a recording. Sending the separator, its refuter and the method is a generator.
- **Fig 10 :52:** `Bring a characterised residual, never the source.` The recipient must be able to check the output before seeing the framework's interior.
- **Fig 2 :40:** `disqualifying for a verdict: agreement about the world is worthless when the agreeing frames share an evidence pool.` A recipient who has already absorbed the theory has joined the frame being checked.
- **Fig 2 :42:** `Buy independence in proportion to the cost of being wrong.` Ask for a refutation from someone whose reading of the historical record was formed separately.
- **Fig 9 :60:** `Use filters for the budget and witnesses for the verdict. Never let a filter hand you a verdict.` Journal prestige and reviewer reputation are filters for where to submit, not witnesses for the claim.

**Recommended order in a reply:** (1) the separator and its four refuters; (2) ask what the recipient would count as a failure and which historical case it handles badly; (3) record that verdict; (4) only then offer the four links, with the note that the figures, not this summary, are the authority.

## The two-stage offer

**Stage 1.** Send the residual and the refuter. Ask for a failure, not agreement. Record the verdict before any theory is shown.

**Stage 2, opt-in only.** If the recipient is curious, offer the figure extract, Collins and Evans, the SEP entry and the CAIB section. Their later agreement is never pooled as corroboration (Fig 2 :40). Collins and Evans shares the question's field, so agreement there is expected and weakly independent.

**Stage 3, if asked.** Offer the eight-step formula as a method to reuse. Each user writes their own refuter before running it and states the mode of the result. `THE_FORMULA.md` "Direction" gives the reason: a frame cannot check the direction of its own question unless the sought-for shape is bound as an ordinary term.

**Hard precondition:** stages 1 to 3 only work if the stage-1 output survives. It is `specified`, not `grounded`, and it is a simulation checked for internal consistency. Say so in the first message. A claim that cannot state its own mode fails before it reaches a ground.
