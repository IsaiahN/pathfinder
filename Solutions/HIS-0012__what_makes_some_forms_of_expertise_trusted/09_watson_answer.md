## QUESTION

Decide, in effect terms, what changes when a form of expertise is trusted in period P1 and rejected in P2. Option A: the change is a property of the expertise itself, so a standing list of trustworthy expertise forms could be written. Option B: the change is a relation among ground contact, independence of checking frames, room composition and habitat, so the same expertise flips valence when any of those changes. What each leads to: A produces a credential list, treats credibility accrued as a verdict, and is a frame scoring itself with a quantity it produces; B produces a per-domain separator, a discriminator requirement, and refuters written in advance. The figures decide B.

## DOMAIN CENSUS

R by arity, symmetry and scale, then one line per domain with actors, currency and anchor; partition or union; order by dependency.

- **R (the residual).** Variation in trust status of a claim-producing frame across periods. Arity: at least four overlapping conditions. Symmetry: trust is not symmetric with correctness, since agreement can be a convention or a verdict (Fig 2 :39-41). Scale: one practitioner is one slot (Fig 1 :19); a period is a room; the handoff between periods is a chain.
- **D1, claim and ground.** Actors: claim, instrument, observation, ground. Currency: residual and settlement. Anchor: whatever settles the question and does not change its answer because of what any frame thinks. Partition boundary: does the frame range against a ground or against other frames?
- **D2, credibility and prestige.** Actors: credentials, titles, reputation, affiliation. Currency: credibility accrued. Anchor: none, and that is the finding; the quantity is frame-internal. Not a verdict domain; a budget filter only.
- **D3, room and institution.** Actors: expert communities, journals, academies, gatekeepers. Currency: agreement and conventions. Anchor: the ground, never agreement. Partition by question-match; union when several conditions bind.
- **D4, habitat and valence.** Actors: practitioners, instruments, conditions, relations. Currency: contact, introduction, capability. Anchor: the ground settles while the habitat updates. Union of overlapping conditions.
- **D5, transmission and public account.** Actors: master, apprentice, public, text. Currency: contact and correction. Anchor: an effect description anyone can check against the world.
- **Order by dependency.** Settle D1 before enumerating D4; assemble D3 from the residual surfaced in D4; transmit D5 last; keep D2 as a firewall across all four. Fig 3 :20: the chain is ordered by dependency, not importance. SEARCHED first, then INWARD, then OUTWARD (Formula step 7).

Strain test applied: Kuhnian changing standards and the non-updating anchor do not conflict after re-census. The standards are epistemic values inside a frame; the anchor settles a question from outside the frame. Different objects. The sleeper effect's decaying source credibility and Fig 1's disqualification of credibility also do not conflict: one is about memory in individuals, the other about what may count as a verdict.

## VERDICT (from the figures)

A form of expertise is never trusted merely because it is credible. Trust is legitimate only as triangulation against a ground that does not update; it becomes conventional when the reference is the community's own agreement. The flip across periods is produced by one or more of four overlapping conditions: a room that stops following the question, a channel to the ground that decays while the ground does not, checking frames that share one evidence pool, or a habitat that changes the valence of the same actors. Credibility accrued is frame-internal and has no verdict weight. Over a fixed atom set the trusted and rejected arrangements are isomers. The standing-list answer is refused; the universal list is a declared `gap`, reported as unreachable rather than silence. Status: `specified` per domain, `general` on the shape, `candidate`, ungrounded.

- **cost if wrong:** bounded and locatable. The packet states mode, refuters and provenance before its conclusion, so an error is findable. The main cost is spent independence: sending a recording of who was trusted instead of a generator would convert outside checkers into members of the frame being checked. Fig 2 :42 prices that cost: `Buy independence in proportion to the cost of being wrong.` The lede with the separator and its refuter avoids it.
- **undoable:** yes. The separator and four refuters can be corrected without withdrawing the shape. A refuter that fires narrows a domain; it does not require inventing a new primitive. A sent packet can be superseded by a corrected one, and the lineage records what changed.
- **reversed by:** a discriminator in the historical record, not agreement. An anchor test showing the reference moved with belief; a room test showing the gatekeepers stayed fixed while the problem list changed; a channel test showing a saturated metric or pooled readings; a standing list whose valence survives a change of residual; or a credential that changes truth status by itself under repeated measurement. Any of these would narrow or break the verdict. Until one fires, the result stays `candidate`.
