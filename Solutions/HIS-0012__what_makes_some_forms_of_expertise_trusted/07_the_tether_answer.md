# The Tether's answer: why expertise changes its trust status

**Mode: `specified` on the per-domain claims, `general` on the structural shape, not `grounded`.** A shape and a route, not a result. Simulated derivation checked against the figures (`05_what_shape_means.md` section 7), not a ground reading.

## 1. The residual it answers

A form of expertise E is trusted in period P1 and rejected in P2. The residual is the variation in trust status of a claim-producing frame, indexed per slot and per period (Fig 1 :19: `The residual is indexed by object slot, not aggregated.`). The question asks for the variable that governs that variation. It is a **union compound** of at least four overlapping conditions, not one hard problem and not a two-list partition (Fig 9 :54-57: `a case in two conditions is not a harder case of either. It stops being a mixture and becomes a specific compound.`). It therefore has its own loop and its own discriminator.

The ordinary answer names a property of E: better evidence, better credentials, better methods. That answer is refused, because Fig 11 :51 says `No standing list of good actors survives a change of residual.`

## 2. The shape, per domain (verdicts kept separate)

1. **A form of expertise is not trusted because it is credible.** Fig 1 :26-27 names `credibility accrued` as frame-internal: `They are frame-internal. A frame cannot score itself with a quantity it also produces.` Credentials and reputation fund a hearing; they cannot settle a claim (Fig 9 :60). Standing is a stake that survives the outcome it was earned on (Fig 10 :61), so the holder of an old reputation has an interest in the old reading.

2. **Trust is legitimate only as triangulation against a ground that does not update.** Fig 2 :29: `An anchor is whatever settles the question and does not change its answer because of what any frame thinks.` Fig 2 :35: `Alignment is triangulation, not negotiation. A mirror cannot correct you.` When the reference becomes the community's own agreement, the expertise is trusted by drift. Fig 2 :34 explains why nobody notices: `everything drifts together, and the drift is coherent, which is why nobody notices.`

3. **The expert room must follow the question.** Fig 10 :71-72: `The room composition is a function of the question. When the question changes, the room should change. A room that stays fixed while the question moves is the failure; the correlation is only its symptom.` A period's consensus is variation inside a room (Fig 7 :35: `A room's agreement is variation, not a verdict. Selection between rooms is where the ground votes.`). Rejection in the next period is the ground voting through a different room.

4. **The ground does not decay; the channel does.** Fig 10 :24: `the ground does not decay, the channel does.` Stale percepts, pooled readings and saturated metrics (Fig 10 :25-26) can sever an expertise from its ground while the ground stays fixed. This is distinct from the room moving; both produce a flip, and they need different instruments.

5. **Valence is relative to the residual.** Fig 11 :49-51: `An actor's valence is relative to the residual, not a property it carries.` and `No standing list of good actors survives a change of residual.` The same practitioners, methods and instruments are a benefactor to one residual and a malfactor to another. Fig 11 :40: `An improvement that does not change contact changes nothing, however much it improves.` A specialty that only becomes more internally coherent does not become more trustworthy.

6. **Over a fixed atom set the flip is an isomer.** Fig 12 :117: `Same atoms, different bond, different substance. The difference is invisible in the formula and decisive in the behaviour.` The trusted and rejected periods hold the same actors and instruments; the bond to the ground and to the room changed. "Expertise" is a functional group, and trust status is the qualifier (Fig 12 :123). The atom-set condition is part of the claim; if a new instrument is required, the relation is import, not isomerism (Fig 6 :23).

7. **The framework returns a shape, not a list.** Fig 9 :32: `you get a kind of thing, never the answer itself.` No figure line names the expertise that will lose trust next or the century in which it will happen. That is a declared `gap` of the unreachable kind, reported as a verdict, not silence (Fig 13 :134: `Unreachable is a verdict here, not a silence`).

## 3. What it explicitly does not say

- Not a list of trusted and untrusted expertise forms. The object is a separator.
- Not "credentials are irrelevant." They are a budget filter (Fig 9 :60), and they can be catalysts when they survive their own use (Fig 12 :119). They are just not witnesses for truth.
- Not "all trust is drift." Trust can be triangulated (Fig 2 :35); the claim is about what would have to be true for it to be legitimate.
- Not a single cause. The four conditions form a union; one lever leaves the intersections standing (Fig 9 :56).
- Not a claim that Kuhnian paradigm change and the non-updating anchor contradict each other. The first is a change of standards inside a frame; the second is what settles a question from outside it. Re-census in `01` section 8.
- Not `grounded`. No historical measurement was taken, and the essays' own rule says a system cannot validate itself.

## 4. What would settle it: the ground and the discriminator

A discriminator in the historical record, not more agreement. From *The Hard Problem is About Source*: an instrument that reads green `cannot comment on the source`; the color case was settled `not by argument, but by the arrival of a better instrument.` For HIS-0012 the discriminators would be:

- **Anchor test:** did the checking community range against something that did not move when the community admired it (Fig 2 :29-30)? If the reference was the community's own agreement, the trust was conventional or drifted.
- **Room test:** did the problem list, instruments or standards change while the expert room stayed fixed (Fig 10 :71-72)? If yes, the flip is predicted by the room condition.
- **Channel test:** did an instrument stop discriminating, a metric saturate, or readings get pooled (Fig 10 :25-26), while the ground stayed fixed? If yes, the flip is predicted by the channel condition.
- **Import test:** did the new standing require an atom absent from the old period (Fig 6 :23)? If yes, the isomer reading narrows to import and needs provenance plus a shadow test.
- **Standing falsifiers:** the five pre-registered conditions in `01` section 5 and `08` section 3.

## 5. The honest stop

**Unreached, not unreachable in principle.** The separator returns which condition is admissible in a case once a discriminator is named. It does not return the name of the next expertise to lose trust, or the date. Fig 9 :32 and Fig 13 :134 bound that stop. The four essays opened this session and the six world sources are all read through the same framework pool or the question's own field, so their agreement is internal consistency, not corroboration. Fig 2 :40: `disqualifying for a verdict: agreement about the world is worthless when the agreeing frames share an evidence pool.` A real second witness would be a historian whose reading of a flip was formed independently of this derivation and who returns a refutation.

## 6. One-paragraph version for outreach

> The Tether does not give a list of expertise forms that are trusted in one century and rejected in another. It says that no such standing list can survive a change of what a period is trying to settle. Trust is legitimate only when a claim-producing frame is checked by something that does not move when the community admires it; credibility accrued is a quantity the field produces about itself and cannot settle anything. A form of expertise loses trust when the expert room stops following the question, when the channel to its ground decays while the ground does not, when checking frames share one evidence pool, or when the habitat changes the valence of the same practitioners and methods. Over a fixed atom set the trusted and rejected arrangements are isomers: the same actors, differently bonded, with different behavior in the world. What the framework offers is a separator, the refuters written in advance, and the instruments that would close a case; it refuses to name the next expertise or its date. The verdict stays `candidate` until a discriminator in the record settles it.
