## DOMAIN CENSUS

R by arity, symmetry and scale, then one line per domain with actors, currency and anchor, partition or union, order by dependency.

- **R (the residual).** A compressed representation is scored as successful by a bit count while the receiving check can still fail. Arity: three-place (source, representation, relevance term at the far end). A two-place reading smuggles "matters" in as an unbound preference (Fig 5 :61-63). Symmetry: bits are exchangeable across positions, which is why an aggregate count can stand in for compression achieved; mattering is ordered, a threshold relation, with the asymmetry of comparison rather than the symmetry of conjunction. Scale: symbol, message, model class, and across-scale via the round trip.
- **D1 Coding and reconstruction.** Actors: source, encoder, channel, decoder, destination. Currency: bits, rate R, distortion D, entropy, mutual information. Anchor: a measured reconstruction error, but only once the distortion measure is pinned to a ground; as stated by the field it is still *"a subject of on-going discussion"*, so the anchor is borrowed, not present. Admitted as a compound partner.
- **D2 The compressor's frame.** Actors: hypotheses H in a class, atoms, bonds, library Γ, candidate φ. Currency: description length and the MDL bargain cost(φ) plus left(R,φ). Anchor: the ground settling a minted term, absent here, so "shortest" is frame-internal. Admitted with warning.
- **D3 The ground and the criterion.** Actors: the ground, frames, the seat that keeps contact. Currency: residuals and whether the ground pays. Anchor: the ground itself, which does not update (Fig 2 :29-30). This domain answers part 2 of the question.
- **D4 Relevance and contact.** Actors: the relevant variable Y, prior preferences and goals, the habitat. Currency: information retained about Y, risk against preferences, effect descriptions. Anchor: the use at the far end, enumerated from contact. This is where "matters" gets a nameable operational handle.
- **X1 "information that matters in itself".** No nameable actors, currency or anchor. Not a domain yet, only a phrase. Recorded as `gap`, not forced into a figure line.

Union, not partition: D1 and D2 supply the mechanics, D3 and D4 supply the criterion, and a single lever that clears the first pair leaves the second pair's conditions standing (Fig 9 :55). Dependency order: perceive what is arriving (D1), get terms for what the use affords (D4), state what counts as preservation (D3), then choose and price the transform (D2), then measure the round trip, then let the ground settle. Strain test: the apparent conflict between Fig 1 (ground is the only metric) and D1's distortion measure dissolves on re-census, since the first governs what settles a learning agent's bets and the second is an instrument that can be wrong on axes it does not measure (Fig 2 :37-38). ## VERDICT (from the figures)

Compression preserves what matters only relative to a bound relevance term imported from the use frame, scored at a ground that does not move, with the round-trip gap measured at the level where the receiving check reads and with the arrangement carrying the information the flat description drops. The compression ratio is a filter for the budget and never a witness for the verdict. The criterion of mattering is fixed by the ground of the use because the question is constitutively about that use; a designer-chosen distortion measure allowed to decide the outcome is a convention that has stopped being one. A universal relevance measure is a `gap`. Mode `specified` on the shape, `general` on the cited laws, `gap` on the member, nothing `grounded`.

- **cost if wrong:** bounded and locatable. Each sub-claim has a stated refuter and the packet records its provenance, so an error is findable rather than invisible. The larger avoidable cost is spending the independence of outside readers by sending a named relevance measure and the full theory before the effect claim is checked. Fig 2 :42 prices that: "Buy independence in proportion to the cost of being wrong." Leading with the shape and the four refuters avoids it.
- **undoable:** yes. The imports (a distortion measure, a relevance variable, a perceptual check) are removable without touching the figure derivation, and each can be replaced by a provenanced alternative from a different frame. A sent claim can be superseded by the corrected packet. No primitive has to be withdrawn to close the gap; it only needs a figure line that names one.
- **reversed by:** a ground reading, not agreement. A receiving check that distinguishes two equal-atom, different-bond representations would confirm C2 or refute it; a lossy transform with a measured zero round trip at the reading level would break F1; a compression ratio that predicts the receiving check across two independent applications would break F2; a criterion that moves under pressure from frames and is still accepted as an anchor would break C3; a verbatim figure line naming a universal relevance primitive would close the gap.
