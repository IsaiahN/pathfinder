## Independent domain census

I redid the census from the residual line alone, before relying on the primary's conclusions, using the WATSON order: R by arity, symmetry and scale; one line per domain with actors, currency and anchor; partition or union; order by dependency.

- **R (arity / symmetry / scale).** The residual is a successful bit count coupled to a failing receiving check. Arity: three-place (source, representation, relevance term). A two-place version has no currency for "matters", so it is not the same residual. Symmetry: the bit count is exchangeable across positions, which is exactly why it can be aggregated into a single success figure; mattering is a threshold relation, so it is ordered. Scale: symbol, message, hypothesis class, and across-scale through the round trip. Fig 1 :19 (**"The residual is indexed by object slot, not aggregated."**) is a useful cross-check here: an aggregate count hides the slot where the check fails.
- **D1 Coding and reconstruction.** Actors: source, encoder, channel, decoder, destination. Currency: bits, rate, distortion, mutual information. Anchor: a measured reconstruction error, but the measure itself is chosen. Question type: mechanics, a compound partner.
- **D2 The compressor's frame.** Actors: hypotheses in a class, atoms, bonds, Γ. Currency: description length and the MDL bargain. Anchor: the ground settling a term, absent inside this frame. Question type: class-relative minimisation.
- **D3 The ground and the criterion.** Actors: the ground, frames, the seat. Currency: residuals and whether the ground pays. Anchor: the ground, which does not update. Question type: the one that answers the second half of the question.
- **D4 Relevance and contact.** Actors: the relevant variable, preferences and goals, the habitat. Currency: information retained about the relevant variable, risk against preferences. Anchor: the use at the far end. Question type: where the criterion is named and imported.
- **X1 "information that matters in itself".** No actors, no currency, no anchor. Not a domain.

Union, not partition. D1 and D2 are the transform side; D3 and D4 are the criterion side; the criterion conditions survive a lever that clears only the transform side (Fig 9 :55-57). Order by dependency: enumerate contact, bind the criterion, state what counts as preservation, choose and price the transform, measure the round trip, let the ground settle.

My census starts from Fig 1 :19 (per-slot residual) and Fig 9 :55 (union compound), where the primary started from the arity analysis and Fig 3 (dependency chain). Different starting figures, same domain boundaries. That is weak agreement between routes, but still one evidence pool.

## Round trip gap R_T

The Symbols table defines R_T as the gap between what was sent up and what came back down, `gap(x, (T_E ∘ T_A)(x))`, measuring what the coarser description cannot hold. Fig 4 :30 requires it to be measured rather than assumed. I ran the round trip on the condensation this folder actually uses.

1. **Sent up (x):** the verbatim figure set, chiefly Fig 1 :27 (ratio is frame-internal), Fig 4 :31-32 (the gap is the report), Fig 12 :94 and :117 (arrangement and isomer), Fig 2 :29 and :36 (anchor), Fig 5 :65-66 (bind the term).
2. **Abstraction (T_A):** the shorthand carried in `07_the_tether_answer.md`: compression preserves only relative to a bound relevance term, the ratio is a filter, the gap must be measured, the arrangement carries what matters, the ground decides.
3. **Concretisation (T_E):** a concrete engineering claim, for example "this codec preserves what matters because it achieves rate R at distortion D with a lower bit count than the alternative".

**R_T is nonzero.** Four things the shorthand holds do not survive the trip back down:

- the **identity of the relevance variable** at the ground. The shorthand says "bound term"; the concrete claim needs a particular distortion or perception measure, which is an import with a source gate, not a figure output;
- the **measurement of R_T itself**. The shorthand says "measure the gap"; the concrete engineering claim reports only a rate and a distortion, so the gap was not actually carried across;
- the **isomer test outcome**. The shorthand says "arrangement is the substance"; the concrete claim must still show that two equal-atom, different-bond representations behave differently, which bit counts cannot establish;
- the **anchor's non-updating property**. The shorthand says "the ground decides"; the concrete claim must show the criterion did not move when two application frames pushed on it.

Conversely, one thing survives cleanly: a quantity a frame produces for itself cannot be that frame's verdict. That is the residue worth sending. R_T was **not** measured numerically, because no receiving check was run; it is recorded qualitatively as the enumerated set of dropped constraints. Treating "the formula is the condensed version of the metalaws" as identity would set R_T to zero and would be the dead-reckoning error Fig 4 :30 names. The honest form is a lossy condensation with a measured gap.
