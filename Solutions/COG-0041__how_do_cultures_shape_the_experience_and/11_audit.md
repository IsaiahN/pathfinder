## 4. Round trip gap R_T

The Symbols table defines R_T as the gap between what was sent up and what came back down, gap(x, (T_E ∘ T_A)(x)), measuring what the coarser description cannot hold. Fig 4 :30 requires it to be measured rather than assumed. I ran the round trip on the condensation the folder actually uses.

2. **Abstraction (T_A):** the shorthand in `07` and `09`: "culture supplies categories, weighting and habitat; shared atoms and private history resist fixation; misinterpretation is channel failure; the inventory of emotions is a gap."
3. **Concretisation (T_E):** a concrete answer a reader might take away, for example "the OURS model is the framework's answer, so relational cultures feel emotion between people and individualist cultures feel it inside them."

**R_T is nonzero.** Three things the shorthand holds do not survive the trip back down:

- the **status of w as unmeasured**. The shorthand says "weighting"; the concrete reading treats it as a measured cultural coefficient. No person-level instrument measures w here (Fig 5 :32 requires settlement).
- the **binding status of the affective atoms**. The shorthand says "shared atoms"; the concrete reading treats core affect and concepts as framework primitives. They are bindings from affective science, recorded as imports with no atom added (Fig 6 :21), not new members of the substrate.
- the **shape-not-a-name limit**. OURS and MINE are culture-psychology names for the habitat half of C1. Treating either as the framework's answer names what the framework returns as a shape (Fig 9 :32).

Conversely, one thing survives cleanly: the claim that capability is person-and-habitat, and that b is a mixture whose w is learnable, so culture shapes without determining. That is the residue worth sending. R_T was **not** measured numerically, because no person-level ground exists; it is recorded qualitatively as the enumerated set of dropped constraints. Treating "the formula is the condensed version of the metalaws" as identity would set R_T to zero and would be the dead-reckoning error Fig 4 :30 names.
