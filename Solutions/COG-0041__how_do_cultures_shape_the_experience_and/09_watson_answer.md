# WATSON answer: COG-0041, culture and emotion

**Mode: `specified`, not `grounded`.** Figures are primary and decide. World sources are secondary, corroborate but never overrule, and a conflict returns the derivation to the domain census. This is a simulated derivation checked for internal consistency against the figures, not a ground reading. No person-level measurement was taken.

## QUESTION

How do cultures shape the experience and interpretation of emotions without determining them entirely?

Restated as an effect: why does R = |Γ(b,a) − o′| take a culturally variable shape that is nonetheless not fixed by culture, and why does it vary again between people who share one culture? R is indexed per episode and per person, never averaged (Fig 1 :19). Anchor: the person's own report of felt quality, because the question is constitutively about people (Fig 2 :36, Fig 2 :37).

## DOMAIN CENSUS

Arity: a four-place relation (person Γ, cultural habitat H, action a, situation-and-body o′), indexed by episode and ordered by development; it carries an ordered bond and a comparison bond, not symmetric ones. Symmetry: "shaped but not determined" is a threshold between the inherited-prior and private-history terms of w, not a presence or absence. Scale: the single person's loop, the culture as population with a shared library, and the developmental time channel; the membrane is lossy (Fig 4 :30).

Union, not partition: D1/D3 and D2/D4 form two compounds; the whole is a union of C1 (culture supplies categories, weighting and habitat) and C2 (shared atoms and private history resist fixation) (Fig 9 :54-57). Dependency order: perception → vocabulary → objective → planning → learn and carry (Fig 3 :20).

Strain test applied: Fig 13's six physical terms fit emotion only by renaming core affect as energy and a category as a spectrum. Re-census sends Fig 13 to the honest-stop role only (S1 in `01` §6). No other strain after re-census.

**Pool B, cultural psychology (one witness for this pool).**

## VERDICT (from the figures)

The shape survives: **a culture shapes emotion by supplying categories, the weighting of inherited prior against private history, and an enumerable habitat; it does not determine it because the loop is scale-invariant, the atoms are shared, b always carries a private-history term, w is learnable, and differently-history'd people diverge.** Culturally specific emotions are isomers over shared atoms, with the functional-group reading as the weaker alternative. Misinterpretation is a channel failure, not a change in what was felt. The union is kept separate per domain, never averaged. Which specific categories are universal or culture-specific is a declared `gap`, not a hidden answer: the source-discriminating instrument is not held here. Mode `specified`.

- **cost if wrong:** bounded and locatable. The packet is mode-labeled, states its five refuters before the conclusion, and records provenance, so an error is findable rather than invisible. The real cost is spending an expert's independence by sending a recording or a list of universal emotions instead of a generator; Fig 2 :42 prices that ("Buy independence in proportion to the cost of being wrong."). That cost is avoidable by leading with the shape claim and its refuter.
- **undoable:** yes. No term is deleted if the claim fails; a refutation is added and the reach falls because "something was added rather than because anything was deleted" (Fig 6 :42-43). The shape and its falsifiers are recorded with provenance so a later reader can locate the error (Fig 10 :62-64).
- **reversed by:** a person-level ground, not agreement. An instrument that measures the weighting w, or that discriminates the sources of an emotion report, would close the gap; a habitat substitution leaving the capability unchanged would break F1; an emotion with no shared atomic ingredient would break the isomer claim; a felt-quality change with body, category supply and channel held fixed would break F5; a single lever restoring both union conditions with no different residue would collapse the union. Until one of these fires, the verdict stands as `candidate`.
