## 1. A shape is a constraint, not a filler

The canonical limit: the framework returns "what a missing thing would have to look like (a shape, not a name)". Fig 9 :32: "you get a kind of thing, never the answer itself." Fig 13 :134: "Unreachable is a verdict here, not a silence," and the framework "cannot say which primitive is missing, because naming it would mean already holding it."

From *The Library of Babel* (fetched): "you can cut your search short if the set of books returned is small and descriptive enough that it provides a general shape of the answer that satisfies your question."

For COG-0041 the shape is: **a loop that is constant across cultures, a habitat that is enumerated rather than composed, a belief that mixes inherited prior with private history, a weighting that is learnable, shared affective atoms under different cultural bonds, and a channel that can lose touch with what was felt.** That is a constraint on any answer. It is not a list of which emotions are universal or which categories a given culture holds. Naming OURS or MINE, or naming anger and shame as "basic", fills the slot; the framework does not.

## 2. How a shape is obtained

- *Library of Babel* (fetched): use the vocabulary already held that is closest to the thing, then "With each new term gained, you are able to narrow the space by definition and deepen your vocabulary. It's this recursive process that will help you converge on the answer."
- Fig 6 :30: "Cast it in effect terms." Fig 9 :30-31: "your words are borrowed too, so describe the effect rather than the cause." So describe "a category that lets a bodily change be read as a feeling", not "the emotion module", and describe "a weighting between inherited and private", not "the culture gene".
- *Library of Babel* on stopping: "you will know that you are done because the finish line will also be the starting place" and the paradox that by then "you have already composed the answer through the process of locating it."
- The unbundling rule is part of the shape: "Perhaps your problem was a bundle or conflation of several problems masquerading as one?" That is why `01` splits "shape" into C1 (habitat and category supply) and C2 (shared atoms and private history) rather than trying to average them.

## 3. What licenses a name

Only two things, both outside the shape itself:

1. **The ground settles it.** Formula step 5: "the ground settles it. Verified only by what pays." The README: the framework "stops at the limit of what the available instruments can distinguish, and records the lineage so the stop is honest." For this question the ground is the person's own report of felt quality (Fig 2 :37), so a name is licensed only by a first-person report under conditions where the category supply is declared.
2. **A provenanced import from a frame whose closure differs** (Fig 6 :23, Fig 8 :51-52), with a source gate and a shadow test. "Convergent derivation and adopted import look identical in the contents. Only the record of where each came from separates them."

*They're Scanning Books* sharpens this: "The real import is not a new term in the existing space. It is a new dimension of the space." Also: "Declaring the primitives does not make them neutral. It makes the choice visible." The six-term substrate is a visible guess, not nature's own list, and the emotion atoms admitted here (core affect and learned categories) are bindings from affective science, recorded as such. Naming one as *the* answer to a slot turns a visible choice into an invisible one.

## 4. The ground and the source problem

*The Hard Problem is About Source* (fetched) gives the mechanism. Metamerism: an instrument reads green and "it cannot comment on the source" (one green light, or yellow plus blue, or RGB, or CMYK, or a scheme not yet discovered). "If several paths create the same behavior, then behavior is a projection with lossy information." And "The answer is unreached until a better instrument exists." It was the spectrometer, not an argument, that resolved it.

Applied here: a report of "anger" or "shame" is a reading. Its source could be high-arousal unpleasant affect plus a Western individualist category, or the same affect plus a relational category, or something not yet distinguished. The New Yorker's Luganda example makes the point empirically: one word produced two conflicting readings (anger and sadness fused, or a language that "readily marks the distinction"). Fig 10 :24: "the ground does not decay, the channel does." What is owed is a discriminator, not a name. This is why F5 and F8 are kept separate: F5 is a live channel failure inside one person; F8 is a missing instrument across cultures.

## 5. Answer to the shape question

**What shapes emotion, in effect terms?** The culture supplies the category library, the inherited prior, and the habitat; the person's loop ranges against the body and the habitat and applies a category; the weighting mixes history and prior; the result is a felt quality that the person can report. The culture shapes all of those supplies.

**Why is it not determining?** Because the loop's own definitions forbid it: b is a mixture; w is learnable; "Two frames with identical architecture diverge because their histories and their w diverge." Because the atoms are shared and only the bonds vary (Fig 12 :117). Because the culture is an enumerable habitat, not a composing agent (Fig 11 :43). And because a critic from a different pool can arrive at the same conclusion: "the OURS model of emotion turns out to be a common human inheritance" (Fig 8 :50).

**Does any source commit the forbidden error?** The strong constructionist reading does, if taken as a total-cause claim: it deletes the private-history term and the shared atoms. The basic-emotion reading does too, if taken as a fully determined-module claim: it deletes the habitat. `01` F9 returns both to the census and re-maps them to D2 and D3. The correct form keeps both conditions and refuses both deletions.

## 6. Consequences for contact

**Prestige is not action.** *The Recursive Self-Improvement Trap* (fetched): "Prestige must never be allowed to validate an action directly... Prestige can fund an action. It can give an action a budget to be tried. But it cannot settle an action. Only the ground can settle it." A packet sent to famous emotion researchers to gather endorsements is the named collapse. The legitimate ask is to give the shape a budget to be tried and let a person-level ground settle it.

**My own audit is a simulation, not a ground reading.** Same essay: "LLMs can quickly simulate an approximate answer/output with the formula found in the architecture post... However, the truly testable output would need to be derived from the real architecture or working the formula yourself." So `01` is a simulated derivation checked for internal consistency against the figures. It is not a test of the claim and must be labelled as such. It cannot settle anything it reports on.
