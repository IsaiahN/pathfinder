## Residual in effect terms

R = |Γ(b,a) − o′| per slot s (Formula step 1). Here b is a person's belief about what they are feeling, formed from private history and inherited priors mixed by the weighting w; a is the act of expressing, concealing, or naming the feeling; Γ is the person's emotion library of core affect and learned categories; o′ is what the situation and the body actually deliver; s is the single emotion episode, indexed per person and per occasion, never averaged across a culture. The phenomenon to explain is why R stays small and stable in shape across cultures yet varies in content between them, and varies again between people inside one culture. "Shape without determination" is the observation to be derived, not the answer.

## What an answer would look like (bound as an ordinary term before the run)

A pair of conditions whose conjunction explains both halves of the observation: (C1) a culture supplies the categories, weighting, and habitat that constrain what can be felt and how it is read; (C2) the same loop, the same shared affective atoms, and each person's private history keep the result from being fixed by culture alone. Plus an instrument that discriminates a failure of C1 from a failure of C2. This shape is bound now so the pre-registered refuter can fire on it (Formula, "Direction").

## What is deliberately not here

- No ranking of researchers by how likely they are to agree with the shape claim.
- No bulk-send plan.
- No claim that this derivation settles the question, and no naming of which specific emotion categories are universal or culture-specific. That is a `gap` until an instrument discriminates sources.

Three reasons, in the framework's own words. First, "A room does not upgrade the label" and adding people "raises the vocabulary and does not add an anchor" (Seat Map, failure genera).
