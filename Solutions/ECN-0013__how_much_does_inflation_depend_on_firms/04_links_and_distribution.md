## Wider literature that bears on the question

Two clusters, kept apart by evidence pool:

1. **The Compustat markup pool.** NBER WP 23687, NBER WP 34513, the Roosevelt brief, the INET piece and the Richmond Fed brief all use or interrogate the same firm-level markup construction. Their agreement that markups rose is one pool talking (Fig 2 :19-21); their disagreement about magnitude and composition is a correction *inside* the pool, not an independent witness. The BIS pass-through paper uses sectoral and firm-level US data with a different identification question, so it is a partial low-overlap witness even though it is still economics.
2. **The aggregation and source pool.** The two Simpson's paradox sources (statistics/philosophy and psychological methods) share no data with Compustat. They corroborate the structural claim that a pooled association can reverse within subpopulations and that cross-level inference is the danger. The framework essays share the framework author, so their agreement with the figures is expected and carries little independent weight; they are recorded as framework-internal canonical readings, not outside witnesses.

No source conflicts with the figure verdict. The apparent conflict between a rising markup reading (De Loecker/Eeckhout) and a flat profit share (Hasenzagl/Perez) was returned to the census and re-mapped as a source problem in `01_figure_grounded_audit.md` F9; it did not flip a verdict.

## Distribution: offer, do not push

Figure-grounded reasons:

- **Fig 4 :22**, *"Only methods cross up. Only head starts come down."* Sending the full framework before anyone has described a residual sends a recording, not a generator. Lead with the shape claim and the refuter; offer the figure files after.
- **Fig 10 :42**, *"A verifier that can reconstruct the claim cannot verify it."* A reader who has absorbed the entire framework before being asked for a verdict has joined the frame being checked.
- **Fig 9 :27-28**, *"describe it before you go looking, or any frame you pick will seem to fit."* Ask what they would expect to see if the shape claim were true before giving them the figure text.
- **Fig 2 :31**, *"Independence, between two frames, is how little they draw on the same evidence."* Selecting recipients from the Compustat pool only buys more spelling of the same witness.
- **Fig 2 :35**, *"Alignment is triangulation, not negotiation. A mirror cannot correct you."* A list ranked by receptiveness is a mirror chain.
- **Fig 1 :27**, *"They are frame-internal. A frame cannot score itself with a quantity it also produces."* Do not advertise the number of people who agreed; there is no number to advertise.

**Recommended order in a reply:** (1) the shape claim and its four refuters; (2) ask what they would count as a failure; (3) record that verdict; (4) then the three local figure files, with the note that the figure text, not the summary, is the authority.

## Submission constraint

This is AI-assisted work. It is a private checking instrument, not a manuscript. The AOM AI policy quoted in the BUS pilot shows the general shape of the constraint; most economics journals similarly require disclosure of AI use and prohibit submitting AI-generated work as one's own. A human researcher must own, write and disclose anything submitted to AEA journals, SSRN, CEPR or any other venue in `03_public_contact_points.csv`. Nothing in this folder is sent by this agent.
