## QUESTION

Decide, in effect terms, what the Tether says about the question "How much does inflation depend on firms' market power and pricing strategies?" Option A: produce a percentage share of inflation attributed to firm pricing. Option B: refuse the requested magnitude and return the shape any such attribution must have. What each leads to: A reports a pooled reading as a cause, scores the frame with a quantity it also produces, and loses the per-slot signal that says where to look; B returns a union of four per-slot conditions with separate verdicts, a named `gap` for the number, and an instrument characterized well enough to be built. The field is firm pricing and inflation research.

## DOMAIN CENSUS

R by arity, symmetry and scale, then one line per domain giving actors, currency and anchor, partition or union, and order by dependency.

- **R (the residual).** The gap between what a firm-slot's pricing rule predicts and what the market returns, indexed by firm `s` and time `t`, then pooled into an index. Arity: at least four operands, belief, action, realized outcome and slot, ordered in time. Symmetry: not symmetric; "high markup" is a threshold comparison, not a symmetric join, and firm and market are not interchangeable. Scale: the single firm's pricing loop, the population of firms and the index that ranks what they produce, and the time channel over which a price response becomes an inflation rate. Fig 9 names a union compound, not one hard question.
- **Domain 1, the firm pricing loop.** Actors: managers, pricing routines, the firm's Γ, its buyers. Currency: price quotes, markup choices, margin protection. Anchor: the realized transaction price and marginal cost at the slot. This is one scale of the membrane in Fig 4.
- **Domain 2, the market habitat and the index.** Actors: firms, consumers, input suppliers, entrants, the monetary authority. Currency: survival, share, the price level. Anchor: realized market outcomes, the market as ground named in Fig 2 :29. The index is a pooled reading of that anchor, not the anchor itself.
- **Domain 3, the cost and pass-through structure.** Actors: marginal cost, overhead, input prices, expectations of future cost. Currency: cost changes, pass-through, the markup as a reading. Anchor: realized cost and price data; the source of a reading is not fixed by the reading.
- **Domain 4, the measurement channel.** Actors: price statisticians, markup estimators, accounts, index construction rules. Currency: contact with the ground. Anchor: the ground persists while the channel decays, Fig 10 :24.
- **Excluded candidate X1, "firms' greed".** No anchor independent of the measured price and margin, so not a domain yet. This is a structural exclusion, not a denial that firms seek margin.
- **Excluded candidate X2, "market power" as one scalar cause.** Measurable, but a reading of domains 1 and 3, already inside them.
- **Partition or union:** union of four conditions, cost reach, pricing response, cast change and measurement, with compounds at the overlaps. Their shared fraction is unassignable.
- **Order by dependency:** perception, vocabulary, objective, planning, learn and carry, Fig 3. The objective link, what "how much" would even count, is the one with no instrument here.

Strain test applied: applying Fig 13's six physical terms to market power only fit by renaming a markup a gradient, so Fig 13 governs only the honest stop. Calling the aggregate index "the ground" also strained, because an index is a pooled reading; re-census assigns the slot-level market to the ground and the index to the instrument. Both strains are resolved in `01` section 7.

## VERDICT (from the figures)

The requested magnitude is refused and a shape is returned. Inflation's dependence on firms' market power and pricing strategies is a union of four per-slot conditions, cost reach, pricing response, cast change and measurement, with verdicts kept separate and never averaged. The market at the slot is the anchor; a markup is a reading whose source is undetermined by the reading; its effect changes sign with the residual; the aggregate partly reflects a change of cast; the firm-to-index transform is lossy; and a variance share is a budget filter, not a witness. No verbatim figure line types or sizes the universal percentage, so that percentage is a `gap` of the unreached kind. Mode `specified` on the substantive claims, `general` on the structural shape, not `grounded`.

- **cost if wrong:** bounded and locatable. If the union verdict is wrong, a single-lever attribution would have been the cheaper object, and the cost is the research budget spent keeping four conditions separate. If the refusal of the number is wrong, the cost is declining to publish a usable share. Both are findable because the refuters are written in advance. The larger avoidable cost is spending the independence of outside readers by sending a recording instead of a generator, which Fig 2 :42 prices: "Buy independence in proportion to the cost of being wrong."
- **undoable:** yes. The union can be re-bundled if a single intervention restores all four conditions with no different residue, and the number can be supplied if a source-discriminating instrument at the firm-slot level pays for it. No primitive has to be withdrawn, and a superseding packet can replace this one.
- **reversed by:** a ground reading, not agreement. A firm-level design that discriminates a barrier from expected future cost at the slot, with readings stable under a change of cost classification, would close the source slot. A markup/pass-through effect with invariant sign across comparable inflationary and disinflationary shocks would break the valence claim. A documented period in which within-firm markup change accounts for the whole aggregate change despite entry and exit would break the cycle claim. A verbatim figure line found on re-census that types and sizes the attribution would break the gap verdict. Until one of these fires, the verdict stands as `candidate`.
