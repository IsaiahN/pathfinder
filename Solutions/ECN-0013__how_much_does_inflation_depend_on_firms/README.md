## What is being decided, in effect terms

Ask what would count as an answer. A number, a percentage share of inflation attributed to firm pricing, would be one answer. A shape, the form any such attribution must have before a number can be meaningful, is another. The residual is stated in effect terms in `01_figure_grounded_audit.md` section 0: it is the gap between what a firm-slot's pricing rule predicts and what the market's price index actually returns, indexed by firm and by time. "How much" asks for an aggregate over those slots. The figures say the aggregate is a pooled reading, not a witness (Fig 1 :19, :22; Fig 10 :25; Fig 9 :60). The verdict is therefore a refusal of the requested object plus a shape that replaces it. That is a `gap` verdict, not a silence.

## Boundary and honesty

Every file in this folder states that the derivation is a **simulation checked for internal consistency against the figures, not a ground reading**. From the essay *The Recursive Self-Improvement Trap*, opened 2026-11-23: *"LLMs can quickly simulate an approximate answer/output with the formula found in the architecture post. It will often be better than the generic outputs that you will usually get otherwise too. However, the truly testable output would need to be derived from the real architecture or working the formula yourself."* This folder cannot settle ECN-0013. It can only say what shape a settleable answer would have and what would reverse it.

Figure quotes are cited as `Fig N :line` against `FRAMEWORK/reference/03-FIGURES_TEXT.md`, opened and checked this session. Every quoted string in `01`, `07` and `09` was validated character by character by `Scripts/validate_solution.py`. World sources are secondary corroboration only; each was opened and is recorded with author, venue, year, accessed date and a quote in `10_source_ledger.csv`.

## What is deliberately not here

- No personal emails of named economists.
- No ranking of people by how likely they are to agree with this reading.
- No bulk-send plan.

Three figure-grounded reasons, recorded in `02_outreach_email.md` and `04_links_and_distribution.md`: a room selected for agreement is a mirror chain, not an anchor (Fig 7 :13, :36; Fig 2 :35); asking a reader to trust the framework before they have checked the residual spends the independence they contribute (Fig 10 :42, :52); and a verdict must be recorded before the theory is shown (Seat Map :163, :171 as quoted in the exemplar).

Legally: unsolicited bulk email selected for receptiveness breaches GDPR and CAN-SPAM style rules in many jurisdictions. Public opt-in channels are used instead.
