## 1. A shape is a constraint, not a filler

The figures README states the limit: the framework returns *"what a missing thing would have to look like (a shape, not a name)"*. Fig 9 :32: *"you get a kind of thing, never the answer itself."* Fig 13 :133-135: *"what it returns is a shape rather than a name"* and *"It cannot say which primitive is missing, because naming it would mean already holding it."*

From *The Library of Babel*, opened in full: *"you can cut your search short if the set of books returned is small and descriptive enough that it provides a general shape of the answer that satisfies your question."*

So a **shape** is the narrowed form of an answer you can state before you can name or size it. It constrains; it does not fill. For ECN-0013:

- `a percentage of inflation attributable to market power` is a **name and a magnitude**;
- `a union of four per-slot conditions with a source-discriminating instrument owed` is a **shape**.

The shape says what any admissible answer must look like: indexed per slot, separated by condition, sourced by an instrument that can comment on the source. It does not say 30 percent, or 50 percent, or any number.

## 2. How a shape is obtained

- *Library of Babel*: use the vocabulary you already have that is *closest* to the thing, look for recurring new terms that overlap or correlate with it, recurse. *"With each new term gained, you are able to narrow the space by definition and deepen your vocabulary."*
- Fig 6 :30: *"The description travels. Fails by being sharpened into terms only we can read. Cast it in effect terms."* Fig 9 :30-31: *"your words are borrowed too, so describe the effect rather than the cause."*
- *Decoding Tacit Knowledge*, opened in full: *"When contact isn't possible, the best you can send is a plain description of what you'd expect to see, because anyone can check that against the world."* It adds that a claim that can only be read in private shorthand *"can only be checked by the pair"*, which is why the residual is described in effect terms before any frame is named.
- *Library of Babel* on stopping: the search ends when *"the finish line will also be the starting place"*, and the paradox is that by then you have *"composed the answer through the process of locating it."*

Applied here: the effect terms are "the per-slot price residual", "a reading whose source is undetermined", "a sign that flips with the residual", "a cast of firms that changes". "Market power" and "greed" are borrowed names and are withheld until the residual is described.

## 3. What licenses a name or a number

Only two things, both outside the shape itself:

1. **The ground settles it.** Formula step 5: *"the ground settles it. Verified only by what pays. A gate is not the ground."* The README: the framework *"stops at the limit of what the available instruments can distinguish, and records the lineage so the stop is honest."*
2. **A provenanced import from a frame whose closure differs** (Fig 6 :23, Fig 8 :51-52), with a source gate and a shadow test.

*They're Scanning Books When They Should Be Studying Nature*, opened in full, sharpens the second condition: *"The real import is not a new term in the existing space. It is a new dimension of the space."* And: *"A minimum viable set of six terms is a human's careful guess at reality's compression axes; another sensorium's minimum set need not be those six."* Also: *"Declaring the primitives does not make them neutral. It makes the choice visible."*

For ECN-0013, a number would be licensed by a source-discriminating instrument at the firm-slot level, or by a provenanced import that explains the residual gap. Neither is held. The Kansas City Fed result (*"markups could account for more than half of 2021 inflation"*, immediately qualified by *"However, the timing and cross-industry patterns..."*) is the world-side demonstration that a number without a source discriminator is a reading, not a settled attribution.

## 4. Why the markup reading cannot settle its own source

*The Hard Problem is About Source*, opened in full, gives the mechanism. Metamerism: an instrument reads green and *"the instrument is speaking only about what it measured, it cannot comment on the source."* The source might be one green light, or yellow plus blue, or RGB, or CMYK, or a scheme not yet discovered. *"If several paths create the same behavior, then behavior is a projection with lossy information."* And: *"An instrument that can't distinguish these cases demonstrates its own inadequacy."* It was the **spectrometer**, not an argument, that resolved it: *"The answer is unreached until a better instrument exists."*

Here the instrument is the markup estimator (price over marginal cost). Its reading is compatible with a barrier to entry, overhead recovery, expected future cost, and a composition shift. The Richmond Fed brief gives the economic version: *"a high markup at a firm with heavy fixed costs may translate into little or no profit"*, and concludes *"the right question is not 'Are markups high?' but 'Are profits persistent, and what barrier protects them?'"*. That is the framework's source question, arrived at from inside economics.

*The Recursive Self-Improvement Trap*, opened in full: *"the output is only as good as the validator. The agent, or the tool, or the frame, or the theory, cannot validate itself."* A variance decomposition that explains itself with its own markup series is the named failure.

## 5. Q: What does "shape" mean for this question?

**It means the answer is a union of four conditions with an open source slot, not a percentage.** The four conditions are cost reach, pricing response, cast change and measurement. Each has its own per-slot residual and its own refuter. The source slot, what produced a given markup reading, stays marked `???` until a discriminator pays for it. This is bound as an ordinary term in `01_figure_grounded_audit.md` section 0, so the pre-registered refuter can fire on it.

**Does the record commit the error the README forbids?** Not by naming market power. Market power is an already-held reading from the economics frame, not a missing primitive. It is named as a binding, with provenance, and carries zero verdict weight until its source is discriminated. The error would be to convert the binding into the framework's answer, or into a magnitude. `01` and `07` refuse both.

## 5b. Correction to my own §3 and §4 (visible, not erased)

I initially overstated the "shape, not a name" rule, following the exemplar's own correction pattern. The README's phrase is *"what a **missing** thing would have to look like (a shape, not a name)."* The subject is a primitive not held in Γ. A markup estimator and a profit share are **already held** in the economics frame; naming them is a binding from that frame onto readings, not the invention of a new primitive. What remains wrong is converting the binding into a source or a magnitude without a discriminator. My §4 metamerism point is **not** retracted: the markup reading still cannot comment on its source, and the Richmond Fed's own conclusion confirms that. But the reason is the missing discriminator, not a ban on naming held readings. Both claims are recorded so neither is quietly lost.

A second correction: I initially wrote that the BIS pass-through asymmetry *establishes* Fig 11 :49's valence rule at the ground. It does not. It is one shock family (oil) in one set of sectors, and Fig 4 :20 requires the anchor to be re-named at a new scale. The claim stays `specified` and the world source is mechanism-shape corroboration only.

## 6. Two consequences for outreach

**Prestige is not action.** From *The Recursive Self-Improvement Trap*: *"Prestige must never be allowed to validate an action directly... Someone let prestige stand in for proof... Prestige can fund an action. It can give an action a budget to be tried. But it cannot settle an action. Only the ground can settle it. The Ground Maintainer must be blind to prestige."* A campaign that gathers prestigious economists to agree with the claim is the named collapse, not corroboration. The legitimate ask is the opposite: give the claim a budget to be tried, and let the ground, a source-discriminating instrument, settle it.

**My own audit is a simulation, not a ground reading.** Same essay: *"LLMs can quickly simulate an approximate answer/output with the formula found in the architecture post... However, the truly testable output would need to be derived from the real architecture or working the formula yourself."* And *Arrival of the Fittest*: LLM outputs using the formula are *"simulacra"* and *"could never be verified if the solutions presented were not contrived to fit the formula."* So `01` is a simulated derivation checked for internal consistency against the figures. It is **not** a test of ECN-0013 and must be labelled as such. It cannot settle anything it reports on.

## 7. One more limit, stated plainly

*Decoding Tacit Knowledge* warns that long, close contact pulls frames together and *"the process that makes communication easy slowly uses up that extra reach"*: two frames that overlap almost completely are *"one frame wearing two names"*. This solution's second seat in `11_audit.md` is written by the same model from the same figure text. Its agreement is internal consistency, not corroboration. A real outside frame with a different evidence pool is still needed, and that is what the expert packet is for.
