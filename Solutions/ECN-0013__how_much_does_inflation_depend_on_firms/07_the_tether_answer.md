# The Tether's answer on ECN-0013

**Mode: `specified` on the substantive claims, `general` on the structural shape. Not `grounded`.** This is a shape and a route, not a result. It is a simulated derivation checked against the figures (`05_what_shape_means.md` §6), not a ground reading.

## 1. The residual it answers

The question asks for a magnitude: how much of inflation depends on firms' market power and pricing strategies. Stated as a residual, it is the gap between what a firm-slot's pricing rule predicts and what the market actually returns, indexed by firm and by time, and then pooled into an index. The question conflates two objects: the per-slot residual and the aggregate reading. Fig 1 :19: *"The residual is indexed by object slot, not aggregated."* Fig 10 :25 names *"pooled readings"* as a way contact with the ground is lost. The union must be unbundled before a lever is chosen (Fig 9 :54-57).

## 2. The shape

1. **The answer is not a single share. It is a union of four conditions.** Cost and expectation reach the pricing rule (C1); the firm's position governs pass-through, shelter or amplification (C2); the cast of firms changes (C3); the channel pools the result into an index (C4). Fig 9 :55: *"A bundled problem is usually a union of overlapping conditions, not a list of separate ones."* Fig 9 :57: *"What remains is smaller and it is also categorically different: a case in two conditions is not a harder case of either."* Verdicts for the four conditions are kept separate and never averaged.
2. **R stays per firm-slot.** Fig 1 :22: *"Aggregating across slots is how a live signal disappears into an average."* The Richmond Fed's 2026 brief gives the world-side instance: *"A percentile marks a position rather than a firm, and the firm in the middle of the distribution in 2020 is not the firm that was there in 1960."* A pooled percentage cannot say which slot is failing.
3. **The market at the slot is the anchor; the markup is a reading, not a source.** Fig 2 :29: *"An anchor is whatever settles the question and does not change its answer because of what any frame thinks."* Fig 9 :32: *"you get a kind of thing, never the answer itself."* The markup reading is compatible with a barrier, overhead recovery, expected future cost, or a composition shift. The Kansas City Fed result (*"markups could account for more than half of 2021 inflation"*, immediately qualified by *"However, the timing and cross-industry patterns..."*) is the world-side demonstration that share and source came apart in one paragraph.
4. **The effect of market power changes sign with the residual.** Fig 11 :49: *"An actor's valence is relative to the residual, not a property it carries."* The BIS paper reports that *"high markups mainly reduce the pass-through of disinflationary oil shocks, while they barely affect that of inflationary oil shocks."* There is no standing coefficient "the effect of market power on inflation"; there is a relation between a firm's position and the sign of the shock it faces.
5. **The aggregate partly reflects a change of cast.** Fig 12 :121: *"cycle. The output re-enters as input. The sign of the loop decides whether it closes, and a chain cannot close by construction."* Hasenzagl and Perez (2026): *"The markups of firms in business throughout the period did not rise... The economy-wide markup rose instead because high-markup firms came to account for a growing share of sales."*
6. **A variance share is a filter, not a witness.** Fig 9 :60: *"Use filters for the budget and witnesses for the verdict. Never let a filter hand you a verdict."* Fig 1 :27: *"They are frame-internal. A frame cannot score itself with a quantity it also produces."* A markup series estimated from the same price data as the inflation series cannot independently certify the attribution.
7. **The firm-to-index transform is lossy.** Fig 4 :30: *"So take the round trip and measure the gap. Without it you are navigating by dead reckoning: confident about a position nobody checked."* The disagreement between the De Loecker/Eeckhout estimate and the Benkard/Miller/Yurukoglu correction is a round-trip gap `R_T`: *"how firm-level markups are combined into an economy-wide average and how costs are classified, which are measurement choices"* (Richmond Fed, 2026).
8. **The universal percentage is a `gap`.** No verbatim figure line types and sizes a per-slot attribution. Fig 13 :134: *"Unreachable is a verdict here, not a silence,"*. Fig 8 :39: *"So a search that finds nothing is never a proof of absence."* The instrument owed is one that discriminates sources of a markup reading at the firm-slot level. It is characterized here and not held.

## 3. What it explicitly does not say

- Not a percentage of inflation, and not a range of percentages.
- Not that "greed" or "market power" is the source of a markup reading. That is a name where a source is owed.
- Not that the price index is the ground. It is an instrument pointed at the market, and it is a pooled reading (Fig 10 :25).
- Not that markups did not rise, or that market power is benign. The claim is about what a reading can settle, not about the direction of the empirical series.
- Not a derivation of inflation from the six-term physical substrate. Fig 13 was sent to the honest-stop role after a strain (see `01` S1).
- Not `grounded`. Nothing here is asserted as settled by measurement.

## 4. What would settle it: the ground

A source-discriminating instrument, not a further argument. From *The Hard Problem is About Source*: an instrument that reads green *"cannot comment on the source"*; the spectrometer resolved it, and *"the answer is unreached until a better instrument exists."* Here:

- Does a given markup reading come from a barrier to entry, overhead recovery, an expectation of future cost, or a composition shift? A firm-level design that separates these, with readings stable under a change of cost classification, would close the source slot.
- **Sign Test:** does the markup/pass-through relation flip with the sign of the cost shock at comparable magnitude? If it does not, the valence claim fails.
- **Cast Test:** how much of an aggregate markup change is within-firm versus a change of cast, across a period with documented entry and exit?
- **Round-trip Test:** is the firm-to-index transform invertible for the quantities in question? If not, report `R_T` rather than a share.
- Standing falsifiers from `01` section 4: an aggregate share that predicts per-slot moves out of sample; an intervention that restores all four union conditions with no different residue; a figure line found on re-census that types and sizes the attribution.

## 5. The honest stop

**Unreached, not underivable.** The missing thing is a source-discriminating instrument at the firm-slot level; a shape for it can be stated, but it has not been built here. The market measures are *"a human's careful guess"* at the relevant axes in the same way any declared primitive set is a visible choice, not a neutral fact (*They're Scanning Books*, opened 2026-11-23: *"Declaring the primitives does not make them neutral. It makes the choice visible."*). The answer stops at the limit of the available instruments and records the lineage so the stop is checkable. *"Indiscernibility is not identity."* (figures README).

## 6. One-paragraph version for outreach

> The Tether does not say what percentage of inflation is caused by firms' market power. It says that the question asks for the wrong object. Inflation's dependence on firms is a union of four conditions: a cost or expectation shock reaching a pricing rule, the firm's position governing how much is passed on or sheltered, a changing cast of firms, and a measurement channel that pools the result. Verdicts for those four must be kept separate, never averaged, because the residual is per firm-slot and an average throws away the signal that says where to look. A markup is a reading that cannot comment on its own source: the same reading is consistent with a barrier, with overhead recovery, with expected future cost, and with a composition shift. Its effect changes sign with the residual, so there is no standing coefficient. What the framework offers is the shape any answer must have, the falsifiers written in advance, and the instrument that would close the source slot. The number stays open until a ground settles it.
