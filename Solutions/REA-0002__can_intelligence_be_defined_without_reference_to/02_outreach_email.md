# Outreach draft - check the residual before you decide about the definition

**Use:** one-to-one, or as a cover letter attached to a submission through the public venues in `03_public_contact_points.csv`. **Not** for bulk send.

**Hard precondition:** do not send until the split in `01_figure_grounded_audit.md` F1 to F4 is stated plainly and the refuter below is written down. The offer collapses on contact if the first message arrives as a new definition rather than as a checkable residual.

---

**Subject:** One residual about defining intelligence, before any theory

> Hi [name],
>
> I have one specific output I would like you to check against what you already know. I am not asking you to agree with a definition, and I am not asking you to read the theory first. If the output does not survive, there is nothing else to look at.
>
> **The residual (`specified`, not `grounded`).** The word "intelligence" is being used as if there are two options: define it with reference to human performance, or define it without. In the material I have read, that pair is not a partition. It bundles at least three different objects: a property of an agent, a performance score against a reference class, and a practice carried on by someone maintaining a habitat and instruments. Those have different anchors, so one definition cannot close all three by agreement. The figure I am using puts it bluntly: "The wrong question, and this figure is the replacement for it." The replacement asks what makes a system capable, where capability is a property of agent-and-habitat rather than of the agent alone.
>
> **What the reading records.** A definition is a convention, not a verdict. A human-performance reference is legitimate exactly where the question is constitutively about people, and is otherwise an instrument that can be wrong on axes it does not measure. Removing that reference does not remove anchoring: the definitions that drop it substitute environments, goals, reward or insufficient resources, and the record of where those came from is the only thing that distinguishes an independent arrival from an imported one. The residual has to stay indexed per slot; a single general score is where the live signal disappears.
>
> **What I am not claiming.** I am not offering a new definition of intelligence. I am not claiming the field has been wrong for a century, or that psychometrics, comparative cognition or AGI research have failed. I am not claiming this derivation settles anything. It is a simulated derivation checked for internal consistency against a fixed figure text, not a ground reading.
>
> **What would refute it, written before this message:**
> 1. a definition settled by a non-updating ground for arbitrary agents and habitats, returning per-slot residuals rather than an aggregate;
> 2. a demonstration that the capability claim governs only biological evolution and not artifacts or machines;
> 3. a source-discriminating instrument showing that human performance is not the operative anchor in the field's actual questions;
> 4. one settled definition with a non-updating anchor, which would collapse the union reading;
> 5. a demonstrably agent-only improvement that moves the ground.
>
> If any one holds, I withdraw the reading. A one-line "this fails because..." is more useful to me than agreement. If you would rather not engage, no reply is needed.
>
> **Before showing you anything else, one question:** if a definition is a convention rather than a verdict, what would you count as a failure of that claim in your own field? I will record your answer before sending any source.
>
> **If it survives, and only then:** the sources are below. The figures are the authority, not my summary of them.

> - Canonical usage rule (local): `FRAMEWORK/reference/FIGURES_README_canonical.md`

Write down what would refute your result *before* you run it, and state the mode of what you get. A reported failure is more useful to me than a reported success.

## Venue-specific cautions recorded in `04`

- Behavioral and Brain Sciences requires disclosure and description of any AI use that generated text or analysed material, and does not permit an AI tool to generate the arguments or substantial portions of a submitted text. This email therefore offers a check and a refuter; it does not submit this folder as findings.
- The Journal of Artificial General Intelligence asks that submissions be based on current research rather than personal opinion or speculation, and accepts position statements. Any submission must be rewritten by a human author with their own provenance.
- ISIR states that artificial intelligence is not currently a focus of the society. A definitions-of-intelligence argument aimed at ISIR should be framed in terms of human intelligence and cognitive abilities, with that scope limit declared.
