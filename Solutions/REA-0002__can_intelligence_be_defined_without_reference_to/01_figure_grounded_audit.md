## 0. The question under audit

> "Can intelligence be defined without reference to human performance?"

It arrives as a yes/no choice. The audit does not answer that choice. It first asks what kind of residual it is, because a false binary is a union that has been mistaken for a partition (Fig 9 :54). Three moves are owed before any answer: describe the residual in effect terms, census the domains with actors, currency and anchor, then quote the figure lines that govern each domain. A line that only fits with a strain governs another quantity, so the census is redone, not the line softened.

## 1. The residual in effect terms

**R = the gap between what the term "intelligence" promises to pick out and what any proposed definition actually pins down.**

Concretely, the definition of "intelligence" is asked to do three incompatible jobs at once:

1. **Name a property of an entity.** Most collected definitions do this: "Is a property that an individual agent has as it interacts with its environment or environments" (Legg and Hutter, *A Collection of Definitions of Intelligence*, 2007, §5, world source, not a figure line).
2. **Name a performance against a reference class.** The psychometric tradition defines the term through tests, grades and outcomes. Hiser and Francis (2000) record the failure directly: "The problem with any definition of intelligence relying on outcomes such as behaviour or performance is the danger of confusing intelligence with other constructs which are more clearly defined, such as attainment."
3. **Name a practice carried on by a maintainer.** Fig 11 :37: "Where a seat exists, something is doing work, and it is exactly what the seat does: enumerate the habitat, rank by cascade, introduce actors, build instruments, read the ground."

These are three different objects. A single definition that closes all three would have to use one anchor for three questions with different constitutive subjects, which Fig 2 :36 forbids in general.

### R by arity, symmetry, scale

- **Arity:** two in the question as asked (with reference, without reference), but at least three in the residual (property, performance, practice). Fig 9 :4-5: "Disagreements that do not shrink with effort, where each rule keeps working well on a different subset, are not one hard question but several well-formed ones. Split it rather than search." The long record of definition failure is exactly a disagreement that does not shrink with effort. Legg and Hutter open with "Despite a long history of research and debate, there is still no standard definition of intelligence." Hiser and Francis (2000) quote Spearman: "'intelligence' has become a mere vocal sound, a word with so many meanings that finally it has none." So the two options are a **union compound**, not a partition.
- **Symmetry:** "with reference" and "without reference" are not mirror options. One supplies an anchor whose legitimacy must be tested (Fig 2 :36-38); the other removes an anchor and owes an account of what settles the term. A missing anchor and a present anchor are categorically different, not opposite values of one variable. Operators table :51: "a goal is a comparison, which is why a vocabulary without it cannot state one" - a vocabulary without a comparison cannot state the second option's success condition.
- **Scale:** the definition operates at the level of a term in a library Γ. The capability it tries to name operates at the level of agent-and-habitat (Fig 11 :39), and the selection history operates at the level of a lineage (Fig 11 :36). Three scales, one word. Fig 4 :20: "Only the actors, the currency and the anchor change." This is a scale-crossing problem, so the same loop applies with renamed actors, currency and anchor.

**Partition or union:** union. Fig 9 :55: "A bundled problem is usually a union of overlapping conditions, not a list of separate ones." The conditions overlap because the same research communities use "intelligence" for the test score, the underlying capacity, and the engineering goal. Fig 9 :57: "a case in two conditions is not a harder case of either." Unbundle before searching.

**Dependency order (Fig 3).** Fig 3 :20: "The chain is ordered by dependency, not by importance." The order here is:

1. Decide whether a definition is a convention or a verdict (Fig 10 :46). This is link 3, the objective. It has no instrument in the definitional literature, so it is the middle link where chains break (Fig 3 :27: "A link with no instrument is not a link that works. It is a link nobody has looked at.").
2. Decide which question the anchor is legitimate for (Fig 2 :36-38). This is link 2, vocabulary.
3. Only then ask which definition, if any, closes the residual. Fig 3 :19: "Optimising a link that already works cannot rescue one that does not."

Refusing the wrong question comes before minting a new definition (THE_FORMULA step 2). Fig 5 :65: "make the sought-for shape an ORDINARY TERM" - the shape (a contact claim with a declared anchor) is written down here and allowed to accrue a residual, so the preference is refusable by the same test as everything else.

| domain | actors | currency | anchor | question type |

**D1 has no nameable anchor that settles a verdict.** That is not a failure of the census, it is the finding. Fig 10 :49: "Declaring a meaning does not ground it." Fig 10 :46: "A convention makes no claim about the world; a verdict and an atom both do. The seat may author what has no truth value and nothing that does." A definition is something a seat authors. Seat Map :163: "High correlation is disqualifying for a verdict and efficient for a convention." Agreement among definers is the product of a convention, not evidence for a verdict, so it cannot be promoted into one.

**D2 anchor: the ground.** Fig 11 :36: "Where nothing maintains anything, intelligence is a label applied afterwards to a lineage that met its ground. Nothing was intelligent; selection ran. The word does no work there." The anchor is survival, which does not update on what anyone calls it. Sternberg (2019) supplies a low-overlap corroboration from biological adaptation: cockroaches have been highly adaptive for roughly 300 million years yet would not be called intelligent in the usual sense, so survival and the label come apart.

**D3 anchor: the ground through instruments.** Fig 11 :39: "So the answerable question is not what intelligence is, but what makes a system capable. And capability is a property of agent-and-habitat, never of the agent alone." Fig 11 :40: "A system cannot be made more capable by improving the agent in isolation. An improvement that does not change contact changes nothing, however much it improves." The framework essay *How Intelligence Actually Works* (world source, high overlap with the figures) states the same object: "The intelligence we experience is the emergent result of extensive mapping of a complex system against unknown and unforgiving environments."

**D4 anchor: the ground, never agreement.** Fig 2 :35: "Alignment is triangulation, not negotiation. A mirror cannot correct you." Fig 2 :40: "agreement about the world is worthless when the agreeing frames share an evidence pool." In this simulated run every seat draws on the same essays, figures and papers, so D4's independence is zero and its agreement is one pool talking.

**Order by dependency:** D1's convention/verdict question comes first; settle D2 (where the label has no work) before D3 (where a practice is named); D4 checks all three and must not see their interiors (Fig 10 :42).

## 4. Findings

### F1 - The question is refused, not answered (decisive for the framing)

Fig 11 :34-35:

> **"What is intelligence?"**
> **"The wrong question, and this figure is the replacement for it."**

Fig 11 :39:

> **"So the answerable question is not what intelligence is, but what makes a system capable. And capability is a property of agent-and-habitat, never of the agent alone."**

Fig 11 :41: "Which is the same move as asking what generates lift rather than whether a thing is a bird. The substrate question dissolves; the contact question does not." The lift move is attributed to Cayley, 1799 (Fig 11 :42). A question that asks whether the word can be defined without a reference class is still asking the substrate question. The contact question, what makes a system capable, does not dissolve.

**Refuter:** show a definition of intelligence that is settled by a non-updating ground for arbitrary agents and habitats, and that returns per-slot residuals rather than an aggregate. If one exists, the replacement question is optional rather than decisive.

**Status:** `specified`, triangulated. Fig 11 :34-35/:39/:41 (habitat capability) and Fig 1 :23 "The ground enters as the only metric." (ground as metric) are low-overlap figure routes to the same instruction. World corroboration from Turing (1950), who refused the definition question in a different field: "Instead of attempting such a definition I shall replace the question by another, which is closely related to it and is expressed in relatively unambiguous words." Turing's replacement was the imitation game, which *does* reference human performance. That is the point of F3 below.

### F2 - A definition is a convention, and a convention that decides an outcome has stopped being one

Fig 10 :18: "a declared meaning is not a grounded one." Fig 10 :46: "A convention makes no claim about the world; a verdict and an atom both do. The seat may author what has no truth value and nothing that does." Fig 10 :47: "a convention that decides an outcome has stopped being one." Fig 10 :49: "Declaring a meaning does not ground it."

This settles the "can it be defined" half without answering yes or no. A definition can be *issued* (a convention, legitimate work for a seat) but it cannot be *settled* by agreement among definers, and it must not be used to decide an outcome such as who counts as intelligent, which systems get funding, or which test score is the ground. Doing so promotes the convention into a verdict, the named output-layer failure.

Pei Wang's distinction (JAGI 2019, world source) is a low-overlap corroboration from AI definition theory: "a dictionary definition is descriptive as it summarizes the existing usage of the term, while a working definition is prescriptive as it specifies a proposed usage of the term." A working definition guides research; it does not settle what the world contains. Sutton's JAGI 2020 note on McCarthy confirms the same practice: "Intelligence is the computational part of the ability to achieve goals in the world."

**Refuter:** produce a definition whose terms are settled by a measurement that does not depend on the definition itself, and show that no decision is taken on its authority. F2 then narrows rather than stands.

**Status:** `general`, triangulated across Fig 10 (seat rules) and the Seat Map's convention/verdict law (checking arrangement), with Wang 2019 as a corroborating witness from a different field.

### F3 - "Without reference to human performance" relocates the anchor, it does not remove anchoring

Fig 2 :29: "An anchor is whatever settles the question and does not change its answer because of what any frame thinks. A measurement, an experiment, a counter, a market, a patient outcome. If it moves when" (line continues) "you push on it, it is not an anchor." Fig 2 :36: "An anchor is legitimate when the question is constitutively about it." Fig 2 :37: "Human judgment is the right anchor for is this a good answer for a person, because the question is about people. It is only an instrument for is this code correct, and instruments can be wrong on axes they do not measure."

So the human-performance reference is neither banned nor mandatory in general. It is legitimate exactly where the question is constitutively about people. For a question about arbitrary systems it is an instrument with known blind axes. But removing it does not leave an anchorless definition. Legg and Hutter's widely cited definition, "Intelligence measures an agent's ability to achieve goals in a wide range of environments", replaces human performance with an ensemble of environments and a reward signal. Their own provenance is explicit: "We take a number of well known informal definitions of human intelligence that have been given by experts, and extract their essential features." The *A Collection of Definitions* paper lists both the psychometric and the AI definitions side by side, with Boring's "Intelligence is what is measured by intelligence tests" and McCarthy's "Intelligence is the computational part of the ability to achieve goals in the world" in the same document.

Fig 8 :50: "Two frames can hold the same atom by convergence, reaching it separately, which is corroboration at maximum independence." Fig 8 :51: "Convergent derivation and adopted import look identical in the contents. Only the record of where each came from separates them." So whether a human-free definition is an independent arrival or an imported human definition in new notation is decided by provenance, not by its wording.

**Refuter:** exhibit an anchor-free definition with a non-updating ground that does not smuggle in a reference class under another name, and show its derivation did not begin from definitions of human intelligence.

**Status:** `specified`, triangulated across Fig 2 (anchor legitimacy) and Fig 8 (provenance of imports), low overlap. World sources are one broad AI-definitions pool (Legg and Hutter, Wang, Sutton, Turing), counted as one witness, not four.

### F4 - The definitional disagreement is a union, so no single definition is owed

Fig 9 :4-5 (quoted in §1), Fig 9 :54: "And when the split lands on a union rather than a partition, unbundle before you search again." Fig 9 :57: "a case in two conditions is not a harder case of either."

The world record shows a disagreement that does not shrink: Legg and Hutter collected 70-odd definitions; Sternberg (2019) writes that "The concept of intelligence has been defined or conceptualized in a number of different ways, some of them consistent with each other but others of them mutually contradictory"; Hiser and Francis (2000) report "almost as many conceptualisations and definitions of intelligence as there are experts to write them." By Fig 9 :4-5 this is several well-formed questions, not one hard one. The union has at least the four domains in §2. One lever cannot clear the intersections.

**Refuter:** show that the competing definitions reduce to one question whose disagreement shrinks with a shared instrument. A single settled definition with a non-updating ground would fire this refuter.

**Status:** `specified`, single-source on the figure side (Fig 9 union law) with three world witnesses that partly share a psychometrics evidence pool. Marked `single-source` for the figure verdict; the world agreement is expected and carries little independent weight.

### F5 - The residual must stay indexed per slot; a single scalar hides it

Fig 1 :19: "The residual is indexed by object slot, not aggregated." Fig 1 :22: "Aggregating across slots is how a live signal disappears into an average." Fig 1 :27: "They are frame-internal. A frame cannot score itself with a quantity it also produces."

A general score "intelligence = X" aggregates across slots and lets a live residual disappear into the average. Sternberg's *g* critique is a low-overlap corroboration: "the fact that IQ tests yield a general factor and a hierarchical structure under g also does not tell us that the tests measure intelligence" and "to state that the tests measure intelligence because they correlate with specific individual outcomes may tell us more about what kinds of behavior our culture values than about what intelligence is." A correlation with a culturally valued outcome is a frame scoring itself with a quantity it also produces.

This is the live tension with the universal-intelligence programme, which returns a single real value Υ(π) for an agent. That value integrates over environments, which is an enumeration of habitat-like conditions, but it is still one scalar for the agent. Whether it preserves per-slot residuals is an instrument question, not an argument (see §5, strain S3).

**Refuter:** show that a single intelligence scalar preserves every live per-slot residual and cannot be saturated by a culturally valued outcome. Then F5 narrows to a caution rather than a constraint.

**Status:** `specified`, triangulated across Fig 1 (per-slot residual) and Fig 11 :48-51 (valence relative to the residual), low overlap.

### F6 - In the unmaintained domain the word does no work

Fig 11 :36 (quoted in §2), Fig 11 :56 (unmaintained mode): "Unmaintained. No maintainer and no live frame in the seat. The habitat is pure contact, nothing selects, and what survives is what the ground kept."

This is a verdict for D2 only, and it is kept separate from D3. Where nothing maintains anything, "intelligence" is applied afterwards to a lineage that met its ground. Asking for a definition there is asking for a name for a selection outcome. Fig 11 :42: "The lift move is Cayley's, 1799: separating the forces from the flapping is what made powered flight a problem anyone could work on." The comparison is structural: the substrate question dissolves, the contact question does not.

**Refuter:** show a lineage in which something maintains a live frame with no seat, so the label does descriptive work before selection settles it. Or show that selection outcomes carry a property that the ground does not settle. Then F6 is re-censused.

**Status:** `specified`, single-source on the figure side (Fig 11), with Sternberg's biological-adaptation discussion and the Science 2026 bumblebee definition as low-overlap corroboration that "adaptation" and "intelligence" are not the same predicate.

### F7 - In the maintained domain a definition of the agent alone is the wrong object

Fig 11 :37, :39, :40 (quoted above). Fig 11 :43: "The habitat is enumerated, never composed." Fig 11 :48: "You do not invent the list. You read it off the world, and what you cannot perceive or measure yet is the residual."

So a definition that lists internal properties of an agent enumerates the wrong habitat. The list must be read off everything in contact with the residual, then outward until the cascade stops mattering (Fig 11 :44). The Science 2026 animal-cognition source defines "Animal intelligence is defined as the ability to change behavior to adapt to new situations by using cognition rather than instinct or trial and error", which already names the environment and the change of contact, not an agent-only stock.

**Refuter:** an agent-only improvement that moves the ground. The metalaws note records this exactly: "an agent-only improvement that moved the ground would refute the capability claim." If one is demonstrated, F7 fails.

**Status:** `specified`, triangulated across Fig 11 (capability) and Fig 11 :52-54 (contact cascade and silent failures) plus the Symbols table's habitat definition. World corroboration from animal cognition is low overlap.

### F8 - A consensus of definers cannot corroborate a definition

Fig 7 :12-13: "same closure, handed along; agreement compounds, evidence does not." Fig 7 :35: "A room's agreement is variation, not a verdict. Selection between rooms is where the ground votes." Fig 2 :42: "Buy independence in proportion to the cost of being wrong." Seat Map :163 quoted in F2.

Applied to this question: the fact that 52 experts signed a mainstream definition (Gottfredson 1997, listed in Legg and Hutter) raises the vocabulary and does not add an anchor. It is efficient for a convention, disqualifying for a verdict. A reviewer pool selected for agreement with a preferred definition would manufacture exactly the correlation that makes its agreement worthless.

**Refuter:** show two definitional frames with genuinely low overlap (different evidence, different closures) that arrive at the same contact claim separately, with the provenance recorded. That would be corroboration at maximum independence, and F8 would narrow to a warning about pooled rooms rather than a ban on agreement.

**Status:** `general`, triangulated across Fig 7 and Fig 2, low overlap.

### F9 - No figure line returns a definition, so the stop is `unreached`, not silence

Fig 9 :32: "you get a kind of thing, never the answer itself." Fig 13 :134: "Unreachable is a verdict here, not a silence," Fig 8 :39: "So a search that finds nothing is never a proof of absence." THE_FORMULA step 7 separates SEARCHED, INWARD and OUTWARD, and says they are three questions, not one.

For this residual:

- **SEARCHED** (asked first, cheapest): the definitional literature has been searched for centuries and returned 70-odd incompatible definitions. More of the same search adds no atom (Fig 8 :32: "Closure is idempotent. Composing inside a closed set never leaves it, so no search, however long, adds an atom.").
- **INWARD**: extend the instrument from one already returning something. The instrument here is the per-slot residual: ask what fails to resolve when a definition is applied to a particular agent in a particular habitat. This is already returning readings, so it is a legitimate inward track (Fig 6 :32-34).
- **OUTWARD**: import a frame whose closure predicts the shape. Animal cognition, psychometrics and AI definition theory have all been tried, and each returns a different atom. Imports need a source gate and a shadow test (Fig 6 :12-13), and "Import from a live peer is debited against that frame's independence" (Fig 6 :24).

INWARD and OUTWARD are opposed and must never be read against each other (THE_FORMULA step 7). The honest report is that no line closes R: a shape is available, a name is not.

**Refuter:** a verbatim figure line that returns a definition of intelligence, or a ground reading that settles one. Until one is produced, F9 stands.

**Status:** `general` (structural), single-source for the shape-not-a-name instruction across Fig 9 :32 and the canonical figures README ("a shape, not a name"), which share an author and are counted as one witness.

## 5. Strain test and re-census

**S1: Fig 11 :36 versus the reality of the term.** Fig 11 says the word "does no work" where nothing maintains anything, yet researchers plainly use it for selection outcomes. Re-census: Fig 11 :36 governs D2 (unmaintained), Fig 11 :37-39 governs D3 (maintained practice). Different domains, no strain. Kept visible because the first reading over-extended :36 to all uses.

**S2: Fig 2 :36-38 versus Fig 11 :35.** Human judgment can be a legitimate anchor, yet the definition question is the wrong question. Re-census: Fig 2 governs *which anchor is legitimate for a given question*; Fig 11 governs *which question is answerable*. Different quantities. No strain.

**S3: Fig 1 :19/:22 versus a single universal intelligence scalar.** A scalar Υ(π) integrates over an ensemble of environments and returns one number. Fig 1 requires per-slot residuals. Re-census: the environment ensemble is a read-off habitat (Fig 11 :43-44), so the two figures govern different objects (ensemble construction versus residual indexing). Whether the scalar preserves live per-slot residuals is **live and unmeasured**, not dissolved. Falsifier recorded: a decomposition of Υ that retains per-slot residuals and a non-updating anchor, or a demonstration that the scalar saturates on a culturally valued outcome. This strain is named, not smoothed.

**S4: Fig 10 :46 (definition as convention) versus Wang (working definitions guide research).** No strain after re-census. Wang's prescriptive definition is a convention used to steer a research programme, which is exactly what a convention is for. The strain appears only if the definition is used to decide an outcome (Fig 10 :47).

**After re-census: one live strain (S3), three dissolved and preserved.**

## 6. Pre-registered refuter

Written before the conclusion, per Seat Map :287: "And a falsifier written before the run is a design instrument, not only an honesty device." The shape claim is refuted if any of the following fires:

1. A definition of intelligence is produced that is settled by a non-updating ground for arbitrary agents and habitats, and that returns per-slot residuals rather than an aggregate.
2. Fig 11 :39-40 is shown to govern only biological evolution and not artifacts or machines, so capability can be a property of the agent alone after all.
3. A source-discriminating instrument shows that human performance is not an anchor at all for the field's actual questions, that is, the field's operative anchor is something else that does not update on what frames think.
4. The field reaches one settled definition with a non-updating anchor, which would refute the union reading in F4.
5. A demonstrably agent-only improvement moves the ground, which would refute the capability claim in F7 (the metalaws note names this as the standing falsifier).

No part of this folder may be labelled `grounded` until one of these has been run and failed to fire.

## 7. Recommendation

1. Lead with the replacement question (F1), not with a new definition. A new definition would be a convention presented as a verdict (F2).
2. State explicitly that the human-performance reference is conditionally legitimate (F3), not simply forbidden. This is the honest answer to the literal question and the part most likely to survive contact with an expert.
3. Unbundle before searching (F4). Ask which of D1 to D4 a proposed definition is answering.
4. Keep residuals per slot and name the habitat (F5, F7). Do not average them into a general factor.
5. Do not treat a signed list, a consensus or a large reviewer pool as corroboration (F8). Fig 4 :22: "Only methods cross up. Only head starts come down." Send the generator, that is the residual, the anchor test and the refuter, not the conclusion.
6. Report the stop as `unreached` with the three routes named (F9).

The figure-grounded verdict is a shape plus a per-domain answer, not a definition. "Intelligence" cannot be settled as a property of an agent by either including or excluding a human-performance reference, because (i) the question is the wrong one (Fig 11 :34-35), (ii) a definition is a convention and not a verdict (Fig 10 :46, :49), (iii) the human reference is conditionally legitimate (Fig 2 :36-38) while removing it only relocates the anchor (Fig 8 provenance), and (iv) capability lives in agent-and-habitat (Fig 11 :39-40). Verdicts for D1 to D4 are kept separate and never averaged. Mode: `general` on the shape, `specified` on D2 and D3, not `grounded`. One live strain (S3) and one honest gap (no figure returns a definition) are recorded. A falsifier exists and was written before this conclusion.
