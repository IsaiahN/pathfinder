## 4. Distribution: offer, do not push

Figure-grounded reasons:

- **Fig 10 :52** - "Bring a characterised residual, never the source." Sending the full framework before anyone has described a residual sends a recording instead of a generator.
- **Fig 10 :42** - "A verifier that can reconstruct the claim cannot verify it." A recipient who has absorbed the architecture before giving a verdict has joined the frame being checked.
- **Fig 9 :27-28** - "describe it before you go looking, or any frame you pick will seem to fit." Ask what they would expect to see if the shape claim were true before giving them the links.
- **Fig 2 :35** - "Alignment is triangulation, not negotiation. A mirror cannot correct you." A list selected for agreement is a mirror.
- **Fig 7 :13** - "agreement compounds, evidence does not." A signed list of definers adds vocabulary, not an anchor.
- **Seat Map :163** - "High correlation is disqualifying for a verdict and efficient for a convention." Agreement about a shared meaning is the product of a convention, not corroboration of a verdict about the world.

**Recommended order in a reply:** (1) the residual and the split into three objects, (2) the question of what they would count as a failure, (3) record their verdict, (4) only then the three local links, with the note that the figures, not this summary, are the authority.

## 5. The two-stage offer

1. **Stage 1:** send the residual, the shape claim and the five pre-registered refuters. Ask what the recipient would count as a failure. Record the verdict before showing any theory.
2. **Stage 2, opt-in only:** if they are curious, offer the three local figure links. Seat 4 must not hold the interiors, so agreement after reading the framework is never pooled as corroboration (Fig 2 :40: "agreement about the world is worthless when the agreeing frames share an evidence pool").
3. **Stage 3:** offer the formula as a method to reuse. Fig 4 :22: "Only methods cross up. Only head starts come down." Each user must write their own ground and refuter and state their mode, because a frame cannot check the direction of its own question (THE_FORMULA, "Direction").

**Hard precondition:** stages 1 to 3 only work if the stage-1 output survives. The first output here is a refusal of the either/or and a per-domain shape, not a definition. If a recipient asks for the definition anyway, that is stage 1 failing, and the correct move is to restate the residual rather than supply the requested name (Fig 9 :32: "you get a kind of thing, never the answer itself").
