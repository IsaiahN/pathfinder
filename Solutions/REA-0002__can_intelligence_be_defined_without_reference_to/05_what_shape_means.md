## 1. A shape is a constraint, not a filler

The canonical limit: the framework returns "what a missing thing would have to look like (a shape, not a name)". Fig 9 :32: "you get a kind of thing, never the answer itself." Fig 13 :134: "Unreachable is a verdict here, not a silence," and the system "cannot say which primitive is missing, because naming it would mean already holding it."

From *The Library of Babel*, opened this session: "This means that you can cut your search short if the set of books returned is small and descriptive enough that it provides a general shape of the answer that satisfies your question."

So a **shape** is the narrowed form of an answer you can state before you can name it. It constrains; it does not fill. For this question the shape is:

> Any answer must be a **contact claim** about agent-and-habitat, indexed per slot, with a declared anchor that does not update on the definition itself, and a written failure condition. It must not be a convention promoted into a verdict.

That is a shape. "Intelligence = the computational part of the ability to achieve goals in the world" is a name.

## 2. How the shape is obtained

- *Library of Babel*: use the vocabulary you already have that is closest to the thing, look for recurring new terms that overlap or correlate with it, recurse. "With each new term gained, you narrow the space and deepen your vocabulary."
- *Library of Babel* on bundles, directly on point for F4 of the audit: "Perhaps your problem was a bundle or conflation of several problems masquerading as one?" The essay's instruction is to "search all of those problems individually to find their answers and then combine them in a satisfactory way." This is Fig 9 :54 in a different vocabulary.
- Fig 6 :30: "The description travels. Fails by being sharpened into terms only we can read. Cast it in effect terms." Fig 9 :30-31: "your words are borrowed too, so describe the effect rather than the cause."
- *Decoding Tacit Knowledge*, opened this session: "When contact isn't possible, the best you can send is a plain description of what you'd expect to see, because anyone can check that against the world."
- *Library of Babel* on stopping: "You will know that you are done because the finish line will also be the starting place, as there is nowhere else to search and nothing else that can describe the answer better." And the paradox: "By the time you learn the EXACT search terms to find the CORRECT book, you won't need to read it anymore, because you have already composed the answer through the process of locating it." That is why the refuter is written down before the run, not after it.

## 3. What licenses a name

Two things, both outside the shape itself:

1. **The ground settles it.** THE_FORMULA step 5: "the ground settles it. Verified only by what pays. A gate is not the ground." The README: the framework "stops at the limit of what the available instruments can distinguish, and records the lineage so the stop is honest."
2. **A provenanced import from a frame whose closure differs** (Fig 6 :23, Fig 8 :50-52), with a source gate and a shadow test.

In this question the candidate names are all available in the literature. Legg and Hutter's "Intelligence measures an agent's ability to achieve goals in a wide range of environments", Wang's "capacity of an information-processing system to adapt to its environment while operating with insufficient knowledge and resources", McCarthy's "the computational part of the ability to achieve goals in the world", and Turing's imitation game are not missing primitives. They are bindings assembled from atoms already held. What is owed is provenance for each binding and a ground that settles which one, if any, pays.

## 4. Why this question's requested output is the specific forbidden error

*Decoding Tacit Knowledge*, opened this session, gives the mechanism that bears directly on the "human performance" half: "Sounding right isn't the same as being checked against the world, which is why I call the Turing test a test of fluency, not of accuracy." The imitation game is the canonical example of defining intelligence by human performance. The essay's objection is not that it references humans; it is that fluency against a human reference class is not contact with a ground.

The same essay: "I've called the written record a lossy encoding of human thought. Training on it reproduces 'the traces the thought left, not the thought'". A definition assembled from a survey of prior definitions, including Legg and Hutter's own extraction of common features from definitions of human intelligence, is a reading of traces. The record of where the atoms came from is the only thing that separates an independent derivation from an adopted import (Fig 8 :51: "Convergent derivation and adopted import look identical in the contents. Only the record of where each came from separates them").

So the forbidden output is not "a definition that mentions humans". It is a definition presented as settled, with no anchor test and no provenance, whether or not it mentions humans.

## 5. Q1: Does "shape, not a name" forbid naming the conditions of the capability question?

**No, and this corrects my own first-pass reading.**

- The README's phrase is "what a **missing** thing would have to look like (a shape, not a name)". The subject is a primitive not held in Γ.
- The atoms here are not missing. They are enumerated off the world: habitat, actors, conditions, relations, instruments, per-slot residual (Fig 11 :43-48). Naming them is enumeration, not invention.
- Fig 11 :48: "You do not invent the list. You read it off the world, and what you cannot perceive or measure yet is the residual."
- So naming a habitat condition or an anchor is a **binding with provenance**, not the creation of a seventh primitive and not an import that moves the wall (Fig 6 :21: "MINT composes inside the closure and can never add an atom.").

**Kept visible:** I first wrote this section as if all naming were forbidden. That overstatement is retracted here rather than erased. The same correction appears in `06_isomers_and_order.md` Q1 and in `01_figure_grounded_audit.md` §8.

## 6. Q2: Why the answer cannot be a definition of an agent alone

*How Intelligence Actually Works*, opened this session: "The intelligence we experience is the emergent result of extensive mapping of a complex system against unknown and unforgiving environments." And: "The definition of what we call General Intelligence is that a system can answer questions to problems it's never seen before and in environments it has no training data for."

Fig 11 :39: "So the answerable question is not what intelligence is, but what makes a system capable. And capability is a property of agent-and-habitat, never of the agent alone." Fig 11 :40: "A system cannot be made more capable by improving the agent in isolation. An improvement that does not change contact changes nothing, however much it improves."

*Decoding Tacit Knowledge*, opened this session: "If intelligence is something each mind rebuilds through contact, then text alone can't hand it over." A definition is text. It can carry context and some subtext and almost no pretext. It cannot hand over the contact. So even a perfectly worded definition would not transfer the capability it names. This is a limit on what an answer to this question can be, not a reason to stop answering.

## 7. Prestige is not action

From *The Recursive Self-Improvement Trap*, opened this session: "Prestige can fund an action. It can give an action a budget to be tried. But it cannot settle an action. Only the ground can settle it. The Ground Maintainer must be blind to prestige."

The 52-signatory "mainstream science on intelligence" statement, the large reviewer pool, and the famous name on a definition all fund an action. They do not settle it. Fig 7 :13: "agreement compounds, evidence does not." A definitions-of-intelligence campaign that collects prestigious endorsements is the named collapse, not corroboration.

## 8. My own audit is a simulation, not a ground reading

Same essay: "LLMs can quickly simulate an approximate answer/output with the formula found in the architecture post." And: "However, the truly testable output would need to be derived from the real architecture or working the formula yourself." Also: "The output is only as good as the validator. The agent, or the tool, or the frame, or the theory, cannot validate itself. An external validator, or ground, is always needed to tether the veracity of the output to reality. Anything else is drift."

So `01`, `06` and this file are simulated derivations, checked for internal consistency against the figures. They are not tests of the question. They cannot settle anything they report on. This limitation is a finding, not a disclaimer appended at the end.

## 9. Summary answer to "what does shape mean here"

A shape here is not a candidate definition. It is the set of constraints any candidate must satisfy: agent-and-habitat rather than agent alone, per-slot rather than averaged, a declared and non-updating anchor, a convention not promoted to a verdict, provenance for every imported atom, and a refuter written before the run. The eight essays agree with the figures on this, but they share an author and an evidence pool with the figures, so their agreement is one witness, not eight.
