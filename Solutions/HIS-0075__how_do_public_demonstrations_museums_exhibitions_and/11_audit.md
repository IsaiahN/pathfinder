## Round trip gap R_T

The Symbols table defines R_T as `gap(x, (T_E ∘ T_A)(x))`, and Fig 4 :30 requires it to be measured rather than assumed. I ran the round trip on the condensation the folder actually uses.

1. **Sent up (x):** the verbatim figure set, chiefly Fig 1 :19, Fig 2 :37 and :40-41, Fig 4 :22-25, Fig 7 :12-13 and :36, Fig 10 :24-25, Fig 11 :40 and :52.
2. **Abstraction (T_A):** the shorthand carried in `07` and `09`: per-slot residuals, slot-specific grounds, two valences of agreement, recording versus method, channel decay, contact over polish, cascade, effect-size gap.
3. **Concretisation (T_E):** a concrete evaluator's claim, for example: "museum exhibitions raise public trust in science because visitors see real research."

**R_T is nonzero.** At least four things the shorthand holds do not survive the trip back down:

- the **bond type**: ⇒ (a new competence), → (a visit preceding a conversation), ⋛ (weighing a dramatised account against a real one) and ¬ (giving up a held belief) collapse into one undifferentiated "raise". The flat formula cannot tell them apart (Fig 12 :94, *"The arrangement is the substance"*).
- the **two-valence split**: an evaluator reading "agreement" in the shorthand will naturally treat a shared public vocabulary as evidence of understanding, which is exactly the Fig 2 :41 case where agreement is the product, not the verdict.
- the **gap**: the shorthand says effect sizes are unfilled. Concretisation tempts a number from a convenient exit questionnaire, which would be a name where the instruments returned only a projection.
- the **cascade**: a concretisation aimed at the visitor misses the second-order effects three steps out.

Conversely, one thing survives cleanly: a structure must be kept per slot, the slots have different grounds, and recordings are not methods. That is the residue worth sending. R_T was **not** measured numerically, because no ground reading exists here; it is recorded qualitatively as the enumerated set of dropped constraints. Treating "the formula is the condensed version of the metalaws" as identity would set R_T to zero and would be the dead-reckoning error Fig 4 :30 names.
