## The question restated as an effect

Two options, with what each leads to:

- **Option A: treat the four channels as one delivery system for one thing called "public understanding of science," and report a single score.** This leads to an aggregate that hides which belief slots are live (Fig 1 :19, :22), mistakes a shared convention for a verdict about the world (Fig 2 :40, :41), and counts frame-internal numbers such as coverage and credibility as if they were ground (Fig 1 :24-27).
- **Option B: treat the question as a union of transmission conditions acting on per-slot beliefs, separate the content anchor from the belief anchor, and state the shape any answer must have before measuring any effect.** This leads to checkable sub-claims, written refuters, and an honest stop where the figures run out. The shape survives; the effect sizes are owed.

**Residual in effect terms:** R is the gap, per person and per belief slot, between the belief a person holds about science and the belief that would be held under unimpeded contact with the relevant ground. It is not one number. It has at least three kinds of slot (factual knowledge, structural knowledge of how science works, and trust or identity), and it is measured across four overlapping transmission conditions (demonstration, museum, exhibition, popular writing).

**Direction written down before the run (Formula, "Direction"):** an answer would look like a per-channel, per-slot map with (i) which ground each slot must range against, (ii) which channels send a method versus a recording, (iii) which channels are independent evidence pools versus mirrors of one another, and (iv) refuters stated in effect terms before measurement. That description is bound here as an ordinary term so it can accrue a residual like anything else.

## What is deliberately not here

- No personal emails of named individuals.
- No ranking of people by how likely they are to agree with the shape claim.
- No bulk-send plan.

Three reasons in the figures' own words:

1. **Agreement is not the anchor.** Fig 2 :35: *"Alignment is triangulation, not negotiation. A mirror cannot correct you."* A list selected for likely agreement manufactures the correlation that would make its agreement worthless (Fig 2 :40).
2. **A room does not upgrade the label.** Fig 7 :35: *"A room's agreement is variation, not a verdict. Selection between rooms is where the ground votes."*
3. **The outside frame must not hold the interior.** Fig 10 :42: *"A verifier that can reconstruct the claim cannot verify it."* The first contact carries the residual and its refuter, never the source.

Legally: scraping personal emails for unsolicited bulk outreach breaches GDPR and CAN-SPAM style rules in many jurisdictions. Public opt-in venues avoid that failure as well as the structural one.
