## QUESTION

Decide, in effect terms, what the Tether says about the proposition that public demonstrations, museums, exhibitions and popular writing shape beliefs about science. Option A: report one "public understanding of science" score and treat the four channels as one delivery system. Option B: treat the question as a union of overlapping transmission conditions acting on per-slot beliefs, with slot-specific grounds, and return a shape with a declared effect-size gap. What each leads to: A hides the live signal, mistakes a convention for a verdict and promotes reach into evidence; B yields checkable sub-claims, written refuters and an honest stop.

## DOMAIN CENSUS

R by arity, symmetry and scale, then one line per domain with actors / currency / anchor, partition or union, and order by dependency.

- **R (the residual).** Per person and per belief slot, the gap between the belief held about science and the belief that would be held under unimpeded contact with the relevant ground. Arity: at least three slot kinds (factual, structural, trust or identity) and four overlapping transmission conditions, so a union compound. Symmetry: the transmission-to-belief relation is not a symmetric join; it is production, sequence, comparison or refusal depending on the slot. Scale: one frame contacting another (a demonstration); a room with a shared narrative (a museum or exhibition); a chain of rooms handed along (popular writing). Fig 9 :54 governs the unbundling.
- **Domain 1, scientific ground.** Actors: claims, instruments, methods, measurements. Currency: settlement by what pays. Anchor: yes, the measurement. Fig 2 :29 names it. Question type: settlement of factual and structural slots.
- **Domain 2, transmission frames.** Actors: demonstrators, curators, exhibit designers, writers, media. Currency: recordings versus methods, attention, coverage, credibility. Anchor: none of its own; it is a channel that decays and must range against D1 and D3, and its own quality numbers are frame-internal (Fig 1 :27). Question type: not yet a domain on its own; a frame that must stay in contact.
- **Domain 3, public belief frame.** Actors: audiences with vocabularies, histories and identities. Currency: agreement, trust, perceived knowledge. Anchor: yes, human judgment, because the question is constitutively about people (Fig 2 :37). Question type: union of a world-directed part and a meaning-directed part.
- **Domain 4, research and verification frame.** Actors: PUS researchers. Currency: residuals and independent evidence. Anchor: yes in principle, the measured record under a fixed protocol. In this simulated run all four domains draw on one evidence pool, so D4 independence is zero and its agreement is not corroboration.
- **Order by dependency.** Perception, then vocabulary and affordances (the middle link), then objective, then mechanism, then learn and carry (Fig 3 :20, :24). Across scales, methods cross up and head starts come down (Fig 4 :22). Across rooms, the next room is selected against the residual the last surfaced (Fig 7 :36).

Strain test: Fig 2 :40 and :41 appear to give opposite valences for agreement. Re-census shows they govern different slots in D3 (agreement about the world versus agreement about shared meaning), so this is evidence of a union, not a contradiction. Kept as a live, named strain. Fig 13's six-term substrate does not bear on belief composition and is recorded as out of scope, not forced.

## VERDICT (from the figures)

The shape survives; the effect sizes do not exist in this run. Beliefs about science are shaped per slot, on slot-specific grounds, by overlapping transmission conditions that mostly send recordings rather than methods, through channels that decay and compound agreement without adding evidence, inside habitats where contact and cascade matter more than polish. Two products must be kept apart: agreement about the world from a shared evidence pool, which is disqualified as a verdict, and agreement about shared meaning, which is the product. The four channels are not a partition and cannot be ranked from the figures; that is a `gap` owed to a discriminating instrument. The deficit model's failure is corroborated from a different frame, but the figure reason is structural: information is a recording, not a method. This is a `general` and `specified`, ungrounded, candidate result.

- **cost if wrong:** bounded and locatable. The packet states mode, per-slot grounds and four refuters before the conclusion, so an error is findable rather than invisible. The real cost is spending the independence of outside readers by sending a polished recording instead of a generator, which Fig 2 :42 prices: "Buy independence in proportion to the cost of being wrong." That cost is avoidable by leading with the shape and the refuter.
- **undoable:** yes. No primitive or channel ranking must be withdrawn, because none was asserted. The gap is declared, so a future instrument can fill it without overturning the derivation. A sent claim can be superseded by a corrected packet in which the two agreement valences are separated.
- **reversed by:** a ground reading, not agreement. A slot-discriminating instrument that shows perceived and factual knowledge are one construct; a method-transfer test showing recipients generate novel predictions from a recording; a hop test showing no decay across re-tellings; low-overlap channels converging on a factual belief without a shared pool; or an aggregate score predicting as well as per-slot residuals. Any of these moves the verdict. Prestige, reach and agreement are not such instruments.
