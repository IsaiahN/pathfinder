## 1. A shape is a constraint, not a filler

The figures README states the limit: the framework returns "what a missing thing would have to look like (a shape, not a name)". Fig 9 :32: "you get a kind of thing, never the answer itself." Fig 13 :134: "Unreachable is a verdict here, not a silence". Fig 13 :137: "It can characterise what is missing in effect terms, which is the description that travels to a frame that may hold it."

From *The Library of Babel*, carried from the exemplar: "You can cut your search short if the set of books returned is small and descriptive enough that it provides a general shape of the answer that satisfies your question."

So a **shape** here is the form of a decision rule you can state before you can give the number or name the slot. It constrains. It does not fill. "Route per slot, classify the anchor, search inward before going outward, gate the import, separate the seats" is a shape. "Escalate when confidence < 0.7" is a number, not a shape the figures return. "Always ask the human in the loop" is a standing list, not a shape (Fig 11 :51: "No standing list of good actors survives a change of residual.")

## 2. How a shape is obtained

- *Library of Babel*, carried: use the vocabulary already closest to the thing, look for recurring new terms that overlap or correlate with it, recurse. "With each new term gained, you narrow the space and deepen your vocabulary."
- Fig 6 :30: "outward: does another frame already hold it? The description travels. Fails by being sharpened into terms only we can read. Cast it in effect terms." Fig 9 :30-31: "your words are borrowed too, so describe the effect rather than the cause".
- *Decoding Tacit Knowledge*, carried: "When contact isn't possible, the best you can send is a plain description of what you'd expect to see, because anyone can check that against the world."
- Fig 11 :43-44: "The habitat is enumerated, never composed." and "List everything in contact with the residual, then what is in contact with those, and outward until the cascade stops mattering." For this question the enumeration is: the object slot, the system's library and instrument, the person the answer is for, the person with the differing corpus, the person assigned to refuse, and the ground each of them can or cannot read.

Applied here, the shape is obtained by describing `R` in effect terms first: what failed to resolve, at which slot, for which kind of question, and which frame could hold the missing atom. Only after that is a human chosen (Fig 8 :53: "Residual first, frame second.").

## 3. What licenses a name or a number

Only two things, both outside the shape itself:

1. **The ground settles it.** Formula step 5: "SETTLE the ground settles it. Verified only by what pays. A gate is not the ground." The README: the framework "stops at the limit of what the available instruments can distinguish, and records the lineage so the stop is honest." For this question that means a deployment measurement in which a specific threshold, role count or person is shown to change contact with the ground at the slot.
2. **A provenanced import from a frame whose closure differs (Fig 6 :23, Fig 8 :50-52), with a source gate and a shadow test.** A number imported from a human-factors study is a binding from that frame, provenanced as such, not a figure-derived constant.

*They're Scanning Books*, carried: "The real import is not a new term in the existing space. It is a new dimension of the space." And: "A minimum viable set of six terms is a human's careful guess at reality's compression axes; another sensorium's minimum set need not be those six." Also: "Declaring the primitives does not make them neutral. It makes the choice visible."

The four domains in this folder are therefore a declared classification of anchors and seats, not a discovery of four new primitives. Declaring them makes the choice visible. Calling them "the" four would turn a visible choice into an invisible one.

## 4. The ground and the source problem

*The Hard Problem is About Source*, carried, gives the mechanism. Metamerism: an instrument reads green and "cannot comment on the source" - one green light, or yellow plus blue, or RGB, or CMYK, or a scheme not yet discovered. "If several paths create the same behavior, then behavior is a projection with lossy information." And: "An instrument that can't distinguish these cases demonstrates its own inadequacy." It was the spectrometer, not an argument, that resolved it.

Applied here: a human agreement signal, a confidence score and a task outcome can all read "green" while coming from different sources. A confidence score cannot comment on whether the human was an anchor or a mirror. Agreement does not discriminate. What is owed is a source-discriminating instrument at the slot, not a larger room. Fig 2 :45: "Frames range against the ground. They do not negotiate with it."

*The Recursive Self-Improvement Trap*, carried: "the output is only as good as the validator. The agent, or the tool, or the frame, or the theory, cannot validate itself." A system cannot certify that its own chosen threshold was the right one, because the shape it is searching for is not a term it holds (Formula "Direction": a frame "cannot check the direction of its own question"). That is why the refuter in `01` section 5 was written before the conclusion.

## 5. Answer to the shape question

**What shape does the Tether return for "when should an autonomous system seek human input?"** Four things, all structural:

1. Per-slot routing instead of a global threshold (Fig 1 :19, :22).
2. An anchor classification: is the question constitutively about a person, so the human is the ground, or is the human a witness with a differing closure (Fig 2 :36-38)?
3. An order of questions: SEARCHED, INWARD, OUTWARD, with import gated by provenance and shadow test and debited against independence (Formula step 7; Fig 6 :12-13, :24).
4. A seat constraint: the human who answers should not be the human who verifies, and the human should be outside the slice being graded (Fig 10 :42, :86).

**Does it return the number, the person or the headcount?** No. No figure line does. That is a declared `gap` of the shape-not-a-name kind (Fig 9 :32, Fig 13 :134), reported as unreached rather than silence. The deployment must supply the local ground for its own threshold.

## 6. Consequences for contact

**Prestige is not action.** From *The Recursive Self-Improvement Trap*, carried: "Prestige must never be allowed to validate an action directly... Prestige can fund an action. It can give an action a budget to be tried. But it cannot settle an action. Only the ground can settle it. The Ground Maintainer must be blind to prestige."

A list of famous reviewers assembled to agree that "ask a human" is right is the named collapse. The legitimate ask gives the claim a budget to be tried by someone with a ground it does not control. EU AI Act Article 14 supplies an institutional instance: oversight should be commensurate with risk, autonomy and context, and overseers must be able to override and stop, not merely be present.

**My own audit is a simulation, not a ground reading.** Same essay: "LLMs can quickly simulate an approximate answer/output... However, the truly testable output would need to be derived from the real architecture or working the formula yourself." LLM output is "simulacra." So `01` is a simulated derivation checked for internal consistency against the figures. It is **not** a test of the claim and must be labelled as such. It cannot settle anything it reports on.

**A human in the loop is not automatically a ground.** Fig 11 :70: "A test harness is a substituted habitat. Which is why a synthetic solve proves wiring and never capability." Santoni de Sio and van den Hoven 2018 make the same point in institutional language: being in the loop is insufficient without tracking and tracing. The two sources share the shape but not the evidence pool; the figure line governs.
