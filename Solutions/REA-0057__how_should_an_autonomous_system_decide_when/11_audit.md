## 1. Independent domain census

I redid the census from the residual line alone before comparing with the primary's conclusions, using the WATSON order: R by arity, symmetry and scale; one line per domain with actors, currency and anchor; partition or union; order by dependency.

- **R (arity / symmetry / scale).** R is the gap between a prediction at a slot and what arrives. Arity: four, because a decision can be inside the closure and unfound, can need a sharper instrument, can be constitutively about a person, or can need an atom the frame lacks. This is a union, not a two-way partition (Fig 9 :4-5, :54). Symmetry: the system-human relation is asymmetric; the human can be a non-updating anchor, a peer frame, or a seat with refusal power. Scale: first order at the slot, second order at the import membrane (Formula step 6).
- **Domain 1, own closure.** Actors: slots, Γ, atoms, bonds, operators, negation, `R⁺_s`. Currency: composition, then settlement in bits and actions. Anchor: task ground. Question type: partition boundary of the closure. Order: SEARCHED first because it is cheapest.
- **Domain 2, human as ground.** Actors: human judge, output, the person the answer is for. Currency: verdicts about a person. Anchor: human judgment, legitimate because the question is constitutively about people. Question type: distinct union member. Order: before any import question.
- **Domain 3, human as peer frame.** Actors: the human's corpus, experience and ground contact, described in effect terms. Currency: imported primitive with provenance. Anchor: the task ground still decides. Question type: union overlapping Domains 1 and 4. Order: after SEARCHED and INWARD.
- **Domain 4, verification seat.** Actors: overseer who may refuse, override or stop, not the author. Currency: conventions, not verdicts. Anchor: ground, never agreement. Question type: union overlapping Domains 2 and 3. Order: constraints checked when the human is chosen.

Order by dependency: classify the anchor, then SEARCHED, then INWARD and OUTWARD as separate tracks, then separate importer and verifier, then test contact at the live slot.

My census matches the primary's four domains and the union reading. ## 4. Round trip gap R_T

The Symbols table defines R_T as the gap between what was sent up and what came back down, `gap(x, (T_E ∘ T_A)(x))`, measuring what the coarser description cannot hold. Fig 4 :30 requires it to be measured rather than assumed. I ran the round trip on the condensation this folder actually uses.

1. **Sent up (x):** the verbatim figure set, chiefly Fig 1 :19/:22 (per-slot residual), Fig 2 :36-38 (anchor versus instrument), Formula step 7 and Fig 6 :12-13/:24 (order and import gates), Fig 10 :42/:86 (seat non-access), Fig 11 :40/:70 (contact).
2. **Abstraction (T_A):** the shorthand carried in `07` and `09`: "route per slot, classify the anchor, search inward before going outward, gate the import, separate the seats, no universal number."
3. **Concretisation (T_E):** a deployment rule, for example "escalate to a named reviewer when the system's confidence is below t and the slot is flagged human."

**R_T is nonzero.** Four things the shorthand holds do not survive the trip back down:

- the **per-slot indexing** collapses when the concrete rule uses one global confidence value (Fig 1 :19, :22);
- the **anchor versus witness distinction** collapses when the same named reviewer is used for "is this good for a person" and "is this code correct" (Fig 2 :36-38);
- the **source gate and shadow test** disappear when the reviewer's answer is adopted without recording its provenance or checking that it explains the described residual (Fig 6 :12-13, Fig 8 :50-52);
- the **non-access and contact conditions** disappear when the person who supplied the answer also signs off on it, or when the reviewer is a sandbox stand-in (Fig 10 :42, Fig 11 :70).

One thing survives cleanly: the order of questions, search the closure before supposing an atom is absent, keep INWARD and OUTWARD as separate tracks. That is the residue worth sending. R_T was **not** measured numerically, because no ground reading exists; it is recorded qualitatively as the enumerated set of dropped constraints. Treating "the formula is the condensed version of the figures" as identity would set R_T to zero and would be the dead-reckoning error Fig 4 :30 names. The honest form is a lossy condensation with a recorded gap, which is what `09_watson_answer.md` states.
