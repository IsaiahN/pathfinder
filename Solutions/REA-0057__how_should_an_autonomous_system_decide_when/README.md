# Solution REA-0057

**Question:** How should an autonomous system decide when to seek human input rather than act independently?

**Source:** reasoning.txt line 70

**Section:** 6. Agency, goals, and autonomy

**Mode:** `specified` on the per-domain boundary and decision rule, `general` on the structural shape, not `grounded`. Nothing here is a measurement. The whole folder is a simulated derivation checked for internal consistency against the figures, not a ground reading, so it cannot settle the question it reports on.

**Status:** done

**Registry status:** pending

**Boundary.** Nothing in this folder was written outside it. The figures were read from `FRAMEWORK/reference/03-FIGURES_TEXT.md`, `FIGURES_README_canonical.md`, `60_FIGURES_AS_METALAWS.md`, `FRAMEWORK/figures/THE_FORMULA.md` and the local Seat Map copy. World pages were opened on 2026-11-23 and are recorded with author, venue, year, accessed date, a quote and an overlap note in `10_source_ledger.csv`. No personal email is in this folder. No bulk-send plan exists here.

**Question as an effect.** A decision event has two options: act from the system's own library, or import a verdict from a human frame. Acting independently keeps the wall where it is and spends actions; seeking human input can move the wall and spends independence. The residual is the gap between what the system predicts at a slot and what arrives, and the decision is about *where the answer lives*, not about a confidence number.

## What is deliberately not here

- No personal emails of named individuals. The NIST and SIGCHI entries are institutional channels or public forms, not private addresses of people.
- No ranking of people by likelihood of agreement. "High correlation is disqualifying for a verdict and efficient for a convention" (Seat Map :163; the same rule is Fig 2 :39-41). Selecting recipients for receptiveness manufactures the correlation that makes their agreement worthless.
- No bulk-send plan. One to one only.
- No universal confidence threshold. The figures return a decision procedure and a shape, not a number. That absence is recorded as a `gap`, not silently filled.
