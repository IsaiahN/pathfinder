## What the wider literature bears on the question

1. **The thermal spectrum is a lossy projection, not a source report.** Page 1993 says the information may be "so spread out" that perturbative analysis never sees it; the overview says the radiation is fixed by mass, charge and spin while many initial states share those values. This is the metamerism case of *The Hard Problem is About Source*: an instrument reading one value cannot comment on which source produced it. It corroborates F1 without supplying a figure line.
2. **The correction is an import from a frame whose closure differs.** Replica wormholes fix the entropy discrepancy by "including new saddles in the gravitational path integral"; Maldacena supplies the holographic duality. These are exactly the imports Fig 6 :23 permits, and they need the provenance and shadow test of Fig 8 :50-52.
3. **The paradox is a union of three conditions, not a choice between two.** AMPS 2012 states that three propositions cannot all hold. Fig 9 :55 predicts this shape for a bundled question.
4. **Consensus changed; the ground did not.** The overview records a famous bet, a concession and a widely held present belief. Fig 7 :35 classifies that as variation in a room, not a verdict.
5. **Logical irreversibility has a physical cost.** Landauer's principle is real and measured, but no figure line states the bound. F9 records this honestly as world-only corroboration.

Overlap declaration: the Wikipedia overviews, the arXiv abstracts and the framework essays are different hosts but they discuss one closely coupled research program; the two Wikipedia articles share an editorial pool and the three essays share an author with the figures. None of these is an independent witness for the framework's verdict. The genuinely lower-overlap source is Landauer's principle (computation and thermodynamics). No world source conflicts with the figure verdict, so the conflict rule was not triggered.

1. **Stage 1:** send the residual in effect terms, the shape claim and the pre-registered refuter. Ask for a refutation. No theory is shown. This is Fig 10 :52, "Bring a characterised residual, never the source."
2. **Stage 2, opt-in only:** if the reader is curious after recording a verdict, offer the verbatim figure text and two physics sources. Their later agreement is never pooled as corroboration (Fig 2 :40).
