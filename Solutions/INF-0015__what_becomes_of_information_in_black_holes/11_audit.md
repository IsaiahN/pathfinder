## 1. Independent domain census

I redid the census before reading the primary's conclusions, using the WATSON order: R by arity, symmetry and scale; one line per domain with actors / currency / anchor; partition or union; order by dependency.

- **R (the residual).** The mismatch between the semiclassical evaporation calculation (radiation fixed by mass, charge and angular momentum, so many distinct pure collapse states map to one coarse mixed final state) and the quantum requirement that a pure state remain pure. Arity three: the semiclassical prediction, the unitarity requirement, and the absence of any ground reading of a complete evaporation. Symmetry: the semiclassical map is many-to-one and non-invertible, the quantum requirement is invertible; crossing between the two frames is an import, which is ordered, not a symmetric bond (Fig 6 :23). Scale: first order inside each frame, second order at their union. The Page turnover is a threshold comparison of two quantities, not a symmetric join, so the union reading follows from the form of R before any physics is named (Fig 9 :55).
- **Domain 1, the quantum frame.** Actors: wavefunctions, density matrices, entanglement, unitaries. Currency: purity, von Neumann entropy, distinguishability of initial states. Anchor: laboratory quantum mechanics, settled. It does not by itself carry the gravitational case.
- **Domain 2, the semiclassical gravity frame.** Actors: horizon, Hawking quanta, mass / charge / spin, horizon area. Currency: temperature, Bekenstein-Hawking entropy, evaporation time. Anchor: the semiclassical calculation and astrophysical observation. Settled in its own regime; it does not carry the fine-grained distinction by construction.
- **Domain 3, the reconciliation frame.** Actors: holography, islands, replica wormholes, fuzzballs, soft hair, remnants. Currency: fine-grained entropy of the radiation and the island rule. Anchor: not reachable at this budget; only internal consistency and the Page-curve shape are available. This is an import domain whose closure differs from both domains 1 and 2 (Fig 6 :23).
- **Domain 4, the verification / outreach frame.** Actors: expert readers. Currency: agreement and residual. Anchor: the ground, never agreement. In this simulated run all four domains draw on one evidence pool, so independence is zero and any agreement is one pool talking (Fig 2 :40).

Order by dependency: describe R in effect terms before naming a frame (Fig 9 :27-28); settle domains 1 and 2 each against its own anchor; refuse before mint; bind the sought-for shape as an ordinary term before choosing a mechanism (Fig 5 :65); only then import a candidate with a source gate and a shadow test (Fig 8 :50-52); do not read inward and outward instruments against each other. A link with no instrument is not a link that works (Fig 3 :27), and the missing ground-reading instrument is that link here.

My census matches the primary's four domains and its union reading. Where the primary derived the union from Fig 9 and Fig 3, I derived it from the arity of R and the ordered nature of the import (Fig 6 :23). Different starting figures, same domain boundaries: low overlap between routes within one framework, so the agreement is weak but not circular. It is still one evidence pool.
