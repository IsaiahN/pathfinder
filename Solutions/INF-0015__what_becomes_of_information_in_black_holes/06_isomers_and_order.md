## DERIVATION

### Q1 - Are the two proposals isomers over a fixed atom set?

Fig 12 :117: "Same atoms, different bond, different substance. The difference is invisible in the formula and decisive in the behaviour."
Fig 12 :92: "Symmetry is what decides whether an arrangement is a new thing."

Write both proposals over the atom set {collapse state, horizon, early quantum, late quantum, remaining hole}:

- **Loss:** the collapse state composes to the horizon; the horizon emits early and late quanta as *thermal and uncorrelated with each other*. The bond between early and late is absent, or is the symmetric bond with no operative correlation. The final radiation is a mixed state; the map is many-to-one.
- **Preservation:** the same atoms, but the late quantum is bonded to the early radiation by a typed relation that carries the distinction. The coarse ingredient list is identical; the fine-grained entropy follows the Page curve. The map is one-to-one.

Under Fig 12 :117 these are **isomers**: same ingredients, different bond, different substance, and the difference is invisible in the coarse formula (the thermal spectrum) but decisive in behaviour (the fine-grained entropy). This is the structural analogue of the exemplar's GR/QM isomer claim, but it is not the same claim: here the shared set is the evaporation atoms, not the six substrate terms.

**Falsifier, written before the run:** if either proposal requires an atom the other does not (a stable remnant adds a new object; a firewall adds a new high-energy structure at the horizon; genuine loss removes the unitarity constraint rather than rearranging atoms), the isomer claim fails. The relation is then composition plus import or refusal, and the answer must be restated as a union with different members rather than two isomers. This is a live condition, not a formality: the remnant and firewall proposals do add physical structure.

### Q2 - Functional group, and where the difference sits

Fig 12 :123: "functional group. A substructure whose presence fixes the outcome. The group says what kind; the qualifier says which member."

Both proposals share the functional group: collapse to a horizon, emit radiation, evaporate. The group says what kind of process is under discussion; the qualifier (which bond holds between early and late quanta) says which member of the group. So the disagreement is not about the gross process at all. It is about a single typed relation, which is exactly the half Fig 12 :5 says goes unrecorded when only ingredients are listed.

### Q3 - The Page turnover is a comparison, not a symmetric join

Fig 12 :87: "a threshold relation between two quantities"
Fig 12 :89: "is it about which is larger, rather than both being present? Yes."
Operators table :52: "a goal is a comparison, which is why a vocabulary without it cannot state one"

The Page time is the point at which the entropy of the radiation begins to exceed, then fall below, the entropy of the remaining black hole. Structurally that is a threshold comparison ⋛ of two quantities, with the operands ordered by the comparison, not a symmetric conjunction. The opened overview records the turnover at about half the black-hole lifetime and notes that many researchers treat deriving the Page curve as synonymous with solving the puzzle.

**What is claimed:** that the *form* of the turnover matches the figure's comparison operator. **What is not claimed:** that the figures derive the Page curve or the time at which it turns. That is physics, not figure derivation. Mode `specified` on the form, `single-source` for the figure definition, and unmeasured on the physics.

### Q4 - Dependency order, and why the mechanism must come last

Fig 12 :104: "A settled molecule becomes an atom for whatever composes over it. So irreducible is a fact about a level and never about the world."
Fig 3 :19: "Optimising a link that already works cannot rescue one that does not."

Order of the decomposition:

1. Settle the quantum frame's unitarity requirement at its own anchor (Fig 2 :29: "An anchor is whatever settles the question and does not change its answer because of what any frame thinks.").
2. Settle the semiclassical calculation in its own regime; do not demand that it carry the fine-grained distinction it was never built to represent.
3. Describe the residual per mode, not as a hole-wide entropy (Fig 1 :19).
4. Bind the sought-for shape as an ordinary term before choosing a mechanism (Fig 5 :65; Fig 9 :27-28).
5. Only then import a candidate mechanism, with provenance and a shadow test.

A mechanism proposed at step 1 is an import against a budget limit: it adds an atom that is not yet owed. The firewall debate in the opened sources is a case in point: three conditions each valid in its own frame were forced into one room, and the disagreement persisted because no anchor could be reached.

### Q5 - Cross-link to the substrate question

Fig 13 :75: "Two states never bond directly. It is not space-time but space, a structure, time; and two structures join only through a state."

This line does **not** govern the information question; the re-census in `01_figure_grounded_audit.md` section 1 assigns it to the composition of space and time, and the completed exemplar `QMGR-0037__spacetime_continuum_or_emergent` handles that claim. It is noted here only because any full quantum-gravity account of evaporation must also say what structure mediates space and time, and that is a different loop with a different residual. Carrying it over as a verdict here would be the strain the re-census refused.

## CONCLUSION

Loss and preservation are **isomers over the evaporation atom set** provided neither proposal adds an atom the other needs; the shared functional group is collapse to radiation, and the decisive difference is one typed bond between early and late quanta, invisible in the coarse thermal formula. The Page turnover has the **form of a threshold comparison** under Fig 12, but the figures do not derive its curve or its time. The falsifier on the fixed atom set is live because remnant and firewall proposals add structure. The order is settled frames first, residual second, shape third, import last. Nothing here is `grounded`.
