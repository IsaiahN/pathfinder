## The question restated as an effect

What is being decided is not the fate of a number called "information" but the invertibility of a map, slot by slot. Let the semiclassical frame (general relativity plus quantum field theory in curved spacetime) predict a radiation state Gamma(b,a) and let the quantum frame require an observation o' in which a pure initial state remains pure. The residual is R = |Gamma(b,a) - o'| measured per radiation mode, not as a single entropy for the hole (Fig 1 :19, Fig 1 :22, Symbols table :38-40).

- Option A, "the map is many-to-one and information is destroyed": leads to a non-unitary evolution, a modification of quantum mechanics, and a final radiation state that cannot distinguish distinct collapse histories.
- Option B, "the map is one-to-one and information is preserved": leads to a unitary evolution and a demand for a mechanism that carries the distinction out through the horizon.
- What an answer would look like, written down and bound as an ordinary term before the run (Formula, "Direction"; Fig 5 :65): a shape that (i) keeps the fine-grained map invertible per slot, (ii) names the kind of bond that carries the distinction across the horizon, (iii) identifies an instrument that would discriminate among the candidate mechanisms, and (iv) records the provenance of whatever is imported from a frame whose closure differs. Until that shape is bound it is a preference, predicts nothing, and no check inside the loop can see it.

The verdict recorded in `09_watson_answer.md` is that the question is a union compound, that the shape of any answer is fixed by the figures, and that the identity of the mechanism is a declared `gap` (unreachable at this budget), with verdicts kept per domain and never averaged.

## What is deliberately not here

- No personal emails of named individuals.
- No ranking of people by how likely they are to agree.
- No bulk-send plan.
- No claim that this derivation is `grounded`, no claim that it settles the black hole information question, and no claim that the framework is the only true account of quantum gravity.

Four reasons, three from the figures and one practical:

1. A room is not an anchor. Fig 7 :35: "A room's agreement is variation, not a verdict. Selection between rooms is where the ground votes." Fig 2 :35: "Alignment is triangulation, not negotiation. A mirror cannot correct you." A list selected for receptiveness manufactures the correlation that disqualifies a verdict.
2. Agreement from one pool is one witness. Fig 2 :40: "agreement about the world is worthless when the agreeing frames share an evidence pool." The physicists who report that information is preserved mostly read the same papers and the same reviews.
3. A verifier must be outside the interior. Fig 10 :42: "A verifier that can reconstruct the claim cannot verify it." The first contact therefore carries the residual and its refuter, not the framework.
4. Legally, unsolicited bulk email to scraped addresses breaches GDPR and CAN-SPAM style rules in many jurisdictions and gets the sending domain blacklisted. Public opt-in routes avoid that failure as well as the structural one.
