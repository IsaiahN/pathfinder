# Expert verification packet - the Tether on black hole information

**What this is:** a claim in a form a physicist can check. Mode, derivation and refuters are stated before the conclusion, so a reader knows what to look for and what would settle it.

**Mode: `specified` on the per-domain claims, `general` on the structural shape, `gap` on the mechanism, not `grounded`.** No measurement of complete evaporation is claimed. This is a shape and a route, not a result.

**Status:** a simulated derivation checked for internal consistency against the figures, not a ground reading. It cannot settle the question it reports on.

## 1. The claim, in one paragraph

The preservation question is a union of three overlapping conditions, not a two-way choice: a semiclassical calculation that records only coarse ingredients, a unitarity requirement settled in the laboratory, and the absence of a ground reading of complete evaporation. If the fine-grained information is preserved, it is carried by the per-mode arrangement, the typed relation between early and late radiation, rather than by the thermal ingredient list. No reconciliation is derivable inside either settled frame, so an import from a differing closure is owed, with provenance and a shadow test. The figures fix that shape and return a `gap` for which mechanism is correct. Consensus and famous bets have zero verdict weight.

## 2. Three checkable sub-claims

### C1 - per-slot residual; checkable by reading Figures 1 and 12

- **Statement:** the residual must be read per radiation mode, and the information, if preserved, is in the bond rather than the ingredient list. Matching the coarse thermal spectrum and the total entropy is not enough.
- **Source, verbatim:** Fig 1 :19 - "The residual is indexed by object slot, not aggregated." Fig 1 :22 - "Aggregating across slots is how a live signal disappears into an average." Fig 12 :5 - "A set of ingredients is not one. The bond is the rest of the information, and it is the half nothing was recording."
- **What to check:** is the Page curve a statement about the fine-grained entropy of the radiation as a function of mode and time, rather than about the coarse spectrum? Does a purely thermal accounting discard exactly the correlation the claim requires?
- **Refuter:** a resolution that reproduces the coarse spectrum and the Bekenstein-Hawking entropy while leaving the per-mode entanglement irreversibly lost; or a demonstration that the typed relation is recoverable from the ingredients alone.

### C2 - union of two anchors; checkable against the two theories and the experiment record

- **Statement:** the two conditions are separately settled at different anchors, and their contact is a compound with its own loop. Averaging them or voting between them decides nothing.
- **Source, verbatim:** Fig 9 :55 - "A bundled problem is usually a union of overlapping conditions, not a list of separate ones." Fig 9 :57 - "a case in two conditions is not a harder case of either. It stops being a mixture and becomes a specific compound." Fig 2 :29 - "An anchor is whatever settles the question and does not change its answer because of what any frame thinks."
- **What to check:** is laboratory unitarity actually settled independently of the gravitational case? Is the semiclassical calculation valid in its own regime without carrying the fine-grained distinction? Does the firewall trilemma have the form of three conditions that cannot all hold, rather than two opposite answers?
- **Refuter:** a demonstration that one anchor moves with the other frame's reading, so the compound collapses into a single updating frame; or a single lever that clears all three conditions at once.

### C3 - import with provenance, and a declared gap; checkable by reading Figures 6, 8, 9 and 13

- **Statement:** reconciliation cannot be derived inside either settled closure, so an import is owed; the framework's relation to holography is a known-case fit, not a forward derivation; and the identity of the mechanism is unreached, not silence.
- **Source, verbatim:** Fig 6 :23 - "No frame escapes its own closure. The bound moves only on contact with a frame whose closure differs." Fig 8 :32 - "no search, however long, adds an atom". Fig 8 :56-57 - "fits four cases already known and has not been tested forward". Fig 13 :134 - "Unreachable is a verdict here, not a silence".
- **What to check:** does the island rule require a new saddle in the gravitational path integral rather than a rearrangement of the semiclassical calculation? Is the record of where that atom came from present? Does any available instrument discriminate among islands, fuzzballs, remnants and loss?
- **Refuter:** derive the fine-grained entropy formula entirely inside semiclassical QFT with no new atom; or show a proposed resolution has no provenance and no shadow test and still closes the gap; or produce a ground reading that selects one mechanism.

## 3. What is explicitly not claimed

- Not that the framework solves the information paradox; no final room is claimed (Fig 7 :6, "the limit: no last room").
- Not that the framework derived holography, the island rule or the Page curve. They are imports and known-case fits.
- Not that the majority view that information is preserved is correct. Consensus is variation in a room, not a verdict.
- Not that information is a seventh primitive. That reading was retracted at re-census.
- Not `grounded`. Nothing here is asserted as settled by experiment.
- Not that the two anchors' orders of business are the same; they are kept separate and never averaged.

## 5. What would settle it

1. A measured fine-grained entropy curve for radiation from a complete evaporation, read per mode rather than as a pooled spectrum.
2. A bond test that discriminates a typed early-late correlation from a thermal one, with a calibrator that could return a negative result.
3. An analogue that reproduces the gravitational entropy itself, not only the wiring of an island computation.
4. The standing framework-internal falsifiers in `01_figure_grounded_audit.md` section 4.

An absence of these after a bounded search is not a proof of absence (Fig 8 :39).
