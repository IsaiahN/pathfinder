## QUESTION

Decide, in effect terms, what the Tether says about the question "What becomes of information in black holes, and how should its apparent preservation be reconciled with quantum theory?" Option A: treat preservation and loss as two answers to one question and decide between them by the weight of expert opinion. Option B: treat the question as a union of conditions, derive the shape any answer must have, record the import owed for reconciliation, and declare a gap for the mechanism. What each leads to: A turns consensus into an anchor and spends the independence of the readers it asks to check it; B sends a per-slot shape, five written refuters and an honest stop at the limit of the instruments. The verdict below is B in `specified` mode, with the mechanism left as `gap`.

## DOMAIN CENSUS

R by arity, symmetry and scale, then one line per domain with actors / currency / anchor, partition or union, and order by dependency.

- **R (the residual).** The gap between the semiclassical prediction (thermal radiation fixed by mass, charge and angular momentum, so many initial pure states map to one mixed final state) and the quantum requirement of pure-to-pure evolution. Arity three: the two settled frames plus the absence of a ground reading of complete evaporation. It is a union compound, not a partition. Symmetry: the semiclassical map is many-to-one and non-invertible; the quantum requirement is invertible; the crossing between frames is an import and therefore ordered, not symmetric. Scale: first order inside each frame, second order at their union, with the Page turnover a threshold comparison of two quantities.
- **Domain 1, the quantum frame.** Actors: wavefunctions, density matrices, entanglement, unitaries. Currency: purity, von Neumann entropy, distinguishability. Anchor: laboratory experiment, settled. Verdict `specified`, triangulated.
- **Domain 2, the semiclassical gravity frame.** Actors: horizon, Hawking quanta, mass, charge, spin, area. Currency: temperature, Bekenstein-Hawking entropy, evaporation time. Anchor: the semiclassical calculation and astrophysical observation. Settled in its own domain; it does not carry the fine-grained distinction by construction. Verdict `specified`, triangulated.
- **Domain 3, the reconciliation frame.** Actors: holography, islands, replica wormholes, fuzzballs, soft hair, remnants. Currency: fine-grained radiation entropy, the island rule. Anchor: not reachable at this budget; only internal consistency and the Page-curve shape are available. This is an import domain. Verdict `specified` on the need for import, `gap` on which mechanism.
- **Domain 4, the verification / outreach frame.** Actors: expert readers. Currency: agreement and residual. Anchor: the ground, never agreement. In this simulated run all four domains draw on one evidence pool, so independence is zero and any agreement is one pool talking. Verdict `general`, triangulated.
- **Order by dependency.** Describe R in effect terms before naming a frame; settle domains 1 and 2 separately; refuse before mint (Formula step 2); then import with source gate and shadow test; do not read inward and outward instruments against one another (Fig 6 :31).

Strain test applied: Fig 13's six-term substrate was first tried on the information question and re-census assigned it to the composition of space and time, a different domain. No strain remains after that re-census (see `01_figure_grounded_audit.md` section 6).

## VERDICT (from the figures)

The question is a union compound, not a two-way choice. The figures fix the shape of any answer: the residual is per radiation mode, so the information, if preserved, is in the typed arrangement between quanta rather than in the thermal ingredient list; reconciliation cannot be derived inside either settled frame, so an import from a differing closure is owed with provenance and a shadow test; consensus and authority have zero verdict weight because the ground is a measurement, not a vote. The figures do not select among islands, fuzzballs, soft hair, remnants and loss. That is a declared `gap`, unreached at this budget, reported as a verdict and not as silence. The framework's own text marks Bekenstein and holography as a case it fits from known work and not tested forward, so no forward solution is claimed. This is `specified` on the per-domain claims, `general` on the shape, `gap` on the mechanism, ungrounded and candidate.

- **cost if wrong:** bounded and locatable. The packet states mode, three checkable sub-claims and five refuters before the conclusion, so an error is findable rather than invisible. The real cost is presenting a shape as a solved mechanism, which would turn an import into an unexamined atom and spend the independence of outside readers (Fig 2 :42, "Buy independence in proportion to the cost of being wrong."). That cost is avoidable by leading with the shape and its refuters and by declaring the gap.
- **undoable:** yes. The gap is declared rather than filled, and no mechanism is committed to, so no atom has to be withdrawn. A later ground reading can select among the candidates without retracting the shape; if the per-slot shape itself fails, `01_figure_grounded_audit.md` section 4 names the exact refuter that fired.
- **reversed by:** a ground reading, not agreement. A measured fine-grained entropy curve from a complete evaporation, a bond test that discriminates a typed early-late correlation from a thermal one, or an analogue reproducing the gravitational entropy rather than the wiring would settle the mechanism. Recovery of the information inside the semiclassical calculation with no new atom, a derivation of one substrate term from the other five, collapse of the two anchors, or a confirmed forward figure prediction would fire the standing refuters and change the verdict. Until one of these fires, the result stands as `candidate`.
