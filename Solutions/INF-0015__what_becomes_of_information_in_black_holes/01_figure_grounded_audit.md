## 0. The residual, stated in effect terms

The semiclassical frame (general relativity plus quantum field theory in curved spacetime) predicts that an isolated black hole radiates thermally, with the radiation fixed by mass, charge and angular momentum rather than by the detailed collapse state. The quantum frame requires that a pure state evolve to a pure state. The residual is the gap between those two readings of the same evaporation:

> R = |Gamma(b,a) - o'| per radiation-mode slot s, not a single entropy for the hole.

Three consequences follow before any figure is chosen:

1. **The slot is the mode, not the hole.** Fig 1 :19: "The residual is indexed by object slot, not aggregated." Fig 1 :22: "Aggregating across slots is how a live signal disappears into an average." The thermal spectrum is a pooled reading; the information, if preserved, lives in the correlations between modes, which the pooled reading drops.
2. **The ground is the only metric here.** Fig 1 :23: "The ground enters as the only metric." Symbols table :38-40: "It is the aim, not only the error signal, and it is always a slice: what cannot be perceived yet is residual and simply cannot be read." No complete evaporation has been measured, so the decisive slice is exactly one that cannot yet be read.
3. **The phrase "apparent preservation" is a stance, not a ground reading.** It is recorded as such. This audit does not adopt it or refuse it as a verdict; it asks what the figures require of any answer that takes either side.

Question as a union: the two conditions above, plus a third about the reachability of the ground, overlap. They are not two answers to one question (Fig 9 :54, Fig 9 :55, Fig 9 :57).

## 1. Domain census before choosing any figure line

R by arity, symmetry and scale, then one line per domain with actors / currency / anchor, partition or union, and dependency order.

- **R (arity / symmetry / scale).** Arity three: (a) the semiclassical prediction, (b) the unitarity requirement, (c) the absence of a ground reading of complete evaporation. Union compound, not a partition. Symmetry: the semiclassical map is many-to-one and non-invertible; the quantum requirement is invertible. The two are not related by a symmetric bond; crossing from one frame to the other is an import, which is ordered (Fig 6 :23). Scale: first order inside each frame (one state evolving, one hole radiating); second order at their union (a reconciliation). The Page turnover is a threshold comparison of two quantities, not a symmetric join (Operators table :47-51).
- **Domain 1, the quantum frame.** Actors: wavefunctions, density matrices, entanglement, unitaries. Currency: purity, von Neumann entropy, distinguishability of initial states. Anchor: laboratory quantum mechanics, settled. No nameable external anchor for the gravitational case, so domain 1 is not itself the domain of the question.
- **Domain 2, the semiclassical gravity frame.** Actors: horizon, Hawking quanta, mass / charge / spin, horizon area. Currency: temperature, Bekenstein-Hawking entropy, evaporation time. Anchor: the semiclassical calculation and astrophysical observation of black holes. It is settled in its own domain; it does not carry the fine-grained distinction by construction.
- **Domain 3, the reconciliation frame.** Actors: holography, islands, replica wormholes, fuzzballs, soft hair, remnants. Currency: fine-grained entropy of the radiation and the island rule. Anchor: not yet reachable; only internal consistency and the Page-curve shape are available. This is an import domain: its closure differs from both domains 1 and 2 (Fig 6 :23).
- **Domain 4, the verification / outreach frame.** Actors: expert readers. Currency: agreement and residual. Anchor: the ground, never agreement. In this simulated run all four domains draw on one evidence pool, so independence is zero and agreement is not a witness.
- **Partition or union:** union. Fig 9 :55: "A bundled problem is usually a union of overlapping conditions, not a list of separate ones." Unbundle before searching (Fig 9 :54).
- **Dependency order (Fig 3 :20: "The chain is ordered by dependency, not by importance.").** Describe R in effect terms before naming a frame (Fig 9 :27-28); settle domains 1 and 2 separately against their own anchors; refuse before mint (Formula step 2); only then seek an import for domain 3 with source gate and shadow test; do not read inward and outward instruments against each other (Fig 6 :31). A link with no instrument is not a link that works (Fig 3 :27), and the missing ground-reading instrument here is that link.

**Strain test applied before conclusions.** An early reading tried to force Fig 13 :75 ("Two states never bond directly...") onto the information question as if information were a sixth or seventh substrate term. Re-census: Fig 13 governs the six primitives of composition; information here is a property of the arrangement of quantum states, a bond-level quantity, not a member of the six. The line does not govern this quantity, so it is not used as a verdict in F1 to F8. It appears only in `06_isomers_and_order.md` where the atom-set question genuinely arises. No strain remains after that re-census.

## 3. Findings

### F1 - The information is in the bond, not the ingredient list (decisive for the shape)

Fig 12 :5: "A set of ingredients is not one. The bond is the rest of the information, and it is the half nothing was recording."
Fig 12 :46: "A bond is the typed relation between two operands."
Fig 12 :94: "The arrangement is the substance."

The semiclassical calculation records ingredients: mass, charge, angular momentum, temperature, a thermal occupation ⟨aω aω†⟩ = 1/(1 - e^{-ω/kT}). It does not record the typed relation between early and late quanta. That is precisely the half Fig 12 says goes unrecorded. Fig 1 :19 and Fig 1 :22 add that the residual must be read per mode, because averaging into one entropy drops the live signal.

**Shape claim (`general`, triangulated across Fig 12 and Fig 1, low overlap between composition theory and per-slot measurement rule).** Any answer must restore the fine-grained, per-mode arrangement; reproducing the coarse spectrum or the total entropy is not enough. The Page curve is the name physics gives to this requirement: the entanglement entropy of the radiation must come back down. The figures do not derive the Page curve; they require that whatever answers the question operate on the arrangement rather than the ingredient list.

**Refuter.** A resolution that matches the coarse thermal spectrum and the Bekenstein-Hawking entropy while leaving the per-mode entanglement irreversibly lost; or a demonstration that the typed relation is recoverable from the ingredients alone, which would falsify Fig 12 :5 for this case.

### F2 - The question is a union compound with two different anchors, not a two-way choice

Fig 9 :55: "A bundled problem is usually a union of overlapping conditions, not a list of separate ones."
Fig 9 :57: "a case in two conditions is not a harder case of either. It stops being a mixture and becomes a specific compound."
Fig 3 :19: "Optimising a link that already works cannot rescue one that does not."

Domain 1 is settled in the laboratory. Domain 2 is settled in its own semiclassical regime. The conflict is at their contact, where neither is the ground for the other. Treating "preserved versus lost" as a partition invites a single lever to decide both, but one lever clears only the region where one condition holds and leaves the intersection standing.

**Verdict (`specified`, triangulated across Fig 9 and Fig 3).** Keep the two verdicts separate. The "apparent preservation" phrase belongs to domain 1 and cannot be transferred to domain 2 by agreement.

**Refuter.** A demonstration that one of the two anchors moves when the other frame's reading changes (the mutual-update collapse of Fig 2), which would dissolve the union into a single frame and falsify the compound reading.

### F3 - Reconciliation is an import, not a derivation inside either frame

Fig 6 :21: "MINT composes inside the closure and can never add an atom."
Fig 6 :23: "No frame escapes its own closure. The bound moves only on contact with a frame whose closure differs."
Fig 8 :32: "no search, however long, adds an atom"
Fig 8 :50-52: "Convergent derivation and adopted import look identical in the contents. Only the record of where each came from separates them"

No amount of iterating QFT in a fixed curved background adds the atom that carries the information out; that is closure idempotence. Holography and the island rule arrive from a frame whose closure differs, so they are imports. An import needs a source gate (provenance) and a shadow test (does it explain the gap it was brought in for). Without that record, an import and a convergent derivation look identical in their contents.

Fig 8 :56-57 supplies the decisive caution: the framework's own text says of the triangulation claim that it "fits four cases already known and has not been tested forward" and names among them "Bekenstein and holography". So the framework records this as a known case, a retrodiction, not a forward prediction it produced. A claim that the Tether derived holography would reverse the direction of that record.

**Verdict (`specified`, triangulated across Fig 6 and Fig 8).** The need for an import is figure-governed. The specific import (which proposal) is not.

**Refuter.** Derive the island rule, or an equivalent fine-grained entropy formula, entirely inside semiclassical QFT without a new atom and show it matches the radiation entropy; or show that a proposed resolution has no provenance and no shadow test and nevertheless closes the gap, which would falsify the import requirement for this case.

### F4 - Consensus is not an anchor

Fig 2 :35: "Alignment is triangulation, not negotiation. A mirror cannot correct you."
Fig 2 :40: "agreement about the world is worthless when the agreeing frames share an evidence pool"
Fig 2 :42: "Buy independence in proportion to the cost of being wrong."
Fig 7 :35: "A room's agreement is variation, not a verdict. Selection between rooms is where the ground votes."
Fig 10 :42: "A verifier that can reconstruct the claim cannot verify it."

The opened Wikipedia account reports that "It is now generally believed that information is preserved in black-hole evaporation", that Hawking conceded the 1997 bet in 2004, and that Thorne refused. Those are influence and convention events. They are not measurements. A room of physicists who read the same reviews is one evidence pool talking (Fig 2 :40). Selecting readers for receptiveness would manufacture the very correlation that disqualifies their agreement as a verdict.

**Verdict (`general`, triangulated across Fig 2, Fig 7 and Fig 10).** Majority belief has zero verdict weight here. It can fund an action (a budget to try an instrument); it cannot settle one.

**Refuter.** An instrument that reads the fine-grained entanglement of the radiation and settles the question without reference to what anyone believes. That would be a ground reading, not a vote.

### F5 - The decisive ground is unreachable at this budget, and that is a verdict

Fig 10 :24: "the ground does not decay, the channel does"
Fig 6 :34-35: "Where nothing is, there is no edge to extend from"
Fig 8 :39: "So a search that finds nothing is never a proof of absence."
Fig 13 :134: "Unreachable is a verdict here, not a silence"

Two different facts must be held apart. (i) Reconciliation is underivable in principle from domain 1 or domain 2 alone: their closures are closed, so this is an import situation (Fig 8 :32, Fig 6 :23). (ii) The measurement that would choose among the imports is unreachable at this budget: no complete evaporation has been observed, and the fine-grained correlations of Hawking quanta are not resolved by any existing instrument. Importing an atom against a budget limit would add an atom that was not needed and would cost more than starting from primitives (Formula step 7). A search that finds no measurement is not a proof that no measurement could exist.

Fig 3 :27: "A link with no instrument is not a link that works. It is a link nobody has looked at." The missing link here is the ground-reading instrument, not an argument.

**Verdict (`specified` on the reachability, `general` on the distinction, triangulated across Fig 10, Fig 8 and Fig 13).** Record `unreached`, not `unreachable in principle`, for the measurement, and `import owed` for the theory. Do not collapse the two.

**Refuter.** A measured Page curve from an evaporating black hole, or an analogue system that genuinely reproduces the fine-grained gravitational entropy rather than only its wiring, would fire either way and close the gap. An absence of such a measurement after a bounded search would not refute the claim; it would only confirm the budget limit.

Because a frame cannot check the direction of its own question, this shape was written as an ordinary term and allowed to accrue a residual like anything else. It is therefore refusable by the same test as the rest.

**Verdict (`general`, `single-source`).** The limit comes from Fig 5 and `THE_FORMULA.md`, which are the same framework, so this is not triangulated with an independent witness. It is declared as a discipline, not as a discovery.

### F7 - The framework does not solve the information paradox

Fig 13 :115: "The set is closed. The arrangements are not. That gap is why import exists."
Fig 9 :32: "you get a kind of thing, never the answer itself"

The canonical figures README describes the framework as "a generator of the shape of an answer at any level, applied recursively until the residual stops registering against the ground", stopping "at the limit of what the available instruments can distinguish, and records the lineage so the stop is honest." Naming islands or fuzzballs as *the* answer would be the shape-to-name error the framework forbids for missing primitives, and would also misread Fig 8's own status for Bekenstein and holography.

**Verdict (refusal, triangulated across Fig 8, Fig 13 and the canonical README).** No forward solution is claimed.

### F9 - The thermodynamics link is a world result, not a figure line

The question sits in a section on information, physics and thermodynamics. Landauer's principle, opened this session, states that "any logically irreversible manipulation of information, such as the erasure of a bit or the merging of two computation paths, must be accompanied by a corresponding entropy increase in non-information-bearing degrees of freedom of the information-processing apparatus or its environment". This is real physics and it sharpens the effect statement: a many-to-one map is logically irreversible. But no figure line gives the Landauer bound, and forcing Fig 13's energy or gradient terms onto it would be a strain. After re-census, this finding is reported as **world corroboration only, `single-source`**, with no figure verdict attached. This is the one place in the audit where the figures genuinely fall silent on a substantive physical point, and it is recorded rather than papered over.

## 4. Pre-registered refuter (written before the conclusion)

Per the seat map: "And a falsifier written before the run is a design instrument, not only an honesty device." The shape claim is refuted if any of the following holds:

1. The fine-grained information is recovered from the semiclassical calculation without importing a new atom, and the per-mode entanglement is restored (breaks F1 and F3).
2. A measurement reads the fine-grained entanglement of Hawking radiation from a complete evaporation, or an analogue that reproduces the gravitational entropy, and shows a Page curve or its definite absence (breaks F5 in either direction).
3. One of the six substrate terms is derived from the other five, so the composition rule used in F1 fails (framework-internal).
4. The two anchors collapse: lab unitarity changes when the gravitational reading changes, so the union reading of F2 is false (breaks Fig 2's non-updating anchor condition).
5. A forward figure-derived prediction about evaporation is confirmed at the ground (clears F7).

If any fires, the relevant finding is withdrawn and the retraction is recorded here, not silently erased. Until one is run, no part of this may be labelled `grounded`.

## 7. Final statement
