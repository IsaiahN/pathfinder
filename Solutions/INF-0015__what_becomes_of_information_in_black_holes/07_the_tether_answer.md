# The Tether's answer on black hole information

**Mode: `specified` on the per-domain verdicts, `general` on the structural shape, `gap` on the identity of the mechanism, not `grounded`.** This is a shape and a route, not a result. Simulated derivation checked against the figures (`05_what_shape_means.md` section 8), not a ground reading.

## 1. The residual it answers

A semiclassical calculation gives radiation fixed by mass, charge and angular momentum, so many distinct collapse states map to one coarse final state. Quantum mechanics requires a pure state to remain pure. The residual is that mismatch read per radiation mode, not as a single entropy for the hole (Fig 1 :19: "The residual is indexed by object slot, not aggregated."). The question's phrase "apparent preservation" is a stance about one domain, not a ground reading, and is treated as such.

## 2. Verdicts by domain, kept separate and never averaged

**Domain 1, the quantum frame.** `specified`, triangulated across Fig 2 :29 and the opened Landauer and Page sources. Unitarity is settled in the laboratory; a logically irreversible many-to-one map has a physical cost; the requirement that the final state stay pure is not in doubt *in this frame*. What is not settled is whether that requirement transfers to gravity, because transferring it is an import.

**Domain 2, the semiclassical gravity frame.** `specified`, triangulated across Fig 1 :22 and the opened Wikipedia overview and Page 1993. The calculation is valid in its regime and genuinely yields a coarse thermal map. It does not carry the fine-grained distinction by construction, and averaging into one spectrum drops the live signal: Fig 1 :22: "Aggregating across slots is how a live signal disappears into an average."

**Domain 3, the reconciliation frame.** `specified` on the need for an import, `gap` on which mechanism. Triangulated across Fig 6 :23 and Fig 8 :32 for the need:

> Fig 6 :23 "No frame escapes its own closure. The bound moves only on contact with a frame whose closure differs."
> Fig 8 :32 "no search, however long, adds an atom"

Neither settled frame can derive the reconciliation. Holography, islands and replica wormholes are imports from a differing closure and need provenance plus a shadow test (Fig 8 :50-52). The framework's own text lists Bekenstein and holography among cases it fits from known work, "fits four cases already known and has not been tested forward" (Fig 8 :56-57), so this is a retrodiction, not a framework result. Which candidate is right is a declared `gap`, unreached at this budget, not silence.

**Domain 4, the verification frame.** `general`, triangulated across Fig 2 :35, Fig 7 :35 and Fig 10 :42. Consensus, famous bets and majority belief carry zero verdict weight. Fig 7 :35: "A room's agreement is variation, not a verdict. Selection between rooms is where the ground votes."

## 3. The shape

1. **The information, if preserved, is in the arrangement, not the ingredient list.** Fig 12 :5: "A set of ingredients is not one. The bond is the rest of the information, and it is the half nothing was recording." A thermal spectrum records the ingredients; it cannot comment on the source.
2. **The residual is per mode.** The hole-wide entropy is a pooled reading. The Page curve is the physics name for the requirement that the fine-grained entropy return to zero.
3. **Reconciliation is a union compound, not a two-way choice.** Three overlapping conditions with two different anchors and one unreachable ground. One lever leaves the intersection standing (Fig 9 :55, :57).
4. **An import is owed, with provenance.** No amount of composing inside the semiclassical closure adds the missing atom. A wrong import costs more than starting from primitives, so the provenance is not decoration.
5. **The identity of the mechanism is not returned.** Fig 9 :32: "you get a kind of thing, never the answer itself." Islands, fuzzballs, soft hair, remnants and genuine loss are candidates; the figures do not select among them.

## 4. What it explicitly does not say

- Not that the framework solves the black hole information paradox. It returns a shape, a route and a recorded stop.
- Not that it derived holography or the island rule. Those are imports, and Fig 8 :56-57 marks the framework's relation to them as a known-case fit, untested forward.
- Not that the majority view is correct, and not that a famous concession is a measurement.
- Not that "information" is a seventh primitive. It is a bond-level property of the arrangement; forcing it onto the six-term substrate was retracted in `01_figure_grounded_audit.md` section 6.
- Not that the question is settled by agreement, and not `grounded`. No complete evaporation has been measured.

## 5. What would settle it - the ground

A source-discriminating instrument, not a further argument. *The Hard Problem is About Source*, opened this session: a color instrument "cannot comment on the source"; the spectrometer resolved the case, not an argument. Here the corresponding tests are:

- **Fine-grained entropy test:** does the radiation's entanglement entropy follow the Page curve, stay near the Hawking curve, or deviate in some third way, as read per mode rather than as a pooled spectrum?
- **Bond test:** is there a typed correlation between early and late quanta that carries the collapse-state distinction, and can it be discriminated from a merely thermal one?
- **Mechanism separation:** can an analogue system reproduce the fine-grained *gravitational* entropy rather than only the wiring of an island computation? If it cannot, the analogue proves wiring and not capability (Fig 11 :70, read in the exemplar and carried).
- **Standing falsifiers** from `01_figure_grounded_audit.md` section 4: recovery of the information without a new atom; a ground reading of the Page curve; derivation of one substrate term from the other five; collapse of the two anchors; a confirmed forward prediction.

## 6. The honest stop

**Unreached, not unreachable in principle.** Fig 10 :24: "the ground does not decay, the channel does". The channel to a complete evaporation is missing, not the ground. Two different facts are held apart: the theory is underivable from either settled frame alone, so an import is owed; the measurement is unreachable at this budget, so more search inside the frame is not the remedy and importing an atom against the budget limit would add an atom not yet needed. Fig 13 :134: "Unreachable is a verdict here, not a silence". The answer stops at the limit of the available instruments and records the lineage so the stop is checkable. Indiscernibility is not identity.

## 7. One-paragraph version for outreach

> The Tether does not solve the black hole information paradox. It says the question is a union of three conditions with two different anchors and an unreached ground, not a vote between preservation and loss. It says that if information is preserved, it is carried in the per-mode arrangement, the typed relation between early and late quanta, rather than in the thermal list of ingredients, because a list of ingredients has recorded half of itself. It says that no reconciliation can be derived inside either the quantum frame or the semiclassical gravity frame, so an import is owed, with provenance and a shadow test, and that the framework's own text marks Bekenstein and holography as a known-case fit not tested forward. It refuses to name which mechanism is right, because no instrument yet discriminates among islands, fuzzballs, remnants and loss. What it offers is the shape any answer must have, five refuters written in advance, and the instrument that would close the question. The stop is recorded until the ground settles it.
