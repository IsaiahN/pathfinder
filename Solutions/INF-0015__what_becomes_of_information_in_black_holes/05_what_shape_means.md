## 1. A shape is a constraint, not a filler

The canonical figures README states the limit: the framework returns "what a missing thing would have to look like (a shape, not a name)". Fig 9 :32: "you get a kind of thing, never the answer itself". Fig 13 :134: "Unreachable is a verdict here, not a silence".

From *The Library of Babel*, opened this session: "you can cut your search short if the set of books returned is small and descriptive enough that it provides a general shape of the answer that satisfies your question." The same essay asks whether "your problem was a bundle or conflation of several problems masquerading as one?" That is exactly the effect statement of F2 in `01_figure_grounded_audit.md`: the preservation question is a union of three conditions, not one question with two answers.

A **shape** is the narrowed form of an answer you can state before you can name it. It constrains; it does not fill. For this question the shape is:

> any answer must keep the per-mode map invertible, identify the typed relation that carries the distinction across the horizon, be checkable by an instrument that would discriminate among candidate mechanisms, and record the provenance of any imported atom.

That is a shape. "The island rule is the answer" is a name.

## 2. How the shape is obtained

- *Library of Babel*, opened: use the vocabulary already closest to the thing, look for recurring new terms that overlap or correlate with it, recurse. The carried text records: "With each new term gained, you narrow the space and deepen your vocabulary." For this question the bootstrapped vocabulary ran from thermal spectrum to entanglement entropy to Page curve to island rule to replica wormhole, and the search stopped when the shape stopped moving.
- Fig 6 :30: "outward: does another frame already hold it? The description travels. Fails by being sharpened into terms only we can read. Cast it in effect terms." Fig 9 :30-31: "your words are borrowed too, so describe the effect rather than the cause." The effect here is the invertibility of a per-mode map, not the name of a microscopic mechanism.
- *Decoding Tacit Knowledge* (carried): "When contact isn't possible, the best you can send is a plain description of what you'd expect to see, because anyone can check that against the world." Contact with a complete evaporation is not possible at this budget, so the shape is a description of what would be seen if a ground-reading instrument existed.
- *Library of Babel*, opened, on stopping: "you know that you are done because the finish line will also be the starting place, as there is nowhere else to search and nothing else that can describe the answer better." Here the stop is reached when the candidate mechanisms all satisfy the same four-part constraint and no available instrument separates them.

## 3. What licenses a name

Only two things, both outside the shape itself:

1. **The ground settles it.** `THE_FORMULA.md` step 5: "the ground settles it. Verified only by what pays. A gate is not the ground." The README says the framework "stops at the limit of what the available instruments can distinguish, and records the lineage so the stop is honest." Here that means a measurement of the fine-grained radiation entropy, which does not exist.
2. **A provenanced import from a frame whose closure differs**, with a source gate and a shadow test (Fig 6 :23, Fig 8 :50-52). Holography and the island rule satisfy the closure condition; their provenance is in the published record. That licenses them as candidates, not as the unique answer.

*They're Scanning Books When They Should* (carried): "The real import is not a new term in the existing space. It is a new dimension of the space." Holography is exactly that: area where volume was expected. The same essay: "Declaring the primitives does not make them neutral. It makes the choice visible."

## 4. Why the thermal spectrum cannot name its source

*The Hard Problem is About Source*, opened this session, gives the governing mechanism. Metamerism: an instrument reads green and "cannot comment on the source"; the source might be one green light, or yellow plus blue, or RGB, or CMYK, or a scheme not yet discovered. "If several paths create the same behavior, then behavior is a projection with lossy information." And: "An instrument that can't distinguish these cases demonstrates its own inadequacy." It was the spectrometer, not an argument, that resolved it.

The Hawking calculation reads the radiation's occupation number and finds a thermal value depending on one parameter. Many collapse states give that same reading. Naming the source from that reading is the metamerism error: a source inferred from a projection that cannot discriminate among sources. What is owed is a source-discriminating instrument, not a name. This is F1 and F5 of `01_figure_grounded_audit.md` in essay form.

The thermal reading is also a pooled reading. Fig 1 :22: "Aggregating across slots is how a live signal disappears into an average." The spectrometer analogue is precisely a move from a pooled color reading to a per-wavelength decomposition.

## 5. Correction to my own earlier reading

I initially treated "shape, not a name" as a categorical ban on naming any mechanism. That overstates it. The canonical wording governs "a missing thing", that is, a primitive not held in the library. Naming an already available binding is provenance, not the invention of a new primitive. The exemplar records the same correction in its `05` section 5b and `06` Q1, and the re-census in `01_figure_grounded_audit.md` section 6 adopts it.

What remains wrong in the naive claim about black holes is not the name itself but the absence of a ground that selects among names. Islands, fuzzballs, remnants and loss are all available bindings; the framework cannot choose among them because the discriminating instrument does not exist. The reason for the `gap` is the missing discriminator, not a ban on naming held atoms. Both readings are recorded so neither is quietly lost.

## 6. Prestige is not action

From *The Recursive Self-Improvement Trap*, opened: "Prestige can fund an action. It can give an action a budget to be tried. But it cannot settle an action. Only the ground can settle it. The Ground Maintainer must be blind to prestige." Also: "You may all agree and be wrong."

The fact that information preservation is now generally believed, and that a famous bet was conceded, is the named collapse. It gives the preservation programme a budget to be tried; it does not establish its truth. The legitimate ask is for a refutation or for an instrument, not for endorsement.

## 7. Provenance separates two identical-looking histories

Fig 8 :50-52: "Convergent derivation and adopted import look identical in the contents. Only the record of where each came from separates them." *The Hard Problem is About Source* gives the instrument-level version: the reading does not determine the source. Both say the same thing at different scales. For this question it means a paper that derives the island rule and a paper that adopts it may look identical on the page; only the dated record of where the atom came from separates them. `10_source_ledger.csv` carries that record for every source used here.

## 8. My own audit is a simulation, not a ground reading

*The Recursive Self-Improvement Trap*, opened: "the output is only as good as the validator. The agent, or the tool, or the frame, or the theory, cannot validate itself." And: "LLMs can quickly simulate an approximate answer/output with the formula" but "the truly testable output would need to be derived from the real architecture or working the formula yourself." Also: "I am not claiming that the Tether is a self-validating axiom system... Metatheories cannot validate themselves."

So `01_figure_grounded_audit.md` and this folder generally are simulated derivations, checked for internal consistency against the figures. They are **not** tests of the claim and must be labelled as such. This folder cannot settle the black hole information question, and it makes no claim to do so.

## 9. The one-paragraph version

A shape is a constraint you can state before you can name its filler. For black hole information it says the map must be invertible per radiation mode, the distinction must live in the arrangement rather than the ingredient list, an instrument must exist that can discriminate among the candidate mechanisms, and any imported atom must carry its provenance. It does not say which mechanism is right, because no ground reading selects among them. Majority belief, famous bets and the framework's own text are not such a reading. What would settle it is a spectrometer for the fine-grained radiation entropy. Until then the stop is recorded, and unreachable is reported as a verdict rather than a silence.
