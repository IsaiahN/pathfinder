## Wider literature that bears on the question, by low-overlap field

1. **Information theory / engineering (Shannon 1948).** The founding measure explicitly excludes semantic aspects. This is not an oversight in the paper; the exclusion is what makes the engineering theory general. It corroborates that statistical predictability is a different quantity from meaning, not a failed attempt at it.
2. **Philosophy of information (Bar-Hillel and Carnap 1952, 1953; Floridi 2004; SEP 2005/2022).** A quantitative "semantic information" measure does exist, but it is an inverse logical-probability measure. It assigns infinite information to a contradiction, which is the paradoxical result the literature spends decades repairing. This corroborates the audit's point that the statistical route, even when relabelled semantic, prices rarity and not aboutness.
3. **Cognitive science / AI (Harnad 1990).** Formal symbol manipulation is systematically interpretable as meaningful but the interpretation is extrinsic. Passing a behavioral Turing test is not enough for intrinsic meaning; grounding requires causal contact. Corroborates F3 and F4.
4. **Animal behaviour (Seyfarth, Cheney and Marler 1980; Price et al. 2015; Fischer 2017).** The classic behavioral assay of "meaning" in vervet alarm calls. The response-based test is powerful and also explicitly limited, and the later reanalysis weakened the one-to-one call-to-context mapping. Corroborates that behavioral effect is a lossy proxy, and supplies the natural experiment for F4.
5. **Engineering / 6G semantic communications (Yang et al. 2022, and the 2024/2025 surveys).** A whole engineering field is trying to transmit meaning rather than bits. Its own literature reports that Weaver's semantic and effectiveness levels remain open, that Bar-Hillel and Carnap style measures are not radically different from Shannon's, and that the meaning factor is not captured. Corroborates F1 and F7 from a field with no stake in the Tether.
6. **Philosophy of mind / representation (SEP Mental Representation).** Semantic properties are aboutness, truth and reference; naturalized information accounts still face the disjunction problem. Corroborates that correlation is not aboutness (F2, F4).

## Distribution: offer, do not push

Figure-grounded reasons:

- **Fig 4 :22-25** — *"Only methods cross up. Only head starts come down. A recording carried upward looks like knowledge and is a description of one occasion."* Sending the full framework before a recipient has described a residual sends a recording, not a generator.
- **Fig 10 :42** — *"A verifier that can reconstruct the claim cannot verify it."* A reader who has absorbed the framework before giving a verdict has joined the frame being checked.
- **Fig 9 :27-28** — *"describe it before you go looking, or any frame you pick will seem to fit."* Ask what they would expect to see if the shape claim were true before giving them the literature.
- **Fig 2 :40** — *"disqualifying for a verdict: agreement about the world is worthless when the agreeing frames share an evidence pool."* A mass send selected for likely agreement produces a convention, not corroboration.
- **Fig 2 :42** — *"Buy independence in proportion to the cost of being wrong."* The cost here is a wrong measure of meaning entering practice; that is high, so buy independent readers rather than many agreeing ones.

**Recommended order in a reply:** (1) the residual, the shape claim and its refuter; (2) ask what they would count as a failure; (3) record their verdict; (4) only then the verbatim figure passages, this links page and the wider literature, with the note that the figures, not the summary, are the authority; (5) offer the formula as a reusable method, with a fresh refuter and a stated mode for each new problem.
