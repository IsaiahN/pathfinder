## 0. The claim under audit

> **Question.** Can semantic content be measured without reducing meaning to statistical predictability or behavioral effect?
>
> **Option A.** Yes: a single measure exists. It is either a refined statistical quantity (entropy, mutual information, logical probability, minimum description length) or a refined behavioral quantity (functional role, task success, discriminative response), or a weighted combination of the two.
>
> **Option B.** The two candidate measures are different currencies. A measure that declares meaning by either route has not grounded it. What can be delivered is the shape any admissible measure must have and the instrument that would have to be built. The honest verdict on a single-number measure is `unreachable with the instruments as they stand`, which is a verdict and not a silence.

The residual is stated in effect terms: two readings of one signal, one from the statistics of the signal and one from the conduct it produces, are being asked to yield a third quantity, "meaning", that neither seems to carry. What fails is the passage from a reading to what the reading is about.

## 1. DOMAIN CENSUS (written before any figure line was chosen)

**R by arity, symmetry, scale.** Arity: three terms minimum, signal / world state / interpreter whose conduct changes, plus the instrument that would read the first. A two-reader seam, not a single variable. Symmetry: the two proposed reductions are **not** interchangeable. One is priced in bits, the other in actions; swapping them changes the unit, not just the label. Scale: the measurement is a step-level operation on one slot, but the question asks about a quantity that may only be visible at the level of a lineage or a comparison between frames.

**One line per domain: actors / currency / anchor.**

| domain | actors | currency | anchor (nameable?) |
|---|---|---|---|
| 1. Statistical frame | signal, code, distribution, compression scheme | bits / logical probability | the source statistics and the declared code; yes, a measurable regularity, but it is not aboutness |
| 2. Behavioral frame | agent, action, observation, task, reward | actions / task effect | experiment, playback, outcome; yes, the ground for "what did it do" |
| 3. Semantic frame | representation, referent, interpretation, instrument | aboutness / source contact | the world state that settles what the reading is about; yes, but only via an instrument that discriminates sources |
| 4. Verification frame | expert reader, submission venue, refuter | residual and correction | the ground, never agreement; yes |

No nameable anchor means not a domain yet. All four have anchors, so all four are domains. There is a **seam** between domain 1 and domain 3 (a reading cannot comment on its source) and a seam between domain 2 and domain 3 (behavior is a lossy projection). The question is a **union compound**, not one hard problem: Fig 9 :57, *"a case in two conditions is not a harder case of either."* It needs its own loop, and the two conditions must be unbundled before a measure is sought.

**Order by dependency (Fig 3).** First establish what the signal's statistics can and cannot carry (domain 1). Then establish what conduct can and cannot carry (domain 2). Then ask what a source-discriminating instrument would have to look like (domain 3). Verification (domain 4) comes last and ranges against the ground, not against agreement.

**Strain test applied during census.** Fig 5 :45 licenses MDL *"put to work on model selection"*, not on semantic content. Reading that line as a licence for a meaning measure is a strain; it governs a different quantity, so re-census rather than stretch it. F5 records the result.

## 3. Findings

### F1: The question is a union compound, and its two candidates are two currencies (decisive)

Fig 9 :57: *"a case in two conditions is not a harder case of either."* Fig 9 :54-58 requires unbundling before search. Fig 12 :125: **"A composition costs in two currencies, and they do not add."** Fig 12 :126: **"Bits price the description. Actions price finding out whether it holds."** Fig 12 :130: **"Summing them into one figure is the error this names, not the fix for it."**

Statistical predictability is a price in bits. Behavioral effect is a price in actions. They are in different units, so a system holding one accounting cannot compare them. A request for "a measure of semantic content that is neither" is a request for a third quantity composed from two incommensurable accounts. By Fig 12 :130, summing them is the named error. By Fig 9 :57, the right move is to split the compound and give each condition its own loop, not to average them.

**Verdict:** `union compound`, triangulated (Fig 9 union/partition rule and Fig 12 two-currency rule, low overlap between search/lookup and composition cost).

**Refuter:** show a single unit in which bits and actions add without loss, or show the two conditions are one condition under a shared evidence pool that the figures would call a single frame.

### F2: Meaning is not read off the ingredients; it is carried by the bond

Fig 12 :5: **"A set of ingredients is not one. The bond is the rest of the information, and it is the half nothing was recording."** Fig 12 :94: **"The arrangement is the substance."** Fig 13 :16: **"it names what is being measured"** (states). Fig 13 :26: **"it decides what the measurement would mean"** (structures).

A signal's statistics price the operands, the ingredient set. The semantic content, on this reading, is the typed relation between operands, which is "the rest of the information" and is "the half nothing was recording." Shannon-style measures record the ingredient distribution; the bond is what they leave out. Fig 13 :26 makes the point at the substrate level: which structure decides what a measurement would mean is not answered by naming what is being measured.

Operators table :66: *"A bond that costs nothing is a bond that carries nothing, which is the flat case."* A measure that ignores the bond is the flat formula of Fig 12, where *"nothing downstream can tell them apart, and nothing signals that anything was lost."*

**Verdict:** triangulated (Fig 12 composition and Fig 13 substrate, low overlap); the "bond carries meaning" reading is single-source at the figure level but corroborated by the world sources in `09`.

**Refuter:** demonstrate that the arrangement is fully recoverable from the operand statistics alone, that is, that the flat formula is lossless. That breaks Fig 12 :5.

### F3: A declared meaning is not a grounded one

Fig 10 :18: **"a declared meaning is not a grounded one"**. Fig 10 :49: **"Declaring a meaning does not ground it."** Fig 10 :47: **"a convention that decides an outcome has stopped being one."**

Any proposal that *stipulates* a semantic measure and then uses the number as evidence of meaning has promoted a convention to a verdict. It may be a useful convention for coordinating agreement about a shared meaning, which Fig 2 :41 calls *"efficient for a convention"*, but the moment it decides the question it stops being a convention (Fig 10 :47). The measure must be paid by the ground, not by definition.

**Verdict:** single-source at figure level (Fig 10), corroborated by Harnad's symbol grounding result in `09`.

**Refuter:** exhibit a meaning metric whose number changes when the ground changes and not when the stipulation changes.

### F4: Behavior is a lossy projection; the anchor is legitimate only for the question it is about

Fig 2 :29: **"An anchor is whatever settles the question and does not change its answer because of what any frame thinks."** Fig 2 :37-38: *"Human judgment is the right anchor for is this a good answer for a person, because the question is about people. It is only an instrument for is this code correct, and instruments can be wrong on axes they do not measure."* Fig 1 :22: **"Aggregating across slots is how a live signal disappears into an average."** Fig 1 :27: **"They are frame-internal. A frame cannot score itself with a quantity it also produces."**

A behavioral assay is the legitimate anchor for "what did the signal make the agent do", because that question is constitutively about conduct. It is an instrument, not an anchor, for "what is the signal about", and instruments can be wrong on axes they do not measure. The Hard Problem is About Source essay states the shared failure directly: *"If several paths create the same behavior, then behavior is a projection with lossy information."* The vervet-money literature supplies the check: playback response is the assay, but Seyfarth et al. cautioned that *"There are limits to how far a semantic analysis can be carried out when it is based solely on the responses that those signals evoke."*

Per-slot discipline matters here. A global behavioral score averages away the very slot at which the source question lives (Fig 1 :22).

**Verdict:** triangulated (Fig 2 anchor legitimacy and Fig 1 metric/aggregation rules, low overlap); corroborated by the animal-behaviour world sources.

**Refuter:** show a behavioral assay that discriminates between two sources producing identical readings, without access to the source. If such an assay exists, behavior is not lossy on this axis.

### F5: The MDL line governs model selection, not meaning (strain test)

Fig 5 :45: **"This is minimum description length, Rissanen 1978, which is itself a composition of Kolmogorov complexity and Shannon coding put to work on model selection."** The same figure, line 26, calls the acceptance test *"does the new term cost less to state than the confusion it removes?"*

This is the strongest apparent friend of Option A, and it must be refused as a meaning measure. It is a bargain for admitting a term into the library. It prices the description of the term and the residual it removes, not what the term is about. Reading it as a semantic metric makes Fig 5 govern a quantity it does not, a strain. Re-census sends it to F1: it is a filter for the budget, not a witness for the verdict (Fig 9 :60). The world literature makes the same point: the Semantic Communications survey notes that in the Bar-Hillel and Carnap tradition *"there is no radical difference between TSI and CIT"* and that such measures *"have no concerns with the essence of SI, the meaning factor."*

**Verdict:** strain resolved by re-census; filtered, not used as a witness.

**Refuter:** produce a derivation in which description length fixes the referent of a term, not merely its admissibility.

### F6: A measure that does not change contact changes nothing

Fig 11 :40: **"An improvement that does not change contact changes nothing, however much it improves."** Fig 11 :68: *"what you failed to reproduce is invisible until the goal fails."*

A more refined statistical measure or a more sensitive behavioral test leaves the system's contact with the source unchanged. By Fig 11 :40 that is not progress on the semantic question. What would change contact is an instrument that discriminates among the sources of a reading, the spectrometer move in the Hard Problem is About Source essay: the answer was *"proven wrong not by argument, but by the arrival of a better instrument."*

**Verdict:** triangulated (Fig 11 contact rule and Fig 6 instrument rule, :34 *"the search for a proto-instrument is a search for one that is already there"*).

**Refuter:** show an internal refinement that moves the source-discrimination boundary without adding an instrument.

### F7: The single-number measure is unreachable, not impossible; an instrument is owed

Fig 13 :134: **"Unreachable is a verdict here, not a silence"**. Fig 13 :139: **"narrowing gets you close while the ground decides."** Fig 8 :39: *"So a search that finds nothing is never a proof of absence."* Fig 6 :34: *"the search for a proto-instrument is a search for one that is already there."*

After the re-census in F1 to F6, no figure line licenses a single measure that is neither statistical nor behavioral. The honest report is `unreachable with the instruments as they stand`, not "meaning cannot be measured" and not silence. What is owed is a description, in effect terms, of an instrument that would discriminate sources among readings that currently look identical. The framework returns a shape, not a name (Fig 9 :32: *"you get a kind of thing, never the answer itself"*), so it cannot name the missing primitive. Fig 8 :39 forbids converting the failed search into a proof of absence.

**Verdict:** triangulated (Fig 13, Fig 8, Fig 6, three figures with low overlap on the report rule).

**Refuter:** exhibit the instrument and show it changes contact. That converts `unreachable` to `grounded` for this question.

### F8: A measure is a lossy condensation; measure the round-trip gap and keep it per-slot

Fig 4 :30: *"So take the round trip and measure the gap. Without it you are navigating by dead reckoning: confident about a position nobody checked."* Fig 4 :31: **"The gap is not an error to eliminate. It is the measurement of what the coarser description cannot hold."** Fig 1 :22 again: *"Aggregating across slots is how a live signal disappears into an average."*

Any proxy measure is an abstraction `T_A` from the rich signal to a number, and a concretisation `T_E` back to a claim about meaning. The gap `R_T = gap(x, (T_E ∘ T_A)(x))` is what the proxy cannot hold. In this question `R_T` includes at least the bond (F2), the source identity behind a reading (F4), and the unit difference between bits and actions (F1). Treating a measure as lossless is the dead-reckoning error.

**Verdict:** single-source at figure level (Fig 4), corroborated by the metamerism essay and the source-ledger sources.

**Refuter:** demonstrate a round trip with zero measured gap on the three named losses.

## 4. Pre-registered refuter (written before the conclusion)

A falsifier written before the run is a design instrument. The shape claim is refuted if any of the following holds:

1. A single measure of semantic content is demonstrated whose value is provably not a function of signal statistics and not a function of behavioral or functional effects, and that changes contact with the ground by discriminating among sources producing the same reading (breaks F1, F6).
2. Bits and actions are shown to be commensurable in one unit without residue (breaks F1, Fig 12 :125-130).
3. The bond and arrangement are shown to be fully recoverable from operand statistics, that is, the flat formula is lossless (breaks F2, Fig 12 :5).
4. A meaning metric is shown to be grounded by declaration or convention alone, with the ground unmoved (breaks F3, Fig 10 :49).
5. A behavioral assay is shown to discriminate sources behind identical readings without source access (breaks F4).

If any fires, the verdict is withdrawn. Until one is run, no part of this may be labelled `grounded`.

## 5. Recommendation

1. Split the question before answering it (F1). Do not offer a weighted sum of entropy and task success; Fig 12 :130 names that as the error.
2. Lead with what a measure must carry, not with a number: the bond, a non-updating anchor, per-slot resolution, and separate currency accounts (F2, F3, F4, F8).
3. Report the stop honestly as `unreachable with the instruments as they stand`, and describe the instrument owed in effect terms (F7). Never convert a failed search into "meaning is unmeasurable" (Fig 8 :39).
4. Keep the refuter in front of the links. Fig 10 :52: *"Bring a characterised residual, never the source."*
5. Send the generator, not the recording. Fig 4 :25: *"A recording carried upward looks like knowledge and is a description of one occasion."*
