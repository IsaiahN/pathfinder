## QUESTION

Decide, in effect terms, what the Tether says about measuring semantic content. A signal, a world state it is taken to be about, and an interpreter whose conduct changes are given. Option A: accept a single measure, either statistical predictability (entropy, compression, logical probability, description length) or behavioral effect (response, task role), or a weighted combination. Option B: the two are different currencies, a declared meaning is not grounded, and what is owed is a source-discriminating instrument described in effect terms; the single-number measure is unreachable with the instruments as they stand. What each leads to: A produces a number that prices the ingredients or the effect and calls it meaning, and it promotes a convention to a verdict; B produces a four-constraint shape, a recorded stop, and a pre-registered refuter.

- **R (arity / symmetry / scale).** The residual is the failed passage from a reading to what the reading is about. Arity: three terms minimum (signal, source, interpreter), so a seam rather than one variable. Symmetry: the two proposed reductions are not interchangeable; one is in bits, the other in actions. Scale: a step-level reading of one slot, but the question concerns a quantity visible only at the level of a comparison between frames. Fig 9 :57 names it a union compound: a case in two conditions is not a harder case of either.
- **Domain 1, the statistical frame.** Actors: signal, code, distribution, compression scheme. Currency: bits and logical probability. Anchor: the source statistics and declared code, a measurable regularity but not aboutness. Question type: what a reading can and cannot carry.
- **Domain 2, the behavioral frame.** Actors: agent, action, observation, task, reward. Currency: actions and task effect. Anchor: experiment, playback, outcome; legitimate for "what did it do". Question type: what conduct can and cannot carry.
- **Domain 3, the semantic frame.** Actors: representation, referent, interpretation, instrument. Currency: aboutness and source contact. Anchor: the world state that settles what the reading is about, reachable only through an instrument that discriminates sources. Question type: the compound with its own loop.
- **Domain 4, verification.** Actors: expert readers, submission venues, refuter. Currency: residual and correction. Anchor: the ground, never agreement. In this simulated run all seats draw on one evidence pool, so their agreement is one pool talking, not corroboration.
- **Order by dependency (Fig 3).** Establish what the statistics can carry, then what conduct can carry, then what the source-discriminating instrument would look like; verification comes last. A great technical or behavioral measure does not rescue an unmeasured semantic link.

## VERDICT (from the figures)

The question is a union compound. Statistical predictability and behavioral effect are two currencies priced in bits and in actions; they do not add, and a weighted combination is the named error. Semantic content is carried by the typed bond between a signal and its source, the part ingredient-level measures leave out; a meaning fixed by stipulation is not grounded; and a behavioral assay is an anchor for conduct and an instrument, with blind axes, for aboutness. The verdict on a single-number measure is `unreachable with the instruments as they stand`, a report of a stop and not a proof of impossibility. What is owed is a source-discriminating instrument described in effect terms. The admissible shape has four constraints: carry the bond, range against a non-updating anchor, resolve per slot, and keep bits and actions in separate accounts. Mode `general` on that shape and `specified` on the falsifiable prediction; nothing here is `grounded`.

- **cost if wrong:** if the two currencies are in fact commensurable, or if the bond is recoverable from operand statistics, then this packet refuses a legitimate measure and stalls a field that could have had one. The cost is bounded and locatable because the four constraints and the five refuters are written down; an error is findable rather than invisible. The larger practical cost is the opposite case: accepting a weighted entropy-and-task score as a meaning measure, which Fig 12 :130 names as the error and which would misdirect instruments and evaluation criteria.
- **undoable:** yes. No primitive is withdrawn and no settled term is deleted. If a refuter fires, the verdict is replaced by the corrected claim; `01` §7 records the re-census and `11_audit.md` records the round trip. A sent packet can be superseded by a corrected one.
- **reversed by:** a ground reading, not agreement. A source-discriminating instrument that separates two readings with identical statistics and identical effects would move the verdict to `grounded` (or refute it). A single unit in which bits and actions add without residue would break C1. A lossless flat formula would break C2. A behavioral assay that discriminates sources without source access would break C3. Until one of these fires, the verdict stands as `candidate`.
