## The residual in effect terms

We hold a signal, a world state it is taken to be about, and an agent whose conduct the signal changes. Two candidate measures of its semantic content are on the table: statistical predictability (entropy, compression, logical probability, description length) and behavioral effect (the response the signal evokes, its functional or task role). Option A asserts that one of these, or a weighted combination, measures meaning. Option B holds that the two are different currencies, that a declared meaning is not a grounded one, and that what is owed is a source-discriminating instrument rather than a new reduction. A leads to a number that prices the wrong object and calls it meaning. B leads to a shape any admissible measure must have, an honest "unreachable with the instruments as they stand", and a stated refuter.

## What is deliberately not here

- No personal emails of named individuals.
- No ranking of people by how likely they are to agree with the verdict.
- No bulk-send plan.

Three reasons in the figures' own words:

1. **A room does not supply an anchor.** *"An anchor is whatever settles the question and does not change its answer because of what any frame thinks."* (Fig 2 :29). A list of people selected for likely agreement is a room, not a ground.
2. **Correlation disqualifies a verdict.** Fig 2 :40: *"disqualifying for a verdict: agreement about the world is worthless when the agreeing frames share an evidence pool."* Selecting recipients for receptiveness manufactures exactly the correlation that voids their agreement.
3. **A verifier must not hold the interiors.** Fig 10 :42: *"A verifier that can reconstruct the claim cannot verify it."* The first contact carries the residual and its refuter, never the framework.

Legally, scraping personal emails for unsolicited bulk outreach breaches GDPR and CAN-SPAM style rules in many jurisdictions and gets the sending domain blacklisted. That is a practical failure on top of the structural one.
