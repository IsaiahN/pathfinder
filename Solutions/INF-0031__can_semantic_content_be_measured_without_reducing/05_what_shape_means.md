## 1. A shape is a constraint, not a filler

The figures README states the limit: the framework returns *"what a missing thing would have to look like (a shape, not a name)."* Fig 9 :32: *"you get a kind of thing, never the answer itself."* Fig 13 :135: *"It cannot say which primitive is missing, because naming it would mean already holding it."*

From *The Library of Babel* (opened this session): *"you can cut your search short if the set of books returned is small and descriptive enough that it provides a general shape of the answer that satisfies your question."*

So a **shape** is the narrowed form of an answer you can state before you can name it. It constrains; it does not fill. For this question, "any admissible measure must carry the bond, range against a non-updating ground, resolve per slot, and keep bits and actions in separate accounts" is a shape. "Semantic content equals 3.7 bits" is a name, and the framework cannot produce it.

## 2. How a shape is obtained

- *Library of Babel* (opened): use the vocabulary you already have that is *closest* to the thing, then *"With each new term gained, you are able to narrow the space by definition and deepen your vocabulary."* The recursion converges on a shape, not a number.
- Fig 6 :30: *"The description travels. Fails by being sharpened into terms only we can read. Cast it in effect terms."* Fig 9 :30-31: *"your words are borrowed too, so describe the effect rather than the cause."* This is why the audit states the residual as "a reading and what it is about", not as "entropy" or "reward".
- *Decoding Tacit Knowledge* (via exemplar provenance): *"When contact isn't possible, the best you can send is a plain description of what you'd expect to see, because anyone can check that against the world."* The outreach email in `02` does exactly this.
- *Library of Babel* on stopping (opened): *"you will know that you are done because the finish line will also be the starting place, as there is nowhere else to search and nothing else that can describe the answer better."* The stop in `01` is recorded, not declared final.

## 3. What licenses a name

Only two things, both outside the shape itself:

1. **The ground settles it.** `THE_FORMULA.md` step 5: *"the ground settles it. Verified only by what pays. A gate is not the ground."* The figures README: the framework *"stops at the limit of what the available instruments can distinguish, and records the lineage so the stop is honest."*
2. **A provenanced import from a frame whose closure differs**, with a source gate and a shadow test (Fig 6 :23, Fig 8 provenance). Here the import would be an instrument that discriminates sources, not another formula.

*They're Scanning Books When They Should* (via exemplar provenance): *"The real import is not a new term in the existing space. It is a new dimension of the space."* And: *"Declaring the primitives does not make them neutral. It makes the choice visible."* A proposed meaning measure is a visible choice until the ground pays for it.

## 4. The source question: metamerism is the sharpest analogue

*The Hard Problem is About Source* was opened this session. Its core statement: *"If several paths create the same behavior, then behavior is a projection with lossy information."* The color example: an instrument reads green and *"cannot comment on the source"*; the source could be one green light, yellow plus blue, RGB, CMYK, or a scheme not yet discovered. The essay's conclusion: *"It was proven wrong not by argument, but by the arrival of a better instrument."* And: *"An instrument that can't distinguish these cases demonstrates its own inadequacy, it cannot claim anything substantial about what is being measured or what causes it."*

For INF-0031 this is the direct mechanism behind the two reductions:

- A statistical measure is an instrument reading the signal's distribution. Several sources can produce the same distribution. It cannot comment on which source produced it.
- A behavioral measure is an instrument reading the response. Several sources can produce the same response (the vervet case in `04`). It cannot comment on which source produced it.
- What is owed is a source-discriminating instrument, the spectrometer move. This is a **shape for an instrument**, not a name for a new primitive.

This is not retracted by anything in the canonical check; it is the load-bearing analogue for F4 and F7 in `01`.

## 5. The two reductions are not interchangeable (shape for this question)

Fig 12 :126: *"Bits price the description. Actions price finding out whether it holds."* Fig 12 :130: *"Summing them into one figure is the error this names, not the fix for it."*

The shape for the answer to this question is therefore a **four-constraint object**:

| constraint | figure | what it excludes |
|---|---|---|
| carries the bond, not only the ingredients | Fig 12 :5, :94 | flat entropy and flat behavioral scores |
| ranges against an anchor that does not update | Fig 2 :29, :33 | definitions that decide their own verdict |
| resolves per slot, not as a global average | Fig 1 :22 | a single corpus-level "meaning score" |
| keeps bits and actions in separate accounts | Fig 12 :125-130 | weighted sums and unified utility numbers |

Anything satisfying all four is an admissible shape. None of them names a number.

## 6. Prestige is not action, and this audit is a simulation

*The Recursive Self-Improvement Trap* was opened this session: *"Prestige must never be allowed to validate an action directly."* And: *"Prestige can fund an action. It can give an action a budget to be tried. But it cannot settle an action. Only the ground can settle it. The Ground Maintainer must be blind to prestige."* A campaign that gathers prestigious names to agree with the shape claim is the named collapse, not corroboration. The legitimate ask is a budget to be tried and a ground that pays.

Same essay: *"the output is only as good as the validator. The agent, or the tool, or the frame, or the theory, cannot validate itself."* And on LLMs: *"LLMs can quickly simulate an approximate answer/output with the formula found in the architecture post"* but *"the truly testable output would need to be derived from the real architecture or working the formula yourself."* So `01_figure_grounded_audit.md` is a simulated derivation checked for internal consistency against the figures. It is **not** a test of the claim and cannot settle anything it reports on.

## 7. Correction carried forward

*Library of Babel* (opened) also allows a question to be *"a bundle or conflation of several problems masquerading as one"*, which must be *"search[ed] all of those problems individually"* before combining. This corrects an earlier tendency in the audit to ask one question, "is meaning measurable?". The question is a union compound (F1), and the four-constraint shape in §5 applies to the compound's own loop, not to either reduction taken alone. Both the correction and the original framing are recorded here so neither is quietly lost.
