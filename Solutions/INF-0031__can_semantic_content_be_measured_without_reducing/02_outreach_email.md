# Outreach draft: check the shape claim before you decide about the theory

**Use:** one-to-one, or as a cover note attached to a submission through a public venue in `03_public_contact_points.csv`. **Not** for bulk send.

**Hard precondition:** do not send until the shape claim and its refuter in `01_figure_grounded_audit.md` §§3-4 survive your own check. The two-stage offer collapses on contact if stage 1 does not hold.

---

**Subject:** One checkable claim about measuring meaning, before any theory

> Hi [name],
>
> I have one specific output I would like you to check against what you already know. I am not asking you to read a theory first, and I am not asking you to agree with anything. If the output does not survive, there is nothing else to look at.
>
> **The residual, in effect terms.** We have a signal, a world state it is taken to be about, and an agent whose conduct the signal changes. Two candidate measures of its semantic content are on the table: statistical predictability (entropy, compression, logical probability, description length) and behavioral effect (the response evoked, the task role). The question is whether meaning can be measured without collapsing into one of them.
>
> **The shape claim (`general` on the shape, `specified` on the prediction, not `grounded`).** The two candidates are not two versions of one number. They are priced in different currencies, bits for the description and actions for the effect, and a figure-governed rule says they do not add; summing them is the named error, not the fix. What semantic content needs is not a third number averaged from the two but a typed relation (a bond) that the ingredient statistics leave out. A meaning that is declared by stipulation is not thereby grounded, and a behavioral assay is the right anchor for "what did it do" but only an instrument, wrong on axes it does not measure, for "what is it about". So the honest report on a single-number measure is: unreachable with the instruments as they stand. That is a verdict, not a silence. What is owed is a source-discriminating instrument, described in effect terms, not a new reduction.
>
> The figures put it more compactly than I can here: "A composition costs in two currencies, and they do not add" and "Declaring a meaning does not ground it." I will send the exact passages and line numbers only after you have recorded your verdict on the paragraph above.
>
> **What I am not claiming.** Not that meaning is unmeasurable in principle; a stopped search is not a proof of absence. Not that entropy or task success is useless; they are excellent measures of their own quantities and are licensed for model selection and for behavioral questions. Not any number, any threshold, or any unique framework. Not grounded: no measurement I can cite settles this.
>
> **What would refute the shape claim, written down before this message:**
> 1. A single measure whose value is provably not a function of signal statistics and not a function of behavioral effect, and that changes contact by discriminating among sources that produce the same reading.
> 2. A single unit in which bits and actions add without residue.
> 3. A demonstration that the arrangement is fully recoverable from the operand statistics alone.
> 4. A meaning metric grounded by declaration alone, with the ground unmoved.
> 5. A behavioral assay that distinguishes sources behind identical readings without source access.
>
> If any one holds, I will retract the claim. A one-line "this fails because..." is more useful to me than agreement. If you would rather not engage, no reply is needed.
>
> **If it survives, and only then:** I will send the verbatim figure passages with their line numbers, the public figure repository, and the wider literature that bears on this question. The figures, not my summary of them, are the authority.
>
> **If you want to reuse the machinery:** I will send the formula separately. Run it on a problem you know well and can check against a ground. Write down what would refute your result *before* you run it, and state the mode of what you get. A reported failure is more useful to me than a reported success.

**Stage order.** (1) Send the residual, the shape claim and the refuter; record the verdict. (2) Opt-in only: send the verbatim passages, the figure repository and the wider literature. (3) Offer the formula as a method to reuse, with the instruction that each user writes a fresh refuter and states a mode. Later agreement is never pooled as corroboration.
