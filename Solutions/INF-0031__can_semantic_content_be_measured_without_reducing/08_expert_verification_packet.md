# Expert verification packet: the Tether on measuring semantic content

**What this is:** a claim in a form a semanticist, information theorist, cognitive scientist or philosopher of information can check. Mode, derivation and refuters are stated before the conclusion, so a reader knows what to look for and what would settle it.

**Mode: `general` on the shape, `specified` on the falsifiable prediction. Not `grounded`.** No measurement of semantic content is claimed. This is a shape and a route, not a result.

**Standing:** a simulated derivation checked for internal consistency against the figure text, not a ground reading. It cannot settle the question it reports on.

## 1. The claim, in one paragraph

Statistical predictability and behavioral effect are two different currencies, priced in bits and in actions, and a single measure of semantic content cannot be obtained by adding, averaging or choosing between them. What semantic content needs is the typed relation between a signal and its source, the bond that ingredient-level measures leave out; a meaning fixed by stipulation is not thereby grounded; and a behavioral assay is the right anchor for conduct but only an instrument, with blind axes, for aboutness. The honest report on a single-number measure is `unreachable with the instruments as they stand`, a verdict and not a silence. What is owed is a source-discriminating instrument described in effect terms. Any admissible measure must carry the bond, range against a non-updating anchor, resolve per slot, and keep bits and actions in separate accounts.

## 2. Three checkable sub-claims

### C1: framework-internal; checkable by reading Figures 12 and 9

- **Statement:** the question is a union compound, and its two candidate measures are incommensurable currencies. Summing them is the named error.
- **Source, verbatim:** Fig 12 :125, *"A composition costs in two currencies, and they do not add."* Fig 12 :126, *"Bits price the description. Actions price finding out whether it holds."* Fig 12 :130, *"Summing them into one figure is the error this names, not the fix for it."* Fig 9 :57, *"a case in two conditions is not a harder case of either."*
- **Refuter:** exhibit a single unit in which bits and actions add without residue, or show that the two conditions are one condition whose frames share a single evidence pool.

### C2: framework-internal with a world-facing check; checkable by reading Figures 12 and 13 and the literature on semantic information

- **Statement:** meaning is carried by the bond, not by the ingredient statistics, and a declared meaning is not grounded. A measure that prices only the signal's distribution records the operands and misses the relation.
- **Source, verbatim:** Fig 12 :5, *"A set of ingredients is not one. The bond is the rest of the information, and it is the half nothing was recording."* Fig 12 :94, *"The arrangement is the substance."* Fig 13 :26, *"it decides what the measurement would mean."* Fig 10 :49, *"Declaring a meaning does not ground it."*
- **What to check in the literature:** the Bar-Hillel and Carnap quantitative theory of semantic information is an inverse logical-probability measure; it assigns infinite information to a contradiction (Stanford Encyclopedia of Philosophy, *Semantic Conceptions of Information*, opened 2026-11-23). The 6G semantic-communications survey reports that such measures *"have no concerns with the essence of SI, the meaning factor"* (Yang et al., arXiv 2207.00427, opened 2026-11-23). Does this match the claim that the statistical route, even relabelled, prices rarity rather than aboutness?
- **Refuter:** show the arrangement is fully recoverable from operand statistics alone, or exhibit a meaning metric grounded by declaration alone with the ground unmoved.

### C3: world-facing; checkable against the animal-signalling and source-discrimination cases

- **Statement:** a behavioral assay is a legitimate anchor for "what did it do" and an instrument, wrong on axes it does not measure, for "what is it about". Several sources can produce the same reading, so behavior is a lossy projection.
- **Source, verbatim:** Fig 2 :29, *"An anchor is whatever settles the question and does not change its answer because of what any frame thinks."* Fig 2 :38, *"instruments can be wrong on axes they do not measure."* Fig 1 :22, *"Aggregating across slots is how a live signal disappears into an average."*
- **What to check:** in the vervet alarm-call case (Price et al. 2015, Scientific Reports; Fischer 2017, Psychonomic Bulletin and Review, both opened), playback response is the behavioral assay, and the original authors cautioned that *"There are limits to how far a semantic analysis can be carried out when it is based solely on the responses that those signals evoke."* In the color case (*The Hard Problem is About Source*, opened), an instrument reads green and cannot comment on the source; the spectrometer, not an argument, separated the sources.
- **Refuter:** exhibit a behavioral assay that distinguishes sources behind identical readings without source access, or show a source-discriminating instrument is unnecessary for the semantic question.

## 3. The stop and what would reverse it

- **Statement:** a single-number measure is unreachable with the instruments as they stand; this is not a proof of impossibility. What is owed is a source-discriminating instrument shaped in effect terms, not a named primitive.
- **Source, verbatim:** Fig 13 :134, *"Unreachable is a verdict here, not a silence"*. Fig 8 :39, *"So a search that finds nothing is never a proof of absence."* Fig 13 :135, *"It cannot say which primitive is missing, because naming it would mean already holding it."*
- **Refuter:** produce the instrument and show that it changes contact; that moves the verdict to `grounded` for this question.

## 4. What is explicitly not claimed

- Not that semantic content is unmeasurable in principle.
- Not that entropy, mutual information, MDL or task measures are invalid. They are licensed for their own quantities, and Fig 5 :45 puts MDL *"to work on model selection."*
- Not a third number, not a weighted sum, not a universal utility axis.
- Not a name for the missing primitive, and not a derivation of the framework from the literature.
- Not grounded. No experiment or instrument was read that settles semantic measurement.
- Not a validation of the framework by this packet. A frame cannot certify its own limit.
- Not a claim about subjective meaning in any animal or machine.

## 6. What an expert can check in twenty minutes

1. Read Fig 12 :125, :126, :130 and Fig 9 :57, four short passages. Decide whether "bits and actions do not add" is a structural claim or a rhetorical one.
2. Check C2 against the Bar-Hillel and Carnap measure: does an inverse-probability measure, even one called semantic, price rarity rather than aboutness? Name one counter-case if not.
3. Check C3 against a case you know: playback response, machine task success, or metameric color matching. Does the readout discriminate the source?
4. Try the source-discrimination test: construct two signals with identical statistics and identical effects but different sources. If you can build an instrument that separates them, send that instead of an endorsement.
5. Send the refutation. A failure is more useful than agreement.
