# The Tether's answer: can semantic content be measured without reduction?

**Mode: `general` on the structural shape, `specified` on the falsifiable prediction. Not `grounded`.** Simulated derivation checked for internal consistency against the figures, not a ground reading (`05_what_shape_means.md` §6).

## 1. The residual it answers

We hold a signal, a world state it is taken to be about, and an agent whose conduct the signal changes. Two measures present themselves: statistical predictability (bits, compression, logical probability, MDL) and behavioral effect (response, task role, functional contribution). The worry is that both price something other than meaning. What failed is the passage from a reading to what the reading is about. That passage is a **union compound** (Fig 9 :57: *"a case in two conditions is not a harder case of either"*), not one hard problem, so it needs its own loop.

## 2. The shape

1. **The two candidates are two currencies, not two versions of one number.** Fig 12 :125: *"A composition costs in two currencies, and they do not add."* Fig 12 :126: *"Bits price the description. Actions price finding out whether it holds."* Fig 12 :130: *"Summing them into one figure is the error this names, not the fix for it."* A weighted average of entropy and task success is the named error.
2. **Meaning is carried by the bond, not by the ingredients.** Fig 12 :5: *"A set of ingredients is not one. The bond is the rest of the information, and it is the half nothing was recording."* Fig 12 :94: *"The arrangement is the substance."* Fig 13 :26: *"it decides what the measurement would mean."* Signal statistics price the operands. The typed relation to a source is what they leave out.
3. **A declared meaning is not a grounded one.** Fig 10 :18: *"a declared meaning is not a grounded one"*; Fig 10 :49: *"Declaring a meaning does not ground it."* A measure that fixes meaning by stipulation has promoted a convention to a verdict (Fig 10 :47). It can still coordinate agreement about a shared meaning, which Fig 2 :41 calls *"efficient for a convention"*, but it does not decide the question.
4. **Behavior is a legitimate anchor for conduct and an instrument, with blind axes, for aboutness.** Fig 2 :37-38: human judgment is the right anchor when the question is about people; *"instruments can be wrong on axes they do not measure."* The vervet case in `06` supplies the check: playback response is a sound behavioral assay and the original authors cautioned that semantic analysis based solely on responses is limited.
5. **The single-number measure is unreachable with the instruments as they stand.** Fig 13 :134: *"Unreachable is a verdict here, not a silence"*; Fig 8 :39: *"So a search that finds nothing is never a proof of absence."* This is a report of a stop, not a proof that meaning is unmeasurable in principle. What is owed is an instrument that discriminates sources behind identical readings, described in effect terms (Fig 6 :34), not a new reduction and not a named primitive (Fig 13 :135).
6. **Any proxy carries a nonzero round-trip gap.** Fig 4 :31: *"The gap is not an error to eliminate. It is the measurement of what the coarser description cannot hold."* The losses here are the bond, the source behind a reading, and the unit difference between bits and actions. Fig 1 :22: *"Aggregating across slots is how a live signal disappears into an average."*

So the admissible shape has four constraints: carry the bond, range against a non-updating anchor, resolve per slot, and keep bits and actions in separate accounts. `06_isomers_and_order.md` gives the supporting structural derivation; `08_expert_verification_packet.md` turns the shape into checkable sub-claims.

## 3. What it explicitly does not say

- Not that semantic content cannot be measured. A stopped search is not a proof of absence (Fig 8 :39). The verdict is `unreachable with the instruments as they stand`.
- Not that entropy, mutual information, MDL or task success are invalid. They are excellent measures of their own quantities. Fig 5 :45 licenses MDL *"put to work on model selection"*, and this is kept as a filter, never a witness (Fig 9 :60).
- Not a third number, not a weighted sum, not a universal utility axis.
- Not a name for the missing primitive. Fig 13 :135 forbids it: *"naming it would mean already holding it."*
- Not grounded, and not a validation of the framework. Fig 8 rule 2: a frame cannot certify its own limit.
- Not a claim about what any particular animal or machine subjectively means.

## 4. What would settle it: the ground

A source-discriminating instrument, not a further argument. From *The Hard Problem is About Source* (opened this session): an instrument that reads green *"cannot comment on the source"*; the spectrometer, not an argument, resolved the case. Here the corresponding tests are:

- **Source-discrimination test:** two signals with identical statistics and identical behavioral effects, different sources. Does any instrument separate them? If yes, the shape claim is refuted at F4/F6.
- **Currency test:** is there a single unit in which bits and actions add without residue? If yes, F1 fails.
- **Bond test:** is the arrangement recoverable from the operand statistics alone? If yes, the flat formula is lossless and F2 fails.
- **Stipulation test:** does a declared meaning metric change with the ground and not with the stipulation? If yes, F3 fails.
- Standing falsifiers from `01` §4, all written before this conclusion.

## 5. The honest stop

**Unreached, not unreachable in principle.** The instrument is described in effect terms only. The six-term substrate and the figure set are a visible guess, not nature's own list; *"Declaring the primitives does not make them neutral. It makes the choice visible"* (*They're Scanning Books*, via exemplar provenance). The answer stops at the limit of what the available instruments can distinguish and records the lineage so the stop is checkable. *"Indiscernibility is not identity."* Prestige or agreement is not an instrument: *"Prestige can fund an action... but it cannot settle an action"* (*The Recursive Self-Improvement Trap*, opened).

## 6. One-paragraph version for outreach

> The Tether does not produce a number for semantic content, and it does not claim meaning is unmeasurable. It says that statistical predictability and behavioral effect are two different currencies, priced in bits and in actions, and that summing them is the named error rather than the fix. Meaning is carried by the typed relation between a signal and its source, the bond that ingredient-level measures leave out, and a meaning declared by stipulation is not thereby grounded. A behavioral assay is the right anchor for "what did it do" but only an instrument, with blind axes, for "what is it about". So the honest report on a single-number measure is unreachable with the instruments as they stand, which is a verdict and not a silence. What is owed is a source-discriminating instrument described in effect terms, not another reduction. What the framework offers is the four-constraint shape any admissible measure must have, falsifiers written in advance, and the tests that would close it. The center stays open until the ground settles it.
