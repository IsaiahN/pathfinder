# Are the answer forms isomers? Union, structure and order

**Mode: `specified`.** This is a simulated derivation checked against the figures, not a ground reading (see `05_what_shape_means.md` §6).

## RESIDUAL

The main audit concludes that a single monetised scalar and a three-loop answer are structurally different, and that the three conditions form a union. Two questions remain:

1. Are the two answer forms **isomers** under Fig 12's definition, or merely different amounts of the same thing?
2. In what **order** are the repairs owed, and which of them can be done inside the existing closure first?

## DOMAIN CENSUS

Atoms in the CBA library: affected persons, effects, money amounts, probabilities, a time index, and a discount rate. Bonds available: a symmetric `+` over effects and persons, and a discount transform over time. Atoms in the wider problem: distributional incidence, contested conceptual models, unknown or disputed weights over outcomes, non-market values, and the sources of a valuation reading. The latter are not in the CBA library; they are candidates for import. No new universal metric is available without import.

## DERIVATION

### Q1 — Are the two answer forms isomers?

- Fig 12 :117: **"isomer. Same atoms, different bond, different substance. The difference is invisible in the formula and decisive in the behaviour."**
- Fig 12 :94: **"The arrangement is the substance."**
- Fig 12 :92: **"Symmetry is what decides whether an arrangement is a new thing."**

Both forms can be written with the same ingredients: the same affected people, the same physical effects, the same money amounts where money applies. They differ in the bond. The CBA form joins effects and persons under a symmetric `+`, so that swapping two recipients leaves the term unchanged. The three-loop form keeps recipients as slots, keeps futures as separate readings, and keeps currencies apart. Under Fig 12 :92 that is a different term, not a more detailed spelling of the same one. They are **isomers**: same atoms, different arrangement, different behaviour in the face of a decision.

This is why "just add the unmonetised effects to the spreadsheet as a sensitivity row" fails. A sensitivity row is still a symmetric sum on the same page; it does not change the bond, so it does not change the substance.

- **Falsifier, written before the run:** if the two forms can be shown to have the same bond structure (that the three-loop form is just the weighted sum written out in more rows), the isomer claim fails and the difference is only one of presentation. Triangulated for the figure definition (Fig 12), single-source for the application to these two forms.

### Q2 — What order are the repairs owed?

- Fig 9 :54: **"And when the split lands on a union rather than a partition, unbundle before you search again."**
- Fig 8 :49: **"more search at the same reach: is it in here and unfound? The closure does not change. Same atoms, same wall, more of the space actually visited."**
- Fig 12 :104: **"A settled molecule becomes an atom for whatever composes over it. So irreducible is a fact about a level and never about the world."**
- Fig 6 :31: **"Neither answer tells you much about the other. Run them as two tracks, and never read one against the other."**

The dependency order is:

1. **Unbundle** the three conditions before choosing a frame (Fig 9 :54). Otherwise any frame will seem to fit.
2. **SEARCHED first** (Fig 8 :49). Distributional analysis with an already agreed weight and sensitivity analysis over already agreed probabilities are inside the CBA closure. They are cheap and they are owed before any import.They are filters on the budget, not witnesses for the verdict (Fig 9 :60).
3. **Refuse before mint.** Where a held regularity is false (for example, "a single WTP reading identifies the source of value"), `THE_FORMULA.md` step 2 owes a refusal, not an explanation. A refusal is a negation, not a bond (Operators table :70-78).
4. **Then INWARD and OUTWARD as two separate tracks** (Fig 6 :29-30, :31). INWARD: improve the valuation instrument from one already returning something. OUTWARD: find a frame whose closure holds robustness or a non-money currency. Never read one against the other.
5. **Settle each loop before composing** (Fig 12 :104). Only a distributional reading settled against the people it describes, or a robustness reading settled against the futures it spans, can become an atom for the final decision.

- **Falsifier:** if a repair can be shown to be owed before the unbundling (that is, if a single lever reliably clears all three conditions at once), the ordering claim fails. A single lever would make the conditions a partition, not a union.

### Q3 — Why "two currencies do not add" is not a plea for a third currency

- Fig 12 :125: **"A composition costs in two currencies, and they do not add."**
- Fig 12 :130: **"Summing them into one figure is the error this names, not the fix for it."**
- Fig 12 :111: **"A vocabulary without bonds can name a state and cannot name a route to one."**

The tempting repair is an "adjusted social BCR" that converts the distributional and non-monetary readings back into money. That is summing the currencies, the exact error Fig 12 :130 names. It also fails Q1: it restores the symmetric `+` bond and therefore destroys the arrangement that carried the information. What the answer needs is not a third currency but a grammar that can state three separate readings and refuse a composite one.

- **Falsifier:** exhibit a third unit in which distribution, robustness and non-market value become commensurable without loss, verified against a ground rather than asserted by construction.

### Q4 — Union or partition, restated

- Fig 9 :55: **"A bundled problem is usually a union of overlapping conditions, not a list of separate ones."**
- Fig 9 :57: **"What remains is smaller and it is also categorically different: a case in two conditions is not a harder case of either."**
- Fig 13 :115: **"The set is closed. The arrangements are not. That gap is why import exists."**

A partition would mean each condition is cleared by the same missing atom. It is not: the instrument that discriminates the source of a WTP reading says nothing about contested probabilities, and a robustness criterion says nothing about who bears the cost. The three conditions overlap but are not interchangeable, so the problem is a union and one lever does not clear it. Fig 13 :115 explains why searching inside money-only CBA cannot produce the missing atoms.

- **Falsifier, written before the run:** if all three residuals are shown to persist and trend down under a single internal refinement of the money atom, the union reading fails and the partition reading stands.

## CONCLUSION

The two answer forms are **isomers**, not different resolutions of one scalar: the ingredients can be the same, the bond is different, and the difference is decisive in behaviour (Fig 12 :117, :94, :92). The repairs are owed in a fixed dependency order: unbundle, SEARCHED, refuse, then INWARD and OUTWARD as separate tracks, then settle each loop before composing. No third universal currency is owed; a composite score would reinstate the symmetric bond that lost the information in the first place. The one live strain is that Fig 8 :40-43 leaves underivable and budget-unreachable empirically distinguishable for a specific decision, so the order is `specified`, not `grounded`.
