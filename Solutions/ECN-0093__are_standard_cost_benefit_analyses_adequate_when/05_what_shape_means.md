## 1. A shape is a constraint, not a filler

The figures README states the limit: the framework returns *"what a missing thing would have to look like (a shape, not a name)."* Fig 9 :32: *"you get a kind of thing, never the answer itself."* `60_FIGURES_AS_METALAWS.md`: *"what it returns is a shape rather than a name"* when a system with a closure answers a decidability question before it searches.

From *The Library of Babel*: *"you can cut your search short if the set of books returned is small and descriptive enough that it provides a general shape of the answer that satisfies your question."*

So a **shape** is the narrowed form of an answer you can state before you can name it. It constrains; it does not fill. For this question the shape is: an answer to a decision must register the person, the future state and the non-market effect separately, and no single money scalar has the right form to do that. The shape does not name a replacement metric, a particular welfare weight, or a specific discount rate.

## 2. How the shape is obtained

- *Library of Babel*: use the vocabulary already held that is closest to the thing, then look for recurring new terms that overlap or correlate with it, recursively. *"With each new term gained, you are able to narrow the space by definition and deepen your vocabulary."* Applied here: start from the effect terms already in the question (uncertain, unequal, hard to value) and let the figure lines narrow each one.
- Fig 6 :30: *"The description travels. Fails by being sharpened into terms only we can read. Cast it in effect terms."* Fig 9 :30-31: *"your words are borrowed too, so describe the effect rather than the cause."* So describe the residual as a loss of signal, not as a flaw in a particular school of economics.
- *Decoding Tacit Knowledge*: *"When contact isn't possible, the best you can send is a plain description of what you'd expect to see, because anyone can check that against the world."* The outreach file therefore sends a claim and a refuter, not a theory.
- *Library of Babel* on bundles: *"Perhaps your problem was a bundle or conflation of several problems masquerading as one? You will need to search all of those problems individually."* This is the essay-level version of Fig 9 :54-58 and it is exactly the three-condition structure of ECN-0093.
- *How Intelligence Actually Works*: *"The recursion converges toward indiscernibility, and indiscernibility is not identity."* A shape can become indistinguishable from an answer to an expert with the right instruments. That does not make it the answer, and the lineage must be kept.

## 3. What licenses a name

Two things, both outside the shape itself:

1. **The ground settles it.** `THE_FORMULA.md` step 5: *"the ground settles it. Verified only by what pays."* The README: the framework *"stops at the limit of what the available instruments can distinguish, and records the lineage so the stop is honest."* For CBA, the ground is a particular decision's realized consequences, not the internal consistency of the appraisal.
2. **A provenanced import from a frame whose closure differs** (Fig 6 :23, Fig 8 :50-52), with a source gate and a shadow test. A distributional weight, a robustness criterion, or a non-monetary valuation becomes usable when a checker can see where it came from and what gap it explains.

*They're Scanning Books* sharpens the second point: *"The real import is not a new term in the existing space. It is a new dimension of the space."* Importing a term extends what can be said; importing a dimension extends what can be perceived. The three conditions here ask for imports of the second kind, because money-only CBA cannot represent them at all, not merely represent them badly.

The same essay adds: *"A minimum viable set of six terms is a human's careful guess at reality's compression axes; another sensorium's minimum set need not be those six."* Declared primitives are a visible choice, not a neutral discovery. The choice of money as the single currency of a CBA is likewise a visible choice, and this solution records it as such rather than treating it as nature's unit.

## 4. Why a single number is the specific forbidden error here

*The Hard Problem is About Source* gives the mechanism. An instrument reads green and *"cannot comment on the source"*; the same reading can come from one green light, or yellow plus blue, or RGB, or CMYK. The essay's general statement is: *"If several paths create the same behavior, then behavior is a projection with lossy information (it cannot define consciousness in full)."* It was the **spectrometer**, not an argument, that resolved the case.

A CBA total is the same kind of projection. The same NPSV can come from a policy that helps the poor and hurts the rich, or the reverse; from a best-estimate future or from a fragile one; from a real preference or from a framing effect. The number does not say which. What is owed is a discriminator, not a more confident number.

*How a Society of Agents Would Work* supplies the second half: *"capability is a property of agent-and-habitat, never of the agent alone."* An appraisal is not made adequate by improving the analyst in isolation; adequacy is a property of the appraisal method together with the decision habitat it ranges against. Adding a fourth decimal place to a discount rate does not change contact with the people or the future.

*The Recursive Self-Improvement Trap*: *"the output is only as good as the validator. The agent, or the tool, or the frame, or the theory, cannot validate itself."* A BCR cannot certify the adequacy of the method that produced it.

## 5. Answer to the question this file owes

**What would an adequate answer look like, before we name one?** It would have three outputs, not one: a per-person distributional reading, a robustness reading over plausible futures, and a separate statement of effects that money cannot price, with `unreachable` declared where no discriminating instrument exists. It would record the provenance of every imported weight or criterion. It would state which part is settled by a ground and which part is `candidate`.

**Does the present answer commit the forbidden error?** It commits the opposite error from the record in the exemplar. The exemplar named a structure where the framework returns a shape. This solution refuses to name a replacement metric where the figures return a shape. It does assert three structural constraints that are figure-derived (per-slot residual, held-apart currencies, union unbundling), and it states their mode as `specified`. That is a shape claim, not a name claim.

## 5b. Correction to my own §3 and §4

I first wrote that "the framework returns a shape, not a name" forbids attaching any specific method to a slot. That overstates the rule, exactly as the exemplar did in the opposite direction. The README's phrase governs a *missing* thing: *"what a missing thing would have to look like (a shape, not a name)."* Distributional weights, robustness criteria and deliberative valuation are not missing primitives of the framework; they are bindings from frames the CBA frame does not currently hold. Naming them is permitted, provided the provenance is recorded and no claim is made that the figures derived them. What remains forbidden is inventing a seventh money-like universal metric and calling it the answer. Both readings are kept here so neither is quietly lost.

Correction two: I first treated the Kahneman and Knetsch result as showing that WTP is *merely* an attitude. *They're Scanning Books* and *The Hard Problem is About Source* both insist the reading underdetermines the source, not that it is always wrong. The honest form is: a WTP number is an instrument that cannot comment on its source, and a discriminating instrument is owed. That is weaker and more defensible than "WTP is meaningless."

## 6. Two consequences for outreach

**Prestige is not action.** From *The Recursive Self-Improvement Trap*: *"Prestige must never be allowed to validate an action directly"* and *"Prestige can fund an action. It can give an action a budget to be tried. But it cannot settle an action. Only the ground can settle it. The Ground Maintainer must be blind to prestige."* A list of famous economists prepared to agree with the shape claim is the named collapse, not corroboration. The legitimate ask is a budget to try the claim against a decision's ground.

**This audit is a simulation, not a ground reading.** Same essay: *"LLMs can quickly simulate an approximate answer/output"* and *"the truly testable output would need to be derived from the real architecture or working the formula yourself."* The Arrival of the Fittest article adds the caveat that outputs *"could never be verified if the solutions presented were not contrived to fit the formula."* So `01` and this file are simulated derivations checked for internal consistency against the figures. They are **not** a test of the claim and must be labelled as such. They cannot settle anything they report on.

**The framework cannot validate itself.** From Arrival of the Fittest: *"Because it is a metatheory, it cannot be validated, nor can it validate itself."* So `09_watson_answer.md` records the verdict as `specified`, `candidate`, and names the instruments that would move it to `grounded`.
