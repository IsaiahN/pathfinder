## Round trip gap R_T

**R_T is nonzero.** Four things the shorthand holds do not survive the trip back down without additional work:

- **which condition applies to a given decision.** Underivable from the closure and unreachable at this budget are different facts with different instructions (Fig 8 :40-43); the shorthand compresses them into "not adequate here".
- **what the imported weight or criterion actually is.** The shape constrains it; it does not name it. A reader who takes the shorthand as naming a method has read a name into a shape.
- **whether a discriminating valuation instrument exists.** The shorthand says "report unreachable"; whether that is `unreachable at this budget` or `underivable in principle` needs an instrument check in the specific case.
- **the provenance of each import.** Convergent derivation and adopted import look identical in the contents (Fig 8 :50-52); the shorthand drops the distinction, so it must be restored at concretisation.

Conversely, one thing survives cleanly: the per-slot residual, the two-currency rule and the union structure. That is the residue worth sending. R_T was **not** measured numerically because no ground reading exists; it is recorded qualitatively as the enumerated set of dropped constraints. Treating "the formula is the condensed version of the metalaws" as identity would set R_T to zero and would be the dead-reckoning error Fig 4 :30 names.
