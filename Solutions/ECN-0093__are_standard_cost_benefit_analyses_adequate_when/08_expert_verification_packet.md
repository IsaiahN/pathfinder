# Expert verification packet — the Tether on cost-benefit adequacy

**What this is:** three claims in a form a researcher in cost-benefit analysis can check. Mode, derivation and refuters are stated before the conclusion, so a reader knows what to look for and what would settle it. This is the one to send.

**Mode: `specified` on the three sub-claims, `general` on the framing. Not `grounded`.** No measurement is claimed to settle adequacy for a particular decision. This is a shape and a route, not a result.

## 1. The claim, in one paragraph

A standard CBA, read as a single discounted monetised net benefit, is adequate only inside a closure where effects are commensurable in money, reversible, and not distributed in a way that matters. Outside that closure the residual is a union of three conditions, each with its own loop: distribution across persons, deep uncertainty about models and weights, and effects with no money price. The money-only frame cannot add the missing atoms by refinement, so each condition needs an import whose provenance is recorded. The three readings are held apart rather than summed into one adjusted score. The framework returns this shape, not a named replacement metric, and reports `unreachable` where no discriminating instrument exists.

## 2. Three checkable sub-claims

### C1 — Framework-internal; checkable by reading Figure 1 and Figure 12

- **Statement:** aggregating per-person effects under a symmetric plus sign destroys the distributional signal, so a single scalar cannot be adequate for unequal effects.
- **Source, verbatim:** Fig 1 :19 *"The residual is indexed by object slot, not aggregated."* Fig 1 :22 *"Aggregating across slots is how a live signal disappears into an average."* Fig 12 :14 *"the ingredients are recorded and the arrangement is not"* and Fig 12 :92 *"Symmetry is what decides whether an arrangement is a new thing."*
- **What to check:** is the plus over persons in a standard CBA really symmetric in the figure's sense (swapping two recipients leaves the term unchanged), and does the total therefore lose the identity of the recipient? Does any standard CBA already record incidence as a first-class output rather than as a supplement? If it does, mark the boundary accordingly; the claim is about what the single scalar carries, not about what a full appraisal report may append.
- **Refuter:** show a money-only CBA that registers each person's stake without importing a distributional weight from a frame whose closure differs, or show that reported values are invariant to income and framing.

### C2 — Framework-internal; checkable by reading Figure 6 and Figure 8

- **Statement:** calculable risk is inside the money-only closure, but deep uncertainty is not, and refining the probability or discount atom cannot import it.
- **Source, verbatim:** Fig 6 :29 *"inward: is our representation adequate to hold it? The instrument extends access. Fails by refining forever on an axis that cannot carry it. Sharpen in our own terms."* Fig 8 :40 *"Underivable-in-principle and unreachable-at-this-budget are"* continued :41 *"different facts, and they issue different instructions."* Fig 8 :49 *"more search at the same reach: is it in here and unfound? The closure does not change. Same atoms, same wall, more of the space actually visited."*
- **What to check:** does the figure's inward/outward distinction map onto the risk/deep-uncertainty distinction used in appraisal practice? Is the phrase "refining forever on an axis that cannot carry it" a fair description of repeated discount-rate and sensitivity refinements under contested models, or does it misdescribe a practice that already handles this through scenario work inside the same method?
- **Refuter:** show a single probability distribution over money outcomes that registers a case where the conceptual model and the weights over outcomes are disputed, with no decision-relevant information lost.

### C3 — Framework-internal; checkable by reading Figure 12 and Figure 2

- **Statement:** money and non-money value are different currencies; summing them into one score is the named error, and a WTP number is an instrument that cannot comment on its own source.
- **Source, verbatim:** Fig 12 :125 *"A composition costs in two currencies, and they do not add."* Fig 12 :130 *"Summing them into one figure is the error this names, not the fix for it."* Fig 2 :37-38 *"instruments can be wrong on axes they do not measure."*
- **What to check:** does the two-currency rule apply to appraisal units as claimed, or is it a claim about description bits and action costs that does not transfer? Is the instrument/source point refuted by a valuation method that demonstrably discriminates among the sources of the same reading? The standing world evidence against the claim here is the contingent-valuation literature on embedding and attitude; the standing evidence for it is any instrument that passes a discrimination test.
- **Refuter:** exhibit a money reading that is invariant to framing and that discriminates among the sources of the same reading, or show that quantities in different units can be added without loss against a ground.

## 3. What is explicitly not claimed

- Not that CBA is useless, nor that a BCR is never informative. Inside its closure it is the right instrument.
- Not that a BCR can certify the method that produced it. Fig 1 :27: *"They are frame-internal. A frame cannot score itself with a quantity it also produces."*
- Not a named replacement metric, welfare weight, discount rate, robustness criterion or valuation method. The framework returns *"a kind of thing, never the answer itself"* (Fig 9 :32).
- Not `grounded`. No measurement in this packet settles a particular project.
- Not a claim that WTP is meaningless. It is an instrument that underdetermines its source until a discriminating instrument exists.
- Not a theory of everything. The three loops stay separate; no final composite metric closes the question.

## 5. What would settle it

1. **Distribution:** a decision where per-person incidence is measured and weighted and unweighted results are both displayed, so the effect of the weight is visible to a checker.
2. **Uncertainty:** a case where competing system models are stated, plausible futures enumerated, and performance measured across them rather than at one best estimate.
3. **Valuation:** a source-discriminating instrument, in the sense of *The Hard Problem is About Source*, where the instrument that reads green *"cannot comment on the source"* was replaced by the spectrometer.
4. The three pre-registered refuters in §2, any one of which withdraws the corresponding sub-claim.

## 6. What an expert can check in twenty minutes

1. Read Fig 1 :19 and :22, Fig 6 :29, Fig 8 :49, Fig 12 :92, :125 and :130. Seven short passages.
2. Say which of C1, C2 and C3 is most likely to be false in their own field, and name one instrument that would test it.
3. Check whether their own standard appraisal already outputs per-person incidence, multi-future robustness and a non-monetised effects table. If it does, say whether those outputs are carried by the headline scalar or appended beside it. That is the boundary the packet claims.
4. Send the refutation. A failure is more useful than an endorsement. If you would rather not engage, no reply is needed.
