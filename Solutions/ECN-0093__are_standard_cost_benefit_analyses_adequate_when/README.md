## What is deliberately not here

- No personal emails of named individuals.
- No ranking of people by likelihood of agreeing with the verdict.
- No bulk-send plan.

Three reasons, in the figures' own words:

1. **A room does not upgrade the label.** *"A room reaching a conclusion without the ground is agreement, and agreement among frames is a weighted average of influence. Adding people raises the vocabulary and does not add an anchor."* (Seat Map :283)
2. **Selecting for agreement manufactures the disqualifying correlation.** *"High correlation is disqualifying for a verdict and efficient for a convention."* (Seat Map :163) A list ranked by likely receptiveness is a list selected to agree.
3. **Trust before checking spends independence.** *"A verifier that can reconstruct the claim cannot verify it."* (Fig 10 :42) The first contact carries the residual and the refuter, not the framework's interior.

Legally: scraping personal emails for unsolicited bulk outreach breaches GDPR and CAN-SPAM style rules in many jurisdictions. Public opt-in channels avoid that failure on top of the structural one.

## Honest standing of the whole folder

This is a **simulated derivation checked for internal consistency against the figures, not a ground reading.** *The Recursive Self-Improvement Trap* calls LLM output *"simulacra"* and says *"the truly testable output would need to be derived from the real architecture or working the formula yourself."* The package cannot settle the question it reports on. It offers a shape, refuters written in advance, and the names of the instruments that would close it.
