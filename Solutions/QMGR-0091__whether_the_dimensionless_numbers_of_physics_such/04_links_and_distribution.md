## What the world literature bears on this question

1. **The anchor is real and precise.** CODATA values and Uzan's 2025 review show that these numbers are measured with increasing accuracy and that their stability is an active experimental programme. This is the ground any common-origin claim must range against. It does not decide the origin.
2. **Partial compression exists, full compression does not.** GUTs reduce independent inputs and predict mass relations and near-unification of couplings, but most mass relations fail and no GUT is generally accepted. Eddington onward, attempts to derive the constants *"have yet produced convincing results."* This is corroboration for F3 (the bargain is not yet paid), not a verdict on whether it can be.
3. **There are two candidate mechanisms, not one.** Derivation (GUTs) and selection (landscape plus anthropic) both claim to explain the values. The framework splits them into composition (Fig 6, Fig 12) and habitat selection (Fig 11). The landscape's scientific status is contested (Gross: unfalsifiable or premature), which is a reason to demand provenance, not a reason to declare it false.
4. **The count itself is frame-dependent.** 19, 25, 26 and 31 appear in the sources read. Under the figures this is partly a notation question (F5), which is why "how many constants" is not the same as "do they share an origin."

Overlap is declared: the four essays share an author with the figures, so their agreement with the figure verdict is expected and carries little independent weight. The Wikipedia, Baez, Duff, GUT, Tegmark et al. and Uzan sources are independent of the framework and are used as corroboration only, never to overrule a figure line.

- **Fig 4 :22-25** — *"Only methods cross up. Only head starts come down. **Recommended order in a reply:** (1) the union and level claims with the refuter; (2) ask what they would count as a failure; (3) record that verdict; (4) only then the local figure links, with the note that the figures, not the summary, are the authority.

## The two-stage offer

- **Stage 1 before stage 2** is Fig 10 :52, *"Bring a characterised residual, never the source,"* and Fig 2 :45, *"Frames range against the ground. They do not negotiate with it."* The recipient checks the claim without holding the framework's interior.
- **Stage 2 is opt-in by construction.** Once a recipient has absorbed the theory, their agreement stops being evidence. Their stage-1 verdict must be recorded before any framework material is shown and is never pooled as corroboration.
- **Stage 3 sends a method, which is permitted.** Fig 4 :22 — *"Only methods cross up. Only head starts come down."* The formula is a generator, so giving it away does not give away an answer.
- **Stage 3 still needs a ground per problem.** `THE_FORMULA.md` "Direction": a frame *"cannot check the direction of its own question."* Each user must write down what would refute their result before running it and label the mode of what they get.
