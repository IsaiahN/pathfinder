# The Tether's answer: do the dimensionless constants have a common origin?

**Mode: `specified` on the structural claims, `general` on the shape, not `grounded`.** A simulated derivation checked against the figures (`05_what_shape_means.md` section 6), not a ground reading. No measurement of a constant was taken, and no common origin is asserted or denied.

## 1. The residual it answers

We hold measured pure numbers (alpha, the proton-to-electron mass ratio, the strong coupling, quark and lepton masses, mixing angles, the cosmological constant). Each is settled by an instrument, yet each is a free slot in the theory that contains it. "Common origin" asks whether those slots are independent atoms or compositions of one generative frame. Option A (yes) leads to a derivation or a selection story that reduces the input count. Option B (no) leads to accepting them as empirical facts. The figures decide neither option directly. They decide what a legitimate answer to the question must look like.

## 2. Domain census (one line each, kept separate)

- **A, measurement.** Actors: CODATA, NIST, clocks, colliders, surveys. Currency: values with uncertainty. Anchor: the measurement. Verdict: every constant is settled here; an origin claim must range against it, not negotiate with it (Fig 2 :29, :45).
- **B, theory.** Actors: couplings, masses, gauge groups, vacua. Currency: predictions and parameter count. Anchor: the values from A. Verdict: partial compression exists (mass relations, near coupling unification) but full compression is not established; no generally accepted GUT and no settled selection measure.
- **C, meta/description.** Actors: six terms, seven bonds, `closure(Γ)`, instruments. Currency: description length and settlement. Anchor: verbatim figures. Verdict: the framework returns a shape, not a name, and an honest unreached stop.

These verdicts are never averaged into one.

## 3. The shape, one loop per domain

**Loop A (the anchor).** Each number is settled against the ground. Fig 2 :29: *"An anchor is whatever settles the question and does not change its answer because of what any frame thinks."* So a common origin cannot move the measured values; it must predict them. Domain A verdict: the anchor stands, and any origin is a claim about the pattern of these numbers, not a revision of them.

**Loop B (composition and selection).** Two mechanisms, not one. Derivation is a composition question (Fig 6 :21: *"MINT composes inside the closure and can never add an atom"*), so an origin arrives as an import from a frame whose closure differs (Fig 6 :23) and owes a source gate and shadow test. Selection is a habitat question (Fig 11: selection runs where nothing maintains arrival), so the anthropic landscape is a different loop with a different ground. Domain B verdict: both are `candidate`; neither is settled; their agreement is not corroboration (Fig 8 :51-52).

**Loop C (the meta test).** Fig 12 :103: *"Composition is recursive, and the level decides what is irreducible."* So the constants are atoms at the Standard Model level and may be molecules at a deeper level. Whether an origin is worth having is priced by Fig 5 :43: *"A new term is worth having when stating it, plus what remains unexplained after it, costs less than what was unexplained before."* Domain C verdict: no candidate has paid this across the full list; the framework returns the shape (a cheaper, provenanced, source-discriminating frame) and refuses to name it (Fig 9 :32).

## 4. What the question is, after re-census

It is a **union of conditions**, not one list (Fig 9 :55: *"A bundled problem is usually a union of overlapping conditions, not a list of separate ones."*). Particle-physics parameters, cosmological parameters and initial conditions require separate loops before an intersection can be called a compound. A single lever clears one region and leaves the intersections standing. So the first move is to unbundle, not to name an origin.

## 5. What it explicitly does not say

- Not that the constants have a common origin, and not that they do not.
- Not a name for the origin. SU(5), SO(10) and the landscape are provenanced candidates from the physics frame, not figure derivations.
- Not that gauge coupling unification or a fermion mass relation settles anything. They are partial compressions; most such relations fail.
- Not that the constants are fine-tuned for life. That is QMGR-0092, a neighbouring question with its own ground.
- Not that the framework's six-term substrate is nature's own list. It is *"a human's careful guess at reality's compression axes"* (essay), a visible, revisable choice.
- Not grounded. No instrument here discriminates among the sources that could produce the same readings.

## 6. What would settle it, the ground

A source-discriminating instrument, not more agreement. From *The Hard Problem is About Source*: an instrument that reads green *"cannot comment on the source,"* and *"the answer is unreached until a better instrument exists."* Concretely:

- A fermion mass relation predicted by a GUT and confirmed at the measured values with the commitment dated before the measurement. That pays part of the MDL bargain and makes one constant a molecule at that level.
- Gauge couplings meeting at a single scale with no added assumption, or demonstrably not.
- A precision constancy test (see Uzan 2025) finding variation in a dimensionless constant, which would open an axis the current frame cannot hold and would be a genuine import of a new dimension.
- A derivation inside the Standard Model closure predicting one free constant from others with no new atom. That would demote F2 from import to mint.
- Standing falsifiers from `01_figure_grounded_audit.md` section 6: show the constants are one condition; show irreducible-at-every-level as a theorem; show an origin that adds as many parameters as it removes is still counted as success.

## 7. The honest stop

**Unreached, not unreachable, and not silence.** Instruments exist (CODATA metrology, colliders, cosmological surveys, Uzan's review of constancy tests), so this is not underivable in principle. But no current instrument settles whether one generative frame predicts the whole pattern. Fig 13 :134: *"Unreachable is a verdict here, not a silence,"* and the README warns that *"Indiscernibility is not identity."* Several origins may fit the same readings; that does not make them the same source, and it does not make any of them right. The route is recorded so the stop is checkable.

## 8. One-paragraph version for outreach

> The Tether does not say whether the dimensionless constants share a common origin, and it does not name one. It says the question must first be unbundled: particle-physics parameters, cosmological parameters and initial conditions are overlapping conditions, and one lever clears only one region. It says irreducibility is a fact about a level, so a constant that is a free input in the Standard Model could be a composition at a deeper level. A common origin would be an import from a frame whose closure differs, owing provenance and a shadow test, and it earns its place only if it predicts a relation among the numbers while costing less to state than the list it replaces. Derivation and anthropic selection are two different mechanisms with two different grounds, and their agreement is not corroboration. The constants are readings; an origin is a claimed source, and only a source-discriminating instrument settles which source, if any, produced them. The framework returns that shape, the falsifiers written in advance, and an honest unreached stop, not a final theory.
