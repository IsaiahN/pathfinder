## 1. A shape is a constraint, not a filler

The canonical figures README states the limit: the framework returns *"what a missing thing would have to look like (a shape, not a name)."* Fig 9 :32: *"you get a kind of thing, never the answer itself."* Fig 13 :134-135: *"Unreachable is a verdict here, not a silence, It cannot say which primitive is missing, because naming it would mean already holding it."*

From *The Library of Babel* (opened 2026-11-23): *"you can cut your search short if the set of books returned is small and descriptive enough that it provides a general shape of the answer that satisfies your question."*

So for this question a **shape** is: a frame whose closure predicts a relation among at least two constants, settled at the measured anchor, cheaper to state than the list, provenanced as derivation or selection, and not final. It is not the name of that frame. `COMMON ORIGIN(???)` is a shape. `COMMON ORIGIN(SU(5))` is a name.

## 2. How the shape is obtained

- *Library of Babel*: use the vocabulary closest to the thing, look for recurring new terms that overlap with it, recurse. *"With each new term gained, you narrow the space by definition and deepen your vocabulary."*
- *Library of Babel* on bundling, the move that governs F4: *"Perhaps your problem was a bundle or conflation of several problems masquerading as one? You will need to search all of those problems individually to find their answers and then combine them in a satisfactory way."*
- *Library of Babel* on anchoring: *"you need something to tether and anchor you to reality, the same way a mountaineer needs a tether when rappelling or climbing."* Here that anchor is CODATA and the experiments behind it.
- Fig 6 :30: *"The description travels. Fails by being sharpened into terms only we can read. Cast it in effect terms."* Fig 9 :30-31: *"your words are borrowed too, so describe the effect rather than the cause."*
- *Decoding Tacit Knowledge* (exemplar reading): *"When contact isn't possible, the best you can send is a plain description of what you'd expect to see, because anyone can check that against the world."*
- *Library of Babel* on stopping: *"the finish line will also be the starting place,"* and the paradox that by then you have *"composed the answer through the process of locating it."*

## 3. What licenses a name

Two things, both outside the shape:

1. **The ground settles it.** Formula step 5: *"the ground settles it. Verified only by what pays."* For the constants, that means a predicted relation confirmed at the measured values.
2. **A provenanced import from a frame whose closure differs** (Fig 6 :23, Fig 8 :51-52), with a source gate and a shadow test. Here that means a derivation or selection mechanism with a recorded provenance and a stated gap it explains.

*They're Scanning Books* sharpens the import rule: *"The real import is not a new term in the existing space. It is a new dimension of the space."* And: *"Declaring the primitives does not make them neutral. It makes the choice visible."* The six-term substrate is a visible guess at compression axes, not nature's own list, so a common-origin frame is another declared choice and must be recorded as such.

## 4. Why a reading from the constants cannot name the source

*The Hard Problem is About Source* gives the mechanism, and it is the exact one at work here. An instrument reads green and *"cannot comment on the source."* Several sources (one green light, yellow plus blue, RGB, CMYK) yield the same reading. *"If several paths create the same behavior, then behavior is a projection with lossy information."* The resolution came from an instrument (the spectrometer), not an argument: *"The answer is unreached until a better instrument exists."*

The dimensionless constants are readings. A common origin is a claimed source. A GUT mass relation, a landscape selection argument and a numerological fit can all reproduce one reading. Without a source-discriminating instrument, naming the source from the reading is the metamerism error. What is owed is a better discriminator, not a name.

*The Recursive Self-Improvement Trap*: *"the output is only as good as the validator. The agent, or the tool, or the frame, or the theory, cannot validate itself."*

## 5. Q: does the framework forbid saying the constants are irreducible?

**Answered: no, and this is a correction to a possible over-reading.** The "shape, not a name" rule governs a primitive **not held in Γ** and the framework's own naming power. The constants are measured values in the physics frame, and a candidate origin is a provenanced import from that frame, not a primitive the framework invents. So discussing SU(5), SO(10) or landscape selection is permitted; asserting that the figures derive them is not. The six are *"a human's careful guess at reality's compression axes"* (*They're Scanning Books*), so declaring a set irreducible is a visible choice and can be revised.

The figures do return a shape here: irreducibility is level-relative (Fig 12 :103-104), so the question must name its level. That is a constraint on the answer, not the answer.

## 5b. Correction to my own section 4

Section 4 must not be read as forbidding all candidate origins. The metamerism point is **retained**: a source named from a reading alone is ungrounded until an instrument discriminates sources. But the correct reason is the missing discriminator, not a ban on mentioning held candidates. Both claims are recorded so neither is quietly lost, and the narrowing is triangulated (essay + Fig 8 :51-52 + Fig 2 :40).

## 6. Two consequences for outreach

**Prestige is not action.** From *The Recursive Self-Improvement Trap* (opened 2026-11-23): *"Prestige must never be allowed to validate an action directly... Prestige can fund an action. It can give an action a budget to be tried. But it cannot settle an action. Only the ground can settle it. The Ground Maintainer must be blind to prestige."* A campaign that gathers famous names to agree that the constants share an origin is the named collapse. The legitimate ask is the opposite: give the shape claim a budget to be tried and let the ground (a measurement, a mass relation, a constancy test) settle it.

**My own audit is a simulation, not a ground reading.** Same essay: LLMs *"can quickly simulate an approximate answer/output with the formula,"* but *"the truly testable output would need to be derived from the real architecture or working the formula yourself."* So `01_figure_grounded_audit.md` is a simulated derivation checked for internal consistency against the figures. It is **not** a test of nature and must be labelled as such. It cannot settle anything it reports on.
