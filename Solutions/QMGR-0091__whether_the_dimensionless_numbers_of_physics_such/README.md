## Residual in effect terms

We hold a list of measured pure numbers (the fine-structure constant alpha, the proton-to-electron mass ratio mu, the strong coupling, quark and lepton masses relative to the Planck mass, mixing angles, the cosmological constant). Each is settled by measurement, yet none is derived by the theory that contains it. The question "common origin" asks whether these slots are independent primitives or compositions of a smaller generative frame, that is, whether a frame exists whose closure predicts their pattern. Deciding one way sends you to a derivation of the numbers; deciding the other sends you to accept them as empirical inputs. The figures do not return a name for that frame. They return a shape and a test.

## What is deliberately not here

- No personal emails of named individuals.
- No ranking of people by how likely they are to believe a common origin.
- No bulk-send plan.

Three reasons, in the figures' own words. First, a room does not upgrade a label: agreement among frames is a weighted average of influence, and adding people raises the vocabulary without adding an anchor (Fig 2 :29-30, Fig 7 :13). Second, correlation disqualifies a verdict: Fig 2 :40 says *"agreement about the world is worthless when the agreeing frames share an evidence pool."* Selecting recipients for receptiveness manufactures the disqualifying correlation. Third, asking for trust before a check spends the only thing an outside frame contributes: Fig 10 :42, *"A verifier that can reconstruct the claim cannot verify it."*

Legally, scraping personal emails for unsolicited bulk outreach breaches GDPR and CAN-SPAM style rules in many jurisdictions. That is a practical failure on top of the structural one. The public venues in `03_public_contact_points.csv` are opt-in or institutional routes.

## Honest standing of this package

This is a **simulated derivation checked for internal consistency against the figures, not a ground reading.** From *The Recursive Self-Improvement Trap* (read 2026-11-23): LLMs *"can quickly simulate an approximate answer/output with the formula,"* but *"the truly testable output would need to be derived from the real architecture or working the formula yourself."* This package cannot settle the question it reports on. The verdict it returns is a shape, a test, and a stated stop, not a measurement of nature.
