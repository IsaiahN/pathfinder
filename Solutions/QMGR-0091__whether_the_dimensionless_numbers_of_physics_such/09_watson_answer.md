## QUESTION

Option A: assert one generative frame predicts them. Option B: accept them as independent empirical inputs. ## VERDICT (from the figures)

The figures do not decide whether the constants have a common origin. They decide the shape of any answer that could count. The constants are a union of conditions, so unbundle before seeking one lever. Irreducibility is level-relative, so an atom at the Standard Model level may be a molecule at a deeper level, and no level is final. A common origin would be an import from a frame whose closure differs, owing provenance and a shadow test, and it earns its place only under the description-length bargain with a relation settled at the measured anchor. Derivation and selection are separate loops, and agreement among candidate origins is not corroboration without independent evidence pools. The framework returns this shape and withholds the name. No candidate in the sources read has paid the bargain across the full list. This is a `specified`, ungrounded, candidate result, with the physics-specific question marked unreached.

- **cost if wrong:** bounded and locatable. The packet states mode, refuters and lineage before the conclusion, so an error is findable rather than invisible. The real cost is spending the independence of outside readers by sending a named origin story instead of a shape and a test. Fig 2 :42 says "Buy independence in proportion to the cost of being wrong." That cost is avoidable by leading with the union claim and the refuter.
- **undoable:** yes. The shape claim is revisable without withdrawing any primitive. An origin term can be appended when it pays the bargain or refused when it does not, and a refused term adds to the record rather than deleting from it. No figure line has to be retracted to reverse this verdict.
- **reversed by:** a ground reading, not agreement. A relation predicted by a candidate origin and confirmed at the measured values with the commitment dated first; a demonstration that the couplings meet at one scale without added assumptions; a precision constancy test finding variation; a derivation inside the Standard Model closure predicting a free constant from others; or a proof that the three conditions are one. Agreement among frameworks, prestige, or a better-sounding narrative are not such instruments.
