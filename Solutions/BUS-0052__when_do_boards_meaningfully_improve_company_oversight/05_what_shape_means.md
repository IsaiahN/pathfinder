# What "shape" means for this question

**Sources opened 2026-11-22.** Local canonical files: `FRAMEWORK/reference/FIGURES_README_canonical.md`, `FRAMEWORK/reference/03-FIGURES_TEXT.md`, `FRAMEWORK/reference/60_FIGURES_AS_METALAWS.md`, `FRAMEWORK/figures/THE_FORMULA.md`, `FRAMEWORK/figures/THE_SEAT_MAP_Field_Guide_amended.md`. ## 1. A shape is a constraint, not a filler

The canonical limit is that the framework returns "a shape, not a name": what a missing thing would have to look like, not which named thing fills the slot.

> Fig 9 :32 "you get a kind of thing, never the answer itself"

For this question, the shape is the set of constraints on a checking arrangement: an unmovable anchor, a differing closure, a live channel, and a refusal operator. It is not "X percent independent directors" and not "a good board". Those are names or scores, not shapes. Four constraints that must hold jointly are a shape; a ratio is a filler.

## 2. How the shape is obtained

The residual is described first, in effect terms, then a frame is looked up that predicts its shape:

> Fig 9 :27 "describe it before you go looking, or any frame you pick will seem to fit"

Here the residual is the missing correction per decision slot: management's report returns one reading and the ground returns another, and the board registers nothing. Described that way, the seat map is the frame whose closure predicts the shape (an anchor, frames ranging against it, and a maintainer who keeps the channel open). Had we picked "board independence" first and then looked for evidence, every governance study would have seemed to fit. That inversion is the apophenia the figure warns against.

From *The Hard Problem is About Source*: "If several paths create the same behavior, then behavior is a projection with lossy information (it cannot define consciousness in full)." A board that records only management's report is reading a projection. The essay's remedy is an instrument that discriminates sources: "An instrument that can't distinguish these cases demonstrates its own inadequacy, it cannot claim anything substantial about what is being measured or what causes it." The board's equivalent is an independent channel, not a better argument.

## 3. What licenses a name

Only two things, both outside the shape itself:

1. **The ground settles it.** `THE_FORMULA.md` step 5: "the ground settles it. Verified only by what pays. A gate is not the ground." A per-decision, ground-verified oversight event would name which board arrangement works.
2. **A provenanced import from a frame whose closure differs**, with a source gate and a shadow test. Fig 6 :23 "No frame escapes its own closure. The bound moves only on contact with a frame whose closure differs."

Until one of those, "Contrarian Director" or "audit committee" is a binding from the governance frame onto an atom of the checking arrangement, recorded as such, not a derivation of which atom must occupy the slot.

## 4. Why an averaged governance score is the specific forbidden error

*The Hard Problem is About Source* gives the mechanism: an instrument reads green and "cannot comment on the source". Several sources can give one reading. A governance score reads one number and cannot say which of the four conditions produced it: a high score may be a live channel with no refusal, or a refusal with a dead channel.

> Fig 1 :22 "Aggregating across slots is how a live signal disappears into an average."
> Fig 1 :27 "They are frame-internal. A frame cannot score itself with a quantity it also produces."

The score is a filter for the budget. It decides what gets tried first; it does not decide whether the board checked anything.

## 5. Q1: does "shape, not a name" forbid naming a condition?

No, and this is a correction to an overreading worth recording. The canonical phrase governs a **missing** primitive, one not held in the frame. The conditions here (anchor, differing closure, channel, refusal) are already held in the seat map and the figures. Naming them is selecting atoms from a frame already in hand, not inventing a new one. What would be forbidden is declaring a seventh condition, or claiming that naming one moves the wall. The shape claim stays at four; adding "board quality" as a fifth was refused in `01` F4 and is recorded in the retraction log.

Both readings are recorded so neither is quietly lost.

## 6. Prestige is not action

From *The Recursive Self-Improvement Trap*: "Prestige can fund an action. It can give an action a budget to be tried. But it cannot settle an action. Only the ground can settle it. The Ground Maintainer must be blind to prestige." A board full of distinguished names is prestige. A board whose channel reports what the ground returns is action. The outreach must ask for the second, never gather the first as endorsement.

## 7. My own audit is a simulation, not a ground reading

Same essay: "LLMs can quickly simulate an approximate answer/output with the formula found in the architecture post. It will often be better than the generic outputs that you will usually get otherwise too (intelligence is essentially arrangement). However, the truly testable output would need to be derived from the real architecture or working the formula yourself."

So `01_figure_grounded_audit.md` is a simulated derivation checked for internal consistency against the figures. It is not a measurement of any board and cannot settle the question it reports on. Mode is stated on every substantive claim: `general` for the structural shape, `specified` for the board application, nothing `grounded`.
