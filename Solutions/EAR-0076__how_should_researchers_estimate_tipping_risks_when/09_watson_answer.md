## VERDICT (from the figures)

The method shape survives; the single-number answer does not. Estimate per component, treat scarcity as a budget gap rather than absence of risk, buy genuinely independent evidence in proportion to irreversibility, pre-register the tipping criterion and refuter, sharpen instruments already returning something, measure the round-trip gap, and stay `candidate` until the ground (future behaviour) settles. The specific refusals: a global average hides the live slot; a clean short record proves nothing; overlapping agreement is not corroboration; EWS does not name a bifurcation; a headline number without R_T is dead reckoning. This is `general`/`specified`, ungrounded, candidate.

- **cost if wrong:** bounded and locatable. The packet labels mode, states five refuters before the conclusion and records the lineage, so an error is findable. The larger avoidable cost is spending readers' independence by sending a conclusion instead of a refuter, which Fig 2 :42 prices ("Buy independence in proportion to the cost of being wrong.").
- **undoable:** yes. The refusals are removable without touching the figure text: the per-component shape and the pre-registered refuter stand on their own. A published estimate can be superseded by a corrected one; no primitive has to be withdrawn.
- **reversed by:** a ground reading, not agreement. A source-discriminating observation that separates threshold-driven from non-tipping resilience loss would move a component toward `grounded`; a short slow-response record that nonetheless resolves a trend, or a demonstration that nothing registers at any resolution, would change the budget verdict; a case where aggregation preserves the per-slot signal, or where a shared evidence pool yields independent corroboration, would break C1 and C2. Until one of these fires, the verdict stands as `candidate`.
