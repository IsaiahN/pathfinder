## Three things in the world literature that bear directly on this question

1. **Timescale mismatch is the central defect.** Lenton et al. 2024: *"For slower-responding tipping systems, the relatively short satellite era of ~50 years is insufficient."* The Greenland, WAIS and AMOC components respond on century to millennial timescales. This is the figure's "unreachable at this budget" made concrete (Fig 8 :40-43). The remedy in that paper (spatial indicators, space-for-time substitution) is an INWARD move on an instrument already returning something (Fig 6 :32).
2. **Resilience loss is not tipping.** Both Lenton et al. 2024 and Rietkerk et al. 2025 state that non-tipping systems can lose resilience. Rietkerk et al. argue for *"better testing of the existence of tipping points, beyond the application of EWSs"*. This is the figure's re-census rule applied by an independent field: the indicator governs one quantity, the mechanism another. It corroborates F2 and F5.
3. **When waiting for certainty fails, change the decision, not the claim.** IPCC AR6 WGII Ch17: *"Waiting until uncertainties are resolved (if they ever can) may leave little or no time to adapt."* The figures price independence by cost of being wrong (Fig 2 :42); the decision-science field independently develops robust methods for the same condition. Low overlap, so this is genuine corroboration, not a pool.

## Distribution: offer, do not push

Figure-grounded rules:

- **Fig 10 :52** — *"Bring a characterised residual, never the source."* Lead with the per-component shape claim and its refuters; offer links only after a verdict is recorded.
- **Fig 2 :45** — *"Frames range against the ground. They do not negotiate with it."* Ask what the reader would expect to see in a short record if the shape claim were true, before giving them the source list.
- **Fig 4 :22** — *"Only methods cross up. Only head starts come down."* A recording of one derivation is not a generator. Send the refuter and the method, not a conclusion.
- **Fig 2 :40** — *"disqualifying for a verdict: agreement about the world is worthless when the agreeing frames share an evidence pool"* — no bulk send selected for likely agreement.

**Recommended order in a reply:** (1) the shape claim and its five refuters; (2) ask what the reader would count as a failure; (3) only then the links, with the note that the figures, not the summary, are the authority; (4) if requested, the formula as a reusable method with the instruction that each user writes their own refuter and states their own mode.

## The two-stage offer

1. **Stage 1:** validate the estimation shape against the ground, using the reader's own knowledge of short-record systems. Record the verdict before any theory is shown.
2. **Stage 2, opt-in:** if curious, read the figure links and the source ledger. Later agreement is never pooled as corroboration, because the reader now holds the interior (Fig 2 :40).
3. **Stage 3:** reuse the formula on another problem, with a self-written refuter and a declared mode.

**Hard precondition:** the shape must survive stage 1. Nothing here has been settled by a measurement, so the packet is labelled `candidate`, not accepted.
