## What is deliberately not here

- No personal emails of named individuals.
- No ranking of people by how likely they are to agree.
- No bulk-send plan.
- No claim that this derivation is a ground reading. It is a simulation checked for internal consistency against the figures.

Three reasons, in the figures' own words:

1. **Agreement is not a verdict.** Fig 2 :35: *"Alignment is triangulation, not negotiation. A mirror cannot correct you."* A list selected for receptiveness manufactures the correlation that would make its agreement worthless (Fig 2 :40, Seat Map :163).
2. **The checker must not hold the interiors.** Fig 10 :42: *"A verifier that can reconstruct the claim cannot verify it."* So the first contact carries the residual and its refuter, not the framework.
3. **Contact is the currency.** Fig 11 :40: *"An improvement that does not change contact changes nothing, however much it improves."* A campaign that only increases the number of readers has not increased contact with a ground.

Legally: unsolicited bulk email to scraped personal addresses breaches GDPR and CAN-SPAM style rules in many jurisdictions. Public opt-in venues avoid that failure as well as the structural one.

## The one-line verdict

Adaptation builds resilience when the adaptive loop keeps an anchor that does not update and measures its residual per slot against it. It becomes exploitation when the score the agents produce replaces that anchor, so the metric drifts while the reading still looks healthy. The distinction is contact, not the amount of adaptation.
