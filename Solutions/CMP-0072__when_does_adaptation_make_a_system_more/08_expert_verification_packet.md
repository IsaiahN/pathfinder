# Expert verification packet - the Tether on adaptation and resilience

**What this is:** a claim in a form a researcher in adaptation or complex systems can check. Mode, derivation and refuters are stated before the conclusion, so a reader knows what to look for and what would settle it.

**Mode: `specified` - not `grounded`.** No controlled perturbation or instrument is claimed. This is a discriminator and a route, not a result.

## 1. The claim, in one paragraph

Capability belongs to agent and habitat, not to the agent alone. Adaptation builds resilience when the adaptive loop keeps an anchor that does not move when the agents push on it and reads its residual per slot against it. It becomes exploitation when the agents can move the score the loop uses as its ground: the score becomes another frame, the population converges on whoever has most influence over it or on a hidden shared source, and the drift is coherent, which is why nobody notices. A score that decides an outcome has stopped being a measurement of it. The framework returns this shape and its refuters, not the name of the right metric for any specific system.

## 2. Three checkable sub-claims

### C1 - framework-internal, checkable by reading Figures 11 and 2

- **Statement:** the discriminating property is contact with a ground that does not update, not the amount of adaptation.
- **Source, verbatim:** Fig 11 :40 - *"A system cannot be made more capable by improving the agent in isolation. An improvement that does not change contact changes nothing, however much it improves."* Fig 2 :30 - *"If it moves when you push on it, it is not an anchor."* Fig 2 :33 - *"The anchor must not update."*
- **Refuter:** a controlled case where an agent-only adaptation moves an independent ground reading while habitat contact is held fixed. If that is shown, this sub-claim is refuted and the primary verdict in `01` F1 falls.

### C2 - the exploitation mechanism, checkable by an adaptation or complex-systems researcher

- **Statement:** exploitation is a named failure genus, not a side effect. Two distinct genera exist: mutual update (coherent drift), where frames converge on whoever has most influence, and common cause (hidden shared source), where frames agree because they draw on one pool. A score made into a target becomes a convention that decides an outcome and stops measuring the ground.
- **Source, verbatim:** Fig 2 :4-7 - *"collapse 1: mutual update"*, *"(coherent drift)"*, *"collapse 2: common cause"*, *"(hidden shared source)"*. Fig 2 :34 - *"If it does, panel 3 turns into panel 1: the anchor becomes another frame, everything drifts together, and the drift is coherent, which is why nobody notices."* Fig 10 :47 - *"a convention that decides an outcome has stopped being one"* (repeated as the guard at Fig 2 :43). Fig 1 :27 - *"They are frame-internal. A frame cannot score itself with a quantity it also produces."*
- **What to check:** recognisably distinct remediations? Breaking the update chain should help collapse 1; adding an independent frame should help collapse 2; restoring an external reading should help the convention case. If one intervention fixes all three, the isomer reading in `06` Q1 fails.
- **Refuter:** a score under sustained high-stakes adaptive pressure that keeps a stable, independently measured correlation with the ground and does not drift. Two reports quoting the same review are one witness, not two (Fig 2 :40).

### C3 - the measurement discipline, checkable on any real adaptive system

- **Statement:** a global score near zero with one live per-slot residual is a legal state, so the residual must be read per slot. Actor valence is relative to the residual, so no standing list of resilience-producing adaptation mechanisms exists.
- **Source, verbatim:** Fig 1 :19 - *"The residual is indexed by object slot, not aggregated."* Fig 1 :22 - *"Aggregating across slots is how a live signal disappears into an average."* Fig 11 :50 - *"The same actor is a benefactor to one residual and a malfactor to another, so mutualism and parasitism are relations rather than kinds."* Fig 11 :51 - *"No standing list of good actors survives a change of residual."*
- **Refuter:** a case where a global score near zero coexists with a live per-slot residual and the system is still correct to treat the problem as solved; or a list of adaptive mechanisms that remains beneficial across two different residuals with no re-enumeration.

## 3. What is explicitly not claimed

- Not that adaptation is harmful in general, or that all metrics are corrupted.
- Not that more reviewers, more data or more agreement settles anything. Fig 7 :13: *"agreement compounds, evidence does not."*
- Not that a rising score is evidence of resilience, or a falling one evidence of exploitation. The reading cannot discriminate sources.
- Not a name for the correct ground or metric in a specific system. The slot stays `???`.
- Not `grounded`, and not a simulation result presented as a measurement.
- Not a new primitive, and not an import that moves a wall. All terms used are already in the figure set.

## 5. What would settle it

1. **Contact test:** an independent ground reading held separate from the agent's own score.
2. **Target test:** a controlled comparison of the same metric with and without high-stakes targeting, measuring its correlation with the ground in both arms.
3. **Slot test:** per-slot residuals published beside the global score.
4. **Cascade test:** habitat enumerated to at least the third order before an adaptive actor is introduced (the DDT case in Fig 11 :52-54 is the negative exemplar).

## 6. What an expert can check in twenty minutes

1. Read Fig 11 :39-40, Fig 2 :4-7 and :29-34, Fig 10 :47, Fig 1 :19 and :27. Six short passages.
2. Check C2 against one case you know: was the failure a shared source, an update chain, or a metric made into a target? Note whether the remediation differed.
3. Name one instrument that would separate "score up because contact improved" from "score up because the score was moved." If none exists, say why.
4. Send the refutation. A failure is more useful than an endorsement, and no agreement will be pooled as corroboration.
