- **Domain 1, agent loop.** Actors: agent, belief, weighting, action, observation, residual per slot, library. Currency: residual reduction and cost. Anchor: a verifiable outcome. Partition question: which compositions keep contact.
- **Domain 2, ranked population.** Actors: many agents, shared library, a rank. Currency: the score. Anchor: the ground only while the rank ranges against it. **Union compound**, two collapses plus the grounded case. Where agents "exploit the system" lives.
- **Domain 3, habitat.** Actors: introduced actors, conditions, relations. Currency: cascade. Anchor: the ground. Enumeration, not composition.
- **Domain 4, checking seat.** Actors: checker, anchor, convention. Currency: verdict versus convention. Anchor: must not update. Governs all readings.

Order by dependency: anchor reachable, per-slot residual, objective still the ground, collapse genus, habitat cascade, round trip across scale.
