## 1. A shape is a constraint, not a filler

The figures README states the limit: the framework returns *"what a missing thing would have to look like (**a shape, not a name**)."* Fig 9 :32: *"you get a kind of thing, never the answer itself."* Fig 13 :134: *"Unreachable is a verdict here, not a silence."*

From *The Library of Babel*: *"you can cut your search short if the set of books returned is small and descriptive enough that it provides a general shape of the answer that satisfies your question."* And on method: *"If you don’t know the right terms to search for, you will have to use the existing vocabulary you do know that is closest to the words that you wish to describe and grasp the shape of."*

So a **shape** is the narrowed form of an answer you can state before you can name its instance. It constrains; it does not fill. For CMP-0072 the shape is:

> Resilience belongs to the contact relation, not to the agent. The discriminator is whether the adaptive loop keeps an anchor that does not update and reads its residual per slot against it. Which instrument is that anchor, in a given system, stays marked `???`.

Naming "the right metric" or "the right reward function" for a specific system would be a name, not a shape.

## 2. How a shape is obtained

- *Library of Babel*: bootstrap vocabulary by proximity, recurse, narrow. *"With each new term gained, you are able to narrow the space by definition and deepen your vocabulary."* The question itself is the anchor: *"This anchor is provided by your own question: If the question is answered by your present collection of books from your search you know whether to stop or continue and which direction to turn next."*
- Fig 8 :53: *"Residual first, frame second."* Fig 9 :30-31: *"your words are borrowed too, so describe the effect rather than the cause."* In this question the effect is "score up while contact down," not "bad reward function."
- *Decoding Tacit Knowledge*: *"When contact isn’t possible, the best you can send is a plain description of what you’d expect to see, because anyone can check that against the world."* That is why `02_outreach_email.md` sends a checkable discriminator rather than a theory.
- *Library of Babel* on stopping: *"you will know that you are done because the finish line will also be the starting place, as there is nowhere else to search and nothing else that can describe the answer better."*

## 3. What licenses a name

Two things, both outside the shape itself:

1. **The ground settles it.** Formula step 5: *"the ground settles it. Verified only by what pays. A gate is not the ground."* The figures README: the framework *"stops at the limit of what the available instruments can distinguish, and records the lineage so the stop is honest."*
2. **A provenanced import from a frame whose closure differs** (Fig 6 :23, Fig 8 :50-52), with a source gate and a shadow test.

*The Hard Problem is About Source* gives the reason a reading is not a name: *"If several paths create the same behavior, then behavior is a projection with lossy information."* The instrument that reads green *"cannot comment on the source."* The spectrometer, not an argument, resolved the case: *"The answer is unreached until a better instrument exists."* In CMP-0072 a rising score is the green reading; which source produced it (real contact or proxy manipulation) is not determined by the reading. What is owed is a source-discriminating instrument, not a better story about the metric.

## 4. Why the question's own vocabulary is the trap

The question asks "when does adaptation make a system more resilient?" That phrasing invites a property of adaptation. Fig 11 :39 refuses it: *"capability is a property of agent-and-habitat, never of the agent alone."* Fig 11 :50: *"The same actor is a benefactor to one residual and a malfactor to another, so mutualism and parasitism are relations rather than kinds."*

So the honest answer is relational: adaptation is resilience-preserving or undermining *relative to a residual and a contact relation*, not in itself. *How a Society of Agents Would Work* makes the same move from a different direction: *"A group is only worth having while its members stay different"* and *"The group’s agreement is never the verdict."* It also names the exact failure class: agents updating on each other converge on *"whoever had the most influence,"* and with a shared source *"the agreement is the pool talking."* That is Fig 2's coherent drift, reported in the language of multi-agent engineering.

## 6. Two consequences for this solution

**Prestige is not action.** From *The Recursive Self-Improvement Trap*: *"Prestige must never be allowed to validate an action directly... Prestige can fund an action. It can give an action a budget to be tried. But it cannot settle an action. Only the ground can settle it. The Ground Maintainer must be blind to prestige."* A list of famous adaptation researchers assembled to agree with the shape claim is the named collapse, not corroboration. The legitimate ask is a budget to be tried, with the ground settling it.

**My own audit is a simulation, not a ground reading.** Same essay: *"LLMs can quickly simulate an approximate answer/output with the formula found in the architecture post... However, the truly testable output would need to be derived from the real architecture or working the formula yourself."* LLM output is *"simulacra."* So `01` is a simulated derivation checked for internal consistency against the figures. It is not a test of the claim and cannot settle anything it reports on.
