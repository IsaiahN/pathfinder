## Relevant wider literature, grouped by evidence pool

One witness, not several: Tyler 2006 and 2014, Mazerolle et al. 2013, Walters and Bolger 2019, and the UVA interview all draw on the procedural-justice and policing literature. Their agreement is expected (Fig 2 :31, :40).

A second, lower-overlap witness: Menzner and Schweizer 2026 and De Micheli and Taylor 2024 come from political science and longitudinal survey work rather than police-dialogue interventions.

A third, genuinely low-overlap witness: Brosnan and de Waal 2003 in primatology, with Wynne 2004 as an inline caveat. It supports the shape that unequal outcomes register before any institutional vocabulary exists, but it does not by itself establish anything about legitimacy.

The essays share an author with the figures, so they carry no independent corroboration weight. They are used only for the canonical reading in `05_what_shape_means.md`.

## Distribution: offer, do not push

- **Fig 4 :22** - *"Only methods cross up. Only head starts come down."* Sending the full framework before anyone has described a residual sends a recording, not a generator. Lead with the claim and its refuter.
- **Fig 10 :42** - *"A verifier that can reconstruct the claim cannot verify it."* Seat 4 must not hold the interiors. A recipient who has absorbed the framework before being asked for a verdict has joined the frame being checked.
- **Fig 9 :28** - *"any frame you pick will seem to fit."* Ask what they would expect to see if the claim were true before giving them the literature list.
- **Fig 9 :60** - *"Use filters for the budget and witnesses for the verdict. Never let a filter hand you a verdict."* A meta-analysis, a journal impact factor, or a society's prestige is a filter for where to spend attention, never a verdict.
- **Fig 2 :40** - *"agreement about the world is worthless when the agreeing frames share an evidence pool"*; never pool later agreement as corroboration.

**Recommended order in a reply:** (1) the shape claim and its refuter; (2) ask what the reader would count as a failure; (3) record that answer; (4) only then, if curious, offer the links, with the note that the figures, not the summary, are the authority.

## The two-stage offer

1. **Stage 1:** the residual, the shape claim, the refuter, and the two anchors. No framework, no literature list, no request for agreement.
2. **Stage 2, opt-in only:** the source links in this file and the figure text. The recipient's stage-1 verdict is already recorded and is never revised by later agreement (Fig 2 :35, :40).
3. **Stage 3, optional:** the formula as a method to reuse. Each user writes their own refuter and states their own mode. *The Recursive Self-Improvement Trap* warns that *"the truly testable output would need to be derived from the real architecture or working the formula yourself."*

**Hard precondition:** stages 1 to 3 only work if the stage-1 claim survives. Eight refuters are written in `01_figure_grounded_audit.md` section 4. A refutation is the desired outcome; it is more useful than an endorsement.
