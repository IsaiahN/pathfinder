## 1. A shape is a constraint, not a filler

The figures README states the limit: the framework returns *"what a missing thing would have to look like (a shape, not a name)."* Fig 9 :32: *"you get a kind of thing, never the answer itself."* Fig 13 :134-135: *"It cannot say which primitive is missing, because naming it would mean already holding it."*

From *The Library of Babel*: *"You can cut your search short if the set of books returned is small and descriptive enough that it provides a general shape of the answer that satisfies your question."*

So a **shape** is the narrowed form of an answer you can state before you can name it. It constrains; it does not fill. For INS-0027 the shape is: a union of a ground condition and a channel condition, a per-subject residual, two separately named anchors, a signed feedback loop, and a stop that refuses to name the reform. "Adopt procedural justice training" is a name. "Keep the channel to actual treatment reachable and renew the subject's reach through contact with a differing closure" is a shape.

*How Intelligence Actually Works* adds the limiting word: *"The recursion converges toward indiscernibility, and indiscernibility is not identity."* A shape that fits every observation is still not the observation's source, which is why the lineage must be kept.

## 2. How a shape is obtained

- *Library of Babel*: use the vocabulary already known that is closest to the thing, look for recurring new terms that overlap or correlate with it, recurse. *"With each new term gained, you narrow the space and deepen your vocabulary."*
- *Library of Babel* on bundles: *"Perhaps your problem was a bundle or conflation of several problems masquerading as one?"* This is the essay-level warrant for Fig 9 :55 in F3 of `01_figure_grounded_audit.md`.
- Fig 6 :30: *"The description travels. Fails by being sharpened into terms only we can read. Cast it in effect terms."* Fig 9 :30-31: *"your words are borrowed too, so describe the effect"* and *"rather than the cause."*
- *Decoding Tacit Knowledge*: *"When contact isn't possible, the best you can send is a plain description of what you'd expect to see, because anyone can check that against the world."*
- *Library of Babel* on stopping: *"You will know that you are done because the finish line will also be the starting place, as there is nowhere else to search and nothing else that can describe the answer better."*
- *How a Society of Agents Would Work*, on why the per-slot requirement is structural: *"Aggregating across slots is how a live signal disappears into an average."*

## 3. What licenses a name

Only two things, both outside the shape itself:

1. **The ground settles it.** Formula step 5: *"the ground settles it. Verified only by what pays. A gate is not the ground."* The README: the framework *"stops at the limit of what the available instruments can distinguish, and records the lineage so the stop is honest."*
2. **A provenanced import from a frame whose closure differs** (Fig 6 :23, Fig 8 :50-52), with a source gate and a shadow test.

*The Hard Problem is About Source* sharpens this into the central methodological warning for INS-0027. An instrument that reads green *"is speaking only about what it measured, it cannot comment on the source."* The source might be one green light, or yellow plus blue, or RGB, or CMYK. *"If several paths create the same behavior, then behavior is a projection with lossy information."* It was the spectrometer, not an argument, that resolved the case: *"The answer is unreached until a better instrument exists."*

Here, a survey score of "trust in institutions" is the projection. Unequal treatment, inconsistent enforcement, and a degraded channel to accurate information about treatment are three possible sources that can produce the same reading. Naming one as *the* cause from the reading alone is the metamerism error. What is owed is an instrument that discriminates sources, not a confident attribution.

*How Intelligence Actually Works* gives the same limit from the search side: *"If the final book in your exhaustive search doesn't answer the question, then there are only two possibilities: the nature of your question was unanswerable, or malformed."* And it warns that the practitioner supplies the content: a replicator that only shapes *"would be a rough draft."* The framework supplies the shape; the institutional researcher supplies the measurements.

## 4. Why naming a specific reform is the specific forbidden error

*They're Scanning Books When They Should Be Studying Nature*: *"A minimum viable set of six terms is a human's careful guess at reality's compression axes; another sensorium's minimum set need not be those six."* And: *"Declaring the primitives does not make them neutral. It makes the choice visible."*

The four components of procedurally just dialogue, the Eastonian reservoir, and Tyler's two-stage model are all careful guesses from one human-facing frame. Declaring one of them *the* answer to institutional legitimacy turns a visible choice into an invisible one. *Arrival of the Fittest* supplies the precedent for refusal under "Three mappings offered and declined": Bayesian updating was declined because *"there is no prior, no likelihood and no posterior anywhere in the mechanism."* The institutional analogy is declined for the same reason: the figures contain no sanction, no norm, no legitimacy term, and no survey item. They contain an anchor, a residual, a channel, a closure, a bond, and a loop.

## 5. Q2 - ANSWERED, no further question needed

**What licenses a particular reform for this institution?** Nothing in the framework. The framework returns a shape. A reform is licensed by the ground settling a minted term in that institution, or by a provenanced import with a shadow test that explains a residual the institution actually has. Until one of those, the name is the researcher's own contribution and must be recorded as such.

**Does the literature commit the error the README forbids?** It does when it treats "procedural justice" as a free-standing cause of legitimacy across all institutions. The four components are a vocabulary from the police-encounter frame. Binding them onto a court, a registry, or a school is a provenanced import from a peer frame, not a derivation from the figures, and it carries zero evidentiary weight until a source gate and a shadow test are supplied for that institution (Fig 6 :23, Fig 8 :50-52).

## 5b. Correction to my own §3-§4 (see `06_isomers_and_order.md` Q1)

I overstated the "shape, not a name" rule. The README's phrase is *"what a **missing** thing would have to look like (a shape, not a name)."* The subject is a primitive not held in Γ. "Procedural justice", "diffuse support", "reservoir of goodwill", and "legal socialisation" are already held in the world literature. Naming them is a **binding from a peer frame**, not the invention of a new primitive. What remains wrong in this solution is calling that binding an import that moves the wall (Fig 6: *"IMPORT adds an atom"* - it adds none in the framework's library) and saying the framework itself names the reform.

My §3 metamerism point is **not** retracted: the survey reading still cannot comment on which treatment produced it, and what is owed is a discriminator. But the reason is the missing instrument, not a blanket ban on naming held atoms. Both claims are now recorded so neither is quietly lost.

## 6. Two consequences for outreach

**Prestige is not action.** From *The Recursive Self-Improvement Trap*: *"Prestige must never be allowed to validate an action directly."* And: *"Prestige can fund an action. It can give an action a budget to be tried. But it cannot settle an action. Only the ground can settle it. The Ground Maintainer must be blind to prestige."* A campaign that gathers well-cited names to agree with the shape is the named collapse, not corroboration. The legitimate ask gives the claim a budget to be tried and lets the ground, a per-subject record plus an independent treatment record, settle it.

**My own audit is a simulation, not a ground reading.** Same essay: *"the output is only as good as the validator. The agent, or the tool, or the frame, or the theory, cannot validate itself."* And: *"LLMs can quickly simulate an approximate answer/output with the formula found in the architecture post."* *Arrival of the Fittest* is blunter: the outputs *"could never be verified if the solutions presented were not contrived to fit the formula."* So `01` and `09` are simulated derivations checked for internal consistency against the figures. They are **not** tests of the institutional claim and must be labelled as such. They cannot settle anything they report on.

## 7. A drift warning specific to this question

*Decoding Tacit Knowledge* observes that *"Long, close contact pulls the frames together, so the process that makes communication easy slowly uses up that extra reach."* This is the occupation hazard of institutional legitimacy research. Researchers who spend years inside one institution, one agency, or one survey programme come to share its pretext, and their agreement with its self-description becomes worthless as a check. The same essay names the cure: *"Frames range against the ground. They do not negotiate with it,"* and *"independence is renewed by separate experience."* In practice: include subjects and records from outside the institution's own reporting channel, and keep them separate from it (Fig 2 :31-32, Fig 10 :80-81).
