# Expert verification packet: the Tether on metabolic inheritance

**What this is:** a claim in a form an origins-of-life researcher can check. Mode, derivation and refuters are stated before the conclusion, so a reader knows what to look for and what would settle it.

**Mode: `specified` on the shape, `general` on the dependency order, not `grounded`.** No experiment is claimed to have demonstrated heritable reaction networks across dividing compartments. This is a shape and a route, not a result. It is a simulated derivation checked for internal consistency against the figures, not a ground reading.

## 1. The claim, in one paragraph

What could be inherited from one compartment generation to the next is the catalytic arrangement, not the ingredient list. What crosses the division must be a generator or a head start, a part that survives its own use inside a cycle whose output re-enters as input, rather than a frozen recording of the parent's concentrations. The daughter is a substituted habitat, so inheritance reproduces required contact and inherits unchosen actors, with two silent failures: a needed catalyst lost by unequal sampling, and an actor that acts later. A lineage needs two factors, fidelity of the handoff and ground pressure; zero either and the chain runs in place. The crossing is lossy and non-invertible, so the output is branches rather than copies. Over a fixed atom set parent and daughter are isomers; over a variable set they share a functional group. The framework does not name the chemical carrier: that is an open gap.

## 2. Three checkable sub-claims

### C1 — framework-internal, checkable by reading Figure 12 and Figure 13

- **Statement:** the heritable object is the bond arrangement, not the molecule list. A catalyst that survives its own use and a cycle whose output re-enters as input are the named structural kinds; the specific chemistry is not supplied.
- **Source, verbatim:** Fig 12 :5 - *"A set of ingredients is not one. The bond is the rest of the information, and it is the half nothing was recording."* Fig 12 :94 - *"The arrangement is the substance."* Fig 12 :119 - *"A part that survives its own use."* Fig 12 :121 - *"The output re-enters as input."* Fig 13 :129 - *"A term carries the atoms it was built from and the bonds that joined them. That record is not documentation of the term. It is the term."*
- **Refuter:** a controlled division in which the daughter re-runs the network with the catalytic relations destroyed or scrambled while the ingredient list is preserved; or a heritable network derived with no catalyst or cycle composition.

### C2 — chemistry-facing, checkable against the autocatalytic-sets and protocell literature

- **Statement:** unequal division can drop a required viable core; a permeant inducer produced in one compartment can trigger a closure in a neighbour; the daughter compartment is a substituted habitat whose internal concentrations are disrupted at division and then reconverge. These are the world-side counterparts of the figure claims about silent habitat failures and the lossy handoff.
- **What to check, with the opened sources:** Hordijk and Steel 2014 states that a viable core *"could be lost during a compartment division with an unequal distribution of molecules"* and that a *"seed" molecule* can bring a core into existence. Hordijk et al. 2018 report a *"toxic element"* that destroys another RAF subset and a *"permeable inducer"* that crosses the membrane. Taneja and Higgs 2025 find that divisions produce daughters of unequal size and that internal concentrations are disrupted at each division then trend back toward stable values. Do those reports support the shape, or is the analogy being stretched across different measured objects? Note honestly that Hordijk et al. 2018 did **not** model division or inheritance, and that Sokolskyi et al. 2024 used vials, not dividing vesicles.
- **Source, verbatim from the figures:** Fig 11 :67 - *"Move the residual and you must reproduce everything its goal required, and you inherit the new habitat's actors, which you did not choose."* Fig 11 :68 - *"what you failed to reproduce is invisible until the goal fails"*; Fig 11 :69 - *"what you unintentionally introduced is invisible until it acts"*. Fig 7 :36 - *"A chain of mirrors is one long room. The next room must differ from the last, and match the residual it surfaced."*
- **Refuter:** a division that removes the habitat rather than substituting it; a daughter inheriting all required contact with no unchosen actor; or a lineage with perfect fidelity and no selection that still accumulates open-ended heritable complexity.

### C3 — framework-internal derivation, checkable by reading Figure 7 and Figure 10

- **Statement:** inheritance with variation needs two factors. Fidelity alone gives persistence without progress; ground pressure alone with no fidelity gives no inheritance. The parent-to-daughter transform is lossy and non-invertible, which is the structural reason branches appear rather than copies.
- **Source, verbatim:** Fig 7 :37 - *"Progress per handoff has two factors. Zero either and the chain runs in place:"*; Fig 7 :38 - *"the bracket's fidelity across the handoff"*; Fig 7 :39 - *"the ground's pressure on the room"*. Fig 10 :70 - *"If levels were copies there would be one lineage drifting and no branches. The tree of life is what a non-invertible transform looks like from outside."* Fig 4 :22 - *"Only methods cross up. Only head starts come down."*
- **Refuter:** show a settled molecule cannot become an atom for the next level (Fig 12 :104), or that the transform from parent to daughter is invertible so no branching is possible.

## 3. What is explicitly not claimed

- Not a theory of how life began, and not a claim that any experiment has demonstrated this crossing in dividing vesicles.
- Not a molecule name, not a membrane composition, not a numerical fidelity threshold. Fig 9 :32 - *"you get a kind of thing, never the answer itself"*.
- Not a claim that the six-term substrate is nature's own list, and not a new primitive or import that moves the wall.
- Not `grounded`. No measurement in this packet settles anything.
- Not agreement from model consensus. RAF theory, GARD and the stochastic corrector model share an evidence pool; their agreement is one witness (Fig 2 :40 - *"disqualifying for a verdict: agreement about the world is worthless when the agreeing frames share an evidence pool"*).

## 5. What would settle it

A source-discriminating instrument, not a further argument. From *The Hard Problem is About Source*: an instrument that reads green *"cannot comment on the source"*; the spectrometer resolved the case. Here:

1. **Arrangement test:** does the daughter re-run the closure after catalytic relations are disrupted but the ingredient list is preserved?
2. **Generator test:** does a frozen one-occasion recording succeed with no seed and no re-applicable relation?
3. **Two-factor test:** does perfect fidelity with no compartment selection still give open-ended heritable complexity?
4. **Habitat test:** can a division remove rather than substitute the habitat, or a daughter inherit all required contact with no unchosen actor?
5. **Discriminator among sources:** can an experiment tell an inherited arrangement from environmental re-supply and re-assembly, or from inducer seeding? A matching concentration profile is not enough.
