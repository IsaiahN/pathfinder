# The Tether's answer: how a reaction network could be inherited across compartments

**Mode: `specified` on the shape, `general` on the dependency order, not `grounded`.** This is a shape and a route, not a result. Simulated derivation checked against the figures (`05_what_shape_means.md` section 6), not a ground reading.

## 1. The residual it answers

A reaction network inside a compartment is a catalytic pattern, not a bag of molecules. At division, the daughter receives a new boundary and a stochastic, unequal sample of the parent's contents. The question is a **union compound** of three conditions: what the heritable object is, how it crosses a substituted habitat, and what makes the crossing a selected lineage rather than bare persistence. Fig 9 :57 - *"a case in two conditions is not a harder case of either. It stops being a mixture and becomes a specific compound."* Verdicts are kept separate per domain and never averaged into one origin story (Fig 1 :19 - *"The residual is indexed by object slot, not aggregated."*).

## 2. The shape

1. **The heritable object is the arrangement, not the ingredient list.** Fig 12 :5 - *"A set of ingredients is not one. The bond is the rest of the information, and it is the half nothing was recording."* Fig 12 :94 - *"The arrangement is the substance."* A daughter that inherits concentrations but not catalytic relations has inherited a recording of one occasion. The specific chemical identity of the carrier is **not** given by the figures.
2. **What crosses is a generator or a head start, not a replay.** Fig 4 :22 - *"Only methods cross up. Only head starts come down."* Fig 4 :24 - *"downward: something that makes the next search cheaper. Never a replay of what worked once."* In chemical vocabulary the head start is a catalyst that survives its own use (Fig 12 :119 - *"A part that survives its own use."*) and a cycle whose output re-enters as input (Fig 12 :121 - *"The output re-enters as input."*).
3. **The daughter is a substituted habitat, not a blank room.** Fig 11 :66 - *"Isolation is not removal of the habitat. It is substitution of one habitat for another."* Fig 11 :67 - *"Move the residual and you must reproduce everything its goal required, and you inherit the new habitat's actors, which you did not choose."* Two failures are silent: a required catalyst lost by unequal sampling (Fig 11 :68 - *"what you failed to reproduce is invisible until the goal fails"*) and an uninvited actor that acts later (Fig 11 :69 - *"what you unintentionally introduced is invisible until it acts"*). Exclusion is not available: Fig 11 :61 - *"Introduction is available; exclusion is not."*
4. **A lineage needs two factors, not one.** Fig 7 :37 - *"Progress per handoff has two factors. Zero either and the chain runs in place:"* - the two are Fig 7 :38 *"the bracket's fidelity across the handoff"* and Fig 7 :39 *"the ground's pressure on the room"*. Fidelity without selection is motion without progress (Fig 7 :32 - *"one long room: motion, no progress"*); selection without fidelity is no inheritance at all. Fig 7 :36 - *"A chain of mirrors is one long room. The next room must differ from the last, and match the residual it surfaced."*
5. **The crossing is lossy and non-invertible, which is why branches appear.** Fig 10 :70 - *"If levels were copies there would be one lineage drifting and no branches. The tree of life is what a non-invertible transform looks like from outside."* Fig 4 :34 - *"A seed carries no small tree: it carries what will grow one, and the conditions it lands in decide what it becomes."* Inheritance is therefore conservation of a shape plus variation, never a photocopy of a parent state.
6. **Parent and daughter are a structural pair.** Over a fixed atom set they are isomers (Fig 12 :117 - *"Same atoms, different bond, different substance."*); over a variable atom set they share a functional group (Fig 12 :123 - *"The group says what kind; the qualifier says which member."*). Type, not degree, is the variable.

## 3. What it explicitly does not say

- Not a claim about how life actually began. No experiment is claimed to have demonstrated this crossing in dividing vesicles.
- Not a name for the molecule or membrane chemistry that does the work. Fig 9 :32 - *"you get a kind of thing, never the answer itself"*. The identity is a `gap`, not a silence and not an invitation to invent one.
- Not a numerical fidelity threshold, not an optimization result, and not a general theory of abiogenesis.
- Not a claim that the six-term substrate is nature's own list. Declaring it makes the choice visible, not neutral.
- Not `grounded`. Nothing here is asserted as settled by experiment. This is a simulated derivation checked for internal consistency against the figures.

## 4. What would settle it: the ground

An instrument, not a further argument. *The Hard Problem is About Source* (opened 2026-11-23) shows the form: a reading of green *"cannot comment on the source"*, and the matter was resolved *"not by argument, but by the arrival of a better instrument"*. Here a daughter with the parent's concentration profile is a reading, not a source. Several paths produce it: inherited catalytic arrangement, environmental re-supply and re-assembly, or a permeant inducer (Hordijk et al. 2018 demonstrate the inducer route). The owed discriminator is one that distinguishes those paths.

Concretely:

- **Arrangement test:** does the daughter re-run the closure after its catalytic relations are disrupted while its ingredient list is preserved? If yes, F1 of `01` is refuted.
- **Generator test:** does a frozen one-occasion recording succeed without any seed or re-applicable relation? If yes, F2 is refuted.
- **Two-factor test:** does perfect fidelity with no selection among compartments still give open-ended heritable complexity? If yes, F5 is refuted.
- **Habitat test:** can a division remove the habitat rather than substitute it, and can a daughter inherit all required contact with no unchosen actor? If yes, F4 is refuted.
- **Standing figure refuters:** derive the network with no catalyst or cycle composition; derive one of the six terms from the other five; show a faithful copy with no branches.

## 5. The honest stop

**Unreached, not unreachable.** The figures return the shape: arrangement, generator, substituted habitat, two-factor handoff, non-invertible crossing. They do not return the chemical identity, the membrane composition, or the fidelity number. The stop is recorded here with the route and the refuter so it is checkable. Fig 8 :39 - *"So a search that finds nothing is never a proof of absence."* Fig 13 :134 - *"Unreachable is a verdict here, not a silence"*. Indiscernibility is not identity: two compartments with the same reading are not thereby the same inherited network.

## 6. One-paragraph version for outreach

> The Tether does not say how life began and does not name a molecule. It says that what could be inherited across a compartment division is the catalytic arrangement, not the ingredient list, because the bond carries the information the species list omits. What crosses the boundary must be a generator or a head start, a part that survives its own use inside a cycle whose output re-enters as input, not a frozen recording of the parent's concentrations. The daughter is a substituted habitat, so inheritance reproduces required contact and also inherits unchosen actors, with two silent failures: a catalyst lost by unequal sampling and an actor that acts later. And a lineage needs two factors, fidelity of the handoff and pressure from the ground; zero either and the chain runs in place. The crossing is lossy and non-invertible, which is why the result is branches rather than copies. The chemical identity of the carrier stays open until an instrument that discriminates sources settles it. What is offered is the shape any answer must have, five refuters written in advance, and the missing instrument.
