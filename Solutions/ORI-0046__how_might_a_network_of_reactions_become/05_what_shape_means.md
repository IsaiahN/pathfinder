## 1. A shape is a constraint, not a filler

The figures README states the limit: the framework returns *"what a missing thing would have to look like (a shape, not a name)."* Fig 9 :32: *"you get a kind of thing, never the answer itself"*. Fig 13 :134: *"Unreachable is a verdict here, not a silence"*.

From *The Library of Babel* (opened): *"you can cut your search short if the set of books returned is small and descriptive enough that it provides a general shape of the answer that satisfies your question."* And: *"you will have to use the existing vocabulary you do know that is closest to the words that you wish to describe and grasp the shape of."*

So a **shape** is the narrowed form of an answer you can state before you can name it. It constrains; it does not fill. For this question:

- `Carrier = arrangement, not ingredient list` is a shape. `Carrier = the formose reaction in a fatty-acid vesicle` is a name.
- `Generator or head start crosses, not a recording` is a shape. `A 20% serial transfer into fresh food set` is a protocol in one model.
- `Two factors: fidelity and ground pressure` is a shape. `A fidelity of 0.97 selects for evolvability` is a number the figures do not return.

## 2. How the shape is obtained

- *Library of Babel* (opened): bootstrapping by proximity, *"With each new term gained, you narrow the space by definition and deepen your vocabulary."* The search is steered by ranging returned books against the question: *"you can ask: Are we getting warmer, or are we getting colder?"*
- Fig 6 :30: *"The description travels. Fails by being sharpened into terms only we can read. Cast it in effect terms."* Fig 9 :30-31: *"your words are borrowed too, so describe the effect rather than the cause."* Hence the residual was stated as "what crosses the division so the daughter can re-run the network", not "what is the genetic takeover mechanism".
- *Decoding Tacit Knowledge* (carried): *"When contact isn't possible, the best you can send is a plain description of what you'd expect to see, because anyone can check that against the world."*
- *Library of Babel* (opened) on stopping: *"you will know that you are done because the finish line will also be the starting place, as there is nowhere else to search and nothing else that can describe the answer better."*

## 3. What licenses a name

Only two things, both outside the shape itself:

1. **The ground settles it.** THE_FORMULA.md step 5: *"the ground settles it. Verified only by what pays."* The canonical README: the framework *"stops at the limit of what the available instruments can distinguish, and records the lineage so the stop is honest."* Here the required instrument is one that discriminates an inherited catalytic arrangement from an inherited ingredient list.
2. **A provenanced import from a frame whose closure differs** (Fig 6 :23, Fig 8 :50-52), with a source gate and a shadow test. Naming a specific catalyst from the chemistry frame is a binding of this kind, provenanced as such. It moves no wall: Fig 6 :21 - *"MINT composes inside the closure and can never add an atom."*

*They're Scanning Books* (carried) sharpens this: *"The real import is not a new term in the existing space. It is a new dimension of the space."* And: *"Declaring the primitives does not make them neutral. It makes the choice visible."* The six-term substrate is a visible guess, not nature's own list.

## 4. Why the source question is the correct shape to ask

*The Hard Problem is About Source* (opened) gives the governing analogy. An instrument reads green and *"cannot comment on the source"*. Several underlying causes produce identical readings: *"If several paths create the same behavior, then behavior is a projection with lossy information."* The case was resolved *"not by argument, but by the arrival of a better instrument"*: the spectrometer, not a further description.

Applied here: a daughter compartment that exhibits the same set of molecule concentrations is a reading, not a source. The parent's arrangement could have been inherited, or the ingredients could have been re-supplied by the environment and re-assembled, or a permeant inducer could have seeded the closure (Hordijk et al. 2018 demonstrate exactly this inducer effect). A concentration profile cannot comment on which source operated. The instrument owed is one that discriminates among those paths. This is why the answer stops at a shape and records the gap.

## 5. Correction to my own reading of the "shape, not a name" rule

I initially treated the rule as a blanket ban on naming any mechanism. That overstates it. The canonical README says *"what a **missing** thing would have to look like (a shape, not a name)"*. The subject is a primitive not held in the library. Catalyst and cycle are already held atoms of Fig 12 (:119 and :121), and network and gradient are held structures of Fig 13 (:23-24, :21-22). Naming them as the kinds that do the work is a binding onto held atoms, not the invention of a new primitive. What remains genuinely forbidden by the rule is naming the *specific chemical identity* that occupies those slots, because that identity is not held in the framework's library. Both the permitted binding and the forbidden naming are recorded so neither is quietly lost.

Triangulation status of the correction: `triangulated` across the canonical README and Fig 12/Fig 13 (low overlap).

## 6. Two consequences for this folder

**Prestige is not action.** *The Recursive Self-Improvement Trap* (carried): *"Prestige must never be allowed to validate an action directly... Prestige can fund an action. It can give an action a budget to be tried. But it cannot settle an action. Only the ground can settle it. The Ground Maintainer must be blind to prestige."* A list of famous origins-of-life researchers who agree with the shape is the named collapse, not corroboration. The legitimate ask is a budget to test the shape.

**This is a simulation, not a ground reading.** Same essay: *"LLMs can quickly simulate an approximate answer/output... However, the truly testable output would need to be derived from the real architecture or working the formula yourself."* LLM output is *"simulacra"*. So `01_figure_grounded_audit.md` is a simulated derivation checked for internal consistency against the figures. It is **not** a test of the origins-of-life claim and must be labelled as such. It cannot settle anything it reports on.
