## QUESTION

Decide, in effect terms, what the Tether says about how a network of reactions might become inherited from one generation of compartments to another. Option A: the network is inherited as a list of molecules that the daughter happens to receive. What each leads to: A sends a recording claim that cannot distinguish an inherited arrangement from environmental re-supply, and spends the independence of the people checking it; B sends a checkable shape, five refuters written in advance, and a named instrument gap.

## VERDICT (from the figures)

What could be inherited is the catalytic arrangement, not the species list, and what crosses the division must be a generator or head start rather than a frozen recording. The daughter is a substituted habitat with two silent failures, and a lineage needs both handoff fidelity and ground pressure; zero either and it runs in place. The crossing is lossy and non-invertible, which yields branches rather than copies. Over a fixed atom set parent and daughter are isomers; over a variable set they share a functional group. The chemical carrier, the membrane composition and any fidelity number are an honest `gap`. This is a `specified`, ungrounded, candidate result. The strongest outside check is not agreement among models but the source-discriminating experiment named in section 4 of `07`.

- **cost if wrong:** bounded and locatable. The packet states its mode and five refuters before the conclusion, so an error is findable. The real cost is spending the independence of outside readers by sending a recording instead of a generator, which Fig 2 :42 prices as "Buy independence in proportion to the cost of being wrong." That cost is avoidable by leading with the shape claim and its refuter.
- **undoable:** yes. The unsupported parts (a named molecule, a copy-fidelity story) are not asserted here. The shape can be narrowed or withdrawn if any refuter fires, and no primitive has to be removed from the record. A sent packet can be superseded by a corrected one.
- **reversed by:** a ground reading, not agreement. An experiment that distinguishes an inherited catalytic arrangement from environmental re-supply or inducer seeding would settle the carrier question; a daughter re-running the network with its catalytic relations scrambled would break the arrangement claim; perfect fidelity with no compartment selection yielding open-ended complexity would break the two-factor claim; a division that removes rather than substitutes the habitat would break the substitution law. Until one of these fires, the verdict stands as `candidate`.
