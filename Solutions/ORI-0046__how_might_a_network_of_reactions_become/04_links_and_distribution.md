## Wider literature that bears on this question

- **RAF theory and viable cores.** Hordijk and Steel 2014 formalize the heritable unit as an irreducible RAF, roughly equivalent to a viable core. This is the low-overlap mathematical witness for the figure claim that a term carries atoms *and* bonds (Fig 12 :5, Fig 13 :129). It is also the main shared evidence pool with Hordijk et al. 2018, so the two are one witness.
- **Experimental heritability without genes.** Sokolskyi et al. 2024 is the only opened wet-lab source that directly measures heritable variation in a prebiotically plausible chemical system. It is a mechanism-shape analogue, not a demonstration of inheritance across dividing compartments, and its own abstract says experimental validation has been lacking.
- **Membrane physics as habitat.** Taneja and Higgs 2025 supply the physical other half: an active internal reaction is required for a sustainable growth and division cycle, and division produces unequal daughters whose internal concentrations are disrupted and then reconverge. This is the world-side counterpart of Fig 11 :66-69.
- **The pessimistic result.** Vasas, Szathmáry and Santos 2010, *"Lack of evolvability in self-sustaining autocatalytic networks constraints metabolism-first scenarios for the origin of life"*, PNAS 107(4):1470-1475. Direct fetch returned 403; bibliographic details and the quoted phrase were confirmed through the Hordijk and Steel 2014 reference list and the search-indexed abstract. Reduced weight, recorded as such in `10_source_ledger.csv`. It is not treated as a refutation of the figure verdict; section 4 of `01` returns the disagreement to the census.
- **Compartments and the origin of heredity.** The opened Springer and PMC articles consistently cite Szathmáry's stochastic corrector model and Gánti's chemoton as prior frames, and note that most protocell heredity models concern an RNA world, a later stage than the question asks about. That is an honest scope limit on the world corroboration.

- **Fig 4 :22** - *"Only methods cross up. Only head starts come down."* Sending the full architecture before anyone has described a residual sends a recording, not a generator. Lead with the shape claim and the refuter; offer links after.

- **Fig 2 :40** - *"disqualifying for a verdict: agreement about the world is worthless when the agreeing frames share an evidence pool"* Agreement from RAF-theory models is one witness, not several.

**Recommended order in a reply:** (1) the residual, shape claim and refuter; (2) ask what they would count as a failure; (3) record their verdict; (4) only then the local figure links, with the note that the figures, not the summary, are the authority.

## The two-stage offer

1. **Stage 1.** Validate the shape against the ground. The recipient checks the claim without holding the framework's interior (Fig 10 :52, Fig 2 :45).
2. **Stage 2, opt-in only.** If curious, read the theory that produced it. Their stage-1 verdict is already recorded and is never pooled as corroboration (Fig 2 :40).
3. **Stage 3.** Offer the formula as a method to reuse. It is a generator, so giving it away does not give away an answer (Fig 4 :22). Each user must write a ground and a refuter for their own problem, because a frame cannot check the direction of its own question.

**Hard precondition:** stages 1 to 3 work only if the stage-1 shape survives. It is `specified`, not `grounded`, and the chemical identity is an open gap. That limitation must be in the first message.
