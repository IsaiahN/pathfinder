# Outreach draft: check one shape before you decide about the theory

**Use:** one to one, or as a cover note attached to a submission through the public venues in `03_public_contact_points.csv`. **Not** for bulk send.

**Hard precondition:** do not send until the shape claim and its refuters are stated in the recipient's own terms. The offer works only if stage 1 can be checked without holding the framework's interior.

---

**Subject:** A checkable claim about how a reaction network could cross a compartment division

> Hi [name],
>
> I have one specific output I would like you to check against what you already know. I am not asking you to read a theory first, and I am not asking you to agree with anything. If the output does not survive, there is nothing else to look at.
>
> **The residual (`specified`, not `grounded`).** A reaction network in a compartment is a pattern of catalytic relations, not a bag of molecules. When the compartment divides, the daughter gets a new boundary and a stochastic, unequal sample of the parent's contents. What has to cross for the daughter to re-run the same network? My derived answer is a shape with three parts, and it deliberately does not name a molecule:
>
> 1. **What crosses is the arrangement, not the ingredient list.** A list of species is not a network; the catalytic relations carry the information the list omits. What is inherited has to be a relation pattern, not a frozen concentration snapshot.
> 2. **What crosses is a generator or a head start, not a recording.** A seed that lets the daughter regenerate the closure makes the next search cheaper. A one-occasion replay does not. In chemical terms this is a catalyst that survives its own use and a cycle whose output re-enters as input.
> 3. **A lineage needs two factors, not one.** Fidelity of the handoff and pressure from the ground. Perfect fidelity with no selection gives persistence, not evolution. No fidelity gives no inheritance. The daughter is also a substituted habitat: it inherits boundary and environmental actors it did not choose, and it can lose a required catalyst by unequal sampling.
>
> **What I am explicitly not claiming.** I am not claiming this is how life actually began, not naming a molecule, not giving a fidelity number, and not claiming a new general theory of abiogenesis. The framework returns a kind of thing, not an answer. The chemical identity of the carrier is an open `gap`, and I am reporting it as unreached rather than filling it.
>
> **What would refute it. I wrote these down before sending.**
> 1. The daughter re-runs the network with the catalytic relations destroyed or scrambled while the ingredient list is preserved.
> 2. A frozen one-occasion recording, with no seed and no re-applicable relation, is sufficient for the re-run.
> 3. Perfect fidelity with no selection among compartments still produces open-ended heritable complexity.
> 4. A division removes the habitat rather than substituting it, or the daughter inherits all required contact with no unchosen actor.
> 5. A heritable network is derived with no catalyst or cycle composition.
>
> If any one holds, I will retract or narrow the claim. A one-line "this fails because..." is more useful to me than agreement. If you would rather not engage, no reply is needed.
>
> **If it survives, and only then:** the sources are below. The figures, not my summary of them, are the authority.
> - Verbatim figure text with line numbers (local framework extract): `FRAMEWORK/reference/03-FIGURES_TEXT.md`
> - Canonical usage rule (local framework file): `FRAMEWORK/reference/FIGURES_README_canonical.md`
> - The eight-step loop and mode rule (local framework file): `FRAMEWORK/figures/THE_FORMULA.md`
>
> **If you want to use the machinery:** the formula is in the figures link. Run it on a problem you can check against a ground. Write down what would refute your result *before* you run it, and state the mode of what you get. A reported failure is more useful to me than a reported success.
>
> Thanks for reading,
> [name / project account]

## What is deliberately absent

- No prestige language, no "breakthrough", no "true answer", no "trust me". Prestige can fund a test but cannot settle one (*The Recursive Self-Improvement Trap*).
- No personal emails, no ranked recipient list, no bulk send.
- No claim that this simulated derivation settles anything. It is checked for internal consistency against the figures only.
