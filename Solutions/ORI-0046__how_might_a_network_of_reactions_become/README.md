## What this package is

A simulated derivation checked for internal consistency against the figures, NOT a ground reading. It cannot settle the origins-of-life question it reports on. Every substantive file states its mode. The verdict is a shape plus a pre-registered refuter, offered to outside frames before any theory is shown.

## Residual in effect terms

A reaction network inside a compartment is a dynamic pattern of catalytic relations. At division the daughter receives a fresh boundary and a stochastic sample of the parent's molecules. The residual is: what must cross the division so the daughter can re-run the *same* network, and what determines whether what crosses is the network (a generator) or only a snapshot of its molecules (a recording)? Arity: at least two conditions, a carrier that crosses and a habitat in which it can re-run. Symmetry: ordered, parent to daughter (the production/sequence bonds, not the conjunction bond). Scale: first order inside a compartment, second order across compartment generations.

## What is deliberately not here

- No personal emails of named individuals.

Reason, in the figures' own words: *"Alignment is triangulation, not negotiation. A mirror cannot correct you."* (Fig 2 :35) and *"High correlation is disqualifying for a verdict and efficient for a convention."* (Fig 2 :40) A list selected for receptiveness manufactures the correlation that makes its agreement worthless.
