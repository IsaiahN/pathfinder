## QUESTION

Decide, in effect terms, what the Tether says about the question: can institutions be deliberately designed for conditions that do not yet exist, or do they mainly adapt through trial and error? Option A: answer "design," meaning a seat can specify an institution for a future condition. Option B: answer "trial and error," meaning selection alone does the work and design is an illusion. What each leads to: A ignores the signal precondition and the asymmetry of introduction, so it produces unenumerated cascades and inert paper reforms; B ignores the maintained seat, so it gives up the only lever that works on arrival. The bound shape: a union of two regimes with different seats, a signal precondition on design for the not-yet-existing, and a ground requirement for the empirical "mainly."

- **Domain 2, the unmaintained selection frame.** Actors: institutional lineages, conditions, the ground. Currency: differential persistence. Anchor: the ground, which does not update. Fig 11 :56: *"Unmaintained. No maintainer and no live frame in the seat. The habitat is pure contact, nothing selects, and what survives is what the ground kept."* Nameable anchor, so a domain.

- **Domain 4, the verification frame.** Actors: expert readers of institutional design research. Currency: agreement and residuals. Anchor: the ground, never agreement. ## VERDICT (from the figures)

The question's either/or is refused as posed. Design and adaptation are two regimes of one habitat relation, distinguished by the presence of a maintainer seat, and their verdicts are kept separate and never averaged. A seat can introduce and cannot subtract, its work is permanent, and a design that leaves contact unchanged is inert. Without a seat, selection keeps what the ground kept, and calling the survivor designed is a label applied afterwards. Designing for a not-yet-existing condition is possible only where something at some resolution is already failing to resolve; theory can point an instrument, as with Neptune, but a first sensor for a never-registered condition cannot be specified. Where no signal registers, what is available is a seed, an enumerated habitat, and a reachable channel to the ground. The union must be unbundled, the cascade enumerated past first order, and the affordance and progress link instrumented before planning. The empirical "mainly" is a `gap` at the ground, not a `grounded` answer. Two strains remain live: the signal-precondition discriminator (Neptune versus no-first-sensor) needs an instrument, not an argument, and the "mainly" wording cannot be answered without averaging what the figures forbid.

- **cost if wrong:** bounded and locatable for the shape, but real for the prescription. If design is over-claimed, the failure mode is the unenumerated cascade, named in Fig 11 :65 as *"Cane toads and kudzu are the same move with the cascade unenumerated."* If adaptation is over-claimed, the only lever that works on arrival is surrendered. The avoidable cost is spending the independence of outside readers by sending a recording instead of a generator, which Fig 2 :42 prices (*"Buy independence in proportion to the cost of being wrong."*). Leading with the shape and its refuters avoids it.
- **undoable:** only partly. The shape claim can be revised and the packet superseded without withdrawing any primitive. But an introduced institutional actor cannot be cleanly subtracted from a habitat: Fig 11 :67 *"Move the residual and you must reproduce everything its goal required, and you inherit the new habitat's actors, which you did not choose."* So a bad introduction is not fully undoable, and that asymmetry is itself one of the findings.
- **reversed by:** a ground reading, not agreement. A documented natural experiment showing that an institution specified with no prior signal at any resolution changed contact and survived would break Fig 6 :40; a seat shown to subtract an actor, or a design shown to permanently solve contact, would break Fig 11 :59 and :61; an instrument showing a material third-order residual absent would break Fig 11 :52; an instrument distinguishing designed from selected persistence would close the source `gap`; and a re-census admitting that "mainly" is one quantity rather than a union would break Fig 9 :54. Until one of these fires, the verdict stands as `candidate`.
