## Independent domain census

I redid the census before reading the primary's conclusions, using the WATSON order: R by arity, symmetry and scale; one line per domain with actors, currency and anchor; partition or union; order by dependency.

- **R (arity / symmetry / scale).** The residual is the gap between present institutional contact and contact required by a condition not yet present. Arity: two named options that do not exhaust the space, so I initially marked **union** and kept it after re-census. Symmetry: asymmetric, one side has a seat and the other does not (Fig 11 :59). Scale: selection across a lineage of trials versus design within one maintained habitat at a chosen moment. Indexed to an anchor, not averaged.
- **Domain 1, maintained design.** Actors: seat, designer, rules, instruments, introduced actors. Currency: introduction. Anchor: the ground plus the enumerated habitat. Fig 11 :58: *"That is the garden, and it is how arrival is worked on rather than waited for."* Real domain.
- **Domain 2, unmaintained selection.** Actors: institutional lineages, conditions, ground. Currency: differential persistence. Anchor: the ground, which does not update. Fig 11 :56: *"what survives is what the ground kept"* Real domain.
- **Domain 3, anticipation.** Actors: designer, residual, proto-instruments. Currency: description and prediction. Anchor: whether anything at any resolution is already returning a failing signal. Fig 6 :34: *"The question is not whether a sensor could exist. It is whether anything, at any resolution, is already returning something that fails to resolve."* Real domain; binds Domain 1.
- **Domain 4, verification.** Actors: expert readers. Currency: agreement and residual. Anchor: the ground, never agreement. In this simulated run its independence is zero.

Order by dependency: enumerate before introducing (Fig 11 :43); establish affordance and progress terms before planning (Fig 3 :26); signal test before specification (Fig 6 :34); refuse before mint (Formula step 2); ground before agreement (Fig 2 :29).

My census matches the primary's four domains and its union reading. Where the primary derived from Fig 11 and Fig 9, I derived from Fig 6's signal rule and Fig 3's dependency chain. Different starting figures, same boundaries: low overlap between routes, so the agreement is weak but not circular within the framework. It is still one evidence pool.

## Round trip gap `R_T`

The Symbols table defines `R_T` as the gap between what was sent up and what came back down, `gap(x, (T_E ∘ T_A)(x))`, measuring what the coarser description cannot hold. Fig 4 :30 requires it to be measured rather than assumed. I ran the round trip on the condensation the folder actually uses.

1. **Sent up (x):** the verbatim figure set, chiefly Fig 11 :55, :58, :59, :61 (two modes and asymmetry), Fig 6 :34, :40 (signal precondition), Fig 9 :54 (union), Fig 3 :26 (middle link).
2. **Abstraction (T_A):** the shorthand carried in `07_the_tether_answer.md`: institutions mainly adapt but a seat can work on arrival; design for the unseen needs a signal; the either/or is a union; enumerate first.
3. **Concretisation (T_E):** a prescription for a specific institution, for example "write a constitution designed for a future climate-migration regime."

**`R_T` is nonzero.** Four things the shorthand holds do not survive the trip back down:

- the **signal test** is silently dropped when a specific design is written, which is the Neptune versus no-first-sensor strain;
- the **asymmetry of introduction** is dropped when the design assumes rules can be withdrawn as easily as added;
- the **third-order cascade** is dropped when enumeration stops at direct contact;
- the **union reading** is dropped when the prescription answers "mainly" with one mechanism.

One thing survives cleanly: a seat can act on arrival only by changing contact, and without a seat selection keeps what the ground kept. That is the residue worth sending. `R_T` was **not** measured numerically, because no ground reading exists; it is recorded qualitatively as the enumerated set of dropped constraints. Treating "the formula is the condensed version of the metalaws" as identity would set `R_T` to zero and would be the dead-reckoning error Fig 4 :30 names. The honest form is a lossy condensation with a measured gap, which is what `09_watson_answer.md` states.
