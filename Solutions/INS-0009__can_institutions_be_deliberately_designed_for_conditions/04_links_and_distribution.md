## Three things in the literature that bear directly on this question

1. **Hayek 1945 is the low-overlap source for the adaptation half.** It is not a Tether source and it does not share the framework's evidence pool. Its claim that the relevant knowledge is dispersed and particular to time and place is the strongest available outside witness for why a central design cannot simply enumerate everything that matters. It corroborates Fig 11 :43 (*"The habitat is enumerated, never composed."*) at the level of a reason, not a quotation. It does not decide the verdict.
2. **The 2007 mechanism-design account is the low-overlap source for the design half.** It states that theory *"provides a tool for characterizing the optimal institution for any given set of conditions."* That is a real, formal design discipline. It corroborates the maintained-seat half of Fig 11 :58 and simultaneously shows that design is conditional on a stated set of conditions, which is exactly the figure's signal requirement.
3. **Gray 1982 is the sharpest statement of the constructivist-rationalist failure.** *"social order cannot be the product of anything resembling conscious control or rational design."* It corroborates the limit on design, but note the overlap: Gray is writing about Hayek, and Hayek is already in the witness list. Under Fig 2 :40 these are one pool, not two independent witnesses. Recorded as such in the ledger.

No world source conflicts with the figure verdict. The conflict rule was not triggered. Agreement here is expected and carries limited independent weight, because the framework essays share an author with the figures.

## Distribution: offer, do not push

Figure-grounded reasons:

- **Fig 4 :22:** *"Only methods cross up. Only head starts come down."* Sending the full architecture before anyone has described a residual sends a recording, not a generator. Lead with the union claim and its refuter; offer links after.
- **Fig 10 :42:** *"A verifier that can reconstruct the claim cannot verify it."* A recipient who has absorbed the entire framework before being asked for a verdict has joined the frame being checked.
- **Fig 9 :27:** *"describe it before you go looking, or any frame you pick will seem to fit"* Ask what they would expect to see if the shape claim were true before giving them the article.
- **Fig 2 :40:** *"disqualifying for a verdict: agreement about the world is worthless when the agreeing frames share an evidence pool"* A send selected for people who already prefer design or already prefer emergence produces a convention, not corroboration.
- **Fig 2 :42:** *"Buy independence in proportion to the cost of being wrong."* The cost here is a wrong institutional prescription, so independent readers are worth more than agreeable ones.

**Recommended order in a reply:** (1) the residual, the union claim and the four refuters; (2) ask what they would count as a failure; (3) record their verdict; (4) only then the links, with the note that the figures, not the summary, are the authority.

## The two-stage offer

1. **Stage 1 before stage 2** is Fig 10 :52, *"Bring a characterised residual, never the source,"* and Fig 2 :45, *"Frames range against the ground. They do not negotiate with it."* The recipient checks the output without holding the framework's interior.
2. **Stage 2 is opt-in by construction.** Once a recipient has absorbed the theory, their agreement stops being evidence. Their stage-1 verdict must be recorded first and never pooled as corroboration.
3. **Stage 3 sends a method**, which Fig 4 :22 permits. The formula is a generator, so giving it away does not give away an answer. Each user still needs a ground per problem and must write their own refuter before the run.

**Hard precondition:** stages 1 to 3 only work if the stage-1 output survives. In this folder it is a shape with two named strains (`01_figure_grounded_audit.md` §6), not a settled prescription. Send it as such.
