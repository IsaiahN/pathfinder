# Tether outreach - figure-grounded package

**Question:** Can institutions be deliberately designed for conditions that do not yet exist, or do they mainly adapt through trial and error?

**Source:** `Questions/institutional questions.txt` line 12 (question 9), section 1, Institutional origins and development.

**Mode:** `specified` on the substantive shape and on each per-domain verdict. `general` on the structural rule that the two named mechanisms are two modes of one habitat relation. Nothing here is `grounded`: no measurement, natural experiment, or ground reading was taken. This is a simulated derivation checked for internal consistency against the figures, not a ground reading, so it cannot settle the question it reports on.

**Status:** done.

**Registry status:** pending (set by the registry pipeline, not by this folder).

**Boundary.** Work was confined to this folder. The framework files read were the canonical local copies under `FRAMEWORK/reference/` and `FRAMEWORK/figures/`. No file outside this folder was modified. World pages actually fetched on 2026-11-23 are listed in `10_source_ledger.csv`.

## What is deliberately not here

- No personal emails of named individuals.
- No ranking of people by how likely they are to agree with the claim.
- No bulk-send plan.

Three reasons, in the figures' own words:

1. **A room is not an anchor.** Fig 2 :35: *"Alignment is triangulation, not negotiation. A mirror cannot correct you."* A list selected for receptiveness manufactures the correlation that would make its agreement worthless (Fig 2 :40).
2. **A verifier must not hold the interiors.** Fig 10 :42: *"A verifier that can reconstruct the claim cannot verify it."* The first contact carries the residual and its refuter, never the framework.
3. **Prestige is not action.** From *The Recursive Self-Improvement Trap*: *"Prestige can fund an action. It can give an action a budget to be tried. But it cannot settle an action. Only the ground can settle it."* The ask gives the claim a budget to be tried; it does not ask anyone to endorse it.

Legally: scraping personal emails for unsolicited bulk outreach breaches GDPR or CAN-SPAM style rules in many jurisdictions and will get a sending domain blacklisted. That is a practical failure on top of the structural one.

## Suggested next step

Take `01`, `08`, and `02` to the public venues in `03`. The audience that can help is the one that has opted in to receiving claims and is positioned to refute them, not a purchased list of believers. Record the stage-1 verdict before showing any theory. A failure is more useful than an endorsement.
