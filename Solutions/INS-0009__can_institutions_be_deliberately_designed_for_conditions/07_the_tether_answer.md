# The Tether's answer: design versus trial and error for conditions that do not yet exist

**Mode: `specified` on the shape and on each per-domain verdict. `general` on the structural claim that the two options are two regimes of one relation. Nothing here is `grounded`.** This is a simulated derivation checked for internal consistency against the figures (`05_what_shape_means.md` §7), not a ground reading.

## 1. The residual it answers

An institution faces a condition that does not yet exist. The question asks which mechanism closes the gap between its present contact and the contact that condition would require: deliberate design, or adaptation through trial and error. The residual is indexed to an anchor, not averaged into a "mainly" score (Fig 1 :19: *"The residual is indexed by object slot, not aggregated."*).

## 2. The shape, in five moves

1. **The either/or is a union, not a partition.** Fig 11 :55: *"Two modes, and only one of them has a seat."* The maintained mode has a maintainer who can choose instruments and introduce actors; the unmaintained mode has no seat and the ground keeps what survives. Every designed institution that persists is still selected; every emergent one that persists usually had a maintainer. Fig 9 :54: *"And when the split lands on a union rather than a partition, unbundle before you search again."* Verdicts stay separate and are never averaged.

2. **Deliberate design is real, with three hard limits.** Fig 11 :58: *"That is the garden, and it is how arrival is worked on rather than waited for."* But the seat *"can add an actor and you cannot subtract the habitat"* (Fig 11 :59), a managed habitat *"is permanent work rather than a solved state"* (Fig 11 :61), and *"An improvement that does not change contact changes nothing, however much it improves"* (Fig 11 :40). A design that changes paper without changing contact is inert.

3. **Trial and error is selection without a seat, not the absence of a mechanism.** Fig 11 :36: *"Where nothing maintains anything, intelligence is a label applied afterwards to a lineage that met its ground. Nothing was intelligent; selection ran. The word does no work there."* Fig 7 :35: *"A room's agreement is variation, not a verdict. Selection between rooms is where the ground votes."* Calling the survivor "well designed" is a label applied after the fact.

4. **Designing for a not-yet-existing condition has a signal precondition.** Fig 6 :34: *"The question is not whether a sensor could exist. It is whether anything, at any resolution, is already returning something that fails to resolve."* Theory may precede the instrument: *"Neptune was calculated before it was seen, and the prediction is what told anyone where to point"* (Fig 6 scope note). But Fig 6 :40: *"What is unavailable is a sensor for something that has never registered anywhere, at any resolution. That is the whole of it."* Where nothing registers, the honest moves are to carry a seed (Fig 4 :34: *"A seed carries no small tree: it carries what will grow one, and the conditions it lands in decide what it becomes."*), enumerate the habitat outward, and keep the channel to the ground reachable.

5. **Enumeration before introduction, and an instrument on the middle link.** Fig 11 :43: *"The habitat is enumerated, never composed."* First-order enumeration misses third-order effects (Fig 11 :52, the DDT cascade). And the usual failure is not in planning but one link earlier: Fig 3 :26: *"The link between them, having terms for what a situation offers and being able to say what would count as progress, has neither, so it attracts the least attention and the fewest instruments."* You cannot plan for a condition until you can say what it would afford and what would count as progress.

## 3. The answer stated plainly

Institutions mainly adapt in the sense that the ground always does the selecting, even inside a designed habitat. But a maintainer seat can work on arrival rather than wait for it, within the asymmetry and permanence limits above. Deliberate design for a genuinely not-yet-existing condition is possible only where some signal at some resolution is already failing to resolve; otherwise what can be designed is the seed and the maintained contact, not the final institution. The question's "or" is the error. The `specified` answer is the union and its limits; the `grounded` answer to the empirical "mainly" is a `gap` until a natural experiment measures it.

## 4. What it explicitly does not say

- Not that deliberation is useless, or that trial and error is blind in an absolute sense. Each is a regime with its own seat condition.
- Not that either mechanism wins, and not a numerical "mainly" score. Fig 1 :22: *"Aggregating across slots is how a live signal disappears into an average."*
- Not that any particular constitution, court, market or policy is the answer. The framework returns a shape, not a name.
- Not that a first sensor can be specified for a condition that has never registered, at any resolution. That is exactly what Fig 6 :40 refuses.
- Not that a measured institution in this folder settled anything. No measurement was taken.
- Not that the held primitive set is nature's own list. It is a visible guess (They're Scanning Books).

## 5. What would settle it - the ground

A ground reading, not further agreement (Fig 2 :29: *"An anchor is whatever settles the question and does not change its answer because of what any frame thinks."*). Concretely:

1. **The signal test.** For a named future condition, document what at any resolution was already returning a failing signal before the design was made. If nothing was, the design is in the Fig 6 :40 regime and the claim is constrained accordingly.
2. **The contact test.** Measure whether the institution's contact with its conditions changed, rather than its rules or its reputation. The inert case changes the latter and not the former (Fig 11 :40).
3. **The cascade test.** Enumerate actors, conditions and relations to second and third order and measure the residual that matters. The DDT case in Fig 11 :52 is the standing example.
4. **The middle-link test.** Instrument what the future condition affords and what counts as progress toward it (Fig 3 :27: *"A link with no instrument is not a link that works. It is a link nobody has looked at."*).
5. **The source-discriminator test.** Since several origins can give the same persistence reading (*The Hard Problem is About Source*: *"If several paths create the same behavior, then behavior is a projection with lossy information"*), build an instrument that distinguishes designed from selected persistence, or record that none exists yet.

Standing falsifiers from `01_figure_grounded_audit.md` §4 apply. Any one firing retracts the corresponding finding.

## 6. The honest stop

**Unreached, not unreachable.** The signal precondition is a `gap` at the ground for any specific future condition, because no instrument in this run decides whether something is registering at every resolution. Fig 8 :39: *"So a search that finds nothing is never a proof of absence."* Fig 2 :42: *"Buy independence in proportion to the cost of being wrong."* The stop is recorded with its lineage so a later reader can locate the error rather than only feel it. Prestige or agreement is not an instrument: *"Prestige can fund an action. It can give an action a budget to be tried. But it cannot settle an action. Only the ground can settle it."* (*The Recursive Self-Improvement Trap*).

## 7. One-paragraph version for outreach

> The Tether does not pick a winner between deliberate design and trial and error, because they are not two answers to one question. They are two regimes of one habitat relation: a maintained one, where a seat can introduce actors but cannot subtract the habitat and the work is never finished, and an unmaintained one, where selection keeps what the ground kept and calling it design is a label applied afterwards. A designed institution is still selected, and an emergent one usually had a maintainer. The hard case, designing for conditions that do not yet exist, has a sharp precondition: you can carry a seed and point an instrument with a prediction, but you cannot specify a first sensor for something that has never registered anywhere at any resolution. Where nothing registers, enumerate the habitat outward, instrument what the future condition would afford and what would count as progress, and keep the channel to the ground reachable. What this offers is a shape, written refuters, and the names of the instruments that would close it. The empirical "mainly" stays open until a natural experiment measures contact, not reputation.
