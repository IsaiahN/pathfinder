# Outreach draft - check one output before deciding about the framework

**Use:** one-to-one, or as a cover letter attached to a submission through the public venues in `03_public_contact_points.csv`. **Not** for bulk send.

**Hard precondition:** do not send until the separator and the refuters in `01_figure_grounded_audit.md` sections 3 and 4 are stable. The output must survive validation or the two-stage offer collapses on contact.

---

**Subject:** One separator to check before we argue about active versus passive management

> Hi [name],
>
> I have one specific output I would like you to check against what you already know from
> ecosystem management. I am not asking you to read a framework first, and I am not asking you
> to agree with anything. If the output does not survive, there is nothing else to look at.
>
> **The output (`specified` on the per-domain claims, `general` on the shape, not `grounded`):**
> the question is not one dial with a right amount of intervention. It is a union of at least
> four overlapping conditions with different anchors, so the two outcomes have to be checked
> per species and patch and kept separate.
>
> 1. A manager is a seat acting on a habitat. The seat can introduce an actor but cannot
>    subtract the habitat, so managed ecosystems are permanent work rather than a solved state.
>    Introduction strengthens resilience when the cascade was enumerated outward until it
>    stopped mattering, and becomes the cane-toad or kudzu case when it was not.
> 2. Isolation is substitution, not removal. A reserve, a translocation site or a model-based
>    reference condition is a different habitat with actors nobody chose. Two failures are
>    silent until late: what you failed to reproduce, and what you unintentionally introduced.
> 3. A convention becomes a verdict the moment it decides an outcome. A target species list
>    that overrides a ground reading of persistence is no longer a convention.
> 4. A habitat's own adaptation is selection between rooms. A management regime that keeps the
>    same target while the question moves is a chain of mirrors: motion without progress.
>
> No actor has a standing positive valence. The same actor is a benefactor to one residual and
> a malfactor to another. So I am not offering a list of good techniques or a universal
> intervention level. The framework returns a shape, not a name.
>
> **What I am not claiming:** that any particular percentage or species list is optimal; that
> non-intervention is generally better than intervention; that a successful pilot plot licenses
> landscape application; that a restored historical structure is the same thing as a restored
> function. Those are bindings and recordings, and they need provenance and a ground reading
> before they carry any weight.
>
> **What would refute the output (written before this message):**
> 1. An intervention that changes only internal bookkeeping, leaves contact with the ground
>    unchanged, and still improves persistence against a non-updating anchor.
> 2. A seat that can remove a contact relation and still keep the ground reachable after it
>    withdraws.
> 3. An introduced actor whose full cascade was enumerated, including third order effects,
>    that still reduced persistence.
> 4. A fixed regime that tracks every change of the question and preserves autonomous
>    selection, measured against an anchor that does not update.
> 5. A pooled "resilience index" that predicts every species and patch as well as per-species
>    readings across a regime shift.
>
> If any one holds, the corresponding condition is wrong and I will retract it. A one-line
> "this fails because..." is more useful to me than agreement. If you would rather not engage,
> no reply is needed.
>
> **If it survives, and only then:** here are the sources. The figure text, not my summary of
> it, is the authority.
> - Canonical figure text with line numbers (local repo path on request).
> - The verification packet with the four figure passages and the three checkable sub-claims.
> - The published figure set and formula: https://github.com/IsaiahN/tether/tree/general-agent/figures
>
> **If you want to reuse the method:** the formula is in the figures link. Run it on a site you
> know well and can check against a ground. Write down what would refute your result *before*
> you run it, and state the mode of what you get. A reported failure is worth more to me than a
> reported success.

## Why this version is ordered this way

| the move | figure-grounded reason |
|---|---|
| Output first, theory second | Fig 10 :52 "Bring a characterised residual, never the source." The recipient checks the separator without holding the framework interior. |
| Do not ask for agreement | Fig 2 :35 "Alignment is triangulation, not negotiation. A mirror cannot correct you." |
| Pre-registered refuter before conclusion | A falsifier written before the run is a design instrument. |
| Per-species and per-patch indexing | Fig 1 :19 "The residual is indexed by object slot, not aggregated." Fig 1 :22 "Aggregating across slots is how a live signal disappears into an average." |
| Union framing, not one dial | Fig 9 :55 "A bundled problem is usually a union of overlapping conditions, not a list of separate ones." |
| No standing list of good techniques | Fig 11 :51 "No standing list of good actors survives a change of residual." |
| Target treated as convention until it decides an outcome | Fig 10 :47 "a convention that decides an outcome has stopped being one" |
| No universal number offered | Fig 9 :32 "you get a kind of thing, never the answer itself"; Fig 13 :133 "what it returns is a shape rather than a name" |
| Source links only after the verdict | Fig 4 :22 "Only methods cross up. Only head starts come down." A recording sent first spends the reader's independence. |
| No appeal to authority or prestige | From *The Recursive Self-Improvement Trap*: "Prestige can fund an action. It can give an action a budget to be tried. But it cannot settle an action. Only the ground can settle it." |
| Ask for a refutation, not an endorsement | Fig 2 :40 "disqualifying for a verdict: agreement about the world is worthless when the agreeing frames share an evidence pool" |
| Offer the formula as method, not answer | Fig 4 :23 "upward: a method that can be reapplied. Never the recording of a particular success." |
