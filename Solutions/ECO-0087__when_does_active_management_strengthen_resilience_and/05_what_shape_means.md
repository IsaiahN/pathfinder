## 1. A shape is a constraint, not a filler

The canonical figures README states the limit: the framework returns "what a missing thing would have to look like (a shape, not a name)." Fig 9 :32: "you get a kind of thing, never the answer itself." Fig 13 :133: "what it returns is a shape rather than a name." Fig 13 :135: "It cannot say which primitive is missing, because naming it would mean already holding it."

From *The Library of Babel* (**carried**): "You can cut your search short if the set of books returned is small and descriptive enough that it provides a general shape of the answer that satisfies your question."

A **shape** is the narrowed form of an answer you can state before you can name it. It constrains; it does not fill. For this question, "a seat can introduce but cannot subtract; enumeration trips before introduction; a target becomes a verdict when it decides an outcome; a fixed room fails when the question moves" is a shape. "Managed at 30 percent intensity beats no management" is a name, and a number, and is not licensed by the figures.

## 2. How a shape is obtained

- *Library of Babel* (**carried**): use the vocabulary you already have that is closest to the thing, look for recurring new terms that overlap or correlate with it, recurse. "With each new term gained, you narrow the space and deepen your vocabulary."
- Fig 6 :30: "outward: does another frame already hold it? The description travels. Fails by being sharpened into terms only we can read. Cast it in effect terms." Fig 9 :30: "your words are borrowed too, so describe the effect rather than the cause."
- *Decoding Tacit Knowledge* (**carried**): "When contact isn't possible, the best you can send is a plain description of what you'd expect to see, because anyone can check that against the world."
- *Library of Babel* (**carried**) on stopping: the answer is reached when "the finish line will also be the starting place", and the paradox is that by then you have "composed the answer through the process of locating it."

For this question the effect description is deliberately free of the words "resilience" and "adaptation" until they are bound. Those words are borrowed from the question. What can be checked in the world is: did contact change, was the cascade enumerated, did the anchor update, did the room match the question.

## 3. What licenses a name

Only two things, both outside the shape itself:

1. **The ground settles it.** `THE_FORMULA.md` step 5 says the ground settles a term, it is verified only by what pays, and a gate is not the ground. The canonical README: the framework "stops at the limit of what the available instruments can distinguish, and records the lineage so the stop is honest."
2. **A provenanced import from a frame whose closure differs** (Fig 6 :23, Fig 8 :51-52), with a source gate and a shadow test.

*They're Scanning Books* (**carried**) sharpens the second: "The real import is not a new term in the existing space. It is a new dimension of the space." And: "A minimum viable set of six terms is a human's careful guess at reality's compression axes; another sensorium's minimum set need not be those six." Also: "Declaring the primitives does not make them neutral. It makes the choice visible."

So "disturbance regime", "keystone actor" and "reference condition" are visible choices, not discoveries of nature's own list. Naming one as *the* answer to a slot turns a visible choice into an invisible one.

## 4. Why a source reading is not a source identification

*The Hard Problem is About Source* (**carried**) gives the mechanism. Metamerism: an instrument reads green and "cannot comment on the source" (one green light, or yellow plus blue, or RGB, or CMYK, or a scheme not yet discovered). The reading does not determine the source. "If several paths create the same behavior, then behavior is a projection with lossy information." And: "An instrument that can't distinguish these cases demonstrates its own inadequacy." It was the spectrometer, not an argument, that resolved it.

Here the corresponding error is naming the cause of a recovery from a single "resilience score". High survival could come from a seat keeping contact, from the system's own selection, from a substituted habitat with easier conditions, or from a metric that stopped updating. One number cannot comment on which. What is owed is a source-discriminating instrument, not a further argument.

## 5. Q: what licenses a particular intervention technique?

Nothing in the framework. The framework returns a shape. A particular technique is licensed only by the ground settling a minted term, or by a provenanced import with a shadow test. Until one of those, the name is the searcher's own contribution and must be recorded as such.

Does the audit commit the error the README forbids? With the narrowing in section 6, no. `01_figure_grounded_audit.md` F4 explicitly refuses a standing list of good techniques and F11 refuses a universal number. It names held bindings (introduction, reference condition, disturbance regime) only as provenance from the management frame, and marks each as ungrounded until a ground pays for it.

## 6. Correction to my own earlier reading of "shape, not a name"

I initially treated the phrase as a ban on naming any technique. That overstates it. The canonical phrase is "what a **missing** thing would have to look like (a shape, not a name)". Its subject is a primitive not held in Γ. "Disturbance", "network", "spectrum" style bindings onto actors already in the habitat are not missing primitives. Naming one is a binding from the management frame onto something already enumerable, not the invention of a seventh term.

What remains wrong when a technique is named without provenance is the source error of section 4, not a ban on naming held atoms. Both claims are now recorded so neither is quietly lost. The same narrowing applies across this folder: the gap in F11 is about a universal rule or number, not about whether one may discuss a named intervention.

## 7. Prestige is not action (opened this session)

From *The Recursive Self-Improvement Trap* (2026-11-19): "Prestige is not equal to actions." And: "Prestige can fund an action. It can give an action a budget to be tried. But it cannot settle an action. Only the ground can settle it. The Ground Maintainer must be blind to prestige." Also: "If the maintainer is swayed by prestige, the channel to the ground is broken. And a broken channel looks exactly like a working one, until the drift is so large that the system collapses."

For ecosystem management, this is the rule against settling a technique by the reputation of the institution that recommends it, or by the number of sites that adopted it. Adoption gives the technique a budget to be tried. It does not settle whether the ground paid.

The same essay gives the bridge to the habitat figure: "Natural selection is what the loop looks like when there is no one maintaining the ground. When there is no one in the Ground Maintainer seat, the environment is the only selector." And: "The same code runs when there is a Ground Maintainer. It runs in cultivation, when humans select which plants to breed. It runs in habitat formation, when beavers build dams and change the selection pressures." This is a direct, non-analogical statement that managed and unmaintained habitats run the same loop with a different seat, which is exactly the isomer claim in `06_isomers_and_order.md`.

## 8. My own audit is a simulation, not a ground reading

Same essay, opened this session: "the output is only as good as the validator. The agent, or the tool, or the frame, or the theory, cannot validate itself. An external validator, or ground, is always needed to tether the veracity of the output to reality. Anything else is drift." And: "LLMs can quickly simulate an approximate answer/output with the formula found in the architecture post... However, the truly testable output would need to be derived from the real architecture or working the formula yourself."

So `01`, `06`, `07`, `09` and `11` are simulated derivations checked for internal consistency against the figures. They are **not** tests of the claim and must be labelled as such. They cannot settle anything they report on. Also, from the same essay: "Metatheories cannot validate themselves." No amount of agreement among these files adds a witness.
