## 4. What the wider literature contributes

1. **The question is a union, not two signs of one dial.** Rewilding gives passive and active variants, adaptive management gives passive and active variants, novel-ecosystem work gives managing against versus managing for. Three independent vocabularies for the same structural split. This corroborates Fig 9 :55 without deciding which variant wins at a site.
2. **A baseline is a convention, not a ground.** Ecological restoration reports shifting baselines, and novel-ecosystem work says climate change often makes historical baselines unattainable. This corroborates Fig 10 :47 "a convention that decides an outcome has stopped being one".
3. **Action bias is a channel failure, not evidence of benefit.** The environmental-decision finding that action is preferred under uncertainty and delayed effect corroborates Fig 2 :34 (coherent drift nobody notices) and Fig 10 :26 (stale percepts, saturated metrics, pooled readings). It explains why "we did something" is not a ground reading.
4. **Per-slot measurement is not a stylistic preference.** Ashby's law gives the arithmetic: a pooled average cannot carry the variety of the disturbance it must regulate. This corroborates Fig 1 :19 and Fig 1 :22 from a genuinely different field.
5. **Unenumerated cascades are the named failure.** Ecological traps and secondary invaders after removal are independent witnesses for Fig 11 :65 and Fig 11 :69.

No world source conflicts with the figure verdict. If one had, the conflict would have been sent back to the domain census rather than used to flip a verdict. Overlap is declared in `10_source_ledger.csv`: the ecology and restoration pages share a broad evidence pool and count as one witness for the union finding; cybernetics and human decision science are the genuinely low-overlap corroborators; the framework essay shares the figures' author and carries no independent witness weight.

## 5. Distribution rules: offer, do not push

- **Fig 4 :22** "Only methods cross up. Only head starts come down." Sending the full architecture before anyone has described a residual sends a recording, not a generator. Lead with the separator and the refuter.
- **Fig 10 :42** "A verifier that can reconstruct the claim cannot verify it." A recipient who has absorbed the framework before giving a verdict has joined the frame being checked.
- **Fig 9 :27-28** "describe it before you go looking, or any frame you pick will seem to fit." Ask what they would expect to see at a site where the separator holds before showing any framework material.
- **Fig 2 :40** "disqualifying for a verdict: agreement about the world is worthless when the agreeing frames share an evidence pool." A send selected for likely agreement produces a convention, not corroboration.
- **Prestige is not action.** From *The Recursive Self-Improvement Trap*: "Prestige can fund an action. It can give an action a budget to be tried. But it cannot settle an action." The ask is for a check, not an endorsement.

**Recommended order in a reply:** (1) the separator and its refuter; (2) ask what they would count as a failure at a site they know; (3) record that verdict; (4) only then offer the links, with the note that the figure text, not the summary, is the authority; (5) if asked, offer the formula as a method with the instruction to write a fresh refuter and state the mode.
