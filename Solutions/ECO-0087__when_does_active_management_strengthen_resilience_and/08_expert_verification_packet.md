## 1. The claim, in one paragraph

The question is not one dial with a right amount of intervention. It is a union of four overlapping conditions with different anchors, so verdicts are indexed per species and patch and kept separate. A manager is a seat acting on a habitat: it can introduce an actor but cannot subtract the habitat, so managed ecosystems are permanent work. The seat strengthens resilience when it keeps an enumerated habitat in contact with a non-updating ground and carries a method rather than a recording of one occasion. It prevents autonomous adaptation when it substitutes a habitat, turns a target convention into a verdict, pools the residual into one averaged score, fixes the room while the question moves, or imports an actor whose cascade was never enumerated. No actor has a standing positive valence, and no figure line returns a universal intervention level. What is offered is the separator, the per-slot indexing and refuters written in advance.

## 2. Three checkable sub-claims

### C1 - habitat and seat; checkable against Fig 11 and against the reader's own sites

- **Statement:** introduction is available and subtraction is not; management is permanent work; the legitimate version is introduction with an enumerated cascade, and the failed version is the same move with the cascade unenumerated.
- **Source, verbatim:** Fig 11 :61 "The seat's power is asymmetric. Introduction is available; exclusion is not. You can add an actor and you cannot subtract the habitat, because contact is a fact about the world and does not consult you." Fig 11 :63 "Which is why a managed ecosystem is permanent work rather than a solved state." Fig 11 :65 "Introducing an actor for its cascade is ecosystem engineering, after Jones, Lawton and Shachak, 1994. Cane toads and kudzu are the same move with the cascade unenumerated."
- **What to check:** at a site you know, list every contact introduced by the intervention, then the second and third order effects. Is there any contact the seat actually removed, or only actors added? Did the cascade stop mattering at the point where the enumeration stopped?
- **Refuter:** a seat that can subtract a contact relation and still keep the ground reachable after it withdraws; or an introduced actor whose full cascade was enumerated, including third order effects, that still reduced persistence. Either fires, and C1 is withdrawn.
- **Triangulation status:** `triangulated`. Fig 11 (habitat) and Fig 12 :119 (catalyst, a part that survives its own use) are low-overlap routes. World corroboration from ecological traps and secondary invaders after removal.

### C2 - measurement and room fit; checkable against Fig 1, Fig 2, Fig 7 and Fig 10

- **Statement:** a success metric produced by the program it grades is not an anchor; if it moves with the program's report, everything drifts together coherently and nobody notices. A fixed room fails when the question moves; adaptation is selection between rooms.
- **Source, verbatim:** Fig 1 :19 "The residual is indexed by object slot, not aggregated." Fig 2 :33 "The anchor must not update." Fig 2 :34 "If it does, panel 3 turns into panel 1: the anchor becomes another frame, everything drifts together, and the drift is coherent, which is why nobody notices." Fig 10 :72 "When the question changes, the room should change. A room that stays fixed while the question moves is the failure; the correlation is only its symptom." Fig 7 :35 "A room's agreement is variation, not a verdict. Selection between rooms is where the ground votes."
- **What to check:** take one program's published success metric. Does it change when the program's own report changes? Does a pooled index predict each species and patch as well as the per-species readings across the last regime shift? Did the target list change when the climate or the interaction network changed?
- **Refuter:** a pooled resilience index that predicts every per-slot outcome as well as per-slot readings across a real regime shift; or a fixed regime that tracks every change of the question and preserves autonomous selection, both against a ground that does not update. Either fires, and C2 is withdrawn.
- **Triangulation status:** `triangulated`. Fig 1 and Fig 2 are different figures with different subjects (per-slot indexing versus anchor independence). Low-overlap world corroboration from Ashby's requisite variety and from adaptive management's process-failure list.

### C3 - targets and imports; checkable against Fig 10, Fig 6 and Fig 8

- **Statement:** a target is a convention until it decides an outcome; when it decides, it has stopped being a convention. The ground does not decay, the channel does. An introduction is an import that adds an atom to the manager's closure, not to the world, and needs provenance and a shadow test; a wrong atom costs more than starting from primitives.
- **Source, verbatim:** Fig 10 :47 "a convention that decides an outcome has stopped being one" Fig 10 :24 "the ground does not decay, the channel does" Fig 6 :26 "An import adds no atom to the world." Fig 6 :24 "Import from a live peer is debited against that frame's independence. A wrong atom costs more than starting from primitives." Fig 8 :51 "Convergent derivation and adopted import look identical in the contents. Only the record of where each came from separates them, and the surplus lives only where the closures differ."
- **What to check:** find a site where a target list overrode a ground reading of persistence or function. Find an introduction with no stated source gate or shadow test. Check whether a saturated metric kept the manager reading a ground that had already moved.
- **Refuter:** an unprovenanced atom with no shadow test that pays better against a non-updating ground than starting from local primitives; or a target that fixes value without deciding any outcome across a real regime shift. Either fires, and C3 is withdrawn.
- **Triangulation status:** `triangulated`. Fig 10 (ground maintainer), Fig 6 (closure wall) and Fig 8 (surplus and provenance) are three separate figures. World corroboration from restoration's shifting-baseline discussion and from novel-ecosystem "managing against versus managing for".

## 3. What is explicitly not claimed

- Not that non-intervention is better than intervention. The figures do not rank the two modes.
- Not that any technique has a standing positive valence: Fig 11 :51 "No standing list of good actors survives a change of residual."
- Not that a restored historical structure equals a restored function. Fig 4 :25 "A recording carried upward looks like knowledge and is a description of one occasion."
- Not that a pilot success licenses landscape application. Fig 5 :70 "A term is settled when the ground pays for it. A term is usable when something can compose over it."
- Not a universal intervention level, percentage, species list or target condition. That is a declared `gap`.
- Not `grounded`. Nothing here was measured. Agreement with this packet is not corroboration (Fig 2 :40 "disqualifying for a verdict: agreement about the world is worthless when the agreeing frames share an evidence pool").

## 5. What would settle it

A ground reading, not further agreement. From *The Hard Problem is About Source*: an instrument that reads green "cannot comment on the source"; the spectrometer resolved it. Here:

1. A discriminator that separates persistence caused by the seat keeping contact from persistence caused by the system's own selection running.
2. A cascade record with second and third order effects, plus the two silent failures: what you failed to reproduce and what you unintentionally introduced.
3. An anchor that stays fixed when the program's report changes.
4. A room-fit record showing the target changed when the question changed.
5. A per-slot prediction test that can fire against a pooled index.

## 6. What an expert can check in twenty minutes

1. Read Fig 11 :61, :63, :65 and Fig 1 :19, Fig 2 :33, Fig 10 :47, Fig 6 :24. Eight short passages.
2. Pick one intervention you know well. List the contacts it added and any it removed. Did the enumeration stop before the cascade did?
3. Name one instrument that would distinguish seat-maintained persistence from self-selected persistence at that site, or say why none is possible.
4. Send the refutation. A failure is more useful than an endorsement.
