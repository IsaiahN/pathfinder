## Independent domain census

I redid the census from the residual line alone before reading the primary's conclusions, using the WATSON order: R by arity, symmetry and scale; one line per domain with actors, currency and anchor; partition or union; order by dependency.

- **R (arity / symmetry / scale).** The residual is the effect of a seat acting on a habitat, with two candidate outcomes. Arity: a union of at least four overlapping conditions (what is introduced, whether the cascade was enumerated, whether the anchor is reachable and non-updating, whether the room matches the question). I derived this from Fig 9 :55 and from the four different vocabularies in the opened world sources, not from the primary's list. Symmetry: "strengthens" and "prevents" are ordered, so a symmetric measure cannot orient them (Operators table, comparison is ordered). Scale: per slot, because the live signal is per object (Fig 1 :19).
- **Domain 1, habitat and seat.** Actors: seat, species, conditions, relations. Currency: contact and cascade. Anchor: the ground. Partition boundary between maintained and unmaintained, with a compound where both operate.
- **Domain 2, measurement.** Actors: managers, monitors, models. Currency: readings and residuals. Anchor: a measurement that does not update on the program's report. Partition question about admissibility.
- **Domain 3, time and adaptation.** Actors: rooms, selectors, fixed regimes. Currency: selection between rooms versus motion inside one. Anchor: ground pressure. Union with Domain 1.
- **Domain 4, import and novelty.** Actors: introductions, provenance, peer frames. Currency: atoms crossing a closure wall. Anchor: ground payment plus source gate. Composition plus import.
- **Order by dependency.** Index per slot, enumerate habitat, fix a non-updating anchor, refuse before mint, choose intervention, check room fit, then source gate and shadow test. Fig 3 :20 governs the order; Fig 11 :44 and Fig 2 :33 govern the first two substantive links.

My census matches the primary's four domains and its union reading. Where the primary derived the seat/habitat split from Fig 11 :56/:58, I derived it from Fig 9 :54-56 (union unbundling) and the world literature's active/passive vocabularies. Different starting points, same domain boundaries: low overlap between routes, so the agreement is weak but not circular inside the framework. It is still one evidence pool.

## Round trip gap R_T

The Symbols table defines R_T as the gap between what was sent up and what came back down, `gap(x, (T_E ∘ T_A)(x))`, measuring what the coarser description cannot hold. Fig 4 :30 requires it to be measured rather than assumed. I ran the round trip on the condensation this folder actually uses.

1. **Sent up (x):** the verbatim figure set, chiefly Fig 11 :43-44/:61-69, Fig 1 :19/:22, Fig 2 :33/:34, Fig 7 :35, Fig 10 :47/:72, Fig 6 :24/:26 and Fig 8 :51.
2. **Abstraction (T_A):** the shorthand carried in `07_the_tether_answer.md`: manage by enumerated introduction and a non-updating anchor; a fixed target is a verdict; a fixed room fails when the question moves; no universal level.
3. **Concretisation (T_E):** an ordinary management recommendation, for example "restore the historical species list at 40 percent of the reserve and monitor a composite resilience index".

**R_T is nonzero.** Four things the shorthand holds do not survive the trip back down:

- **Per-slot indexing is lost.** The concrete recommendation uses one composite resilience index, which is exactly the aggregation Fig 1 :22 disqualifies.
- **The non-updating anchor is replaced by a program-owned metric.** The composite index moves when the program's report changes, so panel 3 becomes panel 1 (Fig 2 :34).
- **Provenance and shadow test are dropped.** The historical species list is an import with no source gate; the introduced actors have no enumerated third-order cascade (Fig 6 :24, Fig 11 :65).
- **The gap itself is erased.** A 40 percent number is a name where the framework returns a shape (Fig 13 :133).

Conversely, one thing survives cleanly: a seat can introduce but cannot subtract, so management is permanent work, and a target that decides an outcome stops being a convention. That residue is worth sending. R_T was **not** measured numerically, because no ground reading exists; it is recorded qualitatively as the enumerated set of dropped constraints. Treating "the formula is a condensed version of the figures" as identity would set R_T to zero and would be the dead-reckoning error Fig 4 :30 names. The honest form is a lossy condensation with a recorded gap, which is what `09_watson_answer.md` states.
