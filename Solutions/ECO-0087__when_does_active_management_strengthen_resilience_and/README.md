## The residual in one line

A manager is a seat acting on a habitat. The residual is whether that seat's action changes contact with the ground in a way that keeps the ecosystem's own selection working, or substitutes a habitat and fixes the room while the question moves. It is indexed per species and patch, not averaged into one "ecosystem health" number.

## What is deliberately not here

- No personal emails of named individuals.
- No ranking of people by how likely they are to agree.
- No bulk-send plan.
- No claim that the figures select a preferred intervention or a universal intervention level. The figures return a shape and refuters, not a name or a number.

Three figure-grounded reasons:

1. **Agreement is not an anchor.** Fig 2 :35 "Alignment is triangulation, not negotiation. A mirror cannot correct you." Selecting recipients for receptiveness manufactures the disqualifying correlation (Fig 2 :40).
2. **The frame cannot score itself.** Fig 1 :27 "They are frame-internal. A frame cannot score itself with a quantity it also produces." A campaign that counts how many people liked the claim is counting the denominator.
3. **Do not hand the interior to the verifier.** Fig 10 :42 "A verifier that can reconstruct the claim cannot verify it." The first contact carries the residual and its refuter, not the framework.

Legally, public opt-in and institutional channels avoid the GDPR and CAN-SPAM style failures that scraping personal emails for unsolicited outreach creates.
