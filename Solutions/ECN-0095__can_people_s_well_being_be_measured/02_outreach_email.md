# Outreach draft: check one separator before you decide about the framework

**Use:** one-to-one, or as a cover letter attached to a submission through the public venues in `03_public_contact_points.csv`. **Not** for bulk send.

**Hard precondition:** do not send until the live strain S4 in `01_figure_grounded_audit.md` §7 is named in the packet, and until the receiver is told that equal weighting is being offered as a convention, not a result. Otherwise the two-stage offer collapses on contact.

---

**Subject:** Would you count income as a reading or as the anchor for a person's well-being?

> Hi [name],
>
> I have one specific separator I would like you to check against what you already know. I am not asking you to read the framework first, and I am not asking you to agree with it. If the separator does not survive, there is nothing else to look at.
>
> **The residual (`specified`, not `grounded`).** A well-being measure has a gap between what its instrument reports and what settles the question for the person. That gap is indexed per slot: income and consumption, health, security, relationships, autonomy, and whatever else the person's habitat contains. A global reading near zero with one live slot is a legal state. A high-income person with no relationships is the example. Fig 1 :22 puts the rule plainly: *"Aggregating across slots is how a live signal disappears into an average."*
>
> **The separator.** For a question that is constitutively about a person, the person's own judgment is the anchor, and a market counter is an instrument that *"can be wrong on axes they do not measure"* (Fig 2 :37-38). That does not make income useless. It makes it one slot, not the ground. The equal-weight composite is the other candidate, and it has a different defect: its weights are bonds, and a bond *"that costs nothing is a bond that carries nothing, which is the flat case"* (Fig 12 :101). So equal weighting is a declared convention, not a measurement result, once it decides an outcome (Fig 10 :47).
>
> **What I am not claiming.** Not that income is never an anchor. It is the right anchor for "can this household pay rent this month?" Not that a five-dimension list is universal; the same slot changes valence with the residual (Fig 11 :49). Not that any figure line returns a weight vector. None does. The gap is reported rather than filled.
>
> **What would refute the separator, written before I sent this:**
> 1. A single scalar is shown to track the person-judgment anchor across all five slots, with no live per-slot residual left.
> 2. A figure line is quoted that returns a specific weight vector as a grounded result.
> 3. The five slots are shown to be one evidence pool read by one instrument, so the union collapses to a partition.
> 4. Equal weights are shown to be paid for at the ground by an instrument that returns all five slots in one currency.
>
> If any one holds, I will withdraw the corresponding claim. A one-line "this fails because..." is more useful to me than agreement. If you would rather not engage, no reply is needed.
>
> **If it survives, and only then:** I will send the sources. The figures are the authority, not my summary of them. I will also ask you one question before showing any framework: what would you expect to see if the separator were true, and what would you expect if it were false? I will record your answer as the verdict on stage 1 before showing you anything else.
>
> **If you want to reuse the method:** the loop is in the figures link. Run it on a problem you know well and can check against a ground. Write down what would refute your result *before* you run it. A reported failure is more useful to me than a reported success.

## Stage order

1. **Stage 1:** send the residual, the separator and the five refuters. Ask what the reader would count as a failure. Record that verdict before any theory is shown.
2. **Stage 2, opt-in only:** if they are curious, send the three links in `04_links_and_distribution.md`, with the note that the figures, not the summary, are the authority.
3. **Stage 3:** offer the formula as a method to reuse, with the instruction that each user states their own mode and writes their own refuter before the run.

A Track B style reply, if one ever occurs, is a conversation, never corroboration (Fig 2 :40). It carries no verdict weight.
