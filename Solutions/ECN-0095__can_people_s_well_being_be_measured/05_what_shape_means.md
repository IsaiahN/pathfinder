## 1. A shape is a constraint, not a filler

The canonical rule states the limit: the framework returns *"what a missing thing would have to look like (a shape, not a name)"*. Fig 9 :32: *"→ you get a kind of thing, never the answer itself"*. Fig 13 :135: *"It cannot say which primitive is missing, because naming it would mean already holding it."*

From the Library of Babel essay, opened this session: *"you can cut your search short if the set of books returned is small and descriptive enough that it provides a general shape of the answer that satisfies your question."*

A **shape** here is the narrowed form of an answer that can be stated before the weights are known. It constrains; it does not fill. For this question the shape is: per-slot readings, the person as anchor, different instruments ranged separately, a convention declared rather than a result asserted, a refusal seat, and a measured round trip from slots to score and back. The shape does not include the vector 20/20/20/20/20. That is a name, and no figure line holds it.

## 2. How a shape is obtained

- **Library of Babel, opened this session:** *"The problem you have determines what makes a book relevant to you or not."* Use the vocabulary already known that is closest to the thing, then *"With each new term gained, you are able to narrow the space by definition and deepen your vocabulary."* Applied here: the term closest to the question is not "utility" or "income" but "slot", because the residual is indexed per slot (Fig 1 :19).
- **The bundle passage, opened this session:** *"Perhaps your problem was a bundle or conflation of several problems masquerading as one? You will need to search all of those problems individually to find their answers and then combine them in a satisfactory way."* This is the essay-level corroboration of the union verdict in F1. It is not a figure line and is not used as one.
- **Fig 9 :30-31:** *"your words are borrowed too, so describe the effect rather than the cause"*. The effect here is a live slot that the aggregate fails to register, not "low utility" or "deprivation".
- **Decoding Tacit Knowledge (carried mapping):** *"When contact isn't possible, the best you can send is a plain description of what you'd expect to see, because anyone can check that against the world."* This is why the outreach email asks what the reader would expect to see if the separator were true before showing any theory.
- **Library of Babel on stopping:** the answer is reached when *"the finish line will also be the starting place"*, and the paradox is that the searcher has *"composed the answer through the process of locating it."* Here the search stops at the shape, not at a number.

## 3. What licenses a name

Two things, both outside the shape itself:

1. **The ground settles it.** `THE_FORMULA.md` step 5: *"SETTLE — the ground settles it. Verified only by what pays. A gate is not the ground."* The canonical README: the framework *"stops at the limit of what the available instruments can distinguish, and records the lineage so the stop is honest."*
2. **A provenanced import from a frame whose closure differs** (Fig 6 :23, Fig 8 :50-52), with a source gate and a shadow test.

For this question: naming a weight vector is licensed only if a source-discriminating instrument pays for it, or if a decision frame declares it as a convention with provenance. The capability approach literature's public-reasoning position (opened 2026-11-23) is an example of the second route, not the first. Anand and colleagues' dashboard of 40 to 50 indicators is a candidate convention. Neither is a figure-derived name.

*They're Scanning Books When They Should* (carried mapping) sharpens the first route: *"The real import is not a new term in the existing space. It is a new dimension of the space."* And: *"Declaring the primitives does not make them neutral. It makes the choice visible."* A five-slot list is a visible choice. Presenting it as nature's own list turns a visible choice into an invisible one.

## 4. The specific forbidden error for this question

*The Hard Problem is About Source* (carried mapping) gives the mechanism. An instrument reads green and *"cannot comment on the source"*: one green light, or yellow plus blue, or RGB, or CMYK, or a scheme not yet discovered. *"If several paths create the same behavior, then behavior is a projection with lossy information."* And: *"An instrument that can't distinguish these cases demonstrates its own inadequacy."* It was the spectrometer, not an argument, that resolved it.

Applied here: an income reading and a relationship reading can be equal while their sources differ. A single life-satisfaction score can be identical for someone whose income is doing all the work and someone whose relationships are. Reading the score and naming the source ("this person's well-being comes from income") is exactly the metamerism error. What is owed is a source-discriminating instrument, not a name derived from a projection. Nussbaum's point, opened this session via the capability approach page, that *"combined capability 'combines internal preparedness with external opportunity in a complicated way, so that measurement is likely to be no easy task'"*, is the same limitation stated from within the measurement field.

## 5. The ordinary-term answer

**What licenses a particular weight vector? Nothing in the figures.** The figures return the shape. A vector is licensed only by the ground settling a minted term, or by a provenanced convention with a shadow test and a refusal seat. Until one of those, the vector is the searcher's own contribution and must be recorded as such.

**Does the question as posed commit the error the README forbids?** Yes, both readings do. The income-only option reads one projection and names the whole source from it. The equal-weight option takes five readings and asserts a symmetry among their sources that no instrument discriminates. Both turn a visible choice into an invisible one. The correct form keeps the slots separate, records the choice of weights as a choice, and reports the round trip.

## 5b. Correction to my own §3 and §4

I initially wrote that the framework *"returns a shape, not a name"* as if it forbade naming any of the five slots. That overstates the rule. The canonical phrase is *"what a **missing** thing would have to look like (a shape, not a name)."* It governs a primitive not held in the library. Income, health, security, relationships and autonomy are not candidates for the six-term physical substrate at all, so the rule does not bear on them in that way.

Two corrections follow, kept visible:

1. Naming a slot already available in a measurement frame is a **binding**, not the invention of a new primitive. The error is not the naming; the error is calling the binding a figure-derived result.
2. My §4 metamerism point is **not** retracted. It still holds that a score does not determine its source, and that an instrument unable to discriminate sources is inadequate for the naming task. The reason is the missing discriminator, not a blanket ban on naming held terms.

Both readings are recorded so neither is quietly lost. This mirrors the correction in the exemplar's `05` §5b.

## 6. Prestige is not action

From *The Recursive Self-Improvement Trap*, opened this session: *"Prestige must never be allowed to validate an action directly... Someone let prestige stand in for proof... Prestige can fund an action. It can give an action a budget to be tried. But it cannot settle an action. Only the ground can settle it. The Ground Maintainer must be blind to prestige."*

A campaign that gathers well-known names to agree that income measures well-being is the named collapse, not corroboration. The legitimate ask is the opposite: give the separator a budget to be tried by someone who can read a ground, and let the ground settle it. Note also the essay's line *"Metatheories cannot validate themselves. This is Godel's insight, and it still holds here. You need an external validator."*

## 7. My own audit is a simulation, not a ground reading

From the same essay, opened this session: *"LLMs can quickly simulate an approximate answer/output with the formula... However, the truly testable output would need to be derived from the real architecture or working the formula yourself."*

So `01_figure_grounded_audit.md`, `07_the_tether_answer.md` and `09_watson_answer.md` are simulated derivations checked for internal consistency against the figures. They are **not** tests of the well-being claim and must be labelled as such. No survey was run, no participant was measured, and no weight vector was validated. The word "reliably" in the question is re-grounded, not answered: it is a property of a channel to a non-updating ground (Fig 10 :24), and no such channel was constructed here.
