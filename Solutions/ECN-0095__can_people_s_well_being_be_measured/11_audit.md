## 1. Independent domain census from the residual line alone

I redid the census from the question as stated, before using the primary's conclusions, in the WATSON order: R by arity, symmetry and scale; one line per domain with actors / currency / anchor; partition or union; order by dependency.

- **R (arity / symmetry / scale).** The residual is the gap between an instrument's reading and what settles the question for the person. Arity: five named slots plus unnamed habitat elements, so a vector. Symmetry: the equal-weight option asserts exchangeability, which is a bond claim. Scale: first order per slot, second order at the person's overall judgment, third order at a policy index. Symbols table :43-44 and Fig 1 :19 govern the per-slot indexing.
- **Domain 1, instrument reading.** Actors: market counters, life-satisfaction scales, affect reports, health, security, relationship and autonomy measures. Currency: readings per slot. Anchor: the person's judgment, because the question is constitutively about a person. Union of readings.
- **Domain 2, aggregation.** Actors: five ingredients, multipliers, normalisation, composite. Currency: bits and actions, which do not add. Anchor: the ground for the decision the index serves. Composition, so bonds must be priced or declared.
- **Domain 3, decision and verification.** Actors: index, decision rule, person, seat. Currency: outcomes. Anchor: a result that does not update on the index. This is where a convention becomes a verdict.
- **Union.** The five conditions overlap. More income clears the income-only region and leaves the intersections bound by the second condition. I reached this from Fig 9 :4-5 and :55-56 independently of the primary's route through the Library of Babel essay.
- **Order.** Read, range against the anchor, settle, compose, measure the round trip. Refuse before minting (Formula step 2).

My census matches the primary's three domains and its union reading. Where the primary derived the union from Fig 9 :55-56 plus the Library of Babel bundle passage, I derived it from Fig 9 :4-5 (disagreements that do not shrink with effort, split rather than search) and Fig 11 :49-51 (valence relative to residual, no standing list). ## 4. Round trip gap R_T

The Symbols table defines R_T as *"The gap between what was sent up and what came back down: gap(x, (T_E ∘ T_A)(x)). Measures what the coarser description cannot hold."* Fig 4 :30 requires it to be measured rather than assumed. I ran the round trip on the condensation the folder actually uses.

2. **Abstraction (T_A):** the shorthand carried in `07` and `09`: split the union, keep slots separate, person is anchor, income is an instrument, weights are a convention, report R_T.
3. **Concretisation (T_E):** the source question's own options: one reliable scalar, or five dimensions with equal weight.

**R_T is nonzero.** Three things the shorthand holds do not survive the trip back down unchanged:

- **The boundary of "the person's own judgment" as an anchor.** Fig 2 :37-38 makes it the anchor for a question about a person, but Diener's account and the OWID validation material show that a self-report is itself an instrument with known failure modes. The shorthand treats the person as a clean anchor; the ground reading would require specifying which reading of the person is the anchor and how it is protected from updating. Dropped constraint 1.
- **Commensurability of the five slots.** Fig 12 :125-126 says the two currencies do not add. The shorthand says "weights are a convention", which is safe, but it does not settle whether the five slots are quantities of the same kind at all. Dropped constraint 2.
- **The identity of the slots under a changed residual.** Fig 11 :51 refuses a standing list. The shorthand keeps five names for the purposes of this question, which is correct for this residual but would not survive a transfer to a different one. Dropped constraint 3.

Conversely, three things survive cleanly: the union structure, the per-slot indexing, and the claim that a convention deciding an outcome is a verdict. That is the residue worth sending. R_T was **not** measured numerically, because no ground reading and no instrument were constructed; it is recorded qualitatively as the enumerated set of dropped constraints. Treating "the formula is the condensed version of the figures" as identity would set R_T to zero and is the dead-reckoning error Fig 4 :30 names. The honest form is a lossy condensation with a recorded gap, which is what `07` §5 and `09` state.
