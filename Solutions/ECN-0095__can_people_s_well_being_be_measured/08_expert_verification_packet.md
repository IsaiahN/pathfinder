# Expert verification packet: the Tether on measuring well-being

**What this is:** a claim in a form a measurement researcher, economist or psychometrician can check. Mode, derivation and refuters are stated before the conclusion, so a reader knows what to look for and what would settle it.

**Mode: `specified` on the separators, `general` on the structural shape, `gap` on the weight vector. Not `grounded`.** No person was measured. This is a shape and a route, not a number.

**Status:** simulated derivation checked for internal consistency against the figures, not a ground reading. It cannot settle the question it reports on.

## 1. The claim, in one paragraph

The question is a union of overlapping conditions rather than an either/or, so one lever clears only the region where one condition holds. For a question that is constitutively about a person, the person's own judgment is the anchor and a market counter is an instrument, so income belongs as one slot and cannot settle the whole question. The residual stays indexed per slot: a global reading near zero with one live slot is a legal state, and aggregation hides the signal that says where to look. A composite is a molecule, so its weights are bonds rather than ingredients; equal weight is a declared convention rather than a ground-paid result, particularly once the index decides an outcome. Independence requires different instruments, not more administrations of one ladder. No figure line returns a weight vector, so the vector is reported as a gap with the instrument and decision frame that would close it.

## 2. Three checkable sub-claims

### C1 — Union and anchor; checkable by a measurement researcher

- **Statement:** the five conditions form a union with overlapping intersections, and the anchor for a question about a person is the person's judgment, not the market counter. Income is a legitimate anchor only when the question is constitutively about material command.
- **Source, verbatim:** Fig 9 :55-56 — *"A bundled problem is usually a union of overlapping conditions, not a list of separate ones. A single lever clears the region where only one condition holds and leaves the intersections standing, because the second condition still binds."* Fig 2 :36 — *"An anchor is legitimate when the question is constitutively about it."* Fig 2 :37-38 — *"Human judgment is the right anchor for is this a good answer for a person, because the question is about people. It is only an instrument for is this code correct, and instruments can be wrong on axes they do not measure."*
- **What to check:** is the union reading faithful to the measurement literature? Do income and the other four dimensions overlap in the way claimed? Is there a question about a person for which a market counter genuinely is the anchor?
- **Refuter:** a single scalar that tracks the person-judgment anchor across all five slots with no live per-slot residual left, or a demonstration that the intersections vanish so the union collapses to a partition.

### C2 — Per-slot residual and independence; checkable by a psychometrician

- **Statement:** the residual must stay indexed per slot, and multiple readings of the same person count as independent only when they use different instruments.
- **Source, verbatim:** Fig 1 :19 — *"The residual is indexed by object slot, not aggregated."* Fig 1 :22 — *"Aggregating across slots is how a live signal disappears into an average."* Fig 1 :13 — *"a global R near zero with one live slot residual is a legal state"*. Fig 2 :31-32 — *"Independence, between two frames, is how little they draw on the same evidence. Two people reading the same report are not two frames; two instruments pointed at the same thing are. Independence is what makes agreement mean something."*
- **What to check:** does the instrument you use return a distinct reading per slot? Are your validation samples using the same ladder, or genuinely different instruments such as self-report, informant report, experience sampling and a behavioural or market counter?
- **Refuter:** an aggregate with a decomposed per-slot residual of zero and a measured round-trip gap of zero on a ground reading, or a set of readings that share an instrument and a dataset and are still shown to be independent frames.

### C3 — Composite structure and the weight gap; checkable by an index-methods researcher

- **Statement:** a composite is a molecule, so weights are bonds and are not read off the ingredient list. Equal weight is a declared convention, not a ground-paid result, and a convention that decides an outcome has stopped being a convention.
- **Source, verbatim:** Fig 12 :5 — *"A set of ingredients is not one. The bond is the rest of the information, and it is the half nothing was recording."* Fig 12 :101 — *"A bond that costs nothing is a bond that carries nothing, which is the flat case."* Fig 12 :125 — *"A composition costs in two currencies, and they do not add."* Fig 10 :47 — *"a convention that decides an outcome has stopped being one"*. Fig 9 :32 — *"→ you get a kind of thing, never the answer itself"*.
- **What to check:** can you name the instrument that pays for each weight? If the index decides a payment or a ranking, is there a non-updating anchor and a seat able to refuse the verdict? Is a common currency available for all five slots at one level?
- **Refuter:** a figure line that returns a specific weight vector as a grounded result, or an instrument that returns all five slots in one currency at one level and pays for the multipliers at the ground. This is the live strain S4 in `01` §7 and is not hidden.

## 4. What is explicitly not claimed

- Not that income and consumption are useless, or that GDP is never a useful measure.
- Not that the five named dimensions are a complete or permanent list.
- Not that equal weighting is forbidden as a practice. It is refused only as an unproven measurement claim once it decides an outcome.
- Not a theory of everything, not a final index, not a universal percentage.
- Not `grounded`. No survey, experiment or participant measurement was performed.
- Not a claim that a composite is impossible. A declared convention with a measured round trip and a refusal seat is permitted.
- Not a claim that the framework names the weights. It returns a shape, and the gap is recorded.
