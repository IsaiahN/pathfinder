# The Tether's answer on measuring well-being

**Mode: `specified` on the separators and per-domain verdicts, `general` on the structural shape, `gap` on the weight vector. Not `grounded`.** This is a shape and a route, not a number. Simulated derivation checked for internal consistency against the figures (`05_what_shape_means.md` §7), not a ground reading.

## 1. The residual it answers

The question offers two instruments for one decision: a market counter (income and consumption), or five dimensions with equal weight. R is the gap between what an instrument reports and what settles the question for the person. It is indexed per slot, never as a single global number. Symbols table :43-44: *"R is measured per slot, never as a single global number."* Fig 1 :13: *"a global R near zero with one live slot residual is a legal state"*. The high-income person with no relationships is that legal state. The question is a union of overlapping conditions, not one hard question with opposite answers (Fig 9 :55-56: *"A bundled problem is usually a union of overlapping conditions, not a list of separate ones. A single lever clears the region where only one condition holds and leaves the intersections standing, because the second condition still binds."*).

## 2. The shape

1. **The question is a union, so one lever cannot settle it.** Split before searching (Fig 9 :4-5, :54). More income clears the income-only region and leaves the intersections standing. Verdicts are kept per domain and never averaged into one reliability percentage.
2. **For a question about a person, the person's judgment is the anchor and the market counter is an instrument.** Fig 2 :36: *"An anchor is legitimate when the question is constitutively about it."* Fig 2 :37-38: *"Human judgment is the right anchor for is this a good answer for a person, because the question is about people. It is only an instrument for is this code correct, and instruments can be wrong on axes they do not measure."* Income is not discarded; it is one slot. It becomes a false anchor only when promoted to settle the whole question.
3. **Aggregation hides the only signal that says where to look.** Fig 1 :22: *"Aggregating across slots is how a live signal disappears into an average."* Publish the live slot alongside any composite. Our World in Data's age/cohort example corroborates this from the statistics side.
4. **Equal weight is a bond claim presented as a measurement result.** A composite is a molecule, not an ingredient list: Fig 12 :5 *"A set of ingredients is not one. The bond is the rest of the information, and it is the half nothing was recording."* Fig 12 :101 *"A bond that costs nothing is a bond that carries nothing, which is the flat case."* And the two currencies do not add: Fig 12 :125 *"A composition costs in two currencies, and they do not add."* Equal weighting is legitimate as a declared convention and illegitimate as a ground-paid result.
5. **A convention that decides an outcome has stopped being a convention.** Fig 10 :47 *"a convention that decides an outcome has stopped being one"*. An index used to set a payment or a ranking is a verdict, so it needs a non-updating anchor and a seat able to refuse it.
6. **Independence comes from different instruments, not more of the same ladder.** Fig 2 :31-32: *"Independence, between two frames, is how little they draw on the same evidence. Two people reading the same report are not two frames; two instruments pointed at the same thing are. Independence is what makes agreement mean something."* Self-report, informant report, experience sampling and a market counter are four frames. A hundred national surveys using one Cantril ladder are one.
7. **Composition waits for settlement.** Fig 12 :104 *"A settled molecule becomes an atom for whatever composes over it. So irreducible is a fact about a level and never about the world."* Each slot must settle before it can be composed. Fig 3 :27 *"A link with no instrument is not a link that works. It is a link nobody has looked at."*
8. **No figure line returns a weight vector.** Fig 9 :32 *"→ you get a kind of thing, never the answer itself"*. Fig 13 :134 *"Unreachable is a verdict here, not a silence,"* The vector is owed from a decision frame as a declared convention, or from a source-discriminating instrument as a measurement. It is a `gap`, not an invented number.

## 3. What it explicitly does not say

- Not that income and consumption are useless. They are the right anchor for a question constitutively about material command (Fig 2 :36).
- Not that the five named dimensions are the only slots. The habitat is enumerated from the residual, not composed from a standing list (Fig 11 :43-44).
- Not that equal weighting is forbidden. It is forbidden only as an unproven measurement claim once it decides an outcome.
- Not that one composite is impossible. It is possible as a declared convention with a measured round trip and a refusal seat.
- Not that reliability means repeatability. Here it means a channel to a ground that does not update (Fig 10 :24).
- Not `grounded`. No person was measured and no survey was run.

## 4. What would settle it: the ground

A source-discriminating instrument, not a further argument. From *The Hard Problem is About Source* (carried mapping, not re-opened this session): an instrument that reads green *"cannot comment on the source"*. The spectrometer resolved it. Here:

- **Indicator discrimination:** an instrument that distinguishes whether a life-satisfaction reading is being driven by income, health, security, relationships or autonomy. Without it, a score cannot name its source.
- **Commensurability test:** an instrument that returns all five slots in one currency at one level, so the weights could be paid for rather than declared. If none exists, the two-currency finding stands (Fig 12 :126).
- **Anchor test:** a reading that does not move when the person's judgment is pushed and does not move when the index itself is published (Fig 2 :33, Fig 10 :24).
- **Round trip:** carry slot readings up to a composite and back down to slot predictions; publish the gap R_T as a measured quantity (Fig 4 :30, Symbols table :77-78).
- **Standing falsifiers** from `01` §4: a single scalar that leaves no live slot, a figure line that returns a weight vector, a collapse of the union to a partition, weights paid at the ground, or a composite that remains a checkable convention while deciding outcomes.

## 5. The honest stop

**Reached a shape, not a number.** The slot list is a visible choice, not nature's own list. *"Declaring the primitives does not make them neutral. It makes the choice visible"* (from *They're Scanning Books*, carried mapping). The answer stops at the limit of what the available instruments can distinguish and records the lineage so the stop is checkable. The canonical rule: *"Indiscernibility is not identity."* One live strain remains, the commensurability of the five slots (Fig 12 :125-126). It is named, not smoothed.

## 6. One-paragraph version for outreach

> The Tether does not return a single reliable number for well-being, and it does not return a universal equal-weight formula. It says that the question is a union of overlapping conditions, not an either/or, so one lever clears only the region where one condition holds. For a question about a person, the person's own judgment is the anchor and income is an instrument, so income belongs in the reading but cannot settle the question; aggregation across slots hides the live signal that says where to look. A composite is a molecule, so the weights are bonds rather than ingredients, and equal weight is a declared convention rather than a ground-paid result, especially once the index decides an outcome. Independence comes from different instruments, not from more administrations of one ladder. No figure line returns a weight vector, so that is reported as a gap with the instruments and the decision frame that would close it. What is offered is the shape any answer must have, five refuters written in advance, and a measured round trip from slots to score and back.

## 7. Verdict fields

- **cost if wrong:** bounded and locatable. The answer keeps per-slot readings and declares the convention, so an error is findable rather than invisible. The real cost is spending the independence of outside readers by sending a conclusion instead of a separator. Fig 2 :42 *"Buy independence in proportion to the cost of being wrong."* That cost is avoidable by leading with the residual and the refuter.
- **undoable:** yes. A declared convention can be revised, a slot added, a weight changed, an instrument replaced, without withdrawing the union structure. No ground reading has been staked on the specific five-item list.
- **reversed by:** a ground reading, not agreement. A source-discriminating instrument would name which slot drives a score; a commensurate instrument would pay for weights; a demonstration that one scalar leaves no live per-slot residual would break F1 to F3; a verbatim figure line returning a weight vector would break F7. Until one of these fires, the verdict stands as `candidate`.
