## 3. Overlap assessment

- The four well-being measurement sources (Easterlin, SWB, capability approach, Stiglitz-Sen-Fitoussi) are largely one broad evidence pool. They cite each other and reuse the same survey literature. They count as **one witness**, not four, for the union verdict.
- Our World in Data is a data-quality and statistics framing of the same surveys. It adds a genuinely different instrument, the cohort confounding argument, but draws on the same datasets. Partial overlap.
- The animal welfare source is the only genuinely low-overlap world corroborator. It supports the shape of the claim, multiple indicators with an unresolved weighting question, not the human well-being fact.
- The two essays share the framework author's evidence pool completely. Their agreement with the figures is expected and carries no independent witness weight.

No world source was used to overrule a figure line. The Easterlin versus Stevenson and Wolfers disagreement was returned to the census and did not flip a verdict (`01` §7 S3).

## 4. Distribution: offer, do not push

Figure-grounded reasons:

- **Fig 10 :52** *"Bring a characterised residual, never the source."* Sending the full framework before anyone has described a residual sends a source, not a separator. Lead with the per-slot residual and the refuter.
- **Fig 9 :27-28** *"describe it before you go looking, or any frame you pick will seem to fit"*. Ask what the reader would expect to see if the separator were true before giving them the links.
- **Fig 2 :35** *"Alignment is triangulation, not negotiation. A mirror cannot correct you."* Do not send to people selected for likely agreement.
- **Fig 2 :40** *"disqualifying for a verdict: agreement about the world is worthless when the agreeing frames share an evidence pool"*. A list of readers who all use the same Cantril ladder is one frame. Do not pool their replies.
- **Fig 4 :22** *"Only methods cross up. Only head starts come down."* Stage 3 sends a method, which is permitted, not a recording of one result.
- **Fig 8 :39** *"So a search that finds nothing is never a proof of absence."* A refusal to engage is not a refutation and not an endorsement.

**Recommended order in a reply:** (1) the separator and its five refuters; (2) the question "what would you expect to see if this were true, and what if it were false?", with the answer recorded first; (3) only if curious, the three framework links, with the note that the figures, not the summary, are the authority; (4) the formula as a reusable method, with an instruction that each user writes their own refuter and states their own mode.

## 5. Legal and editorial constraints

- The journal venues in `03` use public submission portals. Use those, not personal editor addresses.
- Springer Nature journals in the list moved to open access transitions with article processing charges (Journal of Happiness Studies from 16 June 2026). Check current fees and AI-disclosure policy before submitting simulated work. An LLM-generated derivation must be declared as such under the publisher's authorship rules.
- ISQOLS and HDCA pages expose institutional addresses. Do not use them for unsolicited bulk outreach. Use the public contact page or submission portal.
- GDPR and CAN-SPAM style rules apply to unsolicited email in many jurisdictions. There is no bulk-send plan in this folder, and no receptiveness ranking of people.
