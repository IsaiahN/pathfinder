## QUESTION

Decide, in effect terms, what the Tether says about the claim that one of two instruments should lead economic stabilization. Option A: monetary policy is the main tool. Option B: fiscal policy should play a larger role. What each leads to: A gives one instrument standing across the inflation, activity, credit, stability and distribution slots and across regime changes, and so answers an aggregate that is not a place where policy acts. B does the same for the other instrument. The derivation tests the shared assumption behind both, namely that a stable ranking exists, and records the separator that survives if it does not.

## DOMAIN CENSUS

R by arity, symmetry and scale, then one line per domain with actors, currency and anchor; partition or union; order by dependency.

- **R (the residual).** Arity: at least six slots, inflation, real activity, credit, financial stability, distribution and expectations. R is a vector, not a scalar. Symmetry: monetary and fiscal are not interchangeable terms; their composition is often ordered (`→` in a funding crisis, `⋛` at a target threshold) and never a flat sum where order matters. Scale: first order within a regime, second order across the membrane from a balance sheet or a budget to realized behaviour, third order where a rate change cascades into credit, asset prices and distribution.
- **Domain 1, the monetary frame.** Actors: the central bank, the policy rate, the balance sheet, reserve requirements, guidance. Currency: base money, the short rate and the expected path of short rates. Anchor: measured market rates and prices, only if they do not update on what the frame thinks. Reach: prices and expectations directly, credit in a crisis, activity indirectly. Fig 2 :29 requires a non-updating anchor; Fig 11 :40 makes capability a property of instrument and habitat.
- **Domain 2, the fiscal frame.** Actors: treasury, legislature, taxes, spending, transfers, debt issuance. Currency: budget authority, disposable income, aggregate demand. Anchor: realized output, employment and receipts, if read independently. Reach: activity and distribution directly, prices with a lag. Fig 12 :121 gives the debt cycle whose sign decides closure.
- **Domain 3, the economy and habitat.** Actors: households, firms, banks, the external sector, institutions. Currency: realized transactions. Anchor: the ground itself. This is not a frame and no seat authors it. It settles which instrument pays in a given regime.
- **Domain 4, measurement and objective.** Actors: statistical agencies, modelers, target committees, data revisions. Currency: metrics, targets, conventions. Anchor: none of its own. It is a frame, not a ground; its metrics can saturate and its target can become a verdict (Fig 10 :26, :47).
- **Domain 5, verification and outreach.** Actors: expert readers and venues. Currency: agreement and residuals. Anchor: the ground, never agreement. In this simulated run all seats share one evidence pool, so independence is zero and agreement is internal consistency only.
- **Partition or union.** Union, not partition. A liquidity trap can coincide with a funding crisis; a supply shock can coincide with a de-anchoring of expectations. A single lever clears the region where one condition holds and leaves the intersections standing.
- **Order by dependency (Fig 3).** Measure the per-slot residual before naming a frame; establish the objective as a comparison per slot before planning; refuse the held ranking before minting a new instrument; carry methods, not recordings of one success.

Strain test: the apparent conflict between Fig 2's non-updating anchor and Fig 11's residual-relative valence is not strained, since Fig 2 governs what settles a verdict and Fig 11 governs which actor carries a residual. Different objects, so re-census dissolves it. One live strain remains: the credibility-to-catalyst type question (Fig 12 :119), recorded rather than smoothed over.

## VERDICT (from the figures)

The standing ranking is refused. What survives is a separator. The economy presents a vector of per-slot residuals; each slot needs a named, non-updating anchor; each instrument's reach is a property of instrument and habitat, so valence flips with the regime; monetary and fiscal are isomers over the shared atom set with a functional-group fallback; a saturated channel moves the stalled slot to the other instrument or to an imported supply-side primitive with a source gate and shadow test; the union surplus exists only where closures differ and a residual was described first; rules are filters and conventions, never witnesses. No figure returns a ratio or a standing priority order, so that object is a declared `gap`, reported as unreachable, not silence. Mode `specified` on per-domain claims, `general` on the separator, `gap` on the number. Simulated derivation, internally consistent with the figures, not a ground reading.

- **cost if wrong:** bounded and locatable. The packet states mode, three checkable sub-claims and five refuters before the conclusion, so an error is findable rather than invisible, and the live catalyst type question is declared. The real avoidable cost is spending the independence of outside readers by sending a conclusion instead of a separator. Fig 2 :42 prices it: "Buy independence in proportion to the cost of being wrong." A second cost is the metamerism error: naming a single instrument as the source of an aggregate reading that several mechanisms could produce.
- **undoable:** yes. The refused ranking is removable without touching the separator; `07_the_tether_answer.md` and `08_expert_verification_packet.md` already remove it. The catalyst binding can be withdrawn if an instrument shows credibility is consumed rather than regenerated, and the isomer claim falls back to the functional-group claim if the atom set is narrowed. No primitive has to be abandoned, and a sent packet can be superseded by a corrected one.
- **reversed by:** a ground reading, not agreement. A source-discriminating per-slot instrument that names what produced an aggregate outcome; a target series that stays non-updating through a full regime change; a channel test at the effective lower bound that separates saturation from other failure modes; a cross-cycle test that separates coupled from separable loops; a catalyst test that separates regenerated credibility from a replayed success; or any of the five pre-registered refuters in `01` §5. One figure line that directly governs a ratio or a standing order would also reverse the `gap` finding, after a genuine re-census. Until one of these fires, the verdict stands as `candidate`.
