## 3. What the wider literature bears on, and what it does not

1. **The zero lower bound is a reach failure, not a preference.** The liquidity-trap source predicts that the rate channel loses control while the fiscal channel still moves output. This corroborates F4 at the level of shape. It does not settle which policy is "better" outside the trap; the same page records dispute about whether a true trap exists in the Keynesian sense.
2. **A saturated actuator keeps accumulating error.** Integral windup gives the engineering mechanism behind "saturated metrics" in Fig 10 :26. A controller that cannot move its output continues to integrate the error and overshoots when the constraint lifts. This is mechanism-shape corroboration from a low-overlap field, not an economic result.
3. **Targets stop being measures.** Goodhart corroborates the non-updating-anchor requirement of Fig 2 and the convention rule of Fig 10 :47. It is one witness from economics/metrology, not an independent statement of the figure.
4. **Fiscal reaches activity.** Romer and Romer corroborate that the fiscal instrument has a measurable effect on output. The paper is about tax shocks identified from the narrative record; it is not a verdict on stabilization doctrine. Its magnitude is not used in this solution.
5. **The habitat can change regime.** Regime-shift ecology corroborates the shape of Fig 10 :72 (a fixed room while the question moves is the failure) and Fig 11 :66 (substitution, not removal). It says nothing about monetary or fiscal policy.
6. **Credibility behaves like a catalyst.** The chemistry source corroborates the atom definition in Fig 12 :119. The binding of credibility to that atom is provenance from the economics frame, and the type question is still live (see `01` §7).

Overlap declaration: Romer and Romer, Goodhart's law and the liquidity-trap page are all economics sources and share a broad evidence pool with each other and with the policy debate; they count as one witness, not three. Integral windup (control engineering), catalysis (chemistry) and regime shift (ecology) are genuinely low-overlap witnesses for mechanism shape only. The Recursive Self-Improvement Trap essay shares the framework author and evidence pool completely and carries no independent witness weight.
