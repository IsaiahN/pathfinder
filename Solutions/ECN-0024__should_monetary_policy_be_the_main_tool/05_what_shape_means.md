## 1. A shape is a constraint, not a filler

The canonical README states the limit: the framework returns *"what a missing thing would have to look like (**a shape, not a name**)."* Fig 9 :32: "you get a kind of thing, never the answer itself." Fig 13 :135: "It cannot say which primitive is missing, because naming it would mean already holding it."

From *The Library of Babel*, opened this session: "you can cut your search short if the set of books returned is small and descriptive enough that it provides a general shape of the answer that satisfies your question." And: "the problem you have determines what makes a book relevant to you or not."

So a **shape** is the narrowed form of an answer you can state before you can name it. It constrains; it does not fill. For ECN-0024, the shape is the separator itself: per-slot residual, non-updating anchor, residual-relative valence, imported supply-side reach where a demand slot has no instrument. A numerical split or a standing "main tool" designation is a name, not a shape. It is not forbidden, but it is a binding from the economics frame and must be provenanced as such.

## 2. How the shape was obtained

- *Library of Babel*: use the vocabulary you already have that is closest to the thing, correlate it with recurring unfamiliar terms, and recurse. "With each new term gained, you are able to narrow the space by definition and deepen your vocabulary." This audit did exactly that: it started with "stabilize the economy," found "per-slot residual," "anchor," "channel saturation," "union compound," and "import with a source gate," and narrowed toward a separator.
- The same essay names the stopping rule: "You will know that you are done because the finish line will also be the starting place, as there is nowhere else to search and nothing else that can describe the answer better." And it names the malformed-question case: "Perhaps your problem was a bundle or conflation of several problems masquerading as one? You will need to search all of those problems individually." That is Fig 9 :4-5 in essay form and it governs F2 of the audit.
- Fig 6 :30: "The description travels. Fails by being sharpened into terms only we can read. Cast it in effect terms." Fig 9 :30-31: "your words are borrowed too, so describe the effect rather than the cause." The stage-1 email therefore describes what a saturated channel looks like, not the theory of it.
- *Decoding Tacit Knowledge*, opened this session: "When contact isn't possible, the best you can send is a plain description of what you'd expect to see, because anyone can check that against the world." This is the governing sentence for the outreach artifacts.

## 3. What licenses a name

Only two things, both outside the shape itself:

1. **The ground settles it.** `THE_FORMULA.md` step 5: "the ground settles it. Verified only by what pays. A gate is not the ground." The canonical README: the framework "stops at the limit of what the available instruments can distinguish, and records the lineage so the stop is honest."
2. **A provenanced import from a frame whose closure differs** (Fig 6 :23, Fig 8 :50-52), with a source gate and a shadow test.

For ECN-0024, a name would be a concrete policy mix (for example, "rate cuts plus a temporary transfer programme of size X"). Nothing in this folder licenses such a name. The essay *They're Scanning Books When They Should*, carried from the exemplar mapping and not re-opened this session, is reported there as saying: "The real import is not a new term in the existing space. It is a new dimension of the space," and "A minimum viable set of six terms is a human's careful guess at reality's compression axes; another sensorium's minimum set need not be those six." The corresponding warning for this question: declaring the policy objective does not make it neutral. A "neutral" inflation target is a visible choice of what to measure and what to ignore.

## 4. Why the "main tool" claim is a metamerism error

*The Hard Problem is About Source*, opened this session, gives the mechanism: "You can have several underlying causes that create identical readings but the definition of the output would be read as the same." And: "If several paths create the same behavior, then behavior is a projection with lossy information." The colour example reads green but "cannot comment on the source"; one green light, yellow plus blue, RGB or CMYK can all produce it. The essay's conclusion: "An instrument that can't distinguish these cases demonstrates its own inadequacy, it cannot claim anything substantial about what is being measured or what causes it," and the case was resolved "not by argument, but by the arrival of a better instrument."

Applied here: a single macro outcome (say, falling inflation with rising unemployment) can be produced by a demand contraction, a supply improvement, a monetary transmission failure, or a fiscal withdrawal. The aggregate reading cannot name the source. Declaring "monetary is the main tool" from that reading is the metamerism error: a source named from a projection where several sources could give the same reading. What is owed is a source-discriminating instrument (a per-slot measurement), not a stronger verdict. This is the mechanism behind F1 and F9 of the audit.

## 5. Correction to my own reading of "shape, not a name"

I initially used the phrase to forbid naming monetary and fiscal instruments at all. That was an overstatement. The canonical wording governs a **missing** primitive, one not held in Γ. Monetary and fiscal instruments are already held atoms in the policy frame. Naming them is a **binding** from the economics frame onto held atoms, not the invention of a new primitive. What remains forbidden is claiming that the binding moves the wall (Fig 6 :21: "MINT composes inside the closure and can never add an atom") or inventing a seventh term to solve a supply slot without a source gate. Both the original claim and this correction are kept visible here; neither is silently erased. This is Method M6 in `Logs/derivation_log.md`.

What the limit *does* still forbid here: returning a number or a standing ranking as if the framework produced it. That is a name where only a shape is available (F12 of the audit).

## 6. Two consequences for outreach

**Prestige is not action.** From *The Recursive Self-Improvement Trap*, opened this session: "Prestige can fund an action. It can give an action a budget to be tried. But it cannot settle an action. Only the ground can settle it. The Ground Maintainer must be blind to prestige." A list of prominent economists who agree with the separator gives it a budget to be tried. It does not settle it. The legitimate ask is for a check against a real per-slot outcome.

**This audit is a simulation, not a ground reading.** Same essay: "the output is only as good as the validator. The agent, or the tool, or the frame, or the theory, cannot validate itself." And: "LLMs can quickly simulate an approximate answer/output with the formula... However, the truly testable output would need to be derived from the real architecture or working the formula yourself." So `01` is a simulated derivation checked for internal consistency against the figures. It is **not** a test of any stabilization claim and must be labelled as such. It cannot settle the question it reports on.
