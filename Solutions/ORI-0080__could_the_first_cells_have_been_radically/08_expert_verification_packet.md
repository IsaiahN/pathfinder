# Expert verification packet: the Tether on the first cells

**What this is:** a claim in a form an origins-of-life researcher or astrobiologist can check. Mode, derivation and refuters are stated before the conclusion, so the reader knows what to look for and what would settle it.

**Mode: `specified` on the per-domain claims, `general` on the structural shape, not `grounded`.** No first-cell measurement is claimed. This is a shape and a route, not a history. Simulated derivation checked for internal consistency against the figures, not a ground reading.

## 1. The claim, in one paragraph

The organisms alive today are not a closed sample of the first cells, so the absence of a radically-different living cell is not evidence that none existed at the origin. Independently, the figures constrain the shape of any cell: a boundary, a loop whose output re-enters as input, and a comparison against a ground that does not update on what a frame thinks about it. They name no chemistry. Over a fixed chemical inventory, an alternative first cell is an isomer; with a different inventory it is an import. Both are reachable as shapes; neither is a history. The original chemistry is a declared `gap` until a ground reading settles it.

## 2. Three checkable sub-claims

### C1: the non-sealed survivor set (framework-internal, checkable by reading two figures)
- **Statement:** searching the living biosphere cannot establish absence of earlier, radically-different cell-forms.
- **Source, verbatim:** Fig 8 :39: *"So a search that finds nothing is never a proof of absence."* Fig 9 :44: *"Only a sealed room can be searched to the end, and no frame is a sealed room. So searching and finding nothing proves nothing."* Fig 11 :68-69: *"what you failed to reproduce is invisible until the goal fails"*, *"what you unintentionally introduced is invisible until it acts."*
- **Why it is triangulated:** Fig 8 derives it from closure idempotence; Fig 9 from the impossibility of a sealed room; Fig 11 from the isolation/substitution law. Three different routes, low overlap.
- **Refuter:** produce a figure line that makes the survivor set a sealed room, or an instrument that reads the original cell-forms directly from living genomes.

### C2: reachability of an alternative cell (figure definition plus chemistry frame)
- **Statement:** "radically unlike" splits into two dimensions. Same atoms, different arrangement is an isomer; different atoms or solvent is an import. Both are composable as shapes.
- **Source, verbatim:** Fig 12 :117: *"isomer. Same atoms, different bond, different substance. The difference is invisible in the formula and decisive in the behaviour."* Fig 6 :23: *"No frame escapes its own closure. The bound moves only on contact with a frame whose closure differs."* Fig 13 :115: *"The set is closed. The arrangements are not. That gap is why import exists."*
- **What to check:** is the split faithful to the ordinary phrase? Does a mirror-chirality or alternative-base organism count as same-atoms-different-bond, and a silicon or ammonia organism as a different inventory? The chemistry catalogue in `10_source_ledger.csv` supplies the examples.
- **Refuter:** show that one of the two dimensions collapses, i.e. that an inventory change is just a rearrangement (which would break Fig 12 :117's "same atoms" condition), or that an arrangement change adds an atom (which would break Fig 6 :21, *"MINT composes inside the closure and can never add an atom."*).

### C3: the shape a cell must have (framework-internal derivation)
- **Statement:** any cell-shape requires a boundary, a re-entrant loop and a part that survives its own use; a chain cannot close.
- **Source, verbatim:** Fig 12 :121: *"cycle. The output re-enters as input. The sign of the loop decides whether it closes, and a chain cannot close by construction."* Fig 12 :119: *"catalyst. A part that survives its own use. Spent and regenerating parts behave differently across uses, and nothing records which a term is."* Fig 11 :39: *"capability is a property of agent-and-habitat, never of the agent alone."*
- **Refuter:** demonstrate a self-maintaining, evolving compartment that can be written as an open chain with no re-entrant output; or show that capability belongs to the agent alone, independent of habitat contact.

### C4: what the figures do not license (guard rail)
- **Statement:** no figure line selects RNA, DNA, a lipid bilayer, L-amino acids, the standard code or any solvent as the original substrate.
- **Source, verbatim:** Fig 9 :32: *"you get a kind of thing, never the answer itself."* Fig 6 :32: *"An instrument is not built from a description. It is improved from a worse instrument already returning something."*
- **Refuter:** a figure line that names a chemistry, or a ground reading that settles one. Until then the chemistry is `gap`, not silence and not a name.

## 3. What is explicitly not claimed

- Not that a radically-different first cell existed. Reachability is not actuality.
- Not that the first cells were like modern bacteria. LUCA reconstruction is about the last common ancestor of *survivors*, not the first cellular forms; the FUCA literature explicitly distinguishes a non-cellular ancestor and extinct sister lineages with no direct modern descendants.
- Not that silicon, mirror or hachimoji life existed at the origin. They are provenanced bindings from the chemistry frame.
- Not that the shared molecular core is unimportant. It is strong evidence for common ancestry among survivors; it cannot certify absence of other forms.
- Not a derivation of one chemical hypothesis from the six-term set, and no new primitive.
- Not `grounded`. Nothing here is asserted as settled by experiment.

## 5. What would settle it

A ground reading or a necessity result, not agreement. From *The Hard Problem is About Source*: an instrument that reads green *"cannot comment on the source"*; the spectrometer resolved it. Here:

1. A dated chemical or isotopic signature of the first cells that fixes their molecular inventory.
2. A result showing self-maintenance plus Darwinian evolution cannot be composed without a specific modern feature (ribosome, DNA, one lipid chemistry).
3. An instrument that discriminates among single origin, horizontal transfer and convergent replacement as sources of the shared core.
4. Any of the six pre-registered refuters in `01` §3, which were written before the conclusion.

## 6. What an expert can check in twenty minutes

1. Read Fig 8 :39, Fig 9 :44 and Fig 11 :68-69. Do you accept that a non-sealed survivor set cannot establish absence?
2. Check C2 against your own knowledge: is the arrangement/inventory split faithful, and are the chemical examples in the right column?
3. Name one instrument that would discriminate the source of the shared molecular core, or say why none is possible.
4. Send the refutation. A failure is more useful than an endorsement. A reply is a conversation, never corroboration, and it carries zero verdict weight until an independent ground reading backs it.
