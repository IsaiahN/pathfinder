## Wider literature that bears on the question

These are the substantive sources used in `09_watson_answer.md` §WORLD. They are secondary corroboration only; the verdict comes from the figures.

1. **Philosophy of biology and astrobiology.** The shadow-biosphere proposal is the direct literature analogue of "radically unlike any organism alive today": it asserts that alternative biochemistries may be present and unnoticed because detection targets the biochemistry of macro-organisms. It also records the honest failure case: the arsenic-DNA claim was retracted in 2025. That retraction is itself evidence for the pre-registered refuter discipline, not against the reachability claim.
2. **Evolutionary biology, primary source.** Budd and Mann 2018 gives the mathematical mechanism for why a survivor set is a biased sample: conditioning on survival generates apparent early bursts and rate slow-downs in otherwise homogeneous birth-death models. It predicts that inferences from extant clades cannot be generalized to the whole historical process without extinct clades. This corroborates Fig 8 :39 and Fig 11 :68-69 at genuinely low overlap with the framework.
3. **Human cognition and statistics.** Survivorship bias, with Wald's returning-aircraft case, is the exact epistemic shape of reconstructing the origin from survivors: reinforce where the returning aircraft was hit and you ignore where the lost aircraft were fatally hit. This corroborates Fig 1 :19 and Fig 9 :27-28.
4. **Institutional science communication.** The NASA Astrobiology FAQ states the lost-information problem in the researcher's own words and also states that extraterrestrial life could be undetectable by current techniques. It is an institutional source, not an independent experimental witness, and is recorded as such in the ledger.
5. **Synthetic biology / alternative biochemistry.** The catalogue of xeno nucleic acids, mirror life and hachimoji DNA demonstrates that "different arrangement over the same elements" is a laboratory-reachable category, which corroborates the isomer dimension of F2. It does not demonstrate that such a form existed at the origin.
6. **Framework essay.** *The Hard Problem is About Source* supplies the metamerism point: a reading does not determine the source, and the answer stays unreached until a better instrument exists. It shares the framework author's evidence pool with the figures, so its agreement carries little independent weight.

Figure-grounded reasons:

- **Fig 4 :22-23**: *"Only methods cross up. Only head starts come down."* Sending the full architecture before a recipient has described a residual sends a recording, not a generator. Lead with the refuter and the shape claim.
- **Fig 10 :52**: *"Bring a characterised residual, never the source."* A recipient who has absorbed the framework before giving a verdict has joined the frame being checked.

- **Fig 2 :40**: *"disqualifying for a verdict: agreement about the world is worthless when the agreeing frames share an evidence pool."* Do not rank recipients by receptiveness and do not pool later agreement.
- **Fig 8 :50-52**: convergent derivation and adopted import look identical in the contents; only the record separates them. Keep the provenance of every source you forward.

**Order in a reply:** (1) the shape claim and its refuter; (2) ask what the recipient would count as a failure and record the answer; (3) only then the relevant links, with the note that the figures, not the summary, are the authority; (4) the loop as a method, with the instruction to write a fresh refuter and state a mode.

## The two-stage offer and the hard precondition

- **Stage 1 before stage 2** is Fig 10 :52 and Fig 2 :45. The checker sees the residual without the framework interior.
- **Stage 2 is opt-in.** Once a reader has the theory, their agreement stops being evidence. The stage-1 verdict must be recorded first and is never pooled as corroboration (Fig 2 :40).
- **Stage 3 sends a method, which is permitted** (Fig 4 :22). The loop is a generator, so giving it away does not give away an answer.
- **Stage 3 still needs a ground per problem.** Fig 5 :65 requires the sought-for shape to be bound as an ordinary term; otherwise the loop cannot check its own direction.
- **Hard precondition:** the offer only works if the stage-1 output survives. The refuter list in `02_outreach_email.md` is the stage-1 test. If a checker fires one of them, stop and withdraw rather than escalate to the theory.
