## QUESTION

Decide, in effect terms, what the Tether says about the claim that the first cells could have been radically unlike any organism alive today. Option A: the shared molecular core of modern life constrains the origin, so radically-different first cells are excluded or improbable. Option B: the survivor set cannot exclude them, and the figures return only the shape a cell must have. What each leads to: A sends backward extrapolation from modern genomes as the route and treats the universal core as an anchor. B sends a shape claim with six written refuters, keeps the original chemistry as a declared gap, and owes an instrument from the early Earth. The residual is indexed per condition (substrate, code, energy coupling, compartment, chirality), so this is a union of conditions, not one dial (Fig 9 :54, :57).

## VERDICT (from the figures)

The figures return a shape with refuters, not a history. A cell-shape requires a boundary, a re-entrant loop and a comparison against a ground that does not update; that is `general` and structural. The survivor set cannot establish absence of radically-different earlier forms; that is `specified` and triangulated across three figures. Over a fixed chemical inventory, an alternative first cell is an isomer; with a different inventory it is an import; both are reachable as shapes and neither is licensed as an event; that is `specified` and ungrounded. The original chemistry is a declared `gap`, not silence and not a name. Overall: **yes in the reachability sense**, `specified` on the per-domain claims, `general` on the structural shape, not `grounded`, status `candidate`, with six pre-registered refuters in `01` §3. No verdict was averaged across domains.

- **cost if wrong:** bounded and locatable. If a specific modern feature turns out to be necessary for Darwinian self-maintenance (refuter 3), the arrangement and import reachability claims fail in that dimension and a search for alternative biochemistries at the origin is wasted budget. The real cost is spending a checker's independence by sending the framework before the residual: Fig 2 :42 says "Buy independence in proportion to the cost of being wrong." That cost is avoidable by leading with the shape claim and its refuter, as `02` and `08` do.
- **undoable:** yes. The claim is a shape with written refuters, not an atom or a primitive. If a ground reading settles the original chemistry, the shape verdict stays and only the chemistry gap closes. No term in the library has to be withdrawn and no lineage has to be rewritten.
- **reversed by:** a ground reading or a necessity result, never agreement. A dated chemical or isotopic signature of the first cells; a demonstration that self-maintenance plus Darwinian evolution cannot be composed without a specific modern feature; an instrument that discriminates among single origin, horizontal transfer and convergent replacement as sources of the shared core; or any of the six written refuters. Until one fires, the verdict stands as `candidate`.
