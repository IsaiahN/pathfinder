# Outreach draft: one checkable output before the theory

**Use:** one to one, or as a cover note attached to a submission through a public venue in `03_public_contact_points.csv`. **Not** for bulk send. No personal emails are used.

**Hard precondition:** do not send until the recipient can see the refuter in the first paragraph. If the refuter is buried after the theory, the two-stage offer collapses on contact (Fig 10 :42, Fig 2 :35).

---

**Subject:** Would you count this as a failure? One claim about the first cells

> Hi [name],
>
> I have one specific output about the earliest cells that I would like you to check against what you already know. I am not asking you to read a framework first, and I am not asking you to agree with anything. If the output does not survive, there is nothing else to look at.
>
> **The output (`specified` on reachability, `general` on the shape, not `grounded`):** the set of organisms alive today is not a closed sample of the first cells. It is what survived roughly 3.8 billion years of extinction, substitution and gene transfer. So the fact that we do not find a cell radically unlike the known biosphere today is not evidence that no such cell existed at the origin. Separately, the figures I am working from constrain the *shape* of any cell: a boundary that holds inside from outside, a loop in which the output re-enters as input, and a comparison against a ground that does not update on what anyone thinks. They do not select a chemistry. A first cell built from different atoms, a different code or a different arrangement is therefore reachable in principle, and the shared molecular core of modern life is a survivorship filter rather than an origin constraint.
>
> **What I am not claiming:** that the figures name RNA, DNA, a lipid bilayer, L-amino acids or a genetic code as the original substrate. They return a shape, not a name. I am also not claiming a first cell has been found, that a particular alternative biochemistry actually existed, or that this is settled. It is a candidate with written refuters.
>
> **What would refute it, written before this message:**
> 1. A figure line that requires cells at any level to use one specific substrate, code or solvent.
> 2. A comparative reading of living genomes that does not move when the genome sample or method moves, i.e. a non-updating anchor for the ancestor.
> 3. A ground result showing that self-maintenance plus Darwinian evolution cannot be composed without one specific modern feature such as the ribosome or DNA.
> 4. A preserved, dated signature of the first cells showing the same molecular core as modern life.
> 5. A self-maintaining, evolving compartment that is an open chain with no re-entrant output.
>
> If any of these holds, I withdraw the claim. A one-line "this fails because..." is more useful to me than agreement. If you would rather not engage, no reply is needed.
>
> **If it survives, and only then:** here are the sources. The figures, not my summary of them, are the authority. I can send them after you have recorded your verdict on the output above.
>
> **If you want to reuse the machinery:** I will send the loop separately. Each person writes their own refuter and states their own mode before running it. A reported failure is more useful to me than a reported success.
>
>: [name]

## Two things not to do

1. Do not add "if you agree, please share." Agreement is not evidence and sharing is not triangulation.
2. Do not attach the full audit to the first message. A reader who has absorbed the interiors before giving a verdict has joined the frame being checked (Fig 10 :52).
