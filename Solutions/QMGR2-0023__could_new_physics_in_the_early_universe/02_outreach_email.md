# Outreach draft - validate the shape first, then decide about the theory

**Use:** one to one, or as a cover letter attached to a submission through the public venues in `03_public_contact_points.csv`. **Not** for bulk send.

**Hard precondition:** do not send until the shape claim in `01_figure_grounded_audit.md` sections 0 to 4 is stated cleanly and the refuter is written down. The offer collapses on contact if the stage-1 output cannot survive checking.

**Subject:** One checkable claim about the Hubble tension, before any theory

> Hi [name],
>
> I have one specific output I would like you to check against what you already know. I am not asking you to read a theory first, and I am not asking you to agree with anything. If the output does not survive, there is nothing else to look at.
>
> **The output (`specified`, not `grounded`).** The expansion rate inferred from the cosmic microwave background is not a direct measurement. It is an inversion of the acoustic scale r_s / D_A under an assumed early-universe model. The standard model fits the acoustic peaks very well, so the global fit looks clean, but one slot in it, H0, disagrees with the local distance-ladder value at roughly 5 to 6 sigma. A clean global fit with one live slot is a legal state, not a reason to average the signal away.
>
> The shape claim is this. Because the inferred H0 is a function of the early-universe closure, an additional pre-recombination contribution that changes the expansion history can shrink the sound horizon and therefore permit a higher H0 under the same measured acoustic scale. That is a statement about what is reachable from a given set of assumptions, not an endorsement of any named model. I am not claiming that early dark energy, extra neutrino species, or modified recombination is the answer.
>
> **What any candidate would have to survive, stated before the theory:** a provenanced source for the new component; a shadow test, meaning it explains the specific gap it was introduced for; and survival of ground readings that were not used to tune it, especially primordial element abundances, baryon acoustic oscillations, large-scale structure, CMB lensing and the growth-parameter constraints. A model that fixes the CMB slot while breaking those has explained one occasion perfectly and fails the bargain. The ground, not the number of papers proposing a model, decides this.
>
> **What would refute the shape claim:**
> 1. the H0 discrepancy falls below 3 sigma under improved calibration on both sides, with no live slot left;
> 2. a sound-horizon-free determination converges on the standard-model value and disagrees with the local ladder, so no import is owed to the early-universe closure;
> 3. every import that reduces the sound horizon is excluded generically by independent data, not merely by a tuning failure of individual models;
> 4. a theorem shows H0 is invariant under all allowed pre-recombination closure changes.
>
> I wrote these down before sending this. If any holds, I will narrow or retract the claim. A one-line "this fails because..." is more useful to me than agreement. If you would rather not engage, no reply is needed.
>
> **If it survives, and only then:** the sources are below. The figures, not my summary of them, are the authority.

> - Canonical usage rule (local framework copy): `FRAMEWORK/reference/FIGURES_README_canonical.md`
