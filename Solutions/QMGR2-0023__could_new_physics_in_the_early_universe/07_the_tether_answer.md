# The Tether's answer on early-universe new physics and the expansion rate

**Mode: `specified` on every substantive claim, `general` on the structural shape, not `grounded`.** This is a shape and a route, not a result. Simulated derivation checked against the figures (`05_what_shape_means.md` section 6), not a ground reading.

## 1. The residual it answers

The expansion rate H0 inferred from the CMB is not directly measured. It is inverted from the acoustic scale θ_s = r_s / D_A under the ΛCDM closure. That inversion fits the acoustic peaks extremely well, so the global residual is near zero, yet the H0 slot disagrees with the local distance-ladder value at roughly 5 to 6 sigma. Fig 1 :13: *"a global R near zero with one live slot residual is a legal state."* Fig 1 :19: *"The residual is indexed by object slot, not aggregated."*

This is a **union compound**, not one hard question (Fig 9 :57: *"a case in two conditions is not a harder case of either."*). Calibration systematics, model-dependence of the inference, sample variance and genuinely new early-universe physics overlap. They must be split before searching.

## 2. The shape

1. **The inferred H0 is closure-dependent.** What the CMB gives is a ratio; H0 is a function of the assumed early-universe model. A clean fit within one closure does not prove that no other closure yields a different H0 (Fig 6 :23: *"No frame escapes its own closure. The bound moves only on contact with a frame whose closure differs."*).
2. **An early-universe import can shift that inferred rate, in principle.** Import is *"the only operator that moves the wall"* (Fig 6 :14). A component that changes the pre-recombination expansion history shrinks the sound horizon r_s, and with θ_s and H0 r_s fixed, permits a higher H0. That is the mechanism; it is `specified`, not a claim that nature used it.
3. **"New physics" must be split before it is answered.** Rearranging held atoms (extra N_eff, curvature) is SEARCHED and cannot move the wall; extending an instrument is INWARD; importing a genuinely new component is OUTWARD. Fig 6 :31: *"Neither answer tells you much about the other. Run them as two tracks, and never read one against the other."*
4. **Any viable import must pass three gates.** A provenanced source (Fig 6 :12, *"source gate: provenance"*), a shadow test (Fig 6 :13, *"shadow test: explains a gap"*), and survival of independent ground readings that were not used to tune it. Fig 5 :44: *"and so does a term that explains one occasion perfectly."* BBN, BAO, large-scale structure, CMB lensing and growth constraints are the cross-checks. The literature records models failing them (Khalife et al 2024; Poulin et al 2023).
5. **Low-overlap instruments decide, not model counts.** Fig 2 :40: *"agreement about the world is worthless when the agreeing frames share an evidence pool."* A sound-horizon-free H0 determination is worth more than another CMB fit because it does not share the tuned atom. Zaborowski et al 2024/2025 find *"no evidence for new early-Universe physics (e.g. early dark energy)"* from such a measurement. That is ground beginning to speak, not a conflict with the shape.
6. **The framework returns a shape, not a name.** Fig 9 :32: *"you get a kind of thing, never the answer itself."* It cannot certify early dark energy, extra neutrino species or modified recombination as *the* answer. That requires the ground.
7. **The two determinations are isomer-like.** Fig 12 :117: *"Same atoms, different bond, different substance."* The acoustic scale is bonded to a model-dependent distance in one route and to calibrated candles in the other. They cannot be averaged or substituted (Fig 12 :94: *"The arrangement is the substance."*).

## 3. What it explicitly does not say

- Not that new physics exists, and not that it is the cause of the tension.
- Not that any named model is favoured, and not that early dark energy is ruled out. Current data are inconclusive on that specific question (Poulin et al 2023).
- Not that the tension is real as opposed to a systematic. That is one of the union conditions and must be split out, not assumed away.
- Not that the CMB inference is wrong. ΛCDM fits the acoustic peaks extremely well; the residual is in one slot.
- Not `grounded`. No measurement at this resolution settles which closure nature used.

## 4. What would settle it - the ground

The instruments named in the world literature are the ground readings that would close the slot:

1. Next-generation CMB experiments (Simons Observatory, CMB-S4, ACT, SPT) measuring acoustic peaks to smaller scales and polarisation at higher signal to noise. Kou and Lewis 2024/2025 forecast that these *"should detect almost any such modification at high significance."*
2. Sound-horizon-free H0 determinations (DESI full-shape, EFTofLSS, shape fit) combined with BBN and uncalibrated supernovae. These do not share the r_s degeneracy.
3. Cross-checks that can kill an import: BBN light-element abundances, BAO, CMB lensing, the BOSS/DESI full-shape power spectrum and S8 growth.
4. A calibration ground on the local ladder that shrinks the H0 slot from the other side.

Standing refuters from `01_figure_grounded_audit.md` section 4: the discrepancy falls below 3 sigma; a sound-horizon-free value converges on the standard-model value and disagrees with the ladder; every r_s-reducing import is generically excluded; a theorem proves H0 invariant under closure changes; or the union dissolves into a single systematic.

## 5. The honest stop

**Reachable in principle, unsettled in fact.** *"Unreachable is a verdict here, not a silence"* (Fig 13 :134) would apply if a re-census showed that no import could close the slot. It does not. The target is reachable; what is missing is a ground reading, not a new argument. The answer stops at the limit of what current instruments distinguish and records the lineage so the stop is checkable. *"Indiscernibility is not identity."*

## 6. One-paragraph version for outreach

> The Tether does not say that new early-universe physics exists, and it does not name a model. It says that the expansion rate inferred from the cosmic microwave background is a function of the assumed early-universe model, not a direct measurement, so it sits in a single live slot even while the overall fit is excellent. Because that inference depends on the closure, importing a genuinely new pre-recombination component that shortens the sound horizon can make a higher expansion rate reachable under the same acoustic-scale data. But "new physics" is three operations, not one: rearranging existing degrees of freedom cannot move the wall, a sharper instrument may, and only a provenanced import with a shadow test can. Any such import must survive light-element abundances, baryon acoustic oscillations, large-scale structure and growth measurements, or it has merely explained one occasion. The verdict is a shape plus four refuters written in advance, not an endorsement of early dark energy or any other proposal. The ground, through new instruments rather than through counts of papers, closes the slot.
