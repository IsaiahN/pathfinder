## What the wider literature bears on this question

1. **The tension is a union, not a single lever.** Di Valentino et al 2021 list many overlapping proposal classes and say the data *"demand a hypothesis with enough rigor to explain multiple observations."* This matches Fig 9's split instruction. It is not independent corroboration of the framework; it is a cosmologist's statement about their own ground.
2. **Reducing the sound horizon is the standard early-universe lever.** Kou and Lewis, Cai and Wang, and Poulin et al all describe the same mechanism: a pre-recombination contribution changes H(z), shrinks r_s, and permits a higher H0 under the fixed θ_s constraint. This is the world-facing content of the figure claim that import moves the wall.
3. **The cross-checks are the live issue.** Khalife et al 2024 (read via the search result at https://arxiv.org/html/2312.09814v3) report that some models are ruled out at more than 3 sigma and *"none of the models considered in this work is favored with enough statistical significance to become the next concordance model of Cosmology."* Note: this source was read as an HTML search result and is listed in the ledger with that status.
4. **A low-overlap instrument now exists.** Zaborowski et al 2024/2025 remove the sound-horizon scale from the inference and find *"no evidence for new early-Universe physics."* By Fig 2 :31 this is worth more than another CMB fit, because it does not draw on the same tuned atom.
5. **Forecast, not verdict.** Kou and Lewis and Poulin et al both point to future CMB experiments as the thing that will settle the question. Fig 9 :42: *"narrowing gets you close, the ground decides."*

No world source was used to hand a verdict to the figures. Where a source bears on settlement, the verdict is still recorded as `candidate` until an instrument fires.

## Distribution: offer, do not push

Figure-grounded reasons:

- **Fig 10 :52** - *"Bring a characterised residual, never the source."* Sending the framework before anyone has described the H0 slot sends a recording, not a generator. Lead with the shape and its refuter; offer links after.
- **Fig 10 :42** - *"A verifier that can reconstruct the claim cannot verify it."* Seat 4 must not hold the interiors. A recipient who has absorbed the whole framework before giving a verdict has joined the frame being checked.
- **Fig 9 :27** - *"describe it before you go looking, or any frame you pick will seem to fit."* Ask what they would expect to see if the shape claim were true before giving them the papers.
- **Fig 2 :40** - *"agreement about the world is worthless when the agreeing frames share an evidence pool"*; Fig 7 :13 - *"agreement compounds, evidence does not."* Do not send to a list chosen for likely agreement, and do not count later agreement as corroboration.
- **Fig 2 :42** - *"Buy independence in proportion to the cost of being wrong."* The cost here is a wrong new atom in the standard model, which Fig 6 :24 prices as worse than starting from primitives.

**Recommended order in a reply:** (1) the shape claim and its refuter; (2) ask what they would count as a failure, and record it; (3) only then, the figure text and formula links, with the note that the figures, not this summary, are the authority.

## The two-stage offer (with the optional third stage)

1. **Stage 1:** the H0 slot residual, the shape claim, and the four pre-registered refuters. No theory, no links. Record the checker's verdict now.
2. **Stage 2, opt-in only:** if they are curious, offer the figure text, the usage rule and the formula. Their agreement at this point is never pooled as corroboration, because they now hold the interiors.
3. **Stage 3:** offer the formula as a method to reuse. This is permitted (Fig 4 :22, *"Only methods cross up. Only head starts come down."*), but each user must write their own refuter and state their own mode, because a frame cannot check the direction of its own question.

**Hard precondition:** stages 2 and 3 only work if stage 1 survives. The claim as written in this folder is `specified`, not `grounded`. Do not present the shape as a result.
