## 1. A shape is a constraint, not a filler

The canonical rule: the framework returns *"what a missing thing would have to look like (a shape, not a name)."* Fig 9 :32: *"you get a kind of thing, never the answer itself."* Fig 13: *"It can say the answer is out of reach with the library as it stands... It cannot say which primitive is missing, because naming it would mean already holding it."*

From *The Library of Babel*, which I opened: *"you can cut your search short if the set of books returned is small and descriptive enough that it provides a general shape of the answer that satisfies your question."*

So for QMGR2-0023 a **shape** is the form of a viable early-universe answer that can be stated before a model is named. It constrains; it does not fill. The shape here is:

> the inferred H0 is a function of the early-universe closure; an import that changes pre-recombination expansion can shrink r_s and move H0; any viable import must pass independent ground readings; an instrument, not a model count, settles it.

"Early dark energy is the answer" is a name. The statement above is a shape.

## 2. How a shape is obtained

- *The Library of Babel*: *"use the existing vocabulary you do know that is closest to the words that you wish to describe and grasp the shape of"*; then recurse on new terms that overlap or correlate. *"Your search space will narrow with every vocabulary word that you believe is in the answer or associated with the answer."*
- Fig 6 :30: *"The description travels. Fails by being sharpened into terms only we can read. Cast it in effect terms."* Fig 9 :30: *"your words are borrowed too, so describe the effect"* rather than the cause. So the effect description is "a component that changes the expansion history before recombination and reduces the sound horizon," not a field name or a Lagrangian.
- *The Library of Babel* on the bundle case, which is directly relevant here: *"Perhaps your problem was a bundle or conflation of several problems masquerading as one? You will need to search all of those problems individually to find their answers and then combine them in a satisfactory way."* This is the essay-level statement of Fig 9's union-compound rule (F2 in the audit).
- *The Library of Babel* on steering: the returned books must be *"tested or ranged against the problem you want answered in order to steer the direction of the next search."* That is why this folder keeps the four refuters in front of the model discussion.

## 3. What licenses a name

Two things, both outside the shape itself:

1. **The ground settles it.** Formula step 5: *"the ground settles it. Verified only by what pays. A gate is not the ground."* The figures README: the framework *"stops at the limit of what the available instruments can distinguish, and records the lineage so the stop is honest."* For this question the instruments are BBN, BAO, large-scale structure, CMB lensing, and next-generation CMB experiments.
2. **A provenanced import from a frame whose closure differs** (Fig 6 :23, Fig 8 :50-52), with a source gate and a shadow test. For this question: a proposed component must state where it comes from and explain the specific gap it was introduced for, not merely enlarge error bars.

In the cosmology literature, the same distinction appears as the difference between a model that improves a fit by adding degrees of freedom and a model that is *"favored with enough statistical significance to become the next concordance model"* (Khalife et al 2024). The former is a candidate; the latter would require the ground.

## 4. The metamerism caution

Carried from the exemplar's reading of *The Hard Problem is About Source* (I did not re-open it this session): an instrument that reads green *"cannot comment on the source"*, and *"If several paths create the same behavior, then behavior is a projection with lossy information."* Applied here: a fit to the acoustic scale is a reading, not a source. Several different pre-recombination histories can produce the same θ_s. Naming one of them as the cause from the fit alone is the same lossy-source error. What is owed is a discriminator, such as the sound-horizon-free H0 determination in Zaborowski et al 2024/2025, not a name.

## 5. The stop

*The Library of Babel*: *"If the final book in your exhaustive search doesn't answer the question, then there are only two possibilities: the nature of your question was unanswerable, or malformed."* For QMGR2-0023 the question is neither; it is a union compound that needs splitting (Fig 9 :4). The honest stop is: the shape is derived, the instruments are named, and the verdict is `candidate`. Fig 13 :134: *"Unreachable is a verdict here, not a silence"* would apply only if a genuine re-census showed no import could close the slot. Here the target is reachable in principle, just unsettled.

## 6. Two consequences for this folder

**Prestige is not action.** From *The Recursive Self-Improvement Trap*, which I opened: *"Prestige must never be allowed to validate an action directly... Prestige can fund an action. It can give an action a budget to be tried. But it cannot settle an action. Only the ground can settle it. The Ground Maintainer must be blind to prestige."* A campaign that gathers famous names to agree that new physics is the answer is the named collapse, not corroboration. The legitimate ask is the opposite: give the shape a budget to be tried, and let the instruments settle it.

**My own audit is a simulation, not a ground reading.** Same essay: *"LLMs can quickly simulate an approximate answer/output... However, the truly testable output would need to be derived from the real architecture or working the formula yourself."* Also: *"the output is only as good as the validator. The agent, or the tool, or the frame, or the theory, cannot validate itself."* So `01_figure_grounded_audit.md` is a simulated derivation, checked for internal consistency against the figures. It is not a test of the Hubble tension and must be labelled as such. It cannot settle anything it reports on.
