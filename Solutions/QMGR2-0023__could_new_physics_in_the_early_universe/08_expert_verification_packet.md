# Expert verification packet - early-universe new physics and the inferred expansion rate

**What this is:** a claim in a form a cosmologist can check. Mode, derivation and refuters are stated before the conclusion, so a reader knows what to look for and what would settle it.

**Mode: `specified` on the substantive claims, `general` on the structural shape, not `grounded`.** No new measurement is claimed. This is a shape and a route, not a result.

## 1. The claim, in one paragraph

The Hubble constant inferred from the CMB is not directly measured; it is inverted from the acoustic scale θ_s = r_s / D_A under an assumed early-universe model. The ΛCDM fit is excellent, so the global residual is near zero, but one slot in it, H0, disagrees with the local distance-ladder determination at roughly 5 to 6 sigma. Because that inference is closure-dependent, an imported pre-recombination component that changes the expansion history can shrink the sound horizon r_s and permit a higher H0 under the same measured acoustic scale. This is a statement about reachability, not an endorsement of any named model. "New physics" splits into three operations with different rules: rearranging atoms already in ΛCDM is search within a fixed closure, extending an instrument is an inward move, and importing a genuinely new component is the only operation that moves the closure wall. Any import must carry provenance, pass a shadow test, and survive independent ground readings (BBN, BAO, large-scale structure, CMB lensing, growth) that were not used to tune it. The ground, not model counts, settles the question. What follows is the shape any viable answer must have, with refuters written in advance.

- **Statement:** a near-zero global fit with one live H0 slot is a legal state, and the disagreement is a union compound that must be split rather than searched as one problem.

- **Refuter:** show that the H0 slot is fully determined by held degrees of freedom so no live mass remains; or show that one condition is sufficient so the union is a partition.

### C2 - physics-facing; checkable by a cosmologist against the acoustic-scale degeneracy

- **Statement:** raising H0 under fixed θ_s and the BAO degeneracy H0 r_s approximately constant requires reducing r_s, which a pre-recombination contribution to H(z) can do. This is the mechanism by which an early-universe import shifts the inferred rate. No model is endorsed.
- **Source, verbatim:** Fig 6 :14 - *"the only operator that moves the wall."* Fig 6 :22 - *"NOVEL means novel relative to atoms, not to the world. IMPORT adds an atom, and the added atom is what expands the closure."* Fig 6 :23 - *"No frame escapes its own closure. The bound moves only on contact with a frame whose closure differs."* Fig 9 :32 - *"you get a kind of thing, never the answer itself."*
- **What to check:** is the degeneracy statement correct in the standard analysis? Does a component that contributes before recombination and dilutes away shrink r_s as claimed? Does the literature agree that this is the standard early-universe lever (Kou and Lewis 2024/2025; Cai and Wang 2026; Poulin et al 2023)?
- **Refuter:** a theorem showing H0 is invariant under all allowed pre-recombination closure changes; or a demonstration that θ_s plus H0 r_s fix H0 independently of the expansion history. That would kill the mechanism.

### C3 - ground-facing; checkable against the acceptance test and the cross-checks

- **Statement:** a viable import must be provenanced, explain the specific gap it was introduced for, and survive BBN, BAO, large-scale structure, CMB lensing and growth constraints. A model that fixes the CMB slot while breaking those fails.
- **Source, verbatim:** Fig 6 :12 - *"source gate: provenance."* Fig 6 :13 - *"shadow test: explains a gap."* Fig 5 :43 - *"A new term is worth having when stating it, plus what remains unexplained after it, costs less than what was unexplained before."* Fig 5 :44 - *"and so does a term that explains one occasion perfectly."* Fig 2 :31 - *"Independence, between two frames, is how little they draw on the same evidence."*
- **What to check:** do the named cross-checks actually bind the class of models that reduce r_s? Is the sound-horizon-free DESI determination (Zaborowski et al 2024/2025) genuinely lower overlap with the CMB acoustic-scale inference than another CMB fit?
- **Refuter:** exhibit a provenanced import that reduces the H0 slot residual, survives every independent cross-check without re-tuning, and is still rejected. That would show the gates are not the right criterion. Or show the cross-checks are irrelevant to the term's standing, which would refute the finding as stated.

## 3. What is explicitly not claimed

- Not that new physics exists, and not that it causes the Hubble tension.
- Not an endorsement of early dark energy, extra relativistic species, modified recombination, or any named model.
- Not that the tension is real rather than a systematic; that is a separate union condition.
- Not that the standard model fails to fit the CMB; it fits very well, and the residual is in one slot.
- Not that any current model is ruled in or ruled out as the concordance model. The literature says current data remain inconclusive on specific proposals.
- Not `grounded`. Nothing here is asserted as settled by experiment.

## 5. What would settle it

1. Next-generation CMB data (Simons Observatory, CMB-S4, ACT, SPT) detecting or excluding a pre-recombination modification at high significance.
2. Sound-horizon-free H0 determinations that do not share the r_s atom, combined with BBN and supernovae, converging on a value that either removes the need for an import or confirms a real offset.
3. Generic exclusion of the r_s-reducing model class by BBN, BAO, LSS, lensing or growth data.
4. Improved local calibration shrinking the H0 slot from the other side.

Agreement among papers is not one of these. Prestige is not one of these.
