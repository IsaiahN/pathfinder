## DERIVATION

### Q1 - Is "new physics" an import or a rearrangement?

- `THE_FORMULA.md` step 7 separates SEARCHED (is it in here and unfound?), INWARD (extend the instrument) and OUTWARD (import a frame that already holds it).
- Fig 6 :22: *"NOVEL means novel relative to atoms, not to the world. IMPORT adds an atom, and the added atom is what expands the closure."*
- Extra relativistic species, spatial curvature and dynamical dark energy are rearrangements of held atoms. Di Valentino et al 2021 report that many such extensions *"merely enlarge the uncertainties"* rather than resolve the tension. That is SEARCHED: the closure does not change, more of the space is visited.
- A new pre-recombination component whose dynamics are not among the held atoms is OUTWARD. It must be described in effect terms before it is imported (Fig 9 :30), carry provenance and pass a shadow test (Fig 6 :12-13).
- **Conclusion:** "new physics" is ambiguous until this split is made. `triangulated` for the distinction (Formula step 7 plus Fig 6 plus the literature classification).

### Q2 - The sound-horizon lever (mechanism, `specified`, ungrounded)

- The measured quantity is the angular acoustic scale θ_s = r_s(z_*) / D_A(z_*), known to high precision. BAO adds the degeneracy H0 r_s approximately constant.
- Therefore raising H0 while keeping θ_s and H0 r_s fixed requires reducing the physical sound horizon r_s (this is the standard statement in Kou and Lewis 2024/2025, Cai and Wang 2026, and Poulin et al 2023).
- r_s is the integral of the sound speed through the pre-recombination expansion history. Adding a component that contributes to H(z) before recombination shortens the time available for sound waves to travel, shrinking r_s. This is the mechanism by which an early-universe import shifts the *inferred* H0.
- Fig 6 :14 calls import *"the only operator that moves the wall."* Here the wall is the set of H0 values reachable under ΛCDM's closure; the import expands it.
- **Caveat, named not hidden:** the lever is a mechanism, not a result. The fact that it works algebraically does not show that nature used it. Khalife et al 2024 find that of eleven models, some are ruled out at more than 3 sigma and none is favoured enough to become the next concordance model.
- **Falsifier, written before the run:** if a theorem shows θ_s and H0 r_s together fix H0 independently of the pre-recombination expansion history, the lever is impossible and Q2 fails.

### Q3 - Why the cross-checks bind (the MDL bargain)

- Fig 5 :43: *"A new term is worth having when stating it, plus what remains unexplained after it, costs less than what was unexplained before."* Fig 5 :44: *"and so does a term that explains one occasion perfectly."*
- An import that fixes the CMB H0 slot while breaking BBN light-element abundances, BAO, CMB lensing, the BOSS full-shape power spectrum or the S8 growth constraint has explained one occasion perfectly.
- The failure mode is recorded in the literature: Poulin et al 2023 discuss challenges to early dark energy from weak-lensing S8 and BOSS EFTofLSS; Cai and Wang 2026 state that an early-Universe resolution comes with *"necessitating modifications to both primordial and late Universe altogether"*; Khalife et al 2024 rule out time-varying-electron-mass and Majoron models at more than 3 sigma.
- Fig 6 :24: *"A wrong atom costs more than starting from primitives."* Introducing a new component that fails cross-checks is more expensive than leaving the residual open.
- **Conclusion:** the acceptance test is a bargain across all ground readings, not a fit to one data set. `triangulated`.

### Q5 - Is the relation between the two H0 determinations an isomer relation?

- Fig 12 :117: *"Same atoms, different bond, different substance. The difference is invisible in the formula and decisive in the behaviour."*
- The same observational ingredient, the acoustic scale, is bonded differently in the two routes. In the early route it is bonded to the sound-horizon integral and a model-dependent distance, giving an inferred H0. In the late route the distance is built from calibrated candles and redshifts, giving a more direct H0. Same raw sky, different bond, different inferred number.
- Under Fig 12 this is isomer-like: the difference is carried by the arrangement, not by a new ingredient. That is a `general` structural observation, not a claim about cosmological physics, and it is why one route cannot simply be substituted for the other.
- Fig 12 :94: *"The arrangement is the substance."* A campaign that compares only the two H0 numbers and ignores the bond that produced them has recorded the ingredients and not the term.
- **Falsifier:** if both routes can be written with the same bond over the same atoms and still disagree, the isomer reading fails and the disagreement is a pure measurement error.

## CONCLUSION

"New physics in the early universe" is three operations, not one. Rearranging held atoms is SEARCHED and cannot move the wall; extending an instrument is INWARD; importing a genuinely new pre-recombination component is OUTWARD and is the only one that can make a previously unreachable H0 reachable. The standard lever is reducing r_s, which is a mechanism and not a result. Any import must pass a source gate, a shadow test and a set of independent ground readings, or it fails the description-length bargain. The two H0 determinations are isomer-like in the framework's sense: the difference is in the bond, so they cannot be averaged or substituted. The order is residual, split, search, import, cross-check, ground.
