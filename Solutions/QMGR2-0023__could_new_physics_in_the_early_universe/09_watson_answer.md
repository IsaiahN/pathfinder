## VERDICT (from the figures)

The shape survives; no model name survives. H0 inferred from the CMB is closure-dependent, so an import that changes pre-recombination expansion can shift it by shortening r_s under the fixed acoustic-scale constraint. That is a `general`/`specified` result about reachability. What the figures do not give is a specific new physics: extra relativistic species, early dark energy and modified recombination are candidate atoms, and which one (if any) nature used is a question for instruments.
