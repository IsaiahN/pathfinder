# Expert verification packet: "before inflation" in a theory of changing spacetime

**What this is:** a claim in a form a cosmologist or philosopher of physics can check. Mode, derivation and refuters are stated before the conclusion, so a reader knows what to look for and what would settle it.

**Mode: `specified` on the structural claims, `general` on the shape, not `grounded`.** No measurement at the pre-inflationary regime is claimed. This is a shape and a route, not a result.

**Status:** simulated derivation checked for internal consistency against the figures, not a ground reading. It cannot settle the claim it reports on.

## 1. The claim in one paragraph

"Before" is an ordering bond, not a state or a primitive, and it runs on time. The phrase "before inflation" is therefore a union of three questions that must be kept separate. Operational precedence (a prior field configuration had to obtain for inflation to start) and structural priority (a settled composition must become an atom before inflation composes over it) survive and need no new primitive. Temporal precedence (an epoch earlier than inflation in an ordered time) has no settled referent with the instruments we have, so it is marked as a boundary shape, `BEFORE(???)`, rather than filled with a name. The answer refuses both the reification of the shape and the inference from a failed search that nothing existed. What it offers is the shape any candidate must have, falsifiers written in advance, and the kind of instrument that would close it.

## 2. Three checkable sub-claims

### C1 - framework-internal; checkable by reading Figures 12 and 13

- **Statement:** "Before" is the sequence bond `→`, not a state or a primitive, and the bond presupposes events that happen. Time is the substrate of ordering.
- **Source, verbatim:** Fig 12 :72 *"A then B; B needs A to have happened"*. Fig 13 :12 *"the ordered succession of differences"*. Fig 12 :112 *"A plan is a sequence. A goal is a comparison. Progress is a subtraction."*
- **Refuter:** exhibit an ordering term whose operands need not happen, with a stated ground for that term; or derive precedence as a seventh primitive from the other six terms.

### C2 - the union claim; checkable by inspection of the three senses

- **Statement:** the three senses use different bonds and different substrates, so one refuter cannot clear them. Operational and temporal precedence both use `→` but over different substrates; structural priority uses production `⇒`.
- **Source, verbatim:** Fig 9 :54 *"And when the split lands on a union rather than a partition, unbundle before you search again."* Fig 9 :57 *"a case in two conditions is not a harder case of either"*. For production, the Operators table: *"A produces B; B did not exist before"* and *"different from sequence: the second operand is not waiting, it is absent until the first fires"*. For structural priority, Fig 12 :104 *"A settled molecule becomes an atom for whatever composes over it. So irreducible is a fact about a level and never about the world"*.
- **What to check:** are the three characterizations faithful to physical practice, in particular is the initial-conditions programme a dependency-order question rather than a claim about a temporal epoch?
- **Refuter:** a single settled anchor that orders all three senses; or a case of inflation composing with no prior settled atom at any level.

### C3 - the unreachability verdict; checkable against instruments and world sources

- **Statement:** no instrument at the regime returns a discriminant that orders pre-inflationary configurations with respect to inflation. The temporal sense is therefore `BEFORE(???)`, a shape whose referent is not held. This is unreachability, not a verdict of absence.
- **Source, verbatim:** Fig 6 :34 *"Where nothing is, there is no edge to extend from, and the search for a proto-instrument is a search for one that is already there."* Fig 13 :134 *"Unreachable is a verdict here, not a silence"*. Fig 13 :135 *"It cannot say which primitive is missing, because naming it would mean already holding it."* Fig 8 :39 *"a search that finds nothing is never a proof of absence"*.
- **World corroboration only:** Hawking, Pontifical Academy of Sciences: *"Asking what came before the Big Bang, is meaningless, according to the no boundary proposal, because there is no notion of time available to refer to."* Hawking Estate, 1996: *"Events before the Big Bang, are simply not defined, because there's no way one could measure what happened at them."* Scoles, Scientific American, 2026, quoting Keating: *"If there isn't a discriminant you can measure, you're doing metaphysics with equations."*
- **Refuter:** a real instrument returning a discriminant that orders pre-inflationary configurations with respect to inflation. That would move the claim from `specified` to `grounded` or refute it outright.

## 3. What is explicitly not claimed

- Not that nothing existed before inflation. Absence is not inferred from a failed search.
- Not that the no-boundary proposal, or any other model, is the answer. World sources disagree among themselves.
- Not that "before" names a smooth continuum. Smoothness is a derivative, not a primitive (Fig 13 :125 *"Smoothness, symmetry, chaos and discreteness are each what a particular composition leaves behind"*).
- Not that one falsifier clears all three senses.
- Not grounded. Nothing here is asserted as settled by experiment.
