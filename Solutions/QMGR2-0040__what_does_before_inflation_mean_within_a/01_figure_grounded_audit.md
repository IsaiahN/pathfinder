## 0. The residual in effect terms

The question asks what "before inflation" means when space and time themselves may have changed. Stated as an effect: we hold a term *"before inflation"* that is used as if it named a settled temporal precedence, and we are asked what it can range over when the substrate that makes precedence possible is itself put in doubt.

The term is a **composition that runs on time as a substrate**. In the figures, time is one of the six states and is defined as *"the ordered succession of differences"* (Fig 13 :12). "Before" is precisely an ordering of differences. So the question is not about the word. It is about whether the substrate of the ordering is available at the regime being described.

Two errors are available and must be refused at the start:

- **Reification:** treating "before" as a name of a time or a primitive. Fig 13 :135: *"It cannot say which primitive is missing, because naming it would mean already holding it."*
- **Verdict of absence:** concluding "nothing existed before inflation" from the failure to find an anchor. Fig 8 :39: *"a search that finds nothing is never a proof of absence"*.

What an answer would look like, written down and bound as an ordinary term before the run (formula step "Direction"): a three-way partition of the senses of "before", with one sense marked as a boundary shape rather than a name, and a stated instrument that would move it. That is the sought-for shape, made refusable.

## 1. Domain census

### R by arity, symmetry and scale

- **Arity.** "Before inflation" is a two-place ordering between a putatively earlier condition and the onset of inflation. There is also a meta-term: what makes ordering possible at all. So the residual is binary at the object level and has a substrate condition.
- **Symmetry.** Asymmetric. "Before" is the sequence bond `→`, whose test is *"A then B; B needs A to have happened"* (Fig 12 :72) and *"swap them. Does it become a different thing? Yes"* (Fig 12 :74). It is not a symmetric conjunction or identity.
- **Scale.** The claim is made at the earliest regime, exactly where the state that an ordering presupposes (time) is in question. This is a scale-crossing claim, so a figure that governs ordering at settled scales must be re-censused before it is applied here.

### One line per domain: actors, currency, anchor

**Domain 1, physical cosmology / initial conditions.** Actors: the inflaton or other prior field configurations, the cosmic microwave background, primordial gravitational waves. Currency: physical predictions and observational signatures. Anchor: instruments and data, for example CMB polarisation. "Before" here is dependency precedence among configurations. This is the ordinary, checkable sense, and it is the one that question 38 ("What set the initial conditions for inflation") already uses. No nameable anchor at the pre-inflationary regime means this domain cannot be extended into a temporal claim by its own instruments.

**Domain 2, framework-internal composition.** Actors: the six terms (space, time, energy as states; spectrum, gradient, network as structures), the seven bonds and negation. Currency: composition inside `closure(Γ)`, then settlement against the ground. Anchor: the figures as metalaws, plus the ground for any measurement. Here "before" is the `→` bond, which requires both operands to have happened. Time is a held atom, but the bond applied where time is not settled composes above a break. Fig 3 :20: *"The chain is ordered by dependency, not by importance."* Fig 3 :23: *"It is a reading of nothing."*

**Domain 3, quantum cosmology / emergent time.** Actors: the wave function of the universe, Euclidean path integrals, imaginary time, lapse, saddle geometries. Currency: probabilities for histories. Anchor: comparison of predicted with observed universe, but time itself is not classical here. Hawking's phrase is that there is *"no notion of time available to refer to"*; Lehners writes that *"time itself is not a priori classical, it is not fixed from outside"*. Here "before" has no settled referent. This is an unreachability verdict, not a report that something is absent.

**Domain 4, verification and outreach.** Actors: expert readers. Currency: agreement and residual. Anchor: the ground, never agreement. Fig 2 :45: *"Frames range against the ground. They do not negotiate with it."* In this simulated run all domains draw on one evidence pool, so domain 4's independence is zero and its agreement carries no corroborating weight.

### Partition or union

**Union, not partition.** The same phrase carries several overlapping conditions: operational precedence (domain 1), ordered composition (domain 2), and no available referent (domain 3). Clearing one does not clear the others. Fig 9 :54: *"And when the split lands on a union rather than a partition, unbundle before you search again."* Fig 9 :57: *"a case in two conditions is not a harder case of either"*. The residue after unbundling is a specific compound, not a harder instance of one sense.

### Order by dependency (Fig 3)

1. Describe the residual in effect terms before choosing a frame: Fig 9 :27 *"describe it before you go looking, or any frame you pick will seem to fit"*.
2. Decide whether the substrate of ordering (time) is settled at the regime, before applying the ordering. A step whose input never arrives cannot be diagnosed (Fig 3 :22).
3. Refuse the bad binding before minting a new one (formula step 2).
4. Ask SEARCHED first, then INWARD, then OUTWARD (formula step 7), keeping them distinct.
5. Name the instrument that would move the verdict, and state the mode.

## 3. Findings

### F1 - "Before" is the sequence bond, and the bond presupposes events that happen (decisive for the ordering sense)

Fig 12 :72: *"A then B; B needs A to have happened"*. Fig 12 :112: *"A plan is a sequence. A goal is a comparison. Progress is a subtraction."* Fig 13 :12: *"the ordered succession of differences"*.

"Before inflation" orders two events. The bond requires both operands to have happened. In a theory where time itself may have changed, the operand "pre-inflationary event" has not been settled as something that happens in an ordered succession. The bond is therefore being applied across a boundary where its own substrate is not available.

Fig 3 :22: *"A step whose input never arrives cannot be diagnosed, only its predecessor can."* The honest predecessor question is: is time available as a state at this regime? Until that is answered, the ordering term is "a reading of nothing" (Fig 3 :23).

**Strain test.** Reading "before" as a symmetric relation (coexistence, conjunction) fails the swap test at Fig 12 :74. It is the ordered bond. The strain is not in the bond's identity but in its substrate, which sends us back to the census: this is a union compound (F2), not a single ordering claim. **Refuter:** a framework that admits an ordering term whose operands need not happen, with a stated ground for that term, would break this finding.

### F2 - The phrase is a union compound, not one question

Fig 9 :54: *"And when the split lands on a union rather than a partition, unbundle before you search again."* Fig 9 :57: *"a case in two conditions is not a harder case of either"*.

The three senses are not three spellings of one question:

1. **Operational precedence.** Some field configuration had to obtain for inflation to start. Checkable against data. Survives.
2. **Structural priority.** A settled lower-level composition becomes an atom over which inflation composes. Fig 12 :104: *"A settled molecule becomes an atom for whatever composes over it. So irreducible is a fact about a level and never about the world"*. Survives.
3. **Temporal precedence.** A time at which things happened earlier than inflation. This is the sense the theory puts in doubt. It does not survive as a name.

Clearing sense 1 leaves sense 3 standing, because the second condition still binds (Fig 9 :56). **Refuter:** a single settled anchor that orders all three senses would collapse the union and break this finding.

### F3 - No anchor at the regime, so the temporal sense cannot be settled (but absence is not proved)

Fig 2 :29: *"An anchor is whatever settles the question and does not change its answer because of what any frame thinks."* Fig 6 :34: *"Where nothing is, there is no edge to extend from, and the search for a proto-instrument is a search for one that is already there."*

World evidence, opened 2026-11-23:

- Hawking (Pontifical Academy of Sciences): *"Asking what came before the Big Bang, is meaningless, according to the no boundary proposal, because there is no notion of time available to refer to."*
- Hawking Estate, *The Beginning of Time* (1996): *"Events before the Big Bang, are simply not defined, because there's no way one could measure what happened at them."*
- Scoles, *Scientific American* (2026), quoting cosmologist Brian Keating: *"If there isn't a discriminant you can measure, you're doing metaphysics with equations."*

These corroborate the framework's anchor test: nothing at any resolution is returning a failing reading that could be sharpened into a pre-inflationary clock. But Fig 8 :39 forbids the stronger conclusion: *"a search that finds nothing is never a proof of absence"*. So the verdict is **unreachable at this library and this budget**, not "there was nothing". Fig 13 :134: *"Unreachable is a verdict here, not a silence"*. **Refuter:** a real instrument returning a discriminant that orders pre-inflationary configurations with respect to inflation.

### F4 - Structural priority survives, and needs no new primitive

Fig 12 :104 (above) and Fig 3 :20: *"The chain is ordered by dependency, not by importance."*

In this sense "before" means "must be settled as an atom before the next level composes over it". It is the recursion the figures describe, and it is licensed without importing a seventh term. It maps onto the physical initial-conditions programme: a prior configuration is a precondition for inflation, and the question is what that configuration was, not whether "earlier" names a time. This is the salvageable, checkable core. **Refuter:** inflation composed with no prior settled atom at any level would break this sense.

### F5 - What "before" cannot mean: a name, a primitive, or a verdict of absence

Fig 13 :134: *"Unreachable is a verdict here, not a silence"*. Fig 13 :135: *"It cannot say which primitive is missing, because naming it would mean already holding it."* Fig 9 :32: *"you get a kind of thing, never the answer itself"*.

The correct form is a shape, analogous to the exemplar's `SPACE(???)TIME`: write `BEFORE(???)` for the boundary. It marks that an ordering is owed and that its referent is not held. It does not say what occupies the slot. Smoothness and continuity are themselves derivatives, not primitives: Fig 13 :125 *"Smoothness, symmetry, chaos and discreteness are each what a particular composition leaves behind"*. So "a smooth pre-inflationary time" is a composition to be derived, never a primitive to be named.

**Refuter:** a figure-sanctioned derivation of a seventh term, or an instrument that names the referent, would break the shape restriction.

### F6 - Mode discipline

Per `THE_FORMULA.md` the mode rule is *"general · specified · grounded. State which"*. Nothing here is grounded. The claim that a temporal referent is unavailable is `specified` (falsifiable by an instrument) with a `general` structural shape. Stating it as "there was no time before inflation, therefore nothing existed" would be a verdict delivered with no measurement, the named output-layer failure.

### F7 - Distribution follows the ground, not the audience
