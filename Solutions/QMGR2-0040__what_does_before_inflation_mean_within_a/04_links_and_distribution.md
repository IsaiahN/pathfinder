## What these sources do and do not give us

1. **They corroborate the anchor test, not the verdict.** Hawking and Lehners both say that the ordering term has no classical referent at the origin. That is a world-source restatement of Fig 2 :29 and Fig 6 :34. It is not a figure-derived result and it does not settle the question.
2. **They disagree among themselves.** Quanta and Scientific American document live disputes about the no-boundary proposal and about whether there was anything before the Big Bang. So no world source may be promoted to "the answer". The framework returns a shape, not a name (Fig 9 :32).
3. **The Keating criterion is a genuine instrument test.** *"If there isn't a discriminant you can measure, you're doing metaphysics with equations."* This is exactly the framework's proto-instrument rule: the question is whether anything at any resolution is already returning something that fails to resolve (Fig 6 :34). It supports the `specified` mode, not a `grounded` verdict.

## Distribution: offer, do not push

Figure-grounded reasons:

- **Fig 10 :52** - *"Bring a characterised residual, never the source."* Lead with the three senses of "before" and the refuter. The links come after a verdict is recorded.
- **Fig 10 :42** - *"A verifier that can reconstruct the claim cannot verify it."* A recipient who has absorbed the whole framework before being asked for a verdict has joined the frame being checked.
- **Fig 9 :27** - *"describe it before you go looking, or any frame you pick will seem to fit"*. Ask what the recipient would expect to see if the shape claim were true before giving them the no-boundary literature.
- **Fig 2 :40** - *"agreement about the world is worthless when the agreeing frames share an evidence pool"*. A mass send selected for people already inclined to agree produces a convention, not corroboration.
- **Fig 8 :39** - *"a search that finds nothing is never a proof of absence"*. Do not let the absence of a pre-inflationary anchor become a positive claim that nothing existed.

**Recommended order in a reply:** (1) the three senses and the refuter; (2) ask what the recipient would count as a failure; (3) record the verdict; (4) only then the links, with the note that the figures, not the summary, are the authority.

## The two-stage offer

1. **Stage 1.** Send the residual in effect terms and the pre-registered refuter. Ask what the recipient would count as a failure. Record the verdict before any theory is shown. This is Fig 10 :52 and Fig 2 :45: *"Frames range against the ground. They do not negotiate with it."*
2. **Stage 2, opt-in only.** If the recipient is curious, offer the three source links. Their later agreement is never pooled as corroboration because they have now seen the interior.
3. **Stage 3.** Offer the formula as a method to reuse. Fig 4 :22: *"Only methods cross up. Only head starts come down."* Each user must write their own refuter and state their own mode, because a loop cannot check the direction of its own question.

**Hard precondition:** stages 1 to 3 only work if the stage-1 output survives. The three senses must be unbundled (Fig 9 :54) and the absence verdict must be refused (Fig 8 :39). Repair before sending.
