## The residual in effect

The phrase "before inflation" is an ordered relation (a `→` sequence in the figures) that presupposes time as a settled substrate. The theory in the question puts that substrate in doubt. The task is to say which senses of "before" survive when the ordering's substrate is not available, and to refuse to reify the remainder into a name or a verdict of absence.
