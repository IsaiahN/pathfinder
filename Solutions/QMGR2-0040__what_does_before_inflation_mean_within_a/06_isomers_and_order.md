### Q2 - Which bond is each sense?

1. **Operational precedence → sequence (`→`).** Fig 12 :72: *"A then B; B needs A to have happened"*. A prior field configuration had to obtain before inflation could start. This is the ordinary dependency reading and it is checkable.
2. **Structural priority ⇒ production (`⇒`).** The Operators table: *"A produces B; B did not exist before"* and *"different from sequence: the second operand is not waiting, it is absent until the first fires"*. A settled atom at one level is absent until the lower composition settles; then it is available as an operand. Fig 12 :104: *"A settled molecule becomes an atom for whatever composes over it. So irreducible is a fact about a level and never about the world"*.
3. **Temporal precedence would also be `→`, but with time as the live substrate.** The bond requires both operands to have happened. Where time has not settled, this sense has no composed operands. It is the one that becomes `BEFORE(???)`.

The three senses are therefore **not the same bond**. Sense 1 and sense 3 both use `→` but over different substrates. Sense 2 uses `⇒`. A single refuter cannot clear all three, which is the formal reason for the union reading in F2 of `01`.

### Q3 - Are they isomers?

- Fig 12 :117: *"isomer. Same atoms, different bond, different substance. The difference is invisible in the formula and decisive in the behaviour."*
- Fig 12 :124: *"functional group. A substructure whose presence fixes the outcome. The group says what kind; the qualifier says which member."*
- As the question is normally written, the senses do not even share an atom set: sense 1 is about field configurations, sense 2 about composition levels, sense 3 about a time substrate. They are **not isomers** under Fig 12's definition.
- The stronger version: write all three over the shared six-term set, with the same atoms and only the bond and substrate differing. Then they are a real isomer claim: same atoms, different arrangement, different substance. That is the form worth testing, because it makes the difference checkable rather than verbal.
- **Falsifier, written before the run:** if any sense requires an atom the others do not (a temporal substrate that cannot be written as one of the six, for example), the isomer claim fails and the senses fall back to a functional-group family with different atom sets.

## CONCLUSION

"Before" is a bond, not a state. Its three senses use `→`, `⇒`, and `→` over a doubtful substrate respectively, so they are not one question and one refuter cannot clear them. Over the full shared six-term atom set they form a genuine isomer claim with a stated falsifier; as normally written they are a functional-group family with different atoms. The temporal sense is the one that strains, and the strain sends us back to the census rather than to a softer reading.

This rescues what is defensible: operational and structural precedence survive with no new primitive. The temporal referent stays as `BEFORE(???)`, a shape whose occupant is owed and not held.
