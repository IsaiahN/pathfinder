## QUESTION

Decide, in effect terms, what the Tether says about balancing national policy autonomy against rules for trade, finance and investment. Option A: treat "balance" as one global scalar and split the difference between sovereignty and integration. Option B: unbundle the union into three per-slot loops, range both frames against a ground that does not update, and keep rules as violable conventions. What each leads to: A averages away the live signal, lets a convention decide outcomes, and collapses the two frames into one; B returns the shape any acceptable answer must have, with written falsifiers and an open clause until a ground settles it.

## DOMAIN CENSUS

R by arity, symmetry and scale, then one line per domain with actors / currency / anchor, partition or union, and order by dependency.

- **R (the residual).** For each slot s in {trade, finance, investment}, R_s is the gap between the national frame's predicted outcome under a rule and what the ground returns. Arity: two sides times three slots. Symmetry: "balance" is a threshold comparison, not a symmetric join; ⋛ is defined as *"a threshold relation between two quantities"* (Operators table :51). Scale: one frame nationally, a population of frames internationally, with a lossy membrane between (Fig 4 :27). Fig 9 names it a union compound: a case in two conditions is not a harder case of either.
- **Domain 1, national policy autonomy.** Actors: governments, legislatures, regulators, domestic firms, voters. Currency: policy instruments, welfare, distribution, legitimacy. Anchor: national outcome data and elections, which must not move when an international frame pushes. Real domain, nameable anchor. Stake: an interest in the outcome is unavoidable.
- **Domain 2, international rule frame.** Actors: WTO, IMF, World Bank, OECD, UNCTAD, treaty bodies, dispute panels. Currency: rules, commitments, access, compliance, rulings. Anchor: outcome data (stability, welfare, crisis counts), not the institution's agreement. Hazard: a rule can become a frame that lists itself as ground.
- **Domain 3, verification / observer frame.** Actors: independent statisticians, researchers, auditors, courts, markets. Currency: measurement, residual, provenance. Anchor: the ground, never agreement. In this simulated run all three domains draw on one evidence pool, so independence is low.
- **Partition or union.** The three domains are a union; the three *slots* are the partition used for measurement. Unbundle before searching (Fig 9 :54-55).
- **Order by dependency.** Is there a fact here; describe R per slot before naming a frame; establish the anchor; separate conventions from verdicts; then design rules. The middle link, what counts as progress in this slot, has no instrument yet.

Strain test applied: the barrier reading (three-at-once unreachable) governs the union of conditions; the convention reading governs shared meaning, not outcomes. Different objects, so no strain after re-census. See `01_figure_grounded_audit.md` §7.

## VERDICT (from the figures)

The union survives; the scalar "balance" does not. Three per-slot loops, each with its own ground, replace one global trade-off. Both national and international frames range against outcomes they cannot move; agreement between them is efficient for shared meaning and worthless as a verdict about outcomes. Rules are conventions while they can be violated and checked, and leave that status the moment they decide a national outcome without an external check. The reading must be owned by someone not measured by it. Methods, not recordings, cross the scale membrane. Policy space is instrumentally valuable because low overlap is what gives the union a surplus. Three-at-once is unreachable; which corner to release is decided at the ground and is not named by the framework. This is `general` and `specified`, ungrounded, candidate.

- **cost if wrong:** bounded and locatable. The packet states mode, refuters and lineage before its conclusion, so an error is findable. The real cost is spending the independence of outside checkers by sending a finished policy conclusion instead of a checkable shape. Fig 2 :42 prices that: "Buy independence in proportion to the cost of being wrong." That cost is avoidable by leading with the per-slot residual and its refuter.
- **undoable:** yes. The scalar framing and the ungrounded clause bindings are removable without touching the derivation. `07_the_tether_answer.md` and `08_expert_verification_packet.md` already remove them. No primitive is withdrawn and no institution has to be dismantled.
- **reversed by:** a ground reading, not agreement. Per-slot outcome data that settles a rule's effect would move a finding to `grounded`; a single lever that makes all three residuals trend down with no intersection would break F1 and F8; a rule shown to settle an outcome with no external check yet remain a convention would break F3; a low-overlap source showing all three goals jointly attainable would break F10. Until one of these fires, the verdict stands as `candidate`.
