## 0. The question and its residual, stated in effect terms

**Question.** How should international institutions balance national policy autonomy with rules for trade, finance, and investment?

**What is being decided.** Whether a national frame may act on its own ground, or must act under a rule that another frame holds; and what each option leads to. More autonomy buys local contact and diverse experiments, and can export cost to other frames. More rule buys coordination, and can turn a convention into a verdict that decides outcomes without consulting the ground.

**The residual R.** For each slot s in {trade, finance, investment}, R_s is the gap between what the national frame predicts will happen under a rule and what the ground returns. It is not one number. "The residual is indexed by object slot, not aggregated." (Fig 1 :19) And: "Aggregating across slots is how a live signal disappears into an average." (Fig 1 :22)

**Arity.** Two sides (autonomy, rule) times three object slots. A relation between frames, indexed per slot.

**Symmetry.** Not symmetric. The "balance" is a threshold comparison between two quantities, not a sum and not a symmetric join. Operators table :51: *"a threshold relation between two quantities."* Fig 12 :112: *"A plan is a sequence. A goal is a comparison."*

**Scale.** Two scales with a membrane between them: the national frame (one agent) and the international frame (a population of frames carrying a library of rules). The transform between them is lossy (Fig 4 :27).

**Sought-for shape, bound as an ordinary term.** An answer is acceptable only if it (a) is per slot, (b) names an anchor that does not update, (c) keeps rules violable and checkable, (d) states which seat owns the reading, and (e) returns a shape rather than a specific tariff or clause. This is the "Direction" discipline: write down what an answer would look like so the preference can accrue a residual and be refused.

**Partition or union.** The three domains are a union, not a partition: they overlap. A national rule changes international outcomes and an international rule changes national outcomes. "A bundled problem is usually a union of overlapping conditions, not a list of separate ones." (Fig 9 :55) The three *slots* (trade, finance, investment) are the partition used for measurement; the three *domains* are the union used for explanation. Unbundle before searching: "And when the split lands on a union rather than a partition, unbundle before you search again." (Fig 9 :54)

**Order by dependency (Fig 3, Fig 9).**

1. First, is there a fact here? "Disagreements that do not shrink with effort, where each rule keeps working well on a different subset, are not one hard question but several well-formed ones." (Fig 9 :4)
2. Describe R per slot in effect terms before naming a frame: "describe it before you go looking, or any frame you pick will seem to fit" (Fig 9 :27-28).
3. Establish the anchor that does not update (Fig 2 :29-30).
4. Separate conventions from verdicts (Fig 10 :47), then design rules.
5. "Optimising a link that already works cannot rescue one that does not." (Fig 3 :19) The middle link here is the objective: what counts as progress for each slot. "A link with no instrument is not a link that works." (Fig 3 :27)

**Independence in this run.** All three domains draw on one evidence pool: the same figure extract, the same essays, the same web sources. By Fig 2 :31-32 this is low independence. ## 3. Findings

Each finding is `triangulated` (two figures with low overlap) or `single-source`, with a refuter. Verdicts are kept per domain; none is averaged.

### F1: "Balance" is a per-slot comparison, not a global number (`triangulated`)

Fig 1 :19: "The residual is indexed by object slot, not aggregated." Fig 1 :22: "Aggregating across slots is how a live signal disappears into an average." Operators table :51: *"a threshold relation between two quantities."* Fig 12 :112: "A plan is a sequence. A goal is a comparison."

- **D1 / D2 / D3:** all three. Trade, finance and investment keep separate R_s. One global "openness score" is the averaging failure Fig 1 names.
- **Refuter:** show that a single scalar lever makes all three slot residuals trend down together and that no intersection remains. If that holds, the per-slot requirement is unnecessary and F1 is withdrawn.
- Low overlap: Fig 1 comes from the agent loop; the Operators table comes from bond typology. Different routes to the same constraint.

### F2: Autonomy and rules are frames, not anchors (`triangulated`)

Fig 2 :35: "Alignment is triangulation, not negotiation. A mirror cannot correct you." Fig 2 :45: "Frames range against the ground. They do not negotiate with it." Fig 2 :29-30: *"An anchor is whatever settles the question and does not change its answer because of what any frame thinks."*

- **D1 / D2:** the balance is not found by bargaining ministries against secretariats. Both range against an outcome ground that neither can move. A government that edits its own outcome series to win a dispute has moved the anchor.
- **Refuter:** exhibit a case where the only available settlement is a negotiated compromise that produces no external measurement and still tracks the ground better than any measured alternative. That would make negotiation a legitimate verdict path and F2 would have to be re-censused.

### F3: A rule is a convention until it decides an outcome (`triangulated`)

Fig 2 :41: "efficient for a convention: agreement about a shared meaning is the product, not a corruption of it." Fig 2 :43: "Guard, from Figure 10: a convention that decides an outcome has stopped being one." Fig 10 :47: "a convention that decides an outcome has stopped being one." Fig 10 :49: "Declaring a meaning does not ground it."

- **D2:** trade and investment rules are largely coordination devices, where agreement is the product. They cross the line when a rule fixes a national outcome without being checked against outcomes. The check must be installable: "install what can be violated" (Fig 10 :19).
- **Refuter:** show a rule that determinately settles an outcome, has no external check, and nevertheless remains a convention with no truth value. That breaks Fig 10 :47 and sends the finding back to census.

### F4: The seat has a stake; declare it, and bar it from the reading (`triangulated`)

Fig 10 :59: "an interest in the outcome is unavoidable, an interest in the reading is disqualifying." Fig 10 :60: "Where a stake cannot be removed, declare it." Fig 10 :86: "Whoever is measured by a reading cannot own it." Fig 10 :61: "And standing is a stake that survives the outcome it was earned on."

- **D1 / D3:** a government legitimately wants a good outcome for its citizens; that is not disqualifying. It is disqualifying when it owns the measurement or the interpretation used to judge compliance. Independent statistics and independent panels are the structural expression of this, not a preference for a particular politics.
- **Refuter:** show a frame that owns the reading and is still corrected by the ground on that same reading. Fig 10 :86 says the occupant cannot be both measured and owner; a working counterexample would refute it.

### F5: Carry methods upward, not national recordings; measure the gap (`triangulated`)

Fig 4 :22: "Only methods cross up. Only head starts come down." Fig 4 :25: "A recording carried upward looks like knowledge and is a description of one occasion." Fig 4 :30: "So take the round trip and measure the gap. Without it you are navigating by dead reckoning: confident about a position nobody checked."

- **D1 to D2:** a policy that worked in one country is a recording of one occasion. What can be internationalized is the reusable method for making and checking the rule, plus a head start for the next frame. The round trip gap R_T must be recorded when a method crosses back down.
- **Refuter:** exhibit a national policy whose exact content carries upward to another country with no loss and no re-test. That would set R_T to zero and F5 would fail.

### F6: Valence is relative to the residual; coordination is permanent work (`triangulated`)

Fig 11 :49: "An actor's valence is relative to the residual, not a property it carries." Fig 11 :51: "No standing list of good actors survives a change of residual." Fig 11 :61: "Introduction is available; exclusion is not." Fig 11 :63: "Which is why a managed ecosystem is permanent work rather than a solved state."

- **D1 / D2:** a rule that is a benefactor for financial stability can be a malfactor for investment. There is no permanent list of "good" international rules, only rules checked against a named residual. An institution can add a coordination mechanism; it cannot subtract the national habitat. So the answer is a process, never a settled constitution.
- **Refuter:** produce a standing list of rules that remains beneficial across a change of residual, verified at the ground in both conditions. That would refute the valence claim the figure imposes on itself.

### F7: Independence is what buys the union surplus (`triangulated`)

Fig 8 :45: "Two frames together reach what neither reaches alone." Fig 8 :47: "Independence is one quantity read twice." Fig 8 :49: "Two frames that overlap almost completely are one frame wearing two names." Fig 2 :42: "Buy independence in proportion to the cost of being wrong."

- **D1 / D2:** national autonomy is not sacred, but low overlap between the national and international evidence pools is the condition under which their union produces anything new. Full harmonization collapses them into one frame with two names, and their agreement becomes worthless as a verdict. Cost of being wrong here is high (crises, protectionism), so independence is worth buying.
- **Refuter:** exhibit two frames with near-total evidence overlap whose union nevertheless produces a novel, ground-settled term. Fig 8 :48 makes that impossible by construction, so the refuter would require re-census of "independence" itself.

### F8: Unbundle the bundle before searching (`triangulated`)

Fig 9 :5: "are not one hard question but several well-formed ones. Split it rather than search." Fig 9 :54: "And when the split lands on a union rather than a partition, unbundle before you search again." Fig 9 :55: "A bundled problem is usually a union of overlapping conditions, not a list of separate ones."

- **All domains:** "balance autonomy with rules for trade, finance, investment" is three overlapping conditions. A single lever ("more rules" or "more autonomy") clears where only one condition holds and leaves the intersections standing. This is the same instruction the Library of Babel essay gives in effect terms.
- **Refuter:** show that trade, finance and investment residuals are fully separable, so a single lever solves each independently with no intersection. Then the union reading is wrong and the question is a list, not a compound.

### F9: Efficiency scorecards are filters, not the ground (`triangulated`)

Fig 9 :60: "Use filters for the budget and witnesses for the verdict. Never let a filter hand you a verdict." Fig 1 :27: "They are frame-internal. A frame cannot score itself with a quantity it also produces."

- **D2 / D3:** growth, efficiency, competitiveness and compliance rates are useful for deciding where to spend search effort. They are not the anchor when the institution that produces them is the one being checked. A score a frame produces about itself has no verdict weight.
- **Refuter:** exhibit a self-produced score that is shown to be invariant to the frame that produces it and to track the external ground exactly. Until then the score is a filter.

### F10: Three-at-once is unreachable from one frame's closure (`triangulated`)

Fig 6 :23: "No frame escapes its own closure. The bound moves only on contact with a frame whose closure differs." Fig 13 :115: "The set is closed. The arrangements are not. That gap is why import exists." Fig 8 :32: "Closure is idempotent. Composing inside a closed set never leaves it, so no search, however long, adds an atom."

- **D1 / D2:** a rule set that demands perfect autonomy, perfect integration and perfect domestic control simultaneously cannot be composed from what one frame holds. Something must be given up, and which one is a decision against the ground, not a discovery inside the frame. The world sources (Rodrik's trilemma, the impossible trinity) corroborate this at low overlap but never decide it for the figures.
- **Refuter:** exhibit a composition from one frame's atoms that jointly realizes all three at full strength, with a ground reading to pay for it. Absent that, the barrier stands as `general`, not `grounded`.

### F11: The framework returns a shape, not a clause (`triangulated`)

Fig 9 :32: "you get a kind of thing, never the answer itself." Fig 13 :133: "what it returns is a shape rather than a name." The canonical README says the framework says *"what a missing thing would have to look like (a shape, not a name)."*

- **All domains:** nothing here selects a tariff rate, a capital control, or a treaty clause. Naming one is a binding imported from an outside frame with provenance, not a derivation from the figures.
- **Refuter:** produce a figure line that names a specific policy term as required. No such line was found in a full census of the extract.

### F12: When a rule is false, the owed term is a refusal (`single-source`, figure-internal)

Fig 5 amendment :53: "refuted: a term we hold is wrong; a term is owed that says so." The Operators table :58: "a refutation is a negation, which is why a vocabulary without it can only accumulate."

- **D2:** an international rule whose residual persists and does not trend down is a candidate for refusal, not for more exceptions. A vocabulary with only `+` can only accumulate waivers.
- **Refuter:** show a rule whose residual is flat and non-trending for which adding another exception settles it at the ground. Then the refusal reading is not required.
- Single-source: only the Mint Pipeline amendment and the Operators table carry this, so it is marked single-source and kept separate from the per-domain verdicts.

## 4. Pre-registered refuter (written before the conclusion)

"A falsifier written before the run is a design instrument, not only an honesty device." The shape claim is withdrawn if any of the following holds:

1. A single global balance metric is sufficient: all three slot residuals trend down under one lever, with no intersection left (breaks F1 and F8).
2. An international rule is shown to settle a national outcome with no external ground reading and still to be a convention (breaks F3 and Fig 10 :47).
3. Two frames with high evidence overlap are shown to produce a genuine, ground-settled union surplus (breaks F7).
4. A frame is shown to own the reading and still be corrected by the ground on that same reading (breaks F4).
5. A source, at low overlap with the figures, demonstrates that full national sovereignty, full democratic autonomy and full economic integration are jointly attainable (breaks F10). A conflict sends us back to the census first; it never flips a verdict by itself.

Until one of these fires, nothing here is labelled `grounded`.

## 5. Recommendation (the conclusion)

Per domain, kept separate and never averaged:

- **Trade (D1/D2):** keep the residual per slot. Treat rules as violable conventions with an independent checker; "install what can be violated" (Fig 10 :19). Do not let a self-produced efficiency score decide the verdict (F9).
- **Finance (D1/D2):** the barrier is the hardest finding (F10). A frame cannot hold free capital mobility, a fixed rate and full autonomous monetary policy at once. State explicitly which one is given up and check it against the ground (crisis and stability data), not against negotiation. The figures do not name which one; that is a binding with provenance.
- **Investment (D1/D2):** valence is relative to the residual (F6). Preserve policy space where the local residual is live, because low overlap is what makes the union productive (F7). Coordination is permanent work, not a solved state.
- **All three:** unbundle before searching (F8); carry methods, not recordings, across the scale membrane (F5); declare the stake and keep the reading out of the measured frame (F4).

The honest form of the answer: **the shape any acceptable answer must have**, not the answer itself. "Unreachable is a verdict, not a silence" applies to the specific policy clause; the shape is reachable.
