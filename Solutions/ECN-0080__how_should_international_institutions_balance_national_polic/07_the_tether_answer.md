# The Tether's answer on autonomy versus rules

**Mode: `general` on the shape, `specified` on the per-domain findings, nothing `grounded`.** Simulated derivation checked for internal consistency against the figures (`05_what_shape_means.md` §7), not a ground reading. No measurement was taken.

## 1. The residual it answers

For each slot s in {trade, finance, investment}, R_s is the gap between what the national frame predicts under a rule and what the ground returns. "The residual is indexed by object slot, not aggregated." (Fig 1 :19) The question as posed is a **union compound**: "A bundled problem is usually a union of overlapping conditions, not a list of separate ones." (Fig 9 :55) It has its own loop and its own ground, so verdicts stay per domain and are never averaged.

## 2. The shape

1. **Do not compute one balance.** "Aggregating across slots is how a live signal disappears into an average." (Fig 1 :22) Trade, finance and investment keep three residuals, three anchors and three checks. "Balance" is a threshold comparison, not a sum: Operators table :51, *"a threshold relation between two quantities."*

2. **Both sides range against a ground; they do not negotiate.** Fig 2 :45: "Frames range against the ground. They do not negotiate with it." Fig 2 :35: "Alignment is triangulation, not negotiation. A mirror cannot correct you." A national government and an international secretariat are two frames. Neither is the anchor. An anchor "does not change its answer because of what any frame thinks" (Fig 2 :29-30).

3. **Rules are conventions until they decide outcomes.** Fig 10 :47: "a convention that decides an outcome has stopped being one." Fig 2 :41: "efficient for a convention: agreement about a shared meaning is the product, not a corruption of it." So rule agreement is not corruption, as long as the rule remains a coordination device that can be violated and checked: "install what can be violated" (Fig 10 :19). The moment a rule fixes a national outcome with no external check, "A ground listed as one of the parties has already become a frame." (Fig 2 :47)

4. **Declare the stake, bar the reading.** Fig 10 :59: "an interest in the outcome is unavoidable, an interest in the reading is disqualifying." Fig 10 :86: "Whoever is measured by a reading cannot own it." A government may want a good outcome; it may not own the statistic used to judge compliance. Independent measurement and independent review are structural requirements, not a preference.

5. **Carry methods, not national recordings, across scales.** Fig 4 :22: "Only methods cross up. Only head starts come down." Fig 4 :25: "A recording carried upward looks like knowledge and is a description of one occasion." A policy that worked in one country is an occasion. What can be internationalized is the method for making and checking the rule, with the round-trip gap measured: Fig 4 :30, "So take the round trip and measure the gap. Without it you are navigating by dead reckoning: confident about a position nobody checked."

6. **No standing list of good rules.** Fig 11 :49: "An actor's valence is relative to the residual, not a property it carries." Fig 11 :63: "Which is why a managed ecosystem is permanent work rather than a solved state." A rule good for stability can be bad for investment. Coordination is work, not a finished constitution. "Introduction is available; exclusion is not." (Fig 11 :61)

7. **Independence is the product, not the enemy.** Fig 8 :47: "Independence is one quantity read twice." Fig 8 :49: "Two frames that overlap almost completely are one frame wearing two names." Preserving national policy space is instrumentally valuable because low overlap is what makes the union produce anything new. "Buy independence in proportion to the cost of being wrong." (Fig 2 :42) Full harmonization buys agreement and loses the surplus.

8. **Three at once is unreachable from one frame.** Fig 6 :23: "No frame escapes its own closure. The bound moves only on contact with a frame whose closure differs." Fig 13 :115: "The set is closed. The arrangements are not. That gap is why import exists." A rule set demanding perfect autonomy, perfect integration and perfect domestic control cannot be composed from what one frame holds. Which corner to release is decided against the ground, not derived from the figures. That is a genuine absence: "you get a kind of thing, never the answer itself" (Fig 9 :32).

## 4. What it explicitly does not say

- Not a policy prescription. No tariff rate, capital control, treaty clause or institutional design is named by the figures. Naming one is a binding from an outside frame with provenance.
- Not that more rules are always better, nor that autonomy is always better. Both are frames; the ground decides.
- Not that the existing institutions are illegitimate. A convention about shared meaning is efficient (Fig 2 :41); the objection starts only when it decides an outcome without a check.
- Not that agreement among independent economists settles anything. Agreement is a convention or a shared pool, not an anchor (Fig 2 :40).
- Not `grounded`. No outcome series, dispute record or crisis count was read.

## 5. What would settle it: the ground

- A source-discriminating instrument per slot: *The Hard Problem is About Source* says an instrument that reads green *"cannot comment on the source"*, and the spectrometer, not an argument, resolved it. Here: which reading distinguishes a rule that coordinates from a rule that decides the outcome?
- A live instrument for the middle link of Fig 3: what counts as progress in this slot, measured rather than assumed.
- A ground reading of the release decision in finance: a stability or crisis outcome that pays for one corner of the trilemma.

## 6. Falsifiers, written before the conclusion

1. One lever makes all three slot residuals trend down together with no intersection (breaks F1 and F8).
2. A rule settles a national outcome with no external check and remains a convention (breaks F3).
3. High-overlap frames produce a genuine ground-settled surplus (breaks F7).
4. A frame owns the reading and is still corrected by the ground on that reading (breaks F4).
5. A low-overlap source shows full sovereignty, full autonomy and full integration jointly attainable (breaks F10; returns to census, never flips a verdict directly).

## 7. The honest stop

**Unreached, not unreachable.** The shape is reachable from the figures. The specific clause is not, and the framework says so: Fig 13 :133, "what it returns is a shape rather than a name." The stop is recorded with its lineage so a reader can locate the error. *Arrival of the Fittest*: *"Because it is a metatheory, it cannot be validated, nor can it validate itself."*

## 8. One-paragraph version for outreach

> The Tether does not say how open an economy should be. It says that "balance" is the wrong shape: there are three separate residuals, one for trade, one for finance, one for investment, and averaging them into one openness score throws away the only signal that says where to look. National governments and international institutions are two frames, not anchors; both must range against outcomes that neither can move. Rules are useful conventions while they remain coordination devices that can be violated and checked; the moment a rule decides a national outcome without an external check it has stopped being a convention and become a frame pretending to be the ground. The reading must be owned by someone who is not measured by it, and what crosses between national and international scales should be a reusable method, not a recording of one country's success. Preserving policy space is not a sentiment: low overlap between the two frames is what makes their union produce anything new, and full harmonization buys agreement while losing the surplus. Three goals at once, perfect autonomy, perfect integration and full domestic control, cannot be composed; which one to release is a decision against the ground, not a result the framework can name. What is offered is that shape, the falsifiers written in advance, and the instruments that would close it. The specific clause stays open until the ground settles it.
