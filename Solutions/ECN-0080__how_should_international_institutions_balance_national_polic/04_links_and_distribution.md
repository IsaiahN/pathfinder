## Two things in the sources that bear directly on this question

1. **The WTO dispute record is the convention check.** "A dispute arises when a member government believes another member government is violating an agreement or a commitment that it has made in the WTO." That is what "install what can be violated" (Fig 10 :19) looks like in an institution. It is not proof that any particular rule is correct; it is a checkable channel. Cite it when explaining why the claim is about the rule's status, not about a preferred policy.
2. **The trilemmas are analogues, not authorities.** Rodrik and the monetary trilemma predict a barrier that the figures also predict (Fig 6 :23). They are low-overlap witnesses, so they corroborate; they do not decide. If they conflicted with a figure line, the census would be reopened.

## Distribution: offer, do not push

Figure-grounded reasons:

- **Fig 4 :22** "Only methods cross up. Only head starts come down." Sending the full architecture before anyone has described a per-slot residual delivers a recording, not a generator. Lead with the shape claim and the refuter; offer links after.
- **Fig 10 :42** "A verifier that can reconstruct the claim cannot verify it." A recipient who has absorbed the whole framework before being asked for a verdict has joined the frame being checked.
- **Fig 9 :27-28** "describe it before you go looking, or any frame you pick will seem to fit." Ask what they would expect to see if the per-slot claim were true before giving them the article.
- **Fig 2 :40** "disqualifying for a verdict: agreement about the world is worthless when the agreeing frames share an evidence pool". A send selected for people already likely to agree produces a convention, not corroboration.
- **Fig 2 :42** "Buy independence in proportion to the cost of being wrong." The cost here is high, so independent, ground-visible venues are worth the extra effort.

**Recommended order in a reply:** (1) the residual and the shape claim; (2) the three refuters, and the question "what would you count as a failure?"; (3) record their verdict; (4) only then the links, with the note that the figure text, not the summary, is the authority. Later agreement is never pooled as corroboration.

## The two-stage offer

1. **Stage 1:** validate the per-slot output against the ground, without showing the theory. Fig 10 :52, "Bring a characterised residual, never the source."
2. **Stage 2, opt-in only:** if curious, read the theory that produced it. The recipient's stage-1 verdict must be recorded first; their later agreement carries no verdict weight.
3. **Stage 3:** offer the formula as a method to reuse. Fig 4 :22 permits sending a generator. Each user writes their own ground, mode and refuter before running it, because `THE_FORMULA.md` "Direction" says a frame cannot check the direction of its own question.

**Hard precondition:** stages 1 to 3 only work if the stage-1 output survives. The refuters in `08_expert_verification_packet.md` are the test. If one fires, stop and retract before any theory is offered.
