## 1. A shape is a constraint that can be checked before it can be named

From *The Library of Babel*: *"you can cut your search short if the set of books returned is small and descriptive enough that it provides a general shape of the answer that satisfies your question."*

So a **shape** is the narrowed, checkable form of an answer you can state before you know the specific term. For ECN-0080 the shape is not "a tariff of 10 percent" or "a capital control." It is:

- three separate residuals, one per slot (Fig 1 :19);
- an anchor that does not move when a frame pushes on it (Fig 2 :29-30);
- rules that are violable conventions until they decide an outcome (Fig 10 :47);
- the reading owned by someone who is not measured by it (Fig 10 :86);
- methods, not recordings, crossing the national/international membrane (Fig 4 :22).

That is a shape: it rules out most answers without filling in the one we want.

## 2. How the shape is obtained

- *Library of Babel*, on vocabulary: use the terms you already have that are closest to the thing, then recurse on recurring terms that overlap with it. *"With each new term gained, you narrow the space by definition and deepen your vocabulary."*
- *Library of Babel*, on bundles: *"Perhaps your problem was a bundle or conflation of several problems masquerading as one?"* That is exactly the "balance autonomy with rules for trade, finance, investment" question. Fig 9 :55 gives the figure form: "A bundled problem is usually a union of overlapping conditions, not a list of separate ones."
- *Decoding Tacit Knowledge*: *"When contact isn't possible, the best you can send is a plain description of what you'd expect to see, because anyone can check that against the world."* Fig 6 :30: "The description travels. Fails by being sharpened into terms only we can read. Cast it in effect terms."
- *How Intelligence Actually Works*: *"indiscernibility is not identity"* and *"a seed is not the recording of a tree, it is instead the encoding of something that will grow one."* A shape is a seed, not a recording of the answer.

## 3. What licenses a name

Two things, both outside the shape itself:

1. **The ground settles it.** Formula step 5: *"the ground settles it. Verified only by what pays. A gate is not the ground."* In this question that means an outcome measurement for the slot, not a committee vote.
2. **A provenanced import from a frame whose closure differs** (Fig 6 :23, Fig 8 :50-52), with a source gate and a shadow test. Naming a specific clause from an economics journal is an import; it must be marked as such.

*They're Scanning Books* sharpens this: *"Declaring the primitives does not make them neutral. It makes the choice visible."* And: *"The real import is not a new term in the existing space. It is a new dimension of the space."* So a claimed "one-size-fits-all balance rule" would not be a term inside the frame; it would be an imported axis, and its provenance has to be shown.

## 4. Why "one balance number" is the specific forbidden error here

*The Hard Problem is About Source* gives the mechanism. An instrument reads green and *"cannot comment on the source"*. Several sources give the same reading. *"If several paths create the same behavior, then behavior is a projection with lossy information."* The reading does not determine the source; the spectrometer, not an argument, resolved it.

In ECN-0080, "we are 60 percent open" is a green reading. National autonomy and external rules can produce the same reading from many different sources: a tariff cut, a capital control, a treaty clause, a domestic subsidy. Naming one as the source of the reading is the metamerism error. What is owed is a better per-slot instrument, not a single number.

*How Intelligence Actually Works* adds the provenance version: a video frame and a photograph can be indistinguishable as artifacts while *"nothing in the image records which"*. Two institutional designs can show the same aggregate score with different lineages. The record is the term.

## 5. Q: answered: what licenses a particular policy clause?

**Nothing in the figures.** The framework returns a shape. A particular clause is licensed only by the ground settling a minted term for that slot, or by a provenanced import with a shadow test. Until one of those, the clause is the searcher's own contribution and must be recorded as such. This matches F11 in `01_figure_grounded_audit.md`.

## 5b. Correction to my own §3 (kept visible)

I first wrote that "shape, not a name" forbids naming *any* concrete term in this answer. That was too broad. The canonical phrase is about a **missing** primitive, one not held in the library. Here the relevant vocabulary (autonomy, rule, anchor, slot, comparison) is already available; what is missing is the **ground reading** for each slot, not a primitive. The restriction is therefore: do not name the clause as if the framework derived it, and do not invent a seventh category. Marking a clause as a binding from an outside frame with provenance is allowed. Both the original overstatement and this narrowing are kept so neither is silently erased.

## 6. Prestige is not action

From *The Recursive Self-Improvement Trap*: *"Prestige must never be allowed to validate an action directly... Prestige can fund an action. It can give an action a budget to be tried. But it cannot settle an action. Only the ground can settle it. The Ground Maintainer must be blind to prestige."*

For this question: the authority of a large institution gives its rule a budget to be tried. It does not make the rule correct. A campaign that gathers famous names to agree with the rule is the named collapse, not corroboration. The legitimate ask is the opposite: give the rule a trial and let an unmovable ground settle it.
