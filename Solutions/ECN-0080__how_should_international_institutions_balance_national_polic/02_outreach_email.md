# Outreach draft: check the output before you decide about the framing

**Use:** one-to-one, or as a cover note attached to a submission through the public venues in `03_public_contact_points.csv`. **Not** for bulk send.

**Hard precondition:** do not send until the three checkable sub-claims in `08_expert_verification_packet.md` survive the recipient's own check. The offer collapses on contact if the stage-1 output does not hold.

**Subject:** One checkable output on trade, finance and investment rules

> Hi [name],
>
> I have one specific output I would like you to check against what you already know. I am not
> asking you to read the framework first, and I am not asking you to agree with it. If the
> output does not survive, there is nothing else to look at.
>
> **The output (`general` on the shape, `specified` on the per-domain findings, not `grounded`):**
> the question "how do we balance national autonomy against rules for trade, finance and
> investment" has the wrong shape for one number. The framework indexes its residual by object
> slot, not by aggregate. So there are three loops, one per slot, each with its own ground.
> Averaging them into a single openness score discards the signal that says where to look.
>
> Two frames, a national government and an international institution, are not anchors. Both
> have to range against outcomes that neither can move. Rules are useful as conventions while
> they remain coordination devices that can be violated and checked. The moment a rule decides
> a national outcome without an external check, it has stopped being a convention and has
> become a frame pretending to be the ground. And whoever owns the compliance reading must not
> be measured by it.
>
> **What I am not claiming:** that any particular tariff, capital control or treaty clause is
> the answer. The framework returns a shape, not a clause. Naming a clause would be an import
> from an outside frame, and until its provenance is shown it carries no weight. I am also not
> claiming that more rules or more autonomy is always better, or that existing institutions
> are illegitimate.
>
> **What would refute the output:**
> 1. one lever makes all three slot residuals trend down together with no intersection term;
> 2. a rule settles a national outcome with no external check and still works as a convention;
> 3. two frames with near-total evidence overlap produce a genuinely new, ground-settled result.
>
> I wrote these down before sending this. If any holds, I withdraw the claim. A one-line
> "this fails because..." is more useful to me than agreement. If you would rather not engage,
> no reply is needed.
>
> **If it survives, and only then:** here are the sources. The figure text, not my summary,

## Why this version is ordered this way

| the move | figure-grounded reason |
|---|---|
| Output first, framework second | Fig 10 :52: "Bring a characterised residual, never the source." A recipient checks the output without holding the framework's interior. |
| Do not ask for agreement | Fig 2 :35: "Alignment is triangulation, not negotiation. A mirror cannot correct you." |
| Pre-registered refuter | A falsifier written before the run is a design instrument. It lets the recipient refuse the claim on their own terms. |
| Mode stated on every substantive part | `THE_FORMULA.md`: "Mode. general · specified · grounded. State which." |
| No policy clause asserted | Fig 9 :32: "you get a kind of thing, never the answer itself." A clause without provenance is the author's own contribution. |
| No request for trust or prestige | *The Recursive Self-Improvement Trap*: prestige can fund a trial but "cannot settle an action. Only the ground can settle it." |
| Links last and opt-in | Fig 4 :22: "Only methods cross up. Only head starts come down." Sending the architecture first delivers a recording, not a generator. |
| Offer the formula as a method | `THE_FORMULA.md` "Direction": a frame cannot check the direction of its own question, so each user writes their own refuter. |
| One-to-one only, no bulk | Fig 2 :40: "disqualifying for a verdict: agreement about the world is worthless when the agreeing frames share an evidence pool". A receptiveness-ranked list manufactures that pool. |

## Stage order

1. **Stage 1:** send the residual, the shape claim and the three refuters. Record the recipient's verdict before showing any theory.
2. **Stage 2, opt-in only:** if they are curious, offer the three links. The figures, not the summary, are the authority.
3. **Stage 3:** offer the formula as a method to reuse, with the instruction that each user states their own ground, mode and refuter.

A later agreement is never pooled as corroboration. The figure reason is Fig 2 :40, quoted above.
