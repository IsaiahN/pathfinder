## 1. A shape is a constraint, not a filler

The canonical README states the limit: the framework returns "what a missing thing would have to look like (a shape, not a name)". Fig 9 :32 "you get a kind of thing, never the answer itself." Fig 13: "It can say the answer is out of reach with the library as it stands" and "It cannot say which primitive is missing, because naming it would mean already holding it."

From *The Library of Babel*: "you can cut your search short if the set of books returned is small and descriptive enough that it provides a general shape of the answer that satisfies your question."

So a **shape** is the narrowed form of an answer you can state before you can name it. It constrains; it does not fill. For this question the shape is: four slots, habitat not anchor, head start not recording, closure plus import, different bonds per slot. The forbidden filler is a sentence of the form "market economics causes belief X". That is a name where the framework returns a shape.

## 2. How the shape is obtained

- *Library of Babel*: use the vocabulary you already have that is closest to the thing, look for recurring new terms that overlap or correlate with it, recurse. "With each new term gained, you narrow the space and deepen your vocabulary. This is a recursive process." The search must be ranged against the question: "you can ask: Are we getting warmer, or are we getting colder?"
- Fig 6 :30 "The description travels. Fails by being sharpened into terms only we can read. Cast it in effect terms." Fig 9 :30-31 "your words are borrowed too, so describe the effect rather than the cause." The four slots are described in effect terms (what I owe, what I may call mine), never in a discipline's private vocabulary first.
- *Decoding Tacit Knowledge*: "When contact isn't possible, the best you can send is a plain description of what you'd expect to see, because anyone can check that against the world." This is the reason the outreach in `02` sends a description of what would count as a failure, not a theory.
- *Library of Babel* on stopping: "the finish line will also be the starting place" and the searcher "leave[s] as the author". Also the warning that the question may be "a bundle or conflation of several problems masquerading as one". COG-0086 is exactly such a bundle: four questions wearing one pronoun set.

## 3. What licenses a name

Only two things, both outside the shape itself.

1. **The ground settles it.** Formula step 5: "the ground settles it. Verified only by what pays." The README says the framework "stops at the limit of what the available instruments can distinguish, and records the lineage so the stop is honest." For this question the instrument is missing, so no name is licensed.
2. **A provenanced import from a frame whose closure differs** (Fig 6 :23, Fig 8 :50-52), with a source gate and a shadow test. An imported atom such as "reciprocal dependence" from economic anthropology must be recorded as coming from that frame.

*They're Scanning Books When They Should Be Studying Nature* sharpens this: "The real import is not a new term in the existing space. It is a new dimension of the space." And: "A minimum viable set of six terms is a human's careful guess at reality's compression axes; another sensorium's minimum set need not be those six." Also: "Declaring the primitives does not make them neutral. It makes the choice visible." The six-term substrate is therefore a visible guess, not nature's own list; it cannot be used to smuggle in a preferred economic ideology.

## 4. Why the economy cannot be named as the cause here

*The Hard Problem is About Source* gives the mechanism. An instrument reads green but "cannot comment on the source". Several paths can create the same reading: "If several paths create the same behavior, then behavior is a projection with lossy information." The reading does not determine the source. "An instrument that can't distinguish these cases demonstrates its own inadequacy, it cannot claim anything substantial about what is being measured or what causes it." It was the spectrometer, not an argument, that resolved it. "The answer is unreached until a better instrument exists."

Applying this: an observed correlation between a regime and an idea about success is a reading. It does not disclose the source. Several mechanisms (head start, closure, import, reward contingency, habitat contact) could produce the same reading. Naming the regime as the cause is the metamerism error. What is owed is a discriminating instrument, not a name. This is why F10 in `01_figure_grounded_audit.md` reports a `gap` at the specific-causal level rather than a forced answer.

## 5. How intelligence is being used in this derivation

*How Intelligence Actually Works*: "the intelligence we experience is the emergent result of extensive mapping of a complex system against unknown and unforgiving environments" and "The mapping is not a means to intelligence; the mapping is the intelligence." Also: "It can be inherited or taught, so the recipient need not pay the bill themselves, but something, somewhere, already paid it." This is the precise sense in which ideas about ownership are inherited: the bill was paid by a lineage in contact, and the child gets a head start rather than a replay. The same essay says "the answer doesn't exist until the question is contacted" and "Contact is Distinction Resolver". Fig 11 :39 "capability is a property of agent-and-habitat, never of the agent alone" is the same claim at the cultural scale.

## 6. Two consequences

**Prestige is not action.** From *The Recursive Self-Improvement Trap*: "Prestige can fund an action. It can give an action a budget to be tried. But it cannot settle an action. Only the ground can settle it. The Ground Maintainer must be blind to prestige." A roster of famous economists agreeing with the shape claim is the named collapse. The legitimate ask is a budget to try the refuter; the ground, not agreement, settles it. The same essay: "You may all agree and be wrong."

**This audit is a simulation, not a ground reading.** Same essay: "the output is only as good as the validator. The agent, or the tool, or the frame, or the theory, cannot validate itself." And: "the truly testable output would need to be derived from the real architecture or working the formula yourself." So `01`, `06`, `09` and `11` are simulated derivations checked for internal consistency against the figures. They are **not** tests of the claim and must be labelled as such. They cannot settle anything they report on.

## 7. Correction to a tempting overstatement

It would be easy to read "shape, not a name" as a ban on using the words "market", "gift" or "obligation" at all. That is wrong. The rule governs a **missing** primitive: something not held in Γ. The habitat actors and the six substrate terms are already held, and the essays use domain vocabulary throughout. What is forbidden is presenting a specific regime-to-slot mapping as a figure result. This solution uses the vocabulary of economic anthropology as provenanced material and names no regime as the cause. Both statements are recorded so neither is quietly lost.
