## 1. The residual, stated in effect terms

Observed regularity: people's answers to four questions vary with the provisioning and exchange regime they live under. (1) How much choice do I have? (2) What do I owe others? (3) What may I call mine? (4) What counts as personal success?

R is the gap between the material arrangement and those four answers. Its description:

- **Arity:** four slots. Fig 1 :19 "The residual is indexed by object slot, not aggregated." Fig 1 :22 "Aggregating across slots is how a live signal disappears into an average." An "economic determinism" scalar that predicts all four with no per-slot residual is exactly the error this forbids.
- **Symmetry:** the four are not interchangeable. Choice and success are comparisons; ownership and obligation are bonds over resources and persons. The claimed direction (arrangement to ideas) is asymmetric until tested, and the test is given in section 6.
- **Scale:** two scales. One person forming an idea, and a population with institutions and a shared library. Fig 4 :19 "Only the actors, the currency and the anchor change." A membrane lies between them.

**Question type (Fig 9).** The four slots are a **union of overlapping conditions**, not a partition. Fig 9 :54-58: "when the split lands on a union rather than a partition, unbundle before you search again." Ownership and obligation overlap (a property claim entails duties); choice and success overlap (merit ideology joins them). Fig 9 :57: "a case in two conditions is not a harder case of either." So one loop per slot, with its own ground reading, not one averaged "mentality".

## 4. Findings, one loop per domain

### F1: R is four slots, and the union must be unbundled (general, structural)

Fig 1 :19 "The residual is indexed by object slot, not aggregated." Fig 9 :54 "when the split lands on a union rather than a partition, unbundle before you search again."

A single claim of the form "the economy determines ideas" cannot be checked. It averages four different residuals. Choice, obligation, ownership and success overlap in pairs, so a lever that clears the choice slot leaves the obligation intersection standing. Fig 9 :57: "a case in two conditions is not a harder case of either." The verdict is per slot, kept separate and never averaged. Triangulated across Fig 1 and Fig 9 (low overlap: slot indexing versus union splitting).

**Refuter:** a single scalar measure of "economic system" predicts all four idea slots with no per-slot residual and no loss of fit. If it does, F1 is false.

### F2: The economic system is habitat and frame; material contact is the anchor (specified; strain resolved by re-census)

The question's phrasing invites "the economy is the ground that settles ideas." That strains against Fig 2 :33 "The anchor must not update." If an economic ideology is the anchor, then a change in ideas about ownership would move the anchor, which is panel 1 of Fig 2 (coherent drift), not triangulation.

**Re-census.** The anchor is not "the economy" as a belief system. It is material contact: what actually sustains the group and clears a market. Fig 2 :29-30 "An anchor is whatever settles the question and does not change its answer because of what any frame thinks." The same figure permits "a market" as an anchor *as a measurement*, not as an ideology. The economic system is therefore (a) the habitat, enumerated (Fig 11 :43 "The habitat is enumerated, never composed."), and (b) a frame whose closure limits what can be thought. Ideas are frames that range against the material ground: Fig 2 :45 "Frames range against the ground. They do not negotiate with it." After re-census there is no strain. The direction in the question means habitat-to-frame contact, not validation by agreement.

World corroboration (secondary, not decisive): cultural materialism's "the infrastructure is in almost all circumstances the most significant force behind the evolution of a culture," and the economic-anthropology finding that "the concept of a 'pure gift' given altruistically only emerges in societies with a well-developed market ideology." Both predict that a change in provisioning registers as a change in an idea. Neither is allowed to decide (Fig 9 :60).

**Refuter:** show an idea about ownership or success that changes material survival while no contact, resource flow or incentive changes. That would make the frame move the anchor and break the re-census.

### F3: Capability is agent-and-habitat; isolation fails (specified)

Fig 11 :39 "capability is a property of agent-and-habitat, never of the agent alone." Fig 11 :40 "An improvement that does not change contact changes nothing, however much it improves." Fig 11 :61 "Introduction is available; exclusion is not."

Ideas about choice, obligation, ownership and success are not properties of an isolated person. They are what a person-in-a-provisioning-regime can form. An intervention that coaches an individual to think differently, then returns them to the same habitat, has not changed contact and has not changed capability. What a seat can do is introduce actors or instruments (new institutions, new measures); it cannot subtract the habitat. Fig 11 :36 "Where nothing maintains anything, intelligence is a label applied afterwards to a lineage that met its ground." The lineage is the thing to describe, not a trait to locate in the head.

**Refuter:** a durable change in all four idea slots produced in a person with no change in habitat, contact or reward structure, that survives return to the original regime.

### F4: What crosses the scale membrane is a head start, not a recording (general plus specified)

Fig 4 :19 "Only the actors, the currency and the anchor change." Fig 4 :22 "Only methods cross up. Only head starts come down." Fig 4 :25 "A recording carried upward looks like knowledge and is a description of one occasion."

Enculturation does not hand a child the answers of the previous generation. It makes the next search cheaper: what to attend to, which payoffs count, which claims are already settled. This explains both continuity within a regime and variation inside it, since a head start is not a replay. World corroboration from a low-overlap field: the overjustification literature's finding that "providing expected external incentive, such as money or prizes, for an already intrinsically rewarding activity can reduce a person's intrinsic motivation to perform that activity." A reward contingency changes the perceived locus of causality, which is a change in what the search is for, not a copy of an answer. The Cultural Evolution Society's "Culture therefore encompasses all of the knowledge, ideas, attitudes, opinions, languages, norms, institutions, music, art and technology that people learn from other people" names the transmission channel without claiming replication fidelity.

**Refuter:** show that children reproduce parents' exact ownership and obligation judgments as a recorded copy, with no change in the search space, and that this replay, not a cheapened search, is the mechanism.

### F5: Closure and import (general plus specified)

Fig 6 :23 "No frame escapes its own closure. The bound moves only on contact with a frame whose closure differs." Fig 13 :115 "The set is closed. The arrangements are not. That gap is why import exists." Fig 13 :118 "infinite effort inside a finite reach."

A culture's thinkable ideas lie inside the closure of its available terms. Longer search inside one regime visits more of the same space and adds no new atom. Contact with a different economic regime (trade, migration, conquest, media) is the only operator that moves the wall (Fig 6 :14 "the only operator that moves the wall"). Such an import is gated: provenance and shadow test (Fig 8), and priced: Fig 6 :24 "A wrong atom costs more than starting from primitives." Two regimes can reach the same idea by convergence; Fig 8 :50 "Two frames can hold the same atom by convergence, reaching it separately, which is corroboration at maximum independence", and only the record separates convergence from adopted import. So a cross-cultural similarity is not automatically an import or automatically corroboration.

**Refuter:** a community generates a genuinely new property or ownership concept with no prior contact and no held atom from which it composes; or an import changes the reach with zero provenance, zero shadow test and zero cost.

### F6: Valence is relative to the slot; no standing good or bad regime (general)

Fig 11 :49 "An actor's valence is relative to the residual, not a property it carries." Fig 11 :51 "No standing list of good actors survives a change of residual."

Market integration can benefit the choice slot (more apparent options, a comparison over a wider spectrum) and damage the obligation slot (the moral-economy record: "public outrage against what rioters perceived to be the immorality of the new economic system"). The same arrangement is benefactor to one residual and malfactor to another. Therefore no answer of the form "regime X produces better ideas" is figure-licensed. World corroboration: the gift/commodity contrast in economic anthropology, where "Commodity exchange is an exchange of alienable objects between people who are in a state of reciprocal independence" and gift exchange is "an exchange of inalienable objects between people who are in a state of reciprocal dependence". Dependence and independence value differently by slot.

**Refuter:** an arrangement that improves all four slots simultaneously with no trade-off, stable across changes of residual.

### F7: The four slots use different bonds over the same substrate (general, structural)

Fig 13 :74 "Everything else is a composition across the two kinds, never within one." Fig 12 :112 "A plan is a sequence. A goal is a comparison. Progress is a subtraction. A refutation is a negation". Operators table :51 "a goal is a comparison, which is why a vocabulary without it cannot state one." Fig 12 :117 "Same atoms, different bond, different substance. The difference is invisible in the formula and decisive in the behaviour."

Personal success is a **comparison** (⋛): a threshold relation over a spectrum of rank or income. Obligation is a **sequence** or **production** bond (→ or ⇒): what I owe follows from what was given, and the return did not exist before the gift. Ownership is a bond between a state (a resource in space or time) and a structure (a network of recognised claims), and its spelling changes the substance: an inalienable possession and an alienable commodity share atoms and differ in the bond. Choice is a **disjunction** or **comparison** over options (∥ or ⋛). Under Fig 12 :104 "A settled molecule becomes an atom for whatever composes over it. So irreducible is a fact about a level and never about the world", a settled idea becomes an atom for the next cultural level. Same atoms, different bonds, different ideas: the isomer claim is real, but it must be checked per slot, not asserted once. See `06_isomers_and_order.md` for the full derivation and its falsifier.

**Refuter:** two idea-sets with identical atoms and identical bonds that nevertheless differ in substance, or a bond whose type cannot be recovered from the arrangement by the six tests in Fig 12.

### F8: Within-regime agreement is variation, not a verdict (general)

Fig 7 :35 "A room's agreement is variation, not a verdict. Selection between rooms is where the ground votes." Fig 7 :36 "A chain of mirrors is one long room. The next room must differ from the last, and match the residual it surfaced." Fig 2 :35 "Alignment is triangulation, not negotiation. A mirror cannot correct you."

A culture's internal consensus about what "success" means is a room. It cannot settle the cross-cultural question. The next room must have low overlap and must be chosen against the residual the last one surfaced. Fig 2 :40 "agreement about the world is worthless when the agreeing frames share an evidence pool" applies with force to this simulated run: the second seat shares every source, so its agreement is one pool talking. Venue ranking is a budget filter, never a witness: Fig 9 :60 "Use filters for the budget and witnesses for the verdict. Never let a filter hand you a verdict."

**Refuter:** a within-culture consensus that settles a cross-cultural causal claim without a low-overlap room and a ground reading.

### F9: The break is at the objective link, and the frame must be named after the residual (general)

Fig 3 :25-27: having terms for what a situation affords has "neither" obvious failures nor obvious tests, so "it attracts the least attention and the fewest instruments"; and "A link with no instrument is not a link that works. It is a link nobody has looked at." Fig 9 :27-28 "describe it before you go looking, or any frame you pick will seem to fit." Fig 1 :27 "They are frame-internal. A frame cannot score itself with a quantity it also produces."

The missing instrument is a per-slot measure of idea change against material contact. Until it exists, "how economic systems influence ideas" is a specification of what to measure, not a verdict. And the discipline is residual first, frame second: describe the four slots in effect terms, then select economic-anthropology or cultural-cognition frames. Picking "cultural materialism" or "moral economy" first would make either seem to fit.

**Refuter:** name and operate an existing instrument that measures per-slot idea change against material contact across at least two regimes, and show it already works.

### F10: What the figures do not give: the name of the cause (gap, honest)

Fig 9 :32 "you get a kind of thing, never the answer itself." Fig 13 :134 "Unreachable is a verdict here, not a silence." Fig 5 :21 "the gap has real mass somewhere."

The figures narrow the shape (habitat, frame, head start, closure, per-slot bonds, import) but do not say which regime produces which idea. That specific causal level is a `gap` in this derivation. It is not a failure of the search: Fig 8 :39 "So a search that finds nothing is never a proof of absence." It is a stopped line with the lineage recorded. A claim of the form "market economies cause meritocratic success beliefs" is a `specified` hypothesis awaiting an instrument, not a figure result.

**Refuter for the gap verdict:** produce a figure line that names a specific regime-to-slot mapping with its source gate. Until one does, the honest report is `gap` at that level.

## 6. Pre-registered refuter (written before the conclusion)

Required by the seat rule that a falsifier written before the run is a design instrument.

The shape claim in `README.md` and `07_the_tether_answer.md` is refused if any of the following holds:

1. A single scalar "economic system" measure predicts all four idea slots with no per-slot residual and no loss of fit (breaks F1).
2. An idea about ownership or success changes material survival with no change in contact, resource flow or incentive (breaks F2 and F3).
3. A community mints a new property concept with no prior regime contact and no held atom (breaks F5).
4. A within-culture consensus settles a cross-cultural causal claim without a low-overlap room (breaks F8).
5. A figure line is produced that names a specific regime-to-slot mapping with its source gate (closes F10 and falsifies the gap verdict).

If any fires, the corresponding finding is withdrawn and `07` and `09` are amended before any outreach. Until a per-slot instrument operates, nothing here is labelled `grounded`.

## 7. Recommendation

1. Lead with the four slots and their refuter, not with a theory of economic determinism (F1, F8).
2. Describe the material arrangement as habitat and enumerate it: actors, conditions, relations, and the cascade three steps out (Fig 11 :52 "And contact cascades." and Fig 11 :53 "the residual that mattered was three steps out").
3. Keep material contact as the anchor and never let agreement, prestige or a dominant ideology stand in for it (F2, Fig 2 :35).
4. Treat enculturation as a head start (F4). Do not ship a list of "what market societies believe"; ship the generator and its refuter (Fig 4 :22).
5. Choose comparison rooms with low overlap and match them to the surfaced residual (F7 room selection, Fig 7 :36).
6. Report the specific-causal level as `gap` until an instrument exists (F10). Fig 13 :134 "Unreachable is a verdict here, not a silence."

## 8. Canonical usage check (figures README + WATSON metalaw note)

Two rules govern how the figures may be used.

- The canonical README says the figures "outrank everything else in the repo" and "the quotes are the authority, not this summary." It also says a reading that makes one figure contradict another has misidentified its domain. That is exactly what the F2 re-census did: the apparent economy-as-anchor strain was a domain error (ideology mistaken for material contact), not a flaw.
- The README's limit phrase is "what a missing thing would have to look like (a shape, not a name)". Applied here, it forbids naming which economic system causes which idea. It does **not** forbid naming atoms already held (Fig 11's habitat actors, the six substrate terms). This solution names no specific regime as the cause, so it stays inside the rule.
- WATSON metalaw note: the figures are primary and decide; world sources corroborate only. A world conflict sends the derivation back to the census. The dual-inheritance feedback claim did exactly that: it conflicted with a one-way reading, so the census was redone and the conflict dissolved into frame coupling versus ground (section 9).
