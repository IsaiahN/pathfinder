## QUESTION

Decide, in effect terms, what the Tether says about the claim that economic systems influence ideas about choice, obligation, ownership and personal success. Option A: treat the economy as a scalar ground that determines one mentality. Option B: unbundle the four slots, treat the material arrangement as habitat and frame with material contact as anchor, and report the specific causal mapping as a gap. What each leads to: A averages live slot signals into an uncheckable determinant and manufactures the disqualifying correlation by selecting agreeing frames; B sends a per-slot shape, written refuters and a named instrument gap, and stops where the ground cannot yet settle.

## DOMAIN CENSUS

R by arity, symmetry and scale, then one line per domain with actors / currency / anchor, partition or union, and order by dependency.

- **R (the residual).** The covariation between a provisioning and exchange regime and four idea slots. Arity: four conditions with pairwise overlaps, so a **union compound**, not one hard question (Fig 9 :54 "when the split lands on a union rather than a partition, unbundle before you search again"). Symmetry: the four slots are not interchangeable; the claimed direction is asymmetric until tested. Scale: individual cognition and population-level institutions, with a membrane between them.
- **Domain 1, material arrangement.** Actors: producers, exchangers, property regimes, division of labour, resource flows. Currency: survival, what clears a market, incentive gradients. Anchor: material contact, what actually pays. Fig 2 :29 names "a market" as a permitted anchor example, but as a measurement, not an ideology. Domain type: habitat, enumerated (Fig 11 :43 "The habitat is enumerated, never composed.").
- **Domain 2, the four idea frames.** Actors: persons holding beliefs, beliefs as terms in Γ. Currency: coherence, plausibility, payoff. Anchor: material contact, never agreement (Fig 2 :45 "Frames range against the ground. They do not negotiate with it.").
- **Domain 3, the scale membrane.** Actors: parents, teachers, peers, institutions, reward contingencies. Currency: head starts (Fig 4 :22 "Only methods cross up. Only head starts come down."). Anchor: the ground reached by the next generation.
- **Domain 4, cross-regime contact.** Actors: peer frames with different closures, reached through trade, migration, conquest or media. Currency: imported atoms, priced. Anchor: the ground still decides; source gate and shadow test apply.
- **Domain 5, observation.** Actors: researchers and instruments. Currency: agreement versus residual. Anchor: measured covariation. In this simulated run all five domains draw on one evidence pool, so this domain's independence is near zero.
- **Order by dependency.** Per slot: perception, vocabulary, objective, planning, learn and carry (Fig 3). The objective link is the likely break (Fig 3 :27 "A link with no instrument is not a link that works. It is a link nobody has looked at."). Describe the residual before selecting a frame (Fig 9 :27-28 "describe it before you go looking, or any frame you pick will seem to fit").

Strain test: the economy-as-anchor reading strained against Fig 2 :33 "The anchor must not update." Re-census moved the anchor to material contact and kept the economy as habitat plus frame; no figure contradiction remains. Dual-inheritance feedback (world source) strained against the same rule; re-census classified it as frame-to-frame coupling, not anchoring.

## VERDICT (from the figures)

The shape survives; the scalar determinant does not. The economic system is habitat and frame, material contact is the anchor, capability is agent-and-habitat, a head start crosses the membrane, closure confines each regime and priced imports move the wall, valence is relative to the slot, the four slots compose under different bonds, within-regime agreement is variation, and the objective link lacks an instrument. The specific mapping of regime to idea is a `gap`, reported plainly. This is a `general` plus `specified`, ungrounded result. The strongest single checkable point is the slot-indexing rule: a determinant that cannot predict the four slots separately has not answered the question.

- **cost if wrong:** bounded and locatable. The packet is mode-labelled, states refuters before the conclusion and records the lineage, so an error is findable rather than invisible. The real cost is spending the independence of outside readers by sending a recording instead of a generator, which Fig 2 :42 prices ("Buy independence in proportion to the cost of being wrong."). That cost is avoidable by leading with the four slots and their refuter.
- **undoable:** yes. The scalar claim is removable without touching the shape; `07_the_tether_answer.md` and `08_expert_verification_packet.md` already remove it. A sent claim can be superseded by the corrected packet, and no primitive must be withdrawn.
- **reversed by:** a per-slot instrument, not agreement. A measure showing that a slot changes with material contact and holds when contact is fixed would settle the habitat claim; a scalar predictor of all four slots with no per-slot residual would break F1; a concept minted with no contact and no held atom would break closure; a consensus settled without a low-overlap room would break F8; and a figure line naming a regime-to-slot mapping with its source gate would close the `gap`. Until one of these fires, the verdict stands as `candidate`.
