# The Tether's answer: how economic systems influence four ideas

**Mode: `general` on the structural shape, `specified` on the mechanism. Not `grounded`.** Simulated derivation checked for internal consistency against the figures (`05_what_shape_means.md` section 6), not a ground reading.

## 1. The residual it answers

The question bundles four questions. People's answers about choice, obligation, ownership and personal success vary with how they provision and exchange. The gap is between the material arrangement and those four answers. It is a **union of overlapping conditions**, not one hard question and not a partition. Fig 9 :54 "when the split lands on a union rather than a partition, unbundle before you search again." It must be looped per slot. Fig 1 :19 "The residual is indexed by object slot, not aggregated." Fig 1 :22 "Aggregating across slots is how a live signal disappears into an average."

## 2. The shape

1. **The economic system is habitat and frame; material contact is the anchor.** Fig 11 :43 "The habitat is enumerated, never composed." The anchor is not an ideology but what settles a question regardless of what any frame thinks: Fig 2 :29-30 "An anchor is whatever settles the question and does not change its answer because of what any frame thinks." Fig 2 :33 "The anchor must not update." So the direction in the question means contact from a habitat into a frame, not validation by agreement. Fig 2 :45 "Frames range against the ground. They do not negotiate with it."
2. **Capability is agent-and-habitat.** Fig 11 :39 "capability is a property of agent-and-habitat, never of the agent alone." Fig 11 :40 "An improvement that does not change contact changes nothing, however much it improves." Ideas about success are not a trait inside a person; they are what a person in that arrangement can form. Fig 11 :61 "Introduction is available; exclusion is not." One can add institutions or instruments, not subtract the habitat.
3. **What crosses scales is a head start, not a recording.** Fig 4 :22 "Only methods cross up. Only head starts come down." Fig 4 :25 "A recording carried upward looks like knowledge and is a description of one occasion." Enculturation makes the next search cheaper; it does not hand over a copy of the previous answers. That is why continuity and variation coexist inside one regime.
4. **Each regime is a closure, and only cross-regime contact moves the wall.** Fig 6 :23 "No frame escapes its own closure. The bound moves only on contact with a frame whose closure differs." Fig 13 :115 "The set is closed. The arrangements are not. That gap is why import exists." Fig 6 :24 "A wrong atom costs more than starting from primitives." New property concepts and success metrics arrive as priced imports with provenance and a shadow test, never as a free gift. Fig 8 :50 "Two frames can hold the same atom by convergence, reaching it separately, which is corroboration at maximum independence." Convergence and adoption look identical in the contents; only the record separates them.
5. **Valence is relative to the slot.** Fig 11 :49 "An actor's valence is relative to the residual, not a property it carries." Market integration can widen the choice comparison and erode the obligation bond; no standing list of good or bad regimes survives a change of residual (Fig 11 :51 "No standing list of good actors survives a change of residual.").
6. **The four slots use different bonds over shared atoms.** Fig 12 :112 "A plan is a sequence. A goal is a comparison. Progress is a subtraction. A refutation is a negation". Success is a comparison, obligation a production or sequence, ownership a bond between a resource and a recognised claim, choice a disjunction or comparison. Fig 12 :117 "Same atoms, different bond, different substance. The difference is invisible in the formula and decisive in the behaviour." See `06_isomers_and_order.md`.
7. **Agreement inside a regime is variation, not a verdict.** Fig 7 :35 "A room's agreement is variation, not a verdict. Selection between rooms is where the ground votes." Fig 7 :36 "A chain of mirrors is one long room. The next room must differ from the last, and match the residual it surfaced." Fig 2 :35 "Alignment is triangulation, not negotiation. A mirror cannot correct you." The comparison room must be low-overlap and chosen against the surfaced residual.
8. **The specific cause is a gap.** Fig 9 :32 "you get a kind of thing, never the answer itself." Fig 13 :134 "Unreachable is a verdict here, not a silence." The figures narrow the shape but do not name which regime produces which idea. There is also no per-slot instrument: Fig 3 :27 "A link with no instrument is not a link that works. It is a link nobody has looked at."

## 3. What it explicitly does not say

- Not "the economy determines ideas" as a scalar claim. That averages the four slots (F1).
- Not that any named regime causes any named idea. The framework returns a shape, not a name.
- Not that the economic system is an anchor that ideas cannot move in the sense of altering material contact. Material contact is the anchor; the regime is habitat and frame.
- Not that a culture can be improved by coaching isolated individuals. That leaves contact unchanged (F3).
- Not that ideas are copies of parental answers. What transfers is a head start (F4).
- Not that similarity across cultures is corroboration. Convergence and import are distinguishable only by the record (F5).
- Not `grounded`. No per-slot measurement settles any of this in this derivation.

## 4. What would settle it: the ground

A per-slot instrument, not more argument. From *The Hard Problem is About Source*: an instrument that reads green "cannot comment on the source"; the spectrometer, not a debate, resolved the case. Here the corresponding questions are:

- Does a given idea slot change when material contact changes and hold when it does not? That separates habitat contact from mere frame agreement.
- Does the transfer across a regime boundary change the recipient's search space (head start) or only its answers (recording)?
- Can a new property concept be shown to have arrived with a source gate and a shadow test, rather than by convergence?
- Standing falsifiers from `01` section 6: a scalar determinant of all four slots; an idea that moves material contact with no change in contact; a concept minted with no contact and no held atom; a within-culture consensus settling a cross-cultural claim.

## 5. The honest stop

**Unreached at the specific-causal level, not silence.** *They're Scanning Books When They Should Be Studying Nature* says "A minimum viable set of six terms is a human's careful guess at reality's compression axes; another sensorium's minimum set need not be those six." The six are a visible choice, not nature's own list. This derivation stops at the limit of the available instruments and records the lineage so the stop is checkable. Fig 8 :39 "So a search that finds nothing is never a proof of absence." Prestige, consensus and a large bibliography are not such instruments: "Prestige can fund an action. It can give an action a budget to be tried. But it cannot settle an action. Only the ground can settle it." (*The Recursive Self-Improvement Trap*)

## 6. One-paragraph version for outreach

> The Tether does not say that the economy determines ideas. It says that "how economic systems influence ideas" is four questions wearing one pronoun set: choice, obligation, ownership, success. The material arrangement is the habitat and a frame; what settles an idea is material contact, not agreement. Capability belongs to the person in the habitat, so changing a person in isolation changes nothing. What passes from a population to a child is a head start for the next search, not a copy of the answers. Each regime is a closure, so only contact with a different regime imports new atoms, and those imports are priced and must be provenanced. The four slots are bonded differently over shared atoms: success is a comparison, obligation a production, ownership a claim on a resource, choice a disjunction. An arrangement that helps one slot can hurt another. Internal consensus is variation, not a verdict. What the framework does not give is the name of which regime causes which idea; that is a gap until a per-slot instrument exists. What it offers is the shape any answer must have, the falsifiers written in advance, and the comparison rooms that would close it.
