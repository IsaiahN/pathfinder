## 1. The claim, in one paragraph

"How economic systems influence ideas" is four overlapping slots, not one mentality. The material arrangement is the habitat and a frame; material contact is the anchor, and the anchor does not update on what any frame thinks. Capability is agent-and-habitat, so an isolated change in a person leaves contact unchanged and changes nothing. What crosses the population-to-person membrane is a head start for the next search, not a recording of previous answers. Each regime is a closure; only contact with a different closure imports new atoms, and imports are priced and provenanced. Valence is relative to the slot. Success is a comparison, obligation a production or sequence, ownership a bond over a resource and a recognised claim, choice a disjunction or comparison. Within-regime agreement is variation, not a verdict. The specific regime-to-idea mapping is reported as a gap until a per-slot instrument exists.

## 2. Three checkable sub-claims

### C1: The four slots are a union and must be looped separately
- **Statement:** an economic-determinism scalar that predicts all four slots with no per-slot residual is not a checkable answer; it throws away the signal.
- **Source, verbatim:** Fig 1 :19 "The residual is indexed by object slot, not aggregated." Also Fig 9 :54 "when the split lands on a union rather than a partition, unbundle before you search again." And Fig 1 :22 "Aggregating across slots is how a live signal disappears into an average."
- **What to check:** do the four slots actually overlap in your data, such that an intervention on ownership leaves an obligation intersection standing? Or do they partition cleanly?
- **Refuter:** a single scalar "economic system" measure predicts all four slots with no per-slot residual and no loss of fit.

### C2: The habitat/anchor distinction and the membrane rule
- **Statement:** the economic arrangement is habitat plus frame; material contact, not agreement, is what settles an idea. What transfers to the next generation is a cheapened search, not copied answers.
- **Source, verbatim:** Fig 2 :29-30 "An anchor is whatever settles the question and does not change its answer because of what any frame thinks." Fig 2 :33 "The anchor must not update." Fig 11 :40 "An improvement that does not change contact changes nothing, however much it improves." Fig 4 :22 "Only methods cross up. Only head starts come down."
- **What to check:** in your ethnography or dataset, does a change in an idea about ownership or success without any change in resources, incentives or contact ever persist? If so, the capability claim is in trouble. Does intergenerational transfer change what recipients attend to, or only what they answer?
- **Refuter:** an idea alone changes material survival with no change in contact; or exact answers transfer and are sufficient with no change in the recipient's search space.

### C3: Closure, import and per-slot bonds
- **Statement:** a regime cannot think what its closure lacks; cross-regime contact is the only operator that adds an atom, and the four slots are different bond arrangements over shared atoms.
- **Source, verbatim:** Fig 6 :23 "No frame escapes its own closure. The bound moves only on contact with a frame whose closure differs." Fig 12 :117 "Same atoms, different bond, different substance. The difference is invisible in the formula and decisive in the behaviour." Fig 12 :112 "A plan is a sequence. A goal is a comparison. Progress is a subtraction. A refutation is a negation". Operators table :51 "a goal is a comparison, which is why a vocabulary without it cannot state one."
- **What to check:** can a new ownership concept be traced to contact with another regime, or did it arise by convergence (same atom reached separately)? Do the four slots use distinguishable bond types: comparison for success, production or sequence for obligation, a claim bond for ownership, disjunction or comparison for choice?
- **Refuter:** a community mints a new property concept with no prior contact and no held atom; or two slots share atoms and bond yet differ in substance; or convergence and import cannot be separated even with a record.

## 3. What is explicitly not claimed

- Not a theory of everything, and not a final vocabulary. There is no last room.
- Not "the economy determines ideas" as a scalar claim.
- Not that any named regime causes any named idea. The specific causal level is a `gap`.
- Not that the economic system is an anchor. Material contact is the anchor; the regime is habitat and frame.
- Not that within-culture consensus corroborates a cross-cultural claim. Fig 2 :40 "agreement about the world is worthless when the agreeing frames share an evidence pool."
- Not that a new atom arrives free. Fig 6 :24 "A wrong atom costs more than starting from primitives."
- Not `grounded`. Nothing here was settled by measurement.
