# Outreach draft: check one shape before deciding about the theory

**Use:** one-to-one, or as a cover note attached to a submission through the public venues in `03_public_contact_points.csv`. **Not** for bulk send.

**Hard precondition:** do not send until the per-slot residual and the refuter below survive your own check. If the four slots cannot be told apart, the offer collapses on contact.

---

**Subject:** Four ideas about economic life, and one thing that would refute my description of them

> Hi [name],
>
> I have one specific output I would like you to check against what you already know. I am not asking you to read a theory first, and I am not asking you to agree with anything. If the output fails, there is nothing else to look at.
>
> **The output (`general` on structure, `specified` on mechanism, not `grounded`):** "How economic systems influence ideas" is not one question. It is four slots that overlap in pairs: choice, obligation, ownership, and personal success. Each needs its own ground reading. An economic determinism claim that predicts all four with one number has thrown away the signal, because the framework's residual is indexed by slot, not averaged.
>
> The shape I get is this. The material arrangement is the habitat and a frame; what settles an idea is material contact (what actually sustains people and clears a market), not agreement. Capability belongs to the person-in-the-habitat, not to the person alone, so a change that does not change contact changes nothing. What passes between the population and the individual is a head start for the next search, not a recording of the previous answers. Each regime's thinkable ideas sit inside its closure, so longer search in one regime cannot find what another regime holds; contact with a different regime is the only thing that moves that wall, and it is priced. The four slots compose under different bonds: success is a comparison, obligation is a sequence or production, ownership is a bond between a resource and a recognised claim, choice is a disjunction or comparison over options.
>
> **What I am not claiming:** that any named economic system causes any named idea. The framework returns a kind of thing, never the answer itself. Market integration may help the choice slot and hurt the obligation slot; there is no standing list of good or bad regimes, because an actor's value is relative to the slot. Agreement inside one culture is variation, not a verdict. I am also not claiming a measurement settled this: no per-slot instrument exists in my derivation, so the specific causal level is reported as a gap, not an answer.
>
> **What would refute the shape (written down before sending this):**
> 1. One scalar measure of "economic system" predicts all four slots with no per-slot residual and no loss of fit.
> 2. An idea about ownership or success changes material survival while contact, resources and incentives stay the same.
> 3. A group generates a new property concept with no prior contact and no held atom.
> 4. A within-culture consensus settles a cross-cultural causal claim without a low-overlap comparison.
>
> If any one holds, I withdraw the corresponding part. A one-line "this fails because..." is more useful to me than agreement. If you would rather not engage, no reply is needed.
>
> **If it survives, and only then:** the public material is listed in the folder. The figures are the authority, not my summary of them.
>
> **If you want to use the method:** write down the four slots, then write down what would refute your own result *before* you run it. Each user supplies their own ground and their own refuter; this derivation cannot check the direction of its own question.
