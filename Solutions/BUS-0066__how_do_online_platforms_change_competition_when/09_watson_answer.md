## QUESTION

Decide, in effect terms, what changes about competition when the scarce thing becomes control over customer access and data. Option A: accept the question's stock picture, treat control as a resource held in amount, and return a winner, a threshold or a single remedy. Option B: treat the question as a union of conditions, identify which frame holds the channel to a ground that does not update, and return a shape plus refuters with the remedy left open. What each leads to: A produces a single-number verdict that Fig 1 :19 disqualifies ("The residual is indexed by object slot, not aggregated"), flattens three anchors into one, and spends the independence of the people checking it; B produces a per-slot, per-condition answer that a checker can refute, with the missing instrument named as `gap`. Figures decide; world sources corroborate only.

## VERDICT (from the figures)

The stock picture is refused; the union shape survives. Access, data and network effects are three overlapping conditions with different anchors, so verdicts are kept separate per domain and never averaged. The decisive change is who holds the channel to a ground that does not update. Data volume is frame-internal and a budget filter, not a witness. A click is a reading with a source problem, and the discriminator is a declared `gap`. Matching efficiency and entrant-access harm are separate verdicts, amplified by a cycle whose sign is measured at the slot. Entry is an import needing provenance and a shadow test, and isolation substitutes a habitat. Business models over a fixed atom set are isomers. No figure line returns a named remedy, threshold or magnitude. Mode `specified` on the sub-claims, `general` on the structure, `gap` on the remedy, not `grounded`.

- **cost if wrong:** bounded and locatable if the refuters are sent with the claim. The real cost is spending the independence of outside readers by sending a conclusion instead of a generator, which Fig 2 :42 prices: "Buy independence in proportion to the cost of being wrong." A second cost is policy: a single-lever remedy applied to a union leaves the intersections standing (Fig 9 :55-56), so the market can be made worse in the condition the lever did not target. Both costs are avoidable by leading with the union split and the refuter, and by measuring the lever sign at the slot.
- **undoable:** yes. The three per-domain verdicts are separable, and the stock reading can be withdrawn without touching the union or channel claims. `07_the_tether_answer.md` and `08_expert_verification_packet.md` already remove it. No primitive has to be retracted, and a sent claim can be superseded by a corrected packet.
- **reversed by:** a ground reading, not agreement. An independent-reading test at a slot would fire C2's refuter; a source-discriminating instrument applied to platform data would close the F4 gap; a lever-sign measurement would show whether a remedy clears access without worsening the data or cycle condition; an atom-count check would show whether an advantage is an import or a recomposition; a re-census showing one anchor for all three conditions would collapse the union. Until one of these fires, the verdict stands as `candidate`.
