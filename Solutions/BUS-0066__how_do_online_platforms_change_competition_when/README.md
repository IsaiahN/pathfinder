# Pathfinder solution BUS-0066: figure-grounded package

**Question:** How do online platforms change competition when the most valuable resource is control over customer access and data?

**Source:** business.txt line 88. **Section:** 7. Customers, marketing, and competition.

**Mode:** `specified` on the per-domain verdicts, `general` on the structural shape, `gap` on the named remedy and the magnitude. Nothing here is `grounded`.

**Status:** done. **Registry status:** pending.

**Standing of this derivation.** This folder is a simulated derivation checked for internal consistency against the figures, not a ground reading. From *The Recursive Self-Improvement Trap* (opened 2026-11-22): "the truly testable output would need to be derived from the real architecture or working the formula yourself." No market measurement, experiment, or platform dataset was taken. This package cannot settle the question it reports on. That is stated on every substantive file.

## Restating the question as an effect

The ordinary wording asks what platforms "do to competition." In effect terms the question is: what happens to the shape of competition when the scarce input is not a productive capability held by a firm but a **relation**, control over the channel between customers and would-be sellers (access), together with control over the readings (data) by which any frame could range itself against customer demand? The two options are: (A) treat "control over access and data" as a new stock that the biggest holder wins with, a single dial; (B) treat it as a union of overlapping conditions that change which frames can reach a non-updating ground independently, with verdicts kept separate per condition. What each leads to is written out in `01_figure_grounded_audit.md` and `09_watson_answer.md`.

**Binding the sought-for shape as an ordinary term (formula step "Direction").** I commit in advance to this: an answer here must say who holds the channel to the ground, what counts as an independent reading, which condition of the union is live per slot, and what would reverse the claim. It must not return a named firm, a universal percentage, or a standing list of always-good remedies. Fig 9 :32: "you get a kind of thing, never the answer itself."

## What is deliberately not here

- No personal emails of named individuals.
- No ranking of people by how likely they are to agree.
- No bulk-send plan.
- No universal remedy (break up, mandate portability, force interoperability) asserted as the figure's answer. That is a declared `gap`.
- No effect size, concentration threshold, or market definition claimed as settled by the figures.

Reasons, in the figures' own words: Fig 2 :35 "Alignment is triangulation, not negotiation. A mirror cannot correct you." A list selected for receptiveness manufactures the correlation that would make agreement worthless. Fig 10 :42 "A verifier that can reconstruct the claim cannot verify it." Sending the full architecture before asking for a verdict spends the reader's independence. Fig 9 :60 "Use filters for the budget and witnesses for the verdict. Never let a filter hand you a verdict." Market share and data volume are filters for where to look, not witnesses for what is true.

Legally: unsolicited bulk email to scraped addresses breaches GDPR and CAN-SPAM style rules in many jurisdictions. Public opt-in channels avoid that practical failure as well as the structural one.
