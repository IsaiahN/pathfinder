## 1. A shape is a constraint, not a filler

The canonical figures README states the limit: the framework returns "what a missing thing would have to look like (**a shape, not a name**)." Fig 9 :32 "you get a kind of thing, never the answer itself." Fig 13 :134 "Unreachable is a verdict here, not a silence". Fig 13: "It cannot say which primitive is missing, because naming it would mean already holding it."

From *The Library of Babel* (carried quote): "You can cut your search short if the set of books returned is small and descriptive enough that it provides a general shape of the answer that satisfies your question."

So a **shape** is the narrowed form of an answer you can state before you can name it. It constrains; it does not fill. For this question, "competition here is a union of conditions with separate anchors, and the decisive object is the channel to a non-updating ground" is a shape. "Break up firm X," "mandate full data portability," or "the right concentration ratio is Y" are names, and the figures do not return them.

## 2. How a shape is obtained

- *Library of Babel* (carried): use the vocabulary you already have that is closest to the thing, look for recurring new terms that overlap or correlate with it, recurse. "With each new term gained, you narrow the space and deepen your vocabulary."
- Fig 6 :30 "outward: does another frame already hold it? The description travels. Fails by being sharpened into terms only we can read. Cast it in effect terms." Fig 9 :30-31 "your words are borrowed too, so describe the effect rather than the cause."
- *Decoding Tacit Knowledge* (carried): "When contact isn't possible, the best you can send is a plain description of what you'd expect to see, because anyone can check that against the world."
- *Library of Babel* on stopping (carried): the answer is reached when "the finish line will also be the starting place" and by then you have "composed the answer through the process of locating it."

Applied here: I described the effect ("who may reach a customer, and who may read the market, independently") before choosing economics, law or animal behaviour as frames (Fig 8 :53 "Residual first, frame second"). Had I chosen the fields first, any of them would have seemed to fit.

## 3. What licenses a name

Only two things, both outside the shape itself:

1. **The ground settles it.** Formula step 5: "the ground settles it. Verified only by what pays." The README: the framework "stops at the limit of what the available instruments can distinguish, and records the lineage so the stop is honest."
2. **A provenanced import from a frame whose closure differs** (Fig 6 :23, Fig 8 :52), with a source gate and a shadow test.

*They're Scanning Books* (carried) sharpens this: "The real import is not a new term in the existing space. It is a new dimension of the space." And: "A minimum viable set of six terms is a human's careful guess at reality's compression axes; another sensorium's minimum set need not be those six." Also: "Declaring the primitives does not make them neutral. It makes the choice visible."

For this question: naming a specific remedy would be a binding from a policy frame, not a derivation from the figures. It is allowed only as provenance, and it is `candidate` until a ground pays for it.

## 4. Why the data question is a source question

*The Hard Problem is About Source* was opened in full this session. The mechanism is metamerism: an instrument reads green and "the instrument is speaking only about what it measured, it cannot comment on the source." One green light, or yellow plus blue, or RGB, or CMYK, or a scheme not yet discovered, all produce the same reading. "If several paths create the same behavior, then behavior is a projection with lossy information (it cannot define consciousness in full)." The resolution was empirical: "It was proven wrong not by argument, but by the arrival of a better instrument."

Mapped here: a click, a view or a purchase is a reading. Genuine preference, a default position, a ranking-induced choice and lack of alternatives can produce the same reading. A platform's data cannot comment on which source produced it. What is owed is a discriminator, not a name. Fig 6 :34-35: "What is unavailable is a sensor for something that has never registered anywhere, at any resolution." This is why F4 of `01` is a `gap` rather than an omission.

*The Recursive Self-Improvement Trap* (opened this session) gives the validation half: "the catch is that the output is only as good as the validator. The agent, or the tool, or the frame, or the theory, cannot validate itself. An external validator, or ground, is always needed to tether the veracity of the output to reality. Anything else is drift." And: "You may all agree and be wrong." A consensus among platform economists that shares one evidence pool is a drift detector, not a ground.

## 5. Correction to my own §1 to §4 (see `06_isomers_and_order.md` Q1)

I overstated "shape, not a name" in the first pass. The README's phrase is "what a **missing** thing would have to look like (a shape, not a name)." Its subject is a primitive not held in Γ. Access and data are not missing primitives. They are bindings from the business and information frames onto atoms the framework already holds (network for relation, spectrum for ordering, observation o′ for a reading). Naming them is provenance, not the invention of a seventh term.

What remains wrong is claiming those bindings as imports that move the wall. Fig 6 :21 "MINT composes inside the closure and can never add an atom." Fig 5 :70 "A term is settled when the ground pays for it. A term is usable when something can compose over it." The binding adds no atom; it is a composition, and its reach depends on whether it composes. Both claims are now recorded so neither is quietly lost. The canonical limit still stands on the *remedy*: no figure line returns which policy occupies the slot, and that is a genuine `gap`.

## 7. Two consequences for outreach

**Prestige is not action.** From *The Recursive Self-Improvement Trap* (opened this session): "Prestige must never be allowed to validate an action directly... Prestige can fund an action. It can give an action a budget to be tried. But it cannot settle an action. Only the ground can settle it. The Ground Maintainer must be blind to prestige." The essay's own instance is instructive: the high-prestige agent "monopolize[s] compute and repeat[s] its past success, and the system will stop exploring." A platform-economy campaign that gathers famous names to agree with the claim is the named collapse, not corroboration. The legitimate ask gives the claim a budget to be tried and lets the ground settle it.

**My own audit is a simulation, not a ground reading.** Same essay: "LLMs can quickly simulate an approximate answer/output with the formula found in the architecture post... However, the truly testable output would need to be derived from the real architecture or working the formula yourself." So `01`, `06`, `07`, `09` and `11` are simulated derivations checked for internal consistency against the figures. They are not tests of the claim and must be labelled as such. They cannot settle anything they report on.
