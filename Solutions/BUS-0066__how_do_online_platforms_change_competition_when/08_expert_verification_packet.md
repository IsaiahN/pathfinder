# Expert verification packet: the Tether on platform competition

**What this is:** a claim in a form a platform economist, competition lawyer or digital-markets researcher can check. Mode, derivation and refuters are stated before the conclusion, so a reader knows what to look for and what would settle it.

**Mode: `specified` on the sub-claims, `general` on the union shape, `gap` on the remedy. Not `grounded`.** Simulated derivation checked for internal consistency against the figures, not a ground reading. It cannot settle the claim it reports on.

## 1. The claim, in one paragraph

"How do online platforms change competition when the most valuable resource is control over customer access and data" is a union of three overlapping conditions (access, data, network effects) with different anchors, not one dial with one answer. The decisive structural change is whose channel reaches a non-updating ground (paying customers): when one actor owns both the gate and the ranking, other frames range against one frame's reading and their agreement is one evidence pool talking. A platform's own data volume is a frame-internal score, a budget filter, not a verdict. The efficiency gain from matching and the harm to entrant discovery are separate verdicts kept apart, never averaged, and network effects are a cycle whose sign is measured per slot. Over a fixed atom set, platform business models are isomers. The framework returns this shape, pre-registered refuters and an owed source-discriminating instrument. It returns no named remedy, no threshold and no effect size.

## 2. Three checkable sub-claims

### C1: union and per-slot indexing; checkable by reading Figures 1 and 9
- **Statement:** the question must be unbundled into conditions with separate anchors, and the verdict recorded per market slot, never as a pooled "platforms help or hurt competition" number.
- **Source, verbatim:** Fig 9 :4-5 "Disagreements that do not shrink with effort, where each rule keeps working well on a different subset, are not one hard question but several well-formed ones. Split it rather than search." Fig 9 :54 "And when the split lands on a union rather than a partition, unbundle before you search again." Fig 1 :19 "The residual is indexed by object slot, not aggregated."
- **What to check:** do access control, data control and network effects actually have different anchors and different levers in the markets you know? If they share one anchor, the union collapses.
- **Refuter:** a re-census showing a single anchor for all three; or a market in which one lever clears all three conditions simultaneously. Either breaks C1.

### C2: channel ownership and the frame-internal score; checkable by reading Figures 2, 10 and 1
- **Statement:** the change that matters is who holds the channel to a ground that does not update, not how much data is held. Data volume cannot be the platform's own score.
- **Source, verbatim:** Fig 10 :24 "the ground does not decay, the channel does." Fig 2 :29 "An anchor is whatever settles the question and does not change its answer because of what any frame thinks." Fig 1 :27 "They are frame-internal. A frame cannot score itself with a quantity it also produces." Fig 2 :40 "disqualifying for a verdict: agreement about the world is worthless when the agreeing frames share an evidence pool."
- **What to check:** in a market you know, do competing sellers range against paying customers through independent readings, or through a ranking supplied by the gatekeeper? Is the platform's data count used as its own evidence of advantage? If the former holds, C2 is weak there; if the latter holds, the figure disqualifies the score.
- **Refuter:** a measurement showing competing frames ranging independently against the same non-updating ground while one actor owns the ranking and the access rule. This breaks the channel-capture claim for that slot.

### C3: import, habitat and separated verdicts; checkable by reading Figures 6, 8 and 11
- **Statement:** entry is an import from a closure that differs, with provenance and a shadow test; moving to another platform substitutes a habitat rather than removing a gate; the efficiency surplus and the gatekeeping harm are separate verdicts.
- **Source, verbatim:** Fig 6 :23 "No frame escapes its own closure. The bound moves only on contact with a frame whose closure differs." Fig 8 :52 "Convergent derivation and adopted import look identical in the contents. Only the record of where each came from separates them, and the surplus lives only where the closures differ." Fig 11 :66 "Isolation is not removal of the habitat. It is substitution of one habitat for another." Fig 8 :45 "Two frames together reach what neither reaches alone."
- **What to check:** for a remedy you know (portability, interoperability, access mandate), name the atom imported, the closure it comes from, the shadow test it passes, and the independence it debits. Does the remedy improve access while worsening the data or cycle condition?
- **Refuter:** a case where a firm's reach grows with data volume alone while its channel, imported atom and habitat stay fixed; or a demonstration that an apparent import added no atom and only recomposed. Either breaks C3.

## 3. What is explicitly not claimed

- Not a verdict on any named firm, and not a net welfare score. Per-domain verdicts are not averaged.
- Not that data stock is a moat. It is frame-internal and a budget filter (Fig 1 :27, Fig 9 :60).
- Not that portability, interoperability, data sharing or breakup is the answer. World sources (Lam and Liu 2020; Martens 2023) show a lever can reverse sign. This is a `gap`.
- Not that the framework names the missing instrument or the remedy. Fig 9 :32 "you get a kind of thing, never the answer itself."
- Not that network effects are a linear cause. They are a cycle, and the sign is measured at the slot (Fig 12 :121).
- Not a standing list of always-good or always-bad actors (Fig 11 :51 "No standing list of good actors survives a change of residual").
- Not `grounded`. No measurement was taken.

## 5. What would settle it

A ground reading, not agreement. In order of cost:

1. An independent-reading test at a slot: do competing firms read paying customers through separate, non-pooled instruments? (would fire C2's refuter)
2. A source-discriminating instrument applied to platform data: which cause produced this reading? (closes the F4 gap)
3. A lever-sign measurement: does a proposed remedy clear access without worsening the data or cycle condition here? (world sources predict the sign can flip)
4. An atom-count check: does the advantage add an atom with provenance, or only recompose inside the closure? (Fig 6 :21)

## 6. What an expert can check in twenty minutes

1. Read Fig 9 :4-5, :54 and Fig 1 :19 (three short passages). Does the union split match the markets you know?
2. Read Fig 10 :24 and Fig 1 :27. Is the channel/stock distinction real in a case you can name?
3. Read Fig 6 :23 and Fig 11 :66. Name one remedy and say what atom it imports and what habitat it substitutes.
4. Send the refutation. A failure is more useful than an endorsement.
