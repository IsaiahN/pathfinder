# The Tether's answer: online platforms and competition over access and data

**Mode: `specified` on the per-domain verdicts, `general` on the structural shape, `gap` on the named remedy and the magnitude. Not `grounded`.** Simulated derivation checked for internal consistency against the figures (`05_what_shape_means.md` section 7), not a ground reading. No market measurement was taken.

## 1. The residual it answers

The question asks what platforms change when the scarce thing becomes control over customer access and data. In effect terms: what changes when one actor can stand between would-be competitors and the paying customer, and can hold the readings by which any of them would range against that customer? The residual is indexed per market slot (Fig 1 :19 "The residual is indexed by object slot, not aggregated"), and it is a **union compound** with at least three overlapping conditions (Fig 9 :54 "And when the split lands on a union rather than a partition, unbundle before you search again"): access, data, and network effects. Verdicts below are kept separate per domain and never averaged.

## 2. The shape

1. **This is three conditions, not one dial.** Access, data and network effects have different anchors and different levers. Fig 9 :55-56: "A bundled problem is usually a union of overlapping conditions, not a list of separate ones. A single lever clears the region where only one condition holds and leaves the intersections standing." World corroboration: Rochet and Tirole 2006 on price *structure*; Simone and Laudando 2025 on multiple sources of gatekeeper power at two levels of competition. These corroborate the union without deciding it.
2. **The decisive change is who holds the channel to the ground.** The ground is realized customer demand, which "does not change its answer because of what any frame thinks" (Fig 2 :29). What a platform mainly changes is the channel: "the ground does not decay, the channel does" (Fig 10 :24). When one frame supplies both the gate and the ranking, other frames range against its reading rather than against the market, and their agreement is one pool talking (Fig 2 :40; Fig 7 :35 "A room's agreement is variation, not a verdict. Selection between rooms is where the ground votes"). Data matters because it is the observation o′ a frame needs in order to range at all, so controlling it controls what can be resolved, not merely how much can be stored.
3. **Data is a reading with a source problem.** Several causes can produce the same click. *The Hard Problem is About Source*: "the instrument is speaking only about what it measured, it cannot comment on the source." A source-discriminating instrument is owed. No figure line supplies one, and no figure line names which remedy occupies the slot (Fig 9 :32 "you get a kind of thing, never the answer itself"). That is a declared `gap`, reported as unreachable at this budget (Fig 13 :134 "Unreachable is a verdict here, not a silence").
4. **Data volume cannot score the platform.** "They are frame-internal. A frame cannot score itself with a quantity it also produces" (Fig 1 :27). Volume, share and count are filters for the budget, never witnesses for the verdict (Fig 9 :60 "Use filters for the budget and witnesses for the verdict. Never let a filter hand you a verdict"). Fig 5 :70: "A term is settled when the ground pays for it. A term is usable when something can compose over it." More records compose inside one closure and "can never add an atom" (Fig 6 :21 "MINT composes inside the closure and can never add an atom").
5. **The efficiency surplus and the gatekeeping harm are separate verdicts.** Fig 8 :45 "Two frames together reach what neither reaches alone." A platform can be a benefactor to the unmet-matching residual and a malfactor to the entrant-access residual at the same time (Fig 11 :50 "The same actor is a benefactor to one residual and a malfactor to another, so mutualism and parasitism are relations rather than kinds"). Network effects are a cycle (Fig 12 :121 "cycle. The output re-enters as input. The sign of the loop decides whether it closes, and a chain cannot close by construction") that amplifies both; the sign is a slot measurement, not a general law. Which sign dominates is not decided here.
6. **Entry is an import with provenance, and isolation substitutes a habitat.** Fig 6 :23 "No frame escapes its own closure. The bound moves only on contact with a frame whose closure differs." An importer needs a source gate and a shadow test (Fig 8 :52), and the import is debited against independence (Fig 6 :24). Moving to another platform does not remove the gate: "Isolation is not removal of the habitat. It is substitution of one habitat for another" (Fig 11 :66), with two silent failures, what you failed to reproduce and what you unintentionally introduced (Fig 11 :68, :69).
7. **Business models are isomers over a fixed atom set.** Fig 12 :117 "isomer. Same atoms, different bond, different substance. The difference is invisible in the formula and decisive in the behaviour." The "most valuable resource" phrasing converts a bond difference into an ingredient list (Fig 12 :5). What changes is the arrangement: who pays whom, whether ranking is a gate or a route, whether data is a catalyst or a spent part (Fig 12 :119).
8. **No standing list of valuable resources.** Fig 11 :51 "No standing list of good actors survives a change of residual." The premise is accepted as a report of the current residual for a class of firms, not as a law. When the residual moves, the identity of the scarce thing moves with it.

## 3. What it explicitly does not say

- Not that any named platform is good or bad for competition, and not a net welfare verdict. Per-domain verdicts are never averaged.
- Not that data volume produces advantage. It is frame-internal and a budget filter.
- Not that portability, interoperability, data sharing or breakup is the answer. No figure line returns a remedy, and Lam and Liu 2020 plus Martens 2023 show a lever can reverse sign. The remedy is a `gap`.
- Not that the framework can name the missing instrument. It returns a shape and an owed discriminator, not a dataset name.
- Not that network effects are a linear cause. They are a cycle whose sign is measured at the slot.
- Not `grounded`. Nothing here was settled by a ground reading.

## 4. What would settle it: the ground

A ground reading, not agreement. Fig 2 :35 "Alignment is triangulation, not negotiation. A mirror cannot correct you." The discriminators owed are:

- **An independent reading test:** do competing firms in the slot range against paying customers through readings that do not share the platform's evidence pool? If they do, the channel-capture claim fails for that slot (refuter 2).
- **A source-discriminating instrument:** can a measurement say which cause produced a given click? If yes, the F4 gap closes and the remedy question moves forward.
- **A lever sign measurement:** for a proposed remedy, does it clear the access condition without worsening the data or cycle condition at the same slot? Lam and Liu 2020 and Martens 2023 predict the sign can flip; only measurement decides.
- **An atom-count check:** does an apparent advantage add a new atom (an import with provenance) or only recompose inside the closure? Fig 6 :21 governs the answer.
- Standing falsifiers from `01` section 4: the six listed conditions, written before the conclusion.

## 5. The honest stop

**Unreached, not unreachable in principle.** The figures README: the framework "stops at the limit of what the available instruments can distinguish, and records the lineage so the stop is honest." *They're Scanning Books* (carried): "A minimum viable set of six terms is a human's careful guess at reality's compression axes; another sensorium's minimum set need not be those six." The business bindings here are visible guesses, so naming them makes a choice visible rather than neutral. "Indiscernibility is not identity." The remedy gap is a verdict (Fig 13 :134), not a silence. In this simulated run all domains draw on one evidence pool, so no second seat or world source is an independent witness (Fig 2 :40).

## 6. One-paragraph version for outreach

> The Tether does not name a winning platform or a single remedy. It says the question is a union of three conditions, access, data and network effects, with different anchors, so one lever clears only the region where one condition holds. The structural change is not that someone accumulates a bigger stock of data. It is that one actor can own the channel through which every would-be competitor reads a non-updating ground, paying customers, so competition becomes reading one frame's reading rather than independent triangulation. A platform's own data volume is a frame-internal score, not a market verdict. The efficiency gain from matching and the harm to entrant discovery are separate verdicts and are never averaged; network effects are a cycle that amplifies both, with a sign that must be measured at the slot. Business models over the same atoms are isomers, same atoms, different bond, different substance. What the framework returns is the shape any answer must have, the refuters written in advance, and the source-discriminating instrument that is still owed. The remedy slot stays open until a ground settles it.
