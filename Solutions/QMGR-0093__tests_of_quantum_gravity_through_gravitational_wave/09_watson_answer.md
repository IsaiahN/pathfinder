## VERDICT (from the figures)

The shape survives; the precision-as-verdict and null-as-absence readings do not. A gravitational-wave test of quantum gravity is a three-condition union with the detector as ground. Precision narrows bounds but is never a witness. Current searches are a live SEARCHED and INWARD track: phenomenology is constrained per channel, no QG signature has registered, and Planck-scale QG is unreached at this budget rather than underivable in principle. This is a `specified`, ungrounded result from a simulated derivation. The theory frame is a candidate-source, not a ground-bearing domain.

- **cost if wrong:** bounded and locatable. The packet states its mode, four refuters and the per-channel lineage, so an error is findable rather than invisible. The real cost is spending the independence of outside readers by sending a verdict before a shape, which Fig 2 :42 prices ("Buy independence in proportion to the cost of being wrong."). That cost is avoidable by leading with the shape claim and its refuter.
- **undoable:** yes. The refused readings (precision as witness, null as absence, aggregate exclusion, theory coherence as ground) are removable without touching the derivation. `07_the_tether_answer.md` and `08_expert_verification_packet.md` already remove them. No atom has to be withdrawn and no channel is closed.
- **reversed by:** a ground reading, not agreement. A high-precision observation returning a deviation uniquely attributable to quantum gravity and settling a candidate model would extend the reach (RF1). A detector ground shown to update on the theory frame's predictions would break the anchor (RF4). A demonstration that the three conditions are a partition, or that a legitimate test needs no per-channel attribution, would force a re-census (RF2, RF5). Until one of these fires, the verdict stands as `candidate`.
