## 1. A shape is a constraint, not a filler

The figures README states the limit: the framework returns *"what a missing thing would have to look like (a shape, not a name)"*. Fig 9 :32: *"you get a kind of thing, never the answer itself"*. Fig 13 :134: *"Unreachable is a verdict here, not a silence"*.

From *The Library of Babel*: *"You can cut your search short if the set of books returned is small and descriptive enough that it provides a general shape of the answer that satisfies your question."* And the paradox: by the time you have the exact search terms, *"you will leave as the author"*, because you *"composed the answer through the process of locating it."*

So a **shape** is the narrowed form of an answer you can state before you can name it. It constrains; it does not fill. For this question:

- A shape: a test is a three-part compound, with a deviation stated in effect terms, a channel, and an independently readable ground; a null result is a bound, not an absence.
- A name: loop quantum gravity is the theory that string theory is not, or a specific echo delay in milliseconds is the signature.

The framework can deliver the first. The detector has to deliver the second.

## 2. How a shape is obtained

- *Library of Babel*: use the vocabulary you already have that is closest to the thing, then look for recurring new terms that overlap or correlate with it. *"With each new term gained, you narrow the space and deepen your vocabulary."*
- *Decoding Tacit Knowledge*: *"When contact isn't possible, the best you can send is a plain description of what you'd expect to see, because anyone can check that against the world."* A plain description of what a QG deviation would look like in strain data is exactly this kind of message; it can be checked by anyone with the released data.
- Fig 6 :30 (quoted in *Decoding Tacit Knowledge*): *"The description travels. Fails by being sharpened into terms only we can read. Cast it in effect terms."* Fig 9 :30-31 (same essay): *"your words are borrowed too, so describe the effect rather than the cause"*. So say "a frequency-dependent phase shift with a specified exponent", not "spacetime foam".
- *How Intelligence Actually Works* introduces the **union horizon**: a projection sinks below it when *"no observer, with any instrument any of them can build, can discriminate its origin"*, and adds that *"Being 'below the union horizon' is never a settled verdict, only a report that nobody has reached it yet."* This is the precise status of Planck-scale QG in gravitational-wave data: below the current union horizon, which is a report about instruments, not a verdict about the world.

## 3. What licenses a name

Two things, both outside the shape itself:

1. **The ground settles it.** `THE_FORMULA.md` step 5: *"the ground settles it. Verified only by what pays. A gate is not the ground."* The README: the framework *"stops at the limit of what the available instruments can distinguish, and records the lineage so the stop is honest."*
2. **A provenanced import from a frame whose closure differs**, with a source gate and a shadow test (Fig 6 :23, Fig 8 :50-52), debited against independence.

*The Hard Problem is About Source* gives the mechanism that makes this more than a rule. An instrument reads green and *"cannot comment on the source"*: one green light, or yellow plus blue, or RGB, or CMYK, or a scheme not yet discovered. The reading does not determine the source. *"If several paths create the same behavior, then behavior is a projection with lossy information"*, and the case was settled *"not by argument, but by the arrival of a better instrument."* Applied here: a strain waveform that matches general relativity does not tell us which microscopic theory produced it, and a null echo search does not tell us there is no near-horizon structure. What is owed is a discriminator, not a name.

*They're Scanning Books When They Should* sharpens the status of the vocabulary: *"A minimum viable set of six terms is a human's careful guess at reality's compression axes; another sensorium's minimum set need not be those six"*, and *"Declaring the primitives does not make them neutral. It makes the choice visible."* It also names the genuinely hard case: *"Reality's structure along axes no primitive captures is invisible to everyone, and the invisibility cannot be fixed by more sampling, because it lives in the representation, not the sample."* So a null GW search can mean either "not there" or "not representable in the observables we currently build", and from inside the frame those look the same.

## 4. What the essays say about the instrument specifically

- *How a Society of Agents Would Work* states the Fig 6 rule plainly: *"General, but not unlimited. It can learn whatever its senses and actions let it reach."* and quotes Fig 6: *"Composition explores what the operators reach; instruments extend what can be represented at all."* A detector extension changes what can be represented; it does not prove the theory.
- The same essay quotes Fig 9 :60 - *"Use filters for the budget and witnesses for the verdict. Never let a filter hand you a verdict."* This is why detector precision cannot be the witness in this question.
- *Arrival of the Fittest: The Tether Architecture* states the demo caveat directly: LLM output is *"simulacra"* and *"the solutions presented were not contrived to fit the formula"* could never be verified. This is the ground of my own honesty statement in `01`.

## 5. The shape claim here is an ordinary term

`THE_FORMULA.md` "Direction" says a frame cannot check the direction of its own question, because the shape it seeks is a preference and not a term. The remedy is a discipline: write down what an answer would look like, bind it, and let it accrue a residual like anything else.

I did that in `01_figure_grounded_audit.md` §0 with `TEST_QG_GW(d, O, G, epsilon)`. That is what makes the claim refutable by RF1 to RF5. Without it, "quantum gravity tests research" is just a field name and any result could be read as progress.

**Correction to my own §3.** I initially read *"shape, not a name"* as forbidding the deviation parameters themselves. That overstates it. The README's phrase governs a *missing* primitive. Dispersion coefficients, a graviton mass and Lorentz-violating coefficients are already bound terms in the phenomenology frame; stating them is a binding from that frame, not the invention of a new primitive. What remains forbidden is naming which QG theory is correct on the strength of the framework. Both statements are recorded here so neither is quietly lost.

## 6. Prestige is not action, and my audit is a simulation

*The Recursive Self-Improvement Trap*: *"Prestige must never be allowed to validate an action directly... Prestige can fund an action. It can give an action a budget to be tried. But it cannot settle an action. Only the ground can settle it. The Ground Maintainer must be blind to prestige."*

A campaign that gathers respected names to agree that quantum gravity is testable is the named collapse, not corroboration. The legitimate ask gives the claim a budget to be tried (public data, a workshop, a submission) and lets the detector settle it.

Same essay on my own status: *"LLMs can quickly simulate an approximate answer/output with the formula"*, but *"the truly testable output would need to be derived from the real architecture or working the formula yourself"*, and the outputs are *"simulacra"*. So `01_figure_grounded_audit.md` is a simulated derivation checked for internal consistency against the figures. It is not a test of the question. It cannot settle anything it reports on.
