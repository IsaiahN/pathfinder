# The Tether's answer on high-precision gravitational-wave tests of quantum gravity

**Mode: `specified` for the shape claims, `general` for the structural rules, not `grounded`.** This is a shape and a route, not a detector result. Simulated derivation checked against the figures (`05_what_shape_means.md` §6), not a ground reading.

## 1. The residual it answers

GR and candidate QG models agree in their own domains and differ at their contact: a detector returns a strain series that matches classical GR to high precision, while QG models predict deviations that have not registered. The contact is a **union compound** of three conditions (a deviation stated in effect terms, an observable channel, an independently readable ground), not one hard measurement. Fig 9 calls a case in several conditions *"a case in two conditions is not a harder case of either"*. It has its own loop and its own ground.

## 2. The shape

1. **The detector is the ground; the model is not.** Fig 1 :23: **"The ground enters as the only metric."** Fig 1 :27: **"They are frame-internal. A frame cannot score itself with a quantity it also produces."** A QG model's coherence is frame-internal. Only a measurement, an experiment, a counter can settle it (Fig 2 :29). This refuses option A as stated.

2. **A test is a three-part ordinary term.** Bind it as `TEST_QG_GW(d, O, G, epsilon)`: state the deviation `d`, range the observable `O` against the detector ground `G`, return either a settled nonzero `d` or a bound `|d| < epsilon`. This is what makes the claim refutable; without it "quantum gravity" is an unbound preference (Formula "Direction").

3. **Precision is a budget filter, never a witness.** Fig 9 :60: **"Use filters for the budget and witnesses for the verdict. Never let a filter hand you a verdict."** Better sensitivity narrows the bound. It does not make a deviation real. The two quantities are in different units and must be held apart.

4. **The scale gap is a reach gap, not a proof of absence.** Fig 8 :39: **"So a search that finds nothing is never a proof of absence."** Fig 13 :134: **"Unreachable is a verdict here, not a silence"**. Current searches are a live SEARCHED and INWARD track: instruments already return strain, ringdown and squeezed-light readings that fail to resolve at the Planck scale. That is different from underivable in principle. Formula step 7 says the two facts issue different instructions, and importing against a budget limit adds an atom that was not needed.

5. **Report per channel.** Fig 1 :19: **"The residual is indexed by object slot, not aggregated."** Speed, dispersion and graviton mass, near-horizon structure and quantum-optical statistics are distinct slots. A single "quantum gravity excluded" number throws away the only signal that says where to look.

6. **Agreement is not corroboration.** Fig 2 :35: **"Alignment is triangulation, not negotiation. A mirror cannot correct you."** Two pipelines reading the same public data are one witness. Independent instruments, ranged separately against the ground, can add reach (Fig 8 :45). Buy that independence in proportion to the cost of being wrong (Fig 2 :42).

7. **The theory frame is a candidate-source, not a ground-bearing domain.** Fig 6 :23: **"No frame escapes its own closure. The bound moves only on contact with a frame whose closure differs."** QG models supply candidate terms to the library; their import needs a source gate and a shadow test and is debited against independence. The world literature says the same in its own terms: more precise model predictions must precede better constraints.

## 3. What it explicitly does not say

- Not that any quantum-gravity theory is correct, and not a derivation of one theory from another.
- Not that a null result excludes quantum gravity, or that a bound is an absence.
- Not that a specific deviation (a particular dispersion exponent, echo delay or graviton mass) is predicted by the framework. Those are bindings from the physics frame, recorded as such.
- Not a final detector or a last experiment. Fig 4 permits no top, so the reach stays open.
- Not grounded. No measurement at the Planck scale is claimed here, and this derivation is a simulation.

## 4. What would settle it: the ground

The world sources already report grounded per-channel readings, but this synthesis did not take them. The instruments that would move the status from `candidate` to `grounded` are:

- **An actual deviation:** a high-precision observation returning a deviation uniquely attributable to a QG effect and settling a candidate model against the detector (RF1). This is the decisive refuter.
- **A source-discriminating instrument for the existing readings:** an observable that distinguishes "no near-horizon structure" from "near-horizon structure with no echo at our sensitivity". The essays' metamerism lesson applies: a green reading cannot comment on its source until a spectrometer arrives.
- **An OUTWARD import:** a QG frame that already predicts a deviation at reachable scales, stated in effect terms, with provenance and a shadow test. This is a different track from INWARD and must not be read against it.
- **Standing structural falsifiers:** a three-condition partition instead of a union; a detector ground shown to update on the theory's predictions; a legitimate aggregate verdict with no per-channel attribution.

## 5. The honest stop

**Unreached at this budget, not underivable in principle.** The six-term set is a visible guess at the compression axes, not nature's own list, and a different instrumentarium need not share it. The answer stops where the instruments stop and records the lineage so the stop is checkable. *"Indiscernibility is not identity"* is the rule: a projection that no current instrument distinguishes from the thing is still a projection, which is why the per-channel bounds and the refuters must be kept even when the data look clean.

## 6. One-paragraph version for outreach

> The Tether does not name a winning theory of quantum gravity, and it does not claim that more detector precision will eventually settle one. It says that a gravitational-wave test of quantum gravity is a three-part compound: a deviation stated as an ordinary term, an observable channel, and a detector ground that can be read independently. The detector, not the model's coherence, is the ground. Precision is a budget filter, never a witness; a null result is a bound, never a proof of absence; and the Planck-scale reach gap is unreachable at this budget, which is a different fact from underivable in principle. Per-channel bounds must stay per channel, and agreement among analyses sharing one public data pool is one witness, not several. What the framework offers is the shape any valid test must have, four refuters written in advance, and the instrument that would move the question off `candidate`. The reach stays open until the ground settles it.
