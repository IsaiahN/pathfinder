# Expert verification packet: the Tether on high-precision GW tests of quantum gravity

**What this is:** a claim in a form a gravitational-wave physicist or phenomenologist can check. Mode, derivation and refuters are stated before the conclusion, so a reader knows what to look for and what would settle it.

**Mode: `specified` for the shape claims, `general` for the structural rules, not `grounded`.** No Planck-scale measurement is claimed. This is a shape and a route, not a result. Simulated derivation checked for internal consistency against the figures, not a ground reading.

## 1. The claim, in one paragraph

A high-precision gravitational-wave test of quantum gravity is a three-condition union compound, not one measurement. It needs (i) a deviation stated as an ordinary term with a mapping to an observable, (ii) an observable channel, and (iii) a detector ground that can be read independently. Bind it as `TEST_QG_GW(d, O, G, epsilon)`: state `d`, range the observable `O` against the ground `G`, and return either a measured nonzero `d` or a bound `|d| < epsilon`. The detector is the ground; a model's internal coherence is not. Precision is a budget filter, never a witness, and a null result is a bound, never a proof of absence. The Planck-scale reach gap is unreachable at this budget, which is different from underivable in principle. Current reports constrain per-channel phenomenology; no quantum-gravity signature has registered.

### C2 - physics-facing; checkable by a gravitational-wave physicist against the reported bounds

- **Statement:** the two classic bounds are per-channel constraints, not detections and not exclusions. GW170817 constrains the propagation-speed difference and Lorentz violation from an arrival-time delay. GW150914 constrains the graviton Compton wavelength under a dispersion assumption and reports no evidence for violations of general relativity. Echo and ringdown searches are separate channels, not independent confirmations of the same one.
- **What to check:** are those characterizations faithful to the papers? Does the bound depend on a stated assumption (for example dispersion like a massive particle)? Are the analyses drawing on one LVK data pool, which would make their agreement one witness rather than two?
- **Source, world:** LIGO/Virgo/Fermi/INTEGRAL, ApJL 848:L13 (2017), arXiv:1710.05834; LIGO/Virgo, PRL 116, 221101 (2016), arXiv:1602.03841; Maggio, Pani and Raposo, arXiv:2105.06410.
- **Refuter:** produce a reported high-precision result that returns a deviation uniquely attributable to quantum gravity and settles a candidate model against the detector. That fires RF1, extends the reach and refutes the "unreachable at this budget" verdict.

SEARCHED asks whether the deviation is in the closure and unfound; INWARD extends an instrument already returning something; OUTWARD imports a frame whose closure predicts the deviation's shape. They are never read against each other. A null result is a bound, not an absence.
- **Source, verbatim:** Fig 8 :39 - **"So a search that finds nothing is never a proof of absence."** Fig 9 :44 - **"Only a sealed room can be searched to the end, and no frame is a sealed room. - **Refuter:** show that the null results already obtained are proof of absence (that would make the figure's no-proof-of-absence rule false for this domain), or show a signature registered by an instrument for which nothing at any resolution was previously failing to resolve (which would refute the Fig 6 proto-instrument application, RF3).

## 3. What is explicitly not claimed

- Not a theory of everything and not a last detector. The reach stays open.
- Not that any quantum-gravity theory is correct, and not a derivation of one theory from another.
- Not that a specific dispersion exponent, echo delay or graviton mass is predicted by the framework. Those are bindings from the physics frame, provenanced as such.
- Not that a null result excludes a theory, and not that a bound is an absence.
- Not `grounded`. Nothing here is asserted as settled by this derivation. World sources report their own grounded measurements; this synthesis did not take them.
- Not that agreement among analyses is corroboration. Two pipelines on one data pool are one witness.

## 5. What would settle it

A source-discriminating instrument or an actual deviation, not more argument. Concretely:

1. Does any current or planned channel return a deviation that is uniquely attributable to quantum gravity and survives an independent ground reading? (RF1)
2. Is there an observable that distinguishes "no near-horizon structure" from "near-horizon structure with no echo at our sensitivity"? If not, the null echo result is a bound under our current representation, not an absence.
3. Does a QG frame already predict a deviation at reachable scales in effect terms, with a source gate and a shadow test? That is an OUTWARD import, not more SEARCHED effort.
4. Do two claimed confirmations draw on disjoint evidence (different detectors, independent calibration and priors)? If not, they are one witness.

## 6. What an expert can check in twenty minutes

1. Read Fig 1 :27, Fig 2 :29, Fig 8 :39, Fig 9 :44 and Fig 9 :60, five short passages.
2. Check C2 against the two abstracts (arXiv:1710.05834 and arXiv:1602.03841): are they bounds under stated assumptions, or detections?

4. Say which of RF1 to RF5 they would fire first, and why. Send the refutation. A failure is more useful than an endorsement.
