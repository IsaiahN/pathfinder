## Wider literature that bears on the question

These are the world sources used as secondary corroboration. Each was opened. Full provenance is in `10_source_ledger.csv`; the field and overlap of each is recorded there.

1. **GW170817 / speed of gravity.** LIGO Scientific Collaboration, Virgo, Fermi GBM and INTEGRAL, *The Astrophysical Journal Letters* 848:L13 (2017), arXiv:1710.05834. The paper says it uses the 1.74 s delay to *"constrain the difference between the speed of gravity and the speed of light to be between -3×10^-15 and +7×10^-16 times the speed of light"* and to *"place new bounds on the violation of Lorentz invariance"*. This is a per-channel bound, not an exclusion of quantum gravity. It is the clearest real instance of the shape claim: a deviation stated as a term, an observable (arrival-time difference), a ground (two independently observed messengers) and a bound.
2. **GW150914 / graviton mass and model tests.** LIGO Scientific and Virgo Collaborations, *Physical Review Letters* 116, 221101 (2016), arXiv:1602.03841. The abstract states *"We constrain the graviton Compton wavelength, assuming that gravitons are dispersed in vacuum in the same way as particles with mass, obtaining a 90%-confidence lower bound of 10^13 km"* and concludes *"we find no evidence for violations of general relativity in the genuinely strong-field regime of gravity."* Note the mode: a bound under a stated assumption, and a null result reported as no evidence, not as absence.
3. **Quantum modifications to black-hole structure.** Steven B. Giddings, *Classical and Quantum Gravity* 33 (2016) 235010, arXiv:1602.03622. The abstract warns that *"the size of these scales relative to those probed by observation of inspiral signals is important in determining the feasibility of finding experimental signatures"* and names the route forward: *"to develop more precise predictions from scenarios proposing quantum modifications"* and to search data for deviations from numerical relativity. This is the vocabulary-link finding of F0/F1 stated in physics language.
4. **Compact objects and echoes.** Elisa Maggio, Paolo Pani and Guilherme Raposo, *Handbook of Gravitational Wave Astronomy* (Springer, 2021), arXiv:2105.06410. The abstract lists *"ringdown tests, and searches for near-horizon structures with gravitational-wave echoes"* as separate test channels. This is per-slot reporting in practice (F9).
5. **Quantum signatures in nonlinear gravitational waves.** Thiago Guerreiro et al., *Quantum* 6, 879 (2022), arXiv:2111.01779. The paper asks *"whether measurable quantum predictions that depart from classical gravity, analogous to quantum optics effects which cannot be explained by classical electrodynamics, can be found"* and studies squeezed-coherent gravitational waves and state reconstruction. This is an OUTWARD track (a different frame holding a deviation at reachable scales), not a claim that current detectors have seen it.
6. **Experimental search for quantum gravity.** Sabine Hossenfelder, arXiv:1010.3420 (CQG chapter, 2011). The survey *"offer[s] a brief survey of existent and planned experimental tests for quantum gravity"* and organizes it around phenomenological models with and without a lowered Planck scale. Useful as a map of candidate-source terms, not as a ground.
7. **Quantum metrology for gravitational-wave astronomy.** Roman Schnabel, Nergis Mavalvala, David E. McClelland and Ping Koy Lam, *Nature Communications* 1, 121 (2010), arXiv:2411.07313. The abstract says squeezed light *"is able to quantum entangle the high-power laser fields in the interferometer arms, and could play a key role in the realization of gravitational wave astronomy"*. This is an INWARD instrument extension from a device already returning something, exactly the Fig 6 :32-35 pattern.

**Overlap warning.** Sources 1 to 4 and 7 draw on the LIGO/Virgo/KAGRA instrument and data ecosystem. Their agreement is one evidence pool talking, not independent corroboration. Source 5 (quantum optics) and the framework essays are lower-overlap frames. The verdict in `09_watson_answer.md` comes from the figures, not from counting these sources.

## Distribution rules: offer, do not push

- **Fig 4 :22-25** - *"Only methods cross up. Only head starts come down. A recording carried upward looks like knowledge and is a description of one occasion."* Sending the full architecture before anyone has described a residual sends a recording. Lead with the shape claim and its refuter; offer links after.
- **Fig 10 :42** - *"A verifier that can reconstruct the claim cannot verify it."* A recipient who has absorbed the framework before being asked for a verdict has joined the frame being checked.
- **Fig 9 :27** - *"describe it before you go looking, or any frame you pick will seem to fit"*. Ask what they would expect to see if the shape claim were true before showing the article.
- **Fig 2 :40** - *"disqualifying for a verdict: agreement about the world is worthless when the agreeing frames share an evidence pool"*. A send selected for people likely to agree produces a convention, not corroboration.

**Recommended order in a reply:** (1) the shape claim and its four refuters; (2) ask what they would count as a failure, and record the answer; (3) only then the links, with the note that the figures, not the summary, are the authority.
