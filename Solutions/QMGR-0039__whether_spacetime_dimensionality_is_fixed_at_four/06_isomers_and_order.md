# Fixed versus emergent: a supporting derivation on three axes

**Mode: `specified`.** This is a simulated derivation checked against the figures, not a ground reading (see `05_what_shape_means.md` section 6).

## RESIDUAL

"Fixed at four, or emergent from quantum dynamics" reads as one either/or. It is actually three predicates that come apart: **status** (primitive versus derived), **value** (which integer), **mechanism** (which dynamics generates it). The residual is whether these three can be collapsed into one axis, and if not, what order they must be answered in.

## DERIVATION

### Q1 : Can "fixed" and "emergent" be one axis? NO.

- Fig 13 :41: **"Each is indivisible: no arrangement of the other five yields it."** This is a claim about **status** of the six.
- Fig 13 :124: **"Smoothness, symmetry, chaos and discreteness are each what a particular composition leaves behind, and none is a seventh member."** This is a claim about **residues**.
- A thing can be primitive in status and have any value; a thing can be derived in status and have a stable value. The two predicates do not determine each other.
- Fig 9 :55: **"A bundled problem is usually a union of overlapping conditions, not a list of separate ones."** The question is a union. It needs unbundling, not a single choice. **Falsifier:** exhibit a frame in which status and value are the same quantity.

### Q2 : What order must they be answered in?

- Fig 3 :20 to :21: *"The chain is ordered by dependency, not by importance. Each link consumes what the one above produces."*
- Order: (1) status, from the six-term census; (2) value, from an instrument; (3) mechanism, from a provenanced import. Asking the value before the status is a reading below the break: Fig 3 :23 *"a reading of nothing."*
- This is why F4 is a `gap` and not a failure: the value question sits downstream and cannot be answered by the figure that decides status. **Falsifier:** show that a value can be settled without first settling whether the quantity is primitive.

### Q3 : Is dimensionality an isomer of anything under Fig 12?

- Fig 12 :117: **"Same atoms, different bond, different substance. The difference is invisible in the formula and decisive in the behaviour."**
- Chemistry's precedent (Fig 12 :97): *"silver fulminate and silver cyanate have the same composition and different properties."*
- Dimensionality is **not** an isomer of the six: it is not built from the same atoms under a different bond. It is a **residue** of compositions over the six (Fig 13 :124). Calling it an isomer would be a type error, the same error as calling the continuum a seventh member.
- The genuine isomer relation is between two *compositions* that use the same six atoms with different bonds. A scale-4 composition and a scale-2 composition of the same substrate could be isomers in this sense. That is a testable structural claim, and it is the one worth taking to a physicist. **Falsifier:** show the two scale regimes share no atom, which kills the isomer reading and leaves the residue reading standing.

### Q4 : What is "fixed" when it is fixed?

- Fig 12 :104: **"irreducible is a fact about a level and never about the world"** (the full line continues *"and there is no bottom"*).
- Fig 4 :33: *"there is no top and no infinite regress."*
- So "fixed at four" can only mean fixed **relative to a level** at which a four-dimensional spacetime is a settled molecule. It cannot mean fixed in the world without qualification. This dissolves the apparent contradiction between the substrate being fixed (six kinds) and dimensionality being derived. Different objects, different levels.
- Analogy: a settled molecule becomes an atom for whatever composes over it. A stable four-dimensional regime can be an atom for the next level without being a primitive of the world. **Falsifier:** exhibit a level at which four-ness is irreducible without reference to any composing level.

### Q5 : Is the mechanism question even well posed?

- Fig 6 :31: *"Neither answer tells you much about the other. Run them as two tracks, and never read one against the other."* Inward (sharpen our instrument) and outward (find a frame that already predicts the shape) are opposed.
- "Emerges from quantum dynamics" is an **outward** answer naming a frame. The spectral-dimension and causal-set results are **inward** answers sharpening an instrument. They do not corroborate each other as mechanism versus measurement; they are different tracks.
- The world source that complicates this is Schneider (2024): *"descriptions of spacetime in general relativity irreducibly include global physical content, which is not effective."* So even the philosophical claim "spacetime emergence is effective description" is not automatic. Recorded as a strain, not smoothed. **Falsifier:** show that the inward and outward answers converge on the same mechanism with low overlap.

## CONCLUSION

The question is a union on three axes, not a single either/or. Status is decided by the figures: dimensionality is a residue, not a primitive member. Value is a `gap` owed to instruments. Mechanism is an unproven binding, with inward and outward tracks that must not be read against each other. "Fixed" is level-relative, and dimensionality is not an isomer of the six but could be a residue relation between two scale regimes of one substrate. This framing rescues what is defensible in both options without letting either hand us a verdict.
