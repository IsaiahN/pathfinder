## VERDICT (from the figures)

The figures decide **status**, not **value** or **mechanism**. Dimensionality is a compositional residue, on the same side as the continuum, smoothness, symmetry, chaos and discreteness, and it is not a primitive member of a six-term substrate that fixes kinds and not counts. Spacetime is itself a composition, so its dimensionality is downstream of one. "Fixed" is level-relative. The integer four is an honest `gap`, owed to an instrument (extra-dimension bounds, scale-dependent dimension estimators), and "emerges from quantum dynamics" is an unproven binding needing provenance. This is `specified` / `general` / `gap`, ungrounded. It is not a theory of everything and it does not certify itself.

- **cost if wrong:** bounded and locatable. The status claim is mode-labeled, the value is an honest `gap`, and the mechanism is withheld, so an error is findable rather than invisible. The real cost is asserting a figure result that does not exist, which would spend the independence of outside readers (Fig 2 :42 "Buy independence in proportion to the cost of being wrong."). That cost is avoidable by leading with the shape claim and its refuter.
- **undoable:** yes. The value and mechanism claims can be added as imports with provenance once an instrument settles them, and the status claim stands independently. No primitive has to be withdrawn. A sent claim can be superseded by the corrected packet.
- **reversed by:** a ground reading, never agreement. An instrument showing dimensionality is irreducible at every scale with no running and no extra dimensions would break the physics corroboration; a figure re-census admitting dimensionality as a primitive member, or a derivation of the integer four from the six, would break the figures verdict; a provenanced inward/outward convergence would clear the mechanism binding. Until one of these fires, the verdict stands as `candidate`.
