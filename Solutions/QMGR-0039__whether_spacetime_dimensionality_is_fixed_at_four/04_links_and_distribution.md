## Two cautions that bear on the pitch

1. **The CDT and causal-set results are one witness on the d=2 claim.** Both draw on the same broad dimensional-reduction result pool. Fig 2 :18 to :21: *"one evidence pool... they never talk, and still agree."* Do not present them as independent corroboration of the same number.
2. **The physics sources corroborate a shape, not the framework.** They support "dimensionality is scale-dependent and derived." They do not support any claim that the Tether derived that result. The canonical article already tags its own black-hole entry as *an instance*, a case arrived at independently. Keep the same discipline here.

## Distribution: offer, do not push

Figure-grounded reasons:

- **Fig 4 :22 to :25** : *"Only methods cross up. Only head starts come down. A recording carried upward looks like knowledge and is a description of one occasion."* Sending the full architecture before anyone has described a residual sends a recording, not a generator. Lead with the shape claim and its refuter; offer the links after.
- **Fig 10 :42** : *"A verifier that can reconstruct the claim cannot verify it."* A recipient who has absorbed the framework before being asked for a verdict has joined the frame being checked.
- **Fig 9 :27 to :28** : *"describe it before you go looking, or any frame you pick will seem to fit."* Ask what they would expect to see if the shape claim were true before giving them the article.
- **Seat Map :163** : *"High correlation is disqualifying for a verdict and efficient for a convention."* A mass send selected for people who already agree produces a convention, not corroboration.

**Recommended order in a reply:** (1) the shape claim and its refuter; (2) ask what they would count as a failure; (3) then the links, with the note that the figures, not the summary, are the authority. Record the stage-1 verdict before any theory is shown.

- **Stage 1 before stage 2** is Fig 10 :52 : *"Bring a characterised residual, never the source"* : and Fig 2 :45 : *"Frames range against the ground. They do not negotiate with it."* The recipient checks the output without holding the framework's interior.
