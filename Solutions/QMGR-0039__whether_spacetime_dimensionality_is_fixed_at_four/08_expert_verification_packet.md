# Expert verification packet: the Tether on spacetime dimensionality

**What this is:** a claim in a form a physicist or philosopher of physics can check. Mode, derivation and refuters are stated before the conclusion, so a reader knows what to look for and what would settle it.

**Mode: `specified` on the status claim, `gap` on the number and mechanism. Not `grounded`.** No measurement at the Planck scale is claimed by the derivation. This is a shape and a route, not a result.

## 1. The claim, in one paragraph

The six-term substrate fixes irreducible kinds (space, time, energy as states; spectrum, gradient, network as structures), not a count of spacetime dimensions. The continuum was removed as a seventh member because its members already produce it, and smoothness, symmetry, chaos and discreteness are explicitly what a composition leaves behind. Therefore dimensionality is a **derived residue**, not a primitive member of the generating set; spacetime itself is a composition, so its dimensionality is downstream of one. The integer four is not figure-governed (a `gap` owed to instruments), and "emerges from quantum dynamics" is an unproven binding, not a derivation. "Fixed" is level-relative, not absolute.

### C2 : physics-facing; checkable against the dimension literature

- **Statement:** the integer four is not settled by the substrate. It is an empirical value, and there is evidence that the effective dimension is scale-dependent.
- **What to check:** Ambjørn, Jurkiewicz and Loll (PRL 95:171301, 2005) report *"While four-dimensional on large scales, the quantum universe appears two-dimensional at short distances."* Carlip (arXiv:1506.08775, 2015) reports *"Results from a number of different approaches to quantum gravity suggest that the effective dimension of spacetime may drop to d=2 at small scales."* The Particle Data Group review lists *"tabletop experiments exploring gravity at sub-mm distances, and collider experiments"* as constraints on extra dimensions. Is a scale-dependent, instrument-settled dimension a fair description of the current evidence?
- **Refuter:** an instrument showing dimensionality is irreducible at every scale, with no running and no extra dimensions, would break the physics corroboration. Note that CDT and causal-set papers share the d=2 result pool and are one witness on that specific claim, not two.

### C3 : framework-internal derivation

- **Source, verbatim:**

## 3. What is explicitly not claimed

- Not that the framework derives or confirms the number four. That is a `gap`.
- Not that dimensionality emerges specifically from quantum dynamics. That is a frame binding needing provenance.
- Not that the continuum is forbidden. Removal from the generating set is not a ban on the composed continuum.
- Not that CDT and causal sets independently corroborate the same number (shared evidence pool).
- Not a theory of everything, not `grounded`, and not a claim that the framework has certified its own completeness.
