**Subject:** Check one structural claim before you decide whether the framework is worth reading

> Hi [name],
>
> I have one specific output I would like you to check against what you already know. I am not asking you to read the theory first, and I am not asking you to agree with it. If the output does not survive, there is nothing else to look at.
>
> **The output (`specified` on the status claim, `gap` on the number):**
>
> 1. The framework's minimum set has six terms in two kinds, states (space, time, energy) and structures (spectrum, gradient, network). What it fixes is the list of kinds, not any count of spacetime dimensions. Fig 13 asks "WHY SIX, AND WHY NOT FIVE OR SEVEN" and rules that six members are indivisible and that no arranging produces a seventh.
> 2. The continuum was proposed as a seventh member and removed, because it is something the members already produce. Smoothness, symmetry, chaos and discreteness are explicitly described as what a composition leaves behind, none of them a seventh member. So dimensionality, in so far as it is the smoothness or discreteness of the continuum approximation, sits on the **derived** side. This is a structural claim about status, not a measurement and not a derivation of quantum dynamics.
> 3. Spacetime itself is a derivative under the framework: "It is not space-time but space, a structure, time." So its dimensionality is downstream of a composition, not a primitive property.
>
> **What I am explicitly not claiming:** that the framework derives the number four, or that it derives the number from quantum dynamics. No figure line mentions 3+1 or fixes an integer count. I record that as a `gap`, not a silence. The integer is owed to instruments (tabletop gravity tests, collider bounds on extra dimensions, spectral-dimension estimators). Quantum dynamics as the specific mechanism is a binding from the physics frame, and I am not presenting it as figure-derived.
>
> **What would refute the shape claim:**
> 1. derive a seventh primitive from the six, or one of the six from the other five;
> 2. show a figure line that fixes a spacetime dimension count as a primitive, or derive four from the six-term set;
> 3. show that dimensionality is a member of the generating set rather than what a composition leaves behind;
> 4. at the ground, an instrument showing dimensionality is irreducible at every scale, with no running and no extra dimensions.
>
> I wrote these down before sending this. If any holds, I retract or narrow the claim. A one-line "this fails because..." is more useful to me than agreement. If you would rather not engage, no reply is needed.
