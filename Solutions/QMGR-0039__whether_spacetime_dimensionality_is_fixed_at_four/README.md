## Question restated as an effect

Two things are being decided and they are not the same thing. (1) The **status** of dimensionality: is a dimension count a primitive, irreducible item a theory starts with, or a quantity that a composition leaves behind? (2) The **value and mechanism**: is the count exactly four, and does it arise specifically from quantum dynamics? Option A (fixed at four) leads to a primitive integer that the six-term substrate would have to list among its irreducibles. Option B (emerges from quantum dynamics) leads to a derived quantity with a named mechanism that the figures would have to license. What an answer would look like: a shape that says which of these the substrate can support, a clear statement of what the figures cannot say, and refuters written before the run.

**Bound answer:** the figures decide the status question (dimensionality is on the derived side, like the continuum that was removed as a seventh member), but they do not fix the integer and do not name quantum dynamics as the mechanism. Those two are an honest `gap`, settled only by instruments.
