# The Tether's answer on spacetime dimensionality

**Mode: `specified` on the status verdict, `general` on the shape, `gap` on the number and the mechanism. Not `grounded`.** This is a shape and a route, not a result. Simulated derivation checked against the figures (`05_what_shape_means.md` section 6), not a ground reading.

## 1. The residual it answers

"Fixed at four, or emergent from quantum dynamics" is a union of three predicates: status, value, mechanism (Fig 9 :55). The figures decide the first, are silent on the second, and do not license the third as stated. Answering them as one either/or manufactures a false choice.

## 2. The shape

1. **The substrate fixes kinds, not counts.** Fig 13 :41: *"Each is indivisible: no arrangement of the other five yields it."* Fig 13 :116: *"Six members, and no amount of arranging produces a seventh"*. What is fixed is the list of six irreducible kinds: space, time, energy as states; spectrum, gradient, network as structures. No spacetime dimension count is among them.

2. **Dimensionality is a compositional residue, not a member.** The continuum was proposed as a seventh and removed because it is produced by the members (Fig 13 :43 to :44). Fig 13 :124: *"Smoothness, symmetry, chaos and discreteness are each what a particular composition leaves behind, and none is a seventh member."* Dimensionality, in so far as it is the smoothness or discreteness of the continuum approximation, sits on the derived side. This is the figure-grounded content of "emergent" here: derived by composition, not primitive.

3. **Spacetime is itself a derivative.** Fig 13 :75: *"Two states never bond directly. It is not space-time but space, a structure, time; and two structures join only through a state."* So its dimensionality is downstream of a composition and cannot be a starting primitive.

4. **The integer four is a `gap`.** No figure line mentions 3+1, four dimensions or any count. Composing the six never yields the integer four, because the arrangements are unbounded while the kinds are fixed (Fig 13 :115). Fig 13 :134: *"Unreachable is a verdict here, not a silence"*; Fig 8 :39: *"So a search that finds nothing is never a proof of absence."* The number is owed to an instrument, not a figure.

5. **The mechanism is not named by the framework.** The figures say dimensionality is compositional but do not name a generator. "Emerges from quantum dynamics" binds a physics frame term onto the vocabulary. Fig 8 :50 to :52: *"Convergent derivation and adopted import look identical in the contents."* Without provenance it carries zero evidentiary weight.

6. **"Fixed" is level-relative.** Fig 12 :104: *"irreducible is a fact about a level and never about the world"*. A stable four-dimensional regime can be fixed *at a level* without being a primitive fact about the world. This dissolves the apparent contradiction between points 1 and 2.

## 3. What it explicitly does not say

- Not that the framework derives or confirms the number four. That is a `gap`.
- Not that the framework derives dimensionality from quantum dynamics. That is a binding.
- Not that the continuum is forbidden. Removal from the *generating set* is not a ban on the derived continuum.
- Not a theory of everything and not a final room. The value and mechanism questions stay open.
- Not `grounded`. No measurement was taken in this derivation.

## 4. What would settle it: the ground

An instrument, not a further argument. From *The Hard Problem is About Source*: a reading of green *"cannot comment on the source."* Here the discriminating questions are:

- **Value:** do tabletop gravity tests and collider searches bound extra dimensions down to a scale where "four" is the only live option, or do they leave room? The Particle Data Group review is the standing anchor.
- **Status at the ground:** does the spectral dimension actually run with scale, as CDT and causal-set estimators report, or is that a lattice artefact? A measurement that shows dimensionality is irreducible at every scale, with no running and no extra dimensions, would break the physics corroboration.
- **Mechanism:** an inward instrument (a sharper dimension estimator) and an outward frame (a provenanced quantum-dynamics derivation) are two tracks. Fig 6 :31: *"Run them as two tracks, and never read one against the other."*
- **Standing falsifiers:** derive a seventh primitive from the six; show a figure line that fixes 3+1 as primitive; show dimensionality is a member of the generating set rather than a residue.

## 5. The honest stop

**Unreached, not unreachable.** The six-term set is *"a human's careful guess at reality's compression axes"* (from *They're Scanning Books*, carried from the exemplar). Declaring it makes the choice visible, not neutral. The answer stops where the instruments stop and records the lineage so the stop is checkable. *"Indiscernibility is not identity."*

## 6. One-paragraph version for outreach

> The Tether does not say spacetime is fixed at four, and it does not derive dimensionality from quantum dynamics. It says the six-term substrate fixes irreducible **kinds**, not dimension counts; that the continuum was removed as a seventh member because it is produced by the members, and that smoothness, symmetry, chaos and discreteness are what a composition leaves behind; so dimensionality is a **derived residue**, not a primitive. Spacetime itself is a composition, so its dimensionality is downstream of one. The integer four is an honest `gap`: no figure line gives it, and it is owed to instruments such as extra-dimension bounds and scale-dependent dimension estimators. The specific mechanism is an unproven binding, not a figure result. What the framework offers is the shape any answer must have, falsifiers written in advance, and the instruments that would close it. The value stays open until the ground settles it.
