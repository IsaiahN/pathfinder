## 0. The residual in effect terms

The question arrives as a two-option choice: dimensionality is **fixed at four**, or it **emerges from quantum dynamics**. Stated as an effect, we want to know what a theory must *start with* versus what it can *leave behind*.

- If dimensionality is a primitive, then a count must appear in the irreducible set. A theory that does not list it has not accounted for it.
- If dimensionality is derived, then it must be a composition over things that are themselves irreducible. A theory that merely renames it has not derived it.
- If neither, the question is malformed and must be split before it is searched again (Fig 9 :55).

The residual R is therefore not one measurement. It is a **status question** (primitive versus derived) bundled with a **value question** (why four) and a **mechanism question** (why quantum dynamics). Fig 9 :57 names the move: *"a case in two conditions is not a harder case of either. It stops being a mixture and becomes a specific compound."*

What an answer would look like, bound as an ordinary term before the run (formula step "Direction"): a statement of which quantities the six-term substrate fixes, which it leaves to composition, and which it cannot address at all.

## 3. Findings

### F1 : The substrate fixes six *kinds*, not a dimension count (`general`, triangulated)

Fig 13 :40 and :41:

> **"WHY SIX, AND WHY NOT FIVE OR SEVEN"**
> **"Each is indivisible: no arrangement of the other five yields it."**

Fig 13 :116:

> **"Six members, and no amount of arranging produces a seventh"**

Fig 13 :115:

> **"The set is closed. The arrangements are not. That gap is why import exists."**

The integer the figures fix is **six members in two kinds**: space, time, energy as states; spectrum, gradient, network as structures (Fig 13 :7 to :24). It is not three space dimensions plus one time dimension. Reading "six" as "four dimensions plus something" is a category error: it would treat a count of irreducible **kinds** as a count of **dimensions of a composed spacetime**. Retracted below in the log.

- **What it decides:** whatever is fixed by the substrate is fixed at the level of kinds. No figure line fixes a spacetime dimension count.
- **Refuter:** derive a seventh primitive from the six, or show the six are not indivisible. Either breaks the figure. Showing a figure line that explicitly fixes 3+1 as a primitive would also flip this finding.

### F2 : Dimensionality sits on the derived side of the substrate (`specified`, triangulated)

Fig 13 :43 to :44:

> **"A continuum was the seventh and was removed for failing exactly this"**
> **"A generating set must not contain what its own members produce."**

Fig 13 :124 to :125:

> **"Smoothness, symmetry, chaos and discreteness are each what a particular composition leaves behind, and none is a seventh member."**

`THE_FORMULA.md` substrate section (not a figure quote):

> "the derived quantities (symmetry, smoothness, chaos, a continuum) are what a turn leaves rather than members of the set."

The continuum was proposed as a seventh member and **removed** because it is a spectrum without gaps, that is, something the members already produce. Smoothness, symmetry, chaos and discreteness are explicitly classified as **what a composition leaves behind**. Dimensionality, in so far as it is the smoothness or discreteness of the continuum approximation, belongs to that derived class. This is the figure-grounded content of the word "emergent" here: derived by composition, not primitive.

- **Important limit.** This decides **status**, not **mechanism**. It does not say the derivation runs through quantum dynamics. See F5.
- **Refuter:** find a figure line that admits continuity, dimensionality or a dimension count into the generating set as an irreducible seventh; or derive dimensionality as a member rather than a residue.

### F3 : Spacetime itself is a derivative, so "spacetime dimensionality" is a property of a composed object (`general`, triangulated)

Fig 13 :74 to :75:

> **"Everything else is a composition across the two kinds, never within one."**
> **"Two states never bond directly. It is not space-time but space, a structure, time; and two structures join only through a state."**

The question's noun phrase presupposes that spacetime is a thing that *has* a dimensionality. Under Fig 13 it is not a thing with a primitive property; it is a composition with a structure in the slot. The slot is not filled by the six alone (exemplar QMGR-0037 established this for the GR/QM case). So "spacetime dimensionality" is downstream of a composition and cannot be a starting primitive.

- **Refuter:** show a direct state-state bond governing spacetime that needs no intervening structure, and that the framework must admit it.

### F4 : The specific number four is a `gap`, unreachable from the figures (`gap`, honest stop)

A genuine re-census of every figure shows no line that mentions 3+1, four dimensions, or any integer count of spacetime dimensions. Fig 13 :35 to :36 separates the kinds rather than counting them:

> **"Space, time and energy are not three readings of one quantity."**
> **"adjacency is not duration, an ordering is not a pathway, a pathway is not a direction."**

Fig 8 :39:

> **"So a search that finds nothing is never a proof of absence."**

Formula step 7 distinguishes underivable-in-principle from unreachable-at-this-budget. Here the number is **not in the closure of the six-term set at all**: composing space, time, energy, spectrum, gradient and network never yields the integer four, because the arrangements are unbounded while the kinds are fixed (Fig 13 :115 to :118). More searching inside the framework cannot produce it. Fig 13 :134:

> **"Unreachable is a verdict here, not a silence"**

- **Verdict:** `gap`. The number is owed to an instrument, not to a figure. The figures can say what dimensionality's *status* is; they cannot say what its *value* is.
- **Refuter:** exhibit a figure line that fixes the count, or a derivation of the integer four from the six-term set.

### F5 : "Emerges from quantum dynamics" is a binding from the physics frame, not a figure derivation (`single-source`, unproven)

The figures say dimensionality is compositional. They do not name a **generator**. In the canonical mapping used in QMGR-0037, quantum mechanics is the Spectrum structure organizing Energy over Time, that is, one of the six, a structure rather than the whole mechanism. To assert that quantum dynamics *is* what produces dimensionality is to bind a physics term onto the vocabulary.

Fig 8 :50 to :52:

> **"Convergent derivation and adopted import look identical in the contents."**

Fig 6 :23:

> **"No frame escapes its own closure. The bound moves only on contact with a frame whose closure differs."**

Without a source gate and a shadow test, the binding carries zero evidentiary weight. The physics sources in section 6 do supply real, independent support for scale-dependent dimensionality, but that is world corroboration of a **shape**, not a figure derivation of a **mechanism**.

- **Refuter:** show the derivation of the dimension count from quantum dynamics inside the closure, with provenance; or show that quantum dynamics adds no atom and is therefore just a rebinding of Spectrum or Gradient.

### F6 : The question is a union and must be unbundled before search (`general`, triangulated)

Fig 9 :55:

> **"A bundled problem is usually a union of overlapping conditions, not a list of separate ones."**

Fig 9 :57 to :58:

> **"a case in two conditions is not a harder case of either. It stops being a mixture and becomes a specific compound."**

"Fixed" mixes two predicates: fixed **in status** (primitive) and fixed **in value** (the integer four). They come apart. A thing can be primitive but have any value, or derived and have a stable value. The compound needs its own loop. Fig 9 :60:

> **"Use filters for the budget and witnesses for the verdict. Never let a filter hand you a verdict."**

A ranking of quantum-gravity programmes by how well they yield four is a budget filter, not a verdict.

- **Refuter:** show that status and value are the same quantity in every admissible frame, so the split is unnecessary.

### F7 : "Fixed" is level-relative, not absolute (`general`, triangulated)

Fig 12 :104:

> **"irreducible is a fact about a level and never about the world"**

Fig 13 :103 to :104 (via Fig 12): *"A settled molecule becomes an atom for whatever composes over it."* If a stable four-dimensional spacetime is a settled molecule at one level, its four-ness is fixed **for that level**, without being a primitive fact about the world. This dissolves the apparent contradiction between F1 (the substrate is fixed) and F2 (dimensionality is derived). Different objects, different levels.

- **Refuter:** exhibit a level at which four-ness is irreducible in the world rather than relative to a level.

## 4. Strain test and re-census

The apparent strain was F1 versus F2: the figures seem to *fix* the substrate (six, indivisible) while I argue dimensionality is *emergent*. Re-census resolves it. What is fixed is the **set of kinds**; what is emergent is the **continuum and its smoothness or discreteness** that arrangements leave behind. Fig 13 :124 says so directly. Different objects, so no strain. This matches the canonical usage rule that a reading making figures contradict one another has misidentified its domain.

A second apparent strain: Fig 13 :43 removes the continuum, but physics measures a spectral dimension. Re-census: removal from the *generating set* is not a claim that no continuum is composed. A derivative is exactly what a generating set must not contain. No strain.

A live strain, named: Tegmark's anthropic argument and the CDT dimensional-reduction result both bear on the number, but they come from different evidence pools and do not say the same thing (one is a selection argument for 3+1, the other a dynamical running to 2 at short scales). They are not pooled as corroboration.

## 5. Pre-registered refuter

Written before the conclusion, per Seat Map :287: *"a falsifier written before the run is a design instrument, not only an honesty device."*

The shape claim is refuted if any of the following holds:

1. A seventh primitive member is derived from the six, or one of the six is derived from the other five (breaks Fig 13 :41, :116).
2. A figure line is found that fixes a spacetime dimension count as a primitive, or derives the integer four from the six-term set (breaks F1 and F4).
3. Dimensionality is shown to be a member of the generating set rather than what a composition leaves behind (breaks Fig 13 :43-44, :124-125).
4. At the ground, an instrument shows dimensionality is an irreducible primitive at every scale, with no running and no extra dimensions (breaks the physics corroboration; record as ground-level, not figure-level).

If any fires, the shape is withdrawn or narrowed. Until one is run, no part of this may be labelled `grounded`.

## 6. World sources read (secondary, corroboration only)

Opened 2026-11-23. Full provenance in `10_source_ledger.csv`. These are independent authors in low-overlap fields, not framework essays.

- **Physics, quantum gravity.** Ambjørn, Jurkiewicz and Loll, "Spectral Dimension of the Universe", PRL 95:171301 (2005). Abstract: *"While four-dimensional on large scales, the quantum universe appears two-dimensional at short distances."* Predicts a scale-dependent, dynamically generated dimension. Corroborates F2 status (derived, not primitive). It does **not** corroborate "fixed at four."
- **Physics, anthropic selection.** Tegmark, "On the dimensionality of spacetime", Class. Quantum Grav. 14 L69 (1997). Abstract: *"all but the 3+1-dimensional one might correspond to 'dead worlds', devoid of observers."* Predicts the value is selected by observer constraints across an ensemble, not fixed by a primitive. Corroborates F4 (value is not figure-governed).
- **Physics, causal sets.** Carlip, "Dimensional reduction in causal set gravity", arXiv:1506.08775 (2015): *"Results from a number of different approaches to quantum gravity suggest that the effective dimension of spacetime may drop to d=2 at small scales."* Corroborates F2.
- **Physics, experiment.** Particle Data Group, "Review of Extra Dimensions", Demiragli and Pomarol (revised August 2025): *"Constraints on extra-dimensional models arise from astrophysical and cosmological considerations, tabletop experiments exploring gravity at sub-mm distances, and collider experiments."* This is the anchor for F8.
- **Philosophy, emergence.** O'Connor, "Emergent Properties", Stanford Encyclopedia of Philosophy (2020): *"in almost all such theories, spacetime is not part of the fundamental ontology, it being supplanted by a framework that lacks at least some of its structure."* Corroborates F3 at the conceptual level.
- **Philosophy of physics, conflict source.** Schneider, "Spacetime Emergence: An (In)effective Story", Philosophy of Physics (2024): *"descriptions of spacetime in general relativity irreducibly include global physical content, which is not effective."* This **complicates** the easy "emergence = effective description" story. It is recorded as a conflict-bearing witness, not pooled with the others.

Overlap note: the CDT and causal-set papers draw on the same broad "dimensional reduction to 2" result pool, so on that specific claim they are one witness wearing two names (Fig 2 :18 to :21). Tegmark and the PDG review both concern extra dimensions but use unrelated evidence (selection argument versus experimental bounds), so they are independent on the question of whether the integer is settled by instruments.
