## What is deliberately not here

- No personal emails of named individuals.
- No ranking of people by how likely they are to agree with the claim.
- No bulk-send plan.

Three reasons, in the framework's own words:

1. **A room does not upgrade the label.** Seat Map :283: *"A room reaching a conclusion without the ground is agreement, and agreement among frames is a weighted average of influence. Adding people raises the vocabulary and does not add an anchor."* A list of agreeable analysts is a room, not an experiment.
2. **Correlation is disqualifying for a verdict.** Fig 2 :40: *"disqualifying for a verdict: agreement about the world is worthless when the agreeing frames share an evidence pool"* and Seat Map :163: *"High correlation is disqualifying for a verdict and efficient for a convention."* Selecting recipients for receptiveness manufactures the correlation that would make their agreement worthless.
3. **Asking for trust before checking spends independence.** Fig 10 :42: *"A verifier that can reconstruct the claim cannot verify it."* Seat 4 "must not hold the interiors, the records, the anchor, any stake, or any authority" (Seat Map :139). The first contact carries the residual and its refuter, never the framework's interior.

Legally: scraping personal emails for unsolicited bulk outreach breaches GDPR and CAN-SPAM style rules in many jurisdictions. The public submission and opt-in routes in `03_public_contact_points.csv` avoid that failure as well as the structural one.

## The honest status of this package

This is a **simulated derivation**, checked for internal consistency against the figures. From *The Recursive Self-Improvement Trap*, opened 2026-11-22: *"LLMs can quickly simulate an approximate answer/output with the formula found in the architecture post."* The real testable output would have to be derived by working the formula directly against a ground. This package cannot settle the question it reports on. No holdout, lift test, or purchase dataset was run here.
