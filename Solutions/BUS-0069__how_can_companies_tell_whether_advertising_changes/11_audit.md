## Independent domain census

I redid the census before comparing with the primary, using the WATSON order: R by arity, symmetry and scale; one line per domain with actors / currency / anchor; partition or union; order by dependency.

- **R (arity / symmetry / scale).** The residual is the gap between purchases observed after exposure and purchases that would have occurred in the same consumer slot without exposure. Arity: three places, consumer Γ, ad action a, purchase o′, indexed by slot, ordered in time. It carries an ordered bond and a comparison bond. Symmetry: "changed" is a threshold against a baseline, not a symmetric join; the persuaded purchase and the would-have-bought purchase are identical under `+` and different under `→` versus conjoined intention, so this is an isomer question (Fig 12 :117). Scale: consumer loop, campaign population with a ranking of what it produces, and the measurement channel across time.
- **D1 Consumer decision loop.** Actors: consumer, belief, exposure, purchase. Currency: decisions and residual reduction. Anchor: realized purchase plus its no-ad counterpart. Admitted.
- **D2 Measurement and attribution frame.** Actors: analysts, platforms, models, dashboards. Currency: impressions, clicks, attributed conversions. Anchor: **none nameable**, since these quantities move when pushed and are produced by the frame. Admitted as instrument only.
- **D3 Market habitat.** Actors: consumers, competitors, platforms, targeting, season. Currency: purchases and spend. Anchor: the market, named in Fig 2 :29. Admitted.
- **D4 Comparison instrument and channel.** Actors: holdouts, geo splits, ghost ads, source discriminators. Currency: contact with the counterfactual. Anchor: the ground, which does not decay (Fig 10 :24). Admitted.
- **X1 Brand affinity as free-floating property.** No anchor independent of the behavior it is meant to predict. Not a domain yet.
- **X2 Ad spend as sole cause.** Already inside D2 and D3. Absorbed.

Union, not partition: D1/D4 and D2/D3 form two compounds; the whole is C1 (did behavior change) plus C2 (did the ad reach likely buyers) (Fig 9 :54-57). Dependency order: perception → counterfactual vocabulary → objective stated as `Δ_s` → planning → carry (Fig 3 :20, :24-27).

My census matches the primary's four domains and two exclusions. Where the primary derived the union from Fig 9, I derived it from Fig 12's isomer definition plus Fig 1's per-slot rule: two molecules with the same flat reading are two conditions that cannot be merged. Different starting figures, same domain boundaries: low overlap between routes, so the agreement is weak but not circular within the framework. It is still one evidence pool.

## Round trip gap `R_T`

The Symbols table defines `R_T` as `gap(x, (T_E ∘ T_A)(x))`, and Fig 4 :30 requires it to be measured rather than assumed. I ran the round trip on the condensation the folder actually uses.

1. **Sent up (x):** the verbatim figure set, chiefly Fig 1 :19 and :27 (per-slot residual, frame-internal quantities), Fig 2 :29 (anchor), Fig 9 :54-57 (union), Fig 12 :117 (isomer), Fig 6 :34 (instrument rule).
2. **Abstraction (`T_A`):** the shorthand in `07_the_tether_answer.md`: per consumer, counterfactual against a fixed assignment, two conditions separate, filters not witnesses, no named tool.
3. **Concretisation (`T_E`):** the concrete practice claim: run a holdout or geo split, compute `⋛(P | A=1, P | A=0)` per slot, keep a selection diagnostic, and do not blend into ROAS.

**`R_T` is nonzero.** Three things the shorthand holds do not survive the trip back down:

- **per-slot heterogeneity**, which a campaign-level ROAS read drops (Blake et al.'s frequent-user result);
- **spillover between geo slots**, which a consumer-level lift read averaged into a campaign decision drops;
- **the budget condition**, since Fig 8 :39 allows a term to be in the closure and still unreachable at this budget, which the shorthand "just run a holdout" silently sets aside.

Conversely, one thing survives cleanly: the need for a comparison whose assignment does not update on the analyst's belief, with C1 and C2 kept separate. That is the residue worth sending. `R_T` was **not** measured numerically, because no ground reading exists; it is recorded qualitatively as the enumerated set of dropped constraints. Treating "the formula is the condensed version of the metalaws" as identity would set `R_T` to zero and would be the dead-reckoning error Fig 4 :30 names.
