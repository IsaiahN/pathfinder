## 1. A shape is a constraint, not a filler

The figures README states the limit: the framework returns *"what a missing thing would have to look like (a shape, not a name)."* Fig 9 :32 *"you get a kind of thing, never the answer itself"*. Fig 13 :134 *"Unreachable is a verdict here, not a silence,"*.

From *The Library of Babel* (opened 2026-11-22): *"you can cut your search short if the set of books returned is small and descriptive enough that it provides a general shape of the answer that satisfies your question."* And: *"Perhaps your problem was a bundle or conflation of several problems masquerading as one? You will need to search all of those problems individually to find their answers and then combine them in a satisfactory way."*

So for this question a **shape** is: two separable conditions that must be searched individually and then combined; an anchor external to the party being measured; a threshold with no stated value; a refused assignment of fixed valence to an owner. It constrains any acceptable answer. It does not supply a percentage or a verdict for a named firm.

## 2. How the shape is obtained

- *Library of Babel*: use the vocabulary already held that is *closest* to the thing, then look for recurring new terms that overlap or correlate with it; *"With each new term gained, you narrow the space by definition and deepen your vocabulary."*
- Fig 9 :27-28 *"describe it before you go looking, or any frame you pick will seem to fit"*; Fig 9 :30-31 *"your words are borrowed too, so describe the effect rather than the cause"*. Here the effect description is the two residuals: management shirking and minority expropriation. Naming "good owner" or "bad owner" first is the inversion the figure forbids.
- *Decoding Tacit Knowledge* (opened 2026-11-22): *"When contact isn't possible, the best you can send is a plain description of what you'd expect to see, because anyone can check that against the world."* This is why `02_outreach_email.md` sends the residual and the four refuters before the theory.
- *Library of Babel* on stopping: *"you will know that you are done because the finish line will also be the starting place"*; and the paradox, *"you have already composed the answer through the process of locating it."* Here the two conditions were located, then the question's either/or was recognised as the conflation. That is the stopping point, not a final answer.

## 3. What licenses a name

Only two things, both outside the shape itself:

1. **The ground settles it.** Formula step 5: *"the ground settles it. Verified only by what pays. A gate is not the ground."* The README: the framework *"stops at the limit of what the available instruments can distinguish, and records the lineage so the stop is honest."*
2. **A provenanced import from a frame whose closure differs** (Fig 6 :23, Fig 8 :50-52), with a source gate and a shadow test.

*They're Scanning Books* sharpens this (mapped via the exemplar): *"The real import is not a new term in the existing space. It is a new dimension of the space."* and *"Declaring the primitives does not make them neutral. It makes the choice visible."*

For this question neither licence is held. No measurement in this folder settles a percentage or names a firm's dominant condition. The world literature (Morck 1988, Claessens 2002) is an import from the finance frame with a source gate and a shadow test only partly supplied, and in any case it describes the world, not the figure set. So the shape keeps the slot empty: a threshold shape, not a 5 percent rule.

## 4. Why the percentage is the specific forbidden error here

*The Hard Problem is About Source* (opened 2026-11-22) gives the mechanism. Metamerism: an instrument reads green and *"cannot comment on the source"*, one green light, or yellow plus blue, or RGB, or CMYK, or a scheme not yet discovered. *"If several paths create the same behavior, then behavior is a projection with lossy information."* And: *"An instrument that can't distinguish these cases demonstrates its own inadequacy."* It was the **spectrometer**, not an argument, that resolved it. *"The answer is unreached until a better instrument exists."*

Applied here: a governance rating, a Tobin's Q, a market price or a board-compliance score is a reading. Several ownership structures, and several combinations of monitoring and diversion, can produce the same reading. Naming the ownership percentage from that reading is the metamerism error: a source named from a projection. What is owed is a discriminator, for example an instrument that separates cash-flow rights from control rights and an anchor the controller cannot move, not a number.

This is also why F5 in `01` refuses the 5 percent disclosure line. It is a convention that decides an outcome, which by Fig 10 :47 *"a convention that decides an outcome has stopped being one"* is no longer a convention.

## 5. Q — What does "shape" mean for BUS-0056?

**Answered from the figures and the essays.** The shape is the four-part constraint in §1. Concretely:

- The question is a **union** of two conditions, not one hard question and not a single scale (Fig 9 :55-57).
- The **owner's sign is relative to the residual** (Fig 11 :49-51), so "powerful owners" is a relation and not a kind. This refuses the question's either/or before answering it.
- The **deciding anchor must be external** to the party being measured (Fig 2 :29-30, Fig 10 :86, :93).
- The **threshold has no name** in the figure set (Fig 9 :32, Fig 8 :39, Fig 13 :134). Declared `gap` in `01` F8.

Does the folder commit the error the README forbids? No percentage is named, no good/bad owner is named, and the two conditions are kept separate in every artifact. The only naming that happens is the binding of the two residual descriptions ("management shirking", "minority expropriation") to the question, which is a description of effects, not the selection of a frame.

## 6. Two consequences for outreach

**Prestige is not action.** From *The Recursive Self-Improvement Trap* (opened 2026-11-22): *"Prestige must never be allowed to validate an action directly."* And: *"Prestige can fund an action. It can give an action a budget to be tried. But it cannot settle an action. Only the ground can settle it. The Ground Maintainer must be blind to prestige."* In this field the prestige proxy is the famous governance expert or the highly cited review. A list of such names agreeing with the union claim would be the named collapse, not corroboration. The legitimate ask gives the claim a budget to be tried and lets the ground settle it.

**My own audit is a simulation, not a ground reading.** Same essay: *"LLMs can quickly simulate an approximate answer/output with the formula found in the architecture post."* and *"the truly testable output would need to be derived from the real architecture or working the formula yourself."* So `01` and this file are simulated derivations, checked for internal consistency against the figures. They are not tests of the claim. They cannot settle anything they report on. The note at the end of the essay is also recorded honestly: the essay itself says *"AI edited, but written by me"*, and the local figures are the authority, not this reading of them.
