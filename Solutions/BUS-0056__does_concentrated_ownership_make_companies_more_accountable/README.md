## The question restated as an effect

The question asks which of two effects follows from one structural change. Concentrated ownership moves both the residual claim and the control rights into fewer hands. Effect A: a large stake gives an owner the incentive and the power to monitor management, reducing the free-rider loss of diffuse ownership. Effect B: the same stake gives an owner power over the firm that can be used for private benefits not shared by minority shareholders. What is being decided is whether these are alternatives or a compound. An answer would look like a map of the conditions under which each effect governs, a named anchor that settles each, and a refuter for the map. The sought-for shape is bound as an ordinary term in `01_figure_grounded_audit.md` so it can accrue a residual like anything else.
