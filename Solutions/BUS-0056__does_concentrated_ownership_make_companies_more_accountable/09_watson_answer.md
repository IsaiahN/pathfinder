## QUESTION

Decide, in effect terms, whether concentrated ownership increases accountability or gives powerful owners too much control. Option A: treat them as alternatives on one scale and report a net effect. Option B: treat them as a union of two conditions that can hold at once, refuse the fixed valence of "powerful owner", require an external anchor, and declare the threshold `gap`. What each leads to: A produces a single `general`-mode verdict with no ground, erases the per-slot residual, and spends the independence of the people asked to check it; B produces a checkable shape, four written refuters, per-domain verdicts kept separate, and an open slot until a source-discriminating instrument closes it.

## DOMAIN CENSUS

R by arity, symmetry and scale, then one line per domain with actors / currency / anchor, partition or union, and order by dependency.

- **R (the residual).** Concentrating ownership moves the residual claim and the control rights into fewer hands. Arity: multi-place (controller, manager, board, minority holders, firm assets, market or regulator as ground); a threshold quantity in the comparison sense of Fig 12 :87. Symmetry: asymmetric, one controller faces many dispersed holders; monitoring (voice or exit) and diversion (subtraction) are different ordered bonds, not inverses. Scale: firm loop, blockholder stratum, population of firms, legal habitat, and time. Fig 1 :19 *"The residual is indexed by object slot, not aggregated."*
- **D1 Monitoring and accountability.** Actors: blockholder, manager, board. Currency: monitoring effort, information, voice or exit threat, reduction of R on management actions. Anchor: realized firm value and the market's payment (Fig 2 :29). Question type: capability of agent and habitat, not of agent alone.
- **D2 Private benefits and control excess.** Actors: controlling shareholder, minority holders, firm assets. Currency: private benefits, diverted assets, control rights exceeding cash-flow rights. Anchor: minority returns, courts, disclosure, market valuation (Fig 10 :93). Question type: a relation to a residual, not a kind of owner.
- **D3 Verification and governance frame.** Actors: directors, regulator, courts, auditors, market for control. Currency: agreement versus residual, convention versus ground. Anchor: the ground, never agreement (Fig 2 :35). Question type: anchor and channel roles, kept distinct.
- **D4 Entrenchment over time.** Actors: incumbent controller, pyramids, dual-class stock, disclosure channel. Currency: continuity of control, decay of challenge. Anchor: the ground, which does not decay; the channel does (Fig 10 :24).
- **X1 "The owner is good" or "the owner is bad" as a fixed property.** Actors: the owner alone. Currency: supposed disposition. Anchor: none nameable independent of a stated residual. **Not a domain yet; refused at census.**
- **X2 "Concentration" as a single lever.** Absorbed into D1 and D2; concentration changes two conditions, so it is not itself a domain.

**Union, not partition.** D1 and D2 overlap in the same firm. Fig 9 :55 *"A bundled problem is usually a union of overlapping conditions, not a list of separate ones."* Fig 9 :57 *"What remains is smaller and it is also categorically different: a case in two conditions is not a harder case of either."* A single lever clears the region where only one condition holds and leaves the intersections standing.

**Order by dependency (Fig 3).** Perception of beneficial ownership and rights -> vocabulary separating cash-flow rights, control rights, monitoring and private benefits -> objective (what counts as accountability and as excess) -> planning (governance mechanism) -> learn and carry (verify against an unmoved ground). The vocabulary link is where the question usually breaks; it has no instrument, so it attracts no attention (Fig 3 :27 *"A link with no instrument is not a link that works. It is a link nobody has looked at."*).

Strain test applied. Reading "valence relative to residual" as "no owner is ever a malfactor" strains Fig 11 :49-51; re-census shows valence is relative to a *named* residual, so the blockholder is a benefactor for shirking and a malfactor for expropriation. Reading a managed disclosure as a moved anchor strains Fig 2 :29-30; re-census separates the unmoved market payment from the decayed channel (Fig 10 :24). Both strains dissolved by re-census; neither hidden.

## VERDICT (from the figures)

The either/or is refused. Concentrated ownership is a union of two conditions that can hold in the same firm: a monitoring or accountability condition that belongs to owner and habitat together, and a control-excess condition in which the same stake is used for private benefits. Owner valence is relative to the residual, so "powerful owners" is a relation and not a kind. The anchor that decides either condition must be external to the party being measured; a controller-authored disclosure or compliance rating is a degraded channel, not a moved ground, and cannot serve as a verdict. Entrenchment is a separate time-channel failure with its own instrument. The figures license a threshold between cash-flow rights and control rights, but no number, so the ownership percentage and any named firm's dominant condition are declared `gap`. Verdict mode `specified` on the union, `general` on the shape, `gap` on the threshold. Per-domain verdicts are kept separate and never averaged.

- **cost if wrong:** misdirected governance reform. A firm failing the monitoring condition would be given anchor instruments it does not need; a firm failing the control-excess condition would be told to add a blockholder, which worsens it. A single lever applied to a union leaves the intersection standing (Fig 9 :56). In outreach, an expert's twenty minutes would be spent refuting a shape that was never the right shape. The cost is real but bounded, because the packet states mode, refuters and lineage before the conclusion, so an error is findable rather than invisible.
- **undoable:** yes. No term is deleted if a refuter fires; a refusal is added and the reach falls because *"something was added rather than because anything was deleted"* (Fig 6 :42-43). The four refuters and the per-domain verdicts are recorded in a provenance trail so a later reader can locate the error (Fig 10 :62-63). A sent packet can be superseded without withdrawing any primitive.
- **reversed by:** a ground reading, not agreement. Fixed owner valence across residuals; one lever moving both conditions in the same direction in every firm with no distinct intersection; a market payment that moves when only the controller's opinion moves; or a challenge channel testing high while the ground declines, any of which would break the shape. A source-discriminating instrument that measures cash-flow rights and control rights separately, with an anchor the controller cannot move, would close the threshold `gap` and could name a percentage. A figure re-census that finds a line directly governing a stake percentage would also move the `gap`. Until one of these fires, the verdict stands as `candidate`.
