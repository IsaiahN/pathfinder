## What the derivation concludes, briefly

The three forms are not a ladder with a best rung. They are overlapping conditions of one channel, and the question is a **union**, not a partition (Fig 9 :54). What changes when the carrier changes is not how many facts fit in the library but what crosses the membrane (Fig 4 :22-25), whether the arrangement and its lineage travel with the atoms (Fig 12 :5, Fig 13 :129-130), whether the handoff chain ranges against a ground that does not update (Fig 2 :33-35, Fig 7 :35), and whether the habitat reproduces the contact the goal required (Fig 11 :66-69). Memory stays indexed per slot (Fig 1 :19). A library that grows is not a reach that grows (Fig 5 :72). Verdicts are kept per domain and never averaged into "media changed memory."

## What is deliberately not here

- No personal emails of named individuals.
- No ranking of people by how likely they are to agree with this reading.
- No bulk-send plan.

The figure-grounded reasons are recorded in `02` and `04`: selecting recipients for likely agreement manufactures the correlation that would make their agreement worthless (Fig 2 :40), and a verifier who has already absorbed the framework cannot verify it (Fig 10 :42). The public venues in `03` are institutional and opt-in. There is a real legal side too: unsolicited bulk email to scraped personal addresses breaches GDPR and CAN-SPAM style rules in many jurisdictions and will get a sending domain blacklisted.
