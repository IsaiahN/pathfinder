## Round trip gap R_T

The Symbols table defines R_T as the gap between what was sent up and what came back down, `gap(x, (T_E ∘ T_A)(x))`, measuring what the coarser description cannot hold. Fig 4 :30 requires it to be measured rather than assumed. I ran the round trip on the condensation this folder actually uses.

1. **Sent up (x):** the verbatim figure set, chiefly Fig 4 :22 and :25 (method, head start, recording), Fig 12 :5 and Fig 13 :130 (bond and lineage), Fig 2 :34 and Fig 7 :13 (drift and agreement), Fig 11 :39 and :66-69 (habitat and silent failures).
2. **Abstraction (T_A):** the shorthand in `01` section 0 and `07` section 2: a carrier extends memory when it carries a method or head start, keeps bond and lineage, keeps a non-updating reference reachable and reproduces required contact; the question is a union; memory is per slot.
3. **Concretisation (T_E):** a concrete claim about a named community, for example "the archive improved this village's memory" or "search engines weakened recall."

**R_T is nonzero.** Four things the shorthand holds do not survive the trip back down:

- the **per-slot indexing**, which a community-wide "improved memory" statement erases;
- the **kind of thing that crossed**, which a concrete statement usually reports only as "information" or "access";
- the **non-updating reference**, which a concrete evaluation commonly replaces with the agreement of the archivists or users themselves;
- the **habitat enumeration**, including what was unintentionally introduced, which a before-and-after storage count never shows.

Conversely, one thing survives cleanly: a carrier that carries only a recording and drops the bond reduces the next generation's reach. That is the residue worth sending. R_T was **not** measured numerically, because no ground reading exists; it is recorded qualitatively as the enumerated set of dropped constraints. Treating "the formula is a condensed version of the figures" as identity would set R_T to zero and would be the dead-reckoning error Fig 4 :30 names. The honest form is a lossy condensation with a measured gap, which `09_watson_answer.md` states.
