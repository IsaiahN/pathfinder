## QUESTION

Decide, in effect terms, what the Tether says about the claim that oral, written and digital forms of knowledge changed what communities can remember and share. Option A: answer with a ranking of the three media, or with a story in which each supersedes the last. Option B: unbundle the question into the conditions of transmission, give a per-slot verdict for each, and refuse the ranking. What each leads to: A returns a `general`-mode verdict with no anchor and no slot structure, and it spends the independence of anyone asked to check it. B returns a checkable shape, a pre-registered refuter, and a named gap until a ground settles each slot.

## DOMAIN CENSUS

R by arity, symmetry and scale, then one line per domain with actors / currency / anchor, partition or union, and order by dependency.

- **R (the residual).** The per-slot gap between what a community can re-find and what its current carrier lets it re-find. Arity: four terms in contact, a knowing community, a carrier, the content with its bonds and lineage, and a ground; a fifth term, the habitat, appears in every real case. Symmetry: the carriers are not interchangeable and are not a rank; a crossing changes role rather than moving up (Fig 13 :4-5). Scale: individual recall, community memory, intergenerational transmission, with the residual indexed per slot (Fig 1 :19). Fig 9 :54 makes it a union, not a partition.
- **D1, the medium as membrane and channel.** Actors: speaker, scribe, printer, indexer, platform, archive. Currency: what crosses, a method, a head start or a recording; bits priced separately from actions. Anchor: the ground does not decay, the channel does. Question type: partition of what may cross.
- **D2, the community as frame with a library.** Actors: members, institutions, curricula, canonical lists. Currency: record versus reach; compositions inside closure(Γ). Anchor: the ground for settlement; the wall moves only on import. Question type: closure and reachability.
- **D3, the handoff chain.** Actors: each reteller and learner. Currency: bracket fidelity across handoffs, agreement versus evidence. Anchor: the ground, against which each frame is corrected separately. Question type: chain with a possible break between vocabulary and objective.
- **D4, the bond and the lineage.** Actors: the knower holding the arrangement, the record carrying atoms and bonds. Currency: typed bonds and provenance. Anchor: a discriminating instrument, not an argument. Question type: structure, isomer, lineage.
- **D5, the habitat of transmission.** Actors: teacher, apprentice, school, archive, platform, algorithm. Currency: contact, introduction versus subtraction, valence relative to the residual. Anchor: capability is a property of agent-and-habitat. Question type: union; isolation is substitution.
- **Order by dependency.** Perceive the slots; acquire terms for what the carrier affords; state what would count as remembering well against a non-updating reference; plan a route; name what worked and carry it (Fig 3 :20). Refuse a wrong term before minting (Formula step 2). Describe the residual before choosing a frame (Fig 9 :27-28).

Strain test applied: Fig 4's membrane and Fig 6's closure wall govern different quantities, what crosses a scale boundary versus what a closure can reach, so no contradiction after re-census. Fig 3's dependency order orders the loop's links, not the historical order of carriers.

## VERDICT (from the figures)

The union reading survives; the ranking does not. What a carrier changes is which typed thing crosses to the next holder (method, head start or recording), whether the bond and the route travel with the atoms, whether the chain ranges against a reference that does not update, and which actors and conditions are in contact with the residual. Corpus growth is record growth, not reach growth. Agreement inside a chain is variation, not evidence. Valence is relative to the residual, so "oral good, written better, digital best" is a standing list with no anchor. The universal ranking is a `gap`, reported as unreachable at this budget and not as silence. Every substantive verdict is `specified` and ungrounded; the structure is `general`.

- **cost if wrong:** bounded and locatable, but not trivial. If the membrane or lineage findings are wrong, a community could be advised to preserve a recording when it needed a method, or to keep atoms when it needed the bond. The figures price that error twice: a wrong atom costs more than starting from primitives (Fig 6 :24), and description cost and action cost cannot be compared in one unit (Fig 12 :125-127). The avoidable part of the cost is spending the independence of outside readers by sending a finished theory before a characterised residual; Fig 2 :42 says to buy independence in proportion to the cost of being wrong. The two-stage packet avoids that cost.
- **undoable:** yes. The ranking narrative is removable without touching the derivation, and no primitive has to be withdrawn. `07_the_tether_answer.md` and `08_expert_verification_packet.md` already remove it. A sent claim can be superseded by the corrected packet because the lineage is recorded.
- **reversed by:** a ground reading, not agreement. A per-slot measurement of a named community across a carrier change, with the reference shown not to move, would settle a slot. A recording that yields a new success without re-deriving the method would break the membrane finding. A stored item that yields its bond and route without its lineage would break the lineage finding. A retelling chain whose agreement tracks a non-updating ground would break the drift finding. Until one of these fires, the verdict stays `candidate`.
