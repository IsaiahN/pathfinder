# Expert verification packet: the Tether on oral, written and digital knowledge

**What this is:** a claim in a form a memory researcher, oral-tradition scholar or cognitive scientist can check. Mode, derivation and refuters are stated before the conclusion, so a reader knows what to look for and what would settle it.

**Mode: `specified` on the per-domain claims, `general` on the structural shape, `gap` on a universal ranking. Not `grounded`.** No community was measured. This is a shape and a route, not a result.

**Status of this packet:** a simulated derivation checked for internal consistency against the figures, not a ground reading. It cannot settle the question it reports on.

## 1. The claim, in one paragraph

Community memory is per slot, not a stock. A change of carrier (speech, inscription, networked storage and search) alters four things: what kind of thing crosses between holders and generations (a method, a head start or a recording of one occasion); whether the typed relation and the route travel with the content; whether the chain ranges against a reference that does not update; and which actors and conditions are in contact with the residual. A bigger library is not a bigger reach, and agreement inside a chain is not evidence. The three forms are overlapping conditions of one loop, not a ladder with a best rung. The framework returns the shape a transmission arrangement must have, the refuters written in advance, and the instruments that would close it. It does not name a winning technology.

## 2. Three checkable sub-claims

### C1: The medium is a membrane; what crosses decides what can be shared

- **Statement:** a carrier transmits one of three kinds of thing. A re-applicable method or a head start extends reach; a recording of one occasion looks like knowledge but is a description of one occasion. Forgetting is a failure of the channel, not of the ground.
- **Source, verbatim:** Fig 4 :22 *"Only methods cross up. Only head starts come down."* Fig 4 :25 *"A recording carried upward looks like knowledge and is a description of one occasion."* Fig 10 :24 *"the ground does not decay, the channel does."*
- **What to check:** in a case you know, identify what actually crossed: the method, a pointer to where the answer can be found, or a frozen instance. Is the receiver able to produce a new success, or only to repeat the recorded one?
- **Refuter:** a recording of one occasion lets a receiving generation produce a new success without re-deriving the method. That breaks the distinction at the membrane.

### C2: The bond and the lineage are the part that is dropped

- **Statement:** a stored item that keeps the atoms but drops the typed relation and the production route makes the next generation pay again. The lineage is not documentation added to the term; it is part of the term.
- **Source, verbatim:** Fig 12 :5 *"A set of ingredients is not one. The bond is the rest of the information, and it is the half nothing was recording."* Fig 13 :130 *"A term carries the atoms it was built from and the bonds that joined them. That record is not documentation of the term. It is the term."* Fig 12 :108-109 *"the result carries forward and the deriving does not, so every lineage pays again."*
- **What to check:** take an item in your field that was transmitted across a medium change. Can a receiver recover whether the original relation was a sequence, a production, an identity or a comparison? Can they recover who found it and by what route? If not, the bond was dropped.
- **Refuter:** a stored item yields its bond type and production route without its lineage, and a receiver can act on the difference. That falsifies the lineage claim for that case.

### C3: A chain needs a reference that does not update

- **Statement:** a handoff chain whose only check is itself drifts coherently, and its agreement is not evidence. Adding links adds motion, not progress. A reference that does not change when the chain changes its mind is the only thing that closes the loop.
- **Source, verbatim:** Fig 7 :13 *"agreement compounds, evidence does not."* Fig 7 :35 *"A room's agreement is variation, not a verdict. Selection between rooms is where the ground votes."* Fig 2 :34 *"If it does, panel 3 turns into panel 1: the anchor becomes another frame, everything drifts together, and the drift is coherent, which is why nobody notices."*
- **What to check:** name the reference in your case and show that it did not move when the tellers changed their account. If the only reference is another member of the same chain, the drift condition holds. Bartlett's retellings and the "telephone game" are the familiar instances.
- **Refuter:** a retelling chain's agreement tracks a non-updating ground rather than the shared pool, with the ground shown not to move when the chain changes its mind.

## 3. What is explicitly not claimed

- Not a ranking of oral, written and digital forms, and not a historical sequence that is also a rank. A crossing changes role rather than moving up (Fig 13 :4-5).
- Not that writing is good or search engines bad. Valence is relative to the residual; no standing list of good actors survives a change of residual (Fig 11 :51).
- Not that a larger corpus increases reach. Fig 5 :72 *"a library that grows is not a reach that grows."*
- Not that the internet has been shown to damage memory. The world evidence on the Google effect is mixed, and this packet asserts no `grounded` digital finding.
- Not that agreement among experts corroborates the claim. Fig 2 :40: agreement from a shared evidence pool is disqualifying for a verdict.
- Not `grounded`, and not a claim that the framework itself derived a named carrier. Naming a binding is provenance, not a derivation.

## 5. What would settle it

1. **Per-slot measurement:** does a named community re-find each named slot after a carrier change, with the reference shown not to move? An aggregate score cannot settle it (Fig 1 :19).
2. **Membrane test:** does the carrier transmit a method or a head start, or only a recording?
3. **Lineage test:** does the stored item carry its bond and its route, or only its atoms?
4. **Ground test:** does the chain range against a reference that does not update, and does its agreement track that reference rather than the pool?
5. **Habitat test:** which actors, conditions and relations did the carrier introduce, and which required contact did it fail to reproduce?

## 6. What an expert can check in twenty minutes

1. Read the three source lines in C1 to C3 (nine short passages) in `FRAMEWORK/reference/03-FIGURES_TEXT.md`.
2. Pick one transmission case you know well. Identify the slot, the kind of thing that crossed, and whether the bond and route travelled.
3. Name the reference in that case and say whether it moved when the account changed.
4. Try one of the three refuters. A failure is more useful than an endorsement.
5. Send the correction with its provenance. No reply is needed if you would rather not engage.
