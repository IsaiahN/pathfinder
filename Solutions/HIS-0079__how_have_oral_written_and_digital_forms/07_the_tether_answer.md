# The Tether's answer on oral, written and digital knowledge

**Mode: `specified` on the per-domain claims, `general` on the structural shape, `gap` on a universal ranking of the three media. Not `grounded`.** Simulated derivation checked against the figures, not a ground reading (`05_what_shape_means.md` section 7).

## 1. The residual it answers

A community's memory is a vector of slots, not one stock. Changing the carrier changes what crosses between holders and generations, whether the arrangement and the route travel with the content, whether the chain ranges against a reference that does not move, and which actors and conditions are in contact with the residual. The question is a **union** of those conditions, not a partition into three media with one winner. Fig 9 :54: **"And when the split lands on a union rather than a partition, unbundle before you search again."**

## 2. The shape, with verdicts kept per domain

**D1, the medium as membrane and channel.** Fig 4 :22: **"Only methods cross up. Only head starts come down."** Fig 4 :25: **"A recording carried upward looks like knowledge and is a description of one occasion."** Fig 10 :24: **"the ground does not decay, the channel does."** What a carrier lets a community remember depends on what kind of thing crosses: a re-applicable method, a head start, or a recording of one occasion. Forgetting is a channel failure, not ground decay. Verdict: `specified`, **triangulated** (Fig 4 and Fig 10, low overlap).

**D2, the community as frame with a library.** Fig 5 :72: **"a library that grows is not a reach that grows."** Fig 6 :49: **"more search at the same reach: is it in here and unfound? The closure does not change. Same atoms, same wall, more of the space actually visited."** Digital storage enlarges the record and the visited space. It does not add a primitive. A new affordance is an import and needs a source gate and a shadow test. Verdict: `specified`, **triangulated** (Fig 5, Fig 6, Fig 13).

**D3, the handoff chain.** Fig 7 :13: **"agreement compounds, evidence does not."** Fig 7 :35: **"A room's agreement is variation, not a verdict. Selection between rooms is where the ground votes."** Fig 2 :34: **"If it does, panel 3 turns into panel 1: the anchor becomes another frame, everything drifts together, and the drift is coherent, which is why nobody notices."** A retelling chain that checks only against itself drifts coherently. Writing can supply an anchor that does not update; a feed tuned to the reader's agreement is a mirror. Verdict: `specified`, **triangulated** (Fig 7 and Fig 2).

**D4, the bond and the lineage.** Fig 12 :5: **"A set of ingredients is not one. The bond is the rest of the information, and it is the half nothing was recording."** Fig 13 :130: **"A term carries the atoms it was built from and the bonds that joined them. That record is not documentation of the term. It is the term."** Fig 12 :108-109: **"the result carries forward and the deriving does not, so every lineage pays again."** A stored fact without its typed relation and its route is an ingredient list, and the next generation re-pays the cost of deriving it. Verdict: `specified`, **triangulated** (Fig 12 and Fig 13).

**D5, the habitat of transmission.** Fig 11 :39: **"capability is a property of agent-and-habitat, never of the agent alone."** Fig 11 :49-51: *"An actor's valence is relative to the residual, not a property it carries"* and *"No standing list of good actors survives a change of residual."* Fig 11 :68-69: **"what you failed to reproduce is invisible until the goal fails"** and **"what you unintentionally introduced is invisible until it acts."** A new carrier substitutes a habitat; it introduces actors it did not choose and can fail to reproduce the contact the goal required. Verdict: `specified`, **single-source** on the figure side (Fig 11), with world corroboration from the oral-tradition and tacit-knowledge sources.

**Cross-domain, the two currencies.** Fig 12 :125: **"A composition costs in two currencies, and they do not add."** Fig 12 :126: **"Bits price the description. Actions price finding out whether it holds."** Storage is priced in bits; checking is priced in actions. A medium that is cheap in one can be expensive in the other. Verdict: `general`, **triangulated** (Fig 12 pricing and catalyst rule).

**The universal ranking.** No figure line returns a best medium, a literacy threshold, a span of retention or a historical sequence that is also a rank. Fig 9 :32: **"you get a kind of thing, never the answer itself."** Verdict: `gap`, reported as unreached at this budget, not as silence (Fig 13 :134: **"Unreachable is a verdict here, not a silence,"**).

## 3. What it explicitly does not say

- Not that writing is better than speech, or that digital media destroy memory. Fig 11 makes valence relative to the residual; every verdict needs a named slot.
- Not that a larger corpus means a larger reach (Fig 5 :72, Fig 6 :32).
- Not that agreement among tellers, readers or reviewers is evidence (Fig 7 :13, Fig 2 :40).
- Not that the three forms are a ladder. A crossing is a change of role, not a step upward (Fig 13 :4-5).
- Not `grounded`. No community was measured and no reference was read.
- Not a name for a winning technology. The framework returns a shape, and naming a binding is provenance, not a derivation.

## 4. What would settle it

A source-discriminating instrument, not a further argument. *The Hard Problem is About Source*: a reading *"cannot comment on the source"*, and several paths can create the same reading. Here the corresponding checks are:

1. **Per-slot measurement.** Does a named community re-find each named slot after a carrier change, with the anchor shown not to move? An aggregate score cannot settle this (Fig 1 :19, :22).
2. **Membrane test.** Does the new carrier transmit a method or a head start, or only a recording of one occasion? A receiver producing a new success without re-deriving the method would falsify the distinction.
3. **Lineage test.** Does the stored item carry its typed bond and its production route, or only its atoms? If the receiver can recover both without the lineage, finding 4 falls.
4. **Ground test.** Does the retelling chain range against a reference that does not update, and does the chain's agreement track that reference rather than the shared pool?
5. **Habitat test.** Which actors, conditions and relations did the carrier introduce, and which required contact did it fail to reproduce?

Standing falsifiers are the seven listed in `01_figure_grounded_audit.md` section 4, written before the conclusion.

## 5. The honest stop

**Unreached, not unreachable in principle.** The canonical README says the framework stops *"at the limit of what the available instruments can distinguish, and records the lineage so the stop is honest."* No instrument was applied to a real community here. The derivation stops at the shape, records the union and the open slot, and reports the gap. *"Indiscernibility is not identity."*

## 6. One-paragraph version for outreach

> The Tether does not rank speech, writing and digital storage. It says that community memory is a vector of slots, not a stock; that what a carrier transmits is a method, a head start or a recording of one occasion, and only the first two carry re-applicable knowledge; that a larger library is not a larger reach, because search inside a fixed closure visits more of the same space; that a fact without its typed bond and its route is an ingredient list that makes the next generation pay again; that a retelling chain which checks only against itself drifts coherently, because agreement compounds while evidence does not; and that a new carrier substitutes a habitat, introducing actors it did not choose while failing to reproduce contact it cannot see was required. The question is a union of those conditions, so verdicts stay per slot and are never averaged into a best medium. What the framework returns is the shape a good transmission arrangement must have, the refuter written in advance, and the instruments that would close it. The slot stays open until a ground settles it.
