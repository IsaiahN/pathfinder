## 1. A shape is a constraint, not a filler

The canonical README states the limit: the framework returns *"what a missing thing would have to look like (a shape, not a name)"*. Fig 9 :32: **"you get a kind of thing, never the answer itself."**

From *The Library of Babel*: *"This means that you can cut your search short if the set of books returned is small and descriptive enough that it provides a general shape of the answer that satisfies your question."* The essay's point is that the library is not the obstacle; the searcher's vocabulary is. *"Your ignorance, of not knowing what you don't know, is the actual obstacle between you and the answers, not the vastness of the library."*

For HIS-0079 a **shape** is therefore the set of conditions any adequate account of a carrier must satisfy, stated before we can say which community, which slot and which technology. It is not a story about the rise of literacy and it is not a ranking of media.

- A shape: "the typed relation and the route travelled with the item, or they did not."
- A name: "the printing press caused the Enlightenment" or "search engines destroyed memory."

The first is checkable per slot. The second is a verdict with no census and no anchor.

## 2. How a shape is obtained

- *Library of Babel*: use the vocabulary already held that is *"closest to the words that you wish to describe and grasp the shape of"*, then look for recurring terms that overlap or correlate, and recurse. *"With each new term gained, you narrow the space by definition and deepen your vocabulary."*
- Fig 6 :30: *"The description travels. Fails by being sharpened into terms only we can read. Cast it in effect terms."* Fig 9 :30-31: *"your words are borrowed too, so describe the effect rather than the cause."*
- *Decoding Tacit Knowledge*: *"When contact isn't possible, the best you can send is a plain description of what you'd expect to see, because anyone can check that against the world."*
- *Library of Babel* on stopping: *"You will know that you are done because the finish line will also be the starting place, as there is nowhere else to search and nothing else that can describe the answer better."*

Applied here, the bootstrap vocabulary was drawn from the question itself: carrier, slot, method, recording, bond, lineage, anchor, habitat. Each figure line then narrowed the set of admissible answers. The search stopped at a shape, not at a historical periodisation.

## 3. What licenses a name

Only two things, both outside the shape itself:

1. **The ground settles it.** Formula step 5: *"the ground settles it. Verified only by what pays. A gate is not the ground."* The README says the framework stops *"at the limit of what the available instruments can distinguish, and records the lineage so the stop is honest."*
2. **A provenanced import from a frame whose closure differs** (Fig 6 :23, Fig 8 :50-52), with a source gate and a shadow test.

*The Hard Problem is About Source* gives the reason a reading cannot licence a name by itself. Metamerism: an instrument reads green and *"it cannot comment on the source"*. Several underlying causes can create identical readings. *"If several paths create the same behavior, then behavior is a projection with lossy information."* The essay's conclusion: *"the answer is unreached until a better instrument exists."* It was the spectrometer, not an argument, that resolved the case.

For this question the direct analogue is: a community's observed recall cannot tell you which carrier produced it. A memory score is a reading; the source is the whole transmission arrangement. Naming "writing" or "the internet" as the cause of a memory change from a memory reading alone is the metamerism error. What is owed is a source-discriminating instrument: a per-slot measurement with a reference that does not move.

## 4. Why the tacit-knowledge essay governs this question

*Decoding Tacit Knowledge* is the closest essay to HIS-0079 that was opened this session, and it should lead the "what shape means" reading for this folder.

- *"Writing carries the context well and the subtext partly, but it almost never carries the pretext, the 'you had to be there' part."* This is a claim about what crosses a membrane (Fig 4 :22), not about whether writing is better than speech.
- *"The understanding has to be rebuilt by the learner, and the way it gets rebuilt is contact: trying, being corrected, and trying again, in both directions."* Fig 11 :40: *"An improvement that does not change contact changes nothing, however much it improves."*
- *"And nobody in Bartlett's study lied"*: details that did not fit the listener's schema were *"omitted from the recollection, or transformed into more familiar forms."* This is Fig 7 :13 (*"agreement compounds, evidence does not"*) at the level of a single retelling.
- *"Two people who only check against each other drift, and they drift together, so neither notices."* Fig 2 :34 names the same failure: *"everything drifts together, and the drift is coherent, which is why nobody notices."*
- *"Long, close contact pulls the frames together, so the process that makes communication easy slowly uses up that extra reach."* This is the independence parameter of Fig 8 :48, applied to a teaching relationship.
- *"Only methods cross up. Only head starts come down"* (Fig 4 :22, quoted in the essay). The essay's own formulation: what passes down is ways of doing things and head starts, never the experience itself.
- *"Settling compresses the description and not what it cost to establish"* (Fig 12 :107, quoted in the essay). A compressed word keeps its meaning; the work behind it stays with whoever did it. Every new learner pays part of the bill again.

The shape that follows is not "oral knowledge is tacit and written knowledge is explicit." It is a condition: **a carrier must either reproduce the contact that rebuilds pretext, or provide an effect description that anyone can check against the world.** Where neither is present, the transmitted item is context without pretext, and it will be read through the receiver's own history.

## 5. This question's shape, stated once

An adequate account of how oral, written and digital forms altered community memory and sharing must:

1. index the effect per slot and refuse a global average (Fig 1 :19, :22);
2. say what crosses the carrier boundary: method, head start or recording (Fig 4 :22, :25);
3. distinguish record from reach, so corpus growth is not mistaken for a new primitive (Fig 5 :72, Fig 6 :32);
4. track whether the bond and the lineage travelled with the atoms (Fig 12 :5, Fig 13 :130);
5. name the non-updating reference the chain ranged against (Fig 2 :34, :45, Fig 7 :35);
6. enumerate the habitat the carrier introduced, including the two silent failures (Fig 11 :43, :66-69);
7. price description and action separately (Fig 12 :125-129);
8. declare overlap between witnesses and treat the question as a union (Fig 2 :40, Fig 9 :54).

A proposed answer that satisfies all eight has the right shape. It still needs a ground before any of its per-slot verdicts can be called `grounded`.

## 6. Correction to my own section 3

I initially stated the rule as "the framework returns a shape, not a name, so a historian may not name a medium." That overstates it. The canonical phrase governs a **missing** primitive, one not held in Γ. Naming an already-available carrier configuration from observation is a **binding** from the world frame onto held terms, not the invention of a new primitive. What remains forbidden is claiming that the framework itself derived the name, or claiming the binding moved the wall (Fig 6 :21: *"MINT composes inside the closure and can never add an atom"*).

Both readings are kept: the name is not a framework deliverable, and naming an observed binding is allowed once its provenance is shown. This mirrors the correction recorded in the exemplar's `05` section 5b.

## 7. Two consequences for outreach and for this folder's status

**Prestige is not action.** From *The Recursive Self-Improvement Trap*: *"Prestige must never be allowed to validate an action directly."* and *"Prestige can fund an action. It can give an action a budget to be tried. But it cannot settle an action. Only the ground can settle it."* A list of famous historians who agree with the shape is the named collapse, not corroboration.

**This audit is a simulation, not a ground reading.** Same essay: *"LLMs can quickly simulate an approximate answer/output with the formula."* and *"the truly testable output would need to be derived from the real architecture or working the formula yourself."* So `01` is a simulated derivation checked for internal consistency against the figures. It is not a measurement of any community and must be labelled as such. It cannot settle anything it reports on.
