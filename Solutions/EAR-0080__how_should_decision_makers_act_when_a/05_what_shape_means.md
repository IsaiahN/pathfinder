## 1. A shape is a constraint, not a filler

The figures README states the limit: the framework returns "what a missing thing would have to look like (a shape, not a name)". Fig 9 :32: "you get a kind of thing, never the answer itself". Fig 13 :134: "Unreachable is a verdict here, not a silence," and Fig 13 :135: "It cannot say which primitive is missing, because naming it would mean already holding it."

From the carried *Library of Babel*: "You can cut your search short if the set of books returned is small and descriptive enough that it provides a general shape of the answer that satisfies your question."

So for this question a **shape** is the set of properties a decision process must have, stateable before a probability or a policy can be named. It constrains; it does not fill. "Keep the live slot indexed and keep the cut reversible" is a shape. "Set a carbon price of 80 per tonne and act when the crossing probability exceeds 0.1" is a name, and no figure line licenses it.

There is a second, sharper use of the word here. A decision maker searching for an answer of a particular kind holds that kind as a **preference**, not as a term, so no internal check can see it (Fig 5 :62-64). The discipline is to bind the wanted shape as an ordinary term before the analysis: "make the sought-for shape an ORDINARY TERM. Write down what an answer would look like, bind it, and let it accrue a residual like anything else" (Fig 5 :65). The one-paragraph "what an answer would look like" in `README.md` is that binding.

## 2. How the shape is obtained

- **Describe the residual in effect terms first.** Fig 9 :27-28: "describe it before you go looking, or any frame you pick will seem to fit". Fig 9 :30-31: "your words are borrowed too, so describe the effect rather than the cause." For this question the effect is: a live slot with positive mass, a one-way consequence, and an unresolved probability. Naming a discipline or a policy first would be apophenia.
- **Use the vocabulary closest to the thing.** Carried *Library of Babel*: "With each new term gained, you narrow the space and deepen your vocabulary." Here the closest terms are the figures' own: slot, support, reach, anchor, channel, cut, habitat.
- **A plain description is sendable when contact is impossible.** Carried *Decoding Tacit Knowledge*: "When contact isn't possible, the best you can send is a plain description of what you'd expect to see, because anyone can check that against the world." This is why `02_outreach_email.md` leads with a failure condition rather than a theory.
- **Stop at a narrowed shape.** Carried *Library of Babel*: the finish line "will also be the starting place", and by then you have "composed the answer through the process of locating it." For this question the stop is the eight-property decision shape plus the instrument owed, not a policy.

## 3. What would license a name

Only two things, both outside the shape itself:

1. **The ground settles it.** Formula step 5: "the ground settles it. Verified only by what pays. A gate is not the ground." Here that means a real instrument that discriminates timing and magnitude at the slot, with a stated failure condition and provenance.
2. **A provenanced import from a frame whose closure differs** (Fig 6 :23, Fig 8 :50-52), with a source gate and a shadow test. A decision method imported from another field must explain the gap it was brought in to explain and must be debited against the independence of the frame it came from.

Carried *They're Scanning Books*: "The real import is not a new term in the existing space. It is a new dimension of the space." Also: "Declaring the primitives does not make them neutral. It makes the choice visible." The six-term substrate is a visible guess, not nature's own list; a decision vocabulary built from it is a visible choice too.

## 4. Why "expected value" is the forbidden error here

Carried *The Hard Problem is About Source* gives the mechanism. Metamerism: an instrument reads green and "cannot comment on the source" - one green light, or yellow plus blue, or RGB, or CMYK, or a scheme not yet discovered. "If several paths create the same behavior, then behavior is a projection with lossy information." And: "An instrument that can't distinguish these cases demonstrates its own inadequacy." It was the spectrometer, not an argument, that resolved it.

A single expected-loss number is the green reading. It merges several distinct per-slot states into one projection and cannot say which slot is live. Fig 1 :22: "Aggregating across slots is how a live signal disappears into an average." What is owed is a discriminator, not a more confidently stated average. This is the same point decision science makes from the other side: robust decision making rejects "the view that a single joint probability distribution represents the best description of a deeply uncertain future" (Lempert and Collins 2007, carried into `09_watson_answer.md` and `10_source_ledger.csv`).

## 5. Correction to my own earlier reading

I initially wrote that "the framework returns a shape, not a name" forbids naming any concrete decision object. That overstates it. The README phrase governs a **missing** primitive, one not held in `Γ`. The decision vocabulary used here (slots, cuts, anchors, channels) is not a new primitive; it is a binding from the framework onto a decision problem, which is provenance, not invention. What remains forbidden is the opposite move: reading a number or a policy off a projection and claiming the framework named it. Both the corrected and the earlier reading are kept here so neither is silently lost. This mirrors the correction recorded in the exemplar's `05` section 5b.

## 6. Prestige is not action, and this audit is a simulation

Carried *The Recursive Self-Improvement Trap*: "Prestige must never be allowed to validate an action directly... Someone let prestige stand in for proof... Prestige can fund an action. It can give an action a budget to be tried. But it cannot settle an action. Only the ground can settle it. The Ground Maintainer must be blind to prestige."

A campaign that gathers famous climate scientists to agree with the decision shape is the named collapse, not corroboration. Fig 2 :42: "Buy independence in proportion to the cost of being wrong." Fig 2 :40: "disqualifying for a verdict: agreement about the world is worthless when the agreeing frames share an evidence pool". The legitimate ask gives the shape a budget to be tried and lets an instrument settle it.

Same essay: "LLMs can quickly simulate an approximate answer/output... However, the truly testable output would need to be derived from the real architecture or working the formula yourself." LLM output is "simulacra." So `01_figure_grounded_audit.md`, `06_isomers_and_order.md`, this file and `09_watson_answer.md` are simulated derivations, checked for internal consistency against the figures. They are **not** tests of the decision question and must be labelled as such. They cannot settle anything they report on.

## 7. What this means for the answer

For EAR-0080, "shape" is the following constrained form, and nothing more:

- The residual stays indexed per slot; no live irreversible slot is averaged away.
- Unresolved timing and probability are reported as `unreachable` or `underivable`, not as zero and not as silence.
- The three-question search order is SEARCHED, then INWARD and OUTWARD on separate tracks.
- Independence is bought in proportion to the asymmetry of consequence.
- Cuts are kept ranked and reversible; the reversal condition is written before the commitment.
- A non-updating ground is kept reachable through a channel that can be violated and can report its own loss.
- Description cost and action cost are held in separate accounts.
- The stake is declared and the verifier sees residual evidence, not evidence about the maker.

The probability, the discount rate, the threshold and the named policy are outside the shape. They are a `gap` reported as `unreached`, not an omission and not an invitation to invent a number.
