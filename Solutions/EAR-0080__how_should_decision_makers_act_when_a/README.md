## Residual in effect terms

A decision maker holds actions `a` in a library `Γ`. For each action and each tracked slot `s` (a tipping element, an exposed population, an infrastructure system), prediction `Γ(b,a)` against observation `o'` leaves `R_s = |Γ(b,a) − o'|`. The question is about one particular kind of live slot: a change whose consequence would be near-irreversible, whose probability and timing are unresolved, and whose plausibility is positive. The question asks what action keeps the ground reachable and the cuts reversible when more deliberation cannot move the wall. In effect terms it is a **union compound** of three overlapping conditions (plausibility, asymmetry of consequence, unresolved probability and timing), not one question with a single optimum (Fig 9 :54, :55, :57).

## What an answer would look like (bound as an ordinary term)

An answer is a decision rule that: (1) keeps the live slot indexed rather than aggregating it into an expected value; (2) treats unresolved probability as `unreachable` or `underivable`, which is a verdict, not silence and not zero; (3) buys independence in proportion to the asymmetry of consequence; (4) keeps every cut ranked and reversible; (5) keeps a non-updating ground reachable through a channel that can be violated; (6) holds description cost and action cost in two separate accounts; (7) declares the decision maker's unavoidable stake; and (8) records what would have had to be true for a different action to be right. It does **not** contain a universal probability threshold, discount rate, or named optimal policy. That absence is a reported `gap`, not a silence.

## What is deliberately not here

- No personal emails of named individuals and no harvested contact lists.
- No ranking of people by how likely they are to agree with the shape claim.
- No bulk-send plan, and no request for trust or endorsement.
- No universal probability threshold, discount rate, or claim that the figures name a specific policy.

Three reasons in the figures' own words. Fig 2 :35: "Alignment is triangulation, not negotiation. A mirror cannot correct you." Fig 2 :40: "disqualifying for a verdict: agreement about the world is worthless when the agreeing frames share an evidence pool". Fig 10 :42: "A verifier that can reconstruct the claim cannot verify it." A list selected for receptiveness manufactures the correlation that would make agreement worthless, and sending the full architecture before a verdict spends the reader's independence.

Legally: unsolicited bulk email to scraped addresses breaches GDPR and CAN-SPAM style rules in many jurisdictions. The venues in `03` are public institutional or opt-in channels. One venue (Copernicus Earth System Dynamics) requires disclosure of AI tool use; another (Springer Climatic Change) states that an LLM cannot satisfy authorship criteria and that use must be documented in Methods. Both constraints are recorded in `04` and `08`.
