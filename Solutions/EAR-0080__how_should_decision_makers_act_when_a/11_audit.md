## Round trip gap `R_T`

The Symbols table defines `R_T` as the gap between what was sent up and what came back down, `gap(x, (T_E ∘ T_A)(x))`, measuring what the coarser description cannot hold. Fig 4 :30 requires it to be measured rather than assumed zero. I ran the round trip on the condensation the folder actually uses.

1. **Sent up (`x`):** the verbatim figure set, chiefly Fig 1 :19-22 (per-slot residual), Fig 8 :39 and Fig 13 :134 (`unreachable` as verdict), Fig 2 :42 (independence price), Fig 11 :61 (asymmetry), Fig 12 :125-127 (two currencies).
2. **Abstraction (`T_A`):** the shorthand in `07_the_tether_answer.md`: keep the slot indexed, report unreachable, buy independence, keep cuts reversible, keep two accounts, declare the stake.
3. **Concretisation (`T_E`):** a real decision process, for example a regulator choosing whether to fund a monitoring network and a staged adaptation measure before a tipping threshold is resolved.

**`R_T` is nonzero.** Three things the shorthand holds do not survive the trip back down:

- the **type of `⋛` at a decision threshold**. The Operators table defines it as a relation between two quantities; a monitored hazard and a tolerated consequence may not be quantities at the same level. Fig 12 :111-112 permits the plan and the comparison, but the operand type needs an instrument.
- the **scope of F4**. Fig 11 :61 governs actors introduced into a habitat. Policy moratoria, cumulative emissions and built infrastructure have different subtractability. The shorthand "keep cuts reversible" is a direction, not a verdict for any specific cut.
- the **status of the channel**. Fig 10 :24-28 requires the monitor to distinguish a physical change from a decayed channel. The shorthand "keep the ground reachable" hides the work of showing that a reading is not stale, saturated or pooled.

Conversely, one thing survives cleanly: a live slot with positive mass is not erased by a small aggregate. That is the residue worth sending. `R_T` was **not** measured numerically, because no ground reading exists; it is recorded qualitatively as the enumerated set of dropped constraints. Treating the formula as identical to the figure set would set `R_T` to zero and would be the dead-reckoning error Fig 4 :30 names.
