# The Tether's answer: acting under plausible irreversible risk with unresolved timing and probability

**Mode: `specified` on the decision-process shape and on each per-domain verdict, `general` on the structural barriers, `gap` on the numerical policy. Not `grounded`.** Simulated derivation checked for internal consistency against the figures (`05_what_shape_means.md` section 6), not a ground reading. No measurement settles any part of it.

## 1. The residual it answers

A live slot carries positive mass: a change with a near-one-way consequence whose probability and timing cannot be resolved by the instruments currently held. The question asks which action is right when more deliberation cannot move the wall. In effect terms this is a union of three overlapping conditions, not one question with one optimum. Fig 9 :55: "A bundled problem is usually a union of overlapping conditions, not a list of separate ones." A single lever such as wait-for-better-information or maximise-expected-value clears the region where only one condition holds and leaves the intersections standing.

## 2. The shape, by domain

Verdicts are kept separate and are never averaged into one number.

### D1, the physical ground (source of the residual)

The physical system does not update on a board's probability estimate. Fig 10 :24: "the ground does not decay, the channel does". What decays is the channel: stale readings, dropped transients, saturated indicators, readings pooled across a change. The ground is the anchor for the physical question and is not in the library (Symbols table :103-106). **Verdict: `specified`, triangulated** (Fig 10 and Fig 2). What would settle a physical crossing is an instrument, not a better argument: Fig 6 :32: "An instrument is not built from a description. It is improved from a worse instrument already returning something."

### D2, the risk-characterisation frame

Fig 1 :19: "The residual is indexed by object slot, not aggregated." Fig 1 :20: "Support is satisfied when some slot carries positive mass, so a global residual near zero with one live slot is a legal state and not an inert one." A global expected loss near zero with one live irreversible slot is a legal state, not an inert one. Aggregation is the named failure: Fig 1 :22: "Aggregating across slots is how a live signal disappears into an average."

Unresolved probability is reported as `unreachable` or `underivable`, not as zero and not as silence. Fig 8 :39: "So a search that finds nothing is never a proof of absence." Fig 13 :134: "Unreachable is a verdict here, not a silence,". The order of questions is fixed: SEARCHED first (is it in the closure and unfound?), then INWARD (is an instrument already returning something that fails to resolve?) and OUTWARD (does another frame hold a discriminating model?) as two opposed tracks (Fig 6 :31). **Verdict: `specified`, triangulated** across Fig 1, Fig 8 and Fig 13.

### D3, the decision and governance frame

Because the question is about people, human judgment is the legitimate anchor here; models are instruments and "can be wrong on axes they do not measure" (Fig 2 :37). The decision object must be a route, a comparison and a subtraction, not an ingredient list: Fig 12 :112: "A plan is a sequence. A goal is a comparison. Progress is a subtraction."

The intervention is asymmetric. Fig 11 :61: "The seat's power is asymmetric. Introduction is available; exclusion is not." Once a commitment is in the habitat, contact is a fact that does not consult the decision maker. Therefore keep every cut ranked and reversible: Fig 9 :40-41: "a wrong cut removes the answer and speeds up so keep every cut ranked and reversible". Write the reversal condition before the commitment, because a refusal is a negation and not a bond (Operators table :58). **Verdict: `specified`, triangulated** across Fig 11, Fig 9 and Fig 2, with the type of reversibility left live (S2 in `01` section 7).

### D4, the verification frame

Fig 2 :42: "Buy independence in proportion to the cost of being wrong." Irreversibility raises that cost, so the decision requires low-overlap frames ranged separately against the ground, not an ensemble of related models. Fig 2 :40: "disqualifying for a verdict: agreement about the world is worthless when the agreeing frames share an evidence pool". A review loop whose rooms all share a closure is one long room (Fig 7 :36). The decision maker's stake is unavoidable but an interest in the reading is disqualifying, and what cannot be removed must be declared (Fig 10 :59-60). **Verdict: `specified`, triangulated** across Fig 2, Fig 7 and Fig 10. In this simulated run all four domains draw on one evidence pool, so their agreement is internal consistency and not corroboration.

### Cross-domain rule: two currencies, one refused fusion

Fig 12 :125: "A composition costs in two currencies, and they do not add." Fig 12 :126: "Bits price the description. Actions price finding out whether it holds." A probability estimate is a price in bits; acting, monitoring and reversing are prices in actions. Summing them is the error, not the fix (Fig 12 :130). Fig 9 :60: "Use filters for the budget and witnesses for the verdict. Never let a filter hand you a verdict." **Verdict: `general`, structural, triangulated** across Fig 12 and Fig 9.

## 3. What it explicitly does not say

- Not a probability of crossing, a discount rate, a threshold probability for action, or a named optimal policy. Fig 9 :32: "you get a kind of thing, never the answer itself". That is a `gap` reported as `unreached`, not silence.
- Not "apply the precautionary principle" as a cost-blind rule. A convention that decides an outcome has stopped being a convention (Fig 10 :47), and the two currencies cannot be ignored (Fig 12 :125-127). The weak, proportional form is compatible; the strong form is refused (S5 in `01` section 7).
- Not "wait for better information." Waiting is a lever that leaves the irreversibility intersection standing (Fig 9 :56), and Fig 8 :39 makes absence of evidence not proof of absence.
- Not "maximise expected value." In this state the aggregate has thrown away the only signal that says where to look (Fig 1 :28).
- Not a verdict that any particular tipping element will cross. No measurement was taken.
- Not a room consensus. Fig 7 :35: "A room's agreement is variation, not a verdict. Selection between rooms is where the ground votes."

## 4. What would settle it - the ground

A source-discriminating instrument, not a further argument. The questions to put to a ground are:

1. **SEARCHED:** is the probability, timing or mechanism already in `closure(Γ)` and unfound? More search at the same reach, not a new atom (Fig 6 :49).
2. **INWARD:** is anything at any resolution already returning something that fails to resolve? Critical slowing down in a time series is the candidate shape; its skill must be measured, not assumed. Fig 6 :34: "The question is not whether a sensor could exist. It is whether anything, at any resolution, is already returning something that fails to resolve."
3. **OUTWARD:** does another frame already hold a discriminating model? Paleo records, other tipping elements and other disciplines are candidates; cast the description in effect terms and debit the import against independence (Fig 6 :30, Fig 6 :24).
4. **Threshold test:** does a `⋛` relation hold between the monitored hazard and the tolerated consequence at the slot, or demonstrably not? Operators table :47 defines `⋛` as "a threshold relation between two quantities"; the operands must be quantities at that level.
5. **Reversal test:** can the specific cut be subtracted at acceptable cost, including second-order contacts? If yes, F4 weakens for that action (refuter item 4 in `01`).
6. **Channel test:** is the ground still reachable, or has the channel gone stale, saturated or pooled? The correct report on a dead channel is "you have lost touch", not a verdict (Fig 10 :27).

Standing refuters from `01` section 4: a resolved slot; a demonstration that the barrier was only a budget limit; a subtractable intervention; low-overlap frames that disagree and a ground that settles between them; a lever that clears the union; a unit-preserving conversion of the two currencies.

## 5. The honest stop

**Unreached, not unreachable in principle.** The figure set returns a shape and a route. It stops where the instruments can no longer distinguish, and records the lineage so the stop is checkable. Fig 13 :138: "And it can say the answer is out of reach with the library as it stands, so something has to be imported before it is asked again." The framework's six-term substrate is "a human's careful guess at reality's compression axes" (carried essay), so declaring the vocabulary makes the choice visible, not neutral. "Indiscernibility is not identity." Prestige or agreement is not a discriminator (Fig 2 :42).

Nothing here is `grounded`. The decision maker must still decide, and the figures do not decide for them: they narrow the shape a defensible decision must have and name what would reverse it.

## 6. One-paragraph version

> The Tether does not tell a decision maker which policy to choose, and it does not return a probability or a discount rate. It narrows the shape the decision must have. A plausible irreversible risk is a live slot: keep it indexed and do not average it into an expected loss, because aggregation is how the only signal that says where to look disappears. Unresolved timing and probability are a verdict of `unreachable`, not zero and not silence, so ask first whether the answer is already in the closure and unfound, then run the inward instrument question and the outward import question as separate tracks. Because the cost of being wrong is asymmetric, buy independence from genuinely different frames and range each against a ground that does not update on anyone's opinion. Keep every cut ranked and reversible, write the reversal condition before the commitment, and keep the monitoring channel able to report its own failure. Hold the cost of a description apart from the cost of an action, declare the decision maker's unavoidable stake, and never let agreement from a shared evidence pool stand as corroboration. What is owed beyond this is a discriminating instrument, not a better argument. Until one fires, the numerical policy remains a reported gap.
