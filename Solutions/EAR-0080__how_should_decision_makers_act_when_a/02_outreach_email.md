# Outreach draft - check one output before deciding whether the shape is worth reading

**Use:** one-to-one, or as a cover letter attached to a submission through the public venues in `03_public_contact_points.csv`. **Not** for bulk send.

**Hard precondition:** do not send until the refuter in `01_figure_grounded_audit.md` section 4 has been read and understood. The claim must survive validation or the two-stage offer collapses on contact.

**Mode of what is offered: `specified` on the decision-process shape, `general` on the structural barriers, `gap` on the numerical policy, not `grounded`.** This is a simulated derivation checked for internal consistency against the figures, not a ground reading.

---

**Subject:** Check one claim about decisions under tipping risk before you decide whether the framework is worth reading

> Hi [name],
>
> I have one specific output to check against what you already know. I am not asking you to read the theory first, and I am not asking you to agree with it. If the output does not survive, there is nothing else to look at.
>
> **The output (`specified`, not `grounded`):** a plausible irreversible risk with unresolved timing and probability is not one decision problem with an optimum. It is a union of three overlapping conditions: a live slot with positive mass, a one-way consequence, and probability and timing that current instruments cannot resolve. From that it follows that:
>
> 1. The live slot must be kept indexed. A global expected loss near zero is compatible with one live irreversible slot, and averaging across slots removes the only signal that says where to act.
> 2. Unresolved probability is a verdict about the reach of the instruments, not zero and not silence. Ask first whether the answer is already in the closure and unfound, then run the inward instrument question and the outward import question as two separate tracks.
> 3. Because the consequence is asymmetric, independence between checking frames is bought in proportion to the cost of being wrong. Cuts stay ranked and reversible, and the reversal condition is written before the commitment.
>
> This is a shape for a decision process, not a measurement and not a policy. I am not claiming a probability of crossing, a discount rate, a threshold probability or a named optimal action. No figure line returns those, and I report them as a gap rather than filling them. I am also not claiming that waiting for better information or maximising expected value is always wrong. Each is a filter for the budget; neither is a witness for the verdict.
>
> **What would refute the shape:**
> 1. A single aggregate score that preserves the per-slot signal for the live slot and keeps a channel to the ground at it.
> 2. A measurement resolving timing and magnitude at that slot, or a demonstration that the barrier was only a budget limit and more search closes it.
> 3. A demonstration that the specific intervention is subtractable at acceptable cost, including second-order contacts.
> 4. Two genuinely low-overlap frames that disagree on the shape and a ground that settles between them.
> 5. One lever that clears all three conditions including their intersections.
>
> I wrote these down before running the derivation. If any one holds, the affected finding is withdrawn. A one-line "this fails because..." is more useful to me than agreement. If you would rather not engage, no reply is needed.
>
> **If it survives, and only then:** the sources are below. The figures are the authority, not my summary of them.
> - Verbatim figure text with line numbers (local extract): `FRAMEWORK/reference/03-FIGURES_TEXT.md` in this solution package
> - Canonical usage rule (local): `FRAMEWORK/reference/FIGURES_README_canonical.md`
> - Public figures repository: https://github.com/IsaiahN/tether/tree/general-agent/figures
>
> **If you want to use the machinery:** the formula is in the figures link. Run it on a problem you know well and can check against a ground. Write down what would refute your result *before* you run it, and state the mode of what you get. A reported failure is more useful to me than a reported success.
>
> **On my stake:** I have an interest in the shape being found useful. That is unavoidable and I declare it. I have no interest in which value the ground returns at any particular tipping element, and I ask that you judge the claim against your own ground rather than against my preference.
>
> Isaiah

## One-to-one only

Send to a public institutional or opt-in venue, one recipient at a time, with an opt-out line. No purchased lists, no scraped addresses, no bulk send, no automation, no misleading subject line. A reply is a conversation, never corroboration (Fig 2 :40). If a recipient returns a refutation, copy it into `11_audit.md` with provenance and have the auditor seat re-evaluate it as if it came from any other source.

The theory is offered only in stage 2, after the recipient's verdict on stage 1 is recorded. Once they have absorbed the framework, their agreement stops being evidence (Fig 10 :42: "A verifier that can reconstruct the claim cannot verify it.").
