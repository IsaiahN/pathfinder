## The answer in one line

A major ice change is warned not by a single abnormal state but by a residual that persists and does not trend down at a named slot: recoveries after melt, snowfall or ocean-heat pulses stop shrinking, and variance, lag-1 autocorrelation, skewness and spatial coherence rise in the residuals of elevation, mass, velocity and fracture observables, with the warning cascading to neighbouring basins at second order.

## Deliberately not here

- No personal emails of named individuals.
- No ranking of people by how likely they are to agree.
- No bulk-send plan.
- No claim that this package settles which particular glacier will change or when.

Three reasons in the figures' own words:

1. A room is not an anchor. Fig 7 :35: "A room's agreement is variation, not a verdict." Agreement among readers cannot substitute for a measurement.
2. Correlation disqualifies a verdict. Fig 2 :40: "agreement about the world is worthless when the agreeing frames share an evidence pool". Selecting recipients for receptiveness manufactures the correlation that makes their agreement worthless.
3. A verifier must not hold the interiors. Fig 10 :42: "A verifier that can reconstruct the claim cannot verify it." The first contact therefore carries the residual and its refuter, not the framework.

Legally: scraping personal emails for unsolicited bulk outreach breaches GDPR and CAN-SPAM style rules in many jurisdictions. Public opt-in channels avoid that failure as well as the structural one.
