# Outreach draft: check one output before you decide whether the framing is worth reading

**Use:** one to one, or as a cover letter attached to a submission through the public venues in `03_public_contact_points.csv`. **Not** for bulk send.

**Hard precondition:** do not send until the slot definition and the inherited-forcing refuter in `01_figure_grounded_audit.md` sections 2 and 4 are explicit in the version you attach. The stage-1 output must survive checking or the two-stage offer collapses on contact.

---

**Subject:** One checkable claim about ice-sheet early warning, before any theory

> Hi [name],
>
> I have one specific output I would like you to check against what you already know. I am not asking you to read the framework first, and I am not asking you to agree with it. If the output does not survive, there is nothing else to look at.
>
> **The output (`specified`, not `grounded`):** early warning of a major glacier or ice-sheet change is not a single extreme reading. It is a residual at a named slot that persists and does not trend down across repeated perturbations. Concretely: take the detrended series of surface elevation, mass, velocity or fracture state for one basin or one glacier; measure recovery time, lag-1 autocorrelation, variance, skewness and cross-slot correlation in rolling windows; and require that (i) the trend survives a null of inherited forcing variability, (ii) a second, low-overlap instrument shows the same trend, and (iii) the slot is named, not averaged into a whole-ice-sheet mean. A global mean can be near zero while one basin carries the live signal.
>
> **What this does not say:** it does not name your glacier, give a date, or assert that any particular ice body is about to tip. It does not say that a record melt summer is a warning. It does not claim that anyone has settled whether a threshold has been crossed. The figures return a kind of thing, never the answer itself, so what is offered is the shape a warning must have, not the warning for a specific site.
>
> **What would refute it, written before I sent this:**
> 1. a documented major change with no persistent non-shrinking residual in any already-returning instrument at any resolution at any enumerated slot;
> 2. persistent rising residuals that recur in slots which never change and discriminate no better than raw state extremes;
> 3. a validated case where the rising statistic is inherited forcing variability and the ice's recovery rate did not change;
> 4. warnings found only in global aggregates and never in per-slot series of comparable quality.
>
> If any one holds, I will narrow or withdraw the claim. A one-line "this fails because..." is more useful to me than agreement. If you would rather not engage, no reply is needed.
>
> **If it survives, and only then:** the sources are below. The figures, not my summary of them, are the authority.
> - Verbatim figure text with line numbers (local framework extract): `FRAMEWORK/reference/03-FIGURES_TEXT.md`
> - Canonical usage rule (local framework extract): `FRAMEWORK/reference/FIGURES_README_canonical.md`
> - The eight-step loop and the mode rule (local framework file): `FRAMEWORK/figures/THE_FORMULA.md`
>
> **If you want to use the machinery:** the formula is in the figures link. Run it on an ice body you know well and can check against data. Write down what would refute your result *before* you run it, state whether your claim is `general`, `specified` or `grounded`, and keep the per-slot residuals rather than the average. A reported failure is more useful to me than a reported success.
>
> Best,
> [name]

## Why this version is ordered this way

| the move | figure-grounded reason |

| Output first, theory second | Fig 10 :52 "Bring a characterised residual, never the source." Fig 2 :45 - frames range against the ground separately. |
| Do not ask for agreement | Fig 2 :35 "Alignment is triangulation, not negotiation. A mirror cannot correct you." |
| Pre-registered refuter | Fig 8 :39 "So a search that finds nothing is never a proof of absence." A written refuter makes a null result a verdict rather than silence. |

| Per slot, not global | Fig 1 :19 "The residual is indexed by object slot, not aggregated." |
| Instruments already returning data only | Fig 6 :32 "An instrument is not built from a description. It is improved from a worse instrument already returning something." |
| Two low-overlap instruments | Fig 2 :31 "Independence, between two frames, is how little they draw on the same evidence." |
| No date or threshold named | Fig 9 :32 "you get a kind of thing, never the answer itself" |

| Offer the formula as a method to reuse | Fig 4 :22 "Only methods cross up. Only head starts come down." |
| Ask recipients to state their own ground and refuter | `THE_FORMULA.md` "Direction" - a frame cannot check the direction of its own question. |

## Stage 2 is opt-in by construction

Once a recipient has absorbed the framework, their agreement stops being evidence. So the stage-1 verdict is recorded **before** any theory is shown, and later agreement is never pooled as corroboration (Fig 2 :40 "agreement about the world is worthless when the agreeing frames share an evidence pool"). Stage 3 sends a method, which is permitted by Fig 4 :22, but each user needs a ground and a self-written refuter for their own problem.
