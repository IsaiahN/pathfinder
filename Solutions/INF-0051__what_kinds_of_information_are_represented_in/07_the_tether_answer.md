# The Tether's answer on representation versus statistical pattern

**Mode: `specified`, not `grounded`.** This is a shape and a route, not a result. Simulated derivation checked against the figures (`05_what_shape_means.md` §6), not a ground reading. No recording, intervention, or behavioural measurement was taken.

## 1. The residual it answers

A neural recording yields tuned responses, correlated pairs, manifolds, and decodable identities. The question asks which of these are information the system represents and which are patterns in the data. The two halves form a **union compound**, not one hard question (Fig 9 :54: *"when the split lands on a union rather than a partition, unbundle before you search again"*). The discriminator is upstream in dependency order, because a taxonomy needs a criterion for what counts as a kind (Fig 3 :20).

## 2. The shape: four gates, run per slot

1. **Slot.** A representation is located per neuron, per region, or per tracked object. *"Aggregating across slots is how a live signal disappears into an average."* (Fig 1 :22) A flat population mean does not close the question when one slot is live.
2. **Bond.** The regularity has a typed relation to an action or to another held term. *"A bond is the typed relation between two operands. There are seven, they are not interchangeable, and which one holds is not recoverable from the operands."* (Fig 12 :46) Raw co-occurrence is the symmetric bond `+`; representation adds order, production, or comparison. A vocabulary without bonds *"can name a state and cannot name a route to one"* (Fig 12 :111).
3. **Refusability.** A represented term can be made false. *"refuted: a term we hold is wrong; a term is owed that says so"* (Fig 5 :53) and *"→ install what can be violated"* (Fig 10 :19). A claim that no observation could revoke is a declared meaning, not a grounded one (Fig 10 :49).
4. **Ground, with low overlap.** Its standing is settled by an outcome that does not move when the frame changes its mind. *"An anchor is whatever settles the question and does not change its answer because of what any frame thinks."* (Fig 2 :29) A second frame helps only if it does not draw on the same recording: *"Independence, between two frames, is how little they draw on the same evidence."* (Fig 2 :31)

**Kinds, under these gates, are bond arrangements and levels, not a list of activation patterns.** Two equal vectors with different bonds are Fig 12 isomers: *"isomer. Same atoms, different bond, different substance. The difference is invisible in the formula and decisive in the behaviour."* (Fig 12 :117) A first-order term is `state → structure → state`; once settled, it becomes an atom for a second-order comparison (Fig 12 :104). What can be named is the functional group, *"the group says what kind; the qualifier says which member"* (Fig 12 :124); the qualifier comes from a provenanced import, not from the loop.

A regularity that fails any gate remains a **statistical pattern with respect to that claim**. It can be strongly decodable, highly predictive, and compressible, and still carry no standing. *"Use filters for the budget and witnesses for the verdict. Never let a filter hand you a verdict."* (Fig 9 :60)

## 3. What it explicitly does not say

- Not that decoding accuracy proves representation. The decoder's own score is frame-internal: *"A frame cannot score itself with a quantity it also produces."* (Fig 1 :27)
- Not that failure to decode proves absence. *"a search that finds nothing is never a proof of absence"* (Fig 8 :39).
- Not the name of a specific cell, region, or population as a derived primitive. *"you get a kind of thing, never the answer itself"* (Fig 9 :32). Such a name is a binding from the neuroscience frame and needs provenance.
- Not a single scalar combining description cost and settlement cost. *"A composition costs in two currencies, and they do not add."* (Fig 12 :125)
- Not `grounded`. No instrument was run and no animal was tested.

## 4. What would settle it — the ground

A source-discriminating instrument, not a further argument. From *The Hard Problem is About Source*: a colour instrument *"cannot comment on the source"*, and it was *"proven wrong not by argument, but by the arrival of a better instrument."* Here the corresponding tests are:

- **Intervention test:** change the candidate regularity and observe whether the animal's judgment moves in the direction the regularity predicts. The Salzman et al. microstimulation result is the existing example: *"Microstimulation biased the animals' judgements towards the direction of motion encoded by the stimulated neurons."*
- **Removal test:** abolish the regularity and observe whether the behaviour degrades. A result here must come with a control for the population mean, per gate 1.
- **Bond-discrimination test:** distinguish two populations with the same firing vector but different downstream effects, so that Fig 12's isomer claim is settled rather than asserted.
- **Independence test:** have a second frame that did not use the same recording range against the same behavioural ground. *"Alignment is triangulation, not negotiation. A mirror cannot correct you."* (Fig 2 :35)
- **Standing falsifiers:** the five in `01` §5. Any one firing retracts the shape.

## 5. The honest stop

**Unreached, not unreachable.** The four gates are a shape, and the figures explicitly stop there: *"Unreachable is a verdict here, not a silence"* (Fig 13 :134). Which instrument in a given animal can discriminate among the sources of a recorded regularity is a `gap` at the figure level. The figures describe what such an instrument must do, *"describe the effect rather than the cause"* (Fig 9 :31), not what it is called. Indiscernibility is not identity, so a statistically indistinguishable pair of regularities is not thereby the same represented kind. The lineage is recorded so the stop is checkable.

## 6. One-paragraph version for outreach

> The Tether does not produce a list of content types stored in neurons, and it does not claim that a decodable pattern is a representation. It says that a represented regularity must pass four gates: it must be located per slot rather than in a population average; it must carry a typed bond to an action or another held term, not mere co-occurrence; it must be capable of being made false; and its standing must be settled by a ground that does not move when the decoder changes its mind, checked by a second frame that does not share the same recording. Decoder accuracy, compression, and population geometry are filters for where to look, not witnesses for what is represented. Two equal firing vectors with different bonds are isomers, so an activation catalogue cannot decide the kind. The framework returns that shape and the refuters written in advance, not the name of a cell. Which instrument would settle a given case is left open until the ground pays.
