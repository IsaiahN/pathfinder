## What is being decided, in effect terms

A neural recording contains many regularities. Two readings compete:

- **Reading A (regularity = representation).** Count every decodable, compressible, or predictable regularity as information represented by the system. This fills the library with quantities the frame itself produces, so it cannot be wrong, and "representation" loses the standing to be refused.
- **Reading B (contact = representation).** Count a regularity as represented only when it is a held term, bonded to an action and to a ground, that can be violated and is settled by something that does not update on the frame's opinion. Regularities that fail a gate remain statistical patterns *with respect to that claim* until an instrument arbitrates.

B is what the figures license. A is the named output-layer failure: *"A frame cannot score itself with a quantity it also produces."* (Fig 1 :27)

An answer would look like: a taxonomy of kinds by bond, level, contact, and source-discriminability, plus a four-gate discriminator that a third party can run without being told the framework. That is the shape bound in advance (formula step "Direction"), so it can accrue a residual and be refused.

## What is deliberately not here

- No personal emails of named individuals.
- No ranking of people by how likely they are to agree.
- No bulk-send plan.

Three reasons, in the figures' own words:

1. **Agreement is not a verdict.** *"Alignment is triangulation, not negotiation. A mirror cannot correct you."* (Fig 2 :35) A list selected for receptiveness manufactures the correlation that disqualifies agreement.
2. **Shared evidence is one witness.** *"disqualifying for a verdict: agreement about the world is worthless when the agreeing frames share an evidence pool"* (Fig 2 :40). A second seat in this folder shares the primary's evidence pool, so it is internal consistency, not corroboration.
3. **Do not spend the checker's independence.** *"A verifier that can reconstruct the claim cannot verify it."* (Fig 10 :42) The first contact carries the residual and its refuter, never the framework's interiors.

Legally: unsolicited bulk email breaches GDPR and CAN-SPAM style rules in many jurisdictions. Public institutional or opt-in channels avoid that failure as well as the structural one.
