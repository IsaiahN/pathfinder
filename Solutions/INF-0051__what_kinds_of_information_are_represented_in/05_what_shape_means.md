## 1. A shape is a constraint, not a filler

The figures README states the limit: the framework returns *"what a missing thing would have to look like (a shape, not a name)"*. Fig 9 :32: *"you get a kind of thing, never the answer itself"*. Fig 13 :135: *"It cannot say which primitive is missing, because naming it would mean already holding it."*

From *The Library of Babel*, opened this session: *"You can cut your search short if the set of books returned is small and descriptive enough that it provides a general shape of the answer that satisfies your question."*

For this question a **shape** is the form a represented kind must have, which can be stated before any particular cell is named: per-slot, bonded to action, refusable, settled by a ground that does not move. A **name** would be "this population in this animal represents motion direction". That requires an instrument and a provenance record, not the loop alone.

## 2. How a shape is obtained

- *Library of Babel*: use the vocabulary already held that is closest to the target, then recurse. *"With each new term gained, you narrow the space and deepen your vocabulary."*
- *Library of Babel* on a bundled question: *"Perhaps your problem was a bundle or conflation of several problems masquerading as one?"* This is exactly the two-condition union in `01` §1, and the reason the taxonomy and the discriminator are run as separate loops.
- **Fig 9 :30-31**: *"your words are borrowed too, so describe the effect rather than the cause"*. Applied here: describe what a representation would *do* (guide action, be violable, survive an independent check), not what it *is* (a firing pattern in a named area).
- **Fig 6 :30**: *"The description travels. Fails by being sharpened into terms only we can read. Cast it in effect terms."*
- *Decoding Tacit Knowledge*, opened this session: *"When contact isn't possible, the best you can send is a plain description of what you'd expect to see, because anyone can check that against the world."* This is the form of the outreach in `02`: a description of what would count as a failure, checkable without accepting the framework.

## 3. What licenses a name

Only two things, both outside the shape itself:

1. **The ground settles it.** Formula step 5: *"the ground settles it. Verified only by what pays. A gate is not the ground."* Here an intervention plus a behavioural readout that moves in the predicted direction is the analogue of the Salzman microstimulation result.
2. **A provenanced import from a frame whose closure differs** (Fig 6 :23, Fig 8 :50-52), with a source gate and a shadow test. A claim imported from decoding practice into the framework must say what gap it explains and where it came from.

*The Hard Problem is About Source*, opened this session, sharpens the reason. An instrument reads green and *"cannot comment on the source"*. The same green reading is possible from one green light, yellow plus blue, RGB, CMYK, or a scheme not yet discovered. *"If several paths create the same behavior, then behavior is a projection with lossy information"*, and *"An instrument that can't distinguish these cases demonstrates its own inadequacy"*. It was *"proven wrong not by argument, but by the arrival of a better instrument."*

Applied here: a firing pattern is a reading. "Neuron N represents direction" names a source off a reading. Several sources can produce the same pattern. Without a source-discriminating instrument, the name is the searcher's contribution and must be recorded as such. What is owed is the instrument, not a better argument.

## 4. Why "decodable" is not "represented"

This is the specific forbidden error for INF-0051.

- Ritchie, Kaplan and Klein (2019), opened this session: *"decodability is a poor guide for revealing the content of neural representations."* A classifier can find information the brain does not use. Good decoding and bad decoding are both unreliable guides.
- Fig 1 :25-27: *"coverage, and compression achieved"*, *"predicates minted, and credibility accrued"*, *"They are frame-internal. A frame cannot score itself with a quantity it also produces."* Decoding accuracy is produced by the analyst's frame, so it cannot score that frame's representation claim.
- Fig 9 :60: *"Use filters for the budget and witnesses for the verdict. Never let a filter hand you a verdict."*
- *Decoding Tacit Knowledge*: *"a measurement with no question is noise, and a reading with no encounter is projection."* A pattern with no represented content is noise; a representation claim with no ground encounter is projection.

## 5. The correction to my own §3

I initially wrote that "shape, not a name" forbids naming any observed regularity. That overstates the rule. The README's phrase governs *"a missing thing"*, a primitive not held in `Γ`. The four gates in this solution are held forms, not new primitives. Naming a cell is a **binding** from the neuroscience frame onto those forms. What is forbidden is presenting the binding as a derivation or claiming that it moves the wall (Fig 6 :23: *"No frame escapes its own closure. The bound moves only on contact with a frame whose closure differs."*). Both readings are kept here so the correction is visible rather than silently applied.

## 6. Two consequences for this question

**Prestige is not action.** From *The Recursive Self-Improvement Trap*, opened this session: *"Prestige can fund an action. It can give an action a budget to be tried. But it cannot settle an action. Only the ground can settle it. The Ground Maintainer must be blind to prestige."* A list of famous laboratories selected for likely agreement is the named collapse. The legitimate ask is a budget to try the discriminator and a ground to settle it.

**This audit is a simulation, not a ground reading.** Same essay: *"LLMs can quickly simulate an approximate answer/output with the formula found in the architecture post"*, but *"the truly testable output would need to be derived from the real architecture or working the formula yourself."* So `01` is a simulated derivation checked for internal consistency against the figures. It is **not** a test of the claim and cannot settle anything it reports on. A real test would need a recording, an intervention, and a behavioural readout, none of which were used here.
