# Expert verification packet: the Tether on wavefunction realism

**What this is:** a claim in a form a quantum foundations researcher or philosopher of physics can check. Mode, derivation and refuters are stated before the conclusion, so a reader knows what to look for and what would settle it.

**Mode: `specified` on the sub-claims, `general` on the structural shape, not `grounded`.** No experiment is claimed. This is a shape and a route, not a result.

**Status: simulated derivation checked for internal consistency against the figures, not a ground reading.** It cannot settle the question it reports on.

## 1. The claim, in one paragraph

The dispute over whether ψ is physically real or only knowledge is a binding residual, not a prediction residual: both readings reproduce the same statistics. In this framework's symbols ψ sits on the belief side of the prediction loop, since it is assigned before measurement and used to compute `Γ(b,a)` rather than being the observation `o′`. That is a claim about its functional role, not about what it is. The ontic reading (world-state) and the epistemic reading (knowledge-state) are both state-like, so the framework's composition rule forbids bonding them directly; a structure must sit between them, and here that structure is a source-discriminating instrument. The framework refuses both names as verdicts and returns the shape of the missing instrument instead. PBR and Spekkens are conditional filters on model classes, not witnesses. The three refuters below were written before this packet was compiled.

## 2. Three checkable sub-claims

### C1: framework-internal; checkable by reading the Symbols table

- **Statement:** ψ's functional role in the loop is belief-side, not observational. ψ is what the frame takes to be the case before it acts; `o′` is what actually happened.
- **Source, verbatim:** Symbols table :19-21: *"What the system takes to be the case before it acts. Formed from its own history and from what it inherited, mixed by w. Not the observation."* Symbols table :31-32: *"What actually happened. Read o-prime."* Symbols table :36-38: *"What the library says should happen, given belief b and action a."*
- **What to check:** is the mapping of a preparation-assigned ψ onto `b` (rather than `o′`) faithful to the loop's definitions? This is a role claim; it does not say ψ is made of knowledge.
- **Refuter:** ψ is shown to enter the loop as `o′`, directly read off a single measurement outcome. That breaks the role claim.

### C2: framework-internal; checkable by reading Figures 13 and 9

- **Statement:** the ontic and epistemic readings cannot be bonded directly; a structure must sit between them, and the framework returns that shape, not a name for the winning reading.
- **Source, verbatim:** Fig 13 :74: *"Everything else is a composition across the two kinds, never within one."* Fig 13 :75: *"Two states never bond directly. It is not space-time but space, a structure, time; and two structures join only through a state."* Fig 9 :32: *"you get a kind of thing, never the answer itself."*
- **What to check:** do both readings count as state-like under the six-term set, and does the cross-kind rule therefore require a structural intermediary? Is the claim honestly a shape claim, with no interpretation named?
- **Refuter:** ψ's ontic binding is shown to compose legally as a state term in the six-term set (conserved, finite, directionless alone), or a figure re-census admits a direct bond between the two readings. That breaks the shape claim.

### C3: physics-facing; checkable against the no-go results

- **Statement:** PBR and Spekkens are conditional filters on model classes, not a ground verdict. Agreement among interpretations cannot supply the missing verdict.
- **Source, verbatim:** Fig 2 :29-30: *"An anchor is whatever settles the question and does not change its answer because of what any frame thinks. A measurement, an experiment, a counter, a market, a patient outcome. If it moves when you push on it, it is not an anchor."* Fig 2 :35: *"Alignment is triangulation, not negotiation. A mirror cannot correct you."* Fig 2 :40: *"disqualifying for a verdict: agreement about the world is worthless when the agreeing frames share an evidence pool."*
- **What to check:** PBR moves when preparation independence is denied; Spekkens' toy theory moves when the knowledge-balance principle is denied. Neither is an anchor under Fig 2 :29-30. Do the two results belong to different closure conditions rather than a consensus?
- **Refuter:** an experiment discriminates the bindings under assumptions both sides accept, so one result settles the residual at the ground. Then this sub-claim is settled and the verdict is rewritten from the ground.

## 3. What is explicitly not claimed

- Not that ψ is physically real, and not that ψ is only knowledge. Both names are refused as verdicts.
- Not that PBR settles realism or Spekkens settles anti-realism. Both are conditional.
- Not that the dispute is dissolved by terminology. `≡` identity is refused: substituting "world" for "knowledge" changes what mechanism is owed (Operators table :25-27: *"two names, one referent"*, test *"substitute one for the other. Is anything lost? No."*).
- Not a new primitive or an import that moves the wall. No import is made.
- Not `grounded`. Nothing here is asserted as settled by experiment.
- Not a claim that the readings are strict isomers. They are a functional-group pair over the shared predictive term ψ; the isomer claim needs a full shared atom set and is unmeasured (Fig 12 :124).
