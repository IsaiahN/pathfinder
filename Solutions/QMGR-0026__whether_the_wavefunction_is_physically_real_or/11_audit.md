## Independent domain census

I redid the census from the residual line alone, before reading the primary's conclusions, using the WATSON order: R by arity, symmetry and scale; one line per domain with actors / currency / anchor; partition or union; order by dependency.

- **R (arity / symmetry / scale).** The residual is the disputed reference of ψ. Arity: two bindings over one formal object. Symmetry: asymmetric; the two are opposite bindings, and adjudication requires a third discriminating term. Scale: first order inside prediction, second order when asking what ψ refers to. Union rather than partition: the functional-role condition and the reference condition overlap, so a single lever clears one and leaves the intersection standing (Fig 9 :54-56).
- **Domain 1, framework symbols.** Actors: Γ, b, w, a, o′, R, the ground. Currency: `Γ(b,a)` against `o′`. Anchor: the ground, which in the Symbols table *"does not change its answer because of what any frame thinks"*. Question type: partition boundary between b-side and o′-side terms.
- **Domain 2, physics and experiment.** Actors: preparations, measurement devices, outcomes, preparation independence, the ontic and epistemic model classes. Currency: statistics and conditional no-go theorems. Anchor: experiment. Question type: union compound, conditions overlap but do not close.
- **Domain 3, interpretation and verification.** Actors: interpreters, readers, theorem authors. Currency: agreement and residual. Anchor: the ground, never agreement. Independence here is zero in this simulated run because all seats draw on one pool, so this domain cannot produce a verdict.

Order by dependency: describe the residual before naming a frame (Fig 9 :27-28); bind the sought-for shape as an ordinary term before refusing it (formula "Direction"); settle functional role before reference; use filters for the budget and witnesses for the verdict (Fig 9 :60).

My census matches the primary's three domains and its union reading. ## Round trip gap R_T

The Symbols table defines `R_T` as the gap between what was sent up and what came back down, `gap(x, (T_E ∘ T_A)(x))`, measuring what the coarser description cannot hold. Fig 4 :30 requires it to be measured rather than assumed. I ran the round trip on the condensation the folder actually uses.

3. **Concretisation (T_E):** the concrete interpretations a reader will actually form, "ψ is only knowledge" or "ψ is physically real".

**R_T is nonzero.** Four things the shorthand holds do not survive the trip back down:

- the **role/reference distinction**, which collapses when a reader turns "b-side role" into "made of knowledge";

- the **order of refusal before binding**, which is invisible in either name;
- the **live strain about which anchor is legitimate**, which a name silently settles by choosing a side.

One thing survives cleanly: a structure is required between two state-like readings, and that structure is an instrument. That is the residue worth sending. `R_T` was **not** measured numerically, because no ground reading exists. It is recorded qualitatively as the enumerated set of dropped constraints.
