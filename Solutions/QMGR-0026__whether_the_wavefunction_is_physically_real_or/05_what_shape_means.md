## 1. A shape is a constraint, not a filler

The canonical figures README states the limit: the framework returns *"what a missing thing would have to look like (a shape, not a name)"*, then *"stops at the limit of what the available instruments can distinguish, and records the lineage so the stop is honest."* Fig 9 :32: *"you get a kind of thing, never the answer itself."*

*The Library of Babel* (Oct 02 2026): *"You can cut your search short if the set of books returned is small and descriptive enough that it provides a general shape of the answer that satisfies your question."* And on how the narrowing works: *"With each new term gained, you narrow the space and deepen your vocabulary."*

So a **shape** is the narrowed form of an answer that can be stated before it can be named. It constrains; it does not fill. For this question:

- A shape: *a source-discriminating structure must sit between the ontic and epistemic readings of ψ*.
- A name: *ψ is physically real*, or *ψ is only knowledge*.

The shape survives the figures; both names are refused as verdicts. The same essay names the union problem directly: *"Perhaps your problem was a bundle or conflation of several problems masquerading as one?"* That is why the question is treated as a union compound in `01` §1.

## 2. How a shape is obtained here

- *Library of Babel*: start from the vocabulary you already have that is closest to the thing, look for recurring terms that overlap or correlate with it, recurse. The search is steered by the question: *"The books that you are returning in your searches need to be tested or ranged against the problem you want answered in order to steer the direction of the next search."*
- Fig 9 :27-28: *"describe it before you go looking, or any frame you pick will seem to fit."* Fig 9 :30-31: *"your words are borrowed too, so describe the effect rather than the cause."*
- *Decoding Tacit Knowledge* (Nov 10 2026): *"When contact isn't possible, the best you can send is a plain description of what you'd expect to see, because anyone can check that against the world."* That is the effect description carried in `02_outreach_email.md`.
- *Library of Babel* on stopping: *"You will know that you are done because the finish line will also be the starting place, as there is nowhere else to search and nothing else that can describe the answer better."* For this question that condition is not satisfied: the source-discriminating instrument has not been built, so the search is still open.

## 3. What licenses a name

Only two things, both outside the shape:

1. **The ground settles it.** Formula step 5: *"the ground settles it. Verified only by what pays. A gate is not the ground."* The README: the framework *"stops at the limit of what the available instruments can distinguish."* Here that means an experiment that discriminates the two bindings under agreed assumptions. No such experiment is cited in this folder.
2. **A provenanced import from a frame whose closure differs**, with a source gate and a shadow test (Fig 6 :12-13, :23). Here a candidate measurement protocol imported from quantum optics would need both. None is imported in this simulation.

*The Hard Problem is About Source* (Aug 28 2026) gives the mechanism that blocks a cheap name: *"An instrument that can't distinguish these cases demonstrates its own inadequacy, it cannot claim anything substantial about what is being measured or what causes it."* And: *"If several paths create the same behavior, then behavior is a projection with lossy information (it cannot define consciousness in full)."* An ontic and an epistemic model that produce the same statistics are two paths to one reading. The reading does not determine the source. What is owed is the spectrometer, not an argument about colour.

## 4. Why neither interpretation is licensed by the figures

**The ontic name** fails twice. It strains when forced onto the substrate: the wavefunction is not Fig 13's energy state, which is *"conserved, finite, directionless alone"* (Strain 1 of `01` §4). And PBR, the theorem most often cited for it, is conditional on preparation independence: move that assumption and the result changes, which Fig 2 :29-30 forbids in an anchor.

**The epistemic name** fails once. ψ's functional role is b-side (F1 of `01`), but functional role does not identify reference. Calling ψ knowledge because it occupies the belief slot is the metamerism error in §3. Fig 8 :50-52: *"Convergent derivation and adopted import look identical in the contents. Only the record of where each came from separates them."*

Both names are held as **preferences**, not bound terms. Fig 5 :61-63: a frame searching for an answer of a particular shape *"holds that shape as a PREFERENCE rather than as a term. It is not bound, it predicts nothing, and it accrues no residual, so nothing here can see it."* The remedy is a discipline: write the shape down as an ordinary term (done in `01` §0) and let it accrue a residual (done in `01` §5).

## 5. Correction to my own §3 earlier reading

I initially wrote the rule as a flat ban: "the framework returns a shape, not a name, therefore it cannot say anything about ψ." That overstates it. The README's phrase governs a **missing** primitive: *"what a **missing** thing would have to look like (a shape, not a name)."* ψ is already in Γ as a b-side predictive term. It is not missing, so the question is not "invent a new primitive"; it is "which binding does the ground settle".

What remains wrong after the correction is not naming a held atom, it is **naming the source of a reading without a discriminator**. Both claims are recorded so neither is quietly lost:

- **Not retracted:** the metamerism point. An instrument reading ψ-dependent statistics cannot comment on whether the source is world or knowledge (§3).
- **Corrected:** the scope of "shape, not a name". It bounds missing primitives and the return of a frame, not the selection among atoms already held. Applied here, it says the framework returns the shape of the missing instrument, not which interpretation wins.

The two claims now agree with Fig 9 :32 and the README, with no strain left in this section.

## 6. Two consequences for outreach and for this document's own status

**Prestige is not action.** *The Recursive Self-Improvement Trap* (Nov 19 2026): *"Prestige must never be allowed to validate an action directly... Prestige can fund an action. It can give an action a budget to be tried. But it cannot settle an action. Only the ground can settle it. The Ground Maintainer must be blind to prestige."* A list of famous interpreters who agree with a reading is the named collapse, not corroboration. The legitimate ask gives the shape a budget to be tried and lets an instrument settle it.

**This audit is a simulation, not a ground reading.** Same essay: *"LLMs can quickly simulate an approximate answer/output with the formula found in the architecture post. It will often be better than the generic outputs that you will usually get otherwise too (intelligence is essentially arrangement). However, the truly testable output would need to be derived from the real architecture or working the formula yourself."* So `01`, `06`, `07` and `09` are simulated derivations checked for internal consistency against the figures. They are **not** tests of the claim and must be labelled as such. They cannot settle anything they report on.

A final consequence from *Decoding Tacit Knowledge*: the essay's own reading of Schrödinger's cat is quoted there as *"Superposition isn't ignorance: it's the state where the distinction hasn't been made yet."* That is the author's structural reading of quantum measurement, offered *"for structure's sake"*, not a physics result. It is recorded here as an instance of the epistemic-side vocabulary and carries no verdict weight in this solution.
