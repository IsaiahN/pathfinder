## Three things in the wider literature that bear directly on this question

1. **PBR and Spekkens are opposed instruments, not a consensus.** PBR excludes fully epistemic readings under preparation independence; Spekkens keeps epistemic readings viable under a weaker knowledge-balance principle and names what the toy model misses. Fig 6 :30-31: *"Neither answer tells you much about the other. Run them as two tracks, and never read one against the other."* Present them as the "worse instruments already returning something" (Fig 6 :32), not as a settled verdict.
2. **The classification is the contribution.** Harrigan and Spekkens give the ontic/epistemic taxonomy that makes the question well formed. Fig 9 :26-28: *"R's own predicates name the habitat that holds φ*... describe it before you go looking, or any frame you pick will seem to fit."* Describe the binding residual first; then the taxonomy is a lookup, not a search.
3. **The distinction is reusable across fields.** The aleatoric/epistemic split in machine learning is a low-overlap corroboration of the shape: a world/knowledge distinction recurs outside quantum foundations. It does not corroborate either interpretation of ψ, and it shares no evidence pool with PBR or Spekkens. Overlap is declared as low in `10_source_ledger.csv`.

## Distribution: offer, do not push

Figure-grounded reasons:

- **Fig 4 :22-25**: *"Only methods cross up. Only head starts come down. A recording carried upward looks like knowledge and is a description of one occasion."* Sending the whole framework before a reader has described the residual sends a recording, not a generator. Lead with the shape claim and the refuter.
- **Fig 10 :42**: *"A verifier that can reconstruct the claim cannot verify it."* A recipient who has absorbed the framework before being asked for a verdict has joined the frame being checked.
- **Fig 9 :27-28**: *"describe it before you go looking, or any frame you pick will seem to fit."* Ask what they would expect to see if the shape claim were true before giving them the sources.
- **Fig 2 :35 and :40**: alignment is triangulation, not negotiation; agreement from a shared pool is disqualified. A mass send selected for people who already hold an interpretation produces a convention, not corroboration.
- **Fig 9 :60**: *"Use filters for the budget and witnesses for the verdict. Never let a filter hand you a verdict."* Journal tier and venue prestige are filters on where to try, never evidence for the claim.

**Recommended order in a reply:** (1) the shape claim and its three refuters; (2) ask what the reader would count as a failure, and record it; (3) only if they ask, the verified links, with the note that the figures, not our summary, are the authority.

## The two-stage offer

1. **Stage 1 before stage 2** is Fig 10 :52: *"Bring a characterised residual, never the source."* The recipient checks the output without holding the framework interior. Their verdict on stage 1 is recorded before any theory is shown.
2. **Stage 2 is opt-in by construction.** Once a recipient has absorbed the theory, later agreement is not evidence and is never pooled as corroboration (Fig 2 :40).
3. **Stage 3 sends a method, which is permitted.** Fig 4 :22: *"Only methods cross up. Only head starts come down."* The formula is a generator; giving it away does not give away an answer. Each user must write down a refuter and state a mode before running it. Formula "Direction": a frame *"cannot check the direction of its own question, because the shape a frame is searching for is not a term it holds."*

**Hard precondition:** stages 1 to 3 work only if stage 1 survives. As written, the output is a shape and a refusal of both names, not a conclusion. That is what makes it sendable. If a reader fires any refuter in `01` §5, withdraw it.
