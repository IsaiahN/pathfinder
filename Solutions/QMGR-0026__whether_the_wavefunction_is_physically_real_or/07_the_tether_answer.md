# The Tether's answer: is the wavefunction physically real or only representation of knowledge?

**Mode: `specified` on the per-domain claims, `general` on the structural shape, not `grounded`.** A shape and a route, not a result. Simulated derivation checked against the figures (`05_what_shape_means.md` §6), not a ground reading.

## 1. The residual it answers

The dispute is not about prediction. Both readings reproduce the same measurement statistics in their domains. It is a **binding residual**: which term does ψ bind to, a state in the world or the belief `b` of the frame doing the predicting? By Fig 9 :54-56 it is a **union compound**, not one hard question, because it bundles the functional role of ψ and the reference of ψ. A single lever, a theorem or an interpretation, clears one condition and leaves the intersection standing. It has its own loop and its own ground.

## 2. The shape, kept per domain and never averaged

1. **In the framework's own symbols, ψ's functional role is b-side.** Symbols table :19-21: the belief is *"what the system takes to be the case before it acts... Not the observation."* ψ is assigned at preparation and used to compute `Γ(b,a)`; it is never a single `o′`. This is a claim about role, not about what ψ is made of. `specified`, triangulated across the b, Γ(b,a) and o′ entries.
2. **The two readings cannot be bonded directly.** Fig 13 :75: *"Two states never bond directly. It is not space-time but space, a structure, time; and two structures join only through a state."* World-state and knowledge-state are both state-like, so a structure must sit between them. In this case that structure is a source-discriminating instrument. This is the figure-licensed shape. `general`, triangulated with Fig 9 :32.
3. **The framework returns a kind of thing, never the answer itself.** Fig 9 :32: *"you get a kind of thing, never the answer itself."* It says an instrument is owed in the slot. It does not say which interpretation wins. Neither "ψ is physically real" nor "ψ is only knowledge" is a figure-licensed verdict.
4. **Identical predictions do not identify the binding.** Fig 8 :50-52: *"Convergent derivation and adopted import look identical in the contents. Only the record of where each came from separates them."* Reading a source off a prediction that several sources can produce is the metamerism error documented in *The Hard Problem is About Source*. `specified`, triangulated, with the essay declared high overlap with the framework.
5. **The world's no-go results are conditional filters on model classes, not witnesses.** PBR excludes fully epistemic readings under preparation independence; Spekkens keeps epistemic readings viable under a weaker principle. Fig 9 :60: *"Use filters for the budget and witnesses for the verdict. Never let a filter hand you a verdict."* Each theorem is single-source for its own assumptions; no consensus is manufactured. This domain is `gap` for the verdict, not silence.
6. **Agreement cannot settle reference.** Fig 2 :35: *"Alignment is triangulation, not negotiation. A mirror cannot correct you."* Fig 2 :40: *"disqualifying for a verdict: agreement about the world is worthless when the agreeing frames share an evidence pool."* In this simulated run all seats draw on one pool, so agreement is one witness talking.
7. **The readings stand as a functional-group pair over one term.** Fig 12 :124: *"the group says what kind; the qualifier says which member."* The shared substructure is the predictive ψ; the qualifier is world or knowledge. The stronger isomer claim needs a full shared atom set and is unmeasured. See `06_isomers_and_order.md`.

Verdicts stay separate. The verdict on role is `specified` and triangulated; the verdict on the shape of the instrument is `general` and triangulated; the verdict on the reference question is `gap`; the verdict on agreement is `specified` and triangulated. None is averaged into a single "answer".

## 3. What it explicitly does not say

- Not that ψ is physically real. The name strains against Fig 13's energy term (*"conserved, finite, directionless alone"*) and PBR is conditional.
- Not that ψ is only knowledge. Functional role does not identify reference; that is the metamerism error.
- Not that the ontic/epistemic dispute is dissolved by terminology. `≡` is refused: substituting "world" for "knowledge" changes what mechanism is owed (Operators table :25-27).
- Not that PBR settles realism, or that Spekkens settles anti-realism. Both are conditional instruments.
- Not a derivation of one interpretation from the other, and no new primitive or import.
- Not `grounded`. No experiment in this package discriminates the bindings.

## 4. What would settle it: the ground

A source-discriminating instrument, not a further argument. From *The Hard Problem is About Source*: an instrument reads green and *"cannot comment on the source"*; the spectrometer resolved it, not an argument. Here:

- **Instrument Test:** a measurement procedure that distinguishes a world-state reading from a knowledge-state reading under assumptions both sides accept. That is the structure Fig 13 :75 requires between the two state-like readings.
- **Ground Refutation:** such an experiment, run, so that one binding settles the residual at the ground.
- **Loop Refutation:** ψ shown to enter as `o′` read off a single outcome, not as `b` in `Γ(b,a)`.
- **Substrate Refutation:** ψ's ontic binding shown to compose legally as a state term in the six-term set (conserved, finite, directionless alone).
- **Precondition Test:** no-go results are named with the assumption each one moves. A reader who cannot name the assumption has not used them as filters correctly (Fig 9 :60).

## 5. The honest stop

**Unreached, not unreachable.** Fig 13 :134: *"Unreachable is a verdict here, not a silence."* Fig 8 :39: *"So a search that finds nothing is never a proof of absence."* The figures return a shape and stop at the limit of what the available instruments distinguish, recording the lineage so the stop is checkable. *"Indiscernibility is not identity."* Two strains are recorded in `01` §4, one resolved by re-census and one left live (which anchor is legitimate for a question that is disputed as being about world or knower). No reading is settled.

## 6. Pre-registered refuters

Written in `01` §5 and `06` before this conclusion: (1) a ground experiment discriminates the bindings; (2) ψ enters as `o′`; (3) ψ's ontic binding composes legally as a substrate state. If any fires, the shape claim is withdrawn. A failure is more useful than an endorsement.

## 7. One-paragraph version for outreach

> The Tether does not decide whether the wavefunction is physically real or only knowledge, and it does not dissolve the question. It says that ψ plays the belief-side role in prediction, since it is assigned before measurement and used to compute an expected outcome rather than being an outcome; that the ontic and epistemic readings are both state-like, so by the framework's composition rule a structure must sit between them, and in this case that structure is a source-discriminating instrument; and that identical predictions do not identify which source produced the reading. It refuses both names as verdicts, because the framework returns a shape, not the answer itself. PBR and Spekkens are conditional filters on model classes, not a consensus, and agreement among interpretations cannot settle a question about the ground. What it offers is the shape any answer must have, three refuters written in advance, and the instrument that would close it. The slot stays open until the ground settles it.
