## VERDICT (from the figures)

The **shape** survives: ψ's functional role is belief-side, the ontic and epistemic readings are two bindings that cannot be bonded directly, and a source-discriminating structure is owed between them. The **names** do not: neither "physically real" nor "only knowledge" is licensed as a verdict. PBR and Spekkens are conditional filters on model classes, and agreement among interpretations is not an anchor. The verdict on the reference question is `gap`, unreached at this budget, not silence. The whole package is `specified`, ungrounded and `candidate`, with two strains recorded (one resolved by re-census, one live) and no average taken across domains.

- **cost if wrong:** bounded and locatable. The packet states mode, three checkable sub-claims and their refuters before the conclusion, so an error is findable rather than invisible. The real cost is spending the independence of outside readers by sending a name instead of a shape, which Fig 2 :42 prices: "Buy independence in proportion to the cost of being wrong." That cost is avoidable by leading with the shape claim and its refuter.
- **undoable:** yes. The refused names are removable without touching the derivation; `07_the_tether_answer.md` and `08_expert_verification_packet.md` already remove them. A sent claim can be superseded by the corrected packet, and no primitive has to be withdrawn. A refused term stays in the record and lowers the reach, which is a safe kind of fall.
- **reversed by:** a ground reading, not agreement. A source-discriminating experiment under assumptions both sides accept would name the slot; ψ entering as `o′` rather than `b` would break the role finding; ψ's ontic binding composing legally as a substrate state (conserved, finite, directionless alone) would break the shape; a figure re-census admitting `⋛` over two settled model classes as quantities at second order would clear the type strain. Until one of these fires, the verdict stands as `candidate`.
