## What is deliberately not here

- No personal emails of named individuals.
- No ranking of people by how likely they are to agree with a reading.
- No bulk-send plan.

Three figure-grounded reasons, recorded in `01` and `04`:

1. A room is not an anchor. "Alignment is triangulation, not negotiation. A mirror cannot correct you." (Fig 2 :35)
2. Selecting for receptiveness manufactures the disqualifying correlation: "disqualifying for a verdict: agreement about the world is worthless when the agreeing frames share an evidence pool" (Fig 2 :40).
3. Giving the recipient the whole framework before their verdict spends the only thing they contribute: "A verifier that can reconstruct the claim cannot verify it." (Fig 10 :42)

Legally: scraping personal emails for unsolicited bulk outreach breaches GDPR and CAN-SPAM style rules in many jurisdictions. That failure is on top of the structural one. Public institutional and opt-in channels are used instead.
