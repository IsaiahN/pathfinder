## Round trip gap R_T

The Symbols table defines R_T as the gap between what was sent up and what came back down, gap(x, (T_E composed with T_A)(x)), measuring what the coarser description cannot hold. Fig 4 :30 requires it to be measured rather than assumed. I ran the round trip on the condensation this folder actually uses.

1. **Sent up (x):** the verbatim figure set, chiefly Fig 12 :111-112 (bond grammar), Fig 6 :21-23 (idempotence and import), Fig 11 :39-43 (habitat), Fig 9 :54-57 (union).
2. **Abstraction (T_A):** the shorthand in `07` and `09`: language supplies bonds; a concept is hard when a bond or atom is missing and no differing closure is in contact; core concepts are nonlinguistic; the list is a gap.
3. **Concretisation (T_E):** the concrete world examples: exact versus approximate number, true versus false belief, verbal versus visual interference.

**R_T is nonzero.** Three things the shorthand holds do not survive the trip back down:

- the **bond-versus-atom distinction at the ground**: the shorthand treats an absent bond and an absent atom as one condition, but the instrument that would discriminate them is not specified, so the two failure modes collapse on the way down;
- the **second-order operator**: recursion (Fig 12 :104) makes second-order composition legal in principle, but the shorthand carries no commitment about whether the negation operator is available nonlinguistically; the concretisation silently assumes it is language-linked (S3);
- the **per-slot indexing**: the shorthand says "core concepts are nonlinguistic", but the world concretization names categories at the population level; Fig 1 :19 requires the slot, not the population average.

Conversely, one thing survives cleanly: language contributes bonds and a grammar, and the boundary is a closure condition rather than a concept list. That is the residue worth sending. R_T was **not** measured numerically, because no behavioral ground reading exists; it is recorded qualitatively as the enumerated set of dropped constraints. Treating "the formula is a condensed version of the metalaws" as identity would set R_T to zero and would be the dead-reckoning error Fig 4 :30 names.
