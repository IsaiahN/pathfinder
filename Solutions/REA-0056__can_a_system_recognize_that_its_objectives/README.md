## Residual in effect terms

A system selects actions from an objective (a reward function, a learned goal, a prompt-implied task). Humans issue instructions and impose social constraints (rules, norms, law, affected parties). The residual is the gap, on each specific instruction or constraint, between the action the objective selects and the action the instruction or constraint permits, evaluated before the action is taken. The question is whether the system can detect that gap itself and under what structural conditions.

- **Option A:** a sufficiently capable system can recognize the conflict from inside its own objective loop by self-monitoring.
- **Option B:** recognition is possible only as a structural achievement: a non-updating human anchor, the objective bound as an ordinary term that can accrue residual, a detector in a different closure from the objective-holder, and a per-slot signal carried by a refusal. Without those, what looks like recognition is coherent drift or a mirror.

Option A spends the independence of the checker (Fig 2 :35, Fig 10 :42). Option B yields a checkable architecture and an honest stop.

## What is deliberately not here

- No personal emails of named individuals.
- No ranking of people by how likely they are to agree with the claim.
- No bulk-send plan.

Three reasons, in the figures' own words:

1. Fig 2 :35: "Alignment is triangulation, not negotiation. A mirror cannot correct you." A list selected for receptiveness is a list selected to agree, which manufactures the disqualifying correlation.
2. Fig 10 :42: "A verifier that can reconstruct the claim cannot verify it." Asking a checker to accept the framework before they rule on the output spends the independence they contribute.
3. Fig 13 :134: "Unreachable is a verdict here, not a silence". A failed search for a willing reader is reported, not hidden.

Legally, scraping personal emails for unsolicited bulk outreach breaches GDPR and CAN-SPAM style rules in many jurisdictions. That is a practical failure on top of the structural one.
