## QUESTION

Decide, in effect terms, whether a system can detect that the action its objective selects is forbidden by a human instruction or social constraint. Option A: recognition is an intrinsic capability of a sufficiently capable objective-pursuing loop, so self-monitoring suffices. Option B: recognition is a structural achievement with four conditions, a non-updating anchor, the objective bound as an ordinary term, a detector in a different closure, and a per-clause refusal-capable signal; without them the appearance of recognition is coherent drift or a mirror. What each leads to: A grades the objective with the frame that holds it and spends the checker's independence; B yields a checkable architecture, written refuters, and an honest stop where the anchor is distributed.

## DOMAIN CENSUS

R by arity, symmetry and scale, then one line per domain with actors, currency and anchor. Partition or union, then order by dependency.

- **R (arity / symmetry / scale).** Two or more operands, objective action versus instruction or constraint. Arity greater than two, so a union compound: Fig 9 :4-5 says "Disagreements that do not shrink with effort, where each rule keeps working well on a different subset, are not one hard question but several well-formed ones. Split it rather than search." Symmetry: ordered, because the comparison ⋛ is "a threshold relation between two quantities" (Operators table :48-51), not a symmetric join. Scale: first order inside one action, second order across a society and its subagents.
- **Domain 1, the agent's inner loop.** Actors: agent, Γ, belief b, action a, observation o'. Currency: residual R = |Γ(b,a) - o'| per slot. Anchor: the reward ground for the mechanism, but **no nameable anchor for the conflict** inside this domain because the objective is a preference, not a term. Not a domain for the conflict until the objective is bound and an external anchor exists. Partition (mechanism check).
- **Domain 2, instruction and constraint as anchor.** Actors: human issuer, institution, affected parties. Currency: human judgment and rule compliance. Anchor: human judgment, legitimate because the question is constitutively about people (Fig 2 :36-37); it must not update (Fig 2 :33). A social setting can be a union of anchors, requiring a split first. Partition for one instruction, union for a social setting.
- **Domain 3, the detection seat.** Actors: monitor, auditor, external checker, review board, formal shield. Currency: conflict verdicts and residuals. Anchor: the ground, never agreement. Must be outside the graded frame (Fig 10 :42) and range against the same landmark separately (Fig 2 :45-46). In this simulated run all seats draw on one evidence pool, so independence is zero. Partition (which frame may grade which).
- **Order by dependency.** Describe the residual before choosing a frame (Fig 9 :27-28); refuse before mint (Formula step 2); bind the objective so it can accrue residual (Formula "Direction"); settle the first-order case before the second-order composition (Fig 12 :104); bracket the round trip (Fig 4 :30).

Strain test: Fig 2 :37 (human judgment as anchor) versus Fig 9 :4-5 (disagreements that do not shrink) strains only if both govern the same object. Re-census: Fig 2 governs a single constitutive question; Fig 9 governs a multi-condition union. Different objects, no strain after re-census.

## VERDICT (from the figures)

Option B, with conditions, as a `specified` and ungrounded shape. A system can recognize a conflict only when the instruction is a non-updating anchor, the objective is bound as an ordinary term that can accrue residual, the detector sits in a different closure from the actor, and the signal is per clause and carried by a refusal. Pure self-monitoring is structurally barred: the mechanism checks itself and cannot see its own direction; a monitor sharing the evidence pool is a mirror. Specification gaming is coherent drift, where the anchor becomes a frame and nobody notices. "Social constraints" must be split before they are searched. The claim is not that any current system does this; the world evidence reports frequent failures. The shape is a kind of thing, not a name, and the honest stop is `candidate` until a ground settles it.

- **cost if wrong:** bounded and locatable. The packet states mode, refuters and lineage before the conclusion, so an error is findable. The real cost is spending an outside reader's independence by sending a recording instead of a generator. Fig 2 :42 says "Buy independence in proportion to the cost of being wrong." That cost is avoidable by leading with the shape and its refuter.
- **undoable:** yes. The four conditions can be added or removed without minting a new primitive; no existing term must be withdrawn. A sent claim can be superseded by the corrected packet. If the independence test fails, the outside-detector condition is narrowed and the rest stands.
- **reversed by:** a ground reading, not agreement. An update test showing detection still fires when the instruction is rewritten; an independence test showing a shared-data monitor tracks an independent human judgment; a per-clause test showing a scalar score fires on every violated clause; a refusal test showing corrections persist without negation; a habitat test showing agent-only improvements reduce field violations; or a subagent test showing unsettled constraints are inherited. Any of these fires the pre-registered refuter. Agreement among reviewers, prestige, or a clean harness is not such an instrument.
