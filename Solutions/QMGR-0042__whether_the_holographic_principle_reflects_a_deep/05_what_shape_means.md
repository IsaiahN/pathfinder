## 1. A shape is a constraint, not a filler

The canonical README states the limit: the framework returns *"what a missing thing would have to look like (a shape, not a name)."* Fig 9 :32: *"you get a kind of thing, never the answer itself."* Fig 13 :134-135: *"It can say the answer is out of reach with the library as it stands... It cannot say which primitive is missing, because naming it would mean already holding it."*

From *The Library of Babel* (opened this session): *"you can cut your search short if the set of books returned is small and descriptive enough that it provides a general shape of the answer that satisfies your question."* And: *"With each new term gained, you are able to narrow the space by definition and deepen your vocabulary."*

So a **shape** is the narrowed form of an answer you can state before you can name it. It constrains; it does not fill. For this question:

- **Shape:** a cross-kind composition in which a structure mediates between a geometric state and an informational structure, with a boundary-indexed residual and a nonzero round-trip gap, whose irreducibility is assigned at a level.
- **Name (withheld):** entanglement, minimal surface, quantum error-correcting code, "geometry reduces to counting", "the boundary is more fundamental". Each is a binding or a verdict, not something the framework returns.

## 2. How a shape is obtained

- *Library of Babel* (opened): use the vocabulary you already have that is *closest* to the thing, look for recurring new terms that overlap or correlate with it, recurse. This is how the residual was described in effect terms before any frame was chosen.
- Fig 6 :30: *"The description travels. Fails by being sharpened into terms only we can read. Cast it in effect terms."* Fig 9 :30-31: *"your words are borrowed too, so describe the effect rather than the cause."*
- *Decoding Tacit Knowledge* (mapping carried from the exemplar): *"When contact isn't possible, the best you can send is a plain description of what you'd expect to see, because anyone can check that against the world."*
- *Library of Babel* on stopping: the answer is reached when *"the finish line will also be the starting place"*, and the paradox is that by then you have *"composed the answer through the process of locating it."*

## 3. What licenses a name

Only two things, both outside the shape itself:

1. **The ground settles it.** Formula step 5: *"the ground settles it. Verified only by what pays."* No experiment currently tests the holographic principle, so nothing here is settled.
2. **A provenanced import from a frame whose closure differs** (Fig 6 :23, Fig 8 :50-52), with a source gate and a shadow test. The entanglement and code bindings are imports with explicit provenance in `10_source_ledger.csv`; they carry no verdict weight until a discriminator pays for them.

*They're Scanning Books* (mapping carried from the exemplar): *"The real import is not a new term in the existing space. It is a new dimension of the space."* And: *"A minimum viable set of six terms is a human's careful guess at reality's compression axes; another sensorium's minimum set need not be those six."* Also: *"Declaring the primitives does not make them neutral. ## 4. The specific error this question invites: source named from a reading

*The Hard Problem is About Source* (opened this session) gives the mechanism. Metamerism: an instrument reads green and *"it cannot comment on the source"*; one green light, or yellow plus blue, or RGB, or CMYK, or a scheme not yet discovered, all give the same reading. *"If several paths create the same behavior, then behavior is a projection with lossy information."* *"An instrument that can't distinguish these cases demonstrates its own inadequacy."* It was the spectrometer, not an argument, that resolved it.

Applied here: reading the entropy-area law and naming its source as "information and geometry are the same thing" is the metamerism error. Several sources can produce an area law: horizon thermodynamics, finite-correlation-length lattice models (Wolf et al. 2008), quantum information of separable systems (Fields et al. 2022). The reading does not determine the source. Adlam 2023 makes the same point in a different register by distinguishing ontological and epistemic interpretations of the covariant bound and asking which direction the explanation runs. What is owed is a source-discriminating instrument, not a name.

## 5. What licenses the word "deep"? ANSWERED

**Nothing in the framework.** The framework returns a shape at a level, and Fig 12 :104 says *"So irreducible is a fact about a level and never about the world, and there is no bottom."* Fig 4 :33: *"Which is why there is no top and no infinite regress."* So "deep" as level-independent irreducibility is a refused question. The re-grounded, answerable form is: at what level is the relation settled, what composes over it, and what would a lower level have to look like? That is a `specified`, ungrounded question. The absolute form is a `gap`, which is a verdict and not silence (Fig 13 :134).

**Does the folder commit the metamerism error?** The physics frame sometimes does: De Haro et al. 2026 note that *"'t Hooft privileges a more fundamental, lower-dimensional, and potentially deterministic boundary description"*, which names a source from a privileged reading. This folder does not adopt that privilege. It records the privilege as a D2 binding with provenance and leaves the source question open.

## 5b. Correction to my own §3 (kept visible)

I initially wrote "shape, not a name" as if it forbade naming entanglement, minimal surfaces or codes. That overstates it. The README's phrase is *"what a **missing** thing would have to look like (a shape, not a name)."* It governs a primitive not held in Γ. Entanglement, minimal surfaces and error-correcting codes are bindings onto held atoms or compositions of them, so naming them is provenance, not the invention of a new primitive. What remains wrong is claiming the framework *derives* which one. That stays a `gap`. Both the narrowed rule and the original overstatement are recorded here so neither is quietly lost. This matches the correction in the exemplar's `05` section 5b.

## 6. Two consequences for outreach

**Prestige is not action.** From *The Recursive Self-Improvement Trap* (opened this session): *"Prestige can fund an action. It can give an action a budget to be tried. But it cannot settle an action. Only the ground can settle it. The Ground Maintainer must be blind to prestige."* A campaign that gathers famous names to agree that holography is deep is the named collapse, not corroboration. The legitimate ask is a refutation from a different closure, recorded before any theory is shown.

**My own audit is a simulation, not a ground reading.** Same essay: *"LLMs can quickly simulate an approximate answer/output with the formula found in the architecture post"* but *"the truly testable output would need to be derived from the real architecture or working the formula yourself"*; LLM output is *"simulacra."* So `01`, `06`, `07` and `09` are simulated derivations checked for internal consistency against the figures. They are not tests of the claim and must be labelled as such. They cannot settle anything they report on.
