# Outreach draft - validate the output first, then decide about the theory

**Use:** one-to-one, or as a cover letter attached to a submission through the public venues in `03_public_contact_points.csv`. **Not** for bulk send.

**Hard precondition:** do not send until the two-stage order is respected. Stage 1 must be answered and recorded before stage 2 is offered. The email never asks for agreement, never asks for trust, and never suggests the recipient is being selected because they are likely to agree.

**Subject:** Check one output about information and geometry before you decide whether the framing is worth reading

> Hi [name],
>
> I have one specific output I would like you to check against what you already know. I am not asking you to read the framework first, and I am not asking you to agree with it. If the output does not survive, there is nothing else to look at.
>
> **The output (`general` on the shape, `specified` on the per-domain claims, not `grounded`):** the question "is the holographic principle a deep fact about how information relates to geometry?" is two questions, not one. They need separate verdicts.
>
> 1. Is there a lawful relation? Under a six-term substrate (states: space, time, energy; structures: spectrum, gradient, network), composition runs across the two kinds and never within one. So information and geometry cannot stand in a direct identity; a structure must mediate. The framework lists "Bekenstein and holography" among four known cases where two low-overlap frames converged on one result. It adds that the claim "has not been tested forward." That is a retrodiction, not a prediction of the framework.
> 2. Is it "deep" in the sense of level-independent irreducibility? That question is refused as malformed. Irreducibility is a fact about a level and never about the world, and there is no bottom. What survives is the answerable level question: at what level is the relation settled, and what composes over it there.
>
> Two further structure claims: bulk to boundary is a transform across a scale membrane with a nonzero round-trip gap, and the gap is what bulk reconstruction is measuring. And discreteness, including bits per Planck area, is a composition residue, not a new primitive. It appears in finite-correlation-length lattice models too, so it is not unique to gravity.
>
> **What I am not claiming:** that the framework derived the entropy-area law. It did not. I am not claiming a microscopic name for the mediating structure. The framework returns a shape, not a name, so "entanglement", "minimal surface" and "quantum error-correcting code" are bindings from the physics literature onto held atoms, recorded with provenance, not derivations. I am not claiming the entropy bound is ontological rather than epistemic; that direction-of-explanation question is open. I am not claiming this settles the question. This is a simulated derivation checked for internal consistency, not a ground reading, and no experiment tests holography.
>
> **What would refute the shape claim (written before this message):**
> 1. derive one of the six terms from the other five, or derive discreteness as a primitive;
> 2. show a direct state-to-information identity is composable under the set;
> 3. demonstrate an exact invertible bulk-boundary map with zero round-trip gap;
> 4. show a frame can certify its own bottom level;
> 5. show the Bekenstein and AdS-CFT lines drew on one evidence pool;
> 6. measure the entropy law to be extensive where boundary-indexing is predicted.
>
> If any one holds, the corresponding claim is withdrawn. A one-line "this fails because..." is more useful to me than agreement. If you would rather not engage, no reply is needed.

## The two-stage offer, explicit

- **Stage 1 (this email):** the shape claim, the two separate verdicts, and the pre-registered refuter. Ask what the recipient would count as a failure. Record their verdict before showing any framework material.
- **Stage 2, opt-in only:** if they are curious, offer the three source links. Fig 10 :42 - *"A verifier that can reconstruct the claim cannot verify it."* Seat 4 must not hold the interiors. Later agreement is never pooled as corroboration (Fig 2 :40).
- **Stage 3, opt-in only:** offer the formula as a method. Fig 4 :22 permits sending a generator. Each user needs a ground per problem and writes their own refuter first.
