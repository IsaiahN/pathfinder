## 1. The claim, in one paragraph

The question "is the holographic principle a deep fact about how information relates to geometry?" is a union of two questions with different anchors. On the relation: the figures name "Bekenstein and holography" as a case where two low-overlap frames converged, so a lawful information-geometry relation is real, though the framework did not predict it. Information and geometry are different kinds, so a structure must mediate; the information is boundary-indexed rather than a volume aggregate; the bulk-boundary map is a scale transform with a nonzero, measurable round-trip gap; and discreteness is a composition residue, not a seventh primitive. On depth: irreducibility is a fact about a level and never about the world, and there is no bottom, so absolute depth is refused as malformed and reported as a `gap`. The framework returns the shape any candidate answer must have, plus refuters written in advance.

## 2. Three checkable sub-claims

### C1 - framework-internal; checkable by reading Figure 8 and Figure 2

- **Statement:** the existence of an information-geometry relation is corroborated by a named case of triangulation between two independent frames, and this is a retrodiction, not a prediction.
- **Source, verbatim:** Fig 8 :56-57 - *"The triangulation claim fits four cases already known and has not been tested forward: Wiles and Frey, Montgomery and Dyson, Bekenstein and holography, the relativization barriers in complexity theory."* Also Fig 2 :31-32 - *"Independence, between two frames, is how little they draw on the same evidence."* and *"Independence is what makes agreement mean something."* And Fig 4 :25 - *"A recording carried upward looks like knowledge and is a description of one occasion."*
- **What to check:** is the Bekenstein thermodynamics line and the AdS-CFT line genuinely low-overlap, or did one derive from the other? Is the framework's "fit" structural, or a post-hoc label?
- **Refuter:** show the two frames drew on one evidence pool, or show no structural match. If either holds, C1 falls to single-source and the retrodiction label becomes the whole claim.

### C2 - framework-internal and physics-facing; checkable by reading Figure 13 and Figure 4

- **Statement:** information and geometry are different kinds, so a structure mediates; the residual is boundary-indexed; and the bulk-boundary map has a nonzero round-trip gap.
- **Source, verbatim:** Fig 13 :75 - *"Two states never bond directly. It is not space-time but space, a structure, time; and two structures join only through a state."* Fig 1 :19 - *"The residual is indexed by object slot, not aggregated."* Fig 4 :27 - *"The transform between scales loses information, and that is the point."* Fig 4 :30 - *"So take the round trip and measure the gap. Without it you are navigating by dead reckoning: confident about a position nobody checked."*
- **What to check:** is the mapping from "state/structure" to geometry/information faithful? Is the round-trip gap a real feature of bulk reconstruction (Almheiri, Dong and Hartnoll 2015: *"the question of whether bulk operator reconstruction works only in the causal wedge or all the way to the extremal surface"*), or is duality an exact invertible map with zero gap?
- **Refuter:** derive a direct state-to-information identity under the six-term set; or demonstrate an exact invertible bulk-boundary map with zero gap; or measure extensive entropy scaling where boundary-indexing is predicted. Any one breaks C2.

### C3 - framework-internal; checkable by reading Figure 12 and Figure 4

- **Statement:** absolute, level-independent "depth" is not an answerable question. Irreducibility is assigned at a level; there is no bottom and no top.
- **Source, verbatim:** Fig 12 :104 - *"So irreducible is a fact about a level and never about the world, and there is no bottom"*; Fig 12 :103 - *"Composition is recursive, and the level decides what is irreducible."* Fig 4 :33 - *"Which is why there is no top and no infinite regress."* Fig 8 :37 - *"A frame cannot certify its own limit."*
- **What to check:** does the no-bottom rule actually follow from the recursion, or is it an assumption? Is the "malformed question" verdict a legitimate re-grounding or an evasion?
- **Refuter:** derive one of the six terms from a genuinely deeper primitive, or show a frame can certify its own bottom. Either revives absolute depth and breaks C3.

## 3. What is explicitly not claimed

- Not that the framework derived or predicted the entropy-area law. Both are retrodictions.
- Not that geometry reduces to counting, or that the boundary is the fundamental description. Those are bindings from the physics frame (De Haro et al. 2026 records 't Hooft's privilege of a *"more fundamental, lower-dimensional, and potentially deterministic boundary description"*), not figure results.
- Not a name for the mediating structure. Entanglement, minimal surfaces and error-correcting codes are provenanced bindings; the framework returns a shape, not a name.
- Not an ontological reading of the entropy bound over an epistemic one. Adlam 2023 leaves both live.
- Not a theory of everything; no last room and no bottom.
- Not `grounded`. Nothing here is asserted as settled by experiment, and no experiment currently tests the holographic principle.

## 4. Six pre-registered refuters (written before the run)

1. Derive one of the six terms from the other five, or derive discreteness as a primitive alone.
2. Show a direct state-to-information identity is composable under the set.
3. Demonstrate an exact invertible bulk-boundary map with zero round-trip gap for all states and subregions.
4. Show a frame can certify its own bottom level.
5. Show the Bekenstein and AdS-CFT lines drew on one evidence pool.
6. Measure the entropy law to be extensive where boundary-indexing is predicted.

If any fires, the corresponding claim is withdrawn. Until one is run, the status is `candidate`.
