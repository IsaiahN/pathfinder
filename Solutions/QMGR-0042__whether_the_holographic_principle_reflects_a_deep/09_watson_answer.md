## QUESTION

Decide, in effect terms, whether the holographic principle reflects a deep fact about how information relates to geometry, and what each option leads to. Option A: assert that the entropy-area law discloses an irreducible, level-independent identity between information and geometry. Leads to: an absolute-depth verdict, a reduction of geometry to counting, a privileged boundary description, and a terminal bottom-level explanation. Option B: treat the question as a union of two questions, one about the existence and form of the relation, one about the meaning of "deep". Leads to: a lawful relation corroborated as a shape and a retrodiction, plus a refusal of absolute depth as malformed, with a `gap` recorded instead of a name. The figures decide between these. World sources are secondary corroboration only.

## VERDICT (from the figures)

Split the question. **On the relation:** yes, a lawful information-geometry relation exists, and the framework names the case as a triangulation between independent frames, but only as a retrodiction. Its shape is a cross-kind composition: information is boundary-indexed, bulk to boundary is a scale transform with a measurable round-trip gap, and discreteness is a composition residue rather than a primitive. **On "deep":** the absolute reading is refused as malformed, because irreducibility is a fact about a level and never about the world and there is no bottom. The answerable level question is left open as a `gap`, not silence. The framework returns no microscopic name. This is a `general` shape with `specified` per-domain relations, ungrounded and `candidate`. The Bekenstein and AdS-CFT convergence is the strongest figure-grounded point; the no-bottom rule is the strongest reason to refuse the yes/no framing.

- **cost if wrong:** bounded and locatable. The packet is mode-labelled, states six refuters before the conclusion and records the lineage, so an error is findable rather than invisible. The real cost is spending the independence of outside readers by sending a recording (a prestige-weighted "deep truth" claim) instead of a generator. Fig 2 :42 prices that: "Buy independence in proportion to the cost of being wrong." The cost is avoidable by leading with the two verdicts and the refuter.
- **undoable:** yes. The refused depth verdict and the withheld names are removable without touching the derivation; `07_the_tether_answer.md` and `08_expert_verification_packet.md` already remove them. A sent claim can be superseded by the corrected packet, and no primitive has to be withdrawn. A refutation adds a term that refuses and removes nothing (Fig 6 :41-42).
- **reversed by:** a ground reading, not agreement. An instrument that discriminates the ontological from the epistemic reading of the bound would close the objective link; an exact invertible bulk-boundary map with zero gap would refute F3; extensive entropy scaling where boundary-indexing is predicted would break F2 at the ground; deriving one of the six from the other five, admitting a direct state-to-information identity, showing a frame can certify its bottom, or showing the Bekenstein and AdS-CFT lines shared one evidence pool would each break a stated finding. Until one fires, the verdict stands as `candidate`, and agreement from the same evidence pool cannot change it.
