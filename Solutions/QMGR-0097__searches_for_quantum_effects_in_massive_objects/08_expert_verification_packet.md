# Expert verification packet: the Tether on quantum effects in massive objects

**What this is:** a claim in a form an experimentalist or foundation-of-physics researcher can check. Mode, derivation and refuters are stated before the conclusion, so a reader knows what to look for and what would settle it.

**Mode: `general` on the shape, `specified` on the three sub-claims, not `grounded`.** No measurement is claimed. This is a shape and a route, not a result, and it is a simulated derivation checked for internal consistency against the figures. It cannot settle the question it reports on.

## 1. The claim, in one paragraph

Searches for quantum effects in massive objects are a licensed inward instrument extension from a platform already returning readings, not a search for a proto-instrument. The phrase "quantum to classical transition with mass" bundles two conditions, instrument reach and theory closure, and must be unbundled before it is searched. The missing middle link is a certificate that decides classical versus quantum from the full measured distribution per run, not simply a larger mass or a single visibility number. A null result at any mass is a bound, not a proof of absence. If a genuine boundary is ever established, it must arrive as an import with provenance and a shadow test, because a visibility falloff is a reading and a reading does not comment on its source. The framework returns that shape, four falsifiers written in advance, and the instruments that would close it. It does not name a mass.

## 2. Three checkable sub-claims

### C1 - framework-internal and physics-facing; checkable against Fig 6 and the experiment lineage

- **Statement:** the mass-scale programme is an INWARD extension of an instrument already returning something. A collapse mechanism is an import from a frame whose closure differs, and an import owes a source gate and a shadow test.
- **Source, verbatim:** Fig 6 :32, **"An instrument is not built from a description. It is improved from a worse instrument already returning something."** Fig 6 :21, **"MINT composes inside the closure and can never add an atom."** Fig 6 :23, **"No frame escapes its own closure. The bound moves only on contact with a frame whose closure differs."**
- **What to check:** is ground-state cooling and mild delocalisation of levitated nanoparticles already a reading, so that the sharper trap is built on a residual the last one produced? Is a collapse term genuinely absent from the standard quantum closure, so that no amount of mass-scaling derives it? Has any claimed boundary supplied provenance for the new term and a shadow test (what gap it explains that the old closure could not)?
- **Refuter:** show that the relevant regime has never registered at any resolution, so this is a proto-instrument search; or derive a collapse rate from standard quantum dynamics with no added parameter or stochastic term. Either kills C1.

### C2 - physics-facing; checkable by an experimentalist against their own practice

- **Statement:** the residual is a union of reach and closure, and the certificate is the uninstrumented middle link. Mass, pressure, visibility and trial count are filters for the budget, never the witness.
- **Source, verbatim:** Fig 9 :54, **"And when the split lands on a union rather than a partition, unbundle before you search again."** Fig 3 :27, **"A link with no instrument is not a link that works. It is a link nobody has looked at."** Fig 9 :60, **"Use filters for the budget and witnesses for the verdict. Never let a filter hand you a verdict."**
- **What to check:** do interferometric and non-interferometric tests really address different conditions, as Carlesso et al. state when they note that the latter *"do not require one to prepare the system in a quantum superposition"*? Does a likelihood-ratio-style certificate distinguish the classical and quantum distributions in a regime where interference visibility has already gone to zero, as Riera-Campeny et al. report? If yes, the certificate is an instrument for the middle link.
- **Refuter:** show that one lever clears both conditions (a partition, so no unbundling is owed); or show that visibility is the only possible certificate, so the budget filter has become the witness; or show that the chosen statistic embeds the answer and a convention has started deciding the outcome (Fig 2 :43, *"a convention that decides an outcome has stopped being one"*).

### C3 - framework-internal; checkable by reading Figures 1, 8, 9 and 13

- **Statement:** the residual is per slot, a null result is not an absence, and the framework returns a shape rather than a mass.
- **Source, verbatim:** Fig 1 :19, **"The residual is indexed by object slot, not aggregated."** Fig 8 :39, **"So a search that finds nothing is never a proof of absence."** Fig 9 :32, **"you get a kind of thing, never the answer itself"**; Fig 13 :134, **"Unreachable is a verdict here, not a silence"**.
- **What to check:** are these four lines present verbatim in `FRAMEWORK/reference/03-FIGURES_TEXT.md`? Is there any figure line that names a mass or selects a collapse model? If not, the slot is correctly left open. Does averaging visibility across mass regimes throw away the one mass where a falloff begins? The certification paper's finding that the full-distribution test needs exponentially fewer trials than visibility is the world-side check on the per-slot claim.
- **Refuter:** exhibit a figure line that, after re-census, names a mass or a mechanism; or demonstrate that the per-run distribution carries no information beyond visibility. Then C3 is withdrawn.

## 3. What is explicitly not claimed

- Not that quantum mechanics fails at any mass. No ground reading at that mass is used here.
- Not that collapse models are wrong, and not that they are established. They are imports with gates that have not been run in this derivation.
- Not that decoherence explains the falloff. Decoherence is a contact reading; absence is a different reading. They are not averaged.
- Not a seventh substrate term. Mass is an ordering (spectrum), not a new kind (Fig 13 :20, :43).
- Not grounded. Nothing in this packet was settled by an experiment in this derivation.
- Not a test of any specific levitated-nanoparticle protocol. This is a simulated derivation (Fig 11 :70: *"A test harness is a substituted habitat. Which is why a synthetic solve proves wiring and never capability."*).

## 5. What would settle it

1. A certificate that still discriminates classical from quantum when visibility has vanished, at a stated mass and with a declared significance level.
2. Two independent instruments with disjoint evidence pools returning the same mass threshold, with separate records of provenance (Fig 8 :50-52: convergent derivation and adopted import look identical in the contents; only the record separates them).
3. A sourced import with a shadow test that explains a gap the old closure could not, and a ground reading that pays for it.
4. A figure re-census that either names a mass or proves the split is a partition. That would refute the shape rather than settle the physics.
