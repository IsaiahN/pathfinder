# The Tether's answer: searches for quantum effects in massive objects

**Mode: `general` on the shape, `specified` on the three per-domain rulings, not `grounded`.** This is a shape and a route, not a result. Simulated derivation checked against the figures (`05_what_shape_means.md` section 6), not a ground reading. No measurement was taken.

## 1. The residual it answers

A thinned channel between a predicted quantum position distribution and a measured one at increasing mass, with three bundled readings: budget (the instrument has not reached the regime), contact (the environment decoheres faster than the protocol), and absence (the dynamics needs a new term). It is a union compound, not one hard question. Fig 9 :57: *"a case in two conditions is not a harder case of either."* It has its own loop and its own ground, the measured distribution under a declared null.

## 2. The shape

1. **The search is a licensed inward extension, not a hunt for a proto-instrument.** Fig 6 :32: **"An instrument is not built from a description. It is improved from a worse instrument already returning something."** Levitated optomechanics already returns ground-state cooling and mild delocalisation, and each sharper trap is built on the residual the last one produced (Fig 6 :33). This changes contact, so it is not an agent-only improvement (Fig 11 :40).
2. **The three readings must be kept separate, per slot.** Fig 1 :19: **"The residual is indexed by object slot, not aggregated."** Each mass and each run is a slot. A single averaged visibility across masses is how a live signal disappears (Fig 1 :22). A null result is a bound, not an absence: Fig 8 :39, **"So a search that finds nothing is never a proof of absence."**
3. **The missing middle link is a certificate, not a bigger mass.** Fig 3 :27: **"A link with no instrument is not a link that works. It is a link nobody has looked at."** The likelihood-ratio test supplies an instrument for deciding classical versus quantum from the full distribution, and it succeeds in regimes where visibility has already vanished. Mass, visibility and trial count remain filters for the budget (Fig 9 :60: *"Use filters for the budget and witnesses for the verdict. Never let a filter hand you a verdict."*).
4. **A genuine boundary would be an import with gates, never a mint.** Fig 6 :21: **"MINT composes inside the closure and can never add an atom."** Fig 6 :23: **"No frame escapes its own closure. The bound moves only on contact with a frame whose closure differs."** A collapse term is a new atom from a different closure. It owes provenance (source gate) and a shadow test: what gap does it explain that the old closure could not? Until those gates are passed, the mass slot stays open.
5. **The framework returns a shape, not a mass.** Fig 9 :32: **"you get a kind of thing, never the answer itself"**; Fig 13 :134: **"Unreachable is a verdict here, not a silence"**. No figure line selects 10^10 amu, CSL, or Diósi-Penrose. The shape is the three-part constraint above. Naming a collapse parameter from a visibility reading is the metamerism error diagnosed in `05_what_shape_means.md` section 3: a reading does not comment on its source.

## 3. What it explicitly does not say

- Not that quantum mechanics fails above any mass. No ground reading at that mass exists in this derivation.
- Not that collapse models are wrong. They are imports with gates, and the gates have not been run here. Their parameters are bounded by experiments, and large regions of parameter space are already excluded (Carlesso et al., 2022), which is a filter result, not a verdict on the superposition principle.
- Not that decoherence is the whole explanation. Decoherence is a contact fact; absence is a different reading, and the two must not be averaged.
- Not that the six-term substrate gains a seventh member. Mass is an ordering (spectrum), not a new kind (Fig 13 :20, :43).
- Not a theory of everything, and not grounded. Nothing here is settled by experiment.

## 4. What would settle it: the ground

A source-discriminating instrument, not a further argument. *The Hard Problem is About Source*: an instrument that reads green *"cannot comment on the source"*; the spectrometer resolved it. Here the corresponding questions are:

- **Does the certificate hold when the visibility does not?** A likelihood-ratio-style test that distinguishes classical from quantum distributions at a mass where fringes have vanished would move the boundary question from budget to contact.
- **Do two instruments with disjoint evidence pools return the same mass threshold?** Interferometric superposition tests and non-interferometric heating or emission tests share almost no evidence. Agreement between them would be corroboration at maximum independence (Fig 2 :31), not the pool talking.
- **Does a sourced import predict the shape?** A collapse model with stated provenance and a shadow test that explains a gap the old closure could not.
- **Standing falsifiers from section 3 of `01_figure_grounded_audit.md`:** a figure line that names a mass after re-census; a partition proof; a settled threshold from two independent instruments plus a gated import; visibility shown to be the only possible certificate; or an improvement that changes vocabulary without changing contact.

## 5. The honest stop

**Unreached, not unreachable.** The six-term set is *"a human's careful guess at reality's compression axes"* as recorded in the exemplar's essay notes; declaring it makes the choice visible, not neutral. This derivation stops at the limit of what the available instruments distinguish and records the lineage so the stop is checkable. The figures permit a shape, an import gate and a refuter, not a mass. *"Indiscernibility is not identity."* Our own simulation is a substituted habitat (Fig 11 :70), so it proves wiring and never capability.

## 6. One-paragraph version for outreach

> The Tether does not name a mass at which quantum mechanics stops working, and it does not pick a collapse model. It says that the levitated-nanoparticle programme is a legitimate inward extension of an instrument already returning readings, that the current "quantum to classical transition" is a union of two conditions which must be unbundled before it is searched, and that the thing missing from the middle of the chain is a certificate for reading the full measured distribution, not simply a larger object. A null result is a bound, not a proof of absence. If a genuine boundary ever appears, it must arrive as a sourced import with a shadow test, because a visibility falloff is a reading and a reading does not comment on its source. What the framework offers is the shape any answer must have, four falsifiers written in advance, and the instruments that would close it. The mass slot stays open until the ground settles it.
