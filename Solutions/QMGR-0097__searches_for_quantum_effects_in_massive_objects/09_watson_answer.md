## QUESTION

Option A: read a falloff of coherence with mass as a verdict that quantum mechanics fails above some mass. ## DOMAIN CENSUS

R by arity, symmetry and scale, then one line per domain with actors / currency / anchor, partition or union, and order by dependency.

- **R (the residual).** A thinned channel between a predicted quantum position distribution and a measured one at increasing mass. Arity: three-place with a threshold, relating prediction, measurement and a mass ordering; it is a union, not a two-body question. Symmetry: broken, coherence is lost within a run and not regained; mass is an ordering (spectrum), and the comparison is a threshold relation, not a symmetric join. Scale: trial, cycle of trials, mass regime, instrument lineage, seat.
- **Domain 1, instrument and habitat.** Actors: nanoparticle, optical or magnetic trap, interferometer, residual gas, blackbody photons, cryostat, hypothesis test. Currency: visibility, coherence length, heating rate, trial count, mass in amu. Anchor: the measured position distribution. A maintained habitat; introduction is available, exclusion is not.
- **Domain 2, theory frames.** Actors: standard quantum mechanics with decoherence, CSL, Diósi-Penrose, gravity-induced collapse. Currency: predicted distributions and collapse parameters lambda, r_C, R_0. Anchor: the same experiment. Different closures; a collapse term is an import, not a mint.
- **Domain 3, certification and verification.** Actors: likelihood-ratio test, visibility test, significance level, statistical power. Currency: evidence against the classical null. Anchor: the data under a declared null, never agreement between reviewers.
- **Partition or union.** Union. D1 and D2 overlap at the mass boundary, which is a compound with its own loop. D2 and D3 overlap where collapse parameters are bounded by experiment. Fig 9 :54: *"And when the split lands on a union rather than a partition, unbundle before you search again."*
- **Order by dependency (Fig 3).** Perception, vocabulary, objective, planning, learn and carry. The middle link, having terms for what the situation affords and saying what counts as progress, is the one historically without an instrument.

## VERDICT (from the figures)

The shape survives; no mass is named. The levitated-nanoparticle programme is a licensed inward extension of an instrument already returning readings. The residual is a union of instrument reach and theory closure and must be unbundled. The missing middle link is a certificate that reads the full measured distribution per slot; mass, visibility and trial count are filters for the budget, never the witness. A null result is a bound, not a proof of absence. If a genuine boundary is established it must arrive as an import with provenance and a shadow test, because a falloff is a reading that cannot comment on its source. This is a `specified`, ungrounded result with a `general` shape. The mass slot stays open until the ground settles it.

- **cost if wrong:** bounded and locatable. The packet states its mode, carries four refuters before the conclusion and records the lineage, so an error is findable rather than invisible. The real cost is spending the independence of outside readers by sending a recording instead of a generator, which Fig 2 :42 prices: *"Buy independence in proportion to the cost of being wrong."* That cost is avoidable by leading with the shape and its refuter. A second cost is mistaking a budget limit for an absence and importing an unnecessary collapse atom, which Fig 8 :43 prices above starting from primitives.
- **undoable:** yes. No primitive is withdrawn and no mass is asserted, so no claim needs retraction if the ground returns a boundary. A sourced import can be added later, stamped with origin and sequence. A sent packet can be superseded by a corrected one.
- **reversed by:** a ground reading, not agreement. A source-discriminating certificate that still works where visibility has vanished would move the question from budget to contact; two independent instruments with disjoint evidence pools returning the same threshold would settle the mass; a sourced import with a shadow test paid for by experiment would name the mechanism; a figure re-census that names a mass or proves the split is a partition would refute the shape itself. Until one of these fires, the verdict stands as `candidate`.
