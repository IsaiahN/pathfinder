## 0. The residual, in effect terms

Levitated optomechanics is pushing the mass at which a centre-of-mass superposition can be prepared and certified upward. Neumeier et al. (PNAS, 2024) target interference of a silica nanoparticle above 10^8 atomic mass units; the Q-Xtreme programme aims at objects of billions of atoms, five orders of magnitude beyond the molecular benchmark; Carlesso et al. (2022) state that interferometric tests currently use particles of about 10^4 amu and that probing the GRW value would need 10^7 amu with a 180 nm superposition held for about 20 s. Collapse models predict a progressive breakdown with mass and have already had large regions of their parameter space excluded by LISA Pathfinder, cantilevers, cold atoms, X-ray emission, gravitational wave detectors, and even the heating of Neptune and neutron stars.

**The residual R:** a thinned channel between a predicted quantum position distribution and a measured one at increasing mass, with three possible readings that the current frame does not separate: (a) the instrument has not reached the regime where the effect would show (budget), (b) the environment decoheres the state faster than the protocol can outrun it (contact), or (c) the superposition principle genuinely breaks down and the dynamics needs a new term (absence). Stated as an effect: the visibility falls, and nothing in the present description says whether the fall is a budget fact, a contact fact, or an absence.

**What is being decided.** Not "is quantum mechanics valid at mass M" but "what would have to be true for a falloff to be a boundary rather than a budget limit, and what instrument would discriminate those two." Option A reads a null as a verdict on quantum mechanics. Option B keeps the three readings separate, extends the instrument from one already returning something, and requires a sourced import before a boundary is asserted.

**Direction bound as an ordinary term.** The sought-for shape is written down now so it can accrue a residual: an answer must (i) name the instrument lineage being extended, (ii) name the certificate that decides classical versus quantum, and (iii) state the gate a genuine departure must pass. This is the remedy in Fig 5 :62-66: *"make the sought-for shape an ORDINARY TERM. Write down what an answer would look like, bind it, and let it accrue a residual like anything else."*

## 1. Domain census (before any figure line was chosen)

### 1a. R by arity, symmetry and scale

- **Arity:** three-place with a threshold. R relates a predicted distribution, a measured distribution, and a mass parameter that orders them. It is not a two-body quantum-versus-classical question; it is a union of instrument reach and theory closure (Fig 9 :22 names *"arity · symmetry · scale"* as the description to write before looking).
- **Symmetry:** mass is an ordering, not a symmetric join. Fig 13 :20: *"ordering, so more and less can be said."* The transition is directional: coherence is lost and is not regained by making the object smaller again within one run. The relevant comparison is a threshold relation, Fig 12 :85: *"a threshold relation between two quantities"*, not the symmetric *"both hold at once"* bond (Fig 12 :55-59).
- **Scale:** step (one nanoparticle trial), cycle (repeated trials accumulating a distribution), level (molecule, nanoparticle, macroscopic object), instrument lineage (optical trapping to ground-state cooling to delocalisation protocols), seat (experimenter, certifier, outside reader). Fig 4 :19-20: *"Only the actors, the currency and the anchor change."*

### 1b. One line per domain: actors, currency, anchor

| domain | actors | currency | anchor |
|---|---|---|---|
| **D1 Instrument and habitat** | nanoparticle, optical or magnetic trap, interferometer, residual gas, blackbody photons, cryostat, hypothesis test | visibility, coherence length, heating rate, trial count N, mass in amu | the measured position distribution and its test statistic |
| **D2 Theory frames** | standard quantum mechanics with decoherence, CSL, Diósi-Penrose, gravity-induced collapse | predicted distribution, collapse rate lambda, lengths r_C and R_0 | the same experiment |
| **D3 Certification and verification** | likelihood-ratio test, visibility test, significance level, statistical power | evidence against the classical null | the data, never agreement between theorists |

No nameable anchor means it is not a domain yet. All three have one: a measured distribution (D1), the same measured distribution (D2), and the data under a declared null (D3). D1 and D2 range against the same anchor separately, which is exactly Fig 2 :46: *"Each frame is corrected by it separately, and what makes the correction sound is that neither frame can move it."*

### 1c. Partition or union

Union, not partition. D1 (can the instrument hold the state at all?) and D2 (does the theory contain a collapse term?) overlap in the compound "mass at which coherence fails." Fig 9 :54: *"And when the split lands on a union rather than a partition, unbundle before you search again."* Fig 9 :57: *"What remains is smaller and it is also categorically different: a case in two conditions is not a harder case of either."* A single lever, adding mass, clears the reach-only region and leaves the intersection standing. D2 and D3 also overlap: the collapse parameters are *"ultimately bounded by experiments"* (Carlesso et al., 2022), so the theory frame's free parameters are narrowed only at the certifier's ground.

### 1d. Order by dependency (Fig 3)

Perception: can position be resolved at this mass at all? Vocabulary: do we have a term that distinguishes decoherence from collapse? Objective: what counts as progress, visibility, likelihood ratio, or excess heating? Planning: which protocol and which trap? Learn and carry: exclusion plots compound across experiments. The chain is *"ordered by dependency, not by importance"* (Fig 3 :20). The middle link, vocabulary plus objective, is the one with no instrument historically; Fig 3 :27: *"A link with no instrument is not a link that works. It is a link nobody has looked at."* The certification paper names this exact gap: *"the certification process itself has received less attention."*

## 2. Figure census and findings

Verbatim quotes are taken from `FRAMEWORK/reference/03-FIGURES_TEXT.md` this session. A strain is named, not smoothed. Each finding has its own verdict; verdicts are never averaged.

### F1 - This is a licensed INWARD instrument-extension programme (triangulated, `specified`)

Fig 6 :32: **"An instrument is not built from a description. It is improved from a worse instrument already returning something."**

Fig 6 :33: **"Nobody specified a telescope from an account of Mars. A wandering point in the sky was already a reading, and every sharper instrument was built on the residual the last one produced."**

Fig 6 :34-35: **"Where nothing is, there is no edge to extend from, and the search for a proto-instrument is a search for one that is already there."**

Levitated optomechanics is not a search for a proto-instrument. It already returns readings: ground-state cooling of dielectric nanoparticles near 10^8 amu (Delić et al. 2020; Magrini et al. 2021; Tebbenjohanns et al. 2021, as cited by Neumeier et al.), and mild delocalisation beyond zero-point fluctuations (the certification paper). Each sharper trap and each shorter pulse is built on the residual the last one produced. Fig 11 :40 corroborates from a different figure: **"An improvement that does not change contact changes nothing, however much it improves."** The programme changes contact (higher mass, lower pressure, colder environment), so it is not an agent-only improvement.

Refuter: show that the relevant mass regime has never registered at any resolution (no proto-instrument), or that the improvements change vocabulary only and leave contact with the mass boundary unchanged.

### F2 - A null result at a given mass is not a proof of absence (triangulated, `specified`)

Fig 8 :39: **"So a search that finds nothing is never a proof of absence."**

Fig 9 :44: **"Only a sealed room can be searched to the end, and no frame is a sealed room. So searching and finding nothing proves nothing."**

Fig 8 :40-43: **"Underivable-in-principle and unreachable-at-this-budget are different facts, and they issue different instructions."** The same passage says that unreachable-at-budget means *"the term is already in the closure and was not found, so the remedy is more search, not another atom."*

This is the decisive practical distinction for this question. Carlesso et al. publish exclusion plots with white regions and state plainly that the bounds are *"ultimately bounded by experiments"*; the review's Figure 2 caption ends, *"The white area is yet to be explored."* A bound is a filter result, not a verdict on the superposition principle. Neumeier et al. note that in their parameter sweep, *"larger particle sizes are not accessible at room temperature"*, a budget fact about a specific protocol, not an absence of quantum behaviour at mass.

Refuter: produce a source-gated, shadow-tested atom that predicts a specific mass threshold and have two independent instruments return that threshold. Then it is no longer a budget fact and the verdict moves to the ground.

### F3 - The residual is a union and must be unbundled before searching (triangulated, `specified`)

Fig 9 :54: **"And when the split lands on a union rather than a partition, unbundle before you search again."**

Fig 9 :57: **"What remains is smaller and it is also categorically different: a case in two conditions is not a harder case of either."**

The "quantum to classical transition" bundles two conditions: can we prepare and read the state (D1), and does the dynamics need a new term (D2). World corroboration comes from a field with low overlap with the framework: Carlesso et al. divide tests into two classes, interferometric (prepare a superposition) and non-interferometric (monitor side effects without preparing one), and show that the latter is *"relatively easier as they do not require one to prepare the system in a quantum superposition."* Two conditions, two instruments, one compound. The certification paper adds a third unbundling: the certificate is separate from the preparation, and *"the certification process itself has received less attention."*

Refuter: show that one lever clears the whole residual, so the split is a partition. Then the unbundling finding fails and the union loop is withdrawn.

### F4 - The residual is indexed per mass and per run, never averaged (triangulated, `specified`)

Fig 1 :19: **"The residual is indexed by object slot, not aggregated."**

Fig 1 :22: **"Aggregating across slots is how a live signal disappears into an average."**

Fig 1 :23: **"The ground enters as the only metric."**

Each mass point and each protocol run is a slot. Averaging visibility across masses, or pooling runs into one headline number, is how the one mass where a falloff begins disappears. The certification paper is an independent witness from mathematical statistics: it shows that the visibility test reduces each run to a single contrast number, while the likelihood-ratio test *"leverages differences across the entire probability distribution"* and can succeed *"even when decoherence fully suppresses quantum interference visibility."* Their result that visibility is *"neither necessary nor efficient"* is the statistical version of Fig 1 :22.

Refuter: demonstrate that the per-run distribution carries no information the visibility does not, so the slot indexing is inert here. The certification paper's exponential reduction in required measurement count is the standing counter-evidence.

### F5 - A genuine collapse mechanism would be an import, not a derivation (triangulated, `specified`)

Fig 6 :21: **"MINT composes inside the closure and can never add an atom."**

Fig 6 :22: **"NOVEL means novel relative to atoms, not to the world. IMPORT adds an atom, and the added atom is what expands the closure."**

Fig 6 :23: **"No frame escapes its own closure. The bound moves only on contact with a frame whose closure differs."**

Fig 8 :32, cited for the mechanism: *"Closure is idempotent. Composing inside a closed set never leaves it, so no search, however long, adds an atom."* No amount of pushing standard quantum mechanics to larger mass manufactures a collapse term; the term arrives from a frame whose closure differs. CSL and Diósi-Penrose do exactly this: they add a nonlinear stochastic term with phenomenological parameters lambda, r_C, R_0. Fig 8 :9-13 requires a **source gate: provenance** and a **shadow test: explains a gap**. Carlesso et al. supply the provenance and the shadow: *"Collapse models provide a mathematically and physically consistent dynamical framework, where quantum superpositions and wavepacket reduction are combined,"* and *"In this framework, the Born rule is not assumed but derived."*

Refuter: derive a collapse rate from standard quantum mechanics alone with no added parameter or stochastic term. Then it is a mint, not an import, and this finding is withdrawn.

**Strain, named after re-census.** The imported atom is a dynamics, not a seventh substrate member. Fig 13 :43 names the danger: **"A continuum was the seventh and was removed for failing exactly this"** because *"a generating set must not contain what its own members produce."* Mass scale is an ordering (spectrum), not a new kind. Re-census resolves the apparent conflict: Fig 6 governs import of an atom into a frame's closure at any level; Fig 13 governs the minimum set of kinds. Different objects, no strain after the census.

### F6 - The instrument for the missing middle link is the certificate (triangulated, `specified`)

Fig 3 :19: **"Optimising a link that already works cannot rescue one that does not."**

Fig 3 :20: **"The chain is ordered by dependency, not by importance."**

Fig 9 :60: **"Use filters for the budget and witnesses for the verdict. Never let a filter hand you a verdict."**

Mass, visibility, pressure and trial count are filters for the budget. The witness is the measured distribution against the classical null. The certification paper supplies an instrument for the previously uninstrumented link between "we have a state" and "we can say what counts as progress": the likelihood-ratio test with a declared significance level and power. It does not hand us a verdict on quantum mechanics; it sharpens a convention for reading the ground. Fig 2 :41 permits this: agreement about a shared meaning *"is efficient for a convention"* even though *"disqualifying for a verdict"* applies to agreement about the world.

Refuter: show that the chosen statistic embeds the answer (a test calibrated so that quantum data must win), so the convention has started deciding an outcome. Fig 2 :43: *"a convention that decides an outcome has stopped being one."*

### F7 - The framework returns a shape, not a mass (triangulated, `general`)

Fig 9 :32: **"you get a kind of thing, never the answer itself"**

Fig 13 :134: **"Unreachable is a verdict here, not a silence"**

Fig 13 :135: **"It cannot say which primitive is missing, because naming it would mean already holding it."**

No figure line names a mass. The shape the figures license is: (i) the search is an inward extension from an instrument already returning something; (ii) the certificate must range against the ground per slot; (iii) a genuine boundary is a sourced import with a shadow test, and until one arrives the slot stays open. This is not silence. It is the recorded limit of what the available instruments distinguish, exactly the canonical rule *"a shape, not a name"* (`FIGURES_README_canonical.md`).

Independent corroboration from a low-overlap field (mathematical statistics, not framework author): Riera-Campeny, Maurer and Romero-Isart (arXiv:2506.22092, 2026) write, *"Distinguishing quantum from classical dynamics in the presence of decoherence is analogous to inferring the shape of an object concealed beneath a cloth. As decoherence increases, the cloth thickens, making the object harder to discern, but the underlying features remain and can therefore be tested."* They also note that *"while decoherence masks quantum interference effects, it does not destroy them."* This is the shape-not-a-name limit reached by a different route. Overlap with the framework is low; it is a genuine second witness for this one claim, still not a ground reading.

Refuter: a figure line that, after re-census, selects a mass or a collapse model. Then the slot is nameable and this finding is withdrawn.

### F8 - Agreement between theory groups is one pool, not corroboration (triangulated, `specified`)

Fig 2 :35: **"Alignment is triangulation, not negotiation. A mirror cannot correct you."**

Fig 2 :31: **"Independence, between two frames, is how little they draw on the same evidence."**

Fig 2 :40: **"disqualifying for a verdict: agreement about the world is worthless when the agreeing frames share an evidence pool"**

Collapse-model reviews, levitated-optomechanics proposals and certification papers cite the same corpus: LISA Pathfinder, cantilever heating, cold-atom diffusion, spontaneous X-ray emission, the Neutron star and Neptune heating bounds. Agreement among them is *"the pool talking, not two frames confirming each other"* (Fig 2 :21). The genuinely independent readings are instruments pointed at the same thing: interferometric superposition tests, non-interferometric heating and emission tests, gravitational-wave detectors, and astronomical heating bounds (Fig 2 :31: *"two instruments pointed at the same thing are"*). Buy independence in proportion to the cost of being wrong (Fig 2 :42).

Refuter: exhibit two readings whose evidence pools are disjoint and whose agreement is convergent derivation rather than adopted import (Fig 8 :50-52). Then the overlap warning does not apply to that pair.

### F9 - The habitat is maintained, enumerated, and cannot be subtracted (single-source for the figure, `specified`)

Fig 11 :40: **"An improvement that does not change contact changes nothing, however much it improves."**

Fig 11 :61: **"The seat's power is asymmetric. Introduction is available; exclusion is not."**

Fig 11 :70: **"A test harness is a substituted habitat. Which is why a synthetic solve proves wiring and never capability."**

The experimental seat can introduce cooling, vacuum, dark potentials and better statistics. It cannot subtract residual gas, blackbody photons, photon recoil or internal heating; contact is a fact about the world and does not consult the seat (Fig 11 :61-62). Fig 11 :43: **"The habitat is enumerated, never composed."** The list is read off the world: gas collisions, thermal photons, magnetic gradient noise, vibration, rotation of the crystal. Neumeier et al. enumerate exactly these sources and show that *"a single collision resolves the particle position"*, which is why the protocol must outrun them in time.

The third quote matters for this folder: a numerical simulation that assumes decoherence rates proves wiring, never capability. Our own derivation is a simulation. It cannot certify that the proposed protocol works against the ground.

Refuter: demonstrate an improvement that moves the ground reading without changing contact with the residual, or show that the habitat list is composed rather than enumerated. Neither has been shown here.

### F10 - The six-term substrate is not extended by this question (single-source, `general`)

Fig 13 :3: **"No system functions without any of them, none is built from the others, and everything else is a composition across the two kinds."**

Fig 13 :74: **"Everything else is a composition across the two kinds, never within one."**

Fig 13 :75: **"Two states never bond directly. It is not space-time but space, a structure, time; and two structures join only through a state."**

Mass is an ordering among states, not a seventh kind. Fig 13 :43 (*"A continuum was the seventh and was removed for failing exactly this"*) is the standing precedent. I initially considered Fig 13 :75 as bearing directly on mass superpositions; re-census shows it governs composition of the six primitives, not experimental mass scales. Withdrawn as a direct finding, retained here as a correction. The question does not add a primitive; it searches within a closed reach and, if a boundary is found, imports an atom from a different frame (F5).

Refuter: derive a new substrate member from this search, or show that the mass scale is composable from the six in a way the figure forbids.

### F11 - Round-trip gap from the figures to a lab claim is nonzero (single-source, `specified`)

Fig 4 :30: **"So take the round trip and measure the gap. Without it you are navigating by dead reckoning: confident about a position nobody checked."**

Sent up (x): the verbatim figure set, chiefly F1, F2, F5 and F7. Abstraction (T_A): "extend the instrument, keep the residual per slot, require an import for a genuine boundary." ## 3. Pre-registered refuter

Written before the conclusion and before the WATSON run, per Seat Map :287: *"a falsifier written before the run is a design instrument, not only an honesty device."*

The shape claim (an inward instrument extension plus a certificate; a genuine boundary would be a sourced import; no figure line names a mass) is refuted if any of the following holds:

1. A figure line, after a genuine re-census, names a mass threshold or selects a collapse mechanism. That breaks the shape-not-a-name limit (F7).
2. The residual is shown to be a partition rather than a union, so one lever clears it and the unbundling finding (F3) fails.
3. Two independent instruments with disjoint evidence pools return the same threshold, and a source-gated import with a shadow test predicts its shape. Then the ground has settled it and F2's budget reading is withdrawn.
4. A low-overlap world source shows that visibility is the only possible certificate, so the filter has become a witness and F4/F6 fail.
5. An instrument is shown to improve contact with the residual without changing contact with the ground, or the habitat is shown to be composed rather than enumerated (F9).

If any fires, the affected finding is retracted in the log below and the verdict is recomputed. Until one is run, no part of this may be labelled `grounded`.

## 4. Recommendation

1. Lead with the three-part shape and the refuter, not with a claim about a mass at which quantum mechanics fails (F7).
2. Keep the three readings (budget, contact, absence) separate in every write-up. Never average them into a single "quantum to classical transition" verdict (F3, Fig 1 :22).
3. Treat mass, visibility, pressure and N as filters for the budget only. The witness is the measured distribution under a declared null (F6, Fig 9 :60).
4. When a boundary is claimed, require the import gates: provenance of the new term and a shadow test that explains a gap the old closure could not (F5).
5. Ask outside readers what they would count as a failure of the shape claim. Record that verdict before showing any theory (Fig 10 :52: *"Bring a characterised residual, never the source."*).
6. Use the public venues in `03_public_contact_points.csv`, one to one. Do not select recipients for likely agreement (Fig 2 :40).

### Seven-question recursive derivation (summary)

1. **Is the search itself licensed?** Yes, INWARD. Fig 6 :32-35; the proto-instrument already returns readings (F1).
2. **Is the residual one question?** No, a union of reach and closure, unbundle first (Fig 9 :54-57, F3).
