## 1. A shape is a constraint, not a filler

The canonical limit in `FIGURES_README_canonical.md`: the framework returns *"what a missing thing would have to look like (a shape, not a name)."* Fig 9 :32: *"you get a kind of thing, never the answer itself"*. Fig 13 :134: *"Unreachable is a verdict here, not a silence"*, and :135: *"It cannot say which primitive is missing, because naming it would mean already holding it."*

From *The Library of Babel*: *"you can cut your search short if the set of books returned is small and descriptive enough that it provides a general shape of the answer that satisfies your question."*

For QMGR-0097 a **shape** is therefore the three-part constraint written down in `01_figure_grounded_audit.md` section 0:

- an inward instrument extension from something already registering,
- a per-slot certificate that ranges against the measured distribution,
- a gate (provenance plus shadow test) that any genuine boundary must pass.

It is not a mass, not a value of lambda, not a choice among CSL, Diósi-Penrose and gravity-induced collapse. `MASS = ???` is a shape. `MASS = 10^10 amu` is a name.

## 2. How a shape is obtained

- *Library of Babel*: use the vocabulary you already hold that is *"closest to the words that you wish to describe and grasp the shape of"*, then bootstrap. *"With each new term gained, you narrow the space and deepen your vocabulary."*
- Fig 6 :30: *"The description travels. Fails by being sharpened into terms only we can read. Cast it in effect terms."* Fig 9 :30-31: *"your words are borrowed too, so describe the effect rather than the cause."*
- *Decoding Tacit Knowledge* (as recorded in the exemplar, read 2026-11-22): *"When contact isn't possible, the best you can send is a plain description of what you'd expect to see, because anyone can check that against the world."*
- *Library of Babel* on the bundle: *"Perhaps your problem was a bundle or conflation of several problems masquerading as one? You will need to search all of those problems individually."* This is Fig 9 :54 written in plain words, and it is the census step that produced the union of reach and closure in `01`.
- *Library of Babel* on stopping: *"the finish line will also be the starting place"*, and the paradox is that by then you have *"composed the answer through the process of locating it."*

## 3. What licenses a name

Only two things, both outside the shape itself:

1. **The ground settles it.** Formula step 5: *"the ground settles it. Verified only by what pays."* The README: the framework *"stops at the limit of what the available instruments can distinguish, and records the lineage so the stop is honest."* For this question that means the measured distribution under a declared null, not a review article and not an exclusion plot alone.
2. **A provenanced import from a frame whose closure differs** (Fig 6 :23, Fig 8 :50-52), with a source gate and a shadow test. A collapse term is such an import; its parameters are licensed only by the ground.

*The Hard Problem is About Source* gives the mechanism for why a reading is not a name. Metamerism: an instrument reads green and *"the instrument is speaking only about what it measured, it cannot comment on the source."* The source might be one green light, or yellow plus blue, or RGB, or CMYK, or a scheme not yet discovered. *"If several paths create the same behavior, then behavior is a projection with lossy information."* It was *"proven wrong not by argument, but by the arrival of a better instrument."*

Applied here: a falloff in visibility is a reading. It does not comment on the source. The source could be insufficient vacuum (gas collisions), blackbody radiation, photon recoil, a frozen-in preparation error, or a collapse term. Naming "collapse" from the falloff is the metamerism error. What is owed is a source-discriminating instrument, not a name. This is the deepest reason the mass slot stays open.

*They're Scanning Books When They Should* (as recorded in the exemplar, read 2026-11-22): *"The real import is not a new term in the existing space. It is a new dimension of the space."* That is the standard a collapse term would have to meet.

## 4. An independent witness for the shape limit, from a different field

Riera-Campeny, Maurer and Romero-Isart (mathematical statistics applied to quantum certification, arXiv:2506.22092) write:

> "Distinguishing quantum from classical dynamics in the presence of decoherence is analogous to inferring the shape of an object concealed beneath a cloth. As decoherence increases, the cloth thickens, making the object harder to discern, but the underlying features remain and can therefore be tested."

And: *"while decoherence masks quantum interference effects, it does not destroy them."*

This is the same limit the figures state in structural terms, reached from hypothesis testing and nowhere near the Tether's evidence pool. By Fig 2 :31, *"Independence, between two frames, is how little they draw on the same evidence."* The agreement is weak but real corroboration of the **shape** verdict, not of the whole framework. It is still not a ground reading: their cloth analogy is an argument, and the instrument that settles this question is the experiment.

## 5. Correction to an overstatement of the limit

An earlier reading in this folder came close to saying "the framework cannot name anything in this question." That is too broad and is narrowed here, kept visible rather than erased.

- The README's phrase is *"what a **missing** thing would have to look like (a shape, not a name)."* The subject is a primitive not held in the library.
- Mass, visibility, lambda, r_C and R_0 are **not** substrate primitives. Fig 13 :20 calls a spectrum *"ordering, so more and less can be said"*; mass is an ordering. Fig 13 :43 records that *"A continuum was the seventh and was removed for failing exactly this"*, so a generating set must not contain what its members produce.
- Naming a collapse parameter is therefore a **binding** from the physics frame onto a quantity that frame already holds, not the invention of a seventh kind. What remains forbidden is claiming that the binding is derived from the six-term set or that it moves the substrate wall (Fig 6 :22: *"IMPORT adds an atom"* applies at the frame level, not to the six kinds).
- The metamerism objection in section 3 is **not** retracted: a source named from a visibility reading is named without a discriminator. The reason is the missing instrument, not a blanket ban on naming.

Both readings are recorded so neither is quietly lost.

## 6. Two consequences for this question

**Prestige is not action.** From *The Recursive Self-Improvement Trap* (opened 2026-11-23): *"Prestige must never be allowed to validate an action directly"* and *"Prestige can fund an action. It can give an action a budget to be tried. But it cannot settle an action. Only the ground can settle it. The Ground Maintainer must be blind to prestige."* A list of famous groups who agree that quantum mechanics has a mass boundary is the named collapse, not corroboration. The legitimate ask gives the shape claim a budget to be tried and lets the experiment decide.

**This audit is a simulation, not a ground reading.** Same essay: *"the output is only as good as the validator. The agent, or the tool, or the frame, or the theory, cannot validate itself."* And: *"LLMs can quickly simulate an approximate answer/output with the formula... However, the truly testable output would need to be derived from the real architecture or working the formula yourself."* Fig 11 :70: *"A test harness is a substituted habitat. Which is why a synthetic solve proves wiring and never capability."* So `01_figure_grounded_audit.md` is a simulated derivation checked for internal consistency against the figures. It is **not** a test of any levitated nanoparticle protocol and must be labelled as such. It cannot settle the question it reports on.
