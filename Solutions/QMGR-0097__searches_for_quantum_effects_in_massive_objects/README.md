## Question restated as an effect

What is being decided is not whether quantum mechanics is true, but what would have to be true for the observed falloff of coherence with increasing mass to be a genuine boundary rather than a limit of the current instrument budget. Option A: read a null result at some mass as "quantum mechanics fails above mass M." Option B: treat the mass scale as a search axis (Fig 6 :29), keep the residual indexed per mass and per run (Fig 1 :19), and require that a genuine boundary arrive as a sourced import with a shadow test (Fig 8 :9-13). A leads to a verdict stated with no ground at the relevant mass and spends the independence of the people asked to check it. B leads to a checkable shape, a stated refuter, and an honest open slot until an instrument settles it.

What an answer would look like, bound as an ordinary term (Formula, "Direction"): a shape with three parts, (1) an instrument lineage already returning something that is being extended inward in mass and outward in isolation, (2) a certificate that decides classical versus quantum from the full measured distribution rather than from a single visibility number, and (3) a rule that a genuine departure must be a new atom with provenance and a shadow test, never a name assigned to a shape. That bound shape then accrues a residual like any other term and is refutable in `01_figure_grounded_audit.md` section 3.

## What is deliberately not here

- No personal emails of named individuals.
- No ranking of people by how likely they are to agree that quantum mechanics has a mass boundary.
- No bulk-send plan.

Three reasons in the framework's own words. Fig 2 :35: *"Alignment is triangulation, not negotiation. A mirror cannot correct you."* Fig 2 :40: *"disqualifying for a verdict: agreement about the world is worthless when the agreeing frames share an evidence pool"*. Fig 10 :42: *"A verifier that can reconstruct the claim cannot verify it."* Selecting recipients for receptiveness manufactures the disqualifying correlation, and sending the full framework before asking for a verdict converts a potential outside frame into a member of the frame being checked.

Legally: unsolicited bulk email to scraped addresses breaches GDPR and CAN-SPAM style rules in many jurisdictions and gets the sending domain blacklisted. That is a practical failure on top of the structural one. The list in `03_public_contact_points.csv` is public and opt-in by construction.
