## The two-stage offer, in practice

**Stage 1.** Send the residual, the shape claim and the four refuters. Ask what the recipient would count as a failure. Record the verdict before any theory is shown.

**Stage 2, opt-in.** If curious, offer the three public links above. State that the figures, not this summary, are the authority. The recipient's later agreement is never pooled as corroboration.

**Stage 3, opt-in.** Offer the formula as a method to reuse, with the instruction that each user writes their own refuter, states their own mode, and names their own ground. This is a generator crossing the membrane, which Fig 4 :22 permits.

**Hard precondition:** stages 1 to 3 only work if the stage-1 output survives. The output in `01_figure_grounded_audit.md` is a simulated derivation checked for internal consistency, not a ground reading (Fig 11 :70: *"A test harness is a substituted habitat. Which is why a synthetic solve proves wiring and never capability."*). Say so in the email rather than letting the recipient assume a measurement was taken.
