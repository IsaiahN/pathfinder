# The Tether's answer: reliable three-way decision principles

**Mode: `specified` on the three-branch routing rule, `general` on the structural shape. Not `grounded`.** Simulated derivation checked against the figures (`05` §6), not a ground reading.

## 1. The residual it answers

A decision-maker with a bounded library faces a gap R between prediction and observation and must commit budget to one of three stances: predict, hold several scenarios, or build resilience. The question asks whether complexity science can make the choice **reliable**. In effect terms the trichotomy is not one question: by Fig 9 :4-5, *"Disagreements that do not shrink with effort, where each rule keeps working well on a different subset, are not one hard question but several well-formed ones. Split it rather than search."* The trichotomy itself is per slot and after a union check, not a global switch (Fig 1 :19, Fig 9 :54-57).

## 2. The shape (three separate verdicts, never averaged)

1. **PREDICT when the live slot's residual is trending down.** Fig 1 :22: *"Aggregating across slots is how a live signal disappears into an average."* Fig 6 :27: *"Which is why some unreachability is depth and some is genuine absence, and from inside the frame the two look the same."* A flat, persistent residual owes a refusal, not a finer forecast (Fig 5 amendment, Formula step 2).

2. **PREPARE FOR MULTIPLE SCENARIOS when the residual is a union compound.** Fig 9 :55: *"A bundled problem is usually a union of overlapping conditions, not a list of separate ones."* Fig 8 :47: *"Independence is one quantity read twice."* Scenarios drawn from one model are one frame wearing several names, so the frames behind them must draw on different evidence. The ground selects between them (Fig 7 :35: *"A room's agreement is variation, not a verdict. Selection between rooms is where the ground votes."*).

3. **FOCUS ON RESILIENCE when contact is the binding constraint.** Fig 10 :24: *"the ground does not decay, the channel does."* Fig 11 :39: *"And capability is a property of agent-and-habitat, never of the agent alone."* Fig 11 :40: *"A system cannot be made more capable by improving the agent in isolation. An improvement that does not change contact changes nothing, however much it improves."* Available move: keep the anchor reachable and introduce actors, never subtract them (Fig 11 :61-63). This is a **precondition** on 1 and 2 in the Fig 3 dependency order, not a last resort.

4. **The strong reading of "reliable" is refused.** No domain-independent numerical switch is licensed. Fig 7 :6: *"the limit: no last room."* Fig 12 :104: *"So irreducible is a fact about a level and never about the world."* Fig 9 :60: *"Use filters for the budget and witnesses for the verdict. Never let a filter hand you a verdict."* A threshold is a filter; the ground is the witness. What survives is the weak reading: a checkable shape, with a pre-registered refuter, settled per case against the ground (Fig 13 :134: *"Unreachable is a verdict here, not a silence"*).

## 3. What it explicitly does not say

- Not a theory of everything, and not a final decision doctrine. No last room.
- Not a single scoring function: the currencies (bits, actions, independence) do not add (Fig 12 :126).
- Not that every forecast is fragile, or that scenarios always beat a forecast, or that resilience is always safer. Each branch has its own falsifier (§4 and `01` F1 to F4).
- Not that resilience is a solved state. Fig 11 :63: *"Which is why a managed ecosystem is permanent work rather than a solved state."*
- Not `grounded`. No decision board, dataset, or experiment was consulted.

## 4. What would settle it (the ground)

A **source-discriminating instrument**, not a further argument. From *The Hard Problem is About Source*: an instrument that reads green *"cannot comment on the source"*; the spectrometer, not an argument, resolved it. Here the corresponding questions are:

- **The discriminator test:** can an instrument tell, for a flat residual on a slot, whether it is depth (term in the closure, unfound), genuine absence (needs an import), or a degraded channel? If yes, the three branches become decidable in that domain and the switch point can be measured there.
- **The trend test:** does the live residual actually trend down under refinement, as F1 requires, or persist flat?
- **The union test:** does the disagreement fail to shrink with effort while each rule works on a different subset, as F2 requires?
- **The contact test:** does an improvement to the agent in isolation, with no change of contact or habitat, move the ground? Fig 11 :72 names this as the standing falsifier of the capability claim.
- Standing refuters from `01` §4: universal threshold certified by a frame; no discriminator among the three regimes; lossless currency conversion.

## 5. The honest stop

**Unreached, not unreachable.** The routing shape stops at the limit of the available instruments and records the lineage so the stop is checkable. The world sources read in `09` supply forms of the three regimes, not switch values. Fig 4 :30: *"So take the round trip and measure the gap. Without it you are navigating by dead reckoning: confident about a position nobody checked."* The three-row condensation carries a round trip gap R_T that is enumerated in `11_audit.md`. *"Indiscernibility is not identity."*

## 6. One-paragraph version for outreach

> The Tether does not give complexity science a universal decision formula, and it does not claim a final doctrine. It says that the choice of predict, prepare for several scenarios, or build resilience is a per-slot routing problem, not a global switch: refine a forecast when the live residual is shrinking; split into scenarios when disagreement does not shrink with effort and each rule works on a different subset; and restore contact and capability when the channel to the ground is degraded, because that condition is upstream of the other two. It refuses to hand over a numerical switch point, because the switch is a fact about a level and is settled by the ground in each case. What it offers is the shape any reliable decision principle must have, the refuters written in advance, and the instruments that would close it. The switch point stays open until the ground settles it.
