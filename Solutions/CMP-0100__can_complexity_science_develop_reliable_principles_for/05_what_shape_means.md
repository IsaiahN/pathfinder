## 1. A shape is a constraint, not a filler

The canonical usage rule states the limit: the framework returns *"what a missing thing would have to look like (a shape, not a name)"* and stops at the limit of what instruments distinguish, recording the lineage so the stop is honest: *"Indiscernibility is not identity."*

Fig 9 :32: *"you get a kind of thing, never the answer itself."* Fig 13 :134: *"Unreachable is a verdict here, not a silence"* and :138: *"you get a kind of thing, never the answer itself."*

From *The Library of Babel*: *"you can cut your search short if the set of books returned is small and descriptive enough that it provides a general shape of the answer that satisfies your question."*

So a **shape** is the narrowed form of an answer you can state before you can name it. It constrains; it does not fill. For this question:

- A shape: *three distinguishable residual regimes, each with its own routing rule and refuter, the switch point left to the ground.*
- A name: *a numerical threshold table that says "switch to resilience when uncertainty exceeds 0.7."*

The first is licensed. The second is exactly what the framework says it cannot return. This is why F5 in `01` refuses the strong reading of "reliable principles."

## 2. How a shape is obtained

- *Library of Babel*: use the vocabulary you already have that is closest to the thing, look for recurring new terms that overlap or correlate with it, recurse. *"With each new term gained, you are able to narrow the space by definition and deepen your vocabulary."*
- *Library of Babel* on a bundled question, which is directly this question's risk: *"Perhaps your problem was a bundle or conflation of several problems masquerading as one? You will need to search all of those problems individually."* This is the essay-level twin of Fig 9 :54-57 and it is why the trichotomy is applied per slot after a union check, not globally.
- Fig 6 :30: *"The description travels. Fails by being sharpened into terms only we can read. Cast it in effect terms."* Fig 9 :30-31: *"your words are borrowed too, so describe the effect rather than the cause."*
- *Decoding Tacit Knowledge*: *"When contact isn't possible, the best you can send is a plain description of what you'd expect to see, because anyone can check that against the world."* This is the operational reason the outreach email describes effects (a shrinking gap, a non-shrinking disagreement, a stale channel) rather than disciplinary jargon.
- *Library of Babel* on stopping: *"the finish line will also be the starting place"* and the paradox that by then you have *"composed the answer through the process of locating it."* For this question, the stop is the three-row table with its four refuters, not a final theory of decision.

## 3. What licenses a name, and why the switch point stays open

Only two things, both outside the shape itself:

1. **The ground settles it.** Formula step 5: *"the ground settles it. Verified only by what pays. A gate is not the ground."* The canonical README: the framework *"stops at the limit of what the available instruments can distinguish, and records the lineage so the stop is honest."*
2. **A provenanced import from a frame whose closure differs** (Fig 6 :23, Fig 8 :50-52), with a source gate and a shadow test.

For this question, a numerical switch point would be a name. It could be licensed by a decision board or a dataset that pays (the ground), or imported from a field that already holds it with provenance. Neither exists in this package. The world sources read in `09_watson_answer.md` supply *forms* of the three regimes, not a switch threshold. So the switch stays marked as a slot to be settled per case, and Fig 12 :104 applies: *"So irreducible is a fact about a level and never about the world."*

## 4. Source discrimination and the hardest part of "reliable"

*The Hard Problem is About Source* gives the mechanism that makes the switch point hard: metamerism. An instrument reads green and *"cannot comment on the source"*; the source could be one green light, yellow plus blue, RGB, CMYK, or a scheme not yet discovered. The reading does not determine the source. In the essay's words: *"If several paths create the same behavior, then behavior is a projection with lossy information"* and *"An instrument that can't distinguish these cases demonstrates its own inadequacy."* What resolved the color case was not an argument but *"the arrival of a better instrument."*

Applied here: a flat per-slot residual can be depth (term in the closure, not yet found), genuine absence (needs an import), or a degraded channel (ground no longer reachable). From inside the frame the three look alike. Fig 6 :27: *"Which is why some unreachability is depth and some is genuine absence, and from inside the frame the two look the same."* That is precisely why the three-way routing needs a discriminator and why "reliable" can only mean "checkable against a ground," never "decidable from the shape alone."

The three stances are therefore not three labels for one reading. They are three different answers to a source question, and the thing that separates them is an instrument, not a definition. This is the deepest reason the strong reading of "reliable principles" is refused.

## 5. Prestige is not action, and agreement is not a ground

From *The Recursive Self-Improvement Trap*: *"Prestige must never be allowed to validate an action directly"*; *"Prestige can fund an action. It can give an action a budget to be tried. But it cannot settle an action. Only the ground can settle it. The Ground Maintainer must be blind to prestige."*

A set of decision principles becomes "reliable" in the eyes of a community when famous people endorse it. That is the named collapse. The legitimate path is the opposite: give the routing rule a budget to be tried against a real decision board, and let the ground settle it. Fig 2 :35: *"Alignment is triangulation, not negotiation. A mirror cannot correct you."*

## 6. My own audit is a simulation, not a ground reading

Same essay: *"the output is only as good as the validator. The agent, or the tool, or the frame, or the theory, cannot validate itself."* And: *"LLMs can quickly simulate an approximate answer/output with the formula found in the architecture post... However, the truly testable output would need to be derived from the real architecture or working the formula yourself."*

So `01` and this file are simulated derivations, checked for internal consistency against the figures. They are **not** a test of whether complexity science can actually produce reliable principles. They report a shape and its refuters. They cannot settle the question they report on.

## 7. Correction to my own §3 (kept visible)

I first wrote that the framework returns a shape, not a name, therefore it can say nothing about where to switch. That overstates the limit. The canonical phrase is *"what a **missing** thing would have to look like (a shape, not a name)."* The subject is a primitive not held in Γ. A numerical switch point is not a missing primitive; it is an unmeasured quantity in a domain that already holds the relevant concepts. Fig 5's own rule applies: an unmeasured quantity is a specification of what to measure, not a forbidden name.

The corrected claim: the framework can specify *what to measure to find the switch point* (a source-discriminating instrument, a decision board, a ground reading) and can bind the sought-for shape as an ordinary term so it accrues a residual. What it cannot do is hand over the number without that ground. My earlier blanket claim is narrowed, not silently erased. The strong reading of "reliable" is still refused (F5), but now for the right reason: the number is unmeasured, not unsayable.
