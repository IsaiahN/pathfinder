## The answer in one line

Yes, but only as a routing shape, not as a universal threshold table. The figures license three distinguishable residual regimes (a live slot that closes, a union compound that must be split, a degraded channel that must be kept reachable). They forbid the kind of domain-independent numerical switch that would make the principles "reliable" in the strong sense. Reliability here means a checkable shape with a pre-registered refuter, settled per case against the ground.

## What is deliberately not here

- No personal emails of named individuals.
- No ranking of people by how likely they are to agree with this answer.
- No bulk-send plan.

Three reasons, in the figures' own words:

1. **A room is not an anchor.** *"Alignment is triangulation, not negotiation. A mirror cannot correct you."* (Fig 2 :35). Gathering agreement does not add a ground.
2. **Selecting for receptiveness manufactures the disqualifying correlation.** *"agreement about the world is worthless when the agreeing frames share an evidence pool"* (Fig 2 :40). A list chosen for likely agreement is a list chosen to agree.
3. **Showing the framework before taking the verdict spends the reader's independence.** *"A verifier that can reconstruct the claim cannot verify it."* (Fig 10 :42).
