## QUESTION

Decide, in effect terms, whether complexity science can develop reliable principles for choosing among three stances when a system's model and its ground disagree: predict, prepare for multiple scenarios, or focus on resilience. Option A: yes, as a universal numerical switch that settles the choice across domains. Option B: yes, but only as a per-slot routing shape with a stated refuter, whose switch point is measured per case against the ground. What each leads to: A promises a final decision doctrine that the figures forbid and that no source supplies; B gives a checkable shape, four refuters, and an honest open slot. The figures decide B.

## DOMAIN CENSUS

R by arity, symmetry and scale, then one line per domain with actors / currency / anchor, partition or union, and order by dependency.

- **R (the residual).** The gap between a prediction Γ(b,a) and an observation o' on a tracked slot. Arity: a vector over object slots, not a scalar (Fig 1 :19), so the decision is per slot. Symmetry: not symmetric between "new" and "refuted"; the fifth kind carries direction, so a refusal branch exists that the missing-kinds cannot reach. Scale: within-scale prediction gap plus a cross-scale round trip gap R_T (Fig 4 :30). The trichotomy is level-relative.
- **Domain 1, prediction frame.** Actors: agent, object slots, library Γ, per-step loop. Currency: per-slot residual R, predictions against observations. Anchor: the ground as the only metric. Question type: partition (which slot is live; refuse versus refine).
- **Domain 2, model-plurality frame.** Actors: frames with differing closures, candidate terms and scenarios. Currency: independence and overlap, union surplus. Anchor: the ground, where elimination compounds (Fig 9 :38). Question type: union (unbundle before searching).
- **Domain 3, habitat / ground-maintenance frame.** Actors: the seat, the habitat (actors, conditions, relations), the channel. Currency: contact, capability of agent-and-habitat. Anchor: the ground, which does not decay; the channel does. Question type: enumeration (habitat enumerated, never composed).
- **Partition or union.** After unbundling, the three stances partition the per-slot decision. Before unbundling they can be a union compound needing all three at once on different slots, so a global switch is the wrong object (Fig 9 :54-57).
- **Order by dependency.** Channel integrity, then per-slot residual, then union check, then stance selection; refuse before mint (Formula step 2); SEARCHED before INWARD before OUTWARD (Formula step 7).

Strain test applied: Fig 1's per-slot rule and Fig 11's whole system-plus-habitat capability are different objects, so there is no strain after re-census. One strain remains, on the word "reliable" (see TETHER).

## VERDICT (from the figures)

**Option B.** Complexity science can develop reliable three-way decision principles only in the weak sense: a per-slot routing shape with three separate falsifiable branches and a pre-registered refuter, whose switch point is settled per case against a ground. Predict when the live residual trends down; split into independent scenarios when disagreement does not shrink with effort and each rule works on a different subset; restore contact and agent-and-habitat capability when the channel is degraded or the habitat has shifted, upstream of the other two. A universal numerical switch is refused (Fig 7 :6, Fig 12 :104, Fig 9 :60). The verdict is `specified`, ungrounded, candidate.

- **cost if wrong:** bounded and locatable. The packet and the four refuters are written down before any claim of reliability, so an error is findable rather than invisible. The real cost is spending the independence of outside readers by presenting a shape as a settled doctrine, which Fig 2 :42 prices ("Buy independence in proportion to the cost of being wrong."). That cost is avoidable by leading with the routing rule and its refuter.
- **undoable:** yes. The strong "reliable" claim is removable without touching the three-branch derivation. `07_the_tether_answer.md` and `08_expert_verification_packet.md` already remove it. A published shape can be superseded by a measured switch point in a specific domain when an instrument pays, and no primitive has to be withdrawn.
- **reversed by:** a ground reading, not agreement. A source-discriminating instrument showing whether a flat residual is depth, absence or a degraded channel would make the branches decidable in that domain; a domain-independent switch that a frame can certify for itself and that survives a round trip across levels would break the refusal; an agent-only improvement that moves the ground would break Fig 11 :40. Until one of these fires, the verdict stands as candidate.
