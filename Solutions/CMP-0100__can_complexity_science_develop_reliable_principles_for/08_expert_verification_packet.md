# Expert verification packet: the Tether on three-way decision principles in complexity science

**What this is:** a claim in a form a complexity researcher or decision scientist can check. Mode, derivation and refuters are stated before the conclusion, so a reader knows what to look for and what would settle it.

**Mode: `specified` on the routing rule, `general` on the structural shape. Not `grounded`.** No decision board, dataset or experiment is claimed. This is a shape and a route, not a result.

**Status of this packet:** a simulated derivation checked for internal consistency against the figures, not a ground reading. It cannot settle the claim it reports on.

## 1. The claim, in one paragraph

The choice among predicting, preparing multiple scenarios, and focusing on resilience is a **per-slot routing problem**, not a global three-way switch and not a single scoring function. On a slot whose live residual is trending down, refine the forecast. When disagreement does not shrink with effort and each candidate rule works on a different subset, treat the residual as a union compound, split it, and hold several independent scenarios until the ground selects between them. When the channel to the ground is degraded, or the habitat has shifted so that contact is binding, no forecast closes the residual; keep the anchor reachable and add capability to the agent-and-habitat, which is permanent work and is upstream of the other two branches. No domain-independent numerical switch is claimed, because the switch is level-relative and the ground settles it per case. What is offered is the shape, the refuters written in advance, and the instruments that would close it.

## 2. Three checkable sub-claims

### C1 — framework-internal; checkable by reading Figures 1, 5 and 6
- **Statement:** prediction applies only to a live, per-slot residual that is trending down; a flat persistent residual owes a refusal, not a finer forecast. Scenarios require independence, not many forecasts from one model.
- **Source, verbatim:** Fig 1 :19 - "The residual is indexed by object slot, not aggregated." Fig 1 :22 - "Aggregating across slots is how a live signal disappears into an average." Fig 6 :27 - "Which is why some unreachability is depth and some is genuine absence, and from inside the frame the two look the same." Fig 8 :47 - "Independence is one quantity read twice."
- **What to check:** is the per-slot claim coherent with the routing taxonomy, and does the depth/absence/channel confusion really defeat a single forecast?
- **Refuter:** a case where refining one forecast closes a residual that is flat and persistent under a source-discriminating instrument, with no new term and no refusal; or a set of scenarios from one model that earns a genuine union surplus.

### C2 — decision-facing; checkable by a complexity researcher against real practice
- **Statement:** the multiple-scenarios branch is licensed exactly when disagreement does not shrink with effort and each rule works on a different subset, and the resilience branch when contact is the binding constraint. Outside those conditions, the branches are not licensed.
- **Source, verbatim:** Fig 9 :4-5 - "Disagreements that do not shrink with effort, where each rule keeps working well on a different subset, are not one hard question but several well-formed ones. Split it rather than search." Fig 9 :55 - "A bundled problem is usually a union of overlapping conditions, not a list of separate ones." Fig 10 :24 - "the ground does not decay, the channel does." Fig 11 :40 - "A system cannot be made more capable by improving the agent in isolation. An improvement that does not change contact changes nothing, however much it improves."
- **What to check:** do ensemble forecasting, scenario planning and resilience engineering actually exhibit these residual shapes, or do they use the vocabulary without the structure? (The scenario-planning source itself says support is mostly anecdotal.)
- **Refuter:** a case where disagreement shrinks monotonically with effort and one rule works on all subsets, yet scenarios outperform prediction; or a case where improving the agent in isolation, with no change of contact or habitat, moves the ground.

### C3 — framework-internal derivation
- **Statement:** the three stances are isomer-like (same residual atoms, different typed bonds, different decisions); their order is by dependency with channel integrity upstream; their costs are in currencies that do not add; and no universal switch is licensed.
- **Source, verbatim:** Fig 12 :117 - "Same atoms, different bond, different substance. The difference is invisible in the formula and decisive in the behaviour." Fig 3 :19 - "Optimising a link that already works cannot rescue one that does not." Fig 12 :126 - "A composition costs in two currencies, and they do not add." Fig 7 :6 - "the limit: no last room." Fig 9 :60 - "Use filters for the budget and witnesses for the verdict. Never let a filter hand you a verdict."
- **What to check:** does the dependence of stance selection on channel integrity follow, and is the refusal of a universal threshold consistent with the no-last-room rule?
- **Refuter:** a lossless conversion among description bits, action cost and independence; or a domain-independent switch that a frame can certify for itself and that survives a round trip across levels without a ground reading.

## 3. What is explicitly not claimed

- Not a universal decision formula, and not a final doctrine. No last room.
- Not a single score. The currencies do not add (Fig 12 :126).
- Not that prediction is always fragile, scenarios always better, or resilience always safer. Each branch has its own falsifier.
- Not that resilience is a solved state. Fig 11 :63 - "Which is why a managed ecosystem is permanent work rather than a solved state."
- Not that the framework names the switch point. It returns a shape and specifies what to measure; the number is owed to a ground, not to a definition.
- Not `grounded`. Nothing here was settled by an experiment, dataset or decision board.

## 5. What would settle it

A source-discriminating instrument, not a further argument. From *The Hard Problem is About Source*: an instrument that reads green "cannot comment on the source"; the spectrometer resolved it. Here:

1. **Discriminator test:** can an instrument tell, for a flat residual, whether it is depth, genuine absence, or a degraded channel? If yes, the branches become decidable in that domain.
2. **Trend test:** does the live residual actually trend down under refinement?
3. **Union test:** does the disagreement fail to shrink with effort while each rule works on a different subset?
4. **Contact test:** does an agent-only improvement, with no change of contact or habitat, move the ground?

## 6. What an expert can check in twenty minutes

1. Read Fig 1 :19, :22, Fig 9 :4-5, :55 and Fig 10 :24, Fig 11 :40. Six short passages.
2. Check C2 against a decision you know: did the three residual shapes actually distinguish which stance worked?
3. Name one instrument that would separate depth from absence from a degraded channel in your field, or say why none is possible.
4. Send the refutation. A failure is more useful than an endorsement.
