- **Domain 3, the verification frame.** Actors: evaluator, claim, ground, second frame. Currency: evidence and verdict. Anchor: a held-out outcome not sharing the predictor's evidence pool (Fig 2 :31-32). Question type: triangulation.
- **Order by dependency.** Anchor, then vocabulary for the intervention, then the objective "reliable", then verification. Refuse before mint (Formula step 2); describe the residual before naming a frame (Fig 9 :27-28).

## Round trip gap R_T

The Symbols table defines R_T as the gap between what was sent up and what came back down, `gap(x, (T_E ∘ T_A)(x))`, measuring what the coarser description cannot hold. Fig 4 :30 requires it to be measured rather than assumed. I ran the round trip on the condensation the folder actually uses.

1. **Sent up (x):** the verbatim figure set, chiefly Fig 2 :29-30/:33-34 (anchor and coherent drift), Fig 11 :43-44/:61 (introduction and cascade), Fig 12 :121 (loop sign), Fig 1 :19 (per-slot residual).
2. **Abstraction (T_A):** the shorthand carried in `07` and `08`: "a forecast stays reliable only while its outcome is an anchor, its residual is per slot, publication is an introduction, and verification is blind."
3. **Concretisation (T_E):** a concrete claim about a real forecast, e.g. "this published credit-risk score remained accurate during the period when lenders acted on it."

**R_T is nonzero.** Four things the shorthand holds do not survive the trip back down:

- the **sign of the loop** (Fig 12 :121), which the shorthand carries as "coupling" but which decides self-fulfilling versus self-defeating;
- the **attachment site** of the bond, substantive versus interpretive sensitivity (King and Mertens 2023), which decides whether the outcome is an anchor at all;
- the **per-slot indexing** (Fig 1 :19), which a single aggregate accuracy figure erases;
- the **provenance of the verifier** (Fig 10 :42, Fig 8 :48), which a single in-house backtest does not supply.

Conversely, one thing survives cleanly: if the outcome updates on the forecast, it is not an anchor and the naive accuracy claim is coherent drift. That is the residue worth sending. R_T was **not** measured numerically, because no ground reading exists; it is recorded qualitatively as the enumerated set of dropped constraints. Treating "the formula is the condensed version of the metalaws" as identity would set R_T to zero and would be the dead-reckoning error Fig 4 :30 names.
