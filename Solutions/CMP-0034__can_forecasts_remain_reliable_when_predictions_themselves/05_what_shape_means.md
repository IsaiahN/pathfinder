## 1. A shape is a constraint, not a filler or a number

The canonical figures README states the limit: the framework returns *"what a missing thing would have to look like (**a shape, not a name**)."* Fig 9 :32: "you get a kind of thing, never the answer itself." Fig 13 :134: "Unreachable is a verdict here, not a silence" (the full line adds that the missing primitive cannot be named, because naming it would mean already holding it).

From *The Library of Babel* (carried): *"You can cut your search short if the set of books returned is small and descriptive enough that it provides a general shape of the answer that satisfies your question."*

For CMP-0034 a **shape** is the narrowed form of a reliability claim that can be stated before any number is measured: an outcome that does not update on the forecast, a residual indexed per slot, the forecast recognised as an actor in the habitat, a loop sign declared, and a verifier that did not see the model. It constrains; it does not fill. "Reliability is conditional on a non-updating held-out ground" is a shape. "The forecast stays within 2 percent error under feedback" is a name (a number), and the figures do not license it.

## 2. How the shape is obtained

- *Library of Babel* (carried): use the vocabulary already closest to the thing, look for recurring new terms that overlap with it, and recurse; *"With each new term gained, you narrow the space and deepen your vocabulary."*
- Fig 6 :30: "The description travels. Fails by being sharpened into terms only we can read. Cast it in effect terms." Fig 9 :30-31: "your words are borrowed too, so describe the effect rather than the cause."
- *Decoding Tacit Knowledge* (carried): *"When contact isn't possible, the best you can send is a plain description of what you'd expect to see, because anyone can check that against the world."* For this question the plain description is: if you publish the forecast and then measure the outcome with a series the forecast could not have moved, the residual should still be small on each slot. Anyone can check that statement.
- *Library of Babel* on stopping (carried): the finish line is also the starting place; by the time you can state the shape you have *"composed the answer through the process of locating it."* Here, by the time we can state the separator we have already enumerated the conditions that would refute it.

## 3. What licenses a name, and what is genuinely missing here

Only two things, both outside the shape itself:

1. **The ground settles it.** Formula step 5: *"the ground settles it. Verified only by what pays."* The README: the framework *"stops at the limit of what the available instruments can distinguish, and records the lineage so the stop is honest."*
2. **A provenanced import from a frame whose closure differs** (Fig 6 :23, Fig 8 :50-52), with a source gate and a shadow test.

*They're Scanning Books* (carried): *"The real import is not a new term in the existing space. It is a new dimension of the space."* Also: *"Declaring the primitives does not make them neutral. It makes the choice visible."*

### Correction to my own first pass (kept visible)

My first pass read "shape, not a name" as forbidding the naming of any forecasting method or outcome measure. That was an overstatement. The README's phrase governs a **missing** primitive, one not held in Γ. A forecasting method, a held-out measure, and an outcome series are bindings onto terms already available in the forecasting and measurement frames. Naming them is provenance, not the invention of a seventh primitive.

What is genuinely missing here is not a name but a **discriminator instrument**: something that lets an observer tell *"the world changed"* apart from *"the forecast changed my reading of the world."* Petalas et al. (2017) report exactly this absence: participants *"did not pick up on this lack of change"* in the burst probabilities. The gap in `01` F9 and F10 is therefore a missing instrument, and it is correctly a shape-not-a-name gap. The correction does not remove the gap; it locates it precisely. Both statements are recorded so neither is quietly lost.

## 4. Why the accuracy report is the forbidden error

*The Hard Problem is About Source* (carried) gives the mechanism. Metamerism: an instrument reads green and *"cannot comment on the source"*; one green light, or yellow plus blue, or RGB, or CMYK can all give the same reading. *"If several paths create the same behavior, then behavior is a projection with lossy information."* And: *"An instrument that can't distinguish these cases demonstrates its own inadequacy."* It was the spectrometer, not an argument, that resolved it.

A post-publication accuracy report reads the outcome and names its source as the underlying regularity. That is the metamerism error. The same observed outcome is consistent with (a) the regularity held and the forecast merely described it, and (b) the forecast was employed and helped produce it. King and Mertens (2023) make this precise for predictive policing: more patrols in a predicted hotspot can raise recorded crime because more of the same behavior is noticed (interpretive sensitivity), or lower it because offenders move away (substantive sensitivity). The reading does not determine the source. What is owed is a discriminator, not a verdict about accuracy.

*The Recursive Self-Improvement Trap* (carried): *"the output is only as good as the validator. The agent, or the tool, or the frame, or the theory, cannot validate itself."* Fig 1 :27 says the same in the figure vocabulary: "A frame cannot score itself with a quantity it also produces."

## 5. Q answered: what would a reliable forecast look like?

**It looks like a claim with a held-out ground, a per-slot residual, a declared loop sign, and a blind verifier.** Nothing in the framework returns a universal reliability coefficient. A particular method is licensed only by a ground settling a minted term, or by a provenanced import with a shadow test. Until one of those, the method is the forecaster's own contribution and must be recorded as such.

Does the literature commit the error the README forbids? Yes, in two places. First, pooled accuracy treats the outcome reading as if it determined its source (metamerism). Second, Goodhart/Campbell/Lucas style "laws" name the collapse but do not supply a discriminator; this solution records that as world corroboration, not as the verdict (Fig 2 :40).

## 6. Two consequences for outreach

**Prestige is not action.** From *The Recursive Self-Improvement Trap* (carried): *"Prestige must never be allowed to validate an action directly... Prestige can fund an action. It can give an action a budget to be tried. But it cannot settle an action. Only the ground can settle it. The Ground Maintainer must be blind to prestige."* A campaign that gathers famous forecasters to agree that forecasts fail under feedback is the named collapse, not corroboration. The legitimate ask is a budget to try the separator against a ground.

**My own derivation is a simulation, not a ground reading.** Same essay (carried): *"LLMs can quickly simulate an approximate answer/output... However, the truly testable output would need to be derived from the real architecture or working the formula yourself."* LLM output is *"simulacra."* So `01` is a simulated derivation checked for internal consistency against the figures. It is not a test of the claim and must be labelled as such. It cannot settle anything it reports on.

The shape reading stands or falls with that instrument, not with agreement about it.
