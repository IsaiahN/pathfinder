## Round trip gap R_T

The Symbols table defines R_T as `gap(x, (T_E ∘ T_A)(x))`, what the coarser description cannot hold. Fig 4 :30 requires it to be measured rather than assumed. I ran the round trip on the condensation this folder actually uses.

1. **Sent up (x):** the verbatim figure set, chiefly Fig 6 :21-23 (import and closure), Fig 2 :13-21 (the two collapses), Fig 7 :12-13 (mirror chain), Fig 11 :49-51 and :61 (relational valence and habitat asymmetry), Fig 8 :50-51 (provenance).
2. **Abstraction (T_A):** the shorthand carried in `01` sections 3 and 8 and in `07` section 2: influence is import; unequal is a collapse or asymmetry; borrowing and convergence need lineage; name no donor.
3. **Concretisation (T_E):** a concrete historical claim of the kind the question invites: system B borrowed practice X from system A under conditions of domination.

**R_T is nonzero.** Three things the shorthand holds do not survive the trip back down:

- the **identity of the atom**, which needs dated evidence the shorthand does not carry;
- **which collapse fired**, since mutual update, common cause, and mirror chain look similar at the level of "they agreed";
- the **moral status of the relationship**, which the shorthand explicitly refuses and which a historical claim demands.

Conversely, one thing survives cleanly: contact with a differing closure is necessary for a reach change, and agreement is not a verdict. That is the residue worth sending. R_T was **not** measured numerically, because no ground reading exists; it is recorded qualitatively as the enumerated set of dropped constraints. Treating "the formula is the condensed version of the metalaws" as identity would set R_T to zero and would be the dead-reckoning error Fig 4 :30 names. The honest form is a lossy condensation with a measured gap, which is what `09_watson_answer.md` states.
