## 3. Distribution rules: offer, do not push

- **Fig 4 :22** - *"Only methods cross up. Only head starts come down."* Sending the full framework before a recipient has described a residual sends a recording, not a generator.
- **Fig 10 :52** - *"Bring a characterised residual, never the source."* Seat 4 must not hold the interiors. A recipient who has absorbed the theory before being asked for a verdict has joined the frame being checked.
- **Fig 9 :27-28** - *"describe it before you go looking, or any frame you pick will seem to fit."* Ask what they would expect to see if the shape claim were true before offering any article.
- **Seat Map :163** - *"High correlation is disqualifying for a verdict and efficient for a convention."* A list selected for people likely to agree produces a convention, not evidence.
- **Fig 2 :42** - *"Buy independence in proportion to the cost of being wrong."* The cost here is high: a false account of unequal exchange can erase whose knowledge was borrowed. Independent, ground-visible readers are worth the extra contact cost.

**Recommended order in a reply:** (1) the shape claim and its six refuters; (2) ask what they would count as a failure, and record it; (3) only then the three links, with the note that the figures, not my summary, are the authority; (4) on request, the formula as a method, with the requirement that each user write their own refuter and state their own mode.

## 4. Legal and channel constraints

- Use only the public institutional channels in `03_public_contact_points.csv`. No personal emails, no scraped lists, no purchased lists, no data brokers.
- One-to-one contact. No bulk send, no automation, no misleading subject lines. An opt-out line in every message.
- GDPR and CAN-SPAM style rules apply to unsolicited outreach in many jurisdictions. Public, opt-in venues avoid the legal failure as well as the structural one.
- Nothing is sent by this agent. Hosting and sending are yours. If you host the packet, prefer an opt-in download page over an attachment pushed to a list.

## 5. The two-stage offer (recap)

Stage 1: residual and refuter, verdict recorded before any theory is shown.
Stage 2 (opt-in only): the three source links, figures first.
Stage 3 (on request): the formula as a method to reuse, with the user's own ground and refuter.

Later agreement is never pooled as corroboration. A failure in stage 1 is the useful outcome.
