# Expert verification packet - the Tether on concepts versus associations

**What this is:** a claim in a form a researcher in machine concepts, interpretability or comparative
cognition can check. Mode, derivation and refuters are stated before the conclusion, so a reader knows
what to look for and what would settle it.

**Mode: `specified` on the shape of the evidence, `general` on the structural claims. Not `grounded`.**
No experiment is claimed to have settled this. This is a shape and a route, not a result. It is a
simulated derivation checked for internal consistency against the figures, not a ground reading.

## 1. The claim, in one paragraph

No fixed-distribution behavioral observation can discriminate a machine that formed concepts from one
that learned useful associations. The two hypotheses can produce identical outputs, and only a record
of where each came from separates them. An adequate discriminator must have four properties: it is
reported per slot, not averaged; it is taken under changed contact with a ground that does not move; it
is triangulated with an internal check for a composed term with a lineage and a refusal operator; and
the four conditions are reported as separate verdicts, never combined into one concept score. A named
concept feature is not sufficient, because such features can leak task information. The framework
returns this shape; it does not name the instrument that will settle it.

## 2. Three checkable sub-claims

### C1 - framework-internal; checkable by reading Figures 8 and 1
- **Statement:** identical behavior does not determine internal source; an averaged accuracy score cannot be the discriminator.
- **Source, verbatim:** Fig 8 :51-52 - *"Convergent derivation and adopted import look identical in the contents. Only the record of where each came from separates them, and the surplus lives only where the closures differ."* Also Fig 1 :19 - *"The residual is indexed by object slot, not aggregated."* and Fig 1 :22 - *"Aggregating across slots is how a live signal disappears into an average."*
- **What to check:** is the "identical in the contents" claim being used only about discrimination from behavior, not as an identity claim about the two internal states? (It must be the former. The figures README says *"Indiscernibility is not identity."*)
- **Refuter:** a proof that training behavior determines internal source uniquely, so concept and association cannot be behaviorally equivalent; or a demonstration that a global score preserves the per-slot discrimination.

### C2 - ground-facing; checkable by an experimental researcher
- **Statement:** the discriminating observation must change contact with a fixed ground; a held-out set from the same distribution and a synthetic test harness prove wiring, not capability.
- **Source, verbatim:** Fig 11 :70 - *"A test harness is a substituted habitat. Which is why a synthetic solve proves wiring and never capability."* Also Fig 11 :40 - *"An improvement that does not change contact changes nothing, however much it improves."* and Fig 2 :29 - *"An anchor is whatever settles the question and does not change its answer because of what any frame thinks."*
- **What to check:** name a target domain in advance, hold the ground fixed, and require baseline performance equal to transfer performance before calling it concept evidence. Independent corroboration from comparative cognition: the abstract-concept criteria in the concept-learning literature require a novel transfer stimulus, no replication of it, and *"an equal amount of baseline performance and transfer performance"* (Wikipedia, Concept learning, citing Katz, Wright and Bodily 2007); transfer learning is defined over a source domain and a *different* target domain (Wikipedia, Transfer learning).
- **Refuter:** a case where a system with no change of contact separates the two hypotheses by a proof internal to the machine with the ground agreeing; or a full-lineage system that fails every changed-contact probe while a matched association-only system passes.

### C3 - interpretability-facing; checkable by inspecting the model
- **Statement:** internally, a concept is a composed term with a typed bond, a recorded lineage and a refusal operator; an association is a flat correlation that can only accumulate. This is a triangulating second frame, not a strictly necessary condition (see the live strain below).
- **Source, verbatim:** Fig 12 :5 - *"A set of ingredients is not one. The bond is the rest of the information, and it is the half nothing was recording."* Fig 13 :127-128 - *"Nothing about a composed term tells you, from looking at it, which route produced it or whether that route was available. The space has no last route and every turn begins from a remainder the last one left, so the order is not recoverable from the artefact. It is recoverable from the lineage."* Operators :58 - *"a refutation is a negation, which is why a vocabulary without it can only accumulate"*
- **What to check:** does the model hold a reusable composed feature with a traceable origin, and can it withdraw a held regularity when refuted rather than only adding a weight? Independent corroboration: mechanistic interpretability *"aims to understand the internal workings of neural networks by analyzing their concrete structures, algorithms and circuits"* using *"causal methods"* (Wikipedia, Mechanistic interpretability). Caution from the same field: concept-based models *"are known to suffer from information leakage, whereby models exploit unintended information encoded within the learned concepts"* (Parisini et al., JMLR 27(174), 2026), so ask for intervention evidence, not the feature name.
- **Refuter:** a system with no typed bond, no lineage record and no refusal operator that passes every changed-contact probe, with independent internal inspection confirming the absence. That would make C3 unnecessary, not merely unproven.

## 3. What is explicitly not claimed

- Not a test of consciousness or subjective experience.
- Not that concepts and associations are the same internal state. The claim is about what behavior can discriminate.
- Not a single benchmark, not a named test, not a new primitive.
- Not that any machine has been shown to have concepts. No ground reading is cited.
- Not `grounded`. Mode is `specified` on the shape and `general` on the structure.
- Not that C3 is strictly necessary. It is a triangulating second frame, and its status is recorded as the one live strain in `11_audit.md`.

## 5. What would settle it

A source-discriminating instrument, not a further argument. The color-science case is the pattern: two
samples match under one illuminant and fail under another; spectroscopy resolved it. Here:

1. **Per-slot result.** Under intervention, which items change, and does the failure cluster where the alleged concept should bind?
2. **Changed-contact result.** Transfer to a domain stated in advance, with the ground fixed and baseline equal to transfer.
3. **Internal result.** A composed feature with a recorded origin, plus a demonstrated refusal after a ground refutation.
4. **Leakage result.** Intervention evidence that the concept feature is not simply carrying the task label.
5. **A ground reading** that reverses any of C1 to C3. Agreement alone is not such a reading (Fig 2 :35 - *"Alignment is triangulation, not negotiation. A mirror cannot correct you."*).

## 6. What an expert can check in twenty minutes

1. Read Fig 8 :51-52, Fig 1 :19 and :22, Fig 11 :40 and :70 - five short passages.
2. Check C1: do you know of a proof that training behavior determines source uniquely? If yes, fire refuter 1 and the claim narrows.
3. Check C2 against your own field: would you accept a transfer result without a stated target domain and equal baseline? If yes, fire refuter 2.
4. Check C3: with the model in front of you, can you find a composed feature with a recorded origin and a refusal event? If the model has neither and still transfers, fire refuter 3.
5. Name one instrument you think would discriminate source here, or say why none is possible.
6. Send the refutation. A failure is more useful than an endorsement.
