## QUESTION

Decide, in effect terms, what would count as evidence that a machine formed concepts rather than learned
useful associations. Option A: treat a sufficiently high score on a broad behavioral benchmark as the
evidence. Option B: require a source-discriminating observation, per slot, under changed contact with an
immovable ground, triangulated with an internal composition and refusal check, with four separate
verdicts. What each leads to: A accepts a reading of the same projection as a verdict about its source
and spends the independence of the people checking it; B states a falsifiable shape, written refuters and
an honest stop where no discriminating instrument has returned a signal.

## DOMAIN CENSUS

R by arity, symmetry and scale, then one line per domain with actors / currency / anchor, partition or
union, and order by dependency.

- **R (the residual).** The discrimination residual between H_C (concepts) and H_A (associations). Arity: two hypotheses, but "concept" bundles four conditions, so this is a **union compound** (Fig 9 :57: *"a case in two conditions is not a harder case of either. It stops being a mixture and becomes a specific compound."*). Symmetry: asymmetric, a failure under changed contact can refute H_C while success cannot confirm it (Fig 8 :39 *"So a search that finds nothing is never a proof of absence."*). Scale: internal composition in one machine, contact at the agent-and-habitat scale, verdict at the checking-arrangement scale.
- **Domain 1, machine frame internal.** Actors: library Γ, atoms, bonds, per-slot residuals, terms. Currency: composition inside closure(Γ) and settlement via R. Anchor: the per-slot residual as the only metric (Fig 1 :23 *"The ground enters as the only metric."*). Question type: partition, which compositions cross kinds.
- **Domain 2, instrument frame.** Actors: probes, interventions, datasets. Currency: residual under intervention. Anchor: a ground that does not update. Question type: is a probe already returning a differential signal?
- **Domain 3, habitat / contact frame.** Actors: environment, distribution, tasks. Currency: contact. Anchor: the ground (Fig 11 :39 *"capability is a property of agent-and-habitat, never of the agent alone."*). Question type: transfer with a stated target domain.
- **Domain 4, verification frame.** Actors: expert readers. Currency: agreement and residuals. Anchor: the ground, never agreement. In this simulated run all four domains draw on one evidence pool, so independence is zero (Fig 2 :40).
- **Order by dependency.** Describe the residual before choosing a frame (Fig 9 :27-28); per-slot before aggregate (Fig 1 :19); an instrument before an internal-structure claim (Fig 6 :34-35); refuse before mint (Formula step 2); settle at the ground before composing over the result (Formula step 5). Fig 3 :20 *"The chain is ordered by dependency, not by importance."*

## VERDICT (from the figures)

The shape survives; the single-test reading does not. No fixed-distribution behavioral observation can
discriminate concept from association, because the two can be identical in the contents and only the
record of origin separates them. Adequate evidence is reported per slot, taken under changed contact
with a ground that does not move, triangulated with an internal composition and refusal check, and
reported as four separate verdicts. A named concept feature is not enough, because it can leak task
information. The internal check remains a triangulating second frame rather than a proved necessary
condition, and that is the one live strain. This is a `specified`, ungrounded result in `general`
structural framing, candidate until the ground settles it.

- **cost if wrong:** bounded and locatable. The packet states its mode, four refuters and provenance before the conclusion, so an error is findable rather than invisible. The real cost is spending the independence of outside readers by sending the framework interior before their stage 1 verdict, which Fig 2 :42 prices (*"Buy independence in proportion to the cost of being wrong."*). That cost is avoidable by leading with the shape and its refuters.
- **undoable:** yes. The four conditions can be narrowed independently without touching the derivation: if the internal check is shown unnecessary, C3 is demoted to an optional triangulation, which `05_what_shape_means.md` §6 already does. `07_the_tether_answer.md` and `08_expert_verification_packet.md` already contain the narrowed form. Nothing has to be withdrawn wholesale.
- **reversed by:** a ground reading, not agreement. A source-discriminating instrument that returns a per-slot differential signal under changed contact, with a stated target domain and equal baseline, would move the changed-contact condition toward `grounded`. A proof that training behavior determines source uniquely would break the no-fixed-benchmark verdict. A system with no composed term and no refusal that passes every changed-contact probe would break the internal-check condition. A figure re-census that fits the isomer definition to the concept/association comparison without strain would change the supporting derivation in `06_isomers_and_order.md`. Until one of these fires, the verdict stands as `candidate`.
