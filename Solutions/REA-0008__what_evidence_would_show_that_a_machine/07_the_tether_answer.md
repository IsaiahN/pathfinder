# The Tether's answer: what evidence would show concepts rather than associations?

**Mode: `specified` on the shape of the evidence, `general` on the structural claims. Not `grounded`.**
This is a shape and a route, not a result. Simulated derivation checked against the figures
(`05_what_shape_means.md` §7), not a ground reading.

## 1. The residual it answers

The two hypotheses produce the same training behavior. The question asks for an observation that would
separate them, not for a definition of "concept". On fixed-distribution behavior the discrimination
residual is zero: *"Convergent derivation and adopted import look identical in the contents. Only the record of where each came from separates them"* (Fig 8 :51-52). A question whose only observation has zero
residual is a question about source, and source questions need a discriminating instrument.

## 2. The shape

1. **No fixed-distribution behavioral result can settle it.** Accuracy on a held-out set drawn from the
   same distribution is still a reading of the same projection. Fig 11 :70: *"A test harness is a substituted habitat. Which is why a synthetic solve proves wiring and never capability."*
2. **The evidence must be per slot.** Fig 1 :19: *"The residual is indexed by object slot, not aggregated."* The discriminator is which items fail under which intervention, not the mean score.
3. **The evidence must change contact with a ground that does not move.** Fig 11 :40: *"An improvement that does not change contact changes nothing, however much it improves."* Fig 2 :29 defines the anchor as what *"does not change its answer because of what any frame thinks."* Transfer learning is defined over a source domain and a different target domain; without stating the target, a transfer claim is not a measurement.
4. **It must be triangulated with an internal check.** A concept is a composed, settled term with a typed bond, a lineage and a refusal operator; an association is a flat correlation that can only accumulate. Fig 12 :5: *"A set of ingredients is not one. The bond is the rest of the information, and it is the half nothing was recording."* Operators :58: *"a refutation is a negation, which is why a vocabulary without it can only accumulate"*. Fig 13 :127-128: *"Nothing about a composed term tells you, from looking at it, which route produced it or whether that route was available. The space has no last route and every turn begins from a remainder the last one left, so the order is not recoverable from the artefact. It is recoverable from the lineage."*
5. **A named concept feature is not enough.** The JMLR 2026 leakage paper records that concept-based models *"are known to suffer from information leakage, whereby models exploit unintended information encoded within the learned concepts"*, and that intervention-based leakage scores predict behavior. Ask for the intervention evidence, not the label.
6. **The four conditions are separate verdicts, never averaged.** Fig 9 :54-55: *"when the split lands on a union rather than a partition, unbundle before you search again."* A single "concept score" repeats the aggregation error at the level of conditions.
7. **The framework returns a shape, not a test name.** Fig 9 :32: *"you get a kind of thing, never the answer itself"*. Which instrument will actually discriminate is a binding from the machine-learning frame, provenanced in `10_source_ledger.csv`, not a figure-derived result.

## 3. What it explicitly does not say

- Not a test of consciousness, qualia, or subjective experience. The question here is about evidence for concepts, not about phenomenal reports.
- Not that concepts and associations are metaphysically identical. Fig 8 :51-52 says they look identical in the contents; the figures README adds *"Indiscernibility is not identity."* The claim is about what behavior can discriminate.
- Not a single benchmark, not a named test, and not a new primitive.
- Not a claim that any machine has been shown to have concepts. No ground reading is cited.
- Not `grounded`. Nothing here is asserted as settled by an experiment.
- Not that the internal check is strictly necessary. `05_what_shape_means.md` §6 narrows it to a triangulating second frame and records the live strain.

## 4. What would settle it - the ground

A source-discriminating instrument, not a further argument. The color-science case in
`04_links_and_distribution.md` is the pattern: two samples match under one illuminant and fail under
another; the discriminator was spectroscopy, not a better description of the color. Here the
corresponding questions are:

- **Per-slot test.** Under intervention, which items change and which do not? Is the failure cluster
  located where the alleged concept should bind?
- **Changed-contact test.** Does the machine transfer to a target domain stated in advance, with the
  ground fixed and baseline equal to transfer (the abstract-concept criterion recorded from the
  comparative-cognition literature)?
- **Internal test.** Does the machine hold a composed term with a recorded origin, and can it refuse a
  previously held regularity when the ground refutes it? An accumulation-only system cannot carry the
  correction (Operators :58).
- **Leakage test.** Is the proposed concept feature causally upstream of the output under intervention,
  or does it simply encode the task label?
- **Standing refuters from the primary audit:** injectivity of training behavior to source; a no-bond,
  no-refusal system passing every changed-contact probe; a full-lineage system failing every such probe;
  or a ground reading reversing any condition.

## 5. The honest stop

**Unreached, not unreachable.** The figure rule separates underivable-in-principle from
unreachable-at-this-budget (Fig 8 :40-43). Nothing here proves that no discriminating instrument exists;
it says that none has been shown to return a differential signal, and that more of the same benchmark is
budget rather than a new witness (Fig 6 :34-35: *"Where nothing is, there is no edge to extend from, and the search for a proto-instrument is a search for one that is already there."*). The answer stops at the limit
of the available instruments and records the lineage so the stop is checkable. *"Unreachable is a verdict
here, not a silence,"* (Fig 13 :134).

## 6. One-paragraph version for outreach

> The Tether does not offer a test that detects concepts, and it does not define the word. It says that a
> machine's accuracy is a reading, not a source: concept and association can produce identical behavior,
> and only a record of where each came from separates them. So the evidence would have to be reported per
> item rather than averaged, taken under changed contact with a ground that does not move, and checked
> against the machine's internal arrangement, where a concept is a composed term with a lineage and the
> ability to refuse and an association is a flat correlation that can only accumulate. A named concept
> feature is not enough, because such features can leak the answer. The four conditions are reported as
> four separate verdicts, never averaged into a concept score. What the framework offers is the shape the
> evidence must have, the refuters written in advance, and an honest stop where no discriminating
> instrument has yet returned a signal.
