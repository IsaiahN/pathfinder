## Three things in the wider literature that bear directly on the claim

1. **Behavior is a lossy projection of source.** The color-science case of metamerism and the framework's
   source essay are two different evidence pools naming the same structure: matching readings under one
   instrument do not determine the source, and the discriminator arrives as a different instrument. This
   is corroboration, not identity: the framework's Fig 8 :51-52 governs provenance between frames, and
   the color case governs readings under a changed illuminant. Keep them separate.
2. **Transfer is domain-relative.** Transfer learning is defined over a source domain and a *different*
   target domain. A "concept" test that does not state the target domain is not a measurement. This
   matches Fig 11 :40: *"An improvement that does not change contact changes nothing, however much it improves."*
3. **A concept feature can leak the answer.** The JMLR 2026 paper makes the same point as Fig 8 :51-52
   in a peer-reviewed setting: the contents of a learned concept feature do not record where the
   information came from. Ask for the intervention evidence, not the feature name.

## Distribution: offer, do not push

Figure-grounded reasons:

- **Fig 10 :52** - *"Bring a characterised residual, never the source."* Sending the full framework before
  anyone has described a residual sends a recording, not a generator. Lead with the four properties and
  the four refuters; offer links after.
- **Fig 10 :42** - *"A verifier that can reconstruct the claim cannot verify it."* A recipient who has
  absorbed the framework before giving a verdict has joined the frame being checked.
- **Fig 9 :27-28** - *"describe it before you go looking, or any frame you pick will seem to fit"*. Ask
  what they would expect to see under the concept hypothesis before giving them any source.
- **Fig 7 :35** - *"A room's agreement is variation, not a verdict. Selection between rooms is where the ground votes."*
  A send selected for likely agreement produces a convention, not corroboration.
- **Fig 2 :40** - *"disqualifying for a verdict: agreement about the world is worthless when the agreeing frames share an evidence pool"*.
  The essays and the figures share an author; their agreement with each other is one pool talking.

**Recommended order in a reply:** (1) the residual and the four properties; (2) ask what they would count
as a failure, and record it; (3) only then the links, with the note that the figures, not the summary,
are the authority.

## The two-stage offer

1. **Stage 1 before stage 2.** Fig 10 :52 and Fig 2 :45 *"Frames range against the ground. They do not negotiate with it."* The recipient checks the output without holding the framework's interior.
2. **Stage 2 is opt-in by construction.** Seat 4 must not hold the interiors. Once a recipient has
   absorbed the theory, their agreement stops being evidence. Their stage 1 verdict must be recorded
   before any theory is shown, and later agreement is never pooled as corroboration (Fig 2 :40).
3. **Stage 3 sends a method, which is permitted.** Fig 4 :22 *"Only methods cross up. Only head starts come down."* The formula is a generator, so giving it away does not give away an answer.
4. **Stage 3 still needs a ground per problem.** `THE_FORMULA.md` Direction: a frame *"cannot check the direction of its own question, because the shape a frame is searching for is not a term it holds."* Each user writes down what would refute their result before running it and labels the mode.

**Hard precondition:** stages 1 to 3 only work if the stage 1 output survives. If a checker fires one of
the four refuters in `01_figure_grounded_audit.md`, narrow the shape before any further contact.
