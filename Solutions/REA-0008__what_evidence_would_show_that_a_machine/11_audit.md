## Round trip gap R_T

The Symbols table defines R_T as `gap(x, (T_E ∘ T_A)(x))`, what the coarser description cannot hold. **R_T is nonzero.** Four things the shorthand holds do not survive the trip back down:

- The **instrument precondition** (Fig 6 :34-35). The shorthand says "take changed-contact evidence"; the figure requires that something already be returning a differential signal. The candidate instruments in world sources are proposals, not confirmed proto-instruments for this residual.
- The **separate anchors** of the four domains. The shorthand can be read as one composite concept test; the figure set requires four verdicts that cannot be averaged.
- The **refusal operator**. Operators :58 puts negation outside the seven bonds, and the fig 12 composition rule does not by itself license "can refuse". The shorthand compresses two different mechanisms (bonded composition and negation) into one "internal check".
- The **shape-not-a-name limit**. Naming the JMLR leakage score or the transfer protocol as *the* test sets R_T to zero by treating a binding as a derivation. The primary correctly records each as provenanced.

Conversely, one thing survives cleanly: no fixed-distribution behavioral score is a source discriminator, and the observation must be per slot under changed contact. That is the residue worth sending. R_T was **not** measured numerically, because no ground reading exists; it is recorded qualitatively as the enumerated set of dropped constraints. Treating "the formula is the condensed version of the metalaws" as identity would be the dead-reckoning error Fig 4 :30 names. The honest form is a lossy condensation with a recorded gap, which is what `09_watson_answer.md` states.
