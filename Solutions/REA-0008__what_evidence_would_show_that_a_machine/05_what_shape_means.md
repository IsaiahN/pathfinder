## 1. A shape is a constraint, not a filler

The canonical limit, from `FRAMEWORK/reference/FIGURES_README_canonical.md` (first-hand): the framework
returns *"what a missing thing would have to look like (a shape, not a name)"*. Fig 9 :32: *"you get a kind of thing, never the answer itself"*. Fig 13 :134: *"Unreachable is a verdict here, not a silence,"*

From The Library of Babel (first-hand): *"you can cut your search short if the set of books returned is small and descriptive enough that it provides a general shape of the answer that satisfies your question."*

So a **shape** is the narrowed form of an answer you can state before you can name it. It constrains; it
does not fill. For REA-0008 the shape is *"a discriminating observation, per slot, under changed contact,
with a lineage and refusal check"*. *"Run OOD benchmark X"* is a name. The distinction matters because
naming a test before describing the residual is exactly the apophenia Fig 9 :27-28 warns about.

## 2. How a shape is obtained

- **Library of Babel** (first-hand): use the vocabulary you already have that is *closest* to the thing,
  look for recurring new terms that overlap or correlate with it, recurse. *"With each new term gained,
  you are able to narrow the space by definition and deepen your vocabulary."*
- **Decoding Tacit Knowledge** (first-hand): *"When contact isn't possible, the best you can send is a plain
  description of what you'd expect to see, because anyone can check that against the world."* This is why
  the outreach artifact states observable properties, not a theory of concepts.
- **Fig 9 :30-31**: *"your words are borrowed too, so describe the effect rather than the cause"*.
- **Fig 6 :30**: *"The description travels. Fails by being sharpened into terms only we can read. Cast it in effect terms."*
- **Library of Babel** on the union trap (first-hand): *"Perhaps your problem was a bundle or conflation of
  several problems masquerading as one?"* This is why the concept question is unbundled into four
  conditions in `06_isomers_and_order.md` instead of searched as one thing.

## 3. What licenses a name

Only two things, both outside the shape itself:

1. **The ground settles it.** Formula step 5: *"the ground settles it. Verified only by what pays. A gate is not the ground."* The figures README: the framework *"stops at the limit of what the available instruments can distinguish, and records the lineage so the stop is honest."*
2. **A provenanced import from a frame whose closure differs** (Fig 6 :23, Fig 8 :51-52), with a source gate and a shadow test.

They're Scanning Books (first-hand): *"A minimum viable set of six terms is a human's careful guess at
reality's compression axes; another sensorium's minimum set need not be those six."* Also: *"Declaring the
primitives does not make them neutral. It makes the choice visible."* Naming a particular concept test
as *the* test turns a visible choice into an invisible one. Record it as a binding from the
machine-learning frame (as `10_source_ledger.csv` does for the JMLR and Wikipedia sources).

## 4. Why behavior cannot settle source, in the essays' own words

The Hard Problem is About Source (first-hand): *"What if you have metrics that do not discriminate about its inputs?"* and *"If several paths create the same behavior, then behavior is a projection with lossy information"*. The instrument *"is speaking only about what it measured, it cannot comment on the source"*. The resolution was not an argument: *"The answer is unreached until a better instrument exists"* and it was *"proven wrong not by argument, but by the arrival of a better instrument."*

Applied here: a machine's accuracy is a reading, not a source. Concept and association are two possible
sources for the same reading. What is owed is a source-discriminating instrument, not a finer argument
about what "concept" means. Fig 6 :34-35 constrains what such an instrument can be: *"Where nothing is,
there is no edge to extend from, and the search for a proto-instrument is a search for one that is already
there."*

## 5. Q: What does "shape" require for this specific question?

**Answer:** four conditions, stated before any instrument is named, each with its own mode and verdict.

1. **Per slot.** Fig 1 :19 *"The residual is indexed by object slot, not aggregated."*
2. **Changed contact with a fixed ground.** Fig 11 :40 *"An improvement that does not change contact changes nothing, however much it improves."* Fig 11 :70 *"A test harness is a substituted habitat. Which is why a synthetic solve proves wiring and never capability."*
3. **A compositional and refusal check inside the machine.** Fig 12 :5 *"A set of ingredients is not one. The bond is the rest of the information, and it is the half nothing was recording."* Operators :58 *"a refutation is a negation, which is why a vocabulary without it can only accumulate"*.
4. **Separate verdicts, never averaged.** Fig 9 :54-55 *"when the split lands on a union rather than a partition, unbundle before you search again."*

The framework does **not** supply the name of the test that satisfies these. That is a binding from the
machine-learning frame. The independent literature in `04_links_and_distribution.md` supplies candidate
bindings (intervention-based leakage scores, transfer protocols, mechanistic circuits), each provenanced
and each a witness, not a verdict.

## 6. Correction to my own §5

I first wrote condition 3 as "the machine must have compositional structure", which would have made the
internal check a necessary condition. That overstates it. Fig 8 :51-52 says the two sources look
identical in the contents and only the record separates them; it does not say the record must be read
from inside the machine. Changed-contact evidence plus an imported, provenanced lineage claim can in
principle do the same work. Condition 3 is therefore recorded as **a triangulating second frame**, not a
necessary condition, and its status remains `specified` and single-source at figure level. Both readings
are kept here so neither is quietly lost. This is the one live strain carried into `11_audit.md`.

## 7. Two consequences for outreach

**Prestige is not action.** The Recursive Self-Improvement Trap (first-hand): *"Prestige must never be allowed to validate an action directly"*; *"Prestige can fund an action. It can give an action a budget to be tried. But it cannot settle an action. Only the ground can settle it. The Ground Maintainer must be blind to prestige."* A list of famous researchers who agree that concepts exist is the named collapse, not corroboration. The legitimate ask is the opposite: give the shape a budget to be tried, and let a ground settle it.

**My own audit is a simulation, not a ground reading.** Same essay (first-hand): *"LLMs can quickly simulate an approximate answer/output with the formula found in the architecture post."* and *"the truly testable output would need to be derived from the real architecture or working the formula yourself."* So `01_figure_grounded_audit.md` is a simulated derivation, checked for internal consistency against the figures. It is **not** a test of the claim and must be labelled as such. It cannot settle anything it reports on.
