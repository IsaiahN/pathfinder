## What an answer would look like (bound as an ordinary term)

An answer is a statement of the *bond* between two quantities, not a list of their shared properties. It must say, in effect terms: (1) whether the two entropies are one referent under two names (Fig 12 identity), (2) whether one produces the other (Fig 12 production), (3) whether they are a cross-scale projection with a measured round-trip gap (Fig 4), and (4) what observation would break each reading. The sought-for shape is written down here, bound as an ordinary term, so it can accrue a residual and be refused like anything else (THE_FORMULA, "Direction").

Effect statement of the residual: two quantities are measured in the same units and scale as area in the same settings, yet one depends on a quantum state and a bipartition while the other depends on a horizon geometry and Newton's constant. What, if anything, makes them the same quantity?

## What is deliberately not here

- No personal emails of named individuals.
- No ranking of people by how likely they are to agree.
- No bulk-send plan.

Three reasons, in the figures' own words: Fig 2 :35 "Alignment is triangulation, not negotiation. A mirror cannot correct you."; Fig 2 :40 "disqualifying for a verdict: agreement about the world is worthless when the agreeing frames share an evidence pool"; Fig 10 :42 "A verifier that can reconstruct the claim cannot verify it." Selecting recipients for receptiveness manufactures the correlation that would make their agreement worthless, and showing them the interior before asking for a verdict spends the independence they contribute. Pushing unsolicited bulk email would also breach GDPR and CAN-SPAM style rules in many jurisdictions.
