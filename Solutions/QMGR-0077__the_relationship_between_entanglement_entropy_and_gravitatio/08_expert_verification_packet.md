# Expert verification packet - the Tether on entanglement entropy and gravitational entropy

**What this is:** a claim in a form a specialist can check. Mode, derivation and refuters are stated before the conclusion, so a reader knows what to look for and what would settle it.

**Mode: `specified` - not `grounded`.** No source-discriminating measurement is claimed. This is a shape and a route, not a result.

## 1. The claim, in one paragraph

"The relationship between entanglement entropy and gravitational entropy" is a union of three conditions, not one question: identity (are they one referent), production (does one produce the other), and transport (how does a quantity cross from the state description to the geometry description). No single bond settles all three. General identity fails the substitution test and survives only as a regime-specific binding after renormalization. Production types a different program about the existence of gravity. Transport is a cross-scale, lossy map with a round-trip gap that must be measured. Both first-order descriptions are settled in their own domains and therefore serve as atoms at a second order. The microscopic source is not named, because an area-law reading cannot comment on its source. What is offered is the shape, the typed bonds, the unbundled union, and the refuters written in advance.

### C2 - physics-facing; checkable by a specialist against the literature

- **Statement:** the transport relation is cross-scale and lossy, with a measured round-trip gap; the regime-specific coincidence is a binding with provenance, not a derivation.
- **What to check:** Ryu and Takayanagi map boundary entanglement to a bulk minimal surface; Engelhardt and Wall map it to a generalized entropy extremized over a surface, `A/4G + S_bulk`. Frolov and Fursaev document that the near-horizon entanglement divergence is absorbed by renormalizing Newton's constant while requiring an infinite negative bare geometrical entropy with no statistical origin. Is that a derivation of one quantity from the other, or a binding with bookkeeping?
- **Source, verbatim:** Fig 4 :27 "The transform between scales loses information, and that is the point." Fig 4 :30 "So take the round trip and measure the gap. Without it you are navigating by dead reckoning: confident about a position nobody checked." Fig 8 :50 "Convergent derivation and adopted import look identical in the contents. - **Refuter:** exhibit a lossless round trip from the geometric description back to the state description (R_T = 0); or show a derivation of the area coefficient from a frame that does not draw on the near-horizon quantum field state.

## 3. What is explicitly not claimed

- Not that black hole entropy *is* entanglement entropy in general. Identity is regime-specific and provenanced.
- Not that entanglement produces gravity. That is a separate, ungrounded program (Jacobson, Verlinde).
- Not that `⋛` types a sum. It is a threshold relation between two quantities (Fig 12 :87).
- Not that the six-term substrate derives either entropy. It governs composition of primitives, not the value relation between derived quantities.
- Not grounded. No measurement settled any of this in this run.
- Not a forward prediction of the framework. Fig 8 :56 names "Bekenstein and holography" as a case already known, "has not been tested forward."
- Not that this simulation is a ground reading. It is internally consistent with the figures and nothing more.

## 5. What would settle it

A source-discriminating instrument, not a further argument. From *The Hard Problem is About Source*: an instrument that reads green "cannot comment on the source"; the spectrometer resolved it.

1. **Source test:** separate two distinguishable microscopic sources that yield the same area reading, or show that no instrument could.
2. **Round-trip test:** measure R_T between the geometry description and the state description (Fig 4 :30).
3. **Substitution test:** show no loss on swapping the two quantities, including regulator and species dependence.
4. **Union test:** show one bond settles all three conditions.
