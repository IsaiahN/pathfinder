**Use:** one-to-one, or as a cover letter attached to a submission through the public venues in `03_public_contact_points.csv`. **Not** for bulk send.

**Hard precondition:** do not send until the refuter in `01_figure_grounded_audit.md` §5 has been read and can be stated without the jargon. If the offer cannot survive a one-line failure report, the two-stage structure collapses on contact.

**Subject:** One checkable claim about entanglement entropy and black hole entropy

> Hi [name],
>
> I have one specific output I would like you to check against what you already know. I am not asking you to read a theory first, and I am not asking you to agree with anything. If the output does not survive, there is nothing else to look at.
>
> **The residual, in effect terms (`specified`, not `grounded`):** entanglement entropy and gravitational entropy are measured in the same units and both scale as an area in the settings where they are compared. Yet one depends on a quantum state and a cut, while the other depends on a horizon geometry and Newton's constant. The question "what is their relationship?" turns out to be three questions, not one: are they the same quantity, does one produce the other, and how does a quantity cross from the state description to the geometry description.
>
> **The shape claim (the output to check):** the three conditions do not share one bond. General identity fails, because you can swap the two quantities in almost every setting and lose something. Production strains, because gravitational entropy did not come into existence when entanglement was identified. What survives is a cross-scale map that is lossy, and the two readings coincide only in a restricted regime after the UV divergence is absorbed into the renormalization of Newton's constant. The round-trip gap between the geometry description and the state description is part of the claim and has to be measured, not assumed to be zero. The microscopic source is not named by this; no instrument in hand separates an entanglement source from a geometric source for the same area reading.
>
> **What would refute it, written before this message:**
> 1. A single bond is shown to settle identity, production and transport, so the three questions are really one.
> 2. The two entropies are shown substitutable with no loss in all respects, including regulator and species dependence, in every setting.
> 3. A source-discriminating instrument returns the same area reading from two distinguishable sources and separates them.
> 4. The round trip from the geometric description back to the state description is shown lossless.
>
> A one-line "this fails because..." is more useful to me than agreement. If you would rather not engage, no reply is needed.

## The two-stage offer, stated plainly

1. **Stage 1.** Send the residual, the shape claim and the refuter. Ask what the recipient would count as a failure. Record their verdict *before* any framework material is shown.
2. **Stage 2, opt-in only.** If they are curious, offer the source links. Their later agreement is never pooled as corroboration, because a recipient who has absorbed the interior has joined the frame being checked (Fig 2 :40, Fig 10 :42).
3. **Stage 3.** Offer the formula as a method to reuse, with the instruction that each user states a ground and writes a refuter before running it.

Nothing here asks for trust, agreement or endorsement. A failure is the desired output.
