## Relevant wider literature (read through the sources above)

The physics sources divide cleanly along the union split in `01`:

- **Identity and renormalization.** The brick-wall and entanglement-across-a-horizon programs (as reviewed by Frolov and Fursaev) support a regime-specific coincidence, with the caveat that renormalization requires an infinite negative bare geometrical entropy with no statistical origin. This is a provenance warning, not a clean identity.
- **Transport.** Ryu-Takayanagi and Engelhardt-Wall give the cross-scale map: boundary entanglement to a bulk area, then to a generalized entropy extremized over a surface. This is Fig 4's transform with a nonzero round-trip gap.
- **Production.** Jacobson and Verlinde give the dynamical program, which this solution keeps as a separate compound rather than treating it as the answer to the value question.
- **Geometry from entanglement.** Maldacena and Susskind supply the strongest entanglement-to-geometry correspondence, again as a map rather than an identity.
- **Information and computation.** Landauer's principle keeps "entropy" physical and relational: an entropy increase lives in non-information-bearing degrees of freedom of the apparatus or environment, not in a bit as a logical entity. This is the low-overlap field witness for the claim that the same word carries different currencies in the two domains.

These sources largely share a physics evidence pool. Their agreement is one pool talking (Fig 2 :40), so they are used to test the shape, not to settle it.
