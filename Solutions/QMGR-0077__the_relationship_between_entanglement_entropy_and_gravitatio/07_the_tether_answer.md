# The Tether's answer on entanglement entropy and gravitational entropy

**Mode: `specified` - not `grounded`.** This is a shape and a route, not a result. Simulated derivation checked against the figures (`05_what_shape_means.md` §6), not a ground reading.

## 1. The residual it answers

Two quantities share units and area-law scaling but have different dependencies: entanglement entropy depends on a state, a cut and a regulator; gravitational entropy depends on a horizon area and Newton's constant. The question "what is their relationship?" is a **union compound**, not one hard problem. Fig 9 :57: "a case in two conditions is not a harder case of either." It has at least three conditions, identity, production and transport, each with its own bond and its own ground.

## 2. The shape

1. **Unbundle before answering.** Fig 9 :54: "And when the split lands on a union rather than a partition, unbundle before you search again." A single sentence such as "black hole entropy is entanglement entropy" answers three questions at once and is therefore not an answer to any of them.
2. **Index the residual, never average it.** Fig 1 :19: "The residual is indexed by object slot, not aggregated." Each bipartition and each horizon is a slot. An "entropy of everything" throws away the cut dependence that makes the first quantity what it is.
3. **General identity fails.** Fig 12 :67: "two names, one referent," with the Operators test "substitute one for the other. Is anything lost? No." Substitution loses something in both directions. In the restricted horizon-vacuum regime, after the UV divergence is absorbed into renormalized G, the two readings coincide. That is a binding from the physics frame, provenanced as such, not a derivation and not a general identity.
4. **Production is a different compound.** Fig 12 :77: "A produces B; B did not exist before." The dynamical program (Jacobson, Verlinde) governs the existence of the Einstein equation as an equation of state, not the value relationship asked here. Kept separate, not conflated.
5. **Transport is cross-scale and lossy.** Fig 4 :27: "The transform between scales loses information, and that is the point." Fig 4 :30: "So take the round trip and measure the gap. Without it you are navigating by dead reckoning: confident about a position nobody checked." Ryu-Takayanagi and the quantum extremal surface construction are such transforms. The round-trip gap R_T is a reported quantity, not an error to hide.
6. **The union is second order.** Both first-order descriptions are settled in their own domains, so each becomes an atom at the next level under Fig 12 :104: "A settled molecule becomes an atom for whatever composes over it." No new primitive is minted and no wall is moved.
7. **The microscopic source is not named.** Fig 9 :32: "you get a kind of thing, never the answer itself." An area-law reading cannot say whether its source is entanglement, geometry, or both. The spectrometer analogy in *The Hard Problem is About Source* is the exact case: the reading does not determine the source. Unreachable is a verdict here, not a silence.
8. **Agreement is not corroboration.** Fig 2 :40: "disqualifying for a verdict: agreement about the world is worthless when the agreeing frames share an evidence pool." Both quantities are computed from the same near-horizon field content, so their numerical agreement is one pool, not two witnesses.

## 3. What it explicitly does not say

- Not that the two entropies are identical in general. Identity is a regime-specific binding.
- Not that entanglement causes gravity. That is a separate, ungrounded program.
- Not that the comparison bond `⋛` types the generalized entropy. `⋛` is "a threshold relation between two quantities" (Fig 12 :87); the generalized entropy is a sum.
- Not that the six-term substrate derives either quantity. The substrate governs composition of primitives, not the value relation between derived quantities.
- Not grounded. No instrument currently discriminates the source of an area-law reading.
- Not that the framework produced the Bekenstein-holography result. Fig 8 :56 says the triangulation claim "fits four cases already known and has not been tested forward."

## 4. What would settle it - the ground

A source-discriminating instrument, not a further argument. From *The Hard Problem is About Source*: an instrument that reads green "cannot comment on the source"; the spectrometer resolved it. Here:

- **Source test:** can two distinguishable microscopic sources be shown to yield the same area reading, and then be separated by an instrument? If yes, the source is named. If no instrument can be built even in principle, the shape's unreachable verdict is confirmed, not refuted.
- **Round-trip test:** measure R_T between the geometry description and the state description. Fig 4 :30 requires the gap to be measured. A lossless round trip would refute the "lossy transform" claim.
- **Substitution test:** show `S_A` and `A/4G` substitutable with no loss, including regulator and species dependence, in every setting. That would restore general identity.
- **Union test:** show one bond settles identity, production and transport. That would collapse the union to a partition.
- **Standing refuters from `01`:** any of the five in §5 of that file.

## 5. The honest stop

**Unreached, not unreachable.** From *They're Scanning Books*: "A minimum viable set of six terms is a human's careful guess at reality's compression axes; another sensorium's minimum set need not be those six," and "Declaring the primitives does not make them neutral. It makes the choice visible." The area law is a reading; the source is not in the reading. The answer stops at the limit of the available instruments and records the lineage so the stop is checkable. *How Intelligence Actually Works* supplies the rule: "indiscernibility is not identity." Fig 8 :39: "So a search that finds nothing is never a proof of absence."

## 6. One-paragraph version for outreach

> The Tether does not say that black hole entropy is entanglement entropy, and it does not claim to name the microscopic source. It says that the question is three questions: are the two quantities the same, does one produce the other, and how does a quantity cross from a state description to a geometry description. General identity fails, because you can swap the two and lose something; production belongs to a different question about why gravity exists; and the third condition is a cross-scale map that is lossy, with a round-trip gap that has to be measured. In the restricted regime where the UV divergence is absorbed into Newton's constant, the two readings coincide as a binding, not as a derivation. The framework returns the shape any answer must have, the typed bonds, the unbundled union, the refuters written in advance, and the instrument that would name the source. The source stays open until the ground settles it.
