**R_T is nonzero.** Three things the shorthand holds do not survive the trip back down:

- **Who may author the objective, and whether a metric moves under push, are measurement questions.** The shorthand reads as though the figures named the mechanism in a particular polity. Fig 2 :30 gives a test, not a finding about a named actor.
- **The F7 domain split is dropped by the shorthand.** The round trip must restore the separation between demand inside an already-fixed frame and the capacity to change the frame, otherwise the scalar reading that F7 refused sneaks back in.
- **The action-price condition is enabling, not a fifth peer mechanism.** The shorthand tends to flatten it into an equal member of the four, which loses its role as the budget that decides how much of the reachable space is tested (Fig 12 :126).

Conversely, several things survive cleanly: the per-slot residual, the anchor test, the requirement of contact with a differing closure, and the union rule all come back as shape constraints. R_T was **not** measured numerically, because no ground reading exists. It is recorded qualitatively as the enumerated set of dropped constraints.
