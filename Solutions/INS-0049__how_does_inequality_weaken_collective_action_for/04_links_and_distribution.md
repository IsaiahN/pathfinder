## Relevant wider literature, grouped by overlap

**Evidence pool A, institutional and political economy (one witness, not several).** Olson's logic of collective action and the "exploitation of the great by the small" (via the Collective action page), Ostrom's zero-contribution critique (JEP 2000), Stiglitz on rent seeking and political power (via Economic inequality), Bourdieu and Fukuyama on social capital reproducing advantage or becoming rent seeking (via Social capital). These sources overlap in the broad institutional-political economy pool, so their agreement is one witness. They corroborate the structural mechanisms F2, F4 and F6, but they cannot decide the figure verdict.

**Evidence pool B, voting and redistribution (the conflict).** Meltzer and Richard (1981) and Milanovic (2000), via the Median voter theorem page. Predicts more inequality, more redistribution demand. Conflicts with a naive "inequality always weakens collective action" reading. Returned to the census in F7; the re-census separated demand inside a frame from the capacity to change the frame, and the verdict did not flip.

**Low-overlap witnesses for the mechanism shape.**

- Mathematics and complex systems: Baronchelli (2018). Corroborates the slot-indexed/hub effect (Fig 1), the "one evidence pool" collapse (Fig 8 :49), and the rigidity of committed minorities and echo chambers (Fig 7 :13). Field is far from institutional economics, so this is a genuinely different witness for the shape of the mechanism, not for the institutional fact.
- Ecology and social-ecological resilience: MathisonSlee et al. (2022). Corroborates the rigidity trap (a stuck configuration loses novelty), multiple thresholds, and the need for response diversity. Different field again; same shape.

**Framework essays, maximal overlap.** The three essays opened this session are by the framework author. They govern the reading of shape, prestige and instrument but carry no independent corroboration weight because they share the figure author's evidence pool.

## Distribution: offer, do not push

- **Fig 10 :52** - *"Bring a characterised residual, never the source."* Sending the full architecture before anyone has described a residual sends a recording, not a generator.
- **Fig 10 :42** - *"A verifier that can reconstruct the claim cannot verify it."* A recipient who has absorbed the framework before giving a verdict has joined the frame being checked.
- **Fig 2 :45** - *"Frames range against the ground. They do not negotiate with it."* The ask is a check, not a conversation about belief.
- **Fig 9 :28** - describe the residual before choosing a frame, otherwise any field you pick will seem to fit.
- **Seat Map :163** - *"High correlation is disqualifying for a verdict and efficient for a convention."* A list selected for people who already agree produces a convention, not corroboration.
- **Fig 2 :42** - *"Buy independence in proportion to the cost of being wrong."* An institutional reform claim is high cost, so buy independence across classes and disciplines.

**Recommended order in a reply:** (1) the shape claim and its three refuters; (2) ask what the recipient would count as a failure, and record the answer before any theory is shown; (3) only then the three links, with the note that the figures, not the summary, are the authority; (4) if asked, the formula as a method to reuse with each user's own ground and refuter.

## The two-stage offer

**Stage 1.** Send the residual in effect terms, the four predicted mechanisms, and the pre-registered refuter. Ask for a counter-example or a failure condition. Record the verdict before showing any framework material.

**Stage 2, opt-in only.** If the recipient is curious, offer the local figure path and the literature links. Their later agreement is never pooled as corroboration (Fig 2 :40: *"agreement about the world is worthless when the agreeing frames share an evidence pool"*); a failure is copied into `11_audit.md` with provenance and re-evaluated.

**Stage 3.** Offer the formula as a generator (Fig 4 :22: only methods cross up, only head starts come down), with the instruction that each user writes their own refuter first and states their mode.

**Hard precondition:** stages 1 to 3 work only if the stage-1 output survives. The package in `08_expert_verification_packet.md` is the stage-1 artifact; the rest is held back.
