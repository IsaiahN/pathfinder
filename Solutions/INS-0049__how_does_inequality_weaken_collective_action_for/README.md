## Restatement as an effect

A reform coalition is a population loop, not one agent. It bets that a coordinated action will change a rule so that an observed failure stops recurring. Inequality is a heterogeneous distribution over the actor slots: different holdings, different weight in the shared belief, different contact with frames whose closures differ, different stake in the metric used to settle the reform, and different capacity to pay the actions required to test a change. The question is which slot-level structures make the population loop stop registering against the ground.

An answer would look like this, written down as an ordinary term before the run so it can accrue a residual and be refused (Formula, Direction): collective capability for reform survives when the ground metric stays ungamed, when classes keep independent contact with differing closures, when the objective link is instrumented, and when the action cost of testing a term is payable across slots. If it fails while those four hold, the shape is wrong.

## What is deliberately not here

- No personal emails of named individuals.
- No ranking of people by how likely they are to agree with the claim.
- No bulk-send plan, no receptiveness rank, no claim that a prestigious venue settles anything.

Three reasons in the figures' own words:

1. **A room does not upgrade the label.** Seat Map :283: *"A room does not upgrade the label. A room reaching a conclusion without the ground is agreement, and agreement among frames is a weighted average of influence. Adding people raises the vocabulary and does not add an anchor."*
2. **Correlation disqualifies a verdict.** Seat Map :163: *"High correlation is disqualifying for a verdict and efficient for a convention."* Selecting recipients for receptiveness manufactures the correlation that makes their agreement worthless.
3. **Asking for trust before checking spends independence.** Fig 10 :42: *"A verifier that can reconstruct the claim cannot verify it."* Fig 2 :35: *"Alignment is triangulation, not negotiation. A mirror cannot correct you."*

Prestige is not action. From The Recursive Self-Improvement Trap (essay read 2026-11-23): *"Prestige can fund an action. It can give an action a budget to be tried. But it cannot settle an action. Only the ground can settle it."*
