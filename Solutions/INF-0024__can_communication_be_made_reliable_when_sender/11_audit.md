## Independent domain census (drafted from the residual alone)

I redid the census using the WATSON order: R by arity, symmetry and scale; one line per domain with actors / currency / anchor; partition or union; order by dependency.

- **R (arity / symmetry / scale).** A signal arrives; the receiver's reading does not match the sender's meaning. Arity: two frames plus a reference, with the reference possibly missing. Symmetry: asymmetric, because one side selects and the other reconstructs. Scale: step (one exchange), cycle (vocabulary-changing conversation), seat (the checking arrangement). This is a seam, not a single frame.
- **Domain 1, the physical channel.** Actors: signal, noise, transmitter, receiver. Currency: bits, error probability. Anchor: measured output against a codeword. Nameable, so it is a domain.
- **Domain 2, the fact layer with a fixed code.** Actors: encoding, decoding, world state. Currency: reproduced message. Anchor: the denoted world state. Nameable, so it is a domain.
- **Domain 3, the seam with no code.** Actors: two libraries Γ_A and Γ_B and the signal. Currency: agreement or disagreement on meaning. Anchor: none yet. Not a domain until an invariant is built.
- **Domain 4, the convention.** Actors: the two frames coordinating. Currency: mutual expectation. Anchor: consistency of the convention, which Fig 2 :36 makes legitimate because *"An anchor is legitimate when the question is constitutively about it."*
- **Domain 5, the checking seat.** Actors: the ground, two frames, an outside reader. Currency: residuals and refutations. Anchor: the ground.

**Partition or union.** Union. Domains 2 and 4 overlap in every exchange; the overlap is a compound with its own loop. Fig 9 :54-57: *"when the split lands on a union rather than a partition, unbundle before you search again."*

**Order by dependency.** Seat before seam, seam before fact and convention. Within the exchange, vocabulary precedes objective, which precedes planning: Fig 3 :20-21 *"The chain is ordered by dependency, not by importance."*

My census matches the primary's five domains and its union reading. I derived from Fig 9's union/partition split and Fig 2's anchor definition, where the primary derived from Fig 3's dependency chain and Fig 10's seam. Different starting figures, same boundaries. Within a shared evidence pool this is expected; it is internal coherence, not independent corroboration.

## Round trip gap R_T

The Symbols table defines R_T as `gap(x, (T_E ∘ T_A)(x))`, the gap between what was sent up and what came back down. Fig 4 :30 requires it to be measured rather than assumed. I ran the round trip on the condensation this folder actually uses.

1. **Sent up (x):** the verbatim figure set, chiefly Fig 9 :4-5 (split the union), Fig 2 :29 and :33 (anchor must not update), Fig 6 :23 and :30 (closure and effect descriptions), Fig 4 :30 (round trip), Fig 11 :40 (contact), Fig 2 :40-41 (verdict versus convention).
2. **Abstraction (T_A):** the shorthand carried in `07` and `08`: two questions with different anchors; no cross-frame derivation from inside; effect descriptions plus two-way contact; convention is not verdict; four refuters.
3. **Concretisation (T_E):** a concrete engineering proposal, for example "adopt protocol P", "use error-correcting code C", "establish shared common knowledge K".

**R_T is nonzero.** Four things the shorthand holds do not survive the trip back down to a protocol:

- **Which invariant is chosen.** Fig 9 :32 permits a shape only. Naming P is a binding from an engineering frame, not a derivation, and claiming it moves the wall would violate Fig 6 :22.
- **Whether the reference genuinely does not update.** Fig 2 :33 is a structural condition. A fielded protocol in which both sides tune the reference in deployments has quietly become panel 1 drift.
- **Independence under prolonged contact.** Fig 8 :48 says the surplus comes from differing closures. A pair that converges until it shares everything has spent the independence that made its agreement meaningful.
- **The metamerism requirement.** Correct decoding is a reading, not a mechanism identification. A protocol that reports only the reading loses the discriminator the figures require.

Conversely, one thing survives cleanly: the split into a fixed-code fact layer and a no-code convention layer, with the requirement of an unupdatable reference for facts. That is the residue worth sending. R_T was **not** measured numerically, because no ground reading exists. It is recorded qualitatively as the enumerated set of dropped constraints. Treating the formula as identical to the figures would set R_T to zero and would be the dead-reckoning error Fig 4 :30 names. The honest form is a lossy condensation with a reported gap, which is what `09_watson_answer.md` states.
