## The question restated as an effect

A sender selects a message. A receiver must act on what arrives. The two do not hold the same mapping from signal to meaning, so the receiver's prediction from the signal, `Γ_R(b_R, a_R)`, does not match what the sender meant or what the world returns, `o′`. The residual `R = |Γ(b,a) − o′|` per slot stays positive and does not trend down when more of the same signal is sent. Reliability fails at the vocabulary link, not at the channel.

Two things this question might mean are bundled together and must be separated before an answer is attempted:

1. **A fact question.** Does the signal get a fact about the world right? This has a nameable anchor (a measurement, an outcome) and is therefore a verdict domain.
2. **A meaning question.** Does the signal carry the same meaning to both sides? This has no external anchor, because the question is constitutively about the convention. It is a convention domain.

Answering "yes" or "no" to the bundled question manufactures a single verdict where Fig 9 requires a split. The verdict in `07` and `09` is kept per domain and never averaged.

## What an answer would look like, bound as an ordinary term

Before the derivation, the sought-for shape is written down so it can accrue a residual like anything else (`THE_FORMULA.md`, step "Direction"). The answer expected here is: **a shared invariant that both frames can range against separately and that can be violated**, so that (a) a fact bearing is checkable without either frame moving the reference, and (b) a convention can be built through two-way contact rather than declared in the signal. A bare code name, a "universal protocol", or "just add redundancy" would not satisfy this shape and would be refused by the refuter in `01` §5.

## What is deliberately not here

- No personal emails of named individuals.
- No ranking of people by how likely they are to agree.
- No bulk-send plan.

Three reasons, in the figures' own words:

1. **A room does not upgrade the label.** Fig 2 :35: *"Alignment is triangulation, not negotiation. A mirror cannot correct you."* Agreement cannot substitute for a bearing on the ground.
2. **Selecting for receptiveness manufactures the disqualifying correlation.** Fig 2 :41: *"efficient for a convention: agreement about a shared meaning is the product, not a corruption of it"* is about conventions, not verdicts. For verdicts, Fig 2 :40 applies. A list chosen for likely agreement is a convention being passed off as a check.
3. **Asking for trust before checking spends the reader's independence.** Fig 10 :42: *"A verifier that can reconstruct the claim cannot verify it."*

Legally, scraping personal emails for unsolicited bulk outreach breaches GDPR and CAN-SPAM style rules in many jurisdictions. That is a practical failure on top of the structural one. The venues in `03` are public submission portals, role based institutional addresses and opt-in channels. They are not a list of believers.
