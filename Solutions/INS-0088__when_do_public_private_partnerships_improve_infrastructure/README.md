## What is deliberately not here

- No personal emails of named individuals. The World Bank PPI "Challenge the Data" page publishes a staff address; this folder records the public feedback route and the page URL, not the address.
- No ranking of people by how likely they are to agree that PPPs work or that they transfer risk.
- No bulk-send plan, no list of believers, no claim that a room of agreeable readers corroborates the shape.

Three reasons, in the figures' own words:

1. Fig 2 :33 "The anchor must not update." Selecting recipients for likely agreement moves the reading instead of the ground.
2. Fig 2 :40 "agreement about the world is worthless when the agreeing frames share an evidence pool" (the leading clause "disqualifying for a verdict:" belongs to the full line). People chosen for the same prior share a pool by construction.
3. Fig 10 :42 "A verifier that can reconstruct the claim cannot verify it." A recipient who has absorbed the whole framework before being asked for a verdict has joined the frame being checked.

Legally, unsolicited bulk email to scraped addresses breaches GDPR and CAN-SPAM style rules in many jurisdictions. Public opt-in channels avoid that practical failure as well as the structural one.
