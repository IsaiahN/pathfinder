## Independent domain census (from the residual line alone)

I redid the census before comparing it with the primary's conclusions, using the WATSON order: R by arity, symmetry and scale; one line per domain with actors / currency / anchor; partition or union; order by dependency.

- **R (arity / symmetry / scale).** The residual is whether a PPP changes contact with the ground or only relocates a reading. Arity: three-place (public frame, private frame, ground) with a contract as the typed relation; indexed per project and per risk slot, so the aggregation error is visible immediately (Fig 1 :19). Symmetry: broken, because a paper transfer can still land on the public, so the bonds are ordered. Scale: delivery, payment, oversight, policy lineage; lossy membrane between project and programme.
- **Domain 1, contract / delivery seam.** Actors: SPV, contractor, operator, public authority, lenders. Currency: residual reduction and payment. Anchor: service actually delivered and whether loss-bearing changes when the event fires. A seam between two frames (Fig 10), not two states of one substrate.
- **Domain 2, public measurement.** Actors: audit office, treasury, legislature, taxpayer. Currency: VfM and balance-sheet treatment. Anchor: real public cost, which must not move with the assessment (Fig 2 :33).
- **Domain 3, appraisal instrument.** Actors: planners and forecasters. Currency: forecast cost, demand, benefits. Anchor: outturns of a reference class (Fig 6 :32).
- **Rejected candidates.** "Private sector efficiency" (no anchor independent of the arrangement); "amount of risk transferred" (risk is per slot).
- **Union, not partition.** C1 (contact) and C2 (bond with fitting valence) overlap; the same transfer can serve or defeat improvement depending on the receiving frame (Fig 9 :55, :57).
- **Order by dependency.** perception -> vocabulary -> objective -> planning -> learn and carry (Fig 3 :20). I reached the break at the objective by a different route from the primary: the primary started from the contract's missing instrument, I started from the fact that "value for money" is a frame-internal score at the middle link that Fig 3 says lacks tests. Same break, different starting figure.

My census matches the primary's three domains and its union reading. ## Round trip gap R_T

The Symbols table defines R_T as `gap(x, (T_E ∘ T_A)(x))`, the gap between what was sent up and what came back down. Fig 4 requires it to be measured rather than assumed. I ran the round trip on the condensation this folder actually uses.

1. **Sent up (x):** the verbatim figure set, chiefly Fig 1 :19, :22 (per-slot residual), Fig 2 :29, :33 (unmoved anchor), Fig 11 :49 (valence relative to residual), Fig 12 :5, :117 (bond, isomer).
2. **Abstraction (T_A):** the shorthand in `07` and `09`: "two conditions, read per project and per risk slot, anchor must not move, risk transfer is a bond, private efficiency refused."
3. **Concretisation (T_E):** a concrete procurement verdict, for example "transfer demand risk to the SPV, declare value for money achieved, keep the liability off balance sheet."

**R_T is nonzero.** Three things the shorthand holds do not survive the trip back down:

- the **per-slot indexing** collapses if a reviewer reads the aggregate VfM as the verdict;
- the **unmoved anchor** collapses if the accounting treatment is allowed to define the cost;
- the **valence rule** collapses if "risk transferred" is counted as a scalar and the receiving frame's capacity is dropped.

Conversely, one thing survives cleanly: a transfer improves only if the loss actually lands on a party that can bear it and the service and real public cost are read against an anchor that does not move. That is the residue worth sending. R_T was **not** measured numerically, because no ground reading exists; it is recorded qualitatively as the enumerated set of dropped constraints. Treating "the formula is the condensed version of the metalaws" as identity would set R_T to zero and would be the dead-reckoning error Fig 4 names. The honest form is a lossy condensation with a measured gap, which is what `09_watson_answer.md` states.
