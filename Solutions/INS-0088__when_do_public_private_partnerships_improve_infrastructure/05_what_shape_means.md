## 1. A shape is a constraint, not a filler

The figures README states the limit: the framework reports "what a missing thing would have to look like (a shape, not a name)". Fig 9 :32 "you get a kind of thing, never the answer itself". Fig 13 :134 "Unreachable is a verdict here, not a silence,".

From *The Library of Babel*: "you can cut your search short if the set of books returned is small and descriptive enough that it provides a general shape of the answer that satisfies your question." The essay also gives the reason: "The problem you have determines what makes a book relevant to you or not." The residual selects the frame; the frame does not select the residual.

For this question a **shape** is the two-condition constraint:

> A PPP improves iff (1) the service and the real public cost are read against an anchor that does not move when the contract or the appraisal changes, and (2) the risk that crosses to the private frame is one that frame can bear, priced in the arrangement.

A **name** would be "use PFI", "use a hybrid PPP with viability gap funding", or "transfer demand risk to the SPV". Those are procurement designs, not answers derivable from the figures. The shape constrains any design that could count as an improvement; it does not pick one.

## 2. How the shape is obtained

- *Library of Babel* on vocabulary: "If you don't know the right terms to search for, you will have to use the existing vocabulary you do know that is closest to the words that you wish to describe and grasp the shape of." Then: "With each new term gained, you narrow the space by definition and deepen your vocabulary." Here the proximate vocabulary was "value for money", "risk transfer", "viability gap", "optimism bias"; each narrowed the search and none was allowed to become the answer.
- Fig 6 :30 "The description travels. Fails by being sharpened into terms only we can read. Cast it in effect terms." The residual was described in effect terms (contact with an unmoved ground; who bears a loss), not in procurement jargon, before any figure line was chosen.
- *Library of Babel* on steering: "The books that you are returning in your searches need to be tested or ranged against the problem you want answered in order to steer the direction of the next search."
- *Library of Babel* on stopping: "You will know that you are done because the finish line will also be the starting place" and "you have already composed the answer through the process of locating it." The stop here is not a procurement recommendation; it is the point where further search inside the same evidence pool stops changing the shape.

## 3. What licenses a name

Two things, both outside the shape:

1. **The ground settles it.** Formula step 5: "SETTLE - the ground settles it. Verified only by what pays. A gate is not the ground." The README: the framework "stops at the limit of what the available instruments can distinguish, and records the lineage so the stop is honest." For PPPs, the missing instrument is per-project, per-risk outturn data with an anchor that does not update.
2. **A provenanced import from a frame whose closure differs** (Fig 6 :23 "No frame escapes its own closure. The bound moves only on contact with a frame whose closure differs."), with a source gate and a shadow test. Importing "private sector efficiency" from an advocate's frame without provenance is not an import that adds a needed atom; it is an adopted reading wearing a derivation's clothes.

*The Hard Problem is About Source* supplies the discriminator. Metamerism: a color instrument reads green and "cannot comment on the source"; the source could be one green light, or yellow plus blue, or RGB, or CMYK. "If several paths create the same behavior, then behavior is a projection with lossy information." And: "The answer is unreached until a better instrument exists." It was "proven wrong not by argument, but by the arrival of a better instrument."

Applied here: a headline "value for money achieved" is a reading, not a source. It cannot tell you whether the improvement came from genuine delivery gains, from a risk genuinely borne by a party who could carry it, or from an accounting reclassification. Several paths give the same green. What is owed is a source-discriminating instrument, not a procurement name.

## 4. Why the shape cannot be a procurement recommendation

*The Recursive Self-Improvement Trap*: "The Tether ties alignment to the ground. Not to human preferences. Not to a reward model trained on human feedback. To the thing that settles a question regardless of what any frame thinks. An outcome. A measurement. A board that pays or does not." A recommendation such as "always transfer demand risk" would be alignment to a preference, not to the ground. Fig 11 :49 "An actor's valence is relative to the residual, not a property it carries." The same transfer is a benefactor to one project and a malfactor to another.

## 5. Answer to the question this file exists to answer

**What does "shape" mean here?** It means the form of any answer that could survive the ground: a two-condition compound, read per project and per risk slot, with an unmoved anchor and a real bond. It does not mean a procurement model.

**Does the framework return a name?** No. Fig 9 :32 "you get a kind of thing, never the answer itself." "Which risk, which sector" is a declared `gap` in `01_figure_grounded_audit.md` F9, not a hidden answer.

### 5b. Correction to my own §3 (kept visible)

I initially wrote the "shape, not a name" rule as if it forbade describing any concrete relation in this domain. That overstates it. The README's phrase governs "a **missing** thing". The atoms here (a public frame, a private frame, a contract, risk slots, an anchor) are already in the habitat; they are not missing primitives. What the framework returns is the **relation** they must stand in if the claim is to pay: contact with an anchor that does not update, and a bond whose valence is relative to the residual. Naming a specific risk allocation is a binding from the procurement frame onto those atoms, provenanced as such, not the invention of a seventh primitive. Both statements are recorded so neither is quietly lost.

The metamerism point in §3 is **not** retracted. A VfM reading still does not determine its source. What changed is the reason: the missing discriminator, not a ban on describing held atoms.

## 6. Two consequences for outreach

**Prestige is not action.** From *The Recursive Self-Improvement Trap*: "Prestige must never be allowed to validate an action directly." And: "Prestige can fund an action. It can give an action a budget to be tried. But it cannot settle an action. Only the ground can settle it. The Ground Maintainer must be blind to prestige." A list of famous PPP proponents who agree with the shape is the named collapse. The legitimate ask gives the claim a budget to be tried and lets the ground, an audit, an outturn dataset, settle it.

**My own audit is a simulation, not a ground reading.** Same essay: "LLMs can quickly simulate an approximate answer/output with the formula", but "the truly testable output would need to be derived from the real architecture or working the formula yourself." So `01` is a simulated derivation, checked for internal consistency against the figures. It is not a test of any PPP and must be labelled as such. It cannot settle anything it reports on.
