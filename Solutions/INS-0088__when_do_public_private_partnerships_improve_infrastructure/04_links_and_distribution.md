## Two evidence pools that bear on the question

These are **one pool each**, not independent witnesses. Agreement within a pool is the pool talking (Fig 2 :40).

1. **Procurement and public-administration record.** Wikipedia's PPP and PFI articles, the World Bank PPI database and blog, and the NAO finding as quoted there. This pool contains both advocate and critic voices, but it draws on the same PFI and project database. It is relevant to the mechanism shape (risk allocation, viability gap, balance-sheet treatment) and weak as a verdict on any single project.
2. **Forecasting and cognitive bias.** Reference class forecasting, Kahneman and Tversky, Flyvbjerg, Lovallo and Kahneman. This pool is lower overlap with the first and with the figures. It corroborates F7, the claim that an appraisal built from the inside view is an instrument pointing inward.

The two pools touch different quantities (one the contract, one the forecast), so their agreement on "the reading can drift from the ground" is weak corroboration of the mechanism, not a second measurement of PPP performance.

## Distribution: offer, do not push

- Fig 4 :22 "Only methods cross up. Only head starts come down." Sending the full architecture before anyone has described a residual sends a recording, not a generator. Lead with the shape claim and its refuter; offer links after.
- Fig 10 :42 "A verifier that can reconstruct the claim cannot verify it." A recipient who has absorbed the framework before being asked for a verdict has joined the frame being checked.
- Fig 9 :27-28 "describe it before you go looking, or any frame you pick will seem to fit." Ask what they would expect to see if the shape claim were true before giving them the article.
- Fig 2 :42 "Buy independence in proportion to the cost of being wrong." In a field where a wrong answer locks in a 25-year contract, spend more on a checker from a different closure, not on more agreeable readers.

**Recommended order in a reply:** (1) the two-condition shape and its three refuters; (2) ask what they would count as a failure; (3) only then the links, with the note that the figures, not the summary, are the authority.

## The two-stage offer (and the optional third)

- **Stage 1 before stage 2:** the recipient checks the output without holding the framework's interior. Fig 10 :52 "Bring a characterised residual, never the source."
- **Stage 2 is opt-in by construction:** record the verdict on stage 1 before any theory is shown; later agreement is never pooled as corroboration (Fig 2 :40).
- **Stage 3 sends a method, not an answer:** the formula is a generator, so giving it away does not give away a result (Fig 4 :22). Each user names their own ground and writes their own refuter before running it.

**Hard precondition:** stages 1 to 3 only work if the stage-1 output survives. The two-condition shape and its refuters are recorded in `08_expert_verification_packet.md`. If a checker fires one of the refuters, the packet is withdrawn or re-censused before any further contact.
