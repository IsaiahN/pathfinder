# The Tether's answer on PPPs, improvement and risk transfer

**Mode: `specified` - not `grounded`.** This is a shape and a route, not a result. Simulated derivation checked against the figures (`05_what_shape_means.md` §6), not a ground reading.

## 1. The residual it answers

The question asks when a PPP improves infrastructure and services and when it transfers public risks to private actors. Stated as an effect: does the arrangement change contact with the ground (service and real public cost), or does it only relocate the reading of cost and risk onto another frame while the ground outcome stays put?

This is a **union compound**, not one hard question with two answers. Fig 9 :55 "A bundled problem is usually a union of overlapping conditions, not a list of separate ones." Fig 9 :57 "What remains is smaller and it is also categorically different: a case in two conditions is not a harder case of either." The two conditions overlap: the same transfer can improve service when the receiver can bear the risk and merely move a reading when it cannot.

## 2. The shape

1. **Read per slot, never as one aggregate.** Fig 1 :19 "The residual is indexed by object slot, not aggregated." Fig 1 :22 "Aggregating across slots is how a live signal disappears into an average." A project can show positive overall value for money while a specific risk slot (demand, refinancing, maintenance) is live and unmanaged. Aggregate VfM is a filter for the budget, not a witness for the verdict: Fig 9 :60 "Use filters for the budget and witnesses for the verdict. Never let a filter hand you a verdict."
2. **The anchor is the delivered service and the real cost to the public, and it must not move.** Fig 2 :29 "An anchor is whatever settles the question and does not change its answer because of what any frame thinks. A measurement, an experiment, a counter, a market, a patient outcome." Fig 2 :33 "The anchor must not update." Moving a liability off the balance sheet changes a reading, not the ground. When an accounting convention decides the procurement, Fig 10 :47 "a convention that decides an outcome has stopped being one."
3. **Risk transfer is a bond, not an atom.** Fig 12 :5 "the bond is the rest of the information, and it is the half nothing was recording." Two PPPs with the same ingredients (private finance, concession, payments, risk matrix) can be different substances: Fig 12 :117 "isomer. Same atoms, different bond, different substance. The difference is invisible in the formula and decisive in the behaviour." The discharge test is whether the loss actually lands on the party that holds the risk when the event fires, and whether the payment changes with it.
4. **The valence of a transfer is relative to the residual and the receiving frame.** Fig 11 :49 "An actor's valence is relative to the residual, not a property it carries." Fig 11 :51 "No standing list of good actors survives a change of residual." There is no risk that is always better transferred. The unit is the risk slot and the receiver's capacity.
5. **Capability lives in the arrangement, not in the private identity.** Fig 11 :40 "A system cannot be made more capable by improving the agent in isolation. An improvement that does not change contact changes nothing, however much it improves." "Private sector efficiency" as a free-standing explanation is refused.
6. **Appraisal must be an outward instrument.** Fig 10 :24 "the ground does not decay, the channel does". A forecast made by the party that wants approval can drift from outturn; a reference class built from completed comparable projects is an instrument extended from a worse one that was already returning something. Fig 3 :27 "A link with no instrument is not a link that works. It is a link nobody has looked at."
7. **Agreement is not an anchor.** Fig 7 :13 "agreement compounds, evidence does not". Fig 2 :35 "Alignment is triangulation, not negotiation. A mirror cannot correct you." More proponents agreeing about PPPs adds vocabulary, not evidence.

## 3. What it explicitly does not say

- Not "PPP" or "public procurement" as a winner. The two conditions are the answer; a procurement model is a design to be tested against them.
- Not "transfer all risks to the private party." Valence is relative to the residual; there is no standing list.
- Not that any particular project delivered value for money. That is a ground reading, and none was taken.
- Not a name for the missing instrument. Which risk, which sector is a declared `gap`. Fig 9 :32 "you get a kind of thing, never the answer itself."
- Not `grounded`. No per-project outturn with an unmoved anchor was measured here.

## 4. What would settle it - the ground

A source-discriminating instrument, not a further argument. *The Hard Problem is About Source* (read 2026-11-23): a color instrument reads green and "cannot comment on the source"; it was "proven wrong not by argument, but by the arrival of a better instrument." Here the corresponding questions are:

- **Contact test:** for each project and each risk slot, does the delivered service and the real public cost read the same when the accounting treatment and the appraisal sponsor change? If the reading moves with the frame, the anchor has been updated and no improvement has been shown.
- **Bond test:** when a risk event fires, does the loss land on the party nominally holding the risk, at a price that changed when the risk crossed? If the public refinances, the bond was subtraction, not production.
- **Outward-instrument test:** does the appraisal survive comparison with outturns of a reference class of comparable completed projects, corrected for optimism bias and strategic misrepresentation, without being produced by the approving party?

Standing falsifiers from `01` §4: (1) a single aggregate VfM number predicts outcomes with no live per-slot residual; (2) the same allocation produces the same loss-bearing regardless of receiver and habitat; (3) appraisals are unbiased against outturn with no reference-class correction; (4) the accounting convention legitimately is the ground for the question. Any one sends the derivation back to census.

## 5. The honest stop

**Unreached, not unreachable.** The World Bank PPI database itself says its public sources "in some cases may not be accurate or contain all the required information" and invites corrections. The instrument exists and is already returning something; it is biased, not absent. Fig 8 :39 "So a search that finds nothing is never a proof of absence." Fig 13 :134 "Unreachable is a verdict here, not a silence,". The answer stops at the limit of what the available instruments can distinguish, and the lineage is recorded so the stop is checkable. Indiscernibility is not identity.

## 6. One-paragraph version for outreach

> The Tether does not pick PPPs over public procurement and does not claim the final answer. It says the question is two conditions, not one: a PPP improves only when the service and the real public cost are read against an anchor that does not move when the contract's accounting or the appraisal's forecast changes, and when the risk that crosses to the private party is one that party can actually bear at a price that changes when the event fires. Aggregate value for money hides the per-risk signal; moving a liability off the balance sheet changes a reading, not the ground; and "private sector efficiency" is not an explanation, because capability lives in the arrangement and its habitat. Risk transfer is a bond, not an atom, so the same contract ingredients can be different substances. What it offers is the shape any candidate answer must have, three falsifiers written in advance, and the instrument that would close it: per-project, per-risk outturns with an anchor that does not update. The specific risk and sector stay open until that instrument pays.
