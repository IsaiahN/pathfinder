**Mode: `specified`, not `grounded`.** Figures are primary and decide. World sources are secondary, corroborate but never overrule, and a conflict returns the derivation to the domain census. This is a simulated derivation checked for internal consistency against the figures, not a ground reading.

## QUESTION

Decide, in effect terms, what the Tether says about the claim that public-private partnerships (PPPs) improve infrastructure and services, and about the companion claim that they transfer public risks to private actors. Option A: treat this as one question with two outcomes and answer "PPP or public procurement". Option B: treat it as a union compound, read per project and per risk slot. What each leads to: A applies a single lever and mistakes a changed reading for a changed ground; B states the two conditions that must hold, the refuters that would break them, and the instrument that would close the question. The verdict below is B.

## DOMAIN CENSUS

R by arity, symmetry and scale, then one line per domain with actors / currency / anchor, partition or union, and order by dependency.

- **R (the residual).** Whether a PPP changes contact with the ground (delivered service, real public cost, risk exposure) or only relocates the reading of cost and risk onto another frame. Arity: three-place, public frame, private frame, ground, with a contract as the typed relation; residual indexed per project and per risk slot. Symmetry: the frames are not interchangeable, and a risk "transferred" on paper can still land on the public, so the bonds are ordered. Scale: delivery loop, payment loop, oversight loop, procurement-policy lineage; the membrane between project and programme is lossy (Fig 4).
- **D1 Delivery loop.** Actors: SPV consortium, contractor, operator, public authority. Currency: residual reduction against the output specification. Anchor: the asset and service actually delivered. Admitted.
- **D2 Contract and risk-allocation frame.** Actors: contract writers, lenders, insurers, refinancers. Currency: risk allocated, debt priced, payment stream. Anchor: whether loss-bearing changes when the event fires, not the contract text. Admitted.
- **D3 Public-accountability frame.** Actors: audit office, treasury, legislature, taxpayer. Currency: value-for-money assessments, balance-sheet treatment. Anchor: real cost to the public and service outcome, which must not move with the assessment. Admitted.
- **D4 Appraisal / instrument frame.** Actors: planners, forecasters, consultants. Currency: forecast cost, demand, benefits. Anchor: outturn costs of a reference class of completed comparable projects. Admitted.
- **X1 "Private sector efficiency" as a free-floating property.** No nameable anchor independent of the arrangement. Not a domain yet; absorbed into D1's habitat.
- **X2 "Risk transferred" as a single scalar.** No anchor; risk is per slot. Not a domain; the aggregation error.
- **Union, not partition.** C1 (contact with an unmoved anchor) AND C2 (a bond whose valence fits the receiving frame). A shared field between D1 and D2 is Fig 10's seam; it has its own loop.
- **Order by dependency (Fig 3).** perception -> vocabulary -> objective (VfM) -> planning (allocation) -> learn and carry (audit, refinance, next procurement). The chain breaks at the objective, which lacks an instrument.

Strain test: an earlier attempt to read Fig 13's two-kinds composition rule onto public and private frames strained; re-census assigned frame composition to Fig 12's bond theory and Fig 13 to the six primitives. The strain is recorded in `01_figure_grounded_audit.md` F10, not hidden.

## VERDICT (from the figures)

The shape survives; a single-lever verdict does not. "Improve" and "transfer risk" are two conditions read per project and per risk slot. A PPP improvement can be established only when C1 holds (service and real public cost read against an anchor that does not move when the accounting or the appraisal changes) and C2 holds (the crossing risk lands on a party that can bear it, at a price that changes when the event fires). Aggregate VfM is a filter, off-balance-sheet treatment is a moved reading, risk transfer is a typed bond rather than an atom, "private sector efficiency" is refused as an agent-only explanation, and appraisal needs an outward instrument. Which risk in which sector is a declared `gap`. This is a `specified`, ungrounded result. Pools A and B corroborate the mechanism shape; they do not settle any project.

- **cost if wrong:** a union treated as one lever. Transferring more risk to a party that cannot bear it, priced as improvement, leaves the public exposed when the event fires and the accounting hides the exposure. The cost is bounded and locatable because the packet states refuters before the conclusion (Fig 2 :42 "Buy independence in proportion to the cost of being wrong."), but it is real: procurement contracts run for decades.
- **undoable:** yes. No term is deleted if the claim fails; a refutation is added and the reach falls because something was added (Fig 6 :42-43). The shape and its falsifiers are recorded with provenance so a later reader can locate the error rather than only feel it (Fig 10 :62-64).
- **reversed by:** a ground reading, not agreement. Per-project, per-risk outturns with an unmoved anchor that show a single aggregate VfM number predicting outcomes with no live slot residual; identical loss-bearing across receivers and habitats; appraisals unbiased against outturn with no reference-class correction; or a case where the accounting convention legitimately is the ground for the question. Until one of these fires, the verdict stands as `candidate`.
