**Overlap warning.** Kaplan, Hoffmann, Wei, Schaeffer, McKenzie, Sorscher and Power all draw on the same pool of large-scale language model and vision benchmarks. They are many measurements from one research tradition, not independent witnesses for the verdict. Sutton (AI history), Brooks (software engineering) and the SEP entry (philosophy of mind) are genuinely low overlap. The figure verdict does not rest on any of them.

## Distribution: offer, do not push

Figure-grounded reasons, quoted verbatim:

- Fig 4 :22: "Only methods cross up. Only head starts come down." Sending the whole framework before a reader has described a residual sends a recording, not a generator. Lead with the branch claim and its refuter.
- Fig 10 :42: "A verifier that can reconstruct the claim cannot verify it." A reader who has absorbed the framework before giving a verdict has joined the frame being checked.
- Fig 9 :27-28: "describe it before you go looking, or any frame you pick will seem to fit". Ask what the reader would count as a failure before offering the source links.
- Fig 2 :40: "disqualifying for a verdict: agreement about the world is worthless when the agreeing frames share an evidence pool". A send selected for people likely to agree is a convention, not corroboration.
- Fig 2 :42: "Buy independence in proportion to the cost of being wrong." Use public opt-in venues rather than private lists.

## The two-stage offer

1. **Stage 1.** Send the residual, the two-branch shape, and the four refuters from `01` §4. Record the reader's verdict before showing any theory. Ask what they would count as a failure.
2. **Stage 2, opt-in only.** If the reader is curious, offer the four framework links above, with the note that the figures, not this summary, are the authority. Their later agreement is never pooled as corroboration.
3. **Stage 3, optional.** Offer the formula as a method to reuse. Each user states their own ground, writes their own refuter before the run, and labels the mode of the result.

**Hard precondition:** stages 1 to 3 only work if the stage-1 output survives. If any of the four refuters fires, stop and retract before sending anything further.

**Zip or hosting.** This agent can assemble the files locally but cannot host or send them. If you host the packet, prefer an opt-in download page that lets a recipient decline contact over an attachment pushed to a list.
