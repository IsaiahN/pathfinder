## QUESTION

Why do employees remain in some organizations despite poor conditions, and leave others despite attractive pay? Restated as an effect: why does `R_s = |Γ(b,a) − o'|` stay small in some employee-slots and grow in others when the pay input is the same? Option A: treat pay as the cause and look for the right amount. Option B: keep the figure-derived shape and treat pay as an actor with relative valence inside a habitat. What each leads to: A spends the budget searching inside a closed frame and manufactures a correlation by selecting for agreement; B sends a checkable shape, written refuters and an open slot until a ground settles it.

## VERDICT (from the figures)

The shape survives: retention is a typed bond between an employee and a habitat, indexed per slot, with pay as an actor of relative valence and no power to move a closed wall by itself; leaving substitutes a habitat and inherits unchosen actors; the channel can decay while the ground does not; and the two surface readings are one union compound to be unbundled. The specific bindings (affective, normative, continuance, embeddedness, fairness, shocks) are imports from the psychology frame with provenance recorded and zero verdict weight until a ground settles them. The grebe case is a mechanism analogue with a stated partial conflict, not a corroboration of the literal staying result. Which bond wins in a slot is a declared `gap`. Mode `specified`, `general` on the shape, not `grounded`. Verdicts were kept separate per domain and never averaged.

- **Cost if wrong:** misdiagnosis at the slot level. A company that fails D3 would be sold pay instruments; a company whose channel is saturated would be told to import more; a single lever would be applied to a union that needs two. The cost is real but bounded and reversible, and it is raised by sending a named cause instead of a refuter, which spends an outside reader's independence (Fig 2 :42 *"Buy independence in proportion to the cost of being wrong."*).
- **Undoable:** yes. No term is deleted if the claim fails. A refutation is added and the reach falls because *"something was added rather than because anything was deleted"* (Fig 6 :42-43). The shape and its eight falsifiers are recorded with provenance so a later reader can locate the error (Fig 10 :62-64 *"The residual it answered, the reasoning, the date, in a record the seats below can read."*).
- **Reversed by:** any of the eight pre-registered falsifiers in `01_figure_grounded_audit.md` section 3, in particular (1) retention invariant to habitat-contact changes while pay varies, (2) an already-held atom reversing a quit with no differing-frame contact, (3) identical atoms and bonds still diverging at the ground, (4) the three commitment components collapsing to one factor with no incremental prediction, (5) one lever clearing both union conditions with no residue, (6) a substitution reproducing everything the goal required, or (7) a low-overlap pool showing retention fully predicted by averaged pay. A source-discriminating instrument naming the bond would move the `gap` to `grounded`, not reverse the shape. Prestige or agreement is not such an instrument.
