## Scope of this package

This is a Tether derivation and a WATSON run for one question. It states a residual in effect terms, runs a domain census before selecting any figure line, quotes the figure text verbatim, keeps verdicts per domain, registers a refuter before the conclusion, and records what would settle the question. It is a **simulated derivation checked for internal consistency against the figures, not a ground reading**. By the framework's own rule (The Recursive Self-Improvement Trap, read 2026-11-22: *"the output is only as good as the validator. The agent, or the tool, or the frame, or the theory, cannot validate itself"*) this package cannot settle the question it reports on. It cannot tell you why any particular employee stayed or left.

Field for public contact points: employee retention and organizational commitment research.

## What an answer would look like (Direction, bound as an ordinary term)

Before the run, the sought-for shape is written down so it can accrue a residual like any other term. An answer here would be a statement of the form: for an employee-slot `s`, the observed stay/leave event `o'` is settled by the typed bond between the employee and the organizational habitat, by the reach of the employee's frame through contact with a differing frame, and by the state of the channel that carries current conditions into the decision. Pay is one atom in that molecule and has valence relative to the residual, not a fixed sign. A name (for example "affective commitment" or "job embeddedness") is a binding from the psychology frame onto a held atom, not the output of the derivation. If this shape is wrong, one of the pre-registered refuters in `01_figure_grounded_audit.md` section 3 should fire.

## What is deliberately not here

- No personal emails of named individuals.
- No ranking of people by how likely they are to agree with the shape.
- No bulk-send plan.

Three reasons, two from the figures and one from the seat rules recorded in the exemplar: Fig 2 :35 *"Alignment is triangulation, not negotiation. A mirror cannot correct you."*; Fig 2 :40 *"agreement about the world is worthless when the agreeing frames share an evidence pool"*; and the seat rule that an interest in the outcome is unavoidable while an interest in the reading is disqualifying. Selecting recipients for receptiveness manufactures the correlation that would make their agreement worthless. Asking for trust before a check spends the only thing an outside frame contributes (Fig 10 :42 *"A verifier that can reconstruct the claim cannot verify it."*).

Legally, scraping personal emails for unsolicited bulk outreach breaches GDPR and CAN-SPAM style rules in many jurisdictions. That is a practical failure on top of the structural one. The venues in `03_public_contact_points.csv` are public submission or contact routes, and several are explicitly results-blind or opt-in.
