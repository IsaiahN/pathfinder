## 1. A shape is a constraint, not a filler

The figures README states the limit: the framework returns *"what a missing thing would have to look like (a shape, not a name)."* Fig 9 :32 *"you get a kind of thing, never the answer itself."* Fig 13 :134 *"Unreachable is a verdict here, not a silence"* and Fig 13 :135 *"It cannot say which primitive is missing, because naming it would mean already holding it."*

From *The Library of Babel*: *"You can cut your search short if the set of books returned is small and descriptive enough that it provides a general shape of the answer that satisfies your question."*

So a **shape** is the narrowed form of an answer you can state before you can name it. It constrains; it does not fill. For BUS-0029:

- Shape: retention is a typed bond between an employee and a habitat, indexed per slot, with a maintained channel and a reach that must be renewed by contact with a differing frame. Pay is one atom among others and has valence relative to each residual.
- Name: "continuance commitment explains why they stay," "job embeddedness explains why they stay," "a fairness shock explains why they leave."

The first is a shape. The second group are names borrowed from the psychology frame.

## 2. How a shape is obtained

- *Library of Babel*, on vocabulary bootstrapping: use the known terms closest to the thing, look for recurring new terms that overlap or correlate, recurse. *"With each new term gained, you are able to narrow the space by definition and deepen your vocabulary."*
- Fig 6 :30 *"outward: does another frame already hold it? The description travels. Fails by being sharpened into terms only we can read. Cast it in effect terms."* Fig 9 :30-31 *"your words are borrowed too, so describe the effect rather than the cause."*
- *The Hard Problem is About Source*: when contact is not possible, the honest thing to send is a plain description of what you would expect to see, because anyone can check that against the world. The essay's own version: an instrument that reads green *"cannot comment on the source"*, and *"The answer is unreached until a better instrument exists."*
- *Library of Babel* on stopping: *"You will know that you are done because the finish line will also be the starting place"*, and the paradox that by the time you have the exact search terms *"you have already composed the answer through the process of locating it."*

Applied here: the search terms are "bond," "habitat," "channel," "closure," "slot" and "valence." The shape is what those terms narrow to before any field name is attached.

## 3. What licenses a name

Only two things, both outside the shape itself:

1. **The ground settles it.** `THE_FORMULA.md` step 5: *"the ground settles it. Verified only by what pays. A gate is not the ground."* The README says the framework *"stops at the limit of what the available instruments can distinguish, and records the lineage so the stop is honest."* For this question, the ground is the revealed event plus an instrument that discriminates bond types.
2. **A provenanced import from a frame whose closure differs** (Fig 6 :23, Fig 8 :50-52), with a source gate and a shadow test. The QIC-WD commitment, embeddedness and compensation summaries are such imports.

*The Hard Problem is About Source* explains why the reading alone cannot give the source: metamerism. One green reading can come from green light, yellow plus blue, RGB, CMYK or a scheme not yet discovered. The reading does not determine the source; the spectrometer did. Here, an engagement score or an exit-interview answer is the reading. The stay/leave event is the ground. The thing that would name the bond is a discriminator we do not yet hold, so the slot stays open.

## 4. Why this question is especially prone to naming the source from the reading

The field itself defines commitment as a tie that binds (QIC-WD organizational commitment, Stepanek and Paul 2023: *"most definitions refer to organizational commitment in terms of a tie that binds employees to their organization"*). "They stayed because they were committed" is therefore close to "they stayed because they stayed." Fig 12 :5 *"A set of ingredients is not one. The bond is the rest of the information, and it is the half nothing was recording."* The shape requires the atoms and the typed relation to be stated before the name does any work.

## 5. Q: which specific driver owns the slot? ANSWERED, no further question needed

**Nothing in the framework.** The framework returns a shape. A particular driver is licensed only by the ground settling a minted term, or by a provenanced import with a shadow test. Until one of those, the name is the searcher's own contribution and must be recorded as such. This was the decisive correction in the exemplar too: the *"shape, not a name"* rule governs a primitive not held in Γ, while naming an atom already held is a binding, not an invention. For BUS-0029 the atoms (pay, tenure, peers, alternatives) are held; the winning bond in a particular slot is not.

## 6. Two consequences for this package

**Prestige is not action.** *The Recursive Self-Improvement Trap*: *"Prestige must never be allowed to validate an action directly... Someone let prestige stand in for proof... Prestige can fund an action. It can give an action a budget to be tried. But it cannot settle an action. Only the ground can settle it. The Ground Maintainer must be blind to prestige."* A list of famous retention scholars who agree with the shape is the named collapse, not corroboration. The legitimate ask is a budget to try the shape against a ground.

**My own audit is a simulation, not a ground reading.** Same essay: *"LLMs can quickly simulate an approximate answer/output... However, the truly testable output would need to be derived from the real architecture or working the formula yourself."* So `01_figure_grounded_audit.md` and `11_audit.md` are simulated derivations checked for internal consistency against the figures. They are **not** tests of the claim and must be labelled as such. Neither can settle why anyone stays or leaves.
