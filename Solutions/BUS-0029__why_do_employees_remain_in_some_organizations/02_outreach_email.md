# Outreach draft: check one output before you decide whether the shape is worth reading

**Use:** one to one, or as a cover letter attached to a submission through the public venues in `03_public_contact_points.csv`. **Not** for bulk send.

**Hard precondition:** do not send until the refuter in `01_figure_grounded_audit.md` section 3 is stated in the first paragraph. The stage-1 output must be checkable on its own or the two-stage offer collapses on contact.

---

**Subject:** One checkable output on why people stay and leave

> Hi [name],
>
> I am writing about the puzzle of employees who stay in poor conditions and leave well-paid ones. I have one specific output I would like you to check against what you already know. I am not asking you to read a theory first, and I am not asking you to agree with anything. If the output does not survive, there is nothing else to look at.
>
> **The output (`specified`, not `grounded`).** The decision is indexed per employee, never averaged. Define `R_s = |Γ(b,a) − o'|`: the gap between what the employee's frame predicts for the action (stay, leave, withdraw) and what actually happened, the revealed quit or stay. The anchor is the revealed event and its date, not a survey intention and not an exit-interview reason. From that, three things follow.
>
> 1. Retention is a property of the employee and the habitat together, not of pay or of the person alone. Changing pay in isolation is an improvement that does not change contact.
> 2. Pay is an actor whose sign depends on the residual. It is not a fixed good. Increasing an atom the employee's frame already holds is search inside a closed frame, and it cannot move the wall when what is missing is a bond or an outside comparison.
> 3. The two halves of the question are one union compound read two ways, not two versions of one puzzle. A person can stay because of links, fit, sacrifice and obligation at once, and can leave well-paid work because one condition fails, such as a shock or an imported outside offer.
>
> What I am **not** claiming: that pay never retains anyone, that commitment is a single cause of retention, that any named component (affective, normative, continuance, embeddedness, fairness) is derived by the framework, or that this is a grounded result. Those names are bindings from your field onto atoms the shape says are held. Until their provenance is shown they carry no evidentiary weight. Exit interviews are reports from inside the frame being checked, not ground readings.
>
> **What would refute the shape** (written down before this message):
> 1. A within-person record in which stay/leave and tenure are invariant to changes in ties, alternatives and channel updates while pay varies systematically, with the residual not trending down.
> 2. An atom already held in the frame, increased with no contact with any different frame, repeatedly reverses a quit at the ground with the residual trending down.
> 3. Two employees with identical atoms and identical bond types still diverge in the observed decision, with no unmeasured atom.
> 4. One lever restores both union conditions with no categorically different residue left over.
>
> A one-line "this fails because..." is more useful to me than agreement. If you would rather not engage, no reply is needed.
>
> **If it survives, and only then:** I will send the sources. The figures are the authority, not my summary of them. The first source is a local extract of the verbatim figure text with line numbers; the rest are listed in the ledger in the package.
>
> **If you want to use the method:** write down what would refute your own result before you run it, and label the mode of what you get. A reported failure is more useful to me than a reported success.

## Two-stage offer, explicit

1. **Stage 1.** Send the residual, the three-point output and the four refuters. Ask what the recipient would count as a failure. Record the verdict **before** showing any theory.
2. **Stage 2, opt-in only.** If they are curious, send the figure text extract, the canonical README and the source ledger. Their later agreement is never pooled as corroboration (Fig 2 :40 *"agreement about the world is worthless when the agreeing frames share an evidence pool"*).
3. **Stage 3.** Offer the formula as a method to reuse, with the instruction that each user writes their own refuter and states their own mode. A generator can be given away without giving away an answer (Fig 4 :22).

**Hard precondition:** stages 1 to 3 only work if the stage-1 output survives. This package does not claim it has been checked against a ground. It asks for that check.
