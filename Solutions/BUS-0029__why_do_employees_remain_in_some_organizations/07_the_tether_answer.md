# The Tether's answer on employee retention

**Mode: `specified` on the substantive claims, `general` on the structural shape, not `grounded`.** This is a shape and a route, not a result. Simulated derivation checked against the figures (`05_what_shape_means.md` section 6), not a ground reading. It cannot say why any particular employee stayed or left.

## 1. The residual it answers

For one employee-slot `s`, `R_s = |Γ(b,a) − o'|`: the gap between what the employee's frame predicts for the action (stay, leave, withdraw) and the revealed stay or leave event. The pay input to the prediction fails in two directions: some slots stay under poor conditions, others leave under attractive pay. The residual is indexed per slot and never averaged (Fig 1 :19 *"The residual is indexed by object slot, not aggregated."*). The anchor is the revealed event and its date, not an intention or an exit-interview reason (Fig 2 :29).

The two halves are one union compound read two ways, not two problems and not one problem (Fig 9 :57 *"What remains is smaller and it is also categorically different: a case in two conditions is not a harder case of either."*).

## 3. What it explicitly does not say

- Not that pay never retains anyone. Pay may be the live atom in a given slot; a global negative would break the per-slot rule (Fig 1 :13).
- Not that commitment is a single cause or that "they stayed because they were committed" is an explanation. As the field defines it, that is the outcome renamed (Fig 12 :5).
- Not that any named component is derived by the framework. Every name is a binding from a world frame, provenance recorded, zero verdict weight until settled.
- Not an exit-interview theory. An exit interview is a report from inside the frame being checked, not a ground reading (Fig 10 :42 *"A verifier that can reconstruct the claim cannot verify it."*).
- Not `grounded`. No measurement discriminates the bond types here.

## 4. What would settle it, the ground

A source-discriminating instrument, not a further argument. *The Hard Problem is About Source* (opened 2026-11-22): an instrument that reads green *"cannot comment on the source"*, and *"The answer is unreached until a better instrument exists."* Here the corresponding questions are:

1. **Bond-type test.** Is the stay decision in a slot driven by `want to`, `ought to` or `have to`, measured independently of the stated reason and of the outcome? An instrument that separates these would name the slot.
2. **Contact test.** Does an outside offer that crosses into a differing closure reverse the decision while pay is held constant? If not, Fig 6 :23 does not govern D3.
3. **Channel test.** Does an updated, unpooled reading of current conditions, with history and ties held, flip the decision? If not, D4's channel-decay claim does not govern.
4. **Habitat test.** Does a move to better pay reproduce the goal-relevant actors without inheriting unchosen ones? If so, F6 is wrong for that case.
5. **Standing falsifiers:** the eight in `01_figure_grounded_audit.md` section 3. Any one firing narrows or withdraws the shape.

## 5. The honest stop

**Unreached, not unreachable.** The bond vocabulary is a binding from one field's careful guess, and declaring it makes the choice visible, not neutral. Another field's vocabulary need not be these three components. The answer stops at the limit of the available instruments and records the lineage so the stop is checkable. *"Indiscernibility is not identity"* (canonical README). Simulated derivation, internal consistency only, not a ground reading.

## 6. One-paragraph version for outreach

> The Tether does not give a single cause of retention and does not claim to say why a particular person stayed. It says that the decision is indexed per employee, not averaged into a company rate; that retention is a property of the employee and the habitat together, so raising pay in isolation is an improvement that does not change contact; that pay is an actor whose sign depends on the residual, so adding more of an atom the frame already holds cannot move the wall when what is missing is a bond or an outside comparison; that staying in poor conditions and leaving well-paid work are one union compound read two ways, which must be unbundled before it is searched; and that a decision can run on a stale or pooled reading while the ground, the revealed event, does not decay. Same atoms under different bonds are different substances, which is why two employees with the same pay and conditions can diverge. The framework returns that shape, not a name. It refuses to say which bond wins in a given slot until an instrument that discriminates bond types exists. What it offers is the shape, the refuters written in advance, and the tests that would close it. The slot stays open until the ground settles it.
