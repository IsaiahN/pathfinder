## 1. The residual it answers

What they can do is constrain the shape of any answer: the numerator is a union compound, its persistence condition is a closing loop, the arrangement of the inventory is decisive, and the count itself is owed by a ground rather than by composition. ## 2. The shape

1. **The question is a union, not one quantity.** Reference class, a test for surface liquid water, a duration, and a maintenance condition must be bound separately. Fig 9 :55: *"A bundled problem is usually a union of overlapping conditions, not a list of separate ones."* A single lever such as orbital position clears only the cases where one condition binds and leaves the intersections standing.
2. **Persistence is a cycle, not a chain.** Fig 12 :121: *"cycle. The output re-enters as input. The sign of the loop decides whether it closes, and a chain cannot close by construction."* The world literature names the loop: carbonate-silicate weathering with mineral resupply, plus deep water cycling. Without resupply *"the feedback's strength wanes"* (Coy, Kite and Graham 2025).
3. **Arrangement decides, inventory does not.** Fig 12 :117: *"isomer. Same atoms, different bond, different substance. The difference is invisible in the formula and decisive in the behaviour."* Two planets with the same water, rock and carbon can differ as a stable ocean world and a runaway greenhouse. "Habitable zone" is a functional group (Fig 12 :123), not a member.
4. **The frequency is unreachable from the figures.** Fig 9 :32: *"you get a kind of thing, never the answer itself."* Fig 13 :134: *"Unreachable is a verdict here, not a silence,"* Six terms and seven bonds compose structures; they do not enumerate planets. This is underivable in principle from the framework, so the instruction is OUTWARD: go to the astronomical and Earth-system frames, characterise R in effect terms, and import with provenance.
5. **The anchor is a measurement, not agreement.** Fig 2 :29: *"An anchor is whatever settles the question and does not change its answer because of what any frame thinks. A measurement, an experiment, a counter, a market, a patient outcome."* Three published occurrence rates using different radius bands and instellation ranges are three objectives, not three witnesses. Agreement among them is a shared pool, not corroboration (Fig 2 :40).

## 3. What it explicitly does not say

- Not a percentage. No published occurrence rate is converted into a persistence rate here.
- Not that being in a habitable zone is sufficient. The habitable zone is a functional group; the member requires a closing loop.
- Not that inventory alone decides. Bulk water fraction is the flat formula the figure warns about.
- Not that the framework derived anything from the astronomical catalogs. Those are imports with provenance, marked as such in `10_source_ledger.csv`.
- Not `grounded`. No instrument has measured gigayear persistence of surface water on an exoplanet.

## 4. What would settle it - the ground

A better instrument and a fixed definition, not a further argument. Fig 6 :34: *"What is unavailable is a sensor for something that has never registered anywhere, at any resolution."* The specific questions are:

- **Which instrument discriminates a persistent reservoir from a transient one** at the surface of a rocky exoplanet? Reflected-light spectra from a future direct-imaging mission are a candidate; no current survey measures the gigayear history.
- **Resupply test:** can the weathering feedback be shown to stay closed on a specific planet over the stated interval, given its tectonic regime and water partitioning? If not, the numerator is zero for that planet regardless of its orbital address.
- **Definition test:** do the published occurrence studies converge once radius band, instellation range, completeness assumption and duration are fixed? They do not at present (F7).
- **Standing falsifiers** from `01` section 5: derive a frequency from the six terms alone; find one lever that clears all four conditions; observe gigayear persistence with no closing loop; show inventory alone predicts persistence; show the published numbers share one definition and converge.
