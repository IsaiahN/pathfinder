## VERDICT (from the figures)

The shape survives; the number does not. The question is a union compound whose persistence condition is a closing feedback cycle with resupply, whose decisive variable is arrangement rather than inventory, and whose frequency is unreachable from the six-term substrate and owed by a ground under a fixed definition. The three published occurrence rates are different objectives, not competing estimates of one quantity. The Earth-system literature corroborates the mechanism but cannot be imported into the figure set as a derivation. Status: `general` on the shape, `specified` on the structure, `gap` on the frequency, ungrounded, candidate.

- **cost if wrong:** bounded and locatable. The packet states its mode, fixes its four bindings and records its refuters, so an error is findable rather than invisible. The real cost is converting a definition-specific occurrence rate into a physical persistence claim, which Fig 2 :42 prices: "Buy independence in proportion to the cost of being wrong." That cost is avoidable by leading with the shape claim and its refuter and by keeping the catalog, definition and access date with any number.
- **undoable:** yes. The four bindings and the three occurrence rates are world imports with provenance; they can be replaced without touching the figure-derived structure. A sent claim can be superseded by a corrected definition, and no primitive has to be withdrawn.
- **reversed by:** a ground reading, not agreement. An instrument that distinguishes a gigayear reservoir from a transient warm episode would name the numerator; a survey under one fixed definition would supply the denominator; a planet observed with gigayear liquid surface water and no closing loop would break the cycle claim (Fig 12 :121); a derivation of a frequency from the six terms and bonds alone would break the gap verdict (Fig 9 :32). Until one of these fires, the verdict stands as candidate.
