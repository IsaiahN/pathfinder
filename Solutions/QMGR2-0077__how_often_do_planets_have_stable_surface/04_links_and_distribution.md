## Wider literature that bears on the question

Three things in the fetched literature matter for the derivation, and none of them is a figure line.

1. **The published numbers use different definitions.** Petigura et al count Earth-size planets at one to four times Earth's intensity; Bryson et al define eta-Earth as 0.5 to 1.5 Earth radii in a conservative or optimistic habitable zone. Mulders et al use a simulated-observation estimator. These are three objectives at Fig 3's broken middle link, not three measurements of one quantity.
2. **Persistence is a maintenance result, not an orbital result.** Coy et al and Cowan and Abbot put the load-bearing condition in mineral resupply and tectonic regime. Gilbert-Janizek et al couple interior and surface evolution over 5 Gyr. That is the world-side instance of Fig 12 :121's closing cycle.
3. **The instrument is still the missing thing.** All three occurrence studies correct for detection completeness; none measures gigayear persistence of surface water on an exoplanet. Fig 6 :34: *"What is unavailable is a sensor for something that has never registered anywhere, at any resolution."* The honest status of the numerator is `unreached`, not `unreachable`.

## Distribution: offer, do not push

Figure-grounded reasons:

- **Fig 4 :22** *"Only methods cross up. Only head starts come down."* Sending the full architecture before anyone has described a residual sends a recording, not a generator. Lead with the shape claim and its refuter; offer links after.
- **Fig 10 :42** *"A verifier that can reconstruct the claim cannot verify it."* A recipient who has absorbed the framework before being asked for a verdict has joined the frame being checked.
- **Fig 9 :27-28** *"describe it before you go looking, or any frame you pick will seem to fit."* Ask what they would expect to see if the shape claim were true before giving them the article.
- **Fig 2 :40** *"agreement about the world is worthless when the agreeing frames share an evidence pool"* A mass send selected for people who already agree produces a convention, not corroboration.

**Recommended order in a reply:** (1) the shape claim and its five refuters; (2) ask what they would count as a failure; (3) record their verdict; (4) only then the three links, with the note that the figures, not the summary, are the authority.

## The two-stage offer

- **Stage 1, before stage 2.** Fig 10 :52 *"Bring a characterised residual, never the source."* The recipient checks the shape without holding the framework's interior.
- **Stage 2 is opt-in by construction.** Seat 4 must not hold the interiors; once a recipient has absorbed the theory, their agreement stops being evidence. Their stage-1 verdict is recorded before any theory is shown and is never pooled as corroboration.
- **Stage 3 sends a method, which is permitted.** Fig 4 :22: only methods cross up. The formula is a generator, so giving it away does not give away an answer.
- **Stage 3 still needs a ground per problem.** The formula's Direction section: a frame *"cannot check the direction of its own question, because the shape a frame is searching for is not a term it holds."* Each user writes down what would refute their result before running it.

## Hard precondition

Stages 1 to 3 work only if the stage-1 output survives. As written, the question itself does not define its property (F7). Fix the four bindings before sending; otherwise the recipient is being asked to check a moving target.
