## 1. A shape is a constraint, not a filler

The figures README states the limit: the framework returns *"what a missing thing would have to look like (a shape, not a name)."* Fig 9 :32: *"you get a kind of thing, never the answer itself."* Fig 13 :134: *"Unreachable is a verdict here, not a silence,"*

From *The Library of Babel* (2026-10-02): *"you can cut your search short if the set of books returned is small and descriptive enough that it provides a general shape of the answer that satisfies your question."*

So a **shape** is the narrowed form of an answer you can state before you can name or count it. It constrains; it does not fill. For this question the shape is: a union compound whose persistence condition is a closing loop with resupply, a count that is owed by a ground, and a reference class that must be fixed before the count means anything. The shape is not "about 30 percent of planets."

## 2. How the shape is obtained

- *Library of Babel*: use the vocabulary you already have closest to the thing, look for recurring new terms that overlap with it, recurse. *"With each new term gained, you are able to narrow the space by definition and deepen your vocabulary."*
- *Library of Babel* on malformed questions: *"Perhaps your problem was a bundle or conflation of several problems masquerading as one?"* This is the exact instruction followed in F1 of `01`. Unbundle, then recombine.
- Fig 6 :30: *"The description travels. Fails by being sharpened into terms only we can read. Cast it in effect terms."* So describe "stable surface water" as an effect test (a reservoir that remains liquid and measurable over a stated interval), not as an inhabitant of a frame's favourite category.
- *Decoding Tacit Knowledge* (2026-11-10): *"When contact isn't possible, the best you can send is a plain description of what you'd expect to see, because anyone can check that against the world."* This is why the outreach email asks what a referee would count as a failure.
- *Library of Babel* on stopping: *"the finish line will also be the starting place,"* and the paradox is that by then you have *"already composed the answer through the process of locating it."* Here the process composes a definition and a maintenance condition, not a percentage.

## 3. What licenses a name or a number

Only two things, both outside the shape itself:

1. **The ground settles it.** Formula step 5: *"the ground settles it. Verified only by what pays. A gate is not the ground."* For a frequency, that means a completed survey plus an instrument that can detect the reservoir, under a definition fixed in advance.
2. **A provenanced import from a frame whose closure differs**, with a source gate and a shadow test. The astronomical occurrence-rate papers and the Earth-system climate papers are such frames. Their numbers import into the demography domain, not into the six-term substrate.

*They're Scanning Books When They Should Be Studying Nature* (2026-09-06): *"The real import is not a new term in the existing space. It is a new dimension of the space."* Carrying an occurrence rate into the figure set would be a new term in the existing space; it would add no atom and would mislabel a measurement as a derivation.

The same essay: *"A minimum viable set of six terms is a human's careful guess at reality's compression axes; another sensorium's minimum set need not be those six."* And: *"Declaring the primitives does not make them neutral. It makes the choice visible."* So the framework's refusal to name a number is not a claim that no number exists; it is a record of which instrument is missing.

## 4. Why the occurrence-rate reading is the metamerism error

*The Hard Problem is About Source* (2026-08-28) gives the mechanism. An instrument reads green and *"the instrument is speaking only about what it measured, it cannot comment on the source."* One green light, or yellow plus blue, or RGB, or CMYK could give the same reading. *"If several paths create the same behavior, then behavior is a projection with lossy information (it cannot define consciousness in full)."*

Here the reading is "planet in the habitable zone," and several sources give it: an orbital address, a reflective cloud layer, a short-lived warm episode, or a genuinely persistent reservoir. The reading does not determine the source. *"An instrument that can't distinguish these cases demonstrates its own inadequacy."* The same essay: *"The answer is unreached until a better instrument exists."* That is why the status of the numerator is `unreached`, and why the solution refuses to convert an occurrence rate into a persistence rate.

## 5. Q: does "shape, not a name" forbid using a published frequency?

**No.** The README's phrase governs a *missing* primitive, one not held in Γ. A frequency is not a primitive; it is a world result from a different closure. The correct form is to record the number with its definition, catalog, completeness assumptions and access date, and to mark it as a world import. What is forbidden is presenting the number as something the six-term set produced.

The recorded strain from the first pass is kept visible in `01` section 6: the initial draft treated three published frequencies as competing answers to one question. They are different objectives. Fixing that is an application of the shape discipline, not a retraction of the figures.

## 6. Two consequences for outreach

**Prestige is not action.** From *The Recursive Self-Improvement Trap* (2026-11-19): *"Prestige must never be allowed to validate an action directly."* And: *"Prestige can fund an action. It can give an action a budget to be tried. But it cannot settle an action. Only the ground can settle it. The Ground Maintainer must be blind to prestige."* Gathering famous names to agree with a frequency claim is the named collapse, not corroboration. The legitimate ask is to give the claim a budget to be tried.

**My own audit is a simulation, not a ground reading.** Same essay: *"LLMs can quickly simulate an approximate answer/output with the formula found in the architecture post."* And: *"the truly testable output would need to be derived from the real architecture or working the formula yourself."* The architecture essay adds that LLM outputs *"could never be verified if the solutions presented were not contrived to fit the formula."* So `01` is a simulated derivation, checked for internal consistency against the figures. It is **not** a measurement and cannot settle anything it reports on. Fig 11 :40: *"An improvement that does not change contact changes nothing, however much it improves."*
