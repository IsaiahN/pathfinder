## Round trip gap R_T

The Symbols table defines R_T as the gap between what was sent up and what came back down, `gap(x, (T_E o T_A)(x))`, measuring what the coarser description cannot hold. Fig 4 :30 requires it to be measured rather than assumed. I ran the round trip on the condensation the folder actually uses.

1. **Sent up (x):** the verbatim figure set, chiefly Fig 9 :55 and :57 (union compound), Fig 12 :121 (cycle), Fig 12 :117 (isomer), Fig 2 :29 (measurement anchor).
2. **Abstraction (T_A):** the shorthand carried in `07`: the question is a bundle; persistence is a loop; arrangement decides; the count is owed by a ground.
3. **Concretisation (T_E):** a concrete frequency claim such as "30 percent of Sun-like stars have stable surface water for a gigayear."

**R_T is nonzero.** Four things the shorthand holds do not survive the trip back down:

- the **duration T**, which the abstracted "stable over long periods" does not fix and which every concrete claim must specify;
- the **reference class C**, which the shorthand leaves open and which changes the denominator;
- the **resupply term**, which the shorthand carries as "loop" but which a concrete planet claim must name and test;
- the **phase distinction**, which the shorthand carries as "arrangement" but which a concrete claim must state as liquid surface water rather than bulk water inventory.

Conversely, one thing survives cleanly: persistence requires a closing loop and the question is a compound whose parts must be counted separately. That is the residue worth sending. R_T is **not** measured numerically, because no ground reading exists for persistence; it is recorded qualitatively as the enumerated set of dropped constraints. Treating a published occurrence rate as identical to the figure-derived shape would set R_T to zero and would be the dead-reckoning error Fig 4 :30 names.
