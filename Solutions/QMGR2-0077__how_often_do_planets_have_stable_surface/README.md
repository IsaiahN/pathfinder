## Restatement in effect terms

The question asks for a ratio: the number of planets that keep a liquid surface-water reservoir over a geological interval, divided by the number of planets in a stated reference class. What is being decided is whether long-term surface water is a common statistical outcome of planet formation and climate, or a contingent compound that survives only where several conditions overlap.

- If it is **common**, then a frequency is a stable ensemble property and can be read off occurrence-rate surveys plus a definition of the property.
- If it is **compound**, then a single lever such as orbital position clears only the cases where one condition binds; the intersections survive, and the frequency is sensitive to how the property is defined (Fig 9 :55, :57).

## Direction: binding what an answer would look like

Per the formula's Direction rule, the sought-for shape is made an ordinary term before the run. The shape predicted here is written down and then allowed to accrue a residual:

> A frequency claim is answerable only after four things are bound: (1) the reference class of planets, (2) an operational test for surface liquid water, (3) a duration for "long periods," and (4) a maintenance condition that keeps the reservoir from being consumed or frozen. The figures constrain (4) structurally and refuse to supply a number for the ratio. The number is owed by a ground: survey counts plus instruments that can see the reservoir.

That shape is stated before conclusion and is refusable by the same test as any other term (see `01_figure_grounded_audit.md` section 5).

## What is deliberately not here

- No personal emails of named individuals.
- No ranking of people by how likely they are to agree with this derivation.
- No bulk-send plan.

Three reasons, in the figures' own words:

1. **Correlation disqualifies a verdict.** Fig 2 :40: *"agreement about the world is worthless when the agreeing frames share an evidence pool."* Selecting recipients for receptiveness manufactures the correlation that makes their agreement worthless.
2. **Agreement is not an anchor.** Fig 2 :35: *"Alignment is triangulation, not negotiation. A mirror cannot correct you."* The anchor for a frequency is a measurement or a counter (Fig 2 :29), not a vote of interested readers.
3. **A verifier who holds the interiors cannot verify.** Fig 10 :42: *"A verifier that can reconstruct the claim cannot verify it."* The first contact carries the residual and its refuter, never the framework's interior.

Legally: scraping personal emails for unsolicited bulk outreach breaches GDPR and CAN-SPAM style rules in many jurisdictions. Public opt-in channels avoid that failure as well as the structural one.
