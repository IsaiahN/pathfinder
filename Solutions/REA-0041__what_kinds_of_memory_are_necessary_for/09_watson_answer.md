## QUESTION

Decide, in effect terms, what the Tether says about the necessary kinds of memory and their organization. Option A: treat the familiar episodic/semantic/procedural taxonomy as the answer and organize memory by capacity and retrieval speed. Option B: keep only what the figures govern, separate the record from the reach, keep methods apart from occasion-records, and order the functions by dependency. What each leads to: A stores more and measures the wrong quantity, so a growing library is mistaken for growing flexibility; B sends a checkable shape, written refuters and an instrument requirement, with the taxonomy left open until the ground settles it.

## DOMAIN CENSUS

R by arity, symmetry and scale, then one line per domain with actors / currency / anchor, partition or union, and order by dependency.

- **R (the residual).** Arity: memory relates at least two encounters and is indexed per object slot. Symmetry: record versus reach are asymmetric (one monotone, one not); method versus instance are asymmetric across the membrane. Scale: settling within a scale, method transfer across scales with a lossy round trip. Fig 9 :22: "arity · symmetry · scale".
- **Domain 1, the store / record.** Actors: primitives, terms, Γ, closure. Currency: bits and description length. Anchor: the ground pays for a term before it can be composed over. Question type: partition (what may enter the library).
- **Domain 2, the reach / usable memory.** Actors: settled molecules, bonds, methods. Currency: derivability, what can be composed now. Anchor: the ground, with the reach falling on refutation. Question type: partition against D1 (two quantities, not one).
- **Domain 3, the membrane / cross-scale memory.** Actors: one agent, the population, methods, recordings. Currency: methods up, head starts down. Anchor: the round-trip gap R_T. Question type: a filter with direction, not a wall.
- **Domain 4, the channel / contact maintenance.** Actors: maintainer seat, percepts, instruments, habitat. Currency: contact, channel decay. Anchor: the ground, which does not decay. Question type: maintenance, a seat's office.
- **Domain 5, the dependency chain of learning.** Actors: perception, vocabulary, objective, planning, carry. Currency: what each link produces for the next. Anchor: the objective link, where chains usually break. Question type: ordered dependency.
- **Partition or union.** D1 and D2 are a partition of quantities. The five memory functions are a union of overlapping conditions, so they are unbundled before a checker is sought (Fig 9 :54-55).
- **Order by dependency.** Store, then settle, then reach, then carry across scales, then maintain contact. The carry link consumes perception, vocabulary and the objective (Fig 3 :20).

Re-census returned them to D1 and D2. After re-census, one named strain remains: the union reading is an application of Fig 9 :54-55 to the census, not itself a figure line.

## VERDICT (from the figures)

The shape survives; a settled taxonomy does not. Flexible intelligence needs a record that only grows and a reach that can fall, kept as separate quantities; methods kept apart from occasion-records; a settled status that is necessary but not sufficient for use; per-slot residual indexing; active channel maintenance; and lineage kept with each term. These are ordered by dependency, form a union of overlapping conditions, and are judged by contact with a ground rather than by a synthetic score. The five functions are load-bearing conditions in a `specified` simulated derivation. The familiar cognitive taxonomy is world corroboration, contested in part, and is not derived by the framework. Nothing here is `grounded`.

- **cost if wrong:** bounded and locatable. The packet states its mode, refuters and lineage before the conclusion, so an error is findable rather than invisible. The real cost is spending an outside reader's independence by sending a settled-looking taxonomy instead of a shape and a refuter, which Fig 2 :42 prices ("Buy independence in proportion to the cost of being wrong."). That cost is avoidable by leading with the shape claim and its refuters.
- **undoable:** yes. The contested taxonomic claims are removable without touching the derivation; `07_the_tether_answer.md` and `08_expert_verification_packet.md` already remove them. A sent claim can be superseded by the corrected packet, and no figure line has to be withdrawn.
- **reversed by:** a ground reading, not agreement. An instrument that discriminates a method from an occasion-record in a memory trace, a measured separation of stored-term count from reachable-set size under refutation, or a per-slot readout that locates a live slot a global average misses would move parts of the shape toward `grounded`. A demonstration that the carry link improves with the objective link unmeasured, or an agent-only memory change that moves the ground, would break it. Until one of these fires, the verdict stands as `candidate`.
