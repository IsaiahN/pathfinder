# The Tether's answer: memory kinds for flexible intelligence

**Mode: `specified` — not `grounded`.** A shape and a dependency order, not a result. Simulated derivation checked against the figures (`05_what_shape_means.md` §7), not a ground reading.

## 1. The residual it answers

Flexible intelligence fails when what is carried from past encounters does not shrink the residual in a new one. Two failure modes are named: the store grows while the reach does not, and the channel to the ground decays. This is a union of overlapping conditions (Fig 9 :54–55), so it is unbundled before answering rather than treated as one storage problem.

## 2. The kinds that are necessary, and why

1. **A record that only grows (Γ).** Fig 6 :42 "What is recorded only grows. What is reachable is derived, and it can fall." The library is append-only; even a refutation adds rather than deletes (Fig 5 :55 "Nothing leaves the library. What falls is the reach."). Necessary because without a retained record nothing can be carried to the next problem.
2. **A reach, kept as a separate quantity.** Fig 6 :43 "So the closure and the reach are two quantities: one monotone, one not." Fig 5 :72 "a library that grows is not a reach that grows." Necessary because the loop is driven by what can be composed now, not by how much is stored (THE_FORMULA.md step 8).
3. **Methods, kept apart from occasion-records.** Fig 4 :22 "Only methods cross up. Only head starts come down." Fig 4 :25 "A recording carried upward looks like knowledge and is a description of one occasion." Fig 10 :68 "a generator crosses, an instance does not" (different figure, low overlap). Necessary because only the reapplicable generator travels between an individual and a population; a recording of one occasion does not.
4. **A settled status, distinct from usability.** Fig 5 :69 "settling is necessary for a term to be composed over, and it is not sufficient." Fig 12 :106 "Nothing composes over what was never admitted as a part." Necessary because a term must be paid for by the ground before composition, yet payment alone does not make it usable.
5. **Per-slot residual indexing.** Fig 1 :19 "The residual is indexed by object slot, not aggregated." Fig 1 :22 "Aggregating across slots is how a live signal disappears into an average." Necessary because the averaged score destroys the only signal that says where to aim next.
6. **An active channel-maintenance office.** Fig 10 :24 "the ground does not decay, the channel does." Fig 10 :29 "→ keep the anchor reachable." Necessary because a stored term whose route to the ground has decayed no longer counts as knowledge; the output is "you have lost touch" (Fig 10 :28).
7. **Lineage kept with each term.** Fig 12 :130 "A term carries the atoms it was built from and the bonds that joined them. That record is not documentation of the term. It is the term." Necessary because the route is not recoverable from the artefact (Fig 12 :129), and reachability cannot be checked without it.

## 3. How they should be organized

- **By dependency, not by importance.** Fig 3 :20 "The chain is ordered by dependency, not by importance." The carry link (Fig 3 :15 "name what worked, verify it, and carry it to the next problem") comes last and consumes perception, vocabulary and the objective. The middle links have no instrument and are where chains break (Fig 3 :25–27).
- **As a union that is unbundled before search.** Fig 9 :54 "when the split lands on a union rather than a partition, unbundle before you search again." The five functions overlap; record versus reach is the one genuine partition of quantities (Fig 6 :43).
- **Recursively, with a measured round trip.** Fig 12 :104 "A settled molecule becomes an atom for whatever composes over it." Fig 4 :30 "So take the round trip and measure the gap. Without it you are navigating by dead reckoning: confident about a position nobody checked."
- **By contact, not by benchmark solves.** Fig 11 :39 "capability is a property of agent-and-habitat, never of the agent alone." Fig 11 :70 "A test harness is a substituted habitat. Which is why a synthetic solve proves wiring and never capability."

## 4. What it explicitly does not say

- Not a settled cognitive taxonomy. The framework returns a shape, not a name. Episodic, semantic and procedural are world labels and their two-system reading is contested.
- Not that more storage produces flexibility. Fig 5 :72 and the essay *They're Scanning Books* both refuse that.
- Not that a benchmark score names the source of the capability. Fig 11 :70 and *The Hard Problem is About Source* require a discriminating instrument.
- Not that memory can be optimized in the agent in isolation. Fig 11 :40 "An improvement that does not change contact changes nothing, however much it improves."
- Not `grounded`. No measurement closes the taxonomy.

## 5. What would settle it — the ground

A source-discriminating instrument, not another argument. *The Hard Problem is About Source*: the spectrometer, not a better theory, resolved metamerism. Here:

1. An instrument that distinguishes a reapplicable method from a one-occasion record in a given memory trace, rather than reading both as "recall."
2. A demonstration that stored-term count and reachable-set size come apart under refutation, measured rather than asserted.
3. A per-slot residual readout that locates a live slot where a global average does not.
4. Standing falsifiers: see §6.

## 6. Falsifiers (pre-registered in `01` §5)

1. A memory whose reachable set equals its stored set under every refutation (breaks the record/reach split).
2. An occasion-record carried across scales that is reapplicable without loss, or a zero-gap round trip (breaks method versus recording).
3. A global error average that preserves a live slot as well as a per-slot record (breaks F5).
4. An agent-only memory change that moves the ground, or a synthetic benchmark that establishes capability rather than wiring (breaks F10).

## 7. The honest stop

**Unreached, not unreachable.** The five functions are load-bearing conditions in a simulated derivation, not a verified list. The figures README: the framework stops *"at the limit of what the available instruments can distinguish, and records the lineage so the stop is honest."* *"Indiscernibility is not identity."* Fig 8 :39 "So a search that finds nothing is never a proof of absence." A memory taxonomy that has not been discriminated is not shown to be false; it is shown to be unclosed until an instrument arrives.

## 8. One-paragraph version for outreach

> The Tether does not offer a new list of memory bins. It says that flexible intelligence needs a record that only grows and a reach that can fall, kept as two different quantities, because counting what is stored is not a measure of what can be used. On that split sit a few load-bearing distinctions: methods that can be reapplied cross between a person and a population while one-occasion records do not; a term must be settled by the ground before it can be composed over, yet settling alone is not enough; the signal for where to look is per item or slot and disappears if you average it; the channel to the ground decays and must be kept reachable; and the lineage of a term is part of the term, not documentation of it. These are organized by dependency, with the "name what worked, carry it forward" link last, and judged by contact with a ground rather than by a synthetic score. What is offered is that shape, four falsifiers written in advance, and the instrument that would close it. The taxonomy stays open until a source-discriminating measurement settles it.
