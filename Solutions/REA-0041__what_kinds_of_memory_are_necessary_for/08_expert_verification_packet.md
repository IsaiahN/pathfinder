# Expert verification packet — the Tether on memory kinds

**What this is:** a claim in a form a memory-systems researcher can check. Mode, derivation and refuters are stated before the conclusion, so a reader knows what to look for and what would settle it.

**Mode: `specified` — not `grounded`.** No measurement is claimed to settle a taxonomy. This is a shape and a dependency order.

## 1. The claim, in one paragraph

Flexible intelligence requires a record that only grows and a reach that can fall, kept as two distinct quantities, because the number of stored terms is not the measure of what can be brought to bear. On that split sit load-bearing distinctions: reapplicable methods cross between an individual and a population while one-occasion records do not; a term must be settled by the ground before it can be composed over, but settling is not sufficient for usability; the residual that says where to look is per slot and is destroyed by averaging; the channel to the ground decays and must be actively kept reachable; and the lineage of a term is part of the term. These functions form a union of overlapping conditions ordered by dependency, with the carry link last, and are judged by contact with a ground rather than by a synthetic benchmark score. The framework returns a shape, not a name.

## 2. Three checkable sub-claims

### C1 — framework-internal; checkable by reading Figures 6 and 5
- **Statement:** the record and the reach are two different memory quantities, one monotone and one revisable. A refutation adds a refusing term and reduces the reach without deleting anything.
- **Source, verbatim:** Fig 6 :42 "What is recorded only grows. What is reachable is derived, and it can fall." Also Fig 6 :43 "So the closure and the reach are two quantities: one monotone, one not." And Fig 5 :55 "Nothing leaves the library. What falls is the reach."
- **Refuter:** show a system whose reachable set is identical to its stored set under every refutation, or that stored-term count is a sufficient measure of what it can bring to bear.

### C2 — framework-internal, world-facing; checkable by a memory researcher
- **Statement:** the transmissible unit is a reapplicable method, not an occasion-record; and settling is necessary but not sufficient for usability.
- **Source, verbatim:** Fig 4 :22 "Only methods cross up. Only head starts come down." Fig 4 :25 "A recording carried upward looks like knowledge and is a description of one occasion." Fig 10 :68 "a generator crosses, an instance does not". Fig 5 :69 "settling is necessary for a term to be composed over, and it is not sufficient."
- **What to check:** does the distinction survive contact with episodic/semantic/procedural memory, systems consolidation and reconsolidation, and memory-augmented architectures? The world sources in `09` rhyme with it but the strict two-system reading is contested, so corroboration is partial.
- **Refuter:** show an occasion-record carried across scales that is reapplicable without loss, or a cross-scale round trip with zero gap, or a term that composes over something never settled by the ground.

### C3 — dependency and evidence; checkable by anyone who runs a memory experiment
- **Statement:** the carry link depends on the objective link, and memory capability is a property of agent-and-habitat, so a synthetic solve proves wiring and never capability.
- **Source, verbatim:** Fig 3 :20 "The chain is ordered by dependency, not by importance." Fig 3 :15 "name what worked, verify it, and carry it to the next problem". Fig 11 :39 "capability is a property of agent-and-habitat, never of the agent alone." Fig 11 :70 "A test harness is a substituted habitat. Which is why a synthetic solve proves wiring and never capability."
- **Refuter:** show the carry link improving while the objective link is unmeasured and the improvement is not a re-description of the same residual; or show an agent-only memory change that moves the ground.

## 3. What is explicitly not claimed

- Not a settled cognitive taxonomy. Episodic, semantic and procedural are world labels, and their separation is contested; the framework returns a shape, not a name.
- Not that more storage produces flexibility. Fig 5 :72: *"a library that grows is not a reach that grows."*
- Not that a benchmark score names the source of the capability.
- Not that memory can be optimized in the agent in isolation.
- Not `grounded`. Nothing here is asserted as settled by experiment.
- Not that the five functions are the six substrate terms of Fig 13. That was tried and retracted as a strain (`01` §1).

## 5. What would settle it

A source-discriminating instrument, not a further argument. From *The Hard Problem is About Source*: a colour instrument *"cannot comment on the source"*; the spectrometer resolved the case. Here:

1. An instrument that distinguishes a reapplicable method from a one-occasion record in a memory trace, rather than reading both as recall.
2. A measured separation of stored-term count from reachable-set size under refutation.
3. A per-slot residual readout that locates a live slot where a global average does not.

## 6. What an expert can check in twenty minutes

1. Read Fig 6 :42–43, Fig 4 :22 and :25, Fig 5 :69, Fig 3 :20, Fig 11 :39 and :70. Eight short passages.
2. Check C2 against their own field: does generator versus instance survive the evidence they know, or does it collapse into the episodic/semantic distinction?
3. Name one instrument that would separate a method from an occasion-record, or say why none is possible.
4. Send the refutation. A failure is more useful than an endorsement.
