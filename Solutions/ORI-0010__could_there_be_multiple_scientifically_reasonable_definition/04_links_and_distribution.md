## Distribution: offer, do not push

Figure-grounded reasons:

- Fig 10 :52 - "Bring a characterised residual, never the source." Lead with the shape claim and refuter. Sending the framework first sends a recording, not a generator.
- Fig 10 :42 - "A verifier that can reconstruct the claim cannot verify it." A recipient who has absorbed the framework before giving a verdict has joined the frame being checked.
- Fig 9 :27 - "describe it before you go looking, or any frame you pick will seem to fit." Ask what they would expect to see if the shape claim were true before giving them any links.
- Fig 2 :41 - "efficient for a convention: agreement about a shared meaning is the product, not a corruption of it." Do not treat later agreement as corroboration.
- Fig 2 :40 - "disqualifying for a verdict: agreement about the world is worthless when the agreeing frames share an evidence pool." Do not select recipients by receptiveness.

**Recommended order in a reply:** (1) the shape claim and its three refuters; (2) ask what the recipient would count as a failure; (3) record their verdict; (4) only then offer the links, with the note that the figure text, not the summary, is the authority.

## The two-stage offer

- **Stage 1 before stage 2.** Fig 10 :52 and Fig 2 :45 ("Frames range against the ground. They do not negotiate with it."). The recipient checks the output without holding the framework's interior.
- **Stage 2 is opt-in by construction.** Seat 4 must not hold the interiors; once a recipient has absorbed the theory, their agreement stops being evidence. Their stage-1 verdict must be recorded first.
- **Stage 3 sends a method, which is permitted.** Fig 4 :22 - "Only methods cross up. Only head starts come down." The formula is a generator, so giving it away does not give away an answer.
- **Stage 3 still needs a ground per problem.** `THE_FORMULA.md` "Direction": a frame "cannot check the direction of its own question, because the shape a frame is searching for is not a term it holds." Each user must write down what would refute their result before running it, and state the mode of what they get.

**Hard precondition:** stages 1 to 3 work only if the stage-1 output survives. If any pre-registered refuter fires, narrow the claim before sending.
