## QUESTION

Decide, in effect terms, whether a plurality of definitions of life is scientifically legitimate or a defect to be eliminated. Option A: there is one true definition D*, and every other candidate is an error. Option B: several definitions can each be reasonable, each scoped to a question, bounded by a guard that stops any one of them deciding an empirical outcome. What each leads to: A requires one term to range over viruses, mules, RNA-world replicators, synthetic cells and hypothetical extraterrestrial life, and turns a convention into a verdict. B keeps the residual visible per question and lets each ground settle its own slot.

## DOMAIN CENSUS

R by arity, symmetry and scale, then one line per domain with actors / currency / anchor, partition or union, and order by dependency.

- **R (the residual).** The persistent disagreement about what counts as life. Arity: it presents as one slot but its symptoms occupy several subsets (self-replicating RNA, metabolic networks, protocells, viruses, sterile individuals, synthetic cells, extraterrestrial life). Symmetry: the candidates are not interchangeable, since a replicator definition fails on a mule and a metabolic definition fails on a virion. Scale: from molecular replicators to lineages and planets. Fig 9 names this a union of overlapping conditions, not one hard question.
- **Domain 1, conceptual / definitional.** Actors: researchers, operational criteria, terms such as replicator, metabolism, autopoiesis, Darwinian evolution. Currency: usefulness and cost of stating. Anchor: none for the definition itself, because a declared meaning is not a grounded one; the seat authors conventions. Question type: partition boundary within a union.
- **Domain 2, empirical origin-of-life.** Actors: chemical systems, RNA, protocells, the early-Earth record, laboratory evolution. Currency: ground contact, which systems actually arose. Anchor: experiment and observation. Question type: union compound, settled per slot.
- **Domain 3, verification (this run).** Actors: this agent, the figures, the world sources. Currency: agreement and residual. Anchor: the figures as metalaws. In this simulated run all three domains draw on one evidence pool, so independence is zero.
- **Order by dependency.** Fix the question, name its anchor, author the convention if needed, then range it against the ground. Fig 3 :20 says the chain is ordered by dependency, not by importance.

## VERDICT (from the figures)

The figures answer yes, with a guard. Multiple scientifically reasonable definitions of life can coexist because the disagreement is a union of well-formed sub-questions (Fig 9 :4-5, :54-55), a definition is a convention that makes no claim about the world (Fig 10 :18, :46), and agreement about a shared meaning is the product of a convention, not corroboration (Fig 2 :41). The guard is that no definition may decide an empirical outcome (Fig 10 :47, Fig 2 :43). On empirical origin questions the anchor is experiment (Fig 2 :29-30, :45). A definition must still pay the MDL bargain (Fig 5 :43-44), and there is no last definition (Fig 7 :6, Fig 9 :32). Mode `general` on the shape, `specified` on the practice predictions, not `grounded`. The competing definitions are a functional group rather than Fig 12 isomers, and that is the weaker, defensible reading.

- **cost if wrong:** bounded and locatable. The packet states its mode, writes its refuters before the conclusion and records the lineage, so an error is findable. The larger cost is using a convention to settle an empirical outcome, which Fig 2 :42 prices as "Buy independence in proportion to the cost of being wrong." That cost is avoidable by keeping the guard in front of the definitions.
- **undoable:** yes. The shape can be narrowed without withdrawing the derivation. If a refuter fires, the affected slot is closed and the four-slot template survives. A published claim can be superseded by a corrected packet, and no primitive has to be removed.
- **reversed by:** a ground reading, not agreement. A historical study showing the definitional disagreement shrinking to one all-subset definition would break the split verdict. A demonstration that a definition makes a world-claim the ground settles true or false, or that a definition itself functions as an anchor for an origin question, would break the convention reading. A demonstration that mandating one definition increases rather than destroys the union surplus would break Fig 8's contribution. Until one of these fires, the verdict stands as `candidate`.
