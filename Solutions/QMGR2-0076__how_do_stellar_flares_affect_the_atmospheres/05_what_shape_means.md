## 1. A shape is a constraint, not a filler

The canonical README states the limit: the framework returns *"what a missing thing would have to look like (a shape, not a name)."* Fig 9 :32: *"you get a kind of thing, never the answer itself."* Fig 13 :134: *"Unreachable is a verdict here, not a silence... It cannot say which primitive is missing, because naming it would mean already holding it."*

From *The Library of Babel*: *"You can cut your search short if the set of books returned is small and descriptive enough that it provides a general shape of the answer that satisfies your question."*

So a **shape** is the narrowed form of an answer you can state before you can name the mechanism. It constrains; it does not fill. For this question the shape is: a union of per-slot residuals, with dependency order, with valence relative to the slot, with a threshold rather than a sum, each slot requiring its own anchor. The filler would be "flares strip atmospheres, so red-dwarf planets are uninhabitable", which asserts a physical verdict the figures do not supply.

## 2. How a shape is obtained

- *Library of Babel*, on proximity: *"use the existing vocabulary you do know that is closest to the words that you wish to describe and grasp the shape of."* Then *"With each new term gained, you narrow the space by definition and deepen your vocabulary."*
- *Library of Babel*, on a malformed bundle: *"Perhaps your problem was a bundle or conflation of several problems masquerading as one? You will need to search all of those problems individually."* This is the exact instruction behind F1 in `01_figure_grounded_audit.md`. The flare-habitability question is such a bundle.
- Fig 11 :44: *"List everything in contact with the residual, then what is in contact with those, and outward until the cascade stops mattering."* The six slots were enumerated from contact, not composed.
- Fig 6 :30, on effect terms: *"Cast it in effect terms."* Fig 9 :30-31: *"your words are borrowed too, so describe the effect rather than the cause."*
- *Decoding Tacit Knowledge* (recorded excerpt): *"When contact isn't possible, the best you can send is a plain description of what you'd expect to see, because anyone can check that against the world."* Hence the outreach email asks what a recipient would expect to see in each slot.
- *Library of Babel* on stopping: *"you will know that you are done because the finish line will also be the starting place"*; the paradox is that by then you have *"composed the answer through the process of locating it."*

## 3. What licenses a name

Only two things, both outside the shape itself:

1. **The ground settles it.** Formula step 5: *"the ground settles it. Verified only by what pays."* The README: the framework *"stops at the limit of what the available instruments can distinguish, and records the lineage so the stop is honest."* For this question, an instrument that measures the coupled flare-to-surface-dose chain would name the dominant mechanism.
2. **A provenanced import from a frame whose closure differs** (Fig 6 :23, Fig 8 :50-52), with a source gate and a shadow test. The photochemistry and hydrodynamic model families are such frames, but the record of where each came from must be kept.

*The Hard Problem is About Source* gives the mechanism. Metamerism: an instrument reads green and *"cannot comment on the source"*, because one green light, or yellow plus blue, or RGB, or CMYK, or a scheme not yet discovered can all produce the same reading. *"If several paths create the same behavior, then behavior is a projection with lossy information."* *"An instrument that can't distinguish these cases demonstrates its own inadequacy."* It was the spectrometer, not an argument, that resolved it.

Applied here: a measured 94% ozone depletion is a reading. Naming its source as protons alone is a metamerism claim if other paths (UV photolysis, differing spectra, differing atmospheric composition) can produce a similar reading. GJ 273's *"right balance of flaring"* is the honest form: the source is a threshold relation among several paths, not one named cause. *They're Scanning Books* sharpens it (recorded excerpt): *"The real import is not a new term in the existing space. It is a new dimension of the space."*

**Correction to my own section 3 (kept visible).** The phrase *"shape, not a name"* governs a missing primitive, one not held in the library. For this question there is no candidate missing primitive at all. The six-term substrate already holds energy, gradient and flow, and the slot structure comes from Fig 11. Naming ion escape or ozone depletion as a physical mechanism is a binding from the astrophysics frame onto an ordinary term, not the invention of a seventh term and not an import that moves the wall (Fig 6: *"IMPORT adds an atom"*; it adds none). The metamerism point stands, but for a different reason: the missing discriminator, not a ban on naming mechanisms. Both readings are recorded so neither is quietly lost.

## 4. Where the ground and the source problem meet

The ground for this question is not agreement among models. Fig 2 :29: *"An anchor is whatever settles the question and does not change its answer because of what any frame thinks."* NASA's 2017 model, Segura's 1D photochemistry, Tilley's repeated-flare extension and Caldiroli's ATES hydrodynamics are four frames. They do not negotiate with a ground; they range against it separately. Tilley extends Segura's model, so their agreement is one pool (Fig 2 :40). GJ 273's threshold claim and Yamashiki's dose calculation come from different instruments, so they are lower-overlap frames, but even they need a measured column, escape rate or dose to decide.

## 5. Answer to the shape question

**What licenses a particular mechanism in a slot?** Only the ground settling a measured effect, or a provenanced import with a shadow test. Until one of those, the mechanism is the searcher's contribution and must be recorded as such.

**Does the question as commonly posed commit an error the README forbids?** Yes. *"How do stellar flares affect atmospheres and habitability?"* asks for one answer to a bundle. *Library of Babel* says to search each problem individually and combine afterwards. Fig 9 :57 says a case in two conditions is not a harder case of either but a specific compound. The correct form is six slot-specific questions with a dependency order, one shared shape, and no scalar verdict.

## 6. Consequences for contact

**Prestige is not action.** From *The Recursive Self-Improvement Trap*: *"Prestige can fund an action. It can give an action a budget to be tried. But it cannot settle an action. Only the ground can settle it. The Ground Maintainer must be blind to prestige."* A campaign that gathers famous names to agree that flares are bad is the named collapse, not corroboration. The legitimate ask gives the claim a budget to be tried and lets an instrument settle it.

**My own audit is a simulation, not a ground reading.** Same essay: *"LLMs can quickly simulate an approximate answer/output with the formula found in the architecture post... However, the truly testable output would need to be derived from the real architecture or working the formula yourself."* LLM output is a simulacrum. So `01_figure_grounded_audit.md` is a simulated derivation, checked for internal consistency against the figures. It is not a test of the claim and must be labelled as such. It cannot settle anything it reports on.

**The framework is not a theory of everything.** *"Metatheories cannot validate themselves. This is Godel's insight, and it still holds here. You need an external validator."* Fig 7 :6 names *"the limit: no last room."* So this solution offers a shape generator with a written refuter, not a final answer about red-dwarf habitability.
