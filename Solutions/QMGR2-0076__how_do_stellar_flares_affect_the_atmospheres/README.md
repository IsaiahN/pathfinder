## What is deliberately not here

- No personal emails of named researchers.
- No ranking of people by how likely they are to agree with the claim.
- No bulk-send plan.

Three reasons, in the figures' own words:

1. **A room does not upgrade the label.** *"A room's agreement is variation, not a verdict."* (Fig 7 :35) Gathering more names to agree that flares are bad for habitability adds vocabulary, not an anchor (Fig 2 :29).
2. **Selecting for agreement manufactures the disqualifying correlation.** *"Independence, between two frames, is how little they draw on the same evidence."* (Fig 2 :31) *"agreement about the world is worthless when the agreeing frames share an evidence pool"* (Fig 2 :40).
3. **Asking people to accept the framework before they check the output spends their independence.** *"A verifier that can reconstruct the claim cannot verify it."* (Fig 10 :42) *"Bring a characterised residual, never the source."* (Fig 10 :52)

Legally: scraping personal emails for unsolicited bulk outreach breaches GDPR and CAN-SPAM style rules in many jurisdictions. Public institutional submission routes and opt-in channels avoid that failure as well as the structural one.
