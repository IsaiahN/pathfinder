## QUESTION

Option A: answer with a list of persuasive arguments and historical conversions, on the view that enough evidence or rhetoric eventually wins. Option B: keep the four-feature shape derived from the figures and refuse the list. ## DOMAIN CENSUS

R by arity, symmetry and scale, then one line per domain with actors / currency / anchor, partition or union, and order by dependency.

- **R (the residual).** Two or more committed frames hold theories with accrued residuals and the disagreement persists under more of the same argument. Arity: at least three positions, two frames plus one anchor (Fig 2 :13-27). Symmetry: the frames are symmetric in that neither can score itself (Fig 1 :27). Scale: three scales carry the effect, individual, community and generational, which are three domains and not one question at three resolutions.
- **Domain 1, the individual expert frame.** Actors: the expert, held theory terms, the sought-for shape held as a preference. Currency: residual per term, settlement, refusal. Anchor: the ground via the channel. Question type: partition, a term is settled, refused, or still accumulating.
- **Domain 2, the community of peers.** Actors: contending schools, reviewers, journals, committees. Currency: agreement, prestige, standing. Anchor: the ground when working; agreement when failing. Question type: union of evidence, social and institutional conditions (Fig 9 :55), so unbundle before searching.
- **Domain 3, the ground and its channel.** Actors: experiment, measurement, instrument, dataset. Currency: a reading that does not move when pushed. Anchor: the ground itself; the channel decays, the ground does not. Question type: partition, contact present or lost.
- **Domain 4, the historical or generational chain.** Actors: successive cohorts, rooms, habitats. Currency: residuals handed along, selection between rooms. Anchor: the ground across time, via rooms that differ. Question type: chain, not partition (Fig 7 :4-6).
- **Order by dependency.** Perceive and describe the residual in effect terms before setting an objective (Fig 3 :19); refuse before mint (Formula step 2); settle before composing (Formula step 5); then select between rooms (Fig 7 :35).

Strain test applied: Kuhnian incommensurability (world source W2) conflicts with the ground-decides verdict if read at Domain 1. Re-census places it at Domain 4, where the ground vote is between rooms rather than a neutral algorithm of choice. Different scale, so no reversal; the strain is live and recorded (01 F8, 11_audit.md).

## VERDICT (from the figures)

The shape survives with four features kept separate: a reading at an anchor that does not update; a refusal of a term with a persistent, non-trending residual; an import from a closure that differs, described first in effect terms; and at community scale, selection between rooms rather than agreement within one. Internal argument, consensus within a shared evidence pool, prestige and a room's agreement are not mind changers at any domain. The three historical cases are retrodictions that fit the shape, not results it produced. This is a `general` and partly `specified`, ungrounded, candidate result. The four domain verdicts are never averaged.

- **cost if wrong:** bounded and locatable. The packet states mode, sub-claims and refuters before the conclusion, so an error is findable. The real cost is spending the independence of outside checkers by sending a list of conversions instead of a shape and a refuter. Fig 2 :42 prices that: *"Buy independence in proportion to the cost of being wrong."* It is avoidable by leading with the five falsifiers.
- **undoable:** yes. The three historical bindings are removable without touching the shape; `07_the_tether_answer.md` and `08_expert_verification_packet.md` already remove any claim that they are derivations. A sent claim can be superseded by a corrected packet, and no primitive has to be withdrawn.
- **reversed by:** a ground reading, not agreement. A registered before/after study with independent coders showing that a change occurred through internal argument alone (features 1 and 2), that an instrument with no prior failing reading settled it (feature 3), that a shared-pool community reached a confirmed novel conclusion by agreement (feature 4), that an anchor moved when pushed, or that a whole cohort changed without turnover or new instrument, would reverse the corresponding feature. A single lever closing all four conditions would reverse the union verdict in `06_isomers_and_order.md`. Until one of these fires, the verdict stands as `candidate`.
