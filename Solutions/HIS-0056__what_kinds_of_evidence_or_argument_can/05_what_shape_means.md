## 1. A shape is a constraint, not a filler

The figures README states the limit: the framework returns *"what a missing thing would have to look like (a shape, not a name)."* Fig 9 :32: *"you get a kind of thing, never the answer itself."* Fig 13 :133: *"what it returns is a shape rather than a name."*

From The Library of Babel (essay, 2026-10-02): *"you can cut your search short if the set of books returned is small and descriptive enough that it provides a general shape of the answer that satisfies your question."*

So a **shape** is the narrowed form of an answer you can state before you can name it. It constrains; it does not fill. For HIS-0056 the shape is not "the experiment that changed Einstein's mind" or "the funeral that changed geology". It is the form an intervention must have: an unmovable anchor, a refusal of a persistent-and-flat term, an import from a differing closure, and selection between rooms at community scale. A list of famous conversions is a name-like collection of instances, not a shape.

**Falsifier for section 1:** a proposed shape that cannot be used to rule out any candidate answer. If it accepts every historical episode equally, it is filler.

## 2. How a shape is obtained

- The Library of Babel method: use the vocabulary you already have that is closest to the thing, then look for recurring new terms that overlap or correlate with it. The essay: *"With each new term gained, you narrow the space by definition and deepen your vocabulary. It's this recursive process that will help you converge on the answer."*
- Fig 6 :30: *"The description travels. Fails by being sharpened into terms only we can read. Cast it in effect terms."* Fig 9 :30-31: *"your words are borrowed too, so describe the effect rather than the cause."*
- The Library of Babel on steering: *"The books that you are returning in your searches need to be tested or ranged against the problem you want answered in order to steer the direction of the next search."* That is the anchor function.
- The Library of Babel on stopping: *"You will know that you are done because the finish line will also be the starting place, as there is nowhere else to search and nothing else that can describe the answer better."*

Applied here: the effect terms are "does not update when pushed", "refuses a held term", "comes from a closure that differs", "selects between rooms". Those are checkable without knowing which historical case will be cited. The figure vocabularies (frame, closure, mint, import) are used only after the effect is stated.

## 3. What licenses a name

Only two things, both outside the shape itself:

1. **The ground settles it.** Formula step 5: *"the ground settles it. Verified only by what pays. A gate is not the ground."* The README: the framework *"stops at the limit of what the available instruments can distinguish, and records the lineage so the stop is honest."*
2. **A provenanced import from a frame whose closure differs** (Fig 6 :23, Fig 8 :45-52), with a source gate and a shadow test, debited against independence.

The Hard Problem is About Source essay (2026-08-28) gives the mechanism for why a reading does not by itself license a name: an instrument that reads green *"cannot comment on the source."* It records several possible compositions of the same reading and then states: *"It was proven wrong not by argument, but by the arrival of a better instrument."*

For this question the corresponding caution is sharp. A historian who reads "the community accepted the theory" cannot name the source of the change from that reading alone: it may be a new instrument, a refusal by one term, an import from another field, or cohort turnover. What is owed is a discriminator (independent coders and a before/after reading), not a confident name.

## 4. Why a preference has to become an ordinary term

Fig 5 :65: *"make the sought-for shape an ORDINARY TERM."* The Formula's Direction section: *"a frame searching for an answer of a given SHAPE carries that shape as a PREFERENCE, not a term. Unbound, predicting nothing, no residual - so nothing above can see it."*

For HIS-0056 the preference is "I want an account in which evidence, not sociology, changes minds". Left unbound, it would steer the search toward cases where a measurement happened and away from cases where a cohort simply turned over. Binding it as an ordinary term means writing down the four features and the five refuters in `01_figure_grounded_audit.md` section 4 before looking at any case. Then the preference can itself be refused.

## 5. Prestige is not action

The Recursive Self-Improvement Trap essay (2026-11-19): *"Prestige must never be allowed to validate an action directly."* And: *"Prestige can fund an action. It can give an action a budget to be tried. But it cannot settle an action. Only the ground can settle it."*

In a history-of-science question the temptation is to cite famous converts as if the fame of the converter were the evidence. That is exactly the named collapse. A Nobel laureate's change of mind is a fact about a person; it is not an anchor for a claim about what changes minds. The same essay: *"You may all agree and be wrong. You may set your clock to someone else's, who was setting your clock to yours, and be off by many deviations over time. You need ground truth to define where you stand."*

## 6. My own audit is a simulation, not a ground reading

Same essay: *"LLMs can quickly simulate an approximate answer/output with the formula found in the architecture post. It will often be better than the generic outputs that you will usually get otherwise too... However, the truly testable output would need to be derived from the real architecture or working the formula yourself."*

So `01_figure_grounded_audit.md` is a simulated derivation, checked for internal consistency against the figures. It is **not** a test of the historical claim and must be labelled as such. It cannot settle anything it reports on. How Intelligence Actually Works adds the boundary condition: *"This process isn't about reaching the truth. It stops when nothing you have registers a difference from it. That's operational, and it's the only version that doesn't require knowing the answer in advance."* And the closing caveat: *"indiscernibility is not identity."*

## 7. The answer to "what would a name require here?"

There are two positions in the How Intelligence Actually Works essay that are worth keeping together, because a one-sided reading of either would be wrong:

- On provenance: a frame of video presented as a photograph is indistinguishable from the artifact alone, and *"nothing in the image records which."* Traces, when they exist, can be recovered by depth and instruments (the diamond/isotope case).
- On instruments: the union horizon *"is always moving because instruments improve, and better properties get discovered, so the horizon is a statement about the current state."* Being below it *"is never a settled verdict, only a report that nobody has reached it yet."*

For this question the honest stop is: the shape is current, the historical cases are retrodictions, and a ground study with independent coders could move the result. Fig 13 :134: *"Unreachable is a verdict here, not a silence."* That verdict is recorded in `07_the_tether_answer.md` section 5 and is exactly what the outreach packet asks a checker to test.
