## Independent domain census

I redid the census using the WATSON order: R by arity, symmetry and scale; one line per domain with actors, currency and anchor; partition or union; order by dependency.

- **R (arity / symmetry / scale).** The residual is a persistent disagreement between committed experts. Arity: at least three, because a two-frame disagreement has no way to settle itself without a third position that does not update (Fig 2 :13-27). Symmetry: the frames are symmetric in the only relevant respect, each produces the quantities it would use to score itself (Fig 1 :27). Scale: the effect appears in an individual, a committee and a sequence of cohorts. I treated the three as separate domains rather than three views of one domain, because the actors and currency differ and the figure that governs each is different.
- **Domain 1, the individual frame.** Actors: expert, held terms, unbound sought-for shape. Currency: residual per term, refusal. Anchor: the ground via a reading. Partition: a term is refused or it is not.
- **Domain 2, the community.** Actors: schools, reviewers, committees. Currency: agreement, prestige, standing. Anchor: the ground when the channel works, agreement itself when it fails. Union: evidence plus social plus institutional conditions, so unbundle before searching.
- **Domain 3, the ground and channel.** Actors: experiment, instrument, dataset. Currency: a reading that does not move when pushed. Anchor: the ground; the channel decays. Partition: contact present or lost.
- **Domain 4, the generational chain.** Actors: cohorts and rooms. Currency: residuals handed along, selection between rooms. Anchor: the ground across rooms. Chain, not partition.

Order by dependency: describe the residual in effect terms before setting an objective (Fig 9 :27-28); refuse before mint (Formula step 2); settle before composing (Formula step 5); select rooms only after a residual has been surfaced (Fig 7 :36).

**My census matches the primary's four domains and its union reading.** Where the primary derived the domains from the figure census (Fig 1, Fig 2, Fig 7, Fig 9), I derived them from the residual's arity and scale first, then checked which figure governs each.
