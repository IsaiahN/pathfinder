## What is deliberately not here

- No personal emails of named individuals.
- No ranking of people by how likely they are to agree.
- No bulk-send plan.

Three reasons, in the figures' own words:

1. **Agreement is not an anchor.** Fig 2 :35: *"Alignment is triangulation, not negotiation. A mirror cannot correct you."* Selecting recipients for receptiveness manufactures the very correlation that makes their agreement worthless.
2. **A shared pool is one witness.** Fig 2 :40: *"agreement about the world is worthless when the agreeing frames share an evidence pool."* An ensemble of models all trained on the same short record is one frame wearing many names (Fig 8 :48-49).
3. **A verifier must not hold the interiors.** Fig 10 :42: *"A verifier that can reconstruct the claim cannot verify it."* The first contact carries the residual and its refuter, not the framework.

Legally: unsolicited bulk email to scraped addresses breaches GDPR and CAN-SPAM style rules in many jurisdictions and gets the sending domain blacklisted. That is a practical failure on top of the structural one. Public opt-in and submission venues avoid both.

## Honest standing of this whole package

Every derivation here is a **simulated derivation checked for internal consistency against the figures, not a ground reading** (*The Recursive Self-Improvement Trap*: LLM output is "simulacra" and "the truly testable output would need to be derived from the real architecture or working the formula yourself"). It cannot settle the question it reports on. No measurement of a future rare extreme was taken.
