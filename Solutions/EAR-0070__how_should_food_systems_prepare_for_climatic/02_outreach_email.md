# Outreach: check the output first, then decide about the framework

**Use:** one-to-one, or as a cover letter attached to a submission through the public venues in `03_public_contact_points.csv`. **Not** for bulk send.

**Hard precondition:** do not send until the shape claim and its refuter fit on one screen and the sender has recorded the checker's stage-1 verdict before showing any framework material. If the output cannot survive on its own, the two-stage offer collapses on contact.

---

**Subject:** One checkable claim about preparing food systems for extremes the record cannot estimate

> Hi [name],
>
> I work on food-system adaptation under deep uncertainty. I have one specific output I would like
> you to check against what you already know. I am not asking you to read a theory first, and I am
> not asking you to agree with me. If the output does not survive, there is nothing else to look at.
>
> **The output (`specified`, not `grounded`):** historical records cannot supply the frequency of
> an extreme that is both rarer than the record and drawn from a climate that has changed. So a
> preparation plan cannot be calibrated on a historical return period. What survives is a shape:
> the plan must be built per component, not per aggregate; it must enumerate the food system's
> contacts outward through the cascade (production, processing, labour, transport, storage, trade,
> access); it must import hazard knowledge from frames that do not share the record's evidence
> pool; and it must sequence actions with signposts and thresholds, keeping cuts reversible. This
> is a constraint on the form of an answer, not a measurement and not a named technology.
>
> **What I am not claiming:** that any particular seed, irrigation scheme, stockpile or model is
> the answer. The framework returns a shape, not a name. Such bindings come from another frame and
> carry no evidentiary weight until a ground reading settles them. I am also not claiming that a
> stress test proves real preparedness; it proves the wiring of the model, not the capability of
> the food system.
>
> **What would refute the shape claim, written down before this message:**
> 1. a procedure that derives an unobserved rare-extreme return level from the record alone, with
>    bounded error under a verified non-stationary hazard;
> 2. evidence that the historical record and the future hazard share one closure, so the problem
>    is only one of sample size;
> 3. an aggregate food-security indicator that fires before every component slot, consistently
>    across regions and hazards;
> 4. an agent-only improvement that changes the ground outcome while the habitat's contact
>    structure is unchanged and measured.
>
> If any holds, I will withdraw or rewrite the claim. A one-line "this fails because..." is more
> useful to me than agreement. If you would rather not engage, no reply is needed.
>
> **Before you answer:** what would you expect to see if this shape claim were true, and what would
> you count as a failure? I am recording that answer before offering anything else.
>
> **If it survives, and only then:** the sources are below. The figures are the authority, not my
> summary of them.
> - Canonical figures text with line numbers (local extract): `FRAMEWORK/reference/03-FIGURES_TEXT.md`
> - The eight-step loop and the mode rule (local): `FRAMEWORK/figures/THE_FORMULA.md`
> - Patel and Schneider 2026, "How short climate records affect GEV-based estimates of rare event probabilities": https://iopscience.iop.org/article/10.1088/2752-5295/ae71fb
>
> **If you want to reuse the machinery:** the formula is in the local file above. Run it on a
> problem you know well and can check against a ground. Write down what would refute your result
> *before* you run it, and state the mode of what you get. A reported failure is more useful to me
> than a reported success.
>
> Best,
> [name]

## Stage discipline

1. **Stage 1:** residual, shape claim and four refuters. Record the checker's verdict *before* showing the links.
2. **Stage 2, opt-in only:** the three sources, with the note that the figures, not the summary, are the authority.
3. **Stage 3, opt-in only:** the formula as a reusable method, with the instruction that each user writes their own refuter and states their own mode.

A stage-2 agreement is never pooled as corroboration (Fig 2 :40). A refusal is a useful result and is copied into `11_audit.md` with provenance if it names a failure class.
