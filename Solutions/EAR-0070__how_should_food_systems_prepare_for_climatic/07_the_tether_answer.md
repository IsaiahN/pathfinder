# The Tether's answer: preparing food systems for inestimable extremes

**Mode: `specified` on the shape and route, `general` on the closure claim, not `grounded`.** Simulated derivation checked for internal consistency against the figures (`05_what_shape_means.md` §6), not a ground reading. It does not settle the question it reports on.

## 1. The residual it answers

A food system must commit actions now, but the quantity that would size those actions, the frequency and magnitude of extremes under a changed climate, cannot be estimated from the historical record. The question bundles two conditions: the record is short, and the record belongs to a different regime. Fig 9 :57: *"a case in two conditions is not a harder case of either."* The compound has its own loop.

## 2. The shape

1. **The verdict comes first.** The needed atom is not in the record closure. Fig 8 :32: *"no search, however long, adds an atom."* Fig 8 :39: *"a search that finds nothing is never a proof of absence."* Absence from a record is not evidence of absence from the future. So a plan cannot be calibrated on a historical return period as if that number were the ground.

2. **Import hazard knowledge from a closure that differs.** Fig 6 :23: *"No frame escapes its own closure. The bound moves only on contact with a frame whose closure differs."* Climate physics and physical bounds, paleoclimate proxies and long control runs are such frames, provided they carry provenance and a shadow test (does the import explain a gap the record cannot?). They are instruments already returning something (Fig 6 :33: *"An instrument is not built from a description. It is improved from a worse instrument already returning something."*)

3. **Enumerate the habitat, per slot, outward through the cascade.** Fig 11 :43: *"The habitat is enumerated, never composed."* Fig 11 :44: *"List everything in contact with the residual, then what is in contact with those, and outward until the cascade stops mattering."* The figure's own examples are agricultural (light, water, temperature, season, terrain; pollination, predation, competition, dependency). Fig 11 :52: *"Scoping the habitat to direct contact is how DDT reached the eagles: first-order enumeration, second-order effect, and the residual that mattered was three steps out."* Measure residual per component: Fig 1 :19, *"The residual is indexed by object slot, not aggregated."* and Fig 1 :22, *"Aggregating across slots is how a live signal disappears into an average."*

4. **Change contact, not just the agent.** Fig 11 :40: *"An improvement that does not change contact changes nothing, however much it improves."* Resilience is a property of the system and its habitat (Fig 11 :39: *"capability is a property of agent-and-habitat, never of the agent alone"*). Diversity, redundancy, soil water holding, independent supply routes and functional fallback are changes in the contact graph; a better forecast on its own is not.

5. **Sequence with thresholds, keep cuts reversible, and treat it as permanent work.** Fig 12 :112: *"A plan is a sequence. A goal is a comparison. Progress is a subtraction."* The Operators table: *"a goal is a comparison, which is why a vocabulary without it cannot state one."* So a plan needs an ordered action chain, an adaptation tipping point at which the current action fails, and a progress measure. Fig 9 :41: *"so keep every cut ranked and reversible."* Fig 11 :63: *"a managed ecosystem is permanent work rather than a solved state."* Fig 11 :61: *"The seat's power is asymmetric. Introduction is available; exclusion is not."* Introduction without enumeration is maladaptation: *"Cane toads and kudzu are the same move with the cascade unenumerated"* (Fig 11 :65).

6. **Buy independence, never pool a single pool.** Fig 2 :40: *"agreement about the world is worthless when the agreeing frames share an evidence pool."* Fig 2 :42: *"Buy independence in proportion to the cost of being wrong."* Food-security errors are costly, so pay for frames that do not share the record. A twenty-model ensemble on one dataset is one witness.

7. **A stress test is a filter, not a witness.** Fig 11 :70: *"A test harness is a substituted habitat. Which is why a synthetic solve proves wiring and never capability."* Fig 9 :60: *"Use filters for the budget and witnesses for the verdict. Never let a filter hand you a verdict."*

8. **Write the refuter before the plan.** Fig 5 :65-66: *"make the sought-for shape an ORDINARY TERM. Write down what an answer would look like, bind it, and let it accrue a residual like anything else."* The four refuters in `08` are part of the output, not an appendix.

## 3. What it explicitly does not say

- Not a number. No return period, no probability of a 1-in-N event, no confidence interval is derived here.
- Not a name. No crop, technology, policy or institution is asserted as the answer. Such bindings carry zero evidentiary weight until the ground settles them, and are recorded as provenanced bindings, not derivations.
- Not a claim that a model ensemble is multiple independent checks, and not a claim that a stress test proves preparedness.
- Not an endorsement of a "resilience" label as an answer. Fig 11 :36 says the word "intelligence" is *"the wrong question"* in the unmaintained case; the same suspicion applies to naming a property instead of describing the contact that produces it.
- Not grounded. No future extreme has been observed and measured against a plan in this derivation.

## 4. What would settle it, the ground

A ground reading, not more agreement. The relevant instruments are:

- an arrival: the actual extreme, with the food system's response measured per slot (availability, access, utilisation, stability), not only as a national average;
- a source-discriminating instrument. *The Hard Problem is About Source*: an instrument that reads green *"cannot comment on the source"*, and the matter was settled *"not by argument, but by the arrival of a better instrument."* Here the analogue is an instrument that separates a tail effect from a trend effect from a supply-chain coupling in the observed failure;
- a refuter firing: a procedure that derives the unobserved tail from the record alone under a verified regime change would break the central claim (Fig 8 :32, Fig 2 :40).

Standing refuters, repeated from `01` and `08`: (1) the tail is derivable from the record alone with bounded error under non-stationarity; (2) record and future hazard share one closure; (3) an aggregate indicator fires before every component slot; (4) an agent-only improvement changes the ground outcome with contact structure unchanged and measured; (5) a synthetic test is sufficient for real capability.

## 5. The honest stop

**Unreached, not unreachable.** The plan stops at the limit of the instruments currently returning something. Some regions may have no paleo-proxy, no long control run and no practitioner record for the hazard that matters; there, the figure's rule is that a search for a proto-instrument is a search for one that already exists elsewhere (Fig 6 :34-35), and if none exists, that is a gap for an instrument builder. Fig 8 :39: *"a search that finds nothing is never a proof of absence."* The lineage is recorded so the stop is checkable. *The Recursive Self-Improvement Trap*: *"the output is only as good as the validator. The agent, or the tool, or the frame, or the theory, cannot validate itself."*

## 6. One-paragraph version for outreach

> The Tether does not tell a food system what crop to plant or what return period to design to. It says that the historical record cannot supply a quantity it does not contain, so a plan calibrated on a historical extreme is calibrated on the wrong thing. What survives is a shape: import hazard knowledge from frames that do not share the record's evidence pool; enumerate the food system's contacts outward through the cascade and measure per slot, because a global average hides the one failing region; change the system's contact with the hazard, not just its internal efficiency; sequence actions with thresholds and signposts, keep cuts reversible, and treat the work as permanent; and buy genuinely independent checks in proportion to the cost of being wrong. A stress test locates failure but does not prove capability. The figures return a shape, not a name, and they carry four refuters written in advance. The center stays open until an extreme actually arrives and the ground settles it.
