## Wider literature that bears on the question

These were read through the same pages above or through indexed abstracts returned this session; each is recorded in `10_source_ledger.csv` with the exact status. They corroborate, they do not decide.

- **Statistics of extremes.** Patel and Schneider 2026 is the cleanest witness for the decisive figure line Fig 8 :32: no search, however long, adds an atom. Their result is not that extreme value theory is useless; it is that the estimate's reliability depends on record length and on modelling non-stationarity. That is exactly the difference between *unreachable at this budget* and *underivable in this closure* (Fig 8 :40-43).
- **Adaptation practice.** DAPP supplies the operational vocabulary for Fig 12's bonds: a sequence of actions (→), an adaptation tipping point at which the current action fails (⋛, Operators :52: *"a goal is a comparison, which is why a vocabulary without it cannot state one"*), and signposts that say when to shift. Its "sell-by year" is a threshold, not a forecast.
- **Human judgment.** The availability heuristic explains why institutions under-weight the events that are absent from memory: the unavailability of information is itself taken as evidence of low probability. That is Fig 8 :39 in cognitive language: *"a search that finds nothing is never a proof of absence."*
- **Food-system structure.** Burkitt et al. 2026 and the IPCC AR6 SYR SPM supply the cascade enumeration that Fig 11 demands, including the second- and third-order effects DDT reached the eagles through (Fig 11 :52). Both also warn that single-sector interventions leave systemic risk standing.
- **Limits of the model.** Fig 11 :70: *"A test harness is a substituted habitat. Which is why a synthetic solve proves wiring and never capability."* Scenario analysis and stress tests locate failure; they do not prove preparedness. This is a filter for the budget, never a witness for the verdict (Fig 9 :60).

## Distribution: offer, do not push

Figure-grounded reasons:

- **Fig 10 :52** - *"Bring a characterised residual, never the source."* Sending the full framework before a checker has described a residual sends a recording, not a generator (Fig 4 :25). Lead with the shape claim and its four refuters.
- **Fig 9 :27-28** - *"describe it before you go looking, or any frame you pick will seem to fit."* Ask what they would expect to see if the claim were true before giving them any source.
- **Fig 2 :35 and :40** - agreement is triangulation, not negotiation, and a shared evidence pool makes agreement worthless. A list selected for receptiveness buys correlation, not corroboration.
- **Fig 2 :42** - *"Buy independence in proportion to the cost of being wrong."* Food-security errors are costly, so the right move is a small number of genuinely independent checkers, not a large list of likely agreeers.
- **Fig 4 :22** - *"Only methods cross up. Only head starts come down."* Stage 3 sends a method, which can be given away without giving away an answer.

**Recommended order in a reply:** (1) the shape claim and the four refuters; (2) the question "what would you count as a failure?"; (3) record that answer; (4) only then the three links, with the note that the figures, not this summary, are the authority; (5) optionally, the formula as a method, with each user writing their own refuter and stating their own mode.

## The two-stage offer, stated plainly

1. **Stage 1.** Validate the output against the ground. The checker sees only the residual, the shape claim and the pre-registered refuters. Their verdict is recorded before any theory is shown.
2. **Stage 2, opt-in only.** If curious, they receive the sources. Later agreement is never pooled as corroboration (Fig 2 :40).
3. **Stage 3, opt-in only.** The formula is offered as a reusable method, with the instruction that each user state their own ground and write their own refuter first.

**Hard precondition.** Stages 1 to 3 work only if the stage-1 output survives. The four refuters in `08_expert_verification_packet.md` are the test. If one fires, the packet is withdrawn or rewritten before it is sent.
