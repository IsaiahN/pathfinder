# Expert verification packet - EAR-0070

**What this is:** a claim in a form a food-system researcher or adaptation practitioner can check. Mode, derivation and refuters are stated before the conclusion, so a reader knows what to look for and what would settle it.

**Mode: `specified` on the shape and route, `general` on the closure claim, not `grounded`.** No future extreme has been measured against a plan here. This is a shape and a checking procedure, not a result. Simulated derivation, not a ground reading.

## 1. The claim, in one paragraph

Historical records cannot supply the frequency of an extreme that is both rarer than the record and drawn from a changed climate, so a preparation plan cannot be calibrated on a historical return period. What survives is a shape: the plan is built per component rather than per aggregate; it enumerates the food system's contacts outward through the cascade; it imports hazard knowledge from frames that do not share the record's evidence pool; it changes the system's contact with the hazard; and it sequences actions with adaptation thresholds and signposts while keeping cuts reversible. A stress test locates failure but does not prove capability. The framework returns this shape, not a named crop, technology or policy.

## 2. Three checkable sub-claims

### C1 - the record closure cannot supply the tail; absence is not evidence of absence

- **Statement:** no quantity derivable from a short historical record under a changing climate can size a plan for an extreme absent from that record. Re-analysis of the same record does not help; a frame whose closure differs must be imported.
- **Source, verbatim:** Fig 8 :32 - *"no search, however long, adds an atom."* Fig 8 :39 - *"a search that finds nothing is never a proof of absence."* Fig 6 :23 - *"No frame escapes its own closure. The bound moves only on contact with a frame whose closure differs."* Fig 2 :29 - *"An anchor is whatever settles the question and does not change its answer because of what any frame thinks."*
- **What to check:** is the distinction between *unreachable at this budget* and *underivable in this closure* respected? Fig 8 :42 - *"the remedy is more search, not another atom"* applies to the former, not the latter. Non-stationarity makes this the latter. Patel and Schneider 2026 measured substantial underestimation from short series and larger bias from ignoring trends.
- **Refuter:** a procedure that derives an unobserved rare-extreme return level from the record alone, with bounded error under a verified non-stationary hazard; or a demonstration that record and future hazard share one closure so the problem is only sample size.

### C2 - the unit of preparation is the slot and the habitat, not the aggregate

- **Statement:** measure and act per component (crop, region, supply node, season), enumerate contacts outward through the cascade, and require interventions to change contact rather than only the agent.
- **Source, verbatim:** Fig 1 :19 - *"The residual is indexed by object slot, not aggregated."* Fig 1 :22 - *"Aggregating across slots is how a live signal disappears into an average."* Fig 11 :43 - *"The habitat is enumerated, never composed."* Fig 11 :52 - *"Scoping the habitat to direct contact is how DDT reached the eagles: first-order enumeration, second-order effect, and the residual that mattered was three steps out."* Fig 11 :40 - *"An improvement that does not change contact changes nothing, however much it improves."*
- **What to check:** name a food-system failure that is fully captured by a national or global average, with no live component slot, across more than one hazard. If one exists, the per-slot requirement is too strong. Also check whether the proposed intervention actually changes a contact (water, route, pollination, redundancy) or only an internal metric.
- **Refuter:** an aggregate food-security indicator that fires before every component slot, consistently across regions and hazards; or an agent-only improvement that changes the ground outcome while the habitat's contact structure is unchanged and measured.

### C3 - the plan is a molecule: sequence, threshold, progress, refusal, and two currencies

- **Statement:** an ingredient list is not a plan. A plan needs an ordered chain (→), an adaptation tipping point (⋛, a comparison), a progress measure (−, a subtraction), and a pre-registered refusal (¬). Description cost in bits and action cost in actions are different units and cannot be summed. A stress test is a substituted habitat and proves wiring, not capability.
- **Source, verbatim:** Fig 12 :112 - *"A plan is a sequence. A goal is a comparison. Progress is a subtraction."* Fig 12 :125 - *"A composition costs in two currencies, and they do not add."* Fig 11 :70 - *"A test harness is a substituted habitat. Which is why a synthetic solve proves wiring and never capability."* Operators :52 - *"a goal is a comparison, which is why a vocabulary without it cannot state one."*
- **What to check:** take any current adaptation plan. Can you point to its threshold, its ordering constraint, its progress measure and its refuter? If two plans share ingredients but different ordering, do they behave differently under a hazard? DAPP's "adaptation tipping point" and "sell-by year" are the operational analogues.
- **Refuter:** two plans with identical ingredients but different bonds behave identically under multiple hazards; or a single currency prices description and action without hiding a shortfall; or a synthetic test is sufficient for real capability.

## 3. What is explicitly not claimed

- Not a return period, probability or confidence interval.
- Not a named crop, seed, irrigation scheme, stockpile, model or institution as the answer. Any such binding is from another frame, provenanced as such, and carries zero evidentiary weight until the ground settles it.
- Not that a multi-model ensemble gives independent confirmation when the models share a record.
- Not that a stress test or scenario exercise proves real-world preparedness.
- Not `grounded`. No future extreme was observed and measured in this derivation.
- Not a claim that this simulated derivation can settle the question it reports on.

## 5. What would settle it

A ground reading, not agreement.

1. An arrival: the extreme actually occurs, and the system's response is measured per slot across availability, access, utilisation and stability, not only as an average.
2. A source-discriminating instrument: one that separates a tail effect, a trend effect and a supply-chain coupling in the observed failure. *The Hard Problem is About Source*: a green-reading instrument *"cannot comment on the source"*, and the case was closed *"not by argument, but by the arrival of a better instrument."*
3. A refuter firing: any of the five in `01` §4 or `07` §4.

Prestige or volume of agreement is not such an instrument. From *The Recursive Self-Improvement Trap*: *"Prestige can fund an action. It can give an action a budget to be tried. But it cannot settle an action. Only the ground can settle it."*

## 6. What an expert can check in twenty minutes

1. Read Fig 8 :32 and :39 and Fig 6 :23, three short passages. Is the closure claim right, and is the budget-versus-closure distinction maintained?
2. Read Fig 1 :19 and Fig 11 :43 and :52. Try to name a food-system failure fully captured by an aggregate, with no live slot and no cascade. If you can, C2 is in trouble.
3. Read Fig 12 :112, :125 and Fig 11 :70. Take one real adaptation plan and locate its threshold, sequence, progress measure, refuter and two budgets. Note what is missing.
4. State one instrument that would discriminate tail from trend from coupling in an observed failure, or say why none is possible.
5. Send the refutation. A failure is more useful than an endorsement. Record the verdict before asking for any framework material.
