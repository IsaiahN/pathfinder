Conversely, one thing survives cleanly: a closed record cannot supply an absent tail, so the plan cannot be calibrated on a historical return period. That is the residue worth sending. `R_T` was **not** measured numerically, because no ground reading exists; it is recorded qualitatively as the enumerated set of dropped constraints. Treating "the formula is the condensed version of the figures" as identity would set `R_T` to zero and would be the dead-reckoning error Fig 4 :30 names.
