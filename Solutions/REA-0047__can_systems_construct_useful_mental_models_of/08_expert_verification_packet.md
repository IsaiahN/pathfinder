# Expert verification packet: the Tether on mental models of unencountered situations

**What this is:** a claim in a form a mental-models researcher can check. Mode, derivation and refuters are stated before the conclusion, so a reader knows what to look for and what would settle it.

**Mode: `specified` on the conditions, `general` on the structural shape, not `grounded`.** No measurement of a system's transfer to a novel ground is claimed here. This is a shape and a route.

**Status of this packet:** a simulated derivation checked for internal consistency against the figures, not a ground reading. It cannot settle the claim it reports on.

## 1. The claim in one paragraph

A system can construct a model of a situation it has never directly encountered if the gap that situation produces is describable in effect terms from contact the system already has, and the parts of the model are either held in its library or reached from a frame whose closure differs. That yields a candidate shape. It does not yield a useful model until the ground settles it. The framework therefore separates two quantities that are usually conflated: constructibility, priced inside a closure, and usefulness, settled by an instrument or outcome. Three conditions are necessary; none is sufficient alone. The refuters were written before the run.

## 2. Three checkable sub-claims

### C1 — framework-internal; checkable by reading Figures 5 and 1

- **Statement:** construction and usefulness are two different quantities. A constructible model is not useful until the ground pays.
- **Source, verbatim:** Fig 5 :70: "A term is settled when the ground pays for it. A term is usable when something can compose over it. Those are two conditions and this figure names one." Also Fig 5 :69: "settling is necessary for a term to be composed over, and it is not sufficient." And Fig 1 :23: "The ground enters as the only metric."
- **What to check:** is there any reading in which a model pays at the ground with no settlement event? If "useful" means only "locally guiding", say so and the sub-claim is restated, not confirmed.
- **Refuter:** a ground-paying model of an unencountered situation with no settlement event, construction alone sufficing. That breaks Fig 5 :69-70 and Fig 1 :23.

### C2 — framework-internal and transfer-facing; checkable by reading Figures 6, 8 and 9

- **Statement:** the bound moves on contact with a frame whose closure differs, not on direct contact with the target situation. The residual must be described in effect terms before a frame is chosen.
- **Source, verbatim:** Fig 6 :23: "No frame escapes its own closure. The bound moves only on contact with a frame whose closure differs." Fig 8 :54: "The atom cannot be derived from inside A. But the union is searchable from contact, so underivable is not unknowable." Fig 9 :27-28: "describe it before you go looking, or any frame you pick will seem to fit".
- **What to check:** does the claim match what analogy and structure-mapping research actually supports? A source frame can supply a relation for a target, but the spontaneous transfer literature is mixed. cite a case either way.
- **Refuter:** a model of X produced with no direct contact, no different-closure frame in its lineage, and no held atom predicting X. That breaks Fig 6 :23.

### C3 — science-facing; checkable against the mental-models and transfer literature

- **Statement:** the literature supports constructibility and transfer, but does not supply a ground test for a model of a never-encountered situation. Reports of transfer are often retrodictions on familiar grounds.
- **What to read, verbatim from sources opened 2026-11-23:** Wikipedia "Mental model": the mind constructs "small-scale models" used "to anticipate events"; models can be built from "perception, imagination, or the comprehension of discourse". Mental Models Global Laboratory: "people reason about the world by constructing small-scale models of possible situations". Wikipedia "Cognitive map": the cross-maze rat "was able to determine the correct way to the food no matter where in the maze it was placed", but Bennett argued there is "no clear evidence for cognitive maps in non-human animals", and rats "fail to use a shortcut" without preexposure. Ha and Schmidhuber, "World Models" (2018): "We can even train our agent entirely inside of its own hallucinated dream generated by its world model, and transfer this policy back into the actual environment." That is a same-environment transfer, not a novel-ground test.
- **What to check:** is there a study in your own reading where a model built without contact with the target ground pays on first contact with it, with no source frame and no relevant priors? If yes, this sub-claim needs narrowing.
- **Refuter:** a controlled study with zero relevant contact, no source frame, above-chance prediction on a genuinely novel ground, and independent replication. That would move C3 from `specified` toward `grounded` and would send the census back to domain D3.

## 3. What is explicitly not claimed

- Not that all systems can do this, or that any system can model any situation. Fig 6 :40 limits what has never registered at any resolution.
- Not that a synthetic solve proves capability. Fig 11 :70: "A test harness is a substituted habitat. Which is why a synthetic solve proves wiring and never capability."
- Not that agreement among experts settles the model. Fig 2 :35: "Alignment is triangulation, not negotiation. A mirror cannot correct you."
- Not that the six-term substrate is nature's own list. It is a visible, revisable guess.
- Not `grounded`. No measurement was taken here.
- Not a claim about personal beliefs of any named researcher. This packet asks for a check, not an endorsement.

## 5. What would settle it

1. **Transfer to a novel ground:** a model trained in one environment and paid by an environment it never contacted, with no import from that environment.
2. **A named settlement event:** an instrument reading, experiment, board or outcome that would make the model pay, stated before the run.
3. **A figure re-census that changes the wall:** a demonstration that composition alone can add an atom to a closure, or that a bound can move without contact of any kind.

## 6. What an expert can check in twenty minutes

1. Read Fig 5 :69-70, Fig 6 :23, Fig 8 :54 and Fig 1 :23. Four short passages.
2. Say whether "useful" in your field requires a ground event, or whether a well-calibrated internal model counts. Write down which you used.
3. Name one study where a model of a never-encountered situation paid on first contact. If you cannot, say so.
4. Name one result that would make you withdraw the three-condition shape. A failure is more useful than an endorsement.
