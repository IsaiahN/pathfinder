# The Tether's answer: can systems construct useful mental models of situations never directly encountered?

**Mode: `specified` on the conditions, `general` on the structural shape, not `grounded`.** This is a shape and a route, not a result. It is a simulated derivation checked against the figures (`05_what_shape_means.md` section 6), not a ground reading.

## 1. The residual it answers

A system holds a library Γ. A situation X has never been an operand of its perception, so no reading of X is held. The question asks for a model φ that predicts X and pays when acted upon. It is a **union compound**, not one hard question: construction and settlement are two conditions, and "a case in two conditions is not a harder case of either." (Fig 9 :57). Keeping them together is how a fluent composition gets mistaken for a grounded model.

## 2. The shape, one loop per domain, verdicts kept separate

**D1, composition inside a closure. Verdict: constructible, yes (`general`, triangulated).** The arrangements over held atoms do not run out: "The set is closed. The arrangements are not. That gap is why import exists." (Fig 13 :115). A settled molecule can become an operand at the next level (Fig 12 :104). But "NOVEL means novel relative to atoms, not to the world." (Fig 6 :22). A model of X assembled from held parts is new to the library, not new to the ground. Triangulated across Fig 13, Fig 12 and Fig 5 :28, three different figures with low overlap.

**D2, transfer between frames. Verdict: reachable without direct contact, yes (`general`, triangulated).** "No frame escapes its own closure. The bound moves only on contact with a frame whose closure differs." (Fig 6 :23). "The atom cannot be derived from inside A. But the union is searchable from contact, so underivable is not unknowable." (Fig 8 :54). And "describe it before you go looking, or any frame you pick will seem to fit" (Fig 9 :27-28). This is how a system reaches a situation it has not met: by describing the residual in effect terms and finding a frame whose closure already predicts its shape. Theory can precede the instrument: "theory routinely precedes its instrument, and is welcome to." (Fig 6 :38). Triangulated across Fig 6, Fig 8 and Fig 9 (wall mechanic, union surplus, search procedure).

**D3, settlement and usefulness. Verdict: open until the ground pays (`specified`, triangulated).** "A term is settled when the ground pays for it. A term is usable when something can compose over it. Those are two conditions and this figure names one." (Fig 5 :70). "The ground enters as the only metric." (Fig 1 :23). "narrowing gets you close, the ground decides" (Fig 9 :42). No amount of internal coherence, analogy, or number of agreeing readers supplies the missing event. This domain is not averaged with the first two. Triangulated across Fig 1, Fig 5, Fig 9 and Fig 2 :35.

**D4, habitat and capability. Verdict: indirect contact counts (`general`, single-source for the cascade claim).** "capability is a property of agent-and-habitat, never of the agent alone." (Fig 11 :39). "And contact cascades. Scoping the habitat to direct contact is how DDT reached the eagles: first-order enumeration, second-order effect, and the residual that mattered was three" (Fig 11 :52). A system can be in contact with the determinants of X without having met X. This is single-source inside the figures (Fig 11), with the animal-behaviour literature as weak secondary corroboration only.

**Combined verdict.** Yes to constructibility, conditional on a different-closure source when the parts are not held. Not yet to usefulness, which requires settlement. In one line: a system can construct a model of a situation it has never directly encountered, but the model is a candidate shape until the ground pays for it, and the only way to reach a part the system does not hold is contact with a frame whose closure differs.

## 3. What it explicitly does not say

- Not that any system can model any situation. Fig 6 :40: "What is unavailable is a sensor for something that has never registered anywhere, at any resolution. That is the whole of it."
- Not that analogy or imagination guarantees usefulness. Transfer is a route to a candidate, not a settlement.
- Not that a synthetic or same-environment simulation proves capability. Fig 11 :70: "A test harness is a substituted habitat. Which is why a synthetic solve proves wiring and never capability."
- Not that agreement among experts settles the question. Fig 2 :35: "Alignment is triangulation, not negotiation. A mirror cannot correct you."
- Not that a provisional steering model is a settled model. It is a budget filter (Fig 9 :60), not a witness.
- Not `grounded`. No measurement was taken in this derivation.
- Not that the six-term substrate is nature's own list. It is "a human's careful guess at reality's compression axes" (*They're Scanning Books*); declaring it makes the choice visible, not neutral.

## 4. What would settle it, the ground

A discriminator, not a further argument. The green-reading case was resolved by a spectrometer, not by discussion (*The Hard Problem is About Source*). Here the corresponding tests are:

1. **Transfer-to-novel-ground test.** Train a model in one environment and deploy it in an environment whose ground it never contacted, with no import from that environment. If the model pays there, D2 moves toward grounded. A same-environment dream does not count (see `01` F7).
2. **Settlement test.** Name the event that would make the model pay: an instrument reading, an experiment, a board, a patient outcome. If nobody can name it, "useful" is being used as a feeling, not a verdict.
3. **No-source test.** Find a system that builds a ground-paying model with no direct contact, no different-closure lineage and no held atom predicting X. That refutes Fig 6 :23 and withdraws the shape.
4. **Agreement test.** Two frames drawing on the same evidence must not be counted as two witnesses (Fig 2 :40). Replications with genuinely separate experience are different.
5. **Standing refuters from the record.** A model that pays with no settlement event; a sensor for something that never registered; a case where internal composition reaches a part the closure does not hold.

## 5. The honest stop

**Unreached, not unreachable.** The model is a candidate shape with a stated refuter. The framework can say what a useful model would need, and can say when a claimed model has not met the conditions. It cannot say which particular model of a never-encountered situation is useful, because that is a name, not a shape, and naming would mean already holding it (Fig 13 :135). It cannot certify itself: "the output is only as good as the validator. The agent, or the tool, or the frame, or the theory, cannot validate itself." (*The Recursive Self-Improvement Trap*). The stop is recorded here so a later reader can locate the error.

## 6. One-paragraph version for outreach

> The Tether answers yes and no. Yes, a system can construct a model of a situation it has never directly encountered: the arrangements over what it already holds do not run out, and it can reach what it lacks through contact with another frame whose vocabulary differs, provided it first describes the gap in effect terms. No, that construction is not yet usefulness. A model is only useful when the ground pays for it, and no amount of internal coherence, analogy, or agreement among readers supplies that event. The framework returns a shape, not a model: residual describable from contact that exists, parts held or imported with their source recorded, and settlement deferred to an instrument. What would settle it is a transfer test to a genuinely novel ground, not another argument. What would refute it is written down in advance. Until one of those fires, the answer is a candidate, not a verdict.
