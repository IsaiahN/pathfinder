## Three findings in the wider literature that bear on this question

1. **Construction from imagination is a documented human capacity.** The mental models overview records that models can be built from "perception, imagination, or the comprehension of discourse", and the Mental Models Lab studies kinematic mental simulations and counterfactual thinking. This corroborates the constructibility half of the shape. Overlap: independent of the framework, but it is a broad overview and not a direct test of ground-paying models of never-encountered situations.
2. **Transfer is real but fragile.** The cognitive-map page records both Tolman's cross-maze result and Bennett's objection that simpler explanations have not been ruled out, plus the finding that rats fail novel shortcuts without preexposure. Gentner's mapping account explains how a known source supplies a predicate for a target. Together these predict condition (ii): contact of a different kind is needed, and the transfer is not automatic.
3. **Skill on known tasks is not generality.** Chollet's measure of intelligence and the MAML result both separate fast adaptation from accumulated priors. They corroborate the distinction between a model carried from a training set and a model of something new, but neither supplies a ground test for the latter. The World Models paper is a substituted habitat, not a never-encountered ground (see `01` F7).

## Distribution: offer, do not push

- **Fig 10 :52**: "Bring a characterised residual, never the source." Send the three conditions and the refuter. Do not lead with the architecture.
- **Fig 2 :35**: "Alignment is triangulation, not negotiation. A mirror cannot correct you." Do not ask for agreement.
- **Fig 9 :27-28**: "describe it before you go looking, or any frame you pick will seem to fit". Ask what the reader would expect to see if the shape claim were true before showing them the source.
- **Fig 2 :40**: "agreement about the world is worthless when the agreeing frames share an evidence pool". A later agreement from a reader who has seen the figures is not evidence.
- **Fig 9 :60**: "Use filters for the budget and witnesses for the verdict. Never let a filter hand you a verdict." A venue's prestige is a budget filter, never a verdict.
- **Seat Map :163**: "High correlation is disqualifying for a verdict and efficient for a convention." Do not choose readers for likely agreement.

**Recommended order in a reply.** (1) The three conditions and the refuter. (2) Ask what would count as a failure. (3) Record the verdict. (4) Only then offer the links, with the note that the figures, not the summary, are the authority.

**The two-stage offer is not a persuasion sequence.** Stage 2 is opt-in because a reader who has absorbed the architecture before giving a verdict has joined the frame being checked. Stage 3 sends a method, which is permitted: "Only methods cross up. Only head starts come down." (Fig 4 :22). Each user must write their own refuter before running it.

**Hosting.** Nothing in this folder is sent by this agent. A public, opt-in download page is preferable to an attachment pushed to a list, because it lets a recipient decline contact before holding the material.
