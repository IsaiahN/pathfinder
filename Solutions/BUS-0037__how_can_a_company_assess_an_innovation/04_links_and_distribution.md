## What the wider literature adds, and its limit

The business sources are **one evidence pool**, not five witnesses. Effectuation, discovery-driven planning, MVP and premortem all converge on acting to reduce uncertainty, testing assumptions cheaply, and designing refusal in advance. That agreement corroborates the figure-derived shape at the level of mechanism, but because they share a field and an era, their agreement is one witness. The figure verdict does not rest on them.

The animal-behaviour source (Charnov's marginal value theorem) is genuinely low overlap. Its rule, leave a patch when the current return falls to the habitat average, is a surprising match for the framework's persistent-and-flat residual test (Fig 5 :59-60). It is an analogue for the *leave* decision, not a business measurement.

## Distribution: offer, do not push

Figure-grounded reasons:

- **Fig 4 :22** *"Only methods cross up. Only head starts come down."* Sending the full architecture before anyone describes a residual sends a recording, not a generator. Lead with the refuter and the shape claim; offer links after.
- **Fig 10 :42** *"A verifier that can reconstruct the claim cannot verify it."* A recipient who has absorbed the entire framework before being asked for a verdict has joined the frame being checked.
- **Fig 9 :27-28** *"describe it before you go looking, or any frame you pick will seem to fit"* Ask what they would expect to see if the shape claim were true before giving them the article.
- **Seat Map :163** *"High correlation is disqualifying for a verdict and efficient for a convention."* A mass send selected for people who already agree produces a convention, not corroboration.

**Recommended order in a reply:** (1) the shape claim and its refuter; (2) ask what they would count as a failure; (3) record their verdict; (4) only then the links, with the note that the figures, not the summary, are the authority.

## The two-stage offer

1. **Stage 1.** Send the residual, the shape claim and the six pre-registered refuters. Ask what the recipient would count as a failure. Record the verdict before any theory is shown.
2. **Stage 2, opt-in only.** If they are curious, offer the four local framework files listed above. Their later agreement is never pooled as corroboration.
3. **Stage 3.** Offer the formula as a method to reuse. Each user writes their own refuter and states their own mode.

**Hard precondition:** stages 1 to 3 only work if the stage-1 output survives a character-by-character quote check. The output is a simulated derivation, not a ground reading, and cannot settle the question it reports on.
