## QUESTION

Decide, in effect terms, what the Tether says about assessing an innovation whose market, customers and business model are all uncertain. Option A: issue one score from internal instruments (pitch, TAM, executive consensus). Option B: treat the three uncertainties as a union compound, build a reachable anchor and a comparison term, and refuse on schedule. What each leads to: A produces a number the frame also produces, which Fig 1 :27 disqualifies as self-scoring; B produces a checkable procedure that never claims to name the winning market before the ground settles it. R is indexed per slot and never aggregated (Fig 1 :19).

## DOMAIN CENSUS

R by arity, symmetry and scale, then one line per domain with actors / currency / anchor, partition or union, and order by dependency.

- **R (the residual).** A candidate innovation must be judged without a paying market, readable customers or a stated business model. Arity: three overlapping conditions, so a union compound (Fig 9 :4-5, :55). Symmetry: ordered action -> observation -> residual, with a threshold verdict, so a comparison bond, not a symmetric conjunction (Operators table :52). Scale: slot (one contact), venture (composition of slots), market (anchor); the membrane between scales is lossy (Fig 4 :30).
- **D1 The company's prediction frame.** Actors: managers, analysts, pitch, TAM model, rubric. Currency: predictions and internal agreement. Anchor: none of its own, because its quantities are frame-internal. Status: frame, not settleable locally.
- **D2 Customer contact.** Actors: potential customers and their behaviour (what they do, not what they say). Currency: payment, repeat use, time spent. Anchor: behaviour that is pinned and does not update when the company argues (Fig 2 :25). Status: admitted.
- **D3 Market habitat.** Actors: customers, competitors, entrants, substitutes, complementors, regulators. Currency: survival, share, profit. Anchor: the market's payment, named as an anchor in Fig 2 :29 but not reachable here. Status: admitted as directional target.
- **D4 Business-model arrangement.** Actors: value proposition, delivery, revenue mechanism and their bonds. Currency: bits for the description, actions for finding out. Anchor: a comparison term not yet bound. Status: compound until the comparison is bound.
- **X1 Founder conviction.** No nameable anchor; evidence bearing only on the maker is a stake-inducing channel (Fig 9 :51). Not a domain.
- **X2 TAM as verdict.** A filter for the budget, not a witness for the verdict (Fig 9 :60). Not a domain.

Union, not partition: customers appear in both D2 and D3, and D4 binds across them, so the whole is a union of overlapping conditions. Dependency order: perception -> vocabulary -> objective -> planning -> learn and carry (Fig 3). The chain breaks at objective when the comparison term is missing. Strain test: Fig 2 :29 names a market as anchor, but for a new innovation that line strains. Re-census: the market anchors D3 (established business) and is a directional target for D2; the local anchor is a behaviour already returning something. Strain dissolved by domain separation, not by softening the reading.

## VERDICT (from the figures)

The figure-derived verdict is a procedure shape with five parts: split the union compound into separately checkable conditions and never average; disqualify frame-internal measures and maker evidence; build a contact instrument from one already returning something at a pinned behavioural anchor; bind the comparison term before the experiment and hold bits and actions in separate columns; and arrange for refusal before the run with independence bought in proportion to the cost of being wrong. Inward and outward are two tracks and must not be read against each other. Which market, customer or revenue model wins is a `gap`, returned as the shape of a missing discriminator, not a name. Verdicts stay separate per domain. Mode `general` on structure, `specified` on the business application, ungrounded. The business evidence pool corroborates the mechanism shape but carries one witness's weight; the foraging analogue corroborates the leave rule at low overlap; neither decides the verdict.

- **cost if wrong:** bounded and locatable. A company that fails D2 would be told to refine its pitch, and a company that fails D4 would be told to run more experiments, so a single lever would be applied to a union that needs separate loops. In outreach terms, an expert's twenty minutes would be spent refuting a shape that was never the right shape. The larger cost is spending the independence of outside readers by sending a recording instead of a generator, which Fig 2 :42 prices.
- **undoable:** yes. No term is deleted if the claim fails; a refutation is added and the reach falls because something was added rather than because anything was struck out (Fig 6 :42). Cuts are kept ranked and reversible (Fig 9 :41), and the procedure is recorded with provenance (Fig 10 :62-64), so a later reader can locate the error rather than only feel it.
- **reversed by:** any of the six pre-registered falsifiers: (1) an aggregate score matches the per-condition procedure on ground-verified outcomes; (2) maker evidence matches behavioural contact; (3) a frame-internal measure settles a dispute; (4) the winning model is derivable by internal composition alone; (5) a synthetic solve transfers with no habitat loss; (6) an agreeing committee matches independent ranging. A source with a genuinely different evidence pool showing that one lever clears all three conditions with no residue would also reverse it. Until one of these fires, the verdict stands as `candidate`.
