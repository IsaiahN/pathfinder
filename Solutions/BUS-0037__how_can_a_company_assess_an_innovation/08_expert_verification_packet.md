# Expert verification packet: the Tether on assessing an uncertain innovation

**What this is:** a claim in a form an entrepreneurship or innovation researcher can check. Mode, derivation and refuters are stated before the conclusion, so a reader knows what to look for and what would settle it.

**Mode: `general` on the structural shape, `specified` on the business application, not `grounded`.** No market measurement is claimed. This is a procedure shape and a declared limit, not a result about any particular innovation.

**Status: simulated derivation checked for internal consistency against the figures, not a ground reading.** It cannot settle the question it reports on.

## 1. The claim, in one paragraph

An innovation whose market, customers and business model are all uncertain cannot be assessed by a single score, because the three uncertainties are a union compound and the company's own measures are frame-internal. The figure-licensed procedure is: split the compound into separately checkable conditions; find a contact instrument already returning something at a pinned behavioural anchor; bind the comparison term (what counts as a win) before the experiment; run inward instrument work and outward analogy search as two separate tracks; price description cost and action cost in different columns; write the refusal criterion before each checkpoint; and report `unreachable` with the shape of what is owed rather than naming a market. Which market, customer or revenue model wins is a declared `gap` until the ground settles it.

## 2. Three checkable sub-claims

### C1  -  framework-internal; checkable by reading Figures 1, 9 and 13

- **Statement:** no internal measure is a verdict, and the winner is not named by the framework. The residual is per slot, not aggregated.
- **Source, verbatim:** Fig 1 :19 *"The residual is indexed by object slot, not aggregated."* Fig 1 :27 *"They are frame-internal. A frame cannot score itself with a quantity it also produces."* Fig 9 :51 *"evidence bearing only on its maker is a stake-inducing channel, and the verifier is denied it"* Fig 13 :135 *"It cannot say which primitive is missing, because naming it would mean already holding it."*
- **Refuter:** an aggregate score, or maker evidence with no behavioural contact, predicts ground-verified venture outcomes as well as the per-condition procedure; or a figure line is produced that directly names a market for an arbitrary innovation.

### C2  -  framework-internal; checkable by reading Figures 3, 6, 9 and 12

- **Statement:** the three conditions are a union compound ordered through a missing comparison term; a contact instrument must be improved from one already returning something; description cost and action cost do not add.
- **Source, verbatim:** Fig 9 :55 *"A bundled problem is usually a union of overlapping conditions, not a list of separate ones."* Fig 3 :27 *"A link with no instrument is not a link that works. It is a link nobody has looked at."* Fig 6 :32 *"An instrument is not built from a description. It is improved from a worse instrument already returning something."* Fig 12 :125 *"A composition costs in two currencies, and they do not add."* Operators table :52 *"a goal is a comparison, which is why a vocabulary without it cannot state one"*
- **Refuter:** one lever clears all three conditions with no residue; an objective can be supplied after the experiment without changing which experiment was run; a valid instrument is built from a pure description with no prior reading; or description cost predicts action cost on ground-verified cases.

### C3  -  framework-internal; checkable by reading Figures 2, 7, 10 and 11

- **Statement:** refusal must be arranged before the run, independence bought by cost of being wrong, the habitat enumerated, and the capability located in innovation-and-habitat rather than in the innovation alone.
- **Source, verbatim:** Fig 10 :82 *"Three panels, one move: arrange for refusal."* Fig 2 :42 *"Buy independence in proportion to the cost of being wrong."* Fig 7 :35 *"A room's agreement is variation, not a verdict. Selection between rooms is where the ground votes."* Fig 11 :43 *"The habitat is enumerated, never composed."* Fig 11 :39 *"capability is a property of agent-and-habitat, never of the agent alone."*
- **Refuter:** a committee sharing one evidence pool corroborates at the same accuracy as independent ranging; a refusal criterion is written after seeing the result; an invariant actor-valence list survives a change of residual; or a capability demonstrated in isolation transfers to the market with no habitat-substitution loss.

## 3. What is explicitly not claimed

- Not a score, ranking or "innovation quotient." Fig 1 :19 forbids aggregate residual.
- Not a name for the winning market, customer or revenue model. That is a `gap`.
- Not that MVP, discovery-driven planning or premortem are figure-derived. They are business-layer instances, corroboration only, and share one evidence pool.
- Not that the figures settle whether a specific innovation will succeed. They return a procedure shape and a limit.
- Not `grounded`. No measurement at any market was used.
- Not a claim that the founder's or sponsor's conviction is evidence. Fig 9 :51 makes it a stake-inducing channel; Seat Map :171 says *"an interest in the outcome is unavoidable, an interest in the reading is disqualifying"* and *"Where a stake cannot be removed, declare it."*
