# Outreach draft: check the procedure before you decide whether the framework is worth reading

**Use:** one-to-one, or as a cover letter attached to a submission through the public venues in `03_public_contact_points.csv`. **Not** for bulk send.

**Hard precondition:** do not send until the figure quotes in `08_expert_verification_packet.md` have been rechecked character by character against `FRAMEWORK/reference/03-FIGURES_TEXT.md`. The output must survive validation or the two-stage offer collapses on contact.

---

**Subject:** Check one claim about assessing uncertain innovations before you decide anything else

> Hi [name],
>
> I have one specific claim I would like you to check against what you already know. I am not asking you to read the framework first, and I am not asking you to agree with anything. If the claim does not survive, there is nothing else to look at.
>
> **The residual, in effect terms.** A company wants to assess an innovation whose market, customers and business model are all uncertain. The usual grounds for a verdict are missing: there is no market that will pay yet, no customer behaviour that can be read, and no comparison term that would say what "success" even means. The question is not how to score the innovation better. It is how a frame obtains a reachable anchor and a comparison term when the anchor it would normally use does not yet exist.
>
> **The claim (`general` on the shape, `specified` on the business application, not `grounded`):** such an assessment cannot be a single score. It is a procedure with five parts: (1) split the three uncertainties into separate conditions, each with its own loop and its own verdict, never averaged; (2) disqualify internal measures, since the frame cannot score itself with a quantity it also produces, and evidence bearing only on the maker is a stake-inducing channel; (3) find a contact instrument already returning something, then sharpen it, because an instrument is not built from a description; (4) run inward (sharpen your own instrument) and outward (describe the residual in effect terms and find a frame whose closure already predicts its shape) as two separate tracks; (5) write the failure criterion before each experiment, keep cuts ranked and reversible, and report "unreachable" with the shape of what is owed rather than manufacturing a verdict.
>
> **What I am not claiming:** that this tells you which market, which customer, or which revenue model will win. The framework returns a shape, not a name. I am also not claiming that the business tactics often attached to this (landing pages, minimum viable products, discovery-driven planning) are figure-derived. They are business-layer instances, offered as examples only, and they carry no figure authority by themselves.
>
> **What would refute the claim.** I wrote these down before sending this:
> 1. A single aggregate score across the three conditions predicts ground-verified venture outcomes as well as the per-condition procedure.
> 2. Founder conviction or pitch quality, with no behavioural contact, predicts outcomes as well as behavioural instruments.
> 3. A frame-internal measure such as TAM settles a live dispute without any contact with customers.
> 4. The winning business model is derivable by internal composition alone, with no import from a differing closure and no new instrument.
> 5. A synthetic or internal solve transfers to market capability with no measurable habitat-substitution loss.
> 6. A committee sharing one evidence pool corroborates the verdict at the same accuracy as independent ranging.
>
> If any one holds, the procedure is wrong and I will retract it. A one-line "this fails because..." is more useful to me than agreement. If you would rather not engage, no reply is needed. **What I would value from you:** what would you count as a failure of this shape, and which of the three conditions do you think is actually a union of further conditions?
>
> **If it survives, and only then:** the sources are below. The figures, not my summary of them, are the authority.
> - Canonical figure text with line numbers (local framework file): `FRAMEWORK/reference/03-FIGURES_TEXT.md`
> - Canonical usage rule (local framework file): `FRAMEWORK/reference/FIGURES_README_canonical.md`
> - The eight-step loop and the mode rule (local framework file): `FRAMEWORK/figures/THE_FORMULA.md`
>
> **If you want to use the machinery:** the formula is in the figures link. Run it on a problem you know well and can check against a ground. Write down what would refute your result before you run it, and state the mode of what you get. A reported failure is more useful to me than a reported success.
>
> Thanks,
> [name]

## Stage recording rule

Record the recipient's verdict on stage 1 **before** showing any theory. Their later agreement is never pooled as corroboration (Fig 2 :40: *"agreement about the world is worthless when the agreeing frames share an evidence pool"*). If they return a refutation, copy it into `11_audit.md` with provenance and re-evaluate it as if it came from any other source.
