# The Tether's answer on assessing an innovation under triple uncertainty

**Mode: `general` on the structural shape, `specified` on the business application, not `grounded`.** This is a simulated derivation checked against the figures (`05_what_shape_means.md` §6), not a ground reading.

## 1. The residual it answers

A company must decide whether to commit resources to a candidate innovation. The three things that would normally settle such a decision are all unavailable: no market that will pay, no readable customer behaviour, and no comparison term stating what would count as success. The residual is therefore not "score the innovation better" but "obtain a reachable anchor and a comparison term when the usual anchor does not yet exist." Fig 9 :4-5: *"First: is there a fact here? Disagreements that do not shrink with effort, where each rule keeps working well on a different subset, are not one hard question but several well-formed ones. Split it rather than search."*

## 2. Verdicts per domain, kept separate and never averaged

**D1, the company's prediction frame. Verdict: no internal verdict is available (`general`, triangulated).** Fig 1 :27: *"They are frame-internal. A frame cannot score itself with a quantity it also produces."* Fig 9 :51: *"evidence bearing only on its maker is a stake-inducing channel, and the verifier is denied it"*. The pitch, the TAM, the founder's conviction and internal consensus are all outputs of the frame that wants the answer. Refuter: a frame-internal measure settles a live dispute without contact.

**D2, customer contact. Verdict: a proto-instrument already returning something is the reachable local anchor (`general` shape, `specified` application, triangulated).** Fig 6 :32: *"An instrument is not built from a description. It is improved from a worse instrument already returning something."* Fig 2 :29: *"An anchor is whatever settles the question and does not change its answer because of what any frame thinks."* Fig 2 :35: *"Alignment is triangulation, not negotiation. A mirror cannot correct you."* The local anchor is behaviour (payment, repeat use), not opinion. Refuter: a company builds a valid instrument from a pure description with no prior reading.

**D3, the market habitat. Verdict: the market is a directional target here, not a reachable anchor; enumerate the habitat (`specified`, triangulated on the anchor rule, single-source on enumeration).** Fig 11 :43: *"The habitat is enumerated, never composed."* Fig 11 :49: *"An actor's valence is relative to the residual, not a property it carries."* Refuter: an invariant actor-valence list survives a change of residual.

**D4, the business-model arrangement. Verdict: a missing comparison term and two incommensurate currencies (`general`, triangulated).** Operators table :52: *"a goal is a comparison, which is why a vocabulary without it cannot state one"*; Fig 12 :125: *"A composition costs in two currencies, and they do not add."* Fig 9 :60: *"Use filters for the budget and witnesses for the verdict. Never let a filter hand you a verdict."* Bind the comparison term before the experiment; keep bits and actions in separate columns. Refuter: description cost predicts action cost.

**Union and order. Verdict: split, then order by dependency (`general`, triangulated).** Fig 9 :55: *"A bundled problem is usually a union of overlapping conditions, not a list of separate ones."* Fig 3 :27: *"A link with no instrument is not a link that works. It is a link nobody has looked at."* Refuter: one lever clears all three conditions with no residue.

**Refusal and independence. Verdict: arrange for refusal before the run (`specified`, triangulated).** Fig 10 :82: *"Three panels, one move: arrange for refusal."* Fig 2 :42: *"Buy independence in proportion to the cost of being wrong."* Fig 7 :35: *"A room's agreement is variation, not a verdict. Selection between rooms is where the ground votes."* Refuter: a committee sharing one evidence pool corroborates at the same accuracy as independent ranging.

**Naming the winner. Verdict: `gap` (`general`, triangulated limit).** Fig 13 :135: *"It cannot say which primitive is missing, because naming it would mean already holding it."* Fig 8 :39: *"So a search that finds nothing is never a proof of absence."* The framework returns the shape of the missing discriminator, in effect terms. Which market, customer or revenue model wins is owed to the ground, not derivable here.

## 3. The procedure in one list

1. Split the three conditions; give each its own loop, instrument and verdict. Never average.
2. Disqualify frame-internal substitutes (pitch, TAM, founder conviction, internal consensus).
3. Find a proto-instrument already returning something, then sharpen it. Registers behaviour at a pinned anchor; discriminates among sources.
4. Run inward (sharpen your own instrument) and outward (describe the residual in effect terms, find a frame whose closure predicts its shape, with provenance and a shadow test) as two separate tracks.
5. Bind the comparison term (what counts as a win) before the experiment.
6. Enumerate the habitat out to the cascade; expect valence to change with the residual.
7. Price bits and actions in separate columns. Filters for budget, witnesses for verdict.
8. Write the refusal criterion before each experiment; keep cuts ranked and reversible; declare the sponsor's stake; assemble reviewers from an unauthored pool.
9. If nothing in the closure closes the residual, report `unreachable` with the shape of what is owed. Do not name the market.

Steps 3 and 4 correspond to the three questions of formula step 7, kept distinct: SEARCHED (is a signal already in the library and unfound), INWARD (extend an instrument already returning something), OUTWARD (import a frame whose closure differs).

## 4. What it explicitly does not say

- Not a single score, ranking or "innovation quotient." There is no global verdict, and Fig 1 :19 says *"The residual is indexed by object slot, not aggregated."*
- Not a name for the winning market, customer or business model. That is a `gap`, not a hidden answer.
- Not that the business tactics (MVP, discovery-driven planning, premortem) are figure-derived. They are business-layer instances and carry no figure authority by themselves.
- Not that the framework settles whether this particular innovation will succeed. It returns a procedure shape and a declared limit.
- Not `grounded`. No measurement at any market was used.

## 5. What would settle it, and what would refute the shape

**Settled by the ground, not by agreement:** a source-discriminating contact that separates, for example, curiosity from willingness to pay; a repeated behaviour at a pinned anchor; a comparison term that was bound in advance and then paid or refused. *The Hard Problem is About Source* (2026-08-28): *"The answer is unreached until a better instrument exists."*

**Six standing falsifiers, written before the run (see `01` §4):** aggregate score matches per-condition procedure; maker evidence matches behavioural contact; frame-internal measure settles a dispute; winning model derivable by internal composition alone; synthetic solve transfers with no habitat loss; agreeing committee matches independent ranging. Any one fires, the procedure is withdrawn.

## 6. The honest stop

**Unreached, not unreachable.** Fig 9 :42: *"narrowing gets you close, the ground decides"*. The shape stops at the limit of the available instruments and records the lineage so the stop is checkable. *"Indiscernibility is not identity"* (canonical figures README). A better instrument may arrive later; nothing in this folder claims to be that instrument.

## 7. One-paragraph version for outreach

> The Tether does not give an innovation a score and does not name the market that will pay for it. It says that three uncertainties (market, customers, business model) are a union compound, so they must be split and settled separately, never averaged; that the company's own pitch, TAM and confidence are frame-internal and cannot be the verdict; that the reachable anchor is a customer behaviour a proto-instrument is already returning, sharpened from a worse one, not an opinion; that inward instrument work and outward analogy search are two tracks that must not be read against each other; that a business plan prices the description while only actions price finding out whether it holds, so the comparison term must be bound before the experiment; and that failure criteria must be written in advance, with independence bought in proportion to the cost of being wrong. It refuses to name the winning market because the framework returns a shape, not a name. What it offers is the shape any such assessment must have, six falsifiers written in advance, and the kind of instrument that would close the gap. The center stays open until the ground settles it.
