## Restating the question as an effect

The company holds a candidate φ (the innovation) and wants a verdict on whether to commit resources. Normally the verdict is ranged against three things: a market that will pay, customers whose behaviour would reveal demand, and a business model that states how value is captured. Here all three are absent or unreadable. So the residual R is the gap between what the company's frame predicts about the innovation and what a not-yet-reachable ground would return.

Binding the sought-for answer as an ordinary term (formula, "Direction"): *an answer would look like a procedure that (a) splits the compound into separately checkable conditions, (b) builds a reachable anchor where the market anchor is not yet available, and (c) states in advance what would count as failure.* That binding is written down so it can accrue a residual and be refused like anything else, instead of lurking as an invisible preference.

What each option leads to: Option A, score the innovation with internal instruments (pitch, TAM, business-plan score, executive consensus), yields a number that a frame produced about itself, which the figures disqualify as a verdict. Option B, build contact and refuse on schedule, yields a checkable procedure that never claims to name the winning market, customer, or model before the ground settles it.

## What is deliberately not here

- No personal emails of named individuals.
- No ranking of people by how likely they are to agree with the claim.
- No bulk-send plan.
- No name for the winning market, customer, or business model. The framework returns a shape, not a name. Naming one before the ground settles it is the error this package refuses.

Three reasons from the figures:

1. **A room does not upgrade the label.** Fig 7 :35: *"A room's agreement is variation, not a verdict. Selection between rooms is where the ground votes."*
2. **Correlation disqualifies a verdict.** Seat Map :163: *"High correlation is disqualifying for a verdict and efficient for a convention."* Selecting recipients for receptiveness manufactures the correlation that makes their agreement worthless.
3. **Asking for trust before checking spends independence.** Fig 10 :42: *"A verifier that can reconstruct the claim cannot verify it."* The outside frame must not hold the interiors.

Legally: scraping personal emails for unsolicited bulk outreach breaches GDPR and CAN-SPAM style rules in many jurisdictions. Public opt-in channels avoid that failure as well as the structural one.
