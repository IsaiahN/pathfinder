## QUESTION

Decide, in effect terms, what would separate a representation that carries structure into a new habitat from one that only fits the training corpus. Option A: treat a low training residual and a small held-out gap as the separator. Option B: treat fit as a frame-internal budget filter and require a per-slot, method-carrying, bond-aware, reach-aware, independently checked and provenanced probe. What each leads to: A produces a convention when the evaluator shares the evidence pool, it conflates a lookup with a method, and it spends the independence of later checkers; B produces a checkable shape, six refuters written in advance and a declared gap for the universal recipe.

## DOMAIN CENSUS

R by arity, symmetry and scale, then one line per domain with actors / currency / anchor, partition or union, and order by dependency.

- **R (the residual).** The gap between prediction and observation at a slot in a habitat the representation was not fitted on. Arity: dyadic per slot, plus a second-order relation across habitats. Symmetry: an aggregate accuracy is symmetric under slot permutation while "generalizes" is ordered (train then probe). Scale: frame-internal fit versus cross-habitat contact, with the round-trip gap `R_T` as the error signal. Fig 9 names a union compound, not one hard question: a case in two conditions is not a harder case of either.
- **Domain 1, the learning frame.** Actors: parameters, training examples, loss, optimizer. Currency: training residual and description length in bits and actions. Anchor: none nameable inside the frame, because the training metric updates on the frame's own outputs. Status: the frame being checked, not a verdict domain on its own.
- **Domain 2, the new habitat and held-out ground.** Actors: unseen slots, new tasks, measurement instruments. Currency: residual at each slot on a habitat not used for fitting. Anchor: a measurement that does not move when the model's prediction changes (Fig 2 :29-30). Verdict domain.
- **Domain 3, the evaluation and selection frame.** Actors: benchmark designers, metric choice, reviewers, reported leaderboards. Currency: reported accuracy and agreement. Anchor: the ground only if the benchmark genuinely touches it, otherwise a mirror (Fig 2 :35). Verdict domain with its own loop.
- **Domain 4, the provenance and lineage frame.** Actors: the derivation record, source gates, shadow test. Currency: provenance, meaning where each atom and bond came from. Anchor: the record itself, checkable by a later reader (Fig 10 :62-64). Verdict domain.
- **Partition or union: union.** D2, D3 and D4 overlap. A single lever clears only the region where the other conditions do not bind; the intersections remain (Fig 9 :55-57).
- **Order by dependency.** See the new slots before choosing a metric (Fig 3 :27); name the residual before choosing a frame (Fig 9 :27-28); refuse a wrong term before minting (Formula step 2); settle each first-order molecule before composing a second-order claim; record the lineage (Fig 10 :62-64).

Re-census assigns the figure line to *stating plus leftover* and the world result to a low-complexity interpolant with good contact. Different objects, so the verdict is not flipped and the strain S2 stays recorded in `01_figure_grounded_audit.md`.

## VERDICT (from the figures)

The shape survives, the universal recipe does not. A low training residual is frame-internal and cannot witness generality. The checkable separator reads the residual per slot against a non-updating anchor in a substituted habitat, requires a carried method rather than a replay, tests bonds rather than ingredients, checks reach before import, buys evaluator independence in proportion to the cost of error, and keeps a lineage that separates convergent derivation from adopted import. Verdicts by domain: D1 `specified` triangulated, D2 `specified` triangulated, D3 `specified` triangulated, D4 `specified` triangulated, F9 `gap` of the shape-not-a-name kind, reported as unreached rather than silence. Nothing is `grounded`.

- **cost if wrong:** if fit is treated as a verdict, the result is a convention, not a check about the world. The real cost is spending the independence of outside readers by asking them to endorse a metric that the frame also produces. Fig 2 :42 "Buy independence in proportion to the cost of being wrong." That cost is avoidable by leading with the separator and its refuters.
- **undoable:** yes. The separator is a discipline, not a primitive. No atom has to be withdrawn; a defective metric can be demoted from witness to filter and the probe can be re-run with a non-updating anchor and a stated lineage.
- **reversed by:** a ground reading, not agreement. A probe that holds the atom set fixed, re-bonds it and measures per-slot residual on a non-updating habitat could separate method from recording; a discriminator that tells convergent derivation from adopted import would settle D4; any of the six refuters in `01_figure_grounded_audit.md` section 5 firing would break the shape; a figure line naming a universal architecture or sample count would close F9. Prestige, leaderboard position and shared-pool agreement are not such instruments.
