## What is deliberately not here

- No personal emails of named individuals.
- No ranking of people by how likely they are to believe the claim.
- No bulk-send plan.
- No universal architecture, sample count or threshold. Fig 9 :32: "you get a kind of thing, never the answer itself." That is a `gap`, reported as a verdict rather than silence (Fig 13 :134).

Three figure-grounded reasons for the first three omissions:

1. **A mirror cannot correct you.** Fig 2 :35: "Alignment is triangulation, not negotiation. A mirror cannot correct you." A list selected for receptiveness manufactures the correlation that would make agreement worthless.
2. **Shared evidence disqualifies a verdict.** Fig 2 :40: "disqualifying for a verdict: agreement about the world is worthless when the agreeing frames share an evidence pool."
3. **A verifier must not hold the interiors.** Fig 10 :42: "A verifier that can reconstruct the claim cannot verify it." The first contact carries the separator and its refuter, never the source.

Legally: scraping personal emails for unsolicited bulk outreach breaches GDPR and CAN-SPAM style rules in many jurisdictions. That is a practical failure on top of the structural one.
