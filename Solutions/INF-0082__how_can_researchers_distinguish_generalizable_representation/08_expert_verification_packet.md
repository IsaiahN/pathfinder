# Expert verification packet: the Tether on generalizable versus training-only representations

**What this is:** a claim in a form a generalization researcher can check. Mode, derivation and refuters are stated before the conclusion, so a reader knows what to look for and what would settle it.

**Mode: `specified` on the separator and the per-domain verdicts, `general` on the structural shape, `gap` on the universal recipe. Not `grounded`.** Simulated derivation checked for internal consistency against the figures, not a ground reading. No model was trained and no measurement was taken.

## 1. The claim, in one paragraph

A training residual cannot witness generality. Any checkable separator must (1) index the residual per object slot rather than by one aggregate score, (2) use a held reading whose anchor does not move when the model's own prediction changes, (3) distinguish a reapplicable method from a recording of one occasion, (4) check typed relations between features rather than the feature list alone, (5) check reach inside a closure before importing a new atom, (6) buy evaluator independence in proportion to the cost of error, and (7) keep a lineage that separates convergent derivation from adopted import. This is a shape and a route, not a recipe. No figure line names an architecture, a sample count or a threshold that guarantees generalization; that is a declared gap. Six refuters were written before this packet.

## 2. Three checkable sub-claims

### C1. Framework-internal, checkable by reading Figures 1, 2 and 5

- **Statement:** training fit and compression achieved are frame-internal quantities and cannot serve as the anchor for a generality claim; a term that explains one occasion perfectly fails the acceptance bargain.
- **Source, verbatim:** Fig 1 :23 "The ground enters as the only metric." Fig 1 :27 "They are frame-internal. A frame cannot score itself with a quantity it also produces." Fig 2 :29 "An anchor is whatever settles the question and does not change its answer because of what any frame thinks." Fig 5 :44 "and so does a term that explains one occasion perfectly."
- **What to check:** is the anchor test applied to the metric, rather than assumed? Does the acceptance test price the statement plus the leftover, rather than the fit alone?
- **Refuter:** exhibit a metric produced by the learning frame itself that does not update on its own outputs and settles the cross-habitat residual at every slot; or a term that explains one occasion perfectly whose description length on an independent ground is also small and whose per-slot residual on a new habitat stays low.
- **Status:** `specified`, `triangulated` (Fig 1 and Fig 2, plus Fig 5). One live strain: benign overfitting, S2 in `01`, recorded rather than hidden.

### C2. Probe-facing, checkable by a researcher against their own system

- **Statement:** the discriminating probe is an isomer test. Hold the atom set fixed, change the bond or the order, and read the residual per slot in a habitat whose ground does not update. A method crosses; a recording of one occasion can only be replayed.
- **Source, verbatim:** Fig 12 :117 "isomer. Same atoms, different bond, different substance. The difference is invisible in the formula and decisive in the behaviour." Fig 4 :22 "Only methods cross up. Only head starts come down." Fig 4 :25 "A recording carried upward looks like knowledge and is a description of one occasion." Fig 11 :70 "A test harness is a substituted habitat. Which is why a synthetic solve proves wiring and never capability."
- **What to check:** does the probe actually substitute a habitat with a different closure, or just re-sample the same one? Does the representation carry the arrangement, or only the ingredient list? Does the held reading come from an anchor that does not update?
- **Refuter:** a verbatim recording of one training occasion, with a lineage showing a lookup and no carried method, that predicts the shape of residuals at new slots in a new habitat as well as a method-carrying representation; or a flat ingredient-only representation that distinguishes two arrangements with identical ingredients at a new slot without extra actions on order.
- **Status:** `specified`, `triangulated` (Fig 4, Fig 11, Fig 12).

### C3. Evaluation-facing, checkable by anyone who runs a benchmark

- **Statement:** agreement from a shared evidence pool is a convention, not a verdict. Independence decides both whether agreement means anything and whether a union of two evaluation frames has surplus. The lineage, not the contents, separates convergent derivation from adopted import. No figure line returns the universal recipe.
- **Source, verbatim:** Fig 2 :40 "disqualifying for a verdict: agreement about the world is worthless when the agreeing frames share an evidence pool" Fig 8 :51 "Convergent derivation and adopted import look identical in the contents. Only the record of where each came from separates them" Fig 9 :32 "you get a kind of thing, never the answer itself" Fig 13 :134 "Unreachable is a verdict here, not a silence"
- **What to check:** do the two evaluations draw on the same corpus, the same preprocessing and the same modeler choices? Is there a blinded, swapped or independently instrumented reading? Is the provenance of each binding recorded?
- **Refuter:** two evaluations sharing one evidence pool predict external-validity failures as well as two blinded evaluations from independent instruments; or a figure line names the architecture or sample count that guarantees generalization, which would close the gap.
- **Status:** `specified` on independence and provenance, `gap` on the recipe. `triangulated` across Fig 2, Fig 8, Fig 9 and Fig 13.

## 3. What is explicitly not claimed

- Not a universal generalization recipe, no named architecture, sample count, regularizer or threshold.
- Not that a held-out accuracy score is worthless. It counts when the anchor does not update and the closures differ.
- Not that every perfect fit is memorization. Benign overfitting is recorded as a live strain.
- Not that generalization requires a new atom. Most generalization is a new arrangement over held atoms (Fig 13 :115 "The set is closed. The arrangements are not. That gap is why import exists.").
- Not that the framework can name the missing primitive (Fig 13 :135 "It cannot say which primitive is missing, because naming it would mean already holding it.").
- Not `grounded`. No probe was run by this agent.
- Not a claim that a famous leaderboard position settles anything. Prestige funds a trial; it does not settle an action.

## 5. What would settle it

1. **Isomer probe:** same atoms, different bond or order, per-slot residual on a non-updating ground.
2. **Method-versus-recording test:** run the same probe with and without the lineage record and compare.
3. **Independence test:** shared-corpus evaluation versus blinded swapped evaluation.
4. **Round trip:** carry the method up, bring it down in the new habitat, measure `R_T` instead of assuming zero (Fig 4 :30 "So take the round trip and measure the gap. Without it you are navigating by dead reckoning: confident about a position nobody checked.").
5. **Any of the six refuters in `01_figure_grounded_audit.md` section 5 firing.**

## 6. What an expert can check in twenty minutes

1. Read Fig 1 :23, Fig 1 :27, Fig 2 :29 and Fig 5 :44. Four short passages.
2. Read Fig 12 :117 and Fig 4 :22/:25. Decide whether your own favorite benchmark is a substituted habitat or a re-sample of the same one.
3. Name one probe in your field that holds the feature vocabulary fixed and changes the relation. Say why it would or would not separate a method from a lookup.
4. Send the refutation, or the probe result. A failure is more useful than an endorsement.
