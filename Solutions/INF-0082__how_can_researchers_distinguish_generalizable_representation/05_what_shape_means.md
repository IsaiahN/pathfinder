## 1. A shape is a constraint you can state before you can name it

From *The Library of Babel*, opened this session: "You can cut your search short if the set of books returned is small and descriptive enough that it provides a general shape of the answer that satisfies your question." Also: "With each new term gained, you are able to narrow the space by definition and deepen your vocabulary." And the stopping rule: "you will know that you are done because the finish line will also be the starting place".

For INF-0082 the shape is **not** a list of techniques (cross-validation, regularization, early stopping, bigger data). Those are names drawn from one evidence pool. The shape is the set of constraints any separator must satisfy:

- residual indexed per slot, not averaged (Fig 1 :19, :22);
- a held reading whose anchor does not update on the model's own outputs (Fig 2 :29-30);
- a method carried across habitats, not a recording of one occasion (Fig 4 :22-26);
- bonds, not just ingredients (Fig 12 :5, :94);
- reach checked against closure, with SEARCHED / INWARD / OUTWARD kept distinct (Formula step 7);
- independence bought in proportion to the cost of being wrong (Fig 2 :42);
- lineage that separates convergent derivation from adopted import (Fig 8 :51).

That is a shape. "Use a 10-fold split with early stopping" is a name, and the framework cannot derive it.

## 2. How the shape is obtained

*The Library of Babel* gives the method: use the vocabulary you already have that is "closest to the words that you wish to describe and grasp the shape of," look for recurring terms that "overlap, correlate, or are even used interchangeably," and recurse. Applied here: the ordinary word "generalizes" is too vague, so it is re-grounded in the effect terms above and then enriched one term at a time (slot, anchor, method, bond, closure, independence, provenance).

Fig 6 :30: "Cast it in effect terms." Fig 9 :30-31: "your words are borrowed too, so describe the effect rather than the cause." The effect here is *what changes contact*, not *what the weights look like*.

*Decoding Tacit Knowledge* (quote carried from the exemplar): "When contact isn't possible, the best you can send is a plain description of what you'd expect to see, because anyone can check that against the world." That is the two-stage offer in `02_outreach_email.md`: send the expected failure pattern first, the sources only after.

## 3. What would license a name

Only two things, both outside the shape:

1. **The ground settles it.** Formula step 5: "the ground settles it. Verified only by what pays. A gate is not the ground." The canonical README: the framework "stops at the limit of what the available instruments can distinguish, and records the lineage so the stop is honest."
2. **A provenanced import from a frame whose closure differs** (Fig 6 :23, Fig 8 :50-52), with a source gate and a shadow test.

*They're Scanning Books When They Should* (quote carried from the exemplar): "The real import is not a new term in the existing space. It is a new dimension of the space." Also: "Declaring the primitives does not make them neutral. It makes the choice visible." So a claim that a particular architecture is *the* generalizable one turns a visible choice into an invisible one.

## 4. Generalization as a source-discrimination problem

*The Hard Problem is About Source*, opened this session, gives the mechanism that bears most directly on F2 and F9. An instrument reads green and "cannot comment on the source." Several sources can produce the same reading; "*If several paths create the same behavior, then behavior is a projection with lossy information*." The resolution came from an instrument, the spectrometer, "not by argument."

Applied here: two representations can produce the same accuracy reading and differ in source (a method versus a lookup, a convergent derivation versus an adopted import). Accuracy is a metamerism-prone instrument. What is owed is a *discriminating probe*, not more accuracy. Fig 3 :27: "A link with no instrument is not a link that works. It is a link nobody has looked at."

The same essay supplies the honest limit: the answer is "unreached until a better instrument exists." That is exactly the F9 gap, reported as a verdict.

## 5. Correction to my own first reading

I initially wrote that the framework's "shape, not a name" rule forbids naming any mechanism. That overstates it. The canonical phrase is "what a **missing** thing would have to look like (a shape, not a name)"; the subject is a primitive not held in the library. Fig 13 :135 says naming a missing primitive would mean already holding it.

Here the candidate mechanisms (bonds, closure, anchors, provenance) are already in the library. Naming them is a **binding** from a world frame onto held atoms, not an invented seventh term. What the figures still refuse is naming an architecture, sample count or universal threshold, because no figure line holds one and no ground settled one. Both claims are recorded so neither is quietly lost.

## 6. Prestige is not action

*The Recursive Self-Improvement Trap*, opened this session: "Prestige must never be allowed to validate an action directly." And: "Prestige can fund an action. It can give an action a budget to be tried. But it cannot settle an action. Only the ground can settle it. The Ground Maintainer must be blind to prestige." The essay also records the failure mode: the highest-prestige agent "would monopolize compute and repeat its past success, and the system would stop exploring."

For INF-0082 a famous leaderboard position or a famous author's endorsement is a budget and a convention, not a verdict on generality. The public contact points in `03_public_contact_points.csv` are needed to *try* the claim, not to endorse it. Fig 2 :35: "Alignment is triangulation, not negotiation. A mirror cannot correct you."

## 7. My own audit is a simulation, not a ground reading

Same essay, verbatim: "LLMs can quickly simulate an approximate answer/output with the formula found in the architecture post. It will often be better than the generic outputs that you will usually get otherwise too (intelligence is essentially arrangement). However, the truly testable output would need to be derived from the real architecture or working the formula yourself."

So `01_figure_grounded_audit.md`, this file and `09_watson_answer.md` are simulated derivations checked for internal consistency against the figures. They are **not** tests of any representation and cannot settle the question they report on. The only thing that could move a claim from `candidate` to `grounded` is a source-discriminating probe with a non-updating anchor and a recorded refuter.
