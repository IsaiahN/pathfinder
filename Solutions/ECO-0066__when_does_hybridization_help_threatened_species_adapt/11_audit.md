## Round trip gap R_T

The Symbols table defines `R_T` as the gap between what was sent up and what came back down, `gap(x, (T_E ∘ T_A)(x))`. Fig 4 :30 "So take the round trip and measure the gap. 3. **Concretisation (T_E):** a management verdict on a named threatened population, for example "introduce donors from lineage D into population P".

**R_T is nonzero.** Five things the shorthand holds do not survive the trip back down without additional work:

- the **identity of the residual in a real case**, which requires a per-slot measurement the figures do not supply;
- **donor provenance and introgression direction**, which determine whether the union is aimed at all;
- the **shadow test**, that the donor atom explains the adaptive gap rather than merely correlating with ancestry;
- the **distinctiveness cutoff**, which is a convention and is absent from the figures (F6);
- the **review independence setting**, which the figures price but do not instantiate.

Two things survive cleanly: the two-residual split, and the rule that an import needs a gate before the boundary moves. That is the residue worth sending. `R_T` was **not** measured numerically, because no ground reading exists; it is recorded qualitatively as the enumerated set of dropped constraints. Treating the condensation as identical to the figures would set `R_T` to zero and would be the dead-reckoning error Fig 4 :30 names.
