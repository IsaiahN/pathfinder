# Expert verification packet: hybridization, adaptation, and distinctiveness

**What this is:** a claim in a form a conservation geneticist, field ecologist, or wildlife manager can check. Mode, derivation, and refuters are stated before the conclusion so the reader knows what to look for and what would settle it.

**Mode: `specified` on the branch conditions, `general` on the structural rule, not `grounded`.** No measurement at any population is claimed. This is a classifier and a route, not a result.

**Status of this packet:** a simulated derivation checked for internal consistency against the figures, not a ground reading. It cannot settle the ecological question it reports on.

## 1. The claim, in one paragraph

Hybridization has no single valence. It acts on two residuals that must be scored separately per population: adaptive capacity and boundary integrity. Three union types separate the reported outcomes. A high-overlap blend replaces ancestry without importing adaptive variation and loses distinctiveness. An undirected union has no described residual, so nothing can be scored. A low-overlap union described against a stated residual can cross a heritable adaptive allele back, and that is the branch where hybridization helps. Any such import needs a provenance gate and a shadow test before the lineage boundary moves, and the receiving habitat decides whether the allele is selected. Prevention is habitat substitution, not a neutral baseline, and an established introgression cannot be subtracted. The numeric cutoff for distinctiveness is a declared convention, not a figure-derived result.

## 2. Three checkable sub-claims

### C1 - Two residuals, not one verdict (framework internal, checkable by reading Figure 11 and Figure 1)

- **Statement:** the effect of gene flow is relative to the residual being scored. Help and threat are separate verdicts and must not be averaged.
- **Source, verbatim:** Fig 11 :49 "An actor's valence is relative to the residual, not a property it carries." Fig 11 :51 "No standing list of good actors survives a change of residual." Fig 1 :19 "The residual is indexed by object slot, not aggregated."
- **Refuter:** a per-population measurement that settles both residuals on one axis with no declared convention. If such a metric exists and is used before interventions, this sub-claim is refuted and F1 must be re-censused.

### C2 - Union type discriminates the branches (biology facing, checkable against case records)

- **Statement:** the blend branch loses the boundary without adaptive gain; the undirected branch cannot be scored because nothing was aimed; the triangulated branch can cross an adaptive atom back.
- **Source, verbatim:** Fig 8 :14 "their union adds nothing"; Fig 8 :21 "nothing was aimed; the union is mute"; Fig 8 :26 "2: the atom crosses back"; Fig 8 :45 "Two frames together reach what neither reaches alone."
- **What to check:** for a case you know, can you identify (a) a described residual stated before the contact, (b) donor and recipient provenance, (c) direction and timing of introgression, and (d) a heritable fitness effect in the recipient habitat? If (a) or (c) is missing, the case belongs in the undirected branch by definition and cannot be used as evidence for either outcome.
- **Refuter:** a documented high-overlap blend in which the boundary persists and fitness rises with no aimed intervention, or a documented low-overlap aimed union that produces no heritable adaptive atom. Either breaks the mapping.

### C3 - Gates and habitat decide, prevention is substitution (framework internal plus ecology facing)

- **Statement:** an introgression is an import. It needs a source gate and a shadow test before the boundary moves; the habitat decides what the allele becomes; isolation substitutes a habitat rather than removing contact.
- **Source, verbatim:** Fig 6 :23 "No frame escapes its own closure. The bound moves only on contact with a frame whose closure differs." Fig 6 :12 "source gate: provenance" and Fig 6 :13 "shadow test: explains a gap". Fig 4 :34 "A seed carries no small tree: it carries what will grow one, and the conditions it lands in decide what it becomes." Fig 11 :66 "Isolation is not removal of the habitat. It is substitution of one habitat for another."
- **Refuter:** a demonstrated reversal of established introgression in the wild without habitat substitution or further import; or an adaptive allele whose fitness effect is identical across two habitats with enumerated, different selection regimes. Either breaks this sub-claim.

## 3. What is explicitly not claimed

- Not that admixture is always a rescue, nor that it always causes extinction. Both universals are refused before minting.
- Not that the Florida panther case is a general rule. It is one ground reading with stated preconditions (small inbred population, historical gene flow, a single aimed event, concurrent habitat protection).
- Not a number for distinctiveness. Fig 10 :18 "a declared meaning is not a grounded one." The cutoff is a convention that must be declared and kept from deciding the outcome.
- Not that a hybrid index or ancestry proportion identifies a union history. It is a reading that cannot comment on its source.
- Not `grounded`. Nothing here was settled by a measurement taken in this derivation.
- Not that this packet settles any real population's management decision. That requires a per-slot ground reading.

## 5. What would settle it

A source-discriminating instrument, not more agreement.

1. **Provenance discriminator:** donor identity, timing, direction, and continuity of introgression, recorded before the fitness comparison.
2. **Per-slot fitness contrast:** survival or reproduction in the actual habitat, with second-order habitat contact enumerated.
3. **Declared boundary metric:** a distinctiveness threshold fixed before the intervention and independent of the desired decision.
4. **Atom test:** does the donor-derived allele explain the adaptive gap (shadow test), or is it only correlated with ancestry?

## 6. What an expert can check in twenty minutes

1. Read Fig 11 :49, :51 and Fig 1 :19 (three short passages). Is the two-residual split faithful to how you would score a case?
2. Read Fig 8 :14, :21, :26, :45. For one population you know, name its union branch and say which of the four pieces of evidence in C2 are missing.
3. Name one instrument that would distinguish a provenanced single aimed introgression from continuous unmonitored gene flow, or say why none exists.
4. Send the refutation. A failure is more useful than an endorsement. Do not send agreement before the verdict on step 2, and note that later agreement is not pooled as corroboration.
