**Restatement as an effect.** A threatened population has an adaptive shortfall in a changing habitat. Gene flow from a second lineage is a candidate intervention. One branch increases fitness while the recipient lineage stays distinguishable; the other erases the boundary, sometimes with demographic cost. The decision is which branch a given case is on, measured per population rather than averaged across a species range. An answer would look like a condition set that separates the two branches and a stated measurement that would settle the branch in any new case. That expected answer is bound here as an ordinary term and made refutable in `01_figure_grounded_audit.md` section 4.

## What is deliberately not here

- No personal emails of named individuals.
- No ranking of people by how likely they are to agree with the shape claim.
- No bulk send plan.

Three reasons, in the framework's own words:

1. A crowd is not an anchor. Fig 2 :35: "Alignment is triangulation, not negotiation. A mirror cannot correct you." Selecting recipients for agreement manufactures the correlation that makes their agreement worthless.
2. Agreement from one evidence pool is one witness. Fig 2 :40: "disqualifying for a verdict: agreement about the world is worthless when the agreeing frames share an evidence pool." All four world sources are drawn from one broad biological literature and are declared as such in `09_watson_answer.md`.
3. Asking a checker to absorb the framework before giving a verdict spends the only thing the checker contributes. Fig 10 :42: "A verifier that can reconstruct the claim cannot verify it."

Legally: public institutional channels avoid the GDPR and CAN-SPAM style failures that come with scraping personal addresses for unsolicited bulk outreach.
