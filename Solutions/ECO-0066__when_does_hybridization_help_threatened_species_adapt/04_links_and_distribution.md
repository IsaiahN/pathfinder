## What the wider literature adds

1. **The two-branch split is not imposed by the framework.** Todesco et al. 2016 open with both outcomes and conclude that "extinction risk is highly situation dependent". Their survey found genetic swamping far more frequent than demographic swamping. That is a warning about the blend branch, not a denial of the rescue branch.
2. **Genetic rescue has a narrower empirical footprint than its advocacy.** In the same survey, "Genetic rescue was a rare outcome", with one definitive case and two putative ones, and the authors note ascertainment bias from searching on "extinction" rather than "rescue". This is why this solution labels the rescue branch `specified` rather than treating it as a settled rate.
3. **A successful case is a single occasion.** Johnson et al. 2010 is a genuine ground reading with measured fitness gains, but it is one case with specific preconditions: small inbred population, historical gene flow between the two populations, a single aimed event, and concurrent habitat protection. Fig 5 :43 "A term that explains everything by saying nothing fails this, and so does a term that explains one occasion perfectly."
4. **Rate matters.** Hedrick 2013 places introgression between standing variation and new mutation as a source of adaptive variants. That is consistent with Fig 4 :34 "A seed carries no small tree: it carries what will grow one, and the conditions it lands in decide what it becomes." The allele is a head start, not a finished adaptation.
5. **The same process can move differentiation in either direction.** Abbott et al. 2013 report both barrier breakdown and adaptive introgression promoting divergence, with the outcome depending on which genomic regions are under selection. That is the Figure 8 union-type discriminator in biological vocabulary.

**Overlap warning.** These four sources come from one broad biological literature, and Todesco et al. cite Abbott, Hedrick, and Johnson. By Fig 2 :40 "disqualifying for a verdict: agreement about the world is worthless when the agreeing frames share an evidence pool." They corroborate the figure verdict but do not multiply its evidence. No low-overlap outside witness was obtained for the biological mapping, and this is recorded rather than concealed.

## Distribution: offer, do not push

- **Fig 10 :52 "Bring a characterised residual, never the source."** Lead with the two residuals and the refuter. Do not attach the framework summary to the first message.
- **Fig 2 :35 "Alignment is triangulation, not negotiation. A mirror cannot correct you."** Do not ask for agreement, trust, or endorsement. Ask what would count as a failure.
- **Fig 9 :27 "describe it before you go looking, or any frame you pick will seem to fit".** Ask for a branch verdict before naming Figure 8 or any figure at all.
- **Fig 2 :40** Record the verdict before the recipient sees the framework. Later agreement is never pooled as corroboration.
- **Fig 4 :22 "Only methods cross up. Only head starts come down."** The third stage sends the formula as a reusable method, with the instruction that each user states their own ground and refuter.

**Recommended order in a reply:** (1) the two-residual split and the classifier; (2) the question "what would you count as a failure here?"; (3) record the verdict; (4) only then the four world links and the canonical figure links, with the note that the figures, not this summary, are the authority.

## The two-stage offer

**Stage 1:** the recipient checks the classifier against a population they know and gives a branch verdict. No framework material is shown before that verdict is recorded.

**Stage 2 (opt-in):** if curious, they read the sources. The figure text is primary; the essays and journal articles are secondary.

**Stage 3 (opt-in):** the formula is offered as a method for other problems, with each user writing their own pre-registered refuter and stating their mode.

A third stage that sends a method is permitted (Fig 4 :22). A first stage that sends a conclusion is not.

**Hard precondition:** stages 1 and 2 work only if the stage-1 output survives. The output in `01_figure_grounded_audit.md` has a named gap (F6, the distinctiveness cutoff) and a named strain (the Figure 8 biological mapping). Those are reported in the packet, not repaired by assertion.
