## 1. A shape is a constraint, not a filler

The canonical usage rule: the framework reports "what a missing thing would have to look like (a shape, not a name)". Fig 9 :32 "you get a kind of thing, never the answer itself."

From *The Library of Babel*, opened this session: "you can cut your search short if the set of books returned is small and descriptive enough that it provides a general shape of the answer that satisfies your question."

For ECO-0066 the shape is: **a two-residual split, a three-way union classifier, a two-gate import test, and a habitat enumeration.** It constrains any acceptable answer about hybridization. It does not fill in a species, a threshold, or a case verdict. "Hybridization helps when X" is a name where the shape is what X must look like.

The same essay gives the reason this question needs unbundling rather than a single search: "Perhaps your problem was a bundle or conflation of several problems masquerading as one?" That is the essay-level counterpart of Fig 9 :54 "And when the split lands on a union rather than a partition, unbundle before you search again."

## 2. How the shape was obtained here

- *Library of Babel*: "you will have to use the existing vocabulary you do know that is closest to the words that you wish to describe and grasp the shape of." The nearest known vocabulary here was gene flow, introgression, swamping, rescue, and reproductive barriers. Recursing on proximity produced union type, import gate, and boundary persistence as the sharper terms.
- Fig 6 :30 "The description travels. Fails by being sharpened into terms only we can read. Cast it in effect terms." So the classifier is stated as effects on per-population fitness and boundary persistence, not as framework jargon.
- Fig 9 :31 "your words are borrowed too, so describe the effect rather than the cause." The classifier does not say "triangulation is occurring"; it says what would be observed if it were.
- *Library of Babel* on stopping: "you know that you are done because the finish line will also be the starting place." Here the stop is when the shape returns only what was put into the residual, and the remaining gap (the distinctiveness cutoff) is named rather than filled.

## 3. What licenses a name in this question

Only two things, both outside the shape:

1. **The ground settles it.** `THE_FORMULA.md` step 5: "the ground settles it. Verified only by what pays. A gate is not the ground." A per-population fitness and ancestry measurement would settle a branch call.
2. **A provenanced import from a frame whose closure differs**, with a source gate and a shadow test (Fig 6 :23, Fig 8 :51). A donor allele with traced origin and a demonstrated role in closing the adaptive gap is licensed in this sense.

The cutoff for "distinctiveness" is licensed by neither. It is a convention, and Fig 10 :18 "a declared meaning is not a grounded one" applies. The honest move is to declare it before the case and keep it from deciding the outcome (Fig 10 :47).

## 4. The source error this question invites

*The Hard Problem is About Source*, opened this session, gives the mechanism. A color instrument reads green and "cannot comment on the source": one green light, or yellow plus blue, or RGB, or CMYK, or a scheme not yet discovered. The essay states: "If several paths create the same behavior, then behavior is a projection with lossy information" and "An instrument that can't distinguish these cases demonstrates its own inadequacy". It was the spectrometer, not an argument, that resolved the case.

Mapped here: an ancestry proportion of 0.25 in a threatened population is a **reading**. It cannot comment on the source. It is consistent with a single aimed introgression event, with continuous unmonitored gene flow, with a blend, or with an undirected swarm. Naming the source from the reading is the metamerism error. What is owed is a discriminator: provenance records, the direction of introgression, the timing of the event, and a fitness effect measured per slot. The shape tells you what the discriminator must distinguish; it does not supply it.

## 5. Prestige is not action in this field

From *The Recursive Self-Improvement Trap*, opened this session: "Prestige can fund an action. It can give an action a budget to be tried. But it cannot settle an action. Only the ground can settle it. The Ground Maintainer must be blind to prestige."

A prominent review or a famous case does not settle a branch call. It gives the question a budget to be tried. The essay adds that "Metatheories cannot validate themselves", which is why this folder's own verdict is a `candidate`, not a ground reading.

## 6. Correction to my own reading

I initially treated the Figure 8 union map as if it directly named biological outcomes: blend equals swamping, triangulation equals rescue. *The Hard Problem is About Source* forces a correction. Those are source claims read off a projection. A high hybrid index can arise from more than one union history. The mapping is retained as a `specified` hypothesis with a refuter, not as an identification. Both the earlier reading and this correction are kept visible here.

A second correction: I treated the distinctiveness cutoff as a mission-critical unknown the framework should resolve. The canonical README says the framework returns "a shape, not a name", and `60_FIGURES_AS_METALAWS.md` says the figures are the ground for design questions while Isaiah is the anchor for what the figures genuinely cannot settle. So the cutoff is a `gap` on a number, not a failure of the derivation. The shape of the missing thing is still reportable.

## 7. My own audit is a simulation, not a ground reading

Same essay: "LLMs can quickly simulate an approximate answer/output with the formula found in the architecture post" and "the truly testable output would need to be derived from the real architecture or working the formula yourself."

So `01_figure_grounded_audit.md` is a simulated derivation checked for internal consistency against the figures. It is **not** a test of the ecological claim and must be labelled as such. It cannot settle anything it reports on.
