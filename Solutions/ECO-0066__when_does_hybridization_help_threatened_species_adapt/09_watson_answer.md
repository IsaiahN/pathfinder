## VERDICT (from the figures)

The shape survives; no universal branch verdict does. Hybridization is neither a rescue nor a threat in general. It is an import into a recipient closure whose effect is relative to the residual scored (adaptive capacity versus boundary integrity), per population. A high-overlap blend removes distinctiveness without adaptive gain; an undirected union cannot be scored because no residual was described; a low-overlap, aimed union with a traced donor and a gated atom can help adaptation. The habitat decides whether the atom is selected. Prevention is habitat substitution, not removal of contact, and an established introgression cannot be subtracted. The numeric distinctiveness cutoff is a `gap` reported as a shape rather than filled with a name. This is a `specified`, ungrounded result; the biological mapping of the three union panels is a stated, refutable fit.

- **cost if wrong:** a lineage can be lost through genetic swamping, or adaptation can be foregone through an unnecessarily substituted habitat. Both are ground-level losses, not merely analytical errors. Fig 2 :42 "Buy independence in proportion to the cost of being wrong." Because the cost can be irreversible, reviewers must come from different closures. That cost is partly avoidable by leading with the classifier and its refuter.
- **undoable:** asymmetrically. Deciding against an introduction can be reversed; an established introgression cannot be subtracted (Fig 11 :61 "The seat's power is asymmetric. Introduction is available; exclusion is not."). A wrong branch call that erases a boundary is not undoable by later agreement. A wrong call that delays an intervention costs time and habitat, which is costly but recoverable.
- **reversed by:** a ground reading, not agreement. A per-population measurement combining fitness, traced donor provenance, direction and continuity of introgression, and a boundary metric declared before the event would name the branch and could break any finding here. Specifically: an aimed low-overlap union that loses the boundary without warning breaks the triangulated branch; a blend that preserves the boundary and raises fitness breaks the blend branch; a convention-free distinctiveness metric breaks the gap; a field reversal of established introgression breaks the substitution claim; habitat-independent outcomes break the habitat dependence. Until one of these fires, the verdict remains `candidate`.
