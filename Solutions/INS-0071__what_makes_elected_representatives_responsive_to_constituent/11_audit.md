## Independent domain census

Using WATSON order: R by arity, symmetry and scale; one line per domain with actors, currency and anchor; partition or union; order by dependency.

- **R (arity / symmetry / scale).** The residual is the observed gap between constituency want and representative action under competing pressures. Arity: four principals. Symmetry: broken; concentrated and diffuse signals have different amplitude per slot, so the relation is a threshold comparison, not a symmetric join. Scale: first order at the representative, second order at the institution. This is a union, not a partition: one policy case can be in the donor condition, the party condition and the interest-group condition at once.
- **Domain 1, constituency responsiveness.** Actors: representative, constituents, independent instruments. Currency: votes and congruence. Anchor: constituency preference and resulting policy, measured independently. Legitimate, because the question is constitutively about it.
- **Domain 2, elite and interest-group influence.** Actors: donors, party, organised interests. Currency: contributions, pressure, information. Anchor: measured impact on the same policy outcomes. Unioned with Domain 1; not an independent witness, because the outcome data overlap.
- **Domain 3, verification.** Actors: expert readers and the two seats. Currency: agreement and residual. Anchor: the ground, never agreement. Independence is zero in this run.
- **Habitat, not a domain.** Electoral rules, boundaries, primaries, finance and disclosure rules, staff, polls, media, oversight and their contacts. No anchor of its own, so it is not admitted as a fourth domain.

Order by dependency: bind the sought-for shape as an ordinary term before the run (Fig 5 :65); describe the residual before naming a literature (Fig 9 :27); refuse the agent-only option before minting reform (Formula step 2); settle first-order congruence before second-order selection (Fig 12 :104).

## Round trip gap R_T

The Symbols table defines R_T as the gap between what was sent up and what came back down, `gap(x, (T_E ∘ T_A)(x))`, measuring what the coarser description cannot hold. Fig 4 :30 requires it to be measured rather than assumed. I ran the round trip on the condensation this folder actually permits.

1. **Sent up (x):** the verbatim figure set, chiefly Fig 1 :19 and :27, Fig 2 :29 and :40, Fig 9 :55 and :56, Fig 10 :24, Fig 11 :40, Fig 12 :112.
2. **Abstraction (T_A):** the shorthand used in `07_the_tether_answer.md`, that responsiveness needs an unmovable anchor, per-slot signal, a maintained channel, habitat change, union splitting and selection between rooms, with no named reform.
3. **Concretisation (T_E):** the concrete policy package a reader is likely to build from that shorthand, for example public financing, independent redistricting, stronger disclosure and more constituent surveys.

**R_T is nonzero.** Four things the shorthand holds do not survive the trip back down:

- the **union condition**. A package of levers can be treated as one big reform, which is exactly the collapse Fig 9 :56 warns about; the intersections are not automatically cleared;
- the **per-slot rule**. Constituent surveys are easily aggregated into a single satisfaction score, which loses the district signal (Fig 1 :22);
- the **no-self-scoring rule**. A survey commissioned by the representative's own office becomes an interested keeper of the reading (Fig 10 :93);
- the **channel-decay rule**. Installing a survey or an oversight body is not maintaining it; the ground does not decay but the reading does (Fig 10 :24);
- the **shape-not-a-name limit**. The package is a binding from the empirical frame, not a figure-derived answer (Fig 9 :32).

One thing survives cleanly: agent-only improvement is insufficient and contact must change (Fig 11 :40). That is the residue worth sending. R_T was **not** measured numerically, because no ground reading exists; it is recorded qualitatively as the enumerated set of dropped constraints. Treating "the formula is the condensed version of the metalaws" as identity would set R_T to zero and would be the dead-reckoning error Fig 4 :30 names. The honest form is a lossy condensation with a recorded gap, which is what `09_watson_answer.md` states.
