## Relevance to the shape claim

1. **The measurement ground exists.** Gilens and Page supply an instrument that separates elite, interest-group and citizen influence on 1,779 policy issues. That is the kind of reading Fig 2 :29 calls an anchor: it does not change because a political frame thinks otherwise.
2. **The collective-action condition is real and is not the only one.** Olson supplies the concentrated-minority mechanism. The Wikipedia account also records Lohmann's information asymmetry and Trumbull's legitimacy objection. Three CONDITIONS, not one lever, which is what Fig 9 :55 predicts for a union compound.
3. **The vocabulary of "responsiveness" is measurable.** The representation page records the accountability view and Pitkin's standard: the quality of substantive representation is assessed by the representative's responsiveness to evolving citizen needs. That is the effect term to bind before the run.
4. **The essays govern shape, contact and drift, not the empirical answer.** They are all by the framework author, so their agreement with the figures is high-overlap corroboration, not an independent witness (Fig 2 :40).

## The two-stage offer

**Stage 1.** Send the residual in effect terms and the refuter. No theory. No request for trust, agreement or endorsement.

**Stage 2, opt-in only.** If the reader is curious, offer the figure extract and the empirical sources. Their stage-1 verdict is already recorded, so later agreement is never pooled as corroboration (Fig 2 :40).

**Stage 3, opt-in only.** Offer the formula as a method to reuse, with the instruction that each user writes their own refuter and states their own mode. Fig 4 :22 "Only methods cross up. Only head starts come down."

**Hard precondition:** stages 1 to 3 only work if the stage-1 shape survives. If a refuter fires, stop and retract before any further distribution.
