## 1. A shape is a constraint, not a filler

The figures README states the limit: the framework returns *"what a missing thing would have to look like (a shape, not a name)."* `Fig 9 :32`: *"you get a kind of thing, never the answer itself."* Figure 13 :134-135: *"So the system can report that it lacks something rather than producing an answer anyway. Unreachable is a verdict here, not a silence,"* *"It cannot say which primitive is missing, because naming it would mean already holding it."*

From *The Library of Babel* (opened this session): *"you can cut your search short if the set of books returned is small and descriptive enough that it provides a general shape of the answer that satisfies your question."*

So a **shape** is the narrowed form of an answer you can state before you can name it. It constrains; it does not fill. For EAR-0008, the shape is the five-part separator in `01` section 6. A list of named interactions with rank numbers is a name, not a shape.

## 2. How a shape is obtained

- *Library of Babel*: use the vocabulary you already have that is closest to the thing, look for recurring new terms that overlap or correlate with it, recurse. *"With each new term gained, you narrow the space and deepen your vocabulary."*
- `Fig 6 :30`: *"The description travels. Fails by being sharpened into terms only we can read. Cast it in effect terms."* `Fig 9 :30-31`: *"your words are borrowed too, so describe the effect rather than the cause."*
- *Decoding Tacit Knowledge* (carried): *"When contact isn't possible, the best you can send is a plain description of what you'd expect to see, because anyone can check that against the world."*
- *Library of Babel* on stopping: *"you know that you are done because the finish line will also be the starting place"*; the paradox is that by then you have *"composed the answer through the process of locating it."*
- *Library of Babel* on malformed questions: *"Perhaps your problem was a bundle or conflation of several problems masquerading as one? You will need to search all of those problems individually to find their answers and then combine them in a satisfactory way."* This is exactly the EAR-0008 census: four scale questions plus a definition question, treated as a union.

## 3. What licenses a name

Only two things, both outside the shape itself:

1. **The ground settles it.** Formula step 5: *"the ground settles it. Verified only by what pays."* The README: the framework *"stops at the limit of what the available instruments can distinguish, and records the lineage so the stop is honest."*
2. **A provenanced import from a frame whose closure differs** (`Fig 6 :23`, `Fig 8 :50-52`), with a source gate and a shadow test.

*They're Scanning Books* (carried) sharpens this: *"The real import is not a new term in the existing space. It is a new dimension of the space."* And: *"Declaring the primitives does not make them neutral. It makes the choice visible."*

For this question, naming atmosphere, ocean, ice, land and ecosystems as the interacting components is a binding from the Earth-system frame onto already-available frames. It is not the invention of a seventh primitive and it is not forbidden. What would be forbidden is presenting a chosen currency or a chosen scale cut as if the framework derived it.

## 4. Why the literal ranking is the specific forbidden error

*The Hard Problem is About Source* (carried) gives the mechanism. Metamerism: an instrument reads green and *"cannot comment on the source"*; one green light, or yellow plus blue, or RGB, or CMYK, or a scheme not yet discovered. The reading does not determine the source. *"If several paths create the same behavior, then behavior is a projection with lossy information."* And: *"An instrument that can't distinguish these cases demonstrates its own inadequacy."* It was the spectrometer, not an argument, that resolved it.

Applied here: a measured "strong interaction" is a reading. Several mechanisms can produce the same reading. Assigning a rank from that reading alone is the metamerism error. What is owed is a source-discriminating instrument, not a name. The world literature already supplies the proto-instruments: the 0.5 degree indirect-effect maps of Zoller et al. 2026, the teleconnection correlation matrices, the tipping-element threshold tables, and the biomagnification factors. They are readings to sharpen (`Fig 6 :32`), not a ranking to assert.

*The Recursive Self-Improvement Trap* (opened): *"the output is only as good as the validator. The agent, or the tool, or the frame, or the theory, cannot validate itself."*

## 5. Q: What licenses a particular interaction in the top slot? ANSWERED

**Nothing in the framework.** The framework returns a shape. A particular interaction is licensed only by the ground settling a minted term, or by a provenanced import with a shadow test. Until one of those, the name is the searcher's own contribution and must be recorded as such. `Fig 11 :51` is categorical: *"No standing list of good actors survives a change of residual."*

Does the question commit the error the README forbids? At the level of the request, yes: it asks for names where the framework returns a constraint, and it asks for names that would hold across four different scale frames. The correct form is the separator with an empty result column until a named residual, currency and anchor are supplied.

## 5b. Correction to my own sections 3 and 4

I overstated the "shape, not a name" rule in the first pass. The README's phrase is *"what a **missing** thing would have to look like (a shape, not a name)."* The subject is a primitive not held in the frame. The components of the Earth system are already held in the Earth-system frame; naming them is a binding, not the invention of a new primitive. What remains wrong is calling such a binding an import that moves the wall. `Fig 6 :25` says *"An import adds no atom to the world."* The binding adds no atom either. Both claims are now recorded so neither is quietly lost.

My section 4 metamerism point is **not** retracted: the source of a strength reading is still underdetermined by the reading. But the reason is the missing discriminator, not a ban on naming held components.

## 6. Two consequences for outreach

**Prestige is not action.** From *The Recursive Self-Improvement Trap* (opened): *"Prestige must never be allowed to validate an action directly... Someone let prestige stand in for proof... Prestige can fund an action. It can give an action a budget to be tried. But it cannot settle an action. Only the ground can settle it. The Ground Maintainer must be blind to prestige."* A campaign that gathers famous Earth-system scientists to agree with a ranking is the named collapse, not corroboration. The legitimate ask is the opposite: give the separator a budget to be tried, and let the ground, an instrument, a dataset or an experiment, settle it.

**My own audit is a simulation, not a ground reading.** Same essay: *"LLMs can quickly simulate an approximate answer/output with the formula found in the architecture post... However, the truly testable output would need to be derived from the real architecture or working the formula yourself."* So `01`, `06`, `07`, `09` and `11` are simulated derivations checked for internal consistency against the figures. They are **not** tests of the claim and must be labelled as such. They cannot settle anything they report on.

## 7. What shape means specifically for EAR-0008

For this question the shape has five constraints and one empty slot:

| constraint | figure | what it rules out |
|---|---|---|
| Per-slot residual stated first | `Fig 1 :19`, `Fig 9 :27-28` | a ranking computed before the outcome is named |
| Habitat enumerated, including second and third-order contacts | `Fig 11 :43`, `Fig 11 :52-54` | a first-order-only contact list |
| Independent non-updating anchor at the scale in question | `Fig 2 :29`, `Fig 2 :35` | a consensus survey or a self-scoring model |
| Declared currency and ordered comparison, two currencies held apart | `Fig 12 :87`, `Fig 12 :125` | a single score that adds carbon, water, albedo and correlation |
| Round-trip gap measured across the scale membrane | `Fig 4 :30` | treating local and global readings as commensurable |

**The empty slot** is the ordered result. It stays empty until a ground pays for a term. `Fig 9 :32`: *"you get a kind of thing, never the answer itself."* `Fig 13 :134`: *"Unreachable is a verdict here, not a silence,"* and the gap here is of the shape-not-a-name kind, not the unreachable-in-principle kind, because conditional per-outcome rankings do exist in the world literature.

## 8. Stop rule

The loop stops when the residual stops arriving, which is either a perfect prediction or a closed channel, and from inside those look the same (`THE_FORMULA.md` "Closure"). Here the residual stops at the missing source-discriminating instrument. The honest report is a narrowed shape, a recorded lineage, and a named instrument that would close it. *"Indiscernibility is not identity."*
