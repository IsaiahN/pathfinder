# Solution EAR-0008

**Question:** Which Earth-system interactions are strongest at local, regional, continental, or global scales?

**Source:** `Questions/earth systems.txt` line 12

**Section:** 1. Earth as a coupled system

**Contact field:** earth system interactions research

**Mode:** `specified` on the per-domain separator and verdicts, `general` on the structural shape, `gap` on the literal ranking of interactions by scale. Nothing here is `grounded`.

**Status:** done

**Registry status:** pending (set by the registry pass, not by this folder)

---

## What this folder is

A figure-grounded, derive-first package for one open question. It states the residual in effect terms, runs a domain census before choosing any figure line, quotes the figures verbatim with `Fig N :line`, keeps verdicts per domain separate, writes a pre-registered refuter before the conclusion, and records world sources that were actually opened. It is a **simulated derivation checked for internal consistency against the figures**, not a ground reading. It cannot settle the question it reports on (see `05_what_shape_means.md` section 6).

## Key result in one line

The question asks for a standing list of strongest interactions by scale. The figures refuse that object: strength is a comparison whose ordering is relative to a named residual per slot (`Fig 1 :19`, `Fig 11 :49`, `Fig 12 :87`), scale is a parameter of the frame where only actors, currency and anchor change (`Fig 4 :20`), and the cross-scale relation is a lossy membrane plus a contact cascade (`Fig 4 :30`, `Fig 11 :52-54`). What survives is a per-domain separator, not a ranking. The universal ranking is a declared `gap`, which is a verdict and not silence (`Fig 13 :134`).

## What is deliberately not here

- No personal emails of named individuals.
- No ranking of people by how likely they are to agree. `Fig 2 :40` disqualifies agreement drawn from a shared evidence pool for a verdict.
- No bulk send plan.
- No universal list of "the strongest interactions." That is the object the question asks for and the figures do not return it. Reporting a list would be inventing an answer at a `gap`.
