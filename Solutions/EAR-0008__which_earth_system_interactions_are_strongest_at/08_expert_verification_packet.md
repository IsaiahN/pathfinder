## 1. The claim, in one paragraph

The question "which Earth-system interactions are strongest at local, regional, continental or global scales" asks for a standing list, and that object is not well formed. Strength is an ordered comparison whose valence is relative to a named residual per slot, so no list survives a change of outcome. Scale is a parameter of the observing frame, where only actors, currency and anchor change, so no scale is intrinsically senior and no single currency covers carbon, water, albedo, correlation and recovery time. Cross-scale influence crosses a lossy membrane, and the residual that matters at the receiving scale is often two or three steps out. What the figures return is a five-part separator, not a ranking: name the slot and residual, enumerate the habitat including higher-order contacts, range an independent non-updating anchor, declare the currency and compare, and measure the round-trip gap. The ordered result stays empty until a ground pays for it.
