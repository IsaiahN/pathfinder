- **Domain 1, observation and measurement.** Actors: instruments, reanalyses, model tiles, datasets. Currency: residual R per slot. Anchor: a measurement that does not move when a frame pushes on it (Fig 2 :29). Nameable, so this is a domain.
- **Domain 2, mechanism and composition.** Actors: atmosphere, ocean, ice, land, ecosystems and their actors, conditions and relations (Fig 11 :45-47). Currency: cross-kind composition and contact. Anchor: experiment, paleo record, process study. Nameable.
- **Domain 3, cross-scale transfer.** Actors: the four scales as nested rooms and the membrane between them. Currency: methods carried up, head starts down, and contact cascades. Anchor: the measured round-trip gap R_T (Fig 4 :30). Nameable.
- **Domain 4, strength and valence ranking.** Actors: candidate interactions as relations. Currency: comparison and spectral ordering. Anchor: the named residual to which valence is relative (Fig 11 :49). Nameable once the residual is written down; until then this is not yet a domain.
- **Domain 5, human alteration.** Actors: land and water use, emissions, introductions. Currency: introduction, not subtraction (Fig 11 :61). Anchor: observation of the altered state. Nameable.
- **Union, not partition.** D1 with D2 is measured mechanism; D2 with D3 is cross-scale cascade; D4 depends on D1; D5 overlaps both D2 and D3. One lever leaves the intersections standing (Fig 9 :54). Verdicts are kept per domain and never averaged (Fig 1 :22).
- **Order by dependency (Fig 3 :20).** Name the residual and slot, enumerate the habitat, rank by comparison against the named residual, check transfer and the round trip, then add human introductions. Residual first, frame second (Fig 9 :27-28).

Strain test applied: an early reading mapped the four scales onto the six-term substrate. Re-census assigns scale to the membrane and dependency ordering, not to the substrate. Scale is a frame parameter with the same kernel and a lossy membrane, so no scale is senior and no single currency spans the four. Human alteration is introduction into an unauthored habitat. Verdicts per domain are kept separate: D1 `specified` triangulated, D2 `specified` triangulated, D3 `specified` triangulated, D4 `specified` for the separator and `gap` for the order, D5 `specified` single-source on the figure side with world corroboration on mechanism shape. - **cost if wrong:** bounded and locatable. The packet states mode, five refuters and the lineage before the conclusion, so an error is findable rather than invisible. The real cost is spending the independence of outside readers by sending a ranking instead of a separator, which Fig 2 :42 prices: *"Buy independence in proportion to the cost of being wrong."* That cost is avoidable by leading with the residual and the refuter. - **undoable:** yes. No component or primitive has to be withdrawn. A published ranking can be superseded by the separator, and the empty result column can be filled later by a settled term without erasing the record.
- **reversed by:** a ground reading, not agreement. A source-discriminating instrument that identifies which component produced a cross-scale effect, a demonstration that one currency makes all four scale readings commensurable, a measured `R_T = 0` for a named local-to-global-to-local descriptor, a figure line that returns the ranking, a seventh derived Earth-system component, or two low-overlap rooms returning incompatible orderings at fixed residual.
