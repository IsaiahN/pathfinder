# The Tether's answer: which Earth-system interactions are strongest at each scale?

**Mode: `specified` on the per-domain verdicts and the separator, `general` on the structural shape, `gap` on the literal ranking. Not `grounded`.** This is a shape and a route, not a result. It is a simulated derivation checked for internal consistency against the figures (`05_what_shape_means.md` section 6), not a ground reading.

## 1. The residual it answers

The question asks for a standing list of strongest interactions at four scales. The residual is the unchosen definition of "strongest": no slot, no currency and no outcome is named, so nothing can be compared. The four scale questions plus the definition question form a **union compound**, not one problem with four answers. A shared condition binds them: strength is relative to a residual. One lever cannot clear the union because it leaves the intersections standing (`Fig 9 :54`). Verdicts below are kept per domain and never averaged (`Fig 1 :22`).

## 2. The shape, per domain

**D1, observation.** Strength must be indexed per slot before it is compared. `Fig 1 :19`: *"The residual is indexed by object slot, not aggregated."* `Fig 1 :22`: *"Aggregating across slots is how a live signal disappears into an average."* A planetary mean near zero can sit beside one live basin, ice margin or reef (`Fig 1 :13`). The anchor is a measurement that does not move when a frame pushes on it (`Fig 2 :29` *"An anchor is whatever settles the question and does not change its answer because of what any frame thinks."*). World corroboration is low-overlap and strong: the modifiable areal unit problem shows that the same pattern yields different correlations under different partitions. Verdict: `specified`, **triangulated**.

**D2, mechanism.** Enumerate the habitat; do not compose a list from intuition. `Fig 11 :43`: *"The habitat is enumerated, never composed."* Actors, conditions and relations are read off the world (`Fig 11 :45-47`), then the cascade is followed outward: `Fig 11 :52-54` *"Scoping the habitat to direct contact is how DDT reached the eagles: first-order enumeration, second-order effect, and the residual that mattered was three steps out."* The dominant interaction at a receiving scale is often not the first-order contact. Verdict: `specified`, **triangulated**.

**D3, cross-scale.** The four scales are four frames of one loop, not a ladder. `Fig 4 :20`: *"Only the actors, the currency and the anchor change."* `Fig 13 :4-5`: *"The two kinds are in lockstep, a crossing is a change of role rather than a step upward, and no member of the pair is senior to the other."* Influence crosses a lossy membrane: `Fig 4 :30` *"So take the round trip and measure the gap. Without it you are navigating by dead reckoning: confident about a position nobody checked."* Verdict: `specified`, **triangulated**; no scale is intrinsically strongest.

**D4, strength and ranking.** Strength is not a property carried by an interaction. `Fig 11 :49`: *"An actor's valence is relative to the residual, not a property it carries."* `Fig 11 :51`: *"No standing list of good actors survives a change of residual."* It is an ordered comparison, `Fig 12 :87` *"a threshold relation between two quantities"*, on a spectral axis, `Fig 13 :20` *"ordering, so more and less can be said"*, with currencies that do not add (`Fig 12 :125` *"A composition costs in two currencies, and they do not add."*). The separator is `specified` and triangulated; **the specific ordering is a `gap`**.

**D5, human alteration.** Human action introduces actors into a habitat it did not author. `Fig 11 :61`: *"Introduction is available; exclusion is not."* Small actors can dominate: the ecosystem-engineering record notes that *"less abundant species can still have a large impact"*, and the 2026 Earth-system article shows indirect effects are strongly heterogeneous and often local. Verdict: `specified`, **single-source on the figure side**, corroborated on mechanism shape by two low-overlap fields.

## 3. What it explicitly does not say

- Not a list. No named interaction is asserted as strongest at any scale until a residual, currency and anchor are supplied and paid for.
- Not a scale ladder. Global is not senior to local, and continental is not a step upward (`Fig 4 :33`).
- Not a single currency. Carbon flux, water flux, albedo, correlation strength and recovery time are not summed (`Fig 12 :126`).
- Not an averaging license. A global mean is not the place where the live residual lives (`Fig 1 :13`).
- Not an expert consensus. A survey selected for agreement is a convention, not a verdict (`Fig 2 :39`, `Fig 2 :40`).
- Not `grounded`. No Earth-system measurement was taken, and no term was settled by a ground in this run.

## 4. What would settle it

A source-discriminating instrument, not another argument. From the metamerism analogue (carried, `05` section 4): a reading cannot comment on its source, and an instrument unable to distinguish sources demonstrates its own inadequacy. The proto-instruments already returning something are the 0.5 degree indirect-effect maps, the teleconnection correlation matrices, the tipping-element threshold tables and the biomagnification factors (`Fig 6 :32`). Sharpen them against named residuals:

1. For a fixed slot and currency, does an outside frame using a different aggregation unit rank the same interaction first, with both frames ranging against separate non-updating grounds?
2. Does the local-to-global-to-local round trip return the original slot values, and if not, what is the gap `R_T`?
3. Does a second and third-order enumeration move the winner? If the dominant effect is three steps out, a first-order ranking is dead.
4. Does a tipping threshold satisfy the six bond tests for `⋛`, or is it a different operator? This is the live type question S3.
5. Standing refuters from `01`: a figure line that returns the ranking; an interaction that ranks first for every residual and currency; a global aggregate that predicts live slots as well as slot measurement; `R_T = 0`; a seventh derived Earth-system component; two low-overlap rooms returning incompatible orderings at fixed residual.

## 5. The honest stop

**Unreached, not unreachable.** The separator stops at the limit of the available instruments and records the lineage so the stop is checkable. `Fig 9 :32`: *"you get a kind of thing, never the answer itself."* `Fig 8 :39`: *"So a search that finds nothing is never a proof of absence."* `Fig 13 :134`: *"Unreachable is a verdict here, not a silence,"* and here the `gap` is of the shape-not-a-name kind: conditional per-outcome rankings exist in the literature, but only the unconditional list is refused. *"Indiscernibility is not identity."*

## 6. One-paragraph version for outreach

> The Tether does not rank Earth-system interactions by scale. It says the requested list is not a well-formed object: strength is a comparison whose ordering is relative to a named residual per slot, so no standing list survives a change of outcome (`Fig 11 :51`); scale is a parameter of the observing frame, where only actors, currency and anchor change (`Fig 4 :20`), so global is not senior to local; and cross-scale influence crosses a lossy membrane (`Fig 4 :30`) in which the residual that matters is often two or three steps out (`Fig 11 :52-54`). What the figures return is a shape: name the slot and residual, enumerate the habitat including second and third-order contacts, range an independent non-updating anchor, declare the currency and compare, and measure the round-trip gap. The ordered result stays empty until a ground pays for it. That is a `gap`, which is a verdict and not silence. The offer is the shape any answer must have, five refuters written in advance, and the instruments that would close it.
