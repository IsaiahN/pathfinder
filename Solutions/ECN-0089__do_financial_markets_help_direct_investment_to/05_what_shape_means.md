## 1. A shape is a constraint, not a filler

The figures README states the limit: the framework returns "what a missing thing would have to look like (a shape, not a name)". Fig 9 :32 says *"you get a kind of thing, never the answer itself"*. Fig 13 :134 says *"Unreachable is a verdict here, not a silence,"* and immediately adds that the framework "cannot say which primitive is missing, because naming it would mean already holding it".

From the Library of Babel essay: "you can cut your search short if the set of books returned is small and descriptive enough that it provides a general shape of the answer that satisfies your question."

So a **shape** is the form of an answer you can state before you can name its filler. It constrains; it does not fill. For ECN-0089, the shape is:

> per investment slot, a verdict against a named non-price anchor, with each actor's valence declared relative to an explicitly chosen residual.

That is a shape. "Financial markets are good" and "speculation is bad" are fillers, and both are refused by the derivation in `01`.

## 2. How the shape is obtained

- **Proximity and bootstrapping.** Library of Babel: "you will have to use the existing vocabulary you do know that is closest to the words that you wish to describe and grasp the shape of." Then "With each new term gained, you narrow the space and deepen your vocabulary." For this question the existing vocabulary is price, capital, horizon, information and incentive; the new terms are slot, residual, anchor and valence.
- **Describe the effect, not the cause.** Fig 6 :30 says "The description travels. Fails by being sharpened into terms only we can read. Cast it in effect terms." Fig 9 :30-31 says "your words are borrowed too, so describe the effect rather than the cause." So the owed instrument is described as "something that ranks productive use and does not move when prices are pushed", not named as a specific index.
- **A checkable description survives without shared history.** Decoding Tacit Knowledge: "When contact isn't possible, the best you can send is a plain description of what you'd expect to see, because anyone can check that against the world." This is why the outreach in `02` leads with a refuter rather than a theory.
- **Stopping.** Library of Babel: "you will know that you are done because the finish line will also be the starting place". Here the loop stops at the limit of the available instrument: the objective link has no independent measure, and that is recorded rather than papered over.

## 3. Splitting a bundle is part of finding the shape

Library of Babel: "Perhaps your problem was a bundle or conflation of several problems masquerading as one? You will need to search all of those problems individually to find their answers and then combine them in a satisfactory way."

Fig 9 :4-5 gives the figure-level rule: disagreements that do not shrink with effort, where each rule keeps working well on a different subset, are several well-formed questions. Fig 9 :57-58 adds that "a case in two conditions is not a harder case of either. It stops being a mixture and becomes a specific compound." This is the direct figure-grounded warrant for F1 in `01`: the "or" in ECN-0089 is a union, not an exclusive choice, and the answer is a set of per-slot verdicts.

## 4. What licenses a name

Only two things, both outside the shape itself:

1. **The ground settles it.** `THE_FORMULA.md` step 5: "the ground settles it. Verified only by what pays. A gate is not the ground." The canonical README says the framework "stops at the limit of what the available instruments can distinguish, and records the lineage so the stop is honest".
2. **A provenanced import from a frame whose closure differs** (Fig 6 :23, Fig 8 :50-52), with a source gate and a shadow test. Fig 6 :14 calls import "the only operator that moves the wall", and Fig 6 :24 warns that "A wrong atom costs more than starting from primitives."

For ECN-0089 this means: naming a specific productivity index as *the* anchor is a binding from the measurement frame, and it must carry provenance and a shadow test. It is not something the six-term substrate or the eight-step loop derives. The derivation here deliberately leaves the anchor characterised in effect terms only.

## 5. Triangulation is a bearing on a fixed landmark, not an agreement between friends

Decoding Tacit Knowledge: "Two navigators don't find their position by agreeing with each other. Each takes a bearing on the same fixed landmark, and nobody can talk the landmark into moving." It also states the three safeguards: "A ground that both are corrected by, separately", "Separate experience", and "Claims in effect terms."

Fig 2 :35: *"Alignment is triangulation, not negotiation. A mirror cannot correct you."* Fig 2 :40 calls shared-pool agreement *"disqualifying for a verdict: agreement about the world is worthless when the agreeing frames share an evidence pool"*.

Applied here: Hayek 1945 and Keynes 1936 can count as two bearings because they are different eras, methods and vocabularies aimed at a similar contact point. The three modern finance surveys cannot count as three bearings, because they cite the same canonical studies. That is a discipline on the witness count, not a comment on their quality.

## 6. Prestige is not action, and this audit is a simulation

From The Recursive Self-Improvement Trap: "Prestige can fund an action. It can give an action a budget to be tried. But it cannot settle an action. Only the ground can settle it. The Ground Maintainer must be blind to prestige." A request that a famous economist endorse the shape is the named collapse. The legitimate ask gives the claim a budget to be tried and lets a ground settle it.

The same essay says: "LLMs can quickly simulate an approximate answer/output with the formula found in the architecture post", but "the truly testable output would need to be derived from the real architecture or working the formula yourself." So `01_figure_grounded_audit.md`, `06_isomers_and_order.md`, `07_the_tether_answer.md`, `08_expert_verification_packet.md`, `09_watson_answer.md` and `11_audit.md` are **simulated derivations** checked for internal consistency against the figures. They are not ground readings. They cannot settle ECN-0089, and no agreement with them should be recorded as corroboration of the claim about financial markets.

## 7. The correction kept visible

An earlier reading of this question treated "shape, not a name" as a blanket ban on naming any element of an answer. That overstates it. The canonical phrase governs a **missing** thing: "what a missing thing would have to look like (a shape, not a name)". It does not forbid naming an atom already held in the library, and it does not forbid naming an import that has passed a source gate and a shadow test. What it forbids here is presenting a binding (a chosen productivity metric, a chosen horizon threshold) as if the framework had derived it. The corrected position is recorded in `01` section 6 and `06`, and the earlier overstatement is preserved here rather than erased.
