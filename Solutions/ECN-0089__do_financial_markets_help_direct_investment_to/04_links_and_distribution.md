## Wider literature that bears on this question

Three clusters appear in the sources actually opened. They are clusters, not independent witnesses.

1. **Price as a knowledge compressor.** Hayek 1945 gives the mechanism: dispersed knowledge of time and place is concentrated into a price, and an arbitrageur acting on fleeting local differences performs a useful function. Jones and Netter state the finance version: if prices reflect information, capital goes to its highest-valued use. This is Domain 1 in `01_figure_grounded_audit.md`.
2. **Price as a convention that can decide the allocation.** Keynes 1936 names the convention explicitly and separates speculating (forecasting market psychology) from enterprise (forecasting yield over the asset's whole life). Malkiel 2003 and the two Econlib entries show the modern empirical dispute and its shared evidence pool. This is Domain 2 and the basis of F2, F3 and F5.
3. **Shape, validation and independent bearings.** The three Isaiah essays opened this session give the canonical reading of "shape", the warning that a frame cannot validate itself, and the two-navigator picture of triangulation. These are framework-author sources, so their agreement with the figures is expected and carries little independent weight.

Hayek and Keynes are genuinely low overlap (different era, method and vocabulary). The three modern finance surveys overlap heavily with one another through a common citation pool. Their agreement is counted once.

## Distribution: offer, do not push

- Fig 4 :22 *"Only methods cross up. Only head starts come down."* Sending the full framework before anyone has described a residual sends a recording, not a generator. Lead with the shape claim and the refuter.
- Fig 10 :42 *"A verifier that can reconstruct the claim cannot verify it."* A recipient who has absorbed the framework before being asked for a verdict has joined the frame being checked.
- Fig 9 :27-28 *"describe it before you go looking, or any frame you pick will seem to fit"*. Ask what they would expect to see if the shape claim were true before giving them the sources.
- Fig 2 :40 agreement is *"disqualifying for a verdict: agreement about the world is worthless when the agreeing frames share an evidence pool"*. A send selected for likely agreement produces a convention, not corroboration.

**Recommended order in a reply:** (1) the shape claim and its four refuters; (2) ask what they would count as a failure and record the answer; (3) only then the links, with the note that the figures, not this summary, are the authority.

## The two-stage offer

1. **Stage 1.** Residual, shape claim and pre-registered refuter. No theory, no links, no request for agreement. Stage 1 comes first because Fig 10 :52 says *"Bring a characterised residual, never the source."*
2. **Stage 2, opt-in.** If they are curious, the four links above. Their Stage 1 verdict is already recorded, and later agreement is never pooled as corroboration.
3. **Stage 3, opt-in.** The formula as a method to reuse. Each user writes their own refuter, states their own mode and names their own ground. This is permitted because a method crosses up, an answer does not.
