## Restating the question as an effect

The question asks which of two mechanisms accounts for a divergence between where capital actually goes and where it would go under full knowledge of long-run productive use. Option A is the price-discovery story: dispersed information is compressed into prices and capital follows the signal. Option B is the distortion story: speculation and short-horizon incentives pull capital away from productive use. What an answer would look like, bound as an ordinary term, is a verdict per investment slot against a named anchor that is not the market's own price. Until that term is bound and accrues a residual, the sought-for shape is a preference, not a term the loop can check.

## What is deliberately not here

- No personal emails of named individuals.
- No ranking of people by how likely they are to agree with the claim.
- No bulk-send plan.

The reason is structural, not merely legal. Fig 2 :35 says *"Alignment is triangulation, not negotiation. A mirror cannot correct you."* Fig 2 :40 says agreement is *"disqualifying for a verdict: agreement about the world is worthless when the agreeing frames share an evidence pool"*. Selecting recipients for receptiveness manufactures the correlation that would make their agreement worthless. Legal constraints on unsolicited bulk email are an additional practical failure on top of that.
