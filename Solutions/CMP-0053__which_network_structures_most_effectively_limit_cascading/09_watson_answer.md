## QUESTION

Decide, in effect terms, what the Tether says about a network structure that must contain a local failure while still passing a beneficial signal. Option A: rank named topologies (modular, lattice, scale-free, multiplex) by a single robustness score. Option B: treat the two requirements as a union compound, derive the shape any candidate must have, and hold the ranking as `gap` until a per-slot ground reading settles it. What each leads to: A averages away the live signal, gives opposite verdicts to the same feature under different flows, and lets a filter hand you a verdict; B sends a checkable shape, flow-relative valence, refuters written in advance, and an honest open slot.

## DOMAIN CENSUS

R by arity, symmetry and scale, then one line per domain giving actors / currency / anchor, partition or union, and order by dependency.

- **R (the residual).** Arity: two conditions on one link set, containment and diffusion, so a union compound (Fig 9 :54), not one hard ranking problem. Symmetry: asymmetric, because failure propagates by overload or threshold while beneficial spread propagates by adoption, reinforcement or control, so the link is not a symmetric connector between equivalent states. Scale: micro (node and link), meso (module, community, bridge), macro (global cascade or system-wide spread); the effect can be three steps out (Fig 11 :52-54).
- **Domain 1, network structure (substrate).** Actors: nodes, links, modules, overlapping memberships. Currency: influence travel (Fig 13 :24). Anchor: the six-term substrate and its cross-kind rule. Question type: partition of legal compositions at first order, union at meso scale.
- **Domain 2, failure dynamics frame.** Actors: overloaded links, thresholds, failed components. Currency: cascade size, giant component, served demand. Anchor: experiment, outage data, physics-based simulation. This frame measures containment.
- **Domain 3, diffusion and contagion frame.** Actors: seeds, adopters, reinforcing neighbours, control signals. Currency: diffusion speed and reach. Anchor: experiment and field data. This frame measures beneficial spread.
- **Domain 4, ecology and finance frame.** Actors: species, sectors, mutualistic partners, exposures. Currency: persistence, stability, diversification. Anchor: empirical persistence and loss data. Low overlap witness to valence reversal.
- **Domain 5, verification and outreach frame.** Actors: expert readers. Currency: agreement and residual. Anchor: the ground, never agreement. In this simulated run all seats share one evidence pool, so independence is zero.
- **Order by dependency.** Describe the two flows in effect terms, decide union versus partition, enumerate the habitat outward, measure per slot, then look up a frame that predicts the shape. Effort spent ranking topologies before this lands above the break.

Strain test: world sources appeared to conflict about modularity and coupling. Re-census showed they differ in flow type, threshold rule, measurement scale and whether a beneficial control function is modelled. The conflict dissolves into the valence finding, not a figure strain.

## VERDICT (from the figures)

The shape survives; the ranking does not. This is a union compound that must be unbundled before any topology is named. Link valence is relative to the flow, so no standing "best structure" survives a change from failure containment to beneficial diffusion. The habitat must be enumerated outward to the step where the cascade stops mattering, and failure probability and diffusion reach must be reported per node and per module. The missing atom is a link typed by its role under the flow (regenerating control versus one-shot redundancy), imported with provenance from the network and contagion frames. No figure line ranks named topologies, so that ranking is `gap`, owed to a per-slot ground reading. This is a `specified`, ungrounded result. The four pre-registered refuters are in `01` section 4.

- **cost if wrong:** bounded and locatable. The packet states mode, per-slot rule and refuters before the conclusion, so an error is findable rather than invisible. The real avoidable cost is sending a global robustness average as if it settled the question, which hides the live slot (Fig 1 :22) and lets a filter hand you a verdict (Fig 9 :60). Independence of outside readers is spent if the source is sent before the residual; Fig 2 :42 says "Buy independence in proportion to the cost of being wrong."
- **undoable:** yes. The global average and the flow-free ranking are removable without touching the shape. `07_the_tether_answer.md` and `08_expert_verification_packet.md` already remove them. A sent claim can be superseded by the corrected packet, and no primitive or figure has to be withdrawn.
- **reversed by:** a ground reading, not agreement. A per-slot comparative measurement with both flows defined on one system would close the `gap`; a link whose cascade and diffusion effects share a sign under the same threshold rule would break the valence claim; a lever that moves both flows in the same direction across every slot would make it a partition; exchangeable per-slot residuals would make the mean sufficient. Until one of these fires, the verdict stands as `candidate`.
