## Distribution: offer, do not push

Figure-grounded reasons:

- **Fig 10 :52** *"Bring a characterised residual, never the source."* Sending the full framework before anyone has described the two flows sends a source, not a residual.
- **Fig 10 :42** *"A verifier that can reconstruct the claim cannot verify it."* A reader who has absorbed the framework before giving a stage-1 verdict has joined the frame being checked.
- **Fig 9 :27-28** *"describe it before you go looking, or any frame you pick will seem to fit"*. Ask what they would expect to see if the union claim were true before naming modularity or any other topology.
- **Fig 2 :35** *"Alignment is triangulation, not negotiation. A mirror cannot correct you."* Do not ask for agreement.
- **Fig 2 :42** *"Buy independence in proportion to the cost of being wrong."* Prefer readers in ecology, infrastructure or social diffusion over a fifth reader of the same percolation review.

**Recommended order in a reply:** (1) the residual and its refuter; (2) ask what they would count as a failure; (3) record that verdict; (4) only then the framework links, with the note that the figures, not the summary, are the authority.

## The two-stage offer

1. **Stage 1.** Send the residual and the four pre-registered refuters in `08_expert_verification_packet.md`. Ask for a failure, not an endorsement.
2. **Stage 2, opt-in.** If they are curious, offer the framework links. Their stage-1 verdict is already recorded and is never pooled as corroboration afterwards.
3. **Stage 3.** Offer the formula as a method to reuse, with the instruction that each user states their own mode and writes their own refuter before running it.

Nothing in this folder is sent by this agent. Hosting the packet and contacting people through the public venues in `03` is yours to do. Prefer an opt-in download page over a pushed attachment.
