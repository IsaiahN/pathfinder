# The Tether's answer on cascades and beneficial diffusion

**Mode: `specified` on the shape, `general` on the union and valence laws, `gap` on the empirical ranking of named topologies. Not `grounded`.** Simulated derivation checked against the figures (`05_what_shape_means.md` §7), not a ground reading.

## 1. The residual it answers

The question asks for a network structure that contains a local failure while still passing a beneficial signal. Both flows use the same links, so the two requirements pull in opposite directions: cutting a link to stop a cascade cuts a path for a benefit; adding a link to help diffusion adds a path for a failure. Fig 9 :4-5 says disagreements that do not shrink with effort, *"are not one hard question but several well-formed ones. Split it rather than search."* The residual is a **union compound**, with its own loop and its own ground (Fig 9 :54-56), not one hard ranking problem.

## 2. The shape

1. **Unbundle before ranking.** Containment and diffusion are two conditions on one link set. Their intersection is a link that must both stop a failure and pass a benefit. Fig 9 :54: *"And when the split lands on a union rather than a partition, unbundle before you search again."* A single lever clears the region where only one condition holds and leaves the intersections standing.
2. **Valence is relative to the flow.** Fig 11 :49: *"An actor's valence is relative to the residual, not a property it carries."* Fig 11 :50: *"The same actor is a benefactor to one residual and a malfactor to another, so mutualism and parasitism are relations rather than kinds."* A bridge that kills a cascade may be the only wide bridge that carries a complex diffusion. There is no flow-free "best structure".
3. **Enumerate the habitat outward.** Fig 11 :52-54: *"And contact cascades. Scoping the habitat to direct contact is how DDT reached the eagles: first-order enumeration, second-order effect, and the residual that mattered was three steps out."* Degree and immediate neighbours are a first-order census. Dependency links and overlapping modules must be enumerated until the cascade stops mattering (Fig 11 :44).
4. **Measure per slot, never as one average.** Fig 1 :19: *"The residual is indexed by object slot, not aggregated."* Fig 1 :22: *"Aggregating across slots is how a live signal disappears into an average."* Report failure probability and diffusion reach for each node and module as vectors.
5. **Type the link by its role under the flow.** Fig 12 :119: *"catalyst. A part that survives its own use."* A regenerating control channel and a one-shot redundant link are different substances even in the same topology. The framework does not hold this distinction; it must be imported from the network and contagion frames with a source gate (Fig 6 :23: *"No frame escapes its own closure. The bound moves only on contact with a frame whose closure differs."*).
6. **The sign of the loop decides.** Fig 12 :121: *"cycle. The output re-enters as input. The sign of the loop decides whether it closes, and a chain cannot close by construction."* Positive reinforcement closes a cascade; the same clustering can close or block a diffusion depending on the threshold. This is why the same topology feature gets opposite verdicts in different studies.
7. **The framework returns a shape, not a name.** Fig 9 :32: *"you get a kind of thing, never the answer itself"*. No figure line ranks modular, lattice, scale-free or multiplex networks. The ranking is `gap`.

## 3. What it explicitly does not say

- Not that modularity, decentralisation, scale-free structure or any named topology is best. That is a name where the framework returns a shape.
- Not that "robustness" is one number. Per-slot residuals must be kept separate.
- Not that the world sources agree. Most share the same percolation model and cite one another; their agreement is one evidence pool (Fig 2 :40).
- Not a derivation of a specific cascade threshold, not a new primitive, and not `grounded`. No measurement was run.
- Not a verdict on any real network. A simulated derivation cannot settle the question it reports on.

## 4. What would settle it: the ground

A per-slot comparative measurement, not a further argument. From The Hard Problem is About Source, a reading *"cannot comment on the source"*; *"The answer is unreached until a better instrument exists."* Here the corresponding questions are:

- **Union Test.** On one system, with one lever, do containment and diffusion move in the same direction across every slot? If yes, the compound was a partition and the unbundling claim is retracted.
- **Valence Test.** Does a link exist whose cascade effect and diffusion effect share a sign under the same threshold rule? If yes, valence is carried by the link.
- **Catalyst Test.** Are control or bridging links regenerating under the flow, or spent after one use? A per-link use-history instrument would settle the typing.
- **Module Test.** Does module-level function fail before node-level connectivity (Bagrow et al. 2015), and does the order hold across systems?
- **Ranking Test.** With both flows defined on the same system and per-slot outcomes recorded, which named topology dominates? This is the test that would turn the `gap` into a name.

## 6. One-paragraph version for outreach

> The Tether does not name a winning network topology, and it does not claim this derivation settles which one wins. It says that containing a failure and passing a benefit are two conditions on the same links, so the question is a union compound that must be unbundled before anything is ranked; that a link's valence is relative to the flow, not a property of the link, so a bridge that is bad for a cascade can be good for a diffusion; that the habitat must be enumerated several steps out, because the residual that matters can be three steps away; and that failure probability and diffusion reach must be reported per node and per module, never averaged into one robustness score. The missing piece is a link typed by its role under the flow, imported with provenance from network science and contagion theory. What the framework offers is that shape, four falsifiers written in advance, and the per-slot instrument that would turn the open ranking into a name. The ranking stays open until a ground settles it.
