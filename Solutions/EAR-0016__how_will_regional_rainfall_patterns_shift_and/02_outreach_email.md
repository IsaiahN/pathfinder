# Outreach draft — check the residual first, then decide about the framework

**Use:** one-to-one, or as a cover note attached to a submission through the public venues in `03_public_contact_points.csv`. **Not** for bulk send.

**Hard precondition:** do not send until the reader has been given the residual and the refuter with no framework material attached. If the stage-1 output does not survive, there is nothing else to look at.

---

**Subject:** One checkable claim about regional rainfall confidence, before any theory

> Hello,
>
> I have one specific output I would like you to check against what you already know about regional climate information. I am not asking you to agree with anything, and I am not asking you to read the framework first. If the output fails, there is nothing else to look at.
>
> **The residual, in effect terms.** A community needs a rainfall statement at its own scale, over its own decision horizon, with an honest confidence. What regional climate projection systems usually deliver is a coarse, model-dependent statement whose confidence is reported at sub-continental scale. The gap between those two is the residual. It is a union of two things: what rainfall will actually do locally, and how confidence may legitimately be stated locally.
>
> **The stage-1 claim (`specified`, not `grounded`).** Confidence in a regional rainfall projection cannot be stated as a single aggregate number. It has to be indexed per place and per decision horizon. Four consequences follow:
>
> 1. The loss between the global projection and the local decision has to be measured, not assumed away.
> 2. Model agreement is not independent corroboration when the models share code, parameterisations, calibration data or a predecessor. Fifty-seven related models are fewer witnesses than fifty-seven.
> 3. "We cannot resolve this with the current grid, record length or ensemble" is a different statement from "this is unpredictable in principle", and the two require different remedies.
> 4. A projection is a candidate until realised rainfall and the decision itself pay for it. Agreement among readers is not that settlement.
>
> **What I am not claiming.** I am not claiming to tell your region it will get wetter or drier. The framework I am using returns a shape, not a direction: it says what any answer must look like and what would settle it. It contains no hydrology, so the physical direction is an explicit gap in this derivation. I am also not claiming that any current ensemble is worthless, that downscaling cannot help, or that low confidence means no change.
>
> **What would refute the claim, written before this message:**
>
> 1. A single aggregate confidence figure shown to decide a community-scale question with a measured cross-scale loss of zero.
> 2. Two models with shared predecessor code shown to be fully independent witnesses for the same regional residual.
> 3. All local disagreement removed by more search at fixed instrument and fixed observations.
>
> If any of those holds, I withdraw the corresponding point. A one-line "this fails because..." is more useful to me than agreement. If you would rather not engage, no reply is needed.
>
> **If it survives, and only then:** I can send the three source links. The figures, not my summary of them, are the authority.
>
> Thank you for your time.

## Stage 2, opt-in only

If the reader returns a stage-1 verdict, and only then, send the links in `04_links_and_distribution.md` with the note that the figures are the authority. Their stage-1 verdict is recorded before any theory is shown; any later agreement is never pooled as corroboration, because *"agreement about the world is worthless when the agreeing frames share an evidence pool"* (Fig 2 :40).

## Stage 3, also opt-in

Offer the formula as a method to reuse, with the instruction that each person state their own ground and write their own refuter before running it. That is a method crossing, which the figures permit: *"Only methods cross up. Only head starts come down."* (Fig 4 :22). No output produced this way is accepted without its own ground reading.
