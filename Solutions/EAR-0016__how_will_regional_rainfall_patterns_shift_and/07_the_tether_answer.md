# The Tether's answer on regional rainfall and decision-scale confidence

**Mode: `specified` on the structural shape, `general` on the scope of the limit, `gap` on the physical direction. Not `grounded`.** Simulated derivation checked for internal consistency against the figures, not a ground reading (`05_what_shape_means.md` §6).

## 1. The residual it answers

The question bundles two effects. E1 asks what rainfall will actually do in a named region over a named period. E2 asks how confident a projection can be at the scale a community uses. They form a **union compound**, not one hard question: the overlap, "what a community may legitimately believe about a local change", has its own loop and its own ground. Fig 9 :57: *"a case in two conditions is not a harder case of either."*

## 2. The answer, per domain, never averaged

**Domain A, physical direction: `gap`.** After a genuine census of the thirteen figures and both tables, no figure line yields a wetter or drier direction for any region. The framework contains a grammar of states and structures, not precipitation physics. The honest report is Fig 13 :134: *"Unreachable is a verdict here, not a silence"*. Climate science can supply candidate directions as provenanced imports with a source gate and shadow test (Fig 6 :14), but they remain secondary in this solution. What can be said figure-groundedly is only that a direction must be per place and per horizon, and that it stays a `candidate` until the ground pays.

**Domain B, inference shape: five surviving constraints.**

1. **Per slot, not aggregate.** Fig 1 :19: *"The residual is indexed by object slot, not aggregated."* Symbols :43-44: *"R is measured per slot, never as a single global number."* A "medium confidence for South Asia" statement averages mountain, coast and delta and hides where the live signal is.
2. **Measure the cross-scale loss.** Fig 4 :30: *"So take the round trip and measure the gap. Without it you are navigating by dead reckoning: confident about a position nobody checked."* Global projection to community statement is lossy; report the gap `R_T`.
3. **Discount correlated witnesses.** Fig 2 :31-32: *"Independence, between two frames, is how little they draw on the same evidence."* Fig 8 :49: *"Two frames that overlap almost completely are one frame wearing two names."* Ensemble size is not witness count.
4. **Keep budget apart from principle.** Fig 8 :40: *"underivable-in-principle and unreachable-at-this-budget are different facts"*. A resolution limit is owed a better instrument; a principle limit is owed a different question. Fig 6 :32: *"An instrument is not built from a description. It is improved from a worse instrument already returning something."*
5. **The ground settles it.** Fig 2 :29: *"An anchor is whatever settles the question and does not change its answer because of what any frame thinks."* Formula step 5: *"the ground settles it. Verified only by what pays. A gate is not the ground."*

**Domain C, decision and community.** Describe the community's residual in effect terms before choosing a method: *"describe it before you go looking, or any frame you pick will seem to fit"* (Fig 9 :26-28). Enumerate the habitat, do not compose it: *"The habitat is enumerated, never composed."* (Fig 11 :43). Regional rainfall depends on second-order contact: large-scale modes, aerosols, land use, topography and catchment behaviour (Fig 11 :44).

**Domain D, verification.** Bring the residual and the refuter, not the framework: *"Bring a characterised residual, never the source."* (Fig 10 :52). A reader who has absorbed the framework before returning a verdict has joined the frame under test (Fig 10 :42).

**Confidence answer in one sentence:** confidence is a per-place, per-horizon report whose independence has been discounted, whose cross-scale loss has been measured, whose budget limits have been separated from principle limits, and whose settlement waits for realised rainfall; it is not a single number averaged over a continent.

## 3. What it explicitly does not say

- Not a wetter/drier direction for any named region. That is the declared `gap`.
- Not that a coarse global projection is useless. It is one line of evidence with a measured loss.
- Not that model disagreement proves no signal. Fig 9 :44: *"Only a sealed room can be searched to the end, and no frame is a sealed room. So searching and finding nothing proves nothing."*
- Not that internal variability makes regional change unknowable. Krishnamurthy 2019 and Kendon et al. 2023 show the opposite: large ensembles and convection-permitting models recover signals that short records hide.
- Not a claim that current ensemble spread fully measures uncertainty. IPCC AR6 WGI Chapter 10 states (very high confidence) that it does not.
- Not `grounded`. No observation was taken and no model was run for this package.

## 4. What would settle it, and the honest stop

| to settle | instrument or event |
|---|---|
| Direction for a named region and horizon | realised rainfall at the decision scale over that horizon, plus a decision whose outcome pays |
| Size of the cross-scale loss | paired coarse and fine simulations over the same region, with the gap measured (Fig 4 :30) |
| Independence of two models | code and parameterisation genealogy, plus out-of-sample tests, not output similarity alone |
| Budget versus principle for a named community | an instrument that returns something at that scale; if none can be imagined from something already returning a reading, classify as principle limit (Fig 6 :34-35) |
| The gap verdict itself | a verbatim figure line, or a derivation from the six-term set, yielding a signed precipitation direction |

**Honest stop.** The shape is derived; the direction is owed. The framework says what an acceptable answer must look like and what would close it, then stops at the limit of what the available instruments distinguish and records the lineage. *"Indiscernibility is not identity."* Neither agreement nor prestige is an instrument.

## 5. Pre-registered refuter (restated from `01`)

The shape claim is refuted if (1) an aggregate confidence figure decides a community question with a measured loss of zero; (2) two models sharing predecessor code are shown to be independent witnesses for the same residual; (3) all local disagreement is removed by more search at fixed instrument and fixed observations; (4) a figure line yields a signed regional precipitation direction; or (5) a ground reading pays for a projection and its rival alike. These were written before the conclusion.

## 6. One-paragraph version for outreach

> The Tether does not predict whether your region will get wetter or drier; it has no hydrology, and it says so. What it does give is the shape any trustworthy regional-rainfall answer must have. Confidence has to be reported per place and per decision horizon, not as one continental average; the loss from a global projection down to a community decision has to be measured rather than assumed away; model agreement has to be discounted when the models share code, parameterisations or calibration data, so fifty-seven related models are not fifty-seven independent witnesses; "we cannot resolve this at the current resolution" has to be kept separate from "this is unpredictable in principle", because the two need different remedies; and settlement waits for realised rainfall, not for agreement among readers. The framework stops there. It returns a shape, the refuters written in advance, and the instruments that would close the open direction. The local direction stays open until the ground pays.
