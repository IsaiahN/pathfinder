- **Domain 4, verification.** Actors: expert readers and public venues. Currency: residuals. Anchor: the ground, never agreement. Independence here is zero in this simulated run.

Order by dependency: describe the residual, index per slot, check independence, measure the round trip, separate budget from principle, wait for the ground.

## Round trip gap R_T

The Symbols table defines R_T as *"The gap between what was sent up and what came back down"*, equal to `gap(x, (T_E ∘ T_A)(x))`. Fig 4 :30 requires it to be measured rather than assumed. I ran the round trip on the condensation this folder actually uses.

1. **Sent up (x):** the verbatim figure set, chiefly Fig 1 :19 and :22 (per-slot residual), Fig 4 :30 (round trip), Fig 2 :31-32 and :44 (independence), Fig 8 :40 (budget versus principle), Fig 13 :134 (gap as verdict).
2. **Abstraction (T_A):** the shorthand carried in `07_the_tether_answer.md`: confidence is per place and horizon; measure the loss; discount correlated witnesses; keep the two limits apart; wait for the ground.
3. **Concretisation (T_E):** a community-facing product: a downscaled precipitation map with one confidence class and a list of ensemble members.

**R_T is nonzero.** Four things the shorthand holds do not survive the trip back down:

- **slot indexing** collapses when the downscaled product reports one confidence class for a whole sub-continental region;
- **measured scale loss** is omitted when the downscaled map is presented without its coarse parent and the gap between them;
- **independence** is lost when a 57-member correlated ensemble is reported as 57 witnesses;
- **budget versus principle** disappears when a low signal-to-noise result is reported as "no robust change" rather than as an instrument limit.

One thing survives cleanly: a single aggregate confidence cannot decide a community question, and the loss must be measured. That is the residue worth sending. R_T was **not** measured numerically, because no ground reading was taken. It is recorded qualitatively as the enumerated set of dropped constraints. Treating the formula as identical to the figures would set R_T to zero, which is the dead-reckoning error Fig 4 :30 names.
