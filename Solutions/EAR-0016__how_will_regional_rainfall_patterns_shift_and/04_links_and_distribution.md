## Two things in the wider literature that bear directly on this question

1. **Scale loss is measurable, not rhetorical.** Kendon et al. 2023 found a roughly 4x versus 2.6x difference in projected extreme hourly rainfall frequency between a convection-permitting model and a coarser regional model. That is the round-trip gap `R_T` of Fig 4 :30 made concrete: *"So take the round trip and measure the gap."* Do not present a downscaled map as if the coarse description had held all the information.
2. **Ensemble size overstates independence.** Pathak et al. 2023 and Abramowitz et al. 2019 both report effective model counts far below nominal counts. This is the collapse Fig 2 :44 names: *"The effective-sample-size correction for correlated observations is standard in survey statistics and meta-analysis; the collapse is the same arithmetic read as a warning."*

## Distribution: offer, do not push

Figure-grounded rules:

- **Fig 10 :52** — *"Bring a characterised residual, never the source."* Sending the full framework before anyone has described a residual sends a recording, not a generator.
- **Fig 10 :42** — *"A verifier that can reconstruct the claim cannot verify it."* A reader who has absorbed the framework before giving a stage-1 verdict has joined the frame being checked.
- **Fig 9 :26-28** — *"describe it before you go looking, or any frame you pick will seem to fit."* Ask what they would expect to see if the shape claim were true before offering the links.
- **Fig 2 :40** — *"agreement about the world is worthless when the agreeing frames share an evidence pool"* (with the leading disqualification clause recorded in the audit). Later agreement is never pooled as corroboration.
- **Fig 7 :35** — *"A room's agreement is variation, not a verdict. Selection between rooms is where the ground votes."* No mass send, no list ranked by likely agreement.

**Recommended order in a reply:** (1) the shape claim and its pre-registered refuter; (2) ask what they would count as a failure and what ground would settle it; (3) record their answer; (4) only then the links, with the note that the figures, not this summary, are the authority.
