## 1. A shape is a constraint, not a filler

The canonical figures README states the limit: the framework returns *"what a missing thing would have to look like (a shape, not a name)."* Fig 9 :32: *"you get a kind of thing, never the answer itself."* Fig 13 :134: *"It cannot say which primitive is missing, because naming it would mean already holding it."*

From *The Library of Babel*: *"You can cut your search short if the set of books returned is small and descriptive enough that it provides a general shape of the answer that satisfies your question."*

So a **shape** is the narrowed form of an answer you can state before you can name it. It constrains; it does not fill. For this question the shape is: a record that only grows, a reach that can fall, a refusal that carries the change, and a seat that keeps the channel open. A specific architecture, a specific memory system, or a specific claim that "the model is now correct" is a name, not a shape.

## 2. How a shape is obtained

- *Library of Babel*: use the vocabulary you already hold that is closest to the thing, look for recurring new terms that overlap with it, recurse. *"With each new term gained, you narrow the space by definition and deepen your vocabulary."*
- Fig 6 :30: *"The description travels. Fails by being sharpened into terms only we can read. Cast it in effect terms."* Fig 9 :30 to :31: *"your words are borrowed too, so describe the effect rather than the cause."* This is why section 0 of `01` states the residual in effect terms before naming any figure.
- *Decoding Tacit Knowledge*: *"When contact isn't possible, the best you can send is a plain description of what you'd expect to see, because anyone can check that against the world."*
- *Library of Babel* on stopping: *"You will know that you are done because the finish line will also be the starting place, as there is nowhere else to search and nothing else that can describe the answer better."*

## 3. What licenses a name

Only two things, both outside the shape itself:

1. **The ground settles it.** THE_FORMULA step 5: *"the ground settles it. Verified only by what pays. A gate is not the ground."* A model may be asserted to track the world only when an instrument, experiment or board pays for that claim.
2. **A provenanced import from a frame whose closure differs** (Fig 6 :23, Fig 8 :50 to :52), with a source gate and a shadow test.

*They're Scanning Books When They Should* sharpens this: *"The real import is not a new term in the existing space. It is a new dimension of the space."* And: *"Declaring the primitives does not make them neutral. It makes the choice visible."* Declaring that a system has a "world model" does not make the model correspond to the world; it makes the commitment visible so it can be checked.

## 4. Why "the model is up to date" is the forbidden error

*The Hard Problem is About Source* gives the mechanism. An instrument that reads green *"cannot comment on the source."* Several underlying causes can create identical readings. *"If several paths create the same behavior, then behavior is a projection with lossy information."* It was the spectrometer, not an argument, that resolved the case. And: *"An instrument that can't distinguish these cases demonstrates its own inadequacy."*

A system's report that its model is current is a reading, not a source discriminate. It cannot distinguish "I am right," "the channel is dead," and "the world did not change." Fig 10 :27 to :28 names the correct output: *"you have lost touch."* What is owed is a better instrument, not a more confident report. This is the essay-level counterpart of F4 in `01_figure_grounded_audit.md`.

## 5. The shape of this particular question

*How Intelligence Actually Works* defines intelligence as *"the emergent result of extensive mapping of a complex system against unknown and unforgiving environments,"* and says *"the mapping is the intelligence."* That is a direct statement that a world model is not a possession added to a system; it is the mapping itself, and it only has a shape under contact. The same essay states *"indiscernibility is not identity,"* which is why a model that behaves correctly on every available probe is still not thereby a copy of the world.

*How a Society of Agents Would Work* adds the temporal case that matters here. It says of a single agent running over time: *"a recent past self is, in Figure 8's words, 'one frame wearing two names.'"* Persistence by self-similarity is not independence; a wide gap and a ground are needed to detect drift. And *"Inside a run the arrangement persists and nothing is re-earned"* gives the precise boundary condition on persistence.

*The Recursive Self-Improvement Trap* contributes two things. First, the validator rule: *"the output is only as good as the validator. The agent, or the tool, or the frame, or the theory, cannot validate itself."* Second, a direct conflict with the strong reading of this question: *"This is why world models will not work. You cannot recreate our reality. It is combinatorically impossible. The best you could do is take traces of patterns."* I treat this as an objection to a copy theory, not as a verdict against a shape that tracks a ground, and I record both readings in `01` and `09`. It is not smoothed over.

## 6. Prestige is not action

From *The Recursive Self-Improvement Trap*: *"Prestige must never be allowed to validate an action directly... Prestige can fund an action. It can give an action a budget to be tried. But it cannot settle an action. Only the ground can settle it. The Ground Maintainer must be blind to prestige."*

A widely cited architecture, a famous lab, or an impressive benchmark is a budget to try the claim, not a settlement of it. The legitimate ask is the opposite of an endorsement campaign: give the claim a ground to be tried against and let the ground settle it.

## 7. My own audit is a simulation, not a ground reading

Same essay: *"LLMs can quickly simulate an approximate answer/output with the formula... However, the truly testable output would need to be derived from the real architecture or working the formula yourself."* LLM output is *"simulacra."*

So `01_figure_grounded_audit.md`, `06_isomers_and_order.md` and `09_watson_answer.md` are **simulated derivations** checked for internal consistency against the figures. They are **not** tests of the claim. They cannot settle anything they report on. Every substantive claim carries `general`, `specified` or `grounded` mode, and none is `grounded`.

## 8. Honest limits of this section

- Seven of the eight canonical essays were opened and quoted. The eighth was not opened in this run and is not quoted. If a reader finds that the eighth essay changes the reading of "shape," this section must be revised and the change logged in `01` rather than silently absorbed.
- All seven opened essays share one author with the figures. Their agreement with the figure verdict is expected and carries little independent weight (Fig 2 :40: *"agreement about the world is worthless when the agreeing frames share an evidence pool"*).
- The essays are philosophical grounding, not authority over the figures. Where they disagree, the figures govern. The world-models passage in the RSI Trap is recorded as a live conflict, not resolved by appeal to the author's other work.
