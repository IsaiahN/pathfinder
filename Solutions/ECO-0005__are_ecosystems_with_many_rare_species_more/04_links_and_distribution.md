## Two things in the ecology literature that bear directly on this question

1. **Both branches occur in the same systems.** Mouillot 2013 finds rare species disproportionately supporting vulnerable functions and also supplying insurance against future uncertainty. This is not a contradiction once the residual is named: the sign depends on which function and which perturbation are at stake. Use this to explain why the question is a union, not a single question with two candidate answers.
2. **The definition of rarity is the comparison.** Jain 2014 finds novel contributions under some definitions and redundancy under others; Violle 2017 formalises twelve forms; Rabinowitz's three axes (abundance, range, habitat specificity) recur throughout. A threshold chosen after seeing the data is a convention that decides an outcome, which Fig 10 :47 forbids: *"a convention that decides an outcome has stopped being one."*

## Distribution: offer, do not push

Figure-grounded reasons:

- **Fig 10 :52** *"Bring a characterised residual, never the source."* Send the residual and its refuter first. A recipient who has absorbed the framework before being asked for a verdict has joined the frame being checked.
- **Fig 4 :22** *"Only methods cross up. Only head starts come down."* Sending the whole architecture before anyone has described a residual sends a recording, not a generator.
- **Fig 9 :27-28** *"describe it before you go looking, or any frame you pick will seem to fit."* Ask which function and which perturbation they mean before giving them the figure set.
- **Fig 2 :35** *"Alignment is triangulation, not negotiation. A mirror cannot correct you."* Do not ask for agreement, and do not select recipients for likely agreement.
- **Fig 2 :40** *"disqualifying for a verdict: agreement about the world is worthless when the agreeing frames share an evidence pool"* (full line). A list ranked by receptiveness is a list selected to agree.

**Recommended order in a reply:** (1) the shape claim and the pre-registered refuter; (2) the question "what would you count as a failure, and which anchor are you ranging against?"; (3) record their verdict; (4) only then offer the local figure paths and the five paper links, with the note that the figures, not the summary, are the authority.

## The two-stage offer, restated

Stage 1 is the residual, the shape claim and its refuter, with no theory attached. Stage 2 is opt-in only: if they are curious, offer the figure text and the ecology links. Stage 3 is the formula as a method to reuse, with the instruction that each user names an anchor, writes a refuter before the run and states the mode of what they get.

Stage 1 before stage 2 is Fig 10 :52 and Fig 2 :45, *"Frames range against the ground. They do not negotiate with it."* Stage 2 is opt-in by construction: once a recipient has absorbed the theory, their agreement stops being evidence, so the stage-1 verdict must be recorded before any theory is shown (Fig 2 :40). Stage 3 sends a method, which is permitted (Fig 4 :22), but every use still needs a ground and a refuter, because a frame cannot check the direction of its own question.

**Hard precondition:** stages 1 to 3 only work if the stage-1 shape survives. It does in this folder only as `general` and `specified`, never as a measured verdict. If a recipient names an anchor and a test that falsifies it, the shape must be withdrawn, not defended.
