## 1. A shape is a constraint, not a filler, and not a signed answer

The canonical rule: the framework returns *"what a missing thing would have to look like (a shape, not a name)."* Fig 9 :32: *"you get a kind of thing, never the answer itself."* Fig 13 :134: *"Unreachable is a verdict here, not a silence,"*

From *The Library of Babel* (opened 2026-11-23): *"you can cut your search short if the set of books returned is small and descriptive enough that it provides a general shape of the answer that satisfies your question."*

For ECO-0005 the shape is not "rarity is good" or "rarity is bad". It is this: **any settling answer must range a per-slot residual against a named anchor, must treat rarity as a position whose valence is relative to that residual, must enumerate contacts outward until the cascade stops mattering, and must keep the union branches separate until the ground decides.** That constrains; it does not fill. It refuses the menu the question offers.

## 2. How the shape is obtained here: proximity, then unbundling

*Library of Babel*: *"If you don’t know the right terms to search for, you will have to use the existing vocabulary you do know that is closest to the words that you wish to describe and grasp the shape of."* And: *"With each new term gained, you are able to narrow the space by definition and deepen your vocabulary."*

The bootstrapped vocabulary in this solution: abundance distribution, occupancy, habitat specificity, functional distinctiveness, functional redundancy, insurance, vulnerability, response trait, effect trait, cascade, contact, anchor, slot. Each term narrowed the question. The decisive narrowing was from "many rare species" to "which rarity definition, which function, which perturbation, which slot".

Fig 6 :30: *"The description travels. Fails by being sharpened into terms only we can read. Cast it in effect terms."* Fig 9 :30-31: *"your words are borrowed too, so describe the effect rather than the cause."* This is why the residual in `01` was stated as a prediction-versus-observation gap rather than as a claim about diversity.

*Decoding Tacit Knowledge* (opened 2026-11-23): *"When contact isn’t possible, the best you can send is a plain description of what you’d expect to see, because anyone can check that against the world."* Hence the outreach email asks what the recipient would expect to see, not whether they endorse a theory.

## 3. The bundle case is the central essay teaching for this question

*Library of Babel*: *"Perhaps your problem was a bundle or conflation of several problems masquerading as one? You will need to search all of those problems individually to find their answers and then combine them in a satisfactory way."*

Fig 9 :54 says the same in the figure language: *"And when the split lands on a union rather than a partition, unbundle before you search again."* The question's "A or B" structure is exactly a bundle masquerading as one question. Both A and B are realised in the empirical literature (Mouillot 2013 reports both), so a forced choice would be an artefact of the menu, not a finding. The shape requires splitting by function, perturbation, scale and rarity definition before any verdict is attempted.

*Library of Babel* also gives the stopping rule: *"you will know that you are done because the finish line will also be the starting place"*, and the paradox that by then you have *"composed the answer through the process of locating it."* Here the finish line is the named anchor: once the function and perturbation are fixed, the question has already changed into a checkable empirical one and the framework's work is done.

## 4. What licenses a name, and why this question must not name one

Two things, both outside the shape itself:

1. **The ground settles it.** `THE_FORMULA.md` step 5: *"the ground settles it. Verified only by what pays. A gate is not the ground."* A measurement of a named function under a named perturbation would settle which branch is live for that case.
2. **A provenanced import from a frame whose closure differs** (Fig 6 :23, Fig 8 :50-52), with a source gate and a shadow test. An ecological term imported from community ecology into the figure vocabulary must be recorded as an import, not presented as a figure derivation.

*The Hard Problem is About Source* (opened 2026-11-23) gives the discriminator rule. An instrument that detects green *"cannot comment on the source"*; *"If several paths create the same behavior, then behavior is a projection with lossy information."* It was *"proven wrong not by argument, but by the arrival of a better instrument."* Applied here: a study that reports "ecosystem stability" as a single green reading cannot tell us whether the source was insurance from redundant rare species, vulnerability from a unique rare mutualist, or a scale effect. What is owed is a discriminating measurement, not a name. Fig 13 :134 again: *"Unreachable is a verdict here, not a silence,"*

## 5. Unmaintained and maintained habitats: the essay grounding for the two domains

*The Recursive Self-Improvement Trap* (opened 2026-11-23): *"Natural selection is what the loop looks like when there is no one maintaining the ground."* And: *"The same code runs when there is a Ground Maintainer. It runs in cultivation, when humans select which plants to breed. It runs in habitat formation, when beavers build dams and change the selection pressures."*

This is the essay-level basis for separating D1 (unmaintained habitat) from D2 (maintained habitat) in the census. Both run the same loop with different actors, currency and ground, so their verdicts must be kept separate and never averaged. Fig 11 :63: *"Which is why a managed ecosystem is permanent work rather than a solved state."* The asymmetry in Fig 11 :61, *"Introduction is available; exclusion is not,"* is the operational difference between the two domains.

## 6. Prestige is not action

*The Recursive Self-Improvement Trap*: *"Prestige must never be allowed to validate an action directly."* And: *"Prestige can fund an action. It can give an action a budget to be tried. But it cannot settle an action. Only the ground can settle it. The Ground Maintainer must be blind to prestige."* Also: *"You may all agree and be wrong. You may set your clock to someone else’s, who was setting your clock to yours, and be off by many deviations over time."*

A campaign that gathers famous ecologists to agree with the shape is the named collapse, not corroboration. Across the five ecology papers in this folder, prestige and citation counts are filters for which papers to read first, never witnesses for the verdict. This implements Fig 9 :60, *"Use filters for the budget and witnesses for the verdict. Never let a filter hand you a verdict."*

## 7. My own audit is a simulation, not a ground reading

Same essay: *"LLMs can quickly simulate an approximate answer/output"*; *"However, the truly testable output would need to be derived from the real architecture or working the formula yourself."* The essay calls the alternative *"simulacra"*. So `01_figure_grounded_audit.md`, `06`, `07`, `09` and `11` are simulated derivations checked for internal consistency against the figures. They are **not** tests of the ecological claim and must be labelled as such. They cannot settle anything they report on.

## 8. Correction recorded rather than erased

Earlier in this session I considered treating the shared authorship across the five ecology papers as a strength, since independent papers agreeing would be stronger than one. That reading is **retracted**. By Fig 2 :40, *"agreement about the world is worthless when the agreeing frames share an evidence pool"* (full line), shared authors and citations make the agreement expected. The correct form is recorded in `01` F6: the individual measurements are checkable, their agreement is one pool talking. Both readings are visible here so the correction cannot be mistaken for a settled answer.

## 9. The shape, in one sentence

For this question "shape" means: a union split by named function, perturbation, scale and rarity definition, with per-slot residuals ranged against a non-updating anchor, with contacts enumerated outward, and with the sign left open until a source-discriminating instrument closes it. That is a kind of answer, not an answer (Fig 9 :32), and it is the honest stop.
