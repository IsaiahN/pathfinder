## QUESTION

Decide, in effect terms, what the Tether says about the claim that economic growth continues through improving productivity, or is constrained by aging populations and resource limits. Option A: treat it as one dial, pick a winner, and return a growth forecast. Option B: treat it as a union of four conditions, return per-domain verdicts with modes, a shape, refuters written in advance, and the instruments that would close each verdict. What each leads to: A produces an averaged number that hides the live per-slot residual, installs a measure as a verdict, and spends the independence of the people checking it. B produces a checkable shape, a declared gap where no ground exists, and a set of failure conditions. The question as posed is a bundle of several problems masquerading as one, so B is the only option that preserves the residual.

## DOMAIN CENSUS

R by arity, symmetry and scale, then one line per domain with actors, currency, anchor, partition or union, and order by dependency.

- **R (the residual).** The gap between what the current aggregate measure predicts for growth and what the ground returns per sector slot when age structure and material channels change. Arity: three terms (arrangement, contact structure, finiteness), so a **union compound**, not a two-sided dial. Symmetry: "improving productivity" is ordered, a sequence or production; aging and resource stocks are threshold relations, comparisons against a count or a stock. Scale: rearrangement is within-frame, aging is population/habitat, material limits are substrate/closure. Fig 9 :57: "a case in two conditions is not a harder case of either."
- **Domain 1, productivity and arrangement.** Actors: firms, workers, capital, methods, research, technologies. Currency: output per combined input, priced in bits and actions that do not add (Fig 12 :125-127). Anchor: a market that pays, a measurement that does not update on the frame's own estimate. Type: composition inside the closure, a partition question about which arrangements are legal.
- **Domain 2, demographics and habitat.** Actors: cohorts, dependents, caregivers, automation, schools, health systems, migration. Currency: contact (workers per dependent, care relations). Anchor: the same market ground read per slot plus a demographic count that does not move with a forecast. Type: habitat, enumerated not composed.
- **Domain 3, materials and closure.** Actors: energy, materials, stocks, conversion technology, research frames that could supply a new atom. Currency: a difference that can be spent against finiteness. Anchor: physical stock-and-flow measurement. Type: closure wall, with import as the only wall-moving operator.
- **Domain 4, measurement and aggregate.** Actors: statistical agencies, national accounts, index-number constructors. Currency: description length and settlement cost. Anchor: the ground, never the measure. Type: frame-internal residual, a convention that can become a verdict.
- **Partition or union.** Union, with overlaps that are compounds with their own loops. Productivity research consumes energy and is shaped by the age of researchers; demographics changes which slots have live productivity residuals; the resource wall conditions which arrangements are affordable.
- **Order by dependency.** Measure per slot first; describe habitat and closure before choosing a lever; refuse the one-dial term before minting; settle each first-order molecule before composing; import only after the wall is characterised in effect terms.
- **Strain test applied.** The reinforcement cycle of compound growth (Fig 12 :121) and the closure wall (Fig 8 :32) govern different quantities, loop dynamics versus reach, so no strain after re-census. The "shape, not a name" limit governs missing primitives, so naming a held atom or a provenanced binding is allowed. Both strains are recorded in `01` section 6.

## VERDICT (from the figures)

The question is a union, so there is no single winner. Productivity improvement is a rearrangement inside a closure and adds no atom; new capability requires an import with a source gate and a shadow test. Aging changes capability through habitat and contact, and its valence is residual-relative, so no standing verdict on "older workers" survives a change of residual. Finite material stocks are a closure wall that efficiency cannot move; which atom would move it is a `gap`. An aggregate growth rate hides the per-slot signal and a measure that decides budgets stops being a measure. Four verdicts are returned, modes attached, never averaged. Mode: `specified` on the structural claims, `general` on the shape, not `grounded`, unmeasured, `candidate`. The strongest outreach point is the measured refusal: no growth rate, no named winner, no last answer.

- **cost if wrong:** bounded and locatable. The packet is mode-labelled, states refuters before the conclusion and records the lineage, so an error is findable rather than invisible. The real cost is spending the independence of outside readers by sending a forecast instead of a generator, which Fig 2 :42 prices: "Buy independence in proportion to the cost of being wrong." That cost is avoidable by leading with the per-slot residual and the four refuters.
- **undoable:** yes. The aggregate framing, the wall/habitat conflation and the measure-as-verdict reading can be removed without touching the figure lines; `07_the_tether_answer.md` and `08_expert_verification_packet.md` already do so. A sent claim can be superseded by the corrected packet, and no primitive has to be withdrawn. The binding of an economic stock onto the held energy atom is provenance, not a wall-moving import, so it can be revised without rebuilding the derivation.
- **reversed by:** a ground reading, not agreement. A per-slot, non-updating market or physical reading showing growth continuing or stopping in a named economy over a named interval settles the empirical instance. A source discriminator that separates a new bond from an imported atom would name the source of a productivity jump. A partition proof with an assignable shared fraction would break the union verdict; a demonstration that composition added an atom would break the closure verdict; a demonstration that agent-only improvement moved the ground would break the habitat verdict. Until one of these fires, the verdict stands as `candidate`. Prestige or agreement is not such an instrument.
