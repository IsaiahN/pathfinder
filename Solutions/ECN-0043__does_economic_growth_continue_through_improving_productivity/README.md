# Tether outreach: figure-grounded package for ECN-0043

**Question:** Does economic growth continue through improving productivity, or do aging populations and resource limits constrain it?

**Source:** economics questions.txt line 59. Section 5, Economic growth, productivity, and technology.

**Mode:** `specified` on the per-domain structural verdicts, `general` on the overall shape, not `grounded`. Every substantive file states that this is a simulated derivation checked for internal consistency against the figures, NOT a ground reading, so it cannot settle the question it reports on.

**Status:** done.

**Registry status:** pending.

## Restatement as an effect

What is being decided is whether a measured quantity, the rate of change of aggregate output, is driven onward by a compositional change (more output per unit input) or held back by two different conditions (an age structure with fewer workers per dependent, and finite spent differences in the form of energy and materials). Option A says growth continues through productivity. Option B says aging and resource limits constrain it. Each option leads to a different measurement, a different anchor, and a different lever. The figures return the shape of the answer, not a growth rate, and the two sides turn out to be a union of overlapping conditions rather than a partition with one winner.

## What an answer would look like (Direction step)

Written down and bound as an ordinary term before the run, per `THE_FORMULA.md` "Direction": an answer would (1) say which residuals stay live when output is indexed per sector slot instead of aggregated, (2) name the actors, currency and anchor in the productivity, demographic and material domains, (3) give a per-domain verdict with a mode, and (4) state what instrument would settle each verdict and what would refute it. Anything that instead returns a single national percentage, a named winning lever, or a standing list of growth drivers fails this description and is refused in `01` and `07`.

## What is deliberately not here

- No personal emails of named individuals.
- No ranking of people by how likely they are to agree.
- No bulk-send plan.
- No national growth forecast, TFP percentage, dependency threshold or resource-stock number presented as a figure result.

Three reasons, in the figures' own words:

1. **Agreement is not an anchor.** Fig 2 :35: "Alignment is triangulation, not negotiation. A mirror cannot correct you." A list selected for receptiveness manufactures the disqualifying correlation (Fig 2 :40).
2. **The measure can become the thing it measures.** Fig 10 :47: "a convention that decides an outcome has stopped being one." Selecting recipients by agreement about GDP growth would install a convention as a verdict.
3. **Asking for trust before checking spends independence.** Fig 10 :42: "A verifier that can reconstruct the claim cannot verify it." The first contact carries only the residual, the shape claim and its refuter.

Legally, scraping personal emails for unsolicited bulk outreach breaches GDPR and CAN-SPAM style rules in many jurisdictions. Public institutional and opt-in venues avoid that failure as well as the structural one.

## Boundary

Everything in this folder is a simulated derivation. No measurement was taken, no expert was contacted, and no national accounts dataset was recomputed. World sources were opened and quoted, but they are secondary corroboration only. The figures decide. This package cannot settle whether growth will continue in any particular economy; it states the shape any answer must have and the instruments that would close it.
