## 1. A shape is a constraint, not a filler

The canonical figures README states the limit: the framework returns "what a missing thing would have to look like (a shape, not a name)". Fig 9 :32: "you get a kind of thing, never the answer itself." Fig 13 :134: "Unreachable is a verdict here, not a silence."

From *The Library of Babel*: "You can cut your search short if the set of books returned is small and descriptive enough that it provides a general shape of the answer that satisfies your question."

For this question a **shape** is the form an answer must have before any growth rate can be stated. It says: index the residual per sector slot; keep three conditions separate; composition adds no atom; the material wall needs an import; aging is habitat and contact; an aggregate measure can become a verdict. It does not say whether growth will be 2 percent or 4 percent, which technology is the missing atom, or which age structure wins. `Growth continues at X percent` is a name and a forecast. `The residual stays live in the slots where the import has arrived and the channel to a non-updating ground is reachable` is a shape.

The Library essay also names the exact defect in the question as posed: "Perhaps your problem was a bundle or conflation of several problems masquerading as one?" That is F1 of `01`, and it is the reason the either/or is refused.

## 2. How the shape is obtained

- *Library of Babel*: use the vocabulary you already have that is closest to the thing, then correlate it with recurring new terms. "With each new term gained, you narrow the space and deepen your vocabulary." The stop condition: "you have already composed the answer through the process of locating it."
- Fig 6 :30: "The description travels. Fails by being sharpened into terms only we can read. Cast it in effect terms." Fig 9 :30-31: "your words are borrowed too, so describe the effect rather than the cause." The residual in `01` section 0 is written in effect terms before any frame (productivity economics, demography, resource economics) is named.
- Fig 9 :27-28: "describe it before you go looking, or any frame you pick will seem to fit." This is why the domain census precedes the figure lines in `01`.
- Fig 1 :19: "The residual is indexed by object slot, not aggregated." The slot here is the industry, region or cohort, not the national total.

## 3. What licenses a name

Only two things, both outside the shape itself:

1. **The ground settles it.** `THE_FORMULA.md` step 5: "the ground settles it. Verified only by what pays. A gate is not the ground." For this question that means a per-slot, non-updating market or physical reading, not an agreement among forecasters.
2. **A provenanced import from a frame whose closure differs** (Fig 6 :23, Fig 8 :52), with a source gate and a shadow test. The new atom is named only after it crosses and pays.

*They're Scanning Books* sharpens the second: "The real import is not a new term in the existing space. It is a new dimension of the space." That is exactly the shape of the missing atom in F3: not a more efficient version of the held energy and material terms, but an axis that lets the economy perceive and use something the current closure cannot represent. The same essay warns: "A minimum viable set of six terms is a human's careful guess at reality's compression axes; another sensorium's minimum set need not be those six," and "Declaring the primitives does not make them neutral. It makes the choice visible." So the four-domain census is a visible choice, not a discovery of nature's own list.

## 4. Why "TFP growth proved a new productive atom" is the specific forbidden error

*The Hard Problem is About Source* gives the mechanism. An instrument reads green and "cannot comment on the source." Several sources can produce the same reading: "If several paths create the same behavior, then behavior is a projection with lossy information." And: "An instrument that can't distinguish these cases demonstrates its own inadequacy." The essay's conclusion: "The answer is unreached until a better instrument exists." It was the spectrometer, not an argument, that resolved the case.

A TFP residual is the same kind of reading. A rise in output per combined input is compatible with at least three sources: a new bond over held atoms (isomer), a genuinely imported atom (new energy conversion or material), or a changed habitat and contact structure that the residual picks up indirectly (Fig 11 :39). The reading cannot comment on the source. Naming any one of them from the residual alone is the metamerism error. What is owed is a discriminator, not a name.

The *Recursive Self-Improvement Trap* supplies the validation limit: "the output is only as good as the validator. The agent, or the tool, or the frame, or the theory, cannot validate itself." This folder is such an output, hence the simulation label in every file.

## 5. Correction to my own §3 (see `06_isomers_and_order.md` Q1)

My first pass read "shape, not a name" as a ban on naming any productive mechanism. That overstates the rule. The README's phrase is "what a **missing** thing would have to look like (a shape, not a name)." Its subject is a primitive not held in Γ. The energy, network and gradient-shaped terms relevant here are already among the six (Fig 13), so naming them, or binding a measured economic stock onto one of them, is a **provenanced binding**, not the invention of a seventh term.

What remains wrong in the naive reading is not the naming of held atoms; it is the claim that such a binding *moves the wall*. Fig 6 :21: "MINT composes inside the closure and can never add an atom." A binding adds no atom. The resource wall moves only on a genuine import with a source gate and a shadow test. Both claims are now recorded so neither is quietly lost.

## 6. Consequences for this question

**Prestige is not action.** From the *Recursive Self-Improvement Trap*: "Prestige can fund an action. It can give an action a budget to be tried. But it cannot settle an action. Only the ground can settle it." A list of well-known economists who agree that growth will continue or stop is the named collapse, not corroboration (Fig 2 :40). The legitimate ask is a budget to try the four refuters against a ground.

**The union is not a failure to decide.** *Library of Babel*: "Perhaps your problem was a bundle or conflation of several problems masquerading as one?" Splitting the question is the remedy, not an evasion. Fig 9 :57 says the same thing in figure terms.
