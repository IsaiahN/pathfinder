## 2. What the wider literature bears on the question

The eight world sources are secondary. They corroborate figure lines; they never overrule them.

1. **Search versus atoms.** Bloom et al. predict that adding researchers at the same reach buys search, not new atoms. This is the world-facing version of Fig 6 :49-50 and Fig 8 :32. It is the strongest low-overlap corroborator for the productivity-versus-wall distinction inside one economics pool.
2. **The measure depends on its own inputs.** The TFP entry predicts the frame-internal finding of F5, and its note that mineral depletion and pollution are outside the standard measure predicts that the material wall can be invisible in the growth statistic.
3. **Efficiency does not remove the wall.** Jevons predicts that a gain in efficiency inside a closure can raise total use. This is the mechanism shadow of Fig 6 :21.
4. **A measure that becomes a target stops measuring.** Goodhart and Campbell predict Fig 10 :47 from the measurement and accountability side, which is not the same evidence pool as the figures.
5. **Closure is idempotent.** The mathematical entry states the theorem behind Fig 8 :32 in neutral notation. Genuinely low overlap.
6. **Capability sits in the agent-and-habitat loop.** Niche construction predicts Fig 11 :39-40 from evolutionary biology. Genuinely low overlap.
7. **Aging's effect flips with the question.** The population ageing entry contains both the drag and the offset hypothesis. That is evidence for the valence-relative claim, not a conflict with it; the conflict rule was not triggered.
8. **The per-slot residual exists in published statistics.** The BLS releases show TFP rising in some industries and falling in others in the same year, which is what per-slot indexing predicts and aggregate growth hides.

**Overlap declared.** Sources 1, 2, 3 and 8 sit in a broad economics and measurement pool; they are separate documents but not four independent witnesses for the same claim. Sources 4, 5, 6 and 7 come from different fields with little overlap with the framework. No world source conflicts with a figure verdict. Agreement between sources that share an evidence pool is one witness talking, per Fig 2 :40.
