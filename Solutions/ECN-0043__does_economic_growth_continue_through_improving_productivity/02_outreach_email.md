# Outreach draft: check one output before you decide about the framing

**Use:** one to one, or as a cover letter attached to a submission through the public venues in `03_public_contact_points.csv`. **Not** for bulk send.

**Hard precondition:** do not send until the measure link in F5 and the union framing in F1 of `01_figure_grounded_audit.md` are understood by the sender. The two-stage offer collapses on contact if stage 1 is a growth forecast.

**Subject:** One checkable output on growth, productivity and demographic change

> Hi [name],
>
> I have one specific output I would like you to check against what you already know. I am not asking you to read the theory first, and I am not asking you to agree with it. If the output does not survive, there is nothing else to look at.
>
> **The output (`general` on the shape, `specified` on the domain verdicts, not `grounded`):** the question as posed is a union of overlapping conditions, not a two-sided dial. Productivity improvement is a rearrangement inside a frame's closure and adds no new atom, so a TFP residual can move without any new stock appearing. New productive capability needs an import from a frame whose closure differs, with provenance and a shadow test. Aging changes capability through habitat and contact, and the valence of an age group flips with the residual at the slot, so it is not a fixed drag. A finite material stock is a closure wall: efficiency inside the wall changes the flow, not the reach. An aggregate growth rate hides the per-slot residual, and a measure that decides budgets stops being a measure.
>
> This is a shape with four separate verdicts, not a forecast. The framework returns a kind of thing, never the answer itself. It does not return a national growth rate, a TFP percentage, a dependency ratio threshold, or the name of the next general-purpose technology.
>
> **What I am not claiming:** that aging necessarily lowers growth, that resource limits set a date, that any particular technology is the missing import, or that a published TFP series is false. Those are bindings and measurements, not figure results. Until provenance is shown they carry zero evidentiary weight in this derivation.
>
> **What would refute the output:** written down before sending this.
> 1. Show that productivity, aging and resource stocks share one anchor and one currency, with an assignable shared fraction, so the union is actually a partition.
> 2. Show a productive capability composed inside a frame's closure with no import from a differing closure, where the new term was not derivable from held atoms before the change.
> 3. Show that an economy's capability can be raised by improving productive agents in isolation while habitat and contact are held fixed.
> 4. Produce a growth statistic that keeps the per-slot residual, does not update on its own input estimates, and remains a measure when used to allocate budgets.
>
> If any one holds, I retract the corresponding claim. A one-line "this fails because..." is more useful to me than agreement. If you would rather not engage, no reply is needed.
>
> **If it survives, and only then:** the sources are below. The figures are the authority, not my summary of them.
> - Figures text extract with line numbers (local framework file, path in `04_links_and_distribution.md`)
> - Canonical usage rule for the figures (same local framework folder)
> - Working paper behind the "ideas get harder to find" check: https://www.nber.org/papers/w23782
>
> **If you want to use the machinery:** the loop is in the framework files. Run it on a problem you know well and can check against a ground. Write down what would refute your result *before* you run it, and state the mode of what you get. A reported failure is more useful to me than a reported success.

## Two-stage rule for the recipient's verdict

1. **Stage 1.** Send the residual, the union shape, the four-domain verdict summary and the refuter set. Ask: what would you count as a failure here? Record the answer *before* showing any framework material.
2. **Stage 2, opt in only.** If the recipient is curious, send the three links. Their later agreement is never pooled as corroboration (Fig 2 :40). A failure is copied into `11_audit.md` with provenance and re-evaluated by the auditor seat as if it came from any other source.

No follow-up sequence, no reminder chain, no bulk send. One message, one person, one check.
