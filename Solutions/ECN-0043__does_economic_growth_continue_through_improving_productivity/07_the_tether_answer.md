# The Tether's answer on growth, productivity, aging and resources

**Mode: `specified` on the per-domain verdicts, `general` on the structural shape, not `grounded`.** This is a shape and a set of routes, not a forecast. Simulated derivation checked for internal consistency against the figures (`05_what_shape_means.md` section 7), not a ground reading.

## 1. The residual it answers

The question asks whether output growth continues because productivity improves, or whether an aging population and finite resources constrain it. In effect terms the residual is the gap between what the current aggregate measure predicts and what the ground returns at each sector slot when the age structure and the material channels change (Fig 1 :19). The two offered answers are not opposite readings of one dial. They are a **union of overlapping conditions** with different actors, currencies and anchors (Fig 9 :55: "A bundled problem is usually a union of overlapping conditions, not a list of separate ones."). Verdicts are kept separate and never averaged.

## 2. The four verdicts

**D1, productivity and arrangement.** Productivity improvement is mostly a rearrangement of held atoms. Fig 6 :21: "MINT composes inside the closure and can never add an atom." Fig 13 :115: "The set is closed. The arrangements are not." Over a fixed atom set two sectors differing only in bond are isomers (Fig 12 :117), so a TFP residual can move decisively without any new stock appearing. Genuinely new productive capability is an import from a frame whose closure differs (Fig 6 :23), with provenance and a shadow test (Fig 8 :52). Bloom et al. corroborate from the economics of research: effort rises while productivity falls, which is more search at the same reach. **Verdict: `specified`, triangulated. Growth can continue through rearrangement; rearrangement does not add a new atom; a new atom requires an import.** No rate is returned.

**D2, demographics and habitat.** Aging is not a wall of the same kind as a finite stock. It changes the habitat and the channel: which actors are in contact with the productive residual, and which relations carry influence (Fig 11 :43-47). Fig 11 :39: "capability is a property of agent-and-habitat, never of the agent alone." Fig 11 :40: "A system cannot be made more capable by improving the agent in isolation. An improvement that does not change contact changes nothing, however much it improves." The valence of an age group flips with the residual (Fig 11 :49-51), which is why the opened demography source contains both a drag and an offset hypothesis. **Verdict: `specified` on the habitat/contact reading, `gap` on the net sign and size in a named economy per slot.**

**D3, materials and closure.** A finite stock is a closure wall. Fig 8 :32: "no search, however long, adds an atom." Fig 13 :14: "conserved, finite, directionless alone." Efficiency inside the wall changes the flow, not the reach, and the Jevons mechanism corroborates. What can move the wall is an import with a source gate and a shadow test (Fig 6 :12-14). **Verdict: `specified` on the wall and the import requirement, `gap` on which atom would be imported.** Fig 9 :32: "you get a kind of thing, never the answer itself."

**D4, measurement.** An aggregate growth rate hides the live per-slot residual (Fig 1 :22: "Aggregating across slots is how a live signal disappears into an average."), and the TFP residual depends on the frame's own estimates of its inputs. Fig 10 :47: "a convention that decides an outcome has stopped being one." Goodhart's law predicts the same from measurement theory. **Verdict: `specified`, triangulated. The measure is a channel that must be kept reachable, not the ground it points at.**

Kept separate: these four verdicts are not combined into a single yes or no. The overlap between them is a compound with its own loop (Fig 9 :57: "a case in two conditions is not a harder case of either").

## 3. What it explicitly does not say

- Not a growth forecast. No percentage, no date, no country ranking.
- Not "productivity always wins" or "aging and resources always lose." Both are conditions with residual-relative valence.
- Not a derivation of one theory from another, and no seventh primitive. Economic bindings onto the six-term set are provenance, not discoveries of new kinds.
- Not that any published TFP series is false. The claim is about what the series can and cannot discriminate.
- Not `grounded`. No per-slot market or physical reading was taken.
- Not an expert consensus. Fig 2 :35: "Alignment is triangulation, not negotiation. A mirror cannot correct you."

## 4. What would settle it: the ground

A source-discriminating instrument, not a further argument. *The Hard Problem is About Source*: an instrument that reads green "cannot comment on the source"; the spectrometer resolved it. Here:

1. **Per-slot channel.** A market or output reading, indexed by industry and region, that does not update on the frame's own estimate of its inputs. Existing BLS industry TFP releases are the worse instrument to sharpen (Fig 6 :32).
2. **Source discriminator.** An instrument that distinguishes a new bond over held atoms from a genuinely imported atom in a measured productivity jump. Without one, the metamerism error stands.
3. **Physical stock-and-flow reading** at the same slot, which would tell whether a rearrangement changed the reach or only the ratio.
4. **Demographic contact count** that does not move with the forecast: workers per dependent, care relations, cohort-specific contact, read against the same ground.
5. **Standing refuters from `01` section 5.** Partition shown, closure composition shown, isolation improvement shown, non-updating measure produced, isomer pair reduced to a mixture.

If a ground reading returns a per-slot trajectory, it settles the empirical instance, not the shape. The shape stands or falls by the seven refuters.

## 5. The honest stop

**Unreached, not unreachable.** The four-domain census is a visible choice: "A minimum viable set of six terms is a human's careful guess at reality's compression axes; another sensorium's minimum set need not be those six." Declaring it makes the choice visible, not neutral. The answer stops at the limit of the available instruments and records the lineage so the stop is checkable. Fig 8 :39: "So a search that finds nothing is never a proof of absence." Fig 13 :134: "Unreachable is a verdict here, not a silence."

The specific gaps are recorded plainly: no growth rate, no dependency threshold, no resource-stock number, and no name for the atom that would move the wall (Fig 9 :32). Each is a verdict of `gap`, not an invitation to invent a number.

## 6. One-paragraph version for outreach

> The Tether does not pick a side in the growth debate, because the debate bundles four different conditions. Productivity improvement is mostly a rearrangement of what an economy already holds, so it can raise output without adding any new resource; genuinely new capability has to be imported from outside the current frame, with provenance and a check that it explains a real gap. Aging changes capability through the habitat and the channels of contact, and whether it helps or hurts depends on which residual you are looking at. Finite material stocks are a closure wall: efficiency inside the wall changes the flow but not the reach. An aggregate growth rate hides the live sector-by-sector signal, and a measure that decides budgets stops being a measure. The framework returns this shape, a set of refuters written in advance, and the instruments that would close each verdict. It returns no growth percentage, no winning technology and no standing list of good or bad age groups. The center stays open until a ground settles it.
