# Expert verification packet: the Tether on ECN-0043

**What this is:** a claim in a form a productivity researcher, economic historian or demographer can check. Mode, derivation and refuters are stated before the conclusion, so a reader knows what to look for and what would settle it.

**Mode: `specified` on the per-domain claims, `general` on the structural shape, not `grounded`.** No forecast is claimed. This is a shape and a route, not a result.

**Status:** a simulated derivation checked for internal consistency against the figures, not a ground reading. It cannot settle the question it reports on.

## 1. The claim, in one paragraph

The question is a union of four conditions, not a two-sided dial. Productivity improvement is a rearrangement inside a frame's closure and adds no atom, so a TFP residual can move without any new stock appearing; genuinely new capability needs an import with provenance and a shadow test. Aging changes capability through habitat and contact, and the valence of an age group flips with the residual at the slot. A finite material stock is a closure wall that efficiency inside the wall cannot move. An aggregate growth rate hides the per-slot residual, and a measure that decides budgets stops being a measure. The framework returns these shapes, refuters written in advance, and the instruments that would close them. It returns no growth rate, no dependency threshold, no resource-stock date and no named winning technology.

## 2. Three checkable sub-claims

### C1. Productivity, framing checkable by reading Fig 6 and Fig 12

- **Statement:** composition adds no atom; over a fixed atom set, two economies differing only in bond are isomers, so two TFP numbers alone do not establish a source.
- **Source, verbatim:** Fig 6 :21: "MINT composes inside the closure and can never add an atom." Fig 13 :115: "The set is closed. The arrangements are not." Fig 12 :117: "Same atoms, different bond, different substance. The difference is invisible in the formula and decisive in the behaviour."
- **Refuter:** exhibit a productive capability composed inside a frame's closure without any import from a differing closure, where the new term was not derivable from held atoms before the change; or exhibit a compared pair whose difference is only a quantity of the same atom in the same bond, which kills the isomer reading.
- **What to check in twenty minutes:** are the three lines present verbatim in the figure extract? Does the closure claim apply to a frame's expressible reach rather than to the world's physical stock? Is the fixed-atom condition on the isomer claim stated, not assumed?

### C2. Demographics and resources, checkable by a productivity economist or demographer

- **Statement:** aging is a habitat/contact condition with residual-relative valence; material stocks are a closure wall, not a habitat. The two have different anchors and different levers.
- **Source, verbatim:** Fig 11 :40: "A system cannot be made more capable by improving the agent in isolation. An improvement that does not change contact changes nothing, however much it improves." Fig 11 :51: "No standing list of good actors survives a change of residual." Fig 8 :32: "no search, however long, adds an atom." Fig 13 :14: "conserved, finite, directionless alone."
- **Refuter:** raise an economy's capability by improving productive agents in isolation with habitat and contact held fixed; or show an age group with invariant valence across two residuals whose contact structures differ; or show the productive closure not finite in the relevant sense.
- **What to check:** is the habitat-versus-wall split defensible in a real growth-accounting setting, or is a resource stock just another habitat condition? If it is only habitat, the import requirement in C1 collapses and the claim is wrong. A one-line answer on this point is the most valuable check in this packet.

### C3. Measurement, checkable by anyone who reads national accounts

- **Statement:** an aggregate growth rate hides the live per-slot residual, and a measure that decides outcomes stops being a measure.
- **Source, verbatim:** Fig 1 :19: "The residual is indexed by object slot, not aggregated." Fig 1 :22: "Aggregating across slots is how a live signal disappears into an average." Fig 10 :47: "a convention that decides an outcome has stopped being one."
- **Refuter:** produce a growth statistic that keeps the per-slot residual, does not update on the frame's own input estimates, and remains a measure when used to allocate budgets. If such a statistic exists, C3 does not apply to it.
- **What to check:** the BLS releases already publish industry-level TFP (for example 70 of 86 four-digit manufacturing industries fell in 2023). Does that practice already satisfy the refuter, or does the aggregate target still override it when budgets are set?

## 3. What is explicitly not claimed

- Not a theory of everything, and no last room.
- Not a growth forecast, a TFP percentage, a dependency-ratio threshold or a resource-depletion date.
- Not that productivity always wins, or that aging and resources always constrain. Valence is residual-relative.
- Not that any published statistical series is false; the claim is about what a reading can discriminate.
- Not that resource stocks are a seventh primitive. Energy is already one of the six; the economic stock is a binding onto it.
- Not that "shape, not a name" bans naming held atoms or provenanced bindings. It governs missing primitives.
- Not `grounded`. Nothing here was settled by measurement.
- Not an expert consensus. Agreement is not an anchor.

## 5. What would settle it

A ground reading, not agreement. From *The Hard Problem is About Source*: an instrument that reads green "cannot comment on the source"; the spectrometer resolved it. Here the three discriminators are:

1. A per-slot market or output reading that does not update on the frame's own input estimates.
2. An instrument that distinguishes a new bond over held atoms from a genuinely imported atom in a measured productivity jump.
3. A physical stock-and-flow reading at the same slot that tells whether a rearrangement changed reach or only ratio.

## 6. What an expert can check in twenty minutes

1. Read Fig 6 :21, Fig 8 :32, Fig 11 :40 and Fig 1 :19/:22, five short passages.
2. Answer the C2 question in one line: habitat or wall for a resource stock, and why?
3. Say whether BLS industry-level TFP already satisfies the C3 refuter in practice.
4. Name one instrument that would distinguish a new bond from an imported atom in a measured productivity jump, or say why none is possible.
5. Send the refutation. A failure is more useful than an endorsement.
