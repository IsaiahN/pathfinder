## 1. A shape is a constraint, not a filler

The figures README states the limit: the framework returns *"what a missing thing would have to look like (a shape, not a name)."* Fig 9 :32: *"you get a kind of thing, never the answer itself."* Fig 13 :138 repeats it. The Symbols table says of `R` that *"what cannot be perceived yet is residual and simply cannot be read."*

From the Library of Babel essay (fresh): *"you can cut your search short if the set of books returned is small and descriptive enough that it provides a general shape of the answer that satisfies your question."*

So a **shape** is the narrowed form of an answer you can state before you can name its mechanism. It constrains; it does not fill. For this question the shape is:

> less enclosed dark-matter mass in the inner few hundred parsecs than the collisionless cusp prediction, in the low-mass slots, with the channel-corrected inner slope and the fixed maximum rotation speed stated separately.

That is a shape. "It is supernova feedback," "it is warm dark matter," and "it is modified gravity" are names. The shape rules in anything that lowers the central density by the required amount; it does not select among them.

## 2. How the shape is obtained

- **Bootstrapping by proximity** (Library of Babel, fresh): *"If you don't know the right terms to search for, you will have to use the existing vocabulary you do know that is closest to the words that you wish to describe and grasp the shape of."* And: *"With each new term gained, you are able to narrow the space by definition and deepen your vocabulary."*
- **Describe the effect, not the cause** (Fig 6 :30): *"The description travels. Fails by being sharpened into terms only we can read. Cast it in effect terms."* Fig 9 :30-31: *"your words are borrowed too, so describe the effect rather than the cause."* Applied here: say "inner mass deficit at fixed V_max," not first "feedback."
- **When contact is not possible, send a plain expectation** (Decoding Tacit Knowledge, exemplar 2026-11-22): *"When contact isn't possible, the best you can send is a plain description of what you'd expect to see, because anyone can check that against the world."*
- **Stopping** (Library of Babel, fresh): *"you will know that you are done because the finish line will also be the starting place, as there is nowhere else to search and nothing else that can describe the answer better."* Here that place is not reached: the instrument limit is live (Fig 8 :39).

## 3. What licenses a name

Only two things, both outside the shape itself:

1. **The ground settles it.** Formula step 5: *"the ground settles it. Verified only by what pays. A gate is not the ground."* For this question that means an independent mass measurement, or a simulation with published convergence tests that reproduces the per-slot shape from observed inputs.
2. **A provenanced import from a frame whose closure differs**, with a source gate and a shadow test (Fig 6 :23, Fig 8 :50-52). Pontzen and Governato's mechanism is such an import, and its record is kept.

*They're Scanning Books* (exemplar, 2026-11-22) sharpens this: *"The real import is not a new term in the existing space. It is a new dimension of the space."* And: *"Declaring the primitives does not make them neutral. It makes the choice visible."* The six terms and the cusp prediction are a visible choice of closure, not nature's own list.

**Correction to my own reading.** I initially wrote that "shape, not a name" forbids naming any mechanism. That overstates the rule. The README's phrase governs a **missing** primitive, one not held in the library. Naming a candidate import is permitted when its provenance is recorded (F3 of `01_figure_grounded_audit.md`). What remains forbidden is presenting the import as a derivation from the six-term set, or claiming it moves the wall without paying the source gate. Both the earlier overstatement and this narrowing are kept visible so neither is quietly lost.

## 4. Metamerism: why an inferred slope is not a measured profile

*The Hard Problem is About Source* (fresh) gives the mechanism. An instrument reads green and *"the instrument is speaking only about what it measured, it cannot comment on the source."* Several sources (green, yellow plus blue, RGB, CMYK) give the same reading. *"If several paths create the same behavior, then behavior is a projection with lossy information"* and *"An instrument that can't distinguish these cases demonstrates its own inadequacy."* It was the spectrometer, not an argument, that resolved it: *"It was proven wrong not by argument, but by the arrival of a better instrument."*

Here, a rotation curve reads a gravitational potential. It does not comment on the dark-matter source without assumptions about mass-to-light ratio, gas distribution, distance, inclination, non-circular motions and pressure support. Sands et al. (2024) shows that non-equilibrium motions and non-thermal stresses can make the reconstructed curve deviate from the true circular velocity by 50 percent or more, and that the culprit is *"not always visibly obvious in HI observations."*

So the metamerism error here would be to name the source (a cuspy or cored dark-matter profile) from a single projection (the H I rotation curve) without an instrument that discriminates among sources. What is owed is a better instrument, not a name. This is F4 of the audit, and it is the one live strain after re-census.

## 5. The shape for each domain, from the essays

- **D1 simulation frame:** the shape it returns is a universal cusp-like family. The Library of Babel essay's warning applies: *"Perhaps your problem was a bundle or conflation of several problems masquerading as one?"* Treating "the simulation" as one frame hides that dark-matter-only and hydrodynamic closures differ.
- **D2 observation frame:** the shape is a distribution of inner slopes at fixed V_max, not a single value. Decoding Tacit Knowledge's *"plain description of what you'd expect to see"* is the right form: the expected reading, stated so anyone can check it against the world.
- **D3 baryonic frame:** this is the imported frame. *How a Society of Agents Would Work* (exemplar, 2026-11-22) supplies the precedent that frames differ in what they can express, so a union of two frames can reach what neither reaches alone (Fig 8 :45).
- **D4 verification:** the shape must be checkable by someone who does not hold the framework's interior. Fig 10 :42: *"A verifier that can reconstruct the claim cannot verify it."*

## 6. Prestige is not action

*The Recursive Self-Improvement Trap* (fresh): *"Prestige must never be allowed to validate an action directly."* And: *"Prestige can fund an action. It can give an action a budget to be tried. But it cannot settle an action. Only the ground can settle it."*

A campaign that gathers famous names to agree that CDM is in trouble is the named collapse, not corroboration. The legitimate ask is the opposite: give the per-slot claim a budget to be tried, and let an instrument, a data release, or a convergence-tested simulation settle it. The public venues in `03_public_contact_points.csv` are chosen for opt-in checkability, not for the prestige of the people behind them (Fig 9 :60, *"Use filters for the budget and witnesses for the verdict. Never let a filter hand you a verdict."*).

## 7. My own audit is a simulation, not a ground reading

Same essay (fresh): *"LLMs can quickly simulate an approximate answer/output with the formula found in the architecture post."* And: *"the truly testable output would need to be derived from the real architecture or working the formula yourself."* And: *"the output is only as good as the validator. The agent, or the tool, or the frame, or the theory, cannot validate itself."*

So `01_figure_grounded_audit.md`, `06_isomers_and_order.md`, `07_the_tether_answer.md` and `09_watson_answer.md` are simulated derivations checked for internal consistency against the figures. They are **not** tests of the cusp-core question and must be labelled as such. None of them settles what the framework cannot settle on its own: which mechanism, if any, closes the residual.
