## What is deliberately not here

- No personal emails of named individuals.
- No ranking of people by how likely they are to agree with the verdict.
- No bulk-send plan.
- No claim that this derivation decides whether cold dark matter is correct.

Three reasons, in the figures' own words:

1. **A room does not upgrade the label.** Fig 7 :13, *"agreement compounds, evidence does not"*. A larger set of agreeing readers raises the vocabulary and does not add an anchor.
2. **Correlation disqualifies a verdict.** Fig 2 :40, *"disqualifying for a verdict: agreement about the world is worthless when the agreeing frames share an evidence pool"*. Selecting recipients for receptiveness manufactures the correlation that makes their agreement worthless.
3. **Asking for trust before checking spends independence.** Fig 10 :42, *"A verifier that can reconstruct the claim cannot verify it."*

Legally: unsolicited bulk email to harvested personal addresses breaches GDPR and CAN-SPAM style rules in many jurisdictions. Public opt-in and submission channels avoid that practical failure as well as the structural one.
