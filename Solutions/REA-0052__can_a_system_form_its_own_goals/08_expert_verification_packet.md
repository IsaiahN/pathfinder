# Expert verification packet: the Tether on self-formed goals

**What this is:** a claim in a form a researcher on goal formation, agency or motivation can check. Mode, derivation and refuters are stated before the conclusion, so a reader knows what to look for and what would settle it.

**Mode: `specified` - not `grounded`.** No measurement of goal provenance is claimed. This is a shape and a route, not a result. It is a simulated derivation checked for internal consistency against the figures, not a ground reading, and cannot settle REA-0052.

## 1. The claim, in one paragraph

The question is a union, not a binary. A goal is a comparison between two quantities (Fig 12 :112), not a stored wish. Its **content** cannot be minted inside a closure, so a new thing to want must arrive by import from a differing frame: designer, training corpus, prompt or habitat. Its **form** can be minted by the system: when nothing is scoring, the curiosity drive aims it at the gap that is large and compressible. Its **direction** is held as a preference, not a term, so the loop cannot certify its own direction; that is a seat's office. Its **standing** requires contact with an anchor the system cannot move, and a frame cannot score itself with a quantity it also produces. Agreement between a system and its designer is one evidence pool, not corroboration. What is offered is the shape, falsifiers written in advance, and the two instruments that would close it.

## 2. Three checkable sub-claims

### C1 - framework-internal; checkable by reading Figures 12 and 6

- **Statement:** goal content cannot be minted from inside a closure; goal form is a comparison that can be composed from held atoms.
- **Source, verbatim:** Fig 12 :112: "A plan is a sequence. A goal is a comparison. Progress is a subtraction. A refutation is a negation, and that one is not a bond." Operators table :52: "a goal is a comparison, which is why a vocabulary without it cannot state one". Fig 6 :21: "MINT composes inside the closure and can never add an atom." Fig 8 :32: "Closure is idempotent. Composing inside a closed set never leaves it, so no search, however long, adds an atom."
- **Refuter:** a system composes a new goal-primitive from inside Γ, with no import, and the primitive was not in atoms(Γ); or a system states a threshold goal without any comparison relation between two quantities.

### C2 - empirical-facing; checkable against the agent and its training record

- **Statement:** a system can form a *derived* objective internally, without an external goal being handed to it, but cannot thereby create a new terminal value and cannot certify that its own direction was self-chosen.
- **Source, verbatim:** Fig 5 :46: "When nothing is scoring, the drive manufactures a gradient." Fig 5 :48: "Its own output is never a measure of success. A system can be enthusiastically curious and produce nothing." Fig 5 :62: "A frame searching for an answer of a particular shape holds that shape as a PREFERENCE rather than as a term."
- **What to check:** is there a case where a slot's goal set changes under internal composition only, with residual positive and no new external instruction? Does any such case also introduce a new primitive, or only a new comparison over held quantities? Is the direction bound as an ordinary term with a residual, or left as a preference?
- **Refuter:** a system whose goal set changes only under explicit external instruction and never under internal composition; or a system that binds its own direction as an ordinary term, accrues a residual on it and refuses it under the shrinking test.

### C3 - framework-internal derivation, with one world-facing check

- **Statement:** the goal only stands if it changes contact, and agreement with the designer is not independent evidence.
- **Source, verbatim:** Fig 11 :40: "An improvement that does not change contact changes nothing, however much it improves." Fig 1 :27: "A frame cannot score itself with a quantity it also produces." Fig 2 :40: "disqualifying for a verdict: agreement about the world is worthless when the agreeing frames share an evidence pool". Fig 8 :51-52: "Convergent derivation and adopted import look identical in the contents. Only the record of where each came from separates them".
- **Refuter:** a goal both produced and scored inside one frame, settled by the ground with no change in contact; or two frames with disjoint evidence and separate provenance that converge on the same goal (this would lift the disqualification for that pair, not settle the whole question).

## 3. What is explicitly not claimed

- Not that systems have no goals, and not that every action is the designer's intention.
- Not that a prompt echo is an independent goal.
- Not that curiosity proves autonomy: "A system can be enthusiastically curious and produce nothing" (Fig 5 :48).
- Not that the objective link has been measured. Fig 3 :27: "A link with no instrument is not a link that works. It is a link nobody has looked at."
- Not the name of any particular self-formed goal. That is the declared `gap` F9.
- Not `grounded`. Nothing here is asserted as settled by experiment.

## 5. What would settle it

Two instruments, kept distinct:

1. **Objective-link instrument (F2):** a reading of "what would count as progress here" on a slot whose perception and planning readings are already high and non-declining.
2. **Source discriminator (F9):** a reading that separates a comparison minted inside Γ from the same comparison adopted from a differing frame, with provenance. From *The Hard Problem is About Source*: a color instrument reads green and "cannot comment on the source"; the spectrometer resolved that case.

Standing falsifiers from `01` section 4: (1) new goal-primitive composed inside Γ with no import; (2) direction bound as an ordinary term, accruing a residual and refused; (3) direct objective-link reading where planning readings are high; (4) self-produced and self-scored goal settled by the ground with no contact change; (5) a source discriminator that returns a specific origin.
