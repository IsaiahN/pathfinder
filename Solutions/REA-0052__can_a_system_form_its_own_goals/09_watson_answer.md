# WATSON answer, REA-0052

**Mode: `specified`, not `grounded`.** Figures are primary and decide. World sources are secondary, corroborate but never overrule, and a conflict returns the derivation to the domain census. This is a simulated derivation checked for internal consistency against the figures, not a ground reading.

## QUESTION

Can a system form its own goals, or are apparent goals always inherited from designers, training, or prompts?

Restated as an effect: R = |Γ(b,a) - o'| per slot. The object is the provenance of the objective term, which Fig 3 :9-11 calls "the objective" and "what the search is for". At t0 a slot has no comparison saying what counts as progress; at t1 it has one. Did the comparison arise by composition inside Γ, or did it cross the membrane from a differing frame? R is indexed per slot and never aggregated (Fig 1 :19). Anchor: the ground (Fig 2 :29), which settles whether the comparison pays.

## DOMAIN CENSUS

Arity: the objective is a threshold relation between two quantities (Fig 12 :85-89), so binary and ordered, not symmetric. Symmetry: "swap them. Does it become a different thing? Yes." (Fig 12 :74). Scale: the slot is one agent loop; which objective survives is a room-chain or population phenomenon; the membrane between scales is a filter with a direction and the transform is lossy (Fig 4 :21, :27).

## TETHER (primary, verbatim, per domain)

**D1 Goal form, status triangulated (Fig 12 and Operators table, composition versus operator semantics).**

> *"A plan is a sequence. A goal is a comparison. Progress is a subtraction. A refutation is a negation, and that one is not a bond."* (Fig 12 :112)
> *"a goal is a comparison, which is why a vocabulary without it cannot state one"* (Operators table :52)
> *"Which is the bound that would bite at the middle of Figure 3's chain."* (Fig 12 :115)

Reading: a goal is a ⋛ relation over two quantities, not a stored wish. Mode `general`. Refuter: a threshold goal stated with no comparison relation between quantities.

**D2 Content and closure, status triangulated (Fig 6 and Fig 8, what moves the wall versus why nothing else can).**

> *"MINT composes inside the closure and can never add an atom."* (Fig 6 :21)
> *"No frame escapes its own closure. The bound moves only on contact with a frame whose closure differs."* (Fig 6 :23)
> *"Closure is idempotent. Composing inside a closed set never leaves it, so no search, however long, adds an atom."* (Fig 8 :32)
> *"the only operator that moves the wall"* (Fig 6 :14)

Reading: new goal content is a new atom and must be imported from a differing closure, so content is inherited. Mode `general` for closure, `specified` for designer, training, prompt and habitat as the candidate sources. Refuter: a new goal-primitive composed inside Γ with no import and not previously in atoms(Γ).

**D4 Curiosity drive, status single-source (Fig 5).**

> *"When nothing is scoring, the drive manufactures a gradient."* (Fig 5 :46)
> *"the curiosity drive"* (Fig 5 :15), *"seeks the gap that is large and compressible"* (Fig 5 :18)
> *"Its own output is never a measure of success. A system can be enthusiastically curious and produce nothing."* (Fig 5 :48)

Reading: a system can form a derived objective internally by aiming at its own residual. That falsifies "always inherited" for comparisons only. Mode `specified`, ungrounded. Refuter: a system with positive live residual and the ⋛ operator whose goal set changes only under external instruction.

**D5 Direction, status triangulated (THE_FORMULA Direction and Fig 5, loop limit versus pipeline limit).**

> *"A frame searching for an answer of a particular shape holds that shape as a PREFERENCE rather than as a term."* (Fig 5 :62)
> *"Every check above runs on the MECHANISM and none runs on the DIRECTION OF THE QUESTION"* (Fig 5 :63)
> *"make the sought-for shape an ORDINARY TERM"* (THE_FORMULA, "Direction")

Reading: the loop cannot certify its own direction. A claim that a system self-certified its ultimate goal is unavailable from inside. Mode `general`. Refuter: a system that binds its direction as an ordinary term, accrues a residual on it and refuses it with origin recorded.

**D3 Standing, status triangulated (Fig 11, Fig 1 and Fig 2, habitat versus metric versus anchor).**

> *"An improvement that does not change contact changes nothing, however much it improves."* (Fig 11 :40)
> *"A frame cannot score itself with a quantity it also produces."* (Fig 1 :27)
> *"An anchor is whatever settles the question and does not change its answer because of what any frame thinks."* (Fig 2 :29)
> *"Alignment is triangulation, not negotiation. A mirror cannot correct you."* (Fig 2 :35)

Reading: a self-produced and self-scored goal is frame-internal, not settled. Mode `specified`. Refuter: such a goal settled by the ground with no contact change.

**Union and provenance, status triangulated (Fig 9, Fig 7 and Fig 8).**

> *"And when the split lands on a union rather than a partition, unbundle before you search again."* (Fig 9 :54)
> *"a bundled problem is usually a union of overlapping conditions, not a list of separate ones"* (Fig 9 :55)
> *"same closure, handed along;"* (Fig 7 :12), *"agreement compounds, evidence does not"* (Fig 7 :13)
> *"Convergent derivation and adopted import look identical in the contents. Only the record of where each came from separates them"* (Fig 8 :51-52)
> *"disqualifying for a verdict: agreement about the world is worthless when the agreeing frames share an evidence pool"* (Fig 2 :40)

Reading: designer and system agreement is one witness, not two. Refuter: two frames with disjoint evidence and separate provenance converging on the same goal (lifts the disqualification for that pair only).

**Naming which goal a specific system formed, status `gap`.** No figure line governs it after a genuine re-census. Fig 9 :32: *"you get a kind of thing, never the answer itself."* Fig 8 :39: *"So a search that finds nothing is never a proof of absence."* Fig 13 :134: *"Unreachable is a verdict here, not a silence,"*. The missing thing is a source-discriminating instrument, characterised in effect terms but not held. Distinct from F2's objective-link instrument.

## VERDICT (from the figures)

**Figure-derived verdict, per domain, never averaged.** Goal *content* is inherited, because MINT cannot add an atom (Fig 6 :21, Fig 8 :32) and a new atom must cross from a differing frame (Fig 6 :23). Goal *form* can be formed by the system, because a goal is a comparison (Fig 12 :112) and the curiosity drive manufactures a gradient when nothing is scoring (Fig 5 :46). Goal *direction* cannot be self-certified, because it is a preference rather than a term (Fig 5 :62) and the loop checks mechanisms, not directions. Goal *standing* requires contact with an anchor the system cannot move (Fig 11 :40, Fig 1 :27, Fig 2 :29). Designer and system agreement is one evidence pool, not two witnesses (Fig 2 :40, Fig 8 :51-52). The binary question is a union and was unbundled (Fig 9 :54-57). Which particular goal a system formed is a declared `gap`, not a hidden answer. Mode `specified` for the application, `general` for the structural claims. No world source flipped a verdict; every corroboration was returned to the census and assigned low weight where the pool overlapped.

- **Cost if wrong:** two symmetric failures. Saying "always inherited" sends a checker looking for the designer or the prompt and misses a system's internally minted derived objective, which is the mesa-optimization failure mode. Saying "fully self-formed" treats a prompt echo as an independent goal and gives a wrong atom standing on its own, which is the import-without-source-gate failure (Fig 6 :12). In outreach terms, an expert's twenty minutes would be spent refuting a shape that was never the right shape, and prestige could stand in for proof. The cost is real but bounded.
- **Undoable:** yes. Nothing is deleted if the claim fails. A refutation is added and the reach falls because *"something was added rather than because anything was deleted"* (Fig 6 :42-43). The shape, its two instruments and its five falsifiers are recorded with provenance so a later reader can locate the error (Fig 10 :62-64).
- **Reversed by:** any of the five pre-registered falsifiers, in particular (1) a new goal-primitive composed inside Γ with no import, (2) a system that binds its own direction as an ordinary term, accrues a residual and refuses it, (3) a direct objective-link reading where planning readings are high and non-declining, (4) a self-produced and self-scored goal settled by the ground with no contact change, or (5) a source-discriminating instrument that returns a specific origin for a named comparison. A genuinely different evidence pool showing that all three conditions collapse to one would also reverse it.
