## 0. The residual in effect terms

R = |Γ(b,a) - o'| per slot. The object under examination is not "purposeful behaviour" and not "an agent that pursues goals," which is question 51. It is the **provenance of the objective term** in Fig 3's chain:

> "3 · the objective / it can say what would count as progress here, in its own terms / what the search is for" (Fig 3 :9-11)

Stated as an effect: at time t0 a slot has no comparison that says what counts as progress; at t1 it has one. Did the comparison arise by composition inside Γ, or did it cross the membrane from a differing frame (designer, training corpus, prompt, habitat)? The two routes leave different records. A mint leaves a lineage of atoms and bonds and an acceptance bargain (THE_FORMULA, step 3). An import leaves a source gate and a shadow test (Fig 6 :12-13).

**Bound shape before the run** (THE_FORMULA, "Direction"): the answer would be a *union* of (a) goal content, which is a primitive not in atoms(Γ) and must be imported, and (b) a goal form, which is the comparison bond ⋛ over held quantities and can be minted. Committing this in advance makes the preference refusable by the same test as everything else. No figure line is quoted until after the census below.

## 1. Domain census (before choosing any figure line)

**Arity.** The objective is a threshold relation between two quantities, so it is binary and ordered, not symmetric (Fig 12 :85-89, :74). Counting it as a one-place "want" drops the comparison and is rejected.

**Symmetry.** Ordered: "swap them. Does it become a different thing? Yes." (Fig 12 :74). A goal is not a conjunction; swapping current and target states gives a different claim.

**Scale.** The slot is one agent loop. The ranking of which objective survives is a population or room-chain phenomenon. The membrane between them is a filter with a direction, and the transform is lossy (Fig 4 :21, :27).

**Partition or union.** Union, not partition. D1 and D4 overlap (a curiosity target is a comparison over a gap), and that overlap is a compound with its own loop. D2 and D5 overlap (a direction held as a preference is inside the frame that searches but not a term in its Γ). Fig 9 :54-57: "And when the split lands on a union rather than a partition, unbundle before you search again." "A bundled problem is usually a union of overlapping conditions, not a list of separate ones." "What remains is smaller and it is also categorically different: a case in two conditions is not a harder case of either." So "always inherited" and "fully self-formed" are not two answers to one question. They are answers to two unbundled conditions.

**Dependency order (Fig 3).** perception -> vocabulary -> objective -> planning -> learn and carry (Fig 3 :4-15). The objective is link 3, and the chain is ordered by dependency, not by importance (Fig 3 :20). Any attempt to answer the goal question by improving planning instruments is effort above the break (Fig 3 :29-30).

**Anchor check.** A nameable anchor exists (the ground, Fig 2 :29), so this is a well-formed question, not yet a `gap`.

## 3. Findings

### F1 - A goal is a comparison, not a held thing (general, triangulated)

Fig 12 :112: "A plan is a sequence. A goal is a comparison. Progress is a subtraction. A refutation is a negation, and that one is not a bond."

Operators table :52: "a goal is a comparison, which is why a vocabulary without it cannot state one"

Fig 12 :115: "Which is the bound that would bite at the middle of Figure 3's chain."

So a system can state a goal only if it holds ⋛ plus two quantities to compare. A vocabulary with only ingredients, or only sequences, can say what a situation contains and what follows what, and cannot say what would count as getting somewhere (Fig 12 :111-113). The provenance question is therefore a question about the provenance of a *comparison*, not about the provenance of a wish.

Status `triangulated`: Fig 12 (composition) and the Operators table (operator semantics) are different figures with low overlap on this point. Refuter: a system that states a threshold goal without any comparison relation between two quantities, a pure sequence or a pure conjunction counted as a goal.

### F2 - The objective link is the unmeasured middle link (specified, single-source)

Fig 3 :9-11: "3 · the objective" and "it can say what would count as progress here, in its own terms" and "what the search is for".

Fig 3 :25-27: "The link between them, having terms for what a situation offers and being able to say what would count as progress, has neither, so it attracts the least attention and the fewest instruments." "A link with no instrument is not a link that works. It is a link nobody has looked at."

This is the middle link where chains usually break (Fig 3 :24). Perception and planning have obvious tests; link 3 does not. So REA-0052 cannot be settled by better planning or better perception instruments, and an affirmative answer cannot be inferred from an agent that plans well. The missing thing is an instrument on link 3.

Status `single-source` (Fig 3). Mode `specified` for the application to goal formation. Refuter: an instrument that measures objective formation directly and returns a non-zero reading in a slot whose perception and planning instruments are already high and non-declining.

### F3 - New goal content cannot be minted from inside; it must be imported (general, triangulated)

Fig 6 :21: "MINT composes inside the closure and can never add an atom."

Fig 6 :23: "No frame escapes its own closure. The bound moves only on contact with a frame whose closure differs."

Fig 8 :32: "Closure is idempotent. Composing inside a closed set never leaves it, so no search, however long, adds an atom."

Fig 6 :12-13 names the gates: "source gate: provenance" and "shadow test: explains a gap"; :14 calls IMPORT "the only operator that moves the wall."

If "forming its own goal" means producing a primitive the system did not hold (a new thing to want, a new terminal value), no amount of internal search can do it. That content arrives by import from a differing closure. In the setting of this question those differing closures are the designer, the training corpus, the prompt, and the habitat. In the broad sense of "inherited," the *content* of a goal is always inherited. Fig 6 :24 adds the price: "Import from a live peer is debited against that frame's independence."

Status `triangulated` (Fig 6 and Fig 8, distinct roles: what moves the wall and why nothing else can). Refuter: a system that composes a new goal-primitive from inside Γ, with a provenance record showing no import, and that the primitive was not already in atoms(Γ).

### F4 - Comparisons over held quantities can be minted by the system (specified, single-source)

Fig 5 :15-18: "the curiosity drive / fires when nothing is scoring / aimed at the new bin / seeks the gap that is large and compressible".

Fig 5 :46-48: "When nothing is scoring, the drive manufactures a gradient." "So a system that starts with nothing can still be shaped, by seeking the gap that is large and compressible, which is the same quantity the acceptance test scores." "Its own output is never a measure of success. A system can be enthusiastically curious and produce nothing."

So the denial "apparent goals are *always* inherited" is too strong in the other direction. A system with a live residual can aim itself at the gap that is large and compressible without an external goal being handed to it. This is a self-minted *derived* objective, not a new primitive. It satisfies the same MDL bargain the acceptance test scores (Fig 5 :42-45), and it is gated by the three guards as a product (Fig 5 :33-41). Crucially it is not self-validating: "Its own output is never a measure of success."

Status `single-source` (Fig 5). Refuter: a system with a positive live slot residual, the ⋛ operator, and the curiosity drive whose goal set changes only under explicit external instruction and never under internal composition.

### F5 - The direction of search is a preference, not a term (general, triangulated with Fig 5)

THE_FORMULA, step 2: "the shape a frame is searching for is not a term it holds." THE_FORMULA, "Direction": "The remedy is a discipline rather than a step: make the sought-for shape an ORDINARY TERM - write down what an answer would look like, bind it, and let it accrue a residual like anything else."

Fig 5 :61-63: "A frame searching for an answer of a particular shape holds that shape as a PREFERENCE rather than as a term. It is not bound, it predicts nothing, and it accrues no residual, so nothing here can see it." "Every check above runs on the MECHANISM and none runs on the DIRECTION OF THE QUESTION"

This is the deepest limit on "the system formed its own goal." The direction of the search is exactly the thing the loop cannot check. So a claim that a system certified its own terminal goal from inside is structurally unavailable to the loop. That certification is a seat's office (THE_FORMULA, "Direction": "What remains outside is a seat's office, not the loop's"). The honest move is the discipline already performed in section 0: bind the shape as an ordinary term and let it be refused.

Status `triangulated` (Fig 5 amendment and THE_FORMULA Direction, low overlap: pipeline limit versus loop discipline). Refuter: a system that binds its own direction as an ordinary term, accrues a residual on it, and refuses it under the same shrinking test with origin recorded.

### F6 - A goal only counts if it changes contact and cannot be self-scored (specified, triangulated)

Fig 11 :39: "capability is a property of agent-and-habitat, never of the agent alone."

Fig 11 :40: "An improvement that does not change contact changes nothing, however much it improves."

Fig 1 :27: "A frame cannot score itself with a quantity it also produces."

Fig 2 :29: "An anchor is whatever settles the question and does not change its answer because of what any frame thinks."

So a comparison that is both produced and scored by the same frame is disqualified as a verdict (Fig 1 :24-27 names coverage, compression, predicates minted and credibility accrued as frame-internal quantities). A goal that changes nothing in contact changes nothing, however impressive its internal derivation. Fig 11 :49-51 adds that valence is relative to the residual: "No standing list of good actors survives a change of residual." A goal can therefore change sign when the residual changes, which is why "formed once, always formed" is not a stable claim.

Status `triangulated` (Fig 11 habitat, Fig 1 metric, Fig 2 anchor). Refuter: a goal both produced and scored inside one frame that is settled by the ground with no contact change, or an invariant goal valence across a documented change of residual.

### F7 - Designer and system agreement is one evidence pool (general, triangulated)

Fig 2 :40: "disqualifying for a verdict: agreement about the world is worthless when the agreeing frames share an evidence pool".

Fig 2 :35: "Alignment is triangulation, not negotiation. A mirror cannot correct you."

Fig 2 :31-32: "Independence, between two frames, is how little they draw on the same evidence."

A system that echoes its prompt is not an independent second witness to the claim that the goal was inherited; it is the prompt talking. Equally, a system that disagrees with its designer is not thereby self-formed: it may have imported a different atom from its training corpus. Convergence is informative only when the records show separate origins: "Convergent derivation and adopted import look identical in the contents. Only the record of where each came from separates them" (Fig 8 :51-52).

Status `triangulated` (Fig 2 and Fig 8). Refuter: two frames with demonstrably disjoint evidence that converge on the same goal, with separate provenance for each, would remove the disqualification for that pair (not prove inheritance false).

### F8 - The binary question is a union and must be unbundled before search (general, triangulated)

Already quoted in section 1: Fig 9 :54, :55, :57. Fig 9 :60: "Use filters for the budget and witnesses for the verdict. Never let a filter hand you a verdict."

Applying it: the single question splits into at least two well-formed ones. (a) Where does the goal *content* come from? Answer: import, by F3. (b) Can the goal *comparison* be formed internally? Answer: yes, by F4, under the F5 limit and the F6 contact test. A third question, who holds the seat that sets direction, is D5 and not answerable by the loop (F5). Searching the bundled question will produce motion, not progress (Fig 7 :12-13: "same closure, handed along;" "agreement compounds, evidence does not").

Status `triangulated` (Fig 9 and Fig 7). Refuter: a demonstration that the two conditions are one condition, for example that every internal comparison necessarily imports its threshold value from a prior, so the union collapses to a partition.

### F9 - Naming which particular goal a given system formed is a gap

Fig 9 :32: "you get a kind of thing, never the answer itself."

Fig 8 :39: "So a search that finds nothing is never a proof of absence."

Fig 13 :134: "Unreachable is a verdict here, not a silence,"

No figure line governs the empirical identification of a specific self-generated terminal goal in a specific system. What is owed is a source-discriminating instrument: one that tells a comparison minted inside Γ from the same comparison adopted from a differing frame. The figures characterise that missing thing in effect terms (F7) but do not supply it. The stop is honest. Note the distinction from F2: F2's missing instrument measures objective formation on link 3; F9's missing instrument discriminates the *source* of a formed comparison.

Status `gap`. Refuter: an instrument whose readings discriminate internal mint from adopted import, with provenance for at least one case.

## 4. Pre-registered refuter (written before the conclusion)

Required before any outreach: "A falsifier written before the run is a design instrument." The shape claim in section 0 and the findings above are refuted if any of the following holds:

1. A system composes a new goal-primitive from inside Γ with no import, and it was not in atoms(Γ) (breaks F3, Fig 6 :21).
2. A system binds its own search direction as an ordinary term, accrues a residual on it, and refuses it under the same shrinking test, with origin recorded (breaks F5).
3. An instrument on Fig 3's link 3 measures objective formation in a slot whose perception and planning instruments are high and non-declining (breaks F2's "a link nobody has looked at" as a current description of the field).
4. A goal both produced and scored inside one frame is settled by the ground with no change in contact, or goal valence stays invariant across a documented residual change (breaks F6).
5. A source-discriminating instrument returns a reading that separates internal mint from adopted import for a named goal (closes F9 and forces a specific verdict).

If any fires, the unbundled shape is wrong at that link and the finding is withdrawn. Until one is run, no part of this may be labelled `grounded`.

## 5. Recommendation

1. Do not answer the binary question. Unbundle the content condition (F3) from the comparison condition (F4) and name the seat condition (F5) separately (F8).
2. State modes on every sentence. "Always inherited" is `general` over content only; "a system can form its own goals" is `specified` over comparison only, and is ungrounded.
3. Stop treating planning benchmarks as evidence about goal formation. They measure links above the break (Fig 3 :29-30).
4. Ask for a refutation of F3 or F4, not agreement with either. A failure is more useful than an endorsement (Fig 10 :42).
5. Figure 4 :25: "A recording carried upward looks like knowledge and is a description of one occasion." Send the generator (the census, the refuter, the figure lines) rather than the one-paragraph conclusion.
6. Record the missing instruments honestly. F2 and F9 are two distinct gaps, not one gap with two names.

### Seven-question recursive derivation (summary)

1. **Is a goal a term?** No. It is a comparison bond ⋛ over two quantities (Fig 12 :112; Operators :52).
2. **Where does the content come from?** Import from a differing closure (Fig 6 :21, :23; Fig 8 :32).
3. **Can the comparison be formed inside?** Yes, under the curiosity drive and the three guards (Fig 5 :46-48, :33-41).
4. **Can the system check its own direction?** No. Direction is a preference, not a term (THE_FORMULA "Direction"; Fig 5 :61-63).
5. **What settles it?** Contact, not internal scoring (Fig 11 :40; Fig 1 :27; Fig 2 :29).
6. **Is designer agreement corroboration?** No, shared evidence pool (Fig 2 :40); only separate provenance separates convergence from import (Fig 8 :51-52).
7. **Which goal did a given system actually form?** `gap`. The source-discriminating instrument is characterised but not held (Fig 9 :32; Fig 8 :39).

### Updated verdict

The unbundled shape survives: goal *content* is inherited by closure, goal *comparison* can be minted by the system from its own residual, goal *direction* is seated rather than looped, and goal *standing* requires contact with an anchor the system cannot move. Everything asserted as a single binary answer fails. Strains after re-census: none unresolved. Agreement between Fig 6 and Fig 5 was reached by re-census of atoms versus compositions, not by softening either line.
