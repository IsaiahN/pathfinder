## 1. A shape is a constraint, not a filler

The canonical README states the limit: the framework returns "what a missing thing would have to look like (a shape, not a name)". Fig 9 :32: "you get a kind of thing, never the answer itself." Fig 13 :134: "Unreachable is a verdict here, not a silence," and :135: "It cannot say which primitive is missing, because naming it would mean already holding it."

From *The Library of Babel*: "You can cut your search short if the set of books returned is small and descriptive enough that it provides a general shape of the answer that satisfies your question."

So a **shape** is the narrowed form of an answer you can state before you can name it. It constrains; it does not fill. For this question the shape is:

> a union of three conditions, goal content by import, goal form by internal comparison, and direction by a seat outside the loop.

That is a shape. "The system formed goal G at time t" is a name, and the framework cannot produce it here.

## 2. How the shape is obtained

- *Library of Babel*: use the vocabulary you already have that is closest to the thing, look for recurring new terms that overlap or correlate with it, recurse. "With each new term gained, you narrow the space and deepen your vocabulary." The search is steered by ranging returned books against the problem: "Are we getting warmer, or are we getting colder?"
- Fig 6 :30: "The description travels. Fails by being sharpened into terms only we can read. Cast it in effect terms." Fig 9 :30-31: "your words are borrowed too, so describe the effect rather than the cause."
- *The Hard Problem is About Source*: "If several paths create the same behavior, then behavior is a projection with lossy information." What is owed is a discriminator, not a name.
- *Library of Babel* on stopping: "you will know that you are done because the finish line will also be the starting place" and the paradox is that by then you have "composed the answer through the process of locating it."

Applied here: the term "goal" was the borrowed word. Ranging it against the figure text narrowed it to a comparison (Fig 12 :112), then split it into content and form (Fig 6 :21 against Fig 5 :46), then located the direction outside the loop (THE_FORMULA, "Direction"). The stop is at F9, where a source-discriminating instrument is still missing.

## 3. What licenses a name

Two things, both outside the shape:

1. **The ground settles it.** THE_FORMULA, step 5: "the ground settles it. Verified only by what pays. A gate is not the ground." The README says the framework "stops at the limit of what the available instruments can distinguish, and records the lineage so the stop is honest."
2. **A provenanced import from a frame whose closure differs** (Fig 6 :23, Fig 8 :50-52), with a source gate and a shadow test.

*The Hard Problem is About Source* sharpens this with the instrument case: a color sensor reads green and "cannot comment on the source", but "The answer is unreached until a better instrument exists." It was the spectrometer, not an argument, that resolved it. For REA-0052 the missing discriminator is an instrument that tells an internally minted comparison from an adopted one. Until it exists, naming a specific self-formed goal is the metamerism error: a source named from a projection.

## 4. "Shape, not a name" does not forbid naming the goal's form

**Correction to my own first reading.** The canonical phrase is about a *missing* primitive: "what a **missing** thing would have to look like (a shape, not a name)." The comparison operator ⋛ is not missing. It is one of the eight named operators (Operators table :2, :53-58) and Fig 12 :112 says plainly "A goal is a comparison." So naming the *form* is permitted and figure-derived. What is forbidden is naming the specific *content* that fills the comparison, because that content, if new, is not held in Γ and could only be named by already holding it (Fig 13 :135). Both readings are kept here so neither is quietly lost.

## 5. Prestige is not action

From *The Recursive Self-Improvement Trap*: "Prestige must never be allowed to validate an action directly... Someone let prestige stand in for proof... Prestige can fund an action. It can give an action a budget to be tried. But it cannot settle an action. Only the ground can settle it. The Ground Maintainer must be blind to prestige."

A campaign that gathers famous names to agree that goals are always inherited, or always self-formed, is the named collapse, not corroboration (Fig 2 :40). The legitimate ask gives the claim a budget to be tried and lets the ground settle it.

## 6. My own audit is a simulation, not a ground reading

Same essay: "LLMs can quickly simulate an approximate answer/output with the formula found in the architecture post" and "the truly testable output would need to be derived from the real architecture or working the formula yourself." LLM output is "simulacra." So `01_figure_grounded_audit.md` is a simulated derivation, checked for internal consistency against the figures. It is **not** a test of REA-0052 and must be labelled as such. It cannot settle anything it reports on. The same essay adds that "You may all agree and be wrong" and "You need ground truth to define where you stand."

## 7. A limit on self-certification, recorded twice

*The Recursive Self-Improvement Trap*: "The catch is that the output is only as good as the validator. The agent, or the tool, or the frame, or the theory, cannot validate itself. An external validator, or ground, is always needed". The same essay says "Metatheories cannot validate themselves. This is Godel's insight, and it still holds here."

THE_FORMULA, "Direction", states the narrower operational version: "the shape a frame is searching for is not a term it holds". Together they give F5: a system cannot certify from inside that its own goal was self-formed. This is a limit, not a step, and it is a seat's office rather than the loop's.
