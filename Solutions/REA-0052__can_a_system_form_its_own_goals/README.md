## Restatement as an effect

R = |Γ(b,a) - o'| per slot. A "goal" is not a thing a system holds; it is the objective link of the dependency chain: "it can say what would count as progress here, in its own terms / what the search is for" (Fig 3 :9-11). The question asks where that comparison term comes from. Does it arise inside the system's closure Γ, or does it arrive by import from a designer, a training corpus, or a prompt? The two options lead to different checks: an internal mint is checked against the system's own residual and the curiosity drive (Fig 5 :46-48); an import is checked against provenance and a shadow test (Fig 6 :12-13, Fig 8 :50-52). What an answer would look like is bound here as an ordinary term before the run: a union of (a) new goal content, which needs an import, and (b) a new comparison over held quantities, which the system can mint. Committing that shape in advance makes it refusable (THE_FORMULA, "Direction").

## What is deliberately not here

- No personal emails of named individuals.
- No ranking of people by how likely they are to agree that goals are always inherited or always self-formed.
- No bulk-send plan.

Reason, in the figures' own words: "disqualifying for a verdict: agreement about the world is worthless when the agreeing frames share an evidence pool" (Fig 2 :40). A list selected for people already committed to one side of the question manufactures exactly the correlation that makes their agreement worthless. "An interest in the outcome is unavoidable, an interest in the reading is disqualifying" (Fig 10 :59).
