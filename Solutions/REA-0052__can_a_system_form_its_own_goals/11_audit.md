## 2. Independent re-census from the residual line

### 2.1 Arity, symmetry, scale

- **Arity:** the object is a threshold relation between two quantities, so at least two operands plus the comparison operator. A one-place reading ("the system has a want") loses the comparison and is rejected. A three-place reading (frame, quantities, ground) is admitted only if the ground is treated as the anchor, not as a third frame (Fig 2 :25).
- **Symmetry:** ordered, not symmetric. A threshold compares which quantity is larger rather than merely conjoining their presence (Fig 12 :87). Swapping operands changes the claim (Fig 12 :74).
- **Scale:** the slot is one agent loop, but "what counts as progress" only gets a ranking at a population or room-chain scale (Fig 4 :5). The residual is intrinsically cross-scale, and the membrane transform is lossy (Fig 4 :27).

## 4. Round-trip gap R_T

Fig 4 :30: "So take the round trip and measure the gap. Without it you are navigating by dead reckoning: confident about a position nobody checked."

- **T_A, going up:** carry the primary's three-condition shape to one sentence. Result: *a goal is a comparison whose content is inherited and whose direction is seated.*
- **T_E, coming back:** put that sentence into a specific agent case. Result: *tell the engineer to check the training corpus and install an external reviewer.*

**R_T = gap(x, (T_E ∘ T_A)(x)).** The abstraction cannot hold four things:

1. It drops the distinction between a new atom and a new composition. Coming back down, it tells an engineer to look only at training data and misses the internally minted curiosity target (Fig 5 :46, Fig 6 :21).
2. It drops the contact test. "Inherited" sounds like a historical fact and does not say that a goal must change contact to stand (Fig 11 :40). Coming back down, "check the corpus" becomes a provenance exercise with no ground.
3. It drops the self-certification limit. "Direction is seated" does not say that the loop cannot see direction at all (Fig 5 :62). Coming back down, "install an external reviewer" could be mistaken for a procedure the loop can run on itself.
4. It drops the two distinct instruments. One sentence cannot hold both F2's objective-link instrument and F9's source discriminator. Coming back down, the engineer reaches for one metric for both.

This gap is not an error to eliminate; it is the measurement of what the coarser description cannot hold (Fig 4 :31). It is why the outreach packet sends the generator, not the one-paragraph version (Fig 4 :25). The version in `07` is a deliberately lossy artefact and is labelled as such.
