I redid the census from the residual line before relying on the primary's conclusions, using the WATSON order: R by arity, symmetry and scale; one line per domain with actors / currency / anchor; partition or union; order by dependency.

- **Domain 2, the population and habitat.** Actors: variants, resources, conditions. Currency: differential persistence. Anchor: the environment that settles which variants remain. Question type: binding, not a new primitive. Unmaintained mode.

- **Domain 4, the framing frame.** Actors: master sequence, mutant cloud, mutation rate. Currency: information conserved. Anchor: localisation about a master sequence. Entered after the residual was described.

Union: Domains 1 and 2 overlap in the replication-with-variation loop, one room with variation inside and selection between (Fig 7 :33). That overlap is a compound with its own loop and own ground, not two problems with one lever.

Order by dependency: contact, vocabulary (per-site indexing), objective (progress at a handoff), route (hold both factors nonzero), carry. Dependency order, not temporal order. In the unmaintained case both conditions bind at once.

## 4. Round trip gap R_T

The Symbols table defines R_T as the gap between what was sent up and what came back down, `gap(x, (T_E ∘ T_A)(x))`, measuring what the coarser description cannot hold. Fig 4 :30 requires it to be measured rather than assumed. I ran the round trip on the condensation the folder actually uses.

3. **Concretisation (T_E):** a concrete claim about an early RNA replicator: a per-site mutation rate in some band, a ribozyme or surface copying it, a habitat applying differential persistence, a master sequence that stays localised.

**R_T is nonzero.** Three things the shorthand holds do not survive the trip back down:

- the **landscape conditionality** of the numeric threshold (Wilke 2005), which the shorthand's "band" silently assumes is sharp everywhere;
- the **source discrimination** problem, since a measured error rate does not by itself name whether misincorporation, slippage, modification or selective loss produced it (Hard Problem is About Source);
- the **objective link's missing instrument** (Fig 3 :26-27), since "what counts as progress at a handoff" is asserted in the shorthand but not measured in any prebiotic system here.

Conversely, one thing survives cleanly: a perfect copy gives no heritable variation and a badly errorful copy loses the information, so the two edges and the two-factor product are the residue worth sending. R_T was **not** measured numerically, because no ground reading exists; it is recorded qualitatively as the enumerated set of dropped constraints. Treating "the formula is the condensed version of the metalaws" as identity would set R_T to zero and would be the dead-reckoning error Fig 4 :30 names. The honest form is a lossy condensation with a measured gap, which is what `09_watson_answer.md` states.
