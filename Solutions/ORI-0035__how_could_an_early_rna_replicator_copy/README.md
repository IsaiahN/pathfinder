# Solution ORI-0035

**Question:** How could an early RNA replicator copy itself accurately enough to persist but inaccurately enough to evolve?

**Source:** origins of life.txt line 48

**Section:** 4. RNA-first and genetic information

**Mode:** `specified` on the substantive claims, `general` on the structural shape. Nothing here is `grounded`: this is a simulated derivation, and the numeric fidelity threshold is a provenanced import from a world frame, not a figure result.

**Status:** done

**Registry status:** pending

**Standing of this package.** This is a simulated derivation checked for internal consistency against the figures, not a ground reading. The framework's own rule applies: LLM output is *simulacra* and *"the truly testable output would need to be derived from the real architecture or working the formula yourself"* (Recursive Self-Improvement Trap, world source, read 2026-11-23). This package cannot settle the question it reports on. No experiment was run, no fidelity was measured.

**Authority.** The figures are primary and decide. Verbatim figure text is cited as `Fig N :line` against `FRAMEWORK/reference/03-FIGURES_TEXT.md`. World sources (quasispecies theory, information theory, cultural transmission, organisational learning) are secondary corroboration only; they never overrule a figure. Where a world source qualifies the figure-shaped answer, the qualification is recorded, not smoothed over.

**Sources used.** Local framework files: `03-FIGURES_TEXT.md`, `FIGURES_README_canonical.md`, `THE_FORMULA.md`, and the three Tether skills. World sources actually opened and quoted: Obermayer and Frey 2010, Wilke 2005, Shannon noisy-channel theorem (Wikipedia, accessed 2026-11-23), March 1991, Smith 2025, plus four essays by Isaiah N and the institutional contact pages listed in `03_public_contact_points.csv`. Every one has a row in `10_source_ledger.csv`.

## What an answer would look like (bound as an ordinary term)

Per the formula's "Direction" step, the sought-for shape is written down and bound so it can accrue a residual like any other term. In effect terms: *a copying fidelity that is high enough to keep the information that worked and low enough to leave a heritable difference that selection can act on.* Bind that as **a band with two failure edges, not an optimum**. An answer that gives a single best error rate without naming the two edges has not answered the residual; it has replaced it with a number. An answer that names only the edges without a ground that votes between variants has also failed. This binding is stated before the derivation so a refuter can be written against it (see `01`, section 3).

## What is deliberately not here

- No personal emails of named individuals. The ISSOL contact page publishes institutional officer addresses; they are not reproduced here. Use the public contact page and the public submission portals.
- No ranking of people by how likely they are to agree with the claim. Fig 2 :40: *"disqualifying for a verdict: agreement about the world is worthless when the agreeing frames share an evidence pool"*. Selecting for receptiveness manufactures the disqualifying correlation.
- No bulk-send plan. One-to-one, opt-in channels only.
