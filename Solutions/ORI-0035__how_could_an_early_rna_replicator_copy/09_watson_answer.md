## DOMAIN CENSUS

R by arity, symmetry and scale, then one line per domain with actors / currency / anchor, partition or union, and order by dependency.

- **R (the residual).** The two-sided fidelity failure. Arity: two conditions, persistence and evolvability, so a **union compound** (Fig 9 :57-58, a case in two conditions is not a harder case of either), not one dial with an optimum. Symmetry: asymmetric, the copy is a directed lossy transform of the template through a complement, and a substitution at one site is not the reverse of one at another. Scale: step (one nucleotide incorporation) to cycle (one replication) to lineage (generations of variation and selection).
- **Domain 1, the copying channel.** Actors: template, complement, nucleotides, catalytic RNA or surface. Currency: per-site copy fidelity, each error a residual on one slot. Anchor: the sequence that actually catalyses, settled by whether the copy still works. Question type: a partition boundary (which compositions survive the handoff).
- **Domain 2, the population and habitat.** Actors: variants, resources, other molecules, conditions. Currency: differential persistence, deaths are the ground's votes. Anchor: the environment that settles which variants remain. This is the unmaintained mode, no seat, no maintainer. Question type: a binding from the habitat onto which variants persist, not a new primitive.
- **Domain 3, the verification frame.** Actors: expert readers. Currency: agreement and residuals. Anchor: an experiment or dataset, never agreement. In this simulated run all seats draw on one evidence pool, so its independence is zero.
- **Domain 4, the framing frame (quasispecies theory).** Actors: master sequence, mutant cloud, mutation rate, selective advantage. Currency: information conserved or lost. Anchor: population localisation, an observable. It is the frame whose closure predicts the residual's shape, entered only after the residual was described in effect terms (Fig 9 :27-28).
- **Union.** Domains 1 and 2 overlap in the replication-with-variation loop: one room with variation inside and selection between. The overlap is a specific compound with its own loop and own ground.
- **Order by dependency (Fig 3).** Contact first (can it be copied at all), then vocabulary (are errors indexable per site), then objective (what counts as progress at a handoff), then route (hold both factors nonzero), then carry. Dependency order, not temporal order. In the unmaintained case both conditions bind at once.

Re-census resolved it: Fig 7 governs handoff progress, the threshold relation governs localisation in one family of population models. Different objects, so no contradiction. ## VERDICT (from the figures)

The shape survives; the single-number answer does not. An early RNA replicator could copy itself accurately enough to persist but inaccurately enough to evolve if it occupied a band in which both factors of the handoff product stay nonzero: the bracket's fidelity carries the information that worked across the generation, and the ground's pressure selects between variants that differ. Perfect copying is a mirror chain with motion but no progress; excessive error is channel decay and dispersal. Errors are per-site residuals, so the band must be measured per slot. The two edges are isomers of one copying relation, and replication is a cycle whose closure is in question. The framework returns that shape, not a critical mutation rate. The rate is a provenanced import, and its sharp form is conditional on the fitness landscape. This is a `specified`, ungrounded, candidate result.

- **cost if wrong:** bounded and locatable. Set the upper edge too high and the lineage is stable but cannot adapt when conditions change, which a serial-transfer challenge would expose. Set the lower edge too low and the information disperses, which per-site tracking would expose. The real cost is presenting an import as a derivation: that hides the two conditions and the landscape conditionality and gives a checker nothing checkable from the figures. Fig 2 :42: "Buy independence in proportion to the cost of being wrong."
- **undoable:** yes. The band is a property of the copying chemistry and the habitat, not a primitive. Adjusting fidelity (temperature, ionic conditions, nucleotide concentrations, enzyme specificity) does not require withdrawing any term. A superseded claim can be replaced by the corrected packet without touching the derivation.
- **reversed by:** a ground reading, not agreement. A serial-transfer experiment with measured per-site fidelity and differential pressure that shows an information-preserving, evolvability-preserving lineage outside the predicted band would reverse the numeric binding. A demonstration of sharp thresholds in landscapes with lethal genotypes would reverse the Wilke qualification. A derivation of the critical rate from the figures alone, or a demonstration that the handoff line governs another quantity, would reverse the shape claim. Until one of these fires, the verdict stands as `candidate`.
