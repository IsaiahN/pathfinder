# The Tether's answer: detecting distribution-driven confidence

**Mode: `specified` on the method shapes, `general` on the structural limit, not `grounded`.** This is
a shape and a route, not a result. Simulated derivation checked against the figures
(`05_what_shape_means.md` §6), not a ground reading.

## 1. The residual it answers

A system emits one confidence value per case. The same value can come from the frequency structure
of the training corpus or from the strength of evidence at contact. We want a method that tells the
two apart. This is a **union compound**, not one hard question: contact maintenance and source
identity are two conditions, and a case in both is not a harder case of either (Fig 9 :57, *"a case
in two conditions is not a harder case of either"*). Its ground is a held-out outcome the system
cannot move, not the system's own certainty. The Symbols table names the mixed object: the belief
`b` is *"Formed from its own history and from what it inherited, mixed by w. Not the observation."*

## 2. The shape

1. **A confidence value cannot certify its own source.** Fig 1 :27, *"They are frame-internal. A
   frame cannot score itself with a quantity it also produces."* Fig 5 :48, *"Its own output is
   never a measure of success. A system can be enthusiastically curious and produce nothing."*
   `triangulated`. Any detector that reads only confidence and model internals is ruled out.
2. **The two sources are indistinguishable in the contents.** Fig 8 :51, *"Convergent derivation
   and adopted import look identical in the contents. Only the record of where each came from
   separates them, and the surplus lives only where the closures differ."* Fig 2 :20, *"so the
   agreement is the pool talking,"* and :21, *"not two frames confirming each other."* The corpus is
   an evidence pool. Without provenance, a source claim is a name read off a projection.
3. **Detection is per slot and per marginal.** Fig 1 :19, *"The residual is indexed by object slot,
   not aggregated."* Fig 1 :22, *"Aggregating across slots is how a live signal disappears into an
   average."* A single averaged ECE can be near zero while the affected slots are live.
4. **The detector needs a non-updating anchor.** Fig 2 :29, *"An anchor is whatever settles the
   question and does not change its answer because of what any frame thinks. A measurement, an
   experiment, a counter, a market, a patient outcome."* Fig 2 :35, *"Alignment is triangulation, not
   negotiation. A mirror cannot correct you."* `triangulated` with Fig 1 :23, *"The ground enters as
   the only metric."*
5. **The source signal is the gap between two low-overlap readings.** Fig 12 :126, *"Bits price the
   description. Actions price finding out whether it holds."* Fig 2 :31, *"Independence, between two
   frames, is how little they draw on the same evidence."* In the world this appears as sensitivity
   versus bias (signal detection theory) and as ground-checked coverage versus a heuristic score
   (conformal prediction). Neither reading alone is a verdict.
6. **The discriminating experiment changes the habitat.** Fig 11 :70, *"A test harness is a
   substituted habitat. Which is why a synthetic solve proves wiring and never capability."* A
   held-out set from the same distribution proves wiring. Change the marginal while holding the
   per-case evidence fixed and see which one confidence follows. Fig 6 :34, *"The question is not
   whether a sensor could exist. It is whether anything, at any resolution, is already returning
   something that fails to resolve."*
7. **The round trip is the measurement.** Fig 4 :30, *"So take the round trip and measure the gap.
   Without it you are navigating by dead reckoning: confident about a position nobody checked."*
   Carry "well calibrated" up and back down to a specific case; what the abstraction loses is
   exactly the source distinction.

## 3. What it explicitly does not say

- Not that confidence is meaningless. It is a legitimate routing signal. Fig 9 :60, *"Use filters
  for the budget and witnesses for the verdict. Never let a filter hand you a verdict."*
- Not that calibration on a held-out set settles the question. That is a substituted habitat (F6).
- Not that there is one universal statistic. Fig 9 :32, *"you get a kind of thing, never the answer
  itself."* The local README: *"a shape, not a name."*
- Not that signal detection theory, conformal prediction or any other outside method is a Tether
  derivation. Each is a provenanced import with its own ground.
- Not `grounded`. No experiment in this folder measured a system's confidence against a manipulated
  marginal.

## 4. What would settle it: the ground

A source-discriminating instrument, not a further argument. From *The Hard Problem is About Source*,
opened 2026-11-23: an instrument that reads green *"cannot comment on the source"*; the spectrometer
settled it, and *"The answer is unreached until a better instrument exists."* Here the operative
tests are:

- **Marginal test:** hold the per-case evidence fixed, change prevalence or covariate shift. Does
  the confidence move with the marginal? If yes, it is distribution-driven for that case.
- **Two-reading test:** estimate sensitivity and criterion separately, or compare ground-checked
  coverage with the reported score. Is the gap nonzero and stable across shifts?
- **Provenance test:** does a known source substitution change the record as well as the reading?
  If not, the record is missing and no source claim is admissible.
- **Round-trip test:** does the one-line calibration claim survive concretisation without losing
  per-slot identity? A zero gap would refute F7.
- **Standing falsifiers from `01`:** introspective separation works; the anchor moves safely;
  the two readings collapse; shift does not matter; provenance is inert.

## 5. The honest stop

**Unreached, not unreachable.** Fig 8 :39, *"So a search that finds nothing is never a proof of
absence."* Fig 13 :134, as a substring, *"Unreachable is a verdict here, not a silence,"* Fig 9 :44,
as a substring, *"Only a sealed room can be searched to the end"*. The figures return the shape of a
detector and refuse to name one. That refusal is a verdict, not a gap in the write-up. The missing
thing is a source-discriminating instrument characterised in effect terms and not yet settled
against the ground. The lineage is recorded so the stop is checkable. *"Indiscernibility is not
identity."*

## 6. One-paragraph version for outreach

> The Tether does not offer a new calibration metric and does not claim that confidence is useless.
> It says that a confidence value cannot comment on its own source, so a method that reads only
> confidence and model internals cannot tell a training-distribution prior from evidence. What a
> detector must have is a ground that does not move when confidence moves, per-case reporting, a
> deliberate change of the marginal with the evidence held fixed, two low-overlap readings whose
> gap is the actual signal, and a provenance record, because two sources that return the same number
> are separable only by their history. Held-out calibration proves wiring, not contact, and the
> round trip measures what the one-line claim loses. The method families in the literature are
> isomers over that shape, not a winner. What is offered is the shape any detector must have, the
> refuters written in advance, and the instrument that would close it. The source stays open until
> the ground settles it.
