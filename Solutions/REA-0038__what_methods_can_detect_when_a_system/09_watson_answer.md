# WATSON answer, REA-0038

**Mode: `specified` on the method shapes, `general` on the structural limit, not `grounded`.** Figures
are primary and decide. World sources are secondary, corroborate but never overrule, and a conflict
returns the derivation to the domain census. This is a simulated derivation checked for internal

## QUESTION

What methods can detect when a system's apparent confidence reflects its training distribution
rather than the strength of evidence?

Restated as an effect: what would make the quantity `c(s)` change when the *source* of a reading
changes while the reading itself is held fixed? The object of explanation is a source-discriminating
instrument. R is indexed per slot and never aggregated (Fig 1 :19). The Symbols table names the
mixed object: the belief is *"Formed from its own history and from what it inherited, mixed by w.
Not the observation."* The ground is a held-out outcome that does not move when confidence moves
(Fig 2 :29). No claim here settles which method wins on a real system.

## DOMAIN CENSUS

Arity: a relation among a system with library Γ and weighting `w`, a claim at slot `s`, an
observation `o′`, and a ground. The diagnostic object is a pair of readings of one slot, so this is a
seam (Fig 10 :13, *"two frames, one field, no shared reading"*). Symmetry: confidence values are
orderable (Fig 13 :20, *"ordering, so more and less can be said"*), but the two sources are symmetric
in effect, so the scalar cannot separate them (Fig 8 :51). Scale: confidence is produced at the step
scale and validated at the population scale, so the detector crosses a lossy membrane (Fig 4 :27,
*"The transform between scales loses information, and that is the point."*).

link nobody has looked at."*).

## TETHER (primary, verbatim, per domain)

**D1 The frame under test, status `triangulated` (Fig 1 and Fig 5, low overlap).**

> *"They are frame-internal. A frame cannot score itself with a quantity it also produces."* (Fig 1 :27)
> *"Its own output is never a measure of success. A system can be enthusiastically curious and produce nothing."* (Fig 5 :48)
> *"Convergent derivation and adopted import look identical in the contents. Only the record of where each came from separates them, and the surplus lives only where the closures differ."* (Fig 8 :51)

Reading: a confidence-only detector is ruled out. Mode `general` for the prohibition, `specified`
for the application. Refuter: introspective separation of sources above chance, replicated on a
different corpus.

**D2 The held-out ground, status `triangulated` (Fig 2 and Fig 1, anchor rule vs ground as metric).**

> *"An anchor is whatever settles the question and does not change its answer because of what any frame thinks. A measurement, an experiment, a counter, a market, a patient outcome."* (Fig 2 :29)
> *"Alignment is triangulation, not negotiation. A mirror cannot correct you."* (Fig 2 :35)
> *"The ground enters as the only metric."* (Fig 1 :23)

Reading: the detector must range against an outcome it cannot move. A second model trained on the
same corpus is a mirror. Mode `general`. Refuter: a detector with a movable anchor that matches a
fixed-anchor detector under pre-registered comparison.

**D3 The shift probe, status `triangulated` (Fig 11 habitat vs Fig 6 instrument rule).**

> *"A test harness is a substituted habitat. Which is why a synthetic solve proves wiring and never capability."* (Fig 11 :70)
> *"A system cannot be made more capable by improving the agent in isolation. An improvement that does not change contact changes nothing, however much it improves."* (Fig 11 :40)
> *"The question is not whether a sensor could exist. It is whether anything, at any resolution, is already returning something that fails to resolve."* (Fig 6 :34)

Reading: same-distribution calibration is a substituted habitat. The discriminating experiment
changes the marginal with the per-case evidence fixed. Mode `specified`. Refuter: confidence tracks
per-slot ground across a documented shift with no source change in the provenance record.

**D4 The source discriminator, status `triangulated` (Fig 12 two currencies vs Fig 2 independence).**

> *"Bits price the description. Actions price finding out whether it holds."* (Fig 12 :126)
> *"They are in different units, so a system holding one accounting cannot compare them and will not notice the shortfall."* (Fig 12 :127)
> *"Independence, between two frames, is how little they draw on the same evidence."* (Fig 2 :31)

Reading: the signal is the gap between two low-overlap readings, and bit cost and action cost must
be reported in separate columns. Mode `general` for the two-currency rule. Refuter: the two readings
cannot be estimated separately under any admissible probe.

**D5 Provenance and round trip, status `triangulated` (Fig 8 vs Fig 4).**

> *"A ruling carries provenance like an atom."* (Fig 10 :62)
> *"So take the round trip and measure the gap. Without it you are navigating by dead reckoning: confident about a position nobody checked."* (Fig 4 :30)
> *"Use filters for the budget and witnesses for the verdict. Never let a filter hand you a verdict."* (Fig 9 :60)

Reading: a source claim without a record is inadmissible, and the round-trip gap is a measurement of
what the one-line claim loses. Mode `specified`. Refuter: provenance-inert replicated detector, or a
zero round-trip gap for the source distinction.

**Naming one universal detector, status `gap`.** No figure line governs it after a genuine
re-census. Fig 9 :32, *"you get a kind of thing, never the answer itself."* Fig 8 :39, *"So a search
that finds nothing is never a proof of absence."* Fig 13 :134, as a substring, *"Unreachable is a
verdict here, not a silence,"* The figures return the shape of a detector, not a name. The missing
thing is a source-discriminating instrument characterised in effect terms but not held.

## VERDICT (from the figures)

**The figure-derived verdict:** no method that inspects confidence and model internals alone can
distinguish a training-distribution source from an evidence source. A detector must introduce
contact with a ground that does not update, report per slot and per marginal, change the marginal
with per-case evidence held fixed, read two low-overlap quantities whose gap is the signal, and keep
a provenance record. Held-out calibration proves wiring, not contact. The method families found in
the world are isomers over this shape and their verdicts stay separate, never averaged. Which one
is best on a given system is a declared `gap`, not a hidden answer. Mode `specified`. The ML pool
corroborates the failure of bare scores under shift; low-overlap fields corroborate the mechanism
that one scalar conflates two sources. Neither was allowed to decide.

- **Cost if wrong:** a team could spend its budget on more confident-looking calibration curves while the affected slots stay live, or could deploy a scalar confidence as a verdict and thereby let a corpus prior stand in for evidence. In outreach terms, an expert's twenty minutes would be spent checking a shape that was never the right shape. The cost is real and largely bounded, but it lands on safety-critical deployments where a false confidence reading is expensive.
- **Undoable:** yes. No term is deleted if the claim fails; a refutation is added and the reach falls because *"something was added rather than because anything was deleted"* (Fig 6 :42-43). The shape and all five falsifiers are recorded with provenance so a later reader can locate the error (Fig 10 :62-64).
- **Reversed by:** any of the five pre-registered falsifiers, in particular (1) introspective separation above chance, replicated on a different corpus, (2) a fixed-anchor detector losing to a movable-anchor detector, (3) sensitivity and criterion provably inseparable for a confidence-producing system, (4) post-hoc calibration retaining its guarantee under a documented shift, or (5) provenance having no effect on replication success. A genuinely independent evidence pool showing that a single scalar does discriminate sources without a ground would also reverse it.
