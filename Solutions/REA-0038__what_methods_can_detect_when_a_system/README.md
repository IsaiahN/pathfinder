## The residual in one line

A confidence scalar is one reading with two possible sources, the frequency structure of the
training corpus and the strength of evidence at contact. The question asks for a method that
separates the sources. The figures answer that a reading cannot comment on its own source, so a
detector must add contact, a second low-overlap reading, and a provenance record.

## What is deliberately not here

- No personal emails of named individuals.
- No ranking of people by how likely they are to agree with the Tether reading.
- No bulk-send plan. A reply to a stage-1 question is a check, never corroboration.

The reason is structural, not politeness. A confidence calibration audience selected for agreement
would share the evidence pool that makes their agreement worthless (Fig 2 :40,
*disqualifying for a verdict: agreement about the world is worthless when the agreeing frames share
an evidence pool*), and a verifier handed the framework interior before giving a verdict stops being
outside the claim (Fig 10 :42, *A verifier that can reconstruct the claim cannot verify it.*).
