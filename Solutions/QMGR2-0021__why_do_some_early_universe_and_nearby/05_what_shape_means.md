## 1. A shape is a constraint, not a filler

The canonical README states the limit: the framework returns *"what a missing thing would have to look like (a shape, not a name)"*. Fig 9 :32: *"you get a kind of thing, never the answer itself."* Fig 13 :134: *"Unreachable is a verdict here, not a silence,"* and :135: *"It cannot say which primitive is missing, because naming it would mean already holding it."*

From *The Library of Babel*: *"you can cut your search short if the set of books returned is small and descriptive enough that it provides a general shape of the answer that satisfies your question."*

For this question a **shape** is the narrowed form of an answer you can state before you can name the cause. It constrains; it does not fill. Concretely here, the shape has four marks:

1. two H0 values are two slots of one residual, not two samples of one number;
2. the disagreement is a union of a channel condition and a closure condition;
3. from inside either frame the two conditions look the same, so an arbiter frame is owed;
4. the specific cause stays open until a source-discriminating instrument returns.

"Calibration error" is a name. "Early dark energy" is a name. "A union of channel failure and a missing primitive, with an arbiter owed" is a shape.

## 2. How a shape is obtained

- *The Library of Babel* on vocabulary: use the closest known terms, then *"With each new term gained, you narrow the space by definition and deepen your vocabulary."* The question itself is the tether: *"This anchor is provided by your own question."*
- *The Library of Babel* on malformed questions: *"Perhaps your problem was a bundle or conflation of several problems masquerading as one?"* This is the direct antecedent of Fig 9 :5, *"are not one hard question but several well-formed ones. Split it rather than search."* The Hubble tension is treated as such a bundle until it is unbundled.
- Fig 6 :30: *"The description travels. Fails by being sharpened into terms only we can read. Cast it in effect terms."* Fig 9 :30-31: *"your words are borrowed too, so describe the effect rather than the cause."* So the residual is described as "an inferred value and a read value disagreeing persistently", not as "the sound horizon is wrong".
- *The Hard Problem is About Source*: the limits of an instrument are part of the shape, because *"An instrument that can't distinguish these cases demonstrates its own inadequacy."*

## 3. What licenses a name

Only two things, both outside the shape itself:

1. **The ground settles it.** `THE_FORMULA.md` step 5: *"the ground settles it. Verified only by what pays. A gate is not the ground."* The README: the framework *"stops at the limit of what the available instruments can distinguish, and records the lineage so the stop is honest."* A name is licensed when an arbiter measurement with a different closure separates C1 from C2.
2. **A provenanced import from a frame whose closure differs**, with a source gate and a shadow test (Fig 6 :23 and the import rule in Fig 8). The literature's preference for pre-recombination physics is an import candidate, not a settled name, until its provenance and its shadow (the gap it explains) are both shown.

*The Hard Problem is About Source* sharpens why a fit is not a name. Metamerism: an instrument reads green and *"cannot comment on the source"*. One green light, yellow plus blue, RGB, CMYK, or a scheme not yet discovered can all give the same reading. *"If several paths create the same behavior, then behavior is a projection with lossy information."* Two H0 values that agree numerically would not by themselves reveal a common source; the source is settled by *"the arrival of a better instrument"*, not by argument. That is exactly the arbiter requirement in F3 of `01_figure_grounded_audit.md`.

## 4. The ground and the source problem

The ground here is the actual expansion rate, not the Planck value and not the SH0ES value. Fig 2 :29: *"An anchor is whatever settles the question and does not change its answer because of what any frame thinks."* Fig 10 :24: *"the ground does not decay, the channel does."*

The source problem appears in a specific form. The early route and the nearby route are two projections of one universe onto two readings. They disagree. The disagreement can be caused by a lossy channel in one projection, by a missing primitive in the model used to make the projection, or by the lossy transform between scales (Fig 4 :30). A name chosen from the size of the discrepancy is a source inferred from a reading, the metamerism error. What is owed is a discriminator, not a preferred cause.

*The Recursive Self-Improvement Trap*: *"the output is only as good as the validator. The agent, or the tool, or the frame, or the theory, cannot validate itself."* This derivation cannot validate its own shape; an outside frame must do that.

## 5. Answer to the shape question

**What is the shape of an answer to "why do the methods disagree?"**

A checkable union with an open slot, not a cause. In full: the residual must be kept per method; the methods must be split into a channel condition and a closure condition; the two must be held as a union because a lever that clears one leaves the intersection; the question of which is actual must be answered by an instrument with a genuinely different closure, whose reading is predicted in advance; and until that reading exists the slot stays marked `???`. This is the Hubble-tension equivalent of `SPACE(???)TIME`: `H0(CHANNEL ??? CLOSURE)ROUTE`.

**Does the published literature commit the error the README forbids?** In part. Any sentence that says the tension *is* early dark energy, or *is* a calibration error, names a source from a projection. The careful reviews decline to do this: SH0ES reports that the source *"remains unknown"*, and Kamionkowski and Riess concede that untestable unknown errors cannot be excluded by definition. Those careful formulations are the ones corroborating the shape. The stronger claims are one-pool reports and carry no verdict weight here.

## 6. Consequences for contact

**Prestige is not action.** From *The Recursive Self-Improvement Trap*: *"Prestige must never be allowed to validate an action directly."* and *"Prestige can fund an action. It can give an action a budget to be tried. But it cannot settle an action. Only the ground can settle it. The Ground Maintainer must be blind to prestige."* A campaign that gathers famous cosmologists to agree with a preferred cause is the named collapse. The legitimate ask gives the shape a budget to be tried and lets an independent measurement settle it.

**This audit is a simulation, not a ground reading.** Same essay: *"LLMs can quickly simulate an approximate answer/output"*, but *"the truly testable output would need to be derived from the real architecture or working the formula yourself."* So `01` is a simulated derivation checked for internal consistency against the figures. It is not a measurement of H0 and must be labelled as such. It cannot settle anything it reports on.

**Contact follows the residual, not the theory.** Fig 10 :52: *"Bring a characterised residual, never the source."* The first message asks what measurement would discriminate the two conditions; the framework appears later, and only if the reader opts in.
