## 0. The claim under audit, in effect terms

The question asks *why* early-universe and nearby-universe methods give different expansion-rate estimates. Stated as an effect rather than a topic:

> The actual expansion rate H0 is the anchor, the thing that settles the question and does not change because a team re-analyses data (Fig 2 :29). Two frames range against it. The early frame measures the angular acoustic scale and the sound horizon, then infers H0 through a model. The nearby frame calibrates candles geometrically, then reads H0 from redshifts. Their two reported values are the two slots of one residual, and R = |Gamma(b,a) - o'| must be read per slot, never averaged (Fig 1 :19).

The competing readings of the residual, held separate:

- **C1, the channel reading.** One frame has lost touch: a calibration zero point, a crowding correction, a dust law, a selection function. The ground did not move; a channel decayed. *"the ground does not decay, the channel does"* (Fig 10 :24). In the mint pipeline's sort this is *"mis-attached: refit what you have, do not invent"* (Fig 5 :13).
- **C2, the closure reading.** The shared model is too small. A primitive is missing from the model's closure, so inference from the early universe lands at the wrong place even with a perfect channel. Fig 6 :23: *"No frame escapes its own closure. The bound moves only on contact with a frame whose closure differs."* In the mint sort this is *"mechanism broken: a new term is owed"* (Fig 5 :14).
- **C3, the statistical reading.** The residuals are a fluctuation. Disallowed as an explanation by the persistence test: Fig 5 :59 names *"a term whose residual PERSISTS AND DOES NOT TREND DOWN is the one to point the refusal at"*, and this residual has persisted and not trended down across a decade of better data.

**What an answer would look like, bound before the run (Formula, "Direction").** A shape with three parts: (i) the union of C1 and C2 kept separate and never averaged; (ii) the characterisation, in effect terms, of an instrument that would discriminate a channel failure from a missing atom; (iii) an explicit refusal to name which one wins until that instrument returns. The sought-for shape is bound here as an ordinary term so it can accrue a residual and be refused (Fig 5 :65-66). Until it is bound it predicts nothing and no check in the loop can see it.

**Important boundary.** This audit does not decide whether the real cause is calibration or new physics. That decision belongs to the ground. The figures decide the *shape* of a checkable answer, not the name of the winning cause. Fig 9 :32: *"you get a kind of thing, never the answer itself."*

## 1. Figure census

R described by arity, symmetry and scale, then one line per domain with actors, currency and anchor. A domain without a nameable anchor is not yet a domain.

- **R, the residual.** Arity: two conditions, one early-inference slot and one nearby-readout slot. Symmetry: the reported numbers are related by a threshold comparison, not a symmetric join (Fig 12 :87: *"a threshold relation between two quantities"*); the two routes are not exchangeable because one infers and one reads. Scale: recombination and extrapolated history versus the local Hubble flow, with a lossy transform between them (Fig 4 :27).
- **Union, not partition.** The disagreement lives in the overlap of C1 and C2. Fig 9 :54: *"And when the split lands on a union rather than a partition, unbundle before you search again."* Fig 9 :57: *"What remains is smaller and it is also categorically different: a case in two conditions is not a harder case of either."*
- **Order by dependency.** Calibration precedes model extension. Fig 3 :19: *"Optimising a link that already works cannot rescue one that does not."* Fig 3 :20: *"The chain is ordered by dependency, not by importance."* The residual must be described before a frame is chosen (Fig 8 :53).

| domain | actors | currency | anchor | status |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| D1 Early inference frame | CMB angular scale, sound horizon, LCDM model | model-dependent inferred H0 | the measured angular acoustic scale | admitted |
| D2 Nearby readout frame | Cepheids, TRGB, SN Ia, parallaxes, masers | calibrated distance plus redshift | geometric parallax and maser distances | admitted |
| D3 Shared model closure | six LCDM parameters and their extensions | predicted expansion history | fit to data, not an independent H0 ground | admitted, flagged as possibly too small |
| D4 Arbiter frame | TRGB, masers, standard sirens, lensing, FRBs, BAO plus BBN | an H0 from a different closure | the actual H0 | admitted, partly instantiated |
| X1 "average the two values" | none nameable | a pooled number | none: averaging is the named error (Fig 1 :22) | not a domain, refused |
| X2 "one team blundered" | a single team | a correction | absorbed into D2 channel maintenance (Fig 10 :24) | absorbed, not separate |

Dependency order: D2 geometric calibration settles the near link before D3 model extension is attempted; D4 is the arbiter that decides between C1 and C2. The four domains draw on at least two different evidence pools (CMB physics versus stellar-distance metrology), which is the minimum condition for triangulation (Fig 2 :31), but the pools are not fully independent because both routes are compared through the same LCDM reporting convention. That partial overlap is declared, not hidden.

## 2. Findings

### F1 - The residual is per method, never an average (governs the reporting rule)

Fig 1 :19: **"The residual is indexed by object slot, not aggregated."**

Fig 1 :22: **"Aggregating across slots is how a live signal disappears into an average."**

Fig 1 :13: **"a global R near zero with one live slot residual is a legal state"**

The two methods are the two slots. A combined fit that quotes one H0 with a small error bar is the aggregation error: it throws away the only signal that says where to look. Every result in this folder is kept per method. The 5-sigma figure quoted by SH0ES is a filter on attention, not a verdict (Fig 9 :60): *"Use filters for the budget and witnesses for the verdict. Never let a filter hand you a verdict."*

**Refuter:** a public analysis showing that, under one identified correction, both slots' residuals fall together and no per-slot residual remains. Until then, any single pooled H0 is reported as a convention, not a result.

### F2 - A disagreement that does not shrink with effort must be split (governs the question type)

Fig 9 :4: **"Disagreements that do not shrink with effort, where each rule keeps working well on a different subset,"**

Fig 9 :5: **"are not one hard question but several well-formed ones. Split it rather than search."**

The tension has not shrunk as data improved; it has hardened. Each method keeps working well on its own subset. Fig 9 therefore does not send us into a longer search for one cause; it sends us to split the question. Fig 9 :54 and :57 (quoted in section 1) make the split a union, not a list.

**Refuter:** a single lever that closes both slots with no categorically different residue. If that is shown, the union reading is wrong and the question was one hard problem after all.

### F3 - The two candidate bins, and why the framework cannot name the winner

Fig 5 :13: **"mis-attached: refit what you have, do not invent"**

Fig 5 :14: **"mechanism broken: a new term is owed"**

Fig 6 :27: **"Which is why some unreachability is depth and some is genuine absence, and from inside the frame the two look the same."**

This is the decisive structural finding. From inside either measurement frame, a drifted channel (C1) and a missing atom in the model (C2) produce the same observable: a persistent residual that will not trend down. The figures do not choose between them; they say the two are indiscernible from inside and that a second frame is required. Fig 6 :32: **"An instrument is not built from a description. It is improved from a worse instrument already returning something."** Fig 6 :34: **"Where nothing is, there is no edge to extend from, and the search for a proto-instrument is a search for one that is already there."** An undefined "unknown systematic" is not an aimable probe; this is the figure-side reason the review literature's phrase *"unknown errors untestable by lack of definition"* cannot count as an explanation.

**Verdict for this finding:** `triangulated` (Fig 5 sort plus Fig 6 indiscernibility, two figures with low overlap). Mode `general` for the indiscernibility, `specified` for the cosmology application.

**Refuter:** an instrument whose reading is different under a known calibration drift and under a changed sound horizon, with a published prediction made before the reading. That instrument would name the winner and dissolve the `gap`.

### F4 - Calibration is a channel failure, not a moving ground

Fig 10 :24: **"the ground does not decay, the channel does"**

Fig 10 :26: **"stale percepts, dropped transients, saturated metrics, pooled readings"**

Fig 10 :27: **"the output is not a verdict, it is"** followed by Fig 10 :28: **"you have lost touch"**

A wrong Cepheid zero point, an unmodelled crowding term, or a SN Ia calibration that drifts with look-back time is a channel failure. The actual expansion rate does not care. The right response to a channel failure is *"keep the anchor reachable"* (Fig 10 :29), which here means geometric calibration traceable to parallaxes and masers, exactly what the distance-ladder teams do (Riess et al 2022 describe Cepheids calibrated from Gaia EDR3 parallaxes, masers in NGC 4258, and eclipsing binaries in the LMC). The figure does not say which channel is drifted; it says what kind of thing a channel failure is and what restores contact.

**Refuter:** a demonstrated change in H0 that tracks an instrument update rather than a calibration error, with the anchor independently confirmed to have moved. No such result is in the sources read.

### F5 - Anchor, independence and the one-witness rule

Fig 2 :29: **"An anchor is whatever settles the question and does not change its answer because of what any frame thinks."**

Fig 2 :31: **"Independence, between two frames, is how little they draw on the same evidence."**

Fig 2 :35: **"Alignment is triangulation, not negotiation. A mirror cannot correct you."**

Fig 2 :40: **"disqualifying for a verdict: agreement about the world is worthless when the agreeing frames share an evidence pool"**

Fig 2 :42: **"Buy independence in proportion to the cost of being wrong."**

The anchor is the actual H0, not the Planck number and not the SH0ES number. Agreement among teams using the same calibrator and the same analysis conventions is variation, not a verdict (Fig 7 :35: **"A room's agreement is variation, not a verdict."**). This is why the TRGB result matters structurally even though it sits between the two headline values: it is a different calibrator, hence a different closure within the late frame, and it reports 69.8 +- 0.8 (stat) +- 1.7 (sys) km/s/Mpc, agreeing with Planck at 1.2 sigma and with SH0ES at 1.7 sigma (Freedman et al 2019). It weakens the claim that the late frame is monolithic without licensing a verdict.

**Refuter:** evidence that the early and late frames draw on a single evidence pool, so their apparent disagreement is one frame talking to itself. Partial overlap is already conceded; total overlap is not.

### F6 - If it is not a channel, a new atom is owed, but the figures cannot name it

Fig 6 :23: **"No frame escapes its own closure. The bound moves only on contact with a frame whose closure differs."**

Fig 8 :32: **"Closure is idempotent. Composing inside a closed set never leaves it, so no search, however long, adds an atom."**

Fig 13 :134: **"Unreachable is a verdict here, not a silence,"**

If C1 is excluded, the early frame's closure is too small: something that fixes the sound horizon or the late expansion history is missing. The shape of the missing thing can be characterised in effect terms, a primitive that changes the inference from acoustic scale to H0, but it cannot be named from inside the closure. Naming it would mean already holding it (Fig 13 :135: *"It cannot say which primitive is missing, because naming it would mean already holding it."*). The world literature's current preference for early-time solutions (Kamionkowski and Riess 2022) is a report from one evidence pool, not a ground reading, and is recorded as corroboration only.

**Verdict for this finding:** `single-source` for the figure's import rule (Fig 6 plus Fig 8 are the same closure argument, so they are one witness); the physics-facing identification is a `gap`.

**Refuter:** a specific extension whose predicted H0 agrees with an independent arbiter measurement at the ground, with provenance showing it was imported rather than fitted to the same data.

### F7 - Residual first, frame second

Fig 8 :53: **"Residual first, frame second."**

Fig 9 :27: **"describe it before you go looking, or"**

Fig 9 :28: **"any frame you pick will seem to fit"**

Fig 9 :32: **"you get a kind of thing, never the answer itself"**

Naming early dark energy before describing the residual is the apophenia the figures forbid. The residual here is described in effect terms first: a persistent two-slot disagreement between an inferred and a directly read expansion rate, with a partial evidence-pool overlap. Only after that description is it legitimate to look for a frame whose closure predicts that shape. This is also why the review literature's ranking of candidate models is a filter for the budget, never a witness for the verdict (Fig 9 :60).

**Refuter:** a frame selected before the residual was described that nevertheless predicts the two-slot shape and its persistence. That would be a genuine convergent derivation (Fig 8 :50: *"Two frames can hold the same atom by convergence, reaching it separately, which is corroboration at maximum independence."*).

### F8 - The inference itself is a round trip with a gap R_T

Fig 4 :30: **"So take the round trip and measure the gap. Without it you are navigating by dead reckoning: confident about a position nobody checked."**

Fig 4 :27: **"The transform between scales loses information, and that is the point."**

The early route abstracts from a measured acoustic scale to a model parameter and concretises back to a present expansion rate. That is a lossy transform T_A then T_E. The difference between the inferred 67.4 and the directly read 73.04 is not automatically an error, nor automatically new physics; it is partly the round-trip gap R_T = gap(x, (T_E compose T_A)(x)) that a coarser description cannot hold. Fig 4 :30 requires measuring that gap rather than assuming it is zero. Treating "LCDM inference is just a measurement of H0" would set R_T to zero and is the dead-reckoning error the figure names. Treating the whole gap as new physics is the opposite error: it ignores the information the abstraction discarded.

**Refuter:** a demonstration that the abstraction from acoustic scale to present H0 is lossless at the precision required, so R_T is zero and the entire residual must be channel or atom.

### F9 - Which cause is actual is a declared gap

Fig 8 :39: **"So a search that finds nothing is never a proof of absence."**

Fig 13 :134 quoted above, and Fig 9 :32 quoted above.

After a genuine re-census no figure line governs the choice between C1 and C2. The framework can say the question is a union, that the two bins are indiscernible from inside a frame, that an arbiter with a different closure is owed, and that the missing thing has a certain shape. It cannot say which cause is real. This is a `gap`, recorded plainly. "Unreachable is a verdict, not a silence."

**Refuter:** the instrument named in F3, or a direct ground reading from an arbiter frame with a different closure that lands unambiguously with one slot.

### F10 - Significance and team count are filters, not witnesses

Fig 9 :60 quoted in F1. Fig 7 :35 quoted in F5. Fig 2 :42 quoted in F5.

The "5-sigma" headline and the number of teams on a measurement set the budget for how hard to look; they do not decide what the disagreement means. buying independence in proportion to the cost of being wrong means prioritising a genuinely different calibrator or a standard-siren measurement over one more analysis of the same supernovae.

**Refuter:** a decision procedure that ranks causes by sigma level or by team size rather than by a discriminating reading.

### F11 - Honest status of the derivation

From *The Recursive Self-Improvement Trap*: *"the output is only as good as the validator. The agent, or the tool, or the frame, or the theory, cannot validate itself."* From *The Hard Problem is About Source*: *"If several paths create the same behavior, then behavior is a projection with lossy information."* Both bear directly on this question: two H0 readings that agree numerically would not determine a common source, and the absence of a source-discriminating instrument is the actual limit. This audit is a projection of the figures onto the literature, not a measurement of H0.

## 3. Pre-registered refuter

Required before any outreach, per the seat rule that a falsifier written before the run is a design instrument. Written here before the recommendation and conclusion.

The shape claim is refuted if any of the following holds:

1. **Union collapsed.** A single identified correction brings both the early-inferred and the nearby-read H0 into agreement within their combined uncertainties, with no categorically different residue. This breaks F2 and F9.
2. **Atom named at the ground.** A specific extension, fitted to one frame, predicts the other frame's H0 and is confirmed by an instrument with a genuinely different closure. This breaks F6 and F9 and converts the `gap` to a `grounded` cause.
3. **Round trip shown lossless.** A demonstration that the acoustic-scale to H0 inference preserves all information at the required precision, so R_T = 0. This breaks F8.
4. **Independence shown zero.** A demonstration that the two frames draw on the same evidence pool in full, so the disagreement is one frame and not two. This breaks F5.
5. **Persistence fails.** The residual trends down to zero under improved data without a change of cause. This breaks F2 and the Fig 5 :59 test.

If any fires, the shape is withdrawn or rewritten. Until one is run, no part of this may be labelled `grounded`.

## 4. Recommendation

1. Report per method, never a pooled H0 (F1). Keep the early-inferred and nearby-read slots and their residuals separate.
2. Split rather than search (F2). Treat the disagreement as a union of a channel condition and a closure condition, and unbundle before looking for a cause.
3. Lead with the indiscernibility result (F3). This is the single most exportable output: from inside either frame the two causes look the same, so an arbiter frame is owed.
4. Describe the missing instrument in effect terms (F3, F6, F9). Do not name a winner from the literature's majority preference; world agreement is one pool (F5, F7).
5. Measure the round-trip gap rather than assuming it away (F8). Distinguish model-extrapolation error from channel error from new atom.
6. Use sigma levels and team counts only to decide where to spend attention (F10). Buy independence with a different calibrator or a standard-siren channel.
7. Send the generator, not the recording: the residual, the split, the refuter. Fig 4 :22: *"Only methods cross up. Only head starts come down."*

### Strain after re-census

One live strain, named rather than hidden: the two measurement frames are not fully independent because their reported values are both interpreted through a shared LCDM reporting convention. ## 6. Conclusion of the audit

The shape survives: a two-condition union kept per method, an indiscernibility result that sends us to an arbiter frame, a measured round-trip gap, and a declared `gap` on the winning cause. What does not survive is any single-cause name drawn from the literature, any pooled H0, and any claim of `grounded` status. Strains after re-census: one, named above. No figure quote was taken from a summary.
