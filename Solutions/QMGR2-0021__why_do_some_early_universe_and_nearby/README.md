## The question restated as an effect

The expansion rate H0 is the anchor: the actual present expansion rate of the universe. It is not a frame and it does not change because two analysis teams disagree (Fig 2 :29). Two families of measurement frame each produce a prediction against that anchor:

- **Early-universe frame.** Measures the angular acoustic scale and the sound horizon, then *infers* H0 by extrapolating 13.8 billion years of expansion under the six-parameter LCDM model. Planck reports H0 = 67.4 +- 0.5 km/s/Mpc as an *inferred* late-Universe parameter.
- **Nearby-universe frame.** Calibrates standard candles geometrically, then *reads* H0 from the local Hubble flow. SH0ES reports H0 = 73.04 +- 1.04 km/s/Mpc; the independent TRGB calibrator reports a value near 69.8 km/s/Mpc.

Written in the loop's notation, R = |Gamma(b,a) - o'| is measured per slot, not averaged (Fig 1 :19). The slots here are the early-inference slot and the nearby-readout slot. The tension is that neither slot's residual trends down under its own best instruments (Fig 5 :59).

What each option leads to:

- Option A, "one method is simply wrong": leads to a single-lever hunt for a culprit, and a single lever clears only the region where one condition holds, leaving the intersection standing (Fig 9 :57).
- Option B, "the disagreement is a union of a channel failure in a frame and a missing atom in the shared model closure": leads to two conditions kept separate, an arbiter frame with a genuinely different closure, and a residual that stays indexed per method.

What an answer would look like, written down and bound as an ordinary term before the run (Formula, "Direction"): a *shape* naming (i) the two conditions that must be separated, (ii) the instrument that would discriminate a channel failure from a missing atom, and (iii) the refusal to name the winning cause until that instrument returns. Until it is bound it predicts nothing and no check in the loop can see it.

The verdict recorded in `09_watson_answer.md` is Option B in `specified` mode, with the specific cause declared a `gap`, kept per domain and never averaged.

## What is deliberately not here

- No personal emails of named individuals.
- No ranking of people by how likely they are to agree with a cause.
- No bulk-send plan.
- No claim that this derivation is `grounded`, no claim that it settles which cause is real, and no claim that the framework is the only account of the tension.

Three figure-grounded reasons, plus one practical one:

1. A room is not an anchor. *"A room's agreement is variation, not a verdict."* (Fig 7 :35) and *"Alignment is triangulation, not negotiation. A mirror cannot correct you."* (Fig 2 :35). A list of teams selected for agreeing with early dark energy manufactures the correlation that disqualifies a verdict.
2. Correlation has valence. *"disqualifying for a verdict: agreement about the world is worthless when the agreeing frames share an evidence pool"* (Fig 2 :40). Two reviews quoting the same SH0ES and Planck numbers are one witness wearing two names.
3. A verifier must be outside the interior. *"A verifier that can reconstruct the claim cannot verify it."* (Fig 10 :42). The first contact therefore carries the residual and its refuter, not the framework.
4. Legally, unsolicited bulk email to scraped addresses breaches GDPR and CAN-SPAM style rules in many jurisdictions and gets the sending domain blacklisted. Public opt-in routes avoid that failure as well as the structural one.
