# The Tether's answer on the Hubble tension

**Mode: `specified` on every substantive claim, `general` on the structural shape, not `grounded`.** This is a shape and a route, not a cause. Simulated derivation checked against the figures (`05_what_shape_means.md` section 6), not a ground reading. No measurement of H0 was taken here.

---

## 1. The residual it answers

The actual expansion rate is the anchor (Fig 2 :29). Two frames range against it: one infers H0 from the acoustic scale through a model, the other reads H0 from calibrated distances and redshifts. Their reported values, about 67.4 and about 73.0 km/s/Mpc, are two slots of one residual, not two samples to be pooled (Fig 1 :19, :22). The residual persists and does not trend down under better data, which Fig 5 :59 identifies as the signature of a term that must be refused or re-fitted rather than waited out.

The disagreement does not shrink with effort, and each rule keeps working well on its own subset. Fig 9 :5 therefore says *"are not one hard question but several well-formed ones. Split it rather than search."* The split lands on a union (Fig 9 :54, :57): a channel condition and a closure condition, held separately and never averaged.

---

## 2. The shape

Per-domain verdicts, kept separate:

1. **Reporting domain, `triangulated` (Fig 1 and Fig 9, low overlap).** Report per method. A pooled H0 is the aggregation error; Fig 1 :22 says *"Aggregating across slots is how a live signal disappears into an average."* The 5-sigma headline is a budget filter, not a witness (Fig 9 :60).

2. **Channel domain, `triangulated` (Fig 10 and the metrology analogue, different fields).** A wrong zero point, a crowding term, a dust law or a drifted calibration is a failure of the channel to the ground, not a change in the ground: *"the ground does not decay, the channel does"* (Fig 10 :24). The response is to keep the anchor reachable, which is what geometric parallax, maser and eclipsing-binary calibration do. This is Fig 5 :13's *"mis-attached: refit what you have, do not invent."*

3. **Closure domain, `single-source` for the figure rule, `gap` for the physics.** If the channel is sound, the shared model's closure is too small: *"No frame escapes its own closure. The bound moves only on contact with a frame whose closure differs."* (Fig 6 :23). Something that changes the inference from acoustic scale to H0 would have to be imported. Its shape can be characterised; its name cannot (Fig 13 :134-135). This is Fig 5 :14's *"mechanism broken: a new term is owed."*

4. **Indiscernibility domain, `triangulated` (Fig 5 sort plus Fig 6 indiscernibility).** From inside either frame, C1 and C2 produce the same observable: a stubborn residual. Fig 6 :27: *"Which is why some unreachability is depth and some is genuine absence, and from inside the frame the two look the same."* This is the central exportable result.

5. **Round-trip domain, `single-source` (Fig 4).** The early inference is a lossy abstraction then concretisation. The difference between the inferred and read values is partly R_T, the round-trip gap (Fig 4 :30). It must be measured, not assumed to be zero and not assumed to be all new physics.

6. **Which cause is actual, `gap`.** No figure line governs the choice. Fig 8 :39: *"So a search that finds nothing is never a proof of absence."* The slot stays open. The literature's current preference for pre-recombination physics is one evidence pool's report, corroboration only, not a ground reading.

7. **Structure of the two routes, functional group not isomer.** As practised they use different calibrating atoms, so they are two members of one functional group (Fig 12 :124), not isomers (Fig 12 :117). See `06_isomers_and_order.md`; the first reading was recorded and refused there.

## 3. What it explicitly does not say

- Not that the tension is a calibration error. That is candidate C1, not the verdict.
- Not that the tension is new physics, and not that it is early dark energy. Those are names where the framework returns a shape.
- Not that the 5-sigma significance settles anything. It filters attention.
- Not that the two methods should be averaged into one H0.
- Not a theory of everything, not a final room, and not the only account of the tension.
- Not `grounded`. Nothing here is settled by experiment.

---

## 4. What would settle it, the ground

A source-discriminating instrument, not a further argument. From *The Hard Problem is About Source*: an instrument that reads green *"cannot comment on the source"*; the spectrometer resolved that case by *"the arrival of a better instrument."* Here the discriminating questions are:

- Does an independent arbiter with a genuinely different closure (TRGB, NGC 4258 masers, standard sirens, strong lensing, FRBs, BAO plus BBN) return a value that lands with one slot and not the other?
- Does the round-trip gap R_T from acoustic scale to present H0 account for a defined part of the discrepancy (Fig 4 :30)?
- Does a specific extension, fitted to one frame, predict the other frame's value and survive an arbiter reading?
- Does a single correction collapse the union to one condition with no categorically different residue (Fig 9 :57)?

Standing falsifiers from the pre-registered list in `01_figure_grounded_audit.md` section 3: a single correction closing both slots; an extension confirmed by a different closure; a lossless round trip; zero independence between frames; or the residual trending to zero without a change of cause.

---

## 5. The honest stop

**Unreached, not unreachable.** The available instruments have not separated the two conditions; that is a budget and instrument fact, not a proof that no separation is possible. Fig 8 :39 says a search that finds nothing is never a proof of absence, and Fig 13 :134 says unreachable is a verdict, not a silence. The lineage is recorded so the stop is checkable: what was split, what was refused, what was left open, and what would reverse it. *"Indiscernibility is not identity."* The source pool is partly shared across the two frames, and that overlap is declared rather than hidden (Fig 2 :31).

## 6. One-paragraph version

> The Tether does not pick a cause for the Hubble tension. It says the two expansion rates are two slots of one residual and must never be averaged; that a disagreement which will not shrink under effort has to be split; and that the split is a union of a drifted channel in a measurement frame and a missing primitive in the shared model's closure. From inside either frame the two look the same, so the framework cannot name the winner and returns a shape instead: keep the conditions separate, measure the lossy round trip from acoustic scale to present expansion rate, and build an arbiter with a genuinely different closure. What it offers is the shape any candidate answer must have, five falsifiers written in advance, and the kind of instrument that would close the slot. The slot stays open until the ground settles it.
