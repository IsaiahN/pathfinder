# Supporting derivation: structure, order and analogues

**Mode: `specified` on the structural claims, `general` on the cycle shape.** Simulated derivation checked against the figures, not a ground reading (see `05_what_shape_means.md` §6).

## RESIDUAL

The question asks whether anticipation is possible when the mechanism and the network change. Before choosing any figure line, two structural questions are owed:

1. Is the relation between "stress test" and "rare cascade" a chain or a cycle? A chain is "degenerate" (Fig 12 :48) and "cannot close by construction" (Fig 12 :121); a cycle re-enters its input and produces a new remainder.
2. Is the question one problem or a union of conditions? If it is a union, a single lever clears only the region where one condition holds and leaves the intersections standing.

## DOMAIN CENSUS

- Atoms available: the six substrate terms (Fig 13), the seven bonds plus negation (Operators table), the habitat actors (Fig 11 :44 to :47). No new primitive is available without import.
- Bonds relevant: `→` sequence (ordered, "A then B; B needs A to have happened"), `⇒` production (ordered, "A produces B; B did not exist before"), `−` subtraction (progress), `⋛` comparison (threshold), and the cycle, which is a structure rather than a sixth bond.
- Scale: first order inside the model, second order at cascade propagation, cross-scale at the round trip R_T.
- Anchor: the ground for Domain 2; the figures as metalaws for Domain 1; never agreement for Domain 3.

## DERIVATION

### Q1 - Chain or cycle?

Fig 12 :121: **"cycle. The output re-enters as input. The sign of the loop decides whether it closes, and a chain cannot close by construction."**
Fig 13 :31 to :32: **"a remainder compounding through a chain that amplifies is chaos, and the detail a crossing discards reappears above it as noise."**

A one-shot stress test with a fixed clearing vector is a chain: shock in, loss out. A real cascade in which participants react to the aggregate they collectively create is a cycle: the output re-enters as input. The Caccioli, Barucca and Kobayashi review states that "individual participants react to the aggregate dynamics of the market that they collectively create, so that to understand systemic risk the feedback loop between individual and collective dynamics has to be accounted for."

⇒ The stress test and the cascade are **not the same structure**. The test's chain cannot close; the cascade's cycle can. A model that omits the loop is not a less accurate version of the cascade; it is a different object. This is the F6 finding of `01_figure_grounded_audit.md` stated structurally.

**Falsifier, written before the run:** exhibit a rare cascade with no feedback loop and no compounding remainder, a pure one-pass chain, and show it covers the mechanism-changing cases. Eisenberg-Noe clearing is a chain, but it is the degenerate case and does not cover mechanism change.

### Q2 - Partition or union?

Fig 9 :55: "A bundled problem is usually a union of overlapping conditions, not a list of separate ones."
Fig 9 :57: "a case in two conditions is not a harder case of either. It stops being a mixture and becomes a specific compound."
Fig 9 :60: "Use filters for the budget and witnesses for the verdict. Never let a filter hand you a verdict."

The two conditions are (a) the mechanism changes and (b) the network structure changes. They overlap: a funding link that appears under stress is both a new mechanism (repo run) and a new edge. So the residual is a **union compound**, not two independent questions and not one harder question.

Consequence: a single lever (more scenarios, a finer loss function) clears the region where only (a) or only (b) holds and leaves the intersection standing. The intersection is where the rare cascade lives. "What remains is smaller and it is also categorically different" (Fig 9 :57).

**Falsifier:** show that mechanism change and structure change are independent and non-overlapping in a real crisis, so the union collapses to a partition and one lever suffices. Not supported by the network-science sources opened.

### Q3 - What is the order by dependency?

Fig 3 :20: "The chain is ordered by dependency, not by importance. Each link consumes what the one above produces, so effort spent above the break arrives at a step that cannot use it."
Fig 3 :25 to :27: the middle link, "having terms for what a situation offers and being able to say what would count as progress, has neither, so it attracts the least attention and the fewest instruments. A link with no instrument is not a link that works."

Ordered links for this question:

| link | status in a standard stress test | consequence |
|---|---|---|
| 1 perceive (mechanism change registered?) | weak: calibrated on historical data | stale percepts (Fig 10 :26) |
| 2 vocabulary (terms for the changed affordance?) | weakest: no instrument | "a reading of nothing" (Fig 3 :23) |
| 3 objective (what counts as anticipating?) | often implicit: no cascade flagged | preference held but unbound |
| 4 planning (scenario generation) | strong | effort lands above the break |
| 5 learn and carry (update after crisis) | partial | replay of past success, not new capability |

Fig 5 :65 to :66 supplies the remedy for link 3: "make the sought-for shape an ORDINARY TERM. Write down what an answer would look like, bind it, and let it accrue a residual like anything else." The pre-registered refuter in `01` §4 is this discipline applied to this question.

**Falsifier:** show a stress-test programme whose middle link has an instrument, that is, a checkable test for whether the model's terms cover the mechanisms available to the crisis. If such an instrument exists and passes, link 2 is not the break and the order must be re-censused.

### Q4 - Is there an isomer claim?

Fig 12 :117: **"isomer. Same atoms, different bond, different substance. The difference is invisible in the formula and decisive in the behaviour."**

A stress test and a real cascade can be written over the same atoms (institutions, exposures, losses). They differ in bond: the test uses `→` sequence (shock then loss); the cascade uses `⇒` production plus cycle (a default the initial state did not contain, which re-enters). **Under Fig 12's definition they are isomers: same atoms, different bond, different substance.** This is stronger than "the model is an approximation." It says a clean test result and a safe system are different substances that share an ingredient list, and nothing in the ingredient list tells them apart.

Fig 12 :97 to :98 names the chemistry precedent: silver fulminate and silver cyanate, same composition, different properties. The analogue is deliberate and bounded: it is a structural analogy, not a claim about financial chemistry.

**Falsifier:** write the test and the cascade over the same bond set and show identical behaviour on the mechanism-changing cases. That would collapse the isomer claim into a variant claim.

### Q5 - The analogue, and its limit

The ecology early-warning literature is the low-overlap analogue. Scheffer et al. (2009, Nature): "work in different scientific fields is now suggesting the existence of generic early-warning signals that may indicate for a wide class of systems if a critical threshold is approaching." This supports the *shape* half of the verdict: a generic signal shape can be stated across domains.

Its limit is also its strength as an analogue. Dakos et al. (2016, PNAS): indicator applicability "was strongly limited by the requirement for ecosystem-specific knowledge of transition-generating mechanisms and their drivers to choose relevant state variables for analysis." The analogue therefore corroborates the **contact constraint**: without terms for the mechanism, you cannot choose what to monitor. It does not corroborate strong anticipation under mechanism change; that case is outside the analogue's domain. Re-census places it in a different domain, so no strain remains (see `01` §5).

## CONCLUSION

The relation is a cycle over a chain, a union compound, and a Fig 12 isomer: same atoms as the real cascade, different bond, different substance. The dependency break is the vocabulary link, not the scenario-generation link. The ecology analogue corroborates the shape and the contact constraint, and stops there. All four sub-claims carry falsifiers written above. Mode: `specified` for Q1 to Q4, `general` for the cycle shape in Q1, ungrounded throughout.
