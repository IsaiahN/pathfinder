## 4. Round trip gap R_T

R_T is the gap between what was sent up and what came back down, `gap(x, (T_E ∘ T_A)(x))` (Symbols table), and Fig 4 :30 requires it to be measured rather than assumed zero. I ran the round trip on the condensation this folder actually uses.

1. **Sent up (x):** the verbatim figure set, chiefly Fig 11 :70 (test harness), Fig 10 :24-29 (channel decay), Fig 8 :32 (idempotent closure), Fig 9 :44 (no sealed room) and Fig 7 :36 (independent rooms).
2. **Abstraction (T_A):** the shorthand in `07_the_tether_answer.md`: a stress test proves wiring not capability; more scenarios add no mechanism; the channel decays; a clean result is not proof of absence; use independent rooms and enumerate outward.
3. **Concretisation (T_E):** a proposed operational stress-testing programme: time-varying exposures, behavioural adaptation rules, higher-frequency liquidity readings, multiple independently built models, and a measured model-versus-outcome comparison.

**R_T is nonzero.** Three things the shorthand holds do not survive the trip back down:

- **Which mechanism to allow to change.** The figures say the channel decays; they do not specify the variable to instrument. The ecology source says the choice requires system-specific mechanism knowledge. A programme must choose, and that choice is an import or a ground reading, not a figure derivation.
- **What independence costs.** Fig 7 :36 requires rooms to differ and to match the surfaced residual; it gives no threshold for how much difference is enough. Building independent models is expensive and the budget decides how many rooms exist.
- **When to stop enumerating.** Fig 11 :44 says outward until the cascade stops mattering; it gives no operational stopping rule. A programme must bind one before the run.

Conversely, one thing survives cleanly: a substituted habitat proves wiring and not capability, and a clean result is not proof of absence. That is the residue worth sending. R_T was **not** measured numerically, because no ground reading exists; it is recorded qualitatively as the enumerated set of dropped constraints. Treating the formula as identical to the law set would set R_T to zero and would be the dead-reckoning error Fig 4 :30 names. The honest form is a lossy condensation with a measured gap, which is what `09_watson_answer.md` states.
