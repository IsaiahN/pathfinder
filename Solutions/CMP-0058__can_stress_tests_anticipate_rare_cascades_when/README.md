## The verdict in one line

A stress test can price cascades that are compositions inside its own model closure, but it cannot anticipate a rare cascade whose mechanism or network structure changes during the crisis: a test harness is a substituted habitat and "proves wiring and never capability" (Fig 11 :70), closure is idempotent (Fig 8 :32), and a clean result "proves nothing" (Fig 9 :44). `specified`, ungrounded, candidate.

## What is deliberately not here

- No personal emails of named individuals.
- No ranking of people by how likely they are to agree.
- No bulk-send plan.

Three reasons in the figures' own words:

1. **Agreement is not evidence.** "agreement compounds, evidence does not" (Fig 7 :13); "agreement about the world is worthless when the agreeing frames share an evidence pool" (Fig 2 :40).
2. **Selecting for receptiveness manufactures the disqualifying correlation.** "High correlation is disqualifying for a verdict and efficient for a convention" (Seat Map :163, quoted in the exemplar).
3. **A verifier must not hold the interiors.** "A verifier that can reconstruct the claim cannot verify it" (Fig 10 :42). The first contact carries the residual and its refuter, never the framework.

Legally: scraping personal emails for unsolicited bulk outreach breaches GDPR/CAN-SPAM style rules in many jurisdictions. Public opt-in venues avoid that failure as well as the structural one.
