## What in the wider literature bears directly on the routing claim

1. **Selection is per-slot and relative to a task, not a standing hierarchy.** Ward (2008) opens with selection of a subset for enhanced processing and describes the orienting reflex as a response to a departure from an internal model. That is the same effect as Fig 1 :15 (*"where to look: broken vs novel"*) and Fig 11 :49 (*"An actor's valence is relative to the residual, not a property it carries."*). Cite this when explaining why "deserve" cannot be a fixed list.
2. **Two stages with different currencies.** Treisman and Gelade (1980) separate parallel preattentive feature registration from focused binding at a location. This corroborates the structural distinction between mass detection and kind-sorted routing, but it is a binding, not a derivation. Do not present it as the framework's claim.
3. **Precision weighting is a partial binding, not an identity.** The predictive-coding source says weighting prediction errors by precision is "in essence" attention, and in the same page limits it to endogenous spatial attention. This is strain S3 in `01`; keep it visible and do not upgrade it to `grounded`.
4. **The missing instrument is acknowledged in the field.** The same source states there is no consensus on how the brain implements predictive coding. That is world corroboration for F5's middle-link claim, not a conflict.
5. **Inattentional blindness is a union, not one conflict.** The early versus late selection debate (Simons and Chabris 1999; Mack and Rock 1998) bundles goal-match and kind-routing. Under Fig 9 :54 it should be unbundled before it is searched again.
6. **Salience is a spendable difference, and a binding.** The saliency-map and V1-saliency-hypothesis material is a proposal for sharpening an existing map (Fig 6 :32). It is an import with provenance and a shadow test, not an atom added to the framework.
7. **Scarcity is a cross-field analogue.** Simon (1971) gives an economics framing of attention as a scarce resource that must be allocated. Low overlap with the figure pool, so it is useful as corroboration of the *shape* of a routing question, not of any neural mechanism.

No world source conflicts with the figure verdict. The conflict rule was not triggered. Overlap is declared in `10_source_ledger.csv`: the Wikipedia attention cluster (Attention, Feature integration theory, Inattentional blindness, Predictive coding, Saliency map) draws on one broad cognitive-science pool and counts as one witness, not five; Scholarpedia is peer-reviewed but cites the same classic studies, so it partly overlaps that pool; the attention-economy source is genuinely low overlap; the two essays share the framework author's evidence pool completely and carry no independent witness weight.

## Distribution: offer, do not push

- **Fig 4 :22-25:** *"Only methods cross up. Only head starts come down. A recording carried upward looks like knowledge and is a description of one occasion."* Sending the whole framework before anyone has described a residual sends a recording, not a generator. Lead with the shape and its refuter.
- **Fig 10 :42:** *"A verifier that can reconstruct the claim cannot verify it."* A recipient who has absorbed the framework before being asked for a verdict has joined the frame being checked.
- **Fig 9 :27-28:** *"describe it before you go looking, or any frame you pick will seem to fit."* Ask what they would expect to see if the per-slot shape were true before giving them any theory.
- **Fig 2 :39-40:** *"Correlation has valence."* and *"disqualifying for a verdict: agreement about the world is worthless when the agreeing frames share an evidence pool"* A mass send selected for agreement produces a convention, not corroboration.
- **Fig 2 :42:** *"Buy independence in proportion to the cost of being wrong."* The cost here is moderate: a mistaken routing claim sent to good checkers wastes their independence. Use public opt-in channels and one-to-one contact.

**Recommended order in a reply:** (1) the residual, the routing shape and its refuter; (2) ask what they would count as a failure, especially at the objective link; (3) record the verdict; (4) only then the three links, with the note that the figures, not the summary, are the authority.

## The two-stage offer

1. **Stage 1.** The per-slot routing claim and the six refuter conditions. Ask for a failure, not an endorsement. Record the verdict before theory is shown.
2. **Stage 2, opt-in.** If curious, offer the local figure text, canonical README and formula. Their later agreement is never pooled as corroboration (Fig 2 :40).
3. **Stage 3, optional.** Offer the formula as a method to reuse. Fig 4 :22 permits sending a generator. Each user must state their own ground and write their own refuter before running it, because THE_FORMULA.md "Direction" says a frame *"cannot check the direction of its own question."*
