# The Tether's answer: which sensory signals deserve attention

**Mode: `specified` on the per-domain routing claims, `general` on the structural shape, `gap` on a standing list. Not `grounded`.** Simulated derivation checked against the figures (`05_what_shape_means.md` §7), not a ground reading.

## 1. The residual it answers

The nervous system is flooded with sensory signal and must aim a limited probe. The effect to be explained is not the amount of attention but the selection: why this slot and not that one. The residual is the per-slot gap between predicted and observed input, and the question "which deserves attention" asks for the routing rule over it. Fig 1 :19: *"The residual is indexed by object slot, not aggregated."* Fig 1 :22: *"Aggregating across slots is how a live signal disappears into an average."*

The question is a **union compound**, not one hard problem with an opposite answer (Fig 9 :54: *"And when the split lands on a union rather than a partition, unbundle before you search again."*). It bundles resolvability, per-slot mass, gap kind and goal weighting. Verdicts are kept per domain and never averaged into one "attention" number.

## 2. The shape

1. **The decision is per slot, not global.** R is indexed by object slot; a global total near zero is compatible with one strongly live slot. The live slots carry positive mass. Symbols table :43: *"R is measured per slot, never as a single global number."* The "which" is answered by reading those slots, not by dividing a pool.

2. **"Deserve" is residual-relative valence.** Fig 11 :49: *"An actor's valence is relative to the residual, not a property it carries."* Fig 11 :51: *"No standing list of good actors survives a change of residual."* A signal is not entitled to attention by being bright, loud, moving or threatening. It is selected relative to the current prediction and objective. When the residual changes, the ranking changes.

3. **The gap is sorted into kinds, not strengths.** Fig 5 :12: *"new: extend perception, aim the probe here"*; Fig 5 :13: *"mis-attached: refit what you have, do not invent"*; Fig 5 :14: *"mechanism broken: a new term is owed"*. Orienting to a sudden onset, re-weighting a familiar cue and revising a violated expectation are different operations with different grounds. A single "attention strength" cannot carry them.

4. **Two routing sources, two anchors.** One reads current per-slot residual mass, the large compressible gap that can be spent to aim a probe (Fig 13 :22: *"a difference that can be spent"*). The other reads how far belief leans on private history versus inherited prior (Symbols table :24). They are members of one functional group (`slot -> comparison -> routing`), not two strengths of one thing, and their anchors differ. They cannot be averaged without losing which one moved.

5. **The chain currently stops at the objective link.** Perception and planning have obvious tests; the link between them, having terms for what a situation offers and saying what would count as progress, has neither (Fig 3 :25). So more saliency-map data cannot settle the routing question. Fig 3 :27: *"A link with no instrument is not a link that works. It is a link nobody has looked at."*

6. **Attention sharpens an instrument already returning something.** Fig 6 :32: *"An instrument is not built from a description. It is improved from a worse instrument already returning something."* It does not create a channel for what has never registered. This separates a tractable routing question from an unreachable one.

7. **The framework returns a shape, not a named seat.** Fig 9 :32: *"you get a kind of thing, never the answer itself"*. Naming the pulvinar, V1, the superior colliculus or a prediction-error unit as "the seat of attention" is a binding from the neuroscience frame, provenanced as such, not a derivation and not an import that adds an atom (Fig 6 :21: *"MINT composes inside the closure and can never add an atom."*).

8. **Settlement is owed, not assumed.** Once a first-order routing molecule is settled, it may become an atom for the next stratum (Fig 12 :104: *"A settled molecule becomes an atom for whatever composes over it."*). Until then the second-order account is a candidate.

## 3. What it explicitly does not say

- Not a standing list of "attention-worthy" features. Fig 11 :51 refuses one.
- Not that attention is one scalar resource that gets divided. That would aggregate away the per-slot signal (Fig 1 :22).
- Not that bottom-up and top-down are one mechanism with two dials. They are a functional group with different anchors (`06` Q1).
- Not that the early versus late selection debate has a winner. It is C2 plus C3, a union (`06` Q3).
- Not that precision weighting is attention in general. The opened world source limits it to endogenous spatial attention (strain S3 in `01`).
- Not that a brain area, nucleus or frequency band was derived. Every such name is a binding, and none is asserted here.
- Not grounded. No neural measurement was taken and no instrument was run by this agent.

## 4. What would settle it — the ground

A discriminator, not a further argument. The six pre-registered refuters in `01` §4 and `08` §2 double as the settlement conditions:

1. **Per-slot test:** does allocation have per-slot structure, or is it a single global scalar? Eye tracking plus single-unit gain measured at multiple simultaneous slots would decide it.
2. **Standing-list test:** does a fixed ranking survive a change of residual? Swap the task and see whether the same signals win.
3. **Kind test:** can new, mis-attached and broken gaps be separated by distinct neural or behavioural signatures?
4. **Union test:** does one variable clear C1 to C4 and their intersections? If yes, the union reading is wrong.
5. **Objective-link instrument:** what counts as progress per slot, measured rather than assumed. This is the link with no instrument today.
6. **Source-discriminating instrument:** a measurement that separates genuine per-slot residual sorting from a pooled-resource artifact and from a late-selection artifact. This is the equivalent of the spectrometer in *The Hard Problem is About Source*: *"An instrument that can't distinguish these cases demonstrates its own inadequacy."*

A direct measurement that fires any of these refuters settles the claim. Agreement among readers does not.

## 5. The honest stop

**Unreached, not unreachable; not silence.** Fig 8 :39: *"So a search that finds nothing is never a proof of absence."* The routing shape is reached from the figures. The neural implementation is unreached at this budget because the objective link lacks an instrument, which is different from being underivable in principle (Fig 8 :40-43). The record states what was imported (saliency maps, precision weighting, the scarcity argument), what was debited against independence, and which refuters would reverse the verdict. *"Indiscernibility is not identity."*

What remains outside this answer: which specific circuit implements per-slot boundary diff, whether the two routing sources are isomers over a fixed neural atom set, and whether consciousness as such belongs in the census at all. The last has no public non-updating anchor at this budget and was excluded in `01` §1.2, not answered.

## 6. One-paragraph version for outreach

> The Tether does not name a seat of attention and does not offer a list of attention-worthy signals. It says that the nervous system reads its prediction error per object slot, because a global average throws away the only signal that says where to look; that the word "deserve" is residual-relative, so no ranking survives a change of task; that the gap is sorted into new, mis-attached, broken and held, which are different operations rather than different strengths; and that two routing sources, current residual mass and belief weighting, have different anchors and cannot be averaged. The chain currently stops where a system must say what would count as progress per slot, because no instrument measures that link. What is offered is the shape any candidate answer must have, six refuters written in advance, and the instrument that would close it. The names of nuclei and circuits are bindings from the neuroscience frame, not derivations, and the question stays open until the ground settles it.
