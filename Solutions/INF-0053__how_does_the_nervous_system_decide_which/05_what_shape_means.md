## 1. A shape is a constraint, not a filler

The figures README states the limit: the framework returns *"what a missing thing would have to look like (a shape, not a name)"*. Fig 9 :32: *"you get a kind of thing, never the answer itself"*. Fig 13 record: *"It can say the answer is out of reach with the library as it stands, so something has to be imported before it is asked again."*

From *The Library of Babel* (opened): *"This means that you can cut your search short if the set of books returned is small and descriptive enough that it provides a general shape of the answer that satisfies your question."*

So a **shape** is the narrowed form of an answer you can state before you can name it. It constrains; it does not fill. For this question:

- **A shape:** a per-slot residual is sorted by boundary diff into new, mis-attached, broken and held, with valence relative to the current residual, and two routing sources with different anchors.
- **A name:** the pulvinar is the seat of attention; attention is gamma-band synchronization; attention is precision weighting of prediction errors. Each may be true, but none is delivered by the figure set.

The distinction matters because the question's phrasing ("which sensory signals deserve attention") invites a named list. The shape refuses the list and returns a routing rule instead.

## 2. How the shape is obtained for this question

- *Library of Babel* (opened): use the vocabulary closest to the thing, look for recurring new terms that overlap or correlate with it, recurse. *"With each new term gained, you narrow the space by definition and deepen your vocabulary."* Applied here: start with "selection", "residual", "slot", "boundary", "anchor" and let the attention literature supply nearer terms (saliency map, precision weighting, exogenous and endogenous orienting).
- Fig 6 :30: *"The description travels. Fails by being sharpened into terms only we can read. Cast it in effect terms."* Fig 9 :30: *"your words are borrowed too, so describe the effect rather than the cause"*. So describe the effect ("a slot with positive residual mass gets the next probe"), not the cause ("a nucleus fires").
- *Decoding Tacit Knowledge* (carried): *"When contact isn't possible, the best you can send is a plain description of what you'd expect to see, because anyone can check that against the world."* This is the warrant for the outreach order in `02` and `04`.
- Fig 9 :27-28: *"describe it before you go looking, or any frame you pick will seem to fit"*. The residual was described in `01` §0 before saliency maps or predictive coding were consulted. This order is recorded, not assumed.
- *Library of Babel* (opened) on bundling: *"Perhaps your problem was a bundle or conflation of several problems masquerading as one?"* This is the warrant for the union reading in `01` §1.3 and F7.

## 3. What licenses a name here

Only two things, both outside the shape itself:

1. **The ground settles it.** THE_FORMULA.md step 5: *"the ground settles it. Verified only by what pays. A gate is not the ground."* The canonical README: the framework *"stops at the limit of what the available instruments can distinguish, and records the lineage so the stop is honest."* For this question: an experiment that shows a specific circuit implements per-slot boundary diff would pay for the name.
2. **A provenanced import from a frame whose closure differs** (Fig 6 :23, Fig 8 :50-52), with a source gate and shadow test. A saliency map imported from computer vision must explain the gap of "how is the probe aimed", and its origin must be recorded. It adds no atom to the framework; it gives the framework access to a term it already had reason to expect (Fig 13 :22, *"a difference that can be spent"*).

*They're Scanning Books* (carried) sharpens this: *"The real import is not a new term in the existing space. It is a new dimension of the space."* And: *"Declaring the primitives does not make them neutral. It makes the choice visible."* So declaring that the system tracks slots and residuals is a visible choice, not a discovery of the brain's own vocabulary.

## 4. Why a reading-off-a-projection is the specific forbidden error

*The Hard Problem is About Source* (carried) gives the mechanism. A spectrometer reads green and *"cannot comment on the source"*: one green light, or yellow plus blue, or RGB, or CMYK, or a scheme not yet discovered. *"If several paths create the same behavior, then behavior is a projection with lossy information."* And: *"An instrument that can't distinguish these cases demonstrates its own inadequacy."* It was the **spectrometer**, not an argument, that resolved it.

Applied here: a saliency map, a firing rate and a behavioural cue effect are readings. Several mechanisms can produce the same reading (genuine per-slot residual sorting, a pooled resource, a late-selection artifact). Naming one mechanism from the reading is the metamerism error. What is owed is a discriminator, not a name. This is the reason `07` §4 asks for an instrument that separates per-slot mass from pooled gain.

*The Recursive Self-Improvement Trap* (opened): *"The catch is that the output is only as good as the validator. The agent, or the tool, or the frame, or the theory, cannot validate itself."*

## 5. Q answered: what licenses "deserve"?

**Nothing in the framework licenses a standing answer.** "Deserve" is re-grounded as residual-relative valence (Fig 11 :49, :51). A signal deserves the probe while it carries positive mass relative to the current prediction and objective. When the residual changes, the ranking changes. Any answer that lists bright, loud, moving or threatening signals as a fixed set has committed the error Fig 11 :51 names.

The specific claim that attention "is" precision weighting is also refused as a general identity. The opened *Predictive coding* page limits it to endogenous spatial attention and notes there is no consensus on implementation. It is a binding with a shadow test, kept `specified`, with the refuter in `01` F4.

## 6. Correction to my own §3 (visible, not erased)

I first wrote §3 as if "shape, not a name" forbade naming any neural structure at all. That was an overstatement, and it is corrected here rather than silently rewritten.

- The canonical phrase is *"what a **missing** thing would have to look like (a shape, not a name)."* Its subject is a primitive not held in Γ.
- The routing atoms used here (residual, slot, boundary, weighting, gradient, network) are already in the figure vocabulary. Naming V1, the pulvinar or a saliency map is a **binding** from the neuroscience frame onto an atom or a settled molecule, not the invention of a seventh primitive.
- What remains forbidden is (a) claiming the framework derived the name, (b) claiming the binding moves the wall (Fig 6 :21: *"MINT composes inside the closure and can never add an atom."*), and (c) presenting a reading as a source-discriminating instrument (§4 above).
- The metamerism point in §4 is **not** retracted: a mechanism named from a projection without a discriminator is still the source error. The reason is the missing discriminator, not a ban on naming held atoms.

Both the overstatement and its narrowing are kept so neither is quietly lost. This mirrors the correction pattern in the exemplar's `05` section 5b.

## 7. Two consequences for this solution

**Prestige is not action.** From *The Recursive Self-Improvement Trap* (opened): *"Prestige must never be allowed to validate an action directly... Someone let prestige stand in for proof... Prestige can fund an action. It can give an action a budget to be tried. But it cannot settle an action. Only the ground can settle it. The Ground Maintainer must be blind to prestige."* A list of famous attention researchers assembled to agree with the routing claim is the named collapse. The legitimate ask gives the claim a budget to be tried by independent frames.

**My own audit is a simulation, not a ground reading.** Same essay (opened): *"LLMs can quickly simulate an approximate answer/output with the formula found in the architecture post. It will often be better than the generic outputs that you will usually get otherwise too. However, the truly testable output would need to be derived from the real architecture or working the formula yourself."* So `01`, `06`, `07`, `09` and `11` are simulated derivations, checked for internal consistency against the figures. They are **not** tests of the routing claim and must be labelled as such. They cannot settle anything they report on.
