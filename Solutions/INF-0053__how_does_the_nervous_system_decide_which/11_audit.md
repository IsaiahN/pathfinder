## 4. Round trip gap R_T

The Symbols table defines R_T as the gap between what was sent up and what came back down, `gap(x, (T_E ∘ T_A)(x))`, measuring what the coarser description cannot hold. Fig 4 :30 requires it to be measured rather than assumed zero. I ran the round trip on the condensation this folder actually sends.

1. **Sent up (x):** the verbatim figure set, chiefly Fig 1 :19 and :22 (per-slot residual), Fig 5 :10-14 (four kinds), Fig 11 :49 and :51 (residual-relative valence), Fig 9 :54 (union), Fig 6 :32 (instrument rule).
2. **Abstraction (T_A):** the shorthand in `02` and `07`, that the system reads its prediction error per slot, sorts the gap by kind, treats "deserve" as relative to the current residual, and has two routing sources with different anchors.
3. **Concretisation (T_E):** a concrete neural claim of the kind found in the literature, for example that a saliency map in V1 implements the probe aim, or that precision weighting of prediction errors is attention.

**R_T is nonzero.** Three things held by the shorthand do not survive the trip down:

- the **kind distinction** between new, mis-attached and broken, which the neural binding of "salience" or "precision" tends to collapse into one strength;
- the **two-anchor condition**, which disappears if bottom-up and top-down are reported as one resource pool;
- the **union structure**, which disappears if early versus late selection is reported as a single resolved debate.

Conversely, one thing survives cleanly: a signal's valence is relative to the current residual, so no fixed feature list is licensed. That is the residue worth sending. R_T was **not** measured numerically, because no ground reading exists; it is recorded qualitatively as the enumerated set of dropped constraints. Treating "the formula is the condensed version of the metalaws" as identity would set R_T to zero and would be the dead-reckoning error Fig 4 :30 names. The honest form is a lossy condensation with a measured gap, which is what `09_watson_answer.md` states.
