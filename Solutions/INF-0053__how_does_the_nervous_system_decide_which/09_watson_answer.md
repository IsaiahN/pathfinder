## DOMAIN CENSUS

R by arity, symmetry and scale, then one line per domain with actors, currency and anchor, partition or union, and order by dependency.

Symmetry: broken, because an actor's valence is relative to the residual (Fig 11 :49). Scale: multiple neural strata with the same kernel and different actors, currency and anchor (Fig 4 :19-20). Question type: union of four overlapping conditions, not a partition (Fig 9 :54).
- **D1, sensory-perceptual frame.** Actors: receptors, feature maps, saliency maps, V1, superior colliculus, pulvinar. Currency: firing rate, prediction error, signal-to-noise gain. Anchor: the physical stimulus and behavioural report. Verdict scope: per-slot indexing and kind-sort, `specified`.
- **D2, selection and control frame.** Actors: controller Γ, belief b, weighting w, dorsal and ventral attention networks. Currency: per-slot residual, boundary diff, description-length bargain. Anchor: behavioural payoff and survival outcome. Verdict scope: two routing sources with different anchors, `specified`.
- **D3, evolutionary habitat frame.** Actors: organism, environment, tasks, predators, prey, conspecifics. Currency: contact, survival, reproduction. Anchor: what actually happens. Verdict scope: valence relative to residual, `general`.
- **D4, verification and outreach frame.** Actors: researchers, experiments, journals, meetings. Currency: measured data, residuals, agreement. Anchor: the ground, never agreement. Verdict scope: stage-1 check before theory, `general`.
- **Excluded candidate**: phenomenal consciousness as such has no public non-updating anchor at this budget, so it is not a domain yet. It is recorded as excluded, not answered.
- **Order by dependency**: resolvability, per-slot mass, gap kind, then goal weighting. Refuse before mint (THE_FORMULA.md step 2); describe the residual before naming a frame (Fig 9 :27-28).

## VERDICT (from the figures)

The routing shape survives; a standing list does not. The nervous system's "which" is a per-slot read of residual R, sorted by boundary diff into new, mis-attached, broken and held, with valence relative to the current residual and objective. Two routing sources, current residual mass and belief weighting, sit in one functional group over a shared atom set, have different anchors, and cannot be averaged. The union order is resolvability, mass, kind, weighting. The chain stops at the objective link, where no instrument currently measures what counts as progress per slot. The specific neural binding is `unreached`, not refuted, not grounded and not silence. Any named circuit, nucleus or frequency band is a binding from the neuroscience frame with provenance, and no such name is asserted as derived. Mode `specified` on the per-domain claims, `general` on the shape, `gap` on a standing list.

- **cost if wrong:** bounded and locatable. The packet is mode-labelled, states six refuters before the conclusion and records provenance, so an error is findable rather than invisible. The real cost is spending the independence of outside readers by sending a recording instead of a generator, which Fig 2 :42 prices ("Buy independence in proportion to the cost of being wrong."). That cost is avoidable by leading with the per-slot shape and its refuter.
- **undoable:** yes. The neural bindings are withheld, not withdrawn; the routing shape stands independently of them. A superseding packet can correct a failed sub-claim without touching the derivation, and no primitive has to be removed. If a refuter fires, the affected domain is narrowed and the remaining domains keep their separate verdicts.
- **reversed by:** a ground reading, not agreement. A per-slot measurement showing no per-slot structure, a fixed ranking surviving task changes, one variable clearing all four union conditions, a validated instrument at the objective link, or a source-discriminating instrument separating per-slot sorting from pooled-gain and late-selection artifacts would move the claim. A figure re-census admitting a standing list would break Fig 11 :51. Until one of these fires, the verdict stands as `candidate`.
