## What an answer would look like (bound as an ordinary term)

Before the run, the sought-for shape is written down and bound so it can accrue a residual like any other term (THE_FORMULA.md, "Direction"). For this question the answer-shape is: **a per-slot routing rule that sorts sensory residuals by kind and aims the probe at slots carrying positive mass, with the valence of a slot relative to the current residual rather than carried by the signal.** A claim of this shape must say (a) what is indexed, (b) what is sorted, (c) what anchor settles the selection, and (d) what would refute it. Anything that merely names a brain area or a neurotransmitter, without (a) to (d), is a binding from the neuroscience frame, not a figure-derived answer.

## What is deliberately not here

- No personal emails of named individuals.
- No ranking of people by how likely they are to agree.
- No bulk-send plan.
- No claim that the framework names the pulvinar, V1, a saliency map, or a prediction-error unit as "the" seat of attention. That would be a name where the framework returns a shape.

Three reasons, in the figures' own words:

1. **A room does not upgrade the label.** Fig 7 :35: *"A room's agreement is variation, not a verdict. Selection between rooms is where the ground votes."* Adding agreeable readers raises the vocabulary and does not add an anchor.
2. **Correlation disqualifies a verdict.** Fig 2 :39-40: *"Correlation has valence."* and *"disqualifying for a verdict: agreement about the world is worthless when the agreeing frames share an evidence pool."* Selecting recipients for receptiveness manufactures the disqualifying correlation.
3. **Asking for trust before checking spends independence.** Fig 10 :42: *"A verifier that can reconstruct the claim cannot verify it."* The first contact carries the residual and its refuter, never the source.

Legally: unsolicited bulk email to scraped personal addresses breaches GDPR and CAN-SPAM style rules in many jurisdictions. Public opt-in institutional channels avoid that failure and the structural one together.
