**What this is:** a claim in a form a researcher in genetic code origins can check. Mode, derivation and refuters are stated before the conclusion, so a reader knows what to look for and what would settle it.

**Mode: `specified` on the shape, `general` on the structural grammar, `gap` on the historical route. Not `grounded`.** No measurement at the claimed transition is asserted. This is a set of constraints and a route description, not a result.

**Status:** a simulated derivation checked for internal consistency against the figures, not a ground reading. It cannot settle the question it reports on.

## 1. The claim, in one paragraph

The transition from a heritable polymer to directed peptide synthesis is a union compound, so it must be split before it is searched. A replicating closure cannot reach coded peptide synthesis without contact from a chemistry frame whose closure differs; the amino-acid and peptide chemistry present in the habitat supplied that contact. The first catalyst was a pre-existing activity refined by selection, not a design from scratch. The coupling was a typed bond joining a sequence to an amino acid, not a list of ingredients, and the beginning was a cycle in which peptides fed back on the carrier that encoded them. Once that catalyst settled against the ground it became an atom for translation, over which protein enzymes and then DNA were composed. The framework returns this shape; it does not name which historical route occurred.

## 2. Three checkable sub-claims

### C1: framework-internal, checkable by reading Figures 6, 8 and 9

- **Statement:** the question is a union compound, and a closure cannot generate the missing chemistry by search alone. An import from a differing closure is required.
- **Source, verbatim:** Fig 9 :57-58: "a case in two conditions is not a harder case of either. It stops being a mixture and becomes a specific compound." Fig 6 :23: "No frame escapes its own closure. The bound moves only on contact with a frame whose closure differs." Fig 8 :32: "Closure is idempotent. Composing inside a closed set never leaves it, so no search, however long, adds an atom. This one is definitional: it is what closure means."
- **What to check:** is one condition sufficient, that is, can a single mechanism produce both a heritable mapping and peptide bonds without a second closure contributing an atom? If so, the union reading is wrong.
- **Refuter:** demonstrate a single closure, with no contribution from a chemistry frame whose atoms differ, producing a heritable sequence-to-peptide mapping. That would mean search had added an atom, contradicting closure idempotence.

### C2: chemistry-facing, checkable against the reconstruction literature

- **Statement:** the first peptidyl transferase activity was a refined precursor, not a fully formed apparatus. The modern ribosome is the settled descendant, not the starting point.
- **Source, verbatim:** Fig 6 :32: "An instrument is not built from a description. It is improved from a worse instrument already returning something." Fig 6 :34: "The question is not whether a sensor could exist. It is whether anything, at any resolution, is already returning something that fails to resolve." Fig 12 :104: "A settled molecule becomes an atom for whatever composes over it."
- **What to check:** does the literature show a selectable precursor activity, that is, short RNAs that form peptide bonds, a symmetric PTC core suggesting a proto-ribosome, and an RNA active site with no protein side chain within 18 Å? Does any reconstruction show a fully formed apparatus with no selectable precursor?
- **Refuter:** a ground reconstruction showing the first activity was a complete protein or RNP apparatus with no precursor state. That breaks F3 in `01_figure_grounded_audit.md`.

### C3: framework-internal derivation, with a world-facing counterpart

- **Statement:** the coupling is a typed bond and the beginning is a cycle. Translation is ordered production, not a symmetric mixture.
- **Source, verbatim:** Fig 12 :5: "A set of ingredients is not one. The bond is the rest of the information, and it is the half nothing was recording." Fig 12 :77: "A produces B; B did not exist before" and Fig 12 :35: "A then B; B needs A to have happened". Fig 12 :121: "cycle. The output re-enters as input. The sign of the loop decides whether it closes, and a chain cannot close by construction."
- **What to check:** does the adapter hypothesis and the later identification of tRNA (with tRNA preceding modern synthetases) fit the bond-first shape? Is any lineage known with a high-fidelity code and no peptide feeding back on carrier replication?
- **Refuter:** a heritable mapping with no typed adapter relation, or a code that persists with no feedback. Either breaks C3.

## 3. What is explicitly not claimed

- Not a name for the route. RNA world, pre-RNA world, peptide-RNA world and metabolism-first are candidate names, not outputs of this derivation. Fig 13 :135: "It cannot say which primitive is missing, because naming it would mean already holding it."
- Not that the modern ribosome was the first peptide-producing system. It is the artefact; Fig 13 :129: "the order is not recoverable from the artefact. It is recoverable from the lineage."
- Not that tRNA, aminoacyl-tRNA synthetases or a specific proto-ribosome were the historical actors.
- Not that the habitat was optional. Fig 11 :43: "The habitat is enumerated, never composed."
- Not grounded, and not a theory of everything.
- Not a claim that an origin-of-life review counts as a second witness when it shares the same evidence pool as another review. Fig 2 :40: "agreement about the world is worthless when the agreeing frames share an evidence pool."

## 5. What would settle it

A source-discriminating observation, not more argument. The colour case in *The Hard Problem is About Source* was settled by a spectrometer, not by a better description of green. Here:

1. A reconstruction that fixes which polymer first catalysed a peptide bond **and** shows the feedback closing.
2. A geological or isotopic trace that discriminates among candidate routes (the diamond isotope case in *How Intelligence Actually Works*).
3. A measurement showing the earliest recoverable PTC was protein, not RNA.
4. A heritable mapping with no adapter, or a code with no peptide feedback.
