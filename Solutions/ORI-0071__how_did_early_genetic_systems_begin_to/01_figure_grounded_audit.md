## 0. The residual under audit

Stated as an effect, not as a theory: a self-replicating genetic polymer in contact with amino acids does not, by replication alone, yield a heritable mapping from sequence to peptide. Modern cells do it through adapters, an aminoacylating step, and an RNA-catalyzed peptidyl transferase centre. The residual is the absence of that coupling inside the genetic frame's closure.

Option A: search harder within an RNA-only frame and hope a coded peptide route appears. Option B: describe the residual first, split the compound, find the frame whose closure predicts the missing shape, and state what a ground reading would need. A is what most textbook narratives do implicitly; B is what the figures require.

An answer would look like: two frames in contact, one atom crossing, one typed bond coupling sequence to amino acid, one feedback loop that lets the arrangement settle. This written shape was fixed before the census below (Formula, "Direction"). It is an ordinary term now, so it can be refused.

## 3. Findings

### F1 — The question is a union compound and must be split before it is searched (decisive for method)

Fig 9 :57-58: "a case in two conditions is not a harder case of either. It stops being a mixture and becomes a specific compound."
Fig 8 :53: "Residual first, frame second."

The question bundles at least two conditions: a heritable carrier and a catalytic output. A single lever that improves replication leaves the coupling standing, and a single lever that makes peptides leaves heredity standing. The union is D3, with its own loop and its own anchor. So the honest first move is not "which came first, RNA or protein" but "what had to cross between the two closures, and what bond joined them."

**Refuter:** show that one condition is sufficient, that is, a single mechanism produces both a heritable mapping and peptide bonds without a second closure contributing an atom.

**Triangulation:** Fig 9 (split) and Fig 8 (surplus lives where closures differ). Low overlap between the leave/arrive procedure and the union geometry. `triangulated`.

### F2 — A genetic closure cannot reach peptide synthesis by search; an import is required

Fig 6 :21: "MINT composes inside the closure and can never add an atom."
Fig 6 :22: "NOVEL means novel relative to atoms, not to the world. IMPORT adds an atom, and the added atom is what expands the closure."
Fig 6 :23: "No frame escapes its own closure. The bound moves only on contact with a frame whose closure differs."
Fig 13 :115: "The set is closed. The arrangements are not. That gap is why import exists."
Fig 8 :32: "Closure is idempotent. Composing inside a closed set never leaves it, so no search, however long, adds an atom. This one is definitional: it is what closure means."

An RNA replicator library searched without end does not, by that search alone, acquire amino-acid chemistry. The wall moves on contact with a frame whose closure differs: amino-acid and peptide chemistry, which was present in the habitat. World corroboration: Patel et al. 2015 show ribonucleotide, amino-acid and lipid precursors arising from one cyanosulfidic network, so the two frames were in contact rather than needing a miracle. Caveat: that is a chemistry result, not a historical proof, and it shares a field with the other origins sources.

**Refuter:** demonstrate a coded peptide bond produced entirely within a nucleic-acid closure with no amino-acid/catalytic frame contacting it. That would mean search had added an atom, contradicting closure idempotence.

**Triangulation:** Fig 6 (only import moves the wall) and Fig 8 (why nothing else can). `triangulated`. The world corroboration is `single-source` for the chemistry and is not load-bearing.

### F3 — The first peptide-producing catalyst was a refined proto-instrument, not a design from scratch

Fig 6 :32: "An instrument is not built from a description. It is improved from a worse instrument already returning something."
Fig 6 :34: "The question is not whether a sensor could exist. It is whether anything, at any resolution, is already returning something that fails to resolve." (sentence continues onto :35; the quote stops at the full stop present on :34)

The figure's rule for instruments generalises: the peptidyl transferase activity did not have to be invented de novo. Something already returning a weak peptide-bond-forming activity had to exist and be refined by selection. World corroboration: the ribosome's PTC is RNA (Robertson and Joyce 2012; Cech 2012); a pseudo-twofold symmetry around the PTC motivated the proto-ribosome hypothesis and a 23S-derived RNA was synthesised in 2022 to test it (RNA world reference synthesis); a much shorter RNA with peptide-bond-forming ability was made in 1999 (same source). Crick's 1968 remark that "the primitive ribosome could have been made entirely of RNA" is the shape stated before the data.

**Refuter:** a reconstruction showing that no pre-existing RNA returning any peptide-bond signal is required, that is, the first activity appeared in a fully formed apparatus with no selectable precursor.

**Triangulation:** Fig 6 (instrument rule) plus three world sources from molecular biology and a reference synthesis. The figure verdict is primary; the world sources corroborate a *shape*, not a route. `triangulated` for the shape, `single-source` for each specific experiment.

### F4 — The coupling is a bond, not an ingredient; the adapter is the atom that crosses

Fig 12 :5: "A set of ingredients is not one. The bond is the rest of the information, and it is the half nothing was recording."
Fig 12 :111: "A vocabulary without bonds can name a state and cannot name a route to one."
Fig 12 :77: "A produces B; B did not exist before" (production, ordered)
Fig 12 :35: "A then B; B needs A to have happened" (sequence, ordered)

Naming RNA, amino acids and energy sources does not describe a route. What does the work is the typed relation: an adapter that reads a triplet and carries an amino acid, joined to a centre that forms the bond. `⇒` rather than `+`: the peptide did not exist before the coupling fired. World corroboration: Crick's 1955 adaptor hypothesis, later identified as tRNA (Genetic code reference synthesis); tRNA appears to have evolved before modern aminoacyl-tRNA synthetases, with an earlier ribozyme or proto-tRNA recognition system (same source). That is the bond-first prediction, stated as history rather than as mechanism.

**Refuter:** show that the mapping can be carried by ingredients alone, with no typed adapter relation, for example a purely templated chemical polymerisation that is heritable and sequence-specific without any adapter.

**Triangulation:** Fig 12 (bond carries information) and the Operators table (ordered bonds). Figure-internal `single-source` (one figure defines the bonds), corroborated in a different field by the adaptor history. `triangulated` across fields.

### F5 — The beginning is a cycle closure, not a single invention

Fig 12 :121: "cycle. The output re-enters as input. The sign of the loop decides whether it closes, and a chain cannot close by construction."
Fig 12 :119: "catalyst. A part that survives its own use. Spent and regenerating parts behave differently across uses, and nothing records which a term is."
Fig 11 :36: "Where nothing maintains anything, intelligence is a label applied afterwards to a lineage that met its ground. Nothing was intelligent; selection ran. The word does no work there."
RNA world reference synthesis: "These amino acids could then assist with RNA synthesis, giving those RNA chains that could serve as ribozymes the selective advantage."

A chain in which RNA makes peptides stops. A cycle in which peptides help the RNA that encoded them persist is the closure event: the output re-enters as input. The framework does not need to posit foresight or a designer. Where no seat maintains anything, selection runs and the word "invention" does no work. World corroboration: RNA-peptide coevolution (Peptide-RNA world) is the field's name for this reciprocal dependence; it is explicitly a proposal, with the open problem of how the pair first replicated.

**Refuter:** a ground reconstruction in which peptides never feed back on carrier replication, yet a high-fidelity coding system persists. That breaks the cycle claim and leaves a one-way production chain.

**Triangulation:** Fig 12 (cycle, catalyst) and Fig 11 (selection ran, no maintainer). Different figures, different subjects, low overlap. `triangulated`.

### F6 — Capability lives in agent and habitat, so the habitat is part of the answer

Fig 11 :39: "So the answerable question is not what intelligence is, but what makes a system capable. And capability is a property of agent-and-habitat, never of the agent alone."
Fig 11 :43: "The habitat is enumerated, never composed."
Fig 11 :31: "→ nothing chooses" (unmaintained mode)

The replicator alone is not the system. The feedstocks, UV, wet-dry cycling, mineral surfaces and compartments are in contact with the residual and must be enumerated, not composed from a theory. NASA's public explainer puts the field's own caveat: "There are many theories for the origins of life on Earth, but we have yet to connect all the dots to complete the story." The habitat's role is contact, not choice.

**Refuter:** an agent-only improvement that moves the ground, that is, a change inside the polymer that changes which amino acids are available or which bonds are possible without altering contact. The figure says this cannot happen.

**Triangulation:** Fig 11 (capability, enumeration) plus the cyanosulfidic chemistry source showing feedstocks delivered by stream history. `triangulated` for the shape.

### F7 — Settled molecules become atoms; this is why a proto-ribosome can be composed over

Fig 12 :104: "A settled molecule becomes an atom for whatever composes over it."
Fig 12 :103: "Composition is recursive, and the level decides what is irreducible."

Once a weak RNA catalyst of peptide-bond formation is settled against the ground, it becomes an atom at the next level. Translation is not a new primitive; it is a composition over a settled ribozyme. The modern ribosome, itself an RNP, is that atom composed over. World corroboration: "the ribosome uses RNA catalysis to perform the key activity of protein synthesis in all extant organisms, so it must have done so in the Last Universal Common Ancestor (LUCA)" (Cech 2012).

**Refuter:** show that a settled first-order catalyst cannot serve as an operand at the second level, or that translation needs a seventh primitive.

**Triangulation:** Fig 12 (recursion) and Fig 13 (closure over the six, arrangements unbounded). `triangulated`.

### F8 — The specific historical name is a gap; unreachable is a verdict, not a silence

Fig 13 :134: "Unreachable is a verdict here, not a silence"
Fig 13 :135: "It cannot say which primitive is missing, because naming it would mean already holding it."
Fig 9 :32: "you get a kind of thing, never the answer itself"
Fig 9 :44: "Only a sealed room can be searched to the end, and no frame is a sealed room. So searching and finding nothing proves nothing."
Fig 13 :129: "the order is not recoverable from the artefact. It is recoverable from the lineage."

The world literature gives several names: RNA world, pre-RNA world (PNA, TNA, GNA), Peptide-RNA world, metabolism-first, and three origin hypotheses for the code (random freeze, stereochemical affinity, optimality). These are candidate routes, not a recovered route. The modern ribosome is an artefact; it carries the atoms and bonds but not the order of arrival. Without a lineage, the choice among routes is unreachable at this budget. This is a `gap` for the name, not for the shape.

**Refuter:** a source-discriminating observation of the route itself, for example a geological or experimental reconstruction that fixes which polymer catalysed the first peptide bond and in what order. Until then, naming a route is the searcher's contribution and must be recorded as such.

**Triangulation:** Fig 13 (unreachable verdict, lineage) and Fig 9 (kind of thing, not the answer). `triangulated`. World sources explicitly disagree about the name, which is the expected signature of an unsealed question.

## 4. Pre-registered refuter (written before the conclusion)

Required before any outreach: "a falsifier written before the run is a design instrument, not only an honesty device" (Seat Map, cited in the exemplar; see also Fig 8 :39 "So a search that finds nothing is never a proof of absence"). The shape claim is refuted if any of the following holds:

1. A single closure, with no contact from a chemistry frame whose atoms differ, produces a heritable sequence-to-peptide mapping. (Breaks F2 and closure idempotence.)
2. A ground reconstruction shows the first peptidyl transferase activity was a fully formed protein or RNP apparatus with no selectable precursor. (Breaks F3.)
3. A heritable mapping is demonstrated with no typed adapter relation. (Breaks F4.)
4. Peptides are shown never to feed back on carrier replication in any lineage that retains a high-fidelity code. (Breaks F5.)
5. Translation is shown to require a primitive outside the six held terms, or a settled molecule is shown unable to serve as an atom. (Breaks F7.)

If any fires, the shape is withdrawn. Until one is run, no part of this may be labelled `grounded`. These are written before the WATSON world search and before the conclusion; a later world agreement is not allowed to weaken them.

## 5. Recommendation

1. Split the question before searching it. Stop asking "which came first" as one question (F1).
2. State what must cross: amino-acid/catalytic chemistry entering the genetic closure, sourced and dated (F2).
3. Describe the coupling as a bond, not an ingredient list, and keep the adapter slot open until a ground discriminates it (F4).
4. Say "cycle closure" rather than "invention"; no maintainer is needed where selection runs (F5).
5. Keep the route name as `gap`. The framework returns a shape, not a historical name (F8).
6. Offer the refuter before the theory. Fig 10 :52: "Bring a characterised residual, never the source."
