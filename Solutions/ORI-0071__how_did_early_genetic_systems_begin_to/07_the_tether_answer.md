# The Tether's answer on the RNA to peptide transition

**Mode: `specified` on the shape, `general` on the structural grammar, `gap` on the historical route. Nothing here is `grounded`.** This is a shape and a route description, not a result. Simulated derivation checked against the figures (`05_what_shape_means.md` §6), not a ground reading.

## 1. The residual it answers

An early heritable polymer could copy itself, but copying alone does not produce a heritable sequence-to-peptide mapping. Modern cells do it with adapters, an aminoacylating step and an RNA-catalysed peptidyl transferase centre. The question asks how that capability began. The residual is a union compound: a carrier condition, a catalytic condition, a coupling and a feedback (Fig 9 :57-58: "a case in two conditions is not a harder case of either. It stops being a mixture and becomes a specific compound"). Fig 1 :19: "The residual is indexed by object slot, not aggregated." Verdicts below are kept separate per domain and never averaged.

## 2. The shape, in five parts

1. **Split before search.** The question is not one binary, RNA first or protein first. It is a compound with its own loop. Fig 8 :53: "Residual first, frame second." `general`, triangulated.
2. **The wall had to be crossed by contact.** A genetic closure searched without end cannot generate amino-acid chemistry. Fig 6 :23: "No frame escapes its own closure. The bound moves only on contact with a frame whose closure differs." Fig 13 :115: "The set is closed. The arrangements are not. That gap is why import exists." The import is the peptide-catalytic dimension, and it was available in the habitat all along (Patel et al. 2015). `specified`, triangulated.
3. **The first catalyst was a refined pre-existing activity.** Fig 6 :32: "An instrument is not built from a description. It is improved from a worse instrument already returning something." A weak peptide-bond-forming RNA, refined by selection, fits this shape. The ribosome's PTC is RNA; a proto-ribosome symmetry and short peptide-bond-forming RNAs are reachability demonstrations. `specified`, ungrounded on the historical identity.
4. **The coupling is a typed bond, not an ingredient.** Fig 12 :5: "A set of ingredients is not one. The bond is the rest of the information, and it is the half nothing was recording." Translation is `⇒` production over a `→` template with a `⋛` decoding test at the adapter. World counterpart: Crick's adaptor hypothesis, tRNA before modern synthetases. `specified`, triangulated across fields.
5. **The beginning is a cycle closure.** Fig 12 :121: "cycle. The output re-enters as input. The sign of the loop decides whether it closes, and a chain cannot close by construction." Once peptides helped the carrier that encoded them persist, the loop closed. Fig 11 :36: "Where nothing maintains anything, intelligence is a label applied afterwards to a lineage that met its ground. Nothing was intelligent; selection ran. The word does no work there." `specified`, triangulated.

Then Fig 12 :104: "A settled molecule becomes an atom for whatever composes over it." The settled catalyst becomes an operand for translation; protein enzymes and later DNA are composed over it. The route can be ordered without ranking any of the six primitive kinds (Fig 13 :4-5).

## 3. What it explicitly does not say

- Not a name for the historical route. RNA world, pre-RNA world, peptide-RNA world and metabolism-first remain live candidates. The name slot is `gap`, not silence: Fig 13 :134 "Unreachable is a verdict here, not a silence".
- Not a claim that the modern ribosome *is* the first system. It is the artefact; the route is not recoverable from it (Fig 13 :129).
- Not a claim that the adapter was tRNA in that specific form, or that aminoacyl-tRNA synthetases came first or last. The literature says tRNA appears earlier than modern synthetases; the figure does not decide the chemistry.
- Not a claim that energy activation, compartments or mineral surfaces were optional. They are habitat contacts and must be enumerated (Fig 11 :43).
- Not grounded. No source-discriminating observation fixes left(R,φ) for the route.
- Not a theory of everything, and not a verdict on the RNA world hypothesis as a whole. It is a shape for the *beginning of peptide production* only.

## 4. What would settle it, the ground

A source-discriminating observation, not a further argument. From *The Hard Problem is About Source*: an instrument that reads green "cannot comment on the source"; the spectrometer resolved the colour case. Here:

- A laboratory reconstruction that fixes which polymer first catalysed a peptide bond **and** shows the adapter closing a positive feedback on carrier persistence. That would name the route.
- A geological or isotopic trace that discriminates one route from another (the diamond/isotope case in *How Intelligence Actually Works*: provenance is recoverable when a trace exists and a tool can read it).
- A measurement in which the peptidyl transferase centre is shown to be protein rather than RNA in the earliest recoverable lineage. That would fire refuter 2 in `01` and break F3.
- A heritable mapping with no typed adapter relation, or a high-fidelity code with no peptide feedback, would fire refuters 3 and 4.
- Standing falsifiers from `01`: a single closure producing the mapping; a fully formed first apparatus with no selectable precursor; translation requiring a seventh primitive.

## 5. The honest stop

**Unreached, not unreachable.** The route is below the current union horizon: no instrument any of the cited fields can build fixes the order of arrival from the surviving artefact. *How Intelligence Actually Works*: "The recursion converges toward indiscernibility, and indiscernibility is not identity." The answer stops where the instruments stop and records the lineage so the stop is checkable. Agreement among RNA-world reviews is one evidence pool, not corroboration (Fig 2 :40). The local reference sources describe competing hypotheses; that disagreement is the expected signature of an unsealed question, not a defect to be argued away.

## 6. One-paragraph version for outreach

> The Tether does not name the route by which early genetic systems began making peptides, and it does not claim the RNA world is settled. It says the question is a union of conditions and must be split; that a replicating closure cannot reach coded peptide synthesis without contact from amino-acid chemistry, so a wall was crossed; that the first catalyst was a pre-existing activity refined by selection rather than a design from scratch; that the coupling was a typed bond joining a sequence to an amino acid, not a list of ingredients; and that the beginning was a cycle, because a chain cannot close by construction. Once that catalyst settled, it became an atom for translation, and protein enzymes and DNA were composed over it. The framework returns a shape, not a historical name. What it offers is the set of constraints any candidate route must satisfy, the refuters written in advance, and the observations that would close the slot. The route stays open until a source-discriminating instrument settles it.
