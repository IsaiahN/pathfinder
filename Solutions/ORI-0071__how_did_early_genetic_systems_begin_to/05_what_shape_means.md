## 1. A shape is a constraint you can state before you can name the route

The canonical rule limits the framework to *"what a missing thing would have to look like (a shape, not a name)."* Fig 9 :32: "you get a kind of thing, never the answer itself". Fig 13 :134: "Unreachable is a verdict here, not a silence", and :135: "It cannot say which primitive is missing, because naming it would mean already holding it."

From *The Library of Babel*: "you can cut your search short if the set of books returned is small and descriptive enough that it provides a general shape of the answer that satisfies your question."

For ORI-0071 the shape is the four-part route description in `README.md`: two closures in contact, one atom crossing, one typed bond coupling sequence to amino acid, one feedback loop that lets the arrangement settle. "RNA world", "pre-RNA world", "peptide-RNA world" and "metabolism first" are *names of routes*; they are what the shape is meant to constrain, not what it supplies. A shape here narrows the space of admissible origin stories without selecting one.

## 2. How the shape is obtained

- *Library of Babel*: proximity and recursion. "With each new term gained, you are able to narrow the space by definition and deepen your vocabulary. It's this recursive process that will help you converge on the answer." Applied here: start with the words the question already gives (replication, peptide, code), then add the terms that recur when those words are checked against the literature (ribozyme, adapter, PTC, proto-ribosome, cycle). Each addition narrows the admissible story.
- *Library of Babel* on bundles: "If your problem was a bundle or conflation of several problems masquerading as one? You will need to search all of those problems individually to find their answers and then combine them in a satisfactory way." This is the essay-level source for F1.
- *Decoding Tacit Knowledge*: "When contact isn't possible, the best you can send is a plain description of what you'd expect to see, because anyone can check that against the world." Hence the outreach leads with expected observations, not with the framework.
- Fig 6 :30: "The description travels. Fails by being sharpened into terms only we can read. Cast it in effect terms." Fig 9 :30-31: "your words are borrowed too, so describe the effect rather than the cause."
- *Library of Babel* on stopping: "You will know that you are done because the finish line will also be the starting place, as there is nowhere else to search and nothing else that can describe the answer better." Here the stop is reached when the four-part shape no longer registers a residual against the figures, not when a favourite hypothesis wins.

## 3. What licenses a name

Two things, both outside the shape itself:

1. **The ground settles it.** Formula step 5: "the ground settles it. Verified only by what pays. A gate is not the ground." A reconstruction that fixes which polymer catalysed the first peptide bond, and in what order, would name the route. Until then the route is a `gap`.
2. **A provenanced import from a frame whose closure differs** (Fig 6 :23, Fig 8 :51), with a source gate and a shadow test. Naming "the RNA world" as *the* answer is an import from molecular biology into the origin question; it must be dated and marked as such.

*They're Scanning Books* sharpens this: "A minimum viable set of six terms is a human's careful guess at reality's compression axes; another sensorium's minimum set need not be those six." And: "Declaring the primitives does not make them neutral. It makes the choice visible, which is worth a great deal, because a visible choice can be revised and an emergent one cannot." So even the six-term substrate is a visible guess. Turning it into "the" machinery of the first cells would be the invisible-choice error.

## 4. The source problem is the live one for origins

*The Hard Problem is About Source* gives the mechanism. An instrument reads green and cannot say whether the source was one green light, yellow plus blue, RGB, CMYK, or an undiscovered scheme. The essay's sentence: "If several paths create the same behavior, then behavior is a projection with lossy information (it cannot define consciousness in full)." And: "An instrument that can't distinguish these cases demonstrates its own inadequacy, it cannot claim anything substantial about what is being measured or what causes it." The spectrometer, not an argument, resolved the colour case.

The modern ribosome is the equivalent reading. It is one artefact with many possible historical sources (RNA-first, peptide-RNA coevolution, a pre-RNA precursor). *How Intelligence Actually Works* states the problem directly: "often, very little about a composed term tells you, from looking at it alone, which route produced it." The essay's own wild-card example is exactly this genre: "we cannot say which state composed first, and we don't know if the order mattered." So the ribosome cannot by itself name the route. What is owed is a source-discriminating observation, not a better narrative.

## 5. The shape here is a cycle, and why that is not a name

*How a Society of Agents Would Work* quotes Fig 11: "capability is a property of agent-and-habitat, never of the agent alone." The prebiotic carrier is not capable alone; the amino-acid stream, the mineral surface and the wet-dry cycle are part of the system. *They're Scanning Books* adds the instrument rule: "The real import is not a new term in the existing space. It is a new dimension of the space." In this question the import is the peptide-catalytic dimension entering the genetic closure, not a new base or a new amino acid.

The cycle claim of F5 is a shape because it constrains without naming. Fig 12 :121: "cycle. The output re-enters as input. The sign of the loop decides whether it closes, and a chain cannot close by construction." It says a feedback must exist; it does not say whether the feedback ran through aaRS, a ribozyme, a peptide cofactor or a surface metabolism. That slot stays open.

## 5b. Correction to my own §3

I initially wrote that "shape, not a name" forbids naming any component of the route. It does not. The canonical phrase governs a **missing primitive**, one not held in the frame's library. The amino-acid frame and the genetic frame are both already held in the world literature. What the framework refuses is the claim that it can recover the **order of arrival** from the artefact. Fig 13 :129: "the order is not recoverable from the artefact. It is recoverable from the lineage." My corrected reading: naming a *candidate* route is allowed if provenanced; naming it *the* route is refused until a ground discriminates. Both the earlier and corrected statements are kept here so the correction is visible.

## 6. Two consequences for outreach and for this derivation

**Prestige is not action.** *The Recursive Self-Improvement Trap*: "Prestige can fund an action. It can give an action a budget to be tried. But it cannot settle an action. Only the ground can settle it." A list of famous origin-of-life researchers who agree with a route is the named collapse. The legitimate ask is for a refutation of the shape, not endorsement of a name.

**This audit is a simulation, not a ground reading.** Same essay: "LLMs can quickly simulate an approximate answer/output with the formula found in the architecture post", but "the truly testable output would need to be derived from the real architecture or working the formula yourself." The architecture article adds that such outputs "could never be verified if the solutions presented were not contrived to fit the formula." So `01_figure_grounded_audit.md` is a simulated derivation checked for internal consistency against the figures. It is **not** a test of the origin question and must be labelled as such. It cannot settle anything it reports on.
