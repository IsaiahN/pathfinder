## Wider literature that bears on the claim

1. **The ribosome as the anchor.** Robertson and Joyce and Cech independently report the same structural fact: the peptidyl transferase centre is RNA, with no protein side chain near the active site. Two papers, but they cite the same structural lineage (Ban, Wimberly, Yusupov, Nissen), so this is **one evidence pool stated twice**, not two witnesses. It corroborates Fig 6 :32 (a pre-existing catalytic activity, refined) and Fig 12 :104 (a settled catalyst becomes an atom at the next level).
2. **The habitat was not empty.** Patel et al. show a common chemistry delivering nucleotide, amino-acid and lipid precursors. This supports Fig 11 :43 ("The habitat is enumerated, never composed"): the two closures were in contact before either was a biological system. It says nothing about which closure crossed first, so it does not settle F8.
3. **A proto-instrument is physically plausible.** The RNA world synthesis reports a twofold-symmetric PTC region, a 23S-derived RNA that dimerised and formed peptide bonds (2022), and a much shorter 1999 RNA able to form peptide bonds. These are reachability demonstrations. They corroborate the *shape* of F3, not the historical route.
4. **The coupling has a documented precursor.** The genetic code synthesis reports Crick's adaptor hypothesis, tRNA, and synthetases appearing after tRNA, with earlier ribozyme or proto-tRNA recognition proposed. This matches F4 (bond first, ingredients second) without naming a historical mechanism.
5. **The route name is still open.** The two reference syntheses list competing hypotheses, and NASA's own public answer says the dots are not connected. This is what F8 predicts for an unsealed question. No conflict with the figure verdict was triggered.

**Overlap declaration.** Sources 1 to 6 draw substantially on the RNA-world evidence pool; their agreement is expected and carries little independent weight. The chemistry source (Patel et al.) is a different pool, but it is the same field of origins research. The journal and society pages are institutional, not evidentiary. Fig 2 :40: "agreement about the world is worthless when the agreeing frames share an evidence pool." We do not pool them as corroboration.
