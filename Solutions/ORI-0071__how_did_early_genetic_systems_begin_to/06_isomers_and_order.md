# Bonds, order and analogues in the RNA to peptide transition

**Mode: `specified`.** This is a simulated derivation checked against the figures, not a ground reading (see `05_what_shape_means.md` §6).

The figure census in `01` says the coupling is a bond and the beginning is a cycle. Three supporting questions are owed: (1) which of the seven bonds describes translation, (2) what order is licensed among replication, catalysis, coupling and enzymes, and (3) which analogues from chemistry are legitimate, and which are strained. Each comes with a falsifier.

## DERIVATION

### Q1 — Which bond is translation?

- Fig 12 :55-58: `+` is conjunction, symmetric, "both hold at once". RNA and amino acids both being present in a pocket is `+`. That is not translation.
- Fig 12 :35: `→` is sequence, "A then B; B needs A to have happened". The order of nucleotide addition and the order of amino-acid addition are both `→`.
- Fig 12 :77: `⇒` is production, "A produces B; B did not exist before". A peptide appears that was not there before the coupling fired. Translation is `⇒`, not `+`.
- Fig 12 :87: `⋛` is comparison, "a threshold relation between two quantities". The genetic code can be read as a comparison (which amino acid fits this triplet), but the *product* is `⇒`. Confusing the two would be a type error, as the exemplar found for ⋛ at second order.
- Fig 12 :111: "A vocabulary without bonds can name a state and cannot name a route to one." Listing RNA, amino acids, ATP and a surface names ingredients, not the transition.

**Conclusion Q1:** translation is an ordered `⇒` composition over a `→` template, with `⋛` as the decoding test at the adapter, not a symmetric mixture. **Falsifier:** a heritable sequence-to-peptide mapping that is genuinely symmetric in its operands (swap template and amino acid, the mapping survives). That would make `+` sufficient and break the ordered-bond reading.

### Q2 — What order is licensed?

- Fig 3 :20: "The chain is ordered by dependency, not by importance. Each link consumes what the one above produces, so effort spent above the break arrives at a step that cannot use it."
- Fig 12 :106: "Nothing composes over what was never admitted as a part."
- Fig 12 :104: "A settled molecule becomes an atom for whatever composes over it."

So the licensed order is: (1) a replicating carrier exists; (2) a catalytic activity is settled against the ground; (3) the coupling bond forms, so the activity is composable over the carrier; (4) protein enzymes can appear as atoms composed over the settled translation system; (5) DNA can later take over information storage. This is a dependency order, not a claim about geological chronology: two adjacent links may have been simultaneous in the habitat, and the figure permits that because the kinds are in lockstep. World source: Cech 2012 states in the Figure 1 caption that proteins large enough to self-fold came about only after RNA was available to catalyse peptide ligation or amino acid polymerization, and that DNA took over the genome more recently. That is the same order, independently reached in molecular biology.

**Falsifier:** a demonstrated protein enzyme preceding any heritable carrier in a lineage that later acquires a genetic code. That would invert the dependency order and break Q2.

### Q3 — Which analogues are legitimate?

- **Catalyst.** Fig 12 :119: "catalyst. A part that survives its own use. Spent and regenerating parts behave differently across uses, and nothing records which a term is." A ribozyme or a ribosome survives its use; the modern PTC is RNA. Legitimate.
- **Functional group.** Fig 12 :123: "functional group. A substructure whose presence fixes the outcome. The group says what kind; the qualifier says which member." The adapter (tRNA-like molecule) is the functional group that fixes what kind of polymerisation happens; which amino acid is attached is the qualifier. Legitimate, and it predicts the adapter history (Crick's adaptor hypothesis, tRNA before modern synthetases) without claiming a date.
- **Cycle.** Fig 12 :121: "cycle. The output re-enters as input. The sign of the loop decides whether it closes, and a chain cannot close by construction." Legitimate as a shape for RNA-peptide coevolution. The sign matters: a peptide that destroys its carrier is a negative loop and does not close a persistent system.
- **Isomer.** Fig 12 :117: "Same atoms, different bond, different substance. The difference is invisible in the formula and decisive in the behaviour." Are RNA and peptide polymers isomers? No: they do not share the same monomer atoms, so the definition is not met. The legitimate isomer claim is weaker and structural: two *arrangements of the same four components* (carrier, adapter, catalyst, compartment) with the catalyst placed in different slots can be functionally identical while being different substances. That is a checkable claim about reconstructions, not about the historical route. **Falsifier:** two reconstructions with the same components and different bond placement that behave identically in every measurable respect. The isomer claim would then collapse into the flat formula the figure warns against.
- **Strained analogue: "chicken and egg".** The literature calls the carrier/catalyst mutual dependence a chicken-and-egg paradox. Fig 12 :121 says a chain cannot close by construction, so a cycle is required; but the figure does not license treating the paradox as a single object with two ends. It is two closures whose surplus is the coupling. Recorded as a strain, not smoothed: the phrase is a useful flag, not a derivation.

### Q4 — The round trip gap R_T

1. **Sent up (x):** the four-part shape (two closures, one crossing, one bond, one feedback) from Fig 6 :21-23, Fig 12 :5 and :121.
2. **Abstraction (T_A):** "a carrier must acquire a catalytic output through a bond and a feedback" in a single sentence.
3. **Concretisation (T_E):** a concrete route, for example "an RNA replicase ribozyme recruited phenylalanine as a cofactor, a proto-tRNA adapter formed, the PTC closed the loop".

**R_T is nonzero.** The abstraction drops: which atom crosses (amino acid versus metal ion versus cofactor), which bond is the adapter (production versus comparison), and which feedback sign closes the loop. Conversely, "an ordered cross-kind composition is required" survives the trip. R_T is recorded qualitatively as the set of dropped constraints, not measured numerically, because no source-discriminating instrument exists. Fig 4 :30: "So take the round trip and measure the gap. Without it you are navigating by dead reckoning: confident about a position nobody checked." Treating a concrete route as identical to the shape would set R_T to zero and be the dead-reckoning error.

## CONCLUSION

Translation is an ordered production bond over a sequence bond, with a comparison at the decoding step and a catalyst that survives its own use. The dependency order is carrier, settled catalyst, coupling, enzymes, DNA, with adjacency permitted but inversion refused. The legitimate analogues are catalyst, functional group and cycle; the isomer analogue applies to component arrangements, not to RNA versus peptide monomers. The chicken-and-egg phrase is a strain flag, not a derivation. R_T is nonzero and unmeasured.
