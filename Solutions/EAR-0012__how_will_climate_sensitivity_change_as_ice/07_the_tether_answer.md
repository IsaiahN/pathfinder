# The Tether's answer on EAR-0012

**Mode: `specified` - not `grounded`.** This is a shape and a route, not a result. Simulated derivation checked against the figures (`05_what_shape_means.md` section 7), not a ground reading.

## 1. The residual it answers

The question asks how climate sensitivity will change as ice, clouds, forests and oceans respond to warming. Restated in effect terms: the modelling frame predicts a top-of-atmosphere energy imbalance and a temperature trajectory; the world returns measured flux, ocean heat content, ice extent and carbon uptake; R = |Γ(b,a) − o′| is indexed per feedback slot and per state, never averaged into one global number (Fig 1 :19, :22). The question is a **union compound**, not one hard scalar: the physical response loop and the biogeochemical response loop overlap (Fig 9 :54-57).

## 2. The shape

1. **Climate sensitivity is a comparison, not an entity.** It is a forcing-to-response ratio, the ⋛ bond, *"a threshold relation between two quantities"* (Fig 12 :87). "It changes" means the operands of the comparison change: forcing, spatial pattern, time scale, response. IPCC AR6 WG1 Ch7 names the measured version: *"an 'effective feedback parameter' that may be different from the equilibrium value of the feedback parameter governing ECS."*
2. **It must be reported per slot.** Fig 1 :19: *"The residual is indexed by object slot, not aggregated."* A global mean can be near zero while one slot carries a live residual (Fig 1 :13). The world agrees at low overlap: IPCC Ch7 reports *"the cloud feedback remains the largest contribution to overall uncertainty"* and *"Radiative feedbacks, particularly from clouds, are expected to become less negative (more amplifying) on multi-decadal time scales as the spatial pattern of surface warming evolves."*
3. **A feedback is a cycle whose sign decides closure.** Fig 12 :121: *"cycle. The output re-enters as input. The sign of the loop decides whether it closes, and a chain cannot close by construction."* Ice albedo, low cloud, forest recycling and ocean carbon uptake are four instances of the same structure with different members and different signs. Fig 13 :31-32 adds the amplifier: *"a remainder compounding through a chain that amplifies is chaos."*
4. **The slots couple by cascades that reach three steps out.** Fig 11 :52: *"Scoping the habitat to direct contact is how DDT reached the eagles: first-order enumeration, second-order effect, and the residual that mattered was three steps out."* Ice loss changes circulation, circulation changes rainfall, rainfall changes forest stress and carbon uptake, and carbon uptake changes the forcing being compared. Enumeration, composition: *"The habitat is enumerated, never composed."* (Fig 11 :43).
5. **The physical and carbon loops stay separate.** C1 (a maintained physical response) and C2 (a maintained biogeochemical response) have different anchors and different instruments; a case in two conditions is not a harder case of either (Fig 9 :57). IPCC AR6 WG1 Ch5: *"Oceanic and terrestrial carbon sinks are projected to continue to grow with increasing atmospheric concentrations of CO2, but the fraction of emissions taken up by land and ocean is expected to decline as the CO2 concentration increases (high confidence)."*
6. **The anchor is the measured energy budget, not agreement.** Fig 2 :29 names *"A measurement, an experiment, a counter, a market, a patient outcome."* Here: CERES fluxes, Argo ocean heat content, surface temperature, ice extent, atmospheric CO2 growth. IPCC Ch7 notes that some high-sensitivity CMIP6 models are less consistent with observations and paleo proxies than models inside the very likely range. Agreement among model families is efficiently produced because they share code and tuning targets (Fig 2 :40), so it is a filter for the budget, not a witness for the verdict (Fig 9 :60).
7. **Thresholds are comparisons on control parameters, and they may include history.** Fig 12 :87; Lenton et al. 2008: *"The term 'tipping point' commonly refers to a critical threshold at which a tiny perturbation can qualitatively alter the state or development of a system."* The EGUsphere sea-ice summary read this session: *"The same global temperature can therefore correspond to different sea-ice states, showing that climate history matters for recovery."*
8. **The numeric trajectory is a `gap` relative to the figures.** The figures return a shape, not a value. Fig 9 :32: *"you get a kind of thing, never the answer itself."* Fig 13 :134: *"Unreachable is a verdict here, not a silence."* Climate science is measuring the values; that is an import from the measurement frame with a source gate and shadow test (Fig 6 :23, Fig 8 :50-52), not a derivation from the six-term set.

## 3. What it explicitly does not say

- Not a new ECS value, and not a forecast of when a named threshold is crossed.
- Not that any of the four systems is certainly a tipping element. Lenton et al.: *"Many of the systems we consider do not yet have convincingly established tipping points."*
- Not "the net cloud feedback is uncertain in sign." IPCC Ch7 assesses it as positive with high confidence; the gap is in the magnitude of its future change.
- Not a claim that model ensembles are useless. They are instruments and budget filters; the anchor lies outside them.
- Not `grounded`. No new measurement was taken, and the shape has not been settled by a checker outside this evidence pool.

## 4. What would settle it - the ground

A source-discriminating instrument, not a further argument. From *The Hard Problem is About Source*: an instrument that reads green *"cannot comment on the source"*; the spectrometer resolved the case. Here the corresponding questions are:

- Does a per-slot, state-resolving observation discriminate a genuine state-dependence from an aggregation artifact? The live candidate is the cloud slot at a stated warming pattern (Fig 1 :13, :22).
- **Sign Test:** for each named slot, does the measured feedback output re-enter with the sign the cycle reading predicts, at a stated state and time scale?
- **Cascade Test:** after enumerating a three-step habitat, does the second- or third-order contribution to the residual remain detectable? (Fig 11 :52).
- **Threshold Test:** for each candidate tipping element, is there a critical control value, and does the same value give the same state on approach and recovery? (Fig 12 :87; the hysteresis summary).
- **Antigen Test (the falsifier already dead in the exemplar sense):** a global mean that predicts per-slot movement would be an averaged statistic doing what only a per-slot instrument can do. If one is demonstrated prospectively, F1 is false.

## 5. The honest stop

**Unreached, not unreachable.** The slot bindings are *"a human's careful guess at reality's compression axes"* made visible, not nature's own list (*They're Scanning Books*). The answer stops at the limit of the available per-slot instruments and records the lineage so the stop is checkable. *"Indiscernibility is not identity"* (*How Intelligence Actually Works*): a model that matches the global imbalance is not thereby the source of it. Fig 8 :39: *"So a search that finds nothing is never a proof of absence."*

## 6. One-paragraph version for outreach

> The Tether does not predict an ECS number, and it does not claim a new tipping threshold. It says that climate sensitivity is a comparison between forcing and response, so when it changes, the operands are changing with state, pattern and time scale. It must be reported per feedback slot, because a global average hides the live residual, and the slot that is live today is the cloud slot. Each feedback is a cycle whose sign decides whether it amplifies or damps, and the four systems you name couple through second- and third-order cascades, so the physical loop and the carbon loop must be kept separate and never averaged. The measured energy budget settles the sign; model agreement is a budget filter, not a witness. The numeric future trajectory is a gap the figures honestly report, to be filled by instruments with provenance, not by the method. What it offers is the shape any answer must have, four falsifiers written in advance, and the instruments that would close it.
