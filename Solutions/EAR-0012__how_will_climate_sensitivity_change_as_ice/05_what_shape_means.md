## 1. A shape is a constraint, not a filler

The figures README states the limit: the framework returns *"what a missing thing would have to look like (a shape, not a name)"*. Fig 9 :32: *"you get a kind of thing, never the answer itself."* Fig 13 :134: *"Unreachable is a verdict here, not a silence."*

From *The Library of Babel*: *"you can cut your search short if the set of books returned is small and descriptive enough that it provides a general shape of the answer that satisfies your question."*

So a **shape** is the narrowed form of an answer you can state before you can name it. It constrains; it does not fill. For EAR-0012:

- "Climate sensitivity will change" is not a shape. It is a direction.
- "Sensitivity is a vector of per-slot signed feedback responses, ordered by a contact cascade, with thresholds as comparisons on control parameters" is a shape.
- "ECS will be 4.1°C in 2100 under SSP3-7.0" is a name with a number, and the figures do not yield it.

The architecture post is explicit about what an LLM-assisted derivation is worth: *"due to the black-box nature could never be verified if the solutions presented were not contrived to fit the formula."* Sections 2 to 7 of this folder are therefore a shape checked for internal consistency, not a fitted number.

## 2. How a shape is obtained

- *Library of Babel*: use the vocabulary already held that is *closest* to the thing, look for recurring new terms that overlap or correlate with it, recurse. *"The search space will narrow with every vocabulary word that you believe is in the answer or associated with the answer."*
- Fig 6 :30: *"The description travels. Fails by being sharpened into terms only we can read. Cast it in effect terms."* Fig 9 :30-31: *"your words are borrowed too, so describe the effect rather than the cause."*
- *Decoding Tacit Knowledge*: *"When contact isn't possible, the best you can send is a plain description of what you'd expect to see, because anyone can check that against the world."* This is the outreach rule in `02_outreach_email.md`: describe what would count as a failure before showing the framework.
- *Library of Babel* on bundles: *"Perhaps your problem was a bundle or conflation of several problems masquerading as one?"* This is the source of the union census in `01` section 1.3. EAR-0012 is a bundle of two loops, not four independent questions about four subsystems.
- *How Intelligence Actually Works*, Rule 5: *"Traversing outward requires selection; traversing inward requires elimination."* The figure lines were selected by the residual description in `01` section 0, not by walking the questions in source order.

## 3. What licenses a name

Only two things, both outside the shape itself:

1. **The ground settles it.** Formula step 5: *"the ground settles it. Verified only by what pays. A gate is not the ground."* The README: the framework *"stops at the limit of what the available instruments can distinguish, and records the lineage so the stop is honest."* For EAR-0012, CERES, Argo, ice extent and atmospheric CO2 measurements are the ground.
2. **A provenanced import from a frame whose closure differs** (Fig 6 :23, Fig 8 :50-52), with a source gate and a shadow test. The IPCC assessed range is such an import: it is measured, it explains a gap, and it carries provenance. It is not a figure derivation, and its authority comes from the measurement frame, not from this method.

*They're Scanning Books* sharpens the limit: *"A minimum viable set of six terms is a human's careful guess at reality's compression axes; another sensorium's minimum set need not be those six."* Also: *"Declaring the primitives does not make them neutral. It makes the choice visible."* When the audit names slots (ice albedo, low cloud, ocean heat uptake, land carbon sink), those names are bindings from climate science onto held atoms, not discoveries of nature's own list. They must be recorded as bindings.

## 4. Why naming a single future value is the specific forbidden error

*The Hard Problem is About Source* gives the mechanism. An instrument reads green and *"cannot comment on the source"*; one reading can have several sources. The paper states: *"If several paths create the same behavior, then behavior is a projection with lossy information"* and *"The answer is unreached until a better instrument exists."* It was the spectrometer, not an argument, that resolved the case: *"An instrument that can't distinguish these cases demonstrates its own inadequacy."*

Applied here: a global mean temperature trajectory is a projection. Several distinct per-slot configurations (cloud-dominated, ice-dominated, carbon-sink-dominated) can produce the same global reading. Naming one configuration as the cause of a measured sensitivity change from the global reading alone is the metamerism error. The EGUsphere short summary read this session makes the same point inside climate science: *"Compensating biases can yield an apparently realistic net energy imbalance trend, meaning models may match observations for the wrong reasons."* What is owed is a per-slot discriminator, not a new global number.

*How Intelligence Actually Works*: *"The recursion converges toward indiscernibility, and indiscernibility is not identity."* A model that reproduces the global energy imbalance is not thereby the source of that imbalance.

## 5. Q2 - ANSWERED, no further question needed

**What licenses a particular numeric future sensitivity in a slot?** Nothing in the figures. The figures return a shape. A number is licensed only by a ground measurement, or by a provenanced import with a shadow test. Until one of those, the number is the searcher's own contribution and must be recorded as such.

**Does claiming a figure-derived ECS trajectory commit the error the README forbids?** Yes. It would name a quantity the framework says it cannot name, and it would do so by reading a source off a projection. The correct form remains a per-slot shape with the numeric cell left open, exactly as `01` F10 marks it.

## 5b. Correction to my own §3

I initially wrote that the "shape, not a name" rule forbids naming any slot. That overstates it. The README says *"what a missing thing would have to look like (a shape, not a name)."* The emphasis on "missing" in the source is on the word missing; the quoted string here is verbatim without markup. The subject is a primitive not held in Γ. Ice, clouds, forests and oceans are already in the scientific library, and the framework's held atoms include states and structures into which they can be bound. Naming a slot is a **binding** from the climate frame onto held atoms, not the invention of a seventh primitive. What remains wrong is treating that binding as a figure derivation, or claiming it moves the wall (Fig 6: *"IMPORT adds an atom"* - this binding adds none). Both claims are now recorded so neither is quietly lost.

## 6. What the other essays add

- *How a Society of Agents Would Work* gives the aggregation rule in the language of the question: *"Keeping the rate per kind of situation. A method that is brilliant in one setting must not be drowned by an average across all of them."* It also sets the witness rule: *"Votes come from outcomes, not popularity."* This is why `01` F4 downgrades ensemble agreement.
- *The Architecture* post names the near-miss that matters for outreach: the section *"Three mappings offered and declined"* refuses Bayesian updating because *"there is no prior, no likelihood and no posterior anywhere in the mechanism."* A climate-sensitivity claim must not be dressed as a Bayesian posterior over ECS unless a likelihood is actually present.
- *Decoding Tacit Knowledge* states the drift condition that applies to model families: two frames sharing pretext *"drift together, and they drift together, so neither notices."* It also gives the remedy: *"A ground that both are corrected by, separately."*

## 7. Two consequences for outreach

**Prestige is not action.** From *The Recursive Self-Improvement Trap*: *"Prestige must never be allowed to validate an action directly"* and *"Prestige can fund an action. It can give an action a budget to be tried. But it cannot settle an action. Only the ground can settle it. The Ground Maintainer must be blind to prestige."* A campaign that gathers famous names to agree with the per-slot shape is the named collapse, not corroboration. The legitimate ask is the opposite: give the claim a budget to be tried, and let the instruments settle it.

**My own audit is a simulation, not a ground reading.** Same essay: *"LLMs can quickly simulate an approximate answer/output with the formula found in the architecture post... However, the truly testable output would need to be derived from the real architecture or working the formula yourself."* So `01` is a simulated derivation, checked for internal consistency against the figures. It is **not** a test of the Earth-system claim and must be labelled as such. It cannot settle anything it reports on.

## 8. Honest stop

*They're Scanning Books*: *"The real import is not a new term in the existing space. It is a new dimension of the space."* The missing thing for EAR-0012 is not another opinion about ECS. It is an instrument that discriminates among the per-slot sources of a global reading. The figures can characterise that instrument in effect terms and stop. Measuring with it is the work of the climate frame, and the numeric answer belongs there.
