## QUESTION

How will climate sensitivity change as ice, clouds, forests and oceans respond to warming?

Restated as an effect: why does R = |Γ(b,a) − o′| change per feedback slot as the slots move into states the modelling frame did not hold before? R is indexed per slot and per state, never aggregated (Fig 1 :19). Anchor: the measured top-of-atmosphere energy imbalance, ocean heat content and temperature record, named as an anchor in Fig 2 :29.

- Option A: sensitivity is a scalar constant acquiring a new value. Leads to one number, a hidden live slot, and ensemble agreement treated as evidence.
- Option B: sensitivity is a per-slot state-dependent comparison whose change is the movement of signed feedback residuals as loops close or amplify. Leads to a vector of feedback strengths, a cascade order, thresholds as comparisons, and an open numeric cell filled only by instruments.
- This run records Option B in `specified` mode. The numeric trajectory is a `gap` relative to the figures.

Carries an ordered bond (Operators table :31, "A then B; B needs A to have happened") and a comparison bond (Fig 12 :87, "a threshold relation between two quantities"). Symmetry: "amplifying" versus "damping" is a threshold relative to the base Planck response, not a symmetric join. Scale: instantaneous radiative step, multi-decadal pattern step, centennial carbon step, millennial ice-sheet step; the membrane between them is lossy (Fig 4).

## VERDICT (from the figures)

The figure-derived verdict: climate sensitivity changes as a set of per-slot, state-dependent comparisons, not as a constant acquiring a new value. A slot changes when its feedback loop's sign closes or amplifies, when the warming pattern moves the ground under it, and when a second- or third-order contact cascade changes the quantity being compared. The physical and carbon-cycle loops are a union kept separate, never averaged. The measured energy budget settles the sign; ensemble agreement is a filter. The numeric future trajectory is a declared `gap` relative to the figures, to be bound by instruments with provenance. Mode `specified`. World sources corroborate the shape at different overlaps; none was allowed to flip the verdict.

- **cost if wrong:** misdirected diagnosis and wasted measurement. A C2 failure (weakening carbon sink) would be sold cloud-resolution instruments, or a C1 failure would be treated as a carbon-cycle problem, and a single lever would be applied to a union that needs two. In outreach terms, an expert's twenty minutes would be spent refuting a shape that was never the right shape. The cost is real, bounded and largely reversible. Fig 2 :42 prices the right response: "Buy independence in proportion to the cost of being wrong."
- **undoable:** yes. No term is deleted if the claim fails; a refutation is added and the reach falls because Fig 6 :42 "something was added rather than because anything was deleted". The shape and its seven falsifiers are recorded in a provenance trail so a later reader can locate the error (Fig 10 :62-64).
- **reversed by:** a ground reading, not agreement. A per-slot instrument that shows a state-invariant forcing-response relation, a loop whose sign fails to close or amplify at a stated state, a sensitivity change explained entirely by first-order effects after a three-step enumeration, or an ensemble agreement settled by independent evidence and a measurement neither model can move, would each reverse the corresponding finding. A figure re-census that derives the numeric trajectory from the six-term set would clear the `gap`. Until one of these fires, the verdict stands as `candidate`.
