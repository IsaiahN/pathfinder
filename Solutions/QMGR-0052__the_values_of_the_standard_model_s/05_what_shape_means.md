## 1. A shape is a constraint, not a filler

The canonical limit: the framework returns *"what a missing thing would have to look like (a shape, not a name)"*. Fig 9 :32: *"you get a kind of thing, never the answer itself."* Fig 13 :133: *"what it returns is a shape rather than a name."*

From *The Library of Babel*: *"you can cut your search short if the set of books returned is small and descriptive enough that it provides a general shape of the answer that satisfies your question."*

For QMGR-0052 a **shape** is therefore this: the missing frame must be a closure in which the parameter values are derived terms rather than inserted atoms, and it must contact the same experimental ground. That constrains any candidate answer. It does not supply one. "Some theory beyond the Standard Model explains the parameters" is a shape; "the string landscape with axion-like moduli fixes the QCD angle" is a name, and no figure licenses it here.

## 2. How a shape is obtained

- *Library of Babel*: use the vocabulary you already have that is closest to the thing, then look for recurring terms that overlap or correlate with it; *"With each new term gained, you narrow the space by definition and deepen your vocabulary."*
- Fig 6 :30: *"outward: does another frame already hold it? The description travels. Fails by being sharpened into terms only we can read. Cast it in effect terms."* Fig 9 :30-31: *"your words are borrowed too, so describe the effect rather than the cause."*
- *Decoding Tacit Knowledge*: *"When contact isn't possible, the best you can send is a plain description of what you'd expect to see, because anyone can check that against the world."*
- The shape is narrowed by stating the residual per slot, not by averaging the slots (Fig 1 :19), and by describing the residual before choosing a frame (Fig 9 :27-28).

Applied here: describe the effect first (numbers enter the theory from measurement; no internal derivation exists; the count is a convention), then look for frames. Do not begin with favourite BSM theories and map backwards. That inversion is the apophenia failure the framework names.

## 3. What licenses a name

Only two things, both outside the shape itself:

1. **The ground settles it.** Formula step 5: *"the ground settles it. Verified only by what pays. A gate is not the ground."* For this question, a prediction of a parameter value or a relation among values, stated before measurement and then confirmed.
2. **A provenanced import from a frame whose closure differs** (Fig 6 :23, Fig 8 :51-52), with a source gate and a shadow test, and debited against that frame's independence (Fig 6 :24, *"a wrong atom costs more than starting from primitives"*).

*They're Scanning Books* sharpens the second: *"The real import is not a new term in the existing space. It is a new dimension of the space."* And: *"A minimum viable set of six terms is a human's careful guess at reality's compression axes; another sensorium's minimum set need not be those six."* Also: *"Declaring the primitives does not make them neutral. It makes the choice visible."*

World sources make the same demand in their own vocabulary. Donoghue 2007: *"attempts to do so with symmetries or a dynamical mechanism have been unsuccessful"*, and even the landscape does not license a name because *"Without a unique ground state, we cannot use the prediction of the parameters as a proof of string theory."* Cahn 1996 states the aspiration, not its fulfilment: *"Until we can explain the origin of these parameters, we cannot say we truly understand why our everyday world is as it is."*

## 4. The ground and the source problem

*The Hard Problem is About Source* gives the mechanism. Metamerism: an instrument reads green and *"cannot comment on the source"*; one green light, or yellow plus blue, or RGB, or CMYK, or an unknown scheme can give the same reading. *"If several paths create the same behavior, then behavior is a projection with lossy information."* And: *"An instrument that can't distinguish these cases demonstrates its own inadequacy."* It was the spectrometer, not an argument, that resolved it: *"The answer is unreached until a better instrument exists."*

Here the corresponding error would be to read the measured value (the projection) and name its source (the mechanism that produced it) without an instrument that discriminates among candidate sources. The global fit returns a number with an uncertainty. It does not tell us whether the number comes from a GUT relation, a flavour symmetry, an environmental selection effect, or bare contingency. The figures' answer is to keep the slot open and record the lineage. Fig 9 :32 again: *"you get a kind of thing, never the answer itself."*

## 5. Answer to the shape question

**What licenses a particular origin for the parameters?** Nothing in the framework. The framework returns a shape. A particular origin is licensed only by a ground settlement (a prediction confirmed) or by a provenanced import with a shadow test. Until one of those, the name is the searcher's own contribution and must be recorded as such.

**Does the question have the shape of a gap?** Yes, and that is a verdict. Fig 13 :134: *"Unreachable is a verdict here, not a silence"*; Fig 8 :39: *"So a search that finds nothing is never a proof of absence."* The per-slot residual is real; the framework can characterize the missing frame in effect terms; it cannot say which primitive that frame holds. The honest output is a shape plus a refuter plus a recorded stop.

**A correction I made during this run.** I first read "shape, not a name" as forbidding all naming. The canonical phrase is about what a **missing** thing would look like. Gradient and Spectrum are atoms already held in the framework's six-term set, so naming them is a binding from another frame, not the invention of a new primitive. For this question the correction does not change the result, because the origin frame is genuinely missing; but the rule is stated correctly here and the overbroad reading is withdrawn. See `01_figure_grounded_audit.md` section 6.

## 6. Consequences for contact

**Prestige is not action.** From *The Recursive Self-Improvement Trap*: *"Prestige must never be allowed to validate an action directly... Someone let prestige stand in for proof. Someone let reputation substitute for result. Someone let the trusted name validate the untested claim."* And the constructive form: *"Prestige can fund an action. It can give an action a budget to be tried. But it cannot settle an action. Only the ground can settle it. The Ground Maintainer must be blind to prestige."*

A campaign that gathers famous physicists to agree with the shape claim is the named collapse, not corroboration. The legitimate ask gives the claim a budget to be tried and lets the ground, an experiment or a proof checker, settle it. The seating rule is the same: the outside frame *"reads shape without referent, which is exactly why it can catch a failure of shape that everyone who knows the content reads straight past"* (Seat Map).

**Pattern seeking is a real hazard, not a rhetorical one.** Love 2023 reports that humans are *"alert for configurations which are meaningful to us"* as a consequence of evolutionary history, and that apophenia is seeing patterns that do not exist. Donoghue's observation that the masses look randomly distributed in log m is therefore a reason for extra care: a pattern found by staring at 28 numbers is not a derivation, and a refuter must be written before the search.

**My own audit is a simulation, not a ground reading.** The same essay: *"LLMs can quickly simulate an approximate answer/output with the formula found in the architecture post... However, the truly testable output would need to be derived from the real architecture or working the formula yourself."* LLM output is a simulacrum. So `01_figure_grounded_audit.md` is a simulated derivation checked for internal consistency against the figures. It is not a test of the claim and must be labelled as such. It cannot settle anything it reports on.
