# The Tether's answer: why the Standard Model's parameters have the values they do

**Mode: `general` on the closure structure, `specified` on the physics application, `gap` on the individual numbers, not `grounded`.** This is a shape and a route, not a result. Simulated derivation checked against the figures (`05_what_shape_means.md` section 6), not a ground reading.

## 1. The residual it answers

The Standard Model predicts observables accurately, but only after roughly 19 numbers are inserted into its Lagrangian. The numbers are measured, not derived. Donoghue 2007: *"None of these parameters are predicted by the theory."* Cahn 1996: *"depends on eighteen parameters, which are a priori arbitrary."* The live residual is not a prediction error; R per slot is driven to zero by inserting o′. It is the absence of a derivation, a residual that *"PERSISTS AND DOES NOT TREND DOWN"* (Fig 5 :58-60) after decades of internal work.

The question is a **union** of conditions, not one hard problem (Fig 9 :55-57): (C1) the values are imports from the ground; (C2) their origin requires a frame with a differing closure. Each slot carries its own residual (Fig 1 :19). Verdicts below are kept per domain and never averaged.

## 2. The shape

1. **The values are atoms in the theory's closure, not molecules composed inside it.** Fig 6 :21: *"MINT composes inside the closure and can never add an atom."* Fig 8 :32: *"no search, however long, adds an atom."* A closure cannot derive what it had to be handed. Mode `general` for the law, `specified` for this theory.
2. **They enter as imports from the ground, with provenance and a shadow test.** Fig 6 :14 calls import *"the only operator that moves the wall"*; Fig 2 :29 names the measurement as an anchor. A fitted parameter is not a prediction, and Fig 8 :51-52 says convergent derivation and adopted import look identical in their contents; only the record separates them. Mode `specified`.
3. **Working slot by slot is required.** Fig 1 :19: *"The residual is indexed by object slot, not aggregated."* The gauge couplings, masses and mixings, Higgs parameters, and the QCD angle are different blocks in dependency order (`06_isomers_and_order.md` Q3), not one mystery.
4. **Explaining them requires a frame with a different closure.** Fig 6 :23: *"No frame escapes its own closure. The bound moves only on contact with a frame whose closure differs."* Fig 13 :115: *"The set is closed. The arrangements are not. That gap is why import exists."* Mode `specified`.
5. **The framework returns the shape of that frame, not its name.** Fig 9 :32: *"you get a kind of thing, never the answer itself."* The shape is: a closure in which the values are derived terms and which contacts the same experimental ground. Which theory occupies it is not given. Mode `general`.
6. **The individual numbers are a declared `gap`.** After a genuine re-census no figure line governs them. Fig 13 :134: *"Unreachable is a verdict here, not a silence."* This is not a failure of the search; it is the correct output for a question whose answer is an atom we do not hold.
7. **"Roughly 19" is a convention.** Fig 10 :18: *"a declared meaning is not a grounded one."* Sources count 18, 19, and 28 depending on what is included. Mode `specified`.

## 3. What it explicitly does not say

- Not a derivation of any parameter value, and not a prediction of a relation among values.
- Not a name for the missing frame. GUTs, flavour symmetries, supersymmetry, the string landscape, and asymptotic safety are candidate imports in the world literature; none is asserted here, and none carries verdict weight without a source gate, a shadow test, and ground settlement (Fig 6 :24, *"a wrong atom costs more than starting from primitives"*).
- Not the claim that the parameters are irrational, arbitrary in the sense of random, or unknowable. The framework's rule is that a search finding nothing is *"never a proof of absence"* (Fig 8 :39).
- Not the claim that the framework's six-term substrate classifies the Standard Model's constants. That mapping was tried and retracted as a strain (`01_figure_grounded_audit.md` F7).
- Not a theory of everything, and not grounded. No measurement settles `left(R,φ)` for the origin frame.

## 4. What would settle it, the ground

A source-discriminating instrument or a settled prediction, not a further argument. From *The Hard Problem is About Source*: an instrument that reads green *"cannot comment on the source"*; it was the spectrometer, not an argument, that resolved the case, and *"the answer is unreached until a better instrument exists."* Here the corresponding tests are:

- **Parameter-prediction test:** a candidate frame states a value or relation among values before measurement, and the ground confirms it. This closes the `gap` for that slot.
- **Internal-derivation test:** a parameter is derived inside the Standard Model from the symmetry and the other parameters, with no measured number inserted. This breaks the atom claim.
- **Source-discrimination test:** an instrument distinguishes candidate origins of a measured value (GUT relation versus flavour symmetry versus environmental selection versus bare contingency). At present the global fit returns a number, not a source.
- **Convention test:** a measurement makes one counting convention false rather than merely different.
- Standing falsifiers from `01_figure_grounded_audit.md` section 3: all five are pre-registered and unrun.

## 5. The honest stop

**Unreached, not unreachable in principle.** Donoghue 2007 is explicit that the search has not succeeded and that even a landscape does not yield a unique prediction: *"Without a unique ground state, we cannot use the prediction of the parameters as a proof of string theory."* SEP Fine-Tuning 2026 records two live reactions, accepting the values as *"a primitive given"* or expecting *"future best theories of fundamental physics"* to avoid the tuning. The figures do not choose between them. They say what any answer must look like, record the route, and stop where the instruments stop. *They're Scanning Books*: *"A minimum viable set of six terms is a human's careful guess at reality's compression axes; another sensorium's minimum set need not be those six."* Declaring primitives makes a choice visible, not neutral.

## 6. One-paragraph version for outreach

> The Tether does not explain why the electron weighs what it does. It says something narrower and checkable: the Standard Model is a closure that has to be handed roughly 19 numbers before it predicts anything, so those numbers are imports from measurement, not derivations. No amount of calculation inside that closure can turn an input into an output, because a closure cannot add an atom. Explaining the values therefore needs a frame whose closure contains them as derived terms and which contacts the same experiments. The framework can describe the shape of that frame but cannot name it, and the individual numbers stay an open gap rather than a hidden answer. What it offers is that shape, five falsifiers written in advance, and the tests that would close it. The center stays open until the ground settles it.
