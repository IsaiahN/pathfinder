# Expert verification packet: the Tether on the Standard Model parameters

**What this is:** a claim in a form a particle physicist or philosopher of physics can check. Mode, derivation, and refuters are stated before the conclusion, so a reader knows what to look for and what would settle it.

**Mode: `general` on the closure structure, `specified` on the physics application, `gap` on the numbers, not `grounded`.** No measurement at any scale is claimed to settle the origin of the parameters. This is a shape and a route, not a result.

## 1. The claim, in one paragraph

The Standard Model is a closure that must be handed roughly 19 numbers before it produces predictions. Those numbers are atoms imported from the ground (measurement) with provenance and a shadow test, not molecules composed inside the theory. More search inside this closure cannot derive them, because composing inside a closed set never adds an atom. Explaining them requires a frame whose closure contains them as derived terms and which contacts the same experimental ground. The framework can characterize the shape of that frame but cannot name it, and the individual values are a declared `gap`. The count "roughly 19" is a convention, not a ground fact. What follows is the shape any candidate answer must have, five falsifiers written in advance, and the tests that would close the gap.

## 2. Three checkable sub-claims

### C1 - framework-internal; checkable by reading Figures 6 and 8

- **Statement:** the parameter values are atoms in Γ, not derived molecules; more search inside the closure cannot produce them.
- **Source, verbatim:** Fig 6 :21 *"MINT composes inside the closure and can never add an atom."* Fig 8 :32 *"Closure is idempotent. Composing inside a closed set never leaves it, so no search, however long, adds an atom. This one is definitional: it is what closure means."*
- **Physics-facing check:** in the Standard Model Lagrangian, the gauge symmetry and field content fix the form of the theory, but the couplings, Yukawa matrices, Higgs parameters, and QCD angle are supplied from outside that derivation. Is that characterization faithful?
- **Refuter:** a parameter derived inside the theory from its symmetry and the other parameters, with no measured number inserted. If one exists, the atom claim fails.

### C2 - framework-internal; checkable by reading Figures 6, 9 and 13

- **Statement:** explaining the values requires an import from a frame with a differing closure; the framework returns the shape of that frame and cannot name it; the specific numbers are a `gap`.
- **Source, verbatim:** Fig 6 :23 *"No frame escapes its own closure. The bound moves only on contact with a frame whose closure differs."* Fig 9 :32 *"you get a kind of thing, never the answer itself"* Fig 13 :115 *"The set is closed. The arrangements are not. That gap is why import exists."* Fig 13 :134 *"Unreachable is a verdict here, not a silence"*
- **Physics-facing check:** does any current framework predict a Standard Model parameter value, or a determinate relation among values, from principle rather than by fitting? Donoghue 2007 reports that attempts with symmetries or dynamical mechanisms have been unsuccessful, and that without a unique vacuum string theory cannot claim to predict the parameters. If a settled prediction exists, the `gap` closes for that slot.
- **Refuter:** a candidate frame that states a value or relation before measurement and is confirmed at the ground. That would close D3 (the parameter-origin domain) and erase the `gap`.

### C3 - cross-domain; checkable against the literature and a counting convention

- **Statement:** the count "roughly 19" is a convention, and the slots should be worked separately in dependency order.
- **Source, verbatim:** Fig 10 :18 *"a declared meaning is not a grounded one"* Fig 1 :19 *"The residual is indexed by object slot, not aggregated."* Fig 3 :20 *"The chain is ordered by dependency, not by importance."*
- **What to check:** Cahn 1996 counts eighteen; the Wikipedia reference table lists nineteen; Donoghue 2007 counts twenty-eight including neutrinos, the cosmological constant, and Newton's constant. Are these differences purely about what is counted and how parameters are traded (pole mass versus MS-bar, v versus G_F, m_H versus λ)? If so, no measurement distinguishes the counts.
- **Refuter:** a parametrization choice that changes a physical prediction while the theory is held fixed, or a measurement that makes one count false rather than merely different. Either would break the convention claim.

## 5. What would settle it

1. **Parameter-prediction test:** a candidate frame states a value or relation among values before measurement and the ground confirms it.
2. **Internal-derivation test:** a parameter is derived from the symmetry and the other parameters with no measured number inserted.
3. **Source-discrimination test:** an instrument distinguishes candidate origins of a measured value, rather than returning only the value.
4. **Convention test:** a measurement makes one counting convention false rather than merely different.
5. **Aggregation test:** a single mechanism explains all slots with equal residual reduction, making per-slot indexing unnecessary.

A reported failure of any of these is more useful than an endorsement. The verdict on stage 1 should be recorded before the framework is shown.
