# WATSON answer, QMGR-0052: the values of the Standard Model's roughly 19 free parameters

**Mode: `general` on the closure structure, `specified` on the physics application, `gap` on the individual numbers, not `grounded`.** Figures are primary and decide. World sources are secondary, corroborate but never overrule, and a conflict returns the derivation to the domain census. This is a simulated derivation checked for internal consistency against the figures, not a ground reading. No measurement was taken.

## QUESTION

The values of the Standard Model's roughly 19 free parameters, which are not predicted by the theory.

Restated as an effect: why does the theory's R = |Γ(b,a) − o′| go to zero on each parameter slot only after the measured value o′ has been inserted into Γ? Option A, "the values are derived inside the Standard Model", predicts that sufficient internal calculation produces them. Option B, "the values are imports from the ground and their origin needs a frame with a differing closure", predicts that internal search cannot produce them and that a source-discriminating frame is owed. What each leads to: A contradicts the fact that the numbers are inputs to the Lagrangian; B yields a checkable shape, a named gap, and falsifiers written in advance. Anchor: the measurement, named as an anchor in Fig 2 :29.

## DOMAIN CENSUS

R by arity, symmetry and scale, then one line per domain giving actors / currency / anchor, partition or union, and order by dependency.

- **R (arity / symmetry / scale).** Arity: roughly 19 slots, organized as a union of coupled blocks (gauge, Higgs scale, Yukawa and mixing, isolated angles), not a flat list; R is indexed per slot (Fig 1 :19). Symmetry: the slots are heterogeneous, dimensionless couplings and angles versus dimensionful masses and the vacuum expectation value; the theory supplies no symmetry exchanging them. Scale: the values are defined at a renormalization scale and run between scales; the transform is lossy (Fig 4 :27).
- **D1 The Standard Model as closure Γ.** Actors: gauge symmetries, field multiplets, parameters-as-atoms. Currency: predicted observables. Anchor: experiment, the ground for physics. This is a partition-boundary question: which terms are derived and which are supplied.
- **D2 The measurement frame.** Actors: experiments, detectors, global fits. Currency: measured values o′ with uncertainties. Anchor: the measurement itself, pinned and not updating on the theory (Fig 2 :25).
- **D3 The parameter-origin frame.** Actors: unknown. Currency: would-be derived parameter values. Anchor: **none nameable yet**. Not a domain yet by the stated rule. Characterized in effect terms only: a closure in which the values are derived terms and which contacts the same ground.
- **D4 The bookkeeping / convention frame.** Actors: physicists choosing parametrizations and counting rules. Currency: reparameterization invariance. Anchor: convention only, a declared meaning, not a ground. The "18 / 19 / 28" counts live here.
- **X1 "the values are brute facts" as an explanation.** No actors, no currency, no anchor nameable. Refused, not a domain. Under Formula step 2 this is the REFUTED bin: a refusal is owed, not an explanation.

**Union, not partition.** The two conditions C1 (values are imports from the ground) and C2 (their origin requires a differing closure) form a compound (Fig 9 :55-57); a single lever clearing one leaves the other binding. **Dependency order:** the ground must be read before the closure is assessed; the residual must be described before a frame is chosen (Fig 9 :27-28); refusal precedes mint (Formula step 2); import follows a demonstrated reachability failure (Fig 6). **Strain test:** Fig 13's six-term substrate governs the framework's own primitives, not the Standard Model constants; mapping one onto the other was tried and retracted as a strain (audit F7). No figure line was forced.

## VERDICT (from the figures)

The figure-derived verdict: the Standard Model's parameter values are atoms imported from the ground, not derivations inside its closure; more search inside that closure cannot produce them; explaining them requires a frame with a differing closure whose shape can be characterized but not named; the individual numbers are a declared `gap`; and "roughly 19" is a counting convention. Verdicts are kept per domain and never averaged. Mode `general` / `specified` / `gap`, never `grounded`. The low-overlap world sources corroborate the residual and the cost structure but do not settle the origin. The conflict rule was not triggered; no source was returned to the census for a flipped verdict.

- **cost if wrong:** bounded and locatable. The shape claim carries a five-item refuter and a per-domain verdict, so an error is findable rather than invisible. The real cost is spending an outside reader's independence by sending a candidate theory or a recording before they have checked the shape. Fig 2 :42 says *"Buy independence in proportion to the cost of being wrong."* That cost is avoidable by leading with the shape claim and its refuter. A second cost is importing a wrong atom, which Fig 6 :24 prices above starting from primitives.
- **undoable:** yes. No term is deleted if the claim fails; a refutation is added and the reach falls because something was added, not because anything was struck out (Fig 6 :42-43). The shape, the gap, and the five falsifiers are recorded with provenance so a later reader can locate the error (Fig 10 :62-64). A sent claim can be superseded by a corrected packet without withdrawing a primitive.
- **reversed by:** a ground reading, not agreement. Specifically: a candidate frame predicting a parameter value or relation before measurement and confirmed at the ground; an internal derivation of a parameter with no measured number inserted; an instrument that discriminates among candidate sources of a measured value; a measurement making one counting convention false; or a single mechanism reducing all slots' residuals equally. Until one fires, the verdict stands as `candidate`. Prestige and agreement are not such instruments.
