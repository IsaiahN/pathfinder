# The Tether's answer on why ideas spread

**Mode: `general` on the four-condition shape, `specified` on each domain prediction, not `grounded`.**
This is a simulated derivation checked against the figures (`05` section 6), not a ground reading. No
diffusion cascade was measured here.

## 1. The residual it answers

The observed effect is a gap between two readings of the same idea: how many frames can compose and reapply
it, and how many people can name it. Fig 1 :19 says "The residual is indexed by object slot, not aggregated."
Fig 1 :22 says "Aggregating across slots is how a live signal disappears into an average." So an adoption
curve is not the ground of this question. It is a frame-internal quantity. Fig 1 :27: "They are frame-internal.
A frame cannot score itself with a quantity it also produces."

The question is a **union compound**, not one hard problem. Fig 9 :55: "A bundled problem is usually a union
of overlapping conditions, not a list of separate ones." Four conditions bind, they overlap, and each has
its own loop and its own refuter.

## 2. The shape

1. **Reach and cost govern uptake.** A receiving frame whose `Gamma` already holds the atoms can mint the
   idea locally; a frame that needs a new atom requires an import with a source gate and a shadow test.
   Fig 5 :18: "seeks the gap that is large and compressible". Fig 5 :26: "does the new term cost less to
   state than the confusion it removes?" Fig 6 :21: "MINT composes inside the closure and can never add an
   atom." Too general says nothing; too specific explains one occasion only (Fig 5 :43). This is a
   `specified` prediction, not a measurement.

2. **Only a method crosses the scale membrane.** Fig 4 :22: "Only methods cross up. Only head starts come
   down." Fig 4 :25: "A recording carried upward looks like knowledge and is a description of one occasion."
   What reaches a population is the generator a receiver can reapply, verified and compressed to
   essentials. A result or slogan can be replayed but cannot generate a new success. Fig 12 :119 names the
   surviving part: "catalyst. A part that survives its own use."

3. **Wide spread has two categorically different kinds, and they are never averaged.** Fig 2 :31:
   "Independence, between two frames, is how little they draw on the same evidence." Fig 2 :40:
   "agreement about the world is worthless when the agreeing frames share an evidence pool". Fig 2 :41:
   "efficient for a convention: agreement about a shared meaning is the product, not a corruption of it".
   Fig 7 :13: "agreement compounds, evidence does not". A specialist community is the high-overlap case:
   a room of mirrors. Fig 7 :36: "A chain of mirrors is one long room. The next room must differ from the
   last, and match the residual it surfaced." Spread through low-overlap frames is the only kind that
   carries evidence: Fig 8 :29 "low overlap: B does not draw on A's evidence".

4. **Spread depends on the habitat, not on the idea alone.** Fig 11 :39: "capability is a property of
   agent-and-habitat, never of the agent alone". Fig 11 :40: "An improvement that does not change contact
   changes nothing, however much it improves." Contact cascades, so the decisive effect can be two or three
   steps out. A specialist community is a substituted habitat: Fig 11 :66 "Isolation is not removal of the
   habitat. It is substitution of one habitat for another."

**Union, not average.** Fig 9 :54: "And when the split lands on a union rather than a partition, unbundle
before you search again." Fig 9 :60: "Use filters for the budget and witnesses for the verdict. Never let a
filter hand you a verdict." Prestige and platform size are filters that grant a budget; they are not
witnesses and they do not settle anything.

**Dependency order.** Settle reach before method form, method form before independence, independence before
habitat. Fig 3 :20: "The chain is ordered by dependency, not by importance. Each link consumes what the one
above produces, so effort spent above the break arrives at a step that cannot use it."

## 3. What it explicitly does not say

- Not that simplicity alone explains spread. The bargain has two sides: a large *and* compressible gap,
  and a term that explains one occasion perfectly also fails (Fig 5 :43).
- Not that the most-cited idea is the best one. Adoption count is frame-internal (Fig 1 :27), and
  correlation is "disqualifying for a verdict" (Seat Map :163).
- Not that high-overlap agreement and low-overlap agreement are degrees of one thing. They are
  categorically different (Fig 2 :40-41).
- Not that any particular idea is currently SEARCHED, INWARD or OUTWARD owed. `06` shows the three are
  distinct and only a ground reading can choose.
- Not `grounded`. No claim here has been settled by a measurement of diffusion.
- Not a name for the winning mechanism. The framework returns a shape, not a name.

## 4. What would settle it, the ground

A source-discriminating measurement, not a further argument. *The Hard Problem is About Source*: an
instrument that reads green "cannot comment on the source"; the spectrometer resolved it. Here the
corresponding questions are:

1. **Reapplication test.** For each receiver frame, can it generate a new success from the idea, or only
   repeat the original? This separates a method from a recording (Fig 4 :22, :25).
2. **Independence axis.** Record which receivers draw on which evidence. Two frames reading the same
   report are one frame wearing two names (Fig 8). If independence is zero, agreement is a convention.
3. **Habitat reproduction.** What contact, actor and condition from the origin habitat was reproduced in
   the destination, and what second-order effect appeared? Fig 11 :52 names the DDT and eagles cascade.
4. **Standing falsifiers.** One intrinsic property predicting spread with no dependence on reach or
   habitat; a recording generating new successes; high-overlap agreement carrying verdict weight; a
   filter changing the verdict. Any one fires the pre-registered refuter in `01` section 4.

## 5. The honest stop

**Unreached, not unreachable.** The four conditions are "a human's careful guess" at the axes of diffusion,
not nature's own list. *They're Scanning Books*: "A minimum viable set of six terms is a human's careful
guess at reality's compression axes; another sensorium's minimum set need not be those six." Declaring the
conditions makes the choice visible, not neutral. The answer stops at the limit of the available
instruments and records the lineage so the stop is checkable. Fig 4 :30: "So take the round trip and measure
the gap. Without it you are navigating by dead reckoning: confident about a position nobody checked."
"Indiscernibility is not identity."

## 6. One-paragraph version for outreach

> The Tether does not give a single reason why some ideas travel. It says the question is a union of four
> conditions that must not be averaged. A receiver can only take up an idea if it can compose it from atoms
> it already holds and if stating it costs less than the confusion it removes. What crosses from a
> specialist group to a population is a re-applicable method, never a recording of one success. And the
> spread itself comes in two kinds: agreement inside one shared evidence pool is a convention, while
> agreement across independent frames is the only kind that carries evidence. Whether the idea travels at
> all also depends on the habitat it lands in, not on the idea alone. What the framework offers is that
> shape, four refuters written in advance, and the measurements that would close it. It does not name the
> winning mechanism and it does not claim to have measured any case.
