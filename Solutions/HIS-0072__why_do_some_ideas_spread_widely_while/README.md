## The question restated as an effect

An idea is a candidate term `phi` in a frame's library. The observed effect is a gap between two readings of
the same term: (a) how many and which frames can compose and reapply it, and (b) how many people merely
know the name. The residual `R` is indexed by receiving frame, not aggregated into an adoption count.
Option A treats wide spread as a property of the idea itself. Option B treats wide spread as a fact about
the relation between an idea, the reach of the receiving frames, the membrane between scales, and the
habitat the idea lands in. A leads to a search for a single intrinsic lever and mistakes a convention for
a verdict. B leads to four separate loops with four separate refuters, and leaves the question of truth
to the ground.

**What an answer would look like (Direction, bound as an ordinary term):** a checkable *shape* with four
named conditions and a declared independence axis, not a name for the winning property. The bound shape is
written down in `01` section 1 and `07` section 2, so it can accrue a residual like any other term.

## What is deliberately not here

- No personal emails of named individuals.
- No ranking of people by how likely they are to agree that wide spread is a good thing.
- No bulk-send plan.

Three reasons, in the figures' own words:

1. **A room does not upgrade the label.** *"A room does not upgrade the label. A room reaching a
   conclusion without the ground is agreement, and agreement among frames is a weighted average of
   influence. Adding people raises the vocabulary and does not add an anchor."* (Seat Map :283)
2. **Correlation disqualifies a verdict.** *"High correlation is disqualifying for a verdict and efficient
   for a convention."* (Seat Map :163) Routing the packet only to people already committed to a favoured
   diffusion theory manufactures the correlation that would make their agreement worthless.
3. **Asking for trust before a check spends independence.** *"A verifier that can reconstruct the claim
   cannot verify it."* (Fig 10 :42) The first contact carries the residual and its refuter, never the
   framework's interiors.

Legally: unsolicited bulk email to scraped personal addresses breaches GDPR and CAN-SPAM style rules in
many jurisdictions and gets the sending domain blacklisted. That is a practical failure on top of the
structural one. Every venue in `03` is public and opt-in.

reading. From *The Recursive Self-Improvement Trap*: "LLMs can quickly simulate an approximate
answer/output with the formula found in the architecture post", but "the truly testable output would need
to be derived from the real architecture or working the formula yourself." This folder cannot settle the
question it reports on. No source outside the folder was modified.
