## 2. What the wider literature bears on the shape

The world sources are secondary. They corroborate or conflict; they do not decide. Overlap is recorded in
`10_source_ledger.csv`.

1. **Rogers names the same compound without the figure vocabulary.** His five characteristics mix a cost
   term (complexity), a reachability term (compatibility with existing tools and values), and an
   observability term. The "marketing chasm" is a threshold between niche and self-sustaining spread, and
   his failed-diffusion case is a cluster that spreads locally and stops at the cluster boundary. This
   corroborates F2 and F4 but does not separate convention from corroboration.
2. **Complex contagion and threshold models name the comparative bond.** Multiple exposures and a
   fraction `q` of adopting neighbours map to Fig 12's `⋛` comparison, and the four mechanisms
   (coordination, credibility, legitimacy, emotional contagion) are habitat conditions in Fig 11's sense.
   It corroborates F5 and the comparability of F4, but it does not by itself give the independence
   criterion.
3. **Granovetter's weak ties are the low-overlap channel.** "Acquaintances know people that we do not"
   gives novel information; cliques bound by strong ties are the high-overlap specialist room. This is a
   strong corroboration of F4 from a field with no contact with the figures.
4. **Kuhn and communities of practice describe the specialist case.** Paragraphs on normal science,
   exemplars, and "legitimate peripheral participation" describe a community whose shared repertoire is
   precisely the high-overlap closure of Fig 7. Corroborates F4 and F5.
5. **Matthew effect and cultural transmission biases describe the filter.** Prestige bias, success bias
   and preferential attachment match F7. The Meme page's line about memes that spread "even when they
   prove to be detrimental to the welfare of their hosts" is the cleanest external statement that spread
   and settlement are different axes. Corroborates F1 and F7.
6. **Dual inheritance gives the categorical split a second witness.** "Conformist biases make it
   difficult for novel cultural traits to spread through a population" while prestige and skill biases
   steer imitation. This corroborates the specialist-confinement side of F4 from evolutionary anthropology,
   a field with low overlap with physics-style frameworks.

**Conflict check.** No world source contradicts the figure verdict. Granovetter's claim that weak ties
carry novel information and Centola and Macy's finding that weak ties can slow *complex* diffusion look
like a conflict, but they govern different quantities: information transfer versus reinforcement-based
adoption. The re-census keeps both as two conditions of F4, exactly as the union reading requires. No
conflict was resolved by flipping a verdict.

## 3. Distribution: offer, do not push

Figure-grounded rules:

- **Fig 4 :22** "Only methods cross up. Only head starts come down." Sending the full architecture before
  a recipient has described a residual sends a recording, not a generator. Lead with the shape and its
  refuter; offer links after.
- **Fig 10 :42** "A verifier that can reconstruct the claim cannot verify it." A recipient who has
  absorbed the framework before being asked for a verdict has joined the frame being checked.
- **Fig 9 :27** "describe it before you go looking, or" "any frame you pick will seem to fit". Ask what
  they would expect to see if the shape were true before giving them the literature.
- **Fig 2 :40** "agreement about the world is worthless when the agreeing frames share an evidence pool".
  A send selected for people who already agree produces a convention, not corroboration.
- **Fig 9 :60** "Use filters for the budget and witnesses for the verdict. Never let a filter hand you a
  verdict." Venue prestige is a budget, not a witness.

**Recommended reply order:** (1) the four-condition shape and its four refuters; (2) ask what they would
count as a failure and record the answer; (3) only then the links, with the note that the figures, not the
summary, are the authority.

## 4. The two-stage offer

1. **Stage 1, before any theory.** Send the residual in effect terms, the bound shape, and the
   pre-registered refuter. Ask for the recipient's failure criterion. Record their verdict *before*
   showing any framework material.
2. **Stage 2, opt-in only.** If they are curious, offer the figure extract, the canonical usage rule, and
   the diffusion literature links. Their later agreement is never pooled as corroboration, because they
   have now seen the interior.
3. **Stage 3, method reuse.** Offer `THE_FORMULA.md` as a generator. Each user states their own ground
   and writes their own refuter before running it, since a frame cannot check the direction of its own
   question.

**Hard precondition:** stages 1 and 2 work only if stage 1 survives. The four-condition shape in `01`
section 0 is a shape claim with refuters, not a result. If a recipient fires one of the refuters, send the
failure to `11_audit.md` with provenance rather than explaining it away.
