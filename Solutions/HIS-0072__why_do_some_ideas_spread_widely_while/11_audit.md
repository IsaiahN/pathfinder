## Independent domain census

I redid the census from the residual line alone, before comparing with the primary's conclusions, using the
WATSON order: R by arity, symmetry and scale; one line per domain with actors, currency and anchor;
partition or union; order by dependency.

- **R (arity / symmetry / scale).** The residual is the difference between counting people who can name an
  idea and counting frames that can reapply it. Arity: one term plus a set of receiving frames, minimum
  two and practically three or more. Symmetry: directed and threshold gated, so `→` or `⋛` rather than the
  symmetric `+`. Scale: one specialist frame versus a population, with a same-scale cross-frame axis.
  I reach the same union compound as the primary, but by a different route: Fig 9 :55 and the negative-Bass
  case (market saturating below full adoption) rather than the four-condition list.
- **Domain 1, composition and cost.** Actors: term, atoms, bonds, receivers' `Gamma`. Currency: bits and
  actions. Anchor: the ground for truth, which is not the same as the ground for spread. Partition:
  which compositions cross the two kinds.
- **Domain 2, the membrane.** Actors: single frame, population. Currency: method versus recording. Anchor:
  re-applicability. Question type: a directional filter, not a wall.
- **Domain 3, independence.** Actors: frames and ties. Currency: agreement versus evidence. Anchor: the
  ground, never agreement. Question type: a categorical split. This is where the negative-Bass case and
  the mirror-chain case meet.
- **Domain 4, habitat.** Actors: actors, conditions, relations in contact with the residual. Currency:
  contact cascade. Anchor: the ground that keeps what survives. Enumeration, not composition.

Order by dependency: reach, method form, independence, habitat. I derived this from Fig 3 :20 and from the
observation that a vocabulary without a re-applicable method cannot even be tested for independence, rather
than from the primary's D1 to D4 labels.

My census matches the primary's four domains and its union reading. Where the primary derived from Fig 5
and Fig 6 (cost and closure), I derived from Fig 3 (dependency) and the world's negative-Bass case.
Different starting points, same boundaries: low overlap between routes, so the agreement is weak but not
circular within the framework. It is still one evidence pool.
