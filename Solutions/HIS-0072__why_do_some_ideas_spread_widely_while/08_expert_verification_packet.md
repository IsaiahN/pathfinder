# Expert verification packet - the Tether on diffusion of ideas

**What this is:** a claim in a form a diffusion researcher can check. Mode, derivation and refuters are
stated before the conclusion, so a reader knows what to look for and what would settle it.

**Mode: `general` on the four-condition shape, `specified` on each condition, not `grounded`.** No
measurement of a diffusion cascade is claimed. This is a shape and a route, not a result.

**Status of this packet:** a simulated derivation checked for internal consistency against the figures, not
a ground reading. It cannot settle the question it reports on.

## 1. The claim, in one paragraph

The question "why do some ideas spread widely" is a union of four conditions, not one lever. First, a
receiver can only take up an idea if the idea composes from atoms the receiver already holds and if stating
it costs less than the confusion it removes. Second, what crosses from a specialist scale to a population
is a re-applicable method, never a recording of one success. Third, wide spread comes in two categorical
kinds: agreement inside a shared evidence pool is a convention and is disqualifying as a verdict, while
agreement across low-overlap frames is the only kind that carries evidence. Fourth, spread depends on the
habitat of actors and conditions in contact with the idea, not on the idea alone. These conditions overlap,
must be cleared in dependency order, and are never averaged. Adoption count is a frame-internal quantity,
not the ground of the question.

## 2. Four checkable sub-claims

### C1 - framework-internal and measurement-facing; checkable by reading Figures 1, 5 and 6

- **Statement:** the gap is per receiving frame, and uptake is governed by reachability plus a
  description-length bargain. Too general or too specific fails.
- **Source, verbatim:** Fig 1 :19 "The residual is indexed by object slot, not aggregated." Fig 5 :26
  "does the new term cost less to state than the confusion it removes?" Fig 5 :43 "A term that explains
  everything by saying nothing fails this, and so does a term that explains one occasion perfectly." Fig 6
  :21 "MINT composes inside the closure and can never add an atom."
- **Refuter:** a single intrinsic property of the idea predicts spread across independent cases with no
  dependence on receiver reach; or a term requiring a genuinely new atom in the receiver spreads as fast as
  one composed from held atoms, with no source gate and no shadow test.

### C2 - cross-scale; checkable against any case of a local practice becoming a public method

- **Statement:** only a generator crosses the scale membrane; a recording repeats a past success and cannot
  generate a new one.
- **Source, verbatim:** Fig 4 :22 "Only methods cross up. Only head starts come down." Fig 4 :25 "A
  recording carried upward looks like knowledge and is a description of one occasion." Fig 12 :119
  "catalyst. A part that survives its own use."
- **What to check:** in a case you know, what was transmitted: a rule a new group could reapply, or the
  output of one occasion? Did receivers generate new successes, or only repeat the original?
- **Refuter:** a recording with no re-applicable rule crosses to a population and is used there to generate
  new successes.

### C3 - cross-frame; checkable against the independence structure of any diffusion dataset

- **Statement:** agreement among frames sharing an evidence pool is a convention, not corroboration; the two
  kinds are not averaged.
- **Source, verbatim:** Fig 2 :31 "Independence, between two frames, is how little they draw on the same
  evidence." Fig 2 :40 "agreement about the world is worthless when the agreeing frames share an evidence
  pool" Fig 2 :41 "efficient for a convention: agreement about a shared meaning is the product, not a
  corruption of it" Fig 7 :13 "agreement compounds, evidence does not" Fig 8 :45 "Two frames together reach
  what neither reaches alone."
- **What to check:** in a diffusion study, are the "independent adopters" actually independent instruments,
  or readers of the same review? If the same source, their agreement is one witness wearing many names.
- **Refuter:** two frames drawing on one evidence pool produce verdict-grade corroboration, or a mirror chain
  adds evidence.

### C4 - habitat; checkable by enumerating contact rather than composing it

- **Statement:** the habitat contributes to spread; contact cascades; isolation substitutes a habitat.
- **Source, verbatim:** Fig 11 :39 "capability is a property of agent-and-habitat, never of the agent alone"
  Fig 11 :40 "An improvement that does not change contact changes nothing, however much it improves." Fig
  11 :66 "Isolation is not removal of the habitat. It is substitution of one habitat for another."
- **What to check:** list the actors, conditions and relations in contact with the idea at origin and at
  destination. What was reproduced, and what second-order effect arrived three steps out?
- **Refuter:** an agent-only change that moves the ground, that is, changes who can reapply the idea without
  changing any contact.

## 3. What is explicitly not claimed

- Not that simplicity alone causes spread. The bargain has two sides (Fig 5 :43).
- Not that adopter count is evidence of truth. It is frame-internal (Fig 1 :27).
- Not that high-overlap and low-overlap agreement can be averaged into one confidence score (Fig 2 :40-41).
- Not that prestige or citation count settles an idea. From *The Recursive Self-Improvement Trap*: "Prestige
  can fund an action. It can give an action a budget to be tried. But it cannot settle an action. Only the
  ground can settle it."
- Not that any real case has been assigned to SEARCHED, INWARD or OUTWARD. `06` shows the three are
  distinct; only a ground reading can choose.
- Not `grounded`. Nothing here has been settled by experiment.

## 5. What would settle it

A source-discriminating measurement, not a further argument. *The Hard Problem is About Source*: an
instrument that reads green "cannot comment on the source"; the spectrometer resolved it.

1. **Reapplication test:** can each receiver generate a new success, or only repeat the original?
2. **Independence axis:** record evidence overlap among adopters. Zero overlap means corroboration; shared
   sources means convention.
3. **Habitat reproduction:** list the contact, actors and conditions reproduced at the destination and the
   second-order effects that followed.
4. **Pre-registered refuters:** any one of C1 to C4 firing, as stated in `01` section 4.

## 6. What an expert can check in twenty minutes

1. Read Fig 1 :19, Fig 4 :22 and :25, Fig 2 :40 and :41, Fig 11 :39 (six short passages).
2. Take one diffusion case you know well. Say which of the four conditions was the actual break, and name
   the evidence that would separate convention from corroboration in that case.
3. Name one instrument that would distinguish a re-applicable method from a recording in your field, or say
   why none is possible.
4. Send the refutation. A failure is more useful than an endorsement.
