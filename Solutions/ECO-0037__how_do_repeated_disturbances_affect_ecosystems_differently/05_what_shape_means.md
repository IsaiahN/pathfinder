## 1. A shape is a constraint, not a filler

The figures README states the limit: the framework returns *"what a missing thing would have to look like (a shape, not a name)"*. Fig 9 :32: *"you get a kind of thing, never the answer itself."* Fig 13 says it *"can say the answer is out of reach with the library as it stands... It cannot say which primitive is missing, because naming it would mean already holding it."*

From *The Library of Babel*: *"you can cut your search short if the set of books returned is small and descriptive enough that it provides a general shape of the answer that satisfies your question."*

So for ECO-0037 a **shape** is the form of the difference between two disturbance regimes that can be stated before the sign or magnitude of that difference is measured. It constrains; it does not fill. "The two regimes are isomers over one disturbance budget, and the recovery interval is a catalyst question" is a shape. "Three small fires leave 18 percent more species than one fire of the same intensity" is a name, and the framework does not supply it.

## 2. How a shape is obtained

- *Library of Babel*: use the vocabulary already held that is closest to the thing, then look for recurring new terms that overlap or correlate with it. *"With each new term gained, you are able to narrow the space by definition and deepen your vocabulary."*
- *Library of Babel* on steering: the returned books *"need to be tested or ranged against the problem you want answered in order to steer the direction of the next search."* That is the pre-registered refuter's job here.
- *How Intelligence Actually Works*: the framework *"cuts down to the shape of a thing, problem or question based on the given information available at this point in time."* It adds the stopping rule: *"indiscernibility is not identity."*
- Fig 6 :30: *"The description travels. Fails by being sharpened into terms only we can read. Cast it in effect terms."* Hence the residual was stated as an effect (what the aggregate fails to explain), not as a cause (which mechanism wins).
- *Decoding Tacit Knowledge*: *"When contact isn't possible, the best you can send is a plain description of what you'd expect to see, because anyone can check that against the world."* This is why the outreach leads with what a refutation would look like.
- *Library of Babel* on stopping: *"You will know that you are done because the finish line will also be the starting place."* The paradox is that by then *"you have already composed the answer through the process of locating it."*

For this question the closest available vocabulary was "compound disturbance", "frequency and intensity interact", "spacing", and "cumulative damage with sequence effects". Each narrows the shape from "dose matters" to "arrangement matters at fixed dose", without yet saying which arrangement wins.

## 3. What licenses a name

Only two things, both outside the shape itself:

1. **The ground settles it.** Formula step 5: *"the ground settles it. Verified only by what pays."* The README says the framework *"stops at the limit of what the available instruments can distinguish, and records the lineage so the stop is honest."* A matched experiment at fixed dose would name the sign and size for a given system.
2. **A provenanced import from a frame whose closure differs** (Fig 6 :23, Fig 8 :50 to :52), with a source gate and a shadow test. The Hall et al. microcosm result is such an import: a different field, a real experiment, used as corroboration rather than as a figure line.

*They're Scanning Books* sharpens the second point: *"The real import is not a new term in the existing space. It is a new dimension of the space."* And: *"A minimum viable set of six terms is a human's careful guess at reality's compression axes; another sensorium's minimum set need not be those six."* Also: *"Declaring the primitives does not make them neutral. It makes the choice visible."*

The six are a visible guess, not nature's own list. This matters because F10 declined to force the disturbance regime into the six-term substrate. Declaring that choice openly is better than smuggling it in.

### Correction to my own reading of §1

I initially read *"shape, not a name"* as a ban on naming any figure-held atom. It is not. The README's phrase governs a **missing** primitive, one not held in the library. The two disturbance regimes are not missing primitives; they are two arrangements of quantities already in hand. Naming the bond and the catalyst is therefore a binding of observed ecology onto held terms, not the invention of a seventh. What remains forbidden is asserting a numeric outcome the framework cannot derive. This correction is recorded rather than quietly applied.

## 4. The ground and the source problem

*The Hard Problem is About Source* gives the mechanism that governs this question most directly. An instrument reads green and *"cannot comment on the source"*: one green light, or yellow plus blue, or RGB, or CMYK, or a scheme not yet discovered. *"If several paths create the same behavior, then behavior is a projection with lossy information."* The resolution came from the spectrometer, not from an argument. *"An instrument that can't distinguish these cases demonstrates its own inadequacy."*

Here the total-dose readout is the green reading. It cannot tell you whether the dose arrived as one pulse or many, and a single number erases the distinction the question is about. What is owed is a measurement that discriminates sources, not a verbal decision about which schedule is "really" the same dose.

*How Intelligence Actually Works* adds provenance: the frame and the photograph *"are indistinguishable as artifacts, and they carry different warrants. One was taken; one was extracted. And nothing in the image records which."* Likewise, two ecosystems with the same post-disturbance biomass can carry different histories, and the final reading records neither. The lineage is the object that makes the claim checkable. *"The recursion converges toward indiscernibility, and indiscernibility is not identity."*

## 5. Answer to the shape question

**What is the shape of an answer to "how do repeated disturbances differ from one of the same total intensity"?** It is a three-part constraint:

1. A sufficient descriptor must name the arrangement, not only the ingredient total. Same atoms, different bond, different substance.
2. The arrangement is a sequence with gaps, and the gaps are where regeneration either happens or fails. The regenerative part is a catalyst, and the standard dose readout does not record which kind of part it is.
3. The outcome must be read per slot (species, patch, functional group) and outward to third-order contacts, because the average and the first-order reading both discard the live signal.

**What is not part of the shape:** the sign of the difference, an optimal frequency, a threshold dose, and any claim that this has been measured here. Those require a ground.

**Does this folder commit the error the README forbids?** No name is asserted for an unheld primitive. It does, however, borrow the word "isomer" from chemistry as a structural description. That is a provenanced analogy, and it is labelled as such in `06_isomers_and_order.md` rather than presented as an ecological measurement.

## 6. Consequences for contact

**Prestige is not action.** From *The Recursive Self-Improvement Trap*: *"Prestige must never be allowed to validate an action directly... Prestige can fund an action. It can give an action a budget to be tried. But it cannot settle an action. Only the ground can settle it. The Ground Maintainer must be blind to prestige."* A list of distinguished ecologists who agree with the shape is the named collapse, not corroboration. The legitimate ask is to give the claim a budget to be tried.

**Agreement is not a witness.** *How a Society of Agents Would Work*: *"Ten copies watching the same thing are one opinion said ten times."* *Decoding Tacit Knowledge*: *"High contact builds exactly the shared understanding that makes communication work, but that same factor makes the learner's agreement with the teacher worthless as a check."* This simulated solution and its second seat share one evidence pool, so their agreement is one pool talking (Fig 2 :40).

**My own audit is a simulation, not a ground reading.** Same essay: *"LLMs can quickly simulate an approximate answer/output"* and *"the truly testable output would need to be derived from the real architecture or working the formula yourself."* The article's demo section is blunter: outputs *"could never be verified if the solutions presented were not contrived to fit the formula."* So `01` is a simulated derivation, checked for internal consistency against the figures. It is **not** a test of the ecological claim and must be labelled as such. It cannot settle anything it reports on.

**What would move it to grounded:** a matched schedule experiment in a named system, with per-slot outcomes and a third-order contact census, run by someone who saw the refuter before the framework.
