## QUESTION

Decide, in effect terms, what the Tether says about the claim that a single disturbance and a series of disturbances with the same total intensity affect an ecosystem differently. Option A: treat equal dose as the same regime and explain any difference as noise or context. Option B: treat them as two arrangements of one disturbance budget and derive what must differ before any sign is measured. What each leads to: A uses a descriptor that discards the return interval and the regenerative state, so it cannot state the difference it is asked to explain; B yields a checkable shape, four refuters written in advance, and an open slot for the sign and size until a ground settles them.

## DOMAIN CENSUS

R by arity, symmetry and scale, then one line per domain with actors, currency and anchor, partition or union, and order by dependency.

- **R (the residual).** The systematic difference in outcome between two disturbance schedules at fixed cumulative dose. Arity: two operands under comparison, each itself a composition. Symmetry: the dose sum is symmetric under permutation, the schedule is not; that mismatch is the seam. Scale: first order is the event and the organism or patch, second order is the regime and its recovery loop, third order is the legacy in soil, seed bank and trophic partners. Fig 9 names a union compound, not one hard question: matching the intensity condition leaves the recovery condition binding.
- **Domain 1, the event frame.** Actors: disturbance agent, dose, recipient organisms and patches. Currency: intensity, frequency, duration, return interval. Anchor: plot or microcosm mortality and regrowth counts. Type: partition boundary, what counts as one event versus several.
- **Domain 2, the recovery frame.** Actors: survivors, seed bank, resprouting tissue, soil biota, mutualists. Currency: regeneration rate, stored propagules, time to threshold. Anchor: measured recovery trajectory between pulses. Type: a loop, does the spent part survive its own use and come back.
- **Domain 3, the regime frame.** Actors: functional groups, trophic network, abiotic state. Currency: diversity, biomass, distance to a threshold. Anchor: observed regime state and crossing event. Type: union compound with D1 and D2, not a harder D1.
- **Domain 4, the verification frame.** Actors: field ecologists, reviewers, datasets. Currency: agreement and residual against the ground. Anchor: the experiment, never agreement. In this simulated run all four domains draw on one evidence pool, so D4 independence is zero.
- **Order by dependency.** Perception first (return interval is observable), vocabulary second (the dose-only term lacks the recovery slot), objective third (what counts as a different outcome), planning fourth (the matched experiment), learn and carry fifth. ## VERDICT (from the figures)

The shape survives; a sign does not. Equal total intensity is an ingredient count, so the two regimes are isomers over one disturbance budget, or at minimum functional-group members with a different qualifier. The return interval is a catalyst and cycle question about which spent part regenerates before the next pulse, and dose accounting does not record which. Outcomes must be read per slot and out to third-order contacts. The series composes from settled events, so no new primitive or import is needed. The six-term substrate mapping was declined as a strain. What is not claimed: any optimal frequency, any threshold dose, any sign, and any measurement. This is a `specified`, ungrounded result.

- **cost if wrong:** bounded and locatable. The packet states mode, three checkable sub-claims and four refuters before the conclusion, so an error is findable. The larger cost is spending the independence of outside readers by sending a conclusion instead of a generator. Fig 2 :42 "Buy independence in proportion to the cost of being wrong." That cost is avoidable by leading with the shape and its refuters.
- **undoable:** yes. The dose-only rival is not deleted; it is kept as the alternative the refuter tests. If refuter 1 or 2 fires, the shape narrows to the functional-group claim or is withdrawn, and no primitive has to be retracted. A sent claim can be superseded by the corrected packet.
- **reversed by:** a matched-schedule experiment in a named system with per-slot outcomes and a third-order contact census. Specifically: a dose-only descriptor winning out of sample (refuter 1); invariance to spacing and ordering (refuter 2); the regenerative part shown idle (refuter 3); third-order enumeration adding nothing (refuter 4). Agreement or prestige is not such an instrument. Fig 8 :39 "So a search that finds nothing is never a proof of absence" defines the honest limit of a null result.
