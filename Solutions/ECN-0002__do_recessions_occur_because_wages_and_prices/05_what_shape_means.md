## 1. A shape is a constraint, not a filler

The canonical figures README states the limit: the framework returns *"what a missing thing would have to look like (a shape, not a name)."* Fig 9 :32: *"you get a kind of thing, never the answer itself."* Fig 13 :134: *"Unreachable is a verdict here, not a silence"* and the same passage says the framework cannot say which primitive is missing, because naming it would mean already holding it.

From *The Library of Babel* (2026-10-02): *"You can cut your search short if the set of books returned is small and descriptive enough that it provides a general shape of the answer that satisfies your question."*

So a **shape** is the narrowed form of an answer you can state before you can measure which instance is real. It constrains; it does not fill. For ECN-0002 the shape is: a union compound with two slots, a per-slot residual, a measured round-trip gap, and a signed amplification loop. "Sticky prices caused the 1981 recession" is a name. "For this episode and sector, the adjustment channel had this measured speed and the amplification loop had this measured sign" is a shape.

## 2. How a shape is obtained

- *The Library of Babel*: use the vocabulary you already have that is closest to the thing, look for recurring new terms that overlap or correlate with it, recurse. *"With each new term gained, you narrow the space and deepen your vocabulary."*
- Fig 6 :30: *"The description travels. Fails by being sharpened into terms only we can read. Cast it in effect terms."* Fig 9 :30-31: *"your words are borrowed too, so describe the effect rather than the cause"*
- *Decoding Tacit Knowledge* (2026-11-10): *"When contact isn't possible, the best you can send is a plain description of what you'd expect to see, because anyone can check that against the world."*
- *The Library of Babel* on stopping: *"you will know that you are done because the finish line will also be the starting place, as there is nowhere else to search and nothing else that can describe the answer better."* The paradox is that by then you have *"composed the answer through the process of locating it."*

For this question the effect description is deliberately neutral between the two schools: "a disturbance hits, the shortfall persists, here is the measured decay of the price channel and here is the measured sign of the quantity feedback." Neither phrase presupposes the answer.

## 3. The bundle passage is the direct hit

*The Library of Babel* (2026-10-02) contains the sentence that governs ECN-0002 before any figure line is needed:

> *"Perhaps your problem was a bundle or conflation of several problems masquerading as one? You will need to search all of those problems individually to find their answers and then combine them in a satisfactory way."*

Fig 9 :4-5 says *"are not one hard question but several well-formed ones. Split it rather than search."* Fig 9 :55 adds *"A bundled problem is usually a union of overlapping conditions, not a list of separate ones."* The essay and the figure agree in different vocabularies. That is corroboration in the sense the framework permits, not two independent instruments: the essay is by the framework's author. It is recorded as such in `10_source_ledger.csv`.

## 4. What licenses a name

Only two things, both outside the shape itself:

1. **The ground settles it.** Formula step 5: *"the ground settles it. Verified only by what pays."* The canonical README: the framework *"stops at the limit of what the available instruments can distinguish, and records the lineage so the stop is honest."*
2. **A provenanced import from a frame whose closure differs** (Fig 6 :23, Fig 8 :45), with a source gate and a shadow test.

*They're Scanning Books When They Should Be Studying Nature* (2026-09-06) sharpens this: *"The real import is not a new term in the existing space. It is a new dimension of the space."* And: *"A minimum viable set of six terms is a human's careful guess at reality's compression axes; another sensorium's minimum set need not be those six."* Also: *"Declaring the primitives does not make them neutral. It makes the choice visible, which is worth a great deal, because a visible choice can be revised and an emergent one cannot."*

Applied here: "price stickiness" and "financial instability" are not new primitives being invented by this derivation. They are imports from the economics frame, each with its own literature and its own instruments. They must be provenanced as such. What the figures forbid is declaring one of them the single ground-decided cause of all recessions.

## 5. Why the aggregate reading cannot name the source

*The Hard Problem is About Source* (2026-08-28) gives the mechanism. Metamerism: an instrument reads green and *"cannot comment on the source."* One green light, or yellow plus blue, or RGB, or CMYK, or a scheme not yet discovered can give the same reading. The essay states: *"If several paths create the same behavior, then behavior is a projection with lossy information (it cannot define consciousness in full)."* And: *"The answer is unreached until a better instrument exists."* Also: *"An instrument that can't distinguish these cases demonstrates its own inadequacy, it cannot claim anything substantial about what is being measured or what causes it."*

The recession analogue is direct. A single observation, "output stayed low for six quarters after a demand shock," is compatible with slow price adjustment, with a balance-sheet feedback loop, and with both acting together. Asking which one caused it from that reading alone is the metamerism error. What is owed is a source-discriminating instrument: separate measurements of adjustment speed and of feedback sign, plus the round-trip gap between micro and macro. This is the essay-side corroboration of F3 and F5 in `01_figure_grounded_audit.md`.

*How Intelligence Actually Works* (2026-09-16) adds the warning: *"The projection that no instrument distinguishes from the thing is still a projection, which is why the lineage has to be kept even when the copy is perfect."* A model that fits every recession equally well is not thereby the cause of them.

## 6. Correction to my own §4 and to the usual reading

I initially wrote the "shape, not a name" rule as if it forbade naming either mechanism. That overstates it. The canonical phrase is *"what a **missing** thing would have to look like (a shape, not a name)."* Its subject is a primitive not held in Γ. Both "slow nominal adjustment" and "feedback instability" are already in the economics library; they are held terms, not missing primitives. Naming them is a binding from the economics frame onto the residual, which is allowed provided the provenance is recorded.

What remains forbidden is the stronger move: asserting from the figures alone that one of them is the ground-decided cause. That needs the measurement. Both the earlier and the corrected reading are left visible here so the correction is auditable rather than silent.

The same essay set supplies a second correction. *They're Scanning Books* says *"Aggregation has a ceiling, and it is human"* and opens that section with the image of a hundred geniuses in a room: *"Put a hundred geniuses in a room. The output is not super-genius."* So a broad survey of economists, however distinguished, raises vocabulary and not an anchor. This matches Seat Map :283: *"Adding people raises the vocabulary and does not add an anchor."*

## 7. Prestige is not action

From *The Recursive Self-Improvement Trap* (2026-11-19): *"Prestige must never be allowed to validate an action directly."* And the operative sentence: *"Prestige can fund an action. It can give an action a budget to be tried. But it cannot settle an action. Only the ground can settle it. The Ground Maintainer must be blind to prestige."*

A campaign that gathers distinguished economists to agree with the split is the named collapse, not corroboration. The legitimate ask is the opposite: give the claim a budget to be tried, and let data settle it. Seat Map :171 adds the honest declaration rule: *"an interest in the outcome is unavoidable, an interest in the reading is disqualifying."* I have an interest in the shape claim surviving; that is declared here and carries no verdict weight.

## 8. Why this derivation is a simulation, not a ground reading

The same essay (2026-11-19) states:

> *"LLMs can quickly simulate an approximate answer/output with the formula found in the architecture post. It will often be better than the generic outputs that you will usually get otherwise too (intelligence is essentially arrangement). However, the truly testable output would need to be derived from the real architecture or working the formula yourself."*

*Arrival of the Fittest: The Tether Architecture* (2026-08-19) is blunter: *"Because it is a metatheory, it cannot be validated, nor can it validate itself."* The same article says of the LLM demo: *"the outputs generated must be seen as simulacra"* and that the demonstration *"could never be verified if the solutions presented were not contrived to fit the formula."*

So `01_figure_grounded_audit.md`, `06_isomers_and_order.md`, `07_the_tether_answer.md`, `08_expert_verification_packet.md`, `09_watson_answer.md` and `11_audit.md` are simulated derivations checked for internal consistency against the figures. They are not tests of the economic claim. They cannot settle anything they report on. The one empirical result in this folder, Ho (2025), is cited from a paper I opened; it is a world source, not a figure verdict, and it is `grounded` for that paper's own sample only.

## 9. What the society essay adds about the reader

*How a Society of Agents Would Work* (2026-10-22) says *"capability is a property of agent-and-habitat, never of the agent alone."* For this question the habitat includes the datasets, the journals, the recession counter and the people who can read them. It also warns: *"Ten copies watching the same thing are one opinion said ten times."* That is the reason the outreach artifacts use one-to-one public channels and never a bulk list, and why the second seat in `11_audit.md` declares that its agreement is internal consistency rather than corroboration.
