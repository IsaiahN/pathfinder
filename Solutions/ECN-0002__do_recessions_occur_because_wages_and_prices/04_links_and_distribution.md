## Two findings in the wider literature that bear directly on this question

1. **Rigidity appears on both sides of the debate, which is the union overlap.** OpenStax presents sticky wages and prices as the reason slow adjustment prolongs recessions. Greenwald and Stiglitz present the same wage and price flexibility as something that can *exacerbate* fluctuations, and state that they *"would regard price and wage rigidity more as a symptom of underlying financial and labor market failures and not as a fundamental cause of business cycles."* Tobin goes further and says Keynesian economics does not require nominal rigidity at all, only that markets are not instantaneously cleared. So the two options in the question are not disjoint mechanisms. This is the world-side corroboration of Fig 9 :55, *"A bundled problem is usually a union of overlapping conditions, not a list of separate ones."*
2. **Slow recovery is not a diagnostic of an unstable system.** In ecology, "critical slowing down" is the name for the lengthening return time near a tipping point. Scheffer et al. 2009 treat it as an early warning of a critical transition. Kéfi et al. 2013 correct that reading: *"a decrease in recovery rate cannot be considered as specific to approaching catastrophic shifts."* The same observable, a slow return, can arise without any approaching instability. That is a low-overlap corroboration of the figure rule that each mechanism needs its own slot and its own ground. Note that Scheffer et al. explicitly include financial markets, so this analogue is not fully independent of the economics pool; the overlap is recorded in `10_source_ledger.csv`.

## Distribution: offer, do not push

Figure-grounded reasons:

- **Fig 4 :22**, *"Only methods cross up. Only head starts come down."* Sending the whole framework before a recipient has described a residual sends a recording, not a generator. Lead with the shape claim and its refuter.
- **Fig 10 :42**, *"A verifier that can reconstruct the claim cannot verify it."* A recipient who has absorbed the framework before giving a verdict has joined the frame being checked.
- **Fig 9 :27-28**, *"describe it before you go looking, or any frame you pick will seem to fit."* Ask what they would expect to see if the shape claim were true before showing the sources.
- **Seat Map :163**, *"High correlation is disqualifying for a verdict and efficient for a convention."* A send selected for people already sympathetic produces a convention, not corroboration.

**Recommended order in a reply:** (1) the residual and the shape claim; (2) the pre-registered refuter; (3) ask what they would count as a failure and record the answer before any theory is shown; (4) only then the three links, with the note that the figures, not the summary, are the authority; (5) offer the formula as a method to reuse, with the instruction that each person write their own refuter and state their own mode.

## The two-stage offer

- **Stage 1 before stage 2** is Fig 10 :52, *"Bring a characterised residual, never the source."* The recipient checks the output without holding the framework's interior.
- **Stage 2 is opt-in by construction.** Once a recipient has absorbed the theory, their agreement stops being evidence. Their stage 1 verdict must be recorded first and later agreement is never pooled as corroboration (Fig 2 :40, *"agreement about the world is worthless when the agreeing frames share an evidence pool"*).
- **Stage 3 sends a method, which is permitted** by Fig 4 :22. The formula is a generator, so giving it away does not give away an answer. Each user still needs a ground and a written refuter (formula step "Direction": a frame cannot check the direction of its own question).
- **No request for trust, agreement or prestige.** Recursive Self-Improvement Trap: *"Prestige can fund an action. It can give an action a budget to be tried. But it cannot settle an action. Only the ground can settle it."*
