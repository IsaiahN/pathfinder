I redid the census from the residual line alone, before reading the primary's conclusions, using the WATSON order: R by arity, symmetry and scale; one line per domain with actors, currency and anchor; partition or union; order by dependency.

Arity: two conditions, a disturbance and a persistence mechanism, so a **union compound** unless the intersection is shown empty. Symmetry: asymmetric, because adjustment speed is a rate and instability is a signed feedback structure; they cannot be averaged without a type error. Scale: individual price setting versus aggregate quantities, so the bridge is lossy.

## Round trip gap R_T

The Symbols table defines R_T as the gap between what was sent up and what came back down, `gap(x, (T_E ∘ T_A)(x))`, measuring what the coarser description cannot hold. Fig 4 :30 requires it to be measured rather than assumed. I ran the round trip on the condensation the folder actually uses.

1. **Sent up (x):** the verbatim figure set, chiefly Fig 9 :4-5 and :55-57 (split, union), Fig 1 :19 and :22 (per slot), Fig 4 :27 and :30 (lossy scale transform), Fig 12 :121 (signed loop) and Fig 5 :53 (refusal).
2. **Abstraction (T_A):** the shorthand in `07_the_tether_answer.md`, namely "split before search; measure per slot; measure the round trip; treat instability as a signed loop; refuse the restoration regularity."
3. **Concretisation (T_E):** a concrete claim about an actual economy, for example "the 1981 recession was caused by slow wage adjustment" or "the 2008 recession was caused by financial instability."

**R_T is nonzero.** Four things the shorthand holds do not survive the trip back down:

- the **identity of the slots**, which the shorthand leaves as variables but the concrete claim fills with one mechanism, silently dropping the other;
- the **measured sign of the feedback loop**, which the shorthand says must be measured and the concrete claim asserts without measurement;
- the **round-trip gap itself**, which collapses to zero in the concrete claim when a micro price frequency is treated as aggregate flexibility;
- the **order of refusal before mint**, which Formula step 2 requires and which any hybrid "some sticky, some unstable" composite reverses.

Conversely, one thing survives cleanly: the observation that a single A-or-B question must be split and that per-slot residuals must not be averaged. That is the residue worth sending. R_T was **not** measured numerically because no ground reading exists in this run; it is recorded qualitatively as the enumerated set of dropped constraints. Treating "the formula is the condensed version of the metalaws" as identity would set R_T to zero and would be the dead-reckoning error Fig 4 :30 names. The folder's honest form is a lossy condensation with a measured gap, which `09_watson_answer.md` states.
