**Source:** economics questions.txt line 6

**Field for public contact points:** macroeconomics and price rigidities research

**Status:** done

## Restating the question as an effect

The observed effect is a recession: a persistent shortfall of output and employment relative to trend, dated by an independent counter rather than by either theory. Two candidate mechanisms claim to explain why the shortfall persists.

- **Slow adjustment.** The price and wage channel decays too slowly. A disturbance moves aggregate demand, and because nominal prices and wages do not move fast enough, quantities carry the correction. The predicted cure is faster adjustment.
- **Deeper instability.** The economy amplifies and prolongs disturbances through internal feedback: credit, balance sheets, incomplete markets, coordination failure. Faster adjustment need not cure this and can worsen it.

What an answer would look like, written down and bound as an ordinary term before the run (formula step "Direction"): a result that states, per recession episode and per sector slot, which condition binds, what the measured adjustment speed is, what the measured amplification is, and what the round-trip gap is between micro price setting and the aggregate response. Anything less leaves the question as an unexamined either/or.

## Boundary and honest standing

This folder contains a **simulated derivation**, checked for internal consistency against the figures. It is not a ground reading. The Recursive Self-Improvement Trap (Isaiah N, 2026-11-19) says LLM output of this kind is a simulation and that *"the truly testable output would need to be derived from the real architecture or working the formula yourself."* This package cannot settle the macroeconomic question it reports on. It can only report the shape a defensible answer would have and the measurements that would close it.

Sources used: the 13 figures, Operators table and Symbols table via `FRAMEWORK/reference/03-FIGURES_TEXT.md`; `THE_FORMULA.md`; the local canonical figures README and seat map; the eight essays by Isaiah N, each opened and quoted in `05_what_shape_means.md`; and the public economics and complex-systems sources listed in `10_source_ledger.csv`. No file outside this Solution folder was modified.

## What is deliberately not here

- No personal emails of named individuals.
- No ranking of people by how likely they are to agree with the claim.
- No bulk-send plan.

Three reasons, in the figures' own words:

1. **Agreement is not an anchor.** Fig 2 :35: *"Alignment is triangulation, not negotiation. A mirror cannot correct you."* A list selected for receptiveness is a list selected to agree.
2. **Correlation disqualifies a verdict.** Seat Map :163: *"High correlation is disqualifying for a verdict and efficient for a convention."* Selecting recipients for likely agreement manufactures exactly the correlation that makes their agreement worthless.
3. **A reader who reconstructs the claim cannot verify it.** Fig 10 :42: *"A verifier that can reconstruct the claim cannot verify it."* This is why stage 1 of the offer carries only the residual, the shape claim and the refuter.

Legally: unsolicited bulk mail to scraped personal addresses breaches GDPR and CAN-SPAM style rules in many jurisdictions and gets the sending domain blacklisted. That is a practical failure on top of the structural one.
