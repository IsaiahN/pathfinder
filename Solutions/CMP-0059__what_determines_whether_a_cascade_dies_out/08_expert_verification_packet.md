# Expert verification packet: the Tether on cascade fate

**What this is:** a claim in a form a cascade or contagion researcher can check. Mode, derivation and refuters are stated before the conclusion, so the reader knows what to look for and what would settle it.

**Mode: `specified` on the shape, `general` on the structural rule. Not `grounded`.** No measurement in this folder settles the claim. It is a simulated derivation checked for internal consistency against the figures, not a ground reading.

## 1. The claim, in one paragraph

A cascade's fate is decided at a live slot, not by a global average, so an apparently dying cascade can still restart. The regime is set by the remainder that re-enters the next step: spent gives extinction, bounded gives localization, compounding gives recurrence. Repeated reignition requires a closed loop with an amplifying sign, or a part that survives its own use; these two conditions form a union and keep separate verdicts. A spendable difference is the fuel, and a localization claim holds only as deep as the contact enumeration, because isolation substitutes a habitat rather than deleting contact. The framework supplies this shape and a pre-registered refuter. It does not supply a universal threshold.

## 2. Three checkable sub-claims

### C1. Framework-internal; checkable by reading Figures 1, 12 and 13 together

- **Statement:** the three outcomes are three states of one loop, distinguished by whether the remainder is spent, bounded or compounding; reignition requires a closed loop with a sign.
- **Source, verbatim:** Fig 13 :31-32 "a remainder compounding through a chain that amplifies is chaos." Fig 12 :121 "The output re-enters as input. The sign of the loop decides whether it closes, and a chain cannot close by construction." Fig 1 :19 "The residual is indexed by object slot, not aggregated."
- **Refuter:** repeated reignition in a demonstrated acyclic propagation structure with no residual carrier; or a global cascade with zero live slots at every slot and only a global average reported. Both break the claim.

### C2. Framework-internal; checkable by reading Figure 12

- **Statement:** there are two reignition mechanisms, a closed amplifying loop and a part that survives its own use. They form a union with two loops, not one averaged verdict.
- **Source, verbatim:** Fig 12 :119 "A part that survives its own use. Spent and regenerating parts behave differently across uses, and nothing records which a term is." Fig 9 :54-58 says a union of overlapping conditions must be unbundled before search; a case in two conditions is not a harder case of either.
- **Refuter:** a single lever that removes both mechanisms at once, with no intersection left standing; or repeated reignition in a system where every part is demonstrably spent on first use and no external reintroduction occurs.

### C3. World-facing; checkable against the recipient's own models and data

- **Statement:** the shape predicts that extinction cannot be read from a global average, and that localization is scoping-relative. The world literature has an exact precedent for the first (Whitney's "near death" cascade that later explodes) and a direct warning for the second (DDT reaching eagles three steps out).
- **What to check:** in your own system, is there a slot that stays live while the global reading is near zero? After enumerating contact outward to second and third order, does a declared local cascade remain bounded?
- **Refuter:** a cascade that stays bounded after contact is enumerated outward until the cascade stops mattering, with a recorded list of actors at each order and no substituted habitat.

## 3. What is explicitly not claimed

- Not a universal number, and not R0, a cascade condition or a percolation threshold. Those are imports settled by their own grounds.
- Not that a falling global average proves extinction.
- Not that first-order contact proves localization.
- Not that isolation deletes contact. It substitutes a habitat.
- Not `grounded`. Nothing here is asserted as settled by experiment.
- Not a claim that the framework derives any particular world model. World sources corroborate; they do not set the verdict.

## 5. What would settle it

A ground reading, not a citation. Specifically:

1. Per-slot tracking through an apparent extinction: does a live slot restart the cascade?
2. Outward contact enumeration: does a declared local cascade remain bounded at second and third order?
3. Loop-sign intervention: break the feedback loop with contact unchanged; does reignition stop, or continue through a reservoir?
4. Reservoir record: mark each participant spent or regenerating after one use, and check whether recurrence tracks that mark.

## 6. What an expert can check in twenty minutes

1. Read Fig 1 :13 and :19, Fig 12 :119 and :121, Fig 13 :31-32, Fig 11 :52 and :66. Six short passages.
2. Check C2 against one system you know: can you name both a closed amplifying loop and a part that survives its own use, or only one?
3. Name one instrument that would distinguish a spent remainder from a compounding one at the slot level, or say why none is possible.
4. Send the refutation. A failure is more useful than an endorsement.
