## 1. A shape is what you can state before you can name

The canonical limit: the framework returns *"what a missing thing would have to look like (a shape, not a name)."* Fig 9 :32 "you get a kind of thing, never the answer itself."

From *The Library of Babel*: "you can cut your search short if the set of books returned is small and descriptive enough that it provides a general shape of the answer that satisfies your question."

For this question, a shape is the set of constraints any answer about cascade fate must satisfy: per-slot support, loop sign, spendable difference, surviving part, enumeration depth. A name would be a specific threshold value or a specific model. The shape constrains the answer; it does not supply the number.

## 2. How the shape is obtained

- *Library of Babel*: use the vocabulary closest to the thing, then look for recurring new terms that overlap or correlate with it. "With each new term gained, you are able to narrow the space by definition and deepen your vocabulary."
- Fig 9 :27-28 "describe it before you go looking, or any frame you pick will seem to fit." Describe the effect, then find the frame. In this solution the effect is the regime change, not the contagion mechanism.
- *Decoding Tacit Knowledge*: "When contact isn't possible, the best you can send is a plain description of what you'd expect to see, because anyone can check that against the world." That is the two-stage offer: describe the expected regime signature, let the checker test it, then offer the theory.
- *Library of Babel* on stopping: "you will know that you are done because the finish line will also be the starting place." Here that means the residual stops registering when the same five features predict the regime in a fresh domain without adding a new primitive.

## 3. What licenses a name

Only two things, both outside the shape:

1. **The ground settles it.** *How Intelligence Actually Works*: "It stops when nothing you have registers a difference from it." That is operational, not a claim about truth. Fig 2 :45 "Frames range against the ground. They do not negotiate with it."
2. **A provenanced import from a frame whose closure differs** (Fig 6 :23), with a source gate and a shadow test. R0, the cascade condition and the connectivity phase transition are imports from epidemiology, network science and economics. They are useful, but their warrant is their own ground, not the figures.

*They're Scanning Books When They Should Be Studying Nature* gives the sharpened version: "The real import is not a new term in the existing space. It is a new dimension of the space." Importing a threshold from another field extends what a model can say; it does not add a figure primitive.

## 4. The specific error this question invites

The obvious reading error is to hand a world number back as a figure result: "the figure says R0 greater than 1 means a cascade spreads." *The Hard Problem is About Source* names why that is a projection error: "If several paths create the same behavior, then behavior is a projection with lossy information." An observed regime can be produced by loop closure, by a surviving reservoir, or by first-order scoping that missed a third-order contact. The reading cannot determine the source. "An instrument that can't distinguish these cases demonstrates its own inadequacy, it cannot claim anything substantial about what is being measured or what causes it." What is owed is a discriminator (per-slot measurement plus outward enumeration), not a number.

The second error is the reverse: treating the shape as if it named a quantity. The five features are structural relations, not variables with units. They travel across domains under Fig 4 :20 "Only the actors, the currency and the anchor change."

## 5. Correction to my own reading of "shape, not a name"

I initially read the limit as forbidding *any* specific quantity in an answer. That is too strong. The README's phrase is about *"what a missing thing would have to look like"*: it governs a primitive not held in Γ. Loop sign, catalyst, spendable difference, per-slot residual and contact enumeration are all already in the figure set (Fig 12, Fig 13, Fig 1, Fig 11). Naming them is not inventing a seventh term.

What *is* forbidden is presenting a world threshold as if the framework derived it, and that remains the operative error here. *They're Scanning Books*: "Declaring the primitives does not make them neutral. It makes the choice visible." The five features are a visible choice of structural axes, not a discovery of nature's own list.

Both readings are recorded so neither is quietly lost.

## 6. Consequences for the answer

- **Per-slot, never aggregate.** *How a Society of Agents Would Work* carries the figure rule into practice: "Keep the rate per kind of situation. A method that is brilliant in one setting must not be drowned by an average across all of them." Fig 1 :19 "The residual is indexed by object slot, not aggregated."
- **Prestige is not action.** *The Recursive Self-Improvement Trap*: "Prestige can fund an action. It can give an action a budget to be tried. But it cannot settle an action. Only the ground can settle it." A list of famous cascade researchers cannot settle the shape; it can only give it a hearing.
- **This audit is a simulation, not a ground reading.** Same essay: "the output is only as good as the validator. The agent, or the tool, or the frame, or the theory, cannot validate itself." And: "the truly testable output would need to be derived from the real architecture or working the formula yourself." *Arrival of the Fittest* adds the warning that a simulated solution "could never be verified if the solutions presented were not contrived to fit the formula." So `01` through `09` are simulated derivations checked for internal consistency against the figures. They cannot settle this question.
- **Indiscernibility is not identity.** *How Intelligence Actually Works*: "The recursion converges toward indiscernibility, and indiscernibility is not identity." A model that reproduces the three regimes is not thereby the mechanism that produced them. The lineage must be kept.
