# The Tether's answer: what decides a cascade's fate

**Mode: `specified` on the shape, `general` on the structural rule. Not `grounded`.** Simulated derivation checked for internal consistency against the figures, not a ground reading (`05_what_shape_means.md` §6).

## 1. The residual it answers

We want to predict which of three regimes a disturbance enters: extinction, a bounded region, or repeated return. The answer must be in effect terms, because importing a named threshold from one field and applying it to another is exactly the error the figures forbid.

## 2. The shape

1. **Support is per slot, never an average.** Fig 1 :19 "The residual is indexed by object slot, not aggregated." Fig 1 :13 "a global R near zero with one live slot residual is a legal state." A cascade is decided at a live slot, including when the global reading is quiet. A "near death" cascade can still explode.

2. **The remainder decides the regime.** Fig 13 :31-32 "a remainder compounding through a chain that amplifies is chaos." When nothing remains after each step (Fig 13 :71 "no direction to drive the flow, and no remainder left after each step"), the cascade dies. When the remainder re-enters without crossing a scale, it is localized. When it compounds through an amplifying chain, it returns.

3. **Reignition needs a closed loop with the right sign; an open chain cannot close.** Fig 12 :121 "The output re-enters as input. The sign of the loop decides whether it closes, and a chain cannot close by construction." The sign, not the mere presence of feedback, is the determining feature.

4. **Reignition also has a second, independent mechanism.** Fig 12 :119 "A part that survives its own use. Spent and regenerating parts behave differently across uses, and nothing records which a term is." A reservoir or endemic pool makes recurrence possible; a one-shot part does not. These two mechanisms are a union compound and get separate verdicts, never an average.

5. **The fuel is a difference that can be spent.** Fig 13 :46 "What makes a gradient a gradient is that it can be spent." When the difference is exhausted, propagation stops even in a still-connected network. In each domain this difference has a different currency: susceptible hosts, unexposed balance sheet capacity, unconverted neighbours. Fig 4 :20 "Only the actors, the currency and the anchor change."

6. **A local claim is only as deep as the enumeration.** Fig 11 :52 "And contact cascades. Scoping the habitat to direct contact is how DDT reached the eagles." Localization declared at first order can be a third-order effect in disguise. And Fig 11 :66 "Isolation is not removal of the habitat. It is substitution of one habitat for another." A quarantine replaces a habitat and inherits actors nobody chose.

7. **The framework gives a shape, not a universal number.** Fig 9 :32 "you get a kind of thing, never the answer itself." R0, the cascade condition and the connectivity phase transition are world names for the comparison in particular closures. They are settled by their own grounds and are not interchangeable.

### Verdicts per domain, kept separate

| domain | determining structure | verdict | mode |
|---|---|---|---|
| D1 seed and residual | live mass at a slot | extinction cannot be declared from a global average | `general` |
| D2 contact / habitat | depth of enumeration | localization is a scoping-relative claim | `specified` |
| D3 response and loop | sign of closure | reignition requires an amplifying closed loop | `general` |

No row is averaged with another. The two D3 rows are a union; if one fires and the other does not, the compound still reignites.

## 3. What it explicitly does not say

- Not a single universal threshold. The figures constrain the form of the answer and stop there.
- Not "dies out" from a falling global average. A live slot can sit inside a near-zero global residual.
- Not "localized" from first-order contact alone. Enumerate outward until the cascade stops mattering.
- Not that isolation deletes contact. It substitutes a habitat.
- Not a derivation of R0, a cascade condition or a percolation threshold. Those are imports with their own provenance.
- Not `grounded`. No measurement in this folder settles any of the seven points.

## 4. What would settle it, the ground

1. Per-slot tracking through an apparent extinction: does any slot retain live mass that later restarts the cascade? A "near death then explode" observation would confirm the per-slot rule and refute a global-average rule.
2. Outward enumeration: after listing actors at first, second and third order, does a declared local cascade remain bounded? If not, it was a scoping artifact.
3. Loop-sign intervention: break the feedback loop while leaving contact unchanged. If reignition stops, the sign claim is supported. If it continues through a surviving reservoir, the union claim is supported instead.
4. Reservoir test: record whether each participating part is spent or regenerating after one use. A model that cannot record this cannot predict recurrence.
5. Standing refuters from `01_figure_grounded_audit.md` §4, written before the run. Any one firing withdraws the shape.

## 5. The honest stop

**Unreached, not unreachable.** The eight-essay reading is explicit: "Declaring the primitives does not make them neutral. It makes the choice visible." The five features are a visible choice of structural axes, not nature's own list. Another instrument set could make different features salient. The answer stops at what the available figures can distinguish and records the lineage so the stop is checkable. Indiscernibility is not identity.

## 6. One-paragraph version for outreach

> The Tether does not give a number for when a cascade dies. It gives the shape any answer must have. A cascade is decided at a live slot, not by a global average, so apparent extinction can hide a restart. The outcome depends on the remainder that re-enters the next step: spent, bounded, or compounding. Repeated reignition needs a closed loop with an amplifying sign, or a part that survives its own use; these are two conditions that can overlap, and their verdicts are kept apart. A spendable difference is the fuel, and when it is gone the cascade stops even if the network is intact. Localization is only as deep as the contact enumeration, because isolation substitutes a habitat rather than deleting contact. The framework supplies this shape and a pre-registered refuter, then stops; the threshold belongs to the domain's own ground.
