## DOMAIN CENSUS

R by arity, symmetry and scale, then one line per domain giving actors / currency / anchor, partition or union, and order by dependency.

- **R (the residual).** The failure to predict which regime a perturbation enters. Arity: two or more, an initiator plus a medium of slots under a local response rule. Symmetry: the cascade is ordered in time (a source fires before its neighbours), even when the contact graph is undirected. Scale: step, cycle, patch or network, lineage of repeated episodes.
- **Domain 1, seed and residual.** Actors: seed, slots, observations. Currency: per-slot residual and live mass. Anchor: a measurement of whether a slot actually flipped. Nameable, so it qualifies.
- **Domain 2, contact and habitat.** Actors: nodes, edges, conditions, relations. Currency: contact and enumeration depth. Anchor: the enumerated world, read off and never composed. Nameable, so it qualifies.
- **Domain 3, response and loop.** Actors: threshold, recovery, injection, reservoir. Currency: sign of the loop and spent versus regenerating parts. Anchor: a measurement of whether the loop closes and whether a part survives. Nameable, so it qualifies.
- **Domain 4, verification.** Actors: researchers, models, instruments. Currency: agreement versus residual. Anchor: the ground, never agreement.
- **Partition or union.** The three outcomes partition at a measurement. "Repeatedly reignites" is a union compound of a closed amplifying loop and a part that survives its own use; overlapping conditions need two loops and two verdicts.
- **Order by dependency (Fig 3).** Per-slot support, then contact enumeration, then loop sign, then surviving part, then scale crossing. A reading below a break is a reading of nothing.

## VERDICT (from the figures)

The shape survives; no universal number does. A cascade is decided at a live slot, so extinction cannot be read from a global average. The regime is set by the remainder that re-enters: spent gives die-out, bounded gives localization, compounding gives recurrence. Reignition requires a closed amplifying loop or a part that survives its own use, and these are a union with two verdicts, never an average. A spendable difference is the fuel, and a localization claim holds only as deep as outward contact enumeration, because isolation substitutes a habitat rather than deleting contact. The figures supply this shape and the pre-registered refuter; the threshold belongs to a domain's ground. This is `specified` and ungrounded, candidate.

- **cost if wrong:** bounded and locatable. The packet states mode, refuters and provenance before the conclusion, so an error is findable rather than invisible. The real cost is spending the independence of outside readers by sending a finished conclusion instead of a generator. Fig 2 :42 "Buy independence in proportion to the cost of being wrong." That cost is avoidable by leading with the shape and its refuter.
- **undoable:** yes. The world thresholds are removable without touching the shape, and the shape itself is stated as candidate with its refuters attached. Nothing in this solution asserts a number that would have to be withdrawn. A sent claim can be superseded by a corrected packet, and no primitive has been withdrawn.
- **reversed by:** a ground reading, not agreement. Per-slot tracking showing a live slot that restarts a cascade would confirm the per-slot rule and refute a global-average rule; outward contact enumeration that leaves a declared local cascade bounded would support localization; a loop-sign intervention that stops reignition with contact unchanged would confirm the sign claim, while continued reignition through a reservoir would support the union claim; a cascade that repeatedly reignites with a demonstrated acyclic structure and no surviving part would break the shape claim. Until one of these fires, the verdict stands as `candidate`.
