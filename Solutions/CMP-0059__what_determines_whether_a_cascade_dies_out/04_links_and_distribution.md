## Two things in the literature that bear directly on this question

1. **Whitney 2009/2010 is the sharpest warning against an early verdict.** A cascade can appear close to death and then explode. This is the world-side counterpart of Fig 1 :13 "a global R near zero with one live slot residual is a legal state". It is why the outreach never accepts a global average as evidence of extinction.
2. **Acemoglu et al. show that the controlling structure changes sign with the shock size.** The same interbank connections enhance stability for small shocks and become the propagation mechanism past a point. That is a world-side instance of Fig 12 :121 "The output re-enters as input. The sign of the loop decides whether it closes, and a chain cannot close by construction." It is corroboration, not a figure derivation.

## Distribution: offer, do not push

- Fig 4 :22-25 *"Only methods cross up. Only head starts come down. A recording carried upward looks like knowledge and is a description of one occasion."* Send the generator, the shape claim and its refuter. Do not send a finished conclusion first.
- Fig 10 :42 "A verifier that can reconstruct the claim cannot verify it." A recipient who has absorbed the framework before being asked for a verdict has joined the frame being checked.
- Fig 9 :27-28 "describe it before you go looking, or any frame you pick will seem to fit." Ask what they would expect to see if the shape claim were true before showing the figures.
- Fig 9 :60 "Use filters for the budget and witnesses for the verdict. Never let a filter hand you a verdict." A list ranked by likely agreement is a budget device, never a witness set.
- Fig 2 :40 "agreement about the world is worthless when the agreeing frames share an evidence pool." Later agreement is never pooled as corroboration.

**Recommended order in a reply:** (1) the shape claim and its five refuters; (2) ask what they would count as a failure, and record the answer; (3) only then the three links, with the note that the figures, not the summary, are the authority.

## The three-stage offer

1. **Stage 1, residual and refuter.** The recipient checks the shape against their own ground without holding the framework's interior. This is Fig 10 :52 "Bring a characterised residual, never the source."
2. **Stage 2, opt-in only.** If they are curious, offer the figure links. Their stage-1 verdict is already on record; any later agreement is a conversation, not a witness.
3. **Stage 3, method.** Offer the formula as a generator with the instruction that each user writes their own refuter and states their own mode before running it. `THE_FORMULA.md` "Direction": a frame cannot check the direction of its own question, because the shape it is searching for is not a term it holds.

**Hard precondition:** stages 1 to 3 only work if the stage-1 output survives. Nothing in this folder has been settled by a measurement, so it is offered as a checkable candidate, never as a result.
