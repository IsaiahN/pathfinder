## The residual in effect terms

A perturbation enters a network and the observed response falls into one of three regimes: it decays to nothing, it stays inside a bounded set of slots, or it returns repeatedly after apparently stopping. The residual is the failure to predict which regime will occur from what we hold. An answer must give the determining structure, not merely a label for the outcome, and it must say which part of the answer is a shape the figures supply and which part is a quantity the world must measure.

## What an answer would look like

Bind the sought-for shape as an ordinary term before the run: the fate of a cascade is a function of (a) per-slot residual support, (b) the sign of the feedback loop, (c) whether a spendable driving difference remains, (d) whether a part survives its own use as a reservoir, and (e) how far contact must be enumerated. The figures constrain this form; they do not supply a number.
