## 0. The residual, in effect terms

A perturbation is introduced. We observe one of three outcomes: the disturbance vanishes, it stays inside a bounded region, or it returns again after apparently stopping. The residual is the gap between what our current vocabulary predicts and which outcome occurs. It is not "what is the reproduction number" (that is a world quantity), but "what structural features determine the regime".

Stated as a sought-for shape before the run: the regime is a function of per-slot support, feedback-loop sign, remaining spendable difference, presence of a part that survives its own use, and the depth of contact enumeration. This is an ordinary term, bound here so it can accrue a residual and be refused.

## 1. Domain census, before any figure line is chosen

### R by arity, symmetry, scale (Fig 9)

- **Arity.** Two or more: an initiator plus a medium of slots under a local response rule. A seed with no medium is not a cascade.
- **Symmetry.** The cascade itself is ordered: a source fires before its neighbours, so the bond is sequence, not a symmetric join. The contact graph may be undirected under simple exposure, but the event is directed in time.
- **Scale.** Step (one transmission), cycle (feedback closure), patch or network (bounded spread), lineage (repeated episodes). A regime change happens when something crosses from one scale to the next.

### One line per domain: actors / currency / anchor

| domain | actors | currency | anchor (nameable?) |
|---|---|---|---|
| D1 seed and residual | seed, slots, observations | per-slot residual R_s, live mass R+_s | yes: a measurement of whether a slot actually flipped |
| D2 contact / habitat | nodes, edges, conditions, relations | contact, enumeration depth | yes: the enumerated world, read off not composed |
| D3 response and loop | threshold, recovery, injection | sign of the loop, spent or unspent difference | yes: a measurement of whether the loop closes |
| D4 verification | researchers, models, instruments | agreement versus residual | yes: the ground, never agreement |

No anchorless line qualifies. "Systemic risk" alone is not a domain; it has no anchor until a measurement is named.

### Partition or union

- The three **outcomes** are a partition at a measurement: one run settles into one regime.
- The **mechanisms** of repeated reignition are a **union compound**: a closed amplifying loop and a part that survives its own use are two conditions that can overlap. Per Fig 9 :54-58, a bundled union must be unbundled before searching, because "a case in two conditions is not a harder case of either". Each mechanism gets its own loop and its own verdict.

### Order by dependency (Fig 3)

1. Is there per-slot support (D1). 2. What is in contact with the residual (D2). 3. Does the loop close, and with what sign (D3). 4. Does any part survive its own use (D3). 5. Does the remainder compound across the scale boundary (D2 to D3). A reading taken below a break is a reading of nothing, so step 1 is not skipped.

## 3. Findings

### F1. The three regimes are three states of one loop, distinguished by whether the remainder decays (decisive, triangulated)

Fig 13 :31-32: "a remainder compounding through a chain that amplifies is chaos"

Fig 13 :71: "no direction to drive the flow, and no remainder left after each step"

The remainder that re-enters the next turn is the cascade's own output. When no direction drives the flow and nothing is left after each step, the chain terminates: that is **dies out**. When the remainder is carried forward without compounding beyond a bounded set of slots, the chain has run but not crossed: that is **localized**. When the remainder compounds through a chain that amplifies, the loop has closed with a positive sign: that is **repeatedly reignites**.

Refuter: show two runs with identical per-slot support, identical loop sign and identical remaining difference that settle into different regimes with no change in contact enumeration. That would break the claim that these are states of one loop.

Status: **triangulated**. Fig 13 (substrate chain) and Fig 12 (bond structure, F2) are low-overlap routes to the same distinction.

### F2. Reignition requires a closed loop whose sign is positive (decisive, single-source on the figure)

Fig 12 :121: "The output re-enters as input. The sign of the loop decides whether it closes, and a chain cannot close by construction."

An open chain cannot close, so it cannot reignite; it terminates. A loop that closes with a damping sign settles. A loop that closes with an amplifying sign repeats. The figure makes the sign, not the mere presence of feedback, the determining feature. This is why two networks with the same average degree can have different fates.

Refuter: produce a cascade that repeatedly reignites with a demonstrated acyclic propagation structure and no residual carrier. Fig 12 :121 forbids it.

Status: **single-source** for the figure definition (Fig 12), corroborated by the signed-network world source in `09_watson_answer.md` (Lee et al., 2023). Agreement from a secondary source does not make a second figure.

### F3. Repeated reignition has a second, distinct mechanism: a part that survives its own use

Fig 12 :119: "A part that survives its own use. Spent and regenerating parts behave differently across uses, and nothing records which a term is."

This is the catalyst structure. A spent part gives one cascade; a regenerating part gives recurrent ones. In contagion terms this is a reservoir, a seed bank, an endemic pool, an immune-waning class or a failed institution that is still connected. The figure adds a checkable warning: nothing in the notation records which a term is, so a model that lists actors without recording regenerating versus spent cannot predict recurrence.

Refuter: show repeated reignition in a system where every participating part is demonstrably spent on first use and no external reintroduction occurs.

Status: **single-source** for the figure. This mechanism is a union partner with F2, not a refinement of it; keep both verdicts.

### F4. The fuel is a difference that can be spent

Fig 13 :46: "What makes a gradient a gradient is that it can be spent"

Fig 13 :22: "a difference that can be spent"

Fig 13 :24: "that entities relate, and influence travels"

A cascade needs directedness and a medium that relates entities. When the difference is spent, propagation stops regardless of how many edges remain. This maps across domains under the framework's own rule, Fig 4 :20: "Only the actors, the currency and the anchor change." In epidemiology the spent difference is the susceptible pool; in finance it is the unexposed balance sheet capacity; in a social cascade it is the number of unconverted neighbours.

Refuter: a cascade that continues after the driving difference is measured to be fully spent.

Status: **triangulated** across Fig 13 (substrate) and Fig 4 (domain-agnostic rule). The specific currency in each domain remains `specified`, unmeasured.

### F5. Localization is a per-slot reading, not an aggregate reading

Fig 1 :19: "The residual is indexed by object slot, not aggregated."

Fig 1 :13: "a global R near zero with one live slot residual is a legal state"

A cascade that is localized is not a small version of a global cascade. It is a live slot inside an otherwise quiet system. Aggregation hides exactly the signal that determines the fate: Fig 1 :22 "Aggregating across slots is how a live signal disappears into an average." The practical instruction is to read slot by slot and never to declare die-out from a global average.

Refuter: a system declared localized that has zero live slots at every measurement while a later measurement shows renewed spread from a slot that was never live.

Status: **triangulated** with Fig 11 (contact enumeration, F6) under the world source Whitney 2009/2010, which reports a "near death" cascade that later explodes.

### F6. A declared local cascade may still be three steps out

Fig 11 :52: "And contact cascades. Scoping the habitat to direct contact is how DDT reached the eagles"

Fig 11 :43-44: "The habitat is enumerated, never composed." and "List everything in contact with the residual, then what is in contact with those, and outward until the cascade stops mattering."

Localization claims are only as deep as the enumeration. First-order scoping misses second- and third-order effects. The figure's DDT example is a direct precedent: the residual that mattered was three steps out. Isolation does not fix this by deleting contact: Fig 11 :66 "Isolation is not removal of the habitat. It is substitution of one habitat for another." A quarantine or a ring fence replaces one habitat with another and inherits actors that were not chosen.

Refuter: a cascade declared localized that remains bounded after contact is enumerated outward until the cascade stops mattering, with a recorded list of actors at each order.

Status: **triangulated** (Fig 11 habitat rule plus Fig 4 round trip). The claim that any particular cascade is localized is world-measured, not figure-decided.

### F7. The determining quantity is a shape, not a universal number

Fig 4 :20: "Only the actors, the currency and the anchor change."

Fig 9 :32: "you get a kind of thing, never the answer itself"

The figures supply the invariant structure (support, loop sign, spendable difference, surviving part, enumeration depth). They do not supply a single threshold value that works in every domain. The world supplies names for the comparison in particular closures: R0 in epidemiology, a cascade condition in threshold models, a connectivity phase transition in financial networks. Those names are not figure derivations and are not interchangeable. Fig 6 :23 "No frame escapes its own closure. The bound moves only on contact with a frame whose closure differs."

Refuter: exhibit a single number, defined without reference to actors, currency or anchor, that correctly predicts all three regimes in epidemiology, finance and social contagion without importing a domain-specific definition.

Status: **triangulated** (Fig 4 plus Fig 9; the canonical figures README agrees).

### F8. Strain and re-census: the mint guards govern term-minting, not cascades

Fig 5 :33: "The three guards are a product, not a checklist."

Fig 5 :41: "Any one of them at zero stops the mint entirely."

This looks like a perfect analogue of takeoff conditions, and it was tried first. Strain test: the guards rank a candidate term for admission to a library; they do not rank the fate of a disturbance in a network. The quantity governed is term admissibility, not cascade regime. Re-census required. After re-census the line is **declined as a governing figure line** for this question. It is retained in `06_isomers_and_order.md` only as a labelled structural analogue, and it contributes to no verdict and no triangulation count.

Status: **strain resolved by re-census**. Recorded visibly rather than smoothed into the answer.

## 4. Pre-registered refuter (written before the conclusion)

A falsifier written before the run is a design instrument. The shape claim is refuted if any of the following holds:

1. A cascade repeatedly reignites in a system with a demonstrated acyclic propagation structure and no part that survives its own use (breaks F2 and F3).
2. A cascade dies out while a spendable driving difference is measured to remain and a closed amplifying loop is present (breaks F1 and F4).
3. Global spread occurs while the per-slot residual is zero at every slot and the global average is the whole report (breaks F5).
4. A single number, defined without reference to actors, currency or anchor, settles all three regimes in every domain (breaks F7).
5. A cascade declared localized remains bounded after contact is enumerated outward until the cascade stops mattering, with no substituted habitat (breaks F6).

If any fires, the shape is withdrawn. Until one is run, nothing here is `grounded`.

## 5. Recommendation

1. Lead with the shape and the refuter, not with a threshold formula. The formula belongs to a domain's ground.
2. Read per slot. Never declare die-out from a global average (F5).
3. Unbundle "reignites" into loop closure and surviving reservoir before modelling; they are a union with two loops (F2, F3).
4. Enumerate contact outward before calling anything localized (F6).
5. Round trip any condensation of these figures into shorthand and measure what the shorthand drops. Fig 4 :30: "So take the round trip and measure the gap. Without it you are navigating by dead reckoning: confident about a position nobody checked."
6. Use the public venues in `03_public_contact_points.csv`. Ask for a refutation, not agreement. Fig 2 :40: "agreement about the world is worthless when the agreeing frames share an evidence pool."
