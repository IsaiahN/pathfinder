# Structure, order and analogues: a supporting derivation

**Mode: `specified`.** Simulated derivation checked against the figures, not a ground reading (see `05_what_shape_means.md` §6).

## RESIDUAL

"Repeatedly reignites" names at least two mechanisms, and "localized" may be either a genuine bounded regime or a scoping artifact. Before modelling, decide the structure: is this one question with three outcomes, or several questions bound together? And in what order must the parts be settled?

## DOMAIN CENSUS

Atoms held: a seed, a slot, contact, a response rule, a remainder, and a threshold. Bonds held: sequence (→), comparison (⋛), cycle (output re-enters as input), catalyst (a part that survives its own use). No new primitive is needed. The world thresholds (R0, cascade condition, phase transition) are imports from other closures, not new figure atoms.

## DERIVATION

### Q1. Are the three regimes isomers, or one thing in three states?

Fig 12 :117 "Same atoms, different bond, different substance." And the full definition continues: "The difference is invisible in the formula and decisive in the behaviour."

The three regimes can be written over the same atom set:

| regime | bond structure | what the bond does |
|---|---|---|
| dies out | open sequence | output does not re-enter; the remainder is spent |
| localized | cycle that closes only within a patched habitat | output re-enters locally but does not cross the scale boundary |
| reignites | amplifying cycle, plus optionally a surviving catalyst | output re-enters with a sign that compounds |

So the regimes are **isomers of one atom set under different bond structures**, not three different substances with three different ingredient lists. This is a figure-grounded reason to model them in one framework and compare them under one threshold relation rather than writing three unrelated models. It is also a warning: a report that lists only the ingredients (nodes, edges, seed size) has recorded half of itself, because the bond is not recoverable from the operands.

No new atom is licensed by this move. Fig 12 :104 "A settled molecule becomes an atom for whatever composes over it." A settled regime becomes an operand for the next comparison, which is how repeated episodes relate to one another.

### Q2. Is "reignites" a partition or a union?

Fig 9 :54-58: a bundled problem is usually "a union of overlapping conditions", and "a case in two conditions is not a harder case of either. It stops being a mixture and becomes a specific compound."

Two mechanisms can produce reignition: a closed amplifying loop (Fig 12 :121), and a part that survives its own use (Fig 12 :119). They can overlap. So reignition is a **union compound**, with two loops. A single lever that removes the loop leaves the reservoir standing, and vice versa. Fig 9 :55-56: "A single lever clears the region where only one condition holds and leaves the intersections standing, because the second condition still binds."

Verdict: keep two separate verdicts for reignition, never averaged. `general` as a structural claim, `specified` as applied to any particular system.

### Q3. Order by dependency (Fig 3)

Fig 3 :20-21: "The chain is ordered by dependency, not by importance. Each link consumes what the one above produces, so effort spent above the break arrives at a step that cannot use it."

The cascade chain, upstream first:

1. **Per-slot residual.** Is there live mass at any slot? (Fig 1 :13)
2. **Contact enumeration.** What is in contact with that slot, and what is in contact with those? (Fig 11 :43-44)
3. **Response rule and loop sign.** Does the remainder re-enter, and with what sign? (Fig 12 :121)
4. **Surviving part.** Is any participant spent on first use, or regenerating? (Fig 12 :119)
5. **Scale crossing.** Does the bounded cycle cross into a new habitat? (Fig 11 :66)

A measurement taken at step 4 while step 2 is unmeasured is not a weak reading; it is "a reading of nothing" (Fig 3 :22-23). Most practical effort goes into step 3 because that is where the models already exist, which is exactly the failure Fig 3 :29-30 describes.

### Q4. The mint-guard analogue, tried and declined

Fig 5 :33 "The three guards are a product, not a checklist." Fig 5 :41 "Any one of them at zero stops the mint entirely."

These lines look like an exact description of cascade takeoff, and they were tried as the governing line for F1 in `01_figure_grounded_audit.md`. Strain test: the guards rank a candidate *term* for admission to a library (support, reachability, novelty); they do not rank the fate of a disturbance in a network. The quantity governed is term admissibility. Re-census: F8 in the audit declines the line as a governing figure line. It is kept here only as a labelled structural analogue, and it contributes to no verdict and no triangulation count.

This is the visible correction the method requires: a line that fits only with a strain governs another quantity, so it goes back to census rather than into the answer.

### Q5. Round trip on the condensation (Fig 4)

Fig 4 :30 "So take the round trip and measure the gap. Without it you are navigating by dead reckoning: confident about a position nobody checked."

1. **Sent up (x):** the verbatim figure set, chiefly Fig 12 :121 and :119, Fig 13 :31-32, Fig 1 :19 and Fig 11 :52.
2. **Abstraction (T_A):** the shorthand in `07_the_tether_answer.md`: "support, sign, spendable difference, surviving part, enumeration depth decide the regime".
3. **Concretisation (T_E):** a world model, for example R0 greater than 1 means spread.

**R_T is nonzero.** The shorthand drops at least three things that the world model needs and the figures warn about:

- the per-slot indexing (a global R0 has no slot);
- the union of two reignition mechanisms (a single reproduction number cannot distinguish loop closure from a surviving reservoir);
- the isolation law, substitution rather than removal of habitat.

Conversely, what survives the trip is the shape itself: a remainder must re-enter, and the sign of that re-entry decides closure. R_T is recorded qualitatively as this enumerated set of dropped constraints, not measured numerically, because no ground reading exists here. Treating "the formula is the condensed version of the figures" as identity would set R_T to zero and would be the dead-reckoning error Fig 4 :30 names.

## FALSIFIER, written before the run

The structural derivation is refuted if any of the following holds:

1. The three regimes cannot be written over one atom set under different bond structures; each requires its own primitives.
2. A single lever removes both reignition mechanisms at once, with no intersection left standing.
3. A chain measured below step 1 (with per-slot support unmeasured) predicts the regime as well as one measured above it.
4. The round trip from the figure set to a world model and back loses nothing, that is, R_T is zero.

If any fires, this supporting derivation is withdrawn. Until one is run, nothing here is `grounded`.
