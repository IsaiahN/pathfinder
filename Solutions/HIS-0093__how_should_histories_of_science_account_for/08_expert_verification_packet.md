## 1. The claim, in one paragraph

A history of science that stays checkable has the shape of a lineage record. It keeps failed and
abandoned work in the archive as refusals, so a reader can tell never-tried from tried-and-refused.
It prices results in two currencies: the description that survived and the actions spent to
establish it. It locates chance discovery in an instrument already returning a reading that failed
to resolve, with an observer prepared to notice it. It distinguishes three reasons a program ends:
refuted, unreachable at the budget, and no proto-instrument anywhere. It keeps the past ground from
updating on the present outcome. And it reports what could have been concluded and what would have
had to be true for the answer to differ. These are constraints on a history, not a verdict about a
named episode. Five refuters were written before the derivation.

## 2. Three checkable sub-claims

### C1 : framework-internal; checkable by reading Figures 6 and 12

- **Statement:** a history that prunes refusals and action costs makes the counterfactual
  uncomputable. Failed work must remain in the record as a refusal, and results must be priced in
  bits and actions separately.
- **Source, verbatim:** Fig 6 :42 *"What is recorded only grows. What is reachable is derived, and it
  can fall."* Fig 6 :43 *"A refutation adds a term that refuses; it removes nothing, and the reach
  falls because something was added rather than because anything was deleted."* Fig 12 :125
  *"A composition costs in two currencies, and they do not add."* Fig 12 :126 *"Bits price the
  description. Actions price finding out whether it holds."*
- **Refuter:** show an archive that reconstructs the counterfactual correctly while recording no
  refusals or action costs, or show that the final description carries its own establishment cost.

### C2 : history-facing; checkable by a historian against a dated record

- **Statement:** chance discovery and abandonment are distinguishable at the ground. A chance event
  requires an instrument already returning a failing reading and a prepared observer. An ended
  program can be refuted, unreachable at the budget, or without any returning instrument. These
  three states are not interchangeable and must not be collapsed into "failed".
- **Source, verbatim:** Fig 6 :34-35 *"Where nothing is, there is no edge to extend from, and the
  search for a proto-instrument is a search for one that is already there."* Fig 8 :39 *"So a search
  that finds nothing is never a proof of absence."* Fig 8 :40-43 *"Underivable-in-principle and
  unreachable-at-this-budget are different facts, and they issue different instructions."*
- **What to check:** pick a dated episode. Is there evidence of an instrument already returning a
  failing reading before the discovery was recognized? Can the ending be classified by the dated
  record into one of the three states, or does the record not decide it? If the record does not
  decide, that is itself the honest report, not a defect of the shape.
- **Refuter:** exhibit a discovery with no instrument at any resolution returning a failing reading,
  or a case where "we searched and found nothing" legitimately proves absence.

### C3 : historiography-facing; checkable by reading Figures 2 and 13

- **Statement:** a narrative written backward from the present lets the past ground update on the
  outcome, which is coherent drift. What a history owes instead is the lineage: the route, the
  refused alternatives, and what would have had to be true for the answer to differ.
- **Source, verbatim:** Fig 2 :33 *"The anchor must not update."* Fig 2 :34 *"If it does, panel 3
  turns into panel 1: the anchor becomes another frame, everything drifts together, and the drift is
  coherent, which is why nobody notices."* Fig 13 :140-141 *"not only what was concluded, but what
  could have been concluded, and what would have had to be true for the answer to differ."*
- **Refuter:** show that the past ground legitimately updates on the present outcome, so presentism
  is not drift; or show a result from which route and counterfactual are recoverable by inspection
  with no lineage.

## 3. What is explicitly not claimed

- Not a verdict about any named discovery, program or historian. The framework returns a shape, not
  a name. Naming a specific episode as lucky or a program as impossible is a ground judgment.
- Not "every failure was useful" and not "abandoned programs should be revived". A refusal keeps its
  place in the record and loses its standing to be composed over.
- Not that the archive is the ground. The archive is the record; the ground is the dated artifact and
  the then-available instrument reading.
- Not a periodization, a canon of great episodes, or a replacement for archival method.
- Not `grounded`. Nothing here is asserted as settled by measurement.
- No claim that world sources corroborate independently. Most share evidence pools, and that is
  declared in `09_watson_answer.md` and `10_source_ledger.csv`.

## 5. What would settle it

A ground reading, not agreement. Select a dated episode and test the five refuters in
`01_figure_grounded_audit.md` §4 against the primary record. The world literature supplies partial
instruments already: registered reports date a protocol before the outcome; publication-bias
