## QUESTION

Decide, in effect terms, what a checkable history of science must contain when it accounts for
chance discoveries, failed projects and abandoned research programs. Option A: write a success-only
narrative that reports the published results and treats the three items as colour. Option B: write a
lineage record that keeps refusals and action costs, locates chance in an already-returning
instrument, distinguishes three kinds of ending, refuses to let the past ground update on the
present, and reports the counterfactual. Option A produces a coherent story in which the present
looks inevitable and the reader cannot tell never-tried from tried-and-refused. Option B produces a
checkable shape with five pre-registered refuters and an honest stop at the limit of the record.

## DOMAIN CENSUS

R by arity, symmetry and scale, then one line per domain with actors / currency / anchor, partition
or union, and order by dependency.

- **R (the residual).** The gap between the finished record and the generative process that produced
  it. Arity: three overlapping conditions (chance, failure, abandonment), so a union compound by
  Fig 9 :54, not a list of three separate topics. Symmetry: the three are not interchangeable; they
  are different bins with different remedies (NOVEL, REFUTED, unreachable-at-budget or lost
  channel). Scale: the research episode is first order, the historical narrative is second order.
- **Domain 1, the archive.** Actors: documents, instruments, notebooks, correspondence, published
  papers. Currency: bits, what is described. Anchor: the dated primary artifact, which does not move
  when the narrative changes. Question type: partition (what stayed in the record).
- **Domain 2, the research episode at the time.** Actors: researchers, instruments, hypotheses, the
  habitat. Currency: actions spent and residuals encountered. Anchor: the instrument reading in its
  own time. Question type: union (chance, failure and abandonment overlap inside one loop).
- **Domain 3, the historiographical narrative.** Actors: historian, categories, periodization,
  readers. Currency: coherence and agreement. Anchor: must be the dated primary record, never
  present consensus. If the anchor updates on the outcome, it becomes a frame and the drift is
  coherent. Question type: partition (what was knowable then versus known now).
- **Order by dependency.** Enumerate the habitat before selecting actors; date the record before
  narrating; describe the residual before naming a frame; refuse or classify a stopped path before
  minting a success narrative; settle first-order episodes before composing the second-order
  narrative.

## VERDICT (from the figures)

The question is answerable in shape, not a gap. The shape is a lineage record. Domain 1: keep
refusals and stopped programs in the archive, because a deleting store cannot compute the
counterfactual. Domain 2: price results in bits and actions; locate chance in an already-returning
instrument and a prepared observer; distinguish refuted, unreachable-at-budget and no-instrument.
Domain 3: do not let the past ground update on the present; report the route, the refused
alternatives and what would have had to be true for the answer to differ. Cross-domain: unbundle the
union before applying any single lever. Verdicts are per domain and are never averaged. Mode:
`specified`, ungrounded, candidate. The prescriptive shape stands; no particular episode is judged.

- **cost if wrong:** bounded and locatable. The packet states mode, five refuters and provenance
  before the conclusion, so an error is findable rather than invisible. The real cost is spending a
  reader's independence by sending a highlight reel instead of a method. Fig 2 :42 "Buy
  independence in proportion to the cost of being wrong." That cost is avoidable by leading with the
  shape claim and its refuter.
- **undoable:** yes. The prescription is a set of constraints, not a theory that must be withdrawn.
  Each constraint can be narrowed independently if a refuter fires. A sent claim can be superseded
  by a corrected packet, and no archive entry has to be deleted to correct it.
- **reversed by:** a ground reading, not agreement. An archive that reconstructs the counterfactual
  without recording refusals; a discovery with no instrument at any resolution returning a failing
  reading; a case where "we searched and found nothing" proves absence; a demonstration that the
  past ground legitimately updates on the present; or a result whose route and counterfactual are
  recoverable by inspection with no lineage. Any one of these narrows or withdraws the shape. Until
  one fires, the verdict stands as `candidate`.
