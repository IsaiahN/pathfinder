## Round trip gap R_T

The Symbols table defines R_T as the gap between what was sent up and what came back down,
`gap(x, (T_E ∘ T_A)(x))`, measuring what the coarser description cannot hold. Fig 4 :30 requires it
to be measured rather than assumed. I ran the round trip on the condensation this folder actually
uses.

1. **Sent up (x):** the verbatim figure set, chiefly Fig 6 :42-43 (record grows, reach falls),
   Fig 8 :39-43 (no proof of absence, distinct instructions), Fig 12 :125-126 (two currencies),
   Fig 2 :33-34 (anchor), Fig 13 :140-141 (counterfactual).
2. **Abstraction (T_A):** the shorthand in `07` §2 and `08` §1: a history is a lineage record that
   keeps refusals, prices in two currencies, distinguishes three endings, keeps the anchor fixed and
   reports the counterfactual.
3. **Concretisation (T_E):** a concrete instruction to a historian: for this archive, include the
   negative results, date the protocols, classify each stopped program, and write the counterfactual
   paragraph.

**R_T is nonzero but small for the prescriptive part.** Two things the shorthand holds do not
survive the trip back down cleanly:

- the **boundary of the archive**. "Keep refusals in the record" assumes an archive that can be
  enumerated. Fig 11 :43 says the habitat is enumerated, never composed, but many historical
  archives are themselves a biased sample (the file-drawer effect). The shorthand could be read as
  assuming a complete record that does not exist. This is a strain on the ground side, not on the
  figure rule.
- the **classification of endings**. "Refuted, unreachable-at-budget, no proto-instrument" is a
  figure-side distinction. Whether a dated record can actually decide which one holds is an
  instrument question. Where it cannot, the honest output is "the record does not decide", which the
  shorthand does not explicitly license.

What survives cleanly: refusals must not be deleted, bits and actions do not add, no search proves
absence, the past anchor must not update, and the lineage carries the counterfactual. R_T was **not**
measured numerically because there is no ground reading of a historiographic prescription. It is
recorded qualitatively as the enumerated set of dropped constraints. Treating the formula as
identical to the law set would set R_T to zero and would be the dead-reckoning error Fig 4 :30
names.
