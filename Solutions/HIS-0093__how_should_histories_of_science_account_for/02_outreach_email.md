# Outreach draft: check the shape of the history before you decide about the framework

**Use:** one-to-one, or as a cover letter attached to a submission through the public venues in
`03_public_contact_points.csv`. **Not** for bulk send.

**Hard precondition:** the shape claim and its refuter must survive stage 1 on their own. The
theory is offered only after a verdict on stage 1 is recorded. Do not send a list of great
discoveries in place of the claim.

---

**Subject:** One checkable claim about how histories of science treat failed work

> Hi [name],
>
> I am working on a specific question about historiography and I have one output I would like you
> to check against what you already know. I am not asking you to read a theory first, and I am not
> asking you to agree with anything. If the output does not survive, there is nothing else to look
> at.
>
> **The output (`specified`, not `grounded`).** A history of science that is checkable has the shape
> of a lineage record, not a highlight reel. Concretely, it does five things. It keeps failed and
> abandoned work in the record as refusals, so a later reader can tell never-tried from
> tried-and-refused. It prices a result in two separate currencies: the description that survived
> and the actions it cost to establish. It locates a chance discovery in an instrument that was
> already returning a reading that failed to resolve, with an observer prepared to notice it. It
> distinguishes three different reasons a program ends: refuted, unreachable at the budget, or no
> instrument anywhere that was returning anything. And it reports what could have been concluded
> and what would have had to be true for the answer to differ.
>
> This is a shape, not a verdict about any particular episode. I am not claiming that a given
> famous discovery was or was not lucky, or that a given abandoned program was impossible. Those
> are judgments about the ground and they are owed to the dated record, not to a framework.
>
> **What would refute the shape claim.** I wrote these down before sending this:
> 1. an archive that reconstructs the counterfactual correctly while recording no refusals or
>    stopped branches;
> 2. a documented discovery where no instrument at any resolution was returning a failing reading;
> 3. a case where "we searched and found nothing" legitimately proves absence;
> 4. a demonstration that the past ground legitimately updates on the present outcome, so
>    presentism is not a failure of the record;
> 5. a finished result from which the route and the counterfactual are recoverable by inspection,
>    with no lineage.
>
> If any one holds, I will retract or narrow the claim. A one-line "this fails because..." is more
> useful to me than agreement. If you would rather not engage, no reply is needed.
>
> **If it survives, and only then:** here are the sources. The figures are the authority, not my
> summary of them.
> - Verbatim figure text with line numbers (local extract, Fig N :line citations):
>   `FRAMEWORK/reference/03-FIGURES_TEXT.md`
> - Canonical usage rule: https://github.com/IsaiahN/tether/blob/general-agent/figures/README.md
> - The eight-step loop and the mode rule: `FRAMEWORK/figures/THE_FORMULA.md`
>
> **If you want to use the machinery:** the formula is in the figures link. Run it on a problem you
> know well and can check against a ground. Write down what would refute your result *before* you
> run it, and state the mode of what you get. A reported failure is more useful to me than a
> reported success.
>
> - [name]

## Two-stage offer, stated plainly

1. **Stage 1:** send the residual, the shape claim and the five refuters. Ask what the recipient
   would count as a failure. Record their verdict **before** showing any theory.
2. **Stage 2, opt-in only:** if they are curious, offer the figure links. Their later agreement is
   never pooled as corroboration, because they now hold the framework's interior (Fig 2 :40).
3. **Stage 3:** offer the formula as a method to reuse. Each user writes their own refuter and
   states their own mode.

**Hard precondition:** stages 1 to 3 only work if the stage-1 shape survives. If a historian can
show that the counterfactual is computable without the refusals, or that an ended program proves
absence, stop and correct `01_figure_grounded_audit.md` before sending anything.
