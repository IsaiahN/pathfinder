## 1. A shape is a constraint you can state before you can name it

The canonical statement in `FIGURES_README_canonical.md` is that the framework returns *"what a missing thing would have to look like (a shape, not a name)"*, then stops where instruments can no longer distinguish and records the lineage. Fig 9 :32: *"you get a kind of thing, never the answer itself."* Fig 13 :134: *"Unreachable is a verdict here, not a silence,"* and :135: *"It cannot say which primitive is missing, because naming it would mean already holding it."*

From the essay *The Library of Babel*, read directly:

> *"You can cut your search short if the set of books returned is small and descriptive enough that it provides a general shape of the answer that satisfies your question."*

So for BUS-0048 a shape is: **the narrowed form of an answer about demand that can be stated before any particular survey, metric, price or probe is named.** It constrains. It does not fill.

- Shape: *genuine demand is registered at a costly, non-reactive, per-slot probe where the customer's judgment ranges against the paying ground; interest is an instrument, not the anchor.*
- Name, withheld: *use a five-question interview script, a 10 percent deposit, a landing-page waitlist, the Lean Startup build-measure-learn loop, the "Mom Test", or a 99 dollar pre-order price.*

The distinction matters here because the entrepreneurship literature is almost entirely in the naming business. Ries names validated learning, innovation accounting and vanity metrics; practitioner methods name the Mom Test and the pre-order page; marketing research names purchase-intent scales and conversion rates. Those are bindings from other frames onto atoms, not outputs of this loop.

## 2. How the shape was obtained

The essay gives the bootstrapping procedure:

> *"If you don’t know the right terms to search for, you will have to use the existing vocabulary you do know that is closest to the words that you wish to describe and grasp the shape of."*
> *"With each new term gained, you are able to narrow the space by definition and deepen your vocabulary."*

Applied here: the closest available terms were "purchase intention", "intention-behavior gap", "hypothetical bias", "self-generated validity", "vanity metrics", "validated learning" and "costly signaling". None was used as the answer. Each was treated as a witness to be ranged against the residual, per Fig 8 :53: *"Residual first, frame second."* The description in effect terms (the persistence of a large per-customer gap between predicted and paid response) came first; the frames were selected only after that description was fixed.

The essay also names the bundle stop:

> *"Perhaps your problem was a bundle or conflation of several problems masquerading as one? You will need to search all of those problems individually to find their answers and then combine them in a satisfactory way."*

That is the canonical warrant for treating BUS-0048 as a union (Fig 9 :54-57), not as one hard question.

The stop condition:

> *"You will know that you are done because the finish line will also be the starting place, as there is nowhere else to search and nothing else that can describe the answer better."*

In this solution the stop is reached when the two conditions C1 and C2 are stated, an instrument for each is named, and the five falsifiers are written. Any further search that only renames the conditions is motion, not progress (Fig 7 :32, *"one long room: motion, no progress"*).

## 3. What licenses a name

Only two things, both outside the shape:

1. **The ground settles it.** Formula step 5: *"the ground settles it. Verified only by what pays. A gate is not the ground."* The README: the framework *"stops at the limit of what the available instruments can distinguish, and records the lineage so the stop is honest."* Here, the ground is a payment at a stated price, per customer.
2. **A provenanced import from a frame whose closure differs** (Fig 6 :23, Fig 8 :50-52), with a source gate and a shadow test. Fig 8 :50: *"But shared atoms are not the signature. Two frames can hold the same atom by convergence, reaching it separately, which is corroboration at maximum independence."*

For BUS-0048, neither has happened in this folder. There is no prospective per-customer payment measurement that pays for "this specific probe caused the purchase", and the startup-practice names were adopted without a shadow test. Hence the `gap` row in F9 of `01_figure_grounded_audit.md`.

## 4. The source error is the specific forbidden move here

From *The Hard Problem is About Source*, read directly:

> *"The Problem: You can have several underlying causes that create identical readings but the definition of the output would be read as the same."*
> *"If several paths create the same behavior, then behavior is a projection with lossy information"*
> *"What the Spectrometer Example Reveals: The answer is unreached until a better instrument exists."*

This is the exact error a discovery story makes when it reads an interested nod and names its source as demand. A nod, a signup, a "definitely would buy" and a 10,000-person waitlist are readings. Each is consistent with several sources: a genuine unmet need, a novelty effect, a promotional incentive, social desirability, a misunderstanding of the product, or a person who will buy from a competitor next week. The reading does not determine the source. Chandon, Morwitz and Reinartz 2005 add a sharper twist: the act of taking the reading can itself change the behavior being read, which is why the survey group's correlation was 58 percent greater than the control group's. The instrument is not even a passive projector here; it feeds back into the thing it measures.

What is owed is a source-discriminating instrument, not a better story and not more agreement among readers. Fig 6 :30: *"outward: does another frame already hold it? The description travels. Fails by being sharpened into terms only we can read. Cast it in effect terms."*

The essay closes: *"An instrument that can’t distinguish these cases demonstrates its own inadequacy, it cannot claim anything substantial about what is being measured or what causes it."* That is why the expert packet asks for a failure condition before a theory, and why F4 is narrowed rather than allowed to claim that cost guarantees honesty.

## 5. Prestige is not action, applied to entrepreneurship

From *The Recursive Self-Improvement Trap*, read directly:

> *"Prestige must never be allowed to validate an action directly."*
> *"Prestige can fund an action. It can give an action a budget to be tried. But it cannot settle an action. Only the ground can settle it. The Ground Maintainer must be blind to prestige."*
> *"If prestige could buy actions, the system would collapse into a monoculture. The highest-prestige agent would monopolize compute and repeat its past success, and the system would stop exploring."*

The last line is an instance, in the author's own agent system, of Fig 7 :36 (*"A chain of mirrors is one long room"*) and Fig 10 :24 (*"the ground does not decay, the channel does"*). Because it is the author's own design, it is framework-internal corroboration, not an independent witness; it is recorded here as an illustration of the rule, not as evidence that founders behave this way.

For outreach this means: a list of famous founders or investors who agree with the shape would be a monoculture, not a check. An accelerator demo day is a room, not an anchor. The legitimate ask gives the claim a budget to be tried by someone outside, and lets the payment settle it.

## 6. Correction to my own §3 (an overstatement, retained as evidence)

I initially wrote that naming any particular probe is forbidden by "shape, not a name". That statement is too broad and is withdrawn here. The canonical phrase governs a **missing** primitive, one not held in Γ. A deposit, a pre-order, a survey question and a retention metric are already in the founder's library in principle. Naming one is a binding from the business frame, not the invention of a new primitive. What remains forbidden is claiming the binding moves the wall (Fig 6 :21, *"MINT composes inside the closure and can never add an atom"*) and claiming the binding is the framework's answer. The binding still needs provenance and a ground test, but it is not a category error in the way naming a seventh physical primitive would be. Both the corrected and the withdrawn formulations are kept so the correction is visible rather than silent.

## 7. This derivation is a simulation, not a ground reading

From the same essay, read directly:

> *"the output is only as good as the validator. The agent, or the tool, or the frame, or the theory, cannot validate itself. An external validator, or ground, is always needed to tether the veracity of the output to reality. Anything else is drift."*
> *"LLMs can quickly simulate an approximate answer/output with the formula found in the architecture post. It will often be better than the generic outputs that you will usually get otherwise too. However, the truly testable output would need to be derived from the real architecture or working the formula yourself."*

So this `05` file, like `01`, is a simulated derivation checked for internal consistency against the figures. It is labelled `specified` wherever it makes a claim about founders. It is not a test of the question and cannot settle it. The point of publishing it is to make the shape and its refuters easy to check by someone whose ground contact is real and whose closure differs.

## 8. The shape, stated once more

For BUS-0048 the canonical shape is: **a two-condition conjunction with two distinct failure modes, a per-slot reading that must not be pooled, and a signal whose cost is what makes it a reading of willingness to pay.** It is not an interest score, not a name of a method, and not a ranking of prospects. The names that appear in the entrepreneurship literature are bindings, provenance owed. The instruments that would convert the shape into a grounded answer are not held here, so the answer stops at `specified` and the stop is recorded.
