## The question restated as an effect

What is being decided is not *how much interest exists* but *which interest signals will close the gap to a purchase*. In effect terms: the founder's library Γ predicts a customer response Γ(b,a) from a belief b (the interest evidence) and a probe a (ask, price, offer, charge); the market returns o′ (money paid at a price, repeated); R = |Γ(b,a) - o′| is measured per customer-slot s, never averaged into a vanity total (Fig 1 :19, Fig 1 :22).

- Option A, "genuine demand is a stronger kind of interest": leads to the prediction that better interest measurement alone closes R.
- Option B, "genuine demand is registered only at a costly, non-reactive, per-slot probe against a paying ground": leads to the prediction that R closes when a commitment costs the customer something and the reading stays unpooled, and that interest alone leaves R open (Fig 2 :29, Fig 10 :24).
- What an answer would look like, written down and bound as an ordinary term before the run (Formula, "Direction"): a *discriminator* that separates a signal that can be ranged against the paying ground from a signal that cannot, plus an instrument that returns the reading per slot. Until bound, that shape predicts nothing and no loop check can see it. It is now bound, so the refuter in `01` section 5 can fire on it.

The verdict recorded in `09_watson_answer.md` is Option B in `specified` mode, kept per domain and never averaged.

## What is deliberately not here

- No personal emails of named individuals.
- No ranking of people by how likely they are to agree.
- No bulk-send plan.
- No claim that this derivation is `grounded`, no claim that it settles the entrepreneurship question, and no claim that the framework is the only true account of customer demand.

Three figure-grounded reasons, plus one practical one:

1. A room is not an anchor. *"A room’s agreement is variation, not a verdict. Selection between rooms is where the ground votes."* (Fig 7 :35) and *"Alignment is triangulation, not negotiation. A mirror cannot correct you."* (Fig 2 :35). Gathering agreeable founders manufactures the correlation that disqualifies a verdict.
2. Correlation has valence. *"disqualifying for a verdict: agreement about the world is worthless when the agreeing frames share an evidence pool"* (Fig 2 :40). A list selected for receptiveness is one witness wearing many names.
3. A verifier must be outside the interior. *"A verifier that can reconstruct the claim cannot verify it."* (Fig 10 :42). The first contact therefore carries the residual and its refuter, not the framework.
4. Legally, unsolicited bulk email to scraped addresses breaches GDPR and CAN-SPAM style rules in many jurisdictions and gets the sending domain blacklisted. Public opt-in routes avoid that failure as well as the structural one.
