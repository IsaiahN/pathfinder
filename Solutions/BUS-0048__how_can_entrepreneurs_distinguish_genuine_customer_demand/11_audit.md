## 2. Independent re-census from the residual line

### 2.1 Arity, symmetry, scale

- **Arity:** a relation among founder-library Γ, probe a and customer return o′, indexed by slot s and ordered by time t. It has at least three operands, one ordering (interest before payment) and one threshold comparison ("genuine" means paid willingness clears the asking price, Fig 12 :87). A two-place reading (interest, purchase) loses the probe and the prediction, so it is rejected.
- **Symmetry:** not symmetric. The market does not update on the founder's opinion (Fig 2 :25). "Genuine" is a threshold comparison, so one operand is a quantity compared with another, not merely conjoined with it (Operators :46-51). It is time-asymmetric: a lost customer is not recovered by reversing the probe.
- **Scale:** the slot is one customer, but "genuine" is judged at a price set for a segment, and retention is a market-scale reading over time. The time index crosses the membrane between the founder's step loop and the market's selection loop (Fig 4 :5, :21). The residual is intrinsically cross-scale, and the round trip is lossy (Fig 4 :30).

## 4. Round-trip gap R_T

Fig 4 :30: *"So take the round trip and measure the gap. Without it you are navigating by dead reckoning: confident about a position nobody checked."*

- **T_A, going up:** carry the primary's two-condition shape to a one-sentence abstraction. Result: *demand is a costly, non-reactive relation between a founder and a paying customer.*
- **T_E, coming back:** put that abstraction into a specific founder situation. Result: *charge something and keep asking people what they think.*

**R_T = gap(x, (T_E ∘ T_A)(x)).** The gap is what the abstraction cannot hold:

1. The one-sentence form collapses the union into one lever. It loses Fig 9 :57, *"a case in two conditions is not a harder case of either."* Coming back down, it tells a founder with a dead channel merely to charge more, which is effort at the wrong link (Fig 3 :19).
2. It loses the reactivity rule. "Non-reactive" does not say that asking changes the reading (Fig 2 :33-34, Chandon's 58 percent). Coming back down, the founder keeps running the same survey and believes the result.
3. It loses the per-slot indexing. "Relation" does not say "indexed by customer" (Fig 1 :19-22), so coming back down the founder reports a total again.
4. It loses the conflict around cost. "Costly" reads as "cost guarantees honesty", which the biology source refutes (Penn and Számadó). The abstraction cannot carry the narrowing to "a free signal is not a reading of WTP".
5. It loses the honest stop. The abstraction cannot say which instrument is missing, only that something is.

This gap is not an error to eliminate; it is the measurement of what the coarser description cannot hold (Fig 4 :31). It is also why the outreach packet sends the generator, not the one-sentence version (Fig 4 :25). The one-paragraph version in `07` is a deliberately lossy artefact and is labelled as such.
