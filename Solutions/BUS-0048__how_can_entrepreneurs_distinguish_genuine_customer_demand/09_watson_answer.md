# WATSON answer, BUS-0048

**Mode: `specified`, not `grounded`.** Figures are primary and decide. World sources are secondary, corroborate but never overrule, and a conflict returns the derivation to the domain census. This is a simulated derivation checked for internal consistency against the figures, not a ground reading.

## QUESTION

How can entrepreneurs distinguish genuine customer demand from interest that does not translate into purchases?

Restated as an effect: why does R = |Γ(b,a) - o′| stay large per customer-slot where interest was high, and close in others? R is indexed per customer and per time, never averaged into an engagement total (Fig 1 :19). Anchor: the purchase with real money, named as a counter and a market in Fig 2 :29. Survey interest is an instrument that moves when pushed (Fig 2 :29-30) and can be wrong on the axis it does not measure (Fig 2 :37-38).

## DOMAIN CENSUS

Arity: a three-place relation (founder Γ, probe a, customer return o′), indexed by slot and ordered by time; carries an ordered bond and a comparison bond, not symmetric ones. Symmetry: "genuine" is a threshold, willingness to pay ⋛ price (Fig 12 :87). Scale: single customer, segment, and market across repeats; the membrane is lossy (Fig 4 :21, :30).

## VERDICT (from the figures)

**The figure-derived verdict:** genuine demand is not a stronger kind of interest. It is a reading taken at a costly, non-reactive, per-slot probe where the customer's judgment ranges against a paying ground. Interest is a frame-internal instrument that can be wrong on the axis it does not measure, and asking the question can change the reading. Genuine demand requires both C1 (an unpooled channel to a paying ground) and C2 (a commitment that costs the customer something); it fails when either condition fails, and the two need different instruments. The verdict is kept separate per domain and never averaged. Mode `specified`. The business and marketing pool corroborates the shape; the two economics reviews are related witnesses from other pools; the biology source conflicts and narrowed F4 rather than flipping the verdict. Which specific probe works is a declared `gap`, not a hidden answer.

- **Cost if wrong:** a founder spends the build budget on a product nobody pays for, or discards a real category because the wrong signal was used. In outreach terms, an expert's twenty minutes would be spent refuting a shape that was never the right shape. The cost is real but bounded and largely reversible.
- **Undoable:** yes. No term is deleted if the claim fails; a refutation is added, and the reach falls because *"something was added rather than because anything was deleted"* (Fig 6 :42-43). The shape and its seven falsifiers are recorded in a provenance trail so a later reader can locate the error (Fig 10 :62-64).
- **Reversed by:** any of the seven pre-registered falsifiers in `01_figure_grounded_audit.md` section 4, in particular (1) a free interest signal prospectively predicting named payers through a habitat change, (2) zero survey reactivity under pre-registration, (3) a pooled engagement count beating a per-slot reading, (4) a zero-cost signal matching payment, or (5) one intervention restoring both conditions with no categorically different residue. A source with a genuinely different evidence pool showing that per-customer payment is fully predicted by stated interest at zero cost would also reverse it.
