# The Tether's answer on genuine demand versus non-converting interest

**Mode: `specified` - not `grounded`.** This is a shape and a route, not a result. Simulated derivation checked against the figures (`05_what_shape_means.md` section 7), not a ground reading.

## 1. The residual it answers

A founder's library predicts a customer response from an interest signal; the market returns a payment or it does not. R = |Γ(b,a) - o′| is measured per customer-slot, never as an engagement total (Fig 1 :19, :22). The phenomenon is the persistence of a large R in slots where interest was high. The contact is a **union compound**, not one hard problem (Fig 9 :54-57): a channel that has lost touch and a signal that costs nothing are not different degrees of one failure. Each has its own loop and its own ground.

## 2. The shape

1. **The anchor is the payment, not the enthusiasm.** Fig 2 :29: *"An anchor is whatever settles the question and does not change its answer because of what any frame thinks. A measurement, an experiment, a counter, a market, a patient outcome."* Survey answers move when the founder pushes, so they are instruments. Chandon, Morwitz and Reinartz 2005 measured the movement: surveyed consumers showed a 58 percent greater intention-to-purchase correlation than otherwise identical unsurveyed consumers.
2. **Interest is a frame-internal metric.** Fig 1 :27: *"They are frame-internal. A frame cannot score itself with a quantity it also produces."* Fig 10 :26 names the decay modes: *"stale percepts, dropped transients, saturated metrics, pooled readings"*. A waitlist total is a pooled reading; it throws away the only signal that says where to look (Fig 1 :22).
3. **The discriminator must cost the customer something.** Operators table :66: *"A bond that costs nothing is a bond that carries nothing, which is the flat case."* A free click is a free bond. A deposit, pre-order, price quote accepted, or repeat visit with a switching cost is a priced bond that can be ranged against the ground. This is a binding from the business frame onto the figure's structural price, not a derivation, and the biology conflict in F4 is recorded: cost does not *guarantee* honesty, it makes the signal a reading of willingness to pay.
4. **The two conditions are a union with different instruments.** C1 is a maintained, unpooled channel to the paying ground. C2 is a costly commitment that pays. A single lever clears one region and leaves the intersection standing (Fig 9 :55). Verdicts are kept separate, never averaged.
5. **The reading must come from a differing closure.** Fig 6 :23: *"No frame escapes its own closure. The bound moves only on contact with a frame whose closure differs."* Interviewing only people who liked the pitch hands the same room to the founder (Fig 7 :13, *"agreement compounds, evidence does not"*). A stated intent and a payment that point to the same slot from independent probes are corroboration at maximum independence (Fig 8 :50); if both came from the same pitch, they are one witness.
6. **The instrument is not named by the framework.** It returns a shape, not a name. Which survey, which deposit, which price, which retention window are bindings that need provenance and a ground test. Which probe actually works is a `gap` at the instrument boundary (Fig 9 :32).

## 3. What it explicitly does not say

- Not a theory of everything about markets. No final room; the center of the question stays a compound with its own loop.
- Not "interest is useless". Interviews build vocabulary (D1 link 2). They are disqualified as the settlement, not as a tool.
- Not "cost proves honesty". Penn and Számadó 2020 conflict with that claim and the audit narrowed it rather than flipping the verdict.
- Not "TAM proves demand". Market size is a budget filter, not a witness (Fig 9 :60).
- Not a derivation of one entrepreneurship method from another, and no new primitive.
- Not grounded. No per-customer payment measurement in this folder settles `left(R,φ)`.

## 4. What would settle it - the ground

A source-discriminating instrument, not a further argument. From *The Hard Problem is About Source*: an instrument that reads green *"cannot comment on the source"*; the spectrometer resolved it. Here:

- **Reactivity test:** a pre-registered survey versus no-survey field comparison in the target category. If the intention-to-purchase correlation is unchanged by asking, the specific F2 mechanism is falsified.
- **Cost test:** matched slots receive a free signal or a priced signal, and the founder tracks which readings predict payment at a stated price through a habitat change. If the free signal predicts as well, F4 is falsified.
- **Per-slot test:** does a named customer's paid conversion beat a pooled engagement count at predicting the next named conversion? If not, Fig 1 :19's consequence is falsified here.
- **Union test:** does one intervention restore both unpooled contact and costly commitment with no categorically different residue? If yes, F5 is false.
- **Standing falsifiers from the audit:** the full list of seven is in section 4 of `01_figure_grounded_audit.md`, written before the conclusion.

## 5. The honest stop

**Unreached, not unreachable.** The missing thing is characterized in effect terms: an instrument that returns a per-slot, non-reactive reading of willingness to pay without collapsing into a vanity metric. It is not held in this folder. Fig 6 :33-34: *"Where nothing is, there is no edge to extend from, and the search for a proto-instrument is a search for one that is already there."* Fig 8 :39: *"So a search that finds nothing is never a proof of absence."* The framework stops where instruments can no longer distinguish and records the lineage so the stop is checkable. *"Indiscernibility is not identity."*

## 6. One-paragraph version for outreach

> The Tether does not tell a founder which survey, waitlist, price or deposit will work. It says that genuine demand is not a stronger kind of interest. It is a reading taken at a costly, non-reactive, per-customer probe where the customer's judgment ranges against the paying ground. Interest is a frame-internal instrument: pooling it hides the signal, and asking about it can change the answer by as much as 58 percent in measured field studies. The two conditions the answer requires are different: a channel to the paying ground that stays unpooled, and a signal that costs the customer something. They fail differently and need different instruments, so one lever cannot fix both. The question stops at a shape with five written falsifiers and an instrument gap. Which specific probe works stays open until the ground pays for it.
