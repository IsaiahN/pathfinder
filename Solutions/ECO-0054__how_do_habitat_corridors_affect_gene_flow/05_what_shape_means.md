## 1. A shape is a constraint, not a filler

The figures README states the limit: the framework returns *"what a missing thing would have to look like (a shape, not a name)."* Fig 9 :32: *"you get a kind of thing, never the answer itself."* Fig 13: *"It can say the answer is out of reach with the library as it stands... It cannot say which primitive is missing, because naming it would mean already holding it."*

From *The Library of Babel*, opened this session: *"you can cut your search short if the set of books returned is small and descriptive enough that it provides a general shape of the answer that satisfies your question."*

For ECO-0054 a **shape** is therefore the narrowed form of an answer we can state before we can name the winning design. It says: a corridor changes contact; the contact imports an atom into a closure; the atom changes interactions at second order; a contagion may use the same path at third order; and valence is relative to the residual. It does **not** say which width, which species or which landscape wins. That would be a name.

## 2. How a shape is obtained

- *Library of Babel*: use the vocabulary you already have closest to the thing, look for recurring new terms that overlap or correlate with it, recurse. *"With each new term gained, you are able to narrow the space by definition and deepen your vocabulary."*
- Fig 6 :30: *"The description travels. Fails by being sharpened into terms only we can read. Cast it in effect terms."* Fig 9 :30-31: *"your words are borrowed too, so describe the effect rather than the cause."*
- *Decoding Tacit Knowledge*: *"When contact isn't possible, the best you can send is a plain description of what you'd expect to see, because anyone can check that against the world."*
- *Library of Babel* on stopping: the answer is reached when *"the finish line will also be the starting place"*, and the paradox is that by then you have *"composed the answer through the process of locating it."*

In this question the descent was: "connectivity" (a borrowed scalar) to "contact" (an effect), to "import of an atom" (the missing term supplied by the ecology frame), to "valence relative to residual" (the relation), to "cascade order" (the arrangement). Each step narrowed the answer without naming a design.

## 3. What licenses a name

Only two things, both outside the shape itself:

1. **The ground settles it.** Formula step 5: *"the ground settles it. Verified only by what pays."* Here that means an F_ST shift, a fruit-set change, a mapped incidence change, measured per slot.
2. **A provenanced import from a frame whose closure differs** (Fig 6 :23, Fig 8 :50-52), with a source gate and a shadow test. Here that means a genetic assay or an incidence study arriving from a discipline that did not hold the corridor advocate's model.

*They're Scanning Books* sharpens this: *"The real import is not a new term in the existing space. It is a new dimension of the space."* And: *"A minimum viable set of six terms is a human's careful guess at reality's compression axes; another sensorium's minimum set need not be those six."* Also: *"Declaring the primitives does not make them neutral. It makes the choice visible."*

The six-term substrate is a visible guess, not nature's own list. Carrying it into ecology as a literal physics analogy is the strain S1 that was retracted in `01` §6. What survives is the domain-agnostic rule: relations, imports and closures, whatever the substrate.

## 4. Why "corridor quality is a number" is the forbidden error

*The Hard Problem is About Source*, opened this session, gives the mechanism. Metamerism: an instrument reads green and *"it cannot comment on the source"*: one green light, or yellow plus blue, or RGB, or CMYK, or a scheme not yet discovered. The reading does not determine the source. *"If several paths create the same behavior, then behavior is a projection with lossy information."* And: *"An instrument that can't distinguish these cases demonstrates its own inadequacy, it cannot claim anything substantial about what is being measured or what causes it."* It was the **spectrometer**, not an argument, that resolved it: *"The answer is unreached until a better instrument exists."*

A corridor-use count is the green reading. It cannot comment on the source: the same count can come from genuine functional connection, from a drift-fence effect, from an animals-only movement that never moves a gene, or from a pathogen using the strip. Horskins et al. 2006 supply the field case. Naming "high-quality corridor" from a use reading is the metamerism error: a source named from a projection where several sources could give the same reading. What is owed is a discriminator (a genetic assay, an incidence map), not a name.

*The Recursive Self-Improvement Trap*: *"the output is only as good as the validator. The agent, or the tool, or the frame, or the theory, cannot validate itself."*

## 5. Does the bundle rule apply here? ANSWERED

*The Library of Babel* asks: *"Perhaps your problem was a bundle or conflation of several problems masquerading as one? You will need to search all of those problems individually."*

**Answer:** yes, and that is the central result of `01`. The question as posed bundles at least four conditions (contact, import, interaction change, contagion), which Fig 9 :54-57 calls a union. They are not four degrees of "connectivity", so they cannot be searched with one lever or scored on one axis. The shape returned is the unbundled union with separate instruments. This is a `general` structural claim from the framework plus a `specified` claim about this field; no ground reading of the bundle itself was taken here.

## 5b. Correction to my own §4 (cf. the exemplar's §5b)

The phrase *"shape, not a name"* governs a **missing** primitive, one not held in Γ (the README's exact wording: *"what a missing thing would have to look like"*). "Import of an atom into a closure" is not a new primitive invented by this derivation: it is a binding from the ecology frame (a disperser, a pollen grain, a seed) onto a relation the figures already hold (Fig 6 :23, Fig 8 :32). So naming it is not the forbidden invention of a seventh term. What remains forbidden is naming **which** corridor design occupies the slot in a landscape nobody measured, and claiming the binding moves a wall it does not move. Both claims are now recorded; neither is quietly erased.

## 6. Two consequences for outreach

**Prestige is not action.** From *The Recursive Self-Improvement Trap*, opened this session: *"Prestige must never be allowed to validate an action directly... Someone let prestige stand in for proof... Prestige can fund an action. It can give an action a budget to be tried. But it cannot settle an action. Only the ground can settle it. The Ground Maintainer must be blind to prestige."*

A campaign that gathers famous ecologists to endorse "corridors work" is the named collapse. The legitimate ask is the opposite: give the shape a budget to be tried against genetic and incidence data, and let the ground settle it. The public contact points in `03` are venues, not endorsements.

**My own audit is a simulation, not a ground reading.** Same essay: *"LLMs can quickly simulate an approximate answer/output with the formula found in the architecture post... the truly testable output would need to be derived from the real architecture or working the formula yourself."* The essay calls such output *"simulacra"*. So `01` is a simulated derivation checked for internal consistency against the figures. It is not a test of the corridor question and must be labelled as such. It cannot settle anything it reports on.
