# What "shape" means for INS-0083

**Mode: `general`.** Sources read on 2026-11-23: `FRAMEWORK/reference/FIGURES_README_canonical.md`, `FRAMEWORK/reference/03-FIGURES_TEXT.md`, `FRAMEWORK/reference/60_FIGURES_AS_METALAWS.md`, and two essays by Isaiah N (*The Library of Babel: Locating The Book of Books*, 2026-10-02; *The Recursive Self-Improvement Trap*, 2026-11-19). The other six essays are mapped in the exemplar but were not opened this session, so they are not quoted here.

## 1. A shape is a constraint, not a filler

The canonical rule: the framework returns "what a missing thing would have to look like (a shape, not a name)" and then "stops at the limit of what the available instruments can distinguish, and records the lineage so the stop is honest." Fig 9 :32 "you get a kind of thing, never the answer itself." Fig 13 :134 "Unreachable is a verdict here, not a silence" and "It cannot say which primitive is missing, because naming it would mean already holding it."

From *The Library of Babel*: "you can cut your search short if the set of books returned is small and descriptive enough that it provides a general shape of the answer that satisfies your question."

So for this question a shape is:

> three separate slots, each with its own anchor, enumerated habitat, explicit bond type, and a `gap` on the empirical sign until a ground reading arrives.

That constrains any acceptable answer. It does not say whether universal or targeted provision raises trust.

## 2. How the shape was obtained

- *Library of Babel*: start from the vocabulary you already have that is closest to the thing, then "With each new term gained, you are able to narrow the space by definition and deepen your vocabulary." Here the starting terms were coalition, trust and tax willingness from the question itself; the new terms were anchor, bond type, habitat, round-trip gap and union.
- Fig 6 :30 "The description travels. Fails by being sharpened into terms only we can read. Cast it in effect terms." So the residual was described as "what failed to resolve in each slot", not as "welfare state legitimacy".
- Fig 9 :30-31 "your words are borrowed too, so describe the effect rather than the cause." Calling something "deservingness" imports a frame; describing "who is eligible and on what evidence" is the effect language that travels.
- *Library of Babel* on stopping: "the finish line will also be the starting place" because by then "you have already composed the answer through the process of locating it." Here the stop is reached when the three slots and their anchors are named and the remaining unknown is stated as a gap.

## 3. What would license a name

Two things, both outside the shape itself:

1. The ground settles it. Formula step 5: "the ground settles it. Verified only by what pays. A gate is not the ground." In this field that means exogenous variation in an institution measured against each slot's anchor.
2. A provenanced import from a frame whose closure differs (Fig 6 :23, Fig 8 :50-52), with a source gate and a shadow test. A welfare-regime typology can be imported into the derivation as a candidate vocabulary, but its provenance must be recorded and it must explain a gap the frame already has. Fig 6 :24 "Import from a live peer is debited against that frame's independence."

The regime typology is therefore usable as a hypothesis generator and not as a verdict. Fig 8 :50-52 "Convergent derivation and adopted import look identical in the contents. Only the record of where each came from separates them."

## 4. The specific error this question invites

The phrase "the welfare state" is a name that hides the union. It invites a single verdict ("the welfare state increases trust" or "erodes work effort") where the residual has three addresses. From *Library of Babel*: "Perhaps your problem was a bundle or conflation of several problems masquerading as one?" That is Fig 9 :54 in essay vocabulary.

A second error is to treat the survey trust item as the ground. *The Recursive Self-Improvement Trap*: "You may all agree and be wrong. You may set your clock to someone else's, who was setting your clock to yours, and be off by many deviations over time. You need ground truth to define where you stand." Many surveys agreeing with one another is a mirror chain, not an anchor.

## 5. Correction to my own reading

I first wrote that "shape, not a name" forbids naming coalition, trust or tax as slots. **Retracted.** The canonical phrase governs a *missing* primitive, one not held in Γ. These three are not missing primitives; they are the object slots named in the question itself and measured in empirical frames. What the rule actually forbids here is using the figures to name which institutional design wins, because that primitive is not in the figure set and has not been imported with a source gate and a shadow test. Both readings are kept visible so the correction can be checked.

A related narrowing: in `01_figure_grounded_audit.md` F3 I treat the trust survey as an anchor only conditionally. That is not a retreat from the shape rule. It is Fig 2 :36 "An anchor is legitimate when the question is constitutively about it" applied to the trust slot.

## 6. Prestige is not action

From *The Recursive Self-Improvement Trap*: "Prestige can fund an action. It can give an action a budget to be tried. But it cannot settle an action. Only the ground can settle it. The Ground Maintainer must be blind to prestige."

In this field, a well-cited researcher or a famous regime typology can fund a study, that is, give the claim a budget to be tried. It cannot settle which effect is real. The external check must be able to refuse the claim without reconstructing the framework first, which is why `02_outreach_email.md` leads with the residual and refuter and withholds the theory.

## 7. My own audit is a simulation, not a ground reading

Same essay: "LLMs can quickly simulate an approximate answer/output with the formula found in the architecture post. It will often be better than the generic outputs that you will usually get otherwise too. However, the truly testable output would need to be derived from the real architecture or working the formula yourself."

So `01_figure_grounded_audit.md`, `06_isomers_and_order.md` and `09_watson_answer.md` are simulated derivations checked for internal consistency against the figures. They are not tests of the welfare claim and must be labelled as such. They cannot settle anything they report on.
