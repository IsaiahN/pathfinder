## QUESTION

Decide, in effect terms, what the Tether says about the question: how do welfare institutions change political coalitions, social trust, and willingness to pay taxes? Option A: treat this as one question and report a single verdict about the welfare state. Option B: treat it as a union of three overlapping conditions, keep per-slot verdicts separate, name the anchors and the bond, and report `gap` on the empirical sign. What each leads to: A folds a live signal in one slot into an average and spends the independence of the people checking it; B produces a checkable shape, written refuters, and an open empirical slot until a ground reading arrives.

## DOMAIN CENSUS

R by arity, symmetry and scale, then one line per domain with actors, currency and anchor; partition or union; order by dependency.

- **R (the residual).** Arity three: coalition change, trust change, tax-willingness change, plus a compound case where two or three bind together. Symmetry: the slots are not interchangeable and no average is admissible. Scale: the institution is enacted at polity scale, experienced at case-worker and household scale, and registered in surveys or administrative records. Fig 9 names this a union compound: "a case in two conditions is not a harder case of either."
- **Domain 1, institutional design.** Actors: legislators, administrators, eligibility workers, financing rules. Currency: rules, entitlements, administrative contacts, budgets. Anchor: enacted statute and case records. Type: intervention, not an effect slot.
- **Domain 2, coalition formation.** Actors: parties, unions, employer bodies, beneficiary groups, voters. Currency: votes, seats, coalition agreements, membership. Anchor: election results and signed agreements. Type: one slot of the union.
- **Domain 3, reported or behavioural trust.** Actors: respondents, survey instruments, experimental participants. Currency: survey responses or incentivised choices. Anchor: conditional. A repeated survey item is the anchor only if the question is constitutively about reported trust; otherwise it is an instrument and a behavioural measure is owed. This condition is a live strain, named rather than hidden.
- **Domain 4, tax behaviour.** Actors: taxpayers, firms, revenue authority. Currency: filed returns, assessments, audit settlements, tax morale items. Anchor: tax records and audits, a money counter. Type: one slot of the union.
- **Domain 5, derivation frame.** Actors: six substrate terms, seven bonds, negation. Currency: composition inside closure(Γ). Anchor: the figures as metalaws, with no empirical ground. Governs shape only.
- **Order by dependency.** Perception (a measure exists per slot) -> vocabulary (terms for what an institution affords) -> objective (what counts as progress per slot) -> planning (which lever) -> learn and carry. The likely break is at vocabulary/objective (Fig 3 :26-27). Describe the residual before selecting a frame (Fig 9 :27-28). Refuse before mint (formula step 2).

Strain test applied: an earlier draft mapped universal benefits to the network bond and targeted benefits to subtraction. Re-census showed this was an import with no source gate (Fig 6 :23), straining both terms. It is retracted in `01_figure_grounded_audit.md` §7. The two surviving strains are the trust anchor condition (D3) and the bond type for social quantities (Fig 12 / Operators table).

## VERDICT (from the figures)

The shape survives; the empirical question does not. The figures decide that this is a three-slot union requiring per-slot anchors, an enumerated habitat, an explicit bond type, and a separate loop for compound cases. They decide that a single aggregate index, a shared evidence pool treated as two witnesses, and a macro correlation replayed as a mechanism are all disallowed. They do not decide whether any welfare design raises or lowers coalitions, trust or tax willingness. That verdict is `gap`: unreachable from the six-term substrate, reachable in principle from exogenous institutional variation measured against each anchor. The trust-slot anchor condition and the bond operand type remain named strains. This is a `general` and `specified` result with no `grounded` component.

- **cost if wrong:** bounded and locatable. The packet states mode, three refuters and provenance before the conclusion, so an error is findable rather than invisible. The real avoidable cost is spending the independence of outside readers by sending a finished theory before the residual, which Fig 2 :42 prices: "Buy independence in proportion to the cost of being wrong."
- **undoable:** yes. The shape claim can be repaired slot by slot without withdrawing any primitive. Restating trust as reported or behavioural, adding a bond-discriminating instrument, or measuring `R_T` are local changes. No atom has to be removed from the record.
- **reversed by:** a ground reading, not agreement. A phased rollout or eligibility threshold measured against coalition agreements with a catalyst test would settle the coalition slot; an incentivised trust measure would settle behavioural trust; audit-file compliance would settle the tax slot; an instrument distinguishing sequence from common-cause production would settle the bond sub-claim. A derivation of an empirical sign from the six-term set alone would refute the `gap` verdict itself. Prestige or volume of agreement is not such an instrument.
