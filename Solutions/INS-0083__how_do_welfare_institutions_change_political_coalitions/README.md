## Restatement as an effect

A welfare institution (eligibility rules, financing, universalism versus targeting, administrative burden) is a bet about who gets what and on what terms. The question asks what changes in three named slots: (s1) political coalitions, (s2) social trust, (s3) willingness to pay taxes. An answer must say, for each slot separately, what observation would count as a change and what anchor would settle it. The shape an answer would have is a per-slot mapping from institutional design to an observable effect, with the bond between the three effects stated rather than assumed. That shape is bound here as an ordinary term so it can accrue a residual and be refused (`THE_FORMULA.md`, "Direction").

## What is deliberately not here

- No personal emails of named individuals, and no purchased or scraped contact list.
- No ranking of researchers by likelihood of agreeing with the shape claim.
- No bulk-send plan.
- No claim that the figures settle the empirical question. They bound the shape and the loop; the sign and size of each effect are ground questions.

The reason for the first three is structural, not merely legal: "High correlation is disqualifying for a verdict and efficient for a convention" (Seat Map, line 163), and Fig 2 :35 "Alignment is triangulation, not negotiation. A mirror cannot correct you." A list selected for receptiveness manufactures the exact correlation that would make its agreement worthless. Legally, unsolicited bulk email to harvested addresses breaches GDPR and CAN-SPAM style rules in many jurisdictions.
