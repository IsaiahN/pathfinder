## 1. A shape is a constraint, not a filler

The figures README states the limit: the framework returns *"what a missing thing would have to look like (a shape, not a name)."* Fig 9 :32: *"you get a kind of thing, never the answer itself"*. Fig 13 :135: *"It cannot say which primitive is missing, because naming it would mean already holding it."*

From *The Library of Babel*, opened this session: *"you can cut your search short if the set of books returned is small and descriptive enough that it provides a general shape of the answer that satisfies your question."*

So a **shape** is the narrow form of an answer that can be stated before the specific item can be named. It constrains; it does not fill. For this question, `one polymer, two cross-kind capacities` is a shape. `RNA was that polymer and it came first` is a name plus a chronology.

The shape also has a negative part. The union of storage, catalysis and priority (F1 of `01`) means the answer cannot be a single verdict. A shape here is a separator: it says what conditions must be met and that they cannot be averaged.

## 2. How a shape is obtained

- *Library of Babel*: use the vocabulary already held that is *closest* to the thing, look for recurring new terms that overlap or correlate with it, recurse. *"With each new term gained, you narrow the space and deepen your vocabulary."*
- *Library of Babel* on a bundled question: *"Perhaps your problem was a bundle or conflation of several problems masquerading as one? You will need to search all of those problems individually to find their answers and then combine them in a satisfactory way."* This is Fig 9 :54-57 in the essay's own words, and it is the governing instruction for ORI-0031.
- Fig 6 :30: *"The description travels. Fails by being sharpened into terms only we can read. Cast it in effect terms."* Fig 9 :30-31: *"your words are borrowed too, so describe the effect rather than the cause."*
- *Decoding Tacit Knowledge*, opened this session: *"When contact isn't possible, the best you can send is a plain description of what you'd expect to see, because anyone can check that against the world."*
- Fig 9 :27-28: *"describe it before you go looking, or any frame you pick will seem to fit."* If RNA is named first and the figures are mapped onto it afterwards, any field will seem to fit. The residual is described first in `01` §1, and the figure lines are quoted before the binding is made.

## 3. What licenses a name

Two things, both outside the shape itself.

1. **The ground settles it.** Formula step 5: *"the ground settles it. Verified only by what pays. A gate is not the ground."* In this field, the ground is replication kinetics, ribozyme structure and the chemical record. It has settled that RNA can be dual-role in the laboratory. It has not settled that RNA was first.
2. **A provenanced import from a frame whose closure differs** (Fig 6 :23, Fig 8 :51), with a source gate and a shadow test. The chemistry literature supplies the binding of RNA into the two slots with both gates visible: Altman 1989 records the independent ribozyme discoveries; Robertson and Joyce 2012 record the ribosome evidence; Lincoln and Joyce 2009 record the cross-replicating cycle.

*They're Scanning Books* (carried quote): *"The real import is not a new term in the existing space. It is a new dimension of the space."* Also: *"Declaring the primitives does not make them neutral. It makes the choice visible."* The six-term set is a visible choice, not nature's own list. Naming RNA as *the* answer to the first-molecule slot would turn a visible choice into an invisible one.

## 4. The source-discriminator problem

*The Hard Problem is About Source*, opened this session, gives the mechanism. An instrument reads green but *"it cannot comment on the source"*: one green light, or yellow plus blue, or RGB, or CMYK, or a scheme not yet discovered. *"If several paths create the same behavior, then behavior is a projection with lossy information (it cannot define consciousness in full)."* And: *"An instrument that can't distinguish these cases demonstrates its own inadequacy."* It was the spectrometer, not an argument, that resolved it. The essay states the general form: *"The answer is unreached until a better instrument exists."*

Applied here: the fact that a molecule is dual-role does not tell you which substrate occupied the slots historically, because several routes could give the same modern reading. The ribosome is a molecular fossil, a reading whose source is not uniquely determined. What is owed is a chronology instrument, not a better argument. This is why F5 of `01` reports `gap` rather than choosing among RNA, peptides, PNA and autocatalytic sets.

*The Recursive Self-Improvement Trap*: *"the output is only as good as the validator. The agent, or the tool, or the frame, or the theory, cannot validate itself."*

## 5. Q: what does "shape" mean for ORI-0031 specifically?

**Answer.** The shape is a three-part separator:

- **S and C are distinct but composable in one molecule.** Fig 13 :35-36 makes them distinct frames; Fig 13 :74 permits the cross-kind composition; Fig 12 :119 and :121 define the catalyst and the cycle that would occupy those slots.
- **The slot occupant is unassigned by the framework.** Fig 9 :32 and Fig 13 :135. RNA is a provenanced binding from chemistry, a `candidate`, not a figure name.
- **Priority is a `gap`.** Fig 13 :134: *"Unreachable is a verdict here, not a silence,"* and Fig 8 :39: *"So a search that finds nothing is never a proof of absence."*

**Does this folder commit the forbidden error?** No on naming, with a caveat. It never says the framework derives RNA. It does say the laboratory binding is real and provenanced, which is allowed under §3. The place where an error would be easy is the move from "RNA can do both in the lab" to "RNA was first." `01` F5 and `07` §3 block that move explicitly.

## 5b. Correction to my own §3 and §4

I initially wrote the "shape, not a name" rule as a ban on naming RNA at all. That overstates it. The README's phrase is *"what a **missing** thing would have to look like (a shape, not a name)."* The subject is a primitive not held in Γ. RNA is an arrangement built from atoms the framework already holds, so naming it is a **binding** from the chemistry frame, not the invention of a missing primitive. What is forbidden is claiming the binding was derived by the figures or that it moves the wall (Fig 6 :21: *"MINT composes inside the closure and can never add an atom."*).

My §4 metamerism point is **not** retracted. The source of a modern reading is not uniquely determined by the reading; a chronology instrument is owed. But the reason is the missing discriminator, not a ban on naming a held binding. Both readings are recorded so neither is quietly lost.

## 6. Two consequences for outreach and for this document's own status

**Prestige is not action.** From *The Recursive Self-Improvement Trap*, opened this session: *"Prestige must never be allowed to validate an action directly... Someone let prestige stand in for proof... Prestige can fund an action. It can give an action a budget to be tried. But it cannot settle an action. Only the ground can settle it. The Ground Maintainer must be blind to prestige."* The field's most studied model (Le Vay and Mutschler 2019 call RNA world *"the most extensively studied model"*) is a budget fact, not a witness for priority. Fig 9 :60: *"Use filters for the budget and witnesses for the verdict. Never let a filter hand you a verdict."*

**My own audit is a simulation, not a ground reading.** Same essay: *"LLMs can quickly simulate an approximate answer/output with the formula found in the architecture post. It will often be better than the generic outputs that you will usually get otherwise too... However, the truly testable output would need to be derived from the real architecture or working the formula yourself."* The essay calls such output *"simulacra."* So `01` is a simulated derivation checked for internal consistency against the figures. It is **not** a chemistry test and must be labelled as such. It cannot settle the question it reports on.

## 7. What would make the shape a name

Only a settled ground event, recorded with its instrument and date. A laboratory demonstration of continuous self-replication from a realistic feedstock without peptides, minerals or environmental cycling would settle the S and C binding at the ground. A chronology instrument that resolves which substrate came first would reverse the `gap` on P. Neither is produced by an argument, by an agreement count, or by a figure quote. Fig 2 :29: *"An anchor is whatever settles the question and does not change its answer because of what any frame thinks."*
