# Expert verification packet: the Tether on the first dual-role molecule

**What this is:** a claim in a form an origins researcher can check. Mode, derivation and refuters are stated before the conclusion, so the reader knows what to look for and what would settle it.

**Mode: `specified` on the shape and per-domain claims, `general` on the structural form, `gap` on historical priority, not `grounded`.** No chronology instrument and no prebiotic feedstock experiment is claimed here.

**Status:** a simulated derivation checked for internal consistency against the figures, not a ground reading. It cannot settle the claim it reports on.

## 1. The claim, in one paragraph

The question is a union of three conditions: heritable storage (S), catalytic function (C) and temporal priority (P). A result on one condition does not carry across the others. Under the six-term substrate, an ordering and a directedness are distinct kinds, and composition across the two kinds is legal while composition within one kind is not; so one polymer holding both storage and catalysis is a permitted shape. The framework also defines a catalyst as a part that survives its own use and a cycle as one whose output re-enters as input, which is what a ribozyme and a self-replicating template respectively do. The framework does not name RNA, and no figure line selects which substrate came first; both are reported as a provenanced binding and a `gap`. The lab cross-replicating enzymes show the shape is reachable, not that the historical route has been reconstructed.

## 2. Three checkable sub-claims

### C1 — framework-internal; checkable by reading Figures 13 and 9

- **Statement:** storage and catalysis are distinct frames, and a single molecule composing them is legal; priority is a separate condition.
- **Source, verbatim:** Fig 13 :35 *"Space, time and energy are not three readings of one quantity. Neither are spectrum, gradient and network."* Fig 13 :36 *"adjacency is not duration, an ordering is not a pathway, a pathway is not a direction."* Fig 13 :74 *"Everything else is a composition across the two kinds, never within one."* Fig 9 :57 *"What remains is smaller and it is also categorically different: a case in two conditions is not a harder case of either."*
- **Refuter:** show that an ordered sequence and a directed catalytic transformation are the same physical degree of freedom, so S and C collapse into one condition; or show the framework must admit a within-kind composition it cannot refuse.

### C2 — chemistry-facing; checkable by an origins researcher against the ribozyme literature

- **Statement:** the two figures that would have to be occupied are exactly the figure's catalyst and cycle, and real RNA systems instantiate them.
- **Source, verbatim:** Fig 12 :119 *"catalyst. A part that survives its own use. Spent and regenerating parts behave differently across uses, and nothing records which a term is."* Fig 12 :121 *"cycle. The output re-enters as input. The sign of the loop decides whether it closes, and a chain cannot close by construction."* Fig 12 :94 *"The arrangement is the substance."*
- **World check:** Lincoln and Joyce 2009 report that *"two enzymes catalyze each other's synthesis from a total of four component substrates"* and that amplification can be continued indefinitely. Altman 1989 records the independent discovery of catalytic RNAs by the Cech and Altman laboratories. Robertson and Joyce 2012 record that the peptidyl transferase centre of the ribosome is RNA.
- **Refuter:** show that the cross-replicating enzymes are spent by their own use, or that their output does not re-enter as input, or that the basis of heredity in those systems is not an arrangement of bases. A demonstration of continuous replication from a realistic feedstock without peptides, minerals or imposed cycling would settle S and C at the ground and move them out of `specified`.

### C3 — framework-internal derivation; checkable by reading Figures 9, 13, 8 and 11

- **Statement:** the framework returns a shape, not a name; priority is a `gap`; the lab system is a harness, not the historical route.
- **Source, verbatim:** Fig 9 :32 *"you get a kind of thing, never the answer itself"*. Fig 13 :134 *"So the system can report that it lacks something rather than producing an answer anyway. Unreachable is a verdict here, not a silence,"* Fig 13 :135 *"It cannot say which primitive is missing, because naming it would mean already holding it."* Fig 8 :39 *"So a search that finds nothing is never a proof of absence."* Fig 11 :70 *"A test harness is a substituted habitat. Which is why a synthetic solve proves wiring and never capability."*
- **Refuter:** derive RNA by name from the six-term set with no chemistry import; or produce a chronology instrument that distinguishes which substrate first occupied both slots; or show that the prebiotic feedstock conditions were reproduced, so the lab system is not a substituted habitat. Agreement among researchers does not count as a refuter (Fig 2 :40, Fig 7 :13).

## 3. What is explicitly not claimed

- Not that RNA was first. Priority is a `gap`, and the world literature is split on it.
- Not an RNA-only world. Le Vay and Mutschler 2019 say RNA in isolation may need help from other molecules or the environment.
- Not that the figures name RNA. The binding is imported from chemistry with provenance and a shadow test.
- Not that the ribosome proves priority. A molecular fossil is a reading with more than one possible source.
- Not that the lab self-replicators are alive or ancestral. They prove wiring in a substituted habitat.
- Not `grounded` on the historical claim. No measurement settles P here.
- Not a strict molecular-isomer claim for RNA versus DNA. It is an arrangement claim over a shared atom vocabulary, with the strain recorded in `06`.

## 5. What would settle or reverse it

1. **S and C at the ground:** continuous RNA self-replication from a realistic feedstock without peptides, minerals or imposed cycling.
2. **P:** an instrument that reconstructs the prebiotic route and identifies which substrate occupied both slots first.
3. **Against the shape:** one of the six falsifiers in `01` §4 and `07` §4, written before this run.

No vote, citation count or prestige measure appears on this list (Fig 9 :60: *"Use filters for the budget and witnesses for the verdict. Never let a filter hand you a verdict."*).

## 6. What an expert can check in twenty minutes

1. Read Fig 13 :35-36 and :74, and Fig 9 :54-57 and :32. Four short passages.
2. Say whether an ordered sequence and a directed catalytic transformation are one physical capacity or two. That decides C1.
3. Say whether a cross-replicating ribozyme survives its own use and re-enters as input. That decides C2.
4. Say what instrument, if any, could distinguish which substrate came first. If none, confirm the `gap` rather than filling it.
5. Send the refutation. A failure is more useful than an endorsement.

**Sending rule:** one to one, through a public institutional or opt-in channel. Ask for the verdict on C1 to C3 before offering any framework material. Record that verdict before sending links. Do not pool later agreement as corroboration.
