## QUESTION

## VERDICT (from the figures)

Yes to the **shape**, no to the **name**, and `gap` on **priority**. One molecule can legally compose a heritable ordering with a directed catalytic function, because the six-term set forbids composition within one kind and permits composition across two. Such a molecule must be a catalyst that survives its own use and a cycle whose output re-enters as input; once settled at the ground it becomes an atom for Darwinian evolution. The laboratory cross-replicating enzymes instantiate the shape without reconstructing the route. The framework cannot name RNA: the binding is an import from chemistry with provenance and a shadow test. No figure line selects which substrate came first, and the world literature is explicitly split on it, so P is reported as `gap` rather than filled. Verdicts per domain are kept separate: D1/D2 `specified` and triangulated; D3 `gap` and single-source; D4 `specified` and triangulated; verification `general`. Nothing here is `grounded` on the historical claim.

- **cost if wrong:** bounded and locatable. The packet states its mode, writes its refuters before the conclusion and records the lineage, so an error is findable. The real cost is spending the independence of outside readers by sending a name or a consensus instead of a shape and a refuter. Fig 2 :42 prices it: *"Buy independence in proportion to the cost of being wrong."* That cost is avoidable by leading with the three-condition separator and the refuter.
- **undoable:** yes. The chemistry binding is removable without touching the composition derivation, and `07_the_tether_answer.md` already removes the priority claim. Nothing has to be withdrawn from the framework, and no primitive is added. A sent claim can be superseded by the corrected packet.
- **reversed by:** a ground reading, not agreement. Continuous self-replication from a realistic feedstock without peptides, minerals or imposed cycling would settle S and C; a chronology instrument that identifies which substrate occupied both slots first would reverse the `gap`; an instrument showing storage and catalysis are one physical capacity, a derived sixth primitive, or a maintainer seat in prebiotic chemistry would break the shape. Until one of these fires, the verdict stands as `candidate`.
