# The Tether's answer: could RNA have been the first dual-role molecule?

**Mode: `specified` on the per-domain claims and the shape, `general` on the structural form, `gap` on the historical priority claim, not `grounded`.** This is a shape and a route, not a result. Simulated derivation checked against the figures (`05_what_shape_means.md` §6), not a ground reading.

## 1. The residual it answers

The question is a union of three conditions, not one (Fig 9 :54: *"And when the split lands on a union rather than a partition, unbundle before you search again."*):

- **S, storage:** a heritable ordering that can be copied.
- **C, catalysis:** a directed chemical function that survives its own use.
- **P, priority:** a chronological claim about which substrate occupied both slots first.

Modern life splits S and C between DNA and proteins, and each depends on the other. Martin, Unrau and Müller 2015 state the dependency in the field's own words: *"DNA, which stores genetic information, requires protein catalysis for its replication, while protein catalysts depend on DNA for their genomic information."* The union is a compound with its own loop; one lever does not clear it (Fig 9 :55-57).

## 2. The shape, per domain

### D1 and D2, molecular composition and framework structure

1. **Storage and catalysis are distinct frames.** Fig 13 :35: *"Space, time and energy are not three readings of one quantity. Neither are spectrum, gradient and network."* Fig 13 :36: *"adjacency is not duration, an ordering is not a pathway, a pathway is not a direction."* An ordered sequence is not a directed transformation. Their co-occurrence in one molecule is a composition, not evidence that they are one fact.

2. **One molecule composing two kinds is legal.** Fig 13 :74: *"Everything else is a composition across the two kinds, never within one."* The forbidden case is within-kind composition; a single polymer carrying an ordering and a directedness is the permitted cross-kind move. The shape *one molecule, two capacities* survives.

3. **The two capacities are named figure structures.** Fig 12 :119: *"catalyst. A part that survives its own use. Spent and regenerating parts behave differently across uses, and nothing records which a term is."* Fig 12 :121: *"cycle. The output re-enters as input. The sign of the loop decides whether it closes, and a chain cannot close by construction."* A ribozyme and a self-replicating template are instances of these two structures, not analogies invented for this question. Lincoln and Joyce 2009 demonstrate a cross-catalytic system where *"two enzymes catalyze each other's synthesis from a total of four component substrates"*, with amplification continued indefinitely.

4. **The framework does not name RNA.** Fig 9 :32: *"you get a kind of thing, never the answer itself"*. Fig 13 :135: *"It cannot say which primitive is missing, because naming it would mean already holding it."* Naming RNA is a provenanced binding from the chemistry frame onto held atoms, with a source gate and a shadow test (Fig 6 :12-13, Fig 8 :51). It is a `candidate`, not a derived term and not an import that moves the wall (Fig 6 :21: *"MINT composes inside the closure and can never add an atom."*).

### D3, geological and historical priority

5. **P is a `gap`, reported as a verdict.** Fig 13 :134: *"So the system can report that it lacks something rather than producing an answer anyway. Unreachable is a verdict here, not a silence,"* and Fig 8 :39: *"So a search that finds nothing is never a proof of absence."* No figure line returns which physical substrate came first. The world frame is split: Robertson and Joyce 2012 say *"arguments regarding whether life on Earth began with RNA are more tenuous"*; Bernhardt 2012 says the hypothesis *"does not necessarily imply that RNA was the first replicating molecule to appear on the Earth"*; Kauffman and Roli 2025 put collectively autocatalytic small-molecule sets first; Le Vay and Mutschler 2019 conclude that RNA in isolation may need help from other molecules or the environment. The split is reported, not averaged. A number of agreeing researchers is a filter for the budget, never a witness for the verdict (Fig 9 :60).

### D4, the population scale

6. **A settled dual-role molecule becomes an atom for evolution.** Fig 12 :104: *"A settled molecule becomes an atom for whatever composes over it. So irreducible is a fact about a level and never about the world, and there is no bottom"*. Once the ground pays for the first replicator, selection runs: Fig 11 :36 *"Nothing was intelligent; selection ran. The word does no work there."* Actor valence is relative to the residual (Fig 11 :49: *"An actor's valence is relative to the residual, not a property it carries."*), so the same RNA strand is a benefactor to a replication residual and a parasite to a fidelity residual.

7. **The lab is a harness.** Fig 11 :66: *"Isolation is not removal of the habitat. It is substitution of one habitat for another."* Fig 11 :70: *"A test harness is a substituted habitat. Which is why a synthetic solve proves wiring and never capability."* The cross-replicating enzymes prove that the shape is physically reachable. They do not reconstruct the historical route. Fig 10 :24: *"the ground does not decay, the channel does"*, and the channel to the prebiotic route is what is missing.

## 3. What it explicitly does not say

- Not that RNA was first. That is the `gap`, and it stays open.
- Not that RNA was necessarily a pure RNA world with no peptides, minerals or lipids. The field itself doubts an RNA-only origin (Le Vay and Mutschler 2019).
- Not that the figures derive or name RNA. The binding is provenanced from chemistry.
- Not that the ribosome proves priority. A molecular fossil is a reading whose source is not uniquely determined (metamerism, `05` §4).
- Not that a lab self-replicator is life or is the ancestor of life. It is a substituted habitat proving wiring.
- Not `grounded` on the historical claim. No chronology instrument was run here.
- Not a single averaged verdict. Three conditions, three verdicts, never pooled (Fig 1 :19: *"The residual is indexed by object slot, not aggregated."*).

## 4. What would settle it, and what would refute it

**To settle P at the ground:** an instrument that reconstructs the prebiotic route and distinguishes which substrate occupied both slots first. Not an argument, not a citation count, not a consensus. Fig 2 :29: *"An anchor is whatever settles the question and does not change its answer because of what any frame thinks."*

**To settle S and C at the ground:** continuous self-replication from a realistic feedstock without peptides, minerals or externally imposed environmental cycling. Le Vay and Mutschler 2019 report this has not yet been done.

**Standing falsifiers of the shape, written before the run:**

1. Show that an ordering and a directedness are one physical capacity, collapsing the union (breaks Fig 13 :35-36).
2. Admit a within-kind composition the framework cannot refuse (breaks Fig 13 :74).
3. Derive one of the six terms from the other five (breaks Fig 13 indivisibility).
4. Show a catalyst in the Fig 12 :119 sense is spent by its own use but still must be counted as a catalyst.
5. Demonstrate a maintainer seat in prebiotic chemistry, so selection did not run (breaks Fig 11 :36).
6. Derive RNA by name from the six-term set with no chemistry import (breaks Fig 9 :32).

## 5. The honest stop

**Unreached, not unreachable in principle.** The six-term set is *"a human's careful guess at reality's compression axes"* (carried essay quote, *They're Scanning Books*); declaring it makes the choice visible, not neutral. It cannot say which primitive is missing (Fig 13 :135), and a search that finds nothing proves no absence (Fig 8 :39). The answer stops at the limit of the available instruments and records the lineage so the stop is checkable. *"Indiscernibility is not identity."*

Strains kept visible: strict isomerism versus shared atom vocabulary (`06` Q1); the ordering and directedness binding to spectrum and gradient (Fig 13 does not assign slots); the world conflict over which substrate came first, returned to census without flipping the verdict (`01` §7).

## 6. One-paragraph version for outreach

> The Tether does not say RNA was the first molecule, and it does not claim to settle origins of life. It says the question is three conditions, not one: storage, catalysis and priority, and a result on one does not carry across the others. It says a single molecule can legally compose an ordered sequence with a directed catalytic function, because the framework forbids composition within one kind, not across two. It says such a molecule must be a catalyst that survives its own use and a cycle whose output re-enters as input, and that once the ground settles it, it becomes an atom for Darwinian evolution. It refuses to name RNA, because the framework returns a shape, not a name, and it reports the question of which substrate came first as a gap rather than an answer. The lab self-replicating enzymes show the shape is reachable, not that the historical route has been found. What is owed is a chronology instrument and a prebiotic feedstock experiment, not more agreement.
