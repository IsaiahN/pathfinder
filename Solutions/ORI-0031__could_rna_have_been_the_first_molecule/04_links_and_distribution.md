## Two things in the literature that bear directly on the question

1. **The field itself separates "RNA world existed" from "RNA was first."** Robertson and Joyce 2012 make the distinction explicitly, and Bernhardt 2012 says the hypothesis does not necessarily imply RNA was the first replicator. This is exactly the S, C, P split of F1. Do not collapse the existence claim into the priority claim in an email.
2. **The lab result is a cycle, and it is a harness.** Lincoln and Joyce 2009 demonstrate a genuine cross-catalytic cycle with heritable variation and selection, which corroborates Fig 12 :119 and :121. Le Vay and Mutschler 2019 state that robust continuous replication from a realistic feedstock has not been achieved. The honest sentence is: the shape is instantiated in the lab, the historical route is not reconstructed. Fig 11 :70 governs the difference.

## Legal and practical constraints

Use public institutional pages and submission portals only. The ISSOL contact page publishes named staff emails; they are deliberately not reproduced in `03_public_contact_points.csv`. Unsolicited bulk email risks breach of GDPR and CAN-SPAM style rules and will get a sending domain blacklisted. On top of that practical failure stands the structural one: a bulk send selected for agreement manufactures the disqualifying correlation (Fig 2 :40).
