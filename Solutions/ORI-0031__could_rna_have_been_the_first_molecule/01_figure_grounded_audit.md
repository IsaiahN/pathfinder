## 0. The question under audit, stated in effect terms

> Could RNA have been the first molecule able to store information and catalyze reactions?

Restated as an effect: the question asks whether one polymer can satisfy three conditions at once.

| condition | effect | usual modern occupant |
|---|---|---|
| S | heritable information storage, an ordering that can be copied | DNA |
| C | catalytic function, a directedness that survives its own use | protein enzymes |
| P | temporal priority over every other candidate substrate | not a function at all, a historical claim |

Option A: RNA satisfied S, C and P, so the chicken-and-egg dependency between storage and catalysis was collapsed by one molecule. Option B: at least one condition was occupied by a different substrate, or by a system rather than a molecule.

**What an answer would look like, bound as an ordinary term before the run.** An answer has the shape `one polymer, two cross-kind capacities, settled at the ground, with the priority claim either returned as `gap` or reversed by a chronology instrument`. This binding is written down now so it can accrue a residual and be refused like any other term (Fig 5 :65-66: *"make the sought-for shape an ORDINARY TERM. Write down what an answer would look like, bind it, and let it accrue a residual like anything else."*).

## 1. Residual and domain census, before any figure line is chosen

### 1.1 R by arity, symmetry and scale

- **Arity.** Three conditions (S, C, P), not one. They are a **union**, not a partition, because a case in two conditions is categorically different from a case in either (Fig 9 :57).
- **Symmetry.** S is an ordering, C is a directedness, P is an ordered claim. `→` is the bond that fits P: *"A then B; B needs A to have happened"* (Fig 12 :71). None of the three is interchangeable with another, so a pooled effect cannot decide them.
- **Scale.** First order inside one molecule, where an ordering and a directedness are composed. Second order at the population, where replication, variation and selection appear once the first molecule settles. A third, geological order carries the priority claim.

### 1.2 One line per domain, with actors, currency and anchor

A domain needs a nameable anchor or it is not yet a domain.

### 1.3 Partition or union

The residual is a **union** of S, C and P. D1 and D2 overlap on the composition of a dual-role molecule, so that overlap is a **compound with its own loop**; it is handled in F3 and F6, not folded into either parent. No figure line is chosen until after this census.

Fig 3 :20: *"The chain is ordered by dependency, not by importance. Each link consumes what the one above produces, so effort spent above the break arrives at a step that cannot use it."* Order: settle whether one molecule can hold S and C (D1/D2) before asking which substrate was first (D3); refuse before mint (Formula step 2); describe the residual before choosing a frame (Fig 9 :27-28). ## 3. Findings

### F1 — The question is a union of three conditions (decisive for framing)

Fig 9 :54: *"And when the split lands on a union rather than a partition, unbundle before you search again."* Fig 9 :55-56: *"A bundled problem is usually a union of overlapping conditions, not a list of separate ones. A single lever clears the region where only one condition holds and leaves the intersections standing, because the second condition still binds."* Fig 9 :57: *"What remains is smaller and it is also categorically different: a case in two conditions is not a harder case of either."*

Showing that RNA can catalyze (C) clears only the region where C alone holds. It leaves the storage condition (S) and the priority condition (P) standing. The frequent rhetorical move *"ribozymes exist, therefore RNA was first"* applies one lever to a union and concludes across all three.

**Mode:** `specified` on the union framing. **Triangulated** across Fig 9 :54-57 and Fig 13 :35-39 (independent figure routes: the leave/arrive split and the independence of the six kinds). **Refuter:** an instrument showing that S, C and P are one physical capacity, so that demonstrating one demonstrates all three.

### F2 — Storage and catalysis are distinct frames, not two names for one

Fig 13 :35: *"Space, time and energy are not three readings of one quantity. Neither are spectrum, gradient and network."* Fig 13 :36: *"cannot: adjacency is not duration, an ordering is not a pathway, a pathway is not a direction. If two members drew on the same underlying fact,"* Fig 13 :38-39: *"Which is Figure 2's condition applied to the set itself: independence is how little two frames draw on the same evidence, and it is what makes their agreement mean anything. Six members that overlapped would be fewer members wearing six names."*

An ordering (a base sequence, storage) is not a pathway (a directed chemical transformation, catalysis). That they co-occur in one molecule is a composition, not a discovery that they are the same thing. If they drew on the same underlying fact, their agreement would carry no information.

Fig 12 :94 reinforces this at the bond level: *"The arrangement is the substance."* The stored information is the arrangement, and the catalytic function is a different structural fact about the same arrangement.

**Mode:** `specified`, triangulated (Fig 13 kinds plus Fig 12 arrangement). **Refuter:** a measurement in which the same physical degree of freedom accounts for both heritable sequence and catalytic rate enhancement, collapsing the two frames into one.

### F3 — One molecule composing two capacities is figure-legal

Fig 13 :74: *"Everything else is a composition across the two kinds, never within one."* The rule forbids composing within one kind. Storage and catalysis are of different kinds under F2, so composing them in one molecule is exactly the move the figure licenses. The first-pass reading that this rule forbids a dual-role molecule is retracted in §6.

Fig 12 :119: *"catalyst. A part that survives its own use. Spent and regenerating parts behave differently across uses, and nothing records which a term is."* Fig 12 :121: *"cycle. The output re-enters as input. The sign of the loop decides whether it closes, and a chain cannot close by construction."* A ribozyme that catalyzes a reaction and remains available for the next is Fig 12's catalyst; a replicator whose product is its own template is Fig 12's cycle.

So the shape *one molecule, two cross-kind capacities* survives the figures. What does not survive is any claim that the figures derive which molecule occupies the slots.

**Mode:** `specified`, triangulated (Fig 13 cross-kind rule plus Fig 12 bond definitions, low overlap). **Refuter:** the framework admits a within-kind composition it cannot refuse, or a catalyst is demonstrated that is spent by its own use and still recorded as a catalyst.

### F4 — The figures cannot name RNA

Fig 9 :32: *"you get a kind of thing, never the answer itself"*. Fig 13 :134: *"So the system can report that it lacks something rather than producing an answer anyway. Unreachable is a verdict here, not a silence,"* Fig 13 :135: *"It cannot say which primitive is missing, because naming it would mean already holding it."*

No figure line selects RNA, DNA, PNA, peptides or a clay mineral. Naming RNA is a **binding** from the chemistry frame onto atoms the framework already holds, not a derivation and not an import that moves the wall. Fig 6 :21: *"MINT composes inside the closure and can never add an atom."* Fig 6 :23: *"No frame escapes its own closure. The bound moves only on contact with a frame whose closure differs."* An import needs a source gate and a shadow test: Fig 6 :12 *"source gate: provenance"*, Fig 6 :13 *"shadow test: explains a gap"*. Fig 8 :51: *"Convergent derivation and adopted import look identical in the contents. Only the record of where each came from separates them, and the surplus lives only where the closures differ."*

The world sources supply that binding as a **candidate with provenance**: the ribosome's peptidyl transferase centre is RNA (Robertson and Joyce 2012), ribozymes were discovered independently by Cech and Altman laboratories (Altman 1989; Martin, Unrau and Müller 2015), and cross-replicating RNA enzymes sustain amplification without proteins (Lincoln and Joyce 2009). These are secondary corroboration of a binding, never a figure verdict.

**Mode:** `general` on the limit, triangulated (Fig 9 :32 plus the canonical figures README). **Refuter:** a derivation from the six-term set that selects RNA by name without an import from chemistry.

### F5 — The "first" claim is a `gap`, reported as a verdict and not as silence

There is no figure line governing which physical substrate was temporally first. Fig 13 :134 makes the honest report possible: *"Unreachable is a verdict here, not a silence,"* and Fig 8 :39: *"So a search that finds nothing is never a proof of absence."*

The world frame is itself split on P. Robertson and Joyce 2012 write: *"There is now strong evidence indicating that an RNA World did indeed exist before DNA- and protein-based life. However, arguments regarding whether life on Earth began with RNA are more tenuous."* Bernhardt 2012: *"The RNA world hypothesis does not necessarily imply that RNA was the first replicating molecule to appear on the Earth."* Kauffman and Roli 2025 propose that small-molecule collectively autocatalytic sets came first and merged with peptide and RNA sets. Le Vay and Mutschler 2019 conclude: *"RNA in isolation may not be sufficient to catalyse its own replication and may require help from either other molecules or the environment."*

This disagreement is not noise to be averaged. It is the union of F1 showing up in the world literature. The figure verdict is `gap` on P, of the kind *unreachable at this budget*, not *underivable in principle*: the chemical record could in principle discriminate, but no instrument currently reconstructs the route.

**Mode:** `gap`. **Single-source on the figure side** (Fig 13 :134 and Fig 8 :39 are the same metalaw family), **triangulated with the world split** only in the weak sense that four independent authors decline the priority claim. **Reversed by:** a chronology instrument, not by agreement among researchers.

### F6 — The cycle and the catalyst have real instances, but the instances are harnesses

Fig 12 :119 and :121 (quoted in F3) are borne out in the laboratory. Lincoln and Joyce 2009 report a system where *"two enzymes catalyze each other's synthesis from a total of four component substrates"* and *"These cross-replicating RNA enzymes undergo self-sustained exponential amplification in the absence of proteins or other biological materials."* The Scripps news release describes it plainly: *"The replication process is cyclic, in that the first enzyme binds the two subunits that comprise the second enzyme and joins them to make a new copy of the second enzyme; while the second enzyme similarly binds and joins the two subunits that comprise the first enzyme."*

This corroborates the **shape**, not the historical route. Fig 11 :66: *"Isolation is not removal of the habitat. It is substitution of one habitat for another."* Fig 11 :70: *"A test harness is a substituted habitat. Which is why a synthetic solve proves wiring and never capability."* Fig 10 :24: *"the ground does not decay, the channel does"*, so the historical channel is what is missing, not the ground.

**Mode:** `specified` on the shape, `single-source` on the figure side (Fig 12 only), triangulated with Fig 11 :36 on selection without a maintainer. **Refuter:** show that the cross-replicators are spent by use, or that their output does not re-enter as input, or that the lab system reproduces the full prebiotic habitat rather than a substituted one.

### F7 — Selection ran; there was no maintainer seat

Fig 11 :36: *"Where nothing maintains anything, intelligence is a label applied afterwards to a lineage that met its ground. Nothing was intelligent; selection ran. The word does no work there."* Fig 11 :43: *"The habitat is enumerated, never composed."* Fig 11 :44: *"List everything in contact with the residual, then what is in contact with those, and outward until the cascade stops mattering."*

Prebiotic chemistry has no seat that chooses which molecule wins. The habitat is read off the world: feedstocks, mineral surfaces, ice, wet-dry cycles, UV, temperature and concentration gradients. Fig 11 :49: *"An actor's valence is relative to the residual, not a property it carries."* The same RNA strand is a benefactor to a replication residual and a malfactor (a parasite) to a fidelity residual. This is why the field's own questions about parasites and error thresholds are not a separate topic but the same actor under a changed residual.

Fig 11 :40: *"An improvement that does not change contact changes nothing, however much it improves."* A catalytic rate enhancement that does not change replication contact with the ground is inert at this stratum.

**Mode:** `general`, triangulated (Fig 11 plus Fig 2 :29 on the non-updating anchor). **Refuter:** demonstrate a maintainer seat acting in prebiotic chemistry, so that introduction rather than selection governed which substrate came first.

### F8 — Filters are filters; witnesses are witnesses

Fig 9 :60: *"Use filters for the budget and witnesses for the verdict. Never let a filter hand you a verdict."* Fig 7 :13: *"agreement compounds, evidence does not"*. Fig 2 :29-30: *"An anchor is whatever settles the question and does not change its answer because of what any frame thinks."*

The fact that RNA world is *"the most extensively studied model"* (Le Vay and Mutschler 2019) is a budget fact: it says where effort went. It is not a witness for P. A tally of papers, reviews or agreeing researchers cannot move a historical anchor. The anchor is the chemical and isotopic record plus replication experiments, each frame ranging against it separately (Fig 2 :45: *"Frames range against the ground. They do not negotiate with it."*).

**Mode:** `general`, triangulated (Fig 9 :60 plus Fig 7 :13 plus Fig 2 :29, low overlap). **Refuter:** a verdict on P produced by counting citations or endorsements.

## 4. Pre-registered refuter

Written before the conclusion, per Fig 5 :65-66 and the seat rule that *"a falsifier written before the run is a design instrument, not only an honesty device."*

The shape claim (one molecule can hold both S and C; the figures cannot name which substrate; the priority claim is a gap) is refuted if any of the following fires:

1. A re-census admits a composition within one kind that the framework cannot refuse (breaks Fig 13 :74).
2. An instrument shows that an ordering and a directedness are the same physical fact, so S and C are one condition (breaks Fig 13 :35-36 and the union reading of F1).
3. A derivation from the six-term set selects RNA by name with no import from chemistry (breaks Fig 9 :32 and F4).
4. One of the six terms is derived from the other five, or the kinds are not independent (breaks Fig 13 :41 and :38-39).
5. The cross-replicating systems are shown to be spent by use, or not to be cycles (breaks F6 at Fig 12 :119, :121).
6. A maintainer seat is demonstrated in prebiotic chemistry (breaks F7 at Fig 11 :36).

Separately, the **gap** on P is reversed, not refuted, by a chronology instrument that reconstructs which substrate actually occupied the two slots first. Agreement among researchers never reverses it (Fig 2 :40, Fig 7 :13).

Until one of these runs, nothing here is `grounded`.
