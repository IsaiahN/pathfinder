## 1. A shape is a constraint, not a filler

The figures README states the limit: the framework returns "what a missing thing would have to look like (a shape, not a name)". Fig 9 :32 "you get a kind of thing, never the answer itself." Fig 13 :134 "Unreachable is a verdict here, not a silence,".

From *The Library of Babel* (as quoted in the exemplar): "You can cut your search short if the set of books returned is small and descriptive enough that it provides a general shape of the answer that satisfies your question."

So a **shape** is the narrowed form of an answer you can state before you can name it. It constrains; it does not fill. For this question the shape is: a union of three overlapping conditions, per-slot indexing, different bonds and anchors, two valences of correlation, a closure wall, a substitutable habitat, and no universal coefficient. That is a shape. "Identity increases transmission by 12 percent" is a name, and it is not licensed by the framework.

## 2. How a shape is obtained

- *Library of Babel*: use the vocabulary you already have that is closest to the thing, look for recurring new terms that overlap or correlate with it, recurse. "With each new term gained, you narrow the space and deepen your vocabulary."
- Fig 6 :30 "cast it in effect terms." Fig 9 :30-31 "your words are borrowed too, so describe the effect rather than the cause." This is why the census in `01` describes identity as a correlation producer, trust as a channel weight and boundary as a closure wall, rather than starting from the discipline names.
- *Decoding Tacit Knowledge*: "When contact isn't possible, the best you can send is a plain description of what you'd expect to see, because anyone can check that against the world."
- Fig 9 :27-28 "describe it before you go looking, or any frame you pick will seem to fit." The residual was described before the social-identity, trust and network sources were picked. Had the sources been picked first, any of them would have seemed to fit.

## 3. What licenses a name

Only two things, both outside the shape itself:

1. **The ground settles it.** Formula step 5: "the ground settles it. Verified only by what pays." The README: the framework "stops at the limit of what the available instruments can distinguish, and records the lineage so the stop is honest."
2. **A provenanced import from a frame whose closure differs** (Fig 6 :23, Fig 8 :50-52), with a source gate and a shadow test.

*They're Scanning Books* sharpens this: "The real import is not a new term in the existing space. It is a new dimension of the space." And: "A minimum viable set of six terms is a human's careful guess at reality's compression axes; another sensorium's minimum set need not be those six." Also: "Declaring the primitives does not make them neutral. It makes the choice visible."

So when I call a boundary a closure wall or identity a symmetric bond, I am binding empirical findings onto held framework terms. That is a visible choice with provenance, not a discovery of nature's own list.

## 4. Why the ordinary reading commits the metamerism error

*The Hard Problem is About Source* gives the mechanism. A spectrometer reads green and "cannot comment on the source": one green light, or yellow plus blue, or RGB, or CMYK, or a scheme not yet discovered. "If several paths create the same behavior, then behavior is a projection with lossy information." And: "An instrument that can't distinguish these cases demonstrates its own inadequacy." It was the spectrometer, not an argument, that resolved it.

The ordinary reading takes an observed adoption pattern (the reading) and names its source: identity, or trust, or boundary. Several different mechanisms can produce the same adoption pattern. Fig 2 :13-15 shows one: a group with no anchor converges on whoever had the most influence; the resulting agreement is indistinguishable in the artifact from triangulation. Fig 8 :50-52: "Convergent derivation and adopted import look identical in the contents. Only the record of where each came from separates them."

What is owed is a discriminator (a per-slot instrument that separates ground contact from channel weight), not a name. This is F8's instrument in `01`.

*The Recursive Self-Improvement Trap*: "the output is only as good as the validator. The agent, or the tool, or the frame, or the theory, cannot validate itself."

## 5. Q2 ANSWERED: what licenses a numerical coefficient in this slot?

**Nothing in the framework.** The framework returns a shape. A numerical transmission coefficient is licensed only by the ground settling a measurement, or by a provenanced import with a shadow test. Until one of those, the number is the searcher's own contribution and must be recorded as such.

**Does the ordinary reading commit the error the README forbids?** Yes, twice. First, it averages across slots, which Fig 1 :22 says throws away the only signal that says where to look. Second, it names a source from a projection without a discriminator, which is the metamerism error. The correct form remains a separator with per-slot residuals and a declared gap where the number would go.

## 5b. Correction to my own section 3 and 4 (kept visible)

I overstated the "shape, not a name" rule on the first pass. The README's phrase is "what a **missing** thing would have to look like (a shape, not a name)." The subject is a primitive not held in Γ. Identity, trust and boundary are not missing primitives: network is already one of the six structures (Fig 13 :24 "network"), and the Operators table already holds the symmetric `+`, the threshold `⋛` and the weighting `w`. Naming them is a binding from the empirical frame onto held terms.

What remains wrong in the ordinary reading is not the naming. It is:
- averaging the residual across slots (Fig 1 :19, :22);
- treating a repeated convention as a settled term (Fig 5 amendment);
- claiming that adding a bridge imports an atom when the two closures share an evidence pool (Fig 8 :48-50);
- presenting a number where the framework returns a shape.

Both the earlier overstatement and this correction are recorded so neither is quietly lost. The same correction appears in `06_isomers_and_order.md` Q1 and in `01` F7.

## 6. Two consequences for outreach

**Prestige is not action.** From *The Recursive Self-Improvement Trap* (as quoted in the exemplar): "Prestige must never be allowed to validate an action directly... Someone let prestige stand in for proof... Prestige can fund an action. It can give an action a budget to be tried. But it cannot settle an action. Only the ground can settle it. The Ground Maintainer must be blind to prestige."

A campaign that gathers well-cited names to agree with the separator is the named collapse, not corroboration. The legitimate ask is the opposite: give the claim a budget to be tried and let the ground, a per-slot measurement, settle it.

**My own audit is a simulation, not a ground reading.** Same essay: "LLMs can quickly simulate an approximate answer/output... However, the truly testable output would need to be derived from the real architecture or working the formula yourself." LLM output is "simulacra." So `01` is a simulated derivation, checked for internal consistency against the figures. It is not a test of the claim and must be labelled as such. It cannot settle anything it reports on.

## 7. What would move this file from `general` to `specified`

A per-slot instrument that returns, for the same adoption event, (a) the receiver's channel weight for the source, (b) the independence between the two frames' evidence pools, and (c) the term's settlement state. With those three readings the shape becomes a checkable separator rather than a Constraint alone. Until then the honest mode for this file is `general`.
