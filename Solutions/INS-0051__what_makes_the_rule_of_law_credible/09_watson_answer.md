## QUESTION

Decide, in effect terms, what the Tether says makes a legal system's verdicts credible when those who enforce the law are under political pressure. Option A: credibility is a property of the enforcer's character, the written rules, or the amount of public approval. Option B: credibility is a property of a checking arrangement in which the anchor that settles a case cannot be moved by the political frames pressing on it. What each leads to: A produces a design that gathers agreement and mistakes it for an anchor, and it spends the independence of the people checking it. B produces a checkable shape, written refuters, and an open question until a per-case instrument settles it.

## DOMAIN CENSUS

R by arity, symmetry and scale, then one line per domain with actors / currency / anchor, partition or union, and order by dependency.

- **R (the residual).** The gap between the law's claim (verdicts settle by evidence and law) and what happens under pressure (rulings track whoever has the most influence). Arity: at least two political frames, one legal anchor, one checking seat. Symmetry: the anchor is deliberately asymmetric with the frames, it is pinned and does not negotiate; correlation has valence, so agreement can be disqualifying. Scale: one ruling, one court over many rulings, one polity over time. Fig 9 names this a union compound, not one hard question: a case in several conditions is not a harder case of one.
- **Domain 1, case-level adjudication.** Actors: judge or jury, parties, evidence. Currency: the ruling. Anchor: facts and law as settled, which neither side can move. Question type: triangulation against a non-updating ground. Verdict status: triangulated.
- **Domain 2, institutional checking arrangement.** Actors: courts, appointing bodies, executive, legislature. Currency: tenure, budget, case assignment, jurisdiction, written reasons. Anchor: the same ground, kept reachable by a seat that stays outside and declares its stake. Question type: seat design. Verdict status: triangulated for the seat rules; single-source for the lottery mechanism.
- **Domain 3, political pressure frame.** Actors: officeholders, factions, public opinion. Currency: influence and patronage. Anchor: none of its own. It is a frame acting on the other domains, not a domain with a ground, so it receives no separate verdict.
- **Domain 4, legitimacy and measurement.** Actors: citizens, litigants, observers. Currency: trust and belief. Anchor: observed behaviour of courts per case, not the belief itself. Question type: instrument design. Verdict status: triangulated.
- **Order by dependency (Fig 3).** Anchor first, then blind seat, then unauthored pool, then per-case instrument. If the anchor moves, nothing downstream matters. Optimising a link that already works cannot rescue one that does not.

## VERDICT (from the figures)

Credibility under political pressure is a checking-arrangement property, not a character, text or approval property. The facts and law that settle a case must not update when power pushes; the applier must be blind to the wanted result, must declare any stake in the reading, and must come from an unauthored pool whose independence is refreshed; and credibility must be measured per case by an instrument that can tell whether a ruling came from law or from pressure. This is a union compound, so no single lever is an answer. The framework does not name a constitution, and it does not treat public approval as evidence. Mode: `specified`, ungrounded, candidate.

- **cost if wrong:** bounded and locatable. The packet (file 08) states mode, three checkable sub-claims, verbatim lines and refuters before the conclusion, so an error is findable rather than invisible. The real cost is treating manufactured agreement as evidence and so spending the independence of outside readers, which Fig 2 :42 prices: "Buy independence in proportion to the cost of being wrong." That cost is avoidable by leading with the shape and its refuter. A further cost is the recorded sortition tension: buying an unauthored pool may reduce expertise unless competence is added separately.
- **undoable:** yes. The concrete bindings (tenure, rotation, disclosure, assignment rules) can be revised without touching the shape, and no primitive has to be withdrawn. A sent claim can be superseded by a corrected packet with the lineage intact.
- **reversed by:** a ground reading, not agreement. Per-case data showing that outcomes track political composition while evidence is held constant, or showing that they do not; an instrument that reads the source of a ruling from the verdict alone with no access to reasons or selection records; a derivation of one of the four conditions from the others (which would collapse the union to a partition); or a figure re-census that brings Fig 13 back into scope for institutional questions. Until one of these fires, the verdict stands as `candidate`.
