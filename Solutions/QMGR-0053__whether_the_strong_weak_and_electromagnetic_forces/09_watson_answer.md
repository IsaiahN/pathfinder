## VERDICT (from the figures)

The shape survives; any claim that the figures settle whether nature is unified does not. The question is a union of four overlapping conditions and must be split before search. The high-energy claim would be an identity tested by a threshold comparison; the three low-energy forces are a functional group, becoming a genuine isomer claim only over a full shared atom set; the number of forces is level-relative, though the level-to-energy reading is a declared binding and the one live strain. A larger gauge group is an import requiring provenance, and the convergence plot is a filter, not a witness. The anchor is proton-decay data and precision coupling measurements, most of the relevant scale beyond current reach. Non-observation kills models, not the shape. No specific group is figure-derived, and a GUT is not a final room. Status `specified`, `candidate`, ungrounded.

- **cost if wrong:** bounded and locatable. The packet states its mode, refuters and lineage before the conclusion, so an error is findable rather than invisible. The real cost is spending the independence of outside readers by sending a fit as a witness. Fig 2 :42 "Buy independence in proportion to the cost of being wrong." That cost is avoidable by leading with the shape claim and its refuter.
- **undoable:** yes. The defective readings (convergence as witness, group as figure-derived, non-observation as refutation) are removable without touching the derivation; `07_the_tether_answer.md` and `08_expert_verification_packet.md` already remove them. No primitive has to be withdrawn, and a superseding packet can replace a sent one.
- **reversed by:** a ground reading, not agreement. A proton-decay observation at a rate compatible with a specific group, or a limit incompatible with every simple-group embedding, would move the verdict toward or against the shape. A direct accessible coupling measurement, a derivation of a single coupling inside the Standard Model closure with no import, a demonstration that irreducibility cannot be read across to energy scales, or a proof that the four conditions form a partition would each break a specific finding. Until one fires, the verdict stands as `candidate`.
