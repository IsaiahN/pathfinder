## 1. A shape is a constraint, not a filler

The canonical figures README states the limit: the framework returns *"what a missing thing would have to look like (a shape, not a name)."* Fig 9 :32: *"you get a kind of thing, never the answer itself."* Fig 13 :134 to :135: *"Unreachable is a verdict here, not a silence,"* and *"It cannot say which primitive is missing, because naming it would mean already holding it."*

From *The Library of Babel*: *"You can cut your search short if the set of books returned is small and descriptive enough that it provides a general shape of the answer that satisfies your question."*

So a **shape** is the narrowed form of an answer you can state before you can name it. It constrains; it does not fill. For this question:

- **Shape:** one simple gauge group with one coupling above a breaking scale, broken to the Standard Model, the three observed couplings as its running images, and a falsifiable low-energy trace.
- **Name:** SU(5), SO(10), E6, or any other specific group. The name is not in the shape.

The shape also carries a warning from the same essay: *"Perhaps your problem was a bundle or conflation of several problems masquerading as one?"* That is the source of finding F1 in `01_figure_grounded_audit.md`.

## 2. How a shape is obtained

- *Library of Babel*: use the vocabulary already held that is closest to the thing, look for recurring new terms that overlap or correlate with it, recurse. *"With each new term gained, you are able to narrow the space by definition and deepen your vocabulary."*
- Fig 6 :30: *"The description travels. Fails by being sharpened into terms only we can read. Cast it in effect terms."* Fig 9 :30 to :31: *"your words are borrowed too, so describe the effect rather than the cause."*
- *Decoding Tacit Knowledge*: *"When contact isn't possible, the best you can send is a plain description of what you'd expect to see, because anyone can check that against the world."*
- *Library of Babel* on stopping: *"You will know that you are done because the finish line will also be the starting place, as there is nowhere else to search and nothing else that can describe the answer better."*

Applied here: describe the residual in effect terms (three couplings versus one, convergence versus reach) before choosing a gauge group or a review article. Otherwise any group will seem to fit, which is Fig 9 :27 to :28.

## 3. What licenses a name

Only two things, both outside the shape itself:

1. **The ground settles it.** `THE_FORMULA.md` step 5: *"the ground settles it. Verified only by what pays. A gate is not the ground."* Here that would be a proton-decay observation or an accessible precision measurement.
2. **A provenanced import from a frame whose closure differs** (Fig 6 :23, Fig 8 :50), with a source gate and a shadow test. A specific GUT is such an import.

*They're Scanning Books*: *"The real import is not a new term in the existing space. It is a new dimension of the space."* And: *"A minimum viable set of six terms is a human's careful guess at reality's compression axes; another sensorium's minimum set need not be those six."* So the six-term substrate is a visible guess, and naming a GUT group is an imported guess on top of it. Declaring either does not make it neutral.

**Correction to my own first reading.** I initially wrote that the "shape, not a name" rule forbids naming any specific gauge group. The README's phrase governs a **missing** primitive, one not held in Γ. SU(5) and SO(10) are not held primitives of the six-term set, so a name carried by a provenanced import is allowed, provided it is recorded as an import. What is forbidden is pretending the figure derived the name. Both readings are kept visible here and in `01` section 6.

## 4. The ground and the source problem

*The Hard Problem is About Source* gives the mechanism. Metamerism: an instrument reads green and *"cannot comment on the source"*. The essay states the problem directly: *"If several paths create the same behavior, then behavior is a projection with lossy information"*, and the resolution: *"The answer is unreached until a better instrument exists."*

The convergence of three couplings is a low-energy reading. Several different high-energy models can produce that same reading. The reading does not determine the source. Naming a GUT from the convergence plot is the metamerism error. What is owed is a source-discriminating instrument (proton decay, or an accessible precision test), not a name.

*How Intelligence Actually Works* sharpens the honesty requirement: *"the projection that no instrument distinguishes from the thing is still a projection, which is why the lineage has to be kept even when the copy is perfect."* And: *"indiscernibility is not identity."* A GUT that fits every current measurement is still a projection until an instrument discriminates it.

## 5. Answer to the shape question

**What is the shape of an answer to "do the three forces unify?"** One coupling above a breaking scale, three running images below it, a threshold comparison among them, and an observable trace that an instrument could in principle discriminate. The framework adds that the number of forces is level-relative (Fig 12 :104) and that no final room exists (Fig 7 :6). It does not answer yes or no about nature.

**Does this folder commit the forbidden error?** No, provided it keeps the group unnamed and the convergence labelled a filter. The shape is bound as an ordinary term in `01` section 0 so it can be refused, which is the discipline `THE_FORMULA.md` "Direction" requires.

## 6. Consequences for contact

**Prestige is not action.** *The Recursive Self-Improvement Trap* states: *"Prestige must never be allowed to validate an action directly"*, and *"Prestige can fund an action. It can give an action a budget to be tried. But it cannot settle an action. Only the ground can settle it."* A list of famous names who agree that unification is elegant is the named collapse, not corroboration.

**My own audit is a simulation, not a ground reading.** Same essay: *"the output is only as good as the validator. The agent, or the tool, or the frame, or the theory, cannot validate itself."* And the *Arrival of the Fittest* note: the outputs *"could never be verified if the solutions presented were not contrived to fit the formula."* So `01` is a simulated derivation checked for internal consistency against the figures. It is not a test of grand unification and must be labelled as such. It cannot settle anything it reports on.

**Diversity of frames matters.** *How a Society of Agents Would Work*: *"a group is only worth having while its members stay different."* Ten GUT enthusiasts reading the same review are one frame. The useful outside reader is one whose closure differs and who can name a failure the derivation did not anticipate.
