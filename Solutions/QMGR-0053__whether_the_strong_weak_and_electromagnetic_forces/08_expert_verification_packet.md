# Expert verification packet: the Tether on force unification

**What this is:** a claim in a form a specialist can check. Mode, derivation and refuters are stated before the conclusion, so a reader knows what to look for and what would settle it.

**Mode: `specified` - not `grounded`.** No measurement at the unification scale is claimed. This is a shape and a route, not a result.

## 1. The claim, in one paragraph

The framework does not say whether nature unifies the three forces. It says that the number of fundamental forces is a fact about the level of description, not an absolute fact about the world; that a unified coupling would be an identity, three names for one referent, tested by a threshold comparison of the running couplings; that the three low-energy forces are a functional group rather than isomers unless written over a full shared atom set; that any larger gauge group is an import, not a derivation inside the Standard Model closure; and that the convergence of couplings is a filter for model selection, not a witness. The ground that would settle it is a source-discriminating instrument, above all proton decay. What the framework offers is the shape any answer must have, falsifiers written in advance, and an honest stop recorded as `candidate`.

## 2. Three checkable sub-claims

### C1 - framework-internal; checkable by reading Figure 12 and the Operators table

- **Statement:** the high-energy unification claim is an identity (≡), not a blend; the convergence test is a comparison (⋛), and the two bonds are not interchangeable.
- **Source, verbatim:** Fig 12 :67 - *"two names, one referent"*; test at :69 - *"substitute one for the other. Is anything lost? No."* Fig 12 :87 - *"a threshold relation between two quantities"*; test - *"is it about which is larger, rather than both being present?"*
- **Refuter:** show that the high-energy claim requires two referents, or that the convergence test is not a threshold relation. Either breaks the bond assignment.

### C2 - physics-facing; checkable by a GUT phenomenologist against known models

- **Statement:** as written in the Standard Model, the three forces are a **functional group** (Fig 12 :123: *"the group says what kind; the qualifier says which member"*), not isomers. Over the full atom set of a candidate unified group, differing only in breaking channel, they become a genuine **isomer** claim (Fig 12 :117: *"Same atoms, different bond, different substance. The difference is invisible in the formula and decisive in the behaviour."*). The number of fundamental forces is level-relative (Fig 12 :104: *"So irreducible is a fact about a level and never about the world."*).
- **What to check:** is each characterisation faithful? Can SU(3), SU(2) and U(1) be written over a single shared representation set differing only in arrangement? Is the reading of irreducibility across to renormalization-group energy defensible, or is it only an analogy? That last question is the one open strain; it is named, not hidden.
- **Refuter:** exhibit a force that requires an atom the others do not, which kills the isomer claim and leaves the functional-group claim standing; or show irreducibility cannot be read across to energy scales at all, which downgrades C2 to an analogy.

### C3 - framework-internal derivation

- **Statement:** a larger gauge group is an import, and the convergence plot is a filter. Fig 6 :21: *"MINT composes inside the closure and can never add an atom."* Fig 9 :60: *"Use filters for the budget and witnesses for the verdict. Never let a filter hand you a verdict."* Non-observation does not refute the shape: Fig 8 :39: *"So a search that finds nothing is never a proof of absence."*
- **Refuter:** derive a single coupling entirely inside the Standard Model closure with no imported group; or show the convergence plot carries independent ground contact beyond the data used to produce it. Either breaks C3.

## 3. What is explicitly not claimed

- Not a theory of everything. No final room (Fig 7 :6: *"the limit: no last room."*).
- Not an answer to whether nature is unified. The figures cannot settle it.
- Not that the MSSM coupling match is a witness. It is a model-dependent fit.
- Not that any specific group, SU(5), SO(10) or E6, is figure-derived. Those are imports; provenance is required and is not supplied here.
- Not that proton decay non-observation kills unification. It kills models.
- Not `grounded`. Nothing here is asserted as settled by experiment.
- Not a claim that the six-term substrate is nature's own list. It is a visible guess.

## 5. What would settle it

A source-discriminating instrument, not a further argument. From *The Hard Problem is About Source*: an instrument that reads green *"cannot comment on the source"*; the spectrometer resolved it.

1. A proton-decay observation at a rate compatible with a specific simple group, or a limit incompatible with every simple-group embedding.
2. A direct, accessible precision measurement that shows whether the three couplings meet, and at what scale, without extrapolation under an assumed particle content.
3. A proof that irreducibility cannot be read across to energy scales, which would clear the one named strain rather than settle the physics.

## 6. What an expert can check in twenty minutes
