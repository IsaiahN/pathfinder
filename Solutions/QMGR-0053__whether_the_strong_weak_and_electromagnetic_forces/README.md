## The residual in effect terms

At low energy three gauge couplings are observed, one each for the strong, weak and electromagnetic (hypercharge) interactions. They run with energy. The question asks whether they converge to a single coupling under one larger gauge group at a high scale. The effect to be decided: does the residual between three low-energy couplings and one high-energy coupling close at a scale, and would that be a single underlying referent or merely three curves crossing? An answer would look like a shape: one simple gauge group, one coupling, spontaneous breaking to the Standard Model at a high scale, plus an observable consequence such as proton decay. We bind that shape as an ordinary term in section 0 of `01_figure_grounded_audit.md`, so it can accrue a residual and be refused.

## What is deliberately not here

- No personal emails of named individuals.
- No ranking of people by how likely they are to believe a unified theory.
- No bulk-send plan.

Three reasons, in the figures' own words:

1. **A room does not upgrade the label.** A list of sympathisers is not an anchor. Fig 2 :35: *"Alignment is triangulation, not negotiation. A mirror cannot correct you."*
2. **Selecting for receptiveness manufactures the disqualifying correlation.** Fig 2 :40: *"agreement about the world is worthless when the agreeing frames share an evidence pool."* GUT phenomenologists reading the same review are one witness, not two.
3. **Asking people to trust before they check spends their independence.** Fig 10 :42: *"A verifier that can reconstruct the claim cannot verify it."*

Legally: scraping personal emails for unsolicited bulk outreach breaches GDPR and CAN-SPAM style rules in many jurisdictions and will get the sending domain blacklisted. That is a practical failure on top of the structural one.

## The single most important caveat

This is a simulated derivation checked for internal consistency against the figures, not a ground reading. It cannot settle whether the forces unify. What it can do is state the shape any answer must have, name the sub-questions that must be split apart, and identify the instrument that would settle it. The world sources in `09_watson_answer.md` are secondary corroboration only, and most of the physics sources share one evidence pool.
