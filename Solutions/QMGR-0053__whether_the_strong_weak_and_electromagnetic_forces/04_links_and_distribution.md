## What in the wider literature bears on this question

1. **The convergence is real but conditional.** Scholarpedia reports that the three couplings do not quite meet under the Standard Model extrapolation but match well once supersymmetric partners are included, at roughly 3 x 10^16 GeV. This is a fit under a model assumption, not a direct reading. Fig 2 :29: *"An anchor is whatever settles the question and does not change its answer because of what any frame thinks."* The fit changes when the assumed particle content changes, so it is not the anchor.
2. **The ground is proton decay, and it has spoken against the simplest model.** Quanta reports that minimal SU(5) was falsified and that Super-K has pushed the proton lifetime limit into the range of several surviving models. Fig 8 :39: *"So a search that finds nothing is never a proof of absence."* Non-observation kills models, not the general shape.
3. **Reach is a separate condition.** Wikipedia notes GUT-scale particles are beyond any foreseen hadron collider. Fig 6 :27 says *"some unreachability is depth and some is genuine absence, and from inside the frame the two look the same."* Do not convert a budget limit into a metaphysical verdict.
4. **Unity as a cognitive preference.** SEP asks whether unity is a feature of reality or of our modes of cognition. This corroborates Fig 9 :60: *"Use filters for the budget and witnesses for the verdict. Never let a filter hand you a verdict."*
5. **The source problem.** The Hard Problem essay's metamerism point transfers directly: several different high-energy models can give the same low-energy coupling reading, so the reading does not determine the source. What is owed is a discriminating instrument, not a name.

## Distribution: offer, do not push

- **Fig 4 :22 to :25:** *"Only methods cross up. Only head starts come down. A recording carried upward looks like knowledge and is a description of one occasion."* Sending a full framework before anyone has described a residual sends a recording. Lead with the shape claim and the refuter; offer links after.
- **Fig 10 :42:** *"A verifier that can reconstruct the claim cannot verify it."* A recipient who has absorbed the entire framework before being asked for a verdict has joined the frame being checked.
- **Fig 9 :27 to :28:** *"describe it before you go looking, or any frame you pick will seem to fit."* Ask what they would expect to see if the shape claim were true before giving them the article.
- **Fig 2 :40:** *"agreement about the world is worthless when the agreeing frames share an evidence pool."* A send selected for people who already favour unification produces a convention, not corroboration.

**Recommended order in a reply:** (1) the shape claim and its three refuters; (2) ask what they would count as a failure; (3) record their verdict; (4) only then, the links, with the note that the figures and the published reviews, not my summary, are the authority.

## The two-stage offer

1. **Stage 1:** send the residual, the shape claim and the pre-registered refuter. Ask what the recipient would count as a failure. Record their verdict before showing any theory. This is Fig 10 :52, *"Bring a characterised residual, never the source,"* and Fig 2 :45, *"Frames range against the ground. They do not negotiate with it."*
2. **Stage 2, opt-in only:** if they are curious, offer the three source links. Their later agreement is never pooled as corroboration (Fig 2 :40).
3. **Stage 3:** offer the formula as a method to reuse. Fig 4 :22 permits sending a generator. Each user must write their own refuter and state their own mode, because a loop cannot check the direction of its own question (`THE_FORMULA.md`, "Direction").

**Hard precondition:** stages 1 to 3 only work if the stage-1 output survives. As currently drafted it does, but only as a `specified` shape. Any attempt to present the MSSM convergence as a witness or a specific group as figure-derived collapses the offer on contact.
