# The Tether's answer: Whether the strong, weak, and electromagnetic forces unify at high energies

**Mode: `specified` - not `grounded`.** This is a shape and a route, not a result. Simulated derivation checked against the figures (`05_what_shape_means.md` section 6), not a ground reading.

## 1. The residual it answers

Three gauge couplings are measured at low energy and the question asks whether they become one at high energy. The framework does not answer yes or no about nature. It answers a different, prior question: what shape would any answer have, which sub-questions must be separated, and what would settle it. The residual is a **union compound**, not one hard question (Fig 9: *"are not one hard question but several well-formed ones. Split it rather than search."*). Its four conditions are model consistency, empirical convergence, experimental reach, and uniqueness plus falsification.

## 2. The shape

1. **The number of fundamental forces is level-relative.** Fig 12 :104: *"So irreducible is a fact about a level and never about the world."* Three couplings at low energy and one at high energy are not a contradiction. The mapping from "level" to "energy scale" is a declared binding, still to be checked, not a figure-given identity.
2. **The high-energy claim is an identity, not a merge.** Fig 12 :67: *"two names, one referent."* At M_X the three couplings would be one coupling under one larger group. The extrapolation test is a comparison, Fig 12 :87: *"a threshold relation between two quantities."* Two different bonds, kept separate.
3. **The three forces are a functional group at low energy.** Fig 12 :123: *"the group says what kind; the qualifier says which member."* Over the full atom set of a candidate unified group they become a real isomer claim, Fig 12 :117: *"Same atoms, different bond, different substance."*
4. **The larger group is an import, not a derivation.** Fig 6 :21: *"MINT composes inside the closure and can never add an atom."* SU(5), SO(10) and E6 cannot be derived by rearranging the Standard Model's own primitives. They require a source gate and a shadow test.
5. **The convergence plot is a filter, not a witness.** Fig 9 :60: *"Use filters for the budget and witnesses for the verdict. Never let a filter hand you a verdict."* It selects which models to try; it does not settle the question.
6. **The anchor is experiment.** Fig 2 :29: *"An anchor is whatever settles the question and does not change its answer because of what any frame thinks."* Proton decay searches and precision coupling data are the ground. Agreement among theorists is not.
7. **No final room.** Fig 7 :6: *"the limit: no last room."* Even a confirmed GUT is not a theory of everything.
8. **The framework returns a shape, not a name.** Fig 9 :32: *"you get a kind of thing, never the answer itself."* It cannot say which group is realised in nature.

## 3. What it explicitly does not say

- It does not answer whether nature is actually unified. That is a question for the ground.
- It does not say the MSSM coupling match is evidence of unification. It is a model-dependent fit.
- It does not name SU(5), SO(10) or E6 as figure-derived. Those are imports with provenance, and the provenance is not supplied here.
- It does not say proton decay non-observation refutes unification. Fig 8 :39: *"So a search that finds nothing is never a proof of absence."* It kills models.
- It does not say a GUT is a theory of everything, and it does not overwrite settled low-energy physics.
- It is not `grounded`. No measurement at the unification scale is claimed.

## 4. What would settle it - the ground

A source-discriminating instrument, not a further argument. *The Hard Problem is About Source*: an instrument that reads green *"cannot comment on the source"*; the spectrometer resolved it. Here:

- **Proton decay:** an observation at a rate compatible with a specific simple group, or a limit incompatible with every simple-group embedding, would discriminate among sources. Non-observation at current reach does not close the general question (Fig 9 :44: *"Only a sealed room can be searched to the end, and no frame is a sealed room."*).
- **Accessibility:** GUT-scale particles are far beyond foreseen colliders. Fig 6 :27 says *"some unreachability is depth and some is genuine absence, and from inside the frame the two look the same."* A budget limit is not a metaphysical verdict.
- **Standing falsifiers from `01`:** a single coupling derived inside the Standard Model closure with no import; two or more referents at high energy; the four conditions shown to be a partition; the level-to-energy binding shown to be invalid.

## 5. The honest stop

**Unreached, not unreachable.** The answer stops at the limit of the available instruments and records the lineage so the stop is checkable. *They're Scanning Books*: *"Declaring the primitives does not make them neutral. It makes the choice visible."* The six-term substrate is a visible guess; a GUT group is a second, imported guess. *How Intelligence Actually Works*: *"indiscernibility is not identity."* A GUT that fits every current measurement remains a projection until an instrument discriminates it. Status `candidate`.

## 6. One-paragraph version for outreach

> The Tether does not answer whether the three forces unify, and it does not claim the final theory. It says that the number of fundamental forces is a fact about the energy level, not an absolute fact about the world; that the high-energy claim would be an identity, three names for one coupling, tested by a threshold comparison of the running couplings; that the three low-energy forces are a functional group rather than isomers unless written over a full shared atom set; that any larger gauge group is an import, not something derived from the Standard Model; and that the beautiful convergence plot is a filter for model selection, not evidence. The ground that would settle it is a source-discriminating instrument, above all proton decay, and most of the relevant scale lies beyond current reach. What the framework offers is the shape any answer must have, four sub-questions kept separate, falsifiers written in advance, and an honest stop recorded as a candidate. The question stays open until the ground speaks.
