## 1. A shape is a constraint, not a filler

The canonical limit: the framework returns *"what a missing thing would have to look like (a shape, not a name)"*. Fig 9 :32: *"you get a kind of thing, never the answer itself"*. Fig 13 :134: *"Unreachable is a verdict here, not a silence,"* and :135 *"It cannot say which primitive is missing, because naming it would mean already holding it."*

From *The Library of Babel*: *"You can cut your search short if the set of books returned is small and descriptive enough that it provides a general shape of the answer that satisfies your question."*

So a **shape** is the narrowed form of an answer you can state before you can name it. It constrains; it does not fill. For this question:

- The shape is not "the cell averages its inputs". That is a name for one candidate bond.
- The shape is not "the winner pathway takes over". That is a name for one candidate collapse.
- The shape is: residual kept per pathway, typed bond named, ordered where order carries information, triangulation against a non-updating ground, and a channel kept reachable.

The first two are candidate answers that the shape test can refuse. The last set is what any answer must satisfy before it is worth testing.

## 2. How a shape is obtained

- *Library of Babel*: use the vocabulary you already have that is closest to the thing, look for recurring new terms that overlap with it, recurse. *"With each new term gained, you are able to narrow the space by definition and deepen your vocabulary."*
- *Decoding Tacit Knowledge*: *"When contact isn't possible, the best you can send is a plain description of what you'd expect to see, because anyone can check that against the world."* The outreach in `02` does exactly this: four constraints a checker can look for, before any framework is shown.
- Fig 6 :30: *"The description travels. Fails by being sharpened into terms only we can read. Cast it in effect terms."* Fig 9 :30-31: *"your words are borrowed too, so describe the effect rather than the cause."*
- *Library of Babel* on stopping: *"You will know that you are done because the finish line will also be the starting place"*, and the paradox that by then you have *"already composed the answer through the process of locating it."*
- Fig 8 :53: *"Residual first, frame second."* The residual was described in effect terms in `01` section 0 before any figure line was selected. Cell biology was not chosen first and then mapped onto the figures.

## 3. What licenses a name

Only two things, both outside the shape itself:

1. **The ground settles it.** `THE_FORMULA.md` step 5: *"the ground settles it. Verified only by what pays. A gate is not the ground."* The canonical README: the framework *"stops at the limit of what the available instruments can distinguish, and records the lineage so the stop is honest."*
2. **A provenanced import from a frame whose closure differs** (Fig 6 :23, Fig 8 :51), with a source gate and a shadow test.

*They're Scanning Books* sharpens this: *"The real import is not a new term in the existing space. It is a new dimension of the space."* And: *"A minimum viable set of six terms is a human's careful guess at reality's compression axes; another sensorium's minimum set need not be those six."* Also: *"Declaring the primitives does not make them neutral. It makes the choice visible."*

Here, naming ERK, NF-kappa B or mTOR is a binding from the cell-biology frame onto a held atom or a provenanced import. It is not the framework naming a missing primitive, and it does not move the wall. What remains owed is the instrument that discriminates among candidate mechanisms, not a name chosen from a reading.

## 4. Why the ordinary word "integration" must be re-grounded

*The Hard Problem is About Source* gives the mechanism. Metamerism: a spectrometer reads green and *"cannot comment on the source"*. *"If several paths create the same behavior, then behavior is a projection with lossy information"*. The reading does not determine the source. *"An instrument that can't distinguish these cases demonstrates its own inadequacy"*. It was the spectrometer, not an argument, that resolved it.

Applied here: observing a single phenotype, such as "the cell divided", cannot tell you which bond produced it. AND, OR, sequence and threshold can all end in division. A pathway-activity reading is a projection with lossy information about the composition. What is owed is an instrument that discriminates sources (a time-resolved, pathway-specific reporter with a known ground), not a name assigned from the output.

*How Intelligence Actually Works* adds provenance loss: the frame and the photograph can be indistinguishable as artifacts, *"and nothing in the image records which"*. Two pathways can compose the same fate by convergent derivation or by one importing the other's output; *"Convergent derivation and adopted import look identical in the contents. Only the record of where each came from separates them"* (Fig 8 :51). Hence the ledger in `10_source_ledger.csv`: the record of which claims came from which frame is part of the result.

## 5. What shape means specifically for INF-0044

Bound as an ordinary term before the run, per the "Direction" section of `THE_FORMULA.md`: a shape for this answer has four load-bearing constraints and one declared stop.

1. **Per-slot indexing.** The residual stays indexed per pathway; a scalar aggregate can be near zero with one live slot residual (Fig 1 :13, :19, :22). The shape refuses a single "total pathway activity" number as the decision variable.
2. **Typed bond.** The relation between pathway outputs is itself information (Fig 12 :46, :94). The shape requires the candidate answer to say whether the bond is +, ∥, →, ⇒, − or ⋛, and to state the identifying test.
3. **Ordered where order matters.** Arrival at different times invokes → or ⇒ with the swap test (Fig 12 :72, :74). The shape requires the answer to say whether the specific decision is order-sensitive, rather than assuming it is noise.
4. **Triangulation.** The reference does not move when the cell decides (Fig 2 :29, :33, :35). The shape requires an external condition and an independence statement, not agreement among correlated pathways.
5. **Stop.** The molecular identity is a `gap` (Fig 9 :32, Fig 13 :134). The shape stops there and records the lineage.

All five are checkable without knowing which proteins are involved. That is what makes the shape useful to the experts in `03_public_contact_points.csv`.

## 6. Correction to my own section 3

I overstated "shape, not a name" on the first pass. The canonical phrase is *"what a **missing** thing would have to look like (a shape, not a name)"*. It governs a primitive not held in the library. A candidate molecular mechanism is not a seventh primitive; it is a binding from the biology frame onto compositions already available, or an import with a source gate. So naming a candidate protein is not forbidden by the shape rule. What is forbidden is presenting that binding as a figure derivation, or claiming it moves the wall when it adds no atom (Fig 6 :22: *"NOVEL means novel relative to atoms, not to the world. IMPORT adds an atom, and the added atom is what expands the closure."* adds none here).

The section 4 metamerism point is **not** retracted. The reason a name from a single phenotype reading is unsafe is the missing discriminator, not a ban on naming held atoms. Both readings are recorded so neither is quietly lost. This mirrors the correction in the imported exemplar's own `05` section 5b.

## 7. Two consequences for this solution

**Prestige is not action.** From *The Recursive Self-Improvement Trap*: *"Prestige must never be allowed to validate an action directly... Someone let prestige stand in for proof... Prestige can fund an action. It can give an action a budget to be tried. But it cannot settle an action. Only the ground can settle it. The Ground Maintainer must be blind to prestige."* A list of famous labs that agree with the shape is the named collapse, not corroboration. The legitimate ask is for a check against a ground.

**This audit is a simulation, not a ground reading.** Same essay: *"LLMs can quickly simulate an approximate answer/output with the formula found in the architecture post"*, but *"the truly testable output would need to be derived from the real architecture or working the formula yourself."* The architecture post itself says such outputs *"could never be verified if the solutions presented were not contrived to fit the formula"* and are *"simulacra"*. So `01`, `06`, `07`, `08`, `09` and `11` are simulated derivations checked for internal consistency against the figures. They are **not** tests of any claim about cells. They cannot settle anything they report on.

*How Intelligence Actually Works* supplies the closing caution: *"The recursion converges toward indiscernibility, and indiscernibility is not identity."* A shape that fits every known case is still a projection until an instrument discriminates among the sources that produce it. The lineage is what keeps that honest.
