# Outreach draft: check the shape claim before you decide about the framework

**Use:** one to one, or as a cover letter attached to a submission through the public venues in `03_public_contact_points.csv`. **Not** for bulk send.

**Hard precondition:** send only after the shape claim and its refuter survive the reader's own check. If the claim fails on contact, there is nothing else to look at.

---

**Subject:** One checkable claim about how a cell keeps pathway signals distinct

> Hi [name],
>
> I have one specific output I would like you to check against what you already know about signal integration. I am not asking you to read a framework first, and I am not asking you to agree with anything. If the output does not survive, there is nothing else to look at.
>
> **The output (`general` on shape, `specified` on the per-domain claims, no mechanism named):** a cell with several pathways must get from k pathway outputs to one fate decision without destroying four things. First, which pathway is live, because a scalar average of total activity can sit at baseline while one pathway carries a large unsolved demand. Second, the relation between the outputs, because the same two pathways under an AND, an OR, a sequence or a threshold are different substances with the same ingredients. Third, arrival order, because a cell that receives antigen then cytokine is not composing the same thing as one that receives cytokine then antigen. Fourth, a reference that does not move when the cell decides, otherwise the pathways update on one another and converge on whoever had the most influence.
>
> **What I am claiming and what I am not:** I am claiming that these four constraints are the shape any answer must have. I am not claiming a molecule. No figure line returns MAPK, NF-kappa B, mTOR, a scaffold or a rate constant. If a published circuit is named, it is an import from your field with provenance and a shadow test, not a result the framework produced. The framework returns a shape, not a name. I am also not claiming that cells simply average their inputs. That is one candidate bond, and it is the one that loses the signal that says where to look.
>
> **What would refute the shape claim.** I wrote these down before this email:
>
> 1. A case with two live conflicting pathways where collapsing their outputs to one scalar preserves every decision-relevant distinction, including which pathway needs attention next.
> 2. A case where AND, OR, sequence and threshold are observationally equivalent for a fixed pathway pair over the tested outcomes, so the relation carries no information.
> 3. A case where arrival order is behaviourally inert for every tested pair, and the background level before a step carries no order-dependent information.
> 4. A case where two pathways reading one upstream source, with no independent condition, still return a calibrated verdict about the outside world when that source is manipulated independently of the world.
>
> If any holds, the corresponding part of the claim is wrong and I will retract it. A one-line "this fails because..." is more useful to me than agreement. If you would rather not engage, no reply is needed.
>
> **What I would value from you:** which of the four constraints is already settled in your work, which one is missing an instrument, and what you would count as a failure. A correction on shape is the useful output.
>
> **If it survives, and only then:** here are the sources. The figures, not my summary of them, are the authority.
> - Verbatim figure text with line numbers (local extract): `FRAMEWORK/reference/03-FIGURES_TEXT.md`
> - Canonical usage rule (local): `FRAMEWORK/reference/FIGURES_README_canonical.md`
> - The eight-step loop and mode rule (local): `FRAMEWORK/figures/THE_FORMULA.md`
> - Public figures repository: https://github.com/IsaiahN/tether/tree/general-agent/figures
>
> **If you want to use the machinery:** the loop is in the figures link. Run it on a problem you know well and can check against a ground. Write down what would refute your result *before* you run it, and state the mode of what you get. The framework cannot check the direction of its own question, so that discipline is yours. A reported failure is more useful to me than a reported success.

## Stage order, kept distinct

1. **Stage 1:** residual, shape claim and pre-registered refuter. Record the reader's verdict before showing any framework material.
2. **Stage 2, opt in:** only if they ask, the three public links. A reader who has absorbed the framework before the verdict has joined the frame being checked.
3. **Stage 3:** the loop as a method to reuse, with the instruction that each person writes their own refuter and states their own mode.

Later agreement is never pooled as corroboration (Fig 2 :40). A failure is more useful than an endorsement.
