## 2. Wider literature actually opened

These are the world sources that bear on the shape claim. Full provenance and quotes are in `10_source_ledger.csv`; predictions and corroboration status are in `09_watson_answer.md`.

1. **Manella, Bolshette, Golik and Asher 2022, PNAS**, *"Input integration by the circadian clock exhibits nonadditivity and fold-change detection"* (PMC9636907). Verified quote: *"Often, the response was nonadditive and could not be readily predicted by the response to the individual signals."* Also: *"In the case of signals administered sequentially, the background levels of a signal attenuated subsequent resetting by the same signal, but not by signals acting through a different pathway."* This is the lowest-overlap corroborator for both nonadditivity and the pathway-specific order effect. It also supplies the boundary condition: two activators of the same pathway were mostly additive, so + is the right bond there and → or ⋛ is owed for different pathways in sequence.
2. **Hart, Mayo, Shoval and Alon 2013, PLOS ONE**, *"Comparing Apples and Oranges: Fold-Change Detection of Multiple Simultaneous Inputs"* (PMC3587607). Verified quote: *"When the two inputs affect two different receptors, other types of integration may be found and generally the system is not constrained to respond in a multiplicative (log-linear) way."* This is a direct world-side statement that integration form depends on the bond and receptor topology, not on the ingredient list.
3. **Grassmann et al. 2026, Immunity**, *"Step-wise epigenetic signal integration governs adaptive programming of cytotoxic lymphocytes"* (PMC13523785). Verified quote: *"This stepwise signal integration enables lymphocytes to acquire memory rather than effector fates."* The same antigen and cytokine signals, swapped in order, produce different fates. A biological instance of the → bond with a stated falsifier.
4. **Kamino et al. 2017, PNAS**, *"Fold-change detection and scale invariance of cell-cell signaling in social amoeba"* (PMC5448168). Verified quote: *"a response to cell-cell communication molecules is fold-change dependent"*. Relative change rather than absolute level is the ⋛ comparison bond on a channel, not a new primitive.
5. **Meyer et al. 2025, Nature Communications**, *"YAP charge patterning mediates signal integration through transcriptional co-condensates"* (PMC12343768). Verified quote: *"YAP/Mediator co-condensation is counteracted by negative feedback from transcription, driving an adaptive transcriptional response that is well-suited for decoding dynamic inputs."* An instance of a cycle (Fig 12 :121) used as a dynamic decoder.
6. **Wong et al. 2019, Biophysical Journal**, *"Fold-Change Detection of NF-kappa B at Target Genes with Different Transcript Outputs"* (PMC6382958). Verified quote: *"Our results suggest that cells use multiple biological mechanisms to tune transcriptional output while maintaining robustness of NF-kappa B fold-change detection."* The same shape is reached by more than one route, which is Fig 13 :121 applied inside one cell type.
7. **Wikipedia contributors, "Signal transduction"**, https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Signal_transduction. Verified quote: *"When signaling pathways interact with one another they form networks, which allow cellular responses to be coordinated, often by combinatorial signaling events."* A broad corroboration that pathways form networks and that coordination is combinatorial; cites Papin, Hunter, Palsson and Subramaniam 2005, Nature Reviews Molecular Cell Biology, which I did not open separately and therefore do not count as a second witness.

Overlap declaration: sources 1, 2, 4 and 6 come from the systems-biology and sensory-adaptation community and partly cite one another (Hart 2013 cites Shoval and Alon; Manella 2022 uses fold-change detection as an imported mechanism). They are one broad evidence pool, not four independent witnesses. Source 3 is immunology, source 5 is biophysics and condensates, source 7 is a general reference. The framework essays share the author's evidence pool completely. No world source conflicts with the figure verdict. Manella et al. refines its scope, which was returned to the census and recorded in `01` F3 as a union condition, not a reversal.

## 4. Distribution: offer, do not push

Figure-grounded reasons:

- **Fig 4 :22:** *"Only methods cross up. Only head starts come down."* Sending a recorded conclusion before a reader has described a residual sends a recording, not a generator. Lead with the shape and its refuter; offer links after.
- **Fig 10 :42:** *"A verifier that can reconstruct the claim cannot verify it."* A checker who has absorbed the whole framework before giving a verdict has joined the frame being checked.
- **Fig 9 :27-28:** *"describe it before you go looking, or any frame you pick will seem to fit"*. Ask what they would expect to see if the shape claim were true before giving them any source.
- **Fig 2 :42:** *"Buy independence in proportion to the cost of being wrong."* Buying a list of people likely to agree manufactures the disqualifying correlation.
- **Fig 8 :53:** *"Residual first, frame second."* The residual is what selects the checker, not a field name chosen for convenience.

**Recommended order in a reply:** (1) the four-constraint shape claim and its refuter; (2) ask what they would count as a failure and which constraint already has an instrument; (3) only then the three local framework links, with the note that the figures, not the summary, are the authority; (4) if they ask, the loop as a reusable method with their own refuter written first.

## 5. Legal and policy note

Unsolicited bulk email is constrained by GDPR and CAN-SPAM style rules in many jurisdictions. The venues in `03_public_contact_points.csv` are public submission or opt-in channels; no personal emails, no purchased lists and no automation are used. Some journals state a policy on AI-generated or simulated work. Check the current policy of the specific venue before submitting; a simulated derivation must be labelled as such rather than presented as a measured result.
