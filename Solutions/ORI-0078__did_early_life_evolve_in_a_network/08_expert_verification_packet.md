# Expert verification packet, the Tether on early gene sharing

**What this is:** a shape claim in a form an HGT researcher can check. Mode, derivation and refuters are stated before the conclusion, so a reader knows what to look for and what would settle it.

**Mode: `specified` on the shape, `general` on the bond rule, `gap` on the historical claim. Not `grounded`.** No measurement of deep time is claimed. This is a simulated derivation checked for internal consistency against the figures, not a ground reading.

## 1. The claim, in one paragraph

Relation is one of the six irreducible structures, so a set of communities with no gene flow is not a composable frame under the substrate. Gene sharing is import, and import is the only operator that adds reach to a closed community; a gene can be new to a lineage without being new to the world. A cleanly separated lineage is the degenerate case of a graph: one bond type, no cross-links. Since identical gene content is consistent with both shared ancestry and later transfer, the figures require per-slot residuals and provenance, not an averaged tree score. The framework returns this shape and stops; whether the first communities actually exchanged genes is a `gap` decided by instruments.

## 2. Three checkable sub-claims

### C1, framework-internal, checkable by reading Figures 13 and 6

- **Statement:** network is irreducible, and a closed community cannot acquire a gene from outside its closure without contact with a differing closure.
- **Source, verbatim:** Fig 13 :24 "that entities relate, and influence travels" and Fig 13 :41 "Each is indivisible: no arrangement of the other five yields it." Also Fig 6 :23 "No frame escapes its own closure. The bound moves only on contact with a frame whose closure differs." and Fig 6 :14 "the only operator that moves the wall".
- **What to check:** is relation genuinely one of six indivisible structures, and is import the only operator that moves a community's reach?
- **Refuter:** derive "entities relate" from the other five structures; or exhibit an operator other than import that moves the wall; or show a closed community acquiring an absent gene with no differing-closure contact.

### C2, framework-internal plus HGT-facing, checkable by a microbiologist or phylogeneticist

- **Statement:** a lineage is the degenerate one-bond network, and two gene trees over the same settled gene set are isomers under Fig 12.
- **Source, verbatim:** Fig 12 :48 "A chain is the degenerate case: one bond type, no branching." and Fig 12 :117 "isomer. Same atoms, different bond, different substance. The difference is invisible in the formula and decisive in the behaviour." and Fig 12 :104 "A settled molecule becomes an atom for whatever composes over it. So irreducible is a fact about a level and never about the world".
- **What to check:** is vertical descent faithfully represented as a single ordered bond, and is cross-community transfer faithfully represented as an additional edge? Does treating each settled gene as an atom at the tree level misdescribe anything about gene trees?
- **Refuter:** show that two discordant gene trees over the same gene set differ in atoms rather than bonds, for example that the genes themselves differ under any settled description; or show that a chain needs a bond unavailable to a graph.

### C3, method-facing, checkable by a phylogeneticist

- **Statement:** identity of gene content does not discriminate inheritance from import, so the residual must be read per gene, not averaged, and the discrimination needs an instrument.
- **Source, verbatim:** Fig 8 :51 "Convergent derivation and adopted import look identical in the contents. Only the record of where each came from separates them" and Fig 1 :19 "The residual is indexed by object slot, not aggregated." and Fig 1 :22 "Aggregating across slots is how a live signal disappears into an average."
- **What to check:** does this match the working problem of gene-tree discordance? Huson and Bryant (2006) introduce a conservative statistical test for whether the conflicting signal in a network is treelike; is that the kind of discriminator the figure is asking for?
- **Refuter:** produce a content-only statistic that distinguishes convergence from import without provenance, or a global tree statistic that preserves a transfer signal erased by per-slot aggregation.

## 3. What is explicitly not claimed

- Not that the figures prove early life was a network. The historical claim is `gap`.
- Not that cleanly separated lineages are impossible. They are the degenerate one-bond case, legal at a level.
- Not that a shared gene proves transfer. Convergence and import are identical in the contents.
- Not that one universal tree is false as a representation. It is a lossy condensation with a nonzero round-trip gap (Fig 4 :30 "So take the round trip and measure the gap. Without it you are navigating by dead reckoning: confident about a position nobody checked.").
- Not that Fig 10 :70 settles biological genealogy. It governs cross-scale transforms and is retained as a declared-strain analogue with zero verdict weight.
- Not `grounded`. Nothing here was settled by an instrument in this run.

## 5. What would settle it

1. Apply a treelike-versus-network signal test at the deepest resolvable gene sets. A decisive treelike result at the relevant scale moves Domain 3 toward clean descent; decisive reticulate signal moves it toward a network.
2. Any instrument that assigns a gene to one origin or to multiple origins independently of its sequence content breaks the convergence and import tie.
3. A per-slot reading that resolves transfer signal before aggregation.
4. Figure-level falsifiers: a closed community acquiring a gene from nowhere; deriving one of the six from the other five; a global statistic preserving an aggregation-sensitive signal; a deep-time reading returning clean lineages with no import.

## 6. What an expert can check in twenty minutes

1. Read seven short passages: Fig 13 :24, :41, Fig 6 :14, :23, Fig 12 :48, :117 and Fig 8 :51.
2. Check C2 against your own knowledge: is a lineage the degenerate graph, and are discordant gene trees isomers over a shared gene set?
3. Name one instrument that would distinguish shared ancestry from later transfer in the deepest resolvable data, or say why none is possible.
4. Send the refutation. A failure is more useful than an endorsement.
