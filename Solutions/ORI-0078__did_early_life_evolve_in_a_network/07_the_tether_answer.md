# The Tether's answer: network or cleanly separated lineages?

**Mode: `specified` on the figure-derived shape, `general` on the structural limit, `gap` on the historical claim. Not `grounded`.** Simulated derivation checked for internal consistency against the figures (`05_what_shape_means.md` section 7), not a ground reading. No instrument reading of deep time was taken.

## 1. The residual it answers

The question bundles three conditions that must not be averaged into one verdict (Fig 9 :54 "And when the split lands on a union rather than a partition, unbundle before you search again"): can a tree and a network both be real frames (compositional), did early communities actually exchange genes (empirical), and what instrument separates a tree from a network in the data (instrumental). Each keeps its own domain and its own verdict.

## 2. The shape, per domain

**Domain 1, molecular frame.** The figures do not name the genes of the first cells. They do say that a closed set cannot gain an atom except by import: Fig 6 :23 "No frame escapes its own closure. The bound moves only on contact with a frame whose closure differs." and Fig 6 :14 "the only operator that moves the wall". Read in effect terms, gene sharing between communities is import, and being new to the lineage is not being new to the world (Fig 6 :22 "NOVEL means novel relative to atoms, not to the world."), since an import adds no atom to the world (Fig 6 :25 "An import adds no atom to the world."). Verdict `specified`, triangulated across Fig 6 (a boundary theorem) and Fig 8 :45 "Two frames together reach what neither reaches alone." (a surplus theorem), low overlap between them.

**Domain 2, arrangement frame.** Network is an irreducible structure, not a derived nicety: Fig 13 :24 "that entities relate, and influence travels" and Fig 13 :42 "There is no route. Ordering does not produce adjacency; duration does not produce conservation; a quantity does not produce the fact that things relate at all." A clean lineage is the degenerate case: Fig 12 :48 "A chain is the degenerate case: one bond type, no branching." Both are legal compositions; the difference is the bond set, not the ingredients (Fig 12 :94 "The arrangement is the substance."). Verdict `general` for the bond rule, `specified` for the mapping to descent and transfer, triangulated across Fig 12 and Fig 13.

**Domain 3, historical frame.** The honest verdict is `gap`. Fig 8 :39 "So a search that finds nothing is never a proof of absence." and Fig 9 :44 "Only a sealed room can be searched to the end, and no frame is a sealed room." Deep time is an unsealed room. The figures contain no reading of the first biosphere, and the absence of a preserved transfer signal is not evidence of clean lineages. The world literature (Woese, Doolittle and Bapteste, Soucy and colleagues, Huson and Bryant) is secondary corroboration for the plausibility of the shape, not a verdict from the figures.

**Cross-cutting method.** Decide per gene, not by an averaged tree score: Fig 1 :19 "The residual is indexed by object slot, not aggregated." and Fig 1 :22 "Aggregating across slots is how a live signal disappears into an average." Identity of gene content does not discriminate inheritance from import: Fig 8 :51 "Convergent derivation and adopted import look identical in the contents. Only the record of where each came from separates them". Verdict `specified`, triangulated across Fig 1 and the phylogenetics instrument line.

## 3. What it explicitly does not say

- Not that the figures prove early life was a network. They constrain a shape; they do not report a history.
- Not that cleanly separated lineages are impossible as a description. They are the degenerate one-bond case and remain legal at a level.
- Not that sharing a gene proves transfer. Convergence and import look identical in the contents.
- Not that one universal tree is false as a representation. It is a lossy condensation with a nonzero round-trip gap (Fig 4 :30 "So take the round trip and measure the gap. Without it you are navigating by dead reckoning: confident about a position nobody checked.").
- Not the tree-of-life sentence as a direct verdict. Fig 10 :70 governs cross-scale transforms and is retained as a declared-strain analogue with zero verdict weight.
- Not a last room (Fig 7 :35 "A room's agreement is variation, not a verdict. Selection between rooms is where the ground votes.").
- Not `grounded`. No reading of deep time was taken in this run.

## 4. What would settle it, the ground

A source-discriminating instrument, not a further argument. From *The Hard Problem is About Source*: "the instrument is speaking only about what it measured, it cannot comment on the source." The concrete discriminators are:

1. **Treelike-signal test.** Apply the Huson and Bryant conservative test for conflicting signal in a network to the deepest resolvable gene sets. A decisive treelike result at the relevant scale moves Domain 3 toward clean descent; decisive reticulate signal moves it toward a network.
2. **Provenance instrument.** Any dating or contextual method that assigns a gene to a single origin or to multiple origins independently of its sequence content would break the convergence and import tie in Fig 8 :51.
3. **Per-slot reading.** A reading that resolves the transfer signal in individual gene slots before aggregation, satisfying Fig 1 :19.
4. **Standing falsifiers from `01_figure_grounded_audit.md`.** A closed community acquiring a gene absent from its closure with no differing-closure contact; deriving one of the six from the other five; a global statistic that preserves a transfer signal erased by aggregation; a deep-time reading returning clean lineages with no import.

## 5. The honest stop

**Unreached, not unreachable.** The figures give a shape and then stop at the limit of what the available instruments distinguish. Fig 13 :134 "Unreachable is a verdict here, not a silence". The record of the route is what keeps this checkable: the retracted reading of Fig 10 :70, the declared analogue strain, and the `gap` on the history are all kept visible rather than erased. Indiscernibility is not identity; a tree seen from outside is not thereby the whole relation.

## 6. One-paragraph version for outreach

> The Tether does not decide whether early life was a network. It says that relation is one of the six irreducible structures, so a purely isolated set of lineages is not a composable frame; that gene sharing is import, the only operator that can add reach to a closed community, with being new to the lineage not meaning new to the world; and that a clean chain is simply the degenerate case of a graph, so the two descriptions are rival bond arrangements rather than rival kinds of thing. It also says that two genomes sharing a gene cannot by themselves distinguish shared ancestry from later transfer: the contents match and only the provenance separates them, so the question must be asked gene by gene and then handed to instruments. The figures return this shape, record a nonzero gap between many gene trees and any one universal tree, and stop. Whether the first communities actually exchanged genes stays a `gap` here, settled by sequence data and source-discriminating instruments, not by a metatheory.
