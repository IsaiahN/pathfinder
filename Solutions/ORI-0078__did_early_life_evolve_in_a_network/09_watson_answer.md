## QUESTION

Decide, in effect terms, what the Tether says about the claim that early life evolved as a network of gene-sharing communities rather than as cleanly separated lineages. Option N, network: the unit is a community in contact, heritable material moves between communities, and any one lineage's genealogy is a lossy projection of that contact structure. Option L, lineages: the unit is a line of vertical descent, each gene has one ancestry, and the tree is fundamental. What each leads to: N sends a `specified` shape with a recorded `gap` on the history and a pre-registered refuter; L is retained as the degenerate one-bond case rather than refused. Answering the compositional question does not answer the empirical one.

## DOMAIN CENSUS

R by arity, symmetry and scale, then one line per domain with actors, currency and anchor, partition or union, and order by dependency.

- **R (the residual).** Two rival descriptions of early evolution. Arity: a relation among two or more entities, so a graph question. Symmetry: both options use ordered bonds (sequence or production for descent, donor to recipient for transfer), so symmetry does not decide between them; the deciding variable is crossability of closure. Scale: genes inside communities inside a biosphere, with a lossy transform between levels.
- **Domain 1, molecular-genetic frame, the atoms.** Actors: replicating and catalytic molecules, genes, plasmids. Currency: heritable sequence and function. Anchor: the ground, that is sequence data and experimental evolution, which does not change because a frame thinks otherwise. Nameable anchor: yes.
- **Domain 2, community-and-bond frame, the arrangement.** Actors: communities, the graph, the tree, the import event. Currency: bonds between entities. Anchor: the figures as metalaws for what may compose, plus the ground for what did compose. Nameable anchor: yes.
- **Domain 3, historical and verification frame.** Actors: sequence data, rocks, isotopes, instruments, the treelike-signal test. Currency: residuals and settlement. Anchor: the ground, which does not update; for deep time it is a partially closed channel. Nameable anchor: yes.
- **Union, not partition.** Three conditions are bundled: compositional (can a tree and a network both be real frames), empirical (did early communities actually exchange genes), instrumental (what would discriminate tree from network in the data). They have different anchors and need separate loops.
- **Order by dependency.** Perception (can discordance be seen) precedes vocabulary (terms for import versus vertical descent) precedes objective (what counts as progress) precedes planning and carrying. The middle link is instrumented in the world by Huson and Bryant's treelike-signal test, so the chain is traversable in principle. That is a claim about instrument availability, not about mode.

Strain test applied: the tree of life line in Fig 10 :70 governs cross-scale transforms, not genealogy at one stratum, so it is retained as a declared-strain analogue with zero verdict weight, not a witness for N.

## VERDICT (from the figures)

The shape survives; the historical claim does not become a figure verdict. Relation is irreducible, so a pure set of isolated lineages is not a composable frame. Gene sharing is import, the only operator that adds reach to a closed community, and being new to a lineage is not being new to the world. A clean chain is the degenerate network, so N and L are rival bond arrangements, not rival kinds. Shared gene content does not discriminate inheritance from import, so the residual is read per gene and handed to instruments. Many gene trees compress into one universal tree with a nonzero round-trip gap. Whether the first communities actually exchanged genes is a `gap`, decided by instruments outside this run. This is `specified` on the shape, `general` on the bond rule, `gap` on the history, ungrounded and candidate.

- cost if wrong: bounded and locatable. The packet states mode, refuters and lineage in advance, so an error is findable rather than invisible. The real cost is spending the independence of outside readers by sending a recording instead of a generator, and Fig 2 :42 "Buy independence in proportion to the cost of being wrong." prices that. It is avoidable by leading with the shape claim and its refuter.
- undoable: yes. The `gap` can be narrowed or closed without withdrawing any primitive: a deep-time instrument reading, a provenance discriminator, or a per-slot reading can update Domain 3. The declared-strain analogue (Fig 10 :70) can be dropped with no effect on Domains 1 and 2. No world source has to be retracted.
- reversed by: a ground reading, not agreement. A treelike-versus-network signal test at the deepest resolvable gene sets returning clean descent at the relevant scale would move Domain 3 toward L; decisive reticulate signal would move it toward N. A content-only statistic that discriminates convergence from import without provenance would break the non-discriminating-content claim. Deriving one of the six from the other five, or showing an operator other than import moves the wall, would break the shape claim. Agreement from the same evidence pool, or prestige, is not such an instrument.
