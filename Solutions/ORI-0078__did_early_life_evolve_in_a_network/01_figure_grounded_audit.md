## 0. The residual, in effect terms

**Question as posed:** did early life evolve in a network of gene-sharing communities rather than as cleanly separated lineages?

**Effect statement.** Two descriptions compete.

- **Option N, network:** the unit of description is a community of cells in contact; heritable material moves between communities; any single lineage's genealogy is a projection of that contact structure. Consequence: mosaic genomes, discordant gene trees, and no single hierarchy that is natural at the base.
- **Option L, lineages:** the unit is a line of vertical descent; each gene has one ancestry; the tree is fundamental. Consequence: a single consistent hierarchy, and gene discordance is error or anomaly.

**What an answer would look like (bound as an ordinary term, formula step "Direction").** A *shape*: a relation among entities in contact, where the bond between entities is import of heritable material and the tree is the lossy projection of that relation when viewed from outside. Binding this as a term lets it accrue a residual rather than remaining an unrefusable preference. The figures can constrain this shape. They cannot report what happened 3.5 billion years ago.

**R by arity, symmetry and scale.**

- **Arity:** a relation among two or more entities. This is a graph question, not a single-object question. Fig 13 :24 "that entities relate, and influence travels" names relation itself as an irreducible structure.
- **Symmetry:** both options use ordered bonds. Vertical descent is sequence or production (→ or ⇒); gene transfer is donor to recipient, also ordered. The distinguishing variable is not symmetry but degree and crossability of closure: can a node's atom set be added to from a peer? Fig 12 :92 "Symmetry is what decides whether an arrangement is a new thing" therefore does not decide between N and L; the relevant test is what crosses the boundary. Symmetry test applied and declined, not ignored.
- **Scale:** genes (atoms) inside communities (molecules), communities inside a biosphere (levels). The transform between levels is lossy (Fig 4 :30 "So take the round trip and measure the gap. Without it you are navigating by dead reckoning: confident about a position nobody checked."). A tree can be real at one level and not fundamental at another.

**Partition or union.** This is a union, not a partition. Fig 9 :54 "And when the split lands on a union rather than a partition, unbundle before you search again" and Fig 9 :57 "a case in two conditions is not a harder case of either". Three conditions are bundled: (i) compositional, can a tree and a network both be real frames; (ii) empirical, did early communities actually exchange genes; (iii) instrumental, what would discriminate a tree from a network in the data. They have different anchors and need separate loops. Answering (i) does not answer (ii).

## 1. Domain census (before choosing any figure line)

One line per domain with actors / currency / anchor. A domain with no nameable anchor is not a domain yet.

- **Domain 1, molecular-genetic frame, the atoms.** Actors: replicating and catalytic molecules, genes, plasmids. Currency: heritable sequence and function. Anchor: the ground, that is sequence data and experimental evolution, which does not change because a frame thinks otherwise (Fig 2 :29 "An anchor is whatever settles the question and does not change its answer because of what any frame thinks. A measurement, an experiment, a counter, a market, a patient outcome."). Nameable anchor: yes.
- **Domain 2, community-and-bond frame, the arrangement.** Actors: communities, the graph, the tree, the import event. Currency: bonds between entities (sequence for descent, import for gene sharing). Anchor: the figures as metalaws for what may compose, plus the ground for any claim about what did compose. Nameable anchor: yes.
- **Domain 3, historical and verification frame.** Actors: sequence data, rocks, isotopes, instruments, the treelike-signal test. Currency: residuals and settlement. Anchor: the ground, which does not update (Fig 2 :33 "The anchor must not update."). Nameable anchor: yes, but for deep time it is a partially closed channel, not a sealed room.

**Order by dependency (Fig 3).** Perception (can discordance be seen at all?) precedes vocabulary (do we have terms for import versus vertical descent?) precedes objective (what counts as progress toward an answer?) precedes planning and carrying. Fig 3 :19 "Optimising a link that already works cannot rescue one that does not." It is a link nobody has looked at." Here the middle link is instrumented by Huson and Bryant's conservative statistical test for treelike signal (opened 2026-11-23), so the chain is traversable in principle. That is a claim about instrument availability, not about mode: it remains `specified`.

**Strain test applied before concluding.** Fig 10 :70 appears, at first reading, to settle the biological question directly. It does not, and the re-census is recorded in F3 and in the retraction log.

## 3. Findings

### F1 — Network is a necessary frame; a lineage is not a primitive (`general`, triangulated)

Fig 13 :24 "that entities relate, and influence travels"
Fig 13 :41 "Each is indivisible: no arrangement of the other five yields it."
Fig 13 :42 "There is no route. Ordering does not produce adjacency; duration does not produce conservation; a quantity does not produce the fact that things relate at all."

The six-term set cannot be built without network. A pure set of isolated lineages, with relation absent from the primitives, is not a composable frame under this figure: the fact that things relate is not derivable from ordering, duration or quantity. This is a `general` structural claim about what any compositional system needs. It does **not** say that early life was in fact a network. Confusing those two is the error this audit refuses.

**Refuter:** derive "entities relate" from the other five structures, or exhibit a system that composes anything while holding no network term. Either breaks indivisibility. Triangulated across Fig 13 and the canonical figures README (which states the six are the substrate the loop assumes throughout), low overlap between a substrate census and a usage rule.

### F2 — Gene sharing is import, and import is the only operator that moves the wall (`specified`, triangulated)

Fig 6 :14 "the only operator that moves the wall"
Fig 6 :23 "No frame escapes its own closure. The bound moves only on contact with a frame whose closure differs."
Fig 6 :22 "NOVEL means novel relative to atoms, not to the world."
Fig 6 :25 "An import adds no atom to the world."

Read in effect terms: a community's gene repertoire is a closure. A community whose closure does not differ from its peer cannot acquire a gene that was not already reachable in its own closure. Only contact with a differing closure adds an atom to the receiver's reach, and the atom was already in the world. That is gene sharing stated in the figures' own vocabulary, without importing biology into the figures or the figures into biology. It also explains why "novel to the lineage" is not "new to the world", which is exactly the distinction a gene-transfer claim needs.

**Refuter:** show a closed community acquiring a gene absent from its closure without contact with a differing closure, or show an operator other than import moving the wall. Triangulated across Fig 6 (closure and reachability) and Fig 8 :45 "Two frames together reach what neither reaches alone." Low overlap: one is a boundary theorem about composition, the other a surplus theorem about union.

### F3 — The tree of life line is an analogue with declared strain, not a governing line (RETRACTED as decisive)

Fig 10 :70 "If levels were copies there would be one lineage drifting and no branches. The tree of life is what a non-invertible transform looks like from outside."

This is the most on-point sentence in the figure set, so it is the most tempting to overuse. Strain test: the sentence governs what a cross-scale, non-invertible transform looks like when seen from outside, that is branches across strata. The biological question concerns genealogical relations among organisms at one stratum. Those are different quantities. Reading Fig 10 :70 as a direct verdict on early phylogeny would be a cross-domain mapping, and the canonical rule says a reading that needs a strain has misidentified its domain.

**Re-census made.** The line is retained as an analogue supporting the weaker claim that a tree can be an appearance of a lossier underlying relation (see Fig 4 :33 "there is no top and no infinite regress."). It is **not** counted as a witness for Option N. This is a visible retraction of my own first reading, not a silent substitution.

**Refuter, kept for the analogue only:** exhibit a non-invertible cross-scale transform whose outside view is a single unbranched line, or show that the biological tree at one stratum is not a projection of any cross-scale relation. If either holds, the analogue has no purchase. Status: single-source analogue, declared strain, zero verdict weight.

### F4 — A chain is the degenerate network; both options are composable (`specified`, triangulated)

Fig 12 :48 "A chain is the degenerate case: one bond type, no branching."
Fig 12 :111 "A vocabulary without bonds can name a state and cannot name a route to one."
Fig 12 :94 "The arrangement is the substance."
Fig 12 :117 "isomer. Same atoms, different bond, different substance. The difference is invisible in the formula and decisive in the behaviour."

"Cleanly separated lineages" is the one-bond-type case: vertical sequence only, no cross-links. "Gene-sharing network" adds a second bond type between nodes. Both are legal molecules under the figures. The figure set therefore does not forbid either description; it says the difference is carried by the arrangement, not by the ingredients. A vocabulary that names only the genes cannot name either route.

**Refuter:** show that vertical sequence alone can reproduce an arbitrary cross-community distribution of a gene with no import bond, so that the network bond is redundant. If that succeeds, Option L is sufficient and F2 loses its target. Triangulated across Fig 12 and Fig 13 :121 "Different routes can arrive at the same substance."

### F5 — Identical genes do not by themselves discriminate inheritance from import (`specified`, triangulated)

Fig 8 :50 "Two frames can hold the same atom by convergence, reaching it separately, which is corroboration at maximum independence."
Fig 8 :51 "Convergent derivation and adopted import look identical in the contents. Only the record of where each came from separates them"
Fig 8 :53 "Residual first, frame second."

Two communities holding the same gene is consistent with descent from a shared ancestor and with a later import. The contents are the same. Only the record, that is the dated provenance of the gene in each frame, separates the two histories. This is the figure-level form of the discordant-gene-tree problem. It also explains why the HGT literature's apparatus (Huson and Bryant's treelike-signal test; Woese's "not an organismal tree at its base") is a ground-facing instrument, not a new figure.

**Refuter:** produce a content-only statistic that distinguishes convergent inheritance from adopted import with no provenance and no independent frame. If it works, the provenance requirement is too strong. Triangulated with the world source from computational phylogenetics; its field differs from molecular evolution, so overlap is partial.

### F6 — The empirical history is a `gap` for the figures; the ground decides (`gap`)

Fig 2 :33 "The anchor must not update."
Fig 8 :39 "So a search that finds nothing is never a proof of absence."
Fig 9 :44 "Only a sealed room can be searched to the end, and no frame is a sealed room."
Fig 13 :134 "Unreachable is a verdict here, not a silence"

No figure line reports a measurement of early Earth. Deep time is an unsealed room: the absence of preserved ancient signal is not evidence of clean lineages, and a search that returns no transfer signal proves no absence. The verdict on what actually happened belongs to Domain 3 and to instruments (sequence phylogenetics, ancient signatures, experimental evolution), not to this derivation. World sources below are secondary corroboration for the plausibility of the network shape; they do not become a figure verdict.

**Refuter:** quote a figure line that states the actual historical distribution of genes in the first communities. There is none. If one is produced, this `gap` is withdrawn. `gap` is the correct label, not a failure to look.

### F7 — Per-slot residual: decide gene by gene, not by one global tree score (`specified`, triangulated)

Fig 1 :19 "The residual is indexed by object slot, not aggregated."
Fig 1 :22 "Aggregating across slots is how a live signal disappears into an average."

A single global "tree-likeness" number is an aggregate over gene slots. If a minority of genes carry a strong transfer signal, averaging across slots is precisely how that signal vanishes. The residual must be read per gene and per community. This is also what the HGT sources report: Doolittle and Bapteste's pattern pluralism across taxa and scales, and Huson and Bryant's test for conflicting signal in a network.

**Refuter:** demonstrate a global statistic that preserves the per-slot transfer signal and still classifies the early layout correctly when per-slot signals conflict. Triangulated across Fig 1 and two world sources with different methods (pluralism argument and a statistical test), low overlap.

### F8 — The dependency chain is instrumented here, so the usual break is not assumed (`general`, single-source)

Fig 3 :19 "Optimising a link that already works cannot rescue one that does not."
Fig 3 :27 "A link with no instrument is not a link that works. It is a link nobody has looked at."

The vocabulary-to-objective link in this question is: we have a term for gene sharing (import) and an instrument that says whether conflicting signal is treelike (Huson and Bryant, 2006). The lack of an instrument cannot be presumed. Note the honest limit: having an instrument that tests treelike signal is not the same as having a reading from the earliest biosphere.

**Refuter:** show the treelike-signal test cannot be applied to deep-time data at all, returning the chain to the break. Single-source for the figure rule; the instrument claim is world-sourced.

### F9 — Filters are not witnesses (`general`, single-source)

Fig 9 :60 "Use filters for the budget and witnesses for the verdict. Never let a filter hand you a verdict."

Any ranking of venues, any estimate of how easy a field is to approach, and any count of sources read are budget filters. They decide what gets tried first. They never decide whether Option N is true.

### F10 — Agreement from one evidence pool is one witness (`general`, triangulated)

Fig 2 :40 "agreement about the world is worthless when the agreeing frames share an evidence pool"
Fig 2 :42 "Buy independence in proportion to the cost of being wrong."
Fig 7 :35 "A room's agreement is variation, not a verdict. Selection between rooms is where the ground votes."

Most world sources opened for this solution are molecular-evolution papers sharing methods, citations and a review literature. Their agreement about a web-like early evolution is one pool talking, not independent corroboration. Huson and Bryant's computational-phylogenetic frame lowers overlap somewhat, but not to zero. The verdict, such as it is, comes from the figures, and the remaining empirical part is `gap`.

## 4. Pre-registered refuter (written before the conclusion)

Required before any outreach: a falsifier written before the run is a design instrument. The shape claim ("a network bond is import, import is the only wall-mover, and a tree is a lossy projection") is refuted if any of the following holds:

1. A community is shown to acquire a gene absent from its closure without contact with a differing closure, breaking Fig 6 :23.
2. "Entities relate" is derived from the other five structures, or any one of the six is derived from the other five, breaking Fig 13 :41 and :42.
3. A global statistic is shown to preserve a per-slot transfer signal that disappears under aggregation, breaking Fig 1 :19 and :22.
4. An instrument reading of the deep-time record returns cleanly separated lineages with demonstrably no import at the relevant scale, closing the empirical `gap` against Option N.

If any fires, the shape is withdrawn or narrowed. Until one is run, no part of this may be labelled `grounded`.

## 5. Recommendation

1. State the shape, not a history: network is a necessary frame, import is the only wall-mover, a tree can be an appearance. Do not say the figures prove early life was a network (F1 to F4).
2. Split the union before searching: compositional, empirical, instrumental (Fig 9 :54).
3. Decide per gene and per community, not by an averaged tree score (F7).
4. Treat identical genes as non-discriminating without provenance (F5), and hand the discrimination to instruments, not to argument.
5. Lead outreach with the refuter and the shape claim; links only after the recipient's verdict is recorded (Fig 10 :52).
6. Keep F3's retraction visible. The tree-of-life line is an analogue with strain, not a witness.
