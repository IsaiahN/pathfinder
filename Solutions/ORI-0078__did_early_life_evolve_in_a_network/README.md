## What is deliberately not here

- No personal emails of named individuals.
- No ranking of people by how likely they are to agree with this answer.
- No bulk-send plan.

Three figure-grounded reasons:

1. **Agreement is not a verdict.** Fig 2 :35 "Alignment is triangulation, not negotiation. A mirror cannot correct you." A list selected for receptiveness manufactures the correlation that would make its agreement worthless (Fig 2 :40 "agreement about the world is worthless when the agreeing frames share an evidence pool").
2. **A room does not add an anchor.** Fig 7 :35 "A room's agreement is variation, not a verdict. Selection between rooms is where the ground votes." Adding readers raises the vocabulary, not the evidence.
3. **Send the residual, not the source.** Fig 10 :52 "Bring a characterised residual, never the source." A recipient who has absorbed this whole folder before being asked for a verdict has joined the frame being checked (Fig 10 :42 "A verifier that can reconstruct the claim cannot verify it.").

Legally: scraping personal emails for unsolicited bulk outreach breaches GDPR and CAN-SPAM style rules in many jurisdictions. Public opt-in venues avoid that failure as well as the structural one.
