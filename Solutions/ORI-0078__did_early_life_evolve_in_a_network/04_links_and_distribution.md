### How this literature bears on the solution

1. **It corroborates the shape, not the derivation.** Woese's communal ancestor and Doolittle's pattern pluralism are exactly the kind of thing Fig 13 :24 ("that entities relate, and influence travels") and Fig 6 :23 ("No frame escapes its own closure. The bound moves only on contact with a frame whose closure differs.") would lead one to look for. Agreement here is retrodiction, not a forward test of the framework.
2. **Huson and Bryant supply the missing instrument.** The hard problem is not whether a network can be drawn; it is whether the conflicting signal is treelike. That is the kind of discriminator "The Hard Problem is About Source" says arrives as an instrument rather than an argument. It is still not a reading of the earliest biosphere.
3. **Pool overlap is declared.** Five of the six sources are molecular-evolution or genomics reviews and arguments that cite each other. Their agreement is one broad evidence pool. Huson and Bryant's computational frame lowers overlap somewhat. This is why the figures, not the literature, carry the verdict.

## 3. Distribution: offer, do not push

Figure-grounded rules:

- **Fig 10 :52 "Bring a characterised residual, never the source."** Lead with the shape claim and its four refuters. Sending this entire folder first sends a recording, not a generator.
- **Fig 10 :42 "A verifier that can reconstruct the claim cannot verify it."** A recipient who has absorbed the framework before giving a verdict has joined the frame being checked. Record the verdict first.
- **Fig 9 :27 "describe it before you go looking"** (full line: "describe it before you go looking, or any frame you pick will seem to fit"). Ask what they would expect to see if gene sharing dominated early evolution before showing the HGT literature.
- **Fig 2 :35 "Alignment is triangulation, not negotiation. A mirror cannot correct you."** Never ask for agreement, never ask for trust.
- **Fig 7 :35 "A room's agreement is variation, not a verdict. Selection between rooms is where the ground votes."** One-to-one contact only; no bulk list.
- **Fig 2 :42 "Buy independence in proportion to the cost of being wrong."** The cheap first step is the recipient's own check of the shape, not a long read.

**Recommended order in a reply:** (1) the shape claim and its refuter; (2) ask what they would count as a failure and what they would expect to see in the data; (3) record that verdict; (4) only then, if they are curious, the primary figure text and the three framework links, with the note that the figures, not this summary, are the authority.

## 4. The two-stage offer (and an optional third stage)

**Stage 1.** Send the shape and its refuter. No theory, no links. The recipient checks it against their own ground.

**Stage 2, opt-in only.** If curious, offer the figure text, the formula and the architecture article. Their stage-1 verdict must already be recorded: after reading the framework their agreement is no longer evidence (Fig 2 :40 "agreement about the world is worthless when the agreeing frames share an evidence pool").

**Stage 3, reuse.** Offer the formula as a method, with the instruction that each user writes their own refuter before running it and states their own mode. The formula is a generator, so giving it away does not give away an answer (Fig 4 :22 "Only methods cross up. Only head starts come down.").

**Hard precondition.** Stages 1 to 3 only work if stage 1 survives. The claim sent in stage 1 is deliberately narrow: import is the only wall-mover, a chain is the degenerate network, and the historical question stays `gap`. Anything stronger fails the canonical usage check in `01_figure_grounded_audit.md` section 6.

## 5. Hosting

This agent cannot upload a file or send a message. Hosting the packet on an opt-in page and sending the link is for the recipient of this solution to do. Prefer a page that lets a visitor decline contact over an attachment pushed to a list: the former preserves the recipient's independence, the latter spends it.
