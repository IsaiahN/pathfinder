## 1. A shape is a constraint, not a filler

The canonical README states the limit: the framework returns "what a missing thing would have to look like (a shape, not a name)". Fig 9 :32 "you get a kind of thing, never the answer itself". Fig 13 :134 "Unreachable is a verdict here, not a silence".

From *The Library of Babel*, opened this session: "You can cut your search short if the set of books returned is small and descriptive enough that it provides a general shape of the answer that satisfies your question."

So a **shape** is the narrowed form of an answer you can state before you can name its source. It constrains; it does not fill. For this question the shape is: relation is irreducible, import is the only operator that adds reach to a closed community, a chain is the degenerate network, and identity of content does not discriminate inheritance from import. The shape does **not** say "early life was a network" as a historical finding.

## 2. How the shape is obtained

- *Library of Babel*: use the vocabulary you already have that is closest to the thing, then "With each new term gained, you are able to narrow the space by definition and deepen your vocabulary." Applied here: start from the residual in effect terms (genes move between communities), then let the figure vocabulary supply "import", "closure", "network" and "degenerate chain".
- Fig 6 :30 "The description travels. Fails by being sharpened into terms only we can read. Cast it in effect terms." Fig 9 :30 "your words are borrowed too, so describe the effect" (full line: "your words are borrowed too, so describe the effect rather than the cause").
- Fig 8 :53 "Residual first, frame second." I described the residual before selecting the HGT literature, not after.
- *Library of Babel* on stopping: "You will know that you are done because the finish line will also be the starting place, as there is nowhere else to search and nothing else that can describe the answer better." Here the stop is the `gap`: the figures return a shape and then stop, recording the lineage.

## 3. The bundle is not one question

*Library of Babel* gives the exact diagnosis for this question: "Perhaps your problem was a bundle or conflation of several problems masquerading as one? You will need to search all of those problems individually to find their answers and then combine them in a satisfactory way." This is the essay-level counterpart of Fig 9 :54 "And when the split lands on a union rather than a partition, unbundle before you search again."

The bundle here: (i) can a tree and a network both be real frames (compositional); (ii) did early communities actually exchange genes (empirical); (iii) what instrument separates a tree from a network in the data (instrumental). Only (i) is figure-governed. Only (iii) has a world instrument. (ii) is `gap` at this budget.

## 4. What licenses a name

Two things, both outside the shape itself:

1. **The ground settles it.** `THE_FORMULA.md` step 5: "the ground settles it. Verified only by what pays. A gate is not the ground." For this question that would be an instrument reading of deep time, or an authenticated ancient signal.
2. **A provenanced import from a frame whose closure differs**, with a source gate and a shadow test (Fig 6 :23, Fig 8 :51). Saying "Woese's communal ancestor" is naming a world result, not naming a figure primitive; it must be provenanced as such.

## 5. The instrument point, and why it governs this question

*The Hard Problem is About Source*, opened this session: "If several paths create the same behavior, then behavior is a projection with lossy information (it cannot define consciousness in full)." And: "the instrument is speaking only about what it measured, it cannot comment on the source." The resolution was not an argument: "It was proven wrong not by argument, but by the arrival of a better instrument."

For ORI-0078 the reading is "two genomes share a gene". That reading cannot comment on its source: vertical inheritance from a shared ancestor and a later import both produce it. What is owed is a source-discriminating instrument, not a better argument about which word to use. Huson and Bryant's "conservative statistical test for whether the conflicting signal in a network is treelike" is exactly that kind of instrument at the sequence level; it is still not a reading of the earliest biosphere.

## 6. Correction to my own §4 and to the first reading of this question

I initially treated "shape, not a name" as forbidding the word "network" in this solution. That was an overstatement. The README's phrase governs "a **missing** thing", a primitive not held in the library. Network is already one of the six structures (Fig 13 :24 "that entities relate, and influence travels"). Naming the network frame is selecting an atom the framework already holds, not inventing a seventh. What is actually missing here is the historical record of the first communities, and that is precisely what the framework says it cannot name.

I also initially let Fig 10 :70 "If levels were copies there would be one lineage drifting and no branches. The tree of life is what a non-invertible transform looks like from outside." carry the biological verdict. Retracted as decisive in `01_figure_grounded_audit.md` F3: the line governs cross-scale transforms, and using it for genealogy at one stratum is a strain. Both readings are recorded so neither is quietly lost.

## 7. Two consequences for this solution

**Prestige is not action.** From *The Recursive Self-Improvement Trap*, opened this session: "Prestige must never be allowed to validate an action directly." and "Prestige can fund an action. It can give an action a budget to be tried. But it cannot settle an action. Only the ground can settle it." Famous names in HGT cannot decide the shape; they can only give it a budget to be tried. This is why `03_public_contact_points.csv` contains public venues rather than personal addresses and why no receptiveness rank exists.

**My own audit is a simulation, not a ground reading.** Same essay: "LLMs can quickly simulate an approximate answer/output with the formula found in the architecture post." and "the truly testable output would need to be derived from the real architecture or working the formula yourself." So `01_figure_grounded_audit.md`, `06_isomers_and_order.md` and `09_watson_answer.md` are simulated derivations checked for internal consistency against the figures. They cannot settle the question they report on.
