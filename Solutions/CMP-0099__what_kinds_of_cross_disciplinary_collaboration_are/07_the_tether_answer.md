# The Tether's answer: what kinds of cross-disciplinary collaboration

**Mode: `specified` on the structural claims, `general` on the shape, not `grounded`.** This is a shape and a route, not a result. Simulated derivation checked against the figures (`05_what_shape_means.md` §8), not a ground reading.

## 1. The residual it answers

Network topology alone cannot settle an institutional, ecological or behavioural outcome; a process account alone cannot say how structure channels it. The gap is what crosses between a structure frame and a process frame. Fig 8 :19: "closures differ, so a surplus exists". Fig 8 :21: "nothing was aimed; the union is mute". The question bundles several conditions, so the answer cannot be one uniform team format (Fig 9 :54: "when the split lands on a union rather than a partition, unbundle before you search again").

## 2. The shape: four kinds, kept separate

1. **Residual-first selection, not discipline-first staffing.** Fig 8 :53: "Residual first, frame second." Fig 9 :27-28: "describe it before you go looking, or any frame you pick will seem to fit". Describe the failure in effect terms (what was expected, what happened, which slot), then look for the frame whose closure predicts its shape. Fig 9 :31: "your words are borrowed too, so describe the effect rather than the cause".

2. **Low-overlap triangulation against one unmoving ground.** Fig 2 :31: "Independence, between two frames, is how little they draw on the same evidence." Fig 2 :35: "Alignment is triangulation, not negotiation. A mirror cannot correct you." Fig 2 :45: "Frames range against the ground. They do not negotiate with it." Fig 7 :35: "A room's agreement is variation, not a verdict. Selection between rooms is where the ground votes." The ground is the system being modelled: cases and outcomes for institutions, field data for ecology, experiments for behaviour. Agreement between disciplines is not the ground.

3. **Provenanced import of a typed bond, not a shared vocabulary.** Fig 6 :14: "the only operator that moves the wall". Fig 6 :23: "No frame escapes its own closure. The bound moves only on contact with a frame whose closure differs." Fig 8 :12-13 name the gates: "source gate: provenance" and "shadow test: explains a gap". Fig 12 :47: "A recipe that names only its ingredients has recorded half of itself." The bond carries information the network and the process variable do not. Which bond holds is left open (Fig 9 :32: "you get a kind of thing, never the answer itself").

4. **A maintained habitat: a room per problem, a commons of methods, and a seat that carries without authoring.** Fig 7 :40: "The room is summoned by the residual rather than composed." Fig 4 :22: "Only methods cross up. Only head starts come down." Fig 10 :41: "carry, never author". Fig 11 :39: "capability is a property of agent-and-habitat, never of the agent alone." Fig 10 :24: "the ground does not decay, the channel does". A collaborator who is selected for likely agreement, or who is shown the full framework before checking the output, spends the independence that makes the collaboration useful (Fig 10 :42: "A verifier that can reconstruct the claim cannot verify it").

## 3. Verdicts per domain, never averaged

| domain | actor / currency / anchor | verdict | mode |
|---|---|---|---|
| Structural (network) | graph descriptors / topology statistics / observed adjacency | topology is a structure, not a process; it cannot name the bond | `general`, triangulated (Fig 13 :23-24, Fig 12 :111) |
| Institutional | rules, monitoring, sanctions / compliance and collective outcomes / case records | needs its own frame and its own ground; a network statistic can read green without naming the source | `specified`, triangulated (Fig 2 :29, Fig 1 :19) |
| Ecological | species and interactions / abundance and persistence / field measurement | needs field contact; isolation of coupled networks overestimates robustness | `specified`, triangulated (Fig 11 :43) |
| Behavioural | individuals, thresholds / choice and payoff / experiment | same network, different process rule gives a different substance | `specified`, triangulated (Fig 12 :117, complex contagion) |
| Intersections (institution x ecology, etc.) | compound conditions / two currencies / their own ground | specific compounds with their own loops; not harder versions of either | `specified`, single-source from Fig 9 plus world corroboration |
| Which bond for which domain | typed relation / info not in operands / needs a discriminating instrument | **gap**, not silence | `general` limit |

No verdict is averaged across these rows. Fig 1 :19: "The residual is indexed by object slot, not aggregated."

## 4. What it explicitly does not say

- Not that a big interdisciplinary team is the answer. Fig 8 :7 calls the undirected union "interdisciplinarity, no mint".
- Not that disciplines should share all data and vocabulary. Fig 2 :40: "agreement about the world is worthless when the agreeing frames share an evidence pool". Fig 2 :41 keeps agreement about a shared meaning as the product; both are needed.
- Not that one team format covers institutions, ecosystems and behaviour. Fig 9 :56: "A single lever clears the region where only one condition holds and leaves the intersections standing".
- Not that the bond can be named from the network pattern. Fig 12 :47 and the source-discrimination essay: the reading does not determine the source.
- Not that contact scores, receptiveness or prestige decide anything. Fig 9 :60: "Use filters for the budget and witnesses for the verdict. Never let a filter hand you a verdict." Seat Map :163: "High correlation is disqualifying for a verdict and efficient for a convention."
- Not `grounded`. No collaboration was measured.

## 5. What would settle it, the ground

A discriminating instrument, not a further argument. The source essay's spectrometer resolved the green reading; here the corresponding questions are:

- **Bond test:** for a given institution, ecosystem or behaviour over a known topology, does a comparison, a sequence, a production, or a conjunction predict outcomes that the others do not? An instrument that discriminates these names the slot.
- **Independence test:** do two frames with genuinely separate evidence converge on the same prediction by separate derivation, or did one adopt the other's atom? Fig 8 :50-52 requires the record to tell them apart.
- **Union test:** does one collaboration lever clear all three process domains and their intersections, or does it leave compounds standing? That decides union versus partition (Fig 9 :54-58).
- **Habitat test:** does a maintained room plus a methods commons change contact with the ground in a way that an equally sized unmaintained team does not? Fig 11 :40: "An improvement that does not change contact changes nothing, however much it improves."
- **Standing falsifiers:** the six in `01` section 5, written before this conclusion.

## 6. The honest stop

**Unreached, not unreachable.** The figures give the shape of the bond and enumerate its kinds, but they do not select one for a process domain. They also cannot certify their own completeness (Fig 8 :39: "So a search that finds nothing is never a proof of absence"). The answer stops where the available instruments cannot discriminate among bonds and records the lineage so the stop is checkable. "Indiscernibility is not identity." Prestige or agreement is not such an instrument.

## 7. One-paragraph version for outreach

> The Tether does not say that a bigger interdisciplinary team is what is needed to connect network structure to institutional, ecological or behavioural processes. It says the undirected mix with no described failure mints nothing. What is needed has four parts: describe the failure in effect terms before choosing any collaborator; choose low-overlap frames that range separately against one unmoving ground, since a mirror cannot correct you; import a typed bond with provenance and a shadow test, because the node and edge list does not carry the information a process model needs; and maintain the arrangement as a room per problem with a commons of methods and a seat that carries without authoring. The three process domains are a union of overlapping conditions, so they keep separate verdicts and their intersections are their own compounds. The figures give the shape of the bond and its kinds but do not select one for any domain, and no collaboration was measured here, so this is a `specified` candidate with refuters written in advance, not a result.
