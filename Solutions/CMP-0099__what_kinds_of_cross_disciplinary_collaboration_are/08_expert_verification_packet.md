# Expert verification packet: the Tether on cross-disciplinary collaboration

**What this is:** a claim in a form a researcher in complex systems or interdisciplinarity can check. Mode, refuters and provenance are stated before the conclusion, so a reader knows what to look for and what would settle it.

**Mode: `specified` on the structural claims, `general` on the shape, not `grounded`.** No collaboration was measured. This is a shape and a route, not a result.

**Status: simulated derivation.** Checked for internal consistency against the verbatim figures, not a ground reading. It cannot settle the question it reports on.

## 1. The claim, in one paragraph

A generic interdisciplinary mix is not the needed kind of collaboration. Fig 8 :7 names the undirected union "interdisciplinarity, no mint": where closures differ but no residual is described, "nothing was aimed; the union is mute" (Fig 8 :21). What is needed is residual-first, low-overlap triangulation: describe the failure in effect terms, choose frames whose closures predict its shape, have each range separately against one unmoving ground, and import a typed bond with provenance and a shadow test. Because institutional, ecological and behavioural processes are a union of overlapping conditions, there is no single uniform team format; each domain and each intersection keeps its own verdict. The shape of the bond is given; which bond holds in a given domain is a recorded `gap`.

## 2. Three checkable sub-claims

### C1. Framework-internal; checkable by reading Figures 8, 9 and 2

- **Statement:** the undirected, residual-free union mints nothing; the surplus exists only where closures differ and a residual is described. Collaboration should be residual-first and low-overlap, with agreement among disciplines excluded from verdict weight.
- **Verbatim lines:** Fig 8 :6-7 "collapse 5: the undirected union", "interdisciplinarity, no mint". Fig 8 :21 "nothing was aimed; the union is mute". Fig 8 :45 "Two frames together reach what neither reaches alone." Fig 8 :53 "Residual first, frame second." Fig 9 :27-28 "describe it before you go looking, or any frame you pick will seem to fit". Fig 2 :31 "Independence, between two frames, is how little they draw on the same evidence." Fig 2 :35 "Alignment is triangulation, not negotiation. A mirror cannot correct you."
- **Refuter:** exhibit an undirected multidisciplinary group with no described residual and no ground check that mints a new composable term the ground pays for; or exhibit two high-overlap frames giving genuinely independent corroboration.

### C2. Modelling-facing; checkable by a complex-systems modeller

- **Statement:** the missing information in a network-to-process model is a typed bond, not another network statistic; the same network under different process bonds is a different substance.
- **Verbatim lines:** Fig 12 :47 "A recipe that names only its ingredients has recorded half of itself." Fig 12 :94 "The arrangement is the substance." Fig 12 :111 "A vocabulary without bonds can name a state and cannot name a route to one." Fig 12 :117 "Same atoms, different bond, different substance. The difference is invisible in the formula and decisive in the behaviour." Fig 13 :74 "Everything else is a composition across the two kinds, never within one."
- **What to check:** take one topology and one process variable. Are conjunction, sequence, production and comparison genuinely different predictive models, or is the difference only notation? The complex-contagion result (Centola and Macy 2007, as reported in the Wikipedia page fetched 2026-11-22) is a candidate instance: information and disease need one contact, behaviour needs reinforcement, on the same network.
- **Refuter:** show that two process domains over the same topology are related by identity with no loss of predictive information, that is, one is a relabelling of the other; or show the bond is fully recoverable from the node and edge list.

### C3. Arrangement-facing; checkable by anyone who has run a cross-disciplinary project

- **Statement:** a maintained room per problem, a commons of methods, and a ground-maintainer seat are required for contact to change; a team that only talks to itself converges, and a team that only shares a source drift together.
- **Verbatim lines:** Fig 7 :40 "The room is summoned by the residual rather than composed." Fig 7 :36 "A chain of mirrors is one long room. The next room must differ from the last, and match the residual it surfaced." Fig 4 :22 "Only methods cross up. Only head starts come down." Fig 10 :41 "carry, never author". Fig 10 :24 "the ground does not decay, the channel does". Fig 11 :40 "An improvement that does not change contact changes nothing, however much it improves."
- **Refuter:** exhibit an equally sized unmaintained team whose contact with the ground changes as much as a maintained room plus methods commons, with no selection of low-overlap frames and no import.

## 3. What is explicitly not claimed

- Not that interdisciplinarity as such is forbidden. Fig 8 :7 governs the undirected, residual-free union; Fig 8 :45 permits two frames in contact with a described residual. The earlier overbroad reading is retracted in `01` section 9.
- Not that agreement is always worthless. Fig 2 :41: "efficient for a convention: agreement about a shared meaning is the product, not a corruption of it." Shared vocabulary is a convention and is useful; it is simply not a verdict about the world.
- Not that one lever covers all three process domains. Fig 9 :56 says the intersections are left standing.
- Not that a bond can be named for any domain. The figures return a shape, not a name; the bond-selection slot is open.
- Not that prestige, receptiveness or contact cost decide a verdict. Fig 9 :60 and Seat Map :163.
- Not `grounded`. No measurement, no field test, no experiment.

## 5. What would settle it

1. **Bond discrimination:** an instrument that shows which typed bond, among the seven, predicts institutional, ecological or behavioural outcomes over a known topology. The source essay's spectrometer is the model: the reading alone could not name the source.
2. **Convergence audit:** a record showing whether a shared prediction arose by convergent derivation or by adopted import. Fig 8 :50-52 says the contents look identical and only the record separates them.
3. **Union test:** evidence that one collaboration lever does or does not clear all three process domains and their intersections.
4. **Contact test:** evidence that a maintained room plus methods commons changes ground contact more than an unmaintained team of equal size.

A falsification is more useful than an endorsement.
