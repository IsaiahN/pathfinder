## Wider literature that bears on this question, read as secondary corroboration

1. **Centola and Macy, 2007, as reported in Complex contagion.** Information and disease need one contact; behaviour needs reinforcement. This is a real-world instance of Fig 12 :117, "Same atoms, different bond, different substance": the same network, a different process, a different dynamic. It predicts that a network-only model cannot choose the process rule by inspection.
2. **Ostrom, 1990, as reported in Common-pool resource.** Long-enduring institutions have design principles, and adaptive governance fits "problems that are complex, uncertain and fragmented". This predicts that the institutional slot needs its own frame and its own ground, not a generic network centrality measure.
3. **Newman/Watts/Strogatz and the NRC definition, as reported in Network science.** The field is defined by "predictive models of these phenomena", and interdependent networks show that isolation misestimates robustness. This predicts that coupled systems need cross-frame contact, and corroborates Fig 11 :43, "The habitat is enumerated, never composed."
4. **Convergent evolution.** Independent arrival at the same structure is a real phenomenon, which is why Fig 8 :50-52 insists that convergent derivation and adopted import need a record to tell them apart. Agreement in the model is not evidence of a shared mechanism; this is question 98's concern, one line up.
5. **Science of team science.** A field exists precisely because assembling a cross-disciplinary team is not the same as making it work. Its existence corroborates F6 without deciding it.

None of these outranks the figures. Where they conflict, the census is reopened; no world source flips a figure verdict.

## Distribution: offer, do not push

Figure-grounded reasons:

- **Fig 4 :22**, "Only methods cross up. Only head starts come down." Sending the whole framework before anyone has described a residual sends a recording, not a generator. Lead with the shape claim and its refuter; offer links later.
- **Fig 10 :42**, "A verifier that can reconstruct the claim cannot verify it." A recipient who has absorbed the framework before giving a stage-1 verdict has joined the frame being checked.
- **Fig 9 :27-28**, "describe it before you go looking, or any frame you pick will seem to fit." Ask what they would expect to see if the shape claim were true before showing the architecture.
- **Fig 2 :31**, "Independence, between two frames, is how little they draw on the same evidence." Do not send to a list drawn from one reading pool.
- **Seat Map :163**, "High correlation is disqualifying for a verdict and efficient for a convention." A mail selected for likely agreement produces a convention, not corroboration.
- **Library of Babel essay**, "communication between them is paramount and would have to be virtually 1:1 to be effective." One-to-one is the contact form this question actually calls for.

**Recommended order in a reply:** (1) the shape claim and its five refuters; (2) ask what the recipient would count as a failure and record the answer; (3) only then, if they opt in, the three links, with the note that the figures, not the summary, are the authority; (4) only then, the formula as a reusable method with the instruction to write a new refuter and state a mode.
