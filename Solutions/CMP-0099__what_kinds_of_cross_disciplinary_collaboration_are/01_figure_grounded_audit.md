## 0. The claim under audit, stated as an effect

Question 99 asks what kinds of cross-disciplinary collaboration are needed to build models that connect network structure to institutional, ecological, or behavioral processes.

Written as a bet, before any figure was consulted: **Option A** says the answer is a generic interdisciplinary team, a mix of disciplines that shares data and vocabulary; **Option B** says the answer is a specific checking arrangement selected by the residual, in which low-overlap frames range separately against one ground and a maintainer carries methods between them. Option A leads to a room that can agree without ever being corrected. Option B leads to a shape that can be refused and a route that can be checked.

What an answer would look like, bound as an ordinary term before the run, per the Formula's "Direction" section: it must (1) name what crosses between frames, (2) name what makes the crossing a check, (3) say why the three process domains do or do not admit one uniform answer, and (4) state what would refute it. That four-part shape is the term under test, not a conclusion.

## 1. Residual, before domain selection

**R by arity, symmetry, scale.**

- **Arity:** four or more. One structural description and three named process domains (institutional, ecological, behavioral), plus the modelling frame that combines them. This is a **union compound**, not one hard question. Fig 9 :54: "when the split lands on a union rather than a partition, unbundle before you search again". Fig 9 :57: "a case in two conditions is not a harder case of either".
- **Symmetry:** asymmetric and cyclic. Structure conditions process, and process remakes structure. Fig 12 :121 names the cycle: "the output re-enters as input"; Fig 12 :123 names the functional group: "a substructure whose presence fixes the outcome".
- **Scale:** micro (node and edge), meso (motif, community, rule, species interaction), macro (institution, ecosystem, population behaviour), with a lossy transform between. Fig 4 :27: "The transform between scales loses information, and that is the point."

**Per-slot, not averaged.** Fig 1 :19: "The residual is indexed by object slot, not aggregated." Fig 1 :22: "Aggregating across slots is how a live signal disappears into an average." So the residual must be read separately for the institutional, ecological and behavioural slots. An average "cross-disciplinary collaboration need" is already a lost signal.

## 4. Findings, one loop per domain

### F1. The question is a union compound, so there is no single uniform collaboration kind

**Verdict per domain:** D0 triangulated, D1 triangulated, D2 triangulated, D3 triangulated. Overall `general`.

Fig 9 :54: "when the split lands on a union rather than a partition, unbundle before you search again". Fig 9 :55: "A bundled problem is usually a union of overlapping conditions, not a list of separate ones". Fig 9 :56: "A single lever clears the region where only one condition holds and leaves the intersections standing, because the second condition still binds". Fig 9 :57: "a case in two conditions is not a harder case of either". Fig 1 :19: "The residual is indexed by object slot, not aggregated."

Corroboration (secondary): Library of Babel essay (2026-10-02) asks "Perhaps your problem was a bundle or conflation of several problems masquerading as one? You will need to search all of those problems individually to find their answers and then combine them in a satisfactory way." Same author and evidence pool as the figures, so this is one witness, not two.

**Consequence:** treat institutional, ecological and behavioural modelling as three loops with a shared structural atom, then re-census the intersections. A single " interdisciplinary team format" is a single lever, and Fig 9 :56 says what it leaves standing.

### F2. The generic interdisciplinary union is a named collapse, but interdisciplinarity as such is not forbidden

**Verdict per domain:** D4 `single-source` for the figure definition, corroborated weakly by world sources. `general`.

Fig 8 :6-7 names "collapse 5: the undirected union" and "interdisciplinarity, no mint". Fig 8 :19-21: "closures differ, so a surplus exists", "but no residual is described", "nothing was aimed; the union is mute". Fig 8 :45: "Two frames together reach what neither reaches alone." Fig 8 :46: "Composition crosses the boundary between them, and that surplus is where imports live."

The line that strains is Fig 8 :7. Read as an absolute ban on working across disciplines, it conflicts with Fig 8 :45. Re-census (see section 9, retraction R1) narrows it: the collapse is the **undirected union with no residual described**, not the act of working across disciplines. The surplus exists only where closures differ and something is aimed.

World corroboration (secondary, low overlap with the framework): Interdisciplinarity (Wikipedia, 2026) records "The most common complaint regarding interdisciplinary programs, by supporters and detractors alike, is the lack of synthesis" and lists the claim that "Disciplinarians often commit errors which can be best detected by people familiar with two or more disciplines." Science of team science (Wikipedia, 2026) defines the field as one that "focuses on understanding and improving cross-disciplinary collaboration in research". Both corroborate the distinction between a mix and a synthesis; neither is a figure line.

### F4. What crosses is an imported atom with provenance, not a shared vocabulary or a pooled dataset

**Verdict per domain:** D4 triangulated (Fig 6 with Fig 8 and Fig 4). `specified`.

The source gate and shadow test are named in the figure but not in the verbatim quote above (Fig 8 :12-13: "source gate: provenance", "shadow test: explains a gap"). Both are required: an import that does not explain the described gap is a borrowed word, not an atom.

World corroboration: convergent evolution (Wikipedia, 2026): "Convergent evolution is the independent evolution of similar features in species of different lineages." and "Functionally similar features that have arisen through convergent evolution are analogous, whereas homologous structures or traits have a common origin but can have dissimilar functions." This is a low-overlap biological analogue of Fig 8 :50-52; it corroborates, it does not decide.

### F5. The dark matter is a typed bond, not another network statistic

**Verdict per domain:** D0 triangulated (Fig 13 with Fig 12). `general` structural.

Fig 13 :23-24 lists network as a structure: "that entities relate, and influence travels". Fig 13 :74: "Everything else is a composition across the two kinds, never within one." Fig 13 :75: "Two states never bond directly. It is not space-time but space, a structure, time; and two structures join only through a state." Fig 12 :47: "A recipe that names only its ingredients has recorded half of itself." Fig 12 :111: "A vocabulary without bonds can name a state and cannot name a route to one." Fig 12 :117: "Same atoms, different bond, different substance. The difference is invisible in the formula and decisive in the behaviour." Fig 12 :94: "The arrangement is the substance."

So the thing owed to a network-to-process model is the bond: conjunction, sequence, production, subtraction, comparison, disjunction, identity, and the unary refusal. Which bond holds is information the operands (the topology and the process) do not carry. This is the shape of what collaboration must deliver, and it is a shape rather than a name: the figures do not say which bond for which process domain.

World corroboration: complex contagion (Wikipedia, 2026), reporting Centola and Macy (2007): "information and disease spread as 'simple contagions', requiring only one contact for transmission, while behaviors typically spread as 'complex contagions', requiring multiples sources of reinforcement to induce adoption." Same network, different process, different dynamics; a low-overlap behavioural analogue of Fig 12 :117.

## 5. Pre-registered refuter

Written before the conclusion, per Seat Map :287: "a falsifier written before the run is a design instrument, not only an honesty device."

The shape claim is refuted if any of the following fires:

1. An undirected multidisciplinary union, with no residual described and no ground check, is shown to mint a new composable term that the ground pays for (breaks F2, Fig 8 :7).
2. A single discipline's closure is shown to predict institutional, ecological and behavioural outcomes over the same network without any import or cross-frame check (breaks F4 and F6).
3. Two frames with high evidence overlap are shown to produce independent corroboration (breaks F3 and F7, Fig 2 :31, :40).
4. The union is shown to be a partition, that is, one lever clears all three process domains and their intersections (breaks F1, Fig 9 :54-57).
5. A network-to-process model is exhibited whose bond information is fully recoverable from its node and edge list (breaks F5, Fig 12 :47, :94).
6. A collaboration design is shown to cross scales with zero round-trip gap (breaks F8, Fig 4 :30).

If any fires, the shape claim is withdrawn or narrowed in a visible log entry. Until one is run, nothing here may be labelled `grounded`.

## 6. Recommendation (filters, not witnesses)

1. Unbundle before staffing. Name the residual per slot (Fig 1 :19, Fig 9 :54).
2. Describe the residual in effect terms, then choose the frame that already predicts its shape (Fig 8 :53, Fig 9 :27-28). Do not pick disciplines first.
3. Buy independence in proportion to the cost of being wrong. Low overlap is the selection criterion; receptiveness is not (Fig 2 :31, :42).
4. Require an import with a source gate (provenance) and a shadow test (explains the gap). Record convergent derivation separately from adopted import (Fig 6 :12-13, Fig 8 :50-52).
5. Run a room per problem and a commons of methods only. Send methods, never recordings (Fig 4 :22, Fig 7 :40).
6. Put a ground maintainer in the seat: conventions, not verdicts; introduce, never subtract; declare any stake (Fig 10 :41, :47, Fig 11 :61, Seat Map :171).
7. Measure R_T rather than declaring the condensation lossless (Fig 4 :30).

These are filters for the budget. They do not hand anyone a verdict (Fig 9 :60).

## 10. Closing verdict

The shape survives: unbundle the union, describe residuals in effect terms, choose low-overlap frames, import a bond with provenance and a shadow test, keep one ground and a maintained seat, send methods not recordings, and measure R_T. The specific choice of bond for institutional, ecological or behavioural processes does not survive as a claim; it is a recorded `gap`. Verdict on the question as asked: the needed kinds of collaboration are residual-first triangulation, provenanced import, per-problem rooms with a commons of methods, and maintained ground contact, with separate verdicts per domain and no averaged collaboration recipe. Mode `specified`, ungrounded, candidate.
