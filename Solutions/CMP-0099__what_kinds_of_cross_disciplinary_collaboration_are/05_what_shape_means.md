## 1. A shape is a constraint, not a filler

From *The Library of Babel*: "you can cut your search short if the set of books returned is small and descriptive enough that it provides a general shape of the answer that satisfies your question." From *How Intelligence Actually Works*: "The replicator is the only one that shows a process. Perceive, act, get corrected at a finer level, go deeper, repeat until the residual stops registering."

So a **shape** here is the form of a collaboration you can state before you can name the exact team, protocol or institution. It constrains; it does not staff. "A generic interdisciplinary team" is a filler. "Residual-first, low-overlap triangulation with an imported bond, a common ground, and a maintained seat" is a shape.

Fig 9 :31: "your words are borrowed too, so describe the effect rather than the cause". This is why the shape is stated in effect terms (what crosses, what checks, what is unbundled) rather than in discipline names.

## 2. How this shape is obtained

- *Library of Babel* on vocabulary and proximity: "With each new term gained, you narrow the space and deepen your vocabulary." The searcher uses "the existing vocabulary you do know that is closest to the words that you wish to describe and grasp the shape of."
- *Library of Babel* on the bundle: "Perhaps your problem was a bundle or conflation of several problems masquerading as one? You will need to search all of those problems individually to find their answers and then combine them in a satisfactory way." This is the essay-side source for F1.
- *Decoding Tacit Knowledge*: "When contact isn't possible, the best you can send is a plain description of what you'd expect to see, because anyone can check that against the world." The description has to be checkable by someone who does not share your history.
- *Library of Babel* on stopping: "you will know that you are done because the finish line will also be the starting place", and the paradox that by then "you have already composed the answer through the process of locating it."
- Fig 6 :30: "Cast it in effect terms." Fig 9 :27-28: "describe it before you go looking, or any frame you pick will seem to fit."

## 3. The shape of collaboration specifically

Three essay passages carry the answer to this question, and they agree with the figures.

1. *Library of Babel*: "Convergence towards the answer is faster with multiple helpers, but communication between them is paramount and would have to be virtually 1:1 to be effective." This is the one-to-one rule, not a bulk rule. It is why `02_outreach_email.md` refuses a mass send.
2. *How a Society of Agents Would Work*: "A group is only worth having while its members stay different." And: "The group shares methods, never recordings." And the summary: "separate frames, one ground, methods shared upward, head starts handed down, a room per problem, and a maintainer who sets conventions but never verdicts." These are Fig 2, Fig 4, Fig 7 and Fig 10 stated in plain words.
3. *Decoding Tacit Knowledge*: "Long, close contact pulls the frames together, so the process that makes communication easy slowly uses up that extra reach." Collaboration has a cost: sustained closeness spends the independence that made the collaboration worth having. The remedy in that essay is "Separate experience" and "Frames whose independence is renewed by separate experience can be maintained" (Fig 10 :80).

This last point is the one most often missed in cross-disciplinary work. A team that talks constantly converges; a team that never talks shares a pool and calls it agreement. The shape requires both contact and separate ground contact.

## 4. What licenses a name

Only two things, both outside the shape itself:

1. **The ground settles it.** THE_FORMULA.md step 5: "the ground settles it. Verified only by what pays. A gate is not the ground." In this question, a name (a specific bond, a specific protocol) is licensed by a model whose prediction is paid by institutional, ecological or behavioural data.
2. **A provenanced import from a frame whose closure differs** (Fig 6 :23, Fig 8 :50-52), with a "source gate: provenance" and a "shadow test: explains a gap" (Fig 8 :12-13).

*They're Scanning Books* sharpens the limit: "The real import is not a new term in the existing space. It is a new dimension of the space." And: "A minimum viable set of six terms is a human's careful guess at reality's compression axes; another sensorium's minimum set need not be those six." *How Intelligence Actually Works* adds: "Declaring the primitives does not make them neutral. It makes the choice visible." So any collaboration design that declares its primitives should mark them as a choice.

## 5. The specific error this question invites

*The Hard Problem is About Source* gives the mechanism. An instrument reads green and "cannot comment on the source": one green light, or yellow plus blue, or RGB, or CMYK, or a scheme not yet discovered. "If several paths create the same behavior, then behavior is a projection with lossy information." It was the spectrometer, not an argument, that resolved it.

The analogue here is the belief that a shared mathematical pattern across institutions, ecosystems and behaviour proves a shared mechanism. It does not: several mechanisms can produce the same network statistic. What is owed is a discriminating measurement, not a larger agreement. This is question 98's concern and it constrains question 99: a cross-disciplinary collaboration must include the instrument that discriminates sources, not just the people who recognize the pattern.

## 6. Prestige is not action

*The Recursive Self-Improvement Trap*: "Prestige must never be allowed to validate an action directly." And: "Prestige can fund an action. It can give an action a budget to be tried. But it cannot settle an action. Only the ground can settle it. The Ground Maintainer must be blind to prestige."

A collaboration assembled around famous names is the named collapse, not corroboration. The legitimate ask is a budget to be tried, with the ground settling it. Seat Map :163: "High correlation is disqualifying for a verdict and efficient for a convention."

## 7. Correction to my own §4 of `01` (preserved)

I first wrote in `01_figure_grounded_audit.md` that "interdisciplinarity is disqualified" on the strength of Fig 8 :7. That was an overreading. The canonical README says a reading that makes figures conflict has misidentified its domain, and Fig 8 :45 says "Two frames together reach what neither reaches alone." Re-census found two objects: :7 governs the undirected union with no residual; :45 governs two frames in contact with a described residual.

**Corrected form:** the undirected, residual-free union mints nothing. Cross-disciplinary work is licensed when a residual is described first and an atom crosses under a source gate and shadow test. The earlier sentence is not deleted; it is recorded in `01` section 9 so the narrowing is visible. This correction is `general` in mode and does not claim a ground settlement.

## 8. My own audit is a simulation, not a ground reading

Same essay: "LLMs can quickly simulate an approximate answer/output with the formula found in the architecture post", but "the truly testable output would need to be derived from the real architecture or working the formula yourself"; the output is "simulacra". The architecture essay adds that outputs "could never be verified if the solutions presented were not contrived to fit the formula".

Therefore `01`, `06`, `07`, `08`, `09` and `11` are simulated derivations checked for internal consistency against the figures. They are not tests of which collaboration works and must be labelled as such. They cannot settle anything they report on.
