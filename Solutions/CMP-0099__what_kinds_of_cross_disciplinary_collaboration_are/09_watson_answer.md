## QUESTION

Decide, in effect terms, what kinds of cross-disciplinary collaboration are needed to build models connecting network structure to institutional, ecological or behavioural processes. Option A: a generic interdisciplinary team that shares data and vocabulary. Option B: residual-first, low-overlap triangulation with a provenanced import, one ground, a maintained seat, and methods rather than recordings crossing frames. What each leads to: A produces a room that can agree without being corrected, because "nothing was aimed; the union is mute" (Fig 8 :21); B produces a checkable shape with separate per-domain verdicts and refuters written in advance.

## DOMAIN CENSUS

R by arity, symmetry, scale, then one line per domain with actors / currency / anchor, partition or union, order by dependency.

- **R.** Arity: four or more slots (one structural, three process, plus the modelling frame). Symmetry: asymmetric and cyclic, structure conditions process and process remakes structure. Scale: micro node, meso motif or rule, macro institution or ecosystem, with a lossy transform. Fig 1 :19: "The residual is indexed by object slot, not aggregated." Fig 9 :54: "when the split lands on a union rather than a partition, unbundle before you search again."
- **D0 Structural frame.** Actors: nodes, edges, degree, motifs, communities. Currency: topological descriptors. Anchor: the observed adjacency and predictive performance. A structure, not a process.
- **D1 Institutional frame.** Actors: rules, norms, monitoring, sanctions. Currency: compliance, collective-action outcomes. Anchor: case records and decisions.
- **D2 Ecological frame.** Actors: species, populations, interactions. Currency: abundance, persistence, stability. Anchor: field measurement and experiment.
- **D3 Behavioural frame.** Actors: individuals, strategies, thresholds. Currency: choice and payoff. Anchor: experiment and observed behaviour.
- **D4 Modelling frame.** Actors: modelers, methods, instruments, vocabularies. Currency: imports between closures and R_T. Anchor: the same system being modelled; agreement is expressly not the anchor.
- **D5 Verification frame.** Actors: outside expert readers. Currency: residuals and refutations. Anchor: the ground, never agreement. In this simulated run all seats share one evidence pool, so independence is zero.
- **Union not partition.** D0 overlaps all process frames and D1 to D3 overlap each other. Intersections are specific compounds with their own loops. Order by dependency: describe residual before frame (Fig 9 :27-28); refuse before mint (Formula step 2); settle first-order models before composing cross-domain ones (Fig 12 :104).

Strain test: "interdisciplinarity" was first entered as a domain and re-censed as an operation (import), because a mix has no actors, currency and anchor of its own. Recorded in `01` section 9.

## VERDICT (from the figures)

The needed kinds are: residual-first selection of low-overlap frames; triangulation of those frames against one unmoving ground rather than negotiation among them; import of a typed bond with a source gate and shadow test; a maintained room per problem with a commons of methods; and a refusal-capable vocabulary that keeps convergent derivation distinct from adopted import. There is no single uniform format, because the institutional, ecological and behavioural slots are a union of overlapping conditions and their intersections are specific compounds. The figures give the shape of the bond and its kinds, not which bond holds in any domain; that is a recorded `gap`. Mode `specified`, ungrounded, candidate. Simulated derivation, not a ground reading.

- **cost if wrong:** bounded and locatable. The packet states mode, shape, refuters and provenance before the conclusion, so an error is findable rather than invisible. The real cost is spending the independence of outside readers by sending a framework before their verdict, which Fig 2 :42 prices: "Buy independence in proportion to the cost of being wrong." That cost is avoidable by leading with the residual and the refuter.
- **undoable:** yes. The generic-team recommendation can be withdrawn without touching the derivation; `07_the_tether_answer.md` and `08_expert_verification_packet.md` already remove it. No primitive is withdrawn, and a sent shape claim can be superseded by a corrected packet.
- **reversed by:** a ground reading, not agreement. An instrument that discriminates among the seven bonds in one process domain, a convergence audit that shows separate derivation rather than adoption, a union test showing one lever clears all intersections, or a contact test showing an unmaintained team changes the ground as much as a maintained room, would each move a domain verdict. Six standing falsifiers are in `01` section 5. Until one fires, the verdict stands as `candidate`.
