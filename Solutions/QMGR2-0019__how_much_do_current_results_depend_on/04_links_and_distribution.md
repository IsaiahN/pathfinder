## Two things in the literature that bear directly on this question

1. **The disagreement is about slots, not about whether dependence exists.** Planck 2018 VI calls its base parameters only weakly model dependent; DESI 2024 VI reports a preference that changes strength with the supernova sample; Heavens et al. 2017 reports dependence on prior widths and on whether an external H0 data set is included. These are different assumption axes. The correct response is a re-census of slots, not a vote between papers (recorded in `01_figure_grounded_audit.md` section 5).
2. **The field already measures the round-trip gap, under a different name.** Hu and McEwen 2026 recommend prior sensitivity analysis; Yang et al. 2026 show that a cheap reweighting recovers only about 17 percent of a full re-run and that a data preference is not a prior preference. That 17 percent is a concrete `R_T`: the cost of the coarser description, measured rather than assumed. This is the strongest bridge between the figure language and current practice.

## The two-stage offer

- **Stage 1:** validate the shape claim against the recipient's own ground. This is Fig 10 :52, *"Bring a characterised residual, never the source"*, and Fig 2 :45, *"Frames range against the ground. They do not negotiate with it."*
- **Stage 2, opt-in only:** if they are curious, offer the three framework links. Their stage-1 verdict must be recorded before any theory is shown, and later agreement is never pooled as corroboration.
