## QUESTION

Decide, in effect terms, what the Tether says about the request for a magnitude of assumption dependence in current dark-energy and expansion results. Option A: deliver one global percentage of how much current results depend on analysis assumptions. ## DOMAIN CENSUS

R by arity, symmetry and scale, then one line per domain with actors / currency / anchor, partition or union, and order by dependency.

- **R (the residual).** A published number (a detection of acceleration, a Hubble constant, a preference for evolving dark energy, a neutrino-mass bound) is presented as what the data gave, yet the same recorded sky re-analysed under an admissible assumption returns a different value and a different strength. Arity: at least six slots (prior width, dataset selection, calibration anchor, nuisance model, likelihood, model class). Symmetry: ordered and directional, not a symmetric join; the governing comparison is `⋛`, a threshold relation between two quantities. Scale: first order inside one analysis, second order across independent analyses, third order along the literature chain where a result is cited as settled.
- **Domain 1, the analysis frame.** Actors: model, priors, dataset selection, calibration zero point, nuisance parameters, fitter, likelihood. Currency: posterior mass, evidence, quoted sigma. Anchor: none of its own; it ranges against the recorded observations in Domain 2 and cannot update them. This is a frame, not a ground.
- **Domain 2, the ground and instruments.** Actors: supernovae, BAO tracers, the CMB, lensing, standard sirens, Cepheids, TRGB stars, eclipsing binaries. Currency: fluxes, redshifts, distances, counts. Anchor: the recorded measurement; it does not change when a team changes its prior. This is a genuine domain.
- **Domain 3, the verification frame.** Actors: independent teams, independent probes, independent calibration chains and pipelines. Currency: agreement or residual between probes. Anchor: the same ground in Domain 2. Independence between frames decides whether agreement means anything.
- **Domain 4, the reporting layer.** Actors: abstracts, press releases, review summaries. Currency: stated significance. Anchor: none of its own; it inherits. **Not a domain yet** by the anchor rule, so it is recorded as a room rather than promoted to a witness.
- **Partition or union.** Union compound, not partition: prior width, dataset selection, calibration and model class overlap on the same sigma, so one lever leaves the intersections standing (Fig 9 :55-57). Separate loops, no averaged verdict.
- **Order by dependency.** Establish the ground reading (Domain 2), then the channel sensitivity (Domain 1), then the independence audit (Domain 3), then interpret reporting (Domain 4). Refuse premature robustness before minting it (formula step 2). Describe the residual before choosing a frame (Fig 9 :27-28).

Strain test applied: the apparent conflict between Planck's weak model dependence and DESI's strong dataset dependence was resolved by re-census as a difference of assumption slots, not a contradiction. Details in `01_figure_grounded_audit.md` section 5.

## VERDICT (from the figures)

The shape survives; the scalar does not. Report dependence per assumption slot with a registered push test, an anchor test per slot, an independence audit by provenance, and a measured round-trip gap `R_T`. Prior width, dataset selection, calibration and model class are a union of overlapping conditions and keep separate loops and separate verdicts. The framework returns a protocol, not a percentage, and does not name which slot currently dominates: that is a `gap` left to a ground measurement. This is a `specified`, ungrounded result.

- **cost if wrong:** bounded and locatable. The packet is mode-labelled, states refuters before the conclusion and records provenance, so an error is findable rather than invisible. The real cost is spending the independence of outside readers by sending them a headline percentage instead of a push protocol. Fig 2 :42 prices that: "Buy independence in proportion to the cost of being wrong." The cost is avoidable by leading with the shape claim and its refuter.
- **undoable:** yes. Slots can be added or removed, admissible ranges revised, and the map re-run without withdrawing any primitive or any published data. A sent claim can be superseded by a corrected table.
- **reversed by:** a ground reading, not agreement. A push-invariant result later overturned by the ground, or a push-moved result later confirmed, would break the anchor-test claim; a dependency scalar invariant under re-indexing of slots would break the per-slot claim; two shared-pool analyses that are counted as independent and whose removal never changes a conclusion would break the independence claim; a full alternative re-run returning `R_T = 0` across all registered slots would break the round-trip claim; and a source-discriminating measurement naming the dominant slot would close the recorded gap. Until one of these fires, the verdict stands as `candidate`.
