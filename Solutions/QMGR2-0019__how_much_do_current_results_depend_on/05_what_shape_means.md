## 1. A shape is a constraint, not a filler

The canonical README states the limit: the framework returns *"what a missing thing would have to look like (a shape, not a name)"*. Fig 9 :32: *"you get a kind of thing, never the answer itself."* Fig 13 :134: *"It can say the answer is out of reach with the library as it stands, so something has to be imported before it is asked again."*

For this question the "missing thing" is not a primitive in a six-term set. It is a number the analyst would like to quote: the fraction of a current cosmological result attributable to analysis assumptions. The framework cannot produce that number from the figures. What it can produce is the shape of an honest answer: a slot-indexed report, a push test that decides whether a result is anchored, an independence audit, and a measured round-trip gap. A shape here is the protocol the number must come from, stated before the number is known.

So:

- shape: a per-assumption sensitivity table with an anchor test and a measured gap.
- name: "37 percent of the dark-energy result is assumption driven." Not available from the figures.

## 2. How a shape is obtained

The exemplar's canonical map of the eight essays records the general method: use the vocabulary closest to the thing, look for recurring terms that overlap with it, recurse until the space is small and descriptive enough, then stop. Applied here, the vocabulary closest to the residual is already in the question itself: "assumptions built into the analysis". The census turns that phrase into named slots (priors, data selection, calibration, nuisance model, model class). Fig 9 :30-31 supplies the rule: *"your words are borrowed too, so describe the effect rather than the cause"*. The effect is "the number moves when this changes", not "the analyst was biased".

*The Hard Problem is About Source* (opened this session) gives the discriminator: *"If several paths create the same behavior, then behavior is a projection with lossy information (it cannot define consciousness in full)."* A result that can be produced by several assumption sets is a projection; the reading does not identify its source. What is owed is an instrument that discriminates sources, not a name. The essay's own resolution is historical: the spectrometer, not an argument, settled the colour case. Here the corresponding instruments are full prior re-runs, independent calibration chains, and independent pipelines.

## 3. What licenses a name

Two things, both outside the shape:

1. **The ground settles it.** `THE_FORMULA.md` step 5: *"the ground settles it. Verified only by what pays. A gate is not the ground."* In this domain, a full re-run under a registered alternative assumption that the ground then settles.
2. **A provenanced import from a frame whose closure differs**, with a source gate and a shadow test (Fig 6 :23, Fig 8 :50-52). Here, an independently calibrated probe or a distinct supernova sample brought in with its provenance recorded.

This is exactly what the LEMAITRE project describes: *"a distinct SN Ia dataset (2800 from ZTF, 170 from SNLS and 300 from HSC), a dedicated calibration program, and a new, independent analysis pipeline"* (Regnault 2026, opened this session). That is a new witness, not a second spelling of the same analysis.

## 4. Why the global percentage is the specific forbidden error

*The Recursive Self-Improvement Trap* (opened this session) gives the mechanism directly:

> *"You may all agree and be wrong. You may set your clock to someone else's, who was setting your clock to yours, and be off by many deviations over time. You need ground truth to define where you stand."*

A global percentage computed by pooling analyses that share priors and data is a clock set from another clock. The framework's own Fig 2 :18-21 names the same collapse: one evidence pool, they never talk and still agree, so the agreement is the pool talking, not two frames confirming each other.

The same essay gives the validation limit: *"the output is only as good as the validator. The agent, or the tool, or the frame, or the theory, cannot validate itself. An external validator, or ground, is always needed to tether the veracity of the output to reality. Anything else is drift."* An analysis cannot certify its own robustness using only the assumptions that produced it. That is why the refuter in `01_figure_grounded_audit.md` section 3 must be registered before the push results are seen.

## 5. Correction to my own section 3

I initially overstated the "shape, not a name" rule in section 1 by treating it as a blanket ban on numbers. The canonical phrase governs a **missing** thing. Here the missing thing is a dependence measure and a validator protocol, not a primitive held in a library. What the figures refuse is the fabricating of a world-level number from figure text; they do not forbid reporting a number that a ground measurement produced.

So the corrected statement is: the Tether supplies the shape of the dependence report and refuses to name which slot currently dominates; a properly instrumented analysis may name it, and that name is an import with provenance, not a figure verdict. The DESI, Planck, Heavens, Yang and LEMAITRE numbers quoted in this folder are exactly that kind of import, recorded as such.

The metamerism point is **not** retracted: several assumption paths producing one reading still leave the source unidentified. What is retracted is the idea that the framework forbids ever reporting a sourced number.

## 6. Two consequences for this solution

**Prestige is not action.** From *The Recursive Self-Improvement Trap* (opened this session): *"Prestige must never be allowed to validate an action directly."* and *"Prestige can fund an action. It can give an action a budget to be tried. But it cannot settle an action. Only the ground can settle it. The Ground Maintainer must be blind to prestige."* A list of famous cosmologists who agree that current results are robust is the named collapse. The legitimate ask is to give the push test a budget to be tried and let the ground settle it.

**My own audit is a simulation, not a ground reading.** Same essay: *"LLMs can quickly simulate an approximate answer/output... However, the truly testable output would need to be derived from the real architecture or working the formula yourself."* So `01_figure_grounded_audit.md` and `11_audit.md` are simulated derivations checked for internal consistency against the figures. They are not tests of the cosmology claim and must be labelled as such. They cannot settle anything they report on.
