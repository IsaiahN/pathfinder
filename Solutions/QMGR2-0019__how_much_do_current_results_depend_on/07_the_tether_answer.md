# The Tether's answer: how much do current results depend on assumptions?

**Mode: `specified` on the shape, `general` on the structural figure laws, not `grounded`.** This is a protocol and a route, not a measurement. Simulated derivation checked against the figures (`05_what_shape_means.md` section 6), not a ground reading.

## 1. The residual it answers

A reported cosmological result (a sigma for evolving dark energy, a Hubble constant, a neutrino-mass bound) is presented as what the data gave. The effect to be explained is that the same recorded sky, re-analysed under a different but admissible assumption, returns a different value and a different stated strength. The residual is per slot, not global: Fig 1 :19 *"The residual is indexed by object slot, not aggregated."* and Fig 1 :22 *"Aggregating across slots is how a live signal disappears into an average."*

## 2. The shape, per domain, verdicts kept separate

1. **The ground is the anchor, the analysis is the channel.** Fig 2 :29-30: *"An anchor is whatever settles the question and does not change its answer because of what any frame thinks."* The recorded observation is the anchor; the posterior is a frame product. Dependence lives in the channel, *"the ground does not decay, the channel does"* (Fig 10 :24). Verdict for Domain 2 and Domain 1: `triangulated` (Fig 2 and Fig 10, low overlap between anchor theory and channel theory).
2. **The discriminator is a registered push test.** For each slot, hold the data fixed, push the assumption within a declared admissible range, re-run, and record displacement and sign change. Push-invariance is a pass for that registered set of pushes only. Verdict: `triangulated` (Fig 2 anchor test plus Fig 1 per-slot indexing).
3. **"How much" is a vector, not a scalar.** The comparison bond is `⋛` between baseline and pushed results (Operators table :47-51), with displacement as a subtraction. A single percentage is the flat-formula error (Fig 12 :5: *"The bond is the rest of the information, and it is the half nothing was recording."*). Verdict: `triangulated` (Fig 1 plus Fig 12).
4. **Agreement counts only with independence.** Fig 2 :31: *"Independence, between two frames, is how little they draw on the same evidence."* Shared data, calibration zero point, or likelihood means one witness. Fig 2 :40: *"agreement about the world is worthless when the agreeing frames share an evidence pool"*. Verdict: `triangulated` (Fig 2 plus Fig 8 :50-52).
5. **Robustness is a measured round trip.** Fig 4 :30: *"So take the round trip and measure the gap. Without it you are navigating by dead reckoning: confident about a position nobody checked."* Report `R_T`, the displacement between a baseline claim and its re-run under the alternative. Yang et al. 2026 give a concrete case: a cheap reweighting recovers only about 17 percent of the full prior shift. Verdict: `triangulated` (Fig 4 plus the Symbols table `R_T` definition).
6. **The conditions form a union.** Prior width, dataset selection, calibration, and model class overlap on the same sigma; one lever clears only the region where one condition binds (Fig 9 :54-57). Verdict: `single-source` for the figure rule (Fig 9), corroborated structurally by the published sensitivity tables.
7. **The world-level dominant slot is a `gap`.** Which assumption currently carries the most leverage is a question for instruments and full re-runs, not for figure text. Fig 8 :39: *"So a search that finds nothing is never a proof of absence."* Verdict: `gap`, reported as a verdict, not a silence.

## 3. What it explicitly does not say

- Not a percentage. No figure yields a global "percent assumption dependent" number.
- Not a claim that the Hubble tension is a systematic or that dark energy does not evolve. Those are world questions and are left open.
- Not a claim that one of the four conditions dominates. That is the named gap.
- Not a ranking of cosmologists. Prestige is a contact-cost proxy and never an evidence column: *"Prestige must never be allowed to validate an action directly."*
- Not `grounded`. No measurement was taken by this derivation, and the published numbers cited are reported as their authors' claims.

## 4. What would settle it: the ground

A registered re-run programme, not more agreement. The concrete questions:

- **Push test:** for each declared slot, does the reported value or strength move, and does the sign flip? Yang et al. 2026 show a prior change moving the weighted median H0 from 77.6 to 87.6 while a binned MAP stays at 70.5, so different summaries of one posterior respond differently and the summary must be declared too.
- **Anchor check:** which of the pushed quantities is the recorded measurement, which is a derived posterior? Freedman et al. 2019 show a calibration-anchor swap moving H0 to a value between Planck and SH0ES, so the anchor chain is itself a slot.
- **Independence check:** do the "independent" confirmations share a supernova sample, a calibrator, or a pipeline? LEMAITRE (Regnault 2026) is an example of a frame built to differ on all three.
- **Gap check:** what fraction of a full re-run does a cheap robustness shortcut recover? The 17 percent from Yang et al. 2026 is a measured `R_T`, not an assumed zero.
- **Standing refuters:** a push-invariant result later overturned by the ground; a dependency scalar invariant under re-indexing of slots; two shared-pool analyses whose removal never changes a conclusion; an `R_T` of zero across all registered slots.

## 5. The honest stop

**Unreached, not unreachable.** The framework reports a shape: an indexed report with a push test, an independence audit, and a measured gap. It cannot name which slot is currently decisive; that name would require an instrument, a full re-run, and a ground reading. The record stops there and keeps the lineage so the stop is checkable. *"Indiscernibility is not identity."* The six essay sources are mostly by the framework's author, so their agreement with the figures is expected and carries little independent weight; the cosmology papers are independent witnesses for the practice, though they draw on overlapping Planck, supernova, and distance-ladder pools.

## 6. One-paragraph version for outreach

> The Tether does not answer "how much" with a percentage. It says that a cosmological result is indexed by the assumptions that produced it, so the honest report is a per-assumption table: hold the data fixed, push each declared assumption within an admissible range, re-run, and record how far the result moves and whether its sign flips. A result that does not move under any registered push passes an anchor test; a result that flips is being carried by that assumption. Agreement between analyses counts only when they do not share the same data, calibrator, or pipeline, and a robustness claim is only a measured gap between a baseline result and a full alternative re-run. Prior-width dependence, dataset choice, calibration, and model class are a union of overlapping conditions, so they get separate loops and separate verdicts. Which one dominates today is left open: that is a measurement, not a figure verdict.
