# Outreach draft: check whether the result moves when you push one assumption

**Use:** one-to-one, or as a cover note attached to a submission through the public venues in `03_public_contact_points.csv`. **Not** for bulk send.

**Hard precondition:** do not send until the push set in `01_figure_grounded_audit.md` section 3 is registered and the claim is stated with its mode. The output must survive validation or the two-stage offer collapses on contact.

---

**Subject:** Would this cosmological result survive one assumption push?

> Hi [name],
>
> I am working on how to report the assumption dependence of a cosmological result. I have one specific output to check. I am not asking you to read the theory first, and I am not asking you to agree with it. If the output does not survive, there is nothing else to look at.
>
> **The output (`specified`, not `grounded`):** a result such as a reported preference for evolving dark energy or a Hubble constant is not a single number that the sky delivered. It is a number indexed by the analysis assumptions that produced it. The honest report is a per-assumption table: for each assumption you are willing to vary within a declared admissible range, what is the baseline result, what is the result after the push, how far did it move, and did the sign flip? If no admissible push moves the result, it passes an anchor test for that registered set of pushes. If one push flips it, that assumption is carrying the result and the result is not anchored there.
>
> This is a shape for a report, not a measurement, and not a percentage. I am deliberately not offering a single "percent assumption dependent" number. Fig 1 in the framework I am using says the residual is indexed by object slot, not aggregated, and that aggregating across slots is how a live signal disappears into an average.
>
> **What I am not claiming:** that any particular current result is mostly assumption, that the Hubble tension is an artifact, or that dark energy does not evolve. Those are world questions that instruments and re-runs settle. I am also not claiming that more analyses agreeing makes a result more true. If two analyses share a supernova sample, a calibration zero point, or a likelihood implementation, their agreement is one witness.
>
> **What would refute the shape claim:**
> 1. Register a push set, then show a result invariant under every push that the ground later overturns, or a result moved by every push that the ground later confirms.
> 2. Show a dependence measure unchanged when two assumptions are merged and a third is split.
> 3. Show that two analyses sharing a calibration or a likelihood are counted as independent, and removing one never changes any conclusion.
>
> I wrote these down before sending this. A one-line "this fails because..." is more useful to me than agreement. If you would rather not engage, no reply is needed.
>
> **If it survives, and only then:** the sources are below. The figures are the authority, not my summary of them.
> - Verbatim figure text with line numbers (local): `FRAMEWORK/reference/03-FIGURES_TEXT.md`
> - Figures README, canonical usage rule: https://github.com/IsaiahN/tether/blob/general-agent/figures/README.md
> - Public figures folder, branch `general-agent`: https://github.com/IsaiahN/tether/tree/general-agent/figures
>
> **If you want to use the machinery:** the formula is in the figures link. Run it on a result you know well and can check against a ground. Write down what would refute your result before you run the pushes, and state the mode of what you get. A reported failure is more useful to me than a reported success.
>
> [your name]

## Contact discipline

Use public institutional or opt-in routes only (`03_public_contact_points.csv`). One message per person. No bulk send, no scraped addresses, no opt-out missing. A reply is a conversation, never corroboration: Fig 2 :40 and the track rules both forbid pooling agreement from a shared evidence pool. Record any refutation verbatim in `11_audit.md` with its provenance and re-evaluate it as if it came from any other source.
