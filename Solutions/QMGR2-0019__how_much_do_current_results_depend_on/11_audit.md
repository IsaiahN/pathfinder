## Independent domain census

I redid the census from the residual line alone, before reading the primary's conclusions, in WATSON order: R by arity, symmetry and scale; one line per domain with actors / currency / anchor; partition or union; order by dependency.

- **R (arity / symmetry / scale).** The residual is the gap between a reported cosmological number and the stability of that number under changes in the analysis. Arity: many slots, so this is not a two-frame triangulation. Symmetry: ordered and directional, a push toward a more negative dark-energy prior is not the same operation as a change of calibration anchor; the right bond is a threshold comparison, not a symmetric join. Scale: first order within one analysis, second order across analyses, third order along the chain of citations that promotes a first-order result to a settled premise.
- **Domain 1, the analysis frame.** Actors: model, priors, dataset choice, calibration zero point, nuisance parameters, fitter, likelihood. Currency: posterior mass, evidence, sigma. Anchor: none of its own; it ranges against the observations. Frame, not ground.
- **Domain 2, the ground and instruments.** Actors: supernovae, BAO tracers, CMB, lensing, standard sirens, distance-ladder stars. Currency: fluxes, redshifts, distances. Anchor: the recorded measurement, which does not change when a prior changes. Genuine domain.
- **Domain 3, the verification frame.** Actors: teams, probes, calibration chains, pipelines. Currency: agreement or residual. Anchor: the same ground; independence is the parameter that decides whether agreement is evidence.
- **Domain 4, the reporting layer.** Actors: abstracts and summaries. Currency: stated significance. Anchor: none. **Not a domain yet** by the anchor rule; it must be recorded as a room, not promoted to a witness.
- **Union, not partition.** The four conditions overlap on the same sigma; a lever clearing one leaves the intersections. Separate loops, no averaged verdict.
- **Order by dependency.** Ground reading before channel sensitivity; registered push set before outcomes; refusal of premature robustness before minting a positive term; independence audit before interpreting agreement.

**R_T is nonzero.** Four things the shorthand holds do not survive the trip back down without extra world information:

- which summary of a posterior is being compared (Yang et al. 2026 show weighted median and binned MAP responding differently to one prior change);
- which "independent" probes actually share a calibration or a likelihood (Fig 2 :31 gives the criterion, not the provenance);
- what fraction of a full re-run a cheap robustness shortcut recovers (Yang et al. 2026 measure about 17 percent, a concrete R_T).

One thing survives cleanly: a result that moves under an admissible push is not anchored at that slot, and a global scalar drops the information the question asked for.
