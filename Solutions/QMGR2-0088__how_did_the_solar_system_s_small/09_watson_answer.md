## QUESTION

Option A: treat "small bodies" as one population and name one dominant mechanism. Option B: treat the question as a union of per-population residuals acquired in unmaintained mode over an enumerated habitat, keep mechanism names as provenanced imports, and let the ground settle each slot. ## VERDICT (from the figures)

The shape survives; no mechanism name is licensed by the figures. "Small bodies" is a union of at least five slots whose residuals do not shrink together, so each needs its own history and its own ground reading. These distributions were acquired in unmaintained mode: no maintainer chose them, differential survival ran, and the surviving residue is what the ground kept. The habitat must be enumerated outward through giant planets, resonances, collisions, the birth cluster and the galactic tide, because contact cascades. Actor valence is slot-relative. Dynamics and cosmochemistry are low-overlap frames whose agreement counts; agreement within one modelling tradition does not. The Oort cloud slot is unreached rather than absent. Grand Tack, the Nice instability, resonance sweeping, collisional families and Oort cloud injection are provenanced imports, `candidate` and ungrounded by this derivation. The live strain is that triangulation has not been tested forward (Fig 8 :56), so it is used as a rule, not as a result.

- **cost if wrong:** bounded and locatable. The packet states its mode, writes five refuters before the conclusion and records each import with provenance, so an error is findable rather than invisible. The real cost is spending an outside reader's independence by sending a mechanism story instead of a shape claim, which Fig 2 :42 prices as "Buy independence in proportion to the cost of being wrong." That cost is avoidable by leading with the union shape and its refuter.
- **undoable:** yes. A wrong slot partition or a bad import is removable without touching the shape or the figure lines. `07_the_tether_answer.md` and `08_expert_verification_packet.md` already strip the named mechanisms of any derived status. A corrected packet can supersede a sent one, and no primitive has to be withdrawn.
- **reversed by:** a ground reading, not agreement. A direct Oort cloud observation with discriminating orbital information would close that slot; isotope and taxonomic readings that separate source reservoirs would settle the belt binding; a Kuiper belt observation distinguishing implantation from in-place survival would settle that slot; and any of the six conditions in `01` section 4 would break the shape claim itself. A figure re-census that makes a mechanism name fall out of the lines would reverse the "shape, not a name" verdict. Until one of these fires, the verdict stands as `candidate`.
