## 0. The residual, stated in effect terms

We hold observed end states (orbital element distributions, taxonomic gradients, size-frequency distributions, reservoir populations) and we owe an account of the histories that produced them. `R` is the gap between what each candidate history predicts for the current distribution and what the ground returns (catalogues, samples, crater record).

- **Arity:** at least five distinct populations, not one: main-belt asteroids, Jupiter Trojans, Kuiper belt objects, Oort cloud comets, near-Earth objects. Centaurs and scattered-disk objects are intermediate slots.
- **Symmetry:** low. A mechanism that clears one slot can populate another. The same actor carries opposite valence at different slots (Fig 11 :49).
- **Scale:** nested. Gas-driven migration acts on roughly 10^5 yr, the giant planet instability on roughly 10^6 to 10^8 yr after a long delayed trigger, and the current era has persisted for roughly 4 Gyr (world sources in `10_source_ledger.csv`). A single-scale account cannot hold all three.

**Bound the sought-for shape before searching** (THE_FORMULA.md, "Direction"). An answer would look like: (i) a distribution history per slot; (ii) an enumerated habitat of actors, conditions and relations; (iii) a contact cascade from those actors to each distribution; (iv) a ground reading that discriminates between candidate histories. Binding this as an ordinary term lets it accrue a residual and be refused.

## 3. Findings

### F1 - The question is a union of slots, not one distribution (decisive for the shape)

Fig 1 :19: "The residual is indexed by object slot, not aggregated."
Fig 1 :22: "Aggregating across slots is how a live signal disappears into an average."
Fig 9 :4: "First: is there a fact here? Disagreements that do not shrink with effort, where each rule keeps working well on a different subset, are not one hard question but several well-formed ones. Split it rather than search."
Fig 9 :55: "A bundled problem is usually a union of overlapping conditions, not a list of separate ones."

The phrase "small bodies" pools five populations whose current distributions are produced by different histories and whose residuals do not shrink together. A single pooled "distribution of small bodies" is the aggregation Fig 1 :22 warns about. The world record agrees it is a union: the National Academies chapter lists Main Belt, near-Earth and Trojan asteroids, comets from two reservoirs, Centaurs and Trans-Neptunian objects as separate dynamical groupings, and says these are "further subdivided based on additional dynamical and also spectroscopic/color characteristics" (NASEM 2023, read 2026-11-23).

**Verdict: triangulated.** Fig 1 (per-slot indexing) and Fig 9 (union splitting) are different figures with low overlap, and the world census of dynamical groupings is a third, independent statement of the same multiplicity.
**Refuter:** one mechanism reproduces all five slot distributions with no population-specific residual left over. If that is shown, the union reading falls.

### F2 - No maintainer; selection ran

Fig 11 :56: "Unmaintained. No maintainer and no live frame in the seat. The habitat is pure contact, nothing selects, and what survives is what the ground kept."
Fig 11 :36: "Nothing was intelligent; selection ran. The word does no work there."
Fig 11 :73: "The habitat is not the ground. The habitat is where actors come from and it updates; the ground is what settles, and it does not."

No seat instruments, distils or introduces planetesimals in the Fig 11 sense. The current distributions are the surviving residue of differential contact, not an arrangement installed by an agent. This is the figure's primary statement about *how* distributions are acquired, and it is a structural claim, not a measured history.

**Verdict: single-source** (Fig 11 only). The world fact that no maintainer is known is corroboration from a different field, but the framework's "unmaintained" is a category, not an observation.
**Refuter:** demonstrate an actual seat acting on these habitats in Fig 11's sense (choosing instruments, distilling impactful actors, introducing what was not there). Absent that, the unmaintained reading stands.

### F3 - Enumerate the habitat; contact cascades carry the history

Fig 11 :43: "The habitat is enumerated, never composed."
Fig 11 :44: "List everything in contact with the residual, then what is in contact with those, and outward until the cascade stops mattering."
Fig 11 :48: "You do not invent the list. You read it off the world, and what you cannot perceive or measure yet is the residual."
Fig 11 :52: "And contact cascades. Scoping the habitat to direct contact is how DDT reached the eagles: first-order enumeration, second-order effect, and the residual that mattered was three steps out."

Enumerated habitat for this residual (read off the world sources, not composed): Sun and gas disk; Jupiter and Saturn; Uranus and Neptune; the snow line; mean-motion and secular resonances; collisions and collisional families; the birth star cluster, passing stars and the galactic tide. Contact cascades: Jupiter's migration reaches the asteroid belt (Walsh et al. 2011); the giant planet instability reaches the Kuiper belt, the Trojans and the inner solar system (Morbidelli et al. 2010; Levison et al. 2008); the galactic tide and stellar flybys reach the Oort cloud (Portegies Zwart et al. 2021). First-order enumeration (asteroid near Jupiter) misses the third-order effect (Oort cloud comets lifted by the galactic tide after scattering).

**Verdict: triangulated.** Fig 11's enumeration law and the world's cascade cases are independent routes, and Fig 13 :74 requires that the structures in the habitat compose across states.
**Refuter:** a habitat list composed from a preferred mechanism rather than read off the world, with a residual that disappears under the composition. Then the enumeration law fails here.

### F4 - Actor valence is relative to the residual

Fig 11 :49: "An actor's valence is relative to the residual, not a property it carries."
Fig 11 :50: "The same actor is a benefactor to one residual and a malfactor to another, so mutualism and parasitism are relations rather than kinds."
Fig 11 :51: "No standing list of good actors survives a change of residual."

Jupiter empties the primordial main belt at one slot (malfactor for retaining primordial belt mass) and implants volatile-rich bodies and sets the S/C gradient at another (benefactor for explaining the observed belt). Neptune destroys the primordial Kuiper belt at one slot and implants the surviving classical belt at another. A phrase like "Jupiter destabilises the small bodies" is not a finding; it is an unattached valence.

**Verdict: triangulated** (Fig 11 valence law plus two independent world cases with opposite signs).
**Refuter:** a standing valence list for one actor that survives all five residuals.

### F5 - Space and time are not the only structures: radial and temporal gradients are different terms

Fig 13 :74: "Everything else is a composition across the two kinds, never within one."
Fig 13 :76: "A gradient over space and one over time are different terms, because which state it is bonded to is part of what it is."
Fig 13 :88: "The residual of the turn just closed is what the next turn composes against, so no two passes begin from the same place."

The snow line is a gradient over space; the delayed instability is a gradient over time. Under Fig 13 :76 these are different terms, so a history that collapses them into "early versus late" or "inside versus outside" has lost the bond. Each turn's residual feeds the next: the gas-disk era leaves the compact giant planet configuration that the planetesimal-disk era composes against, which leaves the architecture the current era composes against.

**Verdict: triangulated** (Fig 13 cross-kind law plus the world's three-era chronology, which is an independently derived sequence).
**Refuter:** derive one of the six substrate terms from the other five, or show that a seventh term is needed to hold the radial and temporal gradients apart.

### F6 - The framework returns a shape, not a mechanism name

Fig 9 :32: "you get a kind of thing, never the answer itself"
Fig 9 :27: "describe it before you go looking, or any frame you pick will seem to fit"
Fig 9 :30: "your words are borrowed too, so describe the effect rather than the cause"

The canonical local usage rule says the framework reports "what a missing thing would have to look like (a shape, not a name)". **Verdict: triangulated** (Fig 9 plus the canonical local README, low overlap between a figure line and a usage document).

### F7 - Triangulation requires low overlap; agreement from one pool is one witness

Fig 2 :35: "Alignment is triangulation, not negotiation. A mirror cannot correct you."
Fig 2 :40: "agreement about the world is worthless when the agreeing frames share an evidence pool"
Fig 8 :45: "Two frames together reach what neither reaches alone."
Fig 8 :51: "Convergent derivation and adopted import look identical in the contents. Only the record of where each came from separates them"

Dynamics (N-body migration), cosmochemistry (isotopes, taxonomies) and observation (catalogues, sample returns) are three low-overlap frames. Their agreement on, for example, the inner S-type / outer C-type belt gradient is worth something because they do not draw on the same evidence. But Grand Tack and the Kuiper-belt implantation work share authors and a modelling tradition (Walsh, Morbidelli, Levison, Raymond); their agreement is a pool talking, not two witnesses. The world source Portegies Zwart et al. (2021) explicitly notes that "the chain of events has never been tested as a causal sequence", which is a warning against treating a bundle of separately fitted imports as one result.

**Verdict: triangulated** (Fig 2 anchor law plus Fig 8 surplus law).
**Refuter:** show that the dynamics and cosmochemistry frames draw on the same evidence and still count as two independent witnesses here.

### F8 - The ground settles; instruments are improved from worse instruments already returning something

Fig 2 :29: "An anchor is whatever settles the question and does not change its answer because of what any frame thinks."
Fig 6 :32: "An instrument is not built from a description. It is improved from a worse instrument already returning something."
Fig 6 :33: "Nobody specified a telescope from an account of Mars. A wandering point in the sky was already a reading, and every sharper instrument was built on the residual the last one produced."
Fig 9 :42: "narrowing gets you close, the ground decides"

The anchors here are catalogues (the National Academies chapter reports more than 1.2 million discovered small bodies, citing the IAU Minor Planet Center), returned samples (Hayabusa, Hayabusa2, OSIRIS-REx, Rosetta, Dawn), crater counts and isotopic measurements. A model is not promoted because it is elegant. Survey upgrades (for example the Vera C. Rubin Observatory anticipated by the NASEM chapter) improve an instrument already returning readings; they are not specified from a description of the answer.

**Verdict: triangulated** (Fig 2 anchor definition plus Fig 6 instrument rule).
**Refuter:** a mechanism adopted on agreement alone, with no ground reading that would discriminate it.

### F9 - Unreachable is a verdict, not a silence

Fig 9 :44: "Only a sealed room can be searched to the end, and no frame is a sealed room. So searching and finding nothing proves nothing."
Fig 13 :134: "Unreachable is a verdict here, not a silence"
Fig 6 :27: "Which is why some unreachability is depth and some is genuine absence, and from inside the frame the two look the same."

The Oort cloud "has not been observed directly, and its origin remains theoretical" (Portegies Zwart et al. 2021, abstract read). Its slot is therefore reached by import from dynamics and galactic-environment models, not by direct ground reading. This is `specified`/`candidate`, and the honest report is that the direct-observation atom is not in the closure of current instruments. A failure to observe it is not proof of absence (Fig 8 :39: "So a search that finds nothing is never a proof of absence.").

**Verdict: single-source for the framework verdict (Fig 9/13), corroborated by an independent world statement about direct observability.**
**Refuter:** a direct observation of Oort cloud objects that discriminates formation models. Until then, the stop is recorded here.

### F10 - The lineage is what makes a history checkable

Fig 12 :104: "A settled molecule becomes an atom for whatever composes over it. So irreducible is a fact about a level and never about the world, and there is no bottom"
Fig 12 :94: "The arrangement is the substance."

From a composed term alone, "Nothing about a composed term tells you, from looking at it, which route produced it or whether that route was available." The current S/C gradient does not display the route that produced it; the record of simulations, dates and imports is the term. Two histories can arrive at the same substance (Fig 13 :121: "Different routes can arrive at the same substance."), so a report of the end state is not a report of the history.

**Verdict: triangulated** (Fig 12 composition law plus Fig 13 route claim).
**Refuter:** show that the current distribution uniquely determines its own history. Since it does not, the lineage must be carried and cited.

## 4. Pre-registered refuter (written before the conclusion)

Per the standing rule that a falsifier written before the run is a design instrument, the shape claim of this solution is refuted if any of the following holds:

1. A single mechanism reproduces all five population distributions with no population-specific residual left (breaks F1, Fig 1 :19 and Fig 9 :4).
2. A seat is demonstrated to have acted on these habitats in Fig 11's specific sense of instrumenting, distilling and introducing (breaks F2, Fig 11 :56).
3. A standing valence list for one actor survives across all five residuals (breaks F4, Fig 11 :51).
4. Sample returns or surveys return a distribution that no imported mechanism predicts under the ground check (breaks F8, Fig 9 :42).
5. One of the six substrate terms is derived from the other five, or a seventh is needed (breaks F5, Fig 13).
6. The figures themselves are shown to return a specific mechanism name for this residual (breaks F6).

No part of this solution may be labelled `grounded` until one of these has been run and the claim survived.
