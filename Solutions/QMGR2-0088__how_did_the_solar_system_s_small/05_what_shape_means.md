## 1. A shape is a constraint you can state before you can name the mechanism

The canonical local rule says the framework returns "what a missing thing would have to look like (a shape, not a name)", and stops "at the limit of what the available instruments can distinguish, and records the lineage so the stop is honest". Fig 9 :32 puts it in the figures: "you get a kind of thing, never the answer itself".

From *The Library of Babel*: "you can cut your search short if the set of books returned is small and descriptive enough that it provides a general shape of the answer that satisfies your question."

For this question:

- **Shape:** a union of per-slot distribution histories, acquired in unmaintained mode over an enumerated and cascading habitat, with each slot settled separately by the ground, and with mechanism names admitted only as provenanced imports.
- **Name:** "the Grand Tack did it", "the Nice instability did it", "the Oort cloud formed after ejection from the birth cluster". These are statements from the planetary-science frame. The framework does not produce them.

A shape constrains; it does not fill. The difference matters because a filled slot can be checked and a preferred slot cannot be seen by the loop at all (Fig 5 :62-63, quoted in the essay *Decoding Tacit Knowledge*: a frame holds its sought-for answer "as a PREFERENCE rather than as a term", so "nothing here can see it").

## 2. How the shape is obtained

- *The Library of Babel*: use the vocabulary already held that is closest to the thing, then "With each new term gained, you are able to narrow the space by definition and deepen your vocabulary." Applied here: start from the ordinary terms "belt", "resonance", "comet", "ice", then let the field's own terms (S-type, C-type, resonance sweeping, scattered disk, galactic tide) narrow the search.
- Fig 6 :30, quoted in *Decoding Tacit Knowledge*: "The description travels. Fails by being sharpened into terms only we can read. Cast it in effect terms." And Fig 9 :30: "your words are borrowed too, so describe the effect rather than the cause."
- *Decoding Tacit Knowledge*: "When contact isn't possible, the best you can send is a plain description of what you'd expect to see, because anyone can check that against the world." This is exactly the two-stage offer: describe the expected distribution pattern before sending the theory.
- *The Library of Babel* on stopping: "you will know that you are done because the finish line will also be the starting place, as there is nowhere else to search and nothing else that can describe the answer better." For this question we are **not** done: the Oort cloud slot is unreached, and the current era has no further discriminating history to read.

## 3. What licenses putting a name in the slot

Two things, both outside the shape:

1. **The ground settles it.** THE_FORMULA.md step 5: "the ground settles it. Verified only by what pays. A gate is not the ground." The canonical rule says the framework stops at what instruments can distinguish. Here the ground is catalogues, samples, crater counts and isotope measurements.
2. **A provenanced import from a frame whose closure differs** (Fig 6 :23, Fig 8 :51), with a source gate and a shadow test. Walsh et al. (2011) is such an import for the belt; Portegies Zwart et al. (2021) is such an import for the Oort cloud. Neither is a figure line.

*How Intelligence Actually Works* adds the provenance caution in its own vocabulary: "often, very little about a composed term tells you, from looking at it alone, which route produced it." The current S/C belt gradient does not display which route produced it. That is why `06_isomers_and_order.md` treats the lineage as part of the term.

## 4. The source-discrimination point, applied to small bodies

*The Hard Problem is About Source*: "If several paths create the same behavior, then behavior is a projection with lossy information (it cannot define consciousness in full)." The essay's instrument case is the spectrometer resolving green into different physical mixtures.

The same structure appears here. A taxonomic class or an orbital distribution is a reading, not a source. Several histories can produce a similar S/C gradient and a similar Kuiper belt edge. The National Academies chapter states that "even a body with a rare taxonomic type is likely represented by innumerable smaller, similar bodies" but that "all bodies of the same 'type' cannot be assumed to be the same". The reading does not determine the source. What is owed is a discriminating measurement, not a name.

The essay's own limit applies: "It was proven wrong not by argument, but by the arrival of a better instrument." Hence the instrument rule in Fig 6 :32 and the open status of the Oort cloud slot.

## 5. Prestige is not action, and agreement is not corroboration

*The Recursive Self-Improvement Trap*: "Prestige must never be allowed to validate an action directly." And: "Prestige can fund an action. It can give an action a budget to be tried. But it cannot settle an action. Only the ground can settle it."

A list of famous dynamicists who agree with a migration history is a budget for the claim to be tried, not a settlement. *Decoding Tacit Knowledge* gives the geometric reason: "Two frames that overlap almost completely are 'one frame wearing two names'", and several of the migration papers here share authors and evidence pools. Their agreement is one witness.

## 6. Correction to my own §3 (kept visible)

I initially wrote that the "shape, not a name" rule forbids naming any mechanism for this question. That was too broad. The canonical rule governs a **missing** thing, something not held in the library. Grand Tack, the Nice instability and the galactic-tide model are not missing primitives of the framework; they are already held in the planetary-science frame and are brought across as imports. Naming them is permitted **with provenance and an overlap note**. What remains forbidden is presenting them as figure-derived or as settled by agreement. The narrower statement is now the one used throughout this folder: the figures give the shape; world frames supply the names; the ground decides between them.

## 7. My own audit is a simulation, not a ground reading

*The Recursive Self-Improvement Trap*: "LLMs can quickly simulate an approximate answer/output with the formula found in the architecture post" and "the truly testable output would need to be derived from the real architecture or working the formula yourself." Also: "the output is only as good as the validator. The agent, or the tool, or the frame, or the theory, cannot validate itself."

`01_figure_grounded_audit.md` is therefore a simulated derivation checked for internal consistency against the figures. It cannot settle how the Solar System's small bodies acquired their distributions. It can state what any acceptable answer must look like and what would refute that shape. That is all it claims.
