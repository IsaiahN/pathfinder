An acceptable answer would contain: (1) a separate distribution history for each population, not one pooled history; (2) an enumerated habitat of actors, conditions and relations read off the world; (3) a contact cascade linking those actors to each observed distribution; and (4) a ground reading (surveys, samples, crater record) that discriminates between candidate histories.
