## 1. A shape is a constraint, not a filler

The figures README states the limit: the framework returns "what a missing thing would have to look like (a shape, not a name)." Fig 9 :32: "you get a kind of thing, never the answer itself." Fig 13 :134: "Unreachable is a verdict here, not a silence," and the framework "cannot say which primitive is missing, because naming it would mean already holding it."

From *The Library of Babel*: "You can cut your search short if the set of books returned is small and descriptive enough that it provides a general shape of the answer that satisfies your question."

So a **shape** is the narrowed form of an answer you can state before you can name it. It constrains; it does not fill. For this question:

- A shape: "development is a property of child and habitat; changes act by changing contact; what crosses generations is a head start and a method, not a recording."
- Not a shape, but content requiring a ground: "two hours of screen time a day causes language delay," "this curriculum works," "this parenting style is best."

The distinction is the whole answer to the question as put. A general-mode verdict that names a specific mechanism would be a name where only a shape is available.

## 2. How a shape is obtained

- *Library of Babel*: use the vocabulary already held that is closest to the thing, look for recurring new terms that overlap or correlate with it, recurse. "With each new term gained, you narrow the space and deepen your vocabulary." The stop condition: "the finish line will also be the starting place," and the paradox is that by then you have "composed the answer through the process of locating it."
- Fig 6 :30: "The description travels. Fails by being sharpened into terms only we can read. Cast it in effect terms." Fig 9 :30-31: "your words are borrowed too, so describe the effect rather than the cause." So the residual is described as a failure of prediction about outcomes, not as a theory about "nature" or "nurture."
- *Decoding Tacit Knowledge*: "When contact isn't possible, the best you can send is a plain description of what you'd expect to see, because anyone can check that against the world." This is why the outreach file leads with checkable consequences rather than with the framework.
- *Library of Babel* on bundling: "Perhaps your problem was a bundle or conflation of several problems masquerading as one? You will need to search all of those problems individually." This is the essay-level source for Fig 9 :55's union reading in F4 of `01`.

## 3. What licenses a name

Only two things, both outside the shape itself:

1. **The ground settles it.** Formula step 5: "the ground settles it. Verified only by what pays. A gate is not the ground." The README: the framework "stops at the limit of what the available instruments can distinguish, and records the lineage so the stop is honest."
2. **A provenanced import from a frame whose closure differs**, with a source gate and a shadow test (Fig 6 :23, Fig 8 :50-52).

For this question the developmental-science sources are imports of the second kind. They are cited with author, venue, year and quote in `10_source_ledger.csv`. Their agreement with the figure shape is corroboration of the *shape*, not a derivation of it. They cannot name the specific mechanism the figures refuse to name.

*They're Scanning Books* sharpens the limit: "A minimum viable set of six terms is a human's careful guess at reality's compression axes; another sensorium's minimum set need not be those six." And: "Declaring the primitives does not make them neutral. It makes the choice visible." A child's developmental primitives are likewise a visible choice in any model, not a discovery of nature's own list. Turning a visible choice into an invisible one is the failure the shape refuses.

## 4. Why "the child in isolation" is the specific forbidden error

*How a Society of Agents Would Work* states the replacement directly: "capability is a property of agent-and-habitat, never of the agent alone. A newborn agent is made capable by the surroundings it is born into." A device or curriculum improves a child only if it changes contact. This is the essay-level source for F1.

*How Intelligence Actually Works* adds the lineage frame: "Not a family tree, a family web." and "we are not the holders of that mapping, we are the mapping, the same way a seed is the seedline." A child is a lineage, not an isolated instance. The transmission rule is stated there: "It can be inherited or taught, so the recipient need not pay the bill themselves, but something, somewhere, already paid it." The bill is paid by the habitat and the lineage, not by the child alone.

*The Hard Problem is About Source* gives the error mechanism. Metamerism: an instrument reads green and "it cannot comment on the source" (one green light, or yellow plus blue, or RGB, or CMYK, or a scheme not yet discovered). "If several paths create the same behavior, then behavior is a projection with lossy information." And: "It was proven wrong not by argument, but by the arrival of a better instrument." In this question, observing that two cohorts both score well does not tell you whether one received a method and the other a recording. The reading does not determine the source. What is owed is a source-discriminating observation, not a name assigned from the reading.

*The Recursive Self-Improvement Trap*: "the output is only as good as the validator. The agent, or the tool, or the frame, or the theory, cannot validate itself." This derivation cannot settle its own claim.

## 5. Q: Does "shape, not a name" forbid saying anything specific about development? ANSWERED with a correction

**First reading, recorded honestly:** I initially read the rule as forbidding all developmental content, so that only "contact changes things" could be said. That overstates it.

**Correction.** The README phrase governs "what a **missing** thing would have to look like." Its subject is a primitive not held in the library. Here the missing thing is not a primitive of the framework; it is an empirical direction (which change, which dose, which child) that no instrument in this derivation discriminates. The honest reason for silence is therefore **no ground in this run**, not a ban on naming held atoms. The shape is still only a shape, but the boundary is drawn by availability of measurement, not by a categorical prohibition on specificity.

**Does the folder commit a name-from-projection error?** Not if it stays with the shape. It would commit that error if it read a cohort outcome and named its source (method versus recording, habitat versus child) without an instrument that discriminates sources. F3 and F5 keep the distinction as a question for the ground. Both readings are recorded so neither is quietly lost.

## 6. Two consequences for outreach

**Prestige is not action.** From *The Recursive Self-Improvement Trap*: "Prestige can fund an action. It can give an action a budget to be tried. But it cannot settle an action. Only the ground can settle it. The Ground Maintainer must be blind to prestige." A campaign that gathers well-known developmental psychologists to agree with the shape is the named collapse, not corroboration. The legitimate ask is the opposite: give the shape a budget to be tried, let the ground settle it, and record the verdict before showing the theory. "You may all agree and be wrong."

**This audit is a simulation, not a ground reading.** Same essay: "LLMs can quickly simulate an approximate answer/output with the formula" and "the truly testable output would need to be derived from the real architecture or working the formula yourself." The architecture essay adds that with an LLM demo "could never be verified if the solutions presented were not contrived to fit the formula." So `01` is a simulated derivation checked for internal consistency against the figures. It is **not** a test of the claim and must be labelled as such. It cannot settle anything it reports on.

## 7. What the shape is, in one sentence

A shape here is the constraint that any answer about major changes in family life, technology and education must run through habitat contact, intergenerational transmission of methods rather than recordings, an unbundled union of overlapping conditions, per-child measurement, an outcome anchor, and a named least-instrumented link, while leaving the specific empirical direction to the ground.
