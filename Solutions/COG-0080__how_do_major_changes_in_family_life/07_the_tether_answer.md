# The Tether's answer: how major changes reshape development across generations

**Mode: `general` on the structural shape, `specified` on the derived predictions, not `grounded`.** This is a shape and a route, not a result. Simulated derivation checked against the figures (`05_what_shape_means.md` section 6), not a ground reading.

## 1. The residual it answers

An inherited model of the child predicts a developmental trajectory for given family, technology and education conditions. When those conditions change, the prediction stops resolving. The gap is indexed per child, not per cohort (Fig 1 :19: "The residual is indexed by object slot, not aggregated."). The three changes are overlapping conditions in one habitat (Fig 9 :55: "A bundled problem is usually a union of overlapping conditions, not a list of separate ones."), so this is a union compound with its own loop, not one hard question with three labels.

## 2. The shape

1. **Development is child-and-habitat, and change acts through contact.** Fig 11 :39: "capability is a property of agent-and-habitat, never of the agent alone." Fig 11 :40: "A system cannot be made more capable by improving the agent in isolation. An improvement that does not change contact changes nothing, however much it improves." A device or curriculum improved in isolation does not move the developmental ground.

2. **The habitat is enumerated outward, and contact cascades.** Fig 11 :43: "The habitat is enumerated, never composed." Fig 11 :52: "And contact cascades. Scoping the habitat to direct contact is how DDT reached the eagles: first-order enumeration, second-order effect, and the residual that mattered was three steps out." Enumerate actors, conditions and relations around the child, then what contacts those. A first-order "screens cause X" misses the third-order effect.

3. **What crosses a generation is a method or a head start, never a recording.** Fig 4 :22: "Only methods cross up. Only head starts come down." Fig 4 :25: "A recording carried upward looks like knowledge and is a description of one occasion." A recording replays a past success and cannot produce a new one. The same method lands differently in a different habitat, so the receiving generation still has to test it: Fig 4 :34: "The level a method lands on remakes it. A seed carries no small tree: it carries what will grow one, and the conditions it lands in decide what it becomes."

4. **The three changes must be unbundled before they are searched.** Fig 9 :57: "What remains is smaller and it is also categorically different: a case in two conditions is not a harder case of either." Single-condition regions, pairwise intersections and the triple compound each need their own loop. A single lever clears only the single-condition region because the second condition still binds at the intersection.

5. **Measure per child, not per cohort.** Fig 1 :22: "Aggregating across slots is how a live signal disappears into an average." A near-zero cohort mean with one live child is a legal state. The conflicting early-childhood programme findings are the predicted signature of this collapse.

6. **The anchor is the measured outcome, not agreement.** Fig 2 :36: "An anchor is legitimate when the question is constitutively about it." Fig 2 :37: "Human judgment is the right anchor for is this a good answer for a person, because the question is about people." Because the question is about people, the ground is the outcome for a person, not expert consensus. Fig 2 :40: "disqualifying for a verdict: agreement about the world is worthless when the agreeing frames share an evidence pool."

7. **Isolation is substitution, not removal.** Fig 11 :66: "Isolation is not removal of the habitat. It is substitution of one habitat for another." A screen-mediated social world installs unchosen actors and may fail to reproduce what the goal required. Both failures are silent until the goal fails or the introduced actor acts.

8. **The least-instrumented link decides where effort belongs.** Fig 3 :27: "A link with no instrument is not a link that works. It is a link nobody has looked at." The affordance and progress links in the middle of the dependency chain have the fewest instruments; effort spent on test scores and screen-time hours above the break cannot rescue them.

## 3. What it explicitly does not say

- Not which device, curriculum or dose helps or harms. That is content requiring a ground, recorded as `gap` in F10 of `01_figure_grounded_audit.md`. The framework returns a shape, not a name.
- Not a literal six-term developmental derivation. The substrate governs its own stratum; applying it literally to psychology is a strain, re-censed as `gap` at F9.
- Not that one of the three changes is the real cause and the others are noise. They are a union compound.
- Not that a cohort average is useless for administration. It is disqualified as a witness for where the developmental signal lives.
- Not that agreement among researchers is corroboration. Agreement about shared meaning is a convention; agreement about the world from one evidence pool is one witness.
- Not grounded. No measurement was taken in this derivation.

## 4. What would settle it: the ground

A source-discriminating observation, not a further argument. From *The Hard Problem is About Source*: an instrument that reads green "it cannot comment on the source"; it was resolved by a better instrument, not by argument. Here the corresponding questions are:

- **Contact test:** does a putative improvement change anything about contact for a specific child, with contact measured rather than asserted? If no contact changes, the F1 prediction is falsified.
- **Recording test:** does the next generation hold a capability it did not have before the handoff, and does it survive re-testing in that generation's own habitat? If a recording suffices, F3 is falsified.
- **Union test:** does a single lever clear the intersections of family, technology and education, or only the single-condition regions? If one lever clears everything, F4 is falsified.
- **Slot test:** does a cohort average preserve the per-child signal that actually steers a successful intervention? If it does, F5 is falsified.
- **Anchor test:** does the claimed outcome move when the researchers who measure it change their mind about the theory? If it does, it was not an anchor.
- **Standing refuters:** all seven in `01_figure_grounded_audit.md` section 4, written before this conclusion.

## 5. The honest stop

**Unreached, not unreachable, for the content; `gap` for the literal substrate derivation.** The shape stops at the limit of the instruments available in this run and records the lineage so the stop is checkable. "Indiscernibility is not identity." The six-term set is "a human's careful guess at reality's compression axes"; declaring it makes the choice visible, not neutral. Nothing here maintains the ground; the ground does not decay, the channel does, and keeping that channel open is a seat's office, not the loop's.

## 6. One-paragraph version for outreach

> The Tether does not tell you which device, curriculum or number of hours is good for children. It tells you the shape any answer must have. Development is a property of the child and the habitat together, so major changes in family life, technology and education reshape it by changing contact, not by rewriting the child. What one generation hands to the next is a method or a head start that makes the next search cheaper, never a recording of past answers, which is why an early boost can fade while a reusable practice lasts. The three changes overlap, so they must be unbundled before any lever is tried; the signal lives in individual children, not in a cohort average; and the thing that settles a claim about people is an outcome for a person, not agreement among researchers who read the same literature. Substituted digital habitats fail silently in two ways: what they do not reproduce and what they introduce. The shape comes with five refuters written in advance. The specific empirical direction stays open until a ground settles it.
