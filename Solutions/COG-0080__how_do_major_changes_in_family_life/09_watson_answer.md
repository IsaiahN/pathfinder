## DOMAIN CENSUS

R by arity, symmetry and scale, then one line per domain with actors / currency / anchor, partition or union, and order by dependency.

- **R (the residual).** The gap between an inherited model of the child and what registers at the ground when family, technology or education conditions change. Arity: three overlapping conditions, so a union compound, not one hard question. Symmetry: not symmetric, a change in the medium alters the habitat that carries the other two. Scale: first order within a childhood, second order across the generational membrane, third order in cascades. Fig 9 :55 names the union: a case in two conditions is not a harder case of either.
- **Domain 1, the developing child.** Actors: child, inherited priors, core knowledge, cognition. Currency: per-slot residual, prediction error, contact. Anchor: the measured child outcome. Type: loop.
- **Domain 2, the habitat.** Actors: caregivers, peers, teachers, devices, algorithms, curricula, conditions, relations. Currency: contact and actor cascades. Anchor: the same ground settles; the habitat itself updates. Type: enumeration field, union of three overlapping conditions.
- **Domain 3, the intergenerational membrane.** Actors: handing-over generation, receiving generation. Currency: methods and head starts versus recordings. Anchor: what settles in the receiver's own contact with its ground. Type: transmission.
- **Domain 4, the verification frame.** Actors: researchers, longitudinal studies, instruments. Currency: evidence versus agreement, residual. Anchor: measured outcomes, never agreement. In this simulated run all four domains draw on one evidence pool, so their agreement is one pool talking, not corroboration.
- **Order by dependency.** Perceive changed conditions, form terms for what they afford, state what counts as progress, plan, then learn and carry across the membrane (Fig 3). Refuse a union before minting a single lever; describe the residual before choosing a frame (Fig 9 :28).

## VERDICT (from the figures)

The shape survives; the specific content does not, and the literal substrate derivation is a gap. Development is child-and-habitat, so major changes act by changing contact, enumerated outward with cascades. What crosses generations is methods and head starts, not recordings. Family, technology and education form a union to be unbundled before any lever is tried. The signal is per child, not per cohort average; the anchor is an outcome for a person, not agreement; isolation is habitat substitution with two silent failure modes; the least-instrumented middle link decides where effort belongs. Which device, curriculum or dose is harmful or helpful is `gap`: only a ground can name it. Nothing here is `grounded`.

- **cost if wrong:** bounded and locatable if the shape is sent with its refuters. The real cost is spending the independence of outside readers by sending a recording instead of a generator, which Fig 2 :42 prices: "Buy independence in proportion to the cost of being wrong." A second, larger cost is acting on a cohort average that hides the one live child who needed a different intervention. Both are avoidable by leading with the shape, the refuter and per-child measurement.
- **undoable:** yes. The gaps are removable without touching the derivation: a ground observation can name a specific mechanism, and a re-census can decide whether the six-term grammar has a legitimate developmental stratum. A claim already sent can be superseded by the corrected packet. No primitive has to be withdrawn.
- **reversed by:** a ground reading, not agreement. A contact test showing an isolated improvement moves the outcome would reverse the habitat claim; a recording shown to create new capability without re-testing would reverse the transmission claim; a lever that clears the union's intersections would reverse the union claim; a cohort average that preserves the steering signal would reverse the slot claim; an outcome that moves when researchers change their mind would show the anchor was not an anchor. A derivation of a developmental primitive from the other five members of Fig 13 would close the recorded `gap`. Until one of these fires, the verdict stands as `candidate`.
