## Two things in the wider literature that bear directly on the shape

1. **Early boosts that fade.** The Head Start record reports early literacy gains that disappeared after several years, alongside long-term returns in some studies and a negligible replication in others. Fig 4 :22 says "Only methods cross up. Only head starts come down." A boost delivered as a recording is a description of one occasion; a method is reusable. The mixed empirical record is therefore not noise to be averaged away, it is the signature the figures predict. Do **not** present the figures as having predicted the result forward; the figure line is structural and the empirical cases were read afterwards.

2. **Cascades three steps out.** The ACEs material documents effects that "can also be passed on to their own children," and the screen-time material documents a device-to-sleep-to-attention pathway. Fig 11 :52 uses DDT and the eagles: "first-order enumeration, second-order effect, and the residual that mattered was three steps out." This is a retrodiction, not a result the framework produced. Say so.

## Distribution: offer, do not push

Figure-grounded reasons:

- Fig 4 :30: "So take the round trip and measure the gap. Without it you are navigating by dead reckoning: confident about a position nobody checked." Sending the full framework before a reader has described a residual sends a recording, not a generator. Lead with the shape claim and its refuter; offer links after.
- Fig 10 :42: "A verifier that can reconstruct the claim cannot verify it." A recipient who has absorbed the whole framework before being asked for a verdict has joined the frame being checked.
- Fig 9 :28: "describe it before you go looking, or any frame you pick will seem to fit." Ask what the reader would expect to see if the shape claim were true before giving them the derivation.
- Seat Map :163: "High correlation is disqualifying for a verdict and efficient for a convention." A mass send selected for likely agreement produces a convention, not corroboration.
- Fig 7 :35: "A room's agreement is variation, not a verdict." More recipients raise the vocabulary, not the evidence.

**Recommended order in a reply:** (1) the shape claim and its refuter; (2) ask what the reader would count as a failure, and record the verdict; (3) only then, the links, with the note that the figures, not the summary, are the authority.

## The two-stage offer

1. **Stage 1 before stage 2** is Fig 10 :52, "Bring a characterised residual, never the source," and Fig 2 :45, "Frames range against the ground. They do not negotiate with it." The reader checks the output without holding the framework's interior.
2. **Stage 2 is opt-in by construction.** Seat 4 must not hold the interiors; once a reader has absorbed the theory, their agreement stops being evidence. The verdict on stage 1 must be recorded before any theory is shown, and later agreement is never pooled as corroboration (Fig 2 :40: "disqualifying for a verdict: agreement about the world is worthless when the agreeing frames share an evidence pool").
3. **Stage 3 sends a method, which is permitted.** Fig 4 :22: "Only methods cross up. Only head starts come down." The formula is a generator, so giving it away does not give away an answer. Each user still needs a ground and a refuter of their own.

**Hard precondition:** stages 1 to 3 only work if the stage-1 shape survives. If a reader fires any of the five refuters in `02_outreach_email.md`, the relevant finding is withdrawn before any theory is sent.
