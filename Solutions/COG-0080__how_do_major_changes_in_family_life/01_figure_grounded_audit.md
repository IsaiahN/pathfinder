## 0. The question restated as an effect

The question asks what a change in the conditions of upbringing does to a developing person and to what is handed to the next generation. Stated as a residual: an inherited model of "the child" predicts a developmental trajectory for given family, technology and education conditions. When those conditions change, the prediction stops resolving. The residual R is the gap between that prediction and what actually registers at the ground for each child.

- **Arity:** three overlapping conditions (family life, technology, education), so a **union compound**, not three independent questions (Fig 9). Not one residual with three labels.
- **Symmetry:** not symmetric. A change in the medium (technology) changes the habitat that carries the other two; the order of changes matters (Fig 12, ordered bonds).
- **Scale:** first order within one childhood, second order across the generational membrane, third order in cascades (parental work conditions to home interaction to child outcome; platform design to sleep to attention to learning).

**What an answer would look like, bound as an ordinary term (Formula step, Direction):** a checkable **shape** stating (i) where the causal work lives, (ii) what crosses between generations, (iii) how the three changes compose, (iv) how the residual is measured, and (v) what anchor settles it. The specific content (which device, which curriculum, how many hours) is explicitly not part of the shape. Binding this in advance lets the preference accrue a residual like any other term.

## 3. Findings

### F1 - Development is a property of child and habitat, not of the child alone (governs D2; triangulated)

Fig 11 :39: "capability is a property of agent-and-habitat, never of the agent alone"
Fig 11 :40: "A system cannot be made more capable by improving the agent in isolation. An improvement that does not change contact changes nothing, however much it improves."

Major changes in family life, technology and education reshape development by changing the habitat, that is, the field of contact around the child. A device, curriculum or parenting technique that is improved "in isolation" does not move the developmental ground unless it changes contact. This is the structural core of the answer.

**Triangulation status:** triangulated, low overlap. The figure line is framework-internal. The CDC child development page says "Early childhood is critical to health and development. Families, health professionals, educators, and others can help children reach their full potential." (CDC, 2026, public health). The CDC ACEs page says toxic stress "can negatively affect children's brain development, immune system, and stress-response systems. These changes can affect children's attention, decision-making, and learning." (CDC, 2026, epidemiology). Neither source draws on the figures. Both predict that changing conditions changes the developing outcome through contact, which corroborates the figure verdict.

**Refuter:** an improvement to the child in isolation, with no change in contact, that reliably moves the measured ground.

### F2 - The habitat is enumerated, never composed, and contact cascades (governs D2; triangulated)

Fig 11 :43: "The habitat is enumerated, never composed."
Fig 11 :52: "And contact cascades. Scoping the habitat to direct contact is how DDT reached the eagles: first-order enumeration, second-order effect, and the residual that mattered was three steps out."
Fig 11 :49: "An actor's valence is relative to the residual, not a property it carries."

The habitat must be read off the world, not assembled from a theory. Enumerate everything in contact with the residual, then what contacts those, outward until the cascade stops mattering: actors (caregiver, peer, teacher, platform, algorithm), conditions (safety, sleep, nutrition, light), relations (attachment, competition, dependency), then second-order links (parental work conditions to home interaction to child; platform design to sleep to attention to learning). A first-order claim such as "screens cause X" scopes the habitat to direct contact and misses the residual that matters three steps out. And a valence is not carried: the same device is a benefactor to one residual (a child with no access to print) and a malfactor to another (a child whose sleep is displaced).

**Triangulation status:** triangulated, low overlap. Bronfenbrenner's ecological systems theory describes the microsystem (family, school, peers), mesosystem (interconnections between microsystems), exosystem (parental work affecting the child), and chronosystem (change over the life course and across generations). The Wikipedia account quotes Bronfenbrenner's criticism of "the science of the strange behavior of children in strange situations with strange adults for the briefest possible periods of time" and notes that "Macrosystems evolve across time and from generation to generation." This is an independent developmental-psychology vocabulary for enumeration and cascades. The screen-time literature supplies a concrete cascade: "Children who spend an increased amount of time on screens have fewer opportunities to interact with adults and caregivers which reduces their language development." (Wikipedia, Screen time, 2026). These are corroborations, not authority.

**Refuter:** a demonstration that a first-order enumeration of direct contacts preserves the developmental signal that matters, with no second- or third-order cascade.

### F3 - What crosses a generation is a head start and a method, never a recording (governs D3; triangulated)

Fig 4 :22: "Only methods cross up. Only head starts come down."
Fig 4 :11: "a head start, never a recording"
Fig 4 :25: "A recording carried upward looks like knowledge and is a description of one occasion."
Fig 4 :34: "The level a method lands on remakes it. A seed carries no small tree: it carries what will grow one, and the conditions it lands in decide what it becomes."

Family and education hand to the next generation methods and head starts that make its search cheaper, not a copy of the previous generation's answers. A recording (a fixed script, a preloaded device, a curriculum delivered as a sequence of correct responses) replays a past success and produces "a system that repeats a past success and cannot produce a new one" (Fig 4 :26). The same method lands differently in a different habitat, so a head start is not a guarantee; the receiving generation still has to test it against its own ground.

This also explains the shape of the empirical record on early childhood programmes. The Head Start entry reports that "early boosts in literacy were observed, these disappeared after several years" and, elsewhere, "sizable, long-term returns to investments in means-tested, public preschool programs." Both can be true if what crossed was sometimes a recording (a boost that fades when the occasion ends) and sometimes a method (a reusable practice the child and family can reapply). The figures say which distinction to look for; they do not say which programmes satisfy it.

**Triangulation status:** triangulated, low overlap. The tacit-knowledge essay states the same rule in a human-development vocabulary: "what passes down is ways of doing things and head starts, never the experience itself." (Isaiah N, Decoding Tacit Knowledge, 2026-11-10). It shares an author with the figures, so it is strong internal corroboration but weak independent corroboration. The Head Start record (Wikipedia, 2026) is independent and low overlap; it predicts fading boosts and conditional long-term returns, matching the reading. Overlap declared: the essay and figures share an evidence pool.

**Refuter:** a recording that produces new capability in the receiving generation without that generation re-testing it against its own ground.

### F4 - The three changes are a union compound; unbundle before searching (governs D2; triangulated)

Fig 9 :55: "A bundled problem is usually a union of overlapping conditions, not a list of separate ones."
Fig 9 :54: "And when the split lands on a union rather than a partition, unbundle before you search again."
Fig 9 :57: "What remains is smaller and it is also categorically different: a case in two conditions is not a harder case of either."
Fig 8 :45: "Two frames together reach what neither reaches alone."

"How do family life, technology and education together reshape development" is a bundled question. A single lever (a screen-time rule, a curriculum change) clears the region where only one condition holds and leaves the intersections standing, because the second condition still binds. The intersections are not harder versions of the single-condition cases; they are a specific compound with its own loop and its own ground. Unbundle into: family-only, technology-only, education-only, each pairwise intersection, and the triple compound. Then search each separately.

**Triangulation status:** triangulated, low overlap. Bronfenbrenner's mesosystem is defined as the interconnections between microsystems (family and teachers, peers and family), which is an independent vocabulary for the same intersections. Fig 8 supplies the metalaw for why the union has a surplus. These two routes have low overlap (metalaw versus developmental theory), so the agreement is weak but not circular.

**Refuter:** a demonstration that the three conditions do not overlap in the relevant habitat, so a single lever clears the whole residual.

### F5 - Measure per child, never as a cohort average (governs D4; triangulated)

Fig 1 :19: "The residual is indexed by object slot, not aggregated."
Fig 1 :22: "Aggregating across slots is how a live signal disappears into an average."

The slot is the child (and within the child, the outcome). A cohort mean throws away the only signal that says where to look. The conflicting Head Start findings are the predicted signature of this collapse: an early average literacy gain, a fade-out, a long-term return in some cohorts and a negligible replication in others. An averaged result cannot tell you which children were live, so it cannot steer the next intervention. Support is satisfied when some slot carries positive mass, so a global residual near zero with one live child is a legal state and not an inert one (Fig 1 :20).

**Triangulation status:** triangulated, single-source for the figure definition plus independent corroboration. The Head Start evidence pool is independent of the framework. The figure definition is, of course, framework-internal. The combination is reported as triangulated because the empirical pattern (gains that fade under aggregation) is low-overlap with the metalaw.

**Refuter:** a demonstration that a cohort average preserves the per-child signal that actually steers a successful intervention.

### F6 - The anchor for a question about people is an outcome, not agreement (governs D4; triangulated)

Fig 2 :29: "An anchor is whatever settles the question and does not change its answer because of what any frame thinks. A measurement, an experiment, a counter, a market, a patient outcome."
Fig 2 :36: "An anchor is legitimate when the question is constitutively about it."
Fig 2 :37: "Human judgment is the right anchor for is this a good answer for a person, because the question is about people."
Fig 2 :40: "disqualifying for a verdict: agreement about the world is worthless when the agreeing frames share an evidence pool"

The question is constitutively about people, so the ground is the measured developmental outcome, not the agreement of researchers, parents or platforms about what "good development" means. Agreement is the product when the question is about shared meaning (what we mean by "well-being"); it is worthless as a verdict about what happens to children when the agreeing frames draw on the same review, the same cohort or the same dataset. Two studies quoting one systematic review are one witness wearing two names. Fig 2 :42: "Buy independence in proportion to the cost of being wrong." The cost of being wrong about children is high, so independence should be bought deliberately.

**Triangulation status:** triangulated, low overlap. The seat map law at :163 independently states "High correlation is disqualifying for a verdict and efficient for a convention." (Seat Map :163; note this is framework-internal, shared pool). The CDC pages use outcomes (learning, attention, well-being, health) as the thing that settles whether an intervention works, which is an independent low-overlap corroboration.

**Refuter:** a demonstration that agreement among studies sharing an evidence pool is independent corroboration of a world claim.

### F7 - Isolation is substitution of a habitat, not removal (governs D2/D3; triangulated)

Fig 11 :66: "Isolation is not removal of the habitat. It is substitution of one habitat for another."
Fig 11 :67: "Move the residual and you must reproduce everything its goal required, and you inherit the new habitat's actors, which you did not choose."
Fig 11 :68: "what you failed to reproduce is invisible until the goal fails"
Fig 11 :70: "A test harness is a substituted habitat. Which is why a synthetic solve proves wiring and never capability."

When a child's social world is substituted by a screen-mediated one, nothing removes social development; a different habitat is installed, with unchosen actors (platform design, engagement optimisation, algorithms). Two failures are silent: what the new habitat failed to reproduce (two-way correction, the "you had to be there" history that tacit knowledge needs) and what it unintentionally introduced (a reward structure that was never chosen). An educational technology that solves in a synthetic harness proves wiring, not capability in the real habitat. The screen-time literature's observation that more screen time means "fewer opportunities to interact with adults and caregivers" is exactly a "failed to reproduce" report.

**Triangulation status:** triangulated, low overlap for the empirical half. Fig 11 is framework-internal. The tacit-knowledge essay says pretext "almost never" travels in text, so it shares the framework pool. The screen-time observation and the Bronfenbrenner family-school microsystem are independent. Reported as triangulated with overlap declared.

**Refuter:** a substituted digital habitat shown to preserve all goal-required contact and introduce no unchosen actors, with the preservation measured rather than assumed.

### F8 - The middle link is where the chain breaks (governs D1 to D3 order; triangulated)

Fig 3 :20: "The chain is ordered by dependency, not by importance."
Fig 3 :19: "Optimising a link that already works cannot rescue one that does not."
Fig 3 :27: "A link with no instrument is not a link that works. It is a link nobody has looked at."

Before asking whether a developmental system is good, ask which link it currently stops at and whether the answer was measured or assumed (Fig 3 :29). The middle link here is having terms for what a changed situation affords and being able to say what counts as progress. It attracts the least instrumentation because it has no obvious test, while test scores and screen-time hours (links above the break) do. Effort spent on the measurable links cannot rescue a missing affordance link. Two-way contact with correction is what builds that link; a one-way delivery cannot correct anything.

**Triangulation status:** triangulated, low overlap. The tacit-knowledge essay gives an independent-seeming account of the two-way correction loop, but shares the author with the figures (high overlap, weak corroboration). The CDC child development framing and Bronfenbrenner's call for ecologically valid study are independent and corroborate the "measure the natural habitat, not the strange situation" reading.

**Refuter:** a demonstration that adding a measurable intervention above the break (more hours, more tests) reliably moves the ground when the affordance link is unmeasured.

### F9 - Strain: applying the six-term substrate literally to development. Re-censed, not forced

Fig 13 :74: "Everything else is a composition across the two kinds, never within one."
Fig 13 :75: "Two states never bond directly. It is not space-time but space, a structure, time; and two structures join only through a state."

An earlier reading tried to write a child's development as a composition of Space, Time, Energy with Spectrum, Gradient, Network, in the manner of the physics exemplar. That only fits with a strain: psychological states are not the substrate states, and the mapping names primitives the framework does not supply. Strain test says a line that only fits with a strain governs another quantity, so re-census.

**Re-census:** Fig 13 governs the substrate of any composing system at every level. It does not supply a developmental psychology. Fig 12 :104 says "A settled molecule becomes an atom for whatever composes over it. So irreducible is a fact about a level and never about the world." At the developmental stratum, the atoms are settled practices, learned methods and institutions, and the six are not re-derived in psychological terms. So the correct verdict is **gap** for a literal six-term derivation of development, and the figure line is left to govern its own level. This is recorded, not silently erased. The figures constrain the shape of any answer at any level; they do not name its content.

**Refuter:** a derivation of a developmental primitive from the other five members of Fig 13, or a demonstration that the six-term grammar, applied literally at the psychological stratum, predicts a developmental outcome that the habitat reading does not.

## 4. Pre-registered refuter (written before the conclusion)

Per Seat Map :287: "a falsifier written before the run is a design instrument, not only an honesty device." The shape claim is refuted if any of the following holds:

1. An improvement to the child in isolation, with no change in contact, reliably moves the measured ground (breaks F1, Fig 11 :40).
2. A first-order enumeration of direct contacts preserves the developmental signal, with no second- or third-order cascade (breaks F2, Fig 11 :52).
3. What crosses a generation is a recording that produces new capability without the receiving generation re-testing it (breaks F3, Fig 4 :25).
4. The three conditions do not overlap, so one lever clears the whole residual (breaks F4, Fig 9 :55).
5. A cohort average preserves the per-child signal that steers a successful intervention (breaks F5, Fig 1 :22).
6. Agreement among studies sharing an evidence pool is shown to be independent corroboration of a world claim (breaks F6, Fig 2 :40).
7. A substituted digital habitat preserves all goal-required contact and introduces no unchosen actors, measured rather than assumed (breaks F7, Fig 11 :67).

If any fires, the relevant finding is withdrawn. Until one is run, no part of this is `grounded`.

## 5. Recommendation

1. Lead with the shape and the refuter, not with a claim about a specific technology (F1 to F4).
2. Enumerate the habitat outward from each child before choosing a lever (F2).
3. Unbundle the union into single-condition regions, pairwise intersections and the triple compound (F4).
4. Measure per child and per outcome; treat a near-zero cohort average with one live child as a legal state (F5).
5. Anchor to measured outcomes and buy independence in proportion to the cost of being wrong (F6).
6. Hand down methods and head starts; mark every recording as a recording (F3).
7. Find the least-instrumented link before adding effort above the break (F8).
8. Say `gap` for the literal six-term derivation and for specific empirical directions (F9, F10). "Unreachable is a verdict, not a silence."
