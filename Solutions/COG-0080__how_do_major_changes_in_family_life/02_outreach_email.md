# Outreach draft: check the output before you decide whether the shape is worth reading

**Use:** one-to-one, or as a cover letter attached to a submission through the public venues in `03_public_contact_points.csv`. **Not** for bulk send.

**Hard precondition:** the shape claim and its refuter below must survive the reader's own check before any theory is shown. If stage 1 fails, there is nothing else to look at.

---

**Subject:** Check one claim about development against what you already know

> Hi [name],
>
> I have one specific claim I would like you to check against your own work. I am not asking you to read a theory first, and I am not asking you to agree. If the claim does not survive, there is nothing else to look at.
>
> **The claim (`specified`, not `grounded`):** when family life, technology and education change, a child's development changes because the habitat around the child changed, not because the child's internal machinery was rewritten. Development is a property of the child and the habitat together. An improvement to the child in isolation, with no change in contact, changes nothing, however much it improves.
>
> Three consequences follow from that:
> 1. What a generation hands to the next is a method or a head start that makes the next child's search cheaper, not a recording of the previous generation's answers. A recording replays a past success and cannot produce a new one. This is why an early boost can fade while a reusable practice lasts.
> 2. Family, technology and education are not three independent levers. They overlap. A single lever clears the place where only one condition holds and leaves the intersections standing. Each intersection needs its own check.
> 3. The signal lives in individual children, not in a cohort average. A near-zero average with one live child is a legal state, not an inert one. An average can hide exactly the child who needs a different answer.
>
> **What I am not claiming:** that any particular device, curriculum or number of hours is harmful or helpful. The framework returns a shape, not a name. It cannot tell you the dose; only a ground can. I am also not claiming that the six-term substrate of the framework derives a developmental psychology. It does not; that question is recorded as a gap.
>
> **What would refute the claim, written before this message:**
> 1. an improvement to the child in isolation, with no change in contact, that reliably moves the measured outcome;
> 2. a recording that produces new capability in the next generation without that generation re-testing it;
> 3. a demonstration that the three conditions do not overlap, so one lever clears the whole residual;
> 4. a cohort average that preserves the per-child signal that actually steers a successful intervention;
> 5. agreement among studies sharing one evidence pool shown to be independent corroboration of a world claim.
>
> If any one holds, the corresponding part of the claim is withdrawn. A one-line "this fails because..." is more useful to me than agreement. If you would rather not engage, no reply is needed.
>
> **If it survives, and only then:** I can send the derivation and the sources. The figures, not my summary of them, are the authority.
>
> **If you want to use the machinery:** the formula is in the public figures link. Run it on a problem you know well and can check against a ground. Write down what would refute your result before you run it, and label the mode of what you get. A reported failure is more useful to me than a reported success.
>
> Thanks,
> [name]

## Stage order

1. **Stage 1.** Send the claim and the five refuters. Record the reader's verdict *before* showing any theory.
2. **Stage 2, opt-in only.** If the reader is curious, send the derivation and links. Their later agreement is never pooled as corroboration (Fig 2 :40).
3. **Stage 3.** Offer the formula as a method, with the instruction that each user names a ground and writes a refuter first.

Nothing is sent by this agent. The contact venues in `03_public_contact_points.csv` are public and opt-in; hosting and sending are yours to do.
