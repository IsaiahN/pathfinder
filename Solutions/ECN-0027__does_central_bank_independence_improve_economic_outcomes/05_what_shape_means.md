## 1. A shape is a constraint, not a filler

The canonical figures README states the limit: the framework returns *"what a missing thing would have to look like (a shape, not a name)."* Fig 9 :32: *"you get a kind of thing, never the answer itself."* Fig 13 :134: *"Unreachable is a verdict here, not a silence"* and the framework *"cannot say which primitive is missing, because naming it would mean already holding it."*

From *The Library of Babel*: *"you can cut your search short if the set of books returned is small and descriptive enough that it provides a general shape of the answer that satisfies your question."*

For ECN-0027, the **shape** is: the either-or is a union compound with two anchors; independence is a non-updating anchor in the outcomes domain; the legitimacy anchor is constitutively human; the reconciliation is a design seam that keeps the channel to the ground reachable. Those are constraints on any admissible answer. The **name** that would fill the slots (a particular inflation number, a particular institutional design, a particular weighting of the two harms) is not supplied by the framework.

## 2. How the shape is obtained here

- *Library of Babel*: use the vocabulary you already have that is closest to the thing, look for recurring new terms that overlap with it, recurse. *"With each new term gained, you narrow the space and deepen your vocabulary."* Here the first narrowing was from "outcomes vs accountability" to "two anchors, two loops."
- *Library of Babel* on bundles: *"Perhaps your problem was a bundle or conflation of several problems masquerading as one? You will need to search all of those problems individually."* This is the essay-level corroboration of Fig 9 :54.
- Fig 6 :30: *"The description travels. Fails by being sharpened into terms only we can read. Cast it in effect terms."*
- *Decoding Tacit Knowledge*: *"When contact isn't possible, the best you can send is a plain description of what you'd expect to see, because anyone can check that against the world."* This is why the outreach email describes effects (what would refute the claim) rather than sending the architecture.
- *How Intelligence Actually Works* on stopping: the loop *"stops when nothing you have registers a difference from it"* and *"indiscernibility is not identity."* A converged shape is not the answer; it is a checkable limit.

## 3. What licenses a name in this question

Only two things, both outside the shape:

1. **The ground settles it.** Formula step 5: *"the ground settles it. Verified only by what pays. A gate is not the ground."* For ECN-0027, an outcome name (does inflation actually fall?) needs measured data; an accountability name (is legitimacy actually worsened?) needs the legitimacy domain's own human anchor.
2. **A provenanced import from a frame whose closure differs** (Fig 6 :23, Fig 8 :50-51), with a source gate and a shadow test. The world literature (Alesina-Summers, Dincer et al., Fernández-Albertos, IMF) is imported this way: recorded with author, venue, year, quote and overlap, never allowed to overrule the figure verdict.

*The Hard Problem is About Source* gives the mechanism for why a reading is not a source: *"If several paths create the same behavior, then behavior is a projection with lossy information."* Applied here: a low-inflation reading does not tell you whether the source was independence, a credible mandate, or luck. Fig 2 :29 supplies the same rule in framework terms: *"An anchor is whatever settles the question and does not change its answer because of what any frame thinks."*

## 4. Why the two effects cannot be named on one scale

*How Intelligence Actually Works* distinguishes a **distinction horizon** (one observer cannot tell two generation methods apart) from a **union horizon** (no available instrument among all observers can). *"Being below the union horizon is never a settled verdict, only a report that nobody has reached it yet."* For ECN-0027 the two effects are not below a union horizon; they are above it on different instruments. That is why the honest answer is two verdicts, not one average.

Fig 12 :125-126: *"A composition costs in two currencies, and they do not add."* and *"Bits price the description. Actions price finding out whether it holds."* The economic and the legitimacy measurements are in different units; a single score would be a category error, not a compromise.

## 5. Q: Does "shape, not a name" forbid naming "independence" as the mechanism?

**No, and this is a correction to a possible over-reading.** The README's phrase governs a *missing* primitive, one not held in Γ. "Independence" here is an ordinary institutional term already in the vocabulary of the field, not a new primitive the framework must invent. Naming it is a binding from the economics/political-science frame onto a shape the figures already license (non-updating anchor). What remains forbidden is claiming that the framework *derives* that a specific institutional design is the unique answer, or that the binding moves the wall (Fig 6: *"IMPORT adds an atom"*; this one adds none).

So ECN-0027's answer may name the mechanism, but must record it as a provenanced binding, not a derivation. Its verdicts stay `specified`.

## 6. Prestige is not action

From *The Recursive Self-Improvement Trap*: *"Prestige can fund an action. It can give an action a budget to be tried. But it cannot settle an action. Only the ground can settle it. The Ground Maintainer must be blind to prestige."*

A campaign that gathers famous economists to agree that independence is good, or bad, is the named collapse, not corroboration. Fig 2 :39-40: *"agreement about the world is worthless when the agreeing frames share an evidence pool"*; Fig 7 :13: *"agreement compounds, evidence does not."* The legitimate ask gives the shape a budget to be tried against a ground.

Also from that essay: *"The catch is that the output is only as good as the validator. The agent, or the tool, or the frame, or the theory, cannot validate itself. An external validator, or ground, is always needed."*

## 7. My own audit is a simulation, not a ground reading

Same essay: *"LLMs can quickly simulate an approximate answer/output with the formula... However, the truly testable output would need to be derived from the real architecture or working the formula yourself."*

So `01_figure_grounded_audit.md` and this file are simulated derivations, checked for internal consistency against the figures. They are **not** tests of the claim and must be labelled as such. They cannot settle whether independence improves outcomes, and they cannot settle the normative weight of the accountability loss. That is the honest stop, and it is why the mode is `specified` / `general` throughout and never `grounded`.
