# The Tether's answer on central-bank independence

**Mode: `general` on the union-compound finding and design shape, `specified` on the per-domain claims. Not `grounded`.** This is a shape and a route, not a result. Simulated derivation checked against the figures (`05_what_shape_means.md` §7), not a ground reading.

## 1. The residual it answers

The question offers an either-or: independence improves economic outcomes, or it weakens democratic accountability. In effect terms these are two different effects with two different anchors. R is the gap between the either-or framing and the two loops actually present. Fig 9 :4: *"are not one hard question but several well-formed ones. Split it rather than search."*

## 2. The shape

1. **It is a union compound, not one question.** The two effects share the institution but not the anchor, so a lever that clears one leaves the other standing. Fig 9 :54: *"when the split lands on a union rather than a partition, unbundle before you search again."* Fig 1 :19: *"The residual is indexed by object slot, not aggregated."* The verdicts are kept per domain and never averaged.

2. **D1, outcomes: independence is a non-updating anchor, and improvement is a ground question.** Fig 2 :33: *"The anchor must not update."* Fig 2 :42: *"Buy independence in proportion to the cost of being wrong."* This licenses the *shape* (insulation from transient political pressure), not the causal result. Whether inflation or output actually improve must be measured; Formula step 5: *"the ground settles it. Verified only by what pays. A gate is not the ground."* The world literature finds an inflation association and little evidence for growth or unemployment, so even the empirical half is narrower than the question's word "outcomes."

3. **D2, accountability: the legitimate anchor is constitutively human, so insulation has a structural correction cost.** Fig 2 :37: *"Human judgment is the right anchor for is this a good answer for a person, because the question is about people."* An anchor that does not update on the political frame cannot be corrected by that frame. Fig 10 :93: *"A ground with a reputation to protect is a ground someone benefits from, and its keeper is thereby disqualified."* The structural effect is figure-licensed; the normative valuation is the domain's own human judgment, not a figure verdict.

4. **D3, the seam: keep the anchor non-updating and the channel to the ground reachable.** Fig 10 :24: *"the ground does not decay, the channel does"*; Fig 10 :29: *"keep the anchor reachable"*; Fig 10 :19: *"install what can be violated."* In this domain that points to clear mandates, transparent reporting, reviewable appointments and removal for cause, conventions that can be checked rather than declared meanings. Fig 11 :40: *"An improvement that does not change contact changes nothing, however much it improves."*

5. **Formal independence is not actual independence.** Fig 12 :117: *"Same atoms, different bond, different substance. The difference is invisible in the formula and decisive in the behaviour."* A statute that declares independence and an institution that actually operates without political contact share vocabulary but not substance. The low-overlap governance and commitment-device sources corroborate this.

## 3. What it explicitly does not say

- Not that independence always lowers inflation, or that all economic outcomes improve.
- Not that accountability necessarily worsens on balance. The structural cost is real; its weight is a human judgment.
- Not that there is a single optimal degree of independence. Fig 2 :42 prices independence but does not compute an optimum.
- Not that economists' consensus settles either half. Fig 7 :13: *"agreement compounds, evidence does not."*
- Not `grounded`. No measurement in this document settles the balance.

## 4. What would settle it - the ground

A ground reading needs instruments for both halves, kept separate:

- **D1 instrument:** comparable cross-country or sub-national data on inflation and output under matched mandates, with a source-discriminating design so that "independence" is not read off a single index. From *The Hard Problem is About Source*: an instrument that reads green *"cannot comment on the source."* Here a low-inflation reading does not identify its cause.
- **D2 instrument:** a measure of actual democratic correction (oversight hearings, removal events, mandate changes, public reporting quality) ranged against the electorate's own judgment, not against an economist's score.
- **Falsifiers that can fire:** no systematic outcome difference under comparable mandates; transparency fully restores correction without making the anchor update on transient politics; the institution demonstrably updates on political pressure (then it is not an anchor); a single anchor settles both effects on one metric (then the union finding fails).

## 5. The honest stop

**Unreached, not unreachable.** Fig 13 :134: *"Unreachable is a verdict here, not a silence"*; Fig 8 :39: *"So a search that finds nothing is never a proof of absence."* The instruments exist for both halves, but no single instrument settles both. The round trip from the figure set to this answer and back loses constraints, and the loss is recorded in `11_audit.md` rather than assumed to be zero (Fig 4 :30: *"So take the round trip and measure the gap. Without it you are navigating by dead reckoning: confident about a position nobody checked."*).

## 6. One-paragraph version for outreach

> The Tether does not answer "yes" or "no" to the independence trade-off, because it finds that the question bundles two effects with different anchors. In the outcomes domain, independence is an attempt to install an anchor that does not update on short-term political pressure, which is a structural shape, not a measured causal result. In the accountability domain, the legitimate anchor is constitutively human, so an institution pinned against the political frame cannot be corrected by it; that is a real structural cost, though its moral weight is a human judgment. The two verdicts are kept separate and never averaged. The reconciliation is a design seam, not a compromise: keep the monetary anchor non-updating while keeping the channel to the democratic ground reachable through mandates, transparency and review. A formal independence statute is not the same term as actual operational independence. What the framework offers is the shape any answer must have, five falsifiers written in advance, and an honest stop where the ground, not agreement, must decide.
