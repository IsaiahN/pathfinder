## VERDICT (from the figures)

The question is a union compound. There is no single A-or-B answer and no average. D1: independence is a non-updating anchor shape; whether outcomes improve is a ground question, and the empirical literature narrows it to inflation. D2: the legitimacy anchor is constitutively human, so insulation has a real structural correction cost, while its moral weight is a human judgment. D3: the reconciliation is a design seam that keeps the channel to the ground reachable, with conventions that can be violated rather than meanings merely declared. Formal independence is not actual independence. Everything here is `general` or `specified`, `candidate`, not `grounded`.

- **cost if wrong:** bounded and locatable, but institutional rather than merely academic. If the compound is wrongly averaged, we either discard a real legitimacy loss or discard a real outcome gain, and institutional design follows the error. Fig 2 :42 "Buy independence in proportion to the cost of being wrong." The avoidable part is spending outside readers' independence by sending a verdict instead of a shape; lead with the refuters.
- **undoable:** yes. The two domain verdicts and the seam can be revised without withdrawing the derivation; the five refuters tell a reader exactly where to apply the correction. No primitive has to be withdrawn and no institution has to be redesigned by this document.
- **reversed by:** a ground reading, not agreement. A matched-design measurement showing no outcome difference under comparable mandates would retire D1's ground support; a demonstration that transparency fully restores democratic correction without making the anchor update would retire D2's cost; a single anchor settling both effects would retire the union finding; a demonstration that the institution updates on political pressure would retire the anchor framing itself. Until one fires, the verdict stands as `candidate`.
