## Restating the question as an effect

The sentence asks for one verdict on a compound. Option A (independence improves outcomes) points at a measurement against economic data. Option B (independence weakens accountability) points at a legitimacy relation between citizens and an institution. What an answer would look like, written down and bound as an ordinary term before the run: a single verdict would require one anchor that settles both effects. The derivation below finds two anchors and therefore a union compound, not one hard question. That is the residual this package answers.
