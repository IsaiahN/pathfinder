## Two things in the literature that bear directly on this question

1. **The outcome claim is narrower than the question.** Alesina and Summers find an inflation association and *"little evidence"* for growth or unemployment. So "improve economic outcomes" cannot be treated as one effect. This is a ground finding, not a figure verdict, and it corroborates F2's warning that the shape is not a causal result.
2. **The accountability claim is not a single effect either.** The IMF says independence and accountability are two sides of one coin bridged by transparency; Dincer et al. find transparency helps while regulatory complexity hurts; Fernández-Albertos says central banks are unavoidably political. These do not contradict each other. They are three different instruments pointed at the same compound, which is exactly why the compound must be unbundled before searching (Fig 9 :54).
