## 1. A shape is a constraint, not a filler

The canonical README states the limit: the framework returns *"what a missing thing would have to look like (a shape, not a name)"*. From *The Library of Babel*: *"You can cut your search short if the set of books returned is small and descriptive enough that it provides a general shape of the answer that satisfies your question."*

So a **shape** is the form of an answer you can state before you can name the quantity. It constrains; it does not fill. For this question:

- *"Habitat is an enumerated field of contact per residual"* is a shape.
- *"30 percent of the landscape"* is a number, and it is not the shape.

The bound shape from `01_figure_grounded_audit.md` §0 is: *for a named residual, name the contacts and conditions that must persist, the depth of cascade counted, and the mode under which persistence is claimed.* A percentage does not satisfy it.

## 2. How the shape is obtained

- *Library of Babel*, on bootstrap vocabulary: use the known words closest to the thing, look for recurring new terms that overlap with them, and recurse. *"With each new term gained, you narrow the space and deepen your vocabulary."*
- *Library of Babel*, on a bundled question: *"Perhaps your problem was a bundle or conflation of several problems masquerading as one?"* This is exactly ECO-0051: population persistence and interaction persistence are bundled.
- *Library of Babel*, on the wrong question: *"If you don't have the answer to a question, it's because you are lacking certain things in the library (instruments, information) that would help, or perhaps you are asking the wrong question altogether."* "How much?" may be the wrong question.
- *Decoding Tacit Knowledge*: *"When contact isn't possible, the best you can send is a plain description of what you'd expect to see, because anyone can check that against the world."* An effect description of habitat (what you would count, how far the cascade goes) travels; a private habitat metric does not.
- *Library of Babel*, on stopping: *"You will know that you are done because the finish line will also be the starting place."* Here the finish is the re-described question, not a number.

## 3. What licenses a number

Only two things, both outside the shape itself:

1. **The ground settles it.** Formula step 5: the ground settles a term, verified only by what pays. Hanski's extinction thresholds are of this kind: ground readings for a named species in a named landscape.
2. **A provenanced import from a frame whose closure differs**, with a source gate and a shadow test. Ecology's area thresholds are imports from the ecology frame into the shape, and they must be labelled as such.

*They're Scanning Books* sharpens the second path: *"The real import is not a new term in the existing space. It is a new dimension of the space."* It also warns: *"A minimum viable set of six terms is a human's careful guess at reality's compression axes; another sensorium's minimum set need not be those six."* and *"Declaring the primitives does not make them neutral. It makes the choice visible."* Calling habitat "amount" is a declared primitive, not a neutral measurement.

## 4. Correction to a tempting reading: habitat is not the ecological niche

This is the most important correction for this question, and it comes from the canonical article's own section **"Three mappings offered and declined"**:

> *"The habitat as an ecological niche. A niche is defined by an organism's relation to its environment. A habitat here is defined by contact with a residual, and the residual moves so the same actors constitute a different habitat when the question changes. The resemblance is real and the definitions do not align, which is why the figure says enumerated, never composed rather than borrowing the ecological term outright."*

My first pass was ready to map Fig 11 onto niche theory and then import the niche literature to fill the answer. **That is retracted.** The mapping is explicitly declined. The framework's habitat is per residual and moves when the residual changes; an ecological niche is per organism-environment relation. They overlap and do not align. Niche theory is a useful world source for other purposes, but it is not licensed to supply this shape.

The same article adds a caveat worth keeping: *"Because it is a metatheory, it cannot be validated, nor can it validate itself."*

## 5. The source problem: an area-only metric cannot comment on what produced persistence

*The Hard Problem is About Source* gives the mechanism. An instrument reads green and *"cannot comment on the source"*, because one green light, or yellow plus blue, or RGB, or CMYK can give the same reading. *"If several paths create the same behavior, then behavior is a projection with lossy information (it cannot define consciousness in full)."* The instrument that cannot discriminate is at fault: *"An instrument that can't distinguish these cases demonstrates its own inadequacy, it cannot claim anything substantial about what is being measured or what causes it."*

The corresponding error here is reading a hectares number and naming its source (persistence) without an instrument that distinguishes area from arrangement, quality, cascade depth and regime. Several arrangements give the same area and different persistence. What is owed is a better discriminator, not a name for the reading.

*How Intelligence Actually Works* makes the ecological version explicit: *"When altering a habitat by unintentionally introducing a predatory threat into the ecosystem, or disturbing the balance by adding or removing vital resource points, you can inadvertently create major unpredictable consequences downstream."* And its standing limit: *"indiscernibility is not identity."*

## 6. Capability, valence and group habitat

- *How a Society of Agents Would Work* restates the habitat rule: *"Figure 11 says 'capability is a property of agent-and-habitat, never of the agent alone.'"* and notes *"Members of a group are each other's habitat and each other's competitors."*
- The same essay keeps valence per residual: *"Figure 11: 'The same actor is a benefactor to one residual and a malefactor to another.'"* (The figure's own spelling is *malfactor*; the figure file governs.)
- These are restatements by the framework author, not independent witnesses. They are recorded with high overlap in `10_source_ledger.csv`.

## 7. Prestige is not action

From *The Recursive Self-Improvement Trap*: *"Prestige must never be allowed to validate an action directly."* and *"Prestige can fund an action. It can give an action a budget to be tried. But it cannot settle an action. Only the ground can settle it."*

A list of famous ecologists who agree with a habitat target is the named collapse, not corroboration. The legitimate ask gives the shape claim a budget to be tried and lets the ground: occupancy data, interaction observations, a threshold experiment: settle it.

## 8. My own audit is a simulation, not a ground reading

Same essay: *"the output is only as good as the validator. The agent, or the tool, or the frame, or the theory, cannot validate itself."* And: *"LLMs can quickly simulate an approximate answer/output with the formula found in the architecture post."* The canonical article adds that such outputs *"could never be verified if the solutions presented were not contrived to fit the formula."*

Therefore `01_figure_grounded_audit.md` is a simulated derivation checked for internal consistency against the figures. It is **not** a test of the shape claim and cannot settle anything it reports on. The world sources I opened are real ground readings, but they corroborate a nearby quantity; they do not convert this derivation into a `grounded` result.

## 9. The shape for ECO-0051, stated once

> A habitat answer is a per-residual enumeration of contacts and conditions, followed outward until the cascade stops mattering, with population and interaction verdicts kept separate, with the regime named (unmaintained selection or maintained permanent work), and with isolation treated as substitution. The number, where one is wanted, is a ground-settled threshold for a named species in a named landscape, imported with provenance. The framework itself returns no universal percentage.
