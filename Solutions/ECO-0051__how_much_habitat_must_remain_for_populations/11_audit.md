## Independent domain census

I redid the census from the residual line alone, before reading the primary's conclusions, using the WATSON order: R by arity, symmetry and scale; one line per domain with actors / currency / anchor; partition or union; order by dependency.

- **R (arity / symmetry / scale).** The gap between a habitat amount and observed persistence. Arity: the question says "populations" (plural) and "ecological interactions" (plural) and "over the long term", so at least two residuals and two regimes. This is a **union compound** under Fig 9 :55. Symmetry: area is presented as symmetric and scalar, but a per-residual valence makes it asymmetric; that is a hypothesis to test, not a premise. Scale: patch to landscape to region, with a delayed time axis (extinction debt in the Hanski source) and contact cascading across scale.
- **D1, unmaintained population persistence.** Actors: individuals, populations, patches, matrix, dispersers, conditions of light, water, temperature, season, terrain. Currency: occupancy and extinction threshold. Anchor: censused presence or absence. No maintainer, so selection decides.
- **D2, interaction persistence.** Actors: species pairs and interaction networks: pollination, predation, competition, dependency. Currency: interaction occurrence and strength. Anchor: observed interaction, which can fail before the species.
- **D3, configuration.** Actors: patches, corridors, barriers, dispersers. Currency: connectivity and arrangement. Anchor: colonisation and extinction data. Cross-cuts D1 and D2.
- **D4, maintained habitat.** Actors: maintainer seat, introduced actors, restoration. Currency: actions, not area. Anchor: the ground still decides. Regime.
- **D5, isolation.** Actors: moved residual, new habitat, inherited actors. Currency: reproduction of required contacts. Anchor: whether the goal fails. Regime.

All five have nameable anchors, so all five are domains. The overlap between D1 and D2 makes this a union, not a partition.

Order by dependency: enumerate contact for each residual, acquire terms for what the landscape affords, state what counts as persistence for that residual, then plan, then carry. Area estimation sits above the break and is already instrumented; the objective link is not (Fig 3 :20, :27).

## Round trip gap R_T

The Symbols table defines R_T as the gap between what was sent up and what came back down, `gap(x, (T_E ∘ T_A)(x))`, measuring what the coarser description cannot hold. Fig 4 :30 requires it to be measured rather than assumed. I ran the round trip on the condensation this folder actually uses.

1. **Sent up (x):** the verbatim Fig 11 rules (enumerate, valence relative, cascade, asymmetry, substitution, habitat not ground), plus Fig 1 :19 and Fig 12 :117.
2. **Abstraction (T_A):** the shorthand in `07_the_tether_answer.md`: habitat is a per-residual contact field; area is one predicate; arrangement matters; regimes differ; no universal number.
3. **Concretisation (T_E):** the question's concrete demand, a single percentage of habitat that guarantees persistence of all populations and interactions over the long term.

**R_T is nonzero.** Four things the shorthand holds do not survive the trip back down:

- **Per-slot indexing.** The concretisation wants one number for all slots; Fig 1 :19 and Fig 11 :49 require separate verdicts.
- **Cascade depth.** A percentage is a first-order area measure; the DDT case requires third-order enumeration.
- **Regime.** The shorthand distinguishes unmaintained selection from maintained permanent work; the percentage collapses both into one stock.
- **Substitution.** The concretisation treats isolation as removal; Fig 11 :66 treats it as substitution with inherited actors.

Conversely, one thing survives cleanly: habitat must be enumerated for a named residual before any number is chosen. That is the residue worth sending. R_T was **not** measured numerically, because no instrument in this folder measures the information lost by the shorthand; it is recorded qualitatively as the enumerated set of dropped constraints. Treating "the formula is the condensed version of the figures" as identity would set R_T to zero and would be the dead-reckoning error Fig 4 :30 names. The honest form is a lossy condensation with a recorded gap, which is what `09_watson_answer.md` states.
