## The residual in one line

The question asks for a number ("how much habitat"), but it bundles at least two residuals (population persistence and interaction persistence) across at least two modes (unmaintained and maintained). Figure 11 governs the *shape* of any answer here and gives no number. The defensible result is a shape: enumerate the habitat of each residual, keep the residuals separate, follow contact out past direct neighbours, and distinguish a selection regime from a managed one. The numeric part is `gap` at the figure level, and is settled, where it is settled at all, per species and per landscape at the ground.

## What is deliberately not here

- No personal emails of named individuals.
- No ranking of people by how likely they are to believe the claim.
- No bulk-send plan.
- No universal habitat percentage presented as a Tether result.

Three reasons, in the figures' own words:

1. **A room does not upgrade the label.** A list of agreeable recipients raises the vocabulary and does not add an anchor (Fig 2 :29, :35).
2. **Correlation disqualifies a verdict.** Agreement selected for receptiveness manufactures the exact correlation that makes agreement worthless (Fig 2 :40).
3. **Asking for trust before checking spends the only thing an outside frame contributes.** "A verifier that can reconstruct the claim cannot verify it" (Fig 10 :42); Figure 10's outside seat must not hold the interiors.

Legally: scraping personal emails for unsolicited bulk outreach breaches GDPR and CAN-SPAM style rules in many jurisdictions. Public opt-in venues avoid that failure as well as the structural one.
