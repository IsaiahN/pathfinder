# Equal area is not equal habitat: isomers, order and analogues

**Mode: `specified`.** This is a simulated derivation checked against the figures, not a ground reading (see `05_what_shape_means.md` §8).

## RESIDUAL

The question asks "how much habitat must remain", which assumes that area is the substance and that one amount gives one answer. Two things are owed: (1) whether two landscapes with the same area can be different kinds of thing, and (2) what order the search should run in if the union is unbundled.

## DERIVATION

### Q1 - Are two landscapes with equal area isomers?

- Fig 12 :5: *"A set of ingredients is not one. The bond is the rest of the information, and it is the half nothing was recording."*
- Fig 12 :94: *"The arrangement is the substance."*
- Fig 12 :95-96: *"Two entries with an identical ingredient set are not variants of one thing. They are different things whose notation cannot tell them apart, and the difference is carried entirely by the bond."*
- Fig 12 :117: *"isomer. Same atoms, different bond, different substance. The difference is invisible in the formula and decisive in the behaviour."*

A hectares figure records the ingredients of a landscape and not its arrangement. Two landscapes with the same area, the same species list and the same interaction list can therefore be **isomers** under the figure's definition: same atoms, different bond, different substance. The difference is invisible in the "how much" formula and decisive in persistence. The bond here is the spatial and trophic arrangement, including cascade depth, which is exactly what Fig 11 :52 orders to be enumerated.

This is not an imported analogy from chemistry. Fig 12 makes it a statement about composition: a flat formula records ingredients and not arrangement, and nothing downstream can tell the cases apart and nothing signals that anything was lost. **Triangulated** for the structural claim (Fig 12 plus Fig 11's cascade rule). The empirical side (Hanski's example where the same total forest cover in a scattered pattern leaves specialists below threshold while a concentrated 30 percent patch would support them) is a world corroboration, not the source of the claim.

### Q2 - What order is the unbundled search?

- Fig 3 :20: *"The chain is ordered by dependency, not by importance."*
- Fig 3 :19: *"Optimising a link that already works cannot rescue one that does not."*
- Fig 9 :27-28: *"describe it before you go looking, or any frame you pick will seem to fit"*

Order: enumerate habitat for each residual (perception), acquire terms for what the landscape affords (vocabulary), state what counts as persistence for that residual (objective), then plan, then carry. Area measurement is above the break and already has instruments; the objective link does not. Effort spent above the break arrives at a step that cannot use it. So the search order is **not** "find the universal percentage, then check populations and interactions". It is "state the persistence criterion per slot, then enumerate the contacts, then estimate a threshold if the ground permits".

### Q3 - Is the threshold a stock or a comparison?

- Fig 12 :86-87: *"a threshold relation between two quantities"*
- Operators table :51: *"a goal is a comparison, which is why a vocabulary without it cannot state one"*
- Fig 12 :112: *"A plan is a sequence. A goal is a comparison. Progress is a subtraction."*

The threshold question is a comparison ⋛ between the amount and what the residual requires. It is not a stock that can be accumulated independently of the requirement. A vocabulary that records only area can say how much there is and cannot state the goal. This is the formal reason "how much" is under-specified until the persistence criterion is bound as an ordinary term (the "Direction" rule in `THE_FORMULA.md`).

### Q4 - Where do ecological interactions sit in the substrate?

- Fig 13 :24: *"that entities relate, and influence travels"*
- Fig 13 :74: *"Everything else is a composition across the two kinds, never within one."*
- Fig 13 :41: *"Each is indivisible: no arrangement of the other five yields it."*

Interactions (pollination, predation, competition, dependency; Fig 11 :47) are what the network structure decides. Network is one of the six, not a seventh primitive, and it does not compose directly with another structure. So interaction persistence is a legitimate structure-governed question, but it is not reducible to the area predicate and it is not a new member of the set. **Single-source** for the substrate placement (Fig 13), corroborated at the world level by Lurgi 2019 on interaction networks.

### A model analogue, kept in its own frame

The mathematics of percolation gives a low-overlap analogue: a threshold exists as a critical occupation probability, but it is defined only relative to an occupation rule and a lattice, and below it no long-range connection exists. This corroborates the claim that "how much" has an answer only inside a specified frame. It is a model, not the ground; the figure set does not depend on it and it is not allowed to decide the ecological verdict.

## CONCLUSION

Two landscapes of equal area are not, by that fact, variants of one thing. Under Fig 12 they can be isomers: same atoms, different arrangement, different persistence, with the difference invisible to the "how much" formula. The order of the work is dependency order: criterion before measurement, contact enumeration before threshold. The threshold itself is a comparison, not a stock. Interactions are governed by the network structure, not by area and not by a new primitive.

## FALSIFIER, WRITTEN BEFORE THE RUN

The isomer claim fails if, across independent taxa and independent interaction types, two landscapes with identical habitat area and identical species and interaction lists show identical persistence for both residuals regardless of configuration and cascade depth. The order claim fails if stating the persistence criterion after the area estimate performs as well across cases. The comparison claim fails if a threshold can be fixed without reference to what the residual requires. Any of these fires, and `01_figure_grounded_audit.md` F10 and F8 are withdrawn.
