**Status:** done

## The question in effect terms

"These proposed settings" are the candidate sites for abiogenesis named in question 18 of the same section: deep-sea hydrothermal systems, shallow ponds, volcanic land, ice, or another setting. Question 17 asks which settings lasted long enough for chemistry to build up, and question 19 asks whether life could have begun in more than one environment with only one lineage surviving. The residual R is a **discrimination failure**: several candidate settings are each internally coherent, but the rock record has not returned an observation that all parties accept as settling which one was actual. The question asks what geological evidence could do that settling.

An answer would look like this, written down as an ordinary term so it can accrue a residual (Formula, "Direction"): a **discriminating observation** is a geological trace that (a) at least one proposed setting predicts and the others cannot produce, (b) is read off the habitat rather than invented, (c) is already registering at some instrument resolution, and (d) survives diagnosis and metamorphism so that it still carries the setting's signature. That is a shape, not a name: it constrains what would count as evidence without declaring which setting wins.

## What is deliberately not here

- No personal emails of named individuals.
- No ranking of people by how likely they are to agree with a setting.
- No bulk-send plan.

Three reasons, in the figures' own words:

1. **A room does not upgrade the label.** Fig 7 :35: *"A room's agreement is variation, not a verdict. Selection between rooms is where the ground votes."* A list of people selected for agreeing with one setting produces a convention, not corroboration.
2. **Correlation disqualifies a verdict.** Fig 2 :40: *"agreement about the world is worthless when the agreeing frames share an evidence pool"*; Fig 2 :42: *"Buy independence in proportion to the cost of being wrong."* Selecting recipients for receptiveness manufactures the disqualifying correlation.
3. **Asking for trust before checking spends the independence that makes a check worth having.** Fig 10 :42: *"A verifier that can reconstruct the claim cannot verify it."* Send the discriminator and its refuter, not the architecture.

Legally: scraping personal emails for unsolicited bulk outreach breaches GDPR and CAN-SPAM style rules in many jurisdictions and gets the sending domain blacklisted. That is a practical failure on top of the structural one.

## Boundary and honest status

This folder is a **simulated derivation checked for internal consistency against the figures, not a ground reading.** The Recursive Self-Improvement Trap says LLM output is *"an approximate answer/output"* and that *"the truly testable output would need to be derived from the real architecture or working the formula yourself."* No rock was measured, no thin section was examined, and no field season was run. The verdict reported here cannot settle which early-Earth setting hosted life's origin; it states the shape of the evidence that would, and records what would reverse it.

Sources used: the local verbatim figure extract `FRAMEWORK/reference/03-FIGURES_TEXT.md`, the canonical figures README, `THE_FORMULA.md`, three framework essays, and six open geology and geochemistry sources. All are listed with accessed dates and quotes in `10_source_ledger.csv`. Nothing outside the solution folder was modified.
