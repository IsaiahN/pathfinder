## VERDICT (from the figures)

The shape survives, triangulated across the anchor rule (Fig 2), the habitat rule (Fig 11) and the instrument rule (Fig 6): a discriminator must be an anchor whose reading does not move with the interpreter, described before the frame is chosen, enumerated from the habitat, already registering at some instrument resolution, source-discriminating, durable through alteration, and sought per unbundled condition of a union. The name of the winning setting does not survive from the figures; it is `gap` and requires a ground reading. Bulk δ13C values and spheroidal textures are not discriminators on their own. A setting-diagnostic facies such as geyserite is a `specified` example of the right logical form, not a settlement of question 20. This is a `specified`, ungrounded, candidate result from a simulated derivation.

- **cost if wrong:** bounded and locatable. The packet is mode-labelled, states refuters before the conclusion and records the lineage, so an error is findable. The real cost is spending the independence of outside readers by sending a preferred setting instead of a shape and a refuter, which Fig 2 :42 prices ("Buy independence in proportion to the cost of being wrong."). That cost is avoidable by leading with the discriminator and its refuter.
- **undoable:** yes. The specific claim that a bulk reading names a setting is removable without touching the shape derivation, and `07_the_tether_answer.md` and `08_expert_verification_packet.md` already remove it. A sent claim can be superseded by the corrected packet; no geological primitive has to be withdrawn.
- **reversed by:** a ground reading, not agreement. An instrument that resolves source for a specific trace in a low-grade succession would move the shape toward `grounded`; a setting-diagnostic facies demonstrably tied to the earliest biosignatures would name a setting; a clean partition of the four labels would break the union verdict; a measurement in which a discriminator's reading moves with the interpreter would break the anchor rule; a derivation of one of the settings' conditions from a different frame with a shadow test would be an import, priced against independence (Fig 6 :24). Until one of these fires, the verdict stands as `candidate`.
