# Expert verification packet: geological evidence that could distinguish the proposed settings

**What this is:** a claim in a form a geologist or geochemist can check. Mode, derivation and refuters are stated before the conclusion, so a reader knows what to look for and what would settle it.

**Mode: `specified` on the shape, `general` on the anchor and instrument rules, `gap` on the name of the winning setting. Not `grounded`.** No measurement was taken in this derivation.

**Status:** simulated derivation checked for internal consistency against the figures. It cannot settle the question it reports on.

## 1. The claim, in one paragraph

The question of which early-Earth setting hosted life's origin is a discrimination failure, not a missing fact. A geological observation can discriminate only if it is an anchor whose reading does not move with the interpreter's preferred setting; if at least one setting predicts it and the others cannot produce the same value; if it is enumerated from the habitat and read off the world; if an existing instrument already returns a reading that fails to resolve; and if it survives diagnosis and metamorphism. Bulk carbon-isotope values and spheroidal textures fail the source test on their own. A facies that one setting produces and the others cannot, such as geyserite and sinter for terrestrial hot springs, has the right logical form. The figures return this shape; they do not name the winner, which is a `gap` requiring the ground.

## 2. Three checkable sub-claims

### C1 - framework-internal; checkable by reading Figures 2 and 6

- **Statement:** a discriminator must be an anchor, and no instrument can be specified for a trace that has never registered anywhere at any resolution.
- **Source, verbatim:** Fig 2 :29 *"An anchor is whatever settles the question and does not change its answer because of what any frame thinks. A measurement, an experiment, a counter, a market, a patient outcome."* Fig 6 :40 *"What is unavailable is a sensor for something that has never registered anywhere, at any resolution. That is the whole of it."* Fig 6 :32 *"An instrument is not built from a description. It is improved from a worse instrument already returning something."*
- **What to check:** does the proposed discriminator's reading depend on the rock rather than the interpreter? Can the reader name a real instrument, at any resolution, already returning a failing reading for the pair of settings in question?
- **Refuter:** a discriminator whose reading changes with the preferred setting; or no nameable existing instrument for a pair, in which case that pair is `unreachable` at this budget, not answered.

### C2 - geology-facing; checkable against the published rock record

- **Statement:** a bulk reading does not determine its source. Low δ13C values and spheroidal microstructures can arise biologically, abiotically, or through later alteration, so neither is by itself a discriminator between settings.
- **Sources, verbatim:** Zeichner, Fischer, Lotem, Moore, Goldford and Eiler (American Journal of Science, 2024): *"it can be challenging to distinguish whether these low values reflect biotic formation, abiotic reactions, or post-depositional processes."* Knoll, Bergmann and Strauss (Phil Trans R Soc B, 2016): *"hydrothermal fluids can mobilize organic matter and redistribute it into spheroidal microstructures that resemble putative Archean microfossils."* Figure-level reason: Fig 12 :117 *"isomer. Same atoms, different bond, different substance. The difference is invisible in the formula and decisive in the behaviour."*
- **What to check:** is the quoted result represented fairly? Does the reader know of a setting-diagnostic trace that survives the same objections? Djokic et al. (Nature Communications, 2017) report: *"neither marine nor lacustrine settings can account for the preservation of geyserite, sinter terracettes or the mineralized remnants of hot spring pools."*
- **Refuter:** a trace produced at the same value by all candidate settings, or a demonstration that low-grade preservation rules out the alteration pathway for a specific sample.

### C3 - framework-internal derivation with a field consequence

- **Statement:** the proposed settings are a union of overlapping conditions, not a partition, and duration is a dependency link that comes before chemistry is scored.
- **Source, verbatim:** Fig 9 :54 *"when the split lands on a union rather than a partition, unbundle before you search again."* Fig 3 :20 *"The chain is ordered by dependency, not by importance. Each link consumes what the one above produces, so effort spent above the break arrives at a step that cannot use it."*
- **What to check:** do the settings in fact overlap in the conditions they supply (mineral surfaces, gradients, water, duration) so that a single lever would leave compound cases standing? Westall et al. (Astrobiology, 2018) tabulate the environments and their properties; Barge and Price (Nature Geoscience, 2022) add shallow-sea alkaline vents, showing the census is open.
- **Refuter:** a trace that separates the four labels as a clean partition with no compound case, or a measurement showing a setting's chain is intact where this shape assumes a break. If found, C3 is withdrawn.

## 3. What is explicitly not claimed

- Not a theory of everything; no final room. No claim that this shape is the only possible one.
- Not that any setting is the answer. The winning setting is `gap` and needs the ground.
- Not that the four labels in question 18 are exhaustive; Barge and Price (2022) and Westall et al. (2018) list more.
- Not that consensus among specialists, or prestige, settles a setting (Fig 7 :35, Fig 2 :40).
- Not `grounded`. No measurement, thin section or field observation was made for this packet.
- Not that Fig 12's isomer rule derives geology from chemistry. It is an analogue with a stated falsifier, not a derivation of rock behaviour.

## 5. What would settle it

A ground reading, not agreement. For the shape claim: an instrument that resolves source for a specific trace in a specific low-grade succession. For the name of the setting: a measurement in which a setting-diagnostic facies or isotope system is demonstrably tied to the earliest biosignatures and cannot be produced by the alternatives. For the union reading: a clean partition, or a compound case whose trace is itself describable. The strongest current candidate in the literature is the hot-spring facies work of Djokic et al. (2017), but it addresses preservation at 3.48 Ga, not the first origin, and is therefore `specified` corroboration only.

## 6. What an expert can check in twenty minutes

1. Read Fig 2 :29, Fig 6 :32 and :40, Fig 9 :54, Fig 12 :117 (four short passages).
2. Check C2 against their own knowledge: can low δ13C or a spheroid, alone, name a setting? If yes, show the discriminating instrument.
3. Name one existing instrument that returns a reading failing to resolve a chosen pair of settings, or say why none exists for that pair.
4. Send the refutation. A failure is more useful than an endorsement.
