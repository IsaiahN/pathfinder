## 1. Independent domain census

I redid the census from the residual line before reading the primary's conclusions. The residual as given: several candidate early-Earth settings are internally coherent, make different predictions about the rock record, and no observation has been accepted as settling which was actual. What observation would?

- **R (arity / symmetry / scale).** Arity: a set of four named settings plus "another"; discrimination, not a two-way choice. Symmetry: asymmetric; a trace necessary for one setting need not be produced by another, and the threshold form of the comparison is not a symmetric join. Scale: mineral and isotopic, through facies and stratigraphy, to duration. Question type: **union compound**, because the settings share conditions, so a case in two conditions is a categorically different object, not a harder case of one (Fig 9 :54).
- **Domain 1, the settings as frames.** Actors: water, temperature, pH, ionic strength, gradients, UV, mineral surfaces, time. Currency: prebiotic pathways hosted. Anchor: none nameable. A frame can be bent to fit a contrary reading, so it cannot settle one. Not yet a domain by the anchor test.
- **Domain 2, the rock record.** Actors: isotopes, mineral facies, morphological traces, stratigraphic contacts. Currency: setting-diagnostic readings. Anchor: the measurement, if it does not move when pushed. This is the only domain with an anchor.
- **Domain 3, verification and contact.** Actors: geologists and reviewers. Currency: agreement and residual. Anchor: the ground, never agreement. In this simulated run its independence is zero.

Order by dependency: describe R; enumerate habitats; check chain integrity and duration; ask which instrument already returns a failing reading; then name a discriminator; record what reverses it. I reached this order from Fig 3's chain and Fig 6's instrument rule, whereas the primary reached it from Fig 9's describe-first rule and Fig 11's habitat rule. Different starting figures, same domain boundaries: low overlap between routes, so the agreement is weak but not circular within the framework. It is still one evidence pool.

## 4. Round trip gap R_T

The Symbols table defines R_T as `gap(x, (T_E ∘ T_A)(x))`: the gap between what was sent up and what came back down. Fig 4 :30 requires it to be measured rather than assumed. I ran the round trip on the condensation the folder actually uses.

1. **Sent up (x):** the verbatim figure set, chiefly Fig 2 :29 (anchor), Fig 6 :32 and :40 (instrument), Fig 9 :54 (union), Fig 11 :44 (habitat enumeration), Fig 12 :117 (source test), Fig 13 :134 (honest stop).
2. **Abstraction (T_A):** the shorthand carried in `07` and `09`: a discriminator must be an anchor, read off the habitat, already registering, source-discriminating, and sought per unbundled condition; the winning setting is `gap`.
3. **Concretisation (T_E):** a concrete field claim, for example: geyserite and sinter exclude marine and lacustrine settings (Djokic et al. 2017), so a hot-spring setting is indicated for that formation.

**R_T is nonzero.** Three things in the shorthand do not survive the trip back down:

- the **source test**: the shorthand says "source-discriminating"; the concrete facies claim must still show that hydrothermal or diagenetic processes cannot mimic geyserite in that specific rock, which is a measurement, not a definition;
- the **union condition**: the shorthand says "per unbundled condition"; the concrete claim names a single setting, and a compound site would require a second discriminator;
- the **setting-versus-preservation distinction**: the shorthand speaks of a setting; the field result speaks of the preservation of a facies in a formation 3.48 Ga old, which need not be the setting of the first origin.

Conversely, one thing survives cleanly: a discriminator must not move when the interpreter pushes on it, and a bulk reading is not a source. That is the residue worth sending. R_T was not measured numerically, because no ground reading exists; it is recorded qualitatively as the enumerated set of dropped constraints. Treating "the formula is the condensed version of the metalaws" as identity would set R_T to zero and would be the dead-reckoning error Fig 4 :30 names.
