## 1. A shape is a constraint, not a filler

The figures README states the limit: the framework returns *"what a missing thing would have to look like (a shape, not a name)"*. Fig 9 :32 *"you get a kind of thing, never the answer itself"*. Fig 13 :135 *"It cannot say which primitive is missing, because naming it would mean already holding it."*

From *The Library of Babel*, opened this session: *"You can cut your search short if the set of books returned is small and descriptive enough that it provides a general shape of the answer that satisfies your question."*

For BUS-0085, a **shape** is the form of a valid determination that can be stated before the firm's own numbers are in. It constrains; it does not fill. "Per-group residual with a non-updating anchor and an outside check" is a shape. "An 8.4 percent lift in the treated cohort" is a name, and it is a name the framework cannot produce from its own vocabulary. The shape sets what any acceptable answer must satisfy; the firm's ground settles which answer actually does.

## 2. How a shape is obtained

- *Library of Babel*: use the vocabulary you already have that is closest to the thing, look for recurring new terms that overlap or correlate with it, recurse. *"With each new term gained, you narrow the space and deepen your vocabulary."*
- The same essay: *"Perhaps your problem was a bundle or conflation of several problems masquerading as one? You will need to search all of those problems individually."* This is the direct essay analogue of Fig 9 :4-5 and Fig 9 :54-57: the cross-group question is a bundle and must be split.
- Fig 6 :30 *"The description travels. Fails by being sharpened into terms only we can read. Cast it in effect terms."* Fig 9 :30-31 *"your words are borrowed too, so describe the effect rather than the cause."* The effect here is a per-group residual; the cause is whatever the firm believes about the model.
- *Decoding Tacit Knowledge*, opened this session: *"When contact isn't possible, the best you can send is a plain description of what you'd expect to see, because anyone can check that against the world."* This is the rule for the outreach artifact: send the four conditions and the refuters, not the framework interior.
- *Library of Babel* on stopping: the answer is reached when *"the finish line will also be the starting place"*, with the paradox that by then you have *"composed the answer through the process of locating it."*

## 3. What licenses a name

Only two things, both outside the shape itself:

1. **The ground settles it.** `THE_FORMULA.md` step 5: *"the ground settles it. Verified only by what pays. A gate is not the ground."* For this question the ground is a per-group comparison in which the outcome does not move because the frame believes the AI works.
2. **A provenanced import from a frame whose closure differs** (Fig 6 :23, Fig 8 :50-52), with a source gate and a shadow test. The statistics, human-factors and AI-evaluation sources in `10_source_ledger.csv` are such imports, recorded with provenance and overlap.

*They're Scanning Books* (carried mapping) sharpens this: *"The real import is not a new term in the existing space. It is a new dimension of the space."* And: *"Declaring the primitives does not make them neutral. It makes the choice visible."* A firm's chosen groups and chosen metric are visible choices, not discoveries of nature's own list.

## 4. The source problem: a per-group reading cannot name its cause

*The Hard Problem is About Source*, opened this session, gives the mechanism. Metamerism: an instrument reads green and *"cannot comment on the source"*; one green light, or yellow plus blue, or RGB, or CMYK, or a scheme not yet discovered can give the same reading. The essay states: *"If several paths create the same behavior, then behavior is a projection with lossy information."* And: *"An instrument that can't distinguish these cases demonstrates its own inadequacy."* It was the **spectrometer**, not an argument, that resolved it.

Applied to BUS-0085: an aggregate performance lift is a reading, not a source. The same lift can come from a real improvement in one group and a degradation in another, from a change in who is Observed at all (selective labels), or from the decision changing the label. The reading does not discriminate among these sources. What is owed is a better instrument, not a name. That is the content of F4 in `01_figure_grounded_audit.md`, and it is why the solution's second condition (an instrument for what counts as progress per group) is a discriminator requirement rather than a metric choice.

The essay also gives the correction rule when an inference fails: *"The answer is unreached until a better instrument exists."* This is exactly how the solution reports the firm-specific numbers: unreached at this budget, with the discriminator named in effect terms.

## 5. What shape does this question get?

The shape is the four-condition separator, stated in `01` section 0 and carried into `07` and `08`:

1. **Per-slot indexing.** The residual is a vector over customer and employee group-slots, and the slots are enumerated from contact (Fig 1 :19, Fig 11 :43).
2. **An instrument at the middle link.** For each slot, write down what would count as progress in that slot's terms and name the instrument that would read it (Fig 3 :25-27).
3. **A non-updating anchor with independent frames.** The comparison must not have its outcome determined by the decision, and at least two frames must range against it separately (Fig 2 :29, :33, :46).
4. **A pre-registered failure condition.** The failure is written before the run so the sought-for shape becomes an ordinary, refusable term (Formula, "Direction"; Operators :58 *"a refutation is a negation, which is why a vocabulary without it can only accumulate"*).

This shape is what a firm's determination must look like. It does not tell the firm which groups, which metric, or how large a lift counts. Those are ground readings.

## 5b. Correction to my own use of "shape, not a name"

I initially overstated the rule. The README's phrase is *"what a **missing** thing would have to look like (a shape, not a name)."* The subject is a primitive not held in the library. Customer groups, employee groups and outcome measurements are already enumerable in a firm's habitat; naming them is a **binding** from that habitat, not the invention of a missing primitive.

What remains forbidden is different and still decisive: the framework cannot name *which* binding is the right one, and it cannot certify a firm-specific number from inside its own vocabulary. Calling a chosen metric "the answer" turns a visible choice into an invisible one. Calling it a filter with a declared provenance is legitimate (Fig 9 :60; Fig 8 :50-52). Both readings are kept here so neither is quietly lost. This mirrors the correction recorded in the exemplar's `05` section 5b and its `06` Q1.

## 6. Prestige is not action

From *The Recursive Self-Improvement Trap*, opened this session: *"Prestige must never be allowed to validate an action directly... Prestige can fund an action. It can give an action a budget to be tried. But it cannot settle an action. Only the ground can settle it. The Ground Maintainer must be blind to prestige."* The essay names the collapse in its own system-building history: if prestige could buy actions, the highest-prestige agent monopolized compute and repeated its past success, and exploration stopped.

For BUS-0085, vendor reputation, a well-known audit firm's badge, adoption counts and employee enthusiasm are all budget inputs. They can fund a careful per-group evaluation. They cannot settle which group improved. A verification packet sent to famous names asking for endorsement is the named collapse, not corroboration (Fig 7 :35, Fig 2 :40).

## 7. My own audit is a simulation, not a ground reading

The same essay states: *"LLMs can quickly simulate an approximate answer/output with the formula... However, the truly testable output would need to be derived from the real architecture or working the formula yourself."* It calls such output *"simulacra."* So `01_figure_grounded_audit.md`, `06_isomers_and_order.md`, `07_the_tether_answer.md`, `08_expert_verification_packet.md`, `09_watson_answer.md` and `11_audit.md` are simulated derivations checked for internal consistency against the figures. They are not tests of any AI deployment, must be labelled as such, and cannot settle the question they report on. No firm data was used and no measurement was taken.
