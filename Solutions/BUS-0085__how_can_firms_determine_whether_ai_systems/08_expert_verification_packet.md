# Expert verification packet - the Tether on AI performance across groups

**What this is:** a claim in a form an AI evaluation researcher or risk practitioner can check. Mode, derivation and refuters are stated before the conclusion, so a reader knows what to look for and what would settle it.

**Mode: `specified` on the separator, `general` on the structural shape, not `grounded`.** No firm measurement is claimed. This is a shape and a route, not a result.

**Status: simulated derivation, not a ground reading.** It is checked for internal consistency against the figures. It cannot settle the question it reports on.

## 1. The claim, in one paragraph

A firm can determine whether an AI system improves performance across customer and employee groups only through a four-condition separator: (1) the residual is indexed per group and the groups are enumerated from contact; (2) an instrument exists for what counts as progress in each group's own terms, which is the middle link of the evaluation chain that usually has no instrument; (3) a non-updating anchor per group is ranged against by at least two independent frames; and (4) a failure condition was written before the run. A pooled lift number is a filter for the testing budget, never a witness for the verdict, and the per-group verdicts are kept separate and never averaged. No metric, group list or numerical threshold is asserted by the framework.

## 2. Three checkable sub-claims

### C1 - framework-internal; checkable by reading Figures 1, 9 and 3

- **Statement:** the residual must be indexed per group; the pooled average is a budget filter; and the objective link, not the accuracy link, is the usual break.
- **Source, verbatim:** Fig 1 :19 *"The residual is indexed by object slot, not aggregated."* Fig 1 :22 *"Aggregating across slots is how a live signal disappears into an average."* Fig 9 :60 *"Use filters for the budget and witnesses for the verdict. Never let a filter hand you a verdict."* Fig 3 :27 *"A link with no instrument is not a link that works. It is a link nobody has looked at."*
- **Refuter:** show a single aggregate residual that is losslessly invertible to the per-group vector under every group partition, including partitions chosen after seeing the result; or show a working per-group objective instrument in place before any group perception or vocabulary. Either breaks C1.

### C2 - evaluation-facing; checkable by an AI evaluator against their own practice

- **Statement:** the anchor for "improves" cannot be an outcome the AI's decision changes. A before/after dashboard is not such an anchor. An internal team that has read the model's criterion and dashboard is no longer outside the frame it grades.
- **Source, verbatim:** Fig 2 :33 *"The anchor must not update."* Fig 2 :29 *"An anchor is whatever settles the question and does not change its answer because of what any frame thinks."* Fig 10 :50 *"Looking inside the frame it grades is how the seat stops being outside."* Fig 10 :42 *"A verifier that can reconstruct the claim cannot verify it."*
- **What to check:** in a lending or hiring deployment, does the observed label depend on the decision? If a higher rate set from predicted risk makes default more likely, or an AI shortlist determines who receives the performance label, the raw outcome is a moving reference. Ask what comparison would hold the label fixed against the firm's belief.
- **Refuter:** exhibit a deployment where the group label is provably unaffected by the AI decision and the frame's belief, with the mechanism stated; or exhibit an internal checker whose reading is structurally independent of the prediction and the criterion even after it has seen the dashboard. Either narrows or breaks C2.

### C3 - framework-internal derivation

- **Statement:** two systems over the same group atoms that differ only in where the comparison bond attaches are isomers, not variants of one system; a firm-level lift is a lossy condensation of per-group residuals; and withdrawing a group verdict requires negation rather than score adjustment.
- **Source, verbatim:** Fig 12 :117 *"isomer. Same atoms, different bond, different substance. The difference is invisible in the formula and decisive in the behaviour."* Fig 4 :30 *"So take the round trip and measure the gap. Without it you are navigating by dead reckoning: confident about a position nobody checked."* Operators :58 *"a refutation is a negation, which is why a vocabulary without it can only accumulate"*
- **Refuter:** show two systems with the same group atoms and different bond attachments that are behaviourally identical for every case; or show the firm-level lift is losslessly invertible to the group vector; or show a framework that withdraws a verdict without a negation operator while preserving reach and provenance. Any one breaks C3.

## 3. What is explicitly not claimed

- Not a theory of everything, not a final evaluation framework. The per-group center stays a compound with its own loop.
- Not a fairness certificate. Passing a test is not a certificate that a system is fair.
- Not a universal group list, metric, threshold or percentage. No figure line returns one.
- Not that internal evaluation is independent after reading the model's interior.
- Not that the pooled and per-group results share a sign. They can reverse.
- Not `grounded`. Nothing here is asserted as settled by experiment.
- Not that this packet settles the business question. It is a simulated derivation checked for internal consistency against the figures.

## 5. What would settle it

A discriminator, not a further argument. From *The Hard Problem is About Source*: an instrument that reads green *"cannot comment on the source"*; the spectrometer resolved it. Here:

1. Is there a per-group comparison whose outcome label does not move when the AI's decision moves? Name the comparison.
2. What would count as progress for each group in its own terms, and what instrument reads it?
3. Do two frames range against that anchor separately, on separate evidence, with the verdict recorded before the framework is shown?
4. Does the failure condition written before the run actually refuse a group verdict when the ground turns, or only lower a score?

## 6. What an expert can check in twenty minutes

2. Check C2 against one deployment you know: does the outcome label depend on the decision? Write the anchor you would use instead.
3. Name one instrument that would discriminate a real per-group improvement from a change in who is observed. Say why your instrument does not move when the frame acts.
4. Send the refutation. A failure is more useful than an endorsement.
