## Three things in the wider literature that bear directly on this question

1. **Convergence is not identity.** The analogue gravity review and the two Steinhauer experiments establish a real, repeatable shape in a lab substrate. The effective field theory source explains why that does not identify the substrate of a different theory: different short distance degrees of freedom can yield the same long distance description. This is the figure claim that *"Convergent derivation and adopted import look identical in the contents. Only the record of where each came from separates them"* (Fig 8 :51), corroborated from a low overlap field.
2. **Weak emergence is the right kind.** The emergence source records that a weakly emergent property can be multiply realised by systems with different components. That is exactly the analogue result: one shape, several substrates. Strong emergence, where the macroscopic property cannot be deduced even in principle, is not what the experiments show and is not claimed here.
3. **Analogies transfer relations, not objects.** The cognitive-science source on structure mapping corroborates that the lab-to-spacemap carries relations, not the identity of the actors. Its warning that an analogy breaks down when stretched beyond its intended meaning is the ordinary-language version of the nonzero round-trip gap.

## Distribution: offer, do not push

Figure-grounded reasons:

- **Fig 10 :52**  -  *"Bring a characterised residual, never the source."* Sending the full framework before anyone has described a residual sends a recording, not a generator. Lead with the two verdicts and the failure conditions; offer the links after.
- **Fig 10 :42**  -  *"A verifier that can reconstruct the claim cannot verify it."* A recipient who has absorbed the framework before giving a verdict has joined the frame being checked.
- **Fig 2 :35**  -  *"Alignment is triangulation, not negotiation. A mirror cannot correct you."* Do not ask for agreement. Ask what would count as a failure.
- **Fig 9 :27-28**  -  *"describe it before you go looking, or any frame you pick will seem to fit."* Ask what the recipient would expect to see if the shape claim were true before giving them the article.
- **Fig 2 :40**  -  *"disqualifying for a verdict: agreement about the world is worthless when the agreeing frames share an evidence pool"*; **Fig 2 :42**  -  *"Buy independence in proportion to the cost of being wrong."* Analogue-gravity researchers often share the same review literature; treat their agreement as one witness, not a crowd.
- **Fig 4 :22**  -  *"Only methods cross up. Only head starts come down."* Stage 3 sends a method, which is permitted; it does not send an answer.

**Recommended order in a reply:** (1) the residual and the two separate verdicts, (2) the failure conditions and the question "what would you count as a failure here?", (3) record their verdict, (4) only then the links, with the note that the figures, not this summary, are the authority.

## The two-stage offer, and the third stage

1. **Stage 1.** Validate the shape claim against the ground the recipient already trusts. No theory is shown. Their verdict is recorded first.
2. **Stage 2, opt-in.** If curious, the three source links. Later agreement is never pooled as corroboration (Fig 2 :40).
3. **Stage 3, opt-in.** The formula as a reusable method, with the instruction that each user writes their own refuter and states their own mode.

**Hard precondition:** stage 1 only works if the shape claim and the gap are kept separate. The exemplar's own warning applies: outputs *"could never be verified if the solutions presented were not contrived to fit the formula."* Write the refuter first.

## Venue constraints found during verification

- `Philosophy of Physics` states that it *"does not publish proposals for new physical theories"* and that submission is restricted to members of the Philosophy of Physics Society. A shape or structural claim may fit; a new physical theory would not. Its page also points to an AI policy, which must be read before submitting simulated work. No submission is made by this agent.
- `Living Reviews in Relativity`, which published the canonical Analogue Gravity review, lists its current article processing charge as zero, but operates a review-led model. Check scope before an unsolicited submission.
- `arXiv` is a preprint server, not a ground. It establishes provenance and priority only.
- `APS Global Physics Summit` requires APS membership to submit an abstract; the 2027 call closed 29 Oct 2026. Check the next cycle.
