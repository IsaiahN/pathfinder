## 0. The residual in effect terms

The question is: can a condensed matter system whose low energy excitations obey equations shaped like a metric and a horizon **model real spacetime behaviour**?

Stated as an effect, not a theory: a laboratory system (an atomic Bose-Einstein condensate, a flowing fluid, a superconducting circuit) returns readings that look like a relativistic prediction, a Hawking temperature, a correlation across a horizon. The residual is the gap between (a) the form those readings reproduce and (b) the thing the question actually asks about, namely the behaviour of real spacetime as settled by its own ground. Two options and what each leads to:

- **Option A, assert the upgrade.** Read the analogue result as showing that real spacetime has the analogue's substrate, or that the lab measurement settles the astrophysical claim. This imports an atom without a source gate or shadow test (Fig 6 :12-14) and treats a shape as a name.
- **Option B, keep the verdicts separate.** Say that the analogue reproduces the *shape* at maximum independence, and that the claim about real spacetime's substrate and ground stays open. This returns a checkable shape, a written refuter, and an honest stop.

An answer would look like: a statement of which figure lines govern the composition, a per-domain verdict with mode attached, the anchor for each verdict, and the instrument that would close the residual. That ordinary term is now bound, so it can accrue a residual like anything else (THE_FORMULA, "Direction").

**R by arity, symmetry, scale:**
- *Arity*: two conditions. Shape reproduction and ground contact. This is a **union compound**, not one hard question (Fig 9 :54).
- *Symmetry*: the ordinary word "models" is used as if it were the symmetric `≡` of two structures. The actual mapping is one way, from the analogue onto the target, and it preserves relations rather than objects. The asymmetry is the residual, not a nuisance to be smoothed away.
- *Scale*: the analogue is an effective, low energy description at one scale; real gravity is tested across many. The between-scale transform is lossy and its round trip has a gap (Fig 4 :30).

## 3. Findings

### F1  -  Emergent geometry is expected, not a refutation, and it adds no seventh member (`general` shape, triangulated)

- Fig 13 :43-44: **"A continuum was the seventh and was removed for failing exactly this: it is a spectrum without gaps, or smoothness over space and time. A generating set must not contain what its own members produce."**
- Fig 13 :124: **"Smoothness, symmetry, chaos and discreteness are each what a particular composition leaves behind, and none is a seventh member."**
- Fig 13 :115: **"The set is closed. The arrangements are not. That gap is why import exists."**
- Fig 13 :116: **"Six members, and no amount of arranging produces a seventh: a new member would have to be a new kind, and composition only ever rearranges the kinds already held."**
- Fig 6 :25: **"An import adds no atom to the world."** Fig 6 :26: **"The world does not gain primitives; a frame gains access."**

The acoustic metric is a derived quantity of the same kind as smoothness or a continuum. The framework already removed the continuum precisely because it is produced by its own members. So an analogue that produces an effective geometry is not overthrowing the six-term set; it is an instance of the rule that arrangements are unbounded while the set is closed. This also fixes the direction of the question: the analogue gives a frame access to a shape, it does not add a primitive to the world.

**Refuter.** Exhibit a term in the analogue's effective description that is not composable from the six terms and that real gravity requires. That would be a genuine seventh kind, and the paragraph above fails.

### F2  -  The analogue is the same kernel with a different cast (`specified`, triangulated)

- Fig 4 :20: **"Only the actors, the currency and the anchor change."**
- Fig 4 :22: **"Only methods cross up. Only head starts come down."**
- Fig 8 :45: **"Two frames together reach what neither reaches alone."**
- Fig 8 :51: **"Convergent derivation and adopted import look identical in the contents. Only the record of where each came from separates them, and the surplus lives only where the closures differ."**

The analogue gravity review by Barcelo, Liberati and Visser describes a research programme that *"explores analogues of general relativistic gravitational fields within other physical systems, particularly but not exclusively in condensed matter systems"*. The kernel (a cross-kind composition yielding an effective geometry) stays; the actors (phonons, flow, atoms) and the anchor (a lab spectrum) change. That is Fig 4 exactly. Because the analogue frame and the relativistic frame reach the same shape from different evidence pools, this is **convergent derivation at maximum independence**, not an adopted import. The record of where each came from is what makes it separable.

**Refuter.** Show that the analogue requires a bond or a term outside the held set, so the kernel differs rather than the cast. Then the Fig 4 reading fails and the surplus is an import, not a convergence.

### F3  -  The lab anchor and the astrophysical anchor are different anchors (`specified`, triangulated)

- Fig 2 :29: **"An anchor is whatever settles the question and does not change its answer because of what any frame thinks."**
- Fig 2 :35: **"Alignment is triangulation, not negotiation. A mirror cannot correct you."**
- Fig 10 :24: **"the ground does not decay, the channel does"**
- Fig 11 :40: **"An improvement that does not change contact changes nothing, however much it improves."**

Steinhauer's 2016 experiment observed spontaneous Hawking radiation *"emanating from an analogue black hole in an atomic Bose–Einstein condensate"*, and the 2019 follow-up found that *"the correlation spectrum of Hawking radiation agrees well with a thermal spectrum, and its temperature is given by the surface gravity"*. That is a settled reading for **D1**. It is not a reading of D2. The anchor for the real-spacetime claim is an astrophysical observation, which does not move when a BEC measurement changes. Treating agreement between the analogue and the theory as a verdict misreads a mirror as an anchor.

**Refuter.** Produce one instrument whose reading ranges against both the analogue and real spacetime on the same axis, such that a change in one forces a change in the other and each frame is corrected by it separately. Without that, the two verdicts stay separate.

### F4  -  Arranging inside the analogue closure cannot move the wall (`specified`, triangulated)

- Fig 6 :21: **"MINT composes inside the closure and can never add an atom."**
- Fig 6 :23: **"No frame escapes its own closure. The bound moves only on contact with a frame whose closure differs."**
- Fig 8 :32: **"no search, however long, adds an atom"**
- Fig 13 :118: **"infinite effort inside a finite reach."**

Fig 6 :14 names import as *"the only operator that moves the wall"*. An import needs a source gate (provenance) and a shadow test (it explains a gap). A lab analogue that keeps returning the same effective metric after ten thousand runs has more of the space visited, not a larger closure. So the inference from "the analogue reproduces gravity" to "real spacetime has this substrate" is a category error: it treats a shape as a wall-moving atom.

**Refuter.** Show a genuine import with provenance and a shadow test that adds an atom to the **real-gravity** frame, not only to the analogue frame. If the import only enlarges D1, the wall in D2 has not moved.

### F5  -  The question is a union, and a shape result is a filter, not a witness (`specified`, triangulated on the figure side)

- Fig 9 :54: **"And when the split lands on a union rather than a partition, unbundle before you search again."**
- Fig 9 :57-58: **"a case in two conditions is not a harder case of either. It stops being a mixture and becomes a specific compound."**
- Fig 9 :60: **"Use filters for the budget and witnesses for the verdict. Never let a filter hand you a verdict."**

"Can it model real spacetime behaviour" bundles at least two conditions: reproduce the relational structure, and match the ground. The effective field theory frame corroborates the split from outside the pool: an effective theory *"includes the appropriate degrees of freedom to describe physical phenomena occurring at a chosen length scale or energy scale, while ignoring substructure and degrees of freedom at shorter distances"*, and the same page notes that GR itself is expected to be a low energy effective theory. Reproducing the low energy shape says nothing about which substructure is real. A thermal spectrum in a BEC is a **filter for where to spend the budget**, not a witness for the D2 verdict.

**Refuter.** Exhibit an analogue result that passes the shape test and demonstrably fails at the real anchor in a way the framework predicts. The BEC's microscopic dispersion and preferred frame are already the ordinary-language version of this; a falsifier needs a stated prediction and a reading, not a vocabulary difference.

### F6  -  The round-trip gap is nonzero and is quantity-specific (`specified`, one live strain)

- Fig 4 :30: **"So take the round trip and measure the gap. Without it you are navigating by dead reckoning: confident about a position nobody checked."**
- Fig 4 :31-32: **"The gap is not an error to eliminate. It is the measurement of what the coarser description cannot hold, and it is the only honest report of how much was given up."**
- Fig 11 :70: **"A test harness is a substituted habitat. Which is why a synthetic solve proves wiring and never capability."**

Carrying "geometry" from the fluid up to a general shape and back down to spacetime drops the preferred frame, the discrete microscopic dispersion, and the finite number of constituents. Those are exactly the things that matter to a substrate claim. `R_T` is therefore nonzero for substrate questions. A strain is kept visible: **universality** (the renormalization group and effective field theory) says that for some quantities the microscopic detail provably drops out, so `R_T` may be effectively zero for a universal critical exponent. The gap is quantity-specific. Claiming a single global value either way would be the dead-reckoning error Fig 4 names.

**Refuter.** Show, by a universality or renormalization-group argument plus a reading, that the specific quantity at issue has `R_T = 0` for the substrate claim as well as for the critical exponent. Then the gap closes for that quantity and the strain becomes a correction.

### F7  -  The analogue does not name real spacetime's substrate (`general` limit, triangulated)

- Fig 9 :32: **"you get a kind of thing, never the answer itself"**
- Fig 13 :134: **"Unreachable is a verdict here, not a silence,"**

The canonical figures README states the limit as *"a shape, not a name"*, and *"Indiscernibility is not identity."* *The Hard Problem is About Source* gives the mechanism: *"If several paths create the same behavior, then behavior is a projection with lossy information (it cannot define consciousness in full)."* An instrument that reads an effective metric *"cannot comment on the source"*. Naming the BEC or the fluid as the substrate of real spacetime is a metamerism error: a source named from a reading where several sources could give the same reading. The honest report is the shape plus the statement that the source is unreached.

**Correction kept visible.** The phrase "shape, not a name" governs a **missing** primitive. The lab substrate is not missing from D1; it is a held atom there. Naming it is a binding from the lab frame with provenance, not the invention of a seventh term. What is forbidden is promoting that binding to the substrate of D2 without a ground reading.

**Refuter.** Name the missing primitive of D2 from a held reading, show the reading discriminates among sources, and have the astrophysical ground pay for it. Until then the slot stays open.

## 4. Strain test and re-census

- **Strain S1, apparent Fig 13 versus emergence.** First reading: "if geometry is emergent, the six-term set is wrong." Re-census: the continuum was *removed* from the set precisely because it is produced (Fig 13 :43-44). No strain after re-census; the two govern different objects (primitives versus their products).
- **Strain S2, live: universality versus the round-trip gap.** Universality can make `R_T` zero for a low energy observable; Fig 4 requires the gap to be measured. Held as a live, quantity-specific strain in F6 rather than resolved by fiat.
- **Strain S3, the type of "models".** `≡` (identity) is symmetric and would collapse the two anchors into one referent. The evidence supports a relation mapping, closer to structure mapping in cognition (Wikipedia, "Analogy": the mapping *"takes place not only between objects, but also between relations of objects and between relations of relations"*). Typed as a relation, not an identity. Triangulated across the Operators table and the cognitive-science source; the figure side alone is single-source and marked as such.
- **Strain S4, strong versus weak emergence.** Wikipedia's "Emergence" entry distinguishes weak emergence, where a property *"can be multiply realised by systems possessing different components"*, from strong emergence, where it cannot be deduced even in principle. The analogue result is weak emergence by that definition. This corroborates F1 and F2 from a low-overlap field. No conflict with the figures; the conflict rule was not triggered.

## 5. Pre-registered refuter

Written before the conclusion, as required by the discipline: a falsifier written before the run is a design instrument.

The shape claim is refuted if any of the following holds:

1. **Primitive test.** One of the six is derived from the other five, or a seventh kind is derived from arrangements of them (breaks Fig 13 :41, :115-116).
2. **Kernel test.** The analogue requires a bond or a term outside the held set, so Fig 4 :20's different cast, same kernel is false.
3. **Cross-anchor instrument.** An instrument ranges against both the analogue and real spacetime on one axis, and the readings diverge where the shape predicts identity (breaks F3).
4. **Provenanced import.** A genuine import with source gate and shadow test adds an atom to the real-gravity frame, moving the wall in D2 (breaks F4).
5. **Zero-gap demonstration.** A universality argument shows the substrate-level round trip has `R_T = 0`, closing the live strain in F6.
6. **Source discrimination.** An instrument named from a discriminating reading shows that the metric-like observation has a unique source in D2 (breaks F7 and upgrades the slot from `gap` to `specified`).

If any fires, the relevant verdict is withdrawn and the retraction is recorded here. Until one is run, no part of this may be labelled `grounded`.
