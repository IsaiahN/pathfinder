# The Tether's answer on emergent gravity and real spacetime

**Mode: `specified` on the shape-level claims, `general` on the structural shape, `gap` on the substrate and anchor upgrade. Not `grounded`.** Simulated derivation checked against the figures (`05_what_shape_means.md` §7), not a ground reading.

## 1. The residual it answers

A condensed matter system returns relativistic-looking readings: an effective metric, a horizon, a Hawking temperature. The question asks whether that can model **real spacetime behaviour**. The residual bundles two conditions with different anchors, so it is a union compound (Fig 9 :54-58), not one question with two opposite answers. Verdicts are kept separate and never averaged.

## 2. The shape

1. **Emergent geometry is the framework's own prediction, not a crisis.** The continuum was removed from the primitive set because it is what a composition leaves behind (Fig 13 :43-44, :124). So a geometry arising from another substrate is exactly the kind of result the six-term set expects. Fig 13 :115: **"The set is closed. The arrangements are not. That gap is why import exists."**

2. **The analogue reproduces the shape at maximum independence.** Fig 4 :20: **"Only the actors, the currency and the anchor change."** Same kernel, different cast. Fig 8 :51: **"Convergent derivation and adopted import look identical in the contents. Only the record of where each came from separates them, and the surplus lives only where the closures differ."** The lab and relativistic frames reach the same form from different evidence pools. That is convergence, not an import that adds an atom.

3. **The shape does not transfer the substrate.** Fig 6 :21: **"MINT composes inside the closure and can never add an atom."** Fig 6 :25: **"An import adds no atom to the world."** Rearranging the analogue closure cannot supply the primitive that would make its substrate the substrate of real gravity. Only import moves the wall, and the import here has neither source gate nor shadow test.

4. **The anchors are different, so there is no single verdict.** Fig 2 :29: **"An anchor is whatever settles the question and does not change its answer because of what any frame thinks."** The lab anchor is a measured spectrum; the real-spacetime anchor is an astrophysical observation. Fig 2 :35: **"Alignment is triangulation, not negotiation. A mirror cannot correct you."** The time to promote the lab result is when one instrument ranges against both anchors.

5. **The relation is not an identity.** Writing "the analogue *is* spacetime" would use `≡` across two anchors. The evidence supports a relation mapping, closer to structure mapping in cognition: relations transfer, objects do not. The analogue and the relativistic core are isomers over a fixed atom set, or a functional group as ordinarily written (`06_isomers_and_order.md`), with a stated falsifier.

6. **The round-trip gap is nonzero for substrate questions and quantity-specific.** Fig 4 :30: **"So take the round trip and measure the gap. Without it you are navigating by dead reckoning: confident about a position nobody checked."** Universality may make the gap vanish for a low energy observable. That is a live strain, not a resolved point.

## 3. What it explicitly does not say

- Not that a condensate, fluid or superconducting circuit is what real spacetime is made of. That is a name where the framework returns a shape.
- Not that a lab measurement settles astrophysical black-hole thermodynamics. Different anchor.
- Not that spacetime does not exist, or that GR is wrong. GR is settled in its own domain and the framework does not overwrite it.
- Not a derivation of one theory from the other, and no new primitive.
- Not `grounded`. No measurement at the relevant cross-anchor point exists.
- Not a theory of everything. The figures have no last room.

## 4. What would settle it  -  the ground

A source-discriminating instrument and a cross-anchor test, not more agreement. From *The Hard Problem is About Source*: an instrument that reads green *"cannot comment on the source"*; the spectrometer resolved it. Here:

- **Cross-anchor instrument.** One measurement axis on which the analogue and real spacetime both range, with independent correction by each. A divergence where the shape predicts identity refutes verdict 2's current form (refuter 3 of `01`).
- **Source discriminator.** An instrument that distinguishes a metric-like reading produced by a discrete preferred-frame substrate from one produced by a relativistic substrate. That would name the slot and move the claim from `gap` to `specified`.
- **Provenanced import.** A genuine import with source gate and shadow test that adds an atom to the real-gravity frame. That is the only wall-moving operator (Fig 6 :14), and none has been supplied.
- **Standing falsifiers.** Derive one of the six from the other five; show the analogue needs a term outside the held set; demonstrate a zero round-trip gap for the substrate claim.

## 5. The honest stop

**Unreached, not unreachable in principle.** Fig 13 :134: **"Unreachable is a verdict here, not a silence,"** The six-term set is *"a human's careful guess at reality's compression axes"*, so declaring it makes the choice visible rather than neutral. Another sensorium's minimum set need not be these six. The answer stops where the available instruments can no longer distinguish, and the lineage is recorded so the stop is checkable. *"Indiscernibility is not identity."* Absence of a cross-anchor instrument is not evidence that real spacetime has no substrate.

## 6. One-paragraph version for outreach

> The Tether does not say that a condensate or a fluid is what spacetime is made of, and it does not claim the final theory. It says that emergent geometry is exactly what a six-term substrate predicts, because the continuum was removed from the primitive list long ago as a derivative of composition. An analogue black hole is the same kernel with different actors, currency and anchor, so it reproduces the *shape* of relativistic behaviour at maximum independence. What it cannot do is add an atom to the real-gravity frame: the lab anchor is a measured spectrum, the spacetime anchor is an astrophysical observation, and a mirror cannot correct you. So the shape claim is `specified` and checkable, while the substrate claim is a declared `gap`. What would close it is a cross-anchor instrument or a source discriminator, not more agreement. The slot stays open until the ground settles it.
