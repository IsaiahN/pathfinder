## 1. A shape is a constraint you can state before you can name the source

The canonical README gives the limit: the framework returns *"what a missing thing would have to look like (a shape, not a name)"*. Fig 9 :32: **"you get a kind of thing, never the answer itself"**. Fig 13 :134-135: **"It cannot say which primitive is missing, because naming it would mean already holding it."**

From *The Library of Babel*: *"you can cut your search short if the set of books returned is small and descriptive enough that it provides a general shape of the answer that satisfies your question."*

So a **shape** is the narrowed form of an answer you can state before you can name its source. It constrains; it does not fill. For this question:

- **Shape:** an effective geometry, with a horizon and a temperature, produced by a cross-kind composition whose continuum is a derivative and whose substrate is not named by the framework.
- **Name:** "the substrate of real spacetime is a Bose-Einstein condensate", or "it is a fluid", or "it is a superfluid helium film". These are bindings from a frame, not outputs of the shape rule.

The distinction matters because the analogue result really does establish the first and cannot establish the second.

## 2. How the shape is obtained

- *Library of Babel*: use the vocabulary you already have that is *closest* to the thing, then look for new terms that overlap or correlate with it, recursively. *"With each new term gained, you are able to narrow the space by definition and deepen your vocabulary."*
- Fig 6 :30: **"The description travels. Fails by being sharpened into terms only we can read. Cast it in effect terms."** Fig 9 :30-31: **"your words are borrowed too, so describe the effect rather than the cause"**
- *Library of Babel* on the tether: the question itself anchors the search, and *"the finish line will also be the starting place"*, with the paradox that by then you have *"composed the answer through the process of locating it."*
- *Decoding Tacit Knowledge* (carried from the exemplar): *"When contact isn't possible, the best you can send is a plain description of what you'd expect to see, because anyone can check that against the world."* This is why `02_outreach_email.md` leads with failure conditions.

For emergent gravity the ordinary words "emergent", "analogue" and "model" are borrowed from the field itself. Restating the residual in effect terms gives: *a shape is reproduced in one frame; a different frame with a different anchor is asked to accept it*. That sentence, not the word "emergence", is what the figures can range against.

## 3. What would license a name

Only two things, both outside the shape:

1. **The ground settles it.** `THE_FORMULA.md` step 5: *"the ground settles it. Verified only by what pays. A gate is not the ground."* The README: the framework *"stops at the limit of what the available instruments can distinguish, and records the lineage so the stop is honest."*
2. **A provenanced import from a frame whose closure differs** (Fig 6 :23, Fig 8 :51), with a source gate and a shadow test.

*They're Scanning Books When They Should* (carried from the exemplar): *"The real import is not a new term in the existing space. It is a new dimension of the space."* And: *"A minimum viable set of six terms is a human's careful guess at reality's compression axes; another sensorium's minimum set need not be those six."* The six are a visible guess, not nature's own list. That is why F1 of the audit insists that producing a geometry from a different substrate is expected rather than surprising.

## 4. The source error, and why it is the specific failure here

*The Hard Problem is About Source* gives the mechanism. An instrument reads green and *"cannot comment on the source"*: one green light, or yellow plus blue, or RGB, or CMYK, or a scheme not yet discovered. *"If several paths create the same behavior, then behavior is a projection with lossy information."* And: *"An instrument that can't distinguish these cases demonstrates its own inadequacy."* It was the **spectrometer**, not an argument, that resolved the case.

Here the reading is an effective metric with a horizon and a thermal spectrum. Several paths produce it: a BEC with a sonic horizon, a water wave tank, an optical fibre, a superconducting circuit, and, in a different sense, relativistic spacetime itself. The analogue experiment reads one of these and cannot comment on which source the target theory has. Asking it to do so is the metamerism error. What is owed is a source-discriminating instrument, not a name.

## 5. Correction to my own §3 (kept visible)

I initially wrote that "shape, not a name" forbids naming the condensate or the fluid as the analogue's substrate. That was an overstatement. The README's phrase governs a **missing** primitive, one not held in `Γ`. The lab substrate is not missing from the lab frame; it is a held atom there, directly measured. Naming it is a binding with provenance, not the invention of a seventh term.

What remains wrong is the **promotion** of that binding to the substrate of the real-gravity frame (D2) without a source-discriminating instrument. §4's metamerism point stands, but for that reason: the missing discriminator, not a ban on naming held atoms. Both readings are recorded so neither is quietly erased.
