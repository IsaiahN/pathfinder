## 1. A shape is a constraint, not a filler

The canonical limit in `FRAMEWORK/reference/FIGURES_README_canonical.md`: the framework returns *"what a missing thing would have to look like (a shape, not a name)."* Fig 9 :32: *"you get a kind of thing, never the answer itself."* Fig 13 :134-135: *"It can say the answer is out of reach with the library as it stands, so something has to be imported before it is asked again. It cannot say which primitive is missing, because naming it would mean already holding it."*

From *The Library of Babel*: *"you can cut your search short if the set of books returned is small and descriptive enough that it provides a general shape of the answer that satisfies your question."*

So a **shape** is the narrowed form of an answer that can be stated before it can be named. It constrains; it does not fill. For this question:

- **Shape:** an unmoved settlement, a bounded and nameable cost of losing, a writable refusal, an enumerated habitat, and priced bonds between roles.
- **Name:** "an independent electoral commission with seven members appointed for non-renewable terms." That is a binding imported from political science, not a figure result.

## 2. How the shape is obtained

- *The Library of Babel*: use the vocabulary already held that is closest to the thing, then look for recurring new terms that overlap with it. *"With each new term gained, you narrow the space by definition and deepen your vocabulary."* Here the first vocabulary is "competition", "loss", "count", "court", "retaliation"; the new terms that narrow it are "anchor", "standing stake", "refusal", "habitat".
- *The Library of Babel* on steering: *"The books that you are returning in your searches need to be tested or ranged against the problem you want answered in order to steer the direction of the next search."* The question is the tether, so a proposed institution is tested against the severity of loss, not against its reputation.
- *Decoding Tacit Knowledge*: *"When contact isn't possible, the best you can send is a plain description of what you'd expect to see, because anyone can check that against the world."* This is why the outreach packet asks what a checker would expect to see, rather than explaining the framework.
- Fig 6 :30: *"The description travels. Fails by being sharpened into terms only we can read. Cast it in effect terms."* Fig 9 :30-31: *"your words are borrowed too, so describe the effect rather than the cause."*
- *The Library of Babel* on stopping: *"you will know that you are done because the finish line will also be the starting place, as there is nowhere else to search and nothing else that can describe the answer better."*

## 3. What licenses a name

Only two things, both outside the shape itself:

1. **The ground settles it.** `THE_FORMULA.md` step 5 states that settling is done by the ground and that *"A gate is not the ground."* A real transfer of power, measured against whether the loser survived and whether the ruling moved, would name the slot.
2. **A provenanced import from a frame whose closure differs** (Fig 6 :23, Fig 8 :50-52), with a source gate and a shadow test. "Judicial independence" and "separation of duties" are such imports, recorded as such in `10_source_ledger.csv`.

*The Hard Problem is About Source* gives the mechanism for why an argument is not enough: *"If several paths create the same behavior, then behavior is a projection with lossy information"*, and the colour case *"was proven wrong not by argument, but by the arrival of a better instrument."* Applied here, an election that looks peaceful can have several sources: a bound on loss, a captive electorate, or a loser who simply did not contest. The reading does not determine the source. What is owed is a discriminator, not a name.

The same essay: *"An instrument that can't distinguish these cases demonstrates its own inadequacy"*. A single "democracy score" is such an instrument; it cannot comment on which slot is live.

## 4. Prestige is not action

From *The Recursive Self-Improvement Trap*: *"Prestige must never be allowed to validate an action directly."* And: *"Prestige can fund an action. It can give an action a budget to be tried. But it cannot settle an action. Only the ground can settle it. The Ground Maintainer must be blind to prestige."*

For this question: a list of famous political scientists who agree with the shape claim would be prestige doing the work of a ground. The legitimate ask is the opposite, give the claim a budget to be tried and let the settlement decide. The same essay: *"You may all agree and be wrong."*

It also gives the reason a second seat in this folder cannot corroborate: *"the output is only as good as the validator. The agent, or the tool, or the frame, or the theory, cannot validate itself."*

## 5. Answer to the question's own "what counts"

**What licenses a particular institution in the shape?** Nothing in the figures. A particular institution is licensed only by the ground settling a minted term, or by a provenanced import with a shadow test. Until one of those, the institution is the searcher's own contribution and must be recorded as such.

**Does this folder commit the forbidden error?** No, provided the four constraints stay in effect terms. The error would be to write "the answer is an independent judiciary" as if the framework named it. That is a binding from the law frame onto a held atom, and Fig 2 :47 warns what happens when a ground is listed as one of the parties.

## 6. My own audit is a simulation, not a ground reading

From *The Recursive Self-Improvement Trap*: LLMs *"can quickly simulate an approximate answer/output"*, but *"the truly testable output would need to be derived from the real architecture or working the formula yourself."* So `01_figure_grounded_audit.md`, `06_isomers_and_order.md`, and `09_watson_answer.md` are simulated derivations checked for internal consistency against the figures. They are **not** tests of the claim and must be labelled as such. They cannot settle anything they report on.

## 7. Correction to my own §3

I first wrote that the shape-not-a-name rule forbids naming any institution at all. That overstates it. The README's phrase governs a **missing** thing, a primitive not held in the library. Naming a set of roles is naming a binding from another frame, which is permitted with provenance, exactly as the exemplar narrows the rule for Gradient and Spectrum. What remains forbidden is claiming the binding is a derivation from the figures, or that it moves the wall on its own. Both statements are kept here so neither is quietly lost.
