## Wider literature and why it bears

Eight world sources were read across seven fields that do not share an author with the figures. They are corroboration only, never verdicts.

1. **Political science** puts the residual on the table directly: power holders risk *"political retribution or retaliation"*, and initial alternation becomes a habit. This matches Fig 7 :35-36 and Fig 10 :61.
2. **Law** supplies the canonical instance of an anchor that does not update: a court independent of the branches it judges. This matches Fig 2 :33 and Fig 10 :59.
3. **Institutional design** prices the standing stake: wealth from office, prospects after office, criminal prosecution on leaving, and control of other branches. This matches Fig 10 :61 and Fig 2 :47.
4. **Game theory** says credibility requires binding future options in advance. This matches Fig 12 :112 (a plan is a sequence) and Fig 5 :65-66 (bind the sought-for shape).
5. **Animal behaviour** shows that when the cost of continuing is unbounded the contest becomes ruinous rather than producing an accepted loser. This matches Fig 3 :25-27, the middle link with no instrument.
6. **Human cognition** adds the severity asymmetry: losses loom larger than gains, especially in competition. This explains why a norm-first design fails exactly when the stakes rise (Fig 10 :59-61).
7. **Engineering** gives the priced version of independence: tolerate F faulty components only with 3F+1 players and 2F+1 independent paths. This matches Fig 2 :42 and Fig 8 :47-48.
8. **Business control** says the roles, not the people, carry the protection. This matches Fig 12 :5, *"The bond is the rest of the information."*

No world source conflicts with the figure verdict, so the conflict rule (back to census) was not triggered. Agreement across these sources is not a second witness: they are separate fields, but this simulated run drew them all from one search session, so overlap is declared in the ledger.

## Distribution: offer, do not push

- **Fig 10 :52** *"Bring a characterised residual, never the source."* Lead with the residual and the refuter. Sending the full framework before anyone has described what they would count as a failure turns a checker into a reader of the frame.
- **Fig 2 :35** *"Alignment is triangulation, not negotiation. A mirror cannot correct you."* Do not ask for agreement. A reply that agrees is a conversation, not corroboration.
- **Fig 9 :27-28** *"describe it before you go looking, or any frame you pick will seem to fit"* Ask what they would expect to see if the shape claim were true before showing the figures.
- **Fig 1 :22** *"Aggregating across slots is how a live signal disappears into an average."* Ask about the specific slot they know (a court, a count, a security service), not about "democracy" in general.
- **Fig 2 :40** *"disqualifying for a verdict: agreement about the world is worthless when the agreeing frames share an evidence pool."* No list selected for receptiveness, no bulk send, no pooled endorsements.

## The two-stage offer

**Stage 1:** send the residual, the four constraints, and the four refuters from `02_outreach_email.md`. Record the recipient's verdict before showing any framework material.

**Stage 2, opt-in only:** if they are curious, offer the three links above. State that the figures, not the summary, are the authority. Their later agreement is never pooled as corroboration (Fig 2 :40).

**Stage 3, also opt-in:** offer the formula as a method. Each user writes their own refuter and states their own mode, because *"a frame cannot check what it is looking for"* (`THE_FORMULA.md`, Direction).

**Hard precondition:** stages 1 to 3 only work if the stage-1 shape survives. If any pre-registered refuter fires, stop and correct the packet before sending.
