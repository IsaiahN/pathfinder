## QUESTION

Decide, in effect terms, what the Tether can say about making political competition credible when losing power may carry severe personal or economic risks. Option A, norm first: losing is safe because contenders are committed to accepting defeat. Option B, ground first: losing is safe because the settlement does not depend on what the loser wants, and the downside is bounded before the contest starts. What each leads to: A relies on a frame state and fails exactly when severity rises, turning the contest into a struggle over the arbiter that has no accepted loser; B returns a shape of constraints in effect terms, with named institutions left as provenanced imports, and a pre-registered refuter that can withdraw it. The residual is indexed by slot, so the verdict is kept per domain and per slot and is never averaged into one credibility score.

## DOMAIN CENSUS

R by arity, symmetry and scale, then one line per domain with actors / currency / anchor, then partition or union, then order by dependency.

- **R (the residual).** A political order is supposed to register an accepted loser, but when the cost of losing is unbounded the loser has no reason to accept the result. Arity: at least two contending frames plus one settlement that is not a negotiation between them. Symmetry: winning and losing are ordered, not interchangeable; the consequence is a sequence of contest, settlement, transfer. Scale: first order at one contest and one arbiter, second order at a durable regime of repeated alternation where a settled contest becomes an atom for the next one.
- **D1, contest.** Actors: incumbent, challenger, parties. Currency: votes, office, patronage. Anchor: no, not yet; who won is exactly what is disputed, so D1 is not a domain until it is coupled to D2.
- **D2, settlement.** Actors: electoral body, courts, legislature, certifying officers. Currency: rulings, counts, certifications. Anchor: yes, a ruling that does not change when a contender pushes on it.
- **D3, habitat / contact field.** Actors: military, bureaucracy, prosecutors, media, business, foreign governments, electorate. Currency: coercion, money, information, loyalty. Anchor: yes, the field of contact, which updates but is not authored by the designer.
- **D4, verification / outside frame.** Actors: monitors, journalists, researchers, opposition inside the loop. Currency: credible reports, residuals. Anchor: yes, the ground, never agreement. In this simulated run all seats draw on one evidence pool, so independence here is zero.
- **Partition or union.** This is a union compound, not a partition. Credibility needs an unmoved arbiter and a bounded loss and a writable refusal and an enumerated habitat; a single lever clears one region and leaves the intersections standing.
- **Order by dependency.** Bind the sought-for shape as an ordinary term, pin the settlement so an outcome can be registered, make refusal writable and sticky, name and bound the standing stake, enumerate the habitat, then expect alternation only after the earlier links hold.

Strain test applied: the six physical primitives belong to the substrate domain and do not map directly onto political offices; that mapping is a binding under the membrane rule, not a derivation. No figure line needed forcing after re-census.

## VERDICT (from the figures)

The shape survives; no institution is named and no ground reading was taken. A credible competition requires an unmoved settlement, contenders ranged separately against it, a named and bounded standing stake, a refusal that is writable as negation and sticks, an enumerated habitat with priced bonds, and alternation as a chain of differing handoffs. These are constraints in effect terms, a union compound. The specific institutions are imports from political science with provenance, not figure derivations and not imports that move the wall. Verdict status is `specified`, ungrounded, candidate. Agreement from a shared pool is not corroboration, and this simulated WATSON run is a mirror with a different name.

- cost if wrong: bounded and locatable. The packet states mode, writes its refuters before the conclusion, and records the lineage, so an error is findable rather than invisible. The real cost is spending the independence of outside readers by sending a recording instead of a generator. That cost is avoidable by leading with the shape claim and its refuter.
- undoable: yes. The named institutional bindings can be dropped without touching the shape. A sent claim can be superseded by a corrected packet, and no primitive or figure line has to be withdrawn. The retraction log shows earlier readings already corrected in place.
- reversed by: a ground reading, not agreement. A documented case of credible alternation with a certifying body that moved under pressure and no repairing constraint, or repeated alternation with severe loss and no bound anywhere, would withdraw C1 or C2. An improvement that changes no contact and still moves credibility, or two same-actors different-bond arrangements with indistinguishable behaviour, would withdraw the habitat or arrangement claims. A derivation of a specific institution from the six-term set alone would withdraw the shape-not-a-name limit. Until one fires, the verdict stands as candidate.
