## Two findings in the wider literature that bear on this question

1. **Warning is possible in shape, hard in timing.** Scheffer et al. 2009 say prediction of critical points is *"extremely difficult"* while generic early-warning signals *"may indicate for a wide class of systems if a critical threshold is approaching."* That is the Tether's own split: a shape and a proto-instrument, not a certified date. Cite this when explaining why a warning structure is not a forecast.
2. **The measured risk depends on the topology you assume.** Ramadiah et al. hold balance-sheet information fixed and vary network density and block structure, finding risk properties *"extremely sensitive to its network features."* Combined with May et al.'s *"latent and often unrecognized"* linkages, this is the world-level corroboration of the closure finding: the hidden edges are not decoration, they are the quantity.

- **Fig 10 :52** *"Bring a characterised residual, never the source"* and **Fig 4 :22** *"Only methods cross up. - **Fig 9 :27-28** *"describe it before you go looking, or any frame you pick will seem to fit."* Ask what they would expect to see if the shape claim were true before giving them any source.
- **Fig 7 :13** *"agreement compounds, evidence does not"* and **Fig 2 :40** *"agreement about the world is worthless when the agreeing frames share an evidence pool."* Do not select recipients for likely agreement and do not pool their later agreement as corroboration.

**Recommended order in a reply:** (1) the per-slot shape claim and its refuter; (2) ask what they would count as failure; (3) record their verdict; (4) only then the source links, with the note that the figures, not the summary, are the authority.

## The two-stage offer

- **Stage 1 before stage 2.** Fig 10 :52 and Fig 2 :45 (*"Frames range against the ground. They do not negotiate with it."*). The recipient checks the output without holding the framework's interior.
- **Stage 2 is opt-in by construction.** Seat 4 must not hold the interiors. Once a recipient has absorbed the theory, their agreement stops being evidence. Their verdict on stage 1 must be recorded before any theory is shown.
- **Stage 3 sends a method, which is permitted.** Fig 4 :22: the formula is a generator, so giving it away does not give away an answer. Each user still needs a ground and a pre-registered refuter for their own problem (Fig 5 :66).

**Hard precondition:** stages 1 to 3 only work if the stage-1 output survives. As drafted in `01`, the sharpest form is the shape plus four refuters; do not pad it with a scalar score or a forecast. Repair before sending.
