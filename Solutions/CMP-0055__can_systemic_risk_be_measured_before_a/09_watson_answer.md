## QUESTION

Decide, in effect terms, what the Tether says about measuring systemic risk before a crisis when connections and exposures are partly hidden. Option A: certify safety with a single pre-crisis score built from reported data and model agreement. Option B: keep the per-slot warning shape, refuse the scalar verdict, and name the data import that would close the hidden slots. What each leads to: A delivers a pooled reading that loses the failing slot, mistakes a shared hidden source for independence, and spends the independence of the people checking it; B delivers checkable sub-claims, written refuters, and an open slot until the realized crisis pays.

## DOMAIN CENSUS

R by arity, symmetry and scale, then one line per domain with actors / currency / anchor, partition or union, and order by dependency.

- **R (the residual).** Gap between the model's predicted losses and the crisis's realized losses, indexed by institution, exposure edge and time. Arity: a many-place network relation carrying sequence (cascade step), production (shock produces loss), comparison (failure threshold) and cycle (feedback). Symmetry: directed exposures and asymmetric information; *"systemic"* is a threshold, not an absolute. Scale: single institution loop, exposure network, coupled habitat, plus the time channel from pre-crisis to crisis. The Symbols table states that *"what cannot be perceived yet is residual and simply cannot be read"*; the hidden exposures are exactly that slice.
- **D1 Model loop.** Actors: modelers, models, reported positions. Currency: residual reduction on predicted losses. Anchor: realized loss at the institution. Admitted.
- **D2 Exposure network.** Actors: institutions, counterparties, edges, common holdings. Currency: exposure and contagion paths. Anchor: settlement records and actual defaults. Admitted, partly hidden.
- **D3 Coupled habitat.** Actors: financial, physical and social network layers, feedback, panic. Currency: cascading failure and liquidity. Anchor: the realized system-wide outcome. Admitted.
- **D4 Measurement channel.** Actors: instruments, stress tests, early-warning indicators, disclosure. Currency: contact with the ground. Anchor: the ground, which does not decay. Admitted.
- **X1 A single global scalar.** No anchor independent of the crisis; it is a pooled reading. Not a domain, absorbed into D4 as a failure mode.
- **X2 Model consensus.** Anchor is internal agreement, which moves when pushed. Not a domain; it is the Fig 2 panel 1 or panel 2 collapse.
- **Partition or union.** Union, not partition. D1/D2, D2/D3 and D4/all overlap, so each overlap is a compound with its own loop. Unbundled conditions: C1 perceive the connections; C2 compose the cascade dynamics from what is perceived.
- **Order by dependency.** Perception -> vocabulary -> objective -> planning -> learn and carry (Fig 3). The break is at perception and vocabulary; effort above the break cannot rescue it.

Strain test applied: the crisis is the legitimate anchor even though it arrives after the prediction, because Fig 2 :36 makes an anchor legitimate when the question is constitutively about it. The temporal asymmetry is the measurement problem, not a contradiction (see F5 in `01`). Fig 8 :40-43 distinguishes unreachable-at-this-budget (hidden data, needs import) from underivable-in-principle, so idempotence is not a claim of unknowability.

## VERDICT (from the figures)

**The figure-derived verdict:** systemic risk can be characterized before a crisis, as a per-slot warning structure that tracks where contact with the partly hidden exposure network is failing. It cannot be certified by a single scale, by more computation on held data, or by model agreement. Hidden common exposures are the common-cause collapse, so apparent diversification may be the pool talking; the missing edges require an import of new exposure-level data; the measure stays `candidate` until the realized crisis pays for it; and the question is a union of perception and composition that must be unbundled before any lever is chosen. The exact pre-crisis number is a declared `gap`. Verdicts are kept separate per domain and never averaged. Mode `specified`. World sources corroborate the mechanism shape from lower-overlap fields but did not decide the verdict.

- **Cost if wrong:** real and locatable. A scalar index that certifies safety from held data can hide a live slot, mistake a shared hidden source for diversification, and spend the independence of outside readers by offering a score instead of a check. Fig 2 :42 prices that: "Buy independence in proportion to the cost of being wrong." The cost is bounded because the packet states refuters before conclusions, so an error is findable rather than invisible.
- **Undoable:** yes. No term is deleted if the claim fails; a refutation is added and the reach falls because, in Fig 6 :42-43, something was added rather than anything deleted. The shape and its seven falsifiers are recorded with provenance so a later reader can locate the error (Fig 10 :62-64).
- **Reversed by:** a ground reading, not agreement. Any of the seven pre-registered falsifiers fires: a scalar naming the failing slot prospectively; documented independence of correlated losses; a missing exposure derived without import; an instrument built from description alone; a first-order-only cascade; a same-pool consensus beating disjoint pools; or one lever restoring both conditions. A disjoint-pool instrument that prospectively reads the hidden edges would move the verdict to `grounded`. Prestige or consensus is not such an instrument.
