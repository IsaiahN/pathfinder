## The residual in effect terms

R = |Γ(b,a) - o′| per slot s, where b is the modeler's belief about the exposure network, a is the measurement action, Γ(b,a) is the model's predicted loss, and o′ is what the crisis realizes. The slot is the institution, the exposure edge and the correlated-position cluster. *"Partly hidden"* means some slots carry positive residual that the instrument cannot read, so R is indexed per slot and never collapsed into a global score (Fig 1 :19, :22).

## Headline verdict

A shape survives; a scalar does not. Systemic risk can be characterized before a crisis as a per-slot warning structure, but it cannot be certified by one score, by more computation on held data, or by model agreement. Hidden common exposures are a common-cause collapse (Fig 2 :6-7, :20), missing edges need an import of new data (Fig 8 :32, Fig 6 :23), the measure stays `candidate` until the crisis pays (Fig 2 :29, Fig 10 :24), and the question is a union of perception and composition that must be unbundled (Fig 9 :54-57). The exact pre-crisis number is a declared `gap`.

## What is deliberately not here

- No personal emails of named individuals.
- No ranking of people by how likely they are to believe the claim.
- No bulk-send plan.

Three reasons, in the figures' own words:

1. **A room does not upgrade the label.** Fig 7 :13: *"agreement compounds, evidence does not"*; Fig 2 :35: *"Alignment is triangulation, not negotiation. A mirror cannot correct you."* A recipient list selected for receptiveness manufactures the correlation that would make its agreement worthless.
2. **Correlation disqualifies a verdict.** Fig 2 :40: *"disqualifying for a verdict: agreement about the world is worthless when the agreeing frames share an evidence pool."*
3. **Asking for trust before a check spends independence.** Fig 10 :42: *"A verifier that can reconstruct the claim cannot verify it."* Fig 10 :52: *"Bring a characterised residual, never the source."*

Legally: scraping personal emails for unsolicited bulk outreach breaches GDPR and CAN-SPAM style rules in many jurisdictions. That is a practical failure on top of the structural one.
