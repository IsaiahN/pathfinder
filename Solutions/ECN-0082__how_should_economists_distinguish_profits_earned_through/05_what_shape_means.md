## 1. A shape is a constraint, not a filler

The figures README states the limit: the framework returns "what a missing thing would have to look like (**a shape, not a name**)." Fig 9 :32 "you get a kind of thing, never the answer itself." Fig 13 :135 "It cannot say which primitive is missing, because naming it would mean already holding it."

From *The Library of Babel* [carried]: "You can cut your search short if the set of books returned is small and descriptive enough that it provides a general shape of the answer that satisfies your question."

A **shape** is the narrowed form of an answer you can state before you can name it. It constrains; it does not fill. For this question, the shape is the set of requirements on a discriminator (per slot, non-updating anchor, two low-overlap readings, refined instrument, union compound). The name of a specific statistic, or the percentage split for a named firm, is outside it.

## 2. How a shape is obtained

- *Library of Babel* [carried]: use the vocabulary you already have closest to the thing, look for recurring new terms that overlap with it, recurse. "With each new term gained, you narrow the space and deepen your vocabulary."
- Fig 6 :30 "outward: does another frame already hold it? The description travels. Fails by being sharpened into terms only we can read. Cast it in effect terms." Fig 9 :30-31 "your words are borrowed too, so describe the effect rather than the cause."
- *Decoding Tacit Knowledge* [carried]: "When contact isn't possible, the best you can send is a plain description of what you'd expect to see, because anyone can check that against the world."
- The economics sources supply the effect terms directly. Dibb (2022) describes the difference between "profits that are generated by firms in exchange for the goods they sell and the services they provide" and "the profits they generate simply because of their size or their market power". UBS describes the two-company comparison in the same vein. These are descriptions of effects, which is what can travel.

## 3. What licenses a name

Two things, both outside the shape itself:

1. **The ground settles it.** `THE_FORMULA.md` step 5: "the ground settles it. Verified only by what pays. A gate is not the ground." The README: the framework "stops at the limit of what the available instruments can distinguish, and records the lineage so the stop is honest."
2. **A provenanced import from a frame whose closure differs** (Fig 6 :23 "No frame escapes its own closure. The bound moves only on contact with a frame whose closure differs."; Fig 8 :50-52 "Convergent derivation and adopted import look identical in the contents. Only the record of where each came from separates them") with a source gate and a shadow test.

*They're Scanning Books* [carried] sharpens this: "The real import is not a new term in the existing space. It is a new dimension of the space." And: "A minimum viable set of six terms is a human's careful guess at reality's compression axes; another sensorium's minimum set need not be those six." Also: "Declaring the primitives does not make them neutral. It makes the choice visible."

In this question, naming a natural-experiment design is a **binding** from econometrics onto held relational terms (anchor, independence, import). It is not the invention of a new primitive. The correction below governs that distinction.

## 4. Why "the markup reads the source" is the named forbidden error

*The Hard Problem is About Source* [carried] gives the mechanism. Metamerism: an instrument reads green and "cannot comment on the source", one green light, or yellow plus blue, or RGB, or CMYK, or a scheme not yet discovered. The reading does not determine the source. "If several paths create the same behavior, then behavior is a projection with lossy information." And: "An instrument that can't distinguish these cases demonstrates its own inadequacy." It was the **spectrometer**, not an argument, that resolved it.

The profit or markup figure reads the excess return (the artifact) and names its source (innovation or market power). That is exactly the metamerism error: a source named from a reading where several sources could give the same reading. What is owed is a better instrument, not a name. This is F1 of `01_figure_grounded_audit.md`.

*The Recursive Self-Improvement Trap* [carried]: "the output is only as good as the validator. The agent, or the tool, or the frame, or the theory, cannot validate itself."

## 5. Correction to my own reading of sections 3 and 4

I initially treated "shape, not a name" as forbidding the naming of any instrument. That overstates the rule. The README's phrase is "what a **missing** thing would have to look like (a shape, not a name)." It governs a primitive not held in the library. Here the relational terms it would bind to (residual, anchor, independence, import, refusal) are already held. Naming an exogenous shock is therefore a provenanced binding, not an invented primitive. What remains wrong in the naive reading is still wrong: the markup is a projection and cannot comment on the source. The reason is the missing discriminator, not a ban on naming held terms. Both claims are now recorded so neither is quietly lost.

## 6. Two consequences for this question

**Prestige is not action.** From *The Recursive Self-Improvement Trap* [carried]: "Prestige must never be allowed to validate an action directly... Prestige can fund an action. It can give an action a budget to be tried. But it cannot settle an action. Only the ground can settle it. The Ground Maintainer must be blind to prestige." A panel of well-known economists who agree with the separation is the named collapse, not corroboration. The legitimate ask is to give the separator a budget to be tried and let the ground, an exogenous shock, an entry decision, a diffusion curve, settle it.

**My own audit is a simulation, not a ground reading.** Same essay [carried]: "LLMs can quickly simulate an approximate answer/output... However, the truly testable output would need to be derived from the real architecture or working the formula yourself." LLM output is "simulacra." So `01` is a simulated derivation, checked for internal consistency against the figures. It is not a test of the claim and must be labelled as such. It cannot settle anything it reports on.

## 7. The shape returned here, stated in one line

Any discriminator between innovation profit and market-power rent must be: indexed per firm-slot; based on a reference that does not update on the firm's or the investigator's classification; built from two low-overlap readings that can be ranged against that reference separately; sharpened from an instrument already returning a noisy reading; and applied to a union compound in which the innovate-then-exclude case keeps its own test. The firm-specific number is not returned. That is a shape, and the `gap` where the number would go is recorded rather than filled.
