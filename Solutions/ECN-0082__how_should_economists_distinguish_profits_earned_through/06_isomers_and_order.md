# Are innovation profit and market-power rent isomers? Structure, order and analogues

**Mode: `specified`.** This is a simulated derivation checked against the figures, not a ground reading (see `05_what_shape_means.md` section 6).

## RESIDUAL

The observation is one excess return at one firm-slot. Two sources can produce it. The question asks what structural relation holds between them and whether a single reading can carry the difference. Two sub-questions are owed: (1) over a fixed set of observable ingredients, are the two profits **isomers** under Fig 12's definition; (2) what **order** distinguishes a newly created lead from a maintained position?

## DOMAIN CENSUS

Atoms held in the measurement frame: the excess return, the cost base, the time path of entry and imitation, the legal position (patent or licence), the consumer-welfare reading. Bonds held: the seven plus negation. No new primitive is available without an import (a natural-experiment design). No claim is made that these are the six physical terms of Fig 13; only the loop and the relational rules are borrowed (Fig 4 :20 "Only the actors, the currency and the anchor change.").

## DERIVATION

### Q1: The isomer claim

Fig 12 :117: **"isomer. Same atoms, different bond, different substance. The difference is invisible in the formula and decisive in the behaviour."** Fig 12 :95-96: **"Two entries with an identical ingredient set are not variants of one thing. They are different things whose notation cannot tell them apart, and the difference is carried entirely by the bond."**

Write both sources over the shared observable set {excess return, cost base, time path, legal position}:

- Innovation profit: a new combination lowers cost or raises value; the lead is temporary and decays as imitators arrive. Its bond to the time path is a sequence that ends in diffusion.
- Market-power rent: a maintained position restricts output; the surplus persists while entry is blocked. Its bond to the time path is a standing relation, not a sequence.

Same ingredients, different bond, different substance. The profit figure records the ingredients and not the bond, so nothing downstream can tell them apart from the figure alone (Fig 12 :14-16 "the ingredients are recorded and the arrangement is not nothing downstream can tell them apart, and nothing signals that anything was lost"). The behaviour discriminates: one diffuses, the other persists.

The chemistry analogue is not a metaphor. Fig 12 :97-98: **"This is not an analogy. Chemistry hit it first: silver fulminate and silver cyanate have the same composition and different properties, which is what forced structure into the notation."** Just as composition alone could not tell fulminate from cyanate, a markup alone cannot tell a Schumpeterian lead from an exclusionary rent.

**Falsifier, written before the run:** if either source can be written without an atom the other requires (for example, market power with no legal or structural entry barrier, or innovation profit with no time path of imitation), or if one is a strict subset of the other over the shared set, the isomer claim fails and the functional-group reading below stands instead.

### Q2: The functional-group claim (weaker, always available)

Fig 12 :123: **"functional group. A substructure whose presence fixes the outcome. The group says what kind; the qualifier says which member."** Fig 12 :124 "the group says what kind; the qualifier says which member."

If the fixed-atom condition of Q1 fails, the two profits still share a functional group: both are "an excess over the return required to keep the factor in place", the standard definition of economic rent recorded in the world sources. The qualifier (innovation versus maintained position) says which member. This is a real, checkable structural relation and does not need the shared-set condition. It is the fallback verdict, not the stronger claim.

### Q3: Order: sequence versus convention

The seven bonds include an ordered one. Fig 12 :71-72 (Operators table :33): **"A then B; B needs A to have happened"** with the test "swap them. Does it become a different thing? Yes." Innovation profit is ordered: the new combination must happen before the lead, and the lead before diffusion. Swap them and you no longer have the same object.

A maintained position is not well read as a sequence. Its nearest figure is the convention that has stopped being a convention once it decides an outcome (Fig 10 :47 "a convention that decides an outcome has stopped being one."; Fig 2 :43). A standing rule that sets price above cost is a verdict about who gets the surplus, not a step in a route. The distinction between the two sources is therefore partly a **bond-type** distinction, not a magnitude distinction: sequence versus standing threshold. This is why a larger reading cannot choose the source.

Fig 12 :111 "A vocabulary without bonds can name a state and cannot name a route to one." A profit figure without a bond vocabulary can name the state (an excess return) and cannot name the route to it.

### Q4: Refusing a source is not subtracting an amount

Operators table :73-74: "− is A with B removed or absent, which is the absence of an operand. Negation is the refusal of a claim." Operators :58 "a refutation is a negation, which is why a vocabulary without it can only accumulate." To classify a rent as market power is not to subtract a number from profit; it is to refuse the claim that the whole excess was earned by value creation. A vocabulary with only subtraction can produce a smaller profit figure, not a corrected source attribution. This is the type point behind F6 of `01`: a threshold that decides an outcome is a verdict, and refusing it requires negation, not arithmetic.

### Q5: Analogues from the literature, ranked by overlap

- **Creative destruction as a cycle** (Schumpeterian growth literature, via the growth-book chapter and Wikipedia's Schumpeterian rent entry): the output of one innovation re-enters as the input that destroys the incumbent's rent. Fig 12 :121 "cycle. The output re-enters as input. The sign of the loop decides whether it closes, and a chain cannot close by construction." Overlap with the figure is high but the object is genuinely different from the physical substrate.
- **Catalyst** (Fig 12 :119 "catalyst. A part that survives its own use. Spent and regenerating parts behave differently across uses, and nothing records which a term is."): a legal position can survive the innovation that earned it and continue yielding after the lead has decayed. This is the innovate-then-exclude compound in F5, and it is why the union needs its own loop.
- **Three-rent taxonomy** (Teece and Coleman, as reported in the laweconcenter source): scarcity, Schumpeterian and monopoly rents are three members of one functional group with different qualifiers. This corroborates Q2, not Q1, because the three need not share a fixed atom set.
- **Rent-seeking transfers** (UBS, Tullock 1967, Krueger 1974): the effort spent winning a privilege consumes resources and creates nothing, so the reading (excess return) and the social effect (transfer plus waste) come apart. This corroborates the behaviour-discriminates claim in Q1.

Overlap declared: the Schumpeterian and rent sources are one broad economics evidence pool, not independent witnesses. The ecology instrumental-variables source is the genuinely low-overlap corroborator for the instrument claim, not for the isomer claim.

## CONCLUSION

Over a fixed set of observable ingredients, innovation profit and market-power rent are **isomers**: same ingredients, different bond, different substance, with the difference invisible in the profit figure and decisive in behaviour. If the fixed-set condition fails, the weaker **functional-group** claim still holds: both are an excess over opportunity cost, qualified by source. The order that separates them is bond type, sequence for a decaying lead versus a standing threshold for a maintained position, so magnitude cannot choose between them. Refusing a source attribution is a negation, not a subtraction. The fixed-atom condition on the isomer claim is a live strain, named here and carried into `09` and `11`.
