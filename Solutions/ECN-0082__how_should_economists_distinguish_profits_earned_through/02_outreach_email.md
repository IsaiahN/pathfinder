# Outreach draft: check the separator before considering the framework

**Use:** one-to-one, or as a cover letter attached to a submission through the public venues in `03_public_contact_points.csv`. **Not** for bulk send.

**Hard precondition:** do not send until the three checkable sub-claims in `08_expert_verification_packet.md` survive a first read. The offer collapses on contact if the stage-1 output does not hold.

---

**Subject:** Would this separation of innovation profit from market-power rent survive your check?

> Hi [name],
>
> I have one specific methodological output I would like you to check against what you already know. I am not asking you to read the framework first, and I am not asking you to agree with anything. If the output does not survive, there is nothing else to look at.
>
> **The output (`specified`, not `grounded`, and one part declared `gap`):** an observed excess return at a firm-slot is a single reading that is consistent with at least two sources, value newly created through innovation and surplus transferred through a maintained position. The profit or markup figure cannot say which produced it. So the separator is not a number, it is a set of requirements on any instrument that claims to do the job:
>
> 1. It must be indexed per firm-slot, not applied to a sector average, because an average hides the very heterogeneity the attribution needs.
> 2. It must rest on a reference that does not update when the firm or the investigator changes its story, for example an exogenous entry or tax shock rather than the firm's own reported markup.
> 3. It must be built from two readings that do not draw on the same evidence, so that their agreement means something.
> 4. It must refine an instrument already returning a noisy reading, not be specified from a definition.
> 5. It must treat the innovate-then-exclude case as a compound with its own test, not force the two sources into separate piles.
>
> **What I am not claiming:** that any particular firm's profit is rent, that a given markup threshold identifies market power, that a named statistic is the discriminator, or that there is a universal percentage split. The framework returns the shape a discriminator must have, not the name of one. The firm-specific attribution is recorded as a gap, and "unreachable is a verdict, not a silence." A threshold that fixes a verdict has stopped being a convention, so I keep the two apart.
>
> **What would refute the shape:**
> 1. a single observable statistic at the slot, with no independent source-discriminating instrument, that reliably separates the two sources across cases;
> 2. evidence that the two sources are mutually exclusive and exhaustive at the slot, so there is no compound case;
> 3. an anchor that moves with the firm's own report or that shifts both sources together;
> 4. entry, diffusion and consumer-welfare readings that do not discriminate the two behaviours at the ground.
>
> I wrote these down before sending this. If any one holds, I will narrow or retract the separator. A one-line "this fails because..." is more useful to me than agreement. If you would rather not engage, no reply is needed.
>
> **If it survives, and only then:** the sources are below. The figures are the authority, not my summary of them.
> - Canonical figures and formula (public repository, branch `general-agent`): https://github.com/IsaiahN/tether/tree/general-agent/figures
> - How to read the figures (canonical usage rule): https://github.com/IsaiahN/tether/blob/general-agent/figures/README.md
> - The eight essays behind the "shape" reading: https://adventuresinml.substack.com/p/the-hard-problem-is-about-source
>
> **If you want to use the machinery:** the formula is in the figures link. Run it on a problem you know well and can check against a ground. Write down what would refute your result *before* you run it, and state the mode of what you get. A reported failure is more useful to me than a reported success.
>
> Thanks,
> [name]

## Two-stage offer, stated plainly

**Stage 1.** Send the five separator requirements and the four refuters. Ask what the recipient would count as a failure. Record that verdict before any theory is shown.

**Stage 2 (opt-in only).** If they are curious, offer the three links and note that the figures, not the summary, are the authority. Their later agreement is never pooled as corroboration (Fig 2 :40 "agreement about the world is worthless when the agreeing frames share an evidence pool").

**Stage 3.** Offer the formula as a method to reuse, with the instruction that each person writes their own refuter and states their own mode.
