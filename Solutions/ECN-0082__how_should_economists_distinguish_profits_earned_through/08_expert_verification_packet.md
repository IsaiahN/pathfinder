# Expert verification packet: the Tether on innovation profit versus market-power rent

**What this is:** a claim in a form an economist or competition expert can check. Mode, derivation and refuters are stated before the conclusion, so a reader knows what to look for and what would settle it.

**Mode: `specified` on the separation, `general` on the structural finding, `gap` on the firm-specific number, not `grounded`.** No claim about any named firm is made.

**Status: SIMULATED DERIVATION, not a ground reading.** This packet is checked for internal consistency against the figures. It cannot settle the question it reports on.

## 1. The claim, in one paragraph

An observed excess return at one firm-slot is not its own source. Innovation (a new combination whose lead decays through diffusion) and market power (a maintained position that restricts output) can produce the same reading, so a profit or markup figure cannot decide between them. Any discriminator must therefore satisfy five requirements: be per slot; rest on a non-updating anchor; use two readings that do not share an evidence pool; sharpen an instrument already returning a noisy reading; and treat the innovate-then-exclude case as a compound with its own test. A classification threshold that fixes an outcome is a verdict, not a convention. The framework returns this shape and a set of refuters; it does not return a universal percentage or a firm-specific attribution, which is recorded as a gap.

## 2. Three checkable sub-claims

### C1: framework-internal, checkable by reading Figures 12 and 9

- **Statement:** the profit reading is not the source; the two sources are isomers over the same observable and the question is a union compound.
- **Source, verbatim:** Fig 12 :117 "isomer. Same atoms, different bond, different substance. The difference is invisible in the formula and decisive in the behaviour." Fig 9 :55 "A bundled problem is usually a union of overlapping conditions, not a list of separate ones."
- **Refuter:** show a single observable statistic at the slot that, with no independent source-discriminating instrument, reliably separates the two sources across cases; or show the two categories are mutually exclusive and exhaustive at the slot, so no compound exists. Either breaks the claim.

### C2: measurement-facing, checkable by an economist against the estimation practice

- **Statement:** separation requires a per-slot, non-updating, low-overlap instrument refined from a worse one, not a pooled figure.
- **Source, verbatim:** Fig 1 :19 "The residual is indexed by object slot, not aggregated." Fig 2 :29 "An anchor is whatever settles the question and does not change its answer because of what any frame thinks." Fig 6 :32 "An instrument is not built from a description. It is improved from a worse instrument already returning something."
- **What to check:** does a sector-average markup hide the heterogeneity that attribution needs? Does the proposed anchor move when the firm's own report or the investigator's classification changes? Is the instrument an improvement on an existing noisy reading, or a definition with no prior instrument?
- **Refuter:** a sector-average figure that reliably classifies each member firm's source; or an anchor that moves with the firm's own report or shifts both sources together; or a proposed discriminator with no prior instrument it refines.

### C3: policy-facing, checkable by a competition expert

- **Statement:** a classification threshold that decides an outcome has stopped being a convention; refusing a source attribution is a negation, not a subtraction.
- **Source, verbatim:** Fig 10 :47 "a convention that decides an outcome has stopped being one." Operators table :58 "a refutation is a negation, which is why a vocabulary without it can only accumulate."
- **What to check:** can the threshold be violated by entry, diffusion or consumer-welfare readings without changing the outcome it was used to justify? Does the classification refuse a claim (the whole excess was earned by value creation) rather than merely report a smaller number?
- **Refuter:** a threshold that remains a convention after it fires, so that the ground can contradict it without altering the decision; or a classification vocabulary that can only accumulate and never refuse.

## 3. What is explicitly not claimed

- Not a verdict on any named firm, and not a percentage split.
- Not that a markup, R&D ratio or patent count is the discriminator.
- Not that the two sources never co-occur; the union and the compound case are central.
- Not that "rent" is always unearned. Tullock's "good rents" and Schumpeterian-rent-as-incentive are recorded (UBS Nobel Perspectives; Wikipedia, *Schumpeterian rent*).
- Not `grounded`. Nothing here is asserted as settled by experiment.

## 5. What would settle it

1. **Diffusion test:** does the excess return decay on an imitation timescale (innovation) or persist while entry is blocked (market power)?
2. **Exogenous-shock test:** does a shock plausibly moving only one source move the reading in the predicted direction?
3. **Threshold Test:** can the classification be contradicted by the ground without changing the decision it justified?
4. **Standing falsifiers:** the four conditions in section 2, and the full list in `01` section 4.

## 6. What an expert can check in twenty minutes

1. Read Fig 12 :117, Fig 9 :55, Fig 1 :19, Fig 2 :29, Fig 6 :32, Fig 10 :47. Six short passages.
2. Check C2 against the markup-estimation practice you know: is the non-updating-anchor requirement already met by any standard estimator, or not?
3. Name one instrument that would discriminate a decaying lead from a maintained exclusion at the firm-slot, or say why none is possible.
4. Send the refutation. A failure is more useful than an endorsement.
