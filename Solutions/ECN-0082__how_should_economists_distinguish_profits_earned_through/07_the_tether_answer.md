# The Tether's answer: distinguishing innovation profit from market-power rent

**Mode: `specified` on the separation, `general` on the structural finding, `gap` on the firm-specific numerical split, not `grounded`.** Simulated derivation checked against the figures (`05_what_shape_means.md` section 6), not a ground reading.

## 1. The residual it answers

An economist observes one above-normal return at one firm-slot. The reading is consistent with at least two sources: value newly created (a temporary Schumpeterian lead that diffuses) and surplus transferred (a maintained position that restricts output). The profit or markup figure cannot say which produced it. Fig 12 :117 "isomer. Same atoms, different bond, different substance. The difference is invisible in the formula and decisive in the behaviour." The question is therefore not "what is the number" but "what would discriminate the source". The answer is a separator, not a statistic.

## 2. The shape

1. **The reading is not the source.** An excess return is a projection; several sources can give the same reading. Fig 9 :32 "you get a kind of thing, never the answer itself." What is owed is a source-discriminating instrument, not a larger reading.
2. **Index per firm-slot, never a sector average.** Fig 1 :19 "The residual is indexed by object slot, not aggregated." Fig 1 :22 "Aggregating across slots is how a live signal disappears into an average." The measurement literature agrees: aggregate markups can rise while the median is unchanged, so the pool hides the heterogeneity the attribution needs.
3. **Use a non-updating anchor and two low-overlap readings.** Fig 2 :29 "An anchor is whatever settles the question and does not change its answer because of what any frame thinks." Fig 2 :33 "The anchor must not update." Fig 2 :35 "Alignment is triangulation, not negotiation. A mirror cannot correct you." A markup from the firm's own report and a classification by the same investigator are one pool talking. Exogenous variation, entry and diffusion, and consumer-welfare readings supply the separation.
4. **Sharpen an instrument already returning something.** Fig 6 :32 "An instrument is not built from a description. It is improved from a worse instrument already returning something." Cost-share versus production-function elasticities, overhead treatment and sample selection are the live sharpening work; the published disagreement is evidence for this requirement, not against the possibility of separation.
5. **Treat the question as a union, not a partition.** Fig 9 :55 "A bundled problem is usually a union of overlapping conditions, not a list of separate ones." A firm can innovate and then exclude. The innovate-then-exclude compound (a catalyst in Fig 12 :119's sense: a part that survives its own use) needs its own test.
6. **Keep the classification convention and the verdict apart.** Fig 10 :47 "a convention that decides an outcome has stopped being one." A threshold that fixes a tax or an antitrust finding is a verdict wearing a convention's clothes. Refusing a source claim is a negation, not a subtraction (Operators table :58 "a refutation is a negation, which is why a vocabulary without it can only accumulate").
7. **The specific split is a gap.** Fig 13 :137-138 "it can say the answer is out of reach with the library as it stands, so something has to be imported before it is asked again." Fig 13 :135 "It cannot say which primitive is missing, because naming it would mean already holding it." No figure line returns a universal percentage or names the discriminator. "Unreachable is a verdict here, not a silence," (Fig 13 :134).
8. **Keep verdicts per domain.** Fig 9 :60 "Use filters for the budget and witnesses for the verdict. Never let a filter hand you a verdict." Measurement, firm and policy domains have different anchors; their verdicts are never averaged into an "X percent is rent" claim.

## 3. What it explicitly does not say

- Not that any named firm's profit is rent, and not a number for the split.
- Not that a markup threshold identifies market power, or that a single statistic separates the sources.
- Not that the two sources are mutually exclusive; the union includes the compound case.
- Not that "rent" is always bad. Valence is relative to the residual (Fig 11 :49 "An actor's valence is relative to the residual, not a property it carries."; Fig 11 :51 "No standing list of good actors survives a change of residual."). Tullock's "good rents" and the Schumpeterian-rent-as-incentive literature are recorded.
- Not a claim that agreement among economists settles the source. Fig 2 :35, Fig 7 :35.
- Not `grounded`. No ground reading was taken in this derivation.

## 4. What would settle it: the ground

A source-discriminating instrument, not a further argument. From *The Hard Problem is About Source*: an instrument that reads green "cannot comment on the source"; the spectrometer resolved it. Here the corresponding tests are:

1. **Diffusion test:** does the excess return decay on a predictable imitation timescale (innovation) or persist while entry is blocked (market power)? An instrument that discriminates these would name the source at the slot.
2. **Exogenous-shock test:** does a shock that plausibly moves only one source (an entry deregulation, a patent expiry, a cost-shock) move the reading in the predicted direction? Angrist and Krueger's "forces of nature or government policy" variant of a randomized experiment is the world-side form.
3. **Threshold Test:** can a classification rule be violated by the ground without changing the outcome it was used to justify? If not, it is a verdict, not a convention (Fig 10 :47).
4. **Standing falsifiers:** the four conditions in `01` section 4 and `08` section 2, written before the run.

## 5. The honest stop

**Reached as a shape, unreached as a number.** The separator's requirements are figure-grounded, but the firm-specific attribution is not returned by any figure line (Fig 9 :32, Fig 13 :134). This is the unreachable-at-this-budget kind of gap, not a claim that no instrument could ever discriminate (Fig 8 :39 "So a search that finds nothing is never a proof of absence."). The lineage is recorded so the stop is checkable. "Indiscernibility is not identity."

A live strain is kept visible: the isomer claim of `06` holds over a fixed set of observable ingredients. Where that fixed-set condition fails, the weaker functional-group claim stands. No strain is smoothed into a stronger verdict.

## 6. One-paragraph version for outreach

> The Tether does not tell you that a given profit is rent, and it returns no universal percentage. It says that an observed excess return is not its own source: innovation and market power can produce the same reading, so the difference is invisible in the profit figure and decisive in behaviour. Any discriminator must therefore be indexed per firm-slot, rest on an anchor that does not update when the firm or the investigator changes its story, use two readings that do not share an evidence pool, sharpen an instrument already returning a noisy reading, and keep the innovate-then-exclude compound as a case with its own test. A classification threshold that fixes an outcome has stopped being a convention. The firm-specific split is a declared gap, recorded rather than filled. What is offered is the shape any answer must have, four refuters written in advance, and the instruments that would close it. The attribution stays open until the ground settles it.
