## QUESTION

Decide, in effect terms, what would let an economist distinguish a profit earned through innovation from a rent earned through market power. Option A: treat the observed profit or markup at a firm-slot as the discriminator. Option B: require a source-discriminating instrument indexed per slot, resting on a non-updating anchor, built from two low-overlap readings, refined from a worse instrument, with the innovate-then-exclude compound tested on its own. What each leads to: A returns a number consistent with both sources and spends the independence of the people checking it; B returns a checkable separator, written refuters and an honest gap where the firm-specific number would go.

## DOMAIN CENSUS

R by arity, symmetry and scale, then one line per domain with actors, currency and anchor; partition or union; order by dependency.

- **R, the residual.** One observed excess return at one firm-slot. Arity: one reading, at least two candidate sources, so a union compound (Fig 9 :55 "A bundled problem is usually a union of overlapping conditions, not a list of separate ones."). Symmetry: the reading is symmetric while the mechanisms are ordered, so a symmetric number cannot choose an ordered cause (Fig 2 :39 "Correlation has valence."). Scale: firm-slot, never a sector average (Fig 1 :19 "The residual is indexed by object slot, not aggregated.").
- **Domain 1, measurement and econometrics.** Actors: economists, accounting statements, markup estimators, R&D and patent counts, natural experiments. Currency: a measurement residual priced in bits and actions (Fig 12 :125 "A composition costs in two currencies, and they do not add."). Anchor: an exogenous shock, entry and diffusion, consumer welfare, which does not update on the classification. Legitimate because the question is about market outcomes.
- **Domain 2, firm and strategy.** Actors: innovator, incumbent, imitators, patent. Currency: appropriated surplus. Anchor: the market outcome. The firm's own accounting report is a reading produced inside the frame, not the anchor.
- **Domain 3, verification and policy.** Actors: regulators, courts, reviewers. Currency: verdicts and residuals. Anchor: consumer welfare and the ground, never agreement (Fig 2 :35 "Alignment is triangulation, not negotiation. A mirror cannot correct you."). In this simulated run all three domains draw on one evidence pool, so this domain's independence is low and its agreement carries no witness weight.
- **Order by dependency.** Read the slot, describe the two mechanisms before choosing a discriminator (Fig 9 :27-28 "describe it before you go looking, or any frame you pick will seem to fit"), sharpen an existing instrument (Fig 6 :32), then settle each domain separately.

Union, not partition. Order by dependency, not importance (Fig 3 :20). Strain test applied: the Fig 13 physical six-term substrate governs physical states, not economic categories; only the loop and relational rules are borrowed (Fig 4 :20 "Only the actors, the currency and the anchor change."). Different objects, so no contradiction (see `01` section 7).

## VERDICT (from the figures)

The separator shape survives; the number does not. An excess return is not its own source (Fig 12 :117), the residual is per slot (Fig 1 :19, :22), separation requires a non-updating anchor and two low-overlap readings (Fig 2 :29, :33, :35), the instrument is sharpened from a worse one (Fig 6 :32), the question is a union with an innovate-then-exclude compound (Fig 9 :55), a verdict-fixing threshold has stopped being a convention (Fig 10 :47), and the firm-specific split is a declared `gap` (Fig 13 :134). Verdicts stay per domain and are never averaged (Fig 9 :60). What does not survive is any claim that a single statistic, a panel's agreement or a declared definition settles the source. Mode `specified` on the separation, `general` on structure, `gap` on the number, not `grounded`.

- **cost if wrong:** bounded and locatable. The packet states its mode, writes its refuters before the conclusion and records the lineage, so an error is findable rather than invisible. The real cost is spending the independence of outside readers by sending a conclusion about a firm instead of a separator, which Fig 2 :42 prices: "Buy independence in proportion to the cost of being wrong." That cost is avoidable by leading with the separator and its refuter.
- **undoable:** yes. The number is not asserted, so nothing numerical has to be withdrawn. The separator can be sharpened, the union can be split further, and the fixed-atom condition on the isomer claim can be narrowed to the functional-group claim, without touching the core shape.
- **reversed by:** a ground reading, not agreement. A diffusion or entry instrument that discriminates a decaying lead from a maintained exclusion at the slot would name the source and close the gap. A demonstration that a single statistic separates the sources would break the isomer finding; evidence of a clean partition would break the union finding; a figure re-census returning a universal split would break the gap finding. Until one of these fires, the verdict stands as `candidate`.
