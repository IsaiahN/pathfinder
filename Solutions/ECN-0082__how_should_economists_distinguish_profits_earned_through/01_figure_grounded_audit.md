## 0. The residual in effect terms

An economist faces one observed reading per firm-slot: an above-normal return, a markup, an excess over opportunity cost. That reading is **underdetermined**. Two mechanisms can produce the same reading:

- **Innovation:** a new combination creates value; the innovator earns a temporary lead that diffuses as imitators arrive (Schumpeterian rent).
- **Market power:** a maintained position restricts output and transfers surplus from buyers to the incumbent (monopoly or rent-seeking rent).

The reading cannot say which source produced it. The question "how should economists distinguish" is therefore a **source-discrimination problem**: what instrument, applied at what level, with what anchor, would separate the two?

Option A: treat the profit or markup number as the discriminator (a larger reading means more market power). Option B: require a source-discriminating instrument, indexed per slot, resting on a non-updating anchor, with the two mechanisms described before the instrument is chosen. A returns a quantity that is consistent with both sources. B returns a separation with a stated failure condition and stops at a gap where the firm-specific split is not returned.

The question is **methodological** ("how should"), so the deliverable is a separator plus a refuter, not a verdict on any named firm.

## 1. Domain census (before any figure line is chosen)

Describe R by arity, symmetry and scale, then one line per domain with actors, currency and anchor. No nameable anchor means it is not a domain yet.

**R, the residual.**
- Arity: one observed reading with at least two candidate sources. This is a union compound, not a single hard question (Fig 9 :57 "What remains is smaller and it is also categorically different: a case in two conditions is not a harder case of either.").
- Symmetry: the reading is symmetric (a dollar of profit is a dollar of profit whichever way it was made), but the mechanisms are not. Innovation is an ordered, disequilibrium sequence that ends in diffusion; a maintained position is a standing relation. A symmetric reading cannot choose an ordered cause (Fig 2 :39 "Correlation has valence.").
- Scale: firm-slot. Fig 1 :19 "The residual is indexed by object slot, not aggregated." Fig 1 :22 "Aggregating across slots is how a live signal disappears into an average." A sector-wide markup is the wrong object.

**Domain 1, the measurement and econometrics frame.**
- Actors: economists, accounting statements, cost-share and production-function markup estimators, R&D and patent counts, natural experiments.
- Currency: a measurement residual R, priced in both bits (a description) and actions (the cost of finding out whether it holds). Fig 12 :125 "A composition costs in two currencies, and they do not add."
- Anchor: a ground that does not update on the investigator's classification: an exogenous shock, entry and diffusion, consumer welfare. Fig 2 :29 "An anchor is whatever settles the question and does not change its answer because of what any frame thinks."
- Failure mode: instruments can be wrong on axes they do not measure (Fig 2 :37-38). This domain has an anchor, so it is a domain.

**Domain 2, the firm and strategy frame.**
- Actors: the innovating firm, the incumbent, imitators, the patent or licence.
- Currency: appropriated surplus.
- Anchor: the market outcome (entry, imitation, price and output), which is a legitimate anchor here because the question is constitutively about market outcomes (Fig 2 :36 "An anchor is legitimate when the question is constitutively about it.").
- Failure mode: the firm's own accounting report is not the anchor, it is a reading produced inside the frame being checked.

**Domain 3, the verification and policy frame.**
- Actors: regulators, competition authorities, courts, journal reviewers.
- Currency: verdicts and residuals.
- Anchor: consumer welfare and the ground, never agreement. Fig 2 :35 "Alignment is triangulation, not negotiation. A mirror cannot correct you."
- Failure mode: a panel selected for agreement is a room, not an anchor (Fig 7 :35 "A room's agreement is variation, not a verdict. Selection between rooms is where the ground votes."). Curators have an unavoidable interest in the outcome, which must be declared (Fig 10 :59).

**Partition or union.** Union, not partition. A firm can earn a Schumpeterian lead and hold a monopoly position at the same time; the same patent is both a reward to innovation and a legal exclusion. Fig 9 :55 "A bundled problem is usually a union of overlapping conditions, not a list of separate ones." Fig 9 :54 "And when the split lands on a union rather than a partition, unbundle before you search again." The intersection is a specific compound with its own loop, cleared by no single lever. This is the first structural finding, not an afterthought.

**Order by dependency (Fig 3).**
1. Perception: can the instrument even read an excess return at this slot with a non-updating reference? Fig 3 :27 "A link with no instrument is not a link that works. It is a link nobody has looked at."
2. Vocabulary: are there terms for "newly created" and "transferred" that are not just the profit figure under two names? Fig 3 :7 "it has terms for what things afford: what can be moved, opened, joined, blocked".
3. Objective: can the economist say what would count as progress toward separation in their own terms? Fig 3 :10 "it can say what would count as progress here, in its own terms".
4. Then plan the discriminator, then settle. Effort spent above a broken middle link arrives nowhere (Fig 3 :19 "Optimising a link that already works cannot rescue one that does not.").

The chain is ordered by dependency, not importance (Fig 3 :20 "The chain is ordered by dependency, not by importance."). Most measurement effort in this literature lands on the markup estimator (step 1) while the middle link, what would count as a discrimination, has no instrument. That is the link to examine first.

## 3. Findings

### F1: the reading is not the source (decisive, triangulated)

The profit or markup figure is a projection. It cannot comment on the source that produced it. Fig 12 :117:

> **"isomer. Same atoms, different bond, different substance. The difference is invisible in the formula and decisive in the behaviour."**

Fig 12 :95-96:

> **"Two entries with an identical ingredient set are not variants of one thing. They are different things whose notation cannot tell them apart, and the difference is carried entirely by the bond."**

Innovation profit and market-power rent, read at one slot, are isomers over the same observable: same ingredients (an excess return), different bond (a newly created lead versus a maintained exclusion), different substance. The difference is invisible in the profit figure and decisive in the behaviour: one diffuses as imitators arrive, the other persists by restricting entry. The world source *The Hard Problem is About Source* names the general error: an instrument that reads green "cannot comment on the source"; several sources can give the same reading. The remedy is a source-discriminating instrument, not a larger reading (Fig 9 :32 "you get a kind of thing, never the answer itself").

**Refuter:** an observable statistic at the slot that, without any independent source-discriminating instrument, reliably assigns source across cases; or a case in which the reading alone determines the source univocally. Either would break the isomer/metamerism finding.

### F2: per slot, never aggregated (triangulated)

Fig 1 :19 and :22 (quoted above) set the scale. Fig 2 :40 says "agreement about the world is worthless when the agreeing frames share an evidence pool". The world literature supplies the corroborating shape: De Loecker, Eeckhout and Unger (2020, QJE) report that the rise in the aggregate markup is "driven mainly by the upper tail of the markup distribution: the upper percentiles have increased sharply. Quite strikingly, the median is unchanged." An aggregate hides the very heterogeneity that a source attribution needs. Tax Justice Network (Dibb 2022) adds that headline corporation tax "does not distinguish between 'productive profits' and rents from market power", a second instance of a pooled figure erasing the distinction.

**Refuter:** evidence that a sector-average figure reliably classifies each member firm's source, so that the per-slot requirement adds nothing.

### F3: a non-updating anchor and independent readings are required (triangulated)

Fig 2 :29 (quoted), :33 "The anchor must not update.", :35 "Alignment is triangulation, not negotiation. A mirror cannot correct you.", :42 "Buy independence in proportion to the cost of being wrong."

A markup computed from the firm's own income statement, then used by the same investigator to classify that firm's profit as rent, is one frame reading one pool. It is Fig 2 panel 1 or panel 2, not panel 3. Two genuinely separate readings are needed: for example an input-demand cost-share markup and an exogenous entry or tax shock, each ranged against a ground that neither can move. The world source Angrist and Krueger (2001, Journal of Economic Perspectives) describes the relevant instrument: "the forces of nature or government policy have conspired to produce an environment somewhat akin to a randomized experiment." The ecology source (Fox 2012, Dynamic Ecology) says "more data" is not the answer: what is needed is "the right sort of data", variation generated by an instrument, because "more of the wrong sort of data on variability in A can actually be harmful".

**Refuter:** an instrument whose value moves with the firm's own report, or that shifts both candidate sources together, so that the two readings share the evidence pool and the separation collapses.

### F4: the instrument is sharpened from a worse one already returning something (triangulated)

Fig 6 :32 "An instrument is not built from a description. It is improved from a worse instrument already returning something." Fig 6 :33 "Nobody specified a telescope from an account of Mars. A wandering point in the sky was already a reading, and every sharper instrument was built on the residual the last one produced."

This forbids designing a perfect "innovation detector" from a verbal definition. The practice is to start from something already returning a noisy reading, a cost-share markup, an R&D-to-sales ratio, a patent count, a measured output elasticity, and sharpen it against the residual it leaves. The measurement controversy around De Loecker, Eeckhout and Unger is exactly this: production-function versus cost-share elasticities, overhead treated as an input, sample selection on near-zero-sales biotech firms. Nordhaus (2004, Cowles) is an earlier instance: he derives equations for Schumpeterian profits, then estimates that "innovators are able to capture about 2.2 percent of the total social surplus from innovation", a number produced by an instrument, not by a definition.

**Refuter:** a proposed discriminator with no prior instrument it refines, or one that cannot say what residual the previous instrument left.

### F5: the question is a union, not a partition (triangulated)

Fig 9 :54, :55 (quoted), :57 "What remains is smaller and it is also categorically different: a case in two conditions is not a harder case of either."

A firm may be both innovative and monopolistic. The laweconcenter source (Teece and Coleman taxonomy as reported in *Towards a Theory of Market Power*, 2020) lists three kinds of rent: scarcity (Ricardian), Schumpeterian (product innovation), and monopoly; it states that "Monopoly rent lacks justification and results from exclusionary conduct, predatory conduct, or government-conferred privilege" while Schumpeterian rent arises "when rivals have not yet matched the surplus that the leading firm provides to consumers." UBS Nobel Perspectives records Tullock's line between "good rents", the rewards from real innovation in a competitive market, and "bad rent-seeking". The intersection, an innovate-then-exclude strategy, is a compound requiring its own loop. One lever ("look at markups", "look at R&D") clears only the region where one condition holds and leaves the intersection standing.

**Refuter:** a demonstration that the two categories are mutually exclusive and exhaustive at the slot, so that no compound exists and a partition is correct.

### F6: a threshold that decides an outcome has stopped being a convention (single-source on the figure, corroborated)

Fig 2 :43 "Guard, from Figure 10: a convention that decides an outcome has stopped being one." Fig 10 :47 "a convention that decides an outcome has stopped being one." Fig 10 :49 "Declaring a meaning does not ground it."

A rule such as "any markup above X is rent" used to levy a tax or open an antitrust case is not a shared meaning, it is a verdict wearing a convention's clothes. The Tax Justice Network source is candid about this: headline corporate tax "does not distinguish between 'productive profits' and rents from market power", and the policy response proposed is to build a toolkit to determine which firms operate with significant market power. A declared definition cannot do the discriminating that the ground must do.

**Refuter:** a threshold that the ground can violate without changing the outcome it was used to justify, so that it remains a convention even after it fires.

### F7: no figure line returns the firm-specific split (gap, triangulated)

Fig 9 :32 "you get a kind of thing, never the answer itself." Fig 8 :39 "So a search that finds nothing is never a proof of absence." Fig 13 :137-138 "it can say the answer is out of reach with the library as it stands, so something has to be imported before it is asked again." Fig 13 :135 "It cannot say which primitive is missing, because naming it would mean already holding it."

After a genuine re-census of all 13 figures, the Operators table and the Symbols table, no line returns a universal percentage, a named statistic, or a rule that assigns a given firm's profit to one source. What the figures return is the **shape** any discriminator must have: per slot, low-overlap readings, a non-updating anchor, a source-discriminating instrument refined from a worse one, and a refuter written before the run. The specific attribution is a `gap`, of the unreachable-at-this-budget kind, not an underivable-in-principle claim (Fig 8 :40-43 "Underivable-in-principle and unreachable-at-this-budget are different facts, and they issue different instructions."). Recording it as `gap` is a verdict, not a silence: "Unreachable is a verdict here, not a silence," (Fig 13 :134).

**Refuter:** a figure line, found after re-census, that returns a universal split ratio or names the discriminator. None was found.

### F8: verdicts stay per domain and are never averaged (triangulated)

Fig 1 :19, Fig 9 :60 "Use filters for the budget and witnesses for the verdict. Never let a filter hand you a verdict.", Fig 12 :125 "A composition costs in two currencies, and they do not add." Fig 12 :126 "Bits price the description. Actions price finding out whether it holds."

A measurement verdict (can this instrument read the slot?), a firm verdict (which source dominates here?) and a policy verdict (should this position be refused?) have different anchors and different currencies. Averages such as "market power accounts for X percent of profits" are filters for the research budget, never witnesses for a cause. Adding a bits cost and an actions cost into one score is the named error, not the fix (Fig 12 :130 "Summing them into one figure is the error this names, not the fix for it.").

**Refuter:** a single objective function that aggregates the three domains without loss, demonstrated by showing the same verdict under each domain's own anchor and under the aggregate.

### F9: prestige and agreement cannot settle it (single-source on the figure set, corroborated)

Fig 2 :35, Fig 7 :35, Fig 10 :59. A panel of famous economists who agree is a room with high overlap, not an anchor. "Prestige can fund an action. It can give an action a budget to be tried. But it cannot settle an action. Only the ground can settle it." (*The Recursive Self-Improvement Trap*, carried from the exemplar's canonical mapping). Selecting recipients for receptiveness manufactures the disqualifying correlation (Fig 2 :39-40).

**Refuter:** a case where agreement among same-pool experts, with no ground reading, correctly settled a source attribution and no later ground reading could overturn it.

### F10: "rent" is not a fixed bad kind (corroborated, valence-relative)

Fig 11 :49 "An actor's valence is relative to the residual, not a property it carries." Fig 11 :51 "No standing list of good actors survives a change of residual." Schumpeterian rent is treated in the literature as an incentive toward efficiency (Wikipedia, *Schumpeterian rent*), and UBS reports Tullock's "good rents" distinction. A patent is a benefactor to a residual defined as "how do we reward fixed-cost innovation" and a malfactor to a residual defined as "how do we keep output unrestricted". Which it is cannot be read off the rent label.

**Refuter:** a standing classification of a given revenue stream as "rent" that keeps its verdict across a change of residual and a change of anchor.

## 4. Pre-registered refuter (written before the conclusion)

Required before any outreach. Seat Map discipline: "a falsifier written before the run is a design instrument, not only an honesty device." The separation shape is refuted if any of the following holds:

1. A single observable statistic at the slot, without an independent non-updating source-discriminating instrument, reliably separates innovation from market power across cases (breaks F1).
2. The two sources are mutually exclusive and exhaustive at the slot, so no union compound exists (breaks F5).
3. The firm's own report or a single shared evidence pool can serve as the anchor without drift (breaks F3).
4. A figure line is found after re-census that returns a universal split percentage or names the discriminator (breaks F7).
5. Entry, diffusion and consumer-welfare readings do not discriminate the two behaviours at the ground (breaks the instrument claim in F4).

If any fires, the separation shape is withdrawn or narrowed. Until one is run, no part of this may be labelled `grounded`.

## 5. Recommendation

1. Lead with F1 and F5: the reading is not the source, and the question is a union. Everything else follows from these two.
2. Reject the pooled figure (F2). Require per-slot verdicts and declare the anchor for each domain.
3. Specify the discriminator in effect terms (F4): a low-overlap, non-updating instrument refined from an existing noisy reading, with a source gate and a shadow test where it is imported (Fig 6 :23 "No frame escapes its own closure. The bound moves only on contact with a frame whose closure differs.").
4. Keep the classification convention and the verdict separate (F6). A threshold that decides an outcome must be declared as a verdict.
5. Record the firm-specific split as a `gap` (F7). Do not manufacture a number. Offer the separator and the refuter, not a conclusion about a named company.
6. Fig 4 :22 "Only methods cross up. Only head starts come down." Send the method of separation, not a recording of one classification.

## 8. Updated verdict

The shape survives: an observed profit is not its source (F1), the residual is per slot (F2), separation requires a non-updating, low-overlap, source-discriminating instrument refined from a worse one (F3, F4), the question is a union with an innovate-then-exclude intersection (F5), a verdict-fixing threshold has stopped being a convention (F6), and the firm-specific split is a `gap` (F7). Verdicts stay per domain and are never averaged (F8). What does not survive is any claim that a single statistic, a panel's agreement, or a declared definition settles the source.
