# Expert verification packet: choosing model detail

**What this is:** a claim in a form a modeller or complex-systems researcher can check. Mode, derivation, and refuters are stated before the conclusion, so a reader knows what to look for and what would settle it.

**Mode: `specified` on the four domain verdicts, `general` on the structural shape, not `grounded`.** No measurement in this packet settles a particular model level. This is a simulated derivation checked for internal consistency against the figures, not a ground reading.

## 1. The claim, in one paragraph

There is no single right level of detail. The choice is a union of four conditions with different anchors, kept separate and never averaged into a complexity dial: (1) the residual stays indexed per slot; (2) choose the coarsest level at which the ground still returns a discriminating reading that does not update on the model's own report; (3) carry the coarse description up and back down and measure the round-trip gap, adding detail only where the gap carries a residual that persists and does not trend down; (4) use a low-overlap second frame, because models that match the same pattern by different mechanisms are isomers whose winner only the ground can pick. Simplicity budgets the search; it never witnesses the verdict. The framework returns this shape, not a universal level or a mechanism name.

## 2. Three checkable sub-claims

### C1. Framework-internal; checkable by reading Figures 1, 2, and 9

- **Statement:** the "one right level" framing is a union misread as a scalar. The residual is per slot, and a global simplicity score hides the live slot. The anchor for the choice must not update on what the model thinks.
- **Source, verbatim:** Fig 1 :19, *"The residual is indexed by object slot, not aggregated."* Fig 1 :22, *"Aggregating across slots is how a live signal disappears into an average."* Fig 2 :29, *"An anchor is whatever settles the question and does not change its answer because of what any frame thinks. A measurement, an experiment, a counter, a market, a patient outcome."* Fig 9 :55, *"A bundled problem is usually a union of overlapping conditions, not a list of separate ones."*
- **What to check:** is the modelling decision actually per slot (different subsystems or observables need different levels), and does the proposed selection score update on the model's own output? If the score is produced by the same frame it grades, it is frame-internal (Fig 1 :27).
- **Refuter:** demonstrate one level giving correct per-slot answers on every slot including one whose live residual is hidden by aggregation, or show a reading that updates on the model's report and still settles the mechanism question. Breaks C1.

### C2. Derivation-internal; checkable by reading Figures 4, 5, and 13

- **Statement:** the audit of a coarse level is the measured round trip, and the signal that detail is owed is a residual that persists and does not trend down. The specific level and mechanism are a declared `gap`, not a hidden answer.
- **Source, verbatim:** Fig 4 :30, *"So take the round trip and measure the gap. Without it you are navigating by dead reckoning: confident about a position nobody checked."* Fig 4 :31, *"The gap is not an error to eliminate. It is the measurement of what the coarser description cannot hold"* (continuing *"and it is the only honest report of how much was"* given up). Fig 5 :59, *"a term whose residual PERSISTS AND DOES NOT TREND DOWN is the one to point the refusal at"*; Fig 5 :65, *"make the sought-for shape an ORDINARY TERM."* Fig 9 :32, *"you get a kind of thing, never the answer itself."* Fig 13 :134, *"Unreachable is a verdict here, not a silence,"*.
- **What to check:** can a fine-to-coarse-to-fine round trip actually be run for your model, and is the returned gap a measurement of lost detail rather than a fitting artefact? Has the sought-for shape (what an answer would look like) been written down before the run, so it can be refused?
- **Refuter:** a round trip with negligible gap on every slot that still misses a mechanism the ground later confirms, or a preference never bound as a term that nevertheless identifies the decisive mechanism correctly. Breaks C2.

### C3. World-facing; checkable by a modeller against their own practice

- **Statement:** two models that reproduce the same pattern with different mechanisms are isomers over a fixed atom set, and simplicity cannot choose between them. A second frame with differing closure is required.
- **Source, verbatim:** Fig 12 :117, *"isomer. Same atoms, different bond, different substance. The difference is invisible in the formula and decisive in the behaviour."* Fig 8 :32, *"Closure is idempotent. Composing inside a closed set never leaves it, so no search, however long, adds an atom."* Fig 8 :53, *"Residual first, frame second."* Fig 12 :125, *"A composition costs in two currencies, and they do not add."* Fig 9 :60, *"Use filters for the budget and witnesses for the verdict. Never let a filter hand you a verdict."*
- **What to check:** do the two candidate models hold the same atoms with different bonds (isomer), or did one drop atoms (subtraction plus import)? Is a second, low-overlap model actually available, or are the two candidates reading the same evidence pool (Fig 2 :31)?
- **Refuter:** two low-overlap models matching the same pattern are shown interchangeable for prediction and intervention without ground contact, or description cost alone is shown to pick the mechanism-correct model. Breaks C3.

## 3. What is explicitly not claimed

- Not a universal level, resolution, agent count, or parameter count.
- Not that finer is better, and not that simpler is better.
- Not a named decisive mechanism in advance. A name requires ground settlement or a provenanced import with a shadow test.
- Not that coarse models are wrong, or that the four conditions can be averaged.
- Not `grounded`, and not a theory of everything. No last room, no final method.
- Not that this packet, written by an LLM, has been checked by a human expert. It has not.

## 5. What would settle it

A source-discriminating instrument for model mechanisms. From *The Hard Problem is About Source*: an instrument that reads the pattern *"cannot comment on the source"*, and the answer *"is unreached until a better instrument exists."* Concretely for this claim:

1. A per-slot round-trip measurement showing which omitted mechanism flips a residual on the ground (settles C2).
2. An anchor test showing the selection reading does not move when the model's own report changes (settles C1).
3. An intervention test distinguishing two pattern-matching isomer models on the decisive slot (settles C3).

## 6. What an expert can check in twenty minutes

1. Read Fig 1 :19, :22, Fig 2 :29, Fig 4 :30-31, Fig 9 :60, Fig 12 :117 (six short passages).
2. Check C1 against a model you know: is the detail decision really per slot, and does your selection score update on the model's own output?
3. Check C2: can you run a fine-to-coarse-to-fine round trip and name what the gap fails to hold?
4. Check C3: name two models in your field that match the same pattern by different mechanisms. Are they isomers or one frame twice?
5. Send the refutation. A failure is more useful than an endorsement. If you would rather not engage, no reply is needed.
