## QUESTION

How can researchers choose the right level of detail when simpler models are easier to analyze but may omit decisive mechanisms?

Restated as an effect: at what scale does a model library `Γ_L` still register `R_s = |Γ_L(b,a) - o′|` for the slot that matters, and how is that known before the ground answers? Fig 1 :19: *"The residual is indexed by object slot, not aggregated."* The object of choice is per slot, never a global complexity score. The question is a union of four conditions with different anchors (Fig 9 :55), not a two-point trade-off. The sought-for shape is bound in advance as an ordinary term: a procedure returning the coarsest per-slot level at which the ground still discriminates, the round trip carries no live residual, and a low-overlap frame is available, with a refuter and an instrument owed for the remaining gap.

## VERDICT (from the figures)

**Figure-derived verdict.** Reject the single-dial framing. Choose by a four-condition separator whose verdicts stay separate per domain and are never averaged: per-slot residual indexing, a ground reading that does not update on the model's own report, a measured fine-to-coarse-to-fine gap whose live residual decides where detail is owed, and a low-overlap second frame that can see what this one cannot. Simplicity is a filter for the budget, never a witness for the verdict. The universal level, the numerical gap threshold, and the mechanism name are a declared `gap` of the shape-not-a-name kind, reported as `unreachable`, not as silence. Mode `specified` on the four verdicts, `general` on the shape, not `grounded`. World sources corroborate the mechanism at genuinely lower overlap (physics, cognition, multilevel statistics) and never flip the figure verdict; the apparent conflicts (RG irrelevance, Occam's razor, aggregation saves information) were returned to the census and re-mapped, not used to change the result.

- **cost if wrong:** a team coarsens to a cheap model that hides a decisive slot, and the failure is discovered only at the ground after a decision is made. The four-condition check costs some measurement effort and one extra frame up front; skipping it can cost an intervention built on the wrong mechanism. Conversely, demanding unlimited detail wastes budget on slots with no live residual. Because the verdict is a union, a single lever aimed at the wrong condition leaves the intersections standing (Fig 9 :57). The cost is real, bounded, and largely reversible if the refuters are run before commitment.
- **undoable:** yes. No term is deleted if the separator fails. Fig 6 :42: *"What is recorded only grows. What is reachable is derived, and it can fall."* A refutation adds a term that refuses and the reach falls because *"something was added rather than because anything was deleted"* (Fig 6 :42-43). The shape, the six refuters, and the provenance trail are recorded so a later reader can locate the error (Fig 10 :62-64).
- **reversed by:** any of the six pre-registered refuters in `01` §4, in particular (1) one universal level giving correct per-slot answers where aggregation hides a live slot, (2) description cost alone selecting the ground-confirmed mechanism with no non-updating contact, (3) a negligible round-trip gap that still misses a decisive mechanism, (4) an updating reading that still settles the question, (5) two low-overlap models that match the pattern being interchangeable without ground contact, or (6) a source-discriminating instrument that fixes a level and names a mechanism for a general class, which would upgrade the `gap` to grounded rather than refute the separator. A world source with genuinely different evidence showing that one scalar orders all four conditions would also reverse it.
