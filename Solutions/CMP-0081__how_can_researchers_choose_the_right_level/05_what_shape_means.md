## 1. A shape is a constraint, not a filler

The canonical README states the limit: the framework gives *"what a missing thing would have to look like (a shape, not a name)."* Fig 9 :32: *"you get a kind of thing, never the answer itself."* Fig 13 :134: *"Unreachable is a verdict here, not a silence,"* and the system *"cannot say which primitive is missing, because naming it would mean already holding it."*

From *The Library of Babel*, read directly: *"you can cut your search short if the set of books returned is small and descriptive enough that it provides a general shape of the answer that satisfies your question."*

For CMP-0081, a **shape** is the narrowed form of a model-selection answer that can be stated before a level or a mechanism is named. The shape is the four-condition separator: per-slot residual indexing, a non-updating ground reading, a measured round-trip gap, and a low-overlap frame. A specific level ("use an agent-based model with 200 agents") is a name. A specific mechanism name ("the omitted variable is X") is a name. The shape constrains the answer; it does not fill the slot.

## 2. How the shape is obtained

- *The Library of Babel*: bootstrap by proximity. *"With each new term gained, you are able to narrow the space by definition and deepen your vocabulary."* The question determines relevance: *"The problem you have determines what makes a book relevant to you or not."*
- Fig 6 :30: *"Cast it in effect terms."* Fig 9 :30-31: *"your words are borrowed too, so describe the effect"* / *"rather than the cause."* Here the effect is: a reading that flips a residual when the mechanism is removed. No mechanism name is needed to describe that test.
- Fig 8 :53: *"Residual first, frame second."* The residual is described before the level or the model family is chosen, *"or any frame you pick will seem to fit"* (Fig 9 :28).
- *The Library of Babel* on the union trap: *"Perhaps your problem was a bundle or conflation of several problems masquerading as one?"* The "simple versus detailed" dichotomy is exactly such a conflation (F6 of `01`).
- Stopping: the search ends when *"the finish line will also be the starting place"*, and the paradox is that the searcher has by then *"composed the answer through the process of locating it."*

## 3. What licenses a name

Only two things, both outside the shape itself:

1. **The ground settles it.** Formula step 5: *"the ground settles it. Verified only by what pays. A gate is not the ground."* The README: the framework *"stops at the limit of what the available instruments can distinguish, and records the lineage so the stop is honest."*
2. **A provenanced import from a frame whose closure differs** (Fig 6 :23, Fig 8 :50-52), with a source gate and a shadow test.

Applied here: a particular level (fine or coarse) is licensed only when a ground reading at that level discriminates the residual and does not update on the model's own report; a particular mechanism is licensed only when the persistent-flat residual test points at a bound term or a provenanced import supplies one. Until then the name is the modeller's own contribution and must be recorded as such.

*The Hard Problem is About Source*, read directly, gives the discriminator argument: an instrument that reads green *"cannot comment on the source"*, and several paths can produce identical readings. *"If several paths create the same behavior, then behavior is a projection with lossy information."* The resolution was *"not by argument, but by the arrival of a better instrument"*: *"The answer is unreached until a better instrument exists."* For this question, two models that both reproduce the observed pattern are the metamer; a model-selection score that cannot tell their mechanisms apart is the inadequate instrument. The round trip and a low-overlap frame are the closest available discriminators, and they are still not a general source-discriminating instrument.

## 4. Why "the simplest adequate model" is the specific forbidden error

The phrasing sounds like a shape but is a name in disguise: it presupposes that simplicity and adequacy are one ordering and that a single cut on it is the answer. Three figure rules refuse it:

- *"Aggregating across slots is how a live signal disappears into an average."* (Fig 1 :22). The "adequate" in a global score hides the live slot.
- *"They are frame-internal. A frame cannot score itself with a quantity it also produces."* (Fig 1 :27). Simplicity is produced inside the frame.
- *"Use filters for the budget and witnesses for the verdict."* (Fig 9 :60). Simplicity is the filter.

The correct form is the shape: *the coarsest per-slot level at which the ground still discriminates and the round trip still carries no live residual.* That is checkable without naming a level in advance.

## 5. Q: Does "shape, not a name" forbid naming a level or a mechanism? ANSWERED with a correction

**Narrow answer.** The README's phrase governs a *missing* primitive, one not held in `Γ`. An already-available level or an already-held mechanism is not missing. Naming one as a candidate binding is provenance, not the invention of a new primitive.

**Correction to my own first reading, kept visible.** In an earlier pass I wrote that the framework forbids naming any specific level or mechanism at all. That overstates the rule. The rule forbids presenting a name as something the framework derived or as a wall-moving import (Fig 6 :22, *"IMPORT adds an atom"*). So:

- "Use a finer spatial grid at slot 7 because the round-trip residual there persists" is a **binding plus a ground result**, allowed and checkable.
- "The right level is a fine grid, derived from the figures" is a **name where a shape is owed**, refused.
- "The decisive mechanism is term X, because X is in our library" is a **preference**, not a derivation, and is exactly the kind of term the Direction rule requires to be written down and made refusable (Fig 5 :65-66).

Both the narrow rule and the correction are recorded so neither is quietly lost. This mirrors the correction in the exemplar's `05` §5b.

## 6. Two consequences for outreach

**Prestige is not action.** From *The Recursive Self-Improvement Trap*, read directly: *"Prestige can fund an action. It can give an action a budget to be tried. But it cannot settle an action. Only the ground can settle it. The Ground Maintainer must be blind to prestige."* A venue's prestige or a recipient's reputation gives the separator a budget to be tried; it does not make the separator true. The ask in `02` is for a check, not an endorsement.

**This audit is a simulation, not a ground reading.** Same essay, read directly: LLMs *"can quickly simulate an approximate answer/output with the formula"*, but *"the truly testable output would need to be derived from the real architecture or working the formula yourself."* And: *"Metatheories cannot validate themselves."* So `01` is a simulated derivation checked for internal consistency against the figures. It is not a test of the separator and must be labelled as such. It cannot settle what level is right for any particular model.

## 7. One-paragraph version

For CMP-0081, "shape" means the form of a model-selection answer that can be written before a level or a decisive mechanism is named: per-slot residuals, a non-updating ground reading, a measured fine-to-coarse-to-fine gap, and a low-overlap second frame. The framework returns that constraint, not an optimal resolution, a parameter count, or a mechanism name. A specific level becomes licensed only by ground settlement or by a provenanced import with a shadow test; until then it is the modeller's binding and is recorded as such. An instrument that cannot tell two pattern-matching models apart is the inadequate instrument named in *The Hard Problem is About Source*, and the answer stays unreached until a better one exists. Unreachable is a verdict here, not a silence.
