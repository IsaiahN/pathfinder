# The Tether's answer: choosing the right level of model detail

**Mode: `specified` on the four domain verdicts, `general` on the structural shape, not `grounded`.** This is a shape and a route, not a result. Simulated derivation checked for internal consistency against the figures (`05` §6), not a ground reading.

## 1. The residual it answers

A research team can make a model cheaper to analyse by coarsening it, but the coarsening can drop the mechanism that decides a residual on some slot. The question asks for the selection rule. Restated as an effect: at what scale does `Γ_L` still register `R_s = |Γ_L(b,a) - o′|` for the slot that matters, and how would we know before the ground answers? The residual is per slot (Fig 1 :19), so the answer cannot be a global complexity dial (F1). The framing is a union of four conditions with different anchors (Fig 9 :55), not a trade-off curve (F6).

## 2. The shape: four verdicts, kept separate, never averaged

1. **Per-slot indexing (D1).** Keep the residual indexed per slot. A single "model complexity" score is how a live signal disappears into an average (Fig 1 :22). Report which slots the coarse model still resolves and which it dropped. Mode `specified`. Status `triangulated` (Fig 1 + Fig 9).

2. **Ground contact that does not update (D2).** Choose the coarsest level at which the reading still decides the question without changing its answer because of what the model thinks. *"An anchor is whatever settles the question and does not change its answer because of what any frame thinks."* (Fig 2 :29). Over-coarsening severs the channel through *"saturated metrics, pooled readings"* (Fig 10 :26) while the ground itself does not decay (Fig 10 :24). If the reading updates on the model's own report, more detail cannot help. Mode `specified`. Status `triangulated` (Fig 2 + Fig 10 + Fig 1).

3. **A measured round trip (D3).** Coarse-graining is `T_A`; the test is `T_E` back down and the gap `R_T`. *"Take the round trip and measure the gap"* (Fig 4 :30), and *"The gap is not an error to eliminate. It is the measurement of what the coarser description cannot hold"* (Fig 4 :31). Where the gap carries a residual that persists and does not trend down, the omitted mechanism was decisive and detail must be added at that slot (Fig 5 :59). Mode `specified` / `general`. Status `triangulated` (Fig 4 + THE_FORMULA + Fig 13).

4. **A low-overlap second frame (D4).** A model cannot escape its own closure (Fig 8 :32). A second frame whose closure differs is needed to see what this one cannot, and it must be chosen after the residual is described (Fig 8 :53). Models that reproduce the same pattern by different mechanisms are isomers (Fig 12 :117); the ground, not simplicity, selects among them. Mode `general` with `specified` application. Status `triangulated` (Fig 6 + Fig 8 + Fig 2 + Fig 12).

**Simplicity's place.** Description cost is a price in bits, and actions price finding out whether the arrangement holds (Fig 12 :125-126). Simplicity decides what gets tried first inside a budget; it does not witness the verdict (Fig 9 :60). It is a filter, not a selector of truth.

## 3. What it explicitly does not say

- Not a universal detail level, resolution, agent count, or parameter count. No figure line returns one (Fig 9 :32, Fig 13 :134).
- Not that simpler is better or that finer is better. Both fallacies are named in the multilevel-methodology literature; the right level is variable- and residual-relative.
- Not a named decisive mechanism in advance. The framework returns a shape, and a name needs ground settlement or a provenanced import with a shadow test.
- Not that coarse models are wrong. A coarse model is right where the gap is empty and the anchor still discriminates.
- Not a claim that the four conditions can be averaged into one score. They are a union with an overlap that is its own compound (Fig 9 :57).
- Not `grounded`. No measurement here has settled any particular level.

## 4. What would settle it: the ground and the instrument

The figure-grounded tests are the refuters of `01` §4. Operationally:

- **Slot test.** Does the coarse description still produce a per-slot residual, or does averaging hide a live slot? (Fig 1 :19, :22)
- **Anchor test.** Does the chosen level's reading decide the question without moving when the model's own report changes? (Fig 2 :29, :34)
- **Round-trip test.** Measure `R_T`. Does any slot carry a residual that persists and does not trend down after the coarse-to-fine return? (Fig 4 :30-31, Fig 5 :59)
- **Isomer test.** Do two low-overlap models that match the pattern differ in behaviour on the decisive slot? (Fig 12 :117)
- **Discriminator owed.** The deeper limit is source, as in *The Hard Problem is About Source*: an instrument that reads the pattern *"cannot comment on the source."* The answer *"is unreached until a better instrument exists."* A source-discriminating instrument for model mechanisms is what would convert the `gap` into a grounded verdict. It is characterized in effect terms but not held (Fig 9 :30-31).

## 5. The honest stop

**Unreached, not unreachable in principle.** The four-condition separator is derived. The specific level and the mechanism name are a declared `gap`: *"you get a kind of thing, never the answer itself."* (Fig 9 :32). *"Unreachable is a verdict here, not a silence."* (Fig 13 :134). The record keeps the lineage: what was refused (a universal dial, simplicity as witness, an updating anchor), what was bound as a term before the run, and what would reverse it. *"Indiscernibility is not identity."* This is a simulated derivation; a real outside frame with a different evidence pool is still needed, and that is what the expert packet is for.

## 6. One-paragraph version for outreach

> The Tether does not return a single right level of model detail. It returns a four-condition separator, each with its own anchor, and keeps the verdicts separate instead of averaging them into a complexity dial. The residual stays indexed per slot, because a global score is how a live signal disappears into an average. Choose the coarsest level at which the ground still returns a discriminating reading that does not update on the model's own report, then carry the coarse description up and back down and measure the gap: where that gap carries a residual that keeps being wrong and is not improving, the omitted mechanism was decisive. Simplicity only budgets the search, never witnesses the verdict. A second frame with a differing closure is needed to see what the first cannot, and models that match the same pattern by different mechanisms are isomers whose winner the ground must pick. The framework refuses to name a universal level or a decisive mechanism in advance; it returns the shape those names must fit, writes its refuters first, and stops at the limit of the available instruments. Unreachable is a verdict, not a silence.
