## 1. A shape is a constraint, not a filler

The canonical README states the limit: the framework returns *"what a missing thing would have to look like (a shape, not a name)"*. Fig 9 :32: *"you get a kind of thing, never the answer itself"*. Fig 13 :135: *"It cannot say which primitive is missing, because naming it would mean already holding it."*

From *The Library of Babel* (carried): *"You can cut your search short if the set of books returned is small and descriptive enough that it provides a general shape of the answer that satisfies your question."*

For this question a **shape** is the constrained form of an answer we can state before we can name the list. It says: a genuine transfer is a method, not a recording; it enters a reproduced habitat; it is paid for by a target anchor that does not update on the transfer claim. It does not say which named lever will work in which domain. The four-condition separator is a shape. "Immunise the hubs, diversify, meter the roads, cull, organise through weak ties" is a name/list.

## 2. How a shape is obtained

- *Library of Babel* (carried): use the vocabulary already closest to the thing, look for recurring new terms that overlap with it, recurse. *"With each new term gained, you narrow the space and deepen your vocabulary."*
- Fig 6 :30: *"outward: does another frame already hold it? The description travels. Fails by being sharpened into terms only we can read. Cast it in effect terms."* Fig 9 :30-31: *"your words are borrowed too, so describe the effect rather than the cause."*
- *Decoding Tacit Knowledge* (carried): *"When contact isn't possible, the best you can send is a plain description of what you'd expect to see, because anyone can check that against the world."* This is why the separator is written as four checkable conditions rather than as a theory.
- Fig 11 :43: *"The habitat is enumerated, never composed."* For this question the enumeration is D1 to D5 plus the transfer relation itself, and it is read off the world (each domain has counters), not composed from the word "complexity".

## 3. What licenses a name

Only two things, both outside the shape itself:

1. **The ground settles it.** FORMULA step 5: *"the ground settles it. Verified only by what pays. A gate is not the ground."* For transfer: a measured pay at the target's own counter, with the source and route recorded.
2. **A provenanced import from a frame whose closure differs** (Fig 6 :23, Fig 8 :50-52), with a source gate and a shadow test.

*They're Scanning Books* (carried): *"The real import is not a new term in the existing space. It is a new dimension of the space."* And: *"A minimum viable set of six terms is a human's careful guess at reality's compression axes; another sensorium's minimum set need not be those six."* So a cross-domain category scheme is a visible guess, not a discovery of nature's own list; promoting it to "the general mechanism" makes the choice invisible.

## 4. The ground and the source problem

*The Hard Problem is About Source* (opened this session) gives the mechanism for why resemblance fails. A colour instrument reads green and cannot comment on the source: one green light, or yellow plus blue, or RGB, or CMYK, or a scheme not yet discovered. The essay's exact wording: *"If several paths create the same behavior, then behavior is a projection with lossy information"*, and *"An instrument that can't distinguish these cases demonstrates its own inadequacy, it cannot claim anything substantial about what is being measured or what causes it."* It adds that the answer is *"unreached until a better instrument exists"*, and that this was proven *"not by argument, but by the arrival of a better instrument"*.

Applied here: five domains showing a power law are five readings of the same projection. The reading does not determine whether the source is a shared mechanism, a shared measurement convention, or an adopted import. What is owed is a discriminator at each domain's counter, not a name. Fig 8 :50-52: *"Convergent derivation and adopted import look identical in the contents."*

*The Recursive Self-Improvement Trap* (carried): *"the output is only as good as the validator. The agent, or the tool, or the frame, or the theory, cannot validate itself."* This applies to the four-condition separator itself: its agreement with the essays is one evidence pool, not a check.

## 5. Answer to the shape question

**What licenses a particular cross-domain insight?** Nothing in the framework returns the list. A particular insight is licensed only by (a) the target ground paying for it at its own counter, and (b) a provenanced import with a shadow test naming the residual it explains. Until one of those, the insight is a candidate binding recorded as the searcher's contribution, not a transfer.

**Does the literal question commit the forbidden error?** Yes, in form. It asks for standing list, and Fig 11 :51 says *"No standing list of good actors survives a change of residual."* The correct form is the four-condition separator with per-domain verdicts. The list is a declared `gap` (Fig 13 :134), not silence and not an invitation to manufacture a catalogue.

## 5b. Correction to my own §3

I initially read *"shape, not a name"* as forbidding any naming of cross-domain concepts such as "network", "threshold" or "cascade". That overstated the rule. The README's phrase governs a *missing* primitive, one not held in Γ. Network, threshold, gradient and cascade are already held atoms; naming them is a binding from a domain frame onto an atom, with provenance, not the invention of a seventh kind. What remains wrong is calling a shared word a settled transfer without a discriminator, which is the metamerism point in §4. Both claims are now recorded so neither is quietly lost. Triangulated (canonical README + Fig 13 + the source essay).

## 6. Consequences for contact

**Prestige is not action.** From *The Recursive Self-Improvement Trap* (carried): *"Prestige must never be allowed to validate an action directly... Prestige can fund an action. It can give an action a budget to be tried. But it cannot settle an action. Only the ground can settle it. The Ground Maintainer must be blind to prestige."* A campaign that collects famous names to agree that insights transfer is the named collapse, not corroboration. The legitimate ask gives the separator a budget to be tried against a counter in the recipient's own domain.

**My own audit is a simulation, not a ground reading.** Same essay (carried): *"LLMs can quickly simulate an approximate answer/output... However, the truly testable output would need to be derived from the real architecture or working the formula yourself."* LLM output is *"simulacra"*. So `01`, `06` and `09` are simulated derivations checked for internal consistency against the figures. They are not tests of cross-domain transfer and must be labelled as such. They cannot settle anything they report on.

**Per-slot, not pooled.** Fig 1 :22: *"Aggregating across slots is how a live signal disappears into an average."* The shape is delivered as five domain verdicts plus the transfer-relation verdict; any single "transfer coefficient" is a pooled reading of the kind the source essay warns about.
