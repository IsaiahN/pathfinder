## The verdict in one line

What transfers is not a list of insights but a separator: a method or generator crosses only with its binding arrangement intact, into a reproduced habitat, through a channel to a non-updating target anchor, and only after a source gate and a shadow test. The five domains share a functional group (actors, currency, anchor, bond), not a standing catalogue of levers. An insight's valence is relative to the residual, so the same lever can help in one domain and harm in another. The literal list is a declared `gap`, not silence.

## What is deliberately not here

- No personal emails of named individuals.
- No ranking of people by how likely they are to agree with the claim.
- No bulk-send plan.

Reasons in the figures' own words:

1. **Correlation disqualifies a verdict.** Fig 2 :40: *"disqualifying for a verdict: agreement about the world is worthless when the agreeing frames share an evidence pool"*. A recipient list selected for receptiveness manufactures exactly the agreement that would be worthless.
2. **A room is not an anchor.** Fig 2 :35: *"Alignment is triangulation, not negotiation. A mirror cannot correct you."* Five domains that share a metaphor are one evidence pool speaking.
3. **Do not hand the receiver the interiors.** Fig 10 :42: *"A verifier that can reconstruct the claim cannot verify it."* The first contact carries the residual and the refuter, not the framework.
