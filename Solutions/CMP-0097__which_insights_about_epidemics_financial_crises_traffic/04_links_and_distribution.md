## What in the wider literature bears on this question

1. **Transfer is real but conditional.** Bak and Paczuski state that historical contingency in geology, biology and economics finds a natural interpretation in a single self-organised-critical phenomenon, and the scale-free and SOC pages list the same coarse structure across many fields. This corroborates Fig 4 :19-20 (*"Only the actors, the currency and the anchor change"*), but only as a shape: the SOC page also says no general set of characteristics guarantees the behaviour, which is a live `gap` on membership, not a refutation.
2. **The same network shape carries opposite valence.** The complex-contagion page shows that weak ties help a simple contagion and slow a complex one; the systemic-risk page shows that connectivity absorbs shocks below a threshold and amplifies them above it. This is the world-side corroboration of Fig 11 :50-51. It is why a standing list of transferable levers fails.
3. **What survives a formal transfer is the exponent, not the material.** The universality-class page says exponents are independent of microscopic details. This is the mathematical instance of Fig 9 :32 (*"you get a kind of thing, never the answer itself"*): the transferable object is a constrained shape, not an identified mechanism.
4. **A method that does not travel as a recording.** The arc-welding study had to regenerate its selected spectral bands when the material and process changed. The method (sequential feature selection) crossed; the recording (the chosen bands) did not. Fig 4 :23: *"upward: a method that can be reapplied. Never the recording of a particular success."* This is the only genuinely low-overlap engineering witness and is counted as one witness, not a general proof.
5. **A source named from a reading is not settled.** The source essay's spectrometer example maps directly onto the transfer question: a shared curve reads the same whether the mechanism was derived independently or a name was borrowed. Fig 8 :50-52: *"Convergent derivation and adopted import look identical in the contents."*
