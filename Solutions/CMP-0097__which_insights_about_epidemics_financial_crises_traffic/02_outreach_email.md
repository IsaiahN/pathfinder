# Outreach draft: check a separator before you decide about the theory

**Use:** one-to-one, or as a cover letter attached to a submission through a public venue in `03_public_contact_points.csv`. **Not** for bulk send.

**Hard precondition:** do not send until the stage-1 output has been checked against a real counter in the recipient's own domain. The output must survive or the two-stage offer collapses on contact.

---

**Subject:** Check one test for cross-domain transfer before you decide whether the framework is worth reading

> Hi [name],
>
> I have one specific output I would like you to check against what you already know about [domain]. I am not asking you to read the framework first, and I am not asking you to agree with it. If the output does not survive, there is nothing else to look at.
>
> **The output (`specified`, not `grounded`).** The question is which insights about epidemics, financial crises, traffic, ecosystems and social movements genuinely transfer. The figures give a four-condition separator, not a list:
>
> 1. What crosses is a re-applicable method (name the actors, currency and anchor, enumerate the habitat, describe the residual, price the lever against the target's ground), never the recording of one success.
> 2. The target habitat must reproduce what the insight needed. You can introduce an actor; you cannot subtract the habitat.
> 3. The target's own counter must pay for it. Agreement among domains is one evidence pool, not a witness.
> 4. A borrowed insight is an import: it needs a source gate (where it came from) and a shadow test (which residual in the target it explains).
>
> **What is explicitly not claimed:** that a named lever transfers everywhere; that a shared power law or a shared word "network" proves a shared mechanism; that five domains agreeing is corroboration. The same intervention can help in one domain and harm in another, because its valence is relative to the residual it is measured against. The literal standing list is a declared gap, which is a verdict and not a silence.
>
> **What would refute this:** (1) one lever paying with the same sign in all five domains against each domain's own non-updating anchor; (2) an insight paying in a new habitat with no change in contact and no re-grounding; (3) two domains with shared evidence corroborating a verdict that then survives an independent instrument; (4) a figure re-census that returns the named list. I wrote these down before sending this. If any fires, the separator is wrong and I will retract it. A one-line "this fails because..." is more useful to me than agreement. If you would rather not engage, no reply is needed.
>
> **If it survives, and only then:** the materials are below. The verbatim figures are the authority, not my summary of them.
> - Full figures text with line numbers (local): `FRAMEWORK/reference/03-FIGURES_TEXT.md`
> - Canonical figures README (local): `FRAMEWORK/reference/FIGURES_README_canonical.md`
> - The eight-step loop: `FRAMEWORK/figures/THE_FORMULA.md`
>
> **If you want to use the machinery:** run the separator on a problem you know well and can check against a counter. Write down what would refute your result *before* you run it. A reported failure is more useful to me than a reported success.
>
> Best,
> [name / role]

## Two-stage discipline

Stage 1 is the separator and its refuter, checked against a counter in the recipient's domain. Record the verdict before any framework material is shown. Stage 2 is opt-in reading of the figures. Stage 3, if invited, is reusing the method on another problem with its own ground. A stage-2 agreement is never pooled as corroboration of stage 1 (Fig 2 :40); it occurs after the recipient has the framework's interiors, so it is the agreement of a frame that now holds them (Fig 10 :42).

One-to-one only. No bulk send, no automation, no misleading subject line, and an opt-out is implicit in "no reply is needed".
