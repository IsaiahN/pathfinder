## VERDICT (from the figures)

What genuinely transfers is a four-condition separator, not a list. A method or generator crosses with its binding arrangement intact, into a reproduced habitat, through a channel to a non-updating target counter, and only after a source gate and a shadow test. The five domains share a functional group of ingredients and, over a fixed full atom set, form a testable isomer claim, not one substance. An insight's valence is relative to the residual, so the same lever can reverse sign across domains. The five verdicts are kept separate and never averaged: D1 immunisation pays only where degree data exist; D2 connectivity is absorber below and amplifier above a threshold; D3 metering is a recording unless re-derived at the road's counter; D4 contact cascades reach several steps out and habitat substitution has two silent failures; D5 weak ties help simple contagion and slow complex contagion. The literal list is a `gap`, a verdict and not silence. This is a `specified`, ungrounded result; the list itself is shape-not-a-name.

- **cost if wrong:** bounded and locatable. The packet states the separator, the refuters and the lineage, so an error is findable rather than invisible. The real cost is in the other direction: sending a standing list and counting cross-domain agreement as corroboration would spend the independence of outside readers and manufacture the disqualifying correlation that Fig 2 :42 prices as *"Buy independence in proportion to the cost of being wrong."* That cost is avoidable by leading with the separator and its refuter.
- **undoable:** yes. The standing-list temptation is removable without touching the derivation; `07` and `08` already remove it. A published list could be superseded by the separator, and no atom has to be withdrawn. The `gap` stays open and recorded, so a later measurement can close it without a retraction of the shape.
- **reversed by:** a ground reading, not agreement. A controlled application of one lever with its binding arrangement in all five domains, registered on each domain's own non-updating counter with matching signs, would break the residual-relative-valence verdict. A generator-versus-recording trial, an import with recorded provenance and shadow test that grew a target's reach, a figure re-census returning the named list, or a domain found to have no nameable counter would each reverse the relevant sub-claim. Until one fires, the verdict stands as `candidate`.
