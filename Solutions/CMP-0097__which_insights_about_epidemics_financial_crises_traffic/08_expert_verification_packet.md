# Expert verification packet: cross-domain transfer of insights (CMP-0097)

**What this is:** a claim in a form a complexity, network or domain specialist can check. Mode, derivation and refuters are stated before the conclusion, so the reader knows what to look for and what would settle it.

**Mode: `specified` on the separator and per-domain verdicts, `general` on the structural shape, `gap` on the literal list. Not `grounded`.** No cross-domain measurement is claimed. This is a shape and a route, not a result. Simulated derivation, not a ground reading.

## 1. The claim, in one paragraph

Which insights about epidemics, financial crises, traffic, ecosystems and social movements genuinely transfer? The figures return a four-condition separator, not a list. (1) What crosses is a re-applicable method, never a recording of one occasion. (2) It must enter a habitat that reproduces what the insight needed; you can add an actor, not subtract the habitat. (3) The target's own non-updating counter decides, per domain; agreement among domains is one evidence pool. (4) A borrowed insight is an import needing a source gate (provenance) and a shadow test (the residual it explains), with a debit to the importer's independence. Because an actor's valence is relative to the residual, the same lever can help in one domain and harm in another, so no standing list of levers survives a change of residual. The five domains share a functional group of ingredients and, over a fixed atom set, form a testable isomer claim, not one substance. The literal list is a declared gap.

## 2. Three checkable sub-claims

### C1 - framework-internal; checkable by reading Figures 4, 8, 11 and 1
- **Statement:** the transferable object is a method/generator, not a recording; valence is residual-relative; verdicts are per slot.
- **Source, verbatim:**
  - Fig 4 :22 - *"Only methods cross up. Only head starts come down."*
  - Fig 4 :23 - *"upward: a method that can be reapplied. Never the recording of a particular success."*
  - Fig 11 :51 - *"No standing list of good actors survives a change of residual."*
  - Fig 11 :50 - *"The same actor is a benefactor to one residual and a malfactor to another, so mutualism and parasitism are relations rather than kinds."*
  - Fig 1 :19 - *"The residual is indexed by object slot, not aggregated."*
- **Refuter:** exhibit one lever paying with the same sign and magnitude in all five domains against each domain's own anchor, with the binding arrangement dropped and no re-grounding. If it pays, valence is carried rather than residual-relative, and C1 falls.

### C2 - world-facing; checkable against network science, finance and social contagion
- **Statement:** the same network structure carries opposite valence depending on the mechanism and residual. Connectivity is a shock-absorber below a tipping point and a shock-amplifier above it in finance; weak ties and small worlds spread simple contagions and slow complex contagions in social systems; targeted protection differs between immunisation and "too connected to fail".
- **What to check:** is that characterisation faithful? World sources opened and quoted in `10_source_ledger.csv`: Systemic risk (Wikipedia, 2026), Complex contagion (Wikipedia, 2026, reporting Centola and Macy 2007), Scale-free network (Wikipedia, 2026), Bak and Paczuski 1995 PNAS. Note that the encyclopedia pages are one broad evidence pool, not four witnesses; the PNAS paper is the low-overlap witness for the shape claim, not for the finance or contagion details.
- **Refuter:** a controlled cross-domain study in which one lever pays identically in all five domains, with the habitat reproduced and each counter read independently. Then the sign-flip claim is false. Equivalently, show that the five domains' evidence is independent at the level that matters and that their agreement survives a fresh instrument.

### C3 - framework-internal derivation about imports and closure
- **Statement:** a borrowed insight is an import, not a mint inside the target; it moves the target's reach only with provenance and a shadow test, and it debits the importer's independence.
- **Source, verbatim:**
  - Fig 6 :23 - *"No frame escapes its own closure. The bound moves only on contact with a frame whose closure differs."*
  - Fig 6 :24 - *"Import from a live peer is debited against that frame’s independence. A wrong atom costs more than starting from primitives."*
  - Fig 8 :12 - *"source gate: provenance"*
  - Fig 8 :13 - *"shadow test: explains a gap"*
  - Fig 6 :21 - *"MINT composes inside the closure and can never add an atom."*
- **Refuter:** show an import into a target domain that grew that domain's reach with no recorded source, no named residual explained, and no independence debit. Or show a mint adding a new atom purely by composition. Either breaks the import model.

## 3. What is explicitly not claimed

- Not a list of named transferable insights. The literal list is a `gap` (Fig 13 :134 - *"Unreachable is a verdict here, not a silence,"*), not an omission.
- Not that the five domains are one system or share a mechanism. Fig 12 :123 - *"the group says what kind; the qualifier says which member."*
- Not that a shared power law or a shared word "network" proves transfer. Fig 8 :50-52 - *"Convergent derivation and adopted import look identical in the contents."*
- Not that a failed transfer proves impossibility. Fig 8 :39 - *"a search that finds nothing is never a proof of absence."*
- Not that cross-domain agreement is corroboration. Fig 2 :40 - *"disqualifying for a verdict: agreement about the world is worthless when the agreeing frames share an evidence pool"*; Fig 7 :13 - *"agreement compounds, evidence does not"*.
- Not `grounded`. No measurement was taken by this derivation.

## 5. What would settle it

A discriminator at each domain's counter, not a further argument. From *The Hard Problem is About Source* (opened and verified): an instrument that reads green *"cannot comment on the source"*; the answer is *"unreached until a better instrument exists"*, proven *"not by argument, but by the arrival of a better instrument"*. Operationally: (1) apply one lever with its binding arrangement in all five domains and record sign per slot; (2) run a generator against a bare recording; (3) check the counter's channel for saturated metrics and pooled readings before declaring the ground changed (Fig 10 :26); (4) require the source gate and shadow test for every import.

## 6. What an expert can check in twenty minutes

1. Read Fig 4 :22-23, Fig 11 :50-51, Fig 1 :19 and Fig 6 :23-24. Five short passages.
2. Check C2 against their own field: does one named lever really change sign across the domains they know, or is the claim overstated?
