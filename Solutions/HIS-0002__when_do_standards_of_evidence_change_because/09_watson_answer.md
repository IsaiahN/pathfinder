## QUESTION

Decide, in effect terms, what follows from observing a field's standard of evidence S change. Option G: a new discovery changed the reference S ranges against. Option P: political or institutional pressure pushed on S. Option C: both, visible in the dated record. What each leads to: G is a re-anchoring, P is a capture (the standard moved when pushed, so it was a convention deciding an outcome), C is a compound that must be unbundled, and `gap` is the honest output where no dated record exists. The question is posed as a binary; the figures return a three-way discriminator plus a stop.

## DOMAIN CENSUS

R by arity, symmetry and scale, then one line per domain with actors / currency / anchor, partition or union, and order by dependency.

- **R (arity / symmetry / scale).** One observed change in S with three candidate causes. Arity three (two causes plus a compound), so a **union compound**: Fig 9 :54 "And when the split lands on a union rather than a partition, unbundle before you search again." Symmetry: G is asymmetric, ground to frame with the anchor not updating on frames; P is symmetric among frames, mutual update or a shared source. Scale: the change is a rule-level event in Γ, while its cause may be below the rule level (an instrument) or at the same level (a committee). This is a cross-level event and the round trip loses information (Fig 4 :30).
- **Domain 1, ground and discovery.** Actors: instruments, experiments, failed replications, refutations. Currency: residual R and settlement, what pays. Anchor: the measurement, experiment, counter or patient outcome, which does not update on frames. Partition or union: the inward and outward routes are two tracks that must not be read against each other (Fig 6 :31).
- **Domain 2, institutional and political.** Actors: committees, editors, funders, states, prestige, rooms. Currency: agreement and influence. Anchor: **none nameable**, because the standard moves when pushed. By the census rule this is not a ground domain; that is the finding. Fig 10 :47 "a convention that decides an outcome has stopped being one."
- **Domain 3, the record and the reader of it.** Actors: historians, dated decisions, provenance, outside frames. Currency: provenance. Anchor: the dated record, which does not move when a later frame reinterprets it. Fig 8 :51 "Convergent derivation and adopted import look identical in the contents. Only the record of where each came from separates them, and the surplus lives only where the closures differ."
- **Order by dependency (Fig 3).** Establish a dated change; sort whether it is one rule or several; decide which changed first (the reading or the rule); only then assess settlement. Describe the effect before picking an analogue: Fig 9 :27-28 "describe it before you go looking, or any frame you pick will seem to fit".

Strain test applied. Reading Fig 2's "anchor" as the standard itself strains, because a standard is a rule in Γ and the anchor is outside Γ. Re-census separates the rule (Fig 10 :47) from the reference (Fig 2 :29-30). Two quantities, no strain. Two further strains are live and named in `01` §4: a discovery is already described in the old vocabulary, and committees often follow ground findings; both are handled by ordering the record in time.

## VERDICT (from the figures)

The forced choice is refused. A standard of evidence is a convention until it decides an outcome; it changes legitimately when the reference it ranges against changes (inward instrument or provenanced import), and illegitimately when it moves under a push (mutual update, common cause, mirror chain) with no new ground contact recorded. Both can appear in one episode, so the output is a three-way discriminator, G / P / compound, plus `gap` where the dated record is missing. Because the two causes write identical prose, only the lineage separates them. The framework returns the shape and refuses the name of any specific case. This is `specified` and ungrounded. The "cost if wrong" is bounded because the refuters are written in advance and the lineage is recorded; the real cost is asking historians to agree before they check, which spends their independence (Fig 2 :42).

- **cost if wrong:** bounded and locatable. The packet states its mode and refuters before the conclusion, so an error is findable rather than invisible. The live cost is spending the independence of outside readers by sending the framework before the discriminator, which Fig 2 :42 prices. That cost is avoidable by leading with the discriminator and its refuter.
- **undoable:** yes. The shape is a test, not a commitment about any episode. A sent claim can be superseded by a corrected record, and no primitive has to be withdrawn. A case sorted to P can be re-sorted to compound when a dated ground reading is produced; the record grows and nothing is deleted.
- **reversed by:** a ground reading, not agreement. The decisive reversal would be a standard of evidence that is itself the anchor, not moving when frames push and with the ground ranging against it. For a case, a dated record showing which changed first, what proto-instrument was already returning something or what donor frame held the standard, and whether the old rule was refused or merely supplemented, would move it out of `gap`. A complete dated record in which both causes are visible yet cannot be ordered would refute the discriminator for that case. Prestige or consensus is not such a ground.
