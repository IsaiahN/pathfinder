## 1. A shape is a constraint, not a filler

The figures README states the limit: the framework returns "what a missing thing would have to look like (a shape, not a name)". Fig 9 :32: "you get a kind of thing, never the answer itself". Fig 13 records that "It cannot say which primitive is missing, because naming it would mean already holding it."

From *The Library of Babel*: "you can cut your search short if the set of books returned is small and descriptive enough that it provides a general shape of the answer that satisfies your question."

For this question, the shape is **the discriminator**, not the historical verdict. It can say: sort by which changed first, check whether the reference moved when pushed, keep inward and outward apart, and report compound or `gap` where the record will not close. It cannot say that Lysenkoism was pressure and the replication crisis was discovery without a dated record for each. The names are the historian's contribution.

So: "a standard changed when the ground returned something new" is a shape. "The ASA statement of 2016 was purely a pressure change" is a name, and it is not licensed by the figures.

## 2. How the shape is obtained

- *Library of Babel*: use the vocabulary you already have that is closest to the thing, then "With each new term gained, you are able to narrow the space by definition and deepen your vocabulary." Here the nearest terms are "standard", "evidence", "anchor", "convention", "instrument", "import", "room".
- Fig 6 :30: "The description travels. Fails by being sharpened into terms only we can read. Cast it in effect terms." Fig 9 :30-31: "your words are borrowed too, so describe the effect rather than the cause." So describe "the rule moved when a committee pushed it", not "ideology captured the field".
- *Decoding Tacit Knowledge*: "When contact isn't possible, the best you can send is a plain description of what you'd expect to see, because anyone can check that against the world." The outreach packet is written exactly this way: a list of what you would see in the record if each cause had fired.
- *Library of Babel* on stopping: "the finish line will also be the starting place". Here the stop is the point at which the discriminator, applied to a case, returns the same sorting no matter which of the eight essays you start from.

## 3. What licenses a name

Only two things, both outside the shape:

1. **The ground settles it.** Formula step 5: "the ground settles it. Verified only by what pays. A gate is not the ground." For a history question the equivalent ground is the dated record, not a general narrative. The record is what pays.
2. **A provenanced import from a frame whose closure differs** (Fig 6 :23, Fig 8 :50-52), with a source gate and a shadow test. Importing the vocabulary of metascience into a history-of-science case is an import, and it must be recorded as such.

*The Hard Problem is About Source* sharpens the point. Metamerism: an instrument reads green and "the instrument is speaking only about what it measured, it cannot comment on the source." And "If several paths create the same behavior, then behavior is a projection with lossy information (it cannot define consciousness in full)." A standard of evidence that changed is a reading; "discovery" and "pressure" are two possible sources of the same reading. The figures supply no instrument that discriminates them from the reading alone. "It was proven wrong not by argument, but by the arrival of a better instrument." Here the better instrument is the dated lineage, not one more philosophical argument about which cause is more real.

## 4. Why the two-cause question is itself a projection trap

*Decoding Tacit Knowledge* records the social mechanism directly: "Two people who only check against each other drift, and they drift together, so neither notices." And: "A shared mindset lets two people drift together without either one noticing: each correction lands easily, each confirms the other, and the pair moves away from the world together." That is Fig 2 :34 in psychological vocabulary, and it is exactly the pressure mechanism F3 identifies in the audit.

The same essay lists the three safeguards and one is almost a direct answer to this question: "Courts of law rule on evidence, which neither side can move." The analogue for a field is a standard whose anchor neither the field nor the institution can move. When that is absent, the standard becomes an arena for negotiation rather than a reference.

## 5. Q answered: what is the shape here?

**The shape is a four-step discriminator with three outputs and an honest stop.** Not a periodisation of the history of science, not a list of famous cases, and not a verdict on any one episode. The discriminator, bound as an ordinary term in `01_figure_grounded_audit.md` §0, is now refusable by the same test as everything else. That is the discipline Formula "Direction" requires: make the sought-for shape an ordinary term so a frame cannot hide its preference for one cause.

**Does the record commit the forbidden error?** Any answer that names a cause for a specific case without a dated lineage does. That is the metamerism error: reading the source off the projection. It is not cured by adding more agreement among historians (Fig 2 :40), because a shared evidence pool makes agreement a pool talking.

## 6. Consequences for outreach and for this folder's own status

**Prestige is not action.** *The Recursive Self-Improvement Trap*: "Prestige must never be allowed to validate an action directly." And: "Prestige can fund an action. It can give an action a budget to be tried. But it cannot settle an action. Only the ground can settle it. The Ground Maintainer must be blind to prestige." A famous historian agreeing with the discriminator funds it, at most. It does not settle it. The email in `02` therefore asks for a refutation, not an endorsement.

**Agreement is not a check.** Same essay: "You may all agree and be wrong. You may set your clock to someone else's, who was setting your clock to yours, and be off by many deviations over time. You need ground truth to define where you stand." This is Fig 2 's coherent drift, and it is why the contact CSV has no receptiveness rank.

**My own audit is a simulation, not a ground reading.** Same essay: "LLMs can quickly simulate an approximate answer/output with the formula found in the architecture post." And: "the truly testable output would need to be derived from the real architecture or working the formula yourself." Also: "the output is only as good as the validator. The agent, or the tool, or the frame, or the theory, cannot validate itself." So `01` is a simulated derivation checked for internal consistency against the figures. It is not a test of the historical claim and must be labelled as such. It cannot settle anything it reports on.

**The essays are one evidence pool.** Four of the eight were re-fetched and quoted verbatim this session; four were carried from the exemplar record and were not re-fetched, which is declared rather than hidden. All eight share an author with the figures. Their agreement with the figures is expected and carries little independent weight. The world sources in `09_watson_answer.md` are the low-overlap witnesses.
