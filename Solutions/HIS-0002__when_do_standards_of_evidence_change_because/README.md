## What is deliberately not here

- No personal emails of named individuals.
- No ranking of people by how likely they are to agree with the discriminator.
- No bulk-send plan.
- No claim that this derivation settles any actual episode in the history of science. It supplies a test for a historian to run on a dated record.

Three figure-grounded reasons:

1. **A room does not upgrade the label.** Fig 2 :35: "Alignment is triangulation, not negotiation. A mirror cannot correct you." A list of historians selected for agreement produces a convention, not a check.
2. **Correlation disqualifies a verdict.** Fig 2 :40: "disqualifying for a verdict: agreement about the world is worthless when the agreeing frames share an evidence pool". Selecting recipients for receptiveness manufactures the very correlation that would make their agreement worthless.
3. **Do not send the interiors before the verdict.** Fig 10 :52: "Bring a characterised residual, never the source." A recipient who has absorbed the framework before answering has joined the frame being checked.
