# Outreach draft, validate the discriminator before you decide about the framework

**Use:** one to one, or as a cover letter attached to a submission through the public venues in `03_public_contact_points.csv`. **Not** for bulk send.

**Hard precondition:** this offer only works if the recipient can run the discriminator on a case they already know. If they cannot name a dated record, there is nothing to check and the two-stage offer collapses on contact.

---

**Subject:** One test for telling a discovery-driven change in evidence standards from a pressure-driven one

> Hi [name],
>
> I am working on a narrow question in the history of scientific standards: when does a field's standard of evidence change because the ground returned something new, and when does it change because an institution, a committee or a prestige gradient pushed on it?
>
> I am not asking you to read a theory first, and I am not asking you to agree with one. I have one output I would like you to check against what you already know. If it does not survive, there is nothing else to look at.
>
> **The output (`specified`, not `grounded`):** a discriminator with four tests. (1) Was there a dated change in the rule, and was it one rule or several bundled together? (2) Which changed first in the record, the ground reading or the rule? (3) Did the thing the rule ranges against change, or did the rule itself move when frames pushed on it? (4) If it was a discovery, did the change come through a sharpened instrument that was already returning something, or through an import from a frame whose closure differs? These are two opposite routes and reading one against the other produces a false answer.
>
> **The result it returns:** three verdicts, not two. Discovery. Pressure. Compound, where both are visible in the dated record. A case that cannot be sorted because the lineage is missing is reported as `gap`, which is a verdict and not a silence.
>
> **What would refute it:**
> 1. Show a standard of evidence that is itself the anchor: it does not move when frames push on it, and the ground ranges against it. That breaks the whole distinction.
> 2. Show a case where a standard changed under open institutional pressure and the change improved contact with an independently nameable anchor, with the ground reading dated before the decision. That reclassifies the case as compound and removes pressure as the cause.
> 3. Show a discovery-driven change where no prior instrument was returning anything and no donor frame held the new standard. That breaks the inward/outward pair.
>
> I wrote these down before sending this. If one holds, I will retract the discriminator. A one-line "this fails because..." is more useful to me than agreement. If you would rather not engage, no reply is needed.
>
> **Note on stake:** I have an interest in the discriminator being right, and that cannot be removed. I am declaring it rather than asking you to ignore it. I am not asking you to adopt any framework, and I will not count later agreement as evidence.
>
> **If it survives, and only then:** the material is below. The figures, not my summary of them, are the authority.
> - Verbatim figure text with line numbers (local framework copy): `FRAMEWORK/reference/03-FIGURES_TEXT.md`
> - Canonical usage rule (local framework copy): `FRAMEWORK/reference/FIGURES_README_canonical.md`
> - The eight-step formula (local framework copy): `FRAMEWORK/figures/THE_FORMULA.md`
>
> **If you want to reuse the method:** the formula is in the figures folder. Run it on a standards case you know well and can check against a dated record. Write down what would refute your result *before* you run it, and state whether your result is `general`, `specified` or `grounded`. A reported failure is more useful to me than a reported success.
>
> Thanks,
> [name]

## Stage sequence

1. **Stage 1.** Send the discriminator and its refuter. Ask which side of it a dated case falls on, and what would move it. Record their answer before showing anything else.
2. **Stage 2, opt in only.** If they are curious, send the three local figure links, with the note that the quotes, not this packet, are the authority. Their later agreement is never pooled as corroboration (Fig 2 :40).
3. **Stage 3.** Offer the formula as a method, with the instruction that each user writes their own refuter and states their own mode. This is a generator, so giving it away does not give away an answer (Fig 4 :22).
