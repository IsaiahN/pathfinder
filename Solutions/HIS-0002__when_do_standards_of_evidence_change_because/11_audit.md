## Independent domain census (written from the residual line alone)

I redid the census before relying on the primary's conclusions, using the WATSON order: R by arity, symmetry and scale; one line per domain with actors / currency / anchor; partition or union; order by dependency.

- **R (arity / symmetry / scale).** The residual is a single observed change in a standard of evidence S with three candidate explanations: a ground reading g, a frame decision d, or both. Arity three, so a **union compound**, not a binary. The symmetry differs by cause: g runs one way, ground to frame, with the anchor not updating on frames; d runs symmetrically among frames, whether by mutual update or by a shared hidden source. Scale: the change is a rule-level event inside Γ, while its cause is either below that level (an instrument) or at that level (a committee). This is a cross-level event, so the round trip between levels has a gap.
- **Domain 1, ground and discovery.** Actors: instruments, experiments, failed replications, refutations. Currency: residual and settlement, what pays. Anchor: the measurement or outcome, nameable and not updatable by frames. Two internal tracks (inward, outward) that must not be read against each other.
- **Domain 2, institutional and political.** Actors: committees, editors, funders, states, rooms, prestige. Currency: agreement and influence. Anchor: **none nameable**, because a standard that moves when pushed is not an anchor. This is not a ground domain, and the absence is the finding.
- **Domain 3, the record.** Actors: historians, dated decisions, provenance, outside frames. Currency: provenance. Anchor: the dated record, which a later reinterpretation cannot move. This is the only domain in which the two causes can be separated.
- **Order by dependency.** Establish a dated change; split bundled rules; order the reading against the rule; decide anchor versus convention; only then assess settlement. Describe the effect before choosing an analogue.

My census matches the primary's three domains and its union reading. Where the primary derived the discriminator from Fig 2 and Fig 10, I derived it from Fig 9's union/partition split and Fig 3's dependency order. Different starting figures, same domain boundaries: low overlap between routes, so the agreement is weak but not circular within the framework. It is still one evidence pool.

## Round trip gap R_T

The Symbols table defines R_T as the gap between what was sent up and what came back down, `gap(x, (T_E ∘ T_A)(x))`, measuring what the coarser description cannot hold. Fig 4 :30 requires it to be measured rather than assumed. I ran the round trip on the condensation this folder actually uses.

1. **Sent up (x):** the verbatim figure set, chiefly Fig 2 :29-30 (anchor test), Fig 7 :35 (room agreement is not a verdict), Fig 8 :51 (only the record separates) and Fig 10 :47 (convention deciding an outcome).
2. **Abstraction (T_A):** the shorthand in `01` §8 and `07` §2: a standard is a convention until it decides an outcome; check which changed first; keep inward and outward apart; return G, P, compound or `gap`.
3. **Concretisation (T_E):** a concrete case, for example the 2016 ASA statement on p-values or the 1948 VASKhNIL session.

**R_T is nonzero.** Three things the shorthand holds do not survive the trip back down:

- the **ordering of the reading and the decision** in a specific case. The shorthand says "which changed first", but only the dated record supplies the order. A committee can follow a ground reading, which is why F5 returns compound.
- the **identity of the instrument or donor frame** for an inward or outward change. The shorthand names two routes; a case requires a named proto-instrument or a named donor frame with a source gate.
- the **stake of the reader**. Fig 10 :59-60 requires an unremovable interest to be declared; the shorthand "check the record" does not carry that requirement back down.

Conversely, one thing survives cleanly: a rule that moves when pushed is not an anchor, and agreement inside a shared closure is not a verdict. That is the residue worth sending. R_T was **not** measured numerically, because no ground reading exists; it is recorded qualitatively as the enumerated set of dropped constraints. Treating the formula as identical to the figures would set R_T to zero and would be the dead-reckoning error Fig 4 :30 names. The honest form is a lossy condensation with a measured gap.
