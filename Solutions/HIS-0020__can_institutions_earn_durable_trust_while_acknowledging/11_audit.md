## Independent domain census

I redid the census from the residual line alone, using the WATSON order: R by arity, symmetry and scale; one line per domain with actors / currency / anchor; partition or union; order by dependency.

- **R (arity / symmetry / scale).** The residual is the persistence of an audience's bet that an institution's readings track the ground. Arity: four-place, institution, action, ground return, audience bet, indexed per claim. Symmetry: the ground is pinned; the audience and the institution are not interchangeable with it, and a ground made a party to the disagreement has already become a frame (Fig 2 :47). Scale: first order for a settled claim, second order for an institutional record, third order for a field of comparing institutions. The claim-to-institution carry is lossy; a gap `R_T` is owed.
- **Domain 1, claim settlement.** Actors: assessors, evidence, the claim. Currency: residual per claim. Anchor: the ground named in Fig 2 :29. Question type: partition (which readings the ground pays).
- **Domain 2, the verifier seat.** Actors: peer readers, auditors, affected people. Currency: agreement versus residual. Anchor: the ground, never agreement. Question type: independence and the admissibility of human judgment as an anchor, which Fig 2 :36-38 limits to the audience half.
- **Domain 3, the institutional chain.** Actors: successive panels. Currency: handoff fidelity and ground pressure (Fig 7 :37-39). Anchor: selection between rooms (Fig 7 :35). Question type: persistence as a sequence of handoffs.
- **Domain 4, the disagreement compound.** Actors: frames whose closures differ. Currency: evidence-pool overlap. Anchor: one pinned ground each frame ranges against separately (Fig 2 :46). Question type: triangulation versus drift.
- **X1 standing as a property.** No anchor independent of what the institution produces. Not a domain yet.
- **X2 agreement counts.** Measurable but already inside Domain 2. Absorbed.

Order by dependency: make the failure statable, make it violable, secure an unauthored pool, then ground contact, then correction, then the lossy carry upward (Fig 3 :20; Fig 10 :16-19).

## Round trip gap R_T

The Symbols table defines `R_T` as the gap between what was sent up and what came back down, `gap(x, (T_E ∘ T_A)(x))`, measuring what the coarser description cannot hold. Fig 4 :30 requires it to be measured rather than assumed. I ran the round trip on the condensation this folder actually uses.

1. **Sent up (x):** the verbatim figure set, chiefly Fig 1 :26-27 (self-scoring disqualified), Fig 2 :29-47 (anchor, independence, non-party ground), Fig 7 :35-39 (selection between rooms) and Fig 10 :42-93 (disqualifiability, declared stake, correctable provenance, reputation).
2. **Abstraction (T_A):** the shorthand carried in `01` and `07`: keep the ground reachable, keep disagreement independent, keep rulings correctable, declare the stake.
3. **Concretisation (T_E):** a concrete institutional policy, for example publish an uncertainty statement, diversify the review panel and keep an audit log.

**R_T is nonzero.** Three things the shorthand holds do not survive the trip back down:

- the **anchor admissibility condition** (Fig 2 :36-38): shorthand "use audience judgment" collapses two halves that the figures keep separate. A concrete policy that measures trust only by survey has dropped the ground half;
- the **unauthored-pool requirement** (Fig 10 :73-74): shorthand "diversify the panel" is an authored room if the institution appoints it. The concrete step can satisfy the wording while violating the condition;
- the **legitimacy versus correctness distinction** (Fig 10 :65): shorthand "keep an audit log" is satisfied by a perfectly provenanced wrong ruling. The concrete policy can look compliant forever without ever correcting anything.

Conversely, one thing survives cleanly: an institution cannot score itself with the credibility it produces, and a ground made a party to the disagreement has become a frame. That is the residue worth sending. R_T was **not** measured numerically, because no per-claim ground record exists here; it is recorded qualitatively as the enumerated set of dropped constraints. Treating the formula as identical to the metalaws would set R_T to zero and would be the dead-reckoning error Fig 4 :30 names.
