## 1. A shape is a constraint, not a filler

The canonical README states the limit: the framework returns *"what a missing thing would have to look like (a shape, not a name)."* Fig 9 :32: *"you get a kind of thing, never the answer itself."* Fig 13 :134-135: *"So the system can report that it lacks something rather than producing an answer anyway. Unreachable is a verdict here, not a silence,"* and *"It cannot say which primitive is missing, because naming it would mean already holding it."*

From *The Library of Babel*, as recorded in the local canonical mapping: *"You can cut your search short if the set of books returned is small and descriptive enough that it provides a general shape of the answer that satisfies your question."*

So a **shape** is the narrowed form of an answer you can state before you can name it. It constrains; it does not fill. For HIS-0020 the shape is: *a conjunction of conditions under which a checker can correct an institution, plus an instrument that distinguishes a failure of one condition from a failure of another.* "Publish more data" is a name. "Keep a ground that does not update, host independent frames, keep the ruling correctable" is a shape.

## 2. How the shape is obtained

- *Library of Babel*: use the vocabulary already closest to the thing, look for recurring new terms that overlap it, recurse; *"With each new term gained, you narrow the space and deepen your vocabulary."*
- Fig 6 :30: *"outward: does another frame already hold it? The description travels. Fails by being sharpened into terms only we can read. Cast it in effect terms."* Fig 9 :30-31: *"your words are borrowed too, so describe the effect rather than the cause."*
- *Decoding Tacit Knowledge*, per the local canonical mapping: *"When contact isn't possible, the best you can send is a plain description of what you'd expect to see, because anyone can check that against the world."* That is exactly the discipline of the outreach email in `02`: state what a failure would look like, not what the framework believes.
- *Library of Babel* on stopping: the finish line is also the starting place, because the answer is composed through the process of locating it.

## 3. What licenses a name

Only two things, both outside the shape itself:

1. **The ground settles it.** `THE_FORMULA.md` step 5: *"the ground settles it. Verified only by what pays. A gate is not the ground."* The canonical README adds that the framework *"stops at the limit of what the available instruments can distinguish, and records the lineage so the stop is honest."*
2. **A provenanced import from a frame whose closure differs** (Fig 6 :23, Fig 8 :50-52), with a source gate and a shadow test.

*They're Scanning Books*, per the local canonical mapping: *"The real import is not a new term in the existing space. It is a new dimension of the space."* Also: *"Declaring the primitives does not make them neutral. It makes the choice visible."* So naming a governance design as *the* trustworthy one converts a visible choice into an invisible one.

## 4. Why "trust score" is the specific forbidden error here

*The Hard Problem is About Source* (opened 2026-11-23) gives the mechanism. An instrument that reads green *"cannot comment on the source"*. *"If several paths create the same behavior, then behavior is a projection with lossy information"*, and *"The answer is unreached until a better instrument exists."* It was the spectrometer, not an argument, that resolved the case.

Applied here: a survey that reads high trust cannot say whether the source was ground contact, independence of verifiers, correctability, or a shared publicity pool. Four sources can produce the same reading. Naming one from the reading is the metamerism error. What is owed is a discriminator that separates the sources, which is exactly what the three conditions and the three refuters in `08_expert_verification_packet.md` are for.

Fig 1 :26-27 closes the loop: *"predicates minted, and credibility accrued"* are *"frame-internal. A frame cannot score itself with a quantity it also produces."* A trust score issued by the institution being scored is the error in its purest form.

## 5. Q: what shape does this question actually return?

**Answer:** a disqualifiability structure, not a trust-seeking policy.

The shape has three load-bearing relations and one stop:

- a ground that the institution's readers cannot move (Fig 2 :29-34);
- frames whose disagreement is independent because they draw on different evidence (Fig 2 :31-32, :46);
- a record in which a wrong ruling stays correctable (Fig 10 :62-66);
- a `gap` at the point where the framework would have to name which design earns trust (Fig 9 :32).

The essays explain why this and not a nicer-sounding answer. From *The Recursive Self-Improvement Trap* (opened 2026-11-23): *"You may all agree and be wrong. You may set your clock to someone else's, who was setting your clock to yours, and be off by many deviations over time. You need ground truth to define where you stand."* And: *"Prestige must never be allowed to validate an action directly."* Reputation is the record of past success, not a license to skip the ground. An institution that trades on reputation is doing exactly what the essay names as the recursive rot of a field.

## 6. Correction to my own §3

I initially wrote this section as if "shape, not a name" forbids naming any concrete institutional practice. That overstates it. The canonical phrase governs a **missing** thing, one not held in Γ (Fig 13 :135). Disqualifiability, independence and correctability are not missing primitives invented for this question; they are figure-held relations (Fig 2, Fig 10). Naming them is a binding from the institutional frame onto relations already in Γ, not the invention of a seventh term. What remains forbidden is naming a particular governance design as *the* answer on the strength of a trust reading alone. Both statements are now recorded so neither is quietly lost. This matches the retraction pattern in the exemplar's own `05_what_shape_means.md` §5b.

## 7. Simulation status and prestige

From *The Recursive Self-Improvement Trap* (opened 2026-11-23): *"the output is only as good as the validator. The agent, or the tool, or the frame, or the theory, cannot validate itself."* And: *"LLMs can quickly simulate an approximate answer/output with the formula found in the architecture post... However, the truly testable output would need to be derived from the real architecture or working the formula yourself."*

This file is therefore a canonical reading checked for internal consistency, not a measurement of any institution and not an independent witness. Its agreement with the figures is expected, because it shares their author and pool.

**Prestige is not action.** The same essay: *"Prestige can fund an action. It can give an action a budget to be tried. But it cannot settle an action. Only the ground can settle it. The Ground Maintainer must be blind to prestige."* For outreach this means: do not gather prestigious names to endorse the shape; give the shape a budget to be tried and let the ground settle it.

## 8. Two consequences for outreach

1. **Lead with what could fail.** *Decoding Tacit Knowledge* makes a plain description of expected observations the only portable thing when contact is impossible. The first contact therefore carries the residual and its refuter, never the architecture (Fig 10 :52).
2. **Do not pool agreement.** Fig 7 :13: *"agreement compounds, evidence does not."* A later endorsement from someone who read the theory is a convention, not corroboration (Fig 2 :41). Record the stage-1 verdict first.
