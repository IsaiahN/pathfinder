## QUESTION

Decide, in effect terms, how conservation planning should account for a species that moves across administrative and national borders. Option A: treat each jurisdiction as the planning unit, enumerate inside the line, and report compliance per unit. Option B: treat the species' contact field across its full range as the planning unit, use the border only as a delivery mechanism, and verify against independent readings of the population. What each leads to: A produces healthy per-jurisdiction reports alongside a failing cross-border population, because the residual is split across slots and the connection between patches is not recorded; B keeps the residual indexed per species, makes the bond between patches the object, and buys independence in proportion to the cost of being wrong. No numerical recipe is requested or returned.

## DOMAIN CENSUS

R by arity, symmetry and scale, then one line per domain with actors, currency and anchor; partition or union; order by dependency.

- **R (residual).** Arity: one species slot held in part by k jurisdictions, repeated over many species, so indexed per slot. Symmetry: the border is a symmetric spatial relation; the migration is ordered (→), because a bird must reach the stopover before the next leg. Scale: local site, region, nation, range, with the membrane between scales as the failure point. Fig 9 names this a union of overlapping conditions, not one hard question: a case in two conditions is not a harder case of either.
- **Domain 1, ecological and habitat frame.** Actors: the focal species, prey, predators, pollinators, competitors, vegetation, water, fire, season, terrain. Currency: contact, survival, reproduction, gene flow, population viability. Anchor: an independent reading of what the population actually does. Union, because breeding, stopover and wintering conditions overlap.
- **Domain 2, administrative and planning frame.** Actors: agencies, legislatures, landowners, Indigenous nations, boundary lines, mandates, budgets. Currency: legal authority, budget, compliance, hectares designated. Anchor: the species outcome if independently read; the mandate is a convention, not a ground. Partition-shaped at the level of legal authority, but it is a delivery frame, not the ecology.
- **Domain 3, coordination and verification frame.** Actors: range states, treaty bodies, scientific councils, local communities, independent monitors. Currency: agreements, data sharing, coordinated action, refusals. Anchor: the ground, the population outcome, never agreement between agencies.
- **Order by dependency (Fig 3).** Perception of movement → vocabulary for cross-border contact → objective over the full range → coordinated plan → carried method. Describe the residual before choosing a frame (Fig 9 :27-28); import with provenance and a shadow test before treating a neighbour's data as settled.

Strain test applied: Fig 13 :75 governs composition of the six substrate terms; the border question governs the scope of an enumeration and the status of a convention. Different objects, so no strain after re-census. `01` §7 records two live type questions and two retracted readings.

## VERDICT (from the figures)

The shape survives; the surgery is on the unit, not the animal. Enumerate the species' contact field across the full range and cascade outward, because a first-order scope misses the effect that matters. Treat the border as a convention that fixes authority and budget, not as a grounded fact about the species. Split the cross-border conditions as the union they are, and keep the residual per species rather than in a national average. Connectivity is a bond between held atoms, so same patches under different connections are different substances and a hectare count cannot decide the case. Coordinate by triangulation between independent readings, not by negotiation, and hold the treaty as one room in a schedule with no last room. The framework does not return a corridor width, patch count, signatory count or fraction of range; that is a declared `gap` of the unreached-at-this-budget kind, and unreachable is a verdict, not a silence. Mode `specified` on the per-domain claims, `general` on the structural shape, not `grounded`.

- **cost if wrong:** bounded and locatable if the packet is sent as written, because it carries mode, refuters and provenance before the conclusion. The real cost is spending the independence of outside readers by sending a recipe or a recording instead of a generator. Fig 2 :42 prices that cost ("Buy independence in proportion to the cost of being wrong."), and Fig 10 :42 warns that "A verifier that can reconstruct the claim cannot verify it." That cost is avoidable by leading with the shape and the refuters.
- **undoable:** yes. The border-as-delivery-mechanism distinction, the per-species indexing and the union split can be revised without withdrawing the derivation. A wrong arrangement can be replaced without a new primitive, and no figure atom is lost. A sent claim can be superseded by a corrected packet with the retraction visible.
- **reversed by:** a ground reading, not agreement. A boundary experiment in which changing only the line changes persistence would make the border a ground; a same-atom experiment showing indistinguishable persistence under different connections would kill the isomer claim; a measured absence of any cascade for a species would narrow Fig 11's example to that species; a national aggregate that predicts a collapse before any per-species reading would reverse the indexing verdict; and a verbatim figure line returning a numerical recipe would close the gap. Until one of these fires, the verdict stands as `candidate`.
