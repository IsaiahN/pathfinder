## 1. A shape is a constraint, not a filler

The canonical figures README states the limit: the framework gives "what a missing thing would have to look like (a shape, not a name)." Fig 9 :32: "you get a kind of thing, never the answer itself." Fig 13 :134: "So the system can report that it lacks something rather than producing an answer anyway. Unreachable is a verdict here, not a silence,"

From *The Library of Babel* (opened 2026-11-23): "You can cut your search short if the set of books returned is small and descriptive enough that it provides a general shape of the answer that satisfies your question."

So a **shape** is the narrowed form of an answer you can state before you can name a final instance. It constrains; it does not fill. For this question:

- **Shape:** enumerate the habitat by contact across the union; the border is a convention, not a ground; the conditions form a union; coordination is triangulation between independent readings.
- **Not a shape, but an instance or a number:** a named corridor, a 200-metre width, a specific treaty, a required number of signatory states.

## 2. How a shape is obtained

- *The Library of Babel*: use the vocabulary already held that is closest to the thing, look for recurring new terms that overlap or correlate with it, recurse. "With each new term gained, you narrow the space by definition and deepen your vocabulary." That is exactly what happened here: "border" was the given word, and proximity produced "range state", "flyway", "metapopulation", "contact cascade", "percolation threshold".
- The same essay diagnoses the failure mode directly: "Perhaps your problem was a bundle or conflation of several problems masquerading as one? You will need to search all of those problems individually to find their answers and then combine them in a satisfactory way." This is Fig 9 :54 in prose and it governs the union finding in `01` F6.
- Fig 6 :30: "Cast it in effect terms." Fig 9 :30-31: "your words are borrowed too, so describe the effect rather than the cause." The effect here is a population that fails while every jurisdiction reports success. The cause is not assumed.
- *Decoding Tacit Knowledge* (carried from the exemplar): "When contact isn't possible, the best you can send is a plain description of what you'd expect to see, because anyone can check that against the world." This is why the outreach email leads with a checkable failure condition.
- *The Library of Babel* on stopping: "You will know that you are done because the finish line will also be the starting place, as there is nowhere else to search and nothing else that can describe the answer better." For this question the stop is reached when the per-species residual no longer registers, not when a treaty is signed.

## 3. What licenses a name

Only two things, both outside the shape itself:

1. **The ground settles it.** Formula step 5: "SETTLE — the ground settles it. Verified only by what pays. A gate is not the ground." The README: the framework "stops at the limit of what the available instruments can distinguish, and records the lineage so the stop is honest." For this question, an instrument that measures survival or gene flow across the range pays for the name.
2. **A provenanced import from a frame whose closure differs**, with a source gate and a shadow test (Fig 6 :23, Fig 8 :50-52). A neighbouring country's monitoring dataset is such an import; it needs provenance and a statement of which gap it explains.

*They're Scanning Books When They Should* (carried from the exemplar): "The real import is not a new term in the existing space. It is a new dimension of the space." Also: "A minimum viable set of six terms is a human's careful guess at reality's compression axes; another sensorium's minimum set need not be those six." And: "Declaring the primitives does not make them neutral. It makes the choice visible."

## 4. Source discrimination is the relevant hard problem

*The Hard Problem is About Source* (opened 2026-11-23) gives the mechanism. Metamerism: an instrument reads green and "it cannot comment on the source." Several sources can create the same reading. "If several paths create the same behavior, then behavior is a projection with lossy information." And: "An instrument that can't distinguish these cases demonstrates its own inadequacy, it cannot claim anything substantial about what is being measured or what causes it." The resolution in the essay's example came from the spectrometer, not an argument: "It was proven wrong not by argument, but by the arrival of a better instrument."

Apply to this question. A national compliance report reads "habitat protected." That reading is a projection. The sources could be: a connected range, three fenced fragments, a paper designation with no management, or a population that has already gone. The reading cannot discriminate. What is owed is an instrument that can, for example independent population monitoring across the range, not a better argument about the meaning of "connectivity." This is the deepest reason the border cannot be the unit: the border is part of the measurement apparatus, and an apparatus that reads the same value for a living population and an empty one is inadequate.

## 5. The correction: "shape, not a name" governs missing primitives

### 5a. The overstatement, preserved

An earlier reading of this folder applied "shape, not a name" to every concrete term in the answer, including "range state", "flyway" and "corridor". That was too broad.

### 5b. The narrowed reading

The canonical phrase is qualified: the framework returns the shape of a **missing** thing. The subject is a primitive not held in the library. "Range state", "flyway" and "corridor" are not missing primitives. They are bindings from the ecology and policy frames onto atoms already held: network ("that entities relate, and influence travels", Fig 13 :24), space ("near, far, between, contained", Fig 13 :10), and sequence (Fig 12 :71: "A then B; B needs A to have happened"). Naming them is provenance work, not the invention of a seventh term.

What remains forbidden:

- claiming the six-term set derived those names by itself;
- claiming the binding moves the wall (Fig 6 :21: "MINT composes inside the closure and can never add an atom");
- treating the name as settled by the ground when only the fragment was observed.

Both readings are recorded so neither is quietly lost. The narrower one governs the folder.

## 6. Prestige is not action

*The Recursive Self-Improvement Trap* (opened 2026-11-23): "Prestige must never be allowed to validate an action directly." And: "Prestige can fund an action. It can give an action a budget to be tried. But it cannot settle an action. Only the ground can settle it. The Ground Maintainer must be blind to prestige."

For this question, the prestige trap is the well-known treaty, the famous reserve, the celebrated corridor project. Any of them can fund an action and none of them can settle whether a given population persists. The verification packet therefore asks the reader to check four failure conditions, not to endorse a pedigree.
