## Relevant wider literature, by field

- **Conservation biology and corridor design.** The wildlife corridor source supports the claim that connectivity is the object, not the patch list, and distinguishes passage users from corridor dwellers. It also records that corridor implementation needs a shared goal and rules across diverse stakeholders, which is a governance claim, not an ecological one.
- **Population ecology and metapopulation theory.** The metapopulation source supports per-patch-plus-connectivity reasoning and gives a mathematical threshold (percolation) for fragmentation effects. It is the closest world source to a figure line about thresholds, but it does not supply a number for any particular species.
- **Movement ecology and flyways.** The flyway source supports the claim that the planning unit is the entire migration space and that flyways cross continents. The CMS quotation inside it is an institutional definition, so it overlaps the CMS source and the two count as one witness for the governance claim.
- **International environmental law.** The CMS source supports the range-state framing. The transboundary protected area source supports the institutional forms (TBPA, TFCA, peace park) and, by recording local criticism, supports the claim that agreement between states is not the species ground.
- **Ecotoxicology.** The biomagnification source independently corroborates the figure's own DDT and eagles example (Fig 11 :52-54). This is the genuinely low-overlap witness in the set: ecotoxicology rather than conservation policy or framework metatheory.
- **Human dimensions and Indigenous knowledge.** The wildlife corridor source records that "Indigenous ideas of mobility hold that being able to make decisions around movement helps produce Indigenous relationality" and that Indigenous knowledge is often substituted with settler landscape ecology in large-scale corridor plans. This is a constraint on the enumeration, not a decorative aside.

Overlap declaration: the five ecology and governance sources form two pools. The corridor, metapopulation and flyway pages are one conservation-ecology pool. The CMS and transboundary protected area pages are one institutional pool. Biomagnification is a separate ecotoxicology pool. The three essays share the framework author's evidence pool completely with the figures and carry no independent witness weight.

## Distribution rules: offer, do not push

Figure-grounded reasons:

- **Fig 4 :22** — "Only methods cross up. Only head starts come down." Sending the full architecture before anyone has described a residual sends a recording of one occasion. Lead with the shape claim and the refuter.
- **Fig 10 :42** — "A verifier that can reconstruct the claim cannot verify it." A recipient who has absorbed the framework before giving a verdict has joined the frame being checked.
- **Fig 9 :27-28** — "describe it before you go looking, or any frame you pick will seem to fit." Ask what they would expect to see if the claim were true before giving them the sources.
- **Fig 2 :40** — "disqualifying for a verdict: agreement about the world is worthless when the agreeing frames share an evidence pool." A list selected for receptiveness produces a convention, not corroboration.
- **Fig 2 :42** — "Buy independence in proportion to the cost of being wrong." Prefer a second, independent monitoring reading over a second agreeing agency.

**Recommended order in a reply:** (1) the shape claim and the four refuters; (2) the question "what would you count as a failure here?"; (3) only after a recorded verdict, the three source links, with the note that the verbatim figure text, not the summary, is the authority; (4) the method, with the instruction to write a fresh refuter and state a mode.

## The two-stage offer

1. **Stage 1 before stage 2.** Fig 10 :52 — "Bring a characterised residual, never the source." Fig 2 :45 — "Frames range against the ground. They do not negotiate with it." The recipient checks the shape claim without holding the framework interior.
2. **Stage 2 is opt in by construction.** Once a recipient has absorbed the theory, their agreement stops being evidence. Their stage-1 verdict must be recorded before any theory is shown, and later agreement is never pooled as corroboration.
3. **Stage 3 sends a method.** Fig 4 :22 permits this: a generator can be given away without giving away an answer. Each user still needs a ground and a refuter written before the run.

**Hard precondition:** the stage-1 output must survive. The refuters in `08_expert_verification_packet.md` are fixed in advance; if one fires, the packet is corrected and the correction is left visible rather than silently rewritten.

## Hosting

Nothing in this folder is sent by this agent. Hosting and sending are the user's to do. Prefer an opt-in download page that lets a recipient decline contact over an attachment pushed to a list. That is closer to the figure's rule about a verifier who must not hold the interiors, and it avoids the legal failure of unsolicited bulk email.
