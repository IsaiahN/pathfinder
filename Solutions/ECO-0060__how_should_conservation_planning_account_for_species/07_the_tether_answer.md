# The Tether's answer: species that cross administrative and national borders

**Mode: `specified` on the per-domain verdicts, `general` on the structural shape, `gap` on the numerical recipe. Not `grounded`.** This is a shape and a route, not a result. It is a simulated derivation checked against the figures (`05_what_shape_means.md` §7), not a ground reading.

## 1. The residual it answers

A species slot is held in part by several jurisdictions. Its habitat is a contact field that does not stop at a line. The planning frame's closure is the jurisdiction; the species' closure is the range. The residual is the gap between them, and it shows up as a population failing across the union while each jurisdiction's own report looks healthy. The question is a union of overlapping conditions, not one hard problem with opposite answers (Fig 9 :57: "What remains is smaller and it is also categorically different: a case in two conditions is not a harder case of either.").

## 2. The shape, one loop per domain

### D1, the ecological frame. Verdict: `specified`, triangulated

- Enumerate the habitat by contact, outward until the cascade stops mattering. Fig 11 :43: "The habitat is enumerated, never composed." Fig 11 :44: "List everything in contact with the residual, then what is in contact with those, and outward until the cascade stops mattering."
- Scope to direct contact misses the third-order effect. Fig 11 :52-53: "And contact cascades. Scoping the habitat to direct contact is how DDT reached the eagles: first-order enumeration, second-order effect, and the residual that mattered was three steps out."
- Connectivity is a bond between atoms already held, not a new atom or a patch count. Fig 12 :5: "A set of ingredients is not one. The bond is the rest of the information, and it is the half nothing was recording."
- Valence is relative to the residual, so there is no standing list of good interventions (Fig 11 :49-51).

### D2, the administrative frame. Verdict: `specified`, single-source on the figure side with world corroboration

- The border is a convention, not a ground for the ecology. Fig 10 :18: "a declared meaning is not a grounded one." A convention that decides an outcome "has stopped being one" (Fig 10 :47).
- A jurisdiction that updates its reference on its own reports drifts coherently with itself (Fig 2 :33-35). The anchor must be an independent reading of the population.
- One jurisdiction's closure cannot derive its neighbour's atom. Fig 6 :21: "MINT composes inside the closure and can never add an atom." The missing fact must be imported with provenance and a shadow test.
- Per-jurisdiction compliance counts are pooled readings. Fig 10 :26: "saturated metrics, pooled readings." They sever the channel while the ground does not decay.

### D3, the coordination frame. Verdict: `specified`, triangulated

- Coordination is triangulation between independent grounds, not negotiation. Fig 2 :35: "Alignment is triangulation, not negotiation. A mirror cannot correct you." Two agencies reading one report are one frame (Fig 2 :31).
- Low overlap is the asset. Fig 8 :47: "Independence is one quantity read twice." Fig 2 :42: "Buy independence in proportion to the cost of being wrong."
- The treaty is a room in a schedule, not a final closure. Fig 7 :6: "the limit: no last room"; Fig 7 :36: "A chain of mirrors is one long room. The next room must differ from the last, and match the residual it surfaced."
- Managed connectivity is permanent work (Fig 11 :63: "Which is why a managed ecosystem is permanent work rather than a solved state.").

**The three verdicts are kept separate and are never averaged into a single conservation score.** Fig 1 :22: "Aggregating across slots is how a live signal disappears into an average."

## 3. The practical rule that follows

1. **Enumerate the species' contact field across the full range.** Read the actors, conditions and relations off the world; cascade outward until the effect stops mattering.
2. **Bind the objective over the full range before buying more monitoring.** Write down what would count as a viable cross-border population, then let that ordinary term accrue a residual.
3. **Keep the residual per species.** Put national indices on the budget side only. Fig 9 :60: "Use filters for the budget and witnesses for the verdict. Never let a filter hand you a verdict."
4. **Buy a second independent reading of the population**, not a second agreeing agency.
5. **Treat the border as a delivery mechanism.** Use it for authority, budget and implementation. Do not use it as the unit of ecology.
6. **Select each coordination room against the residual the last one surfaced.** No final room.

## 4. What it explicitly does not say

- Not that borders are unreal. They are real as law and budget; they are just not grounded facts about the species.
- Not that every species needs a corridor. A strict endemic may have no cross-border contact; the falsifier in `01` F2 covers that case.
- Not that translocation is a solution. Fig 11 :66-69 gives two silent failure modes for a substituted habitat.
- Not a universal width, patch count, signatory count or fraction of range. That is a declared `gap` (Fig 9 :32, Fig 13 :134).
- Not a final institutional design. Fig 7 :6: "the limit: no last room."
- Not `grounded`. Nothing here was settled by a measurement.

## 5. What would settle it — the ground

A source-discriminating instrument, not a further argument. *The Hard Problem is About Source*: "It was proven wrong not by argument, but by the arrival of a better instrument." Here the corresponding tests are:

- **Boundary test.** Does changing only the line, with habitat, movement and demography fixed, change persistence? If yes, F1 fails.
- **Bond test.** Do identical atoms under different arrangements give different persistence and gene flow? If no, F4 fails.
- **Cascade test.** Is there a measured effect two or more steps out from the breeding site? If no for a given species, F3 does not apply to it.
- **Union test.** Do the cross-border conditions form a union with intersections rather than a clean partition? If partition, F6 does not apply.
- **Aggregate test.** Does a national index predict a collapse before any per-species reading does? If yes, F9 fails.
- **Discriminator.** Can an independent monitoring reading tell a real population signal from a pooled reading of several declines? Until it can, the compliance report is a projection with lossy information.

Standing falsifiers from `01` §4 are repeated in `08` and `09`. If any fires, the claim is narrowed or withdrawn with the retraction left visible.

## 6. The honest stop

**Unreached, not unreachable.** The figures return the shape: enumerate contact, keep the residual per slot, triangulate, no last room. They do not return the number. The corridor width, stepping-stone spacing and signatory count require an instrument that was not run here. Fig 8 :39: "So a search that finds nothing is never a proof of absence." The lineage is recorded so the stop is checkable. "Indiscernibility is not identity."

## 7. One-paragraph version for outreach

> The Tether does not give a corridor width or a treaty design. It says that the unit to enumerate is the species' contact field, not the jurisdiction: where it breeds, feeds, rests and disperses, outward until the cascade stops mattering, because scoping to direct contact is how DDT reached the eagles. A border is a convention that fixes authority and budget, not a grounded fact about the animal, and a national compliance score is a projection that cannot say whether the range is connected. The cross-border conditions form a union of overlapping conditions, so one lever leaves the intersections standing; coordination is triangulation between independent readings of the population, not negotiation between agencies, and the treaty is a room in a schedule rather than a final closure. Two plans with the same patches but different connections are isomers, which is why hectares cannot decide the case. What the framework offers is that shape, four refuters written in advance, and the instruments that would close it. The number stays open until the ground pays for it.
