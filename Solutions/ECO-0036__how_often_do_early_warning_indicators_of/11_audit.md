## Independent domain census

I redid the census before reading the primary's conclusions, using the WATSON order: R by arity, symmetry and scale; one line per domain with actors / currency / anchor; partition or union; order by dependency.

- **R (arity / symmetry / scale).** The residual is the gap between an EWS alarm and an independently observed transition, indexed by system, indicator, lead-time window and threshold convention. Arity: two conditions joined by "or" plus an averaging layer, so a **union compound**, not one hard question. Fig 9 :5 "are not one hard question but several well-formed ones. Split it rather than search." Symmetry: the conditions are ordered, not symmetric; the Operators table calls `⋛` "a threshold relation between two quantities" with the test "is it about which is larger, rather than both being present?" Scale: a local statistic is carried to a system verdict, so a lossy transform with an owed R_T.
- **Domain 1, indicator frame.** Actors: variance, lag-1 AC, skewness, kNDVI clusters, the analyst choosing window and detrending. Currency: signal strength. Anchor: the time series, an instrument not a ground. Question type: one frame whose reading does not settle the transition.
- **Domain 2, ecological ground.** Actors: ecosystem, stressor, bifurcation, observed regime shift. Currency: the transition. Anchor: the transition only if fixed independently of the indicator. Question type: the thing that settles the question.
- **Domain 3, monitoring channel.** Actors: sensors, sampling interval, observers. Currency: contact with the ground. Anchor: the ground, which does not move while the channel decays. Question type: a separate office, not a frame to be corrected by agreement.
- **Domain 4, selection and aggregation.** Actors: studies, databases, meta-analyses, the frequency statistic. Currency: pooled counts. Anchor: borrowed from D2, and collapses when studies are selected for having collapsed. Question type: union of failure conditions followed by an illegitimate average.
- **Order by dependency.** Maintain contact (D3), describe the residual per slot (D1), unbundle the union (Fig 9 :54), fix the anchor before selection (D2), then compute a rate (D4); refuse a wrong term before minting (Formula step 2).

## Round trip gap R_T

The Symbols table defines R_T as the gap between what was sent up and what came back down, `gap(x, (T_E composed with T_A)(x))`, measuring what the coarser description cannot hold. Fig 4 :30 requires it to be measured rather than assumed zero. I ran the round trip on the condensation the folder actually uses.

1. **Sent up (x):** the verbatim figure set, chiefly Fig 1 :19 and :22 (per-slot residual), Fig 9 :54 (union split), Fig 6 :27 and Fig 10 :24 (three-way split of a miss), Fig 2 :29 and :33 (anchor), Fig 5 :13 and Fig 11 :52 (mis-attachment).
2. **Abstraction (T_A):** the shorthand carried in `07` and `08`: split the "or" into two rates; publish per-slot rates with denominators; split a miss three ways; fix the anchor before selection; a false alarm may be a different residual.
3. **Concretisation (T_E):** the concrete thing a field study would do with it: gather published EWS studies and compute a false-alarm fraction and a miss fraction.

**R_T is nonzero.** Four things the shorthand holds do not survive the trip back down unless the study is designed for them:

- **per-slot indexing** collapses to a study-level count unless the denominator is declared per system, indicator, lead time and convention;
- **the three-way split of a miss** collapses to one "no alarm" bucket unless a source-discriminating instrument is available;
- **the convention and selection bindings** drop out unless the collapse definition and selection rule are registered before the trajectories are examined;
- **the round-trip requirement itself** is easy to omit, so a study can report a rate without ever measuring R_T.

Conversely, two things survive cleanly: the union of two denominators (Fig 9 :54) and the rule that the anchor must not move (Fig 2 :33). Those are the residues worth sending. R_T was **not** measured numerically, because no ground reading exists; it is recorded qualitatively as the enumerated set of dropped constraints. Treating "the summary in `07` is the figure set" as identity would set R_T to zero and would be the dead-reckoning error Fig 4 :30 names. The honest form is a lossy condensation with a measured gap, which is what `09_watson_answer.md` states.
