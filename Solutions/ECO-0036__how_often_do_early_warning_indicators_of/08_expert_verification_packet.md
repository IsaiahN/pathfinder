# Expert verification packet - the Tether on early-warning failure rates

**What this is:** a claim in a form an ecologist or complex-systems researcher can check. Mode, derivation and refuters are stated before the conclusion, so a reader knows what to look for and what would settle it.

**Mode: `general` on the structural constraints, `specified` on the predictions about failure modes, `gap` on the frequency. Not `grounded`.** No measurement is claimed. This is a shape and a route, not a result.

## 1. The claim, in one paragraph

The question "how often do early-warning indicators of collapse give false alarms or fail to appear?" is a union of two conditions with different denominators, and a single pooled rate answers neither. A false alarm is an alarm not followed by collapse, counted against systems that did not collapse; a missed warning is a collapse not preceded by an alarm, counted against systems that did. They are isomers over a shared atom inventory: same atoms, different arrangement, different consequence (Fig 12 :117). A rate has meaning only with a per-slot denominator, a collapse outcome settled independently of the indicator, a declared alarm threshold and a stated study-selection design. "Fail to appear" must be split into genuine absence, a signal below instrument resolution and a decayed monitoring channel, which look identical from inside the indicator frame. A false alarm can be a correct reading of a different residual three steps out in the habitat. The figures return these constraints, not the frequency. The requested number is a `gap` at this budget and by this method, reachable only by a pre-registered, per-slot ground reading with a fixed anchor, a maintained channel and a measured round-trip gap.

## 2. Three checkable sub-claims

### C1 - the two failure modes are a union with separate denominators (checkable by reading Figures 9 and 12, and by comparing with signal detection theory)

- **Statement:** false alarms and missed warnings cannot be added into one rate. They have different denominators, different base rates and different costs. The question's "or" is a union, not a partition.
- **Source, verbatim:** Fig 9 :54 - "And when the split lands on a union rather than a partition, unbundle before you search again." Fig 9 :57-58 - "It stops being a mixture and becomes a specific compound." Fig 12 :117 - "isomer. Same atoms, different bond, different substance. The difference is invisible in the formula and decisive in the behaviour." Operators :49 defines `⋛` as "a threshold relation between two quantities," with the test "is it about which is larger, rather than both being present?"
- **Refuter:** show one mechanism, one denominator and one cost structure for both failure modes, or show a single pooled rate invariant to per-system, per-indicator, per-lead-time and per-convention decomposition. Then the union reading fails and a single rate is legitimate.

### C2 - "failure to appear" is three states, not one (checkable by reading Figures 6, 8 and 10)

- **Statement:** a collapse with no alarm can be genuine absence, a real signal the instrument could not resolve, or a monitoring channel that decayed while the ground kept the transition. From inside the indicator frame these are one observation, so counting them as one attributes a channel defect to the ecosystem.
- **Source, verbatim:** Fig 6 :27 - "Which is why some unreachability is depth and some is genuine absence, and from inside the frame the two look the same." Fig 10 :24 - "the ground does not decay, the channel does"; Fig 10 :28 - "you have lost touch"; Fig 10 :25-26 - "stale percepts, dropped transients," and "saturated metrics, pooled readings." Fig 8 :39 - "So a search that finds nothing is never a proof of absence."
- **Refuter:** a source-discriminating instrument showing that a given "failure to appear" was genuine absence at every resolvable scale, not depth and not channel decay. Fig 8 :32 - "Closure is idempotent. Composing inside a closed set never leaves it, so no search, however long, adds an atom." A failed search alone cannot settle the distinction; an instrument that discriminates sources can.

### C3 - the ground must be settled independently of the indicator (checkable by reading Figures 2, 5, 10 and 11, and against the selection-bias literature)

- **Statement:** the collapse definition and the study-selection rule must be fixed before trajectories are seen. If "collapse" is defined after the path is observed, or studies are included because they collapsed, the ground becomes another frame and the reported rate is manufactured by its own convention. A related consequence: an alarm not followed by the monitored collapse may be a correct reading of a different residual in the habitat, so it must not be booked as a pure error without source discrimination.
- **Source, verbatim:** Fig 2 :29 - "An anchor is whatever settles the question and does not change its answer because of what any frame thinks." Fig 2 :33 - "The anchor must not update." Fig 10 :47 - "a convention that decides an outcome has stopped being one." Fig 1 :27 - "A frame cannot score itself with a quantity it also produces." Fig 5 :13 - "mis-attached: refit what you have, do not invent." Fig 11 :52-53 - "And contact cascades. Scoping the habitat to direct contact is how DDT reached the eagles: first-order enumeration, second-order effect, and the residual that mattered was three steps out."
- **Refuter:** exhibit a collapse definition and a study-selection rule invariant to the indicator trajectory and to analyst expectation, and show the reported rate does not move under their variation; and show, with an instrument that discriminates sources, that an alarm not followed by collapse registered nothing at all in the habitat. Either result narrows this sub-claim; both together break it.

## 3. What is explicitly not claimed

- Not that early-warning indicators never work, and not that any named statistic (variance, lag-1 autocorrelation, skewness, kNDVI) should be abandoned.
- Not a pooled frequency, not an average of the two failure modes, and not a correction to a published rate. No number is reported; the number is a `gap`.
- Not that "unreachable from the figures" means "unreachable by an instrument." Fig 8 :40-43 - "Underivable: go outside. Unreachable-at-budget: the term is already in the closure and was not found, so the remedy is more search, not another atom."
- Not that the three-way split of a miss is permanent. Fig 6 :32 - "An instrument is not built from a description. It is improved from a worse instrument already returning something."
- Not `grounded`. This is a simulated derivation checked for internal consistency against the figures, not a ground reading. It cannot settle the frequency it reports on.

## 5. What would settle it

A per-slot ground reading, not agreement. The refuters were written before the conclusion (`01` section 4, `06` FALSIFIER). In practical terms:

1. Does a single pooled rate survive decomposition by system, indicator, lead time and convention? If yes, C1 falls.
2. Is there an instrument that discriminates absence, depth and channel decay for a named miss? If yes, that miss is no longer a compound.
3. Does the reported rate move under a pre-registered collapse definition and a selection design with replicates? If it does not move, C3 is answered in its first part.
4. Is the round trip from local alarm to system verdict measured, with a stated gap R_T? Fig 4 :30: "So take the round trip and measure the gap. Without it you are navigating by dead reckoning: confident about a position nobody checked."

Agreement with this packet is not a refutation or a confirmation. Fig 2 :35: "Alignment is triangulation, not negotiation. A mirror cannot correct you." Fig 10 :42: "A verifier that can reconstruct the claim cannot verify it."

## 6. What an expert can check in twenty minutes

1. Read Fig 9 :54, Fig 12 :117, Fig 6 :27, Fig 10 :24 and Fig 2 :33 - five short passages.
2. Check C1 against signal detection theory: are false alarms and misses independent axes with different costs in your field? If not, say so.
3. Check C2 against your own practice: when an indicator failed to appear, could you tell absence from depth from a decayed channel? Name the instrument, or say there is none.
4. Check C3 against a study you know: was the collapse definition and the selection rule fixed before the trajectories were examined? If yes, give the citation.
5. Send the refutation, not the endorsement. A failure is more useful than agreement. If you would rather not engage, no reply is needed.
