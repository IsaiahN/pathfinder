## Wider literature that bears, noted but not cited as read

The reference lists of the papers above point to further work that would matter for a real frequency study: Boettiger, Ross and Hastings 2013, "Early warning signals: The charted and uncharted territories" (Theoretical Ecology 6:255-264); Carpenter and Brock 2006 on rising variance; Dakos et al. 2008 on slowing down before climate transitions; van Nes and Scheffer 2007 on recovery rate; Biggs et al. 2009 on detecting an impending regime shift in time to avert it. ## Two things in the fetched literature that bear directly on the question

1. **A reading is not a source.** Robel et al. (2026) find variance rising in more than 98% of simulations far from a tipping point, and autocorrelation trends of either sign at roughly chance frequency. Hirsch and Koren (2026) find that detrended autocorrelation is affected by external forcing and stochastic variability. Both are the metamerism error in ecological clothing: an instrument that reads green cannot say whether the source is one green light or a mixture. A count of alarms without source discrimination is a count of readings, not a count of impending collapses.
2. **Selection sets the base rate.** Boettiger and Hastings (2012) show that conditional selection of systems known to have experienced a transition elevates the false-positive rate of common statistics, and that replicated experiments avoid the pitfall. This is why any "how often" figure must carry its selection design in the same sentence.

## The two-stage offer

1. **Stage 1:** send the residual and the pre-registered refuter. The recipient tests the claim against their own knowledge of EWS performance. Their verdict is recorded before any theory is shown.
2. **Stage 2, opt-in only:** if they are curious, offer the figures link and the three papers. Their later agreement is never pooled as corroboration (Fig 2 :40: "agreement about the world is worthless when the agreeing frames share an evidence pool").
3. **Stage 3:** offer the formula as a method to reuse. Each user must state their own ground and write their own refuter before running it, because a frame "cannot check the direction of its own question" (THE_FORMULA.md, Direction).

**Hard precondition:** stages 1 to 3 only work if the stage-1 claim survives. If one of the four refuters fires, the packet is withdrawn and the failure is recorded in `11_audit.md` with provenance.
