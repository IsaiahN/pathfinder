## What an answer would look like

A rate only has meaning with four things attached: a per-slot denominator (which ecosystem, which indicator, which lead time), a ground-defined outcome (did collapse occur, settled independently of the indicator), a stated threshold convention (what counts as an alarm), and a study-selection design (were systems chosen because they collapsed). The sought-for shape is therefore not one number but a set of conditional, per-slot rates with the selection and convention exposed. The audit binds that shape as an ordinary term and lets it accrue a residual, per the formula's Direction step, rather than treating "how often" as a phrase whose direction needs no checking.

## What is deliberately not here

- No personal emails of named individuals.
- No ranking of people by how likely they are to agree with the derivation.
- No bulk-send plan.
- No single pooled frequency presented as a verdict. Fig 1 :19: "The residual is indexed by object slot, not aggregated." Fig 9 :54: "And when the split lands on a union rather than a partition, unbundle before you search again."

Three figure-grounded reasons, in the figures' own words:

1. **Averaging destroys the only signal that says where to look.** Fig 1 :22: "Aggregating across slots is how a live signal disappears into an average."
2. **Agreement is not corroboration when the pool is shared.** Fig 2 :35: "Alignment is triangulation, not negotiation. A mirror cannot correct you."
3. **A convention that decides an outcome stops being a convention.** Fig 10 :47: "a convention that decides an outcome has stopped being one". A threshold chosen after seeing the collapse manufactures the alarm rate it then reports.
