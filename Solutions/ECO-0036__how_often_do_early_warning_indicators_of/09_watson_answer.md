## VERDICT (from the figures)

The figures govern the shape of any answer at `general` and `specified` mode and they govern it decisively. They do not govern a frequency; that is a `gap` at this budget and by this method. The surviving claims: a single pooled rate discards the only signal that says where failure occurs; false alarms and misses are isomers with separate denominators; a miss must be split into absence, depth and channel decay; a false alarm can be a correct reading of a different residual; the collapse anchor and threshold convention must be fixed before selection; the local-to-system transform carries a measurable round-trip gap. The number would require a pre-registered, per-slot ground reading with a fixed anchor, a stated convention, a maintained channel and a reported R_T. This is a `candidate`, simulated result, checked for internal consistency only.

- **cost if wrong:** bounded and locatable. The packet states mode, three checkable sub-claims, verbatim lines and refuters before the conclusion, so an error is findable rather than invisible. The real cost is the opposite error: answering with one unbracketed number and sending it to experts, which discards the per-slot signal (Fig 1 :22) and spends the independence of the readers who check it. Fig 2 :42 "Buy independence in proportion to the cost of being wrong." The cost is avoidable by leading with the shape claim and its refuter.
- **undoable:** yes. No primitive and no published rate is withdrawn. The repair is to split the question, publish per-slot rates with denominators, register the collapse definition and selection rule, and report R_T or say it was not measured. `07_the_tether_answer.md` and `08_expert_verification_packet.md` already carry the corrected form. A sent packet can be superseded.
- **reversed by:** a ground reading, not agreement. A single pooled rate shown invariant to per-system, per-indicator, per-lead-time and per-convention decomposition would break the union verdict; a source-discriminating instrument showing a named miss was genuine absence at every resolvable scale would collapse the three-way split; a collapse definition and selection rule under which the rate does not move would answer the anchor concern; a measured zero round-trip gap would clear the cross-scale claim; a figure line determining a numerical rate without a ground reading would fill the gap. Prestige or repeated endorsement is not such a reading (Fig 2 :35). Until one of these fires, the verdict stands as `candidate`.
