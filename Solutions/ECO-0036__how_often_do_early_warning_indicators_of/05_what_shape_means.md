## 1. A shape is a constraint, not a filler

The figures README states the limit: the framework returns "what a missing thing would have to look like (a shape, not a name)." Fig 9 :32: "you get a kind of thing, never the answer itself." Fig 13 :134: "Unreachable is a verdict here, not a silence."

From *The Library of Babel*: "you can cut your search short if the set of books returned is small and descriptive enough that it provides a general shape of the answer that satisfies your question."

From *How Intelligence Actually Works*: "a tool that provides the shape of something that you described" and "The recursion converges toward indiscernibility, and indiscernibility is not identity."

For ECO-0036 the shape is not a frequency. It is the set of constraints any honest frequency must satisfy: separate denominators, per-slot indexing, an anchor that does not update, a maintained channel, a split between absence, depth and channel decay, and a declared transformation from local reading to system verdict. "An honest rate of `f`" is a shape. "`f` equals 18 percent" is a ground reading we did not take.

## 2. How the shape is obtained

- *Library of Babel*: use the vocabulary closest to the thing, look for new terms that overlap or correlate with it, recurse. "With each new term gained, you are able to narrow the space by definition and deepen your vocabulary."
- Fig 6 :30: "The description travels. Fails by being sharpened into terms only we can read. Cast it in effect terms." Fig 9 :30 to :31: "your words are borrowed too, so describe the effect rather than the cause."
- *Decoding Tacit Knowledge*: "When contact isn't possible, the best you can send is a plain description of what you'd expect to see, because anyone can check that against the world."
- *Arrival of the Fittest*: "Because a Metatheory is recursive, each time you apply it, you find more to map until you have so much of general shape that you no longer need to hunt for the traces of lineage."

The effect terms for this question are the two failure modes themselves: an alarm not followed by collapse, and a collapse not preceded by an alarm. Those are checkable by anyone with a time series and an outcome record, without adopting the framework.

## 3. What licenses a name or a number

Only two things, both outside the shape itself:

1. **The ground settles it.** Formula step 5: "the ground settles it. Verified only by what pays. A gate is not the ground." The README: the framework "stops at the limit of what the available instruments can distinguish, and records the lineage so the stop is honest."
2. **A provenanced import from a frame whose closure differs** (Fig 6 :23, Fig 8 :50 to :52), with a source gate and a shadow test.

*They're Scanning Books* sharpens the second: "The real import is not a new term in the existing space. It is a new dimension of the space." Also: "A minimum viable set of six terms is a human's careful guess at reality's compression axes; another sensorium's minimum set need not be those six." And: "Declaring the primitives does not make them neutral. It makes the choice visible, which is worth a great deal, because a visible choice can be revised and an emergent one cannot."

For ECO-0036, choosing NDVI or kNDVI as the monitored state variable is a visible choice about what counts as the system. It is not neutral, and publishing it as part of the rate is what makes the rate revisable.

## 4. Why a count of alarms is not a count of collapses

*The Hard Problem is About Source* gives the mechanism. Metamerism: an instrument reads green and "cannot comment on the source" - one green light, or yellow plus blue, or RGB, or CMYK, or a scheme not yet discovered. "If several paths create the same behavior, then behavior is a projection with lossy information." And: "An instrument that can't distinguish these cases demonstrates its own inadequacy, it cannot claim anything substantial about what is being measured or what causes it." It was the spectrometer, not an argument, that resolved it.

Here, rising variance can come from critical slowing down near a bifurcation or from atmospheric warming on a low-accumulation ice sheet (Robel et al. 2026). The reading is the same; the source is not. Counting readings as if they were collapses is the metamerism error. Fig 13 :32 adds the noise side: "a remainder compounding through a chain that amplifies is chaos, and the detail a crossing discards reappears above it as noise."

*How a Society of Agents Would Work* carries the same rule into the practice of counting: "Use the success rate per try, not the total. Whatever gets tried most piles up votes." And directly: "Keep the rate per kind of situation. A method that is brilliant in one setting must not be drowned by an average across all of them." It cites Fig 1 : "Aggregating across slots is how a live signal disappears into an average." That is the closest the essays come to answering ECO-0036 in advance, and it supports F1 of the audit.

## 5. What the essays say about the two-stage offer

- *The Recursive Self-Improvement Trap*: "Prestige must never be allowed to validate an action directly... Someone let prestige stand in for proof... Prestige can fund an action. It can give an action a budget to be tried. But it cannot settle an action. Only the ground can settle it. The Ground Maintainer must be blind to prestige." A list of famous EWS researchers asked to endorse the shape claim would be the named collapse.
- *Decoding Tacit Knowledge*: "When contact isn't possible, the best you can send is a plain description of what you'd expect to see." Fig 10 :52: "Bring a characterised residual, never the source." The outreach packet leads with the refuter.
- *The Library of Babel*: "If the final book in your exhaustive search doesn't answer the question, then there are only two possibilities: the nature of your question was unanswerable, or malformed." ECO-0036 is malformed as a single number and well formed once split into two conditional rates. This is a claim about the question, not a claim that ecology has no answer.
- *How Intelligence Actually Works*: "the framework if applied effectively provides us a tool that cuts down to the shape of a thing, problem or question based on the given information available at this point in time." That is the shape offered here.

## 6. My own audit is a simulation, not a ground reading

Same essay: "LLMs can quickly simulate an approximate answer/output with the formula found in the architecture post. It will often be better than the generic outputs that you will usually get otherwise too... However, the truly testable output would need to be derived from the real architecture or working the formula yourself." The *Arrival of the Fittest* appendix is blunter: outputs "could never be verified if the solutions presented were not contrived to fit the formula." The public demo note calls them "simulacra."

So `01_figure_grounded_audit.md`, `06_isomers_and_order.md`, `07_the_tether_answer.md` and `09_watson_answer.md` are simulated derivations checked for internal consistency against the figures. They are not tests of any ecological claim and must be labelled as such. They cannot settle the frequency they report on.

## 7. Correction to my own §3

I initially wrote that "shape, not a name" forbids naming any particular quantity in the answer. That overstates it. The README's phrase governs a **missing** primitive. The failure modes here are not missing primitives; they are observable conditions in the habitat. What they lack is a settled estimate, and the framework declines to supply an estimate without ground contact. The correct form of the correction: the framework returns the constraints on the rate (a shape) and declines to return the rate itself (a ground reading). Both claims are recorded so neither is quietly lost.
