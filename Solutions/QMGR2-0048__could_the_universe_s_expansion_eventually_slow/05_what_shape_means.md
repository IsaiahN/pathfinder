## 1. A shape is a constraint, not a filler

The figures README states the limit: the framework returns "what a missing thing would have to look like (a shape, not a name)." Fig 9 :32: "you get a kind of thing, never the answer itself." Fig 13 :134: "Unreachable is a verdict here, not a silence," and :136: "It can say the question is not one question and must be split before it is searched again."

From *The Library of Babel*: "you can cut your search short if the set of books returned is small and descriptive enough that it provides a general shape of the answer that satisfies your question."

So a **shape** is the narrowed form of an answer you can state before you can name it. It constrains; it does not fill. For this question the shape is: an answer is a ground-settled extrapolation on which all viable model families converge. "Big Rip", "heat death" and "Big Crunch" are names. `WILL_EXPANSION_SLOW(???)` is the shape.

## 2. How a shape is obtained

- *Library of Babel*: use the vocabulary you already have that is closest to the thing, look for recurring new terms that overlap or correlate with it, recurse. "With each new term gained, you are able to narrow the space by definition and deepen your vocabulary."
- Fig 6 :30: "The description travels. Fails by being sharpened into terms only we can read. Cast it in effect terms." Fig 9 :30: "your words are borrowed too, so describe the effect rather than the cause."
- *Decoding Tacit Knowledge*: "When contact isn't possible, the best you can send is a plain description of what you'd expect to see, because anyone can check that against the world."
- *Library of Babel* on stopping: "You will know that you are done because the finish line will also be the starting place, as there is nowhere else to search and nothing else that can describe the answer better." The paradox: "you have already composed the answer through the process of locating it."

For the cosmic fate question, the closest existing vocabulary is the measured expansion history (H(z), the acoustic scale, supernova distances). Recursing on the residual, the recurring new terms are "dark energy", "w(z)", "curvature". The narrowing stops at the union horizon: the question is not malformed, but it is a bundle (Fig 9 :57), and the missing instrument is the discriminator that resolves w(z).

## 3. What licenses a name

Only two things, both outside the shape itself:

1. **The ground settles it.** `THE_FORMULA.md` step 5: "the ground settles it. Verified only by what pays. A gate is not the ground." The README: the framework "stops at the limit of what the available instruments can distinguish, and records the lineage so the stop is honest."
2. **A provenanced import from a frame whose closure differs** (Fig 6 :23, Fig 8), with a source gate and a shadow test.

*They're Scanning Books* sharpens the second: "The real import is not a new term in the existing space. It is a new dimension of the space." And: "A minimum viable set of six terms is a human's careful guess at reality's compression axes; another sensorium's minimum set need not be those six." Also: "Declaring the primitives does not make them neutral. It makes the choice visible."

So the six terms are a visible guess, not nature's own list. Naming a fate from them turns a visible choice into an invisible one.

## 4. Why "no fate named" is not the same as "no answer possible"

*The Hard Problem is About Source* gives the mechanism. Metamerism: an instrument reads green and "cannot comment on the source." Multiple sources (green, yellow plus blue, RGB, CMYK) give the same reading. "If several paths create the same behavior, then behavior is a projection with lossy information." And: "It was proven wrong not by argument, but by the arrival of a better instrument."

Here the observed w(z) is the green reading. ΛCDM, quintessence and phantom can produce indistinguishable readings over the observed redshift range while implying different futures. Naming the fate from w(z) alone would be the metamerism error. What is owed is a discriminator (a source-discriminating cosmological instrument), not a name.

*The Recursive Self-Improvement Trap*: "the output is only as good as the validator. The agent, or the tool, or the frame, or the theory, cannot validate itself." So this simulated derivation cannot settle the fate it reports on.

## 5. Q: What licenses a particular fate? ANSWERED

**Nothing in the framework.** The framework returns a shape. A particular fate is licensed only by the ground settling a measurement, or by a provenanced import with a shadow test. Until one of those, the name is the searcher's own contribution and must be recorded as such.

**Does this folder commit the forbidden error?** No. It keeps the slot marked `WILL_EXPANSION_SLOW(???)` and records `gap` on the literal physical question (`01` F1, `07` §2). ## 6. Prestige is not action, and this is a simulation

**Prestige is not action.** From *The Recursive Self-Improvement Trap*: "Prestige must never be allowed to validate an action directly ... Prestige can fund an action. It can give an action a budget to be tried. But it cannot settle an action. Only the ground can settle it. The Ground Maintainer must be blind to prestige." A campaign that gathers famous cosmologists to agree with a fate is the named collapse. The legitimate ask is to give the shape claim a budget to be tried.

**This audit is a simulation, not a ground reading.** Same essay: "LLMs can quickly simulate an approximate answer/output ... However, the truly testable output would need to be derived from the real architecture or working the formula yourself." And the architecture essay is explicit: LLM output is "simulacra" and "could never be verified if the solutions presented were not contrived to fit the formula." So `01` is a simulated derivation checked for internal consistency against the figures. It is not a cosmological measurement and must be labelled as such. It cannot settle anything it reports on.
