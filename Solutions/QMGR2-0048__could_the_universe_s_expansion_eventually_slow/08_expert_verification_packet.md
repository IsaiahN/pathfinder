# Expert verification packet: the Tether on the universe's expansion

**What this is:** a claim in a form a cosmologist can check. Mode, derivation and refuters are stated before the conclusion, so a reader knows what to look for and what would settle it.

**Mode: `gap` on the literal physical question; `specified` on the epistemic shape; `general` on the structural rule. Not `grounded`.** No asymptotic fate is claimed.

## 1. The claim, in one paragraph

The figures do not predict whether the expansion slows, continues forever or changes dramatically. No verbatim figure line governs that physical question without strain, so the literal answer is `gap`. What the figures do settle is the shape of a checkable answer: the ground (the cosmological observations) decides, not agreement among models; a null search for evolving dark energy is not proof of absence; the three fates are a union compound that must be unbundled rather than averaged; and the measurement of w(z) and curvature is upstream of any extrapolation. Over the shared atom set {Ω_m, Ω_k, w(z)} the model families stand as isomers, same atoms arranged differently, decisive in behaviour. The framework returns a shape, not a name: the slot stays marked `WILL_EXPANSION_SLOW(???)` until a ground settles it.

## 3. What is explicitly not claimed

- Not a prediction of a fate: no Big Rip, heat death or recollapse is asserted.
- Not a theory of cosmology. The figures are a metatheory about the shape of a checkable answer.
- Not that DESI's preference is a discovery of new physics, and not that it is meaningless. It is a sub-decisive signal on the discrimination link.
- Not that "dark energy is the gradient" or any other physics-to-figure binding. Such bindings would be imports with a source gate and shadow test; none is supplied.
- Not `grounded`. Nothing here is asserted as settled by a cosmological measurement.
