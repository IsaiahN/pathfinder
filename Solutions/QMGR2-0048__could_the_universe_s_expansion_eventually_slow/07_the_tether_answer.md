# The Tether's answer on the universe's expansion

**Mode: `gap` on the literal physical question; `specified` on the epistemic shape; `general` on the structural rule. Not `grounded`.** This is a shape and a route, not a cosmological result. Simulated derivation checked against the figures (`05_what_shape_means.md` §6), not a ground reading.

## 1. The residual it answers

Observations show the expansion accelerating today (Riess 1998; Nobel Prize 2011; DESI 2024). They do not fix the asymptotic fate: ΛCDM, quintessence and phantom fit the same data and diverge on the future. DESI 2024 VI reports a "2.6σ" preference for evolving dark energy with DESI+CMB, rising to "3.9σ" with DES-SN5YR, but that is a live signal, not a settlement. The residual is the underdetermined extrapolation from a finite observed redshift range to t going to infinity. Fig 9 :57: "A bundled problem is usually a union of overlapping conditions, not a list of separate ones." Treat it as a union compound with its own loop and its own ground.

## 2. The shape

1. **The figures do not name the fate.** No verbatim figure line predicts slowing, eternal expansion or a dramatic change from the six-term set without strain (`01` F1). Fig 9 :32: "you get a kind of thing, never the answer itself." The slot stays marked `WILL_EXPANSION_SLOW(???)`. This is `gap`, said plainly: "Unreachable is a verdict here, not a silence" (Fig 13 :134).

Triangulated across Fig 2, Fig 10 and Fig 8.

## 3. What it explicitly does not say

- Not "the universe will end in ice", "the universe will recollapse", or "a Big Rip will happen." Those are names; the framework returns a shape.
- Not that the figures are a theory of cosmology. They are a metatheory about how a checkable answer is framed.
- Not that the DESI preference is a discovery of new physics. It is a 2.6 to 3.9 sigma signal on the discrimination link.
- Not that "dark energy is the gradient" or any other binding of physics terms onto the six. Such bindings are provenance-bearing imports, not derivations, and none is supplied here.
- Not `grounded`. No measurement of the asymptotic fate settles `left(R,φ)`.

## 4. What would settle it, the ground

A source-discriminating instrument, not a further argument. *The Hard Problem is About Source*: a colour instrument "cannot comment on the source"; the spectrometer resolved it. Here:

- A measurement of w(z) and Ω_k precise enough that all viable model families converge on one fate (DESI DR2/DR3, Euclid, Rubin, Roman). This closes the gap in F1 and F3.
- A direct signature of a phase transition, such as a gravitational-wave background from vacuum decay, which would make the "dramatic change" branch observable.
- A measurement of the sign of the expansion's second derivative at a redshift where the models still disagree.
- Standing refuters from `01` §4: a verbatim figure line that predicts a fate without strain; a demonstration that the ground rule does not transfer to cosmology; a provenanced import naming a fate and settled by observation.

## 5. The honest stop

**Unreached, not unreachable.** The six-term set is "a human's careful guess at reality's compression axes" (*They're Scanning Books*); declaring it makes the choice visible, not neutral. The answer stops at the limit of the available instruments and records the lineage so the stop is checkable. "Indiscernibility is not identity" (*How Intelligence Actually Works*): two model families indistinguishable at current precision are not thereby the same universe. Fig 13 :113: "The array is unbounded and the reach is not." Endless extrapolation does not enlarge what the observations can reach.

## 6. One-paragraph version for outreach

> The Tether does not predict whether the universe's expansion will slow, continue forever or change dramatically. No figure line governs that physical question, and the honest verdict is a gap. What the figures do settle is how a checkable answer must be framed: the ground, meaning the cosmological observations, decides, not agreement among models; a null search for evolving dark energy is not proof that it is constant; the three fates are a union compound that must be unbundled, not averaged into one forecast; and the measurement of the dark-energy equation of state and curvature is upstream of any extrapolation. The framework returns the shape of the answer, an extrapolation all viable models converge on, not a name such as Big Rip or heat death. What it offers is that shape, falsifiers written in advance, and the instruments that would close the gap. The slot stays open until the ground settles it.
