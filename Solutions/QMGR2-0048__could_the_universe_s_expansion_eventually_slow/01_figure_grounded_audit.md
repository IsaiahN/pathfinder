## 0. The residual, in effect terms

The observed expansion history is accelerating today (Riess 1998; Nobel Prize 2011; DESI 2024). The asymptotic fate is not fixed by that history: several model families fit the same data and diverge on the future.

- **Slow / recollapse:** deceleration and possible Big Crunch if matter or curvature dominates, or if dark energy decays.
- **Continue forever:** eternal expansion and heat death under a cosmological constant (w = -1) or a slowly rolling field.
- **Change dramatically:** a Big Rip if phantom energy (w < -1) grows without bound (Caldwell, Kamionkowski, Weinberg 2003); a vacuum-decay phase transition; a bounce; or an evolving equation of state. DESI 2024 VI reports a "preference is 2.6σ for the DESI+CMB combination" for w0 > -1, wa < 0, rising to a "3.9σ" discrepancy with ΛCDM when DES-SN5YR is added.

The residual R is not "which sentence is true." It is: the observed data underdetermine the extrapolation, because the discriminating parameter w(z) and curvature are measured only over a finite redshift range. State the sought-for shape as an ordinary term and bind it: an answer would be a measurement that makes all viable model families converge on one fate. That binding is refusable by the same test as anything else (Fig 5 :66, "Committing a prediction before a run is this, already working").

## 1. Domain census (before choosing any figure line)

R by arity, symmetry and scale, then one line per domain with actors, currency and anchor. No nameable anchor means it is not a domain yet.

- **R (the residual).** Arity: three or more conditions (matter density Ω_m, curvature Ω_k, dark-energy equation of state w(z) and its derivative). Symmetry: time-asymmetric, expansion has a direction and an arrow of time. Scale: the largest physical scale, the whole observable universe. Fig 9 :21-22 names the describing vocabulary: "arity · symmetry · scale."
- **Domain 1, observational cosmology.** Actors: type Ia supernovae, the CMB, baryon acoustic oscillations, gravitational lensing, the DESI and Planck instruments. Currency: distance-redshift measurements, H(z), the acoustic angular scale. Anchor: the observations themselves, which do not change because a model prefers a fate. Fig 2 :29: "An anchor is whatever settles the question and does not change its answer because of what any frame thinks." This is a domain; its anchor is nameable.
- **Domain 2, theoretical cosmology / general relativity.** Actors: the Friedmann equations, the scale factor a(t), the cosmological constant Λ, quintessence fields, phantom energy. Currency: predictions of expansion history. Anchor: consistency with GR plus the Domain 1 observations. Multiple closures here (ΛCDM, wCDM, w0waCDM, phantom) fit current data; their agreement is one evidence pool, not corroboration.
- **Domain 3, the figure framework (metatheory).** Actors: frames, closure(Γ), the ground, the residual R, imports. Currency: reachability, residuals, settlement. Anchor: the figures as metalaws. This domain governs the shape of the reasoning. It is a domain with a nameable anchor, but its anchor is not the physical universe.
- **Domain 4, verification and outreach.** Actors: expert readers. Currency: agreement and residuals. Anchor: the ground, never agreement. In this simulated run all seats draw on one evidence pool, so its independence is zero.

**Partition or union.** Union. Domains 1 and 2 overlap at the data-model interface, and Domains 3 and 4 are frames about the reasoning. The physical question itself is a union compound: "A bundled problem is usually a union of overlapping conditions, not a list of separate ones" (Fig 9 :57). "And when the split lands on a union rather than a partition, unbundle before you search again" (Fig 9 :54). The three fates are not three harder versions of one question; they are different compounds over shared conditions.

**Order by dependency (Fig 3).** "The chain is ordered by dependency, not by importance" (Fig 3 :20). Measure H(z) and w(z) (perception) before defining the terms Ω and w (vocabulary) before stating what counts as a fate (objective) before extrapolating (planning). The break that matters is whether an instrument discriminates among models at the needed precision: "A link with no instrument is not a link that works. It is a link nobody has looked at" (Fig 3 :27). Effort spent extrapolating above that break cannot rescue a missing measurement: "Optimising a link that already works cannot rescue one that does not" (Fig 3 :19).

## 3. Findings F1..Fn

### F1  /  No figure line governs the physical fate (decisive, `gap`)

I re-censed for a line that predicts slowing, eternal expansion or a dramatic change directly from the six-term set. None exists without strain.

- Candidate strain A: Fig 13 :46, "What makes a gradient a gradient is that it can be spent." One could try to read dark energy as a spendable gradient. Strain: the figure's gradient is a directed difference between states in the thermodynamic/state sense, not the energy density of the vacuum. It governs a different quantity, so re-census.
- Candidate strain B: Fig 13 :14, "conserved, finite, directionless alone". One could try to read dark energy into it. Strain: vacuum energy in an expanding spacetime is not conserved in the figure's sense; again a different quantity.
- Candidate strain C: Fig 13 :75, "Two states never bond directly. It is not space-time but space, a structure, time; and two structures join only through a state." This governs what composes with what, not how fast the scale factor grows.

After a genuine re-census, no line closes the physical residual. Say so plainly: "Unreachable is a verdict here, not a silence" (Fig 13 :134). This is `gap` on the literal question. It is not an absence of research; it is the correct reading that the physical fate is owed to a ground the framework does not contain.

**Refuter:** produce a figure line whose verbatim text predicts the sign of the expansion's second derivative or the asymptotic w without strain. Until one is shown, F1 stands.

### F2  /  The figures do govern the epistemic shape (`specified` / `general`)

What the figures settle is how a checkable answer must be framed, not which fate occurs.

- Fig 2 :29: "An anchor is whatever settles the question and does not change its answer because of what any frame thinks." The anchor here is the cosmological observation.
- Fig 2 :35: "Alignment is triangulation, not negotiation. A mirror cannot correct you." Agreement among ΛCDM practitioners is not a verdict.
- Fig 8 :39: "So a search that finds nothing is never a proof of absence." The current null result on evolving w is not a proof that w is constant.
- Fig 9 :27: "describe it before you go looking, or any frame you pick will seem to fit." Describe the residual, then pick the model.
- Fig 9 :57: "A bundled problem is usually a union of overlapping conditions, not a list of separate ones." The three fates are a union compound.
- Fig 10 :24: "the ground does not decay, the channel does." Data settle; models go stale.
- Fig 13 :113: "The array is unbounded and the reach is not." Endless extrapolation does not enlarge what the observations can reach.

Triangulated: the ground-decides claim is carried by Fig 2 and Fig 10 (low overlap between the alignment figure and the ground-maintainer figure) and Fig 8 (the closure figure). Single-source: the union-compound reading rests on Fig 9 alone. Mode: `specified` for these transfer claims; `general` for the structural rule.

**Refuter:** show that the figures' ground rule does not transfer to physical cosmology, i.e. that model agreement rather than observation is the legitimate anchor for an asymptotic extrapolation. Then F2 falls.

### F3  /  The discrimination link is the dependency break (Fig 3)

"Optimising a link that already works cannot rescue one that does not" (Fig 3 :19). The limiting link is the instrument that distinguishes w = -1 from w(z) varying and Ω_k from zero. DESI's reported "2.6σ" to "3.9σ" preference is a live signal on that link, not a settled fate. "A link with no instrument is not a link that works. It is a link nobody has looked at" (Fig 3 :27). Conversely, future surveys (DESI DR2/DR3, Euclid, Rubin, Roman) are instruments already returning something that fails to resolve, which is exactly where Fig 6 :34 says instruments are extended: "Where nothing is, there is no edge to extend from, and the search for a proto-instrument is a search for one that is already there."

**Refuter:** show that w(z) and Ω_k are already measured to the precision needed to force one fate, so the break is downstream of measurement. Then the gap in F1 closes at the ground and the verdict changes.

### F4  /  The shape, not a name (Fig 9 / canonical README)

Fig 9 :32: "you get a kind of thing, never the answer itself." The canonical figures README says the framework reports a missing thing's shape, not its name. The figure-grounded shape here is: the answer is a ground-settled extrapolation that all viable models converge on; the framework cannot name which fate. Naming "Big Rip" or "heat death" as the framework's answer would be a binding from the physics frame, provenanced as such, not a derivation (see `05_what_shape_means.md` §3).

**Refuter:** a figure line that names a specific fate, or a provenanced import with a shadow test that does so. Both are absent.

### F5  /  Isomers over the same atoms (Fig 12)

ΛCDM, quintessence and phantom fit the same observed history but diverge on the future. Fig 12 :117: "isomer. Same atoms, different bond, different substance. The difference is invisible in the formula and decisive in the behaviour." The shared atoms are {Ω_m, Ω_k, w(z)}; the difference is invisible in current data and decisive in the future. Worked out in `06_isomers_and_order.md`, with a falsifier.

**Refuter:** show that two of these families are the same substance under Fig 12's bond tests (same asymptotic fate and same second-order behavior), in which case the isomer claim fails and they are one model under two names.

### F6  /  This is not a mint, and the refusal comes first

`THE_FORMULA.md` step 3 gates a mint by SUPPORT, REACHABILITY and NOVELTY as a product. For this question:
- SUPPORT: there is residual mass, the underdetermined extrapolation (Fig 5 :21 "the gap has real mass somewhere"). Pass.
- REACHABILITY: no φ in the figure closure predicts a physical fate (F1). Fail.
- NOVELTY: "Big Rip", "heat death", "ΛCDM" are already in the physics library. Fail as figure mints.
- SETTLEMENT: no ground reading of the asymptotic fate. Fail.

So no figure term is minted for the physical fate, and the honest output is `gap`. Formula step 2 owes a refusal before a mint: the claim that the figures can name the fate is refused. `¬` is a unary operator, not a bond (Operators table amendment), so the refusal is recorded as an added term, not a deletion.

**Refuter:** demonstrate that a novel, reachable φ in the figure closure predicts a fate and is settled by observation. Then a mint is licensed.

### F7  /  Filters are not witnesses

Ranking, tractability and tier are budget tools. Fig 9 :60: "Use filters for the budget and witnesses for the verdict. Never let a filter hand you a verdict." A question being high-priority or well-studied says nothing about which fate is true. Fig 1 :22: "Aggregating across slots is how a live signal disappears into an average." Averaging the three fates into a "consensus fate" destroys the only signal that says where to look.

**Refuter:** none internal; this is a rule, not a finding. It governs how F1 to F6 are used.
