# Outreach draft: check the shape claim, then decide about the framework

**Use:** one-to-one, or as a cover letter attached to a submission through the public venues in `03_public_contact_points.csv`. **Not** for bulk send.

**Hard precondition:** do not send until F1 to F3 in `01_figure_grounded_audit.md` are checked. The output must survive validation or the two-stage offer collapses on contact.

---

**Subject:** One checkable claim about how cosmic-fate questions get settled

> Hi [name],
>
> I have one specific output I would like you to check against what you already know. I am not asking you to read a theory first, and I am not asking you to agree with me. If the output does not survive, there is nothing else to look at.
>
> **The output (`specified`, not `grounded`):** the question "will expansion slow, continue forever, or change dramatically" is underdetermined by the data we have. Several model families (ΛCDM, quintessence, phantom) fit the same observed expansion history and diverge on the future. The figures I work from make no prediction about which fate occurs. What they do settle is the shape of a checkable answer: an anchor that does not move when a model wants it to, which here means the observations; agreement among models is not that anchor. Their own line is "Alignment is triangulation, not negotiation. A mirror cannot correct you." And "So a search that finds nothing is never a proof of absence," so a null result on evolving dark energy is not a proof that the equation of state is constant.
>
> **What I am not claiming:** that the framework names the fate. It returns a shape, not a name. I am not claiming Big Rip, heat death or recollapse is the answer. I am not claiming this is a theory of everything, or that it is the only true one. I am not claiming that a 2.6 to 3.9 sigma preference for evolving dark energy settles anything.
>
> **What would refute the shape claim:**
> 1. a figure line whose verbatim text predicts the sign of the expansion's second derivative or the asymptotic equation of state without strain;
> 2. a measurement of w(z) and curvature precise enough that all viable model families converge on one fate;
> 3. a demonstration that the ground rule does not transfer to physical cosmology, so model agreement, not observation, is the legitimate anchor here.
>
> I wrote these down before sending this. If any holds, I retract the claim. A one-line "this fails because ..." is more useful to me than agreement. If you would rather not engage, no reply is needed.
>
> **If it survives, and only then:** the sources are below. The figures are the authority, not my summary of them.
> - Figures and formula for direct download (SVG, branch `general-agent`): https://github.com/IsaiahN/tether/tree/general-agent/figures
> - Full architecture (Medium): https://medium.com/@IsaiahNwukor/arrival-of-the-fittest-the-tether-architecture-1de7edf641ec
> - Canonical usage rule: https://github.com/IsaiahN/tether/blob/general-agent/figures/README.md
>
> **If you want to use the machinery:** the formula is in the figures link. Run it on a problem you know well and can check against a ground. Write down what would refute your result *before* you run it, and state the mode of what you get. A reported failure is more useful to me than a reported success.
