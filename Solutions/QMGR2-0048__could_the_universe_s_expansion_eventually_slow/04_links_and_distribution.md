## Wider literature that bears on the question

The cosmology sources divide the question into its three branches rather than answering it. That is exactly the residual the figures describe as a union compound (Fig 9 :57, "A bundled problem is usually a union of overlapping conditions, not a list of separate ones"). The DESI result is the live signal; the Peebles and Ratra review is the frame that says the energy density could be dynamical; Caldwell et al. is the existence proof for the dramatic branch. Their agreement that the universe is accelerating is agreement from one evidence pool (supernovae, CMB, BAO), so by Fig 2 :40 it is not independent corroboration of a fate.

## Distribution: offer, do not push

- **Fig 4 :30:** "So take the round trip and measure the gap. Without it you are navigating by dead reckoning: confident about a position nobody checked." Sending the full architecture before anyone has described a residual sends a recording, not a generator. Lead with the shape claim and its refuter; offer the links after.
- **Fig 10 :42:** "A verifier that can reconstruct the claim cannot verify it." A recipient who has absorbed the whole framework before being asked for a verdict has joined the frame being checked.
- **Fig 9 :27:** "describe it before you go looking, or any frame you pick will seem to fit." Ask what they would expect to see if the shape claim were true before giving them the article.
- **Fig 2 :40:** "agreement about the world is worthless when the agreeing frames share an evidence pool." A mass send selected for people who already agree produces a convention, not corroboration.

**Recommended order in a reply:** (1) the shape claim and its refuter; (2) ask what they would count as a failure; (3) then the links, with the note that the figures, not the summary, are the authority.

## The two-stage offer

1. **Stage 1:** validate the output against the ground. Fig 10 :52, "Bring a characterised residual, never the source." Fig 2 :45, "Frames range against the ground. They do not negotiate with it."
2. **Stage 2, opt-in only:** if curious, read the theory. Their verdict on stage 1 is recorded *before* any theory is shown, and later agreement is never pooled as corroboration (Fig 2 :40).
3. **Stage 3:** offer the formula as a method to reuse. Fig 4 :22, "Only methods cross up. Only head starts come down." Each user must write down what would refute their result and state their own mode.

**Hard precondition:** stages 1 to 3 only work if the stage-1 output survives. As drafted, the output is a `gap` verdict plus an epistemic shape, not a physical prediction. Send it as such. Do not upgrade it to a fate claim to make it more interesting; that would breach the mode rule and spend the reader's independence.
