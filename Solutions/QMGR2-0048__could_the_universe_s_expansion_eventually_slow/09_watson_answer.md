## VERDICT (from the figures)

The figures do not decide which cosmic fate occurs; that is a `gap` owed to the cosmological ground. They do decide the shape of a checkable answer: the ground (observations), not agreement among models, settles an asymptotic extrapolation; a null search for evolving dark energy is not proof it is constant; the three fates are a union compound that must be unbundled and kept per slot, not averaged; the measurement of w(z) and Ω_k is upstream of extrapolation; filters are for the budget, never the verdict. Over the shared atom set {Ω_m, Ω_k, w(z)} the model families are isomers in the strong form and a functional group in the weaker form, with a stated falsifier. This is `specified` on the epistemic shape, `general` on the structural rule, and `gap` on the literal physical question. It is not `grounded`.

- **cost if wrong:** bounded and locatable. The packet is mode-labeled, states refuters before the conclusion and records the lineage, so an error is findable rather than invisible. The real cost is spending the independence of outside readers by upgrading a `gap` into a fate claim to make it more interesting. Fig 2 :42 "Buy independence in proportion to the cost of being wrong." That cost is avoidable by leading with the shape claim and its refuter.
- **undoable:** yes. No cosmological primitive is withdrawn. The shape claim and its refuters can be superseded by a settled measurement, and the `gap` can close when the ground decides. The isomer reading stands or falls on its own falsifier without touching the rest of the folder.
- **reversed by:** a ground reading, not agreement. A measurement of w(z) and Ω_k precise enough that all viable families converge on one fate would close the gap; a direct signature of vacuum decay would make the dramatic branch observable; a verbatim figure line predicting a fate without strain would break the `gap`; a demonstration that the ground rule does not transfer to cosmology would break the epistemic shape. Until one fires, the verdict stands as `candidate`.
