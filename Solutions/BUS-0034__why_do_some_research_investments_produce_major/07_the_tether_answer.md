# The Tether's answer on why some research investments pay

**Mode: `specified` - not `grounded`.** This is a shape and a route, not a result. Simulated derivation checked against the figures (`05_what_shape_means.md` section 6), not a ground reading.

## 1. The residual it answers

Why does the gap between what a research investment's library predicts and what the ground returns stay small and pay hugely in some project slots, while in others a real result settles and still yields little commercial value, or nothing settles at all? Fig 1 :19: *"The residual is indexed by object slot, not aggregated."* An average return on R&D has already discarded the signal.

The question is a **union compound**, not one hard question (Fig 9 :54-57). It splits into three conditions with different instruments, kept separate and never averaged:

- **C1, settlement and contact:** the project settles against a measurement or a market, and the channel to that ground stays reachable.
- **C2, reach renewal:** a new atom enters from a closure that differs, so a major term is composable at all.
- **C3, composition and payment:** the settled term becomes an atom that composes into something the market pays the investor for.

## 2. The shape

1. **A research investment is not a stock that buys an innovation.** Its outcome is a property of investment-and-habitat (Fig 11 :39: *"capability is a property of agent-and-habitat, never of the agent alone"*). An agent-only improvement that does not change contact changes nothing (Fig 11 :40). More spend is a filter, not a witness (Fig 9 :60).
2. **Quick loss is channel failure, not the ground going dead.** Fig 10 :24: *"the ground does not decay, the channel does."* The named decay modes are stale percepts, dropped transients, saturated metrics and pooled readings (Fig 10 :26). A decade of advantage is a chain of handoffs; if bracket fidelity or ground pressure goes to zero, the chain runs in place (Fig 7 :37-39).
3. **A major innovation needs an import.** Fig 6 :21: *"MINT composes inside the closure and can never add an atom."* Fig 6 :23: *"No frame escapes its own closure. The bound moves only on contact with a frame whose closure differs."* Composing harder inside the same library adds search, not atoms (Fig 8 :32). What a funder needs to distinguish is underivable (go outside) from unreachable-at-this-budget (more search), because the two issue opposite instructions (Fig 8 :42).
4. **A true result is necessary but not sufficient for commercial value.** Fig 5 :70: *"A term is settled when the ground pays for it. A term is usable when something can compose over it. Those are two conditions and this figure names one."* A settled term that cannot be composed over is exactly the case of a real discovery with little commercial value. Fig 12 :104 lets a settled molecule become an atom for the next level, which is how a result eventually composes into a product.
5. **The strength that produced past success can turn against it.** Fig 11 :49: *"An actor's valence is relative to the residual, not a property it carries."* A routine is a catalyst if it regenerates across uses and a spent part if it does not, and nothing records which (Fig 12 :119). The low-overlap instance is biological exaptation: a trait's present function is not the function it was produced for.
6. **The framework does not name the winning project.** Fig 9 :32: *"you get a kind of thing, never the answer itself."* It returns the shape any paying research investment must have, plus falsifiers written in advance and the instruments that would close the conditions.

## 3. What it explicitly does not say

- Not a name of a specific project, field, input mix, or funding level. Those are candidate libraries whose reach must be tested.
- Not "spend more on R&D", and not "talent is irrelevant". Both are filters or candidate Γ, never verdicts (Fig 9 :60).
- Not a single cause. The question is a union of three conditions; a blended innovation score is a pooled reading (Fig 10 :26) that destroys the diagnostic.
- Not a derivation of absorptive capacity, appropriability, or recombinant uncertainty from the figures. They are bindings from the management frame, provenanced as such, with zero evidentiary weight until a source gate and shadow test are supplied.
- Not `grounded`. No per-project market measurement settles left(R,phi) here.

## 4. What would settle it - the ground

A prospective, per-project instrument, not a further argument. The hard-problem essay's spectrometer is the model: an instrument that reads a result *"cannot comment on the source"*, and metamerism was resolved *"not by argument, but by the arrival of a better instrument."* Here the corresponding questions are:

- **C1 instrument:** can we tell, prospectively and per project, whether the channel to the ground is still carrying a live residual rather than pooled readings and saturated metrics (Fig 10 :26)?
- **C2 instrument:** can we tell whether a new atom has entered from a differing closure, rather than more search inside the same one (Fig 6 :21, :23)?
- **C3 instrument:** can we tell whether a settled term can be composed over into a paying product, and whether the investor is positioned to receive the payment (Fig 5 :69-70; Teece 1986, single-source for the business name)?
- **Standing falsifiers from `01`:** an internal metric that predicts per project which investment will pay; more search in a fixed closure producing a new atom; a settled term that always composes and pays; one lever restoring all conditions with no categorically different residue.

## 5. The honest stop

**Unreached, not unreachable.** The three-condition shape is the most the figures return here. *"what a missing thing would have to look like (a shape, not a name)"* governs the stop, and Fig 13 :134 says *"Unreachable is a verdict here, not a silence."* Fig 8 :39: *"So a search that finds nothing is never a proof of absence."* The instrument that would discriminate the three failures is not held in this folder. The lineage is recorded so the stop is checkable.

## 6. One-paragraph version for outreach

> The Tether does not tell you which research investment will pay, and it does not claim a single cause of innovation. It says that a research investment's outcome belongs to the investment and its habitat, not to the investment alone; that quick loss is a failure of the channel to a paying ground, not the ground going dead; that a major innovation requires an import from a closure that differs, because more search inside the same library adds atoms only by import; and that a true, settled result is necessary but not sufficient for commercial value, because it must also compose into something the market pays for. These are three conditions with different instruments, not three degrees of one thing, and the capability that produced past success can change valence when the residual changes. What is offered is the shape any paying investment must have, falsifiers written in advance, and the instruments that would close it. The slot stays open until the ground settles it.
