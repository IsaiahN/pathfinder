## Independent domain census

I redid the census before relying on the primary's conclusions, using the WATSON order: R by arity, symmetry and scale; one line per domain with actors / currency / anchor; partition or union; order by dependency.

- **Domain 5, closure and import.** Actors: the organization's Γ and frames whose closures differ. Currency: reach and atoms. Anchor: settlement (Fig 5 :32). Overlaps all.

- **Union, not partition.** D1/D3, D2/D4 and D5 overlap. Unbundle into C1 settlement and contact, C2 reach renewal, C3 composition and payment. ## Round trip gap R_T

The Symbols table defines R_T as the gap between what was sent up and what came back down, `gap(x, (T_E o T_A)(x))`, measuring what the coarser description cannot hold. Fig 4 :30 requires it to be measured rather than assumed. I ran the round trip on the condensation the folder actually uses.

1. **Sent up (x):** the verbatim figure set, chiefly Fig 1 :19-23 (per-slot indexing), Fig 6 :21-23 (import), Fig 5 :69-70 (settling not sufficient), Fig 9 :54-57 (union).
2. **Abstraction (T_A):** the shorthand carried in `07` and the outreach email: three conditions, settle, import, compose and be paid; quick loss is channel failure; capability changes valence.
3. **Concretisation (T_E):** the concrete funding claim a reader wants, "invest more in basic research" or "hire better people" or "increase R&D spend".

**R_T is nonzero.** Three things the shorthand holds do not survive the trip back down:

- the **per-project indexing**, which the concrete metric (average R&D return, total patents) drops (Fig 1 :22);
- the **differing-closure requirement**, which the concrete claim replaces with "more of the same search" (Fig 6 :21, Fig 8 :32);
- the **settlement/composition distinction**, which the concrete claim collapses into "a true result is valuable" (Fig 5 :69-70).

Conversely, one thing survives cleanly: a result must be settled before it can be composed over. R_T was **not** measured numerically because no per-project ground reading exists; it is recorded qualitatively as the enumerated set of dropped constraints. Treating "research input to innovation output" as an identity would set R_T to zero and would be the dead-reckoning error Fig 4 :30 names. `06_isomers_and_order.md` records the same gap.
