## 1. A shape is a constraint, not a filler

The figures README states the limit: the framework returns *"what a missing thing would have to look like (**a shape, not a name**)."* Fig 9 :32: *"you get a kind of thing, never the answer itself."* Fig 13: *"It can say the answer is out of reach with the library as it stands... It cannot say which primitive is missing, because naming it would mean already holding it."*

From *The Library of Babel*: *"you can cut your search short if the set of books returned is small and descriptive enough that it provides a general shape of the answer that satisfies your question."*

So a **shape** is the narrowed form of an answer you can state before you can name it. It constrains; it does not fill. For BUS-0034 the shape is the conjunction of conditions C1 (settlement and reachable contact), C2 (reach renewal by import from a differing closure), and C3 (composition into something the market pays the investor for). "Fund basic research", "hire better people", and "increase R&D spend" are names, not shapes: each fills the slot with a candidate library instead of constraining what any answer must look like.

## 2. How a shape is obtained

- *Library of Babel*: use the vocabulary you already have that is *closest* to the thing, look for recurring new terms that overlap or correlate with it, recurse. *"With each new term gained, you are able to narrow the space by definition and deepen your vocabulary."*
- The same essay gives the stop condition that governs this question directly: *"Perhaps your problem was a bundle or conflation of several problems masquerading as one?"* The shape for BUS-0034 was obtained by taking the question apart into three conditions, not by finding a single cause.
- Fig 6 :30: *"The description travels. Fails by being sharpened into terms only we can read. Cast it in effect terms."* Fig 9 :30-31: *"your words are borrowed too, so describe the effect rather than the cause."* The residual was stated as a gap between what an investment's library predicts and what the ground returns, before any management vocabulary was applied.
- *Decoding Tacit Knowledge*: *"When contact isn't possible, the best you can send is a plain description of what you'd expect to see, because anyone can check that against the world."* This is why the outreach packet leads with the shape and its refuter.
- *Library of Babel* on stopping: *"You will know that you are done because the finish line will also be the starting place, as there is nowhere else to search and nothing else that can describe the answer better."*

## 3. What licenses a name

Only two things, both outside the shape itself:

1. **The ground settles it.** Formula step 5: *"the ground settles it. Verified only by what pays."* The README: the framework *"stops at the limit of what the available instruments can distinguish, and records the lineage so the stop is honest."* For BUS-0034 the market and the measurement are named grounds (Fig 2 :29), and the question is constitutively about commercial value (Fig 2 :36).
2. **A provenanced import from a frame whose closure differs** (Fig 6 :23, Fig 8 :50-52), with a source gate and a shadow test. "Absorptive capacity", "appropriability regime", and "recombinant uncertainty" are imports from the management and economics frame. Each is bound as an ordinary term and recorded as such; none is a derivation from the figures and none moves the wall for this framework.

*They're Scanning Books When They Should* sharpens the import rule (as recorded in the exemplar package this session): the real import is not a new term in the existing space but a new dimension of the space, and declaring the primitives makes a choice visible rather than neutral. The six-term substrate is a visible guess, not nature's own list. Naming one of the management phrases as *the* answer to BUS-0034 would turn a visible choice into an invisible one.

## 4. Why a name read off a metric is the specific forbidden error

*The Hard Problem is About Source* gives the mechanism. Metamerism: an instrument reads green and *"cannot comment on the source"*, whether the source was one green light, or yellow plus blue, or RGB, or CMYK, or a scheme not yet discovered. *"If several paths create the same behavior, then behavior is a projection with lossy information."* The decisive line: *"It was proven wrong not by argument, but by the arrival of a better instrument."*

Publication counts, citation counts and patent counts are readings of a research outcome. Reading "innovation" or "commercial value" off them directly is the metamerism error: several sources can produce the same reading. What is owed for BUS-0034 is a discriminator that distinguishes a failure of C1 from a failure of C2 from a failure of C3, not a name attached to a metric.

The same essay supplies the simulation warning: *"the output is only as good as the validator. The agent, or the tool, or the frame, or the theory, cannot validate itself."*

## 5. Q answered - what licenses a particular research investment as the answer?

**Nothing in the framework.** The framework returns a shape. A particular investment is licensed only by a ground settling a minted term, or by a provenanced import with a shadow test. Until one of those, the name is the searcher's own contribution and must be recorded as such.

**Does this folder commit the forbidden error?** No, with one caveat. It does not name a winning project or a winning input mix. It does import names from the management literature ("absorptive capacity", "appropriability"), but it records them as bindings from another frame with zero evidentiary weight until a source gate and shadow test are supplied. The caveat is that a reader may treat the three-condition shape itself as if it were a measurement. It is not. It is `specified` and ungrounded.

## 5b. Correction to my own section 3

I initially wrote that the framework's "shape, not a name" rule forbids importing the management terms at all. That is too strong. The README's phrase is *"what a **missing** thing would have to look like (a shape, not a name)."* It governs a primitive not held in Γ. "Absorptive capacity" and "appropriability" are not missing primitives for this framework; they are bindings from the management frame onto conditions the figures already describe (import from a differing closure, and payment in the market currency). What remains wrong is treating those bindings as derivations or as imports that move the wall, since Fig 6 says *"IMPORT adds an atom"* and these add none to the framework's own closure. Both readings are recorded here so neither is quietly lost.

## 6. Two consequences for outreach

**Prestige is not action.** From *The Recursive Self-Improvement Trap*: *"Prestige must never be allowed to validate an action directly... Someone let prestige stand in for proof... Prestige can fund an action. It can give an action a budget to be tried. But it cannot settle an action. Only the ground can settle it. The Ground Maintainer must be blind to prestige."*

A campaign that gathers famous innovation scholars to agree with the shape is the named collapse, not corroboration. The legitimate ask is the opposite: give the shape a budget to be tried, and let the ground, an experiment, a market, a dataset, settle it. The AOM member directory notice explicitly forbids compiling its list for unsolicited communications, which is a practical expression of the same boundary.

**My own audit is a simulation, not a ground reading.** Same essay: *"LLMs can quickly simulate an approximate answer/output with the formula found in the architecture post. It will often be better than the generic outputs that you will usually get otherwise too (intelligence is essentially arrangement). However, the truly testable output would need to be derived from the real architecture or working the formula yourself."* So `01` is a simulated derivation, checked for internal consistency against the figures. It is **not** a test of the business claim and must be labelled as such. It cannot settle anything it reports on.

## 7. What would make this `grounded`

A prospective, per-project measurement in which the three conditions are instrumented separately, the prediction of which projects will settle, compose, and be paid is registered in advance, and the market or a measurement confirms or refutes it. A source-discriminating instrument in the sense of *The Hard Problem is About Source* would tell a C1 failure from a C2 failure from a C3 failure. Until such an instrument exists, the honest report is a shape, three falsifiers, and an open slot.
