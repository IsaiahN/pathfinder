# Expert verification packet: the Tether on experimentation and reliable operations

**What this is:** a claim in a form an organisation researcher or reliability practitioner can check. Mode, derivation and refuters are stated before the conclusion, so a reader knows what to look for and what would settle it.

**Mode: `specified` on the substantive claims, `general` on the shape, not `grounded`.** No organisation was measured. This is a shape and a route, not a result.

## 1. The claim, in one paragraph

For each operation slot, there is a residual between what an organisation's repertoire predicts and what the ground returns. Reliability and experimentation are two different ways of failing that residual, not two terms in one objective: a settled routine can drift while its metric still reads zero, and a novel slot can have no predicting term at all. The figure-derived shape is that these two loops keep separate seats and different currencies against one unmoving ground, joined by a directed membrane that carries verified reapplicable methods from experiments to operations and head starts back, never recordings or replays, with a measured round-trip gap at each crossing and a named compound where both conditions bind in the same unit. This packet offers the shape, the refuters written in advance, and the instruments that would close it. It does not name a management programme.

## 2. Three checkable sub-claims

### C1 — Organisation-facing; checkable against the two bodies of literature

- **Statement:** the tension is a union of two conditions, not a single objective with an optimal blend. Each rule works well on a different subset, and a combined KPI does not make the disagreement shrink.
- **Source, verbatim:** Fig 9 :4-5: "Disagreements that do not shrink with effort, where each rule keeps working well on a different subset, are not one hard question but several well-formed ones. Split it rather than search." Fig 12 :125: "A composition costs in two currencies, and they do not add." Fig 12 :130: "Summing them into one figure is the error this names, not the fix for it."
- **What to check:** does a combined score (uptime plus experiment throughput, say) predict ground-settled improvement better than two separate readouts? Is there a single unit in which both currencies really price?
- **Refuter:** a single process, with no separate seats and no directed membrane, that drives per-slot residuals to zero on settled routines *and* mints ground-settled terms on novel slots, in two independent organisations, against an anchor that does not move when pushed.

### C2 — Reliability-facing; checkable by an auditor or safety practitioner

- **Statement:** reliability is maintenance of the channel to an unmoving anchor, measured per slot; a global number near zero is a legal state with a live failing slot, and a falling incident count is a saturated metric until the reporting channel is shown reachable.
- **Source, verbatim:** Fig 10 :24: "the ground does not decay, the channel does." Fig 10 :25-26: "stale percepts, dropped transients, saturated metrics, pooled readings." Fig 1 :19: "The residual is indexed by object slot, not aggregated." Fig 2 :29-30: "An anchor is whatever settles the question and does not change its answer because of what any frame thinks."
- **What to check:** take an organisation with a healthy global KPI. Can it name a slot with a live residual that the ground (customer, patient, incident, market) can see and the KPI cannot? If yes, C2 stands. If no such case can be found after a genuine search, C2 weakens.
- **Refuter:** an organisation where a global KPI near zero coexists with a persistently live, invisible slot residual, and no degradation at the ground follows over a full operating cycle.

### C3 — Experiment-facing; checkable by a pilot owner or risk engineer

- **Statement:** a controlled pilot is a substituted habitat, so it proves wiring and not capability; what the test dropped is invisible until the goal fails, and what it introduced is invisible until it acts. The result transfers only after habitat enumeration and a measured round-trip gap.
- **Source, verbatim:** Fig 11 :70: "A test harness is a substituted habitat. Which is why a synthetic solve proves wiring and never capability." Fig 11 :68-69: "what you failed to reproduce is invisible until the goal fails" and "what you unintentionally introduced is invisible until it acts." Fig 4 :30: "So take the round trip and measure the gap."
- **What to check:** pick a pilot that succeeded and failed on rollout. Can the gap be enumerated in the three categories the figure gives (actors, conditions, relations) and does the round trip expose it? If a successful pilot transfers with no such enumeration and no gap, C3 fails.
- **Refuter:** a controlled pilot that transfers to production capability with no enumeration of the habitat difference and no measured `R_T`.

## 3. What is explicitly not claimed

- Not a best percentage split, not a balanced scorecard, not a single KPI.
- Not "do both in the same team with the same dashboard." Fig 12 :55-59 defines `+` as a symmetric bond: "swap the operands. Does the meaning change? No." The arrangement would carry nothing.
- Not that "ambidextrous organisation", "high reliability organisation", "lean" or any other name is the figure's answer. Those are world imports with their own provenance; they converge with the shape and corroborate it, they do not derive it.
- Not that experiment count or incident count is a verdict. Fig 5 :48: "Its own output is never a measure of success."
- Not that a successful pilot is evidence of capability.
- Not `grounded`. Nothing here has been settled by measuring an organisation.

## 5. What would settle it

A ground reading, not agreement. In priority order:

1. A per-slot channel check that discriminates a hidden live residual from a genuine near-zero reading.
2. A measured round-trip gap `R_T` for a method crossing from a pilot into operations.
3. A habitat enumeration for that pilot: actors, conditions and relations dropped and introduced.
4. Any one of the four refuters in `01_figure_grounded_audit.md` section 3 firing.
