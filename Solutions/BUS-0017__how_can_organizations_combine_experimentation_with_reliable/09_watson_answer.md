## QUESTION

Decide, in effect terms, what shape an answer must have. R_s = |Γ(b,a) - o'| is the gap between what an organisation's repertoire predicts and what the ground returns, per slot. Option A: treat experimentation and reliable operations as one objective and find the optimal blend. Option B: treat them as two conditions with different currencies against one unmoving ground, joined by a directed membrane, with a compound where both bind. What each leads to: A produces a combined KPI that cannot see the shortfall on either side and cannot make the disagreement shrink; B produces a checkable shape with refuters and a measured gap at each handoff. The residual is a union, not one hard question (Fig 9 :4-5).

## DOMAIN CENSUS

R by arity, symmetry and scale, then one line per domain giving actors / currency / anchor, partition or union, and order by dependency.

- **R (the residual).** Arity: two conditions, hence a union compound (Fig 9 :57: "a case in two conditions is not a harder case of either"). Symmetry: not symmetric, the membrane has a direction (Fig 4 :22) and the costs are in different units (Fig 12 :125-126). Scale: first order inside each loop, and the handoff crosses a scale boundary carrying R_T (Fig 4 :30).
- **Domain 1, operations and reliability.** Actors: operators, routines or SOPs, metrics, incidents, customers or patients. Currency: bits, the cost of stating and maintaining the routine; per-slot residual kept near zero. Anchor: the ground (customer outcome, patient outcome, incident, market), legitimate because the question is constitutively about people (Fig 2 :36). Type: a seat's office, not a loop's output. Partition side A.
- **Domain 2, experimentation and minting.** Actors: experimenters, hypotheses, prototypes, instruments. Currency: actions spent to find out whether a candidate holds. Anchor: the same ground reached through a different closure. Type: a gated mint whose own output is never a measure of success (Fig 5 :48). Partition side B.
- **Domain 3, the membrane and handoff.** Actors: methods, head starts, records, review boards. Currency: what crosses and the loss across the crossing. Anchor: the measured round-trip gap R_T. Type: a one-way filter, neither side. Depends on both first loops having something to carry.
- **Domain 4, the compound intersection.** Actors: both sets in one unit. Currency: both, unmerged. Anchor: the ground for the compound. Type: union, not partition (Fig 9 :54-58); a specific compound with its own loop.
- **Order by dependency.** Perceive first (per-slot residual), then vocabulary (terms for what a slot affords and what progress would mean), then objective in each currency, then plan, then learn and carry (Fig 3 :19-21). The middle link has no instrument and is therefore usually unwatched (Fig 3 :27).

Strain test applied: Fig 9 :4-5 (split) versus Fig 9 :54-58 (unbundle a union) governs different quantities, question type versus residual intersection, so no strain after re-census. Reliability in ordinary language (low variance) versus Fig 10 :24 (channel maintenance) also re-censes: the reading versus the instrument.

## VERDICT (from the figures)

The shape survives; a name does not. Experimentation and reliable operations are two conditions with different currencies against one unmoving ground, not one objective to be optimised. Reliability is maintained contact with the ground, measured per slot, and its failure is a dead channel (stale, saturated or pooled readings), not merely a variable process. Experimentation is a gated mint whose own output is not a measure of success. The two are joined by a directed membrane: verified methods up, head starts down, never recordings or replays, with a measured round-trip gap; a pilot is a substituted habitat and proves wiring, not capability, until its dropped and introduced actors are enumerated. Where both conditions bind in one unit, the intersection is a compound with its own loop and an open price. There is no final settled state. All of this is `specified` and ungrounded; nothing here was settled by measuring an organisation.

- **cost if wrong:** bounded and locatable. The packet states its mode, registers four refuters before the conclusion and records the lineage, so an error is findable rather than invisible. The real cost is spending an outside reader's independence by sending a recording (a branded programme and a success story) instead of a generator (the residual, the shape and the refuter). Fig 2 :42: "Buy independence in proportion to the cost of being wrong." That cost is avoidable by leading with the shape claim and its refuter.
- **undoable:** yes. The shape can be restated without withdrawing a primitive. No term has to be deleted to change it; a refutation only removes a term's standing to be composed over (Fig 6 :42, "What is recorded only grows"). A sent packet can be superseded by a corrected one, and the four refuters tell a reader exactly which claim to discard.
- **reversed by:** a ground reading, not agreement. A source-discriminating instrument showing a live slot residual behind a healthy global KPI would settle C2; a measured round-trip gap for a transferred method would settle C3; a single process that does both loops with no seats and no membrane, in two organisations, would fire refuter 1 and break the shape. A figure re-census that admits one currency for bits and actions would dissolve the two-currency finding. Until one of these fires, the verdict stands as `candidate`.
