## Wider literature that bears on this question

These are independent of the figures' author. They corroborate, they do not decide. Full provenance and quotes are in `10_source_ledger.csv`.

1. **March 1991, Organization Science**. https://pubsonline.informs.org/doi/10.1287/orsc.2.1.71 . The foundational statement that adaptive processes refine exploitation faster than exploration and can be "effective in the short run but self-destructive in the long run," and that adaptation tends "to increase the reliability of performance rather more than its mean." This is the business-side version of the two-currency finding.
2. **O'Reilly and Tushman 2004, Harvard Business Review**. https://hbr.org/2004/04/the-ambidextrous-organization . Separate exploratory units "allowing for different processes, structures, and cultures" with "tight links across units at the senior executive level," and "cross-fertilization among units while preventing cross-contamination." This is a world import that converges with Fig 4's membrane shape. Its name is not the figure's name.
3. **Oxford Bibliographies, Organizational Ambidexterity, 2023**. https://doi.org/10.1093/obo/9780199846740-0214 . "Structural differentiation is accompanied by structural integration," with "targeted integration" effected by the senior team. A review-level witness that the separation plus integration shape is the field's own convergent result, not our invention.
4. **Weick and Sutcliffe, Managing the Unexpected**. https://catdir.loc.gov/catdir/samples/wiley031/2001001341.pdf . HROs "treat any lapse as a symptom that something is wrong with the system" and are "better able to notice the unexpected in the making and halt its development." "Experience by itself is no guarantee of expertise."
5. **PSNet High Reliability primer, UC Davis / AHRQ, last reviewed 2024**. https://psnet.ahrq.gov/primer/high-reliability . "high reliability is better described as a condition of persistent mindfulness within an organization," safety is "an emergent, rather than a static, property," and "High reliability is an ongoing process or an organizational frame of mind, not a specific structure."
6. **Edmondson 1999, Administrative Science Quarterly**. https://web.mit.edu/curhan/www/docs/Articles/15341_Readings/Organizational_Learning_and_Change/Edmondson_1999_Psychological_safety.pdf . Psychological safety, not team efficacy, predicts learning behavior when controlling for the other; learning behavior includes "talking about errors, and experimenting." A check on whether a residual can actually be reported.
7. **Addicott et al. 2017, Neuropsychopharmacology**. https://www.nature.com/articles/npp2017108 . "the choice between exploiting a familiar option for a known reward and exploring unfamiliar options for unknown rewards" is studied across behavioural ecology and computational neuroscience. A low-overlap field witness to the same arity.
8. **Cinotti et al. 2019, Scientific Reports**. https://www.nature.com/articles/s41598-019-43245-z . In rats, "decreasing dopaminergic activity does not affect learning rate but is equivalent to an increase in random exploration rate." Two parameters, separately controlled, at a different stratum. Corroborates the "split, do not average" shape.

**Overlap warning.** Sources 1 to 3 overlap each other: March is cited by O'Reilly and Tushman and by the Oxford Bibliographies entry. That is one evidence pool with three witnesses, not three independent votes. Sources 4 to 6 overlap within the reliability literature. Sources 7 and 8 are a different pool (animal behaviour and neuroscience) and carry more independent weight for the arity claim, while saying nothing about organisations. No world source conflicts with the figure verdict; the conflict rule was not triggered.

## The two-stage offer

- **Stage 1 before stage 2** is Fig 10 :52, "Bring a characterised residual, never the source," and Fig 2 :45, "Frames range against the ground. They do not negotiate with it." The recipient checks the shape without holding the framework's interior.
- **Stage 2 is opt in by construction.** Once a recipient has absorbed the theory, their agreement stops being evidence. Their stage-1 verdict must be recorded before any theory is shown, and later agreement is never pooled as corroboration (Fig 2 :40).
- **Stage 3 sends a method, which is permitted.** "Only methods cross up. Only head starts come down" (Fig 4 :22). The formula is a generator, so giving it away does not give away an answer.
- **Stage 3 still needs a ground per problem.** THE_FORMULA.md, "Direction": a frame "cannot check the direction of its own question, because the shape a frame is searching for is not a term it holds." Each user must write down what would refute their result before running it and label the mode of what they get.

**Hard precondition:** stages 1 to 3 only work if the stage-1 shape survives the refuters in `01_figure_grounded_audit.md` section 3. It has not been tested against any organisation here.
