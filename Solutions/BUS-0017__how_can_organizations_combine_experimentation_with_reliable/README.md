## What is deliberately not here

- No personal emails of named individuals.
- No ranking of people by how likely they are to believe the claim.
- No bulk-send plan.

Three reasons, in the figures' own words:

1. **Agreement is not a verdict.** Fig 2 :35: "Alignment is triangulation, not negotiation. A mirror cannot correct you." A list selected for agreement is a mirror.
2. **Correlation disqualifies.** Fig 2 :40: "agreement about the world is worthless when the agreeing frames share an evidence pool." The world sources read for this solution are independent of the figures' author, but the business sources overlap each other. The ledger records that.
3. **Do not spend a reader's independence before they check.** Fig 10 :42: "A verifier that can reconstruct the claim cannot verify it." So the first contact carries the residual and the refuter, not the framework.

Legally, public opt-in and submission venues avoid the failure of unsolicited bulk outreach as well as the structural one.
