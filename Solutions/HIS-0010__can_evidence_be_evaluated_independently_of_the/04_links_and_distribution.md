## What the wider literature bears on the question

1. **The strong reading already exists in the field.** Longino, via SEP, says there is "no higher authority or transcendent aperspectival position." That is the philosophical form of Option A's failure in `01` F5. It corroborates the figure verdict from a genuinely different evidence pool (philosophy of science, not the Tether corpus).
2. **The strong programme and its critics split along the same seam the figures name.** The symmetry thesis treats social causes as explanatory for true and false beliefs alike; Sokal and Bricmont reply that nature and logic, not social pressure, validate findings. Re-census, not a flip of the verdict: the symmetry thesis describes the *producing frame* (D2), the critics' reply points at the *ground* (D1). The figure framework keeps both domains and refuses to average them. This is Fig 2 :35 in another vocabulary: *"Alignment is triangulation, not negotiation. A mirror cannot correct you."*
3. **Independence is already a measured quantity in the field.** The SEP information-cascade example and the note that "searchers drawing on the same evidence are one searcher wearing many names" in Seat Map :207 are the same arithmetic. Fig 2 :44 names the instrument: *"The effective-sample-size correction for correlated observations is standard in survey statistics and meta-analysis; the collapse is the same arithmetic read as a warning."*
4. **The source-discrimination point transfers.** The Hard Problem essay's spectrometer case is the model for what would settle this question: not a further argument about whether communities capture evidence, but an instrument that discriminates "the producer moved the verdict" from "the ground moved the verdict." Naming a source from a projection is the error the shape refuses.
5. **A concrete norm set already exists.** SftP's four Longino norms (venues for criticism, shared public standards, sensitivity to criticism, equally distributed intellectual authority) are a world analogue of the figure arrangement: an unauthored pool, a non-updating anchor, and a verifier that is not the maker. They are corroboration, not authority.
