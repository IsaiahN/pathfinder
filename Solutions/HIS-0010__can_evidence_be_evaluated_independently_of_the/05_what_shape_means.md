## 1. A shape is a constraint, not a filler

The figures README states the limit: the framework returns *"what a missing thing would have to look like (**a shape, not a name**)."* Fig 9 :32: *"you get a kind of thing, never the answer itself."* Fig 13 :134-135: *"Unreachable is a verdict here, not a silence. It cannot say which primitive is missing, because naming it would mean already holding it."*

From *The Library of Babel*: *"you can cut your search short if the set of books returned is small and descriptive enough that it provides a general shape of the answer that satisfies your question."*

So a **shape** here is the narrowed form of an answer you can state before you can name the specific arrangement that satisfies it. It constrains; it does not fill. For this question:

- `INDEPENDENCE(?, ?, ?, ?)` is a shape: it says a relation holds among evidence, producer, evaluator and a non-updating ground.
- "Peer review at journal X makes evidence independent" is a name, and worse, it is a name of one of the variables inside the relation.

The shape does not name an institution. It states what any institution would have to satisfy to count.

## 2. How a shape is obtained

- *Library of Babel*: use the vocabulary you already have that is *closest* to the thing, look for recurring new terms that overlap or correlate with it, recurse. *"With each new term gained, you are able to narrow the space by definition and deepen your vocabulary."* Applied here: start with "independent," "unbiased," "neutral," "objective," then notice the terms the field actually uses for the operative variable, "overlap," "correlation," "effective sample size," "shared evidence pool." The vocabulary converges on a relation, not a standpoint.
- Fig 6 :30: *"The description travels. Fails by being sharpened into terms only we can read. Cast it in effect terms."* Fig 9 :30-31: *"your words are borrowed too, so describe the effect rather than the cause."*
- *Decoding Tacit Knowledge*: *"When contact isn't possible, the best you can send is a plain description of what you'd expect to see, because anyone can check that against the world."* That is exactly what the stage-1 outreach artifact does: it sends the four verdicts and the refuters, not the framework.
- *Library of Babel* on stopping: *"You will know that you are done because the finish line will also be the starting place, as there is nowhere else to search and nothing else that can describe the answer better."* The paradox is that by then you have *"already composed the answer through the process of locating it."*

## 3. What licenses a name

Only two things, both outside the shape itself:

1. **The ground settles it.** Formula step 5: *"the ground settles it. Verified only by what pays."* The README: the framework *"stops at the limit of what the available instruments can distinguish, and records the lineage so the stop is honest."*
2. **A provenanced import from a frame whose closure differs** (Fig 6 :23, Fig 8 :50-52), with a source gate and a shadow test.

*They're Scanning Books When They Should Be Studying Nature* sharpens this in a way that bears directly on this question: *"there is no view from nowhere to compress toward."* And: *"Declaring the primitives does not make them neutral. It makes the choice visible, which is worth a great deal, because a visible choice can be revised and an emergent one cannot."*

So naming a particular institution as the independent evaluator is a declaration of a choice, not a discovery that the choice is neutral. The choice becomes auditable only when its provenance is recorded. *"Proof of provenance does not remove the priors. It makes them auditable, which is the most that honesty allows when the priors are unavoidable."*

And the deeper point from the same essay: *"The real import is not a new term in the existing space. It is a new dimension of the space."* Adding a second reviewer to a shared evidence pool is a new term inside the same space. Adding an evaluator whose instrument samples a different axis is a new dimension. Only the second changes what the evaluation can register.

## 4. Why "the independent evaluator" is the specific forbidden error

*The Hard Problem is About Source* gives the mechanism. Metamerism: an instrument reads green and *"the instrument is speaking only about what it measured, it cannot comment on the source."* One green light, or yellow plus blue, or RGB, or CMYK, or a scheme not yet discovered. *"If several paths create the same behavior, then behavior is a projection with lossy information (it cannot define consciousness in full)."* And: *"It was proven wrong not by argument, but by the arrival of a better instrument."*

Applied here: a verdict "the evidence was independently evaluated" is a reading. It does not determine the source of the independence. The source could be a genuinely different evidence pool, an outside verifier with non-access, a non-updating anchor, or simply the producer's standards restated. All four produce the same reading. What is owed is a discriminator, not a name. The analogue of the spectrometer here is an instrument that distinguishes "the producer moved the verdict" from "the ground moved the verdict."

*The Recursive Self-Improvement Trap* gives the self-validation form: *"the output is only as good as the validator. The agent, or the tool, or the frame, or the theory, cannot validate itself. An external validator, or ground, is always needed to tether the veracity of the output to reality. Anything else is drift."* And the drift case in exactly these words: *"You may all agree and be wrong. You may set your clock to someone else's, who was setting your clock to yours, and be off by many deviations over time. You need ground truth to define where you stand."*

## 5. Q2 - ANSWERED: what licenses a particular independent arrangement?

**Nothing in the framework names one.** The framework returns the conditions an arrangement must meet: a non-updating anchor, low overlap between evaluator and producer evidence pools, a verifier that cannot reconstruct the claim, an unauthored pool, provenance for every import. A particular institution is licensed only by the ground settling a check of those conditions, or by a provenanced import with a shadow test. Until then the name is the searcher's own contribution and must be recorded as such.

**Does the field already contain the same conclusion?** Yes, from an independent evidence pool. Helen Longino, in the SEP entry *The Social Dimensions of Scientific Knowledge*, is reported as holding that *"There is no higher authority or transcendent aperspectival position from which it is possible to adjudicate among foundational assumptions."* The world source and the figure verdict agree, but on different routes: Longino arrives from underdetermination and the social character of inference, the figures from the anchor definition and the no-last-room rule. This is convergence at low overlap, so it is weak corroboration rather than a shared-pool echo (Fig 8 :50-52).

## 5b. Correction to my own §3-§4

I initially wrote the "shape, not a name" rule as if it forbade naming any institution. That overstates it. The README's phrase is *"what a **missing** thing would have to look like (a shape, not a name)."* The subject is a primitive not held in Γ. An institution is not a missing primitive; it is a binding from the social frame onto an arrangement the framework already describes.

What remains wrong is treating that binding as if it were a derivation, or as if declaring it made it neutral. The error is not naming, it is unrecorded naming. Both the original claim and this correction are kept visible so neither is quietly lost.

Separately, my §4 metamerism point is **not** retracted: a verdict still does not determine its source, and the right remedy is a discriminator. The correction narrows the reason, it does not remove the finding.

## 6. Two consequences for outreach

**Prestige is not action.** From *The Recursive Self-Improvement Trap*: *"Prestige can fund an action. It can give an action a budget to be tried. But it cannot settle an action. Only the ground can settle it. The Ground Maintainer must be blind to prestige."* A campaign that gathers prestigious evaluators to agree that evidence is independent is the named collapse, not corroboration. The legitimate ask is the opposite: give the shape a budget to be tried, and let the ground, an instrument, a record, a count, settle it.

**My own audit is a simulation, not a ground reading.** Same essay: *"LLMs can quickly simulate an approximate answer/output with the formula found in the architecture post. It will often be better than the generic outputs that you will usually get otherwise too (intelligence is essentially arrangement). However, the truly testable output would need to be derived from the real architecture or working the formula yourself."* So `01` is a simulated derivation, checked for internal consistency against the figures. It is **not** a test of the question and must be labelled as such. It cannot settle anything it reports on.

## 7. What the shape is, in one line

`INDEPENDENCE = non-updating anchor AND low evidence-pool overlap AND verifier non-access AND recorded provenance`, each term measurable in principle, none of them a person, a journal, or a community. That is a shape and an instrument, not a name and not a verdict.
