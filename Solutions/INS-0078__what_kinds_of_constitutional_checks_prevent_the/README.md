## Restating the question in effect terms

The question names a two-sided design constraint. A constitution must keep two things true at once:

1. **The governing frame must not drift into self-serving abuse.** In figure terms the government is a frame with a library and an action. If its checks update on the government's own account of its conduct, correction stops. That is collapse 1, mutual update (coherent drift), and collapse 2, common cause (hidden shared source), in Fig 2 :4-7. A check that agrees with the government because it reads the government's own report is a mirror, not an anchor.
2. **The government must still be able to act.** If checks are absolute vetoes held by every office, or prohibitions no real occupant can satisfy, the checking arrangement removes the government's capacity to govern. In figure terms the ground's pressure on the chain goes to zero and the chain runs in place (Fig 7 :37-39). That is paralysis.

What each option leads to: a constitution with strong but anchored checks preserves both; a constitution with mirror checks preserves neither (abuse goes unchecked); a constitution with saturated vetoes stops abuse by stopping government.

**The sought-for shape, bound as an ordinary term.** The formula's "Direction" rule says a loop cannot check the direction of its own question, so the shape must be written down, bound, and allowed to accrue a residual like anything else. The bound term used throughout this folder is:

> An effective constitutional check is a relation in which the checker ranges against a ground the checked party cannot move, draws on evidence the checked party does not author, and is satisfiable by a real office without zeroing the government's capacity to act.

That term is refusable by the same test as any other (see the pre-registered refuter in `01_figure_grounded_audit.md` §4). It is not assumed to be true.

## What is deliberately not here

- No personal emails of named individuals.
- No ranking of people by how likely they are to agree with the derivation.
- No bulk-send plan.

Three reasons, in the figures' own words:

1. **Agreement is not an anchor.** Fig 2 :35: *"Alignment is triangulation, not negotiation. A mirror cannot correct you."* Selecting recipients for likely agreement manufactures exactly the correlation that makes their agreement worthless (Seat Map :163: high correlation is disqualifying for a verdict and efficient for a convention).
2. **The checker must not be able to reconstruct the claim.** Fig 10 :42: *"A verifier that can reconstruct the claim cannot verify it."* Sending the full framework before asking for a verdict spends the only thing an outside frame contributes.
3. **The shape is not the theory.** The figures return a kind of thing, never the answer itself (Fig 9 :32). What is offered for checking is the shape of an effective check and its refuter, not a claim to have settled constitutional design.

Legally, scraping personal emails for unsolicited bulk outreach breaches GDPR and CAN-SPAM style rules in many jurisdictions and gets the sending domain blacklisted. That is a practical failure on top of the structural one.
