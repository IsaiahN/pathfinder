# The Tether's answer on constitutional checks and paralysis

**Mode: `specified`, not `grounded`.** This is a shape and a route, not a result. Simulated derivation checked against the figures (`05_what_shape_means.md` §7), not a ground reading.

## 1. The residual it answers

A constitution must stop a government from drifting into self-serving abuse while leaving it able to act. The two conditions overlap in the same institutions: the office that anchors power is often the office that can veto action. By Fig 9 :55, *"A bundled problem is usually a union of overlapping conditions, not a list of separate ones."* It needs one loop, not two averaged verdicts.

## 2. The shape

1. **A check must be an anchor, not a mirror.** Fig 2 :29: *"An anchor is whatever settles the question and does not change its answer because of what any frame thinks."* Fig 2 :35: *"Alignment is triangulation, not negotiation. A mirror cannot correct you."* A court, auditor or legislature that updates its view of its own power on the government's view has become another frame, and the drift is coherent.
2. **Its evidence must be independent of the checked party.** Fig 2 :31: *"Independence, between two frames, is how little they draw on the same evidence."* Fig 2 :40: *"disqualifying for a verdict: agreement about the world is worthless when the agreeing frames share an evidence pool"*. The count, the published record, the opposition's own information, are structural, not moral.
3. **It must be satisfiable by a real office.** Fig 10 :53: *"And a prohibition nothing can satisfy is a prohibition stated wrong."* An absolute veto over every action demands an occupant who can act and never act. Qualified and reviewable powers survive this test; absolute negatives do not.
4. **It must keep the ground's pressure on the chain.** Fig 7 :37-39: *"Progress per handoff has two factors. Zero either and the chain runs in place:"* the bracket's fidelity *"the bracket's fidelity across the handoff"* and *"the ground's pressure on the room"*. Perfect procedure with no answerability to results is paralysis. Madison's proportionality and the separation-of-duties source both corroborate: *"The provision for defense must in this, as in all other cases, be made commensurate to the danger of attack."* and *"Increased protection from fraud and errors must be balanced with the increased cost/effort required."*
5. **Strength should be bought in proportion to the cost of being wrong.** Fig 2 :42: *"Buy independence in proportion to the cost of being wrong."* More checks are not always better; veto-player research records that change becomes *"more slow and difficult with increases in the number of veto players"*.
6. **The actors are a habitat, not a standing list of good institutions.** Fig 11 :50: *"The same actor is a benefactor to one residual and a malfactor to another, so mutualism and parasitism are relations rather than kinds."* Enumerate the actual offices in contact with the residual (Fig 11 :43) and judge their valence there.
7. **The framework returns a shape, not a name.** Fig 9 :32: *"you get a kind of thing, never the answer itself"*. It does not select between judicial review, a constitutional court, parliamentary sovereignty, federalism or a bill of rights. That selection needs a ground this package does not hold.

## 3. What it explicitly does not say

- Not that any particular constitution is best. No name is licensed by the figures.
- Not that more checks are better. Past a point they buy gridlock.
- Not that the derivation is settled. It is `specified`, ungrounded, and a simulation.
- Not that agreement among constitutional theorists would corroborate it. They share an evidence pool (Fig 2 :40).
- Not that the political sources decide the verdict. Madison, Tsebelis and the separation-of-powers literature are secondary witnesses; the figures decide.

## 4. What would settle it: the ground

A discriminator, not a further argument. *The Hard Problem is About Source*: *"An instrument that can't distinguish these cases demonstrates its own inadequacy"*, and *"The answer is unreached until a better instrument exists."* Here:

- **Anchor test:** does the reviewing office's ruling change when the government's account of its own conduct changes, with no loss of corrective force? If yes, the check is a mirror.
- **Independence test:** do the checkers draw on at least one evidence source the checked party does not author? If no, their agreement is the pool talking.
- **Satisfiability test:** can a real office execute the check without ceasing to be able to act? If no, the check is paralysis.
- **Pressure test:** does any check remain answerable to the result of government action? If no, the chain runs in place.
- Standing falsifiers: any of the five in `01_figure_grounded_audit.md` §4 firing.

## 5. The honest stop

**Name unreached, not unreachable.** The figures narrow the properties an effective check must have and stop there. Fig 13 :134: *"Unreachable is a verdict here, not a silence,"*. The choice of a specific institution is a `gap` in this package, reported rather than filled. The lineage, the refuter and the instrument that would close it are recorded so the stop is checkable. *Decoding Tacit Knowledge*: *"Two navigators don't find their position by agreeing with each other. Each takes a bearing on the same fixed landmark, and nobody can talk the landmark into moving."*

## 6. One-paragraph version for outreach

> The Tether does not name the best constitution. It narrows what any effective constitutional check must be. A check that updates on the government's own account of its conduct is a mirror, not an anchor, so it cannot correct anything; a check whose evidence comes only from the checked party has zero independence, so its agreement is worthless. At the other end, a check no real office can satisfy produces paralysis, not accountability, and a chain with perfect procedure but no pressure from results runs in place. So the shape is: checks that range against a record the checked party cannot move, that draw on independent evidence, that are satisfiable by real offices, and whose strength is bought in proportion to the cost of being wrong. The anti-abuse and anti-paralysis conditions overlap, so they must be tested together, never averaged. What is offered is that shape, the refuters written in advance, and the instruments that would settle it. The choice of a specific constitution stays open until a ground decides.
