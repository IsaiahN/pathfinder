## Relevant wider literature and why it bears on the derivation

1. **Madison 1788** is the canonical statement of the abuse-versus-capacity problem in the question's own field. It corroborates F1 and F4 in `01`: checks must be *"commensurate to the danger of attack"* and government must both control the governed and control itself. It does **not** supply the figure verdict; it is a witness from political theory.
2. **Tsebelis 2002, veto players** supplies the paralysis mechanism from comparative political science. It corroborates F4: more veto players make change *"more slow and difficult."* The Wikipedia summary also records a criticism that veto-player theory's prediction about consensus democracy *"hasn't been confirmed"*, so the empirical status is contested. Recorded as contestation, not as a settled law.
3. **Separation of powers** supplies the independence condition in the question's own vocabulary. It corroborates F2 with the sentence about institutions dominated by one another never challenging each other, and F4 with the gridlock sentence.
4. **Separation of duties** is the low-overlap witness from accounting and information security. It states the same two-sided bargain in a different currency: more protection must be balanced with more cost and effort, and more than one person is required to complete a task. It corroborates F2 and F4 without sharing the political-science evidence pool.
5. **Swiss cheese model** is the low-overlap witness from safety engineering. It corroborates F8: many imperfect independent barriers beat a single point of failure, and failures happen when the holes align (Fig 2's common cause). I record the source's own caution that the model is *"used too broadly"*; it is an analogue, not a proof.

**Overlap warning.** Items 1, 2 and 3 are one broad evidence pool (constitutional and political science). Their agreement is one witness with three names. The genuinely independent corroboration for the figure verdict comes from items 4 and 5.

## Distribution: offer, do not push

Figure-grounded reasons:

- **Fig 4 :22-25**, *"Only methods cross up. Only head starts come down."* Sending a copied constitution before anyone has described a residual sends a recording, not a generator. Lead with the shape claim and its refuter; offer the links after.
- **Fig 10 :42**, *"A verifier that can reconstruct the claim cannot verify it."* A recipient who has absorbed the framework before being asked for a verdict has joined the frame being checked.
- **Fig 9 :27-28**, *"describe it before you go looking, or any frame you pick will seem to fit."* Ask what they would expect to see if the shape claim were true before giving them the derivation.
- **Seat Map :163**, *"High correlation is disqualifying for a verdict and efficient for a convention."* A send selected for people who already agree produces a convention, not corroboration.
- **Fig 2 :42**, *"Buy independence in proportion to the cost of being wrong."* One careful outside reader is worth more than a list of likely agreeers when the cost of being wrong is high.

**Order in a reply:** (1) the residual, the shape claim and the refuter; (2) the question "what would you count as a failure here?", with their answer recorded before any theory; (3) only if curious, the links above, with the note that the figures, not the summary, are the authority; (4) only on request, the formula as a method to reuse, with a warning that each user must state their own mode and write their own refuter.

## The two-stage offer (stage 3 is opt-in)

- **Stage 1 before stage 2** is Fig 10 :52, *"Bring a characterised residual, never the source"*, and Fig 2 :45, *"Frames range against the ground. They do not negotiate with it."*
- **Stage 2 is opt-in by construction.** Once a recipient holds the theory, their agreement stops being evidence, so the stage-1 verdict must be recorded first and later agreement is never pooled as corroboration (Fig 2 :40, *"disqualifying for a verdict: agreement about the world is worthless when the agreeing frames share an evidence pool"*).
- **Stage 3 sends a method**, which is permitted (Fig 4 :22). Each user still needs a ground and a pre-registered refuter for their own problem, because a frame *"cannot check the direction of its own question"* (formula, Direction).

**Hard precondition:** stages 1 to 3 only work if the stage-1 output survives. If the refuter fires, the claim is withdrawn or re-censused before any theory is shown.
