## VERDICT (from the figures)

The shape survives; the name is not given. Effective constitutional checks are anchors that do not update on the checked party, draw on independent evidence, are satisfiable by real offices, and keep the ground's pressure on the chain with strength proportional to the cost of being wrong. A mirror check cannot correct, an absolute check paralyses, and a chain with perfect procedure but no pressure runs in place. The abuse and paralysis conditions overlap, so the compound needs one loop and no averaging. Which precise institution achieves this in a polity is a `gap`, because no figure supplies an anchor for that name. This is a `specified`, ungrounded result, and it is a simulation checked for internal consistency, not a ground reading.

- **cost if wrong:** bounded and locatable. The packet states mode, three checkable sub-claims, refuters and provenance before the conclusion, so an error is findable rather than invisible. The real cost is spending the independence of outside readers by sending a name or a theory instead of the shape and its refuter; Fig 2 :42 prices that (*"Buy independence in proportion to the cost of being wrong."*). That cost is avoidable by leading with the shape and the refuter.
- **undoable:** yes. The name is not asserted, so nothing has to be withdrawn to correct the shape. A failed anchor test, independence test, satisfiability test or pressure test removes the corresponding sub-claim without touching the others. A sent packet can be superseded by a corrected one.
- **reversed by:** a ground, not agreement. An instrument that discriminates an anchor from a mirror in a real constitution would settle D4; a demonstrated effective check whose anchor updates on the government would break C1; a demonstrated check that no real office can satisfy yet still prevents abuse would break C2; a chain with zero ground pressure that still progresses would break C3; a proof that the two conditions never overlap would dissolve the union compound. Until one of these fires, the verdict stands as `candidate`.
