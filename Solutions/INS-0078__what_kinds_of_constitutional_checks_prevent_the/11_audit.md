## Round trip gap R_T

The Symbols table defines R_T as the gap between what was sent up and what came back down, `gap(x, (T_E ∘ T_A)(x))`, measuring what the coarser description cannot hold. Fig 4 :30 requires it to be measured rather than assumed. I ran the round trip on the condensation this folder actually uses.

2. **Abstraction (T_A):** the shorthand in `07_the_tether_answer.md` §2: checks must be anchors with independent evidence, satisfiable by real offices, proportional, and the pair is a union compound.
3. **Concretisation (T_E):** the concrete question the reader actually wants answered, namely which institutions a given constitution should adopt.

**R_T is nonzero.** Three things the shorthand holds do not survive the trip back down:

- the **bond distinction** between a symmetric mutual veto and an ordered anchor check (Fig 12 :59 vs :74). The shorthand says "independent", which does not by itself exclude a mutually agreed veto;
- the **refusal operator** (Operators table :57-58). A vocabulary that only adds procedures cannot carry the correction that a check has become a mirror;
- the **name**, which is a `gap`. Concretising the shape into a named constitution would add an atom the figures do not supply, which Fig 6 prices above starting from primitives.

One thing survives cleanly: a check that updates on the checked party cannot correct it, and a checking chain with zero ground pressure runs in place. That residue is the part worth sending. R_T was **not** measured numerically, because no ground reading exists; it is recorded qualitatively as the enumerated set of dropped constraints. Treating the formula as identical to the figures would set R_T to zero and would be the dead-reckoning error Fig 4 :30 names. The honest form is a lossy condensation with a measured gap, which is what `09_watson_answer.md` states.
