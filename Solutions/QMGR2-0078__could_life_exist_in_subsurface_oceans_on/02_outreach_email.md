**Use:** one to one, or as a cover note attached to a submission through a public venue in `03_public_contact_points.csv`. **Not** for bulk send, not to a purchased list, and not to personal addresses harvested from a page.

**Hard precondition:** do not send until F1, F3 and F6 in `01_figure_grounded_audit.md` are understood as stated. The stage-1 offer is the shape claim and its refuter, not the theory. If the shape does not survive, there is nothing else to look at.

**Subject:** One checkable claim about ocean-world life detection, and what would refute it

> Hello,
>
> I have one specific output about life in subsurface oceans on icy worlds that I would like checked against what you already know. I am not asking you to read a framework first, and I am not asking you to agree with anything. If the output does not survive, there is nothing further to look at. A one-line failure is more useful to me than an endorsement. If you would rather not engage, no reply is needed.
>
> **The output, `specified`, not `grounded`:**
>
> 1. "Could life exist" and "how could it be detected" are two conditions, not one question with two halves. They have different anchors. The first is about an ocean habitat; the second is about attributing a reading to a source. A single lever that settles one leaves the other standing.
> 2. Whether a candidate can live somewhere is a property of the candidate and the habitat together, not of the ocean alone and not of the organism alone. So the habitat has to be enumerated from the world: water, rock, a spendable chemical difference, pH, redox pairs, transport through the ice, power from tides or radioactivity, and time. Each of those is a separate slot. None is averaged into a single habitability score.
> 3. A self-maintaining physical system needs a spendable difference. Energy on its own is directionless. So an ocean with energy but no gradient, no ordering or no network is not a habitat in this sense. The framework returns that shape. It does **not** say which structure occupies the slot, and I am not claiming it can.
> 4. Detection is not a single magic molecule. A reading does not determine its source: several abiotic pathways can produce the same spectrum, the same isotope ratio or the same amino-acid excess. What is owed is a source-discriminating arrangement of instruments, an abiotic baseline and a stated failure condition. A mass spectrometer is the next sharpening of an instrument already returning unresolved material, not a sensor invented from a description of life.
> 5. A non-detection is a bound, not an absence. There is no sealed room here, so a search that finds nothing proves nothing about what is outside the swept region.
>
> **What is deliberately not claimed:** that any named moon is inhabited; that a particular biochemistry is the answer; that chirality, methane, phosphates or molecular assembly is *the* biosignature; that one Earth example licenses a universal template; or that this derivation is a measurement. It is a shape and a route, stated in `specified` mode, and it has no ground reading of its own.
>
> **What would refute the output:** these were written before I sent this.
> 1. A structure rule that yields a specific biochemistry or a named biosignature from the framework alone.
> 2. A single measured signature shown to determine a biological source uniquely under a quantified abiotic baseline, with no contextual or construction-history information.
> 3. A reading shown to be independent of the ocean's habitability conditions, so that contact fails.
> 4. Two teams counting as two witnesses while using the same spectra, the same Earth analogue library and the same abiotic model.
> 5. A non-detection settled as an absence over a scope that is not sealed.
>
> If any one holds, I will narrow or withdraw the claim. **What would you count as a failure here?** An answer to that question is the part I actually want.
>
> **If, and only if, the output survives:** I can send three public sources. The figures, not my summary of them, are the authority.
> - The figure set and formula, public repository: https://github.com/IsaiahN/tether/tree/general-agent/figures
> - NASA Ladder of Life Detection, the published rung structure with false-positive and false-negative criteria: https://astrobiology.nasa.gov/news/the-ladder-of-life-detection/
> - Abiotic-baseline framework for ocean worlds, Higgins et al. 2026: https://arxiv.org/abs/2605.15337
>
> **If you want to reuse the method:** the formula is in the figures link. Run it on a problem you know well and can check against a ground. Write down what would refute your result *before* you run it, and state the mode of what you get. A reported failure is more useful to me than a reported success.
>
> Thank you for your time,
> [name and institutional affiliation]

## Why the parts are in this order

| the move | figure-grounded reason |
|---|---|
| Residual and refuter first, theory last | Fig 10 :52, *"Bring a characterised residual, never the source."* The recipient ranges the output against what they know before holding the framework interior. |
| Do not ask for agreement or trust | Fig 2 :35, *"Alignment is triangulation, not negotiation. A mirror cannot correct you."* Asking for agreement selects for the correlation that disqualifies a verdict (Fig 2 :40). |
| Refuter written before the run | Seat Map discipline: a falsifier written before the run is a design instrument. It also makes the claim refusable by an outside frame that has seen no interiors. |
| Mode on every substantive claim | `THE_FORMULA.md`: *"Mode. general · specified · grounded. State which."* |
| Two conditions, not one | Fig 9 :54-55, union before partition; a case in two conditions is not a harder case of either. |
| Per-slot habitat, never averaged | Fig 1 :19 and :22; a live signal disappears into an average. |
| Shape not a name for the structure | Fig 9 :32, *"you get a kind of thing, never the answer itself."* |
| Source discrimination over signature naming | *The Hard Problem is About Source*: the instrument *"cannot comment on the source"*; the spectrometer, not an argument, resolved the case. |
| Instrument grows from an existing reading | Fig 6 :33-35; no sensor from a description of Mars, and no edge where nothing is already returning something. |
| Non-detection reported as a bound | Fig 8 :39 and Fig 9 :44; no sealed room. |
| Prestige kept out of the ask | *The Recursive Self-Improvement Trap*: prestige *"can fund an action"* but *"cannot settle an action. Only the ground can settle it."* The ask gives the claim a budget to be tried; it does not request endorsement. |
| Stage 2 is opt-in | Fig 10 :42, *"A verifier that can reconstruct the claim cannot verify it."* A recipient who has absorbed the framework before giving a verdict has joined the frame being checked. |
| Stage 3 sends a method | Fig 4 :22-23, *"Only methods cross up. Only head starts come down."* The formula is a generator, so giving it away gives away no answer. |
| No personal emails, no bulk send | Public opt-in channels avoid the structural failure and the legal one (GDPR and CAN-SPAM style rules) at the same time. |
