## Round trip gap R_T

The Symbols table defines R_T as the gap between what was sent up and what came back down, `gap(x, (T_E ∘ T_A)(x))`, measuring what the coarser description cannot hold. Fig 4 :30 requires it to be measured rather than assumed. I ran the round trip on the condensation the folder actually uses.

1. **Sent up (x):** the verbatim figure set, chiefly Fig 9 :54-55 (union before partition), Fig 11 :39/:43/:44 (habitat agent-and-pair, enumeration), Fig 13 :74/:76 (cross-kind flow), Fig 6 :32/:34 (instrument chain), Fig 8 :39 (null is not absence), Fig 9 :32 (shape not a name).
2. **Abstraction (T_A):** the shorthand carried in `07` and `09`: the question is two compounds; habitability needs a spendable difference; detection needs source discrimination; occupancy is ground-owned; nulls are bounds.
3. **Concretisation (T_E):** a concrete claim about a named moon, for example "Enceladus is inhabited by methanogens using H2 and CO2, and the signature is isotopically light methane."

**R_T is nonzero.** Four things the shorthand holds do not survive the trip back down:

- the **per-slot index**, which collapses into a global yes/no when the shorthand is applied to "icy worlds" as one class (Fig 1 :19, :22);
- the **shape-not-a-name limit**, which the concrete claim breaches by naming a metabolism from measurements alone (Fig 9 :32);
- the **abiotic baseline and construction-history requirement**, which the concrete claim omits, turning a reading into a source (Hard Problem is About Source; Higgins et al. 2026);
- the **status of the null**, which the concrete claim converts from a bound into a verdict of absence (Fig 8 :39).

Conversely, two things survive cleanly: habitability is agent-and-habitat with a spendable difference, and detection must grow from an already-returning instrument with a stated discrimination. Those are the residue worth sending. R_T was **not** measured numerically, because there is no ground reading; it is recorded qualitatively as the enumerated set of dropped constraints. Treating "the formula is the condensed version of the metalaws" as identity would set R_T to zero and would be the dead-reckoning error Fig 4 :30 names. The honest form is a lossy condensation with a measured gap, which is what `09` states.
