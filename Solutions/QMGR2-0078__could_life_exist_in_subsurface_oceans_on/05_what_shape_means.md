## 1. A shape is a constraint, not a filler

The canonical limit: the framework returns *"what a missing thing would have to look like (a shape, not a name)."* Fig 9 :32: *"you get a kind of thing, never the answer itself."* Fig 13 :134-135: *"Unreachable is a verdict here, not a silence,"* and *"It cannot say which primitive is missing, because naming it would mean already holding it."*

From *The Library of Babel*, opened this session: *"you can cut your search short if the set of books returned is small and descriptive enough that it provides a general shape of the answer that satisfies your question."* The same essay: *"The problem you have determines what makes a book relevant to you or not,"* and *"If you don't know the right terms to search for, you will have to use the existing vocabulary you do know that is closest to the words that you wish to describe and grasp the shape of."*

So a **shape** is the narrowed form of an answer that can be stated before it can be named. It constrains; it does not fill. For this question:

- Shape: *a self-maintaining ocean system needs a spendable difference and a cross-kind composition, and a detection needs a source-discriminating instrument above an abiotic baseline.*
- Name (not delivered by the figures): *Enceladus is inhabited by methanogens using H2 and CO2, and the signature is isotopically light methane.*

The first is checkable and has a refuter. The second is a ground-owned fact that this derivation does not hold.

## 2. How a shape is obtained

*The Library of Babel* gives the search procedure:

- Bootstrap by proximity: use *"the existing vocabulary you do know that is closest to the words that you wish to describe"*, then look for *"commonly recurring new terms that you are unfamiliar with"* that *"overlap, correlate, or are even used interchangeably with your known terms."* Each new term *"narrow[s] the space by definition and deepen[s] your vocabulary."*
- Steer by the question: the returned books *"need to be tested or ranged against the problem you want answered in order to steer the direction of the next search."*
- Stop at the shape: *"The books that you are returning in your searches need to be tested"* until the set is *"small and descriptive enough that it provides a general shape of the answer."*
- On a stalemate: *"Perhaps your problem was a bundle or conflation of several problems masquerading as one? You will need to search all of those problems individually."* This is Fig 9 :54-55 applied to QMGR2-0078: existence and detection must be unbundled.

Fig 6 :30: *"The description travels. Fails by being sharpened into terms only we can read. Cast it in effect terms."* Fig 9 :30-31: *"your words are borrowed too, so describe the effect rather than the cause."* So the residual was stated in section 0 of `01` as a gap between readings and predictions, not as "we lack a life detector." The latter already assumes the answer's shape.

## 3. What licenses a name

Only two things, both outside the shape itself:

1. **The ground settles it.** Formula step 5: *"the ground settles it. Verified only by what pays. A gate is not the ground."* The canonical README: the framework *"stops at the limit of what the available instruments can distinguish, and records the lineage so the stop is honest."* For this question: an in-situ or returned-sample measurement that discriminates biological from abiotic source under a stated baseline.
2. **A provenanced import from a frame whose closure differs** (Fig 6 :23, Fig 8 :50-52), with a source gate and a shadow test. There is no such frame here: Earth life is the single instance held inside D2, not a frame whose closure differs. Naming its biochemistry as the answer is a binding, not an import that moves a wall (Fig 6 :14, *"the only operator that moves the wall"*).

A useful check from the framework essays mapped in the exemplar: *"The real import is not a new term in the existing space. It is a new dimension of the space"* (*They're Scanning Books When They Should*, mapped from the exemplar, not re-opened this session). Nothing in the ocean-world literature supplies a new dimension to the six-term set; it supplies bindings of measured conditions onto held atoms.

## 4. The source problem is the metamerism problem

*The Hard Problem is About Source*, opened this session:

> *"The Problem: You can have several underlying causes that create identical readings but the definition of the output would be read as the same."*
>
> *"If several paths create the same behavior, then behavior is a projection with lossy information"*
>
> *"the instrument is speaking only about what it measured, it cannot comment on the source."*
>
> *"What the Spectrometer Example Reveals: The answer is unreached until a better instrument exists."*
>
> *"It was proven wrong not by argument, but by the arrival of a better instrument."*

The last two lines are the instruction for D3. A biosignature reading is a projection. The source (biological, abiotic organic chemistry, radiation chemistry, contamination) is not determined by the projection. An instrument that cannot distinguish those cases *"demonstrates its own inadequacy."* What is owed is a better instrument and an abiotic baseline, not a more confident name. Higgins et al. 2026 state the field version: neglecting the baseline *"risks false negative life detection claims for both isotopic and chiral biosignatures."*

Fig 12 :104: *"A settled molecule becomes an atom for whatever composes over it. So irreducible is a fact about a level and never about the world, and there is no bottom."* The lineage is part of the term. Rutter et al. 2025 state the measurement version: molecular assembly *"reflects the assembly of molecules with their bonds as building blocks, in contrast to approaches that discount construction history."* That is why source-discriminating detection is a shape the figures can license even while they withhold the name of the source.

## 5. Correction to my own reading of section 3

I initially wrote "the framework cannot name any structure or condition in an ocean, full stop." That overstates the rule, and the correction is kept here rather than silently erased.

- The canonical phrase is *"what a **missing** thing would have to look like (a shape, not a name)."* Its subject is a primitive not held in Gamma.
- Gradient, spectrum and network are all already among the six (Fig 13). Naming a spendable difference in an ocean is a **binding onto a held atom**, not the invention of a missing primitive.
- What remains forbidden is (a) deriving which of the held structures must occupy the slot from the six alone, and (b) claiming that the binding adds an atom or moves the wall (Fig 6 :14, Fig 8 :32, *"Closure is idempotent. Composing inside a closed set never leaves it, so no search, however long, adds an atom."*).
- My section 4 metamerism point is **not** retracted. The reason a measured signature cannot be named as biological is the missing source-discriminating instrument, not a ban on naming held atoms. Both claims are recorded so neither is quietly lost.

This is the same correction the exemplar made for "metric tensor = Gradient": naming a held atom is a binding with provenance, while claiming the framework derived the name is the error.

## 6. Two consequences for this solution

**Prestige is not action.** From *The Recursive Self-Improvement Trap*, opened this session:

> *"Prestige must never be allowed to validate an action directly."*
>
> *"Prestige can fund an action. It can give an action a budget to be tried. But it cannot settle an action. Only the ground can settle it. The Ground Maintainer must be blind to prestige."*

A list of famous astrobiologists who agree with a detection claim is the named collapse, not corroboration. The legitimate ask gives the claim a budget to be tried: an instrument, a sample, a refuter, a public venue.

**This audit is a simulation, not a ground reading.** Same essay:

> *"LLMs can quickly simulate an approximate answer/output with the formula found in the architecture post. It will often be better than the generic outputs that you will usually get otherwise too."*
>
> *"However, the truly testable output would need to be derived from the real architecture or working the formula yourself."*

So `01`, `06`, `07`, `09` and `11` are simulated derivations checked for internal consistency against the verbatim figure text. They are **not** tests of whether life exists in any ocean, and none of them can settle the question it reports on. Every mode label in the folder is to be read with that limit attached.

## 7. What "shape" is for a detection question, in one sentence

A shape here is the set of co-holding conditions and discriminations that any answer about ocean-world life must satisfy, stated before a source is named: the union split, the per-slot residual, the habitat enumeration, the cross-kind spendable difference, the instrument chain from an already-returning reading, the abiotic baseline, and the rule that a null result is a bound and never an absence.
