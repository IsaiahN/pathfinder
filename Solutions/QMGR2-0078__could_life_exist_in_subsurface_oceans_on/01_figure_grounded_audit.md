## 0. The residual, stated before any figure is chosen

R_s = |Gamma(b,a) - o'| per object slot s (Symbols table :38, *"The gap between prediction and observation."*; Fig 1 :19, *"The residual is indexed by object slot, not aggregated."*).

- **Slots:** Europa, Enceladus, Ganymede, Callisto, Titan, Triton, and the smaller icy bodies. Each carries its own residual. Averaging across them would *"throw away the only signal that says where to look"* (Fig 1 :28). Enceladus already returns plume material; Europa returns a surface and a likely ocean but no confirmed plume; the others return less. These are different slots with different instruments, not repeated samples of one slot.
- **Arity:** the question is a three-place contact relation (ocean material, instrument, frame) indexed by slot and ordered in time, and it is **two conditions bound into one sentence**: (i) could life exist, (ii) how could it be detected. Those are not one hard problem with two halves.
- **Symmetry:** "habitable" and "inhabited" are threshold relations, not symmetric joins (Fig 12 :86, *"a threshold relation between two quantities"*). The proposed detection route is an ordered production claim (life produces a signature, ⇒) whose reading is also producible abiotically, so the order cannot be recovered from the reading alone. That is a source problem, not a quantity problem.
- **Scale:** molecular (monomers, bonds, chirality, isotopes) to ocean (redox gradients, pH, hydrothermal flow) to moon (ice shell, tidal dissipation, plume) to system (several ocean worlds). The membrane between scales is lossy (Fig 4 :30).
- **What fails to resolve:** a plume spectrum, an isotope ratio, a chirality measurement, an induced magnetic field, an ice thickness, a gravity harmonic. Each returns something and none yet settles source or habitation.

**Binding the sought-for shape as an ordinary term (formula step "Direction"), written before the run:**

> An answer to QMGR2-0078 would look like a per-world habitability shape (conditions that must co-hold in an ocean habitat), plus a detection route whose success condition is stated in advance, whose abiotic baseline is quantified, and whose failure condition is a named instrument or a named failure of contact. It would not look like a named organism, a single magic molecule, or a global yes/no averaged over all icy moons.

That sentence is a term now. It accrues a residual like anything else and is refusable by the same test (Fig 5 :65-66).

## 3. Findings

### F1. The question is a union compound, and the two halves need different anchors (`general`)

Fig 9 :54-55 and :57 (quoted in section 1) govern the split. The existence half is a contact-and-composition question anchored by the ocean material. The detection half is a source-attribution question anchored by the same material through a discriminating instrument, with the abiotic baseline as part of the frame. They must be unbundled before a search: asking "is it habitable?" and "what would count as a detection?" in one breath lets a positive habitability result stand in for a detection, which is exactly the substitution the figures forbid (Fig 1 :24-27: *"coverage, and compression achieved"* and *"predicates minted, and credibility accrued"* are frame-internal).

**Refuter:** a single observation that settles both conditions without a described residual for each, with the success condition written after the result.

### F2. Habitability is agent-and-habitat, and the habitat is enumerated, never composed (`general`, triangulated)

Fig 11 :39: *"So the answerable question is not what intelligence is, but what makes a system capable. And capability is a property of agent-and-habitat, never of the agent alone."* Fig 11 :40: *"A system cannot be made more capable by improving the agent in isolation. An improvement that does not change contact changes nothing, however much it improves."* Fig 11 :43: *"The habitat is enumerated, never composed."* Fig 11 :44: *"List everything in contact with the residual, then what is in contact with those, and outward until the cascade stops mattering."*

For D1 the habitat list is read off the world, not invented: water activity, pH (Glein and Truong 2025 report pH 10.1 to 11.6 for Enceladus), redox pairs, H2 as an energy source (Oberg, Magnabosco and Tosca 2026), silica and hydrothermal circulation, tidal dissipation (Tjoa, Mueller and van der Tak 2020), ice-shell thickness, plume transport time, and the surface irradiation environment. Fig 11 :48: *"You do not invent the list. You read it off the world, and what you cannot perceive or measure yet is the residual."* Fig 11 :49: *"An actor's valence is relative to the residual, not a property it carries."* H2 is a benefactor to a methanogen-shaped residual and just a molecule to a different one; there is no standing list of good actors (Fig 11 :51).

The world literature here is corroboration only and is not the verdict. The verdict is the structural claim: whether life could exist is a property of the ocean-and-candidate together, so "the ocean is habitable" and "life exists" are different slots and must not be averaged.

**Refuter:** a demonstration that habitability is fixed by an intrinsic property of the ocean alone, unchanged when the candidate metabolism, the energy source and the transport route are all varied, with the ocean's contact structure held.

### F3. A structure must sit between the states; the structure is not named by the figures (`general` on the rule, `specified` on the application, triangulated)

Fig 13 :74: *"Everything else is a composition across the two kinds, never within one."* Fig 13 :76: *"Energy and a gradient compose a flow."* Under the six-term set, a gradient is *"a difference that can be spent"* (Fig 13 :21-22) and a state with no structure has no magnitude (Fig 13 :29). So an ocean with energy but no spendable difference is not a habitat in the figure sense: the difference (redox, pH, thermal, chemical) is the structure that lets energy compose into a flow.

What is forbidden is naming which structure from the six alone. Fig 9 :32: *"you get a kind of thing, never the answer itself."* Fig 13 :135: *"It cannot say which primitive is missing, because naming it would mean already holding it."* Gradient, spectrum and network are all already in the library, so naming one is a **binding from the ocean-chemistry frame**, not the invention of a seventh primitive and not an import that moves a wall (Fig 6 :14, *"the only operator that moves the wall"*). The correct form is: *a spendable difference must mediate; which one is present in Enceladus is a measured binding with provenance.* Naming "hydrothermal gradient" as *the* answer to life-in-oceans is the same class of error the exemplar retracted for "metric tensor = Gradient."

**Refuter:** a figure line that selects one of the three structures from the six-term set alone, or a measurement showing a self-maintaining ocean biosphere with no spendable difference at all. The first breaks shape-not-a-name; the second breaks the flow composition.

### F4. The actual existence of life is a `gap` owned by the ground, not a silence (`specified`)

Fig 2 :29: *"An anchor is whatever settles the question and does not change its answer because of what any frame thinks. A measurement, an experiment, a counter, a market, a patient outcome."* Fig 2 :35: *"Alignment is triangulation, not negotiation. A mirror cannot correct you."* The figures return the shape of a habitable-and-inhabited world and the route to a check, not the fact of habitation on a named moon. No figure line yields "Europa is inhabited" or "Enceladus is sterile." Fig 13 :134: *"Unreachable is a verdict here, not a silence,"* and Fig 8 :39: *"So a search that finds nothing is never a proof of absence."*

This is a `gap` of the **ground-owned** kind, distinct from an in-principle underivability. The measurement is possible in conception (sample return, plume flyby, lander) and has not been made at the required discrimination. Importing a factory-made answer (an inference from one Earth biosphere) against this budget would add an atom that was not needed and would cost more than starting from primitives (Fig 6 :51-52, Fig 8 :41-43).

**Refuter:** a figure re-census that yields a per-world occupancy value without an anchor, or a ground measurement that settles habitation on one named world and is then mislabelled as a figure derivation.

### F5. Detection is an instrument chain, not a hypothetical magic sensor (`specified`, triangulated)

Fig 6 :32: *"An instrument is not built from a description. It is improved from a worse instrument already returning something."* Fig 6 :33: *"Nobody specified a telescope from an account of Mars. A wandering point in the sky was already a reading, and every sharper instrument was built on the residual the last one produced."* Fig 6 :34: *"The question is not whether a sensor could exist. It is whether anything, at any resolution, is already returning something that fails to resolve."* Fig 6 :35: *"Where nothing is, there is no edge to extend from, and the search for a proto-instrument is a search for one that is already there."*

There is an edge here. Cassini's plume pass returned material that fails to resolve source and habitation; Europa's induced field and surface salts return an ocean signal that fails to resolve composition; mass spectrometry of plume grains already returns masses that fail to resolve construction history. So a detector is not asked to spring from a description of life. It is the next sharpening of instruments that already return something (Rutter, Sharma, Seet et al. 2025 build exactly this argument for molecular assembly measured by mass spectrometry). X2 is refused as a domain: it has no nameable anchor and no existing proto-instrument.

**Refuter:** a proposal that starts from a description of life-as-such with no predecessor instrument already returning unresolved material, and that is defended by saying the sensor could in principle be built.

### F6. A reading does not determine its source: the metamerism rule governs biosignatures (`specified`, triangulated)

From *The Hard Problem is About Source* (opened 2026-11-23): an instrument reads green and *"the instrument is speaking only about what it measured, it cannot comment on the source"*; several underlying causes create identical readings, and *"If several paths create the same behavior, then behavior is a projection with lossy information."* It was *"the spectrometer, not an argument"* that resolved the case.

The same structure is explicit in the ocean-world literature. Higgins, Chen, Warr, Fifer, Kang, Cockell and Sherwood Lollar (2026) write that *"identifying potential life on other worlds requires observing clear and unambiguous biosignature signals above the existing abiotic baseline"* and that *"neglecting the abiotic baseline risks false negative life detection claims for both isotopic and chiral biosignatures."* NASA's *Ladder of Life Detection* orders rungs by *"decreasing strength of evidence for life"* and *"increasing ease of measurement"* and lists *"contamination-free likelihood of false positive"* and *"survivable likelihood of false negative"* as explicit criteria. A methane line, an amino-acid excess or a phosphate concentration is a reading; only a source-discriminating arrangement of instruments, context and baseline can attribute it.

Fig 12 :104: *"A settled molecule becomes an atom for whatever composes over it. So irreducible is a fact about a level and never about the world, and there is no bottom"* makes the lineage part of the term. Rutter et al. 2025 state the physical version: molecular assembly *"reflects the assembly of molecules with their bonds as building blocks, in contrast to approaches that discount construction history."* Construction history is the bond information, not documentation added afterwards.

**Refuter:** a demonstration that one measured signature uniquely determines a biological source under a quantified abiotic baseline, with no contextual or geophysical observation and no construction-history information.

### F7. A false positive is a refusal added to the record, not a molecule deleted (`general`)

Operators table :58: *"a refutation is a negation, which is why a vocabulary without it can only accumulate."* Fig 5 :53: *"refuted: a term we hold is wrong; a term is owed that says so"* and :55: *"What it removes is not a term but a term's standing to be composed over. Nothing leaves the library. What falls is the reach."*

In D3 this is the difference between discarding a bad biosignature claim and refusing its standing. When an abiotic pathway is shown for a candidate signature, the signature stays in the record with its provenance and its refusal; what falls is the reach of missions that composed over it as if it were settled. The same applies to a contamination event: the reading is retained, the standing to compose over it is removed. Deleting it would lose the ability to tell "never held" from "held and given up" (Fig 6 :44-46).

**Refuter:** a detection protocol that removes a refuted signature from its library rather than stamping the refusal, and that can therefore re-mint it unchanged in the next run.

### F8. Independence is bought between instruments, not between reviews (`specified`)

Fig 2 :31: *"Two people reading the same report are not two frames; two instruments pointed at the same thing are."* Fig 2 :35 (quoted above). Fig 2 :42: *"Buy independence in proportion to the cost of being wrong."* Fig 7 :35: *"A room's agreement is variation, not a verdict. Selection between rooms is where the ground votes."*

Higgins et al. 2026 prescribe *"complimentary geophysical observations such as constraining internal temperatures to within ~10-100 degrees C, and improving characterisation of the target's rheology, lithology, initial abiotic organic inventory and ocean transport timescales."* That is a low-overlap instruction in the figures' own terms: the isotope frame and the geophysics frame draw on different evidence, so their agreement would mean something. Twenty reviews of the same mass spectrum are one frame wearing twenty names (Fig 8 :48-49).

This simulated second seat shares the primary's evidence pool, so its own agreement is one pool talking (Fig 2 :40). That limit is recorded in `11_audit.md`, not hidden.

**Refuter:** a detection claim that rests only on agreement among analysts using the same spectra, the same Earth analogue library and the same abiotic model, with no second instrument or independent geophysical constraint.

### F9. Filters size the budget, they never deliver the verdict (`general`)

Fig 9 :60: *"Use filters for the budget and witnesses for the verdict. Never let a filter hand you a verdict."* A tier, a mission cost, a plume-detection probability and a signal-to-noise ratio are filters: they decide what gets tried first and how much space is swept. They do not decide whether a world is inhabited. Fig 1 :24-27 disqualifies *"coverage, and compression achieved"* and *"predicates minted, and credibility accrued"* as frame-internal scoring.

The world literature supplies useful filters with provenance: Lingam and Loeb (2018) model that bioessential-element scarcity makes putative ocean biospheres *"oligotrophic, and perhaps harder to detect"*; OPAG (Moore et al. 2020) prioritises *"a life detection Ocean World mission, along with additional technological development for life detection."* Both are budget inputs. Neither is a witness for habitation.

**Refuter:** an argument that moves from a high detection probability or a high mission priority to an assertion of life.

## 4. Pre-registered refuter

Written before the conclusion, per the exemplar's Seat Map rule that *"a falsifier written before the run is a design instrument, not only an honesty device."* The shape claim is refuted if any of the following holds:

1. A figure line yields a specific biochemistry, a named structure or a named biosignature for icy oceans from the six-term set alone (breaks F3 and Fig 9 :32).
2. A single measured signature is shown to determine a biological source uniquely under a quantified abiotic baseline, with no construction-history or geophysical context (breaks F6).
3. A reading is shown to be independent of the ocean's habitability conditions, so that contact fails (breaks F2 and Fig 11 :40).
4. The same detection claim is returned by two frames drawing on the same spectra, the same analogue library and the same abiotic model, and is counted as two witnesses (breaks F8 and Fig 2 :31).
5. A non-detection is settled as an absence over a scope that is not a sealed room (breaks F4 and Fig 8 :39).

If any fires, the claim is narrowed or withdrawn and the refusal is added to the record with provenance (F7). Until one is run, no part of this may be labelled `grounded`.

## 5. Recommendation

1. Lead with F1: unbundle existence from detection and keep per-moon residuals separate (Fig 9 :54, Fig 1 :19).
2. Enumerate the ocean habitat from the world and state which conditions are already returning values and which are residual (Fig 11 :44, :48).
3. Keep the structure slot marked as a binding with provenance; do not name a biochemistry from the figures (Fig 9 :32).
4. Aim detection at source discrimination and the abiotic baseline, and grow the instrument from plumes and existing spectra (Fig 6 :33, Hard Problem is About Source).
5. Report nulls as bounds and write the refuter before the run (Fig 8 :39, Fig 5 :65).
6. Send the generator, not the recording: Fig 4 :23, *"upward: a method that can be reapplied. Never the recording of a particular success."*

- `FRAMEWORK/figures/THE_FORMULA.md`: mode rule, the three guards as a product, settling necessary but not sufficient, and the three distinct questions of step 7 (SEARCHED first, then INWARD, then OUTWARD).

Step 7 applied to D2: **SEARCHED** is anything in closure(Gamma) unfound? The six-term set and the bonds are already held, so a generic inhabitant shape is in the closure. **INWARD** sharpen our instruments? Yes: mass spectrometry, chirality, isotope ratios and geophysical context, all already returning something. **OUTWARD** import a frame whose closure predicts a second genesis? No such frame is held; Earth is one instance inside D2, not a differing closure. INWARD and OUTWARD are not read against each other (Fig 6 :31).
