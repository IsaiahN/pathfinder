# Expert verification packet: the Tether on ocean-world life

**What this is:** a claim in a form an ocean-world researcher can check. Mode, derivation and refuters are stated before the conclusion, so a reader knows what to look for and what would settle it. Send this, not the whole framework.

**Mode: `general` on the union split and habitat rule, `specified` on the per-domain claims, nothing `grounded`.** This is a simulated derivation checked for internal consistency against the verbatim figure text, not a ground reading. It cannot settle the existence question it reports on.

## 1. The claim, in one paragraph

"Could life exist" and "how could it be detected" are two conditions with different anchors, so they must be unbundled before a search. Existence is a property of a candidate and a habitat together, not of an ocean alone; the habitat is enumerated from the world, never composed, and a self-maintaining ocean system needs a spendable difference because energy alone is directionless. Detection is not a named molecule: a reading does not determine its source, so an instrument chain must range a measured excess against a quantified abiotic baseline and record its construction history. The framework returns this shape and the instrument instruction. It returns neither a biochemistry nor an occupancy value for any named moon, and a non-detection is a bound, not an absence.

## 2. Three checkable sub-claims

### C1. Habitat and shape (checkable by reading Figure 11 and Figure 13)

- **Statement:** habitability belongs to agent-and-habitat; the habitat must be enumerated from the world; a cross-kind spendable difference is required; which difference occupies the slot is not derivable from the six-term set.
- **Source, verbatim:**
  - Fig 11 :39 *"So the answerable question is not what intelligence is, but what makes a system capable. And capability is a property of agent-and-habitat, never of the agent alone."*
  - Fig 11 :43 *"The habitat is enumerated, never composed."*
  - Fig 11 :44 *"List everything in contact with the residual, then what is in contact with those, and outward until the cascade stops mattering."*
  - Fig 13 :74 *"Everything else is a composition across the two kinds, never within one."*
  - Fig 13 :76 *"Energy and a gradient compose a flow."*
  - Fig 9 :32 *"you get a kind of thing, never the answer itself"*
- **What to check:** is there any case of a self-maintaining ocean biosphere with no spendable difference at all? Is the habitat list being treated as composed from principles rather than read off the world? Is a specific chemistry being asserted as a figure result rather than as a provenanced binding?
- **Refuter:** a figure line that selects one of the held structures from the six-term set alone, a seventh primitive derived from the other six, or a measured biosphere with no spendable difference. Any one breaks C1.

### C2. Detection as source attribution (checkable by an astrobiologist against current biosignature practice)

- **Statement:** a single measured signature does not determine its source; the abiotic baseline and the construction history are part of the claim; the detection route is ordered (acquisition, production, comparison, subtraction, refusal), and a flat ingredient list cannot carry it.
- **Source, verbatim:**
  - Fig 6 :32 *"An instrument is not built from a description. It is improved from a worse instrument already returning something."*
  - Fig 6 :34 *"The question is not whether a sensor could exist. It is whether anything, at any resolution, is already returning something that fails to resolve."*
  - Fig 12 :104 *"A settled molecule becomes an atom for whatever composes over it. So irreducible is a fact about a level and never about the world, and there is no bottom"*
  - Operators table :58 *"a refutation is a negation, which is why a vocabulary without it can only accumulate"*
  - From *The Hard Problem is About Source* (essay, not a figure): the instrument *"cannot comment on the source"*, and *"If several paths create the same behavior, then behavior is a projection with lossy information."*
  - World corroboration: Higgins et al. 2026 write that identifying life requires *"clear and unambiguous biosignature signals above the existing abiotic baseline"* and that neglecting it *"risks false negative life detection claims for both isotopic and chiral biosignatures."*
- **What to check:** for methane isotope fractionation or amino-acid enantiomeric excess in a plume, can the biological source be separated from the abiotic pathways under the stated baseline? Is the construction history recorded, or only the final composition? Rutter et al. 2025 argue that molecular assembly *"reflects the assembly of molecules with their bonds as building blocks, in contrast to approaches that discount construction history"*; is that distinction measurable with the proposed instrument?
- **Refuter:** one signature shown to determine a biological source uniquely under a quantified abiotic baseline with no contextual or construction-history information, or a report whose success condition is unchanged when the order of acquisition, comparison and refusal is varied.

### C3. Verification and null results (checkable by reading Figures 2, 8 and 9)

- **Statement:** agreement among analysts reading the same data is one evidence pool; independence is bought between instruments; a non-detection is a bound and not an absence.
- **Source, verbatim:**
  - Fig 2 :31 *"Two people reading the same report are not two frames; two instruments pointed at the same thing are."*
  - Fig 2 :35 *"Alignment is triangulation, not negotiation. A mirror cannot correct you."*
  - Fig 2 :42 *"Buy independence in proportion to the cost of being wrong."*
  - Fig 8 :39 *"So a search that finds nothing is never a proof of absence."*
  - Fig 9 :44 *"Only a sealed room can be searched to the end, and no frame is a sealed room. So searching and finding nothing proves nothing."*
  - Fig 9 :60 *"Use filters for the budget and witnesses for the verdict. Never let a filter hand you a verdict."*
- **What to check:** does the proposed detection chain include a second, low-overlap frame (geophysics, mineralogy or transport modelling) rather than more readings of the same spectrum? Are nulls reported with a swept scope and a bound?
- **Refuter:** two frames counted as two witnesses while sharing spectra, analogue library and baseline model, or a non-detection settled as an absence over a scope that is not sealed.

## 3. What is explicitly not claimed

- Not that Europa, Enceladus or any other moon is inhabited or sterile. That is ground-owned and unreached here.
- Not a biochemistry, a named structure or a named biosignature. Chiral excess, isotopically light methane, phosphates and molecular assembly are candidates with baselines still to be quantified, not settlements.
- Not that Earth life is a universal template. One instance is held inside D2; it is not a frame whose closure differs.
- Not that a null result is an absence.
- Not a theory of everything and not a final room (Fig 7 :6, Fig 13 :90).
- Not `grounded`. The packet is a `specified` shape plus a route, and it is a simulated derivation, not a measurement.
- Not a claim that planetary-protection cleaning removes the habitat. It is an introduction policy acted on by a seat, not an exclusion of contact (Fig 11 :66-67).

## 5. What would settle it

1. An in-situ or returned-sample measurement that establishes the ocean's pH, redox inventory, energy flux and transport time, then measures a candidate signature and quantifies its abiotic production pathways under those conditions.
2. A source-discriminating difference that survives an independent geophysical constraint (internal temperature, rheology, lithology, initial organic inventory, ocean transport timescale).
3. Conversely, a measurement of contact failure: a reading shown to be independent of the ocean's habitability conditions. That would narrow or withdraw the shape claim, not merely lower a probability.

## 6. What an expert can check in twenty minutes

1. Read Fig 11 :39, :43, :44 and Fig 13 :74, :76 (five short passages) and say whether C1's habitat claim is a fair reading.
2. Take one biosignature you know well (methane isotopes or amino-acid chirality) and say whether the biological source is separable from the abiotic baseline with current instruments. That is C2.
3. Name one independent, low-overlap observation that would change the attribution. If no such observation exists, say so; that is a result, not a failure of the question.
4. Say what would count as a failure of C3. A null result should come with a swept scope and a bound, not a verdict of absence.
5. Send the refutation. A failure is more useful than an endorsement.
