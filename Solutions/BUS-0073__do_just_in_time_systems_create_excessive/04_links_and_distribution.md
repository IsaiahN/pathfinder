## Wider literature that bears on this question, and how far it goes

1. **Operations and lean (one evidence pool, not several witnesses).** The lean and supply-chain-resilience Wikipedia entries describe the same practical literature: just-in-time reduces inventory cost and waste, and the same concentration that makes it efficient removes alternatives when a disruption arrives. The lean entry's "Supplier concentration" section and the resilience entry's "Balancing efficiency and resilience" section are one pool stating one tension, so they count as one witness.
2. **Ecology (low overlap).** Walker et al. 2004 gives the canonical ecological definition of resilience and the four aspects latitude, resistance, precariousness and panarchy. Its remark that recently developed systems with short co-evolutionary histories cross thresholds more readily is a generic mechanism, not a measurement of any supply chain. It corroborates Fig 11's cross-scale cascade and the need to enumerate beyond direct contact, but the transfer is a shape transfer, and Fig 9 :30 warns that *"your words are borrowed too, so describe the effect rather than the cause."*
3. **Safety science and resilience engineering (low overlap).** The resilience engineering material distinguishes resilience from robustness: robustness is the ability to deal with challenges anticipated in advance, while resilience concerns fundamental surprise and graceful extensibility. This corroborates the figure claim that the channel can decay and that a static "managed well" label is a solved-state error. It is a different field with a different evidence pool, so it is the strongest independent witness here, though still not a supply-chain measurement.
4. **Network and infrastructure science (low overlap).** Cascading failure and interdependent-network work shows that systemic risk lives in the interconnections, so a focal firm's residual can be several tiers out. This corroborates Fig 11 :52 at the level of mechanism, not at the level of any particular supplier graph.

No world source was found that conflicts with the figure verdict. The conflict rule was therefore not triggered. Agreement from the lean literature and the resilience literature together is still one broad operations pool plus low-overlap ecology, safety science and network science; those are distinct pools, so their agreement is weak corroboration of the mechanism shape, not a measurement of "excessive vulnerability".

## Distribution: offer, do not push

Figure-grounded reasons:

- **Fig 10 :52** : *"Bring a characterised residual, never the source."* Sending a full framework before anyone has described a disruption they care about sends a source, not a residual. Lead with the shape claim and its five refuters.
- **Fig 10 :42** : *"A verifier that can reconstruct the claim cannot verify it."* A recipient who has absorbed the frame before being asked for a verdict has joined the frame being checked.
- **Fig 9 :27-28** : *"describe it before you go looking, or any frame you pick will seem to fit."* Ask what they would expect to see if the shape claim were true before giving them the links.
- **Fig 2 :40** : *"agreement about the world is worthless when the agreeing frames share an evidence pool."* A note sent only to people already committed to lean or already committed to resilience-first thinking manufactures the disqualifying correlation.
- **Fig 4 :22** : *"Only methods cross up. Only head starts come down."* Offering the formula as a method to reuse is permitted; offering a conclusion to be endorsed is not.

**Recommended order in a reply:** (1) the shape claim and its pre-registered refuter; (2) ask what they would count as a failure and what disruption they would test it against; (3) record their verdict; (4) only then the three source links, with the note that the figures, not this summary, are the authority; (5) only if asked, the formula with the instruction to write a fresh refuter and state a mode.

- **Stage 1 before stage 2** is Fig 10 :52 and Fig 2 :45 : *"Frames range against the ground. They do not negotiate with it."* The recipient checks the output without holding the framework's interior.
