**Restated as an effect.** For a focal firm's supply slot s, let R = |Γ(b,a) - o′| where the prediction is that an input arrives just in time and the observation is what actually arrived. "Excessive vulnerability" is the claim that R grows large and stays large under disruption. "Resilient when managed well" is the claim that R falls back toward zero and does not propagate. An answer would look like: a shape for the conditions under which R returns to zero, plus the refuter that would break that shape. The sought-for shape is bound as an ordinary term in `01` before the derivation runs, per the formula's Direction rule.

**Boundary and honest standing.** This is a simulated derivation checked for internal consistency against the figures, not a ground reading. It cannot settle the operations question it reports on. Per *The Recursive Self-Improvement Trap*, LLM output is *"simulacra"* and *"the truly testable output would need to be derived from the real architecture or working the formula yourself."* No disruption experiment, supplier dataset, or firm record was consulted as an anchor. ## What is deliberately not here

- No personal emails of named individuals.
- No ranking of people by how likely they are to agree that JIT is vulnerable or resilient.
- No bulk-send plan.

Three reasons, in the figures' own words:

1. **Agreement is not an anchor.** Fig 2 :35 : *"Alignment is triangulation, not negotiation. A mirror cannot correct you."* Fig 2 :30 : *"If it moves when you push on it, it is not an anchor."* "Managed well" moves when you push on it, so it cannot settle the question.
2. **Selecting for receptiveness manufactures the disqualifying correlation.** Fig 2 :40 : *"agreement about the world is worthless when the agreeing frames share an evidence pool."* The lean-operations literature and most of this folder's business sources are one pool.
3. **Sending the full frame before asking for a verdict spends independence.** Fig 10 :42 : *"A verifier that can reconstruct the claim cannot verify it."* Fig 10 :52 : *"Bring a characterised residual, never the source."*

Legally: scraping personal emails for unsolicited bulk outreach breaches GDPR and CAN-SPAM style rules in many jurisdictions. Public opt-in venues avoid that failure as well as the structural one.
