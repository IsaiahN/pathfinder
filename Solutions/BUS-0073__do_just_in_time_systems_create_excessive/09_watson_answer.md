## QUESTION

Decide, in effect terms, whether just-in-time systems create excessive vulnerability or can be resilient when suppliers and logistics are managed well. Restated: for supply slot s, R = |Γ(b,a) - o'|, where the prediction is that an input arrives just in time and the observation is what actually arrived. Option A holds the habitat and channel fixed and varies the schedule, then calls the result "vulnerable". Option B varies the habitat and channel and calls the result "managed well". What each leads to: A treats a frame-relative predicate as a property of the rule alone and is answered at a firm average that can hide a live slot; B treats management as a terminal state and ignores that the seat can introduce alternatives but cannot subtract contact. The figure-derived answer refuses the partition, keeps the two conditions separate, and marks the selection among specific levers as `gap`.

## VERDICT (from the figures)

The figure-derived verdict: the question's "or" is a false partition. Resilience is a property of the JIT-and-habitat-and-channel compound, indexed per slot, not of the schedule in isolation and not of management as a terminal state. The habitat must be enumerated past direct suppliers because the actor that matters can be three tiers out and valence is relative to the residual. A firm average can hide a live slot because the channel, not the ground, decays. Management introduces alternatives but cannot subtract contact, so "managed well" is permanent work. Which specific lever wins is a declared `gap`. Verdicts are kept separate per domain and never averaged. Mode `specified`; the kernel claims are `general`; nothing is `grounded`. The cost-residual success of lean from the business source was re-mapped rather than allowed to flip the verdict.

- **cost if wrong:** bounded and locatable. A firm failing the channel condition would be sold habitat instruments, or a firm failing the habitat condition would be told to improve metrics, and a single lever would be applied to a union that needs two (Fig 9 :55-57). The larger cost is spending an outside reader's independence by sending a conclusion instead of a residual; Fig 2 :42 prices that: "Buy independence in proportion to the cost of being wrong." Both are avoidable by leading with the shape claim and its refuter.
- **undoable:** yes. No term is deleted if the claim fails; a refutation is added and the reach falls because something was added, per Fig 6 :42-43 "A refutation adds a term that refuses; it removes nothing, and the reach falls because something was added rather than because anything was deleted." The lineage is recorded so a later reader can locate the error (Fig 10 provenance rule).
- **reversed by:** a ground reading, not agreement. A controlled comparison varying habitat with schedule fixed and channel with habitat fixed; a per-slot continuity counter that fires; a tier-3 trace that finds no live residual where the claim expects one; a demonstration that "managed well" survives seat inactivity across habitat changes; a source-discriminating instrument that selects the winning lever; or any of the six pre-registered refuters above. Until one fires, the verdict stands as `candidate`. The round-trip gap R_T is nonzero: the coarse shorthand "JIT is resilient if managed well" drops per-slot indexing, the three-step cascade, channel decay and the gap status. Fig 4 :30 "So take the round trip and measure the gap. Without it you are navigating by dead reckoning: confident about a position nobody checked."
