## QUESTION

Decide, in effect terms, what the Tether says about enzyme free linking of RNA nucleotides into long strands. What supplies the free energy to form a phosphodiester bond in water, the orientation of two reacting groups, a catalytic role that persists across uses, and net elongation against hydrolysis, when no protein enzyme is present? Three options and what each leads to. Option A, ingredients only: leave nucleotides in water longer; this leads to hydrolysis dominating condensation because the form of the bond is not in the form of the ingredients, and it reports an omission as an answer. Option B, an imported closure: consult frames whose closures differ from nucleotide chemistry, which leads to activation, orientation, persistence and templating as separate conditions whose union closes the residual; this yields a checkable shape without naming a specific chemical. Option C, the framework names a mechanism: present montmorillonite or imidazole activation as a derived result; this leads to a binding from the world frame dressed as a derivation, which the figures forbid and which spends the independence of the people checking it. Option B is the figure licensed route.

## VERDICT (from the figures)

The shape survives; no chemical route is licensed. A strand is the degenerate chain case of a molecule, so the bond is the information the ingredients omit. Any enzyme free route must supply activation that pays for the bond, a part that survives its own use to fill the catalyst role, an orienting functional group, and a union of closures; its product should be a mixture of 2',5' and 3',5' linkages, and its elongation should be recursion over a settled oligomer. Montmorillonite, imidazole activation, wet dry cycling and helper oligos remain bindings from the chemistry frame with provenance, unproven as answers. The status is specified, ungrounded, candidate. The verdict is open, not unreachable, because instruments already return oligomers and an identified intermediate. The specific chemical name is a gap, reported rather than filled. The strongest outreach point is that the figures return the shape any answer must have, with five refuters written in advance, and stop where instruments cannot distinguish.

cost if wrong: bounded and locatable. The packet carries mode, per-domain verdicts, verbatim lines and refuters before the conclusion, so an error is findable rather than invisible. The real cost is spending the independence of outside readers by sending a recording instead of a generator, or by naming a mineral as if the framework had derived it. That cost is avoidable by leading with the shape and the refuter.

undoable: yes. The defective form of the claim, in which a chemical occupant is presented as a derived answer, is removable without touching the derivation; `07_the_tether_answer.md` and `08_expert_verification_packet.md` already remove it. A sent claim can be superseded by the corrected packet, and no primitive has to be withdrawn.

reversed by: a ground reading, not agreement. A measurement that gives a single linkage type, long strands from unactivated nucleotides with no catalyst or energy cycle, a route with no persistent part, or random collision matching surface assisted yield and regiochemistry would each break the corresponding shape claim. A re-census showing the six term substrate governs chemical monomers would send the derivation to a different governing figure. A discriminating experiment that identifies which route ran would move the verdict from candidate to grounded. Prestige or consensus is not such an instrument.
