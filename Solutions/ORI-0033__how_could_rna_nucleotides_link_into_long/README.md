## What is being decided, in effect terms

Modern cells link nucleotides into RNA with polymerases, which are enzymes, using nucleotide triphosphates whose phosphate activation pays for the bond. The residual is the gap between (a) free mononucleotides in a prebiotic environment and (b) a long phosphodiester linked strand, when the enzyme is absent. Three options and what each leads to:

- **Option A, ingredients only.** Nucleotides in water, left longer. This leads to hydrolysis dominating condensation, because the form of the bond is not present in the form of the ingredients. Fig 12 :5, *"A set of ingredients is not one. The bond is the rest of the information, and it is the half nothing was recording."*
- **Option B, an imported closure.** A frame whose closure differs from nucleotide chemistry supplies what that chemistry lacks: activation free energy, a surface that orients, a catalyst that survives its own use, and eventually a template. This leads to oligomers, then to strands long enough to fold. Fig 8 :45, *"Two frames together reach what neither reaches alone."*
- **Option C, the framework names one mechanism.** This leads to a binding from the world frame presented as a derivation, which the figures forbid. Fig 9 :32, *"you get a kind of thing, never the answer itself."*

An answer would look like a shape: a constraint any viable mechanism must satisfy, stated before a mechanism is named. That shape is bound as an ordinary term in `01_figure_grounded_audit.md` and carried through `07_the_tether_answer.md`.
