# Expert verification packet: the Tether on nonenzymatic RNA polymerization

**What this is:** a claim in a form a prebiotic chemist or origins of life researcher can check. Mode, derivation and refuters are stated before the conclusion, so a reader knows what to look for and what would settle it. Send this only after the shape claim has survived the three sub-claims below.

**Mode: `specified` on the shape claims, `general` on the structural facts, not `grounded`.** This is a shape and a route, not a result. It is a simulated derivation checked for internal consistency against the figures, not a ground reading, and it cannot settle the claim it reports on.

## 1. The claim, in one paragraph

A strand is the degenerate chain case of a molecule, one bond type with no branching, so the bond is the information that an inventory of nucleotides omits. From that, any route to long phosphodiester strands without enzymes must supply four conditions: activation that pays for the bond in water, a part that survives its own use to fill the catalyst role, an orienting functional group that decides which linkage forms, and a union of closures, because a nucleotide only frame cannot compose what it does not hold. The product should not be one substance but a distribution of 2',5' and 3',5' linkages, same atoms under different bonds. Elongation should be recursion over an oligomer that has already settled against the ground. The framework does not name the mineral, leaving group, surface or energy cycle; it returns the shape any candidate answer must have, with refuters written in advance.

## 2. Three checkable sub-claims

### C1, framework-internal: a strand is a chain, and the linkage is a substance

- **Statement:** a strand introduces no new kind of object; a chain is one bond type, no branching. Because the phosphodiester bond is ordered, the 2',5' and 3',5' products are different substances with one ingredient set, so the product of a nonenzymatic route is an isomer distribution rather than a single name.
- **Source, verbatim:**
  - Fig 12 :48: "A molecule is a bonded arrangement. A chain is the degenerate case: one bond type, no branching. Nothing about a chain is a different kind of object."
  - Fig 12 :5: "A set of ingredients is not one. The bond is the rest of the information, and it is the half nothing was recording."
  - Fig 12 :117: "isomer. Same atoms, different bond, different substance. The difference is invisible in the formula and decisive in the behaviour."
  - Fig 13 :124: "the same ingredients under a different bond are a different substance."
- **What to check:** is the degenerate-chain reading faithful to the figure? Are 2',5' and 3',5' phosphodiesters isomers under that definition, and is the behavioural difference observable in duplex structure or copying fidelity?
- **Refuter:** a prebiotic route yielding a single phosphodiester linkage type under plausible conditions, or a demonstration that the two linkages are merely two spellings of one term under the governing bond. Either breaks the isomer shape and leaves the rest of the derivation standing.

### C2, chemistry-facing: the catalyst role and the orienting group are load bearing

- **Statement:** removing enzymes removes one occupant of the catalyst role, not the role, because the figure defines a catalyst by persistence across uses. The reacting hydroxyl and the activated phosphate form a functional group whose orientation decides yield and regiochemistry; the figure calls such a link the middle link that needs an instrument.
- **Source, verbatim:**
  - Fig 12 :119: "catalyst. A part that survives its own use. Spent and regenerating parts behave differently across uses, and nothing records which a term is."
  - Fig 12 :123: "functional group. A substructure whose presence fixes the outcome. The group says what kind; the qualifier says which member."
  - Fig 3 :27: "A link with no instrument is not a link that works. It is a link nobody has looked at."
- **World evidence, opened and recorded in `10_source_ledger.csv`:** Kaddour and Sahai 2014 report that "At least three different catalysts have been found to enhance the non-enzymatic polymerization of RNA, namely montmorillonite surfaces, lipid surfaces and, more recently, a peptide." Ferris 2006 writes that "The formation of biopolymers from monomers in aqueous solution required the presence of catalysts." Zhang et al. 2017 describe the primer hydroxyl "oriented to attack the 5ʹ-phosphate of the adjacent G residue", an instrument-level reading of orientation.
- **What to check:** is persistence across uses the right abstraction of what a prebiotic catalyst does? Does the chemistry distinguish orientation from mere concentration, for example by comparing yields and regiochemistry with and without a surface or a preorganised intermediate?
- **Refuter:** a route in which nothing survives its own use and no catalytic role is played, or a result in which random collision of activated groups gives the same yield and regiochemistry as surface or template assisted alignment. The first breaks the catalyst shape, the second breaks the orientation claim.

### C3, framework-internal derivation: the mechanism is a union of closures and the framework returns a shape

- **Statement:** activation, orientation, persistence and templating are separate closures whose intersection has a surplus none has alone. A nucleotide only frame cannot compose a bond it does not hold, so searching harder inside it cannot close the residual. What the framework returns is the shape of the missing thing, not its chemical name.
- **Source, verbatim:**
  - Fig 8 :45: "Two frames together reach what neither reaches alone."
  - Fig 6 :23: "No frame escapes its own closure. The bound moves only on contact with a frame whose closure differs."
  - Fig 8 :32: "Closure is idempotent. Composing inside a closed set never leaves it, so no search, however long, adds an atom."
  - Fig 9 :32: "you get a kind of thing, never the answer itself."
  - Fig 8 :39: "So a search that finds nothing is never a proof of absence."
- **What to check:** are the four conditions genuinely different closures rather than four names for one reaction? Does the literature support importing frames (activation chemistry, surfaces, energy cycles, templates) rather than extending nucleotide chemistry alone?
- **Refuter:** long phosphodiester strands forming reliably from unactivated nucleotides in water with no catalyst, surface or energy cycle under plausible conditions. That closes the residual inside the nucleotide frame and removes the import requirement. A secondary refuter is a re-census showing that Fig 13's six term substrate governs chemical monomers rather than only the physical primitives, because that would send the derivation back to the wrong governing figure.

- **Not a named prebiotic route.** Montmorillonite, 2-aminoimidazole activation, wet dry cycling, eutectic ice, cyclic nucleotides and helper oligos are bindings from the chemistry frame, recorded with provenance, not outputs of a figure.

## 5. What would settle it

A discriminating measurement, not more agreement. The ground is an experiment that reports chain length, linkage distribution, persistence of the catalyst across uses and copying fidelity under plausible prebiotic conditions. Specifically: does a route yield one linkage or a mixture; does removing the candidate surface or catalyst remove the product; does the same yield arise from random collision; and can an oligomer that has formed serve as an operand for a second round? A result that fires any refuter in section 2 is more useful than an endorsement.

## 6. What an expert can check in twenty minutes

1. Read four short passages: Fig 12 :48 and :117, Fig 12 :119, Fig 6 :23 and Fig 8 :45. They are the whole framework-internal load.
2. Check C1 against what you know about 2',5' versus 3',5' phosphodiesters. Are they isomers under the figure's definition, and does the difference show up in behaviour?
3. Check C2 against one result you know well: does the catalyst survive its own use, and is there evidence that orientation rather than concentration changed the outcome?
4. Name one instrument that would distinguish the candidate routes by linkage distribution or persistence across uses, or say why none is available.
5. Write down what would refute the shape claim in your own words before offering a verdict.
6. Send the refutation if you have one. A failure is more useful than an endorsement, and a reply that arrives after you have adopted the framework is a conversation, not corroboration.
