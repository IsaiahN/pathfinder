## 1. A shape is a constraint, not a filler

The canonical rule is in the figures README: the framework returns *"what a missing thing would have to look like (a shape, not a name)"* and stops at the limit of what instruments can distinguish, recording the lineage. It adds *"Indiscernibility is not identity."* Fig 9 :32: *"you get a kind of thing, never the answer itself."*

From *The Library of Babel*: *"This means that you can cut your search short if the set of books returned is small and descriptive enough that it provides a general shape of the answer that satisfies your question."*

So a **shape** is the narrowed form of an answer you can state before you can name it. It constrains; it does not fill. For ORI-0033 the shape is *activation plus a persistent catalyst plus an orienting group plus a union of closures, yielding an isomer distribution of chains*. The name would be *montmorillonite catalysed oligomerisation of 2 aminoimidazole activated nucleotides*. The first is derived. The second is a binding from the chemistry frame.

## 2. How a shape is obtained

- *Library of Babel*: use the vocabulary already held that is closest to the thing, then look for recurring new terms that overlap or correlate with it. *"With each new term gained, you are able to narrow the space by definition and deepen your vocabulary. It's this recursive process that will help you converge on the answer."*
- *Library of Babel* on anchoring: *"This anchor is provided by your own question: If the question is answered by your present collection of books from your search you know whether to stop or continue and which direction to turn next."* This is Fig 9's *"describe it before you go looking, or any frame you pick will seem to fit"* in essay form.
- *Library of Babel* on stopping: *"You will know that you are done because the finish line will also be the starting place, as there is nowhere else to search and nothing else that can describe the answer better."* And the paradox: *"you have already composed the answer through the process of locating it."*
- *How Intelligence Actually Works* on the recursive carve: the replicator thought experiment shows that each pass *"adds content, not just constraint"*, and the process stops when *"the recursion converges toward indiscernibility, and indiscernibility is not identity."*

Applied here: the residual was described in effect terms first (bond, orientation, persistence, elongation), then figure lines were quoted, and only then were chemistry frames consulted. If the frames had been named first, any of them would have seemed to fit.

## 3. What licenses a name

Only two things, both outside the shape itself:

1. **The ground settles it.** `THE_FORMULA.md` step 5: *"the ground settles it. Verified only by what pays. A gate is not the ground."* Here that means a measurement of chain length, linkage distribution and copying fidelity under plausible conditions, not an argument.
2. **A provenanced import from a frame whose closure differs.** Fig 6 :23: *"No frame escapes its own closure. The bound moves only on contact with a frame whose closure differs."* Fig 8 :50 to 52: *"Convergent derivation and adopted import look identical in the contents. Only the record of where each came from separates them."*

*How Intelligence Actually Works* gives the sharper version with the union horizon: a case is *"below the union horizon"* when no observer with any instrument they can build can discriminate its origin, and that is *"never a settled verdict, only a report that nobody has reached it yet."* So the four candidate mechanisms are not ranked by plausibility. They are held as candidates until an instrument discriminates.

## 4. Why naming a mechanism from one reading is the specific forbidden error

*The Hard Problem is About Source* gives the mechanism. Metamerism: an instrument reads green and *"it cannot comment on the source."* The source could be one green light, or yellow plus blue, or RGB, or CMYK, or a scheme not yet discovered. The essay states: *"If several paths create the same behavior, then behavior is a projection with lossy information (it cannot define consciousness in full)."* And: *"An instrument that can't distinguish these cases demonstrates its own inadequacy."* It was the spectrometer, not an argument, that resolved it: *"It was proven wrong not by argument, but by the arrival of a better instrument."*

For ORI-0033 the reading is *a long strand exists in the experiment*. Several paths can produce that reading: clay catalysis, lipid assisted dehydration, cyclic nucleotide condensation, peptide catalysis, template directed extension. The reading cannot comment on the source. Naming montmorillonite from the reading is the metamerism error. What is owed is a discriminating instrument, not a name.

This is also why the isomer finding matters. The product is not one reading but a distribution over linkages, and the distribution is part of what an instrument must resolve.

## 5. Shape and scale

Fig 4 :19 to 20: *"The kernel is the part that does not change with size: the same loop and the same rule for naming what worked, whether one agent is running it or a thousand. Only the actors, the currency and the anchor change."* The bond forming kernel is the same for a dimer and a 50 mer. What changes across scale is the operand: monomer, oligomer, settled strand, template. Fig 12 :104: *"A settled molecule becomes an atom for whatever composes over it."*

So the shape is scale invariant in its rules and scale specific in its cast. A shape claim at the monomer scale does not by itself settle the polymer scale; that is a different loop with a different anchor.

## 6. Why this does not license a final theory

*How Intelligence Actually Works*: *"Because it is a metatheory, it cannot be validated, nor can it validate itself."* *The Recursive Self Improvement Trap*: *"the output is only as good as the validator. The agent, or the tool, or the frame, or the theory, cannot validate itself. An external validator, or ground, is always needed."* And: *"Metatheories cannot validate themselves."*

There is no last room here. Fig 7 :6 names *"the limit: no last room"*. A claim that this shape is the final answer to prebiotic polymerisation would be a terminal room, which the figures rule out.

## 7. Prestige is not action, and my own audit is a simulation

*The Recursive Self Improvement Trap*: *"Prestige must never be allowed to validate an action directly."* And: *"Prestige can fund an action. It can give an action a budget to be tried. But it cannot settle an action. Only the ground can settle it. The Ground Maintainer must be blind to prestige."* A list of famous laboratories who agree with the shape is the named collapse, not corroboration.

The same essay states: *"LLMs can quickly simulate an approximate answer/output with the formula found in the architecture post."* But *"the truly testable output would need to be derived from the real architecture or working the formula yourself."* So `01_figure_grounded_audit.md` is a simulated derivation checked for internal consistency against the figures. It is **not** a test of the claim. It cannot settle anything it reports on.

## 8. Consequence for this question

The defensible result is the shape, stated as constraints and refuters. The specific chemistry stays in the provenance column. When an instrument discriminates among the candidate routes, a name becomes licensed. Until then, the honest output is: here is what any enzyme free route must look like, here is what would break it, and here is what would settle it.
