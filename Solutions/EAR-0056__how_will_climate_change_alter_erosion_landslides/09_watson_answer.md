## VERDICT (from the figures)

The shape survives, the number does not. The question is a union compound of overlapping conditions; residuals are per slot; climate acts through conditions in a cascade with relative valence; flows are directed and thresholded with a compounding remainder; feedback cycles must have their sign measured; the ground, not agreement, settles each slot; and the monitoring channel must be checked. Sign and magnitude for a specified slope are an honest `gap`, owed to INWARD instrument work (SEARCHED was run first and found no magnitude line; OUTWARD is conditional and must be described in effect terms). This is a `specified`, ungrounded result.

- **cost if wrong:** bounded and locatable. The packet states mode, binds three checkable sub-claims and writes refuters before the conclusion, so an error is findable rather than invisible. The real avoidable cost is spending the independence of outside readers by sending a single global headline that averages away live slot residuals (Fig 1 :22) or by selecting recipients for agreement (Fig 2 :40). Fig 2 :42 says *"Buy independence in proportion to the cost of being wrong."*
- **undoable:** yes. The slot census can be refined, the union can be rebundled if the partition refuter fires, and the channel check can be added, without withdrawing any primitive. A sent claim can be superseded by a corrected packet.
- **reversed by:** a ground reading, not agreement. Paired catchments, pore-pressure loggers, repeat topography and sediment budgets that discriminate climate forcing from fire, land use and sediment exhaustion would close the `gap`; a measured homogeneous, threshold-free, linear slot response, or a clean partition of the three processes, or a monitoring record shown immune to degradation, would break the shape claim; a figure re-census that yields a magnitude without instrument or import would also reverse it.
