# Expert verification packet: How will climate change alter erosion, landslides, and sediment flows?

**What this is:** a claim in a form a geomorphologist or climate scientist can check. Mode, derivation and refuters are stated before the conclusion, so a reader knows what to look for and what would settle it.

**Mode: `specified` on the shape, `general` on the frame, not `grounded`.** No measurement at a specific slope is claimed. This is a shape and a route, not a result.

## 1. The claim, in one paragraph

Erosion, landslides and sediment flows form a union of overlapping conditions, not three independent trends. The residual must be indexed by slot (a slope, a reach, a catchment), because a global average can hide a live threshold crossing. Climate acts as conditions in a contact cascade, with second-order effects through vegetation, sediment storage and antecedent state; the figures do not sign those conditions. Sediment flows are directed energy-and-gradient compositions with thresholded failure and a compounding remainder, and the output re-enters as input, so the response is a cycle whose sign must be measured. The ground, not model agreement, settles each slot, and the monitoring channel must itself be checked for decay. Sign and magnitude for a given slope remain a declared `gap`, owed to instruments.

## 2. Three checkable sub-claims

### C1 - framework-internal; checkable by reading Figures 1, 9 and 11

- **Statement:** the question is a union compound requiring unbundling, and the residual is per slot, not per global mean.
- **Source, verbatim:** Fig 9 :55 - *"A bundled problem is usually a union of overlapping conditions, not a list of separate ones."* Fig 9 :57 - *"a case in two conditions is not a harder case of either"*. Fig 1 :19 - *"The residual is indexed by object slot, not aggregated."* Fig 1 :22 - *"Aggregating across slots is how a live signal disappears into an average."* Fig 11 :46 - *"conditions: light, water, temperature, season, terrain"*.
- **What to check:** do the three processes in fact share actors such that a single lever clears the single-condition regions but leaves the intersections? Are there monitored slots with opposite or null trends under the same regional forcing?
- **Refuter:** show a clean partition (each process expressible without the others' actors, whole response an arithmetic sum), or show homogeneous, threshold-free slot response such that the global mean preserves every live residual.

### C2 - geomorphology-facing; checkable against field data

- **Statement:** failure is a threshold comparison and response is a cascade with feedback, so nonlinearity and antecedent state are structural, not noise.
- **Source, verbatim:** Operators table :49 - *"a threshold relation between two quantities"*. Fig 13 :76 - *"Energy and a gradient compose a flow."* Fig 13 :31-32 - *"a remainder compounding through a chain that amplifies is chaos, and the detail a crossing discards reappears above it as noise"*. Fig 12 :121 - *"cycle. The output re-enters as input. The sign of the loop decides whether it closes, and a chain cannot close by construction."*
- **What to check:** USGS reports rainfall pore-pressure triggering and quantitative post-fire debris-flow thresholds. Sheikh et al. (2026) find that *"geomorphic effectiveness emerges from the interaction between hydraulic forcing, sediment availability and evolving channel conditions, and depends on the temporal scale of assessment."* Does the expert's own data show threshold behaviour, legacy effects and a sign for the sediment-cover feedback?
- **Refuter:** a measured response that is linear, reversible and threshold-free at the relevant scale, with no hysteresis, no amplification and no re-entry of output as input.

### C3 - verification-facing; checkable against monitoring practice

- **Statement:** the ground, not agreement, settles each slot; monitoring channels decay; sign and magnitude for a given slope are a `gap` until an instrument resolves the source.
- **Source, verbatim:** Fig 2 :29 - *"An anchor is whatever settles the question and does not change its answer because of what any frame thinks."* Fig 2 :35 - *"Alignment is triangulation, not negotiation. A mirror cannot correct you."* Fig 10 :24 - *"the ground does not decay, the channel does"*. Fig 9 :32 - *"you get a kind of thing, never the answer itself"*. Fig 13 :134 - *"Unreachable is a verdict here, not a silence"*.
- **What to check:** can the claimed trend survive a change of gauge, inventory or satellite product? Can a single sediment-yield reading distinguish climate forcing from fire, land use and sediment exhaustion?
- **Refuter:** an anchor whose answer changes when frames change, or a monitoring record whose degradation cannot in principle alter the reading, or a figure line that yields the sign and magnitude without an instrument.

## 3. What is explicitly not claimed

- Not a direction or magnitude: no claim that climate change increases or decreases erosion, landslides or sediment flux in general.
- Not a global number, date or regional ranking.
- Not that a model ensemble's agreement is evidence; shared evidence pools disqualify it (Fig 2 :40).
- Not that the six-term substrate or habitat list is neutral or nature's own list.
- Not that fire, drought or vegetation are uniformly harmful or helpful; valence is relative to the residual (Fig 11 :49).
- Not grounded. Nothing here is asserted as settled by measurement.

## 5. What would settle it

A source-discriminating instrument, not a further argument. *The Hard Problem is About Source* gives the metamerism case: a reading of green *"cannot comment on the source"*, resolved by the spectrometer. Here:

1. Paired catchments, pore-pressure loggers, repeat topography and sediment budgets that discriminate climate forcing from fire, land use and sediment exhaustion.
2. Threshold test: does failure occur at a reproducible driving-versus-resisting comparison, and does the threshold move?
3. Cycle-sign test: armour (damping) or tools (amplifying)?
4. Channel check: does the trend survive replacement of the observing system?
