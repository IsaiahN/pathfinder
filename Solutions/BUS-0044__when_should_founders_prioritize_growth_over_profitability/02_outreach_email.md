# Outreach draft - validate the output first, then decide about the advice

**Use:** one to one, or as a cover letter attached to a submission through the public venues in `03_public_contact_points.csv`. **Not** for bulk send.

**Hard precondition:** do not send until the stage-1 output below has been checked by the recipient. The two-stage offer collapses on contact if the shape claim cannot survive a twenty-minute refutation attempt.

---

**Subject:** Check one diagnostic claim before you decide whether the growth/profit framing is worth reading

> Hi [name],
>
> I have one specific, falsifiable output about the growth-versus-profitability decision and I would like you to check it against what you already know. I am not asking you to read the theory first, and I am not asking you to agree with me. If the output does not survive, there is nothing else to look at.
>
> **The output (`specified`, not `grounded`):** a startup's library predicts what customers will do; the market returns what they actually do. The gap between the two must be read per cohort, not as one blended growth rate. From that it follows that "should we grow or preserve cash?" is not one question. It is two conditions that need two instruments:
>
> 1. **C1, the bracket condition:** is the link from growth spend to retained, paying customers sound and settling against the ground?
> 2. **C2, the channel condition:** is the channel to a paying ground still reachable, meaning the company is not on a trajectory where cash ends before the model works?
>
> The claim is that growth is the right action when both hold, and that preservation is the right action when either fails. Which numeric threshold belongs in each condition is not answered here. The framework returns a shape, not a name, and a universal ratio would need an instrument this derivation does not have.
>
> **What I am not claiming:** that growth is always good, that profitability is always safe, that a valuation or a benchmark ratio settles anything, or that "default alive" is a complete answer. Those are bindings from the venture-finance frame. Agreement with them would be one evidence pool talking, not corroboration.
>
> **What would refute the shape claim:**
> 1. one intervention that restores both conditions in both failure types and leaves no categorically different residue;
> 2. an averaged growth-rate threshold that predicts prospectively and per named cohort which cohort creates value, with no cohort-level measurement;
> 3. a company metric that moves neither with spend nor with narrative yet settles the value question by itself;
> 4. growth spend that settles no new paying customer relation while the ground keeps paying across a market change;
> 5. a startup that loses its ability to fund growth while bracket fidelity and ground pressure both measure high and are not declining.
>
> I wrote these down before sending this. If any one holds, the shape is wrong and I will retract it. A one-line "this fails because..." is more useful to me than agreement. If you would rather not engage, no reply is needed.
>
> **If it survives, and only then:** the sources are below. The figures are the authority, not my summary of them.
> - Figures and formula for direct download (branch `general-agent`): https://github.com/IsaiahN/tether/tree/general-agent/figures
> - Canonical usage rule (figures README): https://github.com/IsaiahN/tether/blob/general-agent/figures/README.md
> - Arrival of the Fittest: The Tether Architecture: https://medium.com/@IsaiahNwukor/arrival-of-the-fittest-the-tether-architecture-1de7edf641ec
>
> **If you want to use the machinery:** the formula is in the figures link. Run it on a decision you know well and can check against a ground. Write down what would refute your result *before* you run it and state the mode of what you get. A reported failure is more useful to me than a reported success.
>
> - [your name]

## Why this version is ordered this way

| the move | figure-grounded reason |
|---|---|
| Output first, theory second | Fig 10 :52 - *"Bring a characterised residual, never the source."* Fig 2 :45 - *"Frames range against the ground. They do not negotiate with it."* |
| Do not ask for agreement | Fig 2 :35 - *"Alignment is triangulation, not negotiation. A mirror cannot correct you."* |
| Pre-registered refuter before the verdict | Seat Map rule: *"a falsifier written before the run is a design instrument, not only an honesty device."* |
| Mode stated on the output | `THE_FORMULA.md` - *"Mode. general · specified · grounded. State which."* |
| Union kept open instead of a single lever | Fig 9 :57 - *"a case in two conditions is not a harder case of either."* |
| Per-cohort indexing instead of a blended rate | Fig 1 :19 - *"The residual is indexed by object slot, not aggregated."* |
| Benchmarks withheld | Fig 9 :60 - *"Use filters for the budget and witnesses for the verdict. Never let a filter hand you a verdict."* |
| No request for trust or prestige | *The Recursive Self-Improvement Trap*: *"Prestige can fund an action. It can give an action a budget to be tried. But it cannot settle an action. Only the ground can settle it."* The ask gives the claim a budget to be tried, it does not ask anyone to endorse it. |
| Offer the formula as a method to reuse | Fig 4 :22 - *"Only methods cross up. Only head starts come down."* |
| Ask users to state their own ground and refuter | `THE_FORMULA.md` "Direction": a frame cannot check the direction of its own question, because the shape it seeks is not a term it holds. |
| No claim to settle the founder's decision | *The Recursive Self-Improvement Trap*: *"the output is only as good as the validator. The agent, or the tool, or the frame, or the theory, cannot validate itself."* |

## One-to-one delivery rule

Send to one person at a time through a public or opt-in channel. Do not use a distribution list, do not CC, and do not follow up with the theory unless the recipient has first recorded a verdict on the stage-1 shape. Their later agreement is never pooled as corroboration (Fig 2 :40: *"agreement about the world is worthless when the agreeing frames share an evidence pool"*).
