## 1. A shape is a constraint, not a filler

The figures README states the limit: the framework returns *"what a missing thing would have to look like (a shape, not a name)."* Fig 9 :32: *"you get a kind of thing, never the answer itself."* Fig 13 :137-138: *"It can say the answer is out of reach with the library as it stands"*; Fig 13 :135: *"It cannot say which primitive is missing, because naming it would mean already holding it."*

From *The Library of Babel*: *"you can cut your search short if the set of books returned is small and descriptive enough that it provides a general shape of the answer that satisfies your question."*

So a **shape** is the narrowed form of an answer you can state before you can name it. It constrains; it does not fill. For BUS-0044 the shape is a conjunction of two conditions with an instrument for each, not a universal ratio such as "LTV/CAC greater than 3" or "Rule of 40 above 40." Those are names borrowed from the venture-finance frame. The shape says: bracket sound, channel reachable, measured per cohort.

## 2. How a shape is obtained

- *Library of Babel*: use the vocabulary you already have that is closest to the thing, look for recurring new terms that overlap or correlate with it, recurse. *"With each new term gained, you narrow the space and deepen your vocabulary."*
- Fig 6 :30: *"The description travels. Fails by being sharpened into terms only we can read. Cast it in effect terms."* Fig 9 :30-31: *"your words are borrowed too, so describe the effect rather than the cause."*
- *Decoding Tacit Knowledge*: *"When contact isn't possible, the best you can send is a plain description of what you'd expect to see, because anyone can check that against the world."*
- *Library of Babel* on stopping: the answer is reached when *"the finish line will also be the starting place"* - and the paradox is that by then you have *"composed the answer through the process of locating it."*

Applied here, the working vocabulary began as "growth," "profit," "runway." The figure census narrowed it to "residual per cohort," "anchor," "bracket," "channel," "import," "valence." Each new term reduced the space. What remains is a shape: two conditions and a gap, not a decision rule that can be copied into a board deck.

## 3. What licenses a name

Only two things, both outside the shape itself:

1. **The ground settles it.** Formula step 5: *"the ground settles it. Verified only by what pays."* The README: the framework *"stops at the limit of what the available instruments can distinguish, and records the lineage so the stop is honest."*
2. **A provenanced import from a frame whose closure differs** (Fig 6 :23, Fig 8 :50-52), with a source gate and a shadow test.

*They're Scanning Books* sharpens this: *"The real import is not a new term in the existing space. It is a new dimension of the space."* And: *"A minimum viable set of six terms is a human's careful guess at reality's compression axes; another sensorium's minimum set need not be those six."*

For BUS-0044, "default alive," "LTV/CAC," "burn multiple" and "rule of 40" are imports from the venture-finance closure. They may be useful instruments, but under Fig 8 :50-52 convergent derivation and adopted import look identical in the contents and only the record separates them. Until a source gate and a shadow test are supplied, they carry no verdict weight.

## 4. Why a growth chart is not a verdict

*The Hard Problem is About Source* gives the mechanism, read this session. Metamerism: an instrument reads green and *"it cannot comment on the source."* The source might be one green light, or yellow plus blue, or RGB, or CMYK, or a scheme not yet discovered. *"If several paths create the same behavior, then behavior is a projection with lossy information."* And: *"An instrument that can't distinguish these cases demonstrates its own inadequacy."* It was the **spectrometer**, not an argument, that resolved it. *"The answer is unreached until a better instrument exists."*

A growth chart is exactly such a projection. Paid acquisition, a price cut, a one-time enterprise deal and a real retention improvement can all produce the same upward line. Naming the source ("we have product-market fit") from the reading is the metamerism error. What is owed is a discriminator: a cohort-level test that separates bought growth from retained value. F2 and F10 in `01_figure_grounded_audit.md` are this essay's rule applied to the business question.

## 5. Q: what an answer to BUS-0044 would look like

**Answered at the shape level.** Nothing in the framework licenses a universal numeric threshold. A particular threshold is licensed only by (i) the ground settling a minted term in a specific market, or (ii) a provenanced import with a shadow test, recorded as such. Until one of those, the number is the searcher's own contribution.

The honest answer form is therefore: *two conditions, each with its own instrument and refuter, kept separate; detection upstream of decision; threshold left open.* That is what `07_the_tether_answer.md` states.

## 6. Prestige is not action

From *The Recursive Self-Improvement Trap*, read this session: *"Prestige must never be allowed to validate an action directly... Someone let prestige stand in for proof... Prestige can fund an action. It can give an action a budget to be tried. But it cannot settle an action. Only the ground can settle it. The Ground Maintainer must be blind to prestige."*

A well-known investor's endorsement, a famous accelerator's brand or a high-prestige journal's acceptance can **fund** the testing of this shape. None of them can settle whether growth created value in a given cohort. The legitimate ask is a refutation, not an endorsement. Fig 2 :35: *"Alignment is triangulation, not negotiation. A mirror cannot correct you."*

## 7. My own audit is a simulation, not a ground reading

Same essay: *"LLMs can quickly simulate an approximate answer/output with the formula found in the architecture post. It will often be better than the generic outputs that you will usually get otherwise too (intelligence is essentially arrangement). However, the truly testable output would need to be derived from the real architecture or working the formula yourself."* And: *"the output is only as good as the validator. The agent, or the tool, or the frame, or the theory, cannot validate itself."*

So `01_figure_grounded_audit.md` and this folder's other derivations are simulated derivations, checked for internal consistency against the figures. They are **not** tests of the business claim and must be labelled as such. They cannot settle anything they report on, including which option a founder should choose on a Tuesday with eighteen months of runway.

## 8. A correction kept visible

An early draft of this note said that "the framework returns a shape, not a name" forbids using any business metric at all. **Narrowed.** The README's phrase governs a *missing* primitive, one not held in Γ. "Payback period" and "retention" are already in the venture-finance frame; using them is a binding, not the invention of a new primitive. What remains forbidden is presenting a binding as a derivation, or claiming it moves the wall (Fig 6: *"IMPORT adds an atom"* - a binding adds none). The metamerism point in section 4 is **not** retracted: the chart still cannot comment on its source. Both readings are recorded so neither is quietly lost.
