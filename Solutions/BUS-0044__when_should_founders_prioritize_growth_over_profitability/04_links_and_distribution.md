## Distribution: offer, do not push

- **Fig 4 :22** - *"Only methods cross up. Only head starts come down."* Sending the full architecture before anyone has described a residual sends a recording, not a generator. Lead with the shape claim and its refuter; offer links after.
- **Fig 10 :42** - *"A verifier that can reconstruct the claim cannot verify it."* A recipient who has absorbed the entire framework before being asked for a verdict has joined the frame being checked.
- **Fig 9 :27-28** - *"describe it before you go looking, or any frame you pick will seem to fit."* Ask what they would expect to see if the shape claim were true before giving them the articles.
- **Fig 2 :40** - *"agreement about the world is worthless when the agreeing frames share an evidence pool."* All the business sources above draw on the same population of startups and the same practitioner folklore. Do not present four sources as four independent witnesses.
- **Seat Map, correlation rule** - *"High correlation is disqualifying for a verdict and efficient for a convention."* A send list selected for people already sympathetic to either growth-first or profit-first doctrine produces a convention, not corroboration.

**Recommended order in a reply:** (1) the shape claim and its five refuters; (2) ask what they would count as a failure; (3) record their verdict; (4) only then the links, with the note that the figures, not this summary, are the authority; (5) offer the formula as a method, with the instruction that each user writes their own refuter and states their own mode.

## Practical constraint: venue AI policies

This folder is a simulated derivation, not human-authored research. Two venues in `03_public_contact_points.csv` state explicit rules that matter before anything is submitted:

- Small Business Economics: *"LLMs, such as ChatGPT, do not currently satisfy our authorship criteria"* and LLM use must be documented in the Methods section (except copy editing).
- Academy of Management: *"Work submitted to AOM must be created by the authors and not the product of artificial intelligence tools unless appropriate to the research question and properly cited."*

So this package is offered as a **checkable object for a human reader**, not as a manuscript. If a human author chooses to develop it, they must write their own derivation, disclose the tool use, and run their own refuter before submission.

## Zip / hosting

Nothing is sent by this agent. Host any archive yourself, preferably on an opt-in download page that lets a recipient decline contact. An attachment pushed to a list fails both the structural rule (Fig 10 :42) and the legal one (GDPR and CAN-SPAM style rules for unsolicited bulk mail).
