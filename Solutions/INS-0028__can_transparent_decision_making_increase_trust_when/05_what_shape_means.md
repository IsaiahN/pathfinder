## 1. A shape is a constraint, not a filler

From *The Library of Babel*: "you can cut your search short if the set of books returned is small and descriptive enough that it provides a general shape of the answer that satisfies your question." The same essay: "you will need to use the existing vocabulary you do know that is closest to the words that you wish to describe and grasp the shape of" and "With each new term gained, you are able to narrow the space by definition and deepen your vocabulary."

For INS-0028, the shape is **not** "yes, transparency increases trust" and **not** a coefficient such as 0.13. It is a constrained conditional form: which kind of disclosure, under which condition on the residual, measured by which instrument, in which slots, and what would refuse it. `SPACE(???)TIME` is the exemplar's shape; the analogue here is `DISCLOSURE(K) -> TRUST_s UNDER(C), MEASURED_BY(I), REFUSED_BY(D)`. The specific K, C, I and D are the search's contribution and must be recorded as such.

## 2. How a shape is obtained

- *Library of Babel*: "The problem you have determines what makes a book relevant to you or not." That is the residual-first rule. Describe the per-slot trust gap in effect terms before choosing a literature or a policy.
- *Library of Babel* on bundling: "Perhaps your problem was a bundle or conflation of several problems masquerading as one?" This is the union finding F5: an unpopular outcome is two conditions, not one harder question.
- Fig 6 :30 (local figure quote): "The description travels. Fails by being sharpened into terms only we can read. Cast it in effect terms." Fig 9 :30-31: "your words are borrowed too, so describe the effect rather than the cause."
- *Decoding Tacit Knowledge*: "When contact isn't possible, the best you can send is a plain description of what you'd expect to see, because anyone can check that against the world." This is why the outreach email asks what the recipient expects to see before showing the framework.
- *Library of Babel* on stopping: "you know that you are done because the finish line will also be the starting place, as there is nowhere else to search and nothing else that can describe the answer better."

## 3. What licenses a name

Only two things, both outside the shape itself:

1. **The ground settles it.** *How Intelligence Actually Works*: "the answer doesn't exist until the question is contacted." Formula step 5 (local): "the ground settles it. Verified only by what pays. A gate is not the ground." Publishing a disclosure is a gate, not settlement.
2. **A provenanced import from a frame whose closure differs**, with a source gate and a shadow test (Fig 6 :23, Fig 8 :50-52). *They're Scanning Books*: "The real import is not a new term in the existing space. It is a new dimension of the space."

*They're Scanning Books* also says: "A minimum viable set of six terms is a human's careful guess at reality's compression axes; another sensorium's minimum set need not be those six" and "Declaring the primitives does not make them neutral. It makes the choice visible." So naming "trust" as a single primitive is a visible choice, not a discovery. Declaring it does not make the pooled reading neutral.

## 4. The specific error this question commits

*The Hard Problem is About Source* gives the mechanism. A color instrument reads green and "cannot comment on the source"; the source could be one green light, yellow plus blue, RGB, CMYK, or a scheme not yet discovered. The essay: "If several paths create the same behavior, then behavior is a projection with lossy information" and "the answer is unreached until a better instrument exists." It was the spectrometer, not an argument, that resolved the case.

Applied here: a rise in an aggregate trust score is a reading with several possible sources. It could come from changed contact with the decision's fairness, from a well-written disclosure, from fatigue, from social desirability, or from a changed sample. Naming the source as "transparency worked" from the reading alone is the metamerism error. What is owed is a source-discriminating instrument, not a larger survey. This is finding F2 and F6 in `01_figure_grounded_audit.md`.

*How Intelligence Actually Works* sharpens it with the distinction horizon and the union horizon: a projection may pass one observer and fail against a pool of experts with better instruments, and "being below the union horizon is never a settled verdict, only a report that nobody has reached it yet." So the `gap` in F6 is honest: no instrument held here discriminates the sources of a trust change.

## 5. Prestige is not action, and agreement is not ground

*The Recursive Self-Improvement Trap*: "Prestige must never be allowed to validate an action directly... Prestige can fund an action. It can give an action a budget to be tried. But it cannot settle an action. Only the ground can settle it. The Ground Maintainer must be blind to prestige."

For this question: a famous reviewer, a large petitions list, or a high response rate can fund a test. None can settle whether transparency changed contact. *Decoding Tacit Knowledge*: "two people who also meet the world apart keep enough difference to catch each other" and "Two people who only check against each other drift, and they drift together, so neither notices." A stakeholder workshop in which everyone reads the same dashboard is the drift case, not corroboration.

## 6. Correction to my own reading of the shape rule

I initially treated "shape, not a name" as if it forbade naming any concrete disclosure form. That overstates it. The canonical phrase governs a **missing** thing in Γ. Here the missing thing is not "transparency" as a word; it is the source-discriminating instrument that would tell contact from attitude. Naming a candidate instrument in effect terms is permitted and required by the refuter. What is not permitted is claiming the framework itself names which disclosure works. That is a world measurement, and it stays a `gap` until a ground pays for it. Both readings are kept so neither is quietly lost.

## 7. This aid is a simulation, not a ground reading

*The Recursive Self-Improvement Trap*: "LLMs can quickly simulate an approximate answer/output with the formula... However, the truly testable output would need to be derived from the real architecture or working the formula yourself." The essay calls such output "simulacra."

So `01_figure_grounded_audit.md`, `06_isomers_and_order.md` and this file are simulated derivations checked for internal consistency against the verbatim figures. They are **not** a test of whether transparency works and cannot settle anything they report on. Mode is `specified` where a falsifiable claim is made, `general` for the structural rules, and `gap` for the magnitude.

## 8. One paragraph for reuse

A shape here is the narrowed form of an answer you can state before you can measure it. It is not a yes, not a coefficient, and not a policy slogan. The figures constrain it to a conditional: trust is per slot, the anchor is human judgment, admissible evidence must bear on the residual rather than on the maker, the channel can saturate and pool, and a verifier must be able to refuse the institution's account. What would settle the condition, and how much trust moves, are not in the shape. They are owed from a ground and declared as a gap until one arrives.
