## QUESTION

Decide in effect terms what the Tether says about the claim that publishing more of an institution's decision process increases citizen trust when the outcome is unpopular. Restated: R_s = |Γ(b,a) - o'| per affected group s, the gap between what the institution's process predicts will be accepted and what citizens actually register. Option A: disclosure of maker reasoning reduces R_s. Leads to: the group gains information, and either the gap closes or a break in contact becomes visible without repair. Option B: disclosure of residual-bearing evidence, verifiable by an outside frame that can refuse the account, reduces R_s. Leads to: contact changes if the evidence can bite. What an answer would look like was bound before the run in `01` §0: a conditional rule with kind, condition, instrument and refuter, so the sought shape is an ordinary term and refusable rather than a preference.

**D1 Institutional decision frame, status `triangulated` (Fig 10 ground maintainer seat and Fig 9 verifier admission, low overlap).**

> *"an interest in the outcome is unavoidable, an interest in the reading is disqualifying"* (Fig 10 :59)

Maker-process disclosure is evidence about the maker; only residual-bearing evidence is admissible. Mode `specified`. Refuter: a demonstrably independent disclosure channel where maker-only disclosure still closes the per-slot residual.

**D2 Citizen judgment frame, status `triangulated` (Fig 2 anchor rule and Fig 1 slot indexing, low overlap).**

> *"An anchor is legitimate when the question is constitutively about it."* (Fig 2 :36)
> *"Human judgment is the right anchor for is this a good answer for a person, because the question is about people."* (Fig 2 :37)
> *"The residual is indexed by object slot, not aggregated."* (Fig 1 :19)

Reading: people are the anchor for a question about trust, but the measurement must be per affected group; a pooled average hides the live group. Mode `general` for the anchor, `specified` for the application. Refuter: an aggregate reading that fully predicts compliance in every slot.

**D3 Disclosure channel, status `triangulated` (Fig 10 channel office and Fig 4 round trip, low overlap).**

> *"the ground does not decay, the channel does"* (Fig 10 :24)
> *"stale percepts, dropped transients, saturated metrics, pooled readings"* (Fig 10 :26)
> *"So take the round trip and measure the gap. Without it you are navigating by dead reckoning: confident about a position nobody checked."* (Fig 4 :30)

Reading: disclosure is a channel, not a ground. More of it can saturate, go stale or pool groups, and the published account is a lossy condensation of the decision. Mode `specified`. Refuter: a measured zero round-trip gap at every resolution with no saturation or pooling.

**D4 Outside verification frame, status `triangulated` (Fig 10, Fig 2, Fig 7, three distinct roles).**

> *"A verifier that can reconstruct the claim cannot verify it."* (Fig 10 :42)
> *"Independence, between two frames, is how little they draw on the same evidence."* (Fig 2 :31)
> *"Buy independence in proportion to the cost of being wrong."* (Fig 2 :42)
> *"A room's agreement is variation, not a verdict. Selection between rooms is where the ground votes."* (Fig 7 :35)

Reading: the checker must be unable to produce what it checks; many readers of one publication are one evidence pool. Independence is a parameter, not a caveat. Mode `general`. Refuter: outside readers with genuinely separate experience whose convergence changes the institution's contact with the ground.

**D5 Unpopular outcome as compound, status `triangulated` (Fig 11 valence and Fig 9 union, low overlap).**

> *"An actor's valence is relative to the residual, not a property it carries."* (Fig 11 :49)
> *"An improvement that does not change contact changes nothing, however much it improves."* (Fig 11 :40)
> *"And when the split lands on a union rather than a partition, unbundle before you search again."* (Fig 9 :54)

Reading: transparency has no standing good valence. Under a repairable misreading, residual-bearing disclosure can help; under a break in contact, maker-only disclosure exposes the break without repairing it. Mode `specified`. Refuter: a fixed disclosure effect across a change of residual with contact held constant.

**Magnitude of the trust gain, status `gap`.** No figure line governs how much. *"you get a kind of thing, never the answer itself"* (Fig 9 :32). *"Unreachable is a verdict here, not a silence"* (Fig 13 :134). *"So a search that finds nothing is never a proof of absence"* (Fig 8 :39). The missing thing is a source-discriminating instrument characterized in effect terms but not held.

Triangulation summary: every admitted domain is triangulated across two or more low-overlap figure lines. No domain is single-source. The one `gap` is the magnitude, declared plainly.

## VERDICT (from the figures)

The figure-derived verdict is a conditional shape, not a yes or no. Under an unpopular outcome, transparency can increase trust only where it changes the institution's contact with the affected group's ground, by putting residual-bearing evidence before an outside verifier who can refuse the institution's account. It cannot do so merely by publishing maker reasoning, by saturating or pooling the channel, or by reporting trust as a single aggregate. Verdicts stay separate per domain and are never averaged. Mode `specified`, not `grounded`. Magnitude is a declared `gap`. The meta-analysis average corroborates the existence of a conditional effect but is not the verdict.

- **Cost if wrong:** a real cost and a bounded one. A regulator who accepts the simple rule "publish more to restore trust" will fund channel instruments for a case whose break is in contact, leaving the affected group's residual live while the population average improves. Under Fig 2 :42, independence should be bought in proportion to the cost of being wrong; here that means an outside verifier before the claim is believed. The cost of wrongly refusing the shape is also real: a useful disclosure route is withheld. Both are recorded so a later reader can locate the error.
- **Undoable:** yes. No term is deleted if the shape fails. A refutation is added and the reach falls because "something was added rather than because anything was deleted" (Fig 6 :42-43). The shape, its five refuters and its provenance are recorded, so a later reader can find the error rather than only feel it (Fig 10 :62-64). The one irreversible cost is spending the independence of outside readers by sending a recording instead of a generator, which is avoidable by leading with the residual and its refuter.
- **Reversed by:** a ground reading, not agreement. Any of the five pre-registered refuters: (1) a fixed disclosure effect across a change of residual with contact held constant; (2) maker-only disclosure closing the per-slot residual without an outside verifier; (3) an aggregate reading fully predicting compliance in every slot; (4) a measured zero round-trip gap with no saturation or pooling; (5) institutional self-verification changing citizen contact. Also a source-discriminating instrument that names the source of a trust change, a derivation of a new audience-selection primitive from the held set, or a genuinely independent frame whose closure predicts the effect shape. Until one of these fires, the verdict stands as `candidate`.
