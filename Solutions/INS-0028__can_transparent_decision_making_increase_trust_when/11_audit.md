## Round trip gap R_T

The Symbols table defines R_T as gap(x, (T_E composed with T_A)(x)), measuring what a coarser description cannot hold. Fig 4 :30 requires it to be measured rather than assumed zero. I ran the round trip on the condensation this folder actually uses.

1. **Sent up (x):** the decision as it ranged against the affected group's ground, with its per-slot residual and the violation path that would let it be refused.
2. **Abstraction (T_A):** the shorthand in `07_the_tether_answer.md`, namely measure per slot, anchor in human judgment, bear on the residual rather than the maker, change contact, install an outside verifier.
3. **Concretisation (T_E):** a concrete policy proposal, publish a reasoned decision with a complaint route, validated by a post-disclosure survey.

**R_T is nonzero.** Four things the shorthand holds do not survive the trip back down:

- the **source discrimination** between contact and attitude, collapsed into a single survey score;
- the **per-slot indexing**, collapsed into a population average;
- the **violation path**, which becomes a complaint form the institution itself processes rather than an outside frame that can refuse the account;
- the **maker versus residual distinction**, which a reasoned decision statement tends to erase.

Conversely, what survives cleanly is the order (perceive, state the objective, plan, verify) and the requirement that the checker be outside the frame. R_T was **not** measured numerically, because no ground reading exists; it is recorded qualitatively as the enumerated set of dropped constraints. Treating the formula as identical to the figures would set R_T to zero, which would be the dead-reckoning error Fig 4 :30 names.
