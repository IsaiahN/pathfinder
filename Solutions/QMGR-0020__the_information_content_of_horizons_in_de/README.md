## What is deliberately not here

- No personal emails of named individuals.
- No ranking of people by how likely they are to believe the claim.
- No bulk-send plan.

Three reasons, in the figures' own words:

1. **A room does not upgrade the label.** Fig 2 :40: *"agreement about the world is worthless when the agreeing frames share an evidence pool"* (the leading clause of the full line is *"disqualifying for a verdict"*). Adding recipients raises the vocabulary, not the anchor.
2. **Selection for agreement disqualifies the verdict.** Fig 2 :35: *"Alignment is triangulation, not negotiation. A mirror cannot correct you."* A list chosen for receptiveness manufactures the correlation that makes its agreement worthless.
3. **Asking for trust before a check spends independence.** Fig 10 :42: *"A verifier that can reconstruct the claim cannot verify it."* Seat 4 must not hold the interiors.

Legally: harvesting personal emails for unsolicited bulk outreach breaches GDPR and CAN-SPAM style rules in many jurisdictions. That is a practical failure on top of the structural one. This folder uses only public institutional submission and contact venues.
