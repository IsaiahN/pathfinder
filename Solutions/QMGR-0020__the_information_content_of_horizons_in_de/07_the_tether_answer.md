# The Tether's answer on de Sitter horizon information content

**Mode: `specified` on substantive claims, `general` on the structural shape. Not `grounded`.** A shape and a route, not a number. Simulated derivation checked against the figures (`05_what_shape_means.md` §6), not a ground reading.

## 1. The residual it answers

An observer in de Sitter space is bounded by a cosmological horizon. Two readings are bundled: the geometric entropy (area proportional) and the information accessible to that observer (a count). They are a union compound, not one hard problem. Fig 9 :57-58: *"a case in two conditions is not a harder case of either"*. Each domain keeps its own verdict and they are never averaged.

## 2. The shape

1. **The horizon is a containment boundary of a finite region.** Fig 13 :10: *"near, far, between, contained"*. The world source *Cosmological horizon* says points outside the event horizon *"can never be observed"*. So the horizon is the boundary of what is accessible.

2. **The information content is finite because the region is finite.** Fig 13 :68: **"finiteness in a finite region is what makes the counting terminate"**. This is the direct bearing. The world source *Bekenstein bound* states the same: information *"must be finite if the region of space and the energy are finite"*. The finiteness is structural; the coefficient is not given.

3. **The count is per patch, not a global average.** Fig 1 :19: **"The residual is indexed by object slot, not aggregated."** Fig 1 :22: **"Aggregating across slots is how a live signal disappears into an average."** The information-theory source *Information content* gives the additive form: *"the total amount of information is the sum of the self-informations of the individual events."*

4. **The framework yields a shape, not a coefficient.** Fig 9 :32: **"you get a kind of thing, never the answer itself"**. No figure line produces A over 4 or a microstate basis. Those belong to the physics frame.

5. **The microscopic count is a gap at the ground, not a silence.** Fig 2 :29: **"An anchor is whatever settles the question and does not change its answer because of what any frame thinks."** The semiclassical area law is a nameable anchor candidate, but it does not discriminate microstates. *Black hole thermodynamics* records that *"There is no known way to verify black hole thermodynamics"* and that the controlled microstate calculation of 1995 covered supersymmetric black holes; *Holographic principle* lists dS/CFT and celestial holography as programmes whose triangulation claim *"fits four cases already known and has not been tested forward."* So D2 has no settled anchor. Fig 13 :134: **"Unreachable is a verdict here, not a silence"**. And Fig 8 :39: **"So a search that finds nothing is never a proof of absence."**

6. **The area law is a provenanced binding, not a figure derivation.** Fig 6 :22: **"IMPORT adds an atom, and the added atom is what expands the closure."** A binding adds no atom. Bousso's review states the physics claim and its own limit in one passage: the area bound is strongly evidenced, and the universal relation *"has yet to be explained."*

## 3. What it explicitly does not say

- Not a number. No coefficient, no bit density, no microstate basis is asserted as figure-derived.
- Not a solution to the black-hole information paradox. That is a different question with its own residual.
- Not that the de Sitter horizon carries the same information structure as a black-hole horizon. They are at most isomers over a shared atom set, with the observer's position as the differing bond (`06_isomers_and_order.md` Q3), and the weaker functional-group claim is the one relied on.
- Not `grounded`. No measurement at a de Sitter horizon settles `left(R,φ)`.
- Not corroborated by agreement. A list of readers chosen for agreement is the collapse named in Fig 2 :35, not a witness.

## 4. What would settle it, the ground

A source-discriminating instrument, not a further argument. *The Hard Problem is About Source*: an instrument that reads green *"cannot comment on the source"*; the spectrometer resolved the case. Here:

- **Finiteness test:** a checkable case of a finite cosmological horizon whose accessible information is infinite or unbounded. That breaks claim 2.
- **Per-slot test:** a demonstration that the degrees of freedom are irreducibly global, so per-patch counting loses information. That breaks claim 3.
- **Anchor test:** a settled microscopic state count for de Sitter horizon entropy, checkable by a frame that does not share its evidence pool. That moves D2 from gap to grounded.
- **Substrate refuters:** derive one of the six from the other five; admit a first-order state-state or structure-structure composition that cannot be refused; show the finiteness rule governs black-hole horizons only and not cosmological ones.

All five are written before the conclusion in `01_figure_grounded_audit.md` §4. A failure is more useful than an endorsement.

## 5. The honest stop

**Unreached, not unreachable.** The six-term set is *"a human's careful guess at reality's compression axes"* (*They're Scanning Books When They Should*); declaring it makes the choice visible, not neutral. *How Intelligence Actually Works* adds: *"Being 'below the union horizon' is never a settled verdict, only a report that nobody has reached it yet."* The answer stops at the limit of the available instruments and records the lineage so the stop is checkable. *"indiscernibility is not identity."*

## 6. One-paragraph version for outreach

> The Tether does not give a number for the information content of a de Sitter horizon, and it does not claim the final answer. It says that the horizon is a containment boundary of a finite region, and that finiteness in a finite region is what makes counting terminate, so the information content is finite and bounded by the horizon. It says the count attaches to patches of the horizon rather than to a global average. It refuses to supply the coefficient or a microstate basis, because the framework returns a shape, not a name, and the area law is a provenanced binding from physics, not a figure derivation. It records the missing microscopic anchor as a gap at the ground rather than filling it with agreement, and it states five falsifiers written in advance. The center stays open until a source-discriminating instrument settles it.
