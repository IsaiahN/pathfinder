## QUESTION

Option B: keep the figure-derived shape and leave the coefficient and the count open at the ground. ## DOMAIN CENSUS

R by arity, symmetry and scale, then one line per domain with actors, currency and anchor; partition or union; order by dependency.

- **R (the residual).** The gap between a finite horizon boundary and an unsettled count of what is accessible to the observer. Arity: two conditions, geometric entropy and accessible information, so a union compound. Fig 9 :57-58: *"a case in two conditions is not a harder case of either"*. Symmetry: the area is a scalar, the count is per patch, so a global average is the wrong operation. Scale: semiclassical at the horizon, microscopic at the Planck cells a count would need.
- **Domain 1, geometry and horizon.** Actors: the horizon, the observer, the cosmological constant. Currency: area, entropy, temperature. Anchor: the semiclassical calculation, nameable but not a discriminator of microstates. Union member.
- **Domain 2, information and counting.** Actors: patches, distinguishable states, bits. Currency: counts and logs of counts. Anchor: none settled for de Sitter space. This is a gap at the ground, reported as `unreached`, not a silence. Union member.
- **Domain 3, verification and outreach.** Actors: expert readers and a public submission venue. Currency: residual and refutation, never agreement. Anchor: the ground, never agreement. Covers both union members.
- **Order by dependency (Fig 3).** Perceive the finite region, have terms for containment and counting, state what would count as progress, then require an instrument. The chain binds at the instrument link; the area coefficient is above the break and cannot rescue it.
- **Strain test.** Fig 13 :75 was strained onto the horizon boundary in a first pass and was withdrawn after re-census. Fig 13 :10 and :68 govern instead. The withdrawal is kept visible in `01` F8.

## VERDICT (from the figures)

The shape survives. The de Sitter horizon bounds a finite region, so the information content is finite and area-bounded, and Fig 1 requires it to be counted per patch rather than averaged. The coefficient and the microstate basis do not survive as figure results: the area law is a provenanced binding from the physics frame, and the microscopic count is a gap at the ground, reported as `unreached` rather than `underivable`. D1 is `specified` and corroborated; D2 is `specified` and `gap`; D3 is `single-source` and never a witness. Verdicts are kept separate and never averaged. This is a `specified`, ungrounded result.

- **cost if wrong:** bounded and locatable. The packet states mode, five refuters and the lineage before the conclusion, so an error is findable rather than invisible. The real cost is spending the independence of outside readers by sending a number instead of a shape, which Fig 2 :42 prices: *"Buy independence in proportion to the cost of being wrong."* That cost is avoidable by leading with the shape claim and its refuter.
- **undoable:** yes. The coefficient and the microstate binding are withheld rather than asserted, so nothing has to be withdrawn to send the corrected packet. If a settled count arrives, it is added as a new term with provenance and the shape claim is re-tested; the record grows rather than being silently rewritten. The strained Fig 13 :75 reading was withdrawn and kept visible, which is the model for any future correction.
- **reversed by:** a ground reading, not agreement. A source-discriminating instrument that distinguishes candidate microstate sources at a cosmological horizon would name the slot; a finite-region horizon with infinite or irreducibly global information would break the shape claim; a figure re-census showing the finiteness rule governs black-hole horizons only would send F2 back to census; a derivation of one of the six from the other five would break the substrate. Until one of these fires, the verdict stands as `candidate`.
