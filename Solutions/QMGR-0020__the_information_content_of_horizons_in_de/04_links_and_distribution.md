## What the wider literature does and does not give us

1. **The area bound is well supported, but its origin is open.** Bousso's review gives the bit density and says the universal relation *"has yet to be explained."* That is exactly why the audit treats the coefficient as a provenanced binding, not a figure derivation.
2. **The black-hole case is partly settled; the de Sitter case is not.** *Black hole thermodynamics* records the 1995 Strominger-Vafa calculation and notes that the far-from-extremal case remains uncharacterised. *Holographic principle* lists dS/CFT and celestial holography as active programmes. None of this gives a settled de Sitter microstate count, so F5's gap at the ground stands.
3. **Observer dependence is the structural difference.** *Cosmological horizon* says points outside the event horizon can never be observed. There is no outside vantage from which to count the horizon's states, which is why the anchor test in Fig 2 :29 fails for D2.
4. **The reading of "information" matters.** *Information content* defines the bit via self-information and makes additivity explicit. That supports the per-slot reading in F3 and stops "information content" being used as an undefined placeholder.

No world source here shares an author with the figures. Their agreement with the figure verdicts is therefore not one evidence pool talking. They are still secondary: the figures decide.

## Distribution: offer, do not push

Figure-grounded reasons:

- **Fig 4 :22** - *"Only methods cross up. Only head starts come down."* Sending the full architecture before anyone has described a residual sends a recording, not a generator. Lead with the shape claim and its refuter; offer the links after.
- **Fig 10 :42** - *"A verifier that can reconstruct the claim cannot verify it."* A recipient who has absorbed the whole framework before being asked for a verdict has joined the frame being checked.
- **Fig 9 :27-28** - *"describe it before you go looking, or any frame you pick will seem to fit"*. Ask what they would expect to see if the shape claim were true before giving them the article.
- **Fig 2 :35** - *"Alignment is triangulation, not negotiation. A mirror cannot correct you."* A send selected for people who already agree produces a convention, not corroboration.
- **Fig 2 :40** - *"agreement about the world is worthless when the agreeing frames share an evidence pool"*. Record the stage-1 verdict before showing any theory; never pool later agreement.

**Recommended order in a reply:** (1) the shape claim, its mode and its refuter; (2) ask what they would count as a failure; (3) then the links, with the note that the figures, not the summary, are the authority.

## The two-stage offer

- **Stage 1 before stage 2** is Fig 10 :52 - *"Bring a characterised residual, never the source"* - and Fig 2 :45 - *"Frames range against the ground. They do not negotiate with it."* The recipient checks the output without holding the framework's interior.
- **Stage 2 is opt-in by construction.** Once a recipient has absorbed the theory, their agreement stops being evidence. Their stage-1 verdict must be recorded first and their later agreement is never pooled.
- **Stage 3 sends a method, which is permitted** (Fig 4 :22). The formula is a generator, so giving it away does not give away an answer.
- **Stage 3 still needs a ground per problem.** `THE_FORMULA.md` "Direction": a frame *"cannot check the direction of its own question, because the shape a frame is searching for is not a term it holds."* Each user writes down what would refute their result before running it.

**Hard precondition:** stages 1 and 2 only work if the stage-1 output survives. The five refuters in `01_figure_grounded_audit.md` §4 are written down and open. If one fires, the packet is withdrawn rather than sent.
