## 1. A shape is a constraint, not a filler

The canonical figures README (local copy) states the limit: the framework says *"what a missing thing would have to look like (a shape, not a name)"*. Fig 9 :32: *"you get a kind of thing, never the answer itself"*. Fig 13 :138-139: *"you get a kind of thing, never the answer itself, and narrowing gets you close while the ground decides"* is the longer restatement in the extract.

From *The Library of Babel*: *"you can cut your search short if the set of books returned is small and descriptive enough that it provides a general shape of the answer that satisfies your question."*

So a **shape** is the narrowed form of an answer you can state before you can name it. It constrains; it does not fill. For this question, "finite, area-bounded, counted per patch, coefficient unstated" is a shape. "S equals A over four with a particular microstate basis" is a name.

## 2. How a shape is obtained

- *Library of Babel*: use the vocabulary you already have that is closest to the thing, look for recurring new terms that overlap or correlate with it, recurse. *"With each new term gained, you narrow the space and deepen your vocabulary."*
- Fig 6 :30: *"The description travels. Fails by being sharpened into terms only we can read. Cast it in effect terms."* Fig 9 :30-31: *"your words are borrowed too, so describe the effect rather than the cause"*.
- *Decoding Tacit Knowledge*: *"When contact isn't possible, the best you can send is a plain description of what you'd expect to see, because anyone can check that against the world."*
- *How Intelligence Actually Works* gives the stop condition. The recursion converges toward indiscernibility, and *"indiscernibility is not identity."* So a shape that cannot be distinguished from the answer by current instruments is still a shape, and the lineage must be kept.

Applied here: the effect description is "a finite region bounded by a horizon, with a count that terminates". That is checkable by anyone. "The entropy is A over 4 in Planck units" is borrowed vocabulary from the physics frame and must be labelled as such.

## 3. What licenses a name, and what this question is missing

Two things, both outside the shape itself:

1. **The ground settles it.** Formula step 5: *"the ground settles it. Verified only by what pays. A gate is not the ground."* *Decoding Tacit Knowledge* is blunter: context, subtext and pretext *"None of them decides whether it is true. Only the ground does that."*
2. **A provenanced import from a frame whose closure differs** (Fig 6 :23, Fig 8 :50-52), with a source gate and a shadow test.

For de Sitter horizons the ground is partly present and partly absent. *Black hole thermodynamics* records a controlled microstate calculation for supersymmetric black holes in 1995, but also that *"There is no known way to verify black hole thermodynamics"* and that the far-from-extremal case remains uncharacterised. *Holographic principle* records dS/CFT and celestial holography as programmes whose triangulation claim *"fits four cases already known and has not been tested forward."* So the geometric shape is corroborated, but the microscopic name is not licensed.

*They're Scanning Books When They Should* names the deeper limit: *"A minimum viable set of six terms is a human's careful guess at reality's compression axes; another sensorium's minimum set need not be those six."* And: *"Declaring the primitives does not make them neutral. It makes the choice visible."* A visible choice is the most the framework can honestly offer here.

## 4. The instrument point, and why it governs the gap

*The Hard Problem is About Source* gives the mechanism. An instrument reads green and *"cannot comment on the source"*; several paths can create the same reading, so *"If several paths create the same behavior, then behavior is a projection with lossy information"*. It was the spectrometer, not an argument, that resolved the case. The essay states the general rule: *"The answer is unreached until a better instrument exists."* And: *"An instrument that can't distinguish these cases demonstrates its own inadequacy"*.

Applied here: an area law reading does not by itself identify the microstates that produce it. Several microscopic accounts could give the same area reading. What is owed is a discriminator, not a name. This is the reason F5 in `01_figure_grounded_audit.md` records a gap at the ground rather than choosing a microstate basis.

## 5. Why this is not an instance of the "shape, not a name" ban on naming held atoms

There is a correction to make, and it is kept visible. The phrase *"a shape, not a name"* governs a **missing primitive**, one not held in the library. In this question the missing thing is a microstate count and a discriminator, which are not among the six terms and are not held. So the limit applies directly: the framework cannot name the count. But the framework can name held atoms. Space and the relation "contained" are already in the set (Fig 13 :10). Calling the horizon a containment boundary is a binding onto a held atom, not the invention of a seventh primitive. This narrows the first draft of F4, which said the framework cannot name anything; it can name held atoms, it cannot name the missing count. Both forms are recorded in `01` §6.

## 6. Prestige is not action, and this audit is a simulation

**Prestige is not action.** From *The Recursive Self-Improvement Trap*: *"Prestige can fund an action. It can give an action a budget to be tried. But it cannot settle an action. Only the ground can settle it."* A campaign that gathers well-known names to agree that horizon entropy is area-proportional is the named collapse, not corroboration. The legitimate ask is the opposite: give the shape claim a budget to be tried, and let a ground settle it.

**My own audit is a simulation, not a ground reading.** Same essay: *"the output is only as good as the validator. The agent, or the tool, or the frame, or the theory, cannot validate itself."* It adds that the testable output *"would need to be derived from the real architecture or working the formula yourself."* The architecture article says LLM outputs *"must be seen as simulacra"* and warns that results *"could never be verified if the solutions presented were not contrived to fit the formula."* So `01` is a simulated derivation, checked for internal consistency against the figures. It is not a test of the claim and must be labelled as such. It cannot settle anything it reports on.

## 7. Two consequences specific to this question

- **The union horizon is live here, not closed.** *How Intelligence Actually Works*: *"Being 'below the union horizon' is never a settled verdict, only a report that nobody has reached it yet."* The absence of a de Sitter microstate count in the sources opened this session is a report about this search, not a proof that no count is possible. That is Fig 8 :39 in the essay's vocabulary.
- **Outputs are not generators.** *They're Scanning Books When They Should* opens with that claim. The area law is an output of the physics frame; copying it into the framework vocabulary does not give the framework the generator that produced it. This is why F7 labels the area law a provenanced binding.
