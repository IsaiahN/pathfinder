## Distribution: offer, do not push

Figure-grounded reasons:

- **Fig 4 :21** - *"The membrane is the boundary between the sizes, and the rule about what may cross it. Not a wall: a filter with a direction."* Sending the full architecture before anyone has described a residual sends a recording across the wrong boundary. Lead with the residual and the refuter.
- **Fig 10 :52** - *"Bring a characterised residual, never the source."* Seat 4 must not hold the interiors. A recipient who has absorbed the framework before being asked for a verdict has joined the frame being checked.
- **Fig 9 :27-28** - *"describe it before you go looking, or any frame you pick will seem to fit."* Ask what they would expect to see if the shape claim were true before giving them the article.
- **Fig 2 :42** - *"Buy independence in proportion to the cost of being wrong."* A mass send selected for people likely to agree buys correlation, not independence.
- **Seat Map :163** - *"High correlation is disqualifying for a verdict and efficient for a convention."*

**Recommended order in a reply:** (1) the shape claim and its refuter; (2) ask what the recipient would count as a failure; (3) record that verdict; (4) only then offer the links, with the note that the figures, not this summary, are the authority.

## The two-stage offer

1. **Stage 1:** validate the output against the candidate's own ground, without the framework. This is Fig 10 :52 and Fig 2 :45 (*"Frames range against the ground. They do not negotiate with it."*). The ground here is the recipient's own laboratory, rock record, or definition practice.
2. **Stage 2, opt-in only:** if they are curious, offer the source links. Their verdict on stage 1 must be recorded before any theory is shown, and later agreement is never pooled as corroboration (Fig 2 :40: *"agreement about the world is worthless when the agreeing frames share an evidence pool"*).
3. **Stage 3:** offer the formula as a method to reuse. Fig 4 :22: *"Only methods cross up. Only head starts come down."* Each user must write their own refuter and label their own mode, because `THE_FORMULA.md` "Direction" says a frame *"cannot check the direction of its own question."*

**Hard precondition:** stages 1 to 3 only work if the stage-1 output survives. The refuter in `02_outreach_email.md` is written for that check. Do not skip it.
