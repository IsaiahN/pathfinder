# Outreach draft: check the residual first, then decide about the framework

**Use:** one-to-one, or as a cover letter attached to a submission through the public venues in `03_public_contact_points.csv`. **Not** for bulk send.

**Hard precondition:** the stage-1 output below must survive an outside check before any theory is offered. If it does not, the two-stage offer collapses on contact (Fig 10 :52: *"Bring a characterised residual, never the source."*).

---

**Subject:** One question about the life boundary that you could refute in five minutes

> Hi [name],
>
> I am working on a narrow question in the foundations of origins-of-life research and would value a check from someone who actually works on it. I am not asking you to read a theory first, and I am not asking you to agree with anything. If the check fails, there is nothing else to look at.
>
> **The output to check (`specified`, not `grounded`):** the question "is there a clear scientific boundary between prebiotic chemistry and the first life?" asks for a criterion C such that every candidate system (a replicator pool, an autocatalytic network, a protocell) gets exactly one label, `prebiotic` or `alive`, the label is the same for every instrument, and the disagreement trends down as more evidence is gathered.
>
> What I find, using a domain-agnostic framework as a checking instrument, is that this criterion cannot be reached from what the relevant domains hold. The classification domain has no anchor: every definition of life moves when a virus, a prion, a dormant spore, or a synthetic cell is pushed against it, and a thing that moves when pushed is not an anchor. The rock-record domain returns life already present; the intermediate steps appear to have been erased or overprinted. And the transition itself is a union of at least three overlapping conditions, chemical self-organization, the onset of heritable evolution, and observational access, which cannot be searched as one question.
>
> What survives is a shape, not a date: the framework treats a boundary between scales as *"a filter with a direction"* rather than a wall, so what crosses is a reusable method (self-maintenance, heredity) and something is lost in the crossing. On that reading, "alive" is a label applied afterwards to a lineage that met its ground, not a property that switches on at an instant, and the searchable twin of "what is life" is "what makes a chemical system capable of open-ended evolution in a habitat."
>
> **What I am not claiming:** that life has no basis in chemistry, that no origin event occurred, that all definitions are equally useless, or that this framework settles the question. I am not claiming a date for the first life, and I am not claiming that "boundary" means a cell membrane (that is a physical structure in the world, whereas a classification cut is a convention). The rock record and laboratory chemistry are real grounds; neither presently returns a boundary label at the disputed slot. That is `unreachable at this budget`, not proof of absence.
>
> **What would refute the shape claim (written down before this message):**
> 1. A measurement protocol that returns one stable label for every candidate system, unchanged across instruments and frames, with the disagreement trending to zero as evidence accumulates.
> 2. A definition that settles the empirical question while demonstrably changing its answer when a counterexample is pushed against it.
> 3. A stratigraphic or chemical marker that every frame reads identically and that separates a prebiotic state from a living state with no residual.
> 4. An intrinsic property of a system, independent of its habitat, that appears at one invariant time in every lineage.
>
> If any one holds, I will retract the shape claim. A one-line "this fails because..." is more useful to me than agreement. If you would rather not engage, no reply is needed.
>
> **If it survives, and only then:** the sources are below. The figures are the authority, not my summary of them.
> - Canonical figures and formula (local extract used for the line citations): `FRAMEWORK/reference/03-FIGURES_TEXT.md`
> - Figures repository, branch `general-agent`: https://github.com/IsaiahN/tether/tree/general-agent/figures
> - The framework's canonical usage rule: https://github.com/IsaiahN/tether/blob/general-agent/figures/README.md
>
> **If you want to use the machinery:** the formula is in the figures link. Run it on a problem you know well and can check against a ground. Write down what would refute your result *before* you run it, and label the mode of what you get. A reported failure is more useful to me than a reported success.
>
> Thanks for reading,
> [name / role]
