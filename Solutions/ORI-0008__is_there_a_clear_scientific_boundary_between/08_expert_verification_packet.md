## 1. The claim, in one paragraph

The partition "prebiotic chemistry | first life" has no decidable, instrument-invariant cut, and the routed answer is REFUTED rather than owed a sixth definition. What the figures license instead is a directional, lossy membrane between levels: *"not a wall: a filter with a direction"*, through which methods (self-maintenance, heredity, open-ended evolvability) cross upward and head starts cross downward. "Alive" is a label applied afterwards to a lineage that met its ground, and the unit called "first life" is irreducible only relative to a level. The question is a union of three sub-questions with different grounds, so it must be unbundled; the historical cut is unreachable at this budget because the rock record is overprinted. What is offered is the shape a boundary would have to have, five refuters written in advance, and the instrument requirement that would close it.
