## QUESTION

Decide, in effect terms, what follows from demanding a clear scientific boundary between prebiotic chemistry and the first life. Option A: assert that such a cut exists and can be stated (a date, a definition, or a decisive property). Option B: refuse the partition as written and return the shape a boundary would have to have, with refuters and an instrument requirement. What each leads to: A promotes a convention to an anchor, averages across slots, and spends the independence of the people asked to check it; B returns a checkable shape, five refuters written in advance, and an honest `unreachable at this budget` for the historical cut. The residual is the failure of a criterion C to return one stable, instrument-invariant label for each candidate system (replicator pool, autocatalytic network, protocell, virus, prion, dormant spore).

## DOMAIN CENSUS

R by arity, symmetry and scale, then one line per domain with actors / currency / anchor, partition or union, and order by dependency.

- **R (arity / symmetry / scale).** Arity: three-place relation (candidate slot s, classification frame f, time t); the proposed cut is a two-operand threshold. Symmetry: the temporal transition is ordered (sequence bond), while the category cut is symmetric under swap of names (identity-like bond); three bonds are bundled under one word (see `06`, Q1). Fig 1 :19 *"The residual is indexed by object slot, not aggregated."*
- **D1 Prebiotic chemistry.** Actors: molecules, reaction networks, mineral surfaces, gradients. Currency: yields, rates, free energy. Anchor: laboratory synthesis and geochemical measurement. Ground-visible, admitted.
- **D2 Definition and classification.** Actors: criteria lists, NASA working definition, autonomy and thermodynamic definitions, definers. Currency: labels. Anchor: none nameable that does not move when pushed. Admitted as the residual source, not as a ground. Fig 2 :30 *"If it moves when you push on it, it is not an anchor."*
- **D3 Historical and geological.** Actors: rocks, isotopes, microfossils, LUCA reconstructions. Currency: dates, signatures. Anchor: the rock record, lossy and overprinted. Admitted, partial.
- **D4 Transition compound.** Actors: autocatalytic sets, protocells, replicators. Currency: the onset threshold. Anchor: a discriminator between chemical self-maintenance and hereditary evolution, not presently nameable. Not a domain yet: a union of D1, D2 and D3 conditions.
- **X1 Life as free-floating essence.** No actors, currency or anchor nameable. Not a domain. Excluded.
- **X2 Pure conventionalism as the whole answer.** Absorbed into D2; a convention that decides an outcome has stopped being one (Fig 10 :47).

**Union, not partition.** Three overlapping conditions must be unbundled before search: chemical capability (D1), objective for crossing (D2), observational access (D3). Fig 9 :54 *"And when the split lands on a union rather than a partition, unbundle before you search again."*

**Order by dependency.** Perception of candidate systems, vocabulary for what they afford, objective for crossing, plan for which threshold to test, learn and carry (Fig 3). The chain breaks at the objective link, which has no instrument (Fig 3 :27 *"A link with no instrument is not a link that works. It is a link nobody has looked at."*).

Re-census resolves it: Popa's cut is a declared convention for a purpose, a level-relative membrane; the continuum reports deny a chooser-free world cut. Different objects, so no strain after re-census. One live strain remains, the operand type of the comparison bond at the boundary, recorded in VERDICT.

## VERDICT (from the figures)

The partition "prebiotic chemistry | first life" is **refused as written** (`specified`, ungrounded). There is no decidable, instrument-invariant cut: the classification domain has no anchor, the transition is a union compound, and the historical record returns life already present. What the figures license is a directional, lossy membrane, a retrospective lineage label, level-relative irreducibility, and the capability question as the checkable twin of "what is life". The refusal is a figure-grounded verdict, not an agnostic silence (Fig 13 :134 *"Unreachable is a verdict here, not a silence"*). The chemistry and the history are real grounds; neither currently returns a boundary label at the disputed slot. One live strain remains: the threshold bond's second operand is a convention, not a quantity in the same units, and that type question needs an instrument rather than an argument.

- **cost if wrong:** bounded and locatable. The packet states its mode, its refuters and its lineage before its conclusion, so an error is findable rather than invisible. The real cost is professional: promoting a convention to an anchor would corrupt a checkable question into a definitional dispute, and asking a candidate to agree before checking would spend their independence. Fig 2 :42 *"Buy independence in proportion to the cost of being wrong."* That cost is avoidable by leading with the residual and the refuter.
- **undoable:** yes. The refusal is removable without withdrawing any chemistry or any rock. A convergent-cut protocol, a source-discriminating instrument, or a stratigraphic marker would replace it. No primitive has to be retracted and no figure line was stretched to reach the verdict.
- **reversed by:** a ground reading, never agreement. A protocol returning one stable label per candidate system across instruments with residual trending to zero; a definition that does not move under counterexamples and still settles the question; a marker read identically by all frames that separates prebiotic from living states; a genuine wall between scales; or a re-census admitting the comparison bond over a convention as its second operand. Until one fires, the verdict stands as `candidate`.
