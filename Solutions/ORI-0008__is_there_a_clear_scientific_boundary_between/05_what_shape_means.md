## 1. A shape is a constraint, not a filler

The canonical limit: the framework returns *"what a missing thing would have to look like (a shape, not a name)."* Fig 9 :32: *"you get a kind of thing, never the answer itself."* Fig 13: *"It can say the answer is out of reach with the library as it stands... It cannot say which primitive is missing, because naming it would mean already holding it."*

From *The Library of Babel*: *"You can cut your search short if the set of books returned is small and descriptive enough that it provides a general shape of the answer that satisfies your question."*

For this question a **shape** is therefore the narrowed form of the answer that can be stated before a cut can be named. It constrains; it does not fill. Concretely:

- A **filler** answer: "Life began 3.8 billion years ago when the first cell formed." This names a date and an object; it says nothing about what would discriminate them.
- A **shape** answer: "Whatever crosses the chemistry-to-life transition must be a directional, lossy membrane between levels; 'alive' will be applied retrospectively to a lineage that met its ground; and any candidate cut must survive an instrument-invariance test that current definitions fail." This narrows the space of admissible answers without pretending to name the cut.

So the shape for ORI-0008 is the *form of a boundary*, not a boundary.

## 2. How a shape is obtained

- *Library of Babel*: use the vocabulary already held that is closest to the thing, look for recurring new terms that overlap with it, recurse. *"With each new term gained, you narrow the space and deepen your vocabulary."*
- Fig 6 :30: *"The description travels. Fails by being sharpened into terms only we can read. Cast it in effect terms."* Fig 9 :30-31: *"your words are borrowed too, so describe the effect rather than the cause."* This is why §0 of `01` restates "a clear scientific boundary" as a decidable, instrument-invariant cut C rather than as a metaphysical intuition.
- *Decoding Tacit Knowledge*: *"When contact isn't possible, the best you can send is a plain description of what you'd expect to see, because anyone can check that against the world."* For this question: an expected convergent label under changes of instrument.
- *Library of Babel* on stopping: the answer is reached when *"the finish line will also be the starting place"*, with the paradox that the searcher has *"composed the answer through the process of locating it."*

## 3. What licenses a name

Only two things, both outside the shape itself:

1. **The ground settles it.** Formula step 5: *"the ground settles it. Verified only by what pays."* The figures README: the framework *"stops at the limit of what the available instruments can distinguish, and records the lineage so the stop is honest."* Here that would be a protocol returning a stable label across instruments (refuter 1 in `01` §4).
2. **A provenanced import from a frame whose closure differs** (Fig 6 :23, Fig 8 :50-52), with a source gate and a shadow test. A definition imported from philosophy into origin-of-life practice must carry its provenance or it is an undeclared convention.

*They're Scanning Books* sharpens the second: *"The real import is not a new term in the existing space. It is a new dimension of the space."* And: *"A minimum viable set of six terms is a human's careful guess at reality's compression axes; another sensorium's minimum set need not be those six."* Also: *"Declaring the primitives does not make them neutral. It makes the choice visible."*

Applied here: the six-term substrate and the bond set are a visible guess about what composes. Declaring "life" as an atom would make a choice visible, not neutral. It would also, by Fig 12 :104, be a level-relative choice, not a discovery of a bottom.

## 4. Why naming the cut is the specific forbidden error

*The Hard Problem is About Source* (re-fetched 2026-11-23) gives the mechanism. An instrument reads green and *"cannot comment on the source"*: one green light, or yellow plus blue, or RGB, or CMYK. Metamerism. *"If several paths create the same behavior, then behavior is a projection with lossy information (it cannot define consciousness in full)."* And: *"An instrument that can't distinguish these cases demonstrates its own inadequacy, it cannot claim anything substantial about what is being measured or what causes it."*

The origin-of-life analogue is exact: a protocell that replicates, metabolizes and maintains a boundary can be read as "chemical self-organization" or as "life" depending on the frame. The reading (the behavior) does not determine the source (the category). What is owed is a discriminating instrument, not a sixth definition. *"It was proven wrong not by argument, but by the arrival of a better instrument"* (same essay, on the spectrometer). Fig 6 :32-34: *"An instrument is not built from a description. It is improved from a worse instrument already returning something."*

*The Recursive Self-Improvement Trap* (re-fetched 2026-11-23): *"the output is only as good as the validator. The agent, or the tool, or the frame, or the theory, cannot validate itself. An external validator, or ground, is always needed."* A definition cannot validate the category it defines.

## 5. Q2 for this question: what does the shape license?

**What licenses a particular cut C?** Nothing in the framework. The framework returns a shape. A cut is licensed only by the ground settling a minted term (refuter 1) or by a provenanced import with a shadow test. Until one of those, the cut is the searcher's own contribution and must be recorded as such.

**Does the question commit the error the README forbids?** Yes, as written. "A clear scientific boundary" asks for a name where the framework returns a shape. And it asks for that name by reading the category off a projection (the observed behaviors of a protocell) without an instrument that discriminates sources. The correct form is the bound residual C in `01` §0, with the refuter attached, not a chosen date.

**Correction to my own §3.** I initially wrote that naming a cut is forbidden in the same sense that naming Gradient was over-forbidden in the exemplar. That overstatement is corrected here, following the exemplar's own `06` Q1: the "shape, not a name" rule governs a *missing* primitive, not a binding onto one already held. In this question there is no held primitive named "life" to bind onto; the six-term set holds states and structures, not life. So the rule applies in full: the cut is missing from Γ and cannot be named from inside. Both the general rule and this narrower scope are recorded, neither quietly lost.

## 6. Two consequences for outreach

**Prestige is not action.** From *The Recursive Self-Improvement Trap* (re-fetched): *"Prestige must never be allowed to validate an action directly... Someone let prestige stand in for proof... Prestige can fund an action. It can give an action a budget to be tried. But it cannot settle an action. Only the ground can settle it. The Ground Maintainer must be blind to prestige."* A campaign that gathers famous names to agree that there is or is not a boundary is the named collapse, not corroboration. The legitimate ask is to give the claim a budget to be checked against a candidate's own ground.

**My own audit is a simulation, not a ground reading.** Same essay: *"LLMs can quickly simulate an approximate answer/output with the formula found in the architecture post. It will often be better than the generic outputs that you will usually get otherwise too... However, the truly testable output would need to be derived from the real architecture or working the formula yourself."* So `01` is a simulated derivation, checked for internal consistency against the figures. It is **not** a test of the question and must be labelled as such. It cannot settle anything it reports on.

## 7. What "shape" is for ORI-0008, in one sentence

The shape is a directional, lossy membrane with a retrospective lineage label and a level-relative unit, plus an instrument requirement that the misnamed "clear boundary" cannot meet; not a date, not a definition, and not a name.
