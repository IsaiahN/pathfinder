## 0. The claim under audit, restated as an effect

> Is there a clear scientific boundary between prebiotic chemistry and the first life?

In effect terms the question asks for a criterion C, decidable from measurements, such that:

- for every candidate system s in a declared habitat (a replicator pool, an autocatalytic network, a protocell, a hydrothermal vent chemistry), C returns one label, `prebiotic` or `alive`;
- the label is invariant across instruments and across frames (Fig 2 :29);
- the residual between label and observation trends down as effort increases;
- there is no slot on which C returns both labels or neither.

Writing down what an answer would look like and binding it as an ordinary term is the Formula's "Direction" discipline: *"make the sought-for shape an ORDINARY TERM - write down what an answer would look like, bind it, and let it accrue a residual like anything else"* (`THE_FORMULA.md`, Direction). The bound shape is **C as an instrument-invariant, decidable cut**. The audit now ranges that term against the figures. It is not treated as a frame's preference that the checks cannot see (Fig 5 :62-64).

Terminology note: "boundary" is used by the source literature for at least three different objects: a classification cut between two categories, a historical date, and a physical membrane of a protocell. This audit keeps them separate. A physical membrane is a structure in a cell (a thing in the world); a classification cut is a convention (Fig 10 :47). Conflating them is the first finding.

## 1. Domain census (before choosing any figure line)

**R by arity, symmetry, scale.**

- **Arity:** three-place relation (candidate slot s, classification frame f, time t). "Boundary" itself is a two-operand threshold relation (Operators :48: *"a threshold relation between two quantities"*), not a symmetric join.
- **Symmetry:** the temporal transition is ordered (prebiotic then life), carrying a sequence bond `→`; the category cut is symmetric under swap of the two names, carrying an identity-like `≡`. Both are present, so any single-bond reading is a collapse (Fig 12 :5: *"A set of ingredients is not one. The bond is the rest of the information, and it is the half nothing was recording."*).
- **Scale:** first order inside a reaction network; second order at the composition of network plus compartment; third order when descent with modification becomes the dominant selector. The membrane between scales is lossy (Fig 4 :27).

**One line per domain: actors / currency / anchor.** A domain with no nameable anchor is not admitted as a domain yet.

**Partition or union.** Union, not partition. The boundary question is a compound of at least three overlapping conditions: chemical self-organization (D1), onset of heritable evolution (D2 criterion plus D4), and observational access to the intermediate states (D3). Fig 9 :54-56:

> **"And when the split lands on a union rather than a partition, unbundle before you search again."**
> **"A bundled problem is usually a union of overlapping conditions, not a list of separate ones."**
> **"A single lever clears the region where only one condition holds and leaves the intersections standing, because the second condition still binds."**

A single lever, for example "Darwinian evolution," clears the D2-only region and leaves the D1 and D3 intersections standing. So "is there a clear boundary?" is not one hard question and must be unbundled before it is searched again. Fig 9 :57: *"a case in two conditions is not a harder case of either."*

**Dependency order (Fig 3).** Perception of candidate systems (we can measure a replicator pool) → vocabulary for what a system affords (replicate, metabolize, compartmentalize, persist out of equilibrium) → objective (what would count as crossing) → planning (which threshold to test) → learn and carry. Fig 3 :24-27 locates the break in this very chain:

> **"Why the middle link is where chains usually break."**
> **"The link between them, having terms for what a situation offers and being able to say what would count as progress, has neither, so it attracts the least attention and the fewest instruments."**
> **"A link with no instrument is not a link that works. It is a link nobody has looked at."**

The definition-of-life literature is that unlooked-at middle link. Fig 3 :19: *"Optimising a link that already works cannot rescue one that does not."* Better chemistry or better rocks cannot rescue a missing objective.

## 3. Findings (F1 to F10)

### F1: The governing figure is the membrane, and it is not a wall (decisive, general)

Fig 4 :21, quoted above. The question asks for "a clear scientific boundary." If "clear boundary" means a wall with a two-valued membership test, the figure that governs boundaries says there is no such object between scales: there is a filter with a direction. Fig 4 :27: *"The transform between scales loses information, and that is the point."* And Fig 4 :33: *"Which is why there is no top and no infinite regress."*

Reading: a boundary between chemistry and life is a membrane, that is, a rule about what may cross in which direction, with an irreversible loss. This does not answer "when did life begin" with a date; it says what kind of thing an answer can be. **Mode `general`.** Triangulated with Fig 12 :104 (level-relative irreducibility) and Fig 13 :90 (*"One chain is one route through a space that has no last route."*).

**Refuter:** exhibit a scale boundary in this domain that is a genuine wall: a two-valued test with no directional filter and no information loss, validated on both sides.

### F2: "Alive" is an afterwards-label on a lineage, not a property that switches on (specified)

Fig 11 :36: *"Where nothing maintains anything, intelligence is a label applied afterwards to a lineage that met its ground. Nothing was intelligent; selection ran. The word does no work there."* Replace "intelligence" with "life" and the structure is unchanged: nothing in a chemical lineage was "alive" at a magical instant; selection over heritable variants ran, and the label was applied retrospectively to the surviving lineage. Fig 11 :37 adds the maintained case: *"Where a seat exists, something is doing work, and it is exactly what the seat does: enumerate the habitat, rank by cascade, introduce actors, build instruments, read the ground."*

This is not a metaphor imported from cognition; it is the figure's own replacement for an essence question. Fig 11 :41: *"Which is the same move as asking what generates lift rather than whether a thing is a bird."* The answerable twin (Fig 11 :39) is about capability, and capability is a property of system-and-habitat, not of the system alone (Fig 11 :40: *"An improvement that does not change contact changes nothing, however much it improves."*).

**Mode `specified`** for the origins-of-life binding. Refuter: show a property P such that a system has P and is alive, P is intrinsic to the system (not relative to a habitat), and P appears at a single, instrument-invariant time in every lineage.

### F3: The classification domain has no anchor, so it cannot arbitrate (specified)

Fig 2 :29: *"An anchor is whatever settles the question and does not change its answer because of what any frame thinks."* Every proposed definition of life moves when a frame pushes it: a virus, a prion, a mule, a dormant spore, an AI, or a synthetic protocell is enough to force a revision (SEP, §1: *"any definition of life will tend to exclude living cases or exclude non-living cases"*). Fig 2 :30: *"If it moves when you push on it, it is not an anchor."*

A definition is a convention. Fig 10 :47: *"a convention that decides an outcome has stopped being one."* Seat Map :167: *"A convention nothing can check is a constant the seat authored, carrying the seat's authority. Declaring a meaning does not ground it."* So D2 produces the residual (disagreement) but cannot settle it; it must range against D1's and D3's grounds. Agreement among definers is disqualified: Fig 2 :35 (*"A mirror cannot correct you"*) and Seat Map :163 (*"High correlation is disqualifying for a verdict and efficient for a convention"*).

**Mode `specified`.** Triangulated (Fig 2 anchor rule plus Seat Map convention rule, low overlap: one is about instruments, one about seat authority). Refuter: exhibit a definition that does not change its answer under any counterexample and that all frames read identically, while still settling the empirical question.

### F4: The routed answer for the partition is REFUTED, and the refuter is owed first (specified)

Formula step 2 names five bins, and the fifth is not reachable from the other four:

> *"REFUTED: a term held is false; what is owed / is a refusal, not an explanation"* (`THE_FORMULA.md` step 2).

Fig 5 :50-56 names the same fifth kind: *"The four kinds sort a gap by what is missing. None of them sorts a gap by what is wrong."* Fig 5 :59: *"a term whose residual PERSISTS AND DOES NOT TREND DOWN is the one to point the refusal at."*

The term held is the two-category partition `prebiotic chemistry | first life`. Its residual persists: more criteria, more definitions, and more data do not make it trend down. Tessera (2012) records the mechanism in the world's terms: *"as one focuses experimentally on any of the 'defining' properties of 'life', the sharp boundary seems to blur, splitting into finer and finer sub-divisions."* This is a REFUTED routing, so what is owed is a refusal of the partition's standing to be composed over, not a sixth definition. Seat Map :184: *"a refutation is a negation, which is not a bond at all - a seat that cannot write it can report that a claim stopped paying and cannot report that it is wrong."*

Order matters. Formula step 2 refuses before minting; the field's standard move (propose a new definition) mints before refusing. **Mode `specified`.** Refuter: show the partition's residual trending monotonically down under a declared protocol and instrument.

### F5: The sought-for "clear boundary" is an unbound preference, so it must be written down (general)

Fig 5 :62-64:

> **"A frame searching for an answer of a particular shape holds that shape as a PREFERENCE rather than as a term. It is not bound, it predicts nothing, and it accrues no residual, so nothing here can see it."**

This is why §0 binds C explicitly. Without that step, every figure check runs on the mechanism (how definitions are built) and none on the direction of the question (the demand for a cut). Formula "Direction": *"make the sought-for shape an ORDINARY TERM... Then the preference is refusable by the same test as everything else."* Once bound, C accrues a residual: it returns no consistent label on viruses, prions, dormant spores, or the intermediate chemical systems catalogued by Haghnejae Azar (2024).

**Mode `general`.** Refuter: a frame that can certify its own search direction from inside, which Fig 5 :67 calls a LIMIT rather than a step: *"a frame cannot certify its own completeness."*

### F6: The transition domain is a union compound; unbundle before searching (specified)

Census established D4 as a union of D1, D2 and D3 conditions. Fig 9 :54: *"And when the split lands on a union rather than a partition, unbundle before you search again."* The intersections are the actual residual: a chemical system that self-maintains but does not yet evolve (D1 and D2 overlap), an evolved lineage whose earliest steps are erased from the rock (D2 and D3 overlap), a laboratory protocell that satisfies a definition but is not a historical ancestor (D1 and D3 overlap). Fig 9 :57: *"a case in two conditions is not a harder case of either. It stops being a mixture and becomes a specific compound."*

After unbundling, the three sub-questions have different grounds and must not be averaged:

1. Can a chemical system become capable of open-ended evolution? (D1, lab ground)
2. What objective would count as crossing? (D2, no anchor, needs a convention that is not allowed to decide)
3. Can we observe the intermediate states? (D3, rock record, lossy)

**Mode `specified`.** Triangulated (Fig 9 union rule plus Fig 3 dependency order; independent permissions). Refuter: show the three conditions collapse to one measure with a single ground, so that a single lever clears all intersections.

### F7: The rock record makes the historical cut unreachable at this budget, not underivable in principle (specified)

Fig 8 :40: *"Underivable-in-principle and unreachable-at-this-budget are different facts, and they issue different instructions."* The Mariscal et al. workshop report states the loss directly: *"the emergence of life and subsequent biological and geological evolution may have erased or over-printed all evidence of any or many intermediate steps between purely abiotic processes and the simplest forms of life."* SEP §4: *"Our earliest reliable records of this planet, some 3.5 billion years ago, contain evidence of microbial fossils... as soon as Earth was not molten, it was filled with life."* The record returns life already present, not the crossing.

This is the "SEARCHED / INWARD" branch of Formula step 7, not the "OUTWARD / import" branch. More search of existing rocks and sharper instruments are the remedy; importing a new definition would add an atom that was not needed. Fig 6 :21: *"MINT composes inside the closure and can never add an atom."* Fig 8 :39: *"So a search that finds nothing is never a proof of absence."*

**Mode `specified`, ungrounded.** Single-source for the specific erasure claim (Mariscal et al. 2019 is one review; corroborated in kind by SEP §4, high overlap through the same literature pool). Refuter: a stratigraphic marker that all frames read identically and that discriminates a prebiotic from a living state with no residual.

### F8: "Irreducible first unit" is a fact about a level, not about the world (general)

Fig 12 :104 (quoted in the census): *"So irreducible is a fact about a level and never about the world, and there is no bottom."* The search for "the first life" treats the first living unit as the bottom atom. The figure says irreducibility is level-relative: a gene, a replicator, a protocell, a cell, and a lineage are each atoms at some level and compounds at another. Fig 12 :105: *"the transform between levels is lossy, and the level a composition lands on remakes it."* So any claim about "the first life" must name its level; without a level it is not a well-formed claim. Fig 13 :33: *"Which is why there is no top and no infinite regress."*

**Mode `general`.** Triangulated (Fig 12 :104 plus Fig 4 :33, no top and no bottom, distinct figures). Refuter: exhibit an absolute bottom unit that cannot be composed out of anything at a lower level and loses nothing across the level transform.

### F9: The historical literature contains a live conflict, and it is resolved by re-census, not by averaging

Popa (2010) proposes a boundary: the step where *"digital control of the system's state becomes dominant over analogical control, and genetic information is irreversibly used for adaptive evolution, as the boundary between non-living and living systems."* Tessera (2012) and Haghnejae Azar (2024) deny any sharp boundary. Both are real sources; they conflict.

Per the method, a conflict sends the derivation back to the census. Re-census: Popa's cut is a **chosen objective** for a purpose (a convention stated as a level-relative membrane), while Tessera and Haghnejae Azar deny a **world-cut with no chooser**. Those are different objects. Fig 10 :47: *"a convention that decides an outcome has stopped being one"*, so Popa's boundary is admissible only if declared as a convention for a question and not used as the ground. Read that way, the conflict dissolves and corroborates the membrane verdict. It is recorded here rather than erased.

**Mode `specified`.** Strains: one conflict, resolved by re-census; one live strain (F10) remains named.

## 4. Pre-registered refuter (written before the conclusion)

Required before any outreach: Seat Map :287 *"And a falsifier written before the run is a design instrument, not only an honesty device."*

The shape claim is: **the partition `prebiotic chemistry | first life` has no decidable, instrument-invariant, non-conventional cut; what the figures license instead is a directional, lossy membrane between levels, a retrospective label on a lineage that met its ground, and a union compound that must be unbundled before search.**

It is refuted if any of the following holds:

1. **Convergent cut.** A measurement protocol C is published and independently replicated such that, for every slot s in a declared habitat, C returns one label, the label does not change when the instrument or frame changes, and the residual trends down to zero with effort. (Breaks F1, F3, F4.)
2. **Moving ground.** A definition is shown to settle the empirical question while demonstrably changing its answer when a frame pushes on it. (Breaks Fig 2 :29-30, F3.)
3. **Discriminating rock.** A stratigraphic or chemical marker is found that every frame reads identically and that separates a prebiotic from a living state with no residual. (Breaks F7.)
4. **Intrinsic life.** A property P is exhibited such that P is intrinsic to a system, not habitat-relative, and appears at a single invariant time across lineages. (Breaks F2, F8.)
5. **Non-directional boundary.** A scale boundary in this domain is shown to be a wall with no directional filter and no loss. (Breaks F1.)

If any fires, the shape claim is withdrawn. Until one is run, no part of this package may be labelled `grounded`.

## 5. Recommendation

1. **Refuse before minting (F4, F5).** Do not answer the question with a sixth definition. Bind the sought cut as an ordinary term, show its persistent residual, and refuse the partition's standing to be composed over.
2. **Unbundle the union (F6).** Split into capability (D1), objective (D2), and access (D3). Keep the verdicts separate; never average them into one origin date.
3. **Replace the essence question with the capability question (F2).** The checkable twin is "what makes a chemical system capable of open-ended evolution in a habitat," not "what is life".
4. **Report the missing discriminator as unreachable at this budget (F7, F10).** Say what instrument would close it. Do not import a definition to fill the gap; Fig 8 :40 distinguishes the two facts and Fig 6 prices the wrong atom above starting from primitives.
5. **State the mode on every claim (header).** An unmeasured quantity is a specification of what to measure, not a verdict. Seat Map :281: *"an unmeasured quantity is a specification of what to measure and is worth having, it is not a verdict."*
6. **Send a generator, not a conclusion.** Fig 4 :25: *"A recording carried upward looks like knowledge and is a description of one occasion."* Lead with the shape and the refuter; offer the links only after a verdict on stage 1 is recorded.

### Seven-question recursive derivation (summary)

1. **Is the question well formed?** No, as written. It is a union compound (Fig 9 :54), not one hard question.
2. **What is sought?** A cut C invariant across instruments. Bound as an ordinary term (Formula "Direction").
3. **Is there an anchor for C?** No. Definitions move under counterexamples (Fig 2 :30), so D2 cannot settle it.
4. **What bin does the partition route to?** REFUTED (Formula step 2, Fig 5 :59). A refusal is owed before any new definition.
5. **Can a new definition be minted from the same criteria?** No atom is added (Fig 6 :21); it would re-enter the same closure.
6. **Is the historical cut reachable?** Not at this budget (Fig 8 :40); the record is overprinted, and sharper instruments, not imports, are the remedy (Fig 6 :32).
7. **What survives?** A shape: a directional membrane, a retrospective lineage label, level-relative irreducibility, and a named instrument requirement. No date, no cut, no name.
