## 3. What it explicitly does not say

- Not that life has no chemical basis. Chemistry is the ground of D1 and the source of the atoms at every level.
- Not that no origin event occurred, or that the historical transition was not real. It says the *cut* is not decidable as posed, not that there was no passage.
- Not a date for the first life and not a definition of life. Both are names where the framework returns a shape.
- Not that "boundary" means a cell membrane. That is a physical structure in the world (Fig 4 is about scale membranes); a classification cut is a convention (Fig 10 :47).
- Not that all definitions are equally useless. Operational definitions such as NASA's *"a self-sustaining chemical system capable of Darwinian evolution"* are useful instruments; they fail only when promoted to an anchor.
- Not that the missing cut does not exist. Unreachable at this budget is a verdict, not a silence (Fig 13 :134: *"Unreachable is a verdict here, not a silence"*).
- Not `grounded`. No measurement at the disputed slot has settled the cut.

## 4. What would settle it: the ground, not agreement

From *The Hard Problem is About Source* (Isaiah N, 2026): *"An instrument that can't distinguish these cases demonstrates its own inadequacy, it cannot claim anything substantial about what is being measured or what causes it."* What is owed is a discriminator, not another argument. Concretely:

1. **Convergent-cut test:** a protocol returning one stable label per candidate system across instruments and frames, with residual trending to zero. This fires refuter 1 of `01` §4.
2. **Source-discriminating instrument:** a measurement that separates a chemical self-maintaining network from a hereditary evolving lineage at the same slot, without referring to a definition. Fig 6 :32-33: *"An instrument is not built from a description. It is improved from a worse instrument already returning something."*
3. **Rock test:** a stratigraphic or chemical marker read identically by all frames that discriminates prebiotic from living states. This fires refuter 3.
4. **Standing falsifiers from `01` §4:** a moving ground that still settles; an intrinsic, habitat-independent life property appearing at one invariant time; a non-directional boundary with no loss.

A definition that *"cannot validate itself"* (*The Recursive Self-Improvement Trap*, 2026) is not such an instrument.

## 6. One-paragraph version for outreach

> There is no clear scientific boundary between prebiotic chemistry and the first life in the sense the question asks for, and that is a figure-grounded result rather than a agnostic shrug. The classification domain has no anchor: every definition of life moves when a virus, a prion, or a synthetic cell is pushed against it, and a thing that moves when pushed is not an anchor. What the framework's boundary figure actually licenses is a membrane, *"not a wall: a filter with a direction"*, so what crosses is a reusable method (self-maintenance, heredity) with information lost in the crossing. "Alive" is a label applied afterwards to a lineage that met its ground, and the first living unit is level-relative, not an absolute bottom. The question is also a union of three separate questions, chemistry's capability, the objective that counts as crossing, and access to the intermediates, which must be unbundled before they are searched. The rock record is overprinted, so the historical cut is unreachable at this budget, not proved absent. What the answer offers is the shape a boundary would have to have, five falsifiers written in advance, and the instruments that would close it. The cut stays open until the ground settles it.
