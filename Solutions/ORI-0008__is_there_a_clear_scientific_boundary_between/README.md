## What this package decides, in effect terms

The question asks for a cut C such that every candidate slot s below C is "prebiotic chemistry" and every slot above C is "the first life," with the same label returned by every instrument. The derivation asks what happens when that cut is treated as an ordinary, bindable term and ranged against the figures (Formula "Direction"; Fig 5 :62-66). The verdict, kept separate per domain, is that no such cut is reachable from what the domains hold: the classification domain has no nameable anchor, the rock-record domain is lossy and unreachable at this budget, and the transition itself is a union compound for which the figures license a directional, lossy membrane rather than a wall. That is `REFUTED` for the partition as written and `gap`-adjacent for the missing discriminator, not a silent non-answer.

## What is deliberately not here

No personal emails of named individuals. No ranking of people by how likely they are to agree. No bulk-send plan. Selection for receptiveness manufactures the correlation that disqualifies a verdict (Seat Map :163: *"High correlation is disqualifying for a verdict and efficient for a convention"*), and asking for trust before a check spends the only independence an outside frame contributes (Fig 10 :42: *"A verifier that can reconstruct the claim cannot verify it."*). The public, opt-in venues in `03_public_contact_points.csv` are contact-cost routes only; they carry no verdict weight.
