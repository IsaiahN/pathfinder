## What in the wider literature bears on this question

1. **Reachability is a live physics statement, not only a framework claim.** Brown et al. 2015 note that the Wheeler-DeWitt patch *"is not a causal patch so no single observer is able to monitor all of W; this is consistent with the complexity not being a conventional quantum mechanical observable."* This corroborates F1: there is no exterior instrument that returns the interior quantity.

2. **The conjectures are conjectures.** Brown et al. 2015: *"This duality is a conjecture but it has passed a number of tests."* Susskind 2018: *"Of course this latter point is a conjecture; it was not derived from first-principles."* Both corroborate F2's `candidate` status. They are one evidence pool (the same authors and program), so this is not two independent witnesses.

3. **CV and CA differ in behaviour.** Fu et al. 2018 find coefficients of opposite sign, temperature and moduli independent, and conclude the gate set cannot be local. This is the nearest available discriminator between the two proposals (F3). It is a calculation, not an instrument, and its authors do not overlap with the Brown/Susskind pool.

4. **Complexity is defined against a convention.** Susskind 2018 distinguishes two metrics on state space and notes that relative complexity depends on an allowed gate set, with *"additive and multiplicative"* ambiguity. Brown et al. 2015 call the factor of π *"a convention to normalize the complexity."* This corroborates F2's point that the gate set is not an anchor for the interior.

5. **There is a physical rate bound.** Lloyd 2000: *"the rate at which a computer can compute is limited by its energy."* Brown et al. 2015 connect this to *"the saturation of Lloyd's conjectured bound"*. This is a low-overlap witness (physics of computation rather than holography) that supports treating growth as a rate-limited route, F4.

6. **The plateau is not final.** Susskind 2018 discusses *"periods of non-increasing complexity which typically only occur after an exponentially long time"* and firewalls. This matches Fig 7's no-last-room claim (F6). It shares the Brown/Susskind evidence pool.

2. **Stage 2, opt-in only.** If they are curious, offer the three local framework extracts.
