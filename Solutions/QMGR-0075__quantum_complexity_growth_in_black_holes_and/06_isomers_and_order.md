### Q2 - What is the structure of "growth"?

- Fig 12 :111: *"A vocabulary without bonds can name a state and cannot name a route to one."*
- Fig 12 :112: *"A plan is a sequence. A goal is a comparison. Progress is a subtraction. A refutation is a negation, and that one is not a bond."*
- Complexity growth is a sequence of gates; interior growth is a changing spatial state. The relation between them needs a bond, and the bond is what a list of ingredients cannot record (Fig 12 :5: *"A set of ingredients is not one. The bond is the rest of the information, and it is the half nothing was recording."*).
- The linear-then-plateau shape is a sequence with a threshold. Fig 12 :112 gives → for the route and ⋛ for the threshold; progress across the threshold is a subtraction.
- Physics check, not a figure line: Susskind 2018 describes complexity growing linearly until a sudden transition to *"complexity equilibrium"* after an exponential time, then recurring on a doubly exponential time. This matches the route-with-threshold shape. Mark `specified`, unmeasured at the ground.

### Q3 - Are CV and CA isomers?

- Fig 12 :117: *"isomer. Same atoms, different bond, different substance. The difference is invisible in the formula and decisive in the behaviour."*
- Fig 12 :123: *"functional group. A substructure whose presence fixes the outcome. The group says what kind; the qualifier says which member."*
- Both proposals take the same boundary state and the same interior region class. They differ in which bulk substructure carries the quantity: CV uses a maximal spacelike slice, CA uses the action of the Wheeler-DeWitt patch. Their growth rates and prefactors differ in the literature, which fits Fig 12's *"decisive in the behaviour"*.
- **Isomer reading (strong):** both are written over a shared bulk atom set, differing only in arrangement. Then they are isomers.
- **Functional-group reading (weak):** they share only the boundary preparation and the interior, so they are members of one `state → structure → state` family with different substructures in the slot.
- Brown et al. 2015 say CA *"subsumes a previous conjecture that characterized the size of the black hole interior by its spatial volume"*; Fu et al. 2018 find coefficients that differ in sign and are independent of temperature and moduli. Neither source derives the isomer claim; both leave it testable.
- **Falsifier, written before the run:** if either proposal can be written without an atom the other requires, or if they are shown to be the same term under Fig 12's bond test, the isomer claim fails and the functional-group reading stands instead.

### Q4 - What is the order of dependency?

- Fig 3 :20: *"The chain is ordered by dependency, not by importance. Each link consumes what the one above produces, so effort spent above the break arrives at a step that cannot use it."*
- Fig 3 :25-27: the middle link, having terms for what a situation offers and being able to say what would count as progress, has neither obvious tests nor obvious instruments.
- FORMULA step 2: refuse before mint. The wrong frame (naming the source from a reading) must be refused before a term is minted.
- Fig 12 :104: *"A settled molecule becomes an atom for whatever composes over it. So irreducible is a fact about a level and never about the world"*. Settle the boundary computational claim, then settle the geometric claim, then assess the binding as a second-order composition.
- Fig 9 :27-28: *"describe it before you go looking, or any frame you pick will seem to fit."* Describe the residual before picking CV or CA.

## CONCLUSION

The growth relation is a **route with a threshold**, not an ingredient list: → for the sequence of steps, ⋛ for the threshold at equilibrium, and a subtraction for progress. CV and CA are **isomers** over the shared bulk atom set in the strong reading, or members of one **functional group** in the weak reading; the strong claim has a stated falsifier and is `specified`, unmeasured. The dependency order is: describe the residual, refuse the wrong frame, settle each first-order side, then assess the binding. This rescues what is defensible in the complexity-growth program without asserting that the framework names the geometric source. The binding `C = A/πℏ` remains a candidate with provenance owed.
