# Outreach draft: validate the shape first, then decide about the framework

**Use:** one-to-one, or as a cover letter attached to a submission through the public venues in `03_public_contact_points.csv`. **Not** for bulk send.

**Hard precondition:** do not send until the findings in `01_figure_grounded_audit.md` are stated honestly. The output must survive checking or the two-stage offer collapses on contact.

---

**Subject:** Check one shape claim before you decide whether the framework is worth reading

> Hi [name],
>
> I have one specific claim I would like you to check against what you already know. I am not asking you to read the framework first, and I am not asking you to agree with it. If the claim does not survive, there is nothing else to look at.
>
> **The claim (`specified`, not `grounded`):** the region behind a black hole horizon is causally unreachable from outside, so any quantity proposed as its dual enters the geometric description as an import, not as something derived there. The shape the framework can state is this: a quantity monotone in boundary time is bonded to a quantity monotone in interior time, and the growth is a route with a threshold, not an ingredient list. Under the framework's bond rule, the two existing proposals, complexity = volume and complexity = action, stand to each other as isomers or at least members of one functional group: same boundary preparation, different bulk substructure carrying the quantity.
>
> **What I am not claiming:** that the framework derives `C = A/πℏ` or `C ~ V/Gℓ`. It returns a shape, not a name. The gate set and reference state are declared conventions inside the computational frame; they settle what complexity means there, not what the interior is. The coefficient π is a normalisation convention in the literature. I am not claiming an exterior observation of the interior, and I am not claiming this is a theory of everything.
>
> **What would refute the shape claim:**
> 1. an exterior instrument reads the interior's growth directly, without importing a new primitive;
> 2. a derivation inside one frame alone yields the other's growth rate including its coefficient;
> 3. complexity = volume and complexity = action are shown to be the same term under the bond test, or one requires an atom the other does not;
> 4. the growth is shown to be a state requiring no sequence bond.
>
> I wrote these down before sending this. If any one holds, I will retract the affected claim. A one-line "this fails because..." is more useful to me than agreement. If you would rather not engage, no reply is needed.
>
> **If it survives, and only then:** the sources are below. The figures are the authority, not my summary of them.
> - Verbatim figure text with line numbers (local framework extract): `FRAMEWORK/reference/03-FIGURES_TEXT.md`
> - Canonical usage rule (local framework extract): `FRAMEWORK/reference/FIGURES_README_canonical.md`
> - The eight-step loop (local framework extract): `FRAMEWORK/figures/THE_FORMULA.md`
>
> **If you want to use the machinery:** the formula is in the local extract. Run it on a problem you know well and can check against a ground. Write down what would refute your result *before* you run it, and state the mode of what you get. A reported failure is more useful to me than a reported success.
>
> - [name]
