## The residual in effect terms

The exterior of a black hole equilibrates on the scrambling time, while the interior keeps growing for an exponentially long time. Quantum computational complexity has been proposed as the counterpart of that growth. The residual is the binding: a quantity defined relative to a gate set and a reference state (a convention inside a computational frame) is asserted to be identical to a geometric quantity deep behind a horizon (a region no exterior instrument reads). Option A asserts the identity as written. Option B keeps the figure-derived shape, records the binding as an import that needs provenance and a shadow test, and leaves the slot open until a ground settles it. A spends the independence of the people asked to check it; B sends a checkable shape with a refuter written in advance.

## What is deliberately not here

- No personal emails of named individuals.
- No ranking of people by how likely they are to agree.
- No bulk-send plan.

Three reasons, in the figures' own words:

1. A room does not add an anchor. Fig 7 :35: *"A room's agreement is variation, not a verdict. Selection between rooms is where the ground votes."*
2. Selecting for receptiveness manufactures the disqualifying correlation. Fig 2 :40: *"disqualifying for a verdict: agreement about the world is worthless when the agreeing frames share an evidence pool"*.
3. Asking someone to trust the framework before they check spends the only thing they contribute. Fig 10 :42: *"A verifier that can reconstruct the claim cannot verify it."*
