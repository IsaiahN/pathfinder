## 0. The claim under audit

> In effect terms: the exterior of a black hole reaches equilibrium quickly, while the region behind the horizon keeps growing for an exponentially long time. Quantum computational complexity, defined as the least number of gates from an allowed set that prepare a boundary state from a reference state, has been proposed as the dual of that interior growth. Two concrete bindings exist: complexity proportional to the volume of a maximal slice behind the horizon (CV), and complexity equal to the action of the Wheeler-DeWitt patch divided by πℏ (CA).

The question asks what the framework can legitimately say about that growth and its relation to the interior. Two options:

- **A.** Assert the binding as an identity, for example `C = A/πℏ`, and present it as a result about the interior.
- **B.** Keep the figure-derived shape, record the binding as an import that still owes a source gate and a shadow test, and leave the slot open until a ground settles it.

What each leads to: A states a name where the framework returns a shape, applies a convention (the gate set) as if it were an anchor, and spends the independence of anyone asked to endorse it. B sends a checkable shape, written refuters, and a lineage that a later reader can audit.

## 3. Findings

### F1 - The interior is unreachable from outside; only import moves the wall (decisive)

Fig 6 :23: *"No frame escapes its own closure. The bound moves only on contact with a frame whose closure differs."* Fig 6 :14 names *"the only operator that moves the wall"*, which is IMPORT. Fig 8 :32: *"Closure is idempotent. Composing inside a closed set never leaves it, so no search, however long, adds an atom."* Fig 6 :34-35: *"the search for a proto-instrument is a search for one that is already there."* Fig 8 :39: *"So a search that finds nothing is never a proof of absence."*

From the exterior frame there is no instrument that returns the interior's growth. Fig 6 :27: *"Which is why some unreachability is depth and some is genuine absence, and from inside the frame the two look the same."* So the interior's growth is not something the boundary frame failed to find; it is beyond the wall of that frame. Complexity can enter the geometric frame only as an import, and the price of a wrong import is high (Fig 6 :24: *"Import from a live peer is debited against that frame's independence."*).

**Verdict: `specified`, structural, triangulated** across Fig 6 and Fig 8 (low overlap: wall mechanics versus union surplus). World corroboration: Brown et al. 2015 write that the Wheeler-DeWitt patch *"is not a causal patch so no single observer is able to monitor all of W; this is consistent with the complexity not being a conventional quantum mechanical observable."* That is a physics statement of the same reachability limit. **Refuter:** exhibit an exterior instrument that reads the interior's growth without importing a new primitive, or show that the wall is not a wall for this quantity.

### F2 - The binding is a name where the framework returns a shape

Fig 9 :32: *"you get a kind of thing, never the answer itself"*. Fig 13 :134-135: *"Unreachable is a verdict here, not a silence"* and *"It cannot say which primitive is missing, because naming it would mean already holding it."* Fig 8 :53: *"Residual first, frame second."* The canonical rule is the same: the framework returns *"what a missing thing would have to look like (a shape, not a name)"* (`FIGURES_README_canonical.md`, local).

A statement of the form `C = A/πℏ` names a specific geometric object as the source of a specific computational reading. The framework can state the shape: a quantity monotone in boundary time is bonded to a quantity monotone in interior time, and the bond is what is owed. It cannot derive which geometric object occupies the slot. That requires a ground.

**Verdict: `specified`, triangulated** across Fig 9, Fig 13, and the canonical README. World corroboration: Susskind 2018 writes *"Of course this latter point is a conjecture; it was not derived from first-principles"*; Brown et al. 2015 write *"This duality is a conjecture but it has passed a number of tests."* Both are exactly the `candidate` status the figures require. **Refuter:** a derivation from within one frame that yields the other's growth rate including its coefficient, without an import.

### F3 - CV and CA are isomers over the bulk atom set

Fig 12 :117: *"isomer. Same atoms, different bond, different substance. The difference is invisible in the formula and decisive in the behaviour."* Fig 12 :123: *"functional group. A substructure whose presence fixes the outcome. The group says what kind; the qualifier says which member."* Fig 12 :92: *"Symmetry is what decides whether an arrangement is a new thing."*

Both conjectures take the same boundary state as input and differ in which bulk substructure carries the quantity: a maximal spacelike slice (CV) versus the action of the whole Wheeler-DeWitt patch (CA). Under Fig 12's test they are **not two spellings of one claim**; they are different substances whose difference is carried entirely by the bond. As the conjectures are usually written they share the boundary preparation and the interior, so the isomer reading is the strong one; the weaker claim is that they are members of one functional group, a `state → structure → state` arrangement with different substructures in the slot.

**Verdict: `specified`, single-source** for the figure definition (Fig 12), with the physics-facing check still unmeasured. World corroboration: Brown et al. 2015 state that the action proposal *"subsumes a previous conjecture that characterized the size of the black hole interior by its spatial volume"*; Fu et al. 2018 find the coefficients of CV and CA independent of temperature and moduli and conclude that *"any CFT gate set defining either complexity cannot be local"*, which constrains both proposals at once. **Refuter:** show the two are the same term under Fig 12's bond test, or that one requires an atom the other does not (which leaves the functional-group reading standing instead).

### F4 - Growth is a route; the interior is a state; the bond is the missing half

Fig 12 :5: *"A set of ingredients is not one. The bond is the rest of the information, and it is the half nothing was recording."* Fig 12 :111: *"A vocabulary without bonds can name a state and cannot name a route to one."* Fig 12 :112: *"A plan is a sequence. A goal is a comparison. Progress is a subtraction."*

A list containing the boundary state, the gate set, the horizon, and the interior volume cannot express their growth relation. A sequence bond (→) and a threshold (⋛) are needed, and Fig 12 prices them: a bond carries information the operands do not. The observed shape, linear growth for an exponentially long time followed by a sudden plateau at complexity equilibrium, is a route with a threshold, not an ingredient list.

**Verdict: `general` structural, triangulated** with Fig 12 :112 and Fig 7 (no last room). **Refuter:** show the growth can be written as a state with no sequence bond, or that the bond is symmetric where Fig 12 says the order matters.

### F5 - The middle link has no instrument (why the question is open)

Fig 3 :25-26: *"The link between them, having terms for what a situation offers and being able to say what would count as progress, has neither, so it attracts the least attention and the fewest instruments."* Fig 3 :27: *"A link with no instrument is not a link that works. It is a link nobody has looked at."*

This question lives exactly at that link. "What the interior offers" is the geometric side; "what counts as progress" is complexity. Both sides have obvious failures and obvious tests in their own fields (Fig 3 :25: *"Perception has obvious failures and obvious tests. Planning has obvious failures and obvious tests."*), but the link between them has no instrument. The absence of a measurement here is therefore not a defect in the proposal; it is the structural signature of a middle link.

**Verdict: `specified`, single-source** (Fig 3), corroborated by Brown et al. 2015 calling complexity *"not a conventional quantum mechanical observable"*. **Refuter:** name an instrument that measures the growth relation at the link, then the finding is dissolved rather than refuted.

### F6 - No last room: the plateau is not an answer

Fig 7 :6 names *"the limit: no last room"*; Fig 7 :30 says *"no finite number of them ever arrives"*; Fig 7 :35 says *"A room's agreement is variation, not a verdict. Selection between rooms is where the ground votes."* Fig 13 :118: *"infinite effort inside a finite reach."*

Complexity equilibrium after an exponentially long time, and recurrence after a still longer time, are not terminal rooms. Each closes part of the gap and none arrives. This bars presenting the plateau as the final answer about the interior.

**Verdict: `general` structural, triangulated** across Fig 7 and Fig 13. **Refuter:** exhibit a terminal room in the sense the figures forbid, that is a handoff after which nothing further can be asked.

### F7 - Provenance decides, not agreement

Fig 8 :50-52: *"Convergent derivation and adopted import look identical in the contents. Only the record of where each came from separates them"*. Fig 2 :35: *"Alignment is triangulation, not negotiation. A mirror cannot correct you."* Fig 2 :42: *"Buy independence in proportion to the cost of being wrong."* Fig 10 :52: *"Bring a characterised residual, never the source."*

A list of people selected for how likely they are to agree with the conjecture produces a convention, not corroboration. The legitimate ask gives the claim a budget to be tried and records the verdict before showing the framework.

**Verdict: `specified`, triangulated** across Fig 8, Fig 2, Fig 10. **Refuter:** show that agreement among readers sharing one evidence pool can settle a physics question. No source read here supports that.

### F8 - Low overlap is the reason to join the two frames, not a licence to name

Fig 8 :45: *"Two frames together reach what neither reaches alone."* Fig 8 :47: *"Independence is one quantity read twice."* Fig 2 :31-32: *"Independence, between two frames, is how little they draw on the same evidence"* and *"Independence is what makes agreement mean something."*

The computational frame (gates, reference state, circuit depth) and the geometric frame (slices, patches, action) draw on almost no common evidence. Their union can therefore have a surplus (Fig 8 :19: *"closures differ, so a surplus exists"*). But Fig 8 :21: *"nothing was aimed; the union is mute"* until a residual is described. The surplus is where the closures differ, so it lies in the **relation**, not in a name carried from one frame into the other.

**Verdict: `specified`, triangulated** across Fig 8 and Fig 2. **Refuter:** show the two frames have identical closures for this quantity, in which case their union adds nothing.

## 4. Pre-registered refuter

Required before any outreach, per the seat rule that *"a falsifier written before the run is a design instrument, not only an honesty device."*

The shape claim is refuted if any of the following holds:

1. An exterior instrument is shown to read the interior's growth directly, without importing a new primitive (breaks F1).
2. A derivation inside one frame alone, boundary complexity or interior geometry, yields the other's growth rate including its coefficient, with no import (breaks F2 and F8).
3. CV and CA are shown to be the same term under Fig 12's bond test, or one shown to require an atom the other does not (breaks F3 and leaves the functional-group reading standing).
4. The growth is shown to be a state requiring no sequence bond (breaks F4).
5. A figure re-census admits a direct binding of a computational quantity to a geometric quantity with no structure in the slot (breaks the cross-kind composition rule).

If any fires, the affected finding is withdrawn and the shape is redrawn. Until one is run, no part of this may be labelled `grounded`.
