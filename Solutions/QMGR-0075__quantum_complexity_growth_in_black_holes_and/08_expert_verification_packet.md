# Expert verification packet: Quantum complexity growth in black holes and its relation to the interior of the horizon

**What this is:** a claim in a form a specialist can check. Mode, derivation and refuters are stated before the conclusion, so a reader knows what to look for and what would settle it.

**Mode: `specified` on the shape claims, `general` on the structural claims. Not `grounded`.** No Planck-scale measurement is claimed. This is a shape and a route, not a result. Simulated derivation, not a ground reading.

## 1. The claim, in one paragraph

The region behind a black hole horizon is causally unreachable from outside, so any quantity proposed as its dual enters the geometric description as an import with provenance owed. The framework can state this shape: a quantity monotone in boundary time is bonded to a quantity monotone in interior time, and the growth is a route with a threshold, not an ingredient list. Under the framework's bond rule, complexity = volume and complexity = action stand to each other as isomers, or at least members of one functional group, over the shared boundary state. The framework does not name which geometric quantity occupies the slot. What it offers is the shape any candidate answer must have, falsifiers written in advance, and the kind of instrument that would close it.

## 2. Three checkable sub-claims

### C1 - framework-internal, checkable by reading Figure 6 and Figure 8

- **Statement:** from the exterior frame the interior is unreachable; only an import moves that wall, and CV or CA is such an import, with a source gate and a shadow test still owed.
- **Source, verbatim:** Fig 6 :23 - *"No frame escapes its own closure. The bound moves only on contact with a frame whose closure differs."* Fig 6 :14 - *"the only operator that moves the wall"*. Fig 8 :32 - *"Closure is idempotent. Composing inside a closed set never leaves it, so no search, however long, adds an atom."* Fig 8 :39 - *"So a search that finds nothing is never a proof of absence."*
- **Physics check:** Brown et al. 2015 state that the Wheeler-DeWitt patch *"is not a causal patch so no single observer is able to monitor all of W; this is consistent with the complexity not being a conventional quantum mechanical observable."*
- **Refuter:** exhibit an exterior instrument that reads the interior's growth directly without importing a new primitive, or show the wall does not apply to this quantity.

### C2 - physics-facing, checkable by a specialist against the two proposals

- **Statement:** CV and CA are isomers over the shared bulk atom set (strong reading), or at least members of one functional group (weak reading): same boundary preparation, different bulk substructure carrying the quantity.
- **Source, verbatim:** Fig 12 :117 - *"isomer. Same atoms, different bond, different substance. The difference is invisible in the formula and decisive in the behaviour."* Fig 12 :123 - *"functional group. A substructure whose presence fixes the outcome. The group says what kind; the qualifier says which member."*
- **What to check:** is the characterisation faithful? CV uses a maximal spacelike slice anchored at the boundary time; CA uses the action of the Wheeler-DeWitt patch. Brown et al. 2015 say CA *"subsumes a previous conjecture that characterized the size of the black hole interior by its spatial volume."* Fu et al. 2018 find coefficients of opposite sign that are temperature and moduli independent. Do those differences make them different substances in Fig 12's sense, or one quantity under two regularisations?
- **Refuter:** show they are the same term under Fig 12's bond test, or that one requires an atom the other does not (which leaves the functional-group claim standing).

### C3 - framework-internal derivation

- **Statement:** growth is a route with a threshold, not an ingredient list; the framework cannot name the geometric source.
- **Source, verbatim:** Fig 12 :111 - *"A vocabulary without bonds can name a state and cannot name a route to one."* Fig 12 :112 - *"A plan is a sequence. A goal is a comparison. Progress is a subtraction."* Fig 9 :32 - *"you get a kind of thing, never the answer itself"*. Fig 13 :134 - *"Unreachable is a verdict here, not a silence"*.
- **What to check:** Susskind 2018 describes linear growth followed by a sudden transition to complexity equilibrium after an exponentially long time. Is that a sequence with a threshold, as claimed, or can it be written as a state with no bond? The gate set is a declared convention in the literature; does that make an anchor for the interior? The figures say no (Fig 2 :36: an anchor is legitimate when the question is constitutively about it, and this question is about the interior, not about a gate set).
- **Refuter:** a state-only writing of the growth that needs no sequence bond, or a derivation from the gate-set convention alone that fixes the interior quantity.

## 3. What is explicitly not claimed

- Not that `C = A/πℏ` or `C ~ V/Gℓ` is derived. Both are candidates with provenance owed (Fig 8 :50-52: *"Convergent derivation and adopted import look identical in the contents. Only the record of where each came from separates them"*).
- Not that the gate set or the factor π is an anchor. They are conventions inside the computational frame; they settle complexity there, not the interior (Fig 2 :36).
- Not an exterior observation of the interior, and no measurement is claimed.
- Not a theory of everything. Fig 7 :6: *"the limit: no last room"*; Fig 7 :30: *"no finite number of them ever arrives"*.
- Not `grounded`. Nothing here is asserted as settled by experiment.
- Not a claim that the framework has named the slot. It returns a shape, not a name.

## 5. What would settle it
