## 1. A shape is a constraint, not a filler

The canonical rule: the framework returns *"what a missing thing would have to look like (a shape, not a name)"* (`FIGURES_README_canonical.md`, local). Fig 9 :32: *"you get a kind of thing, never the answer itself"*. Fig 13 :134-135: *"Unreachable is a verdict here, not a silence"* and *"It cannot say which primitive is missing, because naming it would mean already holding it."*

*The Library of Babel*: *"You can cut your search short if the set of books returned is small and descriptive enough that it provides a general shape of the answer that satisfies your question."*

So a shape is the narrowed form of an answer you can state before you can name it. It constrains; it does not fill. For this question the shape is: a monotone growth quantity in the boundary frame is bonded to a monotone growth quantity behind the horizon, and the bond is what the claim owes. `Complexity = A/πℏ` is a name. The shape leaves the geometric source marked `???` until a ground settles it.

## 2. How a shape is obtained

- *Library of Babel*, on proximity: *"If you don't know the right terms to search for, you will have to use the existing vocabulary you do know that is closest to the words that you wish to describe and grasp the shape of."* And the recursion: *"With each new term gained, you are able to narrow the space by definition and deepen your vocabulary."*
- Fig 6 :30: *"The description travels. Fails by being sharpened into terms only we can read. Cast it in effect terms."* Fig 9 :30-31: *"your words are borrowed too, so describe the effect rather than the cause"*.
- *Decoding Tacit Knowledge*: *"When contact isn't possible, the best you can send is a plain description of what you'd expect to see, because anyone can check that against the world."* The interior is exactly a case where contact is not possible from outside.
- *Library of Babel*, on stopping: *"You will know that you are done because the finish line will also be the starting place, as there is nowhere else to search and nothing else that can describe the answer better."* The plateau of complexity growth is not that finish line; Fig 7 :6 says there is no last room.

## 3. What licenses a name

Two things, both outside the shape:

1. **The ground settles it.** FORMULA step 5: *"the ground settles it. Verified only by what pays. A gate is not the ground."* The canonical README: the framework *"stops at the limit of what the available instruments can distinguish, and records the lineage so the stop is honest."* For this question, an instrument that reads the interior growth from outside would name the slot. None was read this session.
2. **A provenanced import from a frame whose closure differs** (Fig 6 :23, Fig 8 :50-52), with a source gate and a shadow test. *They're Scanning Books When They Should Be Studying Nature*: *"The real import is not a new term in the existing space. It is a new dimension of the space."* And: *"Declaring the primitives does not make them neutral. It makes the choice visible."*

The gate set is such a visible choice. Complexity is defined relative to it; Susskind 2018 calls the ambiguity *"additive and multiplicative"* and says that *"A basic assumption is that there a set of definitions of quantum complexity that exhibit a universal behavior up to additive and multiplicative ambiguities."* An assumption is declared provenance, not a ground result. That is why the coefficient in `C = A/πℏ` is a *"convention to normalize the complexity"* (Brown et al. 2015), not a measurement.

## 4. The ground and the source problem

*The Hard Problem is About Source* gives the mechanism. An instrument that detects green *"cannot comment on the source."* Several causes give one reading. *"If several paths create the same behavior, then behavior is a projection with lossy information"* and *"An instrument that can't distinguish these cases demonstrates its own inadequacy."* It was the spectrometer, not an argument, that resolved it.

The same error is available here. From outside one reads a boundary state and a clock, then names the source of the growth as a maximal slice or a Wheeler-DeWitt action. Those are two candidates for one reading, and the reading does not decide between them. Fu et al. 2018 show that the coefficients of CV and CA differ in sign and are independent of temperature and moduli, which is precisely a case where the reading fails to discriminate among sources. What is owed is a discriminator, not a name.

## 5. Answer to the shape question

**What licenses a particular geometric quantity in the slot?** Nothing in the framework. The framework returns a shape. A particular quantity is licensed only by the ground settling a minted term, or by a provenanced import with a shadow test. Until one of those, the name is the searcher's own contribution and must be recorded as such.

**Does treating `C = A/πℏ` as a result commit the forbidden error?** Yes, in `specified` mode. It names the source of a reading where several sources could give the reading, with no instrument that discriminates among them. The correct form stays: a monotone growth quantity bonded across the horizon, with the geometric source marked `???`.

### Correction to my own §1 and §3 (see `06_isomers_and_order.md` Q1)

I first wrote the shape-not-a-name rule as if it forbade naming any occupied slot. The canonical phrase is about a **missing** primitive, one not held in Γ. In the exemplar's GR/QM case, Gradient and Spectrum are among the six, so naming one is a binding, not the invention of a new member. Here the parallel is weaker and must be stated honestly: complexity is **not** among the six, so `complexity` itself is a computational-frame term that would have to be imported into the geometric frame. Naming a geometric source for it is therefore not forbidden by the shape-not-a-name rule in the same way; it is an import with provenance owed (Fig 6 :23, Fig 8 :50-52). Both claims are kept: the shape rule governs a missing primitive, and the import rule governs this binding. Neither is silently erased.

## 6. Consequences for contact

**Prestige is not action.** *The Recursive Self-Improvement Trap*: *"Prestige must never be allowed to validate an action directly."* And: *"Prestige can fund an action. It can give an action a budget to be tried. But it cannot settle an action. Only the ground can settle it."* A list of famous believers is the named collapse, not corroboration. The legitimate ask is a budget to be tried and a refuter to fire.

**My own audit is a simulation, not a ground reading.** Same essay: *"LLMs can quickly simulate an approximate answer/output"* and *"the truly testable output would need to be derived from the real architecture or working the formula yourself."* So `01` is a simulated derivation checked for internal consistency against the figures. It is not a test of the claim and cannot settle anything it reports on.
