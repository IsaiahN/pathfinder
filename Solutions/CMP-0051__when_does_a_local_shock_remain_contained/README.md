## What this folder is

A figure-grounded, derive-first solution in the Pathfinder format. It derives what the Tether figures say about the containment-versus-cascade question, records a pre-registered refuter, adds WATSON world corroboration from shock-containment and network-resilience research, and provides a one-to-one expert verification packet. It is a **simulated derivation checked for internal consistency against the figures**, not a ground reading, so it cannot settle the question it reports on.

## The residual in one line

A shock is a per-slot residual R_s. It stays contained when that residual trends down to zero as it is composed outward through contact; it cascades when the residual persists and does not trend down, so that other slots go live. The figures decide the **shape** of that distinction (four separate conditions, a union not a single threshold), not the number.

## Key verdict (from the figures)

- **Per slot, never aggregate.** Fig 1 :19 "The residual is indexed by object slot, not aggregated." A global average hides the live slot.
- **Cascade equals a compounding remainder.** Fig 13 :31-32 "a remainder compounding through a chain that amplifies is chaos"; Fig 5 :59-60 "a term whose residual PERSISTS AND DOES NOT TREND DOWN".
- **Containment is a directional filter, not a wall.** Fig 4 :21 "Not a wall: a filter with a direction." (analogue, strain named in `01` and `11`).
- **Correlated failure is not contagion.** Fig 2 :19-21 "they never talk, and still agree"; independence decides (Fig 2 :31-32).
- **The sign of the loop decides.** Fig 12 :121 "The sign of the loop decides whether it closes".
- **It is a union of conditions, not one lever.** Fig 9 :55 "A bundled problem is usually a union of overlapping conditions, not a list of separate ones."

Verdict per domain is kept separate and never averaged. Triangulated: D1 (topology/contact), D3 (correlation), D4 (feedback sign). Single-source with named strain: D2 (boundary as membrane analogue). No numeric threshold is derived; that belongs to the ground.

## What is deliberately not here

- No personal emails of named individuals.
- No ranking of people by how likely they are to agree.
- No bulk-send plan.

Three figure-grounded reasons: a room is not an anchor (Fig 2 :35 "Alignment is triangulation, not negotiation. A mirror cannot correct you."); selecting for agreement manufactures the disqualifying correlation (Fig 2 :40 "agreement about the world is worthless when the agreeing frames share an evidence pool"); and a verifier that holds the interiors cannot verify (Fig 10 :42 "A verifier that can reconstruct the claim cannot verify it.").
