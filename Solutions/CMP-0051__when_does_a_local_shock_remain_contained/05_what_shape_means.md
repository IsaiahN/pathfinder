## 1. A shape is a constraint, not a filler

The figures README states the limit: the framework returns "what a missing thing would have to look like (a shape, not a name)". Fig 9 :32 "you get a kind of thing, never the answer itself." Fig 13 :134-135 "Unreachable is a verdict here, not a silence, It cannot say which primitive is missing, because naming it would mean already holding it."

From *The Library of Babel*: "You can cut your search short if the set of books returned is small and descriptive enough that it provides a general shape of the answer that satisfies your question."

For this question the distinction is concrete:

- **shape:** "the decision turns on the sign of the per-slot residual as it is composed outward, under four separable conditions";
- **name (not given by the figures):** "the critical infection rate is 0.184 for this network", "R0 = 3.2", "the cascade threshold is at degree 2.445."

A shape constrains what any acceptable answer must look like. It does not fill the slot with a number. The number is ground work, measured with an instrument in a particular network.

## 2. How a shape is obtained

- *Library of Babel*: use the vocabulary you already have that is closest to the thing, look for recurring new terms that overlap or correlate with it, recurse. "With each new term gained, you are able to narrow the space by definition and deepen your vocabulary." For this question the bootstrapped vocabulary was: residual, slot, threshold, contact, filter, common cause, feedback sign.
- Fig 6 :30 "The description travels. Fails by being sharpened into terms only we can read. Cast it in effect terms." Fig 9 :30-31 "your words are borrowed too, so describe the effect rather than the cause." This is why the residual was stated in effect terms (what R_s does step by step), not in domain jargon (SIS, bond percolation, DebtRank).
- *Decoding Tacit Knowledge*: "When contact isn't possible, the best you can send is a plain description of what you'd expect to see, because anyone can check that against the world." That is exactly the outreach discipline in `02_outreach_email.md`.
- *Library of Babel* on stopping: the answer is reached when "the finish line will also be the starting place", and the paradox is that by then you have "composed the answer through the process of locating it." Applied here: after the four-condition census, the sought-for shape and the derived shape coincide, which is the signal to stop and record the lineage.

## 3. What licenses a name (a number, here)

Only two things, both outside the shape itself:

1. **The ground settles it.** Formula step 5: "the ground settles it. Verified only by what pays. A gate is not the ground." The README: the framework "stops at the limit of what the available instruments can distinguish, and records the lineage so the stop is honest." A measured cascade footprint in a named network would license the number.
2. **A provenanced import from a frame whose closure differs** (Fig 6 :23 "No frame escapes its own closure. The bound moves only on contact with a frame whose closure differs"; Fig 8 :50 "But shared atoms are not the signature"), with a source gate and a shadow test. Importing the finance literature's R0 or percolation threshold into the Tether is a binding from another frame, not a derivation, and it must be recorded as such.

*They're Scanning Books* sharpens this: "The real import is not a new term in the existing space. It is a new dimension of the space." And: "A minimum viable set of six terms is a human's careful guess at reality's compression axes; another sensorium's minimum set need not be those six." Also: "Declaring the primitives does not make them neutral. It makes the choice visible."

So the six-term substrate is a visible choice, not nature's own list. Naming a numeric threshold as *the* answer would turn a visible choice into an invisible one.

## 4. Why a pooled measurement is not a discriminator

*The Hard Problem is About Source* gives the mechanism. Metamerism: an instrument reads green and "the instrument is speaking only about what it measured, it cannot comment on the source." Several sources can give the same reading. "If several paths create the same behavior, then behavior is a projection with lossy information (it cannot define consciousness in full)." And: "It was proven wrong not by argument, but by the arrival of a better instrument."

Applied to this question: a single observation that many nodes failed at once is a reading, not a source. Transmission (contagion) and common cause (correlated failure) can produce the same reading. What is owed is a discriminating instrument, two low-overlap measurements (Fig 8 :45 "Two frames together reach what neither reaches alone"), not a name assigned from the projection. This is F4 in `01_figure_grounded_audit.md`.

*The Recursive Self-Improvement Trap*: "The catch is that the output is only as good as the validator. The agent, or the tool, or the frame, or the theory, cannot validate itself."

## 5. Q: does the framework give the cascade threshold?

**ANSWERED: no.** The framework returns a shape. A numeric critical threshold is licensed only by a ground measurement or by a provenanced import with a shadow test. Until one of those, the number is the searcher's own contribution and must be recorded as such. This is F6.

**Does `01` commit the forbidden error?** No, after the correction in §6 below. It states the shape (four conditions, sign of the per-slot residual, union not partition) and explicitly refuses the number. Where it uses world thresholds (R0, percolation thresholds), it marks them as secondary bindings from other frames, not figure outputs.

## 5b. Correction to my own §1 wording (kept visible)

I first wrote that the README forbids "naming" outright, following the exemplar's initial reading. That is too broad. The canonical phrase governs a **missing primitive**, one not held in Γ. For this question, the analogous mistake would be to claim the figures *name* a specific mechanism, for example "cascade is a bond-percolation transition" or "containment is modularity". Those are bindings from the network-science frame onto held structural atoms (network, gradient, spectrum), not new primitives. Naming them is not forbidden; claiming the framework derived them, or that they move the wall (Fig 6 :21 "MINT composes inside the closure and can never add an atom"), is. The distinction is now applied in `01` F3 and F6. My §4 metamerism point is **not** retracted: a source still cannot be named from a reading without a discriminator. Both claims are recorded so neither is quietly lost.

## 6. Two consequences for outreach

**Prestige is not action.** From *The Recursive Self-Improvement Trap*: "Prestige must never be allowed to validate an action directly." And: "Prestige can fund an action. It can give an action a budget to be tried. But it cannot settle an action. Only the ground can settle it. The Ground Maintainer must be blind to prestige." A campaign that gathers famous names to agree with the shape claim is the named collapse, not corroboration. The legitimate ask is the opposite: give the claim a budget to be tried and let the ground, an instrument, a cascade trace, or a stress test, settle it.

**This audit is a simulation, not a ground reading.** Same essay: "LLMs can quickly simulate an approximate answer/output with the formula found in the architecture post. It will often be better than the generic outputs that you will usually get otherwise too (intelligence is essentially arrangement). However, the truly testable output would need to be derived from the real architecture or working the formula yourself." So `01` is a simulated derivation, checked for internal consistency against the figures. It is **not** a test of the claim and must be labelled as such. It cannot settle anything it reports on.

*How a Society of Agents Would Work* adds the operational version: "Frames range against the ground. They do not negotiate with it." A group's agreement is never the verdict. This is why `02_outreach_email.md` asks for a refutation, not an endorsement, and why `09_watson_answer.md` states that this simulated second seat is one evidence pool, not a witness.
