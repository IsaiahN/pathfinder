## VERDICT (from the figures)

The shape survives; the number is refused. A local shock is a per-slot residual. It remains contained when that residual trends down to zero as the disturbance is composed outward through contact, and it cascades when the residual persists and does not trend down so that more slots go live. This decision is a union of four conditions with a product gate: topology and outward contact, boundary as directional filter, transmission versus common cause, and feedback sign. All four must pass for containment; one failing condition opens the cascade. No universal numeric threshold is derivable from the six-term set, and a co-failure is not evidence of contagion without an independence measurement. This is a `specified`, ungrounded result. The strongest single figure-grounded point is the per-slot rule: aggregating across slots throws away the only signal that says where to look.

- **cost if wrong:** bounded and locatable, because the packet names four conditions and five refuters, so an error is findable rather than invisible. The real cost is practical: treating a filter as a wall, or a shared-pool cofailure as contagion, misdirects intervention and can make a system more fragile. That cost is priced by Fig 2 :42 "Buy independence in proportion to the cost of being wrong." It is avoidable by leading with the shape claim, the two-instrument discrimination, and the refuter.
- **undoable:** yes. The four conditions can be re-weighted, re-measured, or re-scoped without changing the shape. A sent claim can be superseded by a corrected packet, and no primitive has to be withdrawn. The D2 analogue can be downgraded further without touching D1, D3, or D4.
- **reversed by:** a ground reading, not agreement. A per-slot trend test showing residuals decaying despite the four conditions, an intervention proving inter-transmission between nodes with a shared source and no path, a single scalar that separates contained from cascaded across all four conditions, a universal numeric threshold derived from the six-term set and confirmed at the ground, or a demonstration that a boundary is a perfect wall at every scale would each fire one of the five written refuters. A source-discriminating instrument, not prestige or consensus, is what moves the verdict to `grounded`.
