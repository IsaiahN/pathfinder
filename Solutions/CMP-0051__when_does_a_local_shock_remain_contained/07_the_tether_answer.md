# The Tether's answer: when does a local shock remain contained?

**Mode: `specified` - not `grounded`.** This is a shape and a set of conditions, not a result. Simulated derivation checked against the figures (`05_what_shape_means.md` §6), not a ground reading.

## 1. The residual it answers

A shock arrives at one slot s. The question asks what decides whether the disturbance stays at s (contained) or spreads so that other slots go live (cascade). Stated in effect terms: what does the per-slot residual R_s do as the disturbance is composed outward through contact? The residual is indexed by slot, not aggregated (Fig 1 :19 "The residual is indexed by object slot, not aggregated."), so a global average is not an acceptable answer. Fig 9 :55 "A bundled problem is usually a union of overlapping conditions, not a list of separate ones" applies: this is four conditions in a union, not one hard question.

## 2. The shape

1. **Measure per slot, never as an average.** Fig 1 :19 "The residual is indexed by object slot, not aggregated." Fig 1 :22 "Aggregating across slots is how a live signal disappears into an average." A system can look globally healthy while one slot is live.

2. **Containment is the shrinking case; cascade is the compounding case.** Fig 13 :31-32 "a remainder compounding through a chain that amplifies is chaos." Fig 5 :59-60 "a term whose residual PERSISTS AND DOES NOT TREND DOWN." Read with the opposite sign, the shrinking test gives the operational distinction: R_s trending down to zero means contained; R_s persisting flat or growing across steps means cascade. Fig 12 :121 "The sign of the loop decides whether it closes, and a chain cannot close by construction." Connectivity alone does not close a loop.

3. **The habitat is enumerated outward.** Fig 11 :43-44 "The habitat is enumerated, never composed." and "List everything in contact with the residual, then what is in contact with those, and outward until the cascade stops mattering." Fig 11 :52 "And contact cascades." The deciding effect may be three steps out. Actor valence is relative to the residual (Fig 11 :49 "An actor's valence is relative to the residual, not a property it carries"), so there is no standing list of safe nodes.

4. **Correlated failure is not contagion.** Fig 2 :19-21 "they never talk, and still agree" and "so the agreement is the pool talking, not two frames confirming each other." Fig 2 :31-32 "Independence, between two frames, is how little they draw on the same evidence." Two nodes failing together need not have transmitted anything. Independence is the discriminating quantity.

5. **The boundary is a directional filter, not a wall.** Fig 4 :21 "Not a wall: a filter with a direction." Fig 11 :66 "Isolation is not removal of the habitat. It is substitution of one habitat for another." Containment is maintained work, and cutting an edge substitutes a habitat with actors you did not choose. (This is a single-source analogue with a named strain; see `01` §2 and `11`.)

6. **It is a union with a product gate, not one threshold.** Fig 9 :54 "And when the split lands on a union rather than a partition, unbundle before you search again." The four conditions are members of one functional group, threshold relations (Fig 12 :87 "a threshold relation between two quantities"), and Fig 5 :41 "Any one of them at zero stops the mint entirely." All four must pass for containment; one failing condition opens the cascade. A single lever clears only the region where one condition binds.

7. **No universal number is given.** Fig 9 :32 "you get a kind of thing, never the answer itself." The critical rate, reproduction number, or percolation threshold belongs to the ground of a specific network, not to the figures.

## 3. What it explicitly does not say

- Not a universal critical threshold, an R0 value, or a degree cutoff. Those are measurements or imports from other frames, never figure outputs.
- Not that modularity always contains. World sources show its sign depends on the process (SIR versus SIS); it is a filter with a direction, not a wall.
- Not that co-failure proves contagion. Common cause is a named collapse (Fig 2 :6 "collapse 2: common cause (hidden shared source)").
- Not that a system-level average is a verdict. Averaging is exactly how the live signal disappears.
- Not `grounded`. No cascade footprint was measured in this derivation.

## 4. What would settle it - the ground

A discriminating instrument, not a further argument. *The Hard Problem is About Source*: an instrument that reads green "cannot comment on the source"; the spectrometer resolved it. Here:

- **Per-slot trend test:** measure R_s step by step across the disturbance. Does it trend down to zero or persist flat or grow? This settles the shrinking-test criterion (F2).
- **Two-instrument discrimination:** one instrument for transmission (did the residual travel along an edge?), one for common cause (did the nodes share a source?), kept low-overlap (Fig 8 :45 "Two frames together reach what neither reaches alone."). This settles F4.
- **Cycle-sign test:** does the propagation graph contain a closing loop with positive sign, and does a component survive its own use (Fig 12 :119 "catalyst. A part that survives its own use.")? This settles Q1 of `06`.
- **Product-gate test:** find a case with three conditions holding and one failing. Is it still contained across repeated trials? This settles F5.
- **Standing falsifiers from `01` §4:** contained cascade with all residuals trending down; inter-transmitting nodes with a proven shared source and no path; one lever separating all four conditions; a figure-derived universal threshold; a perfect boundary wall.

## 5. The honest stop

**Unreached, not unreachable.** The numeric threshold is behind a union horizon: no instrument in this derivation resolves it, and the figures cannot name it. Fig 8 :39 "So a search that finds nothing is never a proof of absence." The answer stops at the limit of the available instruments and records the lineage so the stop is checkable. *The Library of Babel* puts the operational version: being below the union horizon "is never a settled verdict, only a report that nobody has reached it yet." Indiscernibility is not identity.

## 6. One-paragraph version for outreach

> The Tether does not give a universal number for when a local shock cascades, and it does not claim the final word. It says the shock is a per-slot residual, so a global average hides the only signal that matters; that a cascade is the case where that residual persists and does not trend down as it is composed outward, while containment is the shrinking case; that the habitat must be enumerated beyond first contact because the effect that decides may be three steps out; that two nodes failing together are not proof of contagion, since they may share a source and never have talked; that a module boundary is a directional filter, not a wall; and that these four conditions form a union with a product gate, so no single lever decides. What it offers is the shape any answer must have, five falsifiers written in advance, and the measurements that would close it. The number stays open until the ground settles it.
