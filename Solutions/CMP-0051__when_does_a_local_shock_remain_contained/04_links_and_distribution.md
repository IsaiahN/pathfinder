## A strain worth keeping visible

The literature disagrees about modularity: RMP 2015 reports it confines SI/SIR outbreaks, while Nadini 2018 reports it lowers the SIS threshold and speeds SIS spreading. This is not a contradiction to be smoothed away. It is exactly F5's point, stated from an independent field: the same structural lever has opposite signs for different processes, so containment is a union of conditions and no single lever decides. The disagreement is recorded in `01` §6 and in `09_watson_answer.md` under WORLD.

## Distribution: offer, do not push

Figure-grounded reasons:

- **Fig 10 :52** - "Bring a characterised residual, never the source." Sending the full architecture before anyone has described a residual sends a recording, not a generator.
- **Fig 10 :42** - "A verifier that can reconstruct the claim cannot verify it." A recipient who has absorbed the framework before being asked for a verdict has joined the frame being checked.
- **Fig 9 :27-28** - "describe it before you go looking, or any frame you pick will seem to fit." Ask what they would expect to see if the shape claim were true before giving them the article.
- **Fig 2 :40** - "agreement about the world is worthless when the agreeing frames share an evidence pool." A mass send selected for people who already agree produces a convention, not corroboration.
- **Fig 9 :60** - "Use filters for the budget and witnesses for the verdict. Never let a filter hand you a verdict." A recipient's affiliation or citation count is a contact-cost filter, never an evidence weight.

**Recommended order in a reply:** (1) the shape claim and the four refuters; (2) ask what they would count as a failure and record it; (3) only then the four framework links, with the note that the figures, not the summary, are the authority.

## The two-stage offer

1. **Stage 1:** residual, shape claim, refuter. Ask for a failure, not an endorsement.
2. **Stage 2 (opt-in):** the figure links. Their verdict on stage 1 stands independent of anything they read later; later agreement is never pooled.
3. **Stage 3 (opt-in):** the formula as a reusable method, with the requirement that each user write their own refuter and state their own mode.

**Hard precondition:** the whole sequence only works if the stage-1 claim survives. The falsifiers in `01` §4 are live, so treat the claim as a candidate until one of them is actually run.
