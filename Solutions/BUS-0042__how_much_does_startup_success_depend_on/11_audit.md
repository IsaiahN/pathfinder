## Round trip gap R_T

The Symbols table defines R_T as `gap(x, (T_E ∘ T_A)(x))`, the gap between what was sent up and what came back down. Fig 4 :30 requires it to be measured rather than assumed. I ran the round trip on the condensation the folder actually uses.

1. **Sent up (x):** the verbatim figure set, chiefly Fig 1 :19/:22 (per-slot indexing), Fig 9 :54-57 and :60 (union, filters), Fig 11 :39-40 (agent-and-habitat), Fig 13 :31-32 (remainder).
2. **Abstraction (T_A):** the shorthand carried in `01` section 8 and `07` section 2: success is a per-startup contact relation; the factors form a union; the market is the anchor; luck is the remainder; no universal split.
3. **Concretisation (T_E):** the question's concrete demand: a five-way percentage split of startup success among founder skill, timing, networks, financing and luck.

**R_T is nonzero.** Four things the shorthand holds do not survive the trip back down to a percentage:

- the **per-slot index** (Fig 1 :19), which a cohort average destroys;
- the **union structure and its unassignable intersections** (Fig 9 :55-57), which an additive split sets to zero;
- the **bond information** (Fig 12 :5, :125), which a flat list does not record and a sum cannot price;
- the **remainder** (Fig 13 :31-32), which a complete five-way decomposition would have to explain away.

Conversely, one thing survives cleanly: a structure must be read at the market ground, and the venture's capability is not located in the founder alone. That is the residue worth sending. R_T was **not** measured numerically, because no source-discriminating instrument exists; it is recorded qualitatively as the enumerated set of dropped constraints. Treating the formula as identical to the figures would set R_T to zero and would be the dead-reckoning error Fig 4 :30 names. The honest form is a lossy condensation with a measured gap, which is what `09_watson_answer.md` states.
