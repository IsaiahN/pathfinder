# Outreach draft - validate the output first, then decide about the theory

**Use:** one to one, or as a cover note attached to a submission through a public venue in `03_public_contact_points.csv`. **Not** for bulk send. No personal emails are used; the recipient must have an opt-in or public institutional route.

**Hard precondition:** the stage-1 output must survive the recipient's check before any theory is shown. The output here is a `specified` shape with no market-ground measurement, so the legitimate ask is a refutation of the shape, never endorsement of it. If the recipient cannot be shown a failure condition they can actually test, do not send.

**Subject:** Check one claim about founder skill versus timing, networks, funding and luck before you decide whether any of this is worth reading

> Hi [name],
>
> I have one narrow claim about startup success that I would like you to check against what you already know. I am not asking you to read a theory first, and I am not asking you to agree with anything. I am asking for a failure. If the claim does not survive, there is nothing else to look at.
>
> **The claim (`specified`, not `grounded`):** "how much success depends on skill, timing, networks, financing and luck" asks for the wrong object. Those five are not additive shares. They are overlapping conditions with different instruments, and a success reading (profit, growth, survival, exit) cannot tell you which one produced it. Four things have to be kept separate:
>
> 1. the venture's channel to the market that actually pays it stays reachable, meaning the readings of demand, retention, revenue and survival are not stale, saturated or pooled into an average;
> 2. its reach keeps moving outward through contact with situations and people whose ways of working differ from its own, so it is not simply re-running the same playbook at a larger scale;
> 3. the window still fits the question, so the room changes when the question moves;
> 4. an instrument exists that can tell skill from timing from network from financing from the unexplained remainder when all five produce the same green reading.
>
> The claim is that these are different failure modes with different instruments, not five degrees of one problem. Quick failure is usually the channel or the window going stale, not a shortage of founder talent. The routine that produced the first win can change sign when the market changes, so the strength and the rigidity can be the same thing. Luck is the remainder the reading cannot assign, not a sixth co-equal cause. The market, not founder confidence or investor sentiment, is the anchor.
>
> **What I am not claiming:** that I can name which founder, which network, which round or which month wins; that any particular startup is a case in point; that this is settled by evidence; that there is a universal split such as 40 percent skill and 60 percent luck; or that this is the only account of startup success. I am offering a shape any candidate answer would have to have. A shape is not a name.
>
> **What would refute it, written down before I sent this:**
> 1. an averaged, prospective per-startup statistic that predicts which named condition will bind next, with the shared fraction assignable;
> 2. a case where the success verdict moves when founder or investor opinion changes while realized market return and survival are unchanged;
> 3. a founder-held-constant comparison across a habitat substitution that removes the market's payment, with unchanged success;
> 4. a decomposition whose intersection terms are proven to be zero, so the union is really a partition;
> 5. a venture that renews its reach purely by internal composition, with no new atom from a differing closure, and still passes the ground test across a habitat change;
> 6. an instrument that assigns all of a startup's outcome to named sources, prospectively validated, with no remainder left over.
>
> If any one holds, I withdraw the claim. A single line saying which one holds and why is more useful to me than agreement. If you would rather not engage, no reply is needed.
>
> **If it survives, and only then:** I can send the public sources below. The figures, not my summary of them, are the authority.
>
> - Figure text with line numbers (I can point to the exact lines): the local canonical figure text in this solution folder.
> - Formula and usage rule: the public `general-agent` branch of the `tether` repository.
> - The essays on source, search shape, tacit knowledge and the prestige/action separation: my Substack archive.
>
> I will send URLs only after you tell me what you would count as a failure.
>
> **If you want to use the machinery:** the formula is public. Run it on a problem you know well and can check against a real ground. Write down what would refute your result *before* you run it and state the mode of what you get. A reported failure is more useful to me than a reported success.
>
> Thanks,
> [name]

## Why this version is ordered this way

| the move | figure-grounded reason |
|---|---|
| Residual and refuter first, theory last | *"Bring a characterised residual, never the source."* (Fig 10 :52). The recipient checks the claim without holding the framework's interior |
| Do not ask for agreement | *"Alignment is triangulation, not negotiation. A mirror cannot correct you."* (Fig 2 :35) |
| Four failure modes kept separate | *"a case in two conditions is not a harder case of either"* (Fig 9 :57); a blended success score is a pooled reading (Fig 10 :26) |
| Refuse the "how much" split | *"Use filters for the budget and witnesses for the verdict. Never let a filter hand you a verdict."* (Fig 9 :60) |
| Market named as anchor | *"a market"* is explicitly named as an anchor in Fig 2 :29 |
| Pre-registered refuter | *"a falsifier written before the run is a design instrument, not only an honesty device"* |
| Mode stated on the claim | *"Mode. general · specified · grounded. State which."* (`THE_FORMULA.md`) |
| No name for the winning founder or factor | *"you get a kind of thing, never the answer itself"* (Fig 9 :32) |
| Luck treated as remainder, not a cause | *"because each turn leaves a remainder (the residual) that re-enters the next, it is also where the randomness comes from"* (Fig 13 :31-32) |
| Links only after a verdict | Fig 10 :42, *"A verifier that can reconstruct the claim cannot verify it."* Once the recipient has the framework, their later agreement is not corroboration (Fig 2 :40) |
| Prestige kept out of the ask | *"Prestige can fund an action. It can give an action a budget to be tried. But it cannot settle an action. Only the ground can settle it."* (*The Recursive Self-Improvement Trap*, opened 2026-11-22). Famous founders and famous investors are not evidence |
| Offer the formula as a method, not an answer | *"Only methods cross up. Only head starts come down."* (Fig 4 :22) |
| Ask the user for their own refuter | Formula, "Direction": a frame cannot check the direction of its own question; make the sought-for shape an ordinary term |

## Sending rules

- One recipient at a time. No distribution lists, no mail-merge, no forwarding chain.
- Use only a public institutional contact, a call for submissions, or an address the person has published themselves. Record the source and date.
- A direct-reply conversation is a conversation, never corroboration. If a recipient returns a refutation, copy it into `11_audit.md` with provenance and have the auditor seat re-evaluate it as if it came from any other source.
- Do not follow up with the theory after a "no". An interest in the outcome is unavoidable; an interest in the reading is disqualifying (Fig 10 :59).
- This packet was produced with LLM assistance. Do not submit it as authored work. The AOM and ETP AI policies are noted in `04` and `08`.
