# Bonds versus ingredient lists, order, and isomers in the startup question

**Mode: `specified` on the structural reading, `general` on the notation claim, not `grounded`.** This is a simulated derivation checked against the figures, not a ground reading (see `05_what_shape_means.md` section 7).

## RESIDUAL

The question presents founder skill, timing, networks, financing and luck as five ingredients and asks for the share of each. Two questions are owed: (1) is a list of ingredients a valid form for this answer, and (2) what order and bond structure do the five candidates actually have? A falsifier is stated in the CONCLUSION.

## DOMAIN CENSUS

Atoms and conditions held: a founder's Γ (routines, know-how, assets), a market ground, a time channel with windows, relations to other closures (the network structure, Fig 13 network), capital as a habitat actor, and the re-entering remainder. Bonds held: the seven, including `+` conjunction, `→` sequence, `⇒` production, `−` subtraction, `⋛` comparison, and the unary `¬` negation. No new primitive is available without import. The task is a reading of arrangement, not a search for a sixth cause.

## DERIVATION

### Q1 - Is a list of five factors a valid answer form? NO

- Fig 12 :5: **"A set of ingredients is not one. The bond is the rest of the information, and it is the half nothing was recording."**
- Fig 12 :6: **"collapse 6: the flat formula"**; :14: **"the ingredients are recorded and the arrangement is not"**; :15-16: *"nothing downstream can tell them apart, and nothing signals that anything was lost"*; :17: **"the parts are not the thing"**.
- Operators table :16: *"the only bond in a flat formula, which is why a formula records ingredients and not arrangement"*.
- The five-way "how much" question is exactly a flat formula. It names `skill + timing + network + financing + luck` and asks how much of each, while recording nothing about how they join. Fig 12 :99: *"A bond costs what it could have been, and nothing more."* So a term that ignores bonds undercharges the description by exactly the information that decides the outcome.
- **Correction to my own earlier overstatement in `05`:** the "shape, not a name" rule governs a **missing** primitive (README: *"what a **missing** thing would have to look like (a shape, not a name)."*). Founder skill, timing, networks, financing and luck are held atoms, habitat conditions or the remainder, so naming them is a binding, not an invented primitive. What is forbidden is treating the list as the answer and the names as measured shares. Retracted as applied to the five names; kept as applied to the list form and the percentages. Triangulated (README + Fig 12).

### Q2 - What bonds actually hold among the five?

The five candidates do not all join the same way. Reading them under Fig 12's six tests:

| candidate | bond to the success reading | test (Fig 12 :55-89) | order? |
|---|---|---|---|
| founder skill (Γ) | `→` sequence into action, then `⋛` against the market threshold | *"A then B; B needs A to have happened"* (Fig 12 :71-72) | ordered |
| timing (season, room) | `−` subtraction if the window is gone; the room changes with the question (Fig 10 :72) | *"A with B removed or absent"* (Fig 12 :81-82) | ordered |
| networks | `⇒` production when a new atom crosses from a differing closure; `+` when it only adds head starts | *"A produces B; B did not exist before"* (Fig 12 :76-77) | ordered |
| financing | `⇒` when it adds reach, `+` when it funds more of the same; can become a verdict if the convention decides the outcome (Fig 10 :47) | *"is there a B before A fires? No."* (Fig 12 :78) | ordered |
| luck | no bond; it is the re-entering remainder (Fig 13 :31-32), the detail the crossing discards reappearing as noise | not a relation between operands; it is what the coarser description cannot hold (Fig 4 :31-32) | not a bond |

- Fig 12 :111-112: **"A vocabulary without bonds can name a state and cannot name a route to one."** *"A plan is a sequence. A goal is a comparison. Progress is a subtraction."* A resource list can name what a startup contains; it cannot say what would count as getting somewhere from it.
- Fig 12 :87: `⋛` is **"a threshold relation between two quantities"**, and :51 of the Operators table says *"a goal is a comparison, which is why a vocabulary without it cannot state one."* "Success" is a threshold at the market, not an amount of any one factor.
- The unary remainder cannot be written as a bond at all: Fig 12 :134-136 distinguishes bonds from unary operators, and Fig 13 :31-32 assigns randomness to the remainder that re-enters each turn. Treating luck as `+ luck` is a type error, not a modest simplification.

### Q3 - Are the five conditions isomers?

- Fig 12 :117: **"isomer. Same atoms, different bond, different substance. The difference is invisible in the formula and decisive in the behaviour."**
- Fig 12 :123: **"functional group. A substructure whose presence fixes the outcome. The group says what kind; the qualifier says which member."**
- Two startups with the same founder calibre, the same market, the same funding and the same network list are **not** the same venture if the bonds differ: one has a weak tie that produces a new atom (`⇒`), the other has a strong tie that only adds agreement (`+`). They are isomers under Fig 12 :117: same atoms, different arrangement, different behaviour. This is the structural reason a resource inventory cannot predict success.
- The weaker, safer claim is the **functional group**: all startups share `Γ → market ground, read at a time` (the group), and the timing, network and financing atoms are the qualifiers (Fig 12 :123). The group fixes the kind; the qualifiers say which member.
- World bearing: Granovetter 1973 draws the same distinction between redundant strong ties and information-bearing weak ties, and Giones et al. 2019 report that the same human-capital atoms have different effects in high-tech and non-high-tech contexts. Both are readings of arrangement, not of ingredient counts.

### Q4 - What order is the union?

- Fig 12 :104: **"A settled molecule becomes an atom for whatever composes over it. So irreducible is a fact about a level and never about the world"**. A startup that has settled its product-market fit becomes an atom for the next round of composition; this is recursion, not a new primitive.
- Fig 3 :20: **"The chain is ordered by dependency, not by importance."** The order is perception of the market → vocabulary for what the situation affords → objective (what counts as progress) → planning → learn and carry (Fig 3 :4-15). The middle link is where chains usually break (Fig 3 :24), and a link with no instrument is a link nobody has looked at (Fig 3 :27).
- Fig 9 :27-28: **"describe it before you go looking, or any frame you pick will seem to fit."** The residual and its refuter come before the choice of any factor list.
- Formula step 2: refuse before mint. The partition reading is refused before any five-way decomposition is attempted. Refusal is `¬`, which is not a bond and *"joins nothing"* (Operators table :70-72).

### Q5 - Can the five be added?

- Fig 12 :125-126: **"A composition costs in two currencies, and they do not add."** *"Bits price the description. Actions price finding out whether it holds."*
- Fig 12 :128-129: *"The two readings come apart in both directions. A long chain of cheap parts is expensive to describe and may cost one action to refute. A short arrangement over a costly operand is cheap to state and may take a whole budget to settle. Neither figure predicts the other, so both are read or the choice is made blind."* Fig 12 :130: **"Summing them into one figure is the error this names, not the fix for it."**
- A five-way percentage is a single figure summed over quantities in different currencies and different bonds. It is the error Fig 12 :130 names, not a measurement.

## CONCLUSION

The flat five-factor list is the **flat formula** of Fig 12 :6, and the question's "how much" grammar asks for a sum that Fig 12 :125-130 forbids. The five candidates carry different bonds: founder skill and timing are ordered (`→`, `−`), networks and financing can be productive (`⇒`) or merely conjunctive (`+`), the success verdict is a threshold (`⋛`), and luck is not a bond at all but the remainder that re-enters each turn. Two ventures with the same atoms and different bonds are isomers; with the same group and different qualifiers they are functional-group variants. Order runs by dependency (Fig 3), with refusal before mint and residual description before frame choice.

**Falsifier, written before the run:** if the five candidates can be written as a single symmetric `+` composition with no bond information lost (Fig 12 :46, *"which one holds is not recoverable from the operands"* fails), or if a five-way percentage prospectively predicts which condition binds at a named startup with no remainder, the arrangement claim is false and the additive reading stands. Equivalently: if two startups with identical atoms and identical bonds can diverge with no re-entering remainder and no habitat difference, the isomer and remainder readings both fail.
