## 1. A shape is a constraint, not a filler

The figures README states the limit: the framework returns *"what a missing thing would have to look like (a shape, not a name)."* Fig 9 :32: *"you get a kind of thing, never the answer itself."* Fig 13: *"It can say the answer is out of reach with the library as it stands, so something has to be imported before it is asked again."* It *"cannot say which primitive is missing, because naming it would mean already holding it."*

From *The Library of Babel*, opened this session: *"you can cut your search short if the set of books returned is small and descriptive enough that it provides a general shape of the answer that satisfies your question."*

So a **shape** is the narrowed form of an answer you can state before you can name the source. It constrains; it does not fill. For BUS-0042, "success is settled by a market anchor, not by frame agreement" is a shape. "Skill explains 40 percent" is a name, and worse, it is a name for a share that the reading cannot discriminate.

## 2. How a shape is obtained

- *Library of Babel*: use the vocabulary you already have that is *closest* to the thing, look for recurring new terms that overlap or correlate with it, recurse. *"With each new term gained, you narrow the space and deepen your vocabulary."* The essay also names the bundle failure: *"Perhaps your problem was a bundle or conflation of several problems masquerading as one?"* That is the governing move for this question (Fig 9 :54-57).
- Fig 6 :30: *"Cast it in effect terms."* Fig 9 :30-31: *"your words are borrowed too, so describe the effect rather than the cause."*
- *Decoding Tacit Knowledge*, opened this session: *"When contact isn't possible, the best you can send is a plain description of what you'd expect to see, because anyone can check that against the world."*
- *Library of Babel* on stopping: *"you will know that you are done because the finish line will also be the starting place"*; the paradox is that by then you have *"composed the answer through the process of locating it."*

For this question the effect description is the set of failure signatures in `01` section 1.3 and the six refuters in `01` section 4. Anyone can check those against startup data; nobody needs to accept the framework first.

## 3. What licenses a name

Only two things, both outside the shape itself:

1. **The ground settles it.** Formula step 5: *"the ground settles it. Verified only by what pays. A gate is not the ground."* The README says the framework *"stops at the limit of what the available instruments can distinguish, and records the lineage so the stop is honest."*
2. **A provenanced import from a frame whose closure differs** (Fig 6 :23; Fig 8 :50-52), with a source gate and a shadow test.

*They're Scanning Books* (quote carried from the exemplar's canonical mapping): *"The real import is not a new term in the existing space. It is a new dimension of the space."* Also: *"A minimum viable set of six terms is a human's careful guess at reality's compression axes; another sensorium's minimum set need not be those six."* Also: *"Declaring the primitives does not make them neutral. It makes the choice visible."*

For BUS-0042, naming "founder skill", "season", "weak ties" or "a Series A" is a binding from the entrepreneurship frame onto held atoms and habitat conditions. It is not the invention of a new primitive. It becomes evidence only with provenance and a shadow test.

## 4. Why the "how much" reading is the forbidden error here

*The Hard Problem is About Source*, opened this session, gives the mechanism directly. An instrument that reads green *"is speaking only about what it measured, it cannot comment on the source."* And: *"If several paths create the same behavior, then behavior is a projection with lossy information (it cannot define consciousness in full)."* The green reading here is startup success: revenue, growth, survival, exit. The candidate sources are Γ, a paying habitat, a timed window, an imported network or financing atom, and the re-entering remainder. Several paths produce the same reading, so the reading does not determine the source.

The essay's own resolution is instrumental, not argumentative: *"Why this Matters: Chalmers' inference: Projection can't distinguish these sources, and nothing could. What the Spectrometer Example Reveals: The answer is unreached until a better instrument exists."* It was the spectrometer, not a further argument, that resolved the color case. Here the corresponding instrument is a source discriminator that can tell skill from timing from network from financing from luck at the level of a single startup, prospectively validated.

*The Recursive Self-Improvement Trap*, opened this session: *"the output is only as good as the validator. The agent, or the tool, or the frame, or the theory, cannot validate itself."* A variance decomposition produced by the same frame whose beliefs it is scoring cannot validate the attribution.

## 5. Does the framework return the universal split? ANSWERED, no further question needed

**What licenses a particular five-way percentage? Nothing in the framework.** Fig 9 :60: *"Use filters for the budget and witnesses for the verdict. Never let a filter hand you a verdict."* Fig 9 :57: *"a case in two conditions is not a harder case of either. It stops being a mixture and becomes a specific compound."* Fig 13 :134: *"Unreachable is a verdict here, not a silence,"* and Fig 8 :39: *"So a search that finds nothing is never a proof of absence."*

What is owed is a discriminator, not a number. The missing thing is characterized in effect terms in `01` section 4, refuter 6: an instrument that assigns all of an outcome to named sources at the slot level, with no remainder, prospectively validated. Until it exists, the split is a `gap`, and the gap is reported rather than filled.

## 6. Correction to my own §3 and §4 (see `06_isomers_and_order.md` Q1)

I initially overstated the "shape, not a name" rule as if it forbade naming any of the five factors. The README's phrase is *"what a **missing** thing would have to look like (a shape, not a name)."* The subject is a primitive not held in Γ. Founder skill is Γ; timing is a habitat condition (Fig 11 :46); networks are the held network structure plus relations (Fig 13 network; Fig 11 :47); financing is a habitat actor and a possible import; luck is the re-entering remainder (Fig 13 :31-32). None of these is a new primitive being smuggled in.

So naming them is **not** forbidden. What remains wrong is treating a name as a measured share, or claiming that naming one adds an atom that moves the wall (Fig 6 : *"IMPORT adds an atom"* - a binding adds none). Both the original overstatement and this correction are kept visible; neither is quietly erased.

My §4 metamerism point is **not** retracted. The reason the reading cannot settle the source is the missing discriminator, not a ban on naming held atoms. Both claims now stand.

## 7. Two consequences for outreach

**Prestige is not action.** From *The Recursive Self-Improvement Trap*, opened this session: *"Prestige must never be allowed to validate an action directly... Someone let prestige stand in for proof... Prestige can fund an action. It can give an action a budget to be tried. But it cannot settle an action. Only the ground can settle it. The Ground Maintainer must be blind to prestige."* A list of famous founders, famous investors or famous accelerators cannot settle this question. The legitimate ask is the opposite: give the shape a budget to be tried, and let the market - an outcome, a measurement, a board that pays or does not - settle it.

**My own audit is a simulation, not a ground reading.** Same essay: *"LLMs can quickly simulate an approximate answer/output with the formula found in the architecture post. It will often be better than the generic outputs that you will usually get otherwise too... However, the truly testable output would need to be derived from the real architecture or working the formula yourself."* LLM output is a simulacrum of a derivation. So `01` is a simulated derivation, checked for internal consistency against the figures. It is **not** a test of the claim and must be labelled as such. It cannot settle anything it reports on.
