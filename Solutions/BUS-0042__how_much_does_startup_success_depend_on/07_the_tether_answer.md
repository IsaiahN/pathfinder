# The Tether's answer on founder skill versus timing, networks, financing and luck

**Mode: `specified` on the per-domain claims, `general` on the structural shape, `gap` on the universal percentage split - not `grounded`.** This is a shape and a route, not a result. Simulated derivation checked against the figures (`05_what_shape_means.md` section 7), not a ground reading. No market measurement pays for any claim here.

## 1. The residual it answers

An answer would explain why the gap between what a founder's library predicts the market will return and what the market actually returns stays small for one new venture and grows for another, and whether that gap can be divided into additive, assignable shares. R is indexed by startup and by time, never averaged (Fig 1 :19, :22). The question is about **attribution per slot**, not about a cohort-wide percentage.

The union must be unbundled before it is searched (Fig 9 :54). It is at least four conditions, not one question with five weights:

- **C1:** the venture's channel to a ground that still pays it stays reachable.
- **C2:** its reach keeps moving outward through contact with closures that differ.
- **C3:** the window and the room still fit the question the venture is asking.
- **C4:** a discriminator exists that can tell sources apart when all produce the same success reading.

## 2. The shape

1. **The "how much" object is the wrong object.** Fig 1 :19: *"The residual is indexed by object slot, not aggregated."* Fig 9 :60: *"Use filters for the budget and witnesses for the verdict. Never let a filter hand you a verdict."* A five-way share is a budget filter, not a witness. Any answer that is an average across startups has thrown away the signal that says which condition binds.
2. **Success is settled by the market, an anchor that does not update.** Fig 2 :29 names *"a market"* explicitly as an anchor. Fig 2 :35: *"Alignment is triangulation, not negotiation. A mirror cannot correct you."* Founder confidence, investor sentiment and press narrative are instruments, not the ground. They are also frame-internal quantities: *"A frame cannot score itself with a quantity it also produces."* (Fig 1 :27).
3. **Founder skill is Γ, not a stock.** Fig 11 :39: *"capability is a property of agent-and-habitat, never of the agent alone."* Fig 11 :40: *"An improvement that does not change contact changes nothing, however much it improves."* The same Γ succeeds in one habitat and fails in another. A list of founder traits is a candidate library whose reach must be tested against the ground, not the answer.
4. **The candidates form a union with unassignable intersections.** Fig 9 :55: *"A bundled problem is usually a union of overlapping conditions, not a list of separate ones."* Fig 9 :57: *"a case in two conditions is not a harder case of either."* Timing and luck share the time channel; networks and financing share the import mechanism; skill and financing share the reach. A single lever clears the region where one condition holds and leaves the intersections standing (Fig 9 :55-56).
5. **Networks and financing are import operators, not percentages.** Fig 6 :23: *"No frame escapes its own closure. The bound moves only on contact with a frame whose closure differs."* Fig 8 :32: closure is idempotent, so no amount of internal search adds an atom. Granovetter's weak ties are the low-overlap witness: they give access to information from different circles. Financing is a wall-moving import when it adds a new atom to reach, and a mere head start when it funds more of the same (Fig 4 :22). Imports need provenance and a shadow test (Fig 8 :50-52).
6. **Timing is a habitat condition; luck is the remainder.** Fig 11 :46 lists *"season"* among habitat conditions. Fig 13 :31-32: *"because each turn leaves a remainder (the residual) that re-enters the next, it is also where the randomness comes from"*. Luck is not a sixth actor with its own ground; it is the leftover the coarser description cannot hold, reappearing as noise (Fig 4 :31-32). Pluchino et al. 2018 and Frank 2017 corroborate the mechanism at low overlap without returning a universal percentage.
7. **A success reading cannot comment on its source.** Fig 9 :30-31: *"your words are borrowed too, so describe the effect rather than the cause."* Fig 9 :32: *"you get a kind of thing, never the answer itself."* The source essay's metamerism rule: an instrument reading green *"cannot comment on the source."* What is owed is a discriminator, not a name or a share.
8. **The strength and the rigidity can be the same object.** Fig 11 :51: *"No standing list of good actors survives a change of residual."* Fig 12 :119: *"catalyst. A part that survives its own use. Spent and regenerating parts behave differently across uses, and nothing records which a term is."* A routine that won the last round can be what prevents the next one.
9. **The five-factor list is a flat formula.** Fig 12 :5: *"A set of ingredients is not one. The bond is the rest of the information, and it is the half nothing was recording."* Fig 12 :125: *"A composition costs in two currencies, and they do not add."* The bonds differ (`→`, `−`, `⇒`, `+`, `⋛`), and luck is not a bond at all. See `06_isomers_and_order.md`.

## 3. What it explicitly does not say

- Not `grounded`. No prospective, per-startup market measurement has paid for this shape.
- Not a universal split. No figure returns "X percent skill, Y percent timing". That is a declared `gap` (Fig 9 :32; Fig 13 :134), not a hidden answer.
- Not a name. It does not say which founder, which network, which round or which month wins.
- Not an average. It makes no claim about the percentage of startup variance attributable to any factor. Such numbers are filters for budget, not witnesses (Fig 9 :60).
- Not a claim that founders, networks, funding or timing are irrelevant. They are Γ and habitat conditions; the claim is that none of them settles persistence on its own (Fig 11 :40).
- Not corroboration by agreement. Giones, Gudmundsson and Lechner, Lerner et al. and Lifchits et al. share a broad management/cognition evidence pool (Fig 2 :31, :40). Pluchino et al., Frank and Granovetter are genuinely lower overlap and corroborate the mechanism shape, not a startup percentage.
- Not a final theory of startups. It stops where the discriminator stops.

## 4. What would settle it - the ground

A source-discriminating instrument, not a further argument. The readings are revenue, growth, survival and exit; the candidate sources are a protected Γ, a paying habitat, a timed window, an imported network or financing atom, and the remainder. Lifchits et al. 2021 show that selected success narratives move beliefs strongly even when readers acknowledge the bias; Lerner et al. 2021 show how truncation inflates longevity by 152 percent in a complete firm population. An instrument must discriminate sources without inheriting those errors.

The falsifiers, pre-registered in `01` and repeated in `08` and `09`:

1. an averaged, prospective per-startup statistic that predicts which named condition will bind next, with the shared fraction assignable;
2. a success verdict that moves with founder or investor opinion while realized market return and survival are unchanged;
3. a founder-held-constant comparison across a habitat substitution that removes the market's payment, with unchanged success;
4. a decomposition whose intersection terms are proven to be zero;
5. a venture renewing reach purely by internal composition, with no new atom from a differing closure, still passing the ground test across a habitat change;
6. a source discriminator that assigns all of a startup's outcome to named sources, prospectively validated, with no remainder.

A prospective study that measures C1 to C4 separately per startup, records bonds as well as atoms, and reports which condition failed before the decline would turn this from `specified` toward `grounded`.

## 5. The honest stop

**Unreached, not unreachable.** Fig 13 :134: *"Unreachable is a verdict here, not a silence,"* and Fig 8 :39: *"So a search that finds nothing is never a proof of absence."* The shape stops at the limit of the available instruments and records the lineage so the stop is checkable. The missing thing is a discriminator of sources, characterized in effect terms but not held. Naming a winner from a green reading would be the metamerism error the source essay forbids. The nine points in section 2 are a shape; the tenth thing, the universal split and the identity of the binding condition at a given startup, is not held and is not pretended.

## 6. One-paragraph version for outreach

> The Tether does not rank founders or return a split such as 40 percent skill and 60 percent luck. It says that startup success is not a stock to be divided into five shares, but a per-startup contact relation: the venture has to keep its channel to a market that still pays it reachable, keep moving its reach outward through contact with ways of working that differ from its own, keep the window and the room matched to the question as that question moves, and be read by an instrument that can tell skill from timing from network from financing from the unexplained remainder when all five produce the same green reading. Those are different conditions with different instruments, not five degrees of one problem. Quick failure is usually the channel or the window going stale, not a shortage of founder talent; networks and financing act as imports that can add a new atom to reach; and the routine that produced the first win can change sign when the market changes. Luck is the leftover the reading cannot assign, not a sixth co-equal cause. The market, not founder confidence or investor sentiment, is the anchor. What is offered is that shape, six falsifiers written in advance, and the instruments that would close it. Which condition binds at a given startup, and any universal percentage, stay open until a source-discriminating instrument exists, and the stop is recorded rather than filled.
