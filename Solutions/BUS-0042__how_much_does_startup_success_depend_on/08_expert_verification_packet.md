## 1. The claim, in one paragraph

Startup success is not a stock to be divided into founder, timing, network, financing and luck shares. It is a per-startup contact relation with at least four separable conditions: the venture keeps a channel to a market that still pays it reachable; it keeps moving its reach outward through contact with situations and people whose ways of working differ from its own; the window and the room still fit the question it is asking; and an instrument exists that can tell the sources apart when all produce the same success reading. These are different failure modes with different instruments, not five degrees of one problem. Founder skill is the venture's Γ, whose reach must be tested against the market, not a free-floating stock. Networks and financing are import operators that can add a new atom to reach, not additive percentages. Timing is a habitat condition, and luck is the remainder the reading cannot assign. The market, not founder confidence or investor sentiment, is the anchor. The claim does not name which founder, network, round or window wins, and it returns no universal split.

## 2. Three checkable sub-claims

### C1 - Framework-internal; checkable by reading Figures 1, 2, 11 and 10

- **Statement:** per-slot indexing and a non-updating market anchor rule out an averaged five-way share and rule in founder-and-habitat contact. Quick failure is a channel or room failure, not ground decay.
- **Source, verbatim:**
  - Fig 1 :19 - *"The residual is indexed by object slot, not aggregated."* Fig 1 :22 - *"Aggregating across slots is how a live signal disappears into an average."*
  - Fig 2 :29 - *"An anchor is whatever settles the question and does not change its answer because of what any frame thinks. A measurement, an experiment, a counter, a market, a patient outcome."* Fig 2 :35 - *"Alignment is triangulation, not negotiation. A mirror cannot correct you."*
  - Fig 11 :39 - *"So the answerable question is not what intelligence is, but what makes a system capable. And capability is a property of agent-and-habitat, never of the agent alone."* Fig 11 :40 - *"A system cannot be made more capable by improving the agent in isolation. An improvement that does not change contact changes nothing, however much it improves."*
  - Fig 10 :24 - *"the ground does not decay, the channel does"*; Fig 10 :26 - *"saturated metrics, pooled readings"*; Fig 10 :72 - *"When the question changes, the room should change."*
- **Refuter:** an averaged, per-startup prospective statistic that predicts which named condition will bind next with its shared fraction assignable; or a success verdict that moves with founder or investor opinion while realized market return is unchanged; or a founder-held-constant comparison across a habitat substitution that removes the market's payment with unchanged success. Any one fires against C1.

### C2 - Framework-internal derivation plus one business-facing check

- **Statement:** the five candidates form a union with unassignable intersections, and networks and financing act as imports that can move the wall rather than as additive shares.
- **Source, verbatim:**
  - Fig 9 :55 - *"A bundled problem is usually a union of overlapping conditions, not a list of separate ones."* Fig 9 :57 - *"a case in two conditions is not a harder case of either. It stops being a mixture and becomes a specific compound."* Fig 9 :60 - *"Use filters for the budget and witnesses for the verdict. Never let a filter hand you a verdict."*
  - Fig 6 :21 - *"MINT composes inside the closure and can never add an atom."* Fig 6 :23 - *"No frame escapes its own closure. The bound moves only on contact with a frame whose closure differs."*
  - Fig 8 :32 - *"Closure is idempotent. Composing inside a closed set never leaves it, so no search, however long, adds an atom. This one is definitional: it is what closure means."*
- **What a researcher can check against their own knowledge:** Granovetter 1973 writes that *"those to whom we are weakly tied are more likely to move in circles different from our own and will thus have access to information different from that which we receive."* Giones, Gozun and Miralles 2019 report that *"the influence of human capital factors are dependent on the context (high vs. non-high technology industries)"* and that *"work experience does not have a statistically significant influence"* in their Kauffman Firm Survey models. Do these not already name the differing-closure import and the context dependence that a fixed-share decomposition erases? A "no" to both is a real refutation of the mapping.
- **Refuter:** a decomposition whose intersection terms are proven to be zero; or a venture that renews its reach purely by internal composition with no new atom from a differing closure and still passes the ground test across a habitat change. Either breaks idempotence and C2 with it.

### C3 - Source discrimination and the flat-formula objection

- **Statement:** a success reading cannot comment on its source, so what is owed is a discriminator, not a name or a percentage; luck is the re-entering remainder, and a list of five factors is a flat formula that loses the bonds.
- **Source, verbatim:**
  - Fig 9 :30-31 - *"your words are borrowed too, so describe the effect rather than the cause."* Fig 9 :32 - *"you get a kind of thing, never the answer itself."*
  - Fig 13 :31-32 - *"because each turn leaves a remainder (the residual) that re-enters the next, it is also where the randomness comes from"*
  - Fig 12 :5 - *"A set of ingredients is not one. The bond is the rest of the information, and it is the half nothing was recording."* Fig 12 :125 - *"A composition costs in two currencies, and they do not add."*
  - Fig 13 :134 - *"Unreachable is a verdict here, not a silence,"*
- **What a researcher can check against their own knowledge:** Lifchits, Anderson, Goldstein, Hofman and Watts 2021 found in a pre-registered experiment that *"even the most compelling success narratives convey little information regarding the true association between a particular feature and success"*; Lerner, Ortiz-Hunt and Jin 2021 found that in a complete firm population *"average longevity was overstated by 152%"* when short-lived failures were truncated. Pluchino, Biondo and Rapisarda 2018 conclude that *"the observed Gaussian shape of P(Cmax|T) is the proof that luck matters more than talent in reaching very high levels of success."* Do these not jointly show that the winner reading is a projection with lossy information, rather than a five-way share? A study that assigns all of an outcome to named sources prospectively, with no remainder, would answer "yes, but it can be done" and would refute the gap.
- **Refuter:** an instrument that assigns all of a startup's outcome to named sources, prospectively validated with no remainder; or a demonstration that the five candidates form a single symmetric `+` composition with no bond information lost. Either breaks C3.

## 3. What is explicitly not claimed

- Not `grounded`. No measurement has paid for any part of this.
- Not a universal split, and no claim about what percentage of startup variance is attributable to founder, industry, timing, funding or luck.
- Not a final or unique theory of entrepreneurship.
- Not that founders, networks, funding or timing are irrelevant. They are Γ and habitat conditions; the claim is that none settles persistence alone.
- Not that any specific routine, founder or investor is the answer. That is a `gap` at the instrument boundary, not a hidden answer.
- Not that the entrepreneurship literature independently corroborates this. Giones, Gudmundsson and Lechner, Lerner et al. and Lifchits et al. share one broad management/cognition evidence pool. Pluchino et al., Frank and Granovetter are lower overlap and corroborate the mechanism shape, not the startup fact.
- Not that this packet, produced with an LLM, is submittable or publishable as authored work. See section 6.

## 5. What would settle it

A prospective, per-startup study that measures the four conditions separately, records bonds as well as atoms, and reports which condition failed before the decline. Concretely:

1. **Channel test:** does the venture's reading of the market degrade while the market keeps paying someone else? Indicators: stale percepts, saturated metrics, pooled readings (Fig 10 :25-26).
2. **Pressure and fidelity test:** are bracket fidelity and ground pressure both nonzero at each handoff? Fig 7 :37 says zero either stops the chain.
3. **Import test:** did any new atom enter the venture's reach from a closure that differs before the run of success? If not, C2 is refuted.
4. **Room test:** did the room change when the question moved, or did the venture keep handing itself the same room (Fig 10 :72, Fig 7 :36)?
5. **Source-discriminating instrument:** can the study distinguish a protected Γ from a paying habitat from a timed window from an imported atom from luck when all produce the same profit reading? The source essay says a reading alone *"cannot comment on the source."*
