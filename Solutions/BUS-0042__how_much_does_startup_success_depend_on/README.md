## The question restated as an effect

The question asks for a split: what share of startup success is owed to founder skill, to timing, to networks, to financing, and to luck. Restated in effect terms, it asks why the gap between what a founder's library predicts the market will return and what the market actually returns stays small for one new venture and grows for another, and whether that gap can be divided into additive, assignable shares. An answer would look like a set of conditions, each with its own instrument, plus a rule for which condition binds at which startup and time. That shape is bound as an ordinary term before the run (Formula, "Direction"), so the refuter in `01` can fire on it.

## What the derivation concludes (see `01`, `07`, `09`)

The "how much" object is the wrong object. Three figure laws converge: the residual is per slot and aggregating across slots destroys the only signal (Fig 1 :19, :22); an averaged share is a frame-internal filter, not a witness (Fig 9 :60); and the five candidates form a **union of overlapping conditions**, not a partition with additive shares (Fig 9 :54-57). A success reading cannot settle its own source: several sources produce the same reading, so what is owed is a source-discriminating instrument, not a percentage. Luck is not a sixth co-equal cause with its own anchor; it is the remainder that re-enters each turn (Fig 13 :31-32). No figure line returns a universal split. That is a declared `gap`, which is a verdict and not silence.

## What is deliberately not here

- No personal emails of named individuals and no scraped contact lists.
- No ranking of people by how likely they are to agree.
- No bulk-send plan. No claim that this package is submittable as authored work (see the AI-policy note in `04` and `08`).
- No universal percentage split, because the figures return a shape and a refuter, not a number.
- No claim that this is a ground reading. It is a simulated derivation checked for internal consistency against the figures. It cannot settle the question it reports on.
