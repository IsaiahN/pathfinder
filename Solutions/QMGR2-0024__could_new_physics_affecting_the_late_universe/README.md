# Solution QMGR2-0024

**Question:** Could new physics affecting the late universe explain the same difference?

**Source:** QM & GR Questions.txt line 170

**Section:** C. The Hubble tension and cosmological measurements

The "same difference" is the Hubble tension: the gap between the expansion rate inferred from
the early universe (CMB under ΛCDM, about 67.4 km/s/Mpc) and the rate measured in the nearby
universe (local distance ladder, about 73 km/s/Mpc). Question 23 asks whether *early*-universe
new physics can shift the inferred rate. This question asks whether *late*-universe new physics
can close the same gap.

**Restated as an effect:** what is being decided is whether an import that changes the late-time
expansion history H(z) can move the late-slot value of H0 toward the early-slot inference without
breaking the other readings the late universe is already anchored to (BAO, supernovae, growth).
Option A, yes in principle, leads to a provenanced import with a stated shape and a refuter.
Option B, no, leads to the conclusion that the residual lives in another slot. An answer would
look like a shape the import must have plus the measurement that would settle it, bound as an
ordinary term so it can accrue a residual.

**Mode:** `specified` on the per-slot and union claims and on the shape of any admissible import,
`general` on the structural barrier form, `gap` on which specific physics occupies the slot and on
whether the live residual is late-sourced at all. Nothing here is `grounded`.

## What is deliberately not here

- No personal emails of named individuals.
- No ranking of people by how likely they are to agree with the shape claim.
- No bulk-send plan.

Three figure-grounded reasons:

1. Fig 2 :35, "Alignment is triangulation, not negotiation. A mirror cannot correct you."
   Selecting recipients for likely agreement manufactures the very correlation that makes their
   agreement worthless.
2. Fig 2 :40, "agreement about the world is worthless when the agreeing frames share an evidence
   pool." A list of believers is one pool wearing many names.
3. Fig 10 :42, "A verifier that can reconstruct the claim cannot verify it." Handing the whole
   framework to a reader before they return a verdict on the shape spends the independence the
   outside reader contributes.

verdict before offering any theory. A failure is more useful than an endorsement.
