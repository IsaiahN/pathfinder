# The Tether's answer: could late-universe new physics explain the same difference?

**Mode: `specified` on the per-slot and union claims and on the shape of an admissible import,
`general` on the structural barrier form, `gap` on which physics occupies the slot and on whether
the live residual is late-sourced. Not `grounded`.** This is a shape and a route, not a result.
Simulated derivation checked for internal consistency against the figures (`05` section 6), not a
ground reading.

## 1. The residual it answers

The Hubble difference is not one number. It is a disagreement among three slots: the CMB-inferred
H0 under ΛCDM (67.4 ± 0.5, a derived quantity depending on the sound horizon), the local distance
ladder (about 73, a calibrated measurement), and the inverse-ladder / sound-horizon-free set
(68.5 to 69.4, independent of Cepheids). Fig 1 :19: "The residual is indexed by object slot, not
aggregated." Fig 9 :54: "And when the split lands on a union rather than a partition, unbundle
before you search again." It is a union of overlapping conditions, not a clean early-versus-late
binary.

## 2. The answer

1. **Yes in principle, no as a derivation.** A new low-redshift degree of freedom is an import into
   the model's closure, the only operator that moves the wall (Fig 6 :14). It cannot be minted
   from inside ΛCDM: "MINT composes inside the closure and can never add an atom" (Fig 6 :21).
   So the figures neither forbid it nor derive it.
2. **The figures return a shape, not a name.** Fig 9 :32: "you get a kind of thing, never the answer
   itself." Any admissible import must (a) change H(z) at low z enough to move the late-slot H0,
   (b) leave r_d and θ_s fixed, because those belong to the early slot and are pinned to 0.03%, and
   (c) remain ranged against BAO, supernovae and growth. That is a shape. It does not select
   w0waCDM, f(R) gravity, decaying dark matter or backreaction.
3. **The world literature, read as secondary corroboration, currently says late-time-only models
   fall short of that shape.** Sabogal et al. 2026 find a "shape wall" of about 2% against the
   roughly 8% needed; Tiwari et al. 2026 find even distance-duality violation cannot rescue a
   late-only fix; Schöneberg et al. 2026 find late-time scenarios do not improve on ΛCDM while
   early models do. These are evidence at a budget, not a figure verdict, and they share datasets
   as one evidence pool.
4. **The early-versus-late frame is itself unstable.** Pantos and Perivolaropoulos 2026 find the
   ladder-versus-rest split is larger than Planck-SH0ES, and DESI 2024 VI gives an inverse-ladder
   H0 of 68.52 ± 0.62. If the live residual is in the ladder calibration, late-*physics* is the
   wrong lever and a measurement fix is owed. The figures require localizing the residual before
   choosing a frame (Fig 9 :27).
5. **The verdict is a union with kept-apart per-domain results.** Early inference: model-dependence
   of a derived H0. Local ladder: calibration chain. Inverse ladder: currently agrees with early.
   Proposed late physics: unanchored import until the shape of (2) is met. These are never averaged.

## 3. What it explicitly does not say

- Not that late-universe new physics is impossible. It is allowed as a provenanced import.
- Not that any named model is the answer. The figures return a shape, not a name.
- Not that early-universe physics (Q23) is excluded. The union has more than one live condition.
- Not a claim that the tension is settled by the world no-go papers. They are secondary and
  share an evidence pool.
- Not `grounded`. No instrument has been claimed to return the required late-time deviation.
- Not that a list of agreeing experts would help. Fig 2 :40: "agreement about the world is
  worthless when the agreeing frames share an evidence pool."

## 4. What would settle it - the ground

A discriminator, not another argument. Fig 2 :29: an anchor "does not change its answer because of
what any frame thinks." Fig 6 :32: "An instrument is not built from a description. It is improved
from a worse instrument already returning something."

- **Null test (Kouniatalis and Saridakis 2026):** combine lensing time-delay distances with GW
  standard-siren luminosity distances into an H0-independent dimensionless ratio. If early and
  late determinations of that ratio disagree, the residual is in the expansion history; if they
  agree, look to the ladder. Combined with an independent siren H0, this distinguishes early from
  late origins without the distance ladder.
- **Shape-wall firing:** a late-time-only modification that delivers at least 8% at fixed r_d and
  θ_s while keeping BAO, SNe and growth fits would refute the current shape constraint.
- **Per-slot checks:** a ladder calibration that moves the local value toward 69 would close the
  residual without new physics; a sound-horizon change would answer Q23, not Q24.

## 5. The honest stop

**Unreached, not unreachable.** Fig 13 :134: "Unreachable is a verdict here, not a silence." Fig 8
:39: "So a search that finds nothing is never a proof of absence." The figures fix the shape and
the refuter and stop at the limit of the available instruments. Which slot is live and which
physics, if any, occupies the open slot are `gap` items, recorded with their lineage so a later
reader can locate the error rather than only feel it. Indiscernibility is not identity.
