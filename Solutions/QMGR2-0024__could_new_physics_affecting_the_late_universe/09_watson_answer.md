# WATSON answer: could late-universe new physics explain the Hubble difference?

**Mode: `specified` on per-slot and union claims and on the shape of an admissible import,
`general` on the structural barrier form, `gap` on the named physics.**

**Status: SIMULATED DERIVATION, not a ground reading.** This run synthesizes material in

`07_the_tether_answer.md`. It is checked for internal consistency against the figures. By its own

## QUESTION

Decide, in effect terms, whether new physics affecting the late universe can close the Hubble
difference. Option A: yes, a new low-redshift degree of freedom moves the late-slot H0 toward the
early inference and closes the gap. Option B: no, the live residual is in the ladder calibration or
in the early sound-horizon inference, so late physics is the wrong lever. What each leads to: A
requires a provenanced import with a stated shape and a refuter; B requires a measurement fix or an
early-universe answer (Q23). The figures are asked for the shape and the reachability verdict, not
for a model name.

## DOMAIN CENSUS

R by arity, symmetry and scale, then one line per domain with actors / currency / anchor, partition
or union, and order by dependency.

- **R (the residual).** The Hubble difference, arity three slots: early inference (67.4 ± 0.5 under
  ΛCDM), local ladder (about 73), inverse ladder / sound-horizon-free (68.5 to 69.4). Symmetry: the
  slots are not exchangeable; one returns a derived quantity, one a calibrated measurement. Scale:
  the value is late-time, the inference crosses to the early scale. Fig 9 :55: "A bundled problem is
  usually a union of overlapping conditions, not a list of separate ones."
- **Domain 1, early inference.** Actors: photons, baryons, r_d, θ*. Currency: comoving distance to
  last scattering over sound horizon. Anchor: the CMB acoustic peaks, a genuine anchor for θ* but
  not for H0. Derived inference under a model.
- **Domain 2, local ladder.** Actors: Cepheids, SNe Ia, TRGB, masers. Currency: calibrated distances.
  Anchor: geometric first-rung distances. Measurement with a calibration chain.
- **Domain 3, inverse ladder.** Actors: BAO tracers, BBN baryon density, uncalibrated SNe. Currency:
  H0 r_d and E(z). Anchor: BAO + BBN + θ*, independent of Cepheids. Low-redshift measurement.
- **Domain 4, proposed late new physics.** Actors: w(z), modified gravity, dark-sector exchange,
  backreaction. Currency: changes to H(z) at low z. Anchor: not yet supplied; must come from D3 or
  D5. This is an import, not yet a domain.
- **Domain 5, verification.** Actors: lensing time delays, standard sirens, future surveys.
  Currency: H0-independent dimensionless ratios. Anchor: an instrument that does not update on any
  model's prediction.
- **Partition or union:** a union of overlapping conditions, not a partition. The three measurement
  slots overlap in data.
- **Order by dependency:** localize the live slot, describe R in effect terms, choose a frame, test
  the import, settle against the ground (Fig 3 :20).

Strain test: "early versus late" as a partition was tried and failed because the inverse-ladder
slot is neither. Re-census kept a three-slot union. No strain remains there.

## VERDICT (from the figures)

Yes in principle, no as a derivation, and not demonstrated at this budget. A late-universe degree
of freedom is an import, the only operator that moves a model's wall, and cannot be minted from
inside ΛCDM. The figures return the shape an admissible import must have: change low-z H(z) enough
to move H0, leave r_d and θ_s fixed, stay anchored to BAO, SNe and growth. They do not name the
physics, and they do not say which slot is live. The difference is a three-slot union, not an
averaged number or a clean binary. World analyses currently find late-only models short of the
shape, but that is secondary evidence at a budget and shares an evidence pool. Verdict mode:
`specified` on the shape and union, `general` on the barrier form, `gap` on the named physics and
the live slot. Ungrounded candidate.

- **cost if wrong:** bounded and locatable. The packet is mode-labelled, states refuters before
  the conclusion and records the lineage, so an error is findable. The real cost is spending the
  independence of outside readers by sending a theory before the residual, which Fig 2 :42 prices
  ("Buy independence in proportion to the cost of being wrong."). That cost is avoidable by leading
  with the shape claim and its refuter.
- **undoable:** yes. The world no-go statements and the three-slot census are separable from the
  figure shape. A new measurement can move a slot without withdrawing the union structure, and no
  figure line that was asserted has to be retracted for a physics result to change.
- **reversed by:** a ground reading, not agreement. An H0-independent lensing-plus-siren null test
  that locates the residual in the expansion history; a late-time-only modification producing the
  required shift at fixed r_d and θ_s with all fits intact; a demonstration that the local ladder
  and inverse ladder share a dominant systematic; or a figure re-census that admits a new operator
  moving the wall. Until one fires, the verdict stands as `candidate`.
