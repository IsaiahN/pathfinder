# Outreach draft: check the output before you decide about the theory

**Use:** one-to-one, or as a cover letter attached to a submission through the public venues in
`03_public_contact_points.csv`. **Not** for bulk send.

**Hard precondition:** do not send until the per-slot residual and the union claim (F1, F2 in
`01_figure_grounded_audit.md`) are stated in the recipient's own terms. The output must survive
validation or the two-stage offer collapses on contact.

---

> measurement slot, not averaged into one number. At least three slots are live: the CMB-inferred
> value under ΛCDM (about 67.4, derived from the acoustic scale and the sound horizon, not a direct

> not one hard question and not a clean early-versus-late split. The figure this comes from says a
> live residual must be indexed by slot: "The residual is indexed by object slot, not aggregated."
>
> **What follows for late-universe new physics.** A genuinely new low-redshift degree of freedom is
> an import into the model's closure, not something derivable by re-arranging the model's existing
> parameters. "MINT composes inside the closure and can never add an atom" (Fig 6 :21). So the
> framework does not categorically forbid it, and it does not derive it. It returns the shape such
> an import must have if it is to close the difference: change the low-redshift expansion history
> enough to move H0, leave the sound horizon and the acoustic scale fixed, and stay ranged against
> BAO, supernovae and growth. "you get a kind of thing, never the answer itself" (Fig 9 :32). It does
> not name the physics.

> 3. Evidence that the local ladder and the inverse ladder share a dominant systematic, so they

## Why this version is ordered this way

| the move | figure-grounded reason |
|---|---|
| Output first, theory second | Fig 10 :52, "Bring a characterised residual, never the source." Frames range against the ground separately (Fig 2). |
| Do not ask for agreement | Fig 2 :35, "Alignment is triangulation, not negotiation. A mirror cannot correct you." |
| Pre-registered refuter | A falsifier written before the run is a design instrument, not only an honesty device. |
| Mode stated on the claim | `THE_FORMULA.md`: "Mode. general · specified · grounded. State which." |
| Shape, not a name | Fig 9 :32, "you get a kind of thing, never the answer itself"; local figures README: "a shape, not a name." |
| Import distinguished from mint | Fig 6 :21-22; the message never claims the physics is derived. |
| Anchor discipline | Fig 2 :29, an anchor "does not change its answer because of what any frame thinks." The acoustic scale qualifies; H0 under ΛCDM does not. |
| No request for trust or prestige | *The Recursive Self-Improvement Trap*: "Prestige can fund an action. It can give an action a budget to be tried. But it cannot settle an action. Only the ground can settle it." |
| Offer the formula as a method to reuse | Fig 4 :22, "Only methods cross up. Only head starts come down." |
| Each user writes their own refuter | A frame cannot check the direction of its own question; the output cannot validate itself. |

## Two-stage offer, explicit

- **Stage 1:** send the per-slot residual, the shape and the refuter. Ask what the recipient would
  count as a failure. Record that verdict before showing any theory.
- **Stage 2, opt-in only:** if they are curious, offer the three local framework files. The figures
  are the authority, not the summary.
- **Stage 3, opt-in only:** offer the formula as a method. Each user states their own ground and
  writes their own refuter before running it.

Their agreement at stage 2 or 3 is never pooled as corroboration (Fig 2 :40: "agreement about the
world is worthless when the agreeing frames share an evidence pool").
