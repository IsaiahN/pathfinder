## What the wider literature contributes

The papers above split into two groups that must not be pooled as one witness:

1. **No-go / constraint papers** (shape wall, distance duality, H0 World Cup, mid-time guide).
   These are one broad evidence pool: they use overlapping CMB, BAO and supernova datasets. Their
   agreement is corroborative within that pool, not three independent witnesses.
2. **Reframing papers** (Pantos and Perivolaropoulos; DESI 2024 VI; the null test). These are lower
   overlap: they change the instrument or the framing rather than fitting another late-time model.
   Pantos challenges the early-versus-late framing itself; the null test proposes an H0-independent
   discriminator. These are the ones that bear on the figure claim that the residual must be
   localized per slot before a frame is chosen.

A physical footnote, not a figure claim: the word "wall" in the shape-wall paper names a likelihood
constraint surface at fixed r_d and θ_s. The figures' "wall" (Fig 6) names closure of a library.
They rhyme; they are not the same object. This is recorded as strain S1 in `01`.

## The two-stage offer

- **Stage 1:** validate the output against the ground. The recipient checks the per-slot claim
  without holding the framework's interior.
- **Stage 2, opt-in:** if curious, read the framework that produced it. Their verdict on stage 1
  must already be recorded, and their later agreement is never pooled as corroboration.
- **Stage 3, opt-in:** use the formula recursively on a problem with its own ground. Each user
  writes a refuter before the run and states the mode of the result.

**Hard precondition:** stage 1 only works if the output survives. As drafted in the source
question, "the same difference" is an averaged H0 number with no slot structure; that is the
defect F1 fixes. Lead with the corrected shape and the refuter.
