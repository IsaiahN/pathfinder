## 1. A shape is a constraint, not a filler

The local figures README states the limit: the framework returns "what a missing thing would have
to look like (a shape, not a name)." Fig 9 :32: "you get a kind of thing, never the answer itself."
Fig 13 :134: "Unreachable is a verdict here, not a silence" and the surrounding lines say the
system "It can say the question is not one question and must be split before it is searched again."

From *The Library of Babel* (opened 2026-11-23): "you can cut your search short if the set of books
returned is small and descriptive enough that it provides a general shape of the answer that
satisfies your question."

So a **shape** is the narrowed form of an answer you can state before you can name it. It
constrains; it does not fill. For this question:

- an averaged "H0 tension" is not a shape, it is an illegitimate average (Fig 1 :19, :22);
- "some low-redshift modification" is not a shape, it is a direction;
- the shape is the **constraint set**: move low-z H(z) enough to shift H0, leave r_d and θ_s fixed,
  remain ranged against BAO, SNe and growth. That can be stated before naming any model.

## 2. How a shape is obtained

- *Library of Babel*: use the vocabulary already held that is closest to the thing, look for
  recurring new terms that overlap or correlate with it, recurse: "With each new term gained, you
  are able to narrow the space by definition and deepen your vocabulary."
- Fig 6 :30: "The description travels. Fails by being sharpened into terms only we can read. Cast it
  in effect terms." Fig 9 :30-31: "your words are borrowed too, so describe the effect rather than
  the cause." For this question the effect terms are "acoustic scale," "sound horizon,"
  "low-redshift expansion shape," not a model name.
- *Library of Babel* on bundles: "Perhaps your problem was a bundle or conflation of several
  problems masquerading as one?" This is the essay-level statement of Fig 9 :54-57. The Hubble
  difference is such a bundle.
- Fig 6 :32: "An instrument is not built from a description. It is improved from a worse instrument
  already returning something." The null-test proposal (Kouniatalis and Saridakis 2026) fits this:
  it extends instruments already returning lensing and siren distances.

## 3. What licenses a name

Only two things, both outside the shape itself:

1. **The ground settles it.** Fig 2 :29: an anchor "does not change its answer because of what any
   frame thinks." Formula step 5: "the ground settles it. Verified only by what pays."
2. **A provenanced import from a frame whose closure differs**, with a source gate and a shadow
   test (Fig 6 :23, Fig 8 :52).

For this question, naming w0waCDM, f(R) gravity or backreaction is currently a binding from the
physics frame onto the open slot, not something the figures derive. It becomes licensed only if an
instrument reports the required low-z deviation (the null test, standard sirens, DESI/Euclid
lensing) and the import pays the MDL bargain (Fig 5 :26).

## 4. The source-discrimination point

The designated essay *The Hard Problem is About Source* (not reopened this session, so not quoted)
argues that a reading does not determine its source. The figure-level version is enough here: Fig 2
:31 says independence is how little two frames draw on the same evidence. The early slot reads the
acoustic scale and infers H0 under a model; the local ladder reads distances; the inverse ladder
reads BAO + BBN. Several sources can give a similar H0 reading, so the reading alone cannot name
the source of the discrepancy. What is owed is a discriminator, not a name.

*The Recursive Self-Improvement Trap* (opened 2026-11-23): "the output is only as good as the
validator. The agent, or the tool, or the frame, or the theory, cannot validate itself." This is
why this whole folder is labelled a simulated derivation.

## 5. Q: does "shape, not a name" forbid discussing late-universe new physics at all?

No. **Answer with correction.** The README's phrase governs a *missing* primitive, one not held in
Γ. Here the new low-redshift degree of freedom really is missing from ΛCDM's atoms, so the rule
applies in its strict form: the figures return the shape of what would close the residual; they do
not select a name. But discussing candidate physics is allowed as `specified` binding work provided
the provenance is recorded and the shape is not claimed as derived.

Earlier in this project I overstated the rule as a ban on naming any occupied slot; that wider ban
was corrected in the exemplar's `05` section 5b and is not repeated here. The narrower, correct
form is: shape-not-a-name governs what the framework can *license*, not what a physicist may
hypothesise.

## 6. Two consequences for this question

**Prestige is not action.** From *The Recursive Self-Improvement Trap* (opened): "Prestige can fund
an action. It can give an action a budget to be tried. But it cannot settle an action. Only the
ground can settle it. The Ground Maintainer must be blind to prestige." A list of famous
cosmologists who agree with a late-time model is the named collapse, not corroboration. The
legitimate ask gives the shape a budget to be tried and lets an instrument settle it.

**My own audit is a simulation.** Same essay: "LLMs can quickly simulate an approximate answer/
output with the formula found in the architecture post." and "the truly testable output would need
to be derived from the real architecture or working the formula yourself." LLM output is a
simulacrum. `01` is therefore checked for internal consistency against the figures; it is not a
test of the Hubble-tension claim and cannot settle anything it reports on.
