# Expert verification packet - late-universe new physics and the Hubble difference

**What this is:** a claim in a form a cosmologist can check. Mode, derivation and refuters are
stated before the conclusion, so a reader knows what to look for and what would settle it.

**Mode: `specified` on the per-slot and union claims and on the shape of an admissible import,
`general` on the structural barrier form, `gap` on the named physics. Not `grounded`.** No
measurement at the required precision is claimed.

**Status:** a simulated derivation checked for internal consistency against the figures, not a
ground reading. It cannot settle the question it reports on.

## 1. The claim, in one paragraph

The Hubble difference must be indexed per measurement slot, not averaged. Three slots are live:
the CMB-inferred H0 under ΛCDM (67.4 ± 0.5, derived from the acoustic scale and the sound horizon),
the local distance ladder (about 73), and an inverse-ladder / sound-horizon-free set (about 68.5 to
69.4, independent of Cepheids). These form a union of overlapping conditions, not a clean
early-versus-late split. Genuinely new low-redshift physics is an import into the model's closure,
not something derivable from its existing atoms. The figures return the shape such an import must
have, change low-z H(z) enough to move H0, leave r_d and θ_s fixed, and remain ranged against BAO,
supernovae and growth; they do not name the physics. Current world analyses find late-time-only
models short of that shape, but that is secondary evidence at a budget, not the verdict.

## 2. Three checkable sub-claims

### C1 - framework-internal; checkable by reading Figure 1 and Figure 9

- **Statement:** the residual is per-slot and the problem is a union, not one H0 number and not a
  two-way partition.
- **Source, verbatim:** Fig 1 :19 - "The residual is indexed by object slot, not aggregated." Fig 9
  :55 - "A bundled problem is usually a union of overlapping conditions, not a list of separate
  ones." Also Fig 9 :57 - "What remains is smaller and it is also categorically different: a case
  in two conditions is not a harder case of either."
- **What to check:** are the three slots genuinely different instruments with different systematics,
  or do they share a dominant systematic that collapses them to one frame?
- **Refuter:** a single global discrepancy with no per-slot structure; or a demonstrated shared
  dominant systematic between the local ladder and the inverse ladder.

### C2 - framework-internal; checkable by reading Figure 6 and Figure 13

- **Statement:** late new physics is an import, not a mint, and the figures return a shape.
- **Source, verbatim:** Fig 6 :21 - "MINT composes inside the closure and can never add an atom."
  Fig 6 :14 - "the only operator that moves the wall". Fig 9 :32 - "you get a kind of thing, never
  the answer itself". Fig 13 :115 - "The set is closed. The arrangements are not. That gap is why
  import exists."
- **What to check:** does a proposed fix add a degree of freedom to ΛCDM (import) or re-fit existing
  parameters (mint)? Does it leave r_d and θ_s fixed and stay anchored to BAO, SNe and growth?
- **Refuter:** an effect already expressible inside ΛCDM's closure that closes the residual by
  re-arrangement alone; or an import that succeeds while changing the acoustic scale, which would
  answer Q23 rather than Q24.

### C3 - physics-facing; checkable against current data

- **Statement:** late-time-only modifications currently fall short of the shape, so the verdict is
  "unreachable at this budget," not "underivable in principle."
- **Source, verbatim (figure side):** Fig 8 :39 - "So a search that finds nothing is never a proof
  of absence." Fig 13 :134 - "Unreachable is a verdict here, not a silence". (The numerical failure
  is a world result, not a figure line.)
- **What to check:** Sabogal et al. 2026 report δH0/H0 ≲ 2% at fixed r_d and θ_s against roughly 8%
  needed; Tiwari et al. 2026 find no late-only resolution even with distance-duality violation;
  Schöneberg et al. 2026 find late scenarios do not improve on ΛCDM. Do these hold under the
  recipient's own dataset choices?
- **Refuter:** a late-time-only modification delivering at least 8% at fixed r_d and θ_s with
  BAO, SNe and growth fits inside current constraints.

## 3. What is explicitly not claimed

- Not that late-universe new physics is impossible; it is allowed as a provenanced import.
- Not a named model. The figures return a shape, not a name.
- Not that early-universe physics (Q23) is excluded. The union has more than one live condition.
- Not that the world no-go papers settle the question. They share datasets as one evidence pool.
- Not `grounded`. No instrument has returned the required late-time deviation.
- Not that agreement among experts would count as corroboration.

## 5. What would settle it

1. **Null test:** an H0-independent lensing-time-delay plus standard-siren ratio (Kouniatalis and
   Saridakis 2026). Disagreement between early and late determinations locates the residual in the
   expansion history; agreement points to the ladder.
2. **Shape-wall firing:** a late-only import producing the required shift at fixed r_d and θ_s.
3. **Ladder check:** a calibration revision that moves the local value toward the inverse-ladder
   value, closing the residual without new physics.

## 6. What an expert can check in twenty minutes

1. Read Fig 1 :19, Fig 6 :21, Fig 9 :32, Fig 13 :115, four short passages.
2. Decide whether the three-slot union in C1 matches the actual measurement landscape.
3. Name one instrument that would distinguish an early from a late origin of the residual, or say

4. Check whether the shape-wall bound in C3 survives your preferred dataset combination.
5. Send the refutation. A failure is more useful than an endorsement.
