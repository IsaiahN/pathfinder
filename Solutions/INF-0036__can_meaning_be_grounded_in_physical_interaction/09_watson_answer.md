## QUESTION

Decide, in effect terms, what the Tether says about grounding meaning in physical interaction without a human-like interpreter. Option A: meaning is anchored in the physical contact that settles what a signal affords and what pays. Option B: meaning is anchored in an interpreter's understanding, with physical interaction supplying only correlation and causation. What each leads to: A gives a checkable anchor and a route to test a system; B gives either an infinite regress of interpreters or a convention, both of which the figures forbid as grounds. The question bundles two conditions, grounding and interpreter-necessity, so this is a union compound with separate loops and separate verdicts (Fig 9 :54).

## DOMAIN CENSUS

- **R (arity / symmetry / scale).** Arity: a four-place relation (signal, system, world or affordance, consequence), fusing a grounding condition and an interpreter condition. Symmetry: aboutness is ordered (signal about X for system Y); physical contact has direction (affordance, production). Ordered bonds apply, symmetric bonds flatten the very difference at issue. Scale: a settled signal-term becomes an atom at the next level (Fig 12 :104), so the interpreter requirement is level-relative.
- **Domain 1, coding / information frame.** Actors: signals, codes, channels, entropy, decoders. Currency: bits, compressibility, correlation. Anchor: none nameable for meaning. Verdict: a `gap`, not a domain for this residual until an outside anchor is supplied.
- **Domain 2, physical interaction / ground.** Actors: systems in contact, affordances, consequences, instruments, habitat. Currency: residual `R = |Γ(b,a) - o'|` per slot, what pays. Anchor: the ground, which settles and does not update (Fig 2 :29-30). Verdict: `yes, grounded in principle` at `general` mode.
- **Domain 3, interpreter / convention.** Actors: interpreters, vocabularies, communities, conventions. Currency: agreement, shared meaning. Anchor: an interpreter's reading, which moves with the frames. Verdict: `no, not as a ground`; conventional meaning is a real product but is ungrounded.
- **Domain 4, composition / structure.** Actors: atoms, bonds, molecules, arrangements. Currency: bits price the description, actions price finding out whether it holds. Anchor: the bond carries information the operands do not (Fig 12 :5). Verdict: content is carried by arrangement; no extra interpreter atom needed.
- **Partition or union:** D2 and D3 overlap in D4, a compound with its own loop. D1 is upstream substrate, not a domain for meaning. Unbundle before searching (Fig 9 :54).
- **Order by dependency:** contact, then terms for affordances, then an objective comparison, then convention. Refuse the interpreter-as-anchor before minting (Formula step 2).

## VERDICT (from the figures)

Per domain, never averaged: D1 is a `gap` for meaning; D2 is `yes, grounded in principle` at `general` mode; D3 is `no, not as a ground`, with conventional meaning real but ungrounded; D4 says content is carried by priced bonds with no extra interpreter atom. Overall the figures license a refusal of the human-like interpreter as the anchor, not a universal claim that all physical interaction is meaningful. Whether a given system has aboutness remains `specified` and unmeasured until an instrument shows terms for affordances and an objective comparison. The convention caveat stays attached: Fig 2 :41 and Fig 10 :49 are both in force.

- **cost if wrong:** bounded and locatable. The shape is mode-labelled, its sought-for form is bound as an ordinary term, and refuters were written before the conclusion, so an error is findable. The avoidable cost is spending a reader's independence by sending the framework before asking for a verdict (Fig 10 :42); Fig 2 :42 prices it: "Buy independence in proportion to the cost of being wrong." That cost is avoided by leading with the shape claim and its refuter.
- **undoable:** yes. The shape can be withdrawn without deleting anything; a failed check removes a term's standing to be composed over rather than striking a record. No primitive is withdrawn and no prior claim has to be repaired to retract it.
- **reversed by:** a ground reading, never agreement. A source-discriminating instrument that settles aboutness without contact; a demonstration of contact-free settlement with no terms for affordances; a signal carrying content by declaration alone that still passes the anchor test; or an exhaustive proof of interpreter absence in a sealed frame. Until one fires, the verdict stands as `candidate`.
