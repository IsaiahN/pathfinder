## 1. A shape is a constraint you can state before you can name the answer

*The Library of Babel*: *"You can cut your search short if the set of books returned is small and descriptive enough that it provides a general shape of the answer that satisfies your question."*

For INF-0036, the shape is:

- a nameable anchor that settles what a signal is about for a system and does not move when a frame pushes on it;
- a typed relation carrying the content, so that the signal is not just correlated with its object;
- a stated failure condition that would withdraw the claim.

That shape is not "the interpreter is unnecessary, full stop." It is the narrower claim that the *anchor* cannot be a frame. A particular mechanism (sensorimotor coupling, affordance detection, a bee's odometer) is a name, and names require a ground or a provenanced import.

**Correction to my own first reading (recorded, not erased).** I initially wrote the shape as "meaning without interpreters." That overstates it. The essays and Fig 2 force the distinction between a human-like interpreter (a frame, not an anchor) and a receiver that is simply a bounded system in contact with the residual (a frame that *can* be an anchor candidate only if it satisfies Fig 2 :29-30, which a moving frame does not). The bee-dance sources make this concrete: the bees interpret the dance, and they are not human-like. The claim is about the *kind* of anchor, not about the presence or absence of receivers. The corrected shape is in `01` §0 and is carried into every other file.

## 2. How a shape is obtained

- *Library of Babel*: use the vocabulary you already have that is closest to the thing, look for recurring new terms that overlap or correlate with it, recurse. *"With each new term gained, you narrow the space and deepen your vocabulary."*
- Fig 6 :30: *"The description travels. Fails by being sharpened into terms only we can read. Cast it in effect terms."* Fig 9 :30-31: *"your words are borrowed too, so describe the effect rather than the cause."*
- *Decoding Tacit Knowledge*: *"When contact isn't possible, the best you can send is a plain description of what you'd expect to see, because anyone can check that against the world."*
- *Library of Babel* on stopping: *"You will know that you are done because the finish line will also be the starting place"*, with the paradox that by then you have *"composed the answer through the process of locating it."*

Applied here: describing the residual in effect terms gave "what settles what a signal is about," which immediately separated the anchor question from the mechanism question. That separation is the shape.

## 3. What licenses a name

Two things, both outside the shape itself:

1. **The ground settles it.** Formula step 5: *"the ground settles it. Verified only by what pays. A gate is not the ground."* The canonical README: the framework *"stops at the limit of what the available instruments can distinguish, and records the lineage so the stop is honest."*
2. **A provenanced import from a frame whose closure differs** (Fig 6 :23, Fig 8 :50-52), with a source gate and a shadow test.

*They're Scanning Books When They Should Be Studying Nature* sharpens this: *"The real import is not a new term in the existing space. It is a new dimension of the space."* And: *"A minimum viable set of six terms is a human's careful guess at reality's compression axes; another sensorium's minimum set need not be those six."* Plus: *"Declaring the primitives does not make them neutral. It makes the choice visible."*

For INF-0036, "the bee's waggle run encodes distance" is a name licensed by a published experiment (the ground of animal behaviour). "A human-like interpreter is not required" is a shape, and it is all the framework supplies.

## 4. Source, not reading: the metamerism warning

*The Hard Problem is About Source* gives the mechanism. An instrument reads green and *"cannot comment on the source"*: one green light, or yellow plus blue, or RGB, or CMYK, or a scheme not yet discovered. *"If several paths create the same behavior, then behavior is a projection with lossy information (it cannot define consciousness in full)."* And: *"An instrument that can't distinguish these cases demonstrates its own inadequacy, it cannot claim anything substantial about what is being measured or what causes it."* It was the spectrometer, not an argument, that resolved it.

Applied here: observing that a system behaves *as if* a signal means something does not settle the source of that aboutness. Several arrangements (grounded contact, shared convention, a sophisticated decoder with no contact) can produce the same reading. Naming the source from the behaviour is the metamerism error. What is owed is a source-discriminating instrument, which is exactly the requirement in F6 of `01_figure_grounded_audit.md`.

*The Recursive Self-Improvement Trap*: *"the output is only as good as the validator. The agent, or the tool, or the frame, or the theory, cannot validate itself."*

## 5. Q for this question: what licenses "has meaning" for a non-human system?

**Answered at shape level, not at name level.** Nothing in the framework licenses a name here. The framework requires: a ground that settles, a bond that carries content, and an instrument that shows terms for affordances and a comparison counting as progress. A name (this signal means *that* nectar source is 2 km away) is licensed by the experiment that settles it, not by the framework.

**Does the record commit the forbidden error?** If someone answers INF-0036 with "therefore this system understands," yes. That names a source from a reading. If someone answers with "physical interaction can be the anchor; whether it is in this system is measured," no. The shape is honoured.

## 5b. Correction to my own §3 and §4 (kept visible)

I initially treated "shape, not a name" as a ban on naming any component of the answer. The canonical phrase governs a *missing* thing, a primitive not held in Γ. Here the relevant components (signal, typed bond, ground, habitat, frame) are already in the framework's vocabulary. So naming them is not forbidden. What remains forbidden is:

- naming a specific mechanism as *the* answer when the framework returns only a shape;
- naming the source of an aboutness reading without a discriminator (the metamerism point, which is **not** retracted);
- turning a binding from an outside frame (bee dance, sensorimotor coupling) into an import that moves the wall, when Fig 6 says *"IMPORT adds an atom"* and this one adds none.

Both the original and the narrowed reading are preserved here so neither is quietly lost. Triangulated (canonical README + Fig 6 + Fig 13).

## 6. Two consequences that govern the rest of the folder

**Prestige is not action.** *The Recursive Self-Improvement Trap*: *"Prestige must never be allowed to validate an action directly... Prestige can fund an action. It can give an action a budget to be tried. But it cannot settle an action. Only the ground can settle it. The Ground Maintainer must be blind to prestige."* A list of prestigious people who agree with the shape is the named collapse. The legitimate ask is a budget to try it, with the ground settling it.

**This audit is a simulation, not a ground reading.** Same essay: *"LLMs can quickly simulate an approximate answer/output with the formula found in the architecture post... However, the truly testable output would need to be derived from the real architecture or working the formula yourself."* The architecture post adds the caveat that such outputs *"could never be verified if the solutions presented were not contrived to fit the formula"* and must be seen as *"simulacra."* So `01`, `06`, `07`, `09` and `11` are simulated derivations checked for internal consistency against the figures. They are **not** tests of the claim and cannot settle anything they report on.

## 7. Tacit knowledge and contact (why the question is hard in practice)

*Decoding Tacit Knowledge*: *"The understanding has to be rebuilt by the learner, and the way it gets rebuilt is contact: trying, being corrected, and trying again, in both directions."* And: *"Context, subtext and pretext decide what a message means to you. None of them decides whether it is true. Only the ground does that."*

That is the practical version of the whole answer. Context, subtext and pretext fix what a message means *to a reader*; they are convention and history, not ground. *"An improvement that does not change contact changes nothing, however much it improves"* (Fig 11 :40, quoted in the essay). A system with a perfect decoder but no contact has the reading and not the aboutness. This is why the question cannot be settled by introspecting an interpreter and why the refuter in `01` §4 points at contact.

## 8. Outputs are not generators

*They're Scanning Books*: *"A corpus is the compressed output of human minds; the capacity that produced it is a generator, and outputs do not contain their generator."* A system trained only on the record of interpretations has the traces of aboutness without the contact that produced them. Fig 4 :25: *"A recording carried upward looks like knowledge and is a description of one occasion."* For INF-0036 this is the sharpest practical warning: a fluent report that a signal "means X" is an output, and it is not evidence that the system has a generator grounded in the world.
