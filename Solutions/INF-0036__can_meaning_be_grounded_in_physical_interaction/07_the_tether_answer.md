# The Tether's answer: can meaning be grounded in physical interaction without a human-like interpreter?

**Mode: `general` on the structural answer, `specified` on any system-facing claim. Not `grounded`.** Simulated derivation checked for internal consistency against the figures, not a ground reading (`05_what_shape_means.md` §6). It cannot settle the question it reports on.

## 1. The residual it answers

The question bundles two conditions (Fig 9 :54, a union not a partition):

1. **Grounding condition:** is there an anchor for aboutness in physical interaction?
2. **Interpreter condition:** must that anchor be a human-like interpreter?

They overlap but are not the same question. The verdicts below are kept separate and are never averaged.

**Overall, as a refusal:** the figures license saying that a human-like interpreter is not *required as the anchor*, because the anchor for a question about contact with the world is the ground. They do **not** license saying that every physical interaction has meaning, or that no system ever has a receiver. Whether a given system has aboutness is a `specified`, unmeasured question (F6).

## 3. The shape

1. **The anchor test decides.** Fig 2 :29-30: *"An anchor is whatever settles the question and does not change its answer because of what any frame thinks."* Fig 2 :36: *"An anchor is legitimate when the question is constitutively about it."* A question constitutively about contact with the world is settled by the world, not by a frame's attribution.
2. **A frame cannot be that anchor.** Symbols table: a frame is any bounded system with a library. If the "meaning" changes when the frame changes its mind, it is Fig 2's mutual-update collapse, not a verdict.
3. **Content is carried by the typed bond, not by a further entity.** Fig 12 :94: *"The arrangement is the substance."* Fig 12 :101: *"A bond that costs nothing is a bond that carries nothing, which is the flat case."* Adding an interpreter as the thing that confers meaning renames the bond as an entity and charges it nothing.
4. **Level-relativity kills the regress.** Fig 12 :104: *"A settled molecule becomes an atom for whatever composes over it. So irreducible is a fact about a level and never about the world"*; Fig 10 :68: *"Different actors, a different currency, a different ground, and a lossy transform between. That is why there is no top rung and no regress: a generator crosses, an instance does not."*
5. **The interpreter label is retrospective.** Fig 11 :36: *"Where nothing maintains anything, intelligence is a label applied afterwards to a lineage that met its ground."* Fig 11 :40: *"An improvement that does not change contact changes nothing, however much it improves."*
6. **Conventions are real but ungrounded.** Fig 2 :41: *"efficient for a convention: agreement about a shared meaning is the product, not a corruption of it"*; Fig 10 :49: *"Declaring a meaning does not ground it."* Both halves are required.

## 4. What it explicitly does not say

- Not that every physical interaction has meaning. The middle link (terms for affordances, a comparison counting as progress) is measured, not assumed (Fig 3 :7, :10, :27).
- Not that receivers are absent. Bees interpret the waggle dance; they are simply not human-like interpreters.
- Not that convention is corruption. Shared meaning is a product of convention (Fig 2 :41).
- Not that a signal's shape determines its content. Same atoms, different bond, different substance (Fig 12 :117).
- Not that this package is `grounded`. No measurement was taken.
- Not a theory of everything, and no last room (Fig 7 :6, Fig 4 :33).

## 5. What would settle it - the ground

A source-discriminating instrument, not a further argument. From *The Hard Problem is About Source*: an instrument that reads green *"cannot comment on the source"*; the spectrometer resolved it. Here:

1. **Affordance instrument:** does the system have terms for what a situation offers (Fig 3 :7)? A measurement, not a declaration.
2. **Objective test:** can it say what counts as progress in its own terms (Fig 3 :10; Fig 12 :112, *"A goal is a comparison"*).
3. **Source-discriminator:** can an instrument distinguish content grounded in contact from content produced by a shared code or a fluent decoder with no contact? If not, the case stays below the instrument limit and the honest report is `specified, unmeasured`.
4. **Standing falsifiers from `01` §4:** anchor reversal, contact-free settlement, free content, exhaustive absence of an interpreter.

## 6. Cost if wrong, undoability, reversal

- **Cost if wrong:** bounded and locatable. The packet states its mode, binds the sought-for shape as an ordinary term and writes refuters before the conclusion, so an error is findable rather than invisible. The real avoidable cost is spending a reader's independence by sending the whole framework before asking for a verdict (Fig 10 :42).
- **Undoable:** yes. The shape can be withdrawn without deleting anything; refuters are written in advance and a failed check simply removes the claim's standing to be composed over. No primitive has to be withdrawn.
- **Reversed by:** a ground reading, never agreement. A source-discriminating instrument that settles aboutness without contact, a demonstration of contact-free settlement, a signal carrying content by declaration alone, or an exhaustive proof of interpreter absence in a sealed frame. Until one fires, the verdict stands as `candidate`.

## 7. The honest stop

**Unreached, not unreachable.** Fig 13 :134: *"Unreachable is a verdict here, not a silence"*; Fig 8 :39: *"So a search that finds nothing is never a proof of absence."* The framework returns a shape, not a mechanism, and stops at the limit of the available instruments. *They're Scanning Books*: *"A minimum viable set of six terms is a human's careful guess at reality's compression axes; another sensorium's minimum set need not be those six."* The record of the route is what keeps this checkable. *"Indiscernibility is not identity."*

## 8. One-paragraph version for outreach

> The figures do not answer "is meaning grounded in physical interaction" by declaring a mechanism. They answer the anchor question. Whatever settles what a signal is about for a system must not change its answer when a frame changes its mind, and a human-like interpreter is exactly such a frame: bounded, library-bearing, and movable. What can play the anchor is the physical contact that settles what the signal affords and what pays when the system acts on it. Content itself is carried by the typed bond between signal and consequence, so the same signal bonded differently carries different content and no extra interpreter atom is needed to explain that. The apparent regress of interpreters is a level error: a settled signal-term becomes an atom for the next level, and actors, currency and ground change at each crossing. Two caveats stay attached. Shared meaning really is a product of convention; it just does not settle aboutness against the world. And whether any particular system has aboutness is a measured question about terms for affordances and a comparison counting as progress, not something this derivation can assert. The result is a shape with refuters written in advance, not a verdict about a system.
