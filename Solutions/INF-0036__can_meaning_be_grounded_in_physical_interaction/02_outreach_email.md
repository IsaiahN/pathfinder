# Outreach draft - check the output before you decide about the framework

**Use:** one-to-one, or as a cover letter attached to a submission through the public venues in `03_public_contact_points.csv`. **Not** for bulk send.

**Hard precondition:** do not send until the shape claim and its refuter in `08_expert_verification_packet.md` survive a reader's check. A failure is more useful than an endorsement.

**Subject:** One checkable claim about meaning and physical contact, before any theory

> Hi [name],
>
> I have one specific output I would like you to check against what you already know. I am not asking you to read a framework first, and I am not asking you to agree. If the output does not survive, there is nothing else to look at.
>
> **The question I worked on:** can meaning be grounded in physical interaction without invoking a human-like interpreter?
>
> **The shape claim (`general` on the structure, `specified` on any particular system):** an anchor is whatever settles a question and does not change its answer because of what any frame thinks. If a signal's "meaning" changes when an interpreter changes its mind, that interpreter is not the anchor for what the signal is about for a system. What can play that role is the physical contact that settles what the signal affords and what pays when the system acts on it. A human-like interpreter is a frame, a retrospective label, or a participant in a convention. It is not the ground.
>
> **What I am not claiming:** that every physical interaction has meaning. To say whether a system has aboutness you still need an instrument that shows it has terms for what a situation affords and can say what would count as progress in its own terms. Until such an instrument exists, my answer stops at `specified` and does not settle the question.
>
> **The convention caveat, stated up front:** agreement about a shared meaning is a real product of a convention. It is not a corruption. The claim is only that a convention does not settle what a signal is about in the world. If I have those two mixed up, the claim is wrong.
>
> **What would refute the shape (written before I ran this):**
> 1. The answer to what a signal is about for a system changes when a frame pushes on the interpreter's attribution, and no physical contact measure settles it.
> 2. Aboutness is settled with no change in contact and no terms for what the signal affords.
> 3. A signal carries content with no priced bond to anything it affords or produces, purely by declaration.
> 4. A sealed frame is exhibited in which the absence of an interpreter is proved to the end.
>
> If any one holds, I withdraw the shape. A one-line "this fails because..." is more useful to me than agreement. If you would rather not engage, no reply is needed.
>
> **If it survives, and only then:** the sources are below. The figures are the authority, not my summary of them.

> - The formula and mode rule (local): `FRAMEWORK/figures/THE_FORMULA.md`

Write down what would refute your result *before* you run it, and state the mode of what you get. A reported failure is more useful to me than a reported success.

## Distribution rule

Stage 1: residual, shape claim, refuter, mode. Record the verdict before showing anything else.
Stage 2, opt-in only: the three links, with the note that the figures, not the summary, are the authority.
Stage 3, opt-in only: the formula as a reusable method, with the instruction that each user writes their own refuter and states their own mode.

A stage-2 or stage-3 agreement is never pooled as corroboration (Fig 2 :40). The person who checks stage 1 has already done the only part that counts.
