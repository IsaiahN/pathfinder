## 3. What the wider literature does and does not do

- Harnad, Shannon, the bee-dance work and the enactivism sources are **Outside** the framework and use independent evidence pools (cognitive science, engineering, animal behaviour, philosophy of biology). Their agreement with the figure verdict is corroboration, not identity. Their disagreement would send us back to the census, never flip the verdict.
- Harnad is the closest published statement of the question itself. He argues that meaning must be *intrinsic* to the system, not parasitic on the interpreter's head, and proposes bottom-up sensorimotor grounding. That is the same direction as F1 and F5, but it is a theory in cognitive science with its own evidence, not a Tether result.
- Shannon is the reason D1 is a `gap`: the quantity information theory measures is explicitly indifferent to semantics. Quoting this is not an argument against information theory; it is a scope statement.
- The bee-dance literature is the strongest empirical case for F2 to F6: a symbolic, directed signal whose content is fixed by what a non-human receiver does, with no human interpreter in the loop. It also supplies the honest limit: the bees *are* interpreters in the generic sense of bounded systems with a library, just not human-like ones. That is exactly the distinction the question asks for.
- The eight essays are high-overlap and are used only to read "shape," "prestige is not action," "source" and "outputs are not generators." They are never counted as an outside witness.

## 4. Distribution: offer, do not push

Figure-grounded reasons:

- **Fig 10 :52** - *"Bring a characterised residual, never the source."* Sending the full architecture before anyone has described a residual sends a recording, not a generator.
- **Fig 10 :42** - *"A verifier that can reconstruct the claim cannot verify it."* A recipient who has absorbed the theory before being asked for a verdict has joined the frame being checked.
- **Fig 9 :27-28** - *"describe it before you go looking, or any frame you pick will seem to fit."* Ask what they would expect to see if the shape claim were true before giving them the article.
- **Fig 2 :41** vs **Fig 10 :49** - state both halves. A pitch that says only "declaring a meaning does not ground it" invites the objection that conventional meaning is real, which it is.
- **Fig 9 :60** - *"Use filters for the budget and witnesses for the verdict. Never let a filter hand you a verdict."* A venue's prestige or an audience's receptiveness is a contact cost, never an evidence weight.

**Recommended order in a reply:** (1) the shape claim, its mode, its convention caveat and its refuter; (2) ask what they would count as a failure; (3) record their verdict; (4) only then the links, with the note that the figures, not the summary, are the authority.
