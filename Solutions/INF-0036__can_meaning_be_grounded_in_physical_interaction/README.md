## Verdict in one line (details in `07` and `09`)

Per domain, never averaged: coding frame gives `gap` for meaning; physical interaction gives `yes, grounded` at `general` mode; interpreter/convention gives `no, not as a ground` (conventional meaning is real but ungrounded); composition gives `meaning is carried by the bond`, with no extra interpreter atom required. Overall the figures license a refusal of the interpreter as the anchor, not a proof that every physical interaction has meaning.

## What is deliberately not here

- No personal emails of named individuals.
- No ranking of people by how likely they are to agree.
- No bulk-send plan.

Three reasons, in the figures' own words:

1. **A room does not upgrade the label.** Fig 7 :35: *"A room's agreement is variation, not a verdict. Selection between rooms is where the ground votes."* A list assembled for agreement is a room, not an anchor.
2. **Correlation disqualifies a verdict.** Fig 2 :40: *"disqualifying for a verdict: agreement about the world is worthless when the agreeing frames share an evidence pool."* Selecting recipients for receptiveness manufactures the disqualifying correlation.
3. **Asking for trust before checking spends independence.** Fig 10 :42: *"A verifier that can reconstruct the claim cannot verify it."* The first contact carries the residual and its refuter, never the framework's interior.

Legally: scraping personal emails for unsolicited bulk outreach breaches GDPR and CAN-SPAM style rules in many jurisdictions. That is a practical failure on top of the structural one.
