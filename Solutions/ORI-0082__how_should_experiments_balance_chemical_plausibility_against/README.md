## What the question is, in effect terms

It is a design question about origin-of-life experiments. Two goods pull against each other:

- **Chemical plausibility:** are the feedstocks, energy sources and pathways ones that could have existed on prebiotic Earth? Its ground is a measured reaction outcome under stated conditions.
- **A complete evolving system:** does the product sustain itself and undergo heritable variation with differential persistence? Its ground is generational counts and a selection response.

An experiment can maximise the first and produce plausible molecules that never evolve (Miller-Urey amino acids). It can maximise the second and produce an evolving system that depends on purified enzymes, engineered templates or hand-picked feedstocks. The residual is the gap between these two failures. The question asks for a design rule, not a number.

## Headline verdict (from the figures, not from the world sources)

There is no scalar weighting to be found. The figures rule out averaging the two into one score and rule out using either as a witness for the other. They licence an **ordered design**: two separate per-slot residuals with two separate grounds; plausibility used as a filter on the budget and never as a witness for the verdict; the evolving system built by composition from the plausible primitives, because evolvability is a derivative arrangement and not a seventh primitive; inward instrumentation preferred over outward import, with every engineered component declared as an import and recognised as a substituted habitat that proves wiring and not capability; two low-overlap frames ranged separately against the ground; and the plausible-and-evolving compound unbundled and given its own loop.

## What is deliberately not here

- No personal emails of named individuals, and no ranking of people by how likely they are to agree.
- No bulk-send plan, and no claim that a public submission venue is a mailbox for uninvited theory.
- No experimental result of our own. This package cannot settle the question it reports on.

Three reasons, in the figures' words:

1. **Agreement is not a ground.** Fig 2 :35: "Alignment is triangulation, not negotiation. A mirror cannot correct you."
2. **Correlation disqualifies a verdict.** The Seat Map :163: "High correlation is disqualifying for a verdict and efficient for a convention." Selecting recipients for receptiveness manufactures the correlation that makes their agreement worthless.
3. **A frame cannot score itself.** Fig 1 :27: "They are frame-internal. A frame cannot score itself with a quantity it also produces." A survey of researchers who like the design is not a measurement of prebiotic chemistry.

## Legal and practical note

Public opt-in channels and submission portals are used instead of harvested addresses. That avoids the GDPR and CAN-SPAM style failure that attaches to unsolicited bulk outreach, and it avoids the structural failure above. Where a society page publishes institutional contact addresses, this package still routes through its public channels and does not list those addresses in `03`.
