# Outreach draft: check the design rule first, then decide about the framework

**Use:** one-to-one, or as a cover note attached to a submission through a public venue in `03_public_contact_points.csv`. **Not** for bulk send.

**Hard precondition:** do not send until the two-slot residual and the refuter in `01_figure_grounded_audit.md` §4 are intact in the text below. If the offer collapses on contact, the two-stage design fails.

---

**Subject:** One design rule for origin-of-life experiments, with its failure conditions stated first

> Hi [name],
>
> I have one specific design claim about origin-of-life experiments that you can check against what you already know. I am not asking you to read a framework first, and I am not asking you to agree with anything. If the claim does not survive, there is nothing else to look at.
>
> **The claim (`general` on the design shape, `specified` on the failure predictions, not `grounded`):** an experiment should not average chemical plausibility and system completeness into one score. It should report two separate residuals with two separate grounds. Plausibility is measured as a reaction outcome under stated prebiotic conditions. Completeness is measured as heritable variation with a selection response over generations. Plausibility is a filter on which systems get tried, never evidence that an evolving system exists.
>
> The reason is structural, not a preference: a single score lets a high yield in one slot hide a dead lineage in the other. That is the Miller-Urey shape, and it is also the failure the directed-evolution literature names when it says that selecting for the assayed function produces specialisation to the proxy.
>
> Two further consequences follow. First, try to reach evolvability by rearranging the feedstocks the plausibility filter admits, before importing engineered components. Evolvability is an arrangement of held primitives, not a new ingredient. Second, report the habitat: if the system only evolves because the harness supplies a purified template that no plausible route supplies, the result proves the wiring and says nothing about what prebiotic chemistry could do.
>
> **What I am not claiming:** that any particular pathway is the one that happened; that a model system is the ground; that the six-term substrate names chemical species; that a published yield is a living system; or that this is settled by experiment. It is a design shape with failure conditions, not a result.
>
> **What would refute it, written before this message:**
> 1. a single scalar that preserves both per-slot signals with no netting out;
> 2. a case where establishing plausibility settles system completeness without a generational measurement;
> 3. transfer of an evolved function from a substituted lab habitat to a plausibly constituted habitat with no engineered component remaining, ground measured in both;
> 4. one loop and one ground closing both conditions, so the plausible-and-evolving intersection has no residual of its own;
> 5. a demonstration that evolvability needs a genuinely new primitive rather than a sourced import.
>
> If any one holds, I will retract or revise the claim. A one-line "this fails because..." is more useful to me than agreement. If you would rather not engage, no reply is needed.
>
> **If it survives, and only then:** I can send the derivation and the public figures. The figures are the authority, not my summary of them. I will not send them before you have recorded a verdict on the claim above, because once you have absorbed the framework your agreement stops being an independent check.
>
> **If you want to reuse the method:** the eight-step formula is in the public figures link below. Run it on a problem you know well and can check against a ground. Write down what would refute your result *before* you run it, and state whether your output is general, specified or grounded. A reported failure is more useful to me than a reported success.
>
> Thanks,
> [name]

## Two-stage protocol

1. **Stage 1:** send only the claim, its two grounds and the five refuters. Ask what the recipient would count as a failure. Record their verdict **before** any theory is shown.
2. **Stage 2, opt-in only:** if they are curious, send the three links in `04_links_and_distribution.md`, with the note that the figures, not the summary, are the authority. Later agreement is never pooled as corroboration (Fig 2 :40: "agreement about the world is worthless when the agreeing frames share an evidence pool").
3. **Stage 3:** offer the formula as a method to reuse, with the instruction that each person states their own ground, mode and refuter before running it.
