# Expert verification packet: the Tether on ORI-0082

**What this is:** a design claim in a form an origin-of-life experimentalist can check. Mode, derivation and refuters are stated before the conclusion, so a reader knows what to look for and what would settle it.

**Mode: `general` on the design shape, `specified` on the failure predictions, not `grounded`.** No experiment in this folder was run. This is a shape and a route, not a result.

## 1. The claim, in one paragraph

An origin-of-life experiment must report two separate residuals, each with its own ground: measured plausibility of feedstocks and conditions under a stated prebiotic scenario, and measured heritable variation with a selection response over generations. They must not be averaged, because a single score lets a high yield hide a dead lineage. Plausibility is a filter on which systems get tried, never a witness that an evolving system exists. Evolvability is an arrangement of the primitives the filter admits, so inward instrumentation is tried before importing engineered components. Every introduced actor is declared, because a substituted habitat that evolves proves wiring and not capability. The two checks are run with low evidence-pool overlap, the union of plausible-and-evolving is treated as a compound with its own ground, and the failure conditions are published before the run.

## 2. Three checkable sub-claims

### C1: framework-internal; checkable by reading Figures 1 and 9

- **Statement:** the two axes must be kept as separate per-slot residuals; a scalar balance is not available; plausibility is a filter, not a witness.
- **Source, verbatim:** Fig 1 :19 "The residual is indexed by object slot, not aggregated." Fig 1 :22 "Aggregating across slots is how a live signal disappears into an average." Fig 9 :60 "Use filters for the budget and witnesses for the verdict. Never let a filter hand you a verdict."
- **What to check:** is there a single score that preserves both per-slot signals with no netting out? If someone supplies one, aggregation is licensed and this sub-claim falls.
- **Refuter:** (a) exhibit such a scalar; or (b) show a case where establishing plausibility settles system completeness without a generational measurement. If either fires, the two-slot separation is withdrawn.

### C2: experiment-facing; checkable by an origin-of-life experimentalist against their own practice

- **Statement:** an evolving system reached in a substituted habitat is a wiring result unless the introduced actors are declared and the function transfers to a plausibly constituted habitat. Tractable model systems are legitimate instruments, but they are not the ground.
- **Source, verbatim:** Fig 11 :70 "A test harness is a substituted habitat. Which is why a synthetic solve proves wiring and never capability." Fig 11 :66 "Isolation is not removal of the habitat. It is substitution of one habitat for another." Fig 11 :40 "A system cannot be made more capable by improving the agent in isolation. An improvement that does not change contact changes nothing, however much it improves."
- **What to check:** list the components your system needs that no plausible route supplies (purified templates, enantiopure feedstocks, hand-set gradients). Does the selection response survive if those are removed or replaced by a plausibly sourced alternative? The RNA-world literature supplies the model-system precedent: "Regardless of its plausibility in a prebiotic scenario, the RNA world can serve as a model system for studying the origin of life." That is a licence to use the model, not to report it as the origin.
- **Refuter:** demonstrate transfer of the evolved function to a plausibly constituted habitat with the ground measured in both and no engineered component remaining. If it transfers, the harness was an instrument and this sub-claim is withdrawn for that case.

### C3: framework-internal derivation, with one cross-field check

- **Statement:** the plausible-and-evolving case is a union compound with its own loop and ground; the two checks must range separately against the ground; agreement between them is evidence only if their pools are independent.
- **Source, verbatim:** Fig 9 :54 "And when the split lands on a union rather than a partition, unbundle before you search again." Fig 9 :57-58 "a case in two conditions is not a harder case of either. It stops being a mixture and becomes a specific compound." Fig 2 :35 "Alignment is triangulation, not negotiation. A mirror cannot correct you." Fig 2 :45 "Frames range against the ground. They do not negotiate with it."
- **Cross-field check (not a figure line):** Goodhart's law, "When a measure becomes a target, it ceases to be a good measure", and the directed-evolution result that selection "can lead to evolutionary specialisation to the proxy without improving the desired activity." Both predict the same failure the design is built to prevent.
- **Refuter:** show one loop and one ground closing both conditions so the intersection has no residual of its own; or show two checks that are demonstrably independent while drawing on one evidence pool. If either holds, the compound and the independence requirement are revised.

## 3. What is explicitly not claimed

- Not a weighting formula, and no `w` is derived.
- Not a specific feedstock, setting, ribozyme, vesicle or pathway.
- Not that the six-term substrate lists chemical species. Only the formal closed-set/open-arrangement law is used.
- Not that a tractable model system is the ground, and not that a harness result is an origin result.
- Not that life has been created in a laboratory. The protocell survey states a functional evolving protocell "has not yet been achieved in a laboratory setting".
- Not `grounded`. No measurement is claimed by this packet.
- Not that agreement with this design is corroboration. Fig 2 :40 "agreement about the world is worthless when the agreeing frames share an evidence pool." A reply is a conversation, never a witness.

## 6. What an expert can check in twenty minutes

1. Read Fig 1 :19, :22 and Fig 9 :60, :54 (four short passages). Decide whether a scalar balance is available.
2. Look at your own system and write down the components it needs that a plausible habitat does not supply. Say whether the selection response would survive without them.
3. Name one instrument that would discriminate a plausible habitat from a substituted one with respect to the evolved function, or say why none is possible.
4. Send the refutation. A failure is more useful than an endorsement.
