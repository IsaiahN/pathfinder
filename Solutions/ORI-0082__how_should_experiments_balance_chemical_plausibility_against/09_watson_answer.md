## QUESTION

Decide, in effect terms, what the Tether says about an origin-of-life experiment that must choose how much to optimise chemical plausibility and how much to optimise a complete evolving system. Option A: treat it as a scalar balance and report a weighting. Option B: treat it as a union compound, keep the two axes as separate per-slot residuals with separate grounds, use plausibility as a filter, build evolvability as an arrangement, declare imports, and publish refuters. What each leads to: A sends a design whose score can be driven by the slot that is easiest to measure, and it lets a harness result be reported as an origin result. B sends a checkable shape, separate grounds, and five failure conditions written in advance.

## DOMAIN CENSUS

R by arity, symmetry and scale, then one line per domain with actors, currency and anchor; partition or union; order by dependency.

- **R (the residual).** The gap between (i) chemically plausible chemistry that never yields a lineage and (ii) an evolving system that depends on a hand-authored habitat. Arity: two conditions with a non-empty intersection, so a **union compound** (Fig 9: a case in two conditions is not a harder case of either), not one lever with two settings. Symmetry: not interchangeable. Plausibility constrains the substrate; completeness constrains the arrangement the substrate enters. Scale: reaction and feedstock scale versus lineage and generational scale, with a lossy transform between.
- **Domain 1, prebiotic chemistry frame.** Actors: feedstocks, energy sources, mineral surfaces, reaction networks. Currency: yield, selectivity, thermodynamic feasibility. Anchor: the measured reaction outcome under stated conditions; Fig 2 :29 "An anchor is whatever settles the question and does not change its answer because of what any frame thinks." A narrative about early Earth is a frame and can update; the measured outcome does not.
- **Domain 2, evolutionary systems frame.** Actors: replicators, vesicles, metabolic cycles, heredity, variation. Currency: heritable variation and differential persistence over generations. Anchor: generational counts and a selection response.
- **Domain 3, experiment-design and verification frame.** Actors: experimenter, reviewer, venue, other labs. Currency: residuals and agreement. Anchor: the D1 and D2 outcomes, never agreement. In this simulated run all three domains draw on the same evidence pool, so this domain's independence is zero.
- **Order by dependency.** Perceive what formed, then have terms for what would count as progress, then plan, then carry. The middle link is where chains break (Fig 3 :27 "A link with no instrument is not a link that works. It is a link nobody has looked at."). Verdict order: plausibility precedes completeness for what may be composed into the claim. Instrument order: a tractable model system may precede a plausible one for sharpening the probe. These are two tracks and are not read against each other (Fig 6 :31).

Strain test applied: Fig 13's six terms are not chemical species, so only the formal closed-set/open-arrangement law is invoked (re-census in `01` §5, strain S1). Fig 11's "substitution" is narrowed to introduced actors, not every laboratory condition (strain S2). No strain remains hidden.

## VERDICT (from the figures)

The figures do not licence a scalar balance. They licence an ordered design: two per-slot residuals with two grounds, reported separately; plausibility as a filter on the budget and never as a witness for the verdict; evolvability as a derivative arrangement of the admitted primitives, not an added ingredient; inward instrumentation before outward import, with every engineered actor declared as a habitat substitution that proves wiring and not capability; two low-overlap checks ranged separately against their grounds; and a pre-registered refuter. The plausible-and-evolving case is a union compound with its own loop, not a harder trade-off. This is a `general` design shape with `specified` failure predictions, ungrounded and still a candidate.

- **cost if wrong:** bounded and locatable. The packet states its mode, keeps the two slots separate and publishes the refuters, so an error is findable. The larger cost is spending the independence of outside readers by sending a conclusion instead of a design; Fig 2 :42 "Buy independence in proportion to the cost of being wrong." That cost is avoidable by leading with the two residuals and the five refuters.
- **undoable:** yes. The design can be revised without withdrawing any figure line. If a scalar is exhibited, or a harness result transfers, the relevant sub-claim is retracted in `01` §5 with the earlier reading left visible. Nothing was sent, so nothing must be recalled.
- **reversed by:** a ground reading, not agreement. A two-slot measurement showing that one score preserves both signals; a transfer test in which an evolved function survives in a plausibly constituted habitat with no engineered component; a demonstration that the intersection has no residual of its own; or a derivation showing evolvability needs a new primitive rather than a sourced import. Until one of these fires, the verdict stands as `candidate`.
