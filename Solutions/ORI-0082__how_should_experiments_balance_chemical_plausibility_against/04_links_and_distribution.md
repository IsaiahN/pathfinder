## Distribution: offer, do not push

Figure-grounded reasons:

- **Fig 4 :22:** "Only methods cross up. Only head starts come down." Send the design rule and the refuter, not a record of one successful experiment. A protocol can travel; a lucky result cannot.
- **Fig 10 :42:** "A verifier that can reconstruct the claim cannot verify it." A recipient who has absorbed the full framework before being asked for a verdict has joined the frame being checked.
- **Fig 9 :27-28:** "describe it before you go looking, or any frame you pick will seem to fit." Ask what they would expect to see if the two-slot design were right, before showing them the derivation.
- **Fig 2 :45:** "Frames range against the ground. They do not negotiate with it." The question to a reviewer is what measurement would settle it, not whether they like the design.
- **Fig 7 :35:** "A room's agreement is variation, not a verdict. Selection between rooms is where the ground votes." A crowd of agreeing readers is variation in a room, not a check.

**Recommended order in a reply:** (1) two residuals and the five refuters; (2) ask what they would count as a failure; (3) only then the framework links, with the note that the figures, not the summary, are the authority.
