## Independent domain census

I redid the census before reading the primary's conclusions, using the WATSON order: R by arity, symmetry and scale; one line per domain with actors, currency and anchor; partition or union; order by dependency.

- **R (arity / symmetry / scale).** The gap between plausible-but-inert chemistry and an evolving-but-hand-authored system. Arity: two conditions with an intersection, so a **union compound**, not one lever. Symmetry: asymmetric, because one condition constrains the admissible substrate and the other constrains the arrangement over generations. Scale: reaction scale versus lineage scale, separated by a lossy transform.
- **Domain 1, chemistry frame.** Actors: feedstocks, energy, minerals. Currency: measured yield under stated conditions. Anchor: the reaction outcome. Question type: admissibility, not verdict.
- **Domain 2, evolution frame.** Actors: replicators, compartments, variation. Currency: heritable variation and differential persistence. Anchor: the generational selection response. Question type: measurement of a derivative arrangement, not of a new substance.
- **Domain 3, design and verification frame.** Actors: experimenter, reviewer, venue. Currency: residuals and agreement. Anchor: the D1 and D2 outcomes, never agreement. Independence is zero in this simulated run because all three domains share this folder's evidence.
- **Order by dependency.** Perceive what formed; acquire terms for what would count as progress; then plan; then carry. Verdict order and instrument order are distinct: plausibility constrains what may enter the claim, while a tractable model system may sharpen the probe first. Fig 6 :31 requires two tracks.

## Round trip gap R_T

The Symbols table defines R_T as the gap between what was sent up and what came back down, `gap(x, (T_E ∘ T_A)(x))`, measuring what the coarser description cannot hold. Fig 4 :30 requires it to be measured rather than assumed.

1. **Sent up (x):** the verbatim figure set, chiefly Fig 1 :19/:22 (per-slot residual), Fig 9 :60 (filter versus witness), Fig 11 :70 (substituted habitat), Fig 6 :29-31 (two instruments).
2. **Abstraction (T_A):** the folder's shorthand carried in `07` §2: two residuals, plausibility as filter, evolvability as arrangement, imports declared, two low-overlap frames, refuters in advance.
3. **Concretisation (T_E):** a concrete lab instruction: measure yield under a stated prebiotic scenario, measure a generational selection response, list introduced actors, run independent checks, publish failure conditions.

**R_T is nonzero.** Three things the shorthand holds do not survive the trip back down:

- **Which quantity a threshold comparison relates.** The compound is written as a comparison (⋛) of two per-slot residuals, but the Operators table defines ⋛ as a threshold relation between two quantities measured in the same set. Whether two residuals at different scales count as quantities at that level is a type question needing an instrument, not an argument. Named in `06` Q3 with its falsifier.
- **Which direction the instrument order runs.** The shorthand says "inward before outward" without carrying Fig 6 :31's warning that the two tracks are never read against each other. A concrete programme can silently reverse them: a tractable model system becomes a substitute for the ground rather than a probe. The folder catches this with the transfer test in `07` §4, but the shorthand alone does not.
- **What counts as an introduced actor.** The shorthand says "declare imports" without carrying S2's narrowing. A concrete lab can declare only reagents and call the harness itself a neutral condition. The enumerate-the-habitat rule (Fig 11 :43 "The habitat is enumerated, never composed.") is needed to close this.

Conversely, one thing survives cleanly: a high yield cannot witness a living lineage, so two slots with two grounds are required. That is the residue worth sending. R_T was **not** measured numerically, because no ground reading exists; it is recorded qualitatively as the enumerated set of dropped constraints. Treating the folder's summary as identical to the figures would set R_T to zero and commit the dead-reckoning error Fig 4 :30 names.
