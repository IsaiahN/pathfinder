## What in the wider literature bears on this question

1. **The publication-refusal clause.** The Declaration of Helsinki's instruction that reports not in accordance with the Declaration should not be accepted for publication is a concrete case of a convention deciding an outcome. It is the clearest world instance of Fig 10 :47, *"a convention that decides an outcome has stopped being one"*, and it carries public provenance (adoption and amendment dates) without thereby becoming correct.
2. **Refinement changes the reading, not just the rule.** The NC3Rs statement that pain and suffering alter animal behaviour, physiology and immunology, impairing reliability and repeatability, is a low-overlap corroboration of Fig 11 :40: *"An improvement that does not change contact changes nothing, however much it improves."* An ethical method rule here reaches the ground.
3. **The cascade is visible in the record.** CDC's Tuskegee page states that the 40-year study *"resulted in drastic changes to standard research practices"* and that the study ended in 1972 following an Ad Hoc Advisory Panel convened after press publication. The Wikipedia Belmont entry dates the National Research Act to 1974 and the report's Federal Register publication to 1979. That is a dependency chain (Fig 3), not one lever, and the dating is a historical claim, not a figure derivation.
4. **A method can be re-admitted with its contact changed.** Burger's Milgram replication obtained IRB approval by halting at the 150-volt switch and telling participants promptly that no shocks were delivered (Milgram experiment page). This is a figure-licensed substitution (Fig 11 :66) observed in a different field: the aim stayed, the habitat and the admissible protocol changed.
5. **The weighting link has no shared instrument.** The Belmont entry records that the report does not specify how its principles should be weighted and that Jonsen assigns the weighting to IRBs. That is the middle link of Fig 3 :25-26: "having terms for what a situation offers and being able to say what would count as progress". Different boards can reach different decisions without either being able to point to a shared instrument.

## Distribution: offer, do not push

- **Fig 4 :22:** *"Only methods cross up. Only head starts come down."* Send the claim and the refuter, not a recorded history lesson.
- **Fig 10 :42:** *"A verifier that can reconstruct the claim cannot verify it."* A recipient who has absorbed the framework before being asked for a verdict has joined the frame being checked.
- **Fig 9 :27-28:** *"describe it before you go looking, or any frame you pick will seem to fit"*. Ask what they would expect to see if the claim were true before naming a code or a case.
- **Fig 2 :35:** *"Alignment is triangulation, not negotiation. A mirror cannot correct you."* A list selected for people likely to agree produces a convention, not corroboration.
- **Fig 7 :13:** *"agreement compounds, evidence does not"*. More signatories do not add an anchor.

**Recommended order in a reply:** (1) the shape claim and its six refuters; (2) ask what they would count as a failure, and record that verdict; (3) only then the links, with the note that the figures, not this summary, are the authority.

## The two-stage offer

1. **Stage 1.** Validate the structural claim against the ground: a documented rule, a documented admissibility decision, or a documented habitat substitution. Record the verdict before any framework is shown.
2. **Stage 2, opt-in only.** If they are curious, offer the figure extract and the essays. Their later agreement is never pooled as corroboration, because their verdict on stage 1 was recorded first.
3. **Stage 3.** Offer the formula as a method to reuse. Each user writes their own refuter and states their own mode. The local Seat Map :287: *"a falsifier written before the run is a design instrument, not only an honesty device."*

**Hard precondition:** stage 1 only works if the claim can be stated without the framework. `02_outreach_email.md` does that. If the six refuters cannot be understood by a historian of research ethics without a tutorial, the packet is not ready to send.
