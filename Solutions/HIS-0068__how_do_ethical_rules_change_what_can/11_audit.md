## 1. Independent domain census

I redid the census from the residual line alone before using the primary's conclusions, using the WATSON order: R by arity, symmetry and scale; one line per domain with actors / currency / anchor; partition or union; order by dependency.

- **R.** A community's rules predicted it could study and admit a certain set; after a rule arrives it can study and admit a different set. Arity: at least two outputs, and a third appears as soon as the subject population itself changes. Symmetry: a rule has the shape of a refusal, which is unary and directed, not a symmetric join and not the absence of an operand. Scale: single slot, whole closure, material habitat. I used Fig 9 :54 (union vs partition) and Fig 2 :36 (anchor legitimacy) as my starting figures, not Fig 5 or Fig 6, which the primary used first.
- **Domain A, the reachable set.** Actors: researchers, topics, instruments. Currency: reachability. Anchor: the ground for the question not yet asked. Partition by slot; union where two conditions overlap.
- **Domain B, the normative rule.** Actors: code authors, regulators, committees. Currency: conventions and refusals. Anchor: conditional. For a question about people, the people are the anchor; for a world-question there is no anchor in this domain and the rule is an instrument. This conditionality is the load-bearing part.
- **Domain C, verification.** Actors: reviewer, protocol, applicant. Currency: grant or withhold admission. Anchor: the ground for the world-question, never the reviewer's agreement. Independence here is a form of not-knowing, and a reviewer who reconstructs the claim has lost it.
- **Domain D, contact.** Actors: subjects, models, data, environment. Currency: contact and cascade. Anchor: the reading that comes back. Union, because moving a residual into another habitat inherits unchosen actors.

Order by dependency: refuse before mint (Formula step 2); perceive harm before vocabulary, vocabulary before objective, objective before protocol, protocol before carry (Fig 3); describe the residual before naming a frame (Fig 9 :27-28).

My census matches the primary's four domains at the boundaries, though I reached them by different figures (Fig 9 and Fig 2 first, versus Fig 5 and Fig 6 first). That is low overlap of route inside one evidence pool. It is weak internal agreement, not corroboration.
