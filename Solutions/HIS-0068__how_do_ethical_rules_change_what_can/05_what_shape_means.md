## 1. A shape is a constraint, not a filler

The canonical rule: the framework returns *"what a missing thing would have to look like (a shape, not a name)"*. Fig 9 :32: *"you get a kind of thing, never the answer itself"*. Fig 13 describes the system reporting *"that it lacks something rather than producing an answer anyway. Unreachable is a verdict here, not a silence,"* and adds that *"It cannot say which primitive is missing, because naming it would mean already holding it."*

From *The Library of Babel*: *"You can cut your search short if the set of books returned is small and descriptive enough that it provides a general shape of the answer that satisfies your question."*

So a shape is the narrowed form of an answer you can state before you can name it. It constrains; it does not fill. For HIS-0068:

- *"Ethical rules shrink science"* is not a shape. It is an aggregate that averages over slots.
- *"A rule is a refusal that lowers the reach per slot and re-sets admissibility, with legitimacy fixed by the kind of question"* is a shape. It says what any answer must look like and what would break it.
- *"The Nuremberg Code of 1947 caused the Belmont Report"* is a name and a causal date. It is history, not a figure result.

## 2. How a shape is obtained

- *Library of Babel*: use the vocabulary already held that is *closest* to the thing, look for recurring new terms that overlap or correlate with it, recurse. *"With each new term gained, you are able to narrow the space by definition and deepen your vocabulary."*
- Fig 6 :30: *"The description travels. Fails by being sharpened into terms only we can read. Cast it in effect terms."* Fig 9 :30-31: *"your words are borrowed too, so describe the effect rather than the cause"*.
- *Decoding Tacit Knowledge*: *"When contact isn't possible, the best you can send is a plain description of what you'd expect to see, because anyone can check that against the world."*
- *Library of Babel* on stopping: *"You will know that you are done because the finish line will also be the starting place, as there is nowhere else to search and nothing else that can describe the answer better."* The paradox is that by then you have *"already composed the answer through the process of locating it."*

Applied here: the effect terms are "refusal", "standing to be composed over", "reach", "admissibility", "anchor", "substitution". They were chosen from the figures before any code was named. That is why the historical cases in `04_links_and_distribution.md` appear as instances, not as the source of the verdict.

## 3. What licenses a name

Two things, both outside the shape itself:

1. **The ground settles it.** `THE_FORMULA.md` step 5: *"the ground settles it. Verified only by what pays. A gate is not the ground."* The canonical README says the framework *"stops at the limit of what the available instruments can distinguish, and records the lineage so the stop is honest."*
2. **A provenanced import from a frame whose closure differs** (Fig 6 :23, Fig 8 :50-52), with a source gate and a shadow test. Fig 6 :23: *"No frame escapes its own closure. The bound moves only on contact with a frame whose closure differs."*

*The Hard Problem is About Source* sharpens the difference between a reading and its source. An instrument reads green and *"the instrument is speaking only about what it measured, it cannot comment on the source."* Several sources can produce one reading. *"If several paths create the same behavior, then behavior is a projection with lossy information (it cannot define consciousness in full)."* And: *"An instrument that can't distinguish these cases demonstrates its own inadequacy."* What resolved the color case was not an argument but *"the arrival of a better instrument."*

For this question that means: reading a code's text and saying "this code expresses respect for persons" names a source from a projection. Several histories could produce the same text. What is owed is a source-discriminating record, not a name.

## 4. The ground and the source problem

History has the same metamerism problem. A rule's adoption, its text, and its effect can line up in several ways: a rule may be adopted after a scandal and later improve outcomes; it may be adopted for prestige and later be used to block work; it may be adopted to protect subjects and also change the data. The published text does not determine which. *The Hard Problem is About Source*: *"The answer is unreached until a better instrument exists."*

The figures stop at that limit. Fig 9 :32 (above) and the canonical phrase *"a shape, not a name"* are not evasions; they are the honest report of what a closure can and cannot derive. Fig 6 :27: *"Which is why some unreachability is depth and some is genuine absence, and from inside the frame the two look the same."*

This is also why this solution does not claim that a specific historical case was *caused* by a specific figure rule. The sources in `04_links_and_distribution.md` are corroboration at the level of shape, and several share an evidence pool. One witness with three labels is still one witness.

## 5. Answer to the shape question

**For HIS-0068, "shape" means the following.** Any adequate answer must state:

1. an operation that is a refusal rather than a subtraction, so the record grows while the reachable set falls (Fig 5 :55, Fig 6 :42);
2. a per-slot index, so no global average is offered as the verdict (Fig 1 :19, :22);
3. two separate verdicts, one for what can be studied and one for what counts as an acceptable method (Fig 9 :6, :51, :60);
4. an anchor condition that distinguishes about-people questions from world-questions (Fig 2 :36-37);
5. a review arrangement in which the checker does not author the claim and no standard is stated that no real occupant can meet (Fig 10 :42, :53-56);
6. a habitat substitution rather than a removal, with both silent failure modes named (Fig 11 :66, :68-69);
7. a stated refuter and a mode, with historical names left as imports that carry provenance (Fig 9 :32, Fig 8 :50-52).

The shape does not include which code, which year, or which committee. Those are answers the method cannot supply without a ground reading.

## 6. Consequences for contact

**Prestige is not action.** From *The Recursive Self-Improvement Trap*: *"Prestige must never be allowed to validate an action directly."* and *"Prestige can fund an action. It can give an action a budget to be tried. But it cannot settle an action. Only the ground can settle it. The Ground Maintainer must be blind to prestige."* A campaign that collects famous ethicists to agree with this shape would be the named collapse, not corroboration. The legitimate ask gives the claim a budget to be tried.

**My own audit is a simulation, not a ground reading.** Same essay: *"LLMs can quickly simulate an approximate answer/output with the formula found in the architecture post."* and *"the truly testable output would need to be derived from the real architecture or working the formula yourself."* So `01_figure_grounded_audit.md` is a simulated derivation checked for internal consistency against the figures. It is not a test of the historical claim and must be labelled as such. It cannot settle anything it reports on.
