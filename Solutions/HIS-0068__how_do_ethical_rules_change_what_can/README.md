## Question restated as an effect

An ethical rule is introduced into a research community. Two things then change: the set of questions that community can pursue, and the set of methods it will admit as acceptable. The question asks for the mechanism that produces both changes, and for the conditions under which a rule is a legitimate settlement rather than a frame pretending to be an anchor. An answer would look like a shape: a class of operations with named failure modes and a stated refuter, not a list of historical codes.

## What is deliberately not here

- No personal emails of named individuals.
- No ranking of people by how likely they are to agree with the derivation.
- No bulk-send plan.
- No claim that an ethical code was right because it was influential, or wrong because it was inconvenient. Prestige is not action. From *The Recursive Self-Improvement Trap*: *"Prestige can fund an action. It can give an action a budget to be tried. But it cannot settle an action. Only the ground can settle it."*

The reason for the first three is figure-grounded: Fig 2 :35, *"Alignment is triangulation, not negotiation. A mirror cannot correct you."* Selecting recipients for likely agreement manufactures the correlation that makes their agreement worthless. Fig 10 :42, *"A verifier that can reconstruct the claim cannot verify it."* The first contact carries the residual and its refuter, never the framework's interior.
