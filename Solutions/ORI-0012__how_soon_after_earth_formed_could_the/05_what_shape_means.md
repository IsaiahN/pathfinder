## 1. A shape is a constraint, not a filler

The canonical figures README (local copy, opened) states the limit: the framework returns "what a missing thing would have to look like (a shape, not a name)." Fig 9 :32: "you get a kind of thing, never the answer itself." Fig 13 :134: "Unreachable is a verdict here, not a silence."

From *The Library of Babel* (opened): "you can cut your search short if the set of books returned is small and descriptive enough that it provides a general shape of the answer that satisfies your question."

For ORI-0012, a **shape** is therefore the narrowed form of an answer that can be stated before the year can be named. The bracket with a dependency order is a shape. "Life appeared 4.1 billion years ago" is a name, and a contested one. The shape constrains: t0 precedes t1; the habitat conditions must compose; the reading cannot comment on its source. It does not fill the slot with a number.

## 2. How the shape is obtained

- *Library of Babel*: use the vocabulary already held that is closest to the thing, then recurse. "With each new term gained, you are able to narrow the space by definition and deepen your vocabulary." For this question the starting vocabulary is the interval, the boundary, the habitat, and the reading; the new terms are habitability boundary, biosignature boundary, source discriminator.
- Fig 6 :30: "The description travels. Fails by being sharpened into terms only we can read. Cast it in effect terms." Fig 9 :30 to :31: "your words are borrowed too, so describe the effect rather than the cause." The residual is described as an ordered interval with a contact channel, not as "the RNA world problem."
- *Decoding Tacit Knowledge* (carried mapping): "When contact isn't possible, the best you can send is a plain description of what you'd expect to see, because anyone can check that against the world." That is the outreach rule in `02`.
- *Library of Babel* on stopping: "You will know that you are done because the finish line will also be the starting place." For this question the stop is not a year but a stable bracket with an owed instrument: the route and the answer coincide at the shape.

## 3. What licenses a date

Only two things, both outside the shape itself:

1. **The ground settles it.** Formula step 5: "SETTLE the ground settles it. Verified only by what pays." The README: the framework "stops at the limit of what the available instruments can distinguish, and records the lineage so the stop is honest." Here that means a source-discriminating measurement on Hadean or Eoarchean carbon, with an anchor that does not update (Fig 2 :29).
2. **A provenanced import from a frame whose closure differs** (Fig 6 :23), with a source gate and a shadow test. A new specimen from a formation not yet sampled is such an import; a re-reading of the same Isua outcrop is not.

*They're Scanning Books* (carried mapping): "The real import is not a new term in the existing space. It is a new dimension of the space." Also: "Declaring the primitives does not make them neutral. It makes the choice visible." The six-term set is a visible guess about compression axes, not nature's own list. Declaring that life needs liquid water makes that dependency visible; it does not make it neutral.

## 4. Why the date is a metamerism problem

*The Hard Problem is About Source* (opened) gives the mechanism directly: "the instrument detects green, and says that it's green. However, the instrument is speaking only about what it measured, it cannot comment on the source." A carbon isotope spectrometer reads light carbon. Whether the source was biology, Fischer-Tropsch synthesis, diffusion fractionation, meteoritic carbon, or siderite disproportionation is not in the reading. The paper by Bell and colleagues lists exactly those alternatives, and Harding and colleagues show the same average value arising from two different processes distinguished only by host mineral and spread.

The essay's own verdict: "It was proven wrong not by argument, but by the arrival of a better instrument." So the honest form for ORI-0012 is "date unreached; discriminator owed," not "date unknowable" and not "date = 4.1 Ga."

*The Recursive Self-Improvement Trap* (opened): "the output is only as good as the validator. The agent, or the tool, or the frame, or the theory, cannot validate itself." A date produced by this derivation cannot validate itself; only a ground reading can settle it.

## 5. Correction to my own section 3 (kept visible)

I first wrote section 3 as if "shape, not a name" forbade attaching any number or any setting to the bracket at all. That overstates the rule. The README phrase governs "a **missing** thing": a primitive not held in Gamma. Liquid water, temperature, and the other habitat conditions are not missing primitives; they are bindings from the planetary-science frame onto conditions the framework already enumerates (Fig 11 :46). Naming them is provenance, not invention.

What remains forbidden, and is not retracted, is claiming that such a binding moves the wall (Fig 6 :23: "No frame escapes its own closure. The bound moves only on contact with a frame whose closure differs."). Naming a setting is a candidate binding with a shadow test owed; it is not a new atom. Both the narrowed form and the original overstatement are recorded here so neither is quietly lost.

## 7. What "shape" means here, in one paragraph

For ORI-0012 the shape is a dependency-ordered interval with four features: a lower bound set by when the enumerated habitat conditions compose; per-site and per-environment indexing rather than one global date; a union of overlapping settings rather than one winner; and a source-discriminating instrument owed before the bound can be named. The figures give that shape and the refuters that would break it. They do not give a year, and they do not give the name of the setting. "Unreachable is a verdict here, not a silence" (Fig 13 :134). The honest stop is at the limit of what the available rocks and instruments can distinguish, with the lineage recorded so the stop is checkable.
