## DOMAIN CENSUS

R by arity, symmetry and scale, then one line per domain with actors, currency and anchor; partition or union; order by dependency.

- **R (the residual).** The interval between t0 (Earth forms, about 4.54 Ga in the world literature) and t1 (first self-maintaining, evolvable lineage). Arity: one interval over several conditions, so a compound. Symmetry: ordered, so the sequence bond governs it; swapping t0 and t1 gives a different physical claim. Scale: planetary at one end, molecular at the other, with a lossy membrane between. Fig 9 :4-5 says disagreements that do not shrink with effort and where each rule works on a different subset are several well-formed questions, so split rather than search. This splits into four domains.
- **D1, planetary habitability.** Actors: accreting embryos, Moon-forming impact, later impacts, crust, liquid water, atmosphere. Currency: habitability, can chemistry run and persist at a slot. Anchor: U-Pb and Sm-Nd ages in zircons and metasediments, which do not change because a frame believes a date. Union member.
- **D2, prebiotic chemistry and first closure.** Actors: molecules, networks, compartments, mineral surfaces, UV, vents, lightning, gradients. Currency: composition inside closure(Gamma), then settlement against the ground. Anchor: repeatable laboratory experiments plus a biosignature that either pays or does not. Union member.
- **D3, the biosignature reading and dating channel.** Actors: instruments (SIMS, U-Pb, Raman), readings (delta-13-C, lamination), competing interpretations. Currency: reading versus source. Anchor: the rock, which does not decay; the channel, preservation and interpretation, does. Crosses D1 and D2.
- **D4, definition and boundary.** Actors: competing definitions of life, the chemistry-to-biology transition. Currency: naming. Anchor: ground settlement of a term, yes; a single boundary date, no. Partition by question type.
- **Order by dependency.** Earth forms -> cooled crust and liquid water -> habitat conditions compose -> feedstocks accumulate -> closure appears -> signature preserved -> instrument reads it. Refuse before mint (Formula step 2); describe the residual before naming a frame (Fig 9 :27-28); settle each first-order link before the next.

Strain test applied: the apparent conflict between "nothing selects" in the unmaintained mode and the standard statement that evolution by natural selection ran is dissolved by re-census. Fig 11 governs the maintenance seat, not whether selection runs. Different objects, no contradiction.

## VERDICT (from the figures)

The figures return a shape and a route, not a date. The interval is dependency-ordered, so the earliest possible life is bounded below by the slowest necessary link (cooled crust, liquid water, enumerated habitat, feedstocks, a self-maintaining closure). Habitability, chemistry, preservation and definition are a union of overlapping conditions, so they carry separate verdicts and are never averaged. The settings over a fixed atom set are isomers, and the origin is a level crossing at which a settled closure becomes an atom for evolution. "First life" is a label applied afterwards to a lineage that met its ground, not a seventh primitive. Because the carbon isotope reading cannot comment on its source, the exact date stays a `gap` of the unreached kind, not a silence. Verdict per domain: D1 `specified` triangulated, D2 `specified` triangulated for the union with `gap` on the winner, D3 `specified` on the lossy channel and `gap` on the date, D4 `general` structural with `gap` on the boundary. Overall mode `specified` and `general`, ungrounded, candidate.

- **cost if wrong:** bounded and locatable. The claim is a bracket with four written refuters, so an error is findable rather than invisible. The real cost is spending the independence of outside readers by sending a preferred date instead of a residual and a refuter (Fig 2 :42 "Buy independence in proportion to the cost of being wrong."). That cost is avoidable by leading with the bracket shape and the failure conditions, as `02_outreach_email.md` does.
- **undoable:** yes. The bracket can be revised as new specimens and instruments arrive without withdrawing the shape. No primitive has to be withdrawn; a source-discriminating result narrows the bound rather than breaking the dependency order. A sent claim can be superseded by a corrected packet.
- **reversed by:** a ground reading, not agreement. Two low-overlap instruments assigning a Hadean carbon specimen to a biotic or abiotic source would close a local gap; a lineage shown before habitability, a derivation of one of six substrate terms from the other five, or a demonstration that the habitat conditions are unnecessary would break the shape claim; an instrument-independent sharp discontinuity at t1 would restore the single-event term; and a figure re-census showing arrival is symmetric rather than ordered would change the bond. Until one of these fires, the verdict stands as `candidate`.
