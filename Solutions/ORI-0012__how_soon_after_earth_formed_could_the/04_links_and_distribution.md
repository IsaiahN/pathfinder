## Wider literature that bears on the bracket

- The habitability-versus-biosignature boundary structure is the closest published analogue to the dependency chain in Fig 3. Use it as the entry point for a reader, not as a verdict.
- The 4.1 Ga zircon and the 3.7 Ga Isua graphite are one broad evidence pool, isotope geochemistry of ancient carbon. They are two specimens, not two independent witnesses to the same date. Their disagreement about source is the point of F4.
- The earliest tangible fossils (Pilbara stromatolites near 3.5 Ga) are the biosignature boundary's firmest anchor; the Hadean claims are chimerical by the literature's own wording.
- Molecular-clock estimates of LUCA are a second method with a different evidence base, but they date a node in a tree, not the origin. Keep that distinction visible (F2).
- The origin-of-life reviews agree that no single setting satisfies all conditions; that is a union corroboration, not a name of a winner.
