## 1. A shape is a constraint, not a filler

The figures README states the limit: the framework returns "what a missing thing would have to look like (a shape, not a name)". Fig 9 :32: "you get a kind of thing, never the answer itself." Fig 13 :134-135: "Unreachable is a verdict here, not a silence" and "It cannot say which primitive is missing, because naming it would mean already holding it."

For this question, a **shape** is the form of a determinant you can state before you can name a mechanism. The shape here is:

> A per-object readout is the gap between a prediction and an observation. Whether it follows the observation, the prediction, or their difference depends on three separable conditions, the reference must not move when the perceiver changes, the channel must still deliver current observations, and the gap must be read per object rather than averaged. From inside, the case where prediction met evidence and the case where evidence stopped arriving are indiscernible when the gap stops.

That is a shape. "Dopamine encodes prediction-error precision in the midbrain" would be a name. The framework does not produce the name.

## 2. How the shape was obtained

- Fig 6 :30: "The description travels. Fails by being sharpened into terms only we can read. Cast it in effect terms." - "Decoding Tacit Knowledge": "When contact isn't possible, the best you can send is a plain description of what you'd expect to see, because anyone can check that against the world." This is why the outreach packet leads with effects and refuters.
- The same essay: "Two people who only check against each other drift, and they drift together, so neither notices." That is Fig 2 :34 read in human terms, and it is exactly why the determinant cannot be "whatever the perceivers agree on".
- Fig 9 :27-28: "describe it before you go looking, or any frame you pick will seem to fit." The question's own three-way framing was treated as a suspicion to audit, not a census to adopt. It became a union compound (`06`).

## 3. What licenses a name

Only two things, both outside the shape:

1. **The ground settles it.** `THE_FORMULA.md` step 5: "the ground settles it. Verified only by what pays. A gate is not the ground." The figures README: the framework "stops at the limit of what the available instruments can distinguish, and records the lineage so the stop is honest."
2. **A provenanced import from a frame whose closure differs**, with a source gate and a shadow test (Fig 6 :23, Fig 8 :50-52).

For this question that means: naming a precision-weighting circuit, a prediction-error neuron class, or a gain parameter is a binding from the neuroscience frame onto an effect the framework named. It is not a derivation. It carries zero evidentiary weight until an instrument pays for it. Note the important caveat from the exemplar, properly applied: the "shape, not a name" rule governs a *missing* primitive. Here no primitive is missing. `Γ(b,a)`, `o′` and `R` are already in the Symbols table. Binding a measured quantity onto those atoms is provenance work, not the invention of a seventh term. What is still forbidden is claiming the binding moves the wall (Fig 6: "IMPORT adds an atom", it adds none).

## 4. Why the "source" essay governs this question

"The Hard Problem is About Source" gives the mechanism that makes the shape necessary. Metamerism: a color instrument reads green and "cannot comment on the source". One green light, or yellow plus blue, or RGB, or CMYK, or a scheme not yet discovered can all give the same reading. The essay's formulation: "If several paths create the same behavior, then behavior is a projection with lossy information." And: "An instrument that can't distinguish these cases demonstrates its own inadequacy, it cannot claim anything substantial about what is being measured or what causes it."

Apply that to perception. Two internal states can produce the same readout: a prediction that genuinely met its evidence, and a channel that stopped delivering evidence. The readout is a projection. It cannot comment on its own source. The essay says the resolution came "not by argument, but by the arrival of a better instrument", specifically the spectrometer for color. For this question the corresponding instrument is one that can tell whether the observation line is live, independently of what the readout says. That is Fig 10 :24, "the ground does not decay, the channel does", expressed in laboratory terms. The answer to "what determines" is therefore partly an instrument question, not a theory question.

"How Intelligence Actually Works" sharpens the same point with a definition that fits this question directly: "Perception is the reception of a projection by something that is not its source." A perceiver never holds the source, only the crossing. And: "The projection of green can't tell you whether it was RGB, CMYK or something else that composed it." The three candidate determinants are exactly three candidate sources of one projection. Distinguishing them needs provenance or a discriminator, not more of the projection.

## 5. The distinction horizon, the union horizon, and the internal limit

"How Intelligence Actually Works" names a "distinction horizon" for every individual observer, the point at which two generation methods read as the same, and a "union horizon" for a group with all its instruments. A case below the union horizon is "never a settled verdict, only a report that nobody has reached it yet."

This is the essay-level version of the Formula closure: "the loop stops only when R stops arriving, which is either a perfect prediction or a closed channel, and from inside those look the same." The essay's most direct sentence for F6 is from "The Recursive Self-Improvement Trap": "a broken channel looks exactly like a working one, until the drift is so large that the system collapses." That is Fig 10 :24-28 in plain language, and it is why the determinant cannot be read off the readout itself.

The same essay supplies the mode discipline: "the output is only as good as the validator. The agent, or the tool, or the frame, or the theory, cannot validate itself." And the correlation rule: "You may all agree and be wrong." These are Fig 2 :40 and Seat Map :163 in narrative form.

## 6. Prestige is not action

From "The Recursive Self-Improvement Trap": "Prestige can fund an action. It can give an action a budget to be tried. But it cannot settle an action. Only the ground can settle it. The Ground Maintainer must be blind to prestige."

For this question, a famous proponent of predictive processing, a large citation count, or a well-funded lab is a budget, not a verdict. The legitimate ask in outreach is to give the shape a budget to be tried against a ground and to report a refutation. It is not to gather endorsements.

## 7. My own audit is a simulation, not a ground reading

Same essay: "LLMs can quickly simulate an approximate answer/output with the formula" and "the truly testable output would need to be derived from the real architecture or working the formula yourself." So `01`, `07`, `09` and this file are simulated derivations checked for internal consistency against the figures. They are **not** tests of the claim. They cannot settle which determinant actually operates in any real perceiver. Only an instrument can do that.

## 8. What "shape" licenses in the answer

| the answer may say | the answer may not say |
|---|---|
| the readout is a per-object residual of prediction against observation | which cortical layer or neurotransmitter implements it |
| interaction is structural, evidence-only and prior-only are limiting cases | that one scalar controls the evidence/prior balance |
| three separable determinants: anchor, channel, per-object granularity | that the anchor is a particular brain structure |
| from inside, a perfect prediction and a closed channel are indiscernible | that this indiscernibility proves the question unanswerable |
| an instrument that discriminates live observation from stale observation would settle part of it | that such an instrument already exists in this folder |

## 9. Falsifier for this shape reading

If a working instrument can determine, from the per-object readout alone and without any ground or second frame, whether the observation line is live, then §5 and F6 of `01` are wrong. The shape would collapse from four conditions to a scalar readout test. Record that as a failure, not as a refinement.
