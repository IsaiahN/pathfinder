# The Tether's answer: what determines whether perception reflects evidence, priors, or their interaction

**Mode: `general` on the structural claim, `specified` on the three determinant claims, not `grounded`.** A simulated derivation checked for internal consistency against the figures (`05_what_shape_means.md` §7), not a ground reading.

## 1. The residual it answers

A per-object perceptual readout sometimes follows the signal that arrived, sometimes the expectation that preceded it, and usually both. The question treats these as three settings of one switch. In effect terms the residual is: *which operand owns the content of a slot, and under what conditions does ownership change?* The answer is a union compound, not one switch, so the verdicts are kept separate and never averaged (Fig 9 :55-58).

## 2. The shape

1. **Perception is the per-slot residual, so interaction is structural.** Fig 1 :9 "perceive · plan · act · predict"; Fig 1 :11 "residual R indexed by object slot". Formula step 1: `R = |Γ(b,a) − o′| per slot s`. Symbols table :36-39: "The gap between prediction and observation. The central quantity: everything the system does is driven by it." Evidence-only and prior-only are limiting cases reached by removing an operand, not alternatives to a composition (`06` Q1).
2. **There is no single evidence/prior knob.** Symbols table :20 says belief is "Not the observation"; Symbols table :24-25 says `w_A + w_B = 1` and is learnable. `w` mixes private history with inherited prior, both sources of belief. It does not mix belief against observation. Reliability weighting of prediction errors in the predictive-coding literature is a different operator with different operands.
3. **Evidence dominance needs a pinned anchor.** Fig 2 :29-30 "An anchor is whatever settles the question and does not change its answer because of what any frame thinks." Fig 2 :33 "The anchor must not update." Fig 1 :23 "The ground enters as the only metric." If the anchor updates on the frame, Fig 2 :14-15 "they converge, and what they converge on is whoever had the most influence"; if both draw on one pool, Fig 2 :20-21 "so the agreement is the pool talking, not two frames confirming each other." A readout that looks evidence-driven can actually be influence-driven or pool-driven.
4. **Prior dominance can come from a decayed channel with an intact ground.** Fig 10 :24 "the ground does not decay, the channel does"; Fig 10 :25-26 "stale percepts, dropped transients, saturated metrics, pooled readings"; Fig 10 :29 "keep the anchor reachable." This is a separate determinant from the anchor condition.
5. **Readout must be per slot.** Fig 1 :19 "The residual is indexed by object slot, not aggregated." Fig 1 :22 "Aggregating across slots is how a live signal disappears into an average." Fig 1 :13 "a global R near zero with one live slot residual is a legal state." A small global fit can coexist with a live, uncorrected object.
6. **From inside, the two limiting states are indiscernible when the residual stops.** Formula closure: "the loop stops only when R stops arriving, which is either a perfect prediction or a closed channel, and from inside those look the same." The determinant is therefore not readable off the perceiver's own output. Fig 2 :45 "Frames range against the ground. They do not negotiate with it." Fig 7 :35 "A room's agreement is variation, not a verdict. Selection between rooms is where the ground votes."
7. **The three conditions are members of one functional group, not three mechanisms.** Fig 12 :123-124: "the group says what kind; the qualifier says which member." They have a dependency order: channel, anchor, slotting, then belief-weighting (Fig 3 :19 "Optimising a link that already works cannot rescue one that does not").

## 3. What it explicitly does not say

- Not that a single scalar controls the evidence/prior balance. `w` is prior versus prior.
- Not that interaction is one option among three. It is the substrate.
- Not a brain area, a neurotransmitter, a cell class, or a numerical precision weight. Those are bindings from the neuroscience frame, provenanced as such and ungrounded.
- Not that predictive coding, Bayesian inference or the free-energy principle is confirmed or refuted by this derivation. World sources corroborate only; they never decide.
- Not that the question is unanswerable. It is answerable only by an instrument that discriminates a live observation line from a closed one, or by a second frame ranged independently against the same ground.
- Not `grounded`. No measurement was taken.

## 4. What would settle it

A source-discriminating instrument, not a further argument. From "The Hard Problem is About Source": an instrument that reads green "cannot comment on the source"; the spectrometer resolved that case. Here the corresponding tests are:

- **Live-line test:** an instrument that reports whether `o′` is current, independently of what the readout says. This separates Fig 10 channel decay from a genuine prediction match.
- **Anchor-movement test:** a measurement of whether the reference moved when the perceiver's belief changed (Fig 2 :33).
- **Slotting test:** compare a global residual with per-object residuals on the same trials; a global near-zero with one live slot refutes the aggregation reading (Fig 1 :13, :22).
- **Precision test:** show whether a single weight scales observation against belief, or whether separate reliability weights act on prediction errors while `w` acts on belief sources. This decides F2.
- Standing falsifiers from `01` §5: a one-operand readout that still closes; `w` shown to mix observation with belief; intact fidelity through a stale channel; correct per-slot reports from a global residual; internal certification of ground contact.

## 5. The honest stop

**Unreached, not unreachable.** The Three options are a shape we can state: a per-object gap with three named determinant conditions and an internal indiscernibility limit. The framework cannot name which biological quantity occupies the slot, and naming it would mean already holding it. Fig 8 :39 "So a search that finds nothing is never a proof of absence." The sources section in `09` lists the witnesses actually read; several share an evidence pool, so their agreement is one witness, not several. The lineage is recorded here so the stop is checkable. "Indiscernibility is not identity."

## 6. One-paragraph version for outreach

> The Tether does not pick one of the three options. It says that a perceptual readout is a per-object gap between a prediction and an observation, so the interaction is structural and the other two are limiting cases. Whether the readout follows the observation, the expectation, or their difference depends on three separable conditions: a reference that does not move when the perceiver changes, a channel that still delivers current observations, and a gap read per object rather than averaged. There is no single evidence-versus-prior knob; the framework's weighting mixes two sources of belief, private history and inherited prior, and is not the observation. Finally, from inside the gap, a perfect prediction and a closed channel look the same when the error signal stops, so the determinant cannot be read off the readout alone. What the framework offers is that shape, five falsifiers written in advance, and the instruments that would close it. The names of circuits and parameters stay open until a ground settles them.
