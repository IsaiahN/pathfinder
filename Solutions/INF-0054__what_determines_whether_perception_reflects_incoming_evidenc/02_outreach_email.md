# Outreach draft — check the output first, then decide about the theory

**Use:** one-to-one, or as a cover letter attached to a submission through the public venues in `03_public_contact_points.csv`. **Not** for bulk send.

**Hard precondition:** the stage-1 output below must survive a check before any theory is shown. It is a shape and a refuter, not a result.

---

**Subject:** One checkable claim about evidence, priors, and perceptual readout

> Hi [name],
>
> I have one specific output I would like you to check against what you already know. I am not asking you to read a theory first, and I am not asking you to agree with anything. If the output does not survive, there is nothing else to look at.
>
> **The question in effect terms.** A perceptual readout sometimes follows the signal that just arrived, sometimes follows what the system already expected, and usually both. I want to know what determines which. The three options are being treated as one switch. I think that is the wrong census.
>
> **The output (`general` on the shape, `specified` on the determinants, not `grounded`):**
>
> 1. A perceptual readout is a per-object residual, the gap between a prediction made from belief and action and the observation that arrived. It is *the interaction by construction*. "Interaction" is not one option among three; it is the substrate. Evidence-only and prior-only are limiting cases.
> 2. There is no single scalar knob for the evidence/prior balance. The framework's weighting mixes two sources of belief, private history and inherited prior. It does not mix belief against observation. Reliability-weighting of prediction errors in the predictive-coding literature is a different operator with different operands.
> 3. Whether a readout follows evidence, prior, or their interaction depends on three separable conditions: a reference that does not move when the perceiver changes (an anchor), a channel that still delivers current observations, and a residual read per object rather than averaged into one global number.
> 4. From inside the residual, a perfect prediction and a closed channel look the same when the error signal stops. The determinant is therefore not something the perceiver can read off its own readout. It needs the ground or a second independent frame ranged against it.
>
> **What would refute it, written before this message:**
>
> 1. A readout that depends on only the prediction or only the observation and still closes against a ground.
> 2. A case where the belief-weighting mixes observation against belief rather than private history against inherited prior.
> 3. A readout that tracks current observations at unchanged fidelity through a demonstrably stale or saturated channel.
> 4. A perceiver that reports one global fit and still gets the correct per-object answer.
> 5. A perceiver that certifies from its own residual alone that it is tracking the world rather than a closed channel.
>
> If any fires, I withdraw the claim. A one-line "this fails because..." is more useful to me than agreement. If you would rather not engage, no reply is needed.
>
> **What I am not claiming:** not a brain area, not a neurotransmitter, not a numerical precision weight, not a settling of the top-down versus bottom-up debate in perception, and not a result. The framework returns a shape, not a name. Agreement from people who share evidence is one witness, not two.
>
> **If it survives, and only then:** the sources are below. The figures are the authority, not my summary of them.
> - Verbatim figures text with line numbers (local extract): `FRAMEWORK/reference/03-FIGURES_TEXT.md`
> - The Figures README, canonical usage rule: https://github.com/IsaiahN/tether/blob/general-agent/figures/README.md
> - The eight-step formula: local `FRAMEWORK/figures/THE_FORMULA.md`
>
> **If you want to use the machinery:** run it on a problem you know well and can check against a ground. Write down what would refute your result *before* you run it. A reported failure is more useful to me than a reported success.
>
> Thanks, [name].

## Recording rule

Stage 1 is the residual, the shape claim and the refuter. Record the recipient's verdict **before** any framework material is shown. Stage 2 is opt-in: offer the three links only if they are curious. Stage 3 offers the formula as a method to reuse, with the instruction that each user writes their own refuter and states their own mode. A later agreement is never pooled as corroboration (Fig 2 :40). A returned refutation goes into `11_audit.md` with provenance and is re-evaluated as if it came from any other source.
