## What is deliberately not here

- No personal emails of named individuals.
- No ranking of people by how likely they are to agree.
- No bulk-send plan.

Three reasons, in the framework's own words:

1. **A room does not upgrade the label.** Seat Map :283: "A room reaching a conclusion without the ground is agreement, and agreement among frames is a weighted average of influence. Adding people raises the vocabulary and does not add an anchor."
2. **Selecting for receptiveness manufactures the disqualifying correlation.** Seat Map :163: "High correlation is disqualifying for a verdict and efficient for a convention." Fig 2 :35: "Alignment is triangulation, not negotiation. A mirror cannot correct you."
3. **Asking for trust before a check spends the only thing an outside frame contributes.** Fig 10 :42: "A verifier that can reconstruct the claim cannot verify it."

Legally: scraping personal emails for unsolicited bulk outreach breaches GDPR and CAN-SPAM style rules in many jurisdictions. That is a practical failure on top of the structural one.

## Honest standing of this folder

This is a **simulated derivation checked for internal consistency against the figures**, not a ground reading. From *The Recursive Self-Improvement Trap*: "the truly testable output would need to be derived from the real architecture or working the formula yourself." LLM output is simulacra. This package cannot settle the question it reports on. Only an instrument, an experiment, a dataset or a patient outcome can do that (Fig 2 :29).
