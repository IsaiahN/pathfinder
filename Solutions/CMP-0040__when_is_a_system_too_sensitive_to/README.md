## What is deliberately not here

- No personal emails of named individuals.
- No ranking of people by how likely they are to agree with the shape claim.
- No bulk-send plan.

Three reasons, in the figures' own words:

1. **Agreement is not an anchor.** Fig 2 :35: "Alignment is triangulation, not negotiation. A mirror cannot correct you."
2. **Correlation disqualifies a verdict.** Fig 2 :40: "disqualifying for a verdict: agreement about the world is worthless when the agreeing frames share an evidence pool." Selecting recipients for receptiveness manufactures the correlation that makes their agreement worthless.
3. **A verifier must not hold the interiors.** Fig 10 :52: "Bring a characterised residual, never the source." Fig 10 :42: "A verifier that can reconstruct the claim cannot verify it."

Legally: harvested personal emails for unsolicited outreach breach GDPR and CAN-SPAM style rules in
many jurisdictions. Public institutional and opt-in venues avoid that failure as well as the
structural one.

## Suggested next step

Take `01` and `08` to the public venues in `03`. Ask what a checker would count as a failure. Record
that verdict before offering any theory. A reported failure is more useful than an endorsement.
