## 1. A shape is a constraint, not a filler

The canonical figures README states the limit: the framework returns "what a missing thing would
have to look like (a shape, not a name)." Fig 9 :32: "you get a kind of thing, never the answer itself."
Fig 13 :134: "Unreachable is a verdict here, not a silence." It cannot name the missing primitive,
because naming it would mean already holding it.

From *The Library of Babel* (Isaiah N, 2026-10-02): "You can cut your search short if the set of books
returned is small and descriptive enough that it provides a general shape of the answer that satisfies
your question."

So a **shape** here is the form of the boundary between two prediction regimes, stateable before any
number or named attractor is attached. It constrains: there must be a live fine slot, a bounded
coarse reach, and a measured loss between them. It does not fill in a Lyapunov exponent or an
ensemble size. "A precise forecast fails but a distribution survives" is a shape. "The exponent is
0.9 per unit time and the attractor is the Lorenz attractor" is a name.

## 2. How the shape is obtained

- *The Library of Babel*: use the vocabulary already held that is closest to the thing, look for
  recurring new terms that overlap or correlate with it, recurse. "With each new term gained, you are
  able to narrow the space by definition and deepen your vocabulary."
- Fig 6 :30: "The description travels. Fails by being sharpened into terms only we can read. Cast it
  in effect terms." Fig 9 :30-31: "your words are borrowed too, so describe the effect rather than
  the cause." So the residual is described as *amplification of a small difference* and *survival of
  a bounded coarse descriptor*, not as equations in a borrowed formalism.
- *Decoding Tacit Knowledge* (Isaiah N, 2026-11-10): "When contact isn't possible, the best you can
  send is a plain description of what you'd expect to see, because anyone can check that against the
  world." This is why `02_outreach_email.md` asks what the reader would expect to see before offering
  any source.
- *The Library of Babel* on stopping: "you will know that you are done because the finish line will
  also be the starting place" and the paradox that by then you have "composed the answer through the
  process of locating it."

## 3. What licenses a name

Only two things, both outside the shape itself:

1. **The ground settles it.** `THE_FORMULA.md` step 5: "the ground settles it. Verified only by what
   pays. A gate is not the ground." The canonical README: the framework "stops at the limit of what
   the available instruments can distinguish, and records the lineage so the stop is honest."
2. **A provenanced import from a frame whose closure differs**, with a source gate and a shadow test
   (Fig 6 :23, Fig 8 :50-52).

They're Scanning Books (Isaiah N, 2026-09-06) sharpens the status of the primitives: "A minimum viable
set of six terms is a human's careful guess at reality's compression axes; another sensorium's minimum
set need not be those six." And: "Declaring the primitives does not make them neutral. It makes the
choice visible." So the six-term substrate is a visible guess, not nature's own list. Naming a
Lyapunov exponent or an attractor as *the* answer would turn a visible choice into an invisible one.

## 4. Why the honest stop matters here

*The Hard Problem is About Source* (Isaiah N, 2026-08-28) gives the mechanism: an instrument that
reads green "cannot comment on the source." "If several paths create the same behavior, then behavior
is a projection with lossy information." The reading does not determine the source. It was the
spectrometer, not an argument, that resolved it.

Applied here: a stable-looking ensemble reading cannot tell us whether it came from a bounded reach
with an honest loss, or from under-dispersion, or from pooling readings until the live slot vanished.
The same projection can have several sources. What is owed is a discriminating instrument (a
reliability check against observations), not a name for the source.

*The Recursive Self-Improvement Trap* (Isaiah N, 2026-11-19): "the output is only as good as the
validator. The agent, or the tool, or the frame, or the theory, cannot validate itself." And the
simulacra warning: "the truly testable output would need to be derived from the real architecture or
working the formula yourself."

## 5. Q: does "shape, not a name" forbid using the word *attractor* or *Lyapunov exponent* here?

**No, with provenance attached.** The README's phrase governs a *missing* primitive, one not held in
Γ. "Attractor", "invariant measure" and "Lyapunov exponent" are bindings from the world frame onto
quantities the coarse and fine descriptions already hold in effect: a bounded reach (D2) and a rate
of separation (D1). They are not new primitives of the framework. What is forbidden is claiming the
figure derives their numerical values or that importing them moves the wall (Fig 6: "IMPORT adds an
atom" - this adds none). The correct form is: a bounded coarse descriptor and an amplifying fine
residual, with the world terms recorded as bindings chosen for their testability.

## 6. Two consequences for outreach

**Prestige is not action.** From *The Recursive Self-Improvement Trap*: "Prestige must never be
allowed to validate an action directly." "Prestige can fund an action. It can give an action a budget
to be tried. But it cannot settle an action. Only the ground can settle it. The Ground Maintainer
must be blind to prestige." A list of famous chaos theorists assembled to agree with the claim is
the named collapse, not corroboration. The legitimate ask is a budget to be tried and a ground to
settle it.

**My own audit is a simulation, not a ground reading.** Same essay: "LLMs can quickly simulate an
approximate answer/output with the formula found in the architecture post." "However, the truly
testable output would need to be derived from the real architecture or working the formula yourself."
So `01` is a simulated derivation, checked for internal consistency against the figures. It is not a
test of the claim and cannot settle anything it reports on.
