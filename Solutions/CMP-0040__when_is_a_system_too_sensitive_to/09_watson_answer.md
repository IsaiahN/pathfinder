## QUESTION

Decide, in effect terms, where the boundary sits between precise prediction and distribution or
regime prediction. Option A: treat this as one question and answer "chaotic systems are predictable
statistically." Option B: treat it as a union compound with two slots and three domains, and answer
with a boundary rule whose conditions are stated and refutable. What each leads to: A averages away
the live fine slot and can hand a verdict to a filter; B keeps the slots separate, requires a measured
round-trip gap, and returns a shape rather than a number.

## DOMAIN CENSUS

R by arity, symmetry and scale, then one line per domain with actors / currency / anchor, partition
or union, and order by dependency.

- **R (the residual).** R = |Γ(b,a) − o′| per slot. Arity: two slots, s1 precise trajectory and s2
  distribution or regime, plus one cross-scale transform. Symmetry: separation of nearby trajectories
  is ordered forward in time; the coarse descriptor is an ordering or threshold over a measure, not a
  symmetric join. Scale: fine and coarse. Fig 1 :13 makes a near-zero global R with one live slot a
  legal state, so the question is a union compound, not one hard question.
- **Domain 1, the fine dynamics.** Actors: state variables, nearby trajectories, the re-entering
  remainder. Currency: whether R_s1 amplifies without trending down. Anchor: a trajectory reading,
  even a failing one. Partition: does the remainder stay inside a bounded reach?
- **Domain 2, the coarse statistics.** Actors: the bounded reach, ensemble, regime frequencies, the
  discarded detail. Currency: information thrown away by the crossing. Anchor: a ground reading of
  the distribution. Partition at the level of the reach; union with D1 when composed.
- **Domain 3, the instrument and verification frame.** Actors: observer, initial-condition resolution,
  ensemble constructor, checker. Currency: bits about the initial state, ensemble spread, round-trip
  gap R_T. Anchor: the ground, which does not update on any frame. Union compound of D1, D2 and D3.
- **Order by dependency.** Perception first (Fig 3 :4-5), amplification second, closure third, coarse
  descriptor and round trip last. Fig 3 :19: "Optimising a link that already works cannot rescue one
  that does not."

Strain test applied: Fig 13 governs the internal re-entering remainder; Fig 4 governs the cross-scale
transform; Fig 12 governs same-level bonds. Three different quantities, so no strain after re-census.

## VERDICT (from the figures)

The boundary rule survives as a shape; the specific bindings do not come from the figures. Precise prediction fails when the fine residual amplifies without trending down beyond the held resolution. Distribution or regime prediction survives when the amplified remainder stays inside a bounded reach and the lossy crossing leaves a coarse descriptor whose round-trip gap has been measured against a ground. The regime is stable because of the discarded detail, not in spite of it. The verdict is per slot, never from a global average, and the fine and coarse instruments are two tracks. This is a `specified`, ungrounded result. The strongest corroboration from a low-overlap field is the attractor result that a chaotic attractor is locally unstable yet globally stable. The strongest operational warning is that an ensemble which looks stable can be under-dispersive and over-confident.

- **cost if wrong:** bounded and locatable. The packet states its mode, its three checkable sub-claims and its five refuters before the conclusion, so an error is findable rather than invisible. The real cost is spending the independence of outside readers by sending a summary instead of a generator, or by handing them a filter that says "statistically predictable" and letting it count as a verdict. Fig 2 :42: "Buy independence in proportion to the cost of being wrong." Both costs are avoidable by leading with the boundary claim and its refuter.
- **undoable:** yes. The world bindings (exponent, attractor, ensemble size) are removable without touching the figure-derived shape, and `07` and `08` already remove them. A sent claim can be superseded by a corrected packet. No primitive has to be withdrawn, and no refusal is silent.
- **reversed by:** a ground reading, not agreement. An instrument that measures the round-trip gap and finds it zero would clear the R_T condition; a chaos regime with no fine amplification would break F1; a chaotic fine slot with no ground-settled coarse descriptor at any scale would break the survival claim; an amplifying remainder that leaves the closure would break Fig 13 :115; a numerical threshold derived from the figures alone would break the shape-not-a-name limit. Until one of these fires, the verdict stands as `candidate`.
