## QUESTION

Decide, in effect terms, what the Tether says about resolving conflicts among training, current observations and external sources. Option A: combine the three, tune the weight between inherited prior and new input, and let each source update on the others. Option B: sort the residual by kind, range each frame separately against an immovable ground, refuse a held term where it is false, gate imports by provenance, and report `unreachable` where no source-discriminating instrument exists. What each leads to: A produces coherent drift or a shared-pool echo and spends the independence of anyone asked to check it; B produces a checkable shape, written refuters, and an open slot until the ground settles it.

## DOMAIN CENSUS

R by arity, symmetry and scale, then one line per domain with actors / currency / anchor, partition or union, and order by dependency.

- **R (the residual).** The per-slot gap between the system's prediction and the observation. Arity: three named sources, but structurally one residual with a kind. Symmetry: the sources are not interchangeable; the ground is not a third party to the disagreement (Fig 2 :46). Scale: first order inside one frame, second order across frames, and a refusal acts on the record so that reach falls while the library grows (Fig 6 :42). Fig 9 names it a union compound, not one hard question.
- **Domain 1, internal belief formation.** Actors: Γ, b, w, a, o′. Currency: per-slot residual R (Fig 1 :19). Anchor: the ground through R. Question type: partition into residual kinds.
- **Domain 2, external source and import.** Actors: nature, corpus, peer frame, ground maintainer. Currency: atoms crossing with provenance. Anchor: the ground, which does not update. Question type: import or shared-pool echo.
- **Domain 3, adjudication and verification.** Actors: the frames being ranged, the seat. Currency: agreement versus residual. Anchor: the ground, never agreement. Question type: triangulation or drift. In this simulated run all seats draw on one evidence pool, so independence here is zero.
- **Order by dependency.** Name the anchor first; run the boundary diff per slot; refuse before mint (Formula step 2); describe the residual before choosing a frame (Fig 9 :27-28); settle the first-order molecule before composing over it (Fig 12 :104).

Strain test applied: contraction in the belief-revision literature can be reversed by the recovery postulate, while the figure's refused term cannot cycle back without new growth. Different record properties, recorded as a strain, not a contradiction. See `01_figure_grounded_audit.md` section 6.

## VERDICT (from the figures)

Do not combine the three sources. Training and external sources are frames; the observation is contact; the anchor is a separate ground that does not move when frames disagree. A conflict is a union of residual kinds, sorted per slot. Four kinds owe perception, re-fitting, minting or import. A fifth owes a refusal of the held term, ranked by a residual that persists and does not trend down, and lowering a weight cannot substitute for it. External sources are imports gated by provenance and a shadow test and debited against their independence; a corpus-scraped external source sharing the training pool is one witness. The ground decides, and with no source-discriminating instrument the honest output is `unreachable`. This is a `specified`, ungrounded result.

- **cost if wrong:** bounded and locatable. The packet is mode-labelled, states refuters before the conclusion and records the lineage, so an error is findable rather than invisible. The real cost is telling a system to refuse a term that is actually sound, or to treat a genuine peer as an echo; that cost is paid at the ground and is paid per domain, never averaged. Fig 2 :42 prices the preparation: "Buy independence in proportion to the cost of being wrong."
- **undoable:** yes. The gate and the refusal can be revised without changing the anchor rule. `07_the_tether_answer.md` and `08_expert_verification_packet.md` already remove the names and keep the shape. A system that refused wrongly can be corrected by new growth; the record keeps the refusal, so the correction is auditable.
- **reversed by:** a ground reading, not agreement. Mutual update that later pays at the ground with a stationary anchor would break F1 and F2; flat-residual closure by re-weighting alone would break F5 and F7; ungated import that moves the reach would break F6; one operation closing two residual kinds would break F4; self-agreement that predicts later ground readings on new slots would break F8. An AGM-style recovery that re-enters the reach without new mint would register the live strain in `06_isomers_and_order.md`. Until one of these fires, the verdict stands as `candidate`.
