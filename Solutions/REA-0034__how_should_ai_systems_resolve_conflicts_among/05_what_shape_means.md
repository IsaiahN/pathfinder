## 1. A shape is a constraint, not a filler

The canonical rule: the framework returns *"what a missing thing would have to look like (a shape, not a name)."* Fig 9 :32: *"you get a kind of thing, never the answer itself."* Fig 13 :134: *"So the system can report that it lacks something rather than producing an answer anyway. Unreachable is a verdict here, not a silence,"* and :135 *"It cannot say which primitive is missing, because naming it would mean already holding it."*

From *The Library of Babel*, fetched this session: *"you can cut your search short if the set of books returned is small and descriptive enough that it provides a general shape of the answer that satisfies your question."*

So a **shape** is the narrowed form of an answer you can state before you can name it. It constrains; it does not fill. For this question, the shape is: *a conflict is a union of residual kinds, each kind owes a distinct operation, and nothing is settled until an immovable ground pays.* The name "training won" or "the external source won" is a name, not a shape.

## 2. How a shape is obtained

- *Library of Babel*: use the vocabulary you already have that is *closest* to the thing, then look for recurring new terms that overlap or correlate with it. *"With each new term gained, you are able to narrow the space by definition and deepen your vocabulary."*
- Fig 6 :30: *"The description travels. Fails by being sharpened into terms only we can read. Cast it in effect terms."* Fig 9 :30-31: *"your words are borrowed too, so describe the effect rather than the cause."*
- *Decoding Tacit Knowledge* (recorded reading): *"When contact isn't possible, the best you can send is a plain description of what you'd expect to see, because anyone can check that against the world."*
- *Library of Babel* on stopping: *"you will know that you are done because the finish line will also be the starting place."* The paradox is that by then you have *"composed the answer through the process of locating it."*

For this question, the effect terms are "residual that persists and does not trend down", "closure that differs", and "anchor that does not move". None of them names a winning source.

## 3. What licenses a name

Only two things, both outside the shape itself:

1. **The ground settles it.** Formula step 5: *"the ground settles it. Verified only by what pays. A gate is not the ground."*
2. **A provenanced import from a frame whose closure differs** (Fig 6 :23, Fig 8 :51-53), with a source gate and a shadow test.

*They're Scanning Books* (recorded reading): *"The real import is not a new term in the existing space. It is a new dimension of the space."* Also: *"Declaring the primitives does not make them neutral. It makes the choice visible."*

A system that names a winning source before a source-discriminating instrument exists has turned a visible choice into an invisible one.

## 4. Why a source named from a reading is the specific forbidden error

*The Hard Problem is About Source*, fetched this session, gives the mechanism. An instrument reads green and *"cannot comment on the source"*: one green light, or yellow plus blue, or RGB, or CMYK. *"If several paths create the same behavior, then behavior is a projection with lossy information."* It was the **spectrometer**, not an argument, that resolved it. The essay's own conclusion: *"the answer is unreached until a better instrument exists."*

Applied here: a conflict reading ("the external source disagreed with training") does not determine the source. The same reading is produced by a genuinely different peer frame, by a shared corpus echo, and by a corrupted observation channel. Naming "the external source" as the winner from that reading is the metamerism error. What is owed is a source-discriminating instrument: a provenance record, an independence measurement, or a ground reading.

*The Recursive Self-Improvement Trap*: *"The catch is that the output is only as good as the validator. The agent, or the tool, or the frame, or the theory, cannot validate itself."*

## 5. The room failure, in the author's own words

*How Intelligence Actually Works*, fetched this session: *"if you are just going off of what is present in your heads, then eventually, the room will be swayed by negotiation which is persuasion, or by coherent drift (both of you found a middle ground to agree on)."* And: *"The ground must be immovable by persuasion and not bi-directional like the frames are. The ground doesn't update on anything that the frames do."*

This is Fig 2 panels 1 and 3 restated in the essay's vocabulary. It is the same author and the same evidence pool, so it is a restatement, not a second witness. It matters here because it shows the question's ordinary framing ("how should the system resolve the conflict") is itself the room framing the framework refuses.

## 6. Two consequences for outreach

**Prestige is not action.** From *The Recursive Self-Improvement Trap*, fetched this session: *"Prestige must never be allowed to validate an action directly"*; *"Prestige can fund an action. It can give an action a budget to be tried. But it cannot settle an action. Only the ground can settle it."* A list of well-known belief-revision researchers gathered to agree with the shape is the named collapse, not corroboration. The legitimate ask is a budget to be tried and an independent check.

**My own audit is a simulation, not a ground reading.** Same essay: *"LLMs can quickly simulate an approximate answer/output"*, but *"the truly testable output would need to be derived from the real architecture or working the formula yourself."* So `01` is a simulated derivation, checked for internal consistency against the figures. It is *"simulacra"* in the essay's term. It cannot settle anything it reports on.

## 7. Correction to my own §4 (narrowing the shape rule)

I initially wrote that "the framework returns a shape, not a name" forbids naming any source in this question. That overstates it. The canonical phrase governs a **missing** primitive, one not held in Γ. Here the operations (refuse, re-fit, mint, import, extend perception) are all already in the figure vocabulary; what is missing is the *ground reading* that would select one for a particular slot. So the shape rule forbids naming the **winner** before a ground settles it, not naming the **operation** the residual owes once its kind is recorded. Both claims are now on record so neither is silently lost. Triangulated (canonical README + Fig 9 :32 + Fig 13 :134-135).

## 8. What would settle the shape

A source-discriminating instrument for the deployment, not a further argument. The corresponding questions:

1. Does a held training term's per-slot residual persist and stay flat under re-weighting? If yes, the fifth kind is live and a refusal is owed.
2. Do the training corpus and the external source draw on the same evidence pool? A provenance record answers this; an assertion of independence does not.
3. Does the item used as the anchor change its answer when a frame pushes on it? If yes, it is a frame and the system has no anchor.

Until those are answered, the answer is a shape and the stop is honest.
