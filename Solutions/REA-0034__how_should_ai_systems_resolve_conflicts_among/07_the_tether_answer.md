# The Tether's answer on resolving a three-source conflict

**Mode: `specified` — not `grounded`.** This is a shape and a route, not a result. Simulated derivation checked against the figures (`05_what_shape_means.md` section 6), not a ground reading.

## 1. The residual it answers

An AI system's training, current observations and external sources disagree. The question asks which one should win. Fig 9 :4-5 refuses that framing: *"Disagreements that do not shrink with effort, where each rule keeps working well on a different subset, are not one hard question but several well-formed ones."* The conflict is a **union compound**, and it must be unbundled before any remedy is applied (Fig 9 :54-58). Its live form is a per-slot residual R = |Γ(b,a) − o′| (Fig 1 :19), not a global average (Fig 1 :22).

## 2. The shape

1. **The three are not symmetric peers, and none of them is the anchor.** Training is a frame (Γ, b, w), an external source is a frame (a different closure), and the observation is contact. Fig 2 :46: *"The anchor is not a third party to the disagreement and it is not the tie-breaker in a vote."* The anchor is a measurement, a board, a counter, a patient outcome. It does not move when a frame thinks differently (Fig 2 :29-30, :33).

2. **Mutual update is the failure, not the method.** Fig 2 :13-15: *"no anchor; each updates on the others"*; *"they converge, and what they converge on is whoever had the most influence"*. Fig 2 :35: *"Alignment is triangulation, not negotiation. A mirror cannot correct you."* Where training and the "external" source share a corpus, Fig 2 :18-21 applies: *"one evidence pool"*; *"so the agreement is the pool talking, not two frames confirming each other"*.

3. **Sort the residual by kind before acting.** Four kinds say something is missing (extend perception, re-fit, mint a mechanism, import an atom). A fifth says something held is wrong (Formula step 2). Fig 9 :5: *"Split it rather than search."*

4. **A false held term owes a refusal, not a smaller weight.** Fig 5 :53: *"refuted: a term we hold is wrong; a term is owed that says so."* The term to point at is the bound, predicting one whose *"residual PERSISTS AND DOES NOT TREND DOWN"* (Fig 5 :59). Fig 5 :55: *"What it removes is not a term but a term’s standing to be composed over. Nothing leaves the library. What falls is the reach."* Lowering w changes belief formation, not contact (Fig 11 :40).

5. **External sources are gated imports.** Fig 6 :23: *"No frame escapes its own closure. The bound moves only on contact with a frame whose closure differs."* They need provenance and a shadow test, and their independence is debited (Fig 6 :24; Fig 8 :51-53). Fig 8 :53: *"Residual first, frame second."*

6. **The verdict comes from the ground, not agreement, and no ground reading is an honest stop.** Fig 7 :35: *"A room’s agreement is variation, not a verdict. Selection between rooms is where the ground votes."* Fig 13 :134: *"Unreachable is a verdict here, not a silence,"* The system reports the operation the residual owes and the kind of thing that is missing, not a name for the winner (Fig 9 :32).

## 4. What it explicitly does not say

- Not that averaging is never useful. It says averaging under shared influence or a shared pool is not a verdict (Fig 2 :40).
- Not that training is wrong and observations are right, or the reverse. Neither is an anchor.
- Not that the belief-revision literature is mistaken. Its contraction operator is a genuine corroboration of F5; its recovery postulate is a recorded strain, not a refutation.
- Not that a particular stored term is false in any deployment. That needs a ground reading.
- Not `grounded`. No source-discriminating instrument was run here.

## 5. What would settle it — the ground

A source-discriminating instrument, not a further argument. From *The Hard Problem is About Source*: an instrument that reads green *"cannot comment on the source"*; the spectrometer resolved it. Here the corresponding tests are:

- **Flat-residual test:** does a held training term's per-slot residual persist and fail to trend down under re-weighting? If yes, the fifth kind is live.
- **Independence test:** can a provenance record show that training and the external source draw on different evidence? An assertion is not a record.
- **Anchor test:** does the item used to settle the case change its answer when a frame pushes on it? If yes, it is a frame.
- **Standing falsifiers:** mutual update that pays at the ground with a stationary anchor; refusal-free closure of a flat residual; ungated import that moves the reach; one operation closing two residual kinds; self-agreement predicting later ground readings.

## 6. The honest stop

**Unreached, not unreachable.** The five bins and the gates are reachable from the figure vocabulary. Which bin a particular live slot belongs to is not reachable without a ground reading. The six-term vocabulary is a visible guess, not nature's own list, and *"indiscernibility is not identity."* The answer stops where the instruments stop and records the lineage so the stop is checkable.

## 7. One-paragraph version for outreach

> The Tether does not pick a winner among training, observation and external sources. It says those three are two frames plus contact, and none of them is the anchor, so averaging them is coherent drift rather than triangulation. A conflict is a union of residual kinds, not one question, so the system must sort it per slot before acting. Four kinds say something is missing and owe perception, re-fitting, minting or import; a fifth says a held term is false and owes a refusal, which is a different operation from lowering a weight. External sources are imports, admitted only with provenance and a shadow test and debited against their independence. The ground decides, and when no source-discriminating instrument exists the honest answer is unreachable rather than a synthesised consensus. What is offered is the shape, the operations written in advance, and the tests that would close it.
