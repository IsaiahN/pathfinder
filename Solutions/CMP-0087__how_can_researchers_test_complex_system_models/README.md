## What is deliberately not here

- No personal emails of named individuals.
- No ranking of people by how likely they are to agree with the claim.
- No bulk-send plan.

Three reasons, in the figures' own words:

1. **Agreement is not the anchor.** `Fig 2 :35`: *"Alignment is triangulation, not negotiation. A mirror cannot correct you."*
2. **Shared evidence disqualifies a verdict.** `Fig 2 :40`: *"agreement about the world is worthless when the agreeing frames share an evidence pool"*. Selecting recipients for receptiveness manufactures exactly the correlation that makes their agreement worthless.
3. **Sending the theory before the residual spends the reader's independence.** `Fig 10 :42`: *"A verifier that can reconstruct the claim cannot verify it."*

Legally: scraping personal emails for unsolicited bulk outreach breaches GDPR and CAN-SPAM style rules in many jurisdictions. Public opt-in channels avoid that practical failure as well as the structural one.

## Suggested next step

Take `01` and `08` to the public venues in `03`. The audience that can help is the one that has opted in to check claims and is positioned to refute them, not a purchased list of believers. Ask what would count as a failure. Record the verdict before showing any theory.
