## QUESTION

Decide, in effect terms, what the Tether says about testing a complex-system model when a controlled experiment is impossible or ethically unacceptable. Option A: substitute agreement, ensemble consensus, in-sample fit or synthetic-harness success for the missing intervention and call the model validated. Option B: find an anchor that settles the same question without the intervention, keep independent checks separate, and report the honest verdict set. What each leads to: A produces a `general`-mode verdict from frame-internal quantities, spends the independence of the checkers, and cannot correct the model; B produces a checkable shape, written refuters, and a stop at `unreachable at this budget` where nothing returns anything.

## DOMAIN CENSUS

R by arity, symmetry and scale, then one line per domain with actors / currency / anchor, partition or union, and order by dependency.

- **R (the residual).** The missing observation that would close the gap between model prediction and target outcome. Arity: model, target, channel, plus the absent intervention as a fourth term. Symmetry: asymmetric, the anchor must not update on the model. Scale: the model's abstraction (T_A) and the target's concrete reading (T_E), with a round-trip gap R_T. Fig 9 names it a union compound: physical impossibility, ethical unacceptability and non-isolability overlap, and a case in two conditions is not a harder case of either.
- **Domain 1, model frame.** Actors: parameters, structure, library Γ, closure. Currency: description length, fit, prediction. Anchor: none of its own, so this is a frame, not an anchor-bearing check domain. Fig 1 :23 says the ground is the only metric.
- **Domain 2, target system / world.** Actors: entities, conditions, relations in contact with R. Currency: observations and outcomes. Anchor: the world, including a measurement, an experiment, a counter, a market and a patient outcome (Fig 2 :29-30).
- **Domain 3, alternative ground.** Actors: exogenous events, natural experiments, historical shocks, routine readings, patient outcomes, markets. Currency: readings from instruments already returning something (Fig 6 :32). Anchor: the same world, contacted without a researcher-controlled intervention. This is where the answer to CMP-0087 lives.
- **Domain 4, independent checking frames.** Actors: other models, instruments, disciplines and datasets. Currency: independence, how little they draw on the same evidence (Fig 2 :31-32). Anchor: the ground, never agreement.
- **Domain 5, verification / governance seat.** Actors: reviewers, registries, pre-registered refuters, provenance records. Currency: refusal and provenance. Anchor: the ground; the seat carries only what it did not author (Fig 10 :37-38).
- **Order by dependency.** Describe R in effect terms before choosing a method (Fig 9 :27-28); instrument the observation that would change the model before planning a route (Fig 3 :27); refuse before mint; settle after the search (Fig 5 :32).

Strain test applied: Sargent's simulation verification and conceptual-model validity concern the model as an artefact, while Fig 11 :70 concerns capability transfer to the original habitat. Different objects, so no strain after re-census. The apparent strain between "no proof of absence" and the practical need to stop dissolves when stopping is treated as a budget filter (Fig 9 :60), not a witness.

## VERDICT (from the figures)

The shape survives; any claim to a universal validation technique does not. An anchor is whatever settles the question and does not move when the model pushes on it, and the controlled experiment is only one member of a set that also includes measurements, counters, markets and patient outcomes. When the intervention is unavailable, the route is to describe the residual in effect terms, prefer a reading already returning something, import a different frame only when needed, count a check only if its evidence pool differs, and refuse to let a synthetic harness or a shared-data ensemble stand in for the target. The verdict set is `grounded`, `candidate / unreached`, `unreachable at this budget`. This is a `specified`, ungrounded result. The strongest single line for outreach is Fig 2 :35: alignment is triangulation, not negotiation; a mirror cannot correct you.

- **cost if wrong:** bounded and locatable. The packet is mode-labelled, states its refuters before the conclusion and records the lineage, so an error is findable rather than invisible. The real cost is spending the independence of outside readers by sending a recording instead of a generator, which Fig 2 :42 prices: "Buy independence in proportion to the cost of being wrong." That cost is avoidable by leading with the residual and its refuter.
- **undoable:** yes. The wrong moves (calling a synthetic harness a validation, pooling same-data checks, reporting "validated") are removable without touching the shape. `07_the_tether_answer.md` and `08_expert_verification_packet.md` already remove them. A superseding packet can be sent; no anchor has to be withdrawn.
- **reversed by:** a ground reading, not agreement. A discriminating reading of the target that settles a model claim would move part of the shape to `grounded`; same-pool agreement surviving an outside ground, settlement with no reading at any resolution, synthetic success transferring without a target reading, a finite proof of always-valid, or a cost filter establishing truth would each break a named finding. Until one fires, the verdict stands as `candidate`.
