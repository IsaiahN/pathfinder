# Expert verification packet  -  testing a complex-system model without the controlled experiment

**What this is:** a claim in a form a modeller, methodologist or domain researcher can check. Mode, derivation and refuters are stated before the conclusion, so a reader knows what to look for and what would settle it.

**Mode: `specified`  -  not `grounded`.** No model was tested and no measurement is claimed. This is a shape and a route, not a result.

## 1. The claim, in one paragraph

When a controlled experiment on a complex system is impossible or unethical, the model can still be tested, but only by ranging it separately against an anchor that did not move because of the model. An experiment is one member of a larger anchor set that includes measurements, counters, markets and patient outcomes. The route is: describe the residual in effect terms before choosing a method; look for a reading already returning something and sharpen it; import a different frame only if needed; keep independent checks separate; and report `grounded`, `candidate / unreached` or `unreachable at this budget`, never `validated by agreement`. The framework returns this shape, not a universal validation technique.

## 2. Three checkable sub-claims

### C1  -  framework-internal; checkable by reading Figure 2

- **Statement:** the anchor is not experiment-only, and agreement among frames sharing evidence cannot substitute for it.
- **Source, verbatim:** `Fig 2 :29-30`  -  *"An anchor is whatever settles the question and does not change its answer because of what any frame thinks. A measurement, an experiment, a counter, a market, a patient outcome."* Also `Fig 2 :31-32`  -  *"Independence, between two frames, is how little they draw on the same evidence."* And `Fig 2 :35`  -  *"Alignment is triangulation, not negotiation. A mirror cannot correct you."*
- **Refuter:** exhibit a valid model claim settled by agreement among frames drawing on one evidence pool, with no independent anchor, where the agreement later survived contact with an outside ground.

### C2  -  practice-facing; checkable by a modeller or methodologist against their own field

- **Statement:** the correct search order is SEARCHED, then INWARD (sharpen a reading already returning something), then OUTWARD (import a frame whose closure differs). Natural experiments, synthetic controls, pattern-oriented validation and robust decision making are provenanced imports, not derivations, and they are not interchangeable with the controlled experiment.
- **Source, verbatim:** `Fig 6 :32`  -  *"An instrument is not built from a description. It is improved from a worse instrument already returning something."* `Fig 6 :34-35`  -  *"The question is not whether a sensor could exist. It is whether anything, at any resolution, is already returning something that fails to resolve."* `Fig 6 :23`  -  *"No frame escapes its own closure. The bound moves only on contact with a frame whose closure differs."* `Fig 12 :94`  -  *"The arrangement is the substance."*
- **What to check:** is there a field case where no reading returned anything at any resolution and the model was nonetheless settled at that budget? Are synthetic controls and natural experiments being treated as drop-in equivalents when their atom sets differ?
- **Refuter:** exhibit a case where no reading returned anything at any resolution, yet the model was settled and the settlement later survived an independent anchor; or where an imported frame settled a residual that was merely unfound in held data without any loss of independence.

### C3  -  framework-internal derivation

- **Statement:** a substituted habitat verifies the artefact, not capability in the original target; and non-refutation is not validation.
- **Source, verbatim:** `Fig 11 :70`  -  *"A test harness is a substituted habitat. Which is why a synthetic solve proves wiring and never capability."* `Fig 8 :39`  -  *"So a search that finds nothing is never a proof of absence."* `Fig 13 :134`  -  *"Unreachable is a verdict here, not a silence"*
- **Refuter:** exhibit a case where success in a substituted habitat established capability in the original target without a subsequent reading of that target; or a finite accumulation of successful checks that proved a model always valid.

## 3. What is explicitly not claimed

- Not a universal validation method, a checklist that guarantees validity, or a named technique the framework derived.
- Not that a synthetic harness, calibration exercise or in-sample fit is a ground reading.
- Not that ensemble or panel agreement counts as independent corroboration when the evidence pool is shared.
- Not that a model can be proven always valid.
- Not that this packet is `grounded`. It is `specified` and a simulated derivation.
- Not a claim about any particular complex-system model. No model was tested.
- Prestige and agreement are not anchors: *"Prestige can fund an action. It can give an action a budget to be tried. But it cannot settle an action. Only the ground can settle it."*

## 5. What would settle it

A discriminating reading of the target, not a further argument. The checks are:

1. **Anchor test:** can you name an observation that would change the model and show it does not move when the model pushes on it?
2. **Independence test:** do the two checks draw on different evidence, or only different analyses of one dataset?
3. **Capability test:** after any substituted-habitat success, can you read the original target? If not, the honest label is `unreachable at this budget`.
