# The Tether's answer: testing a complex-system model without the controlled experiment

**Mode: `specified`  -  not `grounded`.** This is a shape and a route, not a universal protocol. Simulated derivation checked against the figures (`05_what_shape_means.md` §6), not a ground reading.

## 1. The residual it answers

A model M predicts an observation for a target system S. The standard validator intervenes, holds the background fixed and reads the gap between prediction and outcome. Here the intervention cannot be performed or must not be performed. What is missing is not effort but the observation that would settle the gap. The impossibility is a **union compound**: physical impossibility, ethical unacceptability, and non-isolability overlap, and *"a case in two conditions is not a harder case of either"* (`Fig 9 :57`). It has its own loop and its own possible anchors.

## 2. The shape

1. **The anchor is not the experiment; it is whatever settles the question and does not move.** `Fig 2 :29-30`: *"An anchor is whatever settles the question and does not change its answer because of what any frame thinks. A measurement, an experiment, a counter, a market, a patient outcome."* Removing the intervention does not remove anchoring. It removes one member of a larger set.
2. **A model is a frame and cannot validate itself.** `Fig 1 :23`: *"The ground enters as the only metric."* `Fig 1 :27`: *"A frame cannot score itself with a quantity it also produces."* In-sample fit, ensemble agreement and expert consensus are frame-internal until they are ranged against something outside.
3. **Do not design the perfect instrument. Sharpen one already returning something.** `Fig 6 :32`: *"An instrument is not built from a description. It is improved from a worse instrument already returning something."* Routine readings, historical records, exogenous events, markets and patient outcomes are the proto-instruments.
4. **Three searches, in this order, never averaged.** SEARCHED (is the reading already in hand and unfound?), then INWARD (sharpen what returns something), then OUTWARD (import a frame whose closure differs). `Fig 6 :23`: *"No frame escapes its own closure. The bound moves only on contact with a frame whose closure differs."* `Fig 8 :41-42`: *"Underivable: go outside. Unreachable-at-budget: the term is already in the closure and was not found, so the remedy is more search, not another atom."*
5. **Independence is earned, not assumed.** `Fig 2 :31-32`: *"Independence, between two frames, is how little they draw on the same evidence. Two people reading the same report are not two frames; two instruments pointed at the same thing are. Independence is what makes agreement mean something."* `Fig 2 :40`: *"agreement about the world is worthless when the agreeing frames share an evidence pool"*
6. **A synthetic harness proves wiring, not capability.** `Fig 11 :70`: *"A test harness is a substituted habitat. Which is why a synthetic solve proves wiring and never capability."* A substituted habitat can verify the artefact; only a reading of the original target can settle its validity.
7. **The verdict set has three members, not two.** `grounded` by an independent anchor, `candidate / unreached`, or `unreachable at this budget`. `Fig 13 :134`: *"Unreachable is a verdict here, not a silence"*. `Fig 8 :39`: *"So a search that finds nothing is never a proof of absence."*
8. **The framework returns a shape, not a name.** No universal validation technique is derived. Natural experiments, synthetic controls, pattern-oriented validation and robust decision making are provenanced imports from frames whose closures differ, not conclusions of the loop.

## 3. What it explicitly does not say

- Not that agreement, consensus or a large ensemble validates a model. A room's agreement is variation (`Fig 7 :35`).
- Not that a synthetic test harness, a calibration exercise or a good fit is a ground reading.
- Not that a technique is licensed because it is popular in the field. `Fig 9 :60`: *"Use filters for the budget and witnesses for the verdict. Never let a filter hand you a verdict."*
- Not that a model can be proven always valid. `Fig 9 :44`: *"Only a sealed room can be searched to the end, and no frame is a sealed room. So searching and finding nothing proves nothing."*
- Not a `grounded` claim. No model was tested here and no measurement was taken.

## 4. What would settle it  -  the ground

A discriminating reading of the target, not a further argument. From *The Hard Problem is About Source*: an instrument that reads green *"cannot comment on the source"*; the spectrometer resolved it. Here:

- **Anchor test:** name the observation that would change the model, and show that it cannot be moved by the model's own claims. If it moves, it is not an anchor (`Fig 2 :29-30`).
- **Independence test:** show that the checks draw on different evidence, not merely different analyses of one dataset. Collins et al. (2024) call the label "independent" on a random split a misnomer.
- **Capability test:** after any substituted-habitat success, read the original target. If none is possible, report `unreachable at this budget`.
- **Standing falsifiers from `01` §4:** same-pool agreement surviving an outside ground; settlement with no reading at any resolution; synthetic success transferring without a target reading; finite checks proving always valid; a cost filter establishing truth.

## 5. The honest stop

**Unreached for any particular model, not unreachable in principle.** The question asks how researchers *can* test; the answer is a shape that any test must have, not a certificate for a specific model. The route stops where the available instruments stop distinguishing anything, and records the lineage so the stop is checkable. *"Indiscernibility is not identity."* There is no last room (`Fig 7 :6`), so no final, all-validating test suite exists. A model can be narrowed; it cannot be closed.

## 6. One-paragraph version for outreach

> The Tether does not offer a universal replacement for the controlled experiment. It says that the anchor is whatever settles the question and does not move when the model pushes on it, and an experiment is only one member of that set alongside a measurement, a counter, a market and a patient outcome. When the intervention is impossible or unethical, first ask whether the needed reading is already in hand and unfound; then sharpen a reading that already returns something; then, only if needed, import a frame whose closure differs, such as a natural experiment, a synthetic control, pattern-oriented validation or robust decision making. Count a check only when it draws on different evidence, and never let a synthetic harness stand in for the real target. The verdict is `grounded`, `candidate / unreached`, or `unreachable at this budget`, never `validated by agreement`. What is offered is the shape any such test must have, five refuters written in advance, and an honest stop where the instruments run out.
