# The Tether's answer on chemical gradients and primitive metabolism

**Mode: `specified` on the per-domain mechanism claims, `general` on the structural shape, `gap` on the historical case. Not `grounded`.** Simulated derivation checked against the figures (`05_what_shape_means.md` §7), not a ground reading.

## 1. The residual it answers

A primitive network has to harvest energy, keep itself going, and do both before it has encoded enzymes or genetics. Bare energy cannot do it: Fig 13 :14: *"conserved, finite, directionless alone"*. The residual is the directedness, plus the question of whether the supply stays in contact with the network. Fig 9 :55: *"A bundled problem is usually a union of overlapping conditions, not a list of separate ones."* This is a union of four conditions, with verdicts kept separate and never averaged.

## 2. The shape, per domain

**D1, the difference (`specified`, triangulated).** A chemical gradient is not an extrapolation from biology. It is one of the six held structures, defined as *"a difference that can be spent"* (Fig 13 :22), with the Symbols table adding that it is *"Not merely a distribution"* and that *"what makes it a gradient is directedness"*. Fig 13 :46: *"What makes a gradient a gradient is that it can be spent, and neither an ordering nor a span of space has directedness."* Energy plus gradient composes a flow: Fig 13 :76, **"Energy and a gradient compose a flow."** Removing the gradient leaves *"no direction to drive the flow, and no remainder left after each step"* (Fig 13 :71). So yes: a spendable difference has exactly the shape required to supply work to a network.

**D2, the network (`specified`, single-source on the figure side).** A flow is not yet a network. Fig 12 :119: **"A part that survives its own use."** Fig 12 :121: **"The output re-enters as input."** A catalyst plus a cycle are the minimum structures for persistence. Fig 11 :36: *"Where nothing maintains anything, intelligence is a label applied afterwards to a lineage that met its ground. Nothing was intelligent; selection ran."* A pre-enzyme network needs no maintainer; selection runs on what the ground keeps. But the figure gives the requirement, not the occurrence.

**D3, the habitat (`specified` condition, contested site, single-source).** Capability belongs to the pair, not the network: Fig 11 :39, *"capability is a property of agent-and-habitat, never of the agent alone."* The supply must be maintained, because Fig 10 :24: **"the ground does not decay, the channel does"**. The habitat is enumerated, never composed (Fig 11 :43). Here that means reading vent fluid composition, ocean pH, temperature, mineralogy and flow off the world, not composing them from the theory. The figures name no site, and the world evidence about alkaline vents is genuinely conflicting (Tutolo et al. 2026 versus Martin and Russell 2007).

**D4, the historical case (`gap`).** Fig 9 :32: **"you get a kind of thing, never the answer itself."** Fig 13 :134: **"Unreachable is a verdict here, not a silence"**. Which gradient, in which pore, at which time, is a ground question. A reactor that produces formate proves composition is legal; Fig 11 :70 says *"A test harness is a substituted habitat. Which is why a synthetic solve proves wiring and never capability."*

## 3. What it explicitly does not say

- Not that this happened. The figures do not return a historical event, and the world evidence is contested.
- Not that alkaline hydrothermal vents are the answer. They are a candidate habitat, read off the world, with a live dispute about whether they maintain a gradient at hydrothermal temperature.
- Not that the reactor results settle it. *"variable and inconsistent between runs"* is the reactor authors' own report (Herschy et al. 2014).
- Not that proton motive force is a seventh primitive. It is a binding onto the held gradient atom; Fig 6 :25: *"An import adds no atom to the world."*
- Not that spectrum drives carbon fixation. Spectrum is ordering (Fig 13 :20); directedness belongs to gradient (Fig 13 :46).
- Not `grounded`. Nothing here was settled by a measurement taken in this derivation.

## 4. What would settle it — the ground

A discriminator, not another argument. *The Hard Problem is About Source* (carried in `05`) says a reading *"cannot comment on the source"*; the spectrometer resolved the analogous case. Here the discriminating questions are:

1. **Maintained-difference test:** does the candidate site hold a spendable difference at the relevant temperature and scale, without an external pump? A field measurement, not a model.
2. **Coupling test:** does that difference compose with energy into a measured flow through a catalytic barrier (Fig 13 :76)?
3. **Persistence test:** does a catalyst survive its own use (Fig 12 :119) and does the loop close (Fig 12 :121) over multiple turnovers, with products that cannot be contamination?
4. **Objective criterion:** what flux, yield and persistence count as "a primitive metabolic network has started"? This is Fig 3 :27's missing link: *"A link with no instrument is not a link that works. It is a link nobody has looked at."*
5. **Standing structural falsifiers:** a self-maintaining network at equilibrium; a spendable difference that does not compose with energy into a flow; a catalyst that cannot survive; a gradient derived from the other five.

## 5. The honest stop

**Unreached, not unreachable.** The six-term set is *"a human's careful guess at reality's compression axes"* (carried essay quote, `05` §3). Declaring it makes the choice visible, not neutral. The answer stops where the instruments stop and records the lineage so the stop is checkable. *"Indiscernibility is not identity."* Fig 8 :39: **"A search that finds nothing is never a proof of absence."** So the `gap` is a verdict, not a silence.

## 6. One-paragraph version for outreach

> The Tether does not say that life began at a hydrothermal vent, and it does not settle the origin of life. It says that energy alone is directionless, and that a gradient is a held structure defined by being spendable; energy plus a gradient composes a directed flow, and a flow can sustain the two things a primitive network needs, a catalyst that survives its own use and a cycle whose output re-enters as input. That is a shape with conditions, not a site and not an experiment. The supply has to be maintained by a habitat, and whether any Hadean habitat did maintain one is contested in the literature, so the historical case stays open. What the derivation offers is the shape any answer must have, four refuters written in advance, and the names of the measurements that would close it. The center stays open until the ground settles it.
