## What this folder claims, in effect terms

The question asks whether a spendable difference that nature already supplies can drive a reaction network before that network has encoded enzymes or genetics. The answer derived from the figures is a **shape plus a condition set**, not an experiment: a gradient is one of the six held substrate structures, it is defined by being spendable, and it composes with energy into a flow; a flow can sustain cycles and catalysts, which are the minimum structural requirements for a network that maintains itself. The claim is therefore `yes in principle`, with the actual historical case left as a `gap` (unreached, not silence). The world literature is genuinely divided on whether the candidate setting (alkaline hydrothermal vents) supplies a maintained gradient at the relevant temperature, and that conflict is recorded rather than used to flip the verdict.

## What is deliberately not here

- No personal emails of named individuals. The ISSOL contact page publishes officer addresses; those are not reproduced here. Use the public contact and submission routes in `03`.
- No ranking of people by how likely they are to agree that gradients powered early metabolism.
- No bulk-send plan.
- No claim that this derivation settles the origins-of-life question. It is a simulated derivation checked for internal consistency against the figures, not a ground reading.

Three figure-grounded reasons for the refusals:

1. **Agreement is not an anchor.** Fig 2 :35: *"Alignment is triangulation, not negotiation. A mirror cannot correct you."* A list selected for receptiveness manufactures the correlation that would make its agreement worthless (Fig 2 :40, *"agreement about the world is worthless when the agreeing frames share an evidence pool"*).
2. **The verifier must not hold the interiors.** Fig 10 :42: *"A verifier that can reconstruct the claim cannot verify it."* So stage 1 sends the residual and the refuter, never the framework.
3. **Filters are not witnesses.** Fig 9 :60: *"Use filters for the budget and witnesses for the verdict. Never let a filter hand you a verdict."*
