# Expert verification packet — the Tether on chemical gradients and primitive metabolism

**What this is:** a claim in a form a chemist, geochemist or origins-of-life researcher can check. Mode, derivation and refuters are stated before the conclusion, so a reader knows what to look for and what would settle it.

**Mode: `specified` on the per-domain claims, `general` on the structural shape, `gap` on the historical case. Not `grounded`.** No measurement was taken by this derivation. It is a simulated derivation checked for internal consistency against the figures.

## 1. The claim, in one paragraph

Energy alone is directionless, and a gradient is a held structure defined by being spendable. Energy plus a gradient composes a directed flow (Fig 13 :76). A flow can sustain the two structures a primitive network needs: a catalyst that survives its own use (Fig 12 :119) and a cycle whose output re-enters as input (Fig 12 :121). The supply must be maintained by a habitat, because the ground does not decay, the channel does (Fig 10 :24). This is a shape with conditions, not a site, not a named gradient, and not an experiment. Which gradient, which network and whether it happened are ground questions; the published evidence is contested and the historical case is reported as a `gap`.

## 2. Three checkable sub-claims

### C1 — framework-internal; checkable by reading Figure 13 and the Symbols table

- **Statement:** a spendable difference is a primitive structure, and it composes with energy into a flow. A pH difference across a barrier is a gradient over space, not a spectrum doing the driving.
- **Source, verbatim:** Fig 13 :22 — *"a difference that can be spent"*; Symbols table — *"A difference that can be spent. Not merely a distribution: what makes it a gradient is directedness."* Fig 13 :46 — *"What makes a gradient a gradient is that it can be spent, and neither an ordering nor a span of space has directedness."* Fig 13 :76 — *"Energy and a gradient compose a flow."* Removal test, Fig 13 :71 — *"no direction to drive the flow, and no remainder left after each step"*. Fig 13 :14 on energy — *"conserved, finite, directionless alone"*.
- **Refuter:** demonstrate a self-maintaining reaction network with no spendable difference across it; or show that a spendable difference does not compose with energy into a directed flow at the relevant scale; or derive gradient from the other five terms.

### C2 — chemistry-facing; checkable against the reactor literature

- **Statement:** a flow becomes a persistent network only if a part survives its own use and the loop closes. A laboratory reactor that holds a gradient by pumping demonstrates legal composition and wiring, not capability in a reproduced habitat.
- **Source, verbatim:** Fig 12 :119 — *"A part that survives its own use. Spent and regenerating parts behave differently across uses, and nothing records which a term is."* Fig 12 :121 — *"The output re-enters as input. The sign of the loop decides whether it closes, and a chain cannot close by construction."* Fig 11 :70 — *"A test harness is a substituted habitat. Which is why a synthetic solve proves wiring and never capability."* Fig 6 :25 — *"An import adds no atom to the world."*
- **What to check:** in Herschy et al. 2014 (J Mol Evol 79:213-227, PMC4247476), thin-walled Fe(Ni)S precipitates transected by pH gradients produced *"low yields of simple 1C organics"*, with formaldehyde that was *"variable and inconsistent between runs"*. Does any reported product survive multiple turnovers as a catalyst, or is every catalytic part spent? Is the gradient natural or pump-maintained?
- **Refuter:** a claimed protometabolic cycle in which every catalytic part is consumed faster than it is regenerated, with no replenishment; or a demonstration that no mineral surface can survive its own use under the claimed conditions.

### C3 — geology-facing; checkable against field measurements

- **Statement:** capability belongs to the network plus its habitat, and the supply must be maintained. The figures name no site. The specific site claim is contested and is deliberately left open.
- **Source, verbatim:** Fig 11 :39 — *"capability is a property of agent-and-habitat, never of the agent alone."* Fig 11 :48 — *"You do not invent the list. You read it off the world, and what you cannot perceive or measure yet is the residual."* Fig 10 :24 — *"the ground does not decay, the channel does"*. Fig 9 :32 — *"you get a kind of thing, never the answer itself"*. Fig 13 :134 — *"Unreachable is a verdict here, not a silence"*.
- **What to check:** Martin and Russell 2007 argue for a *"naturally pre-existing proton gradient at the vent-ocean interface"*; Herschy et al. 2014 report gradients of *"several pH units ... across distances of a few micrometres"*; Tutolo et al. 2026 (PNAS) argue that *"neither modern nor ancient serpentinizing systems are characterized by strong pH gradients at hydrothermal temperatures"*. These disagree about a measurement, not about the shape of what would be required.
- **Refuter:** a ground measurement showing that no plausible habitat maintains a spendable difference at the relevant scale; or a published operational criterion for "a primitive metabolic network has started" that two groups actually use (that would close the missing objective link and is a success condition, not a refutation of the shape).

## 3. What is explicitly not claimed

- Not that the figures settle the origin of life, or name a geological site, or select a gradient.
- Not that alkaline vents are confirmed. The site claim is contested (Tutolo et al. 2026).
- Not that a reactor that makes formate has settled the historical question. A harness proves wiring, not capability.
- Not that proton motive force is a new primitive. It is a binding onto a held atom, provenanced as such.
- Not that spectrum drives carbon fixation. Spectrum is ordering (Fig 13 :20); directedness belongs to gradient (Fig 13 :46).
- Not `grounded`. Mode is `specified` on the mechanism and `gap` on the case.
- Not a standing list of good habitats. Fig 11 :51: *"No standing list of good actors survives a change of residual."*

## 5. What would settle it

1. **Maintained-difference test:** a field measurement, at the relevant temperature and scale, of a spendable difference that persists without an external pump.
2. **Coupling test:** a measured flow through a catalytic barrier that composes that difference with energy.
3. **Persistence test:** multiple turnovers with a catalyst that survives and a loop that closes, with contamination ruled out.
4. **Objective criterion:** an agreed operational definition of the point at which a network counts as self-maintaining.
5. **Discriminator:** an instrument that distinguishes a natural gradient from a pump-maintained one in a reported result.

## 6. What an expert can check in twenty minutes

1. Read Fig 13 :14, :22, :46, :71, :76 and Fig 12 :119, :121. Seven short passages.
2. Check C2 against the Herschy et al. 2014 reactor paper: were the catalytic solids spent or regenerating across runs?
3. Check C3 against one field measurement at hydrothermal temperature, and say whether the gradient survives the temperature correction.
4. Name the discriminator you would trust. Or say why none is possible.
5. Send the refutation. A failure is more useful than an endorsement.
