## QUESTION

Decide, in effect terms, whether a naturally occurring chemical gradient has the shape required to power a primitive metabolic network before encoded enzymes or genetics. Option A: assert that gradients did power the first networks, and name the site and gradient. Option B: keep the figure-derived shape and the conditions, and leave the historical case open. What each leads to: A sends a `general`-mode verdict about an event no instrument in this derivation measured, and it spends the independence of the people checking it; B sends a checkable mechanism shape, four refuters written in advance, and an honest `gap` where the ground has not spoken. The residual is directedness plus maintenance of the supply, indexed per site (Fig 1 :19: *"The residual is indexed by object slot, not aggregated."*).

## DOMAIN CENSUS

R by arity, symmetry and scale, then one line per domain with actors, currency and anchor, partition or union, and order by dependency.

- **R (the residual).** A reaction mixture has energy but no directed flow and no guarantee that any difference persists. Arity: four conjoined conditions, so a **union compound** (Fig 9 :55: *"A bundled problem is usually a union of overlapping conditions, not a list of separate ones"*), not a partition. Symmetry: a gradient is asymmetric between its endpoints; directedness is its definition, so the relation is ordered, not a symmetric join. Scale: molecular and pore scale for the gradient and barrier, geological scale for the supply. Fig 9 :57: *"a case in two conditions is not a harder case of either."*
- **Domain 1, thermodynamics of the difference.** Actors: energy (state), gradient (structure), spectrum (ordering), space. Currency: spendable free energy and the remainder after each step. Anchor: a measured difference that does work. Question type: legality of a cross-kind composition. Verdict: `specified`, triangulated.
- **Domain 2, the network.** Actors: reactions, Fe(Ni)S catalysts, cycles, products. Currency: flux and persistence across uses. Anchor: an experiment that produces and maintains products. Question type: minimum structural requirements. Verdict: `specified`, single-source on the figure side.
- **Domain 3, the habitat and supply.** Actors: vent fluid, ocean water, mineral barrier, serpentinization. Currency: maintained disequilibrium. Anchor: a field measurement at the relevant temperature. Question type: an enumerated habitat, read off the world. Verdict: `specified` condition, contested site, single-source.
- **Domain 4, verification of the historical claim.** Actors: experimenters, reactor, instrument, claim. Currency: residual and agreement. Anchor: the ground, never agreement. Verdict: `gap`, unreached at this budget. In this simulated run all four domains draw on one evidence pool, so their agreement is one pool talking, not corroboration (Fig 2 :40).
- **Order by dependency.** First a readable difference, then a vocabulary of flows, then an objective criterion for a self-maintaining network, then a reactor or field campaign. Fig 3 :19: *"Optimising a link that already works cannot rescue one that does not."* Describe the residual before choosing a frame (Fig 8 :53: *"Residual first, frame second."*).

Strain test applied: a pH difference read as spectrum strains, because ordering has no directedness. Re-census assigns it to the gradient over space, with spectrum as a qualifier (Fig 13 :76). Conflict rule applied: the Tutolo versus Martin/Russell dispute returns to the census and narrows D3 from a site claim to a condition claim; the figure verdict is not flipped.

## WORLD (secondary sources read)

- **Physical chemistry / non-equilibrium thermodynamics.** Kondepudi, De Bari and Dixon, *Entropy* 22(11):1305, 2020. Quote: *"Thermodynamic forces are gradients of intensive variables X (e.g., pressure, temperature, and chemical affinity) divided by temperature, and flows are time-derivatives of the corresponding extensive variable."* Also: *"living organisms are undoubtedly dissipative structures, given that their existence and behaviors are sustained by continuous flows of energy and matter"*. ## VERDICT (from the figures)

Yes as a shape, with conditions; no settlement as a fact. A spendable difference is a held structure that composes with energy into a directed flow, and that flow can sustain a catalyst that survives and a cycle that closes. The supply must be maintained by a habitat, and capability belongs to network plus habitat. The figures do not name a gradient, a network or a site, and the world evidence about alkaline vents is contested. D1 `specified` and triangulated; D2 `specified` and single-source; D3 `specified` condition with contested site; D4 `gap` (unreached, not silence). Mode `specified` on mechanism, `general` on structure, `gap` on the case. Candidate, not accepted.

- **cost if wrong:** bounded and locatable. The packet states mode, refuters and provenance before the conclusion, so an error is findable rather than invisible. The real cost is sending a site claim as if the figures named it, or treating a pump-maintained reactor as the historical event; Fig 11 :70 and Fig 2 :42 price that cost ("Buy independence in proportion to the cost of being wrong."). It is avoidable by leading with the shape and its four refuters.
- **undoable:** yes. The site claim and the reactor-as-settlement claim are removable without touching the derivation; `07_the_tether_answer.md` and `08_expert_verification_packet.md` already remove them. A sent claim can be superseded by the corrected packet, and no primitive has to be withdrawn.
- **reversed by:** a ground reading, not agreement. A field measurement showing no maintained spendable difference at the relevant temperature would break D3's condition; a self-maintaining network at equilibrium would break Fig 13 :46 and :71; a spendable difference that does not compose with energy into a flow would break Fig 13 :76; a gradient derived from the other five would break Fig 13 :41; an operational objective criterion used by two groups would close the missing link in D4. Until one of these fires, the verdict stands as `candidate`.
