## 1. A shape is a constraint, not a filler

The figures README states the limit: the framework returns "what a missing thing would have to look like (a shape, not a name)." Fig 9 :32: "you get a kind of thing, never the answer itself." Fig 13 :134: "Unreachable is a verdict here, not a silence."

From *The Library of Babel*: "you can cut your search short if the set of books returned is small and descriptive enough that it provides a general shape of the answer that satisfies your question."

So a **shape** is the narrowed form of an answer you can state before you can name it. It constrains; it does not fill. For this question:

- Shape: "reach is fixed by an inventory of atoms and bonds, by channel fidelity, and by which closures are contacted."
- Not a shape: "corpus C of size N yields accuracy A on benchmark B."
- Not a shape: "the missing primitive is named X."

The shape is what any answer must satisfy; it is not the answer.

## 2. How the shape is obtained

- *The Library of Babel*: "With each new term gained, you narrow the space and deepen your vocabulary." The searcher uses "the existing vocabulary you do know that is closest to the words that you wish to describe and grasp the shape of."
- Fig 6 :30: "The description travels. Fails by being sharpened into terms only we can read. Cast it in effect terms." Fig 9 :30-31: "your words are borrowed too, so describe the effect rather than the cause."
- *Decoding Tacit Knowledge*: "When contact isn't possible, the best you can send is a plain description of what you'd expect to see, because anyone can check that against the world."
- *The Library of Babel* on stopping: "you will know that you are done because the finish line will also be the starting place", and the paradox is that "you have already composed the answer through the process of locating it."

Applied here: the residual was described in effect terms (what fixes the reach) before any figure line was chosen, and only then were figures quoted. That order is the discipline Fig 9 :27-28 names: "describe it before you go looking, or any frame you pick will seem to fit."

## 3. What licenses a name

Only two things, both outside the shape itself:

1. **The ground settles it.** Formula step 5: "the ground settles it. Verified only by what pays. A gate is not the ground." The figures README: the framework "stops at the limit of what the available instruments can distinguish, and records the lineage so the stop is honest."
2. **A provenanced import from a frame whose closure differs** (Fig 6 :23, Fig 8 :50-51), with a source gate and a shadow test.

*They're Scanning Books When They Should Be Studying Nature* sharpens this for exactly our question: "A corpus is the compressed output of human minds; the capacity that produced it is a generator, and outputs do not contain their generator." And: "The escape is not more data. It is a built discoverer, and a way to import not new terms but new axes." The same essay records the limit on declared inventories: "A minimum viable set of six terms is a human's careful guess at reality's compression axes; another sensorium's minimum set need not be those six." Also: "every reasoner, biological, artificial, or composed, can only be surprised along axes its primitives can already represent."

So naming the atoms and bonds a corpus supplies is a **binding** from the ML frame onto held framework terms. It is not a derivation of those terms from the figures and not an import that moves the wall (Fig 6 :22: "IMPORT adds an atom" — the binding adds none). Provenance is what keeps it honest.

## 4. Why the record-level answer is the specific forbidden error

*The Hard Problem is About Source* gives the mechanism. An instrument reads green and "cannot comment on the source": one green light, or yellow plus blue, or RGB, or CMYK, or a scheme not yet discovered. "If several paths create the same behavior, then behavior is a projection with lossy information." The reading does not determine the source. "The answer is unreached until a better instrument exists." It was "proven wrong not by argument, but by the arrival of a better instrument", the spectrometer.

In our question the analogue is precise: a benchmark score reads green. It cannot comment on whether the system holds an atom or a bond, learned a route or memorized an ingredient list, has a maintained channel or a saturated metric. Fig 1 :27: "They are frame-internal. A frame cannot score itself with a quantity it also produces." What is owed is a discriminating instrument, not a larger number.

*The Recursive Self-Improvement Trap*: "the output is only as good as the validator. The agent, or the tool, or the frame, or the theory, cannot validate itself."

## 5. Two consequences specific to "structure"

**Structure is the bond, not the ingredient list.** *They're Scanning Books*: "Adding data and parameters to a fixed architecture is the whale strategy: more units, same structure, diminishing returns." And: "the right structure causes emergence, and scale only helps once the structure is right." *How Intelligence Actually Works* frames the same thing as mapping: "The mapping is not a means to intelligence; the mapping is the intelligence." *Decoding Tacit Knowledge* states what text carries: "text is mostly context with some subtext and almost no pretext. A model trained on it learns a statistical stand-in for the history and sounds fluent." So a corpus is structurally thin in exactly the dimension (pretext, the history that makes a compressed message decodable) that would let a model refuse a wrong reading. Fig 12 :111: "A vocabulary without bonds can name a state and cannot name a route to one."

**Q: can a corpus name the missing primitive?** No. *They're Scanning Books*: "The real import is not a new term in the existing space. It is a new dimension of the space." A corpus can extend what can be said inside held axes; reaching structure along an axis no primitive captures needs a new axis, which the framework can characterize in effect terms and cannot name (Fig 13 :134). F8 records this as a gap.

## 6. Correction to my own section 3 (kept visible)

I initially wrote that "shape, not a name" forbids naming any specific structure in an answer. That overstates the rule. The README's phrase is "what a **missing** thing would have to look like (a shape, not a name)." It governs a primitive not held in Γ. Atoms and bonds already held can be named; what is required is provenance for the binding.

What remains wrong in a volume-only answer is **not** that it names held atoms. It is (a) treating volume as a witness where Fig 9 :60 makes it a filter, (b) treating a benchmark reading as a source reading where the green-light essay shows the loss, and (c) claiming a numeric reach where only a shape is available. Both the original claim and the corrected one are recorded here so neither is quietly lost.

## 7. Two standing consequences for outreach

**Prestige is not action.** From *The Recursive Self-Improvement Trap*: "Prestige must never be allowed to validate an action directly." And: "Prestige can fund an action. It can give an action a budget to be tried. But it cannot settle an action. Only the ground can settle it. The Ground Maintainer must be blind to prestige." A list of famous names agreeing with the shape claim is the named collapse, not corroboration. The legitimate ask gives the claim a budget to be tried and let a ground settle it.

**My own audit is a simulation, not a ground reading.** Same essay: "LLMs can quickly simulate an approximate answer/output with the formula found in the architecture post... However, the truly testable output would need to be derived from the real architecture or working the formula yourself." *How a Society of Agents Would Work* (exemplar quote, not re-fetched) records the multi-agent analogue. So `01_figure_grounded_audit.md` is a simulated derivation checked for internal consistency against the figures. It is **not** a test of the claim and must be labelled as such. It cannot settle anything it reports on.
