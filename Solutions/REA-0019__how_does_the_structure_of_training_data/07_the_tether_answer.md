# The Tether's answer: training-data structure and the in-principle reach

**Mode: `general` on the closure and bond laws, `specified` on the application to training corpora, not `grounded`.** Simulated derivation checked for internal consistency against the figures (`05_what_shape_means.md` section 7), not a ground reading. No learning system was run.

## 1. The residual it answers

Unlike corpora with the same volume can give a learner different reaches. "Reach" means the set of terms the learner could in principle represent and compose, written `closure(Γ)`. The question is not which corpus scores higher; it is what property of a corpus fixes the ceiling at all. The answer is a **shape of dependence** with a written refuter and a declared unreachable remainder.

## 2. The shape, one loop per domain (verdicts kept separate, never averaged)

**D1, library and reach, `triangulated` (Fig 6, Fig 8, Fig 13).**
In principle means the reach, and the wall is set by the atom inventory. Fig 6 :21: "MINT composes inside the closure and can never add an atom." Fig 8 :32: "Closure is idempotent. Composing inside a closed set never leaves it, so no search, however long, adds an atom. This one is definitional: it is what closure means." Fig 13 :115: "The set is closed. The arrangements are not. That gap is why import exists." Data that never presents a distinction leaves it outside the reach. Mode `general` -> `specified`. Refuter: a new ground-verified atom from in-closure search alone.

**D3, bond inventory, `triangulated` (Fig 12, Operators, Fig 3).**
Structure is bonds, not an ingredient list. Fig 12 :5: "A set of ingredients is not one. The bond is the rest of the information, and it is the half nothing was recording." Fig 12 :111: "A vocabulary without bonds can name a state and cannot name a route to one." Fig 12 :112: "A plan is a sequence. A goal is a comparison. Progress is a subtraction. A refutation is a negation, and that one is not a bond." An ingredient-only corpus supports states, not routes, goals, progress or refusal. Same items under a different bond are different substances (Fig 12 :117). Mode `specified`. Refuter: plan/goal/progress/refusal learned from a pure ingredient list with zero relational signal, bias or supervision.

**D2, data as habitat, `triangulated` (Fig 11, Fig 2, Fig 4).**
Capability belongs to learner-and-data, never the learner alone. Fig 11 :39: "So the answerable question is not what intelligence is, but what makes a system capable. And capability is a property of agent-and-habitat, never of the agent alone." Fig 11 :70: "A test harness is a substituted habitat. Which is why a synthetic solve proves wiring and never capability." Fig 11 :43: "The habitat is enumerated, never composed." In-distribution success is wiring. Mode `general` -> `specified`. Refuter: an agent-only change at fixed habitat and contact moves a ground-measured capability.

**D4, channel and indexing, `triangulated` (Fig 1, Fig 3, Fig 10).**
The corpus decides what signal exists. Fig 1 :19: "The residual is indexed by object slot, not aggregated." Fig 1 :22: "Aggregating across slots is how a live signal disappears into an average." Fig 3 :27: "A link with no instrument is not a link that works. It is a link nobody has looked at." Fig 10 :24: "the ground does not decay, the channel does." A corpus that averages away per-item residuals destroys the signal the loop runs on; a corpus without comparison structure leaves the objective link uninstrumented. Mode `specified`. Refuter: ground-checked per-slot residual and objective from purely global aggregate signal.

**D5, cross-closure contact, `triangulated` (Fig 6, Fig 8, Fig 7).**
The wall moves only through contact with a differing closure. Fig 6 :23: "No frame escapes its own closure. The bound moves only on contact with a frame whose closure differs." Fig 8 :45: "Two frames together reach what neither reaches alone." Fig 7 :13: "agreement compounds, evidence does not." Independence of sources, not their count, decides whether a new atom can arrive. Mode `general` -> `specified`. Refuter: a ground-verified new atom from sources with evidence overlap one.

**X1/X2, volume and scale, `triangulated` (Fig 9, Fig 1).** They are filters for the budget, never witnesses. Fig 9 :60: "Use filters for the budget and witnesses for the verdict. Never let a filter hand you a verdict." Fig 1 :27: "They are frame-internal. A frame cannot score itself with a quantity it also produces."

**F8, the numeric boundary, `gap`.** Fig 9 :32: "you get a kind of thing, never the answer itself." Fig 8 :39: "So a search that finds nothing is never a proof of absence." The missing thing is an instrument that reports which atoms and bonds a corpus actually supplies, described in effect terms and not held. Mode `general` on the limit. This is a verdict, not a silence.

## 3. What it explicitly does not say

- Not a theory of learning curves, not a scaling law, and not a number for any named corpus.
- Not that more data never helps. It buys search budget and coverage; it just does not add an atom (F1, F7).
- Not that the corpus equals Γ. It supplies atoms and bonds to Γ and is a substituted habitat (correction in audit section 6).
- Not that model size is irrelevant. Size that changes contact is not agent-only; size at fixed contact is a budget change (Fig 9 :60).
- Not that the figure set certifies its own completeness. Fig 8 :39 and the canonical README rule that out.
- Not `grounded`. No measurement or instrument settles any of the above.
- Not an endorsement of the framework as a final theory. It is a generator of a shape with a stop and a lineage.

## 4. What would settle it: the ground

A source-discriminating instrument, not more argument. From *The Hard Problem is About Source*: an instrument that reads green "cannot comment on the source"; the spectrometer resolved it. Here the questions are:

1. Does a ground-checked probe distinguish a corpus that supplies atom A from one that does not, at fixed volume and fixed architecture?
2. Does a ground-checked probe distinguish a corpus carrying bond → or ⋛ or ¬ from an ingredient-only corpus at the same item set? (F2 refuter.)
3. Does a ground-checked per-slot residual survive when the corpus is aggregated? (F4 refuter.)
4. Does reach extend to a new ground-verified atom only when a differing closure is contacted? (F1 and F5 refuters.)
5. Does an agent-only change at fixed habitat and contact move the ground? (F3 refuter.)

Standing falsifiers are the five in `01_figure_grounded_audit.md` section 4, written before the conclusion. If one fires, the corresponding finding is withdrawn and the census rerun.

## 5. The honest stop

**Unreached, not unreachable.** The figure set constrains the shape and refuses the number. "Unreachable is a verdict here, not a silence" (Fig 13 :134). The six-term substrate and the seven bonds are "a human's careful guess", not nature's own list; declaring them makes the choice visible, not neutral. The answer stops at the limit of the available discriminating instruments and records the lineage so the stop is checkable. "Indiscernibility is not identity." Any claim to have named the numeric reach of a corpus would be a name where only a shape is available.

## 6. One-paragraph version for outreach

> The Tether does not produce a scaling law. It says that what an AI system can learn in principle is its reach, and that a training corpus sets that reach by four things it supplies rather than by its size: the primitive distinctions it lets the system hold, the relations it carries (order, production, comparison, absence, refusal), the fidelity of its per-item signal, and whether it contacts genuinely independent sources. A corpus of ingredients without relations can name states and cannot name routes, goals, progress or refusals; more search over the same inventory is idempotent and adds no primitive. The wall moves only on contact with a differing closure, so duplicated sources add budget, not reach. Volume and benchmark scores are filters for the budget, never witnesses for the reach. The framework refuses to name the numeric reach of any corpus: that is a declared gap, owed to a source-discriminating instrument described in effect terms. What it offers is the shape any answer must have, five falsifiers written in advance, and the lineage that makes the stop checkable.
