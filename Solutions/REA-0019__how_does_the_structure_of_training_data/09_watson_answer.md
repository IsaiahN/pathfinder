## QUESTION

Decide, in effect terms, how the structure of training data shapes what an AI system can learn in principle. Option A: volume and model scale are the whole story, so reach grows smoothly with quantity. Option B: the corpus fixes the reach by supplying an atom inventory, a bond inventory, channel fidelity and contact with independent closures; volume is a filter for the budget, never a witness for the reach. What each leads to: A predicts smooth unbounded gains from more of the same data; B predicts hard boundaries at missing atoms and bonds, delayed hidden failures under channel decay, and a step change only when a differing closure is contacted. The residual is indexed per slot, never aggregated (Fig 1 :19). Anchor: the ground (Fig 2 :29).

## DOMAIN CENSUS

R by arity, symmetry and scale, then one line per domain with actors / currency / anchor, partition or union, and order by dependency.

- **R (the residual).** Dependence of `closure(Γ)` on corpus structure. Arity: four-place (atoms, bonds, channel fidelity, closures contacted), not one-place in token count. Symmetry: not symmetric; data shapes reach, not the reverse, and inside the data symmetric bonds (+, ∥, ≡) differ from ordered bonds (→, ⇒, −, ⋛), so arrangement is not reducible to ingredients (Fig 12 :92). Scale: within-run loop, population of examples, membrane between closures (Fig 4 :20).
- **D1 Library and reach.** Actors: atoms(Γ), candidate terms φ, bonds, closure(Γ). Currency: composition then settlement. Anchor: the ground. Question type: partition (what composes across kinds). Admitted.
- **D2 Data as habitat.** Actors: examples, labels, actors, conditions, relations in contact. Currency: contact. Anchor: the ground the data is a projection of. Question type: union with D1. Admitted.
- **D3 Bond inventory.** Actors: seven bonds plus negation. Currency: bits for description, actions for testing. Anchor: task ground. Question type: union with D1 (overlap is a compound). Admitted.
- **D4 Channel and instrument.** Actors: percepts, metrics, per-slot indexing, objective link. Currency: contact fidelity. Anchor: the ground, which does not decay. Admitted.
- **D5 Cross-closure contact.** Actors: frames with differing closures, imports. Currency: union surplus. Anchor: ground via triangulation. Admitted.
- **X1 Model size as sole determinant of in-principle reach.** No nameable anchor for "in principle". Absorbed into D1 and D4 as a budget change. Not a domain.
- **X2 Corpus volume as a quality.** No nameable anchor; a frame-internal score (Fig 1 :27). Absorbed into D4 as a filter.

**Union, not partition.** The failure conditions overlap; a single lever clears only the region where one condition binds and leaves intersections standing (Fig 9 :55, Fig 9 :57). Each condition keeps its own verdict; no averaging.

**Order by dependency.** Perception -> vocabulary -> objective -> planning -> learn and carry (Fig 3 :20). The vocabulary/objective link is where corpus structure binds and where the instrument is usually missing (Fig 3 :27).

**Strain test applied.** The apparent strain between Fig 11 :40 (agent-only improvement changes nothing) and scaling results is resolved by re-census: scaling that changes contact is not agent-only; scaling at fixed contact is a budget change (Fig 9 :60). Different quantities. The apparent strain between Fig 12 :117 (isomers) and disjoint corpora is resolved: those are functional-group relatives (Fig 12 :124), not isomers.

## VERDICT (from the figures)

The shape survives; the number is a gap. Training-data structure fixes the learnable reach by fixing the atom inventory and the bond inventory, by maintaining the per-slot channel to the ground, and by determining which differing closures are contacted. Search inside a fixed inventory is idempotent and adds no atom. Volume, scale and benchmark score are filters for the budget, never witnesses for the reach. The four conditions are a union compound with separate verdicts, never averaged. The numeric reach of any named corpus is `gap`, owed to a source-discriminating instrument described in effect terms and not held. Mode `specified`, ungrounded, candidate. The strongest single figure-grounded points are F1 (idempotence) and F2 (arrangement is the substance), triangulated across three figures each.

- **cost if wrong:** bounded and locatable. The packet states mode, derivation and five refuters before the conclusion, so an error is findable rather than invisible. The real cost is spending an outside reader's independence by sending a volume-based story instead of a generator, which Fig 2 :42 prices: "Buy independence in proportion to the cost of being wrong." That cost is avoidable by leading with the shape claim and its refuter.
- **undoable:** yes. No term is deleted if a finding fails; a refutation is added and the reach falls because something was added rather than struck out (Fig 6 :42-43). The shape and its five falsifiers are recorded with provenance so a later reader can locate the error (Fig 10 :62-64).
- **reversed by:** a ground reading, not agreement. Specifically: (1) a new ground-verified atom from in-closure search alone; (2) a plan, goal, progress measure or refusal from a pure ingredient list with zero relational signal, bias or supervision; (3) ground-checked per-slot residual and objective from purely global aggregate signal; (4) an agent-only change at fixed habitat and contact moving the ground; (5) a new ground-verified atom from sources with evidence overlap one; or (6) an instrument that discriminates the atom and bond inventories of two corpora and closes F8. Until one fires, the verdict stands as `candidate`.
