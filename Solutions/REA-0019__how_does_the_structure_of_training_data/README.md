## What an answer would look like (bound as an ordinary term)

An answer is a **shape of dependence**: a statement of which properties of a corpus (atom inventory, bond inventory, per-slot indexing, channel fidelity, independence of sources) fix the set of representable and composable terms, together with a written refuter and a declaration of what remains unreachable. The sought-for shape is bound as a term in `01_figure_grounded_audit.md` so it can accrue a residual like anything else, rather than remaining an invisible preference (Formula, "Direction").

## What is deliberately not here

- No personal emails of named individuals.
- No ranking of people by how likely they are to agree with the shape claim.
- No bulk-send plan.

Three reasons, in the figures' own words:

1. **A room does not upgrade the label.** Fig 7 :13: "agreement compounds, evidence does not." A list assembled to produce agreement buys a convention, not a check.
2. **Correlation disqualifies a verdict.** Fig 2 :40: "agreement about the world is worthless when the agreeing frames share an evidence pool." Selecting reviewers for receptiveness manufactures the correlation that would make their agreement worthless.
3. **Asking for trust before checking spends the only thing an outside frame contributes.** Fig 10 :42: "A verifier that can reconstruct the claim cannot verify it." So stage 1 of the offer carries only the residual, the shape claim and the pre-registered refuter.

Legally: scraping personal emails for unsolicited bulk outreach breaches GDPR and CAN-SPAM style rules in many jurisdictions. That is a practical failure on top of the structural one.
