# Expert verification packet: the Tether on training-data structure

**What this is:** a claim in a form a machine-learning researcher can check. Mode, derivation and refuters are stated before the conclusion so a reader knows what to look for and what would settle it.

**Mode: `general` on the closure and bond laws, `specified` on the application to corpora, not `grounded`.** No learning run or instrument is claimed. This is a shape and a route, not a result.

## 1. The claim, in one paragraph

What a system can learn in principle is its reach, the set of terms it could represent and compose. A corpus does not set that reach by volume. It sets the reach by four things it supplies: the atom inventory (primitive distinctions), the bond inventory (order, production, comparison, absence, refusal), the fidelity and per-item indexing of the channel, and the independence of the closures it contacts. Search inside a fixed inventory is idempotent and adds no atom; the wall moves only on contact with a differing closure. Volume, parameter count and benchmark score are filters for the budget, not witnesses for the reach. The numeric reach of a named corpus is a declared gap, owed to a source-discriminating instrument described in effect terms.

## 2. Three checkable sub-claims

### C1. Arrangement is the substance, not the ingredient list (framework-internal, checkable by reading Fig 12)

- **Statement:** two corpora with identical item sets and different bond inventories have different learnable substances; a corpus of bare ingredients can name states and cannot name routes, goals, progress or refusals.
- **Source, verbatim:** Fig 12 :5, "A set of ingredients is not one. The bond is the rest of the information, and it is the half nothing was recording." Fig 12 :111, "A vocabulary without bonds can name a state and cannot name a route to one." Fig 12 :112, "A plan is a sequence. A goal is a comparison. Progress is a subtraction. A refutation is a negation, and that one is not a bond." Fig 12 :117, "isomer. Same atoms, different bond, different substance. The difference is invisible in the formula and decisive in the behaviour."
- **What to check:** is the bond inventory of a corpus a property distinct from its token inventory, and does the distinction survive a ground-checked probe rather than a benchmark score?
- **Refuter:** a ground-checked plan, goal, progress measure or refusal acquired from a pure ingredient list with zero relational signal in the data, zero relational inductive bias and zero external relation supervision. If this holds, the flat-formula collapse is not in play for learning systems and C1 is withdrawn.

### C2. The four-part dependence, with volume as a filter (framework-internal plus empirical check)

- **Statement:** the reach depends on atoms, bonds, channel fidelity and closure independence; quantity and scale are budget filters.
- **Source, verbatim:** Fig 6 :22, "NOVEL means novel relative to atoms, not to the world. IMPORT adds an atom, and the added atom is what expands the closure." Fig 1 :19, "The residual is indexed by object slot, not aggregated." Fig 10 :24, "the ground does not decay, the channel does." Fig 8 :29, "low overlap: B does not draw on A's evidence." Fig 9 :60, "Use filters for the budget and witnesses for the verdict. Never let a filter hand you a verdict."
- **What to check:** do the four conditions bind separately? A single lever (more data) should clear the region where only one binds and leave the intersections standing (Fig 9 :55). If the dependence is really one-dimensional in volume, C2 fails.
- **Refuter:** a ground measurement on which pure volume, at fixed atoms, bonds, contact and source independence, adds a new atom; or a demonstration that the four conditions collapse to one quantity under ground-checked probes.

### C3. The reach boundary is a gap, not a hidden answer (framework-internal limit)

- **Statement:** the figures give the shape of the dependence and refuse the numeric reach of a named corpus. The missing thing is an instrument described in effect terms, not a primitive the framework can name.
- **Source, verbatim:** Fig 8 :32, "Closure is idempotent. Composing inside a closed set never leaves it, so no search, however long, adds an atom. This one is definitional: it is what closure means." Fig 6 :23, "No frame escapes its own closure. The bound moves only on contact with a frame whose closure differs." Fig 9 :32, "you get a kind of thing, never the answer itself." Fig 13 :134, "Unreachable is a verdict here, not a silence." Fig 8 :39, "So a search that finds nothing is never a proof of absence."
- **What to check:** does any figure line license a numeric reach or a named missing primitive after a genuine re-census? If yes, the gap is closed.
- **Refuter:** an instrument whose readings discriminate the atom and bond inventory supplied by two corpora and whose output is checked against a ground. That converts the gap to a measurement, it does not flip the verdict.

## 3. What is explicitly not claimed

- Not a scaling law, a predicted accuracy, or a number for any corpus.
- Not that more data never helps. It buys search budget and coverage.
- Not that the corpus *is* the library Γ. It supplies atoms and bonds to Γ and is a substituted habitat.
- Not that model size is irrelevant. Size that changes contact is not agent-only; size at fixed contact is a budget change.
- Not a claim to have named the missing primitive. The framework returns a shape, not a name.
- Not `grounded`, and not a ground reading. The derivation is simulated and checked for internal consistency only.
- Not a final theory. The framework stops at the limit of the instruments and records the lineage.

## 5. What would settle it

1. Does a ground-checked probe distinguish a corpus supplying atom A from one that does not, at fixed volume and architecture?
2. Does a ground-checked probe distinguish a corpus carrying →, ⋛ or ¬ from an ingredient-only corpus at the same item set?
3. Does a per-slot live residual survive aggregation of the corpus signal?
4. Does reach extend to a new ground-verified atom only when a differing closure is contacted?
5. Does an agent-only change at fixed habitat and contact move the ground?

Standing falsifiers are the five in `01_figure_grounded_audit.md` section 4, written before the conclusion. Agreement from readers is not one of them.

## 6. What an expert can check in twenty minutes

1. Read Fig 12 :5 and :111 to :112, then Fig 6 :21 to :23 and Fig 8 :32. Four short passages.
2. Check C2 against your own work: are volume and scale filters in your field, or do they appear to change the representable inventory? Name one case that decides it.
3. Name one instrument that would distinguish an atom-supplying corpus from an atom-absent one, or say why none is possible.
4. Send the refutation. A failure is more useful than an endorsement.
