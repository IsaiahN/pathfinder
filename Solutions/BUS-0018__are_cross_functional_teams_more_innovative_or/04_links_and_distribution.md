## Wider literature actually read (secondary corroboration only)

These are open on 2026-11-22. Full provenance, verbatim quotes and overlap for each are in `10_source_ledger.csv` and `09_watson_answer.md`.

1. **Hong and Page, PNAS 2004** (mathematics / computational social science). "a team of randomly selected agents outperforms a team comprised of the best-performing agents" and "diversity trumps ability". The same paper records the cost side: "identity-diverse groups often have more conflict, more problems with communication, and less mutual respect and trust among members" and "problem solvers with diverse perspectives may have trouble understanding solutions identified by other agents." This is a low-overlap witness that mirrors the figure claim that independence is one quantity read twice (Fig 8 :47).
2. **Jehn, Administrative Science Quarterly 1995** (organizational psychology). "whether conflict was beneficial depended on the type of conflict and the structure of the group in terms of task type, task interdependence, and group norms." Corroborates Fig 11 :49, that valence is relative to the residual.
3. **De Wit, Greer and Jehn, Journal of Applied Psychology 2012** (meta-science). "conflict had stronger negative relations with team performance in highly complex (decision making, project, mixed) than in less complex (production) tasks." Corroborates the actions-cost half of Fig 12 :125-127.
4. **Wang and colleagues, Journal of Occupational and Organizational Psychology 2019** (organizational psychology). "Cognitive diversity encouraged IWB via the task reflexivity pathway, but impeded IWB via the relationship conflict pathway." A direct empirical instance of two opposite-signed paths from one input; corroborates the refusal to average.
5. **Wallrich and colleagues, Journal of Business and Psychology 2024** (registered-report meta-analysis). "the average linear relationships between demographic, job-related and cognitive diversity, and team performance are significant and positive, but insubstantial (|r| < .1)" and "Correlations were more positive when tasks were higher in complexity or required creativity and innovation." Corroborates the `specified`, not `general`, mode: no main-effect verdict, context matters.
6. **Loyd, Wang, Phillips and Lount, Organization Science 2012** (decision-making). "disagreeing dyads with social category diversity elaborate more prior to meeting and, as a result, perform better on a decision-making task than those with social category homogeneity." Corroborates Fig 2 :35: disagreement is raw material for triangulation when a task ground exists.
7. **Aggarwal and Woolley, Management Science 2019** (team cognition). "team cognitive diversity both enhances and inhibits team cognition". Corroborates the dual-path shape from a third independent author group.

No world source conflicts with the figure verdict, so the conflict rule (back to the census) was not triggered. Sources 2 and 3 share Jehn, and sources 4 and 6 share two authors, so their agreement is one pool in those pairs, declared rather than counted twice.

## Distribution: offer, do not push

Figure-grounded reasons:

- **Fig 10 :52**: "Bring a characterised residual, never the source." Sending the whole framework before anyone has described the team residual sends the source, not the residual.
- **Fig 10 :42**: "A verifier that can reconstruct the claim cannot verify it." A recipient who has absorbed the framework before giving a verdict has joined the frame being checked.
- **Fig 9 :27-28**: "describe it before you go looking, or any frame you pick will seem to fit." Ask what they would expect to see if the split claim were true before showing the derivation.
- **Fig 2 :35**: "Alignment is triangulation, not negotiation. A mirror cannot correct you." A list selected for people who already like the claim produces a convention, not a check.
- **Fig 4 :22**: "Only methods cross up. Only head starts come down." The formula may be given away as a method; the conclusion may not be pushed as an answer.

**Recommended order in a reply:** (1) the split and the two refuters; (2) ask what they would count as a failure and what ground they would use; (3) only after their verdict is recorded, the three framework links, with the note that the figures, not this summary, are the authority.

## The two-stage offer

- **Stage 1 (characterised residual first).** Present the union split, the two currencies and the refuters. No theory link yet. Record the recipient's verdict on stage 1 before any stage 2 material is shown.
- **Stage 2 (opt-in only).** If they are curious, offer the figure text, the figures repo and the canonical README. Their later agreement is never pooled as corroboration: "agreement about the world is worthless when the agreeing frames share an evidence pool" (Fig 2 :40).
- **Stage 3 (method reuse).** Offer the formula as a generator, with the instruction that each user names their own ground, writes their own refuter before the run, and states the mode of what they get.

**Hard precondition:** stages 1 to 3 work only if the stage-1 output survives. The refuters in `08_expert_verification_packet.md` are the test. If one fires, stop and narrow the claim rather than sending links.
