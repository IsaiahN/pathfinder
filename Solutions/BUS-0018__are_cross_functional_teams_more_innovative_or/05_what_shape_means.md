## 1. A shape is a constraint, not a filler

The figures README states the limit: the framework returns "what a missing thing would have to look like (a shape, not a name)". Fig 9 :32: "you get a kind of thing, never the answer itself". Fig 13 :133-135: "what it returns is a shape rather than a name" and "It cannot say which primitive is missing, because naming it would mean already holding it."

From *The Library of Babel* (carried): "You can cut your search short if the set of books returned is small and descriptive enough that it provides a general shape of the answer that satisfies your question."

For BUS-0018 a shape is the narrowed form of the answer we can state before we can name a winning team composition. It constrains: the two outcomes must be split, the union needs a residual and an anchor, independence is one parameter, and the two currencies cannot be added. It does not fill: it does not say that a particular mix of functions in a particular organization will win.

- **Shape:** a union of two loops, with conditions on each, reported as two verdicts.
- **Name (refused):** "cross-functional teams are the innovative choice" or "cross-functional teams are the conflictful choice".

## 2. How the shape is obtained

- *Library of Babel* (carried): use the vocabulary closest to the thing, look for recurring new terms that overlap with it, recurse. "With each new term gained, you narrow the space and deepen your vocabulary."
- Fig 6 :30: "outward: does another frame already hold it? The description travels. Fails by being sharpened into terms only we can read. Cast it in effect terms." Fig 9 :30-31: "your words are borrowed too, so describe the effect rather than the cause."
- *Decoding Tacit Knowledge* (carried): "When contact isn't possible, the best you can send is a plain description of what you'd expect to see, because anyone can check that against the world."
- Fig 9 :43: "The verdict tells you to leave. The lookup tells you where to go. Elimination is what you do after you arrive."

Applied here: describe the residual in effect terms (surplus of settled terms; cost of settling a decision), then look up the frames that already predict those shapes (union surplus in Fig 8, triangulation in Fig 2, two currencies in Fig 12), rather than picking a team-design ideology first and fitting the residual to it.

## 3. What would license a name

Only two things, both outside the shape itself:

1. **The ground settles it.** Formula step 5: "the ground settles it. Verified only by what pays. A gate is not the ground." Here that means team-level data with a named residual and an external anchor (a measured innovation output and a measured decision cost), not a survey of preferences.
2. **A provenanced import from a frame whose closure differs** (Fig 6 :23, Fig 8 :50-52), with a source gate and a shadow test. The world literature in `09_watson_answer.md` is such an import: it is used to corroborate the shape, never to overrule the figures.

*They're Scanning Books* (carried): "The real import is not a new term in the existing space. It is a new dimension of the space." And: "Declaring the primitives does not make them neutral. It makes the choice visible."

## 4. The specific forbidden error for this question

The forbidden move is treating a preference for "collaboration" or for "decisiveness" as if it were a term the loop had measured. Fig 5 :62-64: "A frame searching for an answer of a particular shape holds that shape as a PREFERENCE rather than as a term. It is not bound, it predicts nothing, and it accrues no residual, so nothing here can see it." The remedy given there is a discipline: "make the sought-for shape an ORDINARY TERM. Write down what an answer would look like, bind it, and let it accrue a residual like anything else."

That is why `01_figure_grounded_audit.md` section 0 binds "what an answer would look like" before any figure is quoted, and why the refuter is written before the conclusion. Without that, a search of the team literature would simply find whichever study agreed with the preferred answer, which is Fig 9 :27-28: "describe it before you go looking, or any frame you pick will seem to fit."

*The Hard Problem is About Source* (carried) gives the mechanism: an instrument that reads green "cannot comment on the source"; "If several paths create the same behavior, then behavior is a projection with lossy information." Here, a single satisfaction survey reads a team's mood and cannot comment on whether the team's closure moved or settled anything. What is owed is a better instrument (a source-discriminating measure), not a name.

## 5. Why there is no one-paragraph verdict with a winner

*The Recursive Self-Improvement Trap* (carried): "Prestige must never be allowed to validate an action directly... Prestige can fund an action. It can give an action a budget to be tried. But it cannot settle an action. Only the ground can settle it." A famous framework or a famous meta-analysis is a budget to try a claim, not a verdict. Fig 11 :39 (read directly): "capability is a property of agent-and-habitat, never of the agent alone." A team's outcome belongs to the team and its habitat, not to a portable slogan about cross-functionality.

## 6. My own audit is a simulation, not a ground reading

Same essay (carried): "LLMs can quickly simulate an approximate answer/output... However, the truly testable output would need to be derived from the real architecture or working the formula yourself." LLM output is "simulacra." So `01_figure_grounded_audit.md`, this file and `09_watson_answer.md` are simulated derivations, checked for internal consistency against the figures. They are **not** tests of any team and must be labelled as such. They cannot settle the question they report on.

## 7. Canonical limit, stated plainly

The framework can say what is reachable from what we hold, what is unreachable and therefore owed (here: a ground reading with a measured residual and an external anchor), and what a missing thing would have to look like (here: a source-discriminating instrument for surplus versus settling cost). Then it stops at the limit of what the available instruments can distinguish and records the lineage so the stop is checkable. "Indiscernibility is not identity" (figures README). Unreachable is a verdict, not a silence (Fig 13 :134).
