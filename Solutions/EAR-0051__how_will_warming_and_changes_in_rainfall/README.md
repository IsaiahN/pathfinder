## What is deliberately not here

- No personal emails of named individuals.
- No ranking of people by how likely they are to agree.
- No bulk-send plan.

Reason, in the figures' own words: Fig 2 :35 "Alignment is triangulation, not negotiation. A mirror cannot correct you." Fig 2 :40 "agreement about the world is worthless when the agreeing frames share an evidence pool". Selecting recipients for receptiveness manufactures the correlation that would make their agreement worthless, and a ground in the room has already become a frame (Fig 2 :47).
