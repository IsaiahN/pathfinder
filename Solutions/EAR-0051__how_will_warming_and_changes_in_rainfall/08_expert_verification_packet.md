# Expert verification packet: soil carbon under warming and rainfall

**What this is:** a claim in a form a soil scientist or agricultural climate impacts researcher can check. Mode, derivation and refuters are stated before the conclusion.

**Mode: `specified` on the shape, `general` on the structural reading, NOT `grounded`.** No magnitude for 2100 is claimed. This is a shape and a route, not a result. It is a simulated derivation checked for internal consistency against the figures, not a ground reading.

## 1. The claim, in one paragraph

Warming and rainfall change are two separately enumerated habitat conditions, not one climate variable. Their bond is a flow: temperature is the energy state and moisture the gradient, and the two are different terms over time and space. The combined question is a union compound, so a single-factor lever leaves the intersection standing. Net soil-carbon change is a comparison between input and output flows with a threshold where the sign flips; carbon fractions are isomers whose different bonds make bulk totals misleading; decomposer valence is relative to carbon storage, and cascade terms (erosion, fire, thaw, snow-cover change) can dominate direct effects. The answer shape is a slot-indexed map with a stated threshold region and a refuter, not a global number.

## 2. Three checkable sub-claims

### C1: Union compound and slot indexing (framework-internal plus empirical)

- **Statement:** the two-condition case is categorically different from either single-condition case, and the residual must be indexed by soil slot.
- **Source, verbatim:** Fig 9 :56 "A single lever clears the region where only one condition holds and leaves the intersections standing, because the second condition still binds." Also Fig 1 :19 "The residual is indexed by object slot, not aggregated."
- **What to check:** do the available warming-only and precipitation-only experiments, taken together, predict the combined fraction-resolved result within error? Beillouin et al. 2023 report small mean direct effects with wide intervals; Chen et al. 2026 report a 55% topsoil SOC increase only at +4 °C combined with increased precipitation.
- **Refuter:** a single-factor response function that predicts the combined result within error at all slots and fractions, and a global mean that predicts each slot's sign and magnitude. If both hold, C1 fails.

### C2: Comparison with a threshold, and isomeric fractions (framework-internal)

- **Statement:** net flux sign is decided by a comparison at a threshold, and carbon fractions with the same bulk total behave differently.
- **Source, verbatim:** the operators table defines ⋛ as "a threshold relation between two quantities"; Fig 12 :117 "isomer. Same atoms, different bond, different substance. The difference is invisible in the formula and decisive in the behaviour."
- **What to check:** is the response fraction-specific and nonlinear near a switch? Chen et al. 2026 attribute their gain mainly to mineral-associated organic carbon linked to fungal residues, with functional thresholds in the 2 to 4 °C range. Is that consistent with what you would expect from the measurements you know?
- **Refuter:** net flux linear and additive in temperature and water with no threshold, and all fractions responding proportionally. If both hold, C2 fails.

### C3: Cascade and loop sign (framework-internal plus empirical)

- **Statement:** indirect effects are first-class operands and the carbon loop's sign can flip.
- **Source, verbatim:** Fig 11 :52 "And contact cascades. Scoping the habitat to direct contact is how DDT reached the eagles: first-order enumeration, second-order effect, and the residual that mattered was three steps out." Fig 12 :121 "cycle. The output re-enters as input. The sign of the loop decides whether it closes, and a chain cannot close by construction."
- **What to check:** in the synthesised record, do erosion, wildfire, thaw and snow-cover change exceed direct temperature and rainfall effects? Both IPCC assessments and Beillouin et al. 2023 say broadly yes. IPCC AR6 also states that land can shift from sink to source under very high emissions.
- **Refuter:** indirect effects strictly smaller than direct effects everywhere, and a loop sign fixed under every reachable temperature and water combination. If both hold, C3 fails.

## 5. What would settle it

A fraction-resolved, multi-level warming-by-precipitation experiment replicated across slots, plus maintained long-term monitoring. An instrument that discriminates the threshold region would name what the figures leave open: where the sign flips and how large the flux is. Agreement among models is not such an instrument when the models share an evidence pool (Fig 2 :40 "agreement about the world is worthless when the agreeing frames share an evidence pool").

## 6. What an expert can check in twenty minutes

1. Read Fig 9 :56, Fig 1 :19, Fig 12 :117 and Fig 11 :52, four short passages.
2. Check C1 against one combined warming-plus-rainfall study you know: did the single-factor results predict it?
3. Name one fraction-resolved measurement that would locate the sign flip, or say why none is possible.
4. Send the refutation. A failure is more useful than an endorsement.
