# The Tether's answer on warming, rainfall and soil carbon storage

**Mode: `specified` on the shape, `general` on the structural reading, not `grounded`.** Simulated derivation checked against the figures, not a ground reading (`05_what_shape_means.md` §6).

## 1. The residual it answers

The question asks for the effect of two conditions on a stock. In effect terms the residual is the gap between what a frame predicts for the net soil-carbon flux under a given temperature and water combination, and what the ground returns, measured per soil slot and per carbon fraction. It has arity three (warming, rainfall, interaction), is not symmetric, and scales from plot to region to cascade. It is a union compound, not one hard question: Fig 9 :57 "What remains is smaller and it is also categorically different: a case in two conditions is not a harder case of either."

## 2. The shape

1. **Two conditions, enumerated separately.** Fig 11 :43 "The habitat is enumerated, never composed." Fig 11 :46 "conditions: light, water, temperature, season, terrain". Warming and rainfall are on the habitat's own condition list. A single blended "climate" index loses the axis the question asks about.

2. **Their bond is a flow, not an addition.** Fig 13 :76 "Energy and a gradient compose a flow. A gradient over space and one over time are different terms, because which state it is bonded to is part of what it is." Temperature is the energy state, moisture the gradient. A warming-over-time result and a rainfall-across-space result are different terms and cannot substitute for one another.

3. **The net flux is a comparison with a threshold.** The operators table defines ⋛ as "a threshold relation between two quantities". Input and output flows are weighed; the sign flips at equality. Fig 12 :123 "functional group. A substructure whose presence fixes the outcome. The group says what kind; the qualifier says which member." Fractions are members of the group and are not interchangeable.

4. **The interaction is where the structure lives.** Fig 9 :56 "A single lever clears the region where only one condition holds and leaves the intersections standing, because the second condition still binds." This is why a warming-only or rainfall-only study cannot answer the compound question. The world data agree in structure: Beillouin et al. 2023 find small mean direct effects, while Chen et al. 2026 find a large fraction-specific response only when warming and increased precipitation are combined.

5. **Actors' valence is relative to this residual.** Fig 11 :49 "An actor's valence is relative to the residual, not a property it carries." A decomposer beneficial for nutrient cycling can be harmful for carbon storage, and the same actor can flip under a new temperature and water regime.

6. **Effects cascade.** Fig 11 :52 "And contact cascades. Scoping the habitat to direct contact is how DDT reached the eagles: first-order enumeration, second-order effect, and the residual that mattered was three steps out." Erosion, wildfire, thaw and snow-cover change are second and third-order terms, and the synthesised literature finds them larger than the direct effects.

7. **The loop can flip sign.** Fig 12 :121 "cycle. The output re-enters as input. The sign of the loop decides whether it closes, and a chain cannot close by construction." Carbon lost to the atmosphere warms the system, which can increase loss again. A linear chain of causes cannot represent that.

8. **Managed soil is permanent work.** Fig 11 :63 "Which is why a managed ecosystem is permanent work rather than a solved state." Management can add residue, trees or biochar; it cannot subtract a changed habitat.

9. **The answer is a map, not a number.** Fig 9 :32 "you get a kind of thing, never the answer itself". Fig 1 :19 "The residual is indexed by object slot, not aggregated." What the figures license is a slot-indexed, fraction-resolved conditional map with a stated threshold region and a refuter, not a global PgC figure.

## 3. What it explicitly does not say

- Not a global soil-carbon number for 2100, not a threshold date, not a ranking of which slot dominates. Those are world claims, `specified` at best.
- Not that CO2 fertilisation will or will not compensate the losses. The figures license the comparison shape, not which operand wins.
- Not that any particular microbe is good or bad for carbon. Valence is relative to the residual (Fig 11 :49).
- Not a derivation of soil respiration from the six-term set. The binding of temperature to energy and moisture to gradient is ours, provenanced as such.
- Not `grounded`. Nothing here was settled by a measurement taken for this folder.

## 4. What would settle it, the ground

1. **A discriminator experiment:** fraction-resolved SOC under multiple warming levels crossed with multiple precipitation levels, at several slots, reporting where the sign flips. This is INWARD: sharpen the instrument already returning something (Fig 6 :33 "Nobody specified a telescope from an account of Mars.").
2. **A long-term channel:** maintained monitoring with the slot index preserved, since the ground does not decay but the channel can (Fig 10 :24 "the ground does not decay, the channel does").
3. **An imported frame with a shadow test:** the critical-transitions literature supplies the threshold shape the soil frame leaves undescribed, at the price of reduced independence. Fig 6 :23 "No frame escapes its own closure. The bound moves only on contact with a frame whose closure differs."
4. **Standing falsifiers from `01`:** single-factor prediction of the combined result, linear-additive flux with no threshold, global mean predicting each slot, indirect effects strictly smaller than direct.

## 5. The honest stop

**Reached for the shape, unreached for the magnitude.** Fig 8 :39 "a search that finds nothing is never a proof of absence." The literature explicitly says data on individual and combined direct effects are very limited, so the missing magnitude is an owed measurement, not a proof that no answer exists. Fig 13 :134 "Unreachable is a verdict here, not a silence". The answer stops at the limit of what the available instruments distinguish and records the lineage so the stop is checkable.

## 6. One-paragraph version for outreach

> The Tether does not give a global soil-carbon number for 2100. It says that warming and rainfall are two separately enumerated habitat conditions whose bond is a flow, not a sum; that warming-only and rainfall-only results leave their interaction standing, so the answer must be a slot-indexed, fraction-resolved conditional map; that the net flux is a comparison with a threshold where sink turns to source, and that the larger carbon loop can flip sign; that decomposers and other actors have no fixed good or bad valence for carbon storage; and that erosion, fire, thaw and snow-cover change are cascade terms that can outweigh the direct effects. What it offers is that shape, the refuters written in advance, and the instruments that would close it. The magnitude stays open until the ground settles it.
