## What the wider literature does to the shape claim

Four findings bear directly, and they are not one evidence pool.

1. **The single-driver result is small; the interaction is where the structure is.** Beillouin et al. find near-zero mean effects for warming and rainfall increase individually, with wide intervals. Chen et al. find a 55% topsoil gain that appears only at +4 °C *with* increased precipitation. This is precisely Fig 9 :56: "A single lever clears the region where only one condition holds and leaves the intersections standing, because the second condition still binds." The two sources are by different authors, from a synthesis and a field experiment, so their agreement on the priority of the interaction is low-overlap corroboration, not a pooled witness.
2. **Indirect effects outrank direct ones.** Both IPCC assessments and Beillouin et al. report that erosion, wildfire, thaw and snow-cover change matter more than the direct temperature effect in the synthesised record. This is Fig 11 :52 contact cascades in data form.
3. **The sign can flip.** IPCC AR6 states that land can transition from sink to source under very high emissions. This is Fig 12 :121: "The sign of the loop decides whether it closes, and a chain cannot close by construction." The location of the flip is a world claim, not a figure output.
4. **Detection is an instrument problem.** Bury et al. and Scheffer et al. show that a system can look unchanged right up to a threshold and that generic early-warning signals can fail to identify which transition is approaching. This corroborates Fig 6 :34: "Where nothing is, there is no edge to extend from" and the INWARD rule: sharpen the instrument already returning something.

**No conflict was triggered.** Beillouin's "small mean effect" and Chen's "55% gain" look opposed but are different questions at different scales: a global mean across heterogeneous slots versus one slot under a specific combination with a threshold. Per the standing rule, a conflict sends the reader back to the domain census, and the re-census (slot indexing, fraction resolution, union compound) dissolves it. If a future study shows the same threshold at the same slot in both frames, that would be convergence at maximum independence, not a pooled witness.
