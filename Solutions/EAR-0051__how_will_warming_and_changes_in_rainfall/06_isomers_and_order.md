## DERIVATION

### Q1: One response or a compound?

- Fig 9 :54 "And when the split lands on a union rather than a partition, unbundle before you search again."
- Fig 9 :57 "What remains is smaller and it is also categorically different: a case in two conditions is not a harder case of either."

The residual is a **union compound**. Warming and rainfall change overlap on the same soil slot; the intersection is not reducible to either driver. Fig 11 :46 "conditions: light, water, temperature, season, terrain" enumerates the two conditions separately. Fig 13 :76 "Energy and a gradient compose a flow" gives the bond: temperature is the energy state, moisture the gradient, and the flow they compose is the process that moves carbon.

The empirical anchor is Chen et al. 2026: at +4 °C the response differed categorically depending on whether precipitation increased. That is the intersection standing after the single-factor lever cleared its region.

**Falsifier:** a single response function with an interaction coefficient indistinguishable from zero across slots and fractions.

### Q2: What kind of structure is a soil-carbon stock?

- Fig 12 :123 "functional group. A substructure whose presence fixes the outcome. The group says what kind; the qualifier says which member."

Particulate organic matter and mineral-associated organic matter share the functional group (a carbon store in soil) but differ in the qualifier (bonded to mineral surfaces or not). The group fixes the kind; the qualifier fixes which member. They cannot be substituted in a flux calculation.

- Fig 12 :117 "isomer. Same atoms, different bond, different substance. The difference is invisible in the formula and decisive in the behaviour."

Two soils with the same total carbon and different arrangements (labile particles versus mineral-associated residues) are **isomers** in the figure's sense: same ingredients, different bond, different behaviour. A bulk carbon percentage records the ingredients and loses the arrangement. This is the figure's "flat formula" failure, Fig 12 :14 "the ingredients are recorded and the arrangement is not".

Chen et al. 2026 report that the +4 °C gain was *primarily* in mineral-associated organic carbon linked to fungal residues. That is a bond-level result, invisible in a bulk stock total.

**Falsifier:** if all carbon fractions respond proportionally under every warming and rainfall combination, the isomer reading fails and the bulk total is sufficient.

### Q3: What survives its own use?

- Fig 12 :119 "catalyst. A part that survives its own use. Spent and regenerating parts behave differently across uses, and nothing records which a term is."

Microbes decompose and are not consumed by the act in the way a reagent is; microbial necromass can persist as mineral-associated carbon. A microbial residue is a catalyst's output becoming a stable atom at the next level. Fig 12 :104 "A settled molecule becomes an atom for whatever composes over it. So irreducible is a fact about a level and never about the world." Once settled as a fraction, that residue becomes an operand for the next turn of the loop.

The practical point: measuring only active microbial biomass misses the spent-and-persisting part that fixes the long-term stock. Fig 12 :119's warning is literal here, "nothing records which a term is".

**Falsifier:** show that microbial residues do not persist differentially from the substrate they processed, so spent and regenerating parts behave identically.

### Q4: What is the order of the whole derivation?

1. **Enumerate** the habitat, conditions first (Fig 11 :43-48). This fixes what is in contact with the residual.
2. **Describe** the residual in effect terms before choosing any explanatory frame (Fig 9 :27-28).
3. **Index by slot** (Fig 1 :19). Never start with the global mean.
4. **Compose the flow** from energy and gradient (Fig 13 :76), separately for the warming-over-time and rainfall-over-space terms.
5. **Resolve the fractions** so the arrangement is recorded, not just the ingredients (Fig 12 :117, :123).
6. **Compare** input and output with ⋛, a threshold relation (operators table :48-49). The sign is decided here.
7. **Close the loop** (Fig 12 :121): the output re-enters as input, and the sign of the loop decides whether the slot amplifies or damps.
8. **Verify at the ground** with independent frames (Fig 2 :29, :31-32). A model ensemble is not a second frame if it shares the evidence pool.

Order matters by dependency (Fig 3 :20-21): effort spent on step 6 before steps 1 to 5 arrives at a step that cannot use it. A global model run before slots and fractions are enumerated is an optimisation above the break.

### Q5: Can the two currencies be added?

- Fig 12 :126 "Bits price the description. Actions price finding out whether it holds. They are in different units, so a system holding one accounting cannot compare them"

The description cost of a fraction-resolved map (bits) is not the measurement cost of getting the fractions (actions). A global number is cheap to state and expensive to settle; a detailed map is expensive to state and may be cheap to refute at one slot. Fig 12 :128 "The two readings come apart in both directions." Budget chooses how much of the space is reached, but the budget is a filter, never a witness (Fig 9 :60).

**Falsifier:** a single scalar that ranks both description cost and settlement cost correctly across all candidate answers. None is claimed here.

## CONCLUSION

The soil-carbon question is a **union compound**, not one response. Its carbon pools form a **functional group** whose members are **isomers** under the figure's definition: same carbon, different bond, different behaviour. Microbial residues are **catalysts** whose spent forms persist as stable atoms at the next level. The correct order is enumerate, describe, index, compose, resolve, compare, close, verify. The sign of the loop can flip, which is why a linear chain of causes cannot represent it.

What this rescues: the empirical findings that looked contradictory (small global mean effects in Beillouin et al. 2023, a 55% fraction-specific gain in Chen et al. 2026) are both expected once slots, fractions and the union compound are respected. What it does not do: supply a magnitude or a threshold location. Those remain world claims.
