## 2. What the wider literature contributes, and what it does not

The economics sources are a single broad evidence pool about firms, contracts and institutions. They corroborate the shape from inside the question's own field, so they are weak independent witnesses. Two points matter most here.

1. **Coase makes the two-currency point before the figures do.** "There are negotiations to be undertaken, contracts have to be drawn up, inspections have to be made, arrangements have to be made to settle disputes, and so on." Drawing up a contract (description bits) and inspecting performance (settling actions) are named as separate activities. That is F4 in a different vocabulary.
2. **Hart and Holmström make the residual-control point.** Where a contract cannot specify future eventualities, it must "specify who has the right to decide what to do when the parties cannot agree." That is why the contractor mode is a compound with its own residual-control structure, and why Fig 9 :55's union reading applies.

Low-overlap fields give the independent checks:

- **Law and employment classification (DOL).** The economic reality test says "No single factor determines a worker's status" and the "totality of the circumstances" governs. That is a different-pool corroboration of F1 (per-slot, no aggregate lever) and F5 (contractor is a compound of conditions). It does not corroborate a numeric threshold.
- **Cognition (Simon via Wikipedia).** Bounded rationality says decision makers "will select a decision that is satisfactory rather than optimal" and that organisms have finite "computational capacities." That corroborates the two-currency limit: a firm cannot compute an all-in optimum over every contingency, so it describes what it can and allocates decision rights for the rest.
- **Biology (symbiogenesis).** A formerly free-living organism can become an organelle as "the loss of autonomy and integration of the endosymbiont with its host" proceeds. As an analogue it corroborates F2 and F5: the boundary between independent partner and integrated component is a real, gradual, observable thing, and autonomy is what changes. It is an analogue, not a firm-level measurement, and is labelled as such in `09_watson_answer.md`.
- **Economic history (Smith and Stigler via Wikipedia).** "The extent of the market limits the division of labour." That is the low-overlap, pre-theoretical statement of F2/F3: the boundary moves with the reach of the market. It shares a field with Coase, so it is not independent of the economics pool.

## 4. The two-stage offer

1. **Stage 1.** Send the residual, the shape claim and the pre-registered refuter. Ask for a failure, not an endorsement. Fig 10 :52: "Bring a characterised residual, never the source."
2. **Stage 2, opt-in.** If they are curious, offer the figure link, the Coase and Nobel sources, and the DOL fact sheet. Their verdict on stage 1 must already be recorded; their later agreement carries zero verdict weight.
3. **Stage 3, opt-in.** Offer the formula as a method to reuse. Each user writes their own refuter and states their own mode. *The Recursive Self-Improvement Trap*: "the output is only as good as the validator. The agent, or the tool, or the frame, or the theory, cannot validate itself."

**Hard precondition:** stages 1 to 3 work only if the stage-1 output survives. If a reader fires any of the four refuters, stop and retract the broken part before showing any framework material.
