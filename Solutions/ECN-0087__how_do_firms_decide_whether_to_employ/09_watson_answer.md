## QUESTION

Decide, in effect terms, how a firm chooses among employing a worker directly, using a contractor, and outsourcing production, and say what each option leads to. Restated: for one activity slot, which governance frame keeps the productive atoms composable at the lowest total of two non-additive costs (description bits and settling actions) while the realized cost and quality ground stays reachable? Option A, assert a universal numeric make-or-buy rule. Option B, keep the figure-derived per-slot shape, refuse the number, and state the ground and refuters. A sends a verdict in `general` mode where only `specified` is licensed and spends the independence of the reader. B sends a checkable shape with an open parameter until a ground settles it.

## DOMAIN CENSUS

R by arity, symmetry and scale, then one line per domain with actors, currency and anchor; partition or union; order by dependency.

- **R (arity / symmetry / scale).** Arity: three named options, structurally a two-place inside/outside relation with a hybrid middle, so a union rather than a partition. Symmetry: the options are not interchangeable; their governance bond differs, so Fig 12 applies, and the comparison is a threshold, not a symmetric join. Scale: first order is the activity slot, second order is the firm boundary, third order is the market that prices the result.
- **D1 Activity-slot frame.** Actors: the activity, its atoms, the manager. Currency: residual reduction per slot. Anchor: realized cost and quality of that activity. Admitted.
- **D2 Firm and market closure frames.** Actors: the firm, suppliers, customers. Currency: internal allocation and prices. Anchor: the market, named as an anchor in Fig 2 :29. Admitted.
- **D3 Contracting compound.** Actors: client, contractor, contract. Currency: specified obligations and residual control rights. Anchor: realized performance under the contract. Admitted but unbundled, since it is hierarchy inside and market at the interface.
- **D4 Measurement channel.** Actors: accounting, audits, benchmarks. Currency: readings of cost and quality. Anchor: the ground, which does not update; the channel can decay. Admitted.
- **X1 Universal make-or-buy number.** No nameable anchor; not a domain yet. Rejected.
- **X2 Price settles everything.** Absorbed into D2 and D4; price is the anchor for cost and an instrument for quality and specificity.

Order by dependency: describe the activity, state what counts as progress, locate the frame, choose, measure (Fig 3 :20). The middle link has no instrument in most firms.

Strain test: Fig 4 "The Membrane" was first read as the firm boundary; re-census assigned it to scale boundaries only and moved the boundary finding to Fig 6 and Fig 8. Fig 13's cross-kind rule was retracted as a governance figure. No strain remains hidden (see `01` §7).

## VERDICT (from the figures)

The shape survives; the number does not. Decide per activity slot; internalize until the firm's closure stops reaching an atom, then go outside; treat overlap as the governor of market versus hierarchy; hold description bits and verification actions apart; treat contracting as a union compound and a different bond over the same atoms; let realized cost and quality, not agreement, settle the choice. The universal numeric threshold is a declared `gap`, reachable only by measurement or by a provenanced import. Mode `specified` on the firm application, `general` on the structural laws, not `grounded`.

- **Cost if wrong:** a single lever is applied to a union. A firm with high-overlap, relationship-specific activities is told to outsource by a scalar that ignores hold-up, or a firm with standard, switchable activities is integrated because its accounting prices description but not verification. The real cost is spending a reader's independence by sending a conclusion instead of a refuter, which Fig 2 :42 prices: "Buy independence in proportion to the cost of being wrong." That cost is avoidable by leading with the shape claim and its four refuters.
- **Undoable:** yes. No term is deleted if the claim fails; a refutation is added and the reach falls because something was added rather than struck out. The shape, the retraction log and the refuters are recorded with provenance, so a later reader can locate the error rather than only feel it. A sent claim can be superseded without withdrawing any primitive.
- **Reversed by:** a ground reading or a genuine conflict from a different evidence pool. Specifically: a firm-level average that predicts per-activity governance as well as per-slot measurement; reach renewed by internal composition alone; a relationship-specific activity repeatedly run by a clean arms-length contract with no hold-up; a single scalar that predicts across contexts without losing the two-currency distinction; a contractor described by one condition alone; or a figure line that directly yields the numeric threshold. Consensus, prestige, and a larger body of agreeing economics papers are not such reversals.
