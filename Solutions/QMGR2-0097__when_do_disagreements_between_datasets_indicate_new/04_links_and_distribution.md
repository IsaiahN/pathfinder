## What the wider literature contributes

Four distinct fields, with overlap declared:

1. **Particle-physics data analysis (Lyons, Gross and Vitells).** This is the field closest to the question. Lyons supplies the operating vocabulary: look-elsewhere effect, subconscious Bayes factor, systematics, and the observation that 3 or 4 sigma effects have historically gone away with more data. Gross and Vitells supply the mechanism: a trial factor measures how much a significance shrinks once you account for searching many places. Both corroborate Fig 9 :60 (a significance value is a budget filter, not a verdict) and Fig 8 :40-42 (unreachable-at-budget versus underivable).
2. **Metrology (Pohl, Antognini, Bezginov).** The proton radius puzzle is the cleanest published instance of a 5 sigma disagreement whose resolutions were all in-closure corrections, not new particles. It corroborates the "underestimated errors" side of the sort rule, and the later independent electron measurements corroborate the need for a differing frame (Fig 8 :47).
3. **Cosmology and experimental particle physics (BICEP2, OPERA).** Both were high-significance claims that dissolved into foreground or instrument faults. BICEP2 is Fig 9 :4-5 (a stubborn gap that splits into components once a new frame, the Planck dust map, is added). OPERA is Fig 10 :24 (the channel decays, not the ground) and Fig 2 :31 (an independent frame beats internal replication).
4. **Cross-field statistics (Benjamin et al.).** A community can propose changing a significance threshold. That is what a convention looks like, and it corroborates Fig 10 :47.

Overlap: Lyons, Gross and Vitells are in one particle-physics methods pool. Pohl and Antognini are one proton-radius pool; Bezginov is the same measurement programme at a later date. BICEP2 and OPERA are separate instruments and separate communities, so they count as genuinely low-overlap corroboration. The framework essays and figures are the author's own pool and are primary, not independent witnesses.

**No world source conflicts with the figure verdict.** If one had, the rule is to return to the domain census, not to flip the verdict.

## Distribution rules: offer, do not push

- **Fig 10 :52** *"Bring a characterised residual, never the source."* Send the sort rule and the refuter first. The full framework goes only after a verdict on stage 1 is recorded.
- **Fig 10 :42** *"A verifier that can reconstruct the claim cannot verify it."* A recipient who has absorbed the interiors before being asked for a verdict has joined the frame being checked.
- **Fig 9 :27-28** *"describe it before you go looking, or any frame you pick will seem to fit"*. Ask what they would expect to see if the rule were right before offering the figure links.
- **Fig 2 :40** *"agreement about the world is worthless when the agreeing frames share an evidence pool"*. A later agreement with the framework is never pooled as corroboration.
- **Fig 2 :42** *"Buy independence in proportion to the cost of being wrong."* Buy the independent frame first, then spend on the argument.

**Recommended order in a reply:** (1) the sort rule and its four refuters; (2) the question of what the recipient would count as a failure; (3) only then the figure links, with the note that the figures, not the email, are the authority; (4) the formula as a reusable method, with the instruction that each user writes their own refuter and states their mode.

## The two-stage offer

Stage 1 is validation of the output against a ground the recipient knows. Stage 2 is opt-in reading of the theory that produced it. Stage 3 is reuse of the formula as a method. Stage 2 never becomes evidence about stage 1, because a reader who has seen the interiors is no longer an outside frame.

## What this agent cannot do

Nothing is sent by this agent. Hosting the folder and sending the link is yours to do. Prefer an opt-in download or a public submission route over an attachment pushed to a list.
