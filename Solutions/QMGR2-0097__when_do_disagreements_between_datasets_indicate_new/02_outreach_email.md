# Outreach draft - check the sort rule before deciding about the framework

**Use:** one-to-one, or as a cover letter attached to a submission through the public venues in `03_public_contact_points.csv`. **Not** for bulk send.

**Hard precondition:** do not send until the two stage-1 output checks below are stated exactly as written. The output must survive validation or the two-stage offer collapses on contact.

---

**Subject:** One decision rule for dataset disagreements, with its refuter, before any theory

> Hi [name],
>
> I have one specific output to check against your own experience with conflicting measurements. I am not asking you to read the framework first, and I am not asking you to agree with anything. If the output does not survive, there is nothing else to look at.
>
> **The output (`general` shape, `specified` mapping, not `grounded`):** a disagreement between two datasets is not one number with two opposite readings. It is a sort with several outcomes. The rule I get from the figures runs in this order:
>
> 1. Is there an anchor, a quantity that does not move when either dataset is pushed?
> 2. Are the two datasets independent frames, or one evidence pool sharing calibration, theory inputs, code or a parent sample?
> 3. Is there a fact here? If the gap will not shrink with effort and each analysis works on a different subset, split it rather than search it.
> 4. Is the residual live in one channel or bin, or is it an artifact of pooling?
> 5. Which of five kinds is it: nothing owed, a probe to aim, a mis-attached term to refit, a broken mechanism that owes a new term, or a held term that is false and owes a refusal?
> 6. Is the explanation already in the closure and merely unfound, in which case more search or a better instrument is the remedy, or is it underivable from what is held, in which case a term from a different frame is owed?
>
> Under this rule, "underestimated errors" and "new physics" are different locations in a sort, not ends of a significance scale. A significance value tells you where to spend effort; it does not decide what the discrepancy means. The rule returns a shape and a route, not the name of the cause in any particular case. Where the cause is not settled, the honest output is "unreachable with what we hold, and here is what would reverse it".
>
> **What I am not claiming:** that the rule names any real anomaly as new physics or as an error. It does not. It does not replace 5σ with a different threshold, and it does not claim that a threshold can decide a verdict. It does not say agreement between two groups is corroboration when those groups share an evidence pool. Convergent derivation and adopted import look identical in the contents; only the record of where each came from separates them.
>
> **What would refute the output.** I wrote these down before sending this:
> 1. Exhibit a dataset-disagreement outcome that none of the five bins and the split rule can hold.
> 2. Show that evidence independence is not the quantity that decides whether a disagreement bears on the ground.
> 3. Derive a numeric significance cut from the figures that settles a verdict without a ground reading.
> 4. Show a case where the ground moved and the channel was intact, but the rule forces a "lost channel" reading.
>
> If any one holds, the sort rule is wrong and I will retract it. A one-line "this fails because..." is more useful to me than agreement. If you would rather not engage, no reply is needed.
>
> **If it survives, and only then:** here are the sources. The figures are the authority, not my summary of them.
> - Canonical figure text with line numbers (local extract): `FRAMEWORK/reference/03-FIGURES_TEXT.md`
> - Figures and formula for direct download: https://github.com/IsaiahN/tether/tree/general-agent/figures
> - The worked derivation and the full WATSON answer are in the folder this letter came from.
>
> **If you want to use the machinery:** the formula is in the figures link. Run it on a disagreement you know well and can check against a ground. Write down what would refute your result *before* you run it, and state the mode of what you get. A reported failure is more useful to me than a reported success.

## Why this version is ordered this way

| the move | figure-grounded reason |
|---|---|
| Output first, theory second | Fig 10 :52 - *"Bring a characterised residual, never the source."* |
| Do not ask for agreement | Fig 2 :35 - *"Alignment is triangulation, not negotiation. A mirror cannot correct you."* |
| Pre-registered refuter | The refuter is written before the run, so it is a design instrument rather than a rationalisation after the fact. |
| Mode stated on every claim | `THE_FORMULA.md` - *"Mode. general · specified · grounded. State which."* |
| Independence before significance | Fig 2 :31, :40 - the frames' evidence overlap decides whether the disagreement carries ground information; a p-value does not. |
| Split before search | Fig 9 :4-5 - *"are not one hard question but several well-formed ones. Split it rather than search."* |
| Five bins, not a two-way split | Fig 5 :11-14, :53 - four kinds sort by what is missing, the fifth by what is wrong. |
| Threshold withheld as a witness | Fig 9 :60 - *"Use filters for the budget and witnesses for the verdict. Never let a filter hand you a verdict."* |
| Case name withheld | Fig 9 :32 - *"you get a kind of thing, never the answer itself"*; Fig 13 :134 - *"Unreachable is a verdict here, not a silence,"* |
| Let agreement be corroboration only at low overlap | Fig 8 :47 - *"Independence is one quantity read twice."* |
| Ask users to state their own ground and refuter | Formula "Direction": a frame cannot check the direction of its own question, because the shape it seeks is not a term it holds. |
| Offer the formula as a method to reuse | Fig 4 :22 - *"Only methods cross up. Only head starts come down."* |

## The two-stage offer, stated plainly

1. **Stage 1.** Send the sort rule and its four refuters. Ask what the recipient would count as a failure. Record the verdict before showing any theory.
2. **Stage 2, opt-in only.** Only if they are curious, offer the three figure links. The figures, not the email, are the authority. Their later agreement is never pooled as corroboration (Fig 2 :40).
3. **Stage 3.** Offer the formula as a method to reuse, with the instruction that each user writes their own refuter and states their own mode before running it.
