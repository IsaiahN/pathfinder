## Independent domain census

I redid the census from the residual line alone before using the primary's conclusions, in the WATSON order: R by arity, symmetry and scale; one line per domain with actors, currency and anchor; partition or union; order by dependency.

- **R (arity / symmetry / scale).** Two observations of one quantity, differing beyond quoted uncertainty. Arity: two slots plus their second-order comparison. Symmetry: interchangeable until an anchor is named; an unanchored pair drifts (Fig 2 collapse 1). Scale: first order inside each analysis, second order at the comparison. My first reading was that this is one hard measurement with two reports. Fig 9 :4-5 corrected me: a gap that will not shrink and works on different subsets is a bundle. Union, not a single question.
- **Domain 1, the datasets as frames.** Actors: instruments, analyses, calibration chains, reports. Currency: measurements plus uncertainties. Anchor: the ground, which must not move when either frame is pushed. Question type: union of overlapping conditions.
- **Domain 2, residual sorting.** Actors: prediction, observation, five bins. Currency: support, reach, novelty, settlement. Anchor: the ground. Question type: a sort, not a magnitude.
- **Domain 3, instrument and channel.** Actors: clocks, cables, foregrounds, pipelines. Currency: resolution and contact. Anchor: the ground, which does not decay while the channel does. Question type: reachability.

Order by dependency: anchor, independence, split, per-slot localization, bin assignment, underivable versus unreachable-at-budget, bargain, settlement. The bargain cannot be run before the independence check, because a p-value on one pool twice has no ground meaning.

My census matches the primary's three domains and its union reading. Where the primary derived from Fig 9's split rule and Fig 8's underivable/unreachable distinction, I derived from Fig 2's collapse panels and Fig 3's dependency rule. Different starting figures, same boundaries: low overlap between routes, so the agreement is weak but not circular within the framework. It is still one evidence pool.

## Round trip gap R_T

The Symbols table defines R_T as the gap between what was sent up and what came back down, `gap(x, (T_E ∘ T_A)(x))`. Fig 4 :30 requires it to be measured rather than assumed. I ran the round trip on the condensation this folder actually uses.

1. **Sent up (x):** the verbatim figure set, chiefly Fig 9 :4-5 (bundle, split), Fig 2 :31 and :40 (independence), Fig 5 :11-14 and :53 (five bins), Fig 8 :40-42 (underivable versus unreachable), Fig 10 :24 (channel decay).
2. **Abstraction (T_A):** the shorthand in `07`: check the anchor, check independence, split, keep the residual per slot, assign a bin, separate underivable from unreachable, run the bargain, settle with an independent frame.
3. **Concretisation (T_E):** a real decision about a real anomaly, for example "is the 5 sigma proton-radius discrepancy new physics or an error?"

**R_T is nonzero.** Three things the shorthand holds do not survive the trip back down:

- the **evidence-overlap census**, which the shorthand compresses into "check independence" but which in practice requires enumerating shared calibration, theory inputs, code and parent samples (Fig 2 :31);
- the **identity of the slots**, which the shorthand compresses into "keep the residual per slot" but which changes when a bundle is split (Fig 9 :54-58);
- the **cost comparison**, which the shorthand compresses into "run the bargain" but which requires stating both the new-physics term and the error hypothesis in the same units of cost plus leftover residual (Fig 5 :43).

Conversely, two things survive cleanly: independence before significance, and the underivable/unreachable distinction. Those are the residue worth sending. R_T was **not** measured numerically, because no ground reading exists; it is recorded qualitatively as the enumerated set of dropped constraints. Treating "the formula is the condensed version of the figures" as identity would set R_T to zero and would be the dead-reckoning error Fig 4 :30 names. The honest form is a lossy condensation with a measured gap, which is what `09_watson_answer.md` states.
