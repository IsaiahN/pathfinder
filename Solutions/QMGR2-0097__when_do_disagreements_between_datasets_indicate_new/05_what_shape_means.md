## 1. A shape is a constraint, not a filler

The figures README states the limit: the framework returns *"what a missing thing would have to look like (a shape, not a name)"*. Fig 9 :32: *"you get a kind of thing, never the answer itself"*. Fig 13 :134: *"It cannot say which primitive is missing, because naming it would mean already holding it."*

From *The Library of Babel*, fetched: *"you can cut your search short if the set of books returned is small and descriptive enough that it provides a general shape of the answer that satisfies your question."*

So a **shape** is the narrowed form of an answer you can state before you can name it. It constrains, it does not fill. For this question the shape is the sort rule itself: independence before significance, split before search, five bins, underivable versus unreachable-at-budget, a bargain for a new term, and a ground for settlement. Naming "a miscalibrated clock" or "a new boson" for a real anomaly is a name, not a shape.

## 2. How the shape is obtained

- *Library of Babel*: use the vocabulary already held that is closest to the thing, then look for new terms that overlap or correlate with it. *"With each new term gained, you are able to narrow the space by definition and deepen your vocabulary."*
- Fig 9 :30-31: *"your words are borrowed too, so describe the effect rather than the cause"*. For dataset disagreement, "effect" means: does the gap shrink with effort, does it split by subset, does an independent instrument reproduce it. That description travels to a checker who does not share our vocabulary.
- *Decoding Tacit Knowledge*, fetched: *"When contact isn't possible, the best you can send is a plain description of what you'd expect to see, because anyone can check that against the world."* This is the reason stage 1 of the outreach sends the refuter, not the framework.
- *Library of Babel* on stopping: *"You will know that you are done because the finish line will also be the starting place, as there is nowhere else to search and nothing else that can describe the answer better."*

## 3. The question as a bundle, in the essay's own words

*The Library of Babel*, fetched, gives the exact structure of this question: *"Perhaps your problem was a bundle or conflation of several problems masquerading as one? You will need to search all of those problems individually to find their answers and then combine them in a satisfactory way."*

This is Fig 9 :4-5 in prose. "New physics or underestimated errors" is a bundle masquerading as one question with two answers. The five-bin sort and the split rule are the separation the essay asks for. The shape is what remains after the bundle is separated, not a choice between the two bundled labels.

## 4. What licenses a name

Two things, both outside the shape:

1. **The ground settles it.** Formula step 5: *"the ground settles it. Verified only by what pays."* An independent instrument, a reanalysis from a genuinely different frame, a source-discriminating measurement.
2. **A provenanced import from a frame whose closure differs** (Fig 6 :23, Fig 8 :50-52), with a source gate and a shadow test.

*The Hard Problem is About Source*, fetched, gives the discriminator argument: *"If several paths create the same behavior, then behavior is a projection with lossy information (it cannot define consciousness in full)."* And: *"An instrument that can't distinguish these cases demonstrates its own inadequacy, it cannot claim anything substantial about what is being measured or what causes it."* And: *"The answer is unreached until a better instrument exists."*

This is the metamerism point applied directly. Two datasets can report the same number from different sources (convergence, maximum independence), or different numbers from the same faulty source (one evidence pool). The reading does not determine the source. What is owed is a discriminator, not a vote.

## 5. Q: does the framework name the cause of a disagreement? ANSWERED, no

Nothing in the framework. The framework returns a shape and a route. A particular cause is licensed only by a ground settlement or by a provenanced import with a shadow test. Until one of those, the name is the searcher's own contribution and must be recorded as such.

**My own earlier overstatement, preserved.** I initially wrote that "shape, not a name" forbids any specific attribution. That is too strong. The README's phrase governs a *missing* primitive, one not held in Γ. "Mis-attached", "refuted", "unreachable-at-budget" and "independent frame" are already in the figures, so assigning them to a discrepancy is a binding onto held terms, not the invention of a new one. What remains forbidden is (i) claiming the framework itself names a specific physical cause, and (ii) claiming a binding moves the wall when it adds no atom. Both claims are recorded so neither is quietly lost.

## 6. Two consequences for this question

**Prestige is not action.** From *The Recursive Self-Improvement Trap*, fetched: *"Prestige must never be allowed to validate an action directly."* And: *"Prestige can fund an action. It can give an action a budget to be tried. But it cannot settle an action. Only the ground can settle it."* The famous collaboration's 7σ claim is not more true than the small independent group's 3σ claim. Prestige buys the reanalysis a hearing; it does not decide the reanalysis.

**My audit is a simulation, not a ground reading.** Same essay, fetched: *"LLMs can quickly simulate an approximate answer/output with the formula found in the architecture post."* And: *"the truly testable output would need to be derived from the real architecture or working the formula yourself."* So `01_figure_grounded_audit.md` is a simulated derivation checked for internal consistency against the figures. It is not a test of any real discrepancy and must be labelled as such. It cannot settle anything it reports on.

## 7. Why the refuter must precede the conclusion

*The Recursive Self-Improvement Trap*, fetched: *"the output is only as good as the validator. The agent, or the tool, or the frame, or the theory, cannot validate itself. An external validator, or ground, is always needed to tether the veracity of the output to reality. Anything else is drift."*

A refuter written after the result is a story about the result. A refuter written before the run is a design instrument: it is the only part of the output that an outside frame can use without first accepting the framework. This is why `01` §4 appears before `01` §5 and before `07`.
