## The residual in effect terms

Two datasets report values that differ by more than their quoted uncertainties. Two readings are on offer. Reading A says a term is owed (new physics, a mint, an import). Reading B says what is owed is a refit, a better instrument, or a refusal of a held assumption (underestimated errors). The question asks for the rule that sorts a disagreement into these outcomes. An answer would look like a decision procedure that (i) tells which outcome a disagreement belongs to, (ii) states what would refute that assignment, and (iii) names the ground that could settle it. That procedure is bound here as an ordinary term, `the sort rule`, so it can accrue a residual like anything else (Formula, "Direction").

## What is deliberately not here

- No personal emails of named individuals.
- No ranking of people by how likely they are to believe the sort rule.
- No bulk-send plan.

Three reasons, in the figures' own words:

1. **A room does not add a ground.** *"Alignment is triangulation, not negotiation. A mirror cannot correct you."* (Fig 2 :35) A list of people selected for agreement cannot decide whether a discrepancy is new physics.
2. **Selecting for receptiveness manufactures the disqualifying correlation.** *"agreement about the world is worthless when the agreeing frames share an evidence pool"* (Fig 2 :40) and *"Use filters for the budget and witnesses for the verdict. Never let a filter hand you a verdict."* (Fig 9 :60)
3. **A verifier must not hold the interiors.** *"A verifier that can reconstruct the claim cannot verify it."* (Fig 10 :42) The first contact carries the residual and its refuter, never the framework.

Legally: scraping personal emails for unsolicited bulk outreach breaches GDPR and CAN-SPAM style rules in many jurisdictions. That is a practical failure on top of the structural one.
