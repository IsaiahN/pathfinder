## Restating the question as an effect

A model M fitted and validated in region A has a small residual against ground A. Applied to region B or a later period, the residual grows. What is being decided is whether that growth is a defect of the model (a bad fit, a wrong algorithm), a defect of the ground (the world changed its answer), or a defect of contact: M was a capability of model-and-habitat A, and moving it substitutes a habitat it did not author. The answer shape binds an ordinary term, "a model's skill is a property of the model and its habitat, and transfer is habitat substitution", and then lets that term accrue a residual against independent sources. An answer would look like a falsifiable shape plus an instrument that would close it, not a claim that any particular model failed for any particular reason.

## What is deliberately not here

- No personal emails of named individuals.
- No ranking of people by how likely they are to agree with this derivation.
- No bulk-send plan.

Three reasons, in the figures' own words:

1. **Agreement is not a verdict.** Fig 2 :35: "Alignment is triangulation, not negotiation. A mirror cannot correct you." Selecting checkers for receptiveness manufactures exactly the correlation that disqualifies their agreement.
2. **Shared evidence is one witness.** Fig 2 :40: "agreement about the world is worthless when the agreeing frames share an evidence pool". Two people reading the same review are one frame with two names.
3. **Show the residual, not the interiors.** Fig 10 :52: "Bring a characterised residual, never the source." A checker who has absorbed the framework before giving a verdict has joined the frame being checked.

Legally, scraped personal emails and bulk unsolicited outreach breach GDPR and CAN-SPAM style rules in many jurisdictions. The public opt-in and submission routes in `03_public_contact_points.csv` avoid that failure as well as the structural one.
