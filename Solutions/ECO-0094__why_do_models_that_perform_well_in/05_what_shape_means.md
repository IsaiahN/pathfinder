## 1. A shape is a constraint, not a filler

The figures README states the limit: the framework returns *"what a missing thing would have to look like (a shape, not a name)"*. Fig 9 :32: "you get a kind of thing, never the answer itself." Fig 13 :134: "Unreachable is a verdict here, not a silence" and :135: "It cannot say which primitive is missing, because naming it would mean already holding it."

From *The Library of Babel*: "you can cut your search short if the set of books returned is small and descriptive enough that it provides a general shape of the answer that satisfies your question."

So a **shape** is the narrowed form of an answer you can state before you can name its mechanism. It constrains; it does not fill. For this question the shape is: "transfer failure is habitat substitution with unenumerated contact." The forbidden filler would be: "species X's response to temperature Y caused the failure in region B." That is a mechanism name, and no figure line supplies it.

## 2. How a shape is obtained

- *The Library of Babel*: use the vocabulary already held that is closest to the thing, then recurse. "This is a recursive process. With each new term gained, you narrow the space and deepen your vocabulary." Stopping is marked by a paradox: "the finish line will also be the starting place", and by the time the exact search terms are known, "you have already composed the answer through the process of locating it."
- Fig 6 :30: "The description travels. Fails by being sharpened into terms only we can read. Cast it in effect terms." Fig 9 :30-31: "your words are borrowed too, so describe the effect rather than the cause."
- *Decoding Tacit Knowledge*: "When contact isn't possible, the best you can send is a plain description of what you'd expect to see, because anyone can check that against the world." This is why the outreach states what would be observable (invariant performance under a measured habitat swap), not what the framework believes.
- *How Intelligence Actually Works* gives the stop rule: "The recursion converges toward indiscernibility, and indiscernibility is not identity." A shape can be checked to the limit of the instruments and still not be the thing.

## 3. What licenses a name

Only two things, both outside the shape itself:

1. **The ground settles it.** Formula step 5: "the ground settles it. Verified only by what pays. A gate is not the ground." The figures README: the framework "stops at the limit of what the available instruments can distinguish, and records the lineage so the stop is honest."
2. **A provenanced import from a frame whose closure differs** (Fig 6 :23, Fig 8 :50-52), with a source gate and a shadow test.

*They're Scanning Books* sharpens the second: "The real import is not a new term in the existing space. It is a new dimension of the space." And it records the limit of the six-term vocabulary: "A minimum viable set of six terms is a human's careful guess at reality's compression axes; another sensorium's minimum set need not be those six." Also: "Declaring the primitives does not make them neutral. It makes the choice visible."

So a declared vocabulary (habitat, actors, conditions, relations, gradient over space versus gradient over time) is a visible choice, not nature's own list. Turning that choice into "the" explanation of a specific failure would make an invisible choice out of a visible one.

### 3b. Correction to my own §1 and §3

I initially wrote the "shape, not a name" rule as a blanket ban on naming anything in this question. That is too broad. The README's phrase governs a **missing** primitive, one not held in Γ. Here the relevant terms are already held: habitat substitution is Fig 11, actor valence is Fig 11, gradient over space versus time is Fig 13. Naming them is not inventing a seventh primitive. ## 4. The error the record would commit if it named a mechanism

*The Hard Problem is About Source* gives the mechanism. An instrument reads green and "cannot comment on the source": one green light, or yellow plus blue, or RGB, or CMYK, or a scheme not yet discovered. The reading does not determine the source. "If several paths create the same behavior, then behavior is a projection with lossy information." It was the spectrometer, not an argument, that resolved it: "The answer is unreached until a better instrument exists" and "It was proven wrong not by argument, but by the arrival of a better instrument."

Applied here: a high AUC in region A is a reading, not a source. Many different habitat configurations could produce the same reading. Naming one of them as the cause of failure in B is the metamerism error. What is owed is a disambiguating instrument (blocked validation, external ground, per-site residual), not a name.

*The Recursive Self-Improvement Trap*: "the output is only as good as the validator. The agent, or the tool, or the frame, or the theory, cannot validate itself." A derivation that certifies its own ecological conclusion would be exactly the named failure.

## 5. Question answered

**What licenses a particular mechanism in this question?** Nothing in the figures. The framework returns a shape. A mechanism is licensed only by the ground settling a minted term, or by a provenanced import with a shadow test. Until one of those, the mechanism name is the searcher's own contribution and must be recorded as such. The correct form is: "a structure of contact was not reproduced", with the slot marked, not "species X did it".

**Does this solution commit the error the README forbids?** No, provided it keeps the mechanism slot open. `01_figure_grounded_audit.md` F8 explicitly marks the specific mechanism as `gap`. If a later draft fills that slot from the derivation alone, it commits the error and should be corrected.

## 6. Two consequences for outreach

**Prestige is not action.** From *The Recursive Self-Improvement Trap*: "Prestige can fund an action. It can give an action a budget to be tried. But it cannot settle an action. Only the ground can settle it. The Ground Maintainer must be blind to prestige." A campaign that gathers respected names to agree with the shape claim is the named collapse, not corroboration. The legitimate ask gives the claim a budget to be tried and lets an external dataset or experiment settle it.

**My own audit is a simulation, not a ground reading.** Same essay: "LLMs can quickly simulate an approximate answer/output" and "the truly testable output would need to be derived from the real architecture or working the formula yourself." So `01` is a simulated derivation checked for internal consistency against the figures. It is **not** a test of the ecological claim and must be labelled as such. It cannot settle anything it reports on.
