## QUESTION

Decide, in effect terms, what the Tether says about a model that predicts well in region A but fails in region B or a later period. Option A: the failure is inside the model, so a better algorithm, more data or more tuning in A is the remedy. Option B: the ground changed its answer, so no fixed model could track it. Option C: the failure is in contact, because model performance was a property of model-and-habitat A and transfer substitutes a habitat the modeller did not author. What each leads to: A spends budget on a recording of one occasion; B abandons transfer without identifying what was missing; C sends a checkable shape, refuters written in advance and the instruments that would close the gap.

## DOMAIN CENSUS

R by arity, symmetry and scale, then one line per domain with actors / currency / anchor, partition or union, and order by dependency.

- **R (the residual).** Arity three: model M, source habitat A, target habitat B, so R(M, A -> B). Symmetry: ordered, because A -> B differs from B -> A (Operators table, → sequence). Scale: first order inside a habitat, second order at the transfer membrane (Fig 4). Fig 9 names the failure a union compound, not one hard question: changed habitat, model rigidity and validation design can each hold, and a case in two conditions is not a harder case of either.
- **Domain 1, the fitted-model frame.** Actors: predictors, parameters, functional form, training records. Currency: fitted residual on A. Anchor: ground A, the training and validation measurements. Question type: partition (which compositions cross kinds inside a model).
- **Domain 2, the habitats.** Actors: species, observers, conditions (light, water, temperature, season, terrain), relations (pollination, predation, competition). Currency: ecological outcomes in A and in B. Anchor: ground B, independent observations in the target. Question type: union with D1 where model rigidity and habitat change intersect.
- **Domain 3, the verification frame.** Actors: validation designs, reviewers, journals. Currency: agreement versus residual on B. Anchor: the ground, never agreement. In this simulated run all seats draw on one evidence pool (the same figure text, essays and four world sources), so their agreement is one pool talking, not corroboration.
- **Order by dependency.** Enumerate the target habitat before fitting or tuning (Fig 11 :43); refuse more-search-within-A before importing (Formula step 2); describe the residual before choosing a frame (Fig 9 :27-28); validate against an independent ground (Fig 2 :45); measure the round trip when a method crosses scales (Fig 4 :30).

## VERDICT (from the figures)

The shape survives; the specific mechanism does not. A model's good performance is a property of model and habitat, so transfer substitutes a habitat the modeller did not choose, and the conditions that enabled the source fit are invisible until the target fails. Predictor valence is relative to the residual and does not carry as a fixed good predictor. A fitted coefficient set is a recording of one occasion, not a generator, and validation inside the source shares an evidence pool with fitting. The remedy is enumeration of the target habitat, independent external validation, a measured round-trip gap and a provenanced import with a shadow test. Which predictor or interaction caused a specific failure is `gap`. The verdict is `general` on the shape, `specified` on its ecological binding, ungrounded, candidate.

- **cost if wrong:** bounded and locatable. The packet states mode, refuters and provenance before the conclusion, so an error is findable rather than invisible. The real cost is spending the independence of outside readers by sending a recording instead of a generator, which Fig 2 :42 prices: "Buy independence in proportion to the cost of being wrong." That cost is avoidable by leading with the shape claim and its refuter.
- **undoable:** yes. The three domains' verdicts are separate and can be withdrawn independently. A refuter that fires removes one claim without touching the others, and no primitive has to be discarded. A sent claim can be superseded by a corrected packet.
- **reversed by:** a ground reading, not agreement. A habitat-swap experiment showing invariant performance under a measured substitution would break the habitat verdict; an instrument showing predictor valence invariant across a change of residual would break the valence verdict; independent external validation that agrees with shared-pool validation across three regions would break the verification verdict; a measured zero round-trip gap would break the recording verdict. Until one of these fires, the verdict stands as candidate.
