## 3. Wider literature that bears on the question

The four scholarly sources in section 1 are the ones actually read. Their predicted observations here:

- **Estrada-Peña 2026** predicts that calibration quality will not predict transfer quality, and that the fraction of the realised environmental niche represented during calibration will. This corroborates F1 and F4 (habitat substitution and incomplete enumeration). Quote used: "Models with excellent calibration statistics often showed poor temporal transferability".
- **Roberts et al. 2017** predicts that random cross-validation of structured data will understate transfer error and encourage reliance on non-causal predictors. This corroborates F6 and F7 (per-slot residual and independence). Quote used: "when performing cross-validation, these structures are regularly ignored, resulting in serious underestimation of predictive error".
- **Oreskes et al. 1994** predicts that validation cannot certify a model of an open natural system, only partially confirm it. This corroborates F3 and F5 (recording, not generator; the ground settles, agreement does not). Quote used: "natural systems are never closed and model results are always nonunique".
- **Adel and Wong 2015** predicts that a learner trained on a source distribution needs adaptation to classify a target domain with few or no labels. This is an analogue from a different field, not ecological evidence. It corroborates F1 and F4 in shape only. Quote used: "trained on labeled data from a source domain, that can classify samples from a target domain in which few or no labeled data are available for training".

No source conflicts with the figure verdict. The conflict rule was therefore not triggered. Agreement here is corroboration of shape, not a second witness to a ground reading.

## 4. Distribution: offer, do not push

Figure-grounded reasons:

- **Fig 10 :52** "Bring a characterised residual, never the source." Sending the full framework before anyone has described a residual sends a recording, not a generator.
- **Fig 4 :25** "A recording carried upward looks like knowledge and is a description of one occasion." The claim and its refuter lead; the links follow only if asked for.
- **Fig 2 :40** "agreement about the world is worthless when the agreeing frames share an evidence pool". A list selected for receptiveness produces a convention, not corroboration.
- **Fig 2 :35** "Alignment is triangulation, not negotiation. A mirror cannot correct you." Do not ask for agreement. Ask what would refute the claim.
- **Fig 9 :27-28** "describe it before you go looking, or any frame you pick will seem to fit". Ask what they would expect to see if the claim were true before offering the figures.

**Recommended order in a reply:** (1) the shape claim and its three refuters; (2) ask what they would count as a failure, and record the answer; (3) only then the links, with the note that the figures, not the summary, are the authority.

## 5. The two-stage offer

1. **Stage 1.** Send the claim and the refuter. Ask for a check against their own validation work. Record their verdict before any theory is shown.
2. **Stage 2, opt-in only.** If they are curious, offer the figure text and the method note. Their later agreement is never pooled as corroboration (Fig 2 :40).
3. **Stage 3.** Offer the formula as a method to reuse. Each user writes their own refuter and states their own mode. A frame cannot check the direction of its own question, so the refuter must be committed before the run (Formula, "Direction").

**Hard precondition:** stages 1 to 3 work only if the stage-1 claim survives. If refuter 1, 2 or 3 fires, withdraw the claim and send the correction instead. No material is hosted or sent by this agent. Hosting and sending are yours to do.
