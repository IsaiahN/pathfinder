## VERDICT (from the figures)

The shape survives; the question as posed does not. The figures licence four structural claims: the question is a union and must be unbundled (Fig 9 :55-56), the effect is per slot and an average destroys the signal (Fig 1 :19, :22), the anchor is external measurement that does not move with a model (Fig 2 :29-30), and agreement within a shared evidence pool cannot decide a channel verdict (Fig 2 :40, Fig 7 :35). They also licence one structural analogy: the four absorption channels are isomers over one cost atom, with "cost absorption" as the functional group (Fig 12 :117, :124). They do not licence a verdict on the sign or size of the employment effect, on which channel dominates at a given bite, or on whether the change is "meaningful" under an undeclared threshold. Those two fields are `gap` after a genuine census, and the gap is owed to matched data, not to another figure line. Mode: `general` on the shape, `specified` on the channel predictions, not `grounded`. This is a candidate, not a settled result.

- **cost if wrong:** bounded and locatable. Because the packet states its mode, names five refuters and records the lineage, an error is findable rather than invisible. The real cost is spending the independence of outside readers by sending a single-number claim instead of a generator. Fig 2 :42 prices that: "Buy independence in proportion to the cost of being wrong." The practical cost of acting on a wrong single number is borne by the slots whose signal was averaged away: teens, low-skill workers and high-bite regions are named in the sources as the groups where effects concentrate.
- **undoable:** yes. The union framing, the per-slot requirement and the channel list can be added to any existing analysis without withdrawing a primitive or discarding data. A published single-number claim can be superseded by a per-slot, per-channel table with its own anchors. A sent claim can be corrected by the same move.
- **reversed by:** a ground reading, not agreement. Matched firm records that separately report price, margin, separation and vacancy responses to one wage change would name the channel and settle C3. A demonstration that per-slot residuals are indistinguishable would refute C2. A declared threshold for "meaningful" with a measurement would close D4. A partition proof for the four channels would refute C1. Until one of these fires, the verdict stands as `candidate`.
