## Round trip gap R_T

The Symbols table defines R_T as `gap(x, (T_E ∘ T_A)(x))`, the gap between what was sent up and what came back down. Fig 4 :30 requires it to be measured rather than assumed. I ran the round trip on the condensation this folder actually uses.

2. **Abstraction (T_A):** the shorthand carried in `07_the_tether_answer.md`, namely "union question, per-slot effect, external anchor, four isomer channels, independence decides".
3. **Concretisation (T_E):** a concrete policy claim of the form "raise the floor by X and employment changes by Y" or "firms will absorb it through prices".

**R_T is nonzero.** Four things the shorthand holds do not survive the trip back down:

- the **buyer-as-operand scope condition** for the price channel (Fig 12 isomer reading is scoped to the cost atom, not the cast of actors);
- the **provenance and shadow-test requirement** for any channel naming (Fig 6 :23, Fig 8 :50-52), which the concrete claim would skip;
- the **undeclared threshold** for "meaningful" (Fig 3 :27), which the concrete claim silently supplies;
- the **contact cascade** (Fig 11 :52), which a direct wage-to-hiring estimate drops.

Conversely, one thing survives cleanly: the union framing and the requirement to report per slot. That is the residue worth sending. R_T was **not** measured numerically, because no ground reading exists; it is recorded qualitatively as the enumerated set of dropped constraints. Treating "the formula is the condensed version of the metalaws" as identity would set R_T to zero and would be the dead-reckoning error Fig 4 :30 names.
