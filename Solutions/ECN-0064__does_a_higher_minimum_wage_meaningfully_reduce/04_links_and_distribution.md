## What the literature bears on this question

Two findings matter and they are reported as secondary corroboration, never as the verdict.

1. **The disagreement is partly about the literature itself.** Neumark and Shirley (2021) quote three mutually incompatible one-line summaries of the same body of work. By Fig 2 :40, *"disqualifying for a verdict: agreement about the world is worthless when the agreeing frames share an evidence pool"*, those summaries are one pool. This corroborates the independence finding F4.
2. **Slot-heterogeneity is reported by both sides.** Neumark and Wascher (2006) report stronger disemployment for the least skilled; the Low Pay Commission (2026) reports that youth employment *"fell more in areas with the lowest youth coverage."* Different signs, same structural point: the effect is per slot. This corroborates F2 without deciding the direction.

The essays all share an author and an evidence pool with the figures. Their agreement with the figure verdict is expected and is one witness, not several. The world sources, by contrast, are genuinely different authors, institutions, countries and data systems, though the two Neumark led reviews overlap heavily with each other and are marked as such in `10_source_ledger.csv`.

## Distribution: offer, do not push

Figure-grounded reasons:

- **Fig 10 :52** - *"Bring a characterised residual, never the source."* Sending the full architecture before a recipient has described a residual sends a recording, not a generator.
- **Fig 10 :42** - *"A verifier that can reconstruct the claim cannot verify it."* A recipient who has absorbed the framework before being asked for a verdict has joined the frame being checked.
- **Fig 9 :27-28** - *"describe it before you go looking, or any frame you pick will seem to fit"* - ask which margin returns a readable signal before giving them the article.
- **Fig 2 :35** - *"Alignment is triangulation, not negotiation. A mirror cannot correct you."* A send selected for people already likely to agree produces a mirror.
- **Fig 9 :60** - *"Use filters for the budget and witnesses for the verdict. Never let a filter hand you a verdict."* Prestige and citation counts are filters for what to read first.

**Recommended order in a reply:** (1) the shape claim and its five refuters; (2) the question of what the recipient would count as a failure, and which margin their data reads first; (3) record the verdict; (4) only then the local links, with the note that the figures, not the summary, are the authority.

## The two-stage offer

1. **Stage 1, before any theory.** Send the residual, the shape claim, and the refuters. Ask for the recipient's failure criterion and their first readable margin. Record the answer before showing anything else. This is Fig 10 :52 and Fig 2 :45, *"Frames range against the ground. They do not negotiate with it."*
2. **Stage 2, opt-in only.** If they are curious, offer the three local links. Their later agreement is never pooled as corroboration (Fig 2 :40).
3. **Stage 3, method reuse.** Offer the formula as a generator. Each user must write their own refuter and state their own mode. Fig 4 :22: *"Only methods cross up. Only head starts come down."*

**Hard precondition:** stages 1 to 3 only work if the stage-1 claim survives. It is a shape claim and a refusal of the single-number framing, not a claim about the sign of the employment effect. If a checker shows that one aggregate number is sufficient, the claim is withdrawn before any theory is shown.
