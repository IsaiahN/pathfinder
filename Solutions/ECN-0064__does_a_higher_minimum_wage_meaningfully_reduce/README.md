## The residual, in effect terms

A binding increase in the wage floor injects a cost into a labor market. The question asks which slot pays for it: new hires (the extensive margin), incumbent margins (profit), consumers (price), or separation costs (turnover). The residual `R` is the gap between predicted and observed outcomes **per slot**, where a slot is a firm, a worker group, a region, and a margin (Fig 1 :19). It is not one national employment number.

Stated as a bindable shape before the run: *an answer to this question must name the slot that absorbs the cost, state the margin on which the effect registers, and identify the anchor that would settle it.* That shape is an ordinary term in this derivation and is refutable in `01_figure_grounded_audit.md` section 3.

## What is deliberately not here

- No personal emails of named individuals. The Low Pay Commission regional-visits page publishes named staff addresses; they are deliberately excluded. Only the institutional enquiries address and the public consultation route are listed.
- No ranking of people by how likely they are to agree with this derivation.
- No bulk-send plan.

Three figure-grounded reasons:

1. **Agreement is not an anchor.** Fig 2 :35, *"Alignment is triangulation, not negotiation. A mirror cannot correct you."* Fig 7 :35, *"A room's agreement is variation, not a verdict. Selection between rooms is where the ground votes."* A list of people selected for receptiveness is a room, not a ground.
2. **Correlation disqualifies a verdict.** Fig 2 :40, *"disqualifying for a verdict: agreement about the world is worthless when the agreeing frames share an evidence pool."* This is the live failure mode in this field: Neumark and Shirley document that researchers do not even agree on what the literature says (source in `10_source_ledger.csv`).
3. **A verifier must not hold the interiors.** Fig 10 :42, *"A verifier that can reconstruct the claim cannot verify it."* The first contact carries the residual and its refuter, not the framework.

Legally: unsolicited bulk email to scraped personal addresses breaches GDPR and CAN-SPAM style rules in many jurisdictions. Public opt-in channels avoid that failure as well as the structural one.

## Status of this derivation

This is a **simulated derivation checked for internal consistency against the figures, not a ground reading**. *The Recursive Self-Improvement Trap* (read 2026-11-23): *"LLMs can quickly simulate an approximate answer/output with the formula found in the architecture post. It will often be better than the generic outputs that you will usually get otherwise too. However, the truly testable output would need to be derived from the real architecture or working the formula yourself."* This package cannot settle the question it reports on. That limit is stated on every substantive file.
