## Wider literature that bears on the question

1. **Provenance is the decisive observable.** The *Hidden Figures* historical-accuracy discussion is a case where a popular account replaced the record. This is the same distinction the figures draw at the seam: convergent derivation and adopted import look identical in their contents, and only the record separates them (Fig 8 :50-52). When a name is recovered, ask what record carries it.
2. **Exclusion changes the channel, not the ground.** The West Area Computers case shows people doing the same work under segregated conditions, and a computer attending meetings uninvited to get information withheld from her. That is a channel being narrowed and an actor working around it, not a change in what the ground would pay.
3. **The institution's blindness is structural.** Scott's legibility argument and the slot-indexing rule in Fig 1 both predict that aggregation and standardization erase local, per-slot signals. The loss is not a lack of goodwill.
4. **Not all of the knowledge is codifiable.** Polanyi's *"we can know more than we can tell"* and the tacit-knowledge literature predict that a recording cannot carry everything: a method and a lineage are needed. This is Fig 4 :25 in a different vocabulary.
5. **The periphery creates.** Von Hippel's lead users and the ethnomathematics field both describe knowledge produced outside the recognized center, which the center later adopts, often without a record of origin.

The eight encyclopedia syntheses above share a platform and an editorial ecosystem, so their agreement is not eight independent witnesses. They cover different fields and cite different primary sources, and they are used only as corroboration of a figure-derived shape. The figures decide.

## Distribution: offer, do not push

- **Fig 10 :52** - *"Bring a characterised residual, never the source."* Lead with the two effects and the refuter. Send the theory only after a verdict on stage 1 is recorded.
- **Fig 9 :27-28** - *"describe it before you go looking, or any frame you pick will seem to fit"* Ask what they would expect to see if the shape claim were true before naming any historical case.
- **Fig 2 :35** - *"Alignment is triangulation, not negotiation. A mirror cannot correct you."* Do not ask for agreement, and do not select recipients for likely agreement.
- **Fig 2 :40** - agreement from a shared evidence pool is disqualifying for a verdict. A mail merged to people who already hold the same view manufactures that pool.
- **Fig 4 :22** - *"Only methods cross up. Only head starts come down."* Stage 3 sends the formula as a method, not an answer.

**Order in a reply:** (1) the shape claim and its refuter; (2) ask what they would count as a failure, and record the answer; (3) only then the three public links, with the note that the figures, not the summary, are the authority.

## The two-stage offer

1. **Stage 1.** Send the residual in effect terms and the refuter. Ask what would refute it. Record their verdict before showing any theory. This is opt-in by construction, because Seat 4 must not hold the interiors.
2. **Stage 2, opt-in only.** If they are curious, offer the figure links. Their later agreement is never pooled as corroboration (Fig 2 :40).
3. **Stage 3.** Offer the formula as a reusable method, with the instruction that each user write their own refuter and state their own mode before running it. A frame cannot check the direction of its own question.

**Hard precondition:** the stages work only if the stage-1 output survives. F7 of `01_figure_grounded_audit.md` records two strained readings that were re-censused; keep the creation/preservation distinction intact when writing stage 1.

## Hosting

Nothing is sent by this agent. The public venues in `03_public_contact_points.csv` are the routes. If a packet is hosted, prefer a page that lets a recipient decline contact over an attachment pushed to a list. An opt-in download page is closer to the figure's rule than an unsolicited bulk send, and it avoids the GDPR/CAN-SPAM style failure that a scraped personal-address list would create.
