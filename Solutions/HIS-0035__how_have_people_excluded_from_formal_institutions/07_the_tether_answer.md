# The Tether's answer on excluded knowledge

**Mode: `specified` on the shape, `general` on the structural rules, not `grounded`.** This is a shape and a route, not a historical result. Simulated derivation checked against the figures (`05_what_shape_means.md` section 6), not a ground reading.

## 1. The residual it answers

An institution's library Γ_inst cannot predict effects that an excluded population's library Γ_ex predicts, because the two frames hold different atoms from different contact with the ground. Two effects need explaining: **creation** (a new term appears in Γ_ex that is not derivable inside Γ_inst) and **preservation** (the term persists across exclusion and time while the institution's own record of it decays). They are a **union compound**, not one hard problem (Fig 9 :57-58): both conditions bind, so neither lever alone clears the case.

## 2. The shape

1. **A closed frame cannot manufacture what it lacks.** Fig 6 :21: *"MINT composes inside the closure and can never add an atom."* Fig 6 :23: *"No frame escapes its own closure. The bound moves only on contact with a frame whose closure differs."* So creation has the shape of an **import** from a low-overlap frame, and the excluded population is that frame (Fig 6 :10: *"nature · corpus · peer frame"*). It is not a derivation from inside, and it is not mere inattention: noticing is not crossing.
2. **Provenance is the only observable that separates creation from borrowing.** Fig 8 :50-52: *"Convergent derivation and adopted import look identical in the contents. Only the record of where each came from separates them, and the surplus lives only where the closures differ."* Without a record, a recovery story cannot tell an independent rediscovery from an adopted import. The residual must be described before a witness is chosen (Fig 9 :27-28), or any witness will seem to fit.
3. **Institutional agreement is not an anchor.** Fig 7 :13: *"same closure, handed along; agreement compounds, evidence does not."* Fig 1 :22: *"Aggregating across slots is how a live signal disappears into an average."* A room whose members read the same reports produces a convention, not a verdict. The per-slot signal the excluded knower holds is exactly what aggregation destroys.
4. **Preservation is agent-and-habitat, not a property of a lone person.** Fig 11 :39: *"capability is a property of agent-and-habitat, never of the agent alone."* Fig 11 :36: *"Where nothing maintains anything, intelligence is a label applied afterwards to a lineage that met its ground. Nothing was intelligent; selection ran. The word does no work there."* What persists is the lineage and the habitat, and the "great mind" framing is a label applied afterwards.
5. **The channel decays; the ground does not.** Fig 10 :24: *"the ground does not decay, the channel does."* Exclusion substitutes a habitat (Fig 11 :66: *"Isolation is not removal of the habitat. It is substitution of one habitat for another."*) and narrows the channel. What crosses the membrane is a **method**, not a recording: Fig 4 :22, *"Only methods cross up. Only head starts come down."* Fig 4 :25: *"A recording carried upward looks like knowledge and is a description of one occasion."*
6. **The two crossings are isomers.** Over the atoms {knower, ground, term}, creation is a frame crossing (import, priced in provenance) and preservation is a scale crossing (method, priced in a round-trip gap). Same atoms, different bond, different substance (Fig 12 :117: *"same atoms, different bond, different substance"*). A recovered name cannot stand in for a transmitted method.
7. **Absence of a record is not absence of knowledge.** Fig 8 :39: *"So a search that finds nothing is never a proof of absence."* The figure gives the shape of what must have happened; it cannot inventory the instances. The inventory is world work and is always incomplete.

## 3. What it explicitly does not say

- Not a roll call of excluded heroes. The framework returns a shape, not a name, and does not certify historical instances. Named cases in `09_watson_answer.md` are world witnesses.
- Not a claim that the institution is a fixed villain. Valence is relative to the residual (Fig 11 :50: *"The same actor is a benefactor to one residual and a malfactor to another"*). What matters is whether contact is maintained.
- Not a claim that any particular person was "rediscovered." A recovered name is a recording of one occasion, not a transmitted method.
- Not a claim that absence from an archive proves absence from the world (F6).
- Not `grounded`. No measurement here settles a historical case.

## 4. What would settle it - the ground

A **source-discriminating record**, not a further argument. From *The Hard Problem is About Source*, verified this session: *"An instrument that can't distinguish these cases demonstrates its own inadequacy."* Here the corresponding questions are:

- **Provenance test:** does a record survive that distinguishes convergent derivation from adopted import for the term in question? If not, attribution stays undecided.
- **Transmission test:** can a practitioner reproduce the result from what crossed, or only cite the occasion? This separates the method from the recording.
- **Channel test:** did the change alter contact with the ground, or only the composition of the room? A change that does not change contact changes nothing (Fig 11 :40).
- Standing falsifiers: derivation of the knowledge inside Γ_inst with no differing frame; agent-only preservation that moved the ground; provenance unnecessary because contents distinguish the cases; institutional agreement that moves when the ground is pushed.

## 5. The honest stop

**Unreached, not unreachable.** The archive is not a sealed room (Fig 9 :44: *"Only a sealed room can be searched to the end, and no frame is a sealed room"*), so a search of it cannot close the question. The answer stops at the limit of what the instruments can distinguish and records the lineage so the stop is checkable. *"Indiscernibility is not identity."* Prestige is not an instrument: it can fund an inquiry but cannot settle it.

## 6. One-paragraph version for outreach

> The Tether does not say which excluded people were right or list the ones history forgot. It says that a closed institution cannot manufacture a term it lacks, so knowledge held outside it enters as an import whose provenance is the only thing that separates creation from borrowing; that the institution's own agreement is a convention, not an anchor, and its aggregation erases the very signal the outsider holds; and that preservation is not a lone person's genius but a lineage keeping contact with a ground that does not decay while the channel does, with methods rather than recordings crossing between generations. Creation and preservation are two bonds over the same atoms, so a recovered name is not a transmitted method. What the framework offers is the shape any answer must have, the refuters written in advance, and the kind of record that would close it. The inventory stays open until the ground settles it.
