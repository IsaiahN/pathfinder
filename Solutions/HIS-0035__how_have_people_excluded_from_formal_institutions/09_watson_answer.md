## QUESTION

Decide, in effect terms, what the Tether says about the question. R is the gap between an institution's library Γ_inst and an excluded population's library Γ_ex: the latter predicts effects the former cannot derive, because the two frames hold different atoms from different contact with the ground. Two effects must be explained: creation (a new term that is not in closure(Γ_inst)) and preservation (the term persists through exclusion and time while the institution's record of it decays). Option A: treat these as one story about rediscovered individuals and let institutional consensus certify it. Option B: keep them as a union compound with two different bonds, and require provenance and transmission for any claim. A spends the independence of the people checking it and mistakes agreement for the ground; B returns a checkable shape, written refuters, and an open inventory until a record settles it.

## DOMAIN CENSUS

R by arity, symmetry and scale, then one line per domain with actors, currency and anchor; partition or union; order by dependency.

- **R (the residual).** Arity: three terms relate a knower, an institution and a ground, so operationally a seam between two frames both ranging against one anchor. Symmetry: the frames are not interchangeable in seat power, but the ground does not update for either, so the asymmetry is in the seat, not in whose prediction settles. Scale: step (an act of knowing), lineage (transmission), frame (a discipline's closure) and habitat (the field of contact). Creation lives at step and frame, preservation at lineage and habitat. Fig 9 names a union compound: a case in two conditions is not a harder case of either.
- **Domain 1, the institutional frame.** Actors: credentialed members, gatekeepers, canons, peer review, Γ_inst. Currency: legitimacy, citation, credentials. Anchor: its own consensus is not an anchor because it updates; only the external ground is. Question type: a frame tending to closure.
- **Domain 2, the excluded knowers' frame.** Actors: practitioners, artisans, indigenous and local knowledge holders, human computers, citizen scientists. Currency: predictions that pay against the ground, practical competence. Anchor: the ground, which does not move when the institution disagrees. Question type: external source for an import.
- **Domain 3, the seam.** Actors: boundary people, translations, attribution decisions. Currency: imports with provenance. Anchor: the ground decides whether an import pays. Union compound, its own loop (the politics of recognition).
- **Domain 4, the habitat and lineage.** Actors: apprentices, oral tradition, artifacts, texts, the substituted habitat. Currency: maintained contact, methods up, head starts down. Anchor: the ground, which does not decay; the channel does. Scale crossing.
- **Order by dependency (Fig 3).** Ground contact before vocabulary; vocabulary before an objective; the objective before the import; the import before the lineage. The middle link, saying what would count as progress, is the one that usually breaks because it has no instrument.
- **Partition or union.** D1 and D2 are two frames; their overlap D3 is a union compound, not a partition. D4 cuts across both as a scale membrane. No verdict is averaged across domains.

## VERDICT (from the figures)

The shape survives; a historical narrative does not come from the figures. A closed institutional frame cannot manufacture the atom it lacks, so creation has the shape of a provenanced import from a differing frame, never a derivation from inside and never mere inattention. Institutional agreement is a convention, not an anchor, and aggregation destroys the per-slot signal the excluded knower holds. Preservation has the shape of maintained contact with a non-decaying ground through a lineage and a habitat, with methods rather than recordings crossing scales. Creation and preservation are two bonds over the same atoms, so a recovered name is not a transmitted method, and absence from a record is not absence from the world. This is a `specified`, ungrounded result. The figures name no person, date or institution.

- **cost if wrong:** bounded and locatable. The claim is mode-labelled, states refuters before the conclusion and records the lineage, so an error is findable rather than invisible. The real cost is spending the independence of outside readers by sending a narrative instead of a generator, which Fig 2 :42 prices: "Buy independence in proportion to the cost of being wrong." That cost is avoidable by leading with the shape and its refuter. A second cost is misattribution: without provenance, an adopted import is credited as an independent derivation, and the record is corrupted in a way that looks like agreement.
- **undoable:** yes. The two strained readings were narrowed without discarding the derivation; no primitive was withdrawn. Any named case can be superseded when a better provenance record appears. A record that distinguishes derivation from import simply changes the attribution, not the shape.
- **reversed by:** a ground reading, not agreement. A provenance record that distinguishes convergent derivation from adopted import would settle attribution; a transmission test showing a practitioner can reproduce the result from what crossed would settle the method claim; an agent-only improvement that moved the ground would break the capability claim; a derivation of the knowledge inside the institution's library with no differing frame would break the import claim; an anchor that moved when a frame pushed on it would break the ground rule. Until one of these fires, the verdict stands as `candidate`.
