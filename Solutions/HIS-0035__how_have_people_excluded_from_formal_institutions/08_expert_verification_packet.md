# Expert verification packet - the Tether on excluded knowledge

**What this is:** a shape claim in a form a historian, ethnobiologist, archivist or STS scholar can check. Mode, derivation and refuters are stated before the conclusion, so a reader knows what to look for and what would settle it.

**Mode: `specified` on the shape, `general` on the structural rules, not `grounded`.** No historical case is asserted as settled by the figures. This is a simulated derivation checked for internal consistency against the figures, not a ground reading.

## 1. The claim, in one paragraph

A closed institutional frame cannot manufacture a term it does not hold, so knowledge created outside it enters as an import whose provenance is the only observable that separates creation from borrowing. The institution's agreement is a convention rather than an anchor, and its aggregation erases the per-slot signal the excluded knower holds. Preservation is not an isolated person's trait: it is a lineage keeping contact with a ground that does not decay while the channel does, with reapplicable methods rather than recordings crossing the membrane. Creation (a frame crossing) and preservation (a scale crossing) are two bonds over the same atoms, so a recovered name is not a transmitted method. The framework offers the shape any answer must have, refuters written in advance, and the kind of record that would close it. It does not name a person or a date.

## 2. Three checkable sub-claims

### C1 - framework-internal; checkable by reading Figures 6 and 8
- **Statement:** MINT cannot add an atom; a new atom crosses into a frame only from a frame whose closure differs. At the seam, convergent derivation and adopted import are identical in content, so provenance is decisive.
- **Source, verbatim:** Fig 6 :21 *"MINT composes inside the closure and can never add an atom."* Fig 6 :23 *"No frame escapes its own closure. The bound moves only on contact with a frame whose closure differs."* Fig 8 :50-52 *"Convergent derivation and adopted import look identical in the contents. Only the record of where each came from separates them, and the surplus lives only where the closures differ."*
- **Refuter:** show the knowledge was derivable inside the institution's library from atoms it held, with no differing frame contacted; or show a case where contents alone distinguish derivation from import with no record.

### C2 - checkable by a historian or archivist against the record
- **Statement:** institutional agreement is not an anchor and aggregation loses the live per-slot signal. A room whose members share one evidence pool produces a convention, not a verdict about the world.
- **Source, verbatim:** Fig 7 :13 *"same closure, handed along; agreement compounds, evidence does not"*; Fig 1 :22 *"Aggregating across slots is how a live signal disappears into an average."*; Fig 2 :35 *"Alignment is triangulation, not negotiation. A mirror cannot correct you."*
- **What to check:** find a case where the institution's consensus changed only when the ground was pushed (a measurement, an instrument, an outcome), not when another member agreed. If consensus moves with agreement alone, C2 fails. Check whether the per-slot detail held by an outsider was erased by aggregation.
- **Refuter:** a ground whose answer changes when a frame pushes on it, which would make it another frame rather than an anchor.

### C3 - checkable against transmission and Fig 4 / Fig 11
- **Statement:** preservation is agent-and-habitat; the ground does not decay, the channel does; what crosses scales is a reapplicable method, not the recording of one occasion. Creation and preservation are therefore different bonds, not one event.
- **Source, verbatim:** Fig 11 :39 *"capability is a property of agent-and-habitat, never of the agent alone"*; Fig 10 :24 *"the ground does not decay, the channel does"*; Fig 4 :22 *"Only methods cross up. Only head starts come down."*; Fig 4 :25 *"A recording carried upward looks like knowledge and is a description of one occasion."*
- **What to check:** can a practitioner reproduce the result from what was transmitted, or only cite the occasion? Did the change alter contact with the ground, or only the membership of the room?
- **Refuter:** an agent-only improvement that moved the ground, or a recording that lets a new practitioner reproduce the method without the lineage.

## 3. What is explicitly not claimed

- Not a list of excluded people who were right, and not a correction to any particular historical narrative.
- Not that any institution is a fixed villain. Valence is relative to the residual (Fig 11 :50: *"The same actor is a benefactor to one residual and a malfactor to another"*).
- Not that absence from an archive proves absence from the world (Fig 8 :39: *"So a search that finds nothing is never a proof of absence."*).
- Not that a recovered name measures preserved knowledge. A name is a recording of one occasion.
- Not `grounded`. Nothing here is asserted as settled by experiment or by the archive.
