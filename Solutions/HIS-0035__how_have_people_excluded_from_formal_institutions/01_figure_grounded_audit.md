## 0. The question under audit, stated in effect terms

The source asks how people excluded from formal institutions created and preserved knowledge. Stated as a residual rather than a topic:

> **R:** The institution's library Γ_inst cannot predict certain effects that an excluded population's library Γ_ex predicts, because Γ_ex holds different atoms acquired through different contact with the ground. Two effects need explaining, and they sit at different scales. **(a) Creation:** new terms appear in Γ_ex that are not derivable inside closure(Γ_inst). **(b) Preservation:** those terms persist across time and across the exclusion, while the institution's own record of them decays, is lost, or is attributed elsewhere.

Bind the sought-for shape as an **ordinary term** before the run, per the "Direction" discipline in `THE_FORMULA.md` (a frame cannot check the direction of its own question, so write down what an answer would look like and let it accrue a residual):

> **Sought-for shape (binding, mode `specified`):** An answer will have the form that excluded knowers are a frame whose closure differs from the institution's; their knowledge crosses into the institution only as an import, a new atom with provenance and a shadow test, never as a derivation from inside; and its preservation is the maintenance of contact with a non-decaying ground through a lineage and a habitat, with methods carried up and head starts sent down, while the institution's own channel decays and its internal agreement compounds without adding evidence.

If this shape is wrong, the refuters in section 3 must fire. They are written before the findings.

## 1. DOMAIN CENSUS (written before any figure line was chosen)

### 1a. R by arity, symmetry, scale (Fig 9 :22)

- **Arity:** three terms relate here, a knower, an institution, and a ground. Operationally the residual is a **seam between two frames** (Γ_inst and Γ_ex) that both range against one anchor. It is not a one-frame question.
- **Symmetry:** the two frames are **not** interchangeable in seat power. The institution can credential, publish, and admit or deny membership; the excluded knower generally cannot. But the **ground does not update for either** (Fig 2 :45), so the asymmetry is in the seat, not in whose prediction settles. The two effects (creation and preservation) are also not symmetric: one is a frame crossing, one is a scale crossing.
- **Scale:** step (an act of knowing), lineage (transmission across generations), frame (a discipline's closure), habitat (the field of contact). "Created" lives at step and cycle; "preserved" lives at lineage. Conflating them is the first error to avoid.

### 1b. One line per domain: actors / currency / anchor

A domain without a nameable anchor is not a domain yet. Each line below names its anchor.

| domain | actors | currency | anchor | question type |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| **D1 The institutional frame** | credentialed members, gatekeepers, canons, peer review, Γ_inst | legitimacy, citation, publication slots, credentials | its consensus is *not* an anchor, it updates (Fig 2 :33); only the external ground is | a frame tending to closure |
| **D2 The excluded knowers' frame** | practitioners, artisans, indigenous and local knowledge holders, "human computers", citizen scientists | predictions that pay against the ground, practical competence | the ground: material, harvest, patient outcome, instrument reading; it does not move when the institution disagrees | external source for an import |
| **D3 The seam (Γ_inst ∩ Γ_ex)** | boundary people, translations, carry operations, attribution decisions | imports (atoms with provenance) | the ground decides whether the import pays | **union compound**, its own loop |
| **D4 The habitat and lineage** | apprentices, oral tradition, artifacts, texts, the substituted habitat of the excluded community | maintained contact; methods carried up, head starts down | the ground, which does not decay; the channel does (Fig 10 :24) | scale crossing (membrane) |

### 1c. Partition or union?

D1 and D2 are two frames. Their overlap D3 is **a union compound, not a partition** (Fig 9 :54-58: *"a case in two conditions is not a harder case of either"*). It has its own loop: the politics of recognition, attribution, and benefit sharing. D4 cuts across both as a **scale axis**, so it is a membrane, not a fourth sibling frame. The census is therefore a union plus a crossing, and no verdict may be averaged across the four lines.

### 1d. Order by dependency (Fig 3)

1. Contact with the ground must exist before any knowledge (perception, link 1).
2. A vocabulary for what the situation affords (link 2, "what the search can reach").
3. An objective, what would count as progress (link 3). This is the link that usually breaks: Fig 3 :25-26 says the middle link *"has neither, so it attracts the least attention and the fewest instruments."* An institution with no instrument for excluded knowledge cannot see it even when it is present, and Fig 3 :27 adds: *"A link with no instrument is not a link that works. It is a link nobody has looked at."*
4. The import crossing the seam (D3), gated by provenance and shadow test.
5. The lineage that preserves contact (D4).

Upstream first. Effort spent on recognition (step 4) cannot rescue a broken instrument at step 3 (Fig 3 :19: *"Optimising a link that already works cannot rescue one that does not."*).

## 2. FINDINGS

### F1 - Creation is an import, not a derivation (decisive for "created")

Fig 6 :21: **"MINT composes inside the closure and can never add an atom."**
Fig 6 :22: **"NOVEL means novel relative to atoms, not to the world. IMPORT adds an atom, and the added atom is what expands the closure."**
Fig 6 :23: **"No frame escapes its own closure. The bound moves only on contact with a frame whose closure differs."**
Fig 6 :10: **"nature · corpus · peer frame"**

If Γ_inst lacks the atom, no amount of search inside Γ_inst produces it. Fig 8 :32: **"Closure is idempotent. Composing inside a closed set never leaves it, so no search, however long, adds an atom."** The excluded population is the *"peer frame"* whose closure differs; its practical knowledge is the external source. This is why "they were there all along and the institution simply failed to notice" is not a complete answer: noticing requires the atom to cross, and crossing is an import with a cost and a provenance.

- **Verdict D2/D3:** `triangulated` (Fig 6 closure/import, Fig 8 idempotence and union surplus, Fig 11 habitat enumeration; low overlap between a closure argument, a two-frame argument, and a contact argument).
- **Refuter:** show the excluded knowledge was fully derivable inside Γ_inst from atoms it already held, with no contact with a differing frame and no import. Then this finding fails.

### F2 - The institution's agreement cannot substitute for the ground (decisive for "formal institutions")

Fig 7 :13: **"same closure, handed along; agreement compounds, evidence does not"**
Fig 2 :35: **"Alignment is triangulation, not negotiation. A mirror cannot correct you."**
Fig 1 :22: **"Aggregating across slots is how a live signal disappears into an average."**
Fig 2 :40: **"disqualifying for a verdict: agreement about the world is worthless when the agreeing frames share an evidence pool"**

Formal institutions are rooms whose members read the same reports. Their agreement is efficient for a convention (Fig 2 :41) and disqualifying for a verdict about the world. The live signal the excluded knower holds is *indexed by slot* (Fig 1 :19: *"The residual is indexed by object slot, not aggregated."*), and institutional aggregation is exactly how it disappears. So exclusion is not only a moral fact about admission; it is an **epistemic** fact about which residuals can register at all.

- **Verdict D1:** `triangulated` (Fig 7 room chain, Fig 2 ground/anchor, Fig 1 slot indexing).
- **Refuter:** show that institutional consensus changed its answer when the ground was pushed (Fig 2 :29-30). If the anchor moves, it was never an anchor.

### F3 - Preservation is agent-and-habitat, not a property of the isolated person (decisive for "preserved")

Fig 11 :39: **"capability is a property of agent-and-habitat, never of the agent alone"**
Fig 11 :40: **"A system cannot be made more capable by improving the agent in isolation. An improvement that does not change contact changes nothing, however much it improves."**
Fig 11 :36: **"Where nothing maintains anything, intelligence is a label applied afterwards to a lineage that met its ground. Nothing was intelligent; selection ran. The word does no work there."**
Fig 11 :43: **"The habitat is enumerated, never composed."**

Preservation is not "great minds remembered." It is the lineage that kept contact with the ground: apprentices, practice, artifacts, oral transmission, the community that kept the habitat in place. Fig 11 :36 is the sharpest line in the whole audit. A label applied afterwards to a lineage that met its ground is not an explanation of the knowledge; the lineage and the ground are.

- **Verdict D4:** `triangulated` (Fig 11 capability/enumeration, Fig 10 channel decay, Fig 4 membrane).
- **Refuter:** an agent-only improvement that moved the ground would refute the capability claim. Fig 11 :71-72 names this as the standing open test.

### F4 - The channel decays; the ground does not

Fig 10 :24: **"the ground does not decay, the channel does"**
Fig 11 :66: **"Isolation is not removal of the habitat. It is substitution of one habitat for another."**
Fig 4 :22: **"Only methods cross up. Only head starts come down."**
Fig 4 :25: **"A recording carried upward looks like knowledge and is a description of one occasion."**

Excluded people are not outside contact; they are in a **substituted habitat**. What is lost when they are cut off is the channel, not the ground. This splits preservation into two honest parts. A *recording* (a text, a name in a citation, a medal) can be carried and can decay without the knowledge surviving. A *method* that can be reapplied is what crosses (Fig 4 :23: *"upward: a method that can be reapplied. Never the recording of a particular success."*). So a list of famous recovered names is a poor measure of preserved knowledge; the method and the lineage are the measure.

- **Verdict D4:** `triangulated` (Fig 10 ground maintainer, Fig 11 isolation law, Fig 4 membrane).
- **Refuter:** show a recording carried across the boundary that lets a new practitioner reproduce the result without the lineage. Then the method-only rule is wrong.

### F5 - Provenance is the whole issue at the seam

Fig 8 :50-52: **"Convergent derivation and adopted import look identical in the contents. Only the record of where each came from separates them, and the surplus lives only where the closures differ."**
Fig 9 :27-28: **"describe it before you go looking, or any frame you pick will seem to fit"**
Fig 11 :61: **"Introduction is available; exclusion is not."**

Attribution is not a side issue; it is the observable that separates creation from borrowing. If a term enters Γ_inst with no record of its origin, we cannot tell convergent derivation from adopted import, so we cannot say who created what. The remedy is not a better memory but a **record** (Fig 10 :62: *"A ruling carries provenance like an atom."*). And the residual must be described before the frame is chosen, or any frame, including a flattering one, will seem to fit.

- **Verdict D3:** `triangulated` (Fig 8 provenance, Fig 9 describe-first, Fig 10 provenance-as-atom).
- **Refuter:** a case where convergent derivation and adopted import are distinguishable from contents alone, with no record. Then provenance is not decisive.

### F6 - Absence of a record is not absence of knowledge

Fig 8 :39: **"So a search that finds nothing is never a proof of absence."**
Fig 9 :44: **"Only a sealed room can be searched to the end, and no frame is a sealed room."**
Operators table :58: **"a refutation is a negation, which is why a vocabulary without it can only accumulate"**

An archive that contains no trace of excluded knowledge proves only that the archive was searched. A vocabulary that cannot refuse can only accumulate its own canon, so the absence is structured, not accidental. This is the limit clause of the answer: the figures narrow the shape of what must have happened (an import across a differing closure, a lineage maintaining contact) but they cannot inventory the instances. The inventory is world work, and it is always incomplete.

- **Verdict:** `general` (limit), triangulated (Fig 8 metaframe bounds, Fig 9 sealed-room rule, Operators negation).
- **Refuter:** a sealed archive, searched to completion, that contains every relevant term. Then absence would be provable. No such archive is available here.

### F7 - What "exclusion" can and cannot mean (strain test and re-census)

I first tried to use Fig 11 :61 (*"Introduction is available; exclusion is not"*) to say that formal institutions cannot exclude people. **That is a strain** and I re-censused. The figure governs what a seat can do to a **habitat**: you can add an actor, you cannot subtract the habitat, because contact is a fact about the world. It does not govern membership in a room. The corrected use is narrower and stronger: an institution can deny credentials and admission, but it cannot subtract the ground contact of the excluded knower. Exclusion changes the channel, never the ground. The strain is recorded, not smoothed away.

A second re-census: Fig 4 (*"Only methods cross up"*) governs the **membrane between sizes**, one agent and a population, not a crossing between two frames. Frame crossings are Fig 6 and Fig 8. So Fig 4 is used only for preservation across generations (D4), and never as the mechanism of creation (D2/D3). Two different quantities, no contradiction.

- **Verdict after re-census:** no live strain. The two strained readings were sent back to the census and resolved by narrowing the quantity each line governs.

## 3. PRE-REGISTERED REFUTER (written before the findings were concluded)

Required by the seat rule that a falsifier written before the run is a design instrument. If any of the following fires, the shape claim is withdrawn or narrowed, and the retraction is recorded here visibly.

1. **Import refuter.** Excluded knowledge is fully derivable inside the institution's closure from atoms it already held, no differing frame contacted and no import made (breaks Fig 6 :21-23).
2. **Capability refuter.** Preservation is a property of the isolated knower, demonstrated by an agent-only change that moved the ground and changed contact (breaks Fig 11 :40).
3. **Lookup refuter.** A residual described in arity, symmetry and scale fails to select a low-overlap external frame, or any frame selected after the fact fits equally well (breaks Fig 9 :27-28).
4. **Anchor refuter.** Institutional agreement changes the answer when the ground is pushed, so the "ground" was another frame (breaks Fig 2 :33-34).
5. **Record refuter.** A case shows convergent derivation and adopted import distinguished from contents alone with no provenance (breaks Fig 8 :50-52).

Until one is run, no part of this may be labelled `grounded`. The verdict stays `candidate`.

## 4. Recommendation

1. Answer at the level of **shape**, not a roll call of heroes. The framework returns a kind of thing, never the answer itself (Fig 9 :32: *"you get a kind of thing, never the answer itself"*). Name people only as world witnesses, with provenance.
2. Keep the two verbs separate. "Created" is a frame crossing and needs an import argument. "Preserved" is a scale crossing and needs a lineage and habitat argument. They are not one mechanism.
3. Describe the residual before choosing a field of witnesses (Fig 9 :27-28). Otherwise any field seems to fit and the search becomes apophenia.
4. Use public, opt-in venues in `03_public_contact_points.csv` and ask for a refutation of the shape, not agreement with a narrative (Fig 2 :35).
5. Measure **methods and lineage**, not names in a record (Fig 4 :22-25). A recovered name is a recording; a reapplicable method is what crosses.
6. Record provenance for every attributed term (Fig 8 :50-52). Without it, creation and borrowing are indistinguishable.

### Updated verdict

The shape survives and is `specified`, not `grounded`. A closed institutional frame cannot manufacture the atoms it lacks; those atoms arrive only through contact with a differing frame, as imports whose provenance is the only thing that separates creation from borrowing. Preservation is maintained contact with a ground that does not decay, carried by a lineage and a habitat, with methods rather than recordings crossing the membrane. Institutional agreement is a convention, not an anchor, and aggregation destroys the per-slot signal. The figures do not name a person, a date, or a victory. Strains after re-census: two were found, both resolved by narrowing the quantity the line governs; none are hidden.
