## DERIVATION

### Q1 - One question or two? UNION, not a list

- Fig 9 :54: **"And when the split lands on a union rather than a partition, unbundle before you search again."**
- Fig 9 :57-58: **"a case in two conditions is not a harder case of either. It stops being a mixture and becomes a specific compound."**
- "Created" requires a differing frame and an import. "Preserved" requires a lineage and a habitat. Both conditions bind in the historical cases, so the conjunction is a union compound with its own loop: the politics of attribution. A single lever that clears one condition (for example, admitting a person to the room) leaves the other binding (the method still cannot cross).
- **Verdict:** `triangulated` (Fig 9 union rule plus Fig 6 import rule plus Fig 11 habitat rule). The compound is a third domain, not an average of the first two.
- **Falsifier:** a case where creation and preservation are demonstrably the same mechanism, so that solving one solves the other with no residue. Then the union reading fails and a single loop suffices.

### Q2 - What is the order of dependence?

- Fig 3 :20: **"The chain is ordered by dependency, not by importance. Each link consumes what the one above produces."**
- Fig 3 :19: **"Optimising a link that already works cannot rescue one that does not."**
- Ordered chain for this question: ground contact (perception) → vocabulary for what the knowledge affords → an objective that says what would count as progress → the import across the seam → the lineage that preserves it.
- The break is usually at link 3. Fig 3 :25-26: **"The link between them, having terms for what a situation offers and being able to say what would count as progress, has neither, so it attracts the least attention and the fewest instruments."** Fig 3 :27: **"A link with no instrument is not a link that works. It is a link nobody has looked at."** An institution can be excellent at links 1 and 2 inside its own domain and still fail to see excluded knowledge, because it has no instrument for what would count as progress there. Recognition campaigns aimed at link 4 cannot repair a missing link 3.
- **Verdict:** `general` on the ordering rule; `specified` on the claim that link 3 is the usual break. The "usual" is a figure claim about where attention goes, not a measured frequency.

### Q3 - Are the two crossings isomers under Fig 12?

- Fig 12 :117: **"isomer. Same atoms, different bond, different substance. The difference is invisible in the formula and decisive in the behaviour."**
- The two crossings share the atom set {knower, ground, term}. They differ in the bond:
  - Creation is a **frame crossing**: `Γ_ex --IMPORT--> Γ_inst`. The operator is the import of Fig 6 :23, and the price is provenance plus a shadow test.
  - Preservation is a **scale crossing**: `method --T_A--> head start --T_E--> next practitioner`, with a nonzero round-trip gap R_T (Fig 4 :30).
- Same atoms, different bond, different substance: by Fig 12 :117 they are **isomers**, not variants of one event. This is why a recovered name (the artefact of a frame crossing) cannot stand in for a transmitted method (the artefact of a scale crossing). The notation cannot tell them apart; the bond does.
- **Falsifier, written before the conclusion:** if one crossing can be written without an atom the other requires (for example, an import with no ground contact, or a method with no frame on the receiving side), the isomer claim fails and the weaker **functional-group** reading stands instead: Fig 12 :123, **"functional group. A substructure whose presence fixes the outcome. The group says what kind; the qualifier says which member."** The group here is `knower --bond--> ground --bond--> term`.

### Q4 - What survives use: the catalyst test

- Fig 12 :119: **"catalyst. A part that survives its own use. Spent and regenerating parts behave differently across uses, and nothing records which a term is."**
- A recording is a spent part: once cited on one occasion it is a description of that occasion (Fig 4 :25). A reapplicable method is a catalyst: it survives its own use and regenerates in the next practitioner. Fig 12 :119 says nothing records which a term is, so this distinction must be read off the lineage, not off the artefact. This is the structural reason the audit measures methods rather than names.
- **Verdict:** `single-source` for the catalyst analogy (Fig 12), triangulated with Fig 4 (method over recording). The low overlap between a bond taxonomy and a scale-membrane rule makes the agreement worth something, but both are framework-internal.

### Q5 - Which living-system analogue governs?

- The living-system test (seat reasoning move 3) maps relations, not looks. The right analogue is the lineage, not the lone genius. Fig 11 :42: **"The lift move is Cayley's, 1799: separating the forces from the flapping is what made powered flight a problem anyone could work on."** Separating the knowledge from the person who held it is the same kind of move: it turns preservation into a problem anyone can work on.
- Fig 11 :50: **"The same actor is a benefactor to one residual and a malfactor to another, so mutualism and parasitism are relations rather than kinds."** An institution is not a fixed villain; its valence is relative to the residual. The same credential that opens a room can sever a channel. So the answer is a relation (contact maintained or not), not a character judgement about a type of person or a type of institution.
- **Verdict:** `general`, triangulated (Fig 11 habitat/valency plus Fig 2 anchor rule). No claim is made that a specific historical institution behaved in a specific way; that is world work.

## CONCLUSION

"Created and preserved" is a union compound, not a list. Its dependency chain usually breaks at the objective link, where no instrument exists for what would count as progress in excluded knowledge. The two crossings are Fig 12 isomers over the same three atoms with different bonds: a frame crossing (import, priced in provenance) and a scale crossing (method, priced in a round-trip gap). The survivor across uses is the catalyst-like method, not the spent recording. This framing rescues what is checkable in the question without turning it into a roll call of heroes or a claim that the figures name a person.
