## 1. A shape is a constraint, not a filler

The canonical README states the limit: the framework returns *"what a missing thing would have to look like (a shape, not a name)."* Fig 9 :32: *"you get a kind of thing, never the answer itself."*

From *The Library of Babel*, verified this session: *"you can cut your search short if the set of books returned is small and descriptive enough that it provides a general shape of the answer that satisfies your question."*

For HIS-0035 the **shape** is therefore not a list of excluded people. It is the constraint any such list must satisfy: creation is a provenanced import across a boundary between frames, and preservation is maintained ground contact through a lineage and a habitat, with methods rather than recordings crossing the membrane. A name (Dorothy Vaughan, a Kayapo classification system, a particular ethnomathematical practice) fills the constraint; it does not define it.

## 2. How the shape is obtained

- *Library of Babel*, verified: use the vocabulary already held that is *closest* to the thing, then *"With each new term gained, you narrow the space and deepen your vocabulary."* This is why the census came before the witness search: without the vocabulary of closure, import, anchor, slot and habitat, every excluded group looks equally relevant.
- Fig 6 :30: *"The description travels. Fails by being sharpened into terms only we can read. Cast it in effect terms."* Fig 9 :30-31: *"your words are borrowed too, so describe the effect rather than the cause."*
- *Decoding Tacit Knowledge* (quote as recorded in the exemplar, 2026-11-22): *"When contact isn't possible, the best you can send is a plain description of what you'd expect to see, because anyone can check that against the world."* This is why stage 1 of the outreach is the residual and the refuter, not a narrative.
- *Library of Babel* on stopping, verified: *"the finish line will also be the starting place"* and *"composed the answer through the process of locating it."* The shape and the route to it co-emerge; this is not a licence to skip the residual.

## 3. What licenses a name in this question

Only two things, both outside the shape itself:

1. **The ground settles it.** Formula step 5: *"the ground settles it. Verified only by what pays."* In history, the ground is the instrument, the harvest, the experiment, the patient outcome, not the archive's own coherence.
2. **A provenanced import from a frame whose closure differs** (Fig 6 :23, Fig 8 :50-52), with a source gate and a shadow test.

*The Hard Problem is About Source*, verified: *"If several paths create the same behavior, then behavior is a projection with lossy information"* and *"An instrument that can't distinguish these cases demonstrates its own inadequacy."* Applied here: a record that names a source without discriminating among sources is doing metamerism. Attribution from an archive reading alone is not enough; the provenance has to survive as a record. The essay's own resolution, *"The answer is unreached until a better instrument exists,"* matches the audit's honest stop: the figures give the shape; an instrument would name the case.

*They're Scanning Books* (quote as recorded in the exemplar, 2026-11-22): *"The real import is not a new term in the existing space. It is a new dimension of the space."* And: *"Declaring the primitives does not make them neutral. It makes the choice visible."* Formal institutions declare their primitives (what counts as a credential, a publication, a canon); making that choice visible is the first step, not the answer.

## 4. Why the popular "great recoveries" narrative is the specific forbidden error

*The Recursive Self-Improvement Trap*, verified this session: *"You may all agree and be wrong."* Agreement about a recovered name is a convention when it draws on one evidence pool, and Fig 2 :40 disqualifies it for a verdict.

The same essay supplies the sharper point for preservation: *"The blank slate was a half-truth. ## 5. The two questions this question is really two of

The question uses two verbs, "created" and "preserved." The essays and the figures agree that they are different mechanisms:

- **Created** is a frame crossing. The vocabulary comes from closure and import (Fig 6), the surplus from low overlap (Fig 8), and the proof of origin from provenance (Fig 10). A frame that cannot refuse can only accumulate (Operators table :58), so an institution's canon is not self-correcting.
- **Preserved** is a scale crossing and a lineage. Fig 4 :22: *"Only methods cross up. Only head starts come down."* Fig 11 :36: *"Where nothing maintains anything, intelligence is a label applied afterwards to a lineage that met its ground."*Human society keeps knowledge the way a child does: as a lineage, not as a blank slate.

Treating them as one mechanism is the semantic error that makes "rediscovery" stories feel complete. They are not. The "shape, not a name" rule does **not** forbid naming a person here; it forbids treating the name as the explanation. The named case is a witness with provenance, nothing more.

## 6. Two consequences for this solution

**Prestige is not action.** *The Recursive Self-Improvement Trap*, verified: *"Prestige can fund an action. It can give an action a budget to be tried. But it cannot settle an action. Only the ground can settle it."* A campaign that gathers famous names to endorse the shape is the named collapse. The legitimate ask is a budget to try the shape against a ground, and a refutation if it fails.

**My own audit is a simulation, not a ground reading.** Same essay, verified: *"LLMs can quickly simulate an approximate answer/output with the formula found in the architecture post."* And: *"the truly testable output would need to be derived from the real architecture or working the formula yourself."* So `01_figure_grounded_audit.md` is a simulated derivation checked for internal consistency against the figures. It is **not** a test of the historical claim and must be labelled as such. It cannot settle anything it reports on.
