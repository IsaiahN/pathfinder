# The Tether's answer on interest rates, inflation and employment

**Mode: `specified` on the structural verdict, `general` on the indexing and union rules, `gap` on any numeric coefficient. Not `grounded`.** This is a shape and a route, not a result. It is a simulated derivation checked against the figures (`05_what_shape_means.md` section 6), not a ground reading.

## 1. The residual it answers

R_s,t = |Γ_s,t(b,a) - o'_s,t| for a = a policy-rate change and o' = observed inflation and employment, indexed by economy slot s and date t. The question asks whether this residual stays small and stable as the cast changes. Fig 1 :19: *"The residual is indexed by object slot, not aggregated."*

## 2. The shape

1. **The two options are a union, not a partition.** "Reliably control" and "effects vary substantially" are overlapping conditions. Fig 9 :57: *"a case in two conditions is not a harder case of either."* Unbundle into a maintained channel (C1), a matching habitat (C2), and terms for the middle transmission link (C3). Each has its own loop and its own ground.

2. **The loop is invariant; the cast is not.** Fig 4 :20: *"Only the actors, the currency and the anchor change."* What is stable across economies and epochs is the loop of perceive, plan, act, predict, re-estimate. What changes is the banking structure, the exchange-rate regime, the credibility of the target, and the instruments measuring outcomes.

3. **Reliability is a property of policy-plus-habitat, not of the rate change.** Fig 11 :39: *"capability is a property of agent-and-habitat, never of the agent alone."* Fig 11 :40: *"A system cannot be made more capable by improving the agent in isolation. An improvement that does not change contact changes nothing, however much it improves."*

4. **The same action changes valence with the regime and the residual.** Fig 11 :49: *"An actor's valence is relative to the residual, not a property it carries."* Under a credible floating inflation target a rate rise appreciates the currency and dampens demand and import prices. Under a fixed exchange rate the interest rate cannot be used to manage aggregate demand at all (CORE Econ 7.2). The variation across economies is therefore structural, not noise around a constant.

5. **Apparent time-variation is often a channel failure.** Fig 10 :24: *"the ground does not decay, the channel does"* and :26 *"saturated metrics, pooled readings"*. Before calling a drift structural, audit instruments, definitions, coverage, and policy implementation. Boivin, Kiley and Mishkin (2010) find the effect more muted in recent decades; that is evidence of change, not a verdict on its source. The D1/D2 split keeps policy-behaviour change and habitat change separate without flipping either.

6. **The middle link of the chain has no direct instrument.** Fig 3 :27: *"A link with no instrument is not a link that works. It is a link nobody has looked at."* The link between measuring inflation and choosing a rate, namely what a rate change affords in this habitat, is usually inferred from the outcomes it is supposed to explain.

7. **A transported model carries a measurable round-trip gap.** Fig 4 :30: *"So take the round trip and measure the gap. Without it you are navigating by dead reckoning: confident about a position nobody checked."* Applying one economy's coefficient to another is a lossy transform; `R_T` must be reported, not assumed zero.

8. **A pooled coefficient is the wrong witness.** Fig 1 :22: *"Aggregating across slots is how a live signal disappears into an average."* Fig 9 :60: *"Use filters for the budget and witnesses for the verdict. Never let a filter hand you a verdict."* A variance decomposition or a cross-country panel average ranks effort; it does not decide the question.

9. **The number is a declared gap.** Fig 9 :32: *"you get a kind of thing, never the answer itself"* and Fig 13 :134: *"Unreachable is a verdict here, not a silence"*. No figure line yields a coefficient for a named economy on a named date. What closes it is a source-discriminating instrument, not more agreement.

## 3. What it explicitly does not say

- Not "rates never work." Central banks demonstrably affect inflation; the ECB's own transmission account and decades of research establish that. The claim is that reliability is conditional, not that influence is absent.
- Not "every economy is unique and nothing can be learned." The loop is shared and the functional group is shared; the arrangement and contact differ. Isomers over a shared atom set are checkable (`06_isomers_and_order.md`).
- Not that the natural rate is an anchor. X1 has no nameable anchor in this census and remains a term inside Γ with its own residual.
- Not that the global financial cycle is the sole cause. It is a condition in D2, not a substitute for the outcome anchor.
- Not a new macro primitive. Fig 13 :115: *"The set is closed. The arrangements are not. That gap is why import exists."*
- Not consensus. Fig 2 :35: *"Alignment is triangulation, not negotiation. A mirror cannot correct you."*
- Not `grounded`. No measurement in this package settles a per-slot coefficient.

## 4. Falsifiers (pre-registered in `01_figure_grounded_audit.md` section 4)

1. Invariant sign and comparable magnitude of the response across fixed, flexible-credible and flexible-noncredible regimes with other habitat conditions controlled.
2. A pooled estimate with no remaining per-slot residual after stratification.
3. A time shift with the measurement channel demonstrably unchanged and the ground itself moved.
4. One lever clearing both variation residues with no categorically different remainder.
5. A per-slot, per-date coefficient settled by a source-discriminating instrument and reported with provenance.

Any one firing narrows or withdraws the conditional verdict. The refuters were written before the derivation was run.

## 5. What would settle it

A source-discriminating instrument, not a further argument. *The Hard Problem is About Source*: an instrument that reads green *"cannot comment on the source"*; the spectrometer resolved it. Here the corresponding questions are:

- Does the measured inflation response come from the credit channel, the exchange-rate channel, expectation de-anchoring, or a combination that reads the same on the aggregate series? An instrument that separates these would name the source.
- Is the channel still in contact with the ground after a change in policy implementation or financial structure? Fig 10 :28: *"you have lost touch"*.
- What is `R_T` for a model transported from economy A to economy B, measured against B's ground?
- Does the middle link have an instrument independent of the outcomes it explains?

## 6. The honest stop

**Unreached, not unreachable.** The figure set yields the shape: per-slot indexing, habitat-relative valence, channel maintenance, union unbundling, and a measured round-trip gap. It does not yield the coefficient. Fig 8 :39: *"So a search that finds nothing is never a proof of absence."* The stop is recorded with its lineage so it is checkable. Indiscernibility is not identity.

## 7. One-paragraph version for outreach

> The Tether does not choose between "interest rates reliably control inflation and employment" and "effects vary", because those are overlapping conditions, not two answers to one question. The loop is the same in every economy, but what changes is the cast: the actors, the currency and the anchor. Reliability is therefore a property of the policy action plus its habitat, not of the rate change itself. The same hike can dampen demand under a credible floating inflation target and be unusable as a demand tool under a fixed exchange rate; what looks like time-varying effectiveness is often a measurement channel that stopped resolving, not an economy that changed. The residual has to be read per economy and per date, a pooled coefficient throws away the signal, and a model transported between economies carries a round-trip gap that must be measured. What this offers is the shape any answer must have, five refuters written in advance, and the instruments that would close it. The coefficient for a named economy on a named date stays open until the ground settles it.
