## Two things in the world sources that bear directly on this question

1. **A scope difference that looks like a conflict.** The ECB explainer says the transmission mechanism has *"long, variable and uncertain time lags"* for the economy and price level. Karau's Bundesbank brief reports that exchange rates respond with no delay. These are different quantities: the exchange rate is an asset price, while prices and activity are slower. The figures do not take a side. Recorded as a scope difference in `01_figure_grounded_audit.md` F8, not smoothed into a single claim.
2. **An attribution tension that was returned to census.** Boivin, Kiley and Mishkin find the effect has become more muted but attribute it to changed policy behaviour and expectations rather than private-sector change. That does not fit a habitat-only story. Re-census separated D1 (the controller and its Γ) from D2 (the habitat). Both change the cast named by Fig 4 :20 (*"Only the actors, the currency and the anchor change."*), so both are inside the figure verdict and neither is given the verdict on its own.

## Distribution: offer, do not push

Figure-grounded reasons:

- **Fig 4 :22**: *"Only methods cross up. Only head starts come down."* Sending the full framework before anyone has described a residual sends a recording, not a generator. Lead with the residual, the shape claim, and the refuter. Offer links after.
- **Fig 10 :42**: *"A verifier that can reconstruct the claim cannot verify it."* A recipient who has absorbed the whole framework before being asked for a verdict has joined the frame being checked.
- **Fig 9 :27**: *"describe it before you go looking, or"* any frame you pick will seem to fit. Ask what the recipient would expect to see if the conditional shape were true before giving them the paper.
- **Fig 2 :35**: *"Alignment is triangulation, not negotiation. A mirror cannot correct you."* A list selected for likely agreement produces a convention, not corroboration.
- **Fig 2 :42**: *"Buy independence in proportion to the cost of being wrong."* Public opt-in venues buy independence; personalised mass email does the opposite and also breaches unsolicited-outreach rules in many jurisdictions.

**Recommended order in a reply:** (1) the residual, the shape claim, and the five refuters; (2) ask what the recipient would count as a failure and record the answer; (3) only then the three local framework files, with the note that the figures, not the summary, are the authority; (4) offer the formula as a reusable method with the instruction that each user writes their own refuter and states their own mode.

## The two-stage offer

- **Stage 1 before stage 2.** Fig 10 :52: *"Bring a characterised residual, never the source."* Fig 2 :45: *"Frames range against the ground. They do not negotiate with it."* The recipient checks the output without holding the framework's interior.
- **Stage 2 is opt-in by construction.** Once a recipient has absorbed the theory, their agreement stops being evidence. The stage-1 verdict must be recorded before any theory is shown, and later agreement is never pooled as corroboration (Fig 2 :40: *"agreement about the world is worthless when the agreeing frames share an evidence pool"*).
- **Stage 3 sends a method, which is permitted.** Fig 4 :22: *"Only methods cross up. Only head starts come down."* The formula is a generator, so giving it away does not give away an answer.
- **Stage 3 still needs a ground per problem.** Formula "Direction": a frame *"cannot check the direction of its own question, because the shape a frame is searching for is not a term it holds."* Each user must write down what would refute their result before running it and label the mode of what they get.
