# Expert verification packet: the Tether on monetary transmission

**What this is:** a claim in a form a monetary economist can check. Mode, derivation and refuters are stated before the conclusion, so a reader knows what to look for and what would settle it.

**Mode: `specified` on the structural verdict, `general` on indexing and union, `gap` on any numeric coefficient. Not `grounded`.** No per-economy, per-date coefficient is asserted.

**Status:** a simulated derivation checked for internal consistency against the figures, not a ground reading. It cannot settle the question it reports on.

## 1. The claim, in one paragraph

The two options in the question are a union of overlapping conditions, not a partition. The central bank's loop is the same in every economy, but the cast changes: the actors, the currency and the anchor. Reliability is a property of the policy action plus its transmission habitat, so the same rate change changes valence with the exchange-rate regime, the credibility of the target and the state of the credit channel. What appears as time-varying effectiveness is often a measurement channel that stopped resolving, not a changed ground. The residual must be read per economy and per date, a pooled estimate throws away the signal, and a model transported between economies carries a round-trip gap that must be measured. The framework returns this shape and five pre-registered refuters. It does not return a coefficient for a named economy on a named date; that is a declared gap.

## 2. Three checkable sub-claims

### C1, framework-internal and regime-facing; checkable by reading Fig 11 and a monetary-regime textbook

- **Statement:** capability is a property of policy plus habitat; the same action changes valence with the regime. Under a fixed exchange rate the interest rate cannot be used to manage aggregate demand.
- **Source, verbatim:** Fig 11 :39 *"So the answerable question is not what intelligence is, but what makes a system capable. And capability is a property of agent-and-habitat, never of the agent alone."* Fig 11 :49 *"An actor's valence is relative to the residual, not a property it carries."* Fig 11 :50 *"The same actor is a benefactor to one residual and a malfactor to another, so mutualism and parasitism are relations rather than kinds."*
- **World check:** CORE Econ, *The Economy* 2.0, section 7.2 (opened 2026-11-23): *"fixing, or targeting a given value of the exchange rate means that the interest rate cannot be used to manage aggregate demand in the economy."*
- **Refuter:** invariant sign and comparable magnitude of the inflation and employment response to the same rate change across fixed, flexible-credible and flexible-noncredible regimes, with other habitat conditions explicitly controlled.

### C2, per-slot indexing; checkable against any cross-country panel study

- **Statement:** the action-to-outcome residual is indexed per economy and per date. A pooled average is a filter for effort, not a verdict about any slot.
- **Source, verbatim:** Fig 1 :19 *"The residual is indexed by object slot, not aggregated."* Fig 1 :22 *"Aggregating across slots is how a live signal disappears into an average."* Fig 9 :60 *"Use filters for the budget and witnesses for the verdict. Never let a filter hand you a verdict."*
- **World check:** Yoldas, FEDS Note, Federal Reserve Board, 2024: *"domestic factors led to notable variation in the timing and extent of monetary policy responses"*; the note also reports large significant intercept terms and low explanatory power for emerging-market currencies. Low-overlap analogue: Dahabreh, Hayward and Kent, *International Journal of Epidemiology* 45(6), 2016: *"determining the best treatment for an individual (the task of a doctor) is fundamentally different from determining the average effect of treatment in a population (the purpose of a trial)."*
- **Refuter:** a pooled estimate that, after regime stratification, predicts each economy's response with no remaining per-slot residual against the ground.

### C3, the channel and the round trip; checkable against measurement practice

- **Statement:** a time shift in the measured effect may be a channel failure rather than a changed ground, and a model transported between economies carries a measurable round-trip gap.
- **Source, verbatim:** Fig 10 :24 *"the ground does not decay, the channel does"*; Fig 10 :26 *"saturated metrics, pooled readings"*; Fig 4 :30 *"So take the round trip and measure the gap. Without it you are navigating by dead reckoning: confident about a position nobody checked."*
- **World check:** ECB, *Transmission mechanism of monetary policy* (institutional page, opened 2026-11-23): *"The transmission mechanism is characterised by long, variable and uncertain time lags. Thus it is difficult to predict the precise effect of monetary policy actions on the economy and price level."* Boivin, Kiley and Mishkin, NBER WP 15879, 2010: *"Monetary policy innovations have a more muted effect on real activity and inflation in recent decades as compared to the effects before 1980."*
- **Refuter:** a documented time shift with the measurement channel demonstrably unchanged (same instruments, definitions and coverage) and the ground itself moved; or a transported model with `R_T` measured at zero against the receiving economy's ground.

## 3. What is explicitly not claimed

- Not "rates never work." Influence is established; reliability is the conditional claim.
- Not that every economy is unique and incomparable. Two mechanisms over a shared atom set are a real isomer claim with a stated falsifier (`06_isomers_and_order.md`).
- Not that the natural rate is an anchor. It has no nameable anchor in this census and remains a term inside Γ.
- Not that the global financial cycle is the sole cause. It is a condition in D2.
- Not a new macro primitive, not a unified transmission theory, and not a single universal multiplier.
- Not consensus. Agreement among economists is a convention, not the ground (Fig 2 :35).
- Not `grounded`. No measurement in this packet settles a coefficient.

## 5. What would settle it

A source-discriminating instrument, not more agreement. *The Hard Problem is About Source*: an instrument that reads green *"cannot comment on the source"*; the spectrometer resolved it. Here:

1. Does the measured inflation response come from credit, the exchange rate, expectation de-anchoring, or a combination that reads the same on the aggregate series?
2. Is the measurement channel still in contact with the ground after a change in policy implementation or financial structure?
3. What is `R_T` for a model transported from economy A to economy B, measured against B's ground?
4. Does the middle transmission link have an instrument independent of the outcomes it explains?

## 6. What an expert can check in twenty minutes

1. Read Fig 1 :19, :22; Fig 11 :39, :49, :50; Fig 10 :24; Fig 4 :30. Six short passages in the local figure text.
2. Check C1 against a monetary-regime textbook: is the fixed-rate statement right, and does the valence claim survive the flexible-credible case?
3. Name one instrument that would separate credit-driven from exchange-rate-driven pass-through in an aggregate inflation series, or say why none exists.
4. Send the refutation. A failure is more useful than an endorsement.
