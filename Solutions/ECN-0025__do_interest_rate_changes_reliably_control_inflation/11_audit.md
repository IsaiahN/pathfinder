## Round trip gap R_T

1. **Sent up (x):** the verbatim figure set governing per-slot indexing, cast change, habitat-relative valence, channel maintenance, union unbundling, and the closed-but-unarranged set.
2. **Abstraction (T_A):** the shorthand carried in `01` and `07`: reliability is conditional, not invariant; the residual is per slot; the channel must be maintained; the question is a union; transport carries a measurable gap.

**R_T is nonzero.** Three things the shorthand holds do not survive the trip back down:

One thing survives cleanly: a structure of conditions must be checked per slot before a lever is chosen, and the transmission chain is ordered with a comparison at its end. `R_T` was **not** measured numerically, because no ground reading exists in this package.
