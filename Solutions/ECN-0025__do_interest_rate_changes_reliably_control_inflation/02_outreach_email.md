# Outreach draft: check one output before you decide about the framework

**Use:** one-to-one, or as a cover letter attached to a submission through the public venues in `03_public_contact_points.csv`. **Not** for bulk send.

**Hard precondition:** the verification packet in `08_expert_verification_packet.md` must be intact (mode, three checkable sub-claims, verbatim figure lines, refuters, provenance). If a sub-claim cannot survive a twenty-minute check, do not send.

**Subject:** One checkable claim about monetary transmission, before any theory

> Hi [name],
>
> I have one specific output I would like you to check against what you already know. I am not asking you to read a framework first, and I am not asking you to agree with anything. If the output does not survive, there is nothing else to look at.
>
> **The residual (`specified`, not `grounded`).** Write the central bank's problem per economy and per date: R_s,t = |Γ_s,t(b,a) - o'_s,t|, where a is a change in the policy rate and o' is observed inflation and employment. The question is not whether a pooled fit is good. It is whether that per-slot residual stays small and stable as the cast changes across economies and across time. Fig 1 :19: "The residual is indexed by object slot, not aggregated." Fig 1 :22: "Aggregating across slots is how a live signal disappears into an average."
>
> **The output to check, stated before any theory was shown.** The two options in the question are a false partition. The loop is the same everywhere, but what changes is the cast: "Only the actors, the currency and the anchor change" (Fig 4 :20). Capability is a property of the policy action plus its habitat, not of the rate change alone (Fig 11 :39-40). The same action changes valence with the regime and the residual: "The same actor is a benefactor to one residual and a malfactor to another" (Fig 11 :50). So the effect of a rate change is conditional on the transmission habitat and on whether the measurement channel is still in contact with the ground (Fig 10 :24: "the ground does not decay, the channel does"). The time-variation and the cross-economy variation are a union of overlapping conditions, not one hard question that a single lever will clear (Fig 9 :55).
>
> **What this does not claim.** It does not claim that rates never work, that central banks have no influence on inflation, or that every economy is different in an unmeasurable way. It does not give a number for "one point of tightening lowers inflation by x and raises unemployment by y" in any named economy at any named date. That quantity is a declared gap in the package, not a hidden answer. The framework returns a kind of thing, not the thing itself: "you get a kind of thing, never the answer itself" (Fig 9 :32).
>
> **What would refute it (written down before the run).**
> 1. The same rate change yields invariant sign and comparable magnitude across fixed, flexible-credible, and flexible-noncredible regimes with other habitat conditions controlled.
> 2. A pooled estimate predicts each economy's response with no remaining per-slot residual after stratification.
> 3. A documented time shift is observed with the measurement channel demonstrably unchanged and the ground itself moved.
> 4. One intervention clears both the time-variation residue and the cross-economy residue with no categorically different remainder.
> 5. A per-slot, per-date coefficient is settled by a source-discriminating instrument and reported with provenance.
>
> If any one holds, I will narrow or retract the claim. A one-line "this fails because..." is more useful to me than agreement. If you would rather not engage, no reply is needed.
>
> **If it survives, and only then:** the sources are below. The figures are the authority, not my summary of them.
> - Verbatim figure text with line numbers (local framework file): `FRAMEWORK/reference/03-FIGURES_TEXT.md`
> - Canonical usage rule (local framework file): `FRAMEWORK/reference/FIGURES_README_canonical.md`
> - The eight-step loop and mode rule (local framework file): `FRAMEWORK/figures/THE_FORMULA.md`
>
> **If you want to reuse the machinery:** the formula is in the third link. Run it on a transmission question you know well and can check against a ground. Write down what would refute your result before you run it, and state the mode of what you get. A reported failure is more useful to me than a reported success.
>
> Thanks,
> [name]

## Why this version is ordered this way

| the move | figure-grounded reason |
|---|---|
| Residual and refuter first, theory withheld | Fig 10 :52: *"Bring a characterised residual, never the source."* Fig 2 :45: *"Frames range against the ground. They do not negotiate with it."* |
| Do not ask for agreement or trust | Fig 2 :35: *"Alignment is triangulation, not negotiation. A mirror cannot correct you."* |
| Pre-registered refuter | The standing seat rule: a falsifier written before the run is a design instrument, not only an honesty device. |
| Mode stated on every substantive claim | `THE_FORMULA.md`: *"Mode. general · specified · grounded. State which."* |
| No coefficient asserted | Fig 9 :32: *"you get a kind of thing, never the answer itself"* and Fig 13 :134: *"Unreachable is a verdict here, not a silence"* |
| Per-slot indexing | Fig 1 :19 and Fig 1 :28: *"The gap is measured per object, not per step."* |
| Union, not partition | Fig 9 :55 and Fig 9 :57: a case in two conditions is not a harder case of either. |
| Links only after the verdict on stage 1 | Fig 4 :22: *"Only methods cross up. Only head starts come down."* A recording sent first is a description of one occasion, not a generator. |
| Ask users to write their own refuter | Formula "Direction": a frame cannot check the direction of its own question because the sought-for shape is not a term it holds. |
| Public institutional venues only | Fig 2 :42: *"Buy independence in proportion to the cost of being wrong."* Selecting recipients for receptiveness manufactures the disqualifying correlation. |

## Stage discipline

1. **Stage 1:** residual, shape claim, and refuter. Ask what the recipient would count as a failure. Record their verdict before showing any framework interior.
2. **Stage 2, opt-in only:** if curious, offer the three local framework files. The figures, not the summary, are the authority. Later agreement is never pooled as corroboration (Fig 2 :40: *"agreement about the world is worthless when the agreeing frames share an evidence pool"*).
3. **Stage 3:** offer the formula as a method to reuse, with the instruction that each user states their own ground and writes their own refuter first.
