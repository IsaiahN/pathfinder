## 1. A shape is a constraint, not a filler

The canonical figures README states the limit: the framework returns *"what a missing thing would have to look like (a shape, not a name)."* Fig 9 :32: *"you get a kind of thing, never the answer itself."* Fig 13 :134: *"Unreachable is a verdict here, not a silence"*

From *The Library of Babel* (read directly, 2026-10-02): *"you can cut your search short if the set of books returned is small and descriptive enough that it provides a general shape of the answer that satisfies your question."*

For this question:

- **A shape:** the stability of the action-to-outcome map is conditional on a maintained channel and a matching habitat; the residual is per economy and per date; the union of time-variation and cross-economy variation must be unbundled before a lever is chosen.
- **Not a shape, and refused here:** a single multiplier of the form "one point of tightening changes inflation by x and unemployment by y in economy s at date t." That is a name/coefficient where only a shape is in hand.

A shape constrains. It does not fill.

## 2. How the shape was obtained

- *Library of Babel*: start with the vocabulary closest to the thing and look for recurring new terms that overlap or correlate with it; each new term narrows the space. The essay also warns that *"Perhaps your problem was a bundle or conflation of several problems masquerading as one?"* That is exactly the union found in `01_figure_grounded_audit.md` F7.
- Fig 9 :30: *"your words are borrowed too, so describe the effect"* rather than the cause. So the first move was to write R_s,t = |Γ_s,t(b,a) - o'_s,t| in effect terms before naming any transmission channel.
- Fig 6 :30: *"The description travels. Fails by being sharpened into terms only we can read. Cast it in effect terms."* The outreach draft in `02_outreach_email.md` therefore leads with the residual, not with framework vocabulary.
- *Decoding Tacit Knowledge* (role as recorded in the exemplar): when contact is not possible, the best that can be sent is a plain description of what one would expect to see, because anyone can check that against the world. This governs the refuters in section 4 of the audit.

## 3. What would license a name, that is, a coefficient

Two things only, both outside the shape:

1. **The ground settles it.** Formula step 5: *"the ground settles it. Verified only by what pays. A gate is not the ground."* A model fit, a consensus, or a significant coefficient is a gate, not the ground.
2. **A provenanced import from a frame whose closure differs**, with a source gate and a shadow test (Fig 6 :23, Fig 8 :50-52). A coefficient imported from another economy without measuring the round-trip gap is an adoption, not a derivation.

*The Hard Problem is About Source* (read directly, 2026-08-28) gives the mechanism: *"If several paths create the same behavior, then behavior is a projection with lossy information"* and *"The answer is unreached until a better instrument exists."* An aggregate inflation series can be produced by several different source configurations (demand-pull, cost-push, expectation de-anchoring, exchange-rate pass-through). Naming one from the reading is the metamerism error. What is owed is an instrument that discriminates sources, not a larger regression.

## 4. Why the pooled coefficient is the specific forbidden error

Fig 1 :19: *"The residual is indexed by object slot, not aggregated."* Fig 1 :22: *"Aggregating across slots is how a live signal disappears into an average."* A pooled estimate answers "what is the average effect" and silently drops the question actually asked, which is whether the effect is reliable per slot.

The low-overlap medical analogue makes the same point from outside economics: Dahabreh, Hayward and Kent (2016) distinguish *"determining the best treatment for an individual"* from *"determining the average effect of treatment in a population."* Same structure, different field, one witness for the shape of the error.

## 5. What "reliably" means under this reading

"Reliably" is Fig 12's comparison bond, not a conjunction. It asks whether `R_s,t` is below a threshold across slots and dates (Fig 12 :87: *"a threshold relation between two quantities"*). A comparison is a goal statement, not a mechanism: Fig 12's operators note says *"a goal is a comparison, which is why a vocabulary without it cannot state one."* So "reliable control" is a goal the framework can state and test per slot; it is not a property that the rate change carries.

## 6. Prestige, consensus, and my own status

**Consensus is not action.** The exemplar's reading of *The Recursive Self-Improvement Trap* records: *"Prestige must never be allowed to validate an action directly... Prestige can fund an action. It can give an action a budget to be tried. But it cannot settle an action. Only the ground can settle it."* A survey of prominent economists agreeing that rates work is the named collapse, not corroboration. The legitimate ask is a budget to try the claim against data.

**My own audit is a simulation, not a ground reading.** The same essay: *"LLMs can quickly simulate an approximate answer/output... However, the truly testable output would need to be derived from the real architecture or working the formula yourself."* So `01_figure_grounded_audit.md` is a simulated derivation checked for internal consistency against the figures. It is not a test of the claim and must be labelled as such. It cannot settle anything it reports on.

## 7. Correction carried forward

An earlier draft of this reasoning treated "shape, not a name" as a blanket ban on stating any coefficient. Corrected: the README's phrase governs a **missing** thing not held in Γ. Here the missing thing is not the policy rate or the inflation series (both are held); it is the per-slot, per-date source-discriminating instrument and the coefficient it would settle. The ban applies to inventing that coefficient from the armchair and calling it a result. It does not ban using published coefficients as provenanced imports with `R_T` measured. Both readings are recorded so neither is quietly lost.
