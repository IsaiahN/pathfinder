## What is deliberately not here

- No personal emails of named individuals.
- No ranking of people by how likely they are to agree with the shape claim.
- No bulk-send plan.
- No assertion that the figures rank the five determinants empirically. That is a `gap`, and a gap is a verdict, not a silence (Fig 8 :39).

Three figure-grounded reasons for the omissions:

1. **Agreement is not an anchor.** Fig 2 :35, "Alignment is triangulation, not negotiation. A mirror cannot correct you." Selecting recipients for receptiveness manufactures the disqualifying correlation (Fig 2 :40).
2. **The verifier must not hold the interiors.** Fig 10 :52, "Bring a characterised residual, never the source." A recipient who has absorbed the framework before being asked for a verdict has joined the frame being checked.
3. **A room is not a ground.** Fig 7 :35, "A room's agreement is variation, not a verdict. Selection between rooms is where the ground votes."

Legally: scraping personal emails for unsolicited bulk outreach breaches GDPR and CAN-SPAM style rules in many jurisdictions. That is a practical failure on top of the structural one.
