# The Tether's answer on workers' bargaining power

**Mode: `specified` on the structural shape, `general` on the cross-kind and anchor claims, `gap` on the empirical ranking. Not `grounded`.** Simulated derivation checked against the figures (`05_what_shape_means.md` section 6), not a ground reading.

## 1. The residual it answers

The question asks which one of five factors determines workers' bargaining power. Restated in effect terms: what makes the worker's claim on the employment surplus move, and how many separate slots must be tracked to answer that? The five are not one question. Fig 9 :5: "are not one hard question but several well-formed ones. Split it rather than search." Fig 1 :19: "The residual is indexed by object slot, not aggregated."

## 2. The shape

1. **The five determinants are a union compound, not a partition.** Unions change effective competition; law changes union viability and wage floors; employer concentration is one reading of the buyer network that competition describes. Fig 9 :55: "A bundled problem is usually a union of overlapping conditions, not a list of separate ones." A single lever clears the region where only one condition holds and leaves the intersections standing.
2. **Productivity is a support condition, not the lever.** It sets how large the surplus is; it does not decide who claims it. Fig 13 :22 calls that surplus "a difference that can be spent", and Fig 11 :40: "An improvement that does not change contact changes nothing, however much it improves." Bargaining power is a property of the worker and the habitat together, not of the worker alone (Fig 11 :39).
3. **Competition and employer concentration are two readings of one network condition.** Fig 13 :24 names the structure "that entities relate, and influence travels". Under Fig 12 :117 they are candidates for isomers, "same atoms, different bond, different substance"; at minimum they are a functional group (Fig 12 :123). Their relative empirical magnitude is open.
4. **Unions are an introduced actor, law is a provenanced import.** Fig 11 :61: "Introduction is available; exclusion is not." Fig 6 :14 calls import "the only operator that moves the wall"; a statute arrives from the polity frame with a date and a jurisdiction, with a source gate and a shadow test still owed. Fig 10 :18: "a declared meaning is not a grounded one" - a written wage floor is not a measurement of what it does.
5. **The empirical ranking is `gap`.** Fig 9 :32: "you get a kind of thing, never the answer itself." Fig 8 :39: "So a search that finds nothing is never a proof of absence." The figures give a shape; they do not give a coefficient, a rank order, or a percentage. This is `unreached`, not `unreachable`: the ground is reachable by measurement, and importing a coefficient here would add an atom that was not needed.

## 3. What it explicitly does not say

- Not that any one of the five is the dominant determinant. No figure line ranks them.
- Not that productivity is irrelevant. It is the support gate: without a surplus there is nothing to split; under perfect competition workers earn their marginal product, which is a claim about one frame's split rule, not about the residual in an imperfect market.
- Not that unions are always beneficial. Fig 11 :49: "An actor's valence is relative to the residual, not a property it carries." The same union raises wages and carries a measured deadweight cost.
- Not that a law's declaration is its effect, and not that law is a seventh bond between worker and firm.
- Not `grounded`. No wage, employment or bargaining outcome was measured by this derivation.

## 4. What would settle it: the ground

Fig 2 :29: "An anchor is whatever settles the question and does not change its answer because of what any frame thinks. A measurement, an experiment, a counter, a market, a patient outcome." For this question the anchors are:

- **Separating instruments.** A research design that moves one slot while holding the others fixed. Productivity with alternatives, union status and law held fixed; a change in employer concentration with outside options measured separately; a legal change with before-and-after wage and employment outcomes. The metamerism point in `05` applies: a wage reading alone cannot name its source.
- **Concentration versus competition.** Repeated measures showing concentration falling while outside options worsen, or the reverse, would refute the one-network reading (F3).
- **Union as proxy.** A union wage effect that vanishes under productivity and composition controls, with no remaining change in the claim, would refute the actor change in F4.
- **Ranking figure.** A figure line that directly governs the empirical ranking would close F6. None was found after SEARCHED, INWARD and OUTWARD were run separately (`01`, section 6).

## 5. The honest stop

The answer stops at the limit of what the available instruments distinguish, and records the lineage so the stop is checkable. The five conditions overlap, so a single headline determinant is an average that throws away the per-slot signal. The six-term substrate is "a human's careful guess at reality's compression axes" (*They're Scanning Books*); declaring it makes the choice visible, not neutral. Fig 4 :30: "So take the round trip and measure the gap. Without it you are navigating by dead reckoning: confident about a position nobody checked." The round-trip gap here is the empirical magnitude the figures cannot hold. More measurement, not another atom, is the remedy.

## 6. One-paragraph version for outreach

> The Tether does not pick a winner from productivity, competition, unions, law and employer concentration. It says the question is a bundle of overlapping conditions miswritten as a list, and that averaging them into one determinant loses the signal about where to look. Productivity sets the size of the surplus, not who can claim it: an improvement that does not change a worker's contact with alternatives changes nothing however much it improves. Competition and employer concentration are two readings of one buyer network, unions are an actor introduced into the workplace habitat, and law is a provenanced import from the polity whose declaration is not its own measurement. The figures deliver that shape and the refuters written in advance; they do not deliver a coefficient. The empirical ranking stays open until a design that separates the slots and a measured ground settle it.
