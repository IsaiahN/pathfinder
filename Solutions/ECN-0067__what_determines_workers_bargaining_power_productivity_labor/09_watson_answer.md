## QUESTION

Decide, in effect terms, what the Tether says about the question: what determines workers' bargaining power, productivity, labor-market competition, unions, law, or employer concentration? Option A: treat the five as independent alternatives and name a winner. Option B: keep the per-slot shape, unbundle the overlaps, and record the empirical ranking as `gap`. What each leads to: A aggregates slots and throws away the only signal that says where to look, then presents a figure-internal preference as an empirical result. B sends a checkable shape, refuters written in advance, and an open slot until a separating design and a measured ground settle it.

## DOMAIN CENSUS

R by arity, symmetry and scale, then one line per domain with actors, currency and a nameable anchor; partition or union; order by dependency.

- **R (arity / symmetry / scale).** R is the gap between what a worker claims on the employment surplus and what they would claim with no leverage. Arity: five candidate slots plus the worker and firm. Symmetry: asymmetric, the firm is normally the buyer of labor. Scale: match (micro), labor market (meso), legal frame (macro). Fig 1 :19 "The residual is indexed by object slot, not aggregated." and Fig 9 :5 "are not one hard question but several well-formed ones. Split it rather than search." => a union compound, not one hard question.
- **D1, the match surplus (productivity).** Actors: worker, firm, marginal revenue product. Currency: the surplus produced by the match. Anchor: measured marginal product and output per worker. Support condition, not a lever (Fig 13 :22).
- **D2, the contact structure (competition and employer concentration).** Actors: employers, workers, search frictions, alternative employers. Currency: outside options and the elasticity of labor supply to the firm. Anchor: measured employer concentration and wage or separation elasticities. Two readings of one network condition.
- **D3, collective organization (unions).** Actors: union, members, employer. Currency: collective voice and the threat of withdrawal. Anchor: measured union wage premium and agreement coverage. An actor introduced into the habitat, persistent across uses.
- **D4, the legal frame (law).** Actors: legislature, courts, regulators, parties. Currency: wage floors, bargaining rights, non-compete limits. Anchor: statutes and court rulings plus measured outcomes after they change. An import from the polity frame.
- **D5, the verification frame.** Actors: researchers, datasets, experiments, reviewers. Currency: residual and agreement. Anchor: measured wage and employment outcomes, never agreement.
- **Order by dependency.** Surplus before split, contact before capture, actor configuration before legal rules, settlement last (Fig 3 :20). Fig 5 :20 "is there anything to explain?" is the support gate.

Strain test: "workers earn their marginal product" belongs to the competitive benchmark, where the split rule is fixed by assumption, not to the residual in an imperfect market. Re-census puts it in one frame rather than letting it swallow D2 to D4. One live strain remains: three of the determinants read as structures under Fig 13, which bars direct structure-structure composition. It is named, not smoothed.

## VERDICT (from the figures)

The shape survives, the question's framing does not. Five overlapping conditions must be unbundled, and the residual tracked per slot (Fig 9 :55, Fig 1 :19). Productivity is the support gate, not the lever: it sets the surplus, and an agent-only improvement that does not change contact changes nothing (Fig 13 :22, Fig 11 :40). Competition and employer concentration are two readings of one buyer network, at least a functional group with an open empirical magnitude (Fig 13 :24, Fig 12 :117/:123). Unions introduce a persistent actor with residual-relative valence (Fig 11 :61/:49, Fig 12 :119). Law is a provenanced import whose declaration is not its own ground (Fig 6 :14/:23, Fig 10 :18). The anchor is measured outcomes, never agreement (Fig 2 :29/:35/:40, Fig 7 :35). The empirical ranking is `gap`, `unreached` rather than `unreachable` (Fig 8 :39, Fig 9 :32). This is `specified` and ungrounded.

- **cost if wrong:** bounded and locatable. The shape is stated with modes, refuters and provenance, so an error is findable rather than invisible. The real cost is spending the independence of outside readers by sending a recording or a league table instead of a generator and a per-slot map. Fig 2 :42 prices it: "Buy independence in proportion to the cost of being wrong." That cost is avoidable by leading with the shape claim and its refuter.
- **undoable:** yes. The league-table framing is removable without touching the derivation; `07_the_tether_answer.md` and `08_expert_verification_packet.md` already remove it. A sent claim can be superseded by the corrected packet, and no primitive has to be withdrawn.
- **reversed by:** a separating ground reading, not agreement. A design that moves productivity with contact terms fixed; repeated measures separating concentration from outside options; a legal change with its provenance and measured outcomes reported separately; a figure line that directly governs the ranking; or a re-census admitting direct structure-structure composition among the three structure-like determinants. Until one of these fires, the verdict stands as `candidate`.
