## How this literature bears on the residual

1. **Stansbury and Summers (NBER 27193)** is the closest measured witness to the residual. It reports lower wages and higher profit shares alongside reduced worker power in a period of rising productivity and profitability. That is the empirical shape of Fig 11 :40: the agent improved, the claim did not follow automatically. It corroborates F2.
2. **Wikipedia, Labour economics** states the competitive benchmark in which workers earn their marginal product. Read alone it looks like a rival to the capability claim. Re-census puts it inside one frame (perfect competition), where the split rule is already fixed by assumption; it does not govern the residual in an imperfect market. This is the strain named in section 1 of `01_figure_grounded_audit.md`, not a hidden contradiction.
3. **Wikipedia, Monopsony** both supports and conflicts. It defines employer power over wages as a function of competition among buyers of labor, which supports the one-network reading in F3; it also says empirical evidence of monopsony power is limited, which conflicts with the strong version of the concentration-lever claim. The conflict sends the derivation back to the census, where D2 is re-censed as two readings with an open magnitude. It does not flip the verdict.
4. **Wikipedia, Collective bargaining** supplies the measured 5 to 10 percent union markup and the legal provenance of bargaining rights in the United States and in ILO conventions. It corroborates F4 (an actor introduced into the habitat) and F5 (a provenanced legal import).
5. **Wikipedia, Bargaining power** supplies the relational definition and the Nash split. Its LLM-maintenance notice is a reason not to lean on it alone; the same relational point is independently available from the NBER paper and from the collective-bargaining page.

**Overlap check:** these sources are not all one pool. The NBER working paper is a specific study with replication files; the Wikipedia pages are secondary syntheses drawing on different literatures (bargaining theory, monopsony, industrial relations). They nevertheless share a broad economics evidence base, so their agreement on the relational point is corroboration of moderate weight, not two fully independent witnesses. The ledger records this per row.
