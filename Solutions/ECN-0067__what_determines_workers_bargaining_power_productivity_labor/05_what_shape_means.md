## 1. A shape is a constraint, not a filler

The canonical figures README states the limit: the framework returns "what a missing thing would have to look like (a shape, not a name)." Fig 9 :32: "you get a kind of thing, never the answer itself." Fig 13 :135: "It cannot say which primitive is missing, because naming it would mean already holding it."

From *The Library of Babel*: "you can cut your search short if the set of books returned is small and descriptive enough that it provides a general shape of the answer that satisfies your question."

So for this question a **shape** is the narrowed form of an answer we can state before we can rank the five determinants. It constrains and does not fill. "Five overlapping conditions, with productivity as a support gate rather than a lever" is a shape. "Unions raise wages by 8 percent and are therefore the main determinant" is a name with a coefficient attached, and the figures do not license it.

The same essay gives the direct parallel to this question: "Perhaps your problem was a bundle or conflation of several problems masquerading as one?" That is F1 in `01_figure_grounded_audit.md`, and it is the reason the "which one?" grammar is refused before any figure line is chosen.

## 2. How a shape is obtained

- *Library of Babel*: use the vocabulary already closest to the thing, look for recurring terms that overlap or correlate with it, and recurse. "With each new term gained, you are able to narrow the space by definition and deepen your vocabulary."
- *Decoding Tacit Knowledge*: "When contact isn't possible, the best you can send is a plain description of what you'd expect to see, because anyone can check that against the world." This is exactly the two-stage offer in `02_outreach_email.md`: send the effect description before the source.
- Fig 6 :30 and Fig 9 :30-31: "The description travels. Fails by being sharpened into terms only we can read. Cast it in effect terms" and "your words are borrowed too, so describe the effect rather than the cause." Hence "moving the worker's claim on the surplus" rather than "being productive."
- *Library of Babel* on stopping: "the finish line will also be the starting place." The loop stops when the residual stops arriving, not when a league table is produced.
- *How Intelligence Actually Works* warns that the mapping "is not a tree with roots and branches, it is a web, with no canonical order and no single path through." That is the structural reason a single-lever answer is the wrong shape: there is no canonical first cause to name.

## 3. What licenses a name

Only two things, both outside the shape itself:

1. **The ground settles it.** `THE_FORMULA.md` step 5: "the ground settles it. Verified only by what pays. A gate is not the ground." For this question the ground is measured wage and employment outcomes: a regression, a natural experiment, a court record, a collective agreement. Fig 2 :29: "An anchor is whatever settles the question and does not change its answer because of what any frame thinks. A measurement, an experiment, a counter, a market, a patient outcome."
2. **A provenanced import from a frame whose closure differs** (Fig 6 :23, Fig 8 :50-52), with a source gate and a shadow test. Law is exactly this: a statute from the polity frame, with a date and a jurisdiction.

*They're Scanning Books* sharpens both: "A minimum viable set of six terms is a human's careful guess at reality's compression axes; another sensorium's minimum set need not be those six." And "Declaring the primitives does not make them neutral. It makes the choice visible." This is why the six-term mapping in `01` is recorded as a candidate binding (a guess made visible), not as a discovery of nature's own list. It is also why the empirical ranking is left open rather than quietly imported from the economics frame: an import must be recorded as such.

## 4. Why the empirical ranking is a gap rather than a name

*The Hard Problem is About Source* gives the mechanism. An instrument that reads green "cannot comment on the source" - one green light, or yellow plus blue, or RGB, or CMYK, or a scheme not yet discovered. "If several paths create the same behavior, then behavior is a projection with lossy information." The reading does not determine the source. "It was proven wrong not by argument, but by the arrival of a better instrument." It was the spectrometer, not a further argument, that resolved it.

Applied here: a wage premium is a reading. Several paths can produce it (higher productivity, a union, a wage floor, a thin employer market, or a combination). Reading one cause off the wage observation is the metamerism error. What is owed is a discriminating instrument (a design that separates the slots), not a name. This is the deepest reason F6 is `gap`: the instruments we have do not cleanly separate five overlapping conditions, and the monopsony literature itself says so (Wikipedia, Monopsony, "Empirical evidence of monopsony power has been relatively limited").

*How a Society of Agents Would Work* adds the aggregation warning in the framework's own words: "Aggregating across slots is how a live signal disappears into an average." A single headline number for "the determinant of bargaining power" is that average.

## 5. Q: Does "shape, not a name" forbid naming one of the five? ANSWERED, with a correction

**Short answer: no, not for that reason.** The canonical sentence says "what a **missing** thing would have to look like (a shape, not a name)." Its subject is a primitive not held in the library. All five candidates here are already held, measurable atoms in the labor-economics frame: marginal product, outside options and concentration, union coverage, statutes. Naming one as the empirically largest is a **binding from the world frame** onto a held atom, not the invention of a seventh primitive.

**What remains wrong is presenting that binding as figure-derived.** The figures do not rank the five. Fig 9 :32 says "you get a kind of thing, never the answer itself." So the honest form is: "a labor-economics study finds factor X has the largest measured effect in sample Y, period Z, provenanced as such." The forbidden form is: "the Tether shows X is the determinant."

### Correction to my own section 1 and to finding F6

I initially wrote that "shape, not a name" forbids naming a determinant. That overstated the rule, exactly as the exemplar's own `05` section 5b overstated it for Gradient. I narrow it here and preserve the earlier sentence so the correction is visible:

- **Earlier reading:** "A shape constrains and does not fill; naming one determinant is forbidden by 'shape, not a name'."
- **Narrowed reading:** the rule governs a primitive not held in Γ. Here the five determinants are held in the world frame. Naming one is allowed only as a provenanced binding from that frame, with a measured ground, and never as a figure derivation.
- **Not retracted:** the `gap` verdict stands. The reason is now precise: the figures cannot rank held atoms, and the empirical instruments do not cleanly separate overlapping conditions. The gap is about magnitude and separation, not about a missing primitive.

Both claims are kept so neither is quietly lost.

## 6. Prestige is not action, and this audit is a simulation

*The Recursive Self-Improvement Trap*: "Prestige can fund an action. It can give an action a budget to be tried. But it cannot settle an action. Only the ground can settle it. The Ground Maintainer must be blind to prestige." A campaign that gathers famous labor economists to agree with the shape claim is the named collapse, not corroboration. The legitimate ask is the opposite: give the claim a budget to be tried and let the ground, a dataset or an experiment, settle it.

*Arrival of the Fittest* says plainly that LLM output "must be seen as simulacra" and warns that solutions "could never be verified if the solutions presented were not contrived to fit the formula." *The Recursive Self-Improvement Trap* is blunter: "the truly testable output would need to be derived from the real architecture or working the formula yourself."

So `01_figure_grounded_audit.md`, `06_isomers_and_order.md`, `07_the_tether_answer.md`, `08_expert_verification_packet.md`, `09_watson_answer.md` and `11_audit.md` are simulated derivations checked for internal consistency against the figures. They are not ground readings. They cannot settle the labor-economics question they report on. That is stated on every substantive file, and it is not a disclaimer to be removed once the argument looks good.
