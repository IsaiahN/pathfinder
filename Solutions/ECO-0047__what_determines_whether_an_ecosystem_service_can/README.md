## The question restated as an effect

What is being decided is not the *size* of a loss or the *number* of species left. Let a service be a slot s (pollination, water purification, nutrient cycling, pest suppression, carbon storage). Before a perturbation the ecological frame predicts a service flow Γ(b,a); the world returns the measured flow o′; R = |Γ(b,a) − o′| is indexed per service slot and never averaged across services (Fig 1 :19, Fig 1 :22). "Can it recover" asks whether R for that slot returns toward zero, and what governs the return.

- Option A, "recovery is a property of the species that remain": leads to the prediction that a species count or a functional-trait list determines the outcome, independent of where and with what the species are in contact.
- Option B, "recovery is a property of the service-providing assemblage and its habitat, read at a non-updating ground": leads to the prediction that recovery fails when contact fails, when the objective is unmeasured, when the habitat is substituted rather than restored, or when the recovery reading itself updates on the manager's opinion (Fig 11 :39, Fig 11 :40, Fig 2 :29).
- What an answer would look like, written down and bound as an ordinary term before the run (Formula, "Direction"): a *separator* naming the conditions that must all hold before "recovered" can be said of a slot, plus an instrument that discriminates a real return of the flow from an apparent one. Until that shape is bound it predicts nothing and no check in the loop can see it.

The verdicts in `09_watson_answer.md` are kept per domain and never averaged. The literal species list and recovery time are a `gap`; the separator is `specified` and triangulated.

## What is deliberately not here

- No personal emails of named individuals.
- No ranking of people by how likely they are to agree.
- No bulk-send plan.
- No claim that this derivation is `grounded`, no claim that it settles which species or which management action produces recovery, and no claim that the framework is the only account of ecosystem recovery.

Three figure-grounded reasons, plus one practical one:

1. A room is not an anchor. *"A room's agreement is variation, not a verdict. Selection between rooms is where the ground votes."* (Fig 7 :35) and *"Alignment is triangulation, not negotiation. A mirror cannot correct you."* (Fig 2 :35). Gathering agreeable readers manufactures the correlation that disqualifies a verdict.
2. Correlation has valence. *"agreement about the world is worthless when the agreeing frames share an evidence pool"* (Fig 2 :40). A list selected for receptiveness is one witness wearing many names.
3. A verifier must be outside the interior. *"A verifier that can reconstruct the claim cannot verify it."* (Fig 10 :42). The first contact therefore carries the residual and its refuter, not the framework.
4. Legally, unsolicited bulk email to scraped addresses breaches GDPR and CAN-SPAM style rules in many jurisdictions and gets the sending domain blacklisted. Public opt-in routes avoid that failure as well as the structural one.
