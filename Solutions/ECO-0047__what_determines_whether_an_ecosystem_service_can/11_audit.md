## Independent domain census

I redid the census from the residual line alone, using the WATSON order: R by arity, symmetry and scale; one line per domain with actors / currency / anchor; partition or union; order by dependency.

- **R (arity / symmetry / scale).** Let a service be slot s; Γ(b,a) predicts a flow, o′ is observed, R = |Γ(b,a) − o′| per slot. Arity: several overlapping conditions, so a **union compound** (Fig 9 :55 *"A bundled problem is usually a union of overlapping conditions, not a list of separate ones."*), not one hard question. Symmetry: broken, because recovery is ordered and non-invertible (addition differs from subtraction of the habitat). Scale: per service slot first order, with second- and third-order contact cascades.
- **D1 Habitat and contact.** Actors: remaining species, conditions, relations. Currency: contact and the delivered flow. Anchor: the flow at the beneficiary, which does not update. Question type: enumerate outward, never compose.
- **D2 Reading and objective.** Actors: monitor, metric, the stated meaning of "recovered". Currency: observation against prediction. Anchor: the ground, reached through a channel that can decay. Question type: dependency chain, break between objective and planning.
- **D3 Maintenance and import.** Actors: manager seat, introduced actors, instruments, provenance. Currency: actions, source gate, shadow test. Anchor: the ground, never the manager's opinion. Question type: two modes; introduction, not exclusion.
- **D4 The specific list.** Actors: candidate species, counts, recovery times. Currency: none bound. Anchor: none nameable. **Not a domain yet.** No figure line returns a name.
- **Partition or union.** D1 through D3 are a union; D4 is excluded as `gap`. A service can fail on the reading link alone or on the contact link alone, and a case in two conditions is categorically different from either.
- **Order by dependency.** Describe the residual; enumerate the habitat outward; check the objective instrument; refuse before mint; import with provenance and a shadow test only if an atom is absent; settle at the ground.

My census matches the primary's three domains plus the excluded fourth, and its union reading. ## Round trip gap R_T

The Symbols table defines R_T as *"The gap between what was sent up and what came back down"*, written gap(x, (T_E ∘ T_A)(x)), and Fig 4 :30 requires it to be measured rather than assumed. I ran the round trip on the condensation this folder actually uses.

1. **Sent up (x):** the verbatim figure set, chiefly Fig 11 :39-44, :49-51, :61-70, Fig 1 :19-22, Fig 2 :29 and Fig 10 :24-26.
2. **Abstraction (T_A):** the shorthand in `07_the_tether_answer.md` and `08_expert_verification_packet.md`: recovery is assemblage-and-habitat, enumerated outward, per service, at a non-updating ground; valence relative to the residual; two modes; asymmetric introduction; isolation as substitution.
3. **Concretisation (T_E):** the ordinary management reading the separator is meant to discipline: add species X at density Y and the service returns in Z years, or restore the site to its historical state and the service recovers.

**R_T is nonzero.** Six constraints held by the shorthand do not survive the trip back down:

- the second- and third-order cascade collapses to a direct-contact list;
- the ground/channel distinction collapses to a metric that returned;
- valence relativity collapses to a standing good-species list;
- asymmetric introduction plus source gate and shadow test collapses to replant and restock;
- isolation as substitution collapses to the idea that degradation was simply reversed;
- per-slot indexing collapses to a single ecosystem-health index.

Two things survive cleanly: capability belongs to assemblage and habitat rather than to the agent alone, and enumeration precedes intervention. That residue is the part worth sending. R_T was **not** measured numerically, because no ground reading exists; it is recorded qualitatively as the enumerated set of dropped constraints. Treating the shorthand as identical to the figures would set R_T to zero and commit the dead-reckoning error Fig 4 :30 names. The honest form is a lossy condensation with a measured gap, which is what `09_watson_answer.md` now states.
