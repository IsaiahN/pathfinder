## 1. A shape is a constraint, not a filler

The figures README states the limit: the framework returns *"what a missing thing would have to look like (a shape, not a name)."* Fig 9 :32: *"you get a kind of thing, never the answer itself."* Fig 13 :135: *"It cannot say which primitive is missing, because naming it would mean already holding it."*

From *The Library of Babel*: *"you can cut your search short if the set of books returned is small and descriptive enough that it provides a general shape of the answer that satisfies your question."*

For ECO-0047 this means the deliverable is a **separator**: a set of conditions any candidate answer about recovery must satisfy, not the name of the species or the number of years. "Recovery is a property of assemblage and habitat, read per service at a ground that does not update" is a shape. "Add species X at density Y and the service returns in Z years" is a name. The first is derivable from the figure set; the second is a binding that needs a ground and provenance.

## 2. How a shape is obtained

- *Library of Babel* on proximity: *"use the existing vocabulary you do know that is closest to the words that you wish to describe and grasp the shape of"*, then *"With each new term gained, you narrow the space by definition and deepen your vocabulary."* Applied here: start from the residual (a service flow failing to return), then borrow the vocabulary of contact, habitat, enumeration and ground, narrowing until the separator is stateable.
- Fig 6 :30: *"The description travels. Fails by being sharpened into terms only we can read. Cast it in effect terms."* Fig 9 :30-31: *"your words are borrowed too, so describe the effect rather than the cause."* So the first contact describes what recovery would look like in the flow, not which mechanism the ecologist believes in.
- *Decoding Tacit Knowledge*: *"When contact isn't possible, the best you can send is a plain description of what you'd expect to see, because anyone can check that against the world."* This is exactly the stage-1 offer in `02_outreach_email.md`.
- *Library of Babel* on stopping: *"you will know that you are done because the finish line will also be the starting place, as there is nowhere else to search and nothing else that can describe the answer better."* The separator stops when no further figure line narrows it; the species question then remains a `gap`.

## 3. What licenses a name

Only two things, both outside the shape itself:

1. **The ground settles it.** Formula step 5: *"the ground settles it. Verified only by what pays."* The README: the framework *"stops at the limit of what the available instruments can distinguish, and records the lineage so the stop is honest."* For a service, the ground is the delivered flow at the beneficiary over the relevant lag, read by something that does not update on the manager's opinion (Fig 2 :29, Fig 10 :24).
2. **A provenanced import from a frame whose closure differs** (Fig 6 :23, Fig 8 :53), with a source gate and a shadow test. Introducing a pollinator, a soil microbe or a predator is an import; *"A wrong atom costs more than starting from primitives"* (Fig 6 :24), and cane toads and kudzu are the same move with the cascade unenumerated (Fig 11 :65).

*They're Scanning Books* sharpens the second point: *"The real import is not a new term in the existing space. It is a new dimension of the space."* And: *"A minimum viable set of six terms is a human's careful guess at reality's compression axes; another sensorium's minimum set need not be those six."* Also: *"Declaring the primitives does not make them neutral. It makes the choice visible."* So naming a service-contact category is a visible choice, not a discovery of nature's own list.

## 4. Why an apparent recovery is not a settled recovery

*The Hard Problem is About Source* gives the mechanism. An instrument reads green and *"cannot comment on the source"*: one green light, or yellow plus blue, or RGB, or CMYK, or a scheme not yet discovered. *"If several paths create the same behavior, then behavior is a projection with lossy information."* *"An instrument that can't distinguish these cases demonstrates its own inadequacy."* It was the **spectrometer**, not an argument, that resolved it: *"It was proven wrong not by argument, but by the arrival of a better instrument."*

Applied here: a water-quality index that returns to its pre-loss value does not say whether the return came from restored riparian contact, from dilution, from a change in the sampling point, or from a metric that saturated. The recovery reading is a projection with lossy information. What is owed is a source-discriminating instrument, not a declaration that the service recovered. Eppinga et al. 2025 supply the empirical version: *"biodiversity-dependent ecosystem service debts may drive initial phases of apparent recovery after disturbance, still followed by system collapse."*

## 5. Q - does "shape, not a name" forbid naming a species? ANSWERED, with a correction

**Nothing in the framework licenses a species name as the answer to "what determines recovery."** The framework returns a separator. A species is licensed only by the ground settling a candidate, or by a provenanced import with a shadow test. Until then the name is the searcher's own contribution and must be recorded as such.

**Correction to my own first pass (kept visible).** I initially read the rule as a blanket ban on naming anything. It is not. The README's phrase governs a **missing** primitive, one not held in Γ. Habitat actors, conditions and relations are already enumerable from the world (Fig 11 :45-48); a species is an entity in that habitat, and naming one as a candidate binding is provenance, not the invention of a new primitive. What remains wrong in a species-list answer is different and serious: it treats an ingredient list as the delivered flow (Fig 12 :5, :111), it ignores that valence is relative to the residual (Fig 11 :49-51), and it names the source of a recovery reading without an instrument that discriminates among sources (section 4). Both the narrowed rule and the original overstatement are recorded here so neither is quietly lost.

## 6. Prestige is not action

From *The Recursive Self-Improvement Trap*: *"Prestige must never be allowed to validate an action directly... Someone let prestige stand in for proof... Prestige can fund an action. It can give an action a budget to be tried. But it cannot settle an action. Only the ground can settle it. The Ground Maintainer must be blind to prestige."*

A campaign that gathers well-known ecologists to agree with the separator is the named collapse, not corroboration. The legitimate ask is the opposite: give the separator a budget to be tried, and let the flow at the beneficiary, read through a channel that can fail, settle it. The same essay gives the author's unavoidable stake: *"the output is only as good as the validator. The agent, or the tool, or the frame, or the theory, cannot validate itself."*

## 7. My own audit is a simulation, not a ground reading

Same essay: *"LLMs can quickly simulate an approximate answer/output with the formula found in the architecture post... However, the truly testable output would need to be derived from the real architecture or working the formula yourself."* LLM output is a simulated approximation. Therefore `01_figure_grounded_audit.md` is a simulated derivation, checked for internal consistency against the figures. It is **not** a test of the ecological claim, and it cannot settle anything it reports on. The mode line on every substantive claim in this folder says `specified`, `general`, or `gap`, never `grounded`.

## 8. What this means for ECO-0047 specifically

The eight-essay reading converges on four consequences for this question:

1. **The answer is a separator, not a list** (sections 1 and 2): the conditions under which a service flow can return, indexed per service, never pooled.
2. **The list question is a `gap`** (section 1, Fig 9 :32, Fig 13 :135): unreachable is a verdict, not a silence. Reporting it is more honest than supplying a plausible species.
3. **The recovery instrument is the scarce thing** (section 4, Fig 3 :27): an index that saturates is a lost channel, not a result.
4. **Enumeration precedes intervention** (section 5, Fig 11 :44): you read the habitat off the world before deciding which actor, if any, to import; and you record the provenance of every actor you add.
