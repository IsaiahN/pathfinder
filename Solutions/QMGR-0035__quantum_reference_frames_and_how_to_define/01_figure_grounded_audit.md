## 0. The residual in effect terms

Not "which observable is correct in quantum gravity". The residual is the failure that registers when two descriptions related by a symmetry disagree on a reading because one of them leans on an unstated background.

- **Arity.** At least two: a system and a reference subsystem, or a clock and a system. A single system with no reference has no reading. This is why the physics literature speaks of relational or complete observables rather than local field values.
- **Symmetry.** The quantity must be invariant under a group acting on both systems (translations, rotations, boosts, or the diffeomorphism group of general relativity). The transform between frames is a group element, and the frame transform of the Symbols table is *"The map between allocentric and egocentric at one scale"* and *"Cannot be run without a pose."*
- **Scale.** Within one scale. The Symbols table separates `F` (frame transform, within scale) from `T_A` and `T_E` (abstraction and concretisation, between scales). This question is about `F`, not about a transition between levels.
- **What an answer would look like, bound before the run:** a non-updating anchor; a per-slot quantity that is a bond between at least two physical systems and is invariant under the symmetry; a transform between frames that names the pose of the reference system. If no figure line closes the last item, the honest result is `gap`.

## 3. Findings F1 to F9, each with verbatim quotes and a refuter

### F1 A fixed background fails the anchor test (triangulated)

Fig 2 :29: **"An anchor is whatever settles the question and does not change its answer because of what any frame thinks."** Fig 2 :30: **"If it moves when you push on it, it is not an anchor."** Fig 2 :47: **"A ground listed as one of the parties has already become a frame."**

A fixed background coordinate grid is a stipulation inside the description. It cannot be the thing that settles which reading is correct, because its own value changes with the coordinate convention. In a diffeomorphism-invariant theory it is gauge, not ground. The figures and the physics agree here for different reasons: the figures by the anchor test, physics by diffeomorphism invariance. Low overlap, so `triangulated`.

**Refuter:** exhibit a fixed structure whose value changes under a frame change and which nevertheless settles a physical reading without a second subsystem. If that works, F1 fails.

### F2 An observable is a per-slot bond between at least two systems (triangulated)

Fig 1 :19: **"The residual is indexed by object slot, not aggregated."** Fig 1 :22: **"Aggregating across slots is how a live signal disappears into an average."** Fig 2 :45: **"Frames range against the ground. They do not negotiate with it."** Fig 12 :5: **"A set of ingredients is not one. The bond is the rest of the information, and it is the half nothing was recording."**

A single-system absolute quantity is not a reading: it needs a reference against which to range. The invariant quantity is a relation between system and reference, indexed per slot, not a global scalar averaged over frames. This is the figure-level shape of the Dirac / complete / relational observable construction. The ingredients of system plus reference are not the quantity; the typed bond between them is. Fig 12 :94: **"The arrangement is the substance."**

**Refuter:** produce an absolute, single-system quantity that is invariant under the relevant symmetry and settles a measurement without naming a reference subsystem. Then F2 fails.

### F3 The continuum and the fixed background are derivatives, not primitives (single-source on the figure side, world corroboration separate)

Fig 13 :43: **"A continuum was the seventh and was removed for failing exactly this: it is a spectrum without gaps, or smoothness over space and time."** Fig 13 :74: **"Everything else is a composition across the two kinds, never within one."** Fig 13 :75: **"Two states never bond directly. It is not space-time but space, a structure, time; and two structures join only through a state."**

The smooth background spacetime of textbook physics is, in the figure's own terms, a spectrum without gaps or smoothness over space and time. It was removed from the minimum set because a generating set must not contain what its own members produce. So "no fixed background" is not an exotic constraint added from outside: under the six-term substrate a fixed smooth continuum is already a derivative, and promoting it to a primitive would be a category error. This is `single-source` on the figure side (Fig 13 only), corroborated by the physics of background independence in section 5.

**Refuter:** derive the continuum from the other five terms and show the set must admit it as a seventh member, or show the six-term set cannot compose a smooth metric-like derivative at all. Either breaks F3.

### F4 Gauge fixing is a convention and must not decide the outcome (triangulated)

Fig 10 :16: **"the convention does not fix the bug"**, continued :17 *"it makes the bug statable"*. Fig 10 :47: **"a convention that decides an outcome has stopped being one"**. Fig 10 :49: **"Declaring a meaning does not ground it."**

Choosing coordinates, fixing a gauge, or declaring what the reference frame's zero is, are conventions. Their legitimate role is to make a relational claim statable. The failure is to let the convention settle which value the observable takes. The complete-observable construction in the physics literature has exactly this shape: a partial observable evaluated where the clock variable reads a chosen value, with the choice of clock system carrying the physical meaning and the gauge label carrying none.

**Refuter:** show a gauge or coordinate convention that changes the value of a properly constructed relational observable while leaving the physical situation and reference reading fixed. Then F4 fails.

### F5 The frame transform needs a pose; superposed frames are where the figures stop (`gap`)

Symbols table :80-82: *"F ... The map between allocentric and egocentric at one scale. Distinct from T_A and T_E, which run between scales. Cannot be run without a pose."* Symbols table :111-112: *"A frame is any bounded system with a library: an agent, a person, a discipline, a model. It is a theory with a signature rather than a coordinate system, so frames differ in what they can express and not in how they label it, and there is no known transform between two of them."*

There is no figure line that writes the transform between two frames when the pose itself is in quantum superposition. The nearest figure limit is Fig 9 :32: **"you get a kind of thing, never the answer itself"**, and Fig 13 :134: **"Unreachable is a verdict here, not a silence"**. So the correct figure-level result for the exact quantum frame transform is `gap`, of the underivable-in-principle kind relative to this figure set. The world literature does supply candidate transforms, but as secondary corroboration only, and it does not turn the figure gap into a figure result (Fig 6 :21: **"MINT composes inside the closure and can never add an atom."**).

**Refuter:** point to a figure line that returns the transformation law between frames in a superposition of poses. That would close the gap and change its status; it would not refute the rest of the shape. A genuine refutation of this finding would be a ground measurement in which a frame in superposed poses returns a relational value that cannot be written as any superposition of the held transforms.

### F6 The pose-reading link has no instrument in the figures

Fig 3 :27: **"A link with no instrument is not a link that works. It is a link nobody has looked at."** Fig 6 :32: **"An instrument is not built from a description. It is improved from a worse instrument already returning something."**

The figures give `F` a precondition (a pose) but no instrument that reads a pose off a quantum reference system. Following Fig 6 :32, an instrument would have to be improved from one already returning something. In the world literature the role is played by covariant positive operator valued measures and localisation of the reference system (Loveridge, Miyadera and Busch 2018), and by clock-state orthogonality in the Page-Wootters style (Adlam 2025). That is a world instrument, not a figure instrument. It stays in the WORLD section.

**Refuter:** exhibit a figure-described instrument whose output is a pose reading for a quantum frame. Then F6 dissolves.

### F7 QRF constructions rearrange held atoms; they do not add a primitive

Fig 13 :115: **"The set is closed. The arrangements are not. That gap is why import exists."** Fig 13 :116: *"Six members, and no amount of arranging produces a seventh: a new member would have to be a new kind, and composition only ever rearranges the kinds already held."* Fig 6 :23: **"No frame escapes its own closure. The bound moves only on contact with a frame whose closure differs."**

The reference subsystem is already physical (a state inside the universe), so promoting it to a frame is a rebonding of held atoms, not an import that moves the wall. The world literature's "reference frame is a physical system" is therefore compatible with the substrate, but it does not add a seventh term. Fig 8 :53: **"Residual first, frame second."** The residual was described before any frame was chosen in section 0.

**Refuter:** show that a quantum reference frame construction requires a primitive not among the six, or that it changes the closure wall. Then F7 fails.

### F8 A frame that does not change contact is not doing work

Fig 11 :39: *"capability is a property of agent-and-habitat, never of the agent alone"*; Fig 11 :40: **"An improvement that does not change contact changes nothing, however much it improves."** Fig 11 :48: **"You do not invent the list. You read it off the world, and what you cannot perceive or measure yet is the residual."**

A reference system is not an abstract coordinate label; it is a physical subsystem in contact with the system, with actors, conditions and relations enumerated from the world (boundary conditions, detector location, clock coupling). This is why a reference in a superposition of positions changes what can be read, and why an infinitely massive, perfectly decoupled frame is only a limiting idealisation (the large-mass limit in the world literature). Verdict `specified`, triangulated with Fig 2 :29.

**Refuter:** exhibit a frame that never couples to or correlates with the system yet determines a physical reading. Then F8 fails.

### F9 A universal recipe for observables is refused; the answer is a shape (single-source, confirmed by canonical usage)

Fig 7 :6: **"the limit: no last room"**; Fig 7 :13: *"agreement compounds, evidence does not"*; Fig 9 :44: **"Only a sealed room can be searched to the end, and no frame is a sealed room."** Fig 9 :60: **"Use filters for the budget and witnesses for the verdict. Never let a filter hand you a verdict."**

No figure line returns a unique observable, a unique clock, or a universal frame-transform algorithm. What survives is the shape: non-updating anchor, per-slot relational bond, explicit pose, recorded lineage. The particular choice of clock, group or relational coordinate is a binding from the physics frame and must be provenanced as such (Fig 8 :50-52: *"Convergent derivation and adopted import look identical in the contents. Only the record of where each came from separates them."*).

**Refuter:** a figure line that selects one observable or one frame uniquely for all background-independent theories. Then F9 fails.

## 4. Pre-registered refuter (written before the conclusion)

*"A falsifier written before the run is a design instrument, not only an honesty device."* The shape claim is refuted if any of the following holds:

1. A local, coordinate-dependent field value is shown to settle a measurement in a diffeomorphism-invariant theory with no reference subsystem (breaks F2).
2. The continuum or fixed background is derived from the other five terms and admitted as a seventh member of the minimum set (breaks F3).
3. A gauge or coordinate convention is shown to change the value of a properly constructed relational observable while the physical situation and reference reading stay fixed (breaks F4).
4. A ground measurement returns, from a frame in a superposition of poses, a relational value that cannot be written as any superposition of the held frame transforms (breaks F5).
5. An anchor is shown to change its answer when frames push on it and still settle the question (breaks F1, Fig 2 :30).

If any fires, the shape is withdrawn. Until one is run, nothing here is `grounded`.

The smooth background is a derivative, not a primitive; gauge fixing is a convention that makes the claim statable and must not decide the reading; the exact transform between frames in superposed poses is a declared `gap`.
