# Expert verification packet: the Tether on quantum reference frames

**What this is:** a claim in a form a quantum foundations or gravity researcher can check. Mode, derivation and refuters are stated before the conclusion, so a reader knows what to look for and what would settle it.

**Mode: `specified` on sub-claims C1 and C2, `general` on the structural claim C3, `gap` on the exact quantum frame transform, not `grounded`.** No measurement is claimed.

**Status:** a simulated derivation checked for internal consistency against the figures, not a ground reading. It cannot settle the question it reports on.

## 1. The claim in one paragraph

In a spacetime with no fixed background, an observable must be a per-slot bond between at least two physical systems, invariant under the relevant symmetry and stated relative to a named reference reading, with an anchor that does not update when the frame changes. A coordinate convention or gauge choice makes the statement possible but must not decide its value. The smooth continuum is a derivative in the six-term substrate, not a primitive. The frame transform presupposes a pose; the exact transform between quantum frames in superposed poses is a declared `gap`. The particular clock, symmetry group and relational coordinate are bindings with provenance, not results. What is offered is the shape, four refuters written in advance, and the instruments that would close it.

## 2. Three checkable sub-claims

### C1 The anchor test and the status of a fixed background (framework-internal + physics-facing)

- **Statement:** a fixed background coordinate grid cannot be the ground that settles a reading, because it changes with the frame; a gauge convention makes a claim statable but must not decide an outcome.
- **Source, verbatim:** Fig 2 :29 *"An anchor is whatever settles the question and does not change its answer because of what any frame thinks."* Fig 2 :47 *"A ground listed as one of the parties has already become a frame."* Fig 10 :47 *"a convention that decides an outcome has stopped being one"*; Fig 10 :16 *"the convention does not fix the bug"* (continued :17 *"it makes the bug statable"*).
- **What to check:** is the diffeomorphism gauge label of a coordinate point a ground or a convention? Does the complete-observable construction (partial observable evaluated at a clock reading) leave the physical value unchanged under a gauge transformation? The world comparator is Tambornino 2012, which notes that de-parameterisable clock models reintroduce *"a fundamental preferred reference frame"* when the clock is treated classically.
- **Refuter:** exhibit a coordinate-dependent field value that settles a measurement with no reference subsystem, or a gauge convention that changes a properly constructed relational value while the physical situation is fixed.

### C2 Relational observable as a priced bond, not an ingredient list (framework-internal + physics-facing)

- **Statement:** an observable is a typed relation between system and reference, indexed per slot, not a global average. Two choices of relational coordinate over the same atoms are isomers or functional-group members, not the same substance under different spelling.
- **Source, verbatim:** Fig 1 :19 *"The residual is indexed by object slot, not aggregated."* Fig 12 :5 *"A set of ingredients is not one. The bond is the rest of the information, and it is the half nothing was recording."* Fig 12 :117 *"isomer. Same atoms, different bond, different substance. The difference is invisible in the formula and decisive in the behaviour."* Fig 12 :94 *"The arrangement is the substance."*
- **What to check:** is the Dirac / complete / relational observable (a partial observable evaluated where a clock variable reads a value) faithful to this shape? Does absolute position become a good approximation only when the reference is suitably localised? The world comparator is Loveridge, Miyadera and Busch 2018, which defines observables as *"symmetry-invariant quantities relating one system to another"*. Check whether two distinct relativisation choices are distinguishable in the predicted statistics; if they are not, the bond claim is empty.
- **Refuter:** produce an absolute single-system quantity invariant under the symmetry that settles a measurement without a reference; or show that changing the relational coordinate over the same atoms never changes the substance.

### C3 The continuum derivative and the pose precondition (framework-internal derivation)

- **Statement:** a smooth continuum is a composition, not a member of the minimum set, and the frame transform requires a pose that the figures do not supply for a quantum superposition of poses. That sub-question is `gap`, and an honest gap is a verdict.
- **Source, verbatim:** Fig 13 :43 *"A continuum was the seventh and was removed for failing exactly this: it is a spectrum without gaps, or smoothness over space and time."* Fig 13 :134 *"Unreachable is a verdict here, not a silence"*. Symbols table on `F`: *"The map between allocentric and egocentric at one scale"* and *"Cannot be run without a pose."*
- **What to check:** does the six-term substrate's removal of the continuum match the claim? Is there any figure line that returns the transform for a superposed-pose frame? If there is, the gap closes. If a ground measurement returns a value not writable as any superposition of held transforms, the shape fails.
- **Refuter:** derive the continuum as a seventh member; or point to a figure line supplying the quantum pose transform; or produce the ground measurement just described.

## 3. What is explicitly not claimed

- Not a unique observable, clock, group or universal transformation algorithm. No last room (Fig 7 :6).
- Not a derivation of general relativity or of any quantum gravity programme.
- Not that gauge fixing is forbidden; it makes the claim statable (Fig 10 :16-17).
- Not that the reference frame must be classical; a massive classical frame is a limiting idealisation.
- Not that the world QRF formalism is a figure result. It is secondary corroboration with provenance recorded in `10_source_ledger.csv`.
- Not `grounded`. No figure instrument reads a quantum pose and no measurement was taken.
