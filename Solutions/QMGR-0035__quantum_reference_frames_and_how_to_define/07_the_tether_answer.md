## 1. The residual it answers

In a generally covariant theory a field value at a coordinate point changes under the symmetry, so it cannot settle a physical reading. The failure is not "we lack the right equation", it is that two symmetry-related descriptions disagree because one has smuggled in a background convention (union compound of definition and transformation, Fig 9 :54). The Tether answers the shape of any acceptable answer, not the answer itself.

## 3. What it explicitly does not say

- Not a unique observable, a unique clock, or a universal frame transformation algorithm. No last room (Fig 7 :6).
- Not a derivation of general relativity or of any particular quantum gravity programme. The figures remove the continuum from the primitive set; they do not derive Einstein's equations.
- Not that gauge fixing is forbidden. It makes the claim statable; it must not decide the value.
- Not that a reference frame must be classical or infinitely massive. That is a limiting idealisation in the physics frame (Fig 11 :40: *"An improvement that does not change contact changes nothing, however much it improves."*).
- Not `grounded`. No instrument in the figure set reads a quantum pose, and no measurement was taken.
