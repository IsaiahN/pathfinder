## 1. A shape is a constraint, not a filler

The canonical figures README states the limit: the framework returns "what a missing thing would have to look like (a shape, not a name)." Fig 9 :32: "you get a kind of thing, never the answer itself." Fig 13 :134: "Unreachable is a verdict here, not a silence." Fig 13 :138-139: the system "can say the answer is out of reach with the library as it stands, so something has to be imported before it is asked again... you get a kind of thing, never the answer itself."

From *The Library of Babel* (carried): "You can cut your search short if the set of books returned is small and descriptive enough that it provides a general shape of the answer that satisfies your question."

For COG-0074 the shape is: *the effect of responsibilities is routed through contact, read per slot, gated by the dependency chain, relative to the residual, arrangement-sensitive, and a union compound.* That is a set of constraints on any answer. It is not an answer, and it does not name chores, caregiving or paid work as "the" cause.

## 2. How a shape is obtained

- *Library of Babel* (carried): use the vocabulary already closest to the thing, look for recurring new terms that overlap with it, recurse. "With each new term gained, you narrow the space and deepen your vocabulary."
- Fig 6 :30: "The description travels. Fails by being sharpened into terms only we can read. Cast it in effect terms." Fig 9 :30-31: "your words are borrowed too, so describe the effect rather than the cause."
- *Decoding Tacit Knowledge* (carried): "When contact isn't possible, the best you can send is a plain description of what you'd expect to see, because anyone can check that against the world."
- Applied here: describe a responsibility by what it changes (actors, currency, anchor), not by its folk name ("chores"). Then ask which slot moves.

## 3. What licenses a name

Only two things, both outside the shape itself:

1. **The ground settles it.** Formula step 5: "the ground settles it. Verified only by what pays." The README: the framework "stops at the limit of what the available instruments can distinguish, and records the lineage so the stop is honest."
2. **A provenanced import from a frame whose closure differs** (Fig 6 :23, Fig 8 :50-52), with a source gate and a shadow test.

*They're Scanning Books* (carried): "The real import is not a new term in the existing space. It is a new dimension of the space." And: "A minimum viable set of six terms is a human's careful guess at reality's compression axes; another sensorium's minimum set need not be those six." Also: "Declaring the primitives does not make them neutral. It makes the choice visible."

For this question, "responsibility" is a folk bundle, not a primitive of the framework. Naming a specific responsibility as the cause of a slot is a binding from the developmental-psychology frame onto a world description. Until the ground pays for it, that binding is the searcher's own contribution and must be recorded as such.

## 4. Why "one maturity score" is the specific forbidden error here

*The Hard Problem is About Source* (carried) gives the mechanism. Metamerism: an instrument reads green and "cannot comment on the source" - one green light, or yellow plus blue, or RGB, or CMYK. The reading does not determine the source. "If several paths create the same behavior, then behavior is a projection with lossy information." And: "An instrument that can't distinguish these cases demonstrates its own inadequacy." It was the spectrometer, not an argument, that resolved it.

Fig 1 :22: "Aggregating across slots is how a live signal disappears into an average." A single "maturity" score is the metamerism error in this question: several different responsibility-to-slot profiles collapse to one reading, and the instrument (the score) cannot comment on which profile produced it. What is owed is a discriminator per slot, not a better adjective.

*The Recursive Self-Improvement Trap* (carried): "the output is only as good as the validator. The agent, or the tool, or the frame, or the theory, cannot validate itself."

## 5. The specific answer to "what shape does this question have"

**What licenses a specific responsibility-to-slot effect? Nothing in the framework.** The framework returns a shape. A specific effect is licensed only by the ground settling a minted term (per-slot measurement), or by a provenanced import with a shadow test. Until one of those, the name (chores, caregiving, paid work) is a world-frame binding and must be recorded as such.

**Does the common framing commit the forbidden error?** Yes. "Responsibilities build independence and cooperation" reads a single source (a score) off a projection (observed competence and helpful behaviour) and names the source without an instrument that discriminates among responsibility types. The correct form is the shape, with the slot marked open: `responsibility -> (which contact changed?) -> slot ???, refuter written in advance`.

## 5b. Correction to my own §3 and §4

I initially overstated "shape, not a name" as if it forbade naming any responsibility at all. The README's phrase is "what a **missing** thing would have to look like (a shape, not a name)." It governs a term not held in the library. Naming an atom already held, or a world-frame entity with provenance, is not the forbidden move. What remains wrong in the common framing is the metamerism point (§4): a single score names the source of a reading without discriminating among sources. So §4 stands, but the reason is the missing per-slot discriminator, not a general ban on naming. Both claims are recorded so neither is quietly lost. (This mirrors exemplar `05` §5b and `06` Q1, carried locally.)

## 6. Two consequences for this question

**Prestige is not action.** *The Recursive Self-Improvement Trap* (carried): "Prestige must never be allowed to validate an action directly... Prestige can fund an action. It can give an action a budget to be tried. But it cannot settle an action. Only the ground can settle it. The Ground Maintainer must be blind to prestige." A campaign that gathers well-known developmental psychologists to agree that chores are good is the named collapse, not corroboration. The legitimate ask is a budget to try the per-slot claim against data.

**My own audit is a simulation, not a ground reading.** Same essay (carried): "LLMs can quickly simulate an approximate answer/output... However, the truly testable output would need to be derived from the real architecture or working the formula yourself." LLM output is "simulacra." So `01` is a simulated derivation checked for internal consistency against the figures. It is **not** a test of the developmental question and must be labelled as such. It cannot settle anything it reports on.
