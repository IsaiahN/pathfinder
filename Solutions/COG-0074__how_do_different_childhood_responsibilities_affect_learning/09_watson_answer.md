Predicts that responsibility builds learning only with tapered support inside the zone; corroborates Fig 3's chain gating. Overlap: low.
- **Evolutionary biology / animal behaviour.** Wikipedia contributors, "Cooperative breeding" (Wikipedia, accessed 2026-11-22): "The evolution of cooperative breeding in early Homo species also promoted other pro-social behaviors such as social learning, increased social tolerance, and shared intentionality especially in food acquisition." Overlap: low.

## VERDICT (from the figures)

The shape survives; the specific answer is unreached. A responsibility acts only by changing what a child contacts, and a duty that changes no contact changes nothing. The effect is read in three separate slots, because learning, independence and cooperation are different bonds and a single maturity score loses the signal. A responsibility builds skill only through an intact dependency chain; no responsibility has a fixed valence; arrangement of responsibilities matters as much as their ingredients; the three outcomes form a union compound; and what crosses scales is a method, not a recording. The framework returns a shape, not the named responsibility or effect size. Four refuters were written before the run in `01` §5: a no-contact-change duty that still moves a slot; a lossless global score; responsibility that succeeds above a broken link; or a fixed, residual-independent valence. This is `general` on the shape and `specified` on slot application, ungrounded, candidate.

- **cost if wrong:** bounded and locatable. The shape claim is modest (necessity of contact, per-slot separation, chain gating, relative valence). If wrong, the error is findable because the packet states mode, refuters and provenance before the conclusion. The real cost is spending the independence of outside readers by sending a consensus-seeking argument instead of a checkable shape; Fig 2 :42 "Buy independence in proportion to the cost of being wrong." That cost is avoidable by leading with the shape and its refuter.
- **undoable:** yes. The specific bindings can be added or removed without touching the shape; a new figure re-census or a per-slot measurement updates it. No primitive has to be withdrawn. A sent claim can be superseded by a corrected packet.
- **reversed by:** a ground reading, not agreement. A per-slot instrument showing that a no-contact-change duty moves a slot, a lossless global score, success above a broken chain link, or a fixed valence would each break a specific finding. A registered report settling a responsibility-to-slot mapping with the refuter written first would move part of the answer toward `grounded`. Prestige or field agreement is not such an instrument.
