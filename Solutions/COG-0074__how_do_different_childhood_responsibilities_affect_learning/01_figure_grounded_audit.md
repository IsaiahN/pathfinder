## 0. The question under audit, restated as a residual

> "How do different childhood responsibilities affect learning, independence, and cooperation?" (cognitive science.txt :91)

In effect terms: a child is a bounded system with a library (skills, terms for what a task affords, norms about who does what). A responsibility is a change to that system's habitat: new actors (a younger sibling, a household routine), new conditions (a deadline, a resource to manage), new relations (I am now the one who does this, someone depends on me). The residual R is the gap between what the child's current library predicts about those demands and what actually happens. The question asks what shape the effect of varying responsibilities takes in three distinct slots: learning (a new term is minted and settles), independence (the child acts without external direction), cooperation (the child contributes to a shared outcome).

What each option leads to:

- **Option A, the common framing.** "Responsibilities build character." This treats the three slots as one output, adds a trait to the child in isolation, and averages away the per-slot signal. It is a `general` verdict with no ground reading, and it selects for the correlation that disqualifies a verdict (Seat Map :163).
- **Option B, the figure-derived framing.** Responsibilities act only through contact; the effect is per slot; it runs through a dependency chain; the valence of a responsibility is relative to the residual; the three outcomes are a union compound; the framework returns a shape, not a name. B is checkable and states what would refute it.

What an answer would look like, bound as an ordinary term (formula "Direction"): a per-slot mapping from a described responsibility (actors, currency, anchor changed) to a predicted direction and a named refuter, with mode stated. Until that mapping is settled against data, it is `specified` at best.

## 1. Domain census before choosing any figure line

**R by arity, symmetry, scale.** Arity: three outcome slots plus a family of responsibility conditions, so R is indexed per slot, not a single number (Fig 1 :19). Symmetry: the slots are not interchangeable and responsibilities are not symmetric; order and arrangement carry information (Fig 12 :112). Scale: one child (intra-personal loop), the family habitat (contact and resource flow), the culture or institution (norms and schooling). Methods cross between scales; recordings do not (Fig 4 :22).

One line per domain, with actors / currency / anchor. No nameable anchor means it is not yet a domain.

| domain | actors | currency | anchor | type |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| **D1 Individual learning** | the child, the task, task feedback | residual R per slot; "mint φ if the description length pays" (Fig 1 :16) | observable competence (an assessment, a demonstration of what the child can do) | partition boundary: which compositions cross from affordance to skill |
| **D2 Family / habitat** | caregivers, siblings, the household, the child as helper | contact, contribution, resource flow | the habitat as field of contact; capability is agent-and-habitat (Fig 11 :39) | union with D3 (overlap: the child-as-helper is both a family actor and a social actor) |
| **D3 Socialization / cooperation** | the child, peers, community, norms | contribution, trust, shared intentionality | actual cooperative behaviour; "Alignment is triangulation, not negotiation" (Fig 2 :35) | union with D2: a child in two conditions is a specific compound (Fig 9 :57) |
| **D4 Research / verification** | researchers, studies, journals | agreement and residual | data, never agreement; "Bring a characterised residual, never the source" (Fig 10 :52) | separate; in this simulated run all seats draw on one evidence pool |

**Partition or union.** D1 and D4 are separate. D2 and D3 are a union compound, not a partition: a child who has high responsibilities and high support is not a harder case of either condition but a different object (Fig 9 :57-58). This must be unbundled before searching (Fig 9 :54).

**Order by dependency (Fig 3).** Settle perception of the affordance before vocabulary for it; vocabulary before an objective; objective before a plan; a plan before carrying what worked (Fig 3 :4-16). In D2, settle contact before capability (Fig 11 :40). In D4, describe the residual before naming a frame (Fig 9 :27-28). Effort spent above a break arrives at a step that cannot use it (Fig 3 :19-20).

## 3. Findings F1 to F9

### F1 - Responsibilities act through contact, not through the child in isolation (decisive, necessary not sufficient)

Fig 11 :39-40:

> Fig 11 :40 "A system cannot be made more capable by improving the agent in isolation. An improvement that does not change contact changes nothing, however much it improves."

Fig 11 :39: "capability is a property of agent-and-habitat, never of the agent alone."

A responsibility is a change to the habitat: it adds actors (a sibling to care for), conditions (a routine to keep), and relations (someone depends on this being done). So the effect on learning, independence and cooperation is a property of the child-and-habitat system. A duty that changes no contact at all (a token chore with no real consequence, a task with no feedback and no dependency) changes nothing, "however much it improves" the child's schedule. This is a **necessary** condition, not a sufficient one: many contact changes harm a given slot (a heavy caregiving load can feed cooperation and cut into study time). Verdict `general`, triangulated across Fig 11 and two low-overlap world frames (cooperative breeding, parental care) that independently place capability in the contact system. Strained after re-census: none.

### F2 - The effect is per slot, never averaged

Fig 1 :19 "The residual is indexed by object slot, not aggregated." And Fig 1 :22 "Aggregating across slots is how a live signal disappears into an average."

Learning, independence and cooperation are three slots with different currencies. A responsibility can light one and leave another dark. A single "child development" or "maturity" score throws away the only signal that says where to look. Verdict `general`, single-source for the figure rule (Fig 1), decisive for the method. World corroboration is partial: self-determination theory distinguishes autonomy, competence and relatedness as separate needs, but it is a different taxonomy, so overlap is low, not zero.

### F3 - Responsibilities only build competence if the dependency chain is intact

Fig 3 :20 "The chain is ordered by dependency, not by importance. Each link consumes what the one above produces..." And Fig 3 :27 "A link with no instrument is not a link that works. It is a link nobody has looked at."

Perception (can the child see what the task affords) -> vocabulary (does the child have terms for what can be moved, opened, joined) -> objective (can the child say what counts as doing it well) -> planning -> learn and carry. A responsibility assigned above a broken link cannot be diagnosed by adding more of it; only its predecessor can (Fig 3 :19, "Optimising a link that already works cannot rescue one that does not."). This is the figure's structural form of the zone of proximal development: responsibility within reach, with a more knowledgeable other and tapered support, builds skill; responsibility far beyond reach produces motion without learning. Verdict `specified`, triangulated across Fig 3 and the ZPD world source (low overlap: educational psychology vs a domain-agnostic metatheory).

### F4 - Valence is relative to the residual, not a property of the responsibility

Fig 11 :49 "An actor's valence is relative to the residual, not a property it carries." And Fig 11 :50 "The same actor is a benefactor to one residual and a malfactor to another, so mutualism and parasitism are relations rather than kinds."

No responsibility is inherently good or bad. The same act of caring for a younger sibling may feed cooperation (someone is relied on) and starve learning (hours removed from study), or feed both if the care itself is structured as a learning task. So "chores build character" is a `general` claim with no residual named and must be `specified` to a slot. Self-determination theory corroborates the mechanism at low overlap: the same task supports autonomy when informational and autonomy-supportive, and undermines it when controlling. Verdict `general`, triangulated (Fig 11 + SDT).

### F5 - Arrangement matters, not just ingredients

Fig 12 :112 "A plan is a sequence. A goal is a comparison. Progress is a subtraction. A refutation is a negation, and that one is not a bond." And Fig 12 :111 "A vocabulary without bonds can name a state and cannot name a route to one."

Which responsibilities, in what order, with what feedback and support, matter as much as whether responsibilities exist at all. The same ingredients (a chore, a care duty, self-care) under a different bond (scaffolded and tapered versus imposed and unexamined) give a different developmental substance. This is Fig 12's isomer point, developed in `06_isomers_and_order.md`. Verdict `specified`, triangulated (Fig 12 + SDT's informational versus controlling feedback + ZPD's tapered scaffolding), low overlap between the three.

### F6 - The three outcomes are a union compound, not three separate questions

Fig 9 :57 "a case in two conditions is not a harder case of either. It stops being a mixture and becomes a specific compound." And Fig 9 :54 "And when the split lands on a union rather than a partition, unbundle before you search again."

A child with high responsibilities and high support is not "more of each"; it is a specific compound with its own loop. A single-lever answer ("add responsibility") clears only the region where one condition holds and leaves the intersections standing, because the second condition still binds. Verdict `general`, single-source for the union rule (Fig 9); the compound is real but its contents are not derivable from the figures.

### F7 - Settlement needs the ground, not agreement

Fig 2 :35 "Alignment is triangulation, not negotiation. A mirror cannot correct you." And Fig 2 :40 "agreement about the world is worthless when the agreeing frames share an evidence pool." And Fig 10 :52 "Bring a characterised residual, never the source."

A field consensus that "responsibility is good for children" is not a settlement. Two studies citing the same review are one witness wearing two names. What would settle the shape is a measurement per slot: an assessment of competence, a behavioural measure of cooperation, a longitudinal or experimental design with the responsibility described in effect terms. Verdict `general` on the method; no `grounded` claim is made.

### F8 - What crosses scales is a method, not a recording

Fig 4 :22 "Only methods cross up. Only head starts come down." And Fig 4 :30 "So take the round trip and measure the gap. Without it you are navigating by dead reckoning: confident about a position nobody checked."

A responsibility hands the child a method (how to plan, how to coordinate, how to name what worked) that can be reapplied at the next scale. It does not hand over a recording of one occasion. The claim "this chore produced independence in this family" is a recording; the mechanism worth sending is the method plus the refuter. Any condensation of the figures into a slogan ("contact builds capability") carries a round-trip gap R_T that must be enumerated, not assumed zero (measured in `11_audit.md`).

### F9 - The framework returns a shape, not a name

Fig 9 :32 "you get a kind of thing, never the answer itself." The canonical figures README: "what a missing thing would have to look like (a shape, not a name)."

The figures can say: effect via contact, per slot, chain-dependent, valence-relative, arrangement-sensitive, union compound, ground-settled. They cannot say which responsibility (chores versus caregiving versus paid work) produces which effect size. That needs a ground reading. This is an honest stop, not a silence: Fig 13 :134 "Unreachable is a verdict here, not a silence." Status here is `unreached at this budget`, not `unreachable in principle` (Fig 8 :40-43).

## 5. Pre-registered refuter (written before the conclusion)

Required by Seat Map :287: "a falsifier written before the run is a design instrument, not only an honesty device." The shape claim is refuted if any of the following holds:

1. A responsibility that demonstrably changes no contact (no new actors, conditions, relations, or feedback) still changes one of the three slots (breaks Fig 11 :40 necessity).
2. A single global "development" or "maturity" score predicts each slot as well as separate scores with no loss of signal (breaks Fig 1 :19-22 per-slot indexing).
3. Adding responsibility above a demonstrably broken dependency-chain link still produces competent action (breaks Fig 3 :19-27).
4. The same responsibility is shown to have a fixed, residual-independent valence across all three slots in all arrangements (breaks Fig 11 :49-50).
5. A ground reading settles a specific responsibility-to-slot mapping with the refuter already fired and not revived.

If any fires, the relevant finding is withdrawn and the census is redone. Until one is run, nothing here is labelled `grounded`.

## 6. Recommendation

1. Lead with the shape and the refuter, not with a verdict about chores (Fig 10 :52, Fig 9 :27-28).
2. Report learning, independence and cooperation separately; never publish a single "responsibility builds maturity" score (Fig 1 :22).
3. Describe each responsibility in effect terms (which actors, currency, anchor changed) before choosing a literature or a frame (Fig 9 :27-28).
4. Ask a checker what they would count as a failure before showing them the framework (Fig 10 :42, Seat Map :163).
5. Use the public submission and contact points in `03_public_contact_points.csv`; ask for a refutation, not belief.
6. Stop at the instruments. Name the ground that would close each slot; do not manufacture an effect size.
