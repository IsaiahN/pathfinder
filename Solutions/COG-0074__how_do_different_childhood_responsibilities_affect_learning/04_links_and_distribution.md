## Two things in the wider literature that bear directly on this question

1. **Scaffolding is tapered, not permanent.** The ZPD page says aid is reduced "as it becomes unnecessary" and that the hardest tasks a learner can do with scaffolding give the greatest learning gains. Read against Fig 3's dependency chain, this is a world-frame statement of the same shape: responsibility lands inside a zone only when the support structure is present and removable. This is corroboration, not a figure derivation; the figure line is Fig 3 :20, not Vygotsky.
2. **Prosocial effects are a by-product of a contact system, not a direct instruction effect.** The cooperative-breeding page reports that cooperative care promoted social learning, shared intentionality and coordination benefits as a by-product. Read against Fig 11 :40, this is a low-overlap frame agreeing that the effect lives in the agent-and-habitat system. Agreement from such a different field is worth having; agreement from another developmental-psychology page citing the same review would not be.

## Distribution: offer, do not push

Figure-grounded reasons:

- **Fig 4 :22** "Only methods cross up. Only head starts come down." Sending a full argument before anyone has described a residual sends a recording, not a generator. Lead with the per-slot claim and its refuter; offer the links after.
- **Fig 10 :42** "A verifier that can reconstruct the claim cannot verify it." A recipient who has absorbed the whole framework before being asked for a verdict has joined the frame being checked.
- **Fig 9 :27-28** "describe it before you go looking, or any frame you pick will seem to fit." Ask what they would expect to see in each slot if the claim were true before giving them the literature.
- **Seat Map :163** "High correlation is disqualifying for a verdict and efficient for a convention." A send selected for people who already think chores are good produces a convention, not corroboration.
- **Fig 2 :42** "Buy independence in proportion to the cost of being wrong." A wrong developmental claim sent to a broad audience has a real cost; the independent check is worth more than the size of the audience.

**Recommended order in a reply:** (1) the per-slot shape claim and its refuter; (2) ask what they would count as a failure in each slot; (3) only then the three figure links, with the note that the figures, not the summary, are the authority; (4) only if asked, the formula as a method to reuse with their own refuter written first.

## The two-stage offer (author's clarification carried from the exemplar)

1. **Stage 1:** validate the shape output against the ground (per slot, in effect terms, with the refuter in front). Record the verdict before any theory is shown. This is Fig 10 :52 and Fig 2 :45.
2. **Stage 2, opt-in only:** if curious, read the framework that produced it. Seat 4 must not hold the interiors; later agreement is never pooled as corroboration (Fig 2 :40).
3. **Stage 3:** use the formula recursively on other problems, writing a fresh refuter and stating a mode per problem. Fig 4 :22 permits sending a method.

**Hard precondition:** stages 1 to 3 only work if the stage-1 output survives. As currently drafted it states only a shape and explicitly declines the empirical effect size, so it is checkable but it does not yet answer the question at the ground. That is the honest position, not a defect to hide.
