## QUESTION

Decide, in effect terms, whether a sovereign's debt path is serviceable without a future break, and choose the instrument that reports it. Option A: a single debt-to-GDP threshold is the verdict. Option B: sustainability is a per-slot, ground-anchored comparison over a named habitat, reported as a shape until a ground settles a name. What each leads to: A hides a live rollover residual inside an average and promotes a filter into a witness; B keeps the live slots visible, ranges against realized servicing, returns five written refuters, and reports the universal threshold as a `gap` rather than inventing a number. The question bundles two conditions with different anchors, so the verdicts are kept separate and never averaged.

## DOMAIN CENSUS

R by arity, symmetry and scale, then one line per domain with actors, currency and anchor, partition or union, and order by dependency.

- **R (the residual).** The distance between what a fiscal model predicts (path stabilizes, financing needs met) and what the ground returns (rollover succeeds or fails, spreads move, restructuring, inflation, forced adjustment). Arity: multiple slots (maturity, currency, creditor class, contingent liabilities, primary balance). Symmetry: comparisons, not symmetric joins. Scale: first order per slot, second order at the sovereign. Fig 9 names a compound: a case in two conditions is not a harder case of either.
- **Domain 1, sovereign fiscal mechanics.** Actors: treasury, tax authority, debt manager, bondholders. Currency: cash flows (revenue, interest, principal, rollover). Anchor: realized servicing, a market or counter that does not move because a model moved. Question type: union of liquidity, solvency, institutional capacity and political feasibility. Order: first order.
- **Domain 2, measurement and forecasting frame.** Actors: DSA models, rating agencies, fiscal councils, debt/GDP, r-g, fiscal gap, S2. Currency: indicators and projections. Anchor: should be the ground; often an internal criterion. Question type: partition boundary (which indicator is admissible evidence). Order: second order, consumes D1 data.
- **Domain 3, verification and outreach frame.** Actors: expert readers, public fiscal institutions. Currency: agreement and residual. Anchor: the ground, never agreement. In this simulated run all seats draw on one evidence pool, so independence is zero. Question type: partition. Order: third order.
- **Refused candidate domain.** "Market sentiment as an anchor" is refused at census: a market that updates on frames is a frame, not a ground (Fig 2 :29-30, :34). It is folded into D2 as an instrument that can be wrong on unmeasured axes.
- **Order by dependency.** Perceive data, acquire vocabulary for what the fiscal situation affords, state the objective (what counts as sustainable), then plan, then carry. The vocabulary-to-objective link is the break (Fig 3 :27). More data above the break cannot rescue it (Fig 3 :19).
- **Strain test applied.** Fig 9 governs question splitting; Fig 11 governs contact enumeration. Different objects, so no contradiction after re-census.

## VERDICT (from the figures)

The shape survives; no universal threshold does. Public debt is sustainable when service capacity ranges above service obligation at every live slot against a ground that does not move when a model moves, for a sovereign whose habitat has been enumerated. The measure is a comparison per slot, not an average stock level. The framework returns a four-condition shape, five refuters written in advance, and the name of the missing instrument (a source discriminator); it returns no number, and the universal threshold is a declared `gap`. A rating is an instrument, not an anchor; agreement among similarly equipped frames is one pool, not corroboration; the inter-temporal budget constraint is a candidate criterion, not the ground. Verdicts per domain stay separate: D1 `specified` triangulated, D2 `specified` with one live strain, D3 `general` triangulated. Overall mode `specified`, ungrounded, candidate.

- **cost if wrong:** bounded and locatable. The packet states mode, refuters and provenance before the conclusion, so an error is findable rather than invisible. The real cost is spending the independence of outside readers by sending a threshold taxonomy instead of a characterised residual. Fig 2 :42 prices that: "Buy independence in proportion to the cost of being wrong." The cost is avoidable by leading with the shape claim and its five refuters.
- **undoable:** yes. A promoted threshold can be demoted back to a filter without touching the four-condition shape; no primitive has to be withdrawn. A sent claim can be superseded by a corrected packet. The comparison-bond type strain is also undoable by an instrument that settles whether second-order compositions count as quantities at that level.
- **reversed by:** a ground reading, not agreement. A rollover failure in one slot with the headline ratio unchanged settles the per-slot claim at the ground; a discriminator that names the source of a rollover-success reading closes the instrument gap; a cross-regime comparison of atom-identical sovereigns settles the habitat claim; any of the five refuters in `01` section 4 firing narrows or withdraws the shape. Until one of these fires, the verdict stands as `candidate`, and a `gap` remains a verdict rather than a silence.
