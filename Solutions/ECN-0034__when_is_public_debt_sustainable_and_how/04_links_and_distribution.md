## Wider literature that bears on the question

1. **The threshold finding and its refusal.** The Wikipedia Debt-to-GDP article records that Herndon, Ash and Pollin (2013, PERI Working Paper 322) found "coding errors, selective exclusion of available data, and unconventional weighting of summary statistics lead to serious errors that inaccurately represent the relationship between public debt and GDP growth among 20 advanced economies in the post-war period." This is a real refusal of a held term, which is what the Operators table calls negation. It does not by itself settle the shape; it shows that a threshold claim can be corrected by a ground-visible check.
2. **Operational definitions are not unique.** The Fiscal sustainability article notes the European Commission definition, the inter-temporal budget constraint, the fiscal gap and the S2 indicator, and explicitly says there is no consensus on a precise operational definition. That is world corroboration for the claim that any single number is a filter, not a witness (Fig 9 :60).
3. **The criterion can fail on its own terms.** The same article records "grave concerns" about the inter-temporal budget constraint and that under plausible assumptions it is "in fact not the correct criterion for sustainability." This is why an instrument, not an argument, is owed (Fig 6 :34-35).
4. **Institutional framing.** The IMF DSF explicitly tailors assessments "to country-specific circumstances," and the World Bank toolkit frames the problem as a balance between financing needs and repayment ability. Both are consistent with the habitat claim (Fig 11 :39), but both are also inside the economics evidence pool, so they are corroboration, not an independent witness.

**Overlap declaration.** Sources 1 and 2 are the same economics pool (three Wikipedia pages that cite overlapping literature). The three framework essays share the figure author's evidence pool completely, so their agreement with the figure verdict is expected and carries no independent witness weight. No world source conflicts with the figure verdict; the conflict rule was not triggered.

## Distribution: offer, do not push

Figure-grounded reasons:

- **Fig 10 :52:** "Bring a characterised residual, never the source." Sending a full threshold taxonomy before anyone has described a residual sends a recording, not a generator.
- **Fig 10 :42:** "A verifier that can reconstruct the claim cannot verify it." A recipient who has absorbed the framework before being asked for a verdict has joined the frame being checked.
- **Fig 9 :27-28:** "describe it before you go looking, or any frame you pick will seem to fit." Ask what they would expect to see if the shape claim were true before showing the sources.
- **Fig 2 :40:** "disqualifying for a verdict: agreement about the world is worthless when the agreeing frames share an evidence pool." Do not select recipients for likely agreement.
- **Fig 7 :35:** "A room's agreement is variation, not a verdict. Selection between rooms is where the ground votes."

**Recommended order in a reply:** (1) the shape claim and its five refuters; (2) ask what they would count as a failure and record the answer; (3) only then the three links, with the note that the figures, not the summary, are the authority.

## The two-stage offer

1. **Stage 1.** Validate the output against realized servicing, not against agreement. Keep the framework interior hidden.
2. **Stage 2, opt-in.** Offer the source links. Their later agreement is never pooled as corroboration (Fig 2 :40).
3. **Stage 3.** Offer the formula as a method to reuse, with the instruction that each user writes their own refuter and states their own mode. Fig 4 :22 allows methods to cross up: "Only methods cross up. Only head starts come down."

**Hard precondition:** stages 1 to 3 only work if the stage-1 output survives. It is a `specified` shape with an open threshold, not a number. If a recipient demands a single sustainable debt ratio, that is the `gap` the framework reports, and saying so is part of the honest output.
