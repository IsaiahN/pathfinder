- the **gap status of the universal threshold** is erased, turning a declared `gap` back into a number.

Conversely, one thing survives cleanly: the requirement that a judgement be a comparison over a named habitat against a ground that does not move. That is the residue worth sending. R_T was **not** measured numerically, because no ground reading exists; it is recorded qualitatively as the enumerated set of dropped constraints. Treating the formula as identical to the figure set would set R_T to zero and commit the dead-reckoning error Fig 4 :30 names. The honest form is a lossy condensation with a measured gap.
