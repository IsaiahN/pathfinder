## 1. A shape is a constraint, not a filler

The canonical figures README states the limit: the framework returns "what a missing thing would have to look like (a shape, not a name)." Fig 9 :32: "you get a kind of thing, never the answer itself." Fig 13 says it can characterise what is missing in effect terms, but "It cannot say which primitive is missing, because naming it would mean already holding it."

From *The Library of Babel* (opened directly): "You can cut your search short if the set of books returned is small and descriptive enough that it provides a general shape of the answer that satisfies your question."

So a **shape** is the narrowed form of an answer you can state before you can name it. It constrains; it does not fill. For this question:

- Shape: "sustainability is a per-slot, ground-anchored comparison over a named habitat."
- Name: "debt is sustainable at 60 percent of GDP."

The first is checkable and refutable. The second is a binding from the empirical frame presented as a derivation.

## 2. How a shape is obtained

- *Library of Babel* (opened directly): use the vocabulary you already have that is closest to the thing. "With each new term gained, you narrow the space by definition and deepen your vocabulary." Recurse until the candidates are few and descriptive.
- Fig 6 :30: "outward: does another frame already hold it? The description travels. Fails by being sharpened into terms only we can read. Cast it in effect terms." Fig 9 :30-31: "your words are borrowed too, so describe the effect" rather than the cause.
- *Decoding Tacit Knowledge* (carried): "When contact isn't possible, the best you can send is a plain description of what you'd expect to see, because anyone can check that against the world." For this question that becomes: if a sovereign is sustainable, you should see rollover succeed across maturity buckets without a forced adjustment, even when the headline ratio is unchanged.
- *Library of Babel* on stopping (opened directly): the answer is reached when "the finish line will also be the starting place", and the paradox is that by then you have "composed the answer through the process of locating it."

Applied here: the starting vocabulary was "debt-to-GDP," "r-g," "primary balance," "fiscal gap," "rollover," "currency of issue." Proximity search over the public literature narrowed these to a four-condition separator rather than a number. That separator is the shape.

## 3. What licenses a name

Only two things, both outside the shape itself:

1. **The ground settles it.** Formula step 5: "the ground settles it. Verified only by what pays. A gate is not the ground." The README: the framework "stops at the limit of what the available instruments can distinguish, and records the lineage so the stop is honest."
2. **A provenanced import from a frame whose closure differs** (Fig 6 :23, Fig 8 :50-52), with a source gate and a shadow test. A 60 percent or 90 percent threshold is exactly such an import: it comes from the empirical economics frame, needs provenance, and needs to explain a gap here before it can be used.

*They're Scanning Books* (carried) sharpens this: "The real import is not a new term in the existing space. It is a new dimension of the space." It also says "Declaring the primitives does not make them neutral. It makes the choice visible." The choice of which slots to enumerate (maturity, currency, creditor, contingent liability) is a visible choice, not a discovery of nature's own list.

## 4. Why a single reading cannot name the source

*The Hard Problem is About Source* (opened directly) gives the mechanism. An instrument reads green and "cannot comment on the source." One green light, or yellow plus blue, or RGB, or CMYK can produce the same reading. "If several paths create the same behavior, then behavior is a projection with lossy information." And "An instrument that can't distinguish these cases demonstrates its own inadequacy." It was the spectrometer, not an argument, that resolved it.

For debt sustainability the analogous readings are: a sovereign services its debt because its debt ratio is low, because r < g, because it issues in its own currency, because its creditor base is captive, or because a central bank backstopped it. A rollover success reading does not discriminate among these sources. Naming one from the reading is the metamerism error. What is owed is a discriminator, not a name.

The 2013 Herndon, Ash and Pollin correction recorded in `04_links_and_distribution.md` is the opposite case: a spreadsheet error was found, so a held term was refused. A refusal is a negation, which is why the operators table includes ¬ and why the pre-registered refuter in `01` section 4 matters.

## 5. Correction to my own §1 to §3 (scope of "shape, not a name")

I initially overstated the rule. The README's phrase is "what a **missing** thing would have to look like (a shape, not a name)." It governs a primitive not held in the library. The quantities that matter here (r, g, maturity, currency of issue, creditor class, primary balance, contingent liabilities) are **already available empirical quantities**. Naming them is a **binding from the economics frame** onto held atoms, not the invention of a new primitive.

What remains forbidden:

- inventing a seventh quantity and claiming the framework produced it;
- calling a universal threshold a derivation, when it is an import that adds no atom to the framework (Fig 6 :21: "MINT composes inside the closure and can never add an atom.");
- claiming the framework names which single threshold settles a given sovereign.

The metamerism point in §4 is **not** retracted. The reason is the missing discriminator, not a ban on naming held quantities. Both claims are recorded so neither is quietly lost. This correction is the direct analogue of the exemplar's own §5b correction.

## 6. Two consequences for this question

**Prestige is not action.** From *The Recursive Self-Improvement Trap* (opened directly): "Prestige must never be allowed to validate an action directly" and "Prestige can fund an action. It can give an action a budget to be tried. But it cannot settle an action. Only the ground can settle it. The Ground Maintainer must be blind to prestige." A panel of famous economists agreeing with a threshold is the named collapse, not corroboration. The legitimate ask is to give the shape a budget to be tried against realized servicing.

**My own audit is a simulation, not a ground reading.** Same essay (opened directly): "LLMs can quickly simulate an approximate answer/output with the formula" but "the truly testable output would need to be derived from the real architecture or working the formula yourself." So `01_figure_grounded_audit.md` is a simulated derivation, checked for internal consistency against the figures. It is **not** a test of any sovereign and must be labelled as such. It cannot settle anything it reports on.

## 7. One-sentence shape

The shape is a four-condition separator (non-updating ground, per-slot residual, comparison structure, named habitat), and the missing thing it constrains is an instrument that discriminates among the several sources of a single rollover-success reading, which no figure line names.
