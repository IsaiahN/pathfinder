## 2. The shape (four conditions, verdicts kept separate)

1. **Judge against a ground that does not move.** Fig 2 :29: "An anchor is whatever settles the question and does not change its answer because of what any frame thinks." The ground is realized servicing: payment, rollover, restructuring, forced adjustment. A rating, a DSA category or a debt ratio is an instrument. Fig 2 :37-38 warns an instrument "can be wrong on axes they do not measure." A market that updates on what frames think is a frame, not an anchor. **Mode:** `general` on the anchor structure, `specified` in application. Status: triangulated (Fig 1 and Fig 2, low overlap).

2. **Measure per slot, not as an average.** Fig 1 :22: "Aggregating across slots is how a live signal disappears into an average." The live slots are maturity, currency of issue, creditor class, contingent liabilities and the primary-balance path. Fig 10 :24: "the ground does not decay, the channel does." Fig 10 :25-26 names "saturated metrics, pooled readings." **Mode:** `specified`. Status: triangulated (Fig 1 and Fig 10).

3. **Express the measure as comparisons.** Fig 12 :87: "a threshold relation between two quantities." The comparisons are r against g, service against revenue, and adjustment required against adjustment available. The framework's goal is a comparison; a vocabulary holding only a stock level "can name a state and cannot name a route to one" (Fig 12 :111). **Mode:** `general` on the bond, `specified` on the r-g and fiscal-gap reading. Status: triangulated (Fig 12, Operators, world literature, low overlap).

4. **Name the habitat.** Fig 11 :39: "capability is a property of agent-and-habitat, never of the agent alone." Fig 11 :40: "An improvement that does not change contact changes nothing, however much it improves." Enumerate the contact before applying a rule (Fig 11 :44: "List everything in contact with the residual, then what is in contact with those, and outward until the cascade stops mattering."). A sovereign issuing in its own currency is a different habitat, not a point on the same ratio scale. **Mode:** `specified`. Status: triangulated (Fig 11 and world literature).

5. **The universal threshold is a `gap`.** Fig 9 :32: "you get a kind of thing, never the answer itself." Fig 9 :60: "Use filters for the budget and witnesses for the verdict. Never let a filter hand you a verdict." No figure line returns 60 percent or 90 percent. A threshold is a binding from the empirical frame, provenanced as such. Fig 13 :134: "Unreachable is a verdict here, not a silence." **Mode:** `general` on the limit, `specified` on this application. Status: single-source on the figure side (same framework pool), world corroborated.

## 3. What it explicitly does not say

- Not a sustainable debt ratio, a safe threshold, or an operational definition. Those are imports with a source gate and a shadow test (Fig 6 :23).
- Not that r < g is sufficient. It is one comparison in one slot and does not close the union (Fig 9 :57).
- Not that the inter-temporal budget constraint is the ground. World sources record grave concerns about it and show it can fail under plausible assumptions.
- Not that a market or a rating agency is the anchor. An anchor must not update on the frames it corrects (Fig 2 :29-30).
- Not that MMT is right or mainstream macro is wrong. That dispute is mostly a habitat and objective-link question, not a figure verdict.
- Not grounded. No measurement at any sovereign settles `left(R,phi)` here.

## 4. What would settle it, the ground

A ground-visible event for a named sovereign, plus a discriminator that distinguishes sources of the same reading. From *The Hard Problem is About Source* (opened directly): an instrument that reads green "cannot comment on the source"; the spectrometer resolved it. Here:

- **Rollover test.** Does rollover succeed across each maturity and currency slot when the headline ratio is unchanged? A failure at one slot with the ratio constant is the live residual the average hid.
- **Source discriminator.** Does a single success reading discriminate among low ratio, r < g, own-currency issuance, captive creditor base, and central-bank backstop? If not, the instrument is inadequate on those axes.
- **Habitat test.** Do two sovereigns with identical ratio, maturity, currency and creditor composition behave differently under a terms-of-trade shock because of monetary regime or fiscal institutions? If not, the habitat claim weakens.
- **Standing falsifiers** from `01` section 4: aggregate preserves every slot; anchor moves when pushed; sustainability is a symmetric conjunction; identical atoms and bonds across regimes give identical outcomes; a universal threshold is derived from the figures; a discriminator names the source from one reading.

## 5. The honest stop

**Unreached, not unreachable.** The enumerated slot list is a choice made visible, not a discovery of nature's own list. *They're Scanning Books* (carried): "Declaring the primitives does not make them neutral. It makes the choice visible." Another frame with a different habitat enumeration need not use these six slots. The answer stops at the limit of available instruments and records the lineage so the stop is checkable. "Indiscernibility is not identity." Indiscernible sustainability sources are not the same source.

One live strain is kept visible: the comparison bond ⋛ is defined over two quantities, and whether second-order service-capacity and service-obligation compositions count as quantities at that level needs an instrument (Fig 12 :87, Operators :51).

## 6. One-paragraph version for outreach

> The Tether does not return a sustainable debt ratio, and it does not settle the MMT debate. It returns the shape any answer must have. Sustainability is judged against realized servicing, a ground that does not move when a model moves, not against a rating or a ratio. The residual is measured per slot (maturity, currency, creditor, contingent liability), because an average hides the rollover signal that matters. The measure is a threshold comparison: interest against growth, service against revenue, adjustment required against adjustment available. And it belongs to a sovereign in its habitat, not to a debt number. Any universal threshold is a filter for attention, not a witness for the verdict. What the framework offers is that four-condition shape, five refuters written in advance, and the name of the missing instrument: a discriminator that can tell which source produced a single rollover-success reading. The threshold stays open until the ground settles it.
