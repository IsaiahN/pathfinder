## Question restated as an effect

We must decide, for a sovereign, whether its present debt path can be serviced without a future break (default, restructuring, inflation, or a disruptive fiscal adjustment), and we must choose the instrument that reports this.

- **Option A (single number):** a debt-to-GDP threshold is treated as the verdict. This leads to a live rollover residual being hidden inside an average, and to a filter being promoted into a witness.
- **Option B (shape):** sustainability is judged against a ground that does not move when a model moves, measured per slot, expressed as a comparison, and reported as a shape until a ground settles a name. This leads to a checkable claim with a refuter and an honest open slot.

An answer would look like a separator: the conditions under which a debt path is serviceable, and a measurement rule that keeps the live slots visible. Per `THE_FORMULA.md` "Direction", that sought-for shape is bound as an ordinary term in `01_figure_grounded_audit.md` so it can accrue a residual and be refused.

## What is deliberately not here

- No personal emails of named individuals.
- No ranking of people by how likely they are to agree with the claim.
- No bulk-send plan.

Three reasons, in the figures' words:

1. **Agreement is not an anchor.** Fig 2 :35: "Alignment is triangulation, not negotiation. A mirror cannot correct you."
2. **Selecting for likely agreement manufactures the disqualifying correlation.** Fig 2 :40: "disqualifying for a verdict: agreement about the world is worthless when the agreeing frames share an evidence pool." Fig 2 :42: "Buy independence in proportion to the cost of being wrong."
3. **Sending the framework before asking for a verdict spends the reader's independence.** Fig 10 :42: "A verifier that can reconstruct the claim cannot verify it." Fig 10 :52: "Bring a characterised residual, never the source."

Legally: the public venues listed are opt-in institutional contact or submission points. Unsolicited bulk email to personal addresses would breach GDPR and CAN-SPAM style rules in many jurisdictions, which is a practical failure on top of the structural one.
