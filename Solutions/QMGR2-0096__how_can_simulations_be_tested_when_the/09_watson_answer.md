## QUESTION

Decide, in effect terms, what counts as testing a simulation when the early universe cannot be recreated in a laboratory. Option A: treat internal consistency, visual fidelity, resolution and agreement among simulators as the test. Option B: range per-slot predictions against the observed universe, buy independence between probes, commit predictions before measurement, use analogues for wiring only, and record the reach limit. What each leads to: A produces a frame scoring itself and a room of correlated frames, which buys a convention; B produces a checkable shape, a pre-registered refuter and an honest stop. The residual is a union compound: no manipulation of the subject, limited observational access, and lossy coarse-graining are three conditions that must be unbundled before searching.

## VERDICT (from the figures)

The shape survives; no particular simulation is validated. A simulation cannot be tested by internal consistency or by a room of correlated simulators. It must be ranged against a ground that does not move when the model changes, and for a question about the early universe that ground is the universe as observed. The test protocol writes per-slot observables first, commits a prediction before the measurement, buys independence across epochs and instruments, uses analogues and harnesses for wiring only, enumerates second-order systematics, reports the round-trip gap and names the reach limit. The missing laboratory changes the route to the ground, not the need for one. This is a `general` structural result and a `specified` early-universe application, ungrounded and therefore `candidate`. The strongest single figure-grounded point is Fig 2 :29-30: the anchor must not update on the frame.

- **cost if wrong:** bounded and locatable. The packet states six refuters before the conclusion and records the lineage, so an error is findable rather than invisible. The real cost is spending the independence of outside checkers by sending a framework instead of a residual (Fig 10 :42), or treating a budget filter as a witness (Fig 9 :60). Both are avoidable by leading with the shape and the refuter.
- **undoable:** yes. No simulation, primitive or dataset is endorsed, so the protocol can be amended without withdrawing a claim about the world. If a refuter fires, the affected finding is retracted in `01` and `11`; the rest of the shape stands or falls separately.
- **reversed by:** a ground reading, not agreement. A pre-committed prediction that fails a new measurement; a demonstration that two codes sharing all inputs are genuinely independent witnesses; an analogue that changes contact with the real universe; a description-built sensor for a never-registered quantity that succeeds; or a case where more search and an import are indistinguishable in result and cost. Until one of these fires, the verdict remains `candidate`.
