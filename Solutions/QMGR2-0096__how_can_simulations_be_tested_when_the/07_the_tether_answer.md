# The Tether's answer: testing a simulation without a laboratory early universe

**Mode: `general` on the structural claim, `specified` on the early-universe application, not `grounded`.** This is a shape and a route, not a validated protocol. Simulated derivation checked against the figures (`05_what_shape_means.md` §6), not a ground reading.

## 1. The residual it answers

We cannot set the initial conditions of the actual universe and rerun it. We can run simulations that predict what we should observe. The residual is the gap between each prediction and each observation, indexed by slot (Fig 1 :19: "The residual is indexed by object slot, not aggregated."). The question is what a test can be when one arm of the experiment, the manipulation, is missing.

This is a **union compound**, not one hard question. "Cannot recreate" bundles no manipulation, limited access and lossy coarse-graining. Unbundle before searching (Fig 9 :54).

## 2. The shape

1. **A simulation cannot validate itself.** Its equations, resolution, visual fidelity and fit to its own tuning data are frame-internal quantities. Fig 1 :23: "The ground enters as the only metric." Fig 1 :27: "They are frame-internal. A frame cannot score itself with a quantity it also produces."

2. **The ground is the observed universe, and it is legitimate.** Fig 2 :29-30: "An anchor is whatever settles the question and does not change its answer because of what any frame thinks. A measurement, an experiment, a counter, a market, a patient outcome. If it moves when you push on it, it is not an anchor." Fig 2 :36: "An anchor is legitimate when the question is constitutively about it." The question is constitutively about the universe we see. The laboratory recreation is one route to a ground, not the only route.

3. **Range, do not negotiate.** Each prediction is corrected by the observation separately; the observation is not a party to a vote (Fig 2 :46-47). Fig 2 :45: "Frames range against the ground. They do not negotiate with it."

4. **Buy independence between probes.** Fig 2 :31-32: "Independence, between two frames, is how little they draw on the same evidence. Two people reading the same report are not two frames; two instruments pointed at the same thing are. Independence is what makes agreement mean something." Two codes from the same initial conditions and priors are one witness. Two independent epochs, CMB and BBN, are two (world source W5 records their concordance test). Fig 8 :29: "low overlap: B does not draw on A's evidence."

5. **Commit the prediction before the measurement.** Fig 5 :43-44: "A term that explains everything by saying nothing fails this, and so does a term that explains one occasion perfectly." Bind the sought-for shape as an ordinary term so it can accrue a residual and be refused (Formula "Direction").

6. **A laboratory analogue proves wiring, never capability.** Fig 11 :70: "A test harness is a substituted habitat. Which is why a synthetic solve proves wiring and never capability." World source W8 describes analogue gravity as applying insights back to the source phenomenon; that is wiring transfer. Lab nuclear reaction rates (world source W5) are instrument extension for one input, not a recreation of the universe.

7. **Extend instruments from what already returns something.** Fig 6 :32: "An instrument is not built from a description. It is improved from a worse instrument already returning something." Fig 6 :33: "Nobody specified a telescope from an account of Mars. A wandering point in the sky was already a reading, and every sharper instrument was built on the residual the last one produced." COBE to WMAP to Planck is this chain (world source W4).

8. **Enumerate the habitat beyond direct contact.** Fig 11 :43: "The habitat is enumerated, never composed." Fig 11 :52-53: "contact cascades. Scoping the habitat to direct contact is how DDT reached the eagles: first-order enumeration, second-order effect, and the residual that mattered was three steps out." Foregrounds, stellar processing, calibration drift and subgrid prescriptions are in the habitat.

9. **Separate the two currencies.** Fig 12 :125-126: "A composition costs in two currencies, and they do not add. Bits price the description. Actions price finding out whether it holds." Particle count and runtime are budget filters, never witnesses (Fig 9 :60).

10. **Measure the round-trip gap and name the stop.** Fig 4 :30: "So take the round trip and measure the gap. Without it you are navigating by dead reckoning: confident about a position nobody checked." Fig 8 :39: "So a search that finds nothing is never a proof of absence." Say `unreachable-at-this-budget`, `underivable-with-this-closure`, or `gap`. Fig 13 :134: "Unreachable is a verdict here, not a silence."

## 3. What it explicitly does not say

- That any named simulation, code, dataset or cosmological model is validated. The framework returns a shape, not a name (Fig 9 :32).
- That agreement among simulators is evidence. Fig 7 :13: "same closure, handed along; agreement compounds, evidence does not."
- That the early universe can be recreated in a laboratory and therefore tested like a bench experiment. The analogue is a substituted habitat.
- That more resolution or more CPU-hours settle truth. They are filters for the budget.
- That this is `grounded`. No measurement was made here.

## 4. What would settle it — the ground

The shape claim is about method, so what settles it is a case where the method is applied and the ground pays or refuses. Concretely:

1. **Pre-commitment test.** Write a prediction for a not-yet-measured slot, run the simulation, then measure. Does the prediction survive? If post-hoc fits carry the same weight, F6 falls.
2. **Independence test.** Compare the leverage of a new probe from a different epoch or instrument against a tenth run of the same code. If the tenth run adds as much, F3 falls.
3. **Harness test.** Find a case where a test harness success changes contact with the real universe, not just wiring. If so, F5 falls.
4. **Instrument test.** A sensor for a never-registered quantity, specified from a description alone, that succeeds. Fig 6 :40 says it is unavailable.
5. **Refuter from an outside frame.** A checker named from the residual, not from agreement, who returns a failure. Send the residual and the refuter, not the source (Fig 10 :52). A failure is more useful than an endorsement.

## 5. The honest stop

**Reached, not terminated.** The shape is reachable from the figures and is corroborated in low-overlap fields (engineering V&V, statistics, cosmology, analogue physics). It stops at the limit of what the available instruments can distinguish: which slots are registered, at what resolution, with what independence. The lineage is recorded in `01`, `06`, `09`, `10` and `11`. *"Indiscernibility is not identity"* (figures README). A search that finds no further figure line would be a recorded verdict, not a silence.

## 6. One-paragraph version for outreach

> The Tether does not say that an early-universe simulation can be validated by cleverer internal checks or by agreement among simulators. It says that a simulation is a frame and cannot score itself: the ground must be something that does not move when the model changes, and for a question about the universe the ground is the universe as observed. So a test must write its observable slots first, range each prediction separately against measurements from different epochs and instruments, commit its prediction before the measurement, use laboratory analogues only to check wiring, enumerate second-order systematics, report what its compression dropped, and say plainly which limit it hit. The absence of a laboratory recreation changes the route to the ground, not the need for one. What is offered is the shape any valid test must have, six refuters written in advance, and the instruments that would close it. The result stays `candidate` until a ground pays.
