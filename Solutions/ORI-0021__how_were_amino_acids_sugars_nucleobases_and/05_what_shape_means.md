## 1. A shape is a constraint, not a filler

The canonical figures README states the limit: the framework returns *"what a missing thing would have to look like (a shape, not a name)"*. Fig 9 :32: *"you get a kind of thing, never the answer itself"*. Fig 13: *"It can say the answer is out of reach with the library as it stands, so something has to be imported before it is asked again."*

From *The Library of Babel*: *"you can cut your search short if the set of books returned is small and descriptive enough that it provides a general shape of the answer that satisfies your question."*

For ORI-0021, a **shape** is the set of constraints any admissible production account must satisfy, stated before the historical route is named. It includes: the residual is indexed per molecular family; production is ordered; ingredients are not a route; provenance discriminates local from imported; the habitat decides the pathway's value; isomer and functional-group tests decide identity; and the literal route is unreachable without a source-discriminating ground reading. A **name** would be "the HCN cyanosulfidic evaporite scenario is what happened at site X". The framework does not return that.

## 2. How the shape is obtained

- *Library of Babel*: use the vocabulary already closest to the thing, then note recurring new terms that overlap or correlate with it. *"With each new term gained, you narrow the space by definition and deepen your vocabulary. It's this recursive process that will help you converge on the answer."* Applied here: start with "amino acid", "sugar", "base", "lipid" as the searcher's existing vocabulary; the recurring new terms from the literature are "Strecker precursor", "glycolaldehyde", "cyanoacetylene", "glycerol-1-phosphate", "isotope ratio", "structural isomer". Each narrows the answer space.
- Fig 6 :30: *"The description travels. Fails by being sharpened into terms only we can read. Cast it in effect terms."* Fig 9 :30: *"your words are borrowed too, so describe the effect"*. So the shape is written as effects: what would be seen if a route ran, not as a mechanism already believed.
- *Decoding Tacit Knowledge*: *"When contact isn't possible, the best you can send is a plain description of what you'd expect to see, because anyone can check that against the world."* This is exactly the Hadean situation: no direct contact with the site, so what is sent is an effect description checkable by anyone with an instrument.
- *Library of Babel* on bundle detection: *"Perhaps your problem was a bundle or conflation of several problems masquerading as one? You will need to search all of those problems individually."* This is Fig 9's union rule in the essay's vocabulary, and it is how the four-family split was found.

## 3. What licenses a name

Two things, both outside the shape itself:

1. **The ground settles it.** Formula step 5: *"the ground settles it. Verified only by what pays."* In chemistry, an isotope ratio, an enantiomeric excess, or a time-resolved product measurement that discriminates among routes is such a ground.
2. **A provenanced import from a frame whose closure differs** (Fig 6 :23, Fig 8 :51), with a source gate and a shadow test. Meteorite delivery is an import of this kind: the source gate is the isotope and isomer record; the shadow test is whether it explains the gap between what local synthesis supplies and what biology needs.

Until one of those, a named route is the searcher's own contribution and must be recorded as such. *Library of Babel*: *"If you don't have the answer to a question, it's because you are lacking certain things in the library (instruments, information) that would help, or perhaps you are asking the wrong question altogether."*

## 4. Why the product alone cannot settle the source

*The Hard Problem is About Source* gives the mechanism. An instrument that reads green *"cannot comment on the source"*. Several underlying causes create identical readings, and *"If several paths create the same behavior, then behavior is a projection with lossy information"*. The reading does not determine the source. *"What the Spectrometer Example Reveals: The answer is unreached until a better instrument exists."* It was the spectrometer, not an argument, that resolved the case.

For ORI-0021 the metamerism is literal. Glycine is glycine whether it came from a spark discharge, from HCN oligomerisation hydrolysed in a parent body, or from an impact plume. Uracil made in the lab and uracil extracted from Murchison are the same reading. The source is decided by carbon isotope ratios, by whether the amino acid is racemic, by which structural isomers co-occur, and by comparison with fall-site soil. Those are the spectrometer here. What is owed when they are missing is a better instrument, not a preferred narrative.

## 5. Correction to my own reading of "shape, not a name"

I initially wrote this section as if the rule forbade naming any pathway. That was an overstatement, and it is corrected here and kept visible.

The README's phrase is *"what a **missing** thing would have to look like (a shape, not a name)."* The subject is a primitive not held in `Γ`. Strecker chemistry, formose chemistry, HCN photoredox chemistry and amphiphile self-assembly are **not missing primitives**; they are bindings from the chemistry frame onto atoms and bonds already held. Naming them is provenance, not the invention of a seventh term. What remains forbidden is (a) claiming the framework derived which route, when it only constrains the shape, and (b) claiming a binding moves the wall, when Fig 6 :25 says *"An import adds no atom to the world."*

So the corrected position is: the framework returns a shape for the *historical* route, which is genuinely missing; it permits, and does not forbid, naming candidate chemical routes as provenanced bindings. Both statements are retained here so neither is quietly lost.

## 6. Prestige is not action

From *The Recursive Self-Improvement Trap*: *"Prestige must never be allowed to validate an action directly... Prestige can fund an action. It can give an action a budget to be tried. But it cannot settle an action. Only the ground can settle it. The Ground Maintainer must be blind to prestige."*

A list of famous origin-of-life advocates who agree with a proposed route is the named collapse, not corroboration. The same essay adds: *"You may all agree and be wrong. You may set your clock to someone else's, who was setting your clock to yours, and be off by many deviations over time. You need ground truth to define where you stand."* That is the situation when several reviews cite the same meteorite assay and the same atmospheric model. The legitimate ask is a budget to try a discriminator, not endorsement.

## 8. Consequences for outreach

1. Send the shape and the four refuters, not a named route (Fig 10 :52).
2. Ask what the recipient would expect to see if the shape were true, before sending the links (Fig 9 :27).
3. Never pool later agreement as corroboration; most readers of this field share one evidence pool (Fig 2 :40).
4. Offer the formula as a method, with each user writing their own refuter and mode (Fig 4 :22).
