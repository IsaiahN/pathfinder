## What is deliberately not here

- No personal emails of named individuals. The ISSOL contact page publishes institutional contact addresses; they are not copied into `03_public_contact_points.csv`. The public society page and meeting routes are used instead.
- No ranking of people by how likely they are to believe the claim.
- No bulk-send plan.
- No claim that a named recipe is the one early Earth used. The figures return a shape and a separator, not a name for the historical route.

Three figure-grounded reasons:

1. **A room does not upgrade the label.** Fig 2 :35, *"Alignment is triangulation, not negotiation. A mirror cannot correct you."* Agreement among people selected for receptiveness is a convention, not a verdict about the Hadean.
2. **Correlation disqualifies a verdict.** Fig 2 :40, *"agreement about the world is worthless when the agreeing frames share an evidence pool"*. Most published prebiotic chemistry draws on the same analytic instruments and the same meteorite specimens. Agreement across those papers is one pool talking.
3. **A verifier must not hold the interiors.** Fig 10 :42, *"A verifier that can reconstruct the claim cannot verify it."* The first contact carries the residual and its refuter, never the framework.

Legally: unsolicited bulk email to scraped addresses breaches GDPR and CAN-SPAM style rules in many jurisdictions and gets a sending domain blacklisted. Public opt-in routes avoid that failure as well as the structural one.

## Suggested next step

Take `08_expert_verification_packet.md` to one of the public venues in `03_public_contact_points.csv`, one to one. Ask what would refute the shape claim. Record the verdict before offering any theory. A failure is more useful than an endorsement.
