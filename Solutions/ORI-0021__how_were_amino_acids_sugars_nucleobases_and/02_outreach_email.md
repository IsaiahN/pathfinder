# Outreach draft: check one separator before deciding whether the framing is worth reading

**Use:** one to one, or as a cover letter attached to a submission through the public venues in `03_public_contact_points.csv`. **Not** for bulk send.

**Hard precondition:** do not send until the four checks in section 6 can be answered in the recipient's own words. The output must survive validation or the two-stage offer collapses on contact.

---

**Subject:** One separator in prebiotic supply that I would like you to try to break

> Hi [name],
>
> I am writing about a narrow question in prebiotic chemistry, not a new theory of the origin of life. I have one specific output I would like you to check against what you already know. I am not asking you to read a framework first, and I am not asking you to agree with anything. If the output does not survive, there is nothing else to look at.
>
> **The residual, in effect terms.** We observe that life uses amino acids, sugars, nucleobases and membrane forming amphiphiles. We do not have one demonstrated geochemical route that produces all four in the same place at the same time with measured selectivity. The question "how were they produced" is therefore at least four questions plus a supply question, not one. A demonstration that a reaction *can* make glycine settles chemical possibility; it does not settle that the reaction ran at a particular Hadean site. Those are different claims and they need different instruments.
>
> **The output (`specified`, not `grounded`).** Three separators that any answer must satisfy:
> 1. **Ingredients are not a route.** Two recipes with the same feedstock but different order, pH, mineral surface or delivery history give different products. The arrangement carries information the ingredient list does not.
> 2. **Local synthesis and meteorite delivery are different operators.** The same uracil made in a laboratory and found in a meteorite are identical in contents. Only the record (carbon isotope ratio, racemicity, structural isomer separation, comparison with fall-site soil) says where it came from. Convergent derivation and adopted import look the same in the product.
> 3. **The habitat decides the pathway's value.** A pathway that works in a concentrated evaporite stream under UV need not work in ice, in a vent pore or in a dilute ocean. There is no habitat-invariant ranking of pathways.
>
> **What I am not claiming:** that I can name the one route early Earth used. I cannot, and I do not think the framework I am using can. It returns the shape any candidate answer must have and the instruments that would distinguish candidates. It does not return a name for the historical event. I am also not claiming that Miller-Urey or formose or meteorite delivery settles the question. Each settles a different sub-question, and each has been over-read as an answer to the whole.
>
> **What would refute the output.** I wrote these down before sending this:
> 1. One route produces all four families in one habitat with measured selectivity, concentration and co-location, and a ground reading confirms it operated there.
> 2. A source-discriminating measurement shows local versus exogenous contribution for all four families.
> 3. A pathway's yield and selectivity are invariant across the habitats I listed.
> 4. Product identity and behaviour turn out to be readable from the molecular formula alone, so the bond carries no information.
>
> If any one holds, that part of the output is wrong and I will retract it. A one-line "this fails because..." is more useful to me than agreement. If you would rather not engage, no reply is needed.
>
> **If it survives, and only then:** here are the sources I actually used. The figures, where I cite them, are the authority, not my summary of them.
> - Cleaves 2012, *Prebiotic Chemistry: What We Know, What We Don't*: https://link.springer.com/article/10.1007/s12052-012-0443-9
> - Patel et al. 2015, *Common origins of RNA, protein and lipid precursors in a cyanosulfidic protometabolism*: https://www.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/pmc/articles/PMC4568310/
> - Oba et al. 2022, nucleobases in carbonaceous meteorites: https://www.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/pmc/articles/PMC9042847/
> - Furukawa et al. 2019, extraterrestrial ribose and sugars: https://www.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/pmc/articles/PMC6900709/
>
> **If you want to use the machinery:** ask what would refute your own result before you run it, and state the mode of what you get. A reported failure is more useful to me than a reported success.
>
> - [name]

## Stage sequence

1. **Stage 1.** Send the residual, the three separators and the four refuters. Ask what the recipient would count as a failure. Record that verdict before any framework is shown.
2. **Stage 2, opt in only.** If they are curious, send the four source links. Their later agreement is never pooled as corroboration, because they now share the evidence pool.
3. **Stage 3.** Offer the eight-step formula as a method to reuse, with the instruction that each user writes their own refuter and states their own mode.

A Track B reply, if one is ever arranged through a published institutional address, is a conversation and never corroboration. Nothing in this folder sends anything.
