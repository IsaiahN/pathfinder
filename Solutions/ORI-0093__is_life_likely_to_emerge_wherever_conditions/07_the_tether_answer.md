# The Tether's answer on the probability of life emerging

**Mode: `specified` on the structure, `general` on the shape, `gap` on the number. Not `grounded`.** This is a shape and a route, not a rate. Simulated derivation checked against the figures, not a ground reading.

## 1. The residual it answers

We hold one prediction, *conditions of kind C yield life*, and one observation, Earth. The residual is indexed by site and is not an average (Fig 1 :19 *"The residual is indexed by object slot, not aggregated."*; Fig 1 :13 *"a global R near zero with one live slot residual is a legal state."*). The question's either/or is a union of overlapping conditions, not a partition (Fig 9 :55), and the sought-for answer, a number with a discriminator, has no nameable anchor (Fig 2 :29).

## 2. The shape

1. **The two options are different question types.** Fig 13 :115 *"The set is closed. The arrangements are not. That gap is why import exists."* If life is reachable from the atoms `C` supplies, search under the same `C` can find it; if it needs an atom `C` lacks, no search adds that atom (Fig 8 :32 *"Closure is idempotent. Composing inside a closed set never leaves it, so no search, however long, adds an atom."*). The first rate is governed by search; the second by import. They cannot be averaged.
2. **The number is a `gap`, not silence.** There is no instrument returning an independent-origin rate. The world literature records estimates for the Drake origin term spanning *"many hundreds of orders of magnitude"*. A gap with a declared discriminator is a verdict (Fig 13 :134 *"Unreachable is a verdict here, not a silence"*).
3. **The premise is a bundle.** Liquid water, free energy, building blocks, concentration and mineral surfaces overlap. Fig 9 :57 *"What remains is smaller and it is also categorically different: a case in two conditions is not a harder case of either."* Finding abundant habitable planets does not clear the intersections.
4. **Arrival is unmaintained and precedes selection.** Fig 11 :56 *"Unmaintained. No maintainer and no live frame in the seat. The habitat is pure contact, nothing selects, and what survives is what the ground kept."* Fig 11 :36 *"Where nothing maintains anything, intelligence is a label applied afterwards to a lineage that met its ground. Nothing was intelligent; selection ran."* Convergent evolution is a D2 fact and cannot be projected back to D1.
5. **The habitat is enumerated, not composed.** Fig 11 :43 *"The habitat is enumerated, never composed."* Contact cascades, so a first-order list of conditions can miss the residual that matters (Fig 11 :52 *"And contact cascades."*).
6. **Non-detection is not rarity.** Fig 8 :39 *"So a search that finds nothing is never a proof of absence."* Fig 9 :44 *"Only a sealed room can be searched to the end, and no frame is a sealed room. So searching and finding nothing proves nothing."*
7. **The chain is production, not sequence.** Fig 12 :77 *"A produces B; B did not exist before"*. See `06_isomers_and_order.md`.

## 3. What it explicitly does not say

- Not a probability, not an interval, not a bet on common or rare.
- Not that a specific ingredient is the missing atom. That is a binding from the chemistry frame and needs provenance and a shadow test.
- Not that convergent evolution makes abiogenesis likely. It acts on lineages.
- Not that Earth's early origin was typical. Fig 1 :22 *"Aggregating across slots is how a live signal disappears into an average."*
- Not that non-detection anywhere supports rarity.
- Not grounded. No independent-origin count exists.

## 4. What would settle it, the ground

A discriminator, not a more confident prior. The Recursive Self-Improvement Trap asks *"But what happened before there was a ground to evolve itself against?"* and the Hard Problem is About Source answers generally: *"The answer is unreached until a better instrument exists."* Here the discriminating readings are:

- **Origin count.** A second independent origin found in the Solar System, with ancestry determined, would move the number from `gap` to a measured rate at one more slot. The NASA Astrobiology FAQ states the scale of the change in the institution's own words: finding life that did not originate on Earth would mean *"our data set, of one, has now doubled."*
- **Closure test.** A fixed, fully inventoried set of conditions run repeatedly in the laboratory. Repeated arrival supports reachability; persistent failure with a demonstrated missing atom supports import. Neither alone gives the natural rate, because the laboratory has a seat and a young planet does not (Fig 11 :58).
- **Discriminating instrument.** Something that can tell a closure pathway from an import pathway at a site, rather than reading the end state and naming a source from a projection.
- **Standing refuters.** The six conditions in `01_figure_grounded_audit.md` section 5, written before the synthesis.

## 5. The honest stop

**Unreached, not unreachable.** The figures return a shape with a hole. The hole is the number and, if there is one, the name of the missing atom. Fig 13 :134 says *"Unreachable is a verdict here, not a silence"*; Fig 9 :32 says *"you get a kind of thing, never the answer itself."* The stop is recorded with its lineage so a later reader can locate the error. Prestige, consensus and the number of papers on the subject are not instruments.

## 6. One-paragraph version for outreach

> The Tether does not say whether life is common or rare, because nothing currently measures an independent-origin rate; that number is a declared gap, not a guess. What it does say is structural. Life is either an arrangement reachable from the atoms that suitable conditions supply, in which case search under those conditions can find it and different routes can converge on the same functional result, or it needs an atom those conditions lack, in which case no amount of search adds it and the rate is set by import. Those are two different questions, not two ends of one probability scale. The premise itself bundles several overlapping conditions, so one discovery will not clear it. Arrival comes before selection, and on a young planet nothing maintains the habitat, so convergent evolution cannot be used as evidence about the origin. Non-detection is not rarity. What would settle it is a second independent origin, a discriminating instrument, or a closure test that shows which case we are in. The shape, its refuters and the instruments that would close it are offered; the number stays open until the ground settles it.
