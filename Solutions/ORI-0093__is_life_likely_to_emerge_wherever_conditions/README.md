# Solution ORI-0093, figure-grounded package

**Question:** Is life likely to emerge wherever conditions are suitable, or could its origin require a very rare chain of events?

**Source:** `Questions/origins of life.txt` line 128.

**Section:** 10. Life beyond Earth and broader implications.

**Mode:** `specified` on the per-domain structural claims, `general` on the shape, `gap` on the numerical value of the probability. Nothing here is `grounded`. Every substantive file labels this a simulated derivation checked for internal consistency against the figures, not a ground reading.

**Status:** done.

**Registry status:** pending.

## The question restated as an effect

Let `C` be a set of conditions called suitable, and let `L` be the event that life emerges. The question asks whether `P(L | C)` is high, so that a second site with `C` is expected to produce life, or low, so that `C` is necessary but not sufficient and something outside `C` must arrive. Option A (likely) leads to a prediction of convergent, repeatable arrival and makes a second independent origin probable. Option B (rare chain) leads to a prediction that arrival is governed by the rate at which a missing element is imported, not by the prevalence of `C`.

An answer would look like a number or a bound on `P(L | C)`, with the elements of `C` named and the discriminator between the two cases stated. That is the sought-for shape. It is bound as an ordinary term in `01_figure_grounded_audit.md` section 1 so it can accrue a residual and be refused by the same test as everything else. The figures do not return the number. They return a shape: the two options are two different question types, closure and import, not two values of one rate. That is recorded as a `gap`, which is a verdict and not a silence.

## What is deliberately not here

- No personal emails of named individuals.
- No ranking of people by how likely they are to agree that life is common or rare.
- No bulk-send plan.

Three reasons, in the figures' own words, are given in `02_outreach_email.md` and `04_links_and_distribution.md`: a room does not upgrade the label, correlation disqualifies a verdict, and a verifier who has already absorbed the interior cannot verify it. Selecting recipients by their prior position on life's prevalence would manufacture exactly the correlation that makes their agreement worthless.
