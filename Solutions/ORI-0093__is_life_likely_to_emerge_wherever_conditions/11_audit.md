## 1. Independent domain census

I redid the census from the residual line, using the WATSON order: R by arity, symmetry and scale; one line per domain with actors, currency and anchor; partition or union; order by dependency.

- **R, the residual.** Arity: one observed instance, indexed by site, from which a distribution over sites is asked. One live slot with all others empty is a legal state, so no average may be taken (Fig 1 :19 *"The residual is indexed by object slot, not aggregated."*). Symmetry: site predicates look exchangeable, but the question joins a threshold comparison over counts (the *likely* half) with an ordered production chain (the *rare chain* half). Operators table :48 defines comparison as *"a threshold relation between two quantities"*, while Operators table :30 defines sequence as *"A then B; B needs A to have happened"*. Two different bonds, so the either/or is a category confusion before it is an empirical question. Scale: molecular replication, protocell assembly, planetary habitat, and the inference across all three. The cross-scale transform is lossy, so a round trip must be measured (Fig 4 :30).
- **D1, prebiotic chemistry.** Actors: molecules, mineral surfaces, free energy inflows, gradients, reaction networks, wet-dry and thermal cycles. Currency: reaction flux, composition, concentration. Anchor: experiment, laboratory synthesis, meteorite assay. Admitted.
- **D2, evolutionary and habitat.** Actors: replicators, lineages, environments, relations. Currency: survival, reproduction, cascade. Anchor: the fossil and genomic record, experiment. Admitted. Consumes D1 because a replicator must exist before a lineage can be selected.
- **D3, observation and inference.** Actors: observers, instruments, sites. Currency: detections and non-detections. Anchor: the actual count of independent origins. Admitted. Consumes both D1 and D2.
- **X1, the free-floating origin probability.** Actors: rate parameters. Currency: numbers. Anchor: none nameable for the origin term. Not a domain yet. The public literature records estimates for the Drake origin term spanning hundreds of orders of magnitude. This is a structural refusal of X1, not a claim that a rate is unknowable in principle.
- **X2, a vital or organising principle supervening on chemistry.** No nameable anchor. Absorbed into D1 and D2 because capability is a property of agent and habitat (Fig 11 :39 *"So the answerable question is not what intelligence is, but what makes a system capable. And capability is a property of agent-and-habitat, never of the agent alone."*).

**Partition or union.** Union. D1, D2 and D3 are ordered by dependency, not ranked by importance. The phrase *conditions suitable* is itself a bundle of overlapping conditions, so a single lever clears only the region where one condition holds and leaves the intersections standing (Fig 9 :55 *"A bundled problem is usually a union of overlapping conditions, not a list of separate ones."*, Fig 9 :57 *"What remains is smaller and it is also categorically different: a case in two conditions is not a harder case of either."*). The headline either/or flattens this union into one switch.

**Order by dependency.** Enumerate the habitat before naming conditions (Fig 11 :43 *"The habitat is enumerated, never composed."*). Settle arrival before selection (Fig 11 :56). ## 4. Round trip gap R_T

The Symbols table defines R_T as the gap between what was sent up and what came back down, `gap(x, (T_E ∘ T_A)(x))`, measuring what the coarser description cannot hold. Fig 4 :30 requires it to be measured rather than assumed. I ran the round trip on the condensation this folder actually uses.

2. **Abstraction (T_A):** the shorthand carried in `07` and `08`: life is either reachable or needs an import; the number is a gap; conditions are a bundle; non-detection is not rarity.
3. **Concretisation (T_E):** a concrete one-line answer of the form *life is likely because conditions are suitable* or *life is rare because a rare chain is needed*.

**R_T is nonzero.** Four things the shorthand holds do not survive the trip back down:

- the **unmaintained-arrival condition**, which a convergent-evolution argument erases (Fig 11 :36);
- the **provenance requirement** on any named import, which a named rare chain erases (Fig 6 :12, Fig 8 :50).

Conversely, one residue survives cleanly: closure and import have different currencies and cannot be averaged. That is the part worth sending. R_T was **not** measured numerically, because no ground reading exists; it is recorded qualitatively as the enumerated set of dropped constraints. Treating the shorthand as identical to the figure set would set R_T to zero and would be the dead-reckoning error Fig 4 :30 names.
