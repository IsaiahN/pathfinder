### Three essay quotes that changed the derivation

Opened directly this session:

1. **The Recursive Self-Improvement Trap** (Isaiah N, Adventures in Machine Learning, 2026-11-19) asks the framework's own version of this question: *"But what happened before there was a ground to evolve itself against? Before there were 'livng' beings?"* and answers *"Natural selection is what the loop looks like when there is no one maintaining the ground."* This is the D1/D2 boundary in the author's own prose, and it is why convergent evolution cannot be used as evidence about origin.
2. **The Library of Babel** (Isaiah N, 2026-10-02) on stopping: *"you can cut your search short if the set of books returned is small and descriptive enough that it provides a general shape of the answer that satisfies your question."* It also names the union error: *"Perhaps your problem was a bundle or conflation of several problems masquerading as one?"*
3. **The Hard Problem is About Source** (Isaiah N, 2026-08-28): *"The answer is unreached until a better instrument exists."* The origin rate is unreached in exactly this sense: a discriminator is owed, not a more confident prior.

All three share the framework author's evidence pool with the figures, so their agreement is internal consistency, not independent corroboration.

## Distribution: offer, do not push

- **Fig 4 :22** *"Only methods cross up. Only head starts come down."* Sending the full architecture before anyone has described the residual sends a recording, not a generator. Lead with the shape and the refuter; offer links after a verdict is recorded.

- **Fig 2 :35** *"Alignment is triangulation, not negotiation. A mirror cannot correct you."* A list selected for people already inclined to agree produces a convention, not corroboration.
- **Fig 8 :39** *"So a search that finds nothing is never a proof of absence."* A mailing that harvests silence as assent is the same error in a different currency.

**Recommended order in a reply:** (1) the shape claim and its five refuters; (2) ask what the recipient would count as a failure; (3) record that verdict; (4) only then offer the three framework links, with the note that the figures, not the summary, are the authority; (5) offer the formula as a method for the recipient's own problem, with the instruction to write a refuter first.

Stage 1 carries the residual in effect terms and the pre-registered refuter. It asks for a failure, not agreement. Stage 2 is opt-in and carries the source links.
