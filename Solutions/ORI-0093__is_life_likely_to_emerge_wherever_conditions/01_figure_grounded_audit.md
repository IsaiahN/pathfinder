## 0. The question under audit

> Is life likely to emerge wherever conditions are suitable, or could its origin require a very rare chain of events?

`Questions/origins of life.txt` line 128, section 10.

**Effect restatement.** The question asks for `P(L | C)`, where `C` is a set of conditions called suitable and `L` is the emergence of life. The word *likely* is a threshold on a count of outcomes. The phrase *rare chain* asserts that `C` is necessary but not sufficient and that an element outside `C` must arrive. Binding the sought-for answer as an ordinary term, as the formula's Direction paragraph requires: an answer would be a number or bound on `P(L | C)`, with the elements of `C` named and a discriminator stated that would tell closure from import. If no such discriminator exists in the figures, the answer shape is a `gap` and that is the verdict.

**What each option leads to.** Option A (likely) commits to convergent, repeatable arrival: two sites with the same `C` should produce life, and different micro-routes should reach the same functional result. Option B (rare chain) commits to contingency: `C` supplies atoms but not the missing element, so the rate is set by the arrival of that element rather than by the prevalence of `C`.

## 1. The residual in effect terms

`R = |Γ(b,a) − o′|` per slot. Here the library `Γ` holds one prediction, *conditions of kind C yield life*, and the observation `o′` is a single live site, Earth. The residual is not a scalar over the universe.

- **Arity.** One observed instance, indexed by site. The question asks for a distribution over sites from a sample of one. Fig 1 :13 *"a global R near zero with one live slot residual is a legal state."* Fig 1 :19 *"The residual is indexed by object slot, not aggregated."* Fig 1 :22 *"Aggregating across slots is how a live signal disappears into an average."* So a universal `P(L|C)` inferred by averaging over the one known site commits the named aggregation error.
- **Symmetry.** Site predicates are exchangeable, so the question looks symmetric. It is not: the word *likely* is a threshold comparison, and the composition that produces a living system is ordered. Operators table :48 *"a threshold relation between two quantities"* defines comparison, and Fig 12 :71 *"A then B; B needs A to have happened"* defines sequence. The rate is a comparison over counts; the chain is a sequence. They are different bonds and cannot be substituted.
- **Scale.** Molecular replication, protocell assembly, planetary habitat, and an inference across all three. The membrane between them is lossy, so the round trip must be measured rather than assumed (Fig 4 :30).

**Dependency order (Fig 3).** Arrival precedes selection. A replicator must exist before a lineage can be selected, so D2 consumes D1. Detection consumes both. The probability frame consumes all three and has no anchor of its own.

## 4. Findings

### F1: One live slot is a legal state; the average is the error (triangulated)

Fig 1 :13 *"a global R near zero with one live slot residual is a legal state."*
Fig 1 :19 *"The residual is indexed by object slot, not aggregated."*
Fig 1 :22 *"Aggregating across slots is how a live signal disappears into an average."*

Earth is one slot with positive mass. Treat it as the whole distribution and the residual vanishes into an average. Mode `general` for the indexing rule, `specified` for this question. **Refuter:** a second independent origin with a measured, different pathway, which would make the per-slot residual directly observable; or a demonstration that sites are not exchangeable for this residual, which would move the question to a different domain.

### F2: The probability slot has no anchor, so it is not a domain yet (triangulated)

Fig 2 :29 *"An anchor is whatever settles the question and does not change its answer because of what any frame thinks. A measurement, an experiment, a counter, a market, a patient outcome."*
Fig 9 :32 *"you get a kind of thing, never the answer itself"*
Fig 13 :134 *"Unreachable is a verdict here, not a silence"*

The world source makes the same point without the framework: the Drake equation's origin term is entirely unknown, and *"estimates varying over many hundreds of orders of magnitude"* are recorded in the Fermi paradox article (Wikipedia, read this session). So the honest verdict on the number is `gap`, not an interval and not a shrug. Mode `specified` for the refusal. **Refuter:** an instrument that returns an independent-origin count across a defined ensemble of sites, which would install an anchor and convert X1 into D4.

### F3: The premise is a union, not a partition (triangulated)

Fig 9 :55 *"A bundled problem is usually a union of overlapping conditions, not a list of separate ones."*
Fig 9 :57 *"What remains is smaller and it is also categorically different: a case in two conditions is not a harder case of either."*
Fig 8 :19 *"closures differ, so a surplus exists"* is not relevant here; the relevant half is that an undirected union is mute, Fig 8 :21 *"nothing was aimed; the union is mute."*

*Conditions suitable* bundles liquid water, energy supply, organic building blocks, concentration mechanisms, mineral surfaces and time. *Rare chain* bundles a different set. The question's either/or flattens a union. Mode `specified`. **Refuter:** a single manipulable condition whose variation moves the origin rate across the whole ensemble with nothing else changed, which would be a partition lever rather than a union.

### F4: Closure and import are two question types, not two rates (triangulated)

Fig 13 :115 *"The set is closed. The arrangements are not. That gap is why import exists."*
Fig 13 :118 *"So a frame can search without end and still not arrive: infinite effort inside a finite reach."*
Fig 8 :32 *"Closure is idempotent. Composing inside a closed set never leaves it, so no search, however long, adds an atom."*
Fig 6 :21 *"MINT composes inside the closure and can never add an atom."*

If life is an arrangement reachable from the atoms that `C` supplies, more search under the same `C` can find it, and the rate is governed by search time and solid angle. If life requires an atom not in that closure, no amount of search adds it, and the rate is governed by the arrival of that atom. Option A and Option B are therefore not two values of `P(L|C)`. They have different currencies and different anchors. Mode `general` for the structural split, `specified` for the binding to this question. **Refuter:** a case where extending search time under a fixed, fully inventoried closure changes the presence or absence of the missing atom, which would violate idempotence.

### F5: The rare chain is an import claim in effect terms, not a name (triangulated)

Fig 6 :23 *"No frame escapes its own closure. The bound moves only on contact with a frame whose closure differs."*
Fig 8 :45 *"Two frames together reach what neither reaches alone."*
Fig 8 :50 *"Two frames can hold the same atom by convergence, reaching it separately, which is corroboration at maximum independence."*

The figure returns a shape: the rare chain is *something that adds an atom to the closure of the conditions*. It does not return which atom. Naming a specific requirement, for example wet-dry cycling or a particular mineral catalyst, is a binding from the chemistry frame onto a gap, and needs a source gate and a shadow test (Fig 6 :12, Fig 8 :52). Mode `specified` for the shape. **Refuter:** a demonstration that a proposed rare step adds no atom, being merely an arrangement of atoms already in `C`, which would demote Option B to Option A.

### F6: Arrival precedes selection; in nature it is unmaintained (triangulated)

Fig 11 :36 *"Where nothing maintains anything, intelligence is a label applied afterwards to a lineage that met its ground. Nothing was intelligent; selection ran."*
Fig 11 :56 *"Unmaintained. No maintainer and no live frame in the seat. The habitat is pure contact, nothing selects, and what survives is what the ground kept."*
Fig 11 :58 *"Maintained. A seat exists and can act: choose the instruments, distil the impactful actors, introduce what was not there. That is the garden, and it is how arrival is worked on rather than waited for."*

The Recursive Self-Improvement Trap asks the framework's own version of this question: *"But what happened before there was a ground to evolve itself against? Before there were 'livng' beings?"* and answers *"Natural selection is what the loop looks like when there is no one maintaining the ground."* So convergent evolution, which operates after a lineage exists, cannot be used as evidence about the origin transition. In the laboratory a seat exists and arrival is worked on; on a young planet there is no seat and the ground decides. Mode `specified`. **Refuter:** a demonstrated natural mechanism in which selection acts on pre-replicator chemical populations in a way that supplies the missing atom, which would move part of D2 below the arrival break.

### F7: The habitat must be enumerated, not composed (triangulated)

Fig 11 :43 *"The habitat is enumerated, never composed."*
Fig 11 :44 *"List everything in contact with the residual, then what is in contact with those, and outward until the cascade stops mattering."*
Fig 11 :48 *"You do not invent the list. You read it off the world, and what you cannot perceive or measure yet is the residual."*
Fig 11 :52 *"And contact cascades."*

A list of conditions called suitable is a first-order enumeration. The origin residual can sit three steps out, as the DDT example in the figure shows. Mode `general` for the enumeration rule, `specified` for its application. **Refuter:** a closed-form composition of conditions that predicts the origin rate without enumeration and is confirmed at the ground, which would mean contact does not cascade for this residual.

### F8: Non-detection is not rarity (single-source on each figure, triangulated together)

Fig 8 :39 *"So a search that finds nothing is never a proof of absence."*
Fig 9 :44 *"Only a sealed room can be searched to the end, and no frame is a sealed room. So searching and finding nothing proves nothing."*

Failure to find a second origin in the Solar System cannot select Option B. The figures say so for two different reasons: idempotent closure gives no proof of absence, and no frame is sealed. Mode `general`. **Refuter:** a genuinely sealed search space with a complete detection instrument, which the figures say does not exist for any frame.

### F9: *Likely* is a held preference, not a term (triangulated)

Fig 5 :62 *"A frame searching for an answer of a particular shape holds that shape as a PREFERENCE rather than as a term."*
Fig 9 :28 *"any frame you pick will seem to fit"*

The word *likely* carries a preference for a high-rate answer. If it is not bound as an ordinary term with a stated residual and a refuter, every piece of evidence will be read to fit it. This section binds it in section 0. Mode `general` for the limit. **Refuter:** a frame that states its preferred rate, derives a prediction from it, and lets the ground refuse it, which would make the preference a term.

### F10: Drake terms are filters for the budget, never witnesses for the verdict (triangulated)

Fig 9 :60 *"Use filters for the budget and witnesses for the verdict. Never let a filter hand you a verdict."*

The relevant caution is in the world literature too: the Fermi paradox article attributes the spread of estimates to *"the overconfidence effect, a common error of probabilistic reasoning about low-probability events."* A rate estimate decides where to spend telescope time. It does not decide whether life is common. Mode `specified`. **Refuter:** a term that both allocates a search budget and is settled by that search without circularity.

### F11: An import needs provenance and a shadow test (triangulated)

Fig 6 :12 *"source gate: provenance"*
Fig 8 :50 *"Two frames can hold the same atom by convergence, reaching it separately, which is corroboration at maximum independence."*

If a researcher imports a chemical requirement from a different closure, for example a mineral surface effect observed in a laboratory, the record must say where it came from and which gap it explains. Otherwise convergent derivation and adopted import look identical in the contents. Mode `specified`. **Refuter:** a named imported requirement with no record of origin that nevertheless explains a previously described gap, which would collapse the distinction the figure draws.

### F12: Convergence is possible without being identity (triangulated)

Fig 13 :121 *"Different routes can arrive at the same substance."*
Fig 12 :117 *"isomer. Same atoms, different bond, different substance. The difference is invisible in the formula and decisive in the behaviour."*
Fig 8 :50 *"Two frames can hold the same atom by convergence, reaching it separately, which is corroboration at maximum independence."*

Convergent evolution (Wikipedia, read this session) reports that analogous structures arise independently under similar niches: *"similar problems can lead to similar solutions."* That makes Option A's mechanism coherent for D2. It does not transfer to D1, because selection is not yet running (F6). This is a genuine re-census, recorded in section 7. Mode `general` for the shape, `specified` for the domain restriction. **Refuter:** a pre-replicator chemical system in which selection-like differential persistence supplies a missing atom, which would let convergence operate at D1.

### F13: The condensation carries a measurable round trip gap (single-source)

Fig 4 :30 *"So take the round trip and measure the gap. Without it you are navigating by dead reckoning: confident about a position nobody checked."*

Condensing figures 6, 8, 11 and 13 into *common or rare* throws away the bond type, the union structure, the unmaintained-arrival condition and the provenance requirement. The gap is reported qualitatively in `11_audit.md`. Mode `general`. **Refuter:** a condensation from which all four constraints can be reconstructed and checked at the ground.

## 5. Pre-registered refuter

Written before the synthesis and before any outreach, per the seat rule that *"a falsifier written before the run is a design instrument, not only an honesty device."*

The shape claim is refuted if any of the following holds:

1. A single instrument returns an independent-origin rate over a defined ensemble of sites, so X1 acquires an anchor and the `gap` verdict is withdrawn.
2. A fixed, fully inventoried closure under extended search produces a new atom, violating Fig 8 :32.
3. A natural pre-replicator mechanism is demonstrated in which differential persistence supplies the missing atom, collapsing the D1/D2 dependency and the unmaintained-arrival finding.
4. The origin condition set is shown to be a partition with one manipulable lever that moves the rate across all intersections, violating Fig 9 :55.
5. Non-detection across a genuinely sealed and completely instrumented search space is shown to prove absence, violating Fig 8 :39.
6. A second independent origin is found that uses the same atoms and a different bond, or the same bond and different atoms, in either case giving a direct measurement of convergence rather than an argument from analogy.

Until one of these is run, nothing in this folder is labelled `grounded`.

## 6. Recommendation

1. Ask a different question first. Replace *how likely* with *which closure, which missing atom, and what discriminator* (F2, F3, F4).
2. Keep the two options separate and never average them. Closure and import have different currencies (F4).
3. Enumerate the habitat outward before naming conditions. Include second-order contact (F7).
4. Write the import with provenance and a shadow test, or leave the slot marked (F5, F11).
5. Treat non-detection as silence from the ground, not as evidence of rarity (F8).
6. In outreach, lead with the refuter and the shape claim. Offer links only after a verdict is recorded (F9, F10).

## 8. Verdict summary

The figures do not return `P(L | C)`. They return a shape with a hole in it. Life is either an arrangement reachable from the atoms a closure supplies, in which case search under the same conditions can find it and different routes can converge on the same functional result, or it requires an atom the closure lacks, in which case no amount of search adds it and the rate is set by import. These are two question types with different currencies, not two ends of one probability scale. The premise bundles several conditions, so one lever will not clear it. In nature arrival is unmaintained, so selection cannot work on it before a replicator exists. Non-detection is not rarity. The number is a declared `gap` of the no-anchor kind. Mode `specified` on the structure, `general` on the shape, not `grounded`.
