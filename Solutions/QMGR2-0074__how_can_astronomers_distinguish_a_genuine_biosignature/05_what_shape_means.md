## 1. A shape is a constraint, not a filler

The canonical figures README says the framework returns *"what a missing thing would have to look like (a shape, not a name)"*, then stops at the limit of what the instruments can distinguish and records the lineage. *"Indiscernibility is not identity."* Fig 9 :32: **"you get a kind of thing, never the answer itself"**

From *The Library of Babel*, fetched this session: *"you can cut your search short if the set of books returned is small and descriptive enough that it provides a general shape of the answer that satisfies your question."*

So for this question a **shape** is the narrowed form of a source attribution you can state before you can name the source. It constrains; it does not fill. The shape is: a six-condition source-attribution procedure (per-slot, channel-checked, refusal-first, habitat-enumerated, independently triangulated, description-length-paid), plus the instrument that would discriminate the source. "O2 plus CH4 means life" is a name. The shape is the set of tests any such claim must survive.

## 2. How the shape is obtained

- *The Library of Babel*: use the vocabulary closest to the thing, look for new terms that overlap or correlate with it, recurse. *"With each new term gained, you are able to narrow the space by definition and deepen your vocabulary."* The essay also gives the bundle warning used in `01` section 1.
- Fig 6 :30: **"The description travels. Fails by being sharpened into terms only we can read. Cast it in effect terms."** Fig 9 :30-31: **"your words are borrowed too, so describe the effect rather than the cause."** In this question: describe *what would discriminate a source*, not *which molecule life would make*.
- *Decoding Tacit Knowledge*, as mapped in the exemplar: *"When contact isn't possible, the best you can send is a plain description of what you'd expect to see, because anyone can check that against the world."* An exoplanet source is exactly a case where contact is not possible.
- *The Library of Babel* on stopping: *"you will know that you are done because the finish line will also be the starting place"* and the paradox that by then you have *"composed the answer through the process of locating it."*

## 3. What licenses a name, and why the source is special here

Only two things license a name, both outside the shape itself:

1. **The ground settles it.** Formula step 5: *"the ground settles it. Verified only by what pays."* The canonical README: the framework *"stops at the limit of what the available instruments can distinguish, and records the lineage so the stop is honest."*
2. **A provenanced import from a frame whose closure differs** (Fig 6 :23, Fig 8 :50-52), with a source gate and a shadow test.

Here there is a third obstacle that the other questions did not have. The thing being named is a **source**, and the source is not in the reading. Fig 8 :50-52: **"Convergent derivation and adopted import look identical in the contents. Only the record of where each came from separates them."** The essay *The Hard Problem is About Source*, fetched this session, states the same limit in its own words: the instrument *"is speaking only about what it measured, it cannot comment on the source"*, and *"This is why the True Hard Problem is about determining the Source."* Its resolution is not an argument but an instrument: *"the answer is unreached until a better instrument exists."*

So the name "life" is licensed only by a source-discriminating instrument or an in situ measurement. Until one exists, the honest status for an exoplanet is `candidate`, unreachable at this budget.

## 4. The specific error the field keeps making

*The Hard Problem is About Source* gives the mechanism: metamerism. An instrument reads green and cannot say whether the source was one green light, yellow plus blue, RGB, CMYK, or a scheme not yet discovered. *"If several paths create the same behavior, then behavior is a projection with lossy information."* It was the spectrometer, not an argument, that resolved it.

Applied here: a single absorption line is a projection. Several sources can create it. Naming one of them from the reading alone is the metamerism error. The same shape appears independently in the colour-science literature (https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Metamerism_(color)): *"different combinations of light across all wavelengths can produce an equivalent receptor response"*. And in the mathematics of inverse problems (https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Inverse_problem): reconstructing causes from effects is typically ill-posed, with *"noise and errors... tremendously amplified"*. Three fields, one shape.

The essay also warns that *"The full set of ways to produce a reading is not known to be closed or determined."* This is Fig 6's wall in biological language: our line lists are closure(Gamma), and an unmatched feature may be a molecule we do not hold rather than nothing at all.

## 5. Q: Does "shape, not a name" forbid naming O2 or CH4 as a biosignature?

**Yes, as a verdict; no, as a labelled example.** The canonical README's phrase governs a *missing* thing: it returns the shape of what a missing primitive would look like. O2 and CH4 are not missing primitives of the framework; they are molecules from the planetary frame. Naming them is a **binding** from that frame onto an atmospheric state, not the invention of a new primitive. What remains forbidden is treating the binding as the answer, because (a) the source is still undiscriminated and (b) Fig 11 :51 says **"No standing list of good actors survives a change of residual."**

So the defensible form is: *O2 plus CH4 in disequilibrium, in a context where abiotic production is excluded by a second mechanism, is a candidate whose source attribution is still open.* The indefensible form is: *O2 plus CH4 is a biosignature.* Both claims are recorded so neither is quietly lost.

## 6. Two consequences for outreach

**Prestige is not action.** From *The Recursive Self-Improvement Trap*, as mapped in the exemplar: *"Prestige must never be allowed to validate an action directly... Prestige can fund an action. It can give an action a budget to be tried. But it cannot settle an action. Only the ground can settle it. The Ground Maintainer must be blind to prestige."* A campaign that gathers famous names to agree that a gas is a biosignature is the named collapse. The legitimate ask gives the shape a budget to be tried and lets the ground, an independent instrument or a re-reduction, settle it.

**Declaring interest.** Fig 10 :59: **"an interest in the outcome is unavoidable, an interest in the reading is disqualifying"** and the figure adds *"Where a stake cannot be removed, declare it."* The desire to find life is a stake in the outcome and must be declared; it must never become a stake in how the reading is interpreted.

## 7. My own audit is a simulation, not a ground reading

Same essay: *"LLMs can quickly simulate an approximate answer/output... However, the truly testable output would need to be derived from the real architecture or working the formula yourself."* LLM output is *"simulacra."* So `01_figure_grounded_audit.md` is a simulated derivation, checked for internal consistency against the figures. It is **not** a test of any biosignature claim and must be labelled as such. It cannot settle anything it reports on. No telescope was pointed anywhere and no spectrum was read.

## 8. Honest stop

Fig 8 :39: **"So a search that finds nothing is never a proof of absence."** Fig 9 :44: **"Only a sealed room can be searched to the end, and no frame is a sealed room."** The answer stops at the limit of the available instruments. The lineage (what was refused, what was imported, what remains unreachable) is the thing that makes the stop checkable. *"Indiscernibility is not identity."*
