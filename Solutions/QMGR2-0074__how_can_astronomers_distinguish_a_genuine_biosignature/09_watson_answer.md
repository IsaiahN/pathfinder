**Mode: `specified` on substantive claims, `general` on the structural shape, not `grounded`.** Figures are primary and decide. World sources are secondary, corroborate but never overrule, and a conflict returns the derivation to the domain census. This is a simulated derivation checked for internal consistency against the figures, not a ground reading. No telescope was pointed and no spectrum was read.

## QUESTION

How can astronomers distinguish a genuine biosignature from confusing chemistry or instrument noise?

Restated as an effect: given a reading r, which source produced it, a biological process, an abiotic chemical or photochemical process, or the instrument plus reduction pipeline? R = |Gamma(b,a) - o'| is indexed per slot (Fig 1 :19), never averaged. Anchor: a source-discriminating instrument or a direct sample. For an exoplanet that anchor is unreachable at this budget, which is a different fact from underivable in principle (Fig 8 :40-43).

## DOMAIN CENSUS

R by arity, symmetry, scale. Arity: many-to-one, at least three source classes project onto one reading. Symmetry: not a symmetric bond; the sources are alternatives under the reading, the route source to reading is a lossy production, and each refusal is a negation, a unary operator outside the seven bonds (Fig 12 :134-136). Scale: per feature, per target and habitat, per survey and instrument; the lab-to-sky membrane is lossy (Fig 4 :27-30).

| domain | actors | currency | anchor | status |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| D1 detector channel | telescope, detector, reduction, telluric and stellar models | photons, counts, systematics | independent instrument or re-reduction | admitted |
| D2 line identification | spectral features, line lists, molecules | line identity, bits | laboratory spectra at relevant conditions | admitted |
| D3 planetary chemistry | host-star UV, temperature, volcanism, photolysis, impacts | production and loss, disequilibrium | lab experiment and constrained model, strained | admitted |
| D4 biological source | metabolisms, fractionation, homochirality | fluxes, fractionation | ground truth from life we know; in situ sampling | admitted as hypothesis, ground unreachable |
| D5 verification frame | expert readers, observatories, journals | agreement and residual | the ground, never agreement | admitted, independence zero in this run |
| X1 "this line is a biosignature" | none nameable | none | none: confuses reading with source | not a domain yet, absorbed into D3/D4 |

Union, not partition: the three confusions overlap and a single lever clears only the single-condition regions (Fig 9 :55-57). Dependency order: perception, vocabulary, objective, planning, learn and carry (Fig 3 :20, :27). Strain test applied: D1 was first written as a symmetric third hypothesis; Fig 10 :24 re-census moved it to channel failure with a different anchor, so it is now a separate loop, not a peer of life.

## VERDICT (from the figures)

A biosignature is a **source attribution**, not a reading. Identical contents from different sources are separated only by the record (Fig 8 :50-52), the instrument half is channel failure (Fig 10 :24), and the discrimination is a refusal-first procedure (Fig 5 :53, Fig 12 :140) across an enumerated habitat (Fig 11 :49-51) with independent mechanisms (Fig 8 :47, Fig 2 :42). Per-domain verdicts are kept separate: D1 and D2 and D3 and D5 are `triangulated`; D4 is `single-source` for admission and `gap` for the ground. The union of domains needs its own loop. One live strain is declared: the D3 laboratory anchor can be wrong on the source axis, so the lab-to-sky R_T must be measured. Status `specified`, ungrounded, candidate.

- **Cost if wrong:** if the shape is wrong, follow-up effort is aimed at the wrong link. A team failing D1 would be sent more spectra instead of an independent reduction; a team failing D3 would be told to add a line instead of enumerating the habitat. The cost is real but bounded and locatable, because the packet records which domain failed and why. The larger avoidable cost is spending an expert's independence by sending a recording instead of a generator, which Fig 2 :42 prices: *"Buy independence in proportion to the cost of being wrong."*
- **Undoable:** yes. Nothing is deleted if the claim fails; a refutation is added and the reach falls because something was added, not because anything was struck out. The refuters, the strain, and the domain verdicts are recorded in a provenance trail so a later reader can locate the error.
- **Reversed by:** a ground reading, not agreement. An independent re-reduction or second instrument that keeps or refuses the feature; a measured isotopic or chiral discriminator; a demonstrated abiotic pathway at the observed rates in the observed context; an in situ sample; or any of the nine pre-registered refuters in `01_figure_grounded_audit.md` section 4. A figure re-census that admits a source name from the framework itself would also reverse the shape-not-a-name limit. Until one of these fires, the verdict stands as candidate.
