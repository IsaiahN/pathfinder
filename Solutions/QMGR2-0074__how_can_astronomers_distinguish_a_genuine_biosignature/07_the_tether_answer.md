# The Tether's answer: distinguishing a genuine biosignature from confusing chemistry or instrument noise

**Mode: `specified` on every substantive claim, `general` on the structural shape, not `grounded`.** This is a shape and a route, not a detection. It is a simulated derivation checked against the figures (`05_what_shape_means.md` section 7), not a ground reading.

## 1. The residual it answers

A telescope returns a reading. The question asks for the **source** of that reading, among biology, abiotic chemistry, and the instrument plus reduction pipeline. Those three can return the same line at the same strength: Fig 8 :50-52, **"Convergent derivation and adopted import look identical in the contents. Only the record of where each came from separates them."** The residual is indexed per slot, never averaged (Fig 1 :19, :22). This is a union compound of overlapping conditions (Fig 9 :55-57), not one hard question, so it has its own loop and each domain keeps its own verdict.

## 2. The shape

1. **A reading is not a source.** Metamerism is the governing fact: a projection cannot comment on what produced it. The essay *The Hard Problem is About Source* puts it as *"If several paths create the same behavior, then behavior is a projection with lossy information"*, and its instrument *"cannot comment on the source."* A biosignature is therefore a **source attribution**, not a line.
2. **Index per slot.** Fig 1 :19: **"The residual is indexed by object slot, not aggregated."** A global fit near zero with one live narrow band is a legal state (Fig 1 :13). Do not let an average erase the one band that matters.
3. **The instrument half is channel failure.** Fig 10 :24: **"the ground does not decay, the channel does"**; Fig 10 :26 names *"stale percepts, dropped transients, saturated metrics, pooled readings."* A detection claimed from the pipeline that reduced it cannot verify itself (Fig 10 :42). Independent re-reduction and a second instrument with different systematics are the refusal here. The Venus phosphine editor's note on an ALMA processing error is the real case.
4. **Refuse before naming.** The fifth kind of gap is the one that is wrong, not missing (Fig 5 :50-53), and Fig 12 :140: **"A vocabulary that cannot refuse can only accumulate."** The order is fixed: rule out the channel, rule out the abiotic pathway, then consider biology. The field's own definition agrees: abiotic causes must be *"eliminated"* before a phenomenon is a biosignature.
5. **Context is the habitat.** Fig 11 :49: **"An actor's valence is relative to the residual, not a property it carries"**; Fig 11 :51: **"No standing list of good actors survives a change of residual."** Enumerate the star, the temperature, the co-gases, the geology, and the second-order cascades (Fig 11 :43-44, :52-53). No gas is a biosignature in isolation.
6. **Triangulate with independence, not volume.** Fig 8 :47: **"Independence is one quantity read twice."** Fig 2 :42: **"Buy independence in proportion to the cost of being wrong."** Two lines from one photochemical network are one witness. Two instruments, two wavelength regimes, or two physical mechanisms with no shared reduction path are two frames. Fig 7 :35: **"A room's agreement is variation, not a verdict."**
7. **Name only after the instrument arrives.** Fig 6 :32-33: instruments are improved from worse instruments already returning something; the unresolved band is the proto-instrument's starting point. An unidentified feature may be depth (a molecule not in the library) or genuine absence, and from inside the frame they look the same (Fig 6 :27). More search inside the same library adds no atom (Fig 8 :32).
8. **Keep the two currencies apart.** Fig 12 :125-126: **"A composition costs in two currencies, and they do not add. Bits price the description. Actions price finding out whether it holds."** Significance is a budget filter; the verdict comes from witnesses (Fig 9 :60).
9. **The honest stop is unreachable, not silence.** For an exoplanet the source is unreachable at this budget; underivable in principle is a different fact (Fig 8 :40-43). Fig 8 :39: **"So a search that finds nothing is never a proof of absence."** Status `candidate` until a source-discriminating observation or an in situ sample settles a specific target.

## 4. What it explicitly does not say

- Not that life exists, or does not exist, on any named exoplanet.
- Not that any gas (O2, CH4, PH3, DMS) is *the* biosignature. O2 plus CH4 in disequilibrium is a functional-group example, not a name (Fig 12 :124).
- Not that a high significance value is a verdict. It is a filter for the budget (Fig 9 :60).
- Not that a non-detection means no life (Fig 8 :39).
- Not that the framework settles which molecule occupies a spectral slot. It returns a shape, not a name (Fig 9 :32).
- Not `grounded`. No source-discriminating measurement was made in this derivation, and none exists for the targets at issue.
- Not that the Tether is the only account of life detection, and not a theory of everything: there is no last room (Fig 7 :6).

## 5. What would settle it: the ground

A source-discriminating instrument or a direct sample, not a further argument. The essay's spectrometer is the model: metamerism was resolved by a better instrument. Here that means:

- An independent re-reduction and a second instrument with different systematics that keeps (or refuses) the feature at the stated significance.
- A laboratory or in situ measurement of an isotopic or chiral discriminator that separates biological from abiotic production of the same molecule.
- A demonstrated abiotic pathway at the observed rates in the observed context, which refuses the biological attribution for that target.
- A direct in situ sample or sample return, which would move that target to `grounded`.
- Any of the nine pre-registered refuters in `01_figure_grounded_audit.md` section 4.

## 6. The honest stop

Fig 4 :30: **"So take the round trip and measure the gap. Without it you are navigating by dead reckoning: confident about a position nobody checked."** The lab-to-sky transfer for abiotic chemistry is lossy; the D3 anchor strain in `01` section 6 is live and is recorded rather than hidden. The answer stops at the limit of the available instruments. What makes that stop checkable is the lineage: what was refused, what was imported, what remains unreachable, and what would reverse it.

## 7. One-paragraph version for outreach

> The Tether does not name a biosignature gas, and it does not claim to have found life. It says that a spectral reading cannot determine its source: biology, abiotic photochemistry, and the reduction pipeline can return the same line, and only the record of provenance separates them. So a genuine discrimination has a shape: index the residual per line and per target; treat the instrument half as channel failure and require an independent reduction or instrument; refuse the abiotic alternatives before saying "life"; enumerate the planetary context because no gas carries its own valence; triangulate across physical mechanisms rather than count correlated lines; keep significance as a budget filter rather than a verdict; and stop honestly at the instrument limit, where a non-detection is not proof of absence. For an exoplanet the source is unreachable at this budget, so the status is candidate. What is offered is that shape, the refuters written in advance, and the instruments that would close it. The source stays open until the ground settles it.
