## What the wider literature bears on the question

1. **The Venus phosphine case is a three-in-one demonstration.** The Nature abstract says the gas is *"unexplained after exhaustive study of steady-state chemistry and photochemical pathways"* and that it *"could originate... from the presence of life."* The same page carries an editor's note about an error in ALMA data processing, and the independent record says the fixed algorithm does not recover the detection. One episode contains the biological hypothesis, the abiotic alternative, and the channel failure. It is the strongest single illustration available that a reading does not determine its source.
2. **The signal-detection frame is already the field's implicit frame.** Hit, miss, false alarm, and correct rejection map exactly onto life present/absent versus detection called/not called. The literature's bias analysis adds something the figures also say: a response criterion set to avoid false negatives raises false alarms. That is a budget filter, not a verdict.
3. **Metamerism and inverse problems say the same thing at two levels.** The colour case shows that identical receptor responses can come from different spectral power distributions. The inverse-problem literature shows that reconstructing causes from effects is typically ill-posed, with non-unique solutions and noise amplification. Together they are independent, low-overlap corroboration of F1 and F6.
4. **The base-rate result constrains "sigma."** A test with a low individual false-positive rate can still be dominated by false positives when the condition is rare. Biology is the rare condition in a large library of candidate atmospheres. This is why Fig 9 :60 demotes significance to a filter.
5. **Mimicry supplies the animal-behaviour analogue.** A harmless species can carry the warning signal of a harmful one. An abiotic pathway can carry the spectral signal of biology. In both cases the signal alone is not a reliable source attribution, and in both cases the receiver needs additional context or a second channel.

No world source conflicts with the figure verdict. If one did, the rule is to return to the domain census, never to flip the verdict. The overlap declarations in `10_source_ledger.csv` are what stop this pool of sources from being counted as eight independent witnesses.

## The two-stage offer

**Stage 1.** Send the residual (a reading does not determine its source), the six-part shape, and the five pre-registered refuters. Ask for a failure. Do not ask for trust or agreement. Record the verdict before any theory is shown.

**Stage 2, opt-in only.** If they are curious, offer the figure text and the two real case sources. Their later agreement is never pooled as corroboration, because Fig 2 :40 says *"agreement about the world is worthless when the agreeing frames share an evidence pool."*

**Stage 3.** Offer the formula as a method to reuse. That is a generator, so giving it away does not give away an answer. Each user must write down what would refute their own result and state the mode of what they get.

**Hard precondition:** stages 1 to 3 only work if the stage-1 shape survives. The D3 lab-to-sky anchor strain in `01_figure_grounded_audit.md` section 6 must be stated in the covering note, not buried.
