# Outreach draft: check the source-attribution shape first, then decide about the theory

**Use:** one to one, or as a cover letter attached to a submission through the public venues in `03_public_contact_points.csv`. **Not** for bulk send.

**Hard precondition:** do not send until the strains in `01_figure_grounded_audit.md` section 6 are stated in the covering note. The stage-1 output must survive validation or the two-stage offer collapses on contact.

---

**Subject:** One testable claim about biosignature validation, before any theory

> Hi [name],
>
> I have one specific output I would like you to check against what you already know. I am not asking you to read a theory first, and I am not asking you to agree. If the output does not survive, there is nothing else to look at.
>
> **The output (`specified`, not `grounded`):** a spectral reading does not determine its source. A biological process, an abiotic photochemical pathway, and the instrument plus reduction pipeline can return the same line at the same strength. The framework's own wording is that convergent derivation and adopted import *"look identical in the contents. Only the record of where each came from separates them"* (Fig 8 :50-52). So "the line is there" cannot be the verdict. What is owed is a **source attribution** with six properties:
>
> 1. The residual is indexed per slot (per line, band, epoch, target), not averaged: *"The residual is indexed by object slot, not aggregated"* (Fig 1 :19).
> 2. The instrument half is treated as channel failure, not as a rival species: *"the ground does not decay, the channel does"* (Fig 10 :24). A detection claimed from the pipeline that reduced it cannot verify itself (Fig 10 :42).
> 3. Abiotic and instrumental alternatives are refused **before** "life" is named. The fifth kind of gap is the one that is wrong, not missing: *"refuted: a term we hold is wrong; a term is owed that says so"* (Fig 5 :53).
> 4. The habitat is enumerated outward, because *"No standing list of good actors survives a change of residual"* (Fig 11 :51). A gas has no fixed valence.
> 5. Confirmation must come from low-overlap mechanisms, not more lines from one network: *"Independence is one quantity read twice"* (Fig 8 :47).
> 6. Significance is a filter for which target to observe next, not a witness for the verdict: *"Use filters for the budget and witnesses for the verdict"* (Fig 9 :60).
>
> This is a shape and a route, not a result. The framework returns a shape, not a name, so I am not offering a particular gas as the answer.
>
> **What I am not claiming:** that any gas is *the* biosignature, that a detection is settled by a sigma value, that a non-detection means no life, or that this derivation is ground truth. For an exoplanet the source is unreachable at this budget; a search that finds nothing *"is never a proof of absence"* (Fig 8 :39).
>
> **What would refute the shape:**
> 1. An instrument that maps a reading uniquely to biological, abiotic, or instrumental source with no contextual record.
> 2. A feature that survives independent re-reduction and an independent instrument with different systematics at the stated significance.
> 3. Two "independent" confirmations shown to share one cause (same line list, same telluric correction, same stellar model).
> 4. A gas that keeps its biosignature status across a documented change of planetary context.
> 5. A direct source measurement at an exoplanet, which would settle it for that target.
>
> I wrote these down before sending this. If any holds, the shape is wrong and I will revise it. A one-line "this fails because..." is more useful to me than agreement. If you would rather not engage, no reply is needed.
>
> **If it survives, and only then:** the public sources are below. The figures, not my summary, are the authority.
> - Figure text with line numbers, for direct checking: `FRAMEWORK/reference/03-FIGURES_TEXT.md` in the local framework; public companion at https://github.com/IsaiahN/tether/tree/general-agent/figures
> - Venus phosphine claim and its editor's note on a data-processing error: https://www.nature.com/articles/s41550-020-1174-4
> - Field definition and false-positive catalogue: https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Biosignature
>
> **If you want to use the method:** run it on a problem you know well and can check against a ground. Write down what would refute your result *before* you run it. A reported failure is more useful to me than a reported success.
>
> Thanks,
> [name]
