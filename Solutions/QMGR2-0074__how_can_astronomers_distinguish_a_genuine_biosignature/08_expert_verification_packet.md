# Expert verification packet: the Tether on biosignature validation

**What this is:** a claim in a form an astronomer or astrobiologist can check. Mode, derivation, and refuters are stated before the conclusion, so a reader knows what to look for and what would settle it. **One to one. Not a bulk send.**

**Mode: `specified` on the substantive claims, `general` on the structural shape, not `grounded`.** No source-discriminating measurement is claimed. This is a shape and a route, not a detection.

## 1. The claim, in one paragraph

A spectral reading does not determine its source. A biological process, an abiotic photochemical pathway, and the instrument plus reduction pipeline can return the same line at the same strength; only the record of provenance separates them (Fig 8 :50-52). A genuine biosignature is therefore not a line but a **source attribution** that survives six tests: per-slot indexing, independent channel verification, prior refusal of the abiotic and instrumental alternatives, habitat-context enumeration, low-overlap triangulation across physical mechanisms, and a description-length bargain. For an exoplanet the source is unreachable at this budget, so the status is `candidate`. What is offered is the shape any candidate must have, refuters written in advance, and the instruments that would close it.

## 2. Three checkable sub-claims

### C1: framework-internal plus observation-facing; checkable by reading Fig 8 and Fig 10

- **Statement:** identical contents from different sources are indistinguishable in the reading; the instrument half is a failure of the carrier, not a rival species. A detection claimed from the same pipeline that reduced it cannot verify itself.
- **Source, verbatim:** Fig 8 :50-52, *"Convergent derivation and adopted import look identical in the contents. Only the record of where each came from separates them."* Fig 10 :24, *"the ground does not decay, the channel does"*; Fig 10 :26, *"stale percepts, dropped transients, saturated metrics, pooled readings"*; Fig 10 :42, *"A verifier that can reconstruct the claim cannot verify it."*
- **What to check:** the 2020 Venus phosphine claim reported a 1-0 PH3 line at up to about 15 sigma and said the gas was *"unexplained after exhaustive study of steady-state chemistry and photochemical pathways"*; the same Nature Astronomy page carries an editor's note recording *"an error in the original processing of the ALMA Observatory data"*, and an independent record reports that re-analysis with a fixed algorithm does not recover the detection. Is this not a direct instance of the claim?
- **Refuter:** a feature that survives independent re-reduction **and** an independent instrument with different systematics at the stated significance. If it does, the channel explanation is refused for that feature.

### C2: astrobiology-facing; checkable against the biosignature literature

- **Statement:** no gas carries its own biosignature status; valence is relative to the planetary context, and the alternatives must be refused before a biological attribution is made.
- **Source, verbatim:** Fig 11 :49, *"An actor's valence is relative to the residual, not a property it carries."* Fig 11 :51, *"No standing list of good actors survives a change of residual."* Fig 5 :53, *"refuted: a term we hold is wrong; a term is owed that says so"*; Fig 12 :140, *"A vocabulary that cannot refuse can only accumulate."*
- **What to check:** the field's own definition says a biosignature is a phenomenon *"where all possible abiotic causes of this phenomenon have been eliminated"*, and warns that *"not all atmospheric disequilibria has biotic causes."* Is the refusal-first order not already the field's standard, merely stated here in figure terms?
- **Refuter:** a gas that retains biosignature status across a documented change of planetary context with all abiotic pathways still available; or a positive attribution published with no refusal step performed.

### C3: framework-internal derivation

- **Statement:** the confusions form a union, not a partition, so one lever cannot clear them; and significance is a filter for the budget, not a witness for the verdict.
- **Source, verbatim:** Fig 9 :55, *"A bundled problem is usually a union of overlapping conditions, not a list of separate ones."* Fig 9 :57, *"What remains is smaller and it is also categorically different: a case in two conditions is not a harder case of either."* Fig 9 :60, *"Use filters for the budget and witnesses for the verdict. Never let a filter hand you a verdict."* Fig 1 :19, *"The residual is indexed by object slot, not aggregated."*
- **Refuter:** one intervention that removes the instrumental, abiotic, and biological ambiguity at once and leaves a categorically simpler residue; or a demonstration that per-slot residuals carry no signal that the averaged spectrum lacks across a controlled sample.

## 3. What is explicitly not claimed

- Not that life exists or does not exist on any named target.
- Not that O2, CH4, PH3, or DMS is *the* biosignature. O2 plus CH4 in disequilibrium is a functional-group example (Fig 12 :124), not a name.
- Not that a sigma value is a verdict; it is a budget filter (Fig 9 :60).
- Not that a non-detection proves absence (Fig 8 :39, *"So a search that finds nothing is never a proof of absence."*).
- Not `grounded`. Nothing here was settled by a measurement, and no telescope was pointed for this derivation.
- Not that the Tether is the only account of life detection, and not a theory of everything: no last room (Fig 7 :6).
- **Live strain, declared:** the laboratory anchor for abiotic plausibility is itself an instrument and can be wrong on the axis it does not measure (Fig 2 :37-38); the lab-to-sky round trip has a gap R_T that must be measured (Fig 4 :30). A laboratory spectrum at Earth conditions does not automatically transfer to a hot exoplanet atmosphere.

## 5. What would settle it

A source-discriminating instrument or a direct sample, not another argument:

1. Independent re-reduction and a second instrument with different systematics that keeps or refuses the feature at the stated significance.
2. A measured isotopic or chiral discriminator that separates biological from abiotic production of the same molecule.
3. A demonstrated abiotic pathway at the observed rates in the observed context, which refuses the biological attribution.
4. An in situ sample or sample return for a specific target, which moves that target to `grounded`.
5. Any of the nine pre-registered refuters in `01_figure_grounded_audit.md` section 4.

## 6. What an expert can check in twenty minutes

1. Read Fig 8 :50-52, Fig 10 :24 and :42, Fig 11 :49 and :51, Fig 9 :55-57 and :60. Six short passages.
2. Check C1 against the Venus phosphine record: is the editor's note on a data-processing error an instance of channel failure?
3. Check C2 against the field definition of a biosignature: is refusal of abiotic causes not already required?
4. Name one instrument that would discriminate a biological from an abiotic source for a specific molecule, or say why none is possible.
5. Send the refutation. A failure is more useful than an endorsement.
