## The question restated as an effect

What is being decided is not the size of the Hubble constant but the location and cause of a residual. In effect terms: one frame (early-universe inference, CMB plus LCDM) predicts a value of H0; another frame (the local distance ladder) measures o-prime; R = |Gamma(b,a) - o'| is indexed per measurement slot, that is per rung, anchor and method, and must never be averaged into a single global tension (Fig 1 :19, Fig 1 :22).

- Option A, "the difference is a measurement calibration or systematic error": leads to the prediction that the drift lives in the channel, not in the ground. The expansion rate is unchanged; the instrument reading it has lost contact (Fig 10 :24).
- Option B, "the difference is new physics": leads to the prediction that the model library Gamma is wrong and a primitive must be imported into the early-universe model.
- What an answer would look like, written down and bound as an ordinary term before the run (Formula, "Direction"): a per-slot decomposition of R into contributions from each ladder rung, plus the description of a source-discriminating instrument that would tell a miscalibrated channel from a wrong model. Until that shape is bound it predicts nothing and no check in the loop can see it.

The verdict recorded in `09_watson_answer.md` is Option A as a live, non-excluded channel hypothesis, kept per domain and never averaged. Option B is not refused; it is moved to a different domain with its own loop. The figures settle the shape of the discrimination, not which term is actually wrong.

## What is deliberately not here

- No personal emails of named individuals.
- No ranking of people by how likely they are to agree.
- No bulk-send plan.
- No claim that this derivation is `grounded`, no claim that it settles whether the Hubble tension is a calibration problem, and no claim that the framework is the only true account of cosmological measurement.

Three figure-grounded reasons, plus one practical one:

1. A room is not an anchor. *"Alignment is triangulation, not negotiation. A mirror cannot correct you."* (Fig 2 :35). Gathering agreeable readers manufactures the correlation that disqualifies a verdict.
2. Correlation has valence. *"agreement about the world is worthless when the agreeing frames share an evidence pool"* (Fig 2 :40). Two teams reducing the same Cepheid photometry are one witness wearing two names.
3. A verifier must be outside the interior. *"A verifier that can reconstruct the claim cannot verify it."* (Fig 10 :42). The first contact therefore carries the residual and its refuter, not the framework.
4. Legally, unsolicited bulk email to scraped addresses breaches GDPR and CAN-SPAM style rules in many jurisdictions and gets the sending domain blacklisted. Public opt-in routes avoid that failure as well as the structural one.
