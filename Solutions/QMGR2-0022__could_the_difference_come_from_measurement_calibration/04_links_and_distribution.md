## What the wider literature bears on the question

1. **The residual is real and not method-specific.** The KITP summary's combination of any three independent late-universe probes giving 4.0 to 5.8 sigma tension is the strongest world corroboration of treating R as a genuine ground-visible residual (Fig 2 :31-32 independence).
2. **A broad calibration search has come back empty, so far.** SH0ES ran approximately 70 variants and reports no indication of measurement error among them. Under Fig 8 :39 this is SEARCHED at fixed reach, not a proof of absence. It does raise the cost of asserting a single-rung calibration explanation.
3. **The independent ladder does not simply agree with the Cepheid ladder.** Freedman's TRGB result sits midway between Planck and SH0ES. That is a nonzero round-trip gap R_T (Fig 4 :30), and it is exactly the kind of measurement the framework says to keep rather than average away.
4. **The union is real.** The Di Valentino review explicitly lists new physics, large-scale structure and "multiple, unrelated errors" as candidate solution classes. Under Fig 9 :54-56 these must be unbundled, not blended.
5. **Dominance of one calibration family is a structural concern.** If several "independent" probes still rely on the same SN Ia calibration chain, their agreement is one pool talking (Fig 2 :40). The overlap must be declared per probe.

## Distribution: offer, do not push

Figure-grounded reasons:

- **Fig 4 :22-25** — *"Only methods cross up. Only head starts come down. A recording carried upward looks like knowledge and is a description of one occasion."* Sending a finished conclusion about who is right before anyone has described a residual sends a recording, not a generator. Lead with the per-slot method and the refuter; offer links after.
- **Fig 10 :42** — *"A verifier that can reconstruct the claim cannot verify it."* Seat 4 must not hold the interiors. A recipient who has absorbed the whole framework before being asked for a verdict has joined the frame being checked.
- **Fig 9 :27-28** — *"describe it before you go looking, or any frame you pick will seem to fit."* Ask what they would expect to see in a per-rung residual table if calibration were the cause before giving them any interpretation.
- **Fig 2 :35** — *"Alignment is triangulation, not negotiation. A mirror cannot correct you."* A mass send selected for people who already favour one explanation produces a convention, not corroboration.

**Recommended order in a reply:** (1) the per-slot shape claim and its four refuters; (2) ask what they would count as a failure; (3) record their verdict; (4) then the links, with the note that the figures, not the summary, are the authority.

## The two-stage offer

- **Stage 1 before stage 2** is Fig 10 :52 — *"Bring a characterised residual, never the source"* — and Fig 2 :45 — *"Frames range against the ground. They do not negotiate with it."* The recipient checks the shape without holding the framework's interior.
- **Stage 2 is opt-in by construction.** Once a recipient has absorbed the theory, their agreement stops being evidence. Their verdict on stage 1 must be recorded *before* any theory is shown, and later agreement is never pooled as corroboration (Fig 2 :40).
- **Stage 3 sends a method, which is permitted.** Fig 4 :22: methods cross up. The formula is a generator, so giving it away does not give away an answer.
- **Stage 3 still needs a ground per problem.** `THE_FORMULA.md` "Direction": a frame *"cannot check the direction of its own question, because the shape a frame is searching for is not a term it holds."* Each user must write down what would refute their result before running it, and label the mode of what they get.

**Hard precondition:** stages 1 to 3 only work if the stage-1 output survives. As currently drafted it is a shape, not a completed per-rung table. Produce the table for real data before sending.
