# The Tether's answer: could the difference come from calibration or systematics?

**Mode: `specified` — not `grounded`.** This is a shape and a discrimination route, not a result. Simulated derivation checked against the figures (`05_what_shape_means.md` section 6), not a ground reading.

## 1. The residual it answers

The Hubble tension is the gap R between H0 inferred from the early universe under LCDM (Planck 2018: 67.4 +/- 0.5 km/s/Mpc) and H0 measured from the local distance ladder (SH0ES 2022: 73.04 +/- 1.04 km/s/Mpc). R is about 5 sigma. The question asks whether calibration or systematic error can produce it. The answer is about the location and kind of a fault, not about which number is correct.

R is a **union compound**, not one hard question (Fig 9 :54-56): zero point, crowding, dust, metallicity, pipeline choices and model assumptions overlap. It is indexed per slot (Fig 1 :19), and averaging it into a single tension is how the live signal disappears (Fig 1 :22).

## 2. The shape, one loop per domain, kept separate

**D4 Comparison frame.** The gap is genuinely ground-visible: combining any three independent late-universe probes gives 4.0 to 5.8 sigma tension with the early-universe value (Verde, Treu and Riess 2019). Independence is *"how little they draw on the same evidence"* (Fig 2 :31-32), so probes sharing the same SN Ia chain are one witness, not three. Verdict, `specified` and triangulated: R is real and not an artefact of one method or team.

**D1 Ladder frame.** A calibration error is a failure of the channel while *"the ground does not decay, the channel does"* (Fig 10 :24). Its named forms are *"stale percepts, dropped transients, saturated metrics, pooled readings"* (Fig 10 :25), and its honest output is *"you have lost touch"* (Fig 10 :27-28), not a physics verdict. So calibration is a well-formed, live explanation here. It must be tested by pushing the anchor: *"If it moves when you push on it, it is not an anchor"* (Fig 2 :29-30). Verdict, `specified` and triangulated: calibration is structurally possible and testable; no single geometric anchor currently moves under a one-at-a-time removal (Riess et al. 2019 report less than 0.7% change), which is a partial pass, not a clearance.

**D3 Systematics frame.** A broad sweep of approximately 70 analysis variants found *"no indication this arises from measurement errors or analysis variations considered to date"* (Riess et al. 2022). Under Fig 8 :39, *"So a search that finds nothing is never a proof of absence"*, that sentence is closure-bounded. An independent TRGB ladder returns 69.8, between Planck and SH0ES (Freedman et al. 2019), which is a nonzero round-trip gap (Fig 4 :30). Verdict, `specified` and ungrounded: no culprit has been located; the union of overlapping conditions remains unbundled; `unreached`, not `refuted`.

**D2 Early-universe frame.** If the ladder channel is shown clean, the residual migrates to the model library: a wrong sound horizon or a missing primitive is an import question (Fig 6 :23), not a calibration question. That is a different domain with its own loop and its own ground. Verdict: not decided here, and not averaged with D1 or D3.

## 3. What it explicitly does not say

- Not "the tension is a calibration error." That is a name, and no ground reading here pays for it.
- Not "systematics have been ruled out." The search was bounded by the variants considered (Fig 8 :39).
- Not "new physics is required." That would be an import before SEARCHED and INWARD are exhausted (Formula step 7).
- Not a new value of H0, and not a derivation of one ladder from another.
- Not `grounded`. Nothing here is asserted as settled by a measurement taken in this folder.

## 4. What would settle it — the ground

A source-discriminating measurement, not a further argument. From *The Hard Problem is About Source*: an instrument that reads green *"cannot comment on the source"*; the spectrometer, not an argument, resolved the case. Here:

1. **Anchor push test.** Force each geometric anchor to vary independently and record the resulting H0 shift. A genuine anchor does not move (Fig 2 :29-30).
2. **Per-rung residual table.** Report R+_s for each rung and method; find the live slot (Fig 1 :19).
3. **Common-cause test.** Three or more probes with no shared calibration all above 71 km/s/Mpc at more than 3 sigma refute a common calibration cause.
4. **Zero-point confirmation.** A documented shift that reconciles ladder and Planck while the anchors still agree confirms calibration.
5. **Sound-horizon test.** A measurement of r_s differing from the LCDM prediction moves the fault to D2.
6. Standing falsifiers from `01` section 4; all were written before this conclusion.

## 5. The honest stop

**Unreached, not unreachable.** The figures can report what a discriminator would have to look like, and they can report that the candidate causes form a union that must be split. They cannot report which term is wrong in our world, because naming it would mean already holding it (Fig 13 :135). *"Unreachable is a verdict here, not a silence"* (Fig 13 :134). If no instrument at any resolution can discriminate a channel fault from a model fault on these readings, that is a recorded limit, not an excuse to pick a side.

The essays add a warning about my own standing: *"Prestige can fund an action... but it cannot settle an action. Only the ground can settle it."* The authority of a team or a review is a budget to try a test, not a verdict.

## 6. One-paragraph version for outreach

> The Tether does not say whether the Hubble tension is a calibration error or new physics. It says something checkable first: the residual must be reported per ladder rung, not averaged into one number, because averaging is how a live signal disappears; a calibration error is a failure of the measuring channel while the expansion rate it measures does not change, so the honest output of such a failure is a loss of contact rather than a physics verdict; and a genuine anchor must not move when you push on it. The search so far has found no bad rung among the variants considered, but an empty search is not a proof of absence, and an independent TRGB ladder sits between the two published values. What the framework offers is the shape of a discriminator, a per-rung residual table and four refuters written in advance. It stops where the available instruments stop discriminating, and records the stop so it can be checked.
