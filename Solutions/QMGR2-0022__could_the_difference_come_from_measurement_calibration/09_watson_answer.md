## VERDICT (from the figures)

Calibration and other systematics are a live, well-formed and testable explanation for the Hubble tension residual, because a calibration fault is a failure of the channel while the ground does not decay. The figures license a discrimination shape, not a culprit: report R per rung, never average it; test the anchor by pushing it; unbundle the union of overlapping causes; and stop at a source-discriminating instrument. They do not license "the tension is calibration" or "the tension is new physics", and no measurement in this folder pays for either. The world sources show a clean broad search (SH0ES), a nonzero gap across independent ladders (TRGB), and genuine low-overlap triangulation across probes (KITP), so the honest status is `specified`, `candidate`, ungrounded, `unreached` on which term is actually wrong. Per-domain verdicts above are kept separate and never averaged.

- **cost if wrong:** bounded and locatable. The packet states mode, per-slot method and refuters before the conclusion, so an error is findable rather than invisible. The real cost is spending the independence of outside readers by sending a conclusion instead of a generator. Fig 2 :42 prices that: "Buy independence in proportion to the cost of being wrong." It is avoidable by leading with the shape and the refuter.
- **undoable:** yes. The culprit claim is not made, so nothing factual has to be withdrawn to send a corrected packet. A completed per-rung residual table can be added when real data are analysed, and the per-domain verdicts can be updated independently. No primitive is created or destroyed.
- **reversed by:** a ground reading, not agreement. A documented zero-point shift that reconciles ladder and Planck while the anchors still agree would confirm calibration; three or more non-shared-calibration probes above 71 km/s/Mpc at more than 3 sigma would refute a common calibration cause; a sound-horizon measurement differing from the LCDM prediction would move the fault to the early-universe domain; a demonstrated instrument limit would make the discriminator shape an honest `unreached`. Until one of these fires, the verdict stands as `candidate`.
