## Distribution: offer, do not push

- **Fig 10 :52**:*"Bring a characterised residual, never the source."* Lead with the two-branch split and the refuter. The method comes only after the recipient has recorded a verdict on stage 1.
- **Fig 2 :35**:*"Alignment is triangulation, not negotiation. A mirror cannot correct you."* Do not ask for agreement. Ask what the recipient would count as a failure.
- **Fig 9 :27**:*"describe it before you go looking, or any frame you pick will seem to fit"* (continued at :28). Describe the residual in effect terms before naming a field or a venue.
- **Fig 2 :40**:*"disqualifying for a verdict: agreement about the world is worthless when the agreeing frames share an evidence pool"*. A list selected for receptiveness manufactures the disqualifying correlation.
- **Fig 4 :22**:*"Only methods cross up. Only head starts come down."* Stage 3 gives away a method, not an answer, so it does not spend anyone's independence.

## The two-stage offer

1. **Stage 1.** Send the residual, the two branches, the per-slot rule and the pre-registered refuter from `02_outreach_email.md`. Record the recipient's verdict before any method is shown.
2. **Stage 2, opt-in only.** If they are curious, offer the framework links. The figures, not this summary, are the authority. Their later agreement is never pooled as corroboration (Fig 2 :40).
3. **Stage 3, opt-in only.** Offer the formula as a method to reuse, with the instruction that each user writes their own refuter and states their own mode. Fig 6 :32: *"An instrument is not built from a description. It is improved from a worse instrument already returning something."*

**Hard precondition:** the offer only works if the stage-1 output survives. In this simulated derivation no dataset was opened, so the correctness of the shape is checked only against the figures. That is the honest limit and it must be stated in any actual contact.

## What is not distributed

No personal emails, no purchased list, no bulk send, no ranking of recipients by likelihood of belief. Fig 7 :35: *"A room's agreement is variation, not a verdict. Selection between rooms is where the ground votes."*
