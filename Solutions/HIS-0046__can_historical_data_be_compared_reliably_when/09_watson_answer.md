## QUESTION

Decide, in effect terms, what the Tether says when two readings of the past were produced by different observers using different categories and instruments, and someone reads the difference as a change in the world. Option A: answer yes or no for historical data in general. Option B: unbundle the question, decide which anchor branch applies, and state the conditions under which a comparison is licensed. What each leads to: A delivers a verdict no frame can certify and hides a channel change inside a trend; B delivers a checkable shape, two branches, per-slot refuters, and an honest stop where no bridge exists.

## DOMAIN CENSUS

R by arity, symmetry and scale, then one line per domain with actors, currency and anchor. Partition or union, and order by dependency.

- **R (the residual).** The gap between a claimed world change and what two differently produced readings license. Arity: three, ground, channel, reading, at two epochs. Symmetry: the numeric difference is antisymmetric but the historical relation is ordered, because the later frame inherits from the earlier one. This is a sequence bond, not identity. Scale: the channel change lives at the measuring arrangement; the alleged change lives at the world, with a lossy transform between.
- **Domain 1, the referent.** Actors: the population, climate, quantity or class the data is about. Currency: its state. Anchor: the referent itself when it exists independently of the rule, or the convention when the question is constitutively about the convention (Fig 2 :36). This is a domain: a nameable anchor exists in both branches.
- **Domain 2, the channel.** Actors: observers, categories, instruments, units, coding rules. Currency: readings and labels. Anchor: a calibration reference that does not move with the channel, namely an overlap or parallel measurement. This is a domain.
- **Domain 3, the record.** Actors: metadata, crosswalks, coding instructions, correspondence tables. Currency: the account of how each reading was derived. Anchor: the record itself, inspectable and unmoved by agreement. This is a domain.
- **Domain 4, the verdict frame.** Actors: analysts, historians, readers. Currency: agreement and residual. Anchor: the ground, never agreement. In this simulated run all four domains draw on one evidence pool (the same figures, essays and fetched pages), so this domain's independence is zero. Its agreement is not a witness.
- **Union, not partition.** "Comparable" bundles same referent, bridged channel and distinguishing record, and they overlap. The compound where both referent and channel drifted is categorically different from either alone (Fig 9 :55).
- **Order by dependency.** Referent, then anchor, then bridge, then round trip, then record, then verdict (Fig 3 :20). Re-coding cannot rescue a broken referent step.

Strain test: the apparent conflict between Fig 10 :18 (a declared meaning is not grounded) and Fig 2 :36 (a convention can be a legitimate anchor) was re-censused. Fig 10 :18 governs a frame declaring a meaning and calling it settled; Fig 2 :36 governs a question constitutively about the convention. Different objects, so no strain survives.

## VERDICT (from the figures)

The question is a union, not one question, and it decides at the anchor. If the quantity exists independently of the category, the referent must be an anchor that does not move when the label changes, and a comparison is licensed only by an overlap measurement whose round-trip gap is measured, not declared; without one the comparison is `unreached` or `unreachable at this budget`, with the missing instrument's shape described. If the quantity is constituted by the classification, changing the rule changed what the series describes and the legitimate comparison is of the conventions, not of a world trend. In both branches a shared label carries zero corroboration until the record separates convergence from borrowing, the check is per category, and agreement among experts is not calibration. This is `general` on the shape and `specified` on the per-domain predictions, ungrounded on every case.

- **cost if wrong:** bounded and locatable. The wrong reading writes a channel change into history as a trend, or suppresses a real change by refusing all comparison. Both errors are per category and findable because the packet states the branch, the bridge, `R_T`, the provenance condition and five refuters before the conclusion. The avoidable extra cost is spending outside readers' independence by asking them to agree rather than to refute; Fig 2 :42 says "Buy independence in proportion to the cost of being wrong."
- **undoable:** yes at the level of a comparison. Re-code, rebuild a bridge from an archived overlap, remeasure, and report a new `R_T`. The underlying data are not consumed by a re-analysis. It is not always undoable in practice: if the raw records and metadata were destroyed, the bridge cannot be rebuilt and the comparison is permanently unreachable at this budget, which is not a proof that the series were never comparable.
- **reversed by:** a ground reading, not agreement. A held-out parallel measurement showing old and new arrangements agree within a stated tolerance would license branch-1 comparisons; a record proving convergent derivation would turn a shared label into real corroboration; a demonstration that a quantity we assigned to branch 2 has a referent surviving the definition change would move it to branch 1; and any of the five pre-registered refuters firing would amend the corresponding claim. Agreement among historians, a larger sample or a higher correlation would reverse none of them.
