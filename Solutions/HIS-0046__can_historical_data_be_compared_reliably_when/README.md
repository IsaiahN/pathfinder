## What is deliberately not here

- No personal emails of named individuals.
- No ranking of people by how likely they are to agree with this answer.
- No bulk-send plan, no purchased list, no receptiveness as a selection criterion.

Three reasons, in the figures' own words:

1. **A room does not upgrade the label.** *"A room reaching a conclusion without the ground is agreement, and agreement among frames is a weighted average of influence. Adding people raises the vocabulary and does not add an anchor."* (Seat Map :283). Gathering more historians to agree does not calibrate an instrument.
2. **Correlation disqualifies a verdict.** *"High correlation is disqualifying for a verdict and efficient for a convention."* (Seat Map :163). Selecting contacts for likely agreement manufactures exactly the correlation that makes their agreement worthless.
3. **A verifier must not hold the interiors.** *"A verifier that can reconstruct the claim cannot verify it."* (Fig 10 :42). The first contact carries the residual and its refuter, not this folder's derivation.

Legally: unsolicited bulk email to scraped personal addresses breaches GDPR and CAN-SPAM style rules in many jurisdictions. The public, opt-in venues in `03_public_contact_points.csv` avoid that failure as well as the structural one.

## Honest standing of this package

This is a **simulated derivation checked for internal consistency against the figures, not a ground reading.** *The Recursive Self-Improvement Trap* says LLM output is *"simulacra"* and that *"the truly testable output would need to be derived from the real architecture or working the formula yourself."* It cannot settle the question it reports on. No dataset was opened, no bridge was measured, no category was recoded. What it offers is the shape any reliable comparison must have, the falsifiers written in advance, and the instruments that would close it.
