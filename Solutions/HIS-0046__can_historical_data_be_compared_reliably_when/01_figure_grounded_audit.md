## 0. The residual in effect terms

Two readings of the same stretch of the world exist: `R1` produced by measuring arrangement `C1` (observers, categories, instruments, coding rules) at time `t1`, and `R2` produced by `C2` at `t2`. Someone reads `Δ = R2 - R1` as a change in the world. The residual is the gap between that assertion and what the two readings can actually license, once `C1` is not `C2`.

Put in the formula's terms: `R = |Γ(b,a) - o'|` per slot. A comparison silently uses one library `Γ` for both epochs. The question is exactly whether that is legal. The harm is two-sided. Calling a channel change a world change writes a trend into history that never happened; refusing every comparison loses real changes. So the answer must distinguish the two, per slot, and must say what would settle the distinction.

Stated as the effect we want: a rule that tells us, for a given pair of readings, whether `Δ` is a ground change or a channel change, and what to demand before we are allowed to call it a ground change. That is the sought-for shape. Per `THE_FORMULA.md` "Direction", it is made an ordinary term here by writing it down and letting it accrue a residual like anything else.

## 2. Domain census

### R by arity, symmetry and scale

- **Arity:** at least three, not two. The comparison relates ground `G`, channel `C`, and reading `R`, at two epochs. A two-term view (number versus number) is the error being audited.
- **Symmetry:** the numeric difference is antisymmetric, but the historical relation is **ordered**. The later frame inherits from the earlier one by adopted import, so the epochs are not interchangeable. This is a `→` sequence, not a `≡` identity. Fig 12 :66 *"two names, one referent"* is the test that fails here.
- **Scale:** the channel change lives at the scale of the measuring arrangement; the alleged change lives at the scale of the world. There is a lossy transform between them (Fig 10 :68). That is why the round trip, not a direct subtraction, is the right instrument.

### One line per domain (actors / currency / anchor)

- **Domain A, the referent.** Actors: the population, climate, illness or quantity the data is about. Currency: the state of that referent. Anchor: the referent itself, which must not move when a category moves. This is a domain: a nameable anchor exists whenever the quantity is not constituted by the measuring rule. Where the referent is constituted by the category (official unemployment, legal "criminal", "illiteracy" by a stated threshold), the anchor is the convention, not an independent world, and Fig 2 :36 applies. That is a different branch, not a failure.
- **Domain B, the channel.** Actors: observers, coding indexes, instruments, units, definitions. Currency: readings and labels. Anchor: a calibration reference that does not move with the channel, namely an overlap or parallel measurement, or a physical standard. This is a domain; the anchor is nameable.
- **Domain C, the record.** Actors: metadata, provenance, coding instructions, correspondence tables. Currency: the account of how each reading was made. Anchor: the record itself, which can be inspected and does not change when a reader agrees with it. This is a domain.
- **Domain D, the verdict frame.** Actors: analysts, historians, readers. Currency: agreement and residual. Anchor: the ground, never agreement. In this simulated run all four domains draw on one evidence pool (the same figures, essays and fetched pages), so Domain D's independence is zero. Its agreement is not a witness.

Note what is **not** a separate domain: "the people changed", "the categories changed", "the methods changed" are three ways the channel can move. They are Domain B, one loop. Splitting them into three domains would triple-count the same failure.

### Partition or union

A **union**, not a partition. "Comparable" bundles at least three conditions: same referent (A), a calibrated channel or a measured bridge (B), and a record that distinguishes convergence from borrowing (C). They overlap. The critical intersection is the case where both the referent drifted and the channel drifted; that is categorically different from either alone and is where a single lever fails.

### Order by dependency (Fig 3)

1. Establish the referent and decide which branch applies (A). A reading taken before this is a reading of nothing: *"A step whose input never arrives cannot be diagnosed, only its predecessor can."* (Fig 3 :22).
2. Establish the bridge or calibration (B) before comparing any numbers.
3. Establish the record (C) before deciding whether agreement means anything.
4. Only then issue the verdict (D).

Fig 3 :19: *"Optimising a link that already works cannot rescue one that does not."* Re-coding a series more finely (step 2 work) cannot rescue a comparison whose referent changed (step 1 broken).

## 3. Findings

### F1: "Comparable" is a union compound, not one hard question

Fig 9 :54: **"And when the split lands on a union rather than a partition, unbundle before you search again."**
Fig 9 :55: **"A bundled problem is usually a union of overlapping conditions, not a list of separate ones."**
Fig 9 :56: **"A single lever clears the region where only one condition holds and leaves the intersections standing, because the second condition still binds."**

A single harmonisation of category labels clears only the label region. Where the referent also changed, the intersection remains and now looks deceptively clean. Fig 9 :57: *"What remains is smaller and it is also categorically different: a case in two conditions is not a harder case of either."*

**Refuter:** show that in the case at hand the three conditions are independent (a partition), so clearing one leaves no compound intersection. Then the union claim is too strong for that case and the comparison can be searched one condition at a time.

### F2: The thing that changed is the channel, not the ground

Fig 10 :24: **"the ground does not decay, the channel does."**
Fig 10 :25: **"stale percepts, dropped transients, saturated metrics, pooled readings"**
Fig 10 :27: **"the output is not a verdict, it is"** (continued at :28) **"you have lost touch"**

Observers, categories and instruments are the channel. When they change, the first thing to suspect is that contact degraded, not that the world changed. The framework's name for the honest output of that state is "you have lost touch", not a trend and not a silence.

**Refuter:** a source-discriminating instrument showing that, across the transition, the same referent returned the same reading under both channels, so the channel change was inert for the measured quantity. Then the channel is not the operative variable here.

### F3: The anchor must not update; this produces two branches

Fig 2 :29: **"An anchor is whatever settles the question and does not change its answer because of what any frame thinks."**
Fig 2 :33: **"The anchor must not update."**
Fig 2 :34: **"If it does, panel 3 turns into panel 1: the anchor becomes another frame, everything drifts together, and the drift is coherent, which is why nobody notices."**
Fig 2 :36: **"An anchor is legitimate when the question is constitutively about it."**
Fig 2 :41: **"efficient for a convention: agreement about a shared meaning is the product, not a corruption of it"**

Branch 1, quantities whose referent exists independently of the measuring rule (temperature, height, crop yield): the referent is the anchor. It must not update when the category is renamed. A comparison is licensed only if both readings range against it.

Branch 2, quantities constituted by the classification (official unemployment, a legal category, a diagnostic threshold): Fig 2 :36 makes the convention a legitimate anchor because the question is constitutively about the convention. When the rule changes, the world described by the series changed too. Then the two datasets measure different things, and the legitimate comparison is of the conventions, where agreement is the product (Fig 2 :41), not of a world trend.

This branch is not a retreat. It is the figure's own anchor rule applied honestly. Forcing every case into branch 1 would be a strain, so the census splits instead.

**Refuter:** a case we assigned to branch 2 shown to have a referent that survives the definition change (for example, a constant underlying reality whose official definition merely narrowed), which moves it to branch 1 and reopens the bridge requirement.

### F4: A declared category is not a grounded category

Fig 10 :18: **"a declared meaning is not a grounded one"**
Fig 10 :49: **"Declaring a meaning does not ground it."**
Fig 10 :19: **"→ install what can be violated"**

Redefining a category and declaring the new series comparable does not make it comparable. What must be installed is something that can be violated: a parallel measurement, a documented crosswalk with error bounds, a check that could fail. This is the direct answer to the "categories changed" part of the question.

**Refuter:** a definition change shown to be purely notational, with a bijective mapping whose preservation of every measured quantity is demonstrated rather than declared. That is a genuine "installed" check and would satisfy the rule.

### F5: A bridge must be built from a worse instrument already returning something

Fig 6 :32: **"An instrument is not built from a description. It is improved from a worse instrument already returning something."**
Fig 6 :34: **"The question is not whether a sensor could exist. It is whether anything, at any resolution, is already returning something that fails to resolve."**
Fig 6 :35: **"Where nothing is, there is no edge to extend from, and the search for a proto-instrument is a search for one that is already there."**

The analogue of the proto-instrument is an overlap: a period, region or sample where old and new methods both read the same referent. Climate science does exactly this, and the WMO treats parallel measurements as a guideline (world source, `10_source_ledger.csv`). Where no overlap was ever recorded, there is no edge to extend from. That is `unreachable at this budget` or genuinely absent, and from inside the frame the two look the same (Fig 6 :27). Fig 8 :39: **"So a search that finds nothing is never a proof of absence."**

**Refuter:** produce a legal bridge that was built from a pure description with no prior returning instrument (so Fig 6 :32 does not govern this quantity), or show the two channels agree on an overlapping sample so the bridge already exists.

### F6: Measure the round trip, do not assume the labels are the same

Fig 4 :30: **"So take the round trip and measure the gap. Without it you are navigating by dead reckoning: confident about a position nobody checked."**
Fig 4 :31: **"The gap is not an error to eliminate. It is the measurement of what the coarser description cannot hold, and it is the only honest report of how much was given up."**
Fig 4 :27: **"The transform between scales loses information, and that is the point."**

Carry an old reading up to the new category (abstraction), then bring it back down to a concrete expectation (concretisation), and compare. The difference is `R_T = gap(x, (T_E ∘ T_A)(x))`. A harmonisation that reports no gap has most likely assumed it to zero. The gap is not noise to be cleaned; it is the report.

**Refuter:** a round trip that returns the original reading to within the measurement error of the instrument, with the tolerance stated in advance and the test run on a held-out sample. Then `R_T` is negligible for that comparison and the bridge is licensed.

### F7: The same label is not the same thing; only the record separates the cases

Fig 8 :50: **"But shared atoms are not the signature. Two frames can hold the same atom by convergence, reaching it separately, which is corroboration at maximum independence."**
Fig 8 :51: **"Convergent derivation and adopted import look identical in the contents. Only the record of where each came from separates them"**
Fig 12 :95: **"Two entries with an identical ingredient set are not variants of one thing."**
Fig 12 :96: **"They are different things whose notation cannot tell them apart, and the difference is carried entirely by the bond."**

Two datasets using the same category name may have reached it independently (corroboration) or the later one may have inherited the name from the earlier one (one frame wearing two names, Fig 8 :49). Their contents are identical. Provenance is the only discriminator, and Fig 10 :65 adds the limit: *"Provenance shows a ruling was reached legitimately. It never shows it was right: the ground does that."* So the record licenses an audit, not a pass.

**Refuter:** independent evidence that the two frames reached the shared label by convergent derivation, with separate histories and no borrowing, plus a ground reading that confirms the referent. That upgrades the label from zero corroboration to real corroboration.

### F8: Check per slot, never average

Fig 1 :19: **"The residual is indexed by object slot, not aggregated."**
Fig 1 :22: **"Aggregating across slots is how a live signal disappears into an average."**
Fig 10 :25: **"saturated metrics, pooled readings"** (named again as a channel failure)

A single trend line can hide one category whose meaning drifted while the other ten were stable. The overall series looks comparable; the one live slot is where the error is. Comparability must be reported per category, with the drifted ones named.

**Refuter:** show that the residual is uniform across all slots in the case at hand, so an aggregate and a per-slot report agree. Then the aggregation warning does not bite there.

### F9: Agreement is not calibration

Fig 7 :35: **"A room's agreement is variation, not a verdict. Selection between rooms is where the ground votes."**
Fig 2 :35: **"Alignment is triangulation, not negotiation. A mirror cannot correct you."**
Fig 2 :40: **"agreement about the world is worthless when the agreeing frames share an evidence pool"**
Fig 9 :60: **"Use filters for the budget and witnesses for the verdict. Never let a filter hand you a verdict."**

Historians agreeing that two series are comparable is a convention, not a measurement. If they learned the category from the same training tradition, they are one evidence pool. Statistical significance, sample size and the effective-sample-size correction (Fig 2 :44) are filters for how much budget to spend, not witnesses for the verdict.

**Refuter:** two genuinely low-overlap frames, each ranged separately against a ground neither can move, both returning comparability. That is triangulation, not agreement, and would count.

### F10: "Not comparable" is a verdict, not a silence

Fig 13 :134: **"Unreachable is a verdict here, not a silence"**
Fig 8 :39: **"So a search that finds nothing is never a proof of absence."**
Fig 9 :32: **"you get a kind of thing, never the answer itself"**

When no bridge exists, the honest output is a shape with a named missing thing: what an overlap measurement would have to look like, what an anchor would have to be. It is not "we cannot say" and not "the data are useless". It is a refusal to compose over a reading whose channel is unknown, with the lineage recorded.

**Refuter:** a bridge found by further search inside the same closure (a missing overlap in an archive that was not fully searched). That would make the case `unreachable at this budget` rather than `genuinely absent` and would change the instruction from import to more search.

## 4. Pre-registered refuter (written before the conclusion)

Per Seat Map :287: *"a falsifier written before the run is a design instrument, not only an honesty device."* The shape claim is refuted if any of the following holds:

1. A pair of readings from demonstrably different channels, with no overlap measurement and no anchor that fails to update, is shown by a source-discriminating instrument to differ exactly as the recorded trend predicts, and the result survives replication. Then the channel was inert and the trend is real.
2. A shared category label used in two epochs is shown by an independent ground reading to refer to exactly the same extension and the same intension at both times, with a record proving the later users did not inherit the label. Then the provenance requirement's bite is zero in that case.
3. A direct comparison of drifted categories is shown to trend down in residual under repeated recoding without a bridge, so the channel difference was depth (searchable inside the closure) rather than a genuine break.
4. The anchor in a case assigned to branch 1 is derived from the channel, so the referent's identity is constituted by the category after all. Then Domain A is not a domain for that case and the census must be redone.
5. A round trip is reported with the original returned to within a stated measurement tolerance on held-out data, so `R_T` is negligible and the bridge is licensed without further import.

If any fires, the corresponding figure claim is withdrawn for that case and the verdict is amended in `11_audit.md`. Until one is run, nothing here is `grounded`.

## 5. Recommendation

1. Lead with the union split, not with a yes or no (F1, F3). Ask which branch the quantity belongs to before asking whether the numbers are comparable.
2. For branch 1, demand a bridge: an overlap or parallel measurement, with a measured round-trip gap `R_T` (F5, F6). A declared crosswalk is not a bridge (F4).
3. For branch 2, compare conventions and say plainly that the world described changed (F3). Agreement about meaning is legitimate there, but it is a convention, not corroboration of a world trend.
4. Report per category. Name the drifted slots (F8).
5. Treat same-label as zero evidence until provenance separates convergence from borrowing (F7).
6. Say "unreached" or "unreachable at this budget" when no bridge exists, and describe the missing instrument's shape (F10). Never convert that silence into a trend.
7. Do not ask colleagues to endorse the comparison. Ask what would refute it (F9).
