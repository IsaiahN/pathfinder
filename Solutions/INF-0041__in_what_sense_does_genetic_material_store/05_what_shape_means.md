## 1. A shape is a constraint, not a filler

From *The Library of Babel* (opened 2026-11-23): **"You can cut your search short if the set of books returned is small and descriptive enough that it provides a general shape of the answer that satisfies your question."** And on the method: *"With each new term gained, you are able to narrow the space by definition and deepen your vocabulary."*

So a **shape** is the narrowed form of an answer you can state before you can name its occupant. It constrains; it does not fill. For this question:

- `CODING(???)MEANING` is a shape: it says the two quantities are held apart and a relation is owed.
- "meaning is in the ribosome" is a name: it fills the slot from the biology frame.
- "meaning is a three-term relation: arrangement, reader, ground that pays" is a shape with the arity fixed and the occupants unbound.

Fig 9 :27-28: **"describe it before you go looking, or any frame you pick will seem to fit."** Had I picked "the ribosome" first and fitted the figures to it, any field would have seemed to agree.

## 2. How the shape is obtained here

- *Library of Babel*: use the vocabulary already held that is closest to the thing, look for recurring new terms that overlap or correlate with it, recurse. The anchor is the question itself: *"This anchor is provided by your own question."*
- Fig 6 :30: **"The description travels. Fails by being sharpened into terms only we can read. Cast it in effect terms."** Fig 9 :30-31: **"your words are borrowed too, so describe the effect rather than the cause"**. So "where does meaning reside" is cast as "what settles a dispute about a codon's effect", not as "which molecule contains a semantic field".
- *Decoding Tacit Knowledge* (opened 2026-11-23): **"When contact isn't possible, the best you can send is a plain description of what you'd expect to see, because anyone can check that against the world."** This is why the outreach file leads with four failure conditions rather than a definition of meaning.

## 3. What licenses a name

Only two things, both outside the shape itself:

1. **The ground settles it.** `THE_FORMULA.md` step 5: *"the ground settles it. Verified only by what pays. A gate is not the ground."* Here the ground is a functional assay, a recoding experiment, or an alignment across homologs. Koonin (2016) says meaning **"can be extracted and quantified from alignments of homologous nucleotide or amino acid sequences but generally not from a single sequence."**
2. **A provenanced import from a frame whose closure differs** (Fig 6 :23, Fig 8 :50-52), with a source gate and a shadow test. Naming the ribosome is such an import from molecular biology. It is licensed as a binding, with provenance, not as a figure derivation.

*They're Scanning Books* (carried from exemplar): *"The real import is not a new term in the existing space. It is a new dimension of the space."* Also: *"A minimum viable set of six terms is a human's careful guess at reality's compression axes; another sensorium's minimum set need not be those six."* Declaring a vocabulary makes a choice visible, not neutral.

## 4. Why this is the source problem, not a definition problem

*The Hard Problem is About Source* (opened 2026-11-23) is the governing essay. Its mechanism: an instrument that reads green **"is speaking only about what it measured, it cannot comment on the source."** Several sources can give one reading: one green light, yellow plus blue, RGB, CMYK, or a scheme not yet discovered. *"If several paths create the same behavior, then behavior is a projection with lossy information."* The case was closed **"not by argument, but by the arrival of a better instrument."**

Apply to DNA:

- The sequence is the reading. The genetic code is degenerate, so several codons give one amino acid (OpenStax 2018, LibreTexts 2021). One reading, many possible sources.
- The same sequence has different effects in different contexts (Wills 2016), so one reading is compatible with several sources.
- What is owed is a discriminator: an assay or a cross-species alignment. A new definition of "information" is not a discriminator.

*Decoding Tacit Knowledge* adds the three-part form of contact: **"Meaning doesn't live in the words. It lives in what the listener brings: the situation, the point and the history behind it."** Pretext is history, context picks the reading, subtext is what the message is for. For a codon: the preceding evolutionary history is pretext, the cell is context, the function is subtext. Remove any one and the reading stays but the meaning fails.

*Recursive Self-Improvement Trap* (opened 2026-11-23): **"the output is only as good as the validator. The agent, or the tool, or the frame, or the theory, cannot validate itself."** A claim that DNA means X because it looks like a program is a frame validating itself against its own metaphor.

## 5. The reader as the held atom, not a missing primitive

Here the figure limit applies precisely. The README's phrase concerns a **missing** primitive. The interpreter (ribosome, tRNA) is already a held, observable part of the biology frame; SEP (2024) quotes the reader-based accounts and LibreTexts names the parts. So:

## 6. Reply to the literal question

**Q: Can the essays name the physical place where biological meaning resides?** No. They give a shape. *Library of Babel*: the answer is reached when *"the finish line will also be the starting place"*, with the paradox that by then *"you have composed the answer through the process of locating it."* Here that means: after the reader and the ground are described, "where is meaning" no longer asks for a place. It asks for a relation that has been checked.

**Q: Does the frame commit the forbidden error?** Only if it answers "in the sequence" or "in the codon table". Fig 10 :18: **"a declared meaning is not a grounded one"**. It does not.

## 7. Correction to my own §3 and §4

I initially wrote "shape, not a name" as if it forbade naming the ribosome or any reader. That overstates it. The canonical phrase is *"what a **missing** thing would have to look like (a shape, not a name)."* It governs primitives not held in Γ. An interpreter is held and observable. Naming it is a binding with provenance, not an invented primitive and not a wall-moving import.

What remains of my §4 metamerism point is **not** retracted: reading a sequence cannot comment on the source, and several sources can give one reading. The reason is the missing discriminator, not a ban on naming held parts. Both readings are recorded so neither is quietly lost. Triangulated (README + Fig 9 :32 + Fig 6 :22).

It can give an action a budget to be tried. But it cannot settle an action. Only the ground can settle it. The Ground Maintainer must be blind to prestige."** A list of famous biologists who agree that DNA "is information" is the named collapse, not corroboration. The legitimate ask is a refutation of the four failure conditions.

**This audit is a simulation, not a ground reading.** Same essay: **"LLMs can quickly simulate an approximate answer/output... However, the truly testable output would need to be derived from the real architecture or working the formula yourself."** So `01` is a simulated derivation, checked for internal consistency against the figures. It is not a test of the claim and cannot settle anything it reports on.
