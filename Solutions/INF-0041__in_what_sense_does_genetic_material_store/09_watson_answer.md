## QUESTION

Option A: meaning is intrinsic to the nucleotide sequence, so the sequence is a self-contained store. Option B: the sequence stores a coding quantity, and biological meaning is a relation among the sequence, a reader outside its closure, and a ground that pays. What each leads to: A makes the sequence its own metaframe (Fig 8 :34) and turns a declared code table into a ground (Fig 10 :18); B returns a shape with three terms, names no physical locus, and owes a discriminator instrument. ## DOMAIN CENSUS

R by arity, symmetry and scale, then one line per domain with actors / currency / anchor, partition or union, and order by dependency.

- **R (arity / symmetry / scale).** Arity three: sequence, reader, ground, with a second cut separating the coding quantity from the semantic quantity. Symmetry: the coding quantity is symmetric (Shannon mutual information runs both ways, SEP 2024 §1.1); the semantic quantity is ordered and asymmetric, since Crick's dogma says *"once 'information' has passed into protein it cannot get out again"* (SEP quoting Crick 1958: 153). Scale: molecular sequence, cellular reader, lineage-level selected effect. The transform between the scales is lossy (Fig 4 :27-32).
- **Domain D1, the sequence as code.** Actors: four bases, codons, the arrangement. Currency: description length and Shannon bits. Anchor: the sequence as a measurable string, plus assays of variants. Nameable, so it is a domain.
- **Domain D2, the interpreter.** Actors: mRNA, ribosome, tRNA anticodons, aminoacylating enzymes. Currency: the realized codon to amino acid mapping. Anchor: the apparatus, which can be violated by a recoded codon or a suppressor tRNA. Nameable.
- **Domain D3, the ground of meaning.** Actors: phenotypes, habitats, lineages, homologous alignments. Currency: biological function and evolutionary conservation. Anchor: the effect that pays, and cross-species alignments. Nameable.
- **Domain D4, the verification frame.** Actors: researchers and the code-as-metaphor literature. Currency: agreement and residual. Anchor: a functional assay or a source-discriminating instrument. Nameable in principle; none taken here. This simulated seat shares the primary evidence pool, so independence is zero.
- **Union:** the codon mapping is the overlap of D1, D2 and D3, a specific compound with its own loop (Fig 9 :57). The storage claim and the meaning claim are not two answers to one question.
- **Order by dependency (Fig 3 :20):** fix the coding quantity first, then the reader, then the ground that fixes which reading survives. A check below the break is a reading of nothing (Fig 3 :22-23).

## VERDICT (from the figures)

The storage sense survives as a coding fact: information is the priced arrangement of four bases, not an intrinsic semantic content. Biological meaning is a different, asymmetric quantity. The sequence cannot state its own reading rule (Fig 8 :34), so the rule is imported from a reader outside its closure (Fig 6 :23); which reading survives is settled by a ground that pays (Fig 2 :29, Fig 11 :39). A declared codon table is a convention, not that ground (Fig 10 :18). Because the code is degenerate, several readings give one product and the reading alone cannot identify the source, so a discriminator is owed. The figures return a three-term shape (arrangement, reader, ground) and no physical locus; the locus is recorded as `gap`, not silence and not a name. This is `specified`, ungrounded, candidate.

- **cost if wrong:** bounded and locatable. The packet states mode, keeps the two currencies apart and writes seven refuters before the conclusion, so an error is findable. The real cost is spending an outside reader's independence by sending a recording, the full architecture, before asking for a verdict. Fig 2 :42 says "Buy independence in proportion to the cost of being wrong." That cost is avoidable by leading with the shape and its refuters.
- **undoable:** yes. Every locus binding is removable without touching the derivation; `07_the_tether_answer.md` and `08_expert_verification_packet.md` already remove them. A sent claim can be superseded by the corrected packet and no primitive has to be withdrawn.
- **reversed by:** a ground reading, not agreement. An isolated sequence producing a determinate effect with no interpreter; a derivation of the codon map from chemistry alone; a single-sequence entropy score that predicts meaning across contexts; a sequence whose meaning is invariant across readers and habitats; or a reading that uniquely determines the effect without an assay. Any of these breaks the shape. A re-census that names a molecular locus from a figure line would also reverse the `gap`. Until one fires, the verdict stands as `candidate`.
