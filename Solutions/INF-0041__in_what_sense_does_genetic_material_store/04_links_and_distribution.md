## What bears on the question

1. **The coding sense is settled and priced.** Barbieri 2016 and Fig 12 :5/:94 agree that the information is the linear order, not the ingredients. This is not where the dispute lives.
2. **The semantic sense is a relation, not a location.** Maynard Smith makes the coder natural selection; Koonin makes meaning vertical across alignments; Ball and Wills make context load-bearing; the SEP records the reader-based accounts. All four say, in different terms, that the sequence alone does not fix the meaning. Fig 8 :34 and Fig 10 :18 give the structural reason.
3. **A discriminator is owed.** The metamerism essay and Koonin's vertical definition both say a better reading, not a better definition, closes the source question. Fig 6 :34 says the instrument is improved from a worse one already returning something. Here that something is a functional assay or a sequence alignment.

## Distribution: offer, do not push

Figure-grounded reasons:

- **Fig 4 :22-25** — *"Only methods cross up. Only head starts come down. A recording carried upward looks like knowledge and is a description of one occasion."* Send the shape claim and its refuter, not the full architecture, before anyone has described a residual.
- **Fig 10 :42** — *"A verifier that can reconstruct the claim cannot verify it."* A recipient who has absorbed the whole framework before being asked for a verdict has joined the frame being checked.
- **Fig 9 :27-28** — *"describe it before you go looking, or any frame you pick will seem to fit."* Ask what they would expect to see if the shape claim were true before giving them the links.
- **Seat Map :163** — *"High correlation is disqualifying for a verdict and efficient for a convention."* A list selected for likely agreement produces a convention, not corroboration.

**Recommended order in a reply:** (1) the two-currency claim and the three-term shape; (2) the four refuters; (3) ask what they would count as a failure; (4) then the three links, with the note that the figures, not the summary, are the authority.

## Two-stage offer

**Stage 1:** the residual, the shape and the pre-registered refuter. Ask what they would count as a failure. Record their verdict *before* showing any theory.

**Stage 2, opt in only:** if they are curious, offer the three links. Their later agreement is never pooled as corroboration (Fig 2 :40).

**Stage 3:** offer the formula as a method to reuse. Each user writes their own refuter and states their own mode, because a frame *"cannot check the direction of its own question"* (`THE_FORMULA.md`, "Direction").

**Hard precondition:** stages 1 to 3 work only if the stage-1 output survives. As written here it does: the coding sense is separated from the semantic sense, the locus is a declared `gap`, and four refuters precede the links. If a reader fires one of them, withdraw the shape and record the correction in `11_audit.md`.
