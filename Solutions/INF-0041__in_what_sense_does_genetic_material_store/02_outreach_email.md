**Subject:** Check one output about genetic information before you decide whether the framework is worth reading

> Hi [name],
>
> I have one specific output I'd like you to check against what you already know. I am not asking you to read the framework first, and I am not asking you to agree with it. If the output does not survive, there is nothing else to look at.
>
> **The output (`specified`, not `grounded`):** the word "information" is doing two different jobs in molecular biology, and they have different anchors.
>
> 1. A sequence over four bases stores a coding quantity. It is the ordered arrangement that carries it, not the ingredients. The price is description length. Fig 12 :5 says *"A set of ingredients is not one. The bond is the rest of the information, and it is the half nothing was recording."* Fig 12 :94 says *"The arrangement is the substance."* This part is measurable now.
> 2. Biological meaning is a different quantity. It is asymmetric, normative and about something. A bare correlation does not pick which of several effects is the meaning: acorn genomes correlate better with rotting than with growing. The sequence cannot state its own semantic rule, because a language cannot define its own truth predicate (Fig 8 :34, *"A frame cannot reach its own metaframe"*). The rule lives in a reader outside the sequence, and which reading survives is settled by an effect that selection kept.
>
> So the shape I get is a three-term relation: coded arrangement, reader, ground. The framework does not name a physical locus for meaning. Fig 9 :32: *"you get a kind of thing, never the answer itself."* I record that as a `gap` with an instrument owed, not as a silence and not as a name.
>
> **What I am not claiming:** that meaning is in the sequence, that the codon table is the ground, that Shannon entropy of one sequence gives biological meaning, or that the framework identifies the ribosome or the fold as the place where meaning lives. Those are bindings from the biology frame, with their own provenance. A declared meaning is not a grounded one (Fig 10 :18).
>
> **What would refute the output:**
> 1. a sequence isolated from every interpreter and every selection history that reliably produces a determinate biological effect;
> 2. a derivation of the full codon map from chemistry alone, with no reader and no import;
> 3. a single-sequence Shannon entropy score that predicts meaning across contexts with no assay;
> 4. a sequence whose meaning is identical across every reader and every habitat.
>
> I wrote these down before sending this. If any one holds, the shape is wrong and I will retract it. A one-line "this fails because..." is more useful than agreement. If you'd rather not engage, no reply is needed.
>
> **If it survives, and only then:** the sources are below. The figures are the authority, not my summary of them.
> - Figures and formula, public repo, branch `general-agent`: https://github.com/IsaiahN/tether/tree/general-agent/figures

Write down what would refute your result *before* you run it, and label the mode of what you get. A reported failure is more useful to me than a reported success.
