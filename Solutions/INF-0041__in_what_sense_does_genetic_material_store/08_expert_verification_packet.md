# Expert verification packet: the Tether on genetic information and biological meaning

**What this is:** a claim in a form a philosopher of biology or molecular biologist can check. Mode, derivation and refuters are stated before the conclusion, so a reader knows what to look for and what would settle it.

**Mode: `specified` on the per-domain claims, `general` on the structural shape, `gap` on the literal locus. Not `grounded`.** No assay is claimed here. This is a shape and a route, not a result, and it is a simulated derivation checked for internal consistency against the figures.

## 1. The claim, in one paragraph

The word "information" names two quantities with different anchors. A nucleotide sequence stores a coding quantity: the information is the ordered arrangement over four bases, priced as description length. Biological meaning is a separate, asymmetric quantity. The sequence cannot carry its own semantic rule, so the rule lives in a reader outside its closure, and which reading survives is settled by an effect that selection kept. A written codon table is a convention, not a ground. Because the code is degenerate, several sequences give one reading and the reading alone cannot identify its source. The Tether returns a shape: a relation among arrangement, reader and ground that pays. It names no physical locus for meaning and records that as a `gap` with an instrument owed.

## 2. Three checkable sub-claims

### C1 — framework-internal plus coding theory; check by reading Fig 12 and Fig 5

- **Statement:** the storage sense is a coding quantity carried by arrangement, not by ingredients, and it does not add to the semantic quantity.
- **Source, verbatim:** Fig 12 :5 **"A set of ingredients is not one. The bond is the rest of the information, and it is the half nothing was recording."** Fig 12 :94 **"The arrangement is the substance."** Fig 12 :125 **"A composition costs in two currencies, and they do not add."**
- **World anchor:** Barbieri 2016, *"the information of genes and proteins is the specific linear order of their sequences"*; Koonin 2016, *"Biological information encoded in genomes is fundamentally different from and effectively orthogonal to Shannon entropy."* These two come from different fields and do not share an author.
- **What to check:** is the coding-quantity claim faithful to Shannon and to MDL? Is the two-currency reading compatible with how your field uses "information"?
- **Refuter:** a single-sequence Shannon entropy score that predicts biological meaning across contexts with no alignment and no assay; or a sequence whose every arrangement is biologically identical.

### C2 — the metaframe and the reader; check by reading Fig 8, Fig 6 and Fig 10

- **Statement:** the sequence cannot state its own reading rule. The rule is imported from a frame whose closure differs, and a declared code table is not a ground.
- **Source, verbatim:** Fig 8 :34 **"A frame cannot reach its own metaframe. Godel's second theorem: a consistent system cannot prove its own consistency. Tarski: truth for a language is not definable within that language."** Fig 6 :23 **"No frame escapes its own closure. The bound moves only on contact with a frame whose closure differs."** Fig 10 :18 **"a declared meaning is not a grounded one"**
- **World anchor:** SEP 2024 records Godfrey-Smith 2014, *"the genome involves a reader (the transcriptional and translational machinery within the cell), but no clear sender"*, and Jablonka 2002, *"information is conferred by the interpreter"*. LibreTexts names the ribosome as machine and tRNA as decoding device. OpenStax reports Crick and Brenner's one/two/three base insertion experiment, which is the checkable ground for the reading frame.
- **What to check:** is the reader-based account a fair reading of the biology? Does the Crick-Brenner result settle the mapping in the way claimed, or only the triplet nature of the code? (If only the latter, narrow C2 accordingly.)
- **Refuter:** derive the full codon map from chemical properties of bases and amino acids with no interpreter; or show that a codon table with no apparatus and no functional test settles a meaning dispute.

### C3 — meaning as a relation; check by reading Fig 11, Fig 2 and the source-discrimination case

- **Statement:** biological meaning resides in the relation among arrangement, reader and a ground that pays, not in any one term. The reading does not determine the source; a discriminator is owed.
- **Source, verbatim:** Fig 11 :39 **"So the answerable question is not what intelligence is, but what makes a system capable. And capability is a property of agent-and-habitat, never of the agent alone."** Fig 2 :29 **"An anchor is whatever settles the question and does not change its answer because of what any frame thinks."** Fig 6 :34 **"Nobody specified a telescope from an account of Mars. A wandering point in the sky was already a reading, and every sharper instrument was built on the residual the last one produced."**
- **World anchor:** Maynard Smith 2000, *"In biology, the coder is natural selection"*; Koonin 2016, meaning *"can be extracted and quantified from alignments of homologous nucleotide or amino acid sequences but generally not from a single sequence"*; Wills 2016, *"single molecules can have vastly different biological effects... depending on the molecular biological context"*; Ball 2016, the genome is *"not a 'blueprint' or 'instruction manual'"* but a *"shorthand crib"*; Hard Problem is About Source, an instrument that reads green *"cannot comment on the source."*
- **What to check:** does the three-term relation fit the cases you know? Is a functional assay or an alignment a genuine source discriminator, or only another reading?
- **Refuter:** a sequence whose meaning is invariant across all readers and habitats with no selection history; or a reading that uniquely determines the effect across all contexts without an assay; or two codons for one amino acid that prove to be distinct biological terms at the audited level.

## 3. What is explicitly not claimed

- Not that DNA is a program, a blueprint or an instruction manual.
- Not that Shannon entropy of one sequence equals biological meaning.
- Not that the codon table is the ground, or that the figures name a molecule as the seat of meaning.
- Not that "meaning" is eliminable as a term. A refusal is a negation (Operators :58), not a deletion.
- Not `grounded`. No laboratory measurement was taken, and the second seat shares the primary evidence pool.
- Not universal over all senses of "information" in biology. The SEP records correlational, teleological, hybrid and eliminativist accounts; this packet addresses the genetic-coding sense only.

## 5. What would settle it

1. **Intrinsic meaning test:** isolate a sequence from every interpreter and every selection history. Determinate effect with no reader breaks the relation claim.
2. **Metaframe test:** derive the codon map from chemistry alone with no interpreter.
3. **Recoding test:** hold the sequence fixed, change the reader, see whether the biological effect changes.
4. **Alignment test:** does meaning track cross-species conservation rather than single-sequence entropy? Koonin 2016 says yes; that is a ground reading the figures cannot do.

## 6. What an expert can check in twenty minutes

1. Read Fig 12 :5, :94, :125 and Fig 8 :34, Fig 10 :18, Fig 11 :39. Six short passages.
2. Check C1 against Shannon 1948 and Koonin 2016: are the two quantities really orthogonal in the sense used here?
3. Check C2 against the Crick-Brenner frameshift account in OpenStax 2018. Does it ground the mapping, or only the triplet reading frame? Narrow the claim if needed.
4. Name one instrument that would distinguish a sequence's reading from its source in your system, or say why none is possible.
5. Send the refutation. A failure is more useful than an endorsement.
