## 1. A shape is a constraint, not a filler

The figures README states the limit: the framework returns *"what a missing thing would have to look like (a shape, not a name)"*. Fig 9 :32: *"you get a kind of thing, never the answer itself."* Fig 13 :133 says a system with a closure *"returns a shape rather than a name"* and :135 says *"It cannot say which primitive is missing, because naming it would mean already holding it."*

From *The Library of Babel*: *"You can cut your search short if the set of books returned is small and descriptive enough that it provides a general shape of the answer that satisfies your question."*

So a **shape** is the narrowed form of an answer you can state before you can name it. It constrains; it does not fill. For this question, *"the four conditions that must jointly hold for targeted intervention to win"* is a shape. *"Target the top five percent by degree"* is a name, and the framework does not supply it.

## 2. How a shape is obtained

- *Library of Babel*: use the vocabulary already closest to the thing, look for recurring new terms that overlap or correlate with it, recurse. *"With each new term gained, you narrow the space and deepen your vocabulary."*
- Fig 6 :30: *"The description travels. Fails by being sharpened into terms only we can read. Cast it in effect terms."* Fig 9 :30-31: *"your words are borrowed too, so describe the effect"* rather than the cause.
- *Decoding Tacit Knowledge*: *"When contact isn't possible, the best you can send is a plain description of what you'd expect to see, because anyone can check that against the world."*
- *Library of Babel* on stopping: the answer is reached when *"the finish line will also be the starting place"*, and the paradox is that by then you have *"composed the answer through the process of locating it."*
- *How a Society of Agents Would Work*: *"A group is only worth having while its members stay different."* and *"The group’s agreement is never the verdict."* Those are the shape of a checking arrangement, not a list of people.

The method word here is **proximity**, not authority. We describe the residual in effect terms, then look for frames whose closure predicts its shape (Fig 8 :53, *"Residual first, frame second"*). This is why the audit excludes X1 (influence as an intrinsic node property) before applying any figure line: if you pick a frame first, any frame will seem to fit (Fig 9 :28).

## 3. What licenses a name

Only two things, both outside the shape itself:

1. **The ground settles it.** Formula step 5: *"the ground settles it. Verified only by what pays."* The README: the framework *"stops at the limit of what the available instruments can distinguish, and records the lineage so the stop is honest."*
2. **A provenanced import from a frame whose closure differs** (Fig 6 :23, Fig 8 :50-52), with a source gate and a shadow test. Fig 8 :51: *"Convergent derivation and adopted import look identical in the contents. Only the record of where each came from separates them."*

*They're Scanning Books When They Should Be Studying Nature* sharpens this: *"The real import is not a new term in the existing space. It is a new dimension of the space."* And: *"A minimum viable set of six terms is a human's careful guess at reality's compression axes; another sensorium's minimum set need not be those six."* Also: *"Declaring the primitives does not make them neutral. It makes the choice visible."*

The centrality measures are a visible guess about which dimension of the network to compress. Naming one as *the* answer to "who is influential" turns a visible choice into an invisible one. The world source that bears hardest here is Liu, Slotine and Barabási 2011, who find driver nodes *"tend to avoid the high-degree nodes"*: the authority of degree as an influence axis is task-relative.

## 4. Why the record's naming move is the forbidden error

*The Hard Problem is About Source* gives the mechanism. Metamerism: an instrument reads green and *"cannot comment on the source"* - one green light, or yellow plus blue, or RGB, or CMYK, or a scheme not yet discovered. The reading does not determine the source. *"If several paths create the same behavior, then behavior is a projection with lossy information."* And: *"An instrument that can't distinguish these cases demonstrates its own inadequacy."* It was the **spectrometer**, not an argument, that resolved it: *"the answer is unreached until a better instrument exists."*

Applied here: a centrality score reads a projection of the adjacency and names a source ("this node drives the cascade"). Several different mechanisms can produce the same score or the same ranking. What is owed is a source-discriminating instrument, not a name. This is the `gap` recorded in F10 of `01_figure_grounded_audit.md`: a better instrument would tell a hub whose removal stops a contagion from a hub whose removal fragments the graph for a different reason, and from a node that merely correlates with both.

*The Recursive Self-Improvement Trap*: *"the output is only as good as the validator. The agent, or the tool, or the frame, or the theory, cannot validate itself."*

## 5. Q2 - ANSWERED, no further question needed

**What licenses a particular node set in the slot?** Nothing in the framework. The framework returns the conditions a winning target set would have to satisfy. A particular node set is licensed only by the ground settling a measured outcome, or by a provenanced import with a shadow test. Until one of those, the chosen set is the searcher's own contribution and must be recorded as such.

**Does naming a centrality measure commit the error the README forbids?** Yes, if it is presented as the framework's answer. Fig 9 :32 returns a kind of thing, never the answer. Fig 2 :31-40 makes the correlated-agreement point: degree, betweenness, eigenvector and PageRank read the same adjacency, so their agreement is one pool talking. The correct form is the conjunction of conditions plus a statement of which instrument would close each one.

## 5b. Correction to my own §3-§4 (see `06_isomers_and_order.md` Q1)

I overstated the "shape, not a name" rule in §3 and §4. The README's phrase is *"what a **missing** thing would have to look like (a shape, not a name)."* It governs a primitive not held in Γ. Here the network, its nodes and its degrees are already in the world's library; nothing is missing in that sense. What is withheld is the **binding** of a goal and a residual onto an existing structure, and the **choice** of which node set to act on. That is a source-discrimination problem (metamerism), not a ban on naming held atoms.

So the precise claim is narrower than §4 states: naming a node set from a centrality reading is not forbidden because the framework cannot name things; it is unsupported because the instrument cannot discriminate among the sources that yield the same reading. Both claims are now recorded so neither is quietly lost. Triangulated (README + *The Hard Problem is About Source* + Fig 2 :31).

## 6. Two consequences for outreach

**Prestige is not action.** From *The Recursive Self-Improvement Trap*: *"Prestige must never be allowed to validate an action directly... Someone let prestige stand in for proof... Prestige can fund an action. It can give an action a budget to be tried. But it cannot settle an action. Only the ground can settle it. The Ground Maintainer must be blind to prestige."*

A campaign that gathers famous names to agree with the conditional claim is the named collapse, not corroboration. The legitimate ask is the opposite: give the claim a budget to be tried, and let the ground (an outcome measurement, a controlled comparison) settle it.

**My own audit is a simulation, not a ground reading.** Same essay: *"LLMs can quickly simulate an approximate answer/output with the formula found in the architecture post. It will often be better than the generic outputs that you will usually get otherwise too. However, the truly testable output would need to be derived from the real architecture or working the formula yourself."* The architecture post adds that LLM outputs *"must be seen as simulacra"* and that results *"could never be verified if the solutions presented were not contrived to fit the formula."* So `01` is a simulated derivation, checked for internal consistency against the figures. It is **not** a test of the claim and must be labelled as such. It cannot settle anything it reports on.

## 7. One more consequence: the framework disowns self-validation

*How Intelligence Actually Works*: *"Because it is a metatheory, it cannot be validated, nor can it validate itself."* The architecture post repeats the limit under "How to Demo": a plausible simulated output *"to an issue in any given domain that the LLM has knowledge of"* is exactly what this folder is, and is not a ground reading. So the correct public posture is the two-stage offer in `02_outreach_email.md`: send the shape and its refuter, invite a failure, withhold the theory until a verdict is recorded.
