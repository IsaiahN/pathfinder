## 2. Independent re-census from the residual line

### 2.1 Arity, symmetry, scale

- **Arity:** a relation among model Γ, action a and outcome o′, indexed by slot and ordered by time. A two-place reading (action, success) loses the prediction and the per-slot indexing, so it is rejected. The comparison between two policies carries a threshold bond (Fig 12 :87), not a symmetric join.
- **Symmetry:** not symmetric. The outcome is an anchor and does not update on the intervener's opinion (Fig 2 :25). "Influential" is a comparison relative to a goal, so one operand is ranked against a residual rather than merely conjoined with the network.
- **Scale:** the slot can be a node, but the ranking of "superior" exists only at the system scale (Fig 4 :5), and the time index crosses the membrane between a step loop and a selection loop (Fig 4). The residual is intrinsically cross-scale.

This seat agrees with the primary ordering, and adds that the residual line itself only supports the first three links; planning and carrying are candidate answers introduced by the frame, not forced by the residual.

## 4. Round-trip gap R_T

Fig 4 :30: *"So take the round trip and measure the gap. Without it you are navigating by dead reckoning: confident about a position nobody checked."*

- **T_A, going up:** carry the primary's four-condition shape to a one-sentence abstraction. Result: *a small intervention wins when the system's signal is concentrated and the intervention stays in contact with what actually settles the outcome.*
- **T_E, coming back:** put that abstraction into a specific decision. Result: *find the most central node and act on it.*

**R_T = gap(x, (T_E ∘ T_A)(x)).** The gap is what the abstraction cannot hold:

1. The one-sentence form loses the valence flip. "Most central" does not say central **for which goal**, so coming back down it permits acting on a node that should be protected (Fig 11 :49-50).
2. It loses the cascade-depth warning. "Signal is concentrated" reads as first-order adjacency, so coming back down it repeats exactly the enumeration error in Fig 11 :52.
3. It loses the reach condition. The abstraction cannot say what to do when the target is not in the held closure, so it recommends more search where an import is owed (Fig 6 :21, :23).
4. It loses the union. "Stay in contact" collapses four conditions into one lever, which is the error in Fig 9 :56.
5. It loses the cost filter rule, so the one-sentence version can be read as "choose whichever is cheaper" (Fig 9 :60).

One thing survives cleanly: a small intervention has a clean object only where the per-slot residual carries positive mass in a few slots. That is the residue worth sending. R_T was **not** measured numerically, because no ground reading exists; it is recorded qualitatively as the enumerated set of dropped constraints. Treating "the formula is the condensed version of the metalaws" as identity would set R_T to zero and would be the dead-reckoning error Fig 4 :30 names. The honest form is a lossy condensation with a measured gap, which is what `09_watson_answer.md` states.
