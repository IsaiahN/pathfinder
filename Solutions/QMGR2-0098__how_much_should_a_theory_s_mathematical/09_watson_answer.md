## QUESTION

Decide, in effect terms, what the Tether says about weighting a theory's mathematical elegance against its testable predictions. Option A: elegance and predictive success are commensurable and an answer is a coefficient. Option B: they are two currencies with two offices, elegance in the budget and prediction in the verdict, and the common scale is refused. What each leads to: A delivers a number the figures do not license and lets a frame-internal quantity enter the verdict; B delivers a checkable shape with refuters and an instrument that would close it, and leaves the coefficient unreached.

## DOMAIN CENSUS

R by arity, symmetry and scale, then one line per domain giving actors / currency / anchor, partition or union, and order by dependency.

- **R (the residual).** The decision of what to accept when a candidate phi has a description cost |phi| and a residual R against observation o'. Arity: two quantities. Symmetry: asymmetric, because elegance is generated inside the frame and prediction is a reading from outside it; Fig 1 :27 *"They are frame-internal. A frame cannot score itself with a quantity it also produces."* Scale: elegance is within-library and within-level; prediction crosses the frame-ground boundary.
- **Domain 1, mint and budget.** Actors: candidate phi, atoms, bonds, library Gamma. Currency: bits, description length, the MDL bargain. Anchor: the residual the frame fails to explain and the library it can build from. This is a budget gate, not an anchor.
- **Domain 2, ground and settlement.** Actors: the frame and the anchor. Currency: actions, R against o'. Anchor: the ground, which does not update when a frame thinks. Fig 2 :29 defines an anchor as whatever settles the question and does not change its answer because of what any frame thinks.
- **Domain 3, verification and community.** Actors: expert readers, reviewers, funders. Currency: agreement, credibility, prestige. Anchor: must be the ground, never agreement. In this simulated run all seats draw on one evidence pool (the same figures, essays and world pages), so their agreement is one pool talking, not corroboration.
- **Domain 1b, heuristic.** Actors: the curiosity drive and the sought-for shape. Currency: compressible gaps. Anchor: the residual that returns something. A sub-office of Domain 1, aimed before admission.
- **Order by dependency.** Refuse what is wrong before minting (Formula step 2); describe R before naming a frame (Fig 9 :28); price and admit phi before settling it (Fig 3 :20); settle at the ground before letting outside frames check (Formula step 5). Effort spent at the checking stage cannot rescue a theory that never reached settlement.

Strain test applied: Fig 1 :27 appears to ban compression while Fig 5 :45 prices it. Re-census resolves this: Fig 1 governs the metric, Fig 5 governs the gate. Different objects, so no strain after re-census. See `01_figure_grounded_audit.md` section 6.

## VERDICT (from the figures)

The verdict is Option B. Elegance and testable predictions are two currencies, priced in bits and actions, and they do not add. Elegance has a real office as a budget filter and a search heuristic; predictions alone have the office of settling the verdict, because the ground is the only metric and the anchor. A more elegant theory with unchanged predictions has not moved toward the ground. Aesthetic consensus is a convention, not corroboration, and buying independence matters in proportion to the cost of being wrong. No coefficient is available from the figures, and reporting that is a verdict rather than a silence. This is a `general` and `specified`, ungrounded result. The strongest single line is Fig 12 :130.

- **cost if wrong:** bounded and locatable. The packet states its mode, three refuters and its provenance before the conclusion, so an error is findable. The real cost is letting a frame-internal score into a verdict, which Fig 2 :42 prices as a failure to buy independence when it matters. That cost is avoidable by keeping the shape and refuter in front of the links.
- **undoable:** yes. The coefficient was never supplied, so nothing has to be withdrawn to correct the answer. If a discriminating case study arrives, the heuristic and budget offices can be re-censused without touching the verdict office. `07_the_tether_answer.md` and `08_expert_verification_packet.md` already carry the answer in supersedable form.
- **reversed by:** a ground reading, not agreement. A study in which different elegance weightings yield different checkable predictions and the ground pays for one would supply a coefficient; a measured case where elegance alone changed a theory's empirical status would break Fig 1 :23 and Fig 11 :40; an additive bits-plus-actions score in the figures would break Fig 12 :130. Until one of these fires, the verdict stands as `candidate`.
