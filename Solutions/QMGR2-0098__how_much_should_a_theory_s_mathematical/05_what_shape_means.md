# What "shape" means for this question

The primary authority for "shape" remains the figures README and the figure text, both opened this session. Provenance is stated so no reading is mistaken for a fresh ground check.

## 1. A shape is a constraint, not a filler

The figures README states the limit: the framework returns *"what a missing thing would have to look like (a shape, not a name)."* Fig 9 :32 *"you get a kind of thing, never the answer itself"*. Fig 13 :134 *"Unreachable is a verdict here, not a silence,"*

From *The Library of Babel* (opened this session): a search can be cut short when the returned set is *"small and descriptive enough that it provides a general shape of the answer that satisfies your question."* And the search is steered by ranging each return against the question: *"Are we getting warmer, or are we getting colder?"*

For this question a **shape** is therefore not a number (not "elegance should count 30 percent"). It is the narrowed form of an answer you can state before you can name a weight: two quantities, two offices, one refusal of the common scale, one instrument named. It constrains any acceptable answer; it does not fill the slot with a coefficient.

## 2. How the shape was obtained here

- *Library of Babel*: use the vocabulary you already have that is closest to the thing, look for recurring terms that overlap with it, recurse. *"With each new term gained, you narrow the space and deepen your vocabulary."* Here the closest held terms were `R`, `|phi|`, `anchor`, `bits`, `actions`, `gate`, `settlement`, taken from the figures, not from a theory of aesthetics.
- Fig 6 :30: *"The description travels. Fails by being sharpened into terms only we can read. Cast it in effect terms."* Fig 9 :30: *"your words are borrowed too, so describe the effect"* The effect described was: elegance is generated inside the frame; prediction is a reading from outside it.
- The sought-for shape was then bound as an ordinary term before the run (Formula, "Direction"), so it could accrue a residual like anything else and be refused by the same test.

## 3. What licenses a name or a number here

Only two things, both outside the shape itself:

1. **The ground settles it.** Formula step 5: *"the ground settles it. Verified only by what pays. A gate is not the ground."* A number for elegance would need an instrument that returns different values under different weightings and pays for the difference. No such instrument is in the figures.
2. **A provenanced import from a frame whose closure differs** (Fig 6 :23, Fig 8 :51), with a source gate and a shadow test. A historical study of theory choice could be such an import. Until then, a coefficient is the searcher's own contribution and must be recorded as such.

*The Hard Problem is About Source* sharpens this. A reading does not determine its source: *"If several paths create the same behavior, then behavior is a projection with lossy information."* It was resolved *"not by argument, but by the arrival of a better instrument."* In this question, two theories with the same predictions but different elegance give the same reading at the ground. Elegance cannot name which source produced the reading; a discriminating experiment can.

## 4. Why the framework's answer is a refusal and not a compromise

Fig 12 :125 *"A composition costs in two currencies, and they do not add."* and Fig 12 :130 *"Summing them into one figure is the error this names, not the fix for it. A price in bits and a price in actions are two readings, held apart."* A compromise (half elegance, half prediction) would be the named error. So the shape is asymmetric by construction: elegance is priced at the gate, prediction is what the ground pays for.

This is not a ban on elegance. It is a refusal to put it in the verdict office. The distinction is the whole answer.

## 5. The prestige and action rule

From *The Recursive Self-Improvement Trap* (opened this session): *"Prestige can fund an action. It can give an action a budget to be tried. But it cannot settle an action. Only the ground can settle it. The Ground Maintainer must be blind to prestige."*

In physics, elegance often functions as prestige: a beautiful theory attracts attention and funding. That is its budget role, and the framework permits it. What it forbids is letting that attention stand in for the ground. Fig 2 :40 *"agreement about the world is worthless when the agreeing frames share an evidence pool"* A field that awards consensus to a beautiful but untested theory has turned a convention into a verdict (Fig 10 :47 *"a convention that decides an outcome has stopped being one"*).

## 6. This audit is a simulation, not a ground reading

Same essay: LLMs *"can quickly simulate an approximate answer/output with the formula"* but *"the truly testable output would need to be derived from the real architecture or working the formula yourself."* And *"the output is only as good as the validator. The agent, or the tool, or the frame, or the theory, cannot validate itself."*

So `01_figure_grounded_audit.md` is a simulated derivation, checked for internal consistency against the figures. It is not a test of the claim and cannot settle anything it reports on. It reports the shape the Tether would return, not a verdict from physics.

## 7. What would turn the shape into a name

An instrument or a case study that returns different outcomes under different elegance weightings, with provenance and a shadow test. Until one is supplied, the honest report is the shape plus the refuter, and the coefficient stays unreached rather than unreachable. Fig 13 :134 makes unreachability a verdict, not a silence.
