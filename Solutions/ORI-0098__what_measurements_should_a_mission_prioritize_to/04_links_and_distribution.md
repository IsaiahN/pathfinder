## Wider literature that bears on the question

1. **The Ladder as an ordering tool, not a verdict tool.** The NASA page says the Ladder is "a starting point to stimulate community discussion, debate, and further research" and that the feature order is environment-dependent. This corroborates F3 and F8: priority is a function of the described residual and the habitat, not a universal ranking.
2. **Source discrimination.** Assembly theory supplies a physically measurable version of "bond over ingredient": copy number combined with assembly index, measurable by mass spectrometry. It corroborates F4 but is not the source of the verdict.
3. **Agnostic and statistical biosignatures.** Smith and Sinapayen show that a distribution-scale signature can reduce single-planet false positives, and explicitly prefer high specificity over sensitivity. This corroborates F2 and F7, and it also supplies a caution: their method rests on a "hinge proposition" that the method itself cannot verify, which is exactly the kind of unstated preference Fig 5 says must be made an ordinary term.
4. **Absence of evidence.** Brewer and MacKenzie corroborate F7 while adding the condition a figure leaves open: a null carries weight when evidence was highly expected, so the instrument set's expected detection must be stated. That is a sharpening of the refuter, not a conflict with the figures.

No world source conflicts with a figure verdict, so the conflict rule was not triggered. The three essays share an author with the figures and carry high overlap; their agreement is expected and is not counted as independent corroboration.

## Distribution: offer, not push

Figure-grounded reasons:

- **Fig 4 :22-25:** *"Only methods cross up. Only head starts come down. A recording carried upward looks like knowledge and is a description of one occasion."* Sending a finished ranked list before anyone has described their residual sends a recording. Lead with the rule and the refuter; offer links after.
- **Fig 10 :42:** *"A verifier that can reconstruct the claim cannot verify it."* A recipient who has absorbed the whole framework before giving a verdict has joined the frame being checked.
- **Fig 9 :27-28:** *"describe it before you go looking, or any frame you pick will seem to fit"* Ask what the reader would expect to see if the rule were true before giving them the article.
- **Fig 2 :40:** agreement from a shared evidence pool is disqualifying for a verdict. A send selected for people likely to agree manufactures that pool.

**Recommended order in a reply:** (1) the structural claim and the five refuters, (2) ask what the reader would count as a failure and record it, (3) only then the three links, with the note that the figures, not the summary, are the authority.
