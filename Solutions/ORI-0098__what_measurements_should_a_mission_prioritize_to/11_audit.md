## Independent domain census

I redid the census from the residual line before relying on the primary's findings, using the WATSON order: R by arity, symmetry and scale; one line per domain with actors / currency / anchor; partition or union; order by dependency.

- **R (arity / symmetry / scale).** The residual is the gap between a closed instrument set and what would settle the life question. Arity: two overlapping conditions, past and present, so a **union compound** (Fig 9 :54), not a single hard question and not a partition. Symmetry: asymmetric; a positive result and a null have different false-positive structures, so no scalar score is a legitimate output form. Scale: observation is local per sample (Fig 1 :19), inference is a cascade out through the habitat (Fig 11 :52-53).
- **Domain 1, perception and context.** Actors: sensor, sample, water, energy, terrain. Currency: resolvable difference and prior state. Anchor: the sample as it was, via repeat and control. Type: partition.
- **Domain 2, vocabulary.** Actors: spectrum, gradient, network and the states they can bond to. Currency: what the environment affords. Anchor: the six-term substrate, assumed. Type: partition.
- **Domain 3, trace discrimination.** Actors: biological, abiotic and contaminant pathways. Currency: independent residuals. Anchor: a source-discriminating measurement, never interpreter agreement. Type: union compound (metamerism).
- **Domain 4, channel.** Actors: blanks, calibration, preservation, contamination control. Currency: contact held or lost. Anchor: the ground, which does not decay. Type: partition.
- **Domain 5, decision.** Actors: planners, mass, power, time. Currency: bits and actions. Anchor: none of its own; it is a filter over domains 1 to 4. Type: filter, not a witness.

Order by dependency: D1 then D2 then D3 then D4, with D5 as a filter. Unbundle the union before search (Fig 9 :54), and describe the residual before choosing an instrument family (Fig 9 :27-28).

**Comparison with the primary census.** My census matches the primary's five domains and its union reading. Where the primary derived from Fig 1 and Fig 13, I derived from Fig 9's union/partition split and Fig 3's dependency chain. Different starting figures, same domain boundaries: low overlap between routes inside the framework, so the agreement is weakly non-circular within the figure set. It is still one evidence pool.
