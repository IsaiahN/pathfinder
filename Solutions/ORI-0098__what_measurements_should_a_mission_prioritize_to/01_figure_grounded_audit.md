## 0. The question under audit, in effect terms

> What measurements should a mission prioritize to test for past or present life?

Restated as an effect: a mission holds a finite set of instruments. Each candidate measurement consumes mass, power, time and sample. The mission chooses an order. The question is which measurement class must be secured first so that later classes can bear on the ground at all, and which classes are frame-internal and therefore cannot bear on it however many there are.

Two options and what each leads to:

- **Option A (the common default):** rank candidate biosignatures by strength of evidence and fly the top ones. This optimises an upper link of the dependency chain, assumes the lower links (perception, vocabulary, objective) were measured, and treats a set of measurements sharing one abiotic pathway as several witnesses.
- **Option B (the figure-derived shape):** describe the residual first, split the union compound into past-life and present-life inquiries, secure context and vocabulary before signifiers, buy independence between measurements rather than similarity, price arrangement above ingredients, keep the channel open, and report a null as unreached rather than as absence.

Option B is derived below. It remains `specified`.

## 1. The residual: arity, symmetry, scale

- **Arity: at least two, and the two are not the same question.** "Past life" and "present life" differ in what could count as evidence, in what a false positive is, and in what a null result means. A mission that bundles them is a union compound, not one hard question. Fig 9 :54: *"And when the split lands on a union rather than a partition, unbundle before you search again."* Fig 9 :58-59: *"a case in two conditions is not a harder case of either. It stops being a mixture and becomes a specific compound."*
- **Symmetry: asymmetric.** A positive and a negative result do not have the same evidential structure. A positive may be abiotic in origin, and a negative may be a channel failure rather than an absent target. There is no operation in the measurement set that maps one to the other. This is why a single scalar "life likelihood" is the wrong output form.
- **Scale: the observation is local, the inference is a cascade.** A measurement reads one sample, one spot, one spectrum. The claim that it bears on life propagates outward through habitat, preservation, transport and contamination. Fig 11 :52-53: *"contact cascades. Scoping the habitat to direct contact is how DDT reached the eagles: first-order enumeration, second-order effect, and the residual that mattered was three steps out."*

**Anchor test (Fig 2 :29-30):** the anchor is *"whatever settles the question and does not change its answer because of what any frame thinks. A measurement, an experiment, a counter, a market, a patient outcome."* Here the anchor is the ground: whatever actually was or is present at the target. No interpretation, no mission review board and no majority of scientists can move it. If a proposed "measurement" moves when a frame pushes on it, it is not an anchor. This rules out what Fig 1 :27 calls frame-internal quantities: *"They are frame-internal. A frame cannot score itself with a quantity it also produces."* Instrument count, data volume, coverage and number of biosignatures screened are frame-internal.

## 2. Domain census

One line per domain: actors, currency, anchor. No domain is admitted without a nameable anchor. Order by dependency (Fig 3 :20-21).

| domain | actors | currency | anchor | partition or union |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| D1: perception and context | instruments in contact with sample; water, energy, minerals, radiation, season, terrain | resolvable difference; what is in front of the sensor versus what was there before | the sample as it was, read through repeat measurement and controls | partition: what can be perceived at all |
| D2: vocabulary of affordance | gradients, redox pairs, isotopic orderings, molecular networks | "what things afford" (Fig 3 :7), which structures can carry a state | the six structures/states are the assumed substrate (Fig 13), not derived here | partition: which structures exist in the library |
| D3: trace discrimination | biological pathway versus abiotic pathway, each with its own null | independent residuals; false-positive and false-negative structure | a source-discriminating measurement, never agreement between interpreters | union compound: biological and abiotic causes can give the same reading (metamerism) |
| D4: channel maintenance | sample handling, calibration, negative controls, forward-contamination control | contact held or lost; stale, dropped, saturated or pooled readings | the ground, which does not decay | partition: channel open or closed |
| D5: mission decision | planners, mass, power, time, cost of being wrong | two non-additive currencies: bits and actions (Fig 12 :125-126) | the ground, via the measurements in D1 to D4 | filter only, never a witness |

**Order by dependency.** D1 precedes D2 precedes D3 precedes D4, and D5 is a filter over all of them. Effort spent in D5 (optimising instrument selection) before D1 and D2 have been measured arrives at a step that cannot use it. Fig 3 :20-21: *"The chain is ordered by dependency, not by importance. Each link consumes what the one above produces, so effort spent above the break arrives at a step that cannot use it."*

**A note on D5.** Cost and mass are *filters for the budget and witnesses for the verdict* only if misused. Fig 9 :60: *"Use filters for the budget and witnesses for the verdict. Never let a filter hand you a verdict."* A high priority score assigned to a measurement because it is cheap says what gets tried first, not that life is present.

## 4. Findings

### F1 - The residual must be indexed per slot, not averaged (decisive for output form)

Fig 1 :19: *"The residual is indexed by object slot, not aggregated."*
Fig 1 :22: *"Aggregating across slots is how a live signal disappears into an average."*
Fig 1 :23: *"The ground enters as the only metric."*

A mission that returns one global "life detected / not detected" score has thrown away the only signal that says where to look. One unresolved sample, one out-of-equilibrium plume, one anomalous fractionation must remain visible even when every other slot is quiet. The output form is a per-sample residual map with a stated detection limit for each slot.

**Refuter:** show that a single aggregate statistic can preserve an arbitrarily small live slot residual while still driving the next measurement. If it can, F1 fails.

### F2 - Prioritise independence between measurements, not their agreement (triangulated)

Fig 2 :31-32: *"Independence, between two frames, is how little they draw on the same evidence. Two people reading the same report are not two frames; two instruments pointed at the same thing are."*
Fig 2 :35: *"Alignment is triangulation, not negotiation. A mirror cannot correct you."*
Fig 2 :40: *"disqualifying for a verdict: agreement about the world is worthless when the agreeing frames share an evidence pool"*
Fig 2 :42: *"Buy independence in proportion to the cost of being wrong."*

Two biosignature claims that share one abiotic production pathway are one witness wearing two names. Three instruments that all read "organic molecule present" by the same pyrolysis-gas-chromatography route are one frame with three labels. What counts as a second frame is a measurement whose closure differs, for example isotopic ordering, redox disequilibrium and spatial co-occurrence, each with its own abiotic null. Because the cost of a false life claim is high, Fig 2 :42 says to buy more independence, not cheaper agreement.

**Refuter:** demonstrate that two measurements sharing a single abiotic mechanism carry independent information about the biological hypothesis. Under the figure's definition they cannot; if a reader shows they can, F2 is withdrawn.

### F3 - Secure the lower links of the dependency chain first (decisive for ordering)

Fig 3 :5: *"the system can tell what is in front of it, and tell it apart from what was there before"*
Fig 3 :7: *"it has terms for what things afford: what can be moved, opened, joined, blocked"*
Fig 3 :10: *"it can say what would count as progress here, in its own terms"*
Fig 3 :26-27: *"A link with no instrument is not a link that works. It is a link nobody has looked at."*
Fig 3 :29-30: *"Before asking whether a system is good, ask which link it currently stops at, and whether the answer was measured or assumed. Most improvement effort in practice lands above the break, because that is where the measurements already exist."*

A mission that selects a flagship biosignature instrument before it can (a) tell what is in front of the sensor and distinguish it from prior state, (b) name what the environment affords (water, gradient, energy, preservation) and (c) state what would count as progress, is optimising above the break. The most neglected link is exactly (b) and (c): having terms for what a situation offers and saying what would count as progress. These have the fewest instruments. So the first priority is context and objective, not signifier.

This finding agrees with the world literature at low overlap: NASA's Ladder warns that *"easier measurements to make (the bottom rungs) might be harder to interpret than rungs further up the Ladder"* (Talbert 2018). Agreement from a different field corroborates but does not decide.

**Refuter:** show a mission that flies only a high-rung signifier instrument, with no context measurement and no pre-stated disconfirmation criterion, whose result nonetheless bears on the ground. If one exists, the dependency ordering is wrong.

### F4 - Prioritise bonds over ingredients (single-source for the figure definition, corroborated at low overlap)

Fig 12 :5: *"A set of ingredients is not one. The bond is the rest of the information, and it is the half nothing was recording."*
Fig 12 :117: *"isomer. Same atoms, different bond, different substance. The difference is invisible in the formula and decisive in the behaviour."*

Detecting amino acids is an ingredient list. Detecting enantiomeric excess across several amino acid types, isotopic fractionation that departs from equilibrium, a polymer with repeating charge, or a correlated network of species are bonds. The same atoms under a different arrangement are a different substance. A measurement suite that inventories ingredients without pricing arrangement has recorded half of itself.

World corroboration, low overlap: Sharma et al. (Nature 2023) write that *"mass spectrometry can be used to measure assembly for molecules, because it can measure how molecules are built by making bonds"* and that *"the assembly index on its own cannot detect selection, but copy number combined with the assembly index can."* That is a physical realisation of the bond-plus-copy-number reading, arrived at independently. It corroborates; it does not decide.

**Refuter:** exhibit a case where ingredient presence alone discriminates biotic from abiotic origin across all known pathways without any arrangement or context information. Under the isomer definition this is impossible in principle; a single counterexample would break the finding.

### F5 - Keep the channel open: contamination and controls are anchor maintenance, not housekeeping (triangulated)

Fig 10 :24: *"the ground does not decay, the channel does"*
Fig 10 :25-28: *"stale percepts, dropped transients, saturated metrics, pooled readings"* ... *"the output is not a verdict, it is you have lost touch"*
Fig 10 :42: *"A verifier that can reconstruct the claim cannot verify it."*
Fig 11 :61: *"Introduction is available; exclusion is not. You can add an actor and you cannot subtract the habitat, because contact is a fact about the world and does not consult you."*

Forward contamination is a one-way addition: an Earth microbe introduced to a sample cannot be subtracted from the habitat. Negative controls, calibration and sample preservation are therefore not overhead around the science; they are the office that keeps the anchor reachable. A saturated detector or a pooled reading does not give a weak verdict, it reports that contact has been lost. And a team that authors the interpretation cannot verify it, which is why the measurement record must be legible to someone who did not build it.

**Refuter:** show a life detection result whose interpretation is robust to an unacknowledged Earth contaminant, a saturated channel and an author who both produced and certified the reading. If such a result exists, F5 is too strong.

### F6 - Prioritise instruments already returning something that fails to resolve (single-source)

Fig 6 :32: *"An instrument is not built from a description. It is improved from a worse instrument already returning something."*
Fig 6 :34-35: *"The question is not whether a sensor could exist. It is whether anything, at any resolution, is already returning something that fails to resolve."*

The priority is not "the most sensitive conceivable sensor." It is the instrument for which something is already registering but not resolving, because that gives an edge to extend. If nothing has ever registered at any resolution for a proposed signature, there is no proto-instrument to sharpen, and that inquiry belongs in a separate, cheaper search. This is the limit the framework places on sensor optimism.

**Refuter:** name an instrument for a target signature that has never registered at any resolution, and show that flying it is a better use of the same mass than sharpening an instrument already returning a failing signal. If so, F6 is a preference, not a rule.

### F7 - A null result is a report about closure, not a verdict of absence (triangulated)

Fig 8 :39: *"So a search that finds nothing is never a proof of absence."*
Fig 8 :32: *"Closure is idempotent. Composing inside a closed set never leaves it, so no search, however long, adds an atom."*
Fig 13 :134-135: *"Unreachable is a verdict here, not a silence,"* and *"It cannot say which primitive is missing, because naming it would mean already holding it."*

"No life detected" by a closed instrument set says something about that set, not directly about the target. More of the same measurement does not add a new kind of evidence because composition inside a closure never leaves it. The honest null names the kind of thing that would have been needed, in effect terms, and records the unresolved slots.

World corroboration at low overlap: MacKenzie (2011, New Zealand Journal of Ecology) writes that *"Failing to find the required evidence that a species is present at a location does not imply that the species is absent, merely that it has not been found."* Brewer (2025, Philosophy of Science) adds the nuance that *"AoE is not EoA, except in cases wherein evidence for the hypothesis in question is highly likely to occur and be found."* Brewer does not contradict the figure; he states the condition under which a null begins to carry weight, which is precisely a statement about the instrument set's expected detection. That is a sharpening of the refuter, not a reversal.

**Refuter:** produce a null result from a closed instrument set that legitimately entails absence without any statement about the set's expected detection. If such a case exists, the figure's rule is too broad.

### F8 - Describe the residual before selecting the instrument family (decisive against apophenia)

Fig 9 :27-28: *"describe it before you go looking, or any frame you pick will seem to fit"*
Fig 9 :26: *"R's own predicates name the habitat that holds φ*"*
Fig 9 :53: *"Nobody chooses. The gap does, and the gap is only as good as its description."*
Fig 10 :52: *"Bring a characterised residual, never the source."*

Naming the instrument family first and then finding a use for it inverts the order. The residual's own predicates (what failed to resolve, at what scale, under which abiotic null) name the habitat of the right instrument. A mission design doc that opens with the payload and closes with the science question has the order backwards.

World corroboration: the NASA Ladder page warns that the Ladder *"is not intended to endorse specific biosignatures or instruments for life-detection measurements, and is by no means a definitive, final product."* That is an institutional statement of the same refusal to let a named instrument stand in for a described residual.

**Refuter:** show an instrument family selected without a described residual that is nonetheless uniquely appropriate to the life question. If appropriateness can be established without the residual, the order is a preference, not a rule.

### F9 - Price bits and actions separately (single-source, named strain)

Fig 12 :125-126: *"A composition costs in two currencies, and they do not add. Bits price the description. Actions price finding out whether it holds."*
Fig 12 :127: *"They are in different units, so a system holding one accounting cannot compare them"*

A mission pays in information (how many distinct measurement axes, how detailed the description) and in actions (sample acquisition, drilling, imaging time, return). These do not add. A suite with many cheap information channels but no action budget cannot settle a deep sample; a suite with deep sample access but one information channel cannot discriminate source. The named failure is summing them into a single "science value" score.

**Refuter:** exhibit a common unit in which description bits and sample actions are commensurable without loss, such that a single score preserves both orderings. The figure denies this; a demonstration would refute it.

### F10 - Three independent structural axes are available, not a redundancy (triangulated)

Fig 13 :35-37: *"Space, time and energy are not three readings of one quantity. Neither are spectrum, gradient and network. Each answers a question the other two cannot"*
Fig 13 :20: *"ordering, so more and less can be said"*
Fig 13 :22: *"a difference that can be spent"*
Fig 13 :24: *"that entities relate, and influence travels"*

The six-term substrate supplies three structurally distinct measurement axes: **spectrum** (ordering: mass distributions, isotopic ratios, size/frequency spectra), **gradient** (spendable difference: redox disequilibrium, pH, thermal or chemical potential), and **network** (relation and influence: co-occurrence, correlation, spatial/temporal structure). These are not three readings of one quantity. A suite that flies three variants of a spectrometer has one axis; a suite that orders, spends and relates has three. This is the structural content behind the independence demand in F2.

**Refuter:** show that spectrum, gradient and network reduce to a single underlying measurement under a declared code, so that their agreement carries no information. Fig 13 :38-39 forbids this for the substrate; a demonstration would break the finding.

## 5. Pre-registered refuter

Required before any outreach, per the seat map's rule that *"a falsifier written before the run is a design instrument, not only an honesty device."* Written 2026-11-23, before the conclusion in `07_the_tether_answer.md` and before the WATSON run in `09_watson_answer.md`.

The structural priority ordering is refuted if any of the following holds:

1. A single measurement, with no independent second frame and no context, settles the life question against all abiotic alternatives (breaks F2 and Fig 2 :31-32).
2. A mission can prioritise a high-rung signifier before establishing context and a stated disconfirmation criterion and still have its result bear on the ground (breaks F3 and Fig 3 :20-21).
3. Ingredient presence, with no arrangement or context information, discriminates biotic from abiotic origin across known pathways (breaks F4 and Fig 12 :117).
4. A non-detection by a closed instrument set is a legitimate verdict of absence with no statement about expected detection (breaks F7 and Fig 8 :39).
5. Two measurements drawn from the same physical process count as two independent witnesses (breaks F2 and Fig 2 :40).
6. Description bits and sample actions are commensurable in one lossless score (breaks F9 and Fig 12 :125-126).

If any fires, the ordering is withdrawn or rewritten. Until one is run, no part of this may be labelled `grounded`.

## 6. Recommendation

1. **Do not open with a payload list.** Describe the residual in effect terms: which trace, at what scale, under which abiotic null, at which slot (F8).
2. **Split the union.** Fly or propose separate inquiry designs for past life and present life; they have different false-positive structures (Fig 9 :54, :58-59).
3. **Buy the lower chain first.** Context (perception), vocabulary (what the environment affords), and objective (what would count as progress) before signifier instruments (F3).
4. **Buy independence in proportion to the cost of being wrong.** Three structurally different axes (ordering, spendable difference, relation), each with its own abiotic null (F2, F10).
5. **Price bonds over atoms.** Prefer patterns that could not be read off an ingredient list (F4).
6. **Fund the channel.** Contamination control, negative controls, calibration and sample preservation are first-order science (F5).
7. **Sharpen what already fails to resolve**; treat never-registered signatures as a separate proto-instrument search (F6).
8. **Report nulls as unreached with a named gap** (F7).
9. **Keep two budgets** (F9).
10. **Send the generator, not the checklist.** Fig 4 :25: *"A recording carried upward looks like knowledge and is a description of one occasion."* The artifact to circulate is the derivation and the refuter, not a final ranked list.

## 7. Canonical usage check

Two local canonical sources were read after the first four sections and govern how the figures may be used:

- `FRAMEWORK/reference/FIGURES_README_canonical.md`: the figures *"outrank everything else in the repo: the code, the rulings and the docs answer to them... the quotes are the authority, not this summary."* It also states that a reading which makes one figure contradict another has misidentified its domain, and that the framework returns *"what a missing thing would have to look like (a shape, not a name)"* then stops at the limit of the instruments and records the lineage. *"Indiscernibility is not identity."*
- `FRAMEWORK/figures/THE_FORMULA.md` step 7: when nothing in the closure closes R, ask SEARCHED first, then INWARD, then OUTWARD, and keep the three distinct. Underivable-in-principle and unreachable-at-this-budget are different facts with different instructions.

Applied here: no figure was made to contradict another. The one apparent tension, that F2 demands many measurements while Fig 8 :32 says more search inside a closure adds no atom, is dissolved by re-census: F2 concerns **different closures** (independent frames), Fig 8 :32 concerns **more of the same closure** (repeated measurements of one kind). Different quantities, so no strain after re-census. This is recorded in the log below, not hidden.

The SEARCHED / INWARD / OUTWARD discipline maps onto F6 and F7 as follows. SEARCHED: has the instrument set already visited its own closure, and is the null a budget issue (more of the same) rather than a missing kind? INWARD: sharpen an instrument already returning a failing signal. OUTWARD: import a frame with a different closure, for example a measurement principle from another field, with a source gate and a shadow test. These three were kept distinct in F6 and F7.

### Round trip gap R_T (qualitative)

The Symbols table defines R_T as `gap(x, (T_E ∘ T_A)(x))`. Fig 4 :30 requires it to be measured rather than assumed. Running it on this folder:

- **Sent up (x):** the verbatim figure set, chiefly Fig 1 :19-23, Fig 2 :31-42, Fig 3 :20-30, Fig 8 :32-39, Fig 12 :5/:117/:125-126, Fig 13 :35-37.

R_T was not measured numerically because no ground reading exists; it is recorded as the enumerated set of dropped constraints.

### Updated verdict

The shape survives intact; the specific names do not. The answer to ORI-0098 is a dependency-ordered priority rule over structural roles, not a list of instruments. Its strongest single line is Fig 2 :31-32: independence is a property of evidence sources, not of interpreters. Its most neglected requirement is Fig 3 :26-27: the link with no instrument is the one to look at first. Status: `specified`, ungrounded, candidate.
