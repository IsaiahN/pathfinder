## 1. A shape is a constraint, not a filler

The figures README states the limit: the framework returns *"what a missing thing would have to look like (a shape, not a name)."* Fig 9 :32: *"you get a kind of thing, never the answer itself."* Fig 13 :134-135 reports that the system can say the answer is out of reach and can narrow what is missing, but *"It cannot say which primitive is missing, because naming it would mean already holding it."*

From *The Library of Babel*: *"You can cut your search short if the set of books returned is small and descriptive enough that it provides a general shape of the answer that satisfies your question."*

So a **shape** is the narrowed form of an answer you can state before you can name it. It constrains; it does not fill. For ORI-0098:

- *"Prioritise context before signifiers"* is a shape.
- *"Fly the MOA / life detector / mass spectrometer as the answer"* is a name.
- *"Buy independence between measurements"* is a shape.
- *"Enantiomeric excess is the one true biosignature"* is a name.

The distinction is not stylistic. A name claims the ground has settled which slot occupant is correct. A shape states what any occupant must satisfy.

## 2. How a shape is obtained

*The Library of Babel* on vocabulary and proximity: *"you will have to use the existing vocabulary you do know that is closest to the words that you wish to describe and grasp the shape of."* And: *"With each new term gained, you are able to narrow the space by definition and deepen your vocabulary."*

Applied here, the closest vocabulary we hold is the six-term substrate: states (space, time, energy) and structures (spectrum, gradient, network). That vocabulary gives three measurement axes (ordering, spendable difference, relation) without naming any instrument. The narrative is the method: start from what the residual already registers, narrow by proximity, stop at a shape.

Fig 6 :30: *"The description travels. Fails by being sharpened into terms only we can read. Cast it in effect terms."* Fig 9 :30-31: *"your words are borrowed too, so describe the effect rather than the cause."* So the shape must be stated in effects: what a measurement would have to register, what a null would mean, what independence would require. Not in the jargon of a single instrument family.

## 3. A bundle is not one question

*The Library of Babel* names the exact failure this question invites: *"Perhaps your problem was a bundle or conflation of several problems masquerading as one? You will need to search all of those problems individually to find their answers and then combine them in a satisfactory way."*

"Past or present life" is such a bundle. Fig 9 :54 says *"unbundle before you search again"* when the split lands on a union rather than a partition. The shape therefore includes a required split: past-life evidence and present-life evidence are two searches with different false-positive structures, different null meanings, and different instrument implications. A single payload described as "life detection" obscures the union.

## 4. Why "source" is the operative word for life detection

*The Hard Problem is About Source* gives the mechanism that governs this question more directly than any other essay. The instrument reads green but *"the instrument is speaking only about what it measured, it cannot comment on the source."* Several underlying causes can produce one reading: *"If several paths create the same behavior, then behavior is a projection with lossy information."* The resolution was not an argument: *"It was proven wrong not by argument, but by the arrival of a better instrument."* And: *"An instrument that can't distinguish these cases demonstrates its own inadequacy."*

For ORI-0098 this is the central caution. A spectrum showing an amino acid, a chromatogram showing a lipid, or an image showing a texture is a projection. Abiotic synthesis, contamination and biology can all produce it. Naming life as the source from the reading is the metamerism error. What is owed is a source-discriminating arrangement of measurements, not a more emphatic interpretation of one reading. This is why F4 (bonds over ingredients) and F2 (independence) are structural requirements and not stylistic preferences.

## 5. What licenses a name

Only two things, both outside the shape itself:

1. **The ground settles it.** `THE_FORMULA.md` step 5: *"the ground settles it. Verified only by what pays. A gate is not the ground."* A returned sample, a reproduced culture, a discriminating experiment, a calibration that separates the sources. Until then the name is a candidate.
2. **A provenanced import from a frame whose closure differs** (Fig 6 :23, Fig 8 :50-52), with a source gate and a shadow test. Importing assembly theory from the physics-of-life literature into mission design is legitimate only if the record shows where it came from and what gap it explains. It adds no atom to the world; it gives our frame access to an atom another frame holds.

*The Library of Babel* adds a stopping rule: *"you will know that you are done because the finish line will also be the starting place, as there is nowhere else to search and nothing else that can describe the answer better."* A mission design has not reached that point while its null results remain uninterpretable.

## 6. Agreement is not the ground

From *The Recursive Self-Improvement Trap*: *"You may all agree and be wrong. You may set your clock to someone else's, who was setting your clock to yours, and be off by many deviations over time. You need ground truth to define where you stand."* And: *"the output is only as good as the validator. The agent, or the tool, or the frame, or the theory, cannot validate itself."*

The same essay states the prestige rule: *"Prestige can fund an action. It can give an action a budget to be tried. But it cannot settle an action. Only the ground can settle it. The Ground Maintainer must be blind to prestige."* For this question: a payload endorsed by a distinguished review panel has been given a budget to be tried. It has not been settled. Prestige must not enter the ordering of measurement classes.

## 7. My own audit is a simulation, not a ground reading

From *The Recursive Self-Improvement Trap*: *"LLMs can quickly simulate an approximate answer/output with the formula found in the architecture post... However, the truly testable output would need to be derived from the real architecture or working the formula yourself."*

So `01_figure_grounded_audit.md`, `06_isomers_and_order.md`, `07_the_tether_answer.md` and this file are simulated derivations checked for internal consistency against the figures. They are not measurements, not mission analyses, and not tests of the claim. They cannot settle anything they report on. A real test requires an instrument, a dataset, a blinded comparison of competing hypotheses, or a sample return.

## 8. Consequences for ORI-0098

1. The deliverable is a priority shape over roles, not a name of instruments or molecules.
2. The union must be split before the search: past life and present life are two inquiries.
3. The central error to avoid is metamerism: a reading named as a source.
4. Independence is a property of evidence sources, not of interpreters, so agreement among readers of one spectrum is no corroboration.
5. A null is a narrowed shape plus a record of what was unreachable, not a silence and not an absence verdict.
6. Everything here is `specified` until a ground pays for it.
