## QUESTION

Decide, in effect terms, what ordering a life detection mission should apply to candidate measurements. Option A: rank candidate biosignatures by strength and fly the top ones. Option B: order measurement roles by dependency, with the residual described first, the union split, independence bought between measurements, bonds priced above ingredients, the channel maintained, and nulls reported as unreached. What each leads to: A optimises an upper link of a chain whose lower links were assumed, and treats correlated readings as multiple witnesses; B sends a checkable dependency rule with written refuters and an open slot until a ground settles which instrument fills it.

## DOMAIN CENSUS

R by arity, symmetry and scale, then one line per domain with actors / currency / anchor, partition or union, and order by dependency.

- **R (the residual).** The gap between what a closed instrument set can represent and what would settle the past-or-present-life question. Arity: two overlapping conditions, so a **union compound**, not one hard question (Fig 9 :54, :58-59). Symmetry: asymmetric; a positive and a null have different false-positive and false-negative structures, so no scalar life score is adequate. Scale: observation is per sample and per slot, inference is a habitat cascade several steps out (Fig 11 :52-53).
- **Domain 1, perception and context.** Actors: instruments in contact with the sample; water, energy, minerals, radiation, season, terrain. Currency: resolvable difference, what is in front of the sensor versus what was there before. Anchor: the sample as it was, read through repeat and control. Question type: partition, what can be perceived at all.
- **Domain 2, vocabulary of affordance.** Actors: gradients, redox pairs, isotopic orderings, molecular networks. Currency: what the environment affords. Anchor: the six-term substrate, assumed, not derived here. Question type: partition, which structures are in the library.
- **Domain 3, trace discrimination.** Actors: biological pathway versus abiotic pathway versus contamination, each with a null. Currency: independent residuals; false-positive and false-negative structure. Anchor: a source-discriminating measurement, never agreement among interpreters. Question type: union compound, several causes can give one reading (metamerism).
- **Domain 4, channel maintenance.** Actors: sample handling, calibration, blanks, preservation, forward-contamination control. Currency: contact held or lost. Anchor: the ground, which does not decay. Question type: partition, channel open or closed.
- **Domain 5, mission decision.** Actors: planners, mass, power, time. Currency: bits and actions, two non-additive prices. Anchor: the ground via domains 1 to 4. Question type: filter only, never a witness.
- **Order by dependency.** D1 perception, D2 vocabulary, D3 objective, D4 channel, then D5 planning. Fig 3 :20-21: effort above the break arrives at a step that cannot use it. Unbundle the union before searching (Fig 9 :54). Describe the residual before naming a frame (Fig 9 :27-28).

Strain test applied: an apparent conflict between buying independence (Fig 2 :42) and the claim that more search adds no atom (Fig 8 :32) is dissolved by re-census. Fig 2 governs between closures; Fig 8 :32 governs within one closure. Different quantities, no strain.

## VERDICT (from the figures)

The ordering survives; the names do not. A mission should describe the residual and split the union, secure perception and vocabulary and a stated disconfirmation criterion before signifier instruments, buy independence between measurement principles rather than agreement among interpreters, use ordering / difference / relation as three structurally independent axes, price bonds above ingredients, maintain the channel as anchor maintenance, sharpen instruments already returning a failing signal, report nulls as unreached with a named gap, keep bits and actions as two prices, and report per slot. It should not name the correct molecule, instrument or target. That step needs a ground reading or a provenanced import. This is a `specified`, ungrounded result.

- **cost if wrong:** bounded and locatable. The artifact carries its mode, three checkable sub-claims, six written refuters and a provenance record, so an error is findable rather than invisible. The real cost is a mission that buys agreement inside one evidence pool, which Fig 2 :42 prices ("Buy independence in proportion to the cost of being wrong."). That cost is avoidable by buying independent closures before adding instruments of the same kind.
- **undoable:** yes. The ordering is separable from any payload selection and can be revised without withdrawing the figure derivation. A null reported as unreached can be re-opened when a new instrument returns something at a resolution where nothing did before. No primitive has to be withdrawn.
- **reversed by:** a ground reading, not agreement. A source-discriminating comparison showing that one ingredient-only measurement separates biology from all abiotic alternatives would break the bond-over-ingredient rule; a demonstration that two of the three axes reduce to one physical process would break the independence rule; a mission that states context and disconfirmation only after payload selection yet still bears on the ground would break the dependency rule; a null from a closed set that legitimately entails absence with a stated expected-detection condition would narrow the null rule. Until one of these fires, the verdict stands as `candidate`.
