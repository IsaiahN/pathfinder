# Solution ORI-0098

**Question:** What measurements should a mission prioritize to test for past or present life?

**Source:** origins of life.txt line 133

**Section:** 10. Life beyond Earth and broader implications

**Mode:** `specified` on the structural priority ordering, `general` on the shape of the answer. No measurement settles the ranking itself, so nothing here is `grounded`.

**Status:** done

**Registry status:** pending

## Residual, stated as an effect

A mission has finite mass, power, time and sample access. It must choose a set of measurements such that the result, positive or negative, changes what can be said about the proposition "life was or is present here." The residual is not "which biosignature is best." It is: given a closed instrument set, what ordering of measurements reduces the gap between what the mission can represent and what would settle the life question at the ground. A second clause matters as much as the first: the mission cannot place life itself inside its library, so it can only build instruments that contact the ground through traces, and a trace is never the source (see `05_what_shape_means.md`).

## What an answer would look like

An answer is a **dependency-ordered priority rule over structural roles**, not a ranked list of named instruments. It says which measurement class must be secured before which, on what independence condition, with what treatment of a null result. Binding the rule to a named molecule or a named instrument for a named target is a separate, grounded step that this folder does not take. The figures return a shape, not a name.

## What is deliberately not here

- No personal emails of named individuals.
- No ranking of people by how likely they are to agree with the claim.
- No bulk-send plan.
- No claim that this derivation is a ground reading. It is a simulated derivation checked for internal consistency against the figures, so it cannot settle the question it reports on (see the mode note in `01` and `05`).
- No claim to name the one correct instrument or molecule for a specific target.

## Scope note

This solution is about the **ordering of measurement classes** in a life detection mission, not about which mission should be flown or which target should be visited. Where the world literature is used, it is secondary corroboration only. The figures decide.
