# Outreach draft: check the priority rule before you read the framework

**Use:** one-to-one, or as a cover letter attached to a submission through the public venues in `03_public_contact_points.csv`. **Not** for bulk send.

**Hard precondition:** do not send until the refuter below has been checked against the reader's own mission experience. The offer collapses on contact if the stage-1 output does not survive.

---

**Subject:** One checkable claim about how a life detection mission orders its measurements

> Hi [name],
>
> I have one specific structural claim I would like you to check against what you already know about life detection mission design. I am not asking you to read a theory first, and I am not asking you to agree with anything. If the claim does not survive, there is nothing else to look at.
>
> **The claim (`specified`, not `grounded`):** a mission should not rank candidate biosignatures and fly the strongest ones. It should order measurement *roles* by dependency. Concretely: (1) describe the residual in effect terms before naming an instrument family; (2) split past-life and present-life into separate inquiries, because their false-positive structures differ; (3) secure context, vocabulary and a stated disconfirmation criterion before any high-rung signifier; (4) buy independence between measurements rather than agreement among them; (5) price arrangement (bond) above mere presence (ingredient); (6) treat contamination control, negative controls and calibration as first-order science, because the ground does not decay but the channel does; (7) sharpen instruments already returning something that fails to resolve; (8) report a null as unreached, with a named gap, not as absence.
>
> This is a shape and an ordering, not a mission design. It does not name the correct molecule, instrument or target. That step needs a ground reading, which this claim does not have.
>
> **What would refute it, written down before I wrote the claim:**
> 1. A single measurement, with no independent second frame and no context, settles the life question against all abiotic alternatives.
> 2. A mission that flies only a high-rung signifier, with no context measurement and no pre-stated disconfirmation criterion, still produces a result that bears on the ground.
> 3. Ingredient presence alone discriminates biotic from abiotic origin across known pathways, with no arrangement or context information.
> 4. A non-detection by a closed instrument set legitimately entails absence, with no statement about the set's expected detection.
> 5. Two measurements drawn from the same physical process count as two independent witnesses.
>
> I wrote these before writing the claim. If any one holds, I will withdraw or rewrite the ordering. A one-line "this fails because..." is more useful to me than agreement. If you would rather not engage, no reply is needed.
>
> **If it survives, and only then:** here are the sources. The figures are the authority, not my summary of them.
> - The Ladder of Life Detection (NASA Astrobiology, public resource page): https://science.nasa.gov/astrobiology/researchers/life-detection-resources/ladder-of-life-detection/
> - Climbing the Ladder to Life Detection (NASA, 2018): https://www.nasa.gov/missions/climbing-the-ladder-to-life-detection/
> - Assembly theory, Sharma et al., Nature 2023, open access: https://pmc.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/articles/PMC10567559/
>
> **If you want to use the machinery:** the formula and figures are in the repository link below. Run them on a problem you know well and can check against a ground. Write down what would refute your result *before* you run it, and label the mode of what you get. A reported failure is more useful to me than a reported success.
>
> - Figures and formula for direct download: https://github.com/IsaiahN/tether/tree/general-agent/figures
>
> - [your name]

## Timing of stages

1. **Stage 1:** send the claim and the five refuters. Ask what the reader would count as a failure. Record their verdict **before** showing any theory.
2. **Stage 2, opt-in only:** if they are curious, send the three public source links. Their later agreement is never pooled as corroboration.
3. **Stage 3:** offer the formula and the figures as a method to reuse, with the instruction that each user writes their own refuter and states their own mode.

No follow-up sequence is automated. One message, one person, one opt-out line.
