# Expert verification packet: the Tether on abilities that resist a body-less AI

**What this is:** a claim in a form a researcher in embodied intelligence, developmental psychology or
social cognition can check. Mode, derivation and refuters are stated before the conclusion.

**Mode: `specified`, not `grounded`.** No experiment on a model or a robot is claimed. This is a
criterion and a stop, not a result.

## 1. The claim, in one paragraph

An ability is hard for AI *because* it depends on embodiment, development or social relationships
exactly when its competence is a property of agent-and-habitat contact, of a lineage that met the
ground, or of a relation to frames with differing closures, rather than of the agent's library
alone. The three conditions are ordered by dependency (body, then development, then social relation)
and form a union with specific compounds at their intersections. A benchmark or simulator score
cannot decide membership, because a harness is a substituted habitat: a synthetic solve proves
wiring and never capability. Physical skills and pose-dependent spatial reasoning, staged
sensorimotor development, and mentalizing, empathy and cumulative tradition fall in the three main
regions; joint attention and learning from a demonstrator fall in the intersections. The names come
from published world sources and are bindings, not figure derivations. The figures deliver the
criterion and decline to deliver an exhaustive inventory.

## 2. Three checkable sub-claims

### C1 - framework-internal, checkable by reading Figures 11 and 3

- **Statement:** capability is a property of agent-and-habitat, and the dependency chain has an
  affordance-vocabulary link with no instrument in a text-only habitat.
- **Verbatim figure lines:** Fig 11 :39 *"So the answerable question is not what intelligence is, but
  what makes a system capable. And capability is a property of agent-and-habitat, never of the agent
  alone."* Fig 11 :40 *"A system cannot be made more capable by improving the agent in isolation. An
  improvement that does not change contact changes nothing, however much it improves."* Fig 3 :7
  *"it has terms for what things afford: what can be moved, opened, joined, blocked"* and Fig 3 :27
  *"A link with no instrument is not a link that works. It is a link nobody has looked at."*
- **Refuter:** demonstrate the ability in an agent with no body and no sensorimotor history, against
  the same ground the human ability is graded against, not a text proxy. If it transfers, C1 fails
  for that ability.

### C2 - engineering-facing, checkable by a robotics or embodied-AI researcher

- **Statement:** a simulator or benchmark is a substituted habitat, so a solve there is evidence of
  wiring and not of capability. This is why the sim-to-real gap is the right kind of measurement and
  a leaderboard score is the wrong kind.
- **Verbatim figure lines:** Fig 11 :66 *"Isolation is not removal of the habitat. It is substitution
  of one habitat for another."* Fig 11 :70 *"A test harness is a substituted habitat. Which is why a
  synthetic solve proves wiring and never capability."*
- **Corroborating world source (secondary):** Wong, Kang, Bai and Zhang, arXiv 2505.01458v3, 2026:
  *"policies that perform well in simulation can degrade on hardware; this discrepancy is the
  sim-to-real gap."*
- **Refuter:** a task where a simulated solve transfers to the physical ground with no unmodelled
  contact term and no simulator-specific artifact, measured on hardware. That would show the
  substituted habitat carried the capability for that task.

### C3 - social and developmental facing, checkable by a cognitive scientist

- **Statement:** a single frame cannot reach what a frame with a differing closure holds, and a
  corpus is a recording of occasions rather than a developmental lineage.
- **Verbatim figure lines:** Fig 6 :23 *"No frame escapes its own closure. The bound moves only on
  contact with a frame whose closure differs."* Fig 2 :35 *"Alignment is triangulation, not
  negotiation. A mirror cannot correct you."* Fig 4 :22 *"Only methods cross up. Only head starts
  come down."* Fig 4 :25 *"A recording carried upward looks like knowledge and is a description of
  one occasion."*
- **Refuter:** one isolated frame, with no second closure in its habitat, that tracks a mental state
  genuinely different from its own on a ground that requires it; or a single training run that
  reaches the developed competence with no staged trajectory and no habitat change. Self-projection
  does not count as tracking a different state.

## 3. What is explicitly not claimed

- Not an exhaustive list. The figures return a kind of thing, never the answer itself (Fig 9 :32).
  The inventory is a reported `gap`, not a filled table asserted as figure output.
- Not a ranking of abilities and not an average across domains (Fig 1 :22).
- Not a claim that any named model lacks these abilities. No model was tested.
- Not `grounded`. No hardware transfer, developmental trajectory or second-frame test was run.
- Not a claim that text contains no human cognition. It contains outputs, not the generator that
  produced them.
- Not a claim that the ability names were derived. They are bindings from the sources in section 4,
  selected by the criterion and recorded with provenance (Fig 8 :50-52).

## 5. What an expert can check in twenty minutes

1. Read Fig 11 :39-40 and :66, :70, and Fig 3 :7, :27. Four short passages. Do the two engineering
   claims follow, or is a line being made to govern a different quantity?
2. Check C2 against your own field: is the sim-to-real gap a measurement of contact, or only of
   calibration? Name one result where hardware and simulator rankings agree without tuning.
3. Check C3: name a ground on which an isolated frame could be said to track a different mental state,
   or say why self-projection cannot pass it.
4. Name one instrument that would separate a fluent behaviour from the contact that produced it, or
   say why none is possible.
5. Send the refutation. A failure is more useful than an endorsement. If you would rather not
   engage, no reply is needed.
