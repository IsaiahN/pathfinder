## 1. A shape is a constraint, not a filler

The canonical README states the limit: the framework returns *"what a missing thing would have to
look like (a shape, not a name)."* Fig 9 :32: *"you get a kind of thing, never the answer itself."*
Fig 13 :134: *"Unreachable is a verdict here, not a silence,"*.

From *The Library of Babel*: *"you can cut your search short if the set of books returned is small
and descriptive enough that it provides a general shape of the answer that satisfies your
question."*

So a **shape** is the narrowed form of an answer you can state before you can name it. It constrains;
it does not fill. For this question the shape is the separator itself:

> An ability is in the hard set iff its competence lives in agent-and-habitat contact, in a
> developmental lineage that met the ground, or in relations to frames with differing closures,
> rather than in the library alone.

That is a shape. A list containing "empathy" and "walking" is a name-level object supplied by other
frames.

## 2. How the shape is obtained here

- *Library of Babel*: use the vocabulary you already have that is *closest* to the thing, look for
  recurring new terms that overlap or correlate with it, recurse. *"With each new term gained, you
  are able to narrow the space by definition and deepen your vocabulary."*
- Fig 6 :30: *"The description travels. Fails by being sharpened into terms only we can read. Cast it
  in effect terms."* Fig 9 :30-31: *"your words are borrowed too, so describe the effect rather than
  the cause."*
- *Decoding Tacit Knowledge*: *"When contact isn't possible, the best you can send is a plain
  description of what you'd expect to see, because anyone can check that against the world."*
- *Library of Babel* on stopping: *"you will know that you are done because the finish line will also
  be the starting place, as there is nowhere else to search and nothing else that can describe the
  answer better."*

The census in `01` followed that order: the residual was described as an effect first (three
conditions binding, a union compound), and only then were figures and world fields chosen.

## 3. What licenses a name

Two things, both outside the shape itself:

1. **The ground settles it.** Formula step 5: *"the ground settles it. Verified only by what pays."
   The canonical README says the framework *"stops at the limit of what the available instruments can
   distinguish, and records the lineage so the stop is honest."*
2. **A provenanced import from a frame whose closure differs** (Fig 6 :23, Fig 8 :50-52), with a
   source gate and a shadow test.

For this question no ability name is settled by such a ground, and the world names are adopted
imports. They are therefore recorded with author, venue, year and a quote in `10_source_ledger.csv`
and labelled as bindings in `01` section 5. Fig 8 :50-52: *"Convergent derivation and adopted import
look identical in the contents. Only the record of where each came from separates them,"*.

*They're Scanning Books When They Should Be Studying Nature* sharpens the point: *"A corpus is the
compressed output of human minds; the capacity that produced it is a generator, and outputs do not
contain their generator."* And: *"A minimum viable set of six terms is a human's careful guess at
reality's compression axes; another sensorium's minimum set need not be those six."* A text-trained
system inherits the output, not the generator. That is the same asymmetry Fig 11 :40 states in
figure terms.

## 4. The three conditions as the essays carry them

The essays are secondary corroboration. They do not decide the verdict; the figures do. What they
add is vocabulary for the three domains.

- **Embodiment.** *They're Scanning Books*: *"Change the sensors, and not just the data changes, the
  shape of the compression changes: the categories, the primitives, what counts as similar to what."*
  *Decoding Tacit Knowledge* names Harry Collins's somatic kind: *"things our bodies can do but we
  cannot describe how, like balancing on a bike"*, and says the pretext *"lives in the body and can
  only be rebuilt by doing."* Fig 3 :7 names the affordance vocabulary; Symbols table :81 adds that
  the allocentric/egocentric transform cannot be run without a pose.
- **Development.** *Decoding Tacit Knowledge*: an apprentice learns *"not only through language but
  also by observation, imitation, and practice"*, and *"If intelligence is something each mind
  rebuilds through contact, then text alone can't hand it over."* Fig 4 :22: *"Only methods cross up.
  Only head starts come down."* Fig 4 :34: *"A seed carries no small tree: it carries what will grow
  one, and the conditions it lands in decide what it becomes."*
- **Social relationships.** *Decoding Tacit Knowledge* names Collins's collective kind: *"knowledge
  we draw that is the property of society, such as the rules for language"*, which you get *"only by
  living among them"*. It also warns: *"Two people who only check against each other drift, and they
  drift together, so neither notices."* And: *"Two frames together reach what neither reaches
  alone."* *How a Society of Agents Would Work*: *"capability is a property of agent-and-habitat,
  never of the agent alone."* Fig 2 :31: *"Independence, between two frames, is how little they draw
  on the same evidence."*

## 5. Q2: does the framework give the inventory? ANSWERED, as a gap

**What licenses a particular ability name as figure-derived?** Nothing in the figures. The figures
give the separator, the dependency order, the union rule, and the stop. They do not enumerate human
abilities. The exhaustive list is a `gap`, reported under F7 of `01`, not a silence. Fig 13 :134:
*"Unreachable is a verdict here, not a silence,"*.

**Does the record commit the forbidden error?** No, after correction. The names are explicitly
labelled as bindings from world frames with provenance, not derived from the six-term set and not
called imports that move the wall (Fig 6 :26: *"An import adds no atom to the world."*).

### Correction to my own §3 (kept visible)

My first draft read "shape, not a name" as a ban on naming anything at all. That is too strong. The
canonical phrase governs a **missing** primitive, one not held in the library. Naming a world-frame
term against an already-derived criterion is a binding, not the invention of a seventh primitive.
The exemplar's `05` section 5b makes the same narrowing. What remains true for this question is the
separate F7 finding: no figure line enumerates abilities, so the inventory cannot be presented as a
figure result regardless of how bindings are labelled. Both statements are recorded here so neither
is quietly lost.

## 6. Prestige is not action, and this audit is a simulation

From *The Recursive Self-Improvement Trap*: *"Prestige must never be allowed to validate an action
directly... Prestige can fund an action. It can give an action a budget to be tried. But it cannot
settle an action. Only the ground can settle it. The Ground Maintainer must be blind to prestige."*
An approach that gathers famous names to agree that an ability is hard is the named collapse, not
corroboration. The legitimate ask is a ground test.

Same essay: *"the output is only as good as the validator. The agent, or the tool, or the frame, or
the theory, cannot validate itself."* And: *"LLMs can quickly simulate an approximate answer/output
with the formula found in the architecture post... However, the truly testable output would need to
be derived from the real architecture or working the formula yourself."* LLM output is *"simulacra."*
So `01` is a simulated derivation, checked for internal consistency against the figures. It is not a
test of the question and must be labelled as such. It cannot settle anything it reports on.

## 7. One further consequence for this question

*The Hard Problem is About Source*: an instrument that reads green *"cannot comment on the source"*,
and *"If several paths create the same behavior, then behavior is a projection with lossy
information."* It was *"proven wrong not by argument, but by the arrival of a better instrument."*
Applied here: a model
that produces fluent, mind-reading-looking text is a projection. The reading does not determine the
source. An instrument that separates contact from fluency is owed, not a stronger claim about what
the model "really" has. That is why the refuters in `01` section 4 are ground tests and why the
verdict in `09` stays `candidate`.
