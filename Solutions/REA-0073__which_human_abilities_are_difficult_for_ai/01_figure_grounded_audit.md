## 0. The residual, in effect terms

The question asks for a set of human abilities and a reason: they are hard for AI *because* they
depend on embodiment, development, or social relationships. Stated as an effect, not a cause:

> A system whose library is fixed by a corpus, whose body is absent or simulated, whose history is a
> single training run rather than a developmental lineage, and whose habitat contains no other frame
> with an independently updated closure, fails to register certain residuals that a human registers.
> The failure is not in its score on the benchmark. It is in what the benchmark cannot carry: the
> contact, the lineage, and the other frame. What is owed is a criterion that separates abilities
> whose competence lives in that contact from abilities whose competence lives in the library.

Option A: answer with a nameable list of abilities. Option B: answer with the criterion that decides
membership in the list, mark the list itself as a world-frame binding, and stop where the figures
stop. What each leads to: A produces an inventory no figure line licenses and invites the
misreading that the framework names abilities; B produces a checkable separator and an honest gap.
The shape claim to bind as an ordinary term (formula step "Direction") is: **an ability is in the
hard set iff its competence is a property of agent-and-habitat, of a lineage that met the ground,
or of a relation between frames with differing closures, rather than of the agent's library alone.**

## 1. DOMAIN CENSUS (before any figure line is chosen)

### 1a. R by arity, symmetry, scale

- **Arity.** The residual relates three conditions (body, development, social relation), not one. It
  is a **union compound**, not a single hard question. Fig 9 :55 says *"A bundled problem is
  usually a union of overlapping conditions, not a list of separate ones"*, and Fig 9 :57 says *"a
  case in two conditions is not a harder case of either. It stops being a mixture and becomes a
  specific compound."* Three conditions binding is three intersections plus the single-condition
  regions, each with its own loop.
- **Symmetry.** Not symmetric. A body supplies physical contact and a pose; development supplies a
  lineage and carried methods; other frames supply independent closures. They are not
  interchangeable, and the order matters: a developed, embodied agent is a prerequisite for most
  social abilities, not a peer of them.
- **Scale.** Step (perception and action), cycle (developmental trajectory), level (individual
  versus social), lineage (selection over generations). The abilities named by the world sources
  span all four, which is why no single instrument settles the list.

### 1b. One line per domain: actors / currency / anchor

| domain | actors | currency | anchor (nameable?) |
|---|---|---|---|
| D1, the embodied contact domain | body, habitat conditions, affordances, poses | residuals from sensorimotor prediction, contact and its failure | yes: physical measurement, an instrument or experiment |
| D2, the developmental lineage domain | the agent over time, the habitat, the maintainer or caregiver that introduces actors | methods carried across stages; head starts sent down | yes: the ground for what the developed ability achieves, read at the end of the trajectory |
| D3, the social / between-frames domain | two or more frames, each with history and closure | agreement, residuals, imports from a closure that differs | yes: the ground, never agreement (Fig 2 :45-46) |
| D4, the compound intersections | embodied + developed + social abilities, for example joint attention, imitation of a skilled model, gaze-and-posture intention reading | residuals that only appear when two or three conditions bind | yes, but only by enumerating the habitat outward (Fig 11 :43-44) |

No domain is admitted without a nameable anchor. D4 is admitted because its anchor is still the
ground; what is harder is enumerating which residuals are three steps out (Fig 11 :52).

### 1c. Partition or union

**Union.** The three conditions overlap. "Learning a manipulation skill from a demonstrator by
watching their gaze and posture" is a specific compound: physical contact, a developmental
history, and a second frame whose closure differs. It stops being a mixture and becomes its own
loop. Fig 9 :54: *"And when the split lands on a union rather than a partition, unbundle before you
search again."*

### 1d. Order by dependency (Fig 3)

1. **Perception and affordance vocabulary** (Fig 3 :5, :7). Upstream. Without terms for what things
   afford, nothing downstream can form an objective.
2. **Development / learn and carry** (Fig 3 :15, Fig 4 :22). It consumes perception and produces
   methods that can be composed over.
3. **Social relations** (Fig 2 :31, Fig 6 :23). It consumes developed frames with differing
   closures and produces imports and triangulation.

A step whose input never arrives cannot be diagnosed, only its predecessor can (Fig 3 :22-23). So a
social deficit measured in a body-less, undeveloped system is a reading of nothing, not a weak
reading. This ordering is part of the specification, not an editorial preference (seat-reasoning
move 9).

## 3. Findings

### F1 - Capability lives in the agent-and-habitat contact (decisive, single-source at the anchor)

Fig 11 :39:

> **"So the answerable question is not what intelligence is, but what makes a system capable. And capability is a property of agent-and-habitat, never of the agent alone."**

Fig 11 :40:

> **"A system cannot be made more capable by improving the agent in isolation. An improvement that does not change contact changes nothing, however much it improves."**

This is the criterion the question asks for, restated. An ability is hard for AI for the stated
reason exactly when its competence is not carried by the agent's library but by its contact with a
habitat, a lineage, or other frames. Improving a model in isolation does not change contact, so it
does not move the capability.

**Mode:** `general` (structural), with a `specified` prediction that body-less training leaves these
residuals live. **Status:** single-source for the anchor claim (Fig 11), triangulated for the
symptom with Fig 3 :27 (*"A link with no instrument is not a link that works. It is a link nobody has
looked at."*), a different figure and a different object. **Refuter:** demonstrate the ability in an
agent with no body, no developmental trajectory and no other frame, against the same ground the human
ability is graded against, not a textual proxy. If it transfers, the contact claim fails for that
ability.

### F2 - A benchmark harness is a substituted habitat (single-source, world-corroborated)

Fig 11 :66:

> **"Isolation is not removal of the habitat. It is substitution of one habitat for another."**

Fig 11 :70:

> **"A test harness is a substituted habitat. Which is why a synthetic solve proves wiring and never capability."**

Text benchmarks and physics simulators substitute a habitat. They inherit actors the designer did
not choose, and what was failed to reproduce is invisible until the goal fails (Fig 11 :68-69). A
high score in that habitat is wiring. This is why the question cannot be settled by pointing at
benchmark results, in either direction.

**World corroboration:** Wong, Kang, Bai and Zhang (2026), a survey of embodied AI simulators,
write: *"policies that perform well in simulation can degrade on hardware; this discrepancy is the
sim-to-real gap."* That is the same effect read in a different field. **Status:** single-source figure,
corroborated. **Refuter:** a task in which a simulated solve transfers to the physical ground with no
unmodelled contact term and no fined-tuned simulator artifact, measured on hardware. That would show
the substitution carried the capability for that task, not only the wiring.

### F3 - Embodiment: affordances, and the pose-dependent frame transform (single-source; pose line is narrow)

Fig 3 :7:

> **"it has terms for what things afford: what can be moved, opened, joined, blocked"**

Fig 3 :27:

> **"A link with no instrument is not a link that works. It is a link nobody has looked at."**

The dependency chain puts the vocabulary of affordances at link 2, between perception and the
objective. A system that can perceive an object but has no terms for what it affords has a broken
middle link. The link has no instrument in a text-only habitat, so it is not a link that works; it is
a link nobody has looked at.

Symbols table :81 (verbatim, spanning two extracted lines): "The map between allocentric and
egocentric at one scale. Distinct from T_A and T_E, which run between scales. Cannot be run without a
pose." This is the one figure line in the whole set that names an embodied prerequisite outright:
the frame transform `F` needs a pose. Egocentric versus allocentric spatial reasoning, perspective
taking over a physical scene, and pose-dependent manipulation are therefore hard for exactly the
stated reason.

**World corroboration:** the Stanford Encyclopedia of Philosophy entry on embodied cognition states
the Conceptualization theme: *"The properties of an organism's body ground, limit, or constrain the
concepts an organism can acquire."* Jacquey, Baldassarre, Santucci and O'Regan (2019) write that an
agent *"should be sensitive to the link between its actions and the consequences of its actions,
called sensorimotor contingencies"*, and review how infants acquire body knowledge, memory,
generalization and goal-directedness through that sensitivity.

**Status:** the affordance-vocabulary claim is single-source in the figures (Fig 3) and corroborated
by two world fields; the pose-transform claim is single-source in the Symbols table only, and is
marked as such, not upgraded by corroboration from another domain. **Refuter:** run the
allocentric/egocentric transform in an agent with no pose against a spatial ground, or show that the
affordance vocabulary is learned from corpus alone and transfers to novel objects on hardware.
Either fires F3 for that ability.

### F4 - Development: methods, recordings, and the lineage that met the ground (triangulated)

Fig 4 :22:

> **"Only methods cross up. Only head starts come down."**

Fig 4 :25:

> **"A recording carried upward looks like knowledge and is a description of one occasion."**

Fig 4 :34:

> **"The level a method lands on remakes it. A seed carries no small tree: it carries what will grow one, and the conditions it lands in decide what it becomes."**

Fig 11 :36:

> **"Where nothing maintains anything, intelligence is a label applied afterwards to a lineage that met its ground. Nothing was intelligent; selection ran."**

A corpus is a recording of many occasions, not a developmental trajectory. What development carries
is a method that can be reapplied, and the conditions it lands in decide what it becomes. Abilities
whose competence is the residue of a lineage that met the ground (staged motor skill, retention and
reactivation, generalization across contexts, intuitive physics built by acting) are hard for
exactly the stated reason. Fig 11 :40 closes the loop: an improvement that does not change contact
changes nothing.

**World corroboration:** Jacquey et al. (2019) review the developmental trajectory from general
body motion to limb-specific action, retention growing from one day to three months, and
generalization that strengthens with age. **Status:** triangulated across Fig 4 (membrane, scale)
and Fig 11 (habitat, lineage), two figures with low overlap on different objects. **Refuter:** a
single training run with no staged trajectory and no habitat change that reaches the same developed
competence on the same ground. If it does, the developmental criterion fails for that ability.

### F5 - Social relations require other frames with differing closures (triangulated)

Fig 2 :31-32:

> **"Independence, between two frames, is how little they draw on the same evidence."**
> **"Independence is what makes agreement mean anything."**

Fig 2 :35:

> **"Alignment is triangulation, not negotiation. A mirror cannot correct you."**

Fig 6 :23:

> **"No frame escapes its own closure. The bound moves only on contact with a frame whose closure differs."**

Fig 10 :59:

> **"The condition is that nothing about which value the ground returns today serves the occupant: an interest in the outcome is unavoidable, an interest in the reading is disqualifying."**

A single frame cannot reach what another frame holds unless their closures differ. Social abilities
are not a matter of more text about minds; they require tracking another agent whose mental state
is not a mirror of one's own, whose stake is not one's stake. Mentalizing, perspective taking,
self-other distinction, empathy, emotion perception, social norms, selective learning from a more
skilled model and cumulative tradition all sit here.

**World corroboration:** Eikelboom et al. (2025) define social cognition as *"the full spectrum of
processing social information; that is, the ability to perceive, interpret, and interact with
emotions, feelings, and intentions of others"* and give empathy as *"the ability to experience
others' affective states while maintaining the distinction from one's own affective states."*
Whiten and van de Waal (2018) conclude that *"social learning pervades primate ontogenetic
development, importantly shaping locally adaptive knowledge and skills that span multiple aspects
of the behavioral repertoire."*

**Status:** triangulated across Fig 2 (independence), Fig 6 (differing closure) and Fig 10 (stake),
figures that overlap in subject but differ in object. **Refuter:** an isolated single frame, with no
other frame in its habitat and no second closure to range against, that passes a ground requiring it
to track a mental state genuinely different from its own. Note the trap: a self-projection that
copies its own state is a mirror, and Fig 2 :35 has already refused mirrors.

### F6 - The three conditions form a union compound (single-source)

Fig 9 :55 (line 503):

> **"A bundled problem is usually a union of overlapping conditions, not a list of separate ones."**

Fig 9 :54 (line 502, preceding sentence): *"And when the split lands on a union rather than a
partition, unbundle before you search again."*

The ability list is therefore not three disjoint buckets. Joint attention, imitation of a skilled
demonstrator, and reading intention from gaze and posture are compounds: body plus development plus
a second frame. Each is a specific compound with its own loop, and a single lever that clears the
single-condition region leaves the intersections standing (Fig 9 :56).

**Mode:** `general`. **Status:** single-source (Fig 9), corroborated by the world sources in `06`.
**Refuter:** show that the three conditions are independent and additive for a given ability, so the
intersection behaves as the sum of its parts. Then the union reading is wrong and a partition should
be used.

### F7 - The figures give a criterion, not an exhaustive nameable inventory (gap, triangulated)

Fig 9 :32:

> **"you get a kind of thing, never the answer itself"**

Fig 13 :134 (line 981):

> **"Unreachable is a verdict here, not a silence,"**

Fig 9 :44:

> **"Only a sealed room can be searched to the end, and no frame is a sealed room. So searching and finding nothing proves nothing."**

A genuine re-census (section 8) does not find a figure line that enumerates human abilities. There is
no figure list of "walking", "theory of mind", "empathy" or "joint attention". The figures govern
the shape of the separator: contact, lineage, differing closure, and the substituted habitat. The
names come from world frames. This is a **gap for the inventory**, not a silence: the criterion is
reported, and the absence of an enumerating line is reported as a verdict.

**Status:** triangulated across Fig 9 and Fig 13 (different figures, different objects), with the
canonical README's *"a shape, not a name"* corroborating. **Refuter:** point to a figure line that
names abilities, or derive the list from the six-term set. Until that is done, any named list in this
folder is a binding and is labelled as such.

### F8 - Verdicts stay per domain and are never averaged (single-source rule)

Fig 1 :19:

> **"The residual is indexed by object slot, not aggregated."**

Fig 1 :22:

> **"Aggregating across slots is how a live signal disappears into an average."**

The answer must not collapse embodiment, development and social relations into a single "difficulty
score". Each domain keeps its own verdict. A system may be strong on the social benchmark and fail
the pose transform; averaging the two throws away the only signal that says where to look.

**Refuter:** a single scalar that predicts all three domains equally well against the ground. That
would show the residuals are not independent and the separation is unnecessary.

### F9 - World names are bindings, not imports that move the wall (triangulated)

Fig 8 :50-52 (line 433):

> **"Convergent derivation and adopted import look identical in the contents. Only the record of where each came from separates them,"**

Fig 8 :53:

> **"Residual first, frame second."**

Fig 9 :27-28:

> **"describe it before you go looking, or any frame you pick will seem to fit"**

The world sources are consulted after the census, and each name they supply is labelled as coming
from that frame. Naming "theory of mind" from Eikelboom et al., or "sensorimotor contingencies" from
Jacquey et al., adds no primitive to the framework and moves no wall (Fig 6 :26: *"An import adds no
atom to the world."*). It binds an existing shape to a term from another closure.

**Refuter:** show that a world term changed the criterion derived in section 3 rather than being
selected by it. That would invert the order and make the reading apophenia.

### F10 - Cost, filters and the right anchor (triangulated)

Fig 2 :42:

> **"Buy independence in proportion to the cost of being wrong."**

Fig 9 :60:

> **"Use filters for the budget and witnesses for the verdict. Never let a filter hand you a verdict."**

Fig 2 :37 (line 87):

> **"Human judgment is the right anchor for is this a good answer for a person, because the question is about people."**

A benchmark ranking or a tier label is a filter for how much checking to do, never a witness for
whether an ability is hard for the stated reason. Because the question is constitutively about
people, human judgment is the legitimate anchor for the *usable* part of this answer, while
instruments and hardware transfer measure the contact. An instrument can be wrong on axes it does
not measure (Fig 2 :37-38), so a text benchmark is not that anchor.

**Refuter:** a verdict that changes when the budget or the venue changes, with no change in contact.
Then the filter was handing over the verdict.

## 4. Pre-registered refuter (written before the conclusion)

Per Fig 10 and the seat rule that *"a falsifier written before the run is a design instrument, not
only an honesty device"*, these are fixed before the synthesis in `07` and `09`. The shape claim is
refuted if any of the following holds:

1. **Contact test.** An ability classified as embodiment-dependent is demonstrated by an agent with
   no body and no sensorimotor history, against the same ground the human ability is graded against,
   not a text proxy. Fires against F1 and F3.
2. **Lineage test.** An ability classified as development-dependent is reached by a single training
   run with no staged trajectory, no carried method and no change in habitat conditions, at equal
   ground performance. Fires against F4.
3. **Other-frame test.** An ability classified as social is demonstrated by one isolated frame with
   no second closure in its habitat, on a ground that requires tracking a mental state different
   from its own. A self-projection does not count. Fires against F5.
4. **Pose test.** The allocentric/egocentric transform is run by an agent with no pose, or the pose
   line turns out to govern a different quantity. Fires against the Symbols table entry and sends it
   back to census.
5. **Union test.** The three conditions prove additive for a named ability, so the intersection is
   just the sum and no compound loop is owed. Fires against F6.
6. **Inventory test.** A figure line is found that names the abilities, or the six-term set derives
   the list. Fires against F7 and turns the gap into a figure-grounded inventory.
7. **Criterion inversion.** A world source changes the criterion rather than being selected by it.
   Fires against F9.

If any fires, the affected verdict is withdrawn and the census is redone. Until one is run, no part
of this answer may be labelled `grounded`.

## 8. Strains and re-census
