## QUESTION

Decide, in effect terms, how inherited tradition and new information or institutions are combined without one simply replacing the other. Option A: treat the question as a trade-off between two mutually exclusive states and look for the rule that balances them. Option B: re-census the question as a union of three domains, then derive the composition, persistence, and anchor for each. What each leads to: A searches for a balance point that the framework's record does not contain, selects for agreement, and cannot discriminate combination from a substitution that preserves the surface; B yields a checkable shape, six pre-registered falsifiers, and an honest stop at the missing instrument.

## VERDICT (from the figures)

The shape survives across all three domains; the question's dichotomy does not. Belief already mixes inherited and private history by a learnable weight, so combination is the default. The record never deletes, so correction is an added refusal that removes a term's standing to compose. New material is an import that expands access without adding a world primitive. What survives between generations is a method or a head start. What looks like replacement is usually a habitat substitution whose failed reproductions and unchosen introductions are not yet measured, or a convention that has started deciding an outcome. The three domains get separate verdicts: the framework returns the shape, not the name of a tradition or institution. No community was measured, so the result is `specified`, ungrounded, candidate, with a named `gap` at the instrument link.

- **cost if wrong:** bounded and locatable if the packet is used as written. Each sub-claim has a refuter, so an error is findable rather than invisible. The real cost is spending the independence of outside readers by sending a recording instead of a generator, or by selecting them for likely agreement. Fig 2 :42 prices that: "Buy independence in proportion to the cost of being wrong." Both are avoidable by leading with the shape and its refuter and by using opt-in institutional channels.
- **undoable:** yes. The shape can be narrowed without withdrawing the derivation. If deletion, fixed `w`, surplus at full overlap, or habitat subtraction is demonstrated, only the affected finding falls; no primitive has to be discarded. A sent claim can be superseded by a corrected packet with its provenance intact.
- **reversed by:** a ground reading, not agreement. A panel study showing whether `w` moves with outcomes; the seven bond tests applied to a named practice; a lineage showing that a refused term stayed in the record; an enumeration of what a new habitat failed to reproduce and introduced unchosen; or an instrument that discriminates combination from substitution. Agreement among researchers who share a literature is not such an instrument (Fig 2 :40). Until one of these fires, the verdict stands as `candidate`.
