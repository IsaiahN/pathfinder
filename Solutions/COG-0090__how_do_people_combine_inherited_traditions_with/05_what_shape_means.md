## 1. A shape is a constraint, not a filler

The canonical local usage rule (`FRAMEWORK/reference/FIGURES_README_canonical.md`) states the limit: the framework returns *"what a missing thing would have to look like (a shape, not a name)"*, then stops at the limit of what the instruments can distinguish and records the lineage. Fig 9 :32: *"you get a kind of thing, never the answer itself."* Fig 13: *"It can say the answer is out of reach with the library as it stands... It cannot say which primitive is missing, because naming it would mean already holding it."*

From essay 1: *"You can cut your search short if the set of books returned is small and descriptive enough that it provides a general shape of the answer that satisfies your question."* From essay 6: the framework *"cuts down to the shape of a thing, problem or question based on the given information available at this point in time."*

For COG-0090 a shape is therefore the set of constraints any answer must satisfy: it must name a non-deleting composition, a cross-generation persistence mechanism, and an anchor that discriminates combination from substitution. It does **not** name which tradition, which institution, or which community.

## 2. How the shape is obtained

- Essay 1: use the vocabulary you already have that is closest to the thing, look for recurring new terms that overlap or correlate with it, and recurse. *"With each new term gained, you narrow the space and deepen your vocabulary."* The same essay warns: *"Perhaps your problem was a bundle or conflation of several problems masquerading as one?"* That is exactly the state COG-0090 starts in.
- Fig 6 :30: *"The description travels. Fails by being sharpened into terms only we can read. Cast it in effect terms."* Fig 9 :30-31: *"your words are borrowed too, so describe the effect rather than the cause."*
- Essay 3: *"When contact isn't possible, the best you can send is a plain description of what you'd expect to see, because anyone can check that against the world."* This is why the outreach email in `02` asks what a failure would look like before showing any source.
- Essay 6: the loop *"stops when nothing you have registers a difference from it"*, and *"indiscernibility is not identity."* A community that looks continuous under a coarse instrument has not thereby been shown to be continuous.

## 3. What licenses a name

Two things, both outside the shape:

1. **The ground settles it.** Formula step 5: *"the ground settles it. Verified only by what pays."* The figures README: the framework *"stops at the limit of what the available instruments can distinguish, and records the lineage so the stop is honest."*
2. **A provenanced import from a frame whose closure differs** (Fig 6 :23, Fig 8 :50-52), with a source gate and a shadow test.

Essay 7 sharpens this: *"The real import is not a new term in the existing space. It is a new dimension of the space."* And: *"A minimum viable set of six terms is a human's careful guess at reality's compression axes; another sensorium's minimum set need not be those six."* Also: *"Declaring the primitives does not make them neutral. It makes the choice visible."* So the weighting `w`, the seven bonds, and negation are declared primitives of a frame. Declaring them makes the choice auditable, not true.

**Correction to my own reading.** I initially wrote that "shape, not a name" forbids identifying which mechanism is at work in a case. It does not. The README's phrase governs a *missing* primitive, one not held in `Γ`. Here `w`, the bonds, and ¬ are already held. Saying "this case is a production bond" is a binding that needs provenance, not a forbidden invention. Both the constraint and the correction are recorded so neither is quietly lost.

## 4. Why the source of a reading is not determined by the reading

Essay 5 gives the mechanism. Metamerism: an instrument reads green and *"cannot comment on the source"*; the same reading could come from one green light, yellow plus blue, RGB, CMYK, or a scheme not yet discovered. *"If several paths create the same behavior, then behavior is a projection with lossy information."* It was the spectrometer, not an argument, that resolved it. Essay 6 adds the distinction horizon and union horizon: a reading may pass one observer and fail an aggregate of them, and *"being below the union horizon is never a settled verdict, only a report that nobody has reached it yet."*

Applied here: observing a ritual still performed does not tell you whether its source is continuity, an added refusal, a substituted habitat reproducing the surface, or a convention deciding an outcome. Fig 6 :27: *"Which is why some unreachability is depth and some is genuine absence, and from inside the frame the two look the same."* What is owed is a lineage and a discriminator, not a name.

Essay 6's provenance cases make the same point from materials: a video frame and a photograph can be indistinguishable as artefacts, *"and nothing in the image records which"*; lab-grown and mined diamonds became distinguishable only when isotope ratios gave an instrument a trace. Some losses are recoverable, some are not, and you cannot tell which from the reading.

## 5. The specific shape for this question

Binding the sought-for shape as an ordinary term before the run (formula "Direction"):

> Any answer must show (a) that inherited and new material are composed without deletion, (b) that the composition survives transmission as a method rather than a replay, and (c) that an anchor discriminates combination from habitat substitution. It must keep separate the verdict about the community, the verdict about the institution, and the verdict about the researcher.

The essays supply the vocabulary for each clause:

- (a) Essay 8 / formula: *"NOTHING IS EVER DELETED, and the reach falls because something was ADDED."* Essay 4: *"The record is never deleted."*
- (b) Essay 4: *"The group shares methods, never recordings."* Essay 3: *"what passes down is ways of doing things and head starts, never the experience itself."*
- (c) Essay 4: *"a group is only worth having while its members stay different"*; essay 3: agreement about shared meaning is the point of a convention and worthless as a check when both sides learned from the same sources.
- Separation of verdicts: essay 4, *"the ground sits under all three: no store's agreement replaces it"*; essay 8, *"natural selection is what the loop looks like when there is no one maintaining the ground."*

## 6. Two consequences for outreach

**Prestige is not action.** Essay 2: *"Prestige must never be allowed to validate an action directly"* and *"Prestige can fund an action. It can give an action a budget to be tried. But it cannot settle an action. Only the ground can settle it."* A list of respected cultural theorists selected for likely agreement is the named collapse, not corroboration. The legitimate ask gives the claim a budget to be tried.

**Public contact points must be ground-visible, opt-in, and institutional.** Essay 4: *"Whoever is measured by a reading cannot own it"* and rooms should be chosen by *"a lottery, everyone who qualifies, a set assembled before the question existed."* Hence `03_public_contact_points.csv` has no personal emails and no receptiveness rank.

## 7. My own audit is a simulation, not a ground reading

Essay 2: *"LLMs can quickly simulate an approximate answer/output with the formula found in the architecture post."* Essay 8: the outputs *"must be seen as simulacra"* and *"could never be verified if the solutions presented were not contrived to fit the formula."* So `01_figure_grounded_audit.md` and this file are simulated derivations checked for internal consistency against the figures. They are **not** tests of the question and must be labelled as such. They cannot settle anything they report on. No community was observed, no weighting measured, no bond identified, no institution evaluated.

## 8. Falsifier for this reading

If any fires, §5's binding must be rewritten and the refuter in `01` §4 updated before outreach.
