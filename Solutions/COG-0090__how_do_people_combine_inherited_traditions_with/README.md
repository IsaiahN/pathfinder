## The question restated as an effect

The question contrasts two outcomes: **combination** (the inherited tradition and the new information or institution both remain operative) and **replacement** (one gives way to the other). What has to be decided is: what operation produces the first outcome, what would make the second outcome genuine rather than apparent, and what ground could tell them apart. The sought-for shape is bound as an ordinary term before the run (formula step "Direction"): an answer must name (1) a composition operation over inherited and new material, (2) a persistence mechanism across generations, and (3) an anchor that discriminates combination from replacement. That binding is stated in `01_figure_grounded_audit.md` §0 and is allowed to accrue a residual.

## What is deliberately not here

- No personal emails of named individuals. Every contact route is a public institutional channel or an opt-in form.
- No ranking of researchers by how likely they are to agree with this derivation. Seat Map :163: *"High correlation is disqualifying for a verdict and efficient for a convention."* Selecting for receptiveness manufactures exactly the disqualifying correlation.
- No bulk-send plan. Fig 2 :35: *"Alignment is triangulation, not negotiation. A mirror cannot correct you."*
- No claim that the figures settle which tradition or which institution a community should keep. The framework returns a shape, not a name.

Legally: public opt-in and institutional submission routes avoid the GDPR/CAN-SPAM failure that scraped personal addresses for unsolicited outreach would create. That is a practical failure on top of the structural one.
