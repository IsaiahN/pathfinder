## What the wider literature contributes, and its limits

These are **secondary corroboration only**. The figures decide; a world source is a witness with its own evidence pool. Overlap is declared in `10_source_ledger.csv`.

- **Cultural evolution (social science).** The Cultural Evolution Society's definition of culture as socially learned information and its statement that humans are the joint product of genetic and cultural inheritances corroborate F2's mixing shape. Its remark that cultural traits are transformed in transit corroborates Fig 4's lossy membrane. Its remark that cultural innovation is often directed toward goals corroborates the bond reading in F5 (a goal is a comparison, Fig 12 :52).
- **Human cognition and tacit knowledge.** The tacit-knowledge essay's apprentice case corroborates Fig 4 :22-25 (methods down, not recordings) and Fig 2 :40-41 (two valences of agreement). It also supplies the drift warning: two people who only check against each other drift together and do not notice.
- **Animal behaviour.** Whiten's chimpanzee diffusion experiments and the animal-culture record corroborate that traditions can be transmitted with substantial fidelity without a human institution, and that prestige and conformity biases are not uniquely human. This is genuinely low overlap with the framework's human-cognition sources.
- **Institutional research.** Historical institutionalism's layering and conversion map onto additive combination; displacement maps onto Fig 11's habitat substitution; drift maps onto what the new habitat failed to reproduce. This is the lowest-overlap field in the set and it independently names the same four-way split.
- **Engineering.** Legacy-system practice (backward compatibility, brownfield deployment, heritage verification history) corroborates that keeping an older component while adding a new one is an engineering discipline, not a failure of nerve. It also prices replacement: flight certification or business-logic rewrite is expensive, which is the Fig 12 two-currency point in another vocabulary.
- **Heritage policy.** UNESCO's community-recognition criterion supplies a nameable anchor for Domain 2. It does not supply a scientific verdict: inscription is a state-mediated process, and this package treats it as an anchor candidate only.

**Evidence-pool warning.** Four of the twelve web sources are essays by the framework's author. Their agreement with the figures is expected and carries little independent weight. The seven independent-field sources do not all agree on mechanism either: cultural-evolution research debates selection versus attraction, and historical institutionalism is explicitly not a unified enterprise. Those disagreements are recorded, not averaged away.

## Distribution rules

Figure-grounded reasons for offering rather than pushing:

- **Fig 4 :22-25**: *"Only methods cross up. Only head starts come down. A recording carried upward looks like knowledge and is a description of one occasion."* Sending the full framework before anyone has described a residual sends a recording. Lead with shape and refuter.
- **Fig 10 :42**: *"A verifier that can reconstruct the claim cannot verify it."* A recipient who has absorbed the architecture before being asked for a verdict has joined the frame being checked.
- **Fig 9 :27-28**: *"describe it before you go looking, or any frame you pick will seem to fit"*. Ask what they would expect to see if the shape claim were true before giving them any source.
- **Seat Map :163**: *"High correlation is disqualifying for a verdict and efficient for a convention."* A send list selected for likely agreement produces a convention, not corroboration.
- **Fig 2 :40-41**: the two valences. Once a researcher has agreed with the framework, their agreement about the world is worthless; inside a tradition, shared meaning is the product.

**Recommended order in a reply:** (1) the shape claim and its pre-registered refuter; (2) ask what they would count as a failure in their own field, and record the answer; (3) only if curious, the three links, with the note that the figures, not this summary, are the authority.

## The two-stage offer

1. **Stage 1.** Send the residual in effect terms and the refuter. Ask for a failure description. Record the verdict with date and source before showing theory. Fig 10 :52: *"Bring a characterised residual, never the source."*
2. **Stage 2, opt-in only.** If they are curious, offer the three links: the figures text extract (local canonical path), the Cultural Evolution Society's "What is cultural evolution?" page, and UNESCO's definition page. Their later agreement is never pooled as corroboration (Fig 2 :40).
3. **Stage 3, method reuse.** Offer the formula as a generator. Each user writes their own refuter and states their own mode. Fig 4 :22 permits giving away a method; Formula "Direction" requires the user to bind their own sought-for shape.

**Hard precondition:** stages 1 to 3 only work if the stage-1 shape survives. This package has not been checked against a ground. That is stated in the packet and in the email.
