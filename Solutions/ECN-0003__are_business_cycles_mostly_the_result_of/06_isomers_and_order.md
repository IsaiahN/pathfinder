## DERIVATION

### Q1. Are "shock" and "feedback" isomers, or different structures?

- Fig 12 :117: *"isomer. Same atoms, different bond, different substance. The difference is invisible in the formula and decisive in the behaviour."*
- Fig 12 :121: *"cycle. The output re-enters as input. The sign of the loop decides whether it closes, and a chain cannot close by construction."*
- Fig 6 :14: *"the only operator that moves the wall"* (IMPORT).

A cycle and an import are **not isomers**. Isomers share atoms and differ in bond. Here one mechanism adds an atom to the closure and the other composes with the atoms already held. They differ in what they do to the reach, not merely in arrangement. Fig 6 :21: *"MINT composes inside the closure and can never add an atom."* Import is the only operator that adds one.

What *would* be isomeric: two sectors with the same atoms and the same sign structure but different bond placement, so that one damps and the other amplifies. That is a checkable structural claim, not the question asked.

- **Falsifier, written before the run:** if an import can be expressed as a rearrangement of atoms already in Γ with no change to the wall, the distinction collapses and the union reading fails.

### Q2. What order do the assessments run in?

- Fig 3 :18: *"The break is drawn between three and four as an illustration. Where a given chain stops is a reading, never a property of the chain."*
- Fig 3 :19: *"Optimising a link that already works cannot rescue one that does not."*
- Fig 3 :27: *"A link with no instrument is not a link that works. It is a link nobody has looked at."*

Dependency order: (1) read the per-slot residual (D4); (2) acquire terms for what a slot can do (D5 vocabulary); (3) say what would count as progress, here a shrinking per-slot residual; (4) plan the test; (5) name what worked and carry it. A variance decomposition computed before step 1 is effort above the break: it cannot rescue a missing instrument.

Shock classification (Formula step 2, the "new" bin) presupposes step 1. Feedback classification (Fig 12's cycle) presupposes that the same slot has been observed over time. Neither can be run on an aggregate first.

- **Falsifier:** a documented case where a shock attribution closes the residual without any per-slot observation.

### Q3. What analogue carries the mechanism, and where does it stop?

- **Physics and complexity, low overlap.** The fetched Green Tea Press chapter on self-organised criticality states that in the sand-pile model "the proximate cause of an avalanche is a grain of sand, but the grain that causes a large avalanche is identical to any other grain, so it offers no special explanation" and that "the ultimate cause of a large avalanche is the structure and dynamics of the systems as a whole." This is a low-overlap witness for treating an "unexpected shock" as a grain and the feedback structure as the thing that makes large events possible. It corroborates F5 of `01`; it does not prove the economy is critical. Fig 9 :30 warns that "your words are borrowed too," so the analogy is carried in effect terms only.
- **Ecology, low overlap.** Scheffer et al. 2009 review early-warning signals for critical transitions in systems ranging from ecosystems to financial markets. The shape transfers: measure the state of the system (slowing recovery, rising variance) rather than tally external events. The limit is also stated: predicting critical points before they are reached is extremely difficult. The source is a corroborator for the per-slot instrument requirement, not a source of that instrument.

- **Falsifier for the analogues:** a demonstration that the economy's fluctuation spectrum is not scale-free and that no critical-slowing signal precedes downturns would remove both analogues without touching the figure census.

### Q4. Round-trip gap R_T

Fig 4 :30: *"So take the round trip and measure the gap. Without it you are navigating by dead reckoning: confident about a position nobody checked."*

1. **Sent up (x):** the figure set, chiefly Fig 1 :19-22 (per-slot residual), Fig 6 :14 and :21 (import versus mint), Fig 9 :55-57 (union), Fig 12 :121 (cycle) and Fig 13 :115 (closed set, inexhaustible arrangements).
2. **Abstraction (T_A):** the shorthand used in `07_the_tether_answer.md`, namely "union of two mechanisms, per-slot, neither decides the proportion."
3. **Concretisation (T_E):** a concrete empirical claim such as "technology shocks explain most of the variance of US output."

**R_T is nonzero** and is recorded qualitatively, because no ground reading exists here. What the shorthand holds and the concrete claim drops: the per-slot indexing (collapsed into a national series), the source gate and shadow test for an import (replaced by a label), the sign of the cycle (assumed rather than measured), and the union's intersection (a case in two conditions treated as a harder case of one). What survives cleanly: both mechanisms are legal and a closed model can oscillate. Treating "the formula is the condensed version of the figures" as identity would set R_T to zero and be the dead-reckoning error Fig 4 names.

- **Falsifier:** a concrete attribution that retains all four dropped constraints and is still falsified at the ground would show the gap is not merely lossy but wrong.

## CONCLUSION

"Shock" and "feedback" are not isomers; they are an import and a cycle, different structures with different effects on the reach. They must be assessed in dependency order after a per-slot residual exists. Low-overlap analogues from physics and ecology corroborate the shape of the mechanism distinction but do not supply the missing instrument, and the condensation of the figures into a model carries a measured, nonzero R_T.
