## 0. The claim under audit

The source question offers two options. Restated in effect terms:

> Let R(s,t) = |Γ(b,a)(s,t) - o'(s,t)| be the prediction residual of a macro forecaster's library Γ for slot s (a sector, a firm, a region, a household cohort) at time t. Option A: the recurring part of R is driven by o' jumping relative to Γ, that is by unexpected shocks (imports into the forecaster's closure). Option B: the recurring part of R is driven by Γ's own composition, that is by ordinary decisions and feedback loops (arrangements inside a closed set of atoms).

What each option leads to: A implies the wall between what the model can reach and what it cannot moves only when something enters from a frame whose closure differs; the practical consequence is shock absorption and better nowcasts. B implies the arrangements over a closed atom set never run out, so cycles can be generated without new primitives; the practical consequence is targeting the feedback itself.

**Direction discipline (Formula, "Direction").** The sought-for shape is bound as an ordinary term before the run: an answer would be a partition of slot-indexed residual into import-driven and composition-driven parts, with a ground test that separates the two and a per-slot verdict that is never averaged. If no figure line yields that partition, the honest output is `gap`, not a forced answer.

## 2. Domain census (before any figure line is used to conclude)

**R by arity, symmetry, scale.** Arity: two mechanisms (import, composition) in one residual R(s,t). Symmetry: the question asks which side is larger, an ordered comparison ⋛, "a threshold relation between two quantities" (Operators table :48-49), not a symmetric conjunction. Scale: single decision loop, sectoral cascade, aggregate cycle, and the time channel; the membrane between scales is lossy (Fig 4). R is a slice: what cannot be perceived yet is residual and cannot be read (Symbols table).

| domain | actors | currency | anchor | status |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| D1 Decision loop | households, firms, their b, a and Γ | residual reduction per slot | realized payment and outcome | admitted |
| D2 Coordination habitat | firms, households, banks, central bank, regulators | prices, quantities, credit, inventories | the market, named as an anchor in Fig 2 :29 | admitted |
| D3 Import / shock frame | exogenous events and policy changes | new atoms entering closure | a frame whose closure differs and does not update on our reading (Fig 6 :23) | admitted |
| D4 Measurement channel | statistical agencies, dating committees, data vintages | percepts, revisions, pooled readings | the ground, which does not decay (Fig 10 :24) | admitted |
| D5 Model / closure frame | modellers and schools | compositions inside closure(Γ), |φ| | prediction residual at the ground | admitted |
| X1 Representative agent as the whole economy | one optimiser | talent or representative preference | no nameable anchor at the agent-alone scale | not a domain yet (Fig 11 :39) |
| X2 "GDP growth is the cause" | an aggregate statistic | a percentage | absorbed into D4 as a percept | not a cause domain |

**Partition or union.** Union, not partition. D1 and D3 are a union compound per slot: a cycle can be both imported and composed, and a single lever that removes one leaves the intersection standing (Fig 9 :55-57). D2 and D5 form a second compound (coordination and the model that predicts it). D4 is the channel above both; it is not a cause but the condition for reading a cause. X1 is excluded because there is no nameable anchor for an agent alone: "capability is a property of agent-and-habitat, never of the agent alone" (Fig 11 :39).

**Order by dependency (Fig 3).** Perception (D4) gives way to vocabulary (D5), then objective (D1), then planning (D2), then learn and carry. A verdict stated before D4 is read is "a reading of nothing" (Fig 3 :23). Shock classification (Formula step 2, "new: extend perception, aim the probe here") presupposes that the residual has been read per slot.

**Strain test and re-census, recorded honestly.**

1. Initial reading: "import moves the wall" (Fig 6) was about to be applied to ordinary decisions. It strains, because ordinary decisions are compositions inside closure, not imports. Re-census: Fig 6 governs D3 only. Fig 8 :32 and Fig 13 :115 govern D1 and D5. No figure was softened.
2. Initial reading: "closure is idempotent" (Fig 8 :32) was about to be read as "endogenous cycles are impossible." It strains. Re-census: the set is closed but "The arrangements are not. That gap is why import exists" (Fig 13 :115); arrangements over a closed set are inexhaustible, so composition can generate cycles without adding an atom. The claim was withdrawn at finding stage and is recorded here.
3. Initial reading: the NBER dating chronology was a verdict. It strains. Re-census: dating is a retrospective percept in D4; the anchor is the economy, not the committee's announcement.

## 3. Findings F1 to F9

Each finding states its mode, quotes verbatim before concluding, names a refuter, and records triangulation status. Quotes are cited against `FRAMEWORK/reference/03-FIGURES_TEXT.md`.

### F1. The binary question is a bundled union, not one hard question (decisive)

**Mode: `general` (structural). Status: triangulated (Fig 9 split rule and Fig 8 union surplus, low overlap).**

Fig 9 :4-5: *"are not one hard question but several well-formed ones. Split it rather than search."*

Fig 9 :55: *"A bundled problem is usually a union of overlapping conditions, not a list of separate ones."*

Fig 9 :57: *"What remains is smaller and it is also categorically different: a case in two conditions is not a harder case of either."*

Fig 8 :45: *"Two frames together reach what neither reaches alone."*

The question asks which of two mechanisms is "mostly" responsible. Fig 9 says that a disagreement which does not shrink with effort, with each rule working well on a different subset, is several well-formed questions. A recession that begins with an oil price jump and propagates through credit and inventory decisions is a case in two conditions, not a harder case of either. Split it per slot and per episode before asking for a proportion.

**Refuter:** one ground test separates the two contributions in every slot and episode with a stable ordering, so the union collapses to a partition. Until that test exists, the binary stays unbundled.

### F2. Both mechanisms are figure-licensed, so the figures do not decide the proportion

**Mode: `general`. Status: triangulated (Fig 6, Fig 8, Fig 13, distinct roles).**

Fig 6 :14: *"the only operator that moves the wall"* (said of IMPORT).

Fig 8 :32: *"Closure is idempotent. Composing inside a closed set never leaves it, so no search, however long, adds an atom. This one is definitional: it is what closure means."*

Fig 13 :115: *"The set is closed. The arrangements are not. That gap is why import exists."*

Fig 6 :21: *"MINT composes inside the closure and can never add an atom."*

Shocks are imports: they add a primitive to the forecaster's closure. Ordinary decisions are compositions: they rearrange a closed atom set, and the arrangements never run out. Both are legal. The figures say what each mechanism is and what each can do; they do not say which occupies a majority of variance in a given decade. Fig 9 :32: *"you get a kind of thing, never the answer itself."*

**Refuter:** show that either composition within closure or import is impossible in an observed cycle. Failing that, the verdict on "mostly" is `gap`.

### F3. Aggregating across slots destroys the signal the question needs

**Mode: `general`, with `specified` application to macro aggregates. Status: triangulated (Fig 1 and Fig 10, distinct quantities).**

Fig 1 :19: *"The residual is indexed by object slot, not aggregated."*

Fig 1 :22: *"Aggregating across slots is how a live signal disappears into an average."*

Fig 1 :20-21: *"a global residual near zero with one live slot is a legal state and not an inert one."*

Fig 10 :26: *"saturated metrics, pooled readings"*

A single GDP series is a pooled reading. It can be nearly on trend while one sector's residual is live, exactly the legal state Fig 1 :20 names. Any claim of the form "cycles are mostly X" that is estimated only on an aggregate is measuring the average, not the slot. This finding governs the method, not the economics verdict.

**Refuter:** a documented case where every slot's residual is proportional to the aggregate residual, so no live slot is hidden by averaging.

### F4. "Unexpected" is not a source gate; a shock owes provenance and a shadow test

**Mode: `specified` on the shock frame. Status: triangulated (Fig 6 and Fig 8, distinct functions).**

Fig 6 :23: *"No frame escapes its own closure. The bound moves only on contact with a frame whose closure differs."*

Fig 8 :51-52: *"Convergent derivation and adopted import look identical in the contents. Only the record of where each came from separates them"*

Fig 5 :12: *"new: extend perception, aim the probe here"*

An "unexpected shock" is the residual's new bin: something happened that Γ did not predict. Calling it unexpected does not establish that it was an import. It may be a novel observation of an arrangement the closure already held. Fig 6 :12-13 names the gates: source gate (provenance) and shadow test (explains a gap). Without both, a named shock is a story, not a witness.

**Refuter:** a named event with a traceable source and a shadow test that explains a per-slot residual and whose removal changes the model's prediction at the ground.

### F5. Feedback loops are cycles, and the sign of the loop decides closure

**Mode: `general`. Status: triangulated (Fig 12 and Fig 13, distinct roles).**

Fig 12 :121: *"cycle. The output re-enters as input. The sign of the loop decides whether it closes, and a chain cannot close by construction."*

Fig 13 :31: *"And because each turn leaves a remainder (the residual) that re-enters the next"*

Ordinary decisions and feedback loops are not a vague alternative to shocks. They are Fig 12's cycle: output re-enters as input, and whether the loop damps or amplifies is a sign, not an atom. The residual of one turn is what the next turn composes against. This is why a closed model can oscillate.

**Refuter:** an economy with no re-entrant output whose cycles match the observed per-slot record without importing anything.

### F6. The ground is the data, but the channel decays and the dating is retrospective

**Mode: `general` with `specified` application to business cycle measurement. Status: triangulated (Fig 2 and Fig 10).**

Fig 2 :29: *"An anchor is whatever settles the question and does not change its answer because of what any frame thinks."*

Fig 10 :24: *"the ground does not decay, the channel does"*

Fig 3 :27: *"A link with no instrument is not a link that works. It is a link nobody has looked at."*

The economy is the anchor; the statistical agency is a channel. Data revisions, pooling and saturation are channel failures, not changes in the ground. The NBER dating committee states that its approach is retrospective and that there is no fixed rule about which measures contribute or how they are weighted (opened 2026-11-22). That is a percept with stated limitations, not an oracle.

**Refuter:** a measurement of per-slot residuals that is invariant across vintages and that resolves the two mechanisms without a dating judgement.

### F7. Agreement between schools is one evidence pool, not corroboration

**Mode: `general`. Status: triangulated (Fig 2 and Fig 7).**

Fig 2 :40: *"disqualifying for a verdict: agreement about the world is worthless when the agreeing frames share an evidence pool"*

Fig 7 :13: *"agreement compounds, evidence does not"*

Fig 2 :31: *"Independence, between two frames, is how little they draw on the same evidence."*

The shocks literature and the endogenous-cycle literature frequently cite the same postwar series, the same crises and the same reductionist modelling tradition. Their disagreement does not shrink with effort because each rule works on a different subset (F1). A head count of adherents on either side is a room, not an anchor (Seat Map :283).

**Refuter:** two schools with disjoint data, instruments and closures that converge on the same per-slot attribution.

### F8. Variance shares are filters for the budget, never witnesses for the verdict

**Mode: `general`. Status: single-source (Fig 9).**

Fig 9 :60: *"Use filters for the budget and witnesses for the verdict. Never let a filter hand you a verdict."*

A statement such as "shocks explain 70 percent of output variance" is a filter: it decides which research gets funded and which instrument is built. It cannot by itself close a slot residual. The only witness is a ground test that separates import from composition per slot.

**Refuter:** a variance share that, without further measurement, closes R(s,t) in every slot and episode.

### F9. What survives: a shape and a route, not a "mostly"

**Mode: `general`. Status: triangulated (Fig 9, Fig 13, Fig 8, distinct functions).**

Fig 13 :134: *"Unreachable is a verdict here, not a silence"*

Fig 8 :39: *"So a search that finds nothing is never a proof of absence."*

Fig 13 :135: *"It cannot say which primitive is missing, because naming it would mean already holding it."*

The figure-derived shape is: the question is a union, the residual is per slot, both mechanisms are legal, and the missing thing is a source-discriminating instrument, characterized in effect terms but not held. The binary "mostly A or mostly B" is not answered. That is a `gap` verdict, stated plainly.

**Refuter:** production of the instrument, or a proof from the figures that one mechanism is the only composable one.

## 4. Pre-registered refuter (written before the conclusion)

Per Seat Map :287: "And a falsifier written before the run is a design instrument, not only an honesty device."

The shape claim (union, per-slot indexing, both mechanisms legal, no global proportion decided) is refuted if any of the following holds:

1. One ground test separates import-driven from composition-driven slot residuals in every episode, and one side holds a stable majority across all slots under the same threshold.
2. Per-slot residuals are proportional to the aggregate residual, so averaging conceals no live slot.
3. An import with a named source and a passing shadow test is shown to be the sole driver of a cycle, or a closed system with no imports reproduces the entire per-slot record with no re-entrant residual.
4. Two frames with genuinely disjoint evidence pools converge on the same per-slot attribution.

If any fires, the shape claim is withdrawn. Until one is run, nothing here is labelled `grounded`.

## 5. Recommendation

1. Do not answer "mostly shocks" or "mostly feedback" from an aggregate series. Split the question per slot and per episode (F1, F3).
2. State a shock's source gate and shadow test before counting it as an import (F4).
3. Treat feedback as a typed cycle whose sign must be measured, not asserted (F5).
4. Treat variance shares as budget filters and keep them out of the verdict field (F8).
5. Send the refuter before the theory. Fig 10 :52: *"Bring a characterised residual, never the source."*
