# The Tether's answer: shocks versus ordinary decisions in business cycles

**Mode: `specified` on the domain applications, `general` on the structural shape. Not `grounded`.** Simulated derivation checked for internal consistency against the figures, not a ground reading.

## 1. The residual it answers

R(s,t) = |Γ(b,a)(s,t) - o'(s,t)| is the per-slot prediction error of a macro forecaster. The question asks whether the recurrence of R comes from o' jumping (shocks) or from Γ's own composition (ordinary decisions and feedback loops). This is a **union compound**, not one hard problem: a case in two conditions is not a harder case of either (Fig 9 :57). It has its own loop and its own ground.

## 2. The shape

1. **Split before you decide.** Fig 9 :55: *"A bundled problem is usually a union of overlapping conditions, not a list of separate ones."* Fig 9 :5: *"are not one hard question but several well-formed ones. Split it rather than search."* The binary is a bundle; split it per slot and per episode.
2. **The residual is per slot, never an aggregate.** Fig 1 :19: *"The residual is indexed by object slot, not aggregated."* Fig 1 :22: *"Aggregating across slots is how a live signal disappears into an average."* A national variance share is a filter for the budget, not a witness for the verdict (Fig 9 :60).
3. **Both mechanisms are legal.** An unexpected shock is an import: Fig 6 :14, *"the only operator that moves the wall"*. Ordinary decisions are compositions: Fig 8 :32, *"Closure is idempotent. Composing inside a closed set never leaves it, so no search, however long, adds an atom. This one is definitional: it is what closure means."* Fig 13 :115, *"The set is closed. The arrangements are not. That gap is why import exists."* A closed model can oscillate without new primitives.
4. **Feedback is a typed cycle, not a slogan.** Fig 12 :121: *"cycle. The output re-enters as input. The sign of the loop decides whether it closes, and a chain cannot close by construction."* The sign must be measured per slot; a chain cannot close by construction.
5. **A shock owes provenance and a shadow test.** Fig 6 :23: *"No frame escapes its own closure. The bound moves only on contact with a frame whose closure differs."* Calling something "unexpected" does not make it an import. Fig 8 :51-52: *"Convergent derivation and adopted import look identical in the contents. Only the record of where each came from separates them"*.
6. **The measurement channel decays; the ground does not.** Fig 10 :24: *"the ground does not decay, the channel does"*. The NBER chronology is a retrospective percept, not an oracle.
7. **Agreement between schools is one pool.** Fig 2 :35: *"Alignment is triangulation, not negotiation. A mirror cannot correct you."* Fig 2 :40: *"disqualifying for a verdict: agreement about the world is worthless when the agreeing frames share an evidence pool"*.

## 3. What it explicitly does not say

- Not "shocks win" and not "feedback wins." The figures license both and select neither.
- Not a percentage. A variance share is a filter (Fig 9 :60), never the verdict.
- Not that an "unexpected shock" has been shown to be an import. No source gate or shadow test was supplied in this run.
- Not that a particular recession had one cause. That would name a slot's history from an aggregate reading.
- Not `grounded`. No per-slot measurement was taken or reproduced here.

## 4. What would settle it, and what is owed

The missing thing is a **source-discriminating instrument**, characterized in effect terms but not held:

- An instrument that, per sector and per episode, separates an observation the forecaster's closure could not have generated from one it could have generated by rearrangement. Fig 9 :32: *"you get a kind of thing, never the answer itself."*
- A measured sign for the re-entrant loop: does the feedback damp or amplify in that slot?
- A source gate and shadow test for each named shock: where did the atom come from, and what gap does it explain that composition cannot?
- A vintage-invariant reading, so the result does not move with data revisions (Fig 10 :26, *"saturated metrics, pooled readings"*).

The four pre-registered falsifiers in `01_figure_grounded_audit.md` §4 are the conditions under which the shape itself is withdrawn. None has fired.

## 5. The honest stop

**Unreached, not unreachable.** Fig 8 :39: *"So a search that finds nothing is never a proof of absence."* Fig 13 :134: *"Unreachable is a verdict here, not a silence"*. The literature shows that both mechanisms can generate cycles; it does not supply the instrument that would attribute an actual per-slot residual. The answer stops where the instruments stop and records the lineage so the stop is checkable. Indiscernibility is not identity.

## 6. One-paragraph version for outreach

> The Tether does not pick a side in the shocks-versus-feedback debate, and it does not claim a percentage. It says the question is a bundle that must be split per sector and per episode; that the prediction residual is indexed by slot, so a national average hides the live signal; that both a shock (an import into the forecaster's closure) and a feedback loop (output re-entering as input) are legal mechanisms, so neither is excluded by the framework; that a named shock owes provenance and a shadow test before it counts as an import; and that what is missing is a source-discriminating instrument, not another school of thought. The graph offers the shape any checkable answer must have, the four falsifiers written in advance, and a description of the instrument that would close it. The "mostly" stays open until the ground settles it.
