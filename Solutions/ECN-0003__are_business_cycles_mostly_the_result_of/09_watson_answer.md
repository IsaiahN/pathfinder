## QUESTION

Are business cycles mostly the result of unexpected shocks, or do they emerge from ordinary economic decisions and feedback loops? Restated in effect terms: R(s,t) = |Γ(b,a)(s,t) - o'(s,t)| is the per-slot prediction error of a macro forecaster; does its recurrence come from o' jumping relative to Γ (import) or from Γ's own composition (cycles)? Option A leads to shock absorption and nowcasting as the practical programme. Option B leads to targeting the feedback structure. The sought-for shape is bound as an ordinary term: a partition of slot-indexed residual into import-driven and composition-driven parts with a ground test that separates them. No such figure-derived partition is available, so the reported verdict for "mostly" is `gap`.

## VERDICT (from the figures)

**The figure-derived verdict:** the question as posed cannot be decided from the figures, and that is itself the result. It is a union compound whose residual is indexed per slot. An unexpected shock is an import and a feedback loop is a cycle; both are legal, the first because only import moves the wall of a forecaster's closure, the second because the arrangements over a closed atom set never run out and output re-enters as input. The figures select neither and supply no proportion, so the "mostly" claim is `gap`, not a silence and not a disguised average. The answerable residue is the shape plus four pre-registered falsifiers plus an effect-term description of the missing source-discriminating instrument. All substantive applications are `specified`; the structural claims are `general`; nothing is `grounded`. This simulated derivation cannot settle the economics question it reports on.

- **cost if wrong:** bounded and locatable. The packet states its mode, refuter and lineage before the conclusion, so an error is findable rather than invisible. The real cost is spending an outside reader's independence by sending an aggregate variance share instead of a characterised per-slot residual; Fig 2 :42 says "Buy independence in proportion to the cost of being wrong." That cost is avoidable by leading with the shape claim and the refuter. A second cost is practical: a wrong attribution sends policy toward shock absorption when the failing link is a damped or amplifying feedback, and a single lever applied to a union leaves the intersection standing.
- **undoable:** yes. No term is deleted if the claim fails; a refutation is added and the reach falls because something was added rather than because anything was struck out. The four falsifiers and the lineage are recorded in `01_figure_grounded_audit.md` and `11_audit.md`, so a later reader can locate the error. A withdrawn "mostly" claim costs no primitive and no instrument.
- **reversed by:** a ground reading, not agreement. Specifically: one per-slot ground test that gives a stable majority to one mechanism across all slots and episodes; per-slot residuals proportional to the aggregate; a closed system with no imports that reproduces the full per-slot record; a single sourced import with a passing shadow test that closes the residual; two frames with genuinely disjoint evidence pools converging on the same attribution; or a figure re-census that shows one mechanism is the only composable one. Until one of these fires, the verdict stands as `candidate` and the proportion stands as `gap`.
