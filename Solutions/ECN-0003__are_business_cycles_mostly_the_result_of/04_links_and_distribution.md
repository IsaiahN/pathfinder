## What in the wider literature bears on this question

1. **Two live research programmes, not one settled dispute.** Kydland and Prescott show that technology shocks plus time-to-build reproduce key comovements; Grandmont and Doshchyn and Giommetti show that deterministic and learning-based mechanisms can generate cycles with no exogenous shocks in the model. These are existence results about mechanisms, not measurements of the share of each in actual cycles. Fig 9 :60 makes that distinction load-bearing: use a share for the budget, not the verdict.
2. **The sand-pile result erodes the sharpness of the dichotomy.** In the fetched Green Tea Press chapter, the grain that triggers a large avalanche is identical to any other grain, so the proximate cause offers no special explanation; the ultimate cause is the structure of the system. Mapped to business cycles, an "unexpected shock" can be the grain, while the figure-derived question is about the structure that makes the economy critical. This corroborates the union reading from a low-overlap field (physics and complexity), not from economics.
3. **Critical transitions add a second low-overlap witness.** Scheffer et al. review tipping points in ecosystems, financial markets and climate, and early-warning signals based on slowing recovery and rising variance. That supports measuring the state of the system per slot rather than counting external events, but the same review warns that prediction before a critical point is extremely difficult. It corroborates the per-slot instrument requirement; it does not supply the instrument.
4. **Measurement is retrospective and pooled.** The NBER page states that dating waits for sufficient data to avoid major revisions and that depth, diffusion and duration are treated as interchangeable. This is the channel-decay finding in world form. It is an institutional description, not evidence for either mechanism.

No world source conflicts with the figure-derived verdict. Where a source appeared to conflict, for example Barney-style resource stability in the BUS-0001 pilot, the conflict would be returned to census; here the two economics programmes are corroborating existence witnesses for opposite mechanisms, which is exactly why the figures decide only the shape.
