## 1. A shape is a constraint, not a filler

From *The Library of Babel* (opened this session): "You can cut your search short if the set of books returned is small and descriptive enough that it provides a general shape of the answer that satisfies your question."

So a shape is the narrowed form of an answer you can state before you can name it. For ECN-0003 the shape is not "shocks" or "feedback." It is:

> a slot-indexed residual from a union of two mechanisms, with a source gate for any claimed import, a signed cycle for any claimed feedback, and an instrument that would separate them at the ground.

That constrains any acceptable answer. An answer that reports a single national variance share does not satisfy the shape, because it has aggregated away the slot.

## 2. How a shape is obtained here

- *Library of Babel*: use the vocabulary already held that is closest to the thing, then recurse. "With each new term gained, you narrow the space and deepen your vocabulary." Applied here: begin with "residual per slot," then "import," then "cycle," rather than beginning with "shocks versus feedback."
- Fig 6 :30: "The description travels. Fails by being sharpened into terms only we can read. Cast it in effect terms." Fig 9 :30-31: "your words are borrowed too, so describe the effect rather than the cause." So "unexpected shock" is recast as "an observation the forecaster's library did not predict," and "feedback loop" as "output that re-enters as input."
- *Decoding Tacit Knowledge* (canonical mapping): "When contact isn't possible, the best you can send is a plain description of what you'd expect to see, because anyone can check that against the world." This is why the outreach draft asks what a failure would look like before showing the framework.

## 3. The bundle clause is part of the canonical reading

*The Library of Babel* (opened this session) contains the clause that governs this question most directly:

> "Perhaps your problem was a bundle or conflation of several problems masquerading as one? You will need to search all of those problems individually to find their answers and then combine them in a satisfactory way."

This is the essay-level counterpart of Fig 9 :55 and :57. It licenses F1 of `01_figure_grounded_audit.md`. The "mostly A or mostly B" phrasing is the conflation; the shape is the combination after searching the parts.

## 4. Why the proportion is not named

Only two things license a name, both outside the shape: the ground settling a minted term, or a provenanced import from a frame whose closure differs. Fig 8's rule is that convergent derivation and adopted import look identical in the contents and only the record separates them. The world sources in `09_watson_answer.md` are existence proofs for mechanisms, not measurements of proportions. Naming "shocks" or "feedback" as *the* cause would turn a visible modelling choice into an invisible one.

*They're Scanning Books When They Should* (canonical mapping) sharpens this: "The real import is not a new term in the existing space. It is a new dimension of the space." What is missing here is a source-discriminating instrument, which is a dimension, not a new word for a variance share.

## 5. Prestige is not action, and this derivation is a simulation

From *The Recursive Self-Improvement Trap* (opened this session):

> "Prestige must never be allowed to validate an action directly."
> "Prestige can fund an action. It can give an action a budget to be tried. But it cannot settle an action. Only the ground can settle it. The Ground Maintainer must be blind to prestige."
> "the output is only as good as the validator. The agent, or the tool, or the frame, or the theory, cannot validate itself."

A count of economists on either side of this debate is prestige, not action. It can fund a study; it cannot settle the residual. The same essay states the standing of this very document: "LLMs can quickly simulate an approximate answer/output" and "the truly testable output would need to be derived from the real architecture or working the formula yourself." Agent output is "simulacra."

**Consequence:** `01_figure_grounded_audit.md` and this file are simulated derivations checked for internal consistency against the figures. They are not a test of the economics claim, and they cannot settle it.

## 6. Correction to my own reading

An early draft of this file treated the shape-not-a-name rule as a blanket ban on naming either mechanism. That was overstated. The rule governs a *missing* primitive or instrument, exactly as the README's wording says. Shocks and cycles are not missing primitives: they are already in the vocabulary of D3 and D5 of the census. What is forbidden is declaring one of them *the* answer before the ground discriminates. The narrower reading is kept, the broader one is retracted, and both are recorded so neither is quietly lost.

## 7. Question-specific one-paragraph shape

For "shocks or feedback," the canonical reading of shape is: not a verdict between two schools and not a percentage, but the narrowed form of a checkable answer, namely a per-slot residual from a union of an import-driven and a composition-driven mechanism, with provenance and a shadow test required for the first, a signed cycle required for the second, and a stated instrument whose absence makes the word "mostly" a `gap` rather than a silence.
