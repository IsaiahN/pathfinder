# The Tether's answer on prebiotic stability and accumulation

**Mode: `specified` on the per-domain claims, `general` on the structural shape, `gap` on the literal standing list. Not `grounded`.** This is a shape and a route, not a measured inventory. Simulated derivation checked against the figures, not a ground reading (`05_what_shape_means.md` section 8).

## 1. The residual it answers

Formation is not the residual. The gap is between what prebiotic chemistry produced and what survived the habitat's degradation channels long enough to become an operand for the next composition. It is indexed per molecular species, never pooled into "the organic inventory" (Fig 1 :19, :22). It is a union of overlapping conditions, not a list (Fig 9 :55, :57): hydrolysis resistance, UV resistance, thermal survival, and a concentrating process must all bind in the same place at the same time. Verdicts are kept per species and per habitat and are never averaged.

## 2. The shape

1. **Stability is not intrinsic.** It is a relation between a bonded arrangement and a habitat. Fig 11 :49: *"An actor's valence is relative to the residual, not a property it carries."* Fig 11 :39: *"capability is a property of agent-and-habitat, never of the agent alone."* A species that persists at one pH and temperature is destroyed at another. A claim without a condition set is unbracketed.
2. **The bond carries the difference.** Fig 12 :5: *"A set of ingredients is not one. The bond is the rest of the information, and it is the half nothing was recording."* Fig 12 :117: *"isomer. Same atoms, different bond, different substance."* A formula is an ingredient list; the arrangement is the substance (Fig 12 :94).
3. **The structures a network keeps are regenerated or protected.** Fig 12 :119 (catalyst), :121 (cycle), :123 (functional group). A species that survives its own use, re-enters as input, or is fixed by a substructure does not pay its destruction cost on every pass. The autocatalytic formose system and borate-stabilized ribose are world instances of that shape, not figure derivations.
4. **Settlement is the gate to accumulation.** Fig 12 :104: *"A settled molecule becomes an atom for whatever composes over it."* Once a species survives a habitat, later chemistry can compose over it. Where the record of that settlement is dropped, every lineage pays again.
5. **Persistence and concentration are two currencies.** Fig 12 :125: *"A composition costs in two currencies, and they do not add."* Accumulation is a threshold relation between a survival timescale and a concentration timescale, not a single half-life. The formose literature's note that concentrating mechanisms are required is the world-side statement of this.
6. **The figures do not return a list.** Fig 11 :51: *"No standing list of good actors survives a change of residual."* Fig 9 :32: *"you get a kind of thing, never the answer itself."* The literal answer is a `gap` of the shape-not-a-name kind, reported as unreachable at this level of description (Fig 13 :134, :135). It is a verdict, not silence and not absence (Fig 8 :39).

## 3. What the bindings look like when they are admitted

These are **not** the Tether's answer. They are chemistry-frame imports with a source gate and a shadow test, each valid only for its stated habitat, each one witness:

- Glycine and beta-alanine identified in the Miller-Urey discharge, with the later finding that nitrites can destroy amino acids unless carbonate buffering and an oxidation inhibitor are present (Miller-Urey entry).
- Adenine produced from concentrated HCN and ammonia (Oró 1961, as recorded there).
- Ribose stabilized by borate minerals (Ricardo et al. 2004, as recorded in the formose entry and the Omran et al. reference list).
- Under alkaline hydrothermal conditions, carboxylic acids rather than sugars as the equilibrium product, with under one percent of formaldehyde remaining as sugars (Omran et al. 2020).

A name earns admission only when the habitat is stated, the source is recorded, and the shadow test (what gap does it explain) is shown. Fig 8 :52: convergent derivation and adopted import look identical in the contents; only the record separates them.

## 4. What it explicitly does not say

- Not a standing list of stable chemicals. That object is structurally refused by Fig 11 :51.
- Not that stability is a property a molecule carries. That is the reading retracted in F1 of `01_figure_grounded_audit.md`.
- Not that a formula determines a fate. Isomers break that inference (Fig 12 :117).
- Not that half-life predicts abundance. The two currencies do not add (Fig 12 :125-126).
- Not that meteoritic delivery settles supply. It is an import with provenance, priced above starting from primitives if wrong (Fig 6 :24).
- Not grounded. No measurement was taken in this derivation, and no world source is treated as a second independent witness merely because it repeats a review's consensus.

## 5. What would settle it, the ground

A source-discriminating instrument, not a further argument. From *The Hard Problem is About Source*: an instrument reading green *"cannot comment on the source"*, and the spectrometer, not an argument, resolved it. Here:

- Measure disappearance rate constants per species under a **stated** pH, temperature, UV flux, and mineral assemblage, with separate channels discriminated (hydrolysis versus photolysis versus adsorption). An instrument that cannot separate them demonstrates its own inadequacy.
- Measure a **concentration** trajectory under the same conditions. Does the species actually accumulate, or only survive?
- **Isomer Test:** run the pair under identical conditions. Different rate constants confirm Fig 12 :117; identical constants across all channels refute it.
- **Cycle Test:** remove the regenerating step. If the species disappears faster, the cycle is load bearing (Fig 12 :121).
- **Threshold Test:** identify the comparison ⋛ between persistence timescale and concentration timescale; a species above it accumulates, one below it does not.

Standing falsifiers from `01` section 4: habitat-invariant persistence; identical isomer profiles; accumulation with no concentration channel; a figure line naming a chemical; settlement by agreement alone.

## 6. The honest stop

**Unreached, not unreachable.** The figure set's closure cannot synthesize a Hadean species (Fig 8 :32), and a search that returns no half-life is not a proof of instability (Fig 8 :39). The chemistry frame must be imported from, with a source gate and a shadow test. The answer stops where the available instruments stop discriminating degradation channels and concentrations, and records the lineage so the stop is checkable. *"Indiscernibility is not identity"* (*How Intelligence Actually Works*). A name fitted to every current observation is still a candidate binding.

## 7. One-paragraph version for outreach

> The Tether does not hand over a list of stable prebiotic chemicals, and it does not claim the question has no answer. It says that stability is a relation between a bonded arrangement and a habitat, so the same molecule can persist in one setting and be destroyed in another; that the bond, not the ingredient list, carries the difference, so same-formula isomers can have different fates; that the species a network keeps are the ones a cycle or a catalyst regenerates or a functional group protects; that a survived species becomes an operand for the next chemistry only once it is settled against the ground; and that persistence and concentration are two currencies whose threshold, not their sum, decides accumulation. It returns a shape, a named habitat for every claim, provenanced bindings for any chemical name, and four refuters written in advance. The literal list stays a declared gap until instruments that discriminate degradation channels settle it.
