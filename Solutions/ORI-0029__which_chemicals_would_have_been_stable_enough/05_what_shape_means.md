## 1. A shape is a constraint, not a filler

The canonical README states the limit: the framework returns *"what a missing thing would have to look like (a shape, not a name)"*. Fig 9 :32: *"you get a kind of thing, never the answer itself"*.

From *The Library of Babel*: *"you can cut your search short if the set of books returned is small and descriptive enough that it provides a general shape of the answer that satisfies your question."*

So a **shape** is the narrowed form of an answer you can state before you can name it. It constrains; it does not fill. For this question:

- `a list of stable chemicals` is a name list;
- `the arrangements whose bonds survive the habitat's degradation channels and whose species a cycle or functional group keeps regenerating, with the habitat stated and a concentration channel given` is a shape.

The shape says what any admissible answer must look like. It does not say which species occupy the slots.

## 2. How a shape is obtained

- *Library of Babel*: use the vocabulary you already have that is closest to the thing, look for recurring new terms that overlap or correlate with it, recurse. *"With each new term gained, you narrow the space and deepen your vocabulary."*
- Fig 6 :30: *"The description travels. Fails by being sharpened into terms only we can read. Cast it in effect terms."* Fig 9 :30-31: *"your words are borrowed too, so describe the effect rather than the cause."*
- *Decoding Tacit Knowledge*: *"When contact isn't possible, the best you can send is a plain description of what you'd expect to see, because anyone can check that against the world."*
- *Library of Babel* on stopping: the answer is reached when *"the finish line will also be the starting place"*, and the paradox is that by then you have *"composed the answer through the process of locating it."*

For ORI-0029 the bootstrapping words were "persistence", "bond", "habitat", "cycle", "concentration". Each narrowed the question. The first pass asked for a list; by the fourth pass the residual was a relation between a bonded arrangement and a habitat, and the list had become a gap rather than a target.

## 3. What licenses a name

Only two things, both outside the shape itself:

1. **The ground settles it.** Formula step 5: *"the ground settles it. Verified only by what pays."* *The Hard Problem is About Source* is sharper: *"The answer is unreached until a better instrument exists."* A half-life measured under stated pH, temperature, and UV conditions is an instrument paying for a name.
2. **A provenanced import from a frame whose closure differs** (Fig 6 :23, Fig 8 :52), with a source gate and a shadow test. *They're Scanning Books*: *"The real import is not a new term in the existing space. It is a new dimension of the space."*

That is why every chemical name in this folder is a binding with an author, a venue, a year, and a condition set. Without those it is the searcher's own contribution and must be recorded as such.

## 4. The metamerism error applied to this question

*The Hard Problem is About Source* gives the mechanism. A color instrument reads green and *"cannot comment on the source"*: one green light, or yellow plus blue, or RGB, or CMYK, or a scheme not yet discovered. The reading does not determine the source. *"If several paths create the same behavior, then behavior is a projection with lossy information."* An instrument that cannot distinguish the cases *"demonstrates its own inadequacy"*; it was the spectrometer, not an argument, that resolved it.

The same error is live here. A measured disappearance rate for a species in a flask does not say which channel removed it: hydrolysis, photolysis, thermal decomposition, adsorption onto the vessel, or conversion to a tar. Fig 12 :97 records that silver fulminate and silver cyanate have the same composition and different properties, which is what forced structure into chemical notation. The 2021 Miller-Urey result that the glass vessel acts as a mineral catalyst is the exact practical case: a persistence reading attributed to the chemistry alone is a source named from a projection. What is owed is a source-discriminating instrument, not a name.

## 5. Stability as selection, not as intelligence

From *The Recursive Self-Improvement Trap*: *"Natural selection is what the loop looks like when there is no one maintaining the ground."* And from *How Intelligence Actually Works*: *"The intelligence we experience is the emergent result of extensive mapping of a complex system against unknown and unforgiving environments. The test that environment applies is what we call fitness."*

Early Earth had no maintainer in the Ground Maintainer seat. There was no bench selecting which molecule to keep. What accumulated is therefore what survived on contact: Fig 11 :36, *"Where nothing maintains anything, intelligence is a label applied afterwards to a lineage that met its ground. Nothing was intelligent; selection ran."* The word "stable" does no more work here than "intelligent" does. The answerable question is not "which chemicals are stable" but "which arrangements maintained contact with the ground under these conditions". This is the same move as the substrate question in the exemplar: ask what generates the persistence, not whether the molecule deserves the label.

## 6. The distinction horizon, and why the gap is honest

*How Intelligence Actually Works* names a *"distinction horizon"* for each observer and a *"union horizon"* across observers: a projection below the union horizon has no instrument, any of us can build, that discriminates its origin. It adds: *"indiscernibility is not identity. The projection that no instrument distinguishes from the thing is still a projection, which is why the lineage has to be kept even when the copy is perfect."*

For prebiotic stability this means two honest stops. First, a persistence profile that no available instrument distinguishes between two degradation channels is below the union horizon, and the correct report is "unresolved which channel", not "stable". Second, a chemical name that fits every current observation is still a candidate binding, and the lineage of how it was measured has to be kept. Fig 8 :39: *"So a search that finds nothing is never a proof of absence."* The gap in F6 of the audit is of this kind.

## 7. Outputs are not generators; prestige is not action

*They're Scanning Books*: *"The corpus contains the problems evolution already solved and someone wrote down. It does not contain the capacity to solve new ones, because that capacity lives in the living process, the mind generating, not in the text it generated."* A published list of stable chemicals is a corpus output. It cannot itself generate the persistence claim for a new habitat, which is why this folder gives the shape and the method rather than the list.

From *The Recursive Self-Improvement Trap*: *"Prestige must never be allowed to validate an action directly."* And: *"Prestige can fund an action. It can give an action a budget to be tried. But it cannot settle an action. Only the ground can settle it. The Ground Maintainer must be blind to prestige."* A well cited review that lists stable molecules gives the question a budget to be tried. It does not settle which molecule persisted in a specific habitat. Agreement among reviews that share one evidence pool is one witness (Fig 2 :40).

## 8. My own audit is a simulation, not a ground reading

*The Recursive Self-Improvement Trap* again: LLMs *"can quickly simulate an approximate answer/output with the formula found in the architecture post"*, and *"the truly testable output would need to be derived from the real architecture or working the formula yourself."* So `01_figure_grounded_audit.md`, `06_isomers_and_order.md`, `07_the_tether_answer.md`, `09_watson_answer.md` and `11_audit.md` are simulated derivations checked for internal consistency against the figures. They are **not** measurements. They cannot settle which chemicals accumulated on early Earth.

## 9. Correction to my own sections 3 and 4, kept visible

I initially overstated "shape, not a name" as a ban on naming any molecule. The canonical phrase governs a *missing* primitive, one not held in the frame. Glycine, adenine, ribose and carboxylic acid are held atoms of the chemistry frame; naming one as the occupant of a slot is a provenanced binding, not the invention of a new primitive and not an import that moves the wall (Fig 6 :26: *"instruments extend what can be represented at all"*). What remains wrong in a naive answer is the reverse error: naming a species from a persistence reading without an instrument that discriminates sources, which is the metamerism failure in section 4. Both claims are now recorded so neither is quietly lost.
