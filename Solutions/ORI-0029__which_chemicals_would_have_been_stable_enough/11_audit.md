## Independent domain census

I redid the census from the residual line alone before reading the primary's conclusions, using the WATSON order: R by arity, symmetry and scale; one line per domain with actors / currency / anchor; partition or union; order by dependency.

- **R (arity / symmetry / scale).** The residual is the gap between formation and persistence-to-concentration. Arity: a molecule is operands joined by a bond, so the residual is indexed per species (Fig 1 :19), not pooled as an inventory. Symmetry: stability is not carried by an ingredient, it is a relation between an arrangement and a habitat (Fig 11 :49). Scale: bond-level degradation and population-level concentration are different scales, with a lossy transform between them.
- **Domain 1, composition and structure.** Actors: atoms, bonds, isomers, catalysts, cycles, functional groups. Currency: which bonded arrangement persists and can be composed over. Anchor: measured persistence under stated conditions. Question type: partition between ingredient lists and arrangements.
- **Domain 2, habitat and geochemistry.** Actors: conditions, mineral surfaces, UV, pH, impacts. Currency: contact and persistence. Anchor: the actual environment, which does not update on what a frame thinks (Fig 2 :29). Question type: enumeration, not composition.
- **Domain 3, accumulation.** Actors: species, degradation channels, concentrating processes. Currency: concentration and survival. Anchor: the ground, since no maintainer sat in the seat (Fig 11 :36). Question type: selection between rooms.
- **Domain 4, verification.** Actors: instruments and chemists. Currency: ground contact, never agreement. Anchor: a measurement that cannot be moved by a frame. In this simulated run its independence is zero, because it reuses the primary's sources.
- **Union, not partition.** Hydrolysis resistance, UV resistance and concentration overlap; a case in two conditions is a specific compound (Fig 9 :57).
- **Order by dependency.** Enumerate habitat, settle the bond and arrangement, assign valence, check concentration, admit a provenanced name. Refuse before mint; describe the effect before choosing a frame (Fig 9 :27-28).

My census matches the primary's four domains and its union reading. Where the primary derived from Fig 12 and Fig 11, I derived from Fig 9's union/partition split and Fig 2's anchor rule. Different starting figures, same domain boundaries: low overlap between routes, so the agreement is weak but not circular within the framework. It is still one evidence pool.
