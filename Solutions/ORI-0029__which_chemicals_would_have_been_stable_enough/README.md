## The question restated as an effect

The question asks for a list, but the operative effect is not formation. It is persistence. A molecule that forms and is destroyed before it reaches a threshold concentration has not accumulated. The residual is the gap between what prebiotic chemistry produced and what survived the habitat's degradation channels (UV photolysis, hydrolysis, thermal decomposition, dilution, and capture into incombustible tar) long enough to become an operand for the next composition.

What an answer would look like, written down and bound as an ordinary term before the run (formula step "Direction"): **a per-species, per-habitat persistence claim, with the binding conditions named, a concentration channel stated, the chemical name provenanced as an import rather than a derivation, and a failure condition written in advance.** Binding this shape lets it accrue a residual like any other term instead of remaining an invisible preference. It is the sought-for shape, not a name list.

## What is deliberately not here

- No personal emails of named individuals.
- No ranking of people by how likely they are to agree.
- No bulk-send plan.
- No standing list of "stable chemicals" presented as if the figures produced it. That is the named failure this folder documents.

The reasons are in the figures' own words: Fig 2 :35, *"Alignment is triangulation, not negotiation. A mirror cannot correct you."*; Fig 11 :51, *"No standing list of good actors survives a change of residual."*; and Fig 10 :42, *"A verifier that can reconstruct the claim cannot verify it."* Selecting recipients for receptiveness manufactures the correlation that would make their agreement worthless (Fig 2 :40). An unsolicited bulk mail to scraped addresses is also a practical failure under GDPR and CAN-SPAM style rules.
