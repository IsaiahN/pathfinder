## Field for public contact points

Climate states and Earth system research. The venues in `03_public_contact_points.csv` are public institutional or opt-in routes in that field. No personal emails are included.

## What is deliberately not here

- No personal emails of named individuals.
- No ranking of people by how likely they are to agree with the claim.
- No bulk-send plan.

Three reasons, in the figures' own words:

1. **A room does not supply an anchor.** Fig 2 :35: *"Alignment is triangulation, not negotiation. A mirror cannot correct you."*
2. **Selecting for receptiveness manufactures the disqualifying correlation.** Fig 2 :40: *"agreement about the world is worthless when the agreeing frames share an evidence pool."* A list chosen for likely agreement is a list chosen to agree.
3. **Asking for trust before a check spends the reader's independence.** Fig 10 :42: *"A verifier that can reconstruct the claim cannot verify it."*

Legally: scraping personal emails for unsolicited bulk outreach breaches GDPR and CAN-SPAM style rules in many jurisdictions, a practical failure on top of the structural one.

## Boundary and standing

Everything in this folder is a **simulated derivation checked for internal consistency against the figures, not a ground reading.** Per *The Recursive Self-Improvement Trap*: the truly testable output "would need to be derived from the real architecture or working the formula yourself". This solution cannot settle the question it reports on. No climate measurement was taken here; the world sources were read only as secondary corroboration.
