# Expert verification packet: the Tether on formal education and three reasoning capacities

**Mode: `specified` on each per-domain claim, `general` on the structural shape, `gap` on the magnitude. Not `grounded`.** No measurement is claimed. This is a shape and a route, not a result.

## 1. The claim, in one paragraph

Access to formal education changes use of abstraction, statistics and hypothetical reasoning by changing what crosses the membrane into the learner's library and which checking instruments are available. A re-applicable method sends down a head start and extends the reach; a replay of one occasion grows the library without the reach. Abstraction changes when the abstraction/concretisation round trip is taught and its gap measured; statistical use changes when representation format and independence discipline change rather than with raw years of exposure; hypothetical reasoning changes when the bond vocabulary, especially negation and the pre-committed prediction, is transmitted. The three changes have different currencies and anchors, so they are kept separate and never averaged. Standardised assessment can sever the channel to a non-updating ground while the ground itself stays intact. The magnitude is a declared gap.

## 2. Three checkable sub-claims

### C1 - framework-internal; checkable by reading Figures 4, 5 and 6

- **Statement:** what crosses an educational membrane decides whether use changes. A method (re-applicable, checkable) extends reach; a recording (a replay of one occasion) does not.
- **Source, verbatim:** Fig 4 :22 "Only methods cross up. Only head starts come down." Fig 4 :24 "downward: something that makes the next search cheaper. Never a replay of what worked once." Fig 4 :25-26 "A recording carried upward looks like knowledge and is a description of one occasion. Replayed downward, it produces a system that repeats a past success and cannot produce a new one." Fig 5 :72 "and a library that grows is not a reach that grows." Fig 6 :23 "No frame escapes its own closure. The bound moves only on contact with a frame whose closure differs."
- **What to check:** is the method/recording distinction drawn at the right place, and does the "reach" criterion (new residuals settled) differ from the "library" criterion (more terms held)? Cross-cultural evidence is suggestive here but does not itself decide it: Fong, Krantz and Nisbett 1986 found that abstract rule training transferred to untrained domains.
- **Refuter:** show a curriculum whose transmitted content is only replays of past successes that nonetheless extends reach to a new residual at the same rate as a method-based curriculum, measured on a residual the replay set did not cover.

### C2 - statistics-facing; checkable by an education or methods researcher

- **Statement:** the statistical claim is per slot and per witness, not a pooled schooling coefficient. Aggregation and correlated agreement can delete the signal they report, and a test cannot validate the capacity it defines.
- **Source, verbatim:** Fig 1 :19 "The residual is indexed by object slot, not aggregated." Fig 1 :22 "Aggregating across slots is how a live signal disappears into an average." Fig 1 :27 "They are frame-internal. A frame cannot score itself with a quantity it also produces." Fig 2 :31 "Independence, between two frames, is how little they draw on the same evidence." Fig 2 :44 "The effective-sample-size correction for correlated observations is standard in survey statistics and meta-analysis; the collapse is the same arithmetic read as a warning." Symbols table :43 "R is measured per slot, never as a single global number."
- **What to check:** in a real study, do years of schooling predict per-slot statistical discrimination after representation format and witness independence are held fixed, against an anchor that does not update on the test? Schulze and Hertwig 2022 found 18% versus 79% correct Bayesian responses in adults and 3% versus 53% in children purely by changing format; Till and Sproesser 2020 found grade-4 students solved Bayesian tasks with natural frequencies. Fong et al. 1986 found genuine transfer from brief formal training. These agree that *something* changes; they disagree on whether it is exposure or format.
- **Refuter:** show a stable years-of-schooling coefficient on per-slot statistical reasoning with format and independence fixed and a non-updating ground. If it holds, the narrowed claim in C2 is withdrawn.

### C3 - cognitive-facing; checkable by a developmental or cognitive psychologist

- **Statement:** abstraction is universal but its round trip can be taught; hypothetical reasoning depends on bonds, especially negation and the pre-committed prediction. Neither adds a primitive.
- **Source, verbatim:** Symbols table :70 "Abstraction. Detail is thrown away, which is what makes the result reusable." Fig 4 :30 "So take the round trip and measure the gap. Without it you are navigating by dead reckoning: confident about a position nobody checked." Fig 12 :111 "A vocabulary without bonds can name a state and cannot name a route to one." Fig 12 :112 "A plan is a sequence. A goal is a comparison. Progress is a subtraction. A refutation is a negation, and that one is not a bond." Operators table :58 "a refutation is a negation, which is why a vocabulary without it can only accumulate" Fig 5 :65 "The remedy is a discipline rather than a branch: make the sought-for shape an ORDINARY TERM. Write down what an answer would look like, bind it, and let it accrue a residual like anything else."
- **What to check:** Scribner and Cole 1973 report that "All culture groups thus far studied have demonstrated the capacity to remember, generalize, form concepts, operate with abstractions, and reason logically." Kroupin et al. 2024 report that "unschooled populations have persistently failed unfamiliar and/or counterfactual-premise syllogisms." Sharp, Cole and Lave 1979 found education's strongest influence "where problems must be treated hypothetically without recourse to real-world knowledge." Do these discriminate capacity from bond vocabulary, or do they show a missing atom? The latter is the live strain named in `06` Q1.
- **Refuter:** show counterfactual-premise reasoning at the same rate in a population with no exposure to formal bond vocabulary, on the same task, with a ground independent of schooling; or show a population that measures its own abstraction/concretisation gap at the schooled rate without formal instruction.

## 3. What is explicitly not claimed

- Not that education installs three new faculties. Capacities are universal; functional systems, instruments and bond vocabularies change.
- Not that more years, more tests or more content produce the change. Those are budget filters (Fig 9 :60 "Use filters for the budget and witnesses for the verdict. Never let a filter hand you a verdict."), and more content can grow the library without the reach.
- Not a coefficient, a years-of-schooling threshold or a curriculum ranking. That is a declared `gap` (Fig 9 :32 "you get a kind of thing, never the answer itself"; Fig 13 :134 "Unreachable is a verdict here, not a silence").
- Not that a mean test-score lift is a verdict on any capacity. Its source is undecided.
- Not `grounded`. Nothing here is asserted as settled by experiment.
- Not that the counterfactual result is settled. The world studies use school-shaped tasks, and Kroupin et al. 2024 call for re-classifying much of that data rather than treating it as universal.

## 5. What would settle it

A source-discriminating instrument, not a further argument. From *The Hard Problem is About Source* (carried in `05`): an instrument that reads green "cannot comment on the source"; the spectrometer resolved it. Here:

1. **Method versus recording:** vary only what crosses the membrane; measure on a residual neither group trained on. Fires C1.
2. **Format versus exposure:** hold representation format and witness independence fixed against a non-updating anchor. Fires C2.
3. **Capacity versus bond:** counterfactual-premise tasks that discriminate "premise not held" from "premise held but not composed." Fires C3 and the live strain in `06`.
4. **Standing falsifiers from `01` section 4:** derive one of the six terms from the other five; admit a seventh primitive; show a replay extends reach as well as a method; show no non-updating assessment can be built.
