## QUESTION

Decide, in effect terms, what changes in a learner when a maintained transmission channel (formal education) sends something across into that learner's library, as registered in three named capacities: abstraction, statistics, hypothetical reasoning. Option A: schooling installs three new faculties, and the magnitude is the schooling coefficient on a standard test. Option B: schooling sends down either a re-applicable method or a replay of one occasion; each capacity changes under a different instrument and anchor; the three verdicts are kept separate; the magnitude is a declared gap. What each leads to: A returns a single number whose source is undecided and whose test cannot validate what it defines; B returns a checkable shape, five failure conditions written in advance, and the instruments that would close the gap.

## VERDICT (from the figures)

Access to formal education changes use of these three capacities by changing what crosses the membrane into the learner's library and which checking instruments are available. A re-applicable method sends down a head start and extends the reach; a replay of one occasion grows the library without the reach. Abstraction changes when the abstraction/concretisation round trip is taught and its gap measured; statistical use changes when representation format and independence discipline change rather than with raw years of exposure; hypothetical reasoning changes when the bond vocabulary, especially negation and the pre-committed prediction, is transmitted. The three changes have different currencies and anchors, so they are kept separate and never averaged. Standardised assessment can sever the channel to a non-updating ground while the ground itself stays intact. The magnitude is a declared `gap`, unreachable at this budget, not silence and not an invitation to invent a coefficient. Mode `specified` on the per-domain claims, `general` on the structural shape, nothing `grounded`.

- **cost if wrong:** bounded and locatable. The packet states its mode, five failure conditions and the union structure, so an error is findable rather than invisible. The real cost is sending a single "education improves reasoning" number that a test both defines and grades, which spends the independence of outside readers (Fig 2 :42 "Buy independence in proportion to the cost of being wrong."). That cost is avoidable by leading with the shape and its refuter.
- **undoable:** yes. The three universal-faculty readings are removable without touching the derivation; `07_the_tether_answer.md` and `08_expert_verification_packet.md` already remove them. A sent claim can be superseded by the corrected shape, and no primitive has to be withdrawn. What cannot be undone is a recipient who has already absorbed the framework before recording a verdict; that is why stage 1 precedes stage 2.
- **reversed by:** a ground reading, not agreement. A curriculum contrast that varies only method versus recording on an untrained residual would fire F2; a format and independence contrast with a non-updating anchor would settle F4; a counterfactual-premise task that discriminates "premise not held" from "premise held but not composed" would settle F5 and the live encoding strain; a derivation of one of the six terms from the other five or the admission of a seventh primitive would break the substrate. Prestige or agreement is not such an instrument.
