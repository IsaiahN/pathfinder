## Distribution: offer, do not push

- **Fig 4 :22 "Only methods cross up. Only head starts come down."** Sending the full architecture before anyone has described a residual sends a recording, not a generator. Lead with the shape claim and its refuter; offer the figure text after.
- **Fig 10 :42 "A verifier that can reconstruct the claim cannot verify it."** A recipient who has absorbed the framework before being asked for a verdict has joined the frame being checked.
- **Fig 9 :27-28 "describe it before you go looking, or any frame you pick will seem to fit"** Ask what they would expect to see if the shape claim were true before giving them the literature.
- **Fig 2 :35 "Alignment is triangulation, not negotiation. A mirror cannot correct you."** A send selected for people likely to agree produces a convention, not corroboration.
- **Fig 9 :60 "Use filters for the budget and witnesses for the verdict. Never let a filter hand you a verdict."** Editorial prestige and publication venue are budget filters here, never evidence for the claim.

**Recommended order in a reply:** (1) the shape claim and its five refuters; (2) ask what they would count as a failure and record the answer; (3) only then the figure text and the world sources, with the note that the figures, not the summary, are the authority.

## The two-stage offer (and the optional third)

1. **Stage 1:** residual, shape and pre-registered refuter. Ask for a failure condition. Record the verdict *before* any theory is shown. Fig 10 :52 "Bring a characterised residual, never the source." and Fig 2 :45 "Frames range against the ground. They do not negotiate with it."
2. **Stage 2, opt-in:** if curious, offer the three local figure files and the seven world sources. Later agreement is never pooled as corroboration (Fig 2 :40 "agreement about the world is worthless when the agreeing frames share an evidence pool").
3. **Stage 3, optional:** offer the eight-step formula as a method to reuse. This is permitted because a generator can be given away without giving away an answer (Fig 4 :22). Each user must state their own ground and write their own refuter before running it, because THE_FORMULA.md "Direction" says a frame cannot check the direction of its own question.

**Hard precondition:** stages 1 to 3 work only if the stage-1 shape survives. Here it does, with two honest limits that must travel with it: the assessment claim is single-source on the figure side, and the premise-encoding question in `06` Q1 is a live strain. Neither may be stripped for brevity.
