## Independent domain census

Arity: three, so a **union compound** (Fig 9 :54-55: a bundled problem is a union of overlapping conditions, not a list of separate ones; a single lever leaves the intersections standing). Symmetry: ordered. "Access changes use" is a directed claim, and a schooling correlation cannot choose the arrow (Fig 12 :72 "A then B; B needs A to have happened"). Scale: first order inside a learner, second order across the population membrane (Fig 4 :19-20, only actors, currency and anchor change with size).
- **Domain 1, the learner's frame.** Actors: primitives, bonds, belief b, weighting w. Currency: composition in closure(Γ) and R per slot. Anchor: the ground as only metric. Question type: partition, what is composable versus merely held.
- **Domain 2, the transmission frame.** Actors: teachers, curriculum, rubric, texts, peers. Currency: methods and head starts versus recordings and replays. Anchor: a change in contact with a ground that does not update on instruction. Boundary question: what crosses the membrane.
- **Domain 3, the statistics and evidence frame.** Actors: per-slot readings, samples, correlated witnesses, assessment instruments. Currency: evidence and independence. Anchor: a non-updating measurement. Union of per-slot signal, independence and representation format.
- **Domain 4, the hypothetical-composition frame.** Actors: seven bonds plus negation, the sought-for shape as an ordinary term. Currency: routes, goals, comparisons, refusals. Anchor: a pre-committed prediction fired or refuted. Partition of the operator vocabulary.

Order by dependency: vocabulary (Fig 3 :7), objective (Fig 3 :10), method transfer (Fig 4 :24), ground contact (Fig 2 :45), kept distinct from the within-abstraction order T_A then T_E with R_T measured between (Symbols :77).

My census matches the primary's four domains and its union reading. ## Round trip gap R_T

The Symbols table defines R_T as the gap between what was sent up and what came back down, `gap(x, (T_E ∘ T_A)(x))`, and Fig 4 :30 requires it to be measured rather than assumed zero. I ran the round trip on the condensation the folder actually uses.

1. **Sent up (x):** the verbatim figure set, chiefly Fig 4 :22-26 (method versus recording), Fig 1 :19 and Fig 2 :31 (per-slot residual and independence), Fig 12 :111-112 and Operators :58 (bonds and negation), Symbols :70/:74/:77 (abstraction round trip).
2. **Abstraction (T_A):** the shorthand carried in `01` section 9 and `07` section 2: schooling sends methods or recordings; three capacities change under three instruments; the magnitude is a gap.
3. **Concretisation (T_E):** the concrete world claims: a years-of-schooling coefficient on a standard test, a single "abstract reasoning" score, and the assumption that a schooled classroom is the same habitat as the world outside it.

**R_T is nonzero.** Four things the shorthand holds do not survive the trip back down:

- the **union structure** flattens into one "education effect" when a single score is used (Fig 9 :54-57);
- the **per-slot indexing** of the statistical claim disappears into a pooled mean (Fig 1 :19, :22);
- the **non-updating anchor** is replaced by a test score produced by the same system it grades (Fig 1 :27, Fig 2 :29-30);
- the **method/recording distinction** is lost, so a replay is credited as a head start (Fig 4 :22-26, Fig 5 :72).

Conversely, one thing survives cleanly: the claim that the change depends on what crosses the membrane and on which instrument is available. That is the residue worth sending. R_T was **not** measured numerically, because no ground reading exists; it is recorded qualitatively as the enumerated set of dropped constraints. Treating "the formula is the condensed version of the figures" as identity would set R_T to zero and would be the dead-reckoning error Fig 4 :30 names.
