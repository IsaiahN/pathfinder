# The Tether's answer: how access to formal education changes three reasoning capacities

**Mode: `specified` on each per-domain verdict, `general` on the structural shape, `gap` on the magnitude. Not `grounded`.** Simulated derivation checked against the figures (`05_what_shape_means.md` section 6), not a ground reading.

## 1. The residual it answers

Access to formal education is a maintained transmission channel whose effect is registered in three places: abstraction, statistics and hypothetical reasoning. The question asks for a change (an ordered delta) in three capacities that have different currencies and different anchors. This is a union compound, not one hard problem: Fig 9 :4-5 "First: is there a fact here? Disagreements that do not shrink with effort, where each rule keeps working well on a different subset, are not one hard question but several well-formed ones. Split it rather than search." The world literature agrees that the interaction between education and task type defeats a single summary (Scribner and Cole 1973; Sharp, Cole and Lave 1979).

## 2. The shape, one verdict per domain

1. **The master condition (all domains): a method crosses, or a recording crosses.** Fig 4 :22 "Only methods cross up. Only head starts come down." Fig 4 :24 "downward: something that makes the next search cheaper. Never a replay of what worked once." Fig 4 :25-26 "A recording carried upward looks like knowledge and is a description of one occasion. Replayed downward, it produces a system that repeats a past success and cannot produce a new one." Access changes use when a re-applicable method crosses; it changes recitation when a replay crosses. Fig 5 :72 "and a library that grows is not a reach that grows." Schooling is contact with a frame whose closure differs (Fig 6 :23), and Fig 10 :39-40 "every atom carried is a discovery the frame did not have to make" names the legitimate gain. Verdict: `specified`, triangulated.

2. **Abstraction (D1): education can add the round trip, not the capacity.** Abstraction is already a primitive operation: Symbols table :70 "Abstraction. Detail is thrown away, which is what makes the result reusable." and :74 "Concretisation. Putting an abstract result back into a specific situation." Scribner and Cole 1973: "All culture groups thus far studied have demonstrated the capacity to remember, generalize, form concepts, operate with abstractions, and reason logically." What varies is the functional system. The figure's contribution is the instrument: Fig 4 :30 "So take the round trip and measure the gap. Without it you are navigating by dead reckoning: confident about a position nobody checked." Symbols table :77 "The gap between what was sent up and what came back down: gap(x, (T_E ∘ T_A)(x)). Measures what the coarser description cannot hold." An education that teaches abstraction without concretisation is the dead-reckoning failure. Verdict: `specified`, triangulated; magnitude `gap`.

3. **Statistics (D3): education changes format and independence discipline, not raw exposure.** Fig 1 :19 "The residual is indexed by object slot, not aggregated." Symbols table :43 "R is measured per slot, never as a single global number." Fig 2 :31 "Independence, between two frames, is how little they draw on the same evidence." Fig 2 :44 "The effective-sample-size correction for correlated observations is standard in survey statistics and meta-analysis; the collapse is the same arithmetic read as a warning." Formal teaching transfers (Fong, Krantz and Nisbett 1986), but representation format and experience carry much of the effect (Schulze and Hertwig 2022; Till and Sproesser 2020). Aggregation can delete the very signal it reports (Fig 1 :22 "Aggregating across slots is how a live signal disappears into an average"), and a test cannot validate the faculty it defines (Fig 1 :27 "They are frame-internal. A frame cannot score itself with a quantity it also produces."). Verdict: `specified`, triangulated; years-of-schooling coefficient refused as a filter pretending to be a witness (Fig 9 :60).

4. **Hypothetical reasoning (D4): education transmits bonds, and negation is the decisive one.** Fig 12 :111 "A vocabulary without bonds can name a state and cannot name a route to one." Fig 12 :112 "A plan is a sequence. A goal is a comparison. Progress is a subtraction. A refutation is a negation, and that one is not a bond." Operators table :58 "a refutation is a negation, which is why a vocabulary without it can only accumulate." Kroupin et al. 2024, reviewing Luria, Cole and Scribner: "unschooled populations have persistently failed unfamiliar and/or counterfactual-premise syllogisms." Sharp, Cole and Lave 1979 found education's strongest influence "where problems must be treated hypothetically without recourse to real-world knowledge." The teachable discipline is named in Fig 5 :65 "The remedy is a discipline rather than a branch: make the sought-for shape an ORDINARY TERM. Write down what an answer would look like, bind it, and let it accrue a residual like anything else." No new primitive is installed: Fig 13 :115 "The set is closed. The arrangements are not. That gap is why import exists." Verdict: `specified`, triangulated, with one live strain on whether premise encoding is a missing atom or an unapplied bond.

## 3. What it explicitly does not say

- Not that education installs three new faculties. The capacities are universal; what changes is the functional system, the instrument and the bond vocabulary.
- Not that more years, more tests or more content produce the change. Those are budget filters (Fig 9 :60), and more content can grow the library without the reach (Fig 5 :72).
- Not that a mean test-score lift is a verdict on any of the three capacities. It is a projection whose source is undecided (Fig 1 :22; *The Hard Problem is About Source*).
- Not that the counterfactual result is settled. World studies use school-shaped tasks; Kroupin et al. 2024 explicitly call for "re-classify[ing] much of our existing data" rather than treating it as universal.
- Not `grounded`. No measurement was taken and no ground fires a pre-committed prediction here.

## 4. What would settle it: the ground

Not a further argument. From *The Hard Problem is About Source* (carried in `05`): an instrument that reads green "cannot comment on the source"; the spectrometer resolved it. Here the discriminating instruments are:

- **Method versus recording contrast:** vary only whether the transmitted content is a re-applicable method or a replay of past successes, and measure on a residual neither group trained on. Fires F2.
- **Format and independence contrast:** hold representation format and witness independence fixed, then vary years of schooling against a ground that does not update on test scores. Fires F4.
- **Counterfactual-premise contrast:** give the same syllogism to schooled and unschooled groups with a ground independent of schooling, and discriminate "premise not held" from "premise held but not composed." Fires F5 and resolves the live strain in `06` Q1.
- **Standing falsifiers from `01` section 4:** derive one of the six terms from the other five; admit a seventh primitive; show a replay extends reach as well as a method; show no non-updating assessment can be built.

## 5. The honest stop

**Unreached, not unreachable.** The three conditions overlap, and one instrument can clear only one region at a time (Fig 9 :55-57). The answer stops at the shape and the falsifiers, and records the lineage so the stop is checkable. Fig 8 :39 "So a search that finds nothing is never a proof of absence." Indiscernibility is not identity: a pooled score that cannot distinguish method from recording does not show they are the same thing.

## 6. One-paragraph version for outreach

> The figures do not return a number for how much formal education changes reasoning. They return a shape. Abstraction, statistics and hypothetical reasoning are three different operations with three different currencies, so they must be kept separate. What education changes in each is not a new faculty but what crosses the classroom membrane: a re-applicable method sends down a head start and extends what a person can reach, while a replay of past successes grows the library without growing the reach. Abstraction changes when the round trip from abstraction back to a specific situation is taught and its gap measured; statistical use changes when representation format and independence discipline change rather than with raw years of exposure; hypothetical reasoning changes when the bond vocabulary, especially the ability to refuse a held claim, is transmitted. Standardised tests can sever the channel to a non-updating ground while the ground itself stays intact. What is offered is the shape any answer must have, five failure conditions written in advance, and the instruments that would close it. The magnitude stays open until a ground settles it.
