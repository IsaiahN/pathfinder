## 0. The question under audit, stated in effect terms

> How does access to formal education change people's use of abstraction, statistics, and hypothetical reasoning?

Restated as an effect, before any figure line is chosen:

- **What is being decided.** Whether a maintained transmission channel (schooling) changes how a learner composes, checks and refuses terms, or only changes what the learner can recite. Option A: schooling installs three new faculties. Option B: schooling sends a head start across a membrane, and the change in each named capacity depends on *what crosses* (a method or a recording) and on *which instrument* is extended.
- **What each option leads to.** A predicts a uniform, positive, measurable lift in all three capacities with years of schooling; it treats test scores as the ground. B predicts a conditional, per-capacity change: abstraction changes when the round trip is taught, statistics changes when representation format and independence discipline change, hypothetical reasoning changes when the bond vocabulary and the discipline of a pre-registered prediction are transmitted. B also predicts that a curriculum transmitting only recordings raises the library without raising the reach, and that school-shaped tests can sever the channel while leaving the ground untouched.
- **The sought-for shape, bound as an ordinary term (formula step "Direction").** An answer would look like: for each capacity C, a statement of (i) which slot of the learner's library C indexes, (ii) what crosses the membrane to change it, (iii) which non-updating anchor would settle a claim about it, and (iv) a failure condition written in advance. Binding this before the run makes the preference refusable by the same test as everything else (Fig 5 :65 "The remedy is a discipline rather than a branch: make the sought-for shape an ORDINARY TERM. Write down what an answer would look like, bind it, and let it accrue a residual like anything else.").

**This is a union, not one question with three examples.** Fig 9 :4-5 "First: is there a fact here? Disagreements that do not shrink with effort, where each rule keeps working well on a different subset, are not one hard question but several well-formed ones. Split it rather than search." The world literature agrees: Scribner and Cole 1973 report "The complexity of education-task interaction prohibits any sweeping generalizations about the 'effects of schooling.'" Sharp, Cole and Lave 1979 found "age and educational experience contributed differentially to performance on different tasks." One lever cannot clear all three conditions.

## 1. Domain census (before choosing any figure line)

### 1.1 R by arity, symmetry and scale

- **Arity: three conditions, not one.** Abstraction, statistics and hypothetical reasoning are three different operations with three different currencies (see the per-domain lines below). They form a union compound (Fig 9 :54-55 "A bundled problem is usually a union of overlapping conditions, not a list of separate ones. A single lever clears the region where only one condition holds and leaves the intersections standing, because the second condition still binds.").
- **Symmetry: the relation between schooling and each capacity is ordered, not symmetric.** "Access changes use" is a directed claim (Fig 12 :72 "A then B; B needs A to have happened"). A correlation between years of schooling and test performance cannot choose the arrow; the world literature itself flags the selection confound (Sharp et al. 1979: school availability, not ability, was "the major constraint on the amount of education obtained," and the IQ measure ruled out "massive selection artifacts as sufficient explanation"). Symmetry versus order matters here and is not recovered from the data alone (Fig 12 :92 "Under a symmetric bond, k operands have k! spellings and one term").
- **Scale: the change happens inside one learner (first order) and across the population membrane (second order).** Fig 4 :19-20 "the same loop and the same rule for naming what worked, whether one agent is running it or a thousand. Only the actors, the currency and the anchor change." The kernel is fixed; what education changes is the actors, the currency and which anchors are reachable.

### 1.2 One line per domain: actors / currency / anchor

| domain | actors | currency | anchor | question type |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| **D1, the learner's frame** | the learner's primitives and bonds, belief b, weighting w (Symbols :20, :24) | composition inside closure(Γ), residual R per slot | the ground, "The ground enters as the only metric." (Fig 1 :23) | partition: what can be composed over, and what is merely held |
| **D2, the educational transmission frame** | teachers, curriculum, texts, peers, tests | methods and head starts versus recordings and replays; the bond arrangement carried across | does the transmission change contact with a ground that does not update? | boundary: what crosses the membrane, a generator or a replay |
| **D3, the statistics and evidence frame** | per-slot readings, samples, correlated witnesses, assessment instruments | evidence, correlation, independence, bits versus actions | a ground that does not update on the report (Fig 2 :29-30) | union: per-slot signal plus independence plus representation format |
| **D4, the hypothetical-composition frame** | the seven bonds plus negation, the sought-for shape as a term | routes, goals, comparisons, progress, refusals | a ground that can fire a prediction made before the run | partition: which bonds are present in the learner's vocabulary |

No nameable anchor means it is not a domain yet. All four have one: D1 the ground as metric, D2 a change in contact, D3 a non-updating anchor, D4 a falsifiable prediction. Test scores and grades are explicitly *not* anchors for D2 or D3; they are frame-internal quantities (Fig 1 :27 "They are frame-internal. A frame cannot score itself with a quantity it also produces.").

### 1.3 Partition or union

Union of four overlapping conditions. D1 is the frame that receives; D2 is the channel; D3 and D4 are two of the capacities the channel changes. D2 overlaps D3 (a test is both a transmission artifact and an evidence instrument) and D2 overlaps D4 (a curriculum transmits bond vocabulary). The overlap is a compound with its own loop, so D2x3 and D2x4 are each examined separately rather than folded into a single "education effect."

### 1.4 Dependency order (Fig 3)

1. Vocabulary: "it has terms for what things afford: what can be moved, opened, joined, blocked" (Fig 3 :7). Without terms for what a situation affords, no capacity can register.
2. Objective: "it can say what would count as progress here, in its own terms" (Fig 3 :10). This is the middle link that has "neither" obvious failures nor obvious instruments (Fig 3 :25-26).
3. Method across the membrane: "downward: something that makes the next search cheaper. Never a replay of what worked once." (Fig 4 :24).
4. Independent ground contact: "Frames range against the ground. They do not negotiate with it." (Fig 2 :45).

Optimising a link that already works cannot rescue one that does not (Fig 3 :19). Most education research effort lands above the break, where the measurements already exist, which is why the middle link is the one to audit first.

## 3. Findings

### F1 - The three named capacities are three operations with different anchors (decisive for the union reading)

- **Abstraction** is already a primitive operation in the framework: Symbols table :70 "Abstraction. Detail is thrown away, which is what makes the result reusable." and :74 "Concretisation. Putting an abstract result back into a specific situation." Its instrument is the round trip, Symbols table :77 "The gap between what was sent up and what came back down: gap(x, (T_E ∘ T_A)(x)). Measures what the coarser description cannot hold." Fig 4 :36 places this in abstract interpretation (Cousot and Cousot, 1977). Currency: bits retained versus discarded. Anchor: the ground, via what the round trip can still predict.
- **Statistics** is indexed by slot: Fig 1 :19 "The residual is indexed by object slot, not aggregated." and Symbols table :43 "R is measured per slot, never as a single global number." Its other primitive is independence: Fig 2 :31 "Independence, between two frames, is how little they draw on the same evidence." World corroboration: Fig 2 :44 "The effective-sample-size correction for correlated observations is standard in survey statistics and meta-analysis; the collapse is the same arithmetic read as a warning." Currency: evidence, correlation, effective sample size. Anchor: a measurement that does not move when a frame pushes on it (Fig 2 :29-30).
- **Hypothetical reasoning** is a bond capability: Fig 12 :111 "A vocabulary without bonds can name a state and cannot name a route to one." Fig 12 :112 "A plan is a sequence. A goal is a comparison. Progress is a subtraction. A refutation is a negation, and that one is not a bond." Operators table :52 and :58 add the two that matter most for counterfactuals and corrections. Currency: composition over terms not currently observed. Anchor: a prediction committed before the run and then fired or refuted.

These do not share a currency, so an averaged "education improves reasoning by X" is a category error, not a cautious summary. Fig 12 :125 "A composition costs in two currencies, and they do not add." Verdict: union compound, triangulated across Fig 1, Fig 2, Fig 4, Fig 12, the Operators and Symbols tables, and the cross-cultural literature. Mode: `general` on the structure. **Refuter:** exhibit one instrument, one currency and one anchor on which all three capacities register such that a single scalar fully predicts all three without residue. Until then, keep three verdicts.

### F2 - Transmission is a head start or a recording, and only the head start changes the reach

Fig 4 :22 "Only methods cross up. Only head starts come down." Fig 4 :23 "upward: a method that can be reapplied. Never the recording of a particular success." Fig 4 :24 "downward: something that makes the next search cheaper. Never a replay of what worked once." Fig 4 :25-26 "A recording carried upward looks like knowledge and is a description of one occasion. Replayed downward, it produces a system that repeats a past success and cannot produce a new one."

Fig 6 :23 "No frame escapes its own closure. The bound moves only on contact with a frame whose closure differs." Schooling is exactly contact with a frame whose closure differs. Fig 10 :39-40 "every atom carried is a discovery the frame did not have to make" names the legitimate gain. But Fig 5 :72 "and a library that grows is not a reach that grows." A learner can hold more terms and reach no new residual.

This is the master conditional of the whole solution: **access to education changes use when what crosses is a method (a generator that can be reapplied and checked); access changes recitation, not use, when what crosses is a recording of one occasion.** Mode: `specified`, triangulated across Fig 4, Fig 5, Fig 6 and Fig 10. **Refuter:** show a curriculum whose entire transmitted content is a replay of past successes that nonetheless extends the learner's reach to a new residual at the same rate as a method-based curriculum, with the new residual registered by a ground that did not train on the replay.

Strain check: "head start comes down" (Fig 4) versus "only import moves the wall" (Fig 6). Re-census: a head start is a method already in the receiving frame's closure made cheaper to reach; an import adds an atom from a frame whose closure differs (Fig 6 :26 "The world does not gain primitives; a frame gains access."). Different objects, no strain after re-census.

### F3 - Abstraction: education supplies the round trip, not the capacity

The capacities are universal. Scribner and Cole 1973: "All culture groups thus far studied have demonstrated the capacity to remember, generalize, form concepts, operate with abstractions, and reason logically." What varies is the "functional learning system" that brings the capacity to bear. Greenfield (Oral or Written Language, UCLA) reports that school children shift sorting criteria, give predicative explanations and answer "why" questions, and interprets these as "a general ability for contextindependent, abstract thinking that only school children displayed," though Scribner and Cole caution that this interpretation was contested.

The figure's contribution is sharper than "schooling creates abstraction." Abstraction is already T_A (Symbols table :70). What education can add is the *instrument* for the loss: Fig 4 :30 "So take the round trip and measure the gap. Without it you are navigating by dead reckoning: confident about a position nobody checked." A school that teaches the abstraction as the answer, without the concretisation leg, produces exactly the dead-reckoning failure the figure names. Kroupin, Davis, Zeng and Henrich 2024 describe schooling as a "Small Game" environment using "a simple, decontextualized model (i.e., representation of the situation) containing only a limited or closed set of abstractions," and warn that "the problem, again, comes when we simply presuppose the degree to which these Small-Game paradigms correspond to reasoning in the broader, Big-Game world." That is the R_T warning in world vocabulary.

Verdict D1: `specified`, triangulated (Fig 4 plus Symbols table plus two independent cross-cultural research groups). Mode of the magnitude claim: `gap`. **Refuter:** find a population with no formal schooling that measures the gap between its own abstraction and its return to a specific situation, and composes over the result, at the same rate as a schooled population on a task whose ground does not update on schooling. World sources report *different* functional systems rather than absent capacities, so this refuter is live and has not fired.

### F4 - Statistics: education changes format and independence discipline, and the effect cuts both ways

Fong, Krantz and Nisbett 1986 (Cognitive Psychology 18:253-292): "People possess an abstract inferential rule system that is an intuitive version of the law of large numbers. Because the rule system is not tied to any particular content domain, it is possible to improve it by formal teaching techniques." Four experiments showed transfer: "Learning from the examples was abstracted to such a extent that subjects showed just as much improvement on domains where the rule was not taught as on domains where it was taught," and a statistics course raised statistical answers in a telephone survey "completely outside of the context of training." That is a clean world report of a method crossing a membrane.

Schulze and Hertwig 2022 (Psychonomic Bulletin & Review, open access PMC) complicate the simple "more schooling" story. In 100 children aged 8-11 and 100 adults, "When the information was conveyed in a description-based probability format, overall, adults correctly solved 18% of the Bayesian problems... Children solved fewer... 3% of the Bayesian problems." With sequential experience, "adults solved 79% of Bayesian problems... children solved 53%." The authors conclude "experience of statistical information plays a key role in shaping children's reasoning under uncertainty." Till and Sproesser 2020 (Frontiers in Education) similarly found grade-4 students could solve Bayesian tasks "when natural frequencies were used," and that a hands-on intervention helped: format and representation, not years alone, carry the change.

The figures predict both directions. Fig 1 :22 "Aggregating across slots is how a live signal disappears into an average." Normalising to a single score is abstraction that throws away the countable per-slot entities (Till and Sproesser describe exactly this: "the information about the absolute numbers within the population disappear"). Fig 2 :40 "agreement about the world is worthless when the agreeing frames share an evidence pool" and Fig 2 :44 make correlated witnesses one witness. Fig 1 :27 "They are frame-internal. A frame cannot score itself with a quantity it also produces." Grades are produced by the same system they are used to validate.

Verdict D3: `specified`, triangulated (Fig 1, Fig 2, Symbols table, plus three statistical-reasoning sources and one cross-sectional schooling source). The claim is conditional: education changes statistical use by changing representation format and independence discipline; raw years of exposure is a filter, not a witness (Fig 9 :60). **Refuter:** show that years of schooling predicts per-slot statistical discrimination and independent-witness discrimination after representation format and independence are held fixed, against a ground that does not update on test scores. F4's narrowed claim fails if this holds.

### F5 - Hypothetical reasoning: education transmits bonds, and the decisive bond is negation

The world evidence is unusually direct. Kroupin et al. 2024, reviewing Luria, Cole, Scribner and replications: "unschooled populations have persistently failed unfamiliar and/or counterfactual-premise syllogisms." The bears-in-the-north syllogism drew the reply "I don't know, I've seen a black bear, I've never seen any others..." Scribner showed "40% dropped the second premise entirely in their recall" and that errors recoded premises into questions about personal experience, an "empirical bias," with the schooled complement an "analytic bias." Sharp, Cole and Lave 1979 found education's "strongest influence on tasks where taxonomic principles are the criterion of correct classification, where correct performance requires the subject to provide structure to the nominal stimulus array, where problems must be treated hypothetically without recourse to real-world knowledge."

The figure's account is structural: Fig 12 :111 "A vocabulary without bonds can name a state and cannot name a route to one." Fig 12 :112 "A plan is a sequence. A goal is a comparison. Progress is a subtraction. A refutation is a negation, and that one is not a bond." Operators table :58 "a refutation is a negation, which is why a vocabulary without it can only accumulate." Holding a counterfactual premise is composing over a term not currently observed; refusing a held regularity is the eighth operator, not one of the seven bonds. Fig 5 :65 names the teachable discipline: "The remedy is a discipline rather than a branch: make the sought-for shape an ORDINARY TERM. Write down what an answer would look like, bind it, and let it accrue a residual like anything else."

So access to education changes hypothetical reasoning when it transmits the bond vocabulary (sequence, comparison, subtraction, negation) and the practice of committing a prediction in advance. It does not install a new primitive: Fig 13 :115 "The set is closed. The arrangements are not. That gap is why import exists." Verdict D4: `specified`, triangulated (Fig 12, Operators table, Fig 5, plus three independent world reports). **Refuter:** show counterfactual-premise reasoning at the same rate in a population with no exposure to formal bond vocabulary, on the same syllogism tasks, with the ground independent of the test. World evidence currently runs the other way, but it is a `specified` result, not a `grounded` one.

### F6 - The assessment loop is where education most often breaks its own channel

Fig 3 :25-26 "The link between them, having terms for what a situation offers and being able to say what would count as progress, has neither, so it attracts the least attention and the fewest instruments." Fig 3 :27 "A link with no instrument is not a link that works. It is a link nobody has looked at." This is the objective link, and schooling supplies it in the form of grading rubrics and tests. Kroupin et al. 2024 list standardized assessment as a defining feature of the "Small Game" of schooling: "Assessment standardized across all learners (e.g. grading rubrics), unrelated to performance in life outside of school."

When that instrument decides the outcome it stops measuring it: Fig 10 :24 "the ground does not decay, the channel does" and Fig 10 :26 "stale percepts, dropped transients, saturated metrics, pooled readings". Fig 11 :70 "A test harness is a substituted habitat. Which is why a synthetic solve proves wiring and never capability." Fig 7 :35 "A room's agreement is variation, not a verdict. Selection between rooms is where the ground votes." A classroom in which everyone agrees with the teacher is panel 1 of Fig 2, not triangulation.

Verdict: `general` structural, single-source on the figure side for the exact failure mode (the Fig 10/11 office rules are framework-imposed, as the figure itself concedes at :83), with strong world corroboration from the schooling-as-confound literature. Honest label: **single-source on the figure side**, corroborated in kind but not independently derived. **Refuter:** exhibit an education assessment whose anchor does not update on the assessment, whose verifier cannot reconstruct the claim being graded, and whose results transfer to an untrained ground task. If none can be built, the assessment claim is unfalsifiable as stated and should be withdrawn.

### F7 - The numerical magnitude is a declared gap

No figure line returns an effect size, a years-of-schooling coefficient or a curriculum ranking. Fig 9 :32 "you get a kind of thing, never the answer itself"; Fig 9 :42 "narrowing gets you close, the ground decides"; Fig 13 :134 "Unreachable is a verdict here, not a silence". The world literature is explicitly heterogeneous: Scribner and Cole 1973 "schooling did not make a difference on all the tasks we experimented with and that different levels of education influenced certain tasks differentially"; Sharp et al. 1979 "For other tasks, both increasing age and educational experience were associated with increased performance, while for a few tasks performance improved as a function of age but was not affected by differences in education." Fig 8 :39 "So a search that finds nothing is never a proof of absence." Declared `gap`, of the unreachable-at-this-budget kind, not underivable in principle. A ground with a real anchor would settle it. Filters never do (Fig 9 :60).

### F8 - Dependency order: effort above the break cannot rescue the middle link

Applying Fig 3 to education: vocabulary (Fig 3 :7), objective (Fig 3 :10), then method transfer (Fig 4 :24), then ground contact (Fig 2 :45). The middle link ("having terms for what a situation offers and being able to say what would count as progress") is exactly what a curriculum supplies and exactly what has the fewest instruments (Fig 3 :25-26). Fig 3 :19 "Optimising a link that already works cannot rescue one that does not." Adding more content, more years or more tests is effort above or below the break; it cannot repair a learner who cannot say what would count as progress in their own terms. Fig 12 :113 "situation contains and cannot say what would count as getting somewhere from it." Verdict: `general`, triangulated with Fig 5 :65. **Refuter:** show a curriculum that raises a ground-anchored transfer measure while the learner cannot state what would count as progress in their own terms on that task.

## 4. Pre-registered refuter (written before the conclusion)

Required before any outreach, per the seat map's rule that "a falsifier written before the run is a design instrument, not only an honesty device." The shape claim is refuted if any of the following fires:

1. **Universal-capacity failure.** A population with no formal schooling performs at the same rate as a schooled population on (a) per-slot residual discrimination, (b) treating correlated witnesses as one witness, and (c) counterfactual-premise reasoning, with a ground that does not update on the test. This would break F3, F4 and F5 together.
2. **Method/recording failure.** A curriculum transmitting only replays of past successes extends reach to new residuals at the same rate as one transmitting re-applicable methods, measured on a residual the replay set did not cover. This breaks Fig 4 :22-26 and F2.
3. **Assessment failure.** No assessment can be built whose anchor does not update on the assessment, whose verifier cannot reconstruct the graded claim, and whose scores transfer to an untrained ground task. This breaks F6; if it also cannot be stated as a testable condition, F6 is withdrawn rather than kept as an unfalsifiable assertion.
4. **Format-independence failure.** Years of schooling predicts per-slot statistical reasoning with representation format and independence held fixed against a non-updating ground. This breaks the narrowed F4.
5. **Substrate failure.** One of the six terms is derived from the other five, or a seventh primitive is admitted. This breaks Fig 13 :115 and the whole framework.
6. **Gap converted to verdict by a filter.** A tier score, sample size, publication venue or prestige is used to return a single numerical magnitude for "how much education changes reasoning." This breaks Fig 9 :60 and is recorded as a design violation, not a new finding.
