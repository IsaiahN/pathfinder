## 1. A shape is a constraint, not a filler

The canonical README (opened this session) states the limit: the framework returns "what a missing thing would have to look like (a shape, not a name)." Fig 9 :32 "you get a kind of thing, never the answer itself". Fig 13 :133 "A system with a closure can answer that before it searches, and what it returns is a shape rather than a name."

From *The Library of Babel* (carried from the exemplar's mapping): "You can cut your search short if the set of books returned is small and descriptive enough that it provides a general shape of the answer that satisfies your question."

A **shape** is the narrowed form of an answer you can state before you can measure it. It constrains; it does not fill. For this question:

- A shape would be: "for each named capacity, state which slot it indexes, what crosses the membrane to change it, which non-updating anchor would settle it, and what would fail."
- An effect size (say, "four years of schooling raises abstraction by 0.4 standard deviations") is a name-with-a-number, not a shape. The figures withhold it (Fig 9 :42 "narrowing gets you close, the ground decides").

## 2. How the shape was obtained for COG-0038

- Enumerate before composing. Fig 11 :43-44 "The habitat is enumerated, never composed. List everything in contact with the residual, then what is in contact with those, and outward until the cascade stops mattering." The education literature supplies a large habitat: teacher, curriculum, rubric, peer group, home language, written language, test, dataset, funding, selection. Only those in contact with the three named capacities enter the census in `01` section 1.
- Describe the effect, not the cause. Fig 9 :30-31 "your words are borrowed too, so describe the effect rather than the cause." The question's words ("formal education," "abstraction," "statistics," "hypothetical reasoning") are borrowed from educational psychology. They were re-grounded as library crossing, per-slot residual plus independence, and bond composition respectively (F1 of `01`).
- From *Decoding Tacit Knowledge* (carried): "When contact isn't possible, the best you can send is a plain description of what you'd expect to see, because anyone can check that against the world." That is why the outreach packet asks what a checker would count as a failure before showing the framework.
- *Library of Babel* on stopping: the answer is reached when "the finish line will also be the starting place," and the paradox is that you have "composed the answer through the process of locating it." Here the finish line is the four-part shape above, which is also the first thing written down in `01` section 0.

## 3. What licenses a name (and the correction that matters here)

Two things, both outside the shape itself:

1. **The ground settles it.** Formula step 5: "SETTLE the ground settles it. Verified only by what pays. A gate is not the ground." Applied here: a capacity claim is settled by a measurement whose anchor does not update on the schooling it measures (Sharp, Cole and Lave 1979 tried exactly this with school availability as an exogenous constraint; Fig 2 :29-30 supplies the rule).
2. **A provenanced import from a frame whose closure differs** (Fig 6 :23, Fig 8 :51-52), with a source gate and a shadow test. The cross-cultural studies (Scribner and Cole 1973, Sharp et al. 1979, Kroupin et al. 2024) are imports from anthropology and developmental psychology into this derivation. Their provenance and overlap are recorded in `10_source_ledger.csv`.

*They're Scanning Books* (carried): "The real import is not a new term in the existing space. It is a new dimension of the space." And: "A minimum viable set of six terms is a human's careful guess at reality's compression axes; another sensorium's minimum set need not be those six." Also: "Declaring the primitives does not make them neutral. It makes the choice visible."

### 3b. Correction to the "shape, not a name" rule (kept visible)

I initially read the rule as forbidding the naming of abstraction, statistics and hypothetical reasoning as the capacities at issue. That overstates it. The canonical README's phrase governs a **missing** primitive, one not held in Γ. Abstraction and concretisation are already primitive operations (Symbols table :70, :74). Per-slot residual and independence are already primitives (Fig 1 :19, Fig 2 :31). The seven bonds and negation are already in the operator set (Fig 12 :112). None is missing. So naming them is a **binding from the education-research frame onto atoms already held**, provenanced as such. What remains forbidden is inventing a seventh primitive called "schooling" or "abstract thinking faculty" and claiming it moves the wall (Fig 13 :115 "The set is closed. The arrangements are not. That gap is why import exists."). Both readings are recorded so neither is quietly lost.

## 4. Why a single mean effect is the specific error here

*The Hard Problem is About Source* (carried) gives the mechanism. Metamerism: an instrument reads green and "cannot comment on the source" - one green light, or yellow+blue, or RGB, or CMYK, or a scheme not yet discovered. "If several paths create the same behavior, then behavior is a projection with lossy information." And: "An instrument that can't distinguish these cases demonstrates its own inadequacy." It was the spectrometer, not an argument, that resolved it.

Here, a mean test-score lift reads green and cannot comment on the source: it cannot distinguish a transmitted method from a replay, a per-slot gain from an aggregate, a format effect from a faculty effect, or a counterfactual competence from an "analytic bias" on a school-shaped task. What is owed is a better instrument, not a single number. Fig 1 :22 "Aggregating across slots is how a live signal disappears into an average."

*The Recursive Self-Improvement Trap* (carried): "the output is only as good as the validator. The agent, or the tool, or the frame, or the theory, cannot validate itself." A test that both defines and grades "abstract reasoning" is precisely a frame validating itself (Fig 1 :27 "They are frame-internal. A frame cannot score itself with a quantity it also produces.").

## 5. The shape returned, stated plainly

**From the figures, access to formal education changes use of these capacities by changing what crosses the membrane into the learner's library and which instruments are available to check the result. A method (re-applicable, checkable) sends down a head start and extends the reach; a recording (a replay of one occasion) grows the library without the reach. Abstraction changes when the abstraction/concretisation round trip is taught and its gap measured; statistics changes when representation format and independence discipline change, not by raw exposure; hypothetical reasoning changes when the bond vocabulary, especially negation and the pre-committed prediction, is transmitted. The three changes have different currencies and anchors, so they are kept separate and never averaged. The magnitude is a declared gap; a ground with a non-updating anchor would settle it.**

That is a shape. It does not say how many years, which curriculum, or how large the effect. It says what an answer must look like and what would refute it.

## 6. Two consequences for outreach

**Prestige is not action.** From *The Recursive Self-Improvement Trap* (carried): "Prestige must never be allowed to validate an action directly... Someone let prestige stand in for proof... Prestige can fund an action. It can give an action a budget to be tried. But it cannot settle an action. Only the ground can settle it. The Ground Maintainer must be blind to prestige." A campaign that gathers famous education researchers to agree with this shape is the named collapse, not corroboration. The legitimate ask is a budget to try the refuter against a ground.

**This audit is a simulation, not a ground reading.** Same essay (carried): "LLMs can quickly simulate an approximate answer/output... However, the truly testable output would need to be derived from the real architecture or working the formula yourself." LLM output is "simulacra." So `01` and this file are simulated derivations, checked for internal consistency against the figures. They are not tests of the claim and must be labelled as such. They cannot settle anything they report on.

## 7. What would move the shape to a name

Ground contact that discriminates among the sources of a test-score reading: (i) a curriculum contrast in which only the method/recording distinction varies, measured on a residual neither group trained on; (ii) a representation-format contrast with an anchor that does not update on the test; (iii) a counterfactual-premise task whose ground is independent of schooling. Until such instruments are run, Fig 9 :42 "narrowing gets you close, the ground decides" is the honest stop.
