# Expert verification packet: the Tether on ecological redundancy

**What this is:** a claim in a form an ecologist can check. Mode, derivation and refuters are stated before the conclusion, so a reader knows what to look for and what would settle it.

**Mode: `general` on the shape, `specified` on the ecology-facing claims, not `grounded`.** No species pair is asserted as interchangeable. This is a decision procedure and its falsifiers.

**Status:** a simulated derivation checked for internal consistency against the figures, not a ground reading. It cannot settle the question it reports on.

## 1. The claim, in one paragraph

Interchangeability of ecological role is the identity bond for a named function, settled by substitution at each species-slot, in a stated habitat, over a horizon that includes indirect effects, and checked by a frame independent of the one that proposed the grouping. Trait similarity and functional-group membership are filters for where to look, not witnesses for the verdict. Apparent redundancy misleads when the reading is aggregated across slots (a global residual near zero with one live slot is a legal state), when species share ingredients but not arrangement (isomers), when scoping stops at direct contact, when the verdict is borrowed from one environmental condition, or when the agreeing frames share an evidence pool. The framework returns this shape and the test; it does not name species.

## 2. Three checkable sub-claims

### C1 — framework internal, checkable by reading Figures 1 and 12

- **Statement:** substitutability is an identity bond settled by measurement, and the residual is per slot. A species list is an ingredient list; arrangement carries the difference.
- **Source, verbatim:** Fig 12 :69 "substitute one for the other. Is anything lost? No." Fig 12 :94 "The arrangement is the substance." Fig 12 :117 "isomer. Same atoms, different bond, different substance. The difference is invisible in the formula and decisive in the behaviour." Fig 1 :19 "The residual is indexed by object slot, not aggregated." Fig 1 :13 "a global R near zero with one live slot residual is a legal state".
- **Refuter:** a controlled reciprocal substitution leaves all per-slot residuals at zero across conditions; or an aggregate curve predicts substitution outcomes as well as per-slot readings; or a same-trait same-position pair is non-interchangeable in a way the seven bonds cannot express.

### C2 — ecology facing, checkable against substitution and environmental-change experiments

- **Statement:** a redundancy verdict depends on the environmental condition and on cascade depth. This is the part a working ecologist can confirm or break.
- **What to check:** does the same species pair remain substitutable under a second contrasting condition? Does the equivalence survive to the third trophic step? Fetzer et al. 2015 (PNAS, open access, read 2026-11-23) report *"Shapes suggesting functional redundancy in one environment can change, suggesting functional differences in another environment"* and *"Species that are functionally redundant in one environment may become pivotal in another."* Fig 11 :52-53 "And contact cascades. Scoping the habitat to direct contact is how DDT reached the eagles: first-order enumeration, second-order effect, and the residual that mattered was three steps out."
- **Refuter:** the same per-slot residuals under two contrasting conditions with the same cascade depth. If that holds, the condition-dependence and cascade claims fail for that pair.

### C3 — verification frame, checkable by inspecting who agrees and on what evidence

- **Statement:** agreement among workers who share one literature is a convention until an independent ground settles the pair.
- **Source, verbatim:** Fig 2 :35 "Alignment is triangulation, not negotiation. A mirror cannot correct you." Fig 2 :40 "disqualifying for a verdict: agreement about the world is worthless when the agreeing frames share an evidence pool". Fig 2 :42 "Buy independence in proportion to the cost of being wrong."
- **Refuter:** a result from a frame that did not draw on the same data or literature, or a direct substitution assay, that confirms the pair. Then the agreement stop is refuted for that pair.

## 3. What is explicitly not claimed

- Not that any named species pair is redundant or non-redundant.
- Not that functional groups should be abandoned. They are filters for the budget.
- Not that redundancy is always misleading. The shape is conditional and the §1 refuter is live.
- Not that the figure's habitat is an ecological niche. The Tether Architecture essay declines that mapping, and the retraction is recorded in `01` §5 and `05` §6.
- Not `grounded`. No measurement at any field site or microcosm was taken by this agent.
- Not a claim about niche theory, competitive exclusion, or coexistence mechanisms beyond the structural use of relations and conditions.
- Not a unique framework. Fig 8 :39 "So a search that finds nothing is never a proof of absence", and a frame cannot certify its own limit.

## 5. What would settle it

A per-slot reciprocal substitution assay, not a further argument. From *The Hard Problem is About Source*: several paths can create one reading, so *"behavior is a projection with lossy information"*; the case was closed *"not by argument, but by the arrival of a better instrument."* Here the instrument must measure: (1) each species-slot separately; (2) both substitution directions; (3) at least two contrasting environmental conditions; (4) a third-order cascade effect or an explicitly declared horizon; (5) by a tester who did not assemble the functional grouping.

## 6. What an expert can check in twenty minutes

1. Read Fig 1 :13 and :19, and Fig 12 :69, :94, :117. Five short passages.
2. Check C2 against a system you know: have redundancy verdicts in that system flipped when the environment changed? If never, say so; that is a real refutation.
3. Name one measurement that would distinguish a genuine substitute from an isomer in your system, or say why none is possible.
4. Send the refutation. A failure is more useful than an endorsement.
