# Outreach draft: check the test before you decide about the claim

**Use:** one to one, or as a cover letter attached to a submission through the public venues in `03_public_contact_points.csv`. **Not** for bulk send.

**Hard precondition:** do not send until the stage-1 output below survives a ground check. If the per-slot prediction fails, there is nothing else to look at.

---

**Subject:** One checkable prediction about "redundant" species, before any theory

> Hi [name],
>
> I have one specific output I would like you to check against what you already know. I am not asking you to read a framework first, and I am not asking you to agree. If the output does not survive, there is nothing else to look at.
>
> **The output (`general` on the shape, `specified` on the ecology-facing claims, not `grounded`):**
>
> Call two species interchangeable in function F and habitat H only if substituting one for the other leaves the measured value of F unchanged at every species-slot that matters, over a horizon long enough to include indirect effects, under more than one environmental condition. Four consequences follow:
>
> 1. Trait similarity alone does not establish interchangeability. It is the hypothesis, not the result. The question a substitution test asks is whether anything is lost when one is put in the other's place.
> 2. A single aggregate curve, such as biomass against species richness, can look flat while one slot carries a live residual. A near-zero total gap with one live slot is a legal state, not evidence of redundancy.
> 3. Two species with the same trait list can still be different substances, because the relation each one has to the rest of the community is part of what it is. Sharing a functional-group label records the ingredients and not the arrangement.
> 4. A species that is substitutable in one environment can become pivotal in another, and a species that looks irrelevant at direct contact can matter three steps out.
>
> So the claim is a decision procedure: a group label is a place to look, the substitution measurement is what settles it, and agreement among people who read the same literature is a convention rather than a verdict.
>
> **What I am not claiming:** that any particular pair of species is or is not redundant. That a functional group is an identity. That a saturation curve proves compensation. That this email is a result. It is a shape plus a test, written before the test was run.
>
> **What would refute it, written down before I sent this:**
> 1. A controlled reciprocal substitution leaves every per-slot residual at zero, including third-order cascade slots, under two contrasting conditions. Then that pair is genuinely interchangeable, and calling its redundancy apparent would be wrong.
> 2. Someone shows that trait descriptions alone license the identity bond, with no substitution measurement.
> 3. Someone shows that an aggregate richness curve predicts substitution outcomes as well as per-slot measurements do.
>
> If any one holds, I will retract the relevant part. A one-line "this fails because..." is more useful to me than agreement. If you would rather not engage, no reply is needed.
>
> **If it survives, and only then:** the sources are below. The figures are the authority, not my summary of them.
> - Verbatim figure text with line numbers: `FRAMEWORK/reference/03-FIGURES_TEXT.md` in this repository copy (local file, not a public link).
> - Public figures and formula, branch `general-agent`: https://github.com/IsaiahN/tether/tree/general-agent/figures
> - The empirical paper that most directly bears on condition dependence: Fetzer et al. 2015, PNAS, open access: https://pmc.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/articles/PMC4672811/
>
> **If you want to use the method:** the formula is in the figures link. Run it on a system you know well and can check against a ground. Write down what would refute your result *before* you run it, and state the mode of what you get. A reported failure is more useful to me than a reported success.
>
> Best,
> [name]

## Two-stage offer, explicit

1. **Stage 1.** Send the four consequences and the refuter. Ask what the recipient would count as a failure. Record that verdict before showing any framework material.
2. **Stage 2, opt-in only.** If they are curious, offer the three links, with the note that the figures, not the summary, are the authority.
3. **Stage 3.** Offer the formula as a method to reuse, with the instruction to write a fresh refuter and state a mode for each new problem.

A stage-2 agreement is never pooled as corroboration (Fig 2 :40). Stage 3 sends a generator, which Fig 4 :22 permits. Nothing is sent by this agent; hosting and sending is yours.
