## Independent domain census

I redid the census from the residual line alone before comparing with the primary's conclusions, using the WATSON order: R by arity, symmetry and scale; one line per domain with actors, currency and anchor; partition or union; order by dependency.

- **R (arity / symmetry / scale).** The residual is the gap between a predicted and observed function after substituting one species for another. Arity: two indices, species-slot and function-slot. This is not one number. Symmetry: the intuitive claim is symmetric (if B replaces A without loss, the reverse should hold at the same slots), but the underlying ecological relations are ordered: sequence, production, comparison. Scale: first-order contact through third-order cascade; one condition through changed condition. Question type: **union compound** of ingredient equivalence, bond equivalence and instrument equivalence (Fig 9 :54 "And when the split lands on a union rather than a partition, unbundle before you search again.").
- **Domain 1, trait and function.** Actors: species as trait bundles and the measured process. Currency: effect on a named function. Anchor: the per-slot measurement. Partition question: identity or not at one level.
- **Domain 2, habitat and contact.** Actors: species, conditions, relations. Currency: contact and cascade. Anchor: the ground reading under a specific condition. Union question: condition crossed with cascade.
- **Domain 3, verification.** Actors: ecologists, reviewers, datasets. Currency: residuals and agreement. Anchor: the ground, never agreement. In this simulated run its independence is zero.
- **Domain 4, nomenclature only.** No anchor, so not a domain. I independently reach the same exclusion as the primary: a group label with no measurement cannot decide the substitution question.

Order by dependency: describe the effect before choosing a literature frame (Fig 9 :27-28); measure per slot before aggregating; state what counts as no loss before testing; settle the bond before composing over it (Fig 12 :104 "Composition is recursive, and the level decides what is irreducible.").

My census matches the primary's three domains plus the excluded fourth, and its union reading. ## Round trip gap R_T

The Symbols table defines R_T as the gap between what was sent up and what came back down, `gap(x, (T_E ∘ T_A)(x))`, which measures what the coarser description cannot hold. Fig 4 :30 requires it to be measured rather than assumed. I ran the round trip on the condensation this folder actually uses.

1. **Sent up (x):** the verbatim figure set, chiefly Fig 1 :13/:19/:22 (per-slot residual), Fig 12 :69/:94/:117 (bond, arrangement, isomer), Fig 11 :49/:50/:52-53 (valence, conditions, cascade), Fig 2 :35/:/:40 (ground and agreement).
2. **Abstraction (T_A):** the shorthand in `07` and `09`: "interchangeability is a settled identity bond per slot, condition-relative, cascade-sensitive, and agreement is not a verdict."
3. **Concretisation (T_E):** a concrete claim of the form "species A and B are interchangeable in pollination in meadow M."

**R_T is nonzero.** Four things the shorthand holds do not survive the trip back down:

- the **identity bond's literal type** (≡ means two names, one referent; two organisms are not literally one referent, so what survives is indiscernibility under a specified test, and *"indiscernibility is not identity"* must be carried along);
- the **condition index** (the shorthand says condition-relative; the concrete claim must name which condition, and a second condition can flip the result, as Fetzer 2015 shows);
- the **cascade horizon** (the shorthand says cascade-sensitive; the concrete claim must state how many steps were actually measured);
- the **independence of the testing frame** (the shorthand says agreement is not a verdict; the concrete claim must say who measured and what pool they drew on).

Conversely, one thing survives cleanly: a substitution with no per-slot residual is the shape of interchangeability, and a group label alone is not a settlement. That is the residue worth sending. R_T was **not** measured numerically, because no ground reading exists; it is recorded qualitatively as the enumerated set of dropped constraints. Treating the formula as identical to the figures would set R_T to zero and commit the dead-reckoning error Fig 4 :30 names. The honest form is a lossy condensation with a measured gap, which is what `09` states.
