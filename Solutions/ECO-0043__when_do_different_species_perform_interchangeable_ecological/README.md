## What is deliberately not here

- No personal emails of named individuals.
- No ranking of people by how likely they are to agree that redundancy is real or misleading.
- No bulk-send plan.

Three reasons, in the figures' own words:

1. **A room does not upgrade the label.** Fig 2 :13 "agreement compounds, evidence does not". A list of ecologists selected for a preferred conclusion raises the vocabulary and does not add an anchor.
2. **Correlation disqualifies a verdict.** Fig 2 :40 "disqualifying for a verdict: agreement about the world is worthless when the agreeing frames share an evidence pool". Selecting recipients for receptiveness manufactures exactly the correlation that would make their agreement worthless.
3. **Asking for trust before a check spends independence.** Fig 10 :42 "A verifier that can reconstruct the claim cannot verify it." The outside frame must not hold the derivation's interior.

Legally: unsolicited bulk email to scraped personal addresses breaches GDPR and CAN-SPAM style rules in many jurisdictions. Public opt-in channels avoid that failure as well as the structural one.

## The verdict in one line

Interchangeability is a ground-settled identity bond at a per-species slot, conditional on habitat and on the full contact cascade. Trait lists and group labels are filters for where to look, never witnesses for the verdict. Apparent redundancy misleads when the reading is aggregated across slots, stops at direct contact, or is borrowed from a single environmental condition. Mode `specified`, ungrounded. This is a simulated derivation checked for internal consistency against the figures, not a ground reading; it cannot settle the question it reports on.
