## What is deliberately not here

- No personal emails of named individuals.
- No ranking of people by how likely they are to believe the claim.
- No bulk-send plan.

Three reasons, in the figures' own words:

1. **A room does not upgrade the label.** Fig 7 :13: "agreement compounds, evidence does not". A list of agreeable readers raises the vocabulary and does not add an anchor.
2. **Selecting for receptiveness manufactures the disqualifying correlation.** Fig 2 :40: "disqualifying for a verdict: agreement about the world is worthless when the agreeing frames share an evidence pool". A list ranked by likely agreement produces a convention, not corroboration.
3. **Asking for trust before checking spends the only thing a reader contributes.** Fig 10 :42: "A verifier that can reconstruct the claim cannot verify it." The outside frame must not hold the interiors.

Legally, scraping personal emails for unsolicited bulk outreach breaches GDPR and CAN-SPAM style rules in many jurisdictions. That is a practical failure on top of the structural one.

## Honest standing

This is a **simulated derivation checked for internal consistency against the figures, not a ground reading**. The Recursive Self-Improvement Trap (read this session) states that LLM output is simulacra and that the truly testable output would need to be derived from the real architecture or working the formula yourself. It cannot settle the question it reports on. The verdicts are candidate verdicts. No measurement was taken.
