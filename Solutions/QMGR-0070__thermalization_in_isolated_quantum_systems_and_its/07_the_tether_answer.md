# The Tether's answer on thermalization and black hole dynamics

**Mode: `specified`, not `grounded`.** This is a shape and a route, not a result. Simulated derivation checked against the figures (`05_what_shape_means.md` section 6), not a ground reading.

## 1. The residual it answers

A closed quantum system evolves reversibly yet approaches thermal equilibrium; a black hole is expected to evolve unitarily yet radiates thermally. The question bundles these two contact failures. Fig 9 :55 **"A bundled problem is usually a union of overlapping conditions, not a list of separate ones."** Fig 1 :19 **"The residual is indexed by object slot, not aggregated."** So the two residuals stay indexed and the verdicts stay per domain. The overlap, quantum chaos and complexity, is a third domain with its own loop.

## 2. The shape, per domain

**Domain 1, isolated quantum thermalization.** A first-order cross-kind composition: energy and a spectrum over a state, with a directed entanglement gradient supplying the time asymmetry. Verdict: the internal mechanism is well supported in this frame, and the first-order shape is `specified` and triangulated across Fig 13 and the eigenstate-thermalization literature. Ground visible through experiment and numerics.

**Domain 2, black hole dynamics.** A first-order cross-kind composition with the same atom set: energy and a horizon spectrum over a state, with a directed entropy or information gradient. Verdict: semiclassical predictions have a ground, but the microscopic dynamics at the quantum-gravity scale has no instrument that settles the shape. Fig 2 :29 **"An anchor is whatever settles the question and does not change its answer because of what any frame thinks."** Verdict `specified` with a declared gap, not `grounded` and not a refusal.

**Domain 3, chaos and complexity.** The shared vocabulary of spectral statistics, scrambling and area laws. Verdict: a real overlap, but one evidence pool cannot corroborate the other. It constrains the shape and does not decide it.

**The relation.** It is not a direct bond of the two thermal endpoints. Fig 13 :75 **"Two states never bond directly. It is not space-time but space, a structure, time; and two structures join only through a state."** Each first-order molecule is settled enough to act as an atom at the next level: Fig 12 :104 **"A settled molecule becomes an atom for whatever composes over it."** The relation is therefore a second-order comparison over a shared atom set. Over the full shared set the two domains are isomers in the figure's sense: Fig 12 :117 **"isomer. Same atoms, different bond, different substance. The difference is invisible in the formula and decisive in the behaviour."** As they are ordinarily described they share only part of the set, which makes the weaker Fig 12 :123 claim: **"functional group. A substructure whose presence fixes the outcome. The group says what kind; the qualifier says which member."**

## 3. What it explicitly does not say

- Not that a black hole is simply the fastest thermalizing gas. The shared word is a reading, not a source.
- Not that one theory is derived from the other, and no new primitive or unified field.
- Not that the structure in the slot is named. The framework returns a shape, not a name.
- Not `grounded` at the quantum-gravity scale. Fig 8 :39 **"So a search that finds nothing is never a proof of absence."** The gap is a verdict, not an absence.
- Not a single averaged verdict across the two domains. Each verdict above stands on its own.

## 4. What would settle it: the ground

A source-discriminating instrument, not a further argument.

1. A measurement or theorem that forces a direct state-state or structure-structure bond between the two domains, with no intervening opposite-kind term. That breaks the second-order reading.
2. A demonstration that a seventh primitive is needed, not composable from the six. That breaks the shared-atom reading.
3. A ground reading at the quantum-gravity scale that returns a different shape. That decides the open slot.
4. A result showing the two conditions share one anchor, so clearing one clears the other. That breaks the union reading.
5. A re-census of the comparison bond that forbids settled molecules as operands at second order. That is the live type strain, named here rather than hidden.

## 5. The honest stop

**Unreached, not unreachable.** The six-term set is a visible guess at the compression axes, not a discovery of nature's own list. The relation stops at the limit of the available instruments and records the lineage so the stop is checkable. Indiscernibility is not identity. The round trip from the figures to a one-sentence slogan and back is lossy: the union structure, the anchor asymmetry and the second-order order do not survive it. That gap is reported, not set to zero.

## 6. One-paragraph version for outreach

> The Tether does not identify thermalization in isolated quantum systems with black hole dynamics, and it does not claim a final theory. It says the question is a union of two conditions with an overlap in quantum chaos, not one hard problem. Each condition is a first-order cross-kind composition over one six-term set; the first has a visible ground and the second has no instrument at the quantum-gravity scale that settles its microscopic dynamics. Their relation is therefore a second-order comparison over a shared atom set, and over the full set the two are isomers in the framework's sense: same atoms, arranged differently. The framework does not name the mechanism in the slot, because it returns a shape, not a name. What it offers is the shape any candidate answer must have, the falsifiers written in advance, and the kind of instrument that would close the gap. The slot stays open until the ground settles it.
