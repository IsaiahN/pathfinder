# Expert verification packet: the Tether on thermalization and black holes

**What this is:** a claim in a form a physicist can check. Mode, derivation and refuters are stated before the conclusion, so a reader knows what to look for and what would settle it.

**Mode: `specified`, not `grounded`.** No measurement at the quantum-gravity scale is claimed. This is a shape and a route, not a result.

## 1. The claim, in one paragraph

The question is a union compound of two conditions, not one problem. Each condition is a first-order cross-kind composition over one six-term atom set: energy and a spectrum over a state, with a directed gradient. Isolated quantum thermalization has a visible ground in experiment and numerics; black hole dynamics has no instrument at the quantum-gravity scale that settles the microscopic information question. Their relation is therefore not a direct bond of two thermal endpoints but a second-order comparison over a shared atom set. Over the full shared set the two are isomers in the framework's sense; as ordinarily described they share a functional group instead. The framework does not name the mechanism in the slot. It offers the shape any candidate answer must have, falsifiers written in advance, and the kind of instrument that would close the gap.
