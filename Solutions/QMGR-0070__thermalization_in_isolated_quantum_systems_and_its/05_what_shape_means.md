## 1. A shape is a constraint, not a filler

The canonical README states the limit: the framework returns what a missing thing would have to look like, a shape not a name. Fig 9 :32 **"you get a kind of thing, never the answer itself"**. Fig 13 :134 **"Unreachable is a verdict here, not a silence"**.

From the Library of Babel essay: you can cut a search short when the returned set is small and descriptive enough to give a general shape of the answer that satisfies the question.

So a **shape** is the narrowed form of an answer you can state before you can name it. It constrains; it does not fill. For this question:

- **Shape:** two first-order cross-kind compositions with unequal anchors, related at second order by a comparison over a shared atom set.
- **Name, withheld:** a single mechanism that is declared to be the same in both domains.

The shape is the thing a candidate answer must have. The name is the thing the framework refuses to supply.

## 2. How the shape is obtained

- **Describe the effect, then pick the frame.** Fig 9 :27-28 **"describe it before you go looking, or any frame you pick will seem to fit"**. The residual here is not "what is the quantum theory of black holes" but "why do two closed descriptions each produce a thermal coarse graining, and why is one anchor visible and the other not".
- **Cast it in effect terms.** Fig 6 :30 **"The description travels. Fails by being sharpened into terms only we can read. Cast it in effect terms."**
- **Split before you search.** Fig 9 :55 **"A bundled problem is usually a union of overlapping conditions, not a list of separate ones."** The union is the shape's first constraint.
- **Keep the slots indexed.** Fig 1 :19 **"The residual is indexed by object slot, not aggregated."** Averaging the thermalization residual and the information residual loses the signal that one has a ground and the other does not.

## 3. What licenses a name

Only two things, both outside the shape itself:

1. **The ground settles it.** `THE_FORMULA.md` step 5: the ground settles a term, verified only by what pays. A gate is not the ground.
2. **A provenanced import from a frame whose closure differs**, with a source gate and a shadow test (Fig 6 :23, Fig 8 :51).

For this question the first route is available in domain 1 and unavailable at the quantum-gravity scale in domain 2. The second route is not available either, because none of the six world sources adds a primitive outside the six-term set. So the mechanism slot stays open. That is a verdict, not an omission.

From the Recursive Self-Improvement Trap: the output is only as good as the validator, and the frame or theory cannot validate itself. This derivation cannot name the mechanism and then certify its own name.

## 4. Why the identity claim is the specific forbidden error here

The identity claim reads a shared word, "thermalize", and promotes it to a shared mechanism. The Hard Problem is About Source gives the mechanism: an instrument reads green and cannot comment on the source, because several distinct sources can produce the same reading. Behavior is a projection with lossy information. Here the shared word is the reading and the source is left undetermined.

The world literature makes the point concretely. In the ETH review, thermalization is an expectation-value statement in an isolated chaotic system. In the fast-scrambling abstract, scrambling is glossed as thermalizing information under an assumption of unitary, rapidly mixing dynamics. Same reading, different source. What is owed is a discriminator, not a name. Fig 10 :24 **"the ground does not decay, the channel does"** applies to the reading, not to the world.

## 5. The anchor question is part of the shape, not an afterthought

A common mistake is to treat the anchor as a practical detail to be resolved later. Fig 2 :29 **"An anchor is whatever settles the question and does not change its answer because of what any frame thinks."** The anchor decides whether a claim is a verdict or a specification. Since the two domains have different anchor statuses, any single-sentence answer that merges them has already averaged away the most important structural fact. The shape therefore includes the asymmetry:

- domain 1: first-order composition, settled in its frame;
- domain 2: first-order composition, settled for semiclassical predictions but not for the microscopic information question;
- the relation: second-order, `specified`, with a declared gap.

This is why `07_the_tether_answer.md` keeps verdicts per domain and never reports a mean.

## 6. Two consequences for outreach

**Prestige is not action.** Prestige can give a claim a budget to be tried, but it cannot settle the action. Only the ground can settle it. A campaign that gathers well known names to agree with the shape is the named collapse, not corroboration. The legitimate ask gives the claim a budget to be tried.

**My own audit is a simulation, not a ground reading.** The Recursive Self-Improvement Trap states that LLMs can simulate an approximate answer and that the truly testable output would need to be derived from the real architecture or working the formula yourself. So `01` and this file are simulated derivations checked for internal consistency against the figures. They cannot settle the question they report on.
