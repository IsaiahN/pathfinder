## Three things in the literature that bear directly on this question

1. **The word "thermalize" is used in both frames, but not with the same ground.** In the ETH literature it is an expectation-value statement checkable against numerics and cold atom experiments. In the fast-scrambling literature it is a conjecture about information retrieval under an assumption of unitary, rapidly mixing dynamics. Fig 2 :29 **"An anchor is whatever settles the question and does not change its answer because of what any frame thinks."** The shared word is not a shared anchor, and a reading that treats it as one has misidentified the domain.
2. **Integrability is the live exception, not a footnote.** The D'Alessio review states that integrable systems violate ETH and need a generalized Gibbs ensemble. This is the closest world-side analogue of the framework's union finding: one lever does not clear both conditions. It corroborates F1 without deciding the shape.
3. **Entanglement area laws are the genuine low-overlap bridge.** The Eisert review places the same structural pattern in black hole physics and many-body physics. That is a corroboration of the shared-atom reading in `06`, and it comes from a different evidence pool from the ETH papers and the scrambling papers. It is still corroboration only, never an import that adds a primitive (Fig 6 :14 **"the only operator that moves the wall"**).

## Distribution: offer, do not push

Figure-grounded reasons:

- **Fig 4 :22: "Only methods cross up. Only head starts come down."** Sending the full architecture before anyone has described a residual sends a recording, not a generator. Lead with the refuter and the shape claim; offer the links after.
- **Fig 10 :42: "A verifier that can reconstruct the claim cannot verify it."** A recipient who has absorbed the whole framework before being asked for a verdict has joined the frame being checked.
- **Fig 9 :27-28: "describe it before you go looking, or any frame you pick will seem to fit."** Ask what they would expect to see if the shape claim were true before giving them the article.
- **Fig 7 :13: "agreement compounds, evidence does not"** and Fig 2 :40 **"disqualifying for a verdict: agreement about the world is worthless when the agreeing frames share an evidence pool"**. A mass send selected for people who already agree produces a convention, not corroboration.
- **Fig 8 :39: "So a search that finds nothing is never a proof of absence."** A reader who cannot find a flaw has not confirmed the shape; record the failure to refute as a non-result.

**Recommended order in a reply:** (1) the union compound, the shape claim and its refuter; (2) ask what they would count as a failure and record the answer; (3) only then the links, with the note that the figures, not the summary, are the authority.

## The two-stage offer (with the optional third stage)

- **Stage 1 before stage 2** is Fig 10 :52 **"Bring a characterised residual, never the source."** The recipient checks the shape without holding the framework's interior. No trust or agreement is requested.
- **Stage 2 is opt-in by construction.** Once a reader has absorbed the theory, their agreement stops being evidence. The stage-1 verdict must be recorded before any theory is shown and is never pooled as corroboration.
- **Stage 3 sends a method, which is permitted.** Fig 4 :22 permits methods to cross up. The formula is a generator, so giving it away does not give away an answer. Each user still needs a ground per problem and must state a refuter before running it.
- **Hard precondition:** stages 1 to 3 only work if the stage-1 output survives. The identity claim does not (F1 to F3 in `01`); the union shape does. Send the latter.
