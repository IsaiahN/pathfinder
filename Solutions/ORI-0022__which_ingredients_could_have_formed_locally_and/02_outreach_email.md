# Outreach draft: check one output before you decide whether the framing is worth reading

**Use:** one-to-one, or as a cover letter attached to a submission through a public venue in `03_public_contact_points.csv`. **Not** for bulk send.

**Hard precondition:** do not send until F3, F5 and F6 in `01_figure_grounded_audit.md` are stated in the first paragraph, not buried after the list. An offer that leads with a molecule list has already lost the distinction it is asking the reader to check.

---

**Subject:** One per-ingredient shape claim about local synthesis versus delivery, with a refuter

> Hi [name],
>
> I have one specific output I would like you to check against what you already know. I am not asking you to read a theory first, and I am not asking you to agree with anything. If the output does not survive, there is nothing else to look at.
>
> **The output (`specified`, not `grounded`):**
>
> "Could this ingredient have formed locally?" and "did it arrive on a meteorite or comet?" are not two boxes that partition the ingredients. They are two independent reachability questions per ingredient slot. Local formation is composition from atoms already in the site's chemical closure; delivery is an import from a frame whose closure differs. A molecule can be both. So the answer I am offering is a per-slot table with three columns: locally reachable, import available, and what observation would tell them apart.
>
> The discriminator is the whole point. An alanine made in a spark-discharge flask and an alanine extracted from Murchison are the same arrangement of the same atoms. The molecule does not carry its origin. Isotopic enrichment, chirality, isomer distribution and a known delivery vector carry it. If you cannot name the discriminator for a slot, the honest entry is "undetermined", not a guess.
>
> **What I am not claiming:** that meteorites delivered the majority of life's building blocks, that any specific molecule must have come from space, or that laboratory synthesis at one atmosphere reproduces the Hadean site. Those are flux and ground questions, and they are open. I also am not claiming the figures give the ingredient list. They give the shape of the classification; the names come from measurements.
>
> **Three things I would count as failures, written down before this message:**
> 1. A molecule I call locally reachable is found at the origin-relevant site with an isotopic and chiral signature no local route can make, and no delivery vector exists.
> 2. A molecule I call delivered is made locally under plausible conditions with the same isotopic and isomeric signature, in sufficient flux, so the import adds nothing.
> 3. An instrument shows origin is indiscernible in principle for a slot, so no discriminator can be named.
>
> I wrote these before sending this. A one-line "this fails because..." is more useful to me than agreement. If you would rather not engage, no reply is needed.
>
> **If it survives, and only then:** the source material is below. The figures are the authority, not my summary of them.
> - The figure text with line numbers (local extract): `FRAMEWORK/reference/03-FIGURES_TEXT.md`
> - Local synthesis and the ingredient inventory: https://pmc.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/articles/PMC13694700
> - Meteorite nucleobases and provenance: https://www.nature.com/articles/s41467-022-29612-x
> - Cometary glycine and phosphorus: https://www.esa.int/Science_Exploration/Space_Science/Rosetta/Rosetta_s_comet_contains_ingredients_for_life
>
> **If you want to use the machinery:** the formula is in the figures link. Run it on a problem you know well and can check against a ground. Write down what would refute your result *before* you run it, and state the mode of what you get. A reported failure is more useful to me than a reported success.
>
> - [name]

## No trust request

The message never asks anyone to trust the framework. Prestige is not action: *"Prestige can fund an action. It can give an action a budget to be tried. But it cannot settle an action. Only the ground can settle it"* (*The Recursive Self-Improvement Trap*). The ask gives the claim a budget to be tried by people who can actually check isotopics and synthesis, and lets the measurement settle it.
