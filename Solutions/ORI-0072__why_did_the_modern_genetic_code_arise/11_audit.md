## Round trip gap R_T

The Symbols table defines R_T as the gap between what was sent up and what came back down, `gap(x, (T_E ∘ T_A)(x))`, measuring what the coarser description cannot hold. Fig 4 :30 requires it to be measured rather than assumed. I ran the round trip on the condensation the folder actually uses.

**R_T is nonzero.** Three things the shorthand holds do not survive the trip back down:

- the **convention guard** (Fig 2 :43), which the phrase "settled convention" tends to erase, since a reader hears "convention" and infers "arbitrary", the exact reading the guard refuses.

Conversely, one thing survives cleanly: a closed atomic set with open arrangements, and universality as a handoff rather than corroboration. That is the residue worth sending. R_T was **not** measured numerically, because no ground reading exists; it is recorded qualitatively as the enumerated set of dropped constraints. Treating "the formula is the condensed version of the figures" as identity would set R_T to zero and would be the dead-reckoning error Fig 4 :30 names.
