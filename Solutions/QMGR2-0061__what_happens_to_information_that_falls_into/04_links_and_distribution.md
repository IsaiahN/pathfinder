## 3. One genuine tension in the world pool, and why it is not a figure conflict

Braunstein and Pati prove that, assuming Hawking's semiclassical radiation is exactly featureless, information cannot hide in correlations between that radiation and the interior. Page, Hayden-Preskill and the island results say the information *is* in correlations, but they also require Hawking's semiclassical prediction to break down (small nonthermal corrections, or a new gravitational saddle after the Page time). The two are conditional on incompatible premises. This is a conflict between world sources, not with the figures. Per the WATSON rule it sends us back to the census: the re-census separates condition (i) "is the radiation exactly semiclassical" from condition (iii) "where is the entropy counted," which is exactly the unbundling F4 already requires. The figures do not take a side. Both sources are recorded as read, with overlap noted in the ledger.

## 5. The two-stage offer

**Stage 1, before any theory.** The recipient checks the shape claim against the ground and against their own knowledge. Their verdict is recorded before stage 2 exists. Fig 10 :52: *"Bring a characterised residual, never the source."*

**Stage 2, opt-in only.** If curious, they receive the figure extract, the canonical README and the architecture article. Their later agreement is never pooled as corroboration, because once they hold the interiors their agreement is the pool talking (Fig 2 :40).

**Stage 3, method only on request.** The formula is a generator, so giving it away gives no answer. Each user writes a refuter before running it and labels the mode of the result. The framework's own warning applies: outputs *"could never be verified if the solutions presented were not contrived to fit the formula."*

**Hard precondition.** Stages 1 to 3 only work if the stage-1 output survives. In this folder it does so only as a shape with a `gap` on the physics. Any claim that this settles the information paradox must be refused before sending.
