## What is deliberately not here

- No personal emails of named individuals.
- No ranking of people by how likely they are to agree.
- No bulk-send plan.

Three reasons, in the figures' own words:

1. **A room does not upgrade the label.** Seat Map :283: *"A room does not upgrade the label. A room reaching a conclusion without the ground is agreement, and agreement among frames is a weighted average of influence. Adding people raises the vocabulary and does not add an anchor."*
2. **Correlation disqualifies a verdict.** Seat Map :163: *"High correlation is disqualifying for a verdict and efficient for a convention."* Selecting recipients for receptiveness manufactures the correlation that makes their agreement worthless.
3. **Asking for trust before checking spends independence.** Fig 10 :42: *"A verifier that can reconstruct the claim cannot verify it."* Seat 4 must not hold the interiors of the frame it checks.

Legally: unsolicited bulk email to harvested personal addresses breaches GDPR and CAN-SPAM style rules in many jurisdictions. Public institutional and opt-in venues avoid that failure and the structural one.
