# The Tether's answer: what happens to information that falls into a black hole

**Mode: `general` on the structural shape, `specified` on the per-domain claims, nothing `grounded`.** This is a shape and a route, not a result. Simulated derivation checked against the figures (`05_what_shape_means.md` §6), not a ground reading.

## 1. The residual it answers

Formation and complete evaporation are described as producing a thermal final state that does not depend on the details of the initial state, and as evolving unitarily so that those details cannot be destroyed. The disagreement does not shrink with perturbative effort. Page 1993 found the information rate *"initially so slowly, or else be so spread out"* that perturbation theory can neither find nor exclude it. Under Fig 9 this is a **union compound**, not one hard question: Fig 9 :57 *"a case in two conditions is not a harder case of either."*

## 2. The shape

1. **Information is an arrangement, not a primitive.** Fig 12 :5: *"A set of ingredients is not one. The bond is the rest of the information, and it is the half nothing was recording."* The six-term set holds states and structures; the dependence on the initial state is carried by what the composition records, not by a seventh member.
2. **The thermal description is an ingredient-level average, and the average discards the signal.** Fig 1 :22: *"Aggregating across slots is how a live signal disappears into an average."* So if the dependence survives, it survives in correlations that a single-mode thermal summary does not record. This is a `general` structural claim, triangulated with Page 1993.
3. **The framework has no deletion operator; the admissible form is "preserved in the record, possibly unreachable," not "destroyed."** Fig 5 :55: *"Nothing leaves the library. What falls is the reach."* Fig 6 :26: *"Which is why some unreachability is depth and some is genuine absence, and from inside the frame the two look the same."* This is a shape about composition, not a physics verdict about Hawking radiation.
4. **The union must be unbundled before any lever is applied.** Fig 9 :54: *"when the split lands on a union rather than a partition, unbundle before you search again."* Purity of the final state, regularity of the horizon and the location of entropy are three conditions with their own loops. Firewall, fuzzball, remnant and island proposals answer different intersections.
5. **The physical outcome is `gap` at this ground.** Fig 8 :39: *"So a search that finds nothing is never a proof of absence."* Fig 13 :134: *"Unreachable is a verdict here, not a silence."* No instrument available here measures the final state of an evaporated astrophysical black hole, so the figures do not select among destruction, return in correlations, a remnant or an island. Fig 9 :32: *"you get a kind of thing, never the answer itself."*
6. **Bekenstein and holography are a retrodiction.** Fig 8 :56: *"The triangulation claim fits four cases already known and has not been tested forward: Wiles and Frey, Montgomery and Dyson, Bekenstein and holography, the relativization barriers in complexity theory."* That is the figure text's own nearest mention of this field, and it is a case already known.
7. **Analogue Hawking experiments extend the instrument; they do not settle the case.** Fig 11 :70: *"A test harness is a substituted habitat. Which is why a synthetic solve proves wiring and never capability."*

## 3. What it explicitly does not say

- Not that the information paradox is solved, and not a theory of everything. There is no last room.
- Not that information is definitely preserved. The world's dominant view corroborates the shape, but a consensus is a filter for the budget, not a witness for the verdict (Fig 9 :60).
- Not that the information is in the island, in fuzzballs, in a remnant, or in nonthermal correlations. Those are bindings from the physics frame, recorded with provenance, not derivations.
- Not that the thermal spectrum is illusionary. It is a correct coarse-grained reading; it simply cannot comment on its source.
- Not an import that adds an atom. Fig 6 :25: *"An import adds no atom to the world."*
- Not `grounded`. No measurement at the evaporation endpoint settles `left(R,φ)`.

## 4. What would settle it, and the ground

A source-discriminating instrument, not a further argument. From *The Hard Problem is About Source*, a colour instrument reading green *"cannot comment on the source"*; the spectrometer resolved it. Here the corresponding questions are:

- Does the complete final-state density matrix depend on the initial pure state after the Page time, with small subsystems still thermal? Page 1993 and the island calculations predict yes. A measurement that establishes no would break the shape.
- Is there a measurement in which the metric's role in the horizon description cannot be recovered from a cross-kind composition at all? That would be a fundamental-metric style falsifier for this case.
- Does a proposed resolution preserve regularity at the horizon while keeping unitarity? That is the firewall intersection, and it needs a ground, not an argument.
- Standing refuters from `01`: a source-discriminating endpoint measurement returning a genuinely mixed state; a proof that the final state is fixed by thermal ingredients alone; a seventh primitive; a figure line governing the physical outcome directly after re-census.

## 5. The honest stop

**Unreached, not unreachable.** The framework returns a shape and the route to a ground, then stops at the limit of what the available instruments can distinguish and records the lineage. Fig 13 :135: *"It cannot say which primitive is missing, because naming it would mean already holding it."* Fig 8 :44: *"Only a sealed room can be searched to the end, and no frame is a sealed room. So searching and finding nothing proves nothing."* Indiscernibility is not identity: several mechanisms remain indiscernible at this resolution, and that is a statement about the instrument, not about the world.

## 6. One-paragraph version for outreach

> The Tether does not solve the black hole information paradox. It says that information is not a primitive but the typed arrangement of a state, so an ingredient-level thermal spectrum is exactly the description that loses it: aggregating across quanta hides the dependence on the initial state. It says the framework has no deletion operator, so the admissible shape is "preserved in the record, possibly unreachable from a given frame," not "destroyed." It says the question is a union of purity, horizon regularity and entropy accounting, which must be unbundled before any proposal is tested. And it says the physical outcome is a `gap` at this ground: the figures return a kind of thing, never the answer. What is offered is the shape any candidate answer must have, falsifiers written in advance, and the instruments that would close it. The slot stays open until the ground settles it.
