# Outreach draft: check the output before deciding about the frame

**Use:** one-to-one, addressed to a researcher in black hole information paradox research, or as a cover note attached to a submission through a public venue in `03_public_contact_points.csv`. **Not** for bulk send.

**Hard precondition:** do not send until the recipient can see the stage-1 output in isolation. The output must survive validation or the two-stage offer collapses on contact (hard precondition from the exemplar, applied here).

---

**Subject:** One checkable output on information loss, before any theory

> Hello [name],
>
> I work on a figure-grounded framework and I have one specific output I would like you to check against what you already know. I am not asking you to read the framework first, and I am not asking you to agree with anything. A one-line failure is more useful to me than agreement. If it does not survive, there is nothing else to look at.
>
> **The residual, in effect terms.** The same process, formation and complete evaporation, is described as producing a thermal final state that does not depend on the details of the initial state, and as producing a unitary evolution in which those details cannot be destroyed. I am not asking which calculation is right. I am asking what an admissible answer would have to look like before either calculation is corrected.
>
> **The stage-1 output (`general` on the shape, `specified` on the per-domain claims, not `grounded`):**
>
> 1. Information is not a primitive of the framework. It is what a typed arrangement carries. Fig 12 :5 puts it this way: "A set of ingredients is not one. The bond is the rest of the information, and it is the half nothing was recording." Fig 1 :22 adds: "Aggregating across slots is how a live signal disappears into an average." A thermal spectrum is an ingredient-level average, so the dependence on the initial state, if it survives, is in correlations the average does not record.
> 2. The framework has no deletion operator. Fig 5 :55: "Nothing leaves the library. What falls is the reach." The admissible shape is therefore "preserved in the record, possibly unreachable from a given frame," not "destroyed." This is a shape about composition, not a claim about what a real black hole does.
> 3. The physical question is a union, not one question: purity of the final state, regularity of the horizon, and where the entropy is counted. Fig 9 :57: "a case in two conditions is not a harder case of either." These need separate loops before they are recombined.
> 4. Fig 8 :39: "So a search that finds nothing is never a proof of absence." From inside the semiclassical frame, depth and genuine absence look the same (Fig 6 :26). My verdict on the physical outcome is `gap`, stated before I show you any theory.
>
> **What would refute it, written down before this message:**
>
> 1. A measurement of the evaporation endpoint with a source-discriminating instrument returning a final state genuinely independent of the initial pure state.
> 2. A demonstration that the full final state is fixed by its single-mode thermal ingredients alone, with no correlation carrying the dependence.
> 3. A derivation of a seventh primitive, or a demonstration that smoothness is load-bearing for an established law.
> 4. A figure line that governs the physical outcome directly after re-census and selects a different outcome.
>
> If any holds, I withdraw the claim. Please tell me what you would count as a failure. I would especially value a correction to the shape rather than a judgement on the framework.
>
> **What I am not claiming:** that the framework solves the information paradox, that it selects a firewall, a fuzzball, a remnant or an island, that it derives unitarity, or that Bekenstein and holography are a forward result of it. The figure text names that case as already known and says the triangulation claim "has not been tested forward" (Fig 8 :56), so I report it as a retrodiction. I also do not claim that analogue Hawking experiments settle the astrophysical case; a harness proves wiring, not capability (Fig 11 :70).
>
> **If it survives, and only then:** I can send the three public sources. The figures, not my summary of them, are the authority.
>
> **If you want to reuse the method:** I can send the formula. Please write down what would refute your own result before you run it, and state the mode of what you get. A reported failure is more useful to me than a reported success.
>
> If you would rather not engage, no reply is needed. Opting out of further contact is fine and I will respect it.
