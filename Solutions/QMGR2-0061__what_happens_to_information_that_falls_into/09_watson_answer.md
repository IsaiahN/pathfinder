## QUESTION

Decide, in effect terms, what an admissible answer to "what happens to information that falls into a black hole" looks like. Option A: the information is destroyed, so a pure state evolves to a mixed thermal state. Option B: it is preserved in correlations in the radiation, so the full final state depends on the initial state. Option C: it is preserved but unreachable from a given frame, in a remnant, an island or a hidden subsystem. What each leads to: A breaks unitarity and denies the framework's no-deletion rule; B requires Hawking's semiclassical prediction to break down; C requires a place or a frame change that no available instrument discriminates. The figures decide the admissible shape; no ground reading available here decides among A, B and C.

## VERDICT (from the figures)

The shape survives; the physical mechanism does not settle. Information is an arrangement, not a primitive, and an ingredient-level thermal average is exactly the description that loses the dependence on the initial state. The framework has no deletion operator, so the admissible shape is "preserved in the record, possibly unreachable from a given frame," not "destroyed." The question is a union of purity, horizon regularity and entropy accounting and must be unbundled before any lever is applied. The figures do not select among destruction, return in correlations, a remnant or an island. Bekenstein and holography are a retrodiction, and analogue experiments are harnesses. The physical outcome is `gap` at this ground; the shape is `general` and the per-domain claims are `specified`, all ungrounded candidates.

- **cost if wrong:** bounded and locatable, because the packet states its refuters before its conclusion. The real cost is spending the independence of outside readers by sending a recording instead of a generator, which Fig 2 :42 prices: "Buy independence in proportion to the cost of being wrong." That cost is avoidable by leading with the shape and its refuter and never pooling later agreement.
- **undoable:** yes. The bindings to islands, remnants or nonthermal correlations are removable without touching the shape, and no primitive is withdrawn. A sent claim can be superseded by a corrected packet with provenance.
- **reversed by:** a ground reading, not agreement. A source-discriminating measurement of the evaporation endpoint showing whether the full final state depends on the initial pure state would name the mechanism; a measurement returning a genuinely mixed final state would break the no-deletion shape as applied to physics; a derivation of one of the six from the other five would break the substrate; a figure re-census admitting a physical deletion operator would reverse the archive claim. Until one fires, the verdict stands as candidate.
