## 1. The residual, stated as an effect

The same physical process, collapse and complete evaporation, is predicted to do two incompatible things:

- Semiclassical gravity: a pure initial state evolves to a thermal, mixed final state whose detailed dependence on the input is gone. Hawking 1976: *"Because part of the information about the state of the system is lost down the hole, the final situation is represented by a density matrix rather than a pure quantum state."*
- Quantum theory: a closed system evolves unitarily, so a pure state must come out pure and the initial state must in principle be recoverable from the final one. Page 1993: *"information would be permanently lost down the black hole, and there would be no S matrix to take an initial pure state to a final pure state."* (Page reporting Hawking's option, which he then refuses.)

**Residual R (effect terms):** there is a mapping whose coarse-grained output is insensitive to distinctions in its input, while the framework we use to describe that mapping says such distinctions cannot be destroyed. The disagreement does not shrink with more perturbative effort (Page 1993: the information rate is *"initially so slowly, or else be so spread out"* that perturbation theory cannot find or exclude it). That is the signal, not a nuisance.

## 4. Findings

### F1: Information is not a primitive. It is the bond and the arrangement (general, triangulated)

Fig 12 :5: *"A set of ingredients is not one. The bond is the rest of the information, and it is the half nothing was recording."*
Fig 12 :94: *"The arrangement is the substance."*
Fig 13 :3: *"No system functions without any of them, none is built from the others, and everything else is a composition across the two kinds."*

"Information" is not one of the six terms. Under the figures it is what a composition leaves: ordering (spectrum), difference (gradient), relation (network). For this question that means the relevant object is not a list of quanta in the radiation but the typed relation among them. This is `general` and structural. It agrees with Page 1993, who writes that with a larger radiation subsystem *"The total information is instead mostly encoded in the correlations between all the parts."* Two figure lines with low overlap (Fig 12 on bonds, Fig 13 on the minimum set) support it, so it is triangulated.

**Refuter:** show that the framework derives information as a seventh primitive, or that the bond carries no information beyond its operands (the flat-form collapse Fig 12 names). Either fires against F1.

### F2: Aggregating across radiation quanta is how the live signal disappears (general, triangulated)

Fig 1 :19: *"The residual is indexed by object slot, not aggregated."*
Fig 1 :22: *"Aggregating across slots is how a live signal disappears into an average."*

Hawking's calculation returns a thermal spectrum, an average over modes. Page's result and the island results say the information is in correlations spread over the whole radiation, which is exactly what an ingredient-only average drops. F1 and F2 together give the shape: **if the information survives, it is in the bond structure that the coarse-grained description does not record.**

**Refuter:** demonstrate a measurable dependence of the full final state on the initial state that is already present in the single-mode thermal spectrum with no inter-mode correlations. That would make the bond reading unnecessary and F2 false as applied here.
