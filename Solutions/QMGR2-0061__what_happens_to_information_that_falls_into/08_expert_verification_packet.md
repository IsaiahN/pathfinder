# Expert verification packet: the Tether on information falling into a black hole

**What this is:** a claim in a form a researcher in black hole information paradox research can check. Mode, derivation and refuters are stated before the conclusion, so a reader knows what to look for and what would settle it.

**Mode: `general` on the shape, `specified` on the per-domain claims, not `grounded`.** No endpoint measurement is claimed. This is a shape and a route, not a result. Simulated derivation, not a ground reading.

## 1. The claim, in one paragraph

Information is not a primitive of the six-term substrate; it is what a typed arrangement carries. A thermal spectrum is an ingredient-level average, and an average discards the dependence on the initial state. The framework has no deletion operator, so the admissible shape is "preserved in the record, possibly unreachable from a given frame," not "destroyed." The physical question is a union of three conditions: purity of the final state, regularity at the horizon, and where the entropy is counted. These must be unbundled before any proposal is tested. The framework does not select among destruction, return in correlations, a remnant or an island. It returns the shape any candidate answer must have, falsifiers written in advance, and the instruments that would close it. The slot stays open.

## 2. Three checkable sub-claims

### C1: framework-internal, checkable by reading Figures 12 and 1

- **Statement:** information is carried by the bond and the arrangement, not by the ingredient list; aggregating across slots removes the live signal.
- **Source, verbatim:** Fig 12 :5 *"A set of ingredients is not one. The bond is the rest of the information, and it is the half nothing was recording."* Fig 1 :22 *"Aggregating across slots is how a live signal disappears into an average."*
- **What to check:** does the framework's notion of a priced bond actually make the arrangement information-bearing, rather than merely relabelling the operands? Is the mapping from "thermal spectrum" to "ingredient-level average" faithful to what Hawking's calculation records?
- **Refuter:** show that the full final-state density matrix is fixed by single-mode thermal ingredients alone with no inter-mode or interior correlation carrying the dependence; or derive information as a seventh primitive.

### C2: framework-internal plus physics-facing, checkable by a researcher against the no-hiding theorem and the Page curve

- **Statement:** "destroyed" has no composable form in the framework; "preserved in the record, possibly unreachable" does. The world pool is internally divided on the conditions under which this maps to physics.
- **Source, verbatim:** Fig 5 :55 *"Nothing leaves the library. What falls is the reach."* Fig 6 :26 *"Which is why some unreachability is depth and some is genuine absence, and from inside the frame the two look the same."*
- **What to check:** Braunstein and Pati prove that, if Hawking's semiclassical radiation is exactly featureless, information cannot hide in correlations. Page 1993, Hayden-Preskill 2007 and the island results require precisely that Hawking's semiclassical prediction break down. Do you agree that these are conditional on incompatible premises, so that the tension is in the world pool and not with the figure verdict?
- **Refuter:** a source-discriminating endpoint measurement returning a final state genuinely independent of the initial pure state; or a demonstration that the no-hiding and island claims are compatible without breaking Hawking's semiclassical assumption.

### C3: framework-internal procedure, checkable by reading Figures 9 and 8

- **Statement:** the question is a union compound, so it must be unbundled before searching; the physical outcome is `gap` at this ground, which is a verdict rather than a silence.
- **Source, verbatim:** Fig 9 :54 *"when the split lands on a union rather than a partition, unbundle before you search again."* Fig 8 :39 *"So a search that finds nothing is never a proof of absence."* Fig 13 :134 *"Unreachable is a verdict here, not a silence"*
- **What to check:** are purity of final state, regularity at the horizon and location of entropy three conditions with separate grounds, or does one variable govern them? Is there an available instrument that discriminates the competing mechanisms at the evaporation endpoint?
- **Refuter:** show that one variable governs all three conditions with no compound residue; or exhibit a figure line that governs the physical outcome directly after re-census and selects a different outcome.

## 3. What is explicitly not claimed

- Not a solution to the information paradox; no final room and no new primitive.
- Not that information is definitely preserved, and not a ranking of the competing proposals.
- Not that the information is in the island, in fuzzballs, in a remnant, or in nonthermal correlations. Those are bindings from the physics frame with provenance, not derivations.
- Not that Hawking's calculation is wrong as a coarse-grained result. It is a correct reading that cannot comment on its source.
- Not that analogue Bose-Einstein condensate experiments settle the astrophysical case. Fig 11 :70: *"A test harness is a substituted habitat. Which is why a synthetic solve proves wiring and never capability."*
- Not that Bekenstein and holography are a forward result of the framework. Fig 8 :56 says the triangulation claim *"has not been tested forward."*
- Not `grounded`. Nothing here is asserted as settled by experiment.

## 5. What would settle it

A source-discriminating instrument, not a further argument. From *The Hard Problem is About Source*, an instrument reading green *"cannot comment on the source"*; the spectrometer resolved that case. Here:

1. Does the complete final state depend on the initial pure state after the Page time while small subsystems remain thermal? A measurement that answers no breaks the shape.
2. Is there an endpoint measurement that discriminates a genuinely mixed final state from a pure state with thermal subsystems? None is available in this derivation.
3. Is horizon regularity compatible with unitarity at the ground? The firewall intersection needs an experiment or a proof, not a preference.
4. Standing refuters from §2 above.

## 6. What an expert can check in twenty minutes

1. Read Fig 12 :5, Fig 1 :22, Fig 5 :55, Fig 6 :26 and Fig 9 :54. Five short passages.
2. Check C1 against what Hawking's calculation actually records: ingredients or arrangement?
3. Check C2 against the no-hiding theorem's premise and the island result's premise. Are they incompatible as recorded?
4. Name one instrument that would discriminate a genuinely mixed final state from a pure one with thermal subsystems, or say why none is possible.
5. Send the refutation. A failure is more useful than an endorsement.
