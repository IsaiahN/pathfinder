## 1. A shape is a narrowed form, not a filler

The figures README states the limit: the framework returns *"what a missing thing would have to look like (a shape, not a name)."* Fig 9 :32: *"you get a kind of thing, never the answer itself."* Fig 13 :135: *"It cannot say which primitive is missing, because naming it would mean already holding it."*

From *The Library of Babel*: *"This means that you can cut your search short if the set of books returned is small and descriptive enough that it provides a general shape of the answer that satisfies your question."*

For this question the shape is:

> The answer, whatever its mechanism, must explain how a dependence on the initial state can survive an ingredient-level thermal description. An admissible answer is therefore about the typed arrangement of the final state, not about the list of quanta in it.

That is a shape. "The information is in the island," "it comes out in nonthermal correlations," "it is destroyed," "it stays in a remnant" are names and mechanisms. The framework does not select among them without a ground.

## 2. How the shape is obtained for this question

- *Library of Babel* on proximity: use the vocabulary already held that is closest to the thing, then look for recurring new terms that overlap with it. *"With each new term gained, you are able to narrow the space by definition and deepen your vocabulary."* Here the closest held terms are spectrum (ordering), gradient (directedness) and network (relation), plus the bond. Narrowing gives "dependence is carried by arrangement," not a mechanism.
- Fig 6 :30: *"The description travels. Fails by being sharpened into terms only we can read. Cast it in effect terms."* Fig 9 :31: *"your words are borrowed too, so describe the effect rather than the cause."* The effect here is the invariance or loss of dependence on the initial state, which anyone with a density matrix can state.
- *Decoding Tacit Knowledge*: *"When contact isn't possible, the best you can send is a plain description of what you'd expect to see, because anyone can check that against the world."* A plain description: if the information survives, the full final-state density matrix must depend on the initial pure state even while each small subsystem looks thermal. Page 1993 and the island calculations give exactly that predicted observation.
- *Library of Babel* on stopping: *"you will know that you are done because the finish line will also be the starting place."* For this question the honest stop comes earlier, because no ground reading exists. Indiscernibility is not identity: several mechanisms remain indiscernible at this instrument resolution.

## 3. What licenses a name here

Only two things, both outside the shape itself:

1. **The ground settles it.** `THE_FORMULA.md` step 5: *"the ground settles it. Verified only by what pays."* A source-discriminating measurement of the evaporation endpoint would name the mechanism.
2. **A provenanced import from a frame whose closure differs**, with a source gate and shadow test (Fig 6 :23, Fig 8 :50). Island formula and replica wormholes are imports from the physics frame, and must be recorded as such.

*The Hard Problem is About Source* gives the reason a name from a reading is dangerous: *"If several paths create the same behavior, then behavior is a projection with lossy information."* A thermal spectrum is a reading. It cannot comment on its source. The spectrometer resolved the colour case, not an argument. Here the equivalent instrument would distinguish a genuinely mixed final state from a pure state whose small subsystems all look thermal. We do not have it for an astrophysical black hole.

## 4. Why the popular answer is not a verdict

Both "information is lost" and "information is preserved" are delivered as settled verdicts in the world literature at different times. Under this framework both are names, and the mode rule applies. From *The Recursive Self-Improvement Trap*: *"Prestige can fund an action. It can give an action a budget to be tried. But it cannot settle an action. Only the ground can settle it."* A consensus among researchers, however strong, is a convention about a shared meaning until a ground reading pays for it (Fig 2 :40: *"agreement about the world is worthless when the agreeing frames share an evidence pool"*).

The figures do allow one structural statement without a ground: the framework has no deletion operator, so a term's standing to be composed over can fall while the record only grows (Fig 5 :55: *"Nothing leaves the library. What falls is the reach."*). That is the strongest thing that can be said here without a measurement, and it is a shape about archives, not a claim about Hawking radiation.

## 5. Q: Does "shape, not a name" forbid discussing islands or correlations at all?

**Answer: no.** The canonical phrase governs *"a missing thing,"* a primitive not held in Γ. Spectrum, gradient and network are already held. Discussing a physics-frame construction such as an island is a binding onto held vocabulary, provenanced as such, not the invention of a seventh primitive. What is forbidden is presenting that binding as a derivation the framework produced. Fig 8 :56 names Bekenstein and holography as a case already known and says the triangulation claim *"has not been tested forward,"* so the correct label is retrodiction.

## 5b. Correction to my own §4

I initially wrote that naming any mechanism in this question is categorically forbidden. That overstates the rule. The narrower, correct form is: the framework cannot **derive** which mechanism realises the shape, and it cannot **settle** the choice without a ground; it can discuss named bindings if their provenance is recorded. The reason to keep the slot open here is evidential, not categorical: no ground reading distinguishes the candidates. Both statements are recorded so neither is quietly lost.

It can give an action a budget to be tried. But it cannot settle an action. Only the ground can settle it. The Ground Maintainer must be blind to prestige."* A list of famous names who agree that information is preserved is the named collapse.

**This audit is a simulation, not a ground reading.** Same essay: *"LLMs can quickly simulate an approximate answer/output"* and *"the truly testable output would need to be derived from the real architecture or working the formula yourself."* So `01` is a simulated derivation checked for internal consistency against the figures. It is **not** a test of the claim and must be labelled as such. It cannot settle anything it reports on.
