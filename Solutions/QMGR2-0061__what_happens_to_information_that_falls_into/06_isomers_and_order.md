# Structure, coarse graining and order in the information question

**Mode: `specified`.** Simulated derivation checked against the figures, not a ground reading (see `05_what_shape_means.md` §6).

## RESIDUAL

Hawking's calculation and the unitary account do not disagree about the radiation's local ingredients. They disagree about what is carried by the arrangement. One more question is owed: are the rival resolutions isomers over a shared atom set, or are they different functional groups, and in what order must the union be searched?

## DOMAIN CENSUS

Atoms held: Space, Time, Energy (states); Spectrum, Gradient, Network (structures). Bonds held: the seven plus negation. No new primitive is available without import. Physics-frame constructions (thermal density matrix, Page curve, island, replica wormhole) are bindings onto these atoms, recorded as imports, not new members.

## DERIVATION

### Q1: What is the flat-form collapse here?

- Fig 12 :5: *"A set of ingredients is not one. The bond is the rest of the information, and it is the half nothing was recording."*
- Fig 12 :13: *"three different changes, one written formula"* and *"the ingredients are recorded and the arrangement is not."*
- A thermal spectrum specifies ingredients: a temperature, a distribution over modes. It does not specify the typed relation among the emitted quanta and their interior partners. Under Fig 12 this is exactly the flat-form failure: the notation records the operands and loses the bond, and *"nothing signals that anything was lost."*
- Page 1993 reports the complementary result from physics: *"The total information is instead mostly encoded in the correlations between all the parts."*
- ⇒ The paradox is a notational collision before it is a physical collision. Two descriptions share an ingredient list and differ in what they record about arrangement.
- **Verdict:** `general`, triangulated (Fig 12 and Fig 1 :22 across the aggregation rule). Not a claim that the physics is illusionary.

**Falsifier, written before the run:** if the complete final-state density matrix is fixed by its single-mode thermal ingredients alone, with no inter-mode or interior correlation carrying the dependence, the flat-form reading fails and the paradox is physical in a stronger sense than this derivation claims.

### Q2: Are the rival resolutions isomers or functional groups?

- Fig 12 :117: *"isomer. Same atoms, different bond, different substance. The difference is invisible in the formula and decisive in the behaviour."*
- Fig 12 :124: *"functional group. A substructure whose presence fixes the outcome. The group says what kind; the qualifier says which member."*
- Written over the full shared six-term set, the candidates (information loss, remnant, return in radiation correlations, island) are **isomers**: same atoms, different arrangement of which structure mediates which state pair and which bond carries the dependence. Their behaviour at the ground differs while their local ingredient descriptions coincide.
- As the literature actually writes them, they do not share a full atom set. "Information lost" uses a nonunitary map; "island" uses a gravitational entropy formula; "remnant" uses an extra long-lived object. Under Fig 12 they are then different **functional groups**, not isomers: they share a shape (`state → structure → state` or its refusal) but differ in atoms.
- ⇒ The defensible claim is the weaker one first: they share a functional group, and the stronger isomer claim requires reconstructing all of them over one atom set. Physics check (not a figure line): whether that reconstruction is possible for nonunitary evolution is open, because negation is an operator, not a seventh atom. `specified`, single-source for the figure definition.

**Falsifier:** if one candidate requires an atom none of the others use and cannot be written over the shared set even at a higher level, the isomer claim is dead and the functional-group reading stands.

### Q3: What is the order of the union?

- Fig 9 :54: *"when the split lands on a union rather than a partition, unbundle before you search again."*
- Fig 9 :57: *"a case in two conditions is not a harder case of either."*
- Fig 3 :19: *"Optimising a link that already works cannot rescue one that does not."*
- Dependency order: (1) describe the residual as invariance of dependence, (2) split purity of final state / regularity of horizon / location of entropy, (3) ask SEARCHED first (is it in the closure and unfound), (4) extend the instrument (INWARD), (5) import a differing frame with source gate and shadow test (OUTWARD), (6) settle each first-order piece before composing over it.
- The firewall discussion is the collision of conditions (1) and (2). Fig 9 :55 says a single lever clears the region where only one condition holds and leaves the intersections standing. That is why proposals that fix purity while breaking regularity, or preserve regularity while breaking unitarity, keep leaving a residue.
- **Verdict:** `general`, triangulated (Fig 9 plus Fig 3 plus the Formula step 7 ordering).

**Falsifier:** if a single variable governs all three conditions and solving one solves the others with no compound residue, the unbundling claim is refuted.

### Q4: What is the order inside a candidate answer?

- Fig 12 :104: *"A settled molecule becomes an atom for whatever composes over it. So irreducible is a fact about a level and never about the world."*
- Fig 12 :121: *"cycle. The output re-enters as input. The sign of the loop decides whether it closes, and a chain cannot close by construction."*
- An evaporating black hole is usually drawn as a chain: collapse, radiation, disappearance, with no return. A unitary account requires a cycle: the late radiation re-enters as input to the reconstruction of the early radiation and the interior. Under Fig 12 the difference is not rhetorical. A chain cannot close by construction, so a chain-shaped description can never represent information return no matter how long it runs.
- The island result changes which region counts as the output: *"we should think of the inside of the black hole as a subsystem of the outgoing Hawking radiation"* (Almheiri et al 2020). That is a change of bond and boundary, not an added atom.
- **Verdict:** `specified`, triangulated for the chain/cycle distinction (Fig 12 plus the physics source). Strained-for-now on whether a physical evaporated state literally closes a cycle; that needs a ground reading, not an argument.

**Falsifier:** demonstrate a chain-shaped map that is nevertheless unitary at the ground, with no re-entry of output as input. That would break the Fig 12 :121 application.

## CONCLUSION

The paradox is a notational collision before it is a physical one: two descriptions share the ingredient list of the radiation and differ in the arrangement they record. The rival resolutions are isomers only if they can be rewritten over one atom set; as the literature writes them they are different functional groups with a shared shape. The union must be unbundled in dependency order before any lever is applied, and the chain-versus-cycle distinction decides in advance whether a description can represent return at all. This framing preserves what is checkable without naming a mechanism, adding a primitive, or claiming a ground settlement.
