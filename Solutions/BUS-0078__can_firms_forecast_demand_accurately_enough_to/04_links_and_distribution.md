## 2. What the wider literature bears on the question

**The operations core is one witness with two faces.** The newsvendor model and the bullwhip literature are both operations research and both make the same structural point from different directions: overage and underage are a two-cost comparison whose optimum is not zero shortage (N1, N2), and local forecast accuracy can amplify orders when the supply line is ignored (B1, E1). They are complementary but share a field, so their agreement counts once, not four times (Fig 2 :31).

- **Newsvendor model (N1, N2).** *"The two cost factors are conflicting; therefore a tradeoff exists between ordering a lot in order to reduce the understock cost and ordering little to reduce overstock cost."* This **conflicts** with the literal question form (zero shortage) and was returned to the census, re-mapped to D3's cost comparison (Fig 12 :112). It **corroborates** the union reading once re-mapped.
- **Bullwhip effect (B1).** Lee, Padmanabhan and Whang 1997: *"the information transferred in the form of 'orders' tends to be distorted and can misguide upstream members in their inventory and production decisions"* and *"sell-through data and information on inventory status at downstream nodes are keys to improving channel coordination and dampening the bullwhip effect"*. This **corroborates** F4 and the contact-before-complexity recommendation at a different stratum from the figures.
- **Forecasting practice and FVA (F1, F2).** IBF defines FVA per step; Doherty's critique shows that accuracy and profit can move in opposite directions and that FVA has an *"absence of a robust financial perspective"*. This **corroborates** F1, F2 and F8.
- **M5 competitions (M1, M2).** *"less than half (48.4%) managed to outperform the Naive benchmark"* and *"combining forecasts of different methods, even relatively simple ones ... results in improved accuracy"*. These **corroborate** Fig 5 :30 (machinery firing without new content) and the independence reading in F7. The accuracy and uncertainty papers share data and authors, so they are one witness.
- **Control and system dynamics (E1, E2).** Sterman's *"Even a perfect forecast will not prevent a manager who ignores the supply line from over ordering"* and Wang and Disney's control-theory review. These are the strongest low-overlap witnesses for F5.

**Low-overlap witnesses, each from a different field and each opened:**

1. **Human cognition (C1).** Kahneman and Tversky's *"illusion of validity"* predicts that confidence in a numerical forecast need not track its accuracy, and their examples include *"the demand for a commodity"*. This corroborates F9 at the individual stratum; it is not a firm-level measurement.
2. **Economics and social science (G1).** The Goodhart/Campbell/Strathern line, *"When a measure becomes a target, it ceases to be a good measure"*, corroborates Fig 1 :27 from a field with no shared evidence pool with the figures.
3. **Mathematics and statistics (S1).** Box's *"Since all models are wrong the scientist must be alert to what is importantly wrong"* corroborates the honest stop and the refusal to name a single winning model. The Wicklin page also corrects a common misattribution of the fuller aphorism, which is recorded rather than smoothed over.
4. **Animal behaviour.** No source in this field was opened this session. This is a declared gap in the WATSON search, not a silent omission (Fig 8 :39: *"So a search that finds nothing is never a proof of absence."*).

**Framework-internal essays** (*The Library of Babel*, *The Recursive Self-Improvement Trap*, *The Hard Problem is About Source*) are used for the canonical reading of "shape" in `05_what_shape_means.md` only. Their author wrote the figures, so they carry no independent world witness weight. Overlap is recorded in `10_source_ledger.csv`.

## 3. Distribution rules

Figure-grounded reasons for how material is offered:

- **Fig 4 :22:** *"Only methods cross up. Only head starts come down."* Send the generator, the residual and the refuter, not the conclusion. A recording carried upward *"looks like knowledge and is a description of one occasion"* (Fig 4 :25).
- **Fig 10 :42:** *"A verifier that can reconstruct the claim cannot verify it."* A recipient who has absorbed the framework before being asked for a verdict has joined the frame being checked.
- **Fig 9 :28:** *"any frame you pick will seem to fit"* if the residual is not described first. Ask what they would expect to see if the shape claim were true before showing the source.
- **Fig 2 :40:** agreement from a shared pool is worthless as a verdict. Later agreement about the framework is never pooled as corroboration.
- **Fig 10 :59:** *"an interest in the outcome is unavoidable, an interest in the reading is disqualifying."* Declare the stake; do not ask for belief.

**Authorship caveat.** This packet is an LLM-assisted simulated derivation and is labelled as such everywhere. It must not be submitted to IIF, INFORMS, IBF or any journal as an authored empirical or theoretical article. The legitimate use is as a private checking instrument: post the residual and the refuter, invite a human to run the check, and let that human author whatever they conclude. Before any submission, consult the target venue's own policy on AI-assisted work; no venue policy was opened in this session, so none is asserted here.

**Order in any reply:** (1) the shape claim and the pre-registered refuter; (2) the question "what would you count as a failure here?"; (3) only after a verdict is recorded, the three source links with the note that the figures, not the summary, are the authority; (4) the formula as a reusable method, with the instruction that each user writes their own refuter and states their own mode.

## 4. The two-stage offer, compressed

**Stage 1.** Send `08_expert_verification_packet.md` sections 1 to 3 only: the one-paragraph claim, the three checkable sub-claims with their verbatim figure lines and refuters, and the non-claims. No provenance bundle, no essay links.

**Stage 2, opt-in only.** If the recipient is curious and has already recorded a verdict on stage 1, offer the three public links: the figure text, the formula and usage rule, and the essays. Their later agreement is a conversation, not corroboration (Fig 2 :40).

**Stage 3.** Offer the formula as a method, never as an answer (Fig 4 :22). Each user writes a refuter before running it and states the mode of what they get.

**Stop rule.** If stage 1 fails, the theory is not offered. A failure is more useful than an endorsement.

**Hosting.** Nothing here is hosted or sent by this agent. The human operator hosts the packet on an opt-in page and sends one to one. Prefer a download page that lets a recipient decline contact over an attachment pushed to a list.
