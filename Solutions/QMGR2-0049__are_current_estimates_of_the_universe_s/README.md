## What is being decided, in effect terms

The residual R is the spread of inferred ages across methods (Fig 1 :19, R is indexed per slot and never aggregated). The slot is the age of the universe, indexed by method. Each method maps a measurement through a model to an age. Two options: A, treat close agreement among some methods as confirmation of one true age; B, ask whether those methods actually draw on independent evidence before counting their agreement, and keep the disagreement between low-overlap camps as a live signal. A spends the independence of the agreeing frames (Fig 2 :40); B treats the question as a union of within-camp and across-camp consistency and refuses to average (Fig 9 :54-57).

**Bound shape** (Formula, "Direction"): the answer would be (i) a rule for deciding when two age estimates are genuinely independent, (ii) a separation of within-pool agreement from cross-pool disagreement, and (iii) an honest stop that names what would have to be imported to close the residual without pretending the framework can name it.

**Honest standing:** this is a simulated derivation checked for internal consistency against the figures, NOT a ground reading. It cannot settle the question it reports on (see `05_what_shape_means.md`).
