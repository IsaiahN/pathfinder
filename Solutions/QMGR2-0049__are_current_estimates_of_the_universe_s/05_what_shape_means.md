## 1. A shape is a constraint, not a filler

The figures README states the limit: the framework returns "what a missing thing would have to look like (a shape, not a name)." Fig 9 :32: "you get a kind of thing, never the answer itself." Fig 13 :133: "what it returns is a shape rather than a name." Fig 8 :39: "So a search that finds nothing is never a proof of absence."

From *The Library of Babel*: "you can cut your search short if the set of books returned is small and descriptive enough that it provides a general shape of the answer that satisfies your question."

So a **shape** is the narrowed form of an answer you can state before you can name it. It constrains; it does not fill. For this question the shape is: *the agreement you are counting must come from low-overlap frames; a non-shrinking disagreement between camps is a union of several questions; an import is owed and cannot be named from inside the current closure.* That is a shape. "Early dark energy caused it" is a name, and is withheld.

## 2. How a shape is obtained

- *Library of Babel*, on vocabulary by proximity: "you will have to use the existing vocabulary you do know that is closest to the words that you wish to describe and grasp the shape of." Then "With each new term gained, you narrow the space by definition and deepen your vocabulary." Applied here: start from the effect words (agreement, independence, shared calibration, non-shrinking gap), not from the names (H0 tension, new physics).
- Fig 6 :30: "The description travels. Fails by being sharpened into terms only we can read. Cast it in effect terms." Fig 9 :30-31: "your words are borrowed too, so describe the effect rather than the cause." So we ask: what does the residual do? It fails to shrink; it splits by anchor; it survives more data. We do not ask first which theory is right.
- *Library of Babel* on bundling: "Perhaps your problem was a bundle or conflation of several problems masquerading as one? You will need to search all of those problems individually." This is Fig 9 :54-57 in essay form and it governs the union reading in `01_figure_grounded_audit.md` section 1.3.
- *Library of Babel* on the anchor for a search: "you need something to tether and anchor you to reality" and the anchor "is provided by your own question." Here the question itself is the tether: do not pick a favourite method and then find reasons it agrees.

## 3. What licenses a name

Only two things, both outside the shape itself:

1. **The ground settles it.** Formula step 5: "the ground settles it. Verified only by what pays. A gate is not the ground." The README: the framework "stops at the limit of what the available instruments can distinguish, and records the lineage so the stop is honest." For cosmic age there is no direct ground reading; only model-mediated inferences and a lower bound from old objects (Cimatti and Moresco 2023). So no age name is settled here.
2. **A provenanced import from a frame whose closure differs** (Fig 6 :23; Fig 8 :51-52), with a source gate and a shadow test. Fig 8 :50: "Two frames can hold the same atom by convergence, reaching it separately, which is corroboration at maximum independence." Convergence and adoption look identical in the contents; only the record separates them.

The six-term substrate is relevant here only as a visible guess, not a licence to name. Fig 13 :134-135: "Unreachable is a verdict here, not a silence, It cannot say which primitive is missing, because naming it would mean already holding it." The same limit applies to a missing physical term in cosmology.

## 4. Why the consensus age is the specific forbidden error

The world literature supplies a clean case. Planck CMB, BAO and supernovae under ΛCDM all return an age near 13.79 to 13.80 Gyr. Verde et al. (2024) say "The shape of the Universe expansion history agrees with the model; it is the normalizations that disagree" and "It appears to be a matter of anchors." Under Fig 2 :40, "agreement about the world is worthless when the agreeing frames share an evidence pool"; under Fig 2 :41 that agreement is "efficient for a convention." These methods share ΛCDM and the sound-horizon calibration, so their concordance is a convention about what "13.8 Gyr" means, not two independent readings of the world.

The error is not that the number is wrong. It is that the number is being asked to do witness work it cannot do. Fig 1 :27: "They are frame-internal. A frame cannot score itself with a quantity it also produces." A weighted mean of agreeing frames is a quantity the frame produces.

*The Recursive Self-Improvement Trap* gives the validation side: "the output is only as good as the validator. The agent, or the tool, or the frame, or the theory, cannot validate itself." A consensus of cosmology frames is still the frame validating itself.

## 5. Q: what does the framework license in answer to QMGR2-0049?

**Answered. No further framework step is owed.**

The framework licenses: (i) a test for independence before counting agreement (Fig 2 :31-32), (ii) a union split into within-pool and cross-pool consistency (Fig 9 :54-57), (iii) the claim that a non-shrinking residual is a real signal and that a chain of agreeing rooms is not progress (Fig 7 :13, :36, :42), and (iv) the statement that an import is owed but cannot be named (Fig 6 :14; Fig 9 :32; Fig 13 :133-134).

It does **not** license: naming early dark energy, modified gravity, a local void, a Cepheid systematic, or any other specific fix; declaring ΛCDM false; or declaring the 13.797 Gyr number "the" age. Those are names, and the framework returns a shape.

**Does the record commit the forbidden error?** The world record does when it pools. The Planck 2020 abstract itself is careful: it calls the parameters "model dependent" and reports the tension in the same breath. The pooled error is in the use of the number downstream, not in the measurement. That distinction is recorded so neither side is quietly lost.

## 5b. Correction to my own §3

I initially wrote that the framework "cannot name any term at all." That overstates the limit. The README's phrase is "what a **missing** thing would have to look like (a shape, not a name)." It governs a primitive or term not held in Γ. Naming a term already held, such as H0, ΛCDM, or a sound horizon, is allowed and necessary; what is forbidden is presenting a selection from held terms as the discovery of a missing one. For this question the fix is missing, so the limit applies to the fix, not to the vocabulary used to describe the residual. Both readings are now recorded.

## 6. Two consequences for outreach

**Prestige is not action.** From *The Recursive Self-Improvement Trap*: "Prestige must never be allowed to validate an action directly... Prestige can fund an action. It can give an action a budget to be tried. But it cannot settle an action. Only the ground can settle it. The Ground Maintainer must be blind to prestige." A campaign that gathers famous cosmologists to agree that "13.8 Gyr is settled" is the named collapse, not corroboration. A reply from a famous name is a conversation; its verdict weight is zero until a ground pays.

**My own audit is a simulation, not a ground reading.** Same essay: "LLMs can quickly simulate an approximate answer/output with the formula found in the architecture post... However, the truly testable output would need to be derived from the real architecture or working the formula yourself." So `01_figure_grounded_audit.md` is a simulated derivation, checked for internal consistency against the figures. It is **not** a test of the claim and must be labelled as such. It cannot settle anything it reports on.
