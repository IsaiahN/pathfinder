## QUESTION

Are current estimates of the universe's age consistent across independent methods? Restated in effect terms: R = |Γ(b,a) - o′| is indexed per estimating method, never aggregated (Fig 1 :19). Each method maps a measurement through a model, usually flat ΛCDM, to H0 then to an age. Option A: treat close agreement among some methods as confirmation of one true age. Option B: test whether the agreeing methods draw on independent evidence, keep the disagreement between low-overlap camps as a live signal, and refuse to average. A spends the independence of the agreeing frames (Fig 2 :40); B treats the question as a union (Fig 9 :54-57). The sought-for shape was bound before the run: an independence test, a within-pool versus cross-pool split, and an honest stop that characterizes an import without naming it.

## DOMAIN CENSUS

- **R (the residual).** The spread of inferred ages across methods. Arity: a family indexed by method (per-slot, Fig 1 :19). Symmetry: "consistent" is a threshold comparison ⋛ (Fig 12 :87), not identity; methods are not interchangeable in their evidence provenance. Scale: the same age today inferred from recombination (z near 1100), intermediate z, the local ladder, standard sirens, and old stars, linked by a lossy model membrane (Fig 4).
- **D1 Early-universe inference.** Actors: CMB photons, sound horizon, BAO, ΛCDM. Currency: angular scale mapped to H0 then age. Anchor: the CMB map is a measurement, but the age is model-derived; the anchor for age is reached only through the model. Admitted.
- **D2 Late-universe distance ladder.** Actors: Cepheids, TRGB, JAGB, SNe Ia, Gaia parallaxes, NGC 4258 masers. Currency: geometric distances and redshifts to H0 then age. Anchor: geometric parallaxes and masers as base rung. Admitted.
- **D3 Standard sirens.** Actors: GW170817, host galaxy. Currency: GW luminosity distance plus EM redshift, no ladder. Anchor: the GW signal itself, currently low precision. Admitted.
- **D4 Old-object lower bound.** Actors: globular clusters, old stars, white dwarfs. Currency: stellar ages as a lower bound. Anchor: stellar evolution models, which can be wrong on axes they do not measure. Admitted as bound only.
- **X1 A single true age readable off a clock.** No anchor independent of a model; there is no t = 0 clock. Not a domain yet.
- **X2 The tension is just statistics.** An error-weighted mean of agreeing frames; already inside D1 and D2, and a frame-internal quantity (Fig 1 :27). Absorbed, not separate.
- **Union, not partition.** D1 and D2 share ΛCDM and the sound-horizon calibration; D2 indicators overlap on the same host galaxies. D3 and D4 are low-overlap. Unbundled conditions: C1 within-camp consistency, C2 cross-camp consistency. Dependency order: perception then vocabulary then objective then planning then learn and carry (Fig 3 :20); the disagreement sits at the vocabulary/objective link.

## VERDICT (from the figures)

Current estimates of the universe's age are **consistent only within shared-assumption clusters, not across genuinely independent methods**. Within the early-universe ΛCDM cluster, ages agree near 13.79 to 13.80 Gyr, and that agreement is a convention bought with a common model and sound-horizon calibration (Fig 2 :41). Across low-overlap camps the implied H0 values split near 67 and 73 km/s/Mpc, implying ages near 13.8 and 12.9 Gyr under the same densities; the split has not shrunk with effort, so by Fig 9 :4-5 it is a real residual and a union, not one hard problem (Fig 9 :54-57). An import is owed but its name is withheld (Fig 9 :32; Fig 13 :133). This is `specified` and ungrounded. The campaign-level implication: ask about independence before counting agreement, keep the two conditions separate, and never average a union into one number (Fig 9 :60).

- **cost if wrong:** bounded and locatable. The packet states mode, refuters and lineage before the conclusion, so an error is findable rather than invisible. The real cost is spending the independence of outside readers by asking them to endorse a pooled number rather than to check an independence claim. Fig 2 :42 prices that: "Buy independence in proportion to the cost of being wrong." That cost is avoidable by leading with the output and its refuter.
- **undoable:** yes. No term is deleted if the claim fails; a refutation is added and the reach falls because "something was added rather than because anything was deleted" (Fig 6 amendment, :42-43). The shape and its four falsifiers are recorded in a provenance trail so a later reader can locate the error (Fig 10 :62-64). A sent claim can be superseded by a corrected packet with no primitive withdrawn.
- **reversed by:** any of the four pre-registered falsifiers, in particular (1) two low-overlap methods agreeing within the precision needed with fully independent evidence, (2) the early-late H0 gap below 2σ under more data and no new model term, (3) a model-independent age measurement, or (4) one lever restoring both conditions with no categorically different residue. A source with a genuinely different evidence pool showing that the split is entirely a single calibration artifact would also reverse it. Until one fires, the verdict stands as candidate.
