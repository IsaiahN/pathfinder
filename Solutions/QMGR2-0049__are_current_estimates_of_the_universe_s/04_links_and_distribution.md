## Two findings in the world literature that bear directly on the output

1. **The camps are calibrated, not merely measured.** Verde et al. (2024) say "The shape of the Universe expansion history agrees with the model; it is the normalizations that disagree" and "It appears to be a matter of anchors." That is the figure 2 independence rule stated in cosmological terms: the disagreement is about which frame sets the scale, not about the shape of the data. Cite this when explaining why agreement inside a camp is a convention, not a verdict.
2. **Independence at low precision still refuses to arbitrate.** The standard-siren measurement (LIGO/Virgo 2017) is "completely independent" of both the ladder and the CMB, gives about 70 km/s/Mpc, and "does not resolve this tension, and is broadly consistent with both." That is the honest shape: a genuinely different closure returns a wide bracket that both camps can accept, so more independent frames are owed before the union closes (Fig 8 :45, "Two frames together reach what neither reaches alone").

## Distribution: offer, do not push

Figure-grounded reasons:

- **Fig 4 :22** - "Only methods cross up. Only head starts come down." Sending the full framework before anyone has described a residual sends a recording, not a generator. Lead with the output and its refuter; offer the links after.
- **Fig 10 :42** - "A verifier that can reconstruct the claim cannot verify it." A recipient who has absorbed the whole framework before being asked for a verdict has joined the frame being checked.
- **Fig 9 :27-28** - "describe it before you go looking, or any frame you pick will seem to fit." Ask what they would expect to see if the output were true before giving them any source.
- **Fig 2 :40** - "disqualifying for a verdict: agreement about the world is worthless when the agreeing frames share an evidence pool." A send selected for people who already agree produces a convention, not corroboration.

**Recommended order in a reply:** (1) the output and its four refuters; (2) ask what they would count as a failure, and record the verdict; (3) only then the three links, with the note that the figures, not the summary, are the authority.

## The two-stage offer

1. **Stage 1 before stage 2** is Fig 10 :52, "Bring a characterised residual, never the source," and Fig 2 :45, "Frames range against the ground. They do not negotiate with it." The recipient checks the output without holding the framework's interior.
2. **Stage 2 is opt-in by construction.** Once a recipient has absorbed the theory, their agreement stops being evidence. Their Stage 1 verdict must be recorded before any theory is shown, and later agreement is never pooled as corroboration (Fig 2 :40).
3. **Stage 3 sends a method, which is permitted** (Fig 4 :22). The formula is a generator, so giving it away does not give away an answer.
4. **Stage 3 still needs a ground per problem.** `THE_FORMULA.md` "Direction": a frame cannot check the direction of its own question, because the shape it is searching for is not a term it holds. Each user must write down what would refute their result before running it, and label the mode. The source essay says outputs "could never be verified if the solutions presented were not contrived to fit the formula."

**Hard precondition:** stages 1 to 3 only work if the Stage 1 output survives. The four refuters in `02_outreach_email.md` are written before the run so that a failure can be recorded rather than explained away.
