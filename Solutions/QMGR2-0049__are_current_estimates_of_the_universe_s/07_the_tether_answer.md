# The Tether's answer on QMGR2-0049

**Mode: `specified` on the world-facing claims, `general` on the structural shape, not `grounded`.** This is a shape and a route, not a result. Simulated derivation checked against the figures (`05_what_shape_means.md` §6), not a ground reading.

## 1. The residual it answers

Ages of the universe are not read off a clock. Each method maps a measurement through a model (usually flat ΛCDM) to H0 and then to an age. The question asks whether those estimates agree across independent methods. R is indexed per method, never pooled (Fig 1 :19). The split is a union of two conditions: within-camp consistency (C1) and cross-camp consistency (C2), each with its own instrument (Fig 9 :54-57). It is not one hard question.

## 2. The shape

1. **Agreement is a threshold, not identity.** "Consistent" means within quoted errors, a ⋛ comparison (Fig 12 :87), not two names for one referent. The error scale sets what the comparison costs.
2. **Independence decides whether agreement counts.** Fig 2 :31-32: "Two people reading the same report are not two frames; two instruments pointed at the same thing are." Planck CMB, BAO and supernovae under ΛCDM agree near 13.79 to 13.80 Gyr, but they share the model and the sound-horizon calibration. That agreement is "efficient for a convention" (Fig 2 :41) and "disqualifying for a verdict" (Fig 2 :40).
3. **There is no direct anchor for age.** Age is a second-order term composed over the settled molecule H0 (Fig 12 :104); the round trip carries a gap R_T (Fig 4 :30). Old stars give a lower bound, not a point (Cimatti and Moresco 2023).
4. **The cross-camp gap is a real, non-shrinking signal.** Early-universe inference gives H0 near 67 km/s/Mpc (age near 13.8 Gyr); the local ladder gives near 73 (age near 12.9 Gyr under the same densities). The gap has survived a decade of better data. Fig 9 :4-5: "Disagreements that do not shrink with effort, where each rule keeps working well on a different subset, are not one hard question but several well-formed ones. Split it rather than search." Fig 7 :42: "The chain approaches the truth only as the world runs out of ways to refuse it."
5. **An import is owed, and its name is withheld.** Fig 6 :14 names import as "the only operator that moves the wall"; Fig 9 :32 returns "a kind of thing, never the answer itself." The framework cannot say which new physics or which systematic closes the union.
6. **Do not average.** A pooled "best age" is a frame-internal quantity (Fig 1 :27) and a pooled reading (Fig 10 :26) that destroys the diagnostic.

## 3. What it explicitly does not say

- Not that ΛCDM is wrong, that new physics is required, or that any collaboration erred.
- Not that the 13.797 Gyr Planck value is "the" age. It is a model-dependent convention.
- Not that the tension is merely statistics, nor that it is proved physical. Both are names; the framework stops at the shape.
- Not `grounded`. No model-independent measurement of cosmic age settles `left(R,φ)`.
- Not a derivation of one camp from the other, and no new primitive.

## 4. What would settle it: the ground

A source-discriminating instrument, not a further argument. Fig 2 :45: "Frames range against the ground. They do not negotiate with it." Concretely:

- **Independence test:** show two low-overlap methods (standard sirens versus CMB, or old stars versus the ladder) drawing on fully separate evidence and agreeing on H0 within the precision needed. That would turn their agreement into triangulation (Fig 2 :31-32).
- **Non-shrinking test:** drive the early versus late gap below 2σ with more data and systematic reanalysis, with no new model term. That would make F4 a pooled artifact.
- **Direct age:** any model-independent measurement of cosmic age breaks F3 and demotes age to first order.
- **Union test:** a single lever that restores both C1 and C2 with no categorically different residue would collapse the union into a partition (Fig 9 :57).
- **Standing falsifiers from the audit:** the four in `01_figure_grounded_audit.md` section 4, plus a failure of Fig 2's independence rule inside the framework.

## 5. The honest stop

**Unreached, not unreachable.** The split is live: standard sirens are independent but too wide to arbitrate (LIGO/Virgo 2017: 70 +12/-8 km/s/Mpc, "does not resolve this tension"), and old stars give a bound. The answer stops where the instruments can no longer distinguish and records the lineage so the stop is checkable. "Unreachable is a verdict here, not a silence," (Fig 13 :134) and "So a search that finds nothing is never a proof of absence." (Fig 8 :39). Prestige or consensus is not an instrument: "Prestige can fund an action... But it cannot settle an action. Only the ground can settle it" (*The Recursive Self-Improvement Trap*).

## 6. One-paragraph version for outreach

> The Tether does not pick a winner in the Hubble tension and does not declare a true age. It says that "consistent" is a threshold against error bars, not identity, and that agreement counts only when the agreeing methods draw on independent evidence. The close agreement among CMB, BAO and supernovae near 13.8 billion years is a shared-ΛCDM convention, not two witnesses, because those methods share the sound-horizon calibration. Across genuinely different methods the implied ages split, near 13.8 billion years from the early universe and about 12.9 from the local distance ladder under the same densities, and that gap has not shrunk with a decade of better data. By the figures a non-shrinking disagreement is several questions, not one, so a single best-fit age cannot settle it. An import is owed, something that moves the boundary between the camps, but the framework returns a shape, not a name. What it offers is the independence test, the union split, falsifiers written in advance, and the instruments that would close it. The stop is honest until a ground pays.
