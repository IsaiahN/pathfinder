## 1. The claim, in one paragraph

Ages of the universe are inferred, not read off a clock. Whether estimates are "consistent across independent methods" therefore depends on whether the methods draw on independent evidence. Close agreement among CMB, BAO and supernovae under ΛCDM near 13.79 to 13.80 Gyr is a convention bought with a shared model and sound-horizon calibration, not two witnesses. Across low-overlap methods the implied H0 values split into two non-shrinking camps near 67 and 73 km/s/Mpc, implying ages near 13.8 and 12.9 Gyr under the same densities. A disagreement that does not shrink with effort is several well-formed questions, so the issue is a union of within-camp and cross-camp consistency and cannot be closed by one averaged age. The framework says an import is owed but cannot name it. What it offers is the independence test, the union split, refuters written in advance, and the instruments that would close it.

- **Refuter:** show two low-overlap frames sharing no evidence that agree within the precision needed, or show that the early versus late H0 gap falls below 2σ with more data and no new model term.

### C2: observable-facing; checkable by a cosmologist against the literature

- **Statement:** the tight age agreement inside the ΛCDM cluster rests on shared calibration; the non-shrinking split between early and late determinations is real at the level of implied age.
- **What to check:** Planck 2020 gives 13.797 ± 0.023 Gyr and reports a 3.6σ tension with local H0; Riess et al. 2022 give 73.04 ± 1.04 km/s/Mpc and a 5σ difference with Planck ΛCDM; Verde et al. 2024 count more than a dozen H0 determinations in two camps near 68 and 73 and write "It appears to be a matter of anchors." BAO's physical scale is calibrated by the sound horizon fixed by the CMB. Under flat ΛCDM with Ωm near 0.3, H0 = 67 gives an age near 14.1 Gyr and H0 = 73 gives about 12.9 Gyr (Cimatti and Moresco 2023).
- **Refuter:** demonstrate that CMB and BAO reach their scales from fully independent calibrations, or that the two camps agree on H0 within errors at the precision needed, or present a model-independent age measurement.

## 5. What would settle it

A source-discriminating instrument, not a further argument (Fig 2 :45, "Frames range against the ground. They do not negotiate with it"):

1. **Independence test:** low-overlap methods agreeing on H0 within the precision needed (standard sirens, old stars, CMB).
2. **Non-shrinking test:** the early versus late gap driven below 2σ with no new model term.
3. **Direct age:** any model-independent measurement of cosmic age.
4. **Union test:** one lever restoring both within-camp and cross-camp consistency with no categorically different residue.
