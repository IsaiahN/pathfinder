## What is deliberately not here

- No personal emails of named individuals.
- No ranking of people by how likely they are to agree with this answer.
- No bulk-send plan.

Three reasons, in the figures' own words:

1. **A room does not upgrade the label.** Seat Map :283: *"A room does not upgrade the label. A room reaching a conclusion without the ground is agreement, and agreement among frames is a weighted average of influence. Adding people raises the vocabulary and does not add an anchor."*
2. **Correlation has valence.** Fig 2 :39-41: agreement about a shared meaning is the product of communication, while agreement about the world is disqualifying as a verdict when the agreeing frames share an evidence pool. Selecting recipients for likely agreement would manufacture exactly the wrong quantity.
3. **Asking for trust before checking spends independence.** Fig 10 :42: *"A verifier that can reconstruct the claim cannot verify it."*

Legally: scraping personal emails for unsolicited bulk outreach risks GDPR/CAN-SPAM style rules in many jurisdictions and will get a sending domain blacklisted. That is a practical failure on top of the structural one.
