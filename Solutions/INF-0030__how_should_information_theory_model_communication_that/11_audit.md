## Independent domain census

I redid the census from the residual alone, in the WATSON order: R by arity, symmetry and scale; one line per domain with actors, currency and anchor; partition or union; order by dependency.

- **R (arity / symmetry / scale).** The residual is the gap between the effect a message is predicted to have on a participant and the effect observed at a ground neither communicator can move. Arity: two participant frames plus a ground; each participant is a slot. Symmetry: message-to-effect is ordered (sequence, production) and comparative (threshold); the shared-code bond is symmetric. Scale: first order is a mint inside a receiver's library; second order is the settled change becoming an atom that propagates through a network.
- **Domain 1, encoding and transmission.** Actors: source, transmitter, channel, noise, receiver. Currency: bits and distortion. Anchor: the sent message reproduced at the destination. This anchor is legitimate here because the question is constitutively about reproduction at this layer.
- **Domain 2, receiver as a frame.** Actors: Γ, b, w, R⁺_s, candidate term. Currency: belief update and description length. Anchor: an observed action or outcome neither frame can move. This is where the effect question lives.
- **Domain 3, convention and meaning.** Actors: sender, receiver, shared code. Currency: agreement about meaning. Anchor: a convention, not a ground. It has no truth value and must never be promoted into a verdict. I reached this boundary from Fig 2 :41, not from the primary's wording.
- **Domain 4, habitat and propagation.** Actors: other participants, network, conditions, relations. Currency: influence and valence relative to the residual. Anchor: a population-scale behavioral outcome. This depends on D2's settled change becoming an atom.
- **Union, not partition.** Faithful reproduction and belief change overlap. A single lever on the channel clears only one region. Unbundle before searching (Fig 9 :54-56).
- **Order by dependency.** Perceive the effect, acquire terms for it, state the objective, design the message, carry the method. The break is at the objective, where no instrument exists, not at the channel (Fig 3 :20-21, :27).

My census matches the primary's four domains and its union reading.
