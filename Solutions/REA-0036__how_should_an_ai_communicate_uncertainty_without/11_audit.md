## Independent domain census

I redid the census from the residual line alone, before using the primary's conclusions, using the WATSON order: R by arity, symmetry and scale; one line per domain with actors, currency and anchor; partition or union; order by dependency.

- **R (arity / symmetry / scale).** The residual is `R = |Γ(b,a) - o'|` indexed per slot, where the slots are factual claims, reported confidence, message and decision. Arity: at least three-place, model state, message and reader decision, so the communication half is not a property of the agent alone. Symmetry: the model-to-reader crossing is ordered, and adding a hedge is not interchangeable with adding evidence. Scale: one loop per slot, the seam, and the population of readers over time. Symbols table :43-44 states that R is measured per slot, never as one global number, which is a different claim from accuracy averaged over answers.
- **Domain 1, model-ground contact.** Actors: model Γ, belief b, weighting w, held-out outcomes. Currency: per-slot residual reduction. Anchor: observed correctness, a counter that does not update on the model's opinion. Question type: calibration, partition boundary on which slots are live.
- **Domain 2, message seam.** Actors: compressed message, reader. Currency: bits to describe and actions to check. Anchor: outcomes for truth and human judgment for usability. Question type: a union of two conditions, not one scalar trade.
- **Domain 3, reader decision habitat.** Actors: reader, task, tools, time, other sources. Currency: reliance and decision accuracy. Anchor: the task outcome. Question type: contact enumeration, not composition.
- **Domain 4, channel maintenance.** Actors: instruments, metrics, percepts, pooled readings. Currency: contact. Anchor: the ground, which does not decay while the channel does. Question type: seat office, outside the loop.
- **Rejected candidate X1, confidence as a self-certifying property.** Actors: the model's self-report. Currency: fluency. Anchor: none nameable, because a frame cannot score itself with a quantity it also produces. Not admitted as a domain.
- **Rejected candidate X2, one universal score.** Absorbed into Domain 2 as a compound of two currencies. Not admitted as a third domain.

Order by dependency: describe what failed to resolve per slot before choosing a message format; find terms for the uncertainty before saying what counts as progress; keep the description cost and the checking cost apart before planning the wording; record provenance so the route is checkable.

My census matches the primary's four domains, its union reading, and its rejection of X1 and X2. ## Round trip gap R_T

The Symbols table defines R_T as the gap between what was sent up and what came back down, `gap(x, (T_E ∘ T_A)(x))`, measuring what the coarser description cannot hold. Fig 4 :30 requires it to be measured rather than assumed zero. I ran the round trip on the condensation this folder actually uses.

1. **Sent up (x):** the verbatim figure set, chiefly Fig 2 :37-38 (anchor), Fig 4 :30-32 (gap as content), Fig 12 :126-130 (two currencies) and Fig 13 :134 (honest stop).
2. **Abstraction (T_A):** the shorthand in `07_the_tether_answer.md`, namely report per slot, make the gap the content, keep two ledgers, carry provenance, output `gap`.
3. **Concretisation (T_E):** a concrete message a model could emit, with one number per claim, a hedge, a source line and a gap verdict.

**R_T is nonzero.** Three things the shorthand holds do not survive the trip back down:

- the **choice of verbal form**. The shorthand says describe effects; a concrete message must choose words, numbers or intervals, and no figure line makes that choice. This is the declared `gap` in F9 and the largest single term in R_T.
- the **reader measurement**. The shorthand says human judgment is the anchor; a concrete message has no instrument attached to that anchor unless a reader study is run. The folder correctly refuses to supply one.
- the **cost units**. The shorthand says two ledgers; a concrete UI tends to collapse them into a single trust display, which is the Fig 12 :130 error. This survives only because the packet states the two readings explicitly.

One thing survives cleanly: a single scalar cannot carry per-slot residuals and two currencies. That is the residue worth sending. R_T was **not** measured numerically, because no ground reading exists; it is recorded qualitatively as the enumerated set of dropped constraints. Treating the shorthand as identical to the figures would set R_T to zero and would be the dead-reckoning error Fig 4 :30 names.
