# Tether outreach - figure-grounded package

**Question:** How should an AI communicate uncertainty without making its answers unusable or misleading?

**Source:** `Questions/reasoning.txt` line 45, section 4 (Knowledge, uncertainty, and truth).

**ID:** REA-0036

**Mode:** `specified` on the substantive claims, `general` on the structural shape. No measurement in this folder settles the human-facing half, so nothing here is `grounded`. The question is constitutively about people, so human judgment is the legitimate anchor for the "usable" half, but this folder does not run that experiment.

**Status:** done

**Registry status:** pending

## What is deliberately not here

- No personal emails of named individuals.
- No ranking of people by how likely they are to agree.
- No bulk-send plan.
- No claim that this simulated derivation settles what people actually find usable.

Three reasons, in the figures' own words:

1. **Agreement is not an anchor.** Fig 2 :35: *"Alignment is triangulation, not negotiation. A mirror cannot correct you."* Fig 7 :13: *"agreement compounds, evidence does not."* A list of people selected for likely agreement produces a convention, not a check.
2. **The question is about people.** Fig 2 :36-38: *"Human judgment is the right anchor for is this a good answer for a person, because the question is about people."* The model's self-reported confidence is *"only an instrument"* for a different question, and instruments can be wrong on axes they do not measure. So the verdict cannot be handed to the model's own score.
3. **A verifier that can reconstruct the claim cannot verify it.** Fig 10 :42: *"A verifier that can reconstruct the claim cannot verify it."* Showing a reader the full interior before asking for a verdict spends the only thing they contribute.

Legally: unsolicited bulk email breaches GDPR/CAN-SPAM style rules in many jurisdictions and will get a sending domain blacklisted. Public opt-in venues avoid that failure and the structural one.

## Suggested next step

Take `08_expert_verification_packet.md` to one venue in `03_public_contact_points.csv`, one-to-one. Ask what would refute the shape claim. Record that verdict before offering any theory. A reported failure is more useful than an endorsement.
