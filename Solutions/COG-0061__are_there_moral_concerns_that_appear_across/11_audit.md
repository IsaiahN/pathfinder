## Independent domain census (written from the residual line alone)

I redid the census before comparing with the primary, using the WATSON order: R by arity, symmetry and scale; one line per domain with actors / currency / anchor; partition or union; order by dependency.

- **R (arity / symmetry / scale).** The residual is the unassigned structure in a pattern where some judgments recur across societies and the same concerns are interpreted differently. Arity: two conditions plus an internal linking condition, so a **union compound** (Fig 9 :55 "A bundled problem is usually a union of overlapping conditions, not a list of separate ones."). Symmetry: recurrence is an invariance over society-slots and behaves like a symmetric bond; interpretation is an ordered binding and behaves like a sequence or comparison. Scale: per judgment and per society, never a single scalar.
- **Domain 1, individual judgment.** Actors: persons judging in a slot. Currency: per-slot residuals. Anchor: observed behavior that does not update on the judge's own account. Live.
- **Domain 2, cultural interpretation.** Actors: societies and shared backdrops. Currency: shared meanings and qualifiers. Anchor: the shared meaning itself, legitimate because the question is constitutively about human meaning (Fig 2 :36). Live, different anchor from D1.
- **Domain 3, research and verification.** Actors: researchers, instruments, samples. Currency: cross-cultural data and agreement. Anchor: an independent measure. Live in principle, often compromised by one evidence pool.
- **Domain 4, the standing list.** No anchor nameable. Not a domain. Declared `gap`.
- **Order by dependency.** Register per slot, acquire terms for what a situation affords, state what counts as invariant versus bound, then compare rooms. The chain stops at the vocabulary-to-objective link (Fig 3 :27 "A link with no instrument is not a link that works. It is a link nobody has looked at.").

My census matches the primary's four domains and its union reading. Where the primary derived from Fig 12's functional group and Fig 9's union rule, I derived from Fig 2's anchor legitimacy and Fig 3's dependency break. Different starting figures, same boundaries. ## Round trip gap R_T

The Symbols table defines R_T as the gap between what was sent up and what came back down, gap(x, (T_E composed with T_A)(x)), measuring what the coarser description cannot hold. Fig 4 :30 requires it to be measured rather than assumed: "So take the round trip and measure the gap. Without it you are navigating by dead reckoning: confident about a position nobody checked."

1. **Sent up (x):** the verbatim figure set, chiefly Fig 9 :54-57 (union), Fig 12 :123 and :117 (functional group and isomer), Fig 2 :36-41 (anchor legitimacy and valence of agreement), Fig 13 :134 (`gap` as verdict).
2. **Abstraction (T_A):** the shorthand carried in `07` and `08`: the invariant is a functional group, cultures are isomers, agreement has opposite valence in the two halves, the list is `gap`.
3. **Concretisation (T_E):** a concrete cross-cultural claim, for example "the universal concern is authority, cultures differ only in how much they emphasize it, and agreement across studies confirms it."

**R_T is nonzero.** Four things the shorthand holds do not survive the trip back down:

- the **source discriminator** is dropped, so "how much they emphasize it" silently names a source from a reading (Fig 9 :32, Hard Problem essay);
- the **shared-atom-set condition** on the isomer claim is dropped, so cultures with different atom sets are mislabelled as isomers;
- the **anchor split** is dropped, so agreement across one pool is wrongly used as a world verdict (Fig 2 :40-41);
- the **`gap`** is dropped, so the standing list is filled as if the framework named it.

Conversely, one thing survives cleanly: a per-slot invariant with a variable qualifier, and the requirement that the two halves be ranged against different anchors. That is the residue worth sending. R_T was **not** measured numerically, because no ground reading exists; it is recorded qualitatively as the enumerated set of dropped constraints. Treating "the formula is a condensed version of the metalaws" as identity would set R_T to zero and would be the dead-reckoning error Fig 4 :30 names.
