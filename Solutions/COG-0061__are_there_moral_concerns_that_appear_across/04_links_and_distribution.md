## 2. Why these sources bear on the question

Three independent evidence pools were read:

1. **Cross-cultural psychology (Moral Foundations theory).** It supplies the empirical shape that a candidate universal has criteria including "Presence across multiple cultures", and that cultures use the same building blocks differently. It also supplies the anchor warning: MFQ structure has failed invariance tests across groups and cultures, so agreement on a questionnaire is not automatically a ground. This corroborates F2 and F5.
2. **Anthropology (Cultural universal, Brown 1991).** It supplies the "no known exception" definition and the hundreds-item habitat of candidates, plus the non-nativist warning that identical behavior does not prove an identical mechanism. This corroborates F4 and F6.
3. **Animal behaviour and evolution (Evolution of morality).** It supplies the shared "premoral sentiments" across social animals and the extra human layer of enforcement, reputation and reason. This is the genuinely low-overlap witness for F3: same building blocks, different arrangement, different moral substance.

The eight framework essays and the Medium architecture article are **not** independent witnesses. They share the framework author and evidence pool with the figures, so their agreement is expected and carries no independent weight. They are used only for the canonical reading of "shape" in `05` and for the simulation caveat. This is declared in `10_source_ledger.csv`.

No world source was found that conflicts with the figure verdict. The conflict rule was therefore not triggered. The one apparent tension, that Moral Foundations theory claims universal foundations while Fig 11 :51 refuses a standing list, was resolved by re-census: the figure refuses a list whose valence is fixed across residuals; the psychological claim is a functional-group shape with variable bindings. The verdict was not flipped to accommodate the world source.

## 3. Distribution: offer, do not push

Figure-grounded reasons:

- **Fig 4 :22 "Only methods cross up. Only head starts come down."** Sending the whole architecture before anyone has described a residual sends a recording, not a generator. Lead with the shape and the refuter.
- **Fig 10 :42 "A verifier that can reconstruct the claim cannot verify it."** A recipient who has absorbed the framework before being asked for a verdict has joined the frame being checked.
- **Fig 9 :27-28 "describe it before you go looking, or any frame you pick will seem to fit."** Ask what they would expect to see if the shape claim were true before giving them the article.
- **Fig 2 :40 "disqualifying for a verdict: agreement about the world is worthless when the agreeing frames share an evidence pool".** A mass send selected for people likely to agree produces a convention, not corroboration.
- **Fig 2 :41 "efficient for a convention: agreement about a shared meaning is the product, not a corruption of it".** Within the interpretation domain, a conversation about shared meaning is legitimate. The rule is not "never agree"; it is "do not use agreement as a world verdict."

**Recommended order in a reply:** (1) the shape claim and its four refuters; (2) ask what they would count as a failure; (3) only then, if they are curious, the three links, with the note that the figure text and canonical README, not the summary, are the authority.

## 4. Two-stage offer

1. **Stage 1:** the residual, the shape claim and the pre-registered refuter. Their verdict is recorded before any theory is shown.
2. **Stage 2, opt-in:** the sources, offered only if they ask. The figures outrank the summary.
3. **Stage 3:** the formula as a method to reuse, with the requirement that each user write their own refuter and state their own mode.

## 5. Who can help, and who is not being approached

The relevant checker is a person whose public work touches the residual: cross-cultural measurement invariance, the source of an apparent universal, or the bond between a concern and its cultural binding. They should be reached through public, opt-in channels in `03`. No personal emails, no purchased list, no receptiveness rank and no bulk send. Prestige is a contact-cost estimate at most, never evidence: *The Recursive Self-Improvement Trap* says "Prestige can fund an action. It can give an action a budget to be tried. But it cannot settle an action."
