# Outreach draft: check the separator before you decide about the theory

**Use:** one-to-one, or as a cover letter attached to a submission through the public venues in `03_public_contact_points.csv`. **Not** for bulk send.

**Hard precondition:** do not send until the mode labels and the refuter in `01_figure_grounded_audit.md` section 4 are intact in the version you forward. The output must survive validation or the two-stage offer collapses on contact.

---

**Subject:** One checkable claim about moral universals, before any theory

> Hi [name],
>
> I have one specific output I'd like you to check against what you already know. I am not asking you to read the framework first, and I am not asking you to agree. If the output does not survive, there is nothing else to look at.
>
> **The output (`specified` on the per-domain claims, `general` on the structure, not `grounded`):** the question "are there moral concerns across all societies, and why do cultures interpret them differently" is two conditions with different anchors, not one question with two halves.
>
> 1. The invariant that recurs across societies is a **functional group**, a substructure whose presence fixes the kind of moral judgment. Its cultural interpretation is the qualifier that says which member. The framework returns that shape, not a named list.
> 2. Two cultures holding the same concerns under different bindings are **isomers**: same atoms, different arrangement, different moral substance. A list of which concerns are present cannot see the difference, because the bond is not in the ingredient list.
> 3. The two halves need opposite treatments of agreement. For "does it recur", agreement among people who read the same samples and the same instruments is one evidence pool talking, disqualifying as a verdict. For "how is it interpreted", agreement about a shared meaning is the product. A single "yes, universals exist, interpreted differently" sentence conflates them.
>
> **What I am not claiming:** that I can name the universal concerns. That is a `gap`, a verdict rather than a silence, and I am not filling it with a list. I am also not claiming cross-cultural measurement settles anything here. No measurement was taken in this derivation. This is a simulated derivation checked for internal consistency against the figures, and by its own rule it cannot settle the question it reports on.
>
> **What would refute or narrow it, written down before this message:**
> 1. a society-slot with no shared functional group in any other slot under an independent reading;
> 2. two cultures with different codes and no shared atom set, so the relation is import rather than isomerism;
> 3. a source-discriminating instrument that shows one and the same source for a cross-cultural regularity everywhere it appears;
> 4. a single anchor that legitimately settles both the recurrence claim and the meaning claim.
>
> If any holds, I will narrow or withdraw the corresponding claim. A one-line "this fails because..." is more useful to me than agreement. If you'd rather not engage, no reply is needed.
>
> **If it survives, and only then:** the sources are below. The figures are the authority, not my summary of them.
> - Figures text with line numbers (local framework extract): `FRAMEWORK/reference/03-FIGURES_TEXT.md`
> - Canonical usage rule (GitHub, branch `general-agent`): https://github.com/IsaiahN/tether/blob/general-agent/figures/README.md
> - Architecture article with the figures and formula: https://medium.com/@IsaiahNwukor/arrival-of-the-fittest-the-tether-architecture-1de7edf641ec
>
> **If you want to use the machinery:** the formula is in the figures link. Run it on a problem you know and can check against a ground. Write down what would refute your result *before* you run it, and label the mode of what you get. The architecture article's own warning is that outputs "could never be verified if the solutions presented were not contrived to fit the formula." A reported failure is more useful to me than a reported success.

## Two-stage offer

1. **Stage 1:** send the shape claim and its four refuters. Ask what they would count as a failure. Record their verdict before showing any theory.
2. **Stage 2, opt-in only:** if they are curious, offer the three links, with the note that the figure text and the canonical README, not the summary, are the authority.
3. **Stage 3:** offer the formula as a method to reuse, with the instruction that each user writes their own refuter and states their own mode.

Their later agreement is never pooled as corroboration. Fig 2 :40: "disqualifying for a verdict: agreement about the world is worthless when the agreeing frames share an evidence pool".
