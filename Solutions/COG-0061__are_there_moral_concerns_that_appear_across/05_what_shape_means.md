## 1. A shape is a constraint, not a filler

Fig 9 :32 "you get a kind of thing, never the answer itself." The figures README: "what a missing thing would have to look like (a shape, not a name)".

From *The Library of Babel*: "you can cut your search short if the set of books returned is small and descriptive enough that it provides a general shape of the answer that satisfies your question." From *How Intelligence Actually Works*: the framework "provides us a tool that cuts down to the shape of a thing, problem or question based on the given information available at this point in time."

So a **shape** is the narrowed form of an answer that can be stated before it can be named or settled. It constrains which answers are admissible. It does not fill the slot.

For COG-0061 the shape is: *the cross-society invariant is a functional group, cultural variations are bindings over a shared atom set, and a source-discriminating instrument is owed before any particular concern is named*. "Harm, fairness, authority, purity, or some list" is a name, not the shape. `CONCERN(???)CULTURE` is the shape, in the same notation the architecture article uses for `SPACE(???)TIME`.

## 2. How the shape is obtained

- *Library of Babel*: use the vocabulary already held that is closest to the thing, then look for recurring new terms that overlap or correlate with it. "With each new term gained, you narrow the space and deepen your vocabulary." This is how the functional-group term was reached here: "universal" and "relative" were too coarse, "recurrent substructure with a qualifier" narrowed the space.
- Fig 6 :30 "outward: does another frame already hold it? The description travels. Fails by being sharpened into terms only we can read. Cast it in effect terms." Fig 9 :30-31: "your words are borrowed too, so describe the effect rather than the cause."
- *Decoding Tacit Knowledge*: "When contact isn't possible, the best you can send is a plain description of what you'd expect to see, because anyone can check that against the world."
- *Library of Babel* on bundling: "Perhaps your problem was a bundle or conflation of several problems masquerading as one? You will need to search all of those problems individually to find their answers and then combine them in a satisfactory way." This is exactly the move that produced the two-domain split in `01`.

## 3. What licenses a name

Only two things, both outside the shape itself:

1. **The ground settles it.** Formula step 5: "the ground settles it. Verified only by what pays. A gate is not the ground." README: the framework "stops at the limit of what the available instruments can distinguish, and records the lineage so the stop is honest."
2. **A provenanced import from a frame whose closure differs** (Fig 6 :23, Fig 8 :50-52), with a source gate and a shadow test.

*They're Scanning Books* sharpens the second point: "The real import is not a new term in the existing space. It is a new dimension of the space." And: "Declaring the primitives does not make them neutral. It makes the choice visible." So importing "harm" or "authority" as the universal concern is adding a binding from the psychology frame, not discovering a new primitive of the moral world. The choice becomes visible when it is recorded.

## 4. The source problem is the specific limit for this question

*The Hard Problem is About Source* supplies the governing mechanism: "If several paths create the same behavior, then behavior is a projection with lossy information (it cannot define consciousness in full)." The instrument reads green and "it cannot comment on the source." Green could be one light, yellow plus blue, RGB, CMYK, or a scheme not yet discovered. "The answer is unreached until a better instrument exists."

This is not an analogy imposed from outside. Fig 13 :128-129 says "Nothing about a composed term tells you, from looking at it, which route produced it or whether that route was available." Fig 9 :32 says "you get a kind of thing, never the answer itself." The anthropology source (*Cultural universal*) states the same limit in cross-cultural terms: "The observation of the same or similar behavior in different cultures does not prove that they are the results of a common underlying psychological mechanism."

So naming the source of a cross-cultural regularity from the regularity alone is the metamerism error. What is owed is an instrument, not another list.

*How Intelligence Actually Works*: "indiscernibility is not identity." A moral code that is behaviorally indistinguishable from another under a coarse measure is not thereby the same code.

## 5. Q2 answered: what licenses a particular concern in the universal slot?

**Nothing in the figures.** A particular concern is licensed only by (a) a ground settling a minted term with a source-discriminating instrument, or (b) a provenanced import with a shadow test. Until one of those, the concern is the searcher's own binding and must be recorded as such.

The architecture article's "Three mappings offered and declined" is the precedent. Bayesian updating was declined because "there is no prior, no likelihood and no posterior anywhere in the mechanism." The same discipline applies here: mapping "authority" onto the universal slot because it appears in many societies is an adopted import until provenance and a shadow test are shown.

## 6. Two consequences for the two halves of the question

**For the recurrence half.** *The Recursive Self-Improvement Trap* asks: "What does it mean to align an AI to human values when human values are a moving target? You cannot align to something that changes." The same problem appears in cross-cultural form: a universal tied to a changing cultural interpretation is not an anchor. *The same essay*: "You may all agree and be wrong. You may set your clock to someone else's, who was setting your clock to yours, and be off by many deviations over time. You need ground truth to define where you stand." Agreement inside one evidence pool is exactly two clocks setting each other.

**For the interpretation half.** *Decoding Tacit Knowledge*: "Agreement is the whole point when you're settling what words mean. It's worthless for settling what's true when both sides learned from the same sources (Fig 2 :40–41)." And: "Culture is a shared backdrop, Collins's collective tacit knowledge. Its members can hold very different mindsets; what they share isn't a set of beliefs but the backdrop they read each other against." Here agreement is legitimate because the question is constitutively about meaning (Fig 2 :36-37). The same essay names the three drift stoppers: "Science's independent replication is separate experience plus something real to check against; ... Courts of law rule on evidence, which neither side can move." Those are models for the recurrence half, not for the interpretation half.

## 7. Correction to my own §3 and §5 (kept visible)

My first-pass reading treated "shape, not a name" as a ban on naming any moral concern. That was overstated. The canonical phrase is "what a **missing** thing would have to look like (a shape, not a name)". It governs a primitive not held in the library. Harm, fairness and authority are not missing primitives; they are atoms the cross-cultural research frame already holds. Naming one as a candidate universal is a **binding from that frame**, which is provenance and allowed, provided it is recorded as a binding and not presented as a figure-derived necessity.

What remains wrong in the naive reading is sharper after the correction: naming the source of a reading without an instrument that discriminates sources is the metamerism error. So the reason to leave the slot marked `???` is the missing discriminator, not a ban on naming held atoms. Both claims are recorded here so neither is quietly lost.

## 8. Prestige is not action, and this derivation is a simulation

*The Recursive Self-Improvement Trap*: "Prestige can fund an action. It can give an action a budget to be tried. But it cannot settle an action. Only the ground can settle it. The Ground Maintainer must be blind to prestige." A list of universals endorsed by famous universalists is prestige, not ground.

However, the truly testable output would need to be derived from the real architecture or working the formula yourself." The architecture article says outputs from a prompted model "must be seen as simulacra" and "could never be verified if the solutions presented were not contrived to fit the formula."
