# WATSON answer, CMP-0085

**Mode: `general` on the shape, `specified` on the isomer reading, not `grounded`.** Figures are primary and decide. World sources are secondary, corroborate but never overrule, and a conflict returns the derivation to the domain census. This is a simulated derivation checked for internal consistency against the figures, not a ground reading. No measurement was taken.

## QUESTION

How can researchers compare models that reproduce the same observed pattern using different underlying mechanisms?

Restated as an effect: two models with different libraries Γ_i return `R_i(s) = |Γ_i(b,a) - o'| ≈ 0` on the same observed pattern o'. Fit is not the residual. The residual is on the source, that is which mechanism generated the pattern and what each model would return in a regime the pattern does not cover. It is indexed per slot, not averaged (Fig 1 :19). Option A ranks the models by fit and picks a winner; that is a filter for the budget. Option B finds a source-discriminating bearing; that is a witness for the verdict. What an answer would look like, bound as an ordinary term before the run (Formula, "Direction"): the shape of a discriminator, not the name of a mechanism.

## DOMAIN CENSUS

R by arity, symmetry and scale: at least three-place (model, intervention, ground reading), indexed by slot; symmetric on the observed pattern and asymmetric in the arrangement where the difference lives; three scales (single fit loop, population of candidates, instrument or differing frame).

| domain | actors | currency | anchor | status |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| D1 Fit frame | k models, pattern o', score | per-slot residual, description length | the measurement that produced o', but o' is a recording | admitted, conditional |
| D2 Mechanism frame | atoms and bonds inside each Γ_i | composition inside closure(Γ_i), cost | settlement, Fig 5 :32 | admitted |
| D3 Discrimination frame | instruments, interventions, low-overlap frames | independence, residual, new readings | a measurement that returns differently under the models and does not move when argued with | admitted, decisive |
| D4 Verification frame | expert readers, reviewers | agreement and residual | the ground, never agreement | admitted |
| X1 Simplicity as truth | a ranking criterion | simplicity | none independent of a declared penalty | not a verdict domain |
| X2 Mechanism unobservable in principle | none nameable | none | no instrument found after census | gap |

Union, not partition: C1 (fit) and C2 (source identity) form a compound and need different instruments (Fig 9 :54, :57). Dependency order: perception -> vocabulary -> objective -> planning -> learn and carry (Fig 3 :20, :27). Strain test: Fig 13's six physical terms do not govern arbitrary model furniture (S1); re-census sends Fig 13 to the honest-stop and isomer-analogue role. More models are either SEARCHED or OUTWARD, never both (S2).

## VERDICT (from the figures)

Two models that fit one pattern are metamers at the current instrument. If written over a shared atom set they are isomers under Fig 12 :117; if they share only a substructure they are members of one functional group. A fit ranking is a filter for the budget, never a witness for mechanism identity. What is owed is a source-discriminating bearing, indexed per slot and answerable to a ground that does not move when the researchers argue, found by SEARCHED then INWARD then OUTWARD as opposed tracks. Verdicts are kept per domain and never averaged. Which mechanism is actually right is a declared `gap`: the framework returns the shape of the discriminator, not the name of a winner. Mode `general` for the shape, `specified` for the isomer reading, ungrounded.

- **cost if wrong:** misdirected research effort. A team facing a source-identity failure would be sold more fitting, more data of the same kind, or a parsimony tie-breaker, while the actual discriminator stays unfound. In outreach terms, an expert's twenty minutes would be spent refuting the wrong shape. The cost is real but bounded and largely reversible, because the packet states its refuters before its conclusion.
- **undoable:** yes. No term is deleted if the claim fails. A refutation is added and the reach falls because something was added rather than because anything was struck out (Fig 6 :42-43). The shape, the falsifiers and the lineage are recorded so a later reader can locate the error rather than only feel it (Fig 10 :62-64).
- **reversed by:** any of the six pre-registered falsifiers in `01` section 5. Concretely: a validated criterion that is sensitive to mechanism while holding the pattern fixed; a case where more of the same pattern separates mechanisms on a known ground; a global score that returns the discriminating signal without per-slot decomposition; two frames sharing one evidence pool discriminating mechanism at maximum independence; a single lever clearing both C1 and C2 with no categorically different residue; or a figure line that names a mechanism from a pattern-only comparison. A source with a genuinely different evidence pool showing that fit ranking already settles mechanism would also reverse it.
