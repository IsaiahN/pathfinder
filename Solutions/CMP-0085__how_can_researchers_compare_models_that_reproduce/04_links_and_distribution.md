## Distribution: offer, do not push

Figure-grounded reasons:

- **Fig 4 :22** - *"Only methods cross up. Only head starts come down."* Sending the full architecture before anyone has described a residual sends a recording, not a generator. Lead with the source residual and the refuter; offer the links after.
- **Fig 10 :42** - *"A verifier that can reconstruct the claim cannot verify it."* Seat 4 must not hold the interiors. A reader who has absorbed the framework before being asked for a verdict has joined the frame being checked.
- **Fig 9 :27-28** - *"describe it before you go looking, or any frame you pick will seem to fit."* Ask what they would expect to see if the shape claim were true before giving them the article.
- **Fig 2 :40** - *"agreement about the world is worthless when the agreeing frames share an evidence pool"* A send selected for people already inclined to agree produces a convention, not corroboration.
- **Fig 2 :42** - *"Buy independence in proportion to the cost of being wrong."* The right purchase is a reader whose instruments and closure differ from the author's.

**Recommended order in a reply:** (1) the source residual and the shape claim; (2) the five pre-registered refuters, and the question "what would you count as a failure here?"; (3) record their verdict; (4) only then, if they are curious, the framework links, with the note that the figures, not the summary, are the authority.

## The two-stage offer

1. **Stage 1, check the output.** The recipient checks the shape (two fitting models are metamers at the current instrument; the discriminator must be a ground-anchored instrument) without holding the framework's interior. Their verdict is recorded before theory is shown.
2. **Stage 2, opt-in only.** If curious, they open the figures. Their later agreement is never pooled as corroboration (Fig 2 :40). A Stage 1 refusal is copied into `11_audit.md` with provenance and re-evaluated as if it came from any other source.
3. **Stage 3, offer the method.** The formula is a generator, so giving it away does not give away an answer (Fig 4 :22). Each user writes their own refuter and states their own mode before running it (Formula, "Direction").

**Hard precondition:** stages 1 to 3 only work if the stage-1 output survives. The refuter in `01` section 5 must be stated before contact, and no link is sent before the verdict is recorded.
