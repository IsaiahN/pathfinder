## 4. Round trip gap R_T

The Symbols table defines R_T as the gap between what was sent up and what came back down, `gap(x, (T_E ∘ T_A)(x))`, measuring what the coarser description cannot hold. Fig 4 :30 requires it to be measured rather than assumed. I ran the round trip on the condensation the folder actually uses.

3. **Concretisation (T_E):** a concrete prediction about a named system, for example "doubling the interaction radius doubles the patch size and doubles the lifetime."

**R_T is nonzero.** Four things the shorthand holds do not survive the trip back down:

- **per-slot indexing** (Fig 1 :19): the shorthand speaks of "the structure", while the figures index the residual per slot; a global size hides where to look;

Conversely, one thing survives cleanly: the rule is invariant with scale while the cast changes, and size and persistence are different quantities. That is the residue worth sending.
