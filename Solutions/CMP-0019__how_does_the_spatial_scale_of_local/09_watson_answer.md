## VERDICT (from the figures)

The shape survives; the single numerical law does not. The rule of emergence is invariant with interaction scale, so `ell` changes cast rather than rule. Size is bounded by the enumerated habitat and the closure: wider reach makes larger structures reachable, but the within-closure shape is growth then saturation, and a jump needs a provenanced import. Persistence is a channel and ground property, not a size property, and the sign of the wider-contact effect is relative to the residual. Size and persistence are two currencies and are never averaged. The exponent is a declared `gap`, unreached rather than silence and not proof of absence. A scale change is a substituted habitat and carries an explicit cost. Mode `specified` on the per-domain verdicts, `general` on the kernel claim, `gap` on the number, not `grounded`, candidate until a ground settles it.

- **cost if wrong:** bounded and locatable, because the verdicts are per domain, mode-labelled and carry refuters. The avoidable cost is spending the independence of an outside reader by sending a recording or a fabricated exponent instead of the generator. Fig 2 :42 "Buy independence in proportion to the cost of being wrong." prices that cost. A wrong import of a model-specific exponent would add an unneeded atom at a cost above starting from primitives (Fig 6 :24).
- **undoable:** yes. The four shape claims and the refuters can be sent and later corrected without withdrawing any primitive. No number is asserted, so no number needs retracting. A sent shape can be superseded by a better-instrumented one.
- **reversed by:** a ground reading, not agreement. An instrument that reports channel state for a declared structure and shows persistence surviving a severed channel would break D3. A size that grows without bound inside a fixed closure would break D2. A change in the loop with scale at fixed cast would break D1. A declared unit in which size and persistence add without loss would break the union verdict. A stable exponent across low-overlap fields would convert the gap into a provenanced import. Until one of these fires, the verdict stands as `candidate`.
