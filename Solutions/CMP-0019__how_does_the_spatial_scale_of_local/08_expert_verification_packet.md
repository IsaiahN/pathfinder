## 1. The claim, in one paragraph

The spatial scale of local interactions does not change the rule of emergence; it changes the cast, that is which actors fall inside the enumerated habitat and how far the contact cascade reaches. Within a fixed closure, a wider reach makes larger settled structures reachable, but composition cannot add an atom, so the shape is growth then saturation with a jump only on import. Persistence is a different quantity with a different anchor: a structure persists while it stays settled against a ground that does not update and the channel to that ground remains reachable, so the effect of wider contact is residual-relative rather than a function of size. Size and persistence are two currencies and cannot be averaged. The numerical exponent is left open as a `gap`. Four refuters are written down below, before this paragraph was finalized.

## 2. Three checkable sub-claims

### C1 - framework-internal, checkable by reading Figure 4 and Figure 10

- **Statement:** the composition rule is invariant with scale; `ell` changes actors, currency and anchor only.
- **Source, verbatim:** Fig 4 :19-20: "The kernel is the part that does not change with size: the same loop and the same rule for naming what worked, whether one agent is running it or a thousand. Only the actors, the currency and the anchor change." Corroborated at low overlap by Fig 10 :68: "Different actors, a different currency, a different ground, and a lossy transform between." and Fig 12 :103: "Composition is recursive, and the level decides what is irreducible."
- **Refuter:** exhibit a case where the loop or the naming rule changes with interaction scale while actors, currency and anchor are held fixed. Or show a bond type that flips from symmetric to ordered because of range alone with the operands fixed. Either breaks C1.

### C2 - framework-internal plus domain check, checkable by reading Figure 11 and Figure 6

- **Statement:** size is bounded by the enumerated habitat and the closure. Within one closure the shape is growth then saturation; a jump requires an import with provenance and a shadow test.
- **Source, verbatim:** Fig 11 :43-44: "The habitat is enumerated, never composed." and "List everything in contact with the residual, then what is in contact with those, and outward until the cascade stops mattering." Fig 6 :21: "MINT composes inside the closure and can never add an atom." Fig 6 :23: "No frame escapes its own closure. The bound moves only on contact with a frame whose closure differs."
- **What to check:** for a system you know, does increasing the interaction reach actually bring new atoms into contact, or does it only increase the number of observations inside the same reach? Is any apparent size increase a substitution of habitat rather than a composition inside one closure?
- **Refuter:** show a structure whose size grows without bound as `ell` grows while the closure and the habitat atom set are fixed, or a size change that occurs with no new atoms entering contact. Either breaks C2.

### C3 - framework-internal, checkable by reading Figure 10 and Figure 11

- **Statement:** persistence is channel and ground contact, not size; the sign of the range effect is relative to the residual.
- **Source, verbatim:** Fig 10 :24: "the ground does not decay, the channel does". Fig 11 :40: "A system cannot be made more capable by improving the agent in isolation. An improvement that does not change contact changes nothing, however much it improves." Fig 11 :49: "An actor's valence is relative to the residual, not a property it carries."
- **What to check:** name the ground for your structure and an instrument that reports channel state. Would severing that channel end persistence while size stays fixed? Does the literature you know show a fixed sign for the range effect, or a sign that flips with the residual?
- **Refuter:** exhibit indefinite persistence after the channel to a non-updating ground is severed, or a strictly size-only persistence law with the channel held fixed. Either breaks C3.

## 4. What is explicitly not claimed

- Not a universal exponent, and not a monotone "wider is better" rule.
- Not that size and persistence are uncorrelated. They are different quantities and can co-vary; they cannot be averaged.
- Not that wider contact always stabilizes. The valence is residual-relative (C3).
- Not that a scale change preserves the habitat. Fig 11 :66: "Isolation is not removal of the habitat. It is substitution of one habitat for another."
- Not that the framework names a seventh primitive. Interaction scale is a parameter of the held space and network atoms.
- Not a derivation of a numerical law from the six-term set. The number is a `gap`.
- Not `grounded`. No measurement is asserted as settled.

## 6. What would settle it

1. **Habitat test:** enumerate contact outward from a declared residual and show whether new atoms enter the reachable set as `ell` grows.
2. **Reach test:** show whether size saturates inside a fixed closure or grows without bound.
3. **Channel test:** measure channel state and sever it for a declared structure; does persistence end while size stays fixed?
4. **Union test:** show whether size and persistence can be expressed in one declared unit with no loss.
5. **Instrument test:** obtain a stable exponent across low-overlap fields under a declared code, then import it with provenance and a shadow test rather than treating it as figure-derived.
