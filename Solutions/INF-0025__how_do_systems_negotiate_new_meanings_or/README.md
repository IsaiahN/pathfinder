## What is deliberately not here

- No personal emails of named individuals.
- No ranking of people by likelihood of agreement.
- No bulk-send plan.

Three reasons in the figures' words:

1. **Agreement is not a verdict.** Fig 2 :35: *"Alignment is triangulation, not negotiation. A mirror cannot correct you."* Selecting recipients for receptiveness manufactures the correlation that would make their agreement worthless.
2. **Correlation has valence.** Fig 2 :41: *"efficient for a convention: agreement about a shared meaning is the product, not a corruption of it"*; Fig 2 :40: *"disqualifying for a verdict: agreement about the world is worthless when the agreeing frames share an evidence pool"*. This question is about conventions, so agreement is the product there, but it must not be smuggled in as a verdict about the ground.
3. **Do not spend the checker's independence.** Fig 10 :42: *"A verifier that can reconstruct the claim cannot verify it."*
