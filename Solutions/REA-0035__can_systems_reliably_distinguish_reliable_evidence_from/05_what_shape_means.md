## 1. A shape is a constraint, not a filler

The canonical rule: the framework returns *"what a missing thing would have to look like (a shape, not a name)"*, then stops where instruments stop discriminating and records the lineage. Fig 9 :32: *"you get a kind of thing, never the answer itself"*. Fig 13: *"It can say the answer is out of reach with the library as it stands... It cannot say which primitive is missing, because naming it would mean already holding it."*

From *The Library of Babel*: "you can cut your search short if the set of books returned is small and descriptive enough that it provides a general shape of the answer that satisfies your question."

For REA-0035 the shape is therefore **not** a list of detector features and **not** the name of a winning architecture. It is the set of constraints any candidate "reliable distinguisher" must satisfy:

- the label must range against an anchor that does not update (Fig 2 :29, :33);
- the residual must be per claim, never averaged away (Fig 1 :19, :22);
- the reading must be sourced, not merely fluent (*Hard Problem is About Source*);
- the checker must not hold the detector's interiors (Fig 10 :42);
- the detector must report unreached slots rather than certify completeness (Fig 8 :37, Fig 13 :134).

`reliable = one number` is a name. `reliable = these five constraints, per slot, with a lineage` is a shape.

## 2. How the shape is obtained

- *Library of Babel*: use the vocabulary already held that is *closest* to the target, then recurse on recurring new terms that overlap it. "With each new term gained, you narrow the space and deepen your vocabulary."
- Fig 6 :30: *"The description travels. Fails by being sharpened into terms only we can read. Cast it in effect terms."* Fig 9 :30: *"your words are borrowed too, so describe the effect rather than the cause"*.
- *Decoding Tacit Knowledge*: "When contact isn't possible, the best you can send is a plain description of what you'd expect to see, because anyone can check that against the world."
- *Library of Babel* on the bundle: "Perhaps your problem was a bundle or conflation of several problems masquerading as one? You will need to search all of those problems individually to find their answers and then combine them in a satisfactory way." This is the essay-level precedent for splitting REA-0035 into four domains before answering it.

The effect description used at first contact in `02_outreach_email.md` is exactly this kind: a plain statement of what would have to be true, checkable without the framework.

## 3. What licenses a name

Two things, both outside the shape itself.

1. **The ground settles it.** Fig 2 :45: *"Frames range against the ground. They do not negotiate with it."* Formula step 5: "the ground settles it. Verified only by what pays. A gate is not the ground." A detector label is a gate until the record pays for it.
2. **A provenanced import from a frame whose closure differs**, with a source gate and a shadow test (Fig 6 :12 to :14). *They're Scanning Books*: "The real import is not a new term in the existing space. It is a new dimension of the space." A provenance channel is a new axis of discrimination, not a bigger feature list.

*They're Scanning Books* also removes any neutrality claim a detector might make about its own vocabulary: "A minimum viable set of six terms is a human's careful guess at reality's compression axes; another sensorium's minimum set need not be those six." Applied here, a detector's feature set is a visible choice, not a neutral reading of the world. *How Intelligence Actually Works* adds the boundary: "The recursion converges toward indiscernibility, and indiscernibility is not identity." A detector that cannot be distinguished from a reliable one by any available instrument is not thereby reliable.

## 4. The source discrimination limit, stated as a shape

*The Hard Problem is About Source*: "If several paths create the same behavior, then behavior is a projection with lossy information" and "An instrument that can't distinguish these cases demonstrates its own inadequacy." The essay's spectrometer example resolves green by a better instrument, not by an argument.

Applied here: a fluent, well-formatted, well-sourced-looking claim is a projection. Its source cannot be read off the projection. The shape of a fix is therefore an instrument that discriminates among possible producers, with the lineage kept. It is not a better style classifier and not a stronger prior that "credible outlets are true." C2PA's principles, opened separately, corroborate the gate distinction: a provenance assertion can be verified as correctly formed and free from tampering without any value judgment about whether the content is good or bad.

## 5. The bundle is part of the shape, not a prelude to it

REA-0035 asks about *systems* (plural) *reliably* distinguishing. Both words hide bundles.

- "systems" spans a model, a team, a platform and a standards body. Fig 4 :19: *"Only the actors, the currency and the anchor change."* Each has a different anchor, so they cannot share one verdict.
- "reliably" spans classification accuracy, correction landing and self-certification. The first has a ground, the second a legitimate human anchor for goodness-for-person, the third no internal anchor at all.

*The Library of Babel* says a bundle "masquerading as one" must be searched as several problems. *How a Society of Agents Would Work* says "a group is only worth having while its members stay different" and "The group's agreement is never the verdict." So adding more checkers to an undifferentiated room enlarges the bundle rather than resolving it.

## 6. Correction to my own first reading (kept visible)

My first reading treated "shape, not a name" as a blanket ban on naming any component of a detection system. That is too strong. The canonical phrase governs a *missing* primitive, one not held in the library. Where a component is already held, naming it is a binding from another frame, not the invention of a new primitive. What is forbidden is presenting the binding as a derivation, or as an import that moves the wall when it adds no atom (Fig 6 :21). The retraction is recorded in `01_figure_grounded_audit.md` section 7 and is not silently reversed here.

A second correction: I initially wrote that Fig 1 :23 ("the ground enters as the only metric") rules out human judgment everywhere. Fig 2 :36 to :38 qualifies it. Human judgment is the right anchor when the question is constitutively about people, and only an instrument otherwise. The correction-delivery domain therefore has a legitimate human anchor for "was this a good correction for this person," while the classification domain does not. Both readings are kept so the distinction stays visible.

## 7. Prestige and simulation, two limits on this file

*The Recursive Self-Improvement Trap*: "Prestige must never be allowed to validate an action directly... Prestige can fund an action. It can give an action a budget to be tried. But it cannot settle an action. Only the ground can settle it." A famous fact-checker endorsing this shape gives it a budget to be tried, not a verdict.

Same essay: "LLMs can quickly simulate an approximate answer/output with the formula found in the architecture post" and "the truly testable output would need to be derived from the real architecture or working the formula yourself." The canonical article is blunter: outputs "must be seen as simulacra" and "could never be verified if the solutions presented were not contrived to fit the formula." So this file, and the whole solution folder, is a simulated derivation checked for internal consistency. It cannot settle the question it reports on. It states what a settlement would have to look like.

## 8. Consequence for outreach

Because a shape is a constraint and not a name, the first contact must not ask "do you agree this detector is reliable?" It must ask "what would you count as a failure of these five constraints?" That is a check a reader can run without adopting the framework, and it preserves the independence that agreement would spend.
