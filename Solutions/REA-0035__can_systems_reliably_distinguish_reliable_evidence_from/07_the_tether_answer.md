# The Tether's answer on reliable evidence and persuasive misinformation

**Mode: `specified` on each per-domain shape verdict, `general` on the unbundling, nothing `grounded`.** This is a simulated derivation checked for internal consistency against the figures, not a ground reading (`05_what_shape_means.md` section 7). No detector, platform or institution was measured.

## 1. The residual it answers

A system labels an item that presents as evidence. Persuasive misinformation is optimised against the judging frames, so its fluency and its agreement with the audience pool are the very quantities a detector might be tempted to score. `R = |Γ(b,a) − o′|` per claim slot, where `o′` is what the ground eventually returns (formula step 1). The question's word "reliably" bundles four conditions with different anchors; answering it with one number averages away the live slots (Fig 1 :22 "Aggregating across slots is how a live signal disappears into an average.").

## 2. The shape, one loop per domain, verdicts kept separate

### D1, claim classification: reachable in shape, not certified

Fig 1 :23 "The ground enters as the only metric." Fig 2 :29 "An anchor is whatever settles the question and does not change its answer because of what any frame thinks." Fig 1 :19 "The residual is indexed by object slot, not aggregated."

A claim-level verdict is well formed only when the label ranges against a record that does not move when the system's frames push on it, and the residual is reported per claim. Fig 1 :13 permits a batch to look clean while one slot is live: "a global R near zero with one live slot residual is a legal state". So a benchmark average is a filter for where to look (Fig 9 :60 "Use filters for the budget and witnesses for the verdict. Never let a filter hand you a verdict."), never the verdict itself. Fluency and confidence are frame-internal (Fig 1 :27 "They are frame-internal. A frame cannot score itself with a quantity it also produces."). FEVER and FEVEROUS corroborate from the engineering pool: ignoring evidence beat using it, and label shortcuts are a documented benchmark bias. **Verdict: `specified`, triangulated between Fig 1, Fig 2 and the two NLP benchmarks. No deployed system is settled here.**

### D2, correction landing: a separate compound with a legitimate human anchor

Fig 2 :41 "agreement about a shared meaning is the product, not a corruption of it". Fig 2 :36 to :38 make human judgment the legitimate anchor when the question is constitutively about people, and only an instrument otherwise. So "did this correction land well for these readers?" is answerable against human judgment; "was the original claim true?" is not answerable against it. Fig 12 :107 "Settling compresses the description and not what it cost to establish." A debunk settled on one platform need not compose over on another that dropped its record. The two cognition studies corroborate at low overlap: enriched corrections did not stop outdated information being activated, and fact-checking reduced belief without eliminating the continued-influence limit. **Verdict: `specified`, triangulated between Fig 2, Fig 12 and the cognition literature.**

### D3, self-certification: foreclosed in principle

Fig 8 :37 "A frame cannot certify its own limit." Fig 8 :32 "no search, however long, adds an atom". Fig 9 :44 "Only a sealed room can be searched to the end, and no frame is a sealed room." Fig 8 :39 "So a search that finds nothing is never a proof of absence."

A detector cannot prove from inside its own closure that it has caught every relevant item. This is not a statement about current accuracy; it is a categorical limit. "Reliable" must therefore mean per-slot, ground-anchored, lineage-recorded and open about unreached cases, never a certificate of completeness. Fig 13 :134 "Unreachable is a verdict here, not a silence". **Verdict: `general`, triangulated across Fig 8 and Fig 9, with the framework's own article citing Godel, Tarski and Chaitin as the reasons.**

### D4, habitat and channel: capability is detector-and-habitat

Fig 11 :40 "An improvement that does not change contact changes nothing, however much it improves." Fig 10 :24 "the ground does not decay, the channel does". Fig 11 :43 "The habitat is enumerated, never composed." Fig 10 :25 "stale percepts, dropped transients".

Persuasive content typically wins by degrading the channel: repetition, saturation, pooling of distinct claims, staleness. The record does not change; access to it does. Fig 11 :49 "An actor's valence is relative to the residual, not a property it carries." A feed algorithm, a repeat bot and a correction can each be benefactor or malfactor depending on the residual. The habitat is read off the world, not composed from a theory of what a platform ought to contain. **Verdict: `specified`, single-source for the framework's habitat claim. No world source actually read in this derivation decides the habitat claim, so it is marked single-source rather than corroborated.**

### What the imports must be

Fig 6 :14 "the only operator that moves the wall". Fig 6 :32 "An instrument is not built from a description. It is improved from a worse instrument already returning something." Fig 9 :27 "describe it before you go looking, or any frame you pick will seem to fit".

When a fabrication appears that the detector's vocabulary cannot hold, more internal search cannot add the missing term. What can move the wall is a source-discriminating instrument with a recorded source gate and shadow test: a provenance chain, a signature, a timestamped retraction, or a second frame with a different closure. C2PA's principles corroborate the gate distinction from the standards field: verified assertions are not value judgments about truth. INWARD (sharpen an instrument already returning a partial signal) and OUTWARD (find the frame whose closure predicts the residual's shape) are opposed and must be run separately.

## 3. What it explicitly does not say

- Not that any named detector, company, platform or fact-checking organisation is reliable or unreliable. No ground reading was taken.
- Not that provenance data decides truth. A gate is not the ground; C2PA itself refuses that value judgment.
- Not that a larger labelled corpus generates detection of a new fabrication type. Fig 4 :25 "A recording carried upward looks like knowledge and is a description of one occasion." Fig 5 :71 "a library that grows is not a reach that grows".
- Not that agreement among checkers is corroboration when they share an evidence pool. Fig 2 :40 "agreement about the world is worthless when the agreeing frames share an evidence pool".
- Not that a search returning nothing proves there is nothing to find (Fig 8 :39).
- Not that the framework settles the question. The canonical article states a metatheory "cannot be validated, nor can it validate itself," and the RSI essay calls LLM outputs "simulacra."

## 4. What would settle it, and the standing falsifiers

Ground instruments, not further argument:

1. **Live-slot test.** Per-slot residuals published against a named non-updating anchor, evaluated on held-out claims where a correction previously failed to land. Falsifies D1 if failure remains above the declared threshold.
2. **Instrument test.** Introduce a source-discriminating instrument and measure whether detection of novel fabrications improves. Falsifies the import claim if it does not.
3. **Correction transfer test.** Measure the round trip for a correction crossing a platform boundary that drops its record. Fig 4 :30 "So take the round trip and measure the gap. Without it you are navigating by dead reckoning: confident about a position nobody checked."
4. **Completeness proof.** Any claim of self-certified reliability must produce the proof Fig 8 :37 forbids. If produced, the framework is wrong here.
5. **Overlap audit.** Two checkers' evidence pools must be described before their agreement is counted. If they share a pool, their agreement is one witness.

The four pre-registered refuters in `01_figure_grounded_audit.md` section 5 are the design instrument; these are the ground routes that would fire them.

## 5. The honest stop

**Unreached, not unreachable, for D1 and D2; unreachable in principle for D3.** The per-domain verdicts are deliberately not averaged. Where no instrument is returning a discriminating signal, there is no edge to extend from (Fig 6 :34 "Where nothing is, there is no edge to extend from, and the search for a proto-instrument is a search for one that is already there."). The answer stops at the limit of what the available instruments distinguish, and records the lineage so the stop is checkable. Indiscernibility is not identity: a detector no available instrument can distinguish from a reliable one has not thereby been shown reliable.

## 6. One-paragraph version for outreach

> The question is not one capability, so the Tether does not give one yes or no. It splits into four loops. A claim-level label is meaningful only when it ranges per claim against a record that does not move when the system pushes on it, and an aggregate accuracy number is a budget filter, never a verdict. A correct label is a different thing from a correction that actually lands, because those have different anchors and a settled debunk does not compose for free across a platform boundary. A system cannot certify its own completeness: a frame cannot certify its own limit, and a search that finds nothing is not proof of absence. Finally, capability is a property of the detector and its habitat, so the failure mode is usually a dead or saturated channel to the record, not a changed record. What the framework offers is the shape any answer must have, the import it needs (source-discriminating instruments with provenance gates), and refuters written in advance. It does not name a winning detector, and it does not claim it has measured one.
