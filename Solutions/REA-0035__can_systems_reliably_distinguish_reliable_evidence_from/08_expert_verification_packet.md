# Expert verification packet: the Tether on reliable evidence versus persuasive misinformation

**What this is:** a claim in a form a misinformation researcher, fact-checking practitioner, NLP engineer or measurement specialist can check. Mode, derivation and refuters are stated before the conclusion, so the reader knows what to look for and what would settle it.

**Mode: `specified` on the per-domain shape verdicts, `general` on the unbundling, not `grounded`.** No detector or platform was measured in this derivation. This packet is a simulated derivation checked for internal consistency against the figures, not a ground reading. It cannot settle the claim it reports on.

## 1. The claim, in one paragraph

"Reliably distinguish" is not one capability. It is a union of four loops with different anchors: claim classification against a non-updating ground, correction landing in an audience, the channel to the live record through a habitat, and the impossibility of a frame certifying its own completeness. Per-slot residuals against a named anchor are the only admissible verdict metric; aggregate accuracy and audience agreement are budget filters or contact signals, never witnesses. Source-discriminating instruments with provenance gates are the import that moves the wall when a novel fabrication appears. A correct label does not compose for free into a corrected belief. No named system is judged here.

## 2. Three checkable sub-claims

### C1 - Framework-internal; checkable by reading Figures 1, 2 and 9

- **Statement:** the verdict metric is per-slot `R` against an anchor that does not update; aggregate accuracy and shared-pool agreement are disqualified from the verdict column.
- **Source, verbatim:**
  - Fig 1 :19 "The residual is indexed by object slot, not aggregated."
  - Fig 1 :23 "The ground enters as the only metric."
  - Fig 2 :33 "The anchor must not update."
  - Fig 2 :40 "agreement about the world is worthless when the agreeing frames share an evidence pool"
  - Fig 9 :60 "Use filters for the budget and witnesses for the verdict. Never let a filter hand you a verdict."
- **What to check:** is there any rule in the figure set under which a batch average or a crowd's agreement settles a live slot? Also check Fig 2 :36 to :38: human judgment is the legitimate anchor when the question is constitutively about people, which is why correction quality is a separate domain and not a contradiction of this sub-claim.
- **Refuter:** exhibit a decision rule in which a global accuracy figure or an agreement figure settles a specific live slot without measuring that slot against a non-updating anchor.

### C2 - Field-facing; checkable by a fact-checking researcher or NLP engineer

- **Statement:** a correct claim label and a correction that lands are different terms, and a growing library of labelled false claims does not generate detection of a novel fabrication type.
- **Source, verbatim:**
  - Fig 12 :107 "Settling compresses the description and not what it cost to establish."
  - Fig 4 :25 "A recording carried upward looks like knowledge and is a description of one occasion."
  - Fig 5 :71 "a library that grows is not a reach that grows"
  - Fig 6 :32 "An instrument is not built from a description. It is improved from a worse instrument already returning something."
- **What to check:** FEVER (Thorne et al., 2018) reports 31.87 percent accuracy with correct evidence against 50.91 percent ignoring it; FEVEROUS (Aly et al., 2021) records label-prediction shortcuts as a dataset bias. Schüler et al. (2026) found that illustrating updated information "did not prevent outdated or false information from being mentally activated during reading." Zhou et al. (2026) found fact-checking reduced belief without eliminating continued influence. Are those faithful descriptions, and do they support the distinction rather than a claim that corrections never work?
- **Refuter:** show a labelled-instance library generating detection of a novel fabrication type with no new instrument and no import, with the lineage demonstrating the new type was not in the atoms; or show a correction that settles on one platform and composes over on another that dropped its record with a measured round-trip gap of zero.

### C3 - Framework-internal limit; checkable by reading Figures 8 and 9

- **Statement:** no system can certify from inside its own closure that it has distinguished every relevant item. "Reliable" must be per-slot, ground-anchored and open about unreached cases, not a completeness certificate.
- **Source, verbatim:**
  - Fig 8 :37 "A frame cannot certify its own limit."
  - Fig 8 :32 "no search, however long, adds an atom"
  - Fig 8 :39 "So a search that finds nothing is never a proof of absence."
  - Fig 9 :44 "Only a sealed room can be searched to the end, and no frame is a sealed room."
  - Fig 13 :134 "Unreachable is a verdict here, not a silence"
- **What to check:** the figure cites Godel, Tarski and Chaitin as proved theorems (Fig 8 :34 to :44, Godel 1931; Tarski 1936; Chaitin 1974). Is the inference from those theorems to a self-certification limit sound as the figure states it? Note that the limit is about completeness certificates, not about whether a detector can be correct on a given claim.
- **Refuter:** produce a proof, from inside a frame's own closure, that the closure contains every item of the relevant kind. If sound, this breaks C3 and the framework's Figure 8.

## 3. What is explicitly not claimed

- Not that any named detector, platform, outlet or fact-checking body is reliable or unreliable. No ground reading was taken.
- Not that provenance data decides truth. The C2PA Guiding Principles say a provenance standard "SHOULD NOT provide value judgments about whether a given set of provenance data is 'good' or 'bad'"; formula step 5 says "A gate is not the ground."
- Not that fact-checking is ineffective. The corpus of experiments cited here generally finds modest but real improvements in belief accuracy; the claim is only that correction landing is a separate loop and that continued influence bounds it.
- Not that a crowd's agreement is worthless for everything. It is disqualifying for a verdict about the world and efficient for a convention about shared meaning (Fig 2 :39 to :41).
- Not that a larger corpus is useless. SEARCHED is asked first and more search with independent searchers legitimately finds missed items at the same reach.
- Not that the figure set settles the question. The canonical article states a metatheory "cannot be validated, nor can it validate itself."

## 5. What would settle it

1. A live-slot evaluation with per-slot residuals against a named non-updating anchor on held-out claims where corrections previously failed to land.
2. An A/B test of a source-discriminating instrument against a surface-feature baseline on novel fabrications, with the import's source gate and shadow test recorded.
3. A round-trip measurement of correction transfer across a platform boundary that drops the correction's record.
4. A completeness proof for a detector, or its absence recorded explicitly as unreached, not silently treated as success.
