## 1. Independent domain census

I reconstructed the census from the residual line in `README.md` alone before comparing it with `01` section 1, using the WATSON order: R by arity, symmetry and scale; one line per domain with actors / currency / anchor; partition or union; order by dependency.

- **R (arity / symmetry / scale).** R is the gap between a label that presents as evidence and what the ground returns later, per claim slot. Arity: the question packages at least three conditions (classify, change belief, certify) plus a habitat, so it is a union compound (Fig 9 :54 "unbundle before you search again"), not one hard question. Symmetry: the labels are not symmetric. Provenance bears on truth while persuasiveness is disqualified for a verdict. Scale: first order per claim, second order at the detector plus checkers, third order at the habitat.
- **D1, claim classification.** Actors: claim, detector, ground record. Currency: per-slot residual, cost of a correction. Anchor: the ground, pinned and non-updating. Question type: union with the others.
- **D2, correction landing.** Actors: audience, message, platform, corrector. Currency: agreement, attention, belief change. Anchor: human judgment is legitimate for a question constitutively about people, an instrument otherwise (Fig 2 :36). Question type: union with D1; a correct label that never lands is a compound.
- **D3, self-certification.** Actors: detector, designers, auditors. Currency: confidence, clearance counts. Anchor: none internal; the ground is external. Question type: union; answered by a refusal, not a loop.
- **D4, habitat and channel.** Actors: feed ranking, repeaters, fact-checkers, platforms. Currency: contact, introduction, cascade. Anchor: the ground, reached at two or more steps out. Question type: union with D2; the habitat itself has no non-updating anchor (Fig 11 :73).
- **Not a domain.** The information ecosystem alone. It has actors and currency but no anchor that does not update, so it is a habitat, where actors come from, not what settles.

Order by dependency: a live channel first, then terms for source and record, then a per-slot objective against an unmoving anchor, then a method that can be carried upward; refusal before mint (formula step 2); self-certification last, answered by the absence of an internal anchor.

My census matches the primary's four domains and its union reading. Different starting points within the same pool, same boundary. Because the pool is shared, that agreement is weak and not circular only in route. It is still one witness.
