## 1. A shape is a constraint, not a filler

The figures README states the limit: the framework returns "what a missing thing would have to look like (a shape, not a name)." Fig 9 :32: **"you get a kind of thing, never the answer itself."** Fig 13 :134: **"Unreachable is a verdict here, not a silence"**, and the same passage says "It cannot say which primitive is missing, because naming it would mean already holding it."

From *The Library of Babel* (opened this session): "you can cut your search short if the set of books returned is small and descriptive enough that it provides a general shape of the answer that satisfies your question."

So for this question a **shape** is the narrowed form of the answer we can state before we can name the channel. It constrains: growth is a per-slot signed cycle against a threshold, the timing gap is a reachability failure in a closed light-seed channel, and the repairs are an import and a re-bonding. It does not fill: it does not say "direct-collapse seeds did it" or "super-Eddington accretion did it." `SHAPE(growth channel) with slot = ???` is a shape. `heavy seed` is a name.

The same essay names the bundle explicitly: "Perhaps your problem was a bundle or conflation of several problems masquerading as one?" That is the world-source form of Fig 9 :54-57, and it is why F5 of the audit refuses the single-mechanism reading.

## 2. How the shape is obtained

- *Library of Babel*: "With each new term gained, you narrow the space and deepen your vocabulary." Use the vocabulary you already have that is closest to the thing, look for recurring new terms that overlap with it, recurse.
- Fig 6 :30: "outward: does another frame already hold it? The description travels. Fails by being sharpened into terms only we can read. Cast it in effect terms." Fig 9 :30-31: "your words are borrowed too, so describe the effect rather than the cause."
- *Decoding Tacit Knowledge* (quote carried): "When contact isn't possible, the best you can send is a plain description of what you'd expect to see, because anyone can check that against the world." For this question, that means: if the shape is true, we expect the earliest quasars to show overmassive black holes relative to hosts, episodic rather than continuous high accretion rates, and a correlation with dense gas and merger signatures. Those are all checkable, and all three were seen in the opened world sources.
- *Library of Babel* on stopping: "you will know that you are done because the finish line will also be the starting place" and "you have already composed the answer through the process of locating it." Here the stopping point is the instrument gap of F9, not a mechanism name.

## 3. What licenses a name

Only two things, both outside the shape itself.

1. **The ground settles it.** Formula step 5: "the ground settles it. Verified only by what pays." The README says the framework "stops at the limit of what the available instruments can distinguish, and records the lineage so the stop is honest." A source-discriminating measurement (mass, spin, obscuration, merger-rate distribution, JWST plus LISA) would name the channel.
2. **A provenanced import from a frame whose closure differs** (Fig 6 :23, Fig 8 :50-52), with a source gate and a shadow test. Naming the heavy-seed channel is such a binding from the halo-physics frame onto the growth frame, with provenance. It is not a derivation from the six terms.

*They're Scanning Books* (quote carried): "The real import is not a new term in the existing space. It is a new dimension of the space." Also: "A minimum viable set of six terms is a human's careful guess at reality's compression axes; another sensorium's minimum set need not be those six." And: "Declaring the primitives does not make them neutral. It makes the choice visible." That last line is why we report the binding rather than hiding it.

## 4. Why "the mass reading names the mechanism" is the forbidden error

*The Hard Problem is About Source* (quote carried) gives the mechanism. Metamerism: an instrument reads green and "cannot comment on the source" - one green light, or yellow plus blue, or RGB, or CMYK, or a scheme not yet discovered. "If several paths create the same behavior, then behavior is a projection with lossy information." "An instrument that can't distinguish these cases demonstrates its own inadequacy." It was the spectrometer, not an argument, that resolved it.

The mass and luminosity readings of a high-redshift quasar are exactly such a projection: several accretion regimes and several seed histories yield the same broad-line width and the same luminosity. The A&A 2024 "Size matters" paper (opened this session) puts the mass uncertainty at up to an order of magnitude and notes that super-Eddington models prefer lower masses and higher rates. Naming the channel from the mass reading alone is the metamerism error. What is owed is a source-discriminating instrument, not a name.

*The Recursive Self-Improvement Trap* (opened this session): "the output is only as good as the validator. The agent, or the tool, or the frame, or the theory, cannot validate itself." Agreement among three reviews that share a simulation literature is not such a validator.

## 5. The question this section answers

**What licenses a particular growth channel as the answer?** Nothing in the framework. The framework returns a shape. A channel is licensed only by the ground settling a term, or by a provenanced import with a shadow test. Until one of those, the channel is the searcher's own contribution and must be recorded as such. This is the answer to the "field for public contact points" request too: the first contact should be asked what would discriminate sources, not which mechanism they believe.

## 6. Correction to my own §1 and §3 (kept visible)

I initially wrote "the framework refuses to name any mechanism, so naming gas, seeds, feedback or thresholds is forbidden." That overstates the rule. The README's phrase is "what a **missing** thing would have to look like (a shape, not a name)." The subject is a primitive not held in the frame. Gas, seeds, Lyman-Werner radiation, the Eddington threshold and accretion discs are all already in the physics frame. Naming them is a **binding** from that frame onto the derivation, which is provenance, not the invention of a new primitive.

What remains wrong in any record that names heavy seeds as *the* answer is not the naming but the promotion: treating a binding as a figure-derived verdict, or claiming it moves the wall in reality. Fig 6 :25 "An import adds no atom to the world." Both claims are now recorded so neither is quietly lost. The same correction is applied in `06_isomers_and_order.md` Q1 and in the retraction log of `01_figure_grounded_audit.md`.

## 7. Two consequences for this folder

**Prestige is not action.** From *The Recursive Self-Improvement Trap* (opened this session): "Prestige can fund an action. It can give an action a budget to be tried. But it cannot settle an action. Only the ground can settle it. The Ground Maintainer must be blind to prestige." A list of famous reviewers who agree with this derivation is the named collapse, not corroboration. The legitimate ask is to give the claim a budget to be tried and let an instrument settle it.

**This whole folder is a simulation, not a ground reading.** Same essay: "LLMs can quickly simulate an approximate answer/output with the formula found in the architecture post... However, the truly testable output would need to be derived from the real architecture or working the formula yourself." So `01` through `11` are a simulated derivation checked for internal consistency against the figures. They are not a test of any astrophysical claim and cannot settle the question they report on. No measurement was taken by this author.
