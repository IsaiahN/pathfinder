# The Tether's answer on early supermassive black hole growth

**Mode: `specified` on the per-domain claims, `general` on the structural shape, `gap` on which channel dominated in nature. Not `grounded`.** Simulated derivation checked for internal consistency against the figures (`05_what_shape_means.md` section 7), not a ground reading.

## 1. The residual it answers

A single high-redshift quasar has a measured mass of order 10^9 solar masses within the first billion years. A light stellar-remnant seed growing at the Eddington limit in a shallow potential well predicts less mass in that time. The residual is the shortfall, indexed per object (Fig 1 :19 "The residual is indexed by object slot, not aggregated"), with the measured mass and redshift as the anchor (Fig 2 :29). The question of how they grow so fast is a union of overlapping conditions, not one mechanism with an opposite answer (Fig 9 :54-57).

## 2. The shape

1. **Growth is a signed cycle, not a chain.** Fig 12 :121: "The output re-enters as input. The sign of the loop decides whether it closes, and a chain cannot close by construction." More mass deepens the potential and increases the inflow; feedback inserts a negative arc. The 2024 LID-568 measurement (Eddington ratio 41.5, opened 2026-11-23) and the 2025 lensed quasar (intrinsic ratio above 4.9) are observations of the positive arc on the high side of the threshold.

2. **The Eddington ceiling is a threshold comparison, not a wall.** Operators :49: "a threshold relation between two quantities." Super-Eddington accretion is a change of loop gain, not the abolition of a limit. Fig 9 :60: "Use filters for the budget and witnesses for the verdict. Never let a filter hand you a verdict." The Eddington ratio is a filter for the time budget, not the verdict about the channel.

3. **The timing gap is a reachability failure inside a closed light-seed channel.** Fig 6 :21: "MINT composes inside the closure and can never add an atom." Fig 8 :32: "Closure is idempotent. Composing inside a closed set never leaves it, so no search, however long, adds an atom." Within that closure, more search over accretion histories adds nothing. Two repairs exist and they are categorically different: a **heavy seed** is an import into the growth frame from the halo frame, which moves the wall (Fig 6 :14 "the only operator that moves the wall") and needs a source gate and a shadow test; **super-Eddington accretion** is a re-bonding inside the closure that changes the gain without adding an atom.

4. **The conditions form a union, not a competition.** Fig 9 :55: "A bundled problem is usually a union of overlapping conditions, not a list of separate ones. A single lever clears the region where only one condition holds and leaves the intersections standing, because the second condition still binds." Seed mass, accretion rate, gas contact, feedback duty cycle, metallicity, radiation field and mergers overlap in individual objects. Volonteri, Habouzit and Colpi 2021 (Nature Reviews Physics, opened 2026-11-23) agree in world terms: "Paths to seed formation and growth are not mutually exclusive." GA-NIFS/EIGER finds an overmassive black hole at z=7.08 in a host "undergoing a major merger," so the conditions co-occur.

5. **The habitat is enumerated, and valence is relative to the residual.** Fig 11 :49: "An actor's valence is relative to the residual, not a property it carries." Lyman-Werner radiation starves a Pop III seed of cold gas and simultaneously enables direct collapse by suppressing H2 cooling. Fig 11 :40: "An improvement that does not change contact changes nothing, however much it improves." Raising disc efficiency without a gas reservoir changes nothing.

6. **The framework does not name the channel.** Fig 9 :32: "you get a kind of thing, never the answer itself." Calling the answer "heavy seeds" or "super-Eddington accretion" is a binding from the physics frame, recorded as provenance, not a figure-derived verdict. A heavy seed is not a seventh primitive; it is an imported atom in the growth frame (Fig 6 :25 "An import adds no atom to the world").

7. **Which channel dominated is a `gap`.** Fig 13 :134: "Unreachable is a verdict here, not a silence." Fig 8 :39: "So a search that finds nothing is never a proof of absence." The opened literature says the duty cycle of super-Eddington accretion is not yet constrained and that distinguishing assembly mechanisms will be difficult. A source-discriminating instrument is owed. That is an honest stop, not an invitation to invent a fraction.

## 3. What it explicitly does not say

- Not that direct-collapse seeds are the answer, and not that super-Eddington accretion is universal. The union verdict assigns conditions per slot, never a winner.
- Not a growth percentage, duty cycle, seed mass function or redshift cutoff. No figure line returns a number, and no opened observation settles one.
- Not a theory of everything, no final mechanism. The framework returns a shape with an open slot.
- Not that the mass readings are pure ground. Their error depends on accretion regime (A&A 2024: up to an order of magnitude). The ground does not decay (Fig 10 :24), but the channel does.
- Not `grounded`. Nothing here was settled by measurement by this author.

## 4. What would settle it, and what the ground actually is

A source-discriminating instrument, not another argument. From *The Hard Problem is About Source* (quote carried in `05`): a spectrometer resolved the green-light metamerism because it could comment on the source. The corresponding instruments here are:

- **Mass and spin distribution at z greater than 6** with accretion-regime-independent calibrators. This tests whether the residual is as large as F7 assumes. Falsifier 3 of the audit fires if masses move down by an order of magnitude.
- **Duty-cycle measurement of super-Eddington phases** from a large sample of LID-568-like objects. The discovery paper itself says "Future studies on a large sample of such objects will help to constrain the duty cycle." Falsifier 2 fires if the duty cycle is too low.
- **Host mass ratio and merger signature** for the faintest high-z quasars. The 2018 ApJL paper predicts heavy seeds will show unusually high BH-to-halo ratios; Abell2744-QSO1 and GA-NIFS/EIGER are early returns. Falsifier 4 fires if one channel alone accounts for every object.
- **Gravitational-wave merger rates with LISA**, which Inayoshi et al. 2020 identify as the complementary probe to electromagnetic accretion signatures. This is the low-overlap instrument that could break the shared evidence pool.
- **Standing audit falsifiers:** derive a light-seed Eddington-limited path to 10^9 solar masses at z above 6 in a realised host; show super-Eddington accretion is unsustainable at the needed density; measure a direct violation of the habitat-contact condition.

## 5. The honest stop

**Unreached, not unreachable.** The seed and accretion vocabulary is a binding from the astrophysics frame onto a general shape; declaring it makes the choice visible, not neutral. The framework stops at the limit of what the available instruments can distinguish and records the lineage so the stop is checkable. "Indiscernibility is not identity" is the canonical usage rule. The 2025 overmassive-black-hole analysis of Luberto and Furlanetto adds a live tension, finding such objects would need to grow "at least an order of magnitude more slowly than expected" to rejoin the local relation. That tension is recorded as evidence for the union, not as a flipped verdict.

## 6. One-paragraph version for outreach

> The Tether does not name one mechanism for early supermassive black hole growth, and it does not claim the final answer. It says that each quasar must be treated as one slot, not averaged into a growth history; that accretion is a reinforcing cycle against an Eddington threshold, so growing above that limit is a change of loop gain rather than a miracle; that a light seed composing inside a closed channel cannot reach a billion solar masses in the available time, so either an import (a heavy seed from direct collapse) or a re-bonding (episodic super-Eddington accretion) is owed; and that the conditions, including Lyman-Werner radiation, gas density, metallicity and mergers, form a union whose valence flips with the residual. It refuses to choose a channel because it returns a shape, not a name. What it offers is the shape any candidate answer must have, six falsifiers written in advance, and the instruments that would close the gap. The slot stays open until a source-discriminating measurement settles it.
