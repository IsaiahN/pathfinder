# Outreach draft: check one derived output before deciding about the framework

**Use:** one-to-one, or as a cover letter attached to a submission through the public venues in `03_public_contact_points.csv`. **Not** for bulk send.

**Hard precondition:** do not send until the shape claim and its refuter have been separated in the sender's own head. The recipient's verdict on stage 1 must be recorded before any theory is shown. If that order is reversed, their later agreement is not evidence (Fig 2 :40 "agreement about the world is worthless when the agreeing frames share an evidence pool").

**Subject:** One checkable claim about early black hole growth, before any theory

> Hi [name],
>
> I have one specific output I would like you to check against what you already know. I am not asking you to read the framework first, and I am not asking you to agree with it. If the output does not survive, there is nothing else to look at.
>
> **The output (`specified`, not `grounded`):** take a single high-redshift quasar as one slot, not the population average. Its measured mass is around 10^9 solar masses within the first billion years. The default growth channel, a stellar-remnant seed accreting at the Eddington limit, predicts less mass in that time. Figures 1 and 9 of the framework I am using give two structural constraints on that gap. First, the residual is indexed per object slot and disappears into an average if you aggregate (Fig 1 :19, :22). Second, the conditions that could close it form a union, not a list of competing answers: a single lever clears the cases where only one condition binds and leaves the intersections standing (Fig 9 :55).
>
> From the framework's bond rules (Fig 12) it follows that growth is a cycle, not a chain: the output re-enters as input, and the sign of the loop decides whether it closes. Accretion is a reinforcing loop; radiative and mechanical feedback are a balancing loop. The Eddington ceiling is a threshold comparison, not a wall, so accretion above it is a change of loop gain, not the abolition of a limit. A heavy seed is an import into the growth frame from the halo frame, which is categorically different from re-bonding inside the frame.
>
> **What the framework does not tell us:** which channel dominated in nature, or the duty cycle of super-Eddington phases. It returns a shape, not a name. I am not claiming that direct-collapse seeds are the answer, that super-Eddington accretion is universally required, or that any figure derives a percentage.
>
> **What would refute the shape claim:**
> 1. A light seed shown to reach 10^9 solar masses by z greater than 6 under Eddington-limited accretion in a realised host, measured against a non-updating anchor.
> 2. Super-Eddington accretion shown to be unsustainable at the required duty cycle in the dense gas environments it needs.
> 3. The high-redshift masses revised downward by the order of magnitude allowed by current virial estimates, so the timing gap disappears.
> 4. A low-overlap instrument assigning every early supermassive black hole to one channel with no overlap.
>
> I wrote these down before sending this. If any one holds, I will retract the claim. A one-line "this fails because..." is more useful to me than agreement. If you would rather not engage, no reply is needed.
>
> **If it survives, and only then:** the sources are below. The figures are the authority, not my summary of them.
> - Local verbatim figure text with line numbers: `FRAMEWORK/reference/03-FIGURES_TEXT.md` in the Project Pathfinder workspace.
> - The canonical figures README, branch `general-agent`: https://github.com/IsaiahN/tether/blob/general-agent/figures/README.md

## Two-stage discipline, made explicit

**Stage 1 before stage 2.** Send only the residual, the shape claim and the four refuters. Ask what the recipient would count as a failure. Record their answer *before* showing any figure text. Fig 10 :52 and Fig 2 :45.

**Stage 2 is opt-in by construction.** A recipient who has absorbed the framework before giving a verdict has joined the frame being checked (Fig 10 :42). Their later agreement is never pooled as corroboration (Fig 2 :40).

**Stage 3 sends a method, which is permitted.** Fig 4 :22. The formula is a generator, so giving it away does not give away an answer. Each user still needs a ground and a self-written refuter per problem.

No request for trust, no request for agreement, no "bombshell" framing, and no bulk distribution.
