## 0. The residual under audit, in effect terms

The observation (o') is a black hole of order 10^9 solar masses shining as a quasar less than about 700 million years after the Big Bang, at redshift z greater than 6. The prediction (Γ(b,a)) from the default frame is a stellar-remnant seed of order 10 to 100 solar masses growing at the Eddington limit inside a shallow potential well. The residual R is the mass shortfall at a fixed cosmic time: what the channel predicts is smaller than what was measured. That is a real effect gap, not a wording puzzle.

Three disciplines must be stated before any figure line is chosen.

1. **Arity.** More than one condition binds. Seed mass, accretion rate relative to a ceiling, gas supply (contact with the reservoir), radiative feedback and time are all load bearing. So this is a **union compound**, not a single hard question with opposite answers (Fig 9 :57 "a case in two conditions is not a harder case of either").
2. **Symmetry.** Mass growth is ordered in time. The relevant bonds are sequence and production (Fig 12, Operators table), not a symmetric join. The Eddington relation is a threshold comparison (Fig 12 :87 "a threshold relation between two quantities"; Operators :49), so it is a `⋛` relation between a luminosity and a ceiling, not an arithmetic equality that can be swapped.
3. **Scale.** The phenomenon runs from the Schwarzschild radius to cosmological filaments, and from subsecond accretion physics to the age of the Universe (Volonteri, Habouzit and Colpi 2021, Nature Reviews Physics, opened 2026-11-23). That makes it a membrane problem (Fig 4) and a level-relativity problem (Fig 12 :103).

**Bound ordinary term (written before the run).** A growth channel is a cycle with a sign, a ceiling, an anchor and a carrier. A candidate answer to "how do they grow so fast" must name, for each object slot, which sign runs the accretion loop, which threshold is crossed, which measurement anchors the mass, and what carries the seed into the frame. This is the sought-for shape made refusable (formula step "Direction"; Fig 5 :65 "make the sought-for shape an ORDINARY TERM").

**What an answer is not allowed to be here.** It is not a standing list of mechanisms that "help", because Fig 11 :51 says "No standing list of good actors survives a change of residual." ## 3. Findings F1 to F9

### F1: The residual is per object slot, never an average (governs D1)

Fig 1 :19: **"The residual is indexed by object slot, not aggregated."**
Fig 1 :22: **"Aggregating across slots is how a live signal disappears into an average."**

The question as written ("supermassive black holes" plural) invites a pooled growth history. The figure refuses the average. Each quasar is a slot with its own mass, redshift, accretion rate and host. The review by Inayoshi, Visbal and Haiman (2020, Annual Review of Astronomy and Astrophysics 58:27-97, opened 2026-11-23) says the ultrarare 10^9 solar mass objects are "only the tip of the iceberg" and that early black holes "likely fill a continuum from the stellar-mass to the supermassive regimes, reflecting a range of initial masses and growth histories." That is exactly the per-slot picture. Mode `general` for the structural requirement, `specified` for the claim that the continuum is real (single-source on the figure side, world corroboration from Inayoshi et al.).

**Refuter:** a result that predicts the population mean well and every individual quasar badly, with no slot-level residual reported, fails this finding.

### F2: Growth is a cycle, and the sign decides whether it closes (governs D2)

Fig 12 :121: **"cycle. The output re-enters as input. The sign of the loop decides whether it closes, and a chain cannot close by construction."**
Fig 12 :5: **"The bond is the rest of the information, and it is the half nothing was recording."**

Accretion is a reinforcing loop: more mass deepens the potential, a deeper potential captures more gas, more gas adds more mass. Radiative and mechanical feedback are a balancing loop that can reverse the inflow. Calling growth a chain of ingredients (seed plus gas plus time) records the atoms and not the bond, which is the flat-formula failure Fig 12 :5 names.

Two genuinely low-overlap world corroborators were opened. The System Dynamics Society page "What is System Dynamics?" (2020) says: "If the tendency in the loop is to reinforce the initial action, the loop is called a positive or reinforcing feedback loop; if the tendency is to oppose the initial action, the loop is called a negative or balancing feedback loop. The sign of the loop is called its polarity." Kefi et al. 2016 in Functional Ecology says "the sign of the loop crucially depends on the number of negative interactions involved" and that positive feedback is "a necessary (but not sufficient) condition" for alternative stable states. Both fields are far from astrophysics, so they are real corroboration of the shape, not of the astrophysical fact. Mode `general` for the cycle shape, `specified` for the claim that the accretion loop is the dominant sign at high z.

**Triangulation:** triangulated (Fig 12 plus two low-overlap fields, though the two world sources share a general systems-theory pool, so they count as one witness on the world side). **Refuter:** a demonstration that the mass growth of a grown quasar is exhaustively a one-pass sequence with no re-entrant input (no dependence of capture cross-section on accumulated mass) would break the cycle reading.

### F3: The Eddington ceiling is a threshold, not a wall (governs D2)

Fig 12 :87: **"a threshold relation between two quantities."** Operators table :49 repeats it.
Operators table :52: **"a goal is a comparison, which is why a vocabulary without it cannot state one."**

The Eddington limit compares luminosity with the radiation-pressure ceiling. It is a `⋛` comparison, ordered, between two quantities. Operating above it is not abolishing the ceiling; it is being on the other side of a threshold, which requires a different bond (photon trapping, a slim disc, a dense envelope) to be statable. Super-Eddington accretion is therefore a change of gain in the cycle of F2, not a miracle. Smith and Bromm 2019 (Contemporary Physics, arXiv:1904.12890, opened 2026-11-23) state "The Eddington limit serves as the canonical criterion for inhibiting black hole growth" but also that "thin-disc configurations allow radiation to preferentially escape in the polar directions" and that a super-Eddington quasar at z=6.6 has been discovered. The LID-568 paper (Suh et al. 2024, Nature Astronomy, opened 2026-11-23) reports an Eddington ratio of 41.5, and the lensed quasar J0025-0145 (2025, Astrophysical Journal Letters, opened 2026-11-23) has an intrinsic ratio above 4.9.

Fig 9 :60: **"Use filters for the budget and witnesses for the verdict. Never let a filter hand you a verdict."** The Eddington ratio is a filter for the time budget. It is not itself the verdict about which channel operated. Mode `specified`, single-source on the figure side (Operators and Fig 12 are one figure pool), world corroboration from four independent observational papers.

**Refuter:** a channel that grows at a rate which does not compare with a ceiling at all (no quantity against which the rate is measured) would not be a `⋛` relation and would break this typing.

### F4: The seed is a reachability question, and a heavy seed is an import with provenance (governs D2)

Fig 6 :21: **"MINT composes inside the closure and can never add an atom."**
Fig 6 :23: **"No frame escapes its own closure. The bound moves only on contact with a frame whose closure differs."**
Fig 6 :14: **"the only operator that moves the wall."**

Inside a closed growth frame, a light stellar-remnant seed composes with gas, time and feedback. If that composition cannot reach 10^9 solar masses within the time budget, more search inside the same frame adds nothing (Fig 8 :32 "Closure is idempotent. Composing inside a closed set never leaves it, so no search, however long, adds an atom"). Two repairs are available and they are categorically different.

- An **import**: a heavy seed from direct collapse of a primordial gas cloud (10^4 to 10^6 solar masses) enters the growth frame from the halo frame. This adds an atom to the growth frame, so it moves the wall (Fig 6 :14). It requires a source gate (provenance) and a shadow test (does it explain the timing gap?) from Fig 8.
- A **different bond**: super-Eddington accretion changes the gain of the cycle of F2 without adding a new atom. It is not an import. It is a re-bonding inside the closure.

These are the two solutions the literature itself names. Smith and Bromm 2019 write that "possible solutions to decrease the growth time are: (i) start the growth from heavy seeds, and (ii) allow extremely large accretion rates in the high-z Universe" (quoting Haiman and Loeb, Begelman, Pacucci and Ferrara, Inayoshi et al.). The detection paper for heavy-seed candidates (2018, Astrophysical Journal Letters, opened 2026-11-23) states that "observations have not yet confirmed or refuted this scenario."

A re-census correction is owed here, and it is kept visible. A heavy seed is **not** a seventh primitive of the six-term substrate. It is an atom imported into the growth frame from a different frame (the atomic-cooling halo). Saying "import adds an atom" (Fig 6) is true of the growth frame, not of reality. Fig 6 :25 "An import adds no atom to the world." Mode `specified`, single-source on the figure side for the import rule, world corroboration from three astrophysics sources.

**Refuter:** a demonstrated light-seed path to 10^9 solar masses by z greater than 6 under Eddington-limited accretion in a realised host, measured against a non-updating anchor, would collapse the necessity of the import or the re-bonding.

### F5: The headline channels form a union, not a competition (governs D2 and D3)

Fig 9 :54: **"And when the split lands on a union rather than a partition, unbundle before you search again."**
Fig 9 :55: **"A bundled problem is usually a union of overlapping conditions, not a list of separate ones. A single lever clears the region where only one condition holds and leaves the intersections standing, because the second condition still binds."**

Heavy seed, super-Eddington accretion, mergers, dense gas and long duty cycles are overlapping conditions. A single lever (say, raising the accretion rate) clears cases where only the rate binds and leaves the intersection where the seed mass also binds. Volonteri, Habouzit and Colpi 2021 state "Paths to seed formation and growth are not mutually exclusive" and that "indirect constraints can confirm that a given path exists but cannot rule out the existence of other paths." That second sentence is the figure's "a search that finds nothing is never a proof of absence" (Fig 8 :39) in astrophysical clothing. The GA-NIFS and EIGER result (2025, Astronomy and Astrophysics, opened 2026-11-23) finds an overmassive black hole of about 1.9 times 10^9 solar masses in a quasar host at z=7.08 that is "undergoing a major merger," so seed, accretion and merger conditions co-occur in one slot. Mode `specified`, triangulated (Fig 9 plus Volonteri et al., different evidence pools but both about conditions rather than a single cause).

**Refuter:** a measurement showing that one channel alone accounts for every high-redshift supermassive black hole, with the others contributing zero, would narrow the union to a partition and would refute this finding.

### F6: The habitat is enumerated, and the valence of each condition is relative to the residual (governs D3)

Fig 11 :43: **"The habitat is enumerated, never composed."**
Fig 11 :49: **"An actor's valence is relative to the residual, not a property it carries."**
Fig 11 :40: **"A system cannot be made more capable by improving the agent in isolation. An improvement that does not change contact changes nothing, however much it improves."**

Lyman-Werner photons are a clean example of valence flip. For a Pop III seed trying to grow, ultraviolet photodissociation of H2 suppresses cooling and star formation in minihaloes, which can starve the seed. For a direct-collapse channel, destroying H2 is exactly what keeps the gas atomic and prevents fragmentation, so the same radiation is a benefactor. Smith and Bromm 2019 state that "The canonical mechanism to destroy H2 molecules is by photodissociation from non-ionizing Lyman-Werner photons" and that DCBH formation "depends heavily on the absence of molecular hydrogen, heavy elements, and dust." Takeo, Inayoshi and Mineshige 2020 (arXiv:2002.07187, opened 2026-11-23) show that hyper-Eddington accretion requires "the BH is embedded in a dense gas core," i.e. a contact condition, and that "the existence of stronger outflow leads to the transition to rapid accretion phases more efficiently," another valence flip. Raising the radiative efficiency of a disc without changing its contact with the gas reservoir changes nothing per Fig 11 :40. Mode `specified`, triangulated (Fig 11 plus two astrophysics sources on opposite sides of the same radiation field).

**Refuter:** an actor whose effect on the timing residual is identical in every environment and does not depend on what else is present would violate relative valence.

### F7: The anchor is instrument-dependent, and instruments are improved from worse instruments (governs D1)

Fig 2 :29: **"An anchor is whatever settles the question and does not change its answer because of what any frame thinks."**
Fig 6 :32: **"An instrument is not built from a description. It is improved from a worse instrument already returning something."**
Fig 6 :33: **"Nobody specified a telescope from an account of Mars. A wandering point in the sky was already a reading, and every sharper instrument was built on the residual the last one produced."**

The measured masses and redshifts are the ground for this question, but the measurement is not neutral. "Size matters: are we witnessing super-Eddington accretion in high-redshift black holes from JWST?" (2024, Astronomy and Astrophysics, opened 2026-11-23) finds "a much larger uncertainty in the MBH mass estimates relative to that of local counterparts. The uncertainty is up to an order of magnitude" and "a potential preference for lower masses and higher accretion rates." Abell2744-QSO1 (2024, Nature, opened 2026-11-23) has a black hole mass of about 4 times 10^7 solar masses and a black-hole-to-galaxy mass ratio of at least 3 percent, possibly as high as 100 percent, and is described as "the missing link between black hole seeds and one of the first luminous quasars." The anchor is real but its pointer has error bars that depend on the accretion regime itself. This is not a reason to abandon the ground (Fig 10 :24 "the ground does not decay, the channel does"); it is a reason to report the channel, not just the reading. Mode `specified`, single-source on the figure side (Fig 6 instrument rule), world corroboration from two observational papers.

**Refuter:** a measurement of mass and redshift independent of accretion regime, with errors negligible relative to the timing gap, would make this finding idle rather than false.

### F9: Which channel dominated is a gap, not a silence (governs all domains)

Fig 9 :32: **"you get a kind of thing, never the answer itself"**
Fig 13 :134: **"Unreachable is a verdict here, not a silence"**

No figure line returns the fraction of early supermassive black holes that began as heavy seeds, nor the duty cycle of super-Eddington accretion. No observation opened here returns it either. LID-568's paper says "Super-Eddington accretion is likely to occur episodically" and that "Future studies on a large sample of such objects will help to constrain the duty cycle." The 2025 JWST analysis of overmassive black holes (Luberto and Furlanetto, arXiv:2508.14164, opened 2026-11-23) finds that such objects "must instead grow at least an order of magnitude more slowly than expected if they are to fall within the observed scatter of the local" relation, a live tension rather than a verdict. That is an instrument owed, not a name to be invented. Mode `general` for the gap status, `specified` for the claim that the instrument is source-discriminating.

**Refuter:** a published measurement that assigns individual high-z black holes to seed channels with disjoint confidence intervals, using an instrument that does not update on the model, would close this gap.

## 4. Pre-registered refuter (written before the conclusion)

Required before any outreach. Seat-map discipline: a falsifier written before the run is a design instrument, not only an honesty device. The shape claim is refuted if any of the following holds.

1. A light stellar-remnant seed is shown to reach 10^9 solar masses by z greater than 6 under Eddington-limited accretion in a **realised** host, measured against a non-updating anchor. This collapses the import or re-bonding necessity of F4.
2. Super-Eddington accretion is shown to be unsustainable at the required duty cycle in the dense environments that F6 requires. This breaks the cycle-gain repair of F2 and F3.
3. The high-z masses are revised downward by the order of magnitude allowed in F7 so that no timing problem exists. Then R collapses and the whole derivation is moot.
4. A low-overlap instrument assigns every high-z supermassive black hole to a single channel with no overlap. The union verdict of F5 narrows to a partition.
5. A source-discriminating observation rules out heavy seeds (for example, the predicted overmassive faint quasar population is absent at the required density). The imported atom of F4 is withdrawn.
6. A light seed is demonstrated to grow without contact with a gas reservoir, which would break F6 and Fig 11 :40.

Until one of these is run, no part of this folder may be labelled `grounded`.

### Seven-question recursive derivation (summary)

1. **Is the residual real?** Yes, per slot: measured 10^9 solar masses at z above 6 (D1 anchor). `specified`.
2. **Is it one question?** No, a union of seed, rate, contact, feedback and time conditions (Fig 9 :55). `general`.
3. **What kind of object is growth?** A cycle with a sign (Fig 12 :121), priced in two currencies: description bits and actions (Fig 12 :125-127). `general`.
4. **Does composition inside the light-seed closure reach the target?** Reachability fails at this budget; the wall moves only on import or re-bonding (Fig 6 :21, :23). `specified`, not measured.
5. **Which repair?** Heavy seed is an import with a source gate and shadow test; super-Eddington is a gain change. Both may operate (Fig 8 :50-52). `specified`.
6. **What changes contact?** The enumerated habitat: gas density, LW field, metallicity, mergers (Fig 11 :43, :49, :40). `specified`, triangulated.
7. **What remains unreached?** The dominant channel and its duty cycle. A source-discriminating instrument is owed (Fig 13 :134, Fig 9 :32). `gap`, unreached rather than silence.

### Updated verdict

The shape survives: the residual is per object; growth is a signed cycle against a threshold; the timing gap is a reachability failure inside a closed light-seed channel; heavy seeds are imports with provenance and super-Eddington accretion is a re-bonding; the conditions form a union with residual-relative valence; and the dominant channel is a `gap` owed to an instrument, not a name. What does not survive is any claim that the figures select one mechanism. After re-census, four strains are named, none hidden, and none left as a contradiction.
