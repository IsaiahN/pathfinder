## Wider literature that bears on the derivation

Two things in the opened literature bear directly on the shape claim.

1. **The reviews agree because they share a pool.** Inayoshi et al. 2020, Volonteri et al. 2021 and Smith and Bromm 2019 cite the same simulations and the same quasar discoveries. Their agreement is one witness (Fig 2 :40). The independent witnesses are the *measurements* (LID-568, J0025-0145, Abell2744-QSO1, GA-NIFS/EIGER) and the two low-overlap feedback sources (System Dynamics Society, Kefi et al. 2016), and even those split into two pools. Do not present review consensus as corroboration.
2. **The anchor has regime-dependent error.** The A&A 2024 virial-mass paper and the LID-568 mass caveat both say the mass estimate moves with accretion regime. In framework terms, the ground does not decay but the channel does (Fig 10 :24). A submission that quotes a single mass without its accretion-regime uncertainty has already lost the anchor it claims to use.

## Distribution: offer, do not push

Figure-grounded reasons:

- **Fig 4 :22** "Only methods cross up. Only head starts come down." Sending the full architecture before a recipient has described a residual sends a recording, not a generator. Lead with the per-slot residual and the refuter; offer the links after.
- **Fig 10 :42** "A verifier that can reconstruct the claim cannot verify it." A recipient who has absorbed the framework before being asked for a verdict has joined the frame being checked.
- **Fig 9 :27-28** "describe it before you go looking, or any frame you pick will seem to fit." Ask what they would expect to see if the shape claim were true before giving them the figure text.
- **Fig 2 :42** "Buy independence in proportion to the cost of being wrong." Buy it by asking for a failure, not an endorsement.
- **Fig 2 :40** agreement among frames that share an evidence pool is worthless as a verdict. No mass send selected for receptiveness.

**Recommended order in a reply:** (1) the residual per object slot and the shape claim; (2) ask what they would count as a failure and record it; (3) only then the figure text and the source links, with the note that the figures, not the summary, are the authority.

## The two-stage offer

1. **Stage 1, opt-in start:** residual, shape claim, four refuters. Ask for a failure condition. This is Fig 10 :52 "Bring a characterised residual, never the source."
2. **Stage 2, only if curious:** the figure text and three source links. The recipient's stage-1 verdict is already on record and is never pooled as later agreement (Fig 2 :40).
3. **Stage 3, method reuse:** the formula. It is a generator, so giving it away gives away no answer (Fig 4 :22). Each user writes their own refuter and states their own mode before running it, because a frame cannot check the direction of its own question.

**Hard precondition:** stages 1 to 3 only work if the stage-1 output survives. This folder does not claim it has been checked against the ground. It is a simulated derivation checked for internal consistency against the figures. That is the honest status to send with it.
