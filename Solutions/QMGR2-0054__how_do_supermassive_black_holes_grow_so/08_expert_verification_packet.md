# Expert verification packet: the Tether on early supermassive black hole growth

**What this is:** a claim in a form an astrophysicist can check. Mode, derivation and refuters are stated before the conclusion, so a reader knows what to look for and what would settle it.

**Mode: `specified` on the per-domain claims, `general` on the structural shape, `gap` on which channel dominated. Not `grounded`.** No new measurement is claimed. This is a simulated derivation checked for internal consistency against the figures, not a ground reading, and it cannot settle the question it reports on.

## 1. The claim, in one paragraph

Per object, not per population average, the timing residual between a high-redshift quasar mass and a light-seed Eddington-limited prediction is a union of conditions with a specific structure. Accretion is a reinforcing cycle with a negative feedback arc, and the Eddington limit is a threshold comparison rather than a wall. Inside a closed light-seed growth channel, composition cannot reach the observed mass in the available time, so either an import (a heavy seed from the halo frame, carrying provenance and a shadow test) or a re-bonding (episodic super-Eddington accretion, a change of loop gain) is owed. The habitat conditions, including Lyman-Werner radiation, gas density, metallicity and mergers, have valence relative to the residual and flip sign between seed channels. The framework does not select a channel: the dominant route and its duty cycle are a `gap` owed to a source-discriminating instrument.

## 2. Three checkable sub-claims

### C1: framework-internal; checkable by reading Figures 1, 9 and 12
- **Statement:** the residual is indexed per object; the conditions form a union, not a partition; growth is a cycle whose sign decides whether it closes.
- **Source, verbatim:**
  - Fig 1 :19 "The residual is indexed by object slot, not aggregated."
  - Fig 9 :55 "A bundled problem is usually a union of overlapping conditions, not a list of separate ones. A single lever clears the region where only one condition holds and leaves the intersections standing, because the second condition still binds."
  - Fig 12 :121 "cycle. The output re-enters as input. The sign of the loop decides whether it closes, and a chain cannot close by construction."
- **Refuter:** a demonstrated light-seed path to 10^9 solar masses at z greater than 6 in a realised host under Eddington-limited accretion, reported per slot against a non-updating anchor, would collapse the union-to-import/re-bonding step (audit F4); or a demonstration that capture cross-section is independent of accumulated mass would break the cycle (audit F2).

### C2: physics-facing; checkable by an astrophysicist against the opened literature
- **Statement:** the two repairs are categorically different: a heavy seed is an import into the growth frame (adds an atom there, needs a source gate and a shadow test), while super-Eddington accretion changes the loop gain without adding an atom. The Eddington ceiling is a `⋛` threshold, not a wall. The observable consequences differ: heavy seeds predict unusually high BH-to-halo ratios in faint quasars; super-Eddington phases predict episodic, high-Eddington-ratio objects.
- **Source, verbatim (figure side):**
  - Fig 6 :21 "MINT composes inside the closure and can never add an atom."
  - Fig 6 :14 "the only operator that moves the wall."
  - Fig 6 :25 "An import adds no atom to the world."
  - Operators table :49 "a threshold relation between two quantities."
- **What to check:** Is the direct-collapse atom genuinely an import into the *growth* frame, not a new primitive of the underlying physics? Is the 2018 ApJL prediction (DCBHs have "BH mass to halo mass ratios that are much higher than expected for BH remnants of Population III stars that have grown to the same mass") still the right discriminator given JWST mass uncertainties? Are LID-568 (Eddington ratio 41.5) and J0025-0145 (intrinsic ratio above 4.9) episodic phases rather than steady states?
- **Refuter:** super-Eddington accretion shown unsustainable at the required duty cycle in the dense environments it needs (audit F2/F3); or a source-discriminating observation ruling out heavy seeds (audit F4, item 5); or masses revised down by the order of magnitude allowed in F7 so the timing gap vanishes.

### C3: measurement-facing; checkable against the anchor and the habitat
- **Statement:** the anchor is instrument-dependent in a specific way (virial masses move with accretion regime), and condition valence flips with the residual, so improving the agent without changing contact changes nothing.
- **Source, verbatim:**
  - Fig 2 :29 "An anchor is whatever settles the question and does not change its answer because of what any frame thinks."
  - Fig 10 :24 "the ground does not decay, the channel does"
  - Fig 11 :49 "An actor's valence is relative to the residual, not a property it carries."
  - Fig 11 :40 "A system cannot be made more capable by improving the agent in isolation. An improvement that does not change contact changes nothing, however much it improves."
- **What to check:** Does the A&A 2024 virial-mass uncertainty ("up to an order of magnitude") change the size of the timing residual? Does Lyman-Werner radiation actually flip from malfactor to benefactor between the two seed channels, as Smith and Bromm 2019 imply (H2 photodissociation suppresses minihalo cooling but is required for DCBH)?
- **Refuter:** an accretion-regime-independent mass measurement with errors small relative to the gap makes C3 idle; an actor whose effect is identical in all environments breaks relative valence.

## 3. What is explicitly not claimed

- Not a channel name as the answer. Heavy seeds and super-Eddington accretion are a provenanced import and a re-bonding respectively, not figure-derived winners.
- Not a number: no seed mass function, duty cycle, fraction or redshift cutoff is asserted.
- Not that the Eddington limit is violated; it is a threshold on the other side of which a different bond operates.
- Not that the ground is a consensus of reviews. The three major reviews share a simulation literature and count as one witness (Fig 2 :40).
- Not `grounded`, and not a claim that this packet was checked by an astrophysicist before sending.
- Not a claim that the mass readings are unbiased. Fig 10 :24 separates the ground from the channel that reports it.

## 5. What would settle it

A source-discriminating measurement, not another argument. From *The Hard Problem is About Source*: an instrument that reads green "cannot comment on the source"; the spectrometer resolved it. Here:

1. Accretion-regime-independent masses and spins at z greater than 6, to size the residual.
2. A duty-cycle measurement for super-Eddington phases from a large LID-568-like sample.
3. Faint-quasar BH-to-host mass ratios and merger signatures to test the heavy-seed discriminator.
4. LISA merger rates as the complementary, low-overlap channel (Inayoshi et al. 2020).
5. The six refuters in section 4 of `01_figure_grounded_audit.md`, written before the run.
