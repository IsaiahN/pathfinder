## QUESTION

Option B: keep the figure-derived shape, split the residual per object, type the bonds, keep the union of conditions, and report the dominant channel as a `gap` owed to an instrument. ## DOMAIN CENSUS

R by arity, symmetry and scale, then one line per domain with actors / currency / anchor, partition or union, and order by dependency.

- **R (the residual).** The per-object shortfall between the measured mass of a high-redshift quasar (about 10^9 solar masses within the first billion years) and the mass a light stellar-remnant seed predicts at the Eddington limit in that time. Arity: at least five binding conditions (seed mass, rate, gas contact, feedback duty cycle, time), so a union compound, not one hard question. Symmetry: ordered in time; the Eddington relation is a threshold comparison between two quantities, not a symmetric join. Scale: Schwarzschild radius to cosmic web, subsecond to a billion years (Fig 4 membrane, Fig 12 level relativity).
- **Domain 1, measurement / ground frame.** Actors: JWST, HST and X-ray instruments, broad emission lines, lensing models. Currency: photons, line widths, luminosities. Anchor: the measured mass and redshift of the individual object, which does not change when a theory pushes on it (Fig 2 :29). Question type: measurement with a regime-dependent channel (Fig 10 :24).
- **Domain 2, growth-channel physics frame.** Actors: seed black hole, gas, radiation, disc, feedback, mergers. Currency: mass added per unit time. Anchor: the observed mass target and cosmic time budget. Question type: reachability; composition inside a closed light-seed channel cannot add an atom (Fig 6 :21).
- **Domain 3, habitat / environment frame.** Actors: cold gas reservoir, Lyman-Werner radiation, metallicity, baryon streaming velocity, dense clusters, companion galaxies. Currency: contact with cold gas, with valence relative to R. Anchor: the gas supply the seed can actually reach. Question type: enumeration (Fig 11 :43), not composition.
- **Domain 4, verification / community frame.** Actors: reviewers, proposal panels, instruments, journals. Currency: agreement and residuals. Anchor: the ground, never agreement (Fig 2 :35). In this simulated run all four domains draw on overlapping evidence pools, so their agreement is one witness talking.
- **Union, not partition.** Fig 9 :54: "And when the split lands on a union rather than a partition, unbundle before you search again." One lever leaves the intersections standing. The two repair channels are not mutually exclusive (Volonteri et al. 2021).
- **Order by dependency.** Anchor first (D1), then reachability (D2), then habitat enumeration (D3), then verification (D4). Fig 3 :20: "The chain is ordered by dependency, not by importance. Each link consumes what the one above produces, so effort spent above the break arrives at a step that cannot use it."

Strain test applied: Fig 6 (import moves the wall) and Fig 12 :104 (settled molecule becomes an atom) govern different boundaries, the growth frame versus the next level. Fig 11 (relative valence) and Fig 2 (non-updating anchor) govern different objects, the habitat versus the ground. Both dissolved by re-census, kept visible in `01` section 6.

## VERDICT (from the figures)

The shape survives; no mechanism name survives. Per object, the timing residual is a union of conditions with a signed accretion cycle and an Eddington threshold. Inside a closed light-seed channel, composition cannot reach the observed mass in the available time, so an import (heavy seed, with provenance and a shadow test) or a re-bonding (episodic super-Eddington accretion, a gain change) is owed. Habitat conditions have valence relative to the residual and flip sign between seed channels. The framework returns a shape, not a name, so the dominant channel and its duty cycle are a `gap`, unreached rather than silence. Mode `specified` on these claims, `general` on the structural shape, not `grounded`. This verdict comes from the figures; the world sources only corroborate.

- **cost if wrong:** bounded and locatable, but real if outreach is mishandled. The packet is mode-labelled, states refuters before the conclusion and records the lineage, so an error is findable. The larger cost is spending the independence of outside readers by sending a mechanism name or a review consensus before the shape claim has been checked, which Fig 2 :42 prices as "Buy independence in proportion to the cost of being wrong." That cost is avoidable by leading with the per-slot residual and its refuter, and by reporting the gap as a gap.
- **undoable:** yes. The mechanism names are withheld, not committed, and withheld claims cannot be retracted. The import and re-bonding are recorded with provenance, so a wrong provenance record can be corrected without touching the shape. A sent claim can be superseded by the corrected packet; no primitive has to be withdrawn. If masses are revised downward by the allowed order of magnitude, the entire derivation becomes idle and is retired, not defended.
- **reversed by:** a ground reading, not agreement. An accretion-regime-independent mass and spin measurement at z greater than 6 would resize the residual; a duty-cycle measurement from a large LID-568-like sample would test the re-bonding repair; a faint-quasar BH-to-host ratio distribution with disjoint confidence intervals would test the import; LISA merger rates would provide a low-overlap channel. Any of the six pre-registered refuters in `01` section 4 would also reverse the verdict. Until one fires, the verdict stands as `candidate`.
