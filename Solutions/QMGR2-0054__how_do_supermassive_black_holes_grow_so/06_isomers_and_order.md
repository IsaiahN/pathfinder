## DERIVATION

### Q1: Cycle or chain? Fig 12 :121

Fig 12 :121: **"cycle. The output re-enters as input. The sign of the loop decides whether it closes, and a chain cannot close by construction."**

Accretion is a cycle: accumulated mass deepens the potential, which increases the capture cross-section, which increases the inflow, which increases the mass. The loop sign is positive and amplifying. Radiative and mechanical feedback insert a negative arc, which can reverse the inflow or shift loop dominance. Smith and Bromm 2019 (opened 2026-11-23) describe accretion as "self-regulating radiative feedback" with "duty cycles" and note that "Maintaining super-Eddington accretion is possible when the black hole is embedded within sufficiently dense gas," which is the external condition that lets the positive arc dominate. Takeo, Inayoshi and Mineshige 2020 report that stronger outflows can actually trigger the rapid-accretion transition, a second valence flip.

If one wrote growth as a chain `seed → gas → time → mass`, the output would never re-enter as input and the chain could not close, by construction. The System Dynamics Society page (opened 2026-11-23) calls the same structure "a positive or reinforcing feedback loop" versus "a negative or balancing feedback loop" and says "the sign of the loop is called its polarity." Kefi et al. 2016 (opened 2026-11-23) add that "the sign of the loop crucially depends on the number of negative interactions involved" and that positive feedback is necessary but not sufficient. Triangulated for the shape (Fig 12 plus two low-overlap fields, one witness on the world side).

**Falsifier:** demonstrate that the capture cross-section of a growing black hole is independent of the mass it has already accumulated, so there is no re-entrant input. The cycle claim then fails and the chain claim stands.

**Falsifier:** show that the same two operands under two different bonds in this table yield an identical observable distribution, with an instrument that can discriminate the bonds. Then the bond is not carrying information and Fig 12 :5 does not apply to this case.

### Q3: Are light and heavy seed channels isomers?

Fig 12 :117: **"isomer. Same atoms, different bond, different substance. The difference is invisible in the formula and decisive in the behaviour."**
Fig 12 :123: **"functional group. A substructure whose presence fixes the outcome. The group says what kind; the qualifier says which member."**

As the literature writes them, a light-seed channel (Pop III remnant plus long-lived accretion) and a heavy-seed channel (direct collapse plus accretion) do **not** share every atom: the heavy channel invokes an atomic-cooling halo and a strong Lyman-Werner field, under the DCBH conditions described by Smith and Bromm 2019 ("DCBHs can only form early in cosmic history under a specific set of rare conditions"). So as written they are two members of a **functional group**: `seed → accretion cycle → mass within the time budget`. The group fixes the kind; the qualifier (light or heavy) says which member.

The stronger claim becomes available if we hold the **full shared atom set** `{seed mass, gas, time, radiation field, disc, feedback, host potential, merger}` fixed and vary only the bond at the seed step and the loop gain. Then the channels are genuine isomers: same atoms, different arrangement, different observable substance (different host mass ratio, different accretion duty cycle, different merger signature). The 2018 ApJL heavy-seed paper predicts exactly such a discriminating signature: DCBHs will have "BH mass to halo mass ratios that are much higher than expected for BH remnants of Population III stars that have grown to the same mass," resolvable by future X-ray and infrared facilities. Abell2744-QSO1 (Nature 2024) shows a black-hole-to-galaxy ratio of at least 3 percent and possibly 100 percent, of the kind that makes the isomer distinction observable.

**Falsifier, written before the run:** if either channel requires an atom the other cannot in principle share, so the atom set is not fixed, the isomer claim fails and the functional-group reading stands. A second falsifier: if a measured population shows the two channels have identical host-mass-ratio distributions and identical duty-cycle distributions with disjoint error bars, the "different substance" condition fails.

### Q4: What is the order of the whole derivation?

Fig 3 :20: **"The chain is ordered by dependency, not by importance. Each link consumes what the one above produces, so effort spent above the break arrives at a step that cannot use it."**
Fig 3 :19: **"Optimising a link that already works cannot rescue one that does not."**

The dependency order is: anchor (measure mass and redshift per slot) before reachability (can the seed get there?) before habitat enumeration (what changes contact?) before verification. A refinement of the accretion-disc model before the mass anchor is settled is effort above the break. The A&A 2024 mass-uncertainty paper is the instrument that shows the break is real: a link with no instrument is not a link that works (Fig 3 :27 "A link with no instrument is not a link that works. It is a link nobody has looked at").

Fig 12 :104: **"A settled molecule becomes an atom for whatever composes over it. So irreducible is a fact about a level and never about the world, and there is no bottom"** permits a grown black hole to become the seed atom at the next level, which is what hierarchical merging means. Volonteri et al. 2021 (opened 2026-11-23) describe black hole mergers as one of "the drivers of their growth inside galaxies," with "several bottlenecks in this journey." That is the recursion, not a new kind of object.

**Strain named, not hidden:** the Operators table defines `⋛` as "a threshold relation between two quantities." At second order, the operands of a comparison between two grown black-hole lines of evidence are settled molecules now acting as atoms. Whether those count as quantities at that level needs an instrument (a spin or merger-rate measurement), not an argument. This is the same live type strain recorded in the exemplar and in the audit's section 6, kept visible here.

## CONCLUSION

Growth is a reinforcing cycle with a negative feedback arc, not a chain (Q1). Its steps are typed by production, sequence, comparison, subtraction and negation; a list of ingredients cannot recover them (Q2). Light and heavy seed channels are a functional group as the literature writes them, and genuine isomers over the fixed shared atom set, with a stated falsifier on each (Q3). The derivation is ordered by dependency, with the measurement anchor first and the mass-uncertainty instrument as the live break (Q4). No new primitive is licensed and no mechanism name is returned.
