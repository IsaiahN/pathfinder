## Round trip gap R_T

The Symbols table defines R_T as `gap(x, (T_E ∘ T_A)(x))`, the gap between what was sent up and what came back down. Fig 4 :30 requires it to be measured rather than assumed. I ran the round trip on the condensation this folder actually uses.

1. **Sent up (x):** the verbatim figure set, chiefly Fig 4 :19 to :20, Fig 13 :31 and :91, Fig 6 :42, Fig 11 :73 and Fig 9 :32 and :54.
2. **Abstraction (T_A):** the shorthand carried in `07_the_tether_answer.md` and `09_watson_answer.md`: predict-kind is licensed, predict-name is not; the kernel is invariant; the reach can fall; the residual compounds into chaos.
3. **Concretisation (T_E):** a concrete claim about a changing-component system, for example "this flock's macro pattern is predictable from its interaction rule even as members are replaced."

**R_T is nonzero.** The following constraints held by the shorthand do not survive the trip back down:

- the **union/partition split** (Fig 9 :54) collapses if the shorthand is read as a simple yes or no;
- the **dependency order** (Formula steps 2 and 7: refuse before mint, SEARCHED before INWARD and OUTWARD) is not preserved in the one-line answer;
- the **ground-anchor rule** (Fig 2 :29) is lost if "prediction is licensed" is read without naming who settles it;

Conversely, several things survive cleanly: a structure/kernel sits between scales; the kinds are closed while arrangements are not; the reach can fall; a compounding residual gives chaos; the framework returns a shape, not a name.
