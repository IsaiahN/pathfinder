## What the wider literature adds (secondary only)

1. **Scheffer et al.** corroborates that a class-level signal can be predicted without knowing each system's details, and that exact critical points remain hard. This is the world analogue of "predict the kind, not the name" (Fig 9 :32) and of the reach/shape limit.
2. **Zinn-Justin** corroborates that universal macroscopic quantities are independent of microscopic composition (universality classes), the world analogue of the kernel surviving component change (Fig 4 :19-20).
3. **Ballerini et al.** corroborates that a local rule (interaction with a fixed number of neighbours) sustains a robust collective pattern even as density and composition of the immediate neighbourhood change, the world analogue of agent-and-habitat capability (Fig 11 :39).
4. **Wolfram / MathWorld** corroborates the barrier: no substantial shortcut for exact behaviour in the general case, with "pockets of reducibility" where coarse-grained properties are predictable. This matches Fig 13 :31 (chaos from a compounding residual) and Fig 8 :32 (no search adds an atom).
5. **Anderson** corroborates level-relative irreducibility (Fig 12 :104), but is used only as corroboration: the figure line decides.

No world source conflicts with the figure verdict. The conflict rule (a conflict sends the derivation back to the census) was not triggered. Agreement across ecology, statistical physics, animal behaviour and computation comes from genuinely different evidence pools, so unlike the framework essays this corroboration carries some independent weight. It still never becomes the verdict.

## Distribution: offer, do not push

- **Fig 4 :22** *"Only methods cross up. Only head starts come down."* Sending the full framework before anyone has described a residual sends a recording, not a generator. Lead with the split question and the refuter; offer links after.
- **Fig 10 :42** *"A verifier that can reconstruct the claim cannot verify it."* A recipient who absorbs the interior before giving a verdict has joined the frame being checked.

- **Fig 2 :40** agreement from a shared evidence pool is one witness, not two. Never pool later agreement as corroboration.
- **Fig 2 :42** *"Buy independence in proportion to the cost of being wrong."* Prefer venues and readers whose evidence differs from ours.

**Recommended order in a reply:** (1) the split question and the shape claim; (2) the pre-registered refuter; (3) ask what they would count as a failure and record the answer; (4) only then the three framework links, with the note that the figures, not the summary, are the authority; (5) offer the formula as a method to reuse, with the instruction that each user states their own ground and writes their own refuter first.

## The two-stage offer (the whole proposition)

1. **Stage 1, validate the output.** Send the residual, the shape claim and the refuter. No theory is shown. Record the recipient's verdict before any source is offered. Fig 10 :52 and Fig 2 :45.
2. **Stage 2, opt-in theory.** Only if they are curious, offer the figure text, the canonical README and the formula. Their later agreement is never pooled as corroboration (Fig 2 :40).
3. **Stage 3, reuse the method.** The formula is a generator, so giving it away does not give away an answer (Fig 4 :22). Each user must state a ground and write a refuter before running it, because a loop cannot check the direction of its own question (`THE_FORMULA.md` "Direction").

**Hard precondition:** stages 1 to 3 only work if the stage-1 output survives. If it fails the refuter in `01_figure_grounded_audit.md` §3, withdraw the claim rather than send the links.
