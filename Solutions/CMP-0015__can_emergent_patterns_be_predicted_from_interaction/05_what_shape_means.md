## 1. A shape is a constraint, not a filler

The canonical limit: the framework returns *"what a missing thing would have to look like (a shape, not a name)."* Fig 9 :32: *"you get a kind of thing, never the answer itself."* Fig 13 says the system *"can say the answer is out of reach with the library as it stands... It cannot say which primitive is missing, because naming it would mean already holding it."*

From *The Library of Babel*: *"you can cut your search short if the set of books returned is small and descriptive enough that it provides a general shape of the answer that satisfies your question."*

So for this question a **shape** is the narrowed form of the answer we can state before we can name the exact macro configuration. It constrains; it does not fill. "Prediction is licensed for the kind of pattern, not for the particular configuration" is a shape. "Pattern P will appear at time t with amplitude A" is a name.

## 2. How a shape is obtained

- *Library of Babel* on vocabulary by proximity: *"use the existing vocabulary you do know that is closest to the words that you wish to describe and grasp the shape of"*; then *"With each new term gained, you are able to narrow the space by definition and deepen your vocabulary."*
- Fig 6 :30: *"The description travels. Fails by being sharpened into terms only we can read. Cast it in effect terms."* Fig 9 :30 to :31: *"your words are borrowed too, so describe the effect rather than the cause."*
- *Decoding Tacit Knowledge*: *"When contact isn't possible, the best you can send is a plain description of what you'd expect to see, because anyone can check that against the world."* This is why the outreach in `02` sends an effect description and a refuter, not a theory.
- *Library of Babel* on stopping: *"You will know that you are done because the finish line will also be the starting place, as there is nowhere else to search and nothing else that can describe the answer better."* The paradox: *"you have already composed the answer through the process of locating it."*

Applied here: describe the residual in effect terms (rules, components, macro pattern, what fails to resolve), then pick the frame. Picking "complex systems" first and mapping the question into it afterwards is the inversion Fig 9 :27 to :28 forbids.

## 3. What licenses a name

Two things only, both outside the shape itself:

1. **The ground settles it.** Formula step 5: *"the ground settles it. Verified only by what pays."* The canonical README: the framework *"stops at the limit of what the available instruments can distinguish, and records the lineage so the stop is honest."*
2. **A provenanced import from a frame whose closure differs** (Fig 6 :23, Fig 8 :50 to :52), with a source gate and a shadow test.

*They're Scanning Books When They Should* sharpens the first: *"The real import is not a new term in the existing space. It is a new dimension of the space."* And: *"Declaring the primitives does not make them neutral. It makes the choice visible."* The six-term set is a visible guess, not nature's own list.

For this question: a particular macro configuration is licensed only by a measurement (a ground reading), or by a provenanced import from a frame that already predicts its shape. An argument from the interaction rules alone licenses the kind, not the name (Fig 13 :31 on chaos from a compounding residual).

## 4. The metamerism error, applied to "prediction"

*The Hard Problem is About Source* gives the mechanism. An instrument reads green and *"it cannot comment on the source."* Several sources (one green light, yellow plus blue, RGB, CMYK, a scheme not yet discovered) can give the same reading. *"If several paths create the same behavior, then behavior is a projection with lossy information"* and *"An instrument that can't distinguish these cases demonstrates its own inadequacy."* It was the spectrometer, not an argument, that resolved it.

*How Intelligence Actually Works* gives the forward version of the same limit: *"Every composition, no matter what it is, introduces new residual to the chain of composition."* It also warns that *"the projection that no instrument distinguishes from the thing is still a projection, which is why the lineage has to be kept even when the copy is perfect."* This is the essay form of Fig 13 :31 and of the canonical *"Indiscernibility is not identity."*

Applied here: observing a macro pattern and naming the interaction rule that "must have produced it" is the metamerism error. Several rule sets and several component casts can give the same macro reading. The prediction asked for in the question, if it means deriving the exact macro state from the rules, is a source named from a projection. What is owed is a source-discriminating instrument, not a better argument. This is why F6 in `01` makes the ground the anchor of the verification domain.

## 5. Q: does "shape, not a name" forbid naming the intermediate or the pattern? CORRECTION TO MY OWN FIRST READING

**My first reading was too broad.** I initially wrote that the framework can never name anything it predicts, so even saying "the kernel is invariant" is forbidden. That is wrong.

- The README's exact phrase is *"what a **missing** thing would have to look like (a shape, not a name)."* The subject is a primitive not held in Γ.
- Space, time, energy, spectrum, gradient and network are all **already held** (Fig 13). Naming one is a **binding** onto an atom already in the library, not the invention of a new primitive.
- *They're Scanning Books* makes the same point from the import side: declaring primitives makes a choice visible, not neutral. A binding must be recorded as our contribution.
- Fig 13 itself: *"It is not space-time but space, a structure, time"* requires *a* structure without selecting among the three it holds.

**What remains forbidden here.** The exact macro configuration of a changing-component system is not a held atom; it is a composition whose residual compounds (Fig 13 :31). Naming it from the rules alone is the metamerism error of §4. So the shape limit bites on the *prediction target*, not on every use of a vocabulary term. Both statements are recorded so neither is quietly lost. Mode `specified`; triangulated (README + Fig 13 + Fig 9 :32).

## 6. Two consequences for this solution

**Prestige is not action.** From *The Recursive Self-Improvement Trap*: *"Prestige must never be allowed to validate an action directly."* And: *"Prestige can fund an action. It can give an action a budget to be tried. But it cannot settle an action. Only the ground can settle it. The Ground Maintainer must be blind to prestige."* A list of famous people who agree that emergence is predictable is the named collapse, not corroboration. The legitimate ask is to give the claim a budget to be tried and let the ground settle it. This is why `03_public_contact_points.csv` has no personal emails and no receptiveness rank.

**This audit is a simulation, not a ground reading.** Same essay: *"LLMs can quickly simulate an approximate answer/output with the formula found in the architecture post."* And: *"the truly testable output would need to be derived from the real architecture or working the formula yourself."* So `01_figure_grounded_audit.md`, `06_isomers_and_order.md`, `07_the_tether_answer.md`, `08_expert_verification_packet.md`, `09_watson_answer.md` and `11_audit.md` are simulated derivations checked for internal consistency against the figures. They are **not** tests of the claim and must be labelled as such. They cannot settle anything they report on.
