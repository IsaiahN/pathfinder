## 2. What the wider literature bears on the question

**One evidence pool, one witness.** Klein and Mazzoni 2025, Coase 1937, Williamson 1975-1996, Fine and Whitney 1996, and Reitzig and Wagner 2010 are all economics, strategy or engineering-institutionalism. They agree on much of the shape, but they draw on overlapping conferences, citation chains and case traditions. Per Fig 2 :31, *"Independence, between two frames, is how little they draw on the same evidence."* Their agreement is one witness, not five.

- Klein and Mazzoni 2025: *"the boundary of the firm, then, is determined by the tradeoff, at the margin, between the relative transaction costs of external and internal exchange."* This is the management-frame name for the make-versus-buy tradeoff. It corroborates F1 and the union in F4, but its *"many shades of gray"* and plural sourcing result is a filter describing the option set, not a witness for which option pays.
- The same chapter reports *"hidden costs" of outsourcing such as "loss of expertise within the firm, reduced ability to detect and respond to competition"* (Reitzig and Wagner 2010). This is the business-pool name for the independence debit in Fig 6 :24 and the channel decay in Fig 10 :24.
- Fine and Whitney, via the MIT records: *"dependency for capacity (the firm retains the knowledge)"* is least risky, *"the most risky is the dependency for that knowledge"*; and *"no outsourcing or architecture modularity policy that seems suitable at a given time is likely to remain suitable, due to such upsetting factors as technological advances, regulatory changes, and other economic shifts"*. These corroborate F2, F7 and F8 at the engineering stratum, in the same broad pool.
- The Williamson efficiency test quoted in Klein and Mazzoni, *"backward integration that lacks a transaction cost rationale or serves no strategic purposes will presumably be recognized and will be undone"* if the firm faces *"an active rivalry"*, corroborates the anchor rule in F5: rivalry is a ground that pushes back, agreement is not.

**Low-overlap witness, genuinely independent.** Hammerstein and Noë 2016 is evolutionary biology, with no shared evidence pool with management or economics. It corroborates the mechanism, not the firm-level fact:

1. *"Trade results mainly from specialization and division of labour."* This is the biological form of the supplier surplus in Fig 8 :45, which requires closures to differ.
2. *"We tend to refuse a particular trade if we know that a better deal can be obtained elsewhere, or if we can pick the cherries ourselves with little effort."* This is a literal make-or-buy boundary in animal behaviour: refuse the trade when a better partner exists or when self-provision is cheap. It corroborates F6.
3. Under market competition *"mycorrhizal fungi would probably have lost the ability to directly acquire carbon"*, and mutualistic ants are *"having lost the ability to digest sucrose"*. These are instances of the independence debit and channel decay in F2 and F7, observed at a different stratum.

**Framework-internal essays** are used for the canonical reading of "shape" in `05_what_shape_means.md` only. They are not independent world witnesses, because their author wrote the figures. That overlap is recorded in the ledger.

**Venue pages opened, no verdict bearing (silent on the question).** POMS and INFORMS pages establish public opt-in routes only. They carry no evidence about firm boundaries.

## 3. Distribution rules

Figure-grounded reasons for how material is offered:

- **Fig 4 :22:** *"Only methods cross up. Only head starts come down."* Send the generator, the residual and the refuter, not the conclusion. A recording carried upward *"looks like knowledge and is a description of one occasion"* (Fig 4 :25).
- **Fig 10 :42:** *"A verifier that can reconstruct the claim cannot verify it."* A recipient who has absorbed the framework before being asked for a verdict has joined the frame being checked.
- **Fig 9 :27-28:** *"describe it before you go looking, or any frame you pick will seem to fit."* Ask what they would expect to see if the shape claim were true before showing the source.
- **Fig 2 :40:** agreement from a shared pool is worthless as a verdict. Later agreement about the framework is never pooled as corroboration.
- **Fig 10 :59:** *"an interest in the outcome is unavoidable, an interest in the reading is disqualifying."* Declare the stake; do not ask for belief.

**A hard practical constraint.** The AOM Publishing page, recorded in the BUS-0001 solution folder and read this session, states: *"Artificial Intelligence: Work submitted to AOM must be created by the authors and not the product of artificial intelligence tools unless appropriate to the research question and properly cited."* This whole solution is a simulated derivation produced with an LLM and is labelled as such everywhere. It must **not** be submitted as an authored empirical or theoretical article. The legitimate use is as a private checking instrument: post the residual and the refuter, invite a human to run the check, and let that human author whatever they conclude. Any submission must be rewritten and owned by a human author who can defend the derivation, with the AI contribution disclosed under the applicable policy. The same caution applies to POMS and INFORMS venues.

**Order in any reply:** (1) the shape claim and the pre-registered refuter; (2) the question "what would you count as a failure here?"; (3) only after a verdict is recorded, the three source links with the note that the figures, not the summary, are the authority; (4) the formula as a reusable method, with the instruction that each user writes their own refuter and states their own mode.

## 4. The two-stage offer, compressed

**Stage 1.** Send `08_expert_verification_packet.md` sections 1 to 3 only: the one-paragraph claim, the three checkable sub-claims with their verbatim figure lines and refuters, and the non-claims. No provenance bundle, no essay links.

**Stage 2, opt-in only.** If the recipient is curious and has already recorded a verdict on stage 1, offer the three public links: the figure text, the formula and usage rule, and the essays. Their later agreement is a conversation, not corroboration (Fig 2 :40).

**Stage 3.** Offer the formula as a method, never as an answer (Fig 4 :22). Each user writes a refuter before running it and states the mode of what they get.

**Stop rule.** If stage 1 fails, the theory is not offered. A failure is more useful than an endorsement.

**Hosting.** Nothing here is hosted or sent by this agent. The human operator hosts the packet on an opt-in page and sends one to one. Prefer a download page that lets a recipient decline contact over an attachment pushed to a list.
