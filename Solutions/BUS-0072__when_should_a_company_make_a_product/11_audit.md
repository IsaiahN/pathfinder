## 2. Independent re-census from the residual line

### 2.1 Arity, symmetry, scale

- **Arity:** a relation among firm-library Γ, component slot s, action a (make or buy) and ground o′, with a second closure (the supplier) present in the buy branch. At least three operands, ordered in time. A two-place reading (component, cost) loses the two closures, which is the whole phenomenon, so it is rejected.
- **Symmetry:** not symmetric. The market is an anchor and does not update on the firm's preference (Fig 2 :25). "Better to make than buy" is a threshold comparison (Fig 12 :87), so one operand is compared with another, not merely conjoined with it. The two branches have asymmetric consequences for reach: buy imports an atom, make does not (Fig 6 :21, :23).
- **Scale:** the slot is a component, but "better" exists only against a supplier offer at the market scale (Fig 4 :5). The retention of know-how crosses the membrane between a single project and the firm's library (Fig 4). The residual is intrinsically cross-scale.

## 4. Round-trip gap R_T

Fig 4 :30: *"So take the round trip and measure the gap. Without it you are navigating by dead reckoning: confident about a position nobody checked."*

- **T_A, going up:** carry the primary's three-condition shape to a one-sentence abstraction. Result: *a component should be made when the firm can compose it and should be bought when a supplier can supply it more cheaply.*
- **T_E, coming back:** put that abstraction into a specific component decision. Result: *compare internal cost with supplier price and pick the lower.*

**R_T = gap(x, (T_E ∘ T_A)(x)).** The gap is what the abstraction cannot hold:

1. It collapses the union into one lever. It loses Fig 9 :57, *"a case in two conditions is not a harder case of either."* Coming back down, it tells a firm failing C3 (lost know-how) to renegotiate a price, which is effort above the break (Fig 3 :29-30).
2. It loses the source gate and the independence debit. "Supplier supplies more cheaply" has no place for provenance or for the knowledge the firm stops keeping (Fig 6 :12-13, :24). Coming back down, the lower quote wins and the debit is invisible.
3. It loses the anchor rule. The abstraction does not say that the market, not internal agreement, settles the question (Fig 2 :29, :35). Coming back down, it permits a firm to score its own sourcing with a quantity it produces (Fig 1 :27).
4. It loses the valence flip. "Pick the lower price" has no place for the possibility that the internal capability changed sign (Fig 11 :50, Fig 12 :119).
5. It loses the source-discriminator requirement. The abstraction cannot say what instrument would distinguish a genuine supplier surplus from a temporary quote, an accounting allocation or a lost capability, which is the actual `gap` in the answer.

This gap is not an error to eliminate; it is the measurement of what the coarser description cannot hold (Fig 4 :31). It is also the reason the outreach packet sends the generator, not the one-sentence version (Fig 4 :25). The one-paragraph version in `07` is a deliberately lossy artefact and is labelled as such.
