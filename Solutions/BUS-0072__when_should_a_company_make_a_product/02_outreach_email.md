# Outreach draft - validate the output first, then decide about the theory

**Use:** one to one, or as a cover note attached to a submission through a public venue in `03_public_contact_points.csv`. **Not** for bulk send. No personal emails are used; the recipient must have an opt-in or public institutional route.

**Hard precondition:** the stage-1 output must survive the recipient's check before any theory is shown. The output here is a `specified` shape with no ground measurement, so the legitimate ask is a refutation of the shape, never endorsement of it. If the recipient cannot be shown a failure condition they can actually test, do not send.

---

**Subject:** Check one claim about where a firm's make-or-buy boundary sits before you decide whether any of this is worth reading

> Hi [name],
>
> I have one narrow claim about the make-or-buy decision that I would like you to check against what you already know. I am not asking you to read a theory first, and I am not asking you to agree with anything. I am asking for a failure. If the claim does not survive, there is nothing else to look at.
>
> **The claim (`specified`, not `grounded`):** the make-or-buy boundary is not a ranked list of "core" versus "non-core" activities, and it is not one cost threshold. It is a relation between two ways of getting a component, and three conditions have to be handled at once:
>
> 1. **Make is internal composition.** A component can be made only where the firm's library of routines, tools and knowledge can actually compose it, and where doing so settles on the ground, meaning internal cost, quality and delivery really converge. Internal search cannot add a capability the firm does not already hold.
> 2. **Buy is an import, and it is not free.** A supplier can carry an atom the firm does not hold, which is the only reason buying can expand what the firm can reach. But that import has to pass a source gate and explain a real gap, and it is debited against the firm's independence. A wrong atom costs more than starting from primitives. The condition to check is not just the quote; it is the loss of internal knowledge that follows.
> 3. **The capability has to survive over time.** A firm that buys for years and loses the ability to make has not seen the market stop paying; its own channel to the make-ground went stale, saturated or pooled into an average.
>
> These are three different failure modes with three different instruments, not three degrees of one problem. A firm that cannot compose the component needs a different intervention from a firm whose supplier offer has no real surplus, or a firm that has lost the know-how. The further claim is that a make-capability can change sign when the component's role changes, so the strength and the rigidity can be the same object. The contract and the market, not internal agreement, are the anchor.
>
> **What I am not claiming:** that I can name which component a particular firm should make, that transaction cost economics or "core competence" is the answer, that any named firm is a case in point, that this is settled by evidence, or that it is the only account of firm boundaries. I am offering a shape that any candidate answer would have to have. A shape is not a name.
>
> **What would refute it, written down before I sent this:**
> 1. a firm adds a genuinely new atom to its capability by internal composition alone, with no contact with a differing closure;
> 2. a supplier's component expands the firm's reach with zero independence debit and no coordination cost, or a wrong import costs less than starting from primitives;
> 3. an averaged make-or-buy ratio predicts prospectively and per component which named slot will fail;
> 4. one intervention restores all three conditions in all failure types and leaves nothing categorically different behind;
> 5. a sourcing decision validated only by internal agreement survives an active-rivalry test with no correction;
> 6. a supplier whose closure is identical to the firm's still produces a durable surplus;
> 7. a firm loses the ability to make a component while its internal readings of cost and quality stay fresh and unpooled;
> 8. a make-capability's measured value stays the same across a documented change in what the market rewards.
>
> If any one holds, I withdraw the claim. A single line saying which one holds and why is more useful to me than agreement. If you would rather not engage, no reply is needed.
>
> **If it survives, and only then:** I can send the three public sources below. The figures, not my summary of them, are the authority.
>
> - Figure text with line numbers (I can point to the exact lines): the local canonical figure text in this solution folder.
> - Formula and usage rule: the `figures` folder on the public `general-agent` branch of the `tether` repository.
> - The essays on source, search shape and the prestige/action separation: my Substack archive.
>
> I will send URLs only after you tell me what you would count as a failure.
>
> **If you want to use the machinery:** the formula is public. Run it on a component you know well and can check against a real ground. Write down what would refute your result *before* you run it and state the mode of what you get. A reported failure is more useful to me than a reported success.
>
> Thanks,
> [name]

## Why this version is ordered this way

| the move | figure-grounded reason |
|---|---|
| Residual and refuter first, theory last | *"Bring a characterised residual, never the source."* (Fig 10 :52). The recipient checks the claim without holding the framework's interior |
| Do not ask for agreement | *"Alignment is triangulation, not negotiation. A mirror cannot correct you."* (Fig 2 :35) |
| Three failure modes kept separate | *"a case in two conditions is not a harder case of either"* (Fig 9 :57); a blended score is a pooled reading (Fig 10 :26) |
| Pre-registered refuter | *"a falsifier written before the run is a design instrument, not only an honesty device"* |
| Mode stated on the claim | *"Mode. general · specified · grounded. State which."* (`THE_FORMULA.md`) |
| No name for the component or the "core" activity | *"you get a kind of thing, never the answer itself"* (Fig 9 :32) |
| Links only after a verdict | Fig 10 :42, *"A verifier that can reconstruct the claim cannot verify it."* Once the recipient has the framework, their later agreement is not corroboration (Fig 2 :40) |
| Prestige kept out of the ask | *"Prestige can fund an action. It can give an action a budget to be tried. But it cannot settle an action. Only the ground can settle it."* (*The Recursive Self-Improvement Trap*, read 2026-11-22) |
| Offer the formula as a method, not an answer | *"Only methods cross up. Only head starts come down."* (Fig 4 :22) |
| Ask the user for their own refuter | Formula, "Direction": a frame cannot check the direction of its own question; make the sought-for shape an ordinary term |

## Sending rules

- One recipient at a time. No distribution lists, no mail-merge, no forwarding chain.
- Use only a public institutional contact, a call for submissions, or an address the person has published themselves. Record the source and date.
- A reply from a directly contacted person is a conversation, never corroboration. If a recipient returns a refutation, copy it into `11_audit.md` with provenance and have the auditor seat re-evaluate it as if it came from any other source.
- Do not follow up with the theory after a "no". An interest in the outcome is unavoidable; an interest in the reading is disqualifying (Fig 10 :59).
