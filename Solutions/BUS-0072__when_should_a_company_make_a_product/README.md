## The question restated as an effect

What is being decided is not a firm's overall posture ("vertically integrate" or "outsource") but the boundary of the firm at each component slot. In effect terms: the firm's library Γ predicts what a component will cost and deliver if made internally; the market returns what it actually costs and delivers if bought; R = |Γ(b,a) - o′| is measured per component slot, never averaged into one make-or-buy ratio (Fig 1 :19, Fig 1 :22).

- Option A, "make when the item is core, buy when it is not": leads to the prediction that the boundary can be drawn from a ranked list of activities. The sought-for term "core" would have to be independently measurable.
- Option B, "the boundary is a maintained relation between two closures": leads to the prediction that the make-or-buy line is per slot, is a union of conditions, and moves when contact or reach changes (Fig 6 :23, Fig 11 :39).

What an answer would look like, written down and bound as an ordinary term before the run (Formula, "Direction"): a two-sided boundary condition stating (1) when internal composition is reachable and settles against the ground, and (2) when an import from a supplier's closure produces a surplus that survives the source gate, the shadow test and the independence debit, plus an instrument that discriminates a failure of one condition from a failure of the other. Until bound, that shape predicts nothing and no check in the loop can see it. It is now bound, so the refuter in `01` can fire on it.

The verdict recorded in `09_watson_answer.md` is Option B in `specified` mode, kept per domain and never averaged.

## What is deliberately not here

- No personal emails of named individuals.
- No ranking of people by how likely they are to agree.
- No bulk-send plan.
- No claim that this derivation is `grounded`, no claim that it settles the make-or-buy question, and no claim that the framework is the only true account of firm boundaries.

Three figure-grounded reasons, plus one practical one:

1. A room is not an anchor. *"A room’s agreement is variation, not a verdict. Selection between rooms is where the ground votes."* (Fig 7 :35) and *"Alignment is triangulation, not negotiation. A mirror cannot correct you."* (Fig 2 :35). Gathering agreeable operations academics manufactures the correlation that disqualifies a verdict.
2. Correlation has valence. *"agreement about the world is worthless when the agreeing frames share an evidence pool"* (Fig 2 :40). A list selected for receptiveness is one witness wearing many names. The transaction-cost and core-competence literatures are largely one economics and strategy pool.
3. A verifier must be outside the interior. *"A verifier that can reconstruct the claim cannot verify it."* (Fig 10 :42). The first contact therefore carries the residual and its refuter, not the framework.
4. Legally, unsolicited bulk email to scraped addresses breaches GDPR and CAN-SPAM style rules in many jurisdictions and gets the sending domain blacklisted. Public opt-in routes avoid that failure as well as the structural one.
