**Mode: `specified`, not `grounded`.** Figures are primary and decide. World sources are secondary, corroborate but never overrule, and a conflict returns the derivation to the domain census. This is a simulated derivation checked for internal consistency against the figures, not a ground reading.

## QUESTION

When should a company make a product or component itself rather than buy it from suppliers? Restated as an effect: why does R = |Γ(b,a) - o′| stay small at some component slots when made and at others when bought? R is indexed per component, never averaged (Fig 1 :19). Anchor: the counter and the market, named in Fig 2 :29. Option A, draw the boundary from a ranked list of core and non-core activities, predicts the list is independently measurable. Option B, treat the boundary as a maintained relation between two closures, predicts it is per slot, is a union of conditions, and moves when contact or reach changes. Option B is the figure-derived verdict.

## DOMAIN CENSUS

Arity: a relation among firm library Γ, component slot, action (make or buy), and in the buy case a second closure (the supplier), ranged against ground o′; at least three operands, two closures when buying. Symmetry: not symmetric, the ground does not update on the firm's preference (Fig 2 :25); "cheaper" is a threshold comparison (Fig 12 :87). Scale: component slot, firm architecture and library, supply network or market, and the time channel; the firm-to-supplier membrane is a filter with a direction (Fig 4 :21).

## VERDICT (from the figures)

The figure-derived verdict: a component belongs inside the firm when it is reachable and settles under internal composition, and outside when an import from a supplier whose closure differs produces a gated surplus that survives the independence debit, provided the make-capability is retained over time so the channel stays reachable. These are three conditions with three failure modes, kept separate per domain and never averaged. Mode `specified`. The economics and engineering pool corroborates the shape but is one witness; the independent biological source corroborates the mechanism, not the firm-level fact. Which specific component a named firm should make is a declared `gap`, not a hidden answer.

- **cost if wrong:** misdirected diagnosis. A firm failing C2 would be sold capability-building instruments, a firm failing C3 would be told to switch suppliers, and a single lever would be applied to a union that needs three. The economics literature's own efficiency test (Williamson via Klein and Mazzoni 2025) says unmotivated integration is eventually undone under rivalry, so the cost is real but bounded. In outreach terms, an expert's twenty minutes would be spent refuting a shape that was never the right shape.
- **undoable:** yes. No term is deleted if the claim fails; a refutation is added and the reach falls because *"something was added rather than because anything was deleted"* (Fig 6 :42-43). A wrong make-or-buy decision can be reversed by re-shoring or re-sourcing, but the lost knowledge cannot be replayed: the head start comes down, the method does not, and the independence debit has to be paid again (Fig 4 :22-25).
- **reversed by:** any of the eight pre-registered falsifiers, in particular (1) a new atom acquired by internal composition alone with no differing closure, (2) a supplier with zero debit and an identical closure producing a durable surplus, (3) an averaged ratio that predicts per component which slot fails, (4) one intervention restoring all conditions, (6) a capability whose value is invariant across a change of reward. A source with a genuinely different evidence pool showing that per-component boundaries are fully predicted by a single averaged transaction-cost variable would also reverse it. Until one of these fires, the verdict stands as `candidate`.
