## 1. A shape is a constraint you can state before you can name it

The canonical statement in `FIGURES_README_canonical.md` is that the framework returns *"what a missing thing would have to look like (a shape, not a name)"*, then stops where instruments can no longer distinguish and records the lineage. Fig 9 :32: *"you get a kind of thing, never the answer itself."* Fig 13 :134: *"Unreachable is a verdict here, not a silence,"* and :135: *"It cannot say which primitive is missing, because naming it would mean already holding it."*

From the essay *The Library of Babel*, read directly:

> *"you can cut your search short if the set of books returned is small and descriptive enough that it provides a general shape of the answer that satisfies your question."*

So for BUS-0072 a shape is: **the narrowed form of an answer about the firm boundary that can be stated before any particular component, supplier, "core activity" or transaction-cost variable is named.** It constrains. It does not fill.

- Shape: *a component sits inside the firm when internal composition is reachable and settles, and outside when an import from a differing closure produces a gated surplus that survives the independence debit, with the make-capability retained over time.*
- Name, withheld: *this firm should make its chips and buy its displays because chips are core and displays are not; or because chip asset specificity is high and display specificity is low.*

The distinction matters here because the make-or-buy literature is almost entirely in the naming business. Coase names transaction costs; Williamson names asset specificity, uncertainty and frequency; Prahalad and Hamel name core competence; Fine and Whitney name modularity and dependency. Those are bindings from the economics and management frames onto atoms, not outputs of the loop.

## 2. How the shape was obtained

The essay gives the bootstrapping procedure:

> *"If you don't know the right terms to search for, you will have to use the existing vocabulary you do know that is closest to the words that you wish to describe and grasp the shape of."*
> *"With each new term gained, you are able to narrow the space by definition and deepen your vocabulary."*

Applied here: the closest available terms were "vertical integration", "make-or-buy", "asset specificity", "core competence", "outsourcing hidden costs", "modularity" and "biological markets". None was used as the answer. Each was treated as a witness to be ranged against the residual, per Fig 8 :53: *"Residual first, frame second."* The description in effect terms (per-component boundary of two closures, with a channel that decays) came first; the frames were selected only after that description was fixed.

The essay also names the bundle stop:

> *"Perhaps your problem was a bundle or conflation of several problems masquerading as one? You will need to search all of those problems individually to find their answers and then combine them in a satisfactory way."*

That is the canonical warrant for treating BUS-0072 as a union (Fig 9 :54-57), not as one hard question. The empirical finding that firms both make and buy the same intermediate good is exactly a bundle that a binary variable hides.

The stop condition:

> *"You will know that you are done because the finish line will also be the starting place, as there is nowhere else to search and nothing else that can describe the answer better."*

In this solution the stop is reached when the three conditions are stated, the instrument for each is named, and the falsifiers are written. Any further search that only renames the conditions is motion, not progress (Fig 7 :32, *"one long room: motion, no progress"*).

## 3. What licenses a name

Only two things, both outside the shape:

1. **The ground settles it.** Formula step 5: *"the ground settles it. Verified only by what pays. A gate is not the ground."* The README: the framework *"stops at the limit of what the available instruments can distinguish, and records the lineage so the stop is honest."*
2. **A provenanced import from a frame whose closure differs** (Fig 6 :23, Fig 8 :50-52), with a source gate and a shadow test. Fig 8 :50: *"But shared atoms are not the signature. Two frames can hold the same atom by convergence, reaching it separately, which is corroboration at maximum independence."*

For BUS-0072 neither has happened here. There is no measurement in this folder that pays for "this specific component belongs inside this specific firm", and the economics names were adopted without a shadow test. Hence the `gap` row in F9 of `01_figure_grounded_audit.md`.

## 4. The source error is the specific forbidden move here

From *The Hard Problem is About Source*, read directly:

> *"You can have several underlying causes that create identical readings but the definition of the output would be read as the same."*
> *"If several paths create the same behavior, then behavior is a projection with lossy information"*
> *"The answer is unreached until a better instrument exists."*

This is the exact error a make-or-buy explanation makes when it reads a cost saving and names its source. A lower unit cost is a reading. It is consistent with a genuinely differing supplier closure, with a temporary quotation, with an accounting allocation, with a subsidised input, or with the firm having quietly lost the internal know-how and stopped counting the debit. The reading does not determine the source. The literature's habit of naming the source from the reading is metamerism, and no amount of agreement among readers fixes it. What is owed is an instrument that discriminates sources, which this solution does not have. Fig 6 :30: *"outward: does another frame already hold it? The description travels. Fails by being sharpened into terms only we can read. Cast it in effect terms."*

The essay closes: *"An instrument that can't distinguish these cases demonstrates its own inadequacy, it cannot claim anything substantial about what is being measured or what causes it."* That is why the expert packet asks for a failure condition before a theory.

## 5. Prestige is not action, applied to operations strategy

From *The Recursive Self-Improvement Trap*, read directly:

> *"Prestige must never be allowed to validate an action directly."*
> *"Prestige can fund an action. It can give an action a budget to be tried. But it cannot settle an action. Only the ground can settle it. The Ground Maintainer must be blind to prestige."*
> *"If prestige could buy actions, the system would collapse into a monoculture. The highest-prestige agent would monopolize compute and repeat its past success, and the system would stop exploring."*

The last line is an instance, in the author's own agent system, of Fig 7 :36 (*"A chain of mirrors is one long room"*) and Fig 10 :24 (*"the ground does not decay, the channel does"*). Because it is the author's own design, it is framework-internal corroboration, not an independent witness; it is recorded here as an illustration of the rule, not as evidence that firms behave this way.

For outreach this means: a list of famous operations or strategy professors who agree with the shape would be a monoculture, not a check. The ask gives the claim a budget to be tried by someone outside; it does not solicit endorsement. A reply from a directly contacted person is a conversation, never corroboration (Fig 2 :40).

## 6. Correction to my own §3 (an overstatement, retained as evidence)

I initially wrote that naming any particular component or "core activity" is forbidden by "shape, not a name". That statement was too broad and is withdrawn here. The canonical phrase governs a **missing** primitive, one not held in Γ. If a capability or a supplier relation is already in a firm's library, naming it is a binding from the management frame, not the invention of a new primitive. What remains forbidden is claiming the binding moves the wall (Fig 6 :21, *"MINT composes inside the closure and can never add an atom"*). The binding still needs provenance and a ground test, but it is not a category error in the way naming a seventh physical primitive would be. Both the corrected and the withdrawn formulations are kept so the correction is visible rather than silent.

## 7. This derivation is a simulation, not a ground reading

From the same essay, read directly:

> *"the output is only as good as the validator. The agent, or the tool, or the frame, or the theory, cannot validate itself. An external validator, or ground, is always needed to tether the veracity of the output to reality. Anything else is drift."*
> *"LLMs can quickly simulate an approximate answer/output with the formula found in the architecture post. It will often be better than the generic outputs that you will usually get otherwise too (intelligence is essentially arrangement). However, the truly testable output would need to be derived from the real architecture or working the formula yourself."*

So this `05` file, like `01`, is a simulated derivation checked for internal consistency against the figures. It is labelled `specified` wherever it makes a claim about firms. It is not a test of the question and cannot settle it. The point of publishing it is to make the shape and its refuters easy to check by someone whose ground contact is real and whose closure differs.

## 8. The shape, stated once more

For BUS-0072 the canonical shape is: **a three-condition conjunction with three distinct failure modes, a debit that changes sign over time, and a boundary that is per component rather than per firm.** It is not a ranked list of activities, not the name of a supplier, not a transaction-cost coefficient, and not a corporate outsourcing percentage. The names that appear in the literature are bindings, provenance owed. The instruments that would convert the shape into a grounded answer are not held here, so the answer stops at `specified` and the stop is recorded.
