## 1. A shape is a constraint, not a filler

The canonical limit: the framework returns *"what a missing thing would have to look like (a shape, not a name)."* Fig 9 :32: *"you get a kind of thing, never the answer itself."* Fig 13 :134: *"Unreachable is a verdict here, not a silence."*

From *The Library of Babel*: *"You can cut your search short if the set of books returned is small and descriptive enough that it provides a general shape of the answer that satisfies your question."*

For this question a **shape** is the constrained form of the answer we can state before we can measure it: a union compound, indexed per slot, with frequency and intensity as separate currencies read at a threshold, embedded in a cascade, and settled only by an independent observing system. A **name** here would be a percentage, a multiplier, or a single dominant region. The shape constrains; it does not fill. Saying "compound extremes are a union" is a shape. Saying "compound-event frequency rises 40 percent by 2 °C" is a number the figures do not supply.

## 2. How the shape is obtained

- *Library of Babel*: use the vocabulary closest to the thing, look for recurring new terms that overlap with it, recurse. *"With each new term gained, you narrow the space and deepen your vocabulary."*
- Fig 6 :30: *"The description travels. Fails by being sharpened into terms only we can read. Cast it in effect terms."* Fig 9 :30-31: *"your words are borrowed too, so describe the effect rather than the cause."*
- *Decoding Tacit Knowledge*: *"When contact isn't possible, the best you can send is a plain description of what you'd expect to see, because anyone can check that against the world."*
- Fig 8 :53: *"Residual first, frame second."* The residual was described in effect terms in `01` section 0 before any climate model was chosen as the frame.

For EAR-0014 the bootstrapping vocabulary came from the residual itself: joint exceedance, per-slot, threshold, count, magnitude, cascade. Only after that description were IPCC, compound-event typologies and extreme-value statistics brought in as frames. Choosing the climate-model frame first would have made any frame seem to fit.

## 3. What licenses a name

Two things, both outside the shape itself:

1. **The ground settles it.** Formula step 5: *"the ground settles it. Verified only by what pays."* The canonical README says the framework *"stops at the limit of what the available instruments can distinguish, and records the lineage so the stop is honest."* For this question: independent joint observations, not an ensemble vote.
2. **A provenanced import from a frame whose closure differs** (Fig 6 :23, Fig 8 :51), with a source gate and a shadow test. Palaeo records and documentary archives can widen the baseline, but their provenance must travel with them.

*They're Scanning Books* sharpens this: *"The real import is not a new term in the existing space. It is a new dimension of the space."* And: *"A minimum viable set of six terms is a human's careful guess at reality's compression axes; another sensorium's minimum set need not be those six."* Also: *"Declaring the primitives does not make them neutral. It makes the choice visible."*

The hazard list we enumerate (heat, drought, fire, flood, wind) is a visible choice of compression axes, not nature's own list. Fig 11 :43 says *"The habitat is enumerated, never composed,"* and Fig 11 :49 says valence is relative to the residual. Naming one hazard as *the* dominant compound driver turns a visible choice into an invisible one.

## 4. Why a single global multiplier is the forbidden error here

*The Hard Problem is About Source* gives the mechanism. An instrument reads green and *"cannot comment on the source"*: one green light, or yellow plus blue, or RGB, or CMYK. *"If several paths create the same behavior, then behavior is a projection with lossy information."* And: *"An instrument that can't distinguish these cases demonstrates its own inadequacy."* It was the spectrometer, not an argument, that resolved it.

A global mean warming multiplier reads one number and names its source as all compound hazards at once. That is the metamerism error: several marginal and dependence paths can produce the same aggregate reading. What is owed is a joint-distribution discriminator, not a multiplier.

*The Recursive Self-Improvement Trap*: *"the output is only as good as the validator. The agent, or the tool, or the frame, or the theory, cannot validate itself."* A model pool cannot validate its own compound-extreme projection.

## 5. Q: does the shape license naming a hazard pair? ANSWERED with a correction

**What licenses a particular pair?** Nothing in the framework. The framework returns a shape. A particular pair (heat with drought, storm surge with rainfall) is licensed only by a ground settlement of a minted term, or by a provenanced import with a shadow test. Until then, the pair is a binding from the climate frame onto held atoms and must be recorded as such.

**Correction to my own first reading (kept visible).** I initially treated the phrase *"shape, not a name"* as forbidding the mention of any specific hazard. That overstates it. The canonical phrase governs a **missing** primitive, one not held in Γ. Heat, drought, fire and the atmospheric conditions that couple them are already available as atoms in the physical frame. Naming them is a binding, not the invention of a new primitive and not an import that moves the wall (Fig 6 :21, *"MINT composes inside the closure and can never add an atom"*). What remains wrong in a single-multiplier claim is the source named from one projection, i.e. the metamerism error in section 4, not the mention of a held atom. Both claims are recorded so neither is quietly lost.

## 6. Two consequences for outreach

**Prestige is not action.** From *The Recursive Self-Improvement Trap*: *"Prestige must never be allowed to validate an action directly... Someone let prestige stand in for proof... Prestige can fund an action. It can give an action a budget to be tried. But it cannot settle an action. Only the ground can settle it. The Ground Maintainer must be blind to prestige."* A campaign gathering respected names to agree with the union claim is the named collapse, not corroboration. The legitimate ask is a budget to try the claim against observations.

**My own audit is a simulation, not a ground reading.** Same essay: *"LLMs can quickly simulate an approximate answer/output... However, the truly testable output would need to be derived from the real architecture or working the formula yourself."* LLM output is *"simulacra."* So `01` is a simulated derivation checked for internal consistency against the figures. It is not a test of the compound-extremes claim and must be labelled as such. It cannot settle anything it reports on.

## 7. What shape means, in one sentence for this question

A shape here is the per-slot, union-structured, two-currency form that any answer about warming and compound extremes must have if the figures govern the question, together with the refuters that would break it, stopping short of the numerical multiplier that only an independent observing system can supply.
