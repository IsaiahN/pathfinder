No new measurement of compound extremes was taken, no climate model was run, and no observation was re-analysed. It cannot settle the question it reports on. The Recursive Self-Improvement Trap calls LLM output *"simulacra"* and says *"the truly testable output would need to be derived from the real architecture or working the formula yourself."* That limit is stated here once and repeated in each substantive file rather than hidden in a footnote.

## Restatement as an effect

The question asks for the change in a quantity, but it does not say which quantity or at what index. Restated in effect terms: for a named compound-extreme slot (a place, a season, a hazard pair, a threshold definition), what does a change in global mean warming do to (a) the count of joint threshold crossings and (b) the magnitude beyond the threshold? Option A treats the compound as one hazard whose frequency and intensity scale with a single global scalar and reports one multiplier. Option B treats it as a compound of overlapping conditions with a residual indexed per slot, reports frequency and intensity as separate verdicts in separate currencies, and records what would reverse each. What an answer would look like is bound here as an ordinary term before the run, per the formula's "Direction": *a per-slot, per-currency verdict with a stated refuter*, not a single global number.

## What is deliberately not here

- No personal emails of named individuals.
- No ranking of people by how likely they are to agree.
- No bulk-send plan.
- No global multiplier for compound-event frequency or intensity. The figures return a shape, not a number (Fig 9 :32, Fig 13 :134).

Three figure-grounded reasons for the refusals:

1. **A room does not upgrade the label.** Fig 7 :12-13, *"same closure, handed along; agreement compounds, evidence does not"*; Fig 2 :35, *"Alignment is triangulation, not negotiation. A mirror cannot correct you."* A list of people selected for receptiveness manufactures the disqualifying correlation.
2. **A verifier must not hold the interiors.** Fig 10 :42, *"A verifier that can reconstruct the claim cannot verify it."* The first contact carries the residual and its refuter, not the framework.
3. **Filters are not witnesses.** Fig 9 :60, *"Use filters for the budget and witnesses for the verdict. Never let a filter hand you a verdict."* Global mean warming is the scenario filter, not the witness for a per-slot verdict.

Legally: scraping personal emails for unsolicited bulk outreach breaches GDPR and CAN-SPAM style rules in many jurisdictions. That practical failure sits on top of the structural one.
