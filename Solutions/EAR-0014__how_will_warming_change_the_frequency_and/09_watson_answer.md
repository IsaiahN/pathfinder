## QUESTION

Decide, in effect terms, what the Tether says about the change in frequency and intensity of compound extremes under warming. Option A: treat compound extremes as one hazard whose two statistics scale with a single global scalar, and report one multiplier. Option B: treat them as a union of overlapping conditions with a residual indexed per slot, report crossing count and exceedance magnitude as separate currencies, and record refuters and a declared gap for the number. What each leads to: A gives a simple number, hides the live slot, conflates count with magnitude and spends the independence of the people checking it; B gives a checkable shape, per-domain verdicts, written refuters and an honest stop at the limit of the joint instruments.

## DOMAIN CENSUS

R by arity, symmetry and scale, then one line per domain with actors, currency and anchor; partition or union; order by dependency.

- **R (the residual).** For a named slot (region, season, hazard pair, threshold definition), the gap between the predicted joint-crossing behaviour and what the observing system returns. Arity: at least two binding conditions, so a union compound, not a scalar hazard. Symmetry: operands are role-ordered (precondition before hazard, succession in time), so a symmetric conjunction is a measurement convenience, not the mechanism. Scale: event scale (hours to years) against global multi-decadal scale, a cross-scale question. Fig 9 :57: "a case in two conditions is not a harder case of either. It stops being a mixture and becomes a specific compound."
- **Domain 1, physical climate.** Actors: greenhouse forcing, temperature and moisture gradients, distributions of daily temperature and precipitation, teleconnections, soil-moisture feedback. Currency: radiative flux and probability. Anchor: the instrumental record plus physical law. Union: thermodynamic and dynamic contributions overlap. First in dependency order.
- **Domain 2, hazard statistics and detection.** Actors: thresholds, return periods, joint exceedances, dependence, reanalyses. Currency: crossing count and exceedance magnitude. Anchor: observed joint-exceedance counts, with reanalysis as a lossy stand-in. Union: marginal shift and dependence both bind. Second.
- **Domain 3, impacts and habitat.** Actors: soil moisture, vegetation, fire, water, energy, transport, health, governance. Currency: damage and cascade reach. Anchor: impact records. Union: several sectors bind at once. Third.
- **Domain 4, verification and outreach.** Actors: model ensembles, reanalyses, attribution studies, expert readers. Currency: agreement and residual. Anchor: the ground, never agreement. Separation from D1-D3 requires differing closures and non-shared evidence. Last.

Order by dependency: perceive the joint event, acquire vocabulary for compound types, state the objective (frequency or intensity), plan a lever, carry what changed contact (Fig 3 :20). Strain test: global warming as control parameter versus per-slot residual resolves by assigning the scalar to the budget filter (Fig 9 :60) and the residual to the slot (Fig 1 :19). No strain after re-census.

## VERDICT (from the figures)

Compound extremes are a union of overlapping conditions, not one hazard scaled by global mean warming. Verdicts are per domain and never averaged: D1 physical climate `specified` (distribution shift, tail sensitivity, thermodynamic over dynamic confidence), D2 hazard statistics `specified` (crossing count and exceedance magnitude separately, dependence as a distinct object, per-slot indexing), D3 impacts `specified` (multi-step cascades, valence relative to the residual), D4 verification `general` structural with a `specified` independence requirement. The numerical multiplier is a declared `gap` of the shape-not-a-name kind and unreachable at this budget, not underivable in principle. The claim remains `candidate` and simulated: it has not been settled by a ground reading.

- **cost if wrong:** adaptation is misallocated when a single multiplier hides the live slot, when count is added to magnitude, or when a lever that clears one marginal hazard is assumed to clear the intersection. The avoidable second cost is spending the independence of outside readers by asking for agreement before they have checked the residual; Fig 2 :42 says "Buy independence in proportion to the cost of being wrong."
- **undoable:** yes. Each per-domain verdict can be revised on new joint observations without withdrawing the union, per-slot or two-currency structure. No physical atom has to be withdrawn, and the gap is explicitly recorded rather than filled with a guessed number.
- **reversed by:** a ground reading, not agreement. An independent joint observing system returning per-slot counts and exceedance magnitudes would settle the number; a sufficient global scalar, a lossless combined index, an unchanged tail under a mean shift, or a first-order-only impact accounting would each fire one of the pre-registered falsifiers; a figure re-census returning a numerical multiplier would close the declared gap; an instrument resolving whether a joint pair can be treated as one quantity at the decision level would clear the live ⋛ type strain. Until one of these fires, the verdict stands as candidate.
