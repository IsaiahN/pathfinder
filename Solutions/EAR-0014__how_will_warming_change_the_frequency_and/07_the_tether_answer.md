# The Tether's answer: warming and compound extremes

**Mode: `specified` on the per-domain claims, `general` on the structural shape, `gap` on the numerical magnitude. Not `grounded`.** This is a shape and a route, not a measurement. Simulated derivation checked against the figures, not a ground reading (`05_what_shape_means.md` section 6).

## 1. The residual it answers

For each compound-extreme slot (region, season, hazard pair, threshold definition) we want the change in (a) joint threshold-crossing frequency and (b) exceedance magnitude under a change in global mean warming. The question as asked bundles these into one answer. Fig 9 :55 refuses the bundle: *"A bundled problem is usually a union of overlapping conditions, not a list of separate ones."*

## 2. The shape, per domain

**D1, physical climate (`specified`, triangulated).** Warming shifts an ordering. Fig 13 :20 calls a distribution *"ordering, so more and less can be said"*; Fig 13 :31-32 says *"a remainder compounding through a chain that amplifies is chaos."* A small motion of the ordering near a threshold converts previously sub-threshold draws into crossings. The thermodynamic contribution (moisture, evaporative demand) is better anchored than the dynamic contribution (circulation), and the assessment itself assigns lower confidence to the dynamic part. Fig 13 :76 separates the two: *"A gradient over space and one over time are different terms, because which state it is bonded to is part of what it is."* A warming trend is not a hotspot map.

**D2, hazard statistics (`specified`, triangulated).** An extreme is declared by a comparison. Operators :48 defines ⋛ as *"a threshold relation between two quantities"*; Fig 12 :87 gives the test *"is it about which is larger, rather than both being present? Yes."* Once the threshold exists there are two currencies: Fig 12 :125, *"A composition costs in two currencies, and they do not add."* Report crossing count and exceedance magnitude separately. IPCC AR6 reports that percentage frequency changes are larger for rarer events, and that hot-extreme frequency rises nonlinearly with warming. Ridder et al. 2020 define the compound as a joint exceedance and show that dependence changes the joint probability. The residual is per slot: Fig 1 :19, *"The residual is indexed by object slot, not aggregated."* Global mean warming is the scenario filter, not the witness (Fig 9 :60).

**D3, impacts and habitat (`specified`, triangulated).** The habitat is enumerated, never composed (Fig 11 :43), and contact cascades: Fig 11 :52-53 uses DDT reaching the eagles as the figure's own earth-systems example of a residual three steps out. Heat and drought propagate through soil moisture, vegetation, fire, air quality, health, energy, transport, grain markets. Valence is relative to the residual (Fig 11 :49), so the sign of the change is not universal: the assessment itself reports regional decreases in agricultural and ecological drought alongside widespread increases. No standing list of hazards survives a change of residual (Fig 11 :51).

**D4, verification (`general` structural, with a `specified` independence requirement).** The anchor is the observing system. Fig 2 :29: *"An anchor is whatever settles the question and does not change its answer because of what any frame thinks."* Fig 10 :24: *"the ground does not decay, the channel does."* Model ensembles sharing reanalysis and assessment literature are one evidence pool, not several witnesses (Fig 2 :40). Fig 2 :42: *"Buy independence in proportion to the cost of being wrong."* Adaptation decisions are costly enough to justify independent joint observations.

**The number (`gap`).** Fig 9 :32: *"you get a kind of thing, never the answer itself."* Fig 13 :134: *"Unreachable is a verdict here, not a silence."* No figure line returns a percentage or multiplier. That is a verdict, not an omission.

## 3. What it explicitly does not say

- Not a global multiplier for frequency or intensity.
- Not that warming increases every compound extreme in every region. Sign is per slot.
- Not that frequency and intensity can be averaged into one risk score (Fig 12 :125-130).
- Not that a model ensemble is a set of independent witnesses (Fig 2 :40).
- Not that a lever on one marginal hazard clears the compound intersection (Fig 9 :55).
- Not a new physical mechanism for any hazard. The atoms are borrowed from the climate frame as bindings, provenanced as such (Fig 8 :51).
- Not `grounded`. Nothing here is settled by a measurement taken in this run.

## 4. What would settle it, and what would break it

**Ground.** A joint observing system that returns, per slot, both the count of joint threshold crossings and the magnitude beyond the threshold, independent of the model frame that predicted them. Fig 6 :32: *"An instrument is not built from a description. It is improved from a worse instrument already returning something."* The proto-instrument already exists in marginal networks; the improvement is joint, not a new sensor specified from nothing.

**Standing falsifiers from `01` section 4:**

1. A single scalar in global mean warming that is a sufficient statistic for every compound type.
2. A lossless single index combining count and magnitude.
3. A distribution shift that leaves joint tail-crossing frequency unchanged.
4. Two studies using the same reanalysis counted as independent confirmations.
5. A lever that clears a marginal hazard and also clears the compound intersection.
6. An impact accounting with no residual three steps out.

## 5. The honest stop

The compound typology is a current enumeration of arrangements over a set that is closed while the arrangements are not (Fig 13 :115). New compound types will appear. The answer stops at the limit of what the available joint instruments can distinguish, and records the lineage so the stop is checkable. Fig 8 :39: *"So a search that finds nothing is never a proof of absence."* Indiscernibility is not identity. The most defensible one-line answer is that warming reorganises the tail and the dependence between hazards, so compound frequency moves faster than the mean while intensity moves on a different currency, and the sign is per slot until the ground says otherwise.

## 6. One-paragraph version for outreach

> The figures do not give a percentage for compound extremes. They give the shape any such answer must have. A compound extreme is a union of overlapping conditions, not one hazard with a bigger number, so a lever that clears a single marginal hazard leaves the intersection standing. The residual must be indexed per place, season, hazard pair and threshold, because a global mean hides the live slot. Frequency and intensity are two currencies that do not add: one is a count of threshold crossings, the other is a magnitude beyond the threshold. Because warming shifts an ordering, rare joint crossings move faster than the mean. Impacts reach three steps out through soil, fire, energy and markets, and the sign of the change is relative to the residual rather than universal. What could settle the number is an independent joint observing system, not more agreement among models that share the same evidence pool. The falsifiers were written before the claim.
