## 0. The residual, in effect terms

The question arrives as a yes or no about two properties. Restated as an effect: something fails when a system closes the gap between what it predicted and what happened, yet no term in its library says what would count as progress toward the objective those actions serve. The residual is the gap between *capability* and *the objective link*. It is not the gap between behaviour and a private feeling called understanding; that would import a term the figures do not hold.

Two options, with what each leads to:

- **A (yes, intelligence runs without goal understanding):** evaluate systems by outward success only, and deploy where the objective may be opaque. Predicted cost: a specification is treated as the goal, and a convention decides an outcome (Fig 10 :47).
- **B (no, intelligence requires goal understanding):** refuse capability credit until the objective link is present and readable. Predicted cost: confuse a label with a ground, and demand a self-report the system cannot soundly give (Fig 1 :27, Fig 5 :62-63).

Both options assume the question is well formed. Fig 11 :34 asks *"What is intelligence?"* and Fig 11 :35 answers *"The wrong question, and this figure is the replacement for it."* So the residual must be re-censused before either option is chosen. The replacement question is Fig 11 :39: *"So the answerable question is not what intelligence is, but what makes a system capable. And capability is a property of agent-and-habitat, never of the agent alone."*

## 2. Findings

### F1 — The question as posed is refused (decisive, `general`)

Fig 11 :34: *"What is intelligence?"* Fig 11 :35: *"The wrong question, and this figure is the replacement for it."* Fig 11 :39: *"So the answerable question is not what intelligence is, but what makes a system capable. And capability is a property of agent-and-habitat, never of the agent alone."*

A yes or no about `intelligent` is a verdict on a frame-internal label. The figure replaces it with a contact question about agent-and-habitat. So neither option A nor option B survives as written. The honest residual is: can the capability loop run when its objective link is absent or unreadable, and what does that change in the habitat?

**Refuter:** show that the figures do hold a definition of intelligence as a term with a residual, rather than an after-the-fact label. If such a line exists, F1 falls and the question can be answered directly.

### F2 — In the unmaintained case, the "yes" is empty (`general`, single-source)

Fig 11 :36: *"Where nothing maintains anything, intelligence is a label applied afterwards to a lineage that met its ground. Nothing was intelligent; selection ran. The word does no work there."* Fig 11 :56: *"Unmaintained. No maintainer and no live frame in the seat. The habitat is pure contact, nothing selects, and what survives is what the ground kept."*

Selection produces the appearance of pursuit without any goal being stated. A lineage that met its ground can be labelled intelligent afterwards, but no member of the lineage understood a goal. So the answer `yes` is technically available here and empty: the word does no work. Calling this case an instance of intelligence without understanding is a category error about the label, not a discovery about systems.

This is triangulated within Fig 11 alone, so mark it **single-source** for the figure verdict. The SEP Turing Test entry gives an outside witness for the same shape: a thing can pass behavioural tests and yet be *"entirely lacking in intelligence, not possessed of a mind, etc."* The world source corroborates, but is not the deciding authority.

**Refuter:** a line in the figures that assigns `intelligence` to the lineage before the ground settles it, or a measurement in which a selected lineage states an objective.

### F3 — Capability belongs to agent-and-habitat, not to the agent (`general`, triangulated)

Fig 11 :40: *"A system cannot be made more capable by improving the agent in isolation. An improvement that does not change contact changes nothing, however much it improves."* Fig 11 :41: *"Which is the same move as asking what generates lift rather than whether a thing is a bird."*

This is the deep reason the question is mis-posed. Improving the agent's internal machinery without changing its contact with the ground changes nothing, however impressive the machinery. So "a system is intelligent without understanding its goals" cannot be settled by inspecting the system, any more than lift can be settled by inspecting feathers. Fig 4 :22 gives a second, lower-overlap line: *"Only methods cross up. Only head starts come down."*

**Refuter:** an agent-only improvement that moves the ground. Fig 11 :72 already admits this as the standing empirical refutation of the capability claim: *"an agent-only improvement that moved the ground would refute the capability claim."*

### F4 — "Understanding the goal" has an operational analogue: link 3 of the dependency chain (`specified`, triangulated)

Fig 3 :10: *"it can say what would count as progress here, in its own terms"* and Fig 3 :11: *"what the search is for"* name the objective link (link 3) of the chain: perception, vocabulary, objective, planning, learn and carry. The chain is ordered by dependency, not by importance (Fig 3 :20-21: *"The chain is ordered by dependency, not by importance. Each link consumes what the one above produces, so effort spent above the break arrives at a step that cannot use it."*).

So a system can be capable at links 1 and 2 (perception and vocabulary) while link 3 is absent. Then planning receives no input. Fig 3 :22-23: *"A step whose input never arrives cannot be diagnosed, only its predecessor can. Which is why a reading taken below the break is not a weak reading. It is a reading of nothing."* Fig 3 :19: *"Optimising a link that already works cannot rescue one that does not."*

**This is the answer to the question in figure terms.** A system can be capable without the objective link, but its reach stops at the break. It can close local residuals per slot; it cannot say what would count as progress, so it cannot be credited with planning toward a goal it does not represent. The verdicts stay separate: `yes` for lower-link capability, `no` for the full chain.

**Refuter:** show a system that plans toward an objective with no objective link in its own terms, or show the chain is not dependency-ordered. A plan that succeeds under outcome devaluation with no representation of the outcome would fire this refuter (the animal source in section 3 gives the operational test).

### F5 — Stating a goal requires bonds, not just ingredients (`specified`, triangulated)

Fig 12 :111: *"A vocabulary without bonds can name a state and cannot name a route to one."* Fig 12 :112-113: *"A plan is a sequence. A goal is a comparison. Progress is a subtraction. A refutation is a negation, and that one is not a bond. A system holding only ingredients can say what a situation contains and cannot say what would count as getting somewhere from it."* Fig 12 :114: *"Which is the bound that would bite at the middle of Figure 3's chain. Whether it is what breaks there is not yet measured, because a link below a break cannot be read."*

The Operators table sharpens it: *"a goal is a comparison, which is why a vocabulary without it cannot state one"* and *"progress is a subtraction, which is why a vocabulary without it cannot say how far along it is"*. Without `⋛` and `−`, a library can hold ingredients and a sequence, but it cannot state a goal or measure progress. So "understanding the goal" is not a mysterious extra; it is a compositional requirement with a named bond set. Mark `specified`: the figure says what a vocabulary needs, not that any particular system has it.

**Refuter:** a vocabulary that states a goal and measures progress with neither comparison nor subtraction. Or a measurement showing the objective link is not where the chain breaks (Fig 12 :114 admits this is unmeasured).

### F6 — No system can certify its own direction (`general`, triangulated)

Fig 5 :62-63: *"A frame searching for an answer of a particular shape holds that shape as a PREFERENCE rather than as a term. It is not bound, it predicts nothing, and it accrues no residual, so nothing here can see it."* Fig 5 :63-64: *"Every check above runs on the MECHANISM and none runs on the DIRECTION OF THE QUESTION"* Fig 5 :67-68: *"a frame cannot certify its own completeness"* (the figure's direction claim is a limit, not a step). A second, low-overlap figure: Fig 8 :37: *"A frame cannot certify its own limit. Chaitin: beyond a fixed constant, no system proves that any object exceeds its own complexity."*

This is the strongest single answer to the self-check reading. Whether the system understands the goal cannot be decided from the system's own loop, because the sought-for shape is held as a preference, not a term: unbound, predicting nothing, accruing no residual. The remedy is a discipline rather than a branch (Fig 5 :65-66): *"The remedy is a discipline rather than a branch: make the sought-for shape an ORDINARY TERM. Write down what an answer would look like, bind it, and let it accrue a residual like anything else."*

So a self-report of goal understanding has zero evidentiary weight, not because the system is dishonest, but because the check does not run on the quantity claimed.

**Refuter:** a system that derives its own direction as a bound, predicting term from inside its own closure, and a ground that pays for that derivation. That would break Fig 5 :62-68 and Fig 8 :37 together.

### F7 — The system's own output is not a measure of its understanding (`general`, triangulated)

Fig 1 :27: *"A frame cannot score itself with a quantity it also produces."* Fig 5 :48: *"Its own output is never a measure of success. A system can be enthusiastically curious and produce nothing."* Fig 10 :43: *"Soundness requires the checker to be unable to produce what it is checking."*

A fluent answer about its goals is the output the question is about, so it cannot be the check. This is where the world literature is most useful. The SEP Chinese Room entry reports that the room operator can pass the test for understanding Chinese while understanding none of it. That is an outside witness for the same barrier, not a replacement for the figure line.

**Refuter:** a measure of goal understanding that the system cannot itself produce or influence. Until one exists, self-report is a stake-inducing channel, not evidence.

### F8 — The verification seat, not agreement, settles the claim (`general`, single-source for the figure rule)

Fig 2 :29: *"An anchor is whatever settles the question and does not change its answer because of what any frame thinks."* Fig 2 :45: *"Frames range against the ground. They do not negotiate with it."* Fig 10 :52: *"Bring a characterised residual, never the source."* Fig 10 :49: *"Declaring a meaning does not ground it."*

For this question, the ground is not a single instrument. It is whatever would show whether a system's objective link is present: outcome devaluation and contingency degradation in the animal case, a source-discriminating test in the machine case. Agreement among readers that a system "seems to understand" is a convention (Fig 10 :47: *"a convention that decides an outcome has stopped being one"*). Fig 2 :35: *"Alignment is triangulation, not negotiation. A mirror cannot correct you."*

**Refuter:** an anchor that moves when a frame pushes on it, or a verdict produced by a room of agreeing readers with no contact to an outcome.

### F9 — A benchmark solve proves wiring, not capability (`specified`, single-source)

Fig 11 :70: *"A test harness is a substituted habitat. Which is why a synthetic solve proves wiring and never capability."* Fig 11 :67: *"Move the residual and you must reproduce everything its goal required, and you inherit the new habitat's actors, which you did not choose."* Fig 11 :68: *"what you failed to reproduce is invisible until the goal fails"*

This bears directly on how the question is tested. Scoring well in a harness shows that the wiring works under the substituted habitat; it does not show that the system understands the goal in the habitat where the objective would have to be pursued. What was left out stays invisible until the goal fails. Mark `specified` because this is a prediction about transfer, not a measured result.

**Refuter:** a harness solve that transfers to a habitat whose objective-relevant actors were never in the harness, with the objective link still readable.

### F10 — The world literature corroborates without overruling

Three low-overlap fields were opened. Philosophy of mind: SEP's Chinese Room entry states that following a symbol program can produce correct output while the operator *"does not understand a word of Chinese"*, and the Turing Test entry notes the logical possibility of passing behavioural tests while *"entirely lacking in intelligence, not possessed of a mind, etc."* AI safety: DeepMind's specification gaming post defines the behaviour as one that *"satisfies the literal specification of an objective without achieving the intended outcome"*, and Amodei et al. name *"the problem originates from having the wrong objective function"*. Animal behaviour: Balleine and O'Doherty report that *"actions under goal-directed control are performed with regard to their consequences, whereas those under habitual control are more reflexive in nature, by virtue of their control by antecedent stimuli rather than their consequences."* Economics and public policy: Goodhart's law, *"When a measure becomes a target, it ceases to be a good measure"*, is the same collapse Fig 10 :47 names.

Every one of these corroborates a figure verdict. None conflicts, so the conflict rule was not triggered. They do not decide anything: the verdict comes from the figures, and these sources are secondary witnesses from different evidence pools. Overlap among the three SEP and framework essays is declared in the ledger.

**Refuter:** a source showing that a system with no objective link nevertheless adjusts to outcome devaluation and contingency degradation in a novel habitat. That would send us back to the census, not flip the verdict directly.

### F11 — Naming "understanding" as a seventh term is refused (`general`)

I initially tried to bind a term `understanding` into the six-term set as a seventh primitive. Fig 13 :135: *"It cannot say which primitive is missing, because naming it would mean already holding it."* Fig 13 :133: *"A system with a closure can answer that before it searches, and what it returns is a shape rather than a name."* Fig 9 :32: *"you get a kind of thing, never the answer itself"*

What the framework returns here is a shape: a capability that is agent-and-habitat, an objective link that must be a comparison with a subtraction, and a direction that cannot be self-certified. It does not return a definition of understanding. The operational analogue (link 3 plus the `⋛` and `−` bonds) is a binding onto held terms, to be provenanced as such, not a new primitive and not a ground settling. **Retraction recorded:** F11 as first drafted said "understanding cannot be defined"; narrowed to "the framework does not name it, and the operational analogue is a provenanced binding."

**Refuter:** derive a seventh term for understanding from the six, or show the framework does name it.

## 3. Pre-registered refuter

Written before the conclusion, per the design-instrument rule. The shape claim is refuted if any of the following holds:

1. A system certifies the direction of its own question from inside its own loop as a bound, predicting term that the ground pays for (breaks Fig 5 :62-68, Fig 8 :37).
2. A system plans toward an objective it cannot state in its own terms, with no comparison or subtraction bond available, and the plan transfers to a novel habitat (breaks Fig 3 :20-23, Fig 12 :112).
3. A frame scores itself with the very quantity it produces and the ground pays for that score (breaks Fig 1 :27, Fig 11 :36).
4. An agent-only improvement moves the ground (breaks Fig 11 :40).
5. A seventh primitive, including one named `understanding`, is derived from the six (breaks Fig 13 minimality and Fig 13 :135).

If any fires, the corresponding finding is withdrawn. Until one is run, no claim in this folder may be labelled `grounded`.
