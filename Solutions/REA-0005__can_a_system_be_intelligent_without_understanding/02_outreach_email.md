# Outreach draft: check the shape claim before you decide about the theory

**Use:** one-to-one, or as a cover note attached to a submission through a public venue in `03_public_contact_points.csv`. **Not** for bulk send. No personal emails, no purchased list, no ranking of recipients by likely agreement.

**Hard precondition:** nothing in this email has been measured. It states a `general` shape and a `specified` operational analogue. Do not let a recipient read it as a result.

---

**Subject:** One checkable claim about intelligence and goal representation, before any theory

> Hi [name],
>
> I have one specific output I would like you to check against what you already know. I am not asking you to read a theory first, and I am not asking you to agree with it. If the output does not survive, there is nothing else to look at.
>
> **The question as asked, restated in effect terms.** "Can a system be intelligent without understanding the goals it is pursuing?" is a bundle of two conditions: being capable, and stating what would count as progress toward an objective. The framework I am using refuses the label question and asks instead what makes a system capable. Capability is a property of the agent together with its habitat, never of the agent alone. So the question I actually put to you is: can the capability loop run when its objective link is absent or unreadable, and what does that change?
>
> **The output (`general` on the shape, `specified` on the analogue):**
>
> 1. Where nothing maintains anything, "intelligent" is a label applied afterwards to a lineage that met its ground. Selection ran. Calling that case intelligence without understanding is true in a degenerate way and empty, because the word does no work there.
> 2. In a maintained system, capability is agent-and-habitat. Improving the agent in isolation does not change contact, so it does not change capability.
> 3. "Understanding the goal" has an operational analogue: the objective link in a dependency chain, the link that can say what would count as progress in the system's own terms. Planning consumes that link. Without it, effort spent above the break arrives at a step that cannot use it.
> 4. Writing that link requires bonds, not just ingredients: a goal is a comparison, progress is a subtraction. A vocabulary holding only ingredients can say what a situation contains and cannot say what would count as getting somewhere from it.
> 5. A system cannot certify its own goal-understanding. The thing it would have to check is held as a preference, not as a bound term, so it predicts nothing and accrues no residual. This is a limit of the loop, not a defect of a particular system.
>
> **What I am not claiming:** that any named system does or does not understand its goals; that this is a measurement; that behaviour or a fluent self-report identifies an inner source; that "understanding" is a seventh primitive. The framework returns a shape, not a name. The operational analogue is a binding onto terms the framework already holds, and I record it as a binding rather than a derivation.
>
> **What would refute the output, written before this email:**
> 1. a system certifies the direction of its own question from inside its own loop, and a ground pays for that derivation;
> 2. a system plans toward an objective it cannot state in its own terms, with no comparison or subtraction available, and the plan transfers to a novel habitat;
> 3. a frame scores itself with the quantity it produces and the ground pays for that score;
> 4. an agent-only improvement moves the ground;
> 5. a seventh primitive, including one named "understanding", is derived from the six.
>
> If any one holds, I withdraw the corresponding claim. A one-line "this fails because..." is more useful to me than agreement. If you would rather not engage, no reply is needed.
>
> **What I would like from you:** what would you count as a failure of claim 3 or claim 5? I want your verdict on those claims before showing you where they came from.
>
> **If it survives, and only then:** the sources are below. The figures are the authority, not my summary of them.
> - Verbatim figure text with line numbers: `FRAMEWORK/reference/03-FIGURES_TEXT.md` in the public Tether repository, `general-agent` branch: https://github.com/IsaiahN/tether/tree/general-agent/figures
> - Canonical usage rule: https://github.com/IsaiahN/tether/blob/general-agent/figures/README.md
> - Worked narrative on capability and contact: https://adventuresinml.substack.com/p/how-intelligence-actually-works
>
> **If you want to use the method:** the formula is in the figures link. Run it on a problem you know well and can check against a ground. Write down what would refute your result *before* you run it, and state the mode of what you get. A reported failure is more useful to me than a reported success.
>
> Thank you for your time,
> [name]

## Two-stage offer, in one line

Stage 1: check the five outputs and the five refuters with no theory shown. Stage 2, opt-in only: then read the sources. A reply that says "claim 3 fails because outcome devaluation leaves habit responding unchanged" is a success, because it locates the error.

## What is deliberately not in this email

No claim that a named system does or does not understand its goals. No request to endorse the framework. No use of the reader's field reputation as evidence. No link before the verdict is recorded. No follow-up sequence; one message, one reply, one optional second message if invited.
