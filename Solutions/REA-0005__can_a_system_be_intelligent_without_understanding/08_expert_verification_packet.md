# Expert verification packet: intelligence without goal understanding

**What this is:** a claim in a form a researcher on intelligence, goal representation or animal cognition can check. Mode, derivation and refuters are stated before the conclusion, so a reader knows what to look for and what would settle it.

**Mode: `general` on the shape, `specified` on the operational analogue, not `grounded`.** No measurement of any named system is claimed. This is a shape and a route, not a result.

**Standing: simulated derivation checked for internal consistency against the figures, not a ground reading.** It cannot settle the question it reports on.

## 1. The claim, in one paragraph

The question "can a system be intelligent without understanding the goals it is pursuing?" bundles two conditions, so it must be unbundled before it is searched. The framework refuses the label question and asks instead what makes a system capable: capability is a property of the agent together with its habitat, not of the agent alone. In the unmaintained case, "intelligent" is a label applied afterwards to a lineage that met its ground, selection ran, and the word does no work there. In a maintained system, the operational analogue of goal-understanding is link 3 of a dependency chain, the link that can say what would count as progress in the system's own terms; writing that link requires a comparison and a subtraction, so a vocabulary of bare ingredients cannot state a goal. A system can be capable at lower links with no objective link, but then planning receives no input. And no system can certify its own direction from inside its loop, because what it would have to check is held as a preference, not as a bound term. Only the ground maintainer can settle the claim. The framework returns those constraints and five refuters written in advance, not a name.

### C2 — bond-level, checkable by reading Figure 12 and the Operators table

- **Statement:** writing the objective link needs comparison and subtraction. A vocabulary holding only ingredients can name a state but cannot state a goal or measure progress.
- **Source, verbatim:** Fig 12 :111 *"A vocabulary without bonds can name a state and cannot name a route to one."* Fig 12 :112-113 *"A plan is a sequence. A goal is a comparison. Progress is a subtraction. A refutation is a negation, and that one is not a bond. A system holding only ingredients can say what a situation contains and cannot say what would count as getting somewhere from it."* Operators :52: *"a goal is a comparison, which is why a vocabulary without it cannot state one"* Operators :46: *"progress is a subtraction, which is why a vocabulary without it cannot say how far along it is"*
- **What to check:** is `⋛` correctly identified as the comparison bond and `−` as subtraction? Is the claim being smuggled into a definition of understanding (it is not; it is a provenanced binding onto held bonds)?
- **Refuter:** a vocabulary that states a goal and measures progress with neither comparison nor subtraction; or a system with no comparison bond that adjusts to outcome devaluation and contingency degradation in a novel habitat. Fig 12 :114 states the location of the real break is *"not yet measured"*, so this remains `specified`. If the refuter fires, C2 falls while C1 may stand.

### C3 — framework limit, checkable by reading Figures 5 and 8, and by an outside check of the claim

- **Source, verbatim:** Fig 5 :62-63 *"A frame searching for an answer of a particular shape holds that shape as a PREFERENCE rather than as a term. - **Refuter:** a system that derives its own direction as a bound, predicting term and a ground that pays for that derivation. That would break Fig 5 :62-68 and Fig 8 :37 together. A fluent self-report is not such a derivation.

## 3. What is explicitly not claimed

- Not a yes or no to the original question; it is refused and replaced.
- Not that any named system does or does not understand its goals. No system was measured.
- Not a definition of understanding, not a seventh primitive, and not a ground result.
- Not that a fluent behaviour or a benchmark score identifies a source. Fig 11 :70: *"A test harness is a substituted habitat. Which is why a synthetic solve proves wiring and never capability."*

## 5. What would settle it

A source-discriminating instrument, not a further argument. From *The Hard Problem is About Source*, carried from the exemplar: an instrument that reads green *"cannot comment on the source"*. Here the question is whether a system's behaviour tracks the value of an outcome and the action-outcome contingency, or only an antecedent stimulus. Balleine and O'Doherty supply the operational discrimination: outcome devaluation and contingency degradation. Applied to a machine, a claim of goal-understanding would need an analogous test that a reader can run, plus the five refuters above. No such test is supplied by this packet.
