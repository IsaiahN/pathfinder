## 1. A shape is a constraint, not a filler

The canonical figures README states the limit: *"what a missing thing would have to look like (a shape, not a name)."* Fig 9 :32: *"you get a kind of thing, never the answer itself"* Fig 13 :133: *"A system with a closure can answer that before it searches, and what it returns is a shape rather than a name."*

From *The Library of Babel*: *"You can cut your search short if the set of books returned is small and descriptive enough that it provides a general shape of the answer that satisfies your question."*

For this question, the shape is not a definition of understanding. It is the narrowed form an answer must have before we can name any system that satisfies it:

- capability belongs to agent-and-habitat, not the agent alone (Fig 11 :39);
- the objective link says what counts as progress in the system's own terms (Fig 3 :10);
- stating a goal needs comparison and subtraction, not just ingredients (Fig 12 :112);
- the direction of the question cannot be checked from inside the loop (Fig 5 :63-64).

That is a shape: it excludes answers that put capability in the agent alone, answers that treat a behaviour reading as a source reading, and answers that let a system certify its own goal. It does not fill the slot with a name.

## 2. How the shape was obtained

- *The Library of Babel*: use the vocabulary closest to the thing, look for recurring new terms that overlap with it, recurse. *"With each new term gained, you narrow the space and deepen your vocabulary."*
- Fig 9 :30-31: *"your words are borrowed too, so describe the effect rather than the cause"* The effect here is the gap between a system closing residuals and its inability to state what the objective was.
- *Decoding Tacit Knowledge*, carried from the exemplar: *"When contact isn't possible, the best you can send is a plain description of what you'd expect to see, because anyone can check that against the world."* Hence the operational analogue: outcome devaluation and contingency degradation, not a private introspection claim.
- *The Library of Babel* on stopping: the answer is reached when *"the finish line will also be the starting place"*, because by then the searcher has *"composed the answer through the process of locating it."* Here: the shape and the four refuters were written before the verdict, so the search stopped at a describable shape rather than at a system name.

## 3. The specific error this question invites

*The Hard Problem is About Source*, carried from the exemplar, gives the mechanism. An instrument reads green and *"cannot comment on the source"*. Several paths can produce the same reading. *"If several paths create the same behavior, then behavior is a projection with lossy information."* And: *"An instrument that can't distinguish these cases demonstrates its own inadequacy."* It was the spectrometer, not an argument, that resolved it.

Applied here: a system's fluent account of its goals, or its high score in a harness, is a projection. Reading "understanding" off it is the metamerism error. Several sources could produce the same reading: a represented objective with comparison and subtraction, a habit driven by antecedent stimuli, a specification that was gamed, or an after-the-fact label applied by an observer. What is owed is a better instrument, not a name. Fig 11 :70: *"A test harness is a substituted habitat. Which is why a synthetic solve proves wiring and never capability."*

## 5. Answer to the question "what does shape mean here?"

**It means the four constraints in section 1, held as ordinary terms with refuters, and nothing more.** In particular:

- The framework does not answer yes or no to the original question. It refuses the label question and replaces it with a contact question (Fig 11 :34-39).
- "Understanding the goals" is not a seventh primitive. Comparison and subtraction are already held bonds (Operators :52/:46), and the objective link is already a held link (Fig 3 :10). Naming the analogue is a binding with provenance, not an invention.
- "Intelligent" is not a measurable property of a system in isolation. It is a label applied afterwards in the unmaintained case, and a property of agent-and-habitat in the maintained case (Fig 11 :36, :39-40).
- The shape excludes self-certification. A system's own account of its goals cannot be the check (Fig 1 :27, Fig 10 :43).

**Mode:** `general` on the constraints, `specified` on the operational analogue, not `grounded`.

## 6. Correction to my own section 3

I first wrote that the shape-not-a-name rule forbids calling anything "goal-understanding". That overstates it. The README's phrase governs a **missing** primitive: *"what a missing thing would have to look like (a shape, not a name)."* Comparison and subtraction are not missing; they are in the bond set. The correct claim is narrower: naming the operational analogue is a binding onto held bonds, and binding is legitimate only when its provenance is shown and a shadow test explains a gap (Fig 8 :51-52: *"Convergent derivation and adopted import look identical in the contents. Only the record of where each came from separates them"*).

The metamerism point in section 3 is **not** retracted. What remains wrong in any naive answer is naming the source of a reading without an instrument that discriminates among sources. Both the narrowed correction and the original point are recorded so neither disappears.

## 7. Two consequences for this question

**Prestige is not action.** From *The Recursive Self-Improvement Trap*, opened this session: *"Prestige must never be allowed to validate an action directly... Prestige can fund an action. It can give an action a budget to be tried. But it cannot settle an action. Only the ground can settle it."* A list of famous people who agree that a system understands its goals is the named collapse. The legitimate ask is a failure condition and an instrument.

**This audit is a simulation, not a ground reading.** Same essay, opened this session: *"LLMs can quickly simulate an approximate answer/output... However, the truly testable output would need to be derived from the real architecture or working the formula yourself."* So `01` and this file are simulated derivations checked for internal consistency against the figures. They are **not** tests of the claim and must be labelled as such. They cannot settle anything they report on.
