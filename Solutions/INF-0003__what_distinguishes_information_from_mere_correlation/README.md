## What is deliberately not here

- No personal emails of named individuals.
- No ranking of people by how likely they are to believe the answer.
- No bulk-send plan.

Three figure-grounded reasons:

1. **A room does not upgrade the label.** Agreement among frames is a weighted average of influence; adding people raises the vocabulary and does not add an anchor. A list selected for receptiveness manufactures exactly the correlation that makes its agreement worthless as a verdict. Fig 2 :39 to :41 makes the distinction explicit: *"Correlation has valence."*
2. **A verifier that can reconstruct the claim cannot verify it.** Fig 10 :42. An outside frame must meet the residual, not the interiors of the derivation.
3. **Independent frames are bought, not assumed.** Fig 2 :42: *"Buy independence in proportion to the cost of being wrong."* One evidence pool wearing many names is one witness, not many.

Legally, scraping personal emails for unsolicited bulk outreach breaches GDPR and CAN-SPAM style rules in many jurisdictions. That is a practical failure on top of the structural one. The venues in `03_public_contact_points.csv` are public submission or contact routes, which are opt-in by construction.

Take `01` and `08` to the public venues in `03`. The audience that can help is the one positioned to refute the residual, not a list of people already inclined to agree. Record their verdict on stage 1 before showing any theory. A failure is more useful than an endorsement.
