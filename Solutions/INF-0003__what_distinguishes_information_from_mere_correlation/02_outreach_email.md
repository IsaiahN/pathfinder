# Outreach draft — check the shape before you decide about the definition

**Use:** one to one, or as a cover letter attached to a submission through the public venues in `03_public_contact_points.csv`. **Not** for bulk send.

**Hard precondition:** do not send until the residual in `01_figure_grounded_audit.md` §0 is stated in your own words and refuters R1 to R4 are written down. The output must survive validation or the two-stage offer collapses on contact.

---

**Subject:** One output to check: same correlation, different information

> Hi [name],
>
> I have one specific output I would like you to check against what you already know. I am not asking you to read a theory first, and I am not asking you to agree with anything. If the output does not survive, there is nothing else to look at.
>
> **The residual, in effect terms.** Two readings, X and Y, move together. The same bare co-variation is consistent with X producing Y, Y producing X, a hidden common cause producing both, or an accident of sampling. "Correlation" names the co-variation and stops. "Information" is the word we use when the co-variation does work: it discriminates, it supports an intervention, it identifies a source, or it changes a prediction at a ground. The question is what has to be added to the first to earn the second.
>
> **The output (`general` shape, `specified` per domain, not `grounded`).** There is no single-domain answer. The framework says the correlation has valence, and the valence is decided by what the question is anchored to:
>
> 1. In composition, the bond carries the information that the operands do not. A correlation records co-presence; it does not say which typed relation holds. Fig 12 :5: "A set of ingredients is not one. The bond is the rest of the information, and it is the half nothing was recording."
> 2. In verification, agreement between frames that share one evidence pool is disqualified as a verdict. It is the pool talking, not two frames confirming each other. Independence is the quantity that controls both whether agreement means anything and whether two frames reach anything new together.
> 3. In perception, a single aggregate coefficient drops the live slot signal. Reading green does not identify the source; the spectrometer, not an argument, did that.
> 4. In convention and meaning, correlation is not a defect at all. Where the question is constitutively about a shared meaning, agreement about that meaning is the product.
>
> So the distinction is: correlation is co-variation under a declared code; information is co-variation plus a typed arrangement, a source-discriminating instrument, and an anchor that does not update when the frames change their minds. Where the anchor is a shared meaning rather than the world, the last condition changes and the fourth verdict applies.
>
> **What I explicitly do not claim.** I am not claiming a single definition of information settles all four domains. I am not claiming mutual information is wrong; it is a precise and useful quantity inside its own code. I am claiming it does not decide which arrangement produced the reading, and that the missing discriminator is a real gap rather than a philosophy-of-language dispute. I am not claiming any of this is `grounded`. No measurement here settles it.
>
> **What would refute the shape.** I wrote these down before running the derivation:
> 1. An invariant mapping from operand sets and all pairwise correlations to a unique typed bond and source, validated at the ground, needing no further instrument.
> 2. Two frames drawing on the identical evidence pool shown to give independent corroboration about the world, or a shared-meaning convention shown to be corrupt when the question is about that meaning.
> 3. Per-slot information proven recoverable without loss from a single global aggregate, for every partition and scale.
> 4. A single definition shown to cover composition, verification, source discrimination and convention without discarding a distinction the field actually uses.
>
> Any one of those breaks the corresponding part of the claim and I will retract it. A one-line "this fails because..." is more useful to me than agreement. If you would rather not engage, no reply is needed.
>
> **Stage 1 question for you:** what would you count as a failure of the claim above? Please answer that before looking at the sources. Once you have given me your verdict, I will not treat any later agreement as corroboration, because by then we would share an evidence pool.
>
> **If it survives, and only then:** the sources are below. The figure text, not my summary of it, is the authority.
> - Verbatim figures with line numbers (local extract): `FRAMEWORK/reference/03-FIGURES_TEXT.md`
> - Canonical usage rule (local): `FRAMEWORK/reference/FIGURES_README_canonical.md`
> - Figures as metalaws (local): `FRAMEWORK/reference/60_FIGURES_AS_METALAWS.md`
>
> **If you want to use the machinery:** the eight-step loop is in the local `THE_FORMULA.md`. Run it on a problem you know well and can check against a ground. Write down what would refute your result *before* you run it, and state the mode of what you get. A reported failure is more useful to me than a reported success.
>
> — [name]

## Why this version is ordered this way

| the move | figure-grounded reason |
|---|---|
| Output and residual first, theory second | Fig 10 :52: *"Bring a characterised residual, never the source."* A recipient checks the output without holding the framework's interior. |
| Do not ask for agreement | Fig 2 :35: *"Alignment is triangulation, not negotiation. A mirror cannot correct you."* |
| Ask what would count as a failure before showing links | Fig 9 :27 to :28: *"describe it before you go looking, or any frame you pick will seem to fit."* |
| Record the verdict before theory is shown | Fig 2 :40: *"agreement about the world is worthless when the agreeing frames share an evidence pool"*, corroborated by Fig 8 :47: *"Independence is one quantity read twice."* |
| Pre-registered refuter | A falsifier written before the run is a design instrument. The four refuters are in §5 of `01_figure_grounded_audit.md` and were not edited after the findings. |
| Mode on every claim | `THE_FORMULA.md`: *"Mode. general · specified · grounded. State which."* |
| Four domains kept separate | Fig 9: a case in two conditions is not a harder case of either; the union is unbundled before search. |
| No single-definition claim | The SEP semantic entry records Shannon's *"informational pluralism"*: *"It is hardly to be expected that a single concept of information would satisfactorily account for the numerous possible applications of the general field."* |
| Figure quotes only, attributed as figure quotes | Every `Fig N :line` string was checked against `03-FIGURES_TEXT.md`; essay and web quotes are attributed to their sources. |
| No request for trust or prestige | *The Recursive Self-Improvement Trap*: prestige can fund an action but cannot settle it; only the ground settles it. |
| Sources after the verdict | Fig 4 :22 to :25: only methods cross up, only head starts come down. Sending the architecture first sends a recording, not a generator. |
| Offer the formula as a method to reuse | Fig 4: a method can be reapplied; a recording is one occasion. Stage 3 gives a generator, not an answer. |

## Distribution note

One to one. No bulk send, no list selected for receptiveness, no pooling of later agreement as evidence. Fig 2 :42: *"Buy independence in proportion to the cost of being wrong."* The venues in `03_public_contact_points.csv` are public and opt-in, so a recipient's decision to engage is their own.
