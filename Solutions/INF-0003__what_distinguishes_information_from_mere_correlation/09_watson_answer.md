## QUESTION

Decide, in effect terms, what distinguishes information from co-variation. Two readings move together. The same co-variation is consistent with X producing Y, Y producing X, a hidden common cause producing both, or an accident of sampling. Option A: co-variation is sufficient, so information is mutual information or covariance and the distinction collapses. Option B: co-variation is necessary but not sufficient, so information requires a typed arrangement, a source-discriminating instrument and an anchor, with the anchor condition changing when the question is about shared meaning. What each leads to: A dissolves the distinction the question asks about and is contradicted by same-reading different-source cases; B gives a checkable shape, four per-domain verdicts and refuters that can actually fire.

## VERDICT (from the figures)

The question has no single-domain answer. In composition and perception, information is the typed, priced arrangement and the source-discriminating reading, while correlation is the flat co-presence that leaves both undetermined. In verification, correlation among frames sharing an evidence pool is disqualified for a verdict, and information from agreement needs low-overlap frames ranged against an anchor that does not update. In semantic and convention matters, correlation is not a defect but the product, because the question there is constitutively about shared meaning. The shape is `general`; the four verdicts are `specified`; nothing is `grounded`. The strongest governing line is Fig 2 :39, because it refuses the false unity of the question rather than answering one side of it.

- **cost if wrong:** bounded and locatable. The packet states mode, three checkable sub-claims and refuters before the conclusion, so an error is findable. The real cost is spending the independence of outside readers by sending a recording instead of a generator, which Fig 2 :42 prices. That cost is avoidable by leading with the residual and the refuter.
- **undoable:** yes. The four domain verdicts can be revised independently without touching the others. `07_the_tether_answer.md` and `08_expert_verification_packet.md` already carry the separation. No primitive has to be withdrawn, and a sent claim can be superseded by a corrected packet.
- **reversed by:** a discriminator and an anchor, not agreement. R1 to R4 in `01` §5 are the stated reversers: a ground-validated invariant from correlations to bond and source; two shared-pool frames shown to corroborate, or a convention shown corrupt about its own meaning; per-slot information recovered losslessly from one aggregate; or one definition shown to cover all four domains without discarding a real distinction.
