## Independent domain census

I redid the census before relying on the primary's conclusions, using the WATSON order: R by arity, symmetry and scale; one line per domain with actors / currency / anchor; partition or union; order by dependency. I started from Fig 2 (valence and anchor) and Fig 8 (independence read twice), whereas the primary started from Fig 12 (the bond). Different starting figures, so the routes are not the same, though the evidence pool is.

- **R (arity / symmetry / scale).** The residual is the gap between observed co-variation and the work we call information. Arity: binary for the observation, three or more to separate a common cause from direct production, and unary for the refusal case (negation joins nothing). Symmetry: covariance and mutual information pass the swap test for `+`; the ordered bonds and negation do not. Scale: the framework's residual is per slot and recursive; a correlation coefficient is one aggregate. **Union:** the question is a bundle of conditions, not one hard question. Fig 9's bundle rule applies.
- **Domain A, arrangement.** Actors: operands, bonds, molecule. Currency: bits priced by choice of relation. Anchor: the code plus settlement. Partition question: which bond holds.
- **Domain B, evidence.** Actors: frames, instruments, pools. Currency: residual and agreement. Anchor: the ground, which does not update. Union or partition question: one pool or two independent witnesses.
- **Domain C, source.** Actors: slots, reading, hidden source, instrument. Currency: per-slot live mass. Anchor: the ground through a discriminator. Partition question: which source produced the reading.
- **Domain D, meaning.** Actors: agents, community, shared meaning. Currency: conventions. Anchor: the shared meaning itself. Partition question: world agreement or meaning agreement.

Order by dependency: perceive slots, name bond and source and anchor, set the objective as a comparison, build the discriminator, settle and promote. This matches the primary's five links. My census has the same four domains and the same union reading, reached from Fig 2 and Fig 8 rather than Fig 12 and the Operators table. Low overlap between routes within one evidence pool: the agreement is weak but not circular in the trivial sense. It is still one pool.

## Round trip gap R_T

The Symbols table defines R_T as the gap between what was sent up and what came back down, `gap(x, (T_E ∘ T_A)(x))`, and Fig 4 :30 requires it to be measured rather than assumed.

1. **Sent up (x):** the verbatim figure set, chiefly Fig 12 :5 and :46 (the bond carries the information), Fig 2 :39 to :41 (valence by anchor), Fig 8 :47 (independence read twice), Fig 1 :19 (per slot).
2. **Abstraction (T_A):** the shorthand actually carried in `07` and `08`, namely "correlation is co-variation under a declared code; information adds a typed arrangement, a discriminator and an anchor".
3. **Concretisation (T_E):** a candidate one-definition answer, for example "information is mutual information", or "information is true justified semantic content".

**R_T is nonzero.** Three things the shorthand holds do not survive the trip back down:

- the **union partition**, which a one-definition answer discards;
- the **anchor condition**, which flips correlation from disqualified to productive between D2 and D4;
- the **per-slot indexing**, which a single aggregate erases.

One thing survives cleanly: co-variation alone does not determine the typed relation, the source, or whether agreement is admissible. That is the residue worth sending. R_T was **not** measured numerically, because no ground reading exists; it is recorded qualitatively as the enumerated set of dropped constraints. Treating "the formula is a condensed version of the metalaws" as identity would set R_T to zero and would be the dead-reckoning error Fig 4 :30 names.
