## 1. A shape is a constraint, not a filler

The figures README states the limit: the framework returns *"what a missing thing would have to look like (**a shape, not a name**)."* Fig 9 :32: *"you get a kind of thing, never the answer itself."* Fig 13: *"It can say the answer is out of reach with the library as it stands... It cannot say which primitive is missing, because naming it would mean already holding it."*

From *The Library of Babel*: *"You can cut your search short if the set of books returned is small and descriptive enough that it provides a general shape of the answer that satisfies your question."*

So for this question a **shape** is the narrowed form of the distinction, statable before choosing which theory of information is correct. It constrains. It does not fill. The shape derived in `01` is:

> correlation is co-variation under a declared code; information is co-variation plus a typed arrangement, a source-discriminating reading, and an anchor that does not update when frames change their minds. Where the anchor is a shared meaning rather than the world, the verdict changes.

That is a shape of an answer. "Information is mutual information" is a name, and a name the framework does not license on its own authority.

## 2. How the shape is obtained

- *Library of Babel*: use the vocabulary already held that is closest to the thing, look for recurring new terms that overlap or correlate with it, recurse. *"With each new term gained, you narrow the space by definition and deepen your vocabulary."* Applied here: start from "co-variation", add "bond", "source", "anchor", "slot", and the space narrows until the four domain verdicts are all that is left.
- *Library of Babel* on bundles: *"Perhaps your problem was a bundle or conflation of several problems masquerading as one?"* This is the essay-level precedent for the four-domain partition in `01` §1.3. The question "what distinguishes information from correlation" is a bundle, and searching it as one question is why it stays unresolved.
- Fig 6 :30: *"The description travels. Fails by being sharpened into terms only we can read. Cast it in effect terms."* Fig 9 :30 to :31: *"your words are borrowed too, so describe the effect rather than the cause."* This is why the residual was stated as "same co-variation, different arrangement" rather than as a dispute between Shannon, Pearl and Floridi.
- *Decoding Tacit Knowledge*: *"When contact isn't possible, the best you can send is a plain description of what you'd expect to see, because anyone can check that against the world."* The outreach packet leads with exactly that (what would you expect to see if the shape claim were true).
- *Library of Babel* on stopping: the answer is reached when *"the finish line will also be the starting place"*; the paradox is that by then you have *"composed the answer through the process of locating it."*

## 3. Contact resolves the distinction

*How Intelligence Actually Works* supplies the most direct essay-level statement of the answer to this exact question:

> *"the answer doesn't exist until the question is contacted."*
> *"Superposition isn't ignorance: it's the state where the distinction hasn't been made yet."*
> *"Measurement doesn't reveal the value. It creates the value by collapsing the space of values."*
> *"Contact is Distinction Resolver"*

Correlation is the state before the distinction is made. Information is what is present after contact resolves it. The same essay gives the artifact case: a video frame and a photograph *"look the same to the untrained eye"*; *"The frame and the photograph are indistinguishable as artifacts, and they carry different warrants. And nothing in the image records which."* That is F3 in `01` (same reading, different source) stated as a general principle.

*The Hard Problem is About Source* gives the instrument version: *"If several paths create the same behavior, then behavior is a projection with lossy information"*; a color instrument *"cannot comment on the source"*; *"The answer is unreached until a better instrument exists."* Unreached, not unknowable, exactly as Fig 8 :39 says.

## 4. What licenses a name

Only two things, both outside the shape itself:

1. **The ground settles it.** Formula step 5: *"the ground settles it. Verified only by what pays. A gate is not the ground."* The README: the framework *"stops at the limit of what the available instruments can distinguish, and records the lineage so the stop is honest."*
2. **A provenanced import from a frame whose closure differs** (Fig 6 :23, Fig 8 :50 to :52), with a source gate and a shadow test. Here those frames are Shannon's coding theory, causal modelling, and semantic information.

*They're Scanning Books* sharpens what an import is: *"The real import is not a new term in the existing space. It is a new dimension of the space. Importing a term extends what can be said; importing an axis extends what can be perceived at all."* Choosing mutual information as the definition imports a term. Asking which arrangement produced the reading imports an axis.

The same essay warns against pretending the choice is neutral: *"A minimum viable set of six terms is a human's careful guess at reality's compression axes; another sensorium's minimum set need not be those six."* And: *"Declaring the primitives does not make them neutral. It makes the choice visible."* So the four domains in `01` are a visible guess recorded as such, not nature's own list.

## 5. The valence of agreement is an essay-level theme, not only a figure line

*Decoding Tacit Knowledge* says it in one sentence: *"Shared pretext is good for one thing and ruins another. Agreement is the whole point when you're settling what words mean. It's worthless for settling what's true when both sides learned from the same sources."* This is Fig 2 :40 and :41 in prose, and it is the reason the outreach asks for a refuter before a theory.

*How a Society of Agents Would Work* makes the counting consequence explicit: *"Ten copies watching the same thing are one opinion said ten times."* And: *"the group's agreement is never the verdict."* The same essay's Kish citation (N correlated samples count as N / (1 + (N-1)ρ) independent ones) is the arithmetic behind Fig 2's effective-sample-size warning. That is why `03_public_contact_points.csv` lists venues and not a list of people likely to agree.

*The Recursive Self-Improvement Trap*: *"You may all agree and be wrong."* And: *"the output is only as good as the validator. The agent, or the tool, or the frame, or the theory, cannot validate itself."*

## 6. Correction to my own §1 and §4

I initially wrote this file as if *"shape, not a name"* forbade any definition of information. That overstates the rule, and the correction is recorded here rather than silently erased.

- The README's phrase governs a **missing** primitive, one not held in Γ. The present question asks about a relation among arrangements, instruments and anchors that are already in play; it does not ask for a seventh primitive.
- What the framework can legitimately give here is the structural shape in §1, because it is a constraint on any candidate answer, not a name for a missing atom.
- What it cannot give is the name of the uniquely correct world-definition. Selecting Shannon, causal-information, or semantic information is a **binding from a world frame**, provenanced as such, not a derivation.
- I also overstated *"correlation is disqualified"* in an earlier draft of `01`. The corrected claim is anchor-conditional: disqualifying in the verification domain (D2), productive in the convention domain (D4), per Fig 2 :39 to :41. Both readings are retained in the audit's retraction table so neither is quietly lost.

## 7. Two consequences for outreach

**Prestige is not action.** From *The Recursive Self-Improvement Trap*: *"Prestige must never be allowed to validate an action directly... Prestige can fund an action. It can give an action a budget to be tried. But it cannot settle an action. Only the ground can settle it. The Ground Maintainer must be blind to prestige."* A list of famous information theorists selected to agree with the shape is the named collapse, not corroboration. The legitimate ask is a budget to try the refuter.

**This audit is a simulation, not a ground reading.** Same essay: *"LLMs can quickly simulate an approximate answer/output with the formula found in the architecture post... However, the truly testable output would need to be derived from the real architecture or working the formula yourself."* And *Arrival of the Fittest*: because it is a metatheory, *"it cannot be validated, nor can it validate itself"*, and a prompt-based demo *"could never be verified if the solutions presented were not contrived to fit the formula."* So `01` is a simulated derivation checked for internal consistency against the figures. It is not a test of the claim and must be labelled as such. It cannot settle anything it reports on.
