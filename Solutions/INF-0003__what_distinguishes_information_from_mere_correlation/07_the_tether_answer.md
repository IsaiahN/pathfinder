# The Tether's answer: what distinguishes information from mere correlation

**Mode: `general` on the shape, `specified` on the four domain verdicts, not `grounded`.** This is a shape and a partition, not a result. Simulated derivation checked against the figures (`05_what_shape_means.md` §7), not a ground reading.

## 1. The residual it answers

Two readings move together. The same co-variation is consistent with X producing Y, Y producing X, a hidden common cause producing both, or an accident of sampling. "Correlation" names the co-variation and stops there. The residual is what must be added before the co-variation is correctly called information. The answer is not one definition. The question is a **union of four conditions**, and a case in two conditions is not a harder case of either; each domain gets its own loop and its own verdict.

## 2. The shape, by domain (verdicts kept separate, never averaged)

**D1, composition.** Correlation is the flat conjunction of co-presence. Information is the typed, priced arrangement. Fig 12 :5: "A set of ingredients is not one. The bond is the rest of the information, and it is the half nothing was recording." Fig 12 :46: "A bond is the typed relation between two operands. There are seven, they are not interchangeable, and which one holds is not recoverable from the operands." Correlation is symmetric under swap; the bonds that do discriminating work can be ordered. Status: `triangulated` (Fig 12, Operators table, and the independent partial-information decomposition).

**D2, verification.** Correlation among frames that share an evidence pool is disqualified for a verdict. Fig 2 :39: "Correlation has valence." Fig 2 :40: "disqualifying for a verdict: agreement about the world is worthless when the agreeing frames share an evidence pool". Information from agreement requires low-overlap frames ranged separately against an anchor that does not update. Status: `triangulated` (Fig 2, Fig 8, Fig 13, SEP Causal Models).

**D3, perception and source.** A global coefficient drops the live slot signal, and a reading does not identify its source. Fig 1 :19: "The residual is indexed by object slot, not aggregated." The discriminator is an instrument, not an argument. Status: `triangulated` (Fig 1, Fig 12, the metamerism essay).

**D4, semantic and convention.** Where the question is constitutively about a shared meaning, correlation is not a defect. Fig 2 :41: "efficient for a convention: agreement about a shared meaning is the product, not a corruption of it". Status: `triangulated` (Fig 2, SEP semantic and SEP information).

**The one-line synthesis.** Correlation is co-variation under a declared code; information is co-variation plus a typed arrangement, a source-discriminating instrument and an anchor that does not move with the frames. Where the anchor is a shared meaning rather than the world, the last condition changes and correlation becomes the product. This is a `general` shape with four `specified` verdicts. It is not a ground reading.

## 3. What it explicitly does not say

- Not that mutual information is wrong. It is a precise quantity inside its own code. The claim is about what it leaves undetermined.
- Not that there is one true definition of information across physics, biology, cognition and society. The SEP semantic entry records Shannon's own informational pluralism on this point.
- Not that a reading's source is unknowable. It is unreached until a discriminator exists. Fig 8 :39: "So a search that finds nothing is never a proof of absence."
- Not that the framework names which world-definition is correct. It returns a shape, not a name; any name is a provenanced import.
- Not `grounded`. No measurement in this run settles the partition.

## 4. What would settle it — the ground

A discriminator and an anchor, not a further argument. The concrete tests are the four refuters in `01` §5:

1. **R1:** an invariant from operand sets and pairwise correlations to a unique typed bond and source, validated at the ground.
2. **R2:** two frames with identical evidence shown to give independent corroboration about the world, or a convention shown corrupt when the question is about that meaning.
3. **R3:** per-slot information proven recoverable without loss from one global aggregate at every scale.
4. **R4:** one definition shown to cover all four domains without discarding a distinction the field uses.

Additional ground-facing questions: does an instrument exist that separates redundancy from synergy behaviourally, not just formally? Does a source-discriminating instrument exist for the reading in question? Each is an instrument question, answerable only by an instrument.

## 5. The honest stop

**Unreached, not unreachable.** The four-domain partition is a visible guess at the joints of the question, not nature's own list. *They're Scanning Books*: "Declaring the primitives does not make them neutral. It makes the choice visible." The answer stops at the limit of what the available instruments distinguish, and records the lineage so the stop is checkable. Indiscernibility is not identity. A simulated derivation checked for internal consistency against the figures cannot settle what it reports on; the truly testable output would have to be derived from the real architecture or by working the formula yourself.

## 6. One-paragraph version for outreach

> The Tether does not offer a new definition of information and does not claim a final one. It says the question is a bundle of four, not one. In composition, correlation is the flat co-presence of two operands while information is the typed bond between them, which the operands do not carry. In verification, agreement between frames sharing one evidence pool is disqualified as a verdict; a mirror cannot correct you. In perception, a single coefficient erases the live slot signal and a reading cannot name its source until a discriminator exists. In matters of shared meaning, correlation is not a defect at all: where the question is about the meaning, agreement is the product. What the answer offers is that shape, four falsifiers written in advance, and the instruments that would close each domain. The ground, not agreement, settles it.
