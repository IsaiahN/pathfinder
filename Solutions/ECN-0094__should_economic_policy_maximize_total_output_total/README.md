## Restating the question as an effect

The decision is which single number, or which weighted bundle of numbers, a policy designer installs as the target that policy must increase. Each option leads somewhere different:

- **Maximize total output** installs a quantity the economic frame itself produces. The frame then scores itself with its own product.
- **Maximize total welfare** collapses many people's positions into one sum, which requires a rule for comparing and adding across people.
- **Maximize individual freedom** names a per-person condition, which does not naturally take a total form.
- **Some combination** bundles three conditions that overlap, so it needs both a bond (how they join) and an unbundling (which overlap gets its own loop).

An answer to this question would bind an objective term, state the ground that settles it, and name what would refute it. The derivation below does exactly that and stops at a shape rather than a name.

## What is deliberately not here

- No personal emails of named individuals.
- No ranking of people by how likely they are to agree with the shape claim.
- No bulk-send plan.

Three reasons, in the framework's own words:

1. **A room does not upgrade the label.** Seat Map :283: *"A room does not upgrade the label. A room reaching a conclusion without the ground is agreement, and agreement among frames is a weighted average of influence."* Adding expert voices raises the vocabulary and does not add an anchor.
2. **Selecting for receptiveness manufactures the disqualifying correlation.** Seat Map :163: *"High correlation is disqualifying for a verdict and efficient for a convention."* Fig 2 :40: *"disqualifying for a verdict: agreement about the world is worthless when the agreeing frames share an evidence pool".*
3. **Asking for trust before checking spends independence.** Fig 10 :42: *"A verifier that can reconstruct the claim cannot verify it."* The recipient sees the residual and its refuter first, and the framework only if curious.

Legally: unsolicited bulk email breaches GDPR and CAN-SPAM style rules in many jurisdictions. Public opt-in venues avoid that failure as well as the structural one.
