## DOMAIN CENSUS

R by arity, symmetry and scale, then one line per domain with actors, currency and anchor, partition or union, and order by dependency.

- **R (the residual).** A policy designer must install one target. Arity: three operands plus an unnamed combination. Symmetry: the operands are not interchangeable, since output is a system product, welfare is an aggregate across people and freedom is a per-slot condition. Scale: two options are totals, one is individual. Fig 9 :22 names the describing predicates: *"arity · symmetry · scale"*. Fig 9 :55: *"A bundled problem is usually a union of overlapping conditions, not a list of separate ones."*
- **Domain 1, policy design.** Actors: planner, instruments, objective function. Currency: the score maximized. Anchor: outcomes for people, which do not move because a frame relabels them. Type: partition boundary, what may be the metric. Verdict: `specified`, triangulated (Fig 1 and Fig 6, different quantities).
- **Domain 2, measurement.** Actors: statistical offices, survey designers, index builders. Currency: metrics. Anchor: the measured thing, while the metric stays an instrument. Type: partition boundary, instrument versus anchor. Verdict: `specified`, triangulated (Fig 2 and an empirical source).
- **Domain 3, social choice and aggregation.** Actors: individuals with preferences and rights. Currency: ordering and interpersonal comparison. Anchor: conditional, since no unique common preference is available under the required conditions. Type: union with overlap, because welfare and freedom conflict. Verdict: `specified`, single-source for the figure rule, corroborated by two social-choice sources.
- **Domain 4, the ethical ground.** Actors: people, their capabilities and freedoms. Currency: well-being, agency, real opportunity. Anchor: human judgment, because the question is constitutively about people. Type: ground, not a frame. Verdict: `specified`, triangulated (Fig 2 and the capability-approach source).
- **Order by dependency.** Refuse a frame-internal metric before minting (Formula step 2). Describe the residual per slot before choosing a frame (Fig 9 :27-28). Settle the objective instrument before optimizing the policy lever (Fig 3 :19, :27). Unbundle the union before assigning weights (Fig 9 :54).

## VERDICT (from the figures)

No single one of total output, total welfare and individual freedom is the licensed maximand, and "some combination" is not licensed as stated. An admissible objective must keep the per-slot signal, avoid being a quantity the scored frame produces, treat its metrics as instruments ranged against human judgment about people's lives, and unbundle the welfare/freedom overlap before assigning weights. The framework returns the shape and the dependency order, not the name of the metric. Five refuters were written before the conclusion in `01_figure_grounded_audit.md` section 5; none has fired. The result is `specified` on the constraints and `general` on the shape, and it is not a verdict that settles the normative question.

- **cost if wrong:** bounded and locatable. The packet states its mode, writes refuters before the conclusion and records the lineage, so an error is findable. The real cost is spending the independence of outside readers by sending a conclusion instead of a residual and a refuter. Fig 2 :42 prices that: "Buy independence in proportion to the cost of being wrong." A second cost is misreading Fig 1 :27 as a ban on output, which would discard a genuinely useful instrument. Both costs are avoidable by leading with the shape and its refuters and by keeping the narrowing recorded.
- **undoable:** yes. The open slot can be filled by a ground-settled instrument without touching the four constraints, and the weighting can be revised once the union is unbundled. No primitive has to be withdrawn. A sent claim can be superseded by a corrected packet.
- **reversed by:** a ground reading, not agreement. A distribution-sensitive instrument that preserves the live per-slot signal would fire refuter 1. A metric shown external to the frame producing it and pinned under push would fire refuter 2. A demonstrated partition of the three objectives would fire refuters 3 and 5. A grounded aggregation rule admitted by all three domains with provenanced weights would fire refuter 4. Until one of these fires, the verdict stands as `candidate`.
