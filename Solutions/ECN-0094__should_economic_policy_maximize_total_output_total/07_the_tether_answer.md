# The Tether's answer to ECN-0094

**Mode: `specified` on the four constraints, `general` on the shape. Not `grounded`.** This is a shape and a route, not a result. Simulated derivation checked against the figures (`05_what_shape_means.md` section 6), not a ground reading.

## 1. The residual it answers

A policy designer must install one target: total output, total welfare, individual freedom, or some combination. The four options are not yet four answers to one well-posed question, because they live in different domains with different currencies: output is a system product, welfare is an aggregate across people, freedom is a per-person condition, and the combination is a bond that has not been named. Fig 9 :55: **"A bundled problem is usually a union of overlapping conditions, not a list of separate ones."**

## 2. The shape

1. **No frame-internal quantity may be the sole objective.** Fig 1 :27: **"They are frame-internal. A frame cannot score itself with a quantity it also produces."** Output is produced by the economy it would score. It can remain an instrument; it cannot be the final judge. `specified`.
2. **No total may discard the per-slot signal.** Fig 1 :19: **"The residual is indexed by object slot, not aggregated."** Fig 1 :22: **"Aggregating across slots is how a live signal disappears into an average."** A total can be flat while one group is badly off. `specified`.
3. **The anchor for a question about people is human judgment about people's lives.** Fig 2 :36-37: **"Human judgment is the right anchor for is this a good answer for a person, because the question is about people."** Fig 2 :37-38: *"It is only an instrument for is this code correct, and instruments can be wrong on axes they do not measure."* So the three candidates are instruments ranged against an anchor, not substitutes for it. `specified`.
4. **"Some combination" is a union to be unbundled, not a weight vector to be assigned.** Fig 9 :54: **"And when the split lands on a union rather than a partition, unbundle before you search again."** The welfare/freedom overlap is a compound with its own loop (Fig 9 :55-58). A weighted sum without provenance is a flat formula that records ingredients and not arrangement (Fig 12 :14-17) and a convention that decides an outcome (Fig 10 :47). `specified`.
5. **The framework returns a shape, not a name.** Fig 9 :32: **"you get a kind of thing, never the answer itself."** It does not select output, welfare or freedom as *the* objective, and it does not certify a unique weighting. Correction from `05_what_shape_means.md` section 5b: this is not because the three are unavailable names; it is because their standing is ungrounded and a frame cannot certify its own choice. `general`.
6. **The open slot is an instrument, not a metric.** What is owed is a per-slot discriminator that can tell different sources of the same reading apart, the way a spectrometer discriminates the source of green. Fig 11 :40: **"An improvement that does not change contact changes nothing, however much it improves."** `specified`, with no measurement claimed.

## 3. What it explicitly does not say

- Not that output is bad, or that economic growth is unimportant. The world source Roser et al. (2023) is explicit that *"Economic growth is, however, central to shaping people's overall living conditions"*; the framework only bars using a frame product as the sole judge.
- Not that averages are always wrong. The claim is scoped to the decision at hand, where the live signal is the aim (Fig 1 :19).
- Not that welfare or freedom is the true objective. All three are held terms with ungrounded standing; the framework does not pick.
- Not that the weighted combination is forbidden. It is **not yet a term** until a typed bond, provenance for the weights and an unbundling of the overlap are supplied.
- Not `grounded`. No measurement settles which metric best serves as instrument in a given polity.
- Not a theory of everything. No final room: the objective stays open to revision as the instrument improves (Fig 7, Fig 13).

## 4. What would settle it : the ground

A source-discriminating instrument, not a further argument. From *The Hard Problem is About Source*: an instrument that reads green *"cannot comment on the source"*; the spectrometer resolved it. Here:

- Does a given metric preserve the per-slot signal, or does it hide a badly off group behind a flat total? A distribution-sensitive reading that changes the decision would fire refuter 1.
- Is the metric external to the frame that produces it and pinned when that frame is pushed (Fig 2 :29 anchor test)? If yes, refuter 2 fires.
- Do output, welfare and freedom form a partition with no overlapping region? A demonstrated partition would fire refuter 3 and allow separate maximization.
- Is there a grounded social-welfare rule that all three domains admit, with grounded rather than authored weights? If yes, refuters 4 and 5 fire and the shape narrows.
- Standing falsifiers from `01` section 5 are reproduced in `08`.

## 5. The honest stop

**Unreached, not unreachable.** The claim stops where the instruments stop. Fig 8 :39: **"a search that finds nothing is never a proof of absence."** The literature says a richer informational basis is needed for social decisions (List 2022) and that resources are means rather than ends (Robeyns 2025); neither supplies the ground reading for a particular polity. The record of the route is what makes the stop checkable. *"Indiscernibility is not identity"* (*How Intelligence Actually Works*): a shape that fits every current reading is still a shape, not the answer.

## 6. One-paragraph version for outreach

> The Tether does not pick output, welfare or freedom as the objective of economic policy, and it does not certify a weighting. It says that an admissible objective must have four properties: it must not be a quantity the economic frame produces and then scores itself with; it must not collapse the per-slot signal into a total; it must rank its metrics as instruments against human judgment about people's lives, which is the legitimate anchor for a question about people; and any combination must be unbundled before weights are assigned, because welfare and freedom overlap and the overlap needs its own loop. What the framework offers is that shape, five falsifiers written in advance, and the description of the instrument that would fill the open slot. The slot stays open until a ground settles it. This is a simulated derivation checked against the figures, not a ground reading.
