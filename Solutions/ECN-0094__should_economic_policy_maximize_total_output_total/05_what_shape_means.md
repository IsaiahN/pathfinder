## 1. A shape is a constraint, not a filler

The figures README states the limit: the framework reports *"what a missing thing would have to look like (a shape, not a name)"*. Fig 9 :32: *"you get a kind of thing, never the answer itself."* Fig 13: *"It can say the answer is out of reach with the library as it stands... It cannot say which primitive is missing."*

From *The Library of Babel*: *"This means that you can cut your search short if the set of books returned is small and descriptive enough that it provides a general shape of the answer that satisfies your question."*

For this question a **shape** is the form any admissible policy objective must take before we can say which metric fills it. *"MAXIMIZE (???)"* is a shape. *"MAXIMIZE GDP"* is a name. The four constraints in `01` F1 to F6 are the shape; choosing output, welfare or freedom is the name the framework withholds.

## 2. How the shape is obtained

- *Library of Babel*: use the vocabulary you already have that is closest to the thing, look for recurring new terms that overlap with it, recurse. *"With each new term gained, you are able to narrow the space by definition and deepen your vocabulary."*
- The same essay names the failure this question is prone to: *"Perhaps your problem was a bundle or conflation of several problems masquerading as one? You will need to search all of those problems individually to find their answers and then combine them in a satisfactory way."* That is Fig 9 :54 in narrative form, and it is why "some combination" is not an answer but an instruction to unbundle.
- Fig 6 :30: *"The description travels. Fails by being sharpened into terms only we can read. Cast it in effect terms."* Fig 9 :30-31: *"your words are borrowed too, so describe the effect rather than the cause."*
- *Decoding Tacit Knowledge*: *"When contact isn't possible, the best you can send is a plain description of what you'd expect to see, because anyone can check that against the world."* The outreach email in `02` is built on this.

## 3. What licenses a name

Only two things, both outside the shape:

1. **The ground settles it.** Formula step 5: *"the ground settles it. Verified only by what pays. A gate is not the ground."* A metric becomes the objective only when an instrument pays for it.
2. **A provenanced import from a frame whose closure differs** (Fig 6 :23, Fig 8 :50-52), with a source gate and a shadow test. Importing a welfare index from another field is legitimate when its provenance and the gap it explains are recorded.

*They're Scanning Books* sharpens the limit: *"A minimum viable set of six terms is a human's careful guess at reality's compression axes; another sensorium's minimum set need not be those six."* And: *"Declaring the primitives does not make them neutral. It makes the choice visible."* Declaring "we maximize output" makes a choice visible; it does not make it grounded.

## 4. Why choosing one objective from a reading is the specific forbidden error

*The Hard Problem is About Source* gives the mechanism. An instrument reads green and *"cannot comment on the source"*. The essay's general claim: *"If several paths create the same behavior, then behavior is a projection with lossy information"*. And: *"An instrument that can't distinguish these cases demonstrates its own inadequacy"*. It was the spectrometer, not an argument, that resolved the case.

Applied here: a rise in measured welfare can be produced by more resources, by better conversion conditions, or by changed expectations. One reading does not determine the source. Installing the reading as the objective names a source from a projection, which is the metamerism error. What is owed is a better instrument, not a name.

*The Recursive Self-Improvement Trap*: *"the output is only as good as the validator. The agent, or the tool, or the frame, or the theory, cannot validate itself."* A metric that scores the frame producing it fails this condition, which is Fig 1 :27 in another vocabulary.

## 5. Q : What licenses a particular metric for the slot?

**Nothing in the framework.** The framework returns a shape. A particular metric is licensed only by the ground settling it, or by a provenanced import with a shadow test. Until one of those, the metric is the searcher's own contribution and must be recorded as such. The open slot in this solution is exactly that: a per-slot instrument that can discriminate sources, for which no measurement is claimed here.

## 5b. Correction to my own sections 3 and 4

I overstated "shape, not a name". The README phrase is *"what a **missing** thing would have to look like (a shape, not a name)."* Its subject is a primitive not held in the library. Output, welfare and freedom are **already held terms** in economics. Withholding them is not the same as being unable to name a missing primitive.

What is still correct: the framework cannot certify a unique choice among three held objectives without a ground reading, because Fig 9 :32 returns a kind of thing and because Fig 1 :27 disqualifies a metric the frame produces. What was wrong in my earlier framing was treating the three as unavailable names. They are available names with ungrounded standing. Both statements are recorded here so neither is quietly lost.

The metamerism point in section 4 is **not** retracted: a reading still does not determine its source. The reason is the missing discriminator, not a ban on naming held terms.

## 6. Two consequences for outreach

**Prestige is not action.** From *The Recursive Self-Improvement Trap*: *"Prestige must never be allowed to validate an action directly... Prestige can fund an action. It can give an action a budget to be tried. But it cannot settle an action. Only the ground can settle it."* Collecting prestigious economists to agree with the shape is the named collapse. The legitimate ask is a budget to try it and a ground that can refuse it.

**My own audit is a simulation, not a ground reading.** Same essay: *"LLMs can quickly simulate an approximate answer/output with the formula found in the architecture post... the truly testable output would need to be derived from the real architecture or working the formula yourself."* The architecture essay adds that outputs *"could never be verified if the solutions presented were not contrived to fit the formula."* So `01_figure_grounded_audit.md` is a simulated derivation checked for internal consistency against the figures. It is **not** a test of the question and must be labelled as such. It cannot settle anything it reports on.
