## Round trip gap R_T

The Symbols table defines R_T as `gap(x, (T_E ∘ T_A)(x))`, the gap between what was sent up and what came back down. Fig 4 :30 requires it to be measured rather than assumed. I ran the round trip on the condensation this folder actually uses.

1. **Sent up (x):** the verbatim figure set, chiefly Fig 1 :19, :22, :27; Fig 2 :36-37; Fig 3 :27; Fig 9 :54; Fig 10 :47.
2. **Abstraction (T_A):** the four-constraint shorthand in `07_the_tether_answer.md`, namely no frame-produced sole metric, no loss of the per-slot signal, metrics as instruments against human judgment, and unbundle the union before weighting.
3. **Concretisation (T_E):** a concrete policy objective of the form "maximize 40 percent GDP growth, 40 percent aggregate well-being and 20 percent freedom index".

**R_T is nonzero.** Four things the shorthand holds do not survive the trip back down:

- the **per-slot indexing** becomes a single weighted number, so the live slot the shorthand protects is exactly what the concretisation erases (Fig 1 :19, :22);
- the **anchor** becomes an index, so a metric that was an instrument is promoted to the thing that decides outcomes (Fig 2 :36-37, Fig 10 :47);
- the **unbundling** becomes a weight vector, so the welfare/freedom overlap is left standing with no loop of its own (Fig 9 :54-55);
- the **open instrument slot** becomes a chosen off-the-shelf metric, so the source-discriminating requirement is silently met by an unprovenanced import (Fig 6 :12-13).

One thing survives cleanly: a frame must not score itself with its own product, so a weighted index whose inputs are all produced by the economy does not satisfy the shape. That is the residue worth sending. R_T was **not** measured numerically, because no ground reading exists; it is recorded qualitatively as the enumerated set of dropped constraints. Treating the formula as identical to the figure set would set R_T to zero and would be the dead-reckoning error Fig 4 :30 names.
