Scale: instrument scale in, habitat and source scale out, with a lossy transform and a nonzero round-trip gap. Fig 9 :55, "A bundled problem is usually a union of overlapping conditions, not a list of separate ones." The conditions are gas presence, maintained disequilibrium, no abiotic source in the enumerated habitat, a source-discriminating instrument, and a non-updating anchor.

- **Domain 3, habitat and source.** Actors: organisms, volcanism, impacts, stellar UV and flares, possible moons. Currency: production and consumption fluxes. Anchor: the biosphere, unreachable at this budget for exoplanets. Question type: ground link, union with D2.
- **Domain 4, verification and community.** Actors: researchers, reviewers, journals. Currency: agreement and residuals. Anchor: the ground, never agreement. Question type: verification link. In this simulated run all four domains draw on one evidence pool, so agreement among them is one pool talking.
- **Order by dependency.** Perception first (can bands be read), then vocabulary (terms for gas, bond and source), then objective (a reading with no abiotic source would count as progress), then planning. The break is at vocabulary to objective: source has no direct instrument, so the objective is stated in effect terms only.

## VERDICT (from the figures)

The shape survives; the literal list does not. A gas combination is a molecule, so the maintained bond carries the evidential information that the ingredient list lacks. That bond is an ordered threshold and a directed gradient, not a symmetric list. No standing gas list is sufficient, because a gas's valence is relative to the enumerated habitat. A spectrum does not determine its source, so a source-discriminating instrument and a non-updating anchor are owed. Candidate pairs, O2 plus CH4, CH4 plus CO2, O2 plus N2 with liquid water, are provenanced bindings from planetary science, not figure outputs. The literal answer is a `gap` of the unreachable-at-budget kind, reported as a verdict and not as silence. This is `specified` on the per-domain claims, `general` on the structural shape, not `grounded`.

- **cost if wrong:** bounded and locatable. The packet states mode, three checkable sub-claims and their refuters before the conclusion, so an error is findable rather than invisible. The real cost is spending an outside reader's independence by sending a gas-pair slogan instead of a characterised residual, which Fig 2 :42 prices, "Buy independence in proportion to the cost of being wrong." That cost is avoidable by leading with the shape and its refuter.
- **undoable:** yes. The frame-specific bindings are removable without touching the derivation; `07_the_tether_answer.md` and `08_expert_verification_packet.md` already remove them. A sent claim can be superseded by the corrected packet, and no primitive has to be withdrawn. The `gap` stays open rather than being filled with a guessed pair.
- **reversed by:** a ground reading, not agreement. A source-discriminating instrument at achievable resolution that assigns bands to single bodies and processes would close the instrument gap; a fully enumerated habitat with no abiotic source maintaining the pair would move a specific verdict toward `grounded`; a figure re-census making conjunction the correct bond would break C1; a figure line licensing a standing list would break C2; and a measurement showing a single gas uniquely determining source in more than one habitat would break the no-standing-list verdict. Until one of these fires, the verdict stands as `candidate`.
