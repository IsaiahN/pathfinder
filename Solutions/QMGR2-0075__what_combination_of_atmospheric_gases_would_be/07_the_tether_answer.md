# The Tether's answer: what gas combination would be persuasive evidence of life?

**Mode: `specified` on the per-domain structural verdicts, `general` on the framework-level shape, not `grounded`.** Simulated derivation checked for internal consistency against the figures (`05_what_shape_means.md` section 6), not a ground reading. No atmosphere was measured.

## 1. The residual it answers

A disk-averaged spectrum is a reading, not a source. The same band pattern can come from life, photochemistry, geology, an impact, or two unresolved bodies blended together. The residual R is that underdetermination, indexed per gas and per candidate source (Fig 1 :19: "The residual is indexed by object slot, not aggregated"). The question's two ordinary terms are re-grounded before answering: a combination is a bonded arrangement (Fig 12 :5), and evidence is settled by an anchor, not by persuasion (Fig 2 :29).

## 2. The shape, domain by domain (verdicts kept separate, never averaged)

**D1, spectroscopy.** We can read band depths, but "instruments can be wrong on axes they do not measure" (Fig 2 :37). A spectrum does not tell us whether O2 and CH4 share one atmosphere. Verdict: `specified`, corroborated by Rein et al. 2014 (triangulated with metamerism in color science).

**D2, atmospheric chemistry.** A gas list is a flat formula. Fig 12 :5: "A set of ingredients is not one. The bond is the rest of the information, and it is the half nothing was recording." The evidential object is a maintained departure from equilibrium, typed as comparison plus directed gradient, not symmetric conjunction (Fig 12 :87; Symbols :97). Verdict: `specified`, triangulated across Fig 12 and the Schwieterman and Krissansen-Totton reviews.

**D3, habitat and source.** No standing list of gases works. Fig 11 :51: "No standing list of good actors survives a change of residual." A large H2, CO2 and water disequilibrium can be an anti-biosignature where chemotrophic life would consume it (Wogan and Catling 2020). Contact cascades outward: star type, flares, temperature, ocean, geology, impacts, companions (Fig 11 :43, :52). Verdict: `specified`, single-source on the figure side (Fig 11) with low-overlap world corroboration from philosophy and color science.

**D4, verification.** "Persuasive" is a fact about a room, not a planet. Fig 2 :35: "Alignment is triangulation, not negotiation. A mirror cannot correct you." Agreement about a shared meaning is efficient for a convention (Fig 2 :41); agreement about the world is worthless when the frames share an evidence pool (Fig 2 :40). Verdict: `specified`, triangulated across Fig 2 and Fig 7 :35.

**The literal gas list.** No figure line returns O2 plus CH4, or CH4 plus CO2, or O2 plus N2 plus liquid water. Fig 9 :32: "you get a kind of thing, never the answer itself." Fig 13 :134: "Unreachable is a verdict here, not a silence". Verdict: `gap`, unreachable at this budget, reported as a verdict rather than as an omission.

## 3. What the answer is

1. **The combination is a bond, not a list.** O2 and CH4 should oxidize to CO2 and H2O; their persistence requires renewed flux. That maintained relation, not either band, is the candidate evidence. Fig 12 :94: "The arrangement is the substance."
2. **The bond is a threshold and a directed gradient.** It is not symmetric conjunction: which species is in excess relative to the equilibrium state matters. Sequence would also be wrong, because the gases are simultaneous, not successive.
3. **No standing gas list is sufficient.** Valence is relative to the enumerated habitat. The same gas is a benefactor to one residual and a false-positive risk to another.
4. **A source-discriminating instrument is owed.** The spectrum is a projection; several sources give the same projection. What is needed is a discriminator, not another argument. Rein et al. 2014 show a concrete case where the resolution required may be physically unavailable at foreseeable technology.
5. **Candidate pairs are bindings, not outputs.** O2 plus CH4, CH4 plus CO2, and O2 plus N2 plus liquid water are frame-specific bindings with their habitat conditions attached. They carry zero verdict weight until a ground settles the source.
6. **The anchor is not agreement.** What would make a combination persuasive is contact with a ground that does not move when opinion changes, not a vote of researchers.

## 4. What it explicitly does not say

- Not that O2 plus CH4, or any other pair, has been detected on any planet.
- Not that any gas is a biosignature independently of habitat.
- Not that the planet-moon degeneracy is resolved; it is recorded as a live strain.
- Not that a consensus of astrobiologists would settle the source. The four astrobiology papers read here are one evidence pool, one witness.
- Not `grounded`. No measurement at the source settles `left(R, phi)`.
- Not a claim that no exoplanet is inhabited. Fig 8 :39: "So a search that finds nothing is never a proof of absence."

## 5. What would settle it, the ground

A source-discriminating instrument, not a further argument. From *The Hard Problem is About Source*: an instrument that reads green "cannot comment on the source"; the spectrometer resolved it. Here:

- **Discriminator test:** can an instrument assign each absorption feature to a single body and a single process, with a stated false-positive rate, at the resolution actually achievable?
- **Context test:** with star type, temperature, water state, volcanism, flares and companions enumerated, does any abiotic source in that habitat maintain the observed departure from equilibrium? If yes, the pair loses its standing as a biosignature for that planet.
- **Temporal test:** does time-resolved monitoring distinguish a maintained flux from a blended static signal? This is an INWARD move, sharpening an instrument that already returns something (Fig 6 :32).
- **Standing falsifiers:** a figure line licensing a standing list; a single gas uniquely determining its source in more than one habitat; a re-census making conjunction the correct bond; or a measured atmosphere with no abiotic source and a non-updating anchor confirming life (which would close the gap).

## 6. The honest stop

**Unreached, not proven unreachable.** Fig 8 :39 forbids reading failure as absence. Fig 6 :34 forbids claiming a sensor for something that never registered; here a spectrum does register and fails to resolve, so this is unreachable-at-budget or instrument-limited, not underivable in principle. The six-term substrate is a visible guess, not nature's own list. The answer stops at the limit of what instruments distinguish and records the lineage so the stop is checkable. The literal gas pair stays open.

## 7. One-paragraph version for outreach

> A gas list is not evidence. The Tether says the evidence lives in the bond: a maintained departure from chemical equilibrium whose persistence needs a renewed flux. Under the framework, three things follow. A combination with no bond is a flat formula, so O2 plus CH4 carries no more information than two band depths until the maintained relation between them is specified. The relation is a threshold and a directed gradient, not a symmetric list, and no standing list of gases works across habitats, because the same gas changes meaning with its environment. A large prebiotic H2, CO2 and water disequilibrium can even be an anti-biosignature. The framework refuses to name the winning pair, because it returns a shape, not a name. O2 plus CH4, CH4 plus CO2 and O2 plus N2 with liquid water are bindings from planetary science, not derivations, and the ground that would choose among them is out of reach at this budget. What it offers is the shape any persuasive claim must have, the source-discriminating instrument that is owed, and four refuters written in advance. The gap is reported as a verdict, not as silence.
