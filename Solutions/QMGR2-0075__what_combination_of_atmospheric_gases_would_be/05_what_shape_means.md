## 1. A shape is a constraint, not a filler

The canonical usage rule says the framework gives *"what a missing thing would have to look like (a shape, not a name)"*. Fig 9 :32: "you get a kind of thing, never the answer itself". Fig 13 :133: "what it returns is a shape rather than a name."

From *The Library of Babel* (quote carried from the exemplar's canonical mapping): "You can cut your search short if the set of books returned is small and descriptive enough that it provides a general shape of the answer that satisfies your question."

For this question the shape is **not** "O2 plus CH4". It is:

> a co-occurrence of species whose persistence requires a renewed flux, whose bond is a threshold departure from equilibrium, which no abiotic source in the enumerated habitat can supply, read by an instrument that discriminates among sources, and ranged against an anchor that does not update on opinion.

That statement constrains any acceptable answer. It does not fill the gas slots. A list such as `O2 + CH4` is a name; a maintained redox disequilibrium with a source gate is a shape.

## 2. How the shape is obtained

- *Library of Babel*: use the vocabulary closest to the thing, look for recurring new terms that overlap with it, and recurse. "With each new term gained, you narrow the space and deepen your vocabulary." Here the closest vocabulary is spectral bands, equilibrium thermodynamics and flux.
- Fig 6 :30: "The description travels. Fails by being sharpened into terms only we can read. Cast it in effect terms." So describe the residual as "a reading whose source is underdetermined", not as "we need a better methane spectrometer".
- Fig 9 :31: "your words are borrowed too, so describe the effect rather than the cause." The effect is a band-depth pattern that fails to discriminate source. The cause, life, is what we are trying to infer.
- *Decoding Tacit Knowledge* (quote carried): "When contact isn't possible, the best you can send is a plain description of what you'd expect to see, because anyone can check that against the world." This is why the outreach email asks what the recipient would expect to see if the source were biological, before showing the framework.
- *Library of Babel* on stopping: the answer is reached when "the finish line will also be the starting place", because the searcher has "composed the answer through the process of locating it". For a gas question this means the list of candidate pairs is written down as bindings during the derivation, not appended afterwards as an illustration.

## 3. What licenses a name

Two things, both outside the shape itself:

1. **The ground settles it.** THE_FORMULA.md step 5: "the ground settles it. Verified only by what pays. A gate is not the ground." The canonical rule says the framework "stops at the limit of what the available instruments can distinguish, and records the lineage so the stop is honest." For an exoplanet atmosphere the source is not currently at that limit; it is beyond it.
2. **A provenanced import from a frame whose closure differs** (Fig 6 :23, Fig 8 :51), with a source gate and a shadow test. Naming O2 plus CH4 is an import from planetary science. The source gate is the Schwieterman and Krissansen-Totton papers; the shadow test is whether the pair explains a gap the single gases do not. It passes the shadow test and fails the settlement test.

*They're Scanning Books* (quote carried): "The real import is not a new term in the existing space. It is a new dimension of the space." Also: "A minimum viable set of six terms is a human's careful guess at reality's compression axes; another sensorium's minimum set need not be those six." And: "Declaring the primitives does not make them neutral. It makes the choice visible."

The six-term substrate is a visible guess. The atmospheric gas vocabulary is another frame's guess. Treating either as nature's own list is the error the framework names.

## 4. The governing essay for this question: source, not ingredients

*The Hard Problem is About Source* (quotes carried from the exemplar's canonical mapping) gives the mechanism that decides this question. Metamerism: an instrument reads green and "cannot comment on the source". One green lamp, or yellow plus blue, or RGB, or CMYK, or a scheme not yet discovered. "If several paths create the same behavior, then behavior is a projection with lossy information." And: "An instrument that can't distinguish these cases demonstrates its own inadequacy." It was the spectrometer, not an argument, that resolved it.

A transmission spectrum is the same structure. A blended planet-moon spectrum can match a single disequilibrium atmosphere. Reading O2 and CH4 band depths and naming the source "life" is the metamerism error: a source named from a projection. What is owed is a discriminator, not a name.

This is corroborated from a genuinely low-overlap field by Wikipedia "Metamerism (color)" (opened 2026-11-23): "In colorimetry, metamerism is a perceived matching of colors with different (nonmatching) spectral power distributions." The framework essay and the color-science page share no evidence pool.

## 5. ANSWER: what "shape" means for QMGR2-0075

**The framework cannot return the gas list.** It returns the constraints above. The specific list is a `gap`, and by Fig 13 :134 that is a verdict: "Unreachable is a verdict here, not a silence".

**Does naming candidate pairs commit a forbidden error?** No, with a caveat. The canonical phrase governs a *missing* primitive, one not held in the library. O2, CH4, CO2 and N2 are not among the six and are not missing primitives of the substrate; they belong to the planetary-science frame. Naming them as candidate bindings is provenance, not invention. The caveat is that the binding must be labelled a binding and must not be presented as a figure output or as a wall-moving import (Fig 6 : "IMPORT adds an atom" adds none here). See `06_isomers_and_order.md` Q1 for the parallel correction.

**What would license a name here?** A source-discriminating instrument ranging against a non-updating ground. Rein et al. 2014 argues that for the planet-moon degeneracy the required spectral resolution may be physically out of reach for the foreseeable future; that is a reason to report `unreached`, not a reason to declare absence (Fig 8 :39).

## 5b. Correction to my own reading, kept visible

My first pass treated "shape, not a name" as if it forbade any gas name. That overstated the rule. The README's phrase is *"what a missing thing would have to look like (a shape, not a name)"*: the subject is a missing primitive. The correction is recorded above in section 5 and is not silently erased here. What remains forbidden is presenting a frame-specific pair as a figure derivation, and what remains owed is an instrument that discriminates sources.

## 6. Two consequences for outreach and for this folder

**Prestige is not action.** From *The Recursive Self-Improvement Trap* (quote carried): "Prestige must never be allowed to validate an action directly... Prestige can fund an action. It can give an action a budget to be tried. But it cannot settle an action. Only the ground can settle it. The Ground Maintainer must be blind to prestige." A list of researchers who find a gas pair convincing is a room, not an anchor. The legitimate ask is a refutation, not an endorsement.

However, the truly testable output would need to be derived from the real architecture or working the formula yourself." LLM output is "simulacra".
