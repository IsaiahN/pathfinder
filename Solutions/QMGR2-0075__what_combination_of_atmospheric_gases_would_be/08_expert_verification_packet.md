## 1. The claim, in one paragraph

A gas list is not evidence of life. The evidence, if any, lives in the bond: a maintained departure from chemical equilibrium whose persistence requires a renewed flux. A combination with no bond is a flat formula; the pair O2 and CH4 carries no more information than two band depths until the maintained relation between them is specified. That relation is a threshold and a directed gradient, not a symmetric list. No standing list of gases works across habitats, because an actor's valence is relative to the residual; a large prebiotic H2, CO2 and water disequilibrium can be an anti-biosignature. A spectrum does not determine its source, so a source-discriminating instrument is owed. The framework returns a shape, not a name, and reports the literal gas list as a `gap` of the unreachable-at-this-budget kind.

## 2. Three checkable sub-claims

### C1: framework-internal, checkable by reading Figure 12

- **Statement:** the evidential object is a molecule (species plus bond), not an ingredient list, and the governing bond is comparison plus gradient rather than symmetric conjunction.
- **Source, verbatim:** Fig 12 :5, "A set of ingredients is not one. The bond is the rest of the information, and it is the half nothing was recording." Fig 12 :94, "The arrangement is the substance." Fig 12 :87 defines comparison as "a threshold relation between two quantities". Symbols table :97: "A difference that can be spent. Not merely a distribution: what makes it a gradient is directedness." The conjunction test is Fig 12 :59, "swap the operands. Does the meaning change? No."
- **Refuter:** show a pair whose evidential force is invariant under exchanging the two species and under replacing the pair with both species at equilibrium. Then the bond carries nothing and conjunction was correct.

### C2: chemistry and planetary-science facing, checkable against the literature

- **Statement:** no standing list of gases is sufficient; valence is habitat-relative, and the two famous pairs are members of one functional group, not isomers as written.
- **Source, verbatim figure lines:** Fig 11 :51, "No standing list of good actors survives a change of residual." Fig 11 :49, "An actor's valence is relative to the residual, not a property it carries." Fig 12 :124, "functional group. A substructure whose presence fixes the outcome. The group says what kind; the qualifier says which member." Fig 12 :117, "Same atoms, different bond, different substance. The difference is invisible in the formula and decisive in the behaviour."
- **World check:** Wogan and Catling 2020, "Thus, the interpretation of disequilibrium requires context. With context, disequilibrium can be used to infer dead or living worlds." Schwieterman et al. 2018: "These two gases should rapidly oxidize to CO2 and H2O, so their persistent presence suggests a continual resupply of both gases from biological sources." Krissansen-Totton et al. 2018 propose a different pair for anoxic atmospheres: "the simultaneous detection of abundant CH4 and CO2 in a habitable exoplanet's atmosphere is a potential biosignature."
- **Refuter:** either pair can be written without an atom the other requires, in every habitat (which is already true as written, so the strong isomer claim is withdrawn and the functional-group claim stands); or a single gas uniquely determines its source against a non-updating anchor in more than one habitat.

### C3: instrument facing, checkable by a spectroscopist

- **Statement:** a band-depth reading does not determine its source; a discriminator is owed, and the planet-moon degeneracy is a live counterexample at foreseeable resolution.
- **Source, verbatim figure lines:** Fig 2 :37, "instruments can be wrong on axes they do not measure." Fig 10 :42, "A verifier that can reconstruct the claim cannot verify it." Fig 8 :39, "So a search that finds nothing is never a proof of absence." Fig 6 :32, "An instrument is not built from a description. It is improved from a worse instrument already returning something."
- **World check:** Rein, Fujii and Spiegel 2014: "an observation of two species does not show that they are in fact in the atmosphere of a single object (the planet)." Their result gives a photon-noise resolution limit, so the question of whether this is unreachable-at-budget or underivable-for-this-instrument-class is recorded as a live strain.
- **Refuter:** an instrument that assigns each absorption band to a single body and a single process with a stated false-positive rate at achievable resolution; or a worked physical argument that no increase in resolution or independent context could ever discriminate the sources.

## 5. What would settle it

1. A source-discriminating instrument with a stated false-positive rate at achievable resolution.
2. A fully enumerated habitat in which no abiotic process maintains the observed departure from equilibrium.
3. Time-resolved monitoring that separates a maintained flux from a blended static signal.
4. A figure re-census that makes conjunction the correct bond, or that licenses a standing gas list. The first would break C1; the second would break C2 and the no-standing-list verdict.

## 6. What an expert can check in twenty minutes

1. Read Fig 12 :5, :87, :94, :117, :124 and Fig 11 :49, :51. Seven short passages.
2. Check C2 against the literature: are O2 plus CH4 and CH4 plus CO2 functional-group members with different habitats rather than isomers? Do you know a habitat in which one gas alone determines source?
3. Check C3 against your own instrument: is the planet-moon degeneracy breakable at the resolution you expect, and by what independent observation?
4. Name the one discriminator you would trust. If you cannot, say so; that is a result too.
5. Send the refutation. A failure is more useful than an endorsement.
