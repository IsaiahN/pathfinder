**Residual in effect terms:** A disk-averaged spectrum is a reading, not a source. Between the reading and the verdict "life" sits a gap. The question asks for a combination of gases that would be persuasive evidence, so two ordinary terms must be re-grounded before any answer is attempted. A combination is a bonded arrangement (Fig 12 :5), not an ingredient list, and evidence is a verdict settled by a non-updating anchor (Fig 2 :29), not by persuasion or agreement. What an answer would look like, written down and bound as an ordinary term so it can accrue a residual: a co-occurrence of two or more species whose persistence requires a renewed flux, which no abiotic process in the enumerated habitat can supply, read by an instrument that discriminates sources, and ranged against an anchor that does not move when the community changes its mind. That description is a shape, not a name (Fig 9 :32).

## What is deliberately not here

- No personal emails of named individuals.
- No ranking of people by how likely they are to believe the claim.
- No bulk-send plan.

Three figure-grounded reasons: Fig 2 :35, "Alignment is triangulation, not negotiation. A mirror cannot correct you."; Fig 2 :41, "efficient for a convention: agreement about a shared meaning is the product, not a corruption of it"; and Fig 10 :42, "A verifier that can reconstruct the claim cannot verify it." Selecting recipients for likely agreement manufactures the disqualifying correlation, and sending the full framework before asking for a verdict spends the independence the outside reader contributes.

Legally: scraping personal emails for unsolicited bulk outreach breaches GDPR and CAN-SPAM style rules in many jurisdictions. Public opt-in channels avoid that failure as well as the structural one.
