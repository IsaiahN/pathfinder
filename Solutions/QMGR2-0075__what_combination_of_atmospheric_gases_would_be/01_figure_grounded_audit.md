## 0. The residual, stated in effect terms

We hold a disk-averaged spectrum of an exoplanet atmosphere. It is a reading, not a source. Between the reading and the verdict "life" there is a gap: the same reading can be produced by a biosphere, by abiotic photochemistry, by geology, by an impact, or by two unresolved bodies blended into one spectrum. The residual R is this underdetermination, indexed per candidate gas and per candidate source (Fig 1 :19, "The residual is indexed by object slot, not aggregated").

The question asks for a **combination of gases** that would be **persuasive evidence of life**. Two terms carry hidden structure and must be re-grounded before any answer:

1. **Combination.** A list of species is a flat formula. Fig 12 :5: "A set of ingredients is not one. The bond is the rest of the information, and it is the half nothing was recording." The evidential object is a molecule, that is, species plus the typed relation between them, not a shopping list.
2. **Persuasive evidence.** Persuasion is an agreement word. Fig 2 :29: "An anchor is whatever settles the question and does not change its answer because of what any frame thinks." A verdict about life is settled by a ground that does not update, not by how many researchers are convinced. Fig 2 :41 permits agreement to be efficient for a convention while Fig 2 :40 disqualifies it for a verdict.

**What an answer would look like, bound as an ordinary term before the run:** a co-occurrence of two or more atmospheric species, sustained by a renewed flux, which no abiotic process in the enumerated habitat can supply, read by an instrument that discriminates among sources, and ranged against an anchor that does not move when opinion changes. That is a shape. It is not a list of names. Fig 9 :32: "you get a kind of thing, never the answer itself."

## 1. Residual census: arity, symmetry, scale

- **Arity.** The sought object has arity at least two. A single gas is a term; a pair is a molecule. The question's own word "combination" already concedes arity above one. The evidential weight lives in the bond, not in either operand (Fig 12 :46, "A bond is the typed relation between two operands. There are seven, they are not interchangeable, and which one holds is not recoverable from the operands.").
- **Symmetry.** A list of gases uses conjunction, which is symmetric: Fig 12 :59, "swap the operands. Does the meaning change? No." But the evidential content of a disequilibrium pair is ordered. One species is in excess relative to the equilibrium expectation, and the relevant quantity is a threshold relation, Fig 12 :87, "a threshold relation between two quantities", plus directedness, Symbols table :97, "A difference that can be spent. Not merely a distribution: what makes it a gradient is directedness." **Strain test:** if the pair is typed as symmetric conjunction, the bond carries zero information and the combination collapses to its ingredients, which the literature says are individually insufficient. Therefore conjunction does not govern; comparison and gradient do. This is a re-census instruction, not a cosmetic choice (see F3 and section 8).
- **Scale.** The reading is at the instrument scale; the verdict is about a source at the habitat scale. The transform between them loses information, so a round-trip gap R_T is owed (Fig 4 :30). A spectrum that registers a band but cannot resolve whether one atmosphere or two produced it is an instrument that already returns something and fails to resolve, not a sensor for something that never registered (Fig 6 :34).

## 4. Findings F1 to F11

### F1: No standing list of gases is a biosignature (decisive against the literal question)

Fig 11 :51: "No standing list of good actors survives a change of residual."
Fig 11 :49: "An actor's valence is relative to the residual, not a property it carries."
Fig 11 :39: "capability is a property of agent-and-habitat, never of the agent alone."

O2 is a benefactor for one residual and a malfactor for another. In an anoxic atmosphere with abundant CH4 it is part of a compelling pair; around a star with strong UV it can be produced abiotically by water photolysis and water loss; on a cold world it can be a relic of a past climate. The same species changes valence with the habitat, not with itself.

**World corroboration (secondary).** Schwieterman et al. 2018, Astrobiology, opened 2026-11-23: "in practice, for almost any conceivable circumstance, a prospective exoplanet biosignature will always be a potential biosignature with other possible explanations." Wogan and Catling 2020, Astrophysical Journal, opened 2026-11-23: "Thus, the interpretation of disequilibrium requires context. With context, disequilibrium can be used to infer dead or living worlds." Their result is stronger: a large prebiotic H2, CO2 and water disequilibrium is an **anti**biosignature, because chemotrophic life would eat it. That is exactly valence reversal relative to the residual.

**Refuter:** a figure line that licenses a standing list as sufficient, or a measured atmosphere in which one gas uniquely determines its source across several habitats, would falsify F1.

### F2: The combination is a molecule, not a list

Fig 12 :5: "A set of ingredients is not one. The bond is the rest of the information, and it is the half nothing was recording."
Fig 12 :94: "The arrangement is the substance."
Fig 12 :46: "A bond is the typed relation between two operands. There are seven, they are not interchangeable, and which one holds is not recoverable from the operands."

O2 and CH4 react to form CO2 and H2O. Their persistent co-occurrence is not a fact about two ingredients; it is a fact about a coupling that requires renewed flux. The bond, that is, the maintained redox separation, carries the evidential information. Remove it and "O2 plus CH4" is just two spectral bands.

**World corroboration.** Schwieterman et al. 2018: "These two gases should rapidly oxidize to CO2 and H2O, so their persistent presence suggests a continual resupply of both gases from biological sources." Krissansen-Totton, Olson and Catling 2018, Science Advances, opened 2026-11-23: "the simultaneous detection of abundant CH4 and CO2 in a habitable exoplanet's atmosphere is a potential biosignature." Note that the second pair is different from the first, which is itself evidence that the gas names are bindings to a habitat, not figure outputs.

**Refuter:** show a pair whose two species are independently at equilibrium and whose evidential force is unchanged when the relation between them is removed. Then the bond carries nothing and F2 fails.

### F3: The bond is typed as comparison and gradient, not conjunction

Fig 12 :87: "a threshold relation between two quantities."
Symbols table :97: "A difference that can be spent. Not merely a distribution: what makes it a gradient is directedness."
Fig 12 :59: "swap the operands. Does the meaning change? No." (the conjunction test)
Fig 12 :92: "Symmetry is what decides whether an arrangement is a new thing."

A written "combination" is symmetric under relabelling, but a disequilibrium is not: it asks which species is in excess and by how much relative to the equilibrium state. Fig 12 :111: "A vocabulary without bonds can name a state and cannot name a route to one." A list can name bands; a comparison can name a departure from equilibrium.

**Refuter:** if the evidential content is invariant under exchanging the two gases and under replacing the pair by the same gases at equilibrium, the comparison typing is wrong and conjunction was correct. See `06_isomers_and_order.md` for the full symmetric versus ordered derivation.

### F4: A reading does not determine its source (metamerism)

Fig 2 :37: "instruments can be wrong on axes they do not measure."
Fig 6 :30: "The description travels. Fails by being sharpened into terms only we can read. Cast it in effect terms."
Fig 1 :19: "The residual is indexed by object slot, not aggregated."

A spectrum is a projection. Several sources can give the same projection. The framework's own essay *The Hard Problem is About Source* names this as metamerism: an instrument reads green and cannot say whether the source was one green lamp or a mixture. What is owed is a discriminator, not a name.

**World corroboration, genuinely low overlap.** Rein, Fujii and Spiegel 2014, PNAS, opened 2026-11-23: "an observation of two species does not show that they are in fact in the atmosphere of a single object (the planet)." They show an unresolved planet plus moon with O2 on one body and CH4 on the other mimics a single atmosphere with both, and that the degeneracy is in general not breakable at foreseeable spectral resolution. Wikipedia "Metamerism (color)", opened 2026-11-23, gives the independent color-science statement: "In colorimetry, metamerism is a perceived matching of colors with different (nonmatching) spectral power distributions." These two fields share no evidence pool with each other or with the framework.

**Refuter:** an instrument that, from the spectrum alone, uniquely assigns each absorption band to a single body and a single process, with a stated false-positive rate, would move this finding toward resolution.

### F5: Persuasion is the wrong anchor

Fig 2 :29: "An anchor is whatever settles the question and does not change its answer because of what any frame thinks."
Fig 2 :35: "Alignment is triangulation, not negotiation. A mirror cannot correct you."
Fig 2 :40: "agreement about the world is worthless when the agreeing frames share an evidence pool"
Fig 2 :41: "efficient for a convention: agreement about a shared meaning is the product, not a corruption of it"
Fig 7 :35: "A room's agreement is variation, not a verdict."

"Persuasive evidence" mixes two quantities. Persuasion is a fact about a community (a convention, Fig 2 :41). Evidence is a relation to a ground (a verdict, Fig 2 :40). A survey of researchers who find a gas pair convincing measures the room, not the planet. The question is answerable only after "persuasive" is re-grounded as "would survive contact with an anchor that does not update on the community's opinion".

**World corroboration, genuinely low overlap.** Stanford Encyclopedia of Philosophy, "Underdetermination of Scientific Theory", opened 2026-11-23: "the evidence available to us at a given time may be insufficient to determine what beliefs we should hold in response to it." Its Duhem and Quine sections explain why agreement among theories fitted to the same data cannot by itself select the source. The SEP entry also warns that underdetermination does not license social construction as a substitute for evidence, which keeps this finding honest: the gap is an instrument gap, not a licence for consensus.

**Refuter:** a case where community agreement and the ground demonstrably coincide because the ground is itself a vote or a definition would not refute this, because the question would then be about a convention. A case where a non-updating physical measurement settles a source verdict without any agreement would weaken the "persuasive" critique, not the anchor requirement.

### F6: The literal gas list is a `gap`, reported as unreachable, not as silence

Fig 9 :32: "you get a kind of thing, never the answer itself"
Fig 13 :133: "what it returns is a shape rather than a name."
Fig 13 :134: "Unreachable is a verdict here, not a silence"
Fig 8 :39: "So a search that finds nothing is never a proof of absence."

No figure line selects O2 plus CH4 over CH4 plus CO2 over O2 plus N2 plus liquid water. The six-term substrate (space, time, energy; spectrum, gradient, network) is closed under composition; the set of atmospheric gases is not in it, and cannot be derived from it. Candidate pairs are bindings from the planetary-science frame, imported with provenance and a shadow test (Fig 6 :23), not figure outputs. The ground that would settle which binding is right, the source of the gases on that planet, is not reachable at this budget.

**Refuter:** derive a specific gas pair from the six-term set plus the seven bonds with no frame-specific input, or exhibit a non-updating anchor that settles the source for an exoplanet now. Either would close the gap.

### F7: Independence is required, and one literature pool is one witness

Fig 2 :31: "Independence, between two frames, is how little they draw on the same evidence."
Fig 8 :50: "Two frames can hold the same atom by convergence, reaching it separately, which is corroboration at maximum independence."
Fig 8 :51: "Convergent derivation and adopted import look identical in the contents."
Fig 9 :60: "Use filters for the budget and witnesses for the verdict. Never let a filter hand you a verdict."

The four astrobiology papers read here share authors, workshops, citations and a common evidence pool (the NExSS workshop series and the Earth analogue record). They count as one witness for the shape, not four witnesses. Rein et al. 2014 (instrument limitation), Wikipedia metamerism (color science), and the SEP entry (philosophy of science) are genuinely low overlap. Even they corroborate the *shape*, not the claim that life exists on any particular planet. Detection significance, SNR and spectral resolution are filters for the budget; they never hand over the verdict.

**Refuter:** two frames with demonstrated low overlap, each ranging against the same non-updating anchor, that converge on the same source assignment would upgrade the corroboration. A pool of papers agreeing because they cite each other would not.

### F8: Contact cascades, so the habitat must be enumerated outward

Fig 11 :43: "The habitat is enumerated, never composed."
Fig 11 :52: "And contact cascades."
Fig 11 :49: "An actor's valence is relative to the residual, not a property it carries."

Gas readings are direct contact. Stellar UV spectrum, flares, surface temperature, ocean state, volcanism, impacts and a possible exomoon are second- and third-order contact. Scoping to direct contact is how a false positive survives: the O2 is on the planet, the CH4 is on the moon, and the blended spectrum looks like disequilibrium in one atmosphere. The habitat is read off the world, not invented, and what cannot yet be measured is the residual.

**Refuter:** a case where a two-species reading is correctly interpreted with no information about star type, temperature, or companion bodies, across multiple such cases, would falsify the claim that the cascade matters.

### F9: The objective link has no instrument

Fig 3 :27: "A link with no instrument is not a link that works. It is a link nobody has looked at."
Fig 6 :34: "What is unavailable is a sensor for something that has never registered anywhere, at any resolution."
Fig 6 :32: "An instrument is not built from a description. It is improved from a worse instrument already returning something."

This is the honest boundary between underivable and unreachable. A sensor for a source that has never registered anywhere is unavailable, but that is not this case: a blended spectrum already registers and fails to resolve. So INWARD is available, by sharpening an instrument that already returns something: higher spectral resolution, temporal monitoring, thermal constraints, independent stellar characterization. Fig 8 :39 forbids reading failure as absence, and the 2025 ApJ paper by Young et al. (opened via abstract, recorded as partial in the ledger) reports that disequilibrium inference is heavily dependent on observational uncertainty in O2, CH4 and temperature, which is an instrument statement, not an absence statement.

**Refuter:** a worked demonstration that no increase in resolution or independent context could discriminate the sources, derived from a physical limit rather than from a budget, would move this from unreachable-at-budget to underivable-in-principle for that instrument class. Rein et al. 2014 argue something close to this for planet-moon blending at photon-noise limited resolution; the status is recorded as a live strain in section 8.

### F10: A candidate pair is a provenanced import, not a mint

Fig 6 :23: "No frame escapes its own closure. The bound moves only on contact with a frame whose closure differs."
Fig 8 :53: "Residual first, frame second."
Fig 8 :51: "Convergent derivation and adopted import look identical in the contents."
Fig 5 :41: "Any one of them at zero stops the mint entirely."

Naming O2 plus CH4 does not add a primitive to the six-term set; it binds an existing atmospheric vocabulary to a candidate relation. SUPPORT is present (a residual exists, the reading does not determine the source). REACHABILITY holds for the shape. NOVELTY fails for the gases; they are already in the library. SETTLEMENT is absent for the source. Fig 5 :41 makes the product explicit: one zero stops the mint. So this is a binding with provenance, not a new term and not a wall-moving import.

**Refuter:** a case where naming a gas pair genuinely extends what the framework can represent, with a documented source gate and shadow test, would change this finding.

## 5. Pre-registered refuter (written before the conclusion)

Per the seat rule, a falsifier written before the run is a design instrument. The shape claim is refuted if any of the following holds:

1. A figure line licenses a standing list of atmospheric gases as sufficient evidence of life, independent of habitat (breaks F1 and Fig 11 :51).
2. A single gas reading uniquely determines its source against a non-updating anchor in more than one habitat (breaks F4).
3. A figure re-census shows the governing bond for a disequilibrium pair is symmetric conjunction rather than comparison or gradient (breaks F3 and F2).
4. A measured exoplanet spectrum yields a gas pair whose co-occurrence no abiotic source in a fully enumerated habitat can produce, with a non-updating anchor confirming life (breaks F6, moves the verdict to `grounded`).
5. Two genuinely low-overlap instruments converge on the same source assignment with measured independence and a fixed anchor (breaks the unreachable-at-budget claim in F9 and F6).
6. Community agreement is shown to be the anchor for a question that is constitutively about a physical source (breaks Fig 2 :36, the anchor legitimacy test).

If any fires, the corresponding finding is withdrawn and the affected file is corrected with the old reading preserved. Until one is run, nothing here is `grounded`.

## 6. Recommendation

1. Stop answering the question as a request for a list. Answer the residual: what shape must any persuasive gas combination have (F1, F2, F6).
2. Re-ground "persuasive" as anchored. Ask what would survive contact with a ground that does not update, not what most researchers would accept (F5).
3. Type the bond as comparison plus gradient, and write the pair in effect terms: a maintained departure from equilibrium requiring renewed flux (F3, F4).
4. Enumerate the habitat outward before naming any gas: star type, flares, temperature, ocean, geology, impacts, companions (F8).
5. Record candidate pairs, O2 plus CH4, CH4 plus CO2, O2 plus N2 plus liquid water, as provenanced bindings from the planetary-science frame with their habitat conditions, not as framework outputs (F10).
6. Lead outreach with the shape and the pre-registered refuter; offer links only after a verdict on stage 1 is recorded (Fig 10 :52; `02_outreach_email.md`).

## 8. Strains, re-census and round trip

- **S1, list versus molecule.** Force: a list is symmetric, a disequilibrium is ordered. Re-census: the question's word "combination" is ordinary language, not a bond type. Fig 12 :92 assigns symmetric and ordered bonds different term counts, so the evidential bond is comparison and gradient. Resolved, and the earlier symmetric reading retained above.
- **S2, Fig 11 valence versus Fig 2 fixed anchor.** Apparent conflict: gases change valence with habitat, yet the anchor must not update. Re-census: valence belongs to the habitat frame (D3); anchor non-update belongs to the verification frame (D4). Different objects. Dissolved, not erased.
- **S3, instrument limit versus Fig 4 round trip.** Rein et al. 2014 give a photon-noise resolution limit that looks like a wall. Fig 4 :30 requires the gap to be measured. R_T is nonzero here: what is sent up is a discriminated source claim; what comes back down is a blended band depth. The dropped constraints are enumerated in `11_audit.md`. Recorded as live, not smoothed.
- **S4, CH4 plus CO2 versus O2 plus CH4.** Both are cited as biosignature pairs in the literature. Re-census: different atmospheric regimes (anoxic Archean analogue versus oxic modern analogue), so both are bindings to different habitats, not competing figure derivations. Dissolved by habitat enumeration.
- **After re-census: three strains recorded, one live (S3), two dissolved (S1, S2, S4 appears as habitat dependence rather than strain).**
