# The Tether's answer: oxygen before oxygen-producing life

**Mode: `general` on the structural shape, `specified` on the per-slot and provenance claims, `gap` on the numerical concentration and the chronology. Not `grounded`.** This is a shape and a route, not a result. Simulated derivation checked against the figures (`05_what_shape_means.md` section 7), not a ground reading.

## 1. The residual it answers

The question bundles three asks with different anchors: how much (a magnitude), if any (an existence claim, hence a negation), and before oxygen-producing life (an ordering). Fig 9 :4-5: *"First: is there a fact here? Disagreements that do not shrink with effort, where each rule keeps working well on a different subset, are not one hard question but several well-formed ones. Split it rather than search."* Fig 9 :57: *"a case in two conditions is not a harder case of either."* It is a union compound and gets three separate verdicts, never an average.

## 2. The shape

1. **Answer per slot, not globally.** Fig 1 :19, *"The residual is indexed by object slot, not aggregated."* Fig 1 :13, *"a global R near zero with one live slot residual is a legal state."* Global atmosphere, shallow ocean, restricted basin, weathering profile and microbial mat are distinct slots. A global average near anoxic is compatible with local oxygen oases and transient whiffs.

2. **"No resolved O2 above trace levels" is the honest existence verdict, not "zero".** Fig 8 :39, *"So a search that finds nothing is never a proof of absence."* Fig 8 :44, *"Only a sealed room can be searched to the end, and no frame is a sealed room. So searching and finding nothing proves nothing."* The channel to the ground is what fails, not the ground: Fig 10 :24, *"the ground does not decay, the channel does."*

3. **A redox reading cannot name its source.** Fig 8 :52, *"Convergent derivation and adopted import look identical in the contents. Only the record of where each came from separates them."* Fig 6 :23, *"No frame escapes its own closure. The bound moves only on contact with a frame whose closure differs."* Molybdenum, rhenium, uranium, chromium and sulfur return redox readings, not process identities. The framework's own source essay says the instrument *"cannot comment on the source"* and *"the answer is unreached until a better instrument exists."*

4. **The quantity is a comparison, not a stock.** Fig 12 :87, *"is it about which is larger, rather than both being present? Yes."* What matters is sources against sinks. Fig 11 :49, *"An actor's valence is relative to the residual, not a property it carries."* Fig 11 :51, *"No standing list of good actors survives a change of residual."* There is no standing list of oxygen sources.

5. **The number and the chronology are gaps.** Fig 9 :32, *"you get a kind of thing, never the answer itself."* Fig 13 :134, *"It can say the answer is out of reach with the library as it stands, so something has to be imported before it is asked again."* No figure line returns ppm or a date. Fig 13 :115, *"The set is closed. The arrangements are not."*

6. **"Before" is a sequence, not a production.** Fig 12 :71, *"A then B; B needs A to have happened."* The temporal order of two producers is not the production of O2 by life. The two bonds are different; see `06_isomers_and_order.md`.

## 3. What it explicitly does not say

- Not a number. No partial pressure, mixing ratio or ppm is asserted by the framework.
- Not "there was no oxygen". Non-detection is not a proof of zero.
- Not "there was oxygen". A local or transient signal is not a global atmosphere.
- Not that any named process (oxygenic photosynthesis, water photolysis, SO2 photolysis, radiolysis, tectonics) produced a given reading. That is a source attribution and needs a discriminator with provenance.
- Not a date for the Great Oxidation Event or for the origin of oxygenic photosynthesis. Those are contested in the world and unreachable in the framework.
- Not grounded. No measurement of the ancient atmosphere was taken.

## 4. What would settle it — the ground

A source-discriminating instrument, not a further argument. From *The Hard Problem is About Source*: the spectrometer, not an argument, resolved metamerism; *"the answer is unreached until a better instrument exists."*

- **Source discriminator:** a measurement that maps one redox reading to one production process with provenance and a shadow test that explains the gap it closes (closes F5, R2).
- **Direct paleo-oxybarometer:** a trapped-gas or equivalent reading for the Archean, validated against an anchor that does not update on the frame's own depositional model (closes F3 and F7, R1).
- **Per-slot conservation test:** a demonstration that per-slot signals are identical and aggregation loses nothing (closes F2, R3).
- **Sealed-room demonstration:** proof that one instrument exhausts the possible O2 space at every resolution (closes F4, R5).
- **Standing figure falsifiers:** derive one of the six substrate terms from the other five; admit a state-state or structure-structure composition that cannot be refused; find a figure line that returns a concentration.

## 5. The honest stop

**Unreached, not unreachable in principle.** Fig 8 :39: *"So a search that finds nothing is never a proof of absence."* Fig 8 :40-42 distinguishes underivable in principle from unreachable at this budget: the number is unreachable at this budget because no instrument returns it, not because the question is meaningless. The six-term set is a guess at compression axes, not nature's own list; declaring it makes the choice visible, not neutral. The answer stops at the limit of the available instruments and records the lineage so the stop is checkable. *"Indiscernibility is not identity."*

## 6. One-paragraph version for outreach

> The Tether does not return a number for pre-biological atmospheric oxygen, and it does not claim there was none. It says the question is three questions with different anchors, so the answer must be given per reservoir rather than as one global average; that a failure to detect oxygen is a limit of the channel, not proof of a zero ground; and that a redox reading cannot name the process that produced it without a source-discriminating instrument carrying provenance. The quantity that matters is a comparison of sources against sinks, not a stock, and no actor is permanently a source or a sink because valence is relative to the atmospheric state. "Before" is a sequence between two producers, not the production of oxygen by life. What the framework offers is the shape any answer must have, five falsifiers written in advance, and the instruments that would close the declared gaps. The slot stays open until the ground settles it.

## 7. World corroboration in one line

The geological literature supplies the same shape in effect terms: near-zero global oxygen with local and transient signals (Wikipedia, *Great Oxidation Event* and *Geological history of oxygen*), proxy interpretations that conflict (chromium versus sulfur; the 2.5 Ga whiff challenged and defended), and a literature that openly says its paleo-oxybarometers are indirect and still debated (Kendall 2021 as cited). Agreement among those sources is one evidence pool talking (Fig 2 :40), not several witnesses. Full provenance is in `09_watson_answer.md` and `10_source_ledger.csv`.
