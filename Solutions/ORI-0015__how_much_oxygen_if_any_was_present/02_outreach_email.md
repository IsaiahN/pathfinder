# Outreach draft: check the output before you decide about the frame

**Use:** one to one, or as a cover letter attached to a submission through the public venues in `03_public_contact_points.csv`. **Not** for bulk send.

**Hard precondition:** the shape claim below must survive the check in stage 1. If it does not, there is nothing else to look at.

---

**Subject:** One checkable claim about pre-GOE oxygen, before any theory

> Hi [name],
>
> I am writing about a narrow point in early Earth oxygen research, not about the framework that produced it. I am not asking you to agree with anything, and I am not asking you to read the theory first. I have one output I would like you to try to break.
>
> **The output (`general` on the shape, `specified` on the per-slot and provenance claims, `gap` on the number):**
>
> The question "how much oxygen, if any, was present before oxygen-producing life evolved?" is not one question. It bundles a magnitude, an existence claim and a time ordering, and those have different anchors. My derivation says an answer must have four properties:
>
> 1. it is indexed per reservoir (global atmosphere, shallow ocean, restricted basin, weathering profile, microbial mat), because a global average near zero can coexist with one live local slot and averaging hides it;
> 2. it says "no resolved atmospheric O2 above trace levels," not "zero," because a search that finds nothing is never a proof of absence;
> 3. it does not name the source of any pre-GOE oxygen from a redox reading alone, because an instrument that reads redox state cannot comment on which process produced the signal;
> 4. it returns a shape and a set of refusers, not a universal ppm number, because no rule in the derivation yields one.
>
> **What I am not claiming:** that there was definitely no oxygen, that any particular "whiff" was biological or abiotic, that chromium or molybdenum or sulfur isotopes settle the source question, or that the framework can name a concentration. Those are bindings from the geological frame onto slots in the derivation. Until a source-discriminating measurement with stated provenance exists, they carry no evidentiary weight in this claim.
>
> **What would refute it, written before this message:**
> 1. a direct paleo-oxybarometer that returns atmospheric pO2 at several slots for the pre-biological interval, validated against an anchor that does not move when the frame pushes on it;
> 2. a measurement that maps one redox reading to one production process, with provenance and a shadow test that explains the gap it closes;
> 3. a demonstration that the per-slot signals are identical, so aggregation loses nothing;
> 4. a derivation that returns a numerical concentration from the rules themselves;
> 5. a demonstration that one instrument exhausts the O2 space at every resolution, making zero a settled verdict.
>
> If any of these holds, I retract the claim. A one-line "this fails because..." is more useful to me than agreement. If you would rather not engage, no reply is needed.
>
> **If it survives, and only then:** the sources are below. The figures, not my summary of them, are the authority.
> - Verbatim figure text with line numbers (local file in this Solution folder): `FRAMEWORK/reference/03-FIGURES_TEXT.md`
> - Canonical usage rule (local): `FRAMEWORK/reference/FIGURES_README_canonical.md`
> - The Great Oxidation Event and its proxies: https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Great_Oxidation_Event
> - Geological history of oxygen and paleo-oxybarometers: https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Geological_history_of_oxygen
>
> **If you want to reuse the machinery:** the formula is in the figures link. Run it on a problem you know well and can check against a ground. Write down what would refute your result *before* you run it, because a frame cannot check the direction of its own question, and label the mode of what you get. A reported failure is more useful to me than a reported success.
>
> — [name]

| No request for trust or prestige | Prestige can fund a trial but cannot settle it; only the ground settles an action. The ask gives the claim a budget to be tried, not an endorsement. |
| The framework returns a shape, not a name | Canonical rule: *"what a missing thing would have to look like (a shape, not a name)."* Here the missing thing is the instrument, so the email states the instrument's shape and refuses the number. |
| Offer the formula as a method to reuse | Fig 4 :22, *"Only methods cross up. Only head starts come down."* |
| Ask recipients to state their own ground and refuter | A frame cannot check the direction of its own question; the sought-for shape must be made an ordinary term before the run. |

## What is deliberately absent

- No request to believe the framework, no "true answer", no "bombshell".
- No personal emails or purchased list. Public institutional and opt-in venues only.
- No pooling of later agreement as corroboration (Fig 2 :40).
- No claim that the geological sources cited are independent witnesses of the same fact. They are a shared evidence pool and are presented as such.
