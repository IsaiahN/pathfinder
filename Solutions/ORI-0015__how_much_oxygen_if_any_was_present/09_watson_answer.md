## VERDICT (from the figures)

The framework returns a shape, not a number. The answer must be per reservoir, must say "no resolved O2 above trace levels" rather than "zero", must withhold source attribution until a discriminator with provenance exists, and must be structured as sources compared against sinks with valence relative to the atmospheric state. Magnitude, existence and chronology are three verdicts, never averaged. The numerical concentration and the chronology are declared gaps of the unreachable-at-this-budget and shape-not-a-name kinds. Nothing is `grounded`. The orbital term "oxygen oasis" and "whiff" are imports from the geological frame with provenance, not new atoms. "Before" is a sequence bond between two producers, not the production of oxygen by life.

- **cost if wrong:** bounded and locatable. The packet states its mode, five refuters and six verbatim figure lines before the conclusion, so an error is findable rather than invisible. The real cost is spending the independence of outside readers by sending a single global number or a named source instead of a generator and its refuter (Fig 2 :42, *"Buy independence in proportion to the cost of being wrong."*). That cost is avoidable by leading with the shape claim and the five refuters.
- **undoable:** yes. The gaps are declared rather than filled, so no number or date has to be withdrawn. A new source-discriminating instrument can be added to a later version without changing the shape. A sent claim can be superseded by a corrected packet, and no primitive has to be removed.
- **reversed by:** a ground reading, not agreement. A direct paleo-oxybarometer validated against a non-updating anchor would name the concentration; a measurement mapping one redox reading to one process with provenance and a shadow test would name the source; a demonstration that per-slot signals are identical would remove the per-slot requirement; a figure line returning a concentration would break the gap verdict; a sealed-room proof would make zero settled. Until one of these fires, the verdict stands as `candidate`.
