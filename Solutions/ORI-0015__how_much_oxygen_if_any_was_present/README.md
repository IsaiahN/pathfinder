## The question as an effect

The question bundles three different asks. "How much" asks for a magnitude, an ordering of more and less (Fig 13 :20, *"ordering, so more and less can be said"*). "If any" asks for existence, which is a negation as much as an assertion (Operators table :58, *"a refutation is a negation, which is why a vocabulary without it can only accumulate"*). "Before oxygen-producing life evolved" fixes an ordering between a producer and its product. What each answer leads to: a single global number hides the per-slot signal (Fig 1 :22); a verdict of zero confuses failure to detect with proof of absence (Fig 8 :39); and naming a source from a redox reading repeats the metamerism error the framework warns against. The shape an answer would have, written down and bound before the run, is: **per slot, source-attributed via a discriminator with provenance, threshold-structured in sources against sinks, and stated as a shape rather than a number.**

## What is deliberately not here

- No personal emails of named individuals.
- No ranking of people by how likely they are to agree with the shape claim.
- No bulk-send plan.

Three reasons, in the figures' own words:

1. **A room does not upgrade the label.** Fig 7 :35, *"A room's agreement is variation, not a verdict. Selection between rooms is where the ground votes."*
2. **Selecting for receptiveness manufactures the disqualifying correlation.** Fig 2 :40, *"disqualifying for a verdict: agreement about the world is worthless when the agreeing frames share an evidence pool"*; Fig 2 :42, *"Buy independence in proportion to the cost of being wrong."*
3. **Asking for trust before a check spends the only thing the outside frame contributes.** Fig 10 :42, *"A verifier that can reconstruct the claim cannot verify it."*

Legally: unsolicited bulk email to harvested addresses breaches GDPR and CAN-SPAM style rules in many jurisdictions. Public opt-in channels avoid that failure as well as the structural one.
