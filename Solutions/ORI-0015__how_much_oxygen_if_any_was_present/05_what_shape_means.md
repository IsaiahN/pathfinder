## 1. A shape is a constraint, not a filler

The figures README states the limit: the framework returns *"what a missing thing would have to look like (a shape, not a name)."* Fig 9 :32, *"you get a kind of thing, never the answer itself."* Fig 13 :134-135: *"narrowing gets you close while the ground decides."*

From the carried *Library of Babel* mapping: *"You can cut your search short if the set of books returned is small and descriptive enough that it provides a general shape of the answer that satisfies your question."*

So a **shape** is the narrowed form of an answer you can state before you can measure it. It constrains; it does not fill. For this question:

- "The answer is per reservoir, not one global number" is a shape.
- "Atmospheric pO2 was 0.001 percent of present atmospheric level" is a number, read from the rock record, not returned by the figures.
- "The source must be identified by a discriminator with provenance" is a shape.
- "The whiff was biological" is a name, and the figures do not license it from a redox reading.

## 2. How a shape is obtained

- *Library of Babel* (carried): use the vocabulary you already have that is closest to the thing, look for recurring new terms that overlap with it, recurse. *"With each new term gained, you narrow the space and deepen your vocabulary."*
- Fig 6 :30, *"The description travels. Fails by being sharpened into terms only we can read. Cast it in effect terms."* Fig 9 :30-31, *"your words are borrowed too, so describe the effect rather than the cause."* For this question: describe the residual as a redox signal that fails to resolve its source, not as "the Great Oxidation Event", which is a borrowed name.
- *Decoding Tacit Knowledge* (carried): *"When contact isn't possible, the best you can send is a plain description of what you'd expect to see, because anyone can check that against the world."* Here contact is possible but indirect, so the same discipline applies: say what a source-discriminating signal would look like.
- Fig 8 :53, *"Residual first, frame second."* The residual was described before the domains were mapped to figure lines.

## 3. What licenses a name

Only two things, both outside the shape itself:

1. **The ground settles it.** Formula step 5: *"the ground settles it. Verified only by what pays. A gate is not the ground."* The README: the framework *"stops at the limit of what the available instruments can distinguish, and records the lineage so the stop is honest."* For oxygen, that would be a direct, validated paleo-oxybarometer paying for a concentration.
2. **A provenanced import from a frame whose closure differs** (Fig 6 :23, Fig 8 :52), with a source gate and a shadow test. "Oxygen oasis" and "whiff" are imports from the geological frame. They are admissible as names for patterns of readings only with provenance.

*They're Scanning Books* (carried): *"The real import is not a new term in the existing space. It is a new dimension of the space."* And: *"Declaring the primitives does not make them neutral. It makes the choice visible."*

## 4. The source problem is the governing one for this question

*The Hard Problem is About Source* (opened in full, 2026-11-23) gives the mechanism in the instrument's own terms:

- *"What if you have metrics that do not discriminate about its inputs?"*
- *"If several paths create the same behavior, then behavior is a projection with lossy information"*
- *"the instrument detects green, and says that it's green. However, the instrument is speaking only about what it measured, it cannot comment on the source."*
- *"The answer is unreached until a better instrument exists."*
- *"This is why the True Hard Problem is about determining the Source."*

Applied here: Mo, U, Cr and S isotopes return a redox reading. Several paths (oxygenic photosynthesis, abiotic photolysis with hydrogen escape, SO2 photolysis, radiolysis, tectonic oxidants) can produce or destroy that reading. The reading does not select among them. The real case supplies the refusal: Archean steranes were read as cyanobacterial and eukaryotic biomarkers, then *"shown to have been contaminated, and so the results are no longer accepted"* (Wikipedia, *Great Oxidation Event*, opened 2026-11-23). That is a source named from a projection without a discriminator.

The genuinely low-overlap cognition source corroborates the shape: *Source-monitoring error* (Wikipedia, opened 2026-11-23) defines the error as one "where the source of a memory is incorrectly attributed to some specific recollected experience", citing Johnson, Hashtroudi and Lindsay 1993, Psychological Bulletin 114(1), 3-28. A recorded signal does not carry an automatic label of its source. This corroborates the figure's provenance requirement, not the geological claim.

## 5. Q — what shape does the framework return for this question?

**Nothing numerical, and nothing that names a source.** What it returns is a four-part constraint:

1. **Per-slot indexing.** The answer is stated separately for the global atmosphere, shallow ocean, restricted basin, weathering profile and microbial mat. Fig 1 :19 and :22.
2. **A refusal of zero.** Non-detection is a channel limit, not a ground fact. Fig 8 :39.
3. **A source gate.** A source attribution is owed, but only a discriminator with provenance can pay it. Fig 8 :52; Fig 6 :12-13.
4. **A threshold structure.** The quantity that matters is sources compared against sinks, not a stock of oxygen. Fig 12 :87; Fig 11 :49.

**The specific concentration and the specific chronology are `gap`s.** Fig 13 :134: *"It can say the answer is out of reach with the library as it stands, so something has to be imported before it is asked again."* A gap is a verdict, not silence.

## 6. Correction to my own §3 (kept visible)

In the first pass I wrote that "shape, not a name" forbids naming the process that produced pre-GOE oxygen. That overstates the rule. The README's phrase is *"what a **missing** thing would have to look like (a shape, not a name)."* It governs a primitive not held in Γ, that is, a thing the frame does not have. Here the missing thing is not oxygen itself, nor the process, but the **instrument that would discriminate among processes**. Naming a held binding (for example, "photodissociation of water with hydrogen escape" from Carver 1981, present in the geological frame) is an import with provenance, not the invention of a new atom. Fig 6 :25: *"An import adds no atom to the world."*

What remains wrong, and is **not** retracted: calling that binding settled on the strength of a redox reading repeats the metamerism error. The reason is the missing discriminator, not a blanket ban on naming held atoms. Both claims are now recorded so neither is quietly lost.

## 7. Two consequences for outreach

**Prestige is not action.** From the carried *Recursive Self-Improvement Trap*: *"Prestige must never be allowed to validate an action directly... Prestige can fund an action. It can give an action a budget to be tried. But it cannot settle an action. Only the ground can settle it."* A campaign that gathers well known names to agree that oxygen was or was not present is the named collapse, not corroboration. The legitimate ask is to give the claim a budget to be tried and let an instrument settle it.

**My own audit is a simulation, not a ground reading.** Same essay (carried): *"LLMs can quickly simulate an approximate answer/output... However, the truly testable output would need to be derived from the real architecture or working the formula yourself."* LLM output is *"simulacra."* So `01` is a simulated derivation checked for internal consistency against the figures. It is **not** a test of the claim and must be labelled as such. It cannot settle anything it reports on.
