# Expert verification packet — the Tether on pre-biological oxygen

**What this is:** a claim in a form an early Earth researcher can check. Mode, derivation and refuters are stated before the conclusion, so a reader knows what to look for and what would settle it.

**Mode: `general` on the structural shape, `specified` on the per-slot and provenance claims, `gap` on the number and the chronology. Not `grounded`.** No measurement of the ancient atmosphere is claimed. This is a shape and a route, not a result. The whole folder is a simulated derivation checked for internal consistency against the figures, not a ground reading, and cannot settle the question it reports on.

## 1. The claim, in one paragraph

The question "how much oxygen, if any, was present before oxygen-producing life evolved?" is three questions with different anchors: a magnitude, an existence claim and a time ordering. The derivation returns a shape rather than a number. An answer must be (i) indexed per reservoir, because a global average near zero can coexist with one live local slot; (ii) phrased as "no resolved atmospheric O2 above trace levels," not "zero," because non-detection is not proof of absence; (iii) silent on which process produced any pre-GOE oxygen until a source-discriminating instrument with provenance exists, because a redox reading cannot comment on its source; and (iv) structured as sources compared against sinks, with no standing list of source actors. The number and the chronology are declared gaps. Five falsifiers were written before the run.

## 2. Three checkable sub-claims

### C1 — framework-internal; checkable by reading Figures 1 and 8
- **Statement:** the answer is per slot, and non-detection is not proof of zero.
- **Source, verbatim:** Fig 1 :19, *"The residual is indexed by object slot, not aggregated."* Fig 1 :22, *"Aggregating across slots is how a live signal disappears into an average."* Fig 8 :39, *"So a search that finds nothing is never a proof of absence."*
- **Refuter:** a demonstration that per-slot signals are identical so aggregation loses nothing, or a sealed-room argument proving one instrument exhausts the O2 space at every resolution.

### C2 — framework-internal plus geochemistry; checkable by an isotope or redox geochemist
- **Statement:** a redox reading cannot name the source of the oxygen. This is the decisive sub-claim.
- **Source, verbatim:** Fig 8 :52, *"Convergent derivation and adopted import look identical in the contents. Only the record of where each came from separates them."* Fig 6 :23, *"No frame escapes its own closure. The bound moves only on contact with a frame whose closure differs."* Fig 10 :52, *"Bring a characterised residual, never the source."*
- **What to check:** is there a published measurement that maps a single Mo, U, Cr or S reading to one production process, with stated provenance, rather than inferring the source from the reading? The Pilbara sterane episode (claimed as cyanobacterial/eukaryotic biomarkers, then abandoned as contamination) is the reference case for the failure mode; the 2.5 Ga whiff, challenged in 2022 and defended in 2023, is the reference case for an unresolved source dispute.
- **Refuter:** a source-discriminating measurement with provenance and a shadow test that explains the gap it closes.

### C3 — framework-internal; checkable by reading Figures 9 and 13
- **Statement:** no figure line returns a concentration or a date; both are declared gaps.
- **Source, verbatim:** Fig 9 :32, *"you get a kind of thing, never the answer itself."* Fig 13 :134, *"It can say the answer is out of reach with the library as it stands, so something has to be imported before it is asked again."*
- **Refuter:** a figure line that returns a numerical concentration, or a direct, validated paleo-oxybarometer for the Archean.

## 3. The five pre-registered refuters

Written before the conclusion and repeated here for the outside frame:

1. A direct paleo-oxybarometer returns atmospheric pO2 at several slots for the pre-biological interval, validated against an anchor that does not move when the frame pushes on it.
2. A measurement maps one redox reading to one production process, with provenance and a shadow test.
3. Per-slot O2 signals are demonstrated identical, so aggregation loses nothing.
4. A figure line returns a numerical oxygen concentration.
5. A demonstration that one instrument exhausts the O2 space at every resolution, making zero a settled verdict.

If any fires, the claim is withdrawn.

## 4. What is explicitly not claimed

- Not a number. No atmospheric pO2, ppm or percent is asserted by the framework.
- Not "there was no oxygen", and not "there was oxygen".
- Not that any named process produced a given reading; that is a source attribution and is withheld.
- No date for the Great Oxidation Event or the origin of oxygenic photosynthesis.
- Not that the geological sources cited are independent witnesses. They are one early Earth evidence pool (Fig 2 :40).
- Not a theory of everything; no final room. The source slot stays open until a discriminator settles it.
- Not grounded. Nothing here was settled by a measurement.
