## DOMAIN CENSUS

R by arity, symmetry and scale, then one line per domain with actors, currency and anchor; partition or union; order by dependency.

- **R (the residual).** Diversity-stability results are reported as positive, negative and null depending on metric and observation window. Arity: three (diversity, stability, scale), with the relation itself a function of scale, so a second-order residual. Symmetry: broken; resistance, initial resilience, recovery and invariability are not interchangeable. Scale: two axes, spatial grain and extent, temporal window and duration. Fig 9 :54-58 names it a union compound, not one hard question.
- **Domain 1, ecological composition (habitat).** Actors: species, functional groups, competition, predation, mutualism, conditions (light, water, season, terrain). Currency: biomass, abundance, energy flow. Anchor: monitoring time series and field experiments. Question type: a habitat to be enumerated, not composed (Fig 11 :43-44).
- **Domain 2, stability measurement (instruments).** Actors: resistance, initial resilience, recovery, invariability, persistence, CV based indices. Currency: variance, return rate, displacement. Anchor: the measurement protocol and its units. Question type: isomers over shared atoms (Fig 12 :117), so a partition by metric.
- **Domain 3, scale transform (membrane).** Actors: grain, extent, aggregation, alpha/beta/gamma, synchrony and asynchrony. Currency: information dropped by aggregation, the round trip gap `R_T`. Anchor: the same system observed at two scales. Question type: a lossy transform between levels (Fig 4 :27-30), not a new primitive.
- **Domain 4, verification (outside frame).** Actors: expert readers. Currency: agreement and residual. Anchor: the ground, never agreement. In this simulated run all four domains draw on one evidence pool, so this domain's independence is zero.
- **Order by dependency.** Enumerate D1, define D2, apply D3 and measure the gap, then D4 checks the result (Fig 3 :19-20). Describe the residual before choosing a frame (Fig 9 :27-28).

Strain test: Fig 4's lossy transform and Fig 12's settled-molecule rule govern different objects (the transform between scales versus composition after settlement at a level), so they do not strain after re-census.
