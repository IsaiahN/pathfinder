## Wider literature that bears on this question (from the verified pages)

1. **Metric-dependence is the live issue.** Jarillo et al. show that resistance and initial resilience are scale-free weighted means, while invariability generally increases at regional and community level. So "the diversity-stability relationship" cannot be one quantity.
2. **The sign is context dependent.** Ebel et al. report that the portfolio effect was not important in their system, and that dominant species effects depended on herbivory. Hodapp et al. report that individual species contributions can be positive or negative depending on community structure. This is why the shape claim refuses a sign.
3. **Scale is two axes.** Zelnik et al. map spatial extent against temporal extent and name two boundaries (locality and homogeneity). Fig 13 :76 says the same thing: a gradient over space and one over time are different terms.
4. **Low-overlap corroboration.** Modern portfolio theory gives the variance mechanism behind asynchronous fluctuations, from finance rather than ecology. It corroborates the mechanism, not the verdict. The verdict comes from the figures.
