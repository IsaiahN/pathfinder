## What is deliberately not here

- No personal emails of named individuals.
- No ranking of people by how likely they are to agree with the shape claim.
- No bulk-send plan.

The figures give three reasons. First, Fig 2 :35: "Alignment is triangulation, not negotiation. A mirror cannot correct you." A list selected for agreement is a mirror. Second, Fig 2 :40: "agreement about the world is worthless when the agreeing frames share an evidence pool." Selecting for receptiveness manufactures the shared pool. Third, Fig 10 :42: "A verifier that can reconstruct the claim cannot verify it." Sending the full framework before asking for a verdict converts a checker into a participant.
