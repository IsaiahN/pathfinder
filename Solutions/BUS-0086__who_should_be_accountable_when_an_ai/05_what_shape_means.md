## 1. A shape is a constraint, not a filler

The figures README states the limit: the framework reports *"what a missing thing would have to look like (a shape, not a name)."* Fig 9 :32: *"you get a kind of thing, never the answer itself."* Fig 13 :133: *"what it returns is a shape rather than a name."*

From *The Library of Babel*: *"you can cut your search short if the set of books returned is small and descriptive enough that it provides a general shape of the answer that satisfies your question."*

For BUS-0086 a **shape** is the form an accountability answer must take before the case is decided: a per-slot attribution, unbundled into conditions, with the four roles ranged against an external human-facing anchor. "The firm is accountable" is a name. "A structure of conditions must be settled per harmed slot by an anchor none of the parties can move" is a shape.

## 2. The essay that names this exact failure

*The Library of Babel* contains the sentence that governs this question most directly:

> *"Perhaps your problem was a bundle or conflation of several problems masquerading as one? You will need to search all of those problems individually to find their answers and then combine them in a satisfactory way."*

A four-way forced choice is a bundle masquerading as one question. Fig 9 :54 says the same thing in the framework's vocabulary: *"When the split lands on a union rather than a partition, unbundle before you search again."* This is the strongest single link between the essays and the finding at F2 of `01_figure_grounded_audit.md`.

## 3. How a shape is obtained

- *Library of Babel*: use the vocabulary you already have closest to the thing, look for recurring new terms that overlap or correlate, recurse. *"With each new term gained, you are able to narrow the space by definition and deepen your vocabulary."*
- Fig 6 :30: *"The description travels. Fails by being sharpened into terms only we can read. Cast it in effect terms."* Fig 9 :30-31: *"your words are borrowed too, so describe the effect rather than the cause."*
- *Decoding Tacit Knowledge*: *"When contact isn't possible, the best you can send is a plain description of what you'd expect to see, because anyone can check that against the world."*
- *Library of Babel* on stopping: *"you will know that you are done because the finish line will also be the starting place, as there is nowhere else to search and nothing else that can describe the answer better."*

So the shape is not produced by surveying opinions about vendor versus firm. It is produced by describing the residual (the harm, per slot) in effect terms and narrowing until a form appears that any reader can check against the world.

## 4. What licenses a name in a real case

Only two things, both outside the shape itself:

1. **The ground settles it.** `THE_FORMULA.md` step 5: *"the ground settles it. Verified only by what pays. A gate is not the ground."* The figures README: the framework *"stops at the limit of what the available instruments can distinguish, and records the lineage so the stop is honest."*
2. **A provenanced import from a frame whose closure differs** (Fig 6 :23, Fig 8 :50-52), with a source gate and a shadow test.

*How Intelligence Actually Works* puts the accountability case plainly:

> *"Alignment asks what the thing is answerable to. A ground, something that refuses, a bill that gets paid. Without it you have a system that can generate things and nothing that can tell it's wrong."*

That is the anchor test in one sentence. A finding by a court or regulator is a "bill that gets paid." An internal RACI chart is not.

## 5. The source problem, applied to responsibility

*The Hard Problem is About Source* gives the mechanism that matters here:

> *"If several paths create the same behavior, then behavior is a projection with lossy information."*

Several causal paths can produce the same harm reading. The reading does not determine the source. The essay's remedy is an instrument, not an argument: the spectrometer, not a debate, distinguished the sources of green. In this question, the equivalent instruments are logs, override records, deployment approvals and an external finding with reasons and a date. Naming "the vendor" or "the decision-maker" from the harm reading alone is the metamerism error: a source named from a projection.

*Decoding Tacit Knowledge* supplies the institutional version:

> *"Courts of law rule on evidence, which neither side can move."*

That is Fig 2's anchor rule stated in the language of human institutions. Also from the same essay: *"Frames range against the ground. They do not negotiate with it,"* and *"Each frame is corrected by it separately, and what makes the correction sound is that neither frame can move it."*

## 6. Prestige is not action, and agreement is not a verdict

*The Recursive Self-Improvement Trap*:

> *"Prestige can fund an action. It can give an action a budget to be tried. But it cannot settle an action. Only the ground can settle it. The Ground Maintainer must be blind to prestige."*

A list of authoritative-sounding people who agree that "the firm is always accountable" is the named collapse, not corroboration. The legitimate ask is the opposite: give the claim a budget to be tried, and let the ground, an actual finding, settle it.

*How a Society of Agents Would Work*: *"The group's agreement is never the verdict."* Fig 7 :35: *"A room's agreement is variation, not a verdict. Selection between rooms is where the ground votes."*

## 7. Standing lists and declared primitives

*They're Scanning Books When They Should Be Studying Nature*:

> *"Declaring the primitives does not make them neutral. It makes the choice visible, which is worth a great deal, because a visible choice can be revised and an emergent one cannot."*

The four offered actors are a declared list. Declaring them makes the choice visible, not neutral. Fig 11 :51: *"No standing list of good actors survives a change of residual."* The list must be checked against the enumerated habitat, not assumed.

The same essay also gives the outputs/generators distinction: *"A corpus is the compressed output of human minds; the capacity that produced it is a generator, and outputs do not contain their generator."* A past liability finding is an output; it is not a generator of the liability rule for a new case, and it cannot be treated as one.

## 8. Why the six-term set is not being used as a naming device here

*Arrival of the Fittest* notes that *"a minimum viable set of six terms is a human's careful guess at reality's compression axes; another sensorium's minimum set need not be those six."* This solution does not use the six substrate terms to name a new kind of actor. Fig 13 :115: *"The set is closed. The arrangements are not. That gap is why import exists."* Adding a fifth candidate to the four would be a rearrangement inside the same kind, not an import that moves a wall. The move required here is a re-census of the residual, not a new actor category.

## 9. Status of this derivation

*The Recursive Self-Improvement Trap*:

> *"the truly testable output would need to be derived from the real architecture or working the formula yourself."*

*Arrival of the Fittest* adds that the outputs *"could never be verified if the solutions presented were not contrived to fit the formula"* and must be seen as *"simulacra."* *How Intelligence Actually Works*: *"Because it is a metatheory, it cannot be validated, nor can it validate itself."*

So `01_figure_grounded_audit.md` and this file are **simulated derivations checked for internal consistency against the figures**. They are not a test of the accountability question and cannot settle it. No measurement, finding, or case record was used as a ground in producing them.

## 10. Correction to my own §4

I initially wrote that a name is licensed only by the ground or by a provenanced import. That overstates the second route for this question. The roles "vendor", "firm", "manager" and "decision-maker" are all already present in the normal vocabulary of the case; importing them adds no atom to any frame's closure (Fig 6 :21, *"MINT composes inside the closure and can never add an atom"*). What is owed is not an import but an **unbundling and a bond structure** (see `06_isomers_and_order.md`). The correction is recorded here rather than silently applied above.
