The Symbols table defines R_T as `gap(x, (T_E ∘ T_A)(x))`, the gap between what was sent up and what came back down. Fig 4 :30 requires it to be measured rather than assumed. I ran the round trip on the condensation the folder actually uses.

3. **Concretisation (T_E):** a real case attribution: "this firm, through this manager, is accountable for this harm."

**R_T is nonzero.** Four things the shorthand holds do not survive the trip back down:

- the **per-slot indexing** of R. The shorthand says "per slot"; the concrete case needs an instrument that actually reads each slot and a finding about each one. Without that instrument the shorthand collapses back into an organizational average.
- the **identity of the external anchor**. The shorthand says "a human-facing institution"; the concrete case needs a named court, regulator or board with jurisdiction, and a finding with reasons and a date. Fig 2 :36 requires legitimacy, not just externality.
- the **bond structure**. The shorthand says "typed relations"; the concrete case needs records showing who produced, who deployed, in what order, where the threshold was crossed and who could have refused. Without the records, "bond structure" is a phrase, not an attribution.
- the **two currencies**. Fig 12 :125 says description cost and action cost do not add. The shorthand does not carry the cost of the settlement action, so a reader can mistake a cheap description for a settled case.

One thing survives cleanly: the four-way choice is a union and none of the four parties can own the reading. That is the residue worth sending. R_T was **not** measured numerically, because no ground reading exists; it is recorded qualitatively as the enumerated set of dropped constraints.
