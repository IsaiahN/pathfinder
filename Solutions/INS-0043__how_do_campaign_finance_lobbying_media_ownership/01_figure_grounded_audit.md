## 0. The claim under audit

> When campaign finance, lobbying, media ownership and professional networks coexist, political power concentrates more than the sum of their separate effects predicts. The concentration is carried by the relations among the four, not by any one of them. A lever that closes one channel clears only the region where that channel acts alone. The compound quantity itself has no ground, so the claim is a shape and a route, not a measurement.

The residual is stated in effect terms. What failed to resolve is not "is money in politics bad" but: why does the joint presence of four channels produce more concentration than any one predicts, and what would a discriminator for that joint quantity look like?

## 2. Findings F1 to F9

Each finding keeps its verdict per domain. Verdicts are never averaged.

### F1: The four form a union compound, not an additive list

**Figure lines, verbatim, before concluding.**

- Fig 9 :55-56: *"A bundled problem is usually a union of overlapping conditions, not a list of separate ones. A single lever clears the region where only one condition holds and leaves the intersections standing, because the second condition still binds."*
- Fig 9 :57-58: *"What remains is smaller and it is also categorically different: a case in two conditions is not a harder case of either. It stops being a mixture and becomes a specific compound."*
- Fig 1 :22: *"Aggregating across slots is how a live signal disappears into an average."*
- Fig 9 :54: *"And when the split lands on a union rather than a partition, unbundle before you search again."*

**Verdict (D5, `general`, triangulated across Fig 9, Fig 1 and Fig 12).** The interaction lives in the intersections. A single-channel reform is a single lever: it clears the non-overlap region and leaves the compounds standing. This is a structural claim about the shape of the problem, not an empirical claim about any country.

**Refuter.** A settled study showing that the joint effect on a ground (policy congruence, for example) equals the sum of the four single-channel effects, with no interaction term in any pair, AND that the channels draw on separate evidence pools. If that holds, the union has no surplus and F1 is withdrawn.

### F2: Concentration is carried by the bonds, not by the channels as atoms

**Figure lines.**

- Fig 12 :5: *"A set of ingredients is not one. The bond is the rest of the information, and it is the half nothing was recording."*
- Fig 12 :94: *"The arrangement is the substance."*
- Fig 12 :95-96: *"Two entries with an identical ingredient set are not variants of one thing. They are different things whose notation cannot tell them apart, and the difference is carried entirely by the bond."*
- Fig 12 :111: *"A vocabulary without bonds can name a state and cannot name a route to one."*
- Fig 12 :125: *"A composition costs in two currencies, and they do not add."*

**Verdict (D5, `general`, triangulated across Fig 12 and Fig 9).** Naming the four channels records the ingredients and not the arrangement. Knowing that a polity has money, lobbying, media and networks tells you nothing about which bond joins them. A catalogue of the four is a flat formula.

**Refuter.** Exhibit two cases with the same four channels and the same currencies but different connecting bonds that produce the same concentration against a ground, or show that a vocabulary holding only the four atoms can already name a route to concentration. Either result makes the bond free and carries nothing.

### F3: The union contains a cycle

**Figure lines.**

- Fig 12 :121: *"cycle. The output re-enters as input. The sign of the loop decides whether it closes, and a chain cannot close by construction."*
- Fig 12 :119: *"catalyst. A part that survives its own use. Spent and regenerating parts behave differently across uses, and nothing records which a term is."*

**Verdict (D5, `specified`, single-source for the figure definition, world-corroborated but not grounded).** The plausible loop: money funds elections and friendly media; media shapes the information environment in which votes and donations occur; elected officials and regulators become nodes in professional networks; network ties route access and donations back to candidates and to the next cycle. The output re-enters as input. Some parts survive their own use: a reputation, a board seat, a relationship, which is the catalyst reading. The sign of the loop is not measured here, so whether it closes and amplifies rather than damps is a `specified` prediction.

**Refuter.** Break one edge with a real instrument (full public financing, a strict and enforced revolving-door ban, and media pluralism enforcement together) and show that concentration against a ground does not fall. Or show the loop has negative sign, so it is a damper rather than an amplifier.

### F4: Contact cascades beyond direct touch

**Figure lines.**

- Fig 11 :43-44: *"The habitat is enumerated, never composed. List everything in contact with the residual, then what is in contact with those, and outward until the cascade stops mattering."*
- Fig 11 :52-53: *"And contact cascades. Scoping the habitat to direct contact is how DDT reached the eagles: first-order enumeration, second-order effect, and the residual that mattered was three steps out."*
- Fig 11 :49: *"An actor's valence is relative to the residual, not a property it carries."*

**Verdict (`general` from the figure's own DDT example, `specified` as applied here; triangulated with the ecology world source).** Scoping to direct bribery or a direct donation is first-order enumeration. The residual that matters can be several steps out. Whether a given actor is a benefactor or malfactor depends on the residual, so there is no standing list of good and bad channels.

**Refuter.** A habitat census that stops at direct contact and still predicts the concentration against a ground, meaning second and third order effects are zero. That would remove the cascade claim.

### F5: The four channels share an evidence pool, so their agreement is not corroboration

**Figure lines.**

- Fig 2 :31-32: *"Independence, between two frames, is how little they draw on the same evidence. Two people reading the same report are not two frames; two instruments pointed at the same thing are."*
- Fig 2 :39: *"Correlation has valence."*
- Fig 2 :40: *"disqualifying for a verdict: agreement about the world is worthless when the agreeing frames share an evidence pool"*
- Fig 2 :41: *"efficient for a convention: agreement about a shared meaning is the product, not a corruption of it"*
- Fig 7 :12-13: *"same closure, handed along; agreement compounds, evidence does not"*
- Fig 8 :47: *"Independence is one quantity read twice."*
- Fig 8 :50: *"But shared atoms are not the signature. Two frames can hold the same atom by convergence, reaching it separately, which is corroboration at maximum independence."*

**Verdict (D5, `general`, triangulated across Fig 2, Fig 7 and Fig 8).** Four indicators that all move together may be one pool wearing four names, not four witnesses. Adding a fifth indicator that tracks the same pool raises vocabulary and adds no evidence. Note the exception the figures themselves allow: two channels could converge on the same conclusion from separate evidence, which is corroboration at maximum independence. Only the record of where each came from separates convergence from a shared pool (Fig 8 :51-52). This is why `10_source_ledger.csv` records overlap for every source.

**Refuter.** Find two channels with genuinely low overlap, established by provenance, that disagree about the concentration in a way a ground settles. That would show independence and break the one-pool reading.

### F6: The compound has no anchor (this is the `gap`)

**Figure lines.**

- Fig 2 :29-30: *"An anchor is whatever settles the question and does not change its answer because of what any frame thinks."*
- Fig 11 :73: *"The habitat is not the ground. The habitat is where actors come from and it updates; the ground is what settles, and it does not."*
- Fig 3 :27: *"A link with no instrument is not a link that works. It is a link nobody has looked at."*
- Fig 9 :51: *"what the verifier may see: evidence bearing on the residual is admissible; evidence bearing only on its maker is a stake-inducing channel, and the verifier is denied it"*
- Fig 8 :39: *"So a search that finds nothing is never a proof of absence."*
- Fig 13 :134: *"Unreachable is a verdict here, not a silence,"*

**Verdict (D5, `gap` after re-census; D1 to D4 individually `grounded` for their own narrow measurements).** Each single channel has an anchor: elections, votes, filings, board records. Those anchors do not change because a frame thinks differently. The joint quantity, how much political power concentrates because of the four together, has no instrument that settles it. Attention to the four is a habitat census, not a ground. By the anchor test, D5 is not yet a domain that can be settled; it is a union with an owed objective. "Unreachable is a verdict, not a silence" (Fig 13 :134; Fig 8 :39).

What a missing discriminator would have to look like, in effect terms (a shape, not a name): an instrument that returns whether observed policy outcomes track the preferences of the concentrated few more than those of the median citizen, and that does not move when any of the four channels pushes on it. The political-science literature records attempts in this direction, but they are contested and are world sources, not grounds here.

**Refuter.** Exhibit an instrument already returning a value for the joint quantity, show it does not move when any frame pushes on it (Fig 2 :29-30), and report that the value shows no excess concentration. Then D5 becomes groundable and the `gap` closes. Alternatively, show the compound quantity is already composed inside the four-channel closure and merely unfound (Fig 8 :40-43), in which case the remedy is more search, not a new instrument.

## 3. Pre-registered refuter

Written before the conclusion, per the local seat-map rule that a falsifier written before the run is a design instrument. The shape claim is refuted if any of the following holds:

1. A settled study shows the joint effect of the four channels on a ground is additive, with no interaction term in every pair.
2. A single-channel lever, applied alone, removes concentration against a ground that moves only when concentration moves.
3. An instrument exists that measures the joint quantity, does not move when any channel pushes on it, and returns a value showing no excess concentration.
4. The four channels are shown to be independent, with separate evidence pools established by provenance, so the union has no surplus and the compound reading is empty.

If any fires, F1 to F3 are withdrawn and the additive model stands. Until one is run, no part of the compound claim may be labelled `grounded`.
