**Residual restated as an effect.** Four channels each show measurable individual effects, yet the concentration of political power observed where they coexist exceeds what any single channel predicts, and a reform that closes one channel often leaves concentration standing. The residual is therefore the interaction among the four, not any one channel's main effect. Option A: treat them as four independent additive causes, which predicts that a single-channel lever is sufficient. Option B: treat them as a union compound whose concentration is carried by the bonds and the cycle between them, which predicts that a single-channel lever clears only the non-overlap region and that the compound quantity lacks a ground. The files below derive B and state what would refute it.

## What is deliberately not here

- No personal emails of named individuals.
- No ranking of researchers by how likely they are to agree.
- No bulk-send plan.

Three reasons, in the figures' own words. First, a room is not an anchor: Fig 2 :35 "Alignment is triangulation, not negotiation. A mirror cannot correct you." Second, selecting recipients for receptiveness manufactures the disqualifying correlation: Fig 2 :41 "efficient for a convention: agreement about a shared meaning is the product, not a corruption of it." Third, asking a checker to absorb the framework before judging spends the only independence they contribute: Fig 10 :42 "A verifier that can reconstruct the claim cannot verify it."

Legally, public institutional and opt-in channels avoid the GDPR and CAN-SPAM style failure that comes with scraping personal addresses for unsolicited bulk outreach. That is a practical failure on top of the structural one.
