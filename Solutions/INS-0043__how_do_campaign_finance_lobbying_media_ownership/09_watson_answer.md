## QUESTION

Decide, in effect terms, what the Tether says about the proposition that campaign finance, lobbying, media ownership and professional networks interact to concentrate political power. Option A: they are four independent additive causes, so a single-channel lever is sufficient. Option B: they are a union compound whose concentration is carried by bonds and a cycle, so a single lever clears only the non-overlap region and the joint quantity lacks a ground. What each leads to: A predicts that more money-data and one reform close the problem; B predicts persistent intersections, a cascade beyond direct contact, one evidence pool rather than four witnesses, and a `gap` at the objective link of the dependency chain.

## DOMAIN CENSUS

R by arity, symmetry and scale, then one line per domain with actors / currency / anchor, partition or union, and order by dependency.

- **R (the residual).** Concentration exceeds the sum of separate channel effects, and single-channel levers leave it standing. Arity: four conditions, so a union compound, not four independent problems (Fig 9). Symmetry: the channels carry different currencies, so they are not interchangeable operands and the bond at each junction matters (Fig 12). Scale: individual, office or organization, institution, polity (Fig 4). Fig 9 :55-56 *"A bundled problem is usually a union of overlapping conditions, not a list of separate ones. A single lever clears the region where only one condition holds and leaves the intersections standing, because the second condition still binds."*
- **Domain 1, campaign finance.** Actors: donors, PACs, candidates, election commissions. Currency: money. Anchor: filed contribution records and election outcomes, which do not move because a frame thinks. Part of the union.
- **Domain 2, lobbying.** Actors: lobbyists, legislators, regulators, agencies. Currency: access, information, drafted language. Anchor: recorded votes, proposed and final rules, contract awards. Part of the union.
- **Domain 3, media ownership.** Actors: owners, editors, platforms, audiences. Currency: attention and framing. Anchor: partly, content and ownership registries; the effect on political power is not anchored. Part of the union.
- **Domain 4, professional networks.** Actors: individuals, firms, schools, clubs, revolving-door personnel. Currency: trust and repeated contact. Anchor: public bios, board memberships, revolving-door records. Part of the union.
- **Domain 5, the compound.** Actors: the four as frames. Currency: influence. Anchor: **none for the joint quantity.** It is a union with its own loop and an owed objective. Fig 11 :43 *"The habitat is enumerated, never composed."*
- **Order by dependency.** Perception of each channel, then vocabulary for what it affords, then the objective for the compound (the break), then composition, then settlement. Refuse the additive reading before minting anything new. Describe the residual before choosing a frame.

## VERDICT (from the figures)

The shape survives, the additive model does not. Four channels form a union compound whose concentration is carried by typed bonds and plausibly by a cycle of unmeasured sign; contacts cascade beyond direct touch; the four indicators may be one evidence pool rather than four witnesses; report per slot. The compound quantity has no ground, so that part of the answer is a `gap`, not a failure and not a completed measurement. This is `general` on the shape, `specified` on the cycle and bonds, and `gap` on the anchor. The strongest checkable point is structural: a single lever clears only the non-overlap region.

- **cost if wrong:** bounded and locatable. The packet states mode, refuters and the live strain before the conclusion, so an error is findable. The real cost is spending a checker's independence by sending a finished conclusion instead of a characterised residual. Fig 2 :42 "Buy independence in proportion to the cost of being wrong." That cost is avoidable by leading with the residual and the refuter.
- **undoable:** yes. The cycle and bond assignments are proposals; the union shape and the per-channel anchors are unaffected if they fail. Nothing in the framework has to be withdrawn for the empirical claim to be corrected, and a sent claim can be superseded by a corrected packet.
- **reversed by:** a ground reading, not agreement. An instrument that returns a value for the joint quantity and does not move when any channel pushes on it, combined with a finding of no excess concentration, would close the gap against the claim. A settled study showing additive effects with no interaction term would remove the union verdict. A negative loop sign would keep the shape but reverse the policy implication. A demonstration that the four draw on independent evidence pools would remove the one-pool reading. Prestige or repetition is not any of these.
