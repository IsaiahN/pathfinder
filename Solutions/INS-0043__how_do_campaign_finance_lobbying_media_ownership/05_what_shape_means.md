## 1. A shape is a constraint, not a filler

The local canonical figures README gives the limit: the framework returns *"what a missing thing would have to look like (a shape, not a name)"*. Fig 9 :32: *"you get a kind of thing, never the answer itself"*. Fig 13 :138-139: *"you get a kind of thing, never the answer itself"* is repeated there. Fig 13 :134: *"Unreachable is a verdict here, not a silence,"*

For this question a **shape** is the form the answer must have before you can name the ruling bond or the missing instrument. Four slots plus a union and a cycle is a shape. "Campaign finance is the cause" is a name. "The network structure is the cause" is also a name, and in this case a strained one (see §5).

From *They're Scanning Books* (opened this session): *"A minimum viable set of six terms is a human's careful guess at reality's compression axes; another sensorium's minimum set need not be those six."* The four political channels are likewise a human's census of an institutional habitat, not nature's own list.

## 2. How the shape is obtained here

- **Describe the effect, not the cause.** Fig 9 :30-31: *"your words are borrowed too, so describe the effect rather than the cause"* and Fig 9 :27-28: *"describe it before you go looking, or any frame you pick will seem to fit"*. So the first move is the residual in effect terms: concentration exceeds the sum, and single-channel levers leave it standing.
- **Enumerate the habitat.** Fig 11 :43-44: *"The habitat is enumerated, never composed. List everything in contact with the residual, then what is in contact with those, and outward until the cascade stops mattering."* The four channels and their overlaps are read off the world, then extended outward until the cascade stops mattering.
- **Do not aggregate.** Fig 1 :19: *"The residual is indexed by object slot, not aggregated."* The shape keeps four slots and names the intersections separately. A single "power concentration" number throws away the only signal that says where to intervene.
- **Follow dependency order.** Fig 3 :20-21: *"The chain is ordered by dependency, not by importance. Each link consumes what the one above produces, so effort spent above the break arrives at a step that cannot use it."* The break is at the objective for the compound, not at the measurement of money.

From *They're Scanning Books* (opened this session): *"The real import is not a new term in the existing space. It is a new dimension of the space."* What D5 owes is a new axis of measurement, not a fifth channel to add to the list.

## 4. The metamerism error is the specific failure here

*The Hard Problem is About Source* (opened this session) gives the mechanism. An instrument reads green and *"cannot comment on the source"*. The same reading can come from one green light, yellow plus blue, RGB, CMYK, or a scheme not yet discovered. *"If several paths create the same behavior, then behavior is a projection with lossy information"*. And: *"An instrument that can't distinguish these cases demonstrates its own inadequacy."* It was the spectrometer, not an argument, that resolved it.

Applied here: if four indicators all rise together, reading the source as "elite capture" from that single projection is the metamerism error. The four could be one pool, or four independent mechanisms that converge. *"You may all agree and be wrong."* (*The Recursive Self-Improvement Trap*, opened this session.) What is owed is a discriminator, not a name. This is F6 in `01_figure_grounded_audit.md`.

## 5. Correction to my own first reading of "network"

I initially wanted to say that the figure's structure "network" (Fig 13 :24, *"that entities relate, and influence travels"*) identifies the professional-networks channel and therefore explains the other three. That is retracted. Fig 13 governs the minimum set of a composing system, not a taxonomy of political institutions. The resemblance is a binding from the institutional frame onto a held structure, and must be recorded as provenance with `specified` mode. It is not a derivation and it does not move any wall (Fig 6 :26: *"An import adds no atom to the world."*). Both the first reading and the correction stay visible here.

A second correction: I first treated the four channels as four independent witnesses whose agreement would strengthen the claim. Fig 2 :40 forbids that: *"disqualifying for a verdict: agreement about the world is worthless when the agreeing frames share an evidence pool"*. Withdrawn. Note the figure's own exception: Fig 8 :50 *"Two frames can hold the same atom by convergence, reaching it separately, which is corroboration at maximum independence."* Convergence is possible; we have not shown it.

## 6. Prestige is not action

From *The Recursive Self-Improvement Trap* (opened this session): *"Prestige must never be allowed to validate an action directly."* The essay adds that prestige can fund an action, giving it a budget to be tried, but cannot settle it; only the ground can settle it.

For this field the failure is familiar: a claim about concentrated power gets a budget when famous institutions repeat it, and the repetition is then treated as evidence that the claim is true. The correct ask is the opposite: give the shape claim a budget to be tried by someone outside the pool, let the ground settle it, and do not count later agreement as corroboration (Fig 2 :40).

## 7. My audit is a simulation, not a ground reading

From *The Recursive Self-Improvement Trap* (opened this session): *"the output is only as good as the validator. The agent, or the tool, or the frame, or the theory, cannot validate itself."* And: *"LLMs can quickly simulate an approximate answer/output with the formula"*.

So `01_figure_grounded_audit.md` is a simulated derivation checked for internal consistency against the figures. It is not a test of the claim, and it cannot settle anything it reports on. The `gap` for D5 is not a weakness in the writing; it is the honest limit of what the available instruments distinguish, and it is recorded with its lineage.
