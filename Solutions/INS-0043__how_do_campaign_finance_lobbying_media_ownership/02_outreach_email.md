# Outreach draft: check the shape claim before you decide about the framework

**Use:** one-to-one, or as a cover letter attached to a submission through the public venues in `03_public_contact_points.csv`. **Not** for bulk send.

**Hard precondition:** do not send until the open items in `01_figure_grounded_audit.md` (the sign of the cycle and the missing anchor) are stated as open, not as solved. The offer collapses on contact if the claim is presented as settled.

---

**Subject:** One checkable claim about how four influence channels compound

> Hello,
>
> I work on political influence channels as a research topic. I have one specific output I would like you to check against what you already know. I am not asking you to read the framework first, and I am not asking you to agree with it. If the output does not survive, there is nothing else to look at.
>
> **The residual, in effect terms.** Campaign finance, lobbying, media ownership and professional networks each have measurable individual effects. Yet where all four coexist, observed political power concentration exceeds what their separate effects predict, and closing one channel often leaves concentration standing. The residual is the interaction, not any one channel.
>
> **The claim, stated with its mode (`general` on the shape, `specified` on the cycle, `gap` for the joint measure).** These four are not four independent causes that add. They are a union of overlapping conditions, so the concentration is carried by the bonds between them and by a loop in which the output re-enters as input: money funds elections and friendly coverage; coverage shapes the information environment in which votes and donations occur; officials and regulators become network nodes; network ties route access and money back into the next cycle. Contacts cascade several steps out, so a direct-contact census misses the residual that matters. The four also tend to move together, which means they may be one evidence pool rather than four independent witnesses, so their agreement cannot be counted as corroboration.
>
> **What the claim explicitly does not say.** It does not say which bond dominates, does not sign the loop as amplifying, does not name a single structure as the cause, does not reduce the four to one power score, and does not claim that the joint quantity has been measured. There is no ground for that joint quantity that I can identify. That is recorded as a gap, not hidden. Election results, votes, filings and board seats are grounds for the individual channels, but not for the compound.
>
> **What would refute it. I wrote these down before sending this.**
> 1. A settled study showing the joint effect of the four channels on a ground is additive, with no interaction term in any pair.
> 2. A single-channel lever, applied alone, removes concentration against a ground that moves only when concentration moves.
> 3. An instrument that measures the joint quantity, does not move when any channel pushes on it, and returns a value showing no excess concentration.
> 4. Evidence that the four draw on separate pools, so the union has no surplus.
>
> If any holds, I withdraw the compound claim and keep only the per-channel findings. A one-line "this fails because..." is more useful to me than agreement. If you would rather not engage, no reply is needed.
>
> **What I would actually like from you:** what would you count as a failure of this shape claim? I will record your verdict before showing you any framework material, and I will not pool a later agreement as corroboration.
>
> **If it survives, and only then,** the public sources are below. The figures, not my summary of them, are the authority.
> - Verbatim figure text with line numbers (local canonical extract): `FRAMEWORK\reference\03-FIGURES_TEXT.md`
> - Canonical usage rule (local): `FRAMEWORK\reference\FIGURES_README_canonical.md`
> - Public political finance data and research: https://www.opensecrets.org/
>
> **If you want to reuse the method:** the eight-step loop is public in the framework. Run it on a problem you know well and can check against a ground. Write down what would refute your result before you run it, and state the mode of what you get. A reported failure is more useful to me than a reported success.
