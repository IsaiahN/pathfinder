# Expert verification packet: the Tether on four political influence channels

**What this is:** a claim in a form a political economist, institutional scholar or network scientist can check. Mode, derivation and refuters are stated before the conclusion, so a reader knows what to look for and what would settle it.

**Mode: `general` on the shape, `specified` on the cycle and bond assignments, `gap` for the compound anchor. Not `grounded`.** No measurement of the joint quantity is claimed. This is a shape and a route, not a result.

**Status: simulated derivation, not a ground reading.** It cannot settle the question it reports on.

## 1. The claim, in one paragraph

Campaign finance, lobbying, media ownership and professional networks are not four additive causes. They form a union of overlapping conditions whose concentration is carried by the bonds between them and by a feedback loop in which money, coverage, official access and network ties feed one another. A lever that closes one channel clears only the region where that channel acted alone. The four indicators share an evidence pool, so their agreement is not four independent witnesses. The compound quantity itself has no instrument that settles it; each individual channel does. What is offered is the shape any candidate answer must have, refuters written in advance, and the kind of discriminator that would close the gap.

## 2. Three checkable sub-claims

### C1: framework-internal and structural; checkable by reading Figures 9 and 1

- **Statement:** the four channels are a union compound, not an additive list, and must be reported per slot rather than as one aggregate score.
- **Source, verbatim:** Fig 9 :55-56 *"A bundled problem is usually a union of overlapping conditions, not a list of separate ones. A single lever clears the region where only one condition holds and leaves the intersections standing, because the second condition still binds."* Also Fig 9 :57-58 *"a case in two conditions is not a harder case of either. It stops being a mixture and becomes a specific compound."* And Fig 1 :22 *"Aggregating across slots is how a live signal disappears into an average."*
- **Refuter:** a settled study showing the joint effect on a ground is additive, with no interaction term in every pair, and that a single-channel lever removes concentration. If that holds, withdraw the union claim.

### C2: framework-internal derivation; checkable by reading Figure 12

- **Statement:** the concentration is carried by the typed bonds and the arrangement, not by the four channels as ingredients. Two cases with the same four channels but different bonds are isomers in the figure's sense; two cases with the same functional group but different occupants are a weaker, functional-group claim.
- **Source, verbatim:** Fig 12 :5 *"A set of ingredients is not one. The bond is the rest of the information, and it is the half nothing was recording."* Fig 12 :94 *"The arrangement is the substance."* Fig 12 :117 *"Same atoms, different bond, different substance. The difference is invisible in the formula and decisive in the behaviour."* Fig 12 :123 *"the group says what kind; the qualifier says which member."*
- **Refuter:** exhibit two cases with the same four channels and different connecting bonds that produce the same concentration against a ground, or show that a vocabulary holding only the four atoms can already name a route to concentration. Then the bond carries nothing.

### C3: world-facing and the live open item; checkable by a political economist or institutional scholar

- **Statement:** the arrangement plausibly contains a feedback loop whose sign is unmeasured, the effects cascade several steps out, and the joint quantity has no ground while the individual channels do.
- **Source, verbatim:** Fig 12 :121 *"cycle. The output re-enters as input. The sign of the loop decides whether it closes, and a chain cannot close by construction."* Fig 11 :52-53 *"And contact cascades. Scoping the habitat to direct contact is how DDT reached the eagles: first-order enumeration, second-order effect, and the residual that mattered was three steps out."* Fig 2 :29-30 *"An anchor is whatever settles the question and does not change its answer because of what any frame thinks."*
- **What to check:** is the loop positive or negative? Does a gradient exist such that a combined lever moves a policy-outcome measure more than any single lever? Is there any instrument that returns a value for the joint quantity and does not move when any channel pushes on it?
- **Refuter:** a negative loop sign (keeps the shape, reverses the policy implication); a chain with no re-entry (withdraw the cycle claim); or an instrument for the joint quantity that returns no excess concentration (close the gap against the claim).

## 3. What is explicitly not claimed

- Not a theory of everything, and not a complete account of political power. There is no last room.
- Not that money, or media, or networks is the single cause. The framework returns a shape, not a name.
- Not that the four are corrupt by nature. Their valence is relative to the residual (Fig 11 :49), so the same channel can be a benefactor for one residual and a malfactor for another.
- Not that the four indicators independently corroborate one another. Shared evidence pool disqualifies agreement as a verdict (Fig 2 :40).
- Not that the compound is measured. That is a `gap`.
- Not `grounded`. Nothing here is asserted as settled by experiment.
- The `⋛` comparison applied to institutions rather than quantities is a type strain, named rather than hidden.

## 5. What would settle it

A discriminator and a ground test, not a further argument. Ordered by cost:

1. **Anchor:** name an instrument that returns whether policy outcomes track concentrated preferences more than median preferences, and show it does not move when any of the four channels pushes on it. From *The Hard Problem is About Source* (opened this session): an instrument that reads green *"cannot comment on the source"*; the spectrometer resolved it.
2. **Interaction:** test whether the joint effect exceeds the sum of the four single effects against that instrument. This distinguishes the union claim from the additive model.
3. **Sign:** measure whether the loop amplifies or damps. This decides whether the policy implication holds or flips.
4. **Lever:** compare a combined lever with each single lever against the instrument.

## 6. What an expert can check in twenty minutes

2. Read Fig 12 :5, :94 and :117. Decide whether the arrangement, not the ingredient list, is where the explanatory work would have to be.
3. From your own field, name one instrument that could return a value for the joint quantity and would not move when the channels push on it, or say why none exists.
4. Say which of the four refuters in C1 to C3 you would fire first, and why.
5. Send the refutation. A failure is more useful than an endorsement. A reply that says "this convinces me" carries no verdict weight.
