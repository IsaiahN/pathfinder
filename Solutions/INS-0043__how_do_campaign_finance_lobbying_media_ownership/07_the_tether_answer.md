# The Tether's answer: how four influence channels concentrate political power

**Mode: `general` on the shape, `specified` on the cycle and the bond assignments, `gap` for the anchor of the compound quantity. Not `grounded`.** This is a simulated derivation checked against the figures (`05_what_shape_means.md` §7), not a ground reading.

## 1. The residual it answers

The four channels each have measurable individual effects, but where they coexist the concentration exceeds what their separate effects predict, and closing one channel often leaves it standing. The residual is the interaction. Fig 9 :55-56: *"A bundled problem is usually a union of overlapping conditions, not a list of separate ones. A single lever clears the region where only one condition holds and leaves the intersections standing, because the second condition still binds."*

## 2. The shape

1. **It is a union compound, not four additive causes.** The four overlap: finance x media, lobbying x networks, finance x lobbying, media x networks. A case in two conditions is categorically different from a case in one (Fig 9 :57-58). This is why single-channel reform disappoints.
2. **The concentration is carried by the bonds, not by the channels.** Fig 12 :94: *"The arrangement is the substance."* Naming the four is a flat formula; knowing which relation holds at each junction is the missing half (Fig 12 :5).
3. **The union plausibly contains a cycle.** Money funds elections and friendly coverage; coverage shapes the information environment in which votes and donations occur; officials and regulators become network nodes; network ties route access and money back into the next cycle. Fig 12 :121: *"cycle. The output re-enters as input. The sign of the loop decides whether it closes, and a chain cannot close by construction."* The sign is unmeasured, so whether this amplifies is a `specified` prediction.
4. **Contact cascades several steps out.** Fig 11 :52-53: *"And contact cascades. Scoping the habitat to direct contact is how DDT reached the eagles: first-order enumeration, second-order effect, and the residual that mattered was three steps out."* A direct-bribery census is first-order enumeration.
5. **The four are not four independent witnesses.** Fig 2 :40: *"disqualifying for a verdict: agreement about the world is worthless when the agreeing frames share an evidence pool"*. If they move together, their agreement may be one pool talking. Convergence from separate evidence remains possible, but only provenance separates it (Fig 8 :51-52).
6. **Report four slots, never one score.** Fig 1 :22: *"Aggregating across slots is how a live signal disappears into an average."* A global power index near zero can coexist with one live channel carrying all the mass.
7. **The joint quantity has no ground.** Each single channel has an anchor (elections, votes, filings, board seats). The compound does not. Fig 2 :29-30: *"An anchor is whatever settles the question and does not change its answer because of what any frame thinks."* This is a `gap`, not a silence.

## 3. What it explicitly does not say

- Not that money, or media, or networks is "the" cause. The framework returns a shape, not a name.
- Not that the cycle is positive. The sign is open and would flip the recommendation if negative.
- Not that the four channels corrupt politics by a single mechanism. They are a functional group of influence routes with different currencies (Fig 12 :123).
- Not that a five-channel list would close the problem. More compositions inside the same closure add no atom (Fig 6 :21: *"MINT composes inside the closure and can never add an atom."*).
- Not `grounded`. No instrument settles the compound quantity here.

## 4. What would settle it: the ground

A discriminator, not more argument. Fig 8 :39: *"So a search that finds nothing is never a proof of absence."* The questions:

- **The anchor question:** is there an instrument that returns whether observed policy outcomes track the preferences of the concentrated few more than those of the median citizen, and that does not move when any of the four channels pushes on it? (*The Hard Problem is About Source*: an instrument that reads green *"cannot comment on the source"*; the spectrometer resolved it.)
- **The interaction test:** does the joint effect of the four channels on that instrument exceed the sum of the four single-channel effects, with interaction terms in at least one pair? This is what would promote the union claim from `general` toward `specified` at the ground.
- **The lever test:** does a combined lever (public financing plus enforced revolving-door rules plus media pluralism) move the instrument more than any single lever, and does a single lever clear only the non-overlap region?
- **The sign test:** does the cycle amplify or damp? A negative sign would keep the shape but reverse the policy implication.
- **Standing falsifiers from `01`:** additive joint effect with no interaction term in any pair; a single-channel lever that removes concentration; an instrument showing no excess concentration; demonstrated independence of the four evidence pools.

## 5. The honest stop

**Unreached, not unreachable.** The four channels are a habitat census, not nature's own list. From *They're Scanning Books*: *"Declaring the primitives does not make them neutral. It makes the choice visible."* The answer stops at the limit of the available instruments and records the lineage so the stop is checkable. The local canonical README: *"Indiscernibility is not identity."* One strain (`⋛` applied to institutions rather than quantities) and one open item (the cycle sign) are named here and in `11_audit.md`, not smoothed over.

## 6. Why the common "elite consensus" story fails the figures

A claim that a small cohesive elite controls everything is often supported by pointing to the same four channels and noting that their leaders know one another. Fig 2 :35: *"Alignment is triangulation, not negotiation. A mirror cannot correct you."* Interlocking board data show connectivity, which is a habitat fact; they do not by themselves show that a ground moved. *"You may all agree and be wrong."* (*The Recursive Self-Improvement Trap*, opened this session.) The legitimate claim is narrower: the arrangement creates a union compound whose interaction is owed a measurement. That claim is checkable and can fail.

## 7. One-paragraph version for outreach

> The Tether does not name a single cause of political power concentration. It says that campaign finance, lobbying, media ownership and professional networks form a union of overlapping conditions, so the concentration is carried by the bonds between them rather than by any one channel. A reform that closes one channel clears only the region where it acted alone. The arrangement plausibly contains a cycle in which money, coverage, official access and network ties feed one another, and its effects cascade several steps beyond direct contact. The four indicators also share an evidence pool, so their moving together is not four independent witnesses. What the framework cannot supply is the measure of the joint quantity: each channel has a ground, the compound does not. That is a gap, recorded rather than filled. What is offered is the shape any answer must have, four refuters written in advance, and the kind of instrument that would close it.
