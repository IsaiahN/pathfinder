## 0. The claim under audit and the residual

> **Question:** What is the correct account of information in systems far from thermodynamic equilibrium?

The definite article is the load-bearing term. "The correct account" presupposes that physical entropy, Shannon entropy, mutual information, algorithmic complexity and structural information are all instances of one thing that can be defined once and settled. The residual is the failure of that presupposition to close: each candidate measure works on a different subset and keeps working there no matter how hard the others are pushed.

Stated as an effect: we need an account that (i) applies where a system is held away from equilibrium by persistent gradients, flows and dissipation, (ii) says what is being measured and in what units, (iii) states the frame and the ground it ranges against, and (iv) carries the arrangement, not only the inventory. The decision is between **one account** (option A) and **a union of well-formed accounts with a shared shape** (option B).

The sought-for shape, written down as an ordinary term before the run (Formula, "Direction"): *a per-slot residual against a ground, generated and degraded by a spendable gradient, priced in two incommensurable currencies, and carried by bonds; no single name until a ground settles one.*

## 2. Domain census (arity, symmetry, scale, then one line per domain)

**R by arity / symmetry / scale.**

- *Arity:* "information" is invoked in at least three relations: a state's entropy (one operand), a correlation between subsystems or between system and frame (two operands), and a typed arrangement of parts (k operands). Arity is therefore at least 2 and variable. A single scalar cannot carry a relation.
- *Symmetry:* Shannon entropy and mutual information are symmetric under relabeling and operand swap; information *about a ground* is directional (frame ranges against anchor, not the reverse, Fig 2). Any account that treats these as one symmetry has mis-censused.
- *Scale:* microscopic states (phase-space cells, qubits), mesoscopic flows and fluctuations, and the observer/frame scale at which a residual is read. Fig 4 requires a lossy transform between scales and a measured gap.

**One line per domain (actors / currency / anchor).**

- **D1 Thermodynamic and statistical-physical domain.** Actors: energy, spectrum, gradient, network, space, time (the six); macrostates, flows, fluctuations. Currency: spendable gradient, dissipation, residual returning as noise. Anchor: thermodynamic law and measurement. Nameable anchor exists, so this is a domain.
- **D2 Coding and communication domain.** Actors: source, channel, receiver, code, probability model. Currency: bits, entropy, channel capacity. Anchor: the actual symbol statistics, a declared codebook. Nameable anchor exists, so this is a domain.
- **D3 Frame / residual domain (the loop's own account).** Actors: Γ, belief b, action a, observation o′, residual R, the ground. Currency: the prediction-observation gap per slot. Anchor: the ground. Nameable anchor exists, so this is a domain.
- **D4 Structural / compositional domain.** Actors: atoms, bonds, molecules. Currency: the information carried by the typed arrangement. Anchor: whether a composition is settled against the ground in D1 to D3. Derivative domain, not independent, because its anchor is borrowed.
- **D5 Verification / outreach domain.** Actors: expert readers, this derivation. Currency: agreement versus residual. Anchor: the ground, never agreement. In this simulated run all five domains draw on one evidence pool, so D5's independence is zero.

**Partition or union.** Union. The question bundles four conditions (physical, quantitative, frame-relative, structural). Fig 9 :54: "when the split lands on a union rather than a partition, unbundle before you search again." Library of Babel: "Perhaps your problem was a bundle or conflation of several problems masquerading as one? You will need to search all of those problems individually to find their answers and then combine them in a satisfactory way." A single lever clears the region where one condition holds and leaves the intersections standing.

**Order by dependency (Fig 3).** Settle D1 first (what a nonequilibrium system is: gradient that can be spent, flow, noise), then D2 (the measure), then D3 (the frame-relative residual), then D4 (the bond that carries it), then D5 (the check). Optimising D2 cannot rescue a broken D1.

### F2 - Information, in the loop, is residual R, per slot, against a ground

Symbols table :38 "The gap between prediction and observation. The central quantity". Fig 1 :19:

> **"The residual is indexed by object slot, not aggregated."**

Fig 1 :23 "The ground enters as the only metric." Recursive Self-Improvement Trap: "The Tether ties alignment to the ground. Not to human preferences... To the thing that settles a question regardless of what any frame thinks. An outcome. A measurement."

So the account is frame-relative in the precise sense that it is a gap between what Γ predicts and what o′ returns, measured per slot. It is not a frame-invariant substance, and it is not a global average (Fig 1 :22 "Aggregating across slots is how a live signal disappears into an average").

- **Refuter:** a proposed account that defines information without any frame, ground or slot, and still settles which slot is live, refutes F2.

### F3 - Far from equilibrium is a spendable gradient; the remainder returns as noise

Fig 13 :14 "conserved, finite, directionless alone" (energy). Fig 13 :22:

> **"a difference that can be spent"** (gradient)

Fig 13 :75-76:

> **"Energy and a gradient compose a flow."**

Fig 13 :31-32:

> **"a remainder compounding through a chain that amplifies is chaos, and the detail a crossing discards reappears above it as noise."**

Equilibrium is the limit in which no gradient can be spent: no flow, no crossing, no new residual arriving. Far from equilibrium, the same crossing that drives the flow discards detail that reappears as noise and chaos. Information is therefore generated and degraded by one process, not two. The world corroborates: Wolpert, *Stochastic thermodynamics of computation* (arXiv:1905.05669, 2019), "One of the most prominent aspects of computers is that they are inherently nonequilibrium systems."

- **Verdict D1:** gradient/flow/noise account. **single-source** for the figure (Fig 13 only), with one low-overlap world corroboration (Wolpert). Not triangulated inside the figures.
- **Refuter:** a far-from-equilibrium system with no spendable gradient, or a crossing whose discarded detail does not reappear as noise anywhere, refutes F3.

### F4 - Bits and actions are two currencies and they do not add

Fig 12 :126:

> **"Bits price the description. Actions price finding out whether it holds. They are in different units, so a system holding one accounting cannot compare them"**

Fig 5 :45:

> **"This is minimum description length, Rissanen 1978, which is itself a composition of Kolmogorov complexity and Shannon coding put to work on model selection."**

The figures cite Shannon, Kolmogorov and Rissanen as *borrowed machinery*, with the provenance written into the line. They are not derived from the six-term set. So an "account of information" that prices a description in bits and the thermodynamic cost of settling it in kT ln 2 as if they were one figure commits the flat-formula collapse the figure names. World corroboration: SEP *Information Processing and Thermodynamic Entropy* (Maroney, 2009) "when an argument is only valid for a particular entropy, or when these entropies disagree, it should be clearly stated which entropy is involved"; Wikipedia *Landauer's principle* "there is no a priori relationship between logical reversibility... and thermodynamic reversibility".

- **Verdict D2:** two-currency account. **triangulated** (Fig 12 + Maroney + Wikipedia, with Fig 5 as provenance for the bits side).
- **Refuter:** a single unit that prices both description and settlement without residue, demonstrated rather than asserted, refutes F4.

### F5 - Information is carried by the bond, not by the ingredient list

Fig 12 :5:

> **"A set of ingredients is not one. The bond is the rest of the information, and it is the half nothing was recording."**

Fig 12 :94:

> **"The arrangement is the substance."**

Two systems with the same state inventory but different typed relations carry different information. This is the isomer point developed in `06_isomers_and_order.md`. SEP *Information* supplies a striking world analogue: thermodynamics and information theory "use the same formulas to describe fundamentally different phenomena."

- **Verdict D4:** structural/relational account. **single-source** inside the figures (Fig 12), with a low-overlap world analogue that is corroboration, not a second figure.
- **Refuter:** show that two arrangements over the same atoms with different bonds are behaviourally indistinguishable in every ground test, so the bond carries nothing.

### F6 - The framework returns a shape, not a name: refuse "the correct account"

Fig 7 :6 "the limit: no last room". `FIGURES_README_canonical.md` states the limit: the framework reports "what a missing thing would have to look like (a shape, not a name)", then stops at the limit of the instruments and records the lineage. ## 4. Strain test and re-census

- **Apparent strain:** Fig 13 calls energy "directionless alone", yet F3 says nonequilibrium information is powered by a directed gradient. **Re-census:** energy is a state, gradient is a structure, and the figure itself composes them ("Energy and a gradient compose a flow"). Different objects, so no strain. Matching the canonical rule: a reading that makes two figures contradict has misidentified its domain.
- **Apparent strain:** Fig 5 :45 already names Shannon, Kolmogorov and Rissanen, so is information not already in Γ and no derivation owed? **Re-census:** those are cited borrowings, i.e., imports whose provenance is recorded in the figure itself (source gate satisfied). They enter as atoms for the measure, not as a derivation of the substrate. This *supports* the union reading rather than collapsing it.
- **Apparent strain:** the SEP *Information* entry presents a unification program (Differential Information Theory). **Re-census:** that is a proposed mathematical descriptive framework, not a ground-settled account of the physical nonequilibrium domain. It is an import candidate under Fig 6/8, not a verdict. A conflict sends back to the census, never flips the verdict; the level mismatch (mathematical description vs physical flow) resolves it without changing F1.
- **After re-census: no hidden strain.** The one live type question is whether a residual measured in bits and a thermodynamic cost measured in kT ln 2 can be composed at all; F4 records this as incommensurable and leaves the instrument question open.

## 5. Pre-registered refuter (written before the conclusion)

The shape claim is refuted if any of the following holds:

1. One measure is shown, against a ground, to settle all four bundled conditions with no persistent residual on any slot (kills F1 and F6).
2. An account of information is defined with no frame, ground or slot and still identifies which slot is live (kills F2).
3. A far-from-equilibrium system is demonstrated with no spendable gradient, or a crossing whose discarded detail never returns as noise (kills F3).
4. A single unit is demonstrated that prices description and settlement without residue (kills F4).
5. Two arrangements over the same atoms with different bonds are shown behaviourally identical on every ground test (kills F5).
6. A seventh primitive is derived from the six, or one of the six from the other five (breaks the substrate the shape rests on).

If any fires, the corresponding finding is withdrawn. Until one is run, no part of this may be labelled `grounded`.
