# Expert verification packet - the Tether on information far from equilibrium

**What this is:** a claim in a form a statistical physicist, information theorist or philosopher of information can check. Mode, derivation and refuters are stated before the conclusion, so a reader knows what to look for and what would settle it.

**Mode: `specified` - not `grounded`.** No measurement is claimed. This is a shape and a route, not a result.

**Status: simulated derivation checked for internal consistency against the figures, not a ground reading.** It cannot settle the claim it reports on.

## 1. The claim, in one paragraph

"The correct account of information far from equilibrium" is a union of at least four conditions, not one hard question. The framework's shape has four constraints: information is a per-slot residual against a ground (not a global scalar); far from equilibrium it is a spendable gradient composing with energy into a flow, with discarded detail returning as noise; bits (description cost) and kT ln 2 (settlement cost) are different currencies that do not add; and information is carried by the typed bond, not the ingredient list. No particular entropy is named as "the answer", because the framework returns a shape, not a name. What it offers is the constraints, the refuters written in advance, and the instruments that would close them.

## 2. Three checkable sub-claims

### C1 - framework-internal plus physics-facing; checkable by reading Fig 13 and Fig 1 plus the SEP entries

- **Statement:** the right object is a per-slot residual, and the nonequilibrium driver is a spendable gradient composing with energy into a flow, with discarded detail returning as noise.
- **Source, verbatim:** Fig 1 :19 "The residual is indexed by object slot, not aggregated." Fig 13 :22 "a difference that can be spent". Fig 13 :76 "Energy and a gradient compose a flow." Fig 13 :32 "a remainder compounding through a chain that amplifies is chaos, and the detail a crossing discards reappears above it as noise."
- **What to check:** can any candidate information measure isolate one live slot while global entropy stays near zero? Does a nonequilibrium information measure track the gradient and the returning noise, vanishing at equilibrium?
- **Refuter:** a far-from-equilibrium system with no spendable gradient; a crossing whose discarded detail never returns as noise; or a measure that preserves every slot's live signal. Any one kills this sub-claim.

### C2 - cross-currency; checkable against statistical mechanics

- **Statement:** description cost in bits and settlement cost in kT ln 2 are incommensurable and must be held apart.
- **Source, verbatim:** Fig 12 :126 "Bits price the description. Actions price finding out whether it holds. They are in different units, so a system holding one accounting cannot compare them". Fig 5 :45 "This is minimum description length, Rissanen 1978, which is itself a composition of Kolmogorov complexity and Shannon coding put to work on model selection."
- **World corroboration:** Maroney, SEP *Information Processing and Thermodynamic Entropy*: "when an argument is only valid for a particular entropy, or when these entropies disagree, it should be clearly stated which entropy is involved." Wikipedia, *Landauer's principle*: "there is no a priori relationship between logical reversibility... and thermodynamic reversibility".
- **Refuter:** a single unit that prices description and settlement without residue, with the conversion factor specified and checked at the ground.

### C3 - framework-internal structural claim

- **Statement:** the bond carries information the atom list does not; therefore two different arrangements over the same atoms are different information-bearing systems, and members of the same functional group are not automatically isomers.
- **Source, verbatim:** Fig 12 :5 "A set of ingredients is not one. The bond is the rest of the information, and it is the half nothing was recording." Fig 12 :94 "The arrangement is the substance." Fig 12 :117 "isomer. Same atoms, different bond, different substance. The difference is invisible in the formula and decisive in the behaviour."
- **What to check:** do two accounts written over the full shared six-term atom set, differing only in bond arrangement, behave differently on a ground test? See `06_isomers_and_order.md` for the falsifier.
- **Refuter:** two arrangements over the same atoms with different bonds shown behaviourally identical on every ground test. This kills the bond sub-claim and leaves the functional-group reading only.

## 3. What is explicitly not claimed

- Not a specific entropy as the answer: not Shannon, von Neumann, thermodynamic, Fisher, algorithmic or semantic information.
- Not a derivation of Landauer's bound or a new thermodynamics.
- Not observer-independent information in the abstract, and not a simple observer-dependent thesis. The precise claim is a per-slot gap against a ground.
- Not a single unified theory of information. The union center stays open with its own loops.
- Not `grounded`. No measurement is asserted as settling any of this.

## 5. What would settle it

1. **Slot test:** a candidate measure that isolates a live slot while global entropy stays near zero.
2. **Gradient test:** a quantity that tracks the spendable gradient and returning noise and vanishes at equilibrium.
3. **Currency test:** a report that keeps bits and kT ln 2 in separate accounts, or a demonstrated conversion between them.
4. **Bond test:** two arrangements over the same atom set with different bonds producing different behaviour on a ground test.
5. **Union test:** a single measure that settles all four conditions with no persistent residual on any slot. This one would refute the whole shape; the others refine it.

## 6. What an expert can check in twenty minutes

1. Read Fig 1 :19, Fig 13 :22, :76, :32, Fig 12 :5, :94, :126 - seven short passages.
2. Check C1 against a driven system you know: is there a live slot and a spendable gradient, and does discarded detail return as noise?
3. Check C2 against Landauer and Landauer-style experiments: are the description cost and the erasure cost in different units?
4. Name one instrument that would discriminate a live slot from a global average, or say why none is possible.
