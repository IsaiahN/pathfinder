## The residual, stated in effect terms

An "account of information" is being asked to do several jobs at once: (a) say what information *is* in a physical system held away from equilibrium, (b) say how it is quantified (bits, entropy, mutual information), (c) say how it relates to an observer or a measuring frame, and (d) say how it is carried by structure rather than by an ingredient list. The effect we need to decide is whether these are one quantity with one correct account (option A), or a union of several well-formed questions each with its own ground (option B). Option A sends a single `general`-mode verdict that flattens jobs the figures keep separate. Option B sends a shape with four constraints, refuters written in advance, and an open slot until a ground settles it.

**Writing down the sought-for shape as an ordinary term (the "Direction" discipline):** an answer would look like a statement of which quantity is being measured, which frame ranges against which ground, how the spendable gradient of a nonequilibrium system enters, why bits and actions are not the same currency, and what would refute it. Binding that description is what makes the preference refusable (`THE_FORMULA.md`, "Direction").

## What is deliberately not here

- No personal emails of named individuals.
- No ranking of people by how likely they are to believe the claim.
- No bulk-send plan.

Three reasons, in the figures' own words:

1. **A room does not upgrade the label.** Fig 7 :35 "A room's agreement is variation, not a verdict. Selection between rooms is where the ground votes." Adding informative recipients cannot substitute for an anchor.
2. **Correlation disqualifies a verdict.** Fig 2 :40 "agreement about the world is worthless when the agreeing frames share an evidence pool." Selecting contacts for receptiveness manufactures the correlation that makes their agreement worthless.
3. **Asking for trust before checking spends independence.** Fig 10 :42 "A verifier that can reconstruct the claim cannot verify it." The first contact carries the residual and its refuter, never the framework's interior.

Legally: scraping personal emails for unsolicited bulk outreach breaches GDPR and CAN-SPAM style rules in many jurisdictions. Public institutional and opt-in channels avoid that failure as well as the structural one.
