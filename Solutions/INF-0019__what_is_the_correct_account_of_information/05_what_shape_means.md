## 1. A shape is a constraint, not a filler

The figures README states the limit: the framework reports "what a missing thing would have to look like (a shape, not a name)". Fig 9 :32 "you get a kind of thing, never the answer itself". Fig 13 "It cannot say which primitive is missing, because naming it would mean already holding it."

From *The Library of Babel*: "you can cut your search short if the set of books returned is small and descriptive enough that it provides a general shape of the answer that satisfies your question."

So a **shape** is the narrowed form of an answer you can state before you can name it. It constrains; it does not fill. For this question the shape is the four constraints in `01` §8: per-slot residual, spendable gradient, two currencies, bond-carried information. "Shannon entropy", "von Neumann entropy" and "thermodynamic entropy" are names that would fill the slot. The shape does not select among them.

## 2. How the shape is obtained

- *Library of Babel*: use the vocabulary you already have that is *closest* to the thing, look for recurring new terms that overlap or correlate with it, recurse. "With each new term gained, you narrow the space by definition and deepen your vocabulary."
- Fig 6 :30 "The description travels. Fails by being sharpened into terms only we can read. Cast it in effect terms." Fig 9 :31 "your words are borrowed too, so describe the effect rather than the cause."
- *RSI Trap*: alignment is to "the thing that settles a question regardless of what any frame thinks. An outcome. A measurement. A board that pays or does not."
- *Library of Babel* on stopping: "you will know that you are done because the finish line will also be the starting place" - and the paradox is that by then you have "composed the answer through the process of locating it."

Applied here: the borrowed vocabulary is *entropy, residual, gradient, bond, currency*. The effect language is "what fails to resolve", "what can be spent", "what carries the difference". That is how the four constraints were obtained.

## 3. What licenses a name

Only two things, both outside the shape itself:

1. **The ground settles it.** Formula step 5: "the ground settles it. Verified only by what pays. A gate is not the ground." The README: the framework "stops at the limit of what the available instruments can distinguish, and records the lineage so the stop is honest."
2. **A provenanced import from a frame whose closure differs** (Fig 6 :23, Fig 8 :50-52), with a source gate and a shadow test.

For this question: a measurement that shows, say, mutual information between a system's microstate record and a coarse-grained flow variable is the quantity that tracks the live slot, would name one candidate. Until such a measurement, the slot stays open. SEP *Information Processing and Thermodynamic Entropy* supplies the exact discipline: "when an argument is only valid for a particular entropy, or when these entropies disagree, it should be clearly stated which entropy is involved."

## 4. Why "the correct account" is the forbidden error for this question

*The Library of Babel* gives the mechanism: "Perhaps your problem was a bundle or conflation of several problems masquerading as one?" The SEP *Information* entry gives the world-side evidence: Shannon-probability, Kolmogorov-computation and Floridi-truth "have next to nothing in common", and thermodynamics and information theory "use the same formulas to describe fundamentally different phenomena".

Asking for "the correct account" is a single-name demand over a bundle. Fig 7 :6 names "the limit: no last room"; a terminal account would be the last handoff after which nothing further can be asked. The canonical README's own section title ("This is not a Theory of Everything") governs the same refusal.

What remains owed is not a definition but a discriminator: an instrument that tells which slot is live and which measure tracks it. No such instrument is claimed here.

## 5. Answer to the question's own Q2 (does the framework name the quantity?)

**No.** The four constraints are licensable from figure lines; any specific identification (Shannon entropy = residual, or von Neumann entropy = bond information) is a binding from the physics frame onto an atom or a derivative, never a derivation of the substrate. Fig 5 :45 already names Shannon, Kolmogorov and Rissanen as borrowed machinery, with the provenance in the line. That is an import with a visible source gate (Fig 8's "source gate: provenance"), not a ground-settled verdict. It is recorded as such in `01` §7.

## 6. Two consequences for outreach

**Prestige is not action.** From *The Recursive Self-Improvement Trap*: "Prestige must never be allowed to validate an action directly... Prestige can fund an action. It can give an action a budget to be tried. But it cannot settle an action. Only the ground can settle it. The Ground Maintainer must be blind to prestige." Gathering well-known names to agree with the shape is the named collapse, not corroboration.

**My own audit is a simulation, not a ground reading.** Same essay: "LLMs can quickly simulate an approximate answer/output with the formula found in the architecture post... However, the truly testable output would need to be derived from the real architecture or working the formula yourself." So `01` is a simulated derivation, checked for internal consistency against the figures. It is **not** a test of the account and must be labelled as such. It cannot settle anything it reports on.
