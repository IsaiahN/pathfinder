## VERDICT (from the figures)

The question is a union of at least four conditions, not one hard question. The shape that all four share: information far from equilibrium is a per-slot residual against a ground; it is driven by a spendable gradient composing with energy into a flow, with discarded detail returning as noise; it is priced in bits for description and kT ln 2 for settlement, currencies that do not add; and it is carried by the typed bond, not the atom list. No particular entropy is named as "the correct account", because the framework returns a shape, not a name, and there is no last room. The four conditions compose in dependency order physical, quantitative, frame-relative, structural, verification, and the frame is the unmeasured middle link. The candidate accounts are members of a functional group as written, with the stronger isomer claim requiring a shared six-term atom set and a stated falsifier. Status: `specified`, ungrounded, candidate.

- **cost if wrong:** bounded and locatable. The packet states mode, three checkable sub-claims and refuters before the conclusion, so an error is findable rather than invisible. The real cost is spending the independence of outside readers by asking them to agree with a named account before they have checked a slot, which Fig 2 :42 prices: "Buy independence in proportion to the cost of being wrong." That cost is avoidable by leading with the shape claim and its refuter.
- **undoable:** yes. No primitive is withdrawn. The four constraints are removable or amendable one at a time against their own ground, and a supplied discriminator (slot test, gradient test, currency test, bond test) would name a quantity without collapsing the union. A sent claim can be superseded by a corrected packet.
- **reversed by:** a ground reading, not agreement. A single measure that settles all four conditions with no persistent residual would kill the union claim; an account with no frame, ground or slot that still identifies the live slot would kill the residual claim; a far-from-equilibrium system with no spendable gradient, or a single unit pricing both currencies without residue, would kill the physical and currency claims; a seventh primitive, or a derivation of one of the six from the other five, would break the substrate. Until one of these fires, the verdict stands as `candidate`.
