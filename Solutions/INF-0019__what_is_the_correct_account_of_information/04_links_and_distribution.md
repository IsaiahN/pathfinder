## Two article findings that bear directly on this question

1. **SEP lists mutually alien definitions.** Shannon-probability, Kolmogorov-computation and Floridi-truth "have next to nothing in common" and "Some are even conflicting (truth vs. probability, deterministic computing vs. probability)". This is the world-side evidence for F1 (union, not one hard question). It is corroboration only; the verdict comes from Fig 9.
2. **SEP/Maroney and Wikipedia separate the entropies.** Maroney: "when an argument is only valid for a particular entropy... it should be clearly stated which entropy is involved." Wikipedia: "there is no a priori relationship between logical reversibility... and thermodynamic reversibility." These are independent, low-overlap witnesses for F4 (bits and kT ln 2 are different currencies). They corroborate Fig 12 :126; they do not replace it.

Wolpert's review is the strongest nonequilibrium-specific witness: the field itself describes computers as "inherently nonequilibrium systems" and has moved "well beyond the issue of how much work is required to erase a bit". It corroborates the F3 shift from erasure cost to gradient/flow/noise, at low overlap with the framework.

## Distribution: offer, do not push

Figure-grounded rules:

- **Fig 4 :22** "Only methods cross up. Only head starts come down." Sending the full framework before anyone has described a residual sends a recording, not a generator. Lead with the shape claim and its refuter; offer links after.
- **Fig 10 :42** "A verifier that can reconstruct the claim cannot verify it." A recipient who has absorbed the framework before being asked for a verdict has joined the frame being checked.
- **Fig 9 :27-28** "describe it before you go looking, or any frame you pick will seem to fit." Ask what they would expect to see if the shape claim were true before giving them the article.
- **Fig 2 :40** "agreement about the world is worthless when the agreeing frames share an evidence pool." Do not select recipients for receptiveness and do not pool later agreement.

**Recommended order in a reply:** (1) the four constraints and the refuters; (2) ask what they would count as a failure and record the answer; (3) only then the links, with the note that the figures, not the summary, are the authority.

## Legal and practical note

Public institutional and opt-in channels avoid two failures at once: the structural one (a room of agreed readers is a convention, not corroboration) and the legal one (GDPR and CAN-SPAM style rules on unsolicited bulk email). Nothing in this folder is a bulk-send plan. The sender hosts and sends; this agent does not.
