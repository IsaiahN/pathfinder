## 1. The residual it answers

"The correct account of information in systems far from equilibrium" bundles at least four conditions: what information physically is in a driven system, how it is quantified, what frame it is relative to, and how it is carried by arrangement. Fig 9 :4-5 "are not one hard question but several well-formed ones. Split it rather than search." ## 3. What it explicitly does not say

- Not "Shannon entropy is the answer", nor von Neumann entropy, nor thermodynamic entropy, nor mutual information. Those are names where the framework returns a shape.
- Not a derivation of Landauer's bound, nor a new thermodynamics. Those are imports with recorded sources (Bennett; SEP; Wikipedia).
- Not that information is observer-independent or observer-dependent in the abstract. It is a per-slot gap between what Γ predicts and what o′ returns, ranging against a ground that does not update (Fig 2).
- Not a single unified "theory of information". The center stays a union compound with its own loops.
- Not `grounded`. No measurement here settles `left(R,φ)`.

## 4. What would settle it - the ground

A discriminator, not a further argument. From *The Hard Problem is About Source* (recorded in `05` only as an essay reference, not quoted as read this session): an instrument that cannot comment on the source is inadequate. Concretely:

- **Slot test:** does a candidate measure isolate one live slot while global entropy stays near zero? A measure that averages the signal away fails Fig 1 :19 and :22.
- **Gradient test:** does the quantity track the spendable gradient and the noise that returns from a crossing, vanishing as the system approaches equilibrium? A measure that does not cannot describe the far-from-equilibrium case.
- **Currency test:** is the description cost (bits) reported separately from the settlement cost (kT ln 2)? A single figure that sums them fails Fig 12 :126.
- **Bond test:** do two arrangements over the same atom set with different bonds produce different behaviour on a ground test? If not, the bond carries nothing (Fig 12 :5).
- **Standing falsifiers from `01` §5:** a single measure settling all four conditions with no residual; an account with no frame/ground/slot that still identifies the live slot; a far-from-equilibrium system with no spendable gradient; a single unit for both currencies; a bond with no behavioural difference; a seventh primitive.

## 6. One-paragraph version for outreach

> The Tether does not name one correct account of information, and it does not claim the final theory. It says the question is a bundle of four well-formed ones: physical, quantitative, frame-relative and structural. Across all four, information is a residual measured per slot against a ground, not a global scalar; far from equilibrium it is powered by a spendable gradient that composes with energy into a flow, with the discarded detail returning as noise; it is priced in two currencies that do not add, bits for the description and kT ln 2 for settling it; and it is carried by the typed bond, not the ingredient list. The framework returns that shape, not a name, so it refuses to identify any particular entropy as the answer. What it offers is the constraints any candidate must satisfy, the falsifiers written in advance, and the instruments that would close them. The slot stays open until the ground settles it.
