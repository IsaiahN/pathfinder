# The Tether's answer: generative AI and specialized expertise

**Mode: `specified` on the substantive claims, `general` on the structural shape. Not `grounded`.** This is a shape and a route, not a measurement. Simulated derivation checked for internal consistency against the figures (`05_what_shape_means.md` §6), not a ground reading.

## 1. The residual it answers

For each task slot, an expert frame predicts what the work needs and checks the prediction against a ground (code that runs, a patient outcome, a client who pays). A generative model is a second frame that can produce artifacts cheaply from the same corpus. The question asks whether the arrival of that frame lowers the standing of the specialist's term or raises the specialist's productivity and influence. The framework refuses to answer with a global average: Fig 1 :19: *"The residual is indexed by object slot, not aggregated."*

## 2. The shape

1. **The "or" is a union, not a partition.** Reduced value of production-only expertise and increased productivity/influence are two conditions in one compound. Fig 9 :57: *"What remains is smaller and it is also categorically different: a case in two conditions is not a harder case of either."* A worker can be faster at producing artifacts while the artifact-production component of their standing falls.
2. **The composer works inside the same closure.** Fig 6 :49: *"more search at the same reach: is it in here and unfound? The closure does not change. Same atoms, same wall, more of the space actually visited."* Fig 6 :21: *"MINT composes inside the closure and can never add an atom."* The defensible core of "more productive" is cheaper search and a head start (Fig 4 :12), not a bigger wall and not a seventh primitive.
3. **The load-bearing term moves from recording to settlement.** Fig 4 :25: *"A recording carried upward looks like knowledge and is a description of one occasion."* Fig 1 :27: *"They are frame-internal. A frame cannot score itself with a quantity it also produces."* Artifact counts cannot be the verdict. What stays scarce is ranging the artifact against a ground; Fig 10 :42: *"A verifier that can reconstruct the claim cannot verify it."*
4. **The sign is relative to the residual.** Fig 11 :49: *"An actor’s valence is relative to the residual, not a property it carries."* Fig 11 :50: *"The same actor is a benefactor to one residual and a malfactor to another, so mutualism and parasitism are relations rather than kinds."* No figure says AI is inherently deskilling or inherently augmenting. Change the task's ground and the same tool changes sign.
5. **Capability is agent-and-habitat.** Fig 11 :39: *"capability is a property of agent-and-habitat, never of the agent alone."* The value of a specialist cannot be read off the specialist. A habitat that adds a composer changes what is abundant and what is scarce; it cannot subtract the contact that experts hold (Fig 11 :61).
6. **The bottleneck is discrimination, not generation.** Fig 3 :27: *"A link with no instrument is not a link that works. It is a link nobody has looked at."* The missing middle link is a term for what a generated artifact affords and an objective that says what counts as progress. Adding more generation is effort above the break (Fig 3 :19).
7. **The occupation-level answer is a `gap`.** Fig 9 :32: *"you get a kind of thing, never the answer itself"*; Fig 13 :134: *"Unreachable is a verdict here, not a silence,"*. No figure names an occupation, a wage, or an elasticity. What is owed is a task-level instrument with a real anchor, not a braver prediction.

## 3. What it explicitly does not say

- Not that AI makes experts obsolete, and not that it only makes them stronger. Both are `general`-mode verdicts with no ground reading.
- Not that a benchmark or output count is a market gain. Such quantities are frame-internal (Fig 1 :25-27) until they pay.
- Not which jobs, sectors, or countries gain. That name is not held.
- Not that the wall moved. Fig 6 :25: *"An import adds no atom to the world."*
- Not that tacit expertise is insoluble. *Decoding Tacit Knowledge* says the history cannot be sent, but a plain description of effects can be checked by anyone; what is needed is contact and a discriminator, not a decree.
- Not `grounded`. No wage series, census, or field experiment was used to settle the claim.

## 4. What would settle it: the ground

A task-level census anchored in something that does not move with opinion. Fig 2 :29: *"An anchor is whatever settles the question and does not change its answer because of what any frame thinks. A measurement, an experiment, a counter, a market, a patient outcome."*

The corresponding questions are:

- **Source discrimination:** for a task slot, does the observed speed-up come from cheaper search at the same wall, from a genuine import that moved the wall, or from a change in who pays? The productivity reading cannot comment on its own source; *The Hard Problem is About Source*: *"If several paths create the same behavior, then behavior is a projection with lossy information."* The spectrometer, not an argument, resolved the analogous case.
- **Anchor test:** does the gain survive contact with a market or an outcome, not only a benchmark or a human rating? Fig 10 :26 names *"saturated metrics, pooled readings"* as the channel failure.
- **Valence test:** does the sign of the effect flip when the residual changes, as Fig 11 :50 predicts, or is it invariant? An invariant sign refutes the central claim.
- **Standing test:** after artifact volume is controlled for, does verification quality predict ground outcomes better than volume? If volume wins, the recording-to-settlement shift is wrong.

## 5. The standing falsifiers

Written before this conclusion, repeated from `01` §5:

1. One lever clears both conditions with no categorically different residue measured on a ground.
2. A generative system adds a settled atom to a worker's closure, with provenance and a shadow test.
3. The sign of the effect is invariant across task slots whose residuals demonstrably differ.
4. Artifact volume, controlling for verification, predicts ground outcomes better than verification does.
5. A task-level census with a real anchor returns stable signs that contradict the shift from recording to settlement.

## 6. The honest stop

**Unreached, not unreachable.** The frame stops at the limit of the instruments available in this derivation. It can report the shape any answer must have, the four falsifiers, and the kind of instrument that would close the gap. It cannot report the answer itself. The lineage is recorded so the stop is checkable. *"Indiscernibility is not identity."* When a real anchor is read, this verdict is replaced, not defended.

## 7. One-paragraph version for outreach

> The Tether does not answer "AI helps experts or hurts them" with a single verdict. It says the "or" is a false partition: a model trained on the same corpus is a cheap composer inside the same closure, so it lowers the price of search and gives juniors a real head start, while the artifact it produces becomes a cheap recording whose value migrates toward the person who can settle it against a ground. Which way that lands for any worker depends on the residual of their task, not on the tool, because an actor's valence is relative to the residual. Capability lives in the worker and their habitat together, and the scarce thing after cheap generation is not more output but the missing instrument that says what counts as progress. The frame returns a shape, four falsifiers written in advance, and the kind of instrument that would close it. Which occupations gain is a declared gap, open until a grounded task census says otherwise.
