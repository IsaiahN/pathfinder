## Distribution: offer, do not push

Figure-grounded rules:

- **Fig 4 :22**: *"Only methods cross up. Only head starts come down."* Send the derivation and the refuter first. The full frame is a recording and can follow later, by opt-in only.
- **Fig 10 :42**: *"A verifier that can reconstruct the claim cannot verify it."* An outside frame that has absorbed the interior before giving a verdict has joined the frame being checked.
- **Fig 9 :27-28**: *"describe it before you go looking, or any frame you pick will seem to fit."* Ask what they would expect to see if the shape claim were true before showing the article.
- **Fig 2 :40**: *"disqualifying for a verdict: agreement about the world is worthless when the agreeing frames share an evidence pool"* Agreement after the theory is shown is not a second witness.
- **Fig 1 :19**: *"The residual is indexed by object slot, not aggregated."* Ask for failures at the task level, not a verdict on AI in general.

**Order in a reply:** (1) residual, shape claim and refuter; (2) ask what they would count as a failure and record it; (3) only then the links, with the note that the figures, not the summary, are the authority; (4) only on request, the formula as a method with the instruction to write a fresh refuter and state a mode.

## The two-stage offer

**(1)** Validate the output against a ground. **(2)** Only if curious, read the frame that produced it. **(3)** Optionally reuse the formula on another problem. Stage 1 precedes stage 2 by Fig 10 :52 and Fig 2 :45: *"Frames range against the ground. They do not negotiate with it."* Stage 2 is opt-in because an outside frame's agreement stops being evidence once it holds the interiors. Stage 3 is allowed because a method crossed a boundary, not an answer: Fig 4 :22. Each user still needs a ground and a self-written refuter, because the formula's "Direction" note says a frame cannot check the direction of its own question.

**Hard precondition:** stages 1 to 3 only work if the stage-1 shape survives. It is stated with four falsifiers in `02_outreach_email.md`. If one fires, the shape is withdrawn and no theory is offered.
