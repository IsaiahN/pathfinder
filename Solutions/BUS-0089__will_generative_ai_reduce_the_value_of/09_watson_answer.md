## QUESTION

Decide, in effect terms, what the Tether says about the claim that generative AI will either reduce the value of specialized expertise or make expert workers more productive and influential. Option A: accept the forced disjunction and pick a side. Option B: keep the figure-derived shape, refuse the partition, and leave the occupation-level sign open. What each leads to: A delivers a global verdict with no slot indexing, treats frame-internal output counts as evidence, and names a winner the frame cannot name; B delivers a union shape, four pre-registered refuters, and an instrument-shaped gap that a ground can close.

## DOMAIN CENSUS

R by arity, symmetry and scale, then one line per domain with actors, currency and anchor. Union or partition, and dependency order.

- **R (the residual).** The change in which term of a worker's loop is load-bearing when a cheap composer enters the habitat. Arity: three-place (composer, worker, ground), indexed by task slot; two overlapping conditions, so a **union compound** (Fig 9 :55, :57), not one hard question. Symmetry: asymmetric; the composer produces and the worker settles, so `→` or `⇒`, not `+` (Fig 12 :70, :76). The phrase more influential is a `⋛` threshold comparison (Fig 12 :86). Scale: individual loop, population or labor market, and the lossy membrane between (Fig 4).
- **D1, generator frame.** Actors: model, corpus, prompt, decoding budget. Currency: artifacts, description length, search cost. Anchor: the training corpus and benchmark, fixed at inference; it does not update on this user's ground. Admitted.
- **D2, expert frame.** Actors: specialist, tacit library `Γ`, instruments, corrections. Currency: settlement, reach, `left(R,φ)`. Anchor: the real ground, code that runs, a patient outcome, a paying client (Fig 2 :29). Admitted.
- **D3, knowledge-work habitat.** Actors: firms, clients, entrants, the new generator, trainees. Currency: tasks, wages, influence, survival. Anchor: willingness to pay, a market (Fig 2 :29 names "a market"). Admitted.
- **D4, channel and instruments.** Actors: metrics, benchmarks, percepts, verification habits. Currency: contact with the ground. Anchor: the ground that does not decay (Fig 10 :24). Admitted.
- **X1, free-floating "value of expertise".** No nameable anchor; if it moves when a frame's opinion moves it is not an anchor. Not a domain yet; absorbed into D2 and D3.
- **X2, productivity as artifact count.** Frame-internal quantity the generating frame also produces (Fig 1 :25-27). Absorbed into D4 as a budget filter, never a witness.
- **Partition or union:** union. The two offered outcomes overlap in the same task slot and have opposite valence by residual.
- **Order by dependency:** perception of the artifact, vocabulary to discriminate, objective saying what counts as progress, planning, learn and carry (Fig 3 :4-15). The predicted break is the vocabulary-to-objective link (Fig 3 :25-27), not generation.

Strain test applied: the Fig 12 isomer reading of human-plus-AI versus AI-alone was tried and withdrawn. Fig 12 governs typed bonds inside one library; a habitat substitution is a different object. Re-census sends it back to the census, it does not soften the reading (see `01` §7).

## TETHER (primary, verbatim, per domain, triangulated | single-source | gap)

Cited against `FRAMEWORK/reference/03-FIGURES_TEXT.md`. Quotes are verbatim from the canonical extract.

**R, union compound. Status: triangulated (Fig 9 union rule plus Fig 11 valence rule, low overlap between partition logic and habitat logic).**

- Fig 9 :55 "A bundled problem is usually a union of overlapping conditions, not a list of separate ones." -> the "or" is a bundle to unbundle, not a choice to make.
- Fig 9 :57 "What remains is smaller and it is also categorically different: a case in two conditions is not a harder case of either." -> the two halves keep separate verdicts, never averaged.

**D1, generator frame. Status: triangulated (Fig 6 closure, Fig 4 membrane, Fig 5 output rule; three distinct roles).**

- Fig 6 :49 "more search at the same reach: is it in here and unfound? The closure does not change. Same atoms, same wall, more of the space actually visited." -> cheaper search, same wall. This is the core of the productivity half.
- Fig 6 :21 "MINT composes inside the closure and can never add an atom." -> no new primitive is claimed.
- Fig 4 :12 "it makes the next agent’s search cheaper" -> the artifact is a head start, not a replacement for the learner.
- Fig 5 :48 "Its own output is never a measure of success. A system can be enthusiastically curious and produce nothing." -> output volume cannot be the verdict.

**D2, expert frame. Status: triangulated (Fig 11 capability, Fig 10 verification, Fig 1 frame-internal rule).**

- Fig 11 :39 "So the answerable question is not what intelligence is, but what makes a system capable. And capability is a property of agent-and-habitat, never of the agent alone." -> value cannot be located in the worker alone.
- Fig 11 :40 "A system cannot be made more capable by improving the agent in isolation. An improvement that does not change contact changes nothing, however much it improves." -> a head start that changes contact matters; a faster generator alone does not.
- Fig 4 :25 "A recording carried upward looks like knowledge and is a description of one occasion." -> cheap recordings lose standing; settlement stays scarce.
- Fig 10 :42 "A verifier that can reconstruct the claim cannot verify it." -> the generator is not its own check.
- Fig 1 :27 "They are frame-internal. A frame cannot score itself with a quantity it also produces." -> output counts are disqualified as evidence.

**D3, habitat. Status: triangulated (Fig 11 valence, Fig 2 anchor, Fig 7 agreement).**

- Fig 11 :49 "An actor’s valence is relative to the residual, not a property it carries." -> no inherent AI-is-good or AI-is-bad fact.
- Fig 11 :50 "The same actor is a benefactor to one residual and a malfactor to another, so mutualism and parasitism are relations rather than kinds." -> sign flips with the task.
- Fig 2 :29 "An anchor is whatever settles the question and does not change its answer because of what any frame thinks. A measurement, an experiment, a counter, a market, a patient outcome." -> payment and outcome settle the standing claim.
- Fig 7 :13 "agreement compounds, evidence does not" -> expert consensus is not corroboration.

**D4, channel. Status: single-source for the failure description (Fig 10), corroborated by Fig 3 on the missing instrument; kept distinct.**

- Fig 10 :24 "the ground does not decay, the channel does" -> verification habits can decay while the ground stays.
- Fig 10 :26 "stale percepts, dropped transients, saturated metrics, pooled readings" -> benchmarks and ratings can saturate.
- Fig 3 :27 "A link with no instrument is not a link that works. It is a link nobody has looked at." -> the discriminator is the scarce term.
- Fig 3 :19 "Optimising a link that already works cannot rescue one that does not." -> more generation is effort above the break.

**Occupation-level sign. Status: `gap` after genuine re-census.**

- Fig 9 :32 "you get a kind of thing, never the answer itself" -> no occupation is named.
- Fig 13 :134 "Unreachable is a verdict here, not a silence," -> this is a recorded stop, not an omission.
- Fig 8 :39 "So a search that finds nothing is never a proof of absence." -> the gap is not a negative result.

## WORLD (secondary, each source read; field, author/venue/year, quote, prediction, corroborates | conflicts)

Sources are listed by low-overlap field first. Agreement from a shared pool is one witness, not two.

- **Labor economics, AI at work.** Erik Brynjolfsson, Danielle Li and Lindsey R. Raymond, *Generative AI at Work*, NBER Working Paper 31161, 2023 (QJE 2025). Opened 2026-11-22. Quote: "Access to the tool increases productivity, as measured by issues resolved per hour, by 14% on average, including a 34% improvement for novice and low-skilled workers but with minimal impact on experienced and highly skilled workers." Prediction: a head start sent down to novices at the same wall. **Corroborates** D1 and D4. The NBER Digest summary of the same study (opened 2026-11-22, "An agent using the AI tool who had just two months' tenure at the firm performed as well as an agent with six months' tenure working without the tool") is the same evidence pool and is not counted as a second witness.
- **Economics of professional writing.** Shakked Noy and Whitney Zhang, *Experimental evidence on the productivity effects of generative artificial intelligence*, Science, 2023. Opened via the Stanford SCALE public record 2026-11-22 because the Science page returned 403. Quote: "The average time taken decreased by 40% and output quality rose by 18%. Inequality between workers decreased." Prediction: gains concentrate where the learner had less practice; compression of differences, not a uniform lift. **Corroborates** D1 and D2. Overlap with the NBER study is moderate: both are labor-productivity experiments but different tasks and teams.

## VERDICT (from the figures)

The shape survives; the forced "or" does not. A generative model trained on the shared corpus is a cheap composer inside the same closure, so it lowers search cost and delivers a real head start; that is the defensible core of the productivity half. Because artifacts become cheap recordings, the load-bearing term shifts toward settlement against a ground, and the standing of production-only expertise falls; that is the defensible core of the value half. For any worker the sign depends on the residual of their task slot, because an actor's valence is relative to the residual. Capability is agent-and-habitat, never the agent alone. The bottleneck is the missing discriminator at the middle of the dependency chain, not more generation. Which occupations gain is a `gap`, closed only by a task-level instrument with a real anchor. Mode `specified`, ungrounded, `candidate`.

- **cost if wrong:** bounded and locatable. The packet states its mode, four refuters and a provenance trail, so an error is findable rather than invisible. The real cost is in outreach: sending a global "AI helps" or "AI hurts" verdict, with output counts as evidence, would convert potential outside frames into members of a pool and would waste their twenty minutes on the wrong shape. Fig 2 :42: *"Buy independence in proportion to the cost of being wrong."* That cost is avoidable by leading with the residual, the union and the refuter.
- **undoable:** yes. No term is deleted if the claim fails. A refutation is added to the record and the reach falls because *"something was added rather than because anything was deleted"* (Fig 6 :42-43). The shape and its four falsifiers are provenanced so a later reader can locate the error (Fig 10 :62-64). No primitive has to be withdrawn.
- **reversed by:** a ground reading, not agreement. A task-level census anchored in payment or outcome that shows one lever clearing both conditions; a settled atom added by a generative system with provenance and a shadow test; an invariant effect sign across task slots with different residuals; artifact volume beating verification on ground outcomes after controls; or a discriminator instrument with residual trending to zero that relocates the gains downstream. Until one of these fires, the verdict stands as `candidate`. Prestige, enthusiasm, or a larger pool of agreeing researchers is not such a ground.
