## The residual in one line

When a cheap composer enters the knowledge-work habitat, does the specialist's ground-contact term lose standing, or does the specialist's loop close more cheaply and gain reach? The figures answer in effect terms: a composer inside the same closure makes search cheaper without adding an atom, and the valence of that composer is relative to the residual, not a property it carries.

## What is deliberately not here

- No personal emails of named individuals.
- No ranking of people by how likely they are to agree that AI helps or hurts experts.
- No bulk-send plan.
- No claim that this derivation settles the question it reports on.

The reasons are the figures' own. Fig 2 :40: *"disqualifying for a verdict: agreement about the world is worthless when the agreeing frames share an evidence pool"*. Fig 2 :35: *"Alignment is triangulation, not negotiation. A mirror cannot correct you."* Fig 10 :42: *"A verifier that can reconstruct the claim cannot verify it."* Selecting recipients for receptiveness manufactures the correlation that would make their agreement worthless, and sending the full frame before asking for a verdict spends the only thing an outside frame contributes.

## Honest standing

This is a simulated derivation checked for internal consistency against the figures, not a ground reading. *The Recursive Self-Improvement Trap*: *"LLMs can quickly simulate an approximate answer/output with the formula found in the architecture post. It will often be better than the generic outputs that you will usually get otherwise too"*, but *"the truly testable output would need to be derived from the real architecture or working the formula yourself."* This package cannot settle whether generative AI raises or lowers anyone's pay, status, or influence. It can only report the shape any answer must have and the instruments that would close it.
