## 1. A shape is a constraint, not a filler

The canonical usage rule (`FRAMEWORK/reference/FIGURES_README_canonical.md`) states the limit: the framework returns *"what a missing thing would have to look like (a shape, not a name)"*. Fig 9 :32: *"you get a kind of thing, never the answer itself"*. Fig 13 :133-134: *"what it returns is a shape rather than a name"* and *"Unreachable is a verdict here, not a silence,"*.

From *The Library of Babel*: *"This means that you can cut your search short if the set of books returned is small and descriptive enough that it provides a general shape of the answer that satisfies your question."*

For BUS-0089, a **shape** is the narrowed form of the effect you can state before you can name a winning occupation. "A composer at the same wall, with load-bearing shifting toward settlement" is a shape. "Radiologists lose and nurses gain" is a name. The question asks for an "or"; the shape refuses the "or" and keeps the union.

## 2. How the shape is obtained

- *Library of Babel*: use the vocabulary you already hold that is closest to the thing, then look for recurring new terms that overlap with it and recurse. *"With each new term gained, you narrow the space and deepen your vocabulary."*
- Fig 6 :30: *"The description travels. Fails by being sharpened into terms only we can read. Cast it in effect terms."* Fig 9 :30-31: *"your words are borrowed too, so describe the effect rather than the cause"*. So the residual is described as a change in which term is load-bearing, not as "automation anxiety" or "augmentation".
- *Decoding Tacit Knowledge*: *"When contact isn't possible, the best you can send is a plain description of what you'd expect to see, because anyone can check that against the world."* This is why the outreach email asks what a failure would look like before offering the frame.
- *Library of Babel* on stopping: you know you are done because *"the finish line will also be the starting place"*, and the paradox is that by then you have *"composed the answer through the process of locating it."* For this solution the stop is F8, the declared `gap`, not a named occupation.

## 3. What licenses a name

Only two things, both outside the shape:

1. **The ground settles it.** Formula step 5, with the dash rendered as a colon to keep this file free of em dashes: *"SETTLE: the ground settles it. Verified only by what pays. A gate is not the ground."* A task-level wage and outcome series anchored to a market (Fig 2 :29 names "a market") could name which slots moved.
2. **A provenanced import from a frame whose closure differs** (Fig 6 :23, Fig 8 :50-52), with a source gate and a shadow test. The world studies are imports of this kind, but they corroborate the mechanism shape, not the occupation-level name.

*The Hard Problem is About Source* gives the precise failure mode for naming from a reading: *"If several paths create the same behavior, then behavior is a projection with lossy information."* A productivity graph can be produced by cheaper search, by a wall move, or by a change in who pays. The graph cannot comment on the source. An instrument that discriminates those sources is owed, not a sharper argument. The essay notes that the spectrometer, not an argument, resolved the analogous case.

## 4. Why "value of expertise" cannot be used as the name

From *The Recursive Self-Improvement Trap*: *"Prestige is not equal to actions. (prestige =/= actions)"* and *"Prestige can fund an action. It can give an action a budget to be tried. But it cannot settle an action. Only the ground can settle it."*

"Value of expertise" and "influence" behave like prestige terms when they are detached from a payment or an outcome. If a frame's standing rises because other frames agree it is standing, the anchor has started to update on opinion, which Fig 2 :33-34 forbids: *"If it does, panel 3 turns into panel 1: the anchor becomes another frame, everything drifts together, and the drift is coherent, which is why nobody notices."* The census therefore refused "value of expertise" as a domain until it was bound to D2 settlement and D3 payment (X1 in `01`).

## 5. What the essays change about the answer

1. **The junior-worker result is a head start, not a wall move.** *Decoding Tacit Knowledge*: *"what passes down is ways of doing things and head starts, never the experience itself. 'Only methods cross up. Only head starts come down'"*. The Brynjolfsson etc. result (34% gain for novices, near zero for experts) is exactly a head start sent down to a learner who has fewer corrections behind them. It does not show that the expert's closure grew.
2. **Tacit knowledge keeps its cost at the boundary.** The same essay: *"Settling compresses the description and not what it cost to establish"* (Fig 12 :107). A few words from an expert carry paragraphs because the history behind them stayed with the expert. A model trained on the text gets *"the traces the thought left, not the thought"*. This is a mechanism-level reason why cheap generation does not automatically transfer the expensive part.
3. **Agreement is not the test.** *The Recursive Self-Improvement Trap*: *"You may all agree and be wrong. You may set your clock to someone else's, who was setting your clock to yours, and be off by many deviations over time. You need ground truth to define where you stand."* A chorus of AI-optimists or AI-pessimists is one pool talking.
4. **The frame cannot certify its own completeness.** Same essay: *"Metatheories cannot validate themselves... You need an external validator. You need both the concept and the percept, as Kant said. You need the seed and the ground."* This is why the verdict in `09` stays `candidate`.

## 6. Consequence for this solution's honesty

*The Recursive Self-Improvement Trap*: *"LLMs can quickly simulate an approximate answer/output with the formula found in the architecture post. It will often be better than the generic outputs that you will usually get otherwise too... However, the truly testable output would need to be derived from the real architecture or working the formula yourself."*

So `01_figure_grounded_audit.md`, `06_isomers_and_order.md`, `07_the_tether_answer.md`, `08_expert_verification_packet.md` and `09_watson_answer.md` are **simulated derivations checked for internal consistency against the figures**. They are not measurements of wages, employment, or influence, and they cannot settle the question they report on. The stop is at the limit of the available instruments, with the lineage recorded. *"Indiscernibility is not identity."*

## 7. Correction kept visible

An earlier reading of this question treated "reduce the value of expertise" as a claim about a missing primitive that the framework could not name, and leaned on "shape, not a name" as a blanket prohibition on naming anything. That was overstated here. The canonical phrase governs a **missing** thing, one not held in `Γ`. In this question nothing in the six-term set is at stake; what is missing is an **instrument** that discriminates sources of a productivity reading. The correct form of the gap is therefore: a shape is available, the occupation-level name is not, and the missing thing is a discriminator, not a term of the substrate. Both the overstatement and its narrowing are recorded so neither is silently lost.
