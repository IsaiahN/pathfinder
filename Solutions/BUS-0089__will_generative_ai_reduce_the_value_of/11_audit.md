## Round trip gap R_T

The Symbols table defines R_T as the gap between what was sent up and what came back down, `gap(x, (T_E ∘ T_A)(x))`, measuring what the coarser description cannot hold. Fig 4 :30 requires it to be measured rather than assumed. I ran the round trip on the condensation this folder actually uses.

1. **Sent up (x):** the verbatim figure set, chiefly Fig 6 :21, :49 (composition and search at the same wall), Fig 11 :39-40, :49-51 (capability and valence), Fig 4 :11-12, :25 (head start versus recording), Fig 9 :55-57 (union).
2. **Abstraction (T_A):** the shorthand carried in `07` and `08`: "the "or" is false; the composer makes search cheaper at the same wall; value moves from recording to settlement; the sign is residual-relative; the occupation is a gap."
3. **Concretisation (T_E):** a concrete claim about wages, hiring and influence in a named occupation, which is what a reader will naturally hear when the shorthand is applied to a labour-market question.

**R_T is nonzero.** Three things the shorthand holds do not survive the trip back down:

- the **slot indexing** (Fig 1 :19), which the shorthand still carries but a workforce-level reading drops;
- the **anchor requirement** (Fig 2 :29), because the world studies offer ratings and task outcomes, not a market that settles wages;
- the **source discrimination** owed by *The Hard Problem is About Source*, because a speed-up reading cannot say whether it came from cheaper search, a wall move, or a change in who pays.

Conversely, two things survive cleanly: the union shape, and the claim that the composer works inside the same closure. That is the residue worth sending. R_T was **not** measured numerically, because no ground reading exists; it is recorded qualitatively as the enumerated set of dropped constraints. Treating "the formula is the condensed version of the figures" as identity would set R_T to zero and would be the dead-reckoning error Fig 4 :30 names. The honest form is a lossy condensation with a recorded gap, which is what `09_watson_answer.md` states.
