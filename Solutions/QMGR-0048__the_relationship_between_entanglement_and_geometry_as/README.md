## The one-line shape

Geometry is not a primitive among the six; it is a composition residue. Entanglement and geometry are two low-overlap frames whose union has a surplus, not one frame wearing two names. The figure-decided verdict refuses the literal ER=EPR identity as written, returns a shape for the bridge, and leaves the constant ungrounded until a discriminator instrument exists. This is a simulated derivation checked for internal consistency against the figures, not a ground reading, so it cannot settle the question it reports on.

## What is deliberately not here

- No personal emails of named individuals.
- No ranking of people by how likely they are to agree with the claim.
- No bulk-send plan.

Three reasons, in the figures' own words:

1. **Agreement is not a verdict when frames share a pool.** Fig 2 :40 "agreement about the world is worthless when the agreeing frames share an evidence pool". A list selected for receptiveness manufactures the correlation that would make its agreement worthless.
2. **A mirror cannot correct you.** Fig 2 :35 "Alignment is triangulation, not negotiation. A mirror cannot correct you." Asking for endorsement before a check turns outside frames into mirrors.
3. **A verifier must not hold the interiors.** Fig 10 :42 "A verifier that can reconstruct the claim cannot verify it." The first contact carries the residual and its refuter, never the theory.

Legally: harvesting personal emails for unsolicited bulk outreach breaches GDPR and CAN-SPAM style rules in many jurisdictions and would get the sending domain blacklisted. That is a practical failure on top of the structural one.
