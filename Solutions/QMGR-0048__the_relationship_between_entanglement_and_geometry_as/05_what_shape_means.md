## 1. A shape is a constraint, not a filler

The figures README states the limit: the framework returns *"what a missing thing would have to look like (a shape, not a name)."* Fig 9 :32: *"you get a kind of thing, never the answer itself"*. Fig 13 :134: *"It cannot say which primitive is missing, because naming it would mean already holding it."*

From *The Library of Babel*: *"You can cut your search short if the set of books returned is small and descriptive enough that it provides a general shape of the answer that satisfies your question."*

For this question a **shape** is the narrowed form of the relationship we can state before we can name a mechanism or a constant. It constrains: an answer must say which held atoms the two frames bind to, what bond joins them, what mode it carries, and what instrument would close it. It does not fill the entropy-to-area constant or assert a literal identity. *"Entanglement relates to geometry through a structure in the slot"* is a shape. *"Entanglement is a wormhole"* is a name.

## 2. How a shape is obtained

- *Library of Babel*: use the vocabulary already closest to the thing, look for recurring new terms that overlap with it, recurse. *"With each new term gained, you narrow the space and deepen your vocabulary."*
- Fig 6 :30: *"The description travels. Fails by being sharpened into terms only we can read. Cast it in effect terms."* Fig 9 :30-31: *"your words are borrowed too, so describe the effect rather than the cause."* So describe the contact residual (entropy against area), not "quantum gravity."
- *Decoding Tacit Knowledge*: *"When contact isn't possible, the best you can send is a plain description of what you'd expect to see, because anyone can check that against the world."*
- *Library of Babel* on stopping: the answer is reached when *"the finish line will also be the starting place"*, and the paradox is that by then you have *"composed the answer through the process of locating it."*

## 3. What licenses a name

Two things, both outside the shape itself:

1. **The ground settles it.** Formula step 5: *"the ground settles it. Verified only by what pays."* The README: the framework *"stops at the limit of what the available instruments can distinguish, and records the lineage so the stop is honest."* No such instrument exists for ER=EPR, so no name is licensed here.
2. **A provenanced import from a frame whose closure differs** (Fig 6 :23, Fig 8 :51-52), with a source gate and a shadow test. The entropy-to-area map is such an import from the physics frame, and Fig 8 :56 records it as a case already known and not tested forward.

*They're Scanning Books* sharpens the point: *"The real import is not a new term in the existing space. It is a new dimension of the space."* And: *"A minimum viable set of six terms is a human's careful guess at reality's compression axes; another sensorium's minimum set need not be those six."* Also: *"Declaring the primitives does not make them neutral. It makes the choice visible."*

The six are a visible guess, not nature's own list. Turning one into *the* answer to the entanglement-to-geometry slot would turn a visible choice into an invisible one.

## 4. Metamerism is the specific error this question invites

*The Hard Problem is About Source* (opened this run) gives the mechanism. An instrument that reads green *"cannot comment on the source"*: one green light, or yellow plus blue, or RGB, or CMYK, or a scheme not yet discovered. *"If several paths create the same behavior, then behavior is a projection with lossy information."* And: *"An instrument that can't distinguish these cases demonstrates its own adequacy is false."* It was *"proven wrong not by argument, but by the arrival of a better instrument."*

Here the entropy reading is the green reading. Many entangled states give the same `S`, so `S` cannot comment on which microscopic configuration produced it, still less on a geometry. The essay puts the general rule plainly: *"An instrument that can't distinguish these cases demonstrates its own inadequacy, it cannot claim anything substantial about what is being measured or what causes it."* Claiming "this entropy is that geometry" from the reading alone is the metamerism error. What is owed is a discriminator instrument, not a name.

*The Recursive Self-Improvement Trap*: *"the output is only as good as the validator. The agent, or the tool, or the frame, or the theory, cannot validate itself."* This derivation therefore cannot settle its own claim.

## 5. Correction to my own §3

I initially overstated "shape, not a name" as a ban on naming any atom. The README's phrase is *"what a **missing** thing would have to look like (a shape, not a name)."* It governs a primitive not held in Γ. Network and space are **both already among the six** (Fig 13 :24, :10). Naming them is a **binding** from each physics frame onto a held atom, not the invention of a new primitive. What remains forbidden is (i) adding a seventh primitive to hold geometry, (ii) claiming the binding moves the wall (Fig 6 : *"IMPORT adds an atom"* - this one adds none), and (iii) turning the binding into a literal identity across two independent frames.

My §4 metamerism point is **not** retracted: the entropy reading still cannot name its source without a discriminator. But the reason is the missing instrument, not a ban on naming held atoms. Both readings are kept visible so neither is quietly lost.

## 6. Two consequences for this solution

**Prestige is not action.** From *The Recursive Self-Improvement Trap*: *"Prestige must never be allowed to validate an action directly... Prestige can fund an action. It can give an action a budget to be tried. But it cannot settle an action. Only the ground can settle it. The Ground Maintainer must be blind to prestige."* A list of famous names agreeing that ER=EPR is true is the named collapse, not corroboration. The legitimate ask is a budget to try the shape against a ground.

**My own audit is a simulation, not a ground reading.** Same essay: *"LLMs can quickly simulate an approximate answer/output... However, the truly testable output would need to be derived from the real architecture or working the formula yourself."* LLM output is *"simulacra."* So `01_figure_grounded_audit.md` is a simulated derivation checked for internal consistency against the figures. It is **not** a test of the claim and is labelled as such. It cannot settle anything it reports on.
