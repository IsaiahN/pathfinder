# Expert verification packet - the Tether on entanglement and geometry (ER=EPR)

**What this is:** a claim in a form a physicist or philosopher of physics can check. Mode, derivation and refuters are stated before the conclusion, so a reader knows what to look for and what would settle it.

**Mode: `specified` - not `grounded`.** No Planck-scale measurement is claimed. This is a shape and a route, not a result.

## 1. The claim, in one paragraph

Geometry is not a primitive among the six substrate terms; it is a residue of composition. Entanglement binds to the network structure (relations that carry influence) and geometry to the space state (adjacency), two distinct held atoms with independent anchors. Their frames are low-overlap and compose at second order, because each first-order core is settled and can serve as an atom at the next level. Over the full shared atom set the cores are isomers; as written they share a functional group. The entropy-to-area map is a provenanced retrodiction, not a framework derivation, and the entropy reading cannot name its source without a discriminator instrument. Literal ER=EPR identity is therefore refused as written: it would collapse two independent frames and destroy the surplus. The framework returns this shape, four falsifiers written in advance, and the kind of instrument that would close the open constant.

## 3. What is explicitly not claimed

- Not a theory of everything; no final room.
- Not a derivation of geometry from quantum mechanics or the reverse. Two frames compose; neither is reduced to the other.
- Not literal identity. "Entanglement is a wormhole" is refused as written, not softened.
- Not a claim that the framework produced the entropy-to-area result. Fig 8 :56 lists it as a case already known and not tested forward.
- Not an entropy-to-area constant. That is a `gap`, not an answer.
- Not `grounded`. Nothing here is asserted as settled by experiment.

## 5. What would settle it

A source-discriminating instrument, not a further argument. From *The Hard Problem is About Source*: a spectrometer resolved the green-reading case where argument could not. Here:

1. **Discriminator test:** an instrument or construction that distinguishes an entropy-equivalent pair from a geometrically connected pair by making different predictions. If none can be described, that is a `gap` on the ground, not a proof of absence (Fig 8 :39 "So a search that finds nothing is never a proof of absence").
2. **Identity test:** a measurement of literal identity between entropy and connectivity at one level. Fires refuter 1 of the audit.
3. **Primitive test:** derive one of the six from the other five, or require a seventh. Fires refuter 2.
4. **Independence test:** show a single evidence pool. Fires refuter 3 and removes the surplus.
