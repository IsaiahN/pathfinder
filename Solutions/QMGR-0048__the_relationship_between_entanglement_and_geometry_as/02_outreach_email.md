**Use:** one-to-one, or as a cover letter attached to a submission through the public venues in `03_public_contact_points.csv`. **Not** for bulk send.

**Subject:** Check one output on entanglement and geometry before you decide whether the theory is worth reading

> Hi [name],
>
> I have one specific output about the relationship between entanglement and geometry that I would like you to check against what you already know. I am not asking you to read a theory first, and I am not asking you to agree with anything. If the output does not survive, there is nothing else to look at.
>
> **The output (`specified`, not `grounded`):** the framework's minimum set has six terms in two kinds, states (space, time, energy) and structures (spectrum, gradient, network), with the rule that composition runs across kinds and never within one. Two things follow before any theory of quantum gravity is invoked:
>
> 1. Geometry is not a primitive. The continuum was removed from the generating set, and smoothness is described as what a composition leaves behind. So "geometry is emergent" is a structural consequence of the six-term set, not a new physical hypothesis.
> 2. Entanglement and geometry bind to different held atoms. Entanglement is a relation that carries influence, which is the network structure. Geometry is adjacency, which is the space state. They are distinct members, each with its own anchor.
>
> **What I am not claiming:** that an EPR pair literally *is* an Einstein-Rosen bridge, that entanglement and geometry are the same quantity, or that the entropy-to-area constant is settled. Literal identity would collapse two frames that have independent evidence, and the framework's own rule says two frames that overlap almost completely are one frame wearing two names. What survives is the weaker, checkable claim: two low-overlap frames compose, the bridge is a correspondence whose provenance must be recorded, and the union has a surplus neither frame has alone. I call the constant a `gap`, not an answer.
>
> **The shape claim written down before the run:** an answer must (i) say which held atom each frame binds to, (ii) name the bond between them, (iii) carry a mode label, and (iv) name a discriminator instrument. My derivation returns (i) and (ii) as a shape and refuses to fill (iii) with `grounded` or (iv) with a measurement, because none exists.
>
> **What would refute it (written before this email, not after):**
> 1. a measurement showing entanglement entropy and geometric connectivity are literally the same quantity at one level, with no lossy transform;
> 2. a derivation of one of the six from the other five, or the discovery that a seventh primitive is needed to hold geometry;
> 3. a demonstration that the two frames draw on one evidence pool, so their agreement carries no surplus;
> 4. a proof that cross-kind composition is forbidden while the entropy-to-area correspondence still holds.
>
> If any one holds, I retract the shape claim. A one-line "this fails because..." is more useful to me than agreement. If you would rather not engage, no reply is needed. Please tell me what *you* would count as a failure before I show you any theory.
>
> **If it survives, and only then:** the public sources are below. The figures, not my summary, are the authority.
> - Verbatim figure text with line numbers (local extract used for this run): `FRAMEWORK/reference/03-FIGURES_TEXT.md` in the Project Pathfinder workspace; public canonical copy: https://github.com/IsaiahN/tether/tree/general-agent/figures
> - Canonical usage rule: https://github.com/IsaiahN/tether/blob/general-agent/figures/README.md
> - Original ER=EPR proposal, Maldacena and Susskind 2013: https://arxiv.org/abs/1306.0533
> - Entanglement and the emergence of geometry, Van Raamsdonk 2010: https://arxiv.org/abs/1005.3035
> - Holographic entanglement entropy, Ryu and Takayanagi 2006: https://arxiv.org/abs/hep-th/0605073
>
> **If you want to use the machinery:** the formula is in the figures link. Run it on a problem you know well and can check against a ground. Write down what would refute your result *before* you run it, and state the mode of what you get. A reported failure is more useful to me than a reported success.

## Distribution rules

- One recipient at a time. No bulk send, no automation, no misleading subject line.
- Record the verdict on stage 1 *before* sending any link. A reply that arrives after the theory is shown is not evidence.
- Do not pool later agreement as corroboration (Fig 2 :40: agreement in a shared evidence pool is the pool talking).
- An opt-out line is implied by "no reply is needed"; make it explicit in the final send if local rules require it.
- Nothing here is sent by this agent. The author hosts and sends.
