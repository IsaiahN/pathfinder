## Independent domain census

I redid the census before reading the primary's conclusions, using the WATSON order: R by arity, symmetry and scale; one line per domain with actors / currency / anchor; partition or union; order by dependency.

- **R (arity / symmetry / scale).** The residual is the failure of `m(b)` to discriminate reduction from displacement. Arity: two quantities under one reading plus a free boundary variable, so a **union compound** (Fig 9: a case in two conditions is not a harder case of either), not one hard question. Symmetry: directed, with source and destination, so the ordered bonds apply, not a symmetric join. Scale: first order in own operations, second at suppliers, third at regions and chains; Fig 11 puts the DDT residual three steps out. Slots: region, supplier, gas, category, stage, time.

- **Domain 2, physical ground.** Actors: mass balance, atmospheric concentration, ecosystem state. Currency: boundary-independent physical units. Anchor: yes. Question type: the anchor. Verdict: the ground.

Anchor: per-slot physical measurement; the habitat itself updates and is not the ground. Question type: enumerated, never composed; a contact cascade. Verdict: where the residual hides.
- **Domain 4, verification frame.** Actors: auditors, standards bodies, regulators, independent instruments. Currency: independence. Anchor: the ground, never agreement. Question type: checking arrangement. Verdict: only low-overlap instruments count.

Order by dependency: fix the anchor, enumerate outward, measure per slot without aggregating, triangulate with low-overlap instruments, settle and record lineage. ## Round trip gap `R_T`

The Symbols table defines `R_T` as the gap between what was sent up and what came back down, `gap(x, (T_E ∘ T_A)(x))`, measuring what the coarser description cannot hold. Fig 4 :30 requires it to be measured rather than assumed. I ran the round trip on the condensation the folder actually uses.

1. **Sent up (x):** the five-constraint shape, chiefly Fig 2 :29-30 (anchor), Fig 11 :43-44 and :52 (enumeration outward), Fig 1 :19 and :22 (per-slot), Fig 2 :31 (independence), Operators `⋛` (threshold comparison).
2. **Abstraction (T_A):** the shorthand in `07` §2 and `08` §1, "a boundary-invariant, full-lifecycle, independently checked measurement."
3. **Concretisation (T_E):** the concrete offer in `04`, "adopt Scope 3 accounting, get third-party assurance, use the consumption-versus-territorial test."

**`R_T` is nonzero.** Three things the shorthand holds do not survive the trip back down:

- the **anchor test** becomes "use a standard"; a convention that decides an outcome has stopped being one (Fig 10), so a standard promoted to verdict is not an anchor;
- the **per-slot rule** becomes category reporting, which can be re-aggregated into the flat formula (Fig 12);
- the **independence requirement** becomes "third-party assurance", which can be three readers of one report (Fig 2 :31).

One thing survives cleanly: harm must be looked for outside the fence line, enumerated outward. That is the residue worth sending. `R_T` was **not** measured numerically, because no ground reading exists; it is recorded qualitatively as the enumerated set of dropped constraints. Treating the shorthand as identical to the shape would set `R_T` to zero and would be the dead-reckoning error Fig 4 :30 names. The honest form is a lossy condensation with a recorded gap, which is what `09_watson_answer.md` states.
