## Distribution: offer, do not push

Figure-grounded reasons:

- **Fig 10 :52** *"Bring a characterised residual, never the source."* Send the residual and the refuter first; offer the links after a verdict on stage 1 is recorded.
- **Fig 10 :42** *"A verifier that can reconstruct the claim cannot verify it."* A recipient who has absorbed the entire framework before being asked for a verdict has joined the frame being checked.
- **Fig 9 :27** *"describe it before you go looking, or any frame you pick will seem to fit."* Ask what they would expect to see if the shape claim were true before giving them the article.
- **Fig 2 :40** *"agreement about the world is worthless when the agreeing frames share an evidence pool."* A mass send selected for people who already agree produces a convention, not corroboration.
- **Fig 2 :42** *"Buy independence in proportion to the cost of being wrong."* The cost of a false improvement claim is high, so spend the effort on a low-overlap checker rather than on a large audience.

**Recommended order in a reply:** (1) the residual and the shape claim; (2) the pre-registered refuter and the question "what would you count as a failure here?"; (3) record their verdict; (4) only then the three links, with the note that the figures, not the summary, are the authority.

## The two-stage offer

- **Stage 1 before stage 2.** The recipient checks the output without holding the framework's interior. Their verdict on stage 1 is recorded before any theory is shown.
- **Stage 2 is opt-in by construction.** Once a recipient has absorbed the theory, their agreement stops being evidence, so later agreement is never pooled as corroboration.
- **Stage 3 sends a method, which is permitted.** Fig 4 :22 *"Only methods cross up. Only head starts come down."* The formula is a generator; giving it away does not give away an answer. Each user still needs a ground and a pre-registered refuter for their own problem.
- **Hard precondition.** The three stages only work if the stage-1 output survives. As drafted, the shape claim is `specified` and ungrounded, with four stated refuters. If a recipient fires one, the procedure shape is withdrawn and no theory is offered.
