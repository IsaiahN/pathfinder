# The Tether's answer: real improvement versus shifted harm

**Mode: `specified` on the substantive claims, `general` on the structural shape. Not `grounded`.** This is a shape and a route, not a result. Simulated derivation checked against the figures (`05_what_shape_means.md` §6), not a ground reading. No emissions inventory, supplier audit or atmospheric measurement was performed.

## 1. The residual it answers

A company reports a lower number inside a boundary it drew. The same number is consistent with total harm falling and with harm moving to a supplier, another region, another lifecycle stage or another impact category. The reported reading cannot discriminate the two. This is a **union compound**, not one hard question: reduction inside the boundary and stability outside it are two conditions, and a case in two conditions is not a harder case of either (Fig 9 :57). It has its own loop and its own ground.

## 2. The shape

1. **The reported number is a frame, not an anchor.** Fig 2 :29: *"An anchor is whatever settles the question and does not change its answer because of what any frame thinks."* Fig 2 :30: *"If it moves when you push on it, it is not an anchor."* `m(b)` moves when the boundary `b` is pushed, so it cannot settle the question. Fig 1 :27: *"A frame cannot score itself with a quantity it also produces."*

2. **What does not move is the ground.** Fig 10 :24: *"the ground does not decay, the channel does."* The anchor is a boundary-invariant physical inventory or mass balance. Fig 10 :18: *"a declared meaning is not a grounded one."* A company's declaration of a boundary or a neutral claim declares a meaning; what can be installed instead is something that can be violated.

3. **Enumerate the habitat outward, per slot.** Fig 11 :43: *"The habitat is enumerated, never composed."* Fig 11 :44: *"List everything in contact with the residual, then what is in contact with those, and outward until the cascade stops mattering."* Fig 11 :52: *"Scoping the habitat to direct contact is how DDT reached the eagles: first-order enumeration, second-order effect, and the residual that mattered was three steps out."* Fig 11 :40: *"An improvement that does not change contact changes nothing, however much it improves."*

4. **Keep the residual per slot and never aggregate it.** Fig 1 :19: *"The residual is indexed by object slot, not aggregated."* Fig 1 :22: *"Aggregating across slots is how a live signal disappears into an average."* Fig 10 :26 names the channel failure: *"stale percepts, dropped transients, saturated metrics, pooled readings."* The headline total is a flat formula (Fig 12 :5); equal totals can be isomers (Fig 12 :117), different substances whose difference is invisible in the number.

5. **Use two instruments, not two readers.** Fig 2 :31: *"Two people reading the same report are not two frames; two instruments pointed at the same thing are."* Fig 2 :35: *"Alignment is triangulation, not negotiation. A mirror cannot correct you."* Fig 2 :40: *"agreement about the world is worthless when the agreeing frames share an evidence pool."* Buy independence in proportion to the cost of being wrong (Fig 2 :42). A third-party assurance of the same inventory is still one frame.

6. **The output is a comparison, not a third number.** The Operators table defines `⋛` as *"a threshold relation between two quantities"*, and Fig 12 notes *"a goal is a comparison, which is why a vocabulary without it cannot state one."* The verdict is whether the off-slot residual cancels the on-slot reduction at a declared threshold. The threshold is a convention and must be declared as one (Fig 10 :18, 47).

7. **The framework returns a shape, not a standard.** Fig 9 :32: *"you get a kind of thing, never the answer itself."* The canonical rule says it reports what a missing thing would look like, a shape rather than a name, then stops at the limit of the instruments and records the lineage. Naming GHG Protocol Scope 3, ISO 14064 or a net-zero standard is a binding from the accounting frame onto this shape, provenanced as such, not a derivation and not an import that moves the wall.

## 3. What it explicitly does not say

- Not that any company's improvement is false. No ground reading was performed.
- Not that a particular disclosure standard is required. That is a name where the framework returns a shape.
- Not that reporting is worthless. It is a necessary frame and an insufficient verdict.
- Not that more reviewers help. Reviewers sharing one evidence pool are one frame.
- Not grounded. Everything here is `specified` or `general` and remains candidate until the ground pays.

## 4. What would settle it - the ground

A source-discriminating instrument, not a further argument. Concretely:

- **Boundary-invariance test:** redraw the reporting boundary outward. If the reported improvement survives the redraw and the number matches a boundary-independent physical inventory across every enumerated slot, world A is supported. If it reverses, world B.
- **Per-slot residual test:** compute the residual at each slot (region, supplier, gas, category, stage) and keep them separate. A positive residual in a supplier or region slot that cancels the local reduction is displacement. The leakage ratio from the carbon-leakage source, increase outside divided by reduction inside, can exceed 100 percent, so cancellation can be more than complete.
- **Consumption-versus-territorial test:** Our World in Data states that if territorial emissions fall while consumption-based emissions rise, that *"would suggest it was 'offshoring' emissions elsewhere."* This is a second instrument pointed at the same thing.
- **Threshold test:** declare the convention that decides "cancels" before running the comparison, and record it. A convention that decides an outcome has stopped being a convention.
- **Standing falsifiers from `01` §3:** any of the five pre-registered conditions fires, and the procedure shape is withdrawn.

## 5. The honest stop

**Unreached, not unreachable.** The five constraints are the shape any valid discrimination procedure must have, but the framework does not hold the instrument that would actually perform the inventory across every supplier and region. Fig 8 :39: *"So a search that finds nothing is never a proof of absence."* Fig 8 :40 distinguishes underivable-in-principle from unreachable-at-this-budget; here the missing item is a measurement obligation, not a new primitive, so the honest report is that a ground reading is owed. The canonical rule says the framework *"stops at the limit of what the available instruments can distinguish, and records the lineage so the stop is honest."* *"Indiscernibility is not identity."*

## 6. One-paragraph version for outreach

> The Tether does not say a company's environmental report is false. It says the reported number cannot do the job being asked of it, because it moves when the reporting boundary moves. A company can distinguish a real improvement from shifted harm only by using an anchor that does not move with the boundary (a boundary-invariant physical inventory), by enumerating suppliers, regions and lifecycle stages outward rather than measuring only its own fence line, by keeping the residual per slot instead of averaging it into one headline total, by using two independent instruments rather than two readers of the same report, and by returning a threshold comparison with a declared convention and a recorded lineage. The figures give that shape; they do not name a standard. No measurement was performed here, so this is a specification of what a check would look like, with four refuters written in advance, not a verdict on any company.
