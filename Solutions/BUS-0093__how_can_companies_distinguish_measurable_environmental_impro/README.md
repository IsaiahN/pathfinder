# Tether outreach - figure-grounded package

**Question:** How can companies distinguish measurable environmental improvements from shifting emissions or harms to suppliers and other regions?

**Source:** business.txt line 124

**Section:** 10. Responsibility, sustainability, and business purpose

**Mode:** `specified` on the substantive claims, `general` on the structural shape. No boundary-independent physical inventory was actually performed here, so nothing in this folder is `grounded`.

**Status:** done

**Registry status:** pending

Boundary. Nothing in this folder was written from a repo file being edited. All figure citations are against the local verbatim extract at `FRAMEWORK/reference/03-FIGURES_TEXT.md`. World sources were fetched and read on 2026-11-22 and are listed in `10_source_ledger.csv`. The residual is stated in effect terms before any figure line is chosen, per the derive-first rule.

## Restatement as an effect

The company reports an improvement: a lower number inside a chosen reporting boundary. That same number is consistent with two different worlds. In world A the total harm fell. In world B the harm moved across the boundary to a supplier, a region, a lifecycle stage or an impact category, and the local number fell only because the harm left the frame. The residual R is the failure of the reported reading to discriminate A from B. An answer must name what would make the discrimination possible, not just praise or condemn the report.

## What is deliberately not here

- No personal emails of named individuals.
- No ranking of people by how likely they are to agree that the framework is useful.
- No bulk-send plan.

Three reasons, in the figures' own words:

1. A room does not become an anchor by getting larger. Fig 2 :35 *"Alignment is triangulation, not negotiation. A mirror cannot correct you."*
2. Selecting recipients for receptiveness manufactures the disqualifying correlation. Fig 2 :40 *"agreement about the world is worthless when the agreeing frames share an evidence pool."*
3. Asking someone to trust the framework before they check the output spends the only thing they contribute. Fig 10 :42 *"A verifier that can reconstruct the claim cannot verify it."*

Legally: harvested personal emails for unsolicited bulk outreach breach GDPR and CAN-SPAM style rules in many jurisdictions and will get the sending domain blacklisted. That is a practical failure on top of the structural one.

## Suggested next step

Take `01` and `08` to the public venues in `03`. The people who can help are those with a checkable public record in environmental measurement or corporate reporting, reached through an opt-in channel, asked for a refutation rather than an endorsement.
