# Outreach draft - check the discrimination procedure before deciding about the framework

**Use:** one-to-one, or as a cover letter attached to a submission through the public venues in `03_public_contact_points.csv`. **Not** for bulk send.

**Hard precondition:** do not send until the refuter in `01_figure_grounded_audit.md` §3 has been restated in your own words and the mode on each claim is checked. The output must survive validation or the two-stage offer collapses on contact.

---

**Subject:** One checkable output on distinguishing real emissions cuts from shifted ones

> Hello,
>
> I have one specific output I would like you to check against what you already know about environmental measurement and corporate reporting. I am not asking you to read a theory first, and I am not asking you to agree with anything. If the output does not survive, there is nothing else to look at.
>
> **The residual (`specified`, not `grounded`).** A company reports a lower environmental number inside a boundary it has drawn. That same number is consistent with two worlds: total harm fell, or the harm moved to a supplier, another region, another lifecycle stage or another impact category and the boundary shrank around it. The reported number alone does not tell you which world you are in. That failure is the residual.
>
> **The shape claim (`specified`).** A discrimination procedure has to do five things. It must use an anchor that does not move when the reporting boundary is redrawn, such as a boundary-invariant physical mass balance. It must enumerate the habitat outward from direct operations to suppliers to regions and lifecycle stages, because the harm that matters is often three steps out. It must keep the residual per slot (region, supplier, gas, category, stage) and refuse to aggregate it into one headline number. It must use at least two low-overlap instruments pointed at the same thing, not two readers of the same report. And it must return a threshold comparison with a declared convention and a recorded lineage, not a third number.
>
> **What I am not claiming.** That any particular standard (Scope 3 accounting, ISO 14064, a net-zero standard) is *the* answer. The shape above is what the derivation gives; a named standard is a binding from the accounting frame onto that shape, and I record it as such. I am also not claiming that any company's reported improvement is false. No measurement was performed here. This is a simulated derivation checked for internal consistency against the figures, and it cannot settle the question it reports on.
>
> **What would refute the shape claim, written before this message:**
> 1. a company-reported metric that is invariant under an outward redraw of the boundary **and** matches a boundary-independent physical inventory across all enumerated slots;
> 2. a single aggregated global number that preserves the per-slot signal under boundary redraw, with no slot-level data;
> 3. a direct-operations measurement that captures a third-order harm without enumerating the intervening actors;
> 4. two readers of the same report counting as two independent frames, or agreement within one evidence pool carrying verdict weight.
>
> If any one holds, the procedure shape is wrong and I will retract it. A one-line "this fails because..." is more useful to me than agreement. If you would rather not engage, no reply is needed.
>
> **If it survives, and only then:** the sources are below. The figures are the authority, not my summary of them.
> - GHG Protocol Corporate Value Chain (Scope 3) Standard, publisher page with the standard PDF: https://ghgprotocol.org/standards/scope-3-standard
> - GHG Protocol article, "Many Companies Inaccurately Estimate the Climate Benefits of Their Products": https://ghgprotocol.org/blog/many-companies-inaccurately-estimate-climate-benefits-their-products
> - Our World in Data, "How do CO2 emissions compare when we adjust for trade?": https://ourworldindata.org/consumption-based-co2
>
> **If you want to use the machinery:** the formula is in the figures link in `04_links_and_distribution.md`. Run it on a measurement problem you know well and can check against a ground. Write down what would refute your result *before* you run it, and state the mode of what you get. A reported failure is more useful to me than a reported success.
>
> With thanks,
> [name]
