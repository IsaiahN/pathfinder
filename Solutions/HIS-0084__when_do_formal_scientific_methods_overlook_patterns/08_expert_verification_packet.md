# Expert verification packet — the Tether on formal methods and local ecological knowledge

**What this is:** a claim in a form a researcher in local ecological knowledge can check. Mode, derivation and refuters are stated before the conclusion, so a reader knows what to look for and what would settle it.

**Mode: `specified` on the conditions, `general` on the shape. Not `grounded`.** No measurement at the ground is claimed by this derivation.

**Status:** a simulated derivation checked for internal consistency against the figures, not a ground reading. It cannot settle the claim it reports on.

## 1. The claim, in one paragraph

A formal scientific method overlooks a pattern that a local practitioner detects when three conditions hold together: the pattern is not expressible in the method's own vocabulary, so its internal search cannot add it; the live case is pooled into an aggregate or the channel at that scale is degraded, so the method's global error reads near zero; and the kind of pattern the method is looking for is a preference it never bound as a term, so its own checks cannot see the preference. The practitioner's frame is a different instrument that has been registering the pattern through long place-based contact. The two frames form a union with extra reach, but only if the transfer is described in effects, its provenance is recorded, and the two frames' independence is bought rather than assumed. The framework returns these conditions, not a list of named disciplines.

## 2. Three checkable sub-claims

### C1 — framework-internal; checkable by reading Figures 6 and 8

- **Statement:** a pattern outside `closure(Γ_f)` is not reached by more internal search; SEARCHED must be asked before an import is declared.
- **Source, verbatim:** Fig 6 :21 "MINT composes inside the closure and can never add an atom." Fig 6 :23 "No frame escapes its own closure. The bound moves only on contact with a frame whose closure differs." Fig 8 :32 "Closure is idempotent. Composing inside a closed set never leaves it, so no search, however long, adds an atom."
- **What to check:** whether the pattern in the case you know was actually outside the formal frame's representable terms, or already representable and merely overlooked. That decision decides whether an import is owed at all.
- **Refuter:** demonstrate that the pattern was inside `closure(Γ_f)` and merely unfound. Then the remedy is more independent search, not another atom (Fig 8 :41-43).

### C2 — measurement-facing; checkable in any dataset that aggregates cases

- **Statement:** a global near-zero residual coexists legally with a live local slot; aggregation removes the only signal that says where to look.
- **Source, verbatim:** Fig 1 :13 "a global R near zero with one live slot residual is a legal state." Fig 1 :19 "The residual is indexed by object slot, not aggregated." Fig 1 :22 "Aggregating across slots is how a live signal disappears into an average." Fig 10 :24 "the ground does not decay, the channel does."
- **What to check:** in your own field data, does the pooled statistic reverse or erase a subgroup pattern? The kidney-stone and Berkeley-admissions examples in the world literature are standard instances; the point here is structural, not statistical novelty.
- **Refuter:** show that the formal residual on the relevant slot is nonzero and trending down, so the miss was registered and not aggregated away.

### C3 — verification-facing; checkable against how a study was actually run

- **Statement:** the union surplus is real only with provenance and independence; convergent derivation and adopted import look identical in their contents.
- **Source, verbatim:** Fig 8 :45 "Two frames together reach what neither reaches alone." Fig 8 :48 "How much two frames draw on the same evidence decides both whether their agreement means anything and whether their union has any surplus." Fig 8 :51 "Convergent derivation and adopted import look identical in the contents. Only the record of where each came from separates them" Fig 2 :40 "agreement about the world is worthless when the agreeing frames share an evidence pool"
- **What to check:** did the two sides use separate instruments and separate experience, or the same dataset and the same review? Is the origin of each term recorded? A study that defines observation limits and runs independent validation is doing this; a study that simply reports agreement is not.
- **Refuter:** show a shared evidence pool, or show the direction of import reversed. Then the agreement is the pool talking and the surplus claim fails.

## 3. What is explicitly not claimed

- Not that local or Indigenous knowledge is always correct, or that a ground cannot refuse it.
- Not that formal science is worthless, or that "science" is a single frame.
- Not that a named discipline is uniquely blind, or that this packet names the missing primitive. The framework returns a shape, not a name (Fig 9 :32).
- Not that more interviews, more data or a larger review panel settles the claim. Fig 7 :13 "agreement compounds, evidence does not."
- Not that consent and community governance are optional. They are a condition of a legitimate import, stated as an ethical and legal requirement, not derived from the figures.
- Not `grounded`. No measurement was taken in this derivation.

## 6. What an expert can check in twenty minutes

1. Read Fig 6 :21 and :23, Fig 1 :13 and :22, and Fig 8 :45 and :51. Six short passages.
2. Pick one case you know well. Was the pattern outside the formal vocabulary, or already inside it and unfound? That one question decides C1.
3. Look at the pooled statistic in that case. Does it erase a subgroup pattern? That decides C2.
4. Ask whether the two sides drew on separate instruments and separate experience, and where each term came from. That decides C3.
5. Send the refutation. A failure is more useful than an endorsement.
