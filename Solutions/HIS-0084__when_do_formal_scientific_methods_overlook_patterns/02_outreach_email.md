**Use:** one-to-one, or as a cover note attached to a submission through the public venues in `03_public_contact_points.csv`. **Not** for bulk send.

**Hard precondition:** do not send until the shape claim and its refuter in `01_figure_grounded_audit.md` §5 are stated in your own words. The two-stage offer collapses on contact if stage 1 does not survive.

**Subject:** One checkable claim about when formal methods miss local patterns

> Hi [name],
>
> I have one specific output I would like you to check against what you already know. I am not asking you to read a theory first, and I am not asking you to agree with it. If the output does not survive, there is nothing else to look at.
>
> **The output (`specified`, not `grounded`):** a formal scientific method is a frame with a fixed vocabulary and instruments. It can only compose from what that vocabulary holds. From that rule it follows that a pattern a local practitioner can see will be overlooked by a formal method when three things hold together: the pattern is not expressible in the formal method's terms, so no amount of internal search adds it; the channel at the scale where the pattern lives pools or drops the signal, so the method's global statistic looks clean while one local case is live; and the kind of pattern the method is looking for is a preference it never wrote down, so its own checks cannot see the preference. The local practitioner's frame is a different instrument that has already been returning the signal for years. That is the shape of the claim. It is not a measurement.
>
> **What I am not claiming:** that local or Indigenous knowledge is always right, that formal science is worthless, that a named discipline is uniquely blind, or that a particular pattern has been established here. The framework returns a shape, not a name. Convergent discovery and adopted import look identical in the contents and only the record separates them. Until provenance is shown they carry zero evidentiary weight. I am also not asking you to treat a room of agreeing researchers as a check. Agreement is not the ground.
>
> **What would refute the shape claim:**
> 1. the pattern is demonstrated to be inside the formal method's own vocabulary and merely unfound, in which case the remedy is more search rather than a new import;
> 2. the formal method's error on that case is already registered and trending down, so nothing was aggregated away;
> 3. the formal and local reports draw on the same underlying data or the same review, so their agreement is one evidence pool rather than two independent frames;
> 4. a measurement at the ground shows the pattern absent or the local prediction failing.
>
> I wrote these down before sending this. If any one holds, the part it touches is withdrawn. A one-line "this fails because..." is more useful to me than agreement. If you would rather not engage, no reply is needed.
>
> **If it survives, and only then:** the sources are below. The figures are the authority, not my summary of them.
> - Canonical figure text with line numbers (local framework file): `FRAMEWORK/reference/03-FIGURES_TEXT.md`
> - Canonical usage rule (local framework file): `FRAMEWORK/reference/FIGURES_README_canonical.md`
> - Figures and formula, branch `general-agent`: https://github.com/IsaiahN/tether/tree/general-agent/figures
>
> **If you want to use the machinery:** the formula is in the figures link. Run it on a problem you know well and can check against a ground. Write down what would refute your result before you run it, and state the mode of what you get. A reported failure is more useful to me than a reported success.

## Two-stage discipline for the reply

**Stage 1.** Send only the shape claim and the four refuters. Ask what the recipient would count as a failure before showing any theory. Record that verdict first.

**Stage 2, opt-in only.** If they are curious, offer the three links with the note that the figures, not the summary, are the authority. Their later agreement is never pooled as corroboration, because once they have absorbed the framework they are no longer an outside frame (Fig 2 :40; Fig 10 :42).

**Stage 3, opt-in only.** Offer the formula as a method to reuse. Each user writes their own refuter and states their own mode.

If the stage-1 output fails, stop. Do not advance to stage 2 on an endorsement.
