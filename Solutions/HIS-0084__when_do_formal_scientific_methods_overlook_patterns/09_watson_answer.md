## QUESTION

Decide, in effect terms, the conditions under which a formal scientific method's residual on a slot reads near zero while a local practitioner's residual on that slot is live. Option A: the pattern was not there, or was not important, so the miss is a defect of the practitioners or of attention. Option B: the pattern is outside the formal frame's closure, or its live slot was pooled away, or the frame's sought-for shape is an invisible preference, while the local frame is a differing closure that already holds the discrimination. A leads to more of the same search and to extraction without provenance. B leads to a described residual, SEARCHED first, then an instrument or a provenanced import, with independence bought and a refuter written in advance.

## VERDICT (from the figures)

The shape survives, the named list does not. A formal method overlooks a local pattern when the pattern is outside its closure, when the live slot is pooled or the channel is degraded at that scale, and when the sought-for shape is an invisible preference. The local frame holds the discrimination as a proto-instrument in an enumerated habitat, so the two frames form a union with a surplus reached only by a provenanced import with a shadow test and bought independence. SEARCHED remains unanswered at this budget, so condition one is conditional, and no part of this is `grounded`. This is `specified` on the conditions and `general` on the shape. The demand for an exact ranked list of fields or cases is `gap`.

- **cost if wrong:** bounded and locatable. The packet states its mode, writes refuters before contact and records the lineage, so an error is findable rather than invisible. The real cost is ethical and structural: extracting local knowledge without provenance or consent, or accepting agreement from a shared evidence pool as corroboration. Fig 2 :42 "Buy independence in proportion to the cost of being wrong." Both are avoidable by leading with the shape claim and its refuter and by treating governance as part of the import gate.
- **undoable:** yes. The conditions are stated separately, so a failed SEARCHED check removes only condition one, and a failed independence check removes only condition five. No primitive has to be withdrawn. A sent claim can be superseded by a corrected packet, and the retraction log in `01` keeps the earlier reading visible.
- **reversed by:** a ground reading, not agreement. A named case in which the pattern is shown to be inside the formal closure and merely unfound would send the remedy back to more independent search. A slot-level residual that is already nonzero and trending down would remove the aggregation mechanism. A shared evidence pool would remove the surplus claim. A reversed provenance trail would remove the direction of import. A measurement at the ground showing the pattern absent would refuse the local prediction. Until one of these fires, the verdict stands as `candidate`.
