## 0. The question under audit, in effect terms

> When do formal scientific methods overlook patterns that local practitioners can detect?

Restated as a residual: an observation `o'` is live for a practitioner (their `R_l⁺_s > 0` on some slot `s`), while the formal method's library predicts it away (`Γ_f(b,a) ≈ o'` on that same slot), so the formal global residual `R_f` reads near zero and no term in `Γ_f` names the pattern. The question asks for the conditions under which that divergence is stable rather than accidental.

What an answer would look like, written down and bound before the run (Formula step "Direction"): a set of conditions on two frames and one shared ground. It is a shape, not a list of named disciplines. Fig 9 :32 "you get a kind of thing, never the answer itself."

## 1. Residual and domain census, before choosing any figure line

### 1.1 R by arity, symmetry and scale

- **Arity:** three-place. A pattern `p`, a formal frame `F`, and a local frame `L`, both ranging against a shared ground `G`. It is not a two-place "science versus tradition" comparison; dropping `G` removes the anchor and leaves a negotiation (Fig 2 :33-34).
- **Symmetry:** asymmetric in the run. `L` registers `p` and `F` does not. The asymmetry does not reverse by adding more of `F`'s own search (Fig 6 :21). The relation is not a symmetric bond in Fig 12's sense; it is a directed import from the frame whose closure differs.
- **Scale:** the pattern lives at a local scale (a place, a season, a lifetime of repeated contact); the formal method aggregates upward to a regional or global statistic. The transform between scales is lossy (Fig 4 :27).

### 1.2 One line per domain: actors, currency, anchor

| domain | actors | currency | anchor (nameable?) | question type |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| **D1, the formal method** | researchers, instruments, statistical models, review boards | prediction residual, significance, description length, replication | yes: experiment, dataset, measurement. Fig 1 :23 "The ground enters as the only metric." | partition: which compositions are inside `closure(Γ_f)` |
| **D2, the local practitioners** | harvesters, fishers, farmers, Elders, navigators, healers, artisans | practical success and failure over a lifetime of contact; fine discrimination | yes: the resource, the returning animal, the harvest, the board that pays. Fig 11 :48 "You do not invent the list. You read it off the world, and what you cannot perceive or measure yet is the residual." | partition: what is in `closure(Γ_l)` and not in `closure(Γ_f)` |
| **D3, verification and translation** | the outside checker, the submission venue, the record, the community that owns the knowledge | independence versus pooled agreement, provenance, refutation | yes: the same ground, never the agreement. Fig 2 :29-30 "An anchor is whatever settles the question and does not change its answer because of what any frame thinks." | union: two conditions, one compound |

No nameable anchor means not a domain yet. All three have one, so all three are domains. D3's anchor is the ground, not the experts' consensus; a consensus that decides an outcome has stopped being a convention (Seat Map :163).

### 1.3 Partition or union

This is a **union compound**, not one hard question and not a clean partition (Fig 9 :54 "And when the split lands on a union rather than a partition, unbundle before you search again."). Fig 9 :4-5: "Disagreements that do not shrink with effort, where each rule keeps working well on a different subset, are not one hard question but several well-formed ones. Split it rather than search." Unbundle into three loops before searching:

- **C1, closure:** what atom or discrimination is absent from `Γ_f`?
- **C2, channel:** what did the instrument pool, saturate or drop at the local scale?
- **C3, translation:** how does `p` travel from `Γ_l` to `Γ_f` without being sharpened into terms only one side can read?

A single lever applied to C1 leaves C2 and C3 standing, because they are different conditions, not degrees of the same one (Fig 9 :55).

### 1.4 Order by dependency (Fig 3)

1. Describe the residual in effect terms before choosing a frame (Fig 9 :27-28).
2. Ask SEARCHED first (Formula step 7), because INWARD and OUTWARD assume its answer.
3. If it is not in `closure(Γ_f)` and unfound, refine the instrument (INWARD) or import from `Γ_l` (OUTWARD). Never read one against the other (Fig 6 :31).
4. Settle each frame separately against the ground (Fig 2 :45), then compose. Optimising a link that already works cannot rescue one that does not (Fig 3 :19).

A link with no instrument is not a link that works. Fig 3 :27 "A link with no instrument is not a link that works. It is a link nobody has looked at." C3 is where this chain usually breaks.

## 3. Findings

### F1 — The overlook is a reachability failure, not an absence (decisive for C1)

Fig 6 :21 "MINT composes inside the closure and can never add an atom."

Fig 6 :23 "No frame escapes its own closure. The bound moves only on contact with a frame whose closure differs."

Fig 8 :32 "Closure is idempotent. Composing inside a closed set never leaves it, so no search, however long, adds an atom."

The pattern `p` is not in `closure(Γ_f)`. More grants, more replicates, more literature search inside `Γ_f` sweep the same wall; they do not move it. Fig 8 :39 "So a search that finds nothing is never a proof of absence." Therefore "science overlooked it because it was not there" is not available as a figure-grounded move, and neither is "the local practitioners are merely noisy anecdotes waiting to be absorbed by more of the same method."

**But SEARCHED comes first.** Fig 6 :49 "more search at the same reach: is it in here and unfound? The closure does not change. Same atoms, same wall, more of the space actually visited." Fig 8 :40-43 "Underivable: go outside. Unreachable-at-budget: the term is already in the closure and was not found, so the remedy is more search, not another atom." Declaring an import before running SEARCHED adds an atom that was not needed, and Fig 6 prices a wrong atom above starting from primitives.

**Refuter:** if `p` is demonstrated to be in `closure(Γ_f)` and merely unfound, the closure diagnosis is wrong. The remedy is distributed, independent search inside the frame, not an import. Status: `triangulated` (Fig 6 closure definition and Fig 8 idempotence theorem, low overlap between them). Mode `specified`.

### F2 — A live slot disappears into the aggregate (decisive for C2)

Fig 1 :13 "a global R near zero with one live slot residual is a legal state."

Fig 1 :19 "The residual is indexed by object slot, not aggregated."

Fig 1 :22 "Aggregating across slots is how a live signal disappears into an average."

Fig 10 :24 "the ground does not decay, the channel does."

Fig 10 :25-26 "stale percepts, dropped transients, saturated metrics, pooled readings"

A formal method that reports a global statistic can be perfectly correct about its global number and still have no error signal on the slot where the pattern lives. The ground has not moved; the channel to it at that scale has. This is the arithmetic content of the world source on Simpson's paradox: "a trend appears in several groups of data but disappears or reverses when the groups are combined" (Wikipedia contributors, 2026). The source is corroboration only; the figure line decides.

**Refuter:** if the formal method's residual on the relevant slot is nonzero and trending down, the pattern was already registered and the aggregation mechanism is wrong. Status: `triangulated` (Fig 1 slot indexing and Fig 10 channel decay, different quantities). Mode `specified`.

### F3 — The formal frame cannot see its own sought-for shape (D1's self-check)

Fig 5 :62 "A frame searching for an answer of a particular shape holds that shape as a PREFERENCE rather than as a term."

Fig 5 :63 "none runs on the DIRECTION OF THE QUESTION"

The Formula's "Direction" section says the same thing: "a frame searching for an answer of a given SHAPE holds that shape as a PREFERENCE, not a term. Unbound, predicting nothing, no residual - so nothing above can see it." Seat Map :190 repeats it as a law. So a discipline can run every internal check, refuse the wrong terms, and still never ask whether the *kind* of pattern it is looking for is the reason it misses this one. A preference is not a term, so it accrues no residual.

The remedy is a discipline, not a step: write down what an answer would look like, bind it, let it accrue a residual (Formula "Direction"). Section 0 does this for this question.

**Refuter:** if the sought-for shape is shown to be a bound, predicting term that accrues a residual in the formal frame, then this limit does not apply there. Status: `triangulated` across Fig 5 and the Seat Map law, but note both are the same framework pool, so this is internal agreement, not independent witness. Mode `general`.

### F4 — The local frame is a proto-instrument already returning something (governs C1/C2 together)

Fig 6 :32 "An instrument is not built from a description. It is improved from a worse instrument already returning something."

Fig 6 :33 "A wandering point in the sky was already a reading, and every sharper instrument was built on the residual the last one produced."

Fig 11 :43 "The habitat is enumerated, never composed."

Fig 11 :48 "You do not invent the list. You read it off the world, and what you cannot perceive or measure yet is the residual."

The practitioner is not an anecdote to be converted into a hypothesis by an argument. Their discrimination is a reading that already registers `p`; it is the worse instrument from which a sharper one can be built. What is unavailable is "a sensor for something that has never registered anywhere, at any resolution" (Fig 6 :34), and here it has registered, for decades, in a habitat enumerated rather than composed. The 2021 walrus study reports that the timing shift was "only possible to understand using rigorous methods to interpret Inuit TEK/LEK" (Martinez-Levasseur et al., *Polar Biology*, 2021), which is a world case of exactly this figure structure.

**Refuter:** if the local report is shown to be a post-hoc reading of a pattern first named by the formal frame, or to draw on the same dataset, it is not a proto-instrument and this finding fails (Fig 8 :51). Status: `triangulated` (Fig 6 instrument rule and Fig 11 habitat rule, different domains). Mode `specified` for the structure; the walrus case is `grounded` in the world literature only.

### F5 — The pattern lives in the surplus of two differing closures (governs the union)

Fig 8 :45 "Two frames together reach what neither reaches alone."

Fig 8 :46 "Composition crosses the boundary between them, and that surplus is where imports live."

Fig 8 :48 "How much two frames draw on the same evidence decides both whether their agreement means anything and whether their union has any surplus."

Fig 8 :51 "Convergent derivation and adopted import look identical in the contents. Only the record of where each came from separates them"

Fig 8 :53 "Residual first, frame second."

The union has a surplus only where the closures differ. The record of which atom came from where is the whole issue: a term that arrived by convergence and a term that arrived by adoption look the same on the page. This is why the outreach artifact sends the residual and the refuter first and withholds the framework.

**Refuter:** if the two frames draw on one evidence pool (the same report, the same review, the same dataset), their agreement is not corroboration and the surplus claim fails (Fig 2 :40). Status: `triangulated` (Fig 8 surplus and Fig 2 independence, low overlap). Mode `specified`.

### F6 — The import is lossy, and provenance is the only protection (governs C3)

Fig 4 :27 "The transform between scales loses information, and that is the point."

Fig 4 :31-32 "The gap is not an error to eliminate. It is the measurement of what the coarser description cannot hold, and it is the only honest report of how much was given up."

Fig 6 :30 "The description travels. Fails by being sharpened into terms only we can read. Cast it in effect terms."

Fig 9 :30-31 "your words are borrowed too, so describe the effect rather than the cause"

*Decoding Tacit Knowledge* (Isaiah N, 2026-11-10) names what is lost: "Writing carries the context well and the subtext partly, but it almost never carries the pretext, the 'you had to be there' part." The transferable form is "a plain description of what you'd expect to see, because anyone can check that against the world." The same essay warns that long close contact uses up the extra reach: "Long, close contact pulls the frames together, so the process that makes communication easy slowly uses up that extra reach."

So the import must be made in effect terms, with its origin stamped, and the round-trip gap measured rather than assumed zero. Fig 8 :50: "But shared atoms are not the signature. Two frames can hold the same atom by convergence, reaching it separately, which is corroboration at maximum independence." Convergence and adoption are separated by the record, not the contents.

**Refuter:** if a compressed transfer from `Γ_l` to `Γ_f` is shown to preserve every discriminating correction, the loss claim fails and `R_T` is zero for that transfer. Status: `triangulated` (Fig 4, Fig 6, Fig 9 plus the essay; note the essay shares an author with the figures, so it is weak corroboration). Mode `general`.

### F7 — Agreement cannot play the role of the ground (governs D3)

Fig 2 :35 "Alignment is triangulation, not negotiation. A mirror cannot correct you."

Fig 2 :40 "agreement about the world is worthless when the agreeing frames share an evidence pool"

Fig 2 :42 "Buy independence in proportion to the cost of being wrong."

Fig 7 :13 "agreement compounds, evidence does not"

Fig 7 :35 "A room's agreement is variation, not a verdict. Selection between rooms is where the ground votes."

A workshop full of sympathetic reviewers cannot settle whether the pattern is real. The walrus study is instructive precisely because it did not rely on agreement: it defined the spatial and temporal limits of participant observation, ran internal validation workshops, and excluded observations that participants could not confirm. The ground was the returning animals and the ice record, not consensus.

**Refuter:** if the agreement is shown to come from two frames with genuinely separate instruments and separate experience, it is corroboration at maximum independence (Fig 8 :50) and this finding does not bar it. Status: `triangulated` (Fig 2 and Fig 7, different figures and failure modes). Mode `general`.

### F8 — The "when" question has a shape answer, not a name answer (honest limit)

Fig 9 :32 "you get a kind of thing, never the answer itself."

Fig 13 :134 "Unreachable is a verdict here, not a silence"

Fig 13 :144 "genuine indeterminacy is the open case, unresolved."

The figures return a shape of the conditions under which an overlook occurs. They do not return a ranked list of named disciplines, named practitioners, or named patterns, and naming one as *the* answer would be the forbidden move (*figures README*: "what a missing thing would have to look like (a shape, not a name)"). Here the missing thing is an atom in `Γ_f`, so the rule applies directly. For the demand "give me the exact list of cases", the honest verdict is `gap`: unreachable with this library, not a silence.

**Refuter:** if a figure line is found that ranks named fields or names a specific missing primitive, this limit is withdrawn. Status: `single-source` for the naming limit (figures README plus Fig 9, same pool). Mode `general`.

### F9 — Three conditions, not one lever (compound verdict)

Fig 9 :55 "A bundled problem is usually a union of overlapping conditions, not a list of separate ones."

Fig 9 :56 "A single lever clears the region where only one condition holds and leaves the intersections standing, because the second condition still binds."

The overlook is C1 and C2 and C3, not a weighted average of them. A policy that only funds more formal search clears C1 where the atom was already in the closure and leaves C2 and C3 standing. A policy that only extracts local knowledge without provenance clears C2 and leaves C1 and C3 standing. Verdicts are kept per domain in `07_the_tether_answer.md` and never averaged.

**Refuter:** if a single intervention is shown to close all three conditions at once without importing a new atom, the union reading is wrong and the problem was a partition after all. Status: `triangulated` (Fig 9 union rule and Fig 3 dependency order). Mode `specified`.

## 4. Three routes kept distinct (Formula step 7)

| route | question | answer here |
|---|---|---|
| **SEARCHED** | Is `p` in `closure(Γ_f)` and merely unfound? | Asked first and not assumed. If yes, F1 is withdrawn and more independent search is the remedy. Unanswered at this budget: no survey of a specific formal closure was run. |
| **INWARD** | Is the formal representation adequate to hold `p`? | Partially no: F2 shows the channel pooled the live slot. An instrument is improved from a worse one already returning something (Fig 6 :32), and the practitioner's discrimination is that something. |
| **OUTWARD** | Does another frame already hold it? | Yes in the world case: `Γ_l` holds the discrimination. Import is gated by provenance and a shadow test and is debited against independence (Fig 8, Seat Map). |

INWARD and OUTWARD are opposed and were not read against each other. No import was declared against a mere budget limit (Fig 8 :41-43).

## 5. Pre-registered refuter

Written here before the conclusion and before outreach, per Seat Map :287 "a falsifier written before the run is a design instrument, not only an honesty device."

The shape claim is refuted if any of the following holds:

1. **SEARCHED fires against F1.** A pattern a practitioner detects is demonstrated to be inside `closure(Γ_f)` and merely unfound. Then the remedy is more search, not import (Fig 8 :41-43).
2. **F2's condition fails.** The formal method's residual on the relevant slot is nonzero and trending down, so the miss was already registered and was not aggregated away (Fig 1 :13, :22).
3. **Independence fails.** The formal and local frames draw on one evidence pool (same dataset, same report, same review), so their agreement is the pool talking and the union surplus is zero (Fig 2 :40; Fig 8 :48).
4. **Provenance fails.** The alleged local term is shown to have been adopted from the formal frame after the fact, so the direction of import reverses (Fig 8 :51).
5. **Ground fires against the local claim.** A measurement at the ground shows the pattern absent or the prediction failing, so the practitioner's discrimination is not a proto-instrument after all.

If any fires, the finding it touches is withdrawn. Until one is run, no part of this may be labelled `grounded`.

## 6. Recommendation

1. Lead with the shape and the pre-registered refuter, not with a list of fields (F8).
2. Run SEARCHED before declaring an import; do not let a budget limit masquerade as underivability (F1).
3. Describe the residual in effect terms and cast the import that way (Fig 9 :30-31, F6).
4. Separate the three conditions and fund each link that is actually broken (F9, Fig 3).
5. Buy independence in proportion to the cost of being wrong, and record which atom came from where (F5, F7).
6. Treat community governance and consent as part of the import gate, not as an afterthought. Fig 11 :61 "Introduction is available; exclusion is not." The figure governs the seat's power over the habitat; it does not license taking knowledge without the holders' agreement.
7. Fig 4 :25 "A recording carried upward looks like knowledge and is a description of one occasion." Send the generator: the residual, the routes and the refuter, not a conclusion.

## 8. Updated verdict

The shape survives and is stated as conditions, not names: an overlook is expected when the pattern is outside the formal closure (F1), when a live slot is pooled into a global statistic or the channel is degraded at that scale (F2), when the formal frame's sought-for shape is an invisible preference (F3), and when a local frame holds the discrimination as a proto-instrument in an enumerated habitat (F4). The two frames form a union whose surplus is reachable only through a provenanced import with a shadow test and bought independence (F5, F6, F7). The demand for an exact named list is a `gap` (F8), and the three conditions must not be averaged (F9).

Per domain: D1 `triangulated` on F1 and F3, D2 `triangulated` on F4, D3 `triangulated` on F5 and F7. F8 is `single-source`. Nothing is `grounded` by the figures. This is `specified` on the conditions and `general` on the shape, a simulated derivation checked for internal consistency, not a ground reading.
