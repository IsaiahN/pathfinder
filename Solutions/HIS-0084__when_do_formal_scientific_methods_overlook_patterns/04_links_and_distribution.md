## Things in these sources that bear directly on the question

1. **The walrus study is the cleanest world case of the figure structure.** It states that long-term scientific datasets "rarely exist for species living in remote areas such as the Arctic," then shows that a migration shift of a month was detectable in the observations of 33 hunters and Elders. That is F2 (no instrument at the scale) and F4 (a proto-instrument already returning something) in one case. It also shows the discipline required: participant observation limits were defined, validation workshops excluded unconfirmed observations, and the sea-ice record was used as a separate anchor. Cite it as a case, not as a proof of the framework.
2. **The tacit-knowledge source names what the import loses.** Polanyi's "we can know more than we can tell" and the essay's context, subtext and pretext distinction explain why a written extraction of local knowledge is lossy. The transferable substitute is "a plain description of what you'd expect to see." That is Fig 6 :30 and Fig 9 :30-31 in other words, and it is the reason the outreach artifact leads with a checkable effect claim.
3. **The statistics source gives the arithmetic of F2.** A pooled trend can reverse the subgroup trend. The figures call the same move "aggregating across slots" (Fig 1 :22) and "pooled readings" (Fig 10 :25-26). Two different fields, one structure, but note the overlap caveat below.
4. **The expert-system source gives the engineering analogue of F1.** A rule-based system is "a closed world with specific knowledge" and has a named "knowledge acquisition problem." It is the most literal instance of "MINT composes inside the closure and can never add an atom" outside the framework.
5. **The animal-culture and RPD sources are the low-overlap field checks.** They show that socially transmitted, experience-loaded discrimination is not unique to human science and is exactly what fast expert detection runs on. They corroborate Fig 11's habitat claim without sharing the framework's vocabulary.

## Distribution: offer, do not push

Figure-grounded reasons:

- **Fig 4 :22-25** "Only methods cross up. Only head starts come down. A recording carried upward looks like knowledge and is a description of one occasion." Sending the whole framework before anyone has described a residual sends a recording, not a generator. Lead with the shape and the refuter.
- **Fig 10 :42** "A verifier that can reconstruct the claim cannot verify it." Seat 4 must not hold the interiors. A recipient who has absorbed the framework before giving a verdict has joined the frame being checked.
- **Fig 9 :27-28** "describe it before you go looking, or any frame you pick will seem to fit." Ask what they would expect to see if the shape claim were true before offering the links.
- **Seat Map :163** "High correlation is disqualifying for a verdict and efficient for a convention." A list selected for people who already agree produces a convention, not corroboration.
- **Fig 2 :42** "Buy independence in proportion to the cost of being wrong." One careful outside checker is worth more than many agreeing insiders here, because misappropriating local knowledge has a real cost.

**Recommended order in a reply:** (1) the shape claim and the four refuters; (2) ask what they would count as a failure and record it; (3) only then the links, with the note that the figures, not the summary, are the authority.
