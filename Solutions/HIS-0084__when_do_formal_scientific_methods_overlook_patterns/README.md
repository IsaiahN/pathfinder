## The question restated as an effect

A formal scientific method is a frame with a library `Γ_f`, an instrument set, and a global residual `R_f`. A local practitioner is a frame with a different library `Γ_l` and a different instrument, long place-based contact. Option A: say the formal method failed because the pattern was not there, or was not important. Option B: say the pattern was live on a slot the formal frame has no term for, the formal frame's global residual read near zero, and the local frame's closure already held the discrimination. A leads to blaming the world or the practitioners and to importing nothing. B leads to a described residual, a search of the formal closure first, then an instrument or an import from a frame whose closure differs, with provenance and a refuter written in advance.

What an answer would look like, bound as an ordinary term before the run: a **set of conditions under which the formal frame's residual on a slot is near zero while the local frame's residual on that slot is live, and the two frames' closures differ**. The sought-for shape is bound here so it can accrue a residual and be refused (Formula step "Direction"; Fig 5 :62).

## What is deliberately not here

- No personal emails of named individuals.
- No ranking of people by how likely they are to agree with the claim.
- No bulk-send plan.
- No claim that local knowledge is always correct, or that formal science is worthless.

Three reasons in the figures' own words:

1. **Agreement is not a verdict.** Fig 2 :35 "Alignment is triangulation, not negotiation. A mirror cannot correct you." Selecting recipients for likely agreement manufactures the correlation that would make their agreement worthless (Seat Map :163).
2. **A verifier must not hold the interiors.** Fig 10 :42 "A verifier that can reconstruct the claim cannot verify it." A practitioner who has absorbed the whole framework before giving a verdict has joined the frame being checked.
3. **Prestige is not action.** *The Recursive Self-Improvement Trap*: "Prestige can fund an action. It can give an action a budget to be tried. But it cannot settle an action. Only the ground can settle it." The ask gives the claim a budget to be tried, not an endorsement.

Legally: bulk unsolicited email to scraped addresses breaches GDPR and CAN-SPAM style rules in many jurisdictions. Public opt-in submission routes avoid that failure as well as the structural one.
