# The Tether's answer: when formal methods overlook patterns local practitioners detect

**Mode: `specified` on the conditions, `general` on the shape. Not `grounded`.** This is a shape and a route, not a result. Simulated derivation checked against the figures (`05_what_shape_means.md` §6), not a ground reading.

## 1. The residual it answers

A pattern `p` is live for a local practitioner (`R_l⁺_s > 0`) and silent for a formal method (`Γ_f(b,a) ≈ o'` on that slot, global `R_f` near zero). The question asks for the conditions under which that split is stable. It is a union compound: closure, channel and translation, not one hard question (Fig 9 :55 "A bundled problem is usually a union of overlapping conditions, not a list of separate ones."). Each condition has its own loop and its own ground.

## 2. The shape

1. **The pattern is outside the formal closure.** Fig 6 :21 "MINT composes inside the closure and can never add an atom." Fig 6 :23 "No frame escapes its own closure. The bound moves only on contact with a frame whose closure differs." No amount of internal search adds the missing discrimination. **But SEARCHED is asked first** (Fig 8 :41-43); if the atom was already in `Γ_f` and merely unfound, this condition does not hold and more independent search is the remedy.
2. **A live slot is pooled into an aggregate, or the channel is degraded at that scale.** Fig 1 :13 "a global R near zero with one live slot residual is a legal state." Fig 1 :22 "Aggregating across slots is how a live signal disappears into an average." Fig 10 :24 "the ground does not decay, the channel does." The formal global number can be right while the local case is live.
3. **The sought-for shape is an invisible preference.** Fig 5 :62 "A frame searching for an answer of a particular shape holds that shape as a PREFERENCE rather than as a term." The formal frame's own checks run on the mechanism, not on the direction of its question, so it can be internally flawless and still never ask whether the kind of pattern it looks for is the cause of the miss.
4. **The local frame is a proto-instrument already returning something.** Fig 6 :32 "An instrument is not built from a description. It is improved from a worse instrument already returning something." Fig 11 :43 "The habitat is enumerated, never composed." Long place-based contact has compressed the world along axes the formal instrument does not represent; this is the differing closure that can supply the import.
5. **The import must be provenanced, effect-described and independently checked.** Fig 8 :45 "Two frames together reach what neither reaches alone." Fig 8 :51 "Convergent derivation and adopted import look identical in the contents. Only the record of where each came from separates them." Fig 2 :42 "Buy independence in proportion to the cost of being wrong." The union has a surplus only where the closures actually differ and the origin of each term is recorded.

## 4. What it explicitly does not say

- Not that local or Indigenous knowledge is always correct, or that a community's report is immune to ground refusal.
- Not that formal science is worthless. The formal frame ranges against the same ground and settles most of its slots; the claim is about a specific class of miss.
- Not that a named discipline is uniquely blind, or that "science" is one frame. The answer returns a shape of conditions, not a name (Fig 9 :32).
- Not that "more data" or "more community interviews" clears the miss by themselves. Volume is a filter for the budget, never a witness for the verdict (Fig 9 :60).
- Not that consent and governance are optional. They are a condition of a legitimate import, stated here as an ethical and legal requirement, not derived from the figures.
- Not `grounded`. No measurement at the ground was taken in this derivation.

## 5. What would settle it — the ground

The pre-registered refuter in `01_figure_grounded_audit.md` §5 is the instrument. Concretely:

1. Run SEARCHED on a named case. Show the pattern was in `closure(Γ_f)` and unfound; condition 1 falls and more independent search is owed.
2. Inspect the formal residual on that slot. If it is nonzero and trending down, condition 2 falls.
3. Measure independence. If both frames read the same dataset or review, their agreement is one pool and condition 5 falls (Fig 2 :40).
4. Trace provenance. If the local term arrived from the formal frame after the fact, the import direction reverses (Fig 8 :51).
5. Check the ground. A measurement showing the pattern absent refuses the local prediction outright.

The world case already shows what a good check looks like: the 2021 walrus study defined observation limits, ran validation workshops, and used the ice record as a separate anchor. It is corroboration only; it does not settle the figure verdict.

## 6. The honest stop

**Unreached, not fully unreachable.** The figures return the shape of the conditions. They do not return the exact list of cases, the ranking of fields, or the name of the missing primitive. For that demand the verdict is `gap`: "Unreachable is a verdict here, not a silence" (Fig 13 :134). The answer stops at the limit of what the available instruments distinguish and records the lineage so the stop is checkable.

## 7. One-paragraph version for outreach

> The Tether does not say that science is wrong or that local knowledge is always right. It says that a formal method overlooks a pattern when the pattern is outside the method's vocabulary and no amount of its own search adds it, when the method aggregates the live case into a clean global statistic, and when the kind of pattern it is looking for is a preference it never wrote down. The local practitioner is a different instrument that has already been registering the pattern for years, so the two frames form a union whose extra reach lies in the difference between them. That extra reach is only real if the transfer is described in effects, its provenance is recorded, and independence is bought rather than assumed. The framework returns those conditions, not a list of named culprits, and it withdraws the claim if the pattern turns out to have been inside the formal vocabulary all along, if the miss was already registered, or if the two reports share one evidence pool.
