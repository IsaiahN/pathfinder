## 1. A shape is a constraint, not a filler

The canonical usage rule states the limit: the framework returns "what a missing thing would have to look like (a shape, not a name)." Fig 9 :32 "you get a kind of thing, never the answer itself." Fig 13 :134 "Unreachable is a verdict here, not a silence."

From *The Library of Babel*: "This means that you can cut your search short if the set of books returned is small and descriptive enough that it provides a general shape of the answer that satisfies your question."

So for this question a **shape** is the set of conditions under which an overlook is expected. It is not a list of named disciplines, named communities or named patterns. The shape says what any candidate answer must satisfy; it does not fill the slot.

## 2. How a shape is obtained

- *Library of Babel*: use the vocabulary already held that is nearest the thing, then look for recurring new terms that overlap or correlate with it. "With each new term gained, you are able to narrow the space by definition and deepen your vocabulary." The obstacle is not the size of the library: "your ignorance, of not knowing what you don't know, is the actual obstacle between you and the answers."
- Fig 6 :30 "The description travels. Fails by being sharpened into terms only we can read. Cast it in effect terms." Fig 9 :30-31 "your words are borrowed too, so describe the effect rather than the cause."
- *Decoding Tacit Knowledge*: "When contact isn't possible, the best you can send is a plain description of what you'd expect to see, because anyone can check that against the world."
- *Library of Babel* on stopping: "you will know that you are done because the finish line will also be the starting place, as there is nowhere else to search and nothing else that can describe the answer better." The paradox is that by then you have composed the answer in the process of locating it.

For HIS-0084 the effect description is deliberately frame-neutral: "a pattern that registers on one frame's slot and not on the other's, where no term in the first frame predicts it." Naming "folk taxonomy" or "fisheries science" at this stage would be naming a frame before the residual is described, which Fig 9 :27-28 forbids.

## 3. What licenses a name

Only two things, both outside the shape itself:

1. **The ground settles it.** Formula step 5: "the ground settles it. Verified only by what pays. A gate is not the ground." The figures README: the framework "stops at the limit of what the available instruments can distinguish, and records the lineage so the stop is honest."
2. **A provenanced import from a frame whose closure differs** (Fig 6 :23, Fig 8 :51), with a source gate and a shadow test.

*How Intelligence Actually Works* sharpens this with the distinction horizon: "The projection that no instrument distinguishes from the thing is still a projection, which is why the lineage has to be kept even when the copy is perfect." Indiscernibility is not identity. *The Hard Problem is About Source* gives the instrument version: an instrument that reads green "cannot comment on the source"; several sources can produce one reading, and the spectrometer, not an argument, resolved it. Applied here, a local report and a formal measurement may agree on an effect while differing on its source, and the record of which frame supplied which term is the only way to tell.

## 4. Why a different sensorium matters here

*They're Scanning Books When They Should Be Studying Nature*: "A minimum viable set of six terms is a human's careful guess at reality's compression axes; another sensorium's minimum set need not be those six." And: "The real import is not a new term in the existing space. It is a new dimension of the space."

That is the strongest essay-level support for treating local practitioners as a differing closure rather than as a data source to be mined. A lifetime of place-based contact compresses the world along axes a short campaign may not represent at all. The price of that difference is that the knowledge is hard to transfer.

*Decoding Tacit Knowledge* names the loss: "Writing carries the context well and the subtext partly, but it almost never carries the pretext, the 'you had to be there' part." Its three-part account (context, subtext, pretext) explains why a written extraction is lossy even when the words are faithful. It also warns that "Long, close contact pulls the frames together, so the process that makes communication easy slowly uses up that extra reach." So a collaborator who becomes perfectly fluent in the formal frame may cease to be an outside check. Independence is spent by the very contact that makes transfer easy (Fig 8 :48).

## 5. What the essays say about groups and verdicts

*How a Society of Agents Would Work*: "A group is only worth having while its members stay different." It distinguishes a method from a recording, and says that what crosses upward is the former. That is Fig 4 :22-25 again. *Arrival of the Fittest* warns that an LLM given the formula produces "a plausible simulated output to an issue in any given domain that the LLM has knowledge of," and that this "could never be verified if the solutions presented were not contrived to fit the formula." This solution is exactly such an output and must be labelled as such.

## 6. Two consequences for this answer

**Prestige is not action.** *The Recursive Self-Improvement Trap*: "Prestige can fund an action. It can give an action a budget to be tried. But it cannot settle an action. Only the ground can settle it. The Ground Maintainer must be blind to prestige." A review panel selected for eminence, or a list of practitioners selected for likely agreement, is the named collapse. The legitimate ask is a budget to try the claim against a ground.

**This audit is a simulation, not a ground reading.** Same essay: "LLMs can quickly simulate an approximate answer/output with the formula" but "the truly testable output would need to be derived from the real architecture or working the formula yourself." So `01`, `06`, `07` and `09` are simulated derivations checked for internal consistency against the figures. They are **not** tests of the claim and must be labelled as such. They cannot settle anything they report on.

## 7. Correction to my own first reading

I initially treated "shape, not a name" as if it forbade naming *any* particular thing in this answer. The canonical phrase governs a **missing** thing: an atom or axis not held in the frame. In HIS-0084 the missing thing is the discrimination absent from `Γ_f`, so the limit applies cleanly to the answer format, but it does not forbid describing an actual case (the walrus migration) as a case. What it forbids is presenting the case as the general answer, or naming a discipline as uniquely blind. Both readings are now recorded so neither is silently lost.
