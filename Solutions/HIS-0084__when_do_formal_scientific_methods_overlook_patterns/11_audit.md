## Round trip gap `R_T`

The Symbols table defines `R_T` as the gap between what was sent up and what came back down, `gap(x, (T_E ∘ T_A)(x))`, measuring what the coarser description cannot hold. Fig 4 :30 requires it to be measured rather than assumed. I ran the round trip on the condensation the folder actually uses.

1. **Sent up (x):** the verbatim figure set, chiefly Fig 6 :21-23 (closure), Fig 1 :13-22 (slot indexing), Fig 8 :45-51 (union surplus and provenance), Fig 9 :32 (shape, not a name).
2. **Abstraction (T_A):** the shorthand carried in `07`: formal frames are closed; local frames hold the missing discrimination; import is needed with provenance and independence.
3. **Concretisation (T_E):** a concrete claim about a specific case, for example that earlier walrus migration was detected by Inuit hunters before a formal dataset existed.

**`R_T` is nonzero.** Four things the shorthand holds do not survive the trip back down:

- the **SEARCHED-first discipline** (Formula step 7, Fig 8 :41-43), which the concrete claim tends to skip;
- the **independence requirement** (Fig 2 :40), because the same animals and the same season are the evidence for both frames, so overlap must be measured rather than presumed absent;
- the **provenance and shadow gates** (Fig 8 :50-51, Seat Map), which a completed report reads as if the term had always been there;
- the **tacit pretext** (Fig 4 :27-32; *Decoding Tacit Knowledge*), the "you had to be there" part that a written extraction cannot carry.

Conversely, one thing survives cleanly: the union of two differing closures reaches a discrimination neither reaches alone, when the channel to the ground is intact. That is the residue worth sending. `R_T` was **not** measured numerically, because no ground reading exists; it is recorded qualitatively as the enumerated set of dropped constraints. Treating "the formula is the condensed version of the metalaws" as identity would set `R_T` to zero and would be the dead-reckoning error Fig 4 :30 names.
