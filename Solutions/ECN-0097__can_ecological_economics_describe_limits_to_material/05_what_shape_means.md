## 1. A shape is a constraint, not a filler

The canonical README says the framework returns *"what a missing thing would have to look like (a shape, not a name)."* Fig 9 :32: **"you get a kind of thing, never the answer itself."** Fig 13 :134: "Unreachable is a verdict here, not a silence," and the system *"cannot say which primitive is missing, because naming it would mean already holding it."*

From *The Library of Babel*, opened this session: "you can cut your search short if the set of books returned is small and descriptive enough that it provides a general shape of the answer that satisfies your question."

So for ECN-0097 a **shape** is the set of constraints any better descriptor of limits must satisfy, stated before we can name which school satisfies them. The five properties in the README are a shape. "Ecological economics wins" is a name.

## 2. How the shape was obtained here

- *Library of Babel*: "If you don't know the right terms to search for, you will have to use the existing vocabulary you do know that is closest to the words that you wish to describe and grasp the shape of." We started from the effect words available in the question (limit, throughput, measure, aggregate) and bootstrapped from figure lines.
- Fig 9 :30-31: "your words are borrowed too, so describe the effect rather than the cause." So the claim is about what a descriptor must do, not about which school's ontology is true.
- *Library of Babel*: "The books that you are returning in your searches need to be tested or ranged against the problem you want answered in order to steer the direction of the next search." Each finding in `01` was ranged against one ground question: does this property help register a limit that is actually there?
- *Library of Babel* on the bundle: "Perhaps your problem was a bundle or conflation of several problems masquerading as one? You will need to search all of those problems individually." This is the direct essay-level parallel to Fig 9 :54's union-not-partition finding, and it is why the school question is unbundled in `06`.

## 3. What licenses a name

Only two things, both outside the shape:

1. **The ground settles it.** Formula step 5: "the ground settles it. Verified only by what pays. A gate is not the ground." Here that means comparing both schools' slot-level predictions with measured flows and boundary values.
2. **A provenanced import from a frame whose closure differs** (Fig 6 :23, Fig 8 :50-52), with a source gate and a shadow test.

Saying "ecological economics occupies the five-property shape" is a binding from the economics frame onto the shape. It is not a derivation, and it is not an import that moves the wall, because the five properties are not primitives of the framework. It is recorded as a binding in `01` F7 and withheld from the verdict.

## 4. Why a single score is the specific forbidden error here

The question invites a ranking, and a ranking invites a single number. Fig 12 :130: "Summing them into one figure is the error this names, not the fix for it. A price in bits and a price in actions are two readings, held apart." Fig 1 :27: "They are frame-internal. A frame cannot score itself with a quantity it also produces."

*The Recursive Self-Improvement Trap* gives the same rule in a different register: "Prestige must never be allowed to validate an action directly... Prestige can fund an action. It can give an action a budget to be tried. But it cannot settle an action. Only the ground can settle it." A school's citation prestige can buy a hearing for the shape claim; it cannot decide whether the shape claim is true.

Goodhart's law, an outside-field witness read this session, predicts the same failure: "When a measure becomes a target, it ceases to be a good measure." All three agree that the yardstick must not be produced by the thing being measured. Since they share this structural point but come from different literatures, they are useful corroboration, not three figure lines.

## 5. Q answered

**What shape must a better descriptor of material limits have?** Five properties: external ground, per-slot residual, load-bearing finiteness and gradients, two currencies held apart, and an enumerated habitat with cascades.

**Does the framework license naming the winning school?** No. It returns the shape. The school name is a binding from the economics frame, ungrounded until a ground reading compares slot-level predictions. See `01` F7 and the retraction log.

**Is this a gap?** No. Figure lines govern the form of the comparison. The empirical answer is unreached in this simulated run, not unreachable and not absent.

## 6. My own audit is a simulation, not a ground reading

*The Recursive Self-Improvement Trap*: "LLMs can quickly simulate an approximate answer/output with the formula found in the architecture post. It will often be better than the generic outputs that you will usually get otherwise too (intelligence is essentially arrangement). However, the truly testable output would need to be derived from the real architecture or working the formula yourself." And: "the output is only as good as the validator. The agent, or the tool, or the frame, or the theory, cannot validate itself. An external validator, or ground, is always needed."

So `01`, `06`, `07` and `09` are simulated derivations checked for internal consistency against the figures. They are **not** a test of which school predicts limits better, and they cannot settle anything they report on. The instrument that would settle it is named in `07` §4 and `08` §5.
