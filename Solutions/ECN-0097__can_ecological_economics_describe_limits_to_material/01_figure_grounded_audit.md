## 0. The claim under audit

> Can ecological economics describe limits to material growth better than conventional growth models?

Stated in effect terms, with the two options bound before the run:

- **Option A (conventional growth models):** the limit, if any, is registered inside a money-denominated model. Scarcity shows up as a price, a diminishing return, or a technological substitute. The anchor is the market and the national accounts.
- **Option B (ecological economics):** the limit is registered at the economy-environment boundary as a physical throughput, a finite stock, a sink, and an irreversibility. The anchor is measurement of flows and thermodynamic facts.
- **"Better":** a comparison of the residuals of A and B against one common ground, not a vote, a citation count, or a school's self-description.

The word "better" is a shape we seek, and a frame cannot check the direction of its own question (Formula, "Direction"). So the shape is made an ordinary term in the README: a list of five properties. That list is what the findings below test, and it is refutable.

## 1. Residual, before any figure line is chosen

**R by arity, symmetry, scale.**

- *Arity:* one ground fact (where the material limit actually binds) described by two frames. "Better" is a comparison, formally a ⋛ threshold relation between two residuals ranged against that one ground. It is not a symmetric merge of the two schools.
- *Symmetry:* broken by the ground. Fig 2 :25-26: "pinned: it does not update on A or B" and "both range against it; it is not a third party to the disagreement." The comparison is ordered: frames are corrected by the limit, the limit is not corrected by the frames.
- *Scale:* the signal lives at the economy-environment boundary (throughput per material and energy slot), not at the aggregate market scale. First order at the flow level; second order at the level of comparing two model families.

**Domain census: one line per domain with actors, currency, anchor.** A domain with no nameable anchor is not a domain yet.

| domain | actors | currency | anchor (nameable?) | question type |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| D1. Conventional growth model frame | representative agent, firms, capital K, labour L, technology A, aggregate output Y | money: GDP, prices, discounted utility | market prices and national accounts, but price is generated inside the frame | a convention that may decide an outcome |
| D2. Ecological economics frame | throughput, natural-capital stocks, sinks, species, energy carriers | joules, tonnes, hectares, with money only where priced | measured material and energy flows, thermodynamic facts, boundary control variables | ground-facing description |
| D3. The ground (the limit itself) | energy, materials, waste sinks, the biosphere | physical quantities | energy finiteness and conservation, spendable gradients, measured flows | what settles the comparison |
| D4. Verification / outreach frame | expert readers | agreement and residual | the ground, never agreement | where independence is spent |

**Partition or union?** The question is phrased as a partition (A or B), but the two frames share atoms: energy, materials, capital, labour, technology. They differ in currency and in which constraints bind. Fig 9 :54: "And when the split lands on a union rather than a partition, unbundle before you search again." Fig 9 :56-58: a single lever clears the region where only one condition holds and leaves the intersections standing, so the residue becomes a specific compound. The compound unbundles into (i) biophysical limit description, (ii) monetary valuation, (iii) dynamical projection. This is carried into `06_isomers_and_order.md`.

**Order by dependency (Fig 3).** Describe R in effect terms before naming a frame (Fig 9 :27-28). Pin the ground before comparing (Fig 2 :25-26). Describe first-order flows before the second-order school comparison. Settle before composing (Fig 5 :69-70). A verdict taken before the ground is pinned is a reading of nothing (Fig 3 :22-23).

**Strain test rule used throughout:** if a figure line fits only by forcing, it governs another quantity and we re-census the actors, currency and anchor rather than softening the reading (Fig 4 :19-20: "Only the actors, the currency and the anchor change.").

## 3. Findings F1 to F8

### F1 : The comparison needs an external ground; one frame's nominal anchor is frame-internal

Fig 1 :23: **"The ground enters as the only metric."**
Fig 1 :24-27: **"They are frame-internal. A frame cannot score itself with a quantity it also produces."**
Fig 2 :29-30: **"An anchor is whatever settles the question and does not change its answer because of what any frame thinks."**
Fig 2 :43: **"a convention that decides an outcome has stopped being one."** (same rule as Fig 10 :47)

Domain verdicts, kept separate:

- **D1:** GDP and market price are produced by the conventional frame. When price is used as the sole indicator of whether a physical limit exists, a convention decides an outcome and stops being a convention. D1 therefore fails the anchor test *for the limit question specifically*. This is not a claim that D1 is bad at other questions.
- **D2 and D3:** measured physical flows and thermodynamic facts do not move when either school changes its mind. They pass the anchor test.
- **Mode:** `specified`, triangulated across Fig 1, Fig 2 and Fig 10 (three figures with low overlap: residual accounting, anchor theory, seat rules).

**Refuter:** a conventional growth model whose scarcity indicator is an external biophysical measurement rather than a price, and which predicts measured limits at least as well as an ecological model. If such a model is exhibited, F1 narrows to "typical" rather than "structural" and must be rewritten.

### F2 : Aggregation throws away the slot-level signal

Fig 1 :19: **"The residual is indexed by object slot, not aggregated."**
Fig 1 :22: **"Aggregating across slots is how a live signal disappears into an average."**
Fig 11 :43: **"The habitat is enumerated, never composed."**
Fig 11 :48: **"You do not invent the list. You read it off the world, and what you cannot perceive or measure yet is the residual."**

A single aggregate (GDP, an aggregate production function) averages across materials. A limit that binds in one slot (a phosphorus sink, a freshwater basin, an energy carrier) disappears into the mean. Ecological accounting decomposes by category and area type, and material-flow analysis tracks substances by type. That is the figure-licensed shape: enumerate slots, then range each against its own boundary.

**Mode:** `specified`; triangulated across Fig 1 (residual indexing) and Fig 11 (habitat enumeration), two figures with low overlap. The world claim that B actually does this is corroborated by the ecological-footprint and industrial-ecology sources in `09`, but corroboration is secondary.

**Refuter:** a demonstration that an aggregated monetary index preserves all slot-level residuals and still registers a binding limit before the slot-level accounts do. That would falsify Fig 1 :22 as applied here and sends us back to the census.

### F3 : The substrate makes thermodynamic finiteness load-bearing

Fig 13 :13-14: "energy" / **"conserved, finite, directionless alone"**
Fig 13 :21-22: "gradient" / **"a difference that can be spent"**
Fig 13 :49: **"Read the established laws of thermodynamics as a test rather than a scorecard."**
Fig 13 :68: **"finiteness in a finite region is what makes the counting terminate"**
Fig 13 :74: **"Everything else is a composition across the two kinds, never within one."**
Fig 6 :23: **"No frame escapes its own closure. The bound moves only on contact with a frame whose closure differs."**

Energy and gradient are not decorative entries in the six-term set. The substrate's own minimality test says each is load-bearing for thermodynamics: energy for the second law through finiteness, gradient for direction and remainder. A growth model that treats material and energy as indefinitely substitutable by capital is composing over a money/capital atom while treating a finite, conserved primitive as if it were elastic. It cannot derive that elasticity inside its own closure (Fig 6 :23); it would have to import an atom that contradicts the load-bearing term. Ecological economics begins from throughput and irreversibility, so it starts with the substrate's own primitives.

**Mode:** `specified`, triangulated across Fig 13 (substrate and thermodynamic test) and Fig 6 (closure and import), which govern different quantities. The Georgescu-Roegen source is secondary corroboration, not part of the verdict.

**Refuter:** derive energy's finiteness and gradient's spend-and-remainder behaviour inside a conventional frame's closure without import, and show its predictions do not depend on them. That breaks F3 and would require re-censing D1's currency.

### F4 : Two currencies cannot be summed into one yardstick

Fig 12 :125: **"A composition costs in two currencies, and they do not add."**
Fig 12 :126: **"Bits price the description. Actions price finding out whether it holds."**
Fig 12 :130: **"Summing them into one figure is the error this names, not the fix for it. A price in bits and a price in actions are two readings, held apart."**
Fig 1 :27: **"They are frame-internal. A frame cannot score itself with a quantity it also produces."**

Conventional models monetise factors into one accounting. Ecological economics insists that monetary valuation and biophysical throughput are incommensurable (the weak versus strong sustainability distinction). The figure does not take a side in that literature; it rules on the form: two currencies are two readings, held apart, and summing them into one number is the named error, not a solution. Therefore a single "green GDP" style scalar cannot be the yardstick for "better." The comparison must present both readings.

**Mode:** `specified`, triangulated across Fig 12 (bond currencies) and Fig 1 (frame-internal metrics), two figures with low overlap.

**Refuter:** exhibit a single index that sums monetary and physical accounts, preserves every slot-level residual, and passes a ground test against measured limits. Under Fig 12 :130 this would be a genuine surprise, not a refinement, and F4 would be withdrawn.

### F5 : "Better" cannot be produced by agreement or prestige

Fig 2 :35: **"Alignment is triangulation, not negotiation. A mirror cannot correct you."**
Fig 2 :39: "Correlation has valence."
Fig 2 :40: **"disqualifying for a verdict: agreement about the world is worthless when the agreeing frames share an evidence pool"**
Fig 2 :42: **"Buy independence in proportion to the cost of being wrong."**
Fig 7 :12-13: "same closure, handed along; agreement compounds, evidence does not"

A verdict for B cannot be earned by counting B citations, B journals, or sympathetic reviewers. If A and B both draw on the same national accounts, their disagreement is one pool talking in two voices. The cost of being wrong here is high (policy built on a missed limit), so independence should be bought in proportion to it.

**Mode:** `general` (the rule about agreement), triangulated across Fig 2 and Fig 7.

**Refuter:** show that two school-level agreement sets are drawn from genuinely non-overlapping evidence and that their convergence predicts a measured limit neither predicted alone. Then they would be corroboration at maximum independence (Fig 8 :50), not one pool.

### F6 : The comparison is reachable, not underivable; it is simply unreached here

Fig 6 :32: **"An instrument is not built from a description. It is improved from a worse instrument already returning something."**
Fig 6 :34-35: **"Where nothing is, there is no edge to extend from, and the search for a proto-instrument is a search for one that is already there."**
Fig 11 :48: **"You do not invent the list. You read it off the world, and what you cannot perceive or measure yet is the residual."**

We do have instruments already returning something: material flow accounts, ecological footprint and biocapacity accounts, planetary-boundary control variables, exergy analysis. So this is not "underivable in principle" (Fig 8 :40-43). The ground test is straightforward: for each slot, compare the limit each frame predicts with the measured flow or boundary value, and keep the residuals per slot. This simulated run took no measurement, so the verdict is **unreached**, not **unreachable** (Fig 13 :134: "Unreachable is a verdict here, not a silence"). Importing a new primitive against a budget limit would add an atom that was not needed (Fig 6 :51-52).

**Mode:** `general` on reachability, `specified` on the proposed test, triangulated across Fig 6 and Fig 11.

**Refuter:** show that the existing instruments do not return a reading for the relevant slots at any resolution (a proto-instrument search with no edge to extend from). Then "unreached" would move toward "unreachable at this budget" and the two facts must not be conflated.

### F7 : The school-to-shape binding is not a figure derivation

Fig 9 :32: **"you get a kind of thing, never the answer itself"**
Fig 8 :50-52: **"Convergent derivation and adopted import look identical in the contents. Only the record of where each came from separates them, and the surplus lives only where the closures differ."**

No figure line names "ecological economics" or "conventional growth models." The figures return the shape of a better descriptor (the five properties bound in the README). Saying "ecological economics occupies that shape" is a binding from the economics frame onto the shape, exactly parallel to the exemplar's refused binding "metric tensor = Gradient." It is recorded as a binding, with provenance, and is not promoted to a derivation. This is the discipline that stops the solution from manufacturing the answer it was asked for.

**Mode:** `general` on the shape-not-a-name limit; the binding is `specified` and ungrounded, single-source to the shape.

**Refuter:** an explicit figure line that names an economic school, or a demonstration that the five properties are not a shape but a name in disguise (for instance, that one property already presupposes a school's vocabulary). Then the binding must be restated in more neutral effect terms.

### F8 : Framing the question as a partition is itself a filter

Fig 9 :27-28: **"describe it before you go looking, or any frame you pick will seem to fit"**
Fig 9 :54: **"And when the split lands on a union rather than a partition, unbundle before you search again."**
Fig 9 :60: **"Use filters for the budget and witnesses for the verdict. Never let a filter hand you a verdict."**

The words "ecological" and "conventional" arrive with the question, before the residual is described. Fig 9 says that is backwards: describe the effect first, then any frame you pick will not automatically fit. Unbundling (§1) is the concrete remedy. The school labels are filters for where to look, not witnesses for the verdict.

**Mode:** `general`, triangulated across Fig 9 and Fig 8 (describe-first, and surplus only where closures differ).

**Refuter:** show that the two frames have identical closures over the relevant slots (same atoms, same wall), in which case the union has no surplus and unbundling is unnecessary. Fig 8 :13-14 would then apply: "their union adds nothing."

## 4. Pre-registered refuter (written before the conclusion)

Required before any outreach: a falsifier written before the run is a design instrument. The shape claim in the README is refuted if any of the following holds:

1. **Anchor refuter.** A conventional growth model is exhibited whose scarcity indicator is an external biophysical measurement, not a price, and whose slot-level predictions match measured limits at least as well as an ecological model. (Breaks F1 and F2.)
2. **Substrate refuter.** Energy finiteness and gradient spend-and-remainder are derived inside the conventional frame's closure without import, and shown not to be load-bearing for its predictions. (Breaks F3.)
3. **Currency refuter.** A single monetary index sums monetary and biophysical accounts, preserves every slot residual, and passes the ground test. (Breaks F4.)
4. **Pool refuter.** Two school-level agreement sets are shown to be drawn from non-overlapping evidence and jointly to predict a measured limit. (Breaks F5 and F7.)
5. **Instrument refuter.** The existing flow, footprint and boundary instruments return no reading for the relevant slots at any resolution, so the comparison is unreachable rather than unreached. (Breaks F6.)

If any fires, the shape claim is narrowed or withdrawn. Until one is run, no part of this may be labelled `grounded`. This list is the first thing in the outreach packet, before any theory.

## 5. Strains and re-census (kept visible)

- **Strain candidate, F3 versus F1.** Fig 13 says finiteness is load-bearing; F1 says the conventional frame's money anchor is frame-internal. Reading them as "one school is forbidden" strains both. Re-census: Fig 13 governs the substrate's primitives; F1 governs the anchor test for one specific question. Different objects, so no strain after re-census. Matches the canonical rule that a reading making figures contradict one another has misidentified its domain.
- **Strain candidate, the word "better."** Fig 12 :130 forbids summing two currencies, yet "better" looks like a single ranking. Re-census: ⋛ compares two quantities against a threshold and is itself an ordered bond (Operators table: "a threshold relation between two quantities"); it does not sum them. The two readings are held apart and compared, so no strain. The word "ranking" in the README is deliberately not used for the verdict.
- **Live strain, not resolved.** Fig 8 :50-52 says convergent derivation and adopted import look identical in content, and only the record separates them. Ecological economics and resource economics sometimes arrive at the same limit from the same data. Whether those are two independent derivations or one pool cannot be settled from the figure text; it needs the provenance record of the particular studies. Named, not hidden, and carried into `11_audit.md` as part of R_T.
- **No silent erasure.** An earlier draft of F1 called the conventional frame "wrong" rather than "failing the anchor test for the limit question." That was a mode violation (a verdict stated in `general` mode) and is recorded here as a retraction, not quietly replaced.
