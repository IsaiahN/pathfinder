# Expert verification packet: the Tether on strange metals and holography

**What this is:** a shape claim in a form a condensed-matter physicist or philosopher of physics can check. Mode, derivation and refuters are stated before the conclusion, so the reader knows what to look for and what would settle it.

**Mode: `specified` on the sub-claims, `general` on the structural limit, not `grounded`.** No measurement of a gravitational dual is claimed. This is a shape and a route, not a physics result. It is a simulated derivation checked for internal consistency against the figures, not a ground reading.

## 1. The claim, in one paragraph

The strange-metal readings are a union of overlapping conditions, not one anomaly, so there is no single "behavior" that could point to a single frame. For any one slot, the shape is stateable: a scale-free relaxation on the time hbar/(kB T) with no quasiparticle and a constrained set of responses. The framework returns that shape, not a name; several mechanisms, including several distinct holographic ones, reach the same reading, so the reading does not determine the source. A gauge/gravity dual is a legitimate candidate frame whose closure differs, but importing it needs provenance and a shadow test, and whether it is owed depends on an open underivable-versus-unreachable question. What is offered is the shaped residual, refuters written in advance, and the discriminating comparison that would close it.

## 3. What is explicitly not claimed

- Not that a cuprate is dual to a black hole, and not that holography is the correct description.
- Not that "only known description" is evidence. It is a statement about the current literature and the budget (Fig 9 :60).
- Not that quantum criticality, phonons, Mottness or a gauge principle are excluded; several mechanisms reach the same reading.
- Not a derivation of one theory from the other, and no seventh primitive. The extra bulk dimension is a parameter of a held structure, not a new kind (Fig 13 :115).
- Not grounded. Nothing here is asserted as settled by experiment.
- Not that the framework selects the word "holographic." It returns a shape.
