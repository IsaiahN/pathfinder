## VERDICT (from the figures)

The shape survives; the name does not. The strange-metal readings are a union compound to be unbundled per observable slot, not one "behavior." For any slot the shape is stateable, a scale-free, no-quasiparticle relaxation on the time hbar/(kB T) with a constrained set of responses, and several mechanisms, including several distinct holographic ones, reach it, so the reading underdetermines the source. A gauge/gravity dual is a legitimate candidate frame with a different closure, but the figures cannot name it, and importing it requires provenance, a shadow test and a measured round-trip gap. Whether an import is owed at all is a declared `gap`: underivable in principle from the conventional closure, or merely unreachable at this budget. The comparison of two settled descriptions is second-order under Fig 12 :104, and over a declared shared observable-atom set they are isomers while some constrained response still separates them. This is a `specified`, ungrounded candidate.

- **cost if wrong:** bounded and locatable. The shape claim carries its own refuter and is never asserted as a physics result, so an error is findable rather than invisible. The real cost is spending an outside checker's independence by sending a name and a literature list before asking what they would count as a failure, which Fig 2 :42 prices as "Buy independence in proportion to the cost of being wrong." That cost is avoidable by leading with the shaped residual and the refuter.
- **undoable:** yes. No physical term is withdrawn by narrowing this claim; the bindings are removable without touching the derivation, and `07_the_tether_answer.md` and `08_expert_verification_packet.md` already remove them. A sent shape can be superseded by a corrected packet, and no primitive has to be abandoned.
- **reversed by:** a ground reading, not agreement. A source-discriminating multi-observable comparison (rho(T), sigma(omega,T), tan(theta_H), kappa, A(k,omega), s(T)) with a prediction dated before measurement; a proof that the conventional closure is underivable; a controlled conventional derivation showing the term was already reachable; or a dated forward prediction from a gravitational frame falsified by experiment. A derivation of one of the six from the other five, or a direct state-state bond, would break the substrate. Prestige, citation counts and the number of agreeing holographic models are not such instruments.
