## 3. Two things in the literature that bear directly on the question

1. **The reading underdetermines the source.** Hartnoll and Mackenzie show that quantum criticality and electron-phonon scattering are "two very different mechanisms leading to a Planckian electronic lifetime," and that conventional metals at room temperature can be Planckian too. The educational guide adds that there are five distinct holographic routes to the same T-linear slope and that "Agreement with rho~T is weak evidence; agreement among constrained response functions is strong evidence." Together these make the same point as Fig 13 :121 "Different routes can arrive at the same substance": a slope is not a mechanism.
2. **The strongest holographic evidence is a multi-observable, measurement-first sequence.** Cinti et al. report that the ARPES group observed the k-dependence of the self-energy "without prior knowledge of the holographic predictions." Phillips, Hussey and Abbamonte's Table 2 grades models against rho(T), sigma(omega), tan(theta_H), quadrature magnetoresistance, extended criticality and experimental predictions, and notes that AD/EMD models have been "the most successful... thus far in reproducing the various characteristics of strange metal physics." Those are constrained comparisons, not a single-slope fit. Note the overlap: this is still one broad evidence pool, so it is corroboration of the *shape* claim, not an independent witness for a dual.

## 5. The two-stage offer

- **Stage 1, before any theory.** The output is the union refusal plus the shape of one slot, and its refuters. The recipient checks it without holding the framework's interior. Fig 2 :45 "Frames range against the ground. They do not negotiate with it."
- **Stage 2, opt-in only.** If they are curious, offer the papers. Seat 4 must not hold the interiors; once a recipient has absorbed the theory, their agreement stops being evidence, so their stage-1 verdict is recorded first and their later agreement is never pooled as corroboration (Fig 2 :40).

**Hard precondition.** Stages 1 to 3 only work if the stage-1 output survives. It is a `specified` shape with a declared `gap`, not a physics result. If an outside checker supplies the conventional derivation (refuter 2), the import is not owed and the outreach claim is narrowed accordingly.
