## What is deliberately not here

- No personal emails of named individual researchers.
- No ranking of people by how likely they are to agree that holography is the right description.
- No bulk-send plan, no scraped list, no purchased list.

Three reasons, in the figures' own words:

1. **A room does not upgrade the label.** "A room reaching a conclusion without the ground is agreement, and agreement among frames is a weighted average of influence. Adding people raises the vocabulary and does not add an anchor." (Seat Map field guide, local copy, line 283). Opened and recorded in `10_source_ledger.csv`.
2. **Selecting for receptiveness manufactures the disqualifying correlation.** Fig 2 :40: "agreement about the world is worthless when the agreeing frames share an evidence pool". A list selected for people already inclined toward holography is one pool wearing many names.
3. **Asking for trust before a check spends the only thing an outside frame contributes.** Fig 10 :42: "A verifier that can reconstruct the claim cannot verify it." Fig 10 :52: "Bring a characterised residual, never the source."
