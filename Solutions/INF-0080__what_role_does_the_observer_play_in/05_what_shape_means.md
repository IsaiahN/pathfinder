## 1. A shape is a constraint, not a filler

The canonical rule says the framework returns *"what a missing thing would have to look like (a shape, not a name)."* Fig 9 :32: *"you get a kind of thing, never the answer itself."* Fig 13: *"It can say the answer is out of reach with the library as it stands... It cannot say which primitive is missing, because naming it would mean already holding it."*

From *The Library of Babel* (opened 2026-11-23): *"you can cut your search short if the set of books returned is small and descriptive enough that it provides a general shape of the answer that satisfies your question."*

So a **shape** here is the narrowed form of an answer that can be stated before the specific state list is available. It constrains; it does not fill.

For this question the shape is:

> The observer authors the convention that maps physical states to computational states, makes the residual statable, and ranges the chosen frame against a ground it cannot author. It does not decide which states count in the grounded sense. The mapping is a typed bond whose legitimacy is conventional and whose correctness is settled by a source-discriminating instrument.

That is a shape. "States 4 through 9 of system S are the register" would be a name, and the figures do not license it.

## 2. How the shape was obtained

- *Library of Babel*: use the vocabulary already closest to the thing, then let recurring new terms narrow the space. *"With each new term gained, you narrow the space and deepen your vocabulary."* Applied here: the closest available vocabulary was the observer-relativity and implementation debate, and the recurring new term supplied by the figures was the **seat** and its distinction between conventions and verdicts.
- Fig 6 :30: *"The description travels. Fails by being sharpened into terms only we can read. Cast it in effect terms."* Fig 9 :30-31: *"your words are borrowed too, so describe the effect rather than the cause."* So the residual was described as "the observer's assignment moves when the observer changes", not as "the observer is a homunculus".
- Fig 8 :53: **"Residual first, frame second."** Fig 9 :27-28: **"describe it before you go looking, or any frame you pick will seem to fit."** The frame (the computational description) was selected after the residual was described, not before.
- *Library of Babel* on stopping: *"you will know that you are done because the finish line will also be the starting place."* The stop here is when the office of the observer is fixed and the only remaining question is which states, in a given system, need an instrument. That is the honest stop in §5.

## 3. What would license a name

Two things, both outside the shape:

1. **The ground settles it.** `THE_FORMULA.md` step 5: *"the ground settles it. Verified only by what pays. A gate is not the ground."* For this question, a measurement or controlled intervention that discriminates among candidate mappings would name the states.
2. **A provenanced import from a frame whose closure differs** (Fig 6 :23, Fig 8 :50-52), with a source gate and a shadow test. An import here would be, for example, a physical information-theoretic criterion that explains a gap in the current mapping.

Neither has been supplied in this folder. The world sources describe the problem and the debate; they do not supply the instrument. So the state list stays unbound.

## 4. Source, not projection

*The Hard Problem is About Source* (opened 2026-11-23) gives the mechanism that matters most for this question. Metamerism: an instrument reads green and *"cannot comment on the source."* And: *"If several paths create the same behavior, then behavior is a projection with lossy information"*. The resolution came from an instrument, not an argument: *"It was proven wrong not by argument, but by the arrival of a better instrument."*

Applied here: an observed input-output regularity does not determine which physical states are the computational states. Several pairings of physical state types to computational states can produce the same behaviour. Naming one of them from the behaviour alone is the metamerism error. What is owed is a source-discriminating instrument. Fig 11 :48: *"You do not invent the list. You read it off the world, and what you cannot perceive or measure yet is the residual."*

This is also why the observer's say-so cannot settle it. The observer is part of the reading apparatus, and an instrument that cannot distinguish the cases *"demonstrates its own inadequacy"* (from *The Hard Problem is About Source*).

## 5. The honest stop

The office of the observer is reachable from the figures. The specific state list is not. Fig 13: *"Unreachable is a verdict here, not a silence"*, and Fig 8 :39: *"So a search that finds nothing is never a proof of absence."* The answer stops at the limit of the available instruments and records the lineage so the stop is checkable. *"Indiscernibility is not identity."*

The world literature reinforces the stop. The SEP entry on computation in physical systems reports there is *"no consensus on where to draw the boundary between representational manipulations that count as computations and representational manipulations that do not."* Curtis-Trudel likewise says there is *"no informative general story"* about when a labelling scheme should be preferred in general, only local, context-bound reasons. That is a published `gap`, not a failure of this derivation.

## 6. Simulation, not ground reading

From *The Recursive Self-Improvement Trap* (opened 2026-11-23): *"LLMs can quickly simulate an approximate answer/output with the formula found in the architecture post"*, but *"the truly testable output would need to be derived from the real architecture or working the formula yourself."* And: *"You may all agree and be wrong. You may set your clock to someone else's, who was setting your clock to yours, and be off by many deviations over time. You need ground truth to define where you stand."*

So `01_figure_grounded_audit.md` and this file are simulated derivations checked for internal consistency against the figures. They are **not** a test of the claim and cannot settle anything they report on.

## 7. Prestige is not action

From *The Recursive Self-Improvement Trap*: *"Prestige can fund an action. It can give an action a budget to be tried. But it cannot settle an action. Only the ground can settle it. The Ground Maintainer must be blind to prestige."*

A list of famous philosophers of mind who agree with the shape claim would be the named collapse, not corroboration. The legitimate ask is the opposite: give the claim a budget to be tried, let a ground settle it, and ask a reader what would refute it before offering any theory.
