## Round trip gap R_T

2. **Abstraction (T_A):** the shorthand carried in `07` and `08`: an idea cannot recur as the same term; it returns through mint, import or instrument; acceptance splits into settlement and convention.
3. **Concretisation (T_E):** a concrete historical claim about a named case, for example "continental drift was rejected until plate tectonics supplied the mechanism" or "germ theory made Semmelweis acceptable".

**R_T is nonzero.** Three things the shorthand holds do not survive the trip back down:

- the **identity of the atom that crossed**. The shorthand says "new growth"; the case requires a name, a source closure and a date, which are provenance, not derivation (Fig 8 :51);
- the **distinction between convergence and adoption**. Both look identical in contents; only the record separates them, and a shorthand that says "reappeared" drops it;
- the **undecidable changed-world branch** (Fig 10 :47). The shorthand asserts a channel change; the full figure set leaves the alternative open, and a concrete historical narrative silently closes it.

Conversely, one thing survives cleanly: a refusal is an addition, a term cannot cycle, and new growth is necessary for restored reach. That is the residue worth sending. R_T was **not** measured numerically, because no ground reading was taken; it is recorded qualitatively as the enumerated set of dropped constraints. Treating the formula as identical to the figure set would set R_T to zero and would be the dead reckoning Fig 4 :30 names.
