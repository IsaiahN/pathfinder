## 1. A shape is a constraint you can state before you can name it

The canonical rule: the framework returns "what a missing thing would have to look like (a shape, not a name)" (`FIGURES_README_canonical.md`). Fig 9 :32: "you get a kind of thing, never the answer itself." Fig 13 :134: "Unreachable is a verdict here, not a silence".

From *The Library of Babel* (fetched): "This means that you can cut your search short if the set of books returned is small and descriptive enough that it provides a general shape of the answer that satisfies your question."

For HIS-0094 the shape is therefore **not** a list of famous cases and **not** the claim "ideas were right all along." It is the constraint any explanation must satisfy:

> A refused term cannot return as the same term. It returns only when new growth restores reach, and what looks like the same idea may be a different route to the same substance.

`SPACE(???)TIME` was the exemplar's shape. Here the equivalent is `IDEA(t) → (???) → IDEA(t+n)`, with the middle slot open. The figures do not fill it with "genius," "prejudice," "zeitgeist" or "the world changed." They constrain it to be a mint, an import or an instrument change.

## 2. How the shape is obtained

- *Library of Babel* (fetched): "If you don't know the right terms to search for, you will have to use the existing vocabulary you do know that is closest to the words that you wish to describe and grasp the shape of." And: "With each new term gained, you are able to narrow the space by definition and deepen your vocabulary."
- Fig 6 :30: "outward: does another frame already hold it? The description travels. Fails by being sharpened into terms only we can read. Cast it in effect terms." Fig 9 :30: "your words are borrowed too, so describe the effect rather than the cause".
- The exemplar's *Decoding Tacit Knowledge* summary (not re-fetched): "When contact isn't possible, the best you can send is a plain description of what you'd expect to see, because anyone can check that against the world."
- *Library of Babel* on stopping (fetched): "You will know that you are done because the finish line will also be the starting place" and "you have already composed the answer through the process of locating it." The stop here is the refuter in `01` §4, written before the world search.

## 3. The reading cannot comment on the source

This is the sharpest essay bearing on the recurrence question. From *The Hard Problem is About Source* (fetched):

- "If several paths create the same behavior, then behavior is a projection with lossy information"
- "the instrument detects green, and says that it's green. However, the instrument is speaking only about what it measured, it cannot comment on the source."
- "It was proven wrong not by argument, but by the arrival of a better instrument."

The historical record gives us the reading "idea X appeared again." That reading cannot comment on its source. The source could be a refused term returning after new growth (Fig 6 :45), a convergent derivation arriving at the same substance (Fig 8 :50; Fig 13 :121), or an adopted import whose contents look identical (Fig 8 :51). The spectrometer, not the argument, discriminates. What is owed to a recurrence case is a better instrument: a provenance record with dates, closures and source gates, not a name for the "cause" of the recurrence.

Fig 6 :27: "Which is why some unreachability is depth and some is genuine absence, and from inside the frame the two look the same." A rejected idea may have been unreachable at that budget or genuinely absent. From inside the old frame those look the same. This is why the later acceptance is informative: it tells us the earlier state was depth, not absence, but only once an instrument discriminates.

## 4. What licenses a name here

Only two things, both outside the shape:

1. **The ground settles it.** `THE_FORMULA.md` step 5: "the ground settles it. Verified only by what pays. A gate is not the ground." The canonical README says the framework "stops at the limit of what the available instruments can distinguish, and records the lineage so the stop is honest."
2. **A provenanced import from a frame whose closure differs** (Fig 6 :23; Fig 8 :12/:13), with a source gate and a shadow test.

So naming "mantle convection" or "germ theory" as *the* reason an idea returned is a binding from the physics or biology frame onto a slot the framework leaves open. It is licensed only by provenance: which closure did it come from, what gap did it explain, when did it cross. Fig 8 :51 again: "Only the record of where each came from separates them."

## 5. Correction to the "shape, not a name" reading (kept visible)

I initially read "shape, not a name" as forbidding any named mechanism in the middle slot. That overstates it. The canonical phrase governs a **missing** primitive, one not held in Γ. An import such as germ theory or mantle convection is not a missing primitive invented by the framework; it is an atom already held in another frame's closure and brought across (Fig 6 :18: "the imported primitive"). What remains forbidden is inventing a seventh term, or calling a binding a derivation.

The exemplar made the same correction in its `05` §5b: "The README's phrase is 'what a missing thing would have to look like (a shape, not a name).' The subject is a missing primitive." I record it again here rather than silently inheriting it. Both the earlier overstatement and its narrowing are visible.

## 6. Two consequences for this solution

**Prestige is not action.** From the exemplar's record of *The Recursive Self-Improvement Trap* (essay not re-fetched this session): "Prestige must never be allowed to validate an action directly... Prestige can fund an action. It can give an action a budget to be tried. But it cannot settle an action. Only the ground can settle it." A story in which a famous convert "made the idea acceptable" gives the claim a budget to be tried. It does not settle it (Fig 2 :35: "Alignment is triangulation, not negotiation. A mirror cannot correct you.").

**This audit is a simulation, not a ground reading.** Same essay, as recorded locally: "LLMs can quickly simulate an approximate answer/output... However, the truly testable output would need to be derived from the real architecture or working the formula yourself." LLM output is "simulacra." So `01` is a simulated derivation checked for internal consistency against the figures. It is not a test of the historical claim and must be labelled as such. It cannot settle anything it reports on.
