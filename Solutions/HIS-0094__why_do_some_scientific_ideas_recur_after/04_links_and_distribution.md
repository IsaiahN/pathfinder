## Distribution: offer, do not push

Figure-grounded reasons:

- **Fig 10 :52** - "Bring a characterised residual, never the source." Send the shape claim and its refuter first. The framework's interiors come only after a verdict on stage 1 is recorded.
- **Fig 2 :35** - "Alignment is triangulation, not negotiation. A mirror cannot correct you." Do not ask anyone to endorse the claim. Ask what would refute it.
- **Fig 4 :22** - "Only methods cross up. Only head starts come down." A method can be given away; a recording cannot be mistaken for one. Lead with the generator.
- **Fig 9 :27** - "describe it before you go looking" - ask what the recipient would expect to see in a recurrence case before giving them the framework.
- **Fig 7 :13** - "agreement compounds, evidence does not." No mass send, no list selected for receptiveness, no pooling of later agreement as corroboration (Fig 2 :40).

**Recommended order in a reply:** (1) the shape claim and its four refuters; (2) ask what the recipient would count as a failure and, if they have a case in mind, which atom they think crossed; (3) only then the three local source links, with the note that the figures, not the summary, are the authority.

## The two-stage offer

- **Stage 1 before stage 2** is Fig 10 :52 and Fig 2 :45 ("Frames range against the ground. They do not negotiate with it."). The recipient checks the output without holding the framework's interior, and the verdict is recorded before any theory is shown.
- **Stage 2 is opt-in by construction.** Once a recipient has absorbed the framework, their agreement stops being evidence. Their stage-1 verdict is never pooled as corroboration.
- **Stage 3 sends a method.** Giving away the loop is permitted (Fig 4 :22). Each user writes down what would refute their own result before running it, and states the mode of what they get (Formula step "Direction").
- **Hard precondition:** stages 1 to 3 work only if the stage-1 output survives. The refuter in `08_expert_verification_packet.md` is the test; if it fires, stop and retract rather than distribute.

## Legal note

One-to-one contact only. The venues in `03_public_contact_points.csv` are public institutional or opt-in channels. No personal emails were collected, no purchased list was used, no bulk send is planned, and every message carries an opt-out line. GDPR and CAN-SPAM style rules apply to unsolicited outreach in many jurisdictions; using public submission and contact routes avoids the structural failure and the legal one.
