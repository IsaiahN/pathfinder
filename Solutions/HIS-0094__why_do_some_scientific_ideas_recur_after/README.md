## Short answer in effect terms

A rejected idea is either a refused term or a term unreachable in the frame that rejected it. The figures say a term cannot cycle in and out: it "becomes reachable again only if something new is minted, which is growth" (Fig 6 :45). So recurrence is never the same idea running again. It is new growth (an imported atom, a minted term, or an extended instrument) that restores reach, after which the ground can settle the term. Most historical "rejections" are conventions, not ground settlements, so they never installed a settled refusal at all. Later acceptance is therefore a change in the frame's contact, not a change in the ground: "the ground does not decay, the channel does" (Fig 10 :24).

## What is deliberately not here

- No personal emails of named individuals.
- No ranking of people by how likely they are to agree that ideas recur for this reason.
- No bulk-send plan.

Three reasons, in the figures' own words:

1. **Agreement is not a verdict.** Fig 2 :35: "Alignment is triangulation, not negotiation. A mirror cannot correct you." A list selected for receptiveness manufactures the correlation that makes agreement worthless (Fig 2 :40: "agreement about the world is worthless when the agreeing frames share an evidence pool").
2. **A room does not add an anchor.** Fig 7 :13: "agreement compounds, evidence does not." More readers of a historiography cannot turn a convention into a measurement.
3. **Send the residual, not the interiors.** Fig 10 :52: "Bring a characterised residual, never the source." An outside frame that has absorbed the framework before giving a verdict has joined the frame being checked.

Legally, scraping personal emails for unsolicited bulk outreach breaches GDPR and CAN-SPAM style rules in many jurisdictions. That is a practical failure on top of the structural one.
