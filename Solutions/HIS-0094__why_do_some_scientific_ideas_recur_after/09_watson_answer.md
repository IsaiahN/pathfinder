## QUESTION

Decide, in effect terms, what explains (R1) the reappearance of a scientific idea after rejection and (R2) its later acceptance. Option A: the idea was right all along and the world or the community eventually caught up. Option B: a refusal is an addition to the record, so the term can return only through new growth, and social acceptance is a convention that is not the settlement. What each leads to: A delivers a retrospective `general` verdict, counts agreement as ground, and cannot distinguish convergence from cycling. B delivers a checkable shape, four refuters written in advance, and an open slot until provenance and a ground reading close it.

## DOMAIN CENSUS

R by arity, symmetry and scale, then one line per domain with actors / currency / anchor, partition or union, and order by dependency.

- **R (the residual).** Arity: two linked events plus a persistence condition (refusal, absence, reappearance, settlement). Symmetry: the repeated label is not evidence of the repeated object; same substance by different routes is convergence, not cycling. Scale: one frame's closure at t and t+n, with a lossy transform between. Fig 9 names a compound, not one hard question: a case in two conditions is not a harder case of either.
- **Domain 1, record and reach (framework internal).** Actors: Γ, atoms(Γ), closure(Γ), refusals (¬), mints (φ), imports. Currency: what is recorded (monotone) versus what is reachable (non monotone). Anchor: the figures as metalaws, plus the ground for any settlement. Question type: partition, because record and reach are two quantities. Dependency: refusal first, reach falls, new growth restores reach.
- **Domain 2, instrument and ground (empirical).** Actors: instruments, observations, the ground, the residual R. Currency: contact. Anchor: the ground, which is not a frame and does not change its answer because of what any frame thinks. Question type: union with D1 at the contact edge. Dependency: perception, then vocabulary, then objective (Fig 3).
- **Domain 3, the historical community (social).** Actors: reviewers, journals, authorities, prestige. Currency: agreement and credibility. Anchor: none of its own; when it uses agreement it is Fig 2 panel 1 or 2, not panel 3. Question type: union with D1/D2, never a separate ground. Dependency: convention follows settlement and never precedes it as a verdict.

Order by dependency: refuse before mint (Formula step 2); settle each first-order re-entry before composing over it (Fig 12 :104); describe the residual before choosing a frame (Fig 9 :27). Strain test applied: "same idea" across time is a type question about identity of substance, not a temporal continuity of one object; re-census under Fig 8 :50 and Fig 13 :121 dissolves it.

## VERDICT (from the figures)

A refused term cannot recur as the same term; it returns only through new growth (mint, import or instrument extension), and what appears to recur is the same substance reached by a different route, separable from an adopted import only by provenance. "No known mechanism" was never a proof of absence. The ground does not decay, the channel does, so later acceptance is a change in the frame's reach and settlement plus a separate change in convention. This is a `general` structural shape with a `specified` reading of the cases, ungrounded. Cost if wrong: if the original rejection is mistaken for a ground settlement, a convention is promoted to an anchor and the frame drifts coherently, which Fig 2 :35 says nobody notices; the avoidable part is spending an outside reader's independence by sending a conclusion instead of a refuter. Undoable: yes, the three domains are separate files in one packet, the convention layer can be withdrawn without touching the reach law, and no primitive is claimed. Reversed by: a case where a refused term returns with no new growth; a third operator that moves the wall; a sealed frame; agreement settling a world-question with no anchor; a demonstrable ground change with no channel change; or a provenance record showing two identical appearances with different routes and no closure difference. Until one of these fires, the verdict stands as `candidate`.
