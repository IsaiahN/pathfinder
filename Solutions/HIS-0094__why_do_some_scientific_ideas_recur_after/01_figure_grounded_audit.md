## 0. The residual, stated in effect terms

The question asks for two things, and the figures require them to be kept apart:

- **R1, recurrence:** why a term that a community refused or could not reach comes to be present again in a later frame.
- **R2, later acceptance:** what explains the later ground settlement, given that the earlier frame withheld it.

Stated as an effect: an item is absent from a frame's reach at time t, present at time t+n, and the question is what moved. The candidates are (a) the world changed, (b) the frame changed by minting, (c) the frame changed by import from a frame whose closure differs, (d) an instrument changed so a previously unresolved residual resolved, or (e) nothing about ground or atoms changed and only the community's convention changed.

What an answer would look like, bound as an ordinary term before the search (Formula step "Direction"): a mechanism that (i) forbids literal cycling of a refused term, (ii) names what may move the wall, (iii) distinguishes a convention change from a ground settlement, and (iv) issues a refuter. I commit to that shape here, before reading the world sources, so it can accrue a residual like any other term.

R by arity, symmetry and scale:

- **Arity:** two linked events plus a persistence condition (refusal, absence, reappearance, settlement).
- **Symmetry:** "the same idea" is claimed across time. Under the figures, identity of substance across different routes is convergence, not cycling (Fig 8 :50; Fig 13 :121). The repeated label is not evidence of the repeated object.
- **Scale:** one frame's closure at t and t+n, with a lossy transform between them (Fig 4 :22, "Only methods cross up. Only head starts come down.").

## 3. Findings

### F1 - A refused term cannot simply recur (decisive for R1)

Fig 6 :42: "What is recorded only grows. What is reachable is derived, and it can fall."
Fig 6 :43: "So the closure and the reach are two quantities: one monotone, one not."
Fig 6 :45: "a term cannot cycle in and out, because the refusal never leaves the record: it becomes reachable again only if something new is minted, which is growth"

The figures do not merely permit recurrence; they govern its form. A refusal is an addition to the record (Fig 6 :42). Reach falls because something was added, not because anything was deleted. Therefore a later appearance of "the same idea" cannot be the old term re-running. The record still holds the refusal, and the term cannot cycle back into reach on its own. What changed is the derivation: something new was minted or imported, and under everything now held the term is derivable again.

**Consequence for the question:** recurrence is a dependent variable. It is explained by new growth, not by the idea's intrinsic merit or by the passage of time. **Mode: `general`, structural.** Triangulated across Fig 6 (reach dynamics) and the Operators table entry for negation (a refusal is a term that stays), with low overlap between a dynamics claim and an operator definition.

**Refuter:** exhibit a case where a refused term re-enters reach with no new mint, no import, no instrument change and no change in the record. That breaks Fig 6 :45 and the whole answer falls.

### F2 - Only import moves the wall; inward instruments extend access (governs the mechanism)

Fig 6 :14: "the only operator that moves the wall"
Fig 6 :23: "No frame escapes its own closure. The bound moves only on contact with a frame whose closure differs."
Fig 6 :29: "inward: is our representation adequate to hold it? The instrument extends access."
Fig 6 :34: "Where nothing is, there is no edge to extend from, and the search for a proto-instrument is a search for one that is already there."

There are exactly two ways the earlier frame's reach can grow: an inward extension of an instrument already returning something, or an outward import from a frame whose closure differs. Fig 6 :34 forbids the magical version, where a sensor is specified from a description of the target. This matches the historical record read below: continental drift waited on mantle convection and on magnetometers already returning stripes; Semmelweis waited on germ theory, an import from Pasteur's frame; the ulcer hypothesis waited on a culture technique that kept a sample past two days.

**Mode: `general`.** Triangulated between Fig 6 :14/:23 (outward) and Fig 6 :29/:34 (inward). Those are the two opposed instruments Fig 6 :31 says must never be read against each other; they are kept as two tracks here.

**Refuter:** show a third operator, besides mint, import and instrument extension, that expands closure(Γ). The figures' set is closed on this point.

### F3 - Introduction is available, exclusion is not

Fig 11 :61: "The seat’s power is asymmetric. Introduction is available; exclusion is not."
Fig 8 :26: "2: the atom crosses back"

A community cannot vote an old convention out of its habitat, because contact is a fact about the world that does not consult it (Fig 11 :62: "You can add an actor and you cannot subtract the habitat, because contact is a fact about the world and does not consult you."). What it can do is introduce an actor, a method or a frame whose closure differs, and let the ground decide. This is why "later acceptance" is an arrival worked on, not a subtraction of the earlier refusal. **Mode: `general`.** Single source for the asymmetry claim (Fig 11), corroborated in kind by the import direction in Fig 8.

**Refuter:** demonstrate a real seat that removes a condition from the habitat rather than substituting a habitat (Fig 11 :66: "Isolation is not removal of the habitat. It is substitution of one habitat for another.").

### F4 - Which term was refused, and why a shape is invisible

Fig 5 :53: "refuted: a term we hold is wrong; a term is owed that says so"
Fig 5 :59: "a term whose residual PERSISTS AND DOES NOT TREND DOWN is the one to point the refusal at"
Fig 5 :62: "A frame searching for an answer of a particular shape holds that shape as a PREFERENCE rather than as a term"

The fifth kind of gap sorts by what is wrong, not by what is missing. The candidates for refusal are enumerable: every bound, predicting term is a candidate, ranked by a residual that persists and does not trend down. But the term most worth refusing may never have been bound at all, because the frame held the sought-for shape as a preference, not a term (Fig 5 :62). This is the structural reason a community can reject the right thing for the wrong reason, or refuse an outcome it did not want instead of the belief it actually holds.

The remedy is a discipline, not a branch: "make the sought-for shape an ORDINARY TERM" (Fig 5 :65). This package does that in §0 by writing what an answer would look like before searching the world sources.

**Mode: `general`.** Single-source for the mechanism (Fig 5 amendment). The pre-registration in §0 applies it.

**Refuter:** show a frame that can certify the direction of its own question without first binding the sought-for shape as a term. Fig 5 :67 says it cannot.

### F5 - Settling is necessary, not sufficient; a growing library is not a growing reach

Fig 5 :72: "a library that grows is not a reach that grows"
Fig 5 :70: "A term is settled when the ground pays for it. A term is usable when something can compose over it."

So an idea can be confirmed and still not compose, or be unconfirmed and still be reachable. Later acceptance must therefore be split into "the ground paid" and "other terms can now compose over it." Collapsing these into one event is the named failure. **Mode: `general`.** Triangulated with Fig 12 :106: "Nothing composes over what was never admitted as a part."

**Refuter:** exhibit a settled term nothing can compose over, yet which the framework still counts as extending reach. Fig 5 :69 to :72 forbids it.

### F6 - "We searched and found no mechanism" was never a proof of absence

Fig 8 :39: "So a search that finds nothing is never a proof of absence."
Fig 8 :32: "Closure is idempotent. Composing inside a closed set never leaves it, so no search, however long, adds an atom."
Fig 3 :27: "A link with no instrument is not a link that works. It is a link nobody has looked at."
Fig 9 :44: "Only a sealed room can be searched to the end, and no frame is a sealed room."

This is the direct answer to the standard form of the historical rejection. Wegener was refused "largely because there was no known geological mechanism which could propel such massive movements" (world source W1). Under Fig 8 :39, that is an argument from failure to find, and the figures forbid treating it as absence. Under Fig 3 :27, the missing link had no instrument, so the rejection was a reading of nothing, not a weak reading.

**Mode: `specified`** when applied to a named case, `general` as the metalaw. Triangulated across Fig 8, Fig 3 and Fig 9, three low-overlap routes (idempotence of closure, dependency position of the instrument, non-sealedness of any room).

**Refuter:** a completed, sealed search inside a closed frame whose failure to find φ does prove φ absent. Fig 9 :44 says no frame is sealed.

### F7 - What recurs is the same shape by a different route, not the same term

Fig 13 :121: "Different routes can arrive at the same substance."
Fig 8 :50: "Two frames can hold the same atom by convergence, reaching it separately, which is corroboration at maximum independence."
Fig 8 :51: "Convergent derivation and adopted import look identical in the contents. Only the record of where each came from separates them"

This resolves the surface paradox of "recurrence." Two appearances with the same content are not one term cycling; they can be a convergent derivation and an adopted import that look identical in the contents. Only the record of provenance separates them, and only where the closures differ is there a surplus (Fig 8 :52). So a historian asking whether this is the same idea back again is asking a question the artifact cannot answer. The lineage, not the idea, is the proof (Fig 13 :128: "It is recoverable from the lineage.").

**Mode: `general`.** Triangulated across Fig 13 and Fig 8, which share the convergence theme but reach it from composition and from independence respectively.

**Refuter:** show two frames holding the same atom by convergence whose records show no provenance difference and no closure difference. Then the surplus claim fails.

### F8 - The ground does not decay; "the world changed" is an open undecidable

Fig 10 :24: "the ground does not decay, the channel does"
Fig 10 :47: "If a term and its refusal are both settled, the record holds a contradiction. Whether that is two scopes, an error in the ground, or a world that changed is not yet decidable here."
Fig 6 :47: "Whether that is two scopes, an error in the ground, or a world that changed is not yet decidable here."

Candidate (a) in §0, "the world changed," is therefore named by the figures but not granted. The default explanation is a channel failure: "stale percepts, dropped transients, saturated metrics, pooled readings" (Fig 10 :25). The later acceptance is a change in what the frame can register, not a change in what settles it. The case where the ground itself changed is not forbidden; it is undecidable from inside, and it must be recorded as such rather than silently chosen.

**Mode: `general`.** Single source (Fig 10 :24, :47, repeated in Fig 6 :47). This is the one place where a world source (Kuhn, W5/W6) strains against the figures; see §6.

**Refuter:** a case where the ground demonstrably changed and the channel demonstrably did not, with an instrument that discriminates the two. The figures currently say the distinction is undecidable here.

### F9 - Social acceptance is a convention, not a verdict (governs R2 and D3)

Fig 2 :35: "Alignment is triangulation, not negotiation. A mirror cannot correct you."
Fig 2 :40: "agreement about the world is worthless when the agreeing frames share an evidence pool"
Fig 2 :41: "efficient for a convention: agreement about a shared meaning is the product, not a corruption of it"
Fig 7 :13: "agreement compounds, evidence does not"
Fig 9 :60: "Use filters for the budget and witnesses for the verdict. Never let a filter hand you a verdict."

The community's later acceptance is real, but it belongs to D3 and it is a convention. It cannot be the ground that settles R2. When a journal or a committee reverses a verdict, that is a change in shared meaning, which Fig 2 :41 says is legitimate for conventions. It becomes corruption only when it is counted as a verdict about the world. Fig 9 :5 requires the split: "Split it rather than search." So the answer to "what explains their later acceptance" has two layers, kept separate and never averaged: a convention layer (D3) and a settlement layer (D2).

**Mode: `general`.** Triangulated across Fig 2, Fig 7 and Fig 9, three different quantities (anchor, accumulation, budget versus witness).

**Refuter:** a community whose agreement, with no anchor and no new ground contact, settles a question about the world. Fig 2 :35 names this as the collapse.

### F10 - A settled idea becomes an atom for the next level

Fig 12 :104: "A settled molecule becomes an atom for whatever composes over it. So irreducible is a fact about a level and never about the world."
Fig 13 :110: "And each composition becomes an atom for whatever composes over it."

This is the positive clause that completes F1. Once the ground pays, the once-refused term is an atom like any other, and further work composes over it without re-earning it inside a run (Fig 12 :108). Across a boundary that drops the record, every lineage pays again (Fig 12 :109), which is why rediscovery is genuine work and not a replay. **Mode: `general`.** Triangulated across Fig 12 and Fig 13.

**Refuter:** show that a settled composition cannot serve as an operand at the next level. That breaks the recursion and with it the composition reading of recurrence.

## 4. Pre-registered refuter

Required before the world search, per the exemplar's seat rule: "a falsifier written before the run is a design instrument, not only an honesty device." Written before W1 to W8 were opened:

The shape claim (an idea cannot recur as the same term; it returns only through new growth, and social acceptance is not the settlement) is refuted if any of the following holds:

1. A refused term re-enters reach with no new mint, no import, no instrument change and no growth in the record (breaks F1 / Fig 6 :45).
2. A third operator besides mint, import and instrument extension is shown to move closure(Γ) (breaks F2 / Fig 6 :14).
3. A frame is exhibited that is sealed, so that a failed search inside it proves absence (breaks F6 / Fig 9 :44).
4. A case shows community agreement settling a world-question with no anchor and no new ground contact (breaks F9 / Fig 2 :35).
5. A case shows the ground demonstrably changing while the channel demonstrably does not (breaks F8 / Fig 10 :24).

Until one of these is run, no part of the historical reading may be labelled `grounded`.

## 5. Recommendation

1. Lead with F1 and F6. They are the only findings that directly answer "why recur," and both are structural, not interpretive.
2. Keep R1 and R2 in separate loops and never average the verdicts (Fig 1 :19: "The residual is indexed by object slot, not aggregated.").
3. State the mode on every historical sentence. A historian's "the idea was right all along" is a `specified` claim, not a verdict.
4. Put the lineage before the name. Ask which atom crossed, from which closure, and with what source gate (Fig 8 :13). Do not ask whether the idea was "ahead of its time."
5. Send the generator, not the conclusion: "Only methods cross up. Only head starts come down." (Fig 4 :22).

## 6. Canonical usage check and the Kuhn conflict

Two local sources govern use: `FIGURES_README_canonical.md`, which says of the figures "the quotes are the authority, not this summary," and that a reading making one contradict another "has misidentified its domain, not found a flaw"; and `THE_FORMULA.md`, whose step 7 separates SEARCHED, INWARD and OUTWARD and forbids reading INWARD against OUTWARD.

### The genuine conflict

World source W5 (Wikipedia, *The Structure of Scientific Revolutions*) reports Kuhn's incommensurability: "it is simply not possible... to construct an impartial language that can be used to perform a neutral comparison between conflicting paradigms." World source W6 (Stanford Encyclopedia of Philosophy, *Thomas Kuhn*) confirms the thesis: "there is no common measure for assessing the different scientific theories."

This **conflicts** with the figures' D2. Fig 2 :29 says "An anchor is whatever settles the question and does not change its answer because of what any frame thinks." Fig 2 :35 says triangulation, not negotiation. Kuhn's "no common measure" is structurally Fig 2 panel 1 (no anchor, frames update on one another) or panel 2 (one evidence pool, agreement is the pool talking). The figures do not deny that this is what communities often do. They deny that it constitutes a settlement.

Per the instruction, a conflict sends us back to the census; it never flips the verdict. Re-census: Kuhn is describing D3 (the social transition between paradigms), which has no anchor of its own. The figures describe D2 (the ground that settles). They are different domains, so the apparent contradiction is a category error, not a flaw. Kuhn is accepted as a description of the convention layer and refused as an account of the settlement layer. The verdict from F1 to F10 is unchanged. Agreement with Kuhn on D3 is not corroboration of the figures on D2.

### Strain that remains live

Fig 10 :47 says a term and its refusal both settled is "not yet decidable here." The historical cases in W1 to W4 are almost all conventions, not settled refusals, so the contradiction case is not triggered. It is recorded as an open item, not hidden. A second strain: world source W3 reports a dispute about whether the rejection of the ulcer hypothesis was prejudice or warranted scepticism ("it has also been argued that medical researchers showed a proper degree of scientific scepticism until the H. pylori hypothesis could be supported by evidence"). The framework resolves it by D3/D2: scepticism is a convention until the ground pays; what settles it is the ground, not the attitude. The strain is named, not smoothed.

## 8. Verdict summary

The structural shape survives all checks: a refused term cannot cycle; it returns only through new growth (mint, import or instrument extension); what looks like recurrence is convergence or adoption, separable only by provenance; social acceptance is a convention and not the settlement; the ground does not decay, the channel does. The three domains are kept separate and their verdicts are never averaged. Strains after re-census: two, both named. Mode: `general` for the shape, `specified` for the reading of the historical cases, nowhere `grounded`.
