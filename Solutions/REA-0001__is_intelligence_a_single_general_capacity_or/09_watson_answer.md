## QUESTION

Decide, in effect terms, what the Tether says about the question "is intelligence a single general capacity, or a collection of abilities that can vary independently?" Option A: one scalar at the apex of a correlation matrix, with residuals aggregated into one global number and capability located in the agent. Option B: a vector of per-slot residuals, positive correlations explained by overlapping component processes or developmental coupling, abilities located in the agent. What each leads to: A gives parsimony and a single intervention target but discards slot-level signal and keeps a refused premise; B keeps the slot signal but still locates capacity in the agent, so it inherits the same refused premise, and it owes an explanation of the positive manifold. The question itself is a union of three conditions, not one hard question.

## VERDICT (from the figures)

The question as posed is refused: capability is agent-and-habitat, so neither a single g nor a list of intelligences is the right object (D1, triangulated). Where measurement is used, the shape is per-slot residuals, never an aggregate that hides the live signal (D2, triangulated), and a positive manifold does not identify one cause rather than many because the rival accounts are isomers (D2, triangulated). Intelligence is a post-hoc label on a lineage that met its ground (D3, single-source). The empirical factor structure of human ability is a `gap` from the figures, reachable only by ground data: no measurement in this folder settles it. Mode: `general` on the structure, `specified` on the operational predictions, nowhere `grounded`.

- **cost if wrong:** bounded and locatable. The packet makes no empirical claim that g is false, so being wrong about the per-slot preference costs a missed abstraction, not a retracted fact. The larger cost is spending an outside reader's independence by sending a recording instead of a generator, which Fig 2 :42 prices: "Buy independence in proportion to the cost of being wrong." That cost is avoidable by leading with the shape and its refuter.
- **undoable:** yes. The verdict is a shape, not a term; no primitive is withdrawn and no factor structure is asserted. A refuter firing removes or narrows one claim and the retraction is recorded in `01_figure_grounded_audit.md`. A sent claim can be superseded by a corrected packet.
- **reversed by:** a ground reading, not agreement. A broad multi-habitat battery in which the scalar leaves no live slot and out-predicts the vector would reverse the per-slot preference. A demonstration that capability is invariant across habitats would reverse D1. Time-series or experimental discrimination between a single latent cause and a coupled network would close the empirical gap. Deriving one of the six substrate members from the other five would break the triangulation. Prestige or agreement is none of these.
