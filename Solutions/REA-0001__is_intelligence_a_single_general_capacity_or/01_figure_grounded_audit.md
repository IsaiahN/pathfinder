## 0. The question, restated as a residual

The question offers two options:

- **A (one general capacity).** One scalar at the apex explains a positive correlation matrix across cognitive tasks. Residuals are aggregated into one global number. The capacity is a property of the individual.
- **B (many independent abilities).** Performance must be read per task slot. Residuals persist per slot. Positive correlations are produced by overlapping component processes or by developmental coupling, not by one common cause. The abilities are properties of the individual.

**Residual in effect terms (mode `specified`).** We observe a matrix of performance over many task slots. The question is whether the correct description of that matrix is one global scalar or a vector of slot-indexed residuals, and whether what is being described lives in the agent or in the agent-and-habitat contact. What each option leads to: A gives parsimony and a single intervention target but discards slot-level signal; B keeps the slot signal and the per-ability intervention target but must explain the positive manifold without a single common cause. Both options share a hidden premise, that capability is located in the agent. Fig 11 :39 refuses that premise before either option is scored.

**Direction bound as an ordinary term (Formula "Direction").** An answer with the right shape would: (i) name the correct unit of description, (ii) state where capability lives, (iii) say what evidence could distinguish the alternatives, and (iv) refuse to answer the empirical factor-structure question from the armchair. This written expectation is the refutable shape. It is not a preference held invisibly.

## 1. Domain census (before choosing any figure line)

**R by arity, symmetry, scale.**

- *Arity:* many. R is a vector over object slots, one residual per task, not a scalar (Fig 1 :19). Arity is the first thing the question gets wrong when it asks "one or many" as if a count could be read off a correlation matrix.
- *Symmetry:* the slots are not interchangeable. Under a symmetric treatment you average them, which is exactly the move Fig 1 :22 forbids. Positive manifold is symmetric in the test items only after the slots have already been flattened.
- *Scale:* two distinct scales are conflated. Interindividual differences (a population correlation matrix, where psychometric g is defined) and intraindividual development (one agent's growth through contact) are not the same object. The *Wiring of Intelligence* paper calls the confusion of these two an "idiographic blind spot." Different scales, different loops.

**One line per domain: actors / currency / anchor. No nameable anchor means it is not a domain yet.**

| domain | actors | currency | anchor | nameable? |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| D1 definitional or metatheoretical | the term "intelligence", the seat, the habitat | capability, which is contact, not a score | Fig 11 as metalaw; the ground for any measurement | yes |
| D2 operational or psychometric | test items, persons, slot residuals, latent factors | per-slot residual and covariance | predictive validity, experiments, interventions | yes |
| D3 evolutionary or comparative | lineages, habitats, social inputs, selecting environment | survival and ground contact | the ground, meaning what survived | yes |

**Partition or union.** Not one hard question. D1, D2 and D3 are a **union compound** (Fig 9 :54: *"And when the split lands on a union rather than a partition, unbundle before you search again."*). A case in three conditions is not a harder case of any one. D1 asks whether the term is legitimate; D2 asks how to measure; D3 asks where the capacity came from. A single lever applied to all three will clear the region where only one condition holds and leave the intersections standing. Verdicts are kept separate and never averaged.

**Order by dependency (Fig 3).** The chain is ordered by dependency, not importance. D1 (vocabulary: what is being measured) must be settled before D2 (measurement), and D2 before D3 (evolutionary explanation). Fig 3 :19: *"Optimising a link that already works cannot rescue one that does not."* Fig 3 :22-23: *"A step whose input never arrives cannot be diagnosed, only its predecessor can. Which is why a reading taken below the break is not a weak reading. It is a reading of nothing."* Answering D2 while D1 is still open produces a number whose referent was never fixed. The frame-internal history of factor analysis is precisely this: a well-measured quantity whose object was assumed.

**Strain test applied before concluding.** An apparent strain between "refuse the term" (Fig 11) and "measure per slot" (Fig 1) dissolves on re-census: Fig 11 governs the definitional domain, Fig 1 governs the operational domain. Different objects, different levels. No figure is forced. Per the canonical README: a reading that makes figures contradict one another has misidentified its domain.

## 3. Findings

### F1 — The question is refused, not answered (decisive, D1)

Fig 11 :34-36 puts the replacement in the figure's own words. Fig 11 :35: *"The wrong question, and this figure is the replacement for it."* Fig 11 :36: *"Where nothing maintains anything, intelligence is a label applied afterwards to a lineage that met its ground. Nothing was intelligent; selection ran. The word does no work there."*

Fig 11 :39: *"So the answerable question is not what intelligence is, but what makes a system capable. And capability is a property of agent-and-habitat, never of the agent alone."* Fig 11 :40: *"A system cannot be made more capable by improving the agent in isolation. An improvement that does not change contact changes nothing, however much it improves."*

Both options in the question put capability inside the agent, whether as one general capacity or as a list of abilities. Both therefore inherit the refused premise. This is not a preference for B. Gardner-style multiple intelligences is refused for the same reason as Spearman-style g: it is still a list of properties of the agent. `THE_FORMULA.md` step 2 names the correct routing: *"REFUTED a term held is false; what is owed is a refusal, not an explanation."* A refutation is a negation, and *"a vocabulary without it can only accumulate"* (Fig 12 amendment, Operators table).

**Mode:** `general` on the structural refusal; `specified` on the prediction that no agent-internal inventory of capacities will close the contact residual.

**World corroboration (secondary, low overlap).** Burkart, Schubiger and van Schaik (2017, *Behavioral and Brain Sciences*) support *"the cultural intelligence approach, which stresses the critical importance of social inputs during the ontogenetic construction of survival-relevant skills."* Savi et al. (2019, *Perspectives on Psychological Science*) state that intelligence *"cannot be understood in isolation. It is a product of genetic, environmental, and developmental factors."* Two different fields, neither drawn from the framework, both putting capacity in the agent-and-habitat. Corroboration only; the figures decide.

**Refuter:** a demonstration that capability is invariant across habitats and unchanged by contact, so that a fixed agent-internal inventory settles every residual, breaks Fig 11 :39-40 and F1 falls.

### F2 — Where measurement is possible, the unit is the slot, never the aggregate (D2)

Fig 1 :19: *"The residual is indexed by object slot, not aggregated."* Fig 1 :22: *"Aggregating across slots is how a live signal disappears into an average."* Fig 1 :13: *"a global R near zero with one live slot residual is a legal state."*

The operational shape is a vector of independently varying slot residuals. A single global score is an aggregation that can be near zero while one slot is still live. This is not a claim that g is empirically absent: Fig 1 :13 explicitly permits a near-zero global residual. It is a claim about which description hides signal. If you collapse the vector to one number to decide where to intervene, you have thrown away the only quantity that says where to look.

Fig 1 :23: *"The ground enters as the only metric."* Fig 1 :27: *"They are frame-internal. A frame cannot score itself with a quantity it also produces."* A g loading is produced by the measurement frame; it cannot then be the metric that validates the frame.

**Mode:** `specified`, ungrounded. **Triangulated** with F3 (Fig 1 and Fig 13 have low overlap: the agent loop versus the substrate set).

**World corroboration.** Poirier et al. (2020, *Proceedings of the Royal Society B*) find across 555 bivariate correlations that *"the average correlation among cognitive abilities is low ( r = 0.185),"* suggesting relatively weak support for general intelligence in animals. Thornton, Clayton and Grodzinski (2012, *Phil. Trans. R. Soc. B*) report for chimpanzees that *"there is no evidence for one single 'general intelligence' factor, but neither were performances entirely unrelated across different tasks,"* supporting *"a number of distinct clusters of abilities."* Hernández-Orallo et al. (2021, *Scientific Reports*) find that *"generality and capability can decouple at the individual level: very specialised agents can show high capability and vice versa."*

**Refuter:** a broad battery in which partialling out one general factor leaves no slot with positive residual mass, repeated across habitats and developmental stages, would refute the per-slot shape claim.

### F3 — Distinct members are not readings of one quantity (D2, triangulates F2)

Fig 13 :34: *"And within a kind the members are distinct too, which is what makes them separate frames."* Fig 13 :35: *"Space, time and energy are not three readings of one quantity. Neither are spectrum, gradient and network."* The figure continues: *"If two members drew on the same underlying fact, their agreement would carry no information, and one of them would be redundant rather than minimal."*

The substrate is the tractable instance of "many independent capacities that cannot be collapsed to one." Its members are independent by construction; collapsing them would make one redundant and destroy the information their agreement carries. This is a structural precedent, not an empirical result about human cognition. It triangulates F2 because it arrives at non-collapse from a different figure and a different object.

**Mode:** `general` (structural). **Triangulated** with F2.

**Refuter:** derive one of the six substrate members from the other five, or show two members drawing on the same underlying fact so that one is redundant. That breaks Fig 13 :34-35 and the triangulation, though not the empirical psychometric question.

### F4 — A positive correlation matrix does not decide one versus many (D2 and D3)

Fig 2 :39: *"Correlation has valence."* Fig 2 :40: *"disqualifying for a verdict: agreement about the world is worthless when the agreeing frames share an evidence pool."* Fig 8 :39: *"So a search that finds nothing is never a proof of absence."* Fig 9 :32: *"you get a kind of thing, never the answer itself."* Fig 13 :134: *"So the system can report that it lacks something rather than producing an answer anyway. Unreachable is a verdict here, not a silence,"*

The positive manifold is not a discriminating observation between a single common cause and multiple coupled causes. The framework's own independence rule says that agreement among frames drawing on one pool is the pool talking, not two witnesses. A correlation matrix is one pool seen through many items. From inside the measurement frame, the one-factor and the multiple-factor accounts are indiscernible on that evidence alone.

**Mode:** `specified` on the underdetermination claim; `general` on the metalaw. **Triangulated** across Fig 2, Fig 8, Fig 9 and Fig 13 (four figures, low conceptual overlap).

**World corroboration, and the conflict rule.** McFarland (2012, *Journal of Clinical and Experimental Neuropsychology*) simulated one-factor and eight-factor models and concludes that *"the presence of a positive manifold in the correlations between diverse cognitive tests does not provide differential support for either single factor or multiple factor models of general abilities."* Paterson and Deary (2026, *Intelligence*) show that *"the presence of hierarchically-ordered, positive correlations might imply, but does not uniquely identify, a single general factor."* Savi et al. (2019) state that *"g theory and sampling theory and factor models and network models cannot be statistically distinguished on the basis of correlation indexes alone."*

There is a genuine conflict in the world literature, and it is recorded rather than hidden: Carroll (2003) concludes that *"Classical hypotheses claiming a general factor g and, orthogonal to it and to each other, two or more second-stratum factors, can be confirmed even when the second-stratum factors include Gf."* Savi et al. also report that psychometric g is *"well established and noncontroversial."* This is a conflict about **what a factor model can confirm**, not about whether a correlation pattern uniquely identifies a cause. Per the method, a conflict sends us back to the census, never to a flipped verdict. Re-census: Carroll's claim is a result inside D2 (a model can be fitted and confirmed); the underdetermination claim is about D1/D2 at the level of causal identification. Different quantities. Both can hold. The verdict is unchanged. Agreement from a shared evidence pool is one witness; these sources do not share one pool (psychometrics, neuropsychology, quantitative psychology, history of mathematics), which is why the convergence has some weight, though it remains secondary to the figures.

**Refuter:** time-series or experimental evidence that statistically distinguishes one latent cause from a coupled network on the same data would move the empirical question from `gap` to ground. Savi et al. explicitly allow that *"Time-series data and experimental interventions may very well distinguish between the models."*

### F5 — It is a union, so split before searching (D1 / D2 / D3)

Fig 9 :4-5: *"Disagreements that do not shrink with effort, where each rule keeps working well on a different subset, are not one hard question but several well-formed ones. Split it rather than search."* Fig 9 :54: *"And when the split lands on a union rather than a partition, unbundle before you search again."*

The century-long failure of the "g or multiple intelligences" debate to converge is the symptom Fig 9 names: each rule works well on a different subset (factor analysis on D2, developmental evidence on D3, definitional intuition on D1). Effort applied above the break does not rescue a link below it. The three domains get three verdicts in §7, kept separate.

**Mode:** `general`. **Single-source** for the union diagnosis (Fig 9), corroborated by the three-domain census.

**Refuter:** show that a single construct closes all three domains without residue, so that the union is really a partition.

### F6 — A g score is a frame-internal quantity (D2)

Fig 5 warns that *"a term that explains everything by saying nothing"* fails the MDL bargain. Fig 1 :25-27 lists what does not count: *"coverage, and compression achieved,"* *"predicates minted, and credibility accrued,"* and *"They are frame-internal. A frame cannot score itself with a quantity it also produces."* A single number that summarizes the whole matrix is maximally compressive and explains nothing about any slot. Fig 12 :94: *"The arrangement is the substance."*

This is a **notation** finding, not a claim that factor analysis is invalid. The arrangement (which process couples to which slot, in which habitat, at which developmental stage) carries the information the single number drops. The substance is in the arrangement.

**Mode:** `general`. **Single-source** (Fig 1 + Fig 12, cited separately).

**Refuter:** exhibit a case where the single scalar predicts a new slot better than the full slot vector, out of sample and across habitats. That would make the aggregate pay, and the finding would be withdrawn.

### F7 — Shape, not a name (all domains)

The framework returns a narrowed form, not a filled slot. Fig 9 :32: *"you get a kind of thing, never the answer itself."* Fig 13 :138-139 restates the same limit and points back to Figure 9. Fig 13 :134-135: *"Unreachable is a verdict here, not a silence,"* *"It cannot say which primitive is missing, because naming it would mean already holding it."*

Applied here: the figures can return the shape *capability is agent-and-habitat; read residuals per slot; do not aggregate; correlation alone does not identify a cause.* They cannot return the empirical factor structure of human ability. Naming one factor structure as the answer would be naming what the figures do not hold. Per `05_what_shape_means.md` §5b, the "shape, not a name" rule governs a **missing** primitive; here the missing thing is an instrument that discriminates causes, not a new ability list.

**Mode:** `general`. **Triangulated** (Fig 9 + Fig 13 + figures README).

**Refuter:** a ground reading that names the causal structure directly would retire the shape claim in favour of a settled term.

## 4. Pre-registered refuter (written before the conclusion)

Required before any outreach, per the seat rule that a falsifier written before the run is a design instrument. The shape claim is refuted if any of the following holds:

1. **Slot refuter.** A broad, multi-habitat battery in which partialling out one general factor leaves no slot with positive residual mass, and the single scalar out-predicts the slot vector out of sample. This breaks Fig 1 :13, :19, :22 and F2.
2. **Contact refuter.** A demonstration that capability is invariant across habitats and unchanged by contact, so that a fixed agent-internal inventory closes every residual. This breaks Fig 11 :39-40 and F1.
3. **Independence refuter.** Two substrate members shown to draw on the same underlying fact, making one redundant. This breaks Fig 13 :34-35 and the F2 triangulation.
4. **Identification refuter.** An intervention or time-series design that statistically distinguishes a single latent cause from a coupled network on the same correlation data. This closes the empirical `gap` and moves it to ground, retiring F4.

If any fires, the affected claim is withdrawn and the retraction is recorded here, not silently edited away. Until one is run, no part of the empirical answer may be labelled `grounded`.

## 7. Updated verdict

The question as posed is **refused** (Fig 11 :39-40, triangulated with Formula step 2). Where it can be made operational, the figure-derived shape **favours multiple independently varying slot descriptions over a single aggregate** (Fig 1 :19, :22, triangulated with Fig 13 :34-35), and it **refuses to let a positive correlation matrix decide the causal question** (Fig 2 :39-40, Fig 8 :39, Fig 9 :32, Fig 13 :134, triangulated). The empirical claim that human abilities are in fact one factor or several is a **`gap` from the figures**, reachable only by the ground. Mode: `general` on the structure, `specified` on the operational predictions, nowhere `grounded`. Strains after re-census: **none hidden**; the one live strain is the world-literature conflict between model confirmation and causal identification, recorded in F4 and resolved by re-census rather than by a softer reading.
