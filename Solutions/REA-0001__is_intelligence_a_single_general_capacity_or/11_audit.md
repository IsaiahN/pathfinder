Symmetry: slots are not interchangeable; the average is not the signal (Fig 1 :22). Scale: two scales are conflated, the interindividual correlation matrix where psychometric g is defined, and the intraindividual developmental trajectory where capability is built. The Savi et al. - **Domain 1, definitional.** Actors: the term "intelligence", the seat, the habitat. Currency: capability as contact, not a score. Anchor: Fig 11 as metalaw, plus the ground for a measurement. Question type: refused term. My route to this was Fig 11 :35-40 plus the seat map's REFUTED bin, which the primary reached through Formula step 2. Different starting documents, same boundary.
- **Domain 2, operational.** Actors: items, persons, slot residuals, latent factors. Currency: per-slot residual and covariance. Anchor: predictive validity, interventions. Question type: union, not partition, because hierarchical models confirm g and broad abilities together.
- **Domain 3, evolutionary.** Actors: lineages, habitats, social inputs, selecting environment. Currency: survival and ground contact. Anchor: the ground, meaning what survived. Question type: selection ran; intelligence is a post-hoc label.

Order by dependency: refuse before mint (Formula step 2); describe the residual before naming a frame (Fig 9 :27-28); settle D1 before D2 before D3 (Fig 3 :20-23). A reading below the vocabulary break is a reading of nothing.

**My census matches the primary's three domains and its union reading.** Where the primary derived from Fig 1 and Fig 13, I derived the D1 boundary from Fig 11 and the seat map, and the D2 union from Fig 9 and the hierarchical-model literature. ## Round trip gap `R_T`

The Symbols table defines `R_T` as the gap between what was sent up and what came back down, `gap(x, (T_E ∘ T_A)(x))`, measuring what the coarser description cannot hold. Fig 4 :30 requires it to be measured rather than assumed. I ran the round trip on the condensation this folder actually uses.

1. **Sent up (x):** the verbatim figure set, chiefly Fig 11 :35-40 (refusal, capability as agent-and-habitat), Fig 1 :13/:19/:22 (per-slot, never aggregate), Fig 9 :32/:54 (shape, union) and Fig 12 :117 (isomer).
2. **Abstraction (T_A):** the shorthand carried in `07`: refuse the premise; measure per slot; correlation does not identify a cause; empirical structure is a gap.
3. **Concretisation (T_E):** a concrete answer to the original question, namely "intelligence is many independent abilities located in the agent."

**`R_T` is nonzero.** Three things the shorthand holds do not survive the trip back down:

- the **refusal of the premise**, which the concrete answer discards by supplying another agent-internal inventory;
- the **near-zero global caveat** in Fig 1 :13, which a flat "not one, many" reading erases;
- the **`gap` on empirical factor structure**, which a concrete answer converts into an empirical assertion.

Conversely, two things survive cleanly: residuals are per slot rather than aggregate, and positive correlations do not identify one cause. That is the residue worth sending. `R_T` was **not** measured numerically, because no ground reading exists; it is recorded qualitatively as the enumerated set of dropped constraints. Treating "the formula is a condensed version of the metalaws" as identity would set `R_T` to zero and would be the dead-reckoning error Fig 4 :30 names. The honest form is a lossy condensation with a measured gap, which `09_watson_answer.md` states.
