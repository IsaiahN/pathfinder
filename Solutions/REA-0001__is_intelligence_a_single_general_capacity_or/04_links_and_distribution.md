Two things in this literature bear directly on the pitch:

1. **The conflict is the asset, not the problem.** Carroll (2003) and Savi et al. (2019) confirm that psychometric g can be fitted and confirmed. McFarland (2012), Paterson and Deary (2026) and Savi et al. (2019) show that the same correlation pattern does not identify one cause. A researcher who knows only the first half will think this solution denies g; it does not. Lead with the distinction, not with a verdict about g.
2. **The agent-and-habitat claim has independent support from a field that never read the figures.** Burkart et al. (2017) put the construction of survival-relevant skills in social inputs during development. Savi et al. (2019) say intelligence cannot be understood in isolation. This is why F1 is not merely a framework preference. It is still corroboration, not a witness: the figures decide.

Independence check: the eight sources come from psychometrics, neuropsychology, history of mathematics, AI evaluation, comparative cognition, evolutionary biology and quantitative psychology. They do not share a single evidence pool. The eight essays, by contrast, all share the framework author and are high overlap; their agreement with the figures is expected and carries little independent weight.

## Distribution: offer, do not push

Figure-grounded reasons:

- **Fig 4 :22** — *"Only methods cross up. Only head starts come down."* Sending the full architecture before anyone has described a residual sends a recording, not a generator. Lead with the shape and its refuter; offer the links after.
- **Fig 10 :42** — *"A verifier that can reconstruct the claim cannot verify it."* A recipient who has absorbed the entire framework before being asked for a verdict has joined the frame being checked.
- **Fig 9 :27-28** — *"describe it before you go looking, or any frame you pick will seem to fit."* Ask what they would expect to see if the shape claim were true before giving them the article.
- **Fig 2 :40** — *"disqualifying for a verdict: agreement about the world is worthless when the agreeing frames share an evidence pool"* and Fig 7 :35 — *"A room's agreement is variation, not a verdict. Selection between rooms is where the ground votes."* A mass send selected for likely agreement produces a convention, not corroboration.

**Recommended order in a reply:** (1) the shape claim and its refuter; (2) ask what they would count as a failure, and record the answer; (3) only then the three links, with the note that the figures, not the summary, are the authority. Later agreement is never pooled as corroboration.

## The two-stage offer

1. **Stage 1.** Send the residual, the shape claim and the four pre-registered refuters. Ask what the recipient would count as a failure. Record their verdict **before** showing any theory. This is Fig 10 :52 — *"Bring a characterised residual, never the source"* — and Fig 2 :45 — *"Frames range against the ground. They do not negotiate with it."*
2. **Stage 2, opt-in only.** If they are curious, offer the three source links. Seat 4 must not hold the interiors; once a recipient has absorbed the theory, their agreement stops being evidence.
3. **Stage 3.** Offer the formula as a method to reuse. This is permitted, because a generator is not an answer (Fig 4 :22). Each user writes their own refuter and states their own mode, per the Formula's "Direction" section.

**Hard precondition:** stages 1-3 only work if the stage-1 claim survives. It is a shape and a refusal, with no empirical factor-structure claim attached. If a check fires one of the four refuters in `01_figure_grounded_audit.md` §4, stop and record the retraction.
