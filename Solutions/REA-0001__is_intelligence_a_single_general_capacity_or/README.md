## Restating the question as an effect

One option says one scalar at the apex of a correlation matrix predicts every task slot, and residuals are aggregated into a single global number. The other says performance must be read per slot, residuals persist independently, and any positive correlation is explained by overlapping component processes or by developmental coupling rather than by one common cause. What is being decided is both (a) the unit of description, one capacity or many, and (b) where the capacity lives, in the agent alone or in the agent-and-habitat contact. The two options are not the two halves of a single axis; Fig 9 :54 says a union must be unbundled before it is searched.

## What is deliberately not here

- No personal emails of named individuals.
- No ranking of people by how likely they are to agree that the Tether is right.
- No bulk-send plan.

Three reasons, in the figures' own words:

1. **A room does not upgrade the label.** Fig 7 :35: *"A room's agreement is variation, not a verdict. Selection between rooms is where the ground votes."* Adding recipients raises the vocabulary and does not add an anchor.
2. **Selecting for receptiveness manufactures the disqualifying correlation.** Fig 2 :39-40: *"Correlation has valence."* *"disqualifying for a verdict: agreement about the world is worthless when the agreeing frames share an evidence pool."* A list ranked by likely agreement is a list selected to agree.
3. **Asking people to trust before they check spends their independence.** Fig 10 :42: *"A verifier that can reconstruct the claim cannot verify it."* The outside frame must not hold the interiors.

Legally: scraping personal emails for unsolicited bulk outreach breaches GDPR/CAN-SPAM-style rules in many jurisdictions. That is a practical failure on top of the structural one.

## The honest bottom line

The figures refuse the question as posed, because both options locate capability in the agent (Fig 11 :39). Where the question can be made operational, the figures favour per-slot residuals over a single aggregate (Fig 1 :19, :22) and non-redundant members over one underlying quantity (Fig 13 :34-35, triangulated). Whether human abilities in fact compose as one factor or many is a **ground** question about correlation matrices and predictive validity, and the figures do not decide it. `gap` on that empirical claim is a verdict here, not a silence (Fig 13 :134, Fig 8 :39).
