## 1. A shape is a constraint, not a filler

The figures README states the limit: the framework returns *"what a missing thing would have to look like (a shape, not a name)."* Fig 9 :32: *"you get a kind of thing, never the answer itself."* Fig 13 :135: *"It cannot say which primitive is missing, because naming it would mean already holding it."*

From *The Library of Babel*: *"You can cut your search short if the set of books returned is small and descriptive enough that it provides a general shape of the answer that satisfies your question."*

So a **shape** is the narrowed form of an answer you can state before you can name it. It constrains; it does not fill. In this question, *"capability is agent-and-habitat; read residuals per slot; do not aggregate; correlation alone does not identify a cause"* is a shape. *"g is one general capacity"* and *"there are eight independent intelligences"* are names.

The essays make the same point with the image of the replicator in *How Intelligence Actually Works*: a tool that recurses on the description of a thing until the shape has cut away so many aspects that it is *"indiscernible"* from the answer. Crucially: *"the recursion converges toward indiscernibility, and indiscernibility is not identity."*

## 2. How a shape is obtained

- *Library of Babel*: use the vocabulary you already have that is closest to the thing, then look for recurring new terms that overlap or correlate with it. *"With each new term gained, you narrow the space and deepen your vocabulary."*
- Fig 9 :30-31: *"your words are borrowed too, so describe the effect rather than the cause."* Fig 6 :30: *"The description travels. Fails by being sharpened into terms only we can read. Cast it in effect terms."*
- *Decoding Tacit Knowledge*: *"When contact isn't possible, the best you can send is a plain description of what you'd expect to see, because anyone can check that against the world."*
- *Library of Babel* on stopping: you are done when *"the finish line will also be the starting place,"* because by then you have *"composed the answer through the process of locating it."* The paradox is that the search built the vocabulary that makes the answer recognizable.

Applied here: the residual was described in effect terms before any figure line was chosen (a matrix over slots; a count question; a location question). Only then were the figures read. Had "g" or "multiple intelligences" been chosen first, every figure would have seemed to fit, which is the exact failure Fig 9 :27-28 names: *"describe it before you go looking, or any frame you pick will seem to fit."*

## 3. What licenses a name

Only two things, both outside the shape itself:

1. **The ground settles it.** Formula step 5: *"the ground settles it. Verified only by what pays."* The README: the framework *"stops at the limit of what the available instruments can distinguish, and records the lineage so the stop is honest."*
2. **A provenanced import from a frame whose closure differs** (Fig 6 :23, Fig 8 :50-52), with a source gate and a shadow test.

*They're Scanning Books When They Should Be Studying Nature* sharpens this: *"The real import is not a new term in the existing space. It is a new dimension of the space."* And: *"A minimum viable set of six terms is a human's careful guess at reality's compression axes; another sensorium's minimum set need not be those six."* Also: *"Declaring the primitives does not make them neutral. It makes the choice visible."*

## 4. Why both options in this question are names, not shapes

The question asks us to choose between two nouns. Fig 11 refuses the frame before the choice:

- Fig 11 :35: *"The wrong question, and this figure is the replacement for it."*
- Fig 11 :36: *"Where nothing maintains anything, intelligence is a label applied afterwards to a lineage that met its ground. Nothing was intelligent; selection ran. The word does no work there."*
- Fig 11 :39: *"So the answerable question is not what intelligence is, but what makes a system capable. And capability is a property of agent-and-habitat, never of the agent alone."*

Both options are inventories of the agent. An inventory is a name. The shape is about contact, and the world literature corroborates from an independent field: Burkart et al. (2017) describe social inputs constructing survival-relevant skills during development, and Savi et al. (2019) say intelligence *"cannot be understood in isolation. It is a product of genetic, environmental, and developmental factors."*

*How Intelligence Actually Works* puts the same claim in its opening: *"The intelligence we experience is the emergent result of extensive mapping of a complex system against unknown and unforgiving environments... the mapping is not a means to intelligence; the mapping is the intelligence."*

## 5. Q: What would license a claim about the factor structure of human ability?

**Nothing in the framework.** The framework returns a shape. A factor structure is licensed only by the ground settling a minted term: longitudinal and interventional data, or an instrument that discriminates among the candidate causes of the positive manifold. Until then, any factor structure is the searcher's own contribution and must be recorded as such.

**Does the literature already contain the answer?** Not in the sense that matters here. Carroll (2003) and Savi et al. (2019) confirm that a general factor can be fitted and confirmed. McFarland (2012), Paterson and Deary (2026) and Savi et al. (2019) show that the same correlation pattern does not identify one cause rather than many. The first result is about a model; the second about causal identification. Both are recorded in `01_figure_grounded_audit.md` F4. This solution makes no claim that g is false.

**Why the instrument matters.** *The Hard Problem is About Source* gives the mechanism. Metamerism: an instrument reads green and *"cannot comment on the source."* One green light, or yellow plus blue, or RGB, or CMYK, or a scheme not yet discovered. *"If several paths create the same behavior, then behavior is a projection with lossy information."* *"An instrument that can't distinguish these cases demonstrates its own inadequacy."* It was the spectrometer, not an argument, that resolved it. Here the positive manifold is the green reading; the candidate causes are the sources. What is owed is a discriminating instrument, not a factor named from a projection.

## 5b. Correction to my own §3-4

I initially wrote the "shape, not a name" rule as if it forbade naming any capacity. That overstates it. The README's phrase is *"what a **missing** thing would have to look like (a shape, not a name)."* The subject is a primitive not held in Γ. Applied to this question, the missing thing is an instrument that discriminates among causes of the positive manifold, not an ability list.

So the correct reading is: the figures may legitimately describe the structure of residuals (which is within what they hold), but they may not name an empirical factor structure (which is not). What remains wrong in any answer that asserts "there is one g" or "there are many independent intelligences" is the source-naming error: a structure named from a reading where several structures could give the same reading. Both the narrowed rule and the metamerism point are recorded here so neither is quietly lost. Triangulated (README + Fig 9 + Fig 13).

## 6. Two consequences for outreach

**Prestige is not action.** From *The Recursive Self-Improvement Trap*: *"Prestige must never be allowed to validate an action directly... Someone let prestige stand in for proof... Prestige can fund an action. It can give an action a budget to be tried. But it cannot settle an action. Only the ground can settle it. The Ground Maintainer must be blind to prestige."* A campaign that gathers famous names to agree with the shape is the named collapse, not corroboration. The legitimate ask gives the claim a budget to be tried and lets a ground settle it.

**This audit is a simulation, not a ground reading.** Same essay: *"LLMs can quickly simulate an approximate answer/output with the formula found in the architecture post... However, the truly testable output would need to be derived from the real architecture or working the formula yourself."* And the architecture article: *"outputs generated must be seen as simulacra."* So `01`, `06`, `07`, `09` and `11` are simulated derivations checked for internal consistency against the figures. They are **not** tests of the question and must be labelled as such. They cannot settle anything they report on.
