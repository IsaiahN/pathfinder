# Expert verification packet - the Tether on advantage versus luck

**What this is:** a claim in a form a strategy researcher, econometrician or research-methods scholar can check. Mode, derivation and refuters are stated before the conclusion, so a reader knows what to look for and what would settle it.

**Mode: `specified` - not `grounded`.** No prospective source-discriminating measurement is claimed. This is a shape and a route, not a result. It is a simulated derivation checked for internal consistency against the figures, not a ground reading (`05_what_shape_means.md` section 7).

**Boundary of the check.** The first three sections are the whole stage-1 ask. Provenance, links and the method offer come later and only if a verdict on stage 1 is recorded first.

## 1. The claim, in one paragraph

A performance reading cannot settle its own source. The same high return is consistent with a genuine firm advantage, timing, favorable market conditions, or luck, so distinguishing them is not a matter of a better reading, more controls, or a lower p value. It requires four conditions at once: a discriminator (an intervention or contrast that maps the same reading to different sources); independence between the frames compared, so agreement means something; an anchor that does not move when pushed; and verdicts kept per firm-slot, never averaged into a signal that says where to look. These are four different failure modes with four different instruments, not four degrees of one problem. A genuine advantage is agent-and-habitat, so the discriminator must show that contact and reach mattered. Luck is not a rival cause to be tested and excluded; it is the residue the instruments cannot source, and an honest report declares it. The claim is a shape that any answer must have; it does not name which firm is genuine or which source produced a given run.

## 2. Three checkable sub-claims

### C1 - Framework-internal; checkable by reading Figures 9, 1 and 2

- **Statement:** the reading does not determine the source; frame-internal quantities cannot witness; independence decides whether agreement counts.
- **Source, verbatim:**
  - Fig 9 :30 - *"your words are borrowed too, so describe the effect"*; Fig 9 :31 - *"rather than the cause"*
  - Fig 1 :27 - *"They are frame-internal. A frame cannot score itself with a quantity it also produces."*
  - Fig 2 :31 - *"Independence, between two frames, is how little they draw on the same evidence."*; Fig 2 :35 - *"Alignment is triangulation, not negotiation. A mirror cannot correct you."*
  - Fig 2 :40 - *"disqualifying for a verdict: agreement about the world is worthless when the agreeing frames share an evidence pool"*
- **What a researcher can check against their own knowledge:** Fama and French write that *"The challenge is to distinguish skill from luck. Given the multitude of funds, many have extreme returns by chance."* Harvey and Liu note that two influential bootstrap papers *"using similar data, they arrive at different conclusions."* Is a shared pool being counted as two witnesses in your field? A "no" backed by a design that fixes independence is a real refutation of the mapping.
- **Refuter:** an instrument that maps an identical reading to its source uniquely, without a contrast, an intervention, or out-of-sample information; or a documented case where shared-pool agreement carries verdict weight. Either fires against C1.

### C3 - Structure, order and competing explanations

- **Statement:** the union of conditions must be unbundled before a lever is chosen; two sources fitting one reading are metamers, while two firms with identical atoms and different bonds are isomers; the middle link of the dependency chain is the usual break.
- **Source, verbatim:**
  - Fig 9 :57 - *"What remains is smaller and it is also categorically different: a case in two conditions is not a harder case of either."*
  - Fig 12 :117 - *"isomer. Same atoms, different bond, different substance. The difference is invisible in the formula and decisive in the behaviour."*
  - Fig 3 :27 - *"A link with no instrument is not a link that works. It is a link nobody has looked at."*

## 5. What would settle it

A prospective, per-firm study that pre-registers its contrast, counts independent evidence pools, measures the sources separately, and reports which discriminator failed. Concretely:

1. **Discriminator test:** can one reading be made to yield different attributions under an intervention that changes only one candidate source? Failing this leaves the reading a projection (Fig 9 :30-31).
2. **Independence test:** do the two frames draw on separate evidence pools, so that their agreement is not the pool talking (Fig 2 :31, :40)?
3. **Anchor test:** does the supposed anchor stay fixed when pushed, or does it become another frame (Fig 2 :29-30)?
4. **Per-slot test:** is the verdict reported per firm, not pooled into an average (Fig 1 :19, :22)?
5. **Source-discriminating instrument:** can the design distinguish a settled Γ term from a paying habitat from a favorable sequence from luck, when all produce the same profit reading? The source essay says a reading alone *"cannot comment on the source."*
