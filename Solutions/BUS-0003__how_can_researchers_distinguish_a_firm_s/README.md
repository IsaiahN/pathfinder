## The question restated as an effect

What is being decided is not whether a firm outperformed (the reading) but which source produced the reading. In effect terms: a firm's library Γ predicts a market return Γ(b,a); the market returns o′; R = |Γ(b,a) - o′| is measured per firm-slot and per time, never averaged (Fig 1 :19, Fig 1 :22). The observation of interest is a persistently low R. Several sources can produce that same observation: a settled term in Γ (a genuine advantage), a favorable sequence of handoffs (timing), a configuration of the habitat (favorable market conditions), or an unattributed configuration (luck).

- Option A, "distinguish by a better reading of the outcome": leads to more controls, more data, and a significance test. This is a frame scoring itself with a quantity it produces (Fig 1 :27) and a filter used as a witness (Fig 9 :60).
- Option B, "distinguish by a source-discriminating instrument plus independent frames against an anchor that does not update": leads to an intervention or contrast that maps the same reading to different sources, checked per slot and never averaged (Fig 2 :29, :31, Fig 11 :39).

What an answer would look like, written down and bound as an ordinary term before the run (Formula, "Direction"): a discriminator that, given an identical reading, returns which source produced it, plus an instrument for each candidate source and a rule that keeps the verdict per slot. Until bound, that shape predicts nothing and no loop check can see it. It is now bound, so the refuter in section 4 of `01` can fire on it.

The verdict recorded in `09_watson_answer.md` is Option B in `specified` mode, kept per domain and never averaged.

## What is deliberately not here

- No personal emails of named individuals.
- No ranking of people by how likely they are to agree.
- No bulk-send plan.
- No claim that this derivation is `grounded`, no claim that it settles the research-methods question, and no claim that the framework is the only account of source attribution.

Three figure-grounded reasons, plus one practical one:

1. A room is not an anchor. *"A room's agreement is variation, not a verdict. Selection between rooms is where the ground votes."* (Fig 7 :35) and *"Alignment is triangulation, not negotiation. A mirror cannot correct you."* (Fig 2 :35). Gathering agreeable readers manufactures the correlation that disqualifies a verdict.
2. Correlation has valence. *"disqualifying for a verdict: agreement about the world is worthless when the agreeing frames share an evidence pool"* (Fig 2 :40). A list selected for receptiveness is one witness wearing many names.
3. A verifier must be outside the interior. *"A verifier that can reconstruct the claim cannot verify it."* (Fig 10 :42). The first contact therefore carries the residual and its refuter, not the framework.
4. Legally, unsolicited bulk email to scraped addresses breaches GDPR and CAN-SPAM style rules in many jurisdictions and gets the sending domain blacklisted. Public opt-in routes avoid that failure as well as the structural one.
