# WATSON answer, BUS-0003

**Mode: `specified`, not `grounded`.** Figures are primary and decide. World sources are secondary, corroborate but never overrule, and a conflict returns the derivation to the domain census. This is a simulated derivation checked for internal consistency against the figures, not a ground reading.

## QUESTION

How can researchers distinguish a firm's genuine strategic advantage from luck, timing, or favorable market conditions?

Restated as an effect: given an identical performance reading o′, what discriminates the source that produced it, a settled term in the firm's library, a favorable sequence, a paying habitat, or luck? R = |Γ(b,a) - o′| is indexed per firm and per time, never aggregated (Fig 1 :19). Anchor: a pre-registered contrast, an experiment or a counter, named as an anchor in Fig 2 :29. The market return anchors "did it pay", not "which source paid".

## DOMAIN CENSUS

Symmetry: not symmetric; reading and source are not interchangeable. Scale: firm loop, population ranking, market habitat, research community; cross-scale, membrane lossy (Fig 4 :21), so R_T must be measured.

## VERDICT (from the figures)

**The figure-derived verdict:** a performance reading cannot settle its own source, so researchers must range the same reading against independent frames with an anchor that does not update, keep the verdict per firm-slot, and declare luck as the residue their instruments cannot discriminate rather than naming it as a rival cause. Four conditions, S, I, A and P, must hold together; they are kept separate per domain and never averaged. A genuine advantage is agent-and-habitat, and a control is a provenance-owed binding. Which specific source produced a given run is a declared `gap`. Mode `specified`. No world source flipped a verdict; the one genuine pool conflict (Fama-French versus Harvey-Liu) was returned to the census as one witness.

- **Cost if wrong:** misdirected method. A study failing I would be sold more teams on the same data, a study failing S would be told to add more controls, and a single lever would be applied to a union that needs four. In outreach terms, an expert's twenty minutes would be spent refuting a shape that was never the right shape. The cost is real but bounded and largely reversible.
- **Undoable:** yes. No term is deleted if the claim fails; a refutation is added, and the reach falls because *"something was added rather than because anything was deleted"* (Fig 6 :42-43). The shape and its nine falsifiers are recorded in a provenance trail so a later reader can locate the error (Fig 10 :62-64).
- **Reversed by:** any of the nine pre-registered falsifiers, in particular (1) a discriminator that maps one reading to its source without a contrast, (2) shared-pool agreement carrying verdict weight, (3) an average predicting per-firm source prospectively, (4) an invariant firm property across habitat substitution, or (5) a positive measurement of luck with an anchor and out-of-sample success. A genuinely different evidence pool showing that source attribution is fully determined by in-sample readings alone would also reverse it.
