# The Tether's answer on distinguishing advantage from luck

**Mode: `specified` - not `grounded`.** This is a shape and a route, not a result. Simulated derivation checked against the figures (`05_what_shape_means.md` section 7), not a ground reading. No prospective source-discriminating measurement pays for any claim here.

## 1. The residual it answers

An answer would explain how, given an identical performance reading, a researcher can tell which source produced it: a genuine firm advantage, timing, favorable market conditions, or luck. R = |Γ(b,a) - o′| is indexed per firm and per time, never averaged (Fig 1 :19, :22). The question is about **source attribution**, not about the level of performance.

The union must be unbundled before it is searched (Fig 9 :54). It is four conditions, not one hard question:

- **S:** a discriminator exists, a contrast or intervention that maps the same reading to different sources.
- **I:** the frames compared are independent, so their agreement means something.
- **A:** an anchor does not update when pushed.
- **P:** the verdict is per firm-slot, never averaged.

## 2. The shape

1. **The reading cannot settle its own source.** Fig 9 :30-31: *"your words are borrowed too, so describe the effect"*, *"rather than the cause"*. The source essay is blunter: the instrument *"cannot comment on the source."* A high return is consistent with four sources; more controls on the same reading do not select among them.
2. **Independence is the currency of a verdict.** Fig 2 :31: *"Independence, between two frames, is how little they draw on the same evidence."* :35: *"Alignment is triangulation, not negotiation. A mirror cannot correct you."* Several teams reading the same dataset are one witness, not several.
3. **A genuine advantage is relational, not a stock.** Fig 11 :39: *"capability is a property of agent-and-habitat, never of the agent alone."* :40: *"An improvement that does not change contact changes nothing, however much it improves."* A discriminator must show that the firm's contact and reach mattered, not merely that the market paid (Fig 11 :49-51).
4. **Luck is a gap, not a rival cause to be ruled out.** Fig 8 :39: *"So a search that finds nothing is never a proof of absence."* Fig 13 :134: *"Unreachable is a verdict here, not a silence,"* and :135: *"It cannot say which primitive is missing, because naming it would mean already holding it."* Luck is the label for what the instruments cannot source. Declare it; do not fill it and do not claim to have excluded it.
5. **Provenance separates a derived control from an adopted one.** Fig 8 :51: *"Convergent derivation and adopted import look identical in the contents. Only the record of where each came from separates them"*. A factor imported from another frame needs a source gate and a shadow test (Fig 6 :23).
6. **Filters are not witnesses.** Fig 9 :60: *"Use filters for the budget and witnesses for the verdict. Never let a filter hand you a verdict."* Fig 1 :27: *"They are frame-internal. A frame cannot score itself with a quantity it also produces."* A p value or a variance share is a gate, and *"A gate is not the ground"* (Formula step 5).
7. **The middle link is the one with no instrument.** Fig 3 :24, :27: the vocabulary and objective link breaks first, and a link with no instrument is a link nobody has looked at. Researchers have data and thresholds; they have no source discriminator.
8. **Instruments grow from worse instruments already returning something.** Fig 6 :32: *"An instrument is not built from a description. It is improved from a worse instrument already returning something."* Bootstrap tests, random-sequence benchmarks and potential-outcome contrasts are real proto-instruments; what is owed is the one that separates the four sources for the same reading.

## 3. What it explicitly does not say

- Not `grounded`. No prospective source-discriminating measurement has paid for this shape.
- Not a name. It does not say which firm is genuine, which routine won, or which factor removes luck. Fig 9 :32: *"you get a kind of thing, never the answer itself."*
- Not an average. It makes no claim about the percentage of returns attributable to firm, industry or year. Such numbers are filters for budget, not witnesses (Fig 9 :60).
- Not a claim that controls, factor models or significance tests are useless. They are Γ. The claim is that Γ alone does not settle source attribution.
- Not a claim that luck does not exist. It is the residue at the instrument boundary, declared rather than named.
- Not corroboration by agreement. Fama-French and Harvey-Liu share a mutual-fund evidence pool (Fig 2 :31, :40). The cognition, meta-science and econometrics sources corroborate the problem shape, not the firm-level fact.

## 4. What would settle it - the ground

A source-discriminating instrument, not a further argument. The source essay's metamerism rule: an instrument that reads green *"cannot comment on the source"*, and *"the answer is unreached until a better instrument exists."* Here the readings are profit, share and survival; the candidate sources are a settled term in Γ, a favorable sequence, a paying habitat, and luck.

The falsifiers, pre-registered in `01` and repeated in `08`:

1. an instrument that maps an identical reading to its source uniquely without a contrast, an intervention, or out-of-sample information;
2. a single evidence pool producing verdict-weight agreement, or a shared pool counted as two witnesses;
3. an averaged industry statistic that predicts prospectively and per firm which named slot is genuine;
4. an invariant firm property that predicts the return across a habitat substitution that removes payment for it;
5. a source-discriminating instrument that returns "luck" as a positive measurement with an anchor and an out-of-sample success rate.

A prospective study that pre-registers the contrast, counts independent evidence pools, measures the sources separately per firm, and reports which discriminator failed would turn this from `specified` toward `grounded`. The Registered Reports format is the institutional name for the right order.

## 5. The honest stop

**Unreached, not unreachable.** Fig 13 :134: *"Unreachable is a verdict here, not a silence,"* and Fig 8 :39: *"So a search that finds nothing is never a proof of absence."* The shape stops at the limit of the available instruments and records the lineage so the stop is checkable. The missing thing is a discriminator of sources, and it is owed. Naming a winner from a reading would be the metamerism error the source essay forbids. The four conditions in section 2 are a shape; the fifth thing, which source actually produced this firm's run, is not held and is not pretended.

## 6. One-paragraph version for outreach

> The Tether does not rank firms or name the control that wins. It says that a performance reading cannot settle its own source: the same high return is consistent with a genuine firm advantage, timing, a favorable market, or luck. Distinguishing them is therefore not a matter of a better reading, more controls, or a lower p value. It requires four things at once: a contrast that maps one reading to different sources; independence between the frames compared, so agreement means something; an anchor that does not move when pushed; and verdicts kept per firm rather than averaged into a signal that says nothing about where to look. A genuine advantage is agent-and-habitat, so the discriminator must show that contact and reach mattered. Luck is not a rival cause to be tested and excluded; it is the residue the instruments cannot source, and an honest report declares it. What is offered is that shape, five falsifiers written in advance, and the instruments that would close it. Which source produced a given run stays open until a source-discriminating instrument exists, and the stop is recorded rather than filled.
