## 2. What the wider literature bears on the question

**Two evidence pools, not six witnesses.** Fama and French 2010 and Harvey and Liu 2022 ask the same mutual-fund question with related data and bootstrap machinery and reach different conclusions. That is one pool showing its own sensitivity. Imbens and Wooldridge 2009 is econometrics proper, defining causal effects through contrasts, and is a genuinely different frame. Ioannidis 2005 is meta-science about research reliability. Gilovich, Vallone and Tversky 1985 is cognitive psychology about reading streaks into random sequences. Deaton 2010 is development economics about mechanisms. Per Fig 2 :31, *"Independence, between two frames, is how little they draw on the same evidence."*

- **Finance, pool one.** Fama and French write that *"The challenge is to distinguish skill from luck. Given the multitude of funds, many have extreme returns by chance."* Their answer is a bootstrap benchmark: *"few funds produce benchmark-adjusted expected returns sufficient to cover their costs."* This corroborates F1 (the reading alone cannot source) and F10 (bootstrap tests are real proto-instruments). It is finance, not strategic management, so the firm-level transfer is a mechanism-shape corroboration only (Fig 4 :19-20).
- **Finance, same pool.** Harvey and Liu state that *"It is essential to attempt to separate luck from skill in the evaluation of fund performance"* and note that two influential papers *"using similar data, they arrive at different conclusions."* This corroborates F2 and F6: agreement from one pool is not two witnesses, and provenance plus design choice decides what the result means. Counted as one witness with Fama and French.
- **Econometrics.** Imbens and Wooldridge: *"Many empirical questions in economics and other social sciences depend on causal effects of programs or policies."* Their framework of potential outcomes, unconfoundedness and instrumental variables is the formal name for F1's discriminator. Corroborates F1 and F10 from a field with a different evidence pool.
- **Meta-science.** Ioannidis: *"a research finding is less likely to be true when the studies conducted in a field are smaller"*, and he recommends that *"what matters is the totality of the evidence"* and that registration be considered. Corroborates F3 and F9 (stake, flexibility, small studies) without sharing a pool with finance or strategy.
- **Cognitive psychology.** Gilovich, Vallone and Tversky: *"The outcomes of previous shots influenced Cornell players' predictions but not their performance."* Observers detect streaks where the data do not support them. Corroborates F5 at the individual stratum: a perceived source is not a measured source. The anchor is re-named at the new scale (Fig 4 :20).
- **Development economics.** Deaton: *"progress in understanding economic development (as in other branches of economics) must come from the investigation of mechanisms"*, using experimental and non-experimental methods in a process of trial and error. Corroborates F4 and F8: a coefficient is not a mechanism, and settlement is not composability.

**Framework-internal essays** are used for the canonical reading of "shape" in `05_what_shape_means.md` only. Their author wrote the figures, so they are not independent world witnesses. That overlap is recorded in the ledger.

## 3. Distribution rules

Figure-grounded reasons for how material is offered:

- **Fig 4 :22:** *"Only methods cross up. Only head starts come down."* Send the generator, the residual and the refuter, not the conclusion. A recording carried upward *"looks like knowledge and is a description of one occasion"* (Fig 4 :25).
- **Fig 10 :42:** *"A verifier that can reconstruct the claim cannot verify it."* A recipient who has absorbed the framework before being asked for a verdict has joined the frame being checked.
- **Fig 9 :28:** *"any frame you pick will seem to fit"* if the residual is not described first. Ask what they would expect to see if the shape claim were true before showing the source.
- **Fig 2 :40:** agreement from a shared pool is worthless as a verdict. Later agreement about the framework is never pooled as corroboration.
- **Fig 10 :59:** *"an interest in the outcome is unavoidable, an interest in the reading is disqualifying."* Declare the stake; do not ask for belief.

**A hard practical constraint.** AOM's publishing guidance states that submitted work must be created by the authors and not be a product of AI tools unless appropriate to the research question and properly cited. This whole solution is a simulated derivation produced with an LLM and is labelled as such everywhere. It must **not** be submitted as an authored empirical or theoretical article. The legitimate use is as a private checking instrument: post the residual and the refuter, invite a human to run the check, and let that human author whatever they conclude. Any submission must be rewritten and owned by a human author who can defend the derivation, with the AI contribution disclosed under the applicable policy.

**Order in any reply:** (1) the shape claim and the pre-registered refuter; (2) the question "what would you count as a failure here?"; (3) only after a verdict is recorded, the three source links with the note that the figures, not the summary, are the authority; (4) the formula as a reusable method, with the instruction that each user writes their own refuter and states their own mode.

## 4. The two-stage offer, compressed

**Stage 1.** Send `08_expert_verification_packet.md` sections 1 to 3 only: the one-paragraph claim, the three checkable sub-claims with their verbatim figure lines and refuters, and the non-claims. No provenance bundle, no essay links.

**Stage 2, opt-in only.** If the recipient is curious and has already recorded a verdict on stage 1, offer the three public links: the figure text, the formula and usage rule, and the essays. Their later agreement is a conversation, not corroboration (Fig 2 :40).

**Stage 3.** Offer the formula as a method, never as an answer (Fig 4 :22). Each user writes a refuter before running it and states the mode of what they get.

**Stop rule.** If stage 1 fails, the theory is not offered. A failure is more useful than an endorsement.

**Hosting.** Nothing here is hosted or sent by this agent. The human operator hosts the packet on an opt-in page and sends one to one. Prefer a download page that lets a recipient decline contact over an attachment pushed to a list.
