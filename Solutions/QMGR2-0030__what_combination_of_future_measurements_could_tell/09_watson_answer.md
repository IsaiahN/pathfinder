## DOMAIN CENSUS

R by arity, symmetry and scale, then one line per domain with actors / currency / anchor, partition or union, and order by dependency.

- **R (the residual).** The H0 reading compatible with two sources. Arity: at least two overlapping conditions, so a **union compound**, not one hard question (Fig 9 :55). Symmetry: ordered, because a lens mass-profile prior changes lensing inference but not a Cepheid calibration. Scale: first order inside each regime, second order at the early-to-late comparison.
- **Domain 1, early-universe inference.** Actors: CMB photons, sound horizon, Boltzmann solver, ΛCDM assumptions. Currency: H0 inferred under a model. Anchor: the CMB measurement itself. One condition of the union.
- **Domain 2, late-universe ladder.** Actors: Cepheids, TRGB, SNe Ia, masers, DEBs, parallaxes, Miras, SBF. Currency: calibrated distances, H0. Anchor: geometric distances. One condition of the union; partly overlaps D3 through SNe Ia host photometry.
- **Domain 3, independent late-time geometry.** Actors: GW standard sirens, lensing time delays, chronometers, clusters, GRBs. Currency: luminosity distance, time-delay distance, H(z). Anchor: GW propagation and lensing physics, independent of ladder and sound horizon. A frame whose closure differs from D1 and D2.
- **Domain 4, consistency / null tests.** Actors: dimensionless distance ratios, early predictions, late measurements. Currency: a null value fixed under the null. Anchor: the measured distances; the identity is a violable convention, not the ground. Where the union becomes describable.
- **Domain 5, the channel.** Actors: pipelines, priors, mass models, calibrations. Currency: systematic error budget, H0 shift under assumption swap. Anchor: the ground, which the channel must not move.
- **Domain 6, verification / outreach.** Actors: independent teams, blind analyses. Currency: agreement and residual. Anchor: the ground, never agreement. In this simulated run all seats draw on the same evidence pool, so this domain's independence is zero.
- **Order by dependency.** State the residual → name the two source conditions → define the null objective → choose low-overlap frames → run blind swaps → report per slot → pre-register refuters (Fig 3, Fig 7 :36).

Re-census places it in D5: it governs a regime and its own analysis chain, not one regime versus another. No strain remains after the re-census. One live type strain is kept (see TETHER).

## VERDICT (from the figures)

The shape survives; a verdict on which source is real does not. The question is a union compound, so the discriminating combination is not more precision and not a vote. It is a low-overlap set with at least one statistic that cannot be shifted by the disputed calibration, run in separate inward and outward tracks, with blind single-assumption swaps, per-method residuals, reversible cuts and a measured early-to-late round trip. An analysis problem is a refusal of a held channel term; a physical discrepancy is what remains when independent frames agree and the source-discriminating statistic still fails. The framework returns that shape and its four refuters, not the name of a telescope and not the answer. Mode `specified`, ungrounded, candidate. The instrument gap (lensed standard sirens) is recorded, not papered over.

- **cost if wrong:** the cost is building an expensive combination whose statistic does not actually separate the sources, then reading its null result as an answer. Fig 2 :42 says "Buy independence in proportion to the cost of being wrong." Here the cost is a 4 to 6 sigma tension that propagates into all inferred cosmological parameters, so independence is worth paying for. The smaller, avoidable cost is sending a recording of this derivation to outside readers before asking for their verdict, which spends their independence (Fig 10 :42).
- **undoable:** yes. The cuts are ranked and reversible (Fig 9 :41), no term is deleted, and the refuters can fire in either direction. A future data set can reverse the conclusion without withdrawing the method. What cannot be undone cheaply is a bulk send that turns outside frames into believers before stage 1 is recorded.
- **reversed by:** a ground reading, not agreement. An actual value of the H0-independent lensing-to-siren ratio from lensed standard sirens; a blind reanalysis in which one named assumption removes the offset while the null statistic stays fixed; a demonstration that all candidate independent methods share a calibration; or a figure re-census that resolves the ⋛ operand type at second order. Until one of these fires, the verdict stands as `candidate`, and a search that finds nothing is still not a proof of absence (Fig 8 :39).
