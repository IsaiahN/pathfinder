# Expert verification packet: what combination of future measurements could tell a real physical discrepancy from an analysis problem?

**What this is:** a claim in a form a cosmologist can check. Mode, derivation and refuters are stated before the conclusion, so a reader knows what to look for and what would settle it.

**Mode: `specified`, not `grounded`.** No measurement at the ground is claimed. This is a shape and a decision order, not a result.

## 1. The claim, in one paragraph

The H0 discrepancy is a reading compatible with two sources: the ground (new physics in the expansion history) or the channel (calibration, modeling prior, pipeline). Because these conditions overlap, the question is a union compound and cannot be closed by more of the same measurement. A discriminating combination must have four properties: low evidence overlap between methods; at least one statistic that does not contain the disputed quantity, so calibration cannot move it; blind single-assumption swaps whose H0 shift is recorded; and per-method residuals with ranked, reversible cuts. Inward refinement of existing ladders and outward imports of independent closures (standard sirens, lensing time delays, distance duality, BAO, chronometers) are two tracks and must not be read against each other. The framework returns that shape. It does not say which source is real and it names no telescope.

## 2. Three checkable sub-claims

### C1: framework-internal, checkable by reading Figures 8, 9 and 2

- **Statement:** "physical discrepancy versus analysis problem" is a union of overlapping conditions, so a single extra measurement inside one family cannot close it; triangulation requires low evidence overlap.
- **Source, verbatim:** Fig 9 :55, "A bundled problem is usually a union of overlapping conditions, not a list of separate ones." Fig 8 :32, "Closure is idempotent. Composing inside a closed set never leaves it, so no search, however long, adds an atom." Fig 2 :31, "Independence, between two frames, is how little they draw on the same evidence."
- **Refuter:** show one additional measurement inside an existing family that removes the offset under every analysis choice, or show that all candidate independent methods share a calibration, data release or prior. Either result withdraws the union/independence claim.

### C2: physics-facing, checkable against the published record

- **Statement:** an H0-independent null statistic exists in the literature and has the differential property required. Kouniatalis and Saridakis (2026) combine strong-lensing time-delay distances with GW standard-siren luminosity distances into a dimensionless ratio that is "exactly independent of the Hubble constant", predicted from early data and measured at late times; with an independent siren H0 it "allows one to unambiguously distinguish between early- and late-time origins of the Hubble tension". A channel failure large enough to mimic the tension also exists: Birrer et al (2020) find the mass-sheet transform is "the dominant source of residual uncertainty in H0" and shift H0 from 73.3 to 67.4 under a mass-profile constraint.
- **What to check:** is the ratio truly independent of H0 and of the sound horizon? Does the TDCOSMO shift survive the stated assumptions? Is there a specified new-physics model and a specified systematics model that give the same value of the ratio? If yes, the statistic is not a discriminant.
- **Refuter:** one explicit pair of models (one physical, one systematic) that produce the same statistic value at the sensitivity claimed. Or a demonstration that the null ratio silently depends on a ladder calibration. Either one demotes C2 to a precision check.

### C3: framework-internal derivation, checkable by reading Figures 10, 1 and 9

- **Statement:** the analysis branch is a refusal of a held term, not a new physics term; the report must be per method with reversible cuts; and the null identity must be able to fire.
- **Source, verbatim:** Fig 10 :24, "the ground does not decay, the channel does"; Fig 10 :18-19, "a declared meaning is not a grounded one" and "→ install what can be violated"; Fig 1 :19, "The residual is indexed by object slot, not aggregated"; Fig 9 :41, "so keep every cut ranked and reversible".
- **Refuter:** show that the late-time discrepancy is located in a ground term that fails the same persistent-flat residual test in every independent frame, and that refusing an analysis term does not remove it. Then the refusal is aimed at the wrong object and the physical branch survives.

## 3. What is explicitly not claimed

- Not that the tension is real, and not that it is an error. No ground reading was taken.
- Not a theory of everything, not a unified field, not a derivation of one cosmological model from another.
- Not a named instrument. Fig 9 :32: "you get a kind of thing, never the answer itself." DESI, Rubin, JWST, LIGO and LISA appear only as imports in the source ledger.
- Not that a null identity is itself the ground. Fig 10 :18: "a declared meaning is not a grounded one." The identity is a convention that can be violated; the measured distances are the ground.
- Not that agreement between teams settles anything. Fig 2 :40: "disqualifying for a verdict: agreement about the world is worthless when the agreeing frames share an evidence pool."
- Not `grounded`. Everything here is candidate.

## 5. What would settle it

1. An actual value of the H0-independent lensing-to-siren ratio from early prediction and late measurement, once lensed standard sirens are available.
2. A blind reanalysis in which changing exactly one named assumption removes the offset while the null statistic stays fixed (analysis problem), or leaves the null statistic failing while independent H0 estimates agree (physical discrepancy).
3. A demonstration that the four pre-registered refuters in `01` section 4 have all failed to fire on a real data set. That would be the limit of current instruments, not a proof of absence (Fig 8 :39).
