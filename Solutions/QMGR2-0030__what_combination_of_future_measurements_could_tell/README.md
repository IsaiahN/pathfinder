## The question as an effect

The observed mismatch is a **reading**: two well-working regimes (early-universe inference under ΛCDM, and late-universe distance measurements) return different values of H0. Two candidate **sources** can produce the same reading: the ground really differs from the model (a physical discrepancy), or the channel carrying the reading is faulty (calibration, crowding, mass-profile priors, pipeline, model assumption, an analysis problem). The question asks for the combination of measurements whose outcome differs between those two sources. It is a source-discrimination question, not a precision question.

The answer is therefore a **shape**, not a named instrument: a set of measurements with stated properties (low evidence overlap, at least one statistic independent of the disputed quantity, source-discriminating predictions, blind and pre-registered analysis, per-method residual reporting, reversible cuts). Which particular telescope or survey fills a slot is an import from the cosmology frame and is recorded as such.

## What is deliberately not here

- No personal emails of named individuals.
- No ranking of people by how likely they are to agree with this answer.
- No bulk-send plan.

Three reasons, in the figures' own words:

1. **Agreement is not an anchor.** Fig 2 :35, "Alignment is triangulation, not negotiation. A mirror cannot correct you."
2. **Selecting for receptiveness manufactures the disqualifying correlation.** Fig 2 :40, "disqualifying for a verdict: agreement about the world is worthless when the agreeing frames share an evidence pool." Selecting recipients by likely agreement creates the shared pool that makes their agreement worthless.
3. **A verifier must not hold the interiors.** Fig 10 :42, "A verifier that can reconstruct the claim cannot verify it." The first contact carries the residual and its refuter, never the framework.

Legally: unsolicited bulk email to scraped personal addresses breaches GDPR and CAN-SPAM style rules in many jurisdictions and gets a sending domain blacklisted. That is a practical failure on top of the structural one.
