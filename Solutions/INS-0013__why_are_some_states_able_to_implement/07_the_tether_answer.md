# The Tether's answer on consistent policy implementation

**Mode: `specified` - not `grounded`.** This is a shape and a route, not a result. Simulated derivation checked against the figures (`05_what_shape_means.md` section 7), not a ground reading. No per-case delivery measurement pays for any claim here.

## 1. The residual it answers

An answer would explain why the gap between what a state's routines predict acting will achieve and what the field actually returns stays small across many decisions in some states and grows quickly in others, so that even a basic decision is not carried out. R is indexed per case and per policy, never averaged (Fig 1 :19, :22). The question is about **duration of contact**, not about the level of capacity.

The union must be unbundled before it is searched (Fig 9 :54). It is two conditions, not one hard question:

- **C1:** the state's channel to what actually happens in the field stays reachable.
- **C2:** the state's reach keeps moving outward through contact with a closure that differs from its own.

## 2. The shape

1. **Consistency is not a stock the state owns.** Fig 11 :39: *"capability is a property of agent-and-habitat, never of the agent alone."* Fig 11 :40: *"An improvement that does not change contact changes nothing, however much it improves."* A list of state attributes is Γ, a candidate library whose reach must be tested against the ground, not the answer.
2. **Quick collapse is channel failure.** Fig 10 :24: *"the ground does not decay, the channel does"*; :26: *"saturated metrics, pooled readings"*; :29: *"→ keep the anchor reachable"*. The state stops hearing what is actually delivered, usually by pooling the very readings that would say where to look. Fig 7 :37-39 gives the two-factor form: bracket fidelity times field pressure, and zero either stops the chain.
3. **Long consistency is a chain of differing rooms.** Fig 7 :13: *"agreement compounds, evidence does not"*; :36: *"The next room must differ from the last, and match the residual it surfaced."* A state that keeps the same review, the same categories and the same room keeps agreeing with itself and stops travelling.
4. **Legibility must become a grammar.** Fig 12 :111: *"A vocabulary without bonds can name a state and cannot name a route to one."* Fig 12 :115: *"having terms for what a situation affords was taken for a dictionary and it is a grammar."* Administrative categories that name cases but cannot state what would count as progress leave the chain broken at the middle link (Fig 3 :24). This is the figure-grounded reading of "even basic decisions".
5. **Renewal needs an import.** Fig 6 :23: *"No frame escapes its own closure. The bound moves only on contact with a frame whose closure differs."* Fig 8 :32: closure is idempotent, so no amount of internal search adds an atom. Frontline staff, local knowledge, courts, citizens and other agencies are not distractions from the core; they are the only operators that move the wall.
6. **The strength and the rigidity are the same object.** Fig 11 :49-51: valence is relative to the residual and no standing list of good actors survives a change of residual. Fig 12 :119: a catalyst is a part that survives its own use, and *"nothing records which a term is"*. When the field changes, the formerly effective procedure can become the thing that prevents the next delivery.
7. **Realized delivery is the anchor, not internal agreement or a ranking.** Fig 2 :29 names *"a measurement, an experiment, a counter, a market, a patient outcome"* as anchors; :35: *"Alignment is triangulation, not negotiation. A mirror cannot correct you."* Governance indices and compliance reports are instruments, and instruments *"can be wrong on axes they do not measure"* (Fig 2 :37-38).
8. **Two states with the same institutions can still differ.** Under Fig 12 :117 they are isomers if the bonds differ: *"same atoms, different bond, different substance."* This is why an institutional inventory alone cannot predict consistency.

## 3. What it explicitly does not say

- Not `grounded`. No prospective delivery measurement has paid for this shape.
- Not a name. It does not say which reform, which staffing rule, which digital system or which institutional design produces consistency. Fig 9 :32: *"you get a kind of thing, never the answer itself."*
- Not an average. It makes no claim about what percentage of implementation variance is "state effect" or "culture effect". Such numbers are filters for budget, not witnesses (Fig 9 :60).
- Not a claim that staff, laws, budgets or leadership are irrelevant. They are Γ. The claim is that Γ does not settle consistency on its own (Fig 11 :40).
- Not a theory of everything about government. It stops where the instrument stops.
- Not corroboration by agreement. The state-capacity and governance-indicator sources share one evidence pool (Fig 2 :31, :40). The low-overlap sources from anthropology, cognition and safety science corroborate the mechanism, not the state-level fact.

## 4. What would settle it - the ground

A source-discriminating instrument, not a further argument. The source essay's metamerism rule: a reading *"cannot comment on the source"*, and *"the answer is unreached until a better instrument exists"*. Here the readings are compliance scores, curb numbers and case throughput; the candidate sources are a paying habitat, a maintained channel, a closed reach, a reporting artefact or simple luck.

The falsifiers, pre-registered in `01` and repeated in `08`:

1. consistent delivery unchanged after the field stops paying for or permitting what the state does;
2. basic decisions lost while bracket fidelity and field pressure both measure high and non-declining;
3. the same closure held for two decades while new field-verified readings keep arriving with no import;
4. a routine's valence invariant across a documented change of residual;
5. one intervention restoring both conditions with nothing categorically different left over.

A prospective study that measures C1 and C2 separately per case and per policy, records the bonds as well as the atoms, and reports which condition failed before the decline would turn this from `specified` toward `grounded`.

## 5. The honest stop

**Unreached, not unreachable.** Fig 13 :134: *"Unreachable is a verdict here, not a silence,"* and Fig 8 :39: *"So a search that finds nothing is never a proof of absence."* The shape stops at the limit of the available instruments and records the lineage so the stop is checkable. The missing thing is a discriminator of sources, and it is owed. Naming a winner from a compliance reading would be the metamerism error the source essay forbids. The eight points in section 2 are a shape; the ninth thing, which reform actually works next, is not held and is not pretended.

## 6. One-paragraph version for outreach

> The Tether does not rank states or name the reform that wins. It says that consistent implementation is a relation, not a stock: the state has to keep its channel to what actually happens in the field reachable, and it has to keep moving its reach outward by contact with ways of working that differ from its own. Those are two conditions with two different failure modes, not two degrees of one problem. Quick collapse is usually the channel going stale, saturated or pooled into a national average, or a vocabulary that can name cases but cannot say what would count as progress, which is why even basic decisions fail. Long consistency is a chain of rooms that each differ from the last and are selected against the residual the last one surfaced. The procedure that produced the earlier capacity can change sign when the field changes, so the strength and the rigidity can be the same object. Realized delivery per case, not a governance ranking, is the anchor. What is offered is that shape, five falsifiers written in advance, and the instruments that would close it. Which particular reforms occupy the slots stays open until a source-discriminating instrument exists, and the stop is recorded rather than filled.
