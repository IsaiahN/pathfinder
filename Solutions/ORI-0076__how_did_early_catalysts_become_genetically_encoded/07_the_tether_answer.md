# The Tether's answer: how did early catalysts become genetically encoded?

**Mode: `specified` on the per-domain claims, `general` on the structural shape, `gap` on the literal historical route. Not `grounded`.** Simulated derivation checked for internal consistency against the figures (`05_what_shape_means.md` §6), not a ground reading.

## 1. The residual it answers

Modern catalysts are genetically encoded: a heritable sequence specifies a protein enzyme (or a catalytic RNA) through the code and translation. Prebiotic catalysts were not so specified: they recurred because chemistry produced them and because they survived their own use. The residual is the bridge between a persisting chemical event and a persisting inherited record. It is a **union of overlapping conditions**, not one hard problem with two opposite answers: catalysis, heritable sequence, sequence-to-product mapping, and a closing habitat are four conditions in contact (Fig 9 :55: "A bundled problem is usually a union of overlapping conditions, not a list of separate ones"). Verdicts are kept per domain and never averaged.

## 2. The shape

1. **A catalyst is a part that survives its own use.** Fig 12 :119: "catalyst. A part that survives its own use. Spent and regenerating parts behave differently across uses, and nothing records which a term is." The prebiotic condition is therefore the existence of *reusable* catalytic parts, distinguishable from spent material. Catalyst is a derived pattern that a compositional system exhibits (Fig 12 :116), not a seventh substrate primitive.

2. **Encoding is the recording of an arrangement, so re-derivation becomes a lookup.** Fig 12 :107-110: "where the arrangement is recorded, re-deriving it is a lookup and the actions were spent once. Where it is not, the result carries forward and the deriving does not, so every lineage pays again." This is the *why* of the transition. An unencoded catalyst recurs only where the work is re-paid; an encoded one is cheap to regenerate. Fig 13 :130: "A term carries the atoms it was built from and the bonds that joined them. That record is not documentation of the term. It is the term." The sequence is the term at the inherited stratum, not an appendix to it.

3. **The crossing is a membrane crossing.** Fig 4 :22-25: "Only methods cross up. Only head starts come down" What can move to the inherited stratum is a re-applicable catalytic method; what comes down is a head start that makes the next generation's search cheaper. A gene that reliably regenerates a working catalyst is a generator. A dead copied sequence is a replay of one occasion. The difference is assay-able, and it is the S3 discriminator.

4. **A settled catalyst becomes an atom for the inherited composition.** Fig 12 :104: "A settled molecule becomes an atom for whatever composes over it. So irreducible is a fact about a level and never about the world." No new primitive is needed for the encoding step. Settlement is necessary (Fig 12 :106) but not sufficient; the substrate must record the arrangement across the boundary (Fig 12 :107-110).

5. **The catalytic function is a functional group, not automatically an isomer.** Fig 12 :123: "functional group. A substructure whose presence fixes the outcome. The group says what kind; the qualifier says which member." Fig 12 :117: "isomer. Same atoms, different bond, different substance." Ribozyme, protein enzyme and metal cluster are members of one functional group. They are isomers only if a shared atom set is declared first; the isomer claim is conditional and has a falsifier (`06` Q2).

6. **The bond from record to product is production (⇒).** Fig 12 :77: "A produces B; B did not exist before." Not identity (gene and product are not intersubstitutable) and not bare sequence (a sequence need not produce a new thing). Fig 12 :111: "A vocabulary without bonds can name a state and cannot name a route to one." A list of molecules can name before and after; only the bond names the route.

7. **A new alphabet enters as import, not composition.** Fig 6 :21-22: "MINT composes inside the closure and can never add an atom. NOVEL means novel relative to atoms, not to the world. IMPORT adds an atom, and the added atom is what expands the closure." If the starting closure is RNA-only, an amino-acid alphabet must be imported from a frame whose closure differs (Fig 6 :14: "the only operator that moves the wall"). Fig 8 :50-52: "Convergent derivation and adopted import look identical in the contents. Only the record of where each came from separates them." Cofactor fossils and exaptation are world-frame candidates for the *shape* of that import, not derivations and not names of the event.

8. **The loop closes when the product re-enters as a component.** Fig 12 :121: "cycle. The output re-enters as input. The sign of the loop decides whether it closes, and a chain cannot close by construction." Once a translated catalyst is part of replication or translation, the chain becomes a cycle. The habitat is enumerated from the residual (Fig 11 :43), not composed from a standing list of important molecules (Fig 11 :51).

9. **Nobody chose.** Fig 11 :36: "Where nothing maintains anything, intelligence is a label applied afterwards to a lineage that met its ground. Nothing was intelligent; selection ran. The word does no work there." In the prebiotic habitat there is no maintainer seat, so the unmaintained mode governs. No designer is needed and none is smuggled in.

10. **The literal route is a `gap`, reported as a verdict.** Fig 9 :32: "you get a kind of thing, never the answer itself." Fig 13 :135: "It cannot say which primitive is missing, because naming it would mean already holding it." Fig 8 :39: "So a search that finds nothing is never a proof of absence." No figure line returns the first encoded catalyst, the date, the setting, or the order of historical events. The world sources agree that the derivation is far from understood. This is unreachable at this budget and shape-not-a-name, not silence.

## 3. What it explicitly does not say

- Not a history. No date, no named first catalyst, no geochemical setting, no claim that RNA-first beats metabolism-first.
- Not that the RNA world is settled. The opened source says even proponents agree there is no conclusive evidence against other paradigms.
- Not that ribozymes and protein enzymes are isomers in the strict sense. That claim is conditional on a declared shared atom set.
- Not that the amino-acid alphabet was present from the start. Under the figures it is an import with a source gate and a shadow test, if it was not already in the closure.
- Not that a gene is automatically a method. That depends on whether the ground pays on reuse, and needs an assay.
- Not a derivation from the figures of cofactor fossilization, exaptation, the adaptor hypothesis or in vitro selection. These are world-frame corroborations of shape, provenanced as such.
- Not `grounded`. No prebiotic measurement was taken. Everything above is `candidate`.

## 4. What would settle it - the ground

A discriminator, not a further argument. *The Hard Problem is About Source* (opened): an instrument that reads green "cannot comment on the source"; it was the spectrometer, not a proof, that resolved the case. Here the corresponding readings are:

- **Bracketing assay:** a closed system measured before and after unencoded catalytic persistence becomes sequence-specified catalytic persistence, with the same ground on both sides. This is the instrument owed for F10 and the historical route.
- **Method/replay assay (S3):** express a candidate sequence, ask whether a working catalyst appears, repeat across generations, measure the round-trip gap. If the arrangement is recorded, re-derivation becomes a lookup; if not, every lineage re-pays. This is Fig 4 :30's "take the round trip and measure the gap" made concrete.
- **Anchor test:** name the anchor for each condition (kinetics for catalysis, replication and readout for heritability, differential persistence for the habitat) and check that none moves when a frame agrees on it (Fig 2 :29).
- **Import shadow test (S2/F6):** does a candidate alphabet import explain a gap that the starting closure cannot close, and can a provenance be shown? If not, it is a wrong atom at a cost above starting from primitives (Fig 6 :24).
- **Standing falsifiers:** the seven in `01` §5, written before the conclusion.

## 6. One-paragraph version for outreach

> The Tether does not tell the story of the first encoded enzyme, and it does not claim to. It says that a catalyst is a part that survives its own use; that genetic encoding is the recording of that arrangement so a later lineage pays a lookup instead of re-deriving the catalyst; that the crossing is a membrane rule, carrying a re-applicable method up and a head start down; that a settled catalyst becomes an atom for the inherited level, so no new primitive is needed for that step; that ribozymes and protein enzymes are members of one functional group and are isomers only over a declared shared atom set; that the bond from record to product is production, not identity; that an amino-acid alphabet enters as an import with provenance; that the loop closes when the product joins replication and translation; and that selection ran in an enumerated habitat with no designer. The literal route stays open as a `gap`. What is offered is the shape any candidate answer must have, seven falsifiers written in advance, and the bracketing assay that would close it.
