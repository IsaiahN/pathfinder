## The question restated as an effect

The question is not asking for a name. It is asking for the route by which a difference in kind was crossed. On one side stand molecules that catalyze but are not inherited as specified sequences (ribozymes, peptide cofactors, metal ions, mineral surfaces). On the other side stand modern catalysts that are genetically encoded (a gene specifies a protein enzyme through the genetic code and translation). The residual is the bridge between these two states: what had to be added, composed, or imported so that a catalytic function could be carried as a heritable sequence rather than only re-occurring wherever chemistry happened to produce it.

Stated per slot (Fig 1 :19, :22), the residual is indexed by catalytic function, not averaged into a single story about "the origin of enzymes." Different catalyst families can cross the membrane at different times and by different bonds. Keeping them separate is the whole discipline here.

**What an answer would look like (ordinary term bound before the run).** Bind the sought-for shape as an ordinary term, per the formula's "Direction" rule: *a route is an answer only if it names (1) the catalytic structure that survives its own use, (2) the arrangement whose recording turns re-derivation into a lookup, (3) the cross-scale filter that carries a method up and a head start down, (4) the anchor that does not update, and (5) the import that supplies any atom not held in the starting closure, with a source gate and a shadow test.* This bound shape is recorded in `01_figure_grounded_audit.md` before any verdict, so it can accrue a residual and be refused like any other term.

Option A: tell the history as a single sequence of named events (RNA world, then peptides, then the code, then DNA). Option B: report the shape the figures license, keep verdicts per domain separate, and mark the literal route as a `gap`. A leads to a `general`-mode verdict presented as history, which is the output-layer defect the framework names. B leads to a checkable set of constraints, a pre-registered refuter, and an honest stop. This solution takes B.

## What is deliberately not here

- No personal emails of named individuals. The ISSOL public contact page publishes officer emails; they are deliberately not copied into `03_public_contact_points.csv` (Fig 2 :40, Seat Map :163).
- No ranking of people by how likely they are to believe the claim.
- No bulk-send plan.
- No claim that this is a history. It is a simulated derivation checked for internal consistency against the figures, not a ground reading. It cannot settle the question it reports on.

Three reasons, in the figures' own words:

1. **Correlation disqualifies a verdict.** Fig 2 :40: "agreement about the world is worthless when the agreeing frames share an evidence pool." Selecting recipients for receptiveness manufactures the correlation that would make their agreement worthless.
2. **A verifier must not hold the interiors.** Fig 10 :42: "A verifier that can reconstruct the claim cannot verify it." The two-stage offer sends the residual and its refuter first.
3. **Prestige is not action.** From *The Recursive Self-Improvement Trap*: "Prestige can fund an action. It can give an action a budget to be tried. But it cannot settle an action. Only the ground can settle it." A famous name is a contact-cost estimate, not a witness.

Legally: scraping personal emails for unsolicited bulk outreach breaches GDPR and CAN-SPAM style rules in many jurisdictions. Public institutional and opt-in channels avoid that failure as well as the structural one.
