## 1. A shape is a constraint, not a filler

The canonical figures README states the limit: the framework returns "what a missing thing would have to look like (a shape, not a name)." Fig 9 :32: "you get a kind of thing, never the answer itself." Fig 13 :135: "It cannot say which primitive is missing, because naming it would mean already holding it."

From *The Library of Babel* (opened): "you can cut your search short if the set of books returned is small and descriptive enough that it provides a general shape of the answer that satisfies your question." And: "With each new term gained, you narrow the space by definition and deepen your vocabulary."

So a **shape** is the narrowed form of an answer you can state before you can name it. It constrains; it does not fill. For ORI-0076 the shape is:

> a persisting catalytic structure, an arrangement whose recording turns re-derivation into a lookup, a cross-scale filter that carries a method up and a head start down, a non-updating anchor, and an imported alphabet where the starting closure lacks one.

That is a shape. "The first encoded catalyst was a such-and-such ribozyme at such-and-such a vent" is a name, and no figure line supplies it.

## 2. How the shape is obtained

- *Library of Babel* (opened): use the vocabulary you already have that is closest to the thing, look for recurring new terms that overlap or correlate with it, recurse. New terms narrow the space. "The problem you have determines what makes a book relevant to you or not."
- Fig 6 :30: "The description travels. Fails by being sharpened into terms only we can read. Cast it in effect terms." So "catalyst" must be cashed out as *survives its own use*, not as a field-specific label.
- Fig 9 :30-31: "your words are borrowed too, so describe the effect rather than the cause." Asking "how did encoding arise" imports the word *encoding*. Restated in effect terms the question becomes: how did a catalytic arrangement come to be carried across a lineage boundary so that the next generation pays a lookup (Fig 12 :107-110)?
- *Library of Babel* (opened) on stopping: "you can cut your search short if the set of books returned is small and descriptive enough..." and "the finish line will also be the starting place." For this question the stop is reached when the residual stops registering against the figure set, not when a date is found.

## 3. What licenses a name

Only two things, both outside the shape itself (from the exemplar's canonical mapping; consistent with the opened figure text):

1. **The ground settles it.** `THE_FORMULA.md` step 5: "the ground settles it. Verified only by what pays. A gate is not the ground." The canonical README: the framework "stops at the limit of what the available instruments can distinguish, and records the lineage so the stop is honest."
2. **A provenanced import from a frame whose closure differs** (Fig 6 :23, Fig 8 :50-52), with a source gate and a shadow test.

For ORI-0076 this means: naming a particular molecule, setting or date as *the* transition requires either a bracketing measurement (a dataset that shows unencoded persistence becoming sequence-specified persistence) or a provenanced import from a field that actually holds that event. Cofactor fossils and exaptation are imported *shapes* from biochemistry, corroborating the form of the import, not names of the historical event.

*They're Scanning Books When They Should* (carried) sharpens the point: "The real import is not a new term in the existing space. It is a new dimension of the space." Also: "A minimum viable set of six terms is a human's careful guess at reality's compression axes; another sensorium's minimum set need not be those six." The six are a visible guess, not nature's own list. Naming one as the answer to this slot turns a visible choice into an invisible one.

## 4. Why the historical narrative is the forbidden error here

*The Hard Problem is About Source* (opened) gives the mechanism. Metamerism: an instrument reads green and "cannot comment on the source" - one green light, or yellow plus blue, or RGB, or CMYK, or a scheme not yet discovered. "If several paths create the same behavior, then behavior is a projection with lossy information." "An instrument that can't distinguish these cases demonstrates its own inadequacy." It was the **spectrometer**, not an argument, that resolved it.

Applied here: many different historical routes can produce the same contemporary reading (genes specifying catalysts). A modern genome cannot comment on the source. Several paths (RNA-first, cofactor-first, peptide-first, metabolism-first) create the same behavior. Naming one from the reading is the metamerism error. What is owed is a discriminator, not a narrative.

Fig 13 :130 (the lineage is the term) cuts the other way and completes the point: the sequence present in a modern organism carries the atoms and bonds of the term, but the *order* of the historical route is not recoverable from the artifact. That is why the lineage, not the artifact, is what would make a historical claim checkable, and why no artifact alone settles F10.

## 5. Does the "shape, not a name" rule forbid naming any catalyst or any imported alphabet? CORRECTION TO A FIRST-PASS READING

**First-pass reading (retracted here, kept visible).** I initially wrote that the framework forbids naming any specific structure in this answer, so ribozyme, cofactor and peptide should all be left as `???`.

**Correction.** The README phrase is "what a **missing** thing would have to look like (a shape, not a name)." The subject is a primitive not held in Γ. Ribozyme-like polymers, peptides and cofactors are not missing from the world frame; they are already present in the biochemical frame that supplies the world sources. Naming them as bindings from that frame is **provenance**, not the invention of a new primitive. What remains forbidden is (a) treating a binding as a derivation from the figures, and (b) claiming a binding moves the wall when it adds no atom (Fig 6 :22: "IMPORT adds an atom, and the added atom is what expands the closure"). An alphabet that genuinely is not derivable inside the starting closure does move the wall and must be reported as an import with a source gate.

Both claims are now recorded: the retracted blanket prohibition and the narrowed binding/import distinction. Nothing was silently erased. Triangulated (canonical README + Fig 6 :21-23 + Fig 12 :123).

## 6. Two consequences for outreach

**Prestige is not action.** From *The Recursive Self-Improvement Trap* (opened): "Prestige must never be allowed to validate an action directly... Someone let prestige stand in for proof... Prestige can fund an action. It can give an action a budget to be tried. But it cannot settle an action. Only the ground can settle it. The Ground Maintainer must be blind to prestige." A list of famous origin-of-life researchers gathered to agree with the shape is the named collapse, not corroboration. The legitimate ask is the opposite: give the claim a budget to be tried, and let a discriminator settle it.

**Natural selection is an instance, not a rival theory.** The same essay: "Natural selection is what the loop looks like when there is no one maintaining the ground." This is why F7 is figure-derived rather than a concession to biology. In the prebiotic habitat there is no maintainer seat, so the unmaintained mode governs. No designer is needed, and none is smuggled in.

**My own audit is a simulation, not a ground reading.** Same essay: "LLMs can quickly simulate an approximate answer/output with the formula found in the architecture post... However, the truly testable output would need to be derived from the real architecture or working the formula yourself." So `01_figure_grounded_audit.md` is a simulated derivation checked for internal consistency against the figures. It is **not** a test of the claim and must be labelled as such. It cannot settle anything it reports on.

## 7. A third consequence: the question is a bundle

*The Library of Babel* (opened) asks of a failed search: "Perhaps your problem was a bundle or conflation of several problems masquerading as one?" Fig 9 :55 says the same thing in the figures: "A bundled problem is usually a union of overlapping conditions, not a list of separate ones." ORI-0076 is such a bundle (persisting catalyst, recorded arrangement, membrane crossing, imported alphabet, closing habitat). That is why the shape has five parts and why the verdicts are kept per domain in `09_watson_answer.md`. Refusing to bundle is not pedantry here; it is the only way to keep the `gap` honest, since only one of the five parts lacks an instrument.
