## 1. A shape is a constraint you can state before you can name the answer

The canonical figures README gives the limit: the framework returns "what a missing thing would have to look like (a shape, not a name)." Fig 9 :32: "you get a kind of thing, never the answer itself." Fig 13 :136-137: "It can say the question is not one question and must be split before it is searched again. It can characterise what is missing in effect terms, which is the description that travels to a frame that may hold it."

The Library of Babel essay: "you can cut your search short if the set of books returned is small and descriptive enough that it provides a general shape of the answer that satisfies your question."

For this question the shape is a **decision procedure**, not a species list: split the union; instrument the objective; enumerate the habitat out to cascades; test the required function against the closure of the historical pool; import only with provenance and an independence debit. "Bring back species X and Y" and "maximise function F" are both names. The shape is the test that decides between them at a site.

## 2. How the shape is obtained: describe the effect first

- *Library of Babel*: use the vocabulary you already hold that is closest to the thing, look for new terms that overlap or correlate with it, recurse. "With each new term gained, you are able to narrow the space by definition and deepen your vocabulary."
- *Decoding Tacit Knowledge*: "When contact isn't possible, the best you can send is a plain description of what you'd expect to see, because anyone can check that against the world."
- Fig 9 :30-31: "your words are borrowed too, so describe the effect rather than the cause."
- Fig 6 :30: "The description travels. Fails by being sharpened into terms only we can read. Cast it in effect terms."

So the first email does not say "the historical baseline is the wrong frame." It says: here is what you would observe if the procedure were right, and here is what would refute it. A restoration practitioner can check that without adopting the framework.

## 3. The bundle, not the single question

The question as posed conflates several problems. The Library of Babel essay names the failure exactly: "Perhaps your problem was a bundle or conflation of several problems masquerading as one?" Fig 9 :54 says "And when the split lands on a union rather than a partition, unbundle before you search again."

The three loops are: historical-fidelity-only sites, future-function-only sites, and the intersection where both conditions bind. The intersection is not "half historical, half function"; Fig 9 :57 says "a case in two conditions is not a harder case of either." It is categorically different and needs its own loop.

## 4. What licenses a name

Only two things, both outside the shape itself:

1. **The ground settles it.** Formula step 5: "SETTLE the ground settles it. Verified only by what pays. A gate is not the ground." Here: a measured site outcome.
2. **A provenanced import from a frame whose closure differs** (Fig 6 :23, Fig 8 :50-52), with a source gate and a shadow test, debited against independence.

Until one of those, the name is the searcher's own contribution and must be recorded as such. Fig 13 :134: "Unreachable is a verdict here, not a silence." A site where no line closes the residual is reported as `gap`, not filled with a plausible species list.

## 5. Source discrimination: why a historical list cannot name the function

*The Hard Problem is About Source* gives the mechanism. An instrument reads green and "cannot comment on the source"; green may be one light, yellow plus blue, RGB, CMYK, or a scheme not yet discovered. The essay states: "If several paths create the same behavior, then behavior is a projection with lossy information" and "An instrument that can't distinguish these cases demonstrates its own inadequacy, it cannot claim anything substantial about what is being measured or what causes it." It was the spectrometer, not an argument, that resolved the case.

Applied here: a site that looks like a reference ecosystem (same species present) may have arrived by several routes and may or may not carry the required function. Presence is a projection; function is the source. What is owed is a discriminating measurement at the site, not a name read off the species list.

*How Intelligence Actually Works* adds the distinction horizon: two things that read the same to an observer "will read as the same thing"; the record of which route produced them is not in the artefact. This is why `01_figure_grounded_audit.md` keeps the lineage of the derivation rather than only its conclusion.

## 6. The practitioner supplies the content

*How Intelligence Actually Works* is explicit that the machinery does not fill a slot by itself. In its replicator analogy, "In this replicator, each recursive cutting adds content, not just constraint", and "the content result is theirs, not the framework's." It says: "The practitioner has built a library of embedded understanding of their domain of expertise, so they naturally get the most use out of the composers." It even names this precise field: "The Slide Rule Principle applies to many things, be it backyard gardens, factory farming, urban planning, landscape engineering or ecological restoration."

So the answer in `07_the_tether_answer.md` is a shape plus a set of tests. The site measurements, the species pool, the climate projections and the function thresholds are supplied by the restoration practitioner. The framework cannot supply them and does not claim to.

*They're Scanning Books When They Should Be Studying Nature* sharpens the limit: "A minimum viable set of six terms is a human's careful guess at reality's compression axes; another sensorium's minimum set need not be those six." And: "Declaring the primitives does not make them neutral. It makes the choice visible." The six-term vocabulary is a visible guess. A function-based frame and a species-based frame may carve the site along different axes; that is a reason to keep both, not to declare one neutral.

## 7. Prestige is not action, and this is a simulation

*The Recursive Self-Improvement Trap*: "Prestige must never be allowed to validate an action directly." And: "Prestige can fund an action. It can give an action a budget to be tried. But it cannot settle an action. Only the ground can settle it. The Ground Maintainer must be blind to prestige." The same essay: "You may all agree and be wrong. You may set your clock to someone else's, who was setting your clock to yours, and be off by many deviations over time. You need ground truth to define where you stand."

The decisive caveat for this folder, from the same essay: "LLMs can quickly simulate an approximate answer/output with the formula found in the architecture post... However, the truly testable output would need to be derived from the real architecture or working the formula yourself." The *Arrival of the Fittest* article agrees: LLM outputs "must be seen as simulacra" and "could never be verified if the solutions presented were not contrived to fit the formula."

Therefore `01_figure_grounded_audit.md` through `11_audit.md` are a **simulated derivation checked for internal consistency against the figures**, not a ground reading. This folder cannot settle the question it reports on. What it can do is make the shape of a checkable answer explicit and record what would refute it.

## 8. Provenance of the figure claims themselves

This is worth stating to an ecology audience. The *Arrival of the Fittest* article has a section, "A note on what is not cited", listing claims in the figures for which no prior source was found. Among them:

- "an actor's valence is relative to the residual rather than a property it carries"
- "introduction is available to a maintainer and exclusion is not"
- "isolation is substitution of one habitat for another rather than removal"

These are exactly the Fig 11 laws that do the most work in this solution. They are **framework claims, not established ecology**. The article says: "Absence of a citation is not a claim of originality. It means a search was made and returned nothing", and cites Fig 8 :39, "a search that finds nothing is never a proof of absence." The world sources in `09_watson_answer.md` corroborate the shape, but they do not convert these laws into ground. This is recorded here so no reader mistakes a figure law for a reviewed ecological result.

## 9. Summary for this question

A shape, not an answer: a per-site procedure that unbundles the union, binds and instruments the objective, enumerates the habitat out to cascades, tests closure, imports with provenance when the wall must move, and reports permanent work. The species names and the function thresholds are supplied by the practitioner and settled at the ground. Everything else in this folder is a simulation of that procedure, not a substitute for it.
