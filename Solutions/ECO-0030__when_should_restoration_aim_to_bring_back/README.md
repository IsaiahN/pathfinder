## What the answer is, in one line

Not a global either/or. It is a per-site decision procedure: unbundle the union, instrument the objective, then compare the required function against the closure of the historical species pool (Fig 8 :32, Fig 6 :23), importing from a different closure only with a source gate and a debit against independence (Fig 6 :24).

## What is deliberately not here

- No personal emails of named individuals.
- No ranking of people by how likely they are to agree with the claim.
- No bulk-send plan.

Three reasons, in the figures' own words: a room is not an anchor (Fig 7 :35); selecting for agreement manufactures the disqualifying correlation (Fig 2 :40); and a verifier who has already absorbed the framework cannot verify it (Fig 10 :42). At scale this is also a legal constraint: unsolicited bulk email breaches GDPR and CAN-SPAM style rules in many jurisdictions.
