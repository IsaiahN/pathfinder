## 1. The claim, in one paragraph

"Initially rejected, later influential" is not one mechanism but a union of two conditions with different anchors. Reception is agreement inside a discipline, and agreement is efficient for a convention and disqualifying as a verdict about the world. A dissenting position matters when it is a frame whose closure differs from the consensus and supplies an atom, a refusal, or a method that the consensus could not derive from inside its own closure; where two frames reach the same atom separately that is convergence, not import. What travels into later research is a method or a head start, never a bare recording of one occasion. An anchor that does not update settles the term, and only then can later research compose over it. The framework returns that shape, its refuters, and the tests that would close a case, not a hero, a fraction or a tipping point.

## 2. Three checkable sub-claims

### C1 , framework-internal; checkable by reading Figures 2, 7 and 9

- **Statement:** reception and settlement are two conditions with different anchors; agreement cannot decide the settlement.
- **Source, verbatim:** Fig 9 :57 "a case in two conditions is not a harder case of either." Fig 2 :40 "disqualifying for a verdict: agreement about the world is worthless when the agreeing frames share an evidence pool." Fig 2 :41 "efficient for a convention: agreement about a shared meaning is the product, not a corruption of it." Fig 7 :35 "A room’s agreement is variation, not a verdict. Selection between rooms is where the ground votes."
- **Refuter:** exhibit a case where reception and settlement share one anchor at the same scope, so that one measurement both makes the claim believed and makes it true; or where the ground moves when the consensus pushes on it (breaks Fig 2 :33).

### C2 , history-facing; checkable against the cases in the ledger

- **Statement:** uptake is a crossing from a differing closure. The minority frame supplies an atom, a refusal or a method; where the atom is reached independently it is convergence, and the record separates convergence from import.
- **Source, verbatim:** Fig 6 :23 "No frame escapes its own closure. The bound moves only on contact with a frame whose closure differs." Fig 8 :45 "Two frames together reach what neither reaches alone." Fig 8 :50 "Two frames can hold the same atom by convergence, reaching it separately, which is corroboration at maximum independence." Fig 8 :51 "Convergent derivation and adopted import look identical in the contents. Only the record of where each came from separates them." Fig 6 :12 "source gate: provenance" and Fig 6 :13 "shadow test: explains a gap".
- **What to check:** in a case you know, name the atom or method that crossed and where it came from. For Mendel, the 1900 duplications by de Vries and Correns are convergence; for Wegener, palaeomagnetism supplied a new reading rather than a vote.
- **Refuter:** show the receiving closure deriving the atom by itself with no differing frame and no imported instrument; or show two frames with identical closure and evidence producing a genuine surplus with no crossing (breaks Fig 6 :21 and Fig 8 :48).

### C3 , ground and channel; checkable against the historical record

- **Statement:** settlement is by an anchor that does not update, and the carrier that survives is a method, not a recording. The channel decays while the ground does not.
- **Source, verbatim:** Fig 2 :29 "An anchor is whatever settles the question and does not change its answer because of what any frame thinks." Fig 2 :33 "The anchor must not update." Fig 10 :24 "the ground does not decay, the channel does." Fig 4 :22 "Only methods cross up. Only head starts come down." Fig 4 :25 "A recording carried upward looks like knowledge and is a description of one occasion." Fig 12 :106 "Nothing composes over what was never admitted as a part."
- **What to check:** can you name a non-updating reading for the case, and does the uptake track it rather than the changing number of adherents? Is what survived a re-applicable method or the story of one success?
- **Refuter:** a case where a bare recording carrying no method produces a new settled term in a different closure; or where a claim is settled by believer count alone with no non-updating reading and later research composes over it successfully.

## 3. What is explicitly not claimed

- Not that every minority position was vindicated, nor that most were.
- Not that rejection is always irrational. A majority that ranges against a non-updating anchor and rejects on that basis is outside C1's exception.
- Not that prestige or standing decides truth. Fig 10 :59 "an interest in the outcome is unavoidable, an interest in the reading is disqualifying." Fig 10 :61 "And standing is a stake that survives the outcome it was earned on."
- Not a general fraction, tipping point, hero, or universal chronology.
- Not a progress narrative. The mechanism recurs at new strata with different actors and anchors (Fig 10 :68).
- Not `grounded`. The case sources are documents read this session, not anchors rebuilt from instruments by this author.
- Not an application of the six-term physical substrate to committees. The composition rules are used as metalaws only.

## 5. What would settle it

1. An **anchor test** on a named case: a reading that did not move when the consensus pushed, with uptake tracking it rather than adherent counts.
2. A **source test**: the atom or method that crossed, with provenance and a shadow test; or a demonstration that the case is convergence, with the two independent routes named.
3. A **refusal test**: the majority-held term that was refused, added to the record rather than deleted from it (Fig 5 :55 "Nothing leaves the library. What falls is the reach.").
4. A **channel test**: method versus recording, and whether the receiving habitat could reproduce the required contact.
5. The seven pre-registered failure conditions in `01` section 5, written before the run.
