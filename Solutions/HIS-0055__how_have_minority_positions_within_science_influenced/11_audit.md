## 4. Round trip gap `R_T`

- the **anchor condition**, which a narrative of changing opinion substitutes for a non-updating reading.

One thing survives cleanly: a differing closure plus a ground anchor is necessary for a ground-settled uptake. That is the residue worth sending. `R_T` was **not** measured numerically, because no ground reading was taken; it is recorded qualitatively as the enumerated set of dropped constraints. Treating "the formula is the condensed version of the metalaws" as identity would set `R_T` to zero and would be the dead-reckoning error Fig 4 :30 names.
