## 3. What the wider literature bears on the question

1. **Cases where rejection was an agreement effect (W1, W2, W5).** Wegener was read as an outsider; Semmelweis's findings were ignored, rejected or ridiculed; McClintock described puzzlement and hostility. These support F2 and F9 at `specified` mode, not at `grounded` mode, because the rejection is documented as a social reception and the settlement came later from a different instrument or mechanism.
2. **A case of convergent derivation (W4).** Mendel's laws were duplicated independently by de Vries and Correns in 1900. This is the cleanest non-import case: two frames reached the same atom separately, which Fig 8 :50 calls "corroboration at maximum independence." It supports F7 and distinguishes convergence from import.
3. **The structure of the disagreement (W3, W6, W9).** Kuhn's anomalies and paradigm-relative manipulations, the paradigm-shift four-stage account, and the Stanford Encyclopedia entry's "considerable disagreement about their import" all bear on F1 and F3. They are philosophy and history of science sources, so they corroborate the shape, not the figure verdict.
4. **A distinct low-overlap witness on credit (W7).** The Matthew effect source gives the social mechanism for F8: credit follows what one already has. It is not a figure source and cannot decide anything; it makes the standing-as-stake reading plausible.
5. **Consensus is a process, not an anchor (W8).** The scientific consensus source describes consensus as built through conferences, publication, replication and peer review. That is a reception process. It is exactly what Fig 2 :40 says cannot serve as a verdict about the world when the frames share a pool.

Overlap declaration: W1, W2, W3, W4, W5, W6, W7 and W8 are all Wikipedia articles and share one editing and citation ecosystem, so they are **one broad evidence pool**, not eight independent witnesses. W9 (Stanford Encyclopedia) is a separate scholarly reference but overlaps the same Kuhnian literature. The five essays opened this session share the framework author's evidence pool completely and carry no independent weight. The low-overlap value of this set is in the variety of *cases* (geology, medicine, genetics, maize cytogenetics), not in the number of hosts.
