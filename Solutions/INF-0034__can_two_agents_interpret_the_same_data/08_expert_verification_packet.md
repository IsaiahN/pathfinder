# Expert verification packet: the Tether on two agents and one reading

**What this is:** a claim in a form an epistemologist, philosopher of science or cognitive scientist can
check. Mode, derivation and refuters are stated before the conclusion, so a reader knows what to look for and
what would settle it.

**Mode: `general` on the structural shape, `specified` on the conditions, nothing `grounded`.** No measurement
of rationality is claimed. This is a shape and a route, not a result.

**Status:** a simulated derivation checked for internal consistency against the figures, not a ground reading.
It cannot settle the question it reports on.

## 1. The claim, in one paragraph

One reading can have more than one rational inverse. A reading is a projection that does not carry its source,
and frames with identical architecture diverge through history and weighting, so two agents can return
different interpretations of one reading without either making an error. The difference lives in the frames,
not in the data, and the agents' agreement or disagreement is not evidence for a verdict, because two people
holding one report are one evidence pool. The licence lasts only until a source-discriminating ground is
applied. When one interpretation fails a discriminator and its holder keeps it unchanged, that is a closed
channel, not a second rational view. The framework offers the shape any answer must have and refuters written
in advance; it does not name which interpretation is correct.

## 2. Three checkable sub-claims

### C1 - divergence from one reading is a legal output of the loop (framework-internal)

- **Statement:** two frames with the same input can rationally diverge; different live slots are a legal
  state even when the global residual is near zero.
- **Source, verbatim:** Symbols table :25-26, *"Two frames with identical architecture diverge because their
  histories and their w diverge."* Fig 1 :19, *"The residual is indexed by object slot, not aggregated."*
  Fig 1 :20, *"a global residual near zero with one live slot is a legal state."*
- **Check:** reopen `FRAMEWORK/reference/03-FIGURES_TEXT.md` and confirm the three strings verbatim.
- **Refuter:** show that w and history are not part of the loop state, or that R is a single global scalar;
  either breaks the legal-state claim.

### C2 - the reading does not determine its source (framework-internal plus world-facing)

- **Statement:** the artefact does not encode its route or source, so more than one inverse is compatible with
  one reading. Two interpretations over the same reading have the structure of isomers: same atoms, different
  bond, different substance.
- **Source, verbatim:** Fig 13 :128, *"Nothing about a composed term tells you, from looking at it, which
  route produced it or whether that route was available."* Fig 13 :129, *"the order is not recoverable from
  the artefact. It is recoverable from the lineage."* Fig 8 :51, *"Convergent derivation and adopted import
  look identical in the contents. Only the record of where each came from separates them."* Fig 12 :117,
  *"Same atoms, different bond, different substance. The difference is invisible in the formula and decisive
  in the behaviour."*
- **Check against the world:** in vision science one tristimulus reading has many spectral sources
  (metamerism); in philosophy of science evidence underdetermines theory. Both predict C2 at low overlap with
  the framework. See `04_links_and_distribution.md` for the fetched URLs.
- **Refuter:** exhibit a data class whose source-to-reading map is injective, so the reading determines one
  interpretation; or show I_A and I_B share every atom and the same bond, so they are one term, not two
  substances.

### C3 - "rational" requires an open channel to the ground, and shared data is not two witnesses (framework-internal)

- **Statement:** loop-correctness alone does not settle rationality. The anchor must not update, the channel
  must stay reachable, and two agents sharing a report are one evidence pool.
- **Source, verbatim:** Fig 2 :29, *"An anchor is whatever settles the question and does not change its answer
  because of what any frame thinks."* Fig 2 :33, *"The anchor must not update."* Fig 10 :24, *"the ground does
  not decay, the channel does."* Fig 2 :31, *"Two people reading the same report are not two frames; two
  instruments pointed at the same thing are."* Fig 2 :40, *"disqualifying for a verdict: agreement about the
  world is worthless when the agreeing frames share an evidence pool."*
- **Check:** the rationality of a retained belief and the rationality of the act of retaining it come apart
  in the SEP entry *Disagreement* (fetched 2026-11-23). Aumann's agreement theorem gives the strict case in
  which rational agents with a common prior and common knowledge of posteriors must converge; the theorem's
  hypotheses are not the question's hypotheses.
- **Refuter:** show a frame can be rational while its channel to the ground is closed and it refuses a
  discriminator that has fired; or show that two agents sharing one report are independent evidence under the
  figures. Either breaks C3.

## 3. What is explicitly not claimed

- Not "any disagreement is rational." The condition in C1-C3 is the claim.
- Not "truth is relative to a frame." The anchor does not update (Fig 2 :33).
- Not "two people with one report are two witnesses." Fig 2 :31 says the opposite.
- Not a new definition of rationality as a primitive, and not an import that moves a wall (Fig 6 :26).
- Not a choice between I_A and I_B. The framework returns a shape, not a name.
- Not `grounded`. No measurement against a discriminator was taken.
- Not that "rational" means "internally consistent." That reading was retracted in `01` §6.

## 5. What would settle it

A discriminator, not more agreement. From *The Hard Problem is About Source*: an instrument reading green
*"cannot comment on the source"*; *"It was proven wrong not by argument, but by the arrival of a better
instrument."*

1. **Discriminator test:** name and run an instrument that separates the two candidate sources for a stated
   data class. If one interpretation fails and its holder keeps it, C3 fires.
2. **Injectivity test:** if `H(source | reading) = 0` for the class, C2 fires.
3. **Peer test:** if the agents share prior, information and common knowledge of posteriors, Aumann's theorem
   requires convergence and the divergence claim fails in that stricter case.
4. **Definition test:** if "rational" is set to "ground-settled," restate the claim; do not inherit it.

## 6. What an expert can check in twenty minutes

1. Read Symbols :25-26, Fig 1 :19-20, Fig 2 :29, :31, :33, :40, Fig 10 :24, Fig 13 :128-129, Fig 12 :117,
   Fig 8 :51. All short passages in the local extract.
2. Check C2 against their own field: is there a real case where one reading has two evidence-supported
   inverses, and what instrument would separate them?
3. Check C3 against the rationality distinction they use: do they evaluate the response or the retained
   belief? Name one discriminator for their data class, or say why none is possible.
4. Send the refutation. A failure is more useful than an endorsement.
