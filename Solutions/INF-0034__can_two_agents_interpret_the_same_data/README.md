# Tether outreach: figure-grounded package

**Question:** Can two agents interpret the same data differently while both interpretations remain rational?

**Source:** `Questions/Information Theory.txt` line 43. **Section:** 4. Information, meaning, and interpretation.

**Mode:** `specified` on the substantive claims, `general` on the shape. No measurement of rationality is
claimed, so nothing here is `grounded`. Every non-structural claim carries its mode at the point of use.

**Status:** done. **Registry status:** pending.

## What is being decided, in effect terms

One reading `D` is held fixed. Agent A derives interpretation `I_A`; agent B derives `I_B`. The question asks
whether the state `I_A != I_B` with both labelled rational is a **legal state** under the figures. The two
options lead to different places. Option A, "no, difference implies error," imports an invertibility the
figures do not give and makes agreement the test of truth (Fig 2 :35). Option B, "yes, until the ground
discriminates," locates the difference in the frames (Γ, b, w, history, object slots), keeps a source
discriminator as the only closer, and refuses to count agreement between two people holding the same
report as evidence (Fig 2 :31). This package derives B with its conditions attached.

The sought-for shape is bound as an ordinary term before the run: *an answer is admissible only if it
locates the difference in the frames rather than the data, names the ground that would close it, and
keeps the verdict per frame and per slot rather than averaging it into a social vote.* Committing to that
shape before searching is the mode discipline `THE_FORMULA.md` "Direction" requires.

## What is deliberately not here

- No personal emails of named individuals.
- No ranking of people by how likely they are to agree with the verdict.
- No bulk-send plan.

Three figure-grounded reasons:

1. **Correlation disqualifies a verdict.** Fig 2 :39-40: *"Correlation has valence."*
   *"disqualifying for a verdict: agreement about the world is worthless when the agreeing frames share an
   evidence pool."* Two agents given the same data to check are a shared pool. Selecting checkers for likely
   agreement manufactures exactly the correlation that voids their agreement.
2. **A mirror cannot correct you.** Fig 2 :35: *"Alignment is triangulation, not negotiation. A mirror cannot
   correct you."* Asking for endorsement before a verdict on the residual spends the only thing an outside
   frame contributes.
3. **A verifier that can reconstruct the claim cannot verify it.** Fig 10 :42. Seat 4 sees the residual and
   the shape claim, not the framework's interiors.

Legally: scraping personal emails for unsolicited bulk outreach breaches GDPR and CAN-SPAM style rules in
many jurisdictions. Public opt-in channels avoid that failure as well as the structural one.

## Suggested next step

pre-registered refuter. The verdict on stage 1 is recorded before any theory is shown, and later agreement is
never pooled as corroboration (Fig 2 :40). A failure is more useful than an endorsement.
