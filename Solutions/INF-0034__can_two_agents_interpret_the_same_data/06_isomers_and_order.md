# Isomers, metamerism and equivocation: a supporting derivation for INF-0034

**Mode: `specified` on the structural claims, `general` on the definition of the analogues.** This is a
simulated derivation checked against the figures, not a ground reading (see `05_what_shape_means.md` §6).

## RESIDUAL

The main audit establishes that the difference between I_A and I_B lives in the frames, not in the reading D.
Two further questions are owed: (1) what **structure** does that difference have, so that we can say why it is
not merely a duplicate or an error? (2) what **order** must the inquiry follow, so that a social verdict is
not mistaken for a ground verdict? Both are answered with analogues, each with a falsifier.

## DOMAIN CENSUS (supporting)

Atoms held: the six substrate terms, the seven bonds, negation, frame, residual, anchor, instrument, slot.
Bonds used here: `+` conjunction and `≡` identity (both symmetric), `→` sequence and `⋛` comparison (both
ordered). No new primitive is introduced. The three analogues below are bindings onto held atoms, not imports
that move the wall (Fig 6 :26: *"The world does not gain primitives; a frame gains access."*).

## DERIVATION

### Q1 - Are the two interpretations isomers? (structure, figure-side)

Fig 12 :117, verbatim:

> **"Same atoms, different bond, different substance. The difference is invisible in the formula and decisive in the behaviour."**

The reading D is the flat formula: it records the ingredients, not the arrangement. I_A and I_B are molecules
over those ingredients. Under different bonds they are different substances, and nothing in D tells you which
is which. Fig 12 :94-96: *"The arrangement is the substance. Two entries with an identical ingredient set are
not variants of one thing. They are different things whose notation cannot tell them apart, and the
difference is carried entirely by the bond."*

This is a structural reason why "they are the same data, so one must be a mistake" fails. Same data, different
bond, different substance is a legal state in the figure's own composition theory. It is also a limit:
isomerism names the difference, not the choice. Fig 12 :99-101: *"A bond costs what it could have been, and
nothing more... A bond that costs nothing is a bond that carries nothing."* Choosing between isomers needs a
cost-bearing discriminator, not another look at the formula.

**Falsifier Q1:** show that I_A and I_B share every atom and the same bond, so they are one term under a
symmetric bond, not two substances. Then the isomer analogue fails and the difference reduces to a
spelling difference. Triangulated for the definition (Fig 12 :117 and :94-96); unmeasured for any particular
pair of interpretations.

### Q2 - Metamerism as the world-side instance (structure, low overlap)

Wikipedia, Metamerism (color) (world source, read 2026-11-23):

> *"a perceived matching of colors with different (nonmatching) spectral power distributions"*

and the observer case: two spectrally dissimilar lights *"may produce a color match for one observer but fail
to match when viewed by a second observer."*

Binding onto held atoms: the tristimulus reading is D, the spectral power distributions are the candidate
sources, and the cone sensitivities are the frames Γ_A and Γ_B. One reading, many sources, and a frame term
in the inverse. This is the same structure as Q1 arrived at from vision science, so it is genuine
corroboration at low overlap, not a restatement of the figure. The discrepancy to record: in colorimetry the
instrument is a cone response and the discriminator is a spectrometer; for arbitrary data there is no
guaranteed spectrometer. Fig 6 :34: *"What is unavailable is a sensor for something that has never
registered anywhere, at any resolution."* So the analogue is a licence for "multiple rational inverses may
exist", not a promise that a discriminator can always be built.

**Falsifier Q2:** exhibit a data class whose source-to-reading map is injective. Then the metamerism analogue
does not apply and refuter 3 in `01` fires.

### Q3 - Equivocation as the formal condition (structure, mathematics)

Wikipedia, Information theory (world source, read 2026-11-23) gives the conditional entropy identity:

> `H(X|Y) = H(X,Y) - H(Y)`

Read X as the source, Y as the reading. `H(X|Y)` is what remains unknown about the source after the reading.
This is the conditional form of the Symbols table definition of R (local): *"it is always a slice: what
cannot be perceived yet is residual and simply cannot be read."*

- If `H(X|Y) > 0`, more than one source remains possible. Multiple rational inverses are compatible with the
  reading. This is F2 in a different notation.
- If `H(X|Y) = 0`, the reading determines the source. Difference requires an error, and the answer collapses
  to the naive "no".

This is not a figure line and is not used as a verdict. It is a checkable reformulation of the condition,
which is what WATSON calls a prediction rather than an authority. The Shannon framing supports the same
point: *"The fundamental problem of communication is that of reproducing at one point, either exactly or
approximately, a message selected at another point"* - reproduction of a message, not recovery of a meaning.

**Falsifier Q3:** a data class with `H(X|Y)=0` for which two rational interpretations are still defended.
That would show the entropy reformulation is the wrong instrument for this residual, and it would send the
derivation back to the census rather than flip the figure verdict.

### Q4 - What order does the inquiry follow? (order, dependency)

Fig 3 :20: *"The chain is ordered by dependency, not by importance."* The chain for INF-0034 is:

```
1 describe the reading and its live slots (Fig 1 :19)
2 unbundle the union: underdetermination vs rationality under a ground (Fig 9 :54)
3 describe the residual in effect terms before choosing a frame (Fig 9 :27-28)
4 name or rule out a source-discriminating instrument (Fig 6 :26, :34)
5 only then read agreement or disagreement between the agents (Fig 2 :31, :40)
```

Fig 9 :27-28, verbatim:

> **"describe it before you go looking, or any frame you pick will seem to fit"**

Step 5 is last on purpose. Asking "who do most people agree with?" before step 4 substitutes a convention for
a ground. Fig 10 :47: *"a convention that decides an outcome has stopped being one."* Fig 3 :19:
*"Optimising a link that already works cannot rescue one that does not."* More checkers at step 5 cannot
rescue a missing instrument at step 4.

**Falsifier Q4:** a case where a source-discriminating instrument exists but the correct order is to poll the
agents first. That would break the dependency claim and require a re-census of D3 and D4.

## CONCLUSION

The difference between two rational interpretations has the structure of an **isomer** on the figure side and
a **metamer** on the vision-science side: same ingredients or same reading, different arrangement or source,
both legal until an instrument discriminates. The formal condition is a positive conditional entropy of the
source given the reading, or, in the figures' vocabulary, a live per-slot residual that the reading alone
cannot close. The order is fixed by dependency: describe the residual, name the discriminator, then read the
social state. Each analogue has a written falsifier; none was smoothed over.
