## Independent domain census (written before reading the primary's conclusions)

I started from the residual as stated in the README, in effect terms: one reading D, two agents, two
interpretations I_A != I_B, both called rational. I did not look at the primary's census until this section was
drafted.

- **R (arity / symmetry / scale).** Arity three: one reading plus two frames, so a two-body "who is right"
  lever cannot clear it. Symmetry: the source-to-reading projection is many-to-one, which makes "consistent
  with the same reading" an equivalence on candidate sources. Scale: first order inside a frame (perceive,
  route, mint, settle), second order at the contact between frames (agreement, corroboration, drift). I
  flagged immediately that two scales with different currencies must not be averaged (Fig 1 :19 per-slot
  residual; Fig 9 :60 filters versus witnesses).
- **Domain 1, a single frame's derivation.** Actors: Γ, b, w, bonds, negation, slots. Currency: residual per
  slot and description length. Anchor: the ground, reached only through settlement. Question: partition,
  which compositions are legal.
- **Domain 2, the shared reading.** Actors: the observation, the projection, the equivalence class of
  metamers. Currency: bits. Anchor: none internal. I marked this explicitly: a reading is not an anchor,
  because it does not change its answer when a frame pushes on it with an instrument it does not contain.
- **Domain 3, the discriminator.** Actors: instrument, measurement. Currency: what pays. Anchor: the ground,
  which does not update. This is the only domain that can close the question.
- **Domain 4, the social state.** Actors: A and B. Currency: agreement and pool overlap. Anchor: the ground,
  never agreement. Since both hold the same reading, independence here is near zero.

Union rather than one question: "data underdetermination" and "rationality under a ground" are two
conditions, and a lever that clears the first does not clear the second (Fig 9 :54, :57). Dependency order:
describe the reading, name the discriminator, then read the social state (Fig 3 :20).

My census matches the primary's four domains and its union reading. Where the primary derived from Symbols
weighting and Fig 1 slot indexing, I derived from the arity analysis and the anchor test in domain 2. Low

## Round trip gap R_T

The Symbols table defines R_T as `gap(x, (T_E ∘ T_A)(x))`, the gap between what was sent up and what came back
down. Fig 4 :30 requires it to be measured rather than assumed. I ran the round trip on the condensation the
folder actually uses.

1. **Sent up (x):** the verbatim figure set, chiefly Symbols :25-26 (history and w), Fig 13 :128-129
   (lineage), Fig 2 :31, :33, :40 (independence, anchor, correlation valence), Fig 10 :24 (channel).
2. **Abstraction (T_A):** the shorthand in `07` and `09`: one reading can have many rational inverses; the
   difference is in the frames; the agents' agreement is not evidence; the licence ends at a discriminator.
3. **Concretisation (T_E):** a concrete claim in interpretation research, for example "this disagreement
   between two analysts over one dataset is rational because views differ."

**R_T is nonzero.** Three constraints held by the shorthand do not survive the trip back down:

- the **anchor condition**: a concrete claim of the form "any difference is rational" drops Fig 2 :33 and
  Fig 10 :24, and becomes a licence for refusing a discriminator that has fired;
- the **independence condition**: a concrete claim that two analysts corroborate each other drops Fig 2 :31
  and :40, because they share the dataset;
- the **per-slot indexing**: a concrete claim that the global residual is near zero drops Fig 1 :19 and :22
  and hides the live local residual that distinguishes the two frames.

Conversely, one thing survives cleanly: a reading does not determine its source, so multiple inverses are
legal until a discriminator arrives. That is the residue worth sending. R_T was **not** measured numerically,
because no ground reading exists; it is recorded qualitatively as the enumerated set of dropped constraints.
Treating the formula as identical to the figure set would set R_T to zero and would be the dead-reckoning
error Fig 4 :30 names. The honest form is a lossy condensation with a recorded gap.
