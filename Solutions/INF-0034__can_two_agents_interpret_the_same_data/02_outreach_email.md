# Outreach draft: check the residual first, then decide whether the theory is worth reading

**Use:** one-to-one, or as a cover letter attached to a submission through the public venues in
`03_public_contact_points.csv`. **Not** for bulk send.

**Hard precondition:** do not send until a stage-1 verdict has been recorded. The recipient's answer to
"what would count as a discriminator here?" must exist before any theory is shown. If stage 1 cannot be
answered, the offer stops there (Fig 10 :52: *"Bring a characterised residual, never the source."*).

---

**Subject:** One question about two readings of the same data, and a refuter written in advance

> Hi [name],
>
> I have one narrow question in interpretation and rationality research and would value a check on it. I am
> not asking you to agree with anything, and I am not asking you to trust a framework. If the residual does
> not survive, there is nothing else to look at.
>
> **The residual (`specified`, not `grounded`).** Fix one reading D. Agent A returns interpretation I_A;
> agent B returns I_B, with I_A different from I_B. Both are called rational. Is that a legal state, or does
> difference imply an error? The question matters because a reading is a projection, not a source: the same
> tristimulus value can come from different spectra, and the same evidence can underdetermine more than one
> theory. The shape claim I want checked is: *the difference lives in the frames (history, weighting, live
> slot, prior terms), not in the data, and it remains a licence for "both rational" only until a
> source-discriminating ground is applied.* That is a shape, not a measurement.
>
> **What I am not claiming.** I am not claiming that any disagreement is rational, that grounding is
> subjective, or that two people holding one report are two independent witnesses. Shared data is one
> evidence pool. Whether A and B agree or disagree carries no verdict weight. If the task is negotiating a
> shared meaning, their agreement may be the product; if the task is interpreting data, it is not evidence
> either way.
>
> **Refuters, written before this run:**
> 1. A discriminator exists for this data class, both interpretations are run against it, one fails, and its
>    holder keeps it unchanged. Then "both rational" fails at the ground.
> 2. "Same data" is tightened to "same frame, same prior, same information, posteriors common knowledge."
>    Then rational agents converge; difference implies an error.
> 3. The map from source to reading is injective for this data class. Then the reading determines one
>    interpretation, and divergence is error.
> 4. "Rational" is defined as "settled by the ground" rather than "loop-derived and refusable." Then the
>    answer must be restated, not inherited.
>
> A one-line "this fails because..." is more useful to me than agreement. If you would rather not engage,
> no reply is needed.
>
> **Stage 2, only if you are curious and only after the verdict above is recorded:** the sources are below.
> The figures, not my summary of them, are the authority.
> - Verbatim figure text with line numbers (local framework extract):
>   `C:\Users\Admin\Desktop\Project Pathfinder\FRAMEWORK\reference\03-FIGURES_TEXT.md`
> - Canonical figures README: https://github.com/IsaiahN/tether/blob/general-agent/figures/README.md
> - Worked architecture and the source-discrimination essay:

>
> **If you want to reuse the method:** the formula is in the figures link. Run it on a data class you know
> well and can check against a ground. Write down what would refute your result *before* you run it, and
> state the mode of what you get. The framework's own warning is that a loop cannot check the direction of
> its own question, so a report of failure is more useful to me than a report of success.
>

## Why this version is ordered this way

| the move | figure-grounded reason |
|---|---|
| Residual and refuter before theory | Fig 10 :52 *"Bring a characterised residual, never the source."* Seat 4 sees the residual and shape, not the interiors. |
| No request for agreement or trust | Fig 2 :35 *"Alignment is triangulation, not negotiation. A mirror cannot correct you."* |
| Verdict recorded before stage 2 | Fig 2 :40 agreement about the world is worthless when frames share an evidence pool; later agreement is never pooled as corroboration. |
| Pre-registered refuter | Fig 9 :60 *"Use filters for the budget and witnesses for the verdict."* A falsifier written before the run is a design instrument. |
| Mode on every claim | THE_FORMULA.md mode rule, stated locally: `general · specified · grounded`. |
| Shared data is not two witnesses | Fig 2 :31 *"Two people reading the same report are not two frames."* |
| Links after the verdict | Fig 4 :22 *"Only methods cross up. Only head starts come down."* Sending the architecture first sends a recording, not a generator. |
| Offer the formula as a method | Fig 10 :42 *"A verifier that can reconstruct the claim cannot verify it."* A generator can be given away without giving away an answer. |
| Ask for a discriminator, not an endorsement | Fig 10 :16 *"the convention does not fix the bug; it makes the bug statable."* The useful reply names an instrument, not a preference. |
| No bulk send, no receptiveness rank | Fig 2 :39 *"Correlation has valence."* Selecting for likely agreement manufactures the disqualifying correlation. |
