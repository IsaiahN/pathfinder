## The wider literature, and what each predicts here

- **Vision science (metamerism).** One tristimulus reading is compatible with many spectral power
  distributions; a spectrometer discriminates sources that the eye cannot. Predicts that two rational
  inverses of one reading exist until a better instrument arrives. **Corroborates** F2 and F3. The
  "observer metamerism" case is the closest analogue of two agents: same physical samples, different
  matches. It is not a perfect analogue, because the agents differ in cone ratios; the question allows a
  difference in Γ or w instead. Recorded as a structural parallel, not an identity.
- **Philosophy of science (underdetermination).** Evidence available at a time may be insufficient to
  determine which belief to hold; a rival equally well supported by the standards of evidence blocks the
  inference from data to one theory. Predicts that both interpretations can be evidence-supported.
  **Corroborates** F2. The Bell-inequality limitation noted in that entry is an important bound: some
  underdetermination is empirical and can be closed by experiment, exactly the F3 discriminator condition.
- **Epistemology (SEP, disagreement).** The rationality of a retained belief and the rationality of the act
  of retaining it can come apart; peerhood depends on evidence, ability, background and bias. Predicts that
  "both rational" must be indexed to what is being evaluated (loop-derived response vs ground-settled
  belief). **Corroborates** F3. The entry's Equal Weight view also predicts that discovering a genuine peer's
  disagreement gives a reason to lower confidence, which is the D3 update rule stated in secular terms.
- **Game theory (Aumann).** With a common prior and common knowledge of posteriors, rational Bayesians cannot
  disagree. Predicts that rational difference requires differing priors, histories, information, or a failure
  of common knowledge. Initially **conflicts** with a naive reading of F1; re-census (01 F8) shows the
  theorem's hypotheses are stricter than the question's, so it corroborates the condition rather than
  flipping the verdict.
- **Information theory (Shannon).** Communication is reproduction of a selected message, not recovery of a
  meaning; conditional entropy measures what remains unknown about the source after the reading. Predicts a
  positive residual `H(source | reading)` as the formal condition for multiple rational inverses.
  **Corroborates** F2. If that quantity is zero for a data class, refuter 3 in `01` fires.
- **Framework essays (high overlap).** The source-discrimination essay supplies the green-reading case and
  the spectrometer; the library essay supplies "shape"; the tacit-knowledge essay supplies
  describe-the-effect-first. All are by the framework author, so their agreement with the figures is
  expected and carries little independent weight. Declared, not hidden.

No low-overlap world source contradicts the figure verdict after re-census. The one apparent conflict
(Aumann) was routed back to the domain census and resolved there. Agreement among the framework essays is
one evidence pool, not corroboration (Fig 2 :40).

## Distribution: offer, do not push

- **Fig 10 :52** - *"Bring a characterised residual, never the source."* Lead with the residual and its
  refuter; the architecture comes only after a stage-1 verdict is on record.
- **Fig 4 :22** - *"Only methods cross up. Only head starts come down."* A derivation sent before a residual
  is described is a recording, not a generator.
- **Fig 9 :27-28** - *"describe it before you go looking, or any frame you pick will seem to fit."* Ask what
  the recipient would expect to see if the shape claim were true, before giving them the material.
- **Fig 2 :35** - *"Alignment is triangulation, not negotiation. A mirror cannot correct you."* Do not ask
  for agreement. Ask for a discriminator.
- **Fig 2 :39-40** - *"Correlation has valence."* Selecting recipients for likely agreement produces a
  convention, not corroboration.
- **Fig 10 :42** - *"A verifier that can reconstruct the claim cannot verify it."* A recipient who has
  absorbed the whole framework before stage 1 has joined the frame being checked.

**Recommended order in a reply:** (1) the residual, the shape claim and the refuters; (2) ask what they would
count as a source-discriminating instrument for their data class, and record the answer; (3) only then the
links, with the note that the figures, not the summary, are the authority. Later agreement is never pooled as
corroboration.

## The two-stage offer

1. **Stage 1.** Validate the residual against the ground. Deliverable: a named discriminator or a named
   reason none is possible. Verdict recorded before theory is shown.
2. **Stage 2, opt-in.** If the recipient is curious and the stage-1 verdict exists, offer the figure links
   and the essays. Their later agreement is a conversation, not a witness.
3. **Stage 3, opt-in.** Offer the formula as a method to reuse. Each user states their own mode and writes
   their own refuter before running it, because the framework cannot check its own direction.

**Hard precondition:** stages 2 and 3 depend on stage 1 producing a real answer. "I agree" is not a stage-1
answer.
