## QUESTION

Decide, in effect terms, what the Tether says when one reading D is given to two agents and they return
I_A != I_B with both called rational. Option A: difference implies an error, so at most one is rational.
Option B: difference is a legal state in the frames and both remain rational until a source-discriminating
ground fires. What each leads to: A imports an invertibility the figures do not give and makes agreement a
test of truth (Fig 2 :35); B locates the difference in history, w, live slot and Γ, keeps a discriminator as
the only closer, and refuses to count two people sharing one report as two witnesses (Fig 2 :31). The sought
shape was bound as an ordinary term before the run: an answer is admissible only if it locates the difference
in frames rather than data, names the ground that would close it, and keeps the verdict per frame and per slot.

## DOMAIN CENSUS

R by arity, symmetry and scale, then one line per domain with actors / currency / anchor, partition or union,
and order by dependency.

- **R (the residual).** The intuition that same data plus rationality should give one answer, set against the
  legal state of two different interpretations. Arity: three relata (one reading, two frames). Symmetry: the
  source-to-reading map is many-to-one, so "consistent with the same reading" is an equivalence on sources.
  Scale: first order inside one frame, second order at the contact between frames. Fig 9 :54 says to unbundle
  a union before searching; the union is (U1) data underdetermination and (U2) rationality under a ground.
  A lever clearing U1 leaves U2 standing.
- **Domain 1, one frame's derivation.** Actors: Γ, b, w, the seven bonds, negation, object slots. Currency:
  residual per slot and description length. Anchor: the ground, reached only through settlement. Question:
  partition (which compositions are legal). Order: after D2, before D4.
- **Domain 2, the shared reading.** Actors: the observation, the projection from source to reading, the
  equivalence class of metamers. Currency: bits the reading can carry. Anchor: none internal, a reading is
  not an anchor. Question: union with D1. Order: first.
- **Domain 3, the ground / discriminator.** Actors: instrument, measurement, experiment. Currency: what
  pays. Anchor: the ground itself, which does not update. Question: settles D1 and D2. Order: before D4.
- **Domain 4, the verification frame.** Actors: A and B, their agreement or disagreement. Currency:
  agreement and evidence-pool overlap. Anchor: the ground, never agreement. Question: union with D1 and D3.
  Order: last.

Dependency order (Fig 3 :20): describe the reading before choosing a frame; settle a frame before reading
social agreement; name or rule out a discriminator before polling the agents. Strain test: Fig 2 :31 calls two
people reading one report "not two frames", while the Symbols table defines a frame as any bounded system
with a library. Re-census: the first "frame" is a unit of evidence independence for agreement, the second is
a bounded system with a library. Different quantities, so no strain after re-census. Both readings are kept
and labelled.

## VERDICT (from the figures)

Yes, conditionally. Two agents can interpret one reading differently while both remain rational when the
reading is a lossy projection that does not determine its source and the frames differ in history, w, live slot
or Γ (D1, D2). The difference is in the frames, not the data; the agents' agreement or disagreement is not
evidence, because sharing the data makes them one pool (D4). The licence ends when a source-discriminating
ground is applied: a frame that refuses the discriminator has a closed channel, not a second rational view (D3).
This is `general` on the shape and `specified` on the conditions, ungrounded, candidate. No new term is
minted, no interpretation is named, and no social average is admitted as a verdict (Fig 9 :60).

- **cost if wrong:** bounded and locatable. The packet states mode, three checkable sub-claims and five
  refuters before the conclusion, so an error is findable rather than invisible. The real avoidable cost is
  treating agreement or disagreement between two agents as a verdict, which Fig 2 :42 prices: "Buy
  independence in proportion to the cost of being wrong." Leading with the residual and its refuter avoids
  it.
- **undoable:** yes. The conditions are removable without touching the shape: tighten "same data" to "same
  frame, same prior, common knowledge" and refuter 2 fires; exhibit an injective map and refuter 3 fires;
  swap the definition of rational and refuter 4 fires. A sent claim can be superseded by the corrected packet,
  and no primitive has to be withdrawn.
- **reversed by:** a ground reading, not agreement. A source-discriminating instrument for a named data class,
  run against both interpretations, would close the question; a proof that `H(source | reading) = 0` for the
  class would collapse it to the naive "no"; a figure re-census that makes two agents holding one reading a
  single frame would void D4. Until one of these fires, the verdict stands as candidate.
