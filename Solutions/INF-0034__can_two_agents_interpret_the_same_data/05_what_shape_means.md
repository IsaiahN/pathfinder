## 1. A shape is a constraint, not a filler

The canonical figures README (local) gives the limit: the framework returns *"what a missing thing would have
to look like (a shape, not a name)."* Fig 9 :32: *"you get a kind of thing, never the answer itself."* Fig 13
(the local extract, near the end of the figure): *"It can say the answer is out of reach with the library as it
stands... It cannot say which primitive is missing, because naming it would mean already holding it."*

From *The Library of Babel*: *"You can cut your search short if the set of books returned is small and
descriptive enough that it provides a general shape of the answer that satisfies your question."*

So a **shape** is the form of an answer you can state before you can settle it. It constrains; it does not
fill. For INF-0034 the shape is: *any admissible answer must locate the difference in the frames, not in the
data; must name the ground that would close it; and must keep the verdict per frame and per slot rather than
averaging it into a social vote.* That is a constraint on every candidate answer. It is not an algorithm for
"rational," not a threshold for divergence, and not a name for the correct interpretation.

## 2. How the shape is obtained

- *Library of Babel*: use the vocabulary already closest to the thing, look for recurring new terms that
  overlap with it, recurse. *"With each new term gained, you narrow the space and deepen your vocabulary."*
- Fig 6 :30: *"The description travels. Fails by being sharpened into terms only we can read. Cast it in
  effect terms."* Fig 9 :30-31: *"your words are borrowed too, so describe the effect rather than the cause."*
  Here the effect is: one reading, two outputs, both derived. The cause (which frame is right) is exactly
  what is not yet given.
- *Decoding Tacit Knowledge*: *"When contact isn't possible, the best you can send is a plain description of
  what you'd expect to see, because anyone can check that against the world."* The checkable expectation
  here is a discriminator: name an instrument that would separate the two inverses.
- *Library of Babel* on stopping: the answer is reached when *"the finish line will also be the starting
  place"*, the paradox being that the answer is *"composed... through the process of locating it."* For this
  question the starting place is the unbundled residual (data underdetermination versus rationality under a
  ground), and the finish is the same distinction with a discriminator named.

## 3. What licenses a name (and why none is licensed here)

Two things, both outside the shape:

1. **The ground settles it.** `THE_FORMULA.md` step 5 (local): *"the ground settles it. Verified only by what
   pays. A gate is not the ground."* The README: the framework *"stops at the limit of what the available
   instruments can distinguish, and records the lineage so the stop is honest."*
2. **A provenanced import from a frame whose closure differs** (Fig 6 :23, Fig 8 :50-52), with a source gate
   and a shadow test.

Neither is present. There is no measurement of the two interpretations against a discriminating instrument,
and no imported primitive that would decide between them. So the framework cannot name the correct
interpretation, and it cannot name a numeric threshold for "rational." It returns the shape.

*They're Scanning Books* sharpens the second condition: *"The real import is not a new term in the existing
space. It is a new dimension of the space."* and *"A minimum viable set of six terms is a human's careful
guess at reality's compression axes."* Saying "rational" is a label applied to a frame's conduct is such a
guess, made visible by being written down. It is not a discovery of nature's own list.

## 4. The source error, and why it is the specific forbidden move here

*The Hard Problem is About Source* gives the mechanism. An instrument reads green and *"cannot comment on
the source"*; the source could be one green light, yellow plus blue, RGB, CMYK, or a scheme not yet
discovered. *"If several paths create the same behavior, then behavior is a projection with lossy
information."* And: *"An instrument that can't distinguish these cases demonstrates its own inadequacy."*

Applied to INF-0034: the shared data D is the reading; I_A and I_B are named sources read off that one
projection. Naming one as "the rational interpretation" from the reading alone is the metamerism error. The
instrument (the data) does not discriminate among its sources. What is owed is a better instrument, not a
verdict. The essay's own resolution is the figure's: *"It was proven wrong not by argument, but by the
arrival of a better instrument."*

The low-overlap world source (Wikipedia, Metamerism (color)) makes the same point physically: *"a perceived
matching of colors with different (nonmatching) spectral power distributions"*, with observer metamerism
where two dissimilar lights match for one observer and fail for another. This is why the shape is a
constraint on instruments, not a definition of rationality.

*The Recursive Self-Improvement Trap*: *"the output is only as good as the validator. The agent, or the tool,
or the frame, or the theory, cannot validate itself."* So this audit cannot validate its own verdict. That is
why the verdict carries a pre-registered refuter and a named discriminator question instead of a claim to
have settled the matter.

## 5. Q: does "shape, not a name" forbid saying "both can be rational"? CORRECTION TO MY OWN FIRST READING

First reading: "shape, not a name" means the framework cannot say anything about rationality at all.
**Corrected.** The README's phrase governs a *missing* primitive, one not held in Γ. "Frame", "residual",
"anchor" and "instrument" are all already in the library (Symbols table). So the framework can state
constraints over them. What it cannot do is name which of I_A or I_B is the correct interpretation, or supply
a numeric cutoff for rational. "Both can remain rational under these conditions" is a binding over held
terms, not the invention of a new one.

The correction does not weaken the metamerism point of §4. It relocates it: the error is naming a *source*
from a reading, not naming held atoms. Both statements are kept so neither is quietly lost. This matches the
retraction pattern in the exemplar's `05` §5b.

## 6. Two consequences for outreach

**Prestige is not action.** From *The Recursive Self-Improvement Trap*: *"Prestige must never be allowed to
validate an action directly... Prestige can fund an action. It can give an action a budget to be tried. But
it cannot settle an action. Only the ground can settle it. The Ground Maintainer must be blind to prestige."*
Gathering respected names to endorse the shape is the named collapse. The legitimate ask is a budget to try
it, plus a discriminator someone can actually run.

**My own audit is a simulation, not a ground reading.** Same essay: *"LLMs can quickly simulate an
approximate answer/output... However, the truly testable output would need to be derived from the real
architecture or working the formula yourself."* LLM output is *"simulacra"*. So `01` is a simulated
derivation checked for internal consistency against the figures. It is not a test of the claim and must be
labelled as such. It cannot settle anything it reports on.

## 7. The shape in one line

> One reading, many sources, difference located in the frames; the answer stops where a source-discriminating
> instrument begins, and no social average of the two agents is allowed to stand in for that instrument.
