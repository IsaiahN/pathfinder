## 1. A shape is a constraint you can state before you can name the value

The figures README states the limit: the framework returns "what a missing thing would have to look like (a shape, not a name)." Fig 9 :32: "you get a kind of thing, never the answer itself." Fig 13 near :134: "So the system can report that it lacks something rather than producing an answer anyway. Unreachable is a verdict here, not a silence,"

From *The Library of Babel* (opened): "you can cut your search short if the set of books returned is small and descriptive enough that it provides a general shape of the answer that satisfies your question." And: "With each new term gained, you narrow the space by definition and deepen your vocabulary."

So a **shape** is the narrowed form of an answer that constrains without filling. For this question the shape is: a union of overlapping conditions, indexed per reach and per wetland and per season, ordered connection then capture then depletion then ecological threshold, with adequacy decided at a comparison threshold, and with a declared gap where a number would go. "More pumping plus less rain means less river flow" is not a shape; it is a trend line that the regional sign flips in the world sources already contradict.

## 2. How a shape is obtained

- *Library of Babel* (opened): use the vocabulary you already have that is closest to the thing, look for recurring new terms that overlap or correlate with it, recurse. The essay calls this bootstrapping convergence by proximity.
- Fig 6 :30: "The description travels. Fails by being sharpened into terms only we can read. Cast it in effect terms." Fig 9 :30 and :31: "your words are borrowed too, so describe the effect rather than the cause."
- *Decoding Tacit Knowledge* (carried, not re-opened): "When contact isn't possible, the best you can send is a plain description of what you'd expect to see, because anyone can check that against the world."
- *Library of Babel* on the bundle case (opened): "Perhaps your problem was a bundle or conflation of several problems masquerading as one? You will need to search all of those problems individually to find their answers and then combine them in a satisfactory way." This is the essay's own warrant for the union reading in `01` F1.
- *Library of Babel* on stopping (opened): "the finish line will also be the starting place." The paradox is that by the time the exact search terms are found, the answer has been composed in the locating.

## 3. What licenses a name or a number

Only two things, both outside the shape itself:

1. **The ground settles it.** Formula step 5: "the ground settles it. Verified only by what pays. A gate is not the ground." The README: the framework "stops at the limit of what the available instruments can distinguish, and records the lineage so the stop is honest."
2. **A provenanced import from a frame whose closure differs** (Fig 6 :23, Fig 8 :50 to :52), with a source gate and a shadow test.

*They're Scanning Books When They Should Be Studying Nature* (carried, not re-opened): "The real import is not a new term in the existing space. It is a new dimension of the space." Also: "Declaring the primitives does not make them neutral. It makes the choice visible."

In this solution the world supplies the numbers that the figures do not: the roughly 20% capture and 16% storage fractions, the 42 to 79% watershed figure, the 5 m wetland depth. Each is a binding from a world frame, recorded with author, venue and year in `10_source_ledger.csv`. None is promoted to a figure verdict.

## 4. Source discrimination is the reason for the gap

*The Hard Problem is About Source* (opened) gives the mechanism. Metamerism: an instrument reads green and "is speaking only about what it measured, it cannot comment on the source." The reading does not determine the source: one green light, yellow plus blue, RGB, CMYK, or a scheme not yet discovered. "If several paths create the same behavior, then behavior is a projection with lossy information." The essay's conclusion: "The answer is unreached until a better instrument exists." It was the spectrometer, not an argument, that resolved the case.

Applied here: a low-flow reading at a gauge cannot say pumping, rainfall, or both. The 2022 JAWRA excerpt makes the same operational point: depletion "cannot be directly measured and is challenging to estimate because pumping impacts are masked by streamflow variability due to other factors." What is owed is a discriminating instrument (paired head, flow, recharge and abstraction records), not a name assigned to a reading.

## 5. Answered question: can the shape say which reach fails?

**No.** The framework returns a shape. Which reach crosses its threshold under which rainfall sequence is a ground question owned by gauges, wells, inundation maps and coupled models. A specific reach is licensed only by a ground reading for that reach, or by a provenanced import with a source gate. Until one of those, naming it is the searcher's own contribution and must be recorded as such.

## 6. Correction to my own "shape, not a name" reading

I overstated the rule in an earlier draft of `01` F5. The README's phrase is "what a **missing** thing would have to look like (a shape, not a name)." Its subject is a primitive not held in Γ. The 5 m wetland depth, the capture fractions and the environmental flow threshold are not missing primitives; they are measured quantities from a world frame. Citing them is a provenanced binding, not the invention of a seventh primitive and not a figure violation.

What remains wrong if one treats those numbers as figure outputs is the metamerism error of section 4: a source named from a reading where several sources could give the same reading. Both the narrow rule and the correction are recorded here so neither is quietly lost. Triangulated (README plus Fig 9 :32).

## 7. Prestige is not action, and this audit is a simulation

*The Recursive Self-Improvement Trap* (opened): "Prestige must never be allowed to validate an action directly." And: "Prestige can fund an action. It can give an action a budget to be tried. But it cannot settle an action. Only the ground can settle it. The Ground Maintainer must be blind to prestige." A list of well-known hydrologists assembled to agree with the shape is the named collapse, not corroboration. The legitimate ask is to give the shape a budget to be tried, and let the ground settle it.

Same essay: "the output is only as good as the validator. The agent, or the tool, or the frame, or the theory, cannot validate itself." And: "LLMs can quickly simulate an approximate answer/output with the formula found in the architecture post... However, the truly testable output would need to be derived from the real architecture or working the formula yourself."

So `01`, `06`, `07`, `08` and `09` are simulated derivations checked for internal consistency against the figures. They are **not** tests of the question and must be labelled as such. They cannot settle anything they report on.

## 8. Consequence for this folder

Lead with the shape and the refuters. Withhold the world numbers until stage 2. Never say "the Tether shows that basin X will lose Y per cent of its low flow." Say "the Tether constrains what any answer to that question must look like, and here is what would refute the constraint."
