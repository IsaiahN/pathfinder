## Sources read through public excerpts, with reduced weight

- **Zipper et al. 2022, *Journal of the American Water Resources Association***, "Quantifying Streamflow Depletion from Groundwater Pumping." Direct fetch returned 403. The public indexed excerpt was read and states: "streamflow depletion cannot be measured directly and is challenging to estimate because pumping impacts are masked by streamflow variability due to other factors"; and depletion "can impact aquatic ecosystems through changes in the availability and temperature of surface water." Recorded as reduced weight in `10_source_ledger.csv`.
- **de Graaf et al. 2019, *Environmental Research Letters***, "Non-renewable groundwater use and groundwater depletion: a review." Read via the public indexed excerpt. Gives the connected versus disconnected losing stream classification and the sequence of capture. One author stream with the 2019 Nature and 2024 ERL papers, so not an independent witness.
- **Synergistic effects of precipitation and groundwater extraction on freshwater wetland inundation**, NSF PAR public repository, 2024. The PDF fetch returned "unsupported file content type." The indexed abstract and findings excerpt were read: "low rainfall, combined with wetland water storage losses, created persistent drought conditions exacerbated by management decision-making that included over-extraction of groundwater." Reduced weight.
- **Global groundwater dependent wetland dynamics**, *Journal of Hydrology* region, 2026. Read via the public indexed abstract: global GDW area declined by about 3 to 4% over 1979 to 2019, with losses in intensive abstraction regions and increases where climate-induced recharge rose. Reduced weight.
- **HESS 2025 global GDE mapping**, article PDF URL fetched as a search result but PDF text not parsed locally. The abstract excerpt was read: wetland GDE defined by saturated area fraction above 50% and groundwater table depth below 5 m. Reduced weight.
- **Victoria wetlands climate vulnerability assessment**, 2013. Read via public excerpt: shallow wetlands depending on local groundwater flow systems are "at most risk" and may "become disconnected from the groundwater system." Reduced weight.

## Distribution rules

Figure-grounded reasons for how this material is offered:

- **Fig 4 :22 to :25:** "Only methods cross up. Only head starts come down." Sending the full architecture before someone has described a residual sends a recording, not a generator. Lead with the shape and the six refuters; offer the links afterwards.
- **Fig 10 :42:** "A verifier that can reconstruct the claim cannot verify it." An outside reader who has absorbed the framework before giving a verdict has joined the frame being checked.
- **Fig 9 :27 and :28:** "describe it before you go looking, or any frame you pick will seem to fit." Ask what the reader would expect to see if the shape were true before giving them the sources.
- **Fig 2 :35:** "Alignment is triangulation, not negotiation. A mirror cannot correct you." A mail-out selected for likely agreement produces a convention, not corroboration.
- **Fig 2 :42:** "Buy independence in proportion to the cost of being wrong." Where getting the effect wrong misdirects river and wetland management, the cost of an independent check is worth paying.
- **Fig 2 :40:** "disqualifying for a verdict: agreement about the world is worthless when the agreeing frames share an evidence pool." A later agreement is never pooled as a second witness.

**Recommended order in a reply:** (1) the shape claim and the six refuters; (2) ask what the reader would count as a failure and record the answer; (3) only then the three links, with the note that the figures, not this summary, are the authority; (4) the formula as a reusable method, if asked.

## The two-stage offer

1. **Stage 1 before stage 2.** Fig 10 :52: "Bring a characterised residual, never the source." The recipient checks the shape without holding the framework's interior.
2. **Stage 2 is opt-in by construction.** Seat 4 in the master plan must not hold the interiors. Once a reader has absorbed the theory, their agreement stops being evidence, so the stage-1 verdict is recorded first and later agreement is never pooled.
3. **Stage 3 sends a method.** Fig 4 :22 permits methods to cross up. The formula is a generator, so giving it away does not give away an answer. Each user still needs a ground and a pre-registered refuter of their own.

**Hard precondition:** stages 1 to 3 only work if the stage-1 output survives. It is a shape with declared gaps, not a basin forecast. If a reader asks for a number for a named reach, say plainly that this solution does not hold one and name the instrument that would produce it.
