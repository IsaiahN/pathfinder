## Question

How do groundwater depletion and changing rainfall interact to affect rivers and wetlands?

Stated as an effect: water managers plan river yield and wetland hydroperiod against a historical hydrograph. The gap between planned and delivered water grows in groundwater-connected slots under the joint pressure of pumping and rainfall change, and stays small elsewhere. An answer has to say (1) whether the two pressures form one problem or a union of overlapping conditions, (2) what order the effects arrive in, (3) where the residual must be indexed, and (4) what instrument would settle the sign and size for a named place. Bound as an ordinary term: "interaction" means the intersection of two conditions in a union, whose behaviour is categorically different from either condition alone (Fig 9 :57), not a smooth average of two trends.

## What is deliberately not here

- No personal emails of named individuals.
- No ranking of people by how likely they are to agree with the claim.
- No bulk-send plan.

Reasons, in the figures' own words:

1. **Agreement is not an anchor.** Fig 2 :35: "Alignment is triangulation, not negotiation. A mirror cannot correct you." A list selected for receptiveness manufactures the disqualifying correlation (Fig 2 :40).
2. **The outside checker must not hold the interiors.** Fig 10 :42: "A verifier that can reconstruct the claim cannot verify it." Fig 10 :52: "Bring a characterised residual, never the source."
3. **A failure is more useful than an endorsement.** Fig 2 :42: "Buy independence in proportion to the cost of being wrong." The first contact asks what would refute the shape, not whether the reader believes it.

Legally, scraping personal emails for unsolicited bulk outreach breaches GDPR and CAN-SPAM style rules in many jurisdictions. Public opt-in channels avoid that practical failure as well as the structural one.
