## The residual in effect terms

The question asks whether the distribution of reasoning strategies is conditional on the institutional setting, or whether the same forms appear everywhere and only their content changes. Two options and what each leads to:

- **Option A, setting-conditioned.** The same person reasons differently as buyer, student, and litigant; each setting rewards and stabilises a different set of inference habits; there is no context-free ranking of strategies, only a ranking relative to a setting's anchor. This leads to a programme of measuring each setting's ground separately and refusing to pool the results.
- **Option B, universal forms.** Reasoning forms are invariant; markets, schools, and courts differ only in subject matter. This leads to a search for one context-free catalogue of strategies and treats observed differences as noise or test familiarity.

A shape claim must be written down before the run so it can be refused. The shape derived here is: **the loop and the rule for naming what worked are invariant, while the strategies that get minted, rewarded, and transmitted are a function of the setting's currency, anchor, and habitat.** That is Option A in a restricted, `specified` form. It is not a measured frequency table.

## What is deliberately not here

- No personal emails of named individuals.
- No ranking of people by how likely they are to believe the claim.
- No bulk-send plan.

Three reasons, in the figures' own words:

1. **A room does not upgrade a label.** `Fig 7 :35` "A room's agreement is variation, not a verdict. Selection between rooms is where the ground votes." Adding readers raises the vocabulary and does not add an anchor.
2. **Correlation disqualifies a verdict.** `Fig 2 :40` "agreement about the world is worthless when the agreeing frames share an evidence pool". Selecting recipients for receptiveness manufactures the correlation that would make their agreement worthless.
3. **Asking for trust spends independence.** `Fig 10 :42` "A verifier that can reconstruct the claim cannot verify it." The first contact carries the residual and its refuter, never the framework's interior.

Legally, scraping personal emails for unsolicited bulk outreach breaches GDPR and CAN-SPAM style rules in many jurisdictions. Public opt-in channels avoid that failure as well as the structural one.

## Honest standing of this package

This is a **simulated derivation checked for internal consistency against the figures, not a ground reading.** The author's own warning applies: "LLMs can quickly simulate an approximate answer/output with the formula found in the architecture post." It cannot settle the question it reports on. The verdict is a shape and a route to a ground test, not a finding about actual market, school, or court frequencies.
