## 1. A shape is a constraint, not a filler

The canonical usage rule says the framework returns "what a missing thing would have to look like (a shape, not a name)." `Fig 9 :32` "you get a kind of thing, never the answer itself"

From *The Library of Babel*: "This means that you can cut your search short if the set of books returned is small and descriptive enough that it provides a general shape of the answer that satisfies your question." And: "The problem you have determines what makes a book relevant to you or not."

So a **shape** is the form of an answer you can state before you can fill in the empirical detail. It constrains; it does not fill. For COG-0033 the shape is *the loop is invariant and the strategy distribution is conditional on the setting's currency, anchor, and habitat*. A frequency table for markets, schools, and courts would be a filling, not a shape.

## 2. How the shape is obtained

- *Library of Babel* proximity method: use the vocabulary already held that is closest to the thing, look for recurring new terms that overlap or correlate with it, and recurse. "With each new term gained, you narrow the space by definition and deepen your vocabulary."
- `Fig 9 :27-28` "describe it before you go looking, or any frame you pick will seem to fit"; `Fig 9 :30-31` "your words are borrowed too, so describe the effect rather than the cause"
- The essay's stopping rule: "You will know that you are done because the finish line will also be the starting place, as there is nowhere else to search and nothing else that can describe the answer better."
- `Fig 4 :30` "So take the round trip and measure the gap. Without it you are navigating by dead reckoning: confident about a position nobody checked."

For this question the effect terms were *what settles the question here*, *what is rewarded here*, and *what is in contact with the reasoner here*. Those terms selected Fig 4, Fig 11, Fig 2, and Fig 9 before any discipline was chosen.

## 3. What licenses a name

Only two things, both outside the shape itself:

1. **The ground settles it.** Formula step 5: "the ground settles it. Verified only by what pays. A gate is not the ground." The canonical rule says the framework stops at the limit of what the instruments distinguish and records the lineage.
2. **A provenanced import from a frame whose closure differs,** with a source gate and a shadow test (`Fig 6 :23` "No frame escapes its own closure. The bound moves only on contact with a frame whose closure differs." and Fig 8's provenance rule).

Here, naming a strategy as characteristic of a market, a school, or a court is a binding from that setting's own record. It is licensed only by a measurement in that setting or by a provenanced import with a shadow test. Without either, the name is the searcher's own contribution and must be recorded as such.

## 4. The split is part of the shape, not a preface to it

*The Library of Babel* gives the diagnostic directly: "Perhaps your problem was a bundle or conflation of several problems masquerading as one? You will need to search all of those problems individually to find their answers and then combine them in a satisfactory way."

`Fig 9 :4-5` "Disagreements that do not shrink with effort, where each rule keeps working well on a different subset, are not one hard question but several well-formed ones. Split it rather than search."

So "markets, schools, or courts" is not a single room with three doors. It is a union of conditions. The shape includes the instruction to unbundle, because a single lever applied to a union clears the region where only one condition holds and leaves the intersections standing.

## 5. Correction to my own §3

I initially read "a shape, not a name" as forbidding the naming of any reasoning strategy in any setting. **That over-reading is retracted.** The canonical phrase is "what a **missing** thing would have to look like (a shape, not a name)." Its subject is a primitive not held in the library. The reasoning moves in this question (perceive, bet, compare, distinguish, mint, refuse) are not missing primitives; they are held terms. Naming one is a binding onto a held term, not the invention of a new one. What remains forbidden is claiming that the binding moves the wall or that the framework itself selected it. Both the original claim and this narrowing are kept visible.

## 6. Prestige is not action, and agreement is not a ground

From *The Recursive Self-Improvement Trap*: "Prestige must never be allowed to validate an action directly." And: "Prestige can fund an action. It can give an action a budget to be tried. But it cannot settle an action. Only the ground can settle it. The Ground Maintainer must be blind to prestige."

In this question the prestige analog is a strategy's reputation inside an institution: the famous case, the celebrated trade, the standard rubric. Its standing in the setting is not evidence that it works. `Fig 2 :40` "agreement about the world is worthless when the agreeing frames share an evidence pool" makes the same point for cross-setting agreement.

## 7. My own audit is a simulation, not a ground reading

Same essay: "LLMs can quickly simulate an approximate answer/output with the formula found in the architecture post." The framework's author adds that it cannot validate itself and requires an external validator. So `01_figure_grounded_audit.md` is a simulated derivation checked for internal consistency against the figures. It is **not** a test of whether these strategies are actually more common in any setting, and it must be labelled as such. It cannot settle anything it reports on.
