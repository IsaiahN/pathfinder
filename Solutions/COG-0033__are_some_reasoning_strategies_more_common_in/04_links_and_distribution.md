## Distribution: offer, do not push

Figure-grounded reasons:

- `Fig 4 :22` "Only methods cross up. Only head starts come down." Sending the full framework before anyone has described a residual sends a recording. Lead with the shape claim and the refuter, offer links after.
- `Fig 10 :42` "A verifier that can reconstruct the claim cannot verify it." A recipient who has absorbed the framework before being asked for a verdict has joined the frame being checked.
- `Fig 9 :27-28` "describe it before you go looking, or any frame you pick will seem to fit." Ask what they would expect to see if the shape claim were true before giving them the sources.
- `Fig 2 :40` "agreement about the world is worthless when the agreeing frames share an evidence pool". A send selected for people who already agree produces a convention, not corroboration.
- `Fig 7 :35` "A room's agreement is variation, not a verdict. Selection between rooms is where the ground votes." Three settings are three rooms. Do not merge their verdicts into one sample.

**Recommended order in a reply:** (1) the residual, the shape claim, and its refuter; (2) ask what they would count as a failure and which anchor they would use for their setting; (3) record that verdict; (4) only then the three links, with the note that the figures, not the summary, are the authority.

## The two-stage offer

**Stage 1.** Validate the shape against the ground. This is `Fig 10 :52` "Bring a characterised residual, never the source." and `Fig 2 :45` "Frames range against the ground. They do not negotiate with it." The recipient checks the shape without holding the framework's interior.

**Stage 2, opt-in only.** If curious, offer the three source links. Their stage 1 verdict is recorded first, and later agreement is never pooled as corroboration.

**Stage 3, opt-in.** Offer the formula as a method to reuse. This is permitted, because `Fig 4 :22` says methods cross up. Each user must still name a ground, write a refuter before the run, and state a mode. The author's own warning applies: "LLMs can quickly simulate an approximate answer/output with the formula found in the architecture post."

**Hard precondition.** Stages 1 to 3 only work if the stage-1 shape survives the anchor decision for schools in `01_figure_grounded_audit.md` F9. Sending before that decision is made is the dead-reckoning error `Fig 4 :30` names: "So take the round trip and measure the gap. Without it you are navigating by dead reckoning: confident about a position nobody checked."
