## 0. The residual, stated in effect terms

The question is not "are institutions good at self-correction?" It is: **what sets the lag between
an error entering an institution's library of held claims and that institution's library being
corrected at the ground?**

- **R in effect terms.** `R = |Γ(b,a) − o′|` per slot. For an institution, Γ is its published
  claims, standards, categories and procedures; `o′` is what the world actually returns; a slot is
  one object, claim, domain or case. The residual is the gap between what the institution predicts
  and what returns. Correction is the point at which a claim in Γ loses its standing to be composed
  over because the ground refuses it. Slow correction means this arrival-and-refusal loop is broken
  upstream of settlement.
- **Arity.** Multi-slot. An institution tracks many claims at once. Fig 1 :19 *"The residual is
  indexed by object slot, not aggregated."* The determinant must therefore be sought per slot.
- **Symmetry.** An error is not a missing term; it is a term held that is false. Fig 5 :53
  *"refuted: a term we hold is wrong; a term is owed that says so"* names the fifth kind, which the
  four "missing" bins cannot reach. The operator required is unary negation, not a bond; its
  symmetry question does not arise (Operators table amendment). This is not a symmetric join of two
  frames; agreement between two frames is a different quantity.
- **Scale.** Two loops are involved. The inner loop is one institution's perceive/route/mint/settle
  cycle (steps 1 to 5 of the formula). The outer loop is the refresh of checking frames across
  scales (Fig 4, Fig 7, Fig 10 shelf life). A lag can be introduced at either; the outer loop is the
  one most often unobserved because every internal instrument reads through it.

**Shape bound before the run.** The sought-for answer is: a set of determinants in dependency
order, each figure-grounded, each with a refuter, distinguishing a non-updating anchor, a reachable
channel, independent frames, a refusal operator, and per-slot measurement, from prestige, agreement
and review volume. Bound as an ordinary term so it can be refused (formula "Direction"; Fig 5 :62 to :66).

**Dependency order (Fig 3 :20).** The chain is ordered by dependency, not by importance:

4. **Objective**: what would count as correction, written down before review (Fig 9, Fig 5 :65).
5. **Planning**: independent frames and a maintained channel (Fig 7, Fig 8, Fig 10).
6. **Learn and carry**: settlement with provenance, then frame refresh (Fig 10 :62 to :66, :80).

Fig 3 :24 to :27 says the middle link, having terms for what a situation offers and being able to
say what would count as progress, attracts the least attention and the fewest instruments, and
*"A link with no instrument is not a link that works. It is a link nobody has looked at."* For
institutions this is step 4: most reviews measure output volume, not whether a failure condition was
committed in advance. F6 below is the finding for that link.

## 3. Findings

### F1: The anchor must not update (D1, decisive upstream condition)

Fig 2 :29: *"An anchor is whatever settles the question and does not change its answer because of what any frame thinks."*
Fig 2 :30: *"If it moves when you push on it, it is not an anchor."*
Fig 2 :33: *"The anchor must not update."*
Fig 2 :34: *"If it does, panel 3 turns into panel 1: the anchor becomes another frame, everything drifts together, and the drift is coherent, which is why nobody notices."*
Fig 2 :35: *"Alignment is triangulation, not negotiation. A mirror cannot correct you."*

An institution corrects quickly when the thing it ranges against cannot be moved by what its members
think. If its standard of correctness is itself revised whenever members disagree with it, the
system is in the mutual-update collapse: it converges on whoever had the most influence, and the
drift is coherent, which is why nobody sees it. Fig 2 :36 adds the legitimacy condition: an anchor
is legitimate when the question is constitutively about it. A patient outcome is an anchor for a
clinical claim; a committee vote is a frame, not an anchor, for a claim about the world.

**Mode:** `specified` (a prediction about institutions from a figure that states a general structural
rule). **Verdict: triangulated** across Fig 2 (triangulation panel) and Fig 10 (the maintainer seat
that keeps the anchor reachable) and the seat map's anchor section, which are different offices and
different questions about the same object. World corroboration is independent by author and field:
Ioannidis (2005) writes that *"Prestigious investigators may suppress via the peer review process the
appearance and dissemination of findings that refute their findings, thus condemning their field to
perpetuate false dogma"*; the Recursive Self-Improvement Trap essay writes *"If the maintainer is
swayed by prestige, the channel to the ground is broken."*

**Refuter:** produce a case where an institution whose recorded standard of correctness demonstrably
moved with member opinion still corrected errors durably, with correction latency measured per slot
against an anchor that did not move. If that exists, F1 is not necessary.

### F2: The ground does not decay; the channel does (D3)

Fig 10 :24: *"the ground does not decay, the channel does"*
Fig 10 :25-26: *"stale percepts, dropped transients, saturated metrics, pooled readings"*
Fig 10 :27-28: *"the output is not a verdict, it is you have lost touch"*
Fig 10 :29: *"keep the anchor reachable"*

The determinant most often missing from institutional accounts is not the quality of the ground but
the state of the connection to it. A saturated metric, a dropped signal, or two measurements quietly
pooled into one all make the channel look healthy from inside, because every internal instrument
reads through it. The maintainer's output in that state is not a judgement about the world; it is
the report that contact has been lost.

The seat map adds the shelf life: every correction raises correlation between corrector and
corrected, and Fig 10 :80-81 *"Frames whose independence is renewed by separate experience can be
maintained; frames whose correlation is fixed at origin can only be rotated, because no amount of
use makes them less alike and replacement is the only refresh."* A checking body that has only ever
been calibrated against the body it checks has spent its independence.

**Mode:** `specified`. **Verdict: single-source** for the figure line (Fig 10). World corroboration
is genuinely low-overlap: the high-reliability-organization literature's "sensitivity to
operations" and "preoccupation with failure, errors are reported promptly so problems can be found
and fixed" (Weick and Sutcliffe via the HRO entry), and Ioannidis on prestige suppressing refuting
findings. Agreement here is not two figures in the framework; it is one figure line plus independent
fields, recorded as such.

**Refuter:** show an institution whose error reports were stale, dropped, saturated or pooled still
catching live errors quickly and durably, measured against a non-updating anchor.

### F3: Independence, not agreement, is what makes a correction possible (D2)

Fig 2 :31-32: *"Independence, between two frames, is how little they draw on the same evidence."* and
*"Independence is what makes agreement mean something."*
Fig 2 :40: *"agreement about the world is worthless when the agreeing frames share an evidence pool"*
Fig 2 :42: *"Buy independence in proportion to the cost of being wrong."*
Fig 7 :12-13: *"same closure, handed along; agreement compounds, evidence does not"*
Fig 7 :35: *"A room's agreement is variation, not a verdict. Selection between rooms is where the ground votes."*
Fig 7 :36: *"A chain of mirrors is one long room. The next room must differ from the last, and match the residual it surfaced."*
Fig 8 :48-49: *"Two frames that overlap almost completely are one frame wearing two names."*

A chamber whose members all read the same report is one frame with many names. Adding reviewers
raises the vocabulary and does not add an anchor. Quick correction needs the next checking room to
differ from the last and to match the residual the last one surfaced. Fig 8 :45 *"Two frames together
reach what neither reaches alone"* is the positive form: the surplus lives where the closures
differ. Note the subtlety at Fig 8 :50-52: two frames can hold the same atom by convergence, reached
separately, which is corroboration at maximum independence. Shared conclusions are not the
signature of collapse; shared evidence is.

**Mode:** `general` for the structural claim, `specified` for the institutional prediction.
**Verdict: triangulated** across Fig 2, Fig 7 and Fig 8, three figures with different subject matter
(triangulation, room handoff, union surplus). World corroboration: Janis's groupthink studies, which
report that *"concurrence-seeking becomes so dominant in a cohesive ingroup that it tends to override
realistic appraisal of alternative courses of action"* and prescribe independent groups and a
rotating devil's advocate; and the red-team literature, which describes a permanent contrarian
function as *"an important defense against false assumptions and groupthink."*

**Refuter:** exhibit two frames drawing on the same evidence that nevertheless produced a correction
neither could reach alone, with the residual and the closures described before the result. If the
surplus appears without differing closures, F3 is wrong in this domain.

### F4: A vocabulary that cannot refuse can only accumulate (D4)

Fig 5 :50-53: *"the gap has a fifth kind"* and *"refuted: a term we hold is wrong; a term is owed that says so"*
Fig 5 :55: *"Nothing leaves the library. What falls is the reach."*
Operators table :58: *"a refutation is a negation, which is why a vocabulary without it can only accumulate"*
Fig 12 :112-113: *"A refutation is a negation, and that one is not a bond."*
Fig 12 :139-140: *"A vocabulary that cannot refuse can only accumulate."*

An institution needs an operator for "this held claim is false", not merely a way to note that
something is missing. Errata, retractions, formal corrections, the REFUTED bin, a named failure
class, are the unary operator ¬. Without it the institution can amend its descriptions and cannot
remove a claim's standing to be composed over. Fig 5 :55 matters for the cost: a refusal adds a term
and removes nothing; the reach falls because something was added, so the correction is safe to
compute and the refused claim cannot cycle back without new growth. An institution that deletes
its errors instead of recording them has neither property and cannot tell never-held from
held-and-given-up.

**Mode:** `general` for the operator claim, `specified` for its institutional form.
**Verdict: triangulated** across Fig 5, Fig 12 and the Operators table, which define the operator at
different levels (pipeline, composition, notation). World corroboration: Popper's *"criteria of
refutation have to be laid down beforehand"* (SEP entry quoting *Conjectures and Refutations*), and
Kuhn's opposing observation that during normal science *"anomalies are ignored or explained away if
at all possible"* and that there is a *"conservative resistance to the attempted refutation of key
theories."* Kuhn is a conflict in emphasis, not a contradiction: it describes what happens when the
operator exists on paper but is not used.

**Refuter:** show an institution with no retraction, correction or refusal mechanism that still
corrected errors durably rather than merely replacing prose, measured over multiple cases.

### F5: Measure the residual per slot, not as an average (D5)

Fig 1 :19: *"The residual is indexed by object slot, not aggregated."*
Fig 1 :22: *"Aggregating across slots is how a live signal disappears into an average."*
Fig 1 :23: *"The ground enters as the only metric."*
Fig 1 :27: *"They are frame-internal. A frame cannot score itself with a quantity it also produces."*

A single institutional accuracy KPI, publication count, or compliance percentage is an aggregate.
It can be near zero while one live slot carries a large residual, which Fig 1 :13 calls a legal
state. Fig 1 :20-21 says support is satisfied when some slot carries positive mass, so a global
residual near zero with one live slot is not inert. The determinant is whether the institution can
see which slot is wrong. Metrics the institution itself produces, such as credibility accrued or
predicates minted, are frame-internal and cannot score it.

**Mode:** `specified`. **Verdict: single-source** for the figure line (Fig 1). World corroboration
from HRO "sensitivity to operations" and from Ioannidis on fields whose claimed findings are *"simply
accurate measures of the prevailing bias."*

**Refuter:** demonstrate an aggregate institutional metric that preserves the live-slot signal and
routes correction to it, across multiple slots and multiple cases.

### F6: Refuse before mint: the objective link is where the chain breaks (D1/D4, middle link)

Fig 9 :27-28: *"describe it before you go looking, or any frame you pick will seem to fit"*
Fig 5 :62-66: a frame searching for an answer of a particular shape holds it *"as a PREFERENCE rather than as a term"*, so *"nothing here can see it"*, and the remedy is to *"make the sought-for shape an ORDINARY TERM"* and let it accrue a residual.
Fig 5 :42: *"The acceptance test is a bargain, not a threshold."*
Fig 9 :40-41: *"a wrong cut removes the answer and speeds up so keep every cut ranked and reversible"*

For institutional error correction, the middle link of Fig 3 is the ability to say, before the
review, what would count as correction and what would count as failure. Most review effort lands
above the break because that is where the measurements already exist (Fig 3 :29-30). An institution
that can only report how much it reviewed cannot report whether it was corrected. A pre-registered
failure condition binds the sought-for shape as an ordinary term; without one, the review can find
the answer it was hoping for and report it as correction. Popper's *"criteria of refutation have to
be laid down beforehand"* is the world-source form of the same rule. The seat map's law is sharper:
this is *"the one check that must ask about the frame's intent rather than its output"* and the one
exception to bringing only a characterised residual.

**Mode:** `specified`. **Verdict: triangulated** across Fig 5, Fig 9 and Fig 3 (pipeline, search
order, dependency chain). World corroboration: Popper (SEP), and the pre-registration recommendation
in Ioannidis: *"efforts may also be more successful with upfront registration of studies."*

**Refuter:** produce a documented correction where no failure condition was committed before the
review and the correction was not a one-off lucky hit, replicated across cases.

### F7: Reform that does not change contact changes nothing (D2/D3)

Fig 11 :40: *"An improvement that does not change contact changes nothing, however much it improves."*
Fig 11 :39: *"capability is a property of agent-and-habitat, never of the agent alone."*
Fig 10 :71: *"When the question changes, the room should change. A room that stays fixed while the question moves is the failure; the correlation is only its symptom."*
Fig 4 :22-23: *"Only methods cross up. Only head starts come down."*

Hiring better people, rewriting the mission statement, or adding a review stage does not by itself
change the institution's contact with the ground. The seat map states the same asymmetry over the
habitat: introduction is available, exclusion is not, so a managed arrangement is permanent work,
not a solved state. The practical form: add an outside frame, rotate a checking body, restore a
dropped reading, or give the maintainer a channel to a different closure. These change contact.
Volume and prestige do not.

**Mode:** `specified`. **Verdict: triangulated** across Fig 11, Fig 10 and Fig 4. World corroboration:
HRO's "reluctance to simplify interpretations" and valuing *"a diversity of experience and opinions"*;
groupthink prevention via outsiders and independent groups.

**Refuter:** exhibit an institutional reform that improved measured correction latency while leaving
the institution's contact with its ground unchanged.

### F8: Where the ground is gone: an honest gap (D1 subset)

Fig 8 :39: *"So a search that finds nothing is never a proof of absence."*
Fig 9 :44: *"Only a sealed room can be searched to the end, and no frame is a sealed room. So searching and finding nothing proves nothing."*
Fig 6 :27: *"Which is why some unreachability is depth and some is genuine absence, and from inside the frame the two look the same."*

Some institutional errors are only visible retrospectively, through records that did not survive or
through a ground that no longer exists in a form that can be consulted. For those cases the
determinants above cannot be verified: there is no nameable anchor left, so by the census rule D1
does not yet exist as a domain. This is a **gap**, and it is reported as a verdict, not a silence.
Fig 6 :27 says depth and absence look the same from inside, so the honest report is "unreached, not
unreachable" until someone produces a surviving anchor. Source question 3 in the same section
(how historians distinguish a genuine shift in evidential standards from a change in the kinds of
records that survived) is precisely this case.

**Mode:** `general`. **Verdict: gap** for that subset. **Refuter:** produce a surviving,
non-updating anchor for one of those historical cases and the gap closes for that case.

### F9: Filters are not witnesses (D6)

Fig 9 :60: *"Use filters for the budget and witnesses for the verdict. Never let a filter hand you a verdict."*
Fig 1 :27: *"They are frame-internal. A frame cannot score itself with a quantity it also produces."*
Fig 2 :42: *"Buy independence in proportion to the cost of being wrong."*

Tier, prestige, publication count, number of reviewers and receptiveness are filters for what gets
tried. None of them is a witness for whether the institution corrected. Selecting expert readers for
likely agreement manufactures exactly the correlation that makes their agreement worthless. This is
why `03_public_contact_points.csv` contains only public, opt-in venues and no ranked individuals.

**Mode:** `general`. **Verdict: triangulated** across Fig 9, Fig 1 and Fig 2. World corroboration:
Ioannidis on prestige suppressing refuting findings, and Merton's "organized dogmatism" counter-norm.

**Refuter:** show a case where a prestige- or volume-ranked panel produced a correction that a
low-overlap, unauthored panel could not, under a pre-committed failure condition.

## 4. Pre-registered refuter

Written before the conclusion, per the seat map: *"a falsifier written before the run is a design
instrument, not only an honesty device."*

The shape claim is: **correction latency is governed by five conditions in dependency order,
non-updating anchor, maintained channel, independent frames, a refusal operator, and per-slot
residuals; agreement, prestige and volume are filters, not witnesses.**

It is refuted if any of the following holds:

1. An institution with all five conditions still shows systematically slow correction across
   multiple cases, with latency measured per slot against a non-updating anchor.
2. A durable correction occurs with no non-updating anchor, where the institution's standard of
   correctness demonstrably moved with member opinion.
3. A durable correction occurs with no refusal operator, where no claim lost its standing to be
   composed over.
4. Two frames drawing on one evidence pool produce a surplus neither had, with the closures and
   residual described before the result.
5. An aggregate metric is shown to preserve the live-slot signal across multiple slots and cases.
6. A reform improves latency while leaving contact with the ground unchanged.
7. The independent second seat finds a figure line governing one of these quantities with low
   overlap that contradicts the mapping; then the census is rerun, not the conclusion softened.

If any fires, the affected finding is withdrawn and the dependency order is re-derived. Until an
actual measurement is taken, no finding is labelled `grounded`.

## 8. Strains and honest stop

- **Strain, named:** the ground for historical institutional questions is often remote or destroyed.
  Fig 2 :36 requires the anchor to be legitimate, that is, constitutively about the question. A
  later historian's consensus is a frame, not an anchor. F8 records this as a gap for those cases.
- **Strain, named:** Fig 10 :59 says *"an interest in the outcome is unavoidable, an interest in the
  reading is disqualifying."* Institutional maintainers (editors, funders, registries) usually have
  standing that survives the outcome they earned it on (seat map :171). The condition cannot be
  removed; it must be declared. This solution's own stake is declared in `09_watson_answer.md`.
- **After re-census:** no figure line was forced. Fig 13 was read and produced no finding; it is
  recorded in the census and not used. Two strains are named, none hidden.

**Stop.** The shape stops where the instruments stop. There is no measurement of correction latency
in this run, so this is a `specified` candidate with a `general` structural spine, not a `grounded`
result. Unreached, not unreachable.
