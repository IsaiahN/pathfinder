## 1. The claim, in one paragraph

Correction latency in institutions is governed by five conditions in dependency order: a
non-updating anchor; the residual measured per case rather than as one average; an operator for
saying "this held claim is false"; a failure condition written down before review; and checking
frames that differ from one another while a maintained channel keeps each of them in contact with
the anchor. Agreement, prestige and review volume are filters for what gets tried, never witnesses
for whether a correction happened. If the anchor updates on opinion, the institution drifts
coherently and nobody notices; if it cannot write a refusal, it can only accumulate. The order is a
sequence of products, not a checklist.

## 2. Three checkable sub-claims

### C1: Framework-internal; checkable by reading Figures 2 and 10

- **Statement:** correction requires an anchor that does not update on the frames, and a channel to
  it that is kept reachable. These are two different conditions, not one.
- **Source, verbatim:** Fig 2 :29 *"An anchor is whatever settles the question and does not change its
  answer because of what any frame thinks."* Fig 2 :33 *"The anchor must not update."* Fig 2 :34
  *"If it does, panel 3 turns into panel 1: the anchor becomes another frame, everything drifts
  together, and the drift is coherent, which is why nobody notices."* Fig 10 :24 *"the ground does
  not decay, the channel does"* and Fig 10 :25-26 *"stale percepts, dropped transients, saturated
  metrics, pooled readings"*.
- **What to check:** are the anchor condition and the channel condition really distinct in the figure
  text, and does the second presuppose the first? Does the drift description match what is known
  about institutions whose standard of correctness moves with member opinion?
- **Refuter:** a durable correction in an institution whose recorded standard of correctness changed
  in response to member opinion, with latency measured per case against an anchor that did not move;
  or a durable correction with stale, dropped, saturated or pooled readings.

### C2: Framework-internal; checkable by reading Figures 1, 5 and 12

- **Statement:** the error is the fifth kind of gap, a term held that is false, and it requires the
  unary operator of negation. A vocabulary with only additive description can accumulate corrections
  to prose and cannot carry the correction.
- **Source, verbatim:** Fig 5 :53 *"refuted: a term we hold is wrong; a term is owed that says so"*.
  Operators table :58 *"a refutation is a negation, which is why a vocabulary without it can only
  accumulate"*. Fig 12 :139-140 *"A vocabulary that cannot refuse can only accumulate."* Fig 5 :55
  *"Nothing leaves the library. What falls is the reach."*
- **What to check:** is a retraction, correction, or named failure class an instance of this operator,
  or is that mapping an import that needs provenance? Does the monotone-record claim match how
  retractions actually behave in a citation record?
- **Refuter:** a durable correction in an institution with no retraction, correction or refusal
  mechanism, where no held claim lost its standing to be composed over.

### C3: Ordering and independence; checkable by reading Figures 3, 7 and 9

- **Statement:** the conditions form a dependency sequence, not a checklist; the failure condition
  must be committed before review; and the next checking frame must differ from the last and match
  the residual it surfaced.
- **Source, verbatim:** Fig 3 :20 *"The chain is ordered by dependency, not by importance. Each link

  use it."* Fig 9 :27-28 *"describe it before you go looking, or any frame you pick will seem to fit"*.

- **What to check:** does the sequence in `06_isomers_and_order.md` Q1 really satisfy the

  condition the institutional form of Fig 5 :65, or a strained analogue?
- **Refuter:** a durable correction in which the channel was restored before an anchor existed, or
  checking frames were selected before the residual was described, across multiple cases; or two
  frames drawing on one evidence pool producing a correction neither could reach alone.

## 3. What is explicitly not claimed

- Not that any named institution is slow or fast. No institution was measured.

## 5. What would settle it

A study measuring correction latency per case, against an anchor that did not move during the study,
varying the five conditions and recording frame-to-anchor overlap. The four essays and the world
literature point at the same instrument: an effect description that anyone can check, and a trend in
correction size rather than a count of corrections issued. Until such a study exists the verdict is
`candidate`. Fig 8 :39 *"So a search that finds nothing is never a proof of absence."*
