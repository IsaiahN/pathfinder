## Wider literature, grouped by field

Kept separate so an evidence pool is not mistaken for two witnesses. Overlap is recorded in the ledger.

- **History and sociology of science.** Merton (1942) on organized skepticism and its counter-norm; Kuhn (1962) on normal science and the conservative resistance to refutation. Both bear directly on the source question's section 3.
- **Philosophy of science.** Popper on criteria of refutation laid down beforehand; the useful disagreement with Kuhn is about whether the operator is used, not whether it exists.
- **Meta-research and methodology.** Ioannidis (2005) on prestige, bias, and the suppression of refuting findings; upfront registration as a remedy.
- **Organizational theory and safety.** Weick and Sutcliffe's high reliability organizing; Perrow's normal accidents as the contrasting deterministic position (read via the HRO entry, not as a separate source).
- **Social psychology and security practice.** Janis on groupthink; red teaming and devil's advocacy as designed outside frames.
- **Framework essays.** Only four of the eight were opened this session (Library of Babel, Recursive Self-Improvement Trap, Hard Problem is About Source, Decoding Tacit Knowledge). The other four were not opened this session and are not quoted here as if they were. This is stated in `05_what_shape_means.md`.

No world source was found that directly measures institutional correction latency against the five
conditions. So none settles the claim; all corroborate or complicate parts of the shape.

## Distribution rules: offer, do not push

Grounded reasons, stated before the rules:

- Fig 10 :52 *"Bring a characterised residual, never the source."* Send the dependency order and its
  refuter first. The recipient checks the output without holding the framework's interior.
- Fig 10 :42 *"A verifier that can reconstruct the claim cannot verify it."* A reader who has
  absorbed the whole framework before being asked for a verdict has joined the frame being checked.
- Fig 9 :27-28 *"describe it before you go looking, or any frame you pick will seem to fit."* Ask what
  they would expect to see if each condition were true before giving them the sources.
- Fig 9 :60 *"Use filters for the budget and witnesses for the verdict. Never let a filter hand you a verdict."* A venue chosen because its readers are likely to agree is a filter, not a witness.
- Fig 4 :22-23 *"Only methods cross up. Only head starts come down."* The formula may be given away
  as a method; a recorded conclusion may not be passed off as a method.

**Recommended order in a reply:** (1) the residual and the five-condition shape; (2) the four
refuters and the question of what the recipient would count as failure; (3) record their stage-1
verdict; (4) only then the links, with the note that the figure text, not this summary, is the
authority. Stage 3, the formula as a reusable method, is opt-in after stage 2.

Only `03_public_contact_points.csv` venues are used. One-to-one contact, no bulk send, no personal
emails. A reply is a conversation and carries `verdict_weight = 0`; it is never pooled as
corroboration. If a reply contains a refutation, copy it into `11_audit.md` with provenance and have
the auditor seat re-evaluate it as if it came from any other source.
