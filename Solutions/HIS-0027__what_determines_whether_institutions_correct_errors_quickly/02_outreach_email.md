# Outreach draft: check the output before you decide about the framework

**Use:** one-to-one, or as a cover note attached to a submission through a public venue in
`03_public_contact_points.csv`. **Not** for bulk send. No personal emails are held or used here.

**Hard precondition:** do not send until the three sub-claims in `08_expert_verification_packet.md`
have been checked against the verbatim figure text. A failed stage-1 output collapses the offer.

**Subject:** One checkable claim about why institutions correct errors slowly or quickly

> Hi [name],
>
> I have one specific output I would like you to check against what you already know about error
> correction in institutions. I am not asking you to read any theory first, and I am not asking you
> to agree with it. If the output does not survive, there is nothing else to look at.
>
> **The residual, in effect terms.** An institution holds a library of claims about its domain. An
> error is a claim in that library that the world does not pay. Correction is the point at which the
> claim loses its standing to be composed over. My question is what sets the lag between the error
> arriving and the library being corrected. The output is a dependency order of five conditions:
> (1) an anchor that does not change its answer when members push on it; (2) a channel to that
> anchor that is kept reachable, because the ground does not decay but the channel does; (3)
> checking frames that draw on different evidence, so their disagreement carries information; (4)
> an operator for saying "this held claim is false", as distinct from noting that something is
> missing; and (5) the residual measured per case, not as one institutional average. Agreement,
> prestige and review volume are ranked as filters for the budget, not witnesses for the verdict.
>
> **What would refute it.** I wrote these down before writing the output:
>
> 1. A durable correction in an institution whose recorded standard of correctness demonstrably
>    moved with member opinion, with latency measured per case against an anchor that did not move.
> 2. A durable correction with no retraction or refusal mechanism, where no held claim lost its
>    standing to be composed over.
> 3. Two checking bodies drawing on one evidence pool producing a correction neither could reach
>    alone, with the closures and the residual described before the result.
> 4. An aggregate institutional metric that preserves the live-case signal across cases.
>
> If any of these holds, the corresponding condition is withdrawn. A one-line "this fails
> because..." is more useful to me than agreement. If you would rather not engage, no reply is
> needed.
>
> **What I am not claiming.** Not that any named institution is slow or fast. Not that this is a
> measured result: it is a `specified` prediction with a `general` structural spine, derived from a
> set of figures and checked only for internal consistency. Not that adding reviewers, prestige or
> a new mission statement corrects anything. Not that the outcome of this email is corroboration if
> you happen to agree; a reply is a conversation, not a witness.
>
> **If it survives, and only then:** the sources are below. The figure text, not my summary of it,
> is the authority.
> - The verbatim figure text with line numbers (local framework file, path supplied on request).
> - The public figures repository: https://github.com/IsaiahN/tether/tree/general-agent/figures
> - The canonical usage rule: https://github.com/IsaiahN/tether/blob/general-agent/figures/README.md
>
> **If you want to use the method:** the formula is in the figures link. Run it on an institution
> you know well and can check against a ground. Write down what would refute your result *before*
> you run it, and state the mode of what you get. A reported failure is more useful to me than a
> reported success.
>
> [your name]

## Why this version is ordered this way

| the move | figure-grounded reason |
|---|---|
| Residual and refuter before the theory | Fig 10 :52 *"Bring a characterised residual, never the source."* The recipient checks the output without holding the framework's interior. |
| Ask what they would count as a failure, not whether they agree | Fig 2 :35 *"Alignment is triangulation, not negotiation. A mirror cannot correct you."* |
| Pre-registered refuter | Seat map: *"a falsifier written before the run is a design instrument, not only an honesty device."* |
| Mode stated on every claim | `THE_FORMULA.md`: *"Mode. general · specified · grounded. State which."* |
| No request for trust or agreement | The Recursive Self-Improvement Trap: prestige can fund an action but *"cannot settle an action. Only the ground can settle it."* |
| Filters kept out of the verdict | Fig 9 :60 *"Use filters for the budget and witnesses for the verdict. Never let a filter hand you a verdict."* |
| Links only after the verdict is recorded | Fig 10 :42 *"A verifier that can reconstruct the claim cannot verify it."* |
| Offer the formula as a method to reuse | Fig 4 :22 *"Only methods cross up. Only head starts come down."* |
| State that agreement is not corroboration | Fig 2 :40 *"agreement about the world is worthless when the agreeing frames share an evidence pool"*; a Track B reply is a conversation, never a witness. |

## Two-stage offer, stated explicitly

1. **Stage 1.** Send the residual, the five-condition shape and the four refuters. Ask what the
   recipient would count as a failure. Record their verdict **before** showing any theory.
2. **Stage 2, opt-in only.** If they are curious, offer the three links. The figure text is the
   authority, not the summary. Their later agreement is never pooled as corroboration.
3. **Stage 3.** Offer the formula as a method to reuse, with the instruction that each person
   writes their own refuter and states their own mode.

**Hard precondition repeated:** stages 1 to 3 only work if the stage-1 output survives. If
`08_expert_verification_packet.md` fails a check, fix the packet first. Do not send a recording of a
conclusion where a generator and a refuter were promised.
