## Round trip gap R_T

The Symbols table defines R_T as the gap between what was sent up and what came back down,
`gap(x, (T_E ∘ T_A)(x))`, measuring what the coarser description cannot hold. Fig 4 :30 requires it
to be measured rather than assumed. I ran the round trip on the condensation this folder actually
uses.

1. **Sent up (x):** the verbatim figure set, chiefly Fig 2 :29 and :33-34 (non-updating anchor),
   Fig 10 :24-26 (channel decay), Fig 5 :53 and :55 (refusal adds a term and does not delete), Fig 1
   :19 (per-slot residual).
2. **Abstraction (T_A):** the shorthand in `07_the_tether_answer.md`: five conditions in dependency
   order, filters are not witnesses, no institution was measured.
3. **Concretisation (T_E):** a concrete institutional review: appoint an ombuds, publish a retraction
   policy, pre-register failure conditions, rotate the reviewers, track errors per case.

**R_T is nonzero.** Three things the shorthand holds do not survive the trip back down:

- the **legitimacy condition on the anchor** (Fig 2 :36). The shorthand says "non-updating anchor";
  a concrete institution may appoint a board that is stable only because it is insulated, which is a
  frame, not an anchor;
- the **refusal semantics** (Fig 5 :55 and Fig 6 :42-46). A concrete retraction policy often deletes
  or hides the old record, which destroys the monotone record and lets a refused claim cycle back;
- the **independence renewal requirement** (Fig 10 :80-81). A concrete "rotate the reviewers" rule
  can become a rotation of people who share one evidence pool, which changes spelling, not search.

One thing survives cleanly: if the standard of correctness moves when members push on it, there is
no ground to correct toward. That is the residue worth sending. R_T was **not** measured numerically,
because no ground reading exists; it is recorded qualitatively as the enumerated set of dropped
constraints. Treating the formula as identical to the figure set would set R_T to zero and would be
the dead-reckoning error Fig 4 :30 names. The honest form is a lossy condensation with a recorded gap,
which is what `09_watson_answer.md` states.
