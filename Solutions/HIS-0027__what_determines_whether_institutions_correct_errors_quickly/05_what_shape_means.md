## 1. A shape is a constraint, not a filler

The canonical figures README, read this session, states the limit: the framework returns *"what a
missing thing would have to look like (a shape, not a name)."* Fig 9 :32 *"you get a kind of thing,
never the answer itself."* Fig 13 :134 *"Unreachable is a verdict here, not a silence,"* and :135
*"It cannot say which primitive is missing, because naming it would mean already holding it."*

From the Library of Babel essay, opened this session: *"you can cut your search short if the set of
books returned is small and descriptive enough that it provides a general shape of the answer that
satisfies your question."*

So a **shape** is the narrowed form of an answer you can state before you can settle it. It constrains;
it does not fill. For HIS-0027 the shape is the dependency order of the five conditions in
`01_figure_grounded_audit.md`. It is not a list of named institutions, named reforms, or a single
score.

## 2. How the shape is obtained

- **Proximity and bootstrapping.** Library of Babel: use the vocabulary you already have that is
  closest to the thing, then find recurring new terms that overlap or correlate with it. *"With each
  new term gained, you are able to narrow the space by definition and deepen your vocabulary."*
- **Describe the effect, not the cause.** Fig 6 :30 *"Cast it in effect terms."* Fig 9 :30-31
  *"your words are borrowed too, so describe the effect rather than the cause."* The residual for
  this question is stated as a lag between an error arriving and a library being corrected, not as
  "bad culture" or "groupthink".
- **Effect descriptions are checkable by anyone.** Decoding Tacit Knowledge: *"When contact isn't
  possible, the best you can send is a plain description of what you'd expect to see, because anyone
  can check that against the world."* That is why the outreach email leads with what would count as
  failure.
- **Stopping.** Library of Babel: you know you are done because *"the finish line will also be the
  starting place"*, with the paradox that by then *"you have already composed the answer through the
  process of locating it."* For this solution the stop is F8 in the audit: a gap where no surviving
  anchor exists.

## 3. What licenses a name

Only two things, both outside the shape itself:

1. **The ground settles it.** `THE_FORMULA.md` step 5: *"the ground settles it. Verified only by what
   pays. A gate is not the ground."* The README: the framework *"stops at the limit of what the
   available instruments can distinguish, and records the lineage so the stop is honest."*
2. **A provenanced import from a frame whose closure differs** (Fig 6 :23, Fig 8 :50-52), with a
   source gate and a shadow test.

Applied to this question: naming "peer review", "registered reports", "red teams" or "independent
replication" as *the* cure is a binding from world sources onto the five conditions. It is not a
derivation of the conditions. Which binding is correct for a given institution is exactly what the
ground would have to settle, and no measurement was taken here.

## 4. The source problem: why agreement does not name the cause

The Hard Problem is About Source, opened this session, gives the mechanism. An instrument reads
green and *"cannot comment on the source"*; one green light, yellow plus blue, RGB, CMYK, or a scheme
not yet discovered can produce the same reading. *"If several paths create the same behavior, then
behavior is a projection with lossy information."* It was the **spectrometer**, not an argument, that
resolved the case: the answer was *"unreached until a better instrument exists."*

For HIS-0027: two institutions can both show "few retractions" (one green reading) because errors
are genuinely rare, because errors are suppressed, because the metric is saturated, or because cases
were pooled. A review that names the source from the reading commits the metamerism error. What is
owed is a discriminator, for example per-case residuals against a non-updating anchor, not a name.
This is the figure-side F2 and F5 restated in the essay's vocabulary.

Fig 2 :34 supplies the same warning in drift form: *"everything drifts together, and the drift is
coherent, which is why nobody notices."* Decoding Tacit Knowledge makes it institutional:
*"Communities and institutions that only check against themselves drift together and don't notice."*

## 5. The three drift-stoppers, in the essay's own words

Decoding Tacit Knowledge names safeguards that match the figure-derived conditions:

- *"Science's independent replication is separate experience plus something real to check against"*:
  F3 independence plus F1 anchor.
- *"Apprenticeship is two-way contact with correction"*: F2 maintained channel, with the learner's
  act visible so it can be corrected.
- *"Courts of law rule on evidence, which neither side can move"*: F1 anchor in its constitutional
  form.

The same essay gives an instrument for measuring correction rather than counting it: *"watch the
trend in correction size, not the count of lessons given."* That is F5's per-slot residual applied to
learning, and it is a concrete proposal for what would settle this question at the ground.

## 6. "Shape, not a name" narrowed

The README's phrase is *"what a missing thing would have to look like (a shape, not a name)."* The
subject is a missing primitive, one not held in Γ. The five conditions in this solution are bindings
from the figure set onto institutional features, not new primitives. So naming them is allowed as
provenance work. What remains forbidden is naming *the* correct reform for a particular institution
without a ground reading, or presenting the binding as a derivation. Both claims are recorded so
neither is quietly lost.

## 7. Prestige is not action

The Recursive Self-Improvement Trap, opened this session: *"Prestige must never be allowed to
validate an action directly."* *"Prestige can fund an action. It can give an action a budget to be
tried. But it cannot settle an action. Only the ground can settle it."* And the failure mode, which
is this question in one sentence: *"If the maintainer is swayed by prestige, the channel to the
ground is broken. And a broken channel looks exactly like a working one, until the drift is so large
that the system collapses."*

So a famous reviewer, a prestigious journal, or a large body of agreement gives a claim a budget to
be tested. It does not tell us whether the institution corrected. This is why `03_public_contact_points.csv`
contains venues, not ranked people, and why a Track B reply carries zero verdict weight.

The same essay also supplies the pariah mechanism relevant to F4: a system that *"avoided an idea
forever after one bad result, it would never learn that the idea was fine."* A refusal must remove a
claim's standing to be composed over without forbidding its re-entry under new evidence (Fig 5 :55,
Fig 6 :42-46). An institution that blacklists a method permanently has over-applied negation.

## 8. My own audit is a simulation, not a ground reading

Same essay: LLM output can *"simulate an approximate answer/output"* but *"the truly testable output
would need to be derived from the real architecture or working the formula yourself."* So
`01_figure_grounded_audit.md` is a simulated derivation checked for internal consistency against the
figures. It is **not** a test of any institution. It cannot settle anything it reports on. Its
correct use is as a question-asking instrument: here are five conditions, here is their dependency
order, here is what would refute each, now go and measure.
