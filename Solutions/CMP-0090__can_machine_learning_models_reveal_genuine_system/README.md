## What an answer would look like (bind the sought-for shape as an ordinary term)

An answer is a rule that tells a researcher what observation would license the word *mechanism* for a fitted model, and what observation would force the word *correlation*. Two candidate shapes were bound before the run:

- **M-shape (mechanism credited):** a regularity in the model that survives a push on the ground from a frame that shares no evidence pool with training, where the arrangement (bond) rather than the mere ingredient set does the work.
- **C-shape (correlation only):** a regularity that scores near zero on the training distribution and fails when the habitat is substituted; the model holds the ingredients and not the arrangement.

Binding both as ordinary terms lets them accrue a residual like anything else, per `THE_FORMULA.md` "Direction": *"make the sought-for shape an ORDINARY TERM - write down what an answer would look like, bind it, and let it accrue a residual like anything else."*

## What is deliberately not here

- No personal emails of named individuals.
- No ranking of people by how likely they are to agree with the verdict.
- No bulk-send plan.

Reasons, in the figures' own words:

1. **Agreement is not an anchor.** Fig 2 :35: *"Alignment is triangulation, not negotiation. A mirror cannot correct you."* Fig 2 :40: *"agreement about the world is worthless when the agreeing frames share an evidence pool"*. A list chosen for receptiveness manufactures the disqualifying correlation.
2. **A verifier that holds the interiors cannot verify.** Fig 10 :42: *"A verifier that can reconstruct the claim cannot verify it."* The first contact therefore carries the residual and its refuter, never the framework.
3. **Prestige is not a ground.** *The Recursive Self-Improvement Trap*: *"Prestige can fund an action. It can give an action a budget to be tried. But it cannot settle an action. Only the ground can settle it."*

Legally: a scraped list of personal addresses for unsolicited bulk outreach breaches GDPR and CAN-SPAM style rules in many jurisdictions. Public opt-in venues avoid that practical failure as well as the structural one.
