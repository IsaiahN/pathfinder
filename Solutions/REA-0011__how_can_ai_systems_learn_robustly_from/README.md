## What is deliberately not here

- No personal emails of named individuals.
- No ranking of people by how likely they are to agree.
- No bulk-send plan.

Three reasons, in the figures' own words: a room of agreeing frames is not an anchor
(Fig 2 :29, :35); agreement drawn from one evidence pool is worthless as a verdict
(Fig 2 :40, Fig 8 :48); and a recipient who has absorbed the framework before being asked for a
verdict can no longer check it (Fig 10 :42). Selecting for receptiveness manufactures the very
correlation that makes agreement uninformative. Legally, unsolicited bulk outreach also breaches
GDPR and CAN-SPAM style rules in many jurisdictions.

## Honest standing of the whole folder

This is a **simulated derivation checked for internal consistency against the figures**, not a
ground reading. The framework's own *Recursive Self-Improvement Trap* says an LLM "can quickly
simulate an approximate answer/output" but "the truly testable output would need to be derived from
the real architecture or working the formula yourself." Nothing in this folder settles the
question it reports on. The verdict is a shape plus a refuter offered to people who can check it
against a ground.
