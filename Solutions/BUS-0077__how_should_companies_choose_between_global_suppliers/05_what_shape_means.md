## 1. A shape is a constraint, not a filler

The canonical rule states the limit: the framework returns "what a missing thing would have to look like (a shape, not a name)." Fig 9 :32: "you get a kind of thing, never the answer itself." Fig 13 :134: "Unreachable is a verdict here, not a silence."

From *The Library of Babel*: "you can cut your search short if the set of books returned is small and descriptive enough that it provides a general shape of the answer that satisfies your question."

So a **shape** is the narrowed form of an answer you can state before you can name it. It constrains; it does not fill. For this question, the shape is the seven-clause decision procedure in `README.md`, not "Vietnam," not "Mexico," and not "reshore." A country name is a value of an option variable, not a constraint on the decision procedure.

## 2. How a shape is obtained

- *Library of Babel* on proximity: use the vocabulary already held that is closest to the thing, then look for new terms that overlap or correlate with it. "With each new term gained, you narrow the space and deepen your vocabulary." In this solution the starting vocabulary was landed cost, lead time, disruption and capability; the figure lines narrowed it to unbundling, anchors, contact cascades, habitat substitution, two currencies and low-overlap frames.
- Fig 6 :30: "The description travels. Fails by being sharpened into terms only we can read. Cast it in effect terms." Fig 9 :30-31: "your words are borrowed too, so describe the effect rather than the cause." The residual was therefore stated as a gap between prediction and realized cost, recovery and quality, not as "offshoring is bad."
- Fig 9 :27: "describe it before you go looking, or any frame you pick will seem to fit." The domain census in `01_figure_grounded_audit.md` §1 was written before the findings, so the frames did not select the conditions.
- *Library of Babel* on stopping: "you will know that you are done because the finish line will also be the starting place, as there is nowhere else to search and nothing else that can describe the answer better." Here the stop is the gap at F2: no figure line selects a country, and further search inside the figure set adds no atom (Fig 8 :32: "no search, however long, adds an atom").

## 3. What licenses a name

Only two things, both outside the shape itself:

1. **The ground settles it.** `THE_FORMULA.md` step 5: "the ground settles it. Verified only by what pays. A gate is not the ground." A firm may name a winning configuration only after its anchors pay in realized cost, recovery and capability.
2. **A provenanced import from a frame whose closure differs** (Fig 6 :23, Fig 8 :50-52), with a source gate and a shadow test. A benchmark report from an industry body could import a threshold, but only with its provenance and only where it explains a gap the firm actually has.

*The Hard Problem is About Source* gives the discriminator. Metamerism: an instrument reads green and "cannot comment on the source." The essay states: "If several paths create the same behavior, then behavior is a projection with lossy information." And: "The answer is unreached until a better instrument exists." It was a spectrometer, not an argument, that resolved the case.

Applied here: a quoted unit price is a reading, not a source. It cannot tell you whether the low number came from labor arbitrage, a subsidy, an unpriced sub-tier risk, or an accounting boundary. Naming the source of that reading from the number itself is the metamerism error. What is owed is a better instrument: realized total landed cost, a fixed-failure recovery test, and a before-and-after capability map.

## 4. Why the answer term stays a procedure

The bound term in `README.md` is a decision procedure with seven clauses. Each clause is checkable without the framework's interior:

- unbundle the union (Fig 9 :54);
- name immovable anchors (Fig 2 :29);
- count second-order contact (Fig 11 :52);
- treat relocation as habitat substitution (Fig 11 :66);
- keep two currencies apart (Fig 12 :125);
- require low-overlap frames (Fig 8 :45);
- keep cuts reversible (Fig 9 :41).

That is a shape. It narrows the space of acceptable sourcing decisions without occupying a slot in it. Fig 12 :111-113: "A vocabulary without bonds can name a state and cannot name a route to one. A plan is a sequence. A goal is a comparison. Progress is a subtraction." A country name names a state. The seven clauses name a route.

## 5. Correction to my own §1 and §3

I initially read "shape, not a name" as a blanket ban on any concrete sourcing recommendation, and I drafted §3 to say the framework forbids naming a supplier at all. **Retracted as an overstatement.** The canonical phrase is "what a **missing** thing would have to look like (a shape, not a name)." It governs a primitive not held in Γ. Country names are not primitives in Γ at all; they are values of an option variable in the world. The correct two claims are:

1. No figure line governs which country to choose. That is a `gap` (F2), reported as a verdict, not a prohibition.
2. A configuration may be named by the firm once its anchors pay, or once a provenanced import with a shadow test supplies the threshold. Naming after settlement is not banned; naming before settlement is a preference presented as a verdict.

Both forms are recorded so neither is quietly lost. The §4 metamerism point is **not** retracted: reading a source off a single price projection still needs a discriminator.

## 6. Two consequences for outreach

**Prestige is not action.** From *The Recursive Self-Improvement Trap*: "Prestige must never be allowed to validate an action directly." And: "Prestige can fund an action. It can give an action a budget to be tried. But it cannot settle an action. Only the ground can settle it." A panel of well-known supply chain figures agreeing with the procedure is the named collapse, not corroboration. The legitimate ask is a budget to test the procedure against a real ground.

**This audit is a simulation, not a ground reading.** Same essay: "the truly testable output would need to be derived from the real architecture or working the formula yourself." The essay also says: "The catch is that the output is only as good as the validator. The agent, or the tool, or the frame, or the theory, cannot validate itself." So `01` is a simulated derivation checked for internal consistency against the figures. It is not a test of any real sourcing decision and must be labelled as such. It cannot settle the question it reports on.
