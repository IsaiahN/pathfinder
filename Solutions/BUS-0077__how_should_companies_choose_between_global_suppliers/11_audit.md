## Independent domain census

From that line alone, using the WATSON order: R by arity, symmetry and scale; one line per domain with actors, currency and anchor; partition or union; order by dependency.

- **R (arity / symmetry / scale).** Arity: four phenomena are named in the line itself (cost, lead time, quality, disruption) plus the coordination burden implied by "configuration." That is a **union compound**, not one hard question. Fig 9 :57 "a case in two conditions is not a harder case of either." Symmetry: none. A supplier three steps out is not interchangeable with one next door; Fig 11 :49 "An actor's valence is relative to the residual, not a property it carries." Scale: the line names the direct configuration and the arriving ground, but the arriving event can propagate, so first, second and third order must all be in scope.
- **Domain 1, landed economics.** Actors: price, freight, duty, inventory, rework, opportunity cost. Currency: money. Anchor: realized total landed cost. Question type: one condition of the union.
- **Domain 2, disruption and recovery.** Actors: supplier sites, lanes, backup capacity, alternate sources. Currency: time to recover and service level. Anchor: observed recovery under a fixed failure or a real event. Question type: one condition of the union.
- **Domain 3, capability and habitat.** Actors: engineers, suppliers, institutions, conditions, relations. Currency: contact and adaptation. Anchor: realized capability of the firm-and-habitat pair. Question type: one condition of the union.
- **Domain 4, verification and governance.** Actors: decision makers, internal frames, outside checkers. Currency: agreement versus residual. Anchor: the ground, never agreement. Question type: checking loop. All seats in this simulated run share one evidence pool, so this domain's independence is zero.
- **Rejected.** Any candidate without a nameable anchor is not a domain yet. Reputation and strategic fit fail that test.
- **Order by dependency.** Slot the residual, unbundle the union, name anchors, range frames against anchors separately, buy independence, keep cuts reversible. Fig 3 :20 "The chain is ordered by dependency, not by importance."

My census matches the primary's four domains and its union reading. Where the primary derived the union from Fig 9 and the per-slot indexing from Fig 1, I derived the union from the arity of the residual line itself and the contact requirement from Fig 11. Different starting points, same domain boundaries. Because both seats share one evidence pool, this is low-overlap routing inside one pool, not independent corroboration.

## Round trip gap R_T

The Symbols table defines R_T as `gap(x, (T_E ∘ T_A)(x))`, the gap between what was sent up and what came back down. Fig 4 :30 requires it to be measured rather than assumed. I ran the round trip on the condensation this folder actually uses.

1. **Sent up (x):** the verbatim figure set, chiefly Fig 9 :54, :57 (union unbundling), Fig 2 :29 (anchor), Fig 11 :52, :66 (contact and habitat) and Fig 12 :125 (two currencies).
2. **Abstraction (T_A):** the seven-clause answer term in `README.md`: unbundle, anchor, count contact, substitute habitat, keep two currencies, triangulate, keep reversible.
3. **Concretisation (T_E):** the tempting concrete claim, "choose the regional supplier because it is closer and safer," or its mirror, "choose the global supplier because the unit price is lower."

**R_T is nonzero.** Four things the shorthand holds do not survive the trip back down:

- the **per-slot indexing of R** (Fig 1 :19); a company-wide rule drops it;
- the **no-update property of the anchor** (Fig 2 :29); a quoted price and a manager's confidence both move with belief;
- the **second-order contact cascade** (Fig 11 :52); a cost rollup records the direct supplier only;
- the **operand type of ⋛ at second order** (Operators table): a threshold relation between two quantities, not obviously a settled configuration.

Conversely, two things survive cleanly: the union structure of the decision and the requirement that relocation substitutes rather than removes a habitat. That is the residue worth sending. R_T was **not** measured numerically, because no ground reading exists; it is recorded qualitatively as the enumerated set of dropped constraints. Treating the seven-clause condensation as identical to the figure set would set R_T to zero and would be the dead-reckoning error Fig 4 :30 names.
