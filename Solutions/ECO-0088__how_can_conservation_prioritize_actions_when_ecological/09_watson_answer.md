## QUESTION

Decide, in effect terms, what the Tether says about allocating scarce conservation effort when outcomes are uncertain. Option A: produce one ranked list from an aggregate score and defend it by expert agreement. Option B: state the shape of a rule that keeps residuals per slot, keeps description cost and action cost apart, enumerates the habitat and its cascades, keeps cost as a filter rather than a witness, and ranges independent frames against a measured ground. Option A hides live slot residuals in an average, mixes two currencies and spends the independence of the people checking it. Option B yields a checkable generator and an open slot until a ground settles it.

## DOMAIN CENSUS

R by arity, symmetry and scale, then one line per domain with actors / currency / anchor, partition or union, and order by dependency.

- **R (the residual).** The gap between predicted and observed ecological state per object slot. Arity: many slots (sites, species, interactions, functions), so a union of conditions rather than one scalar. Symmetry: not symmetric across slots; valence is relative to the residual (Fig 11 :49). Scale: local action to third-order cascade (Fig 11 :52). Fig 9 names it a compound: a case in two conditions is not a harder case of either.
- **Domain 1, the habitat.** Actors: species, conditions (light, water, temperature, season, terrain), relations (pollination, predation, competition, dependency). Currency: contact and cascade. Anchor: measured ecological state. Question type: enumeration, never composition; a union of direct contact, cascade and asymmetry.
- **Domain 2, the budget and acceptance rule.** Actors: candidate actions, the library, live slot residuals. Currency: two incommensurate costs, bits for description and actions for testing. Anchor: what the ground pays. Question type: partition of candidates into held, novel, misattached, mechanism-broken and refuted.
- **Domain 3, verification and monitoring.** Actors: managers, monitors, expert elicitors, independent methods. Currency: agreement and residual. Anchor: the ground, never agreement. Question type: triangulation, not negotiation. In this simulated run all seats draw on one evidence pool, so their agreement is one pool talking, not corroboration.
- **Domain 4, dependency order.** Actors: perception, vocabulary, objective, planning, learn and carry. Currency: a route under incomplete information. Anchor: the broken link, if it has an instrument. Ordered by dependency, not importance.
- **Order by dependency.** Enumerate the habitat before pricing actions; settle the objective's instrument before ranking actions; refuse a held assumption before minting a new one (formula step 2); describe the residual before choosing a frame (Fig 9 :27-28).

Strain test applied: Fig 12's bits-and-actions pair and the conservation literature's money-and-knowledge pair are different objects, so no strain after re-census. Fig 11's addition-only asymmetry and conservation triage are also different objects; the ethical disagreement stays in the world pool. Both re-censuses are logged in `01` §7.

## VERDICT (from the figures)

The shape survives; a priority list does not. Prioritization is per slot, with live residuals shown and never averaged; two costs are held in separate accounts; the habitat is enumerated outward to the cascade and re-derived when the residual changes; cost is a filter and never a witness; independent frames range against a ground that does not update; an action is accepted by the bargain cost(φ) + left(R,φ) < cost(R), and the assumption to refuse is the one with a persistent, non-trending residual. The broken middle link of the management chain is the objective's instrument, not the selection algorithm. This is a `specified`, ungrounded result. A concrete list is `unreachable-at-this-budget`, stated plainly rather than filled.

- **cost if wrong:** bounded and locatable. The rule is mode-labelled, states refuters before the conclusion and records the lineage, so an error is findable. The real cost is spending the independence of outside readers by sending a plan instead of a generator, which Fig 2 :42 prices: "Buy independence in proportion to the cost of being wrong." That cost is avoidable by leading with the rule and its five refuters.
- **undoable:** yes. The five parts are separable and testable one at a time. A rejected part can be withdrawn without touching the others; no ecological primitive has to be abandoned. A circulated claim can be superseded by a corrected packet, and every cut stays ranked and reversible (Fig 9 :41).
- **reversed by:** a ground reading, not agreement. A run on a specified habitat with an instrument that discriminates causes and a measured outcome would pay for the rule or refute it. A lossless aggregate score, a cost conversion preserving the budget decision, an outcome settled without a ground, a direct-contact-only plan that avoids a third-order failure, or a refusal-free list that keeps working as conditions change would each break a named part. Fig 8 :39 holds: a search that finds nothing is not proof of absence, so none of this is a claim that no good list is possible.
