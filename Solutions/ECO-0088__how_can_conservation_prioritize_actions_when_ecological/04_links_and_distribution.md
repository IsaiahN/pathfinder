## 3. Distribution: offer, do not push

- **Fig 4 :22-25:** "Only methods cross up. Only head starts come down." A recording of one plan sent before anyone has described a live residual is a description of one occasion, not a generator. Lead with the five-part rule and its refuter.
- **Fig 10 :42:** "A verifier that can reconstruct the claim cannot verify it." A recipient who has absorbed the whole framework before being asked for a verdict has joined the frame being checked.
- **Fig 9 :27-28:** "describe it before you go looking, or any frame you pick will seem to fit." Ask what they would expect to see if the rule were true before giving them the article.
- **Fig 2 :35:** "Alignment is triangulation, not negotiation. A mirror cannot correct you." Do not select recipients for likely agreement.

**Recommended order in a reply:** (1) the shape claim and its five refuters; (2) ask what they would count as a failure in their own system; (3) record the verdict; (4) only then, if they are curious, the three links in `02_outreach_email.md`, with the note that the figures, not the summary, are the authority.

## 4. The two-stage offer, restated

Stage 1 is the rule and the refuter, checked against the recipient's own ground. Stage 2 is opt-in: the sources follow only if the recipient asks. Stage 3 offers the formula as a method to reuse, with the instruction that each user names a ground and writes a refuter before running it. A later agreement is never pooled as corroboration (Fig 2 :40). This agent does not send anything. Hosting and sending are yours to do; prefer an opt-in download or a public submission route over an attachment pushed to a list.

## 5. What would make us stop

If a stage-1 recipient returns a refutation that fires any of the five refuters, the rule is repaired or withdrawn, the change is logged in `01` §7 and `11_audit.md`, and the packet is not circulated further until re-validated. Prestige or agreement is not an instrument (Recursive Self-Improvement Trap). A failed check is the desired outcome.
