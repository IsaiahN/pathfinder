## Round trip gap R_T

The Symbols table defines R_T as the gap between what was sent up and what came back down, `gap(x, (T_E ∘ T_A)(x))`, measuring what the coarser description cannot hold. Fig 4 :30 requires it to be measured rather than assumed. I ran the round trip on the condensation the folder actually uses.

1. **Sent up (x):** the verbatim figure set, chiefly Fig 1 :19, :22 (slot indexing), Fig 12 :125-127 (two currencies), Fig 9 :60 (filters), Fig 11 :43-44 (enumeration), Fig 2 :45 (ground).
2. **Abstraction (T_A):** the five-part shorthand in `07`: per slot; two accounts; enumerate outward; filters not witnesses; independent frames against a ground.
3. **Concretisation (T_E):** a plausible concrete plan, for example "rank the ten candidate sites by a weighted score, spend the survey budget this quarter, fence the top two, report progress annually."

**R_T is nonzero.** Four things the shorthand holds do not survive the trip back down:

- **Slot identity.** A ten-site weighted score is an aggregate; the live slot that Fig 1 :22 says disappears into an average is gone in the concrete plan.
- **The budget boundary in each currency.** A single "survey plus fencing" line item collapses bits and actions, which Fig 12 :130 names as the error.
- **Cascade depth.** "Rank the ten sites" does not carry the second- and third-order contacts required by Fig 11 :44; it is first-order enumeration.
- **Reversibility.** "Report progress annually" is not the same as keeping every cut ranked and reversible (Fig 9 :41), because a cut that has been applied is not automatically revisable.

Conversely, one thing survives cleanly: cost is a filter and the ground decides whether an action worked, provided the annual report contains a measurement that can fail. R_T was **not** measured numerically, because no habitat and no ground reading exist; it is recorded qualitatively as the enumerated set of dropped constraints. Treating "the five-part rule is the condensed version of the figures" as identity would set R_T to zero and would be the dead-reckoning error Fig 4 :30 names. The honest form is a lossy condensation with a recorded gap, which is what `09_watson_answer.md` states.
