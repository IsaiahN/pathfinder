# Outreach draft: check the output before you decide whether the rule is worth reading

**Use:** one-to-one, or as a cover letter attached to a submission through the public venues in `03_public_contact_points.csv`. **Not** for bulk send.

**Hard precondition:** do not send until the five refuters in `01_figure_grounded_audit.md` §4 are written into any summary you circulate. The shape must survive validation or the two-stage offer collapses on contact.

**Subject:** Check one output before you decide whether the prioritization rule is worth reading

> Hi [name],
>
> I have one specific output I would like you to check against what you already know about conservation decisions. I am not asking you to read the framework first, and I am not asking you to agree with it. If the output does not survive, there is nothing else to look at.
>
> **The output (`specified`, not `grounded`):** the figures' minimum set treats every decision as a gap between a prediction and an observation, indexed by object slot, never by a single global number. Applied here, a site, a species, an interaction and an ecosystem function are separate slots, each with its own live residual. From that follows a prioritization rule with five parts:
>
> 1. keep the residual per slot and refuse to sum the slots into one score;
> 2. keep two costs in separate accounts: bits for describing and modelling an action, actions for finding out whether it works. They are in different units and the budget boundary is set in each;
> 3. enumerate the habitat outward from the residual to second- and third-order contacts, and re-derive the action list when the residual changes;
> 4. use cost, feasibility and political acceptability as filters for what gets tried, never as witnesses for whether an action worked;
> 5. range independent checks (field experiment, monitoring, remote sensing, local and Indigenous knowledge, models) separately against a measured outcome that does not move because the reviewers agree.
>
> This is the **shape of a rule**, not a priority list and not an approved action. I am not saying which species or site to save. The framework returns a kind of thing, not the answer itself, and a concrete list is out of reach until a habitat and a live measurement are supplied.
>
> **What would refute it:**
> 1. a single score across slots that preserves everything the per-slot residual tells you about where to act;
> 2. a conversion of description cost into action cost that leaves the budget boundary and the chosen actions unchanged;
> 3. expert agreement settling which action worked with no independent ground reading;
> 4. a plan scoped to direct contact that still avoids a documented third-order cascade failure;
> 5. a fixed ranked list with no refusal step that reliably reduces live residuals as conditions change.
>
> I wrote these before sending this. If any holds, I will retract or repair the rule. A one-line "this fails because..." is more useful to me than agreement. If you would rather not engage, no reply is needed.
>
> **If it survives, and only then:** the sources are below. The figures are the authority, not my summary of them.
> - Canonical figures and formula (SVG, branch `general-agent`): https://github.com/IsaiahN/tether/tree/general-agent/figures
> - Conservation Evidence, documented evidence for the effectiveness of conservation actions: https://www.conservationevidence.com/
> - McCarthy 2014, open review of uncertainty methods in conservation decisions: https://pmc.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/articles/PMC4312896/
>
> **If you want to use the machinery:** the formula is in the figures link. Run it on a decision you know well and can check against a ground. Write down what would refute your result *before* you run it, and state the mode of what you get. A reported failure is more useful to me than a reported success.
>
> [your name]

## One-to-one rule

Send to one person at a time through a public institutional channel or a venue that has opted in. Do not pool later agreement as corroboration (Fig 2 :40). A reply that returns a refutation is copied into `11_audit.md` with provenance and re-evaluated by the auditor seat as if it came from any other source. No follow-up is sent to someone who does not reply.
