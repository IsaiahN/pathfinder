## QUESTION

Decide in effect terms what the Tether says about contracting out a public function. Option A: treat "does it improve performance" and "does it weaken accountability" as one trade-off and report a net effect. Option B: split the compound into two predicates against two different anchors, derive a verdict for each, and record the gaps. What each leads to: A aggregates away the per-case signal and hands the checker a finished frame; B returns two checkable conditions, refuters written in advance, and an honest stop. The question's own wording is the trap, because "improve performance" and "weaken accountability" are two conditions sharing one service, not one harder question.

## DOMAIN CENSUS

R by arity, symmetry and scale, then one line per domain with actors / currency / anchor, partition or union, and order by dependency.

- **R (the residual).** A public function fails to meet its outcome and a change of provider or procurement is proposed. Arity: two predicates against one intervention, so a **union compound** (Fig 9 :57-58: "a case in two conditions is not a harder case of either. It stops being a mixture and becomes a specific compound"). Symmetry: asymmetric, since introduction is available and exclusion is not (Fig 11 :61: "The seat's power is asymmetric. Introduction is available; exclusion is not."). Scale: first order at service delivery, second order at oversight.
- **Domain 1, performance (capability / contact).** Actors: the function, provider, users, outcome. Currency: contact with the ground. Anchor: the service outcome, which does not move when a frame pushes on it (Fig 1 :23: "The ground enters as the only metric."). Question type: partition boundary (what changes contact, what only relabels the actor).
- **Domain 2, accountability (ground reachability).** Actors: public principal, contractor, oversight, metric, users. Currency: whether a per-case failure stays detectable and correctable. Anchor: the ground, which does not update (Fig 10 :24: "the ground does not decay, the channel does."). Question type: partition boundary (what keeps the channel intact, what lets a convention decide the outcome).
- **Domain 3, the contracting mechanism.** No independent anchor. It is settled only by D1's outcome and D2's reachability, so it is not admitted as a domain; it gets a loop inside each. Treating it as a domain with one effect is the census error this answer refuses.
- **Order by dependency.** Refuse before mint (Formula step 2); establish contact before attributing a differential outcome; keep the ground reachable while judging; describe the residual before picking a frame (Fig 9 :27-28).

Strain test applied: competition (Fig 7) and import (Fig 6) looked like two answers to one question. Re-census: Fig 7 governs selection among existing providers; Fig 6 governs arrival of a capability the closure lacks. Different objects, no strain. The market-as-anchor line (Fig 2 :29) and the convention-that-decides line (Fig 10 :47) were similarly re-censused as two conditions on one instrument, not a conflict.

## TETHER (primary, verbatim)

Cited against `FRAMEWORK/reference/03-FIGURES_TEXT.md`. Quotes are verbatim. Verdicts are per domain and are never averaged.

**Domain 1, performance.** `triangulated` across Fig 11, Fig 7 and Fig 2 (low overlap between the capability claim, the room-selection rule and the anchor definition).

- Fig 11 :39 "capability is a property of agent-and-habitat, never of the agent alone." -> an ownership label is not a capability change.
- Fig 11 :40 "A system cannot be made more capable by improving the agent in isolation. An improvement that does not change contact changes nothing, however much it improves." -> the criterion is contact, not who performs the function.
- Fig 7 :35 "A room's agreement is variation, not a verdict. Selection between rooms is where the ground votes." -> contestability is the operative mechanism.
- Fig 7 :36 "A chain of mirrors is one long room. The next room must differ from the last, and match the residual it surfaced." -> a sole incumbent is motion without progress.
- Fig 2 :29-30 "An anchor is whatever settles the question and does not change its answer because of what any frame thinks. A measurement, an experiment, a counter, a market, a patient outcome. If it moves when you push on it, it is not an anchor." -> a self-reported figure is not an anchor; an outcome that does not move is.
- Fig 11 :72 "an agent-only improvement that moved the ground would refute the capability claim." -> the figures supply the refuter themselves; the verdict stays `candidate`.
- **D1 verdict (`specified`):** contracting improves performance when it changes contact with the outcome, through a new method or through selection among independent rooms. Changing the actor alone does not establish improvement.

**Domain 2, accountability.** `triangulated` across Fig 10, Fig 11 and Fig 1 (low overlap between reachability, habitat substitution and per-slot residual rules).

- Fig 10 :24 "the ground does not decay, the channel does." -> a longer contract chain has more places to lose the ground.
- Fig 10 :47 "a convention that decides an outcome has stopped being one." -> a target substituted for the outcome is no longer a measurement.
- Fig 11 :66 "Isolation is not removal of the habitat. It is substitution of one habitat for another." -> outsourcing substitutes a habitat, it does not simply remove one.
- Fig 11 :67 "Move the residual and you must reproduce everything its goal required, and you inherit the new habitat's actors, which you did not choose." -> goal-required accountability conditions must be reproduced.
- Fig 11 :68 "what you failed to reproduce is invisible until the goal fails." -> the failure mode is silent until it bites.
- Fig 1 :22 "Aggregating across slots is how a live signal disappears into an average." -> per-case residuals must stay visible.
- Fig 12 :119 "catalyst. A part that survives its own use. Spent and regenerating parts behave differently across uses, and nothing records which a term is." -> an oversight body must be checked across uses, not merely exist.
- **D2 verdict (`specified`):** accountability weakens when the channel decays, a target decides the outcome, the substitution fails to reproduce goal-required conditions, or per-case failures are averaged away. It is not weakened by the actor change as such.

**Method layer.** `single-source` for the union rule (Fig 9), corroborated structurally by Fig 3's dependency order and Fig 12's isomer definition. Two sub-questions are `gap`: the distributional weight between cost and access, and the legal enumeration of delegable duties. No figure line governs either, and "Unreachable is a verdict, not a silence."

## VERDICT (from the figures)

Keep the pair, refuse the net. D1: contracting improves performance only when it changes contact with the outcome, with contestability and low overlap as the operative mechanism and the measurement itself qualified as an anchor; an ownership label alone does not establish improvement. D2: accountability weakens when the channel decays, a target decides the outcome, the habitat substitution fails to reproduce goal-required conditions, or per-case failures are averaged away; the actor change as such is not the cause. Two sub-questions are `gap`: distributional weighting and legal delegability. The capability claim is `candidate` because Fig 11 says it "have not been tested forward, so they can still come back wrong." This is a `specified`, ungrounded result. A list of agreeing researchers is not a ground.

- **cost if wrong:** bounded and locatable, because the packet states its mode, its two anchors and three refuters before the conclusion, so an error can be found rather than absorbed in a net score. The larger cost is spending a checker's independence by sending a finished frame instead of a residual, which Fig 2 :42 prices: "Buy independence in proportion to the cost of being wrong." That cost is avoidable by leading with the conditions and the refuters.
- **undoable:** yes. The two verdicts can be revised independently without touching the derivation. No primitive is withdrawn if D1 fails, and no D2 finding depends on D1 being right. A sent claim can be superseded by a corrected packet. The gaps can be filled by other frames without disturbing the figure-derived shape.
- **reversed by:** a ground reading, not agreement. A fixed-contact provider-label comparison that reliably moves the outcome would break D1; a per-case audit showing every failure stays detectable and correctable with a metric that cannot absorb it would break D2; a single out-of-sample scalar predicting both anchors would collapse the union. Fig 11 :72 names the first of these. Until one fires, both verdicts stand as `candidate`.
