## 1. A shape is a constraint, not a filler

The figures README states the limit: the framework returns "what a missing thing would have to look like (a shape, not a name)." Fig 9 :32: "you get a kind of thing, never the answer itself." Fig 13: "It can say the answer is out of reach with the library as it stands... It cannot say which primitive is missing, because naming it would mean already holding it."

From *The Library of Babel*: "You can cut your search short if the set of books returned is small and descriptive enough that it provides a general shape of the answer that satisfies your question."

So a **shape** is the narrowed form of an answer you can state before you can name the specific instrument, contract or jurisdiction that fills it. For INS-0018 the shape is a pair of conditions with separate anchors, not a verdict on any particular privatization. "Privatization improves performance" is a name, a claim about a specific instrument; "performance follows contact, not ownership, and accountability follows the reachability of the ground" is a shape.

## 2. How the shape was obtained here

- *Library of Babel*: use the vocabulary you already have closest to the thing, then look for new terms that overlap or correlate with it. "With each new term gained, you are able to narrow the space by definition and deepen your vocabulary." The same essay gives the exact move this question forced: "Perhaps your problem was a bundle or conflation of several problems masquerading as one? You will need to search all of those problems individually." That is the split of performance and accountability into two domains in `01`.
- Fig 6 :30: "The description travels. Fails by being sharpened into terms only we can read. Cast it in effect terms." Fig 9 :30-31: "your words are borrowed too, so describe the effect rather than the cause." Hence "contact with the ground" and "reachability of the ground" rather than "market incentives" and "oversight structures."
- *Decoding Tacit Knowledge*: "When contact isn't possible, the best you can send is a plain description of what you'd expect to see, because anyone can check that against the world." This is why the outreach email asks what a checker would expect to see rather than asking them to endorse a theory.
- *Library of Babel* on stopping: "you will know that you are done because the finish line will also be the starting place, as there is nowhere else to search and nothing else that can describe the answer better." Here the stop is earlier and more honest: the residual stops registering on the two anchors, and the two gaps (distributional weight, legal delegability) are recorded as `gap` rather than filled.

## 3. What licenses a name in this question

Only two things, both outside the shape itself.

1. **The ground settles it.** Formula step 5: "the ground settles it. Verified only by what pays. A gate is not the ground." For INS-0018 that means a measured service outcome on D1 and a demonstrated correctability of per-case failures on D2.
2. **A provenanced import from a frame whose closure differs** (Fig 6 :23, Fig 8 :51-52), with a source gate and a shadow test. A management method imported from a private frame into public delivery is licensed only if it explains a gap the public frame had, and only if the record says where it came from. Fig 8 :51-52: "Convergent derivation and adopted import look identical in the contents. Only the record of where each came from separates them."

*The Hard Problem is About Source* gives the instrument point that governs this: "If several paths create the same behavior, then behavior is a projection with lossy information." A contract that reports a clean metric can have several sources: real improvement, quality shading, creaming, or gaming. The reading does not determine the source. The essay's resolution is instrumental, not argumentative: "The answer is unreached until a better instrument exists. It was proven wrong not by argument, but by the arrival of a better instrument." And: "An instrument that can't distinguish these cases demonstrates its own inadequacy." That is the figure-grounded reason F5 treats a target as a possible failure mode rather than as evidence.

## 4. Why the net verdict is the specific forbidden error

*The Hard Problem is About Source* supplies the mechanism: a reading that cannot distinguish sources cannot tell you what produced it. An aggregate "net performance and accountability" score is the same lossy projection applied to two anchors. Fig 1 :19: "The residual is indexed by object slot, not aggregated." Fig 1 :22: "Aggregating across slots is how a live signal disappears into an average."

*Decoding Tacit Knowledge* supplies the drift warning: two frames that only check against each other drift together, and "a shared mindset lets two people drift together without either one noticing." The same essay names the safeguards that matter here: "Courts of law rule on evidence, which neither side can move." That is Fig 2's anchor in an institutional costume, and it is why F4 and F5 are about the ground staying reachable rather than about how many oversight bodies exist.

*The Recursive Self-Improvement Trap*: "You may all agree and be wrong... You need ground truth to define where you stand." A literature review in which every paper says contracting works is one pool talking, not a verdict.

## 5. Q: What shape should a public answer to INS-0018 have?

**Answer.** It should be a pair of condition statements, each with its own anchor and its own refuter, plus an explicit list of what is not determined. Performance: a change in provider improves the function only if it changes contact with the outcome, with contestability and low overlap as the operative mechanism, and with the measurement itself qualified as an anchor. Accountability: the change weakens accountability when the channel decays, a target decides the outcome, or the habitat substitution fails to reproduce the conditions the original habitat supplied, with per-case residuals left visible. The framework does not name which contract, which provider or which jurisdiction satisfies these conditions. Those are bindings from other frames, provenanced as such.

**Does this solution commit the error the README forbids?** No name is asserted where the framework returns a shape. The one apparent name, "contestability," is a structural condition (selection between rooms, Fig 7 :35-36), not a named instrument. Where a specific instrument appears, it appears only in the world literature as corroboration, never as the framework's output. The two gaps are left open rather than filled by a plausibility story.

## 5b. Correction to my own §5

I initially wrote that the framework "cannot name the mechanism" and that contestability was therefore an illicit name. That overstates the rule. The README's "shape, not a name" governs a primitive not held in the library. Selection between rooms is held in the library (Fig 7 :35-36), so naming it is a binding onto a held structural term, not the invention of a new one. What remains forbidden is naming a *specific* contract form, firm or jurisdiction as the framework's answer, and claiming that such a binding moves the wall (Fig 6 :21: "MINT composes inside the closure and can never add an atom"). The correction is kept here rather than silently applied. Triangulated: README + Fig 7 + Fig 6.

## 6. Two consequences for outreach, and the standing of this audit

**Prestige is not action.** From *The Recursive Self-Improvement Trap*: "Prestige can fund an action. It can give an action a budget to be tried. But it cannot settle an action. Only the ground can settle it. The Ground Maintainer must be blind to prestige." A list of well-known privatization researchers who agree with the shape is the named collapse, not corroboration. The legitimate ask is a budget to try the claim against an outcome, and an expert's account of what would refute it.

**This audit is a simulation, not a ground reading.** Same essay: "LLMs can quickly simulate an approximate answer/output... However, the truly testable output would need to be derived from the real architecture or working the formula yourself." And the essay calls such output "simulacra." So `01_figure_grounded_audit.md`, this file and every other file here are simulated derivations checked for internal consistency against the figures. They are not tests of privatization policy and cannot settle anything they report on. That is why the mode is `specified`, not `grounded`, throughout.
