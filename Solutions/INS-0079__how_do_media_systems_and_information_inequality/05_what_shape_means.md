## 1. A shape is a constraint, not a filler

The figures README states the limit: the framework returns "what a missing thing would have to look
like (a shape, not a name)". Fig 9 :32: "you get a kind of thing, never the answer itself." Fig 13 :134:
"Unreachable is a verdict here, not a silence," and the README adds that "Indiscernibility is not
identity."

From *The Library of Babel*: "This means that you can cut your search short if the set of books
returned is small and descriptive enough that it provides a general shape of the answer that satisfies
your question." The same essay gives the warning that decides this question: "Perhaps your problem was
a bundle or conflation of several problems masquerading as one?"

So a **shape** is the narrowed form of an answer you can state before you can name it. For INS-0079
the shape is the union separator: channel to a non-updating record, discriminating distribution,
and a refusal-capable checking seat, indexed per accountability slot. A universal coefficient, a
media-freedom threshold and a standing list of good outlets are fillers, not shapes.

## 2. How a shape is obtained

- *Library of Babel*: use the vocabulary already closest to the thing, then look for recurring new
  terms that overlap or correlate with it. "With each new term gained, you are able to narrow the
  space by definition and deepen your vocabulary." Applied here: start with "answerability",
  "exposure" and "sanction", then let "anchor", "channel", "independence", "weighting" and
  "refusal" narrow the space.
- Fig 9 :30–31: "your words are borrowed too, so describe the effect rather than the cause." Fig 6 :30:
  "The description travels. Fails by being sharpened into terms only we can read. Cast it in effect
  terms." So the first contact with a media researcher is "what would have to be true for a failure
  of power to be detected and sanctioned", not "which figure governs this".
- *Decoding Tacit Knowledge* (carried from the exemplar's canonical mapping): "When contact isn't
  possible, the best you can send is a plain description of what you'd expect to see, because anyone
  can check that against the world."
- *Library of Babel* on stopping: "you will know that you are done because the finish line will also
  be the starting place", and the paradox is that by then "you have already composed the answer
  through the process of locating it."

## 3. What licenses a name

Only two things, both outside the shape itself:

1. **The ground settles it.** `THE_FORMULA.md` step 5: "the ground settles it. Verified only by what
   pays." The README: the framework "stops at the limit of what the available instruments can
   distinguish, and records the lineage so the stop is honest."
2. **A provenanced import from a frame whose closure differs** (Fig 6 :23, Fig 8 :51), with a source
   gate and a shadow test.

*They're Scanning Books* (carried from the exemplar's mapping) sharpens this: "The real import is not
a new term in the existing space. It is a new dimension of the space." And: "Declaring the primitives
does not make them neutral. It makes the choice visible." Naming "independent record" or "refusal
seat" is therefore a binding from the world frame onto the shape, stamped with its origin, not a
discovery of a seventh kind.

## 4. Why "more information" is the specific forbidden error here

*The Hard Problem is About Source* gives the mechanism, and it was re-opened in full this session.
Metamerism: an instrument reads green and "cannot comment on the source",  one green light, or
yellow plus blue, or RGB, or CMYK, or a scheme not yet discovered. "If several paths create the same
behavior, then behavior is a projection with lossy information." And: "An instrument that can't
distinguish these cases demonstrates its own inadequacy." It was the spectrometer, not an argument,
that resolved it.

Applied here: a claim that publication volume rose, or that a newspaper was founded, is a reading of
the same kind. Several sources can produce it: a free press, a captured outlet serving influence, a
platform subsidy, a state information campaign. The reading does not determine the source, and the
accountability verdict needs a discriminator. What is owed is a better instrument (per-slot
discernment and sanction), not a larger number.

*The Recursive Self-Improvement Trap*, re-opened in full: "the output is only as good as the
validator. The agent, or the tool, or the frame, or the theory, cannot validate itself."

## 5. Correction to my own §3 and §4 (kept visible)

I initially wrote "shape, not a name" as if it forbade naming any media mechanism. That is an
overstatement. The README's phrase is "what a **missing** thing would have to look like (a shape, not
a name)". It governs a primitive not held in Γ. An independent record, a channel, a weighting and a
refusal seat are **bindings** from the political-science, ecology and economics frames onto the
existing vocabulary. Naming them is legitimate provenance, not the invention of a new primitive. What
remains wrong when a source names a mechanism off a single reading (volume, outlet count) is the
missing discriminator of §4, not a ban on naming held concepts. Both claims are retained so neither
is quietly lost.

## 6. Two consequences for outreach

**Prestige is not action.** *The Recursive Self-Improvement Trap*, re-opened this session: "Prestige
must never be allowed to validate an action directly... Someone let prestige stand in for proof...
Prestige can fund an action. It can give an action a budget to be tried. But it cannot settle an
action. Only the ground can settle it. The Ground Maintainer must be blind to prestige." A list of
well-known journals to agree with the shape is the named collapse. The legitimate ask gives the
claim a budget to be tried and lets a ground settle it.

**My own audit is a simulation, not a ground reading.** Same essay: "LLMs can quickly simulate an
approximate answer/output with the formula... However, the truly testable output would need to be
derived from the real architecture or working the formula yourself"; the output is "simulacra". `01`
is therefore a simulated derivation checked for internal consistency against the figures, not a test
of the question, and it cannot settle anything it reports on.
