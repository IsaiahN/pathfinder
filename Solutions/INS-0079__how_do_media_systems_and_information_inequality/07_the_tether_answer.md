# The Tether's answer on media systems, information inequality and democratic accountability

**Mode: `specified` on the per-domain findings, `general` on the structural shape, `gap` on any
universal coefficient or named institutional design. Not `grounded`.** Simulated derivation checked
for internal consistency against the figures (`05_what_shape_means.md` §6), not a ground reading.

## 1. The residual it answers

A polity has formal answerability: elections, oversight, courts, audit. Yet failures of power often go
undetected, unnamed or unsanctioned. The residual is the gap between formal answerability and actual
detection and correction, indexed per named institution, issue and public. Fig 1 :19: "The residual is
indexed by object slot, not aggregated." It is a **union compound**, not one hard question: Fig 9 :57,
"a case in two conditions is not a harder case of either."

## 2. The shape

1. **Three conditions, not one lever.** A channel to a non-updating record (D1), a distribution that
   lets the relevant public discriminate it (D2), and a checking seat able to refuse without profiting
   from its reading (D3). Conditions overlap; one lever clears only the region where its condition
   alone binds. Fig 9 :55: "A bundled problem is usually a union of overlapping conditions, not a list
   of separate ones." Verdicts are kept per domain and never averaged.
2. **The media system is a channel, not the ground.** Fig 2 :29: "An anchor is whatever settles the
   question and does not change its answer because of what any frame thinks." The act does not change
   when coverage does; Fig 10 :24: "the ground does not decay, the channel does." Press freedom is a
   channel condition (Freedom of the press "contributes to the reduction of corruption by exposing
   corrupt actions", per the *Accountability* source), not an anchor and not a verdict.
3. **Plurality is independence, not headcount.** Fig 2 :31: "Independence, between two frames, is how
   little they draw on the same evidence." Fig 7 :35: "A room's agreement is variation, not a verdict.
   Selection between rooms is where the ground votes." Outlets sharing owners, wires, platform ranking
   or messaging are "one long room" (Fig 7 :36). Buy independence where the cost of being wrong is
   highest (Fig 2 :42).
4. **Information inequality is a weighting and support condition.** The Symbols table defines `w` as
   how far a belief leans on private history versus inherited prior, and says two frames with the
   same architecture diverge because histories and `w` diverge. The knowledge-gap source reports that
   "mass media amplifies this gap to another level" and that pre-existing disparities decide who
   benefits most from exposure. Equal access does not equal equal benefit; volume is a budget filter,
   not a witness (Fig 9 :60).
5. **The checking seat must be unable to profit from its reading.** Fig 10 :93: "A ground with a
   reputation to protect is a ground someone benefits from, and its keeper is thereby disqualified."
   Fig 10 :59: "an interest in the outcome is unavoidable, an interest in the reading is disqualifying."
   The principal-agent source names the structure: officials are agents, citizens are principals, and
   the problem worsens with information asymmetry and a weak means to punish.
6. **The chain breaks between channel and objective.** Fig 3 :20: "Optimising a link that already
   works cannot rescue one that does not." The middle link, what a situation affords and what would
   count as progress, has no instrument (Fig 3 :27). Sanction reform above a broken channel is motion
   without progress.
7. **Over a fixed atom set, media regimes are isomers.** Fig 12 :117: "isomer. Same atoms, different
   bond, different substance. The difference is invisible in the formula and decisive in the
   behaviour." A public-service channel and a distributed network of small outlets can carry the same
   four atoms and behave differently. Without the fixed set they are only a functional group (Fig 12
   :124). No standing list of good outlets survives a change of residual (Fig 11 :51).
8. **No scalar is returned.** Fig 9 :32: "you get a kind of thing, never the answer itself." No figure
   line returns a universal percentage by which media systems and information inequality change
   accountability quality. That is a declared `gap`, a verdict and not a silence (Fig 13 :134).

## 3. What it explicitly does not say

- Not that press freedom is the ground of accountability. It is a channel.
- Not that more outlets entail more independence, or that more information entails more accountability.
- Not that any named medium is good or bad regardless of the residual being traced (Fig 11 :49).
- Not that a national accountability score can carry a per-slot verdict (Fig 1 :22).
- Not that the framework can name the institution that will work. It returns a shape; the office is a
  provenanced binding from a world frame.
- Not `grounded`. No measurement of detection and sanction was taken.

## 4. What would settle it :  the ground

A discriminator, not a further index. From *The Hard Problem is About Source*: an instrument that reads
green "cannot comment on the source"; the spectrometer resolved it. Here the corresponding questions
are:

- For a named slot, does the channel actually reach a record that does not move when the subjects of
  the report push on it? The Fig 10 :93 test is role-relative: whose interest does the keeper serve,
  and does the reading change with that interest?
- Do two frames drawn from genuinely different evidence pools converge on the same failure, rather
  than agreeing because one source fed both (Fig 2 :31, :40; Fig 8 :51)?
- Can the middle link be measured: does discernment of the failure actually precede a sanction, or
  only a approval-rating movement (the operational test in the *Accountability* source)?
- Does equalising access change who benefits from exposure, or only how many people are exposed
  (the Mondak and Anderson result as reported in the knowledge-gap source)?
- Standing falsifiers from `01` §5: one latent variable that clears all intersections; a national score
  moving with every slot chain intact; a media metric settling a contest with the act held fixed;
  access equalisation closing the gap with weighting fixed; a stake-holding keeper settling a contest;
  volume alone moving a ground verdict.

## 5. The honest stop

**Unreached, not unreachable.** The four-atom core is a binding assembled from political science,
ecology and economics, not a discovery of nature's list. Declaring it makes the choice visible, not
neutral ("They're Scanning Books", carried from the exemplar's mapping). Another discipline may hold a
frame whose closure predicts the shape more tightly, and importing it costs independence (Fig 6 :24).
Fig 8 :39: "So a search that finds nothing is never a proof of absence." The answer stops at the limit
of the available instruments and records the lineage so the stop is checkable. Indiscernibility is not
identity.

The live type question: in a polity where every record is authored, can anything occupy the anchor
seat? The answer here is **role-relative and time-bounded**: a record is an anchor for a given frame
and a given contest if it does not move when that frame pushes on it, and the keeper has no stake in
the reading. Fig 10 :93 makes this an operational test, not a property. This is stated openly rather
than smoothed into a claim that free media are the ground.

## 6. One-paragraph version for outreach

> The Tether does not return a percentage for how media systems and information inequality change
> democratic accountability. It returns a shape. The question is a union of three overlapping
> conditions: a channel to a record that does not move when the powerful push on it, a distribution of
> information that lets the relevant public discriminate that record, and a checking seat able to
> refuse without profiting from its own reading. Media systems are that channel, not the ground, and
> plurality counts as independence rather than as outlet count; information inequality is a weighting
> and support condition rather than a volume condition. The chain usually breaks between channel and
> objective, where there is no instrument. Over a fixed set of atoms, two different media regimes can
> be isomers, same structure arranged differently and decisive in behaviour. No standing list of good
> outlets and no universal score is licensed. What is offered is the separator, the per-slot indexing,
> the instruments that would close it, and six refuters written in advance. The center stays open
> until a ground settles it.
