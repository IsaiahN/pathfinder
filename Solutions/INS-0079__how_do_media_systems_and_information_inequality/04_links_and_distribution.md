## Wider literature that bears on the question

1. **Mass communication / information inequality.** The knowledge-gap and digital-divide entries are
   one broad evidence pool, not two independent witnesses. Their shared finding is that exposure is
   not uniformly beneficial: pre-existing disparities decide who benefits. This corroborates F5 and
   F9 at the mechanism-shape level, not the institutional verdict.
2. **Political economy / institutional theory.** *Media capture* and *Principal–agent problem* share
   an economics and institutional-theory pool (Besley and Prat appear in the former; Jensen and
   Meckling in the latter). Their convergence on "information asymmetry plus weak punishment" is
   corroboration inside one pool, not two witnesses. It is the genuinely low-overlap check against
   the ecology source and the cognition essays.
3. **Ecology.** Wanless, Wardle and Scales is the one source from a natural-science field. Its claim
   that structure precedes dynamics and that threshold claims need a bounded system and a fixed unit
   of analysis is what licenses the per-slot indexing of F2 and the habitat enumeration of F8.
4. **Journalism studies.** *Fourth estate* supplies the benchler diversity-plus-irresponsibility
   tension that stops F10 from becoming a standing list of good outlets.
5. **Canonical framework essays.** Three essays were opened this session: *The Hard Problem is About
   Source*, *The Recursive Self-Improvement Trap*, and *The Library of Babel: Locating The Book of
   Books*. All are by the framework author, so their agreement with the figures is maximal-overlap
   internal consistency, not world corroboration. The other five were not re-opened and are marked as
   carried from the exemplar's canonical mapping in `05_what_shape_means.md`.

No world source conflicts with the figure verdict after re-census, so the conflict rule was not
triggered. Where a source could be read as conflicting (media capture is a systemic corruption
frame, not a ground-maintainer frame), re-census assigned the two to different domains: capture is a
channel failure in D1, the ground-keeper test is a D3 role. The verdict was not flipped.

## Distribution: offer, do not push

- **Fig 4 :22** :  "Only methods cross up. Only head starts come down." Sending the architecture before
  a reader has described a residual sends a recording, not a generator. Lead with the shape claim and
  its refuter; offer links afterwards.
- **Fig 10 :42** :  "A verifier that can reconstruct the claim cannot verify it." A recipient who has
  absorbed the whole framework before being asked for a verdict has joined the frame being checked.
- **Fig 9 :27–28** :  "describe it before you go looking, or any frame you pick will seem to fit." Ask
  what the reader would expect to see if the shape claim were true before giving them the ecology
  article.
- **Fig 2 :40** :  "disqualifying for a verdict: agreement about the world is worthless when the
  agreeing frames share an evidence pool." A send selected for people who already agree produces a
  convention, not corroboration.
- **Fig 11 :61** :  "The seat's power is asymmetric. Introduction is available; exclusion is not."
  An opt-in download page lets a recipient decline contact; an attachment pushed to a list does not.

**Recommended order in a reply:** (1) the shape claim and its six refuters; (2) ask what the reader
would count as a failure; (3) record their verdict before showing any theory; (4) only then the three
links, with the note that the figures, not the summary, are the authority; (5) offer the formula as a
method, with the instruction that each user writes their own refuter and states their own mode.

## The two-stage offer

**Stage 1.** Send the residual, the per-slot shape and the pre-registered refuter. Fig 10 :52:
"Bring a characterised residual, never the source." Fig 2 :45: "Frames range against the ground. They
do not negotiate with it."

**Stage 2, opt-in only.** If curious, offer the figures and formula. Seat 4 must not hold the
interiors; once a reader has absorbed the theory, agreement stops being evidence, so their stage-1
verdict must be recorded first and their later agreement is never pooled as corroboration (Fig 2 :40).

**Stage 3.** Offer the formula as a method. Fig 4 :22 permits method transfer. `THE_FORMULA.md`
"Direction" forbids a frame from checking the direction of its own question, so each user writes down
what would refute their result and states the mode of what they get.

**Hard precondition.** Stages 1 to 3 only work if stage 1 survives. The live type question in `01` §8,
whether an authored record can occupy the anchor seat, is stated role-relative in `07` and `08`. Do
not soften it into a property before sending.
