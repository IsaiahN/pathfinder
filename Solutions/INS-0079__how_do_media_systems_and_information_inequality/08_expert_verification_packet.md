# Expert verification packet :  the Tether on media systems, information inequality and accountability

**What this is:** a claim in a form a media or accountability researcher can check. Mode, derivation
and refuters are stated before the conclusion, so a reader knows what to look for and what would
settle it.

**Mode: `specified` on the sub-claims, `general` on the structural shape, `gap` on any universal
coefficient. Not `grounded`.** Simulated derivation checked for internal consistency against the
figures, not a ground reading. It cannot settle the question it reports on.

## 1. The claim, in one paragraph

Democratic accountability quality is a union of three overlapping conditions, not one lever with an
additive effect: a channel to a record that does not move when the powerful push on it, a
distribution of information that lets the relevant public discriminate that record, and a checking
seat able to refuse without profiting from its own reading. The effect must be indexed per named
institution, issue and public, never averaged into a national score. Media systems occupy the channel
seat, not the ground seat; plurality counts as independence of evidence, not as outlet count.
Information inequality is a weighting and support condition, not a volume condition, and publication
volume is a filter for the budget, not a witness for the verdict. The chain usually breaks between
channel and objective, where there is no instrument. Over a fixed atom set, different media regimes
can be isomers, same atoms arranged differently and decisive in behaviour. The framework returns no
universal percentage and no standing list of good outlets.

## 2. Three checkable sub-claims

### C1 :  framework-internal, checkable by reading Figure 9 and Figure 1

- **Statement:** the question is a union of overlapping conditions, and the residual is indexed per
  slot. A national accountability score cannot carry the verdict.
- **Source, verbatim:** Fig 9 :55 "A bundled problem is usually a union of overlapping conditions,
  not a list of separate ones." Fig 9 :57 "a case in two conditions is not a harder case of either."
  Fig 1 :19 "The residual is indexed by object slot, not aggregated." Fig 1 :22 "Aggregating across
  slots is how a live signal disappears into an average."
- **What to check:** is the intersection between media capture, unequal distribution and a stake-holding
  keeper non-empty in the cases you know? Does a national index ever move while a named slot stays
  broken?
- **Refuter:** show one latent variable such that one lever clears all three intersections with the
  intersections measured rather than assumed; or exhibit a failure visible only in the national
  average and invisible at every slot.

### C2 :  accountability-facing, checkable against a named institution

- **Statement:** an independent record occupies the anchor role, and the media system is the channel
  to it. Independence is how little the frames draw on the same evidence; headcount is not evidence.
- **Source, verbatim:** Fig 2 :29 "An anchor is whatever settles the question and does not change its
  answer because of what any frame thinks." Fig 10 :24 "the ground does not decay, the channel does".
  Fig 2 :31 "Independence, between two frames, is how little they draw on the same evidence." Fig 7 :35
  "A room's agreement is variation, not a verdict. Selection between rooms is where the ground votes."
  Fig 10 :93 "A ground with a reputation to protect is a ground someone benefits from, and its keeper
  is thereby disqualified."
- **What to check:** in an oversight case you know, does the record move when the subject pushes, and
  does the keeper have a stake in the reading? Are the outlets that "confirm" a finding actually
  drawing on separate evidence, or on one wire, owner or platform ranking?
- **Refuter:** name a media metric that settles a contest while the underlying act is held fixed and
  coverage is pushed; or show a stake-holding keeper settling a contest against the subject's own
  reading with the stake held constant.

### C3 :  information-facing, checkable against measurement practice

- **Statement:** information inequality is a weighting and support condition, not a volume condition;
  the missing link is the channel-to-objective link, which has no instrument.
- **Source, verbatim:** Fig 9 :60 "Use filters for the budget and witnesses for the verdict. Never let
  a filter hand you a verdict." Fig 3 :27 "A link with no instrument is not a link that works. It is a
  link nobody has looked at." Fig 12 :126 "Bits price the description. Actions price finding out
  whether it holds." Fig 12 :117 "isomer. Same atoms, different bond, different substance. The
  difference is invisible in the formula and decisive in the behaviour."
- **What to check:** does exposure data record who benefits from exposure, or only who was exposed? Is
  there an instrument that records discernment followed by sanction, as distinct from an approval
  movement? Do two regimes with the same four conditions differ only in the bond between them?
- **Refuter:** show that publication volume alone, with channel, independence, weighting and sanction
  held fixed, moves the ground verdict on a slot; or show a regime that works with a different atom
  set, which kills the isomer claim and leaves only the functional-group claim.

## 3. What is explicitly not claimed

- Not that press freedom is the ground of accountability. It is a channel and can decay (Fig 10 :24).
- Not that more outlets entail more independence, or that more information entails more accountability.
- Not that any named medium is good or bad regardless of the residual (Fig 11 :51).
- Not that a national accountability score can carry a per-slot verdict (Fig 1 :22).
- Not that the framework names the institution that will work. It returns a shape; the office is a
  provenanced binding from a world frame.
- Not `grounded`. Nothing here was settled by an accountability measurement.

## 5. What would settle it

A discriminator, not another index. From *The Hard Problem is About Source*: an instrument that reads
green "cannot comment on the source", and the spectrometer resolved it. Here:

1. For one named slot, does the record stay fixed when the subjects of the report push on it, and is
   the keeper free of a stake in the reading (Fig 10 :93)?
2. Do two genuinely low-overlap frames converge on the same failure, rather than agreeing from one
   source (Fig 2 :31, :40)?
3. Is discernment of the failure followed by an actual sanction, measured separately from an approval
   movement (the test quoted from the *Accountability* source)?
4. Does equalising access change who benefits from exposure, not just how many are exposed (the
   Mondak and Anderson result reported in the knowledge-gap source)?

## 6. What an expert can check in twenty minutes

1. Read Fig 9 :55 and :57, Fig 1 :19 and :22, Fig 2 :29 and :31, Fig 10 :24 and :93, Fig 3 :27,
   Fig 12 :117 and :126. Ten short passages.
2. Check C2 against one oversight case you know: is the record an anchor or a frame, and is the keeper
   disqualified by a stake?
3. Check C3 against one measurement you know: does an exposure figure price bits or actions?
4. Name one instrument that would distinguish discernment plus sanction from an approval movement, or
   say why none is possible.
5. Send the refutation. A failure is more useful than an endorsement.
