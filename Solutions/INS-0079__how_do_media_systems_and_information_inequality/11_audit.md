## Round trip gap R_T

2. **Abstraction (T_A):** the shorthand carried in `07`: union of three conditions, per-slot verdicts,

3. **Concretisation (T_E):** a media policy claim, for example "funding independent journalism will

**R_T is nonzero.** Three things the shorthand holds do not survive the trip back down:

- the **anchor role**, which the concrete claim silently assigns to a funding metric or an outlet
  count, so the metric becomes a frame that updates on what it grades (Fig 2 :33–34);
- the **union structure**, which the ten-percent coefficient flattens into one lever, leaving the
  D1∩D2 and D2∩D3 intersections standing (Fig 9 :55–57);
- the **two currencies**, because the coefficient sums bits (outlets, access) and actions (sanction)
  in one unit (Fig 12 :125–127).

One thing survives cleanly: a structure must mediate between the record and the sanction, and the
verdict must be checked per slot. That is the residue worth sending. R_T was **not** measured
numerically because no accountability ground reading was taken; it is recorded qualitatively as the
enumerated set of dropped constraints. Treating "the formula is the condensed version of the figures"
as identity would set R_T to zero and commit the dead-reckoning error Fig 4 :30 names.
