## 0. The question restated as an effect

> How do media systems and information inequality affect the quality of democratic accountability?

Stated in effect terms: what accounts for the gap between a polity's **formal** right of answerability
(electoral replacement, oversight, courts, audit) and the **actual** detection, naming and sanctioning
of failures of power? The three options the question seems to offer are:

- **A:** media-system structure is the lever (regulate ownership, protect editorial independence,
  fund public service media);
- **B:** information distribution is the lever (close the access and knowledge gaps, raise literacy);
- **C:** accountability quality is just a summary of formal institutional design.

What each leads to: A and B each clear only part of the residual if the conditions overlap; C deletes
the residual by definition rather than measuring it. The answer would look like a **separator**: a set
of conditions that must hold for a failure of power to register against a ground able to sanction it,
stated per accountability slot, with a refusal written in advance. Bind that sought-for shape as an
ordinary term now, per `THE_FORMULA.md` "Direction", so it can accrue a residual like anything else.

## 1. Residual R by arity, symmetry and scale

- **Arity:** at least three conditions are in contact with R (the media channel, the distribution of
  information across the population, and the accountability institution's own contact with a
  non-updating record). Three conditions with an overlap are a **union compound**, not one question
  with three answers and not a partition. Fig 9 :57: "a case in two conditions is not a harder case
  of either."
- **Symmetry:** "affect" is an **ordered** relation. A media-freedom index correlating with
  accountability quality is a symmetric reading; the causal arrow is not recoverable from it. The
  Operators table distinguishes the symmetric `+` from ordered `→`, `⇒`, `−`, `⋛`. Fig 12 :23
  ("one substance", under a symmetric bond) is the warning: same measured ingredients do not license
  the same route.
- **Scale:** one citizen judging one official on one issue is a slot; a national accountability score
  is an aggregate taken across slots and across conditions. Fig 1 :19: "The residual is indexed by
  object slot, not aggregated." Fig 1 :22: "Aggregating across slots is how a live signal disappears
  into an average." The membrane between scales is lossy (Fig 4 :30), so a national index can move
  while a live slot stays broken.

**R is not** "the public feels less trust" and not "the country has a lower freedom score". Both are
frame-internal readings. Fig 1 :23: "The ground enters as the only metric." Public judgment and media
metrics are instruments that can be wrong on the axis of correspondence (Fig 2 :37–38, applied here by
re-census, not as a physical claim).

and being able to say what would count as progress (Fig 3 :25–27). Fig 3 :27: "A link with no
instrument is not a link that works. It is a link nobody has looked at."

## 4. Findings (each with its own refuter)

### F1 :  The question is a union compound, so no single lever decides it (triangulated)

Fig 9 :55: "A bundled problem is usually a union of overlapping conditions, not a list of separate ones."
Fig 9 :57: "a case in two conditions is not a harder case of either."

World corroboration: *Accountability* (Wikipedia, 2026) records that "when organizations are unable to
monitor elections and provide information to voters, then voters struggle to sanction the incumbent",
and separately that "elections may instead lead to less egalitarian policy outcomes, because those who
hold the government accountable tend to be from wealthier segments of society". Two conditions, two
different failures, one outcome. *Media pluralism* adds a third: "having several news outlets doesn't
always mean people are getting a wide variety of opinions" (Graz, Ots and Sjøvaag 2024, as reported
there). A channel-count lever clears the first condition and leaves the other two standing.

**Verdict `specified`, triangulated across Fig 9 and two low-overlap world fields (political science
and media studies).** **Refuter:** show that the three conditions collapse to one latent variable such
that one lever clears every intersection, with a measurement, not a correlation.

### F2 :  Accountability quality must be indexed per slot, never averaged (triangulated)

Fig 1 :19: "The residual is indexed by object slot, not aggregated." Fig 1 :22: "Aggregating across
slots is how a live signal disappears into an average."

A national accountability score can rise while a named oversight body on a named issue stays unable to
register a failure. Fig 4 :30: "So take the round trip and measure the gap. Without it you are
navigating by dead reckoning: confident about a position nobody checked." *Knowledge gap hypothesis*
(Wikipedia, 2026) reports that "the most consistent result is the presence of knowledge differentials,
regardless of topic, methodological, or theoretical variations" (Gaziano 1997), which is exactly a
persistent per-group signal that an average hides.

**Verdict `general` on the indexing rule, `specified` on this domain.** **Refuter:** exhibit an
accountability failure that is only visible in the national average and invisible at every slot.

### F3 :  An independent record is an anchor; media plurality is a channel, not the ground (triangulated)

Fig 2 :29: "An anchor is whatever settles the question and does not change its answer because of what
any frame thinks." Fig 2 :30: "If it moves when you push on it, it is not an anchor." Fig 2 :35:
"Alignment is triangulation, not negotiation. A mirror cannot correct you."

The corrupt act does not change when a reporter is fired or a platform changes its ranking, so the
record of the act is the anchor and the media system is the channel to it. Fig 10 :24: "the ground
does not decay, the channel does." Treating number of outlets, a press-freedom index, or public
approval as the ground is the common-cause collapse of Fig 2 :18–21: two readings agreeing because
one source feeds both. *Media capture* (Wikipedia, 2026) states the channel failure directly:
"Media capture reduces government accountability by limiting public access to independent information."
*Accountability* adds that "Freedom of the press contributes to the reduction of corruption by exposing
corrupt actions", which is a channel claim, not a claim that the press is the ground.

**Verdict `specified`, triangulated across Fig 2, Fig 10 and two low-overlap world fields.
Refuter:** name a media-system metric whose value changes when the underlying act is held fixed and
the coverage is pushed, and show it still settles the verdict.

### F4 :  Independence, not headcount, is what makes plurality evidence (triangulated)

Fig 2 :31: "Independence, between two frames, is how little they draw on the same evidence."
Fig 2 :40: "disqualifying for a verdict: agreement about the world is worthless when the agreeing
frames share an evidence pool." Fig 7 :35: "A room's agreement is variation, not a verdict. Selection
between rooms is where the ground votes."

*Media pluralism* records the UNESCO finding that "even if multiple kinds of information and
programming are available, each segmented group may only ingest one branch of the whole." Many outlets
 fed by the same owners, the same wire, the same platform ranking or the same messaging are "one long
room" (Fig 7 :36). Fig 2 :42: "Buy independence in proportion to the cost of being wrong." When the
cost of a wrong verdict is a captured office or a wrongful sanction, low-overlap frames are worth
paying for.

**Verdict `specified`, triangulated across Fig 2, Fig 7 and the UNESCO report as carried in the media
studies source.** **Refuter:** show two outlets with demonstrably independent evidence sources whose
agreement still fails to move the verdict, or one shared-source outlet whose agreement does.

### F5 :  Information inequality is partly a weighting condition, not only an access condition (single-source figure ruling; world corroborated)

The Symbols table defines `w` as "How far the belief leans on private history versus inherited prior"
and notes that "Two frames with identical architecture diverge because their histories and their w
diverge." Fig 1 :19 requires the registration to be per slot.

*Knowledge gap hypothesis* reports the mechanism: "Since there is already a pre-existing gap in
knowledge between groups in a population, mass media amplifies this gap to another level." Mondak and
Anderson (2004, as reported there) found that "increased media exposure can enhance political
knowledge, pre-existing socioeconomic and gender disparities often determine who benefits the most,
reinforcing rather than reducing knowledge inequities." That is a `w` and a support condition, not an
access-only condition. *Digital divide* (Wikipedia, 2026) calls the divide "fundamentally a
socio-institutional gap" and distinguishes first-level access from second-level skills and
third-level ability to translate use into advantage.

**Verdict `specified`, single-source on the figure side (Symbols table only, no second figure
governs `w`), with world corroboration that is not upgraded to a second witness because the two
world sources share a mass-communication evidence pool.** **Refuter:** show a case where equalising
access alone closes the accountability gap without changing who benefits from exposure.

### F6 :  A keeper who benefits from the answer is not an anchor (triangulated)

Fig 10 :93: "A ground with a reputation to protect is a ground someone benefits from, and its keeper
is thereby disqualified." Fig 10 :59: "an interest in the outcome is unavoidable, an interest in the
reading is disqualifying." Fig 2 :34: "If it does, panel 3 turns into panel 1: the anchor becomes
another frame, everything drifts together, and the drift is coherent, which is why nobody notices."

*Principal–agent problem* (Wikipedia, 2026) names the structural case: "elected officials (agent) and
citizens (principal)", and "The problem worsens when there is a greater discrepancy of interests and
information between the principal and agent, as well as when the principal lacks the means to punish
the agent." When the oversight body depends for its budget, tenure or narrative on the actor it is
measuring, the reading moves with the actor. That is why an outside reader, not the institution's own
report, must range against the record.

**Verdict `specified`, triangulated across Fig 10, Fig 2 and economics/institutional theory.
Refuter:** exhibit an oversight keeper with a protectable stake whose reading still settles a contest
against the subject's own reading, with the stake held constant.

### F7 :  The broken link is usually between affordance and objective, and it has no instrument (single-source)

Fig 3 :25–27: the middle link "has neither [obvious failures and obvious tests], so it attracts the
least attention and the fewest instruments"; Fig 3 :27: "A link with no instrument is not a link that
works. It is a link nobody has looked at."

In this domain, having terms for what a situation affords (an official is answerable for X, a media
system can publish Y) and being able to say what would count as progress (a sanction actually
followed, a correction actually reached the affected public) are usually assumed rather than measured.
*Accountability* gives the testable form: "Governments are 'accountable' if voters can discern
whether governments are acting in their interest and sanction them appropriately". Discernment plus
sanction is the objective link; a turnout figure or an approval rating measures neither.

**Verdict `general` on the chain rule, `specified` on this domain, single-source figure side.
Refuter:** exhibit a working instrument for the affordance-to-objective link in a named polity and
show the chain still breaks above it.

### F8 :  Introduction is available, exclusion is not; habitat must be enumerated outward (single-source figure ruling, low-overlap world analogue)

Fig 11 :52–53: contact cascades, and "the residual that mattered was three steps out" (the DDT
example). Fig 11 :61: "The seat's power is asymmetric. Introduction is available; exclusion is not."

A reform that funds independent journalism introduces an actor; it cannot subtract the ownership
concentration, the platform ranking, the literacy gap or the legal environment already in contact
with the residual. Fig 11 :39: "capability is a property of agent-and-habitat, never of the agent
alone." *How can approaches from physical ecology help research and policy on the information
environment?* (Wanless, Wardle and Scales, HKS Misinformation Review, 2026, ecology field) supplies
the genuinely low-overlap analogue: "Structure is both a precondition and a partner for understanding
dynamics", and "a variable cannot be said to have crossed a threshold until it has been defined, its
system bounded, and its unit of analysis fixed." Enumerate the habitat (actors, conditions,
relations) before ranking actors.

**Verdict `general` structural, `specified` on the asymmetry; single-source figure side with one
low-overlap ecology corroborator.** **Refuter:** exhibit a reform that removed a habitat condition
rather than adding an actor, and show the residual fell without a compensating introduction.

### F9 :  More information is a budget filter, not a witness (triangulated)

Fig 1 :24–27: coverage, compression, predicates minted and credibility accrued "are frame-internal.
A frame cannot score itself with a quantity it also produces." Fig 9 :60: "Use filters for the budget
and witnesses for the verdict. Never let a filter hand you a verdict."

Increasing the volume of published information can buy more attempts, which is a budget use. It cannot
by itself show that a non-updating record was reached and a sanction followed. Fig 5 :48: "Its own
output is never a measure of success. A system can be enthusiastically curious and produce nothing."
Fig 12 :126: "Bits price the description. Actions price finding out whether it holds." A policy that
reports bits (pages, outlets, clicks) in the currency of actions (verdicts, sanctions) is making the
unpriced-sum error Fig 12 :127–128 names.

**Verdict `specified`, triangulated across Fig 1, Fig 5, Fig 9 and Fig 12.** **Refuter:** show a case
where publication volume alone, with channel, independence, weighting and sanction held fixed, moves
the ground verdict on a named slot.

### F10 :  A regime is a functional group, not a standing list of good outlets (triangulated)

Fig 11 :51: "No standing list of good actors survives a change of residual." Fig 12 :124:
"functional group. A substructure whose presence fixes the outcome. The group says what kind; the
qualifier says which member."

Over a fixed atom set (an independent record, a channel with differing closure, a discriminating
public, a refusal-capable seat), two national media regimes that differ only in which atom mediates
which failure are Fig 12 :117 isomers: "Same atoms, different bond, different substance. The
difference is invisible in the formula and decisive in the behaviour." A public-service broadcaster
and a distributed network of small outlets can be members of the same functional group; which one
works is a binding to be provenance-stamped, not a kind to be canonised. *Fourth estate* (Wikipedia,
2026) records Benkler's "Networked Fourth Estate" as "a diverse set of actors instead of a small
number of major presses", while the same article records the rival worry in the title of Benkler's
2011 paper, "Free Irresponsible Press: Wikileaks and the Battle over the Soul of the Networked Fourth
Estate". Diversity of actors is not by itself a verdict.

**Verdict `specified` on the functional-group reading, `general` on the isomer form; triangulated
across Fig 11, Fig 12 and the journalism source.** **Refuter:** exhibit a standing list of outlets
that keeps its valence across two changed residuals, which would break Fig 11 :51.

### F11 :  The ground, not agreement, decides; a `gap` remains for the scalar (single-source, declared)

Fig 13 :134: "Unreachable is a verdict here, not a silence," and Fig 8 :39: "So a search that finds
nothing is never a proof of absence." No figure line selects a universal percent by which media
systems and information inequality change accountability quality. Fig 9 :32: "you get a kind of thing,
never the answer itself."

This is a `gap` of the shape-not-a-name kind, not underivable in principle. The remedy is SEARCHED
first (is the scalar already in the closure and unfound?), then INWARD (sharpen the instrument on a
named slot), then OUTWARD (find a frame whose closure predicts the shape), kept distinct per
`THE_FORMULA.md` step 7.

**Verdict `gap`, single-source figure side.** **Refuter:** exhibit a figure line that returns a
universal coefficient, or a measurement on a named slot that the shape cannot accommodate.

## 5. Pre-registered refuter (written before the recommendation or conclusion)

"Write down what an answer would look like, bind it, and let it accrue a residual like anything else."
The shape claim is refuted if any of the following holds:

1. Three conditions (channel, distribution, checking arrangement) collapse to one latent variable such
   that a single lever clears every intersection, measured and not merely correlated (breaks F1).
2. A national accountability index moves while every named slot's channel-to-sanction chain is shown
   intact (breaks F2 and the Fig 1 indexing rule).
3. A media-system metric settles a contest while held fixed against changes in the underlying act, so
   the metric is an anchor rather than a channel (breaks F3).
4. Two frames with a demonstrably shared evidence pool produce a verdict that moves the ground, while
   low-overlap frames with the same shape do not (breaks F4 and Fig 2 :40).
5. Equalising access closes the accountability gap with weighting and benefit-from-exposure held
   fixed (breaks F5).
6. A named oversight keeper with a protectable stake settles a contest against the subject's own
   reading with the stake held constant (breaks F6).
7. Publication volume alone, with channel, independence, weighting and sanction held fixed, moves the
   ground verdict on a slot (breaks F9).

If any fires, the shape is withdrawn or narrowed in the retraction log. Until one is run, nothing is
`grounded`.

## 6. Recommendation

1. Lead with the union separator and the per-slot indexing, not with a media-freedom score (F1, F2).
2. State the anchor and the channel separately for each named accountability slot; do not let an
   index occupy the anchor seat (F3, F6).
3. Buy independence where the cost of a wrong verdict is highest, and declare the stake of every
   keeper (F4, F6).
4. Enumerate the habitat three steps out before ranking any single medium as good or bad (F8, F10).
5. Price bits and actions separately; report the round-trip gap rather than treating an index as the
   thing itself (F2, F9).
6. Keep the scalar as a `gap`, and send the shape plus the refuter, not a universal percentage (F11).

### SEARCHED / INWARD / OUTWARD, kept distinct (formula step 7)

- **SEARCHED (asked first, cheapest).** Is a scalar or a named mechanism already in the closure and

## 8. Updated verdict

The shape survives: **democratic accountability quality is a union of three conditions with different
anchors, indexed per slot; media systems are a channel whose evidentiary weight comes from
independence rather than headcount; information inequality is a weighting and support condition, not
a volume condition; and the checking arrangement needs a keeper that cannot profit from the reading.
No scalar and no standing list of good outlets is returned.** Strains after re-census: none hidden;
one live type question is carried forward, whether a "record" can be treated as a non-updating anchor
in a polity where records are authored, which Fig 10 :93 turns into a role-relative test rather than
a property. Everything is `specified` or `general`, ungrounded, candidate.
