## What is deliberately not here

- No personal emails of named individuals.
- No ranking of people by how likely they are to agree with the claim.
- No bulk-send plan.

Three reasons, in the figures' own terms:

1. Fig 2 :35: "Alignment is triangulation, not negotiation. A mirror cannot correct you." A list
   chosen for receptiveness is a list chosen to agree, which manufactures the correlation that makes
   the agreement worthless (Fig 2 :40).
2. Fig 10 :42: "A verifier that can reconstruct the claim cannot verify it." Seating a recipient
   inside the framework before asking for a verdict spends the only thing they contribute.
3. Fig 9 :60: "Use filters for the budget and witnesses for the verdict. Never let a filter hand you
   a verdict." Contact cost chooses how to approach someone; it never decides whether their answer
   counts.

Legally: scraping personal emails for unsolicited bulk outreach breaches GDPR and CAN-SPAM style
rules in many jurisdictions and gets a sending domain blacklisted. That is a practical failure on top
of the structural one.
