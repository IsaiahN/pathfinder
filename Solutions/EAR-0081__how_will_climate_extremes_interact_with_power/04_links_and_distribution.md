## D. Distribution rules

- **Fig 10 :52:** *"Bring a characterised residual, never the source."* Lead with the shape claim and the pre-registered refuter. Send the five source links only after a stage-1 verdict is recorded.
- **Fig 1 :27:** frame-internal quantities (agreement, number of readers, credibility accrued) cannot score the claim. A large recipient list is not a witness.
- **Fig 7 :13:** *"agreement compounds, evidence does not"*. An ensemble of reviewers reading the same six sources is one witness wearing many names.
- **Fig 2 :40:** selecting recipients for receptiveness manufactures the correlation that disqualifies their agreement. No receptiveness rank exists in this folder.
- **Fig 9 :27-28:** describe the effect before choosing a frame, otherwise any frame will seem to fit. Ask what the responder would expect to see before naming any framework.
- **Fig 4 :22:** *"Only methods cross up. Only head starts come down."* Stage 3 offers a reusable method, not a recording of one result.

## E. The two-stage offer

1. **Stage 1.** Send the residual, the per-slot shape claim and the four refuters. Ask what the recipient would count as a failure. Record that verdict before showing anything else.
2. **Stage 2, opt-in only.** If curious, offer the public links in section A. The figures, not the summary, are the authority.
3. **Stage 3, opt-in only.** Offer the formula as a method. Each user writes their own refuter and states their own mode before running it.

**Zip or hosting:** no archive is created or sent by this agent. Hosting and sending are the user's actions. Prefer an opt-in download page that lets a recipient decline contact over an attachment pushed to a list.
