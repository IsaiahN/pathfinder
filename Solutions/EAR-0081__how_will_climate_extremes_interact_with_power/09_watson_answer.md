## QUESTION

Decide, in effect terms, what the Tether says about the interaction of climate extremes with power grids, transport, water, communications and health. Option A: treat the five as one risk object and answer with an aggregated risk score and a ranked adaptation list. Option B: keep five per-slot residuals, treat the compound as a union, enumerate the contact field, and stop at the ground with a declared `gap`. What each leads to: A produces a convention that decides an outcome and spends the independence of the people asked to endorse it. B produces a checkable shape, four refuters written in advance, and an honest stop at the limit of the instruments. The figures decide: B.

## DOMAIN CENSUS

R by arity, symmetry and scale; one line per domain with actors / currency / anchor; partition or union; order by dependency.

- **R (the residual).** Gap between predicted and observed service state in five slots, plus the gap in predicted propagation across their couplings. Arity: five slots plus edges, not a scalar. Symmetry: asymmetric and directed; power disables pumping, not the reverse in the same way. Scale: asset, network, system of systems, region; the coupling crosses scales. The compound is a union of hazard, exposure, vulnerability and interdependency (Fig 9 :55).
- **Domain 1, climate hazard.** Actors: heat, flood, drought, storm, sea-level rise. Currency: hazard intensity and frequency. Anchor: measured climate and event records; climate models are frames ranging against it.
- **Domain 2, the five service slots.** Actors: assets, operators, demand. Currency: service delivered versus capacity, outage duration. Anchor: outage and service records. One domain with five distinct slots (Fig 13 :34), not five domains and not a partition.
- **Domain 3, the coupling habitat.** Actors: dependency edges, repair access, backup fuel. Currency: cascade propagation. Anchor: event forensics. Nameable, so it is a domain.
- **D4, the assessment frame (dissolved).** Actors: modellers, scenarios, ensembles. Currency: agreement. Anchor: none of its own; realized impact belongs to domains 1 to 3. By the anchor test it is a frame, not a domain.
- **Order by dependency (Fig 3).** Perception to vocabulary to objective to planning to learn and carry. The break is at vocabulary and objective: there are few instruments for what an asset affords under stress and no common failure metric across sectors. Effort aimed at planning arrives at a step whose input never arrived.

Strain test: the no-direct-bond rule of Fig 13 governs the six primitives; the cascade rule of Fig 12 governs arrangements of settled molecules. Different objects, so no strain after re-census. One live strain is named: applying comparison to sector service losses assumes they are quantities at that level, and that needs an instrument.

## VERDICT (from the figures)

The shape survives; the forecast does not exist in this closure. Keep five per-slot residuals, treat the question as a union compound, enumerate the contact field outward, use ordered and cyclic bonds, and anchor on realized service outcome. The five sectors are one functional group with five qualifiers, not isomers as written. The empirical claim is `gap`: the figures contain no hazard distribution, fragility curve or repair-time law. This is unreachable at this budget with these instruments, not underivable in principle. Overall mode `specified` on structure, `general` on rules, `gap` on the forecast. Nothing is `grounded`, and four pre-registered refuters stand until an instrument fires one.

- **cost if wrong:** bounded and locatable. The packet states mode, refuters and lineage before the conclusion, so an error is findable rather than invisible. The real cost is spending the independence of outside readers by sending a convention that decides an outcome instead of a generator. Fig 2 :42 *"Buy independence in proportion to the cost of being wrong."* That cost is avoidable by leading with the shape and its refuter.
- **undoable:** yes. The aggregated-verdict option is refused without touching the derivation. `07_the_tether_answer.md` and `08_expert_verification_packet.md` already remove it. A sent claim can be superseded, and no primitive has to be withdrawn.
- **reversed by:** a ground reading, not agreement. Per-slot outage and recovery records by hazard and asset; event forensics tracing trigger to third-order loss; night-time lights or vessel-tracking proxies where utility data are confidential; any of the four pre-registered refuters firing on held-out events; or a genuine re-census finding a figure line that governs a specific outage probability. Until one of these, the verdict stands as `candidate`.
