# The Tether's answer: climate extremes and five infrastructure systems

**Mode: `specified` on the shape, `general` on the structural rules, `gap` on the empirical forecast. Not `grounded`.** Simulated derivation checked for internal consistency against the figures, not a ground reading (`05_what_shape_means.md` §6).

## 1. The residual it answers

The question asks what happens when a climate extreme meets power grids, transport, water, communications and health. Stated as an effect: the gap between predicted service state and observed service state in each of five slots, plus the gap between predicted and observed propagation across their couplings. The five are a **union compound**, not five separate questions and not one average (Fig 9 :57, *"a case in two conditions is not a harder case of either. It stops being a mixture and becomes a specific compound."*).

## 2. The shape, one claim at a time

1. **Keep the residuals separate.** Fig 1 :19 *"The residual is indexed by object slot, not aggregated."* and Fig 1 :22 *"Aggregating across slots is how a live signal disappears into an average."* A single infrastructure risk score is the named failure mode, not a summary.
2. **Unbundle before searching.** Fig 9 :54 *"And when the split lands on a union rather than a partition, unbundle before you search again."* Separate hazard, exposure, vulnerability and interdependency, then recombine at the couplings.
3. **Enumerate the habitat, never compose it.** Fig 11 :43 *"The habitat is enumerated, never composed."* Fig 11 :52 *"Scoping the habitat to direct contact is how DDT reached the eagles: first-order enumeration, second-order effect, and the residual that mattered was three steps out."* For infrastructure the analogue is power to pumps to patients, or wind to road closure to unreachable spare part. The loss that matters sits several steps out.
4. **The bonds are ordered and cyclic, not symmetric dominoes.** Fig 12 :121 *"cycle. The output re-enters as input. The sign of the loop decides whether it closes, and a chain cannot close by construction."* Storm Éowyn's power-to-telecom-to-recovery loop is a real cycle. The five sectors are one functional group with five qualifiers (Fig 12 :123), not isomers as written.
5. **The anchor is realized service outcome, not model agreement.** Fig 2 :29 *"An anchor is whatever settles the question and does not change its answer because of what any frame thinks."* Fig 2 :35 *"Alignment is triangulation, not negotiation. A mirror cannot correct you."* Agreement among ensembles that share a review pool is one witness (Fig 7 :13, *"agreement compounds, evidence does not"*).
6. **The assessment frame is not a ground-bearing domain.** Fig 1 :27 *"They are frame-internal. A frame cannot score itself with a quantity it also produces."* The question which model is right dissolves into which model ranges against the ground.
7. **The forecast itself is a gap.** Fig 13 :134 *"Unreachable is a verdict here, not a silence"* and Fig 8 :39 *"So a search that finds nothing is never a proof of absence."* The figures carry no hazard distribution, no fragility curve and no repair-time law. That is unreachable at this budget with these instruments, not underivable in principle.

## 3. What it explicitly does not say

- No probability, timing, magnitude or first-failing sector. Any such claim would name what the framework does not hold.
- No aggregated verdict. No single score that decides adaptation spending.
- Not that the figures are climate science. They are a generator of the shape of a checkable answer.
- Not that model agreement is evidence. It is a budget to be tried.
- Not `grounded`. No outage record was used to settle any claim here.

## 4. What would settle it: the ground

A discriminator, not a further argument. *The Hard Problem is About Source* (read this session): an instrument that reads green cannot comment on the source, and *"An instrument that can't distinguish these cases demonstrates its own inadequacy"*. Here the discriminating readings are already partial and already returning something:

1. **Per-slot outage and recovery records** by hazard and by asset, rather than a single damage total.
2. **Event forensics** that trace the channel from trigger to third-order service loss, including repair access and backup fuel.
3. **Night-time lights and vessel-tracking proxies** for outages and port disruption where utility data are confidential (named in the PLOS review).
4. **The four pre-registered refuters** in `01_figure_grounded_audit.md` §4, run on held-out events. If any fires, the corresponding shape claim is withdrawn.

INWARD extends these instruments from what already registers. OUTWARD imports atoms from climate assessment, infrastructure review, complex-systems science and disaster theory, each with a source gate and shadow test. SEARCHED was asked first and cannot manufacture an atom (Fig 8 closure is idempotent).

## 5. The honest stop

The five named systems come from the question, not from the six-term substrate. Binding the substrate's network to a power grid is an import with no source gate, so it is not claimed. The answer stops where the instruments stop distinguishing, and the lineage is recorded so the stop is checkable. The canonical rule is *"Indiscernibility is not identity."* The round trip from the figures to a city and back loses the bond types, the union rule and the three step-7 questions; that loss is `R_T` and is recorded, not assumed zero (Fig 4 :30).

## 6. One-paragraph version for outreach

> The Tether does not forecast which infrastructure fails first in a climate extreme, and it says so. What it gives is the shape any checkable answer must have. Power, transport, water, communications and health are five object slots whose residuals must be reported separately, because aggregating them is how a live signal disappears into an average. They form a union compound, so unbundle hazard, exposure, vulnerability and interdependency before searching and recombine them at the couplings. The contact field must be enumerated rather than composed, because the loss that matters is often three steps out, as when a blocked road stops a repair crew and a power outage becomes a water outage. The bonds are ordered and include feedback loops, so a domino chain is the wrong picture, and the anchor is realized service outcome, not agreement among models. The forecast itself stays a gap until discriminating instruments, not further argument, close it. Refuters were written before the run, and a failure is more useful than an endorsement.
