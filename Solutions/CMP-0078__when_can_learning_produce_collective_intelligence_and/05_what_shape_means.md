## 1. A shape is a constraint, not a filler

The canonical README states the limit: the framework returns "what a missing thing would have to look like (a shape, not a name)". Fig 9 :32: "you get a kind of thing, never the answer itself". Fig 13 :134: "Unreachable is a verdict here, not a silence". Fig 13 :135: "It cannot say which primitive is missing, because naming it would mean already holding it."

From *The Library of Babel* (as quoted in the exemplar's mapping): "You can cut your search short if the set of books returned is small and descriptive enough that it provides a general shape of the answer that satisfies your question."

So a **shape** is the narrowed form of an answer you can state before you can name it. For this question the shape is:

> collective intelligence = anchored, independent variation with selection between; shared-error amplification = correlated convergence with no independent ground.

That is a shape because it says what any candidate answer must look like without naming the number of agents, the field, the metric, or the platform. "Use 200 independent raters" is a name, not a shape.

## 2. How the shape is obtained here

- Fig 9 :31: "your words are borrowed too, so describe the effect rather than the cause". The residual was described in effect terms (does error against a ground fall?) before any figure was selected.
- Fig 6 :30: "The description travels. Fails by being sharpened into terms only we can read. Cast it in effect terms." The field says "collective intelligence"; the figures say "independence, anchor, selection, per-slot residual". The translation is the work.
- *Decoding Tacit Knowledge* (as mapped in the exemplar): "When contact isn't possible, the best you can send is a plain description of what you'd expect to see, because anyone can check that against the world." For this question the checkable expectation is: de-correlate the evidence and agreement should change; pin the anchor and drift should stop.
- *How a Society of Agents Would Work* makes the same move in the framework's own vocabulary: "A group is only worth having while its members stay different. Figure 2 defines independence as 'how little they draw on the same evidence.' Ten copies watching the same thing are one opinion said ten times." That sentence is the shape of the headcount answer.

## 3. What licenses a name (and why this question refuses one)

Only two things license a name, both outside the shape itself:

1. **The ground settles it.** Formula step 5: "the ground settles it. Verified only by what pays. A gate is not the ground."
2. **A provenanced import from a frame whose closure differs** (Fig 6 :23, Fig 8 :50-52), with a source gate and a shadow test.

For this question, naming a specific threshold (overlap below 0.2, say, or a particular voting rule) would be a name. The figures do not return it. *They're Scanning Books When They Should* sharpens the point: "The real import is not a new term in the existing space. It is a new dimension of the space." And: "Declaring the primitives does not make them neutral. It makes the choice visible." The choice of which metric counts as intelligence is a visible choice; turning it into an invisible one is the error this file refuses.

The PNAS experiment (Lorenz et al. 2011) gives an empirical threshold-like finding, that "even mild social influence can undermine the wisdom of crowd effect in simple estimation tasks", but it is a world source, secondary and field-specific. It can corroborate the shape; it cannot name a universal constant for the framework.

## 4. The specific trap for this question: agreement as a self-score

*The Hard Problem is About Source* (exemplar mapping) gives the mechanism. An instrument reads green and "cannot comment on the source": one green light, or yellow plus blue, or RGB, or CMYK all give the same reading. "If several paths create the same behavior, then behavior is a projection with lossy information." What resolves it is a spectrometer, not an argument.

Applied here: a group's agreement is a reading. It cannot comment on its source. The source might be a shared ground correctly tracked, or a shared corpus, or mutual updating. The instrument that discriminates those is an independent ground, not more agreement. Fig 1 :27: "They are frame-internal. A frame cannot score itself with a quantity it also produces." This is the metamerism error applied to collective intelligence: naming "the crowd is wise" from the reading "the crowd agrees".

*The Recursive Self-Improvement Trap* states the validator side: "the output is only as good as the validator. The agent, or the tool, or the frame, or the theory, cannot validate itself." And: "You may all agree and be wrong. You may set your clock to someone else's, who was setting your clock to yours, and be off by many deviations over time. You need ground truth to define where you stand." That is Fig 2 panel 1 in plain language, and it is the exact mechanism this question asks about.

## 5. Q: Does "capability is agent-and-habitat" name a structure for this question?

**Answered, no further question needed.** Fig 11 :39: "So the answerable question is not what intelligence is, but what makes a system capable. And capability is a property of agent-and-habitat, never of the agent alone." Fig 11 :36: "Where nothing maintains anything, intelligence is a label applied afterwards to a lineage that met its ground. Nothing was intelligent; selection ran. The word does no work there."

So "collective intelligence" is not a property a crowd either has or lacks. It is a label applied afterwards to a configuration that met its ground. The answerable question is what makes a configuration capable, and the figure names the components: independent frames, a ground that does not update, a habitat enumerated rather than composed, and selection between variants. *How a Society of Agents Would Work* states the practical consequence: "The ground sits under all three: no store's agreement replaces it."

## 6. Two consequences for outreach

**Prestige is not action.** *The Recursive Self-Improvement Trap*: "Prestige must never be allowed to validate an action directly... Someone let prestige stand in for proof. Someone let reputation substitute for result. Someone let the trusted name validate the untested claim." The essay's author reports building a `PrestigeFirewall` after observing that the multi-agent system collapsed into a monoculture when prestige could buy actions. That is F2 and F8 at the level of a built system, and it is why `03_public_contact_points.csv` contains venues and no personal emails.

**This audit is a simulation, not a ground reading.** *The Recursive Self-Improvement Trap*: "LLMs can quickly simulate an approximate answer/output with the formula found in the architecture post... However, the truly testable output would need to be derived from the real architecture or working the formula yourself." So `01`, `06` and `09` are simulated derivations checked for internal consistency against the figures. They are **not** a test of the claim and must be labelled as such. They cannot settle anything they report on.

## 7. Canonical limit, stated plainly

Fig 9 :32: "you get a kind of thing, never the answer itself." The canonical README: "a generator of the shape of an answer at any level, applied recursively until the residual stops registering against the ground." What this solution offers is the shape any answer to CMP-0078 must have, plus refuters written in advance and the instruments that would close it. It does not offer a number, a platform, or a verdict about any particular crowd. "Indiscernibility is not identity."
