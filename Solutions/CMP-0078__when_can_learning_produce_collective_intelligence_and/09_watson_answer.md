## QUESTION

Decide, in effect terms, the fork in complexity science.txt line 95. Option A: more learning and more agents produce collective intelligence, so the remedy is more of the same. Option B: the outcome depends on four structural conditions that decide whether the collective's error against a ground falls or whether agreement rises without accuracy. What each leads to: A optimises a quantity the frames produce themselves and cannot distinguish the two regimes; B separates ground error from agreement, states the two amplification mechanisms, and supplies refuters that can fire. The sought-for shape was bound as an ordinary term before the run: "collective intelligence is anchored, independent variation with selection between; amplified shared error is correlated convergence with no independent ground."

## DOMAIN CENSUS

R by arity, symmetry and scale, then one line per domain with actors / currency / anchor, partition or union, and order by dependency.

- **R (the residual).** The fork in collective outcome. Arity: plural and indexed by object slot, not by agent count; "The residual is indexed by object slot, not aggregated." Symmetry: not invariant under averaging; "Aggregating across slots is how a live signal disappears into an average." Scale: the loop closes within one scale; crossing to the population is a membrane crossing with a lossy transform and round-trip gap R_T.
- **Domain 1, the single learning loop.** Actors: one agent with Γ, belief b, action a, observation o'. Currency: per-slot residual, cost(phi), settlement. Anchor: yes, a paying ground (Fig 1). Partition: which terms may be admitted.
- **Domain 2, the population.** Actors: many agents, a shared library, something that ranks what they produce. Currency: methods up, head starts down, agreement between frames. Anchor: conditional, exists only when pinned: "the anchor pinned: it does not update on A or B both range against it; it is not a third party to the disagreement". Union: the two regimes can co-occur.
- **Domain 3, the design and verification seat.** Actors: an outside checker and a habitat. Currency: evidence versus agreement. Anchor: yes, the ground, never agreement. Partition: which conditions to install.
- **Partition or union.** Two regimes by condition, but a real system can instantiate both, so the split lands on a union and must be unbundled before search. A single lever (more learning) enlarges both and leaves the intersection standing.
- **Order by dependency.** Per-slot perception, vocabulary for independence and anchor, objective (ground error versus agreement, the usually broken middle link), planning (which intervention), then learn and carry. Order from Fig 3.

Strain test applied: Fig 1 :22 (averaging hides the live slot) initially strained against the crowd literature (averaging improves prediction). Re-census resolved it: prediction aggregator versus per-slot search signal, different objects. No strain after re-census.

## VERDICT (from the figures)

The fork survives with its conditions. Learning produces collective intelligence when it changes contact: frames independent and renewed by separate experience, an anchor that does not update, residuals kept per slot, and selection between variants against the ground rather than blending. Learning amplifies shared errors when it increases correlation without changing contact: agents updating on one another with no fixed anchor, or agents drawing on one evidence pool and agreeing because the pool is talking. Agreement and headcount are budget filters, never witnesses. An improvement that does not change contact changes nothing, however much it improves. The two regimes are isomers over the same atoms, distinguishable by their bond to the ground, so count-based answers cannot tell them apart. This is `general` on the shape, `specified` on the conditions, ungrounded and `candidate`.

- **cost if wrong:** bounded and locatable because the refuters were written before the run. The avoidable cost is spending outside readers' independence by sending a success story instead of a refutable shape; Fig 2 :42 says "Buy independence in proportion to the cost of being wrong." It is avoided by leading with the shape and the refuters, as `08` does.
- **undoable:** yes. The four conditions can be revised or dropped without withdrawing the fork, and a sent packet can be superseded. Nothing has to be deleted from the record; a refutation adds a term and removes the standing of what it refuses. The measured failure becomes part of the lineage.
- **reversed by:** a ground reading, not agreement. A mutual-update collective whose held-out error falls with overlap above 0.8 refutes the coherent-drift mechanism; correlated frames gaining accuracy from more rounds with no new experience refute the rotation claim; a blend beating selection at equal budget with a readable per-slot residual refutes F4; an agent-only improvement that moves ground error refutes Fig 11 :40; a system whose exogenous source of agreement is identified and optimised refutes F1. Until one fires, the verdict stands as `candidate`.
