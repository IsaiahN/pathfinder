# What "shape" means for this question

**Mode: `specified`.** Simulated derivation, not a ground reading (see section 6).

**Sources opened this session:** the canonical figure README (local), `THE_FORMULA.md` (local), and three of the eight essays by Isaiah N: *The Library of Babel: Locating The Book of Books* (2026-10-02), *The Recursive Self-Improvement Trap* (2026-11-19) and *The Hard Problem is About Source* (2026-08-28). The other five essays were not opened and are not quoted here. This is stated so the record does not pretend to a reading it did not do.

## 1. A shape is a constraint, not a filler

The canonical README (local) states the limit: the framework returns "what a missing thing would have to look like (a shape, not a name)." Fig 9 :32: "you get a kind of thing, never the answer itself." The Symbols table defines the residual as "the gap between prediction and observation" and says it "is always a slice: what cannot be perceived yet is residual and simply cannot be read."

*The Library of Babel*: "you can cut your search short if the set of books returned is small and descriptive enough that it provides a general shape of the answer that satisfies your question."

So a **shape** here is the form a defensible conservation procedure must have, statable before any community or policy is named. It constrains; it does not fill. The shape for ECO-0084 is not "use Traditional Ecological Knowledge" (a name) but "keep each knowledge-holding community as a separate slot with its own ground contact and declared stake, describe the residual before choosing a frame, keep imports provenanced and unbundled, and carry methods across scales" (a constraint).

## 2. How the shape was obtained

- *Library of Babel*: use the vocabulary already held that is closest to the thing, then look for new terms that "overlap, correlate, or are even used interchangeably with your known terms"; "With each new term gained, you are able to narrow the space by definition and deepen your vocabulary."
- Fig 6 :30: "The description travels. Fails by being sharpened into terms only we can read. Cast it in effect terms." Fig 9 :30-31: "your words are borrowed too, so describe the effect rather than the cause." So the residual was described as an effect (a per-community signal that disappears on pooling), not as a cause (a cultural mismatch).
- *Library of Babel* on the bundle: "If your problem was a bundle or conflation of several problems masquerading as one? You will need to search all of those problems individually to find their answers and then combine them in a satisfactory way." That is Fig 9 :54 in another vocabulary.
- *Library of Babel* on stopping: "you will know that you are done because the finish line will also be the starting place." A shape is reached when further search returns no new constraint, not when a preferred answer is found.

## 3. What would license a name

Two things, both outside the shape itself:

1. **The ground settles it.** `THE_FORMULA.md` step 5: "the ground settles it. Verified only by what pays. A gate is not the ground." Here the ground is the ecological measurement plus the communities' own outcome records, which neither the conservation frame nor the analyst can move by thinking.
2. **A provenanced import from a frame whose closure differs** (Fig 6 :23, Fig 8 :50), with a source gate and a shadow test. For community knowledge, the CARE "Authority to Control" principle (opened 2026-11-23) is the external version of the same requirement: the record of where a datum came from and who may control it is part of the datum.

*The Hard Problem is About Source* gives the failure mechanism: an instrument that reads green "cannot comment on the source." "If several paths create the same behavior, then behavior is a projection with lossy information." A conservation dashboard that records "community input received" without distinguishing who observed what, on which evidence pool, is that instrument. What is owed is a better discriminator, not a name.

## 4. Why "the Indigenous perspective" is the forbidden kind of name

The canonical README says the framework returns "a shape, not a name" for a *missing* thing. "The Indigenous perspective" is not a missing primitive waiting to be found; it is a slot the conservation frame authored because it needed one input. It has no anchor (audit section 1b) and fails the dependency test: it cannot be ranged against anything that does not move when the frame is pushed. Naming it as the thing to be consulted turns a visible choice into an invisible one.

A correction to myself, kept visible: in the first pass I wrote that "shape, not a name" forbids naming any community at all. That was too broad. Naming a community's contribution is a provenanced binding from that frame, which F6 requires be recorded. What is forbidden is (a) inventing a single aggregate voice that no frame holds, and (b) claiming the framework derives which community must be right. Both claims are now stated so neither is quietly lost.

## 5. Consequences for this question

**Prestige is not action.** *The Recursive Self-Improvement Trap*: "Prestige can fund an action. It can give an action a budget to be tried. But it cannot settle an action. Only the ground can settle it. The Ground Maintainer must be blind to prestige." Inviting the most high-profile Indigenous scholar or the most cited TEK review to endorse a policy is the named collapse. The legitimate ask gives the shape a budget to be tried and lets the ground, a measurement and the communities' own outcome records, settle it.

**My own audit is a simulation.** Same essay: "LLMs can quickly simulate an approximate answer/output" and "the truly testable output would need to be derived from the real architecture or working the formula yourself." So `01_figure_grounded_audit.md` is a simulated derivation checked for internal consistency against the figures. It is not a test and it cannot settle the question it reports on. Nothing in it substitutes for asking the communities and measuring the residual.

**Unreachable is a verdict.** Fig 8 :39: "So a search that finds nothing is never a proof of absence." If no single perspective is found, that is not a failed search. It is the result the bundle predicted.
