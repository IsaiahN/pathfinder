## 0. The claim under audit

### 0.1 The residual in effect terms

A conservation decision (a reserve boundary, a fire regime, a harvest rule, a restoration target) is a prediction about an ecological residual: what is changing, what is at risk, and which intervention pays. Evidence about that residual is held by several distinct Indigenous and local communities, each with a long, place-specific observation record and its own account of what counts as a good outcome.

The failure named in the question is this: N distinct knowledge-holding communities are collapsed into one input slot called "the Indigenous perspective." In figure terms that move (a) aggregates across slots, (b) swamps variation to a mean, (c) treats a union of overlapping conditions as one problem, and (d) imports a datum without recording which frame it came from. It is not a kindness to communities. It destroys the only signal that says where to look.

### 0.2 Binding the sought-for shape as an ordinary term

Per `THE_FORMULA.md` "Direction": write down what an answer would look like before the search, so the preference is refusable. The sought-for shape, stated as an ordinary term:

> **A decision procedure that keeps each community as a separate slot with its own ground contact and stake, enumerates the habitat from contact before composing a plan, describes the residual before choosing which knowledge frame to consult, keeps every imported datum provenanced and unbundled, and carries methods across scales rather than recordings.**

If that shape cannot survive the refuters in section 3, it is withdrawn. Nothing in this document asserts that any community actually holds this or that fact; that is a ground question for the communities and the ecological measurements themselves.

### 1b. Domain census (derived before any figure line was chosen)

**R by arity, symmetry, scale.** Arity: N communities plus one conservation decision frame plus one synthesis arrangement, so many slots, not a binary. Symmetry: communities are not exchangeable. Fig 1 :28 says it plainly: "The gap is measured per object, not per step. A system that averages across objects has thrown away the only signal that says where to look." Under the symmetric `+` bond, Fig 12 :59 asks "swap the operands. Does the meaning change? No." Swapping two communities changes the residual, because they observe different places. `+` is therefore the wrong operator for this residual. Scale: place-based observation versus regional or national decision, with a lossy membrane between (Fig 4).

**One line per domain (actors / currency / anchor). A domain with no nameable anchor is not a domain yet.**

- **Domain 1, the conservation decision frame.** Actors: managers, agency scientists, planners, regulators. Currency: conservation outcomes, budgets, legal mandates. Anchor: measured ecological state and the institutional record, which do not change because a frame wants them to. Nameable: yes (a population count, a fire frequency, a court or board record). Question type: partition boundary (which slot's residual is live).
- **Domain 2, the knowledge-holding communities (many, distinct).** Actors: each community, its Elders, harvesters and knowledge keepers, including internal disagreement within a community. Currency: place-based observations, practices, values, oral history, held under authority to control. Anchor: the lived place and each community's own outcome record (did the harvest return, did the burning reduce fuel). Nameable: yes, per community. Question type: many separate frames, not one.
- **Domain 3, the synthesis / co-management arrangement.** Actors: boards, review panels, conveners, boundary objects. Currency: agreement and residuals. Anchor: the ground (ecological plus community-defined), never agreement (Fig 2 :45). Nameable: yes. Question type: union versus partition, bundle versus unbundled compound.
- **Candidate, "the Indigenous perspective" as one slot.** Actors: none of its own; it is produced by Domain 1's need for one input. Currency: none of its own. Anchor: **none**. Its only "anchor" is the conservation frame's preference for a single input slot, which moves when that frame is pushed. It fails the anchor test and is refused as a domain. This refusal is the first substantive result.

**Partition or union.** The residuals of distinct communities overlap (same watershed, same species, different observations and different stakes), so this is a **union of overlapping conditions**, not a list of separate ones. Fig 9 :55: "A bundled problem is usually a union of overlapping conditions, not a list of separate ones." It must be unbundled before search. The false single-perspective move treats it as one slot, which is the aggregation error of Fig 1 and the blend of Fig 7.

**Order by dependency (Fig 3).** (1) Perception: can the decision frame tell this community's signal from what was there before? (2) Vocabulary: terms for what each community's knowledge affords. (3) Objective: what counts as progress, in each frame's own terms. (4) Planning. (5) Learn and carry, as a method rather than a recording. The chain is "ordered by dependency, not by importance" (Fig 3 :20). Effort spent on a plan before step 2 arrives at a step that cannot use it.

### 1c. Strain test applied before findings

There is a possible strain between "keep communities separate and select between them" (Fig 7) and "combine knowledge for a decision" (the Two-Eyed Seeing and co-management world sources). Re-census resolves it: combining two low-overlap frames at a described residual is a union with surplus (Fig 8 :45), while agreement inside a shared pool is not surplus and cannot be selected between. The two govern different quantities. No strain after re-census. A second strain, about independence versus cultural difference, is handled in F3 and re-checked in `11_audit.md`.

## 2. Findings

### F1 — The single-perspective move is an aggregation error (decisive; triangulated)

Fig 1 :19: "The residual is indexed by object slot, not aggregated."
Fig 1 :22: "Aggregating across slots is how a live signal disappears into an average."
Fig 1 :13: "a global R near zero with one live slot residual is a legal state"

A global conservation picture can look settled while one community's slot carries a live, unmet signal. Pooling communities into one "Indigenous input" is exactly the averaging that makes that signal vanish. The world source *Traditional ecological knowledge* corroborates: TEK "does not function as a universal set of principles, but as a family of culturally situated rules and practices rooted in specific places and ecological worldviews." The CARE principles corroborate from data governance: they are "people and purpose-oriented," a complement to data-oriented FAIR.

**Refuter:** exhibit a conservation decision in which pooling N communities into one knowledge slot preserves a live per-community residual at equal or lower cost than keeping N slots, or produce an instrument that resolves the aggregate signal as cheaply as the per-slot signal.

**Status:** triangulated (Fig 1 error signal + Fig 12 operator symmetry + two low-overlap world literatures).

### F2 — Preserve variation, select between rooms, do not blend (triangulated)

Fig 7 :14: "blending: novelty swamped to the mean"
Fig 7 :33: "variation inside, selection between"
Fig 7 :35: "A room's agreement is variation, not a verdict. Selection between rooms is where the ground votes."
Fig 7 :36: "A chain of mirrors is one long room. The next room must differ from the last, and match the residual it surfaced."

The design rule is not "add more voices to the room." It is "keep the rooms different and pick the next one against the residual the last one surfaced." A single Indigenous seat on a board is one room, not many. The world source *Fisheries co-management* supplies the operational caution: "Conflicting views and economic versus conservation issues appear to be some of the main limitations to a successful co-management process." The figures say the same thing in structure: the conflict is information, not noise to be blended away.

**Refuter:** show that a blended single-perspective input selects actions against a measured ground as well as separately maintained independent community frames, with no loss of the novel signal either frame alone carried.

**Status:** triangulated (Fig 7 selection + Fig 2 anchor).

### F3 — Agreement means something only at independence, and independence is not identity (triangulated)

Fig 2 :35: "Alignment is triangulation, not negotiation. A mirror cannot correct you."
Fig 2 :40: "agreement about the world is worthless when the agreeing frames share an evidence pool"
Fig 2 :42: "Buy independence in proportion to the cost of being wrong."
Fig 2 :31: "Independence, between two frames, is how little they draw on the same evidence."
Fig 13 :39: "Six members that overlapped would be fewer members wearing six names."

A correction to my own first reading, recorded in section 6: I initially treated "distinct communities" as sufficient for independence. It is not. Two communities that both take their picture of the fishery from the same agency survey are one frame wearing two names, however different their cultures. Independence is a property of evidence pools, not of identity. Conversely, two communities that separately observe the same return from different boats are corroboration at maximum independence (Fig 8 :50). This is the test conservation should apply, not a headcount of stakeholders.

**Refuter:** show that two frames drawing on the same report or readout are independent for this residual, or that two frames with disjoint observation histories are not.

**Status:** triangulated (Fig 2 independence + Fig 13 minimality condition).

### F4 — Enumerate the habitat from contact; do not compose it from one story (triangulated)

Fig 11 :43: "The habitat is enumerated, never composed."
Fig 11 :44: "List everything in contact with the residual, then what is in contact with those, and outward until the cascade stops mattering."
Fig 11 :49: "An actor's valence is relative to the residual, not a property it carries."
Fig 11 :51: "No standing list of good actors survives a change of residual."

This is the figure that most directly licenses Indigenous knowledge as a contribution: it is a fine-grained, long-duration enumeration of actors, conditions and relations in contact with a residual, including second- and third-order cascades. It also blocks the romantic version: no community is a permanently "good actor," because valence is relative to the residual. The same practice that benefits one species under one residual can harm another under a changed residual. Two-Eyed Seeing corroborates from education: "Western-trained academics and researchers can unconsciously or consciously categorize it within classifications to understand the concept within Western science philosophies, missing the foundational make-up of Indigenous knowledge as fluid, adaptable and continuously evolving."

**Refuter:** exhibit a conservation habitat correctly specified by composition from one knowledge system, with no enumeration of contact, that still catches a third-order cascade.

**Status:** triangulated (Fig 11 enumeration + Fig 1 per-slot + Two-Eyed Seeing source).

### F5 — It is a union that must be unbundled before search (triangulated)

Fig 9 :5: "are not one hard question but several well-formed ones. Split it rather than search."
Fig 9 :54: "And when the split lands on a union rather than a partition, unbundle before you search again."
Fig 9 :55: "A bundled problem is usually a union of overlapping conditions, not a list of separate ones."
Fig 9 :58: "It stops being a mixture and becomes a specific compound."

The Library of Babel essay, opened 2026-11-23, says the same in its own vocabulary: "If your problem was a bundle or conflation of several problems masquerading as one? You will need to search all of those problems individually to find their answers and then combine them in a satisfactory way." A single-perspective requirement bundles several communities' residuals. Unbundling yields a specific compound with its own loop, not a harder version of one community's problem.

**Refuter:** show that several communities' residuals form a strict partition with no overlapping conditions, so one lever clears each and the union is empty.

**Status:** triangulated (Fig 9 split/unbundle + Library of Babel essay, different domains).

### F6 — Imports need provenance and a shadow test; convergence is not extraction (single-source for the mechanism, triangulated with the world source)

Fig 8 :45: "Two frames together reach what neither reaches alone."
Fig 8 :50: "Convergent derivation and adopted import look identical in the contents"
Fig 8 :53: "Residual first, frame second."
Fig 6 :23: "No frame escapes its own closure. The bound moves only on contact with a frame whose closure differs."

Taking an IK observation and placing it in a conservation model is an import. Fig 8 says the surplus lives only where closures differ and the record of where each datum came from is what separates an adopted import from a convergent derivation. A dataset labelled "TEK" with no community, place, observer and authority to control has erased the only thing that makes it evidence. The Indigenous data governance source corroborates: "Indigenous data that lacks strong data governance often misrepresent Indigenous peoples, help inform policies that have discriminatory impacts on Indigenous peoples, and uphold colonial practices."

**Refuter:** exhibit an IK datum entering a conservation closure with no provenance and no shadow test that nevertheless expands the reach (adds a prediction that was not already available) and can be audited.

**Status:** triangulated for the conclusion (Fig 8 + Fig 6 + Indigenous data governance), single-source for the exact source-gate mechanism (Fig 8 only).

### F7 — Declare the stake; do not let interest in the outcome become interest in the reading (single-source)

Fig 10 :59: "an interest in the outcome is unavoidable, an interest in the reading is disqualifying"
Fig 10 :60: "Where a stake cannot be removed, declare it."
Fig 10 :52: "Bring a characterised residual, never the source."

A conservation agency wants a particular outcome (a viable population, a closed reserve); a community wants a particular outcome (a returning harvest, access to a place). Both are legitimate interests in the outcome. Neither may become an interest in the reading of the ecological residual. The dashboard, the target and the success metric are conventions authored by the seat, and Fig 10 warns that "a convention that decides an outcome has stopped being one." The declaration belongs in the record, not in a pledge of neutrality.

**Refuter:** show a co-management arrangement in which the agency's stake in a specific outcome is absent from the record and the decision is still ground-settled and auditable.

**Status:** single-source (Fig 10). Recorded as single-source, not upgraded by the world sources, which make a related but different point about authority to control data.

### F8 — What crosses scales is a method, not a recording (single-source)

Fig 4 :22: "Only methods cross up. Only head starts come down."
Fig 4 :25: "A recording carried upward looks like knowledge and is a description of one occasion."

A community's observation record is a description of one place and one stretch of time. Lifting it unchanged into a regional policy is carrying a recording upward, which looks like knowledge and is not. What can cross is the method: enumerate contact, keep provenance, range against the ground. Fig 4 requires the round trip: "So take the round trip and measure the gap. Without it you are navigating by dead reckoning: confident about a position nobody checked."

**Refuter:** show a single community's observation record, lifted unchanged to a regional decision, predicting as well at the new scale as it did locally, with the round-trip gap measured at zero.

**Status:** single-source (Fig 4). Strained? No. The scale gap is real and is measured qualitatively in `11_audit.md`.

### F9 — The break is at the middle link, and that link has no instrument (single-source, with a retraction)

Fig 3 :27: "A link with no instrument is not a link that works. It is a link nobody has looked at."
Fig 3 :22: "A step whose input never arrives cannot be diagnosed, only its predecessor can."

The middle links of the chain are (2) terms for what a community's knowledge affords and (3) what counts as progress in that community's terms. These have "neither" obvious failures nor obvious tests. Most conservation effort lands above the break, on plans and targets, because that is where instruments already exist. Funding a better plan cannot rescue a missing vocabulary. My first pass treated this as a call for more participation; re-census shows it is a call for an instrument at the vocabulary/objective link, which is a different and harder thing. See section 6.

**Refuter:** exhibit a decision process that already has a working, checkable instrument for what a given community's knowledge affords and for what counts as progress in that community's terms.

**Status:** single-source (Fig 3).

### F10 — The framework returns a shape, not a name (triangulated)

Fig 9 :32: "you get a kind of thing, never the answer itself"
Fig 9 :44: "Only a sealed room can be searched to the end, and no frame is a sealed room."
Fig 8 :39: "So a search that finds nothing is never a proof of absence."

This derivation does not name the right policy, the right community representative, or the correct balance of knowledge systems. It narrows the form any defensible answer must take and states what would refute it. "Unreachable is a verdict, not a silence" (canonical figures README). A decision that fails to find a single perspective is not a failure of the search; the single perspective may not exist.

**Refuter:** derive a unique correct conservation policy for this question from the figures alone, or show that the figures name which community should be consulted.

**Status:** triangulated (Fig 9 + canonical README + Recursive Self-Improvement Trap essay).

### 2b. Per-domain verdicts, kept separate and never averaged

- **Domain 1, conservation decision frame: triangulated.** Fig 1 (per-slot residual), Fig 11 (enumerate habitat) and Fig 3 (dependency order) are low-overlap routes to the same boundary: keep slots live, enumerate contact, then plan. Verdict: the decision must not pool communities before the residual is described per slot.
- **Domain 2, knowledge-holding communities: triangulated.** Fig 7 (variation and selection), Fig 2 (anchor and independence) and Fig 13 (independence between frames) agree that each community is a separate frame with its own ground contact, and that independence is about evidence pools, not labels. Verdict: separate frames, convergent agreement credited only at independence.
- **Domain 3, synthesis arrangement: mixed.** Unbundling and provenance are triangulated (Fig 9 + Fig 8 + Fig 6). Stake declaration (Fig 10), scale membrane (Fig 4) and the middle-link instrument (Fig 3) are single-source. Verdict: the synthesis must be a union unbundled at a described residual, with declared stakes, provenanced imports and methods (not recordings) crossing scales. No verdict is averaged across these three domains.

## 3. Pre-registered refuter

Written before the recommendation, per the exemplar's seat-map rule that "a falsifier written before the run is a design instrument, not only an honesty device."

The shape claim is refuted if any of the following holds:

1. Pooling N communities into one knowledge input preserves a live per-community residual at equal or lower cost than N separate slots (breaks F1).
2. A blended single-perspective input selects actions against a measured ground as well as independent separate frames, with no loss of novel signal (breaks F2 and F3).
3. Two frames drawing on the same evidence pool are shown to be independent for this residual, or disjoint observation histories are shown not to be (breaks F3).
4. An IK datum enters a conservation closure with no provenance and no shadow test and demonstrably expands the reach (breaks F6).
5. A single community's observation record lifts unchanged to a regional decision with a measured round-trip gap of zero (breaks F8).
6. The several communities' residuals form a strict partition with no overlapping conditions (breaks F5).
7. A working instrument already exists at the vocabulary/objective link for the communities in question (breaks F9).

If any fires, the shape is withdrawn or narrowed accordingly. Until one is run, no part of this may be labelled `grounded`.

## 4. Recommendation

1. Refuse the input slot called "the Indigenous perspective." It has no anchor (section 1b). Replace it with N explicitly named community slots, each with its own ground contact, authority to control, and stake.
2. Describe the ecological residual before choosing whose knowledge to consult (Fig 9 :27-28). A residual first search prevents any frame from seeming to fit.
3. Enumerate the habitat outward from contact (Fig 11 :44), including second- and third-order cascades, and record which community's observation supports each actor and relation.
4. Keep independence as the combination rule (Fig 2 :31), not cultural distinctness. Record the evidence pool behind each contribution.
5. Unbundle the union before searching (Fig 9 :54). Treat overlapping community residuals as a specific compound with its own loop.
6. Send the generator, not the recording: the refuter and the enumeration method, with provenance, rather than an extracted dataset (Fig 4 :22-25).
7. Build the missing instrument at the vocabulary/objective link (Fig 3 :27). This is the highest-leverage item and the least funded.

## 6. Final verdict

The shape survives; the single-perspective premise does not. Conservation should account for Indigenous knowledge by refusing the aggregate slot, enumerating each knowledge-holding community as a separate frame with its own anchor and declared stake, describing the residual before choosing a frame, keeping every imported observation provenanced and unbundled, and carrying methods rather than recordings across scales. This is a `specified`, ungrounded design shape, triangulated in Domains 1 and 2 and mixed in Domain 3. After re-census, one strain is named and none are hidden: F7 and F8 and F9 rest on single figure lines, and Fig 10's stake rule is a rule the framework imposes on itself rather than a measurement. The instrument at the vocabulary/objective link is the thing to build next, and the communities themselves are the ground that would settle it.
