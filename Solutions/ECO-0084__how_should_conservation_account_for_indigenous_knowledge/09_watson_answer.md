## QUESTION

Decide, in effect terms, what the Tether says about a conservation process that wants to use Indigenous knowledge but has one input slot called "the Indigenous perspective." Option A: keep the slot and fill it with an aggregated knowledge input. Option B: refuse the slot as anchorless and keep each community as a separate frame with its own ground contact and declared stake. What each leads to: A averages away a live per-community signal and blends distinct evidence pools into a false consensus, so agreement inside it cannot corroborate anything; B sends a checkable design shape with refuters written in advance and leaves the actual policy to the ground, which is the communities and ecological measurement.

## DOMAIN CENSUS

R by arity, symmetry and scale, one line per domain with actors, currency and anchor, partition or union, and order by dependency.

- **R (the residual).** A conservation decision is a prediction about what is changing, what is at risk and which intervention pays; the evidence is held by N distinct communities. Arity: N slots plus a decision frame plus a synthesis, so many, not binary. Symmetry: communities are not exchangeable. Fig 1 :28 "The gap is measured per object, not per step. A system that averages across objects has thrown away the only signal that says where to look." Scale: place-based observation versus regional decision, with a lossy membrane (Fig 4). Fig 9 names it a union of overlapping conditions, not one hard question.
- **Domain 1, conservation decision frame.** Actors: managers, agency scientists, planners, regulators. Currency: outcomes, budgets, legal mandates. Anchor: measured ecological state and institutional record, which do not move when a frame thinks. Nameable, so a domain. Question type: partition boundary (which slot's residual is live).
- **Domain 2, knowledge-holding communities (many, distinct).** Actors: each community, Elders, harvesters, internal dissent. Currency: place-based observation, practice, value, oral history under authority to control. Anchor: the lived place and each community's own outcome record. Nameable per community, so a domain. Question type: many separate frames, not one.
- **Domain 3, synthesis / co-management arrangement.** Actors: boards, review panels, conveners. Currency: agreement and residuals. Anchor: the ground, ecological plus community-defined, never agreement (Fig 2 :45). Nameable, so a domain. Question type: union versus partition, bundle versus unbundled compound.
- **Candidate, "the Indigenous perspective" as one slot.** Actors: none of its own; produced by Domain 1's need for one input. Currency: none. Anchor: none, because its "anchor" is the frame's preference and moves when pushed. It fails the anchor test and is refused as a domain. This refusal is the first result.
- **Order by dependency.** Describe the residual before choosing a frame (Fig 9 :27-28); build the vocabulary/objective link before planning (Fig 3 :20, :27); enumerate the habitat before composing a plan (Fig 11 :44); keep the membrane lossy and measure the gap (Fig 4 :30).

Strain test applied: Fig 7 (selection between rooms) and Fig 8 (union surplus) initially look opposed. Re-census resolves it: selection governs which distinct frame is booked next, union surplus governs what two low-overlap frames reach at a described residual. Different objects. One live strain remains, that F7, F8 and F9 each rest on a single figure line; they are marked single-source, not promoted by repetition.

## WORLD (secondary, each source read)

World sources corroborate only. They are from genuinely low-overlap fields: ecology/anthropology, Indigenous education, data governance, fisheries management and narrative humanities. None of them settles the conservation ground, so none is a witness for the verdict.

- **Ecology / anthropology.** Wikipedia contributors, "Traditional ecological knowledge" (Wikipedia, accessed 2026-11-23): "does not function as a universal set of principles, but as a family of culturally situated rules and practices rooted in specific places and ecological worldviews." Predicts that a single perspective cannot be faithful; corroborates Fig 1 and Fig 7.
- **Indigenous education / method.** Wikipedia contributors, "Two-Eyed Seeing" (Wikipedia, accessed 2026-11-23): Albert Marshall describes seeing "from one eye with the strengths of Indigenous ways of knowing, and to see from the other eye with the strengths of Western ways of knowing, and to use both of these eyes together." The same page warns that researchers "categorize it within classifications" and miss that Indigenous knowledge is "fluid, adaptable and continuously evolving." Predicts keeping two distinct frames; corroborates Fig 7, with the caveat that Two-Eyed Seeing is not itself a single recipe.
- **Information / institutional governance.** Wikipedia contributors, "Indigenous data governance" (Wikipedia, accessed 2026-11-23): "the CARE principles are people and purpose oriented rather than data oriented, and are rooted in Indigenous world views." Predicts that provenance and purpose are part of the datum; corroborates Fig 8.
- **Data governance.** Wikipedia contributors, "CARE Principles for Indigenous Data Governance" (Wikipedia, accessed 2026-11-23): CARE is "people and purpose-oriented" and a complement to "data-oriented perspective of other standards such as FAIR data," and includes Authority to Control. Predicts authority-to-control as a condition on imports; corroborates Fig 8 :50.
- **Fisheries / co-management.** Wikipedia contributors, "Fisheries co-management" (Wikipedia, accessed 2026-11-23): "co-management is the combination of scientific and traditional knowledge" and "Conflicting views and economic versus conservation issues appear to be some of the main limitations to a successful co-management process." Predicts that conflict is information to be handled, not blended away; corroborates Fig 7.
- **Narrative / humanities.** Chimamanda Ngozi Adichie, "The danger of a single story" (TEDGlobal, 2009, transcript accessed 2026-11-23): "if we hear only a single story about another person or country, we risk a critical misunderstanding." Predicts the aggregation error from a non-ecological field; corroborates Fig 1 at low overlap.
- **Framework essays (high overlap with the figures, so weak corroboration).** Isaiah N, "The Library of Babel: Locating The Book of Books" (Substack, 2026-10-02): "you can cut your search short if the set of books returned is small and descriptive enough that it provides a general shape of the answer that satisfies your question." ## VERDICT (from the figures)

The single-perspective premise is refused and the shape survives. "The Indigenous perspective" has no anchor and is not a domain. Conservation should keep each knowledge-holding community as a separate slot with its own ground contact, declared stake and authority to control; describe the ecological residual before choosing a frame; enumerate the habitat from contact including cascades; use evidence-pool independence rather than identity to decide when agreement counts; select between distinct rooms rather than blending them; keep imports provenanced and unbundled; carry methods, not recordings, across scales; and build the missing instrument at the vocabulary/objective link. This is a `specified`, ungrounded design shape. Domain 1 and Domain 2 verdicts are triangulated; Domain 3 is mixed, with the stake, scale and middle-link rules single-source. Nothing here settles which policy is correct or who should represent whom.

- **cost if wrong:** bounded and locatable. The packet states mode, refuters and provenance before the conclusion, so an error is findable rather than invisible. The larger cost is spending the independence of outside readers and the authority of communities by sending an extracted recording instead of a generator with provenance. Fig 2 :42 "Buy independence in proportion to the cost of being wrong." That cost is avoidable by leading with the shape and its refuter and by never presenting an unprovenanced datum as evidence.
- **undoable:** yes. The aggregate slot can be removed without touching the derivation; `07_the_tether_answer.md` and `08_expert_verification_packet.md` already remove it. A sent claim can be superseded by a corrected packet, and no community's standing is withdrawn by the correction. Provenance makes the reversal traceable.
- **reversed by:** a ground reading, not agreement. A per-slot experiment showing that pooling communities preserves the live residual at equal or lower cost; a demonstration that two frames sharing an evidence pool are independent for this residual; a measured round-trip gap of zero for a local record lifted to a regional rule; a working instrument at the vocabulary/objective link; or a figure re-census that supplies an anchor for the aggregate slot. Until one of these fires, the verdict stands as `candidate`. "So a search that finds nothing is never a proof of absence" (Fig 8 :39).
