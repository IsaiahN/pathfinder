## What is deliberately not here

- No personal emails of named individuals.
- No ranking of people or communities by how likely they are to agree with this shape.
- No bulk-send plan and no extraction of knowledge from communities without provenance and authority to control (the CARE "Authority to Control" point, opened 2026-11-23).

Three reasons, in the figures' own words:

1. **An anchor is not a vote.** *"An anchor is whatever settles the question and does not change its answer because of what any frame thinks."* (Fig 2 :29). A community is a frame ranging against its own ground, not a constituency to be averaged into a consensus.
2. **Correlation disqualifies a verdict.** *"agreement about the world is worthless when the agreeing frames share an evidence pool"* (Fig 2 :40). Selecting respondents for likely agreement manufactures the correlation that makes their agreement worthless.
3. **A verifier must stay outside.** *"A verifier that can reconstruct the claim cannot verify it."* (Fig 10 :42). The first contact carries the residual and its refuter, never the framework's interior.

Legally: scraping personal emails for unsolicited bulk outreach breaches GDPR and CAN-SPAM-style rules in many jurisdictions, and it reproduces the same error the figures name, treating people as a list to be mined rather than frames to range against a ground.
