## 1. A shape is a constraint, not a filler

The canonical usage rule is that the framework returns *"what a missing thing would have to look like (a shape, not a name)."* Fig 9 :32 says *"you get a kind of thing, never the answer itself."* Fig 13 :134 says *"Unreachable is a verdict here, not a silence."*

From *The Library of Babel*, opened 2026-11-23: *"you can cut your search short if the set of books returned is small and descriptive enough that it provides a general shape of the answer that satisfies your question."*

So a **shape** is the narrowed form of an answer you can state before you can name it. It constrains; it does not fill. For this question:

- "the harm account must range against an external ground" is a shape;
- "use GRI Standard 102" is a name;
- "the harm residual must be enumerated per slot through the contact cascade" is a shape;
- "mandate a specific carbon price" is a name.

The first member of each pair can be derived from the figures. The second cannot, because naming a standard is a binding from a world frame onto atoms already held, and no source gate was supplied in this folder.

## 2. How a shape is obtained

- *Library of Babel*, 2026-11-23: use the vocabulary already held that is closest to the thing, then *"With each new term gained, you narrow the space by definition and deepen your vocabulary."*
- *Library of Babel* on bundling: *"Perhaps your problem was a bundle or conflation of several problems masquerading as one?"* This is the essay's version of Fig 9 :54-57 and it is the first move in `01`, before any figure line is applied.
- Fig 9 :30-31: "your words are borrowed too, so describe the effect rather than the cause." So the residual was described as a dropped channel reading per slot, not as "an externality," which is a borrowed name from economics.
- *Library of Babel*: the answer is reached when *"the finish line will also be the starting place"*, and the paradox is that by then you have *"composed the answer through the process of locating it."*

## 3. What licenses a name

Only two things, both outside the shape itself:

1. **The ground settles it.** Formula step 5: "the ground settles it. Verified only by what pays. A gate is not the ground." The canonical README says the framework *"stops at the limit of what the available instruments can distinguish, and records the lineage so the stop is honest."*
2. **A provenanced import from a frame whose closure differs** (Fig 6 :23, Fig 8 :50-52), with a source gate and a shadow test.

From *They're Scanning Books When They Should*, carried from the exemplar reading: *"The real import is not a new term in the existing space. It is a new dimension of the space."* And: *"Declaring the primitives does not make them neutral. It makes the choice visible."*

For ECN-0090, the names that circulate in the field, externality, social cost, materiality, double materiality, are bindings from the economics or accounting frame. They are useful vocabulary for a search. They are not licenses to fill the slots of the figure-derived shape.

## 4. Why the price cannot comment on the harm: the source error

*The Hard Problem is About Source*, opened 2026-11-23, gives the mechanism. Metamerism: an instrument reads green and *"cannot comment on the source."* One green light, or yellow plus blue, or RGB, or CMYK, or a scheme not yet discovered can produce the same reading. *"If several paths create the same behavior, then behavior is a projection with lossy information."* And: *"An instrument that can't distinguish these cases demonstrates its own inadequacy."* It was the **spectrometer**, not an argument, that resolved it. The essay's line: *"The answer is unreached until a better instrument exists."*

Applied here: a price reads a transaction. It cannot comment on the harm source. Two very different production paths, one clean and one damaging, can carry the same price. Calling the price the ground for the harm question is the metamerism error: a source named from a reading where several sources give the same reading. What is owed is a discriminator, not a verdict from the price.

This is the deepest reason the question cannot be answered as a simple yes or no. "Not reflected in market prices" is a statement about instrument inadequacy. Fig 2 :37-38 says an instrument "can be wrong on axes they" do not measure. The remedy is to extend the instrument, and Fig 6 :32 warns that an instrument is improved from a worse instrument already returning something, not specified from an account of what it should see.

## 5. What shape means for this question

The bound shape is four conditions, kept separate (Fig 9 :57):

| condition | shape statement | why it is a shape, not a name |
|---|---|---|
| C1 external ground | harm must be measured from something that does not move when the firm reports | Fig 2 :36: anchor legitimacy is question-relative; no instrument is named |
| C2 cascade enumeration | the habitat must be enumerated outward per slot until the cascade stops mattering | Fig 11 :43; no boundary is named |
| C3 violable convention | a mandate makes the bug statable and installs what can be violated | Fig 10 :16-19; no standard is named |
| C4 two readings | the money reading and the physical reading are held apart | Fig 12 :125-130; no unit conversion is specified |

**Q: Does the framework license a "yes, require it" verdict?** No. Fig 9 :32 returns a kind of thing, never the answer itself. What it does license is a conditional shape: *if* an account is required, then it must satisfy C1 to C4; and a purely voluntary regime fails C3 because self-report is frame-internal (Fig 1 :27) while a purely mandatory regime fails C3 if the metric cannot be violated (Fig 10 :47-49). That is the strongest statement the figures support, and it is `specified`, not `grounded`.

**Q: Does the framework license naming a particular harm accounting standard?** No. *The Hard Problem is About Source* says the answer is unreached until a better instrument exists. A standard is a convention, and a convention that decides an outcome has stopped being one (Fig 10 :47).

## 6. Two consequences for outreach

**Prestige is not action.** From *The Recursive Self-Improvement Trap*, opened 2026-11-23: *"Prestige must never be allowed to validate an action directly"*; *"Prestige can fund an action. It can give an action a budget to be tried. But it cannot settle an action. Only the ground can settle it."* A well-known standard setter gives a regime a budget to be tried. It does not make the account true. Do not gather prestigious names to agree with the shape.

**This audit is a simulation, not a ground reading.** Same essay: *"LLMs can quickly simulate an approximate answer/output with the formula found in the architecture post"* but *"the truly testable output would need to be derived from the real architecture or working the formula yourself."* So `01` is a simulated derivation checked for internal consistency against the figures. It is **not** a measurement of any firm's harm and must be labelled as such. It cannot settle anything it reports on.
