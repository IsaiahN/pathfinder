## Three findings in the wider literature that bear on this question

1. **The voluntary pole and the mandatory pole both leave grey zones.** Gatti, Seele and Rademacher 2019 find that a wholly voluntary regime enables greenwashing and that an exclusively mandatory one also creates room to work around rules. Their proposal is a combination of the two. This corroborates F7 in `01`: a union needs two conditions, not one lever. It is one witness from business ethics, not a second verdict.
2. **Reporting mandates can be ceremonial.** The AAAJ review of more than 130 archival studies reports no significant change in reporting quality and a continuing ceremonial pattern after regulation. That is the world-language version of Fig 10 :47, "a convention that decides an outcome has stopped being one." It is abstract-level evidence and carries no weight for the figure verdict.
3. **There is already a per-slot instrument shape in the field.** Forliano et al. 2025 record that researchers have built indicators measuring the gap between perceived and actual environmental performance. That is the shape of `R = |Γ(b,a) − o′|` at the firm level, arrived at independently. Under Fig 8 :50-52, convergent derivation and adopted import look identical in the contents; only the record separates them. It corroborates the shape, and the record here says it was found after the derivation, not imported before it.

## Distribution: offer, do not push

Figure-grounded rules:

- **Fig 10 :52:** "Bring a characterised residual, never the source." Sending the whole framework before anyone has described the residual sends a recording, not a generator.
- **Fig 10 :42:** "A verifier that can reconstruct the claim cannot verify it." A recipient who has absorbed the interiors before giving a verdict has joined the frame being checked.
- **Fig 9 :27-28:** "describe it before you go looking, or any frame you pick will seem to fit." Ask what they would expect to see if the shape claim were true before giving them any framework material.
- **Fig 2 :40:** "agreement about the world is worthless when the agreeing frames share an evidence pool." Do not select recipients by likely agreement and do not pool later agreement as corroboration.
- **Fig 4 :22:** "Only methods cross up. Only head starts come down." The loop may be offered as a method after a verdict is recorded; the conclusion may not be pushed as a belief.

**Order in a reply:** (1) the residual and the four conditions; (2) the pre-registered refuter and the question "what would you count as a failure?"; (3) record the verdict; (4) only if curious, the two local framework links; (5) offer the formula as a method, with the instruction that each person writes their own refuter and states their own mode.

## The two-stage offer

1. **Stage 1, headline first.** Send the residual in effect terms, the four separate conditions and the refuter. Ask for a correction on shape. No theory, no links, no request for agreement or trust.
2. **Stage 2, opt-in only.** If they are curious after recording a verdict, offer the two framework links and note that the figures, not this summary, are the authority.
3. **Stage 3, method only.** Offer `THE_FORMULA.md` as something to reuse on a problem they can check against a ground. Each user writes their own refuter before the run and states the mode of what they get.

**Hard precondition:** stages 1 to 3 only work if stage 1 survives. If the four conditions are blended into one "impact score" or the answer is delivered as a yes or no policy verdict, the packet fails Fig 10 :26 (pooled readings) and Fig 9 :32 (a kind of thing, never the answer itself). Repair before sending.
