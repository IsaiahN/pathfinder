# The Tether's answer on corporate harm accounting

**Mode: `specified` — not `grounded`.** This is a shape and a route, not a policy verdict. Simulated derivation checked against the figures (`05_what_shape_means.md` §6), not a ground reading.

## 1. The residual it answers

The firm's price signal predicts what the market will pay. What actually happens includes harm that never enters the price. `R = |Γ(b,a) − o′|` per exposed slot, never as a corporate total. Fig 1 :19 "The residual is indexed by object slot, not aggregated." The question asks whether to require an account of the dropped portion of `o′`.

This is a **union compound**, not one hard question. Fig 9 :54 "And when the split lands on a union rather than a partition, unbundle before you search again." The unbundled conditions are C1 external ground, C2 cascade enumeration, C3 violable convention, C4 two readings held apart. They have different instruments and are never averaged.

## 2. The shape

1. **The price is not the anchor for the harm axis.** Fig 2 :36 "An anchor is legitimate when the question is constitutively about it." The question is about harmed people and ecosystems, not about the market's clearing price. Fig 2 :37 "It is only an instrument for is this code correct, and instruments can be wrong on axes they" (Fig 2 :38 "do not measure."). So "not reflected in market prices" is a statement about instrument coverage, not about the harm's existence.

2. **The harm is a channel failure, not ground decay.** Fig 10 :24 "the ground does not decay, the channel does", with Fig 10 :26 "stale percepts, dropped transients, saturated metrics, pooled readings". A corporate total is a pooled reading; it destroys the live signal that says where to look.

3. **The harm is usually three steps out.** Fig 11 :43 "The habitat is enumerated, never composed." Fig 11 :52 "Scoping the habitat to direct contact is how DDT reached the eagles: first-order enumeration, second-order effect, and the residual that mattered was three steps out." An account scoped to direct transactions misses the residual that mattered.

4. **A mandate is a convention that makes the bug statable.** Fig 10 :16 "the convention does not fix the bug" and Fig 10 :17 "it makes the bug statable"; Fig 10 :19 "→ install what can be violated". But Fig 10 :47 "a convention that decides an outcome has stopped being one" and Fig 10 :49 "Declaring a meaning does not ground it." A self-reported account is a frame scoring itself (Fig 1 :27 "They are frame-internal. A frame cannot score itself with a quantity it also produces.").

5. **The harm term is an import that must pay.** Fig 6 :14 "the only operator that moves the wall"; Fig 6 :23 "No frame escapes its own closure. The bound moves only on contact with a frame whose closure differs." The import needs a source gate and a shadow test (Fig 8 :12 "source gate: provenance"; Fig 8 :13 "shadow test: explains a gap") and must pass Fig 5 :43 "A new term is worth having when stating it, plus what remains unexplained after it, costs less than what was unexplained before." A metric that changes no decision is "nothing new here" (Fig 5 :29).

6. **Two readings are held apart.** Fig 12 :125 "A composition costs in two currencies, and they do not add" and Fig 12 :130 "Summing them into one figure is the error this names, not the fix for it." The money reading and the physical reading are reported together, not added. This is a cross-level binding from Fig 12's bits-and-actions claim, licensed by Fig 4 :20, and recorded as a strain in `01` F6 rather than treated as a direct externality law.

7. **Agreement is not a verdict.** Fig 7 :13 "agreement compounds, evidence does not", Fig 7 :35 "A room’s agreement is variation, not a verdict. Selection between rooms is where the ground votes." An industry voluntary standard in which everyone agrees is a room, not a ground. Fig 2 :42 "Buy independence in proportion to the cost of being wrong."

8. **The answer stops at a shape.** Fig 9 :32 "you get a kind of thing, never the answer itself", Fig 13 :134 "Unreachable is a verdict here, not a silence." No figure line names the standard, the threshold or the jurisdiction. That is a declared `gap`.

## 3. What it explicitly does not say

- Not yes and not no. The figures do not return a policy verdict; they return four conditions any answer must satisfy.
- Not that markets never work. They are the right anchor for questions constitutively about allocation; they are an instrument on the harm axis.
- Not that a voluntary regime is worthless. It fails only C3 when self-report is the sole checker; the shared functional group (`06` Q2) is intact.
- Not that a mandatory regime settles anything. It fails C3 when the metric cannot be violated and becomes an authored constant.
- Not that a corporate total, a social cost figure or a single money number is an account of the residual. Each is a pooled reading or a summed currency.
- Not that a particular standard, carbon price or jurisdiction fills the slots. Those are bindings from world frames with no source gate in this folder.
- Not `grounded`. No physical harm measurement pays for any of these bindings here.

## 4. What would settle it — the ground

A source-discriminating instrument, not a further argument. *The Hard Problem is About Source*, opened 2026-11-23: a meter that reads green *"cannot comment on the source"*; the spectrometer resolved it, not an argument. Here the corresponding tests are:

- Does a price track the physical harm per named slot with the reporting frame held fixed? If yes, C1 is satisfied by the market and F1 falls.
- Does the harm register at zero beyond direct contact in a given domain? If yes, C2's specified cascade claim weakens there.
- Can the mandated metric be violated by any possible observation, and does an external checker use evidence not drawn from the firm's pool? If not, C3 fails.
- Does a single monetized aggregate prospectively predict both decision and physical trajectory with no residual on either axis? If yes, C4 falls.
- Does one instrument resolve all four conditions with no categorically different residue? If yes, the union collapses into a partition (Fig 9 :57).

**Standing falsifiers** are the nine listed in `01` §4, written before this conclusion. Prestige or agreement is not such an instrument.

## 5. The honest stop

**Unreached, not unreachable.** The missing thing is an external source-discriminating instrument for the harm, characterized in effect terms but not held here. Fig 8 :39: "So a search that finds nothing is never a proof of absence." Fig 6 :32: "An instrument is not built from a description. It is improved from a worse instrument already returning something." The account stops at the limit of the available instruments and records the lineage so the stop is checkable.

## 6. One-paragraph version for outreach

> The Tether does not answer yes or no to mandatory harm accounting, and it does not name a standard. It says that an unpriced harm is a reading the price channel drops, not an absent fact, and that the market is an instrument on the harm axis rather than the ground that settles it. The harm sits several steps out in a contact cascade, so an account must be enumerated per exposed party, not totalled as a corporate score. Requiring an account authors a convention that makes the harm statable and violable, but only an external, independent ground settles it; a self-reported metric is a frame scoring itself. Adding the harm term to the firm's library is an import that needs provenance and must pay for itself, and the money reading and the physical reading are held apart rather than summed. Voluntary and mandatory regimes are isomers over the same atoms: same ingredients, different bond, and the difference is decisive in behaviour. What the framework offers is that shape, written falsifiers, and the instruments that would close it. Which standard fills the slot stays open until a ground settles it.
