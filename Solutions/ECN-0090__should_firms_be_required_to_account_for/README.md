## What is deliberately not here

- No personal emails of named individuals.
- No ranking of people by how likely they are to agree with the claim.
- No bulk-send plan.
- No policy verdict of the form "yes, mandate it" or "no, leave it to firms." Fig 9 :32 says *"you get a kind of thing, never the answer itself."*

The reasons are figure-derived. Fig 2 :35 says *"Alignment is triangulation, not negotiation. A mirror cannot correct you."* Fig 2 :40 says *"agreement about the world is worthless when the agreeing frames share an evidence pool."* Selecting recipients for likely agreement manufactures the correlation that makes their agreement worthless, and asking them to trust the framework before they check the residual spends the only thing they contribute.
