## VERDICT (from the figures)

The shape survives; a yes or no does not. The unpriced harm is a dropped channel reading, not an absent fact; the market is an instrument on the harm axis, not the anchor for it; the harm sits several steps out in a contact cascade that must be enumerated per slot; a requirement to account is a convention that makes the harm statable and violable but is not itself the ground; importing the harm term into the firm's library needs provenance and must pay the acceptance bargain; and the money reading and the physical reading are held apart, not summed. Voluntary and mandatory regimes are isomers over the same atom set, with different bonds and different failure modes. Which standard, threshold or jurisdiction fills the slots is a `gap`. Mode `specified`, ungrounded, candidate. The world sources corroborate the mechanism shape but never decide it.

- **cost if wrong:** a single lever is applied to a union. Fig 9 :55 says a single lever "clears the region where only one condition holds and leaves the intersections standing, because the second condition still binds." Concretely, a disclosure template is mandated, reporting quality stays ceremonial, and the external ground, cascade scope and two-currency separation remain unexamined. The cost is bounded and locatable because the four conditions are recorded separately and each has a refuter; it is avoidable by leading with the conditions rather than the template. Fig 2 :42 "Buy independence in proportion to the cost of being wrong."
- **undoable:** yes. A convention is revisable, and a refutation is an addition rather than a deletion, so the reach falls "because something was added rather than because anything was deleted" (Fig 6 :42-43). No primitive has to be withdrawn, and the lineage in `01` lets a later reader locate the error rather than only feel it (Fig 10 :62-64).
- **reversed by:** a ground reading, not agreement. A prospective measurement showing price tracks physical harm per slot with the reporting frame fixed would break F1; an aggregate that predicts per-slot breaches with no live signal lost would break F2; a domain with zero cascade contribution would break F3; a metric that cannot be violated would break F4; a single monetized aggregate predicting both trajectories on both axes would break F6; one instrument resolving all four conditions would break F7. The figures themselves would be broken by deriving one of the six terms from the other five or by a failure of closure idempotence. Until one fires, the verdict stands as `candidate`.
