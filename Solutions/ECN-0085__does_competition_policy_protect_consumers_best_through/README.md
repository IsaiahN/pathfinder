## Restating the question as an effect

A regulator (a seat) acts on a market habitat. The effect in question is consumer welfare, indexed per consumer slot, never aggregated (Fig 1 :19). The three offered options are three instruments that return partial readings on different axes. "Best" is a threshold comparison (Operators table :51), so it needs a ground that decides, not agreement among frames (Fig 2 :35). The options as written assume a partition (one lever), or a sum (all three). The figures decide which shape is licensed before any lever is named.

## What an answer would look like

A binding of "best" onto a term. Under the formula's "Direction" step, the sought-for shape is made an ordinary term, written down before the run, and allowed to accrue a residual like anything else. The shape claim here is: consumer welfare is the anchor; the levers are partial instruments per slot; they form a union of overlapping conditions, not a menu or a sum; and a per-slot ranking is unavailable until a source-discriminating instrument exists.
