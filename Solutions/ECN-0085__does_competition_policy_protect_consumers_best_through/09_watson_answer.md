## QUESTION

Decide, in effect terms, what the Tether says about the offered menu. Option A: one of price, choice or innovation is generally best. Option B: "all three" is a composite score. Option C: the four options misdescribe a union compound, and no general ranking is available until a source-discriminating instrument exists. ## DOMAIN CENSUS

R by arity, symmetry and scale, then one line per domain giving actors / currency / anchor, partition or union, and order by dependency.

- **R (the residual).** Consumer welfare per consumer slot. Arity: three-place (policy action, market habitat, consumer slot). Symmetry: the levers are not interchangeable; "best" is a threshold comparison, and a symmetric conjunction would collapse arrangements that evidence says differ. Scale: price returns immediately, choice over a medium horizon, innovation over a long one; the transform between scales is lossy. R is per slot: "The residual is indexed by object slot, not aggregated." (Fig 1 :19).
- **D1 Consumer ground.** Actors: consumers per slot and their judgments. Currency: welfare. Anchor: consumer welfare itself, legitimate because the question is about people. Admitted, ground.
- **D2 Market habitat.** Actors: firms, entrants, suppliers, customers. Currency: price, variety, innovation, survival. Anchor: customer choice and the market. Admitted. The habitat is "enumerated, never composed" (Fig 11 :43).
- **D3 Policy seat.** Actors: competition authority, courts. Currency: enforcement actions and remedies. Anchor: the ground, kept reachable. Admitted with declared stake, because "an interest in the outcome is unavoidable, an interest in the reading is disqualifying" (Fig 10 :59).
- **D4 Instrument room.** Actors: price index, variety index, innovation count, dashboard. Currency: readings in bits and cost of settling in actions. Anchor: the ground, never the index, because "instruments can be wrong on axes they do not measure" (Fig 2 :38).
- **X1 Total welfare as the goal.** Actors: producers and shareholders. Currency: total surplus. Anchor: none for this question, because "An anchor is legitimate when the question is constitutively about it" (Fig 2 :36). Not a domain; it answers a different question.
- **X2 Best lever in the abstract.** No nameable anchor, so not a domain yet.

**Union, not partition.** "And when the split lands on a union rather than a partition, unbundle before you search again." (Fig 9 :54). "A bundled problem is usually a union of overlapping conditions, not a list of separate ones." (Fig 9 :55). The overlaps are compounds with their own loops. **Order by dependency:** perception (read R per slot), vocabulary (terms for what each lever affords), objective (progress in consumer terms), planning (choose lever), learn and carry (Fig 3).

Strain test applied: Fig 13 :74 "Everything else is a composition across the two kinds, never within one" was tried on the three levers and failed. The levers are not the six substrate terms; the line is declined as governing this question, and the decline is preserved in `01_figure_grounded_audit.md` F9.

## VERDICT (from the figures)

The offered menu is the wrong shape. The figures decide that consumer welfare is the anchor and the three options are partial instruments read per consumer slot; that they form a union of overlapping conditions, so one lever leaves the intersections standing; that their axes are two currencies that do not add, making a composite score the error rather than the fix; and that the seat introduces and cannot subtract, making this permanent work. "All three" is the honest structural form only as a compound with its own ground, never as a summed index. No figure line names a generally best lever; that is a `gap`, not a hidden answer. Mode `general` on the shape, `specified` on the application, not `grounded`.

- **cost if wrong:** applying one lever to a union leaves the intersections standing, and a composite index hides the hard axis behind the cheap one. The cost is misdirected enforcement across whole markets, and it is bounded and locatable because the shape is mode-labelled and the four refuters are recorded before the conclusion. "Buy independence in proportion to the cost of being wrong." (Fig 2 :42).
- **undoable:** yes. No term is deleted if the claim fails; a refutation is added and the reach falls because something was added rather than struck out. The shape and its refuters are recorded in a provenance trail so a later reader can locate the error rather than only feel it.
- **reversed by:** a ground reading, not agreement. Specifically: a lossless three-axis instrument per slot; one intervention that clears the intersections; a ground reading showing welfare invariant to one axis across all slots; a composite whose weights are ground-verified compensable for every slot; a one-time introduction that settles welfare permanently; or a genuinely independent evidence pool showing per-slot welfare fully predicted by one axis with no interaction term. Until one fires, the verdict stands as `candidate`.
