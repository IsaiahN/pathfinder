# Expert verification packet: the Tether on coupled-network crisis amplification

**What this is:** a claim in a form a network-science, infrastructure or systemic-risk researcher can check. Mode, derivation and refuters are stated before the conclusion, so a reader knows what to look for and what would settle it.

**Mode: `general` on the structural shape, `specified` on the crisis reading, not `grounded`.** No coupling-specific sign or threshold is claimed. This is a shape and a route, not a result. Simulated derivation checked for internal consistency against the figures, not a ground reading.

## 1. The claim, in one paragraph

Amplification in coupled networks is a signed cycle, not a long chain: the output of a cross-network dependency returns to its own input, and the sign of the loop decides damping or growth. What compounds is the remainder of each crossing (unserved flow, unsettled payment, missed control signal). Two networks are both structures, so they join only through a state that can be named and measured, such as power flow or settlement finality. The coupled system is a union compound, so unbundling precedes any single lever, and a coupling's valence flips with the residual, so connectivity is not a standing good. The monitoring channel degrades before the ground does, which explains warning-free collapses. The specific coupling, loop sign and threshold are a declared gap.

## 2. Three checkable sub-claims

### C1: framework-internal; checkable by reading Figures 12 and 13

- **Statement:** a cascade is a cycle whose sign decides amplification; the returned remainder compounds; two networks need a state-carrying joint.
- **Source, verbatim:** Fig 12 :121: "cycle. The output re-enters as input. The sign of the loop decides whether it closes, and a chain cannot close by construction." Fig 13 :32: "comes from: a remainder compounding through a chain that amplifies is chaos, and the detail a crossing discards reappears above it as noise." Fig 13 :75: "Two states never bond directly. It is not space-time but space, a structure, time; and two structures join only through a state."
- **Refuter:** measure persistent growth in a genuine chain with no returned signal; or document a coupled failure whose residual is non-increasing at every handoff with no hidden slot; or show influence travelling between two networks with no intervening state.

### C2: world-facing; checkable against an outage or settlement record

- **Statement:** the coupling's valence is relative to the residual, and cross-network effects run several steps out; a first-order-only account is insufficient.
- **What to check:** in a documented event (Italy 2003 is the worked instance), does the same interconnection help at small disturbance and harm at large; is there a third-order residual; does a single lever clear the physical-only region but leave the coupled intersection standing?
- **Sources world-side:** Buldyrev et al., Nature 2010: "a failure of a very small fraction of nodes in one network may lead to the complete fragmentation of a system of several interdependent networks." Acemoglu et al., AER 2015: "the same factors that contribute to resilience under certain conditions may function as significant sources of systemic risk under others." 2003 Italy blackout record: "the shutdown of power stations directly led to the failure of nodes in the Internet communication network, which in turn caused further breakdown of power stations."
- **Figure support:** Fig 11 :49: "An actor’s valence is relative to the residual, not a property it carries." Fig 11 :52: "And contact cascades. Scoping the habitat to direct contact is how DDT reached the eagles: first-order enumeration, second-order effect, and the residual that mattered was three" (continuing "steps out"). Fig 9 :54: "And when the split lands on a union rather than a partition, unbundle before you search again."
- **Refuter:** an invariant coupling valence across a documented residual change in both directions; or a first-order enumeration that accounts for every observed cross-network failure; or one intervention that restores both regions with no categorically different residue.

### C3: verification-internal; checkable by an operator or regulator

- **Statement:** in a crisis the channel degrades before the ground, so apparent absence of warning is a channel failure; the residual must be read per slot.
- **Source, verbatim:** Fig 10 :24: "the ground does not decay, the channel does." Fig 10 :26: "stale percepts, dropped transients," and "saturated metrics, pooled readings". Fig 1 :19: "The residual is indexed by object slot, not aggregated." Fig 2 :29: "An anchor is whatever settles the question and does not change its answer because of what any frame thinks. A measurement, an experiment, a counter, a market, a patient outcome."
- **World-side check:** the Cascading failure page records that "the speed of the control signal is no faster than the speed of the propagating power overload", and the Italy blackout record says the operator "was not aware of the urgency".
- **Refuter:** a crisis where the channel stayed unsaturated, unpooled and faster than the overload, yet warnings were unreadable for a reason that is not channel-related; or a measurement in which an averaged index identifies the failing slot with no loss and no per-slot reading.

## 3. What is explicitly not claimed

- Not that connectivity alone causes crises, or that any density threshold is known.
- Not that a named coupling (telecom depending on power, interbank exposure, fuel delivery) is *the* amplifier. Naming it is a binding from the engineering or finance frame, provenanced as such, and still unproven here.
- Not a single systemic-risk index. Per-slot residual is the signal (Fig 1 :22: "Aggregating across slots is how a live signal disappears into an average").
- Not that "no warning" implies "no residual". It implies a channel failure, and the distinction is empirical for each event.
- Not a new primitive, not a derivation of network physics from finance or vice versa.
- Not `grounded`. Nothing here is asserted as settled by an instrument in this run.
- Not a theory of everything. The coupled intersection remains a compound with its own loop.

## 5. What would settle it

A per-slot, source-discriminating instrument, not a further argument. The exemplar's *The Hard Problem is About Source* entry (quote recorded there, not re-opened this session) notes that a reading cannot comment on its source; several sources can give the same reading. The corresponding tests:

1. **Sign Test:** does the returned signal at a cross-network handoff reduce or increase the originating slot residual? Persistent growth with a reducing returned signal, no second positive loop and no external drive refutes C1.
2. **Medium Test:** can influence be shown to travel with no intervening state? If yes, C1 fails.
3. **Order Test:** does first-order enumeration account for every cross-network failure with no third-order residual? If yes, C2 is refuted.
4. **Channel Test:** does the monitoring channel stay readable while the cascade runs, and does a source-discriminating reading name the live coupling? That would move the gap in section 3 toward a ground answer.

4. Send the refutation. A failure is more useful than an endorsement.
