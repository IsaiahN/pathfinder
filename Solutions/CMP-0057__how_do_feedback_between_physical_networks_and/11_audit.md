## Round trip gap `R_T`

The Symbols table defines `R_T` as the gap between what was sent up and what came back down, `gap(x, (T_E ∘ T_A)(x))`, and Fig 4 :30 requires it to be measured rather than assumed. I ran the round trip on the condensation this folder actually uses.

1. **Sent up (x):** the verbatim figure set, chiefly Fig 12 :121 (cycle and sign), Fig 13 :32 (compounding remainder), Fig 13 :75 (state-carrying joint), Fig 11 :49 (valence), Fig 10 :24 (channel), Fig 1 :19 (per-slot residual).
2. **Abstraction (T_A):** the shorthand in `07_the_tether_answer.md`: amplification is a signed cycle; the remainder compounds; networks join through a state; the compound is a union; valence flips; the channel degrades.
3. **Concretisation (T_E):** a claimed answer to the actual question, for example "the telecom-to-power dependency closed a positive loop in the 2003 Italy blackout and that is what amplified the crisis".

**`R_T` is nonzero.** Three things the shorthand holds do not survive the trip back down:

- the **sign of any particular loop**, which the figures leave as a measurement and the shorthand can only label as "positive";
- the **identification of the live joint**, since Fig 13 :75 requires a state but does not select which state was carrying the 2003 event;
- the **channel reading**, since Fig 10 :24 says the meter decays and a post-event narrative can silently convert an unreadable warning into an absent one.

Conversely, one thing survives cleanly: a cascade that amplifies must be a cycle with a returned remainder and a state-carrying joint, and the compound must be unbundled. That is the residue worth sending. `R_T` was **not** measured numerically, because no per-slot instrument was run; it is recorded qualitatively as the enumerated set of dropped constraints. Treating the condensed shape as identical to the full figure set would set `R_T` to zero and would be the dead-reckoning error Fig 4 :30 names. The honest form is a lossy condensation with a recorded gap, which is what `09_watson_answer.md` states.
