## VERDICT (from the figures)

Amplification is a signed cycle with a state-carrying joint, not a length of chain or a density. The remainder of each cross-network turn re-enters enlarged; two networks join through a measurable state; the compound is a union that must be unbundled; coupling valence flips with the residual; the channel degrades before the ground; and the residual is read per slot. The specific coupling, loop sign and threshold are a declared `gap` awaiting a source-discriminating instrument. Mode `general` for the shape, `specified` for the crisis reading, never `grounded`. Verdicts are kept per domain and never averaged. The six refuters are in `01_figure_grounded_audit.md` section 3.

- **cost if wrong:** bounded and locatable. If the cycle-sign claim fails, the corrected packet still states its mode, refuters and lineage, so the error is findable rather than invisible. The larger cost is operational: a union compound treated as a list gets one lever applied to the physical-only region while the coupled intersection keeps amplifying, and outside readers are spent by a recording instead of a generator. Fig 2 :42 prices that cost: "Buy independence in proportion to the cost of being wrong." It is avoidable by leading with the shape claim and its refuter.
- **undoable:** yes. The coupling-specific bindings are not asserted, so nothing has to be withdrawn to correct them. A refutation is added and the reach falls because, as Fig 6 :42-43 says, "something was added rather than because anything was deleted". The shape and its refuters are recorded with provenance so a later reader can locate the error (Fig 10 :62-64).
- **reversed by:** a ground reading, not agreement. A per-slot instrument that measures the returned signal's sign at a cross-network handoff would settle the cycle claim; a coupling shown to act with no intervening state would break the state-joint claim; a first-order enumeration that accounts for every cross-network failure would break the order and union claims; an invariant coupling valence across a residual change in both directions would break the valence claim; a channel that stays readable while warnings go unheeded for a non-channel reason would narrow the channel claim. Until one of these fires, the verdict stands as `candidate`.
