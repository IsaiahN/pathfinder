# The Tether's answer: how feedback between physical and social or financial networks amplifies crises

**Mode: `general` on the structural shape, `specified` on the crisis reading, not `grounded`.** A shape and a route, not a result. Simulated derivation checked against the figures (`05_what_shape_means.md` §6), not a ground reading.

## 1. The residual it answers

A small missed prediction in one slot returns enlarged after crossing between a physical network and a social or financial network. Fig 1 :19: "The residual is indexed by object slot, not aggregated." The contact is a **union compound** (Fig 9 :55, "A bundled problem is usually a union of overlapping conditions, not a list of separate ones"), not one hard problem, so it has its own loop and its own ground.

## 2. The shape

1. **Amplification is a signed cycle, not a long chain.** Fig 12 :121: "cycle. The output re-enters as input. The sign of the loop decides whether it closes, and a chain cannot close by construction." A chain cannot close; a cycle can be positive (amplify) or negative (damp). The question is asking for the sign of a specific loop.

2. **What grows is the remainder.** Fig 13 :32: "comes from: a remainder compounding through a chain that amplifies is chaos, and the detail a crossing discards reappears above it as noise." The unserved flow, the unsettled payment, the missed control signal is the remainder that re-enters the next turn. Buldyrev et al. (2010) report the world-facing version: "a failure of a very small fraction of nodes in one network may lead to the complete fragmentation of a system of several interdependent networks."

3. **The two networks join through a state, not directly.** Both are structures ("network": Fig 13 :24, "that entities relate, and influence travels"), and Fig 13 :75 says "two structures join only through a state." The joint is power flow, settlement finality, fuel delivery or a service level. It can be named and measured; the question is which one is live in a given event.

4. **The coupling is a union, so unbundle before acting.** Fig 9 :54: "And when the split lands on a union rather than a partition, unbundle before you search again." A lever that clears the physical-only region leaves the coupled intersection standing. Fig 9 :57: "a case in two conditions is not a harder case of either."

5. **Coupling valence flips with the residual.** Fig 11 :49: "An actor’s valence is relative to the residual, not a property it carries." Dense connections diversify a small shock and propagate a large one (Acemoglu et al. 2015; Buldyrev et al. 2010). There is no standing list of good or bad interconnections (Fig 11 :51).

6. **The channel degrades before the ground does.** Fig 10 :24: "the ground does not decay, the channel does." Saturated meters, dropped transients and pooled readings (Fig 10 :26) explain why a collapse appears to arrive with no warning. The warning existed in the residual; it could not be read.

7. **The system-of-systems failure is second order.** Each single-network cascade is settled against the ground and becomes an atom for the next level: Fig 12 :104, "A settled molecule becomes an atom for whatever composes over it." The residue that matters is several steps out (Fig 11 :52). No new primitive is required.

8. **The specific coupling, sign and threshold are a gap.** Fig 9 :32: "you get a kind of thing, never the answer itself." Fig 13 :134: "Unreachable is a verdict here, not a silence." The framework returns the shape; it does not name the link that closed the loop in a particular crisis.

## 3. What it explicitly does not say

- Not that connectivity alone causes crises. Its valence is relative to the residual (5).
- Not that a particular coupling (for example, telecom dependence on power) is *the* amplifier in every event. That is an unproven binding from the engineering frame, requiring a source gate and a shadow test.
- Not a single systemic-risk index as the answer. Per-slot residual is the signal (Fig 1 :22, "Aggregating across slots is how a live signal disappears into an average").
- Not that "no warning" means "no residual". It means the channel failed (6).
- Not grounded. No instrument reported here settles a coupling-specific sign or threshold.
- Not a theory of everything. The coupled intersection remains a compound with its own loop.

## 4. What would settle it: the ground

A per-slot, source-discriminating instrument, not more agreement. The reading must (i) identify the state that carried the crossing, (ii) measure the returned signal's sign, and (iii) keep working while the cascade runs. Concretely:

- **Sign Test:** at a cross-network handoff, does the returned signal reduce or increase the originating slot residual? A persistent growth with a demonstrably reducing returned signal, no second positive loop and no external drive would refute claim 1.
- **Medium Test:** can influence be shown to travel between the two networks with no intervening state? If yes, claim 3 fails.
- **Order Test:** does first-order enumeration account for every cross-network failure, with no third-order residual? If yes, claims 4 and 7 are in trouble.
- **Valence Test:** does one coupling keep the same sign across a documented change of residual in both directions? If yes, claim 5 fails.
- **Channel Test:** does the monitoring channel stay unsaturated, unpooled and faster than the propagating overload while warnings remain unread? If not, claim 6 is supported; if the channel is healthy and no residual was present, claim 6 is still supported only as a description of that event, never as a universal.

## 5. The honest stop

**Unreached, not unreachable.** The six-term set is "a human's careful guess at reality's compression axes" (exemplar's *They're Scanning Books* entry, recorded there and not re-opened this session). Declaring the set makes the choice visible, not neutral. Another sensorium's minimum set need not be these six. The answer stops at the limit of the available instruments and records the lineage so the stop is checkable. "Indiscernibility is not identity" (canonical README). The missing instrument is characterised in effect terms in section 4; it is not claimed to be held.

## 6. One-paragraph version for outreach

> The Tether does not name the coupling that caused your favourite crisis, and it does not claim a final theory of cascades. It says that amplification is a signed cycle, not a long chain: the output of a dependency returns to its own input, and the sign decides whether the disturbance is damped or grown. What compounds is the remainder each crossing leaves, the unserved flow or unsettled payment or missed control signal. A physical and a financial network are both networks, so they cannot bond directly; they compose through a state that can be measured, such as power flow or settlement finality. The coupled system is a union of conditions, so one lever clears the wrong region, and the value of a coupling flips with the residual: dense connections steady a small shock and carry a large one. In a crisis the channel fails before the ground, so "no warning" often means a warning that could not be read. The framework returns that shape, six pre-registered refuters and the instrument questions that would close it. Which loop, which sign and which threshold stays open until the ground settles it.
