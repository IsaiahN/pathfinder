## 1. A shape is a constraint, not a filler

The canonical README states the limit: the framework returns "what a missing thing would have to look like (a shape, not a name)". Fig 9 :32 says "you get a kind of thing, never the answer itself". Fig 13 says the system "cannot say which primitive is missing, because naming it would mean already holding it."

From *The Library of Babel*: "you can cut your search short if the set of books returned is small and descriptive enough that it provides a general shape of the answer that satisfies your question."

So a **shape** is the narrowed form of an answer you can state before you can name an instance of it. It constrains; it does not fill. For BUS-0033 the shape is a conditional rule over three domains. A named startup, a named capability, or a numerical threshold would be a filler put in where the figures return only a constraint.

## 2. How the shape is obtained

- *Library of Babel*: use the vocabulary you already have that is closest to the thing, then correlate it with recurring new terms. "With each new term gained, you are able to narrow the space by definition and deepen your vocabulary." The same essay warns that if the final search fails, "Perhaps your problem was a bundle or conflation of several problems masquerading as one?" That is exactly the union finding in F9 of `01_figure_grounded_audit.md`.
- Fig 6 :30: "The description travels. Fails by being sharpened into terms only we can read. Cast it in effect terms." Fig 9 :30-31: "your words are borrowed too, so describe the effect rather than the cause."
- *Decoding Tacit Knowledge*: "When contact isn't possible, the best you can send is a plain description of what you'd expect to see, because anyone can check that against the world." This is why the first contact in `02_outreach_email.md` carries a refuter expressed in effects, not a theory.
- *Library of Babel* on stopping: "the finish line will also be the starting place" and the searcher "will leave as the author". The analogue here: when the residual stops registering, the rule is a description the reader can test, not a claim to be believed.

## 3. What licenses a name

Only two things, both outside the shape itself:

1. **The ground settles it.** Formula step 5: "the ground settles it. Verified only by what pays. A gate is not the ground." For a firm, the ground is a paying market reading that does not move when the firm changes its account of the deal (Fig 2 :29, :45).
2. **A provenanced import from a frame whose closure differs** (Fig 6 :23), with a source gate and a shadow test. Buying a startup is such an import. The record of origin is part of what you get.

*They're Scanning Books* sharpens the second point: "The real import is not a new term in the existing space. It is a new dimension of the space." And: "A minimum viable set of six terms is a human's careful guess at reality's compression axes; another sensorium's minimum set need not be those six." Also: "Declaring the primitives does not make them neutral. It makes the choice visible, which is worth a great deal, because a visible choice can be revised and an emergent one cannot."

Applied here: the firm's existing capability set is a visible guess, not nature's own list. A new arrangement is reachable inside it; a new axis is not. Naming a particular target is a binding from the business frame and must be recorded as such.

## 4. Why the post-deal artifact cannot settle provenance

*How Intelligence Actually Works*: a video frame and a photograph "are indistinguishable as artifacts, and they carry different warrants. One was taken; one was extracted. And nothing in the image records which." A capability built in-house and one imported can be indistinguishable in the same way. "The recursion converges toward indiscernibility, and indiscernibility is not identity. ... which is why the lineage has to be kept even when the copy is perfect."

*The Hard Problem is About Source* gives the instrument rule: "If several paths create the same behavior, then behavior is a projection with lossy information". The green reading does not say whether the source was one green light, yellow plus blue, RGB, CMYK, or a scheme not yet discovered. What resolved that case was "the arrival of a better instrument", not an argument. The business analogue: a finished capability reading cannot name its source. Either keep the provenance or build a discriminator.

This is why F8 in `01_figure_grounded_audit.md` matters and why the outreach packet asks for a refutation rather than an endorsement.

## 5. The habitat point, from three directions

*How a Society of Agents Would Work* states it directly: "capability is a property of agent-and-habitat, never of the agent alone." A new agent is made capable by the surroundings it is born into. It also says "A group is only worth having while its members stay different." Both bear on an acquisition: the target's capability lives in a habitat, and the value of the combined group comes from the difference between the two frames, which the import spends (Fig 6 :24).

*Decoding Tacit Knowledge* adds the contact mechanism: "we can know more than we can tell". Most of what a team knows is unstated history; writing carries context and some subtext but almost never that pretext. So a playbook is not the capability. Fig 4 :22: "Only methods cross up. Only head starts come down." A method is reapplicable; a recording is a description of one occasion.

*Arrival of the Fittest* records three mappings it declines, including "the habitat as an ecological niche", precisely because the definitions do not align. The figure's habitat is enumerated relative to a residual, not borrowed from ecology. The acquisition reading in this solution keeps that distinction.

## 6. Why "shape, not a name" is narrowed here, not applied absolutely

**Correction to my own first reading.** In the first draft of this file I treated the limit as a blanket ban on naming anything in a venture decision. That overstated it. The README's phrase governs "a missing thing", a primitive not held in Γ. The firm's own atom set is nameable, and so is the class of frame whose closure might differ. What is not licensed is naming a specific startup as *the* answer before the residual has been described, or inventing a new primitive inside the derivation. That would be a binding from the business frame presented as a derivation. The correction is recorded here rather than erased, and it matches the correction the exemplar makes in its own `05` §5b.

## 7. Two consequences for this question

**Prestige is not action.** From *The Recursive Self-Improvement Trap*: "Prestige can fund an action. It can give an action a budget to be tried. But it cannot settle an action. Only the ground can settle it. The Ground Maintainer must be blind to prestige." A shortlist of celebrated acquirers, or a famous investor's preference, gives a deal a budget to be tried. It does not say whether the build or buy lever was right. Marketing the rule by association with famous firms is the named collapse.

**This audit is a simulation, not a ground reading.** Same essay: "LLMs can quickly simulate an approximate answer/output with the formula found in the architecture post" and "the truly testable output would need to be derived from the real architecture or working the formula yourself." *Arrival of the Fittest* adds the caveat that simulated outputs "could never be verified if the solutions presented were not contrived to fit the formula." So `01`, `06`, `07` and `09` are simulated derivations checked for internal consistency against the figures. They are not tests of the decision rule on any firm. They cannot settle the question they report on, and they are labelled accordingly.

## 8. What the essays do not give us

No essay supplies a numerical threshold for "how large a gap must be before buying", a ranking of target firms, or a method for valuing a habitat in money. Those are bindings from the business frame, to be supplied by a ground reading if at all. The canonical limit is honest about this: the framework stops at what the available instruments can distinguish and records the lineage so the stop is checkable. Here the stop is a gap, recorded as `unreachable` in `01` rather than hidden.
