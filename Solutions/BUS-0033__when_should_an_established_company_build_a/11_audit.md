## Round trip gap R_T

The Symbols table defines R_T as the gap between what was sent up and what came back down, `gap(x, (T_E ∘ T_A)(x))`, measuring what the coarser description cannot hold. Fig 4 :30 requires it to be measured rather than assumed. I ran the round trip on the condensation the folder actually uses.

1. **Sent up (x):** the verbatim figure set, chiefly Fig 6 :14, :21, :24 (mint, import, debit), Fig 8 :32, :50 (idempotence, provenance), Fig 11 :39, :66 (habitat), Fig 9 :54-57 (union).
2. **Abstraction (T_A):** the shorthand in `07` and `08`: build reachable new arrangements; acquire otherwise with a habitat plan; keep two prices and a provenance record; three domains, not two answers.
3. **Concretisation (T_E):** an actual build or buy decision for a named firm with a price, a target and an integration plan.

**R_T is nonzero.** Three things the shorthand holds do not survive the trip back down:

- the **debit** in Fig 6 :24 is qualitative; the shorthand carries "cost" but not the measured independence loss or the price of a wrong atom at this deal;
- the **habitat contents** in Fig 11 are enumerated relative to the residual, so the shorthand's "reproduce the habitat" does not say which actors, conditions or relations matter for this venture; a second read of the habitat is required;
- the **isomer condition** in `06` Q4 holds only over a shared atom set; the shorthand "built and bought look the same" silently drops that condition, so it overstates the claim when one route requires an atom the other does not.

Two things survive cleanly: a new arrangement is reachable by mint while a missing primitive is not, and an imported atom must settle before it can compose over. Those are the residue worth sending. R_T was **not** measured numerically, because no firm or deal was read; it is recorded qualitatively as the enumerated set of dropped constraints. Treating "the formula is the condensed version of the figures" as identity would set R_T to zero and would be the dead-reckoning error Fig 4 :30 names. The honest form is a lossy condensation with a stated gap, which is what `09_watson_answer.md` says.
