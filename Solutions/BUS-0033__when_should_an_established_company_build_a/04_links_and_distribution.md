## 3. What the wider literature bears on the shape claim

1. **The gap is the object, and it is routinely misread.** Capron: firms "wrongly assess the gap and over-estimate what they have internally". That is the SEARCHED mistake: treating an unreachable atom as a search problem. Fig 6 :29 says the inward instrument "Fails by refining forever on an axis that cannot carry it". Corroborates F1 and F2. Overlap with the figures: low (different field, different method).
2. **Internal development is recombination; acquisition is integration of two entities.** Ego defines both exactly as the figure census does. Corroborates F1. Overlap: low, but Ego cites the same strategic-management pool as Capron and Mitchell, so those three are one witness, not three.
3. **Buying is conditional on an integration plan.** Mitchell says buy only after the other modes are ruled out and only with a viable integration plan. That is the third domain and the habitat requirement (Fig 11 :39, :66). Corroborates F5.
4. **Absorptive capacity is prior-related knowledge, cumulative and path dependent.** Cohen and Levinthal corroborate Fig 8 :32 at the level of a firm: if the firm has not invested in a field, external knowledge in that field may be inaccessible no matter how valuable. Their statement that firm-specific absorptive capacity "cannot be bought and quickly integrated" corroborates F5. This is the lowest-overlap substantive source in the set (cognitive basis of learning inside an organizational-learning paper), and it carries the most weight as an outside check.
5. **Make-or-buy is a governance question with hidden costs.** TCE names search, negotiation and monitoring costs, asset specificity and opportunism. It corroborates the two-price reading (Fig 12 :125) and Fig 6 :24. Overlap: low relative to the figures, moderate relative to the management sources (both are economics-adjacent).

No world source conflicts with the figure verdict. By the rule that a conflict sends us back to the census, none was triggered. The management sources share a pool (Capron, Mitchell, Ego's reviewed literature); the Cohen and Levinthal source and the TCE source are genuinely lower overlap. Agreement across these is recorded as weak corroboration, not as two independent witnesses for the same claim.

## 4. Distribution: offer, do not push

- **Fig 10 :42** "A verifier that can reconstruct the claim cannot verify it." A recipient who has absorbed the whole framework before being asked for a verdict has joined the frame being checked. So stage 1 carries the residual and the refuter only.
- **Fig 9 :27** "describe it before you go looking, or any frame you pick will seem to fit." Ask what they would expect to see if the rule were true, before offering the source links.
- **Fig 4 :22** "Only methods cross up. Only head starts come down." Sending the full architecture before anyone has described a residual sends a recording, not a generator.
- **Fig 2 :35** "Alignment is triangulation, not negotiation. A mirror cannot correct you." Do not ask for agreement.
- **Fig 2 :40** "agreement about the world is worthless when the agreeing frames share an evidence pool." A list ranked by receptiveness manufactures the disqualifying correlation. No such list exists in this folder.
- **Fig 7 :35** "A room's agreement is variation, not a verdict. Selection between rooms is where the ground votes." A portfolio of agreeable readers adds vocabulary, not an anchor.

**Recommended order in a reply:** (1) residual and decision rule; (2) the refuter and the question "what would you count as a failure?"; (3) record the answer; (4) only then, the three local framework links, with the note that the figures, not this summary, are the authority; (5) if wanted, the formula as a method to reuse, with each user writing their own refuter and mode.

## 5. The two-stage offer, restated

1. **Stage 1:** check the output against the ground. The output is a conditional rule over three domains with five written refuters.
2. **Stage 2, opt-in:** read the theory that produced it. The verdict on stage 1 must already be recorded. Later agreement is never pooled as corroboration (Fig 2 :40).
3. **Stage 3:** reuse the formula, with a ground and a pre-registered refuter per problem.

**Hard precondition:** stages 1 to 3 work only if the stage-1 output survives. This folder contains the rule and its refuters; it contains no measurement of a firm or a deal, so the output is `specified`, not `grounded`. Treat it as a generator to be refuted, not a conclusion to be adopted.
