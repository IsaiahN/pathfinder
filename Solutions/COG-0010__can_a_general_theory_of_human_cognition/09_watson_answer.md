## QUESTION

Decide, in effect terms, what the Tether says about a general theory of human cognition facing deep cultural variation. R is the live group-level residual left after a theory built on one population has explained what it can: systematic differences in attention, categorisation and reasoning that do not shrink with more within-group study and are not always the sum of within-group individual traits. Option A: hold an invariant kernel and let variation live in the arrangement. Option B: dissolve the group-level signal into individual differences. A keeps a general theory and a real cultural level. B keeps generality only by denying the thing the residual names. The sought-for shape was bound as an ordinary term before the run (README), so it can accrue a residual like anything else.

## VERDICT (from the figures)

**Yes, conditionally.** A general theory of human cognition can accommodate deep cultural variation without reducing it to individual differences, if it is a kernel-plus-arrangement theory. The kernel is invariant across size; actors, currency and anchor change. Universal primitives form a closed set and cultural forms are the unbounded arrangements over it, so two cultures related that way are isomers rather than averages of individual traits. Capability is agent-and-habitat, so removing the cultural habitat and explaining the residual from individuals alone is a level error. The framework does not name the universal set; that is a provenanced binding awaiting ground settlement. The question must first be split into kernel, primitive and mechanism universality, which have different answers. This is a `specified`, ungrounded result, and this run is a simulated derivation, not a ground reading.

- **cost if wrong:** bounded and locatable. The packet states mode, refuters and provenance before the conclusion, so an error is findable rather than invisible. The real cost is spending the independence of outside readers by sending a universal list as if the figures named it, which Fig 2 :42 prices: "Buy independence in proportion to the cost of being wrong." That cost is avoidable by leading with the shape claim and its refuter. A second cost is the false split: if the question is one question after all, the derivation over-segments it and the union claim is wasted effort.
- **undoable:** yes. The universal list, if anyone supplies one, is removable without touching the shape. The isomer and habitat claims stand or fall separately. `07_the_tether_answer.md` and `08_expert_verification_packet.md` already withdraw the named-set claim and keep the form. No primitive has to be withdrawn to reverse the outreach material.
- **reversed by:** a ground reading, not agreement. A source-discriminating cross-cultural instrument showing that a group-level construct is fully determined by individual traits would break F3 and C2; a cultural form requiring a new primitive would break the closed-set claim; a case in which two cultures require different atoms would break the isomer claim; an agent-only change that moves the ground of cross-cultural cognition would break the habitat claim; a demonstration that the same loop and naming rule cannot be written at both scales would break the kernel claim. Until one of these fires, the verdict stands as `candidate`.
