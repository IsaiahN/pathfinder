## 1. A shape is a constraint, not a filler

The figures README states the limit: the framework returns *"what a missing thing would have to look like (a shape, not a name)."* Fig 9 :32: "you get a kind of thing, never the answer itself". Fig 13 :134: "Unreachable is a verdict here, not a silence,".

From *The Library of Babel* (opened 2026-11-22): "you can cut your search short if the set of books returned is small and descriptive enough that it provides a general shape of the answer that satisfies your question."

So a **shape** is the narrowed form of an answer you can state before you can name it. It constrains; it does not fill. For COG-0010 the shape is:

> a cognitive kernel that does not change with size + a closed set of shared primitives + cultural variation living in the arrangements (bonds) over that set + capability located in agent-and-habitat.

That is a shape. A specific list of universals, for example "all humans have X, Y and Z", is a name.

## 2. How the shape was obtained

- *Library of Babel*: use the vocabulary already known that is closest to the thing, then "connect and correlate combinations of the existence of your known words and their relative proximity to commonly recurring new terms that you are unfamiliar with." With each term the space narrows.
- Fig 6 :30 "The description travels. Fails by being sharpened into terms only we can read. Cast it in effect terms." Fig 9 :30-31 "your words are borrowed too, so describe the effect rather than the cause". The residual was described in effect terms before any figure line was chosen (see `01`, section 1).
- *Decoding Tacit Knowledge* (carried reading): when contact is not possible, the best that can be sent is a plain description of what one would expect to see, because anyone can check that against the world. That is why the outreach email leads with refuters.
- *Library of Babel* on stopping: "you will know that you are done because the finish line will also be the starting place, as there is nowhere else to search and nothing else that can describe the answer better." Here the stop is reached when the shape stops registering against the ground, not when a universal list is declared.

## 3. The union is part of the shape, not noise

*The Library of Babel* names exactly the COG-0010 failure: "Perhaps your problem was a bundle or conflation of several problems masquerading as one? You will need to search all of those problems individually to find their answers and then combine them in a satisfactory way." Fig 9 :4-5 says the same: "Disagreements that do not shrink with effort, where each rule keeps working well on a different subset, are not one hard question but several well-formed ones. Split it rather than search."

So any honest shape for this question must first split "a general theory" into kernel invariance, primitive universality and mechanism universality. Those three have different answers. A shape that answers only one of them and calls it a verdict is a collapsed question.

## 4. What licenses a name

Only two things:

1. **The ground settles it.** Formula step 5: "the ground settles it. Verified only by what pays. A gate is not the ground." For this field the ground is cross-cultural behavioural data, with instruments that can distinguish a group-level effect from an individual-level one (Na et al. 2010 is an instance).
2. **A provenanced import from a frame whose closure differs** (Fig 6 :23, Fig 8 :50), with a source gate and a shadow test. Norenzayan and Heine's four levels of universals are such an import from the psychological frame; their provenance is recorded in `10_source_ledger.csv`.

*They're Scanning Books When They Should* (carried reading) sharpens this: the real import is not a new term in the existing space but a new dimension of the space, and declaring primitives does not make them neutral, it makes the choice visible. Applied here, declaring a universal set makes the theorist's sampling choice visible. It does not make the set universal.

## 5. Why this question's answer cannot be a name

*The Hard Problem is About Source* (carried reading) gives the mechanism: a spectrometer reads a colour and "cannot comment on the source"; several different sources can produce the same reading. Applied here, a near-zero average fit across cultures is a reading of light, not a reading of the source. It is compatible with a shared kernel plus different arrangements, and also with no shared kernel at all. The reading does not discriminate. What is owed is a source-discriminating instrument, not a name for universals.

Fig 8 :39: "So a search that finds nothing is never a proof of absence." A failure to find a universal is not a proof that none exists, and a failure to find a cultural difference is not a proof that none exists. Both are verdicts about the reach of the instrument, recorded honestly.

## 6. Prestige is not action, and my own audit is a simulation

From *The Recursive Self-Improvement Trap* (opened 2026-11-22): "Prestige must never be allowed to validate an action directly." And: "Prestige can fund an action. It can give an action a budget to be tried. But it cannot settle an action. Only the ground can settle it. The Ground Maintainer must be blind to prestige." A list of famous cross-cultural psychologists who agree with the shape would spend the independence of the check rather than add evidence (Fig 2 :40).

Same essay: "LLMs can quickly simulate an approximate answer/output with the formula found in the architecture post. It will often be better than the generic outputs that you will usually get otherwise too (intelligence is essentially arrangement). However, the truly testable output would need to be derived from the real architecture or working the formula yourself."

So `01_figure_grounded_audit.md`, `06_isomers_and_order.md`, `07_the_tether_answer.md`, `09_watson_answer.md` and `11_audit.md` are **simulated derivations**, checked for internal consistency against the figures. They are not ground readings, they are not tests of the hypothesis, and they cannot settle COG-0010. The mode line on every substantive claim says so.
