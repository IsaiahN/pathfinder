## 1. A shape is a constraint, not a filler

The canonical usage rule states the limit: the framework returns *"what a missing thing would have to look like (a shape, not a name)."* Fig 9 :32: *"you get a kind of thing, never the answer itself."* Fig 13: *"It can say the answer is out of reach with the library as it stands... It cannot say which primitive is missing, because naming it would mean already holding it."*

From *The Library of Babel*: *"you can cut your search short if the set of books returned is small and descriptive enough that it provides a general shape of the answer that satisfies your question."*

So a **shape** is the narrowed form of an answer you can state before you can name the winning instance. It constrains; it does not fill. For this question the shape is: *a maintained, anchored, per-slot, introduced-not-subtracted, union-splitting composition over independent frames.* Naming "carbon pricing" or "network seeding" as the winner is a name, not a shape.

## 2. How a shape is obtained

- *Library of Babel*: use the vocabulary you already have that is *closest* to the thing, look for recurring new terms that overlap or correlate with it, recurse. *"With each new term gained, you are able to narrow the space by definition and deepen your vocabulary."* And the bundle warning: *"Perhaps your problem was a bundle or conflation of several problems masquerading as one?"* That is the union diagnosis in `01` F5.
- Fig 6 :30: *"The description travels. Fails by being sharpened into terms only we can read. Cast it in effect terms."* Fig 9 :30-31: *"your words are borrowed too, so describe the effect rather than the cause."*
- *Decoding Tacit Knowledge*: *"When contact isn't possible, the best you can send is a plain description of what you'd expect to see, because anyone can check that against the world."* This is why the outreach note in `02` leads with a description of what would count as failure.
- *Library of Babel* on stopping: *"you will know that you are done because the finish line will also be the starting place"*, and the paradox: *"you have already composed the answer through the process of locating it."*

## 3. What licenses a name

Only two things, both outside the shape itself:

1. **The ground settles it.** Formula step 5: *"the ground settles it. Verified only by what pays."* The canonical rule: the framework *"stops at the limit of what the available instruments can distinguish, and records the lineage so the stop is honest."*
2. **A provenanced import from a frame whose closure differs** (Fig 6 :23, Fig 8 :50-52), with source gate and shadow test.

*They're Scanning Books* sharpens this: *"The real import is not a new term in the existing space. It is a new dimension of the space."* And: *"A minimum viable set of six terms is a human's careful guess at reality's compression axes; another sensorium's minimum set need not be those six."* Also: *"Declaring the primitives does not make them neutral. It makes the choice visible."*

For this question: naming a winning intervention requires a source-discriminating instrument in the target domain, not further derivation. That instrument is the `gap` in `01` F7.

## 4. The source/provenance rule decides the anchor question

*The Hard Problem is About Source* gives the mechanism. Metamerism: an instrument reads green and *"it cannot comment on the source"* - one green light, or yellow plus blue, or RGB, or CMYK, or a scheme not yet discovered. *"If several paths create the same behavior, then behavior is a projection with lossy information."* And *"The answer is unreached until a better instrument exists."* It was the spectrometer, not an argument, that resolved it.

Applied here: a compliance score, a reach statistic, a network-density metric are projections. They can be produced by genuine improvement or by strategic adaptation to the measure. Reading the source off the projection, and calling the intervention that produced it "the one that works", is the metamerism error. What is owed is a discriminator, not a ranking of levers.

*How Intelligence Actually Works* adds provenance loss: *"the frame and the photograph are indistinguishable as artifacts, and they carry different warrants. One was taken; one was extracted. And nothing in the image records which."* An intervention that appeared to work because agents adapted to the metric and one that worked because the ground improved can be indistinguishable in the report. Only the lineage separates them. *"indiscernibility is not identity."*

## 5. The society-of-agents essay is the applied case for this question

*How a Society of Agents Would Work* is the closest the essays come to answering CMP-0092 directly. It gives four rules for intervening on adaptive agents whose connections change:

- **Votes from outcomes, not popularity:** *"When an agent uses a shared method, the result in its own world is a vote: up if it helped, down if it did not. That is the ground voting, which Figure 7 says is where selection belongs: 'Selection between rooms is where the ground votes.'"*
- **Rate per situation, not total:** *"Keep the rate per kind of situation. A method that is brilliant in one setting must not be drowned by an average across all of them. Figure 1: 'Aggregating across slots is how a live signal disappears into an average.'"* This is Fig 1 :22 applied to intervention evaluation.
- **Independence is a design dial:** *"A group is only worth having while its members stay different. Figure 2 defines independence as 'how little they draw on the same evidence.' Ten copies watching the same thing are one opinion said ten times."* And on correlated rooms: the effective-sample-size correction means *"ten clones count as one."*
- **Fade, so a failed intervention can be retried:** *"If a group avoids an idea after one bad result, it never tries it again, so it never learns the idea was fine."* And bad news must travel in the same lane as good news, because *"nobody who avoids something ever tests it."*

It also states the habitat rule in plain terms: *"It adds, it never removes. Figure 11: 'Introduction is available; exclusion is not.' A new method is introduced to a few agents first, and its knock-on effects are watched before it spreads. Figure 11 names the mistake to avoid: cane toads and kudzu, 'the same move with the cascade unenumerated.'"* This is `01` F3.

## 6. Two consequences for outreach

**Prestige is not action.** From *The Recursive Self-Improvement Trap*: *"Prestige must never be allowed to validate an action directly... Someone let prestige stand in for proof... Prestige can fund an action. It can give an action a budget to be tried. But it cannot settle an action. Only the ground can settle it. The Ground Maintainer must be blind to prestige."* A campaign that gathers famous names to agree with an intervention shape is the named collapse, not corroboration. The legitimate ask is to give the claim a budget to be tried.

**My own audit is a simulation, not a ground reading.** Same essay: *"LLMs can quickly simulate an approximate answer/output with the formula found in the architecture post... However, the truly testable output would need to be derived from the real architecture or working the formula yourself."* The architecture post adds: LLM outputs *"must be seen as simulacra"* and the claim that they test the formula *"could never be verified if the solutions presented were not contrived to fit the formula."* So `01` is a simulated derivation, checked for internal consistency against the figures. It is **not** a test of any intervention and must be labelled as such. It cannot settle anything it reports on.

## 7. Correction to my own §3

I initially wrote *"the framework returns a shape, not a name"* as if it forbade evaluating named interventions. The canonical rule's subject is a **missing** thing: *"what a **missing** thing would have to look like (a shape, not a name)."* Here the missing thing is an instrument, not an intervention label. So the correct form is: the figure returns the **conditions** an intervention must satisfy; it does not select among candidate interventions. Evaluating a named intervention against those conditions is allowed. Declaring one the winner without a ground reading is not. Both claims are now recorded so neither is quietly lost.
