## Wider literature that bears on the residual

1. **Street-level discretion.** Lipsky's claim that implementation comes down to the people who carry it out supports per-slot `R`: the unit of analysis is the official-case pair, not the agency average. The same page records that compliance accountability defines rules and procedures and then checks compliance, while performance accountability measures outputs. Those are two different frames, which is the F1 split.
2. **Formal and informal rules.** New institutionalism is defined by the constraining and enabling effects of both formal and informal rules, and the record notes that formal rules and administrative structures were not accurately describing behaviour. This corroborates F3: neither class alone predicts the per-slot choice.
3. **Clientelism and monitoring.** The four elements (dyadic, contingent, hierarchical, iterated) and the claim that quid pro quo dissolves without monitoring describe a convention whose answer changes with members, exactly the F4 failure of the anchor test.
4. **Selectorate and standing.** The observation that laws and titles mean nothing without support, and that a larger selectorate makes dissenters easier to replace, corroborates F6: standing survives outcomes, and an unauthored pool reduces dependence on any one tie.
5. **Normalization of deviance.** A practice becomes normal when it does not immediately cause catastrophe. This is the coherent drift of Fig 2 :34 at the organizational stratum, and it is why the mirror room cannot see its own failure.
6. **Principal-agent and slippage.** Divergent interests and information, plus weak means to punish, predict that the formal rule will be reported as followed while the ground is not paid. That is the channel failure of Fig 10 :24-28.
7. **Effective sample size.** Kish's design effect and correlated observations give the standard-variance arithmetic behind Fig 2 :44. correlated observers add spelling, not evidence.
8. **Legibility.** Scott's argument that standardized knowledge discards local expertise corroborates Fig 11 :43-44: enumerate the habitat, do not compose it from the centre.

All eight are secondary. None overrules a figure line. Where a source was read through a Wikipedia summary, the ledger records that, and agreement between summaries of the same pool is one witness, not two.

## The two-stage offer

**Stage 1.** Send the residual in effect terms, the shape claim, and the refuters. No theory, no framework, no request for belief.

**Stage 2, opt-in only.** If the recipient is curious, offer the three local links. Their verdict on stage 1 must be recorded first, because once a recipient has absorbed the framework their agreement stops being evidence (Fig 2 :40, Fig 10 :42).

**Stage 3, opt-in only.** Offer the formula as a method to reuse. Each user writes their own refuter before running it and states their own mode. The *Recursive Self-Improvement Trap* warns that such outputs can be simulated; the write-up must say so.

**Hard precondition:** stages 1 to 3 work only if the stage-1 output survives. `01_figure_grounded_audit.md` records one declared gap (F9) and two narrowed readings. Do not present the gap as a result.
