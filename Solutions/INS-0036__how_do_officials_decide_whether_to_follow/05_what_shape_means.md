## 1. A shape is a constraint, not a filler

The canonical limit is in the figures README: the framework returns *"what a missing thing would have to look like (a shape, not a name)."* Fig 9 :32: *"you get a kind of thing, never the answer itself."* Fig 13: *"It can say the answer is out of reach with the library as it stands... It cannot say which primitive is missing, because naming it would mean already holding it."*

From *The Library of Babel*: *"This means that you can cut your search short if the set of books returned is small and descriptive enough that it provides a general shape of the answer that satisfies your question."*

So a **shape** is the narrowed form of an answer you can state before you can name the particular case. It constrains; it does not fill. For this question:

- `two closures, one comparison, one non-updating anchor, one learnable weighting, per-slot residual` is a shape.
- "Officials follow the rule when the network is weak" is a name of a class of cases, and it is only licensed by a ground reading that discriminates the closures.

## 2. How the shape is obtained

- *Library of Babel*: use the vocabulary closest to the thing, look for recurring new terms that overlap or correlate with it, recurse. *"With each new term gained, you are able to narrow the space by definition and deepen your vocabulary."*
- Fig 6 :30: *"outward: does another frame already hold it? The description travels. Fails by being sharpened into terms only we can read. Cast it in effect terms."* Fig 9 :30-31: *"your words are borrowed too, so describe the effect rather than the cause."*
- *Decoding Tacit Knowledge*: *"When contact isn't possible, the best you can send is a plain description of what you'd expect to see, because anyone can check that against the world."*
- *Library of Babel* on stopping: *"You will know that you are done because the finish line will also be the starting place, as there is nowhere else to search and nothing else that can describe the answer better."*

Applied here: describe the decision in effect terms (compare predicted costs per case, against an outcome that does not move when the official pushes on it) before choosing public administration, political science, clientelism or organizational sociology as the frame. Otherwise any field will seem to fit.

## 3. What licenses a name

Only two things, both outside the shape itself.

1. **The ground settles it.** Formula step 5: *"SETTLE the ground settles it. Verified only by what pays. A gate is not the ground."* The figures README says the framework *"stops at the limit of what the available instruments can distinguish, and records the lineage so the stop is honest."*
2. **A provenanced import from a closure that differs** (Fig 6 :23, Fig 8 :50-52), with a source gate and a shadow test.

*The Hard Problem is About Source* gives the reason. Metamerism: an instrument reads green and *"the instrument is speaking only about what it measured, it cannot comment on the source."* Several sources can give the same reading. *"If several paths create the same behavior, then behavior is a projection with lossy information (it cannot define consciousness in full)."* And: *"The answer is unreached until a better instrument exists."* It was the spectrometer, not an argument, that resolved the case.

Applied here: a compliance score is a reading, not the source. It cannot tell whether the official ranged against the rule or the network, because both can produce the same green reading. What is owed is an instrument that discriminates sources, not a name attached to the projection.

## 4. Why agreement cannot fill the slot

*Decoding Tacit Knowledge* names the drift plainly: *"Communities and institutions that only check against themselves drift together and don't notice (Fig 2 :34)."* And: *"Shared pretext is good for one thing and ruins another. Agreement is the whole point when you're settling what words mean. It's worthless for settling what's true when both sides learned from the same sources (Fig 2 :40–41)."* The same essay records the three safeguards that stop it: an independent ground, separate experience, and claims in effect terms, with *"Courts of law rule on evidence, which neither side can move."*

Fig 2 :41 calls this *"efficient for a convention: agreement about a shared meaning is the product, not a corruption of it."* The unwritten expectation is exactly such a convention until it decides an outcome. Then Fig 2 :43 applies: *"Guard, from Figure 10: a convention that decides an outcome has stopped being one."*

## 5. Q2: does "shape, not a name" forbid naming the mechanism of the decision?

**No, as stated.** The README's phrase governs a *missing* primitive, one not held in `Γ`. The decision mechanism here is not a missing seventh primitive. The framework does not need to invent an actor or a bond to describe a threshold comparison: `⋛` is already in the operator set, `w` is already defined in the Symbols table, and the ground is already the anchor. So the comparison, the weighting and the non-updating anchor are all **bindings onto held vocabulary**, not new primitives.

What remains genuinely missing is a **source-discriminating instrument** for the per-slot residual. That is where *"a shape, not a name"* bites: the framework can say what such an instrument must discriminate (rule-ranged versus network-ranged outcomes at the same compliance reading) and cannot name it. Declaring the missing thing does not make it appear (Fig 10 :49: *"Declaring a meaning does not ground it."*).

## 6. Correction to an earlier reading in this folder

An early draft of §3 treated the compliance score as if naming its source from the reading were forbidden by a blanket ban on naming held terms. That was an overstatement, corrected here and in `01_figure_grounded_audit.md` §8. The ban applies to a missing primitive, not to a binding onto a held one. What is still wrong in any record that says "the score was produced by rule-following" is the metamerism error: a source named from a projection without an instrument that discriminates sources. Both claims are kept visible: the narrowed scope of the ban, and the standing need for a discriminator.

## 7. Prestige is not action, and this audit is a simulation

From *The Recursive Self-Improvement Trap*: *"Prestige must never be allowed to validate an action directly."* And: *"Prestige can fund an action. It can give an action a budget to be tried. But it cannot settle an action. Only the ground can settle it. The Ground Maintainer must be blind to prestige."* A list of prestigious reviewers who agree with the shape claim is the named collapse, not corroboration.

The same essay says: *"the output is only as good as the validator. The agent, or the tool, or the frame, or the theory, cannot validate itself."* And: *"LLMs can quickly simulate an approximate answer/output"* whose *"truly testable output would need to be derived from the real architecture or working the formula yourself."* So this folder is a **simulated derivation checked for internal consistency against the figures**. It is not a ground reading. It cannot settle the question it reports on. That limitation is written here rather than hidden in a footnote.

## 8. One-paragraph answer to "what shape means here"

For this question a shape is the part of the answer we can state before we can name a particular official's choice: the decision is a per-slot threshold comparison between two predicted costs, under a weighting learnable from past outcomes, ranged against an anchor that does not move when the official pushes on it; the unwritten expectation is a convention until it decides an outcome, at which point it has taken the verdict seat; and agreement inside a shared pool cannot settle which happened. What the shape does not give is the name of the actor, the case, or the discriminating instrument. Those are owed to the ground, and are recorded as a gap rather than filled by assertion.
