## QUESTION

How do officials decide whether to follow formal rules or the unwritten expectations of political and social networks? Restated as an effect: for official-slot `s`, why does `R_s = |Γ(b_s,a_s) - o'_s|` stay small under one action and grow under the other, where `a_s` is either the codified rule or the network expectation? `b_s` mixes private history and inherited prior by a learnable `w` (Symbols table :22-26). Arity: three-place relation indexed by slot and time. Symmetry: the comparison is a threshold, not a symmetric join (Fig 12 :87). Scale: single decision, oversight room, habitat, career channel. What each option leads to: reading it as one question averages away the per-slot signal and invites a character explanation with no anchor; reading it as a union of two conditions keeps the grounds separate and ends in a declared gap where no source-discriminating instrument exists.

## VERDICT (from the figures)

The figure-derived verdict: the question is a union of two conditions, not one decision with two answers. The decision is a per-slot threshold comparison of two predicted costs, under a weighting learnable from past outcomes, against an anchor that must not move when the official pushes on it. The unwritten expectation is efficient as a convention and becomes a defect when it takes the verdict seat; a room that agrees with the room it oversees produces variation, not a verdict. Standing is a stake that survives outcomes and is declared, checking pools must be unauthored, and a tie's valence is relative to the residual. Verdicts are kept separate per domain and never averaged. Mode `general` for the shape, `specified` for the institutional application. The world sources corroborate the mechanism in effect terms; none overrules a figure line. Which official followed which action is a declared `gap`, not a hidden answer.

- cost if wrong: misdirected reform. A room captured by the unit it checks would be sold more compliance training, while a unit failing the formal anchor would be told to import more oversight, and one lever would be applied to a union that needs two. The larger cost is spending an outside checker's independence by sending a recording rather than a generator. Fig 2 :42 prices it: "Buy independence in proportion to the cost of being wrong." That cost is avoidable by leading with the shape claim and its refuter.
- undoable: yes. No domain is deleted if the claim fails; a refutation is added and the reach falls because something was added rather than struck out (Fig 6 :42-43). X1 was withdrawn without touching any finding, and the narrower convention reading replaced the broader one in a visible retraction log (`01_figure_grounded_audit.md` §8). A sent claim can be superseded by the corrected packet.
- reversed by: any of the seven pre-registered refuters in `01_figure_grounded_audit.md` §5, in particular a single variable predicting both conditions with residual trending down, a network expectation that holds its answer under pressure, an unauthored differing room that still fails to range, or an invariant actor valence across a change of residual. A source-discriminating instrument that returns the per-slot residual and names its source would close F9 and move the answer toward `grounded`. Agreement, prestige and office are not such instruments.
