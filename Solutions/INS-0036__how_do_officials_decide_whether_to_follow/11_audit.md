I redid the census from the residual line alone before comparing with the primary's conclusions, using the WATSON order: R by arity, symmetry and scale; one line per domain with actors / currency / anchor; partition or union; order by dependency.

Arity: a three-place relation (belief, action, outcome) indexed by slot and time. Symmetry: the choice is a threshold comparison, not a symmetric join, so it carries an ordered comparison bond and a sequence bond. Scale: single decision loop, oversight room, habitat, career channel. This is a **union compound**, not one hard question: a case in two conditions is not a harder case of either (Fig 9 :57).

- **D2 Formal rule frame.** Actors: rules, auditors, courts, inspectors, merit procedures. Currency: compliance and sanction. Anchor: an enforcement record that does not move when pushed. Admitted only when enforcement genuinely ranges; otherwise a frame pretending to be an anchor.
- **D3 Network expectation frame.** Actors: patrons, brokers, peers, ties. Currency: standing, reciprocal favour, reputation. Anchor: network continuation. Admitted as a convention. Its answer moves with members' influence, so it fails the anchor test.

- **X1 Character alone.** Actors: the person. Currency: talent or virtue. Anchor: none nameable. **Not a domain yet.** Withdrawn.
- **X2 The rule alone.** Absorbed into D2.

Order by dependency: perceive, name, state the objective, plan, learn and carry (Fig 3). Describe the residual before choosing a frame (Fig 9 :27-28). Settle each condition before comparing them at the union.

## Round trip gap R_T

The Symbols table defines R_T as `gap(x, (T_E ∘ T_A)(x))`, the gap between what was sent up and what came back down. Fig 4 :30 requires it to be measured rather than assumed. I ran the round trip on the condensation this folder actually uses.

2. **Abstraction (T_A):** the shorthand in `07_the_tether_answer.md`, namely "two conditions, threshold comparison, learnable weighting, non-updating anchor, convention until it decides an outcome, declared standing, declared gap".
3. **Concretisation (T_E):** a concrete institutional claim, for example "the inspectorate is captured because its members come from the same pool as the unit they inspect, so the unwritten expectation decides promotions".

**R_T is nonzero.** Three things the shorthand holds do not survive the trip back down:

- the **source-discriminating instrument** that would turn a compliance reading into a named source. The shorthand says "a ground reading"; the concrete claim needs an instrument that discriminates rule-ranged from network-ranged outcomes at the same reading. None is held. That is F9.
- the **independence of the checking pool**. The shorthand says "unauthored"; the concrete claim needs a mechanism (lottery, everyone who qualifies, a set assembled before the question existed, or the enumerated habitat) and a demonstration that the room's closure differs. Fig 10 :73-79 requires the mechanism, not the adjective.
- the **normative / descriptive anchor distinction**. The shorthand says "the ground"; the concrete claim must say whether it is asking "is this a good answer for a person" (human judgment is the right anchor, Fig 2 :37-38) or "which closure ranged" (the enforcement record). Conflating them produces a verdict from the wrong anchor.

Conversely, one thing survives cleanly: a threshold comparison per slot, against an anchor that does not move, with a convention kept separate from a verdict. That is the residue worth sending. R_T was **not** measured numerically, because no ground reading exists. It is recorded qualitatively as the enumerated set of dropped constraints. Treating "the formula is a condensed version of the metalaws" as identity would set R_T to zero and commit the dead-reckoning error Fig 4 :30 names. The honest form is a lossy condensation with a recorded gap, which is what `09_watson_answer.md` states.
