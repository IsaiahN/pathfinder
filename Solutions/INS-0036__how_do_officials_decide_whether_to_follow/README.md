## The question restated as an effect

`R_s = |Γ(b_s, a_s) - o'_s|` for official-slot `s`. Before acting, an official holds belief `b_s` formed from private history and inherited prior, mixed by `w` (Symbols table :22-26). The action `a_s` is one of two candidates: follow the codified rule, or follow the unwritten network expectation. `o'_s` is what the ground actually returns for that slot: a sanction, a protection, a promotion, a dismissal. The residual is indexed per official and per case, never aggregated (Fig 1 :19, :22).

**What an answer would look like, bound as an ordinary term (formula step "Direction"):** a shape stating which closure may range against which non-updating anchor, how the two predicted residuals are compared at a threshold, and how `w` is learnable from past outcomes. It is a rule for a class of slots, not the name of a particular official and not a prediction of a particular case. Bound this way, the sought-for shape can accrue a residual and be refused by the same test as anything else.

Read the two options as two frames with different closures and different anchors, not as two answers to one problem. The question is a union of conditions, not one hard question (Fig 9 :4-5, :55, :57).

## Deliberately not here

- No personal emails of named individuals.
- No ranking of people by how likely they are to agree.
- No bulk-send plan.

Three figure-grounded reasons. First, *"A room's agreement is variation, not a verdict. Selection between rooms is where the ground votes."* (Fig 7 :35). Second, *"High correlation is disqualifying for a verdict and efficient for a convention"* (Seat Map :163): selecting recipients for receptiveness manufactures the correlation that makes their agreement worthless. Third, *"A verifier that can reconstruct the claim cannot verify it."* (Fig 10 :42): showing the whole framework before asking for a verdict spends the outside frame's independence.

Legally, scraping personal emails for unsolicited bulk outreach breaches GDPR and CAN-SPAM style rules in many jurisdictions. Public opt-in channels avoid that practical failure as well as the structural one.
