## 1. A shape is a constraint, not a filler

The figures README states the limit: the framework returns *"what a missing thing would have to look like (a shape, not a name)."* Fig 9 :32: *"you get a kind of thing, never the answer itself."* Fig 13: *"It can say the answer is out of reach with the library as it stands... It cannot say which primitive is missing, because naming it would mean already holding it."*

From *The Library of Babel*: *"You can cut your search short if the set of books returned is small and descriptive enough that it provides a general shape of the answer that satisfies your question."*

So a **shape** is the narrowed form of an answer you can state before you can name it. It constrains; it does not fill. For this question the shape is: an admissible measure is agent-and-habitat, per slot, anchored to something that does not update on the score, and refusable. A universal intelligence index is a name, not a shape.

## 2. How a shape is obtained

- *Library of Babel*: use the vocabulary already closest to the thing, look for recurring terms that overlap with it, recurse. *"With each new term gained, you narrow the space and deepen your vocabulary."*
- Fig 6 :30: *"The description travels. Fails by being sharpened into terms only we can read. Cast it in effect terms."* Fig 9 :30-31: *"your words are borrowed too, so describe the effect rather than the cause."*
- *Decoding Tacit Knowledge*: *"When contact isn't possible, the best you can send is a plain description of what you'd expect to see, because anyone can check that against the world."*
- Fig 9 :27-28: *"describe it before you go looking, or any frame you pick will seem to fit."* The three candidate measures were named before the residual was described, which is the inversion this audit corrects.

## 3. What licenses a name

Only two things, both outside the shape itself:

1. **The ground settles it.** Formula step 5: *"the ground settles it. Verified only by what pays."* The README: the framework *"stops at the limit of what the available instruments can distinguish, and records the lineage so the stop is honest."*
2. **A provenanced import from a frame whose closure differs** (Fig 6 :23, Fig 8 :50-52), with a source gate and a shadow test.

*They're Scanning Books* sharpens this: *"The real import is not a new term in the existing space. It is a new dimension of the space."* And: *"Declaring the primitives does not make them neutral. It makes the choice visible."*

Applied here: choosing "contact" as the settling quantity is a binding from the framework onto a measurement practice, recorded as such. It is not a new primitive and it does not move a wall by itself.

## 4. Why an output reading cannot settle its own source

*The Hard Problem is About Source* gives the mechanism. Metamerism: an instrument reads green and *"cannot comment on the source"*; one green light, or yellow plus blue, or RGB, or CMYK, or a scheme not yet discovered. The reading does not determine the source. *"If several paths create the same behavior, then behavior is a projection with lossy information."* And: *"An instrument that can't distinguish these cases demonstrates its own inadequacy."* It was the spectrometer, not an argument, that resolved it.

This is the deepest reason "what a system can do" cannot be the measure: a behaviour is a projection, and a projection cannot comment on its source. What is owed is a discriminator, not more behaviour. Fig 11 :70 (*"A test harness is a substituted habitat. Which is why a synthetic solve proves wiring and never capability."*) is the framework's own version of the same point.

## 5. Two consequences for outreach

**Prestige is not action.** From *The Recursive Self-Improvement Trap*: *"Prestige must never be allowed to validate an action directly... Prestige can fund an action. It can give an action a budget to be tried. But it cannot settle an action. Only the ground can settle it. The Ground Maintainer must be blind to prestige."*

A campaign gathering famous names to agree with a measurement claim is the named collapse, not corroboration. The legitimate ask is the opposite: give the claim a budget to be tried and let a ground settle it.

**My own audit is a simulation, not a ground reading.** Same essay: *"LLMs can quickly simulate an approximate answer/output... However, the truly testable output would need to be derived from the real architecture or working the formula yourself."* So `01`, `06`, `07`, `09` and `11` are simulated derivations checked for internal consistency against the figures. They are not tests of the claim and must be labelled as such.

## 6. Correction to my own reading (kept visible)

**First pass:** I read Fig 11 :39 as forbidding any agent-level measure at all, and nearly wrote "you may only measure the habitat."

**Correction.** Fig 11 :39 says capability is a property of agent-and-habitat, not that the agent is unmeasurable. Fig 1 :19 indexes the residual per object slot, which is an agent-level reading; what is forbidden is the *isolated* agent reading that changes nothing in contact (Fig 11 :40). Likewise the README's "shape, not a name" governs a *missing* primitive; naming an already-available binding such as "per-slot residual" is provenance, not an invented primitive or a wall-moving import (Fig 6 : *"IMPORT adds an atom"*). Both the first pass and its correction are preserved so neither is quietly lost. Triangulated (Fig 11 :39-40 with Fig 1 :19 and the README).

## 7. What "shape" means for this question, stated plain

> An admissible measure of capability is a relation, not a property: it is read at the slot where the residual lives, against a ground that does not move when the score moves, across the habitat that the system actually contacts. It returns a shape (contact-changing, per slot, anchored, refusable), not a number. It stops at the limit of the available discriminator and records the lineage. Whether one particular index realises that shape is a ground question, and this folder does not answer it.

`How Intelligence Actually Works` (re-opened this session) states the same thing in its own vocabulary: *"The intelligence we experience is the emergent result of extensive mapping of a complex system against unknown and unforgiving environments."* The mapping is the intelligence, and the environment applies the test. That is Fig 11 :39-40 in essay form. It is framework-author material with maximal overlap, so it corroborates but does not independently witness.
