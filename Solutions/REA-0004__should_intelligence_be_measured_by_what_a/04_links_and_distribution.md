## 3. What the wider literature bears, and its limits

1. **The output-reading tradition has a named failure mode.** Goodhart's law and Turing's replacement of "can machines think?" with a behaviour test both show that a measure can be substituted for the thing and then gamed or mistaken for it. This matches Fig 1 :27 and Fig 11 :70, but neither source shares the figures' anchor, so they corroborate the shape only.
2. **The habitat is part of the object in cognitive science.** The SEP entry on embodied cognition and the ecological-validity literature in comparative cognition independently reach a relation between agent and environment. This is the strongest low-overlap corroboration for Fig 11 :39.
3. **Flexibility is not a discriminator.** The cephalopod review says behavioural flexibility alone cannot establish complex cognition and that underlying mechanisms must be measured. This agrees with F5 and explains why a transfer score is not yet a capability measure.
4. **Agreement from one pool is one witness.** All six sources could be read as saying variations of "look at the environment." The SEP and comparative-cognition sources are the genuinely low-overlap ones; the framework essay shares the author and evidence pool with the figures and carries no independent weight (Fig 2 :40).

No world source conflicts with the figure verdict. The conflict rule was not triggered; if it had been, the answer would have gone back to the domain census, not flipped.

## 4. Distribution: offer, do not push

- **Fig 4 :22-25:** *"Only methods cross up. Only head starts come down. A recording carried upward looks like knowledge and is a description of one occasion."* Sending the full framework before anyone has described a residual sends a recording. Lead with the claim and its refuter; offer links later.
- **Fig 10 :42:** *"A verifier that can reconstruct the claim cannot verify it."* A recipient who has absorbed the framework before being asked for a verdict has joined the frame being checked.
- **Fig 9 :27-28:** *"describe it before you go looking, or any frame you pick will seem to fit."* Ask what they would expect to see if the capability claim were true before giving them the source links.
- **Fig 2 :40:** agreement is worthless when the frames share a pool. A list selected for people likely to agree produces a convention, not corroboration.

**Recommended order in a reply:** (1) claim and refuter; (2) ask what they would count as a failure; (3) only after a recorded verdict, the three links, with the note that the figures, not this summary, are the authority.

## 5. The two-stage offer

**(1)** Check the separator against the ground. **(2)** Only if curious, read the theory that produced it. **(3)** Use the loop recursively on other problems, writing a fresh refuter and mode each time.

- Stage 1 before stage 2 is Fig 10 :52, *"Bring a characterised residual, never the source,"* and Fig 2 :45, *"Frames range against the ground. They do not negotiate with it."*
- Stage 2 is opt-in by construction. Once a recipient has absorbed the theory, their agreement stops being evidence, so the stage-1 verdict must be recorded first and later agreement is never pooled as corroboration.
- Stage 3 sends a method, which is permitted: Fig 4 :22, *"Only methods cross up. Only head starts come down."*
- Stage 3 still needs a ground per problem. A frame *"cannot check the direction of its own question, because the shape a frame is searching for is not a term it holds"* (THE_FORMULA, Direction), so each user writes down what would refute their result before running it and labels the mode of what they get.

**Hard precondition:** the two-stage order works only if the stage-1 claim survives. As written it is a simulated derivation with no ground reading, so it is offered for refutation, never as a settled result.

## 6. Filters are not witnesses

Venue prestige, journal impact factor, and the number of readers are **filters for the budget** (Fig 9 :60: *"Use filters for the budget and witnesses for the verdict. Never let a filter hand you a verdict."*). They decide what gets tried first. They say nothing about whether the capability claim is true. The verdict comes only from a non-updating ground reading.
