# Expert verification packet — the Tether on measuring intelligence

**What this is:** a claim in a form a measurement researcher or cognitive scientist can check. Mode, derivation and refuters are stated before the conclusion, so a reader knows what to look for and what would settle it.

**Mode: `specified` — not `grounded`.** No measurement is claimed to have settled the shape. This is a simulated derivation checked for internal consistency against the figures, not a ground reading. It cannot settle the claim it reports on.

## 1. The claim, in one paragraph

The question's subject term is refused: intelligence is not a nameable property of an agent alone, so the answerable question is what makes a system capable, and capability is a property of agent-and-habitat (Fig 11 :35, :39). Consequently no agent-internal reading is a sufficient measure. A score that rises without changing contact with a ground that does not move on the score has not measured capability (Fig 11 :40). A test harness is a substituted habitat, so a synthetic solve proves wiring, never capability (Fig 11 :70). Output, learning and adaptation form a union of overlapping conditions with separate anchors and verdicts, never a weighted average (Fig 9 :54-58, Fig 12 :125-127). Any admissible reading is per slot (Fig 1 :19, :22). No universal index is returned; that is a declared gap (Fig 9 :32).

## 2. Three checkable sub-claims

### C1 — framework-internal; checkable by reading Figure 11
- **Statement:** the question is the wrong question, and capability is agent-and-habitat, not agent alone. A contact-neutral improvement changes nothing.
- **Source, verbatim:** Fig 11 :35 *"The wrong question, and this figure is the replacement for it."* Fig 11 :39 *"So the answerable question is not what intelligence is, but what makes a system capable. And capability is a property of agent-and-habitat, never of the agent alone."* Fig 11 :40 *"A system cannot be made more capable by improving the agent in isolation. An improvement that does not change contact changes nothing, however much it improves."*
- **Refuter:** demonstrate an agent-only improvement that moves the ground without changing habitat contact, the falsifier the figure itself names at Fig 11 :72 (*"an agent-only improvement that moved the ground would refute the capability claim"*). Or produce a reading in which intelligence predicts ground contact per slot with no habitat reference.

### C2 — measurement-facing; checkable by a psychometrician or AI evaluator
- **Statement:** an output score inside a harness is not capability, and a global score hides live slots.
- **Source, verbatim:** Fig 11 :70 *"A test harness is a substituted habitat. Which is why a synthetic solve proves wiring and never capability."* Fig 1 :27 *"They are frame-internal. A frame cannot score itself with a quantity it also produces."* Fig 1 :19 *"The residual is indexed by object slot, not aggregated."* Fig 1 :22 *"Aggregating across slots is how a live signal disappears into an average."*
- **What to check:** take a benchmark suite you know. Does a rise in its score coincide with a change in contact with a ground that does not update on the score? Decompose the score per item or slot; is any live residual hidden by the aggregate? Does a source-discriminating instrument separate a synthetic solve from a solve in the contacted habitat?
- **Refuter:** show a synthetic solve carrying the same warrant as a contacted solve under such an instrument, or show a global score that preserves every live slot residual against the ground.

### C3 — structural; checkable against Figures 9, 12 and 3
- **Statement:** the three candidates form a union, not a partition; their verdicts must not be averaged; the objective link is where an instrument is first owed.
- **Source, verbatim:** Fig 9 :54 *"And when the split lands on a union rather than a partition, unbundle before you search again."* Fig 12 :125 *"A composition costs in two currencies, and they do not add."* Fig 3 :27 *"A link with no instrument is not a link that works. It is a link nobody has looked at."*
- **What to check:** can output, learning and adaptation be written over one shared atom set with different bonds? If yes, Fig 12 :117 makes them isomers; if each needs an atom the others lack, they are a functional group (Fig 12 :124). Which link stops the system under test, and was that measured or assumed (Fig 3 :29-30)?
- **Refuter:** show the three candidates are a clean partition with no overlap, or that a weighted sum preserves the two currencies rather than conflating them.

## 3. What is explicitly not claimed

- Not that output, learning or flexibility are useless. Each is admissible as an external ground reading, not as a self-score.
- Not that a universal intelligence index exists or is imminent. The framework returns a shape, not a name (Fig 9 :32).
- Not that "the environment" is one anchor. The habitat is enumerated, never composed (Fig 11 :43-44); the anchor is what settles the question and does not move on the score (Fig 2 :29).
- Not `grounded`, and not a claim that this simulated derivation settles anything.
- Not that the world sources are independent witnesses. Most share the framework author's evidence pool; the low-overlap sources (SEP embodied cognition, comparative cognition) corroborate the shape only.

## 5. What would settle it

A source-discriminating instrument, not a further argument. From *The Hard Problem is About Source*: a reading that reads green *"cannot comment on the source"*; the spectrometer resolved it. Here:

1. **Contact test:** does the measure's rise track a change in contact with a non-updating ground?
2. **Harness test:** can an instrument distinguish a synthetic solve from a contacted solve, and do they carry different warrants?
3. **Slot test:** after decomposition, is a live residual hidden by the average?
4. **Governing falsifier (Fig 11 :72):** an agent-only improvement that moves the ground.

## 6. What an expert can check in twenty minutes

1. Read Fig 11 :35, :39, :40, :70 and :72, five short passages.
2. Take one benchmark you know and apply the contact test and the slot test. Name the ground that does not update on the score.
3. Say which of the three readings requires an atom the others do not, and therefore whether the functional-group or isomer reading holds.
4. Name one instrument that would discriminate a synthetic solve from a contacted solve, or say why none is possible.
5. Send the refutation. A failure is more useful than an endorsement.
