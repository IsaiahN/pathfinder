## QUESTION

Decide, in effect terms, what the Tether says about three candidate bases for a measure: what a system can do, how it learns, how flexibly it adapts. Option A: pick one and defend it as *the* measure of intelligence. Option B: weight all three into one index. Option C: refuse the subject term, replace the question with what makes a system capable, and return the shape an admissible measure must have with a stated refuter. What each leads to: A and B optimise an agent-internal reading and can raise a score without changing contact, which Fig 11 :40 says changes nothing; C returns a per-slot, anchored, refusable decision procedure and declares the missing universal index as a gap rather than silence.

## VERDICT (from the figures)

The question as posed is refused, not answered. Capability is a property of agent-and-habitat, and contact is the settling quantity, so no agent-internal reading (output, learning, or adaptation taken alone) is a sufficient measure. Output through a harness proves wiring, not capability; learning is blind without an external correction; flexibility must be read per slot and as a relation. Assessment, learning and habitat are a union of overlapping conditions whose verdicts are kept separate and never averaged into one index. No universal measurement formula is returned; that is a declared `gap`, reported as unreachable rather than as silence. Mode `specified` on the substantive claims, `general` on the structural shape, not `grounded`.

- **cost if wrong:** bounded and locatable. The packet states mode, refuters and lineage before the conclusion, so an error is findable rather than invisible. The real cost is spending the independence of outside readers by sending a framework recording instead of a residual and a refuter, which Fig 2 :42 prices: "Buy independence in proportion to the cost of being wrong." That cost is avoidable by leading with the claim and its refuter.
- **undoable:** yes. The shape is recoverable without withdrawing a primitive: restate the question, name the per-slot ground, and split the union. A sent claim can be superseded by a corrected one. The universal index, if ever grounded, is added, not substituted for the shape.
- **reversed by:** a ground reading, not agreement. An instrument showing an agent-only improvement that moves the ground (Fig 11 :72) would break the capability claim; a source-discriminating instrument showing a synthetic solve carries the same warrant as a contacted solve would break Fig 11 :70; a decomposition showing a global score preserves every live slot residual would break Fig 1 :19, :22; a figure re-census admitting the three candidates as a clean partition would break the union reading. Until one of these fires, the verdict stands as `candidate`.
