## The question restated as an effect

Three candidate bases for a measure are offered: outputs (what a system can do), process (how it learns), and transfer (how flexibly it adapts). Choosing one changes what gets optimised, what gets credited, what instruments get built, and which failures become invisible. An answer is a decision procedure for which reading is admissible, at what scale, with what anchor, and what would refute it. That sought-for shape is bound as an ordinary term in `01_figure_grounded_audit.md` section 2 so it can accrue a residual like anything else.

## What is deliberately not here

- No personal emails of named individuals.
- No ranking of people by how likely they are to agree with the verdict.
- No bulk-send plan.

Three reasons, in the figures' own words:

1. **A room does not upgrade the label.** Fig 2 :13-15 describes two collapse modes, mutual update and a shared source, where frames converge on influence or on one pool rather than on a ground. Adding readers raises vocabulary and adds no anchor.
2. **Correlation disqualifies a verdict.** Fig 2 :40: agreement is worthless when the agreeing frames share an evidence pool. Selecting recipients for receptiveness manufactures exactly the correlation that voids their agreement.
3. **Asking for trust before checking spends independence.** Fig 10 :42: "A verifier that can reconstruct the claim cannot verify it." The first contact carries only the residual and its refuter.

Legally: scraping personal emails for unsolicited bulk outreach breaches GDPR and CAN-SPAM style rules in many jurisdictions and gets the sending domain blacklisted. That is a practical failure on top of the structural one.
