## Two things in the world sources that bear directly on this question

1. **The same-level question is underdetermined by design.** Dougherty (SEP, 2025) reports that gauge-variant differences in the potential are treated as theoretical surplus, reflecting no worldly differences. The philosophical dispute is over interpretation, not over a reading that would discriminate. This corroborates F6 in `01`: the missing thing is a source-discriminating instrument, not another argument. It does not decide the figure verdict, which comes from Fig 2 :29-30.
2. **Emergence is a scale claim with a ground.** Bass (2021) states that with emergence the gauge symmetries would dissolve above the scale of emergence, and reports observed emergent gauge symmetries in the low-energy limit of the Fermi-Hubbard model, string-net condensation and superfluid 3He-A. This corroborates the D3 split in `01`. It is not a derivation from the figures, and it is not evidence for F or E at the same level. Fig 8 :50-52: *"Convergent derivation and adopted import look identical in the contents. Only the record of where each came from separates them."*

A possible conflict was checked and not found. The framework says symmetry is never a substrate member; the physics literature says gauge symmetry is a principle used to build the Standard Model. These are different objects: a principle of theory construction and a member of the six-term set. Re-census resolves the apparent strain, so the conflict rule was not triggered.
