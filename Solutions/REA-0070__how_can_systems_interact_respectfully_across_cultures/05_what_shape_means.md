## 1. A shape is a constraint, not a filler

The canonical limit is in the figures README: the framework returns *"what a missing thing would have to look like (a shape, not a name)."* Fig 9 :32: **"you get a kind of thing, never the answer itself"**. Fig 13 :134: **"Unreachable is a verdict here, not a silence,"**.

From *How Intelligence Actually Works*: *"The mapping is not a means to intelligence; the mapping is the intelligence. And that mapping is not a tree with roots and branches, it is a web, with no canonical order and no single path through."*

For this question, a **shape** is the form of an interaction that can be stated before the specific culture, the specific people, or the specific etiquette is named. It constrains: each frame ranges against a ground that does not move; the reading is per person rather than pooled; the source of each reading is recorded; the criterion being sought is written down before the run. It does not fill: it says nothing about what bow, greeting, silence, or turn-taking counts as respect in a particular place.

- A shape for this question: `two conditions, C1 and C2, plus an instrument that discriminates their failures`.
- A name would be: `respect in culture X is behaviour Y`. The figures do not license that move.

## 2. How a shape is obtained

From *How Intelligence Actually Works*, the replicator passage: each pass of recursion *"adds content, not just constraint"*, and the process stops when *"the recursion converges toward indiscernibility, and indiscernibility is not identity."* The figures README says the same: *"Indiscernibility is not identity."*

Applied here: you narrow by asking the other what they would expect to see, in effect terms, then narrowing again with each failed prediction. Fig 6 :30: **"The description travels. Fails by being sharpened into terms only we can read. Cast it in effect terms."** Fig 9 :30-31: **"your words are borrowed too, so describe the effect rather than the cause"**.

The correct stopping report is not "we now know this culture." It is "the interaction no longer registers a fresh per-person residual against the ground we agreed to range against, and here is the lineage of how we got there."

## 3. The room warning is the core of this question

*How Intelligence Actually Works* names the exact failure the question asks about:

> *"if you and another person are trying to find the answers to a problem, and go into a brainstorming room, if you are just going off of what is present in your heads, then eventually, the room will be swayed by negotiation which is persuasion, or by coherent drift (both of you found a middle ground to agree on)."*

And the consequence:

> *"the room session only produces an outcome that you agree on, having come from diverse frames with different vantage points of the problem. It does not mean that the output was objective truth."*

And the remedy:

> *"The ground must be immovable by persuasion and not bi-directional like the frames are. The ground doesn't update on anything that the frames do."*

This is the framework's own wording for why "we all got along and agreed on a shared model" is not evidence of respect. It corroborates Fig 2 :35 and Fig 2 :45 directly. Mode `specified` for the cross-cultural binding; `general` for the structural claim about rooms.

The same essay notes that a frame is substrate-independent: *"A frame could be a person, a book of text, or a system."* That is why the question's word "systems" can include AI systems without changing the derivation. It also warns that bidirectionality degrades into *"the average mean"* with overuse, which is the stereotype mechanism in Fig 1 :22 at the group level.

## 4. What licenses a name

Only two things, both outside the shape itself:

1. **The ground settles it.** `THE_FORMULA.md` step 5: *"the ground settles it. Verified only by what pays. A gate is not the ground."* For a question about a person's experience, Fig 2 :37 makes human judgment the right kind of anchor, with the instrument caveat that follows it.
2. **A provenanced import from a frame whose closure differs** (Fig 6 :23, Fig 8 :51-52), with a source gate and a shadow test, debited against independence. The other's own account, attributed to them and dated, is the relevant import here.

Until one of those happens, the name is the searcher's own contribution and must be recorded as such. This is why `01_figure_grounded_audit.md` F11 declares the specific content of respect a `gap` rather than inventing a practice to fill it.

## 5. The prestige and source rules bear on outreach, not on the verdict

From *The Recursive Self-Improvement Trap*:

> *"Prestige must never be allowed to validate an action directly."*
> *"Prestige can fund an action. It can give an action a budget to be tried. But it cannot settle an action. Only the ground can settle it. The Ground Maintainer must be blind to prestige."*

For outreach this means: a list of respected names willing to agree with the shape gives the claim a budget to be tried, and gives it nothing more. A failure from an unknown reader is worth more than an endorsement from a famous one.

The same essay states the source rule for validation:

> *"The catch is that the output is only as good as the validator. The agent, or the tool, or the frame, or the theory, cannot validate itself. An external validator, or ground, is always needed to tether the veracity of the output to reality. Anything else is drift."*

And the limit of self-validation:

> *"Because it is a metatheory, it cannot be validated, nor can it validate itself."* (from *How Intelligence Actually Works*)

So the first contact carries the residual and the refuter, not the theory (Fig 10 :52), and later agreement is not pooled as corroboration (Fig 2 :40).

## 6. My own audit is a simulation, not a ground reading

*The Recursive Self-Improvement Trap*:

> *"LLMs can quickly simulate an approximate answer/output with the formula found in the architecture post. It will often be better than the generic outputs that you will usually get otherwise too... However, the truly testable output would need to be derived from the real architecture or working the formula yourself."*

So `01_figure_grounded_audit.md`, `06_isomers_and_order.md`, `07_the_tether_answer.md`, `09_watson_answer.md` and `11_audit.md` are simulated derivations checked for internal consistency against the figures. They are **not** tests of the claim. They cannot settle what respect is, and they cannot certify that any actual cross-cultural interaction was respectful. The human judgment anchor in Fig 2 :37 would have to do that work, in the actual situation, with the people in it.

## 7. Consequence for this question

The honest answer the figures support is a shape plus an open slot:

- Shape: triangulate, index per person, maintain the channel, keep convention and verdict apart, bind the criterion before the run.
- Open slot: what respect is here. Owed to the other's own account and to an instrument that discriminates it from the system's self-report.
- Stop: Fig 13 :115 - *"The set is closed. The arrangements are not. That gap is why import exists."* The stop is recorded with its lineage so a later reader can locate the error. It is not presented as a final answer.
