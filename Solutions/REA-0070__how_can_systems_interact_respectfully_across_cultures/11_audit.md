Arity: three places, since without a third term that does not update on either frame there is no anchor and only drift or mirroring is possible (Fig 2 panels 1 to 3). Symmetry: "closure differs" is symmetric, checking is not; the anchor is not a third party to the disagreement. Scale: single interaction, repeated chain, community, with a lossy transform between sizes. I read this off Fig 1 and Fig 2, not off the primary's census.
- **D1 System frame.** Actors: Γ, b, w, a. Currency: per-slot residual reduction. Anchor: its own prediction against `o'`, an instrument on axes it may not measure. Admitted provisionally.
- **D2 Other frame.** Actors: the other person or culture, their vocabulary, own account. Currency: their ranging against the ground. Anchor: the lived consequence for them. Admitted.
- **D3 Shared ground and habitat.** Actors: situation, conditions, history, relations, people affected. Currency: what settles. Anchor: the ground, which does not update, legitimate because the question is about people. Admitted.
- **D4 Interaction channel.** Actors: percepts, signals, bracket, metrics. Currency: contact and fidelity. Anchor: the ground, kept reachable; the channel decays. Admitted.
- **X1 Universal respect metric.** No anchor that does not update. Not a domain yet.
- **X2 System self-consistency.** A mirror. Refused as anchor.

Union, not partition: two conditions, independent ranging and a maintained per-person channel. ## Round trip gap R_T

Conversely, one thing survives cleanly: the requirement that each frame be corrected by a ground that does not move, and that the next encounter be selected against the residual.
