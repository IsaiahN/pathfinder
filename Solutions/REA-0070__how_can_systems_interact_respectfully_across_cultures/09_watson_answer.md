## QUESTION

How can systems interact respectfully across cultures without flattening differences or relying on stereotypes?

Restated as an effect: a system holds belief `b` about another frame, acts `a`, predicts `Γ(b,a)` about the other's experience, and observes `o'`. The residual `R_s = |Γ(b,a) - o'|` is indexed per person and per occasion, never averaged (Fig 1 :19). Option A, *achieve respect through a shared model of the culture*, predicts that once both sides share a vocabulary the interaction is respectful. Option B, "triangulate against what the interaction actually does to the people in it", predicts that each frame ranges against a ground that does not move, with a maintained per-person channel, and that a shared vocabulary is a convention rather than evidence about a person. Option A leads to a convention deciding an outcome; Option B leads to a checkable shape, written refuters, and an open slot for the specific content of respect. "Systems" means any bounded frame with a library, so persons, teams, institutions and models are in scope.

## DOMAIN CENSUS

R by arity, symmetry and scale, then one line per domain with actors / currency / anchor, partition or union, and order by dependency.

- **R.** Arity: three-place contact (system frame, other frame, a shared situation both are in contact with). Symmetry: "closure differs" is symmetric but the checking relation is not; each frame is corrected by the anchor separately, and the anchor is *"not a third party to the disagreement"* (Fig 2 :27). Scale: single interaction, repeated chain of interactions, community or institution, with a lossy transform between scales (Fig 4).
- **D1 System frame.** Actors: library Γ, belief b, weighting w, action a. Currency: per-slot residual reduction. Anchor: its own prediction against `o'`, an instrument that can be wrong on axes it does not measure (Fig 2 :37-38). Admitted provisionally.
- **D2 Other frame.** Actors: the other person or culture, their vocabulary, expectations and own account. Currency: their own ranging against the ground. Anchor: the lived consequence for them. Admitted.
- **D3 Shared ground and habitat.** Actors: situation, conditions, history, relations, people affected. Currency: what settles, the lived outcome. Anchor: the ground, which does not update. Legitimate here because the question is constitutively about people (Fig 2 :37). Admitted.
- **D4 Interaction channel.** Actors: percepts, signals, bracket across handoffs, metrics. Currency: contact and fidelity. Anchor: the ground kept reachable; the channel decays while the ground does not (Fig 10 :24). Admitted.
- **X1 A universal respect metric.** No nameable anchor that does not update on the frames. Not a domain yet.
- **X2 The system's own self-consistency.** No anchor; a mirror cannot correct you (Fig 2 :35). Refused as an anchor.

Union, not partition: C1 (independent ranging against a legitimate ground) and C2 (maintained per-person channel) must both hold; a case in two conditions is not a harder case of either (Fig 9 :57). Dependency order: perception to vocabulary to objective to planning to learn and carry (Fig 3 :4-15). Strain test applied: Fig 13 :75 governs the six substrate terms, not frames; the frame-level claim is re-censed to Fig 2 :45. Recorded in `01_figure_grounded_audit.md` F12.

## TETHER (primary, verbatim, per domain, triangulated / single-source / gap)

**D1 System frame, status triangulated (Fig 1 and Fig 10, per-slot rule vs channel decay, distinct figures).**

- Fig 1 :19 "The residual is indexed by object slot, not aggregated." -> group averages destroy the only signal that says where to look.
- Fig 1 :22 "Aggregating across slots is how a live signal disappears into an average." -> a stereotype is an aggregation failure, not merely an oversimplification.
- Fig 10 :26 "saturated metrics, pooled readings" -> the channel decays through pooled readings and stale percepts.

Reading: stereotyping is a notation and channel failure. Mode `specified`. Refuter: a pooled parameter that leaves every per-slot residual unchanged and still predicts each person's own account.

**D2 Other frame, status triangulated (Fig 6 and Fig 8, closure vs idempotence, distinct roles).**

- Fig 6 :23 "No frame escapes its own closure. The bound moves only on contact with a frame whose closure differs." -> the other's expectations cannot be derived from the system's library.
- Fig 8 :32 "Closure is idempotent. Composing inside a closed set never leaves it, so no search, however long, adds an atom. This one is definitional: it is what closure means." -> no amount of introspection supplies the missing contact.
- Fig 8 :51 "Convergent derivation and adopted import look identical in the contents. Only the record of where each came from separates them, and the surplus lives only where the closures differ." -> provenance of the other's account is the whole issue.

Reading: contact with a differing closure is required; what crosses is attributed and shadow-tested. Mode `specified`. Refuter: derive a specific other's expectations from the system's own closure with no contact and close the residual.

**D3 Shared ground and habitat, status triangulated (Fig 2 and Fig 11, anchor rule vs habitat valence).**

- Fig 2 :35 "Alignment is triangulation, not negotiation. A mirror cannot correct you." -> flattening is the mirror collapse, not a different preference.
- Fig 2 :45 "Frames range against the ground. They do not negotiate with it." -> direct frame-frame merge is not the checking relation. The Fig 13 :75 strain is re-censed to this line.
- Fig 2 :37 "Human judgment is the right anchor for is this a good answer for a person, because the question is about people." -> the lived outcome and the other's own account are the right kind of ground here.
- Fig 2 :41 "efficient for a convention: agreement about a shared meaning is the product, not a corruption of it" -> shared vocabulary is legitimate, as a convention.
- Fig 10 :47 "a convention that decides an outcome has stopped being one" -> using it as proof of respect is the collapse.
- Fig 11 :40 "A system cannot be made more capable by improving the agent in isolation. An improvement that does not change contact changes nothing, however much it improves." -> capability belongs to system-and-habitat.
- Fig 11 :51 "No standing list of good actors survives a change of residual." -> no fixed cultural trait list survives a change of what is at stake.

Reading: triangulate against a legitimate ground; keep convention and verdict separate; attribute capability to the pair. Mode `specified` for the binding, `general` for the anchor and habitat rules. Refuter: a system-only improvement with no contact change, or a convention that is also the verdict on a person with no separate reading.

**D4 Interaction channel, status triangulated (Fig 10 and Fig 7, maintainer vs room chain).**

- Fig 10 :24 "the ground does not decay, the channel does" -> channel maintenance is permanent work.
- Fig 10 :52 "Bring a characterised residual, never the source." -> first contact carries the shape and refuter, not the framework.
- Fig 7 :35 "A room's agreement is variation, not a verdict. Selection between rooms is where the ground votes." -> a room of agreeable readers cannot corroborate the claim.
- Fig 7 :36 "A chain of mirrors is one long room. The next room must differ from the last, and match the residual it surfaced." -> repeated interaction must differ and be chosen against the last residual.

Reading: maintain the channel, select the next encounter against the residual, and never pool agreement as evidence. Mode `specified`. Refuter: a chain of identical rooms that nonetheless reduces the per-slot residual with ground pressure measured high.

**Method and order, status triangulated (Fig 9, Fig 5, Fig 3).**

- Fig 9 :27 with :28 "describe it before you go looking, or any frame you pick will seem to fit" -> describe the effect before naming a cultural framework.
- Fig 6 :30 "The description travels. Fails by being sharpened into terms only we can read. Cast it in effect terms." -> use terms the other can check.
- Fig 5 :65 with :66 "make the sought-for shape an ORDINARY TERM. Write down what an answer would look like, bind it, and let it accrue a residual like anything else." -> bind the criterion before the run.
- Fig 3 :25 with :26 "The link between them, having terms for what a situation offers and being able to say what would count as progress, has neither, so it attracts the least attention and the fewest instruments." -> the objective link is the one that usually breaks.
- Fig 9 :60 "Use filters for the budget and witnesses for the verdict. Never let a filter hand you a verdict." -> a cultural average is a budget filter, never a witness.

**Naming what respect is in a given culture, status `gap`.** No figure line governs after a genuine re-census. The missing thing is characterizable in effect terms: an instrument that registers the other's own account of whether what mattered was respected and discriminates it from the system's self-report. It is not named here. Mode `general`.

## VERDICT (from the figures)

The shape survives and the specific content does not. Respectful interaction is a union: each frame ranges against a ground that does not move, and the channel to that ground is maintained per person and per occasion. A shared vocabulary is a convention about meaning, not evidence about a person, and a cultural average is a budget filter, not a witness for the verdict. Flattening is the mirror or identity-bond collapse; stereotyping is aggregation plus a recording used as a generator. Capability belongs to system-and-habitat, and repeated interaction is a room chain with no last room. What respect is in a given culture is a declared `gap`, owed to an instrument and to the other's own account, not filled with a name. Mode `specified`, ungrounded, candidate. Verdicts are kept per domain and never averaged.

- **cost if wrong:** if the anchor is mis-set to agreement, failures become invisible because the frames converge, and the cost arrives three steps out as a relationship that looks fine while the person in it was not respected (Fig 11 :52 and :53). If the reading is pooled, the live per-slot signal is buried in the average (Fig 1 :22) and the system cannot see where to look. Both costs are avoided by leading with the shape and its refuter rather than with a trait list or a shared model.
- **undoable:** yes. A refused binding is added to the record rather than deleted, so the reach falls because something was added (Fig 6 :41-43). The shape and its six falsifiers are recorded with provenance so a later reader can locate the error (Fig 10 :62-64). No primitive has to be withdrawn.
- **reversed by:** a ground reading, not agreement. The six pre-registered falsifiers are in `01_figure_grounded_audit.md` §4; in particular a system-only improvement with no contact change, a pooled parameter that preserves every per-slot residual and predicts each person's own account, a specific other derived entirely from the system's closure, or a convention that is itself the verdict on a person with no separate reading. A figure re-census that moved the anchor inside a frame would also reverse it, but it would contradict Fig 2 :33 and would be a strain requiring re-census, not a simple flip.
