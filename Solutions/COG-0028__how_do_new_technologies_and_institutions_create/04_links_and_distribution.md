## Wider literature that bears on the mechanism

These are secondary sources opened this session. They are corroboration only; none can overrule a figure line. Overlap is stated honestly.

1. **Human cognition, classical versus prototype categories** (Wikipedia, *Cognitive categorization*, 2026). *"Categorization is grounded in the features that distinguish the category's members from nonmembers."* Corroborates the per-slot discriminator in F4 rather than a global notion of "thought".
2. **Classification versus category creation** (Wikipedia, *Classification*, 2026). *"Classification is the activity of assigning objects to some pre-existing classes or categories. This is distinct from the task of establishing the classes themselves."* Directly supports the refusal of the naming-only reading in F4.
3. **Conceptual change** (Wikipedia, *Conceptual change*, 2026). The knowledge-in-pieces view says *"conceptual change is seen as the reorganization of these resources"*, which is the world-side analogue of Route B in F8. The page also records Kuhn's point that successive concepts are embedded in *"a distinct set of relationships with other concepts and investigative techniques"*, an arrangement claim.
4. **Ecological psychology / STS / HCI** (Wikipedia, *Affordance*, 2026). Gibson 1979: *"The affordances of the environment are what it offers the animal, what it provides or furnishes, either for good or ill."* Davis 2016/2020: technologies *"request, demand, encourage, discourage, refuse, and allow social action, conditioned on users' perception, dexterity, and cultural and institutional legitimacy."* This is the lowest-overlap corroborator for F2 and F5.
5. **Extended mind** (Wikipedia, *Extended mind thesis*, 2026). Clark and Chalmers' active externalism: *"the external objects must function with the same purpose as the internal processes."* The page also records Adams and Aizawa's *"causal-constitutional fallacy"* objection, which the figures answer with their own contact test (Fig 11 :40).
6. **Animal behaviour, genuinely low overlap** (Japyassú and Laland 2017, *Animal Cognition* 20(3):375 to 395, PMC open access). *"We conclude that the web threads and configurations are integral parts of the cognitive systems."* And: *"niche-constructed structures emerge as prime candidates for extending animal cognition."* Corroborates the artifact-as-instrument mechanism in F2 without sharing the human-language evidence pool. Spiders also show *"the categorisation of prey types"* and a sense of numerosity.
7. **STS / social construction of technology** (Wikipedia, *Social construction of technology*, 2026). *"technology does not determine human action; rather, human action shapes technology."* Its *interpretative flexibility* and *closure* stages are the world analogue of F5; its Principle of Symmetry is a methodological analogue of independence. Corroborates, not decides.
8. **Science and technology studies** (Wikipedia, *Science and technology studies*, 2026). *"Technoscience excels at shaping human thoughts and behavior by opening up new possibilities that gradually or quickly come to be perceived as necessities."* A direct statement of the effect in the question, from a different field.
9. **History of mathematics, low overlap** (Wikipedia, *History of mathematical notation*, 2026). Notation moves through rhetorical, syncopated and symbolic stages, and *"The increasing pace of new mathematical developments, interacting with new scientific discoveries, led to a robust and complete usage of symbols."* An inscription technology making new concepts writable; corroborates F2 and Route B.
10. **Sociology of technology, concrete case** (Wikipedia, *Selfie*, 2026). First known written use 2002; Oxford word of the year 2013; front-facing cameras and apps precede the word. *"improvements in design, especially the front-facing camera of the iPhone 4 (2010), mobile photo apps such as Instagram and Snapchat led to the resurgence of selfies in the early 2010s."* Illustration only, not a measurement.
11. **Language and thought** (Wikipedia, *Linguistic relativity*, 2026). Strong determinism *"has fallen out of acceptance"*; weak influence has empirical support. Whorf's "agreement" formulation and the criticism of the "empty barrels" anecdote are both recorded. Governs the refusal in F4 and the conflict entry in `10_source_ledger.csv`.

Overlap declaration: sources 1 to 5, 7, 8, 10 and 11 are Wikipedia syntheses drawing partly on overlapping psychology and sociology literature, so they are **one broad evidence pool**, not eleven witnesses. Source 6 (animal behaviour) and source 9 (history of mathematics) are the genuinely low-overlap corroborators. All eight framework essays share the framework author's pool completely and carry no independent witness weight. ## Distribution: offer, do not push

- **Fig 4 :22** *"Only methods cross up. Only head starts come down."* Sending the full architecture before anyone has described a residual sends a recording, not a generator. Lead with the shape claim and its refuter; offer links after a verdict is recorded.
- **Fig 10 :42** *"A verifier that can reconstruct the claim cannot verify it."* A recipient who has absorbed the framework before being asked for a verdict has joined the frame being checked.
- **Fig 9 :27-28** *"describe it before you go looking, or any frame you pick will seem to fit."* Ask what they would expect to see if the shape claim were true before giving them the links.
- **Fig 2 :41** *"efficient for a convention: agreement about a shared meaning is the product, not a corruption of it."* Agreement about the vocabulary is fine; agreement about the world is not evidence when the frames share a pool.
- **Fig 7 :13** *"agreement compounds, evidence does not."* A mass send selected for likely agreement manufactures a convention.

**Recommended order in a reply:** (1) the shape claim and the union split; (2) the pre-registered refuter; (3) ask what they would count as a failure and record the answer; (4) only then the links, with the note that the figures, not the summary, are the authority; (5) offer the formula as a method for their own problem, with their own refuter written first.
