# The Tether's answer: how new technologies and institutions create new categories

**Mode: `specified` on the per-domain mechanism, `general` on the structural shape, `gap` on which specific category is produced, not `grounded`.** These are shapes and routes, not results. Simulated derivation checked for internal consistency against the figures, not a ground reading (`05_what_shape_means.md` §7).

## 1. The residual it answers

The question bundles at least four overlapping conditions (Fig 9 :55 *"A bundled problem is usually a union of overlapping conditions, not a list of separate ones"*). Per domain, with verdicts kept separate:

- **D1, instrument / perception (`specified`, triangulated).** An artifact changes what registers at a slot. Fig 6 :26 *"instruments extend what can be represented at all."* Gibson's affordance and the spider-web case corroborate from low-overlap fields. An instrument extends representation within held axes; a new axis needs import.
- **D2, mint / library (`specified`, triangulated).** A new term is licensed by the product of support, reachability and novelty, then by the MDL bargain, then by ground settlement. Fig 5 :42 *"The acceptance test is a bargain, not a threshold."*
- **D3, convention / institution (`specified`, triangulated).** Institutions maintain shared meanings; agreement is the product (Fig 2 :41), but a convention that decides an outcome stops being one (Fig 10 :47). Closure by agreement is not settlement.
- **D4, import (`specified`, triangulated for the figure rule).** A primitive from a differing closure moves the wall, with source gate and shadow test. Fig 6 :14 *"the only operator that moves the wall."*
- **The cross-cutting split (`specified`, triangulated).** A new category is either a new axis (Route A) or a new arrangement over held atoms (Route B, an isomer). Fig 13 :115 *"The set is closed. The arrangements are not."*

No verdict is averaged with another. A lever that fixes D1 leaves D3 standing.

## 2. The shape

1. **The naming-only reading is refused first.** Fig 5 :65 *"make the sought-for shape an ORDINARY TERM"*; Operators table :58 *"a refutation is a negation, which is why a vocabulary without it can only accumulate."* A word laid on an unchanged world is a recording, not a creation.
2. **Contact precedes category.** The artifact is an actor in a habitat that is *"enumerated, never composed"* (Fig 11 :43). It changes the per-slot residual before it changes the vocabulary. Fig 3 :7 *"it has terms for what things afford: what can be moved, opened, joined, blocked."*
3. **The new term is minted under a product of guards** (Fig 5 :21, :23, :25, :26), then settled by a ground. Fig 5 :70 *"A term is settled when the ground pays for it. A term is usable when something can compose over it."* Gating is not settling.
4. **Institutions stabilise conventions, not verdicts.** Fig 2 :40 *"disqualifying for a verdict: agreement about the world is worthless when the agreeing frames share an evidence pool."* Because this question is constitutively about people, human judgment is the legitimate anchor (Fig 2 :37), provided it does not update on what the frame thinks.
5. **Descent is a head start.** Fig 4 :22 *"Only methods cross up. Only head starts come down."* A category that crosses to others makes their next discrimination cheaper; a verbatim recording does not.
6. **"Alter everyday thought" is per-slot.** Fig 1 :19 *"The residual is indexed by object slot, not aggregated."* The weak form of linguistic relativity corroborates influence without determinism; the strong form is refused.
7. **The specific category is a `gap`.** Fig 9 :32 *"you get a kind of thing, never the answer itself."* What is owed is a discriminator, not a forecast list.

## 3. What it explicitly does not say

- Not that language determines thought. Strong determinism has fallen out of acceptance in the world literature and is disqualified here by Fig 2 :40.
- Not that every technology creates a category. Fig 5 :41: *"a system that is minting nothing may have nothing wrong with its minting."*
- Not that a new instrument is always required. A new arrangement over held atoms needs no new instrument (`06` Q1).
- Not that the framework names which category appears. That is a binding from the world frame, provenanced as such.
- Not that institutions are good or bad category-makers. Fig 11 :51 *"No standing list of good actors survives a change of residual."*
- Not a final taxonomy. Fig 7 :6 *"the limit: no last room."*
- Not `grounded`. No measurement of human categorization was taken.

## 4. What would settle it, and what is owed now

The anchor for this question can be human judgment, because the question is about people (Fig 2 :36-37). That still requires a reading that does not update on the frame's own report.

1. **The discriminator.** An instrument that distinguishes a new axis by import from a new arrangement over held atoms at the moment the category appears, instead of inferring the route from the word. Metamerism: the reading *"cannot comment on the source"*.
2. **The slot test.** Per-slot residuals before and after learning the category under an instrument capable of detecting a change. If nothing changes at any slot, F4 is refuted.
3. **The head-start test.** Whether a transmitted category makes a newcomer's next discrimination cheaper, rather than producing a replay. Fig 4 :24.
4. **Standing falsifiers from `01` §5.** Support zero with a live mint; novelty that was already in atoms(Γ); a category that changes no prediction; agreement used in place of a ground; the two routes collapsing into one.

**Honest stop.** One live strain remains: bits and actions are two currencies that do not add (Fig 12 :125-127). The cost claim is structural, not numerical. Unreached, not unreachable. The lineage is recorded so the stop is checkable. *"Indiscernibility is not identity."*

## 5. One-paragraph version for outreach

> The Tether does not say that language determines thought, and it does not return a list of technologies and the words they produced. It says the question is a union of four conditions, not one lever. An artifact first changes what registers at a slot; a category is licensed only when that residual has support, composes from what is held, is genuinely new, costs less than the confusion it removes, and is paid for by a ground; institutions stabilise the resulting shared meaning as a convention, but agreement cannot serve as a verdict about the world; and the category reaches others as a head start that makes their next search cheaper, not as a recording of one occasion. There is also a split that is easy to miss: a new category can be a new axis imported from a different frame, or a new arrangement of atoms already held, an isomer. Only the first moves a wall. Both alter per-slot prediction, which is what "alter everyday thought" means here. Which specific category a given technology will force is a declared gap: what is owed is a discriminator and a lineage, not a guess. The falsifiers were written before the run.
