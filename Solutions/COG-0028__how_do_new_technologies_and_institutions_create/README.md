## What an answer would look like (the bound shape)

An answer is a mechanism with four parts: (1) a new distinction becomes registerable at a per-slot residual, (2) a term is minted or imported and settles against a ground, (3) the term crosses to other people as a head start rather than a recording, and (4) the same situation now yields a different per-slot prediction in everyday reasoning. Anything that returns only a list of technologies and the words they produced is a recording of one occasion, not this shape.

## What is deliberately not here

- No personal emails of named individuals.
- No ranking of people by how likely they are to agree.
- No bulk-send plan.
- No claim that the framework names which category a given technology will produce. It returns a shape and a discriminator, not a name.

Three reasons, in the figures' own words:

1. **Agreement is not a verdict.** Fig 2 :40: *"disqualifying for a verdict: agreement about the world is worthless when the agreeing frames share an evidence pool."*
2. **A room does not add an anchor.** Fig 7 :13: *"agreement compounds, evidence does not."*
3. **Do not hand the interior to the checker.** Fig 10 :42: *"A verifier that can reconstruct the claim cannot verify it."*
