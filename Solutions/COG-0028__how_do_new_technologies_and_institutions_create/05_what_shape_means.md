## 1. A shape is a constraint, not a filler

The canonical README states the limit: the framework returns *"what a missing thing would have to look like (a shape, not a name)."* Fig 9 :32: *"you get a kind of thing, never the answer itself."* Fig 13 :135: *"It cannot say which primitive is missing, because naming it would mean already holding it."*

From *The Library of Babel*, opened this session: *"you can cut your search short if the set of books returned is small and descriptive enough that it provides a general shape of the answer that satisfies your question."*

So a **shape** is the narrowed form of an answer you can state before you can name it. It constrains; it does not fill. For this question:

- **Shape:** a mechanism in which a new distinction registers at a per-slot residual, a term is paid for and settles, the term crosses as a head start, and per-slot predictions change. Plus the two-route split: a new axis by import, or a new arrangement over held atoms.
- **Not the shape:** a list pairing technologies with the words they produced (front-facing camera yields "selfie", smartphone yields "phubbing"). That is a recording of one occasion, Fig 4 :25.

## 2. How a shape is obtained here

- *Library of Babel*: use the vocabulary already held that is closest to the thing, then look for recurring new terms that overlap or correlate with it. *"With each new term gained, you narrow the space by definition and deepen your vocabulary."*
- Fig 6 :30: *"outward: does another frame already hold it? The description travels. Fails by being sharpened into terms only we can read. Cast it in effect terms."* Fig 9 :30-31: *"your words are borrowed too, so describe the effect rather than the cause."* This is why the residual in `01` is written as positive mass at a slot rather than as "language influences thought".
- *Decoding Tacit Knowledge*, opened this session: *"When contact isn't possible, the best you can send is a plain description of what you'd expect to see, because anyone can check that against the world."* This governs the outreach order in `02` and `04`.
- *Library of Babel* on the bundle: a failed search may mean *"your problem was a bundle or conflation of several problems masquerading as one?"* That is Fig 9 :55 in narrative form, and it is why F1 of `01` refuses the one-lever question.
- The stopping test in the same essay: *"you will know that you are done because the finish line will also be the starting place"*, and the paradox that by the time you have the exact terms *"you have already composed the answer through the process of locating it."* In this solution the stop is the shape plus the refuter, not a forecast list.

## 3. What licenses a name

Only two things, both outside the shape itself:

1. **The ground settles it.** Formula step 5: *"the ground settles it. Verified only by what pays. A gate is not the ground."* For a question about everyday thought, Fig 2 :36-37 makes human judgment the legitimate anchor, provided it does not update on the frame's own report.
2. **A provenanced import from a frame whose closure differs** (Fig 6 :23, Fig 8 :50-52), with a source gate and a shadow test.

*They're Scanning Books*, opened this session, adds the distinction that governs this question: *"The real import is not a new term in the existing space. It is a new dimension of the space. Importing a term extends what can be said; importing an axis extends what can be perceived at all, and only the second reaches structure the current primitives cannot represent."*

It also says: *"Instruments extend the sensory window but not the conceptual one, they reach what we cannot see but can imagine measuring."* So an instrument extends representation within held axes; a new axis requires a different frame. This is the world-side statement of F8's two routes.

And the refusal of neutrality: *"A minimum viable set of six terms is a human's careful guess at reality's compression axes; another sensorium's minimum set need not be those six."* Also: *"Declaring the primitives does not make them neutral. It makes the choice visible."*

## 4. Correction to my own reading of "shape, not a name"

I first wrote that the rule forbids naming any category in this answer. That is wrong, and the correction stays visible.

- The README's phrase is *"what a **missing** thing would have to look like (a shape, not a name)."* The subject is a primitive not held in Γ.
- The categories discussed here (a self-portrait category, a "volatile residue" category) are bindings from the world frame onto terms already available. Naming them is provenance, not the invention of a seventh primitive.
- What **is** forbidden is claiming that the framework predicts which category a technology will force, or that naming one moves the wall. Fig 6 :25: *"An import adds no atom to the world."*
- What remains genuinely unreachable is the **discriminator** that would let an instrument decide the case before the word is coined. That is the `gap` in F7.

Both the original overstatement and the narrowed form are recorded here and in the retraction log of `01`, so neither is silently lost.

## 5. The metamerism point, kept

*The Hard Problem is About Source*, opened this session: an instrument reads green and *"cannot comment on the source."* Yellow plus blue, RGB, CMYK or a scheme not yet discovered can all produce the same reading. *"If several paths create the same behavior, then behavior is a projection with lossy information."* The spectrometer resolved the case, not an argument.

Applied here: observing that people use a new word after a technology arrives does not tell us which route produced the category. A new axis by import and a new arrangement over held atoms can be indistinguishable in the word alone (Fig 8 :50-52: *"Convergent derivation and adopted import look identical in the contents. Only the record of where each came from separates them"*). What is owed is a discriminator and a lineage, not a confident attribution. This point was not retracted by the correction in section 4; its reason changed from "naming is banned" to "the reading does not determine the source".

## 6. Prestige is not action

From *The Recursive Self-Improvement Trap*, opened this session: *"Prestige must never be allowed to validate an action directly."* And: *"Prestige can fund an action. It can give an action a budget to be tried. But it cannot settle an action. Only the ground can settle it. The Ground Maintainer must be blind to prestige."*

In this field that matters because the obvious move is to gather respected names who already agree that technology shapes thought. That is a convention, not a check. Fig 2 :42: *"Buy independence in proportion to the cost of being wrong."*

## 7. My own audit is a simulation

Same essay: *"LLMs can quickly simulate an approximate answer/output with the formula found in the architecture post."* But *"the truly testable output would need to be derived from the real architecture or working the formula yourself."*

So `01`, `06`, `07`, `08`, `09` and `11` are simulated derivations, checked for internal consistency against the figures. They are **not** a test of the claim and must be labelled as such. This solution cannot settle whether a given technology creates a given category. It can only state the shape such an answer must have, the refuter that would break it, and the instrument that would be needed to close it.
