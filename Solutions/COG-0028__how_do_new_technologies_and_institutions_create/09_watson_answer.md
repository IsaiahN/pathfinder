## QUESTION

Decide, in effect terms, how an artifact or institution produces a new category that changes everyday prediction. Option A: categories are labels laid on an already-divided world, so "create" is naming and "alter thought" is substituting words. Option B: contact changes what registers at a slot, a term is minted or imported and settles, the term crosses to others as a head start, and per-slot predictions change, with a split between a new axis and a new arrangement. What each leads to: A gives a vocabulary claim with no discriminator between a new category and a new name; B gives a union mechanism with an anchor, a discriminator, per-domain verdicts and refuters, and a declared gap where no figure line governs the specific category.

## DOMAIN CENSUS

R by arity, symmetry and scale, then one line per domain with actors, currency and anchor, partition or union, and order by dependency.

- **R (the residual).** Arity: per-object slot, not a global "thought" value (Fig 1 :19). Symmetry: ordered, since contact precedes category and category precedes changed prediction; a correlation cannot choose that arrow. Scale: two, the individual loop and the population membrane. Content: after contact with a new actor or instrument, at least one slot carries positive mass the held category system did not predict, with no term that composes a response at acceptable cost.
- **Domain 1, instrument and perception at the individual scale.** Actors: the person and the artifact as instrument or affordance. Currency: per-slot residual and representational access. Anchor: the ground the person ranges against; because the question is constitutively about people, human judgment is legitimate here (Fig 2 :36-37). Type: first link of a chain. Verdict: `specified`, triangulated.
- **Domain 2, mint and library at the individual scale.** Actors: the learner and the candidate term. Currency: description length in bits and actions in trials. Anchor: ground settlement, not the guards (Fig 5 :69-72). Type: partition boundary on which terms compose. Verdict: `specified`, triangulated, with the numerical bargain unmeasured.
- **Domain 3, convention and institution at the population scale.** Actors: members of a practice community and institutions as seats maintaining meanings. Currency: shared meaning and agreement. Anchor: the ground for a verdict; agreement is the product for a convention (Fig 2 :40-41). Type: union with Domain 2. Verdict: `specified`, triangulated.
- **Domain 4, import and differing closure.** Actors: the technology or institution as an external frame and the primitives carried across a wall. Currency: atoms crossing the boundary. Anchor: source gate plus shadow test. Type: union with Domains 1 and 2; inward and outward are never read against each other (Fig 6 :31). Verdict: `specified` on the figure rule, triangulated.
- **Order by dependency.** Refuse the naming-only reading before minting (Formula step 2); perception before vocabulary (Fig 3 :20); settlement before crossing (Fig 5 :70, Fig 4 :22); describe the residual before choosing a frame (Fig 8 :53).
- **Partition or union.** The four conditions overlap, so the residual is a union compound. Verdicts stay per domain and are never averaged. One lever leaves the intersections standing (Fig 9 :55, :57).
- **Strain test.** Fig 6 instrument rule and Fig 12 isomer rule first looked contradictory. Re-census showed they govern different objects: access to new atoms versus arrangements over held atoms. The apparent conflict dissolved; the distinction became F8.

## VERDICT (from the figures)

The union mechanism survives; the naming-only reading and strong determinism do not. An artifact changes what registers at a per-object slot; a term is licensed by support, reach, novelty, a bargain and ground settlement; institutions stabilise the shared meaning as a convention whose agreement is a product rather than a verdict; the term crosses to others as a head start, not a recording; and "alter everyday thought" is a change in per-slot prediction, not an aggregate relabelling. The new category is either an imported axis, which moves the wall, or an isomer over held atoms, which does not. Both routes alter thought. Which specific category a given technology will force is a declared `gap`: the framework returns a shape and a discriminator, not a name, and `unreached` is reported rather than silence. This is `specified` on the mechanism, `general` on the shape, `gap` on the specific category, ungrounded, candidate.

- **cost if wrong:** bounded and locatable. The packet states mode, writes refuters before the conclusion and records the lineage, so an error is findable rather than invisible. The real avoidable cost is handing outside readers a recording instead of a generator, which Fig 2 :42 prices (*"Buy independence in proportion to the cost of being wrong."*). A second cost is conflating a new axis with a new arrangement, which would spend a whole budget importing an atom that was not needed.
- **undoable:** yes. The refused readings (strong determinism, naming-only, closure-as-settlement, new-instrument-always) are removable without touching the derivation. `07_the_tether_answer.md` and `08_expert_verification_packet.md` already remove them. No primitive has to be withdrawn, and the lineage remains visible.
- **reversed by:** a ground reading, not agreement. A discriminator that separates an imported axis from a held-set rearrangement at the moment a category appears would settle F8; per-slot measurements showing no change before and after learning would refute C1; a category whose verdict owes its weight to a shared evidence pool would refute C3; a demonstration that Fig 6 inward/outward and Fig 12 isomer govern the same quantity would collapse the two routes. Until one of these fires, the verdict stands as `candidate`.
