## What the wider literature adds, and what it does not decide

The business literature in `09_watson_answer.md` is one evidence pool. It supplies candidate market attributes (market versus technology uncertainty, buyer count, ease of modification, resource slack, community of inquiry), but the figures decide the verdict. A conflict inside that pool was returned to the census rather than allowed to flip a verdict: Harms and Schwery report that performance was not related to uncertainty or business type while other sources report moderation by uncertainty and business type. Re-census resolved it by slot: project performance (on-time, on-budget, high-quality solution) is not venture survival, and market uncertainty is not technology uncertainty (Fig 1 :19, residual per slot, never aggregated). Both can be true at different slots. That is a re-census, not a compromise verdict.

The cognitive-science source on effectuation is a genuinely low-overlap field. It corroborates the shape of control under non-existent markets but does not decide the business claim. Its agreement is one extra witness for the mechanism shape, not a second ground reading.

## Distribution: offer, do not push

- **Fig 4 :22** - *"Only methods cross up. Only head starts come down."* **Fig 4 :25** - *"A recording carried upward looks like knowledge and is a description of one occasion."* Lead with the shape claim and its refuter; offer the links only after a stage-1 verdict is recorded.
- **Fig 10 :52** - *"Bring a characterised residual, never the source."* Send the residual, not the framework interior.
- **Fig 9 :27-28** - *"describe it before you go looking, or any frame you pick will seem to fit"* - ask what the reader would expect to see if the shape claim were true before showing any source.
- **Fig 2 :40** - *"agreement about the world is worthless when the agreeing frames share an evidence pool"* - a later endorsement is never pooled as corroboration.
- **Seat reasoning rule** - an interest in the outcome is unavoidable, an interest in the reading is disqualifying. Declare any stake in the reply.
- **AOM AI policy** is a practical submission constraint, not evidence about lean startup. A simulated derivation cannot be submitted as if it were an author's own empirical work. Check the target venue's policy before sending anything.

**Recommended order in a reply:** (1) the shape claim and its five refuters; (2) ask what the reader would count as a failure and what instrument would measure ground pressure; (3) record that verdict; (4) only then, if they opt in, the three framework links, with the note that the figures, not the summary, are the authority; (5) offer the formula as a method to reuse, with the instruction that each user writes their own refuter and states their own mode.

**Hard precondition:** stages 1 to 3 only work if the stage-1 shape survives. `01` F1, F2 and F4 are where it either does or does not. If a reader fires one of the five refuters, the packet is withdrawn or narrowed before any theory is shown.
