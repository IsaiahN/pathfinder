## 1. A shape is a constraint, not a filler

The figures README states the limit: the framework returns *"what a missing thing would have to look like (**a shape, not a name**)."* Fig 9 :32: *"you get a kind of thing, never the answer itself"*. Fig 13 :134: *"Unreachable is a verdict here, not a silence,"*.

From *The Library of Babel*, verified this session: *"you can cut your search short if the set of books returned is small and descriptive enough that it provides a general shape of the answer that satisfies your question."* And: *"The problem you have determines what makes a book relevant to you or not."*

So a **shape** is the narrowed form of an answer you can state before you can name it. It constrains; it does not fill. For BUS-0043 the shape is *"a loop that maintains contact with a ground that pays and pressures, plus a closure that differs"*. *"B2C consumer software"* is a name. *"High market uncertainty, low technology uncertainty"* is a name from a world source, provenanced as such.

This is why the forced either/or in the question is malformed. Both offered answers are names of candidate causes. The shape under them is a two-condition compound (Fig 9 :55-57).

## 2. How a shape is obtained

- *Library of Babel*, verified: use the vocabulary you already have that is *closest* to the thing, look for recurring new terms that overlap or correlate with it, recurse. *"With each new term gained, you narrow the space by definition and deepen your vocabulary."*
- Fig 6 :30: *"The description travels. Fails by being sharpened into terms only we can read. Cast it in effect terms."* Fig 9 :30-31: *"your words are borrowed too, so describe the effect rather than the cause"*.
- *Decoding Tacit Knowledge* (canonical mapping): *"When contact isn't possible, the best you can send is a plain description of what you'd expect to see, because anyone can check that against the world."*
- *Library of Babel* on stopping, verified: *"the finish line will also be the starting place"* and *"you have already composed the answer through the process of locating it."*

Applied here: describe the residual in effect terms (loop speed measured by the loop's own output versus ground-visible outcome), then let figure lines narrow which quantities can be witnesses. The sector labels arrive afterwards, if at all.

## 3. What licenses a name

Only two things, both outside the shape itself:

1. **The ground settles it.** Formula step 5: *"the ground settles it. Verified only by what pays. A gate is not the ground."* The README: the framework *"stops at the limit of what the available instruments can distinguish, and records the lineage so the stop is honest."*
2. **A provenanced import from a frame whose closure differs** (Fig 6 :23, Fig 8 :50-52), with a source gate and a shadow test.

*They're Scanning Books When They Should* (canonical mapping): *"The real import is not a new term in the existing space. It is a new dimension of the space."* Also: *"Declaring the primitives does not make them neutral. It makes the choice visible."*

A market attribute becomes a name only when an instrument discriminates it. Harms, Marinakis and Walsh (world source W1) distinguish market uncertainty from technology uncertainty; Zhuge et al. (W2) report that market dynamics and technology dynamics have opposite moderation signs. These are candidate names from the business frame. They are not figure returns and they carry zero verdict weight until a ground settles them.

## 4. Why "faster learning" is a source error, not just an incomplete metric

*The Hard Problem is About Source*, verified this session: *"If several paths create the same behavior, then behavior is a projection with lossy information"*. An instrument that reads green *"cannot comment on the source"*; several underlying causes can create the same reading. And: *"the answer is unreached until a better instrument exists."*

The analog here is direct. A short build-measure-learn cycle can be produced by (a) a venture actually ranging bets against a paying ground, (b) a venture asking only friendly respondents, (c) a venture with a metric that saturates, or (d) a venture iterating on a technology that cannot yet be built. The loop count is the projection. It cannot comment on the source. The error in option A is naming the source (learning) from a reading (loop speed) that several sources can produce. What is owed is a discriminator, not a faster loop.

*The Recursive Self-Improvement Trap* (canonical mapping): *"the output is only as good as the validator. The agent, or the tool, or the frame, or the theory, cannot validate itself."*

## 5. What is the shape for BUS-0043?

**Answer:** the shape is a two-condition compound, each condition stated so it can be measured and refused.

- **C1, contact maintained.** The measurement re-enters as the next bet (Fig 12 :122, cycle) and the loop contains an operator that can refuse a held hypothesis (Operators :58, negation). Ground pressure is nonzero (Fig 7 :39). The channel is not saturated or pooled (Fig 10 :26).
- **C2, reach renewed.** The market supplies at least one term the team did not hold (Fig 6 :23, contact with a differing closure), so that composition inside the same closure is not mistaken for discovery (Fig 6 :32).

Effectiveness is not C1 or C2; it is the compound under both (Fig 9 :57). Which concrete market attributes satisfy C1 and C2 is a declared `gap` (01 F8). The shape returns a kind of thing, not a sector list.

## 5b. Correction to my own §3

I initially wrote that naming a market attribute is forbidden by *"shape, not a name."* That overstates the rule. The README's phrase governs a **missing** primitive, one not held in Γ. Market and technology uncertainty are already held in the business frame's library; naming them is a **binding** from that frame onto terms the frame already has. What is forbidden is presenting the binding as a figure return, or claiming it moves the wall when it adds no atom to the framework (Fig 6 :22: *"IMPORT adds an atom, and the added atom is what expands the closure."*). My §4 metamerism point is **not** retracted: loop speed still cannot comment on its source. The reason is the missing discriminator, not a ban on naming world terms. Both readings stay visible.

## 6. Two consequences for outreach

**Prestige is not action.** From *The Recursive Self-Improvement Trap* (canonical mapping): *"Prestige must never be allowed to validate an action directly... Someone let prestige stand in for proof... Prestige can fund an action. It can give an action a budget to be tried. But it cannot settle an action. Only the ground can settle it. The Ground Maintainer must be blind to prestige."* A list of well-known entrepreneurs who agree with the shape is the named collapse, not corroboration. The legitimate ask is a budget to try the refuter against a ground.

**My own audit is a simulation, not a ground reading.** Same essay: *"LLMs can quickly simulate an approximate answer/output... However, the truly testable output would need to be derived from the real architecture or working the formula yourself."* So `01` and `09` are simulated derivations checked for internal consistency against the figures. They are **not** tests of the lean startup question and must be labelled as such. They cannot settle anything they report on.
