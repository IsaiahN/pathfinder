## The question restated as an effect

What is being decided is where the effectiveness of lean startup methods lives. Option A puts it inside the venture: the build-measure-learn loop runs faster, so residual R = |Γ(b,a) - o'| falls per unit time. Option B puts it in the market: the same loop pays off only where the habitat answers quickly, cheaply, and with pressure that can be ranged against. What each leads to: A prescribes accelerating the loop everywhere; B prescribes first checking whether the ground can settle the bets the loop makes. The answer this solution reaches is that A and B are a **union compound**, not a partition, so a single lever leaves the intersection standing (Fig 9 :55, :57).

**What an answer would look like, bound as an ordinary term before the run (Formula "Direction"):** a statement of the two conditions that must both hold for lean methods to move a ground-visible outcome, the shape of a market in which the loop can hold contact, and a refuter written in advance that would fire if either condition were unnecessary. This is the sought-for shape made refusable, not a preference smuggled past the checks.

## What is deliberately not here

- No personal emails of named individuals.
- No ranking of people by how likely they are to agree with the claim.
- No bulk-send plan.

Three reasons in the figures' own words:

1. **A room does not upgrade the label.** A list of agreeable readers raises vocabulary, not anchor. Fig 2 :35: *"Alignment is triangulation, not negotiation. A mirror cannot correct you."*
2. **Selecting for receptiveness manufactures the disqualifying correlation.** Fig 2 :40: *"agreement about the world is worthless when the agreeing frames share an evidence pool."* Fig 9 :60: *"Use filters for the budget and witnesses for the verdict. Never let a filter hand you a verdict."*
3. **Asking for trust before a check spends the reader's independence.** Fig 10 :42: *"A verifier that can reconstruct the claim cannot verify it."*

Legally: scraping personal emails for unsolicited bulk outreach breaches GDPR and CAN-SPAM style rules in many jurisdictions. That is a practical failure on top of the structural one. Use the public submission routes in `03`.

## Authority and boundary

Figures are primary and decide. The world literature in `09` and `10` is secondary corroboration only, and most of it comes from one evidence pool, which is declared rather than hidden. Sources used were the local figure text and canonical README, `THE_FORMULA.md`, the seat map, the eight essays where quoted, and the public web pages recorded with accessed dates in `10_source_ledger.csv`. Nothing in this folder was written into the framework, and no file outside this folder was modified.
