## QUESTION

Are lean startup methods effective because they produce faster learning, or are they better suited to certain types of markets? Restated as an effect: where does the variance in a ground-visible outcome under lean startup use live, in the loop's speed or in the habitat that answers it? Option A prescribes accelerating the loop everywhere. Option B prescribes checking the market first. Each leads to a different intervention and a different diagnosis. R = |Γ(b,a) - o'| is indexed per venture and per time and never aggregated (Fig 1 :19). "Effective" is a threshold comparison, not a symmetric join (Fig 12 :112).

## DOMAIN CENSUS

R by arity, symmetry and scale, then one line per domain with actors / currency / anchor, partition or union, and order by dependency.

- **R.** Arity: at least three, a loop, a market ground, and a comparison against a non-lean alternative. Symmetry: ordered (build then measure then learn) and comparative, not symmetric. Scale: one venture loop; repeated loops as a cycle; a population of ventures ranked by a market; a habitat that updates underneath them.
- **D1 The venture learning loop.** Actors: founders, hypotheses Γ, MVP, experiments, pivots. Currency: residual reduction per slot, validated learning. Anchor: the observation o' from the market. Admitted.
- **D2 The market habitat.** Actors: customers, buyers, competitors, capital, regulators. Currency: payment, adoption, survival. Anchor: customer choice, named as an anchor in Fig 2 :29. Admitted.
- **D3 The method as generator.** Actors: build-measure-learn, MVP, pivot (¬), hypothesis. Currency: reach and cost of a term |φ|. Anchor: settlement against the ground. Admitted.
- **D4 The instrumental channel.** Actors: interviews, funnels, cohorts, metrics, prototypes. Currency: contact. Anchor: the ground, which does not decay while the channel does. Admitted.
- **X1 "Faster learning" as an intrinsic property of the method.** Actors: the loop's own outputs. Currency: experiments run, terms minted. Anchor: none nameable, because the loop scores itself with what it produces. **Not a domain yet.**
- **X2 "Market type" as a fixed list of good markets.** Absorbed into D2. Its valence is relative to the residual, so no standing list survives a change of residual.

**Union, not partition.** A and B are overlapping conditions. Fig 9 :55-57. The compound is a loop that maintains contact (bracket fidelity) and a ground that supplies pressure plus a differing closure. One lever clears only the region where one condition holds. **Order by dependency:** perception to vocabulary to objective to planning to learn and carry (Fig 3). Refuse before mint (Formula step 2). Describe the residual before naming a frame (Fig 9 :27-28).

Strain test: Fig 11 :40 governs an agent-only capability improvement; Fig 6 :32 governs search inside a closure; Fig 2 :29 governs what counts as an anchor. Different quantities, so no strain is needed to hold them together.

## VERDICT (from the figures)

The forced either/or is refused. Lean startup methods are effective when a build-measure-learn loop maintains contact with a ground that pays and pressures **and** when that ground supplies a term the team did not hold; faster learning is necessary as the contact mechanism and insufficient as the witness, and "market type" names the right location of the anchor but not a fixed taxonomy. A loop scored by its own output is frame-internal (Fig 1 :27). An agent-only improvement that does not change contact changes nothing (Fig 11 :40). Faster search in a closed set adds no atom (Fig 6 :32). The two conditions form a union compound (Fig 9 :57). Which market attributes satisfy them is a declared `gap`. Mode `specified`, ungrounded, candidate.

- **cost if wrong:** bounded and locatable. The packet states mode, three checkable sub-claims, refuters and provenance before the conclusion, so an error is findable rather than invisible. The real cost is spending an outside reader's independence by sending a conclusion instead of a generator. Fig 2 :42 prices that: *"Buy independence in proportion to the cost of being wrong."* A second cost is prescribing a single lever for a union compound, which clears only the region where one condition holds. Both are avoidable by leading with the shape and its five refuters.
- **undoable:** yes. No term is deleted if the claim fails; a refutation is added and the reach falls because *"something was added rather than because anything was deleted"* (Fig 6 :42-43). The shape and its falsifiers are recorded in a provenance trail so a later reader can locate the error (Fig 10 :62-64). A sent claim can be superseded by a corrected packet.
- **reversed by:** a ground reading, not agreement. A measure of ground pressure independent of the outcome it explains, a contact discriminator that separates real customer response from friendly respondents at equal loop counts, a pivot that refuses no held term yet moves outcomes identically, or one intervention restoring both conditions with no categorically different residue would each break a stated condition. A source with a genuinely different evidence pool showing that venture outcomes are fully predicted by averaged loop speed with ground pressure held fixed would also reverse it. Until one of these fires, the verdict stands as `candidate`.
