# The Tether's answer: lean startup effectiveness

**Mode: `specified` - not `grounded`.** This is a shape and a route, not a result. It is a simulated derivation checked against the figures (`05_what_shape_means.md` §6), not a ground reading.

## 1. The residual it answers

The question offers two causes for the same effect: faster learning inside the venture, or suitability to certain markets. Stated as an effect, R = |Γ(b,a) - o'| per venture and per time, measured against a ground-visible outcome rather than a frame-internal count (Fig 1 :19, :27). The two offered causes are not alternatives to be chosen. Fig 9 :55: *"A bundled problem is usually a union of overlapping conditions, not a list of separate ones."* They are a compound with its own loop.

## 2. The shape

1. **A loop's own output cannot witness effectiveness.** Fig 1 :27: *"They are frame-internal. A frame cannot score itself with a quantity it also produces."* Fig 5 :48: *"Its own output is never a measure of success. A system can be enthusiastically curious and produce nothing."* Experiments run and cycles completed are budget filters (Fig 9 :60), never verdict witnesses.

2. **Capability is agent-and-habitat.** Fig 11 :40: *"A system cannot be made more capable by improving the agent in isolation. An improvement that does not change contact changes nothing, however much it improves."* Faster iteration matters only where it changes what the ground returns.

3. **The market is the anchor.** Fig 2 :29: *"An anchor is whatever settles the question and does not change its answer because of what any frame thinks. A measurement, an experiment, a counter, a market, a patient outcome."* Internal agreement, however fast it forms, is a mirror (Fig 2 :35).

4. **Faster search inside a closure adds no atom.** Fig 6 :32: *"Closure is idempotent. Composing inside a closed set never leaves it, so no search, however long, adds an atom."* Reach renews only on contact with a differing closure (Fig 6 :23). So the market's second job is to supply what the team did not hold.

5. **The loop must be a cycle and must be able to refuse.** Fig 12 :122: *"cycle. The output re-enters as input. The sign of the loop decides whether it closes, and a chain cannot close by construction."* Operators :58: *"a refutation is a negation, which is why a vocabulary without it can only accumulate"*. A pivot that refuses nothing is accumulation, not learning.

6. **The two conditions are a union, not a mixture.** C1 contact maintained (bracket fidelity, ground pressure, a channel that is not saturated) and C2 reach renewed (a differing closure supplies a new atom). Fig 7 :37-39: *"Progress per handoff has two factors. Zero either and the chain runs in place:"*, *"the bracket’s fidelity across the handoff"*, *"the ground’s pressure on the room"*. Fig 9 :57: *"a case in two conditions is not a harder case of either."* One lever clears only the region where one condition holds.

7. **No sector list is returned.** Fig 9 :32: *"you get a kind of thing, never the answer itself"*. Which market attributes satisfy C1 and C2 is a declared `gap` (01 F8). Harms, Marinakis and Walsh (2015) and Zhuge et al. (2023) supply candidate attributes; they are world bindings, not figure returns.

**Answer in one line:** lean startup methods are effective when the build-measure-learn loop maintains contact with a ground that pays and pressures **and** when that ground supplies a term the team did not already hold; faster learning is necessary as the contact mechanism and insufficient as the witness, and "market type" names the right anchor but not a fixed taxonomy.

## 3. What it explicitly does not say

- Not that faster learning is irrelevant. It is necessary for C1; it is the wrong score for effectiveness.
- Not that lean startup is universally effective, nor universally ineffective.
- Not that a fixed list of markets decides the answer once and for all (Fig 7 :6, *"the limit: no last room"*).
- Not that market uncertainty, technology uncertainty, buyer count or resource slack are figure-derived market attributes. They are world-source bindings with no source gate against the figures.
- Not a derivation of customer behaviour from the six-term substrate.
- Not `grounded`. Nothing here is asserted as settled by an experiment.

## 4. What would settle it - the ground

A source-discriminating instrument, not a further argument. From *The Hard Problem is About Source*: an instrument that reads green *"cannot comment on the source"*; the spectrometer resolved it. Here:

- **Ground pressure instrument:** a measure of the market's willingness to pay and of how quickly a bet is answered, independent of the venture's own loop metrics. Must not be inferred from the outcome it is supposed to explain.
- **Contact discriminator:** a measure that distinguishes a bet ranged against real customers from a bet ranged against friendly or internal respondents, where loop count is held equal.
- **Refusal test:** a documented pivot that refuses a held hypothesis, versus one that accumulates a feature while keeping the hypothesis. If both move outcomes equally, the negation requirement fails.
- **Union test:** a 2x2 crossing contact maintained / not with reach renewed / not. If one lever restores both conditions and leaves no categorically different residue, the union claim fails.
- **Standing falsifiers from `01` §3:** the five numbered conditions, all pre-registered before this answer.

## 5. The honest stop

**Unreached, not unreachable.** The market attributes that satisfy C1 and C2 are not held in Γ after a genuine census. Fig 13 :134: *"Unreachable is a verdict here, not a silence,"* and Fig 8 :39: *"So a search that finds nothing is never a proof of absence."* The answer stops at the limit of the available instruments and records the lineage so the stop is checkable. *"Indiscernibility is not identity."*

## 6. One-paragraph version for outreach

> The Tether does not choose between "faster learning" and "certain markets." It says the two are a union compound. A build-measure-learn loop can only count as learning when its output re-enters as the next bet and when a pivot can refuse a held hypothesis; a loop that merely runs faster is scoring itself with its own output, which the figures disqualify as a witness. Capability is a property of agent and habitat, so an agent-only speed improvement changes nothing unless it changes contact with a paying ground. The market is the anchor, and its second job is to supply a term the team did not already hold, because faster search inside the same closure adds no new atom. So effectiveness needs both a maintained channel and a renewed reach, and "market type" names where that can happen rather than a fixed sector list. What is offered is the shape, five refuters written in advance, and the instruments that would close it. Which attributes of a market satisfy the two conditions stays open until a ground settles it.
