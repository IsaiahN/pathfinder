# Expert verification packet - the Tether on lean startup effectiveness

**What this is:** a claim in a form an entrepreneurship researcher can check. Mode, derivation and refuters are stated before the conclusion, so a reader knows what to look for and what would settle it.

**Mode: `specified` - not `grounded`.** No experiment in this folder is claimed. This is a shape and a route from a simulated derivation checked for internal consistency against the figures, not a ground reading.

## 1. The claim, in one paragraph

Lean startup effectiveness is a union compound, not a choice between two causes. A build-measure-learn loop is necessary only when its measurement re-enters as the next bet and a pivot can refuse a held hypothesis; counting cycles or hypotheses is a frame-internal score and cannot witness effectiveness. Capability belongs to agent and habitat, so a speed improvement that does not change contact with a paying ground changes nothing the question asks about. The market is the anchor and, as a frame whose closure differs, it also supplies a term the team did not hold, because faster search inside a closed set adds no atom. Effectiveness therefore needs both a maintained channel to the ground and a renewed reach, and a market type is the name of where that can happen, not a fixed taxonomy. Which market attributes satisfy the two conditions is a declared gap, not a hidden answer.

## 2. Three checkable sub-claims

### C1 - framework-internal; checkable by reading Figures 1, 5 and 9

- **Statement:** a loop's own output is not the ground metric for "effective"; it is a budget filter.
- **Source, verbatim:** Fig 1 :27 - *"They are frame-internal. A frame cannot score itself with a quantity it also produces."* Fig 1 :23 - *"The ground enters as the only metric."* Fig 5 :48 - *"Its own output is never a measure of success. - **Refuter:** show the framework admitting experiments-run or cycles-completed as the ground metric for venture effectiveness; or a controlled case where loop count tracks a ground-visible outcome with the market held fixed.

### C2 - framework-internal plus entrepreneurship-facing; checkable by reading Figures 11, 2 and 6

- **Statement:** an agent-only learning-speed improvement changes nothing unless contact changes; the market settles effectiveness and supplies new atoms.
- **Source, verbatim:** Fig 11 :40 - *"A system cannot be made more capable by improving the agent in isolation. An improvement that does not change contact changes nothing, however much it improves."* Fig 2 :29 - *"An anchor is whatever settles the question and does not change its answer because of what any frame thinks. A measurement, an experiment, a counter, a market, a patient outcome."* Fig 6 :32 - *"Closure is idempotent. Composing inside a closed set never leaves it, so no search, however long, adds an atom. - **What to check as a researcher:** is there a field measure of ground pressure independent of the outcome it is supposed to explain? If not, the claim is a shape, not a finding, and should be treated as such.
- **Refuter:** faster internal iteration improves a ground-visible outcome while a habitat substitution removes ground pressure and the outcome is unchanged; or reach renewed by internal composition alone with no new atom from a differing closure.

### C3 - framework-internal derivation; checkable by reading Figures 12, 7 and 9

- **Statement:** the loop must be a cycle with a refusal operator, and the compound needs both contact and reach, so a single lever leaves the intersection standing.
- **Source, verbatim:** Fig 12 :122 - *"cycle. The output re-enters as input. The sign of the loop decides whether it closes, and a chain cannot close by construction."* Operators table :58 - *"a refutation is a negation, which is why a vocabulary without it can only accumulate"*. - **Refuter:** a lean loop closes without its output re-entering as input; or a pivot that refuses no held term moves outcomes identically; or one intervention restores both conditions with no categorically different residue.

## 5. What would settle it

A source-discriminating instrument. From *The Hard Problem is About Source*: an instrument that reads green *"cannot comment on the source"*; the spectrometer resolved it, not an argument.

1. **Ground pressure instrument:** willingness to pay and speed of answer, measured independently of the venture's loop metrics and of the outcome being explained.
2. **Contact discriminator:** distinguishes bets ranged against real customers from bets ranged against friendly respondents, with loop count held equal.
3. **Refusal test:** compares a pivot that refuses a held hypothesis with one that merely accumulates a feature.
4. **Union test:** the 2x2 of contact maintained or not against reach renewed or not, with a check for a categorically different residue in the intersection.
