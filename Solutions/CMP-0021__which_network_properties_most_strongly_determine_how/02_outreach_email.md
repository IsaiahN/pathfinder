# Outreach draft: check the output first, then decide about the theory

**Use:** one-to-one, or as a cover letter attached to a submission through the public venues in `03_public_contact_points.csv`. **Not** for bulk send.

**Hard precondition:** do not send until the three checkable sub-claims in `08_expert_verification_packet.md` survive a reader's own check. If the stage-1 output does not survive, there is nothing else to look at.

---

**Subject:** One claim about spreading on networks that you could refute in twenty minutes

> Hi [name],
>
> I am working on a figure-grounded derivation of a question in network science, and I have one specific output I would like you to check against what you already know. I am not asking you to read the framework first, and I am not asking you to agree with it. If the output does not survive, there is nothing else to look at.
>
> **The question (in effect terms):** given a process spreading over a network, which structural property most strongly determines how fast or how widely it spreads: degree distribution, clustering, community structure, or something else? I write the residual per network/process/seed case, never as an average across cases, because Fig 1 :19 says "The residual is indexed by object slot, not aggregated."
>
> **The output (`specified` for the shape, `gap` for the ranking):** the figures give the *shape* of an answer but not the ranking the question asks for. They say that a spread is influence travelling over a network structure, that it needs a spendable difference and a finite resource to travel on, that degree distribution, clustering and community structure are overlapping conditions rather than rival options, and that which property matters changes with the residual. No figure line ranks the three named properties, so I report that ranking as a `gap`, not as a hidden answer. What I offer is the shape any answer must have and a list of what would settle the ranking at the ground.
>
> **What I am explicitly not claiming:** that degree distribution always wins, that hubs are always the answer, that clustering is irrelevant, or that the framework has settled an empirical debate. "Something else" is at minimum the process dynamics and the seed policy, which are on the same footing as topology here. A `gap` is a verdict, not a silence, and not an invitation to put the most familiar property in the slot.
>
> **What would refute the shape claim (written before this message):**
> 1. a controlled study in which one property dominates both speed and reach across process types, seed policies, network sizes and spatial embeddings, with no residual left for the others;
> 2. a documented process that spreads with no spendable difference and no finite resource;
> 3. a demonstration that the three properties form a disjoint partition, so one lever clears the intersections;
> 4. a figure line I have overlooked that ranks the three properties directly.
>
> If any of these holds, I withdraw or revise the shape. A one-line "this fails because..." is more useful to me than agreement. If you would rather not engage, no reply is needed.
>
> **If it survives, and only then:** the sources are below. The figures are the authority, not my summary of them. Stage 2 is entirely opt-in; I will not send the framework to someone who has not asked for it.
> - The figures and formula, public repo: https://github.com/IsaiahN/tether/tree/general-agent/figures
> - Arrival of the Fittest: The Tether Architecture: https://adventuresinml.substack.com/p/arrival-of-the-fittest-the-tether
> - How Intelligence Actually Works: https://adventuresinml.substack.com/p/how-intelligence-actually-works
>
> **If you want to reuse the machinery:** the formula is in the figures link. Run it on a problem you know well and can check against a ground. Write down what would refute your result *before* you run it, and state the mode of what you get. The architecture's own demo note warns that LLM outputs are simulacra unless derived from the real architecture or the formula worked by hand. A reported failure is more useful to me than a reported success.
>
> Best,
> [name]

## Why this version is ordered this way

| the move | figure-grounded reason |
|---|---|
| Residual and refuter before theory | Fig 10 :52 "Bring a characterised residual, never the source." Fig 2 :45 "Frames range against the ground. They do not negotiate with it." |
| Verdict recorded before any theory is shown | A reader who has absorbed the framework before giving a verdict has joined the frame being checked; their later agreement is never pooled as corroboration (Fig 2 :40). |
| Do not ask for agreement or trust | Fig 2 :35 "Alignment is triangulation, not negotiation. A mirror cannot correct you." |
| Pre-registered refuter | Seat material: a falsifier written before the run is a design instrument, not only an honesty device. |
| Mode stated on the claim | `THE_FORMULA.md`: "Mode. general · specified · grounded. State which." |
| The ranking is withheld as a gap, not filled | Fig 9 :32 "you get a kind of thing, never the answer itself"; Fig 13 :134 "Unreachable is a verdict here, not a silence". |
| Union named before properties compared | Fig 9 :55 "A bundled problem is usually a union of overlapping conditions, not a list of separate ones." |
| One-to-one, not bulk | Fig 2 :31 independence is how little frames draw on the same evidence; selecting for receptiveness manufactures the disqualifying correlation. |
| No prestige framing | Recursive Self-Improvement Trap: "Prestige can fund an action. It can give an action a budget to be tried. But it cannot settle an action. Only the ground can settle it." |
| Offer the formula as a method, not an answer | Fig 4 :22 "upward: a method that can be reapplied. Never the recording of a particular success." |
| Ask users to write their own ground and refuter | `THE_FORMULA.md` "Direction": a frame cannot check the direction of its own question; the sought-for shape must be made an ordinary term. |

## What is deliberately absent from the email

- No request for belief, endorsement, or a show of hands.
- No claim that the framework is the only one, or that it has settled the question.
- No personal data about the recipient and no evidence about their likely agreement.
- No links in stage 1. The three links appear only after the verdict on the shape claim is on record.
