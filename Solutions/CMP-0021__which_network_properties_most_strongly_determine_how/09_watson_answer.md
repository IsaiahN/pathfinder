## VERDICT (from the figures)

The shape survives; the ranking is withheld. Spreading is influence travel over a network structure (Fig 13 :24) and is a flow requiring a spendable difference and a finite resource (Fig 13 :22, :76), so the process type and seed policy belong in any answer. Degree distribution, clustering and community structure are overlapping conditions of one arrangement (Fig 9 :55-56), and their valence is relative to the residual (Fig 11 :49), so the comparison must be per network/process/seed slot (Fig 1 :19, :22) and never averaged. As arrangements of the same atom set they are isomer-like (Fig 12 :117, :94): the difference is invisible in an ingredient-only formula and decisive in behaviour. No figure line ranks them, so that ranking is a declared `gap`. The world literature corroborates the process-conditionality and conflicts with any universal single-winner answer. Mode `specified` for the shape, `general` for the substrate rule, `gap` for the ranking, nothing `grounded`.

- **cost if wrong:** bounded and locatable. The deliverable is a shape with four refuters, not a winner, so an error is findable rather than invisible. The real cost is spending an outside reader's independence by sending a conclusion or a citation list before the refuter (Fig 10 :42 "A verifier that can reconstruct the claim cannot verify it"), and that cost is avoidable by leading with stage 1. A second cost is medical or policy misdirection if a single property is prescribed for an intervention where the union needs two different levers.
- **undoable:** yes. No term is deleted if the claim fails; a refutation is added and the reach falls because something was added, per Fig 6 :42-43. The shape and its refuters are recorded with provenance in `01_figure_grounded_audit.md`, so a later reader can locate the error (Fig 10 :62-64). A sent packet can be superseded by a corrected one.
- **reversed by:** a ground reading, not agreement. Specifically: (1) one property dominating speed and reach across all process types, seed policies, sizes and spatial embeddings; (2) a process spreading with no spendable difference and no finite resource; (3) the three properties shown to be a disjoint partition so one lever clears the intersections; (4) a figure line found on re-census that ranks them directly; (5) "network" derived from the other five substrate terms, or influence travel shown not to be its defining content. A source-discriminating comparator that fixes process and quantity and varies one property at a time would close the `gap` at the ground. Until one of these fires, the verdict stands as `candidate`.
