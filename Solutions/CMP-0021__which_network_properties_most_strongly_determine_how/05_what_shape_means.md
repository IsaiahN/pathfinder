## 1. A shape is a constraint, not a filler

The canonical README states the limit: the framework returns *"what a missing thing would have to look like (a shape, not a name)."* Fig 9 :32 "you get a kind of thing, never the answer itself." Fig 13 :134 "Unreachable is a verdict here, not a silence".

From *The Library of Babel*: *"You can cut your search short if the set of books returned is small and descriptive enough that it provides a general shape of the answer that satisfies your question."*

For CMP-0021, a **shape** is the form an answer must have before we can name the winning property: a union of structure and process, compared per network/process/seed slot, with property valence relative to the residual. It constrains. It does not fill the slot marked `???`.

`??? = property P such that per-slot spread residual shrinks across process types, with P's standing recorded as relative to the residual`.

That is a shape. "Degree distribution is the answer" is a name.

## 2. How the shape is obtained

- *Library of Babel*: use the vocabulary already held that is *closest* to the thing, then look for recurring new terms that overlap or correlate with it, and recurse. *"With each new term gained, you narrow the space and deepen your vocabulary."*
- Fig 6 :30 "The description travels. Fails by being sharpened into terms only we can read. Cast it in effect terms." Fig 9 :30-31 "your words are borrowed too, so describe the effect rather than the cause".
- *Decoding Tacit Knowledge*: *"When contact isn't possible, the best you can send is a plain description of what you'd expect to see, because anyone can check that against the world."*
- *Library of Babel* also warns that a stubborn question may be *"a bundle or conflation of several problems masquerading as one"*. This is exactly CMP-0021's condition: the three named properties are a union, not a partition (Fig 9 :55).

Concretely here: we began from the effect description ("influence travels over a network; a spread is a flow that needs a spendable difference"), then read the figure lines, then opened network-science sources. The shape was stated before the sources were read, so the world could corroborate or conflict but not choose the frame (Fig 9 :27-28).

## 3. What licenses a name or a ranking

Only two things, both outside the shape itself:

1. **The ground settles it.** `THE_FORMULA.md` step 5: "the ground settles it. Verified only by what pays. A gate is not the ground." For CMP-0021 that means a controlled study with fixed process, fixed quantity (speed versus reach), fixed seed policy, and per-slot residuals. Not a list of citations.
2. **A provenanced import from a frame whose closure differs** (Fig 6 :23, Fig 8 :50-52), with a source gate and a shadow test. A binding from network science onto a held atom (for example, "gradient" onto a susceptible/infected difference) is a binding, not a new primitive, and must be recorded as such.

*They're Scanning Books* sharpens the second point: *"A minimum viable set of six terms is a human's careful guess at reality's compression axes; another sensorium's minimum set need not be those six."* And: *"Declaring the primitives does not make them neutral. It makes the choice visible."* Treating degree, clustering and community as nature's own list would repeat the error the essay names.

## 4. The source problem for this question

*The Hard Problem is About Source* gives the mechanism that matters most here. Metamerism: an instrument reads green and *"cannot comment on the source"*. One green light, or yellow plus blue, or RGB, or CMYK, or a scheme not yet discovered, can produce the same reading. *"If several paths create the same behavior, then behavior is a projection with lossy information."* And: *"An instrument that can't distinguish these cases demonstrates its own inadequacy."* The spectrometer, not an argument, resolved it.

A spread curve is exactly such a projection. A fast, wide epidemic can be produced by hubs, by short path lengths from clustering, by weak community boundaries, by a spatial embedding, or by a process with a long infectious period. The observed curve does not name its source. Naming degree distribution as "the" cause from the curve alone is the metamerism error. What is owed is a source-discriminating instrument (a controlled intervention that varies one property while holding the process fixed), not a more confident name.

*How Intelligence Actually Works* adds: *"indiscernibility is not identity."* Two networks whose spread curves are indiscernible under current instruments are not thereby the same network. The lineage, not the curve, is the evidence.

## 5. Q: What does "shape" mean for CMP-0021?

**Answer:** the shape is the narrowed form of the answer we can state before naming a winner. It has four constraints:

1. spreading is influence travel over a network structure (Fig 13 :23-24);
2. the travel is a flow requiring a spendable difference and a finite resource (Fig 13 :14, :22, :76);
3. the named properties form a union of overlapping conditions (Fig 9 :54-57), and the residual is per slot (Fig 1 :19);
4. the valence of any property is relative to the residual (Fig 11 :49, :51).

The specific ranking is **not** part of the shape. It is a `gap`. No figure line governs it, and the world sources conflict with any single-winner reading (degree dominates in one model, geography beats hubs in another). "Unreachable is a verdict, not a silence" (Fig 13 :134).

## 6. Correction to my own §3 and §4

I initially wrote that the README's *"shape, not a name"* rule forbids naming any property as important. That overstates it. The README's phrase governs a **missing** primitive, one not held in Γ. Degree distribution, clustering and community structure are not missing primitives of the six-term set; they are derived descriptions of the network structure, held in the network-science frame. What is forbidden is:

- inventing a seventh substrate member to soak up the ranking;
- claiming the ranking is derived from the six when it is not;
- naming a winner from a projection without a source-discriminating instrument.

The metamerism point in §4 is **not retracted**. It still says the spread curve cannot name its own cause. But the reason is the missing discriminator, not a ban on naming held properties. Both readings are recorded so neither is quietly lost. This is the same narrowing the exemplar made in its `05` §5b and `06` Q1.

## 7. Two consequences for this question

**Prestige is not action.** From *The Recursive Self-Improvement Trap*: *"Prestige must never be allowed to validate an action directly... Prestige can fund an action. It can give an action a budget to be tried. But it cannot settle an action. Only the ground can settle it."* A list of famous network scientists who agree with "hubs matter" is the named collapse. The legitimate ask is a controlled check that could refuse the shape.

**My own audit is a simulation, not a ground reading.** Same essay: *"LLMs can quickly simulate an approximate answer/output with the formula... However, the truly testable output would need to be derived from the real architecture or working the formula yourself."* *"The answer is only as good as the validator... I put the theory out there... and this is simulacra."* So `01_figure_grounded_audit.md` is simulated, checked for internal consistency against the figures. It cannot settle CMP-0021. *How a Society of Agents Would Work* adds the operational form of the same limit: *"Use the success rate per try, not the total. Whatever gets tried most piles up votes"*, and quotes Fig 1 :22 against averaging. A count of agreeing papers is the denominator, not the verdict.
