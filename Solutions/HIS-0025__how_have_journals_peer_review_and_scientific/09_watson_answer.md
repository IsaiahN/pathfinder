## QUESTION

Decide, in effect terms, what the three institutions changed about the path a claim takes to a community (circulation) and the test it undergoes before being relied on (evaluation). Option A: they were neutral conduits that transmitted and filtered claims better, so the effect is volumetric. Option B: they relocated seats (anchor, maker, verifier, outside frame) and a membrane, so the effect is structural and has specific failure modes. What each leads to: A predicts more claims, faster, with the same checking relation and no new failure class; B predicts independent outside frames, a directed filter, a kept channel to the ground, a habitat of unauthored peers, and named collapses when those conditions fail. The sought-for shape is bound as "change in seat geometry", written down before the run so it can be refused.

## DOMAIN CENSUS

- **R (the residual).** The gap between what a claim predicts about itself when it leaves its maker and what a community can rely on when it arrives. Arity: two effects (circulation, evaluation) across three institutions, a union compound (Fig 9 :55-56), not one hard question. Symmetry: circulation is directed, a filter with a direction (Fig 4 :21); evaluation is triangulation, not symmetric agreement (Fig 2 :35). Scale: one claim at first order, an institution persisting across claims at second order.
- **Domain 1, evaluation.** Actors: author as maker, reviewers as outside frames, editor as ground maintainer, experiment as ground. Currency: verdicts and residuals. Anchor: replication and experiment, nameable. Question type: partition of who may hold which information. Verdict: triangulated.
- **Domain 2, circulation.** Actors: manuscripts, editors, readers, indexes. Currency: claims, citations, attention, methods. Anchor: the ground via the channel that stays reachable, and the reach, what becomes composable. Question type: a directed membrane plus an import gate. Verdict: single-source for the membrane, with a second figure for the import half.
- **Domain 3, community.** Actors: peers, societies, meetings, journals as rooms. Currency: agreement, novelty, selection. Anchor: the ground, which votes between rooms. Question type: habitat enumeration, not composition. Verdict: triangulated.
- **Union not partition.** A journal is both membrane and certifier; a society both supplies reviewers and hosts rooms. Unbundle before searching (Fig 9 :54).
- **Order by dependency.** Evaluation, then circulation, then habitat, following Fig 3 :20. The lever sits at link 3, "what would count as progress", where instruments are fewest (Fig 3 :25-27). Effort at link 5 cannot rescue a broken link 3 (Fig 3 :19).

## VERDICT (from the figures)

The shape survives; the chronology is a `gap`. Evaluation is a checking arrangement whose validity depends on independence, not agreement (Fig 2 :35, Fig 2 :40). Circulation is a directed membrane with a second duty, keeping the channel to the ground reachable (Fig 4 :21, Fig 10 :24); records are not methods (Fig 4 :25). Societies supply an unauthored habitat of outside frames (Fig 11 :43, Fig 10 :73-74). The dependency order is evaluation, then circulation, then habitat (Fig 3 :19-20). Volume measures are filters, never witnesses (Fig 9 :60). The three verdicts are kept separate and never averaged. This is `general`/`specified`, ungrounded, candidate.

- **cost if wrong:** bounded and locatable. The packet states mode, refuters and provenance before the conclusion, so an error is findable rather than invisible. The real cost is spending the independence of outside readers by sending a chronology before the shape, which Fig 2 :42 prices: "Buy independence in proportion to the cost of being wrong." That cost is avoidable by leading with the shape and its refuter.
- **undoable:** yes. The historical binding is removable without touching the figure derivation; `07_the_tether_answer.md` and `08_expert_verification_packet.md` already remove it. A sent claim can be superseded by the corrected packet, and no seat or primitive has to be withdrawn.
- **reversed by:** a ground reading, not agreement. An archival reading of the 1752 and 1832 Royal Society changes and the 1973 *Nature* reform that reverses the order E -> C -> S would break the shape; a citation-network result in which reviewer-pool independence does not predict later corrections better than prestige would break F2; a citation chain where evidence compounded with no new frame would break F5; a verbatim figure line governing a dated transition would withdraw the `gap`. Until one of these fires, the verdict stands as `candidate`.
