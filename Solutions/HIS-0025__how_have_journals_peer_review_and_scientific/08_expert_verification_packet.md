# Expert verification packet: the Tether on journals, peer review and societies

**What this is:** a claim in a form a historian of science, bibliometrician, or editor can check. Mode, derivation and refuters come before the conclusion so the reader knows what to look for and what would settle it.

**Mode: `general` on the shape, `specified` on the historical binding, `gap` on the chronology. Nothing `grounded`.** Simulated derivation checked for internal consistency against the figures, not a ground reading.

## 1. The claim, in one paragraph

Journals, peer review, and scientific societies did not mainly change the volume of claims; they changed the geometry of verification. Peer review placed independent outside frames between a maker and a verdict, so the load-bearing quantity is independence rather than agreement. A journal is a directed membrane and a kept channel to the ground, not the ground itself; only methods, not records of one occasion, grow what a community can compose over. Societies supply the habitat from which independent frames are drawn, but a pool the society authors for itself is a room rather than a habitat. The dependency order is evaluation, then circulation, then habitat. The figures do not give dates; the chronology is a `gap` that requires archival ground reading.

## 2. Three checkable sub-claims

### C1 : framework-internal and checkable by reading Figures 2, 9 and 10

- **Statement:** evaluation is triangulation, not negotiation. A verifier must not be able to reconstruct the claim, and evidence bearing only on the maker is inadmissible.
- **Source, verbatim:** Fig 2 :35 *"Alignment is triangulation, not negotiation. A mirror cannot correct you."* Fig 9 :51 *"evidence bearing only on its maker is a stake-inducing channel, and the verifier is denied it"*. Fig 10 :42 *"A verifier that can reconstruct the claim cannot verify it."*
- **Refuter:** a documented historical case where evaluation became more reliable while maker and verifier shared the same evidence pool and no independent outside frame was introduced; or a case where a journal's prestige reliably settled a claim and its keeper was not disqualified (Fig 10 :93).

### C2 : framework-internal and checkable by reading Figures 4 and 10

- **Statement:** circulation is a directed membrane with a second function, keeping the channel to the ground reachable. Volume of records is not reach.
- **Source, verbatim:** Fig 4 :21 *"The membrane is the boundary between the sizes, and the rule about what may cross it. Not a wall: a filter with a direction."* Fig 4 :25 *"A recording carried upward looks like knowledge and is a description of one occasion."* Fig 10 :24 *"the ground does not decay, the channel does"*.
- **What to check:** can you name a case where the volume of published claims grew and the community's reach grew with it, with no method crossing and no import? The world source *Science Citation Index Expanded* describes a *"rigorous selection process"* and a *"seal of quality"*, which is a filter with a direction, not a conduit.
- **Refuter:** such a case would break this sub-claim. It is the second pre-registered refuter in `01` section 4.

### C3 : framework-internal and checkable by reading Figures 11 and 7

- **Statement:** societies supply a habitat of independent outside frames; the pool must be unauthored and the next room must differ from the last.
- **Source, verbatim:** Fig 11 :43 *"The habitat is enumerated, never composed."* Fig 7 :35 *"A room's agreement is variation, not a verdict. Selection between rooms is where the ground votes."* Fig 10 :73-74 *"A candidate set chosen by a participant is an authored room however well the selection is reasoned, so the pool must be unauthored first and examined second."*
- **What to check:** does the historical record show reviewer pools assembled by a mechanism (lottery, open qualification, a set fixed before the claim) rather than hand-selected by the author or editor? The world source *Learned society* records that societies hold *"regular conferences"* and sponsor journals, which is the habitat form.
- **Refuter:** a case where a society authored its reviewer pool and the verdicts stayed reliable against the ground over time.

## 3. What is explicitly not claimed

- Not a chronology. The figures give no dates, and none is asserted here.
- Not that any journal is the ground, nor that a disclaimer of responsibility is itself proof of reliability. It is evidence of an unoccupied anchor seat.
- Not that peer review is a vote, nor that consensus is a verdict. Fig 2 :35.
- Not that impact factor, citation count, or journal prestige settles a claim. Fig 9 :60 *"Use filters for the budget and witnesses for the verdict. Never let a filter hand you a verdict."*
- Not `grounded`. No archive, dataset, or instrument was opened for the historical claim.
- Not a causal claim of the form "X caused Y". The output is a shape with refuters, not a history.

## 5. What would settle it

A ground reading, not more agreement. Three concrete tests are in `07_the_tether_answer.md` section 4: an archive test of the 1752 and 1832 Royal Society changes and the 1973 *Nature* reform; a citation-network test of whether independence between reviewer pools predicts later correction events better than prestige; and a room-chain test of whether the next venue differed from the last and matched its residual. From *The Hard Problem is About Source*: *"It was proven wrong not by argument, but by the arrival of a better instrument."*

## 6. What an expert can check in twenty minutes

1. Read Fig 2 :35, Fig 4 :21 and :25, Fig 7 :35, Fig 10 :24 and :42, Fig 11 :43. Six short passages.
2. Check C1 against your own field: when a reviewer shares the author's evidence pool, is that a check or an echo? Name one case.
3. Check C2: do you know of a case where publication volume and reach rose together with no new method or import? If yes, send it; it is the refuter.
4. Check C3: how is a reviewer pool in your field assembled, and is it authored by a participant?
5. Name one instrument that would distinguish a membrane from an anchor in a journal, or say why none is possible.
6. Send the refutation. A failure is more useful than an endorsement. If you would rather not engage, no reply is needed.
