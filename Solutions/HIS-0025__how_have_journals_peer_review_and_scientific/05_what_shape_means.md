## 1. A shape is a constraint you can state before you can name the answer

From *The Library of Babel*: *"you can cut your search short if the set of books returned is small and descriptive enough that it provides a general shape of the answer that satisfies your question."* And the method: use the vocabulary you already have that is closest to the thing, then *"With each new term gained, you narrow the space and deepen your vocabulary."*

For HIS-0025 the shape is: **a directed membrane for circulation, a triangulation arrangement for evaluation, and a maintained habitat of independent frames.** That constrains any historical answer without pretending to be the chronology. The chronology is the name, and it is owed from archives.

The same essay names the union problem directly: *"Perhaps your problem was a bundle or conflation of several problems masquerading as one? You will need to search all of those problems individually."* That is the warrant for unbundling circulation from evaluation in `01_figure_grounded_audit.md` section 1.

## 2. How the shape is obtained

- *Library of Babel*: proximity and recursion, not a leap to the answer. The searcher's ignorance is the obstacle, not the size of the library.
- Fig 9 :27-28: *"describe it before you go looking, or any frame you pick will seem to fit"*; Fig 9 :30-31: *"your words are borrowed too, so describe the effect rather than the cause."*
- *Decoding Tacit Knowledge*: *"When contact isn't possible, the best you can send is a plain description of what you'd expect to see, because anyone can check that against the world."* This is why the outreach email sends a shape and a refuter rather than the framework.
- *Arrival of the Fittest* records the stopping rule: by the time you have the exact terms, *"you have already composed the answer through the process of locating it."*

## 3. What licenses a name, and why this question cannot have one yet

## 4. Why agreement is not evidence here

*How Intelligence Actually Works* describes what happens when a room has no ground: *"if you are just going off of what is present in your heads, then eventually, the room will be swayed by negotiation which is persuasion, or by coherent drift"*, and *"That is where RLVR steps in... The ground must be immovable by persuasion and not bi-directional like the frames are."*

*Decoding Tacit Knowledge* states the same law for people: *"High contact builds exactly the shared understanding that makes communication work, but that same factor makes the learner's agreement with the teacher worthless as a check."* This is why a list of receptive historians cannot corroborate the answer, and why `03_public_contact_points.csv` contains no personal emails or receptiveness rank.

*How a Society of Agents Would Work* adds the operational form: *"A group is only worth having while its members stay different"* and *"Votes come from outcomes, not popularity."* Citation counts are popularity; replication is an outcome.

## 5. Prestige is not action

From *The Recursive Self-Improvement Trap*: *"Prestige can fund an action. It can give an action a budget to be tried. But it cannot settle an action. Only the ground can settle it."* A high-impact journal gives a claim a budget to be tried. It does not settle the claim. This governs F7 of `01` and the editorial policy in `04`.
