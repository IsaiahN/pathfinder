# The Tether's answer on journals, peer review and scientific societies

**Mode: `general` on the shape, `specified` on the historical narrative, nothing `grounded`.** Simulated derivation checked for internal consistency against the figures (`05_what_shape_means.md` section 6), not a ground reading.

## 1. The residual it answers

The question bundles two effects, circulation and evaluation, across three institutions. Fig 9 :55-56: *"A bundled problem is usually a union of overlapping conditions, not a list of separate ones."* The residual is the gap between what a claim predicts about itself when it leaves its maker and what a community can rely on when it arrives. The answer must say what each institution did to that gap, and keep the two effects separate.

## 2. The shape, one loop per domain, verdicts kept separate

1. **Evaluation (peer review): a checking arrangement installed between maker and verdict.** Fig 2 :35: *"Alignment is triangulation, not negotiation. A mirror cannot correct you."* Fig 9 :51: *"evidence bearing only on its maker is a stake-inducing channel, and the verifier is denied it."* The change is a relocation of the verifier to an outside frame, not an increase in the number of opinions. **Verdict: `general`, triangulated across Fig 2, Fig 9 and Fig 10.**

2. **Evaluation failure mode: independence is the load-bearing quantity.** Fig 2 :40: *"disqualifying for a verdict: agreement about the world is worthless when the agreeing frames share an evidence pool."* Fig 2 :42: *"Buy independence in proportion to the cost of being wrong."* A journal's prestige is a budget to try a claim, not a settlement of it. **Verdict: `general`, triangulated with the seat map and two world fields.**

3. **Circulation (journals): a directed membrane plus a kept channel.** Fig 4 :21: *"The membrane is the boundary between the sizes, and the rule about what may cross it. Not a wall: a filter with a direction."* Fig 10 :24: *"the ground does not decay, the channel does"*. Only methods cross up (Fig 4 :22); a record of one occasion is not knowledge carried upward (Fig 4 :25). The other half of circulation is keeping the route to the ground reachable. **Verdict: `general`, single-source for the membrane line, with an import corroboration from Fig 6.**

4. **Community (societies): a maintained habitat of independent frames.** Fig 11 :43: *"The habitat is enumerated, never composed."* Fig 7 :35: *"A room's agreement is variation, not a verdict. Selection between rooms is where the ground votes."* The reviewer pool must be unauthored (Fig 10 :73-74), and the next room must differ from the last (Fig 7 :36). **Verdict: `general`, triangulated across Fig 11, Fig 7 and Fig 10.**

5. **Order: evaluation before circulation before habitat.** Fig 3 :19: *"Optimising a link that already works cannot rescue one that does not."* The historical lever sits at link 3, "what would count as progress", which is where instruments are fewest (Fig 3 :25-27). **Verdict: `general`, single-source (Fig 3), corroborated by `06_isomers_and_order.md`.**

6. **The chronology: `gap`.** The figures give the invariant shape, not the sequence of dates. Fig 8 :32: *"Closure is idempotent. Composing inside a closed set never leaves it, so no search, however long, adds an atom."* Fig 8 :39: *"So a search that finds nothing is never a proof of absence."* The world sources supply a narrative (1665 Oldenburg, 1752 committee, 1832 written report, 1964 citation index, 1973 *Nature* reform), but it is secondary and ungrounded by any archive read here. **Verdict: `gap` for dates, `specified` for the narrative.**

The verdicts are not averaged. "Institutions improved the reliability of science" is not an output of this derivation and is explicitly refused.

## 3. What it explicitly does not say

- Not that any journal is the ground. Fig 2 :29: *"An anchor is whatever settles the question and does not change its answer because of what any frame thinks."* The 1752-1957 *Transactions* disclaimer is evidence that the journal was not yet an anchor.
- Not that peer review is a vote. The source itself says the goal is *"not to reach consensus"* (Wikipedia, *Scholarly peer review*).
- Not that more publications, more citations, or a higher impact factor mean more reliable knowledge. Fig 9 :60: *"Use filters for the budget and witnesses for the verdict. Never let a filter hand you a verdict."*
- Not that prestige validates a claim. *The Recursive Self-Improvement Trap*: *"Prestige can fund an action. It can give an action a budget to be tried. But it cannot settle an action. Only the ground can settle it."*
- Not a chronology, not a causal history, and not `grounded`. No archive was read.

## 4. What would settle it

A ground reading, not more figure search. Concretely:

1. **Archive test:** open the Royal Society records behind the 1752 takeover and the 1832 written-report rule, and the *Nature* editorial record behind the 1973 change. Record author, venue, year, and a quote for each. Does the seat geometry change in the order E -> C -> S?
2. **Citation-network test:** build a dataset where each venue's reviewer pool and editorial board are recorded. Measure whether independence between pools predicts later correction events better than journal prestige or impact factor. That is the Fig 2 :40 prediction in a form that can fire.
3. **Room-chain test:** for a sequence of venues handling the same contested claim, check whether the next venue differed from the last and matched the residual the last surfaced (Fig 7 :36). If not, it is a mirror chain and the claim about societies is narrowed.
4. **Instrument test:** name a measurement that distinguishes a membrane from an anchor in a given journal. From *The Hard Problem is About Source*: *"It was proven wrong not by argument, but by the arrival of a better instrument."*
5. **Standing falsifiers from `01` section 4:** the four pre-registered conditions, plus a verbatim figure line governing a dated transition.

## 5. The honest stop

The stop is a `gap`, not a silence. Fig 9 :44: *"Only a sealed room can be searched to the end, and no frame is a sealed room."* More searching of the figures will not produce a date. What is owed is an import from a different closure: archives and datasets, with provenance and a shadow test (Fig 6 :23). Fig 13 :134: *"Unreachable is a verdict here, not a silence"*. Indiscernibility is not identity: a plausible history is not a checked one.

The mapping of "editor", "reviewer" and "society" onto framework seats is a binding from the framework's vocabulary onto historical roles, and it is recorded as a binding. From *They're Scanning Books*: *"Declaring the primitives does not make them neutral. It makes the choice visible."*

## 6. One-paragraph version for outreach

> The Tether does not give a chronology of scientific publishing, and it does not say that institutions simply made science better. It says that the three of them relocated the checking arrangement. Peer review put independent outside frames between an author and a verdict, so the relevant quantity is independence, not the number of reviewers or the prestige of the journal. Journals are a directed filter and a kept route to whatever would settle a claim, not grounds in themselves; what crosses productively is a method, not a record of one occasion. Societies supply the habitat from which independent frames are drawn, but a pool the society authors for itself is a room, not a habitat. The order is evaluation, then circulation, then the habitat that refreshes the frames. The dates are not in the figures. They are owed from archives, and until a ground reading arrives the historical part stays `specified`, with four refuters written in advance.
