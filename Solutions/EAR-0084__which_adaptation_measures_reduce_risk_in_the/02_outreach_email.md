# Outreach draft : check the separator before deciding about the framework

**Use:** one-to-one, or as a cover letter attached to a submission through the public venues in `03_public_contact_points.csv`. **Not** for bulk send.

**Hard precondition:** do not send until the failure conditions in `01_figure_grounded_audit.md` section 5 have been read and understood. The first contact carries the residual and the refuter, never the framework's interiors (Fig 10 :52: "Bring a characterised residual, never the source").

---

**Subject:** One check on how to tell a durable adaptation from a new vulnerability

> Dear [name],
>
> I have one specific output I would like you to check against what you already know. I am not asking you to read the framework first, and I am not asking you to agree with anything. If the output does not survive, there is nothing else to look at.
>
> **The residual, in effect terms.** A measure is supposed to lower observed loss at a named place and hazard, against a reference that does not change its answer because the implementing agency reports success. The hard part is the second half of the question: the same measure can lower loss at one place and raise it downstream, in a neighbouring community, or in a later decade. So "which measures reduce risk and which create new vulnerabilities" is not two lists; it is one measure whose sign depends on the residual and the slot.
>
> **The output (`specified`, not `grounded`).** A separator. For each (measure, place, hazard, horizon) it returns one of three verdicts: durable reduction, new vulnerability, or indeterminate pending an instrument. A measure is called durable only if all eight conditions hold:
> 1. loss is indexed per place, not averaged;
> 2. the hazard is described in effect terms before a measure is chosen;
> 3. what crossed into the place was a method, not a recording of one past success;
> 4. the habitat, including downstream and future actors, was enumerated out to the cascade where the residual actually sits;
> 5. no other place or later time carries a newly positive residual;
> 6. benefit and burden are kept in separate columns, not summed into one net figure;
> 7. the plan can change when the hazard changes, and the verdict stays refusable;
> 8. the gap between modelled benefit and observed benefit is measured, not assumed zero.
>
> If any condition fails, the measure is classified as a new vulnerability, or as indeterminate when no instrument exists yet. This is a shape and a route, not a result. Nothing here is settled by measurement.
>
> **What I am not claiming:** that I can name the measures that pass. The derivation returns a shape, not a name. Naming a particular measure is a binding from the climate field onto that shape, and it needs its own provenance and its own ground reading. I am also not claiming a universal percentage, a single net-benefit score, or that adaptation can ever be finished.
>
> **What would refute the separator.** I wrote these down before sending this:
> 1. a case where a measure's sign is invariant across every residual, place, and horizon tested;
> 2. a risk metric whose decline entails that every place's loss declined;
> 3. an evaluation whose reference updates on its own success reports, yet still predicts observed loss at unmeasured places and later times;
> 4. a measure with a fully enumerated cascade and no positive residual anywhere else or later;
> 5. a finite set of measures after which no further loss residual arrives at any place.
>
> If any one holds, I will retract the corresponding condition. A one-line "this fails because..." is more useful to me than agreement. If you would rather not engage, no reply is needed.
>
> **If it survives, and only then:** the sources are below. The figures are the authority, not my summary of them.
> - Framework figures and formula for direct download (branch `general-agent`): https://github.com/IsaiahN/tether/tree/general-agent/figures
> - Canonical figures README and citation rule: https://github.com/IsaiahN/tether/blob/general-agent/figures/README.md
> - Worked architecture essay: https://adventuresinml.substack.com/p/arrival-of-the-fittest-the-tether
>
> **If you want to use the machinery:** the formula is in the figures link. Run it on an adaptation decision you know well and can check against an outcome. Write down what would refute your result *before* you run it, and state the mode of what you get. A reported failure is more useful to me than a reported success.
>
> With thanks,
> [name]

## Why this version is ordered this way

|---|---|
| Output and residual first, theory second | Fig 10 :52, "Bring a characterised residual, never the source"; Fig 2 :45, "Frames range against the ground. They do not negotiate with it." |

| Three verdicts, not two lists | Fig 9 :54 to :57: the split is a union of overlapping conditions; a case in two conditions is not a harder case of either. |
| Per-slot indexing | Fig 1 :19, "The residual is indexed by object slot, not aggregated." |
| No named measures | Fig 9 :32, "you get a kind of thing, never the answer itself"; the figures README returns "a shape, not a name". |
| No net-benefit score | Fig 9 :60, "Use filters for the budget and witnesses for the verdict"; Fig 12 :125, "A composition costs in two currencies, and they do not add." |
