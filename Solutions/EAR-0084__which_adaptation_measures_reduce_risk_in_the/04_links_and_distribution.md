## Wider literature that bears on the derivation

- The IPCC maladaptation definition quoted above is the canonical field statement. Its phrase "now or in the future" is the same shape as the figure requirement that a residual be tracked per slot over time, and "increased or shifted vulnerability" is the same shape as Fig 11 :66 to :69 (isolation substitutes a habitat; you inherit actors you did not choose). It is in the question's own field, so its overlap with the question is high and it is weak independent corroboration.
- The evolutionary biology and animal behaviour sources are low overlap. They support the structural claim that valence is relative to a changing residual and that a cue can decouple from the ground it indicated. They do not settle any climate measure.
- The engineering and human behaviour sources are low overlap. They provide concrete first and second order cases of a measure that increases the residual it was meant to reduce. The risk-compensation source is honest about effect size, and that honesty is preserved: it is a mechanism witness, not a magnitude witness.
- Three framework essays were opened directly for the canonical "shape" reading. Five other essays are mapped in `05_what_shape_means.md` from the validated exemplar and are explicitly marked as carried, not re-opened this session.

## AI and submission constraint

The *Climatic Change* submission page states that LLMs "do not currently satisfy our authorship criteria" and that "Use of an LLM should be properly documented in the Methods section". Any submission derived from this simulated derivation must declare the LLM role, carry a human author accountable for the final text, and include the pre-registered refuter.
