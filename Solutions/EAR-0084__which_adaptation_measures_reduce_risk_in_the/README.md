## The question stated as an effect

The question asks for two lists: measures that reduce risk in the long term, and measures that create new vulnerabilities. Stated as an effect rather than a category, the question is this. For a named place and hazard (a slot `s`), find an intervention `m` such that (a) after `m`, the observed loss residual `R_s` against a ground that does not change its answer because of what the implementing frame thinks is lower at a later time than before `m`; and (b) `m` does not raise `R` at another slot (a neighbouring community, a downstream reach, a future state) and does not remove the standing to refuse `m` later. The second half of the question, "which create new vulnerabilities", is not a second kind of object. It is the same measure whose valence has flipped because the residual or the slot changed (Fig 11 :49 to :51).

**What an answer would look like, bound as an ordinary term (formula step "Direction"):** a separator. It takes (measure, slot, hazard, time horizon, enumerated habitat cascade) and returns one of `durable reduction`, `new vulnerability`, or `indeterminate pending an instrument`, each with a refuter. The sought-for shape is exactly that separator. A standing list of named good measures is the preference the loop cannot check, so it is made an ordinary term and tested (Fig 5 :65 to :66).

## What is deliberately not here

- No personal emails of named individuals.
- No ranking of people by how likely they are to agree with the derivation.
- No bulk-send plan.
- No universal percentage, no universal list of "best" measures, and no claim that any measure is settled by this document.

Three figure reasons, plus a legal one:

1. **A standing list is refused structurally.** Fig 11 :51: "No standing list of good actors survives a change of residual." An adaptation measure is an actor introduced into a habitat (Fig 11 :65), so its sign is a relation to a residual, not a property it carries.
2. **Correlation disqualifies a verdict.** Fig 2 :40: "disqualifying for a verdict: agreement about the world is worthless when the agreeing frames share an evidence pool". Selecting recipients for receptiveness manufactures the correlation that would make their agreement worthless.
3. **Asking for trust before a check spends independence.** Fig 10 :42: "A verifier that can reconstruct the claim cannot verify it." The first contact carries the residual and its refuter, never the framework's interiors.
4. **Legal.** Scraping personal emails for unsolicited bulk outreach breaches GDPR and CAN-SPAM style rules in many jurisdictions. Public opt-in channels avoid that failure as well as the structural one.
