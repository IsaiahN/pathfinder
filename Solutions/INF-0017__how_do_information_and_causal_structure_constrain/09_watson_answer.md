## QUESTION

Decide, in effect terms, what constrains what between an information quantity and a causal structure in a physical theory. Option A: they are one principle and information is the deeper one. Option B: they are one principle and causal structure is the deeper one. Option C: they are two conditions of a union whose overlap is a compound, information constraining causal structure by exclusion and causal structure constraining what information can be registered, with no named primitive. What each leads to: A and B collapse a union and select for agreement; C keeps the two verdicts separate, states a shape with falsifiers, and leaves the specific measure and structure open until an instrument discriminates them.

## VERDICT (from the figures)

The shape survives, the names do not. Information and causal structure are two conditions of a union whose contact is a compound with its own loop. Information constrains causal structure by exclusion, not generation; causal structure constrains what information can be registered; a genuine mutual constraint needs two closures that differ; and bit cost and action cost are separate currencies. No final informational theory is available, and no physical measure or structure is named. Which measure and which structure are correct is a `gap` reported plainly rather than filled. The operand type of `⋛` at second order is a live strain. This is a `general` / `specified` result, ungrounded, candidate. The cost if wrong is bounded and locatable; the error would be in the census or in a published physics result, both findable. The real avoidable cost is spending an outside reader's independence by sending a theory before the refuter.

- **cost if wrong:** bounded. The packet is mode-labelled, states its refuters before its conclusion and records provenance, so an error is findable rather than invisible. If the union census is wrong, that is a figure-internal re-census owed at `01`. If the 2009 or 2015 reading is wrong, that is a factual correction to a public source. Neither changes the other claim. The larger cost is spending independence by leading with theory instead of residual, which Fig 2 :42 prices: *"Buy independence in proportion to the cost of being wrong."*
- **undoable:** yes. The two directional claims can be withdrawn independently without touching the shape. `07_the_tether_answer.md` and `08_expert_verification_packet.md` already withhold every physical name. Nothing has to be deleted from the record; a refusal would only remove a term's standing to be composed over (Fig 6 :41-42).
- **reversed by:** a ground reading, not agreement. A discriminator that lets one information reading distinguish the causal structures which could produce it would refute the two-sided shape. A measure shown to generate rather than exclude an admissible structure would break the exclusion claim. Two causally distinct structures giving the same complete record for every possible instrument would break the causal-side constraint. A derived common quantity clearing the `⋛` operand-type strain would remove the strain. A figure re-census that turns the union into a partition would change the whole verdict. Until one of these fires, the verdict stands as `candidate`.
