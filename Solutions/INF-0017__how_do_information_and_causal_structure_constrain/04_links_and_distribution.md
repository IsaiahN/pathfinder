## Wider literature that bears on this question

1. **The 2009 principle is an exclusion result, not a construction.** Pawlowski et al. derive a bound on accessible information given m classical bits and show it excludes correlations stronger than the quantum bound. This is the cleanest published instance of "information constrains causal possibility" that does not require accepting any framework. Cite it in stage 1.
2. **The 2015 causal-graph result runs the other direction.** Chaves et al. start from a directed acyclic graph of causal dependencies and derive information-theoretic constraints on the correlations. Together the two papers show the dependence is genuinely two-way in an effect sense, with low overlap (different authors, different venues, different methods). They are still World sources and therefore secondary; the figure verdict stands on Fig 6 and Fig 9.
3. **Causal sets make causal order primary.** The opened overview records Sorkin's "Order + Number = Geometry" and Malament's theorem. Use it as the clearest case of a causal structure constraining what geometry and therefore what information can exist. Do not present it as a result the Tether produced; it is a parallel frame.
4. **MDL prices the description, not the settling.** Rissanen's 1978 two-part code and the Fig 12 rule (*"Bits price the description. Actions price finding out whether it holds."*) are the same bargain at different levels. This is why an "information explains causation" slogan that uses one currency is the named error, not a simplification.
