The sought-for shape is bound as an ordinary term before the run, so it can accrue a residual and be refused: a mutual constraint is an answer only if it (a) states which of information and causal structure is the measured quantity and which is the arrangement, (b) gives the direction of the dependence without collapsing the two into one, (c) names what instrument would settle a specific instance, and (d) stays silent where the figures only give a shape. ## What is deliberately not here

- No personal emails of named individuals.
- No ranking of people by how likely they are to agree.
- No bulk-send plan.
- No claim that the figures settle a specific physical law. They give a shape.

Three reasons, in the figures' own words:

1. **A room does not upgrade the label.** *"A room does not upgrade the label. A room reaching a conclusion without the ground is agreement, and agreement among frames is a weighted average of influence. Adding people raises the vocabulary and does not add an anchor."* (Seat Map :283)
2. **Correlation disqualifies a verdict.** *"High correlation is disqualifying for a verdict and efficient for a convention."* (Seat Map :163) Selecting recipients for likely agreement manufactures the correlation that makes their agreement worthless.
3. **Asking for trust before a check spends independence.** *"A verifier that can reconstruct the claim cannot verify it."* (Fig 10 :42) The outside frame must not hold the interiors.

Legally: scraping personal emails for unsolicited bulk outreach breaches GDPR and CAN-SPAM style rules in many jurisdictions. That is a practical failure on top of the structural one. Every venue in `03` is public and opt-in or a submission portal.
