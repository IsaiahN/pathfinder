## 1. A shape is a constraint, not a filler

The canonical README states the limit: the framework returns *"what a missing thing would have to look like (**a shape, not a name**)."* Fig 9 :32: *"you get a kind of thing, never the answer itself."* Fig 13 :134-135: *"It can say the answer is out of reach with the library as it stands, so something has to be imported before it is asked again. ... It cannot say which primitive is missing, because naming it would mean already holding it."*

From *The Library of Babel* (2026-10-02): *"you can cut your search short if the set of books returned is small and descriptive enough that it provides a general shape of the answer that satisfies your question."*

So a **shape** is the narrowed form of an answer you can state before you can name it. It constrains; it does not fill. For this question the shape is: information is the measurement-side quantity, causal structure is the arrangement-side quantity, a genuine mutual constraint needs two closures that differ, and neither side produces a physical primitive by search alone. Naming causal sets, or a specific entropy measure, as *the* answer would be a name where only a shape is available.

## 2. How a shape is obtained

- *Library of Babel*: use the vocabulary already held that is *closest* to the thing, then look for new terms that overlap or correlate with it, and recurse. *"With each new term gained, you are able to narrow the space by definition and deepen your vocabulary."*
- Fig 6 :30: *"The description travels. Fails by being sharpened into terms only we can read. Cast it in effect terms."* Fig 9 :30-31: *"your words are borrowed too, so describe the effect rather than the cause."*
- *Decoding Tacit Knowledge* (2026-11-10): *"When contact isn't possible, the best you can send is a plain description of what you'd expect to see, because anyone can check that against the world."* This is why stage 1 of the outreach leads with a checkable effect, not with a definition of information.
- *Library of Babel* on stopping: *"You will know that you are done because the finish line will also be the starting place"*, and by then you have *"composed the answer through the process of locating it."*

## 3. What licenses a name

Only two things, both outside the shape itself:

1. **The ground settles it.** Formula step 5: *"the ground settles it. Verified only by what pays. A gate is not the ground."* The README: the framework *"stops at the limit of what the available instruments can distinguish, and records the lineage so the stop is honest."*
2. **A provenanced import from a frame whose closure differs** (Fig 6 :23, Fig 8 :50-52), with a source gate and a shadow test.

*They're Scanning Books* (2026-09-06) sharpens this: *"The real import is not a new term in the existing space. It is a new dimension of the space."* And: *"A minimum viable set of six terms is a human's careful guess at reality's compression axes; another sensorium's minimum set need not be those six."* Also: *"Declaring the primitives does not make them neutral. It makes the choice visible."*

The six are a visible guess, not a discovery of nature's own list. This is a live limit for the present question: information theory generally declares its primitives (bits, distributions, channels), and causal theories declare theirs (events, order, edges). Neither declaration is neutral. The honest move is to record both as choices, not to present one as the ground.

## 4. The specific error this question invites

*The Hard Problem is About Source* (2026-08-28) gives the mechanism. An instrument reads green and *"cannot comment on the source"*; one green light, or yellow plus blue, or RGB, or CMYK, or a scheme not yet discovered. *"If several paths create the same behavior, then behavior is a projection with lossy information."* And *"An instrument that can't distinguish these cases demonstrates its own inadequacy."* It was the **spectrometer**, not an argument, that resolved it.

Applied here: an information reading of a system does not determine the causal structure that produced it, unless an instrument discriminates among the sources. A claim like "this entropy measures that causal structure" is a source named from a reading. What is owed is a discriminator, not a name. *How Intelligence Actually Works* (2026-09-16) adds the union horizon: a case below every observer's horizon is only *"a report that nobody has reached it yet"*, never a settled verdict.

*The Recursive Self-Improvement Trap* (2026-11-19): *"the output is only as good as the validator. The agent, or the tool, or the frame, or the theory, cannot validate itself."*

## 5. ANSWERED: what licenses a particular physical measure or structure here?

Nothing in the framework. The framework returns a shape. A particular measure or structure is licensed only by the ground settling a minted term, or by a provenanced import with a shadow test. Until one of those, the name is the searcher's own contribution and must be recorded as such.

The 2009 information-causality principle and the 2015 causal-structure result are **world** licenses, not framework licenses: they were settled in their own fields by their own grounds (theorems and peer review), and they corroborate the figure shape from a low-overlap direction. They do not make a physical primitive one of the six.

## 6. Two consequences for outreach

**Prestige is not action.** From *The Recursive Self-Improvement Trap*: *"Prestige must never be allowed to validate an action directly... Someone let prestige stand in for proof... Prestige can fund an action. It can give an action a budget to be tried. But it cannot settle an action. Only the ground can settle it. The Ground Maintainer must be blind to prestige."*

A campaign that gathers prestigious names to agree with a shape is the named collapse, not corroboration. The legitimate ask is the opposite: give the claim a budget to be tried, and let the ground, an instrument, an experiment or a board settle it.

**My own audit is a simulation, not a ground reading.** Same essay: *"LLMs can quickly simulate an approximate answer/output with the formula found in the architecture post... However, the truly testable output would need to be derived from the real architecture or working the formula yourself."* LLM output is *"simulacra."* The architecture post adds that outputs *"could never be verified if the solutions presented were not contrived to fit the formula."* So `01` is a simulated derivation, checked for internal consistency against the figures. It is **not** a test of the question and must be labelled as such. It cannot settle anything it reports on.

## 7. A correction I keep visible

An earlier draft of this line read simply "the framework returns a shape, not a name" and used it to forbid naming any structure. The canonical phrase is *"what a **missing** thing would have to look like (a shape, not a name)"*: it governs a primitive not held in Γ. In this question, neither "information" nor "causal structure" is one of the six terms of Fig 13, so the restriction applies with full force. The narrower reading used for held atoms in the exemplar (`05` §5b there) does not transfer here. I keep both statements recorded so the correction is not silently lost.
