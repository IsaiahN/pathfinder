# Expert verification packet: the Tether on information and causal structure

**What this is:** a claim in a form a physicist or information theorist can check. Mode, derivation and refuters are stated before the conclusion, so a reader knows what to look for and what would settle it.

**Mode: `general` on the shape, `specified` on the directional claims, not `grounded`.** No experiment beyond the two cited published results is claimed here. This is a shape and a route, not a result.

## 1. The claim, in one paragraph

Information and causal structure are two conditions of one union, not one principle seen twice. Information is the measurement-side quantity, causal structure the arrangement-side quantity. An information quantity constrains causal structure by **exclusion**: a communication bound rules out correlations, it does not construct a structure. A causal structure constrains information by fixing what can reach a record at all. A genuine mutual constraint needs two frames whose closures differ, and the two currencies, bits for description and actions for settling, do not add. The framework names no physical measure and no physical structure; those remain a `gap` until an instrument discriminates them. What is offered is the shape, falsifiers written in advance, and the discriminators that would close it.

## 2. Three checkable sub-claims

### C1: The two sides are not one principle (framework-internal; checkable by reading Figures 9 and 13)

- **Statement:** information and causal structure are two conditions of a union whose overlap is a compound with its own loop; they must not be averaged into one "information explains causation" principle.
- **Source, verbatim:**
  - Fig 9 :54-55: *"when the split lands on a union rather than a partition, unbundle before you search again."*
  - Fig 9 :57-58: *"a case in two conditions is not a harder case of either. It stops being a mixture and becomes a specific compound."*
  - Fig 13 :16 and :26: *"it names what is being measured"* and *"it decides what the measurement would mean"*.
- **Refuter:** produce a closed principle deriving both the measure and the order inside one frame, with provenance showing no import, so the problem is a partition.

### C2: Information constrains causal structure by exclusion (physics-facing; checkable against the 2009 result)

- **Statement:** a communicated-bit bound excludes admissible correlations; it does not generate a causal structure.
- **Source, verbatim:** Fig 6 :21: *"MINT composes inside the closure and can never add an atom."* Fig 6 :23: *"No frame escapes its own closure. The bound moves only on contact with a frame whose closure differs."*
- **World check:** Pawlowski, Paterek, Kaszlikowski, Scarani, Winter and Zukowski, *Information Causality as a Physical Principle*, Nature 461, 1101 (2009), arXiv:0905.2292. The abstract states that if Alice communicates m bits, *"the total information access that Bob gains to her data is not greater than m"* and that the principle *"is violated by all the no-signaling correlations which are stronger that the strongest quantum correlations."*
- **Refuter:** exhibit a physical theory whose admissible correlations are derived, not merely excluded, by an information measure with a legitimate source gate and shadow test; or produce a no-signalling correlation beyond the quantum bound that violates no information principle, which would break the 2009 result as a constraint.

### C3: Causal structure constrains what can be registered, and the currencies differ (physics-facing plus framework-internal)

- **Statement:** the causal structure determines which events can influence which, hence what can reach a record; and a bit cost and an action cost cannot be summed into one figure.
- **Source, verbatim:** Fig 6 :26: *"Composition explores what the operators reach; instruments extend what can be represented at all."* Fig 13 :24: *"that entities relate, and influence travels"*. Fig 12 :125: *"A composition costs in two currencies, and they do not add."*
- **World check:** Chaves, Majenz and Gross, *Information-Theoretic Implications of Quantum Causal Structures*, Nature Communications 6, 5766 (2015), arXiv:1407.3800: *"The correlations that can be observed between a set of variables depend on the causal structure underpinning them."* Causal-set overview: Sorkin's *"Order + Number = Geometry"* and Malament's conformal-isomorphism theorem.
- **Refuter:** show two causally distinct structures giving the same complete record for every possible instrument with no residual; or exhibit a common derived unit pricing description bits and settling actions with the conversion derived rather than stipulated.

## 3. What is explicitly not claimed

- Not that information is more fundamental than causal structure, or the reverse.
