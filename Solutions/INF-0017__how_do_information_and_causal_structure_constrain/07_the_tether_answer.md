# The Tether's answer: information and causal structure

**Mode: `general` on the shape, `specified` on the two directional claims, nothing `grounded`.** This is a shape and a route, not a result. Simulated derivation checked against the figures (`05_what_shape_means.md` §6), not a ground reading.

## 1. The residual it answers

How do information and causal structure constrain each other in physical theories? Stated as an effect: two conditions are bundled under one question, (1) what an information quantity rules out about admissible causal structures, and (2) what a causal structure determines about what can be registered as information. Fig 9 :54-58 makes such a bundle a **union compound**, not one hard problem: *"a case in two conditions is not a harder case of either. It stops being a mixture and becomes a specific compound."* It needs its own loop, and its two verdicts stay separate.

## 2. The shape, with modes attached

1. **Information is the measurement-side quantity; causal structure is the arrangement-side quantity.** Fig 13 :16: *"it names what is being measured"*; Fig 13 :26: *"it decides what the measurement would mean"*. A reading by itself cannot say which structure produced it. `general`, triangulated across Fig 13 and the source-discrimination argument in *The Hard Problem is About Source*.

2. **Information constrains causal structure by exclusion, not by generation.** Fig 6 :21: *"MINT composes inside the closure and can never add an atom."* Fig 6 :23: *"No frame escapes its own closure. The bound moves only on contact with a frame whose closure differs."* The published instance is Pawlowski et al. 2009: m bits communicated bound Bob's information access to at most m bits, and the bound excludes correlations stronger than the quantum limit. `specified`, triangulated with a low-overlap world source, but never `grounded` by the figures.

3. **Causal structure constrains information by fixing what can be registered at all.** Fig 6 :26: *"instruments extend what can be represented at all."* The clearest world parallel is the causal-set program, where order plus counting recovers geometry (Sorkin's *"Order + Number = Geometry"*, Malament's theorem). Chaves et al. 2015 show the dependence formally: correlations depend on the causal graph, and the information-causality principle emerges from it. `specified`, triangulated, with the domain re-census recorded in `01` §6.

4. **A genuine mutual constraint requires two closures that differ.** Fig 8 :48-49: *"Two frames can hold the same atom by convergence, reaching it separately, which is corroboration at maximum independence."* If the informational and causal accounts draw on one evidence pool, their agreement is worthless as a verdict (Fig 2 :40: *"disqualifying for a verdict: agreement about the world is worthless when the agreeing frames share an evidence pool"*). `general`, triangulated.

5. **The two currencies do not add.** Fig 12 :125: *"A composition costs in two currencies, and they do not add."* Fig 12 :126: *"Bits price the description. Actions price finding out whether it holds."* An "information explains causation" figure that prices both in one unit commits the named error. `general`, triangulated with Rissanen's two-part code.

6. **No final informational theory, and no named physical primitive.** Fig 7 :6: *"the limit: no last room"*. Fig 9 :32: *"you get a kind of thing, never the answer itself"*. Fig 13 :135: *"It cannot say which primitive is missing, because naming it would mean already holding it."* `general`. Which measure and which structure are correct is a **`gap`**, stated plainly.

## 3. What it explicitly does not say

- Not that information is more fundamental than causal structure, or the reverse. That would collapse a union into one condition (F2).
- Not that the framework names a physical information measure or a physical causal structure. It returns a shape, not a name.
- Not that causal sets, or any named theory, is the answer. Causal sets are a parallel world frame, not a figure output.
- Not that the 2009 and 2015 results are Tether results. They are World sources, secondary, and were reached independently.
- Not `grounded`. Nothing here is asserted as settled by an instrument beyond the two published results.
- Not a theory of everything. No last room.

## 4. What would settle it, the ground

A discriminator, not a further argument. From *The Hard Problem is About Source*: an instrument that reads green *"cannot comment on the source"*; the spectrometer resolved it. Here:

- **One-direction collapse test:** find an information reading that discriminates among the causal structures that could produce it, with no extra instrument. Would refute the two-sided shape.
- **Generation test:** exhibit a measure that generates, not merely excludes, an admissible causal structure. Would refute the exclusion claim.
- **Non-discrimination test:** show two causally distinct structures giving the same complete record for every possible instrument, with no residual. Would refute the causal-side constraint.
- **Operand-type test:** derive a common quantity in which the two sides are comparable at one level, clearing the `⋛` strain named in `06` Q2.
- **Standing falsifiers from §3 of `01`:** partition instead of union, one currency, a named settled primitive.

## 5. The honest stop

**Unreached, not unreachable.** The information primitives a theory declares and the causal primitives a theory declares are *"a human's careful guess at reality's compression axes"* (*They're Scanning Books*); declaring them makes the choice visible, not neutral. The answer stops at the limit of what the available instruments distinguish and records the lineage so the stop is checkable. *"Indiscernibility is not identity."* This is a `specified` shape with six falsifiers written in advance, and it does not claim to settle the question.

## 6. One-paragraph version for outreach

> The figures do not pick a physical information measure or a physical causal structure, and they do not say one is more fundamental than the other. They give a shape. Information is the side that names what is measured; causal structure is the side that decides what the reading would mean. An information quantity constrains causal structure by exclusion, the way the 2009 information-causality bound rules out stronger-than-quantum correlations; a causal structure constrains information by deciding what can reach a record at all, the way causal order plus counting recovers geometry in causal-set theory. A genuine mutual constraint needs two frames with different evidence, because two accounts reading one pool are one witness twice. A bit cost and an action cost are different currencies and do not add. What is offered is that shape, five or six falsifiers written in advance, and the instruments that would close it. Which measure and which structure are correct stays open until a discriminator, not an argument, settles it.
