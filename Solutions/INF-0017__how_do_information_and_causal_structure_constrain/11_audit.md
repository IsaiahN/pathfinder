## Independent domain census

I redid the census before reading the primary's conclusions, using the WATSON order: R by arity, symmetry and scale; one line per domain with actors / currency / anchor; partition or union; order by dependency.

- **R (arity / symmetry / scale).** The residual is the mutual-constraint question between an information quantity and a causal structure. Arity: two conditions, so a **union compound** (Fig 9 :57-58: *"a case in two conditions is not a harder case of either"*), not one hard question. Symmetry: the question asks whether the dependence is one-way or mutual, so I cannot assume it. Scale: first order within one theory, second order where a theory meets a different frame or its ground.
- **Domain 1, measuring information.** Actors: system states, an observer, a record. Currency: bits, entropy, description length. Anchor: an instrument that returns a reading (Fig 6 :34-35). Question type: one side of a union.
- **Domain 2, causal structure.** Actors: events, precedence, allowed influences. Currency: order, functional dependence. Anchor: an experiment distinguishing two structures. Question type: other side of the union.
- **Domain 3, the contact.** Actors: the two frames and the residual at their boundary. Currency: mutual constraint, cross-frame surplus. Anchor: a ground that fires on the joint. Question type: overlap, a compound with its own loop.
- **Domain 4, verification.** Actors: expert readers, tests, instruments. Currency: agreement and residual. Anchor: the ground, never agreement. In this simulated run its independence is near zero.

Order by dependency: perceive each side before acquiring terms (Fig 3 links 1 and 2), state a progress criterion before planning a joint account (link 3 before link 4), describe the residual before choosing a frame (Fig 9 :27-28).

My census matches the primary's four domains and its union reading.
