# The Tether's answer: distinguishing uncertainty about meaning from uncertainty about reception

**Mode: `specified` on the operational distinction, `general` on the structural shape, not `grounded`.** This is a two-test procedure and a route, not a measurement. Simulated derivation checked against the figures (`05_what_shape_means.md` §7), not a ground reading.

## 1. The residual it answers

A receiver forms a residual per object slot. Two failures can register on the same slot: no reading is arriving, or a reading is arriving but what it is about is underdetermined. They are a **union compound**, not one hard question and not a clean partition (Fig 9 :54: "And when the split lands on a union rather than a partition, unbundle before you search again."). Each has its own output and its own remedy. Conflating them spends effort on the wrong link: a convention convened over a dead channel is a reading of nothing, and a new instrument demanded for a pure seam problem imports an atom that was not needed.

## 2. The shape

1. **They are two failure genera, not two ends of one scale.** Fig 10 opens with two panels, "1 · the seam" and "2 · the channel". The channel side outputs a verdict on the connection: "the output is not a verdict, it is / you have lost touch" (Fig 10 :27-28). The seam side outputs a convention that makes a previously unstatable disagreement statable: "a disagreement nothing can state" (Fig 10 :14), "the convention does not fix the bug / it makes the bug statable" (Fig 10 :16-17). Different outputs, different remedies.
2. **Reception uncertainty is a channel question, measured per slot.** "The residual is indexed by object slot, not aggregated" (Fig 1 :19), and "Aggregating across slots is how a live signal disappears into an average" (Fig 1 :22). The ground does not decay; the channel does (Fig 10 :24). The named failure modes are "stale percepts, dropped transients, saturated metrics, pooled readings" (Fig 10 :25-26). The positive test is a known-positive fixture read through the same channel; a global low error is not a channel check.
3. **Reception is upstream, so it is tested first.** Fig 3 :19: "Optimising a link that already works cannot rescue one that does not." Fig 3 :22-23: a step whose input never arrives cannot be diagnosed, and "a reading of nothing" is not a weak reading. Semantic work performed while contact is uncertain is void, not merely inconclusive.
4. **Meaning uncertainty survives a live channel because the arrangement is lost, not the ingredients.** Fig 12 :14-16: "the ingredients are recorded and the arrangement is not", "and nothing signals that anything was lost". Fig 12 :46: "which one holds is not recoverable from the operands". The substrate states the same split in its own terms: states "it names what is being measured" (Fig 13 :16), structures "it decides what the measurement would mean" (Fig 13 :26).
5. **The remedies are opposed, and that is how a misdiagnosis is detected.** If contact is lost, restore the reading, do not adjudicate meaning. If the channel is live and meaning is still uncertain, it is a seam: author a convention to make the bug statable, or import a discriminator with provenance and a shadow test (Fig 6 :12-13). Do not add bandwidth: "An instrument is not built from a description. It is improved from a worse instrument already returning something" (Fig 6 :32). Whichever remedy leaves the residual persisting and not trending down is the wrong one (Formula step 2).
6. **Agreement is the wrong test for reception and the right test only for a convention.** Fig 2 :40: "agreement about the world is worthless when the agreeing frames share an evidence pool". Fig 2 :41: "agreement about a shared meaning is the product, not a corruption of it". But "a convention that decides an outcome has stopped being one" (Fig 10 :47), and "Declaring a meaning does not ground it" (Fig 10 :49).
7. **When both are present, unbundle (Fig 9 :54-58), and never let a score hand the verdict** (Fig 9 :60: "Use filters for the budget and witnesses for the verdict. Never let a filter hand you a verdict.").

## 3. What it explicitly does not say

- Not a new measure of semantic content, and not a reduction of meaning to channel statistics.
- Not that "syntax versus semantics" is the figure's own vocabulary. That label is an adopted import with no source gate; the figures speak of the seam and the channel.
- Not that more bandwidth, a larger corpus or a better code resolves an interpretive disagreement.
- Not that a convention settles what a message was about, or that agreement among readers checks whether a signal arrived.
- Not that the source-discriminating instrument is known. Its slot is recorded, not filled.
- Not `grounded`. No per-slot channel check and no source-discriminating instrument was run in this derivation.

## 4. What would settle it: the ground

A source-discriminating instrument and a per-slot channel check, not a further argument. From *The Hard Problem is About Source*: an instrument that reads green "cannot comment on the source", and "An instrument that can't distinguish these cases demonstrates its own inadequacy". It was resolved "not by argument, but by the arrival of a better instrument." Here:

1. **Channel Test:** a known-positive fixture at each slot, read through the same channel. If it does not return, the diagnosis is contact lost and all meaning conclusions drawn meanwhile are void (Fig 3 :22-23; Seat Map :275).
2. **Source Test:** an instrument that discriminates among the sources that would produce the same reading. Its shape is recorded in `01_figure_grounded_audit.md` §6 Q7; it is not named.
3. **Shadow Test and provenance:** any imported discriminator must explain a gap and carry a source record, or convergent derivation and adopted import are indistinguishable in the contents (Fig 8 :50-52).
4. **Standing refuters:** meaning fully determined by reception probability; a declaration that grounds meaning; a below-channel reading that discriminates meaning; a single scalar uncertainty that absorbs both; a global fit passing with a dead slot (all in `01` §4).

## 5. The honest stop

**Unreached, not unreachable.** The framework returns the shape of the missing discriminator, not its name (Fig 13 :134-135: "Unreachable is a verdict here, not a silence"). It cannot say which instrument will close the seam, because naming it would mean already holding it. The stop is recorded with its lineage so a later reader can locate the error rather than only feel it. "Indiscernibility is not identity" (figures README and *How Intelligence Actually Works*): a reading that no current instrument distinguishes from its source is still a reading.

## 6. One-paragraph version for outreach

> The Tether does not offer a new theory of semantic information, and it does not merge the two uncertainties into one scale. It says they are two failure genera with different outputs and opposed remedies. On the channel side, the ground does not decay, the channel does: check contact per slot with a signal you know is there, and if contact is lost, restore the reading rather than debate what it meant. On the seam side, a signal can arrive with every ingredient intact while the arrangement that carries meaning is lost, and nothing signals the loss. The remedy there is a convention that makes the disagreement statable, never a declaration that grounds meaning, and never more bandwidth for a problem the channel is already returning. Agreement between readers is the product of a convention, not a check on whether the signal arrived. When both failures are present, unbundle them. What the framework returns is that procedure, its refuters written in advance, and the shape of the source-discriminating instrument that would close the seam. The slot stays open until the ground settles it.
