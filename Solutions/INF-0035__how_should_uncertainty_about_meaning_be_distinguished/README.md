## The residual in effect terms

A receiver holds a residual between what its library predicted and what it observed. Two failures can produce a similar-looking residual:

1. **No signal arrived at the object slot** (channel or contact failure).
2. **A signal arrived but its reading is underdetermined** (seam or meaning failure).

Conflating them sends the wrong remedy. A meaning convention convened over a dead channel is a reading of nothing (Fig 3 :22-23). A new instrument or extra bandwidth demanded for a semantic underdetermination imports an atom that was not needed (Fig 6 :24, Fig 8 :42-43). The answer this folder derives is an ordered two-test procedure that separates them, keeps their verdicts apart, and never lets agreement between readers stand in for a channel check.

## What is deliberately not here

- No personal emails of named individuals.
- No ranking of people by how likely they are to believe the distinction, and no receptiveness column.
- No bulk-send plan.

Three figure-grounded reasons:

1. **Agreement has valence.** "High correlation is disqualifying for a verdict and efficient for a convention" (Seat Map :163). Selecting readers for agreement about meaning manufactures the correlation that would make their agreement worthless as a channel verdict (Fig 2 :40).
2. **A verifier that holds the interiors cannot verify.** Fig 10 :42: "A verifier that can reconstruct the claim cannot verify it." The first contact carries the residual and its refuter, not the framework.
3. **Prestige is not action.** *The Recursive Self-Improvement Trap*: "Prestige can fund an action. It can give an action a budget to be tried. But it cannot settle an action. Only the ground can settle it."
