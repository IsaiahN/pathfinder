Order by dependency: check contact before reading (Fig 3 :19, :22-23); carry a characterised residual, never the source (Fig 10 :52); describe before looking (Fig 9 :27-28).

## Round trip gap R_T

The Symbols table defines R_T as `gap(x, (T_E ∘ T_A)(x))`, the gap between what was sent up and what came back down. Fig 4 :30 requires it to be measured rather than assumed. I ran the round trip on the condensation the folder actually uses.

1. **Sent up (x):** the verbatim figure set, chiefly Fig 10's two panels, Fig 1's per-slot residual, Fig 3's dependency order, Fig 12's bond argument and Fig 2's valence split.
2. **Abstraction (T_A):** the shorthand carried in `07_the_tether_answer.md`, that channel uncertainty and meaning uncertainty are two failure genera with opposed remedies, checked in order, with agreement reserved for conventions.
3. **Concretisation (T_E):** the world-facing form in `09_watson_answer.md`: Shannon's source/channel split, the vervet alarm-call case, metamerism, and a known-positive fixture test.

**R_T is nonzero.** Three things the shorthand holds do not survive the trip back down:

- the **anchor rule**: the ground does not decay, only the channel does. The Shannon and vervet analogues carry no equivalent of an immovable anchor; importing them as if they did would make the analogue do the deciding (Fig 9 :60).
- the **convention/verdict valence**: Fig 2 :39-41 distinguishes agreement about meaning from agreement about the world. The engineering analogue has no convention concept, so the shorthand's rule about agreement is dropped on descent unless it is restated explicitly.
- the **dependency order and the void-reading claim**: Fig 3 :22-23 has no counterpart in the Shannon or vervet material. A reader could take the analogues to support two independent rather than ordered uncertainties.

Conversely, one thing survives cleanly: arrival and interpretation are separable, and a complete signal can leave meaning underdetermined. That is the residue worth sending. R_T was **not** measured numerically, because no ground reading exists; it is recorded qualitatively as the enumerated set of dropped constraints. Treating "the figures are the same thing in fewer words" as identity would set R_T to zero and would be the dead-reckoning error Fig 4 :30 names. The honest form is a lossy condensation with a measured gap, which is what `09_watson_answer.md` states.
