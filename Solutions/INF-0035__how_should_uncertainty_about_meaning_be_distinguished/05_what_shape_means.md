## 1. A shape is a constraint, not a filler

The canonical figures README states the limit: the framework returns "what a missing thing would have to look like (**a shape, not a name**)." Fig 9 :32: "you get a kind of thing, never the answer itself". Fig 13 :134 "Unreachable is a verdict here, not a silence," and Fig 13 :135 "It cannot say which primitive is missing, because naming it would mean already holding it."

From *The Library of Babel*: "This means that you can cut your search short if the set of books returned is small and descriptive enough that it provides a general shape of the answer that satisfies your question."

So a **shape** is the narrowed form of an answer you can state before you can name it. It constrains; it does not fill. For this question, "two failure genera, one channel, one seam, in that dependency order" is a shape. "The missing thing is a particular semantic theory" would be a name, and the framework does not license that name here.

## 2. How a shape is obtained

- *Library of Babel* on bootstrapping vocabulary: "If you don't know the right terms to search for, you will have to use the existing vocabulary you do know that is closest to the words that you wish to describe and grasp the shape of." "With each new term gained, you narrow the space and deepen your vocabulary."
- *Library of Babel* on stopping: "You will know that you are done because the finish line will also be the starting place, as there is nowhere else to search and nothing else that can describe the answer better." And the paradox: "you have already composed the answer through the process of locating it."
- Fig 6 :30: "The description travels. Fails by being sharpened into terms only we can read. Cast it in effect terms." Fig 9 :30-31: "your words are borrowed too, so describe the effect rather than the cause".
- *Decoding Tacit Knowledge*: "When contact isn't possible, the best you can send is a plain description of what you'd expect to see, because anyone can check that against the world."
- *How Intelligence Actually Works* on the process: "Perceive, act, get corrected at a finer level, go deeper, repeat until the residual stops registering."

Here the shape was obtained by describing the residual in effect terms first (loss of contact versus underdetermined reading), then grepping the figure text, then re-censusing when a line seemed to strain. The vocabulary came from the figures, not from information-theory jargon imported at the start.

## 3. What licenses a name

Only two things, both outside the shape itself:

1. **The ground settles it.** Formula step 5: "the ground settles it. Verified only by what pays. A gate is not the ground." The README: the framework "stops at the limit of what the available instruments can distinguish, and records the lineage so the stop is honest."
2. **A provenanced import from a frame whose closure differs** (Fig 6 :23; Fig 8 :50-52), with a source gate and a shadow test.

*They're Scanning Books*: "The real import is not a new term in the existing space. It is a new dimension of the space." And: "A minimum viable set of six terms is a human's careful guess at reality's compression axes; another sensorium's minimum set need not be those six." Also: "Declaring the primitives does not make them neutral. It makes the choice visible."

For this question: the source-discriminating instrument that would name the reading's source is **not held**. Fig 6 :32-35: "An instrument is not built from a description. It is improved from a worse instrument already returning something." "Nobody specified a telescope from an account of Mars." So the name stays a slot, recorded as such (Fig 13 :134-135).

## 4. Why naming the source from the reading is the specific forbidden error

*The Hard Problem is About Source* gives the mechanism, and it is directly on point:

- Metamerism: an instrument reads green "It's possible that the source might be one green light. But the 'source' could also be a mix of one Yellow light and one Blue light." "The instrument is speaking only about what it measured, it cannot comment on the source."
- "If several paths create the same behavior, then behavior is a projection with lossy information (it cannot define consciousness in full)."
- "An instrument that can't distinguish these cases demonstrates its own inadequacy, it cannot claim anything substantial about what is being measured or what causes it."
- "It was proven wrong not by argument, but by the arrival of a better instrument."

*How Intelligence Actually Works* adds the version that matters for a signal: "The projection of green can't tell you whether it was RGB, CMYK or something else that composed it." The video frame versus photograph case: "The frame and the photograph are indistinguishable as artifacts, and they carry different warrants. One was taken; one was extracted. And nothing in the image records which." That is Fig 12 :14-16 in narrative form: the ingredients are recorded, the arrangement is not, and nothing signals the loss.

So the error is not "having an interpretation." It is **declaring the source from a reading that does not discriminate among sources**. The remedy is a better instrument, not a better argument, and until it arrives the lineage is recorded.

*The Recursive Self-Improvement Trap*: "the output is only as good as the validator. The agent, or the tool, or the frame, or the theory, cannot validate itself."

## 5. Q: Does the framework give an operational shape for this distinction, or only a label?

**ANSWERED, no further question needed.** Two checks were run:

1. **Is "two panels" a real derivation or a restatement?** Fig 10 names "1 · the seam" and "2 · the channel" as two offices with different outputs and prohibitions. The seat map independently specifies the maintainer's channel duty: "The seat's output here is not a verdict on the world but a verdict on the connection: contact has been lost, and the remedy is to restore the reading, not to adjudicate what it would have said." Two documents, low overlap, so the separation is a figure-derived shape, not a Myers-Briggs-style partition invented for the occasion.
2. **Is there a dependency order, not just a list?** Fig 3 puts perception before vocabulary and states "A step whose input never arrives cannot be diagnosed, only its predecessor can." Fig 6 adds that an instrument is improved from a worse one already returning something. So the two tests are ordered and their remedies are opposed. That is enough for an operational shape: test contact per slot, then test recoverability of the arrangement, then unbundle the compound.

**What remains a slot:** the discriminator that would map a reading to its source. Fig 10 :18 and :49 forbid declaring it grounded; Fig 8 :50-52 requires the record to show whether it was derived or imported. The slot is named as a shape (a source-discriminating instrument), not filled with a theory.

## 6. Correction to my own §3 and §4

I initially wrote §3 as if "shape, not a name" forbade saying anything at all about meaning until a ground settles it. That overstates the rule. The README's phrase is "what a **missing** thing would have to look like (a shape, not a name)." It governs a primitive not held in Γ, not an operational procedure. Fig 10 and Fig 3 both give procedures, so those are licensable at `specified` mode. Retracted as applied to the procedure; retained as applied to the source-discriminating instrument, which is genuinely not held.

I also overstated §4 as a blanket ban on interpretation. The essays' target is a declaration that a source is settled from a reading that cannot discriminate sources. Ordinary interpretation under a live channel, recorded as a convention with provenance, is exactly what Fig 10 :16-17 licenses ("the convention does not fix the bug / it makes the bug statable"). Both readings are now recorded so neither is quietly lost.

## 7. Two consequences for outreach

**Prestige is not action.** From *The Recursive Self-Improvement Trap*: "Prestige must never be allowed to validate an action directly... Someone let prestige stand in for proof... Prestige can fund an action. It can give an action a budget to be tried. But it cannot settle an action. Only the ground can settle it. The Ground Maintainer must be blind to prestige." A list of readers chosen for likely agreement about meaning is the named collapse, not corroboration. The legitimate ask is a failure condition, with the ground (a per-slot channel check, a known-positive fixture, a source-discriminating instrument) doing the settling.

**My own audit is a simulation, not a ground reading.** Same essay: "LLMs can quickly simulate an approximate answer/output with the formula found in the architecture post... However, the truly testable output would need to be derived from the real architecture or working the formula yourself." The architecture article adds: "the outputs generated must be seen as simulacra" and "due to the black-box nature could never be verified if the solutions presented were not contrived to fit the formula." So `01` is a simulated derivation checked for internal consistency against the figures. It is **not** a test of the distinction and must be labelled as such. It cannot settle anything it reports on.
