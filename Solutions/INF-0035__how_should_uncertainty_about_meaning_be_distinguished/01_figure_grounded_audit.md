## 0. The residual, stated in effect terms

A receiver predicts, acts, observes, and forms a residual `R = |Γ(b,a) − o′|` per object slot (Symbols table; Fig 1 :11). Two different failures can register on the same slot:

- **U_reception:** no reading is arriving. The channel to the ground has failed. The effect is loss of contact.
- **U_meaning:** a reading is arriving, but what it is about is underdetermined. The effect is a seam between two frames that have no shared reading.

What is being decided: which failure genus is present, and therefore which remedy applies. What each option leads to:

- Treat U_meaning as U_reception and you demand a better instrument or more bandwidth for a problem the channel is already returning. That imports an atom that was not needed (Fig 6 :24; Fig 8 :42-43).
- Treat U_reception as U_meaning and you convene a convention, negotiate an interpretation, and build a reading of nothing (Fig 3 :22-23; Fig 2 :35).

**Binding the sought-for shape as an ordinary term** (Formula "Direction"; Fig 5 :65-66): an answer would look like a decision procedure that (a) tests contact per slot before (b) testing whether the arrangement that carries the reading is recoverable, and that (c) keeps the two verdicts in separate columns with separate anchors. Writing that down now makes the preference refusable by the same test as everything else.

## 1. Domain census (before choosing any figure line)

### R by arity, symmetry and scale

- **Arity: two conditions.** Reception and meaning can both fail at once on one slot, so this is a **union compound**, not one hard question and not a clean partition. Fig 9 :54: "And when the split lands on a union rather than a partition, unbundle before you search again." Fig 9 :57: "a case in two conditions is not a harder case of either". A single lever (more bandwidth, or more context) clears the region where only one condition holds and leaves the intersection standing.
- **Symmetry: ordered, not symmetric.** Reception is upstream of meaning (Fig 3). You cannot read meaning below a broken channel. Reversing the order is the named failure.
- **Scale: two adjacent links of one chain.** Reception sits at perception (Fig 3 link 1); meaning sits at vocabulary/interpretation (Fig 3 link 2). The crossing between them is the seam (Fig 10 panel 1) and the membrane (Fig 4).

### One line per domain (actors / currency / anchor)

A domain is admitted only if it has a nameable anchor.

| domain | actors | currency | anchor | nameable? |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| D1, the channel (contact) | the ground, the channel, the sensor/instrument, the maintainer (Seat 3) | whether R arrives at slot s; health of contact | the ground, which does not decay | yes: the ground |
| D2, the seam (meaning) | two frames, a reading, the convention authored at the seam (Seat 2/3) | shared meaning, a convention | the ground, reached only through the reading; a reading underdetermines its source | yes: the ground, but only through a discriminator |
| D3, the checking arrangement | maker (Seat 1), maintainer (Seat 3), outside frame (Seat 4) | residuals and agreement | the ground, never agreement | yes: the ground |

If a fourth candidate had no nameable anchor, it would not be a domain yet and would not get a loop. None is admitted here.

### Partition or union

Union (Fig 9 :54-58). D1 and D2 can overlap on one slot. The overlap is a compound with its own loop, not a harder case of either (Seat Map :211). D3 is not a content domain but the arrangement that keeps D1 and D2 honest, so it is ordered after them as the checking layer.

### Dependency order (Fig 3)

Settle D1 before D2: "Optimising a link that already works cannot rescue one that does not" (Fig 3 :19), and "A step whose input never arrives cannot be diagnosed, only its predecessor can" (Fig 3 :22). Then run D3 as the seat that keeps the anchor reachable (Seat Map :93). Describe the residual before choosing a frame (Fig 9 :27-28).

**Strain test applied at the census:** Fig 2 :40 says agreement about the world is worthless for a shared evidence pool, while Fig 2 :41 says agreement about a shared meaning is the product. Read as one sentence about agreement, these strain. Re-census resolves it: Fig 2 :39 announces "Correlation has valence"; the two clauses govern different objects (a verdict about the world versus a convention about a shared meaning). Different objects, no strain (canonical re-census rule in the figures README).

## 3. Findings F1 to F8

### F1: Fig 10 separates the two uncertainties into two different offices (decisive)

Fig 10 names the panels directly:

> Fig 10 :4 "1 · the seam"
> Fig 10 :5 "2 · the channel"

The seam panel gives the meaning side:

> Fig 10 :13 "two frames, one field, no shared reading"
> Fig 10 :14 "a disagreement nothing can state"
> Fig 10 :16 "the convention does not fix the bug"
> Fig 10 :17 "it makes the bug statable"
> Fig 10 :18 "a declared meaning is not a grounded one"

The channel panel gives the reception side:

> Fig 10 :23 "R never arrives"
> Fig 10 :24 "the ground does not decay, the channel does"
> Fig 10 :25 "stale percepts, dropped transients,"
> Fig 10 :26 "saturated metrics, pooled readings"
> Fig 10 :27 "the output is not a verdict, it is"
> Fig 10 :28 "you have lost touch"

The figure itself assigns them different outputs. The channel panel's output is a verdict on the connection, not on the world: Seat Map :93 says "The seat's output here is not a verdict on the world but a verdict on the connection: contact has been lost, and the remedy is to restore the reading, not to adjudicate what it would have said." The seam panel's output is a convention that makes a previously unstatable disagreement statable (Fig 10 :16-17).

So the distinction is not a difference of degree on one uncertainty scale. It is a difference of failure genus, anchor reading, output and remedy. **Mode: `specified` (structural, figure-derived).** Triangulation: within-framework triangulated across Fig 10 and the seat map (Seat Map :93, :167), two different documents with low overlap. Both share the framework author, so this is internal consistency, not independent corroboration.

**Refuter for F1:** show a case in which the meaning of a signal is fixed entirely by the probability that it was received, so no seam panel is needed and a declaration of meaning grounds it. That would collapse the two offices into one and break Fig 10 :18.

### F2: reception is per slot, and aggregation hides a dead channel

> Fig 1 :19 "The residual is indexed by object slot, not aggregated."
> Fig 1 :22 "Aggregating across slots is how a live signal disappears into an average."
> Fig 1 :13 "a global R near zero with one live slot residual is a legal state"

A global low error is not evidence that all channels are alive. The question "was a signal received?" must be asked per slot. Fig 10 :25-26 names the ways a channel dies while internal instruments read green: "stale percepts, dropped transients, saturated metrics, pooled readings". Seat Map :285: "Every instrument points inward unless one is built to point out. Wiring, consumption and behaviour are all read from inside; whether the record that was supposed to reach the outside arrived is read by nothing."

This gives the positive test for U_reception: a known-positive fixture at each slot, that is, a signal the receiver knows is there, read through the same channel. A channel that cannot return the fixture is dead regardless of how well the aggregate fits. **Mode: `specified`.** Triangulated across Fig 1 and the seat map's failure genera (Seat Map :275: "a known-positive fixture proves sensitivity, never specificity. Both are required").

**Refuter for F2:** a per-slot known-positive fixture that passes while no reading from that slot reaches the decision, with the channel still counted as live by the figures. That breaks Fig 1 :19 and Fig 10 :23-28.

### F3: reception is upstream of meaning; the reverse order produces a reading of nothing

> Fig 3 :5 "the system can tell what is in front of it, and tell it apart from what was there before"
> Fig 3 :6 "2 · vocabulary"
> Fig 3 :19 "Optimising a link that already works cannot rescue one that does not."
> Fig 3 :22 "A step whose input never arrives cannot be diagnosed, only its predecessor can. Which is why a reading taken below the break is not a weak"
> Fig 3 :23 "reading. It is a reading of nothing."

Meaning work performed while reception is uncertain is not weak meaning work. It is work on nothing. Conversely, once a channel is returning something, more of the same channel cannot resolve a seam:

> Fig 6 :32 "An instrument is not built from a description. It is improved from a worse instrument already returning something."
> Fig 6 :33 "Nobody specified a telescope from an account of Mars."

So the two uncertainties have **opposed remedies**. Reception failure: restore the reading. Meaning failure under a live channel: author a convention or import a discriminator, not another sensor. Running one remedy for the other is detectable precisely because the residual does not trend down (Formula step 2). **Mode: `specified`.** Triangulated across Fig 3 and Fig 6, two figures with different subjects and low overlap.

**Refuter for F3:** a case in which a reading taken while R does not arrive still discriminates between meanings, or a case in which refining the same already-returning instrument resolves a pure seam disagreement. That breaks Fig 3 :22-23 or Fig 6 :32.

### F4: a signal can be fully received while its meaning is lost silently

Fig 12 explains why the two uncertainties are independent rather than complementary:

> Fig 12 :14 "the ingredients are recorded and the arrangement is not"
> Fig 12 :16 "and nothing signals that anything was lost"
> Fig 12 :46 "A bond is the typed relation between two operands. There are seven, they are not interchangeable, and which one holds is not recoverable from the operands."
> Fig 12 :94 "The arrangement is the substance."
> Fig 12 :95 "Two entries with an identical ingredient set are not variants of one thing. They are different things whose notation cannot tell them apart, and the difference is carried entirely by the bond."

A receiver can have every ingredient (the signal arrived) and still not have the arrangement (the meaning), with "nothing signals that anything was lost". This is the flat-formula collapse Fig 12 :6-17 names. It is the structural reason a channel check cannot settle a meaning question, and why "the marginal bits that would decide meaning are not a larger version of the bits that decide arrival".

The substrate states the same split in its own vocabulary:

> Fig 13 :16 "it names what is being measured"
> Fig 13 :26 "it decides what the measurement would mean"

States name the measurement; structures decide what it would mean. Fig 13 :29-30: a state with no structure has no magnitude and a structure with no state is empty. **Mode: `general` for the structural claim, `specified` for the operational consequence.** Triangulated across Fig 12 and Fig 13 (different figures, different objects: bonds versus the six-term set).

**Refuter for F4:** a representation in which the bond/arrangement is recoverable from the operand set alone, so identical ingredients always fix one meaning. That breaks Fig 12 :46 and :95 and removes the seam.

### F5: agreement is the wrong test for reception and the right test for a convention, never for truth

> Fig 2 :39 "Correlation has valence."
> Fig 2 :40 "agreement about the world is worthless when the agreeing frames share an evidence pool"
> Fig 2 :41 "efficient for a convention: agreement about a shared meaning is the product, not a corruption of it"
> Fig 2 :35 "Alignment is triangulation, not negotiation. A mirror cannot correct you."
> Fig 2 :45 "Frames range against the ground. They do not negotiate with it."

For U_reception, agreement between readers is worthless: if they share a pool, they are "one frame wearing many names" (Fig 8 :48-49), and a mirror cannot correct you. For U_meaning, agreement is the product: a convention is a shared meaning across a seam, and nothing else can see across one (Seat Map :67). But the convention must not be allowed to decide an outcome:

> Fig 10 :46 "A convention makes no claim about the world; a verdict and an atom both do."
> Fig 10 :47 "a convention that decides an outcome has stopped being one"
> Fig 10 :49 "Declaring a meaning does not ground it."

So the correct handling of a meaning convention is to record it as an authored convention, with provenance, and never to promote it to a verdict about whether the signal arrived or what the world is. **Mode: `specified`.** Triangulated across Fig 2 and Fig 10 (two different figures and different failure analyses).

**Refuter for F5:** a case in which shared agreement between two frames from one evidence pool settles whether a signal arrived, or in which a declared convention is a grounded verdict. That breaks Fig 2 :40 and Fig 10 :47/:49.

### F6: the union case must be unbundled, and filters must not hand the verdict

> Fig 9 :54 "And when the split lands on a union rather than a partition, unbundle before you search again."
> Fig 9 :57 "a case in two conditions is not a harder case of either. It stops being a mixture and becomes a specific"
> Fig 9 :60 "Use filters for the budget and witnesses for the verdict. Never let a filter hand you a verdict."

When both U_reception and U_meaning are present on one slot, the remedy is not a single bigger investigation. Unbundle: run the known-positive channel test first, then treat the remaining seam as its own compound with its own loop. Cost estimates, tier labels and confidence scores are filters for the budget, never witnesses for the verdict.

**Refuter for F6:** a single scalar uncertainty measure in which every increase in reception quality reduces meaning uncertainty monotonically, so the compound is really one quantity and no unbundling is owed. That breaks Fig 9 :57.

### F8: the round trip is lossy and must be recorded, not assumed

Compressing the distinction into a slogan such as "syntax versus semantics" drops the anchor rule (the ground does not decay), the per-slot requirement, the dependency order, and the convention/verdict valence. The round-trip gap `R_T` is enumerated in `11_audit.md` §Round trip gap. **Mode: `general`.**

## 4. Pre-registered refuter (written before the conclusion)

Per Seat Map :287: "And a falsifier written before the run is a design instrument, not only an honesty device." These were written before the recommendation in §5:

1. **R1:** A case where the meaning of a signal is fully determined by its reception probability, so the seam panel is unnecessary and a declaration of meaning grounds it. Breaks F1/F4.
2. **R2:** A case where a convention can decide an outcome and still count as a convention, or where declaring a meaning grounds it. Breaks F5.
3. **R3:** A case where a reading taken while R does not arrive discriminates between meanings, or where refining an already-returning instrument resolves a pure seam disagreement. Breaks F3.
4. **R4:** A single scalar uncertainty measure in which more reception always reduces meaning uncertainty, so the compound is one quantity. Breaks F6.
5. **R5:** A global channel fit that passes while no reading reaches a particular slot, with the channel still counted as live. Breaks F2.

If any fires, the distinction as stated is withdrawn or narrowed, and the retraction is recorded below rather than silently edited away.

## 5. Recommendation

1. State the residual per slot before discussing meaning (F2). A global average is not a channel check.
2. Run the channel test first with a known-positive fixture (F3). If contact is lost, stop semantic work and restore the reading.
3. If the channel is live and meaning is still uncertain, treat it as a seam, not an instrument problem (F1, F3, F4). Author a convention that makes the disagreement statable, with provenance, and refuse to promote it to a verdict (F5).
4. If both hold, unbundle before searching (F6).
5. Send the generator, not a recording: the two tests, the refuters and the figure lines, in that order, before any source link (Fig 4 :22-25; Fig 10 :52).

### Seven-question recursive derivation (summary)

1. **Is R arriving?** Per slot, not aggregated (Fig 1 :19). The ground does not decay, the channel does (Fig 10 :24).
2. **What is the output if not?** A verdict on the connection: contact lost, restore the reading (Seat Map :93).
3. **Can meaning be read while that fails?** No. A reading below the break is a reading of nothing (Fig 3 :22-23).
4. **If the channel is live, why is meaning still uncertain?** The arrangement is not recoverable from the ingredients, and nothing signals the loss (Fig 12 :14, :16, :46).
5. **Can more of the same channel close it?** No. An instrument is improved from a worse one already returning something (Fig 6 :32). This is a seam, so author a convention to make the bug statable (Fig 10 :16-17).
6. **Does the convention ground meaning?** No. Declaring a meaning does not ground it (Fig 10 :49); a convention that decides an outcome has stopped being one (Fig 10 :47).
7. **What would close the seam?** A source-discriminating instrument, provenanced and shadow-tested (Fig 6 :12-13; Fig 8 :50-52), or settlement by the ground. Unreached, not unreachable: the instrument is not held, so the slot is recorded, not named.

### Updated verdict

Strains after re-census: **none live**. The open item is not conceptual but instrumental: no source-discriminating instrument was run here, so the answer is `specified` and `candidate`, not `grounded`.
