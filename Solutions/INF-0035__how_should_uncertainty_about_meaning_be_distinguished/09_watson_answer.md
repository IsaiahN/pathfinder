## QUESTION

Decide, in effect terms, what the Tether says about two uncertainties that register on one object slot: uncertainty about whether a signal was received, and uncertainty about what the signal means. Option A: treat them as two ends of one uncertainty scale and apply one remedy, typically more data or more interpretation. Option B: treat them as two failure genera with different outputs, different anchors' readings and opposed remedies, ordered so that contact is checked before meaning. What each leads to: A spends a convention over a dead channel or imports an instrument for a pure seam problem, and the residual persists without trending down; B gives a per-slot channel test, a seam procedure, a union rule and refuters written in advance.

## DOMAIN CENSUS

- **R (the residual).** The gap between prediction and observation at a slot. Arity: two conditions, so a union compound (Fig 9 :57 "a case in two conditions is not a harder case of either"). Symmetry: ordered, because reception is upstream of meaning (Fig 3). Scale: adjacent links of one chain, perception then vocabulary, with the seam at their crossing.
- **Domain 1, the channel (contact).** Actors: the ground, the channel, the sensor, the maintainer. Currency: whether R arrives at slot s. Anchor: the ground, which does not decay. This is a contact question, so the output is a verdict on the connection, not the world. Nameable anchor: yes.
- **Domain 2, the seam (meaning).** Actors: two frames, a reading, a convention. Currency: shared meaning. Anchor: the ground reached through a reading that underdetermines its source. Nameable anchor: yes, but only through a discriminator that is not held.
- **Domain 3, the checking arrangement.** Actors: maker, maintainer, outside frame. Currency: residuals and agreement. Anchor: the ground, never agreement. In this simulated run all seats draw on one evidence pool, so this domain's independence is zero.
- **Order by dependency.** Check contact per slot before reading meaning (Fig 3 :19, :22-23); bring a characterised residual, never the source, across the membrane (Fig 10 :52); describe the residual before choosing a frame (Fig 9 :27-28).

## VERDICT (from the figures)

The distinction survives. The answer is a `specified`, ungrounded procedure: check contact per slot with a known-positive fixture before doing any meaning work; if contact is lost, the output is a connection verdict and the remedy is to restore the reading; if the channel is live and meaning is still uncertain, the problem is a seam, remedied by a convention that makes the disagreement statable with provenance, never by a declaration that grounds meaning and never by more bandwidth. The two are ordered, not isomeric, and can co-occur as a union that must be unbundled. Agreement checks conventions, not signals. The slot for a source-discriminating instrument stays open. The "syntax versus semantics" shorthand is refused as an adopted import with no source gate, and the isomer reading of `06_isomers_and_order.md` Q1 is retracted in favour of the dependency-order reading.

- **cost if wrong:** bounded and locatable. The packet labels every claim with a mode, states five refuters before the conclusion, and keeps the verdicts in separate columns, so an error is findable rather than invisible. The real cost is applying the wrong remedy: a convention built over a dead channel (a reading of nothing, Fig 3 :23), or an unnecessary instrument imported for a seam problem (a wrong atom, Fig 6 :24 and Fig 8 :42-43). Both are avoidable by running the two tests in order, and both leave a residual that persists and does not trend down, which is the detector.
- **undoable:** yes. The two tests are separable and can be re-run after the fact; no primitive has to be withdrawn, and a misapplied convention can be demoted back to a convention with its provenance intact. A sent claim can be superseded by the corrected packet. What is not undoable is a reader's independence once the framework's interiors have been shown before a verdict (Fig 10 :42), so the two-stage order is the part to get right the first time.
- **reversed by:** a ground reading, not agreement. A per-slot known-positive fixture that passes where contact was thought lost; a source-discriminating instrument that maps readings to sources, provenanced and shadow-tested; a demonstration that meaning is fully determined by reception probability, or that a declaration grounds meaning; a single scalar uncertainty in which more reception always reduces meaning uncertainty. Any of these fires the standing refuters in `01_figure_grounded_audit.md` §4 and changes the verdict. Until one fires, the result stands as `candidate`.
