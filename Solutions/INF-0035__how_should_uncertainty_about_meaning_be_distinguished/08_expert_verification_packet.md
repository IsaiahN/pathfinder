# Expert verification packet: the Tether on uncertainty about meaning versus uncertainty about reception

**What this is:** a claim in a form a researcher can check. Mode, derivation and refuters are stated before the conclusion, so a reader knows what to look for and what would settle it.

**Mode: `specified` on the operational distinction, `general` on the structural shape, not `grounded`.** No per-slot channel measurement is claimed. This is a procedure and a route, not a result.

## 1. The claim, in one paragraph

Reception uncertainty and meaning uncertainty are two failure genera, not two ends of one uncertainty scale. Reception uncertainty is a verdict on the connection to the ground: check it per slot with a known-positive fixture, and if contact is lost, restore the reading rather than adjudicate what it would have said. Meaning uncertainty survives a live channel because the arrangement that carries meaning is not recoverable from the ingredients, and nothing signals the loss; its remedy is a convention that makes the disagreement statable, with provenance, never a declaration that grounds meaning and never more bandwidth. The two are ordered: reception is upstream, because a reading taken below a broken channel is a reading of nothing. Agreement between readers is the product of a convention about meaning and worthless as a check on whether a signal arrived. When both failures are present, unbundle them before searching.

## 2. Three checkable sub-claims

### C1: framework-internal; checkable by reading Figures 10 and 1

- **Statement:** the two uncertainties are two offices with different outputs. Channel uncertainty is measured per slot and outputs "contact has been lost"; meaning uncertainty is a seam that outputs a convention making an unstatable disagreement statable.
- **Source, verbatim:** Fig 10 :4 "1 · the seam"; Fig 10 :5 "2 · the channel"; Fig 10 :13 "two frames, one field, no shared reading"; Fig 10 :14 "a disagreement nothing can state"; Fig 10 :18 "a declared meaning is not a grounded one"; Fig 10 :24 "the ground does not decay, the channel does". Also Fig 1 :19 "The residual is indexed by object slot, not aggregated."
- **Seat map corroboration (not a figure line):** Seat Map :93 states that the maintainer's output "is not a verdict on the world but a verdict on the connection: contact has been lost, and the remedy is to restore the reading, not to adjudicate what it would have said."
- **Refuter:** show a case where meaning is fully determined by reception probability, so no seam office is needed and a declaration of meaning grounds it; or show a global channel fit that passes while no reading reaches a particular slot, with the channel still counted as live. Either breaks the two-office or the per-slot claim.

### C2: framework-internal derivation; checkable by reading Figures 3, 12 and 6

- **Statement:** reception is upstream of meaning, and the two remedies are opposed. A reading taken while no signal arrives is not weak but void. A live channel returning a reading cannot, by more of the same refinement, resolve a seam.
- **Source, verbatim:** Fig 3 :19 "Optimising a link that already works cannot rescue one that does not." Fig 3 :22-23 "A step whose input never arrives cannot be diagnosed, only its predecessor can. Which is why a reading taken below the break is not a weak" / "reading. It is a reading of nothing." Fig 12 :14-16 "the ingredients are recorded and the arrangement is not" / "and nothing signals that anything was lost". Fig 6 :32 "An instrument is not built from a description. It is improved from a worse instrument already returning something."
- **Refuter:** a case where a reading taken while R does not arrive still discriminates between meanings, or where refining an already-returning instrument resolves a pure seam disagreement. That breaks Fig 3 :22-23 or Fig 6 :32 and collapses the opposed remedies.

### C3: a valence claim; checkable by reading Figures 2, 9 and 10

- **Statement:** agreement between readers is the right product for a convention about meaning and the wrong test for whether a signal arrived; the two uncertainties can co-occur and must then be unbundled.
- **Source, verbatim:** Fig 2 :40 "agreement about the world is worthless when the agreeing frames share an evidence pool"; Fig 2 :41 "efficient for a convention: agreement about a shared meaning is the product, not a corruption of it"; Fig 10 :47 "a convention that decides an outcome has stopped being one"; Fig 9 :54 "And when the split lands on a union rather than a partition, unbundle before you search again."
- **Refuter:** a case where agreement between two frames sharing one evidence pool settles whether a signal arrived, or a single scalar uncertainty in which more reception always reduces meaning uncertainty so no unbundling is owed. That breaks Fig 2 :40 or Fig 9 :54.

## 3. What is explicitly not claimed

- Not a new measure of semantic content, and not a reduction of meaning to channel statistics.
- Not that "syntax versus semantics" is the figures' vocabulary. That label is an adopted import with no source gate; the figures speak of the seam and the channel.
- Not that more bandwidth, a larger corpus or a better code resolves a seam.
- Not that a convention grounds meaning or checks the arrival of a signal. Fig 10 :49: "Declaring a meaning does not ground it."
- Not that the source-discriminating instrument is known. The slot is recorded, not filled (Fig 13 :134-135).
- Not `grounded`. Nothing here is asserted as settled by a measurement.
- Not a claim that the framework validates itself. *The Recursive Self-Improvement Trap*: "the output is only as good as the validator. The agent, or the tool, or the frame, or the theory, cannot validate itself."

## 5. What would settle it

A per-slot channel check and a source-discriminating instrument, not a further argument. From *The Hard Problem is About Source*: an instrument that reads green "cannot comment on the source", and an instrument that cannot distinguish the cases "demonstrates its own inadequacy". The case was resolved "not by argument, but by the arrival of a better instrument." Here:

1. A known-positive fixture at each slot read through the same channel. Failure means contact lost and all semantic conclusions drawn meanwhile are void.
2. An instrument that discriminates among sources producing the same reading. Its shape is recorded; its name is not asserted.
3. A provenance and shadow test for any imported discriminator (Fig 6 :12-13; Fig 8 :50-52).
4. The five standing refuters in `01_figure_grounded_audit.md` §4.

## 6. What an expert can check in twenty minutes

1. Read Fig 10 :4-18 and :23-28, Fig 3 :19-23, Fig 12 :14-16 and Fig 2 :39-41. Six short passages.
2. Check C2 against your own field: is there a case where a reading taken with no arriving signal still discriminates meaning, or where more of the same instrument resolves a seam? If yes, the order is wrong.
3. Name one instrument that would discriminate sources for a reading in your domain, or say why none is possible. If you name one, the framework's slot is filled by an import and must be provenanced.
4. Send the refutation. A failure is more useful than an endorsement.
