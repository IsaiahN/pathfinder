## The question restated as an effect

The ordinary word doing the work is **coherent**. It has at least two readings that the figures keep apart:

1. **Internally self-consistent over time.** The system's account of itself at t2 agrees with its account at t1. This is a symmetric, stability-shaped property.
2. **Ranged against something that does not move because the system moves.** The account stays in contact with an anchor that is not one of its own frames. The figures call this triangulation.

Option A: read "coherent" as (1) alone, and answer yes whenever the self-description persists through learning and interaction. What it leads to: the system can be perfectly self-consistent while its reference drifts, and nobody notices, because the drift is coherent (Fig 2 :34). This is a named failure genus, not a success.

Option B: re-ground "coherent" as (2), keep the two conditions of the question in a union, and ask for the shape of an invariant that survives change without a self-authoured reference. What it leads to: a checkable shape, refuters written in advance, and an honest stop where no figure line supplies the content of the self-model or a universal coherence metric.

This solution derives B. The residual is not "how do we keep the self-image stable". It is: what would have to be true of a system's self-contact so that persistence of the account is evidence of anything at all.

**What an answer would look like, bound as an ordinary term before the run:** a four-condition separator (per-slot indexing, a non-updating reference outside the model, a maintained channel, an outside verifier), with the literal list of self-model contents and any coherence number left as an explicit `gap`. Binding this before the run is the formula's "Direction" discipline, so the sought-for shape can accrue a residual like any other term (Fig 5 :65).

## What is deliberately not here

- No personal emails of named individuals.
- No ranking of people by how likely they are to agree.
- No bulk-send plan.

Three reasons in the figures' own words:

1. **A room does not upgrade the label.** "A room's agreement is variation, not a verdict. Selection between rooms is where the ground votes." (Fig 7 :35)
2. **Shared evidence disqualifies agreement as a verdict.** "agreement about the world is worthless when the agreeing frames share an evidence pool" (Fig 2 :40)
3. **Asking for trust before a check spends independence.** "A verifier that can reconstruct the claim cannot verify it." (Fig 10 :42)

Legally: scraping personal emails for unsolicited bulk outreach breaches GDPR and CAN-SPAM style rules in many jurisdictions, and it will get the sending domain blacklisted. That is a practical failure on top of the structural one.
