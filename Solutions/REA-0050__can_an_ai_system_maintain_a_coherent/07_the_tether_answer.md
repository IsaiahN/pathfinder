# The Tether's answer: can a system maintain a coherent self-model as it changes?

**Mode: `specified` on the separator and the per-domain verdicts, `general` on the structural shape, `gap` on self-model content and on any universal coherence metric. Not `grounded`.** This is a shape and a route, not a measurement. Simulated derivation checked against the figures (`05_what_shape_means.md` section 7), not a ground reading.

## 1. The residual it answers

"Can an AI system maintain a coherent model of itself as it changes through learning and interaction?" asks whether persistence of a self-account is evidence of contact with the system as it actually is. The residual is per slot: `R_s = |Gamma(b,a) - o'|` for the system's own state or capability. Fig 1 :19: "The residual is indexed by object slot, not aggregated." Fig 1 :27: "They are frame-internal. A frame cannot score itself with a quantity it also produces."

The question is a **union of two conditions**, not one dial. Fig 9 :54: "And when the split lands on a union rather than a partition, unbundle before you search again." One lever (more consistency training) leaves the other condition (contact with a non-updating reference) standing.

## 2. The verdict, per domain, kept separate

**D1, the self-model as a library term. `specified`, triangulated.** A self-model is a molecule in the library Gamma, composed from held atoms and bonds and indexed per slot. It can be settled or not. Settlement is necessary but not sufficient for something to be composed over: Fig 5 :69, "settling is necessary for a term to be composed over, and it is not sufficient." Over a fixed atom set the before and after self-models are isomers (Fig 12 :117: "isomer. Same atoms, different bond, different substance."). When learning mints or refuses, the relation is composition plus import or subtraction instead.

**D2, the self-contact channel. `specified`, triangulated.** "Coherent" must be re-grounded. Fig 2 :34: "everything drifts together, and the drift is coherent, which is why nobody notices." A self-account kept consistent by mutual update is the named failure, not a success. What survives is triangulation: "Alignment is triangulation, not negotiation. A mirror cannot correct you." (Fig 2 :35). The anchor cannot be a term inside the model: "The anchor must not update." (Fig 2 :33). And the channel is the part that fails: "the ground does not decay, the channel does" (Fig 10 :24).

**D3, change through learning. `specified`, triangulated.** Persistence of the record is not persistence of the reach: "What is recorded only grows. What is reachable is derived, and it can fall." (Fig 6 :42). A self-model that is replayed from storage is "a description of one occasion" (Fig 4 :25), not a generator. What must cross a scale boundary is "Only methods cross up. Only head starts come down." (Fig 4 :22). Weight updates that do not change contact do not change capability: "An improvement that does not change contact changes nothing, however much it improves." (Fig 11 :40).

**D4, verification. `general` structural, `specified` in application, triangulated.** A self-check is a frame certifying its own limit: "A frame cannot certify its own limit." (Fig 8 :37). "A verifier that can reconstruct the claim cannot verify it." (Fig 10 :42). Self-agreement is one evidence pool talking: "agreement about the world is worthless when the agreeing frames share an evidence pool" (Fig 2 :40). The check must come from a seat that cannot produce what it checks.

**The content gap. `gap`, after re-census.** No figure line returns a list of what a self-model must contain, a slot count, or a coherence number. Fig 13 :134: "Unreachable is a verdict here, not a silence". Fig 9 :32: "you get a kind of thing, never the answer itself". The figure returns the shape of the invariant, not a design. "Unreachable is a verdict, not a silence."

The verdicts above are NOT averaged into a yes or no. That is the rule: verdicts per domain stay separate.

## 3. The shape, in one list

An AI system can maintain a checkable, non-drifting self-contact, in the `specified` sense, only if:

1. the residual is indexed per slot, not pooled into a single coherence score;
2. the reference for "what I am" is outside the model and does not update on the model's own report;
3. the channel to that reference is kept readable by a real instrument, with the round trip measured;
4. the self-model is a method or generator, not a recording of one occasion;
5. a refusal is possible, because a vocabulary without negation can only accumulate (Operators :58);
6. the check is made by a verifier that cannot reconstruct the claim, and the verdict is recorded before any theory is shown.

Without 2, "coherent" means coherent drift. Without 3, contact is lost while the record still looks complete. Without 6, the system is certifying its own limit.

## 4. What it explicitly does not say

- Not a yes to the ordinary reading of "coherent". Internal consistency by self-report is refused as a verdict.
- Not a `grounded` result. No instrument was run on any system.
- Not a design: no architecture, representation, loss function, memory module or coherence coefficient is recommended. The content is a declared gap.
- Not a claim that a system has or lacks a "self" in any metaphysical sense. The question is re-grounded as a contact question, not answered as a substrate question.
- Not "self-models are impossible". A checkable self-contact is possible in shape. What is impossible under the figures is a coherence claim whose reference is authored by the claimant.
- Not a theory of everything. Fig 7 :6 names "the limit: no last room".
- Not a derivation from catastrophic forgetting or the mirror test. Those are secondary corroboration, not the verdict's source.

## 5. What would settle it, the ground

A source-discriminating instrument, not a further argument. *The Hard Problem is About Source* (opened this session) states that an instrument reading green "cannot comment on the source", and "An instrument that can't distinguish these cases demonstrates its own inadequacy." The matching questions here:

- **Slot discriminator:** does a reading of the system's self-account distinguish the system's actual capability from its self-report, where several accounts could produce the same reading? The two-rater discrepancy in the "Self-awareness" source (accessed 2026-11-23) is the human-clinical shape of such an instrument. Fig 5 :62: "A frame searching for an answer of a particular shape holds that shape as a PREFERENCE rather than as a term."
- **Non-updating reference test:** is there a reference at the slot whose answer does not move when the system's report moves? Fig 2 :33.
- **Channel test:** does the residual keep arriving after N updates, or do saturated metrics and pooled readings take over (Fig 10 :25-26)?
- **Verifier non-access:** can the verifier produce the self-account it checks? If yes, it cannot verify it (Fig 10 :42).
- **Standing falsifiers from `01` section 4:** an external-anchor success with no system-authored reference; a distinct-mechanism coherence metric; a governing figure line for self-model content; reach growing identically with record across refutations; a source-discriminating instrument for self-recognition.

## 7. One-paragraph version for outreach

> The question is a union, not one dial. A self-account that stays internally consistent by being updated on itself is coherent drift, a named failure in the figures: "everything drifts together, and the drift is coherent, which is why nobody notices" (Fig 2 :34). What can be maintained is a different thing: contact between the self-account and a reference that does not change because the system changes, measured per slot, with the channel kept readable and the check made from outside the frame. The record of what a system believes can keep growing while what it can reach falls, so counting terms is not counting progress. The framework returns the shape of that invariant, four conditions and six tests, and refuses to name the representation, the metric or the self-model's contents. That gap is reported, not filled. Prestige, self-report and internal agreement are not instruments. A failure found against the refuters written in advance is more useful than agreement.
