## 1. A shape is a constraint, not a filler

The canonical figures README states the limit: the framework returns "what a missing thing would have to look like (a shape, not a name)". Fig 9 :32: "you get a kind of thing, never the answer itself". Fig 13 :135: "It cannot say which primitive is missing, because naming it would mean already holding it."

From the *Library of Babel* essay, opened this session: "you can cut your search short if the set of books returned is small and descriptive enough that it provides a general shape of the answer that satisfies your question."

For REA-0050 a **shape** is the four-condition separator in `01`: per-slot indexing, an external non-updating reference, a maintained channel, an outside verifier. It constrains any acceptable answer about a coherent self-model. It does not say which representation, architecture, or metric occupies the slots. "The separator is the shape. 'Use a world model' or 'use a self-supervised objective' is a name."

## 2. How the shape is obtained

- *Library of Babel*, opened this session: "With each new term gained, you are able to narrow the space by definition and deepen your vocabulary." The method is proximity: start with the vocabulary you have, look for recurring terms that overlap with it, recurse.
- Fig 6 :30: "outward: does another frame already hold it? The description travels. Fails by being sharpened into terms only we can read. Cast it in effect terms."
- Fig 9 :30-31: "your words are borrowed too, so describe the effect rather than the cause."
- The same essay, carried from the exemplar's mapping of *Decoding Tacit Knowledge*: "When contact isn't possible, the best you can send is a plain description of what you'd expect to see, because anyone can check that against the world."
- *Library of Babel*, opened this session, on bundling: "Perhaps your problem was a bundle or conflation of several problems masquerading as one?" This is the direct warrant for treating "coherent" and "as it changes" as a union in `01` section 0.

## 3. What licenses a name

Only two things, both outside the shape:

1. **The ground settles it.** `THE_FORMULA.md` step 5: "the ground settles it. Verified only by what pays. A gate is not the ground." The canonical README says the framework "stops at the limit of what the available instruments can distinguish, and records the lineage so the stop is honest."
2. **A provenanced import from a frame whose closure differs** (Fig 6 :23, Fig 8 :50-52), with a source gate (provenance) and a shadow test (explains a gap).

The carried reading of *They're Scanning Books When They Should* sharpens this: "The real import is not a new term in the existing space. It is a new dimension of the space." Also: "Declaring the primitives does not make them neutral. It makes the choice visible." For this question, saying "the self-model is the vector h_t" is a binding from the machine-learning frame onto held library terms. It is not a seventh primitive and it is not a derivation.

## 4. Why "self-recognition behaviour" is not the source

This is the question's own metamerism trap, and the *Hard Problem is About Source* essay, opened this session, gives the mechanism. An instrument reading green "cannot comment on the source": one green light, or yellow plus blue, or RGB, or CMYK, or a scheme not yet discovered. "If several paths create the same behavior, then behavior is a projection with lossy information." "An instrument that can't distinguish these cases demonstrates its own inadequacy." It was the spectrometer, not an argument, that resolved it.

The world source *Mirror test* supplies the matching case: a mark-directed response can be read as self-recognition, or as the animal treating a controllable image (Povinelli's alternative), and a failure to touch the mark "does not prove that the animal does not recognize themselves." The behavioural reading is a projection. What is owed is a discriminator, not a name. This is F3 in `01`.

## 5. Correction to my own first reading (kept visible)

My first pass applied "shape, not a name" as a blanket ban on describing a self-model at all. That was an overstatement, the same error the exemplar records and corrects in its `05` section 5b. The README's phrase governs a **missing** thing. A self-model is a binding from the AI frame onto atoms already held in the library (Fig 13 :115: "The set is closed. The arrangements are not."). Naming the binding is provenance. Claiming the binding adds a new primitive, or that it moves the wall, is what is forbidden (Fig 6 :22: "IMPORT adds an atom, and the added atom is what expands the closure." this binding adds none). The gap in F10 is about which bindings and how many, not about whether a self-model may be spoken of.

I also first read "coherent" as a desirable invariance. Re-census reversed that: Fig 2 :4-5 names mutual update as "collapse 1" and :34 makes coherence the signature of the drift. The term is refused and re-grounded in `01` F1 and F2. Both readings are recorded so neither is quietly lost.

## 6. Prestige is not action

From the *Recursive Self-Improvement Trap*, opened this session: "Prestige must never be allowed to validate an action directly... Prestige can fund an action. It can give an action a budget to be tried. But it cannot settle an action. Only the ground can settle it. The Ground Maintainer must be blind to prestige."

A list of famous researchers who agree that systems have coherent self-models is the named collapse, not corroboration. The legitimate ask gives the claim a budget to be tried and lets an instrument settle it.

## 7. My own audit is a simulation, not a ground reading

Same essay, opened this session: "LLMs can quickly simulate an approximate answer/output with the formula... However, the truly testable output would need to be derived from the real architecture or working the formula yourself." The essay also states: "the output is only as good as the validator. The agent, or the tool, or the frame, or the theory, cannot validate itself. An external validator, or ground, is always needed to tether the veracity of the output to reality. Anything else is drift."

So `01` and every other substantive file here are simulated derivations checked for internal consistency against the figures. They are NOT a test of any AI system and must be labelled as such. They cannot settle the question they report on.
