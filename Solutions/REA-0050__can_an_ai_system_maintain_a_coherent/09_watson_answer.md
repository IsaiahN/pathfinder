## QUESTION

Decide, in effect terms, whether a system can keep a coherent account of itself while it changes through learning and interaction. Option A: read "coherent" as internal consistency across time and answer yes whenever the self-description persists. What it leads to: the account can stay consistent while its reference drifts, and nobody notices because the drift is coherent (Fig 2 :34). Option B: refuse that reading, split the question into its two conditions, and ask for the shape of a self-contact that survives change without a self-authored reference. What it leads to: a four-condition separator, refuters written in advance, and an honest stop where no figure line supplies the self-model's contents or a coherence number. This run derives B.

## VERDICT (from the figures)

The unqualified yes is refused. The question is a union of conditions with different anchors. A self-account coherent by mutual update is coherent drift, a named failure. What can be maintained is a non-drifting self-contact: a self-account ranged against a reference that does not update on it, indexed per slot, through a channel kept readable, checked by a verifier that cannot reconstruct the claim, with the self-model acting as a generator rather than a recording and with negation available so a refused self-claim can be carried. Over a fixed atom set the before and after self-models are isomers. When learning mints or refuses, that relation fails. No figure line returns a self-model's contents, a slot count or a coherence metric, so those remain a declared `gap` of the shape-not-a-name kind. The verdict is `specified`, ungrounded, candidate. Prestige, self-report and internal agreement are not instruments.

- **cost if wrong:** bounded and locatable if the offer is made correctly. The packet states mode, refuters and provenance before the conclusion, so an error is findable rather than invisible. The real cost is telling a self-modelling field that coherence is the enemy when it is actually the symptom of a broken reference, and doing so without a discriminator. Fig 2 :42 says "Buy independence in proportion to the cost of being wrong." That cost is reduced by leading with the shape and its refuter and by sourcing the discriminator before any claim is published.
- **undoable:** yes. The separator can be narrowed without touching the figure text. If a governing line for self-model content is found, F10 closes; if an external-anchor success is shown, F1 and F2 narrow to the cases where the reference is authored by the system. No primitive has to be withdrawn, and a sent claim can be superseded by a corrected packet.
- **reversed by:** a ground reading, not agreement. A source-discriminating instrument at the self-slot that distinguishes self-report from actual capability; a reference whose answer demonstrably does not move when the report moves; a channel that keeps returning a residual across a stated number of updates; a verifier that cannot produce what it checks; a verbatim figure line governing self-model content; or a case where reach grows identically with record across refutations. Until one of these fires, the verdict stands as `candidate` and the content question stands as `gap`.
