## Independent domain census (written from the residual line alone)

I redid the census before re-reading the primary's findings, using the WATSON order.

- **R (arity / symmetry / scale).** The residual is the gap between what a public communication leads an audience to
  treat as settled and what the ground settles. Arity: several claims bundled (existence, cause, magnitude, response),
  so a union compound under Fig 9 :4-5. Symmetry: symmetric presentation of voices versus asymmetric correction by a
  ground. Scale: first order inside a discipline, second order at the interface about who may speak. Secondary
  observation, reached independently: the union is not just a bundle of topics but a bundle of **different anchors**.
  D1 has a measurement; D2 borrows it or loses it; D3 has the factual ground plus a convention that must not decide;
  D4 has no anchor at all. That is a stronger reason for the union reading than topic multiplicity alone.
- **D1, evidence frame.** Actors: researchers, instruments, data. Currency: residuals. Anchor: measurement. Domain.
- **D2, public and media frame.** Actors: editors, platforms, audiences. Currency: agreement and attention. Anchor:
  borrowed from D1 only if separately ranged; otherwise none. Conditionally a domain.
- **D3, policy frame.** Actors: committees, regulators. Currency: decisions and conventions. Anchor: the factual
  ground, with the convention barred from deciding the outcome. Conditionally a domain.
- **D4, stake frame.** Actors: funders, campaigns. Currency: influence. Anchor: none. Not a domain; a stake channel.
- **Partition or union:** union. The overlaps are D1/D4 (a funded researcher), D2/D4 (a financed voice), D2/D3 (a
  committee using public framing). One lever cannot clear a union compound.
- **Order by dependency:** perceive the separate claims, build refutation vocabulary, state what counts as progress,
  plan the communication, carry the method. Refuse the false open term before minting any new message. Describe the
  residual before selecting a frame.

**Where my census differs from the primary's.** The primary reached the union reading from Fig 9's split rule and the
failure of "the controversy" as one slot. I reached it from anchor heterogeneity: the four domains do not share one
thing that settles them. Different starting figure (Fig 2 anchor definition plus Fig 9), same domain boundaries. Low
overlap between routes inside the framework, so the agreement is weakly non-circular, but still one evidence pool.

## Round trip gap R_T

The Symbols table defines R_T as gap(x, (T_E o T_A)(x)), the amount the coarser description cannot hold. Fig 4 :30
requires it to be measured rather than assumed. I ran the round trip on the condensation this folder actually uses.

1. **Sent up (x):** the verbatim figure set, chiefly Fig 2 :29-30, :35, :40-41, :46; Fig 7 :35-36; Fig 9 :4-5,
   :27-31; Fig 10 :24, :52, :59-60; Fig 11 :40; Fig 12 :117.
2. **Abstraction (T_A):** the shorthand carried in `07`, namely: split the dispute, balance is a convention, the
   channel decays, declare the stake, pre-register the refuter, report a shape.
3. **Concretisation (T_E):** a concrete communication guideline for a public statement about a contested scientific
   claim.

**R_T is nonzero.** Four things the shorthand holds do not survive the trip back down:

- **The anchor's legitimacy condition.** Fig 2 :36-38 requires the question to be constitutively about the anchor, and
  warns that an instrument can be wrong on axes it does not measure. "Range against a ground" does not say which
  ground is legitimate for a public claim about values and policy.
- **Per-slot indexing.** The shorthand says "split the dispute"; it drops Fig 1 :19's requirement that R is measured
  per slot and Fig 1 :22's warning about averaging. A guideline can still tell people to publish one "understanding
  score" and think it has followed the shorthand.
- **Refusal before mint.** The shorthand says "write a refuter"; it drops the ordering in `THE_FORMULA.md` step 2.
  A refuter written after the claim has different standing.
- **The convention/verdict boundary.** The shorthand says "balance is a convention"; it drops Fig 2 :41's explicit
  permission, which applies to shared meaning. Without that clause the guideline reads as a ban on all agreement,
  which the figures do not impose.

One thing survives cleanly: a ground that is not a party, frames ranged separately against it, and a channel that can
be lost. That is the residue worth sending. R_T was **not** measured numerically, because no ground reading exists; it
is recorded qualitatively as the enumerated set of dropped constraints. Treating "the formula is the condensed version
of the metalaws" as identity would set R_T to zero and would be the dead-reckoning error Fig 4 :30 names.
