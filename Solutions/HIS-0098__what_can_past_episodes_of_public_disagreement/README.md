## What an answer would look like (bound as an ordinary term)

An answer is in shape form when it states what any defensible practice of communicating uncertainty must do before it
names a slogan, a label system, or a campaign. In effect terms: given a public claim whose evidence is incomplete, an
answer must say how each frame is corrected, what the public is being asked to treat as settled, what would falsify the
claim, and where the stake is declared. A name, such as "use confidence intervals" or "adopt the deficit model", is not
the answer the figures can produce. It is a binding from a world frame and must be provenanced as such.

## Restatement as an effect

Given a disputed scientific claim, two practices are compared. Practice A stages balanced disagreement and supplies
more information, treating public agreement as the sign that uncertainty has been handled. Practice B ranges each
frame separately against a ground that does not update, states the residual per slot in effect terms, declares the
interest, and writes the refuter before the claim. A leads to a convention that can settle a public narrative without
ever consulting the thing the claim is about. B keeps the channel to the ground reachable and makes the remaining
uncertainty checkable. The residual R is the gap between what an audience takes the claim to settle and what the
evidence actually settles, indexed per claim, never averaged across a debate.

## What is deliberately not here

- No personal emails of named individuals.
- No ranking of people by how likely they are to agree.
- No bulk-send plan.

Reason, in the figures' own words: "Alignment is triangulation, not negotiation. A mirror cannot correct you."
(Fig 2 :35). Selecting recipients for receptiveness manufactures the correlation that makes their agreement worthless.
The public venues in `03` are opt-in submission and contact routes, not a list of believers.
