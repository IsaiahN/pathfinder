## Wider literature that bears on the answer

Four strands, deliberately chosen from different fields. Overlap is declared rather than hidden.

1. **History of science and ignorance studies.** *Merchants of Doubt* and *Agnotology* both document manufactured
   doubt across tobacco, environmental issues and climate. They share a evidence pool with each other (Proctor reviewed Oreskes
   and Conway), so they are one witness on the "hidden stake" claim, not two.
2. **Science communication research.** The *Information deficit model* and *False balance* pages disagree about what
   extra information does. That conflict is handled in `01` F10 and F4 by re-census against Fig 11 :40, not by
   choosing a favourite. JCOM's current articles show the same live tension in practice, for example the study on
   practical barriers to psychological inoculation and the content analysis of evidencing practices in genomic news.
3. **Risk and decision research.** SRA and the National Academies report supply the institutional framing: risk
   communication as a named specialty, and a research agenda for contentious topics. These are distinct hosts from the
   Wikipedia cluster, so their agreement is low-overlap corroboration.
4. **Human cognition and tacit knowledge.** *Cultural cognition* and *Decoding Tacit Knowledge* approach the same
   failure from experiments and from practice respectively: shared pretext decides what a message means, and more
   comprehension need not produce more agreement.

No world source was allowed to overrule a figure. Where a world source appeared to strain a figure, the census was
redone (F10); where two world sources conflicted, the conflict was recorded (F4, F10).

## Distribution: offer, do not push

Figure-grounded rules:

- Fig 10 :52: "Bring a characterised residual, never the source." Lead with the residual and the refuter.
- Fig 9 :27-28: "describe it before you go looking, or any frame you pick will seem to fit". Ask what the recipient
  would expect to see if the shape claim were true before sending any article.
- Fig 2 :35: "Alignment is triangulation, not negotiation. A mirror cannot correct you." Never ask for agreement or
  trust.
- Fig 2 :40: "agreement about the world is worthless when the agreeing frames share an evidence pool". Record the
  stage-1 verdict before any theory is shown; never pool later agreement.
- Fig 4 :22: "Only methods cross up. Only head starts come down." A method may be handed on; a recording of one
  campaign's success may not be pushed as knowledge.
- Fig 9 :60: "Use filters for the budget and witnesses for the verdict. Never let a filter hand you a verdict."
  Venue choice is a budget filter, never a witness.

**Recommended order in a reply:** (1) residual and shape claim; (2) the five refuters; (3) ask what the recipient would
count as a failure; (4) record that answer; (5) only then, and only if invited, the figure links; (6) offer the formula
as a method, with the instruction to state a mode and write a refuter before running it.

## The two-stage offer

**Stage 1.** Send the residual, the shape claim and the pre-registered refuter. No framework, no links, no request for
agreement. The recipient's verdict is recorded here, before seeing the source.

**Stage 2, opt-in.** If the recipient is curious, send the figure text and the verified links above, with the note
that the figures, not this summary, are the authority. Their later agreement is a conversation, not corroboration.

**Stage 3, optional.** Offer the method for reuse. Each user writes a refuter before running it and labels the mode of
what they get. From *The Recursive Self-Improvement Trap*: "the output is only as good as the validator" and "Metatheories
cannot validate themselves."

**No bulk send.** Messages go one-to-one through the public or opt-in routes in `03_public_contact_points.csv`.
Nothing is automated and no list of likely believers is assembled.
