## 1. A shape is a constraint that can be stated before a name is available

The canonical usage rule: the framework returns "what a missing thing would have to look like (a shape, not a name)".
Fig 9 :32: "you get a kind of thing, never the answer itself". Fig 13's commentary adds: "It cannot say which primitive
is missing, because naming it would mean already holding it."

From *The Library of Babel*: "you can cut your search short if the set of books returned is small and descriptive
enough that it provides a general shape of the answer that satisfies your question."

So a shape is the narrowed form of an answer that can be stated before it can be filled. It constrains; it does not
supply a slogan. For HIS-0098:

- **Shape:** any defensible practice must split the disagreement into slots, range frames separately against a ground
  that does not move, declare interests, state a refuter before the claim, and report the remaining uncertainty in
  effect terms.
- **Names the framework cannot derive:** a particular confidence-label scheme, a particular institutional format, a
  particular "consensus percentage", a particular campaign. Those are bindings from a world frame and must be
  provenanced as such.

## 2. How the shape was obtained

- *Library of Babel* prescribes proximity-based bootstrapping: use the vocabulary already held that is closest to the
  thing, then "With each new term gained, you narrow the space and deepen your vocabulary."
- The same essay gives the union warning that governs this question: "Perhaps your problem was a bundle or conflation
  of several problems masquerading as one?"
- Fig 6 :30: "The description travels. Fails by being sharpened into terms only we can read. Cast it in effect terms."
- Fig 9 :30-31: "your words are borrowed too, so describe the effect rather than the cause"
- *Decoding Tacit Knowledge*: "When contact isn't possible, the best you can send is a plain description of what
  you'd expect to see, because anyone can check that against the world."

In this solution the effect description is: what an audience would be expected to say, do, or predict if the claim were
settled in the way the communication implies. That can be checked by anyone without first joining the frame.

## 3. What licenses a name

Only two things, both outside the shape itself:

1. **The ground settles it.** `THE_FORMULA.md` step 5: "SETTLE: the ground settles it. Verified only by what pays. A
   gate is not the ground." The canonical README says the framework "stops at the limit of what the available
   instruments can distinguish, and records the lineage so the stop is honest."
2. **A provenanced import from a frame whose closure differs**, with a source gate and a shadow test (Fig 6 :23, Fig 8
   :50-52). Fig 8 :51: "Convergent derivation and adopted import look identical in the contents. Only the record of
   where each came from separates them".

So if a science communicator says "this is what uncertainty communication should look like" and names a specific
format, the name is their contribution. It may be useful. It is not derived from the figures until a ground settles it
or the import is recorded.

## 4. Why "more information" is a name, not a shape

*The Hard Problem is About Source* gives the mechanism directly: an instrument can read green and "cannot comment on
the source". Several sources can give the same reading. "If several paths create the same behavior, then behavior is a
projection with lossy information." The correction came "not by argument, but by the arrival of a better instrument".

Applying that to this question: an audience's apparent agreement, or the amount of information it received, is a
reading. It does not determine the source, which may be ground contact or may be a shared evidence pool. The shape
requires an instrument that discriminates sources (a held-out ground test, an independent frame); "give more facts" is
a name for one possible source and cannot be derived from the reading.

## 5. The specific limit on "shape, not a name" for this question

The README's phrase governs a **missing** thing. For HIS-0098 the missing thing is a practice that keeps contact in a
public setting. The six substrate terms are not at issue; nor is any missing primitive. So "shape, not a name" here
means: the figures can constrain the form of a communication practice but cannot select a specific format. Naming a
format is a binding from a world frame (JCOM practice insight, SRA risk-communication protocol, National Academies
report), provenanced as such. This is the same narrowing the exemplar applied in its own §5b: the rule is not a ban on
all naming, it is a ban on presenting a binding as a derivation.

## 6. Prestige is not action

*The Recursive Self-Improvement Trap*: "Prestige can fund an action. It can give an action a budget to be tried. But it
cannot settle an action. Only the ground can settle it. The Ground Maintainer must be blind to prestige."

For this question: a famous scientist, a prestigious journal, or a large audience can give a communication a budget to
be tried. None of them settles whether the communication kept contact with the ground. Selecting expert leads by
fame, or selecting recipients by how likely they are to agree, is the prestige collapse under another name.

## 7. My own audit is a simulation, not a ground reading

Same essay: "LLMs can quickly simulate an approximate answer/output with the formula found in the architecture post.
It will often be better than the generic outputs that you will usually get otherwise too. However, the truly testable
output would need to be derived from the real architecture or working the formula yourself." And: "Metatheories cannot
validate themselves."

So `01_figure_grounded_audit.md` is a simulated derivation checked for internal consistency against the figures. It is
not a test of the communication practices it discusses, and it cannot settle the question it reports on. The honest
verdict is a shape with a refuter and a stated stop, not an answer.

## 8. Correction recorded from the exemplar

The exemplar's `05` §5b corrected an overstatement of "shape, not a name": the phrase governs a missing primitive, not
the selection of atoms already held. I carry that correction forward rather than re-arguing it. Applied here, it means
the shapes in `07_the_tether_answer.md` are constraints on a practice, and the practices named in `04` are bindings
from world frames. Both are recorded, and neither is quietly promoted into the other.

## 9. What shape means in one sentence for HIS-0098

A shape here is the answer a science communicator can write down before choosing a slogan: a checkable list of what
any practice must do to the disagreement, the ground, the interest and the claim, with the part that only an instrument
can fill left explicitly open.
