## Wider literature that bears on the question

These were read as part of the sources above (they are the reference lists and linked items of opened pages) and are recorded in the ledger with the source through which they were accessed. They are not independently opened papers unless listed in the table above.

1. **Cloud controlling factors and phase.** The Zelinka 2020 paper traces the CMIP6 change to liquid condensate fraction in mixed-phase clouds, and cites Frey and Kay 2017, Tan et al. 2016, Bodas-Salcedo et al. 2019 and Gettelman et al. 2019 for the same mechanism. These are one evidence pool, not four witnesses.
2. **Regime decomposition and satellite simulators.** COSP exists because model and satellite clouds were not defined the same way; this is the concrete instance of a convention that makes a bug statable (Fig 10 :17, *"it makes the bug statable"*).
3. **Expert synthesis and pluralism.** Zelinka et al. 2022 compare models against expert judgment; the Stanford pluralism entry explains why a plurality of partial representations is normal in science rather than a defect. These are low-overlap framings of the same shape claim.
4. **Fetch record.** All listed pages returned content to `webfetch` on 2026-11-22. Two earlier candidate URLs returned 404 (a GMD `about.html` path and a WCRP CFMIP path) and were replaced by the working URLs above; this is recorded rather than hidden. No source in the ledger comes from an unopened page.

## Distribution: offer, do not push

Figure-grounded reasons:

- **Fig 10 :52** — *"Bring a characterised residual, never the source."* Sending the full figure set before anyone has described a residual sends a recording, not a generator. Lead with the per-slot spread and the compensating-errors claim; offer links after.
- **Fig 10 :42** — *"A verifier that can reconstruct the claim cannot verify it."* A recipient who has absorbed the framework before giving a verdict has joined the frame being checked.
- **Fig 9 :27-28** — *"describe it before you go looking, or any frame you pick will seem to fit."* Ask what they would expect to see if the union claim were true before giving them the literature.
- **Fig 2 :40** — *"agreement about the world is worthless when the agreeing frames share an evidence pool"* CMIP centers share code, forcing, reanalyses and tuning targets. Later agreement is not corroboration.

**Recommended order in a reply:** (1) the shape claim and its four refuters; (2) ask what they would count as a failure; (3) record that verdict; (4) only then the links, with the note that the verbatim figure text, not the summary, is the authority.

## Venue constraints worth stating plainly

- GMD and AGU require code and data availability statements. A simulated derivation has no code or measurement to deposit, so it must be recast as a method, protocol or evaluation design to be submittable. That is a real constraint, not a formality.
- EGU27 needs membership, an abstract processing charge, and a session-specific submission; the one-abstract rule applies.
- CFMIP is opt-in and community-driven: use the public group and the project contact group, not individual addresses found on a page.
- None of these venues is being asked to endorse the framework. They are places where the checkable sub-claims can be tested by people whose ground contact is public.
