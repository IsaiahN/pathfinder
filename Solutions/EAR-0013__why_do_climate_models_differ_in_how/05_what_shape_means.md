## 1. A shape is a constraint, not a filler

The canonical figures README states the limit: the framework returns *"what a missing thing would have to look like (**a shape, not a name**)."* Fig 9 :32: *"you get a kind of thing, never the answer itself."* Fig 13 :134-135: *"Unreachable is a verdict here, not a silence, It cannot say which primitive is missing, because naming it would mean already holding it."*

From *The Library of Babel* (carried): *"You can cut your search short if the set of books returned is small and descriptive enough that it provides a general shape of the answer that satisfies your question."*

So a **shape** is the narrowed form of an answer you can state before you can name it. For EAR-0013 the shape is: the spread is a union of overlapping conditions; the residual is per model and per cloud regime; a non-updating measured anchor is required; the difference between schemes lives in the bond, not in the ingredient list; a flat scalar can hide cancelling errors; and no figure selects a winning parameterization. That is a set of constraints on any adequate explanation. It is not the explanation's numerical content.

## 2. How a shape is obtained

- *Library of Babel* (carried): use the vocabulary you already have closest to the thing, look for recurring new terms that overlap or correlate with it, recurse. *"With each new term gained, you narrow the space and deepen your vocabulary."*
- Fig 6 :30: *"The description travels. Fails by being sharpened into terms only we can read. Cast it in effect terms."* Fig 9 :30-31: *"your words are borrowed too, so describe the effect rather than the cause."*
- *Decoding Tacit Knowledge* (carried): *"When contact isn't possible, the best you can send is a plain description of what you'd expect to see, because anyone can check that against the world."*
- *Library of Babel* on stopping (carried): the answer is reached when *"the finish line will also be the starting place"*, and the paradox is that by then you have *"composed the answer through the process of locating it."*

Applied here: the effect-level description is "models disagree in the cloud slot even though they broadly match present-day fluxes." Naming "the ice fraction scheme" as *the* cause would be naming before describing, and any field you then pick would seem to fit (Fig 9 :27-28).

## 3. What licenses a name

Only two things, both outside the shape itself:

1. **The ground settles it.** Formula step 5: *"the ground settles it. Verified only by what pays."* The README: the framework *"stops at the limit of what the available instruments can distinguish, and records the lineage so the stop is honest."* For clouds, this is the measured top-of-atmosphere flux, the active-sensor profile, and a process-level discriminator, not a model's self-report.
2. **A provenanced import from a frame whose closure differs** (Fig 6 :23, Fig 8 :50-52), with a source gate and a shadow test. A parameterization imported from a cloud-resolving model into a GCM is exactly such a crossing, and needs provenance.

*They're Scanning Books* (carried): *"The real import is not a new term in the existing space. It is a new dimension of the space."* And: *"A minimum viable set of six terms is a human's careful guess at reality's compression axes; another sensorium's minimum set need not be those six."* Also: *"Declaring the primitives does not make them neutral. It makes the choice visible."*

So "what a model represents" is a visible choice, not nature's own list. Calling one scheme *the* correct representation turns a visible choice into an invisible one.

## 4. Why "matching the flux" is the source error this question commits

*The Hard Problem is About Source* (carried) gives the mechanism. Metamerism: an instrument reads green and *"cannot comment on the source"* one green light, or yellow plus blue, or RGB, or CMYK, or a scheme not yet discovered. *"If several paths create the same behavior, then behavior is a projection with lossy information."* And: *"An instrument that can't distinguish these cases demonstrates its own inadequacy."* It was the **spectrometer**, not an argument, that resolved it.

This is F6 in `01`: net cloud radiative effect is a projection. Cloud fraction plus liquid water path plus effective radius, arranged differently, can produce the same projection. Zhao et al. 2022 measured exactly this (opened; see `04`). What is owed is a source-discriminating instrument, not a name for the source. Fig 5 :70: *"A term is settled when the ground pays for it. A term is usable when something can compose over it."*

*The Recursive Self-Improvement Trap* (carried): *"the output is only as good as the validator. The agent, or the tool, or the frame, or the theory, cannot validate itself."*

## 5. Q: Does "shape, not a name" forbid naming a cloud scheme? ANSWERED

**No, with a constraint.** The README's phrase is *"what a **missing** thing would have to look like (a shape, not a name)."* The subject is a primitive not held in the library. Cloud fraction, optical depth, phase and water path are **already held** in the climate-modeling library. Naming one as the mediator is a **binding** from the observation or process frame onto an atom already in the closure, not the invention of a new primitive. Fig 6 :21: *"MINT composes inside the closure and can never add an atom."*

What remains forbidden:

- inventing a seventh primitive and calling it a cloud process that moves the wall (Fig 13 :115);
- claiming a binding adds an atom when it does not (Fig 6: IMPORT adds an atom; this one adds none);
- naming the source of a reading without an instrument that discriminates among sources (the metamerism error of §4).

The honest form: a scheme is a **candidate binding**, provenanced as such, awaiting a discriminator. That is consistent with Fig 9 :32 and with the correction recorded in the exemplar's `05` §5b and `06` Q1.

## 5b. Correction to my own first pass (kept visible)

In the first pass of this file I wrote that "the framework refuses to name the cloud scheme." That overstated the rule in the same way the exemplar's author corrected in their §5b. Corrected position: the rule governs **missing** primitives. Naming an already-held binding is provenance, not a forbidden invention, and refusing to name it would have hidden the actual question, which is which discriminator would settle between competing bindings. The §4 metamerism point is **not** retracted: the reason a binding is only a candidate is the missing discriminator, not a ban on naming held atoms.

A second overstatement, also corrected: I initially called resolution an import from high-resolution modeling into a GCM. Re-census assigns it to INWARD instrument extension (Fig 6 :32), because it sharpens an instrument already returning a residual. Both readings are recorded so neither is quietly lost.

## 6. Two low-overlap corroborations opened this session

These are not framework essays, so their agreement is not expected in the way the essays' agreement is.

- **Stanford Encyclopedia of Philosophy, *Scientific Pluralism*** (opened 2026-11-22): *"models, simulations, and related media deliver partial, interest-dependent, hence contingent representations of the world."* It uses the map analogy: a plurality of partial representations, whose conformity to the world is assessed on empirical grounds. This corroborates the union verdict from philosophy of science, a genuinely different evidence pool. It does not corroborate any specific figure line; it corroborates the *shape* of a plural answer.
- **Stanford Encyclopedia of Philosophy, *Models in Science*** (opened): lists *"general-circulation models of the global climate"* among central scientific models and defines an idealization as *"a deliberate simplification or distortion of something complicated with the objective of making it more tractable or understandable."* It adds that learning from a model requires corresponding parts or aspects in the target. That is the bond question in other words: which parts of a cloud scheme correspond to which parts of the atmosphere, and can that correspondence be checked.

Note the overlap: the two encyclopedia entries share a venue and an editorial tradition, so they count as one low-overlap witness relative to the climate pool, not two.

## 7. Two consequences for outreach

**Prestige is not action.** *The Recursive Self-Improvement Trap* (carried): *"Prestige must never be allowed to validate an action directly... Prestige can fund an action. It can give an action a budget to be tried. But it cannot settle an action. Only the ground can settle it. The Ground Maintainer must be blind to prestige."* A list of famous modelers who agree with the shape claim is the named collapse, not corroboration.

**My own audit is a simulation, not a ground reading.** Same essay (carried): *"LLMs can quickly simulate an approximate answer/output... However, the truly testable output would need to be derived from the real architecture or working the formula yourself."* LLM output is *"simulacra."* So `01` is a simulated derivation checked for internal consistency against the figures. It is **not** a test of the claim and must be labelled as such. It cannot settle anything it reports on.
