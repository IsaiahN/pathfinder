## 0. The claim under audit, as a residual in effect terms

The question asks for a condition that separates two outcomes when a numerical measure M is introduced into an accountability relation:

- **Outcome A, accountability increases.** M tracks the underlying goal G; drift from G becomes visible and correctable; an actor who drifts is corrected by a reading that is not their own.
- **Outcome B, measure optimization.** M rises while G does not, or falls, because the measured party acts on M rather than on G. This is the family Strathern named "When a measure becomes a target, it ceases to be a good measure" and Campbell named with his "pessimistic laws" (world sources, ledger).

**Residual R, stated in effect terms.** Let M_s be the reported number for slot s (a patient, a student, a school, a quarter, a case) and G_s the actual state of the goal in that slot. Then R_s = |M_s − G_s|, measured **per slot, never as a single global number** (Fig 1 :19). Outcome B is the state in which M_s rises while R_s does not trend down: the number and the report move together while the ground is untouched. The question is not "do metrics corrupt?" It is: under what relationship between M and the ground does R_s trend down, and under what relationship does it persist while M rises?

**Direction: bind the sought-for shape as an ordinary term before searching** (`THE_FORMULA.md` "Direction"; Fig 5 :65-66). I write it down now so it can accrue a residual like anything else:

> **Candidate shape (provisional, refutable):** M increases accountability when M is an *anchor the measured frame cannot move*, read *per slot*, used as a *filter or witness to the ground rather than as the verdict*, and adopted only where the question is *constitutively about the number*. M inverts into a target when the measured frame authors M, when M updates under their push, when slot residuals are averaged away, or when M is promoted to the verdict.

This shape is written *before* any conclusion and is exposed to the refuter in §4.

## 2. Domain census (before choosing any figure line)

### R by arity, symmetry and scale

- **Arity.** The residual is indexed per slot (Fig 1 :19). Accountability lives in one unit's residual, not in a portfolio average.
- **Symmetry.** "measure tracks goal" is not symmetric under exchanging the frame that produces the number with the ground. Fig 2 :39-40: "Correlation has valence." A self-report and an independent reading can agree in value while carrying opposite evidence.
- **Scale.** First order is the measured activity acting on G. Second order is the measurement layer acting on M. The pathology is a scale-crossing error: a second-order reading (the number) is used as if it were the first-order state (the goal).

### One line per domain: actors, currency, anchor

- **Domain 1, the measurement frame.** Actors: metric designers, auditors, the institution that sets and reads M. Currency: reported numbers and the map from activity to score. Anchor: the ground G, which should not change because a frame thinks about it. No nameable anchor if M is self-referential, in which case this is not yet a domain. Verdict: **triangulated** (Fig 2 + Fig 1, low overlap between anchor law and self-scoring law).
- **Domain 2, the measured frame.** Actors: the teachers, clinicians, firms and officials whose performance is measured. Currency: the actions that move M and M itself, now self-produced. Anchor: their local goal, which becomes M once M is the target; this anchor **updates**, so collapse 1 (mutual update) is live. Verdict: **triangulated** (Fig 1 :27 + Fig 2 :33-34 + Fig 10 :47).
- **Domain 3, the ground (the underlying goal).** Actors: patients, students, citizens, the world. Currency: actual outcomes, not their representations. Anchor: the ground itself, which does not decay. Verdict: **triangulated** (Fig 11 :40 + Fig 10 :24 + Fig 2 :29-30).
- **Domain 4, the verification frame.** Actors: external checkers, peer frames, and this simulated second seat. Currency: agreement and residual. Anchor: the ground, never agreement. In this run every seat draws on one evidence pool (the same figures, essays and ledger), so independence is approximately zero and agreement is internal consistency only. Verdict: **single-source, declared limitation** (Fig 2 :40).

### Partition or union

A **union compound**, not a partition (Fig 9 :55-59). The failure has at least four conditions that overlap:

- c1: anchor mobility (M moves when the measured party pushes on it);
- c2: frame-internal production (the measured party authors M);
- c3: aggregation (per-slot residuals vanish into one average);
- c4: filter promoted to verdict (M becomes the final judgment of the goal).

A single lever, for example "add more metrics" or "raise the penalty," clears the region where only one condition holds and leaves the intersections standing, "because the second condition still binds" (Fig 9 :56). "What remains is smaller and it is also categorically different: a case in two conditions is not a harder case of either. It stops being a mixture and becomes a specific compound." (Fig 9 :58-59). Each condition needs its own loop; they must be unbundled before another search.

### Order by dependency (Fig 3)

1. **Anchor first:** fix G and test that M does not move when pushed (before any measurement is trusted).
2. **Vocabulary / objective (link 3):** have terms for what the activity affords and for what counts as progress toward G. This is the middle link that breaks and has the fewest instruments (Fig 3 :25-26).
3. **Planning (link 4):** agents act to close R_s toward G. Effort here cannot rescue a broken link 3: "Optimising a link that already works cannot rescue one that does not." (Fig 3 :19).
4. **Learn and carry:** name what worked against the ground, not against M.

## 3. Findings F1 to F10

### F1 - A measure that moves when pushed is not an anchor (decisive for outcome B)

Fig 2 :29-30: "An anchor is whatever settles the question and does not change its answer because of what any frame thinks. A measurement, an experiment, a counter, a market, a patient outcome. If it moves when you push on it, it is not an anchor."

Fig 2 :33-34: "The anchor must not update. If it does, panel 3 turns into panel 1: the anchor becomes another frame, everything drifts together, and the drift is coherent, which is why nobody notices."

When M becomes the target, the measured party pushes on M. M then moves when pushed, so by the figure's own test M is not an anchor; it becomes another frame. The number and the reported performance drift together, and the drift is **coherent**, which is exactly why the failure is invisible from inside the accountability system: the dashboard is green. This is the structural form of Goodhart's and Campbell's laws. **Mode: `general` on the structure, `specified` on the prediction that a high-stakes targeted M decouples from an independent G.** Status: **triangulated** (Fig 2 anchor law + Fig 1 :23 "The ground enters as the only metric"). World corroboration: Strathern 1997 and Rodamar 2018 (ledger), CNA 2022. Refuter: a controlled high-stakes comparison in which a self-authored, pushable M keeps tracking an independent ground with no decoupling.

### F2 - A frame cannot score itself with a quantity it also produces

Fig 1 :27: "They are frame-internal. A frame cannot score itself with a quantity it also produces." The preceding lines name what is disqualified: "coverage, and compression achieved" and "predicates minted, and credibility accrued" (Fig 1 :24-26). Fig 10 :42: "A verifier that can reconstruct the claim cannot verify it."

When the measured party produces the number (self-report, self-grading, locally chosen proxies), M is frame-internal and cannot settle accountability; it is a quantity the frame also produces. **Mode: `specified`.** Status: **triangulated** (Fig 1 + Fig 10 + the independent-observer remedy in Campbell via Fordham). World corroboration: CNA 2022 recommends "Use measurement data not generated by the organization being measured" and Campbell's Texarkana and Seattle examples of independent external observers (Fordham Institute). Refuter: show a self-authored number that has been checked against a ground the author cannot move, with the check recorded before the stakes were attached.

### F3 - Aggregation is how the live signal disappears

Fig 1 :19: "The residual is indexed by object slot, not aggregated." Fig 1 :22: "Aggregating across slots is how a live signal disappears into an average."

A single overall score lets good slots mask a catastrophic residual in one slot, so raising the average is compatible with leaving the worst case untouched. Accountability requires the per-slot reading. **Mode: `general`.** Status: **single-source** in the figures (Fig 1), corroborated in the world by the OECD/HLEG call for "more granular and timely data that better capture the true state of the economy, the varied situations of different population groups" (Carnegie UK 2019). Refuter: demonstrate that one aggregate preserves correction in each slot as well as a per-slot reading does.

### F4 - Legitimacy is constitutive, not blanket (this retracts the naive reading)

Fig 2 :36: "An anchor is legitimate when the question is constitutively about it." Fig 2 :37-38: "Human judgment is the right anchor for is this a good answer for a person, because the question is about people. It is only an instrument for is this code correct, and instruments can be wrong on axes they do not measure."

A number increases accountability when the question is constitutively about the number (a ballot count where the count is the election, a count of adverse events where the count is the safety outcome). It fails when the number is only an instrument standing in for a richer goal (quality of care, quality of education, welfare), because an instrument "can be wrong on axes they do not measure." Fig 13 :16: "it names what is being measured"; Fig 13 :26: "it decides what the measurement would mean." **Mode: `specified`.** Status: **triangulated** (Fig 2 + Fig 13). World corroboration: Stiglitz-Sen-Fitoussi 2009 "What we measure affects what we do; and if our measurements are flawed, decisions may be distorted"; Campbell's "Achievement tests may well be valuable indicators of general school achievement under conditions of normal teaching aimed at general competence." Refuter: show a number on an axis it provably does not measure maintaining ground contact under high stakes.

### F5 - Filters for the budget, witnesses for the verdict

Fig 9 :60: "Use filters for the budget and witnesses for the verdict. Never let a filter hand you a verdict." Fig 10 :47: "a convention that decides an outcome has stopped being one."

M is safe as a filter (decide where to look, allocate a budget, triage) and unsafe when promoted to the verdict (the final judgment of whether the goal was achieved). Promoting a filter to a verdict is the moment it becomes a convention that decides an outcome and stops being a convention. World corroboration: CNA 2022 distinguishes hard-to-manipulate measures of effectiveness from easy-to-manipulate measures of performance and recommends "Use MOEs instead of MOPs whenever practicable and possible"; Koretz via Fordham names "a primary cause of Campbell's Law is incomplete measurement of desired outcomes." **Mode: `specified`.** Status: **triangulated** (Fig 9 + Fig 10). Refuter: show a measure used only as a triage filter that nevertheless closes R_s without ever being promoted, and show that the promotion step was not the cause of the drift.

### F6 - The objective link breaks first, and it has the fewest instruments

Fig 3 :25-26: "having terms for what a situation offers and being able to say what would count as progress, has neither, so it attracts the least attention and the fewest instruments." Fig 3 :19: "Optimising a link that already works cannot rescue one that does not." Fig 3 :22-23: "A step whose input never arrives cannot be diagnosed, only its predecessor can. Which is why a reading taken below the break is not a weak reading. It is a reading of nothing."

The metric is meant to be link 3, the objective: it should say what counts as progress toward G. When the metric stops tracking G, link 3 is broken. All the effort spent raising M (link 4, acting) arrives at a step that cannot use it, and a reading taken there is "a reading of nothing." **Mode: `general`.** Status: **triangulated** (Fig 3 + Fig 11 :40). Refuter: show that improving the measured party's execution of M, with link 3 demonstrably broken, still closed the goal residual.

### F7 - Contact, not improvement in isolation

Fig 11 :40: "An improvement that does not change contact changes nothing, however much it improves." Fig 10 :24: "the ground does not decay, the channel does" (the figure lists the channel failures: "stale percepts, dropped transients," "saturated metrics, pooled readings").

Raising M without changing contact with G changes nothing, however impressive the number. The ground has not rotted; the channel to it has been saturated or pooled (F3, F2). Fig 2 :42: "Buy independence in proportion to the cost of being wrong." **Mode: `general`.** Status: **triangulated** (Fig 11 + Fig 10 + Fig 2). Refuter: a case where M rose, contact with G was independently shown to be unchanged, and the goal nonetheless moved.

### F8 - A number is an ordering, which is empty without a state

Fig 13 :20: "ordering, so more and less can be said" (spectrum). Fig 13 :29-30: "A state with no structure has no magnitude: three of what, ordered how, varying across what? A structure with no state is empty: an ordering over nothing, a network with nothing at the nodes."

A numerical measure supplies ordering (spectrum), but a naked KPI is "an ordering over nothing": it does not by itself decide what is being measured or what the measurement means. That requires a bond to a state and a structure (Fig 13 :16, :26). Fig 12 :111: "A vocabulary without bonds can name a state and cannot name a route to one." The world parallel is the metamerism argument in *The Hard Problem is About Source*: an instrument that reads green "cannot comment on the source," so several sources can give the same reading. **Mode: `general`.** Status: **triangulated** (Fig 13 + Fig 12 + essay, fields distinct). Refuter: exhibit a number whose ranking alone determines the correct corrective action without any specification of what was measured or what the ranking meant.

### F9 - It is a union of conditions, not one hard problem

Fig 9 :55-56: "A bundled problem is usually a union of overlapping conditions, not a list of separate ones." Fig 9 :58-59: "a case in two conditions is not a harder case of either. It stops being a mixture and becomes a specific compound."

c1 through c4 overlap. More measurement, steeper penalties and more transparency each clear a different region; none clears the intersections. "And when the split lands on a union rather than a partition, unbundle before you search again." (Fig 9 :54). **Mode: `general`.** Status: **single-source** in the figures (Fig 9), corroborated by the multiple-measure and independent-check recommendations in Campbell/Fordham and CNA. Refuter: show that one lever closes all four conditions simultaneously across a set of real cases, with the intersections measured rather than assumed.

### F10 - Prestige, reputation and the appearance of accountability are not the ground

From *The Recursive Self-Improvement Trap*: "Prestige must never be allowed to validate an action directly... Prestige can fund an action. It can give an action a budget to be tried. But it cannot settle an action. Only the ground can settle it. The Ground Maintainer must be blind to prestige."

An accountability system that rewards the reputation for good numbers, or the appearance of rigor, is the named collapse: the measure is funded as an action but a proxy for worth has been allowed to settle it. **Mode: `general`.** Status: **single-source** (essay). Refuter: a controlled case where a prestige or reputational signal, with contact held fixed, correctly tracks the goal without a ground reading.

## 4. Pre-registered refuter (written before the conclusion)

Per the standing instruction, "a falsifier written before the run is a design instrument." The candidate shape in §0 is refuted if any of the following holds:

1. A controlled high-stakes comparison shows that a measure the measured frame authors and can move keeps tracking an independent ground, with no decoupling (breaks F1 and F2).
2. A single aggregate is shown to preserve per-slot residuals for correction as well as a per-slot reading (breaks F3).
3. A number on an axis it provably does not measure is shown to keep goal contact unchanged under stakes (breaks F4).
4. A room of checkers selected for their prior agreement is shown to produce a correction that a ground-visible, independent checker missed (breaks the outreach rule in Fig 2 :40 and Fig 7 :35).

If any fires, the shape is withdrawn or narrowed, and the withdrawal is recorded here rather than silently erased. Until one is run, nothing is `grounded`.

## 5. Recommendation

1. Treat M as an **anchor**: source it outside the measured frame, make it hard to manipulate, and test that it does not move when pushed (F1, F2). CNA's list is operational: independent data, authoritative definitions, randomized measures, red-teaming.
2. **Keep the residual per slot.** Publish the distribution and the worst cases, not just the average (F3).
3. **Keep M as filter and witness, not verdict.** If it decides the outcome, it has stopped being a convention (F5).
4. **Adopt a number only where the question is constitutively about it.** Everywhere else, name the axes the instrument cannot measure and provide a second, low-overlap reading (F4, F8).
5. **Instrument link 3.** Ask what would count as progress toward G before asking people to hit M (F6). Use multiple measures, qualitative as well as quantitative, and independent external checks (Campbell via Fordham).
6. **State mode on every claim and write the falsifier before the run.** Prestige and agreement are budgets, not verdicts (F10, Fig 2 :42).

### Round-trip note

The condensation "accountability requires an anchor the measured frame cannot move" drops three constraints that the verbatim set carries: per-slot indexing (Fig 1 :19), filter versus verdict (Fig 9 :60), and constitutive legitimacy (Fig 2 :36-38). The round-trip gap `R_T` is therefore nonzero; the shorthand is a lossy generator, not an identity. `11_audit.md` measures it qualitatively.

### Updated verdict

The shape survives and is sharpened: a numerical measure increases accountability when it is an unmoved, externally sourced, per-slot reading used as a filter or witness to a goal the question is constitutively about; it becomes a target when the measured frame authors it, can move it, hides slots in an average, or promotes it to the verdict. Verdicts are kept per domain and never averaged. No domain is `grounded`; the whole is `specified` with a `general` structural core. Strains after re-census: one, named above.
