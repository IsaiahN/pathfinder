## QUESTION

Decide, in effect terms, what the Tether says about the two outcomes when a numerical measure M is used to make an actor accountable for a goal G. Option A: a numerical measure is an accountability device, because it makes drift visible and correctable. Option B: a numerical measure becomes a target, so people optimize the number rather than the goal. What each leads to: A without conditions ignores the anchor law and ends in coherent, invisible drift; B as a blanket verdict contradicts Fig 2 :36 and the Campbell/Koretz literature, which say measurement is essential. The derived third option is a conditional shape: the outcome depends on the relation between M and the ground.

## DOMAIN CENSUS

- **R (the residual).** R_s = |M_s − G_s| per slot. Arity: one residual per measured unit, never a global average. Symmetry: "measure tracks goal" is not symmetric under exchanging the number producer with the ground; correlation has valence. Scale: first order is acting on G, second order is acting on M; the pathology is a scale-crossing error. Fig 9 names this a union compound, not one hard question: four overlapping conditions, and a case in two conditions is a specific compound rather than a harder case of either.
- **Domain 1, the measurement frame.** Actors: designers, auditors, the institution setting and reading M. Currency: reported numbers and the activity-to-score map. Anchor: the ground, which should not change because a frame thinks about it. Question type: anchor legitimacy. Verdict **triangulated** (Fig 2 + Fig 1, low overlap).
- **Domain 2, the measured frame.** Actors: teachers, clinicians, firms, officials. Currency: actions that move M, and M itself now self-produced. Anchor: a local goal that becomes M once M is the target; this anchor updates, so coherent drift is live. Question type: frame-internal scoring. Verdict **triangulated** (Fig 1 + Fig 2 + Fig 10).
- **Domain 3, the ground.** Actors: patients, students, citizens, the world. Currency: actual outcomes, not representations. Anchor: the ground itself, which does not decay. Question type: contact. Verdict **triangulated** (Fig 11 + Fig 10 + Fig 2).
- **Domain 4, the verification frame.** Actors: external checkers and this simulated second seat. Currency: agreement and residual. Anchor: the ground, never agreement. In this run all seats draw on one evidence pool, so independence is approximately zero and agreement is internal consistency, not corroboration. Verdict **single-source, declared limitation**.

**Order by dependency.** Fix the anchor before trusting a measurement; settle link 3 (what counts as progress toward G) before acting on M; refuse the promotion of M to a verdict before minting any governance rule over it; describe the failure before selecting a literature (Formula step 2, Fig 3, Fig 9 :27-28).

## VERDICT (from the figures)

A numerical measure increases accountability when it is an unmoved, externally sourced, per-slot reading used as a filter or witness to a goal the question is constitutively about, with a second low-overlap reading for axes the number cannot see. It becomes a target when the measured frame authors it, can move it, hides slot residuals in an average, or promotes it to the deciding outcome. The failure is a union of overlapping conditions; an accountable measure and a gamed target are isomers over the same atoms, differing in the bond to ground versus reward. A bare number is an ordering over nothing until a comparison and a subtraction bond make it a goal and a progress signal. The blanket verdicts "metrics corrupt" and "metrics always help" are both refused. This is a `specified`, ungrounded result with a `general` structural core.

- **cost if wrong:** bounded and locatable. The packet is mode-labelled, states its refuters before its conclusion and records provenance, so an error is findable. The real cost is spending the independence of outside checkers by sending them a framework to agree with instead of a claim to refute. Fig 2 :42 "Buy independence in proportion to the cost of being wrong." That cost is avoidable by leading with the shape and the four refuters.
- **undoable:** yes. A measure can be re-sourced externally, decomposed per slot, demoted from verdict to filter, paired with a second reading, and red-teamed, without withdrawing the underlying goal. No primitive has to be abandoned, and a circulated claim can be superseded by a corrected packet.
- **reversed by:** a ground reading, not agreement. A controlled high-stakes comparison showing a self-authored movable number still tracking an independent ground would break the anchor verdict; a demonstration that one aggregate preserves per-slot correction would break the aggregation verdict; a demonstration that a number on an unseen axis preserves goal contact would break the constitutive-legitimacy verdict; a figure re-census that permits a settled measure to stay unmoved at the next level would clear the one live strain. Until one of these fires, the verdict stands as `candidate`, never `grounded`.
