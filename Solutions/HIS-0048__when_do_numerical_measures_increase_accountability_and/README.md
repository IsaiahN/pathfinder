## What is deliberately not here

- No personal emails of named individuals.
- No ranking of people by how likely they are to agree that measures corrupt goals.
- No bulk-send plan.

Three reasons in the figures' own words:

1. **A room is not an anchor.** *"A room's agreement is variation, not a verdict. Selection between rooms is where the ground votes."* (Fig 7 :35). A list of people selected because they already dislike metrics manufactures the agreement that would make their agreement worthless.
2. **Correlation disqualifies a verdict.** *"agreement about the world is worthless when the agreeing frames share an evidence pool"* (Fig 2 :40). Selecting checkers for receptiveness creates exactly the shared pool that cancels their evidence.
3. **Asking for trust before checking spends independence.** *"A verifier that can reconstruct the claim cannot verify it."* (Fig 10 :42). Stage 1 of the offer must be recorded before any theory is shown.

Legally: harvesting personal emails for unsolicited bulk outreach breaches GDPR and CAN-SPAM style rules in many jurisdictions, a practical failure on top of the structural one.
