## What in the wider literature bears directly on the question

1. **The condition, not the slogan.** Campbell's "under conditions of normal teaching aimed at general competence" and Fordham's reading of Campbell ("a more complicated and constructive message") both reject the blanket anti-measurement conclusion. This corroborates F4 in `01_figure_grounded_audit.md`: legitimacy is constitutive, not universal. Do not present the result as "metrics always fail."
2. **Independence is the load-bearing property.** CNA's operational rules (independent data, hard-to-manipulate definitions, randomized measures, red teaming) and Campbell's independent external observers are the world-field equivalents of Fig 2's anchor and Fig 10's verifier non-access. They corroborate F1 and F2 from a field with a different evidence pool (defense analysis and evaluation practice, not the Tether figures).
3. **Granularity versus aggregation.** The OECD/HLEG call for more granular data is the world-field twin of Fig 1 :22 "Aggregating across slots is how a live signal disappears into an average." Corroborates F3.
4. **The disturbance analogy.** McIntyre's Cambridge page likens Goodhart's law to the uncertainty principle: measuring a system disturbs it. That is a cross-field analogue of an anchor that moves when pushed (Fig 2 :33-34). It is an analogue only; the figures decide the verdict.

**Overlap warning.** Rodamar, Strathern, Campbell, CNA, Fordham, Stiglitz and Carnegie discuss the same family of cases from different fields, but several quote one another. Agreement among them is a shared evidence pool about a phenomenon, not independent corroboration of the *Tether* derivation. The ledger records overlap per row. No world source conflicts with the figure verdict, so the conflict rule (back to the census) was not triggered.

## Distribution: offer, do not push

Figure-grounded reasons:

- **Fig 4 :22-25** - "Only methods cross up. Only head starts come down. A recording carried upward looks like knowledge and is a description of one occasion." Sending the whole framework before anyone has described a residual sends a recording, not a generator. Lead with the conditional shape and its four refuters; offer links after.
- **Fig 10 :42** - "A verifier that can reconstruct the claim cannot verify it." A recipient who has absorbed the framework before giving a verdict has joined the frame being checked. Their later agreement is never pooled as corroboration.
- **Fig 9 :27-28** - "describe it before you go looking, or any frame you pick will seem to fit." Ask what they would expect to see if the shape claim were true before showing them the article.
- **Fig 7 :35** - "A room's agreement is variation, not a verdict. Selection between rooms is where the ground votes." No list ranked by receptiveness, no bulk send.
- **Fig 2 :42** - "Buy independence in proportion to the cost of being wrong." The cost here is a badly governed metric in a real institution, so independent checking is worth buying.

**Recommended order in a reply:** (1) the conditional shape and the four refuters; (2) ask what they would count as a failure and which of the four conditions they have actually seen fail; (3) only then the links, with the note that the figures, not the summary, are the authority.

## The two-stage offer

- **Stage 1 before stage 2** is Fig 10 :52 "Bring a characterised residual, never the source" and Fig 2 :45 "Frames range against the ground. They do not negotiate with it." The recipient checks the output without holding the framework's interior.
- **Stage 2 is opt-in by construction.** Seat 4 must not hold the interiors; once a recipient has absorbed the theory, their agreement stops being evidence. Record their verdict on stage 1 before any theory is shown, and never pool later agreement as corroboration (Fig 2 :40 "agreement about the world is worthless when the agreeing frames share an evidence pool").
- **Stage 3 sends a method, which is permitted.** Fig 4 :22 "Only methods cross up. Only head starts come down." The formula is a generator, so giving it away does not give away an answer.
- **Stage 3 still needs a ground per problem.** `THE_FORMULA.md` "Direction": a frame "cannot check the direction of its own question, because the shape a frame is searching for is not a term it holds." Each user must write down what would refute their result before running it, and label the mode of what they get.

**Hard precondition:** stages 1 to 3 only work if the stage-1 output survives the four refuters. Do not send before then.
