## 1. A shape is a constraint, not a filler

The figures README states the limit: the framework returns "what a missing thing would have to look like (a shape, not a name)." Fig 9 :32 "you get a kind of thing, never the answer itself." Fig 13: "It cannot say which primitive is missing, because naming it would mean already holding it."

From *The Library of Babel* (verified): "This means that you can cut your search short if the set of books returned is small and descriptive enough that it provides a general shape of the answer that satisfies your question."

For this question a **shape** is the narrowed form of the condition you can state before you can name a specific metric or a specific institution. It constrains; it does not fill. "A measure is an unmoved, per-slot witness to the goal, used as a filter and not a verdict" is a shape. "Use the net promoter score," or "ban all KPIs," is a name or a slogan, not a shape.

## 2. How a shape is obtained

- *Library of Babel* (verified): "With each new term gained, you are able to narrow the space by definition and deepen your vocabulary." Also: use the vocabulary you already have that is closest to the thing, look for recurring new terms that overlap or correlate with it, recurse.
- Fig 6 :30: "The description travels. Fails by being sharpened into terms only we can read. Cast it in effect terms." Fig 9 :30-31: "your words are borrowed too, so describe the effect rather than the cause." Here the effect is: the number rises while the goal does not, and the drift is invisible from inside because the readings agree.
- *Decoding Tacit Knowledge* (from the exemplar's reading): "When contact isn't possible, the best you can send is a plain description of what you'd expect to see, because anyone can check that against the world."
- *Library of Babel* on stopping (verified): "You will know that you are done because the finish line will also be the starting place," and "you have already composed the answer through the process of locating it."

## 3. What licenses a name

Only two things, both outside the shape itself:

1. **The ground settles it.** Formula step 5: "the ground settles it. Verified only by what pays." The README: the framework "stops at the limit of what the available instruments can distinguish, and records the lineage so the stop is honest."
2. **A provenanced import from a frame whose closure differs** (Fig 6 :23, Fig 8 :50-52), with a source gate and a shadow test. Here, the world literature (Goodhart, Campbell, CNA) is such an import: it is corroboration with provenance, not a derivation of the shape.

*They're Scanning Books When They Should* (from the exemplar's reading): "The real import is not a new term in the existing space. It is a new dimension of the space." Also: "Declaring the primitives does not make them neutral. It makes the choice visible."

So choosing "spectrum" as the figure-name for a numerical ordering is a visible binding from the six-term set, not a discovery that the framework produces a particular metric. Naming a specific KPI as *the* accountability answer would turn a visible choice into an invisible one.

## 4. The ground and the source problem

*The Hard Problem is About Source* (re-fetched and verified) gives the mechanism for the failure mode in F3 and F8. Metamerism: an instrument reads green and "it cannot comment on the source." The essay states: "If several paths create the same behavior, then behavior is a projection with lossy information". One number can be produced by genuine improvement, by selection of easy cases, by relabeling, or by outright fabrication. The reading does not determine the source. The essay is blunt: "An instrument that can't distinguish these cases demonstrates its own inadequacy, it cannot claim anything substantial about what is being measured or what causes it." And the resolution was instrumental, not argumentative: "It was proven wrong not by argument, but by the arrival of a better instrument."

An accountability dashboard that reads "95% compliance" and names its source as "real improvement" commits exactly the metamerism error. What is owed is a better instrument (an independent, per-slot, unmoved reading), not a name for the source.

*The Recursive Self-Improvement Trap* (from the exemplar's reading): "the output is only as good as the validator. The agent, or the tool, or the frame, or the theory, cannot validate itself."

## 5. Canonical answer: what shape does this question have?

The shape is a **four-part condition on the relation between a measure M and a ground G**, not a metric and not a blanket verdict:

1. **Unmoved anchor:** M does not change when the measured frame pushes on it (Fig 2 :29-30, :33-34).
2. **Per-slot residual:** M is read per unit and the distribution and worst cases are visible (Fig 1 :19, :22).
3. **Filter or witness, not verdict:** M decides where to look and what to resource, but not whether the goal was met (Fig 9 :60, Fig 10 :47).
4. **Constitutive legitimacy or a second reading:** either the question is genuinely about the number, or a low-overlap instrument covers the axes M cannot measure (Fig 2 :36-38, Fig 13 :26).

These are necessary together, not independently sufficient; the failure is a union compound (Fig 9 :55-59). The shape does **not** name a specific metric, a specific threshold, a specific jurisdiction or a specific reform. It says what any candidate must be made of. Four refuters are written in `01_figure_grounded_audit.md` §4 before any conclusion.

## 5b. Correction to my own §3 and §4

I initially wrote §4 as if any single number that fails to discriminate among sources were useless for accountability. That overstates the case. The figure rule is about a **missing** thing: "what a missing thing would have to look like (a shape, not a name)." Spectrum (ordering) is already one of the six held terms (Fig 13 :20), so a number is not a missing primitive; it is a held structure bonded to a state. What is forbidden is not having a number, but treating an ordering over nothing as if it named and settled the goal (Fig 13 :29-30). The metamerism point survives, narrowed: the failure is the missing discriminator of sources, not the presence of measurement. Both the original claim and this narrowing are recorded here so neither is quietly lost.

## 6. Two consequences for outreach

**Prestige is not action.** From *The Recursive Self-Improvement Trap*: "Prestige must never be allowed to validate an action directly... Someone let prestige stand in for proof... Prestige can fund an action. It can give an action a budget to be tried. But it cannot settle an action. Only the ground can settle it. The Ground Maintainer must be blind to prestige." A campaign that gathers prestigious names to agree that metrics fail is the named collapse, not corroboration. The legitimate ask is the opposite: give the conditional claim a budget to be tried and let the ground settle it.

**My own audit is a simulation, not a ground reading.** Same essay: "LLMs can quickly simulate an approximate answer/output... However, the truly testable output would need to be derived from the real architecture or working the formula yourself." LLM output is "simulacra." So `01` is a simulated derivation, checked for internal consistency against the figures. It is not a test of the claim and must be labelled as such. It cannot settle anything it reports on.
