## What is deliberately not here

- No personal emails of named individuals.
- No ranking of people by how likely they are to agree that data is or is not a moat.
- No bulk-send plan.

Three figure-grounded reasons:

1. **A room does not upgrade the label.** Fig 7 :35: *"A room’s agreement is variation, not a verdict. Selection between rooms is where the ground votes."* A list of people chosen for receptiveness manufactures the agreement it would then cite.
2. **Correlation disqualifies a verdict.** Fig 2 :40: *"disqualifying for a verdict: agreement about the world is worthless when the agreeing frames share an evidence pool."* Strategy researchers who read the same RBV canon are one pool, not two witnesses.
3. **The verifier must not hold the interiors.** Fig 10 :42: *"A verifier that can reconstruct the claim cannot verify it."* The first contact carries the residual and its refuter, never the framework.

Legally, scraping personal emails for unsolicited bulk outreach breaches GDPR and CAN-SPAM style rules in many jurisdictions. That is a practical failure on top of the structural one. Public opt-in channels avoid both.
