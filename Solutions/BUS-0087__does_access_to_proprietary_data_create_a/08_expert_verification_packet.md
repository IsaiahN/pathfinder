# Expert verification packet: the Tether on proprietary data and competitive advantage

**What this is:** a claim in a form a strategy researcher or data-strategy practitioner can check. Mode, derivation and refuters are stated before the conclusion, so a reader knows what to look for and what would settle it.

**Mode: `specified` on the three sub-claims, `general` on the anchor rule. Not `grounded`.** No market test or entry study was run for this package. This is a shape and a route, not a result.

**Status:** a simulated derivation checked for internal consistency against the figures, not a ground reading. It cannot settle the question it reports on.

## 1. The claim, in one paragraph

The question is a union compound, so one yes or no is the wrong output. Four verdicts must be kept separate. (1) Proprietary data is a barrier to derivation but only a conditional barrier to acquisition: a rival cannot compose an atom its frame lacks, but import is possible and failure to find is not proof of absence. (2) Data alone is a head start, not a capability: capability is a property of agent and habitat, and a copied dataset without the habitat proves wiring, never capability. (3) Durability is a substrate property: where the enabling arrangement is recorded and portable, rivals get it as a lookup and the gap erodes; where it is tacit, every lineage pays again and the gap persists. (4) The market, not a data-volume metric, is the anchor that settles the case, and firms drawing on one evidence pool are one frame with several names. The framework returns this shape, not a dataset or a duration.

## 2. Three checkable sub-claims

### C1: framework-internal; checkable by reading Figures 6 and 8

- **Statement:** the data atom is underivable inside a rival's closure, but import moves the bound, and failure to find is not proof of absence.
- **Source, verbatim:** Fig 6 :21 *"MINT composes inside the closure and can never add an atom."* Fig 8 :32 *"Closure is idempotent. Composing inside a closed set never leaves it, so no search, however long, adds an atom."* Fig 6 :23 *"No frame escapes its own closure. The bound moves only on contact with a frame whose closure differs."* Fig 8 :39 *"So a search that finds nothing is never a proof of absence."*
- **What to check:** does the strategy literature distinguish a resource that cannot be derived from one that is merely not yet acquired? Barney's imitability criterion and Cohen and Levinthal's absorptive capacity bear directly on this.
- **Refuter:** a rival reaches the same market outcome from public atoms alone, with no import and no copy of the habitat, and the gap closes; or the atom is shown derivable from atoms already in the rival's closure.

### C2: business-facing; checkable against capabilities and routines research

- **Statement:** the durable object is the firm-and-habitat system and the unrecorded arrangement, not the dataset.
- **Source, verbatim:** Fig 11 :39 *"capability is a property of agent-and-habitat, never of the agent alone."* Fig 11 :40 *"An improvement that does not change contact changes nothing, however much it improves."* Fig 12 :109 *"Which boundary matters is the one the record does not cross."* Fig 12 :125 *"A composition costs in two currencies, and they do not add."*
- **What to check:** does the empirical literature treat the advantage as embedded routines and reconfiguration capacity rather than as a stock of data? Teece, Pisano and Shuen (1997) on dynamic capabilities, Makadok (2001) on organizationally embedded non-transferable capabilities, and Polanyi (1966) on tacit knowledge are the relevant checks.
- **Refuter:** supplying the data alone, with the habitat otherwise fixed, produces and sustains the market gap; or the gap persists after the method is fully codified, published and adopted, or erodes while it stays tacit.

### C3: framework-internal anchor rule

- **Statement:** the market is the anchor, data volume is frame-internal, and overlapping evidence pools dissolve the advantage.
- **Source, verbatim:** Fig 2 :29 *"A measurement, an experiment, a counter, a market, a patient outcome."* Fig 2 :45 *"Frames range against the ground. They do not negotiate with it."* Fig 1 :27 *"They are frame-internal. A frame cannot score itself with a quantity it also produces."* Fig 8 :49 *"completely are one frame wearing two names."*
- **What to check:** is the outcome measured at the market per slot, and is evidence-pool overlap reported before exclusivity is claimed?
- **Refuter:** a gap that grows in internal metrics after the market has stopped paying; or a persistent measured difference between firms demonstrably drawing on the same evidence pool.

## 3. What is explicitly not claimed

- Not that data cannot be copied, and not that a rival's failure to find it proves impossibility.
- Not that any named dataset guarantees a moat of any length. The framework returns a shape, not a name.
- Not that expert agreement is corroboration. Fig 7 :35 *"A room’s agreement is variation, not a verdict. Selection between rooms is where the ground votes."*
- Not a theory of everything, and no seventh primitive. Fig 13 :112 *"More compositions never add a member."*
- Not `grounded`. Nothing here is asserted as settled by experiment.
- Not an assertion that the business sources cited agree because they are independent. RBV, dynamic capabilities and absorptive capacity partly share a strategy canon and are counted as one witness at most.

## 5. What would settle it

A ground reading, not agreement. Five tests are specified in `07` §5: import test, data-only transfer test, codification test, overlap test, market test. Any one of them, run with a pre-registered criterion, can fire the refuters in section 2 and move the claim toward `grounded` or force a re-census.

## 6. What an expert can check in twenty minutes

1. Read Fig 6 :21, :23, Fig 8 :32, :39 and Fig 11 :39. Five short passages.
2. Check C1 against the imitability and absorptive-capacity literature: is derivation from acquisition actually distinguished?
3. Check C2 against a capability or routines study you know: is the durable object the dataset or the arrangement?
4. Name one instrument, study design or dataset that would distinguish import from derivation in a real market, or say why none is possible.
5. Send the refutation. A failure is more useful than an endorsement.
