# Outreach draft: check the output before you decide about the reading

**Use:** one-to-one, or as a cover letter attached to a submission through the public venues in `03_public_contact_points.csv`. **Not** for bulk send.

**Hard precondition:** do not send until the shape claim and its refuter in `08_expert_verification_packet.md` survive the recipient's own check. The offer collapses on contact if stage 1 does not hold.

**Subject:** One checkable claim about why a single outcome occurs

> Hi [name],
>
> I have one specific output I would like you to check against what you already know. I am not asking you to read any framework first, and I am not asking you to agree with anything. If the output does not survive, there is nothing else to look at.
>
> **The output (`specified`, not `grounded`):** the single-outcome question is a live residual on one slot, not a failure of the statistics. The theory predicts a distribution over components; one detector reading arrives. The Born rule matches the frequencies across many runs while the one-run question stays open. The figures put this in effect terms: a global residual near zero with one live slot residual is a legal state, and aggregating across slots is how a live signal disappears into an average.
>
> The question also comes apart into at least three conditions: which basis, why no interference at macroscopic scale, and why one definite result. Decoherence handles the first two and leaves the third. A lever that clears two conditions of a compound is not a solution to the compound.
>
> **The verdict I am recording before showing you any theory:** under the figures, a definite outcome is not reachable by composing inside the unitary closure alone. The framework can characterise what a missing term must look like, but it cannot name which of collapse, hidden variables, branching or a cut is right. That part is an honest gap, not a claim. I am not asserting that one interpretation is correct.
>
> **What I am not claiming:** that this settles the interpretation debate, that consciousness causes collapse, that the wavefunction is "just information," or that the framework has derived quantum mechanics. Those are imports or readings whose provenance has not been shown. A room of people agreeing about them would be a convention, not a check.
>
> **What would refute the output:**
> 1. a derivation of a unique definite outcome inside unitary quantum mechanics with no added or reinterpreted term;
> 2. a resolution-preserving reading in which the per-slot residual trends down, so the live slot was an artefact of aggregation;
> 3. a single mechanism shown to close basis, interference and outcomes together, making the compound reading false;
> 4. a source-discriminating experiment that names the mechanism without an import.
>
> I wrote these down before sending this. If any fires, I withdraw the shape claim. A one-line "this fails because..." is more useful to me than agreement. If you would rather not engage, no reply is needed.
>
> **If it survives, and only then:** the figures, the essay set and the figure-grounded audit are below. The figures, not my summary of them, are the authority.
> - Figures and formula for direct download (branch `general-agent`): https://github.com/IsaiahN/tether/tree/general-agent/figures
> - Full architecture (Medium, 19 min): https://medium.com/@IsaiahNwukor/arrival-of-the-fittest-the-tether-architecture-1de7edf641ec
> - Worked narrative with the per-slot residual and the six-term derivation: https://adventuresinml.substack.com/p/how-intelligence-actually-works
>
> **If you want to use the machinery:** the formula is in the figures link. Run it on a problem you know well and can check against a ground. Write down what would refute your result *before* you run it: the framework's own limit is that a loop cannot check the direction of its own question, and the essay warns that outputs "could never be verified if the solutions presented were not contrived to fit the formula." A reported failure is more useful to me than a reported success.

## Two-stage offer, stated as a rule

1. **Stage 1.** Send the residual, the shape claim and the four refuters. Ask what the recipient would count as a failure. Record their verdict **before** any theory is shown.
2. **Stage 2, opt-in only.** If they are curious, offer the links. Keep the figures as the authority and the summary as a summary.
3. **Stage 3, opt-in only.** Offer the formula as a method to reuse. Each user writes their own refuter and states their own mode.

A reply at stage 1 that refutes the claim is a success: it adds a term to the record and removes standing from a composition, which is how the reach falls safely (Fig 6 amendment on reachability). No reply is silence, not consent, and it is never counted as corroboration.
