## 1. A shape is a constraint, not a filler

The figures README states the limit: the framework can say "what a missing thing would have to look like (a shape, not a name)." Fig 9 :32 "you get a kind of thing, never the answer itself." Fig 13 :135 "It cannot say which primitive is missing, because naming it would mean already holding it."

From *The Library of Babel* (opened this session): "you can cut your search short if the set of books returned is small and descriptive enough that it provides a general shape of the answer that satisfies your question."

So a **shape** is the narrowed form of an answer you can state before you can name it. It constrains; it does not fill. For this question:

- **Shape:** a term outside the unitary closure that closes the definite-outcome condition of the compound after basis and interference are handled, and arrives with a provenanced source gate and a shadow test.
- **Name:** collapse, hidden variables, branching, a cut, or consciousness. Each is a candidate import; none is licensed by the figures.

The distinction matters because the answer to the user's question, "why a single outcome occurs," is often taken to *be* a name. The framework can say the shape that any such name must have. It cannot supply the name. That is the honest stop (§5).

## 2. How a shape is obtained

- *Library of Babel*: use the vocabulary you already have that is closest to the thing, look for recurring new terms that overlap or correlate with it, recurse. "With each new term gained, you narrow the space by definition and deepen your vocabulary."
- Fig 6 :30 "The description travels. Fails by being sharpened into terms only we can read. Cast it in effect terms." Fig 9 :30-31 "your words are borrowed too, so describe the effect rather than the cause."
- *Decoding Tacit Knowledge* (opened this session): "When contact isn't possible, the best you can send is a plain description of what you'd expect to see, because anyone can check that against the world."
- *Library of Babel* on stopping: "You will know that you are done because the finish line will also be the starting place, as there is nowhere else to search and nothing else that can describe the answer better." The paradox is that by then "you have already composed the answer through the process of locating it."

Applied here: describe the residual per slot (not the cause), split the compound, then let the field names arrive as imports with provenance. Naming an interpretation first is the inversion Fig 9 :27 warns about.

## 3. What licenses a name

Only two things, both outside the shape itself:

1. **The ground settles it.** Formula step 5: "the ground settles it. Verified only by what pays. A gate is not the ground." *The Hard Problem is About Source* (opened this session): "The answer is unreached until a better instrument exists." For the measurement problem this means a source-discriminating experiment, not a further argument about interpretations.
2. **A provenanced import from a frame whose closure differs** (Fig 6 :23, Fig 8 :50-52), with a source gate and a shadow test.

*They're Scanning Books* (opened this session) sharpens this: "The real import is not a new term in the existing space. It is a new dimension of the space. Importing a term extends what can be said; importing an axis extends what can be perceived at all." And: "Declaring the primitives does not make them neutral. It makes the choice visible, which is worth a great deal, because a visible choice can be revised and an emergent one cannot."

A missing term that would yield definite outcomes is not a new arrangement of the unitary closure; it is a new axis. Calling it a mere missing term understates what would have to be imported. That is why F6 in `01_figure_grounded_audit.md` records a gap rather than a name.

## 4. Why "consciousness causes collapse" is the specific forbidden error

*The Hard Problem is About Source* gives the mechanism. Metamerism: an instrument reads green and "cannot comment on the source" - one green light, or yellow+blue, or RGB, or CMYK, or a scheme not yet discovered. The reading does not determine the source. "If several paths create the same behavior, then behavior is a projection with lossy information." And: "An instrument that can't distinguish these cases demonstrates its own inadequacy." It was the spectrometer, not an argument, that resolved it.

The definite outcome is a reading. Several mechanisms (collapse, hidden variables, branching, a cut) can produce that same reading. Naming one from the reading alone is the metamerism error. What is owed is a discriminating instrument, not a name.

There is a second, sharper point. *Decoding Tacit Knowledge* (opened this session) applies the framework directly to this exact question:

> "Superposition isn't ignorance: it's the state where the distinction hasn't been made yet. Measurement doesn't reveal the value. It creates value by collapsing the space of values."
>
> "Opening the box settles it. (for both the person, and the cat in question)."
>
> "Physicists still argue over how to interpret quantum measurement. This is just my reading, used here for structure's sake."

The author's own caveat is the important part. This is a **structural reading**, not a physical result. It says what measurement *does* in the framework's terms (contact resolves a distinction), not which physical mechanism performs it. Quoting the first sentence as if it settled the interpretation debate would be exactly the error the caveat forbids. It is recorded here as a framework-author source with high overlap, so it corroborates the shape at low independent weight (Fig 2 :40).

The same reading appears in *How Intelligence Actually Works* (opened this session), in a "Stress Test 2" that treats Schrodinger's cat and the halting problem together:

> "Schrodinger's cat: the answer doesn't exist until the question is *contacted*. Superposition isn't ignorance: it's the state where the distinction hasn't been made yet. Measurement doesn't reveal the value. It creates the value by collapsing the space of values."
>
> "Opening the box answers whether Schrodinger's cat is alive or dead, but opening the box also answers, for the cat, whether the world it once knew is alive or dead."
>
> "One measurement can dissolve a whole cluster of questions. Not because they were secretly the same question, but because they were all undetermined at the same joint where contact was required."

That last sentence is the strongest available corroboration of `01` F2. The framework's own author describes **a cluster of questions, not secretly one question**, dissolved at one contact. It does not name the physical mechanism, and the same essay states the standing limit: "Because it is a metatheory, it cannot be validated, nor can it validate itself." So even the author's direct treatment of the cat ends at a shape, not a physical answer. It is high-overlap corroboration (same author as the figures), not an independent witness.

## 5. Q: what licenses a particular interpretation as the answer to "why one outcome"?

**Answered, no further question needed.** Nothing in the framework does. An interpretation is licensed only by the ground settling a proposed mechanism, or by a provenanced import with a shadow test. Until one of those, the name is the searcher's own contribution and must be recorded as such.

**Does the question itself need re-census?** Partly. "Why a single outcome occurs" bundles a factual question (what physically happens in a measurement) with a constitutive one (what counts as an outcome for a detector). Fig 2 :36: "An anchor is legitimate when the question is constitutively about it." The detector reading is legitimate as an anchor for the second. It is only an instrument for the first, and "instruments can be wrong on axes they do not measure" (Fig 2 :37-38). So the single-question framing is exactly what §2 of `01` unbundles.

## 6. Two consequences for outreach and for this document

**Prestige is not action.** From *The Recursive Self-Improvement Trap* (opened this session): "Prestige must never be allowed to validate an action directly." And: "Prestige can fund an action. It can give an action a budget to be tried. But it cannot settle an action. Only the ground can settle it. The Ground Maintainer must be blind to prestige." A list of famous interpreters who agree with a reading is the named collapse, not corroboration. The legitimate ask is the opposite: give the claim a budget to be tried, and let the ground, an instrument or an experiment, settle it.

**This audit is a simulation, not a ground reading.** Same essay: "LLMs can quickly simulate an approximate answer/output with the formula found in the architecture post. It will often be better than the generic outputs that you will usually get otherwise too. However, the truly testable output would need to be derived from the real architecture or working the formula yourself." And: "You may all agree and be wrong... You need ground truth to define where you stand." So `01`, `07`, `09` and this file are simulated derivations checked for internal consistency against the figures. They are **not** tests of the claim and must be labelled as such. They cannot settle anything they report on.

## 7. Correction record

An earlier reading in this folder's planning treated the rule as "the framework returns a shape, never a name, so no interpretation may be mentioned." **Narrowed.** The README's phrase governs a *missing* primitive, one not held in Γ. The six held terms are space, time, energy, spectrum, gradient, network; a mechanism that yields definite outcomes is not among them, so the rule applies here in full, not by analogy. What is still allowed is to *list* the candidate names as imports with provenance, which is what `01` F6 and `06` do. The prohibition is on asserting one as the framework's result. Both the rule and its scope are recorded so neither is quietly lost.
