## What is deliberately not here

- No personal emails of named individuals.
- No ranking of people by how likely they are to believe a reading of the measurement problem.
- No bulk-send plan.

Three reasons, in the figures' own words:

1. **A room does not upgrade the label.** Fig 2 :35 "Alignment is triangulation, not negotiation. A mirror cannot correct you." Gathering more interpreters who agree does not add an anchor.
2. **Selecting for receptiveness manufactures the disqualifying correlation.** Fig 9 :60 "Use filters for the budget and witnesses for the verdict. Never let a filter hand you a verdict."
3. **Asking for trust before a check spends the only thing an outside frame contributes.** Fig 10 :42 "A verifier that can reconstruct the claim cannot verify it."

Legally: scraping personal emails for unsolicited bulk outreach breaches GDPR and CAN-SPAM style rules in many jurisdictions. That is a practical failure on top of the structural one.

## Honest standing of every claim here

This is a **simulated derivation checked for internal consistency against the figures, not a ground reading.** From *The Recursive Self-Improvement Trap* ( essay, verified 2026-11-23): "the truly testable output would need to be derived from the real architecture or working the formula yourself." It cannot settle the question it reports on. The verdict is about the **shape** of an answer and about which figure lines govern, not about which interpretation of quantum mechanics is true.
