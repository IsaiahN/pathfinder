## QUESTION

Decide, in effect terms, what the Tether says about this question: why a single outcome occurs when a quantum superposition is measured. Option A: the question is one hard problem whose answer is a particular interpretation (collapse, hidden variables, branching, a cut). Option B: the question is a union compound with a live per-slot residual, whose resolving mechanism is outside the unitary closure and whose name the framework cannot supply. What each leads to: A asserts a name the framework says it cannot give, aggregates the live slot into ensemble statistics, and spends the independence of the people asked to check it; B states a shape, splits the compound, writes refuters in advance, and leaves the mechanism open until an instrument settles it.

## DOMAIN CENSUS

R by arity, symmetry and scale, then one line per domain with actors / currency / anchor; partition or union; order by dependency.

- **R (the residual).** Many superposition components map to one detector value. Arity: many-to-one. Symmetry: components interchangeable with respect to "which outcome" until registration; registration breaks that symmetry, and the basis choice is a second symmetry also broken at registration. Scale: the step (one run) and the cycle (many runs), with the aggregation between them.
- **Domain 1, the unitary formalism frame.** Actors: state vector, Hamiltonian, amplitudes, basis, tensor product, Born rule. Currency: unitary evolution and probability. Anchor: prepared state and detector reading. A nameable anchor exists.
- **Domain 2, the detector and record frame.** Actors: pointer, environment, observer's record. Currency: definite outcomes and frequencies. Anchor: the detector click. Legitimate because the question is constitutively about definite outcomes (Fig 2 :36).
- **Domain 3, the Tether composition frame.** Actors: six terms, seven bonds, negation, Γ, R, ground. Currency: composition in closure(Γ) then settlement. Anchor: figures as metalaws and the ground for measurement.
- **Domain 4, verification and outreach.** Actors: expert readers, interpretations as frames. Currency: agreement and residuals. Anchor: the ground, never agreement. In this simulated run all domains share one evidence pool, so this seat's independence is zero (declared, not hidden).
- **Union, not partition.** Basis, interference and outcomes overlap; the overlap is a compound with its own loop (Fig 9 :54-57).
- **Order by dependency.** Describe R per slot; hold the detector as anchor; settle basis and interference; refuse a false held term before minting; only then import (Fig 3 :20, Formula step 2).

Strain test applied: the six-term substrate does not compose Hilbert-space states. Forcing it to do so would contradict ordinary quantum mechanics, so Fig 13 is used only for its meta-level unreachability and shape claims. That exclusion is a refused strain, recorded in `01` F4 and §7, not a hidden one.

## VERDICT (from the figures)

The structural verdict stands and is triangulated: the single-outcome question is a live per-slot residual that ensemble accuracy does not remove; it is a union compound of basis, interference and outcomes; the detector reading is the anchor and the interpretations are frames; a definite outcome is not reachable by composing inside the unitary closure, so any resolution imports a primitive; and the framework returns a shape, not a name. The physical mechanism is a **gap**: collapse, hidden variables, branching and a cut are candidate imports, and nothing in the figures, and no source read here, selects among them. This is `specified` on the structure, `general` on the shape, and `gap` on the mechanism. Four pre-registered refuters are in `01` §5. Nothing here is `grounded`.

- **cost if wrong:** bounded and locatable. The packet states its mode, writes refuters before the conclusion and records the lineage, so an error is findable rather than invisible. The real cost is spending the independence of outside readers by sending an interpretation as if it were a derivation, which Fig 2 :42 prices: "Buy independence in proportion to the cost of being wrong." That cost is avoidable by leading with the per-slot residual and its refuter.
- **undoable:** yes. The named mechanisms are withheld rather than asserted, so no physical claim has to be withdrawn. A later instrument can name the mechanism without touching the shape, and a sent packet can be superseded by the corrected one. The refuters stay in the record either way.
- **reversed by:** a ground reading, not agreement. A source-discriminating experiment that names the resolving mechanism would close the mechanism gap; a pure derivation of a unique outcome from unitary dynamics with no added atom would break the unreachability verdict; a reading under which the per-slot residual trends down would break the residual verdict; a single mechanism closing all three conditions would break the compound verdict. Until one of these fires, the verdict stands as `candidate`.
