# The Tether's answer on the measurement problem

**Mode: `specified` on the structural verdicts, `general` on the shape. Not `grounded`.** This is a shape and a route, not a physical mechanism. Simulated derivation checked against the figures (`05_what_shape_means.md` §6), not a ground reading. No measurement was taken.

## 1. The residual it answers

A prepared state has k components; the prediction Γ(b,a) for the outcome slot is a distribution over them; the detector returns one definite value. R on that slot is live even though the global R is near zero, because Born statistics match the frequencies across many runs.

> Fig 1 :13 "a global R near zero with one live slot residual is a legal state"
> Fig 1 :22 "Aggregating across slots is how a live signal disappears into an average."

The question "why one outcome" is therefore not answered by pointing at the accuracy of the statistics. That move aggregates the live signal away. The question is a union compound of at least three conditions: preferred basis, non-observability of interference, and definite outcomes. Fig 9 :4 "Split it rather than search."

## 2. The shape

1. **The live signal is per slot.** The single-run question survives the many-run success. Any answer that does not address the per-slot residual has answered a different question. (`specified`, triangulated: Fig 1, Symbols table)
2. **The problem is a compound, not one lever.** Decoherence handles interference and largely basis, and leaves outcomes standing. A lever that clears two conditions leaves a categorically different residue. (`specified`, triangulated: Fig 9 plus independent reviews)
3. **The detector reading is the anchor.** The question is constitutively about definite outcomes, so the click is a legitimate anchor. Interpretations are frames; they range against the ground separately and do not negotiate with it. (`general`, triangulated: Fig 2, Fig 7)
4. **Definite outcomes are unreachable inside the unitary closure.** Composition cannot add an atom, and closure is idempotent. Every proposed resolution imports something: collapse, hidden variables, branching, or a cut. (`specified`, triangulated: Fig 6, Fig 8)
5. **The framework returns a shape, not a name.** What the missing term must look like is sayable; which of the four families it is, is not. "Unreachable is a verdict here, not a silence." (`general`, triangulated: Fig 9, Fig 13)
6. **A refusal may be owed before a mint.** The held term most worth refusing is the one whose residual persists and does not trend down, likely "the quantum state is complete." The sought-for shape must be bound as an ordinary term before the run, because the loop cannot check its own direction. (`specified`, single-source: Fig 5, Formula "Direction")

## 3. What it explicitly does not say

- Not that any interpretation is correct. The verdict on the physical mechanism is a **gap**.
- Not that consciousness causes collapse, that the wavefunction is "just information," or that decoherence solves outcomes.
- Not a derivation of quantum mechanics from the six-term substrate. A Hilbert-space state is not one of the six; that reading was refused at the strain test.
- Not a theory of everything. No final room: there is always an outside frame to import from.
- Not `grounded`. No source-discriminating instrument was run.

## 4. What would settle it - the ground

A source-discriminating instrument, not a further argument. From *The Hard Problem is About Source* (essay, opened 2026-11-23): an instrument that reads green "cannot comment on the source"; the spectrometer resolved it. Here:

- **Instrument test:** an experiment that distinguishes the candidate mechanisms at the relevant scale, so that one reading of the outcome is discriminated from the others. This converts the gap into a grounded result.
- **Closure test:** the F1 refuter in `01` §5: a pure derivation of a unique outcome from unitary dynamics with no added or reinterpreted term. If it succeeds, verdict 4 falls.
- **Slot test:** a resolution-preserving reading in which the per-slot residual trends down. If it succeeds, verdict 1 falls.
- **Compound test:** one mechanism closing basis, interference and outcomes together. If it succeeds, verdict 2 falls.
- **Standing refuters:** the four in `01` §5, written before the conclusion. Non-refuters: agreement, prestige, and assertions of a solution without a checkable prediction.

The observations that would move this to `grounded` are experiments, not consensus. "You may all agree and be wrong... You need ground truth to define where you stand" (*The Recursive Self-Improvement Trap*).

## 5. The honest stop

**Unreached, not unreachable.** The missing mechanism is not in the unitary closure, so it is owed; the framework can characterise its shape and cannot name it. Fig 13 :135 "It cannot say which primitive is missing, because naming it would mean already holding it." The answer stops at the limit of the available instruments and records the lineage so the stop is checkable. Indiscernibility is not identity.

There is one more limit to declare: this whole answer is a simulation checked for internal consistency against the figures. It cannot settle the question it reports on. That is a property of the derivation, not an evasion of the physics.

## 6. One-paragraph version for outreach

> The Tether does not pick a winner among the interpretations of quantum measurement, and it does not claim to have derived one. It says that the single-outcome question is a live residual on one slot, which the correctness of the Born statistics does not remove because statistics are an aggregate; that the problem is a compound of basis, interference and outcomes, so a lever that clears the first two leaves the third standing; that the detector reading is the anchor and the interpretations are frames that must range against it rather than negotiate with it; and that a definite outcome is not reachable by composing inside the unitary closure alone, so any resolution imports something. The framework can say what that import must look like, but not which one it is. What it offers is the shape any answer must have, four refuters written in advance, and the instrument that would close the gap. The mechanism stays open until the ground settles it.
