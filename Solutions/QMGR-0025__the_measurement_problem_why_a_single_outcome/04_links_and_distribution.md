## Distribution: offer, do not push

- **Fig 10 :52 "Bring a characterised residual, never the source."** Sending the full architecture before anyone has described a residual sends a recording. Lead with the refuter and the shape claim.
- **Fig 10 :42 "A verifier that can reconstruct the claim cannot verify it."** A recipient who has absorbed the framework before being asked for a verdict has joined the frame being checked.
- **Fig 9 :27 "describe it before you go looking, or any frame you pick will seem to fit"** Ask what they would expect to see if the shape claim were true before giving them the links.
- **Fig 9 :60 "Use filters for the budget and witnesses for the verdict. Never let a filter hand you a verdict."** A list selected for people who already agree produces a convention, not corroboration.
- **Fig 7 :35 "A room's agreement is variation, not a verdict"** A show of hands among interpreters is not a measurement.

**Recommended order in a reply:** (1) the per-slot residual and its refuter; (2) ask what the recipient would count as a failure; (3) record their verdict; (4) only then the links, with the note that the figures, not the summary, are the authority.

## The two-stage offer

1. **Stage 1 before stage 2.** Fig 10 :52 and Fig 2 :45 "Frames range against the ground. They do not negotiate with it." The recipient checks the output without holding the framework's interior.
2. **Stage 2 is opt-in by construction.** Seat 4 must not hold the interiors; later agreement is never pooled as corroboration (Fig 2 :40).
3. **Stage 3 sends a method, which is permitted.** Fig 4 :22 "Only methods cross up. Only head starts come down." The formula is a generator, so giving it away does not give away an answer.
4. **Stage 3 still needs a ground per problem.** `THE_FORMULA.md` "Direction": a frame "cannot check the direction of its own question." Each user must write down what would refute their result before running it, and label the mode of what they get.

**Hard precondition:** stages 1 to 3 only work if the stage-1 output survives. `01_figure_grounded_audit.md` F1, F2 and F4 are the load-bearing claims. If a recipient refutes F1 or F4, the packet is withdrawn, not defended.
