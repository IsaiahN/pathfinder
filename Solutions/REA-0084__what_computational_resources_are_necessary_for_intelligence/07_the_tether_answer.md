# The Tether's answer on computational resources and intelligence

**Mode: `specified` on the substantive claims, `general` on the structural shape. Not `grounded`.** Simulated derivation checked against the figures (`05_what_shape_means.md` §6), not a ground reading.

## 1. The residual it answers

The question asks for the resources necessary for intelligence and for fundamental efficiency limits. The figure census shows that this is a **union** of at least four conditions: the description price, the settlement price, the habitat, and the kind of limit (Fig 9 :54: *"And when the split lands on a union rather than a partition, unbundle before you search again."*). It is also the wrong question before it is the right one: Fig 11 :35: **"The wrong question, and this figure is the replacement for it."**

## 2. The shape

1. **The denominator is not the agent.** Fig 11 :39: **"So the answerable question is not what intelligence is, but what makes a system capable. And capability is a property of agent-and-habitat, never of the agent alone."** A resource budget aimed at the agent in isolation is aimed at the wrong object. Fig 11 :40: **"A system cannot be made more capable by improving the agent in isolation. An improvement that does not change contact changes nothing, however much it improves."**

2. **Cost has two currencies that do not add.** Fig 12 :125-126: **"A composition costs in two currencies, and they do not add."** One prices the description, one prices finding out whether it holds. Fig 12 :129: **"Summing them into one figure is the error this names, not the fix for it. A price in bits and a price in actions are two readings, held apart."** An efficiency frontier in FLOPs or watts prices one side only.

3. **There are two limits, and they issue different instructions.** Fig 8 :32: **"Closure is idempotent. Composing inside a closed set never leaves it, so no search, however long, adds an atom. This one is definitional: it is what closure means."** Fig 8 :40: **"Underivable-in-principle and unreachable-at-this-budget are different facts, and they issue different instructions."** If the missing thing is already in the closure, more search is the remedy. If it is outside, an import is required, with a source gate and a shadow test. Fig 8 :43: importing against a budget limit adds an atom that was not needed.

4. **No finite budget arrives.** Fig 7 :30-31: *"every room closes some of the gap; no finite number of them ever arrives."* Fig 7 :42: **"The chain approaches the truth only as the world runs out of ways to refuse it."** This is a barrier on arrival, not a number on a curve. The room must differ from the last one: Fig 7 :36: *"A chain of mirrors is one long room. The next room must differ from the last, and match the residual it surfaced."*

5. **The metrics of the field are filters, not witnesses.** Fig 1 :27: **"They are frame-internal. A frame cannot score itself with a quantity it also produces."** Fig 9 :60: **"Use filters for the budget and witnesses for the verdict. Never let a filter hand you a verdict."** FLOPs, parameter counts, benchmark scores, and compression achieved rank what to try first. They do not settle whether capability moved.

6. **A test harness is a substituted habitat.** Fig 11 :70: **"A test harness is a substituted habitat. Which is why a synthetic solve proves wiring and never capability."** A benchmark result licenses a claim about wiring, not about the original habitat.

## 3. What it explicitly does not say

- Not a resource number. Fig 9 :32: **"you get a kind of thing, never the answer itself."**
- Not that compute never helps. For a budget-limited gap, more search is the correct remedy (Fig 8 :41).
- Not that physical ceilings are unreal. Landauer's bound, the measured confirmation, and Lloyd's cosmological bounds are real; they price physical resources, not capability, and they are secondary corroboration here.
- Not a claim that the two readings are the same kind of object. As normally written they share no atoms and are not isomers; over the shared six-term set they are a checkable isomer claim (`06_isomers_and_order.md`).
- Not grounded. No measurement of capability against a ground was taken.

## 4. What would settle it - the ground

A ground reading, not agreement. The pre-registered refuter from `01_figure_grounded_audit.md` §5:

1. A measurement in which a purely agent-internal increase in compute, with contact held fixed, moves the ground (breaks claim 1).
2. A single accounting that prices description and settlement cost in the same units without loss (breaks claim 2).
3. A search inside a fixed closure that adds a primitive to that closure (breaks claim 3).
4. A case in which underivability and budget limitation issue the same instruction (breaks claim 3).
5. A finite last room after which nothing further can be asked (breaks claim 4).

An outside checker can also name the discriminator they would use. Fig 6 :34: *"The question is not whether a sensor could exist. It is whether anything, at any resolution, is already returning something that fails to resolve."*

## 5. The honest stop

**Unreached, not unreachable.** The shape stops at the limit of the available instruments and records the lineage so the stop is checkable. Two strains remain named, not hidden: the action price of Fig 12 is not the Landauer energy price, and the barrier limit of Fig 8 is not the asymptotic no-last-room limit of Fig 7. Fig 6 :27: **"Which is why some unreachability is depth and some is genuine absence, and from inside the frame the two look the same."** The canonical rule applies: indiscernibility is not identity.

## 6. One-paragraph version for outreach

> The Tether does not give a number of FLOPs or a parameter count, and it does not claim that compute is irrelevant. It says that the question as posed re-censes: capability is a property of agent and habitat, so a budget aimed at the agent alone is aimed at the wrong denominator. Cost has two currencies, one for describing a solution and one for checking it against the ground, and summing them into one efficiency figure is the named error. There are two different limits: a budget ceiling, which more search closes, and a barrier, which only an import from a different frame crosses. No finite budget arrives at a last room, because there is no last room. What the framework offers is that shape, five refuters written in advance, and the instruments that would close it. The measurements stay open until the ground settles them.
