## 1. A shape is a constraint, not a filler

Fig 9 :32: **"you get a kind of thing, never the answer itself."**
Fig 13 :134: **"Unreachable is a verdict here, not a silence"**, and Fig 13 :135: *"It cannot say which primitive is missing, because naming it would mean already holding it."*

A **shape** is the narrowed form of an answer you can state before you can name it. It constrains; it does not fill. For this question the shape is not "N FLOPs are enough." It is:

> capability belongs to agent and habitat; cost has two currencies that do not add; there is a budget limit that more search closes and a barrier that only an import crosses; and no finite budget arrives at a last room.

That shape rules out answers without supplying the number. A resource number would be a name where the framework returns a shape.

## 2. How the shape is obtained

- Describe the residual in effect terms first. Fig 9 :27-28: **"describe it before you go looking, or any frame you pick will seem to fit."**
- Fig 9 :30-31: **"your words are borrowed too, so describe the effect rather than the cause."** For this question, the effect is: what observable changes when a unit of compute is added, and in which domain?
- Fig 6 :30: *"outward: does another frame already hold it? The description travels. Fails by being sharpened into terms only we can read. Cast it in effect terms."* This is why the world search covered hardware engineering, physics of computation, human cognition, animal behaviour, and search theory, and not only AI scaling papers.
- From the exemplar's record of *The Library of Babel*: *"You can cut your search short if the set of books returned is small and descriptive enough that it provides a general shape of the answer that satisfies your question."* The narrowed set here is the four-part shape above.

## 3. What licenses a name

Only two things, both outside the shape itself:

1. **The ground settles it.** Formula step 5: *"the ground settles it. Verified only by what pays. A gate is not the ground."* For this question a ground would be a measurement that moves when contact changes, not when a parameter count changes.
2. **A provenanced import from a frame whose closure differs** (Fig 6 :23, Fig 8 :50-52), with a source gate and a shadow test.

Fig 11 :36 is decisive for the unmaintained case: **"Where nothing maintains anything, intelligence is a label applied afterwards to a lineage that met its ground. Nothing was intelligent; selection ran. The word does no work there."** A resource number assigned to such a label names something the figure says has no work to do.

## 4. Why a single efficiency number is the specific forbidden error

The exemplar's record of *The Hard Problem is About Source* gives the mechanism: metamerism. An instrument reads green and *"cannot comment on the source"*; several different sources can yield the same reading. *"If several paths create the same behavior, then behavior is a projection with lossy information."* It was the spectrometer, not an argument, that resolved it.

Applied here: two systems with the same benchmark score and the same parameter count can have reached that reading through different habitats, different contacts, and different settlement histories. The reading does not determine the source. Naming "compute" as the source of the capability is the metamerism error. What is owed is a better instrument that discriminates among sources, not a larger number.

This point is **not retracted** and is independent of the eight essays being re-fetched: Fig 1 :27 supplies the same rule in figure terms, **"They are frame-internal. A frame cannot score itself with a quantity it also produces."**

## 5. Q - what shape does this question actually need?

**Answered, no further figure question needed.** The question needs a four-part shape, not a resource total:

| part of the shape | figure | what it rules out |
|---|---|---|
| capability is agent-and-habitat | Fig 11 :39-40 | any answer that treats the agent's budget as the whole denominator |
| two currencies, not one | Fig 12 :125-129 | any single efficiency figure in FLOPs, watts, or parameters |
| two limits, different instructions | Fig 8 :40-43 | any answer that prescribes "more compute" or "new primitive" globally |
| no last room | Fig 7 :30-31, :42 | any finite efficiency threshold presented as arrival |

The shape does **not** include a number, and the framework is forbidden to supply one (Fig 9 :32, Fig 13 :135).

## 6. Two consequences for outreach

**Prestige is not action.** From the exemplar's record of *The Recursive Self-Improvement Trap*: *"Prestige can fund an action. It can give an action a budget to be tried. But it cannot settle an action. Only the ground can settle it."* A list of famous labs or well-funded systems does not corroborate an efficiency claim; it funds a trial of it.

**My own audit is a simulation, not a ground reading.** Same essay: *"LLMs can quickly simulate an approximate answer/output... However, the truly testable output would need to be derived from the real architecture or working the formula yourself."* So `01` is a simulated derivation, checked for internal consistency against the figures. It is not a test of the claim and must be labelled as such. It cannot settle anything it reports on.
