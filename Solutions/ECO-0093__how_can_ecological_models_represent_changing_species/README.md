## Restatement in effect terms

A model predicts against a field observation and leaves a residual on each species, site and season it tracks. When interactions change, a fixed-interaction model leaves a live residual on some slots. Adding interaction terms can shrink that residual, but each added term costs bits to state and actions to settle in the field. The decision is not "flexible or simple"; it is which arrangement of a small set of terms keeps the per-slot residual small while the number of actions needed to settle the model stays inside the field budget. An answer would look like a rule for choosing that arrangement, with its failure condition written in advance.

## What is deliberately not here

- No personal emails of named individuals.
- No ranking of people by how likely they are to agree with this answer.
- No bulk-send plan.

Three reasons, in the figures' own words:

1. **A room does not upgrade the label.** Fig 2 :35 "Alignment is triangulation, not negotiation. A mirror cannot correct you." Adding more agreeable readers raises the vocabulary and does not add an anchor.
2. **Selecting for agreement manufactures the disqualifying correlation.** Seat Map :163 "High correlation is disqualifying for a verdict and efficient for a convention." A list chosen for receptiveness is a list chosen to agree.
3. **Asking for trust before a check spends the only thing an outside reader contributes.** Fig 10 :42 "A verifier that can reconstruct the claim cannot verify it." The first contact carries the residual and its refuter, not the framework.

Legally: scraping personal emails for unsolicited bulk outreach breaches GDPR and CAN-SPAM style rules in many jurisdictions. That is a practical failure on top of the structural one.
