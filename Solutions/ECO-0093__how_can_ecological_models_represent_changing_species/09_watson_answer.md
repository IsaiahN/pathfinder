## QUESTION

Decide, in effect terms, what shape an ecological model must have if it is to track changing species interactions while remaining testable. Option A: add interaction terms until every species-site-season slot fits, accepting the resulting description and field cost. Option B: treat the question as a union compound, enumerate relations from contact, split the conditions, price description and settlement separately, and admit only terms that pay. What each leads to: A can fit one season perfectly and proposes arrangements it cannot afford to test; B leaves some slots explicitly unresolved and states a field failure that could fire, but requires a per-slot residual and a recorded field budget before it can be used.

## DOMAIN CENSUS

R by arity, symmetry and scale, then one line per domain with actors, currency and anchor; partition or union; order by dependency.

- **R (the residual).** The gap between a model prediction and a field observation on a species-site-season slot. Arity: many slots; a global fit near zero with one live slot is a legal state. Symmetry: an interaction's value is context-relative and ordered, not carried by the species pair. Scale: within-season rewiring is first order; species turnover and cross-habitat cascades are second order.
- **Domain 1, the model frame (Γ).** Actors: species variables, interaction coefficients, functional groups, model-selection criteria. Currency: fit and description length. Anchor: independent field data and held-out seasons. Question type: partition of legal compositions and their prices.
- **Domain 2, the habitat / real ecosystem.** Actors: species, conditions (light, water, temperature, season, terrain), relations (pollination, predation, competition, dependency). Currency: contact and cascade. Anchor: what the ground keeps; it does not move when the model changes. Question type: enumeration of what contacts the residual, not composition of a standing list.
- **Domain 3, verification / field testing.** Actors: field observers, experiments, holdout sites, reviewers. Currency: actions spent to settle and residual against ground. Anchor: the ground, never agreement. Question type: triangulation between frames with low overlap.
- **Candidate rejected at census: "model complexity" alone.** No anchor outside the frame; Fig 1 :27 "They are frame-internal. A frame cannot score itself with a quantity it also produces." Not yet a domain.
- **Partition or union.** Union compound. Fig 9 :4-5 "are not one hard question but several well-formed ones. Split it rather than search." Representation and settlement have different grounds; turnover and rewiring have different mechanisms.
- **Order by dependency.** Describe R per slot before choosing a formalism (Fig 9 :27-28); split the union; choose arrangement not ingredients; price both currencies; settle against held-out ground; carry the method up, not the recording.

## VERDICT (from the figures)

The shape survives; no binding to a particular model class survives, because none is derived. Score per slot and report live slots explicitly. Enumerate the habitat from contact and carry context as part of the relation. Split the union into turnover, rewiring and context loops. Price bits and field actions in separate accounts, and admit an interaction term only if it beats the acceptance bargain on held-out slots. Treat a synthetic test as a substituted habitat and keep order and refusal in the vocabulary. If the model's closure cannot represent the change, use SEARCHED, INWARD or OUTWARD as separate questions, never as one. The framework returns a shape, not a name. This is a `specified`, ungrounded result.

- **cost if wrong:** bounded and locatable. The packet is mode-labelled, states refuters before the conclusion and records the lineage, so an error is findable rather than invisible. The real cost is spending the independence of outside readers by sending a recording instead of a generator, which Fig 2 :42 prices: "Buy independence in proportion to the cost of being wrong." That cost is avoidable by leading with the per-slot residual and its refuter.
- **undoable:** yes. The shape carries no binding to a species, an interaction type or a model class, so nothing ecological has to be withdrawn to correct it. A superseded packet can replace the old one, and the record keeps both. What is not undoable is a field season already spent settling the wrong term; that is why the action budget is reported up front.
- **reversed by:** a ground reading, not agreement. A held-out slot comparison that favours a fixed matrix over a relation model, a recorded field budget that does not grow with term count, a pooled model that beats split loops, or a synthetic-only calibration that predicts a novel real context would each fire a refuter in `01` §3. A source-discriminating field design that distinguishes rewiring from turnover would name the mechanism a fit cannot name. Until one of these fires, the verdict stands as `candidate`.
