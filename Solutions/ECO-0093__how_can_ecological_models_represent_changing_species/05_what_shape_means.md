## 1. A shape is a constraint, not a filler

The figures README states the limit: the framework returns "what a missing thing would have to look like (a shape, not a name)." Fig 9 :32 "you get a kind of thing, never the answer itself." Fig 13 :134 says "Unreachable is a verdict here, not a silence," and :138-139 "you get a kind of thing, never the answer itself, and narrowing gets you close while the ground decides."

From *The Library of Babel*: "You can cut your search short if the set of books returned is small and descriptive enough that it provides a general shape of the answer that satisfies your question."

A **shape** is the narrowed form of an answer you can state before you can name it. It constrains; it does not fill. For this question the shape is: score per slot, enumerate from contact, split the union, price both currencies, admit only terms that pay, and keep the substituted-habitat caveat. A specific formalism (a named model class, a fixed interaction matrix, a particular prior) is a **name**, and is not delivered by the framework.

## 2. How a shape is obtained

- *Library of Babel*: use the vocabulary already held that is closest to the thing, look for recurring new terms that overlap or correlate with it, recurse. "With each new term gained, you narrow the space and deepen your vocabulary." The searcher's own question is the anchor: the books returned are "tested or ranged against the problem you want answered in order to steer the direction of the next search."
- *Library of Babel* on bundles, directly on this question: "Perhaps your problem was a bundle or conflation of several problems masquerading as one? You will need to search all of those problems individually to find their answers and then combine them in a satisfactory way." This is the essay-level counterpart of Fig 9 :4-5 and :54-58. "Changing interactions without becoming too complex to test" is exactly such a bundle.
- Fig 6 :30 "The description travels. Fails by being sharpened into terms only we can read. Cast it in effect terms." Fig 9 :30-31 "your words are borrowed too, so describe the effect rather than the cause."
- *Decoding Tacit Knowledge*: "When contact isn't possible, the best you can send is a plain description of what you'd expect to see, because anyone can check that against the world." That is why `02_outreach_email.md` leads with a checkable failure condition rather than a theory.
- *Library of Babel* on stopping: the answer is reached when "the finish line will also be the starting place," and the paradox is that by then you have "composed the answer through the process of locating it."

## 3. What licenses a name, and the correction that applies here

Only two things license a name, both outside the shape itself:

1. **The ground settles it.** Formula step 5: "the ground settles it. Verified only by what pays. A gate is not the ground." The README says the framework "stops at the limit of what the available instruments can distinguish, and records the lineage so the stop is honest."
2. **A provenanced import from a frame whose closure differs** (Fig 6 :23, Fig 8 :51-52), with a source gate and a shadow test.

*They're Scanning Books* sharpens this: "The real import is not a new term in the existing space. It is a new dimension of the space." And: "A minimum viable set of six terms is a human's careful guess at reality's compression axes; another sensorium's minimum set need not be those six." Also: "Declaring the primitives does not make them neutral. It makes the choice visible."

**Correction to the blanket reading (compare the exemplar `05` §5b).** The README phrase is "what a **missing** thing would have to look like (a shape, not a name)." It governs a primitive not held in Γ. It does not forbid naming an atom already held. In this question, the slots of the six-term set (species as states, relations as network or gradient structures) are already held, but **the choice of a particular ecological model class is not one of the six**. So naming "a state-space model" or "a joint species distribution model" as *the* answer would be a binding from the ecology frame onto a held structure, not a derivation, and not an import that moves the wall. The correct form stays a shape: a rule for which arrangements are admissible, with the binding recorded as the modeler's own contribution.

## 4. Why the record's own analogy is forbidden here

*Arrival of the Fittest: The Tether Architecture* has a section titled "Three mappings offered and declined." One entry is exactly this question's temptation:

> "The habitat as an ecological niche. A niche is defined by an organism's relation to its environment. A habitat here is defined by contact with a residual, and the residual moves so the same actors constitute a different habitat when the question changes. The resemblance is real and the definitions do not align, which is why the figure says enumerated, never composed rather than borrowing the ecological term outright."

So Fig 11's habitat must **not** be silently identified with an ecological niche. Both involve relations to an environment, but the habitat is enumerated relative to a moving residual while a niche is a relation of an organism to its environment. The resemblance is a near miss, and the article declines it. This solution keeps the distinction: `01` F3 uses "habitat" in the figure's sense and cites the ecology niche article only as a secondary description of biotic relations.

## 5. Why naming a source from a reading is the specific error

*The Hard Problem is About Source* gives the mechanism. A color instrument reads green and "cannot comment on the source"; the same reading could come from one green light, yellow plus blue, RGB, CMYK, or a scheme not yet discovered. "If several paths create the same behavior, then behavior is a projection with lossy information." And: "An instrument that can't distinguish these cases demonstrates its own inadequacy." It was the spectrometer, not an argument, that resolved it. "The answer is unreached until a better instrument exists."

Applied here: a good fit is a reading, not a source. Several different interaction mechanisms can produce the same per-slot trajectory. Naming one mechanism as *the* cause of a fit is the metamerism error. What is owed is a discriminating field instrument or design, not a name. That is why `07_the_tether_answer.md §4` asks what measurement would distinguish rewiring from turnover, rather than asserting which one a given fit proves.

*How Intelligence Actually Works* states the general limit: "The recursion converges toward indiscernibility, and indiscernibility is not identity." A model that is indistinguishable from the field on the slots measured is not thereby identical to the ecosystem.

## 6. Q answered: what licenses a particular changing-interaction term in this question?

Nothing in the framework names it. A term is licensed only by (i) the ground settling it on held-out slots, or (ii) a provenanced import with a shadow test, in both cases with the lineage recorded. Until one of those, the chosen term is the modeler's own contribution and must be marked as such. The framework returns a shape: enumerate from contact, split the union, keep both currencies, accept only what pays.

## 7. Two consequences for outreach

**Prestige is not action.** From *The Recursive Self-Improvement Trap*: "Prestige must never be allowed to validate an action directly." And: "Prestige can fund an action. It can give an action a budget to be tried. But it cannot settle an action. Only the ground can settle it. The Ground Maintainer must be blind to prestige." Also: "You may all agree and be wrong." A campaign that gathers prestigious ecologists to agree with this shape is the named collapse, not corroboration. The legitimate ask is the opposite: give the claim a budget to be tried and let a field dataset settle it.

**My own audit is a simulation, not a ground reading.** Same essay: "the output is only as good as the validator. The agent, or the tool, or the frame, or the theory, cannot validate itself." And: "LLMs can quickly simulate an approximate answer/output with the formula" but "the truly testable output would need to be derived from the real architecture or working the formula yourself." LLM output is "simulacra." So `01` is a simulated derivation checked for internal consistency against the figures. It is not a test of the claim and must be labelled as such. It cannot settle anything it reports on.

**A shape is not a method that runs itself.** *How a Society of Agents Would Work* records that "capability is a property of agent-and-habitat, never of the agent alone," and *How Intelligence Actually Works* says the framework "cuts down to the shape of a thing, problem or question based on the given information available at this point in time." The shape stops where the available field instruments stop.
