## Wider literature that bears on the question

Two lines of world evidence, both secondary to the figures:

1. **Representation of change.** Yahaya et al. 2026 partition interaction turnover into species turnover and interaction rewiring and find different drivers for turnover and for network structure. Abel and Lutscher 2026 show that the order in which species emerge from dormancy changes the kind of instability. These are low-overlap witnesses: primary research articles, not framework essays. They corroborate F2, F4 and F8.
2. **Cost of testing.** The overfitting source provides the statistics-side corroboration of F5 and F6: parameters must be justified by data, and validation on unseen data is the check. The ecosystem-model source corroborates F7: independent data sets are the accepted validation route.

None of these sources was used to decide a figure verdict. They corroborate a shape the figures derived first. The Wikipedia ecology articles share a general reference pool with each other (three of them link to the same underlying literature), so their agreement is one broad witness, not three independent ones. The two Springer articles are independent of the framework author and of each other in authorship, though both are theoretical ecology and therefore share a disciplinary frame.

## Distribution: offer, do not push

- **Fig 4 :22 "Only methods cross up. Only head starts come down."** Sending a finished model prescription before anyone has described their residual sends a recording. Lead with the slot residual and the refuter.
- **Fig 10 :42 "A verifier that can reconstruct the claim cannot verify it."** A recipient who absorbs the full framework before being asked for a verdict has joined the frame being checked.
- **Fig 9 :27-28 "describe it before you go looking, or any frame you pick will seem to fit."** Ask what they would expect to see if the shape claim were true before giving them the source links.
- **Seat Map :163 "High correlation is disqualifying for a verdict and efficient for a convention."** A mailing list selected for people who already agree produces a convention, not corroboration.
- **Fig 11 :70 "A test harness is a substituted habitat. Which is why a synthetic solve proves wiring and never capability."** Do not present a simulation-only result as a field result.

**Recommended order in a reply:** (1) the per-slot residual and the shape claim; (2) ask what they would count as a failure; (3) record that verdict; (4) only then offer the three public source links, with the note that the figures, not this summary, are the authority.

## The two-stage offer

1. **Stage 1 before stage 2.** Fig 10 :52 "Bring a characterised residual, never the source." The recipient checks the claim without holding the framework's interiors.
2. **Stage 2 is opt-in by construction.** Once a reader has absorbed the theory, their agreement stops being evidence. The stage-1 verdict is recorded before any theory is shown, and later agreement is never pooled as corroboration (Fig 2 :40 "disqualifying for a verdict: agreement about the world is worthless when the agreeing frames share an evidence pool").
3. **Stage 3 sends a method.** Fig 4 :22 permits this: a generator can be given away without giving away an answer. Each user still needs a ground and a self-written refuter (`THE_FORMULA.md`, "Direction").
4. **Hard precondition.** Stages 1 to 3 only work if the stage-1 output survives. If a reader finds that a fixed interaction matrix predicts every slot as well as the relation model, that part of the shape is withdrawn.

All links are public and can be sent as plain URLs. Nothing in this folder uploads, hosts or sends anything. If a packet is hosted, prefer a page that lets a recipient decline contact; an opt-in download page is closer to the figures' rule than an attachment pushed to a list.
