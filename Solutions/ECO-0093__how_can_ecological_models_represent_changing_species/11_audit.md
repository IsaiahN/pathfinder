## Round trip gap `R_T`

The Symbols table defines `R_T` as `gap(x, (T_E ∘ T_A)(x))`, what the coarser description cannot hold. Fig 4 :30 requires it be measured, not assumed zero. I ran the round trip on the condensation this folder actually uses.

1. **Sent up (x):** Fig 1 :19-22 (per-slot residual), Fig 5 :42-44 (bargain), Fig 9 :4-5 and :54 (split), Fig 11 :43-51 and :66-70 (habitat, valence, substituted habitat), Fig 12 :125-130 (two currencies).
2. **Abstraction (T_A):** the shorthand in `07` and `09`: score per slot, enumerate, split, price both, admit only what pays, keep the field caveat, record the lineage.
3. **Concretisation (T_E):** a concrete claim such as "a season-indexed interaction coefficient with a cost penalty is the right model for every plant-pollinator network."

**`R_T` is nonzero.** Four things the shorthand holds do not survive the trip back down:

- Which contexts count as distinct slots must be **enumerated**; the shorthand invites a modeler to assume a season label instead.
- The **field-action currency** is dropped whenever a modeler reports only a description-length or parameter score.
- The **substituted-habitat caveat** disappears when a synthetic result is written as a field result.
- The **order and refusal** requirements are invisible in an ingredient-list specification; a coefficients-only vocabulary can lower a term and cannot refuse it.

What survives cleanly: score per slot, split before search, price both currencies, state the refuter. `R_T` was not measured numerically because no ground reading exists; it is recorded qualitatively as the enumerated set of dropped constraints. Treating "the formula is the condensed version of the metalaws" as identity would set `R_T` to zero and would be the dead-reckoning error Fig 4 :30 names. The honest form is a lossy condensation with a measured gap, which is what `09_watson_answer.md` states.
