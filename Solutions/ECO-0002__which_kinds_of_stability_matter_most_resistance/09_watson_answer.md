## VERDICT (from the figures)

The shape survives; the superlative does not. The three candidates are distinct predicates with distinct currencies and distinct slices, so no figure line and no source read supports a global winner. The residual must be indexed per function and slot, the vocabulary must be split before measurement, and which predicate is live is relative to a declared objective and anchor. The specific mapping of resistance to comparison, recovery to subtraction plus sequence, and persistence to comparison over a time state is our binding onto the figures, single-source and ungrounded. This is a `specified`, ungrounded result with a `gap` on the literal question, reported openly rather than filled with a name.

- **cost if wrong:** bounded and locatable. The packet states mode, refuters, per-slot indexing and provenance before the conclusion, so an error is findable rather than invisible. The real cost is in the ecology, not in the folder: a manager who instruments resistance while function silently declines monitors a proxy that is blind to the failure that matters (the conserve-biomass-lose-biodiversity case). Fig 9 :40 warns that "a wrong cut removes the answer and speeds up". That cost is avoidable by naming the objective and anchor first.
- **undoable:** yes. The split is a vocabulary change, reversible without withdrawing any figure line or any ecological finding. Fig 9 :41: "so keep every cut ranked and reversible". A published ranking can be superseded by a slot-indexed version; no primitive has to be retracted.
- **reversed by:** a ground reading, not agreement. An instrument returning all three as one observable would collapse the currencies; a derivation of one predicate from the other two would break the isomer reading; across two or more low-overlap systems with the same objective, one predicate's failure consistently preceding the other two under independent instruments would establish a local winner and force the anchor-relativity claim to be narrowed. A figure re-census that ranks the three would break the split outright. Until one of these fires, the verdict stands as `candidate`.
