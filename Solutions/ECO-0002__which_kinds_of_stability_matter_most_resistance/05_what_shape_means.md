## 1. A shape is a constraint, not a filler

The canonical rule: the framework returns "what a missing thing would have to look like (a shape, not a name)". Fig 9 :32: "you get a kind of thing, never the answer itself". Fig 13 :134: "Unreachable is a verdict here, not a silence". From the Library of Babel essay: "you can cut your search short if the set of books returned is small and descriptive enough that it provides a general shape of the answer that satisfies your question."

So a **shape** is the form an answer must have, statable before the particular occupant is known. It constrains; it does not fill. For ECO-0002 the shape is:

> A stability claim is admissible only when it names the function and slot, the disturbance, the horizon, the objective and the anchor. A ranking of resistance, recovery and persistence without those bindings is not an answer to this question; it is an unbound preference.

"Resistance matters most" is a name. "Which predicate is live, for this function, under this disturbance, over this horizon, for this objective?" is a shape.

## 2. How the shape is obtained

- Library of Babel: use the vocabulary you already have that is closest to the thing, look for recurring new terms that overlap or correlate with it, and recurse. "With each new term gained, you are able to narrow the space by definition and deepen your vocabulary."
- Fig 6 :30: "The description travels. Fails by being sharpened into terms only we can read. Cast it in effect terms." Fig 9 :30-31: "your words are borrowed too, so describe the effect rather than the cause". The effect here is operational: how much it moves, how fast it comes back, whether it keeps happening.
- Tacit Knowledge: "When contact isn't possible, the best you can send is a plain description of what you'd expect to see, because anyone can check that against the world." So the packet asks what a researcher would expect to observe if the split claim were true.
- Library of Babel on the failure mode that matches this question exactly: "Perhaps your problem was a bundle or conflation of several problems masquerading as one? You will need to search all of those problems individually to find their answers and then combine them in a satisfactory way."

## 3. What licenses a name

Only two things, both outside the shape.

1. **The ground settles it.** Formula step 5: the ground settles a term, "Verified only by what pays. A gate is not the ground." The canonical rule says the framework stops at the limit of what the instruments can distinguish and records the lineage so the stop is honest.
2. **A provenanced import from a frame whose closure differs**, with a source gate and a shadow test (Fig 6, Fig 8). In this question the three ecological property names are imports from the ecology frame, not primitives of the figures. They must be recorded as imports.

The Tacit Knowledge essay sharpens the point with drift: "two people who only check against each other drift, and they drift together, so neither notices." A field that agrees on a definition of stability has not thereby checked it against what ecosystems do.

## 4. The specific forbidden error in this question

Naming one of the three as the universal winner from the vocabulary alone is the error. The metamerism case in the exemplar (many sources give the same reading) has a mirror here: many different failure modes give the same single "stability" number. The diversity-stability literature supplies the concrete version: a vegetation community can "conserve biomass but lose biodiversity" under drought (Wikipedia contributors, "Ecological stability", 2026). One scalar, two opposite readings. What is owed is a slot-indexed instrument, not a winner.

## 5. The shape returned here, stated once

The shape is a three-part constraint with an open slot: distinct predicates, per-slot indexing, anchor-relative selection. It does not name which predicate wins for any real system, because no anchor has been named in the question and no measurement was taken in this run. The slot stays open. The framework "cannot say which primitive is missing, because naming it would mean already holding it" (Fig 13 :135), and in this case the missing thing is the declared objective and anchor, not a fourth kind of stability.
