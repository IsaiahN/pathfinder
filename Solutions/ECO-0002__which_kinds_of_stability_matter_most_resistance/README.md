## What is deliberately not here

- No personal emails of named individuals.
- No ranking of people by how likely they are to agree that one stability kind wins.
- No bulk-send plan.

Three reasons in the figures' own words. First, a room is not an anchor: "Alignment is triangulation, not negotiation. A mirror cannot correct you." (Fig 2 :35). Second, selecting for receptiveness manufactures the disqualifying correlation: "Correlation has valence." and "disqualifying for a verdict: agreement about the world is worthless when the agreeing frames share an evidence pool" (Fig 2 :39-40). Third, a reader who has absorbed the theory before giving a verdict has joined the frame being checked: "A verifier that can reconstruct the claim cannot verify it." (Fig 10 :42).

Legally, scraping personal emails for unsolicited bulk outreach breaches GDPR and CAN-SPAM style rules in many jurisdictions. The venues in `03` are public and opt-in.
