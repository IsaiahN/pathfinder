# The Tether's answer on kinds of stability

**Mode: `specified` on the structural claims, `general` on the shape, not `grounded`.** This is a shape and a route, not a result about any named ecosystem. Simulated derivation checked for internal consistency against the figures; not a ground reading.

## 1. The residual it answers

A decision maker must choose which property to measure, fund and manage for when a system meets a disturbance. The three offered candidates are not three values on one scale:

- resistance, a magnitude at impact;
- recovery, a rate along a return trajectory;
- persistence of functions, a duration over a management horizon.

Fig 1 :19: "The residual is indexed by object slot, not aggregated." So the question "which matters most?" is asked at the wrong level before a slot, objective and anchor are named.

## 2. The shape

1. **Split, do not rank.** Fig 9 :4-5: "Disagreements that do not shrink with effort, where each rule keeps working well on a different subset, are not one hard question but several well-formed ones. Split it rather than search." The global superlative is a `gap`, not a silence: Fig 13 :134 "Unreachable is a verdict here, not a silence".
2. **Three independent predicates, not three readings of one quantity.** Fig 13 :35: "Space, time and energy are not three readings of one quantity. Neither are spectrum, gradient and network." Fig 13 :36: "adjacency is not duration, an ordering is not a pathway, a pathway is not a direction." Displacement is not return rate, and return rate is not duration of function.
3. **The currencies do not add.** Fig 12 :125: "A composition costs in two currencies, and they do not add." Fig 12 :130: "Summing them into one figure is the error this names, not the fix for it." A single stability score is that error.
4. **Which predicate is live is anchor-relative.** Fig 2 :36: "An anchor is legitimate when the question is constitutively about it." Shock survival selects resistance; transient displacement selects recovery; a service delivered over a horizon selects persistence. No anchor-free winner exists.
5. **Order by dependency.** Vocabulary, then slot and objective, then anchor, then measurement, then decision (Fig 3 :20). A ranking before the first three links is a reading below the break.
6. **The predicate-to-figure mapping is our binding.** Resistance to comparison, recovery to subtraction plus sequence, persistence to comparison over a time state. The figures do not name the ecological properties. This mapping is recorded as an import, not a derivation (Fig 8 :50-52: convergent derivation and adopted import look identical in the contents; only the record separates them).

## 3. What it explicitly does not say

- Not that one stability kind is universally best, and not that "resilience" is an umbrella under which the others are ranks. The field's own union (Walker et al., as reported in "Resistance (ecology)") is unbundled per Fig 9 :54.
- Not that ecology has settled on a single measure. The sources report 70 stability concepts and a terminological dispute.
- Not which predicate wins for any real ecosystem. No anchor and no measurement were supplied in the question.
- Not `grounded`. Nothing here was settled by an instrument in this run.
- Not that persistence of function is the ground. It is an objective; the ground is what the system actually does (Fig 11 :24 "the ground does not decay, the channel does").

## 4. What would settle it: the ground

A ground reading, not more agreement.

- For a named system, function and objective, pre-register the failure event and run independent instruments for all three predicates per slot. If one predicate's failure consistently precedes the other two under low-overlap instruments, a local winner is established for that objective.
- Test the unit claim directly: does a measurement exist that returns all three as the same observable with no loss? If yes, F3 fails and the currencies merge.
- Test the isomorphism: can one predicate be derived from the other two over the shared atom set? If yes, the isomer reading fails.
- Fig 2 :35: "Alignment is triangulation, not negotiation. A mirror cannot correct you." Agreement among reviewers is not one of the settling operations.

## 5. The honest stop

**Reachable locally, unreachable globally.** A per-slot answer is reachable once an objective and an anchor are named; a global answer is not reachable with the terms given. The framework returns a shape, not a name, and stops where the instruments stop distinguishing. "Unreachable is a verdict here, not a silence" (Fig 13 :134). The lineage is recorded in `01` and checked in `11` so the stop is checkable.

## 6. One-paragraph version for outreach

> The Tether does not say that resistance, recovery or persistence of function is the stability that matters most. It says those three are different predicates in different units, measured on different slices of the same system: a magnitude at impact, a rate of return, a duration of function. Fig 13 calls such things "not three readings of one quantity", and Fig 9 says a bundle of questions must be split rather than searched as one. Which one is live depends on the objective and the anchor you name; shock survival, bounce-back time and continued service select different predicates. So the answer is a shape, not a winner: name the function, the disturbance, the horizon, the objective and the anchor, then measure. A global winner is a `gap`, not a silence. What is offered is that shape, three refuters written in advance, and the instruments that would close it locally.
