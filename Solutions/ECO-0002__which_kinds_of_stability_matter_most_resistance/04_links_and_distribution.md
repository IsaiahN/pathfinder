## Wider literature that bears on the residual

These are reported inside the pages above, so they are recorded as cited-within rather than as sources opened directly this session.

- Grimm, V. and Wissel, C. (1997), "Babel, or the ecological stability discussions", *Oecologia* 109(3): 323-334. The 70-concepts inventory and the recommendation to split the vocabulary.
- Holling, C. S. (1973), "Resilience and Stability of Ecological Systems", *Annual Review of Ecology and Systematics* 4: 1-23. The engineering/ecological resilience distinction and the four aspects.
- Walker, B., Holling, C. S., Carpenter, S. R. and Kinzig, A. (2004), "Resilience, adaptability and transformability in social-ecological systems", *Ecology and Society* 9(2): 5. Resistance as a component of resilience, hence a union.
- Donohue, I. et al. (2016), "Navigating the complexity of ecological stability", *Ecology Letters* 19(9): 1172-1185.
- Ives, A. R. and Carpenter, S. R. (2007), "Stability and Diversity of Ecosystems", *Science* 317: 58-62.
- Woods, D. D. (2015), "Four concepts for resilience and the implications for the future of resilience engineering", *Reliability Engineering & System Safety* 141: 5-9. Robustness versus resilience.
- Ding, X., Chen, H. Y. H. and Reich, P. B. (2026), "Ecosystem stability is shaped by resource-trait coordination under multiple interacting global change factors", *Nature Ecology & Evolution*. Title verified on the journal page; not opened as a full text.

Overlap warning: the two reference pages on ecological stability and resistance share the Grimm and Wissel source. They are one witness for that inventory, not two. The resilience engineering page is a genuinely different field and is the least overlapping corroboration.
