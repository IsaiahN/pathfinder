## The question restated as an effect

A finite channel resource (bandwidth) and a finite energy budget must be arranged so that a receiver gets a message that is correct, timely, and still correct when the channel fails. The four named goods are not four portions of one pie. Two are descriptions (accuracy, robustness), one is a rate (speed), and one is a physical expenditure (energy). The residual is the gap at the receiver between what the design predicts and what is actually received, measured per slot. An answer would look like a set of hard floors plus a rule for buying independence in proportion to the cost of being wrong, with the numeric split left open until a ground is named.

## What is deliberately not here

- No personal emails of named individuals.
- No ranking of people by how likely they are to agree with the shape claim.
- No bulk-send plan.

Three figure-grounded reasons:

1. **Agreement is not an anchor.** Fig 2 :35: *"Alignment is triangulation, not negotiation. A mirror cannot correct you."* A list chosen for receptiveness is a list chosen to agree.
2. **Shared evidence disqualifies a verdict.** Fig 2 :40: *"agreement about the world is worthless when the agreeing frames share an evidence pool."*
3. **Do not hand over the interior before the check.** Fig 10 :42: *"A verifier that can reconstruct the claim cannot verify it."*

Legally, scraping personal emails for unsolicited bulk outreach breaches GDPR and CAN-SPAM style rules in many jurisdictions. Public opt-in channels avoid that failure as well as the structural one.
