# Outreach draft - check the discriminator before you decide about the claim

**Use:** one-to-one, or as a cover letter attached to a submission through a public venue in `03_public_contact_points.csv`. **Not** for bulk send.

**Hard precondition:** the refuter in section 4 of `01_figure_grounded_audit.md` must be written down and dated before this is sent. The offer collapses on contact if the recipient receives the conclusion before the test.

---

**Subject:** One checkable point about turning points in movement data

> Hi [name],
>
> I am working on a narrow methods question in social movement research and would value a check on one output. I am not asking you to read a framework first, and I am not asking you to agree with anything. If the output fails your check, there is nothing else to look at.
>
> **The residual, in effect terms.** A movement's apparent activity shows a break at time T. The same observed break is compatible with at least two different producers: the number of people actually coordinating crossed a threshold (a ground change), or the observation channel changed, for example media attention, a platform ranking rule, a dataset's coverage, or a coding decision (a channel change). A change-point test finds T in both cases. It does not say which producer was at work.
>
> **The output I would like checked (`specified`, not `grounded`).** The figures I use force three conditions on any discriminating procedure. First, the anchor must not update on the thing it is used to judge. Second, a second frame must draw on a different evidence pool, so that two reports of the same coverage are one witness, not two. Third, the residual must be kept per slot rather than averaged into one movement-wide number, because a live local signal can disappear into a global mean. From these it follows that a break visible only in reporting data is not, by itself, evidence of a coordination threshold. The refit for such a break is the observation model, not a new theory of mobilisation.
>
> **What is explicitly not claimed.** I am not claiming which producer operated in any particular movement. I have not examined a movement for this work. I am not claiming the figures name the right anchor for your field; they constrain its properties and stop there. The specific record that can serve as an anchor has to be read off the case and provenanced as a choice, not derived.
>
> **What would refute the output.** I wrote these down before writing the conclusion:
> 1. A single reporting series, with no external anchor and no second frame, uniquely determines whether the ground or the channel changed.
> 2. Two instruments that both draw on the same reporting pool are shown to give independent corroboration of a ground change.
> 3. A movement break is found whose producing cause is recoverable from the step alone, with no lineage or habitat information.
> 4. A channel-only break is found that genuinely requires a new coordination mechanism rather than a refit of the observation model.
>
> If any holds, I withdraw the corresponding claim. A one-line "this fails because..." is more useful to me than agreement. If you would rather not engage, no reply is needed.
>
> **If it survives, and only then:** here are the sources. The figures are the authority, not my summary of them.
> - Figures and formula (public repository, branch `general-agent`): https://github.com/IsaiahN/tether/tree/general-agent/figures
> - Canonical usage rule: https://github.com/IsaiahN/tether/blob/general-agent/figures/README.md
> - Local verbatim figure text with line numbers, if you prefer an offline check: `FRAMEWORK/reference/03-FIGURES_TEXT.md`
>
> **If you want to reuse the machinery:** the formula is in the figures link. Run it on a problem you already know and can check against a ground. Write down what would refute your result *before* you run it, and state the mode of what you get. A reported failure is more useful to me than a reported success.
>
> - [your name]
