# What "shape" means for this question

**Mode:** `general` on the canonical limit, `specified` where the shape is made ordinary and testable. This is a simulated derivation checked for internal consistency against the figures, not a ground reading (see section 6).

**Sources opened on 2026-11-22.** Four of the eight canonical essays were fetched and quoted directly here: *The Hard Problem is About Source*, *The Library of Babel: Locating The Book of Books*, *The Recursive Self-Improvement Trap*, *Decoding Tacit Knowledge: Why Context, Subtext, and Pretext Matter*. The other four essays are in the canonical list but no quote from them is used in this folder, so they are not claimed as read. Local canonical files: `FRAMEWORK/reference/FIGURES_README_canonical.md`, `FRAMEWORK/figures/THE_FORMULA.md`, `FRAMEWORK/reference/03-FIGURES_TEXT.md`.

## 1. A shape is a constraint, not a filler

The canonical figures README states the limit: the framework returns "what a missing thing would have to look like (a shape, not a name)" and adds "Indiscernibility is not identity." Fig 9 :32: "you get a kind of thing, never the answer itself". Fig 13 :134: "Unreachable is a verdict here, not a silence,".

From *The Library of Babel*: "you can cut your search short if the set of books returned is small and descriptive enough that it provides a general shape of the answer that satisfies your question."

For this question, a **shape** is the form of a discriminator that can be stated before the discriminating instrument is named. It constrains:

- the anchor must not update on the thing it judges;
- the second frame must draw on a different evidence pool;
- the residual must be kept per slot;
- the producer of a break is a bond, not an ingredient, so the step alone is not the answer.

It does not fill: it does not say which record, which dataset, or which movement.

Concretely, the shape here is *"a per-slot comparison between a non-updating ground record and a declared channel record, with a second low-overlap frame, that returns one of {ground crossed, channel changed, both, undecidable at this instrument}."* That is a shape. *"Use on-site counts from the 2019 organiser roster in city X"* is a name, and must be provenanced as a binding from outside the figures.

## 2. How the shape is obtained

- *Library of Babel*: "use the existing vocabulary you do know that is closest to the words that you wish to describe and grasp the shape of"; then "With each new term gained, you are able to narrow the space by definition and deepen your vocabulary." Applied here: start with the ordinary words " tipping", " coverage", " reporting", and let the figures supply the nearer terms " anchor", " channel", " slot", " mis-attached", " union".
- Fig 6 :30: "outward: does another frame already hold it? The description travels. Fails by being sharpened into terms only we can read. Cast it in effect terms." Fig 9 :30-31: "your words are borrowed too, so describe the effect rather than the cause."
- *Decoding Tacit Knowledge*: "When contact isn't possible, the best you can send is a plain description of what you'd expect to see, because anyone can check that against the world." This is the outreach rule: describe the effect (a break that survives an instrument-change covariate in a non-reporting record), not the cause (a theory of mobilisation).
- *Library of Babel* on stopping: "you will know that you are done because the finish line will also be the starting place, as there is nowhere else to search and nothing else that can describe the answer better." For this question the stop is when the residual stops registering against a non-updating anchor, not when a narrative becomes satisfying.

## 3. What licenses a name

Only two things, both outside the shape itself:

1. **The ground settles it.** Fig 9 :42: "narrowing gets you close, the ground decides". The canonical README: the framework "stops at the limit of what the available instruments can distinguish, and records the lineage so the stop is honest."
2. **A provenanced import from a frame whose closure differs** (Fig 6 :23, Fig 8 :50-52), with a source gate and a shadow test. In this question, importing an on-site count protocol from a field that already uses it is legitimate if the source and the gap it explains are recorded. Claiming the figures derived that protocol is not.

Fig 6 :21: "MINT composes inside the closure and can never add an atom." The figures already contain the *properties* of an anchor. They do not contain a particular movement's records, so no amount of rearranging them produces one. Fig 13 :115: "The set is closed. The arrangements are not. That gap is why import exists."

## 4. The metamerism error is the exact failure mode here

*The Hard Problem is About Source* gives the mechanism with a colour instrument: "You have an instrument that can detect color. the instrument detects green, and says that it's green. However, the instrument is speaking only about what it measured, it cannot comment on the source." Then: "If several paths create the same behavior, then behavior is a projection with lossy information (it cannot define consciousness in full)." And: "An instrument that can't distinguish these cases demonstrates its own inadequacy, it cannot claim anything substantial about what is being measured or what causes it."

The observed break is the green reading. A coordination threshold and a reporting change are two different sources of that same reading. Naming the source from the reading is the metamerism error. What is owed is a discriminator, not a more confident interpretation of the step. The essay's own resolution is by instrument, not argument: "It was proven wrong not by argument, but by the arrival of a better instrument."

In movement data the analogue of the spectrometer is the non-updating anchor plus the low-overlap second frame. Until one exists, the source of the break has not been measured, only the break.

## 5. Naming what is already held is allowed, and still needs provenance

A careful reading avoids overstating the rule. The canonical phrase governs a **missing** thing: "what a **missing** thing would have to look like (a shape, not a name)." The properties "anchor", "channel", "slot", "second frame" are already held in the figures (Fig 2, Fig 6, Fig 1, Fig 8). Naming them is not inventing new primitives. What remains forbidden is claiming that the figures name a *particular empirical record* for a particular movement, or that doing so moves the wall (Fig 6 :22: "IMPORT adds an atom, and the added atom is what expands the closure"; choosing an existing record adds no atom to the framework).

So the distinction is:
- naming held structural roles: allowed, figure-derived;
- naming an empirical anchor: allowed only as a provenanced binding from the habitat, with a source gate and shadow test;
- claiming the framework derived that empirical name: refused.

This correction is kept visible rather than folded into a clean final statement.

## 6. The output is a simulation, not a ground reading

*The Recursive Self-Improvement Trap*: "LLMs can quickly simulate an approximate answer/output with the formula found in the architecture post. It will often be better than the generic outputs that you will usually get otherwise too (intelligence is essentially arrangement). However, the truly testable output would need to be derived from the real architecture or working the formula yourself." The same essay states the validator limit: "the output is only as good as the validator. The agent, or the tool, or the frame, or the theory, cannot validate itself. An external validator, or ground, is always needed to tether the veracity of the output to reality. Anything else is drift."

Therefore `01_figure_grounded_audit.md` and this file are internal-consistency checks against the figures. They cannot settle whether any movement crossed a coordination threshold, and they cannot certify that the proposed discriminator works in the field. That requires a ground reading, which has not been performed.

## 7. Prestige is not action, and agreement is not a discriminator

*The Recursive Self-Improvement Trap*: "Prestige can fund an action. It can give an action a budget to be tried. But it cannot settle an action. Only the ground can settle it. The Ground Maintainer must be blind to prestige."

In this question, the tempting prestige move is to ask well-cited movement scholars whether the movement "really tipped." That gives the claim a budget to be tried. It does not settle the source of the break. Fig 2 :40: "disqualifying for a verdict: agreement about the world is worthless when the agreeing frames share an evidence pool." Fig 2 :41: "efficient for a convention: agreement about a shared meaning is the product, not a corruption of it." The two uses of agreement are different and must not be mixed.

*Decoding Tacit Knowledge* states the drift direction: "Two people who only check against each other drift, and they drift together, so neither notices." A research community that reads the same news archive and the same commentaries can converge on "the movement tipped" with perfect coherence and no ground contact. Fig 2 :35: "Alignment is triangulation, not negotiation. A mirror cannot correct you."

## 8. Consequences for this folder

1. The answer file (`07`) states a shape and a route, never a finding about a movement.
2. The instrument name is declared `gap`, with the habitat enumerated as the place it would come from.
3. Outreach leads with the residual and the refuter; links come second and only on request.
4. Every substantive file labels itself a simulated derivation.
5. The second-seat audit in `11_audit.md` says plainly that it shares the evidence pool, so its agreement is internal consistency, not corroboration.
