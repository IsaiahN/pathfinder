## What is here, in effect terms

The question asks for a discrimination procedure, not a new theory of movements. The residual is an observed break in a movement's apparent activity. Two candidate producers of that break are on offer: a change in the ground, where the number of people actually coordinating crossed a threshold, or a change in the channel, where the same activity became more visible or more heavily reported. The figures decide that the break alone cannot settle its source, that a non-updating anchor and a second low-overlap frame are owed, and that the residual must be kept per slot. They do not name which instrument is the anchor in a particular movement. That is a declared `gap`, reported as a verdict rather than a silence.

## What is deliberately not here

- No personal emails of named individuals.
- No ranking of people by how likely they are to agree with the answer.
- No bulk-send plan.
- No claim that this derivation has been checked against a ground. It has not. Fig 2 :31 says *"Two people reading the same report are not two frames; two instruments pointed at the same thing are."* A simulated second seat that reads the same evidence pool is one instrument wearing two names.
