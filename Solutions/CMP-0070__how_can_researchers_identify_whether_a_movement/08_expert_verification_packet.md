# Expert verification packet - the Tether on turning points in movement data

**What this is:** a claim in a form a social movement researcher or methodologist can check. Mode, derivation, and refuters are stated before the conclusion, so a reader knows what to look for and what would settle it.

**Mode: `specified` on the method shape, `general` on the structural limits, `gap` on the name of the instrument. Not `grounded`.** No measurement of any movement is claimed. This packet is a simulated derivation checked for internal consistency against the figures; it cannot settle the question it reports on.

## 1. The claim, in one paragraph

An observed break in a movement's apparent activity cannot identify its own producer. The ground may have crossed a coordination threshold, or the visibility and reporting channel may have changed, or both. The figures require any discriminating procedure to have three properties: a non-updating anchor, a second frame with a declared low-overlap evidence pool, and a per-slot residual that is never averaged into a single movement-wide verdict. A channel-only break is a mis-attachment of the observation model, not a new mobilisation mechanism, and the correct move is to refit and refuse. Coverage and participation can form a feedback loop, so the anchor must sit outside it. The figures return the shape of this discriminator and its refuter, not the name of the record for a particular movement; that name is a declared gap, owed to the case and provenanced as a choice.

## 2. Three checkable sub-claims

### C1 - Framework-internal; checkable by reading Figures 10 and 2

- **Statement:** a break in one reporting series is insufficient to identify the producer, because the channel can change while the ground does not, and any anchor that updates on the reporting becomes another frame.
- **Source, verbatim:** Fig 10 :24 "the ground does not decay, the channel does"; Fig 10 :25 "stale percepts, dropped transients, saturated metrics, pooled readings"; Fig 2 :29 "An anchor is whatever settles the question and does not change its answer because of what any frame thinks."; Fig 2 :33 "The anchor must not update."; Fig 2 :31 "Two people reading the same report are not two frames; two instruments pointed at the same thing are."
- **Refuter:** exhibit a case where a single reporting series, with no external anchor and no second frame, uniquely determines whether the ground or the channel changed. Or show an anchor that updates on the reporting and still settles the question.

### C2 - Framework-internal; checkable by reading Figures 1, 5, and 9

- **Statement:** the residual must be kept per slot; a channel-only break is a mis-attachment, not a new mechanism; the producer of a break is a bond and is invisible in the step alone.
- **Source, verbatim:** Fig 1 :19 "The residual is indexed by object slot, not aggregated."; Fig 1 :22 "Aggregating across slots is how a live signal disappears into an average."; Fig 5 :13 "mis-attached: refit what you have, do not invent"; Fig 12 :5 "A set of ingredients is not one. The bond is the rest of the information, and it is the half nothing was recording."; Fig 9 :57 "a case in two conditions is not a harder case of either".
- **Refuter:** show that coordination thresholds in this domain are genuinely global and simultaneous at every slot so that per-slot reporting carries no more information than the mean; or show a channel-only break that genuinely requires a new coordination mechanism with all three mint guards passed and settlement at the ground.

### C3 - Method-facing; checkable against the researcher's own practice

- **Statement:** an anchor cannot be specified from a description; use a record already returning a reading, enumerate the reporting habitat outward, and keep description cost and settlement cost in separate accounts.
- **Source, verbatim:** Fig 6 :32 "An instrument is not built from a description. It is improved from a worse instrument already returning something."; Fig 6 :34-35 "Where nothing is, there is no edge to extend from, and the search for a proto-instrument is a search for one that is already there."; Fig 11 :43 "The habitat is enumerated, never composed."; Fig 12 :125 "A composition costs in two currencies, and they do not add."
- **What to check:** name one record in your field that is already returning a reading and does not update on media attention. Name one instrument-change log that would let you test whether the break disappears when coverage change is entered. Say what the two currencies would be in your budget.
- **Refuter:** exhibit an instrument built from a pure description with no prior reading that returned a discrimination, or show a domain where coverage and participation are provably independent so the habitat enumeration is unnecessary.

## 3. What is explicitly not claimed

- Not which producer operated in any actual movement. No dataset was examined.
- Not that reporting is always the culprit, or that thresholds are unreal.
- Not that the figures name an anchor, a lag, a dataset, or a required number of frames. Those are a `gap` and budget choices.
- Not a new theory of social movements, and not `grounded`.
- Not corroborated by a second seat that read the same files. By Fig 2 :31 such an agreement is one evidence pool, not two frames.
- Not that agreement among researchers establishes a discriminator. Fig 7 :35 "A room's agreement is variation, not a verdict. Selection between rooms is where the ground votes."

## 5. What would settle it

1. A per-slot non-updating ground record that shows a sustained change at the break while the instrument-change log is held fixed.
2. A second frame with declared low overlap showing the same change against the same ground, reached by a different route, with the record of where each came from preserved (Fig 8 :51-52).
3. A documented instrument-change timestamp. If the break vanishes when it is entered, the producer was the channel and the tipping term is refused.
4. Any of the six pre-registered failure conditions in `01` section 4 firing, in which case the corresponding finding is withdrawn.

## 6. What an expert can check in twenty minutes

1. Read Fig 10 :24-25 and Fig 2 :29, :31, :33, four short passages. Is the channel/ground ambiguity real as stated?
2. Read Fig 1 :19, :22 and Fig 5 :13. Do you agree that a channel-only break is a refit rather than a new mechanism?
3. Name one record in your field that is already returning a reading, does not update on reporting, and has a documented instrument-change log alongside it. If you cannot, the instrument-name gap is real and the method stops at a shape.
4. Say which of the six refuter conditions you would try first, and why.
5. Send the refutation. A failure is more useful than an endorsement.
