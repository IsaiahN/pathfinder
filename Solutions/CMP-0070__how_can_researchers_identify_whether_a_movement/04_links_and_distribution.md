## 2. How the wider literature bears on the question

1. **Google Flu Trends is the cleanest low-overlap analogue.** A search-volume series looked like a ground signal for influenza and partly tracked media prominence instead. The remedy reported in the same synthesis is combination with an independent surveillance series (CDC) and recalibration. This predicts exactly the figure-derived requirement: a non-updating anchor plus a second low-overlap frame.
2. **The threshold literature supplies the "real threshold" half.** A threshold is a property of a distribution of individual thresholds, not a single global switch. It predicts that a genuine coordination threshold should be visible as a per-slot, ordered process, which is what Fig 1 requires methodologically. This source corroborates the existence of the concept; it does not corroborate any figure line.
3. **Spiral of silence supplies a pure visibility-change mechanism.** Expressed opinion can shift while private conviction does not, because people read the climate and self-censor. It predicts an apparent turning point in public expression with no coordination-ground change. It is a communication-studies witness, distinct evidence pool.
4. **Detection and reporting bias name the channel failure directly.** Both are properties of the observation process, not of the phenomenon. The statistics synthesis also proposes the same remedy as the figures: rerun with a different independent variable and see whether the phenomenon survives.
5. **Change detection marks the boundary of what a time-series method can do.** It finds when a distribution changes. It does not say what changed. The question asked here starts where that article stops.

These sources are secondary corroboration only. They cannot overrule a figure, and agreement among them is not automatically two witnesses: the Wikipedia syntheses are distinct pages with distinct topics and low topical overlap, but all are encyclopedic syntheses drawn from public literature, so overlap is recorded per row in `10_source_ledger.csv`.

## 3. Distribution rules: offer, do not push

- **Fig 10 :52** - "Bring a characterised residual, never the source." Lead with the break, the two candidate producers, and the refuter. Do not open with the theory.
- **Fig 4 :22** - "Only methods cross up. Only head starts come down." A recording of someone else's conclusion is a description of one occasion. Send the discriminator and its refuter, which are reusable.
- **Fig 9 :27-28** - "describe it before you go looking, or any frame you pick will seem to fit." Ask what the recipient would expect to see if each producer were at work before showing any links.
- **Fig 2 :35** - "Alignment is triangulation, not negotiation. A mirror cannot correct you." Do not ask for agreement.
- **Fig 2 :31** - "Two people reading the same report are not two frames; two instruments pointed at the same thing are." Do not collect a pile of responses from researchers who all study the same news archive and call it corroboration.
- **Fig 7 :35** - "A room's agreement is variation, not a verdict. Selection between rooms is where the ground votes."

**Recommended reply order:** (1) the residual and the two candidate producers; (2) the three conditions on a discriminator and the pre-registered refuter; (3) ask what they would count as a failure; (4) only then the figures link, with the note that the figures, not this summary, are the authority; (5) only if asked, the formula as a method to reuse with its own refuter and mode.

## 4. The two-stage offer

- **Stage 1.** Send the residual and the refuter from `02_outreach_email.md`. Record the recipient's verdict *before* any theory is shown. A later agreement is never pooled as corroboration (Fig 2 :40).
- **Stage 2, opt-in.** If they are curious, offer three links: the figures folder, the canonical README, and one world source from section 1 that is relevant to their field.
- **Stage 3, opt-in.** Offer the formula as a method. Each user writes their own refuter and states their own mode. The framework's own warning is that a frame cannot check the direction of its own question, so a user who skips the refuter has not run the method.

**Hard precondition.** Stages 1 to 3 work only if the stage-1 output survives. This output is a *design* claim, so the realistic stage-1 failure is not "the movement did not tip" but "your discriminator cannot distinguish the two producers even in principle." If a checker shows that, the correct response is to withdraw the claim and rerun the census, not to send the links anyway.

## 5. Hosting and sending

Nothing here is sent by this agent. Hosting and sending are the user's to do. Prefer an opt-in download or submission page over an attachment pushed to a list: the recipient should be able to decline contact without consequence, which is closer to the figure's contact rule than a mass attachment. All venues in `03_public_contact_points.csv` are public institutional or opt-in submission routes; no personal emails of named individuals are included, and there is no bulk-send plan.
