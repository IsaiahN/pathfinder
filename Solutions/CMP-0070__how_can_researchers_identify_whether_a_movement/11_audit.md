## Independent domain census

I redid the census from the residual line alone before reading the primary's conclusions, using the WATSON order: R by arity, symmetry and scale; one line per domain with actors, currency and anchor; partition or union; order by dependency.

- **R (arity / symmetry / scale).** The residual is an observed break R(s,T) in a slot of a movement's apparent activity. Arity: two producing conditions plus a mixed compound, so **union**, not a dial (Fig 9 :54-57). Symmetry: asymmetric, because the visible series is a product of ground and channel and the comparison of two readings is ordered. Scale: first order inside a series, second order at the union of channels, third order if coverage and participation feed each other.
- **Domain 1, coordination ground.** Actors: participants, local groups, organisations. Currency: coordinated acts and commitments. Anchor: a record that does not move when media attention moves. Admitted.
- **Domain 2, visibility and reporting channel.** Actors: outlets, ranking systems, dataset builders, coders. Currency: mentions, coverage, categories, sampling rules. Anchor: a log of instrument changes. Admitted, with the caveat that it anchors *when the instrument changed*, not what the movement did.
- **Domain 3, inference and verification.** Actors: researchers and outside frames. Currency: residuals and agreement. Anchor: the ground, never agreement. Admitted; its independence is zero in this simulated run.
- **Domain 4, mixed compound.** Both conditions with a lag or feedback. Anchor: a slot-level record of which condition still binds after one lever is applied. Admitted as a union compound.
- **Refused domains.** News-derived event counts, sentiment read from the same posts, and expert consensus. Each updates on the process under test, so none has a nameable non-updating anchor.

Order by dependency: secure a non-reporting ground reading before any vocabulary, objective or design (Fig 3 :20-21); describe the effect before choosing a frame (Fig 9 :27-28); refuse the "break means tip" term before minting a new mechanism (Fig 5 :53).

My census matches the primary's four domains, its union reading, and its refused domains. ## Round trip gap R_T

The Symbols table defines R_T as `gap(x, (T_E ∘ T_A)(x))`, the gap between what was sent up and what came back down, and Fig 4 :30 says "So take the round trip and measure the gap. 1. **Sent up (x):** the verbatim figure set, chiefly Fig 10 :24-25 (channel decay and its failure modes), Fig 2 :29-34, :40 (anchor, independence, coherent drift), Fig 1 :19-22 (per-slot residual), Fig 9 :54-57 (union) and Fig 5 :13 (mis-attached).

3. **Concretisation (T_E):** a researcher's concrete plan: pick the largest hashtag archive, run a change-point detector, add a news-coverage covariate, declare the movement tipped if the break survives.

**R_T is nonzero.** Three constraints held by the shorthand do not survive the trip back down:

- the second frame must have a declared **low-overlap evidence pool**, not merely a different variable from the same archive (Fig 2 :31, Fig 8 :48-52);
- the residual must be kept **per slot**, so the global detector in step 3 has already discarded the signal (Fig 1 :19-22);
- the instrument must be **already returning a reading**; a brand-new protocol designed for this study has no prior residual to sharpen against (Fig 6 :32-35).

A fourth item is partly lost: the intervention is asymmetric, since a researcher can add a channel but cannot subtract the reporting habitat from the world (Fig 11 :61). Treating the covariate as if it removed the channel is the dead-reckoning error.

Conversely, one thing survives cleanly: the break alone does not identify its producer, and a channel-only break is a refit rather than a new mechanism. That is the residue worth sending. R_T was **not** measured numerically, because no ground reading exists. It is recorded qualitatively as the enumerated set of dropped constraints. Treating the formula as identical to the figures would set R_T to zero and would be the error Fig 4 :30 names. The honest form is a lossy condensation with a recorded gap, which is what `09_watson_answer.md` states.
