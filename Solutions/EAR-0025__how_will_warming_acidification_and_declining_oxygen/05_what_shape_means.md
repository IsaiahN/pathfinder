The figures outrank the essays where they disagree. The canonical local rule states the framework returns *"what a missing thing would have to look like (a shape, not a name)"* and stops *"at the limit of what the available instruments can distinguish."*

## 1. A shape is a constraint, not a filler

Fig 9 :32: *"you get a kind of thing, never the answer itself."* Fig 13 :134: *"Unreachable is a verdict here, not a silence."*

From *The Library of Babel*: *"you can cut your search short if the set of books returned is small and descriptive enough that it provides a general shape of the answer that satisfies your question."*

For EAR-0025, the shape is: a region-indexed union compound with enumerated habitat, residual-relative valence, and separate space and time gradient terms. It is not a table of regional pH or oxygen values. The first constrains; the second is a ground report that this package does not possess.

## 2. How the shape is obtained

- *Library of Babel* on vocabulary: *"With each new term gained, you are able to narrow the space by definition and deepen your vocabulary."* The bootstrapped terms here are "slot", "union compound", "cascade", "valence" and "anchor".
- *Library of Babel* on bundles: *"Perhaps your problem was a bundle or conflation of several problems masquerading as one?"* This is the essay-level source for F1. The question phrasing ("warming, acidification and declining oxygen") invites a three-item list; the figure and the essay both say to unbundle the union instead.
- *Decoding Tacit Knowledge*: *"When contact isn't possible, the best you can send is a plain description of what you'd expect to see, because anyone can check that against the world."* Hence the outreach sends a failure criterion, not a theory.
- Fig 9 :27-28: *"describe it before you go looking, or any frame you pick will seem to fit."* The residual was described before the field sources were selected.

## 3. What licenses a name or a number

Two things, both outside the shape itself:

1. **The ground settles it.** Formula step 5: *"the ground settles it. Verified only by what pays. A gate is not the ground."* A measured regional value is admissible once an instrument pays for it.
2. **A provenanced import from a frame whose closure differs** (Fig 6 :23), with a source gate and a shadow test.

*They're Scanning Books* sharpens the limit: *"A minimum viable set of six terms is a human's careful guess at reality's compression axes; another sensorium's minimum set need not be those six."* And: *"Declaring the primitives does not make them neutral. It makes the choice visible."* The slot and union vocabulary is a visible choice, not a discovery of nature's own categories.

## 4. The source-discrimination point, applied

*The Hard Problem is About Source* gives the mechanism: an instrument reads green and *"cannot comment on the source"*; *"If several paths create the same behavior, then behavior is a projection with lossy information."* It was *"proven wrong not by argument, but by the arrival of a better instrument."*

For EAR-0025 the analogue is exact in structure. A global oxygen inventory or a single pH trend is a projection that cannot say which regional process produced it. Several combinations of solubility, stratification, respiration and transport can yield the same global mean. What is owed is a source-discriminating regional instrument and lineage, not a name assigned from the aggregate. This does not forbid naming measured sources; it forbids naming one from a reading that cannot distinguish among them.

## 5. Frames, rooms and independence

- *How a Society of Agents Would Work*: *"A group is only worth having while its members stay different."* CMIP ensemble members that share generation and heritage are close to one frame.
- *How Intelligence Actually Works*: *"the room then slides into what Figure 2 calls coherent drift"*, i.e. agreement without a ground.
- Fig 7 :35: *"A room's agreement is variation, not a verdict. Selection between rooms is where the ground votes."*
- *Arrival of the Fittest* records three mappings offered and declined, and notes the triangulation claim *"fits four cases already known and has not been tested forward."* The regional compound claim offered here is a forward application, so it is `candidate`.

## 6. Prestige is not action

*The Recursive Self-Improvement Trap*: *"Prestige can fund an action. It can give an action a budget to be tried. But it cannot settle an action. Only the ground can settle it."* A list of well known reviewers who agree with the shape claim is the named collapse. The legitimate ask is a budget to try the claim against independent observations.

## 7. My own audit is a simulation, not a ground reading

Same essay: *"LLMs can quickly simulate an approximate answer/output with the formula found in the architecture post."* And: *"the truly testable output would need to be derived from the real architecture or working the formula yourself."* *Arrival of the Fittest* adds that outputs *"could never be verified if the solutions presented were not contrived to fit the formula."*

So `01_figure_grounded_audit.md`, `06_isomers_and_order.md`, `07_the_tether_answer.md`, `08_expert_verification_packet.md` and `09_watson_answer.md` are simulated derivations checked for internal consistency against the figures. They are **simulacra** in the essay's sense. They cannot settle EAR-0025. The mode line on every substantive claim says `specified` or `general`, never `grounded`.

## 8. Corrections to my own reading

**Correction 1 (to §1).** I first wrote that "shape, not a name" forbids reporting any regional quantity. That overstates the rule. The figures forbid the framework from *deriving* a quantity it does not hold; they do not forbid an instrument from reporting one. Corrected in `01` §5. The package still reports none of its own.

**Correction 2 (to §4).** I first treated the metamerism example as a general ban on naming a source. The essay's actual claim is narrower: a reading that cannot discriminate among sources cannot name one from that reading alone. The remedy is a better instrument, not a permanent silence. Both forms are recorded so neither is lost.
