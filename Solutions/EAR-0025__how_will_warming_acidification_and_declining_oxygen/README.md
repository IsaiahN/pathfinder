## The residual, in effect terms

Three stressors (warming, acidification, declining dissolved oxygen) are being changed at once by one driver, and we want their joint, region-resolved trajectory. The failure is not that we know nothing about any one stressor. Each has a literature and a measurement anchor. The failure is at their intersections and at the regional scale: a global mean can sit near zero residual while one region carries a live signal, and the interaction type changes with region, depth and trophic level. What is owed is the shape of a region-indexed, three-condition compound prediction, plus the anchors that would settle it. Not owed: one global number, a ranked list of three stressors, or a single mechanism name.

## What an answer would look like (bound as an ordinary term)

A candidate answer must, at minimum: (1) treat the three as a union compound and say what is true of the two- and three-condition intersections, not just of each condition alone; (2) index the residual by region and depth, so a near-zero global result cannot erase a live local one; (3) enumerate the physical, chemical, oxygen and biological actors and follow second- and third-order cascades; (4) keep a spatial gradient term distinct from a temporal trend term; (5) range against independent observational anchors rather than model agreement; (6) return a shape and name instruments, not fabricated regional numbers. This paragraph is the sought-for shape written down before the run, so it can accrue a residual and be refused like any other term (`THE_FORMULA.md` step, "Direction").

## What is deliberately not here

- No personal emails of named individuals.
- No ranking of people by how likely they are to agree.
- No bulk-send plan.
- No invented regional projections. The framework returns a shape, not a number.

Three reasons, in the figures' own words:

1. **Agreement is not a verdict.** Fig 2 :35: *"Alignment is triangulation, not negotiation. A mirror cannot correct you."* Fig 7 :13: *"agreement compounds, evidence does not."*
2. **Selecting for receptiveness manufactures the disqualifying correlation.** Fig 2 :40: *"agreement about the world is worthless when the agreeing frames share an evidence pool."*
3. **Asking for trust before checking spends independence.** Fig 10 :42: *"A verifier that can reconstruct the claim cannot verify it."*
