## 1. A shape is a constraint, not a filler

The canonical usage rule: the framework returns *"what a missing thing would have to look like (a shape, not a name)"*. Fig 9 :32: **"you get a kind of thing, never the answer itself"**.

From *The Library of Babel*: *"You can cut your search short if the set of books returned is small and descriptive enough that it provides a general shape of the answer that satisfies your question."*

So a **shape** is the narrowed form of an answer you can state before you can name it. It constrains; it does not fill. For this question the shape is:

> Any organization structure that works for a decision must keep a channel to a ground that does not update on the organization's own report, keep a seat able to refuse a verdict, and carry influence across nodes. It must be read per decision slot, never as an org-wide average.

An org-wide score like "decision quality went up 12%" is a filler in this sense: it names a number without constraining where the signal came from or what would settle it.

## 2. How the shape is obtained

- *Library of Babel*: use the vocabulary you already have that is closest to the thing, then look for recurring new terms that overlap with it. *"With each new term gained, you narrow the space by definition and deepen your vocabulary."*
- Fig 6 :30: *"The description travels. Fails by being sharpened into terms only we can read. Cast it in effect terms."* Fig 9 :30 to :31: *"your words are borrowed too, so describe the effect rather than the cause"*.
- *Decoding Tacit Knowledge* (mapped from the exemplar, not opened this session): *"When contact isn't possible, the best you can send is a plain description of what you'd expect to see, because anyone can check that against the world."*
- *Library of Babel* on stopping: *"the finish line will also be the starting place"*, because by then you have *"composed the answer through the process of locating it."*

For this question the bootstrapping went: "better decisions" (borrowed) to "residual per slot against a non-updating anchor" (effect terms) to "channel and refusal seat" (figure-owned vocabulary). That is proximity recursion, not a literature average.

## 3. The bundle clue in the essay itself

*The Library of Babel* names the exact structural finding of `01_figure_grounded_audit.md` F1:

> *"Perhaps your problem was a bundle or conflation of several problems masquerading as one? You will need to search all of those problems individually to find their answers and then combine them in a satisfactory way."*

The question "flatter: better decisions, or coordination problems?" is such a bundle. The essay's instruction is to search the two individually and combine, which is the same instruction as Fig 9 :54 to :57. This is internal consistency within one evidence pool, not independent corroboration.

## 4. What licenses a name

Two things, both outside the shape itself:

1. **The ground settles it.** `THE_FORMULA.md` step 5: *"the ground settles it. Verified only by what pays. A gate is not the ground."* For this question, a per-slot measurement paid by an outcome that does not move when the org reports success.
2. **A provenanced import from a frame whose closure differs** (Fig 6 :23, Fig 8 :50 to :52), with a source gate and a shadow test. The Martela and Dunbar sources in `04_links_and_distribution.md` are candidate shadows, not imports that add a primitive.

*They're Scanning Books When They Should* (mapped from the exemplar, not opened this session): *"The real import is not a new term in the existing space. It is a new dimension of the space."* Also: *"Declaring the primitives does not make them neutral. It makes the choice visible."*

## 5. The metamerism caution, and the correction to it

*The Hard Problem is About Source* gives the instrument rule. Metamerism: an instrument reads green and *"cannot comment on the source"*. *"If several paths create the same behavior, then behavior is a projection with lossy information."* The passage that matters here: *"The answer is unreached until a better instrument exists."* It was the spectrometer, not an argument, that resolved the case.

Applied here: a survey score saying "decisions are faster and better" is a reading that cannot say which source produced it. Faster may come from a shortened channel to local knowledge (F4's contact change), from a suppressed refusal seat (F5's accountability loss), or from both. Without a discriminator, naming the source is a metamerism error.

**Correction to my own §4 reading.** The phrase *"shape, not a name"* governs a **missing** primitive, one not held in `Γ`. The structure "network" is already among the six (Fig 13 :24: **"that entities relate, and influence travels"**). So naming the channel or the network is a binding onto a held atom, not the invention of a new primitive. What remains genuinely unnamed is the **span** or layer count that works in a given habitat (F9). Both claims are recorded so neither is quietly lost. This is the same correction the exemplar made in its `05` §5b, reached here from Fig 13 rather than copied.

## 6. Two consequences for this derivation

**Prestige is not action.** *The Recursive Self-Improvement Trap*: *"Prestige must never be allowed to validate an action directly."* And: *"Prestige can fund an action. It can give an action a budget to be tried. But it cannot settle an action. Only the ground can settle it."* A list of famous flat organizations (or famous critics of flat organizations) funds a trial; it does not settle the question.

**This audit is a simulation, not a ground reading.** Same essay: *"the output is only as good as the validator. The agent, or the tool, or the frame, or the theory, cannot validate itself."* And: *"LLMs can quickly simulate an approximate answer/output with the formula found in the architecture post."* So `01_figure_grounded_audit.md`, `06_isomers_and_order.md` and this file are simulated derivations checked for internal consistency against the figures. They are **not** a test of any organization and cannot settle anything they report on. The honest mode is `specified` throughout, with no part labelled `grounded`.
