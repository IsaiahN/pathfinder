## What this is

A simulated derivation checked for internal consistency against the figures. It is NOT a ground reading. By the framework's own rule (*The Recursive Self-Improvement Trap*, 2026-11-19): *"LLMs can quickly simulate an approximate answer/output with the formula found in the architecture post."* This folder cannot settle the question it reports on. It offers the shape any answer must have, the refuters written in advance, and the instruments that would close it.

## Residual in effect terms

Let `R = |Γ(b,a) - o'|` be the gap between an organization's decision model's prediction and what the ground returns, indexed per decision slot `s` and never aggregated (Fig 1 :19). The question asks whether reducing hierarchy depth shrinks `R` (better decisions) or grows it (coordination failure and unclear accountability). The two options are not two answers to one question. They are two conditions of a union (Fig 9 :55).

An answer would look like: for each slot, which condition holds, what anchor measures it, and what would reverse the call. That shape is bound here as an ordinary term so it can accrue a residual like anything else.

## What is deliberately not here

- No personal emails of named individuals.
- No ranking of people by how likely they are to agree.
- No bulk-send plan.

Three reasons from the figures: a room does not add an anchor (Fig 2 :35, *"Alignment is triangulation, not negotiation. A mirror cannot correct you."*); selecting for agreement manufactures the disqualifying correlation (Fig 2 :40); and a verifier who has absorbed the interiors cannot verify (Fig 10 :42).
