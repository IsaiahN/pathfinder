## Wider literature actually read, and what it bears on

These are secondary corroboration only. The figures decide. Where a source is in the same broad management pool as another, overlap is declared in `10_source_ledger.csv` so a pool is not mistaken for a witness.

1. **Organization design, business.** Martela, Mäkkeli and Kostamo, *"The ten functions of middle managers - Identifying their functional substitutes in a decentralized organization without traditional middle managers"*, Journal of Organization Design, published 28 September 2026, DOI 10.1007/s41469-026-00207-w. Open access at https://link.springer.com/article/10.1007/s41469-026-00207-w. Quote: *"However, we discovered that the teams were not able to take care of all managerial functions, and thus the fulfilment of these functions required purpose-built supportive structures."* This corroborates F4 and F5: removing the layer removes the bond, not the function. It does not corroborate a layer count, and it is a single case (Reaktor, 500+ employees, 2019 to 2020 data), so it is a shadow, not a ground for the general claim.
2. **Practitioner synthesis, business.** Pim de Morree, *"Advantages & disadvantages of flat organizational structures (from 14 case studies)"*, Corporate Rebels, 8 January 2026. https://www.corporate-rebels.com/blog/advantages-disadvantages-flat-organizational-structures. Quote: *"Flat organizations do not eliminate coordination work; they redistribute it."* Also: *"where designed and supported well, they enable faster decisions... At the same time, they demand more from individuals, require explicit coordination mechanisms"*. This is the clearest world-side statement of the union: both options in the question occur, under different conditions. Same broad management pool as source 1, so one witness, not two.
3. **Healthcare management and organizational psychology.** Svanström, Skytt, Lindberg and Lindberg, *"Expanded span of control, leadership and management performance, work-related stress, and job satisfaction among first-line managers: A repeated cross-sectional study"*, Work 81(3):2952-2963, 2025, PMC12231798. https://pmc.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/articles/PMC12231798. Quote: *"limited decision-making authority and unclear role boundaries contribute to stress"*. Also: *"as the expanded span of control grows, managers often find it harder to maintain close connections with their staff"*. Corroborates the accountability and channel mapping in F4 and F5. Its outcome is job satisfaction, not decision quality, so it is silent on the D1 verdict.
4. **Team cognition, medical education.** Edgar, Jones, Harsy, Passiment and Hauer, *"Better Decision-Making: Shared Mental Models and the Clinical Competency Committee"*, Journal of Graduate Medical Education 13(2 Suppl):51-58, 2021, PMC8078083. https://pmc.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/articles/PMC8078083. Quote: *"An SMM does not require or guarantee complete agreement among members."* Also: *"The shared beliefs, understanding, and experiences on which SMMs are based are increasingly difficult to achieve as groups grow in size and are separated in time and space."* Corroborates F7's distinction between a convention (shared meaning) and a verdict, and F4's channel claim at the team stratum.
5. **Animal behaviour and anthropology, genuinely low overlap.** Matt Webb, *"Dunbar's number and how speaking is 2.8x better than picking fleas"*, Interconnected, 5 April 2022. https://interconnected.org/home/2022/04/05/dunbar. Quoting Dunbar 1993 and MacCarron, Kaski and Dunbar 2016: *"the average cumulative layer turns out to hold 4.1, 11.0, 29.8, and 128.9 users"*; and the workplace observation that *"the likelihood of having friends within the workplace reaches an asymptote at a shop size of 90-150 individuals."* Predicts a contact ceiling that does not disappear because the org chart is redrawn. Corroborates F6 at a different stratum. It is a number about relationships, not span of control, so it must not be converted into an optimal layer count (F9).
6. **Complexity science, genuinely low overlap.** Simon A. Levin, *"The Architect of Complexity"*, SFI Press excerpt, Foundational Papers in Complexity Science pp. 675-712, DOI 10.37911/9781947864535.21, quoting Herbert Simon's 1962 *"The Architecture of Complexity"*. https://www.sfipress.org/21-simon-1962. Quote attributed to Simon: *"Metaphor and analogy can be helpful, or they can be misleading. The two pages quote the same Simon 1962 paper, so they are one witness.

## Distribution: offer, do not push

- **Fig 4 :22** - *"Only methods cross up. Only head starts come down."* Sending the full architecture before someone has described a residual sends a recording, not a generator. Lead with the shape claim and its refuter; offer the links after.
- **Fig 10 :42** - **"A verifier that can reconstruct the claim cannot verify it."** A recipient who has absorbed the framework before being asked for a verdict has joined the frame being checked.
- **Fig 9 :27 to :28** - **"describe it before you go looking, or any frame you pick will seem to fit"**. Ask what they would expect to see if the shape claim were true before giving them the essay links.
- **Fig 2 :40** - **"agreement about the world is worthless when the agreeing frames share an evidence pool"**. Do not select recipients for likely agreement and do not pool later agreement as corroboration.

**Recommended order in a reply:** (1) the shape claim and its four refuters; (2) ask what they would count as a failure, and record their verdict; (3) only then the figure links, with the note that the figures, not this summary, are the authority; (4) offer the formula as a method for their own problem, with their own ground and refuter written first.

## Two-stage offer

**Stage 1.** Residual, shape claim and pre-registered refuter. No theory. The recipient checks the output without holding the framework's interiors (Fig 10 :52).

**Stage 2, opt-in only.** Sources, after the stage 1 verdict is recorded. Once a recipient has absorbed the theory, their agreement stops being evidence (Fig 2 :40).

**Stage 3.** The formula as a method to reuse. This is permitted because a generator crosses and a recording does not (Fig 4 :22). Each user must write down what would refute their result and state the mode of what they get (`THE_FORMULA.md`, "Direction").
