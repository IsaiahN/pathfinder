## Independent domain census

I redid the census before reading the primary's conclusions, using the WATSON order: R by arity, symmetry and scale; one line per domain with actors / currency / anchor; partition or union; order by dependency.

- **R (arity / symmetry / scale).** The residual is the gap between a decision model's prediction and what the ground returns, indexed per decision slot. Arity: three places (`Γ`, `a`, `o'`) plus a fourth for the network edge carrying coordination. Symmetry: "flatter" is a spectrum ordering, not a sequence; "better" is a threshold comparison, an ordered bond; coordination is a network relation. None of the governing bonds is symmetric. Scale: the single decision loop, the organizational network across the membrane, and the time channel.
- **Domain 1, decision loop.** Actors: decision makers, `Γ`, actions, observations. Currency: residual reduction. Anchor: the outcome that pays, which must not update on the org's report. Question type: per-slot measurement.
- **Domain 2, channel and network.** Actors: people, teams, reporting and information edges. Currency: information fidelity and coordination. Anchor: whether the signal reaches the slot; the channel that decays. Question type: edge-by-edge measurement.
- **Domain 3, verification seat.** Actors: the seat that keeps the ground reachable, the convention author, the verifier. Currency: verdicts versus conventions, the power to refuse. Anchor: the ground, never agreement. Question type: structural position, not job title.
- **Domain 4, habitat.** Actors: customers, competitors, regulators, conditions. Currency: payment, survival. Anchor: the ground that settles. Question type: the environment against which contact is measured.
- **X1, flatness as a virtue.** No anchor. Not a domain; a parameter of Domain 2.
- **X2, culture as sole cause.** Absorbed into Domains 1 and 3.

**Union, not partition.** Contact (D1 and D2) and verification (D3 and D4) are two conditions; a case in both is categorically different (Fig 9 :57). Dependency order: perception, vocabulary, objective, planning, learn and carry (Fig 3), with the chain diagnosis before the structure verdict.

My census matches the primary's four domains, its union reading and its two excluded candidates. I reached it from Fig 2's anchor rule and Fig 9's union/partition split, whereas the primary led with Fig 1's slot indexing and Fig 10's channel rule. Different starting figures, same boundaries: low overlap between routes, so the agreement is weak but not circular within the framework. It is still one evidence pool.
