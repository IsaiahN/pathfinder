## QUESTION

Do flatter organizations make better decisions, or do they create coordination problems and unclear accountability?

Restated in effect terms: does reducing hierarchy depth shrink `R = |Γ(b,a) - o'|` per decision slot, or grow it through channel failure and the loss of a refusal seat? Option A asserts that flat is better. Option B asserts that flat creates coordination and accountability failures. What each leads to: A averages per-slot residuals into an org-wide score and treats internal agreement as a verdict; B treats a coordination failure as the opposite of decision quality. The figures say both options are conditions of one union, and each must be anchored and reported separately (Fig 9 :55).

## DOMAIN CENSUS

R by arity, symmetry and scale, then one line per domain with actors, currency and anchor; partition or union; order by dependency.

- **R (arity / symmetry / scale).** Arity: at least three places, `Γ`, `a`, `o'`, indexed by slot and ordered in time, with a fourth place for the network edge carrying coordination. Symmetry: "flatter" is a spectrum ordering (Fig 13 :20) and "better" is an ordered comparison, not a symmetric join (Operators table :51). Scale: single decision loop, organizational network across the membrane, and the time channel.
- **D1 Decision loop.** Actors: decision makers, `Γ`, actions, observations. Currency: per-slot residual reduction. Anchor: the outcome the ground pays, a market, customer choice or patient outcome. Admitted.
- **D2 Communication and authority network.** Actors: people, teams, reporting and information edges. Currency: information fidelity, coordination, handoff speed. Anchor: whether the signal reaches the deciding slot; the channel. Admitted.
- **D3 Verification and accountability seat.** Actors: the seat that keeps the ground reachable, convention author, verifier. Currency: verdicts versus conventions, refusal. Anchor: the ground, never agreement. Admitted.
- **D4 Habitat.** Actors: customers, competitors, regulators, conditions. Currency: survival and payment. Anchor: the ground that settles. Admitted.
- **X1 Flatness as a free-floating virtue.** No nameable anchor. Not a domain; it is a parameter of D2.
- **X2 Culture as sole cause.** Already inside D1 and D3. Absorbed.

**Union, not partition.** D1 and D2 form the contact compound; D3 and D4 form the verification compound. Fig 9 :54: **"And when the split lands on a union rather than a partition, unbundle before you search again."** Fig 9 :57: **"What remains is smaller and it is also categorically different: a case in two conditions is not a harder case of either."**

**D3 Verification seat, status triangulated (Fig 10 and Fig 2, verifier non-access vs anchor must not update).**

Reading: accountability is structural. A seat that can refuse a decision it did not author is the thing that goes missing when a delayer removes every outside checker. Purpose-built substitutes are seats if they can refuse. Mode `specified`. Refuter: high measured accountability with no seat able to refuse and verifiers able to reconstruct the claims they check.

**D4 Habitat and the bond change, status triangulated (Fig 11 and Fig 4, capability vs membrane).**

Reading: an org-only redraw counts only if contact edges move. Over a fixed atom set, tall and flat are isomers. Mode `general` for the capability rule, `specified` for organizations. Refuter: a per-slot gain with no contact edge changed, or a reorganisation that necessarily changes the atom set (which kills the isomer form and leaves only the functional-group form).

## VERDICT (from the figures)

The figure-derived verdict: the either/or is a union. Flattening improves decisions where it keeps a channel to a non-updating ground and keeps a seat able to refuse, and it creates coordination and accountability failures where it removes the coordinating structure or the refusal seat. Verdicts are kept separate per domain and never averaged: D1 depends on a per-slot outcome anchor; D2 is a channel that can improve on one edge and fail on another; D3 is a seat with refusal power; D4 sets which structure pays and an org-only change without a contact change does nothing. Tall and flat are isomers over a fixed atom set. The layer count is a declared `gap`. Mode `specified`, ungrounded, candidate. The management evidence pool corroborates the union shape but carries no verdict weight; the low-overlap witnesses corroborate the mechanism, not the organizational fact. No world source was found that conflicts with the figure verdict, so the conflict rule was not triggered.

- **cost if wrong:** misdirected redesign. A firm failing the refusal-seat condition would be sold faster decision forums, and a firm failing the channel condition would be told to add more autonomy, so a single lever would be applied to a union that needs two. The cost is bounded and locatable because the shape, its refuters and the lineage are recorded, so an error is findable rather than invisible. The avoidable part is spending an outside reader's independence by sending a recording instead of a generator; Fig 2 :42 says *"Buy independence in proportion to the cost of being wrong."*
- **undoable:** yes. Nothing is deleted if the claim fails; a refutation is added and the reach falls because something was added rather than struck out (Fig 6, amendments). The shape and its four falsifiers are in a provenance trail so a later reader can locate the error (Fig 10 :62 to :64). A sent packet can be superseded by a corrected one without withdrawing a primitive.
- **reversed by:** a ground reading, not agreement. Any of the four pre-registered falsifiers: (1) a single condition that moves decision quality and accountability together across all slots in every habitat; (2) a per-slot decision gain with channel fidelity falling and no refusal seat; (3) a figure line deriving a unique optimal layer count; (4) a decision improvement measured below a demonstrably missing vocabulary or objective link. A source with a genuinely different evidence pool showing that per-slot decision quality is fully predicted by averaged reporting structure would also reverse the D1 verdict. Until one of these fires, the verdict stands as `candidate`.
