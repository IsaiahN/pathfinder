## Wider literature actually read, and what it bears on the question

Grouped by evidence pool so a shared pool is not mistaken for two witnesses.

**Pool A, ethnoscience and indigenous/local knowledge studies (one witness, not five).**

- **Development studies and ethnoscience.** Arun Agrawal, *Dismantling the Divide Between Indigenous and Scientific Knowledge*, Development and Change 26(3):413-439, 1995. Opened via the Virginia Tech SANREM record, 2026-11-22. Quote: *"To productively engage indigenous knowledge in development, we must go beyond the dichotomy of indigenous vs. scientific, and work towards greater autonomy for 'indigenous' peoples."* Bearing: **corroborates F1 at the level of method** in refusing a clean two-box divide, and it refuses the naive "local is a deficient science" reading. It conflicts with any attempt to rank the two frames. It does not decide the verdict, because the figure-derived difference is about anchor legitimacy, not about a sociological dichotomy.
- **Philosophy of ecology / TEK.** David Ludwig and Luana Poliseli, *Relating traditional and academic ecological knowledge: mechanistic and holistic epistemologies across cultures*, Biological Philosophy 33(5):43, 2018, read via PMC, 2026-11-22. Quote: *"a simple holism–mechanism divide misrepresents the epistemic resources of both TEK and AEK."* Also: *"holders of TEK are perfectly capable of identifying mechanisms that underlie ecological phenomena."* Bearing: **corroborates F9 and the retraction log** by refusing the easy "local is holistic, science is mechanistic" mapping. This is the strongest single check on an over-reading of the question. Same broad pool as Agrawal, so it is not an independent witness for the verdict.
- **Ecology and local knowledge modeling.** Annie Claude Bélisle, Hugo Asselin, Patrice LeBlanc and Sylvie Gauthier, *Local knowledge in ecological modeling*, Ecology and Society 23(2):14, 2018, read 2026-11-22. Quote: *"Scientific ecological knowledge (SEK) generally arises from hypothetico-deductive approaches, while LEK stems from direct contact of people with the environment."* Also: *"local people interact with the environment on a daily basis, yearlong, and over the long term."* Bearing: **corroborates F2** (contact and timescale) and **F7** (the concrete inventory is domain work, not framework work). It also reports that most of 23 studies involved local knowledge only at the data-collection stage, which is a measured instance of an import with no source gate.
- **Ecology and Society response.** R. K. Brook and S. M. McLachlan, *On Using Expert-Based Science to "Test" Local Ecological Knowledge*, Ecology and Society 10(2):r3, 2005, read via the journal DOI record and the response text, 2026-11-22. Quote: *"to assume that the ecological studies are error free and without any bias or limitation is perhaps somewhat misguided."* Bearing: **corroborates F1 and Fig 2 :37** that instruments can be wrong on axes they do not measure, and refuses the one-way validation of local by scientific knowledge.
- **World Bank policy report.** Stanford Zent, *Indigenous Knowledge, Local Knowledge, and Climate Change: Interconnections for Policy and Practice*, Indigenous Latin America Series 2, World Bank, 2025, opened 2026-11-22. Quote: *"ILK perspectives typically consist of many variables, qualitative measurement, high resolution, and knowledge sharing networks, while scientific knowledge operates on the basis of few variables, quantitative measurement, low resolution, and modeling based on seasonal or annual averages."* Bearing: **corroborates F2 and F7** with an explicit evidence-protocol contrast. Note the cost if wrong is concrete here: climate adaptation policy.

**Pool B, cognitive science of expertise (a different field, low overlap with Pool A).**

- **Expert cognition.** Michelene T. H. Chi, Paul J. Feltovich and Robert Glaser, *Categorization and Representation of Physics Problems by Experts and Novices*, Cognitive Science 5(2):121-152, 1981, read via full-text reproduction, 2026-11-22. Quote: *"experts initially abstract physics principles to approach and solve a problem representation, whereas novices base their representation and approaches on the problem's literal features."* Bearing: **corroborates F3 at a different stratum**: what a frame takes as the question depends on the structure of its library. Important limit: this is an expert/novice contrast, not a local/scientific contrast, so a local expert is an expert in their own frame and is not placed on the novice side of this result. Using it that way would be an echo without a shadow.
- **Journal of Expertise editorial.** David Z. Hambrick and Guillermo Campitelli, *Editorial: Introducing the Journal of Expertise*, Journal of Expertise 1(1), 2018, read 2026-11-22. Quote: *"JoE encourages authors to pre-register their studies, with specific plans for how they will analyze the data."* Bearing: venue evidence only, silent on the question. It also says publication is not contingent on results but on methodological soundness, which is the right norm for a refuter-first offer.

**Framework-internal essays, canonical reading only, not independent world witnesses.** Isaiah N, *The Library of Babel*, Oct 02 2026; *Decoding Tacit Knowledge*, Nov 10 2026; *The Recursive Self-Improvement Trap*, Nov 19 2026; *The Hard Problem is About Source*, Aug 28 2026; all Adventures in Machine Learning, read 2026-11-22. Overlap with the figures is maximal (same author), so they carry no independent corroboration weight. Their use is confined to `05_what_shape_means.md` and to the simulation-status declaration.

## Distribution: offer, do not push

Figure-grounded reasons:

- **Fig 4 :22:** *"Only methods cross up. Only head starts come down."* Sending the full architecture before anyone has described a residual sends a recording, not a generator. Lead with the shape claim and the refuter; offer links after.
- **Fig 10 :42:** *"A verifier that can reconstruct the claim cannot verify it."* A recipient who has absorbed the framework before being asked for a verdict has joined the frame being checked.
- **Fig 9 :28:** *"any frame you pick will seem to fit"* is the tail of *"describe it before you go looking, or any frame you pick will seem to fit"*. Ask what they would expect to see if the shape claim were true before giving them the literature.
- **Fig 2 :40:** *"disqualifying for a verdict: agreement about the world is worthless when the agreeing frames share an evidence pool."* A list selected for people who already agree produces a convention, not corroboration.

**Recommended order in a reply:** (1) the shape claim and its three refuters; (2) ask what they would count as a failure and record the answer; (3) only then the three links, with the note that the figures, not the summary, are the authority; (4) optionally, the formula as a method to reuse with a freshly written refuter.

## Venue use

The public venues in `03_public_contact_points.csv` are submission and contact routes in expertise studies and ethnoscience. Use the public submission portal or the public contact form. Do not send unsolicited personal email, do not harvest addresses, do not bulk send. Several venues publish AI-use policies: the Journal of Expertise requires a declaration, Journal of Ethnobiology requires an ethics statement, and Journal of Ethnobiology and Ethnomedicine points to BMC and Springer Nature policy requiring LLM use to be declared in Methods. This package is a private checking instrument; a human researcher owns and writes anything submitted.

## Zip and hosting

This agent cannot upload or send anything. If the package is hosted (Drive, Dropbox, a repository release), prefer a link that lets a recipient decline contact. An opt-in download page is closer to the figure's rule than an attachment pushed to a list.
