# The Tether's answer on local and scientific expertise (COG-0039)

**Mode: `specified` on the difference between the two frames, `general` on the loop shape. Not `grounded`.** This is a shape and a route, not a measurement. Simulated derivation checked against the figures (`05_what_shape_means.md` section 7), not a ground reading.

## 1. The residual it answers

Local expertise and scientific expertise are two expert frames, each betting, acting, observing and ranging a per-slot residual R = |Γ(b,a) - o′| against a ground. The question asks what differs in (a) which observations are admissible as o′ that move R, and (b) the direction of the residual each tries to close, that is, the questions each asks. Verdicts must be kept per frame, never averaged into "expertise" (Fig 1 :19: *"The residual is indexed by object slot, not aggregated."*).

## 2. The shape

1. **They run the same loop; the difference is in actors, currency and anchor, not in a different machine.** Fig 4 :19-20: *"The kernel is the part that does not change with size: the same loop and the same rule for naming what worked, whether one agent is running it or a thousand. Only the actors, the currency and the anchor change."* Neither frame is the anchor for the other; the surplus exists where their closures differ (Fig 8 :45: *"Two frames together reach what neither reaches alone."*).

2. **Evidence difference one: which anchor is legitimate is set by what the question is constitutively about.** Fig 2 :36: *"An anchor is legitimate when the question is constitutively about it."* Fig 2 :37: *"Human judgment is the right anchor for is this a good answer for a person, because the question is about people."* Fig 2 :37-38: *"It is only an instrument for is this code correct, and instruments can be wrong on axes they do not measure."* A question about a particular place and community has that place and community outcome as its legitimate anchor; a question about a frame-independent feature uses an instrument, with the instrument's blind spots inherited. This is not a rigor ranking.

3. **Evidence difference two: in-place enumerated habitat versus substituted habitat.** Fig 11 :39: *"capability is a property of agent-and-habitat, never of the agent alone."* Fig 11 :43: *"The habitat is enumerated, never composed."* Fig 11 :66: *"Isolation is not removal of the habitat. It is substitution of one habitat for another."* Local evidence is long-horizon contact with an enumerated habitat, including relations several steps out (Fig 11 :52-54). Scientific evidence is gathered in a substituted habitat where contact is narrowed, so it carries *"what you failed to reproduce"* (Fig 11 :68) and *"what you unintentionally introduced is invisible until it acts"* (Fig 11 :69).

4. **Question difference: the direction of the residual is an invisible preference that must be made a term.** Fig 5 :62-63: *"A frame searching for an answer of a particular shape holds that shape as a PREFERENCE rather than as a term. It is not bound, it predicts nothing, and it accrues no residual, so nothing here can see it."* Fig 5 :65: *"The remedy is a discipline rather than a branch: make the sought-for shape an ORDINARY TERM."* The difference in questions asked sits at the uninstrumented middle of Fig 3's chain (vocabulary to objective), not in the output.

5. **Agreement is not automatically corroboration.** Fig 2 :31: *"Independence, between two frames, is how little they draw on the same evidence."* Fig 8 :50: *"Two frames can hold the same atom by convergence, reaching it separately, which is corroboration at maximum independence."* If the two frames re-describe the same observations, that is one pool speaking twice, not two witnesses.

6. **The concrete evidence inventory is a `gap`.** Fig 9 :32: *"you get a kind of thing, never the answer itself"*; Fig 13 :134: *"Unreachable is a verdict here, not a silence,"* The framework gives the shape of the difference. Which species, seasons, indicators and protocols belong in a particular local evidence system is a habitat enumeration read off the world, not derivable here.

## 3. What it explicitly does not say

- Not that one kind of expertise is better, more rigorous, more rational, or more "real" than the other. Each has a legitimate anchor for its own question.
- Not that local expertise is proto-science, or that science is just formalised local knowledge. Both are bindings with no source gate; the world literature actively refuses them (Agrawal 1995; Ludwig and Poliseli 2018).
- Not that local frames are holistic and scientific frames mechanistic. That divide is refused by the literature and is not a figure line.
- Not that community consensus or peer prestige makes a claim true. Fig 1 :27: *"A frame cannot score itself with a quantity it also produces."* Fig 7 :35: *"A room’s agreement is variation, not a verdict. Selection between rooms is where the ground votes."*
- Not that the two frames are isomers under Fig 12's definition. They share the loop form, a functional group, not a proven atom set (`06`).
- Not `grounded`. Nothing here is asserted as settled by experiment.

## 4. What would settle it: the ground, not a further argument

The missing thing is a source-discriminating observation, characterized in effect terms. *The Hard Problem is About Source* (read 2026-11-22): an instrument that reads green *"cannot comment on the source"*; the spectrometer resolved it. Here:

- **Anchor test.** Is there an observation that discriminates whether a given decision was settled by the community outcome (a legitimate anchor for a question about that community) or by an instrument that happens to correlate with it? If yes, F1 is testable; if no, the `gap` stands.
- **Contact/cascade test.** Measure whether a scientific result obtained in a substituted habitat reproduces a third-order cascade that the local frame tracks in place, and measure what was not reproduced. This is the F2 test.
- **Direction test.** Ask each frame to write down its sought-for shape as an ordinary term, then check whether that term accrues a residual and can be refused. If a frame cannot, F3 is false for it.
- **Independence test.** Disclose the evidence pool behind a purported local/scientific convergence and see whether the verdict survives. If it does not, Fig 8 :50 was never satisfied.

## 5. The honest stop

**Unreached, not unreachable.** The concrete inventory of evidence for a specific domain is out of reach with the current library. It is not absent from the world; it lives in a frame whose closure differs, namely the local experts themselves and the ethnoscientific literature that has worked with them. Importing it requires a source gate and a shadow test and is debited against independence (Fig 6 :23: *"No frame escapes its own closure. The bound moves only on contact with a frame whose closure differs."*). *"Indiscernibility is not identity."* The answer stops at the limit of the available instruments and records the lineage so the stop is checkable.

## 6. One-paragraph version for outreach

> The Tether does not rank local and scientific expertise. It says they run the same corrective loop and differ in three places: each ranges against a different legitimate anchor, because each question is constitutively about something different; each keeps contact with a differently constituted habitat, local knowledge enumerated in place and scientific knowledge gathered in a substituted one with named blind spots; and each pursues a direction of search that its own mechanism cannot see until it is written down as an ordinary term. Agreement between the two is corroboration only if they reached it by separate routes. The framework gives the shape of the difference, the falsifiers written in advance and the instruments that would close it; the concrete evidence list for any particular place is left open until a ground settles it.
