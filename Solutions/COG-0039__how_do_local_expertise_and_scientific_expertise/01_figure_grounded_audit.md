## 0. The residual, stated in effect terms

The question compares two expert frames. An expert frame is a bounded system with a library Γ: a person, a craft, a discipline, a model (Symbols table :111: *"A frame is any bounded system with a library: an agent, a person, a discipline, a model."*). Each frame bets, acts, observes, and ranges against a ground through R = |Γ(b,a) - o′| per slot.

The unexplained gap is this: **given two expert frames, local and scientific, what differs in (a) which observations are admissible as o′ that move the per-slot residual, and (b) the direction of the residual each frame tries to close, that is, the questions each asks?** Fig 1 :19: *"The residual is indexed by object slot, not aggregated."* We must keep per-slot and per-frame statuses separate, never average them into "expertise in general."

Two failed ways of stating the residual were refused before the census:

1. "Local expertise has less evidence, science has more." This names an answer (a deficit) before describing the effect, against Fig 9 :28: *"any frame you pick will seem to fit"* and Fig 8 :53: *"Residual first, frame second."*
2. "Local expertise is tacit, science is explicit." This is a vocabulary from one pool (the expertise-studies pool) imported before the residual was described. It may be used later as corroboration, never as the derivation.

What an answer would look like, bound as an ordinary term before the run (formula step "Direction"): **a difference in which anchor is legitimate and in which habitat is in contact with the residual, with the two frames' verdicts kept separate.** That binding is written down now so it can accrue a residual and be refused by the same test as everything else.

**Anchor check.** A nameable anchor exists for each frame: the ground it ranges against, which does not change because of what the frame thinks (Fig 2 :29: *"An anchor is whatever settles the question and does not change its answer because of what any frame thinks."*). So this is a well-formed question, not a `gap` at the anchor step.

## 1. Domain census, before choosing any figure line

### 1.1 Arity, symmetry, scale

- **Arity.** The core relation is three-place plus an anchor: expert frame Γ, action a, observation o′, ranged against a ground. "Difference between two kinds of expertise" adds a comparison of two such relations, so one axis is a threshold relation between frames, not an identity. Fig 12 defines comparison (⋛) as a threshold between two quantities, which makes the comparison ordered/unordered question live in the bond, not in the atoms.
- **Symmetry.** Swapping the two frames does not leave one term in the way a symmetric bond would; they have different closures and different anchors. But neither is senior: their comparison has a surplus only where the closures differ (Fig 8 :45: *"Two frames together reach what neither reaches alone."*). So the relation is asymmetric in content and symmetric in standing.
- **Scale.** The slot is one expert (step loop), the ranking of "expertise" exists only at a population/community scale (Fig 4 :19-20: *"The kernel is the part that does not change with size: the same loop and the same rule for naming what worked, whether one agent is running it or a thousand. Only the actors, the currency and the anchor change."*), and the habitat crosses a membrane that is lossy (Fig 4 :27: *"The transform between scales loses information, and that is the point."*). The residual is intrinsically cross-scale.

### 1.3 Partition or union

**Union, not partition.** D1 and D3 (loop and closure) and D2 and D4 (habitat and anchor) are two compounds, and their overlap has its own loop. Fig 9 :54: *"And when the split lands on a union rather than a partition, unbundle before you search again."* Fig 9 :57: *"What remains is smaller and it is also categorically different: a case in two conditions is not a harder case of either."* The difference between the two expert frames is a union of an anchor difference and a habitat difference; a single lever that fixes one leaves the other binding.

### 1.4 Dependency order

Per Fig 3: perception, vocabulary, objective, planning, learn and carry. For expertise: first perceive the residuals in the habitat, then have terms for what things afford, then say what counts as progress (this is where "the questions they ask" lives), then a route, then name what worked. Fig 3 :27: *"A link with no instrument is not a link that works. It is a link nobody has looked at."* The middle link (vocabulary to objective) is the one with no instrument, which is why the question is hard.

## 3. Findings, each with a verbatim quote and a refuter

### F1. The difference in evidence is an anchor-legitimacy difference, not a rigor difference

Fig 2 :36: **"An anchor is legitimate when the question is constitutively about it."**

Fig 2 :37: **"Human judgment is the right anchor for is this a good answer for a person, because the question is about people."** Fig 2 :37-38: **"It is only an instrument for is this code correct, and instruments can be wrong on axes they do not measure."**

Fig 2 :29: **"An anchor is whatever settles the question and does not change its answer because of what any frame thinks. A measurement, an experiment, a counter, a market, a patient outcome."**

Reading. Two expert frames ask questions constitutively about different things. A local expert's question is constitutively about a particular habitat and the community living in it, so the state of that habitat and the community outcome are legitimate anchors, and human judgment is in its proper place because the question is about people and a place. A scientific expert's question is constitutively about a feature that should hold for any frame, so an instrument is the legitimate anchor. But the instrument is an instrument, and *"instruments can be wrong on axes they do not measure."* Neither frame is more rigorous; each ranges against a different legitimate anchor. This is `specified` (the mapping of the figure's person/code example onto the two expertise frames is ours), triangulated for the general anchor rule (Fig 2 :29 and :36 are two distinct passages with low overlap within the figure).

**Refuter.** Exhibit a case where an instrument measures the very axis the local question is constitutively about, settles it without loss, and is not moved by the community's opinion. Then the two anchors converge on that axis and the claimed difference does not hold there. Reporting convergence from a shared evidence pool does not count (Fig 2 :40).

### F2. Local expertise is capability bound to an enumerated habitat; scientific expertise works by substituting a habitat

Fig 11 :39: **"So the answerable question is not what intelligence is, but what makes a system capable. And capability is a property of agent-and-habitat, never of the agent alone."** Fig 11 :40: **"A system cannot be made more capable by improving the agent in isolation. An improvement that does not change contact changes nothing, however much it improves."**

Fig 11 :43: **"The habitat is enumerated, never composed."** Fig 11 :48: **"You do not invent the list. You read it off the world, and what you cannot perceive or measure yet is the residual."**

Fig 11 :66: **"Isolation is not removal of the habitat. It is substitution of one habitat for another."** Fig 11 :68: **"what you failed to reproduce,"** Fig 11 :69: **"what you unintentionally introduced is invisible until it acts"** Fig 11 :70: **"A test harness is a substituted habitat. Which is why a synthetic solve proves wiring and never capability."**

Fig 11 :52-54: **"And contact cascades. Scoping the habitat to direct contact is how DDT reached the eagles: first-order enumeration, second-order effect, and the residual that mattered was three steps out."**

Reading. Local evidence is long-horizon, in-situ contact with an enumerated habitat including actors, conditions and relations, and the residual that matters can be several steps out in the cascade. Scientific evidence is gathered in a substituted habitat where contact is deliberately narrowed and variables are held; it therefore inherits things it failed to reproduce and things it unintentionally introduced, and a result in that habitat proves wiring rather than full capability. The questions differ with the habitat: local questions are read off the whole habitat, scientific questions are posed where control is possible. `specified`, triangulated (Fig 11 habitat rules and Fig 6 instrument rules are separate passages with different subjects).

**Refuter.** Show a local expertise practice that is portable without loss of contact (its capability survives moving the residual to a substituted habitat with everything reproduced), or a scientific result whose substituted habitat demonstrably reproduces the third-order cascade of the target. Either would collapse the habitat difference.

### F3. The "questions they ask" difference is a residual-direction difference the loop cannot see from inside

Fig 5 :62-63: **"A frame searching for an answer of a particular shape holds that shape as a PREFERENCE rather than as a term. It is not bound, it predicts nothing, and it accrues no residual, so nothing here can see it."** Fig 5 :63: **"Every check above runs on the MECHANISM and none runs on the DIRECTION OF THE QUESTION"**

Fig 5 :65-66: **"The remedy is a discipline rather than a branch: make the sought-for shape an ORDINARY TERM. Write down what an answer would look like, bind it, and let it accrue a residual like anything else."**

Reading. Both kinds of expertise run the same loop (Fig 4 :19-20: only actors, currency and anchor change). What differs in the questions they ask is the direction of the residual, which is held as a preference and is therefore invisible to the mechanism. To compare the two sets of questions you must bind each frame's sought-for shape as an ordinary term and let it accrue a residual. This is the deep reason the question is hard: a check of the mechanism cannot see the direction. `general` for the direction limit, `specified` for the two expertise frames.

**Refuter.** Show a frame whose question direction is fully determined by its mechanism and ground, so that no free preference term remains. Then there is no direction difference to bind and F3 is false for that frame.

### F4. Independence: the two kinds are two frames, and agreement means something only without a shared pool

Fig 2 :31: **"Independence, between two frames, is how little they draw on the same evidence."** Fig 2 :40: **"disqualifying for a verdict: agreement about the world is worthless when the agreeing frames share an evidence pool"**

Fig 8 :45: **"Two frames together reach what neither reaches alone."** Fig 8 :50: **"Two frames can hold the same atom by convergence, reaching it separately, which is corroboration at maximum independence."** Fig 8 :51: **"Convergent derivation and adopted import look identical in the contents. Only the record of where each came from separates them"**

Reading. The value of comparing local and scientific expertise is that they are low-overlap frames. Where they converge by separate routes, that is corroboration at maximum independence. Where scientific "validation" of local knowledge merely re-describes the same observations in scientific terms, that is one pool wearing two names and carries zero corroboration weight. The record of where each came from is the whole issue. `specified`, triangulated (Fig 2 independence and Fig 8 surplus are distinct figures with different proof structures).

**Refuter.** Exhibit a purported corroboration in which the two frames demonstrably draw on the same observations, and show that the verdict did not change when that was disclosed. Under the figure that agreement was never a witness; the refuter tests whether we actually treat it as one.

### F5. Neither frame may score itself with a quantity it also produces

Fig 1 :27: **"A frame cannot score itself with a quantity it also produces."**

Fig 7 :35: **"A room’s agreement is variation, not a verdict. Selection between rooms is where the ground votes."**

Fig 10 :59: **"an interest in the outcome is unavoidable, an interest in the reading is disqualifying."**

Reading. Local expertise validated only by community agreement is a mirror chain. Scientific expertise validated only by publication counts or peer prestige is also a mirror chain. Both need a ground that does not update on the frame. This is a shared failure mode, not a difference, and it is recorded so the verdict does not mistake either community for an anchor. `general`, triangulated (Fig 1, Fig 7, Fig 10, three distinct figures).

**Refuter.** Identify a self-scoring mechanism that nonetheless tracks a named external ground across a change of residual, with the tracking measured rather than assumed. Then the ban is too strong as stated and must be narrowed.

### F6. The instruments themselves are improved from worse instruments, in both frames

Fig 6 :32: **"An instrument is not built from a description. It is improved from a worse instrument already returning something."**

Fig 6 :29: **"inward: is our representation adequate to hold it? The instrument extends access. Fails by refining forever on an axis that cannot carry it. Sharpen in our own terms."** Fig 6 :30: **"outward: does another frame already hold it? The description travels. Fails by being sharpened into terms only we can read. Cast it in effect terms."**

Reading. The trained perceptual and practical skill of a local expert is an instrument built across a lifetime from a worse one already returning readings. The laboratory instrument is built the same way. Neither is specified from a description; each grows out of a previous instrument that already returned something. This is a genuine similarity between the frames and is kept separate from the differences. `general`.

**Refuter.** Show an instrument in either frame specified from a description alone, with no predecessor already returning a reading. Then Fig 6 :32 is false as a universal instrument rule and must be limited.

### F7. The specific evidence inventory is a `gap`; the framework returns a shape, not a list

Fig 9 :32: **"you get a kind of thing, never the answer itself"**

Fig 13 :134: **"Unreachable is a verdict here, not a silence,"** Fig 13 :135: **"It cannot say which primitive is missing, because naming it would mean already holding it."**

Fig 9 :39: **"a search that finds nothing is never a proof of absence."**

Reading. The figures govern the shape of the difference (legitimate anchor, habitat contact, residual direction) but not the concrete list of what counts as evidence in a particular fishery, forest or clinic. That list is a habitat enumeration read off the world (Fig 11 :43, :48), not derivable from the six-term set. Declaring `gap` here is a verdict, not a silence. `general`.

**Refuter.** Derive a concrete evidence protocol for a specific local domain from the figure set alone, with no import from that domain. Since the figures say they return a kind of thing rather than the answer, this falsifier is expected to fail; recording it keeps the stop honest.

### F8. Prestige is not action, in either frame

Fig 2 :42: **"Buy independence in proportion to the cost of being wrong."**

From *The Recursive Self-Improvement Trap*, read 2026-11-22: *"Prestige can fund an action. It can give an action a budget to be tried. But it cannot settle an action. Only the ground can settle it."*

Reading. A famous expert, a long pedigree, or a large community of believers gives a claim a budget to be tried, not a verdict. The cost of being wrong here is real (resource management, health, education), so independence is worth buying in proportion. This applies symmetrically to both frames and is why `03_public_contact_points.csv` contains venues, not a list of authorities. `general`.

**Refuter.** Produce a case where the prestige of the holder changed the ground's return, measured as a change in the ground rather than a change in the room's opinion. Then prestige has settled an action and the framework's separation is wrong.

## 4. Pre-registered refuter, written before the conclusion

Required before outreach: a falsifier written before the run is a design instrument. The shape claim is refuted if any of the following holds:

1. An instrument settles the axis a local question is constitutively about, without loss and without moving with the community's opinion, so the anchor-legitimacy difference collapses on that axis (F1).
2. A local capability is shown to be portable with no contact loss, or a scientific result's substituted habitat is shown to reproduce the target's third-order cascade (F2).
3. A frame's question direction is fully determined by its mechanism and ground, leaving no preference term to bind (F3).
4. A purported corroboration is shown to draw on one evidence pool and the verdict survives disclosure of that (F4).
5. A concrete evidence protocol for a specific domain is derived from the figures alone with no domain import (F7). This one would expand the framework's claimed reach and would require rewriting the honest-stop claim, not withdrawing the verdict.

If any of 1 to 4 fires, the corresponding finding is withdrawn. Until one is run, no part of this may be labelled `grounded`.

### Three-question step-7 check

- **SEARCHED.** Is the anchor-legitimacy answer already in our closure? Yes for the general rule (Fig 2), no for the specific domain inventory. More search at the same reach will not produce the inventory.
- **INWARD.** Sharpen our own terms: bind each frame's sought-for shape as an ordinary term (Fig 5 :65), so the direction of its questions can accrue a residual. This is the cheap next step.
- **OUTWARD.** Import in effect terms: find a frame whose closure predicts the shape of the missing evidence protocol. The ethnoscience and expertise-studies literature is exactly such a frame, used as corroboration with a recorded provenance and no verdict weight (Fig 8 :51).

These are kept distinct; INWARD and OUTWARD are never read against each other.

## 6. Recommendation

1. Send the shape claim and the refuter first, not the theory (Fig 10 :52: *"Bring a characterised residual, never the source."*).
2. State the mode on every sentence. "Local and scientific expertise differ in legitimate anchor and habitat contact" is `specified`, not `grounded`.
3. Keep the two frames' verdicts separate and never average them into "expertise." The surplus lives where the closures differ (Fig 8 :51).
4. Do not ask either pool for agreement. Ask what they would count as a failure (Fig 2 :35; Fig 7 :35).
5. Use the public submission routes in `03_public_contact_points.csv`. A room is not an anchor (Fig 7 :13).
6. Record the concrete evidence inventory as a `gap` to be filled by an outside frame, never as a list the framework pretends to derive (Fig 9 :32).

## 7. Updated verdict

The shape survives: **the two kinds of expertise differ because each ranges against a different legitimate anchor and keeps contact with a differently constituted habitat, and because each pursues a different residual direction that its own mechanism cannot see; they share the same loop and neither is senior.** Everything concrete in the common story (local equals tacit and place-bound, science equals explicit and universal) does not survive as a figure line: it is a world-pool binding with no source gate, and parts of it are contradicted by the world pool itself (see `09_watson_answer.md`). Strains after re-census: **none unresolved**; the two apparent strains were returned to the census and resolved by distinguishing the quantities they govern.
