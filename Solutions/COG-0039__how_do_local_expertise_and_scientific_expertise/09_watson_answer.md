## QUESTION

How do local expertise and scientific expertise differ in the evidence they rely on and the questions they ask? (Source: cognitive science.txt line 48, section 4 Reasoning, causation, and knowledge.)

Restated as an effect: two expert frames each hold R = |Γ(b,a) - o′| per slot against a ground. What differs in which observations are admitted as o′ and in the direction of the residual each frame pursues? R is indexed per frame and per slot, never aggregated (Fig 1 :19: "The residual is indexed by object slot, not aggregated."). Both frames have a nameable anchor, so this is a well-formed question and not yet a `gap`.

## DOMAIN CENSUS

Scale: one expert (step loop), a community or discipline (population ranking), and the habitat across a lossy membrane (Fig 4 :27).

**D5, triangulated (Fig 5 and Fig 3, direction limit and uninstrumented middle link).**

## VERDICT (from the figures)

**The figure-derived verdict, per domain, never averaged:** the two kinds of expertise run one shared loop, so neither is senior (D1, Fig 4). They differ in evidence because each ranges against a different legitimate anchor determined by what its question is constitutively about (D4, Fig 2 :36-38) and because each keeps contact with a differently constituted habitat, local enumerated in place, scientific substituted with named blind spots (D2, Fig 11 :39, :43, :66). They differ in questions asked because that difference is the direction of the residual, an invisible preference that must be written down as an ordinary term before it can be checked, and it sits at the uninstrumented middle of the dependency chain (D5, Fig 5, Fig 3). Convergence between them is corroboration only at maximum independence; community agreement and peer prestige are mirrors in both frames (Fig 2 :40, Fig 8 :50, Fig 7 :35). The concrete evidence inventory is a declared `gap`, not a hidden answer. Mode `specified`, not `grounded`. One world conflict, the attempt to read a local/scientific divide as a rigor ranking, was returned to the census and resolved by re-mapping to anchor legitimacy; it did not flip the verdict.

- **Cost if wrong:** real and practical. If the anchor-legitimacy difference is missed, local evidence is treated as defective science and discarded, and a substituted habitat is used where the cascade was not reproduced (Fig 11 :68). Conversely, if instruments are refused altogether, the independent anchor is lost and both frames drift in mirror chains (Fig 2 :35). Resource management, health and education decisions turn on this. In outreach terms, an expert's twenty minutes would be spent refuting a shape that was never the right shape.
- **Undoable:** yes. No term is deleted if the claim fails; a refutation is added and the reach falls because something was added rather than because anything was deleted (Fig 6 :42-43). The shape, its five refuters and the two re-mappings are recorded in a provenance trail so a later reader can locate the error rather than only feel it (Fig 10 :62-64).
- **Reversed by:** any of the five pre-registered falsifiers in `01` section 4, in particular (1) an instrument settling the local axis without loss or movement with community opinion, (2) a portable local capability with no contact loss, or a substituted habitat reproducing the third-order cascade, (3) a frame whose question direction is fully determined by mechanism and ground, (4) a shared-pool convergence whose verdict survives disclosure, or (5) a concrete evidence protocol derived from the figures alone with no domain import. A genuinely independent evidence pool showing that the anchor differences reduce to one metric without residual would also reverse it.
