## What is deliberately not here

- No personal emails of named individuals. The one public journal office address encountered is an organisational address published on a journal submission page, and is referenced through the page, not harvested.
- No ranking of people by how likely they are to agree with the claim. Selecting for receptiveness manufactures the disqualifying correlation (Fig 2 :40: *"disqualifying for a verdict: agreement about the world is worthless when the agreeing frames share an evidence pool"*).
- No bulk-send plan. A room is not an anchor; adding recipients raises vocabulary, not evidence (Fig 7 :13: *"agreement compounds, evidence does not"*).
- No claim that this package decides which expertise is better. The verdict keeps the two domains' statuses separate and never averages them.
