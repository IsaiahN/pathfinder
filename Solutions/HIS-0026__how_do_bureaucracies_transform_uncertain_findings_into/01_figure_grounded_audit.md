## 0. The question, restated in effect terms

> How do bureaucracies transform uncertain findings into rules, statistics, or official categories?

The ordinary wording hides three different outputs behind one verb, "transform." Restated as an effect:

- **Input state:** a finding with positive residual on at least one object slot. It is provisional, contestable, and still answerable to some ground (a measurement, a case outcome, a field observation). Fig 1 :19: *"The residual is indexed by object slot, not aggregated."*
- **Output state:** an artifact, namely a rule, a statistic, or an official category, that is treated as settled, authoritative, and capable of deciding outcomes.
- **Effect to explain:** by what operation does an unsettled claim acquire the standing of a settled one without the ground paying for it?

Two options with different consequences:

| option | what it leads to |
|---|---|
| A. The artifact carries ground contact, so it remains revisable and refuses when the ground turns against it. | Residual stays visible per slot. The artifact is a convention in the Fig 2 :41 sense: agreement about shared meaning is the product, not a corruption. |
| B. The artifact is a convention promoted into an outcome-deciding constant. | Residual is masked. The artifact stops being checkable and carries institutional authority instead (Fig 10 :48). Drift is coherent and nobody notices (Fig 2 :34). |

The derivation does not assume A or B. It derives from the figures what the transition looks like when it succeeds as settlement and what it looks like when it is manufactured. It then states what would discriminate between them at the ground.

**Direction bound as an ordinary term.** Formula step "Direction" requires that the sought-for shape be written down, bound, and allowed to accrue a residual rather than remain an invisible preference. The bound shape is: *declared convention to statable residue to averaged slot signal to outcome-deciding constant, with the ground channel replaced by institutional authority.* That shape is the object under test, not a conclusion smuggled in as a premise.

## 1. Domain census, before any figure line is chosen

### 1.1 R by arity, symmetry, and scale

- **Arity.** Three named outputs: rule, statistic, official category. These are not variants of one object. A rule is an ordered or threshold relation that decides who qualifies. A statistic is a symmetric aggregation over object slots. An official category is a declared boundary or meaning. R is therefore at least three conditions, a union, not one hard question. Fig 9 :5: *"are not one hard question but several well-formed ones. Split it rather than search."* One lever cannot clear all three; a fix that repairs the statistic leaves the category binding standing.
- **Symmetry.** A statistic is symmetric: swapping two object slots leaves the aggregate unchanged, so the arrangement information is discarded. A rule is ordered (Operators table :30, sequence) or a threshold (Operators table :48, comparison). A category is an identity or boundary assertion (Operators table :24, identity). Different bonds, so different terms under Fig 12 :46.
- **Scale.** The finding originates in a local epistemic frame with direct contact to the ground. The artifact circulates at institutional scale. There is a membrane between the two with a lossy, directional transform (Fig 4 :27). The round trip gap is not zero and must not be assumed zero.

### 1.2 One line per domain (actors / currency / anchor)

A domain needs a nameable anchor. If nothing settles a question in it and does not move when a frame pushes on it, it is not yet a domain.

**D1, the epistemic frame that produced the finding.**
- Actors: researchers, instruments, observations, the object slots under study.
- Currency: per-slot residuals, the gap between prediction and observation.
- Anchor: the ground, that is the measurement or experiment that would settle the finding. Nameable: yes, the instrument reading. Fig 2 :29: *"An anchor is whatever settles the question and does not change its answer because of what any frame thinks."*
- Question type: can the residual be read at all, and is it indexed per slot?

**D2, the bureaucracy as a frame.**
- Actors: officials, committees, registries, standards bodies, review chains.
- Currency: records, classifications, counts, compliance decisions.
- Anchor: the declared convention and the institution's own prior outputs. This is nameable as the official record, but it fails the anchor test, because it updates on what the frame thinks. Fig 2 :33: *"The anchor must not update."* Fig 2 :34: *"If it does, panel 3 turns into panel 1: the anchor becomes another frame, everything drifts together, and the drift is coherent, which is why nobody notices."* So D2 has a *purported* anchor, not a genuine one. That is the structural defect the question asks about.
- Question type: partition boundary, which compositions may cross from finding to artifact, and by what gate.

**D3, the artifact in its outcome field.**
- Actors: regulated parties, cases, citizens, downstream users of the statistic.
- Currency: decisions and material consequences (a benefit paid, a permit granted, a school classified).
- Anchor: the outcome in the world, which is nameable: the payment cleared or did not, the patient was harmed or was not.
- Question type: does the artifact still range against this ground, or does it decide the outcome by its own authority?

### 1.3 Partition or union

Union, not partition. The three output kinds overlap: a statistic becomes a threshold in a rule, a category determines which slots enter the statistic. Fig 9 :54: *"And when the split lands on a union rather than a partition, unbundle before you search again."* Fig 9 :56: *"A bundled problem is usually a union of overlapping conditions, not a list of separate ones."* A single lever, such as "publish the underlying data," clears the region where only the statistic binds and leaves the intersections standing, because the category still decides which slots count and the rule still decides outcomes. This is why the verdicts in section 7 are kept per domain and never averaged.

### 1.4 Dependency order

Ordered by dependency (Fig 3), not by importance:

1. **Perception.** Does the bureaucracy register the finding at all, and tell it apart from what was there before?
2. **Vocabulary.** Does it have terms for what the finding affords, that is what can be counted, filed, compared, refused?
3. **Objective.** Can it say what would count as progress here, in its own terms: legibility, throughput, closure, compliance?
4. **Planning.** A route to that objective under incomplete information.
5. **Learn and carry.** Name what worked, verify it, carry it to the next case.

Fig 3 :19: *"Optimising a link that already works cannot rescue one that does not."* The characteristic break for this question is at link 3. Fig 3 :25-26 says the link between having terms for what a situation offers and being able to say what would count as progress *"has neither"* obvious failures nor obvious tests. Fig 3 :27: *"A link with no instrument is not a link that works. It is a link nobody has looked at."* A bureau can count fluently and file accurately while its objective, legibility, has no instrument that checks whether it still tracks the original finding. Effort spent on counting arrives at a step that cannot use it.

## 3. Findings F1 to F8, each with a refuter

### F1. The decisive mechanism is promotion of a convention into an outcome-deciding constant

Fig 10 :16-18:

> **"the convention does not fix the bug"**
> **"it makes the bug statable"**
> **"a declared meaning is not a grounded one"**

Fig 10 :47-49:

> **"a convention that decides an outcome has stopped being one"**
> **"a convention nothing can check is a constant the seat authored, carrying the seat's authority"**
> **"Declaring a meaning does not ground it."**

Fig 2 :43: **"Guard, from Figure 10: a convention that decides an outcome has stopped being one."**

The derivation runs as follows. A bureau meets an uncertain finding. It cannot proceed without a term for it, so it declares a meaning: a code, a category, a threshold, a reported figure. At this first step the convention is legitimate. It does not fix the bug; it makes the bug statable (Fig 10 :17). The failure occurs at the second step, when the same convention is used to decide an outcome: who receives a benefit, which case is closed, which school is failing. At that moment it stops being a convention (Fig 10 :47). Because nothing can check it, it becomes a constant authored by the seat, carrying the seat's authority (Fig 10 :48). The residual it was created to hold is still live; the artifact merely removes the instrument that could see it.

This is triangulated across two figures with low overlap. Fig 10 reaches the conclusion from the checking arrangement (what a seat may author). Fig 2 reaches it from anchor dynamics (what happens when the pinned thing updates). Convergence from different quantities is weak corroboration inside the framework, but both are the same evidence pool, so this is internal structure, not two independent witnesses.

**Refuter F1:** show a case in which the artifact decides an outcome yet remains a checkable convention whose meaning the ground can revise, with a recorded instance of the ground revising it. That breaks the promotion claim.

### F2. The statistic discards the only signal that says where to look

Fig 1 :19: **"The residual is indexed by object slot, not aggregated."**
Fig 1 :22: **"Aggregating across slots is how a live signal disappears into an average."**
Fig 1 :28: **"The gap is measured per object, not per step. A system that averages across objects has thrown away the only signal that says where to look."**

A statistic is the named operation the framework forbids while the residual is still live. The per-slot mass is summed or averaged into a single number; the identity of the slot carrying the positive mass is lost. The result looks more precise than the finding, because a number is easier to quote than a distribution of objections. But the number cannot say where to look, which is the only thing the residual was for. Fig 10 :26 names the downstream channel failures: **"saturated metrics, pooled readings."**

Note what this is not. It is not a ban on aggregation. Aggregation after the residual has been settled is a legitimate compression; Fig 5 :43 licenses any term whose statement plus leftover costs less than the confusion it removes. The claim is narrower: aggregation *before* the slot has been settled throws away the correction signal. World corroboration: Scott's *Seeing Like a State* describes exactly this trade in the conversion of a field measured by what it could sustain into a field measured in hectares, which lets officials understand the outline while blinding them to local interactions (Wikipedia, "Seeing Like a State," opened 2026-11-23).

**Refuter F2:** show a published statistic that retains and circulates the per-slot residuals alongside the aggregate, and that is used to locate the next probe rather than to close the case. That breaks the claim that the statistic necessarily discards the where-to-look signal.

### F3. The producing frame scores itself with the quantity it produces

Fig 1 :24-26 names the disqualified quantities: **"coverage, and compression achieved,"** **"predicates minted, and credibility accrued."** Fig 1 :27: **"They are frame-internal. A frame cannot score itself with a quantity it also produces."**

A bureau produces the count, then reports the count as evidence that the bureau is working: cases cleared, compliance rate, categories harmonized. This is a frame scoring itself with a quantity it also produces. Formula step 5 adds the governing rule: *"SETTLE: the ground settles it. Verified only by what pays. A gate is not the ground."* Institutional acceptance is a gate. It is not settlement. Conflating the two is the output-layer failure the figures name.

This claim is `single-source` on the figure side (Fig 1), corroborated by THE_FORMULA mode/settlement rule, which is a different document but the same authority pool. World corroboration: the UN-derived quality criteria for official statistics require that the statistical system be *"free from interference that could influence decisions on the choice of sources, methods used for data collection, the selection of results to be released as official, and the timing and form of dissemination"* (Wikipedia, "Official statistics," opened 2026-11-23). The field itself recognises the self-scoring hazard; its independence principle is an institutional attempt to keep the gate from handing the verdict.

**Refuter F3:** show a bureau whose reported performance measure is produced by an independent frame that does not draw on the bureau's records, and whose verdict is separately paid by a ground the bureau cannot move. That breaks the self-scoring claim.

### F4. The official category becomes a frame posing as an anchor

Fig 2 :29: **"An anchor is whatever settles the question and does not change its answer because of what any frame thinks."**
Fig 2 :33-34: **"The anchor must not update."** **"If it does, panel 3 turns into panel 1: the anchor becomes another frame, everything drifts together, and the drift is coherent, which is why nobody notices."**
Fig 7 :13: **"agreement compounds, evidence does not"**
Fig 7 :35: **"A room's agreement is variation, not a verdict."**

Once the artifact is authoritative, downstream users treat it as the pinned reference: the official number is cited as if it did not move, the official category as if nature drew the boundary. But the artifact was authored inside the frame and updates on the frame's own reports, so it is a frame, not an anchor. Review chains then amplify agreement without adding evidence, because each reviewer ranges against the same record rather than against the world. The drift is coherent, which is why nobody notices.

Triangulated: Fig 2 gives the anchor test and panel-1 collapse; Fig 7 gives the handoff dynamics. Low overlap between the two arguments, same evidence pool. World corroboration: Foucault's governmentality literature describes the "techniques and strategies by which a society is rendered governable" and the normalisation technology in which a norm is *"socially worthy, statistically average, scientifically healthy and personally desirable"* (Wikipedia, "Governmentality," opened 2026-11-23). That is the statistic-to-norm conversion stated in the field's own vocabulary. Hacking's "transcendental nominalism" traces *"the mutual interactions over time between the phenomena of the human world and our classifications of them"* (Wikipedia, "Ian Hacking," opened 2026-11-23), which is the loop by which a category becomes a frame that acts back on what it classifies.

**Refuter F4:** demonstrate a category that is used as a reference by several frames yet is corrected separately by each frame against an unmoved ground, with logged cases where the category was refused. That breaks the frame-posing-as-anchor claim.

### F5. The cross-scale transform drops the bond, and the round trip is not measured

Fig 4 :27: **"The transform between scales loses information, and that is the point."**
Fig 4 :30: **"So take the round trip and measure the gap. Without it you are navigating by dead reckoning: confident about a position nobody checked."**
Fig 12 :5: **"A set of ingredients is not one. The bond is the rest of the information, and it is the half nothing was recording."**
Fig 12 :46: **"A bond is the typed relation between two operands. There are seven, they are not interchangeable, and which one holds is not recoverable from the operands."**

A finding is local and embedded: it carries the relations among the slots, the conditions under which each observation was made, the objections that were raised. When it is carried up into an institutional artifact, what crosses is a compressed report. The arrangement, which is the substance (Fig 12 :94), is not carried. Bringing the artifact back down to the case cannot reconstruct the dropped relations. Unless the round trip gap R_T is measured, the bureau is navigating by dead reckoning.

This is triangulated across Fig 4 (between-scale membrane) and Fig 12 (bond invisibility), again low overlap in argument, one evidence pool. Low-overlap world corroboration from information theory: lossy compression is *"the class of data compression methods that uses inexact approximations and partial data discarding to represent the content"* and suffers generation loss because *"the data already lost cannot be recovered"* (Wikipedia, "Lossy compression," opened 2026-11-23). This corroborates the mechanism shape; it does not establish anything about a particular bureau.

**Refuter F5:** show a transmission that carries the typed relations and the per-slot lineage along with the aggregate, with a measured round trip gap reported to the user. That breaks the claim that the bond is necessarily dropped.

### F6. No standing list of correct categories survives a change of residual

Fig 11 :51: **"No standing list of good actors survives a change of residual."**
Fig 10 :72: **"When the question changes, the room should change. A room that stays fixed while the question moves is the failure; the correlation is only its symptom."**
Fig 12 :117: **"isomer. Same atoms, different bond, different substance. The difference is invisible in the formula and decisive in the behaviour."**
Fig 12 :123: **"functional group. A substructure whose presence fixes the outcome. The group says what kind; the qualifier says which member."**

Official categories are often defended as permanent. The figures refuse the permanence: valence is relative to the residual, and a room that stays fixed when the question moves fails. What can be permanent is the *form* of the classification (a functional group: state, boundary, qualifier), not the binding of particular cases into it. Over a fixed atom set, two rival classification schemes can be isomers: same terms, different bond, different substance, indistinguishable in a flat list of ingredients. That is a checkable structural claim, detailed in `06_isomers_and_order.md`.

Low-overlap world corroboration from human cognition: psychological essentialism is described as a default assumption that *"things, people and events have invisible essences that make them what they are"* and that *"Observable changes in features of an entity are not salient enough to alter its essential characteristics"* (Wikipedia, "Essentialism," opened 2026-11-23). That is the cognitive mechanism by which an official category comes to be treated as immutable, which is the human-side analogue of Fig 11 :51.

**Refuter F6:** exhibit a category whose memberships were revised whenever the residual moved, with an instrument that detected the move. That breaks the standing-list claim for that category, though not the figure line itself.

### F7. The chain breaks at the objective link, where there is no instrument

Fig 3 :27: **"A link with no instrument is not a link that works. It is a link nobody has looked at."**
Fig 3 :19: **"Optimising a link that already works cannot rescue one that does not."**

Bureaux are good at perception and vocabulary: they record findings and name them. They are good at planning: they process cases. The weak link is the objective, being able to say what would count as progress in its own terms. Legibility, throughput and closure are measurable; whether those measures still track the original finding is not. So improvement effort lands above the break, on counting faster and filing more consistently, and the residual below the break is never diagnosed. Fig 8 :39: **"So a search that finds nothing is never a proof of absence."** A bureau that reports no contrary cases has not proved them absent.

**Refuter F7:** show an instrument in actual use that tests whether the bureau's objective still tracks the originating finding, with a logged occasion on which it stopped the process. That breaks the claim about where the chain breaks.

## 4. Pre-registered refuter (written before the conclusion)

Required by the discipline: a falsifier written before the run is a design instrument, not only an honesty device. The shape claim is refuted if any of the following holds:

1. **Ground-contact counterexample.** A bureau's artifact remains revisable and is actually revised when the ground pushes back, with a logged case, so the artifact never became an outcome-deciding constant (breaks F1 and F4).
2. **Per-slot counterexample.** A published statistic circulates per-slot residuals alongside the aggregate and is used to aim the next probe, not to close the case (breaks F2).
3. **Independent-score counterexample.** The bureau's performance measure is produced by a frame drawing on different evidence, and the verdict is paid by a ground the bureau cannot move (breaks F3).
4. **Refusal counterexample.** An outside checker, denied the bureau's classification interiors, can refuse a category and has done so (breaks F4 and Fig 10 :42).
5. **Instrument counterexample.** An instrument is in routine use that tests whether the objective still tracks the originating finding and has halted a process (breaks F7).
6. **Figure re-census.** A legitimate settlement path from finding to rule is derived that does not promote a convention into an outcome-deciding constant (breaks the whole shape).

If any fires, the affected finding is withdrawn, not softened. Until one is run, nothing in this folder may be labelled `grounded`.

## 5. Recommendation

1. Keep the three artifact kinds in separate loops. A fix for the statistic does not clear the rule or the category (Fig 9 :54).
2. Publish per-slot residuals with every aggregate, while the case is still live, so the where-to-look signal survives (Fig 1 :28).
3. Keep the convention checkable after it is used. If it decides an outcome, supply a separate refusal seat that can be wrong about the bureau (Fig 10 :47, :42).
4. Maintain a non-updating ground contact for the class of cases, not for the bureau's report. A registry that updates on its own outputs is panel 1 (Fig 2 :33-34).
5. Measure the round trip gap R_T where a finding is carried from a local frame to an institutional report (Fig 4 :30).
6. Do not let the rule hand the verdict. Use filters for the budget and witnesses for the verdict (Fig 9 :60).
7. Send the generator (the shape claim and its refuter), not a recording of a conclusion (Fig 4 :22).

## 7. Conclusion (mode `specified`)

Bureaucracies do not convert uncertainty into knowledge. In the figure-derived shape, they convert a live, per-slot residual into an artifact by three moves: a declared convention makes the finding statable, aggregation removes the where-to-look signal, and the convention is then promoted into an outcome-deciding constant that carries the seat's authority instead of a ground payment. The artifact is treated as an anchor while updating on the frame's own reports, so review chains compound agreement without adding evidence and the drift is coherent, hence invisible from inside. The round trip from case to report and back loses the typed relations, and the gap is not measured. The three outputs (rule, statistic, category) are a union with different bonds, so verdicts are kept separate and never averaged.

What the framework cannot do is name a particular bureau, statute, statistic, or historical episode. That is a `gap`, and unreachable is a verdict, not a silence (Fig 13 :134). What would settle the empirical version is a ground reading: logged cases in which a category was refused, per-slot data published alongside aggregates, an independent score, and an instrument that halted a process when the objective stopped tracking the finding. None of that is in this folder. This remains a simulated derivation, checked for internal consistency, not a ground reading, and it cannot settle the question it reports on.
