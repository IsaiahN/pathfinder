## QUESTION

Decide, in effect terms, what the Tether says about the transition from a contested finding to a rule, a statistic, or an official category. Option A: the artifact carries ground contact and stays revisable when the ground pushes back. Option B: the artifact is a convention promoted into an outcome-deciding constant, so the residual is masked rather than closed. What each leads to: A keeps the per-slot signal and a refusal path; B produces an authoritative artifact whose authority comes from the seat that authored it, with coherent drift that is invisible from inside. The three outputs must be kept separate, because they use different bonds.

## DOMAIN CENSUS

R by arity, symmetry and scale, then one line per domain with actors / currency / anchor, partition or union, and order by dependency.

- **R (the residual).** A finding with positive residual on at least one object slot leaves as an artifact treated as settled. Arity: three output kinds (rule, statistic, category), so a union compound, not one hard question (Fig 9 :5). Symmetry: a statistic is a symmetric aggregation over slots; a rule is ordered or a threshold; a category is an identity assertion. Scale: local epistemic frame to institutional artifact, a lossy membrane crossing (Fig 4 :27).
- **Domain 1, the epistemic frame.** Actors: researchers, instruments, observations, object slots. Currency: per-slot residuals. Anchor: the measurement that would settle the finding (Fig 2 :29). Question type: can the residual be read per slot at all?
- **Domain 2, the bureaucracy as frame.** Actors: officials, committees, registries, review chains. Currency: records, counts, classifications, compliance. Anchor: the declared convention and the institution's own prior outputs, a purported anchor that updates on the frame, so it fails the anchor test (Fig 2 :33-34). Question type: partition boundary, what may cross from finding to artifact and by what gate.
- **Domain 3, the artifact in its outcome field.** Actors: regulated parties, cases, citizens. Currency: decisions and material consequences. Anchor: the outcome in the world (a benefit paid, a case closed wrongly). Question type: does the artifact still range against this ground, or decide by its own authority?
- **Order by dependency.** Declare the category first (identity bond), aggregate the statistic second (symmetric bond), set the rule threshold third (comparison bond), refuse fourth (negation, not a bond). Refuse before mint (Formula step 2); describe the residual before naming a frame (Fig 9 :28).
- **Union, not partition.** Fig 9 :54: *"And when the split lands on a union rather than a partition, unbundle before you search again."* Verdicts are kept per domain and never averaged.

Strain test: Fig 2 :41 (*"efficient for a convention"*) and Fig 2 :40 (*"disqualifying for a verdict"*) govern different quantities, shared meaning and world claims, so no conflict after re-census. Published statistics are revised, which strains Fig 2 :33; re-census assigns revision to the channel (Fig 10 :24) and keeps the ground unmoved, so the strain is evidence for the frame-posing-as-anchor claim. Fig 4 :27 (between-scale loss) and Fig 1 :22 (within-slot averaging) are two operations at different steps.

## VERDICT (from the figures)

Bureaucracies do not convert uncertainty into knowledge. In the figure-derived shape they convert a live, per-slot residual into an artifact by promoting a declared convention into an outcome-deciding constant, after aggregation has removed the where-to-look signal and the producing frame has begun scoring itself with its own count. The artifact is treated as an anchor while updating on the frame's own reports, so review chains compound agreement without evidence and drift is coherent and invisible. Rule, statistic and category are different bonds, a union of conditions whose verdicts are kept separate and never averaged. The cross-scale transform drops the typed relations and the round trip gap is not measured. No figure line names a bureau, a statute or a case; that is a declared `gap`. This is a `specified`, ungrounded result.

- **cost if wrong:** bounded and locatable, because the claim is mode-labeled and carries five pre-registered failures and a provenance trail. The real cost is spending an outside reader's independence by sending the full framework before asking for a verdict, which Fig 2 :42 prices: *"Buy independence in proportion to the cost of being wrong."* That cost is avoidable by leading with the shape claim and its refuter.
- **undoable:** yes. Each sub-claim is separately withdrawable without touching the figures or the other two. `07_the_tether_answer.md` and `08_expert_verification_packet.md` already withhold the named-instance claim. No primitive has to be retracted.
- **reversed by:** a ground reading, not agreement. A logged case of a refused and revised category; per-slot residuals published with an aggregate and used to aim a probe; an independent score paid by a ground the bureau cannot move; an outside verifier denied the classification interiors; an instrument that halts a process when the objective stops tracking the finding; or a figure re-census that derives a legitimate settlement path without promotion. Until one of these fires, the verdict stands as `candidate`.
