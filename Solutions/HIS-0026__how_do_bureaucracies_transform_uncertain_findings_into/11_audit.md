## Independent domain census

I redid the census from the residual line alone before relying on the primary's conclusions, using the WATSON order: R by arity, symmetry and scale; one line per domain with actors / currency / anchor; partition or union; order by dependency.

- **R (arity / symmetry / scale).** The residual is the conversion of a contested finding into a settled artifact. Arity: three named outputs, rule, statistic, category, so a **union compound**, not one hard question (Fig 9 :5). Symmetry: the statistic is symmetric under swapping slots; the rule is ordered or a threshold; the category is an identity assertion. So the three cannot share a bond, and a single lever cannot clear all three. Scale: the finding is local and case-embedded, the artifact circulates at institutional scale, so a lossy membrane crossing is implicated (Fig 4 :27).
- **Domain 1, epistemic frame.** Actors: researchers, instruments, object slots. Currency: per-slot residual. Anchor: the measurement that settles the finding, nameable and not moved by a frame. Question type: whether the residual can be read at the slot.
- **Domain 2, bureaucracy as frame.** Actors: officials, committees, registries. Currency: records, counts, classifications. Anchor: the declared convention, nameable as the official record but updating on the frame, so it fails the anchor test (Fig 2 :33-34). Question type: which compositions may cross the boundary and by what gate.
- **Domain 3, outcome field.** Actors: cases, regulated parties, citizens. Currency: decisions and consequences. Anchor: the material outcome, nameable (benefit paid or not). Question type: does the artifact still range against this ground or decide by authority?
- **Order by dependency.** Declare category, aggregate statistic, set rule threshold, refuse. Each consumes what the previous produces (Fig 3 :20). Refuse before mint (Formula step 2); describe the residual before naming a frame (Fig 9 :28).
- **Union, not partition.** The three conditions overlap (a category defines which slots the statistic counts; the statistic sets the rule's threshold), so unbundle before searching (Fig 9 :54-57).

My census matches the primary's three domains and its union reading. ## Round trip gap R_T

The Symbols table defines R_T as the gap between what was sent up and what came back down, `gap(x, (T_E ∘ T_A)(x))`, measuring what the coarser description cannot hold. Fig 4 :30 requires it to be measured rather than assumed zero.

**R_T is nonzero.** Four things the shorthand holds do not survive the trip back down:

- the **identity of the case**, which the framework refuses to name and which a concrete claim would have to supply from a ground reading or provenanced import;

Conversely, what survives cleanly is the shape itself: a declared convention that decides an outcome has stopped being a convention; aggregation removes the aiming signal; a report that updates on its frame is not an anchor; the three outputs have different bonds. That is the residue worth sending. R_T was **not** measured numerically, because no ground reading exists; it is recorded qualitatively as the enumerated set of dropped constraints.
