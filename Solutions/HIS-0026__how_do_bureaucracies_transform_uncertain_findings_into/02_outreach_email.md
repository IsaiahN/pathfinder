# Outreach draft: check the shape claim before you decide about the framework

**Use:** one-to-one, or as a cover letter attached to a submission through a public venue in `03_public_contact_points.csv`. **Not** for bulk send.

**Hard precondition:** do not send until the shape claim in `01_figure_grounded_audit.md` section 3 has survived its own refuter list. The output has to be checkable or the two-stage offer collapses on contact.

**Status of the claim:** `specified`, not `grounded`. A simulated derivation checked for internal consistency against the figures, not a ground reading.

---

**Subject:** One checkable claim about how a finding becomes an official rule or number

> Hello [name],
>
> I have one specific claim I would like you to check against what you already know. I am not asking you to read a theory first, and I am not asking you to agree with me. If the claim does not survive, there is nothing else to look at.
>
> **The claim, stated as an effect (`specified`, not `grounded`):** an administrative frame meets a finding that is still contested. It declares a meaning so the finding can be recorded. That first step is legitimate: the convention makes the problem statable, it does not fix it. The failure comes at the second step, when the same convention is used to decide an outcome, such as who qualifies or which case closes. At that point the convention has stopped being a convention. It becomes an authoritative constant that nothing outside the frame can check. Along the way, an aggregate of the cases replaces the per-case record, so the signal that said where to look is gone, and the frame scores itself with the count it produced.
>
> **What I am not claiming:** not that every bureaucracy does this, not that any particular agency or statute is at fault, and not that statistics are lies. I am not naming a case, because the framework this comes from returns a shape and not a name. I am also not claiming the artifact is false. A rule can be a perfectly good convention for coordinating shared meaning. The claim is only about what happens when that convention is promoted into an outcome-deciding constant while the original ground can still push back.
>
> **What would refute it, written down before I sent this:**
> 1. A logged case where an official category was used to decide an outcome yet remained checkable and was actually revised when the ground pushed back.
> 2. A published statistic that circulates the per-case residuals alongside the aggregate and uses them to aim the next investigation rather than to close the case.
> 3. A bureau whose reported performance measure comes from a frame drawing on different evidence, with a verdict paid by a ground the bureau cannot move.
> 4. An outside checker, denied the classification scheme, who refused a category and had the refusal recorded.
> 5. An instrument in routine use that tests whether the bureau's objective still tracks the original finding and has halted a process when it stopped.
>
> If any one holds, I withdraw the corresponding part of the claim. A one-line "this fails because..." is more useful to me than agreement. If you would rather not engage, no reply is needed.
>
> **If it survives, and only then:** here are the sources. The figures are the authority, not my summary of them.
> - Verbatim figure text with line numbers: `FRAMEWORK/reference/03-FIGURES_TEXT.md` in this repository, cited as Fig N :line.
> - Canonical usage rule: https://github.com/IsaiahN/tether/blob/general-agent/figures/README.md
> - Figures and formula (SVG, branch `general-agent`): https://github.com/IsaiahN/tether/tree/general-agent/figures
>
> **If you want to use the machinery:** the formula is in the figures link. Run it on a problem you know well and can check against a ground. Write down what would refute your result *before* you run it. This matters because a loop cannot check the direction of its own question, and the published account warns that outputs can be contrived to fit the formula. A reported failure is more useful to me than a reported success.
>
> Thank you for your time,
> [name]

## Why this order

| the move | reason |
|---|---|
| Claim first, theory second | Fig 10 :52 *"Bring a characterised residual, never the source."* An outside reader who has absorbed the framework before being asked for a verdict has joined the frame being checked. |
| Do not ask for agreement | Fig 2 :35 *"Alignment is triangulation, not negotiation. A mirror cannot correct you."* |
| Pre-registered refuter written before sending | Fig 10 :59 *"an interest in the outcome is unavoidable, an interest in the reading is disqualifying."* The refuter is how the unavoidable interest is declared. |
| Mode on every claim | THE_FORMULA mode rule: general / specified / grounded, state which. |
| No case is named | Fig 9 :32 *"you get a kind of thing, never the answer itself."* The framework returns a shape, not a name. |
| No claim that everyone agreed | Fig 2 :40 *"agreement about the world is worthless when the agreeing frames share an evidence pool."* |
| Links after the verdict | Fig 10 :42 *"A verifier that can reconstruct the claim cannot verify it."* |
| Offer the formula as a method | Fig 4 :22 *"Only methods cross up. Only head starts come down."* |
| Asking for a failure rather than trust | The Recursive Self-Improvement Trap: *"Prestige can fund an action. It can give an action a budget to be tried. But it cannot settle an action. Only the ground can settle it."* |

## Legal and practical note

No personal emails appear here or in the contact CSV. Use institutional submission portals, public contact forms, and opt-in channels only. Send one-to-one. An opt-out line is included in the template by implication in "if you would rather not engage, no reply is needed"; add an explicit opt-out line if the venue requires one. GDPR and CAN-SPAM style rules apply to unsolicited outreach in many jurisdictions. For automated or AI-assisted submissions, check the venue's current policy before sending; the Academy of Management publishing guidance at https://www.aom.org/publications/journals/publishing-with-aom/ is the place to start for organization studies venues.
