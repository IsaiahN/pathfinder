# The Tether's answer: how uncertain findings become rules, statistics, and categories

**Mode: `specified` on the per-domain mechanism claims, `general` on the structural shape. Not `grounded`.** Simulated derivation checked for internal consistency against the figures, not a ground reading (`05` section 7). Verdicts are kept separate per domain and are never averaged into a single claim about "bureaucracy."

## 1. The residual it answers

A finding arrives with positive residual on some object slots. It is provisional and still answerable to a ground. It leaves as a rule, a statistic, or an official category treated as settled and capable of deciding outcomes. The effect to explain is the acquisition of settled standing without a ground payment.

This is a **union compound**, not one hard question. Fig 9 :5: *"are not one hard question but several well-formed ones. Split it rather than search."* The three outputs have different symmetries and different bonds (`06`), so one lever leaves the intersections standing.

## 2. The shape, per domain

**D1, the epistemic frame that produced the finding.** The finding is a per-slot residual (Fig 1 :19). It is answerable to a ground that does not update when a frame pushes on it (Fig 2 :29). *Verdict:* `specified`, triangulated across Fig 1 and Fig 2. The residual must be readable at the slot before anything can be legitimately settled. This domain is where the answer starts; it is not where it fails.

**D2, the bureaucracy as frame.** The bureau declares a meaning to make the finding statable. That is legitimate: *"the convention does not fix the bug,"* *"it makes the bug statable,"* *"a declared meaning is not a grounded one"* (Fig 10 :16-18). The failure is the next step: the convention is used to decide an outcome, so *"a convention that decides an outcome has stopped being one"* (Fig 10 :47) and becomes *"a constant the seat authored, carrying the seat's authority"* (Fig 10 :48). *Verdict:* `specified`, triangulated across Fig 10 (what a seat may author) and Fig 2 :43 (the guard imported from Fig 10). Low overlap in argument, one evidence pool.

**D3, the artifact in its outcome field.** The statistic aggregates slots, and *"aggregating across slots is how a live signal disappears into an average"* (Fig 1 :22), so *"the only signal that says where to look"* is gone (Fig 1 :28). The bureau then scores itself with the count it produced, which Fig 1 :27 disqualifies: *"They are frame-internal. A frame cannot score itself with a quantity it also produces."* Downstream agreement compounds without adding evidence (Fig 7 :13, :35), because the official number is treated as a pinned anchor while updating on the reports of the frame that produced it (Fig 2 :33-34). The cross-scale transform drops the typed relations (Fig 4 :27, Fig 12 :5) and the round trip gap is not measured (Fig 4 :30). *Verdict:* `specified`; the aggregation and self-scoring claims are `single-source` on the figure side (Fig 1), the anchor-collapse claim is triangulated across Fig 2 and Fig 7.

**Across domains.** The chain breaks at the objective link: the bureau can say what it is counting but not instrument whether the count still tracks the finding (Fig 3 :27). There is no standing list of correct categories, because *"No standing list of good actors survives a change of residual"* (Fig 11 :51). Over a fixed atom set, rival classification schemes can be isomers (Fig 12 :117); as the question writes them they share a functional group instead (Fig 12 :123).

## 3. What it explicitly does not say

- Not that every bureau does this. The figures give a structural failure mode, not an empirical prevalence.
- Not that any named agency, statute, statistic, or historical episode is at fault. The framework returns a shape, not a name (Fig 9 :32, Fig 13 :134). That is a declared `gap`, a verdict and not silence.
- Not that statistics are lies or that categories are useless. A convention is *"efficient for a convention: agreement about a shared meaning is the product, not a corruption of it"* (Fig 2 :41). The failure begins when it decides an outcome.
- Not that aggregation is forbidden. Fig 5 :43 licenses compression whose statement plus leftover costs less than the confusion it removes. Only pre-settlement aggregation that discards the live slot is at issue.
- Not a derivation of one output kind from another, and not a new primitive.
- Not `grounded`. Nothing here was settled by a measurement of an actual bureau.

## 4. What would settle it: the ground

A source-discriminating reading, not a further argument. *The Hard Problem is About Source*: an instrument that reads green *"cannot comment on the source."* Here:

1. **Log a refused category.** A case in which the artifact was used to decide an outcome and was then revised when the ground pushed back. That is the F1 refuter.
2. **Publish the per-slot residuals** beside the aggregate and watch whether they are used to aim the next probe rather than to close the case. That is F2.
3. **Independent score.** A performance measure produced by a frame drawing on different evidence, with a verdict paid by a ground the bureau cannot move. That is F3.
4. **Outside verifier.** An outside checker denied the classification scheme who can refuse a category. Fig 10 :42: *"A verifier that can reconstruct the claim cannot verify it."*
5. **Objective instrument.** A check that the bureau's objective still tracks the originating finding and has halted a process when it stopped. That is F7.
6. **Standing falsifiers** from `01` section 4, including a figure re-census that finds a legitimate settlement path without promotion.

## 5. The honest stop

**Unreached, not unreachable.** The six-term set is a visible guess, and declaring the primitives does not make them neutral (They're Scanning Books). The figures describe what would have to be true for the transition to be settlement rather than promotion; they do not report whether it happened in a given office. Indiscernibility is not identity. The answer stops at the limit of what the available instruments distinguish, and the lineage is recorded so the stop is checkable.

## 6. One-paragraph version for outreach

> The Tether does not say that bureaucracy is a thing that lies. It says that an administrative frame meets a finding that is still contestable, declares a meaning so it can be recorded, and then, at the moment that meaning is used to decide an outcome, stops being a convention and becomes an authoritative constant that nothing outside the frame can check. The aggregate of cases replaces the per-case record, so the signal that said where to look is gone; the frame scores itself with the count it produced; review chains compound agreement without adding evidence; and the official number is treated as an anchor even though it updates on the frame's own reports. Rule, statistic and category are three different bonds, not three names for one object, so a fix for one leaves the others standing. What the framework offers is that shape, refuters written in advance, and the instruments that would close it. It does not name an agency or a case. Nothing here is settled by a measurement; it is a simulated derivation checked against the figures, and it stops where the instruments stop.
