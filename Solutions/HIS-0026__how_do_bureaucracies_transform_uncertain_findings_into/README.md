## Residual in effect terms

A finding enters an administrative frame with positive residual on some object slots: it is contestable, provisional, and still answerable to a ground. The bureaucracy issues an artifact (a rule, a statistic, or an official category) that is treated as settled and authoritative and that decides outcomes. The effect to explain is how an unsettled claim acquires the standing of a settled one without the ground ever paying for it. Option A: the artifact genuinely carries ground contact, so it stays revisable when the ground pushes back. Option B: the artifact is a convention promoted into an outcome-deciding constant, so the residual is masked rather than closed. The derivation asks which figure lines govern the transition from finding to artifact, per output kind, and never averages the three into one story.
