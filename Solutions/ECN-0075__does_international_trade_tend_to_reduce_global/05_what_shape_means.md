## 1. A shape is a constraint, not a filler

The canonical README states the limit: the framework returns *"what a missing thing would have to look like (a shape, not a name)."* Fig 9 :32 "you get a kind of thing, never the answer itself". Fig 13 :134 "Unreachable is a verdict here, not a silence".

A **shape** is the form of an answer you can state before you can fill it. It constrains; it does not supply the empirical content. For this question the shape is:

> two slot-indexed inequality residuals, embedded in a union compound, each answered against its own ground, with no single scalar "inequality" to aggregate them into.

That is a shape. "Trade reduced the world Gini by X points between 1990 and 2010" is a measurement, not a shape, and no figure line returns it.

## 2. How the shape is obtained

*The Library of Babel* (read this session) describes narrowing by proximity: use the vocabulary already held that is closest to the thing, look for recurring new terms that overlap with it, and recurse. *"With each new term gained, you are able to narrow the space by definition and deepen your vocabulary."* The stopping condition is that *"you can cut your search short if the set of books returned is small and descriptive enough that it provides a general shape of the answer that satisfies your question."*

The essay also gives the exact move this question needs: *"Perhaps your problem was a bundle or conflation of several problems masquerading as one? You will need to search all of those problems individually to find their answers and then combine them in a satisfactory way."* That is Fig 9 :54 in search language: "And when the split lands on a union rather than a partition, unbundle before you search again."

The figures add the rule that our descriptions must be in effects, not causes. Fig 9 :30 "your words are borrowed too, so describe the effect". So the answerable form is "dispersion across slot G moves down under a trade shock", not "globalization is equality".

## 3. What licenses a name or a direction here

Two things, both outside the shape:

1. **The ground settles it.** Formula step 5 (local `THE_FORMULA.md`): *"the ground settles it. Verified only by what pays. A gate is not the ground."* For this question the ground is the data series for each slot.
2. **A provenanced import from a frame whose closure differs** (Fig 6 :23, Fig 8 :51). A trade-theory mechanism imported into the measurement frame must carry provenance, or it is just a binding that looks like a derivation. Fig 8 :51 "Convergent derivation and adopted import look identical in the contents. Only the record of where each came from separates them".

### Correction to my own first reading

I initially read "shape, not a name" as forbidding any concrete vocabulary at all here, including the words "between-country" and "within-country". **Retracted.** The README's phrase governs a *missing* primitive, one not held in the library. The two slots are already named by the question and by the measurement frame; they are not missing primitives. What the shape forbids is substituting a direction ("trade reduces inequality") for the structured claim. The narrower reading is the one carried into `01_figure_grounded_audit.md` and `07_the_tether_answer.md`. Both readings are recorded so the correction is visible.

## 4. Why the empirical answer cannot be a shape

*The Recursive Self-Improvement Trap* (read this session): *"The catch is that the output is only as good as the validator. The agent, or the tool, or the frame, or the theory, cannot validate itself. An external validator, or ground, is always needed to tether the veracity of the output to reality."* And: *"Metatheories cannot validate themselves."*

The figure set is a generator of shape. It can say that the question must be split and that each part needs a ground. It cannot say which way the Gini moved. Anyone who writes the empirical direction into the Tether answer has smuggled a world result in under a figure label, which is the misuse this section exists to prevent.

## 5. Prestige is not action

*The Recursive Self-Improvement Trap*: *"Prestige can fund an action. It can give an action a budget to be tried. But it cannot settle an action. Only the ground can settle it."*

For outreach this means: a well known economist agreeing with the shape does not make it true, and a list of agreeable recipients is a convention, not corroboration (Fig 2 :40). The legitimate ask is for a refutation of the shape in each slot, from people whose public work touches the measurement or the mechanism.

## 6. Simulation warning

Same essay: *"LLMs can quickly simulate an approximate answer/output with the formula found in the architecture post. It will often be better than the generic outputs that you will usually get otherwise too... However, the truly testable output would need to be derived from the real architecture or working the formula yourself."*

Therefore `01_figure_grounded_audit.md`, `06_isomers_and_order.md`, `07_the_tether_answer.md` and this file are **simulated derivations checked for internal consistency against the figures**. They are not tests of the empirical claim and cannot settle it. Mode is `specified` on the shape, `general` on the meta-claim, nothing `grounded`.
