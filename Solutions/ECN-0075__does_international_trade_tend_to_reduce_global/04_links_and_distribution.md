## 2. Wider literature relevant to the question

The world sources split into three groups, and their overlap is declared rather than hidden.

- **Distributional data and measurement** (Our World in Data articles, LIS, World Bank PIP, WID). These share data providers, so their agreement about the direction of global inequality is one evidence pool talking (Fig 2 :40), not three independent witnesses. They are valuable for the *shape* of the decomposition, not as corroboration of each other.
- **Trade mechanism** (Stolper-Samuelson and the criticism summarized there). This predicts that the sign inside a nation depends on factor endowment, which is why "tend to" must be a per-slot specification. It is a different frame from the distribution datasets, so it is a low-overlap witness for the conditionality claim.
- **Causal and critical review** (Ravallion 2018). This conflicts with the strong version of the question's premise. The conflict rule applies: it sends us back to the census, not to a flipped verdict. The census already separates structural shape (settled by figures) from empirical tendency (owed to data), so the conflict is recorded and the structural verdict is untouched.
- **Low-overlap corroboration for the structural claim** (Simpson's paradox, MAUP). Statistics and geography do not draw on the inequality datasets, so their agreement with Fig 1's per-slot rule is genuine triangulation across low-overlap fields. They corroborate the *form* of the answer, never its empirical content.

## 3. Distribution rules

- **Fig 4 :22** "Only methods cross up. Only head starts come down." Sending the full framework before anyone has described the residual sends a recording, not a generator. Lead with the shape claim and the refuter.
- **Fig 10 :42** "A verifier that can reconstruct the claim cannot verify it." A recipient who has absorbed the framework before being asked for a verdict has joined the frame being checked.
- **Fig 9 :27** "describe it before you go looking, or any frame you pick will seem to fit" Ask what they would expect to see in each slot before showing the literature.
- **Fig 2 :42** "Buy independence in proportion to the cost of being wrong." Ask a distributional-data person and a trade-mechanism person separately; do not let them share the frame before their stage-1 verdict.
- **Fig 2 :40** "disqualifying for a verdict: agreement about the world is worthless when the agreeing frames share an evidence pool". Never pool later agreement as corroboration.

**Recommended reply order:** (1) the shape claim, (2) the pre-registered refuter, (3) the question of what they would count as a failure in each slot, (4) only then the three local links, with the note that the figures, not this summary, are the authority.
