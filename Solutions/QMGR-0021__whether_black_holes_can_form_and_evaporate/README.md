## Restating the question as an effect

Two things are being decided, and they are not the same thing:

1. **Endpoint.** Does a formed black hole that evaporates terminate with no residual object, or does a localized late state persist? What each option leads to: complete evaporation with information destroyed gives a pure-to-mixed map and breaks unitarity; a persistent state keeps contact with the infalling information but must survive the species and entropy objections.
2. **Remnant shape.** If such a state persists, what would it be? What each option leads to: naming a specific microphysics (Planck mass extremal hole, regular black hole, loop-quantum-gravity white hole) imports a physics binding; describing it in effect terms gives a shape the figures can actually license.

An answer would look like: a verdict on the endpoint plus a characterisation of the residual, each with a mode and a refuter. That sought-for shape is bound as an ordinary term in `01` section 0 and `07`, so it can accrue a residual like anything else rather than steering the search from outside it.
