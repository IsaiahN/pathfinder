## QUESTION

Decide, in effect terms, what the Tether says about a formed black hole evaporating to an endpoint: Option A, evaporation completes with nothing left and the information destroyed; Option B, a localised late state persists. Then decide what can be said about that state. What each leads to: A gives a pure-to-mixed map and an absence conclusion the semiclassical frame cannot license; B preserves contact but must face the species and entropy objections and must not be named from a projection. The figures are asked for the shape of a permissible answer, not for a verdict on which nature realises.

## VERDICT (from the figures)

The figures do not settle whether a black hole leaves a remnant. They settle the shape of an admissible answer. Formation and evaporation are settled and are unbundled from the endpoint. "Complete evaporation with the information destroyed and nothing left" is an absence claim the semiclassical frame cannot license (triangulated), and it misidentifies the failing object: the channel fails, the ground does not decay (triangulated). A legitimate answer must preserve contact and must present its closure move as an import with provenance and a shadow test. A remnant, if any, is a shape, not a name: a localised, settled, next-level-composable endpoint composition that survives its own use, not a seventh kind and not a continuum (triangulated for the shape, `specified` for the structure). The live residual sits at the endpoint slot (single-source, not used to carry the verdict). This is `specified` and `general`, ungrounded, candidate.

- **cost if wrong:** bounded and locatable. The verdict is mode-labelled, states three refuters before the conclusion and records the lineage, so an error is findable. The real cost is presenting a structural constraint as a physical result, which would spend an outside reader's independence; Fig 2 :42 says "Buy independence in proportion to the cost of being wrong." That cost is avoidable by leading with the residual and the refuter.
- **undoable:** yes. All per-domain claims are structural constraints, not new physics; no primitive is withdrawn and no derivation is overturned if a ground reading reverses them. A sent shape claim can be superseded by the corrected packet.
- **reversed by:** a ground reading, not agreement. A source-discriminating instrument at the endpoint showing whether information is radiation-carried or remnant-retained; a successful or failed microscopic state count; a theorem establishing pure-to-mixed evolution; a derivation of unitarity inside the unexpanded semiclassical closure; a seventh primitive; or a figure re-census permitting an inside proof of absence. Prestige and expert votes are not such instruments.
