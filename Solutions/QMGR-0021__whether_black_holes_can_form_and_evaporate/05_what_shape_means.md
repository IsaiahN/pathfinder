**Source discipline.** The canonical eight-essay set is: *How Intelligence Actually Works*, *The Recursive Self-Improvement Trap*, *Decoding Tacit Knowledge*, *How a Society of Agents Would Work*, *The Library of Babel: Locating the Book of Books*, *They're Scanning Books When They Should*, *The Hard Problem is About Source*, and *Arrival of the Fittest: The Tether*. ## 1. A shape is a constraint, not a filler

The canonical rule from `FRAMEWORK/reference/FIGURES_README_canonical.md` (read this session): the framework returns *"what a missing thing would have to look like (a shape, not a name)"*. Fig 9 :32: *"you get a kind of thing, never the answer itself"*. Fig 13 :134: *"Unreachable is a verdict here, not a silence"*, and Fig 13 :135: *"It cannot say which primitive is missing, because naming it would mean already holding it."*

From *The Library of Babel* (2026-10-02): *"You can cut your search short if the set of books returned is small and descriptive enough that it provides a general shape of the answer that satisfies your question."*

So a **shape** is the narrowed form of an answer you can state before you can name it. It constrains; it does not fill. For this question:

- **Shape:** a localised, settled endpoint state that keeps contact with the infalling information and can be composed over at the next level.
- **Name:** a Planck-mass extremal hole, a regular black hole with two horizons, a loop-quantum-gravity white hole, or a specific microstate count.

The first is licensed; the second is a binding from a physics frame and must be provenanced as such.

## 2. How a shape is obtained

- *The Library of Babel*: use the vocabulary already held that is closest to the thing, then bootstrap. *"With each new term gained, you are able to narrow the space by definition and deepen your vocabulary."*
- Fig 6 :30: *"The description travels. Fails by being sharpened into terms only we can read. Cast it in effect terms."* Fig 9 :30-31: *"your words are borrowed too, so describe the effect rather than the cause."* So describe the residual as an endpoint-contact failure, not as a particular object.
- Fig 9 :27-28: *"describe it before you go looking, or any frame you pick will seem to fit."* This is why the domain census in `01` comes before the choice of holography versus remnant models.
- *The Library of Babel* on stopping: *"You will know that you are done because the finish line will also be the starting place, as there is nowhere else to search and nothing else that can describe the answer better."* For this question we are not done: the endpoint has no nameable anchor (D3 in `01`).

## 3. What licenses a name

Only two things, both outside the shape itself:

1. **The ground settles it.** `THE_FORMULA.md` step 5: *"the ground settles it. Verified only by what pays. A gate is not the ground."* The canonical README: the framework *"stops at the limit of what the available instruments can distinguish, and records the lineage so the stop is honest."* For the endpoint, that instrument does not yet exist at a physical black hole.
2. **A provenanced import from a frame whose closure differs** (Fig 6 :23, Fig 8 :50-52), with a source gate and a shadow test. An island prescription imported from holography, or a remnant state imported from a quantum-gravity model, is admissible only with its origin and the gap it explains recorded.

The world sources make the point for us without being authority: Ong's review says remnants *"remain a viable possibility"*; Raju's review says the exterior retains a complete copy of the interior information. Two frames, different closures. The figures do not adjudicate between them; they tell us what an admissible answer from either must look like.

## 4. The metamerism error, applied to the endpoint

*The Hard Problem is About Source* (2026-08-28) gives the mechanism. An instrument reads green and *"cannot comment on the source"*: one green light, or yellow plus blue, or RGB, or CMYK, or a scheme not yet discovered. *"If several paths create the same behavior, then behavior is a projection with lossy information."* The conclusion: *"An instrument that can't distinguish these cases demonstrates its own inadequacy, it cannot claim anything substantial about what is being measured or what causes it."* It was the spectrometer, not an argument, that resolved the case.

Applied here: a semiclassical evaporation history is a projection that cannot distinguish "information escaped in subtle correlations" from "information persists in a remnant" from "information was destroyed". Naming one of these from the projection alone is the metamerism error. What is owed is a discriminator, not a name. This is the concrete reason F4 withholds the remnant's identity and F1 withholds the absence verdict.

## 5. Q: what licenses a particular remnant state? ANSWERED, no further question needed

**Nothing in the framework.** The framework returns a shape. A particular endpoint state is licensed only by the ground settling a minted term or by a provenanced import with a shadow test. Until one of those, the name is the searcher's own contribution and must be recorded as such.

**Does naming a remnant commit the forbidden error?** Yes if it is presented as a figure derivation. The canonical rule governs a primitive not held in Γ; the endpoint state is not held. Naming it as the framework's answer turns a visible choice (which physics frame you borrowed from) into an invisible one. The correct form stays: a settled, contact-preserving, next-level-composable endpoint state, with the slot marked open.

## 6. Two consequences for outreach

**Prestige is not action.** From *The Recursive Self-Improvement Trap* (2026-11-19): *"Prestige must never be allowed to validate an action directly... Prestige can fund an action. It can give an action a budget to be tried. But it cannot settle an action. Only the ground can settle it. The Ground Maintainer must be blind to prestige."* A campaign that gathers famous names to agree about remnants is the named collapse. The legitimate ask gives the shape claim a budget to be tried and lets the ground settle it.

**This audit is a simulation, not a ground reading.** Same essay: *"LLMs can quickly simulate an approximate answer/output with the formula found in the architecture post"* and *"the output is only as good as the validator. The agent, or the tool, or the frame, or the theory, cannot validate itself."* So `01` is a simulated derivation, checked for internal consistency against the figures. It is **not** a test of the endpoint question and must be labelled as such. It cannot settle anything it reports on.
