# Expert verification packet: the Tether on evaporation endpoints and remnants

**What this is:** a claim in a form a physicist or philosopher of physics can check. Mode, derivation and refuters are stated before the conclusion, so a reader knows what to look for and what would settle it.

**Mode: `specified` on the per-domain verdicts, `general` on the structural shapes, not `grounded`.** No Planck-scale measurement is claimed. This is a shape and a route, not a result.

## 1. The claim, in one paragraph

The endpoint of Hawking evaporation is the live residual; formation and evaporation are settled and are unbundled from it. "The hole evaporates completely and the information is destroyed, leaving nothing" is a claim of absence that the semiclassical frame cannot establish, and it misidentifies the failing thing: the channel fails at the endpoint, not the ground. A legitimate answer, remnant or not, must preserve contact and must treat the endpoint as a union compound with its own ground. On "what a remnant would be", the framework gives a shape and withholds the name: a localised, settled endpoint composition that survives its own use and can be composed over at the next level, not a seventh kind and not a smooth continuum. Specific microphysics is an import and must be provenanced as such.

## 2. Three checkable sub-claims

### C1 - framework-internal; checkable by reading Fig 8 and Fig 9

- **Statement:** a conclusion of "nothing remains, the information is gone" drawn from within the semiclassical frame is an absence claim that the frame cannot license.
- **Source, verbatim:** Fig 8 :39 - *"So a search that finds nothing is never a proof of absence."* Fig 9 :44 - *"Only a sealed room can be searched to the end, and no frame is a sealed room."*
- **Refuter:** exhibit a ground reading that shows the carrier is absent in principle, not merely unfound, or a figure re-census admitting that a frame can prove its own closure's absence claim.

### C2 - framework-internal; checkable by reading Fig 10 and Fig 11

- **Statement:** the object that changes at the endpoint is the channel; the ground does not decay. A pure-to-mixed story is a category error about which thing changes.
- **Source, verbatim:** Fig 10 :24 - *"the ground does not decay, the channel does"*; Fig 11 :40 - *"An improvement that does not change contact changes nothing, however much it improves."* Fig 2 :29 defines the relevant ground: *"An anchor is whatever settles the question and does not change its answer because of what any frame thinks."*
- **Refuter:** a verified pure-to-mixed evolution at the endpoint, or a figure re-census showing the ground itself can decay.

### C3 - structure, physics-facing; checkable against Fig 12 and against the candidate models

- **Statement:** a remnant, if any, is a settled endpoint composition, a catalyst that survives its own use, composable at the next level, and not a new primitive or a continuum. The radiation route and the remnant route are isomers over the shared six atom set if neither needs a private atom.
- **Source, verbatim:** Fig 9 :32 - *"you get a kind of thing, never the answer itself"*; Fig 12 :119 - *"catalyst. A part that survives its own use. Spent and regenerating parts behave differently across uses, and nothing records which a term is."*; Fig 12 :117 - *"isomer. Same atoms, different bond, different substance. The difference is invisible in the formula and decisive in the behaviour."*; Fig 13 :43-44 - *"A continuum was the seventh and was removed for failing exactly this: it is a spectrum without gaps, or smoothness over space and time. A generating set must not contain what its own members produce."*
- **What to check:** is a stable Planck-scale remnant really a part that survives its own use, rather than a spent or regenerating configuration? Can both candidate resolutions be written over the same six atoms, or does one smuggle in a private information-bearing primitive? If the latter, the isomer claim demotes to a functional group and the shape claim stands.
- **Refuter:** exhibit a candidate endpoint state that is a genuine seventh primitive, or one that cannot be written as a composition of the held set, or a ground reading showing the late state is spent rather than surviving.

## 3. What is explicitly not claimed

- Not a verdict for or against remnants. The figures decide answer shapes, not which nature realises.
- Not a derivation of unitarity and not a refutation of Hawking's original calculation.
- Not "the metric is the Gradient", not any GR/QM binding, and no new primitive.
- Not a name for the remnant. Planck-mass extremal holes, regular black holes and loop-quantum-gravity white holes are external bindings, provenance owed, and carry zero figure weight until shown.
- Not a theory of everything. No terminal room.
- Not `grounded`. Nothing here is asserted as settled by experiment.

## 5. What would settle it

A source-discriminating instrument, not a further argument. From *The Hard Problem is About Source*: a reading *"cannot comment on the source"*, and *"An instrument that can't distinguish these cases demonstrates its own inadequacy."* Here:

1. An instrument that distinguishes information carried in late radiation from information retained in a localised state.
2. A microscopic state count that succeeds or fails for a candidate remnant.
3. A theorem establishing pure-to-mixed evolution at the endpoint, or ruling it out under weak assumptions.

## 6. What an expert can check in twenty minutes

1. Read Fig 8 :39, Fig 9 :44, Fig 10 :24, Fig 9 :32 and Fig 12 :119 - five short passages.
2. Check C3 against their own knowledge of remnant models: is a stable remnant a part that survives its own use, or a spent configuration the notation cannot distinguish?
3. Name one instrument that would discriminate radiation-carried from remnant-retained information at the endpoint, or say why none is possible.
4. Send the refutation. A failure is more useful than an endorsement.
