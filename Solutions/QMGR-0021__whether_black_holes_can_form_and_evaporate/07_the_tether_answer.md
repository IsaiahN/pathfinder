# The Tether's answer on black hole evaporation and remnants

**Mode: `specified` on the per-domain verdicts, `general` on the structural shapes. Not `grounded`.** This is a shape and a route, not a result. Simulated derivation checked against the figures (`05_what_shape_means.md` section 6), not a ground reading.

## 1. The residual it answers

The endpoint of Hawking evaporation. Formation and evaporation are settled; the open gap is whether the process terminates with no residual object and, if not, what the residual would be. Fig 9 :54 *"when the split lands on a union rather than a partition, unbundle before you search again"*: this is a union compound with its own loop and its own ground, not one hard question.

## 2. The shape, by domain

**D1, formation and evaporation.** Not a residual. The figures have nothing to add to settled semiclassical physics here, and they do not overwrite it. Status: settled by physics, figure-silent.

**D2, the endpoint.** Two figure verdicts, kept separate and never averaged:

1. **"Nothing left, information destroyed" is an absence claim and cannot be established from inside the frame.** Fig 8 :39 *"So a search that finds nothing is never a proof of absence."* Fig 9 :44 *"Only a sealed room can be searched to the end, and no frame is a sealed room."* Triangulated, `specified`.
2. **The channel fails at the endpoint; the ground does not decay.** Fig 10 :24 *"the ground does not decay, the channel does"*, with Fig 11 :40 *"An improvement that does not change contact changes nothing, however much it improves."* Triangulated, `specified`, ungrounded.

**D3, the remnant shape.** Fig 9 :32 *"you get a kind of thing, never the answer itself"*; Fig 13 :134 *"Unreachable is a verdict here, not a silence"* and :135 *"It cannot say which primitive is missing, because naming it would mean already holding it."* The figures can say a remnant would be a localised, settled endpoint composition that preserves contact and can be composed over at the next level. Fig 12 :119 *"catalyst. A part that survives its own use"* names the structural analogue. Fig 12 :103-104 makes its irreducibility level-relative. They cannot name its microphysics, and Fig 13 :43-44 forbids treating it as a smooth continuum seventh member. General, triangulated.

**D4, verification.** The anchor is a measurement, a state count or a theorem, never agreement. Fig 2 :29 on anchors, Fig 2 :35 *"Alignment is triangulation, not negotiation. A mirror cannot correct you."*, Fig 7 :13 *"agreement compounds, evidence does not"*. General, triangulated.

**Order.** Evaporation is a first-order process over a settled black hole; the endpoint is a second-order composition (Fig 12 :104), and both candidate resolutions are imports owing a source gate and a shadow test (Fig 6 :21-24, Fig 8 :32). They are isomers over the shared atom set if neither needs a private atom (`06_isomers_and_order.md` Q2), with a falsifier attached.

## 3. What it explicitly does not say

- Not a verdict on whether nature leaves a remnant. The figures decide which answer shapes are admissible, not which one is realised.
- Not a derivation of unitarity, not a refutation of remnants, and not a new primitive.
- Not "information is preserved", full stop. The claim is that the *ground does not decay*, which is a structural constraint on how the question may be posed, not a measurement of the radiation entropy.
- Not a name for the remnant. Planck-mass extremal holes, regular black holes and loop-quantum-gravity white holes are bindings from physics frames, provenance owed, and carry zero figure weight until shown.
- Not `grounded`. No Planck-scale measurement settles `left(R,φ)`.

## 4. What would settle it: the ground

A source-discriminating instrument, not a further argument. *The Hard Problem is About Source*: a reading cannot comment on its source, and *"An instrument that can't distinguish these cases demonstrates its own inadequacy"*; the spectrometer, not an argument, resolved the colour case. Here:

- Does the late radiation carry the information in subtle correlations, or does a localised state retain it? An instrument that discriminates these at the endpoint would name the slot.
- A microscopic state count that succeeds or fails for a candidate remnant.
- A theorem establishing pure-to-mixed evolution at the endpoint, or ruling it out under weak assumptions.
- Standing falsifiers from `01` section 4: a ground reading showing absence in principle; a derivation of unitarity inside the unexpanded semiclassical closure; a seventh primitive; a figure re-census permitting an inside proof of absence.

## 6. One-paragraph version for outreach

> The Tether does not decide whether black holes leave a remnant, and it does not pick a favourite. It unbundles the question: formation and evaporation are settled, the endpoint is the live slot. It says that "evaporated with nothing left and the information destroyed" is an absence claim that the semiclassical frame cannot prove, because a horizon is a sealed room and a search that finds nothing is never a proof of absence. It says that what fails at the endpoint is the channel, not the ground, so the information-loss story is a category error about which thing changes. On "what a remnant would be" it gives a shape, not a name: a localised, settled endpoint composition that keeps contact and can be composed over at the next level, a catalyst that survives its own use, not a seventh kind and not a smooth continuum. The specific microphysics is withheld pending provenance. What it offers is the shape any admissible answer must have, three refuters written in advance, and the instruments that would close it. The endpoint stays open until the ground settles it.
