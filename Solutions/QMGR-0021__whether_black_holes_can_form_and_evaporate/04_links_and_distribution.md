## Wider literature that bears on the endpoint question

The sources split cleanly into two camps, which is exactly why the figures refuse to pick one for us and why consensus cannot be an anchor here.

1. **Remnant-viable camp.** Ong's 2024 review argues that objections to remnants are not decisive and that *"black hole remnants remain a viable possibility."* Chen, Ong and Yeom's 2015 review concludes a remnant is a possible end state and usefully supplies a working definition: a remnant is a localised late stage that is either absolutely stable or long-lived. Both are broad reviews, aimed at students and newcomers. They do not claim a measurement.
2. **No-remnant / unitarity-in-radiation camp.** Raju's review states the exterior retains a complete copy of the interior information; Almheiri et al. show a controlled path-integral mechanism (replica wormholes) for the Page curve in models. Note the honest limit in their abstract: the fix is shown in JT gravity coupled to matter, a model, not at a physical astrophysical endpoint. The SEP entry lists both camps as live responses and explicitly notes the philosophical field is *"wide open"*.

For the Tether derivation these are **secondary corroboration only.** They corroborate the figure-derived claim that the endpoint is unresolved and that absence cannot be proved, and they conflict with any attempt to read the figures as settling the physics. A conflict would send us back to the domain census, never flip the figure verdict.

- **Fig 4 :22-25** - *"Only methods cross up. Only head starts come down. A recording carried upward looks like knowledge and is a description of one occasion."* Sending the full framework before anyone has described the residual sends a recording, not a generator. - **Fig 10 :42** - *"A verifier that can reconstruct the claim cannot verify it."* A recipient who has absorbed the framework before being asked for a verdict has joined the frame being checked.
- **Fig 9 :27-28** - *"describe it before you go looking, or any frame you pick will seem to fit."* Ask what they would expect to see if the shape claim were true before giving them the article.
- **Fig 7 :13** - *"agreement compounds, evidence does not"* and Fig 2 :40 on shared evidence pools. A send selected for people who already agree produces a convention, not corroboration.

**Order in a reply:** (1) the endpoint residual and the shape claim; (2) ask what they would count as a failure; (3) record that verdict; (4) only then the three links, with the note that the figures, not the summary, are the authority; (5) offer the formula as a reusable method with the instruction to write its own refuter.
