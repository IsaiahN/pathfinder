## What is deliberately not here

- No personal emails of named individuals.
- No ranking of people by how likely they are to agree that tax systems shape these outcomes.
- No bulk-send plan.

Three reasons, in the figures' own words:

1. **A room does not upgrade the label.** A room reaching a conclusion without the ground is agreement, and agreement among frames is a weighted average of influence. Adding people raises the vocabulary and does not add an anchor (Seat Map, cited in the exemplar). Fig 2 :35: *"Alignment is triangulation, not negotiation. A mirror cannot correct you."*
2. **Correlation disqualifies a verdict.** Fig 2 :40: *"disqualifying for a verdict: agreement about the world is worthless when the agreeing frames share an evidence pool"*. Selecting recipients for likely agreement manufactures exactly the correlation that makes their agreement worthless.
3. **Asking for trust before checking spends independence.** Fig 10 :42: *"A verifier that can reconstruct the claim cannot verify it."* The first contact carries the residual and its refuter, never the framework interiors.

Legally: scraping personal emails for unsolicited bulk outreach breaches GDPR and CAN-SPAM style rules in many jurisdictions. Public institutional and opt-in channels avoid that failure as well as the structural one.

## Honest status of the whole folder

Every substantive file states that this is a **simulated derivation checked for internal consistency against the figures**, not a ground reading. It cannot settle the question it reports on. Which tax design actually improves all four slots, and whether any single design does, remains a matter for measurement: revenue data, compliance audits, distributional statistics, and the judgments of the people living under the system.
