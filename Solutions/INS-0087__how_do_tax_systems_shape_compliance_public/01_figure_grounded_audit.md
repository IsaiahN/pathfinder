## 0. The claim under audit

The question, in effect terms. A tax system is a designed library Γ of rules, rates, bases, records and enforcement instruments acting on a population. Let

`R_s = | Γ(b,a) − o′ |` for slot `s`, with `s` in {compliance, public capacity, inequality, perceptions of fairness}.

`R` is the gap between what the design predicts or expects and what is actually observed in that slot. The question asks how the design shapes those four gaps.

What an answer would look like, bound as an ordinary term so it can accrue a residual (formula step "Direction"): a **four-slot shape** is a claim that constrains all four residual gaps without asserting which named design fills them. The sought-for shape is written down here before the run so it is refusable by the same test as everything else.

The audit does **not** test "which tax system is best." That is a name, not a shape, and it is recorded as a `gap` in F9.

## 1. Domain census (before any figure line is chosen)

**R by arity, symmetry, scale.** Arity: a relation among a designed Γ, a population of taxpayers, an administration, and an observed four-vector; four indexed slots, not one scalar. Symmetry: mixed. The relation between a citizen's own burden and a reference group is a comparison (`⋛`, *"a threshold relation between two quantities"*, Fig 12 :87), not a symmetric join. The relation between contribution and benefit is ordered. Scale: individual return, household, firm, administration, national distribution, intergenerational, with a lossy membrane between each (Fig 4).

| domain | actors | currency | anchor | status |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| D1 Compliance loop (micro) | taxpayers, audits, penalties, revenue authority | perceived payoff, deterrence, tax morale | actual payment, a counter (Fig 2 :29) | admitted |
| D2 Fiscal capacity (state) | revenue authority, tax base, records, third parties | revenue collected, reach of the state | revenue actually collected; information-gathering instruments | admitted |
| D3 Distribution (macro) | households, income groups, incidence | income shares, effective rates | measured distributional data | admitted |
| D4 Fairness perceptions (cognitive/social) | citizens, norms, procedural experience, trust | perceived fairness, legitimacy | human judgment, legitimately (Fig 2 :37) | admitted |
| D5 The designed system as Γ | rules, bases, rates, exemptions, instruments | description length, reach | closure(Γ) and the four anchors above | admitted |
| X1 "the optimal tax system" as a single name | none nameable | none | no nameable anchor | not a domain yet |

**Union, not partition.** The four conditions overlap. A lever that clears the region where only compliance binds leaves the intersections standing (Fig 9 :55, :57). This is a union compound, so each condition keeps its own loop and its own anchor. It is not one hard question and the verdicts are never averaged.

**Order by dependency (Fig 3).** Perception (a citizen perceives the system and their own stake) → vocabulary (terms for obligation, fairness, public return) → objective (what counts as compliance or fairness) → planning (how to respond) → learn and carry (across years). A link below a break cannot be diagnosed; only its predecessor can (Fig 3 :22-23).

**Strain test applied before conclusions.** An early reading tried to make Fig 11 :39-40 ("capability is a property of agent-and-habitat") govern D1 alone. It strained, because D1's anchor is a payment counter while D2's anchor is revenue. Re-census: Fig 11 governs capability across D1 and D2 jointly; the anchors differ, which is exactly why the union must not be averaged. No strain remains after re-census.

## 3. Findings F1 to F10

### F1  -  Four slots, four anchors, never averaged (structural, decisive)

Fig 1 :19: *"The residual is indexed by object slot, not aggregated."*
Fig 1 :22: *"Aggregating across slots is how a live signal disappears into an average."*
Fig 1 :27: *"They are frame-internal. A frame cannot score itself with a quantity it also produces."*

A single index of "tax system quality" would let compliance, revenue, distribution and fairness trade off against one another numerically. That is how a live signal disappears into an average. Each slot needs its own anchor: actual payment (D1), revenue collected and information instruments (D2), distributional data (D3), and human judgment (D4). Fig 2 :29 names the anchor as *"A measurement, an experiment, a counter, a market, a patient outcome."* For fairness the question is constitutively about people, so Fig 2 :37 applies: *"Human judgment is the right anchor for is this a good answer for a person, because the question is about people."*

**Mode:** `general` for the per-slot rule, `specified` for the four-slot census. **Status:** triangulated (Fig 1 and Fig 2, low overlap between the agent figure and the ground figure). **Refuter:** a composite indicator that predicts all four slots with no live residual left in any one of them.

### F2  -  This is a union, not one question (decisive)

Fig 9 :5: *"are not one hard question but several well-formed ones. Split it rather than search."*
Fig 9 :55: *"A bundled problem is usually a union of overlapping conditions, not a list of separate ones."*
Fig 9 :57: *"What remains is smaller and it is also categorically different: a case in two conditions is not a harder case of either."*

Deterrence research works well on the compliance subset; procedural-fairness research works well on the perception subset; public-finance research works on the revenue subset. Each rule works on a different subset, which is Fig 9's diagnostic for several well-formed questions. A single lever (raise the penalty, widen the base, flatten the rate) clears the region where only its own condition holds and leaves the intersections standing.

The framework essay *The Library of Babel* (Isaiah N, 2026-10-02) says the same in search terms: *"Perhaps your problem was a bundle or conflation of several problems masquerading as one? You will need to search all of those problems individually to find their answers and then combine them in a satisfactory way."* This is an essay, not a figure line; it corroborates but does not decide.

**Mode:** `general` for the union rule, `specified` for its application here. **Status:** triangulated (Fig 9 plus the essay, different vocabularies). **Refuter:** a single intervention that improves all four slots with no categorically different residue left in any intersection.

### F3  -  Capability is agent-and-habitat, never agent alone

Fig 11 :39: *"So the answerable question is not what intelligence is, but what makes a system capable. And capability is a property of agent-and-habitat, never of the agent alone."*
Fig 11 :40: *"A system cannot be made more capable by improving the agent in isolation. An improvement that does not change contact changes nothing, however much it improves."*

Compliance and collection capacity cannot be located in the taxpayer's "morale" alone, nor in the authority's enforcement alone. They live in the contact between design and habitat: who reports third-party income, whether records exist, whether a citizen sees a return for payment. Improving the audit manual without changing contact changes nothing, however much it improves.

World corroboration (secondary): Besley and Persson (2014, *Journal of Economic Perspectives*) report that *"weak institutions, fragmented polities, and a lack of transparency due to weak news media"* and *"a weak sense of national identity and a poor norm for compliance"* shape collection. These are habitat conditions, not properties of an isolated taxpayer. One evidence pool, weak independent weight.

**Mode:** `specified`. **Status:** triangulated (Fig 11 plus Fig 2 anchor rule; the world source is one pool). **Refuter:** sustained change in compliance or collection with documented unchanged contact between design and habitat.

### F4  -  Instrument valence is relative to the residual

Fig 11 :49: *"An actor’s valence is relative to the residual, not a property it carries."*
Fig 11 :51: *"No standing list of good actors survives a change of residual."*

An audit, a penalty, an amnesty, a withholding rule: none is good or bad in itself. The same audit is a benefactor to the compliance residual and a malfactor to the fairness-perception residual if it is experienced as arbitrary. A standing list of "best practices" in tax administration survives only if the residual never changes, which it does. This is the mechanism behind the union in F2, at the level of instruments.

World corroboration (secondary, low overlap): Fehr and Gächter (2002, *Nature*) find that *"cooperation flourishes if altruistic punishment is possible, and breaks down if it is ruled out."* Sanctioning helps cooperation in one habitat; it is not a standing good, and its fairness valence is a separate question.

**Mode:** `specified`. **Status:** triangulated (Fig 11 plus a low-overlap experimental source). **Refuter:** a lever that keeps the same sign for compliance and fairness across a documented change of residual.

### F5  -  Public capacity is reach; more search inside the same frame adds no atom

Fig 6 :23: *"No frame escapes its own closure. The bound moves only on contact with a frame whose closure differs."*
Fig 8 :32: *"Closure is idempotent. Composing inside a closed set never leaves it, so no search, however long, adds an atom. This one is definitional: it is what closure means."*
Fig 6 :49: *"more search at the same reach: is it in here and unfound? The closure does not change. Same atoms, same wall, more of the space actually visited."*
Fig 13 :115: *"The set is closed. The arrangements are not. That gap is why import exists."*

A revenue authority searching harder within the same taxpayer population, the same forms and the same legal base is doing SEARCHED, not OUTWARD. If the atom is not in the closure, the remedy is an import: a new record, a third-party reporter, a cadastral register, a different jurisdiction's data. Formula step 7 asks SEARCHED first, because it is cheapest, then INWARD (sharpen an instrument already returning something), then OUTWARD (import). These are three questions and must not be collapsed.

Underivable-in-principle and unreachable-at-this-budget are different facts with different instructions (Fig 8 :40-43); importing against a budget limit adds an atom that was not needed.

World corroboration (secondary): the *State capacity* reference (Wikipedia, read 2026-11-23) notes that *"State capacity may involve an expansion of the state's information-gathering abilities. In processes of state-building, states began implementing a regular and reliable census, the regular release of statistical yearbooks, and civil and population registers"*. Those are imports of new atoms, not more search at the same reach.

**Mode:** `general` for idempotence, `specified` for the fiscal application. **Status:** triangulated (Fig 6, Fig 8, Fig 13, plus a political-science source). **Refuter:** a capacity gain achieved by pure internal search with no new instrument, record or counterparty entering the authority's closure.

### F6  -  Distribution is a per-slot composition; incidence arrangements are isomers

Fig 1 :19 applies to households and groups, not to a national average: the distributional residual is indexed by slot. Fig 12 :117: *"isomer. Same atoms, different bond, different substance. The difference is invisible in the formula and decisive in the behaviour."*
Fig 12 :99: *"A bond costs what it could have been, and nothing more."*
Fig 9 :60: *"Use filters for the budget and witnesses for the verdict. Never let a filter hand you a verdict."*

Two tax systems with the same atoms (a rate, a base, a threshold, an exemption, an enforcement instrument) but different incidence arrangements are isomers, not variants of one thing. The difference is invisible in the statutory schedule and decisive in behavior. A Gini coefficient is a filter for the budget, not a witness for the verdict; it compresses the per-slot signal the question asks about.

World corroboration (secondary, same public-finance pool as F3): the *Tax incidence* reference (Wikipedia, read 2026-11-23) states that *"The key concept of tax incidence is the finding that the tax burden does not depend on where the revenue is collected, but on the price elasticity of demand and price elasticity of supply."* Nominal and economic incidence differ: same atoms, different arrangement, different substance.

**Mode:** `specified`. **Status:** triangulated for the isomer definition (Fig 12 and the incidence source), single-source for the figure definition itself. **Refuter:** two systems with identical incidence arrangements and identical atoms producing different per-slot distributions with no change in any instrument.

### F7  -  Fairness is anchored in human judgment; trust is a channel that decays

Fig 2 :37 (quoted in F1) makes human judgment the legitimate anchor because the question is about people. Fig 2 :35: *"Alignment is triangulation, not negotiation. A mirror cannot correct you."*
Fig 10 :24: *"the ground does not decay, the channel does"*
Fig 10 :26: *"stale percepts, dropped transients, saturated metrics, pooled readings"*
Fig 10 :49: *"Declaring a meaning does not ground it."*

Perceived fairness is a maintained channel, not a declaration. A system can declare itself fair without grounding the claim; the declaration is a convention that has decided an outcome, which Fig 10 forbids. Surveying citizens and then telling them the survey says they are fairly treated is negotiation with a mirror, not triangulation. Trust decays through pooled readings: an aggregated satisfaction score can hide the very slot that is failing.

World corroboration (secondary, low overlap): the *Procedural justice* reference (Wikipedia, read 2026-11-23, citing Tyler and Leventhal) reports that *"research by Tom R. Tyler and colleagues found that giving disgruntled group members a voice regardless of whether it is instrumental ... or non-instrumental ... is sometimes enough for a process to be viewed as fair"*, and lists Leventhal's rules as *"consistency, bias suppression, accuracy, correctability, representativeness, and ethicality"*. Voice and correctability are contact conditions, consistent with a channel that must be maintained.

**Mode:** `specified`. **Status:** triangulated (Fig 2 plus Fig 10 plus a social-psychology source). **Refuter:** a fairness judgment shown to be movable by authoritative declaration alone with no change in experienced contact or procedure.

### F8  -  Dependency order: the middle link usually has no instrument

Fig 3 :20: *"The chain is ordered by dependency, not by importance."*
Fig 3 :27: *"A link with no instrument is not a link that works. It is a link nobody has looked at."*

In tax systems the middle link (having terms for what a tax base affords and being able to say what counts as progress or fairness) is the least instrumented. There are plenty of revenue statistics (perception and outcome) and plenty of compliance audits (planning and outcome), but far fewer instruments for "what does this base afford" and "what would count as fair here." Fig 3 :26 says this link attracts the least attention and the fewest instruments. Effort spent above the break arrives at a step that cannot use it.

**Mode:** `specified`. **Status:** single-source (Fig 3), with the framework essays as corroboration. **Refuter:** a documented instrument that returns a reading on "what this base affords" and on "what counts as fair," where none existed before, with no change above the break.

### F9  -  The name of the best tax system is a `gap`

Fig 9 :32: *"you get a kind of thing, never the answer itself"*
Fig 13 :134: *"Unreachable is a verdict here, not a silence,"*
Fig 8 :39: *"So a search that finds nothing is never a proof of absence."*

After a genuine re-census, no figure line governs the choice of a named optimal design across all four slots. The figures constrain the shape (per-slot, union, capability through contact, reach through import, isomers, human anchor); they do not name the design. X1 in the census has no nameable anchor and is therefore not a domain yet. This is `gap`, recorded plainly, not hidden and not filled with a name.

**Mode:** `general` for the limit, `specified` for its application here. **Status:** single-source (Fig 9 and Fig 13 agree; Fig 8 adds the search caveat). **Refuter:** a figure line that directly governs the four-slot question and yields a single named design with a stated ground settlement.

### F10  -  Outreach design is governed by the same figures

Fig 2 :40 (quoted in README): agreement with a shared evidence pool is disqualifying for a verdict. Fig 2 :42: *"Buy independence in proportion to the cost of being wrong."*
Fig 10 :42: *"A verifier that can reconstruct the claim cannot verify it."*

A list selected for receptiveness to a claim about tax and fairness manufactures the disqualifying correlation. The first contact therefore carries the four-slot residual and its refuter, never the framework. A reply is a correction on shape and a named failure class, not corroboration. See `02_outreach_email.md` and `04_links_and_distribution.md`.

**Mode:** `general`. **Status:** triangulated (Fig 2, Fig 7, Fig 10). **Refuter:** a method of selecting checkers by likely agreement that still yields an independent verdict. None is known here.

## 4. Pre-registered refuter

Written before the conclusion, per the framework rule that a falsifier written before the run is a design instrument. The four-slot shape claim is refuted if any of the following holds:

1. A single composite indicator predicts all four slots with no live residual left in any slot (breaks Fig 1 :19, :22).
2. One lever improves compliance, capacity, equality and fairness simultaneously with no valence reversal across a documented change of residual (breaks Fig 11 :49, :51 and Fig 9 :55).
3. A capacity gain is achieved by pure internal search with no new instrument, record or counterparty entering the authority's closure (breaks Fig 6 :23 and Fig 8 :32).
4. Fairness judgments are shown to be movable by declaration alone with no change in experienced contact (breaks Fig 10 :24, :49).
5. A figure line is found that governs the full four-slot question and yields one named design with a ground settlement (replaces F9's `gap`).

If any fires, the shape is withdrawn or revised. Until one is run, nothing here is labelled `grounded`.

## 5. Recommendation

1. Never report a single "impact of tax systems" number. Report four slot residuals with four anchors (F1).
2. Split the question into its four loops before searching; keep the intersections as compounds (F2).
3. State capability as design-and-habitat, and record what contact changed (F3).
4. Price every instrument in two currencies, its effect on compliance and its effect on fairness; no standing good list (F4).
5. Distinguish SEARCHED, INWARD and OUTWARD for capacity, and do not import against a budget limit (F5).
6. Use distributional statistics as filters, not witnesses; report per-group residuals (F6).
7. Anchor fairness in human judgment and maintain the channel; do not declare it (F7).
8. Mark the name of the best system as `gap` and keep it visible (F9).
9. Lead outreach with the residual and the refuter (F10).
