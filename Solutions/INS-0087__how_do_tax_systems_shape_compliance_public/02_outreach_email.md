# Outreach draft: check one shape claim before deciding about the theory

**Use:** one-to-one, or as a cover letter attached to a submission through the public venues in `03_public_contact_points.csv`. **Not** for bulk send. The recipient is an institutional contact or a person who has opted in, never a scraped personal address.

**Hard precondition:** do not send until findings F1, F2 and F9 in `01_figure_grounded_audit.md` are understood by the sender. The output must survive validation or the two-stage offer collapses on contact.

---

**Subject:** One checkable claim about tax systems and four outcomes, before any theory

> Hello,
>
> I am writing from a figure-grounded research project. I have one specific output about how tax systems relate to four outcomes, and I would like you to check it against what you already know. I am not asking you to read the framework first, and I am not asking you to agree with it. If the output does not survive, there is nothing else to look at.
>
> **The output (`specified`, not `grounded`).** A tax system should not be scored on a single scale. It leaves four separate residual gaps, and each has a different thing that settles it:
>
> 1. **Compliance** is settled by actual payment, a counter.
> 2. **Public capacity** is settled by revenue actually collected and by the information instruments the authority can bring to the base.
> 3. **Inequality** is settled by distributional data, per group, not by a national average.
> 4. **Perceptions of fairness** are settled by human judgment, because that question is constitutively about people.
>
> From this it follows that the four cannot be averaged into one "impact of tax systems" number; that a single lever which helps compliance can hurt perceived fairness; that collection capacity does not grow by searching the same population harder but by a new record, reporter or counterparty entering the authority's reach; that two systems with the same instruments but different incidence arrangements are structurally different things; and that calling a system fair does not make it fair.
>
> This is the shape any answer must have. It is not a claim about which named tax system is best. The framework returns a kind of thing, not a name, so the slot for the best design stays open and is recorded as a gap.
>
> **What I am not claiming:** that any particular rate, base or audit is always beneficial; that enforcement alone explains compliance; that a Gini coefficient settles the distributional question; or that a fairness survey settles how citizens are actually treated. Those are bindings from other frames and carry no weight here until provenance and a ground reading are shown.
>
> **What would refute the shape claim:**
> 1. A composite indicator predicts all four slots with no live residual left in any one of them.
> 2. One lever improves compliance, capacity, equality and fairness at once, with no valence reversal across a documented change of conditions.
> 3. A collection capacity gain is achieved with no new instrument, record or counterparty entering the authority's reach.
> 4. A fairness judgment is shown to be movable by authoritative declaration alone, with no change in experienced procedure.
>
> I wrote these down before writing this message. If any holds, I will retract the claim. A one-line "this fails because..." is more useful to me than agreement. If you would rather not engage, no reply is needed.
>
> **What I am asking for:** only your judgment on whether the four-slot split and those refuters are right, given what you work with. If you are willing, send that verdict before reading anything else below.
>
> **If it survives, and only then:** the sources are listed below. The figures, not my summary of them, are the authority.
> - Canonical figure text with line numbers (local extract): `FRAMEWORK/reference/03-FIGURES_TEXT.md`
> - The eight-step formula and the mode rule (local): `FRAMEWORK/figures/THE_FORMULA.md`
> - Public figures repository, branch `general-agent`: https://github.com/IsaiahN/tether/tree/general-agent/figures
>
> **If you want to use the machinery:** run it on a problem you know well and can check against a ground. Write down what would refute your result *before* you run it, and state whether your claim is `general`, `specified` or `grounded`. A reported failure is more useful to me than a reported success.
>
> Thank you for your time,
> [name / project]

## Why this version is ordered this way

| the move | figure-grounded reason |
|---|---|
| Output first, theory second | Fig 10 :42: *"A verifier that can reconstruct the claim cannot verify it."* Bring a characterised residual, never the source. |
| Do not ask for agreement | Fig 2 :35: *"Alignment is triangulation, not negotiation. A mirror cannot correct you."* |
| Refuter written before the message | Seat Map rule carried in the exemplar: a falsifier written before the run is a design instrument, not only an honesty device. |
| Mode stated on every claim | `THE_FORMULA.md`: *"Mode. general · specified · grounded. State which."* |
| Four anchors, not one score | Fig 1 :19: *"The residual is indexed by object slot, not aggregated."* |
| Fairness anchored in people | Fig 2 :37: *"Human judgment is the right anchor for is this a good answer for a person, because the question is about people."* |
| The best design is a declared gap | Fig 9 :32: *"you get a kind of thing, never the answer itself"* |
| No ranking of recipients by likely agreement | Fig 2 :40: agreement from a shared evidence pool is disqualifying for a verdict. |
| Offer the formula as a method, not an answer | Fig 4 rule: only methods cross up; head starts come down. |
| Ask each user to write their own refuter | `THE_FORMULA.md` "Direction": a loop cannot check the direction of its own question. |

## Two-stage offer, restated

1. **Stage 1.** Send the four-slot residual, the four anchors and the refuter. Ask what the recipient would count as a failure. Record their verdict **before** showing any framework material.
2. **Stage 2, opt-in only.** If they are curious, offer the three links, with the note that the figures are the authority and the summary is not. Their later agreement is never pooled as corroboration.
3. **Stage 3, optional.** Offer the formula as a method to reuse, with the instruction that each user states a mode and writes a refuter before running it.

No request for trust, no request for endorsement, no bulk send. A failure is more useful than an endorsement.
