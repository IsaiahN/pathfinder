# Expert verification packet: the Tether on tax systems and four outcomes

**What this is:** a claim in a form a public-finance specialist, tax administrator, or fairness researcher can check. Mode, derivation and refuters are stated before the conclusion, so a reader knows what to look for and what would settle it.

**Mode: `specified` on the four slot verdicts, `general` on the structural shape, not `grounded`.** No revenue record, audit or survey is claimed here. This is a shape and a route, not a result.

## 1. The claim, in one paragraph

A tax system leaves four separate residual gaps, and they must not be averaged: compliance is settled by actual payment, public capacity by collected revenue and information instruments, inequality by per-group distributional data, and perceived fairness by the judgments of the people under the system. Compliance and capacity are properties of design-and-habitat, not of taxpayers or authorities alone, so no instrument has a standing good valence across all four slots. Capacity grows when a new record, reporter or counterparty enters the authority's reach, not from more search at the same reach. Two systems with the same instruments but different incidence arrangements are isomers, different substances hidden by the same statutory formula. Fairness is a maintained channel, not a declaration. The framework does not name the best design; it returns the shape any answer must have, with refuters written in advance.

## 2. Three checkable sub-claims

### C1  -  framework-internal; checkable by reading Figures 1 and 9

- **Statement:** R is indexed per slot, never aggregated; the four conditions form a union of overlapping conditions, so a lever clearing one region leaves the intersections standing.
- **Source, verbatim:** Fig 1 :19 *"The residual is indexed by object slot, not aggregated."* Fig 1 :22 *"Aggregating across slots is how a live signal disappears into an average."* Fig 9 :55 *"A bundled problem is usually a union of overlapping conditions, not a list of separate ones."* Also Fig 9 :57 *"What remains is smaller and it is also categorically different: a case in two conditions is not a harder case of either."*
- **Refuter:** a composite indicator that predicts all four slots with no live residual left in any one; or one intervention restoring all four with no categorically different residue.

### C2  -  public-finance facing; checkable against records and administrative history

- **Statement:** compliance and collection capability live in the contact between design and habitat; collection capacity moves only when a new instrument, record or counterparty enters the authority's reach.
- **Source, verbatim:** Fig 11 :39 *"So the answerable question is not what intelligence is, but what makes a system capable. And capability is a property of agent-and-habitat, never of the agent alone."* Fig 11 :40 *"A system cannot be made more capable by improving the agent in isolation. An improvement that does not change contact changes nothing, however much it improves."* Fig 6 :23 *"No frame escapes its own closure. The bound moves only on contact with a frame whose closure differs."* Fig 8 :32 *"Closure is idempotent. Composing inside a closed set never leaves it, so no search, however long, adds an atom. This one is definitional: it is what closure means."*
- **What to check:** did documented revenue gains follow new information atoms (third-party reporting, cadastral or VAT records, cross-agency data), or only more enforcement effort on the same base? Does a capacity gain ever occur with no change in what the authority can see?
- **Refuter:** a capacity gain achieved by pure internal search with no new instrument, record or counterparty entering the closure; or sustained compliance change with unchanged contact.

### C3  -  distribution and fairness facing; checkable against incidence data and citizen judgment

- **Statement:** same atoms under different incidence bonds are different substances; fairness is anchored in human judgment, and trust is a channel that decays if contact is not maintained.
- **Source, verbatim:** Fig 12 :117 *"isomer. Same atoms, different bond, different substance. The difference is invisible in the formula and decisive in the behaviour."* Fig 2 :37 *"Human judgment is the right anchor for is this a good answer for a person, because the question is about people."* Fig 10 :24 *"the ground does not decay, the channel does"* Fig 10 :49 *"Declaring a meaning does not ground it."*
- **What to check:** for two systems over the same atom set (rate, base, threshold, exemption, record, reporter, audit, penalty), does changing only the incidence arrangement change per-group outcomes? Is fairness measured by expressed judgment under described procedures, or declared by the authority?
- **Refuter:** identical per-slot residuals for full-set incidence isomers; or a fairness judgment moved by authoritative declaration alone with no change in experienced procedure.

## 3. What is explicitly not claimed

- Not a named best tax system, not an optimal rate, not a policy recommendation.
- Not that enforcement alone explains compliance, or that tax morale alone does.
- Not that one lever improves all four slots.
- Not that a Gini coefficient or a compliance rate settles its slot; both are filters, not witnesses.
- Not that calling a system fair grounds the claim.
- Not `grounded`. Nothing here was settled by measurement.
- Not a second witness. This packet is a simulated derivation, internally consistent with the figures, not a ground reading.

## 5. What would settle it

A ground reading, not agreement. Per slot: a source-discriminating instrument for compliance; a documented new atom in the authority's reach for capacity; per-group effective-rate data over a defined incidence change for distribution; expressed citizen judgment with procedure recorded for fairness. Any of the five refuters in `01` would break the shape claim, and a figure re-census finding a line that governs all four slots would replace the `gap` in C1's domain.

## 6. What an expert can check in twenty minutes

1. Read Fig 1 :19, :22 and Fig 9 :55, :57 (four short passages). Does the four-slot split match what you see in practice, or is a composite score defensible?
2. Check C2 against one case you know: did collection change come from new information instruments or only from more effort on the same base? Name the atom that entered, or say none did.
3. Check C3 against one incidence reform you know: same atoms, different arrangement, different per-group outcome? And is fairness in your field anchored in expressed judgment or in authority declarations?
4. Name one instrument that would discriminate among deterrence, norm and legitimacy as sources of a compliance reading, or say why none is available.
5. Send the refutation. A failure is more useful than an endorsement. If you would rather not engage, no reply is needed.
