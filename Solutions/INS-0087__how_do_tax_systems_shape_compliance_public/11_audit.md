## 4. Round trip gap R_T

The Symbols table defines R_T as the gap between what was sent up and what came back down, `gap(x, (T_E ∘ T_A)(x))`; Fig 4 :30 requires it to be measured rather than assumed. I ran the round trip on the condensation this folder actually uses.

One thing survives cleanly: the four-slot shape with a ground per slot and no named optimum. That is the residue worth sending. R_T was **not** measured numerically, because no ground reading exists; it is recorded qualitatively as the enumerated set of dropped constraints.
