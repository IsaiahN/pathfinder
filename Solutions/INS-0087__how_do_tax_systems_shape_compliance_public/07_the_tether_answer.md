# The Tether's answer: how tax systems shape four outcomes

**Mode: `specified` on the four slot verdicts, `general` on the structural shape, not `grounded`.** Simulated derivation checked for internal consistency against the figures (`05_what_shape_means.md` §6), not a ground reading.

## 1. The residual it answers

A tax system leaves four gaps between what its design expects and what is observed: compliance, public capacity, inequality, and perceptions of fairness. The question bundles four loops that overlap. Fig 9 :55: *"A bundled problem is usually a union of overlapping conditions, not a list of separate ones."* Each loop has its own anchor: actual payment, collected revenue and information instruments, per-group distributional data, and human judgment. This is a union compound, not one hard problem (Fig 9 :57: *"What remains is smaller and it is also categorically different: a case in two conditions is not a harder case of either."*).

## 2. The shape, per slot

**1. Never average the four.** Fig 1 :19: *"The residual is indexed by object slot, not aggregated."* Fig 1 :22: *"Aggregating across slots is how a live signal disappears into an average."* A single "impact of tax systems" score lets a compliance gain cancel a fairness loss. That is exactly the signal the question asks us to keep. Report four residuals with four anchors.

**2. Compliance and capacity are properties of design-and-habitat.** Fig 11 :39: *"So the answerable question is not what intelligence is, but what makes a system capable. And capability is a property of agent-and-habitat, never of the agent alone."* Fig 11 :40: *"A system cannot be made more capable by improving the agent in isolation. An improvement that does not change contact changes nothing, however much it improves."* Neither a taxpayer's morale nor an authority's rulebook alone explains payment or collection. The unit is the contact between them: who reports, what records exist, what the citizen sees in return.

**3. No standing list of good instruments.** Fig 11 :49: *"An actor’s valence is relative to the residual, not a property it carries."* Fig 11 :51: *"No standing list of good actors survives a change of residual."* The same audit that closes the compliance gap can open the fairness gap. Every instrument must be priced in two currencies, and the sign must be restated whenever the residual changes.

**4. Capacity is reach, moved only by an import.** Fig 6 :23: *"No frame escapes its own closure. The bound moves only on contact with a frame whose closure differs."* Fig 8 :32: *"Closure is idempotent. Composing inside a closed set never leaves it, so no search, however long, adds an atom. This one is definitional: it is what closure means."* Searching the same population harder does not grow the base. A new atom must enter: a third-party report, a cadastral record, a different jurisdiction's data. Formula step 7 keeps SEARCHED, INWARD and OUTWARD distinct, asks SEARCHED first, and never imports against a budget limit.

**5. Distribution is a per-slot composition with isomers.** Fig 12 :117: *"isomer. Same atoms, different bond, different substance. The difference is invisible in the formula and decisive in the behaviour."* Two systems with the same instruments but different incidence arrangements are different substances, not variants of one thing. A national average hides the per-group residual, and a Gini coefficient is a filter for the budget, not a witness for the verdict (Fig 9 :60: *"Use filters for the budget and witnesses for the verdict. Never let a filter hand you a verdict."*).

**6. Fairness is anchored in people and maintained as a channel.** Fig 2 :37: *"Human judgment is the right anchor for is this a good answer for a person, because the question is about people."* Fig 10 :24: *"the ground does not decay, the channel does"* Trust in a tax system is contact that must be maintained; it is not a declaration. Fig 2 :35: *"Alignment is triangulation, not negotiation. A mirror cannot correct you."* Surveying citizens and then telling them the survey says they are fairly treated is negotiation with a mirror.

**7. The name of the best design is a `gap`.** Fig 9 :32: *"you get a kind of thing, never the answer itself"* Fig 13 :134: *"Unreachable is a verdict here, not a silence,"* The figures constrain the shape of any answer; they do not select a rate schedule. X1 in the census has no nameable anchor and is not yet a domain. This is a declared limit, not an omission.

## 3. What it explicitly does not say

- Not that any named tax system is best. The framework returns a shape, not a name.
- Not that enforcement alone explains compliance, or that morale alone does. Capability is design-and-habitat.
- Not that one lever improves all four slots. The question is a union and the intersections bind.
- Not that a distribution statistic settles the inequality slot. It is a filter, and R is per slot.
- Not that declaring a system fair makes it fair. Declaring a meaning does not ground it (Fig 10 :49).
- Not `grounded`. No revenue record, audit, survey or distributional dataset was read for this verdict.

## 4. What would settle it, the ground

A ground reading per slot, not a further argument:

- **Compliance:** a payment record that separates deterrence, norm and legitimacy as sources of the same reading. A compliance rate alone is metamerism; *The Hard Problem is About Source* says an instrument *"cannot comment on the source."*
- **Capacity:** a documented new atom entering the authority's reach (a new reporter, register or record) with a measured revenue change, as distinct from more search at the same reach.
- **Distribution:** per-group effective-rate data before and after a defined incidence change, with the full shared atom set held constant for the isomer test.
- **Fairness:** the expressed judgments of the people under the system, with procedure and contact recorded, and no pooling into an average that hides a live slot.
- Standing refuters from `01`: a composite indicator predicting all four slots; a lever with no valence reversal across a documented change of residual; a capacity gain with no new atom; a fairness judgment moved by declaration alone; or a figure line yielding one named design with a ground settlement.

## 5. The honest stop

**Unreached, not unreachable.** The stop is at the limit of what the available instruments can distinguish. The four anchors exist in principle, so this is not a claim that no answer can be had. It is a claim that no single figure line governs the four-slot question and that the framework cannot name the best design from inside its own closure. The lineage is recorded so the stop is checkable: unions, anchors, imports, isomers and the one live ⋛ strain are all shown rather than smoothed. *"Indiscernibility is not identity"* (canonical figures README), so a shape that fits four slots is not thereby the answer.

## 6. One-paragraph version for outreach

> The Tether does not name a best tax system. It says the question is four questions in one, and that the four must never be averaged: compliance is settled by payment, capacity by collected revenue and information instruments, inequality by per-group data, and perceived fairness by the judgments of the people living under the system. Compliance and capacity are properties of design-and-habitat, not of taxpayers or authorities alone, so no standing list of good instruments survives a change of conditions; the same audit can close one gap and open another. Capacity grows when a new record, reporter or counterparty enters the authority's reach, not from searching the same population harder. Two systems with the same instruments but different incidence arrangements are isomers, different substances hidden by the same schedule. And fairness is a channel that decays unless contact maintains it; declaring a system fair does not make it so. What the framework offers is that shape, its refuters written in advance, and the instruments that would close each slot. The name stays open until the ground settles it.
