## What in the wider literature bears on this question

1. **The enforcement literature governs one subset, not the answer.** Allingham and Sandmo build the deterrence model; Andreoni, Erard and Feinstein review theory and evidence on enforcement. Both address the compliance loop. Read as a whole-question theory they conflict with the four-slot shape, which is why F2 splits the question instead of flipping the verdict. The conflict was returned to the census and resolved by assigning deterrence to D1 alone.
2. **The state-capacity literature corroborates reach, not more search.** Besley and Persson and the state-capacity reference both describe collection as depending on institutions, information and norms. This is the habitat half of Fig 11 and the import half of Fig 6, not evidence for a single best rate.
3. **Incidence literature corroborates the isomer reading.** Nominal incidence and economic incidence differ. Same statutory atoms, different arrangement, different substance, which is Fig 12's definition.
4. **Procedural justice and common-pool research are the low-overlap witnesses.** Tyler's voice finding and Ostrom's design principles come from social psychology and institutional ecology rather than public finance. They corroborate the mechanism shape (contact, voice, graduated sanction, no panacea) without sharing the tax-compliance evidence pool.
5. **Framework essays are high-overlap and are used for canonical reading only.** All three are by the figures' author. Their agreement with the figures is expected and carries no independent corroboration weight.

No source's disagreement flipped a verdict. Two apparent conflicts were returned to the census: the deterrence literature's single-lever framing (resolved by assigning it to D1) and the "poor norm for compliance" phrasing (resolved by reading the norm as a habitat condition).

## Distribution: offer, do not push

- **Fig 10 :42**  -  *"A verifier that can reconstruct the claim cannot verify it."* A recipient who has absorbed the framework before being asked for a verdict has joined the frame being checked. Lead with the four-slot residual and the refuter.

**Recommended order in a reply:** (1) the four-slot shape and the refuter; (2) ask what the recipient would count as a failure and record the verdict first; (3) then the three links, with the note that the figures, not the summary, are the authority; (4) only if asked, offer the formula as a method with a mode label and a self-written refuter.

## The two-stage offer

Stage 1 is Fig 10's "bring a characterised residual, never the source" applied to a public-finance question. Stage 2 is opt-in by construction: once a recipient has absorbed the theory, their agreement stops being evidence, so the stage-1 verdict must be recorded before any theory is shown. Stage 3 sends a method, which is permitted because only methods cross up (Fig 4). Each user still needs a ground: a revenue record, an audit, a survey or a distributional dataset, plus a refuter written before the run.

**Hard precondition:** stages 1 to 3 only work if the stage-1 output survives. Findings F1, F2 and F9 in `01_figure_grounded_audit.md` are the parts that must be checked first.
