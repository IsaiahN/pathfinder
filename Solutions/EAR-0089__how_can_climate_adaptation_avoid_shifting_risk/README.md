## The question restated as an effect

An adaptation A in slot s (a community now, or the present generation) lowers the risk measured inside A's own frame. The same action raises positive residual R⁺ in other slots s' that the frame did not count: a downstream community, a downwind community, a community that loses sediment or water, or a later generation that inherits lock-in, deferred cost, and foreclosed options. The effect to be decided is therefore not "does A reduce risk here" but "does A leave any live slot carrying a positive residual, measured against a ground the acting frame cannot move." Shifting risk is the name for a falling local R accompanied by a rising R⁺ elsewhere. Avoiding the shift means closing R across every slot in the enumerated habitat and across the temporal chain, without averaging the live slot away.

An answer would look like a constraint on any candidate adaptation, not a list of approved technologies. Bind that constraint as an ordinary term (formula step, Direction): "non-shifting = per-slot residual at zero across the full habitat and the future chain, ranged against a non-updating ground." Once bound, the sought-for shape can accrue a residual and be refused by the same test as everything else.

## What is deliberately not here

- No personal emails of named individuals.
- No ranking of people by how likely they are to believe the claim.
- No bulk-send plan.

Three reasons, in the figures' own words:

1. **A room is not an anchor.** Fig 7 :13 "agreement compounds, evidence does not". A list of agreeable readers raises the vocabulary, not the ground.
2. **Selecting for receptiveness manufactures the disqualifying correlation.** Fig 2 :40 "agreement about the world is worthless when the agreeing frames share an evidence pool". A set chosen for likely agreement is one pool wearing many names.
3. **Asking for trust before checking spends the only independence a reader contributes.** Fig 10 :42 "A verifier that can reconstruct the claim cannot verify it."

Legally: unsolicited bulk mail breaches GDPR and CAN-SPAM style rules in many jurisdictions. That is a practical failure on top of the structural one. Public submission and opt-in venues are used instead.
