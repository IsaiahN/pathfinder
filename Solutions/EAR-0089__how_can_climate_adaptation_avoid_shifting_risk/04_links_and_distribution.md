## Two things in the wider literature that bear directly

1. **Risk introduced by responses is now part of the risk concept itself.** The IPCC SPM says "The risk that can be introduced by human responses to climate change is a new aspect considered in the risk concept" and defines effectiveness as reducing vulnerability and risk while it "avoids maladaptation". The institution whose definition the field uses has already conceded the shape: an adaptation can create the risk it claims to remove. Cite this when explaining why the per-slot test is not an external import.
2. **Risks transmit through interconnected systems and across regions.** The SPM's "Complex risks result from multiple climate hazards occurring concurrently, and from multiple risks interacting, compounding overall risk and resulting in risks transmitting through interconnected systems and across regions" is the world-language version of Fig 11's cascade and Fig 13's network. Chapter 17 adds that "coastal infrastructure is an example that has particularly high risk for maladaptation through trade-offs for natural system functioning and human vulnerability over time." These corroborate the figure verdict; they do not derive it.

## Distribution: offer, do not push

Figure-grounded reasons:

- **Fig 4 :22 "Only methods cross up. Only head starts come down."** Sending the full framework before anyone has described a residual sends a recording, not a generator. Lead with the shape claim and its refuter; offer links after.
- **Fig 10 :42 "A verifier that can reconstruct the claim cannot verify it."** A recipient who has absorbed the theory before being asked for a verdict has joined the frame being checked.
- **Fig 9 :27-28 "describe it before you go looking, or any frame you pick will seem to fit."** Ask what they would expect to see if the shape claim were true before giving them the IPCC chapter.
- **Fig 2 :42 "Buy independence in proportion to the cost of being wrong."** The cost here is a maladapted investment in a neighboring or future community, so independent instruments are worth their price.
- **Fig 2 :40 "agreement about the world is worthless when the agreeing frames share an evidence pool."** A list selected for people likely to agree produces a convention, not corroboration.

**Recommended order in a reply:** (1) the shape claim and its four refuters; (2) ask what they would count as a failure and record the answer; (3) only then the three links, with the note that the figures, not the summary, are the authority.

## The two-stage offer

- **Stage 1 before stage 2** is Fig 10 :52 "Bring a characterised residual, never the source" and Fig 2 :45 "Frames range against the ground. They do not negotiate with it."
- **Stage 2 is opt-in by construction.** Once a recipient has absorbed the framework, their agreement stops being evidence. Their stage-1 verdict must be recorded before any theory is shown, and later agreement is never pooled as corroboration.
- **Stage 3 sends a method, which is permitted.** The eight-step loop is a generator; giving it away does not give away an answer. `THE_FORMULA.md` "Direction" still applies: a frame "cannot check the direction of its own question", so each user writes down what would refute their result and states the mode of what they get.
- **Hard precondition:** stages 1 to 3 work only if the stage-1 output survives. The refuters in `01_figure_grounded_audit.md` section 4 are written for that check.
