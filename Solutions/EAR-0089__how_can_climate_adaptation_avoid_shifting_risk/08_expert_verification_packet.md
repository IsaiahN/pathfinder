# Expert verification packet : the Tether on adaptation and displaced risk

**What this is:** a claim in a form an adaptation or risk researcher can check. Mode, derivation, and refuters are stated before the conclusion, so a reader knows what to look for and what would settle it.

**Mode: `specified` on the substantive claims, `general` on the structural shape. Not `grounded`.** No per-slot measurement of an actual adaptation is claimed. This is a shape and a route, not a result.

## 1. The claim, in one paragraph

An adaptation has not avoided risk if it has only moved it. Risk is indexed per slot (community, generation), so a regional average can fall while one downstream community carries a live residual (Fig 1 :19). The adapting frame cannot be its own anchor; the ground is the measured outcome in the affected slots (Fig 2 :29-30). The habitat is enumerated outward because the decisive residual is often three steps from the project (Fig 11 :43-44, :52-53). Neighboring communities and future generations are two conditions in a union and are settled in separate loops (Fig 9 :54). Introduction is available, exclusion is not, so adaptation is permanent work and relocation substitutes a habitat rather than removing the residual (Fig 11 :61, :66-67). There is no last room, so success is a monitored handoff, not a terminal closure (Fig 7 :6). The framework returns this shape, not a named technology (Fig 9 :32).

## 2. Three checkable sub-claims

### C1 : framework-internal; checkable by reading Figures 1, 2, and 11

- **Statement:** success cannot be an aggregate; it must be per slot, and the actor cannot score it.
- **Source, verbatim:** Fig 1 :19 "The residual is indexed by object slot, not aggregated." Fig 1 :22 "Aggregating across slots is how a live signal disappears into an average." Fig 2 :29-30 "An anchor is whatever settles the question and does not change its answer because of what any frame thinks." Fig 11 :52-53 "the residual that mattered was three steps out".
- **Refuter:** show that the framework admits an aggregate index with one live positive slot as a settled success, or show a complete enumeration whose second- and third-order residual is zero in every slot. If either holds, C1 fails.

### C2 : field-facing; checkable by an adaptation researcher against the literature

- **Statement:** the spatial transfer and the temporal transfer are different compositions. The first runs through a network (influence travels); the second through a gradient (a difference that can be spent, here lock-in and deferred cost). They must not be averaged.
- **Source, verbatim:** Fig 13 :24 "that entities relate, and influence travels"; Fig 13 :22 "a difference that can be spent"; Fig 9 :54 "And when the split lands on a union rather than a partition, unbundle before you search again." Corroborating world source: IPCC AR6 WGII SPM, "risks transmitting through interconnected systems and across regions".
- **What to check:** is each characterization faithful to how risk actually propagates in the system under study? Does a single lever clear both conditions, or only the region where one holds? IPCC AR6 WGII Chapter 17 states that "coastal infrastructure is an example that has particularly high risk for maladaptation through trade-offs for natural system functioning and human vulnerability over time" and that "path dependencies are avoided in order to not limit future options for climate resilient development".
- **Refuter:** one instrument that resolves both transfers as a single condition, or a system in which the intersection behaves exactly like either condition alone.

### C3 : framework-internal derivation with an institutional test

- **Statement:** a future-generation slot requires a declared convention that cannot decide the outcome, and adaptation is a monitored cycle with no terminal closure.
- **Source, verbatim:** Fig 10 :47 "a convention that decides an outcome has stopped being one"; Fig 10 :59 "an interest in the outcome is unavoidable, an interest in the reading is disqualifying"; Fig 7 :6 "the limit: no last room"; Fig 12 :121 "The output re-enters as input. The sign of the loop decides whether it closes, and a chain cannot close by construction."
- **Refuter:** a convention whose declared meaning fixes the measured result, or a one-off adaptation whose residual stays at zero for every future slot with no feedback loop.

## 3. What is explicitly not claimed

- Not a theory of everything and not a final answer. No last room.
- Not that any named measure is maladaptation, and not that retreat beats protection. Those are bindings and must be provenanced.
- Not that a present proxy settles the future. The proxy makes a slot statable; it is a convention, not a verdict.
- Not that agreement among reviewers is corroboration. Fig 2 :40 "agreement about the world is worthless when the agreeing frames share an evidence pool."
- Not grounded. Nothing here is asserted as settled by experiment.
- Not a derivation of any climate model from the figures. The figures constrain the shape of a check; they do not produce climate data.

## 5. What would settle it

A per-slot measurement, not an argument. From *The Hard Problem is About Source*, an instrument that reads green "cannot comment on the source"; a better instrument resolved it. Here:

1. **Slot test:** publish the residual vector for every enumerated community and generation band, second and third order included, against an outcome no frame can move.
2. **Independence test:** range upstream and downstream instruments separately. If they share one model, they are one witness (Fig 2 :31-32).
3. **Convention test:** vary the future-generation proxy and show the measured outcome does not move with it.
4. **Cycle test:** show the monitoring output re-enters the next decision.
