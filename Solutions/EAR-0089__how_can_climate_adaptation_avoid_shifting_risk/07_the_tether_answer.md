# The Tether's answer on adaptation and displaced risk

**Mode: `specified` on the substantive claims, `general` on the structural shape. Not `grounded`.** This is a shape and a route, not a result. Simulated derivation checked for internal consistency against the figures (`05_what_shape_means.md` §7), not a ground reading.

## 1. The residual it answers

An adaptation lowers measured risk in the acting community, while positive residual rises in a neighboring community or a future generation. The question asks for the constraint that prevents the relocation. The two conditions named are a union compound, not one hard problem: Fig 9 :55-57 "a case in two conditions is not a harder case of either". Each condition gets its own loop and its own refuter; their verdicts are never averaged.

## 2. The shape

1. **Risk is indexed per slot.** Fig 1 :19 "The residual is indexed by object slot, not aggregated." A regional total that falls while one downstream community carries a live residual is a legal state (Fig 1 :13), not a success. Fig 1 :22 "Aggregating across slots is how a live signal disappears into an average."
2. **The actor is not the anchor.** Fig 2 :29-30 "An anchor is whatever settles the question and does not change its answer because of what any frame thinks." An avoided-damage figure produced by the builder is a frame-internal quantity. The ground is the measured outcome in the affected slots. Fig 2 :35 "Alignment is triangulation, not negotiation. A mirror cannot correct you."
3. **Enumerate the habitat outward.** Fig 11 :43-44 "The habitat is enumerated, never composed." Fig 11 :52-53 "the residual that mattered was three steps out". The neighbor is second order; the future generation is reached through lock-in and deferred cost, often third order. The same structure is "a benefactor to one residual and a malfactor to another" (Fig 11 :49-51), so "protective" is a relation, not a property.
4. **Addition without subtraction.** Fig 11 :61 "Introduction is available; exclusion is not." The river and the sediment budget cannot be subtracted from the habitat. Fig 11 :63-64 "a managed ecosystem is permanent work rather than a solved state". Relocation is not removal: Fig 11 :66-67 "Isolation is not removal of the habitat. It is substitution of one habitat for another."
5. **Two loops, not one.** Fig 9 :54 "And when the split lands on a union rather than a partition, unbundle before you search again." Spatial transfer (through the network, "that entities relate, and influence travels", Fig 13 :24) and temporal transfer (through a gradient, "a difference that can be spent", Fig 13 :22) are composed differently and are settled separately.
6. **No last room.** Fig 7 :6 "the limit: no last room"; Fig 7 :13 "agreement compounds, evidence does not". Adaptation is a cycle of handoffs selected against the residual each one surfaces, not a terminal fix. Fig 13 :90 "One chain is one route through a space that has no last route."
7. **A shape, not a technology.** Fig 9 :32 "you get a kind of thing, never the answer itself". The framework returns per-slot closure across the full habitat and the future chain against an unmoving ground. It does not select seawall, wetland, zoning, or retreat. Those are provenanced bindings from the local frame, imported when the affected frames are brought in: Fig 6 :23 "No frame escapes its own closure. The bound moves only on contact with a frame whose closure differs." Fig 8 :45 "Two frames together reach what neither reaches alone."

## 3. What it explicitly does not say

- Not that any named measure is maladaptation. Naming a technology as *the* answer is the metamerism error described in `05_what_shape_means.md` §4.
- Not that managed retreat is always better than protection. Fig 11 :66-67 says it substitutes a habitat with actors you did not choose.
- Not that a present-generation proxy can settle what a future generation will experience. Fig 10 :47 "a convention that decides an outcome has stopped being one." The proxy makes the slot statable; it does not decide it.
- Not that agreement among reviewers corroborates the shape. Fig 2 :40 "agreement about the world is worthless when the agreeing frames share an evidence pool."
- Not grounded. No per-slot measurement of an actual adaptation was taken in this run.
- Not that the spatial and temporal shifts are one problem, and not an average of their verdicts.

## 4. What would settle it : the ground

A source-discriminating instrument, not a further argument. From *The Hard Problem is About Source*, an instrument that reads green "cannot comment on the source"; the spectrometer resolved it. Here the corresponding tests are:

- **Slot test:** publish R⁺ for every enumerated community and generation band, including second and third order, with the enumeration. If any slot stays positive after the program, the program has shifted risk.
- **Independence test:** range the upstream and downstream instruments separately against outcomes neither can move. If their agreement comes from one model, it is one witness (Fig 2 :31-32).
- **Convention test:** state the future-generation proxy as a convention and show that changing it does not change the measured outcome. If it does, the convention has become a verdict.
- **Cycle test:** show that monitoring output re-enters as input to the next adaptation decision. A chain cannot close by construction (Fig 12 :121).
- **Standing refuters from `01` section 4:** per-slot closure with no contact at second or third order; aggregate success admitted with a live slot; a terminal adaptation closing all residual forever; a single frame seeing all slots from inside its closure.

## 5. The honest stop

**Unreached, not unreachable.** The six-term set is a visible guess at reality's compression axes, not nature's own list. Another habitat, another sensorium, need not use these six. The answer stops where the available instruments stop discriminating among sources, and the lineage is recorded so the stop is checkable. Fig 8 :39 "So a search that finds nothing is never a proof of absence." Fig 13 :134 "Unreachable is a verdict here, not a silence".

## 6. One-paragraph version for outreach

> The Tether does not tell you which climate adaptation to build, and it does not claim a final answer. It says that risk is measured per community and per generation, so a falling regional average can hide a live downstream slot; that the builder cannot score its own avoided damage, because the anchor is the measured outcome in the affected slots; that the habitat must be enumerated outward, because the residual that matters is often three steps away; that neighbors and future generations are two loops and must be unbundled; that you can add a defense to a habitat but cannot subtract the river, so adaptation is permanent work and relocation substitutes a new habitat rather than removing the risk; and that there is no last room, so success is a monitored handoff, not a terminal fix. What it offers is the shape any non-shifting adaptation must have, four falsifiers written in advance, and the instruments that would close it. The slot where a specific measure is named stays open until the ground settles it.
