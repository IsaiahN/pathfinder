## 1. A shape is a constraint, not a filler

The canonical README states the limit: the framework returns "what a missing thing would have to look like (a shape, not a name)". Fig 9 :32 "you get a kind of thing, never the answer itself". Fig 13, on the missing primitive: "It cannot say which primitive is missing, because naming it would mean already holding it."

From *The Library of Babel*: "you can cut your search short if the set of books returned is small and descriptive enough that it provides a general shape of the answer that satisfies your question."

So a **shape** is the narrowed form of an answer you can state before you can name it. It constrains; it does not fill. For EAR-0089 the shape is:

> Any adaptation counts as non-shifting only if every slot in the enumerated habitat, including the future chain, carries zero live residual against a ground that does not move when the actor revalues it, and the bound moves only when the affected frames are imported.

That is a constraint. "Build a seawall", "restore the mangrove", "retreat", and "pay compensation" are fillers. The shape admits all of them as candidates and settles none of them.

## 2. How the shape is obtained

- *Library of Babel*: use the vocabulary already closest to the thing, then "With each new term gained, you narrow the space and deepen your vocabulary." Here the starting vocabulary is per-slot residual, anchor, habitat, cascade, union, and import.
- Fig 6 :30 "The description travels. Fails by being sharpened into terms only we can read. Cast it in effect terms." Fig 9 :30-31 "your words are borrowed too, so describe the effect rather than the cause". So the shape is stated in effects: displaced exposure, deferred cost, foreclosed options, not in the vocabulary of one agency's impact model.
- *Decoding Tacit Knowledge*: "When contact isn't possible, the best you can send is a plain description of what you'd expect to see, because anyone can check that against the world." This is why the outreach packet asks what a failure would look like before showing any framework.
- *Library of Babel* on stopping: "you will know that you are done because the finish line will also be the starting place." Here that place is the per-slot residual, which is both the start of the census and the thing that must stop arriving.

## 3. What licenses a name

Only two things, both outside the shape itself:

1. **The ground settles it.** `THE_FORMULA.md` step 5: "SETTLE: the ground settles it. Verified only by what pays. A gate is not the ground." The README: the framework "stops at the limit of what the available instruments can distinguish, and records the lineage so the stop is honest."
2. **A provenanced import from a frame whose closure differs** (Fig 6 :23, Fig 8 :50-52), with a source gate and a shadow test.

For this question, a future-generation proxy is a convention authored by a seat, not a name of what the future will experience: Fig 10 :47 "a convention that decides an outcome has stopped being one". Naming a specific measure as *the* non-shifting adaptation turns a visible choice into an invisible one.

## 4. The metamerism problem for "risk reduced"

*The Hard Problem is About Source* gives the mechanism. An instrument reads green and "cannot comment on the source": one green light, or yellow plus blue, or RGB, or CMYK, or a scheme not yet discovered. "If several paths create the same behavior, then behavior is a projection with lossy information." It was the spectrometer, not an argument, that resolved it.

The adaptation analogue is exact. A program reports avoided damage of X. That single reading is compatible with at least three sources: risk genuinely removed; risk moved to a neighbor; risk deferred to a successor. The reading cannot comment on the source. "An instrument that can't distinguish these cases demonstrates its own inadequacy." What is owed is a better instrument, here a per-slot measure across the habitat and the temporal chain, not a name attached to the aggregate.

## 5. What shape means for this question, answered

**It is a constraint on any candidate adaptation, not an approved technology.** The framework can say that an aggregate index with a live slot is not a verdict (Fig 1), that the actor cannot be its own anchor (Fig 2), that the neighbor is second order and the future often third (Fig 11), that the two shifts are a union (Fig 9), and that no last room exists (Fig 7). It cannot say which of seawall, mangrove, zoning, retreat, or compensation is the right answer in a given basin. That is a binding from the local frame, and it must carry provenance, a shadow test, and a per-slot measurement against a ground.

If a reader demands the name of the measure, the honest answer is a shape plus a falsifier, not a technology. Fig 8 :32 "you get a kind of thing, never the answer itself."

## 6. Correction to my own §3 (compare the exemplar's Q2 correction)

I initially read "shape, not a name" as forbidding the selection of any specific measure. That overstated it. The README's phrase governs "a **missing** thing", a primitive not held in the library. Seawalls, wetlands, networks, and gradients are not primitives the framework is missing; they are actors and structures in the habitat, enumerated from the world (Fig 11 :43-48). Selecting one is a binding, not the invention of a new primitive.

What remains wrong in any record that names a measure as *the* answer is the metamerism point of §4: a source is being named from an aggregate reading that cannot discriminate among sources. Both claims are kept visible here. The prohibition is on treating the binding as a figure output, not on making it.

## 7. Two consequences for outreach

**Prestige is not action.** From *The Recursive Self-Improvement Trap*: "Prestige can fund an action. It can give an action a budget to be tried. But it cannot settle an action. Only the ground can settle it. The Ground Maintainer must be blind to prestige." A campaign that gathers distinguished names to agree that an adaptation is good is the named collapse. The legitimate ask is a budget to try the per-slot test and let the ground decide.

**My own audit is a simulation, not a ground reading.** Same essay: "LLMs can quickly simulate an approximate answer/output with the formula... However, the truly testable output would need to be derived from the real architecture or working the formula yourself." LLM output is "simulacra". So `01_figure_grounded_audit.md` is a simulated derivation checked for internal consistency against the figures. It is not a test of any adaptation and must be labelled as such. It cannot settle anything it reports on.
