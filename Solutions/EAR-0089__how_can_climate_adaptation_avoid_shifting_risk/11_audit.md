## Independent domain census (written from the residual line alone)

I redid the census before reading the primary's conclusions, using the WATSON order: R by arity, symmetry, and scale; one line per domain with actors / currency / anchor; partition or union; order by dependency.

- **R (arity / symmetry / scale).** The residual is positive mass in slots the adapting frame did not count: a neighbor now, a successor later. Arity: several slots (vector), not one scalar, so the aggregate is structurally unable to carry the signal. Symmetry: directed, since the same structure is benefactor here and malfactor there (Fig 11 :49-51). Scale: first order at the project, second and third order at the basin and the intergenerational chain (Fig 11 :52-53). It is a union of two conditions, so unbundle before searching (Fig 9 :54).
- **Domain 1, the local adaptation frame.** Actors: planner, measure. Currency: claimed avoided damage. Anchor: none while it ranges only against its own model; a self-scored quantity is not an anchor (Fig 2 :29-30). Actor closure.
- **Domain 2, the habitat.** Actors: neighboring communities, water, sediment, markets, ecosystems. Currency: measured exposure per slot. Anchor: gauges and loss records that do not move when revalued (Fig 2 :29-30). Partition of roles with Domain 1.
- **Domain 3, the temporal chain.** Actors: successors, lock-in, debt, depleted buffers. Currency: foreclosed options and deferred cost (Fig 13 :22). Anchor: future measured states; a convention is owed and must not decide (Fig 10 :47). A second loop.
- **Domain 4, verification.** Actors: monitoring, auditors, affected communities. Currency: residuals and refuters. Anchor: the ground, never agreement (Fig 2 :40). In this simulated run its independence is zero.

Order by dependency: enumerate slots, name affordances, define the per-slot objective, plan, carry methods forward (Fig 3 :27, Fig 4 :22); split the union before searching (Fig 9 :54); refuse before mint (formula step 2).

## Round trip gap `R_T`

The Symbols table defines `R_T` as `gap(x, (T_E ∘ T_A)(x))`, the gap between what was sent up and what came back down. Fig 4 :30 requires it to be measured rather than assumed. I ran the round trip on the condensation this folder actually uses.

1. **Sent up (x):** the verbatim figure set, chiefly Fig 1 :19 (per-slot), Fig 2 :29-30 (anchor), Fig 9 :54 (union), Fig 11 :43-44, :52-53, :66-67 (habitat, cascade, isolation), Fig 7 :6 (no last room), Fig 9 :32 (shape not a name).
2. **Abstraction (T_A):** the shorthand carried in `07` and `09`: measure per slot, enumerate outward, range against a ground, split two loops, add not subtract, no terminal fix, name no technology.
3. **Concretisation (T_E):** a concrete adaptation program with a regional risk-reduction target, a monitoring dashboard, and a compensation fund for affected communities.

**`R_T` is nonzero.** Three things the shorthand holds do not survive the trip back down:

- the **definition of the slot**, which the shorthand leaves as "affected community" and which a program must operationalize as a specific enumeration with specific instruments; an under-enumerated slot turns per-slot measurement back into an aggregate;
- the **convention for the future slot**, which the shorthand marks as non-deciding and which a compensation fund makes into a present payment that can easily become the verdict;
- the **refusal step**, which the shorthand places before mint and which a program with a fixed target tends to reverse, treating the falling dashboard as settled.

Conversely, one thing survives cleanly: a falling regional average with a live downstream slot is not success, and no terminal adaptation is figure-licensed. That is the residue worth sending. `R_T` was **not** measured numerically, because no ground reading exists; it is recorded qualitatively as the enumerated set of dropped constraints. Treating "the formula is the condensed version of the metalaws" as identity would set `R_T` to zero and would be the dead-reckoning error Fig 4 :30 names.
