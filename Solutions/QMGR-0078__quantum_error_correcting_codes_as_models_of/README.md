## What is deliberately not here

- No personal emails of named individuals.
- No ranking of people by how likely they are to believe the claim.
- No bulk-send plan.

Three reasons, in the figures' own words:

1. **A room is not an anchor.** Fig 7 :35: "A room's agreement is variation, not a verdict. Selection between rooms is where the ground votes."
2. **Correlation disqualifies a verdict.** Fig 2 :40: "disqualifying for a verdict: agreement about the world is worthless when the agreeing frames share an evidence pool." Selecting recipients for receptiveness manufactures exactly the correlation that makes their agreement worthless.
3. **Asking for trust before checking spends independence.** Fig 10 :42: "A verifier that can reconstruct the claim cannot verify it." Show the full framework before the verdict and the reader has joined the frame being checked.

Legally: unsolicited bulk email to scraped personal addresses breaches GDPR and CAN-SPAM style rules in many jurisdictions and blacklists the sending domain. Public opt-in channels avoid that failure as well as the structural one.
