## 0. The question in effect terms

> Can a quantum error-correcting code serve as a model of holographic bulk-boundary correspondence?

Option A: assert that a QEC code *is* the mechanism of AdS/CFT, that the tensor network is the bulk geometry. Option B: keep the figure-derived shape and refuse the identity.

What each leads to:

- **A** asserts a name and an equivalence the framework does not supply. It reads a projection (a tensor network that reproduces an area law) as the source (the mechanism of quantum gravity), which *The Hard Problem is About Source* names as the metamerism error. It also spends the independence of the people asked to check it, because they are handed a conclusion before a residual.
- **B** offers a checkable shape, written refuters, and an open slot until a ground settles it. It records where the code came from (an import from the information frame) rather than pretending it was derived inside gravity.

**The residual R, stated as an effect.** The bulk frame (a (d+1)-dimensional gravity theory in anti-de Sitter space: local bulk operators, extremal surfaces, an area law) and the boundary frame (a d-dimensional CFT: unitary, no manifest bulk locality) each work in their own domain and agree on the dictionary. What fails to resolve is *how* the bulk and boundary compose: radial commutativity and subregion duality are, in Harlow's words, "somewhat mysterious from the CFT point of view." The residual is the missing rule about what crosses between the two scales, plus the loss on the round trip. It is a **union compound** (Fig 9 :54), not one hard question: a case in two conditions is not a harder case of either (Fig 9 :57).

An answer would look like this, bound as an ordinary term before the run (formula step "Direction"): *a directional, lossy, redundant encoding with local recoverability and an area-like cost, sourced from a frame whose closure differs from gravity, that predicts the shape of the bulk-boundary map without naming the unique CFT or bulk.* Write that down now; it is refusable by the same test as everything else.

## 1. Domain census

**R by arity, symmetry and scale.**

- **Arity:** two frames plus a map. Bulk and boundary are the operands; the encoding and recovery maps are the typed relation between them. Not a single quantity with two readings.
- **Symmetry:** directional and many-to-one. Many boundary subregions can represent one bulk operator; the encoding is an isometry only on a code subspace; the map is not a symmetric equivalence at one level. Bulk and boundary are different sizes.
- **Scale:** boundary is d-dimensional, bulk is (d+1)-dimensional. This puts the governing law in Fig 4 (membrane between scales), not in Fig 12/13's same-stratum composition. Testing that placement is finding F1.

**One line per domain (actors / currency / anchor). No nameable anchor means it is not yet a domain.**

| domain | actors | currency | anchor | partition or union |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| D1 framework-internal composition | six terms, bonds, closure(Γ) | composition then settlement | the figures as metalaws; the ground for any measurement | partition: what may cross the two kinds and the two sizes |
| D2 physics / holography | bulk operators, extremal surfaces, boundary subregions, entanglement entropy | checkable predictions (RT formula, entanglement-wedge reconstruction, erasure threshold) | mathematical consistency and toy-model calculation; no direct experiment for AdS/CFT | **union compound**: bulk locality and boundary unitarity are two conditions |
| D3 quantum information / coding | logical qubits, physical qubits, encoding isometry, error sets, recovery maps | correctability and erasure threshold | the Knill-Laflamme type conditions and coding theorems, a proof-checker ground | import source: a closure that differs from gravity |

**Order by dependency (Fig 3).** Describe R first (which link fails: radial commutativity, subregion duality, the area law), then ask what may cross the membrane (Fig 4), then check whether the QEC closure predicts that shape (Fig 8 :53), then measure the round trip (Fig 4 :30). Effort spent optimising a code before the residual is described lands at a step that cannot use it (Fig 3 :20-21).

**Census result.** Partition inside D1, union across D2 to D3. Unbundle before searching: dictionary/encoding, emergence of geometry and dynamics, and robustness/error correction are three conditions that QEC does not clear with one lever. This is finding F6.

## 3. Findings

### F1 - Bulk-boundary is a membrane between two sizes, not a same-level bond (governing finding)

Fig 4 :21: "The membrane is the boundary between the sizes, and the rule about what may cross it. Not a wall: a filter with a direction."

Fig 4 :20: "Only the actors, the currency and the anchor change."

Fig 10 :68: "Different actors, a different currency, a different ground, and a lossy transform between."

The bulk is the larger size, the boundary the smaller. The kernel (the loop and the rule for naming what worked) is the same on both; what changes is actors, currency and anchor. So the thing a code must model is *the rule about what may cross a directional filter between sizes*. Fig 13 :75 ("Two states never bond directly. It is not space-time but space, a structure, time; and two structures join only through a state.") governs composition inside one size and is **not** the law for a bulk-boundary pair. Reading it as law here was my first draft and is recorded as a retraction in §6.

- **Mode:** `general` on the structural shape.
- **Status:** triangulated. Fig 4 (membrane / round trip) and Fig 10 (levels are not copies) are two low-overlap passages that agree. The expanse of the two sizes is physics, not a figure line.
- **Refuter:** show the bulk-boundary relation is a same-level equivalence with no lossy directional transform, i.e., that the two are one frame wearing two names (Fig 8 :49). If that holds, Fig 4 is the wrong figure and this finding falls.

### F2 - Generators cross up, head starts come down

Fig 4 :22: "Only methods cross up. Only head starts come down."

Fig 4 :25: "A recording carried upward looks like knowledge and is a description of one occasion."

Applied here: the bulk-to-boundary encoding (an isometry from logical to physical degrees of freedom) is the upward generator; boundary-to-bulk reconstruction is the head start sent down. It is a method for rebuilding bulk operators from boundary subregions, never a recording of one particular bulk state. Pastawski et al state exactly this shape: "The entire tensor network is an encoder for a quantum error-correcting code, where the bulk and boundary degrees of freedom may be identified as logical and physical degrees of freedom respectively." A tensor network presented as a *picture of geometry* rather than an *encoder* is the recording error Fig 4 :25 names.

- **Mode:** `specified`, triangulated (Fig 4 :22-25 with Fig 10 :68-69: "a generator crosses, an instance does not").
- **Refuter:** exhibit a bulk-boundary correspondence in which the boundary holds only a fixed recording of bulk states with no reusable reconstruction method. Then it is not a generator and the code analogy fails.

### F3 - The map is lossy; measure the round-trip gap

Fig 4 :27: "The transform between scales loses information, and that is the point."

Fig 4 :30: "So take the round trip and measure the gap. Without it you are navigating by dead reckoning: confident about a position nobody checked."

Fig 31 continues: "The gap is not an error to eliminate. It is the measurement of what the coarser description cannot hold". Formula step 6 defines `R_T = gap(x, (T_E ∘ T_A)(x))`. The round trip here is operational: encode a bulk operator (T_A), reconstruct it from a boundary subregion (T_E), compare. The gap is nonzero unless the operator lies inside that subregion's entanglement wedge. Harlow's quantum-corrected RT formula, `S(ρ_A) = Tr(ρ L_A) + S_bulk(ρ_E_A)`, gives the area term that prices the gap, and the reconstruction theorem tells us which operators come back. This is computable in the HaPPY toy models, so it is a real instrument, not a metaphor.

- **Mode:** `specified`, triangulated (Fig 4 :30-32 with the formula's `R_T` and the Symbols table's gap definition).
- **Refuter:** show `R_T = 0` for an operator demonstrably outside the subregion's entanglement wedge, or show the area term cannot be recovered from the code. Either breaks the lossy-map claim.

### F4 - QEC is an import with a source gate and a shadow test, not a derivation from gravity

Fig 6 :23: "No frame escapes its own closure. The bound moves only on contact with a frame whose closure differs."

Fig 6 :14: "the only operator that moves the wall"

Fig 8 :53: "Residual first, frame second."

Fig 6 :12-13 names the gates: "source gate: provenance" and "shadow test: explains a gap". QEC comes from the computation and information frame, whose closure differs from gravity. It can add an atom (the encoding map plus the correctability condition) that expands what the gravity frame can say. The provenance is in the world record: Almheiri, Dong and Harlow proposed the connection in 2014; Pastawski, Yoshida, Harlow and Preskill built the toy models in 2015; Harlow proved the RT formula from QEC in 2016. The shadow test is positive: the import explains radial commutativity, subregion duality and the area law, which is why the papers exist. Convergent derivation and adopted import look identical in the contents (Fig 8 :51), so the dates are the whole issue.

- **Mode:** `specified`, triangulated across three world sources at maximum independence from the framework (different authors, different evidence pool). The *figure* that it is an import is single-source (Fig 6 :23 + Fig 8 :12-14 read together).
- **Refuter:** derive the encoding map from the CFT closure alone, with no imported coding structure. If that is possible, QEC adds no atom and does not move the wall; the import claim falls.

### F5 - The code returns a shape, not the name of the theory

Fig 9 :32: "you get a kind of thing, never the answer itself"

The code predicts the shape of the bulk-boundary map: redundant, locally recoverable, entanglement priced, with an area-like cost. It names neither a unique CFT nor a unique bulk and does not prove AdS/CFT. The authors' own title supplies the check: "Toy models for the bulk/boundary correspondence." The string "toy models" is a provenance label, not a dismissal. Treating a toy model as the mechanism is naming from a projection, the metamerism error in *The Hard Problem is About Source*: "the instrument is speaking only about what it measured, it cannot comment on the source."

- **Mode:** `specified`, triangulated (Fig 9 :32 with the canonical README's "a shape, not a name" and the authors' own "toy models" wording).
- **Refuter:** single out a unique CFT and bulk pair from code structure alone, with no extra measurement or import. Then the framework returned a name and this finding is false.

### F6 - It is a union; unbundle before searching

Fig 9 :54: "And when the split lands on a union rather than a partition, unbundle before you search again."

Fig 9 :57: "a case in two conditions is not a harder case of either"

The bulk-boundary problem is a union of at least three conditions: (a) the dictionary or encoding, (b) the emergence of bulk geometry and dynamics, (c) robustness under erasure and error. QEC clears (a) and (c) in the toy models and the general theorems. It does not, by itself, deliver (b): the emergence of the Einstein equations from the boundary. Fig 9 :58 says the residue is "a specific compound", not a harder case of the cleared ones. So after importing the code, the remaining question is categorically different and gets its own loop. Claiming QEC solves "holography" bundles conditions and hides the residue.

- **Mode:** `specified`, single-source for the union rule (Fig 9 :54-58), corroborated by the literature split between reconstruction results and the separate dynamics programme.
- **Refuter:** show the code structure also yields bulk dynamics without a further import. Then the three conditions were one and the unbundling was wrong.

## 4. Pre-registered refuter

Required before any outreach. Seat Map :287: "a falsifier written before the run is a design instrument, not only an honesty device."

The shape claim is refuted if any of the following holds:

1. **Wrong figure.** The bulk-boundary relation is a same-level equivalence with no lossy directional transform, so Fig 4 is not the governing membrane (breaks F1).
2. **Recording, not generator.** A correspondence exists in which the boundary holds only a fixed recording of bulk states, with no reusable reconstruction method (breaks F2).
3. **Zero gap off the wedge.** `R_T = 0` for an operator outside a subregion's entanglement wedge, or the area term cannot be recovered from the code (breaks F3).
4. **No import.** The encoding map is derived from the CFT closure alone with no imported coding atom (breaks F4), or no source gate or shadow test can be supplied.
5. **A name, not a shape.** A unique CFT and bulk pair is singled out from code structure with no extra measurement (breaks F5).
6. **One condition, not a union.** The code structure yields bulk dynamics without a further import (breaks F6).
7. **Indistinguishable slots.** A single transform collapses the five live residuals into one (breaks F8).

If any fires, withdraw the shape claim. Until one is run, no part of this may be labelled `grounded`.

## 7. Updated verdict

The shape survives: the bulk-boundary correspondence is a directional, lossy membrane (Fig 4), and a QEC code is a provenanced import from a different closure whose encoding and recovery maps realize that membrane's rule, with a measurable round-trip gap and a shape-not-a-name limit. What does not survive is the identity claim: a code does not prove AdS/CFT, does not name the unique bulk or boundary, and does not deliver bulk dynamics without a further import (union, Fig 9 :54). Strains after re-census: **none hidden**; the one live qualification is that AdS/CFT has no direct experimental anchor, so the world-side support is mathematical and toy-model based, which is recorded rather than upgraded to `grounded`.
