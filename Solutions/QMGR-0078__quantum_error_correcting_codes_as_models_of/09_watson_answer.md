## VERDICT (from the figures)

The shape survives; the identity claim does not. The bulk-boundary correspondence is a directional, lossy membrane between two sizes (Fig 4), and a QEC code is a provenanced import from a different closure whose encoding and recovery maps realise that membrane's rule, with a measurable round-trip gap and a shape-not-a-name limit (Fig 9 :32). What does not survive: a code does not prove AdS/CFT, does not name a unique bulk or boundary, and does not deliver bulk dynamics without a further import (union, Fig 9 :54). This is a `specified`, ungrounded result. AdS/CFT has no direct experimental anchor, so the world-side support is mathematical and toy-model based and is recorded as such, never upgraded to `grounded`.

- cost if wrong: bounded and locatable. The packet is mode-labelled, states its refuters before its conclusion and records the lineage, so an error is findable rather than invisible. The real cost is spending the independence of outside readers by sending a recording (a conclusion) instead of a generator (a checkable residual and refuter). Fig 2 :42 prices that cost and recommends buying one careful outside frame rather than a crowd of believers. The cost is avoidable by leading with the shape claim and its refuter.
- undoable: yes. The identity claim is removable without touching the derivation; `07_the_tether_answer.md` and `08_expert_verification_packet.md` already remove it. The import stays where it is and no primitive has to be withdrawn. A sent claim can be superseded by the corrected packet.
- reversed by: a ground reading, not agreement. Seven refuters were written in `01_figure_grounded_audit.md` §4 before this conclusion. Any one of them firing reverses the verdict: the bulk-boundary relation shown to be a same-level equivalence with no lossy directional transform; a correspondence where the boundary holds only a fixed recording with no reusable reconstruction method; R_T = 0 for an operator outside the entanglement wedge or the area term not recoverable from the code; the encoding map derived from the CFT closure alone with no imported coding atom; a unique CFT and bulk pair singled out from code structure with no extra measurement; the code structure yielding bulk dynamics without a further import; or a single transform collapsing the five live residuals into one. A figure re-census that places bulk and boundary at one size would also reverse it. Until one of these fires, the verdict stands as candidate.
