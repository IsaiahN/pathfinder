## Why these sources, in figure terms

1. **The world sources are low overlap with the framework.** Harlow, Pastawski, Almheiri, Ryu and Takayanagi are different authors publishing in physics venues. They are not Isaiah's essays. Their agreement with the figure-derived shape is genuine corroboration from a different evidence pool, not a pool talking to itself (Fig 2 :40: "disqualifying for a verdict: agreement about the world is worthless when the agreeing frames share an evidence pool"). Their agreement is still secondary: figures decide the verdict.
2. **The essays are high overlap and declared as such.** All five essays are by the framework's author. They govern the meaning of "shape" and the simulation caveat, not the truth of the holography claim. Their agreement with the figures is expected and carries little independent weight.
3. **Bekenstein and holography are a retrodiction, not a produced result.** The canonical article records the Bekenstein and holography entries as instances the framework describes, arrived at independently. Harlow's 2016 theorem is a world result that the figure-derived shape fits; it is not a prediction the framework issued in advance. Do not present it as one.
