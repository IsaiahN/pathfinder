# Expert verification packet: the Tether on QEC codes as models of holography

**What this is:** a claim in a form a holography researcher can check. Mode, derivation and refuters are stated before the conclusion, so a reader knows what to look for and what would settle it.

**Mode: `specified`, not `grounded`.** No experiment on AdS/CFT is claimed. This is a shape and a route, not a result. It is a simulated derivation checked for internal consistency against the figures, not a ground reading; it cannot settle the question it reports on.

## 1. The claim, in one paragraph

Bulk and boundary are two sizes joined by a directional, lossy membrane, not one theory at one level. A quantum error-correcting code supplies the crossing rule: an isometry from logical (bulk) to physical (boundary) degrees of freedom, with reconstruction of bulk operators from boundary subregions and a nonzero round-trip gap off the entanglement wedge. The code is a provenanced import from the information frame whose closure differs from gravity, and it passes a shadow test on radial commutativity, subregion duality and the area law. It returns the shape of the bulk-boundary map, not the name of a unique CFT, bulk or mechanism; the emergence of bulk dynamics is a separate condition in a union that this import does not clear.

## 2. Three checkable sub-claims

### C1: framework-internal, checkable by reading Figure 4 and Figure 10

- **Statement:** bulk-boundary is a membrane between two sizes, directional and lossy; generators cross up and head starts come down.
- **Source, verbatim:** Fig 4 :21: "The membrane is the boundary between the sizes, and the rule about what may cross it. Not a wall: a filter with a direction." Fig 4 :22: "Only methods cross up. - **Refuter:** show the bulk-boundary relation is a same-level equivalence with no lossy directional transform, so Fig 4 is the wrong figure; or exhibit a correspondence where the boundary holds only a fixed recording of bulk states with no reusable reconstruction method (Fig 4 :25, "A recording carried upward looks like knowledge and is a description of one occasion").

### C2: physics-facing, checkable by a holography researcher against the three papers

- **Statement:** the code is an import that passes a shadow test and yields a measurable round-trip gap. Fig 6 :23: "No frame escapes its own closure. The bound moves only on contact with a frame whose closure differs." Fig 8 :53: "Residual first, frame second." Fig 4 :30: "So take the round trip and measure the gap. - **What to check:** is the provenance right (Almheiri, Dong and Harlow 2014; Pastawski, Yoshida, Harlow and Preskill 2015; Harlow 2016)? Does Harlow's theorem really give a quantum-corrected RT formula in any QEC code with the relevant recovery properties? Is `R_T` nonzero for an operator outside the entanglement wedge in the toy models?
- **Refuter:** show `R_T = 0` off the wedge, or that the area term cannot be recovered from the code; derive the encoding from the CFT closure alone with no imported coding structure; or show no source gate or shadow test can be supplied.

### C3: framework-internal derivation, checkable by reading Figure 9 and Figure 12

- **What to check:** does the HaPPY paper itself call these "toy models" (it does), and does Harlow state a conditional theorem about codes rather than a claim that gravity is a code (he does)? Does the QEC import deliver bulk dynamics, or only encoding and robustness?
- **Refuter:** single out a unique CFT and bulk pair from code structure with no extra measurement; or show the code structure yields bulk dynamics without a further import (then the union unbundling is wrong); or break the isomer claim as specified in `06_isomers_and_order.md` Q1.

## 3. What is explicitly not claimed

- Not that a tensor network *is* spacetime, nor that QEC proves AdS/CFT. The HaPPY authors' own "toy models" wording is the provenance label.
- Not a unique CFT, a unique bulk, or a named microscopic mechanism. Fig 9 :32 governs here.
- Not bulk dynamics or the Einstein equations. That is the uncleared condition of the union (Fig 9 :54).
- Not a seventh primitive. QEC is an import with a source gate, not a new member of the six-term set.
- Not `grounded`. Nothing here is asserted as settled by experiment.
- Not a claim that the second seat's agreement corroborates the verdict. It shares the evidence pool (Fig 2 :40).

## 5. What would settle it

A source-discriminating ground, not a further argument. From *The Hard Problem is About Source*: an instrument that reads green "cannot comment on the source." Here:

1. **Round-trip test:** measure the gap for a bulk operator outside a subregion's entanglement wedge. Zero breaks the lossy-map claim.
2. **Area test:** recover the area term from the code, or show it also arises with no tensor-network arrangement. The first supports, the second breaks the bond claim (Fig 12 :94).
3. **Import test:** derive the encoding from the CFT closure alone, or show no gate can be supplied.
4. **Union test:** derive bulk dynamics from the code without a further import.
