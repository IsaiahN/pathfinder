# The Tether's answer on QEC codes as models of holographic bulk-boundary correspondence

**Mode: `specified`, not `grounded`.** This is a shape and a route, not a result. Simulated derivation checked against the figures (`05_what_shape_means.md` §6), not a ground reading.

## 1. The residual it answers

The bulk (gravity in anti-de Sitter space) and the boundary (a CFT) agree on the dictionary and fail to compose in a way the boundary frame can derive: radial commutativity and subregion duality are "somewhat mysterious from the CFT point of view" (Harlow 2016). The residual is the missing rule about what crosses between the two sizes, plus the loss on the round trip. It is a **union compound**, not one hard question: "a case in two conditions is not a harder case of either" (Fig 9 :57). The three bundled conditions are encoding, the emergence of dynamics, and robustness under erasure.

## 2. The shape

1. **Bulk and boundary are two sizes joined by a directional, lossy filter.** Fig 4 :21: "The membrane is the boundary between the sizes, and the rule about what may cross it. Not a wall: a filter with a direction." Fig 4 :20: "Only the actors, the currency and the anchor change." The kernel is the same on both sides; what differs is actors, currency and anchor.
2. **A code supplies the crossing rule, not a picture of the bulk.** Fig 4 :22: "Only methods cross up. Only head starts come down." The encoding isometry carries logical (bulk) information up to physical (boundary) degrees of freedom; recovery is the head start sent down. Fig 4 :25: "A recording carried upward looks like knowledge and is a description of one occasion." A tensor network presented as geometry rather than as an encoder is that recording error.
3. **The map is lossy and the gap is measurable.** Fig 4 :30: "So take the round trip and measure the gap. Without it you are navigating by dead reckoning: confident about a position nobody checked." Encode a bulk operator, reconstruct from a boundary subregion, compare. The gap is nonzero off the entanglement wedge, and the area term in Harlow's quantum-corrected RT formula prices it. This is computed in the HaPPY toy models.
4. **QEC is a sourced import, not a derivation from gravity.** Fig 6 :23: "No frame escapes its own closure. The bound moves only on contact with a frame whose closure differs." Fig 8 :53: "Residual first, frame second." The provenance is 2014 (Almheiri, Dong and Harlow), 2015 (Pastawski, Yoshida, Harlow and Preskill), 2016 (Harlow), with a source gate and a positive shadow test on radial commutativity, subregion duality and the area law.
5. **The code returns a shape, not a name.** Fig 9 :32: "you get a kind of thing, never the answer itself." It licenses neither a unique CFT, nor a unique bulk, nor the mechanism of AdS/CFT. The authors' own phrase "toy models" is the provenance label that keeps this honest.
6. **The union does not close with one import.** Fig 9 :54: "And when the split lands on a union rather than a partition, unbundle before you search again." QEC clears encoding and robustness; bulk dynamics remains a categorically different compound with its own loop.

## 3. What it explicitly does not say

- Not that a tensor network *is* spacetime. The area law is a reading; naming its source is the metamerism error *The Hard Problem is About Source* describes.
- Not that QEC proves AdS/CFT, nor that it names a unique bulk or boundary.
- Not that it derives bulk dynamics or the Einstein equations. That is the uncleared condition of the union.
- Not a seventh primitive. QEC is an import from the information frame, with provenance, not a new member of the six-term set.
- Not `grounded`. No experiment settles AdS/CFT itself; the supporting results are mathematical and toy-model based, and are recorded as such.

## 4. What would settle it: the ground

A source-discriminating instrument or a proof checker, not a further argument.

- **Round-trip test:** measure `R_T` for a bulk operator outside a boundary subregion's entanglement wedge. Zero gap breaks the lossy-map claim.
- **Area test:** show the area term cannot be recovered from the code's entanglement structure, or that it can be recovered from a code with no tensor-network arrangement (then the bond carries nothing, Fig 12 :94).
- **Import test:** derive the encoding map from the CFT closure alone with no imported coding structure. Then QEC adds no atom and does not move the wall.
- **Union test:** show the code structure also yields bulk dynamics without a further import. Then the three conditions were one and the unbundling was wrong.
- **Standing falsifiers:** the full list of seven is in `01_figure_grounded_audit.md` §4, written before this conclusion.

## 5. The honest stop

**Unreached, not unreachable.** Fig 8 :39: "So a search that finds nothing is never a proof of absence." Underivable-in-principle and unreached-at-this-budget are different facts with different instructions (Fig 8 :40-43). The framework stops at the limit of what the available instruments distinguish and records the lineage. Fig 13: "Unreachable is a verdict here, not a silence" and "Indiscernibility is not identity." The geometry-emergence residue stays open; prestige or agreement is not an instrument that closes it.

## 6. One-paragraph version for outreach

> The Tether does not claim that a quantum error-correcting code proves AdS/CFT or that a tensor network is spacetime. It says that bulk and boundary are two sizes joined by a directional, lossy membrane; that a code supplies the crossing rule, an isometry up and a reconstruction head start down, with a measurable round-trip gap off the entanglement wedge; that the code is a provenanced import from the information frame which passes a shadow test on radial commutativity, subregion duality and the area law; and that a code and an ordinary code over the same qubits are isomers, same atoms arranged differently. It refuses to name the unique bulk or boundary, because the framework returns a shape, not a name. What remains open is the emergence of bulk dynamics, a separate condition in a union. What it offers is that shape, seven falsifiers written in advance, and the instruments that would close it.
