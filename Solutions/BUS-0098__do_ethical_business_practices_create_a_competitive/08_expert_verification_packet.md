## 1. The claim, in one paragraph

The question "do ethical practices create an advantage, or are they obligations regardless of return" is not one question with opposite answers. It is two conditions with two different anchors, and they must be kept separate. The pay condition is a relation between firm and habitat: a practice keeps paying while the firm's channel to a ground that still rewards it stays reachable, while its reach is renewed by contact with ways of working that differ from its own, and while free riders can be detected. A practice that can be copied adds no atom, and its value can flip sign when the market changes, so the same code can be trust in one setting and rigidity in another. The obligation condition is an anchor-selection claim: where the question is constitutively about harm to persons, the market return is an instrument and not the verdict, so "regardless of return" does not assert that ethics never pays. An ethics commitment with no detection or breach cost is a convention, not a grounded obligation. Reputation and ratings are the frame scoring itself. Which specific practice pays in a given market is not claimed; it is a `gap` at the instrument boundary.

## 2. Three checkable sub-claims

### C1 - Framework-internal; checkable by reading Figures 2, 9 and 11

- **Statement:** the either/or is a union of two conditions with different anchors; capability and valence are properties of firm-and-habitat and of the residual, not standing properties of a practice.
- **Source, verbatim:**
  - Fig 9 :57 - *"What remains is smaller and it is also categorically different: a case in two conditions is not a harder case of either."*
  - Fig 2 :29 - *"An anchor is whatever settles the question and does not change its answer because of what any frame thinks."* The same line names *"a market"* as an instance.
  - Fig 2 :36 - *"An anchor is legitimate when the question is constitutively about it."*; Fig 2 :37 - *"Human judgment is the right anchor for is this a good answer for a person, because the question is about people."*
  - Fig 11 :39 - *"So the answerable question is not what intelligence is, but what makes a system capable. And capability is a property of agent-and-habitat, never of the agent alone."*
  - Fig 11 :49 - *"An actor’s valence is relative to the residual, not a property it carries."*
- **What a researcher can check against their own knowledge:** Donaldson and Preston (1995) distinguish the instrumental aspect, which links stakeholder management to corporate goals, from the normative aspect, which states *"moral or philosophical guidelines for the operation and management of the corporation"*. Do those not name two anchors rather than two predictions? Goodpaster's paradox calls the two collapses *"business without ethics"* and *"ethics without business"*. A "no" to both is a real refutation of the mapping.
- **Refuter:** a case where the market return alone legitimately settles whether a practice is right, with the question not about harm to any person; or a practice whose measured contribution to return is invariant across a habitat substitution that removes the market's payment. Either fires against C1.

### C2 - Framework-internal derivation plus a business-facing check

- **Statement:** internal optimisation cannot add a practice's missing reach; renewal requires contact with a closure that differs and repeated interaction with cheater detection; a costless signal is not a reliable one.
- **Source, verbatim:**
  - Fig 8 :32 - *"Closure is idempotent. Composing inside a closed set never leaves it, so no search, however long, adds an atom. This one is definitional: it is what closure means."*
  - Fig 6 :23 - *"No frame escapes its own closure. The bound moves only on contact with a frame whose closure differs."*
  - Fig 1 :27 - *"They are frame-internal. A frame cannot score itself with a quantity it also produces."*
  - Fig 10 :24 - *"the ground does not decay, the channel does"*; Fig 10 :26 - *"saturated metrics, pooled readings"*
- **What a researcher can check against their own knowledge:** the opened CSR page reports Reinhardt's (1998) result that a CSR-based strategy *"could only sustain an abnormal return if it could prevent competitors from imitating it"*. It also reports that a 2000 study found CSR neutral when properly specified and that innovation, not CSR, drove earlier positive findings. Does that not say exactly that an internal practice cannot move the wall by itself and that a reading cannot name its source? The opened Reciprocal altruism page adds that altruism needs *"A mechanism for detecting 'cheaters' must exist."* and repeated opportunities. A "no" to all three is a real refutation of the mapping.
- **Refuter:** a firm that renews an ethics-based advantage purely by internal composition, with no new atom, no repeated interaction and no cheater detection, across a habitat change; or a costless ethics signal that reliably changes what the ground pays with cheap imitators present. Either breaks idempotence and C2 with it.

### C3 - Structure, obligation and competing explanations

- **Statement:** obligation is a negation aimed at the market's standing to decide a question about persons, and it must be installable as something that can be violated; two firms with the same code but different bonds are isomers, so a code list alone cannot predict outcomes; the union must be unbundled before a lever is chosen.
- **Source, verbatim:**
  - Fig 10 :18 - *"a declared meaning is not a grounded one"*; Fig 10 :19 - *"→ install what can be violated"*; Fig 10 :47 - *"a convention that decides an outcome has stopped being one"*
  - Fig 12 :117 - *"isomer. Same atoms, different bond, different substance. The difference is invisible in the formula and decisive in the behaviour."*
  - Fig 12 :5 - *"A set of ingredients is not one. The bond is the rest of the information, and it is the half nothing was recording."*
  - Fig 9 :32 - *"you get a kind of thing, never the answer itself"*
- **Refuter:** an ethics commitment whose breach has no possible detection or cost yet which changes the firm's R against a non-updating ground; or two firms with identical atoms and identical bonds whose persistence diverges with no difference in contact or imported atoms, which would move the explanation out of composition and into habitat alone; or one intervention that restores both conditions with no categorically different residue.

## 3. What is explicitly not claimed

- Not `grounded`. No measurement has paid for any part of this.
- Not a theory of everything about business, and no final or unique theory claim.
- Not that ethics never pays, and not that it always pays. Both are standing-property claims.
- Not an average. No claim about the percentage of variance attributable to ethics, stakeholders, strategy or year.
- Not that duties, codes, stakeholder relationships or leadership are irrelevant. They are Γ and D2; the claim is that neither Γ nor the market alone settles the question.
- Not that any specific practice is the answer. That is a `gap` at the instrument boundary, not a hidden answer.
- Not that the strategy and business-ethics literature independently corroborates this. Donaldson and Preston, Freeman, Goodpaster, the CSR and Friedman pages share one evidence pool. ## 5. What would settle it

A prospective, per-firm study that measures C1 and C2 separately, records bonds as well as atoms, states which anchor is used for each verdict, and reports which condition failed before the decline. Concretely:

1. **Pay test:** does the firm's reading of what pays degrade while the market keeps paying someone? Indicators: stale percepts, saturated metrics, pooled readings (Fig 10 :25-26).
2. **Import and detection test:** did any new atom enter the firm's reach from a closure that differs, and is there a way to detect free riders? If not, C2 of the pay condition is refuted.
3. **Violation test:** what does it cost the firm to breach its own code? If the answer is nothing detectable, the commitment is a convention (Fig 10 :18, :47).
4. **Source-discriminating instrument:** can the study distinguish an ethical practice from a protected resource, a paying habitat and luck when all four produce the same profit reading? The source essay says a reading alone *"cannot comment on the source"*.
