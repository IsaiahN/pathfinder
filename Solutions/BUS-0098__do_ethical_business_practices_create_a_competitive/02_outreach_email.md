# Outreach draft - validate the output first, then decide about the theory

**Use:** one to one, or as a cover note attached to a submission through a public venue in `03_public_contact_points.csv`. **Not** for bulk send. No personal emails are used; the recipient must have an opt-in or public institutional route.

**Hard precondition:** the stage-1 output must survive the recipient's check before any theory is shown. The output here is a `specified` union claim with no ground measurement, so the legitimate ask is a refutation of the shape, never endorsement of it. If the recipient cannot be shown a failure condition they can actually test, do not send.

---

**Subject:** Check one claim about business ethics before you decide whether any of this is worth reading

> Hi [name],
>
> I have one narrow claim about business ethics that I would like you to check against what you already know. I am not asking you to read a theory first, and I am not asking you to agree with anything. I am asking for a failure. If the claim does not survive, there is nothing else to look at.
>
> **The claim (`specified`, not `grounded`):** the question "do ethical practices create an advantage, or are they obligations regardless of return" is not one question with two opposite answers. It is two conditions with two different anchors, and they have to be kept separate.
>
> 1. **The pay condition.** Whether a practice pays is a relation between the firm and its market, not a property of the practice. The market is the thing that settles that question. A practice keeps paying while the firm's reading of what the market rewards stays reachable and not pooled into an average, and while the firm keeps taking in something new from situations and competitors whose ways of working differ from its own. A practice that can be copied does not add anything to what the firm can reach. And the practice's value can flip sign when the market changes, so the same code can be the source of trust in one setting and a rigid ritual in another.
> 2. **The obligation condition.** Whether a practice is right is a different question. When it is constitutively about harm to people, the market return is an instrument, not the verdict. "Regardless of return" selects the anchor; it does not assert that ethics never pays. An ethics code that costs nothing when breached is a declared convention, not an obligation that changes anything.
>
> These are two different failure modes with two different instruments. A firm that loses the market's payment needs a different check from a firm that has let its obligation become a slogan. A blended ethics score destroys the diagnostic.
>
> **What I am not claiming:** that I can name which practice pays in a given market, that any particular firm or industry is a case in point, that this is settled by evidence, or that it is the only account of business ethics. I am offering a shape that any candidate answer would have to have. A shape is not a name. I am also not claiming that ethics never pays, or that stakeholder language is wrong. Those are names from one literature, recorded as bindings, not verdicts.
>
> **What would refute it, written down before I sent this:**
> 1. a case where the market return alone legitimately settles whether a practice is right, and the question is not about harm to any person;
> 2. a practice's measured contribution to return is unchanged when the market stops paying for what it does;
> 3. an ethics commitment whose breach has no possible detection or cost still changes what the market pays;
> 4. a costless ethics signal reliably changes what the market pays even with cheap imitators present;
> 5. a firm renews an ethics-based advantage purely from inside its own routines, with nothing new from outside, no repeated interaction and no way to detect cheaters, across a change of market;
> 6. one intervention restores both conditions in both failure types and leaves nothing categorically different behind.
>
> If any one holds, I withdraw the claim. A single line saying which one holds and why is more useful to me than agreement. If you would rather not engage, no reply is needed.
>
> **If it survives, and only then:** I can send the three public sources below. The figures, not my summary of them, are the authority.
>
> - Figure text with line numbers (I can point to the exact lines): the local canonical figure text in this solution folder.
> - Formula and usage rule: the `figures` folder on the public `general-agent` branch of the `tether` repository.
> - The essays on source, search shape and the prestige/action separation: my Substack archive.
>
> I will send URLs only after you tell me what you would count as a failure.
>
> **If you want to use the machinery:** the formula is public. Run it on a problem you know well and can check against a real ground. Write down what would refute your result *before* you run it and state the mode of what you get. A reported failure is more useful to me than a reported success.
>
> Thanks,
> [name]

## Why this version is ordered this way

| the move | figure-grounded reason |
|---|---|
| Residual and refuter first, theory last | *"Bring a characterised residual, never the source."* (Fig 10 :52). The recipient checks the claim without holding the framework's interior |
| Do not ask for agreement | *"Alignment is triangulation, not negotiation. A mirror cannot correct you."* (Fig 2 :35) |
| Two conditions kept separate | *"a case in two conditions is not a harder case of either"* (Fig 9 :57); a blended score is a pooled reading (Fig 10 :26) |
| Anchor stated for each condition | *"An anchor is legitimate when the question is constitutively about it."* (Fig 2 :36) |
| Pre-registered refuter | *"a falsifier written before the run is a design instrument, not only an honesty device"* |
| Mode stated on the claim | *"Mode. general · specified · grounded. State which."* (`THE_FORMULA.md`) |
| No name for the winning practice | *"you get a kind of thing, never the answer itself"* (Fig 9 :32) |
| Links only after a verdict | Fig 10 :42, *"A verifier that can reconstruct the claim cannot verify it."* Once the recipient has the framework, their later agreement is not corroboration (Fig 2 :40) |
| Prestige kept out of the ask | *"Prestige can fund an action. It can give an action a budget to be tried. But it cannot settle an action. Only the ground can settle it."* (*The Recursive Self-Improvement Trap*, read 2026-11-22) and *"They are frame-internal. A frame cannot score itself with a quantity it also produces."* (Fig 1 :27) |
| Offer the formula as a method, not an answer | *"Only methods cross up. Only head starts come down."* (Fig 4 :22) |
| Ask the user for their own refuter | Formula, "Direction": a frame cannot check the direction of its own question; make the sought-for shape an ordinary term |

## Sending rules

- One recipient at a time. No distribution lists, no mail-merge, no forwarding chain.
- Use only a public institutional contact, a call for submissions, or an address the person has published themselves. Record the source and date.
- A Track B style reply is a conversation, never corroboration. If a recipient returns a refutation, copy it into `11_audit.md` with provenance and have the auditor seat re-evaluate it as if it came from any other source.
- Do not follow up with the theory after a "no". An interest in the outcome is unavoidable; an interest in the reading is disqualifying (Fig 10 :59).
