## 2. What the wider literature bears on the question

**One evidence pool, one witness.** The Wikipedia business-ethics, stakeholder-theory, CSR and Friedman pages, together with Donaldson and Preston, Freeman, Goodpaster, Barney-derived CSR work, Reinhardt and McWilliams and Siegel, all draw on the same management-and-business-ethics literature. Per Fig 2 :31, *"Independence, between two frames, is how little they draw on the same evidence."* Their agreement is one witness, not several. The pages disagree with each other about the sign of the CSR-performance relationship, which is evidence for the union and the gap (F1, F7 in `01`), not a second verdict.

- The stakeholder page's instrumental/normative split is the business pool's own name for the two anchors in F1 and F3. Goodpaster's *"business without ethics"* and *"ethics without business"* are the two ways of collapsing the union. They corroborate the shape but carry no independent weight.
- The CSR page's specification dispute is the clearest world-side statement of F7. A single profit reading is consistent with ethics, innovation, a protected habitat, or specification choices. The reading does not name the source.
- Reinhardt's imitability result and the reciprocal-altruism conditions jointly corroborate F5: a practice keeps paying only where it cannot be copied and where there is repeated interaction with detection.
- The corporate-irresponsibility examples (Savar, horse meat) corroborate F3's point that a declared obligation becomes real when a breach has a cost, and that an unbreachable code is a convention.

**Low-overlap witnesses**, each from a different field and each read directly:

1. **Animal behaviour and evolutionary biology (Reciprocal altruism, Trivers 1971).** Cooperation can evolve only where cheaters can be detected and encounters repeat. This is C1 in a different currency, with no shared evidence pool with management strategy. It also shows why "ethical practice pays" is a conditional, not a standing property.
2. **Behavioural economics and evolutionary anthropology (Strong reciprocity).** People cooperate without apparent benefit and punish non-cooperators at cost. The same construct is *"self-regarding when rare... altruistic when common"*, which is Fig 11 :49-51 observed in a different field. The criticism that lab results may not reflect the field is kept visible; it is evidence for the gap, not against the shape.
3. **Evolutionary biology and signalling theory (Cooperation (evolution)).** By-product benefit is not cooperation, and a reliable signal must be costly. These are the biological forms of F4 and F9: two firms can show the same benefit with different bonds, and a costless ethics claim is not a reliable signal.

**Framework-internal essays** are used for the canonical reading of "shape" in `05` only. They are not independent world witnesses, because their author wrote the figures. That overlap is recorded in the ledger.

## 3. Distribution rules

Figure-grounded reasons for how material is offered:

- **Fig 4 :22:** *"Only methods cross up. Only head starts come down."* Send the generator, the residual and the refuter, not the conclusion. A recording carried upward *"looks like knowledge and is a description of one occasion"* (Fig 4 :25).
- **Fig 10 :42:** *"A verifier that can reconstruct the claim cannot verify it."* A recipient who has absorbed the framework before being asked for a verdict has joined the frame being checked.
- **Fig 9 :28:** *"any frame you pick will seem to fit"* if the residual is not described first. Ask what they would expect to see if the union claim were true before showing the source.
- **Fig 2 :40:** agreement from a shared pool is worthless as a verdict. Later agreement about the framework is never pooled as corroboration.
- **Fig 10 :59:** *"an interest in the outcome is unavoidable, an interest in the reading is disqualifying."* Declare the stake; do not ask for belief.

**Two hard practical constraints.**

The AOM Publishing page states: *"Artificial Intelligence: Work submitted to AOM must be created by the authors and not the product of artificial intelligence tools unless appropriate to the research question and properly cited."* The Journal of Business Ethics instructions are in the same direction: *"Large Language Models (LLMs), such as ChatGPT, do not currently satisfy our authorship criteria"* and their use *"should be properly documented in the Methods section"*. This whole solution is a simulated derivation produced with an LLM and is labelled as such everywhere. It must **not** be submitted as an authored article. The legitimate use is as a private checking instrument: post the residual and the refuter, invite a human to run the check, and let that human author whatever they conclude, with disclosure as required by the venue.

**Order in any reply:** (1) the union claim and the pre-registered refuter; (2) the question "what would you count as a failure here?"; (3) only after a verdict is recorded, the public links with the note that the figures, not the summary, are the authority; (4) the formula as a reusable method, with the instruction that each user writes their own refuter and states their own mode.

## 4. The two-stage offer, compressed

**Stage 1.** Send `08_expert_verification_packet.md` sections 1 to 3 only: the one-paragraph claim, the three checkable sub-claims with their verbatim figure lines and refuters, and the non-claims. No provenance bundle, no essay links.

**Stage 2, opt-in only.** If the recipient is curious and has already recorded a verdict on stage 1, offer the public links: the figure text, the formula and usage rule, and the essays. Their later agreement is a conversation, not corroboration (Fig 2 :40).

**Stage 3.** Offer the formula as a method, never as an answer (Fig 4 :22). Each user writes a refuter before running it and states the mode of what they get.

**Stop rule.** If stage 1 fails, the theory is not offered. A failure is more useful than an endorsement.

**Hosting.** Nothing here is hosted or sent by this agent. The human operator hosts the packet on an opt-in page and sends one to one. Prefer a download page that lets a recipient decline contact over an attachment pushed to a list.
