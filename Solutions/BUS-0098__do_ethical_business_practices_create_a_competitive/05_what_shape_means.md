## 1. A shape is a constraint you can state before you can name it

The canonical statement in `FIGURES_README_canonical.md` is that the framework returns *"what a missing thing would have to look like (a shape, not a name)"*, then stops where instruments can no longer distinguish and records the lineage. Fig 9 :32: *"you get a kind of thing, never the answer itself."* Fig 13 :134: *"Unreachable is a verdict here, not a silence,"* and :135: *"It cannot say which primitive is missing, because naming it would mean already holding it."*

From *The Library of Babel*, read directly:

> *"You can cut your search short if the set of books returned is small and descriptive enough that it provides a general shape of the answer that satisfies your question."*

So for BUS-0098 a shape is: **the narrowed form of an answer about ethics and advantage that can be stated before any particular practice, duty, stakeholder theory or firm is named.** It constrains. It does not fill.

- Shape: *an ethical practice pays while its channel to a paying ground stays reachable and its reach is renewed from a differing closure; a question about harm is settled by an anchor constituted for persons, not by the market return; the two conditions are separate.*
- Name, withheld: *this firm wins because of its fair-trade supply chain, its whistleblowing policy, its stakeholder capitalism commitment, or its ethics training programme.*

The distinction matters here because the business-ethics literature is almost entirely in the naming business. Donaldson and Preston name three aspects (descriptive, instrumental, normative); Freeman names stakeholders; Carroll names four responsibilities; Friedman names shareholder primacy. Those are bindings from the management frame onto atoms, not outputs of the loop.

## 2. How the shape was obtained

The essay gives the bootstrapping procedure:

> *"If you don’t know the right terms to search for, you will have to use the existing vocabulary you do know that is closest to the words that you wish to describe and grasp the shape of."*
> *"With each new term gained, you are able to narrow the space by definition and deepen your vocabulary."*

Applied here: the closest available terms were "competitive advantage", "instrumental stakeholder theory", "normative obligation", "social license to operate", "reputation", "reciprocal altruism" and "strong reciprocity". None was used as the answer. Each was treated as a witness to be ranged against the residual, per Fig 8 :53: *"Residual first, frame second."* The description in effect terms (two anchors, one residual per firm) came first; the frames were selected only after that description was fixed.

The essay also names the bundle stop:

> *"Perhaps your problem was a bundle or conflation of several problems masquerading as one? You will need to search all of those problems individually to find their answers and then combine them in a satisfactory way."*

That is the canonical warrant for treating BUS-0098 as a union (Fig 9 :54-57), rather than as one hard question with two answers. Goodpaster's paradox, found in the opened stakeholder page, is the same bundle seen from inside the field: strategic stakeholder synthesis becomes *"business without ethics"*, multi-fiduciary synthesis becomes *"ethics without business"*. Neither is a mistake in the practice; each is a collapse of one condition into the other.

The stop condition:

> *"You will know that you are done because the finish line will also be the starting place, as there is nowhere else to search and nothing else that can describe the answer better."*

In this solution the stop is reached when the two conditions are stated, the anchor for each is named, and the instrument that would discriminate the sources of a profit reading is described. Any further search that only renames the conditions is motion, not progress (Fig 7 :32, *"one long room: motion, no progress"*).

## 3. What licenses a name

Only two things, both outside the shape:

1. **The ground settles it.** Formula step 5: *"the ground settles it. Verified only by what pays. A gate is not the ground."* The README: the framework *"stops at the limit of what the available instruments can distinguish, and records the lineage so the stop is honest."*
2. **A provenanced import from a frame whose closure differs** (Fig 6 :23, Fig 8 :50-52), with a source gate and a shadow test. Fig 8 :50: *"But shared atoms are not the signature. Two frames can hold the same atom by convergence, reaching it separately, which is corroboration at maximum independence."*

For BUS-0098 neither has happened for the specific-practice claim. There is no prospective measurement that pays for "this code caused the decade of advantage", and the business-pool names were adopted without a shadow test. Hence the `gap` row in F10 of `01`.

The low-overlap sources are in a different position. The reciprocal-altruism conditions and the handicap principle are genuine imports from different closures. They are recorded with provenance (author, venue, year in `10_source_ledger.csv`) and they pass a shadow test: they explain why the pay condition depends on repeated interaction and costly signalling, which the framework's channel rule already predicted. They corroborate the mechanism shape, not the firm-level fact.

## 4. The source error is the specific forbidden move here

From *The Hard Problem is About Source*, read directly:

> *"The Problem: You can have several underlying causes that create identical readings but the definition of the output would be read as the same."*
> *"If several paths create the same behavior, then behavior is a projection with lossy information"*
> *"What the Spectrometer Example Reveals: The answer is unreached until a better instrument exists."*

This is the exact error a business-ethics explanation makes when it reads sustained profit and names its source. A high return is a reading. It is consistent with an ethical practice, a protected resource, a favourable habitat, a temporary information asymmetry, an accounting choice, or a monopoly granted by regulation. The reading does not determine the source. The CSR page's own specification dispute is the business pool admitting this: change the model and the sign of "CSR pays" changes. No amount of agreement among readers fixes it. What is owed is an instrument that discriminates sources, which this solution does not have. Fig 6 :30: *"outward: does another frame already hold it? The description travels. Fails by being sharpened into terms only we can read. Cast it in effect terms."*

The essay closes: *"An instrument that can’t distinguish these cases demonstrates its own inadequacy, it cannot claim anything substantial about what is being measured or what causes it."* That is why the expert packet asks for a failure condition before a theory.

## 5. Prestige is not action, applied to business ethics

From *The Recursive Self-Improvement Trap*, read directly:

> *"Prestige must never be allowed to validate an action directly."*
> *"Prestige can fund an action. It can give an action a budget to be tried. But it cannot settle an action. Only the ground can settle it. The Ground Maintainer must be blind to prestige."*
> *"If prestige could buy actions, the system would collapse into a monoculture. The highest-prestige agent would monopolize compute and repeat its past success, and the system would stop exploring."*

The last line is an instance, in the author's own agent system, of Fig 7 :36 (*"A chain of mirrors is one long room"*) and Fig 10 :24 (*"the ground does not decay, the channel does"*). Because it is the author's own design, it is framework-internal corroboration, not an independent witness; it is recorded here as an illustration of the rule, not as evidence that firms behave this way.

Fig 1 :26-27 gives the figure-side version: *"predicates minted, and credibility accrued"* and *"They are frame-internal. A frame cannot score itself with a quantity it also produces."* An ethics award, a CSR rating and a public commitment are all credibility accrued inside the frame. The low-overlap signalling source agrees: *"If a signal is to remain reliable, and generally resistant to falsification, the signal has to be evolutionarily costly."*

For outreach this means: a list of famous business ethicists who agree with the shape would be a monoculture, not a check. The ask gives the claim a budget to be tried by someone outside; it does not solicit endorsement.

## 6. Correction to my own §3 (an overstatement, retained as evidence)

I initially wrote that naming any particular ethical practice is forbidden by "shape, not a name". That statement was too broad and is withdrawn here. The canonical phrase governs a **missing** primitive, one not held in Γ. If a practice is already in a firm's library, naming it is a binding from the management frame, not the invention of a new primitive. What remains forbidden is claiming the binding moves the wall (Fig 6 :21, *"MINT composes inside the closure and can never add an atom"*). The binding still needs provenance and a ground test, but it is not a category error in the way naming a seventh physical primitive would be. Both the corrected and the withdrawn formulations are kept so the correction is visible rather than silent.

## 7. This derivation is a simulation, not a ground reading

From the same essay, read directly:

> *"the output is only as good as the validator. The agent, or the tool, or the frame, or the theory, cannot validate itself. An external validator, or ground, is always needed to tether the veracity of the output to reality. Anything else is drift."*
> *"LLMs can quickly simulate an approximate answer/output with the formula found in the architecture post. It will often be better than the generic outputs that you will usually get otherwise too. However, the truly testable output would need to be derived from the real architecture or working the formula yourself."*
> *"You may all agree and be wrong."*

So this `05` file, like `01`, is a simulated derivation checked for internal consistency against the figures. It is labelled `specified` wherever it makes a claim about firms. It is not a test of the question and cannot settle it. The point of publishing it is to make the shape and its refuters easy to check by someone whose ground contact is real and whose closure differs.

## 8. The shape, stated once more

For BUS-0098 the canonical shape is: **a union of two conditions with two different anchors, one residual-relative and one anchor-selective, with a valve that changes sign and a source-discriminating instrument still owed.** It is not a list of practices, not the name of a duty, not a verdict that ethics pays, and not a verdict that ethics is merely a cost. The names that appear in the business literature are bindings, provenance owed. The instruments that would convert the shape into a grounded answer are not held here, so the answer stops at `specified` and the stop is recorded.
