## 2. Independent re-census from the residual line

### 2.1 Arity, symmetry, scale

- **Arity:** a relation among firm-library Γ, practice a, return o′ and the persons affected by a. A three-place reading (firm, practice, return) loses the very thing that makes the question two-part, so it is rejected. The comparison "show up as a low residual" is a threshold relation (Fig 12 :87), not mere conjunction.
- **Symmetry:** not symmetric. The market is an anchor and does not update on the firm's claim that its practice is ethical (Fig 2 :25). The relation between practice and return is ordered in time: the code is written before the return arrives, so a `→` bond is present.
- **Scale:** the slot is a firm, but "competitive advantage" exists only at the population scale (Fig 4 :5), and "harm to persons" exists at the scale of the affected parties, which is a different population. The time index crosses the membrane between the firm's step loop and the market's selection loop (Fig 4). So the residual is intrinsically cross-scale and crosses two different membranes.

## 4. Round-trip gap R_T

Fig 4 :30: *"So take the round trip and measure the gap. Without it you are navigating by dead reckoning: confident about a position nobody checked."*

- **T_A, going up:** carry the primary's union shape to a one-sentence abstraction. Result: *ethical practice pays while the firm keeps contact with what rewards it; being ethical is a duty anyway.*
- **T_E, coming back:** put that abstraction into a specific firm situation. Result: *listen to customers and keep a code of ethics on the wall.*

**R_T = gap(x, (T_E ∘ T_A)(x)).** The gap is what the abstraction cannot hold:

1. The one-sentence form collapses the union into two independent slogans. It loses Fig 9 :57, *"a case in two conditions is not a harder case of either."* Coming back down, it tells a firm failing the pay condition merely to write a stronger code, which is effort above the break (Fig 3 :29-30).
2. It loses the anchor rule. "A duty anyway" does not specify that the question is constitutively about persons and that the market has no standing there (Fig 2 :36-37). Coming back down, it permits a market reading to decide the moral question, which is precisely the collapse the shape refuses.
3. It loses the breach test. "Keep a code on the wall" has no place for the requirement that an obligation be installable as something that can be violated (Fig 10 :18-19, :47).
4. It loses the bond distinction. "Keep a code" records ingredients and not arrangement, so it cannot distinguish an isomer from a functional group (Fig 12 :5, :117).
5. It loses the source-discriminator requirement. The abstraction cannot say what instrument would distinguish an ethical practice from a protected habitat, which is the actual `gap` in the answer.

This gap is not an error to eliminate; it is the measurement of what the coarser description cannot hold (Fig 4 :31). It is also the reason the outreach packet sends the generator, not the one-sentence version (Fig 4 :25). The one-paragraph version in `07` is a deliberately lossy artefact and is labelled as such.
