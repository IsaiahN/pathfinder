## What is deliberately not here

- No personal emails of named individuals.
- No ranking of people by how likely they are to believe the result.
- No bulk-send plan.

Three reasons, in the figures' own words:

1. **A room does not upgrade the label.** Fig 7 :35 "A room's agreement is variation, not a verdict. Selection between rooms is where the ground votes." Adding readers raises the vocabulary and does not add an anchor.
2. **Correlation disqualifies a verdict.** Fig 2 :42 "Buy independence in proportion to the cost of being wrong." Selecting recipients for receptiveness manufactures the correlation that would make their agreement worthless.
3. **Asking for trust before checking spends independence.** Fig 10 :42 "A verifier that can reconstruct the claim cannot verify it." The outside frame must not hold the interiors before it gives a verdict.

Legally, scraping personal emails for unsolicited bulk outreach breaches GDPR and CAN-SPAM style rules in many jurisdictions. That is a practical failure on top of the structural one. The contact file uses public competition and submission routes only.

## The one-sentence residual

The word "charge" is borrowed from an electromagnetic closure where it is a signed, conserved, quantized scalar; gravity's source is positive energy-momentum, so the useful question is what composition of the six-term substrate could leave a discrete residue, and that is a ground question, not a further argument.
