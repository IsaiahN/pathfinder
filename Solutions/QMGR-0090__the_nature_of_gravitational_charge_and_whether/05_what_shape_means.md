## 1. A shape is a constraint, not a filler

The canonical README states the limit: the framework returns "what a missing thing would have to look like (a shape, not a name)". Fig 9 :32 "you get a kind of thing, never the answer itself". Fig 13 :135 "It cannot say which primitive is missing, because naming it would mean already holding it."

From *The Library of Babel*: "you can cut your search short if the set of books returned is small and descriptive enough that it provides a general shape of the answer that satisfies your question."

So a **shape** is the narrowed form of an answer you can state before you can name it. It constrains, it does not fill. For this question the shape is: a positive conserved tensor source, plus the claim that if discreteness appears it is a leftover of a composition, with the unit left to an instrument. A named graviton mass or a named Planck-scale unit would be a name, not a shape.

## 2. How a shape is obtained

- *Library of Babel*: use the vocabulary you already have that is closest to the thing, "connect and correlate combinations of the existence of your known words and their relative proximity to commonly recurring new terms that you are unfamiliar with", recurse. "With each new term gained, you narrow the space by definition and deepen your vocabulary."
- Fig 6 :29 "inward: is our representation adequate to hold it? The instrument extends access. Fails by refining forever on an axis that cannot carry it. Sharpen in our own terms." Fig 6 :30 "outward: does another frame already hold it? The description travels. Fails by being sharpened into terms only we can read. Cast it in effect terms." Fig 9 :30-31 "your words are borrowed too, so describe the effect rather than the cause."
- Fig 13 :124-125 "Smoothness, symmetry, chaos and discreteness are each what a particular composition leaves behind, and none is a seventh member." The vocabulary for "quantized" already exists as a leftover term; no new primitive is needed.
- *Library of Babel* on stopping: "you will know that you are done because the finish line will also be the starting place" and the paradox that "you have already composed the answer through the process of locating it." Here the question is not done, because no instrument has returned.

## 3. What licenses a name

Only two things, both outside the shape itself:

1. **The ground settles it.** Fig 2 :29 "An anchor is whatever settles the question and does not change its answer because of what any frame thinks. A measurement, an experiment, a counter, a market, a patient outcome." The canonical README says the framework "stops at the limit of what the available instruments can distinguish, and records the lineage so the stop is honest."
2. **A provenanced import from a frame whose closure differs** (Fig 6 :23), with a source gate and a shadow test.

*The Hard Problem is About Source* sharpens the first condition with metamerism: an instrument reads green and "cannot comment on the source". "If several paths create the same behavior, then behavior is a projection with lossy information (it cannot define consciousness in full)." The spectrometer resolved the case, not an argument: "It was proven wrong not by argument, but by the arrival of a better instrument."

Applied here: reading a smooth metric and naming its source as "discrete charge" is the same metamerism error in form. Several underlying descriptions could give the same reading. What is owed is a discriminator, not a name. The single-graviton proposal in the world literature is precisely such a proposed discriminator, and its authors decline to call it proof.

## 4. Why the question's own wording is the specific forbidden error

The word "charge" carries a U(1) scalar, signed, quantized meaning from electromagnetism. Gravity's source is positive energy-momentum. *The Library of Babel* warns that "analogies work so well to explain concepts: they are translating structural relationships and problems through language and scenarios that you are already familiar with". The translation is useful for finding vocabulary, but it is not a derivation. Fig 9 :27-28 "describe it before you go looking, or any frame you pick will seem to fit" governs the order.

The shape is therefore stated in effect terms: whatever plays the source role is conserved, positive, and arranged over space and time by structures. Whether a discrete residue appears is a separate, ground-owned question.

## 5. Answer to the shape question

**What is the shape of an answer to "the nature of gravitational charge and whether it can be quantized"?**

The framework returns: (i) the source role is filled by a conserved state, Energy, arranged through structures, not by a new scalar substance; (ii) discreteness is not a primitive, so "quantized" can only mean that a settled composition leaves a discrete residue in the source spectrum; (iii) that residue is not derivable from the classical closure and would arrive as an import from the quantum frame; (iv) its value is set by an instrument, and the framework does not name it.

What the framework does not return: a graviton mass, a quantization of the source in units of some mass, a choice between quantum gravity programs, or a verdict that the field must be quantized.

## 6. Consequences for this package

**Prestige is not action.** From *The Recursive Self-Improvement Trap*: "Prestige must never be allowed to validate an action directly... Prestige can fund an action. It can give an action a budget to be tried. But it cannot settle an action. Only the ground can settle it. The Ground Maintainer must be blind to prestige." A campaign that gathers famous names to agree with the shape is the named collapse, not corroboration.

**This audit is a simulation, not a ground reading.** Same essay: "LLMs can quickly simulate an approximate answer/output with the formula found in the architecture post... However, the truly testable output would need to be derived from the real architecture or working the formula yourself." The author also notes the essay was "AI edited, but written by me." So `01_figure_grounded_audit.md` is a simulated derivation checked for internal consistency against the figures. It is not a test of the claim, cannot settle anything it reports on, and is labelled as such everywhere.
