## What in the wider literature bears on this question

1. **The nature half is largely settled.** Hossenfelder and Fragkos and Pikovski independently describe the same object: energy-momentum, positive, tensor-valued, coupling to a spin-2 field. These two sources overlap in physics content but arrive from different questions (antigravitation and active/passive mass tests), so they are two witnesses for the object, not two for the interpretation.
2. **The quantization half is open at the ground.** SEOP states there is no logical necessity to quantize the gravitational field and that experiment must decide. The single-graviton paper offers an experiment but explicitly declines to call it proof. Fragkos and Pikovski state there is no empirical evidence for how a quantum source mass produces the field. These are three different subfields (philosophy of physics, quantum sensing, quantum phenomenology) with low overlap.
3. **Discreteness is a prediction of a composition, not an observed primitive.** SEOP on loop quantum gravity and Hod on Bekenstein's black-hole area spectrum both give discrete spectra as consequences of a theory, not as inputs. This is the world analogue of Fig 13 :124-125.
4. **The electric analogy has a known mechanism that does not transfer.** Heras gives the Dirac monopole explanation for electric charge quantization. Nothing in the sources read supplies an analogous condition for gravity. This corroborates the figure-derived instruction to describe the effect rather than the cause.
