## 0. The residual in effect terms

The question imports a word from a different closure. In electromagnetism, electric charge is (a) the source of the field, (b) a conserved scalar tied by Noether to a U(1) symmetry, and (c) quantized in discrete units. The question asks whether gravity's source has the same nature and whether it has property (c).

Stated as an effect: what must the source of the gravitational field be like for the word "charge" to be apt, and what would have to register for the word "quantized" to be apt for it? Two things fail to resolve:

- The noun. "Gravitational charge" is not a single scalar. It is energy-momentum, a symmetric rank-two tensor source, and it is positive in general relativity. The analogy strains at arity, symmetry and sign.
- The predicate. "Quantized" conflates a discrete spectrum of an operator with the claim that the field is a quantum field. Both are composition questions, not primitives.

What an answer would look like, bound as an ordinary term before the run (formula step "Direction", Fig 5 :65-66): a statement of which substrate term or composition plays the source role, and a statement of which composition, if any, leaves a discrete residue, together with the instrument that would discriminate it from a continuous one. Until that instrument returns, the slot stays open.

## 2. Findings

### F1 - Split the question before searching it (structural, `general`)

Fig 9 :4-5: **"are not one hard question but several well-formed ones. Split it rather than search."** Fig 9 :54: **"And when the split lands on a union rather than a partition, unbundle before you search again."**

The nature question and the quantization question bind different grounds. The first is settled by the stress-energy tensor and its positivity (Domain 2). A single search that tries to answer both will find whichever frame it picks first, because Fig 9 :27-28 says **"describe it before you go looking, or any frame you pick will seem to fit"**.

**Refuter:** show that the two halves share one ground, so that a single measurement settles both without a prior split. **Status:** triangulated (Fig 9 split rule plus Fig 3 dependency order), low overlap between the two figure lines.

### F2 - "Charge" is a borrowed word; describe the effect (`specified`)

Fig 9 :30-31: **"your words are borrowed too, so describe the effect rather than the cause"**. Fig 12 :46: **"A bond is the typed relation between two operands. There are seven, they are not interchangeable, and which one holds is not recoverable from the operands."**

**Strain test.** Electric charge is a signed Lorentz scalar coupling to a spin-1 field; like charges repel. Gravity's source is positive energy-momentum coupling to a spin-2 field; like charges attract. World corroboration: Hossenfelder, "Antigravitation" (arXiv:0909.3456, 2009): "In General Relativity (GR) the gravitational charge of the source, its energy, is always positive." and "Unlike Electrodynamics which is mediated by a spin-1 field, the gravitational interaction is transmitted by a spin-2 field, thus like charges will attract and unlike charges repel." The word "charge" only fits with a strain. By the strain rule, it governs another quantity: it names the coupling role (how a source enters the field equation), not a substance or a new atom. Re-census done; the substance is energy-momentum, and the charge-role is a bond between that state and the field.

**Refuter:** exhibit a signed scalar gravitational charge with experimentally accessible negative values, or show the tensor source can be replaced by a scalar without loss. **Status:** triangulated (Fig 9 effect-description rule and Fig 12 bond definition, with the physics world as low-overlap corroboration).

### F3 - The framework already holds the source (`specified`)

Fig 13 :14: **"conserved, finite, directionless alone"** (energy). Fig 13 :62: **"no spatial symmetry for Noether, and no region to make isolated mean anything"**. Fig 13 :65: **"no time-translation symmetry, and no finite operations to leave a remainder"**.

Conservation laws are tied to symmetries of the substrate in the figure's own minimality table. The source role is therefore played by Energy, a conserved state, arranged over Space and Time by structures. Discreteness of the *label* (electric charge comes in integer multiples) is a separate question from discreteness of the *source* (energy-momentum eigenvalues). World corroboration: Fragkos and Pikovski (arXiv:2503.16198, 2025, revised 2026): "The gravitational 'charge' is replaced by the stress-energy tensor T mu nu", and "the gravitational charge itself must be quantized" is stated as unique to gravity in the quantum domain.

**Refuter:** derive a conserved source term from outside the six that is not a composition of them, or show energy is not the conserved quantity that sources gravity. **Status:** triangulated (Fig 13 substrate plus the physics world), but the mapping onto the figure vocabulary is a binding, not a derivation. Not `grounded`.

### F4 - Discreteness is a composition leftover, not a primitive (framework-internal, `general` structural + `specified` application)

Fig 13 :124-125: **"Smoothness, symmetry, chaos and discreteness are each what a particular composition leaves behind, and none is a seventh member."** Fig 13 :43: **"A continuum was the seventh and was removed for failing exactly this"**. Fig 13 :44: **"A generating set must not contain what its own members produce."** Fig 12 :103: **"Composition is recursive, and the level decides what is irreducible."**

This is the central figure-grounded answer to "can it be quantized". The framework refuses both continuum and discreteness as primitive members. They are what an arrangement leaves. So the question is well posed only as: does a particular composition of the six, when settled against the ground, leave a discrete residue? A yes or no at the level of a primitive is not available, because there is no such primitive to add (Fig 13 :116-118, **"infinite effort inside a finite reach."**).

World corroboration is split and recorded honestly. SEOP, "Quantum Gravity" (first published 2005, substantive revision 2024): "In the loop quantum gravity program, the area and volume operators have discrete spectra." and "no logical necessity demanding that we quantize the gravitational field." Hod (arXiv:1505.04718, 2015): "Bekenstein has put forward the idea that, in a quantum theory of gravity, a black hole should have a discrete energy spectrum with concomitant discrete line emission." Both corroborate the shape (discreteness is a derived prediction of a composition), and the SEOP quote explicitly refuses a logical guarantee, matching the figure's ground requirement.

**Refuter:** derive discreteness or the continuum as a seventh member from the other six (breaks minimality), or show a settled composition of the six leaves neither. **Status:** triangulated (Fig 13 plus Fig 12 plus two low-overlap world sources from philosophy and mathematical physics).

### F5 - From the classical closure, quantization is not derivable (`specified`)

Fig 6 :21: **"MINT composes inside the closure and can never add an atom."** Fig 8 :32: **"no search, however long, adds an atom"**. Fig 13 :115: **"The set is closed. The arrangements are not. That gap is why import exists."** Fig 6 :29: **"inward: is our representation adequate to hold it? The instrument extends access. Fails by refining forever on an axis that cannot carry it. Sharpen in our own terms."** Fig 6 :30: **"outward: does another frame already hold it? The description travels. Fails by being sharpened into terms only we can read. Cast it in effect terms."**

Run the two reachability questions separately and never read one against the other. Inward: sharpening a classical metric measurement forever will not produce a quantum jump, because the axis cannot carry it. Outward: quantum theory is the frame whose closure predicts the shape of a discrete residue, and the description must travel in effect terms, not in the classical frame's borrowed words. Import is debited against independence (Fig 6 :24 "A wrong atom costs more than starting from primitives."). By Fig 8 :40-43 this is underivable-in-principle from the classical frame, not unreachable-at-budget, so the remedy is an import, not a longer search.

**Refuter:** derive a discrete source spectrum from classical general relativity alone with no imported quantum primitive. **Status:** triangulated (Fig 6, Fig 8, Fig 13; three figure lines with distinct roles).

### F6 - The verdict belongs to an instrument, not to agreement (`specified`)

Fig 2 :29: **"An anchor is whatever settles the question and does not change its answer because of what any frame thinks. A measurement, an experiment, a counter, a market, a patient outcome."** Fig 13 :134: **"Unreachable is a verdict here, not a silence,"** Fig 9 :32: **"you get a kind of thing, never the answer itself"**.

World corroboration: SEOP (2024 revision) states that "all attempts at making this argument stick have so far failed, meaning that there is no logical necessity demanding that we quantize the gravitational field" and that "it is a matter for experiment to decide, not logic." Tobar, Manikandan, Beitel and Pikovski, "Detecting single gravitons with quantum sensing", Nature Communications 15, 7229 (2024): "The quantization of gravity is widely believed to result in gravitons - particles of discrete energy that form gravitational waves." and, crucially, "the experiment therefore cannot be used as proof of the quantization of gravity." Fragkos and Pikovski (2025/2026): "There is currently no empirical evidence how a quantum mechanical source mass would produce the gravitational field." So the ground has not returned. The framework reports a shape and an open slot, and stops.

**Refuter:** produce a ground reading that names a unique discrete source unit, or one that demonstrates continuity at all resolutions. **Status:** triangulated (Fig 2 and Fig 9 plus three world sources), with a declared gap on the specific name and scale.

### F7 - The electromagnetic quantization mechanism is an import, not a shared atom (`specified`)

Heras, "Dirac quantisation condition: a comprehensive review", Contemporary Physics 59, 331 (2018), arXiv:1810.13403: "a single magnetic monopole in the Universe would explain the electric charge quantisation." Fig 6 :23: **"No frame escapes its own closure. The bound moves only on contact with a frame whose closure differs."**

Electric charge quantization has a specific explanation (a monopole plus single-valuedness of the wavefunction). No analogous condition exists for the gravitational source in the sources read: Hossenfelder shows the gravitational charge is positive and spin-2 coupled; Fragkos and Pikovski show the quantum active mass is not empirically established. The asymmetry is a physics finding; the figure governs only the general rule that such a mechanism would have to arrive as an import from a frame with a different closure.

**Refuter:** exhibit a gravitational Dirac-type quantization condition with the same logical status as the electromagnetic one. **Status:** single-source for the physics fact (Heras), triangulated for the general import rule (Fig 6 plus Fig 8).
