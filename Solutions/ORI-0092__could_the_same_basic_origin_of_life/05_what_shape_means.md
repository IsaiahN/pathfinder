## 1. A shape is a constraint, not a filler

The canonical README states the limit: the framework returns *"what a missing thing would have to look like (a shape, not a name)."* Fig 9 :32: *"you get a kind of thing, never the answer itself"*; Fig 13 :134: *"what it returns is a shape rather than a name."*

From *The Library of Babel*: *"You can cut your search short if the set of books returned is small and descriptive enough that it provides a general shape of the answer that satisfies your question."*

For this question a **shape** is the form an answer must have before we can name the chemistry on a named planet. It is a three-part constraint: an operation sequence, a habitat enumerated from that planet, and a ground test. `SAME_PATHWAY_ISOMER_OR_FUNCTIONAL_GROUP` is a shape. `CYANOSULFIDIC_CHEMISTRY_WORKS_ON_EUROPA` is a name, and the framework does not supply it.

## 2. How the shape is obtained

- *Library of Babel*: use the vocabulary you already have that is closest to the thing, look for recurring new terms that overlap with it, recurse. *"With each new term gained, you are able to narrow the space by definition and deepen your vocabulary."*
- *Library of Babel* on the bundle: *"Perhaps your problem was a bundle or conflation of several problems masquerading as one? You will need to search all of those problems individually"*. This is the essay-level source for the union split in `01` F1.
- Fig 6 :30: *"The description travels. Fails by being sharpened into terms only we can read. Cast it in effect terms."* Fig 9 :30-31: *"your words are borrowed too, so describe the effect rather than the cause."*
- *Decoding Tacit Knowledge*: *"When contact isn't possible, the best you can send is a plain description of what you'd expect to see, because anyone can check that against the world."* The shape is an effect description that any checker can range against a habitat.
- Fig 11 :48: *"You do not invent the list. You read it off the world, and what you cannot perceive or measure yet is the residual."* For this question the "list" is the habitat: atmosphere, solvent, minerals, energy sources.

## 3. What licenses a name

Only two things, both outside the shape:

1. **The ground settles it.** Formula step 5: *"the ground settles it. Verified only by what pays. A gate is not the ground."* Here that means an observed second genesis or a controlled experiment under the target planet's measured conditions.
2. **A provenanced import from a frame whose closure differs** (Fig 6 :23 *"No frame escapes its own closure. The bound moves only on contact with a frame whose closure differs."*), with a source gate and a shadow test.

*They're Scanning Books* sharpens this: *"The real import is not a new term in the existing space. It is a new dimension of the space."* And: *"A minimum viable set of six terms is a human's careful guess at reality's compression axes; another sensorium's minimum set need not be those six."* Finally: *"Declaring the primitives does not make them neutral. It makes the choice visible."*

The six-term substrate is a visible guess. On this question it matters because a pathway written in the six-term vocabulary is still a guess about which axes the chemistry uses. Declaring them makes the choice auditable, not neutral.

## 4. Why the source-discrimination problem bites here

*The Hard Problem is About Source* gives the mechanism. An instrument reads green and *"cannot comment on the source"*: one green light, or yellow plus blue, or RGB, or CMYK, or a scheme not yet discovered. *"If several paths create the same behavior, then behavior is a projection with lossy information."* And *"the answer is unreached until a better instrument exists."* It was the **spectrometer**, not an argument, that resolved it.

Applied here: observing that life exists in two habitats is a reading. It does not tell us that the *same pathway* produced both. Several formation histories can yield the same living projection. What is owed is a source-discriminating instrument, which in this field means measurements that distinguish independent origin from shared ancestry and that resolve which chemical route was taken.

*How Intelligence Actually Works* adds the lineage point: *"the mapping is not a tree with roots and branches, it is a web, with no canonical order and no single path through."* And: *"indiscernibility is not identity."* Two origins that look identical to our instruments are not thereby the same event.

## 5. Why "same pathway" is the forbidden inference

Fig 12 :94: *"The arrangement is the substance."* *Arrival of the Fittest* records under "Three mappings offered and declined" that the habitat as an ecological niche was declined: *"A niche is defined by an organism's relation to its environment. A habitat here is defined by contact with a residual, and the residual moves so the same actors constitute a different habitat when the question changes."* This is exactly the error in carrying an Earth recipe to another planet: the actors look similar, but the residual and the habitat are defined by contact, not by a standing list.

Formula, Habitat note: *"Valence is relative to R, not carried. Introduction is available; exclusion is not. Isolation substitutes a habitat, it does not remove one."* A mineral is not "good for life"; it is good or bad for a specific residual in a specific habitat.

## 6. Convergence is about multiple frames, not multiple agreeers

*How a Society of Agents Would Work*: *"A group is only worth having while its members stay different."* And: *"The ground sits under all three: no store's agreement replaces it."* Fig 2 :31: *"Independence, between two frames, is how little they draw on the same evidence."*

For this question, two origin-of-life groups reading the same Sutherland paper are one frame. A prebiotic chemist, a planetary spectroscopist, and a statistician of biosignature false positives are three frames, provided each ranges against the ground separately. Agreement among them is meaningful only to the degree their evidence pools differ.

## 7. Prestige is not action, and this audit is a simulation

From *The Recursive Self-Improvement Trap*: *"Prestige must never be allowed to validate an action directly... Prestige can fund an action. It can give an action a budget to be tried. But it cannot settle an action. Only the ground can settle it. The Ground Maintainer must be blind to prestige."* A famous laboratory's result gives a route a budget to be tried; it does not make the route portable.

Same essay: *"LLMs can quickly simulate an approximate answer/output with the formula found in the architecture post... However, the truly testable output would need to be derived from the real architecture or working the formula yourself."* LLM output is *"simulacra."* So `01_figure_grounded_audit.md` and this file are simulated derivations checked for internal consistency against the figures. They are **not** a test of the claim and must be labelled as such. They cannot settle anything they report on.

*Arrival of the Fittest* states the same limit at the framework level: *"Because it is a metatheory, it cannot be validated, nor can it validate itself."*

## 8. What "shape" means for this question, in one paragraph

A shape here is the narrowed form of the answer we can state before we can name the chemistry on a target planet: the method can cross habitats, the concrete arrangement cannot, the new habitat must be enumerated from the new planet, and what recurs is an isomer or a functional analogue. The framework does not name the pathway, does not say life is common, and does not count agreement as evidence. It records the lineage, the refuter, and the stop at the limit of what the instruments distinguish. *"Unreachable is a verdict here, not a silence,"* (Fig 13 :135).
