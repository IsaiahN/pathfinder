## Wider literature that bears on the question

- **Sasselov, Grotzinger and Sutherland 2020** is the closest published statement of the residual itself. They assume a pathway *"robust yet linked to specific environmental conditions"* and note that *"life has never been observed to originate from inanimate materials on modern Earth despite the abundance of potential feedstocks and the myriad conditions that now pertain"*. That is a world-source statement of the same union the figures return: method robustness plus habitat specificity. It is also high-overlap with Patel 2015 through Sutherland, so the two together are one evidence pool.
- **Sharma et al. 2023** is the lowest-overlap witness. Assembly theory is a different method (assembly index and copy number) in a different field (complex systems), yet it reaches the same shape: objects carry their formation history, and functionally equivalent classes can be built through multiple realisations. It corroborates the *"lineage is the proof"* reading in Fig 13 and the functional-group reading in Fig 12 :124. It does **not** corroborate the habitat law; no world source here does, and Fig 11 remains `single-source`.
- **England 2013** supplies a genuinely low-overlap physical constraint: self-replication has a thermodynamic cost independent of the chemical substrate. This supports the claim that a *generator* constraint is portable while the molecule list is not. It says nothing about which pathway closes R on a named planet.
- **National Research Council 2007** supplies the caution against terracentricity and a public, institutional statement of the limit. It corroborates the `gap` declaration, not a specific verdict.
- **Patel et al. 2015** is a worked instance, not a derivation: a common cyanosulfidic chemistry binding UV light, sulfide, and Cu cycling. Use it as the example of a concrete pathway bound to conditions, not as evidence that it is universal.

No world source conflicts with the figure verdict. The conflict rule was not triggered. If a source had conflicted, the derivation would return to the domain census rather than flip the verdict.
