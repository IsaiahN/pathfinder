## QUESTION

Decide, in effect terms, what the Tether says about the claim that the same basic origin-of-life pathway could work on planets with very different oceans, atmospheres, and minerals. Option A: assert that the concrete pathway is portable. Option B: keep the figure-derived shape, namely that the generator crosses between habitats while the concrete arrangement does not, and that what recurs is an isomer or functional analogue found by re-enumerating the new habitat. What each leads to: A sends an ungrounded generalisation, hides a union, and misdirects instrument design; B sends two separate verdicts, written refuters, and an open gap until a ground settles it.

## DOMAIN CENSUS

R by arity, symmetry, and scale, then one line per domain with actors, currency, and anchor; partition or union; order by dependency.

- **R (the residual).** The gap between a prebiotic habitat and a living, evolving system. Arity: three-place (pathway P, habitat H, outcome), indexed per planet, never aggregated (Fig 1 :19). Symmetry: the bonds in a pathway are ordered (production ⇒ and sequence →), so the map H to P is not symmetric; the only comparison is the threshold ⋛ for whether R reaches zero. Scale: molecular, protocell, planetary, and the membrane between a working pathway and its re-run elsewhere. Fig 9 names a union compound, not one hard question.
- **D1 Prebiotic chemistry.** Actors: feedstocks, catalysts, minerals, UV, water. Currency: yield, concentration, copy number. Anchor: measured products in a laboratory experiment.
- **D2 Pathway as generator.** Actors: operations (produce, activate, polymerise, encapsulate, select, copy). Currency: reapplicability and round-trip gap R_T. Anchor: re-closure of R in a fresh habitat.
- **D3 Planetary habitat.** Actors: ocean, atmosphere, mineral inventory, energy gradients. Currency: flows, gradients, availability. Anchor: measured planetary conditions (spectra, rover data).
- **D4 Verification frame.** Actors: experiments, missions, independent origins. Currency: residuals and agreement. Anchor: the ground, never agreement. In this simulated run all seats draw on one evidence pool, so independence here is zero.
- **X Not a domain.** "Life" as a free-floating property, and "the one true pathway", have no nameable anchor. Not admitted.
- **Union, not partition.** C1 (method transfer) and C2 (concrete chemistry transfer) overlap; a single lever clears only one condition (Fig 9 :55, :57).
- **Order by dependency.** Enumerate the habitat before evaluating the pathway (Fig 11 :43), then perception to vocabulary to objective to planning to learn and carry (Fig 3). The break that matters is between vocabulary and objective: terms for what a planet affords, and what would count as progress toward life.

Strain test applied: Fig 4's membrane governs crossing between *scales*, not between *habitats*. Re-census demotes Fig 4 to an analogue and installs Fig 11 as the governing figure (audit §4). A second strain, the molecule-to-pathway level move, is licensed by Fig 12 :104 but remains `specified`.

## VERDICT (from the figures)

The shape survives; the literal claim does not. **Only the generator crosses between habitats, not the concrete pathway.** The question is a union, so the verdicts stay separate: C1, the operation sequence can be re-applied (mode `general`, candidate); C2, the same molecules in the same bond order close R in a very different habitat (mode `specified`, candidate, default expectation no); and the exact pathway on a named planet is a declared `gap`. What recurs elsewhere is an isomer if the feedstocks are shared and an analogue in a functional group if they are not. The habitat is enumerated from the new planet, actor valence is relative to the residual, and a laboratory solve under familiar conditions proves wiring and never capability. No part of this is `grounded`.

- **cost if wrong:** bounded and locatable but real. If portability is over-assumed, missions and laboratory programmes are tuned to Earth-like chemistry and a genuinely different origin is missed, the failure Fig 11 :68 names as *"what you failed to reproduce is invisible until the goal fails"*. If portability is over-refused, a universal generator is overlooked. The packet states the refuter before the conclusion, so the error is findable rather than invisible. The concealable cost is spending an outside checker's independence by sending a recording instead of a generator.
- **undoable:** yes. No term is deleted if the claim fails; a refutation is added and the reach falls because *"What is recorded only grows. What is reachable is derived, and it can fall."* (Fig 6 :42). The shape, the union split, and the five refuters are recorded in a provenance trail so a later reader can locate the error (Fig 10 :62-64).
- **reversed by:** a ground reading, not agreement. An independently originated biosphere with measured chemistry using the same atoms in the same bond order under a disjoint habitat would establish literal portability (R1). A universal method that fails to re-close R in a fresh habitat after all required conditions were reproduced would break C1 (R2). An actor whose valence is invariant across a documented habitat change would break Fig 11 :49-51 (R3). A habitat shown composable from the pathway alone would break Fig 11 :43 (R4). Expert agreement with no new ground reading changing the state of R would break the anchor rule (R5). Until one fires, the verdict stands as `candidate`.
