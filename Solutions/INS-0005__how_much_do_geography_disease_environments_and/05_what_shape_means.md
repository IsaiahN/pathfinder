## 1. A shape is a constraint, not a filler

From *The Library of Babel*: *"You can cut your search short if the set of books returned is small and descriptive enough that it provides a general shape of the answer that satisfies your question."* The shape narrows the space; it does not occupy the slot.

From the same essay, on recursive narrowing: *"With each new term gained, you narrow the space and deepen your vocabulary."*

For INS-0005 the shape is therefore **the four properties the answer must have**, not the answer itself:

- the conditions are unbundled, not ranked (union, not partition);
- each slot is ranged against its own anchor;
- the frames are kept independent, and their overlap is recorded;
- the result stops where the instruments stop, with the lineage kept.

"Geography 40 percent, institutions 60 percent" would be a **name**, a filling of the slot. The figures return a shape. A number is licensed only by a ground settling a term (formula step 5) or by a provenanced import with a source gate and shadow test (Fig 6 :23, Fig 8 :50-52). Neither exists for the aggregate here.

## 2. How the shape is obtained

- *Library of Babel*: start from *"the existing vocabulary you do know that is closest to the words that you wish to describe and grasp the shape of"*, then recurse on terms that overlap.
- Fig 6 :30: *"The description travels. Fails by being sharpened into terms only we can read. Cast it in effect terms."* Fig 9 :30-31: *"your words are borrowed too, so describe the effect rather than the cause."* So the residual was described in effect terms (what fails to resolve when each argument is used alone) before any frame was chosen.
- *Decoding Tacit Knowledge*: *"When contact isn't possible, the best you can send is a plain description of what you'd expect to see, because anyone can check that against the world."* That is the form of the outreach email in `02`.
- *Library of Babel* on the bundle case, which governs this question directly: *"Perhaps your problem was a bundle or conflation of several problems masquerading as one? You will need to search all of those problems individually to find their answers and then combine them in a satisfactory way."* This is the essay-level counterpart of Fig 9 :54-57.

## 3. What licenses a name, and why the aggregate stays unnamed

Only two things license a name: the ground settles a minted term, or a provenanced import crosses from a frame whose closure differs. For the aggregate comparative magnitude neither holds.

*The Hard Problem is About Source* gives the mechanism. A measurement that cannot discriminate among its sources is inadequate, not decisive: *"If several paths create the same behavior, then behavior is a projection with lossy information"*, and *"An instrument that can't distinguish these cases demonstrates its own inadequacy."* The instrument that resolved the colour case was *"the spectrometer"*, not an argument. Here the corresponding instrument would have to discriminate a geographic contribution from a political one in a single institutional outcome. Settler mortality does not do it; nor does an institutional quality index.

*They're Scanning Books When They Should* (mediated quote) sharpens the import condition: *"The real import is not a new term in the existing space. It is a new dimension of the space."* An import that adds no dimension moves no wall (Fig 6 :26: *"An import adds no atom to the world."*).

## 4. Why a second seat cannot settle this by agreeing

*Decoding Tacit Knowledge*: *"High contact builds exactly the shared understanding that makes communication work, but that same factor makes the learner's agreement with the teacher worthless as a check."* Fig 2 :40: *"disqualifying for a verdict: agreement about the world is worthless when the agreeing frames share an evidence pool."* Both environmental and institutional accounts here use overlapping colonial samples, income series and cases. So `11_audit.md` is explicitly an internal consistency check, not corroboration.

## 5. Q: does the "shape, not a name" rule forbid any quantitative claim here? CORRECTION TO MY OWN READING

**No, and the scope of the rule matters.** The README phrase is *"what a **missing** thing would have to look like (a shape, not a name)"*. It governs a primitive not held in the library. In INS-0005 there is no claim that a seventh condition is missing. The four conditions are already in the vocabulary. So the rule does not forbid discussing magnitudes; it forbids (i) inventing an unnamed fifth condition to carry the residual and (ii) presenting an aggregate number as if the framework had named it. Published magnitudes remain admissible as world-source readings with provenance. They are not figure verdicts.

This corrects an over-broad first reading in which I treated every number as forbidden. Retracted as applied to already-held quantities; retained as applied to the aggregate comparative magnitude, which the figures do not licence. Both readings are kept visible.

## 6. Two consequences for this package

**Prestige is not action.** From *The Recursive Self-Improvement Trap*: *"Prestige must never be allowed to validate an action directly."* And: *"Prestige can fund an action. It can give an action a budget to be tried. But it cannot settle an action. Only the ground can settle it."* A list of famous development economists assembled to agree with the shape claim would be the named collapse. The legitimate ask gives the claim a budget to be tried and lets the ground, a clean instrument or a separable design, settle it.

**My own audit is a simulation, not a ground reading.** Same essay: *"LLMs can quickly simulate an approximate answer/output with the formula found in the architecture post. It will often be better than the generic outputs that you will usually get otherwise too (intelligence is essentially arrangement). However, the truly testable output would need to be derived from the real architecture or working the formula yourself."* So `01_figure_grounded_audit.md` is a simulated derivation checked for internal consistency against the figures. It is not a test of the claim and must be labelled as such. It cannot settle anything it reports on.
