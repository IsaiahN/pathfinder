## Two-stage discipline

1. **Stage 1:** send the shape claim and its refuter. Ask what they would count as a failure. Record their verdict **before** showing any framework material.
2. **Stage 2, opt-in only:** if they are curious, offer the source links. Their later agreement is never pooled as corroboration (Fig 2 :40: *"disqualifying for a verdict: agreement about the world is worthless when the agreeing frames share an evidence pool"*).
3. **Stage 3, opt-in only:** offer the formula as a method to reuse, with the instruction that each user writes their own refuter and states their own mode.
