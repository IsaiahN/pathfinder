## Independent domain census

I redid the census from the residual line before relying on the primary's conclusions, using the WATSON order: R by arity, symmetry and scale; one line per domain with actors, currency and anchor; partition or union; order by dependency.

- **R (arity / symmetry / scale).** The residual is the gap between an institutional outcome and what any one of four arguments predicts alone. Arity four, so a **union compound** (Fig 9 :4-5: a disagreement that does not shrink with effort across different subsets is several well-formed questions). Symmetry: the arguments are not swappable; a persistent constraint and a discrete choice are different operand kinds. Scale: centuries and continents for biophysical conditions, years and polities for choice.
- **Domain 1, environmental-material.** Actors: terrain, climate, pathogens, soils, crops, minerals. Currency: settlement feasibility and yield. Anchor: measured mortality, land quality, latitude. Partition question for its own slot.
- **Domain 2, institutional-formal.** Actors: rules, courts, property rights, suffrage. Currency: enforcement. Anchor: an observed rule. Partition question for its own slot.
- **Domain 3, political-agency.** Actors: leaders, coalitions, voters. Currency: contingent choice. Anchor: a recorded decision, weak. Union domain, overlapping D1 and D2.
- **Domain 4, verification / meta.** Actors: researchers, instruments, indices. Currency: agreement and residuals. Anchor: the checkable outcome, but no controlled origin experiment; independence near zero.

Order by dependency: describe the residual before choosing a frame (Fig 9 :27-28); refuse the aggregate before minting a share (formula step 2); settle each slot before any comparison; the chain breaks at vocabulary and objective, not at measurement (Fig 3 :20-21, :29-30).

My census matches the primary's four domains and its union reading. Where the primary derived the union from Fig 9's union/partition split and the two-currency bar from Fig 12, I derived the union from the arity of R itself (four arguments, non-swappable, different scales) and the currency bar from Fig 3's dependency argument (a link whose input never arrives cannot be assessed).
