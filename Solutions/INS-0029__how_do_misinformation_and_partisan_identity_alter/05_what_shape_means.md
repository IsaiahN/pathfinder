## 1. A shape is a constraint, not a filler

The figures README states the limit: the framework returns *"what a missing thing would have to look like (a shape, not a name)."* Fig 9 :32: *"you get a kind of thing, never the answer itself."* The line immediately before it in the SVG, Fig 13's dictum, is that a system with a closure can answer decidability before it searches and what it returns is a shape rather than a name.

From *The Library of Babel*, fetched 2026-11-23: *"you can cut your search short if the set of books returned is small and descriptive enough that it provides a general shape of the answer that satisfies your question."*

So a **shape** is the narrowed form of an answer you can state before you can name its occupant. It constrains; it does not fill. For INS-0029 the shape is: per institution-slot, two distinguishable conditions with different instruments, an anchor of measured performance, and an open `???` where the specific false claim or party cue would go. That is a shape. Naming "the claim that did it" or "the cue that did it" is a name.

## 2. How a shape is obtained

- *Library of Babel*: use the vocabulary you already have that is closest to the thing, look for recurring new terms that overlap or correlate with it, recurse. *"With each new term gained, you are able to narrow the space by definition and deepen your vocabulary."*
- Fig 6 :30: *"The description travels. Fails by being sharpened into terms only we can read. Cast it in effect terms."* Fig 9 :30-31: *"your words are borrowed too, so describe the effect rather than the cause."*
- Fig 9 :27-28: *"describe it before you go looking, or any frame you pick will seem to fit."* In this audit the residual is described in effect terms (a per-slot gap growing along two routes) before any of misinformation research, ecology or survey methodology was used as a witness.
- *Library of Babel* on stopping: the answer is reached when *"the finish line will also be the starting place"*, and the paradox is that by then you have *"composed the answer through the process of locating it."*

## 3. What licenses a name

Only two things, both outside the shape itself:

1. **The ground settles it.** Formula step 5: *"the ground settles it. Verified only by what pays."* The README: the framework *"stops at the limit of what the available instruments can distinguish, and records the lineage so the stop is honest."* For this question that means an experiment or a performance measurement, not a poll.
2. **A provenanced import from a frame whose closure differs** (Fig 6 :23, Fig 8 :50-52), with a source gate and a shadow test.

They're Scanning Books sharpens this, per the exemplar's mapping: *"The real import is not a new term in the existing space. It is a new dimension of the space."* And: *"Declaring the primitives does not make them neutral. It makes the choice visible."* I did not re-open that essay this session, so these are carried from the exemplar's canonical record, not independently verified here.

The six-term substrate is a visible guess, not a discovery of nature's own list. For INS-0029 the analogous caution is that "misinformation" and "partisan identity" are visible choices of vocabulary from the world frame. Naming one as *the* cause turns a visible choice into an invisible one.

## 4. The ground and the source problem

*The Hard Problem is About Source*, fetched 2026-11-23, gives the mechanism. Metamerism: an instrument reads green and *"cannot comment on the source"*; one green light, or yellow plus blue, or RGB, or CMYK, or a scheme not yet discovered can all produce the same reading. *"If several paths create the same behavior, then behavior is a projection with lossy information."* And: *"An instrument that can't distinguish these cases demonstrates its own inadequacy."* It was the **spectrometer**, not an argument, that resolved it, because *"the answer is unreached until a better instrument exists."*

Applied here: a survey item reading "low trust in institution X" does not say whether the source was a false input or a biased weight. Several paths create the same reading. The metamerism error would be to name the source from the reading alone. What is owed is a source-discriminating instrument, not a confident attribution.

*The Recursive Self-Improvement Trap*, fetched 2026-11-23: *"the output is only as good as the validator. The agent, or the tool, or the frame, or the theory, cannot validate itself."* So this audit cannot certify its own shape.

## 5. Answer to the shape question

**What licenses a particular false claim or party cue as the cause of a particular gap?** Nothing in the framework. The framework returns a two-condition shape with an open slot. A particular claim or cue is licensed only by a ground reading, that is, an experiment or a performance measurement that discriminates among the paths producing the same judgment reading, or by a provenanced import with a shadow test. Until one of those, the name is the searcher's own contribution and must be recorded as such.

**Does the strong public-discourse version commit the error the README forbids?** Yes when it names a source from a single reading: "misinformation caused this drop in trust" or "partisans never trust institutions" both read the source off a projection without a discriminator. The correct form is the two-condition shape with the slot marked `???`.

## 6. Consequences for contact

**Prestige is not action.** From *The Recursive Self-Improvement Trap*, fetched 2026-11-23: *"Prestige must never be allowed to validate an action directly... Prestige can fund an action. It can give an action a budget to be tried. But it cannot settle an action. Only the ground can settle it. The Ground Maintainer must be blind to prestige."* A campaign that gathers well-known names to agree with the shape is the named collapse, not corroboration. The legitimate ask gives the claim a budget to be tried and lets a ground settle it.

**My own audit is a simulation, not a ground reading.** Same essay: *"LLMs can quickly simulate an approximate answer/output with the formula found in the architecture post... However, the truly testable output would need to be derived from the real architecture or working the formula yourself."* So `01` is a simulated derivation, checked for internal consistency against the figures. It is **not** a test of the question and must be labelled as such. It cannot settle anything it reports on.
