## The residual in one sentence

Why does the gap between what citizens believe about an institution and what that institution actually does stay small in some institution-slots and grow large in others, and why does it grow along two different routes that a single lever cannot clear together?

## What is deliberately not here

- No personal emails of named researchers.
- No ranking of people by how likely they are to agree with the shape claim.
- No bulk-send plan, no scraped list, no claim that agreement from a receptive audience is evidence.

Three figure-grounded reasons:

1. **Agreement is not a verdict.** Fig 7 :13: *"agreement compounds, evidence does not"*. A list selected for receptiveness manufactures the very correlation that makes its agreement worthless (Fig 2 :40).
2. **An anchor is not a frame.** Fig 2 :29 names a measurement, an experiment, a counter, a market, a patient outcome as anchors. A panel of agreeable readers is none of these.
3. **Do not spend the reader's independence before asking for a verdict.** Fig 10 :42: *"A verifier that can reconstruct the claim cannot verify it."* The first contact carries the residual and its refuter, never the framework's interiors.

Legally: unsolicited bulk email to scraped addresses breaches GDPR and CAN-SPAM style rules in many jurisdictions and gets the sending domain blacklisted. That is a practical failure on top of the structural one.
