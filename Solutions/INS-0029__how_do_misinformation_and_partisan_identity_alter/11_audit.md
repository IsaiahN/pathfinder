## 1. Independent domain census

I redid the census before reading the primary's conclusions, using the WATSON order: R by arity, symmetry and scale; one line per domain with actors, currency and anchor; partition or union; order by dependency.

- **R (arity / symmetry / scale).** The residual is the gap between belief about an institution and measured institutional performance. Arity: four places (belief with weight, information input, performance, judgment), indexed by slot and ordered in time. It carries an ordered sequence bond and a threshold comparison bond. Symmetry: a false positive is not the reverse of a false negative, and the comparison is non-exchangeable under a party cue. Scale: citizen loop, population habitat, institutional stratum and the time channel; the membrane between scales is lossy.
- **D1 Citizen belief loop.** Actors: citizen, Γ, claims and cues. Currency: residual reduction. Anchor: a factual benchmark that does not move with frames. Admitted.
- **D2 Information and media habitat.** Actors: platforms, ranking, producers, fact-checkers. Currency: attention and measured exposure. Anchor: exposure counts. Admitted.
- **D3 Partisan identity weighting.** Actors: identity, inherited priors, group norms. Currency: `w`. Anchor: self-identification and voting. Admitted.
- **D4 Institutional performance ground.** Actors: institution, outputs, audits. Currency: performance. Anchor: the measurement. Admitted.
- **D5 Measurement frame.** Actors: researchers and pollsters. Currency: validity. Anchor: the experiment. Admitted.
- **X1 "Misinformed voter" type.** No anchor independent of the label. Not a domain.
- **X2 "Partisan bias" scalar.** Absorbed into D1 and D3.

Union, not partition: the citizen and identity domains overlap, the habitat and identity domains overlap, and the measurement frame overlaps all. Dependency order: perception, vocabulary, objective, planning, learn and carry (Fig 3 :20). I reached this from the union/partition split in Fig 9 and the anchor test in Fig 2, rather than from the primary's starting point in the residual indexing of Fig 1. Different starting figures, same domain boundaries: low overlap between routes within the framework, but still one evidence pool.

## 4. Round trip gap R_T

The Symbols table defines R_T as the gap between what was sent up and what came back down, `gap(x, (T_E ∘ T_A)(x))`, measuring what the coarser description cannot hold. Fig 4 :30 requires it to be measured rather than assumed. I ran the round trip on the condensation this folder actually uses.

1. **Sent up (x):** the verbatim figure set, chiefly Fig 1 :19, :22 (per-slot indexing), Fig 2 :29, :37-38 (anchor and instrument), Fig 9 :54-57 (union), Fig 11 :39, :49-51 (habitat), and Fig 13 :134 (honest stop).
2. **Abstraction (T_A):** the shorthand carried in `07` and `08`: per institution, two conditions, measured performance is the anchor, no scalar, slot stays open.
3. **Concretisation (T_E):** a concrete institutional claim, for example "a drop in trust in the elections agency was caused by a viral false claim" or "partisans never trust the health agency".

**R_T is nonzero.** Three things the shorthand holds do not survive the trip back down:

- the **order of `w` relative to the comparison** (Fig 12 :72, Symbols :23-24), which a flat "bias score" erases;
- the **binding provenance** of `w` as partisan identity, which a concrete claim silently promotes from a world-frame binding to a figure-derived law;
- the **source-discriminating requirement**, which a concrete causal attribution breaches when it names the source from a single judgment reading (the metamerism point in *The Hard Problem is About Source*).

Conversely, two things survive cleanly: a judgment gap belongs to a named institution, and the two conditions have different instruments and cannot be added. That is the residue worth sending. R_T was **not** measured numerically, because no ground reading exists; it is recorded qualitatively as the enumerated set of dropped constraints. Treating "the formula is the condensed version of the metalaws" as identity would set R_T to zero and would be the dead-reckoning error Fig 4 :30 names. The honest form is a lossy condensation with a recorded gap, which is what `09_watson_answer.md` states.
