# The Tether's answer: which labor-market institutions best balance three goods

**Mode: `specified` on the per-domain claims, `general` on the structural shape, `gap` on the literal ranking, not `grounded`.** This is a shape and a route, not a result. Simulated derivation checked against the figures (`05_what_shape_means.md` section 7), not a ground reading. No labor-market measurement was taken.

## 1. The residual it answers

A single rule can protect the worker who has a job and exclude the worker who wants one, and the same rule can help or hurt adaptation depending on the shock. The question asks for the institution that "best balances" security, adaptation and opportunity. The effect to be decided is: what would an answer have to look like if the three goods cannot be ranged against one anchor? What each option leads to: a ranking plus a weight vector (Option A) gives a decision rule that hides per-slot losses; a per-condition separator (Option B) gives a checkable shape and leaves the ranking open until a discriminator exists. Fig 9 :4-5: "Disagreements that do not shrink with effort, where each rule keeps working well on a different subset, are not one hard question but several well-formed ones."

## 2. The shape

1. **The residual is per slot, never per average.** Fig 1 :19: "The residual is indexed by object slot, not aggregated." Fig 1 :22: "Aggregating across slots is how a live signal disappears into an average." A country "balance score" erases both the insider kept and the outsider excluded.
2. **The three goods are a union, not one quantity.** Fig 9 :55: "A bundled problem is usually a union of overlapping conditions, not a list of separate ones." A single lever clears the single-condition regions and leaves the intersections standing. So the answer to "which one institution" is not a name; it is a structural refusal of the question's form.
3. **Each good needs its own non-updating anchor, and the anchors must be independent.** Fig 2 :29: "An anchor is whatever settles the question and does not change its answer because of what any frame thinks." Fig 2 :31: "Independence, between two frames, is how little they draw on the same evidence." Three readings from one dataset by one agency are one witness, not three (Fig 2 :40).
4. **An institution's value is relative to the residual and the habitat.** Fig 11 :39: "capability is a property of agent-and-habitat, never of the agent alone." Fig 11 :51: "No standing list of good actors survives a change of residual." Strict protection is a benefactor to "keep the current job" and a malfactor to "reallocate after a shock". The Danish flexicurity result is the world-side version: flexicurity in itself is insufficient; the active programs and the surrounding tax and bargaining habitat bind with it (Kreiner and Svarer 2022; Wikipedia, *Flexicurity*).
5. **The chain currently stops at the objective link.** Fig 3 :27: "A link with no instrument is not a link that works. It is a link nobody has looked at." Security and employment have instruments; adaptation at the worker slot does not, and the weights across the three are undeclared. Effort spent on choosing a package (planning) arrives at a step that cannot use it (Fig 3 :19).
6. **"Balance" is a bond-carrying arrangement, not a scalar.** Fig 12 :5: "A set of ingredients is not one. The bond is the rest of the information, and it is the half nothing was recording." Fig 12 :125: "A composition costs in two currencies, and they do not add." Pairwise comparisons, production, subtraction and sequence are all present; three-way balance is not one of the eight operators (see `06_isomers_and_order.md`).
7. **The ranking is a declared gap.** Fig 9 :32: "you get a kind of thing, never the answer itself". Fig 13 :134: "Unreachable is a verdict here, not a silence". No figure line selects EPL over activation over bargaining, and no weight vector is ground-settled. Inventing one would be a name where the framework returns a shape.

## 4. What it explicitly does not say

- Not "institutions do not matter". That is the opposite verdict in the same wrong mode.
- Not "flexicurity is best", "employment protection is best", or "activation is best". Those are bindings from the labor frame, ungrounded until a discriminator pays.
- Not a universal weight vector, a country score, or an optimal level of protection.
- Not a derivation of labor institutions from the six-term substrate. The six terms govern composition, not this domain's entity list.
- Not `grounded`. Nothing here is asserted as settled by a labor-market measurement.

## 5. What would settle it — the ground

A discriminator, not another review. From *The Hard Problem is About Source*: an instrument that reads green "cannot comment on the source"; the spectrometer resolved it, "not by argument, but by the arrival of a better instrument." Here the corresponding needs are:

1. **A slot-level adaptation instrument** that reads reallocation or skill change at the worker slot and does not update on the institution being evaluated. With it, the objective link (Fig 3 link 3) exists and packages can be compared end to end.
2. **Independent readings of the three goods** from frames with genuinely different evidence pools, each ranging separately against the same outcome (Fig 2 :31, :46).
3. **A bond discriminator:** a measurement that distinguishes two packages with the same three scores by their internal arrangement, e.g. whether income support produces participation or merely precedes it (Fig 12 :77 versus :72).
4. **Standing falsifiers from `01`:** a lossless aggregation that preserves the live residual; a partition proof for the three conditions; a named institution with invariant valence across shocks and habitats; a fixed weighting rule that survives changing the evaluated institution; or a figure re-census that returns a ranking.

## 6. The honest stop

**Unreached, not unreachable.** The labor categories are a visible binding from one research tradition. The European Commission's own "no size fits all" principle and its rejection of "one single labour market or working life model" (Wikipedia, *Flexicurity*) say the same thing from inside the field. The stop is recorded at the limit of the available instruments: a large literature measures levels and flows, but nothing opened here reads adaptation at the slot independently of the policy that produced it. Fig 8 :39: "So a search that finds nothing is never a proof of absence." Fig 6 :34-35: a sensor for something that has never registered anywhere is not the same as a sensor that needs sharpening. The lineage is recorded so a later reader can locate the error rather than only feel it.

## 7. One-paragraph version for outreach

> The Tether does not name a best labor-market institution and does not claim the three goods can be traded on one dial. It says the residual has to be read per worker, not per country average; that security, adaptation and opportunity are a union of overlapping conditions with different anchors, so one lever leaves the intersections standing; that an institution's value depends on the shock and the surrounding habitat, so no standing winner survives a change of residual; and that the literature currently stops at the objective link, where adaptation has no instrument independent of the policy being graded. What it offers is the shape any answer must have, four refuters written in advance, and the kind of discriminator that would close it. The ranking stays open until a ground settles it.
