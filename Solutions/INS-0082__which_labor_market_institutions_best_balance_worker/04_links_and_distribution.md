## Wider literature that bears on the question

1. **Kreiner and Svarer 2022 is the strongest world corroborator for the union verdict.** Flexicurity alone is insufficient; the active programs are a second binding condition. This is Fig 9 :55 in world language: a single lever clears the flexibility-and-security region and leaves the intersection with activation standing. It also kills the Lazy Reading "flexicurity is the best balance".
2. **The OECD EPL literature is a live disagreement, not a settled trade-off.** Blanchard and Portugal, Lazear, and Bertola and Bentolila reach different conclusions about the same institution. Fig 2 :31 makes the point about what such agreement would be worth: it depends on whether the frames draw on the same evidence. The disagreement is evidence for the union, not a reason to average the estimates.
3. **The ILO What Works project makes the joint-provision point directly.** Income support sustains income so workers can participate in ALMPs; effectiveness hinges on implementation. Two conditions bind together. This is a habitat claim (Fig 11 :39), not a property of an institution in isolation.
4. **The exploration/exploit analogue is the low-overlap witness.** It shows the temptation this answer refuses: an "exploration reward" that converts a two-strategy dilemma into a single sum. That is the flat-formula move of Fig 12 :5. It is an analogue only; a machine-learning result cannot settle a labor question.
5. **The rigidity/fragmentation result is a contact cascade.** Product-market regulation correlates with employment protection at 0.73 and reforming one market alone is less effective (Wikipedia, *Employment protection legislation*). Fig 11 :52: "And contact cascades. Scoping the habitat to direct contact is how DDT reached the eagles: first-order enumeration, second-order effect, and the residual that mattered was three steps out."

Overlap declared: the four Wikipedia pages and the three ILO pages share a broad labour-economics evidence pool; they are not four or seven independent witnesses. Kreiner and Svarer is the paper behind one of those summaries, so it is one witness with the flexicurity page. The exploration/exploit and metamerism sources are genuinely lower overlap. The framework essays share the framework author's evidence pool completely and have zero independent witness weight for the verdict.

## Distribution: offer, do not push

Figure-grounded reasons:

- **Fig 10 :52:** "Bring a characterised residual, never the source." Sending the entire architecture before anyone has described a residual sends a recording, not a generator. Lead with the residual, the shape claim and the refuter.
- **Fig 10 :42:** "A verifier that can reconstruct the claim cannot verify it." A recipient who has absorbed the framework before the verdict has joined the frame being checked.
- **Fig 8 :53:** "Residual first, frame second." Ask what they would expect to see if the separator were true before naming any literature.
- **Fig 7 :35:** "A room's agreement is variation, not a verdict. Selection between rooms is where the ground votes." A list selected for likely agreement produces a convention, not corroboration.
- **Fig 2 :42:** "Buy independence in proportion to the cost of being wrong." A wrong labor reform harms identifiable workers, so independent readings are worth the extra effort.

**Recommended order in a reply:** (1) the residual and the four refuters; (2) ask what the recipient would count as a failure; (3) record that verdict; (4) only then offer the links, noting that the figures, not this summary, are the authority; (5) offer the formula as a method to reuse, with the instruction that each user writes their own refuter and states their own mode.

## The two-stage offer

- **Stage 1 before stage 2** is Fig 10 :52 and Fig 2 :45: "Frames range against the ground. They do not negotiate with it." The recipient checks the output without holding the framework's interior.
- **Stage 2 is opt-in by construction.** Once a recipient has absorbed the theory, their agreement stops being evidence (Fig 2 :40). The stage-1 verdict must be recorded first and later agreement never pooled.
- **Stage 3 sends a method, which is permitted.** Fig 4 :22: "Only methods cross up. Only head starts come down." A generator can be given away without giving away an answer.
- **Stage 3 still needs a ground per problem.** `THE_FORMULA.md` "Direction": a frame cannot check what it is looking for, because the shape it seeks is not a term it holds. Each user must write down what would refute their result and label the mode.
