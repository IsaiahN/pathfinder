## The question restated as an effect

Decide what an answer would have to look like if one were choosing a labor-market institution (employment protection, unemployment insurance, active labour market policy, wage-setting institutions, collective bargaining, training systems) so as to obtain three things at once: worker security, economic adaptation, and employment opportunities. Each option leads to a different distribution of those three goods. An answer would bind a single object, an institution or a bundle, as the thing that produces the best joint outcome. The ordinary term "balance" is the sought-for shape, and per Figure 5's direction limit it must be made an ordinary term with a residual before the search starts, otherwise it stays an invisible preference that no check can see.

What "best balance" presupposes, and what the audit tests: that the three goods are commensurable, that they can be ranged against one anchor, and that a single lever can settle them together. If those presuppositions fail, the question is a union of conditions rather than one optimisation, and the honest answer is a per-domain separator plus a declared `gap`, not a ranking.

## What is deliberately not here

- No personal emails of named individuals.
- No ranking of people by how likely they are to believe the claim.
- No bulk-send plan.

Three reasons, in the figures' own words:

1. **A room does not upgrade the label.** Fig 7 :35: "A room's agreement is variation, not a verdict. Selection between rooms is where the ground votes." Multiplying recipients raises the vocabulary and does not add an anchor.
2. **Selecting for receptiveness manufactures the disqualifying correlation.** Fig 2 :40: "agreement about the world is worthless when the agreeing frames share an evidence pool". A list ranked by likely agreement is a list selected to agree.
3. **Asking people to trust before they check spends their independence.** Fig 10 :42: "A verifier that can reconstruct the claim cannot verify it." A recipient who has absorbed the framework before giving a verdict has joined the frame being checked.

Legally: scraping personal emails for unsolicited bulk outreach breaches GDPR and CAN-SPAM style rules in many jurisdictions and gets the sending domain blacklisted. That is a practical failure on top of the structural one.
