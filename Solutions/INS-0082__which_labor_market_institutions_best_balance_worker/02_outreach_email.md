**Use:** one-to-one, or as a cover letter attached to a submission through the public venues in `03_public_contact_points.csv`. **Not** for bulk send.

**Hard precondition:** do not send until the live type strain S3 in `01_figure_grounded_audit.md` is stated in the text as a strain. The output must survive the check or the two-stage offer collapses on contact.

**Subject:** One checkable claim about "balancing" security, adaptation and jobs

> Hi [name],
>
> I have one specific output I would like you to check against what you already know. I am not asking you to read a framework first, and I am not asking you to agree with anything. If the output does not survive, there is nothing else to look at.
>
> **The residual, stated first.** A worker can be made more secure by a rule that makes it harder for a different worker to get a job, and the same rule can help or hurt adaptation depending on the shock. The question asks which labor-market institution "best balances" security, adaptation and opportunity. The three are not one quantity. The resemblance to a single dial is where I think the trouble is.
>
> **The shape claim (`specified`, not `grounded`).** I derive that (1) the residual has to be read per worker and firm slot, not per country average, because an average kills the live signal; (2) the three goods form a union of overlapping conditions with different anchors, so one lever clears the single-condition cases and leaves the intersections standing; (3) an institution's value is relative to the residual and the habitat, so no standing list of "best" institutions is licensed; and (4) the literature currently stops at the objective link, where "adaptation" has no instrument that reads it independently of the institution being evaluated. What is owed is a discriminator, not another weight vector. I do not return a ranking. That is a declared gap, not a silence.
>
> **What I am not claiming.** Not that institutions do not matter. Not that any named institution, flexicurity, employment protection, activation or collective bargaining, is the winner or the loser. Not a universal weight vector or a country score. Not grounded. The specific claim "flexicurity works" is too strong in the same way: the published evaluation says flexicurity in itself is insufficient and needs active programs, which is a union claim, not a verdict on one lever.
>
> **What would refute the shape claim.** I wrote these down before sending this:
>
> 1. A scalar measure exists that reads all three goods at the worker slot, does not move when the institution being graded changes, and preserves the live residual under aggregation.
> 2. The three conditions form a partition, not a union: disjoint, singly anchored, and closed by one lever.
> 3. A named institution keeps the same valence for the same residual across a change of shock and habitat, with a ground that pays the same way.
> 4. An instrument for adaptation at the worker slot exists, with a declared weighting rule that stays fixed when the evaluated institution changes.
>
> A one-line "this fails because..." is more useful to me than agreement. If you would rather not engage, no reply is needed.
>
> **If it survives, and only then:** here are the sources. The figures are the authority, not my summary of them.
> - Verbatim figures with line numbers (local framework extract): `FRAMEWORK/reference/03-FIGURES_TEXT.md`, cite as `Fig N :line`.
> - Canonical usage rule (local): `FRAMEWORK/reference/FIGURES_README_canonical.md`.
> - The eight-step loop (local): `FRAMEWORK/figures/THE_FORMULA.md`.
>
> **If you want to use the machinery:** the formula is in the figures link. Run it on a labor problem you know well and can check against a ground. Write down what would refute your result *before* you run it. The framework's own rule is that a loop cannot check the direction of its own question, and its essays warn that outputs can be contrived to fit the formula. A reported failure is more useful to me than a reported success.
>
> — [name]

## Sequence of the two-stage offer

1. **Stage 1:** send the residual, the shape claim and the four refuters. Ask what the recipient would count as a failure. Record that verdict *before* showing any theory.
2. **Stage 2, opt-in only:** if they are curious, offer the three figures links with the note that the quotes, not the summary, are the authority. Their later agreement is never pooled as corroboration (Fig 2 :40: "agreement about the world is worthless when the agreeing frames share an evidence pool").
3. **Stage 3:** offer the formula as a method to reuse, with the instruction that each user writes their own refuter and states their own mode. A method may cross a scale; a recording of one success may not (Fig 4 :22-25).
