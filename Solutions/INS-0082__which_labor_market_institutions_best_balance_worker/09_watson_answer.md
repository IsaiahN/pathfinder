## QUESTION

Decide, in effect terms, what the Tether says about the claim that some labor-market institution or bundle "best balances" worker security, economic adaptation and employment opportunities. Option A: return a ranking with a weight vector. Option B: keep the figure-derived shape and declare the ranking a gap. What each leads to: A produces a policy decision rule that hides per-slot losses and fixes a convention that decides an outcome; B produces a checkable separator, four written refuters and an open slot until a discriminator exists. Fig 9 :4-5: "Disagreements that do not shrink with effort, where each rule keeps working well on a different subset, are not one hard question but several well-formed ones."

## DOMAIN CENSUS

R by arity, symmetry and scale, then one line per domain with actors / currency / anchor, partition or union, and order by dependency.

- **R (the residual).** A rule protects an insider and excludes an outsider, and its effect on adaptation flips with the shock. Arity: three conditions, so a union compound, not a two-way trade-off. Symmetry: not symmetric, because the three goods have different currencies and the bond between them is ordered or productive in places. Scale: the claim is at the country level and the residual is at the worker and firm slot. Fig 1 :19: "The residual is indexed by object slot, not aggregated."
- **Domain 1, measured-outcome frame.** Actors: workers, firms, jobs, unemployment and employment spells. Currency: tenure, income, job-finding and re-employment rates. Anchor: a measured outcome that does not change because a policy frame praises it (Fig 2 :29). Union with domain 3, not a partition of the question.
- **Domain 2, institution frame.** Actors: employment protection, unemployment insurance, active labour market policy, wage coordination, training systems. Currency: rules, transfers, incentives, obligations. Anchor: the outcomes in domain 1. The question is a partition boundary question inside this frame: which condition each institution binds.
- **Domain 3, adaptation and habitat frame.** Actors: technology, trade, demand shifts, firm entry and exit, skills. Currency: reallocation. Anchor: the ground over time plus a shock not authored by the policy frame. Valence is relative to the residual, so the same institution flips sign across residuals (Fig 11 :50).
- **Domain 4, research and verification frame.** Actors: labor economists and international organisations. Currency: estimates and agreement. Anchor: the ground, never agreement. In this simulated run all four domains draw on one evidence pool, so their agreement is one pool talking.
- **Order by dependency.** Perception, then vocabulary, then objective, then planning, then carry (Fig 3 :20). The chain stops at the objective link, because no independent instrument reads adaptation at the worker slot (Fig 3 :27). Refuse the scalar before minting; describe the residual before choosing a frame (Fig 8 :53).

Strain test applied: Fig 12 :125 (two currencies do not add) and the Operators table definition of ⋛ as a threshold relation between two quantities strain the three-good comparison. Re-census assigns ⋛ to pairwise comparisons and the three-way "balance" to a bond compound, so the figure is not forced onto three operands. The strain is named in `01` S3 and `06` Q2.

## VERDICT (from the figures)

The figure-derived shape survives; the ranking does not. Security, adaptation and opportunity are a union with different anchors, so one lever leaves the intersections standing and no scalar "best balance" carries the bond between them. The residual is per worker and firm slot. Institution valence is relative to the residual and the habitat, so no standing winner is licensed. The chain stops at the objective link, where an independent worker-slot instrument for adaptation is missing; what is owed is a discriminator, not another weight vector. The literal ranking is a declared `gap` of the unreachable-at-this-budget kind, not underivable in principle and not silence. Mode `specified` on the per-domain claims, `general` on the structure, `gap` on the ranking, not `grounded`. A simulated derivation checked for internal consistency against the figures.

- **cost if wrong:** bounded and locatable. The separator is mode-labelled, states four refuters before the conclusion and records the lineage, so an error is findable rather than invisible. The real cost is spending the independence of outside readers by sending a ranking (a convention that decides an outcome, Fig 10 :47) instead of a generator. Fig 2 :42: "Buy independence in proportion to the cost of being wrong." That cost is avoidable by leading with the residual and the refuter.
- **undoable:** yes. The ranking, if one were proposed, is withdrawable without touching the separator; `07_the_tether_answer.md` and `08_expert_verification_packet.md` already avoid it. No primitive has to be withdrawn, and no standing list of institutions is asserted that would have to be unsaid.
- **reversed by:** a ground reading, not agreement. A worker-slot instrument for adaptation that does not update on the policy would open link 3; two genuinely independent readings of the three goods would make a verdict possible; a bond discriminator would show whether two packages with equal scores behave the same; and any of the five pre-registered refuters in `01` would break the shape. Fig 8 :39: "So a search that finds nothing is never a proof of absence." Until one of these fires, the verdict stands as `candidate`.
