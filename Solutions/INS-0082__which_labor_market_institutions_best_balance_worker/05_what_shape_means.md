## 1. A shape is a constraint you can state before you can name the filler

The figures README states the limit: the framework returns *"what a missing thing would have to look like (a shape, not a name)."* Fig 9 :32: "you get a kind of thing, never the answer itself". Fig 13: "It cannot say which primitive is missing, because naming it would mean already holding it."

From *The Library of Babel*: "you can cut your search short if the set of books returned is small and descriptive enough that it provides a general shape of the answer that satisfies your question." And the same essay names the failure relevant here: "Perhaps your problem was a bundle or conflation of several problems masquerading as one? You will need to search all of those problems individually."

So a **shape** is the narrowed form of an answer you can state before you can fill it. For INS-0082 the shape is the four-condition separator (per-slot indexing, independent anchors, habitat-relative valence, an instrument at the objective link). The forbidden filler is a named "best" institution or a weight vector presented as the answer. `SECURITY(???)OPPORTUNITY` is the analogue of `SPACE(???)TIME`: three conditions with an unstated bond, not a named institution in the gap.

## 2. How the shape was obtained

- *Library of Babel*: use the vocabulary you already have that is closest to the thing, look for recurring new terms that overlap or correlate with it, recurse. "With each new term gained, you are able to narrow the space by definition and deepen your vocabulary."
- Fig 6 :30: "The description travels. Fails by being sharpened into terms only we can read. Cast it in effect terms." Fig 9 :30-31: "your words are borrowed too, so describe the effect rather than the cause." So the residual was described as an effect: a worker made secure by a rule while another is kept out by it, rather than as "employment protection has a trade-off".
- *Decoding Tacit Knowledge*: "When contact isn't possible, the best you can send is a plain description of what you'd expect to see, because anyone can check that against the world." This is why `02_outreach_email.md` leads with what a failure would look like.
- *Library of Babel* on stopping: "you know that you are done because the finish line will also be the starting place", with the paradox that you have "composed the answer through the process of locating it". Here the loop stopped when the same structural finding, one lever leaves the intersections standing, kept arriving from the Figures, the EPL literature, the ALMP literature and the flexicurity evaluation. That is convergence on a shape, not a measurement.

## 3. What licenses a name

Only two things, both outside the shape itself:

1. **The ground settles it.** Formula step 5: "the ground settles it. Verified only by what pays. A gate is not the ground." The README: the framework "stops at the limit of what the available instruments can distinguish, and records the lineage so the stop is honest."
2. **A provenanced import from a frame whose closure differs** (Fig 6 :23, Fig 8 :50-52), with a source gate and a shadow test.

*They're Scanning Books When They Should Be Studying Nature* sharpens the second: "The real import is not a new term in the existing space. It is a new dimension of the space." And: "A minimum viable set of six terms is a human's careful guess at reality's compression axes; another sensorium's minimum set need not be those six." Also: "Declaring the primitives does not make them neutral. It makes the choice visible."

So declaring that the residual is per worker-slot, or that the three goods are a union, is a visible choice with provenance (Fig 1, Fig 9), not a discovery of nature's own list. Naming one institution as *the* best turns a visible choice into an invisible one.

## 4. Why the scalar "balance" is the error this essay set names

*The Hard Problem is About Source* gives the mechanism. An instrument reads green and "cannot comment on the source": one green light, or yellow plus blue, or RGB, or CMYK, or a scheme not yet discovered. "If several paths create the same behavior, then behavior is a projection with lossy information." And "An instrument that can't distinguish these cases demonstrates its own inadequacy." It was the spectrometer, not an argument, that resolved it: "It was proven wrong not by argument, but by the arrival of a better instrument."

A national "balance index" is the green reading. Flexicurity, a protection-plus-activation regime, and a coordinated bargaining regime can produce the same index while differing in the bond that produced it. Naming the source (which institution is best) from the reading (the score) is the metamerism error. What is owed is a discriminator, not a name.

*How Intelligence Actually Works* adds the provenance version: "the frame and the photograph are indistinguishable as artifacts, and they carry different warrants." Two regimes with the same three scores are indistinguishable at the level of the score, and the difference that decides behaviour is carried entirely by the lineage. "The recursion converges toward indiscernibility, and indiscernibility is not identity."

## 5. Q: Does "shape, not a name" forbid naming any institution? ANSWERED, with a correction

**No, with a scope condition.** The README's phrase is "what a **missing** thing would have to look like (a shape, not a name)." The subject is a primitive not held in Γ. An institution (employment protection, activation, insurance, bargaining) is not a missing primitive of the framework at all. It is an entity in the labor domain, and naming one as a candidate is a binding from the labor frame, not an invention of a seventh term.

What remains forbidden is:

- presenting a binding as a derivation from the six-term set;
- claiming the binding moves the wall (Fig 6: "IMPORT adds an atom" - it adds none);
- presenting a scalar score as if it carried the bond between the three goods (Fig 12 :5).

**Correction to my own first reading.** In `01_figure_grounded_audit.md` section 0 I initially wrote as if "shape, not a name" might forbid mentioning any named institution. I narrowed that in the audit's section 6 retraction log. The narrow form is kept here. Naming a binding is allowed with provenance; naming the winner is not licensed by any figure line.

## 6. Why "balance" itself had to be bound before searching

`THE_FORMULA.md` "Direction": "a frame cannot check the direction of its own question, because the shape a frame is searching for is not a term it holds. The remedy is a discipline rather than a step: make the sought-for shape an ORDINARY TERM - write down what an answer would look like, bind it, and let it accrue a residual like anything else."

That is exactly what section 0 of `01` does with P1, P2 and P3. Without that binding, the word "balance" would have stayed an unbound preference, and every institution would have seemed to fit it. Fig 5 :62-63: a frame holds the sought-for shape "as a PREFERENCE rather than as a term", so "nothing here can see it". Fig 9 :27-28: "describe it before you go looking, or any frame you pick will seem to fit."

## 7. Two consequences for this solution

**Prestige is not action.** From *The Recursive Self-Improvement Trap*: "Prestige must never be allowed to validate an action directly." And the exact rule: "Prestige can fund an action. It can give an action a budget to be tried. But it cannot settle an action. Only the ground can settle it. The Ground Maintainer must be blind to prestige." A list of high-prestige labor economists who agree with the separator is the named collapse. The legitimate ask is a budget to try the discriminator against a ground.

**My own audit is a simulation, not a ground reading.** Same essay: "LLMs can quickly simulate an approximate answer/output with the formula found in the architecture post." And: "the truly testable output would need to be derived from the real architecture or working the formula yourself." The architecture article adds the caveat in its demo section: outputs "must be seen as simulacra" and "could never be verified if the solutions presented were not contrived to fit the formula." So all substantive files in this folder are labelled simulated derivations checked for internal consistency against the figures. They cannot settle which labor-market institutions actually balance the three goods, and they do not claim to.

## 8. One further consequence, from the society-of-agents essay

*How a Society of Agents Would Work* states the scaling condition in a way that bears directly on outreach: "successive states of a running process are correlated. Two snapshots only count as independent once they are further apart than the process's autocorrelation time." Ten studies from one evidence pool count as one frame wearing ten names. The essay also gives the practical filter: "Use the success rate per try, not the total" and "Keep the rate per kind of situation. A method that is brilliant in one setting must not be drowned by an average across all of them." That is Fig 1 :19 and Fig 9 :60 in a different vocabulary: per-slot, per-situation, never a pooled total. It is used here as corroboration from a low-overlap application, not as a figure line.
