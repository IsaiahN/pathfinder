# Expert verification packet: the Tether on crop and farming-system adaptation

**What this is:** a claim in a form a crop scientist, breeder, agronomist or agricultural ecologist can check. Mode, derivation and refuters are stated before the conclusion, so a reader knows what to look for and what would settle it.

**Mode: `general` on the shape, `specified` on the per-domain verdicts, `gap` on the literal winner list. Not `grounded`.** No measurement is claimed. This is a shape and a checking discipline, not a result.

**Status of this packet:** a simulated derivation checked for internal consistency against the figures, not a ground reading. It cannot settle the question it reports on.

## 1. The claim, in one paragraph

The question asks for a nameable winner list, and the figures structurally refuse that object. Success is not a property carried by a crop. It is maintained contact, per slot, between a genotype, a management set and an enumerated habitat, against a ground that does not update on the frame's own report. Genotype, environment and management form a union of overlapping conditions, not a partition one lever can clear, so a rank that flips with the environment list is local adaptation rather than a standing winner. The farming system is a maintained seat whose power is introduction, not exclusion, so a method travels where a recording of one season does not. The literal list, a universal diversification percentage and the identity of the next winning crop are a declared `gap`. What the packet offers is the shape any candidate must have, five refuters written in advance, and the imports needed to check it.

## 2. Three checkable sub-claims

### C1 :framework-internal; checkable by reading Figures 11 and 1
- **Statement:** no standing list of winners; valence is relative to the residual; the residual must stay indexed per slot.
- **Source, verbatim:** `Fig 11 :51` "No standing list of good actors survives a change of residual." Also `Fig 11 :49` "An actor's valence is relative to the residual, not a property it carries." Also `Fig 1 :19` "The residual is indexed by object slot, not aggregated."
- **What to check:** is the per-slot reading the right object for a non-stationary target, or is a global superlative defensible in your system? World corroboration, secondary only: Romagosa et al. write that "it is very hard to identify the 'best' variety across a diverse set of environments subjected to complex biotic and abiotic factors and interactions generally causing significant changes in varietal rank."
- **Refuter:** a crop-and-system pair that keeps its rank across all enumerated condition classes at multiple slots under a non-updating ground, with no valence flip; or a global statistic whose per-slot residuals are all near zero and trending down.

### C2 :agronomy-facing; checkable against G x E x M and trial design
- **Statement:** the residual is a union of at least genotype, environment and management conditions; such systems over a fixed atom set with different bonds are Fig 12 isomers, and those sharing only a pattern are a functional group.
- **Source, verbatim:** `Fig 9 :55` "A bundled problem is usually a union of overlapping conditions, not a list of separate ones." Also `Fig 12 :117` "isomer. Same atoms, different bond, different substance. The difference is invisible in the formula and decisive in the behaviour." Also `Fig 12 :123` "functional group. A substructure whose presence fixes the outcome."
- **What to check:** in your own data, does one practice clear the intersections of genotype, environment and management, or only the regions where one condition binds? Is a simultaneous strip intercrop and a sequential legume rotation the same substance over the same atom set? World source, secondary only: Cooper et al. 2021 state that climate change introduces "directional changes in the environmental dimension of the G × E × M factorial" and call for "co-design new G–M technologies for a non-stationary TPE".
- **Refuter:** exhibit a partition into disjoint crop classes with one lever that clears every class including the intersections; or show two systems over a fixed atom set with different bonds that produce the same outcome bundle under the same declared shock at the same slot.

### C3 :measurement-facing; the broken link
- **Statement:** "adapt successfully" has no discriminating instrument at the objective link. Yield, soil carbon, income, biodiversity and diet are proxies or different currencies, not a demonstration of shock adaptation.
- **Source, verbatim:** `Fig 3 :27` "A link with no instrument is not a link that works. It is a link nobody has looked at." Also `Fig 12 :125` "A composition costs in two currencies, and they do not add."
- **What to check:** name an instrument in your system that separates direct shock adaptation from a good mean season, irrigation masking, or an unshifted pest complex. World source, secondary only: Handayani et al. 2026 state that "improvements in soil health, biodiversity, yield, income, or diet do not demonstrate the capacity to absorb, recover from, or adapt to climatic shocks", and that "heterogeneous evidence precluded a pooled effect estimate". Tamburini et al. 2020 report a neutral yield effect (lnRR = 0.01) alongside strong context dependency; Sridhar et al. report 20-38% gains. These disagree and are not pooled here.
- **Refuter:** a discriminating measurement showing that one of those proxy outcomes is sufficient for demonstrated adaptation at the slot under a declared shock; or a common unit in which yield, stability, labour, biodiversity and risk are commensurable with a declared conversion.

## 3. What is explicitly not claimed

- Not a winner list, a ranking, a diversification percentage or a named crop.
- Not "diversification always wins". The figure rule refuses standing lists, and the world syntheses conflict.
- Not a derivation of G x E x M from the six-term set. That is an import with provenance and a shadow test.
- Not a literal mapping of the six substrate terms onto agronomy. It is a `general` recursion analogue.
- Not that "adapt successfully" has been measured. It is bound as an ordinary term with an instrument owed.
- Not grounded. No multi-environment, multi-year shock reading was taken here.

## 5. What would settle it

1. A shock-discriminating instrument operationalising the direct-versus-proxy distinction.
2. A rank-invariance test under a second enumerated environment-type distribution at the same slots.
3. A translocation test: reproduce every required condition, enumerate the inherited actors, hold rank for multiple seasons against a non-updating ground.
4. The five standing falsifiers in `01_figure_grounded_audit.md` section 4, plus the import and figure-revision conditions.

## 6. What an expert can check in twenty minutes

1. Read `Fig 11 :49`, `Fig 11 :51`, `Fig 1 :19`, `Fig 9 :55`, `Fig 12 :117` and `Fig 3 :27`. Six short passages, verbatim in the figure text.
2. Check C2 against your own G x E x M data: does one practice clear the intersections, or only the single-condition regions?
3. Name one instrument that distinguishes direct shock adaptation from a proxy at your slot, or say why none is possible.
4. If you disagree, send the failure. A failure is more useful than an endorsement, and a disagreement sent before stage 2 is recorded as a verdict rather than pooled as later agreement.
