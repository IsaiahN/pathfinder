## QUESTION

Decide, in effect terms, what the Tether says about the request for a list of crops and farming systems most likely to adapt successfully to future climate conditions. Option A: treat the superlative as a nameable standing list and rank candidates. Option B: refuse the standing list, return the shape any adapting option must have, keep verdicts per slot and per domain, and name the imports and instruments owed. What each leads to: A produces a `general`-mode verdict expressed as a ranking, aggregates away the live slot signal, and spends the independence of the people asked to endorse it; B produces a checkable shape, a separator with pre-registered refuters, and an open binding until a ground settles it.

## DOMAIN CENSUS

R by arity, symmetry and scale, then one line per domain with actors, currency and anchor; partition or union; order by dependency.

- **R (the residual).** Per-slot gap between the predicted performance of a genotype-and-management package and what happens as conditions move. Arity: at least three interacting dimensions plus their intersections, so a **union compound**, not a one-variable question. Symmetry: "adapt successfully" is an ordered comparison against a threshold, not a symmetric join. Scale: crop organism, field and farm seat, region and food supply. Fig 9 names the compound case: a case in two conditions is not a harder case of either.
- **Domain 1, crop genotype and physiology.** Actors: genotypes, traits, seed systems. Currency: yield and persistence under sampled environment-types. Anchor: multi-environment trials, measured yield. A binding from the agriculture frame onto held slot terms, not a new primitive.
- **Domain 2, farming system as maintained seat.** Actors: the manager and practices (variety choice, sowing date, density, irrigation, soil management, diversification, livestock integration). Currency: introduced actors and changed contact. Anchor: multi-year on-farm outcomes and a seat able to refuse a failing practice.
- **Domain 3, climate and habitat.** Actors: conditions (light, water, temperature, season, terrain) and relations (pollination, predation, competition, dependency). Currency: selection pressure and cascading effects. Anchor: observations, which update, so this is habitat, not the ground. Enumerated, never composed.
- **Domain 4, verification and research frame.** Actors: breeders, agronomists, crop modellers, reviewers, farmers. Currency: agreement and residual. Anchor: measured yield that does not update on the frame's own report. In this simulated run all four domains draw on overlapping evidence pools, so their agreement is one pool talking, not independent corroboration.
- **Order by dependency.** Perceive the conditions, acquire terms for what they afford, bind an objective with an instrument, then plan, then carry what worked (Fig 3 :20). Settle a first-order candidate before composing it into a regional claim (Fig 12 :104). Describe the residual before choosing a frame (Fig 9 :27-28). The chain currently breaks between vocabulary and objective, where no shock-discriminating instrument exists.

Strain test applied: the substrate analogue in Fig 13 governs a physical minimum set and is used here only as a cross-scale recursion analogue (Fig 4 :20). Pressed literally, it would strain; re-census assigns it to `general` mode. The diversification meta-analyses conflict on a pooled yield number; re-census assigns them different estimands and sends them back to the census rather than flipping the verdict.

## VERDICT (from the figures)

The shape survives; the literal object does not. There is no standing list of crops and farming systems that are most likely to adapt. Success is per-slot maintained contact between genotype, management and an enumerated habitat against a non-updating ground, with rank that can flip when the environment list changes. The farming system is a maintained seat whose power is introduction, not exclusion, so adaptation is permanent work and a method travels where a one-season recording does not. Mean yield, stability, labour, biodiversity and risk are different currencies and do not add into one superlative. The literal list, a universal diversification percentage and the named next winner are a `gap`: unreached at this budget, with INWARD (a shock-discriminating instrument) and OUTWARD (G x E x M, ecology, earth-system assessment, each with provenance) routes named. Mode `general` on the shape, `specified` on the per-domain verdicts, not `grounded`.

- **cost if wrong:** the cost is spending a season and a reputation on a recommendation whose rank flips when the sampled environment-types change, or on a pooled number that hides the one live slot. Fig 2 :42 prices the corrective: "Buy independence in proportion to the cost of being wrong." That cost is avoidable by leading with the shape, the per-slot indexing and the refuters, and by buying independent readings before recommending a binding.
- **undoable:** yes. A binding from the agriculture frame onto a held slot term is removable without touching the shape. A recommendation can be superseded by a corrected one as new environment-types arrive, and no primitive has to be withdrawn. The redistribution rule is the same one that makes a managed ecosystem permanent work: introduction is available, and the seat keeps the right to refuse.
- **reversed by:** a ground reading, not agreement. A shock-discriminating instrument that separates direct adaptation from its proxies would settle the objective link; a rank-invariance test under a second environment-type distribution at the same slots would licence or kill a standing winner; a translocation that reproduces every required condition and holds rank for multiple seasons would narrow the import cost; a figure re-census yielding a verbatim line that names a crop or percentage would break the `gap`. Prestige, endorsement and pooled agreement are not such instruments.
