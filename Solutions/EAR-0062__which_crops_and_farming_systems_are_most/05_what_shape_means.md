## 1. A shape is a constraint, not a filler

The canonical usage rule says the framework returns "what a missing thing would have to look like (a shape, not a name)", then stops at the limit of what the instruments can distinguish and records the lineage. `Fig 9 :32`: "you get a kind of thing, never the answer itself". `Fig 13 :134-135`: "Unreachable is a verdict here, not a silence", and "It cannot say which primitive is missing, because naming it would mean already holding it."

From *The Library of Babel* (opened): "you can cut your search short if the set of books returned is small and descriptive enough that it provides a general shape of the answer that satisfies your question."

For this question a **shape** is therefore the narrowed form of a successful adaptation that can be stated before a crop is named. It constrains; it does not fill. Concretely, the shape returned in `07_the_tether_answer.md` is:

> A successful option maintains contact, per slot, between a genotype, a management set and an enumerated habitat, against a ground that does not update on the frame's own report; it carries a method rather than a recording of one occasion; it imports only with provenance and a shadow test; and it is refusable by a failure condition written in advance.

That is a shape. "Sorghum with intercropping in the Sahel is the most adaptive system" is a name, and the ground has not settled it.

## 2. How the shape is obtained

- *Library of Babel*: use the vocabulary you already have that is closest to the thing, then recurse. "With each new term gained, you narrow the space by definition and deepen your vocabulary." Here the starting vocabulary is per-slot residual, environment-type, contact, introduction and anchor. The recurring new terms from the agriculture frame are G x E x M, target population of environments, environment-type frequency, direct resilience evidence.
- `Fig 9 :30-31`: "your words are borrowed too, so describe the effect rather than the cause". So we describe the effect (a rank that does not flip when the condition list changes) rather than the cause (a named gene, a named practice).
- `Fig 6 :30`: "The description travels. Fails by being sharpened into terms only we can read. Cast it in effect terms." Cooper et al.'s "non-stationary TPE" is a good travelling term because it names an effect (environment-type frequencies move), not a mechanism.
- *Library of Babel* on bundles: "Perhaps your problem was a bundle or conflation of several problems masquerading as one?" That is the exact case here, and it is also `Fig 9 :55`: "A bundled problem is usually a union of overlapping conditions, not a list of separate ones."
- *Library of Babel* on stopping: "the finish line will also be the starting place", because by then the answer has been composed through the locating. Here the stop is different and more honest: we stop at the instrument limit, record the lineage, and report `gap`.

## 3. What licenses a name

Two things, both outside the shape itself:

1. **The ground settles it.** Formula step 5: "the ground settles it. Verified only by what pays. A gate is not the ground." For this question that means multi-environment, multi-year, shock-period measurement with a seat able to refuse a failing practice. `Fig 2 :29`: "An anchor is whatever settles the question and does not change its answer because of what any frame thinks."
2. **A provenanced import from a frame whose closure differs**, with a source gate and a shadow test (`Fig 6 :23`, `Fig 8 :50`). G x E x M modelling, ecology and earth-system assessment are such frames. Their terms are admitted with provenance, not as answers.

From the carried exemplar reading of *They're Scanning Books When They Should* (not re-opened this session): "The real import is not a new term in the existing space. It is a new dimension of the space." Also: "Declaring the primitives does not make them neutral. It makes the choice visible." The six-term set, and the crop-slot vocabulary built on it, are a visible choice, not nature's own list.

## 4. The source error, and why it bites here

*The Hard Problem is About Source* (opened): "If several paths create the same behavior, then behavior is a projection with lossy information (it cannot define consciousness in full)." The essay's metamerism case: one instrument reads green, and the source might be one green light, or yellow plus blue, or RGB, or CMYK, or a scheme not yet discovered. The reading does not determine the source. "An instrument that can't distinguish these cases demonstrates its own inadequacy, it cannot claim anything substantial about what is being measured or what causes it." It was the spectrometer, not an argument, that resolved it.

The analogy for crop adaptation is direct. A high mean yield at a slot is a reading. Its source might be a genuinely shock-stable genotype, or favourable environment-types that happened to be sampled, or irrigation that masked water stress, or a pest complex that had not yet shifted, or a combination not yet characterised. Handayani et al. 2026 make the same point without the framework's vocabulary: "improvements in soil health, biodiversity, yield, income, or diet do not demonstrate the capacity to absorb, recover from, or adapt to climatic shocks." Naming "drought tolerance" as the source of a good reading, without an instrument that discriminates among those paths, is the metamerism error. What is owed is a better instrument, not a name.

## 5. Correction to my own first pass

I initially applied "shape, not a name" to forbid naming any crop at all. **That was an overstatement.** The canonical phrase governs a *missing* primitive, one not held in the library. Maize, sorghum, millet, wheat, rice, intercropping and agroforestry are bindings from the agriculture frame onto terms the derivation already holds (a genotype, an environment-type, a management act). Naming them is provenance, not the invention of a seventh primitive. What remains wrong is treating any of those bindings as *settled by the framework*. The framework returns the shape; the ground settles the binding. Fig 6 :21: "MINT composes inside the closure and can never add an atom."

Similarly, I initially read Fig 11 :51 as a ban on recommending diversification. **Narrowed:** it is a ban on a standing list of winners. A maintained method, with its arrangement stated and a refuter written in advance, is admissible. `Fig 11 :63`: "a managed ecosystem is permanent work rather than a solved state."

Both corrections are kept visible rather than silently applied.

## 6. Why "most likely" is the wrong object for a shape

The superlative is a comparison (`Fig 12 :87`: "a threshold relation between two quantities"; Operators table: "a goal is a comparison, which is why a vocabulary without it cannot state one"). But a shape is not a quantity to be ranked. It is the form any candidate must have before ranking is meaningful. Asking which crop is most likely to adapt, before binding what adaptation would look like at a slot, is asking for the ranking before the goal exists. That is the broken middle link of Fig 3 :25-27, where "having terms for what a situation affords was taken for a dictionary and it is a grammar".

Romagosa et al. supply the world-side version: "it is very hard to identify the 'best' variety across a diverse set of environments ... generally causing significant changes in varietal rank." A ranking that changes with the environment list is not a shape; it is a local reading.

## 7. Prestige, simulation, and the honest stop

From *The Recursive Self-Improvement Trap* (opened): "Prestige can fund an action. It can give an action a budget to be tried. But it cannot settle an action. Only the ground can settle it. The Ground Maintainer must be blind to prestige." An institute's endorsement of a crop gets the claim a budget to be tried. It does not make it true. `Fig 2 :42`: "Buy independence in proportion to the cost of being wrong."

The same essay gives the standing of this whole folder: "LLMs can quickly simulate an approximate answer/output with the formula found in the architecture post. It will often be better than the generic outputs that you will usually get otherwise too (intelligence is essentially arrangement). However, the truly testable output would need to be derived from the real architecture or working the formula yourself." So `01_figure_grounded_audit.md` and this file are simulated derivations, checked for internal consistency against the figures. They are not tests of any crop claim and must be labelled as such. They cannot settle anything they report on.

**The honest stop:** the separator is stated, the refuters are written, the imports are provenanced, and the name of the winning crop is left to a ground that has not been read here. `Fig 13 :134`: "Unreachable is a verdict here, not a silence". Indiscernibility is not identity: two crops with the same trial reading need not have the same source of adaptation, and the record of the route is what keeps that checkable.
