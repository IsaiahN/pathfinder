## The question stated in effect terms

A crop or farming system "adapts successfully" when it keeps contact with a ground (measured yield, persistence, food security at a slot) while the habitat around that slot changes (temperature, water, season, pests, soil, markets). The question asks for a nameable winner list. What each option leads to:

- **Option A, take the question literally and return a list.** This binds "most likely" as a property carried by named crops and systems. It flattens a per-slot residual into one global average and, by the figures' own valence rule, a standing list of good actors does not survive a change of residual.
- **Option B, return the shape plus a separator.** This keeps the verdicts per crop-slot and per domain, names what any adapting option must look like, states a refuter written in advance, and leaves the specific names as provenanced bindings from the agriculture frame that the ground has not yet settled.

The answer returned here is B. It is a shape and a checking discipline, not a ranking.

## What is deliberately not here

- No personal emails of named individuals.
- No ranking of people by how likely they are to agree.
- No bulk-send plan.
- No global winner list, no optimal diversification percentage, no universal crop recommendation. No figure line returns those, and `Fig 9 :32` says the frame gives "you get a kind of thing, never the answer itself".

Three reasons, in the figures' own words:

1. **A standing list is refused at the ground.** `Fig 11 :51`: "No standing list of good actors survives a change of residual." Valence is relative to the residual (`Fig 11 :49`), so a crop that is a benefactor at one slot is a malfactor at another.
2. **The average hides the live signal.** `Fig 1 :19`: "The residual is indexed by object slot, not aggregated." `Fig 1 :22`: "Aggregating across slots is how a live signal disappears into an average."
3. **Selecting for receptiveness manufactures the disqualifying correlation.** `Fig 2 :40`: "agreement about the world is worthless when the agreeing frames share an evidence pool". A mailing list of people already convinced that a crop wins is a convention, not a witness.

Legally: unsolicited bulk email to scraped addresses breaches GDPR and CAN-SPAM style rules in many jurisdictions and gets the sending domain blacklisted. That is a practical failure on top of the structural one. Every venue in `03_public_contact_points.csv` is institutional and opt-in.

## The one-paragraph answer

The question as posed asks for a nameable winner list, and the figures structurally refuse that. What survives is a shape and a separator. Success is not a property carried by a crop. It is maintained contact between a genotype, a management set and a specific habitat, registered per slot against a ground that does not update on the frame's own report (`Fig 11 :39`, `Fig 11 :40`, `Fig 2 :29`). The residual is a union of overlapping conditions, not a partition one lever can clear (`Fig 9 :55`), which is what the crop-science literature's G x E x M factorial independently describes. The farming system is a maintained seat that can introduce actors and cannot subtract the habitat (`Fig 11 :61`), so adaptation is permanent work rather than a solved state (`Fig 11 :63`). The literal list, a universal diversification percentage and the identity of the next winning crop are declared `gap`, recorded as unreached and not as silence (`Fig 13 :134`).
