## Three things in the world literature that bear directly on this question

1. **The proxy trap is named in the literature itself.** Handayani et al. 2026 say that improvements in soil health, biodiversity, yield, income or diet "do not demonstrate the capacity to absorb, recover from, or adapt to climatic shocks". This is the same object as Fig 3 :27, "A link with no instrument is not a link that works. It is a link nobody has looked at." Cite it when explaining why a pooled yield or carbon number is not a verdict on adaptation.
2. **The three syntheses disagree about diversification's price, and the disagreement is the finding.** Tamburini et al. find a neutral yield effect with strong context dependency; Sridhar et al. report 20-38% better average yields; Handayani et al. say heterogeneous evidence prevented any pooled estimate. They are not three witnesses to one quantity. `Fig 9 :60`: "Use filters for the budget and witnesses for the verdict. Never let a filter hand you a verdict." The percentages tell us where to spend the next season of work, not which system wins.
3. **The G x E x M frame is a method, not an answer.** Cooper et al. describe moving "from the current unprepared state of describing G × E × M outcomes to a future responsive state equipped to predict the crop productivity consequences of G–M technology combinations". That is a generator, and `Fig 4 :22` permits methods to cross: "Only methods cross up. Only head starts come down." Do not present their TPE framework as a result the Tether produced.

A fetch failure is recorded honestly: the ScienceDirect article page for the pest-and-resilience review returned HTTP 400 on direct fetch; its abstract was seen in the search index but no quote from it is used as a primary source here, and the relevant claim was rerouted to the IPCC chapter, which was opened in full.

## Distribution: offer, do not push

- `Fig 4 :25`: "A recording carried upward looks like knowledge and is a description of one occasion." Sending the full figure set before anyone has described a slot-level residual sends a recording, not a generator. Lead with the shape and the refuters. Offer links after.
- `Fig 10 :42`: "A verifier that can reconstruct the claim cannot verify it." A recipient who has absorbed the framework before being asked for a verdict has joined the frame being checked.
- `Fig 9 :27-28`: "describe it before you go looking, or any frame you pick will seem to fit." Ask what they would expect to see if the shape claim were true before giving them the article.
- `Fig 2 :42`: "Buy independence in proportion to the cost of being wrong." One-to-one contact through public venues; no bulk send, no list ranked by likely agreement.
- *The Recursive Self-Improvement Trap*: "Prestige can fund an action. It can give an action a budget to be tried. But it cannot settle an action. Only the ground can settle it." An institute's endorsement is a budget to try the separator, not a verdict.

## The two-stage offer

**Stage 1.** Send the residual in effect terms, the shape claim and the five pre-registered refuters. Ask: what would you count as a failure here? Record the answer before any theory is shown. This is `Fig 10 :52`, "Bring a characterised residual, never the source", and `Fig 2 :45`, "Frames range against the ground. They do not negotiate with it."

**Stage 2, opt-in only.** If they are curious, offer the three local framework links. The figure text, not the summary, is the authority. Later agreement is never pooled as corroboration, because `Fig 2 :40` says "agreement about the world is worthless when the agreeing frames share an evidence pool".

**Stage 3.** Offer the formula as a method to reuse. Each user writes down what would refute their result before running it and states the mode of what they get. A frame cannot check the direction of its own question; the "Direction" section of THE_FORMULA makes the sought-for shape an ordinary term so it can be refused.

**Hard precondition:** stages 1 to 3 work only if the stage-1 output survives. As drafted, the objective-measurement link still needs an instrument (F4 in `01_figure_grounded_audit.md`). Do not send a claim that "adapt successfully" has been measured when it has not.

## Legal and venue cautions

Public institutional contact pages and opt-in submission routes avoid the GDPR and CAN-SPAM failure that comes with scraped personal addresses. Check each journal's policy on simulated or AI-assisted work before submitting: an assessment-style claim generated by a model and checked only for internal consistency is not a ground reading, and several publishers require that to be declared.
