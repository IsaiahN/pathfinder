# The Tether's answer: which crops and farming systems are most likely to adapt successfully

**Mode: `general` on the structural shape, `specified` on the per-domain verdicts, `gap` on the literal winner list. Not `grounded`.** Simulated derivation checked for internal consistency against the figures, not a ground reading. No trial, measurement or field observation was taken by this author.

## 1. The residual it answers

The residual is per slot: a crop genotype, a place, a season and a management set together. It is the gap between what the current package predicts and what happens as conditions move. `Fig 1 :19`: "The residual is indexed by object slot, not aggregated." `Fig 10 :24`: "the ground does not decay, the channel does". The channel fails when the trial network stops sampling new environment-types, when a yield metric saturates, or when a regional average pools away the live slot.

The question asks for a superlative, which is a comparison (`Fig 12 :87`: "a threshold relation between two quantities"), not a property carried by a crop (`Fig 11 :49`: "An actor's valence is relative to the residual, not a property it carries."). The residual is a union of overlapping conditions (Fig 9 :55), so verdicts are returned per domain and never averaged into one ranking.

## 2. The shape

1. **No standing winner list.** `Fig 11 :51`: "No standing list of good actors survives a change of residual." Success is maintained contact between a genotype, a management set and an enumerated habitat, not a trait carried by a crop (Fig 11 :39-40). The named crops and systems are bindings from the agriculture frame onto held terms, provenanced as such; the framework returns the shape, not the binding.
2. **The residual must be enumerated in effect terms.** `Fig 11 :43`: "The habitat is enumerated, never composed." The list includes conditions and relations (Fig 11 :46-47), plus the genotype and management dimensions that Cooper et al. 2021 supply as an imported frame. Contact cascades (Fig 11 :52), and the DDT case in Fig 11 :54 shows that the residual that matters can be three steps out.
3. **The system is a maintained seat, not a product.** Its power is asymmetric: "Introduction is available; exclusion is not." (Fig 11 :61). It keeps the channel to a non-updating ground reachable and keeps a seat able to refuse a failing practice, so adaptation is "permanent work rather than a solved state" (Fig 11 :63). Methods cross; recordings of one occasion do not (Fig 4 :22, :25).
4. **The union needs low-overlap frames, and imports need provenance.** `Fig 8 :50`: "Convergent derivation and adopted import look identical in the contents." The G x E x M and target-population-of-environments frame from Cooper et al., the direct-versus-proxy evidence distinction from Handayani et al., and the hazard cascades in IPCC AR6 WGII Ch 5 enter with a source gate and a shadow test. Agreement from a shared pool is one witness (Fig 2 :40).
5. **"Success" is a comparison with no instrument at the crucial link.** Handayani et al. 2026: "improvements in soil health, biodiversity, yield, income, or diet do not demonstrate the capacity to absorb, recover from, or adapt to climatic shocks." Fig 3 :27: "A link with no instrument is not a link that works. It is a link nobody has looked at." Until a shock-discriminating measurement is bound, the objective is `specified` and owed an instrument.
6. **The superlative sums incommensurable currencies.** `Fig 12 :125`: "A composition costs in two currencies, and they do not add." Mean yield, stability, labour, biodiversity and risk do not add into one "most likely" score.

## 4. What it explicitly does not say

- Not a ranking. No crop, system or percentage is named as the winner.
- Not "diversification always wins". `Fig 11 :51` refuses standing lists, and the three world syntheses disagree on the yield effect. The disagreement is recorded, not resolved by picking one.
- Not a derivation of G x E x M from the six-term set. That frame is an import with provenance and a shadow test.
- Not a claim that the six substrate terms map literally onto agronomy. The mapping is a recursion analogue at `general` mode (Fig 4 :20).
- Not a claim that "adapt successfully" has been measured. It has been bound as an ordinary term with an owed instrument.
- Not grounded. No multi-environment, multi-year shock reading was taken here.

## 5. What would settle it: the ground

A source-discriminating instrument, not a further argument. From *The Hard Problem is About Source* (opened): "An instrument that can't distinguish these cases demonstrates its own inadequacy, it cannot claim anything substantial about what is being measured or what causes it." The corresponding questions:

- **Shock-recovery discriminator.** A measurement that separates genuine capacity to absorb, recover from and adapt to a climatic shock from favourable environment-types, irrigation masking, an unshifted pest complex or a good mean season. Handayani et al. supply the direct-versus-proxy distinction; an instrument must operationalise it.
- **Rank-invariance test.** Re-rank the same candidate set under a second enumerated environment-type distribution at the same slots. A rank that flips is local adaptation, not a standing winner (Fig 11 :49-51; Romagosa et al. on varietal rank).
- **Translocation test.** Move the candidate into a new habitat, reproduce every condition the goal required, enumerate the inherited actors and hold rank for multiple seasons against a non-updating ground (Fig 11 :66-69). The two silent failures are what you failed to reproduce and what you unintentionally introduced.
- **Standing falsifiers** from `01_figure_grounded_audit.md` section 4: five conditions written before the conclusion, plus the import and figure-revision conditions.

## 6. The honest stop

**Unreached, not unreachable.** The figure set gives the shape any adapting option must have and the refuters that would withdraw it. It does not give the name. `Fig 13 :134`: "Unreachable is a verdict here, not a silence". Formula step 7 keeps SEARCHED, INWARD and OUTWARD distinct: more search inside the current closure cannot settle a non-stationary target; INWARD owes an objective instrument; OUTWARD imports G x E x M, ecology and earth-system assessment with provenance. The loop stops at the limit of the available instruments and records the lineage so the stop is checkable. Indiscernibility is not identity: two crops with the same trial reading need not have the same source of adaptation.

## 7. One-paragraph version for outreach

> The Tether does not name the crops or systems that will win under future climate conditions, and it does not claim a final answer. It says that success is not a property carried by a crop: it is maintained contact between a genotype, a management set and an enumerated habitat, registered per slot against a ground that does not update on the frame's own report. The question is a union of genotype, environment and management conditions, so one lever clears only the regions where a single condition binds; a rank that changes with the environment list is local, not a standing winner. The farming system is a maintained seat whose power is introduction, not exclusion, so adaptation is permanent work and a method travels where a recording of one season does not. What the framework returns is the shape any candidate must have, plus refuters written in advance and the imports needed to check it. "Adapt successfully" still owes an instrument that separates a real shock response from its proxies. The winner stays open until the ground settles it.
