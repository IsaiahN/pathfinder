# Outreach draft: check the separator before you decide whether the framing is worth reading

**Use:** one-to-one, or as a cover letter attached to a submission through the public venues in `03_public_contact_points.csv`. **Not** for bulk send.

**Hard precondition:** do not send until the objective-measurement link in `01_figure_grounded_audit.md` F4 is repaired in your own terms. The offer collapses on contact if "adapt successfully" has no threshold and no instrument.

---

**Subject:** One checkable output on crop adaptation, before any theory

> Hi [name],
>
> I have one specific output I would like you to check against what you already know from trials and on-farm work. I am not asking you to read the framework first, and I am not asking you to agree with it. If the output does not survive, there is nothing else to look at.
>
> **The output (`specified`, not `grounded`):** the question "which crops and farming systems are most likely to adapt successfully" asks for a nameable winner list. The derivation returns that the figures structurally refuse the list and return a separator instead. Success is not a property carried by a crop. It is maintained contact between a genotype, a management set and a specific habitat, registered per slot against a ground that does not update on the frame's own report. The condition classes have to be enumerated, not composed: light, water, temperature, season, terrain, plus relations such as pollination, predation, competition and dependency, plus the genotype and management dimensions. This is a shape and a checking discipline, not a measurement and not a ranking.
>
> **What I am not claiming:** that maize, millet, sorghum, intercropping, agroforestry or any particular diversification percentage is the answer. Those are bindings from the agriculture frame onto terms the derivation already holds. The framework returns a shape, not a name. Convergent derivation and adopted import look identical in the contents and only the record separates them, so until provenance and a shadow test are shown, a named winner carries zero evidentiary weight. I am also not claiming that pooled yield effects settle the question: mean yield, stability, labour, biodiversity and risk are different currencies and do not add.
>
> **What would refute the shape claim:**
> 1. a crop-and-system pair that keeps its rank across all enumerated condition classes at multiple slots under a non-updating ground, with no valence flip;
> 2. a global winner statistic whose per-slot residuals are all near zero and trending down;
> 3. a partition, rather than a union, of genotype, environment and management conditions, such that one lever clears every class including the intersections;
> 4. a discriminating instrument showing that a proxy outcome such as soil carbon, income or mean yield is sufficient for demonstrated shock adaptation at the slot;
> 5. an agent-only crop improvement that moves the ground without changing contact.
>
> I wrote these down before sending this. If any one holds, I will retract the relevant claim. A one-line "this fails because..." is more useful to me than agreement. If you would rather not engage, no reply is needed.
>
> **If it survives, and only then:** the sources are below. The figure text, not my summary of it, is the authority.
> - Verbatim figure text with line numbers (local framework file): `FRAMEWORK/reference/03-FIGURES_TEXT.md`
> - Canonical usage rule (local framework file): `FRAMEWORK/reference/FIGURES_README_canonical.md`
> - Eight-step loop and mode rule (local framework file): `FRAMEWORK/figures/THE_FORMULA.md`
>
> **If you want to use the machinery:** the formula is in the figures link. Run it on a crop-slot you know well and can check against a ground. Write down what would refute your result *before* you run it, and state the mode of what you get. The essays warn that outputs can be contrived to fit the formula, and that a theory cannot validate itself. A reported failure is more useful to me than a reported success.
>
> - [name]

## Why this version is ordered this way

| the move | figure-grounded reason |
|---|---|
| Output first, theory second | `Fig 10 :52`: "Bring a characterised residual, never the source." Frames range against the ground separately, they do not negotiate with it. |
| Do not ask for agreement | `Fig 2 :35`: "Alignment is triangulation, not negotiation. A mirror cannot correct you." |
| Pre-registered refuter before conclusion | "a falsifier written before the run is a design instrument, not only an honesty device." A refuter written after the result is an explanation, not a test. |
| Mode stated on the output | `THE_FORMULA.md`: "Mode. general · specified · grounded. State which. Accepted means the ground settled it; otherwise candidate." |
| No winner list, no percentage | `Fig 11 :51`: "No standing list of good actors survives a change of residual." `Fig 9 :32`: "you get a kind of thing, never the answer itself" |
| Enumerate the habitat rather than name stressors first | `Fig 11 :43`: "The habitat is enumerated, never composed." `Fig 9 :27-28`: "describe it before you go looking, or any frame you pick will seem to fit" |
| Provenance withheld until asked | `Fig 8 :50`: "Convergent derivation and adopted import look identical in the contents." |
| No claim that yield effects settle adaptation | Handayani et al. 2026: "improvements in soil health, biodiversity, yield, income, or diet do not demonstrate the capacity to absorb, recover from, or adapt to climatic shocks." This is a world source, secondary corroboration only. |
| No request for endorsement or prestige | *The Recursive Self-Improvement Trap*: "Prestige can fund an action. It can give an action a budget to be tried. But it cannot settle an action. Only the ground can settle it." |
| Offer the formula as a method to reuse | `Fig 4 :22`: "Only methods cross up. Only head starts come down." |
| Ask each user to state their own ground and refuter | A frame cannot check the direction of its own question (Formula "Direction"), so the sought-for shape must be made an ordinary term before the run. |
| Institutional venues only, one to one | `Fig 2 :42`: "Buy independence in proportion to the cost of being wrong." Bulk sends selected for agreement manufacture the disqualifying correlation. |

## Recording the verdict before showing the theory

Stage 1 is the paragraph above plus the five refuters. Record the recipient's answer in the packet log *before* any figure or essay link is shown. Their later interest in the framework is a separate event and is never pooled as corroboration. `Fig 2 :40`: "agreement about the world is worthless when the agreeing frames share an evidence pool". If a recipient returns a refutation, copy it into `11_audit.md` with provenance and have the auditor seat re-evaluate it as if it came from any other source.
