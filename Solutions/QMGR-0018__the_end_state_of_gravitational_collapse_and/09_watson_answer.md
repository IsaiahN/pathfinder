## VERDICT (from the figures)

The shape survives; the names do not. The singularity is a term held by classical GR that owes a refusal. The replacement atom cannot be composed inside classical GR's closure and must be imported from a frame whose closure differs. It sits at second order over the settled GR and QM molecules, at the threshold where the two regimes compare. The framework returns the shape of that replacement and cannot name it, so the identity of the end state is a `gap` by design, not a silence. The search is blocked at the objective link of Fig 3, so inward instrument work and outward import are the two distinct remedies. The candidate list is a union of overlapping conditions (regularity, unitarity, causal extension) and must be unbundled. This is a `specified`, ungrounded, candidate result.

- **cost if wrong:** bounded and locatable. The packet is mode-labelled, states refuters before the conclusion and records the lineage, so an error is findable rather than invisible. The real cost is spending the independence of outside readers by sending a named binding instead of a shape. Fig 2 :42 prices that: "Buy independence in proportion to the cost of being wrong." That cost is avoidable by leading with the shape claim and its four refuters.
- **undoable:** yes. The forbidden names are withheld without touching the derivation, and `07_the_tether_answer.md` and `08_expert_verification_packet.md` already do so. A sent claim can be superseded by the corrected packet, and no primitive has to be withdrawn.
- **reversed by:** a ground reading, not agreement. A source-discriminating instrument at the contact that bounds the curvature invariant, fixes the information endpoint and tests causal extension would move the relevant condition toward `grounded`. A classical derivation with no added atom returning a finite terminal state, or a figure re-census in which the six-term set uniquely selects one name, would break the shape claim. An instrument shown to have registered about the terminal slot would reopen the objective link of Fig 3. Until one of these fires, the verdict stands as `candidate`.
