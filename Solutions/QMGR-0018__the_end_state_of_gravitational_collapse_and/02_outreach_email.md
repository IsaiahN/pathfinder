The bound moves only on contact with a frame whose closure differs."* Fig 8 :32 says *"Closure is idempotent. Composing inside a closed set never leaves it, so no search, however long, adds an atom."* So the end state cannot be derived by searching harder inside classical GR. Whatever replaces the singularity must arrive as an import from a frame whose closure differs, and it must compose over the settled GR and quantum-mechanical molecules at the second-order contact.
>
> The framework returns **a shape, not a name**. Fig 9 :32: *"you get a kind of thing, never the answer itself."* So I am not claiming that the answer is a fuzzball, a Planck star, a regular black hole, a remnant or a bounce. Each of those is a binding from the physics frame onto a structure, with a provenance debt. Until a ground settles one, they carry no evidentiary weight here.
>
