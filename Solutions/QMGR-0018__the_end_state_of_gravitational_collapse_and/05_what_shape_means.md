## 1. A shape is a constraint, not a filler

The figures README states the limit: the framework returns *"what a missing thing would have to look like (a shape, not a name)."* Fig 9 :32: *"you get a kind of thing, never the answer itself."* Fig 13: *"It can say the answer is out of reach with the library as it stands... It cannot say which primitive is missing, because naming it would mean already holding it."*

From *The Library of Babel*: *"you can cut your search short if the set of books returned is small and descriptive enough that it provides a general shape of the answer that satisfies your question."*

So a **shape** is the narrowed form of an answer you can state before you can name it. It constrains; it does not fill. For this question:

- shape: *a finite end state that composes over settled GR and QM, removes the divergent residual at finite cost, and sits at the second-order contact where the two regimes compare*;
- name: *fuzzball*, *Planck star*, *regular black hole*, *remnant*, *bounce*.

The first is figure-governed. The second is a binding from the physics frame, provenanced as such.

## 2. How a shape is obtained

- *Library of Babel*: use the vocabulary you already have that is *closest* to the thing, look for recurring new terms that overlap or correlate with it, recurse. *"With each new term gained, you are able to narrow the space by definition and deepen your vocabulary."*
- Fig 6 :30: *"The description travels. Fails by being sharpened into terms only we can read. Cast it in effect terms."* Fig 9 :30: *"your words are borrowed too, so describe the effect rather than the cause"*.
- *Decoding Tacit Knowledge*: *"When contact isn't possible, the best you can send is a plain description of what you'd expect to see, because anyone can check that against the world."* For collapse: describe what a finite end state would predict (a bounded curvature invariant, a specific ringdown signature, an evaporation endpoint), not which model produces it.
- *Library of Babel* on stopping: the answer is reached when *"the finish line will also be the starting place"*, and the paradox is that by then you have *"composed the answer through the process of locating it."*

## 3. What licenses a name

Only two things, both outside the shape itself:

1. **The ground settles it.** Formula step 5: *"the ground settles it. Verified only by what pays."* The README: the framework *"stops at the limit of what the available instruments can distinguish, and records the lineage so the stop is honest."*
2. **A provenanced import from a frame whose closure differs** (Fig 6 :23, Fig 8 :50 to :52), with a source gate and a shadow test.

*They're Scanning Books* sharpens this: *"The real import is not a new term in the existing space. It is a new dimension of the space."* And: *"A minimum viable set of six terms is a human's careful guess at reality's compression axes; another sensorium's minimum set need not be those six."* Also: *"Declaring the primitives does not make them neutral. It makes the choice visible."*

The six are a visible guess, not a discovery of nature's own list. Naming one as *the* end state of collapse turns a visible choice into an invisible one. This is why the figure returns a shape when the replacement atom is not held: the name would be the searcher's own contribution, and must be recorded as such.

## 4. Why naming a source from a reading is the forbidden error

*The Hard Problem is About Source* gives the mechanism. Metamerism: an instrument reads green and *"cannot comment on the source"*; one green light, or yellow plus blue, or RGB, or CMYK, or a scheme not yet discovered. The reading does not determine the source. *"If several paths create the same behavior, then behavior is a projection with lossy information"*. And: *"An instrument that can't distinguish these cases demonstrates its own inadequacy"*. It was the spectrometer, not an argument, that resolved the case. The essay: *"The answer is unreached until a better instrument exists."*

Applied here: a bounded ringdown spectrum or a horizon-scale image is a reading. Fuzzball, Planck star, regular black hole and bounce are several possible sources that could produce the same exterior reading. Naming one from the reading is the metamerism error. What is owed is a source-discriminating instrument, not a name.

*How Intelligence Actually Works* gives the stopping rule: *"indiscernibility is not identity"*. Two candidate end states that no current instrument distinguishes are not thereby the same end state; the lineage must be kept because the indistinguishability is a fact about the instruments, not about the world.

*The Recursive Self-Improvement Trap*: *"the output is only as good as the validator. The agent, or the tool, or the frame, or the theory, cannot validate itself."*

## 5. Q: what licenses a particular replacement? ANSWERED, no further question needed

**Nothing in the framework licenses a particular replacement name.** The framework returns a shape. A particular replacement is licensed only by the ground settling a minted term, or by a provenanced import with a shadow test. Until one of those, the name is the searcher's own contribution and must be recorded as such. That is a `gap` verdict, delivered plainly: *"Unreachable is a verdict here, not a silence"* (Fig 13 :134).

### 5b. Correction to my own §3 (kept visible)

I initially wrote the "shape, not a name" rule as if it forbade naming any term in a slot. That overstates it. The README's phrase is *"what a **missing** thing would have to look like (a shape, not a name)."* The subject is a primitive not held in Γ. My §3 conclusion stands; only its reason is now stated precisely. Both readings are recorded so neither is quietly lost.

## 6. Two consequences for outreach

**Prestige is not action.** From *The Recursive Self-Improvement Trap*: *"Prestige can fund an action. It can give an action a budget to be tried. But it cannot settle an action. Only the ground can settle it."* A campaign that gathers famous names to agree that a particular replacement is correct is the named collapse, not corroboration. The legitimate ask is the opposite: give the shape claim a budget to be tried, and let the ground, an instrument or an observation, settle it.

**My own audit is a simulation, not a ground reading.** Same essay: *"LLMs can quickly simulate an approximate answer/output with the formula found in the architecture post."* And: *"the truly testable output would need to be derived from the real architecture or working the formula yourself."* From *Arrival of the Fittest: The Tether*: such outputs *"could never be verified if the solutions presented were not contrived to fit the formula"* and *"outputs generated must be seen as simulacra"*. So `01` is a simulated derivation, checked for internal consistency against the figures. It is **not** a test of the claim and must be labelled as such. It cannot settle anything it reports on.
