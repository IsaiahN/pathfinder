## 0. The residual under audit, stated in effect terms

The question is: what is the end state of gravitational collapse, and what replaces the singularity?

In effect terms, not theory terms: a mass larger than the neutron-star support limit collapses. Classical general relativity predicts that once a trapped surface forms, the inward runs of null geodesics end after a finite extension. The exterior settles to a Kerr geometry that is well checked against observation (ringdown, shadows), but the interior prediction terminates in a region where curvature invariants diverge and the equations no longer return a state. Call that terminus **the singularity term**.

The residual R is the gap between what the classical prediction returns at the terminal slot and what a ground could resolve. It is not a small error to be tuned away: it *persists and does not trend down* under any refinement of the classical equations (Fig 5 :59, formula step 2). The question "what replaces the singularity" asks for the term that closes this residual.

Two things must be separated before any figure line is chosen:

- **The singularity as an object in the world** (a point of infinite density) is a *term held by a frame*, not a reading of the ground. Fig 2 :29: *"An anchor is whatever settles the question and does not change its answer because of what any frame thinks."* The singularity has never been observed; it is hidden behind a horizon and, under cosmic censorship, is not in the causal past of any outside observer. It is not an anchor.
- **The divergence is a reading that fails to resolve.** Something is returning (geodesic incompleteness, unbounded curvature), so this is not the case where nothing has ever registered. Fig 6 :40: *"What is unavailable is a sensor for something that has never registered anywhere, at any resolution."* does not apply to the divergence itself. It may apply to the microphysical end state, which has not registered at any resolution. Keeping those two apart decides which instrument question is owed.

**What an answer would look like, bound as an ordinary term (formula "Direction").** Before the run: an answer has the shape of a term that (a) composes over the settled first-order GR and QM molecules, (b) removes the divergent residual at a finite description cost, (c) is settled by a ground, and (d) is not a seventh primitive of the six-term substrate. The sought-for name (fuzzball, Planck star, regular black hole, remnant, bounce) is held as a *preference*, not a term, and therefore accrues no residual. Fig 5 :62: a frame holds the sought shape *"as a PREFERENCE rather than as a term."* Binding the shape as an ordinary term is what makes it refusable.

## 1. Domain census, before choosing any figure line

### 1.1 R by arity, symmetry and scale

- **Arity:** one live slot, the terminal state of the collapsing object. Fig 1 :19: *"The residual is indexed by object slot, not aggregated."* Fig 1 :13: *"a global R near zero with one live slot residual is a legal state"*. The rest of the collapse (exterior geometry, ringdown) is already settled and contributes near-zero residual. Aggregating the whole lifecycle would hide the one live slot.
- **Symmetry:** the classical terminus is scale-free (no preferred length, self-similar approach). The replacement must introduce a comparison at a scale where two regimes meet. That is a ⋛ threshold relation, not a symmetric join: the Operators table defines ⋛ as *"a threshold relation between two quantities"*, and Fig 12 :89 tests it by *"is it about which is larger, rather than both being present?"*
- **Scale:** the classical core is first order (`state - structure - state`, one crossing). The replacement sits at the second-order union of settled GR and QM molecules (Fig 12 :104). First order and second order must not be averaged into one verdict.

### 1.2 One line per domain: actors, currency, anchor

| domain | actors | currency | anchor | question type |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| D1 classical GR frame | metric, connection, trapped surface, horizon, singularity theorems | curvature predictions, geodesic completeness | exterior observation (ringdown, shadow); the interior terminus itself is not observable | partition boundary: what the classical closure can compose |
| D2 quantum-gravity frame | wavefunction, Planck-scale degrees of freedom, discrete spectra | physical predictions at the contact | experiment, currently returning nothing at the relevant scale | union compound: collapse endpoint plus microphysics (Fig 9 :55, *"a case in two conditions is not a harder case of either"*) |
| D3 verification / outreach frame | expert readers, gravitational-wave and horizon-scale instruments | agreement and residual | the ground, never agreement | contact question; in this simulated run all three domains draw on one evidence pool, so independence is near zero |

No nameable anchor means it is not a domain yet. D1 has an anchor (exterior observation). D2 has an anchor in principle (an instrument that could register Planck-scale structure) but nothing currently returns a reading at that scale, so its verdict must be `candidate`. D3 has the ground as anchor, but the simulated second seat shares the primary's pool, so its agreement is one witness, not two (Fig 2 :40).

**Partition or union.** The three domains form a **union with a dependency order**, not a partition. The overlap (collapse endpoint plus microphysics) is a compound with its own loop and its own ground. Fig 9 :54: *"when the split lands on a union rather than a partition, unbundle before you search again."*

### 1.3 Order by dependency (Fig 3)

Fig 3 orders links by dependency, not importance: perception, vocabulary, objective, planning, learn and carry. Applied here:

1. **Perception** works: the divergence is already returning a reading.
2. **Vocabulary** works: horizon, geodesic incompleteness, curvature invariant are held terms.
3. **The objective link is broken.** Classical GR cannot say, in its own terms, what would count as progress at the terminal slot. The singularity theorems guarantee incompleteness but *"the proof does not say what type of singularity occurs"* (world source: Penrose-Hawking singularity theorems, Wikipedia).
4. **Planning and carrying** therefore receive no usable input.

Fig 3 :23: *"A step whose input never arrives cannot be diagnosed, only its predecessor can."* Fig 3 :27: *"A link with no instrument is not a link that works. It is a link nobody has looked at."* So the first move is not to search harder for a classical end state. It is to refuse the singularity term and supply the missing objective from a frame whose closure differs. Refuse before mint (formula step 2). Describe the residual before choosing a frame (Fig 9 :27).

Strain test applied during the census: the classical singularity theorems (D1) and the quantum-gravity models (D2) appear to conflict over whether a singularity forms. They do not, once re-censed as different domains at different scales. The theorems govern the classical closure; the models govern a different closure with an added atom. This is the README's re-census rule, not a contradiction.

## 3. Findings F1 to F8, with verbatim quotes and refuters

### F1 - The singularity is a term to refuse, not an object to explain (decisive, framework-internal)

Fig 5 :69 amendment adds a fifth gap kind: *"settling is necessary for a term to be composed over, and it is not sufficient."* The relevant bin is REFUTED: formula step 2, *"a term held is false; what is owed is a refusal, not an explanation."* The singularity term persists and does not trend down (Fig 5 :59): no classical calculation makes it go away.

The bargain still applies. Fig 5 :43: *"A term that explains everything by saying nothing fails this, and so does a term that explains one occasion perfectly."* A term called "singularity" that simply labels the divergence explains nothing; it has zero support as a composable part.

**Refuter:** show that the singularity term has positive resolved mass at the terminal slot (an observation or a regularised classical solution that returns a finite state), so the residual trends down and REFUTED is the wrong bin. If that fires, F1 falls.

### F2 - Classical GR's closure cannot compose the replacement (triangulated)

Fig 6 :23: *"No frame escapes its own closure. The bound moves only on contact with a frame whose closure differs."* Fig 6 :14 names the one operator: *"the only operator that moves the wall"* (import). Fig 13 :115: *"The set is closed. The arrangements are not. That gap is why import exists."* Fig 8 :32: *"Closure is idempotent. Composing inside a closed set never leaves it, so no search, however long, adds an atom."*

So a search inside classical GR for the end state cannot produce the replacement atom. This is why the question has stayed open: the wall is definitional, not a matter of effort. Fig 8 :54: *"underivable is not unknowable"*, because the union is searchable from contact (Fig 8 :45: *"Two frames together reach what neither reaches alone."*).

**Refuter:** exhibit a derivation inside classical GR's closure that returns a finite terminal state without adding an atom. If that fires, import is not owed and F2 falls.

### F3 - The framework returns a shape, not a name; the identity of the replacement is a `gap` (decisive, honest)

Fig 9 :32: *"you get a kind of thing, never the answer itself"*. Fig 13 :133: *"what it returns is a shape rather than a name"*. Fig 13 :135: *"It cannot say which primitive is missing, because naming it would mean already holding it."* Fig 13 :134: *"Unreachable is a verdict here, not a silence"*.

Therefore: the figures govern the **shape** of the replacement (what it must do to close the residual), and are **silent by design** on its name. Fuzzball, Planck star, regular black hole, remnant and bounce are candidate bindings from the physics frame, not figure derivations. Each carries a provenance debt under Fig 8 :51: *"Convergent derivation and adopted import look identical in the contents. Only the record of where each came from separates them"*.

This is a `gap` verdict, not a failure to search. The framework cannot name a primitive it does not hold. It can say the answer is out of reach with the library as it stands (Fig 13 :134) and characterise what is missing in effect terms (Fig 6 :30: *"Cast it in effect terms."*).

**Refuter:** show that the figures' six-term set, with no import, uniquely selects one replacement. Since the README states the framework returns a shape and selects no term, this would require a re-census that changes the six-term rule. If it fires, F3 falls.

### F4 - The search is being run below a broken objective link (framework-internal)

Fig 3 :23: *"A step whose input never arrives cannot be diagnosed, only its predecessor can."* Fig 3 :27: *"A link with no instrument is not a link that works. It is a link nobody has looked at."* The objective link (link 3) has no instrument: classical GR cannot say what would count as progress at the terminal slot. Effort aimed at planning (link 4) or numerical refinement lands on a step that cannot use it (Fig 3 :19: *"Optimising a link that already works cannot rescue one that does not."*).

**Refuter:** identify an instrument already returning a reading about the end state, so link 3 has a metric and the chain is intact below it. Ringdown measurements of the exterior do not qualify; they do not reach the terminal slot. If such an instrument exists, F4 falls.

### F5 - Depth and genuine absence are different facts, and inward and outward are two tracks

Fig 6 :27: *"Which is why some unreachability is depth and some is genuine absence, and from inside the frame the two look the same."* Fig 9 :44: *"Only a sealed room can be searched to the end, and no frame is a sealed room. So searching and finding nothing proves nothing."*

The two instruments are opposed and must not be read against each other (Fig 6 :31: *"Neither answer tells you much about the other. Run them as two tracks, and never read one against the other."*):

- **Inward:** sharpen the instrument already returning something. Gravitational-wave ringdown, black-hole shadows and numerical relativity are already returning readings that fail to resolve the interior. Inward work extends those.
- **Outward:** characterise the residual in effect terms and look for a frame whose closure predicts its shape (quantum-gravity models). This imports an atom and is debited against that frame's independence.

Importing against a budget limit adds an atom that was not needed (Fig 6 :51 to :52), so the inward track must be run first and honestly before an import is bought.

**Refuter:** show that nothing at any resolution is returning a reading about the terminal state, in which case there is no proto-instrument to extend and the inward track is closed (Fig 6 :34 to :35). If that fires, the inward track is removed from the plan and only outward remains.

### F6 - The ground settles it, not agreement (triangulated across Fig 2, Fig 9, Fig 10)

Fig 2 :29: *"An anchor is whatever settles the question and does not change its answer because of what any frame thinks."* Fig 2 :35: *"Alignment is triangulation, not negotiation. A mirror cannot correct you."* Fig 9 :60: *"Use filters for the budget and witnesses for the verdict. Never let a filter hand you a verdict."* Fig 10 :52: *"Bring a characterised residual, never the source."* Fig 10 :42: *"A verifier that can reconstruct the claim cannot verify it."*

Agreement among string-theory, loop-quantum-gravity and regular-black-hole communities is not corroboration of any one shape; they are different closures, so their agreement on "no singularity" is convergent derivation at best and a shared evidence pool at worst. The verdict must come from a ground, not a vote.

**Refuter:** exhibit a ground reading that distinguishes the candidate end states (a signature in ringdown, an evaporation endpoint, a horizon-scale structure measurement). If that fires, the verdict moves from `candidate` toward `grounded`.

### F7 - The candidate end states are structures over the same atoms, not new primitives (supporting, `specified`)

Fig 12 :121: *"cycle. The output re-enters as input. The sign of the loop decides whether it closes, and a chain cannot close by construction."* A bounce or a black-hole-to-white-hole transition is a cycle at a level; a remnant is a product that survives its use (Fig 12 :119, catalyst: *"A part that survives its own use."*); the family of regular black holes shares a functional group (Fig 12 :123: *"functional group. A substructure whose presence fixes the outcome."*).

Fig 12 :104: *"A settled molecule becomes an atom for whatever composes over it. So irreducible is a fact about a level and never about the world"*. GR and QM are each settled against the ground, so each can serve as an atom at the next level. Their comparison at the contact is second order and needs no seventh primitive. See `06_isomers_and_order.md` for the full derivation and its falsifier.

**Refuter:** show that a candidate end state requires an atom outside the six-term set that is not an import, or that a settled theory cannot serve as an atom. If either fires, F7 falls.

### F8 - Cost discipline: no free name, no free import

Fig 5 :43 rejects a term that explains everything by saying nothing. Fig 6 :24: *"Import from a live peer is debited against that frame's independence. A wrong atom costs more than starting from primitives."* Fig 9 :60 forbids letting a filter hand a verdict. Each candidate replacement therefore pays twice: once in description cost (does it actually reduce the divergent residual?) and once in action cost (what instrument would settle it?). These two currencies do not add (Fig 12 :126: *"Bits price the description. Actions price finding out whether it holds. They are in different units"*).

**Refuter:** exhibit a candidate that removes the residual at lower description cost than the confusion it removes and is settled by an existing instrument at negligible action cost. If that fires, it is the first grounded replacement and F8 is confirmed, not broken.

## 4. Pre-registered refuter (written before the conclusion)

A falsifier written before the run is a design instrument. The shape claim of this solution is refuted if any of the following holds:

1. A classical derivation, with no added atom, returns a finite terminal state whose residual trends down (breaks F1 and F2).
2. A ground reading at the contact distinguishes the candidate end states and shows that a structure must sit between the two first-order molecules (breaks the shape, not just a name).
3. An instrument already returning a reading about the terminal slot is identified, so the objective link of Fig 3 is intact (breaks F4).
4. A figure re-census shows the six-term set uniquely selects one replacement name (breaks F3).
5. A settled first-order molecule cannot serve as an atom for the second-order comparison (breaks F7).

If any fires, the shape claim is withdrawn or rewritten. Until one is run, no part of this may be labelled `grounded`.

## 5. Recommendation

1. Refuse the singularity term before minting a replacement (F1, formula step 2). The order matters: the figures refuse before they compose.
2. Run the inward track (ringdown, shadows, numerical relativity) before buying an import (F5). Keep the two tracks separate.
3. Describe the residual in effect terms first (F3, Fig 9 :27). Do not pick fuzzball, Planck star or remnant and then map the residual onto it; that is echo without shadow.

5. Lead the outreach with the shape claim and the pre-registered refuter (Fig 10 :52). Fig 4 :25: *"A recording carried upward looks like knowledge and is a description of one occasion."* Send the generator, not the conclusion.
