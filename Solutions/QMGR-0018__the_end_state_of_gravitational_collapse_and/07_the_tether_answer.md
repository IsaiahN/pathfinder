# The Tether's answer on the end state of collapse

**Mode: `specified`, not `grounded`.** This is a shape and a route, not a result. Simulated derivation checked against the figures (`05_what_shape_means.md` §6), not a ground reading.

## 1. The residual it answers

A mass above the neutron-star support limit collapses. Classical general relativity returns a terminal state in which curvature diverges and geodesics end after a finite extension. The exterior is settled against observation; the interior terminus is not. The residual is live in one slot and persists under every classical refinement (Fig 1 :19: *"The residual is indexed by object slot, not aggregated."*). The question "what replaces the singularity" asks for the term that closes it.

The problem is a **union compound**, not one hard problem (Fig 9 :55: *"a bundled problem is usually a union of overlapping conditions, not a list of separate ones"*). Regularity, unitarity and causal extension are three conditions with three grounds. Keep the verdicts separate.

## 2. The shape

1. **The singularity is a term to refuse, not a thing to compose over.** Fig 5 step REFUTED: a term held is false and what is owed is a refusal. Fig 5 :43: *"A term that explains everything by saying nothing fails this, and so does a term that explains one occasion perfectly."* The divergence stays as a live residual; the name "singularity" does not become a part.
2. **The replacement cannot be derived by searching inside classical GR.** Fig 6 :23: *"No frame escapes its own closure. The bound moves only on contact with a frame whose closure differs."* Fig 8 :32: *"Closure is idempotent. Composing inside a closed set never leaves it, so no search, however long, adds an atom."* The missing atom must be imported from a frame whose closure differs, with a source gate and a shadow test.
3. **The search is currently blocked at the objective link.** Fig 3 :23: *"A step whose input never arrives cannot be diagnosed, only its predecessor can."* Classical GR cannot say, in its own terms, what would count as progress at the terminal slot. Work below that break is a reading of nothing (Fig 3 :27: *"A link with no instrument is not a link that works. It is a link nobody has looked at."*).
4. **The replacement sits at second order.** Both GR and QM are settled first-order molecules; each becomes an atom for the next level (Fig 12 :104: *"A settled molecule becomes an atom for whatever composes over it. So irreducible is a fact about a level and never about the world"*). The comparison at the contact is a ⋛ threshold relation, not a unified field.
5. **The framework returns a shape, not a name.** Fig 9 :32: *"you get a kind of thing, never the answer itself."* Fig 13 :134: *"Unreachable is a verdict here, not a silence"*. Fig 13 :135: *"It cannot say which primitive is missing, because naming it would mean already holding it."* Fuzzball, Planck star, regular black hole, remnant and bounce are bindings from the physics frame, each with a provenance debt. The identity of the replacement is a `gap` by design.
6. **Inward and outward are two tracks and must not be read against each other.** Fig 6 :31: *"Neither answer tells you much about the other. Run them as two tracks, and never read one against the other."* Inward extends the instruments already returning readings (ringdown, shadows, numerical relativity). Outward imports a microphysical atom from a differing closure.

## 3. What it explicitly does not say

- Not a theory of everything. No final room. The terminal slot stays a compound with its own loop.
- Not a name for the replacement. It does not say the answer is a fuzzball, a Planck star, a regular black hole, a remnant, a bounce or a white hole.
- Not that the singularity is unreal. The divergence is a live residual; only the term held by the classical frame is refused.
- Not that GR or QM is wrong. Both are settled in their own domains and serve as atoms at the next level.
- Not a derivation of one theory from the other, and no new primitive.
- Not `grounded`. No measurement at the contact settles `left(R,φ)`.

## 4. What would settle it - the ground

A source-discriminating instrument, not a further argument. From *The Hard Problem is About Source*: a colour instrument *"cannot comment on the source"*; the spectrometer resolved that case. Here:

- **Regularity test:** does a curvature invariant stay bounded at the terminal slot, or diverge? A bounded invariant would close the divergence residual.
- **Information endpoint test:** does evaporation end in a pure state, a remnant, or a mixed state? This separates the unitarity condition from the regularity condition.
- **Causal test:** does the infalling world line extend beyond the classical boundary, or terminate? This addresses the causal condition.
- **Threshold test:** does the ⋛ comparison between the settled GR and QM molecules return a finite scale at the contact, or demonstrably not?
- **Standing falsifiers:** a classical derivation with no added atom returning a finite terminal state; an instrument already returning a reading about the terminal slot; a figure re-census in which the six-term set uniquely selects one name. All are recorded before the run in `01_figure_grounded_audit.md` §4.

## 5. The honest stop

**Reached for the shape, unreached for the name.** The six-term set is *"a human's careful guess at reality's compression axes"* (*They're Scanning Books*); declaring it makes the choice visible, not neutral. Another sensorium's minimum set need not be these six. The framework stops at the limit of what the available instruments distinguish and records the lineage so the stop is checkable. *"Indiscernibility is not identity"* (*How Intelligence Actually Works*): two candidates no instrument currently separates are not thereby the same.
