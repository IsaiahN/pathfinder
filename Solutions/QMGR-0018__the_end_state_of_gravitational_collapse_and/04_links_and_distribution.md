## What these sources do and do not settle

1. **The field agrees on the residual and disagrees on the replacement.** The gravitational collapse and singularity pages both state that the classical prediction ends in a singularity and that the nature of the end state is controversial, with competing theories. That is exactly what Fig 13 :115 predicts when a set is closed and the arrangements are not: many candidate arrangements, no new member. It corroborates the shape claim and does not select a name.
2. **No candidate is grounded.** Fuzzball: no direct experimental evidence. Planck star: confirmation would require emissions from rebounding black holes. Nonsingular models: no stable model established. All three are `candidate` bindings with a provenance debt under Fig 8 :51.
3. **The instrument question is live, not empty.** Ringdown and horizon-regularity papers in the arXiv gr-qc listing are already returning readings that fail to resolve the interior. That keeps the inward track of Fig 6 open. It does not yet settle the terminal slot.
4. **A shared evidence pool warning.** Wikipedia pages on related topics cross-link each other, so their agreement is one pool talking (Fig 2 :40). They are background, not independent witnesses. The physics claim is decided by the figures in this package, with world sources as secondary corroboration only.

## Distribution: offer, do not push

- **Fig 4 :22:** *"Only methods cross up. - **Fig 2 :42:** *"Buy independence in proportion to the cost of being wrong."* A wrong end-state claim is costly, so independent checkers are worth seeking through public, opt-in venues in `03_public_contact_points.csv`.

**Recommended order in a reply:** (1) the residual and the shape claim; (2) the pre-registered refuter and the question "what would you count as a failure?"; (3) record their verdict; (4) only then the three framework links, with the note that the figures, not the summary, are the authority.

## The two-stage offer (and the optional third stage)

- **Stage 1 before stage 2** is Fig 10 :52, *"Bring a characterised residual, never the source"*, and Fig 2 :45, *"Frames range against the ground. They do not negotiate with it."* The reader checks the output without holding the framework's interior.
- **Stage 2 is opt-in by construction.** Once a reader has absorbed the theory, their agreement stops being evidence. The stage-1 verdict must be recorded before any theory is shown, and later agreement is never pooled as corroboration (Fig 2 :40).
- **Stage 3 sends a method, which is permitted.** Fig 4 :22, *"Only methods cross up. Only head starts come down."* The formula is a generator; giving it away does not give away an answer.
- **Stage 3 still needs a ground per problem.** A user must write down what would refute their result before running it and label the mode of what they get.

**Hard precondition:** stages 1 to 3 only work if the stage-1 output survives. As drafted, the output is a shape claim with four refuters, not a named end state. It is checkable without the framework. If a reader refutes it, record the refutation in `11_audit.md` with provenance and re-evaluate it as if it came from any other source.
