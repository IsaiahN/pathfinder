## What is deliberately not here

- No personal emails of named individuals.
- No ranking of people by how likely they are to believe the claim.
- No bulk-send plan.

Three reasons, in the figures' own words:

1. **A room is not an anchor.** Fig 2 :35, *"Alignment is triangulation, not negotiation. A mirror cannot correct you."* A list selected for agreement is a mirror with a mailing list.
2. **Correlation disqualifies a verdict.** Fig 9 :60, *"Use filters for the budget and witnesses for the verdict. Never let a filter hand you a verdict."* Receptiveness is a contact-cost filter, never an evidence column.
3. **Asking for trust before a check spends independence.** Fig 10 :42, *"A verifier that can reconstruct the claim cannot verify it."* The first contact carries the residual and its refuter, not the architecture.

Legally: scraping personal emails for unsolicited bulk outreach breaches GDPR and CAN-SPAM style rules in many jurisdictions. That is a practical failure on top of the structural one.
