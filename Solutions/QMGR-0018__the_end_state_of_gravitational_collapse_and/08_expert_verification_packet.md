# Expert verification packet: the Tether on collapse end states

**What this is:** a claim in a form a gravitational-collapse researcher can check. Mode, derivation and refuters are stated before the conclusion, so a reader knows what to look for and what would settle it.

**Mode: `specified`, not `grounded`.** No measurement at the contact is claimed. This is a shape and a route, not a result. It is a simulated derivation checked for internal consistency against the figures, not a ground reading.

## 1. The claim, in one paragraph

The singularity is a term held by classical general relativity that owes a refusal, not an explanation. The divergence it names is a live residual in one slot and persists under classical refinement. No search inside classical GR's closure can produce the replacement, because closure is idempotent; the missing atom must be imported from a frame whose closure differs. Whatever replaces the singularity sits at second order, over the settled GR and quantum-mechanical molecules, at the threshold where the two regimes compare. The framework returns the shape of that replacement and cannot name it; fuzzball, Planck star, regular black hole, remnant and bounce are bindings from the physics frame with provenance debts. Inward instrument work and outward import are two tracks and are never read against each other.

## 2. Three checkable sub-claims

### C1 - framework-internal; checkable by reading Figures 5, 6 and 8
- **Statement:** a persistent residual on one slot routes to REFUTED, and importing is the only operator that moves the closure wall.
- **Source, verbatim:** Fig 5 :59: *"a term whose residual PERSISTS AND DOES NOT TREND DOWN is the one to point the refusal at"*. Fig 6 :23: *"No frame escapes its own closure. The bound moves only on contact with a frame whose closure differs."* Fig 8 :32: *"Closure is idempotent. Composing inside a closed set never leaves it, so no search, however long, adds an atom."*
- **What to check:** is the divergence at the classical terminus a residual that persists and does not trend down under classical refinement? If it trends down, the REFUTED bin is wrong.
- **Refuter:** a classical derivation, with no added atom, that returns a finite terminal state. If it fires, the claim falls.

### C2 - physics-facing; checkable against collapse and strong-gravity observations
- **Statement:** ringdown, shadow and numerical-relativity work are instruments already returning readings that fail to resolve the interior, so the inward track is open (Fig 6 :29: *"inward: is our representation adequate to hold it? The instrument extends access. Fails by refining forever on an axis that cannot carry it. Sharpen in our own terms."*)
- **What to check:** is any such reading actually bearing on the terminal slot rather than only on the exterior geometry? Does any currently resolve regularity, information loss and causal extension at once?
- **Refuter:** an instrument already returning a reading about the terminal slot, so the objective link of Fig 3 is intact; or evidence that nothing at any resolution has ever registered about the interior, which would close the inward track. Both change the plan, and the second is reported as a different fact from underivability.

### C3 - framework-internal plus structural; checkable by reading Figures 9, 12 and 13
- **Statement:** the replacement is second order over settled molecules, the candidate list is a union of overlapping conditions, and the framework returns a shape and not a name.
- **Source, verbatim:** Fig 12 :104: *"A settled molecule becomes an atom for whatever composes over it. So irreducible is a fact about a level and never about the world"*. Fig 9 :32: *"you get a kind of thing, never the answer itself"*. Fig 13 :135: *"It cannot say which primitive is missing, because naming it would mean already holding it."*
- **What to check:** is the second-order comparison legal, given that the Operators table defines ⋛ as *"a threshold relation between two quantities"* and the operands here are settled molecules now serving as atoms? Are the three conditions (regularity, unitarity, causal extension) genuinely overlapping rather than independent?
- **Refuter:** show that a settled molecule cannot serve as an atom, or that ⋛'s operands must be bare quantities at every level, or that the three conditions reduce to one instrument reading. The operand type is the one strain still open; it is named, not hidden.

## 3. What is explicitly not claimed

- Not a theory of everything; no final room. The terminal slot stays a compound with its own loop.
- Not a name for the replacement. No commitment to fuzzball, Planck star, regular black hole, remnant, bounce or white hole.
- Not that the singularity is unreal. The divergence is a live residual; only the classical term composed over it is refused.
- Not that GR or QM is wrong in its own domain, and no new primitive is asserted.
- Not `grounded`. Nothing here is asserted as settled by experiment.
- Not a claim that agreement among model communities corroborates the shape. Fig 2 :40: *"agreement about the world is worthless when the agreeing frames share an evidence pool"*.
