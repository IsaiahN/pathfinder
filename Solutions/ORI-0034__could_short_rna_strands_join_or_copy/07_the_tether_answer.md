# The Tether's answer: could short RNA strands join or copy one another often enough to support evolution?

**Mode: `general` on the shape, `specified` on the derived constraints, `gap` on the literal frequency. Not `grounded`.** This is a synthesized shape and a route, not a result. Simulated derivation checked for internal consistency against the figures (`05_what_shape_means.md` §8), not a ground reading.

## 1. The residual it answers

The question unbundles into three overlapping conditions (Fig 9 :54: "And when the split lands on a union rather than a partition, unbundle before you search again."): strands joining, strands copying, and both happening at a rate where heritable variation persists and selection has purchase. "Often enough" is the intersection, not a separate fourth item. Answering with a single ligation rate answers one condition and leaves the compound standing.

## 2. The shape, one loop per domain, verdicts kept separate

**D1, chemical joining. `specified`, triangulated.** Fig 12 :48: "A molecule is a bonded arrangement. A chain is the degenerate case: one bond type, no branching." Joining produces a chain. That is a legal composition and has instruments that measure it. It does not by itself close a replication loop, and more joining events do not add a new monomer kind: Fig 8 :32: "Closure is idempotent. Composing inside a closed set never leaves it, so no search, however long, adds an atom. This one is definitional: it is what closure means." Feedstock and mineral or activation chemistry are imports with a source gate, not more of the same search.

**D2, copying. `specified`, triangulated after re-census.** Fig 12 :121: "cycle. The output re-enters as input. The sign of the loop decides whether it closes, and a chain cannot close by construction." The single strand is the chain; the replication loop is a two-strand cycle closed by complementary base pairing, a second bond type. Fig 12 :119: "catalyst. A part that survives its own use." The evolving unit must be the regenerating part, not a strand consumed or locked in a duplex after one round. Re-census resolved the apparent contradiction between the chain line and the cycle line: they govern different quantities.

**D3, evolution. `general` shape, `specified` instance, triangulated.** Fig 7 :26: "progress per handoff: R = h² × S". Fig 7 :33: "→ variation inside, selection between". Fidelity and ground pressure are two positive factors; zero either and the product is zero. Fig 11 :36: "Where nothing maintains anything, intelligence is a label applied afterwards to a lineage that met its ground. Nothing was intelligent; selection ran. The word does no work there." Fig 11 :39: "So the answerable question is not what intelligence is, but what makes a system capable. And capability is a property of agent-and-habitat, never of the agent alone." So the capable subject is strand plus habitat, not strand alone. Fig 11 :40: "A system cannot be made more capable by improving the agent in isolation. An improvement that does not change contact changes nothing, however much it improves."

**D4, verification. `general`, not a witness.** Fig 2 :40: "agreement about the world is worthless when the agreeing frames share an evidence pool." In this simulated run the second seat shares the primary's evidence pool, so its agreement is internal consistency, not corroboration. The outside check goes first, through the two-stage offer.

## 3. What it explicitly does not say

- Not "yes, short RNAs did it." The figures do not name a historical polymer. Naming RNA is an import from the world literature, provenance-marked, with zero verdict weight until a source gate and shadow test are shown.
- Not "no, short RNAs cannot." Fig 9 :44: "Only a sealed room can be searched to the end, and no frame is a sealed room. So searching and finding nothing proves nothing." A failed lab demonstration is not a proof of impossibility.
- Not a number for "often enough." The error threshold (fidelity q over n reactions must beat advantage s; roughly 2 percent per position for a 50-nucleotide functional RNA) is a world result with its own provenance, not a figure line.
- Not that joining plus copying in a flask equals evolution. Selection needs differential survival and spatial localization, which are habitat conditions.
- Not a claim about which polymer came first, and not a claim about RNA before pre-RNA polymers. That is the source gap at question 38 of the same section, answered elsewhere.
- Not `grounded`. Nothing here was settled by a measurement.

## 4. What would settle it - the ground

Fig 2 :29: "An anchor is whatever settles the question and does not change its answer because of what any frame thinks." The anchor here is an experiment, not an argument. The instruments already returning something are:

1. **Fidelity measurement.** Does template-directed copying of arbitrary short sequences reach an error rate below the reciprocal of the functionally important bases? Szostak reports around 17 percent average, reducible toward 10 percent or about 5 percent on GC-rich templates; the functional threshold is around 2 percent. The gap is the difference, not the existence of a copying reaction.
2. **Cycle closure and strand separation.** Can the product become a template repeatedly, with strand separation under the same plausible conditions? The virtual circular genome model is a concrete proposal; it is untested.
3. **Selection purchase.** Does a variant with a copying advantage keep that advantage across serial transfers under plausible conditions? Spiegelman's Monster is an instance with a supplied replicase, not a prebiotic demonstration.
4. **Standing refuters from the pre-registered list:** a single one-bond-type chain copying itself with no complement; sustained evolution with the product R = h² × S equal to zero; sustained evolution with habitat contact held constant. Any one firing retracts the corresponding constraint.

## 5. The honest stop

**Unreached, not unreachable.** The figures give the shape any answer must have and stop where the instruments stop. The literal frequency is an honest `gap`: "Unreachable is a verdict, not a silence," and the verdict here is that the number is owed to the ground. The lineage of this stop is recorded in `01_figure_grounded_audit.md` §6 and `11_audit.md`. Indiscernibility is not identity: showing that a copied strand reads like its template does not show which route produced the lineage.

## 6. One-paragraph version for outreach

> The Tether does not answer yes or no to whether short RNAs could support evolution, because that is a measurement question and the figures are not a measurement. It says the question is a union of three conditions: joining, copying, and a rate with heredity and selection. Joining makes a chain, and a chain cannot close a replication cycle by itself, so copying needs two complementary strands and a second bond type, with the strand surviving its own use as a catalyst. "Often enough" is not one rate but two positive factors, fidelity and selection pressure, and zero either ends evolution. The capable subject is the strand plus its habitat, never the strand alone. The figures return that shape, state what would refute it, and stop before the number. The number is owed to an experiment, not to an argument.
