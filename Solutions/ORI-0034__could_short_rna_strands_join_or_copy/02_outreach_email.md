# Outreach draft: check one shape claim before you decide about the theory

**Use:** one to one, or as a cover note attached to a submission through the public venues in `03_public_contact_points.csv`. **Not** for bulk send.

**Hard precondition:** the shape claim and its refuter must travel together. If the refuter is stripped out, the two-stage offer collapses on contact and the recipient has joined the frame being checked.

---

**Subject:** Check one shape claim about early RNA replication, then decide if the theory is worth reading

> Hi [name],
>
> I have one specific output from a figure-grounded derivation I would like you to check against what you already know. I am not asking you to read the theory first, and I am not asking you to agree with anything. If the output does not survive, there is nothing else to look at.
>
> **The output (`general` shape, `specified` constraints, `gap` on the number):** the question "could short RNA strands join or copy one another often enough to support evolution?" is not one question. It is a union of three overlapping conditions: joining, copying, and a rate at which heritable variation persists and selection has purchase. A lever that clears one condition leaves the other two standing. The figures describe the shape any yes answer must have:
>
> 1. A single linear strand is a chain with one bond type. A chain cannot close a replication cycle by construction. The cycle closes only across two complementary strands using base pairing as a second bond type. The evolving unit must also survive its own use, so it is a catalyst, not a consumed reagent.
> 2. "Often enough" is not a single rate. It is the product of two positive factors: copying fidelity and the ground's pressure on the variants (selection). Zero either and there is no evolution, however fast the chemistry runs.
> 3. The capable thing is the strand plus its habitat, never the strand alone. Monomers, energy, cycling conditions and spatial localization are part of the answer, not background.
>
> **What I am not claiming:** that the figures say RNA was the first genetic polymer, that they name a specific replicator, or that they produce a number for the required fidelity. They return a shape, not a name and not a measurement. The literal frequency stays an honest `gap` until an instrument settles it. I am also not claiming that a pile of laboratory demonstrations already constitutes a sustained evolutionary system; that is the source gap the question is about.
>
> **What would refute the shape claim, written before this message:**
> 1. a single linear strand, with no complement and no second bond type, copying itself with its output re-entering as input;
> 2. sustained heritable evolution with one of the two factors demonstrably zero, meaning every copy random or no differential survival;
> 3. sustained evolution with contact with the habitat held strictly constant, the strand improved in isolation only.
>
> If any one holds, I retract the corresponding claim. A one-line "this fails because..." is more useful to me than agreement. If you would rather not engage, no reply is needed.
>
> **If it survives, and only then:** here are three public sources I actually opened. The figures, where cited, are the authority, not my summary of them.
> - The Origins of the RNA World, Robertson and Joyce, 2012: https://pmc.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/articles/PMC3331698
> - The eightfold path to non-enzymatic RNA replication, Szostak, 2012: https://link.springer.com/article/10.1186/1759-2208-3-2
> - The virtual circular genome model for primordial RNA replication, Zhou, Ding and Szostak, 2021: https://pmc.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/articles/PMC7749632
>
> **If you want to use the machinery:** the figures and formula are public. Run them on a problem you know well and can check against a ground. Write down what would refute your result before you run it, and state the mode of what you get. A reported failure is more useful to me than a reported success.
>
> Thanks,
> [name]

## Two-stage discipline

Stage 1: the recipient checks the shape claim and the three refuters. Their verdict is recorded **before** any framework material is shown. Stage 2, opt-in only: the three links. Stage 3, if asked: the formula as a method to reuse, with each user writing their own refuter and mode. A later agreement is never pooled as corroboration, because the same sources would be the shared evidence pool.
