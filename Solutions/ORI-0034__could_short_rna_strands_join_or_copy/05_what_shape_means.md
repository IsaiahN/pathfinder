## 1. A shape is a constraint, not a filler

The canonical usage rule says the framework returns "what a missing thing would have to look like (a shape, not a name)." Fig 9 :32: "you get a kind of thing, never the answer itself." *How Intelligence Actually Works* states the operational version: the framework "cuts down to the shape of a thing, problem or question based on the given information available at this point in time."

From *The Library of Babel*: "you can cut your search short if the set of books returned is small and descriptive enough that it provides a general shape of the answer that satisfies your question."

So a **shape** is the narrowed form of an answer you can state before you can measure it. It constrains; it does not fill. For ORI-0034, the shape is not "RNA did it" or "RNA did not do it." It is the set of constraints any yes-answer must satisfy: a two-strand replication cycle closed by a second bond type, a catalyst that survives its use, fidelity times selection, and a strand-plus-habitat subject. "RNA" itself is a candidate from the world literature, not a term the figures derive.

## 2. How the shape is obtained

- *The Library of Babel*: "your ignorance, of not knowing what you don't know, is the actual obstacle between you and the answers." The method is proximity: "With each new term gained, you are able to narrow the space by definition and deepen your vocabulary."
- Fig 9 asks for the residual in effect terms before choosing a frame, or any frame will seem to fit. Here the residual was described as three conditions (joining, copying, rate) before the RNA literature was brought in as corroboration.
- *The Library of Babel* also warns about exactly this question: "Perhaps your problem was a bundle or conflation of several problems masquerading as one?" That is F1 in `01_figure_grounded_audit.md`, from a source with low overlap with the figures.
- *Decoding Tacit Knowledge* was not opened this session, so no quote from it is used. The general rule it is known to state (describe what you would expect to see when contact is impossible) is already available from Fig 9 and the essays that were opened.

## 3. What licenses a name, and what this question may not name

Only two things license a name: the ground settling a minted term, or a provenanced import from a frame whose closure differs (Fig 6, Fig 8). *They're Scanning Books When They Should Be Studying Nature* sharpens the second: "The real import is not a new term in the existing space. It is a new dimension of the space."

Here the import is the RNA-world literature itself, with its authors, venues and dates, recorded in `10_source_ledger.csv`. It supplies the chemistry, the error threshold and the laboratory ground cases. The figures supply the shape. Keeping those two roles separate is the whole discipline.

The same essay adds: "A minimum viable set of six terms is a human's careful guess at reality's compression axes; another sensorium's minimum set need not be those six." The six-term substrate is a visible guess, not nature's own list. Naming RNA as *the* first polymer would turn a visible choice into an invisible one.

## 4. Why an instrument is owed, not another argument

*The Hard Problem is About Source* gives the mechanism with metamerism: an instrument reads green and "cannot comment on the source." Several sources can produce one reading. The essay says the color case "was proven wrong not by argument, but by the arrival of a better instrument."

For ORI-0034 the analog is direct: observing that strands join, or that a ribozyme copies a short template, does not identify the source process that sustained evolution. The reading underdetermines the history. What is owed is an instrument that discriminates sustained multi-generation copying fidelity and rate under plausible conditions, not another argument about whether the RNA world is plausible. Szostak's eight obstacles and the error threshold are the instruments already returning something; the honest report is the `gap` on the number.

*The Recursive Self-Improvement Trap*: "the output is only as good as the validator. The agent, or the tool, or the frame, or the theory, cannot validate itself." This file and its companions are such outputs. They do not validate themselves.

## 5. What the essays say about evolution specifically

*The Recursive Self-Improvement Trap*: "Natural selection is what the loop looks like when there is no one maintaining the ground. When there is no one in the Ground Maintainer seat, the environment is the only selector." This is the closest essay-level statement of Fig 11 :36, and it is why F5 refuses "strands alone" as the subject.

*How Intelligence Actually Works*: "indiscernibility is not identity." A copied strand that reads the same as its template does not thereby carry the same lineage; the route has to be recorded. That is why the source ledger and the retraction log are part of the answer, not documentation attached to it.

## 6. Correction to my own reading of "shape, not a name"

I initially read "shape, not a name" as forbidding the mention of RNA in the answer at all. That was too strong. The phrase governs a **missing** primitive or an unnamed slot, not a candid binding to a candidate already held in the world literature. For ORI-0034 the figures do not hold "RNA first" as a term, so the binding is an import with provenance, which is permitted but carries zero verdict weight until a source gate and a shadow test are shown.

What remains forbidden is asserting that RNA was necessarily the first replicating polymer, or that the figures derive a particular fidelity number. Both claims would turn a provenance-marked import into a ground verdict. Both are recorded as non-claims in `07_the_tether_answer.md` and `08_expert_verification_packet.md`.

## 7. Prestige is not action

*The Recursive Self-Improvement Trap*: "Prestige can fund an action. It can give an action a budget to be tried. But it cannot settle an action. Only the ground can settle it."

A list of famous names agreeing that the RNA world is plausible would be the named collapse. The legitimate ask is the opposite: give the shape claim a budget to be tried by someone who can refute it, and let an experiment settle it. This is why `03_public_contact_points.csv` contains institutions and opt-in venues, not people ranked by likely agreement.

## 8. My own audit is a simulation, not a ground reading

*The Recursive Self-Improvement Trap* again: "the truly testable output would need to be derived from the real architecture or working the formula yourself." *Arrival of the Fittest: The Tether Architecture* warns that outputs "could never be verified if the solutions presented were not contrived to fit the formula," and states plainly: "Because a metatheory, it cannot be validated, nor can it validate itself."

So `01_figure_grounded_audit.md`, `06_isomers_and_order.md`, `07_the_tether_answer.md`, `09_watson_answer.md` and `11_audit.md` are simulated derivations checked for internal consistency against the figures. They are NOT ground readings. They cannot settle whether short RNA strands could join or copy often enough to support evolution. Only an experiment can, and the honest stop is recorded where the figures run out.
