# Expert verification packet: the Tether on technology and memory

**What this is:** four domain outputs in a form a memory or technology-studies researcher can check.
Mode, derivation and refuters are stated before the conclusion, so a reader knows what to look for
and what would settle it.

**Mode: `general` on the union and contact claims, `specified` on the domain predictions. Not
`grounded`.** No measurement in this folder is claimed to settle anything.

## 1. The claim, in one paragraph

The question is four questions, not one: attention, memory, communication and the sense of what can
be known have different actors, currencies and anchors, and their verdicts must never be averaged.
A technology changes cognition by changing contact, not by upgrading the person. In attention, a
boundary diff is re-aimed and aggregation erases the live slot signal. In memory, the record grows
while the reach grows only if a re-applicable method crosses the membrane. In communication, the
channel decays while the ground does not, and the quantity is the round-trip gap. In epistemics, an
instrument moves the wall by import while search inside the same closure adds no atom. Each claim
has a refuter written in advance. Where no ground reading exists, the packet reports a gap.

## 2. Three checkable sub-claims

### C1  -  framework-internal; checkable by reading Figures 9, 3 and 11

- **Statement:** the bundle must be unbundled, and the relevant change is in contact.
- **Source, verbatim:** Fig 9 :54  -  *"And when the split lands on a union rather than a partition, unbundle before you search again."* Fig 11 :40  -  *"An improvement that does not change contact changes nothing, however much it improves."* Fig 3 :20  -  *"The chain is ordered by dependency, not by importance."*
- **Refuter:** one measure that moves all four domains in lockstep with low overlap; or a device that changes capability with every contact held fixed; or a demonstration that the knowable changes with perception and vocabulary held fixed.

### C2  -  memory-facing; checkable against the record versus reach distinction

- **Statement:** an external store expands the record, not necessarily the reachable set. A head start is a different bond from a replay, and the two behave differently.
- **Source, verbatim:** Fig 6 :42  -  *"What is recorded only grows. What is reachable is derived, and it can fall."* Fig 4 :22  -  *"Only methods cross up. Only head starts come down."* Fig 5 :72  -  *"a library that grows is not a reach that grows."* Fig 12 :117  -  *"isomer. Same atoms, different bond, different substance. The difference is invisible in the formula and decisive in the behaviour."*
- **What to check:** does the saved item generate a novel success, or only repeat an old one? Is the distinction between a re-applicable method and a recording observable in your material? Does the world literature actually show the claimed split, or only a fall in recall that could have four sources?
- **Refuter:** a stored recording that, merely replayed, yields a novel success it could not yield before; or two arrangements that behave identically under every available instrument, making the bond free.

### C3  -  communication and epistemics; checkable as a channel and a wall

- **Statement:** communication change is channel loss measured by a round trip, not agreement; an instrument moves the wall by import, and search inside a closure adds no atom.
- **Source, verbatim:** Fig 10 :24  -  *"the ground does not decay, the channel does"*. Fig 4 :30  -  *"So take the round trip and measure the gap. Without it you are navigating by dead reckoning: confident about a position nobody checked."* Fig 6 :25  -  *"An import adds no atom to the world."* Fig 8 :32  -  *"no search, however long, adds an atom"*. Fig 2 :40  -  *"agreement about the world is worthless when the agreeing frames share an evidence pool"*.
- **Refuter:** a measured zero round-trip gap at every available resolution; or an instrument that creates a new world primitive rather than giving access to one held elsewhere; or agreement between frames sharing an evidence pool carrying the same verdict weight as independent frames against a separately measured ground.

## 3. What is explicitly not claimed

- Not that technology erases memory. The record grows; the reach may fall.
- Not that any technology has a fixed good or bad valence. Fig 11 :49: *"An actor's valence is relative to the residual, not a property it carries."*
- Not that a filter bubble is a sealed frame. Fig 7 :6: *"the limit: no last room"*.
- Not a causal effect size, not a single number for "technology", not a ground reading.
- Not that the framework settles the question. It returns a shape and a list of refuters.
- Not that the three framework essays read for this packet are independent witnesses. They share an author with the figures.

## 5. What would settle it

A ground reading with a nameable anchor, not further agreement. For C2: a test separating novel generation from replay. For C3: a measured round-trip gap against a separately measured ground, with overlap between frames recorded. For C1: an instrument that violates the dependency order. For the single-causal-number gap: a preregistered, well-powered causal estimate. Until one of these fires, every claim stays `candidate`.

## 6. What an expert can check in twenty minutes

1. Read Fig 9 :54, Fig 11 :40, Fig 6 :42, Fig 4 :22, Fig 10 :24 and Fig 8 :32. Six short passages.
2. For C2, name one result in your field that distinguishes a re-applicable method from a replay. If none exists, say so.
3. For C3, name one instrument that would measure a round-trip loss against a ground, or say why none is possible.
4. State which of the four domains you work on, and name a failure condition you would accept.
5. Send the refutation. A failure is more useful than an endorsement. Record stage-1 verdicts before showing the framework.
