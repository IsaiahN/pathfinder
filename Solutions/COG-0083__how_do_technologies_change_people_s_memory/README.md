## What is deliberately not here

- No personal emails of named individuals.
- No ranking of people by how likely they are to agree.
- No bulk-send plan.

Three figure-grounded reasons, in the figures' own words:

1. **A room does not upgrade the label.** Fig 7 :35: *"A room's agreement is variation, not a verdict."* Fig 7 :13: *"agreement compounds, evidence does not"*. Adding recipients raises the vocabulary and does not add an anchor.
2. **Correlation disqualifies a verdict.** Fig 2 :40: *"agreement about the world is worthless when the agreeing frames share an evidence pool"*. Selecting recipients for receptiveness manufactures exactly the correlation that makes their agreement worthless.
3. **Asking for trust before checking spends independence.** Fig 10 :42: *"A verifier that can reconstruct the claim cannot verify it."* Fig 10 :52: *"Bring a characterised residual, never the source."*

Legally: unsolicited bulk outreach using scraped personal emails breaches GDPR and CAN-SPAM style
rules in many jurisdictions. Public opt-in channels avoid the structural failure and the legal
one at the same time.
