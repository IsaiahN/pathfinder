# The Tether's answer: how technologies change memory, attention, communication and the knowable

**Mode: `general` on the structural claims, `specified` on the four domain predictions. Not
`grounded`.** This is a shape and a route, not a result. It is a simulated derivation checked for
internal consistency against the figures (`05_what_shape_means.md` section 7), not a ground reading.

## 1. The residual it answers

The question arrives as one bundle. Fig 9 :54 says *"And when the split lands on a union rather than a partition, unbundle before you search again."* The residual is four residuals, each with its own actor, currency and anchor. The effect to be decided is: which contact changes, what is recorded versus what is reachable, where the boundary diff points, what crosses the channel, and which closure presents itself as the whole of the knowable. The verdicts below are kept separate and are never averaged.

## 2. Verdict by domain

### D1, Attention: the allocation moves; aggregation erases the signal

Fig 1 :19: *"The residual is indexed by object slot, not aggregated."* Fig 1 :22: *"Aggregating across slots is how a live signal disappears into an average."*

A ranking or notification system is a boundary-diff allocator. It decides which object slots carry positive mass. The failure the figure names is not generic distraction but aggregation: pooling many slots into one engagement metric throws away the signal that says where to look. **Mode: `specified`. Status: `triangulated`** with Fig 10's channel-decay line, which governs a different mechanism.

### D2, Memory: the record grows; the reach need not

Fig 6 :42: *"What is recorded only grows. What is reachable is derived, and it can fall."* Fig 4 :22: *"Only methods cross up. Only head starts come down."* Fig 5 :72: *"a library that grows is not a reach that grows."*

An external store expands Γ, the library. It expands closure(Γ) only if what crosses the membrane is a re-applicable method or a head start. A recording crossed upward is a description of one occasion; replayed downward it *"produces a system that repeats a past success"* (Fig 4 :26). The operational test is whether the saved item generates a novel success. So the honest claim is not "technology erases memory" but "the record and the reach decouple". **Mode: `specified`. Status: `triangulated`** across three figures.

### D3, Communication: the channel decays; the ground does not

Fig 10 :24: *"the ground does not decay, the channel does"*. Fig 4 :30: *"So take the round trip and measure the gap. Without it you are navigating by dead reckoning: confident about a position nobody checked."*

A communication technology changes what crosses between frames and how much is lost. It does not change what settles a claim. The quantity to measure is the round-trip gap R_T, not the amount of agreement. With a shared feed, Fig 2 :40 applies: *"agreement about the world is worthless when the agreeing frames share an evidence pool"*. **Mode: `specified`. Status: `triangulated`** across Fig 10, Fig 4 and Fig 2.

### D4, The knowable: the wall moves by import; search adds no atom

Fig 6 :23: *"No frame escapes its own closure. The bound moves only on contact with a frame whose closure differs."* Fig 6 :25: *"An import adds no atom to the world."* Fig 8 :32: *"no search, however long, adds an atom"*.

An instrument extends what can be represented at all (Fig 6 :26), so it moves the wall by giving access to atoms held in another frame's closure. It does not add a primitive to the world. Because a frame cannot certify its own limit, an expanded reach feels like the whole of the knowable from inside. Fig 9 :32: *"you get a kind of thing, never the answer itself"*. A curated feed is a room, not a terminal room: Fig 7 :6 names *"the limit: no last room"*. **Mode: `specified`. Status: `triangulated`** across Fig 6 and Fig 8.

## 3. The single-causal-number question is a gap

There is no answer in this folder to "what is the causal effect of technology X on memory Y in population Z". Fig 9 :32 gives a kind of thing, never the answer itself. The canonical README says the framework returns *"a shape, not a name"*. Fig 13 states the principle plainly: *"Unreachable is a verdict here, not a silence."* **Mode: `general`. Status: `gap`.** A ground reading with a nameable anchor would close it.

## 4. What it explicitly does not say

- Not that technology erases memory. The record grows; the reach may fall.
- Not that any technology has a fixed valence. Fig 11 :49: *"An actor's valence is relative to the residual, not a property it carries."*
- Not that a filter bubble is a sealed frame. There is no last room and no sealed room.
- Not that agreement among users of the same medium is corroboration.
- Not that this is grounded. No measurement was taken.
- Not that the framework settles the original question. It returns the shape each answer must have.

## 5. What would settle it

A ground reading, not more agreement. Concretely:

1. **Attention:** a per-slot residual measurement that changes under the technology and recovers when the allocation is removed. A single self-report score is the aggregate Fig 1 disqualifies.
2. **Memory:** a test of whether a saved item generates a novel success rather than repeating an old one. If replay alone generates novelty, the head-start/replay bond is wrong.
3. **Communication:** a measured round-trip gap against a separately measured ground, with the frames' overlap recorded. If pooled readings carried verdict weight, Fig 2 :40 would be false.
4. **Knowable:** an instrument that changes which items are reachable, with the import's provenance recorded. A demonstration that more search inside the same closure adds an atom would break Fig 8 :32.
5. **The gap:** a preregistered, well-powered causal estimate with a nameable anchor. Until one exists, F8 stays a gap and nothing is `grounded`.

## 6. The honest stop

**Unreached, not unreachable.** The four shapes are triangulated inside the framework, but the framework essays share an author with the figures and the world sources partly share a literature. Their agreement is one evidence pool talking (Fig 2 :40), not corroboration. The answer stops where the instruments stop distinguishing, and the lineage is recorded so the stop is checkable. *"Indiscernibility is not identity."*

## 7. One-paragraph version for outreach

> The Tether does not say that technology is destroying memory, and it does not offer a single
> causal number. It says the question is four questions. Attention changes because a boundary diff
> is re-aimed, and pooling slots into one metric erases the live signal. Memory changes because an
> external store grows what is recorded while what is reachable grows only if a method, not a
> replay, crosses the membrane. Communication changes in the channel, not the ground, so the thing
> to measure is the loss across the round trip, not the amount of agreement. The sense of what can
> be known changes because an instrument moves the wall by import while search inside the same
> closure adds no atom. Each claim is stated with the failure condition written in advance. Where a
> ground reading is missing, the answer reports the gap rather than filling it.
