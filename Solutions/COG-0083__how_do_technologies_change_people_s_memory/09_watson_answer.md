## QUESTION

Decide, in effect terms, how a technology changes four things: memory, attention, communication and
the sense of what can be known. Option A: answer with one claim, "technology changes cognition".
Option B: split the bundle into four domains, derive a shape and a failure condition for each, and
report a gap where no ground reading exists. What each leads to: A sends a general-mode verdict over
a union, averages verdicts that must stay separate, and manufactures the correlation that
disqualifies corroboration; B sends four checkable shapes, written refuters, and an honest stop.
Figures are primary and decide; world sources are secondary corroboration only.

## DOMAIN CENSUS

R by arity, symmetry and scale, then one line per domain with actors, currency and anchor, partition
or union, and order by dependency.

- **R (the residual).** Arity: four conditions, so a union compound (Fig 9 :54: *"And when the split lands on a union rather than a partition, unbundle before you search again."*). Symmetry: asymmetric, introduction is available and exclusion is not (Fig 11 :61). Scale: one agent and many agents across the membrane (Fig 4 :22).
- **Domain 1, attention and perception.** Actors: perception, object slots, boundary diff. Currency: per-slot residual, where to look broken versus novel. Anchor: eye tracking, recall tests and signal-detection measures. Question type: partition of which slots are live.
- **Domain 2, memory and learn-and-carry.** Actors: person, library, external store, ground. Currency: recorded versus reachable, method versus replay. Anchor: verified recall and learning outcomes. Question type: partition of what crosses the membrane.
- **Domain 3, communication and the channel.** Actors: frames, channel, medium. Currency: agreement and residuals crossing the channel. Anchor: the ground each frame ranges against separately. Question type: partition of what survives the transform.
- **Domain 4, the sense of what can be known.** Actors: frame, closure, external sources, instruments. Currency: reachability and import. Anchor: an instrument or experiment that settles. Question type: partition of what is composable from what is held.
- **Order by dependency.** Attention (perception) before memory (vocabulary and learn-and-carry) before the knowable (the objective) before communication (the membrane). Fig 3 :20: *"The chain is ordered by dependency, not by importance."*
- **Partition or union.** Union. The overlap of memory and communication on "remember where, not what" is a compound with its own loop, not a mixture to be averaged.

Strain test applied: mapping a biological memory store onto the framework library is a binding from
the cognitive-science frame, not a derivation from Fig 13. It is labelled `specified` and carries a
source gate. No other strain survived re-census.

## VERDICT (from the figures)

The bundle splits into four domains with separate verdicts. Attention: a boundary diff is re-aimed
and aggregation erases the live slot signal. Memory: the record grows, reach grows only on a method
or head start, and replay produces repetition. Communication: the channel decays while the ground
does not, and the round trip gap is the quantity. The knowable: an instrument moves the wall by
import and search inside a closure adds no atom. The master claim is that technology changes contact,
not the agent. Each verdict is `specified` and `candidate`, not `grounded`. The question "what is
the causal effect of technology X on memory Y" is a `gap`: the framework returns a shape, and a
ground reading with a nameable anchor is owed.

- **cost if wrong:** bounded and locatable. Every claim is mode-labeled, carries a refuter written
  in advance and keeps its lineage, so an error is findable rather than invisible. The real cost is
  spending an outside reader's independence by sending the framework before the output, which Fig 2
  :42 prices as *"Buy independence in proportion to the cost of being wrong."* That cost is
  avoidable by leading with the four outputs and their failure conditions.
- **undoable:** yes. The four domain shapes can be revised without touching the union method or the
  master claim. A retracted output is added to `01`'s retraction log rather than erased, and the
  reach falls because a refusal was added, not because a term was deleted.
- **reversed by:** a ground reading, not agreement. A per-slot residual measurement that changes
  under the technology would settle D1; a test that replay alone generates novelty would reverse
  D2; a measured zero round-trip gap would reverse D3; an instrument that adds a world primitive or
  a search that adds an atom would reverse D4; a preregistered causal estimate would close the gap.
  Agreement from people selected for receptiveness is not such an instrument.
