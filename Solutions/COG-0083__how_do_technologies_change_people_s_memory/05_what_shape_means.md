## 1. A shape is a constraint, not a filler

The canonical figures README states the limit: the framework returns *"what a missing thing would have to look like (a shape, not a name)"*. Fig 9 :32: *"you get a kind of thing, never the answer itself"*. Fig 13 describes a system that *"can report that it lacks something rather than producing an answer anyway."*

From the Library of Babel essay, read directly: *"you can cut your search short if the set of books returned is small and descriptive enough that it provides a general shape of the answer that satisfies your question."*

So a **shape** is the narrowed form of an answer you can state before you can name or measure it.
It constrains; it does not fill. For this question:

- "technology changes memory" is a topic, not a shape;
- "the record grows while the reachable set need not" is a shape;
- "a specific technology raises recall by 12 percent in population P" is a name-and-number, which
  the framework cannot produce.

## 2. How a shape is obtained

- **Library of Babel, proximity bootstrapping:** *"If you don't know the right terms to search for, you will have to use the existing vocabulary you do know that is closest to the words that you wish to describe and grasp the shape of."* And: *"With each new term gained, you are able to narrow the space by definition and deepen your vocabulary."*
- **The question is the anchor:** *"you need something to tether and anchor you to reality... This anchor is provided by your own question"*. This is the essay's version of Fig 2's anchor: the
  question settles whether to stop or continue, and it must not be replaced by the frame you hoped
  to find.
- **Fig 9 :27:** *"describe it before you go looking, or"* otherwise any frame will seem to fit.
  The essay's "problem behind the problem" is the same: *"The Problem Is You"*, the searcher's
  vocabulary, not the library.
- **Stopping:** the essay says *"you will know that you are done because the finish line will also be the starting place"*, and the paradox is that by then you have *"composed the answer through the process of locating it."* For COG-0083, the finish line is a list of contact changes with
  anchors, not a sentence about technology in general.

## 3. What licenses a name

Only two things, both outside the shape itself:

1. **The ground settles it.** `THE_FORMULA.md` step 5: *"SETTLE - the ground settles it. Verified only by what pays. A gate is not the ground."* Here that means a measurement with a nameable
   anchor: a per-slot attention measure, a verified recall test, a round-trip loss measurement.
2. **A provenanced import from a frame whose closure differs** (Fig 6 :23, Fig 8 :50-52), with a
   source gate and a shadow test. Transactive memory and the extended mind are such frames for
   this question, and both are debited against independence.

The Library of Babel essay adds a warning that applies directly to "technology studies": researchers
and librarians *"lose themselves in the details"*, and *"Just In Time Learning keeps you from being overwhelmed by each field of study's nuanced stances on a problem, and prevents you from being limited by their jargon and specifics of the applications of what they learned."* That is a reason
to keep the four domains separate rather than import the jargon of all four at once.

## 4. The source problem, and why "what technology does to memory" is the wrong form

The Hard Problem is About Source, read directly, gives the mechanism. Metamerism: an instrument
reads green and *"the instrument is speaking only about what it measured, it cannot comment on the source"*. One green light, or yellow plus blue, or RGB, or CMYK, or a scheme not yet discovered, all
produce the same reading. *"If several paths create the same behavior, then behavior is a projection with lossy information."* And: *"An instrument that can't distinguish these cases demonstrates its own inadequacy."*

Applied here: a fall in measured recall is a reading, not a source. It could come from a changed
contact (the store is now external), a changed allocation (attention was aimed elsewhere at
encoding), a changed channel (the retrieval cue is stale), or a changed closure (the person no
longer treats the item as knowable and stops rehearsing it). Four sources, one reading. The
framework's answer is to refuse to name the source until an instrument discriminates among them,
and that refusal is exactly why the question had to be split into four domains.

## 5. What shape means for COG-0083, domain by domain

| domain | shape (stateable before measurement) | what would turn it into a name |
|---|---|---|
| Attention | a boundary diff is re-aimed; what is lost when slots are aggregated into one metric is the live signal | a per-slot residual measurement that changes under the technology and recovers when the allocation is removed |
| Memory | the record grows; reach grows only if a re-applicable method crosses the membrane | a test of whether a saved item generates a novel success rather than repeating an old one |
| Communication | the channel decays while the ground does not; the round-trip gap is the quantity | a measured loss across the round trip against a separately measured ground |
| Sense of what can be known | an instrument moves the wall by import; search inside the same closure adds no atom | an instrument that changes which items are reachable, with the import's provenance recorded |

None of these rows is a verdict about a specific technology. Each is a constraint any candidate
answer must satisfy.

## 6. Prestige is not action

From the Recursive Self-Improvement Trap, read directly: *"Prestige can fund an action. It can give an action a budget to be tried. But it cannot settle an action. Only the ground can settle it. The Ground Maintainer must be blind to prestige."* A campaign that gathers famous names to agree that
"technology is changing cognition" is the named collapse. The legitimate ask is the opposite: give
the four outputs a budget to be tried and let the ground decide.

## 7. My own audit is a simulation, not a ground reading

Same essay, read directly: *"LLMs can quickly simulate an approximate answer/output with the formula found in the architecture post... However, the truly testable output would need to be derived from the real architecture or working the formula yourself."* And the essay's own caveat:
*"I am not claiming that the Tether is a self-validating axiom system. Metatheories cannot validate themselves."*

Therefore `01_figure_grounded_audit.md` is a simulated derivation checked for internal consistency
against the figures. It is **not** a test of the claim and cannot settle anything it reports on.
Every mode label in this folder is either `general` or `specified`; none is `grounded`.

## 8. A narrowing I should record, not erase

An earlier draft of this file treated "shape, not a name" as a blanket ban on naming any mechanism.
That is too strong. The canonical README's phrase governs *"a missing thing"*, a primitive not held
in Γ. For COG-0083 the mechanisms (an introduced instrument, a slot-indexed residual, a channel, an
import) are already held framework atoms. Naming them is a binding from cognitive science onto held
atoms, not the invention of a new primitive. What remains forbidden is turning a binding into a
measurement without a ground, or using a shared author's essay as an independent witness. Both
claims are kept visible here rather than the stronger one being quietly retained.
