## 0. The claim under audit, restated as an effect

The question arrives as a bundle: memory, attention, communication, and the sense of what can be
known. In effect terms the question is not "what does technology do to the mind" but:

> A technology is an introduced actor or instrument in a human habitat. When it is introduced,
> which contact changes, what is recorded versus what is reachable, where the boundary diff
> points, what crosses between frames, and which closures come to present themselves as the whole
> of the knowable? What would we have to measure to tell a change in contact from a change in the
> agent?

What an answer would look like, bound as an ordinary term before the run (Formula step,
"Direction"): **a per-domain list of changed contact quantities, each with a nameable anchor and
a refuter**, not a single verdict about "technology". This binding is what makes the sought-for
shape refusable by the same test as everything else.

Two readings are refused at the outset, with reasons:

- **Refused A:** "technology erases memory." The figures do not say the record shrinks. They say
  *"What is recorded only grows. What is reachable is derived, and it can fall."* (Fig 6 :42).
  The honest claim is a decoupling of record from reach, not erasure.
- **Refused B:** "technology is a tool, so it is neutral." The figures say contact is what a
  system's capability consists in. *"A system cannot be made more capable by improving the agent in isolation."* (Fig 11 :40). A tool that changes contact is not neutral.

## 1. Domain census

### 1.1 R by arity, symmetry and scale

- **Arity: four conditions, so a union, not one hard question.** Memory, attention, communication
  and epistemics each keep working well on a different subset of the evidence. Fig 9 :54:
  *"And when the split lands on a union rather than a partition, unbundle before you search again."* Fig 9 :4-5 adds: disagreements that do not shrink with effort are *"not one hard question but several well-formed ones. Split it rather than search."* Mode: `general`.
- **Symmetry: asymmetric.** Introduction is available, exclusion is not. Fig 11 :61: *"The seat's power is asymmetric. Introduction is available; exclusion is not."* You can add a search engine
  to a habitat; you cannot subtract the habitat it lands in. Mode: `general`.
- **Scale: one agent and many agents, across a membrane.** Fig 4 :22: *"Only methods cross up. Only head starts come down."* A personal memory aid and a shared archive sit on opposite sides
  of that boundary. Mode: `general`.

No domain is admitted without a nameable anchor. D4's anchor is harder to build than D1's, and that

### 1.3 Partition or union

Union. The four domains overlap: an external store is both a memory actor (D2) and a channel
actor (D3), and a search engine is both an instrument (D4) and an attention allocator (D1). Fig 9
:55-58 says a bundled problem is usually *"a union of overlapping conditions, not a list of separate ones"* and that a single lever clears the region where only one condition holds and
leaves the intersections standing. Each overlap is therefore a compound with its own loop. The
compound of "remember where, not what" sits in D2 and D3 at once; it gets one loop, not an average
of two.

### 1.4 Order by dependency (Fig 3)

1. **Attention / perception** (Fig 3 :4-5, link 1). You cannot name what a situation affords if
   perception has not selected a slot.
2. **Memory / vocabulary and learn-and-carry** (Fig 3 :6-7, :14-16, links 2 and 5). It has terms
   for what things afford, and names what worked.
3. **Sense of what can be known** (Fig 3 :9-11, link 3, the objective). It can say what would count
   as progress, in its own terms. This is the middle link where chains usually break: Fig 3 :27
   says the link between having terms and saying what would count as progress *"has neither, so it attracts the least attention and the fewest instruments."*
4. **Communication** (Fig 4 membrane). You communicate what you attended to, remembered and took
   as knowable. Fig 3 :20: *"The chain is ordered by dependency, not by importance."*

Effort spent above the break cannot rescue a link below it (Fig 3 :19). So better search engines
(D4) do not repair an unmeasured attention signal (D1).

## 2. Findings

### F1  -  The four-item question is a union, and must be unbundled (decisive for method)

**Mode: `general`. Triangulated:** Fig 9 (union/partition) and Fig 3 (dependency order) are two
figures with low overlap: one is about search geometry, the other about link status.

Fig 9 :54: *"And when the split lands on a union rather than a partition, unbundle before you search again."* Fig 9 :4-5: *"Disagreements that do not shrink with effort, where each rule keeps working well on a different subset,"* and *"are not one hard question but several well-formed ones. Split it rather than search."*

A single claim of the form "technology changes cognition" keeps working on the memory subset,
the attention subset, the communication subset and the epistemic subset, with no single measure
that moves all four. By the figure it is four well-formed questions. Fig 9 :55-58 adds that even
after a lever clears the single-condition regions, *"what remains is smaller and it is also categorically different: a case in two conditions is not a harder case of either. It stops being a mixture and becomes a specific compound."*

**Refuter:** a single instrument or measure that moves all four sub-questions in lockstep with low
overlap between them would collapse the union to a partition, and this finding would be withdrawn.

### F2  -  Technology changes contact, not the agent (the master claim)

**Mode: `general`. Triangulated:** Fig 11 (capability is agent-and-habitat) and Fig 6 (an import
adds access, not a world primitive) are independent frames.

Fig 11 :40: *"A system cannot be made more capable by improving the agent in isolation. An improvement that does not change contact changes nothing, however much it improves."* Fig 11 :73:
*"The habitat is not the ground. The habitat is where actors come from and it updates; the ground is what settles, and it does not."* Fig 11 :61: *"The seat's power is asymmetric. Introduction is available; exclusion is not."*

So a technology is an introduced actor or instrument in the habitat (Fig 11 :43: *"The habitat is enumerated, never composed."*). What changes is which things the person is in contact with: which
instruments extend representation, which stores hold records, which channel carries readings. The
ground under those contacts, what actually settles a claim, does not move.

This is also where an honest stop enters. Fig 11 :70: *"A test harness is a substituted habitat. Which is why a synthetic solve proves wiring and never capability."* A lab task that measures
clicks in a stripped interface proves the interface works, not that cognition changed in the wild.

**Refuter:** a technology that demonstrably changes capability while leaving every contact
unchanged (no new instrument, no new actor in the habitat, no changed channel, no changed range
against the ground) would refute Fig 11 :40 and the whole derivation built on it.

### F3  -  Attention: the boundary diff is aimed, and aggregation erases the live signal

**Mode: `specified`. Triangulated:** Fig 1 (slot-indexed residual) and Fig 10 (channel decay:
*"stale percepts, dropped transients"*) govern different mechanisms.

Fig 1 :19: *"The residual is indexed by object slot, not aggregated."* Fig 1 :22: *"Aggregating across slots is how a live signal disappears into an average."* Fig 1 :15: *"where to look: broken vs novel"*.

A feed, a notification stream or a ranking algorithm is a boundary-diff allocator. It chooses
which object slots carry positive mass and therefore where the person looks next. The failure mode
the figure names is not "distraction" in the abstract; it is aggregation. When many slots are
pooled into one engagement number, the live slot residual disappears into the average and there is
nothing left to aim with. World corroboration (secondary): the attention-economy frame records
Herbert Simon's 1971 line, *"What information consumes is rather obvious: it consumes the attention of its recipients"* (source read, see ledger).

**Refuter:** show a technology that leaves slot-indexed residual untouched and only adds capacity
to perceive (a genuinely wider instrument with no allocation effect). Then F3 does not apply.

### F4  -  Memory: the record grows; the reach may not

**Mode: `specified`. Triangulated:** Fig 4 (membrane direction) and Fig 5 plus Fig 6
(monotonicity of record versus reach) are three low-overlap statements of the same asymmetry.

Fig 5 :72: *"and a library that grows is not a reach that grows."* Fig 6 :42: *"What is recorded only grows. What is reachable is derived, and it can fall."* Fig 4 :22: *"Only methods cross up. Only head starts come down."* Fig 4 :25: *"A recording carried upward looks like knowledge and is a description of one occasion."*

An external store is a real expansion of Γ. What it does **not** automatically do is expand
closure(Γ): what the person could derive if they searched forever. A recording crossed upward is a
description of one occasion. It becomes a head start for the next search only when it is a method
that can be reapplied, verified and compressed to its essentials (Fig 4 :8-9). Replayed downward
instead, *"Replayed downward, it produces a system that repeats a past success"* (Fig 4 :26): the
person retrieves the saved answer and cannot produce a new one.

The world literature corroborates the shape, weakly and from one evidence pool. The Google-effect
page records that *"if the information is saved, people are much more likely to remember where the information is located than to recall the information itself"* (source read). A meta-analysis of
35 studies reports that *"google effects on memory challenges the way individuals seek and read information, and it may lead to changes in cognitive and memory mechanisms"* (Gong and Yang 2024,
source read). These are secondary witnesses about direction, not grounds for the figure verdict,
and they share a literature.

**Refuter:** demonstrate a stored recording which, merely replayed, yields a novel success that
could not have been produced before the storage. That would make a recording a generator crossing
upward and break the Fig 4 / Fig 5 distinction.

### F5  -  Communication: the channel decays, not the ground

**Mode: `specified`. Triangulated:** Fig 10 (channel maintainer) and Fig 2 (anchor must not update)
plus Fig 4 (round trip) are three figures with low overlap.

Fig 10 :24: *"the ground does not decay, the channel does"*. Fig 10 :25: *"stale percepts, dropped transients"*. Fig 4 :27: *"The transform between scales loses information, and that is the point."*
Fig 4 :30: *"So take the round trip and measure the gap. Without it you are navigating by dead reckoning: confident about a position nobody checked."*

A communication technology changes the channel: what crosses, how much is lost, how stale the
readings are. It does not change the thing the frames range against. What must be measured is the
round-trip gap R_T, the difference between what was sent up and what came back down. Treating the
condensed message as identical to the original sets R_T to zero and is the dead-reckoning error
the figure names.

The same finding covers agreement. Fig 2 :40: *"agreement about the world is worthless when the agreeing frames share an evidence pool"*. Fig 2 :19: *"they never talk, and still agree"*. Fig 7
:13: *"agreement compounds, evidence does not"*. A shared feed that makes people converge by
pooling readings manufactures the disqualifying correlation, not corroboration. Fig 8 :47:
*"Independence is one quantity read twice."*

**Refuter:** show a communication technology that lowers overlap between frames (increases
independence) rather than pooling readings, and whose agreement then tracks a separately measured
ground. That would falsify the claim that this particular change is a channel failure.

### F6  -  The sense of what can be known: the wall moves by import, and a search adds no atom

**Mode: `specified`. Triangulated:** Fig 6 (closure mechanics) and Fig 8 (metaframe theorems) are
independent routes to the same limit.

Fig 6 :21: *"MINT composes inside the closure and can never add an atom."* Fig 6 :23: *"No frame escapes its own closure. The bound moves only on contact with a frame whose closure differs."*
Fig 6 :25: *"An import adds no atom to the world."* Fig 6 :26: *"instruments extend what can be represented at all."* Fig 8 :32: *"no search, however long, adds an atom"*.

A search engine, a telescope or a database is an instrument that extends what can be represented.
It moves the wall inward by giving access to atoms already held in some other frame's closure. It
does not add a primitive to the world, and more searching inside the same closure cannot manufacture
one. Fig 13 :115: *"The set is closed. The arrangements are not."* :116: *"Six members, and no amount of arranging produces a seventh"*.

This is also why the technology changes the **sense** of what can be known without changing what
is knowable. A frame cannot certify its own limit (Fig 8, rule 2, :36-37), so an expanded reach
feels like the whole world from inside. Fig 9 :32: *"you get a kind of thing, never the answer itself"*. The honest report is a shape and a list of what is unreachable with the current library,
not a verdict about the world's contents.

**Refuter:** an instrument that creates a new primitive in the world rather than giving access to
one already held elsewhere would break Fig 6 :25 and Fig 8 :32.

### F7  -  A filter bubble is a room, not a last room, and that is a correction

**Mode: `general`. Triangulated:** Fig 7 (no last room) and Fig 2 (mirror chains) agree from
different diagrams.

Fig 7 :6 names *"the limit: no last room"*. Fig 7 :35: *"A room's agreement is variation, not a verdict."* Fig 2 :35: *"Alignment is triangulation, not negotiation. A mirror cannot correct you."*

It is tempting to describe an algorithmically curated feed as a sealed frame that traps a person
forever. The figures forbid "forever": there is no last room and no sealed room. Fig 9 :44: *"Only a sealed room can be searched to the end, and no frame is a sealed room."* A bubble is a closure
that can be exited on import from a frame whose closure differs. The practical consequence is that
the remedy is an import (a different channel, a low-overlap witness), not an argument delivered
inside the same room.

**Refuter:** a demonstrated frame with no outside left to import from would break the no-last-room
rule. None has been produced, and the figures rule it out definitionally.

### F8  -  The causal, quantitative question is a gap at this budget

**Mode: `general` on the verdict, `specified` on what a ground reading would look like. Single
source for the limit, confirmed by the canonical figures README.**

The figures can return the shape of the answer: which contact changed, which record decoupled from
reach, which channel lost information, which wall moved. They cannot return which technology, in
which population, with what effect size. Fig 9 :32 says *"you get a kind of thing, never the answer itself"*. The canonical figures README states the framework returns *"what a missing thing would have to look like (a shape, not a name)"* and stops where instruments stop distinguishing.

So for the original question, the honest answer is: **four domain shapes, each triangulated within
the framework, and a gap for the single causal number**. This is not silence. Fig 13 :134:
*"Unreachable is a verdict here, not a silence."* Fig 8 :39: *"So a search that finds nothing is never a proof of absence."*

**Refuter:** a well-powered, preregistered experiment with a nameable anchor that estimates the
causal effect of a specific technology on a specific slot would move this domain from `candidate`
to `grounded` and close the gap.

## 3. Pre-registered refuter

Written before the conclusion, per the standing instruction that a falsifier written before the
run is a design instrument. The shape claim is refuted if any of the following holds:

1. A technology changes capability while every contact remains unchanged (no new actor in the
   habitat, no new instrument, no changed channel, no changed range against the ground). This
   breaks Fig 11 :40.
2. A stored recording, merely replayed, produces a novel success it could not previously produce.
   This breaks Fig 4 :22-26 and Fig 5 :72.
3. Search inside a closed frame adds an atom. This breaks Fig 8 :32 and Fig 6 :21.
4. A single measure moves all four sub-questions in lockstep with low overlap, collapsing the
   union to a partition. This breaks F1 and Fig 9 :54.
5. Agreement between frames that share an evidence pool is shown to carry the same verdict weight
   as agreement between frames with low overlap, against a separately measured ground. This
   breaks Fig 2 :40 and Fig 8 :47.
6. A ground reading with a nameable anchor returns a single causal effect size that contradicts
   the domain shapes. This closes F8's gap and overrides the `candidate` status.

If any fires, the relevant finding is withdrawn in the retraction log below with the date and the
source. Until one is run, nothing in this folder is `grounded`.

## 4. Recommendation

1. Unbundle before measuring (F1). Ask four questions, and keep each verdict separate. Do not
   average them.
2. For each domain, name the contact that changed and the anchor that would settle it. If no
   anchor can be named, it is not yet a domain (section 1.2).
3. Treat external stores as record, not reach, until a method crosses the membrane (F4). The
   operational test is whether the saved item generates a new success or only repeats an old one.
4. Measure the round-trip gap R_T for any communication claim (F5). Do not treat a condensed
   message as identical to the original.
5. Report what is unreachable with the current library rather than manufacturing a number (F8).
   *"Unreachable is a verdict, not a silence."*
6. In outreach, lead with the residual and its refuter, never with the framework. Fig 10 :52:
   *"Bring a characterised residual, never the source."*

### Strain after re-census

One strain remains: the figures' Γ is the library of a frame, a formal set of primitives and
everything built from them. A human biological memory is not that object. The mapping is used as
an analogue with a source gate and a shadow test, and is labelled `specified`. It is not allowed to
carry a verdict on its own. No other strain was found after re-census.

### SEARCHED / INWARD / OUTWARD for F8

- **SEARCHED:** is the causal number in the current literature and unfound? The meta-analysis
  reports a pooled effect but also regional heterogeneity and a replication crisis in the
  underlying studies. More search at the same reach will not settle it (Fig 6 amendment, :49-50).
- **INWARD:** sharpen the slot instrument. Fig 1 :19 demands per-slot residuals; a single
  self-report "my memory is worse" is exactly the aggregate the figure disqualifies.
- **OUTWARD:** import a frame whose closure predicts the shape. Transactive memory and the extended
  mind are such frames, but both are secondary and one shares an author with the figures, so the
  import is debited against independence (Fig 6 :24). They corroborate the shape; they do not set
  the verdict.
