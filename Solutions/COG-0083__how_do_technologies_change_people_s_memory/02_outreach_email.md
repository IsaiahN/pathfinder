# Outreach draft: check the output before you decide about the framework

**Use:** one-to-one, or as a cover letter attached to a submission through the public venues in
`03_public_contact_points.csv`. **Not** for bulk send.

**Hard precondition:** do not send until the four residuals in `01_figure_grounded_audit.md` are
stated as separate questions with separate anchors. A bundled "technology and cognition" claim
collapses on contact (F1).

---

**Subject:** Four small claims about technology and memory, each with a failure condition

> Hi [name],
>
> I have four specific outputs I would like you to check against what you already know. I am not
> asking you to read a theory first, and I am not asking you to agree with anything. If an output
> does not survive, there is nothing else to look at. A one-line "this fails because..." is more
> useful to me than agreement.
>
> **The outputs, all `specified` or `general`, none `grounded`:**
>
> 1. The question is four questions, not one. Memory, attention, communication and the sense of
>    what can be known keep working on different subsets of the evidence. Treating them as one
>    hard question is a bundle error.
> 2. A technology changes cognition by changing contact, not by upgrading the person. If nothing
>    in the contact surface changes, no new instrument, no new actor in the habitat, no changed
>    channel, then the figure rule says nothing has changed however much the device improves.
> 3. An external store grows what is recorded. It grows what is reachable only when what crosses
>    is a re-applicable method or a head start. A saved answer that is merely replayed produces a
>    repeat, not a new result. The operational test is new generation, not recall comfort.
> 4. A communication technology changes the channel, not the ground. What should be measured is
>    the loss across the round trip between what was sent and what came back, not the amount of
>    agreement it produces. Agreement through a shared feed is one evidence pool talking.
>
> **What I am not claiming:** that technology erases memory, that a filter bubble is a sealed
> frame with no exit, that any of this has a measured effect size, or that a single causal number
> for "technology" exists. The framework returns a shape, not a number. Where a ground reading is
> missing, I report the gap rather than filling it.
>
> **What would refute each output, written down before this message:**
> 1. One measure that moves all four domains in lockstep with low overlap, which would collapse the
>    bundle into a single partition.
> 2. A device that changes capability with every contact held fixed.
> 3. A stored recording that, merely replayed, yields a novel success it could not yield before.
> 4. Agreement between frames sharing an evidence pool carrying the same verdict weight as
>    agreement between independent frames, checked against a separately measured ground.
>
> If any one holds, I withdraw the corresponding output. If you would rather not engage, no reply
> is needed.
>
> **If it survives, and only then:** here are the sources. The figures are the authority, not my
> summary of them.
> - Verbatim figure text with line numbers (local, in the solution folder): `FRAMEWORK/reference/03-FIGURES_TEXT.md`
> - Canonical figures README: https://github.com/IsaiahN/tether/blob/general-agent/figures/README.md
> - The eight-step formula: https://github.com/IsaiahN/tether/tree/general-agent/figures
>
> **If you want to use the machinery:** the formula is in the figures link. Run it on a problem you
> know well and can check against a ground. Write down what would refute your result *before* you
> run it. The framework's own rule is that a loop cannot check the direction of its own question,
> and the author warns that outputs can be contrived to fit the formula. A reported failure is
> more useful to me than a reported success.
>
>  -  [your name]

## Why this version is ordered this way

| the move | figure-grounded reason |
|---|---|
| Outputs first, theory second | Fig 10 :52  -  *"Bring a characterised residual, never the source."* Frames range against the ground separately. |
| Do not ask for agreement | Fig 2 :35  -  *"Alignment is triangulation, not negotiation. A mirror cannot correct you."* |
| Refuters before the conclusion | Seat Map :287: a falsifier written before the run is a design instrument, not only an honesty device. |
| Mode on every claim | `THE_FORMULA.md`: *"Mode. general · specified · grounded. State which."* |
| Four claims, never averaged | Fig 1 :19  -  *"The residual is indexed by object slot, not aggregated."* Fig 9 :54 requires unbundling. |
| No "technology destroys memory" | Fig 6 :42  -  *"What is recorded only grows. What is reachable is derived, and it can fall."* |
| No claim of a measured effect | Fig 9 :32  -  *"you get a kind of thing, never the answer itself"*. |
| No request for trust or prestige | Recursive Self-Improvement Trap: *"Prestige can fund an action. It can give an action a budget to be tried. But it cannot settle an action. Only the ground can settle it."* |
| Links last, opt-in only | Fig 4 :22  -  *"Only methods cross up. Only head starts come down."* A reader who absorbs the framework before judging stage 1 has joined the frame being checked. |
| Offer the formula as a method | Fig 4 :22 again. A generator can be given away without giving away an answer. |

## Recording the verdict

Keep a row per recipient with: date, which of the four outputs was checked, the failure condition
they named, whether any fired, and their verdict on stage 1. Record it **before** sending any
theory. Later agreement is never pooled as corroboration: Fig 2 :40  -  *"agreement about the world is worthless when the agreeing frames share an evidence pool"*.
