# WATSON answer: what meaningful human control requires at machine speed and scale

**Mode: `specified`, not `grounded`.** Figures are primary and decide. World sources are secondary, corroborate but never overrule, and a conflict returns the derivation to the domain census. This is a simulated derivation checked for internal consistency against the figures, not a ground reading. No instrument reading, experiment or human-subject study was run.

## QUESTION

Decide, in effect terms, what the Tether says about this claim: meaningful human control requires a supervisor to intervene when a system acts faster or at larger scale than that supervisor. Option A: give a number, a latency or an operator-to-agent ratio, and treat presence of a human as sufficient. Option B: keep the figure-derived shape and refuse the number. What each leads to: A produces a clean-looking dashboard and a mirror chain, spends the independence of the people checking it, and asserts a threshold no instrument supplied; B produces a checkable set of necessary conditions on the arrangement, four falsifiers written in advance, and an honest gap where the number would go.

R stated as an effect: R = |Γ(b,a) − o′| per slot s, where the supervisor is a seat whose office is to keep the ground reachable and the ground is whatever settles whether an action was acceptable without moving because a frame thinks so.

## DOMAIN CENSUS

R by arity, symmetry and scale, then one line per domain with actors, currency, anchor, and partition or union, ordered by dependency.

- **R.** Arity: three terms, the supervisor seat, the system frame, and a ground that is not a frame. A two-place human-machine reading has no place for the thing that settles the question. Symmetry: asymmetric, the ground must not update on the frames (Fig 2 :33) and frames range against it (Fig 2 :45). Scale: two membranes, cycle time and object count, with an invariant kernel (Fig 4 :19-20). Union: speed and scale are overlapping conditions with a compound at their intersection (Fig 9 :55-57).
- **D1 Supervisory seat / ground maintenance.** Actors: human supervisor, action stream, ground. Currency: contact and residual reduction. Anchor: the non-updating ground. Status: admitted.
- **D2 System loop.** Actors: system Γ, action a, observation o′, object slots s. Currency: per-slot residual and actions. Anchor: the same ground read at a different cycle time; closure may differ from the seat's. Status: admitted.
- **D3 Speed and scale membrane.** Actors: one-agent loop, many-agent population, instruments, head starts. Currency: methods up, head starts down, round trip gap R_T. Anchor: the invariant kernel and the reachable anchor. Status: admitted.
- **D4 Verification and independence.** Actors: supervisor frame, system frame, outside verifier. Currency: agreement versus evidence, overlap. Anchor: low overlap, that is independence. Status: admitted.
- **X1 "Meaningful" as free-floating intent.** No nameable anchor until the shape is bound as an ordinary term (Fig 5 :65-66). Not a domain yet; bound in `01` §0.
- **X2 "Control equals a fixed latency T".** Absorbed into D3 and D4. The number is a `gap`, not a hidden term.

Union, not partition: D1/D4 form the verification compound; D2/D3 form the membrane compound. Dependency order: perception, vocabulary, objective, planning, learn and carry (Fig 3). Strain test: Fig 4 (kernel invariant with size) and Fig 10 (channel decays) govern different quantities, so no strain after re-census. The field's "tracking" condition and Fig 2's anchor rule govern different axes (people questions versus code-correctness questions), so no strain there either.

## TETHER (primary, verbatim)

Cited against `FRAMEWORK/reference/03-FIGURES_TEXT.md`. Quotes are verbatim from the figure text. Per-domain verdicts are kept separate and never averaged.

**D1 Supervisory seat, status `triangulated` (Fig 10 channel decay and Fig 2 anchor rule, low overlap).**

- Fig 10 :24 "the ground does not decay, the channel does" -> the failure is contact, not intent.
- Fig 10 :25-26 "stale percepts, dropped transients, saturated metrics, pooled readings" -> the named instruments fail at speed and scale.
- Fig 10 :27-29 "the output is not a verdict, it is you have lost touch" and "keep the anchor reachable" -> the prescription is structural.
- Fig 2 :33 "The anchor must not update." -> a supervisor that ratifies the system's output is not a ground.
- Fig 11 :40 "A system cannot be made more capable by improving the agent in isolation. An improvement that does not change contact changes nothing, however much it improves." -> training alone is not an instrument.

Reading: meaningful control requires a maintained seat whose anchor is non-updating and whose channel is live. Mode `specified`. Refuter: a saturated, pooled seat still produces ground-validated refusals with the live slot named.

**D2 System loop, status `triangulated` (Fig 1 per-slot indexing and Fig 6 closure rule, low overlap).**

- Fig 1 :19 "The residual is indexed by object slot, not aggregated." -> at scale the signal is per object.
- Fig 1 :22 "Aggregating across slots is how a live signal disappears into an average." -> the global dashboard is the failure instrument.
- Fig 1 :13 "a global R near zero with one live slot residual is a legal state" -> green can coexist with a live case.
- Fig 6 :23 "No frame escapes its own closure. The bound moves only on contact with a frame whose closure differs." -> the seat cannot derive the system's behaviour from inside its own closure.

Reading: control requires per-slot contact and a seat whose closure differs from the system's. Mode `specified`. Refuter: an aggregate metric with no per-slot access still lets the seat locate and refuse the live case on a real ground.

**D3 Speed and scale membrane, status `triangulated` (Fig 3 dependency chain and Fig 4 membrane rule, low overlap).**

- Fig 3 :27 "A link with no instrument is not a link that works. It is a link nobody has looked at." -> speed moves the break point.
- Fig 3 :29-30 "Before asking whether a system is good, ask which link it currently stops at, and whether the answer was measured or assumed." -> the honest diagnostic.
- Fig 4 :19-20 "The kernel is the part that does not change with size: the same loop and the same rule for naming what worked, whether one agent is running it or a thousand. Only the actors, the currency and the anchor change."
- Fig 4 :22 "Only methods cross up. Only head starts come down." -> a copied protocol is a recording, not a method.
- Fig 4 :30 "So take the round trip and measure the gap. Without it you are navigating by dead reckoning: confident about a position nobody checked." -> R_T must be measured.

Reading: the chain must be intact at the system's speed, and scale transfers require a measured round trip. Mode `specified`, with the kernel claim `general`. Refuter: a procedure copied across scale retains its refusal rate with no R_T measurement.

**D4 Verification and independence, status `triangulated` (Fig 2, Fig 8 and Fig 7, three low-overlap routes).**

- Fig 2 :35 "Alignment is triangulation, not negotiation. A mirror cannot correct you." -> a supervisor fed only the system's output is a mirror.
- Fig 2 :31 "Independence, between two frames, is how little they draw on the same evidence." -> independence is a quantity, not a virtue.
- Fig 8 :48 "How much two frames draw on the same evidence decides both whether their agreement means anything and whether their union has any surplus." -> low overlap buys both verdict and surplus.
- Fig 7 :36 "A chain of mirrors is one long room. The next room must differ from the last, and match the residual it surfaced." -> oversight that ratifies is motion without progress.
- Fig 10 :59 "an interest in the outcome is unavoidable, an interest in the reading is disqualifying." -> declare the stake, do not let it enter the reading.
- Fig 10 :65-66 "Provenance shows a ruling was reached legitimately. It never shows it was right: the ground does that, and a wrong ruling stays wrong however clean its record." -> a clean trail is not a verdict.

Reading: the seat must be an independent frame with a declared stake, and agreement is never pooled as corroboration. Mode `specified`. Refuter: a mirror supervisor still passes an independence test.

**The vocabulary condition, status `triangulated` (Fig 12 and the Operators table).**

- Fig 12 :112 "A plan is a sequence. A goal is a comparison. Progress is a subtraction. A refutation is a negation, and that one is not a bond." -> a seat needs comparison and negation to state a goal and refuse a case.
- Operators table :57 "a refutation is a negation, which is why a vocabulary without it can only accumulate" -> without negation the seat only collects alerts.
- Fig 5 :66 "Committing a prediction before a run is this, already working." -> the shape is bound as an ordinary term and the refuter precedes the run.

**The numeric threshold, status `gap`.** No figure line after a genuine re-census states a latency, an attention share or a ratio. Fig 9 :32 "you get a kind of thing, never the answer itself" and Fig 13 :134 "Unreachable is a verdict here, not a silence," record the limit. Fig 8 :39 "So a search that finds nothing is never a proof of absence." The missing thing is an instrument characterized in effect terms but not held.

## VERDICT (from the figures)

Meaningful control at machine speed and scale requires a maintained seat, not a number and not a human presence token. The seat ranges against a non-updating ground, draws on evidence different from the system's, reads the residual per slot at the system's speed, has a comparison and a negation with which to state a goal and refuse a case, declares its stake, and measures the round trip when a method crosses a scale boundary. The number (latency, attention share, operator-to-agent ratio) is a declared `gap`, owed to an instrument. The verdict is the union of D1 through D4 kept separate per domain, never averaged. Mode `specified`, ungrounded, `candidate`. World sources corroborate the shape from different pools but do not decide it.

- **cost if wrong:** a control arrangement that looks clean and is a mirror. The cost is a live slot hidden behind a green aggregate, and a supervisor whose agreement is mistaken for evidence. Fig 2 :42 prices the remedy: "Buy independence in proportion to the cost of being wrong." That cost is avoidable by leading with the shape and the four refuters; not paying it is what makes the failure invisible.
- **undoable:** yes. No term is deleted if a refuter fires; a refusal is added and the reach falls because something was added rather than anything struck out (Fig 6 :42-43). The shape, the gap and the falsifiers are recorded in a provenance trail so a later reader can locate the error (Fig 10 :62-64). A sent packet can be superseded by a corrected one.
- **reversed by:** any of the four pre-registered falsifiers in `01` §4: (1) a saturated, pooled seat still produces ground-validated refusals at system speed with the live slot named; (2) a mirror supervisor still passes an independence test; (3) an arrangement with no comparison and no negation still produces refusable refusals the ground validates; (4) an operator-only improvement that changes no contact still moves the refusal rate. A ground instrument that settles a numeric threshold would close F7 without reversing the shape. Agreement, prestige and a clean audit trail are not such instruments.
