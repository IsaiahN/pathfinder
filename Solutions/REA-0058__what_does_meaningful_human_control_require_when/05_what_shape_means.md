## 1. A shape is a constraint, not a filler

The canonical limit is that the framework returns *"what a missing thing would have to look like (a shape, not a name)"*, then stops at what the instruments can distinguish and records the lineage. Fig 9 :32: *"you get a kind of thing, never the answer itself"*; Fig 13 :134: *"Unreachable is a verdict here, not a silence,"*

From *The Library of Babel*: *"you can cut your search short if the set of books returned is small and descriptive enough that it provides a general shape of the answer that satisfies your question."*

So a **shape** is the narrowed form of an answer you can state before you can name it. It constrains; it does not fill. For this question:

- "The supervisor must remain a seat with a live, non-updating channel to the ground" is a **shape**.
- "The supervisor must have 4.7 seconds to intervene" is a **name**, and no figure line supplies it.

The field agrees about the status of the number. Santoni de Sio and van den Hoven write that *"policy-makers and technical designers lack a detailed theory of what 'meaningful human control' exactly means"* and answer with two necessary conditions, tracking and tracing, not a latency. The ICRC and UN materials likewise list retained elements (information, judgment, limits on targets) rather than a number.

## 2. How a shape is obtained

- *Library of Babel*: use the vocabulary already held that is *closest* to the thing, look for recurring new terms that overlap or correlate with it, recurse. *"With each new term gained, you are able to narrow the space by definition and deepen your vocabulary."*
- Fig 9 :30-31: *"your words are borrowed too, so describe the effect rather than the cause"*. The effect here is a decaying channel, not a moral failing of the operator.
- Fig 6 :30: *"outward: does another frame already hold it? The description travels. Fails by being sharpened into terms only we can read. Cast it in effect terms."* This is why `09_watson_answer.md` searches human factors, automation research and evolutionary biology rather than staying inside the MHC vocabulary.
- *Library of Babel* on the anchor: *"you need something to tether and anchor you to reality, the same way a mountaineer needs a tether."* The question itself is the tether when the figure lines are read: it selects which figure lines are relevant before any field is chosen (Fig 9 :53: *"Nobody chooses. The gap does, and the gap is only as good as its description."*).

## 3. What licenses a name

Only two things, both outside the shape itself:

1. **The ground settles it.** `THE_FORMULA.md`, step 5: *"the ground settles it. Verified only by what pays. A gate is not the ground."* For this question, a name (a specific latency, a specific operator-to-agent ratio) is licensed only by an instrument that shows a ground-validated change in refusals when the number changes.
2. **A provenanced import from a frame whose closure differs** (Fig 6 :23, Fig 8 :50-52), with a source gate and a shadow test. An imported number must explain a gap in the receiving frame and be debited against that frame's independence.

*The Hard Problem is About Source* gives the failure mode. An instrument that reads green *"cannot comment on the source"*: one green light, or yellow plus blue, or RGB, or CMYK. A reading does not determine the source. *"If several paths create the same behavior, then behavior is a projection with lossy information."* The same structure applies here: a global risk score reading "safe" does not tell you whether the ground is reachable or whether all slots are clear. The instrument that reads the aggregate cannot discriminate the source. *"The answer is unreached until a better instrument exists."*

The six conditions in `01` §0 are a visible guess at what a control arrangement needs, not nature's own list. Declaring them makes the choice visible, not neutral. Naming one of them as *the* answer turns a visible choice into an invisible one.

## 4. The ground and the source problem

Two figure lines govern this section:

> Fig 2 :36: **"An anchor is legitimate when the question is constitutively about it."**
> Fig 2 :37-38: **"Human judgment is the right anchor for is this a good answer for a person, because the question is about people. It is only an instrument for is this code correct, and instruments can be wrong on axes they do not measure."**

This is the correction I owed myself. I first wrote that the human must be the anchor of meaningful control. That is too strong. The human is the legitimate anchor for "is this acceptable to people"; for "does the system implement its specification" the ground is an instrument, and instruments can be wrong on axes they do not measure. A control arrangement needs both readings, kept separate. Conflating them is how a technically correct system becomes an unacceptable one and how a popular system becomes an unsafe one.

> Fig 10 :24: **"the ground does not decay, the channel does"**

The source problem here is not "what does the human intend?" It is "does the reading at the seat still come from the ground?" A high-risk score, an alert count and a human signature are all readings. None of them comments on the source. The remedy is an instrument that discriminates sources, which is what F7 in `01` declares as a gap.

## 5. Answer to the shape question

**What does "shape" mean for this question?** It means the set of necessary conditions on the *control arrangement* that can be stated before the numeric threshold is known:

1. a non-updating ground, kept reachable (Fig 2 :33, Fig 10 :29);
2. an independent supervisor frame, not a mirror (Fig 2 :35, Fig 8 :48);
3. per-slot residual contact at the system's speed and scale (Fig 1 :19, Fig 3 :27);
4. a vocabulary with comparison, subtraction and negation, so a goal and a refusal can be stated (Fig 12 :111-112);
5. a measured round trip when a control method moves across scales (Fig 4 :30);
6. a declared stake and a provenance trail that does not pretend to be a verdict (Fig 10 :59, :65-66).

**What is not licensed?** A number. No figure line closes F7. The honest report is a shape plus a declared gap, not a filled slot.

**Does the field's own answer commit the forbidden error?** No, if read correctly. Tracking and tracing are conditions on an arrangement, not a latency. The error would be to take them as a complete theory and stop, because Fig 5 :67 says *"a frame cannot certify its own completeness"* and the field's own abstract says the detailed theory was missing in 2018 and is still a design requirement rather than a settled measurement.

## 6. Consequences for contact

**Control is a property of the arrangement, not of the person.** Fig 11 :39: *"capability is a property of agent-and-habitat, never of the agent alone."* So the outreach target is a person positioned to check the *arrangement*, not to endorse a theory about human nature. The first contact carries the residual and the refuter.

**Prestige is not action.** *The Recursive Self-Improvement Trap*: *"Prestige can fund an action. It can give an action a budget to be tried. But it cannot settle an action. Only the ground can settle it. The Ground Maintainer must be blind to prestige."* A list of famous names willing to agree with a definition of "meaningful" is the named collapse, not corroboration. The legitimate ask is a budget to try the shape against a ground.

**A clean record is not a correct ruling.** Fig 10 :65-66: *"Provenance shows a ruling was reached legitimately. It never shows it was right: the ground does that, and a wrong ruling stays wrong however clean its record."* This is why `08_expert_verification_packet.md` states provenance but asks for a refutation.

## 7. This audit is a simulation, not a ground reading

Same essay: *"LLMs can quickly simulate an approximate answer/output with the formula found in the architecture post... However, the truly testable output would need to be derived from the real architecture or working the formula yourself."* So `01_figure_grounded_audit.md`, `06_isomers_and_order.md`, `07_the_tether_answer.md` and this file are simulated derivations checked for internal consistency against the figures. They are **not** tests of the claim, and must be labelled as such. They cannot settle anything they report on. No human-subject study, experiment or instrument reading was run for this solution.
