# The Tether's answer on the emergence of locality

**Mode: `specified` on the substantive claims, `general` on the structural shape, not `grounded`.** This is a shape and a route, not a result. Simulated derivation checked for internal consistency against the figures (`05_what_shape_means.md` section 6), not a ground reading.

## 1. The residual it answers

Physics partitions one connected spacetime into regions that can be treated as separate systems. The question asks what licenses that partition. The residual is the gap between a single connected substrate and the observed fact that regions have their own properties and dynamics. Arity: two conditions, connectivity and independence, so a union compound (Fig 9 :57: "a case in two conditions is not a harder case of either"). Scale: the decomposition holds at some levels and fails at others. Symmetry: separateness is a relation, and the figures' independence is evidence overlap, not distance.

## 2. The shape

1. **A region is not a primitive.** Fig 13 :10 gives space the content "near, far, between, contained". Fig 13 :62 gives the removal test: "no spatial symmetry for Noether, and no region to make isolated mean anything". Without the space state there is no region and no isolated thing. So locality is owed to a state in the minimum set, not the other way round.

2. **The smooth continuum is a derivative, not a floor.** Fig 13 :43: "A continuum was the seventh and was removed for failing exactly this". Continuity is a spectrum without gaps, a composition, not a member of the floor. "Spacetime" in the question is already a derived term.

3. **"Separate" is independence, not distance.** Fig 2 :31: "Independence, between two frames, is how little they draw on the same evidence." Fig 2 :31 also: "Two people reading the same report are not two frames; two instruments pointed at the same thing are." A hidden shared source makes two apparently separate frames one pool (the common-cause panel, Fig 2 :18-21). Fig 8 :47: "Independence is one quantity read twice." So distance supplies nearness; non-overlap of evidence supplies separateness. Both are needed, which is why this is a union.

4. **The entangled-to-separate transition is a level-relative membrane crossing.** Fig 4 :21: "The membrane is the boundary between the sizes, and the rule about what may cross it. Not a wall: a filter with a direction." Fig 13 :104: "irreducible is a fact about a level and never about the world". At one level regions entangle and fail the independence test; at another they pass it. Both are readings of different levels, not a contradiction. The loss across the crossing is a round-trip gap R_T that must be measured (Fig 4 :30).

5. **The physical binding is not named by the framework.** Fig 9 :32: "you get a kind of thing, never the answer itself". The figures can say two pools must not overlap; they cannot say which physical operation makes them not overlap. Spacelike separation, commuting algebras, and decoherence are bindings from the physics frame onto held atoms. They carry zero evidentiary weight until provenance is shown (Fig 8 on convergent derivation versus adopted import).

## 3. What it explicitly does not say

- Not that locality is fundamental, and not that spacetime is a substance. The figures give a removal test, not an ontology.
- Not that distance alone explains separateness; a shared common cause defeats separateness at any distance.
- Not that the continuum is the ground of regions; it was removed as a seventh member.
- Not a derivation of quantum field theory, decoherence, or entanglement. No new primitive is minted and no wall is moved (Fig 6 :25: "An import adds no atom to the world.").
- Not `grounded`. No instrument was read in this run, so every finding is `candidate`.
- Not a theory of everything. Fig 7 :6: "the limit: no last room".

## 4. What would settle it: the ground

A source-discriminating instrument, not a further argument. *The Hard Problem is About Source*: a colour instrument "cannot comment on the source"; the spectrometer settled it, not an argument. Here:

- **Discriminator test:** an instrument that distinguishes two regions sharing a source from two regions with no shared source, independent of their spatial separation. That would name the physical realization of Fig 2's independence.
- **Common-cause test:** prepare two regions with a hidden shared source and vary only their separation. If distance alone restores separateness, F3 breaks.
- **Removal test at the ground:** exhibit a coherent account of a region and an isolated system with no space state. That would break Fig 13 :62.
- **Continuum test:** exhibit a genuine seventh primitive required for smooth locality. That would break Fig 13 :43.
- **Standing falsifiers:** derive adjacency from the other five terms; exhibit two fully overlapping evidence pools legitimately treated as separate; measure a direct state-to-state bond; derive a settled region that cannot serve as an atom at second order.

## 5. The honest stop

**Unreached, not unreachable.** The shape is complete: region, independence, level, and an open physical slot. The physical realization is out of reach of these instruments, and "Unreachable is a verdict here, not a silence" (Fig 13 :134). *The Recursive Self-Improvement Trap* states the level-relative case directly: "If you are far apart enough, it looks like you are not connected at all, even if you were." That is a shape, not a mechanism, and it is by the framework's author, so it is not independent corroboration. The lineage is recorded so the stop is checkable (Fig 13 :141: "The composition is white box because the lineage is, and every claim in this figure is checkable the same way."). Indiscernibility is not identity.

## 6. One-paragraph version for outreach

> The Tether does not give a mechanism for locality, and it does not claim a final account. It says that a region is not primitive: without the space state there is "no region to make isolated mean anything". It says that "separate" is not distance but independence, "how little they draw on the same evidence", so two regions sharing a hidden source are one pool however far apart they look. It says the smooth continuum is a derivative that was removed as a seventh member, and that the move from entangled regions to separate ones is a level-relative crossing with a lossy gap that must be measured. It refuses to name the physical operation that makes the pools not overlap, because the framework returns a shape, not a name. What it offers is the shape any candidate answer must have, four falsifiers written in advance, and the instruments that would close it. The physical slot stays open until the ground settles it.
