## 1. A shape is a constraint, not a filler

The canonical README states the limit: the framework returns "what a missing thing would have to look like (a shape, not a name)". Fig 9 :32: "you get a kind of thing, never the answer itself". Fig 13 on the record and the reach: "It cannot say which primitive is missing, because naming it would mean already holding it."

From *The Library of Babel*: "you can cut your search short if the set of books returned is small and descriptive enough that it provides a general shape of the answer that satisfies your question."

For this question a **shape** is the form any account of separateness must have before we can name the physical operation that realizes it. The shape is not a mechanism. It says: a region presupposes the space state; two regions count as separate only when their evidence pools do not overlap; the smooth continuum is a derivative, not a floor. That constrains a candidate answer without filling it. It does not say "commuting algebras" or "decoherence".

## 2. How the shape is obtained

- *Library of Babel*: use the vocabulary already closest to the target and recurse. "With each new term gained, you are able to narrow the space by definition and deepen your vocabulary."
- Fig 6 :30: "The description travels. Fails by being sharpened into terms only we can read. Cast it in effect terms." Fig 9 :30-31: "your words are borrowed too, so describe the effect rather than the cause".
- The effect terms here are deliberately not "region", "separate", or "locality", because those are already the contested words. They are: a pool of evidence, a shared source, a removal test, and a level at which a difference can be spent.
- *Library of Babel* on stopping: "the finish line will also be the starting place". The paradox is that by the time you can name the right search term you have "already composed the answer through the process of locating it."

## 3. What licenses a name

Only two things, both outside the shape itself:

1. **The ground settles it.** Formula step 5: "SETTLE the ground settles it. Verified only by what pays. A gate is not the ground." The canonical README: the framework "stops at the limit of what the available instruments can distinguish, and records the lineage so the stop is honest."
2. **A provenanced import from a frame whose closure differs** (Fig 6 :23, Fig 8), with a source gate and a shadow test.

For this question, naming the physical realization of separateness is a binding from the physics frame (spacelike separation, commuting algebras, decoherence) onto atoms the framework already holds. It is not the invention of a missing primitive, so the strict "shape, not a name" rule does not forbid it. But it must be recorded as a binding with provenance, not presented as a derivation. That is the same correction the exemplar made in its own section 5b, and it applies here too.

## 4. The essay that speaks directly to this question

*The Recursive Self-Improvement Trap* contains the closest thing in the eight essays to a statement of the locality problem:

> "On every level of a system, data is lost. Context changes based on the environment. That loss is enough to create the distinction of difference. If you are far apart enough, it looks like you are not connected at all, even if you were."

Read against the figures, this is the level-relative separateness of F4 stated in prose: a lossy transform between scales (Fig 4 :27-30) makes two things look unconnected, and "irreducible is a fact about a level and never about the world" (Fig 13 :104). It is corroboration, not authority, and it is by the framework's author, so it shares the evidence pool and carries little independent weight. Its value is that it makes the shape concrete: separateness can be a fact about a level without being a fact about the world.

## 5. The source-discrimination point

*The Hard Problem is About Source* gives the mechanism for why a binding is not a derivation:

> "If several paths create the same behavior, then behavior is a projection with lossy information"
>
> "the instrument detects green, and says that it's green. However, the instrument is speaking only about what it measured, it cannot comment on the source."
>
> "It was proven wrong not by argument, but by the arrival of a better instrument."

Applied here: observing that two regions behave as separate does not reveal the source of their separateness. Several underlying arrangements (no coupling, decoherence, a shared source that has not yet acted, a coarse instrument) can give the same reading of "separate". What is owed is an instrument that discriminates sources, not a name. This is why F5 marks the physical binding as unproven and why the honest stop in `07_the_tether_answer.md` asks for a discriminator rather than another argument.

## 6. Prestige is not action, and this is a simulation

From *The Recursive Self-Improvement Trap*: "Prestige can fund an action. It can give an action a budget to be tried. But it cannot settle an action. Only the ground can settle it. The Ground Maintainer must be blind to prestige."

A campaign that gathers famous names to agree that locality is emergent is the named collapse, not corroboration (Fig 2 :35, Fig 7 :35). The legitimate ask is a budget to be tried and a ground to settle it.

Same essay: "the output is only as good as the validator. The agent, or the tool, or the frame, or the theory, cannot validate itself." And: "the truly testable output would need to be derived from the real architecture or working the formula yourself." So this entire solution is a **simulated derivation checked for internal consistency against the figures, not a ground reading**. It cannot settle the question it reports on. Prestige or agreement is not an instrument.
