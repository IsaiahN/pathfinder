# Expert verification packet: the Tether on the emergence of locality

**What this is:** a claim in a form a physicist or philosopher of physics can check. Mode, derivation, and refuters are stated before the conclusion, so a reader knows what to look for and what would settle it.

**Mode: `specified` on the substantive claims, `general` on the structural shape, not `grounded`.** No measurement at any scale is claimed. This is a shape and a route, not a result. It is a simulated derivation checked for internal consistency against the figures, not a ground reading, so it cannot settle the question it reports on.

## 1. The claim, in one paragraph

A region is not primitive: the minimum set's space state supplies "near, far, between, contained", and its removal test says "no spatial symmetry for Noether, and no region to make isolated mean anything". The smooth continuum is a derivative that was removed as a seventh member. "Separate" is not distance but independence, defined as "how little they draw on the same evidence", so two regions sharing a hidden source are one evidence pool however far apart they look. The move from entangled regions to separate ones is a level-relative membrane crossing with a lossy round-trip gap that must be measured. The framework does not name the physical operation that makes two pools not overlap; spacelike separation, commuting algebras, and decoherence are bindings from the physics frame, recorded as such and carrying no evidentiary weight until provenance is shown. What is offered is a shape, four falsifiers written in advance, and the instruments that would close the physical slot.

## 2. Three checkable sub-claims

### C1: framework-internal; checkable by reading Figure 13

- **Statement:** a region presupposes the space state; adjacency is not derivable from the other five terms; the continuum is not a primitive.
- **Source, verbatim:** Fig 13 :10 "near, far, between, contained". Fig 13 :62 "no spatial symmetry for Noether, and no region to make isolated mean anything". Fig 13 :43 "A continuum was the seventh and was removed for failing exactly this". Fig 13 :74 "Everything else is a composition across the two kinds, never within one."
- **Refuter:** derive adjacency or a region from the other five terms without space; or exhibit a genuine seventh primitive required for smooth locality; or measure a direct state-to-state bond that cannot be refused.

### C2: framework-internal plus physics-facing; checkable by a physicist against entanglement and decoherence

- **Statement:** "separate" means non-overlapping evidence, not distance. A shared source defeats separateness at any separation. The entangled-to-separate transition is a level-relative crossing, not a contradiction.
- **Source, verbatim:** Fig 2 :31 "Independence, between two frames, is how little they draw on the same evidence." Fig 8 :47 "Independence is one quantity read twice." Fig 4 :21 "The membrane is the boundary between the sizes, and the rule about what may cross it. Not a wall: a filter with a direction." Fig 13 :104 "irreducible is a fact about a level and never about the world".
- **What to check:** does Bell's common-cause condition play the role the figure assigns to a shared evidence pool? Does decoherence deliver effective dynamical independence of branches at a level without making separateness fundamental? Does the source-discriminating instrument asked for in section 5 exist in principle?
- **Refuter:** exhibit two frames whose evidence pools fully overlap being legitimately treated as separate for this residual; or measure a case where mere distance restores separateness with a shared common cause still present; or show that a settled region cannot serve as an atom at second order.

### C3: framework-internal boundary of the claim

- **Statement:** the physical realization is not named by the framework. Spacelike separation, commutativity, and decoherence are imports, not figure results, and carry zero evidentiary weight until provenance is shown.
- **Source, verbatim:** Fig 9 :32 "you get a kind of thing, never the answer itself". Fig 6 :25 "An import adds no atom to the world." Fig 2 :40 "disqualifying for a verdict: agreement about the world is worthless when the agreeing frames share an evidence pool".
- **Refuter:** produce a verbatim figure line that names spacelike separation, a commutator, or decoherence as the framework's independence term; or show a provenanced import with a shadow test that moves the wall. Until one is produced, this sub-claim stands.

## 3. What is explicitly not claimed

- Not a theory of everything, and no final room. Fig 7 :6 "the limit: no last room".
- Not that locality is fundamental, not that spacetime is a substance, and not that distance alone explains separateness.
- Not that the continuum is the ground of regions. It was removed in Fig 13 :43.
- Not a derivation of quantum field theory, decoherence, or entanglement. No new primitive is minted.
- Not `grounded`. No instrument was read in this run.
- Not a claim that the framework names the physical operation realizing independence. It returns a shape, not a name.

## 5. What would settle it

A source-discriminating instrument, not a further argument. From *The Hard Problem is About Source*, an instrument that reads green "cannot comment on the source"; the spectrometer settled it. Here:

1. An instrument that distinguishes two regions sharing a source from two with no shared source, independent of spatial separation. This would name the physical realization.
2. A common-cause experiment that varies only separation while holding the shared source fixed. If distance alone restores separateness, C2 breaks.
3. A coherent account of a region and an isolated system with no space state. This would break C1.
4. A seventh primitive required for smooth locality. This would also break C1.

## 6. What an expert can check in twenty minutes

1. Read Fig 13 :10, :43, :62, :74 and Fig 2 :31, five short passages. Confirm the quotes are verbatim.
2. Check C2 against their own field: does a shared source defeat the separateness of two regions, and is decoherence a level-relative rather than fundamental separateness?
3. Name one instrument that would distinguish a shared source from no shared source at fixed separation, or say why none is possible.
4. Send the refutation. A failure is more useful than an endorsement.
