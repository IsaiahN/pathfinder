## VERDICT (from the figures)

The shape survives; the primitive readings do not. A region presupposes the space state, and without space there is no region and no isolated thing. "Separate" is independence, how little two pools draw on the same evidence, so a shared source defeats separateness at any distance. The continuum is a derivative that was removed as a seventh member, and the move from entangled to separate regions is a level-relative crossing with a lossy round-trip gap. The physical realization is an open slot: spacelike separation, commuting algebras, and decoherence are bindings with provenance, not figure results. This is a `specified`, ungrounded, candidate answer. The strongest single figure-grounded point is the removal test at Fig 13 :62.

- **cost if wrong:** bounded and locatable. The packet states mode, four refuters and provenance before the conclusion, so an error is findable rather than invisible. The real cost is spending the independence of outside readers by sending a recording instead of a generator. That cost is avoidable by leading with the shape claim and its refuters.
- **undoable:** yes. The physical bindings are withheld rather than asserted, so nothing has to be withdrawn to change them. If a refuter fires, the shape claim is retracted without disturbing the outreach rule; if an instrument names the slot, a provenanced binding can be added without a new primitive.
- **reversed by:** a ground reading, not agreement. A source-discriminating instrument at fixed separation would name the slot; a common-cause experiment in which distance alone restores separateness would break Domain 2; a coherent account of a region with no space state, or a seventh primitive required for smooth locality, would break Domain 1; a settled region that cannot serve as an atom at second order would break the level claim. Until one of these fires, the verdict stands as `candidate`. Prestige or agreement is not such an instrument.
