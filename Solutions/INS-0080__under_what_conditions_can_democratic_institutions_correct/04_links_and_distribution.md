## Distribution: offer, do not push

- Fig 4 :22 "Only methods cross up. Only head starts come down." Sending the framework before a recipient has described a residual sends a recording, not a generator. Lead with the four-condition shape and the seven refuters; offer links after.
- Fig 10 :42 "A verifier that can reconstruct the claim cannot verify it." A recipient who has absorbed the framework before giving a verdict has joined the frame being checked.
- Fig 9 :27-28 "describe it before you go looking, or any frame you pick will seem to fit". Ask what they would expect to see if the shape claim were true before showing them the essays.
- Seat Map :163 "High correlation is disqualifying for a verdict and efficient for a convention." A send selected for people already likely to agree produces a convention, not corroboration.
- Fig 8 :48-49: two frames that overlap almost completely are one frame wearing two names. A single reading pool cited five times is one witness.

**Recommended order in a reply:** (1) the Q-failure / Q-trust split and the four-condition shape; (2) the pre-registered refuter; (3) ask what they would count as a failure; (4) only then the links, with the note that the figures, not the summary, are the authority; (5) the formula as a reusable method, each user writing a fresh refuter and stating a mode.

## The two-stage offer

Stage 1: validate the output against the ground. Stage 2, opt-in only: read the theory that produced it. Stage 3: reuse the formula on another problem.

- Stage 1 before stage 2 is Fig 10 :52 "Bring a characterised residual, never the source." The recipient checks the shape without holding the framework interior.
- Stage 2 is opt-in by construction. Seat 4 must not hold the interiors; once a recipient has absorbed the theory, later agreement is never pooled as corroboration (Fig 2 :40).
- Stage 3 sends a method, which is permitted (Fig 4 :22). It still needs a ground per problem, because a frame cannot check the direction of its own question.
- Hard precondition: stages 1 to 3 only work if the stage-1 shape survives the seven refuters. If one fires, retract rather than reframe.
