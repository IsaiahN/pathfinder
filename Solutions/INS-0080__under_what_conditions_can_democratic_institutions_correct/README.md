## The residual in effect terms

An institution predicts and acts (Γ(b,a)), observes an outcome (o'), and carries a residual R = |Γ(b,a) - o'| per slot. The question asks when that live slot residual gets corrected (a term refused, the reach changed) before the channel to the ground fails. Public trust is treated here as a reading of that channel, not as the ground itself, a distinction that the audit re-census complicates and then scopes rather than hides.
