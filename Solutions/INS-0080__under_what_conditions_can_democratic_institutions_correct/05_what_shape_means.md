## 1. A shape is a constraint, not a filler

The canonical rule: the framework returns "what a missing thing would have to look like (a shape, not a name)." Fig 9 :32 "you get a kind of thing, never the answer itself." The Symbols table says R "is always a slice: what cannot be perceived yet is residual and simply cannot be read."

From *The Library of Babel*: "you can cut your search short if the set of books returned is small and descriptive enough that it provides a general shape of the answer that satisfies your question."

So for INS-0080 a **shape** is the narrowed form of the conditions under which correction can register, statable before it can be named as a specific reform. It constrains: there must be an unmovable anchor, a live per-slot channel, outside frames with bought independence, and an operator of refusal. It does not fill: it does not say which agency, which law, or which voting system.

A shape here is **four conditions in a union**, not a checklist of four independent sufficient causes. That distinction comes from Fig 9 :55, not from the essays, and is recorded in `01` §3 F8.

## 2. How the shape is obtained

- *Library of Babel*: use the vocabulary already closest to the thing, look for recurring new terms that overlap or correlate with it, recurse. "With each new term gained, you are able to narrow the space by definition and deepen your vocabulary." And: "you need something to tether and anchor you to reality," where the anchor is the question itself.
- Fig 6 :30 "The description travels. Fails by being sharpened into terms only we can read. Cast it in effect terms." Fig 9 :30-31: "your words are borrowed too, so describe the effect rather than the cause."
- *Decoding Tacit Knowledge*: "When contact isn't possible, the best you can send is a plain description of what you'd expect to see, because anyone can check that against the world." This is why the outreach lead is a refuter, not a theory.
- *Library of Babel* on stopping: "the finish line will also be the starting place," and the paradox that by then you have "composed the answer through the process of locating it."

Applied here: the effect terms are "the reading the frames range against," "the live slot," "the outside frame," "the refused term." The borrowed words "trust," "legitimacy," "accountability" were treated as names to be re-described as effects before use. This is the discipline Fig 9 :27-28 requires.

## 3. What licenses a name

Two things, both outside the shape:

1. **The ground settles it.** `THE_FORMULA.md` step 5: "the ground settles it. Verified only by what pays. A gate is not the ground." The canonical README: the framework "stops at the limit of what the available instruments can distinguish, and records the lineage so the stop is honest."
2. **A provenanced import from a frame whose closure differs**, with a source gate and shadow test (Fig 6 :23, Fig 8 :50-52). Federalist 51's "auxiliary precautions," Janis's devil's advocate, and judicial independence findings are such bindings from political science. They are recorded with author, venue and year in `10_source_ledger.csv`, and none is asserted as a figure derivation.

*The Hard Problem is About Source* names the failure this avoids. A spectrometer reading green "cannot comment on the source," and "If several paths create the same behavior, then behavior is a projection with lossy information." "The answer is unreached until a better instrument exists." A national trust reading cannot say which institutional arrangement produced it. Naming reform X from reading Y is the metamerism error; what is owed is a discriminating instrument.

## 4. Why public trust cannot be the answer variable without a split

This question has a special difficulty that the exemplar's QM/GR case did not: one of its own terms is a reading of the channel.

- For **Q-failure** ("did the institution fix the outcome?"), the ground is the outcome. An approval number is an instrument, and instruments "can be wrong on axes they do not measure" (Fig 2 :37).
- For **Q-trust** ("is this answer good for these people?"), Fig 2 :36 says "An anchor is legitimate when the question is constitutively about it," and the essay's fuller line is "Human judgment is the right anchor for is this a good answer for a person, because the question is about people."

*Decoding Tacit Knowledge* gives the mechanism for both: "Community... institutions that only check against themselves drift together and don't notice (Fig 2 :34)." And: "Agreement is the whole point when you're settling what words mean. It's worthless for settling what's true when both sides learned from the same sources (Fig 2 :40-41)." That is exactly the two-question split. Shared meaning is the product for Q-trust; shared meaning is the corruption for Q-failure.

## 5. Correction to my own §3 reading of "shape, not a name"

The first pass of this file treated "shape, not a name" as a blanket ban on naming any institutional mechanism. **That was overstated and is recorded here rather than silently fixed.**

The canonical phrase is "what a **missing** thing would have to look like (a shape, not a name)." Its subject is a primitive not held in Γ. For INS-0080, the four conditions are not missing primitives to be invented; they are structural constraints already present in the figures. What is genuinely missing is the discriminating instrument that would tell which specific reform fires which condition. That remains a `gap`: Fig 9 :32 "you get a kind of thing, never the answer itself."

So the rule is narrower than I wrote: the framework will not name the missing instrument, but it can constrain the shape of the arrangement that would make one possible. Naming Federalist-style checks and balances or a devil's advocate is a provenanced binding from an outside closure, not a forbidden invention and not a claim that the framework named it.

The metamerism point of §3 is **not** retracted. Calling a trust reading evidence that the failure was fixed remains a source named from a projection.

## 6. Two consequences for outreach

**Prestige is not action.** *The Recursive Self-Improvement Trap*: "Prestige must never be allowed to validate an action directly... Prestige can fund an action. It can give an action a budget to be tried. But it cannot settle an action. Only the ground can settle it." A campaign that gathers respected names to agree that trust is restored is the named collapse. The legitimate ask is a ground reading of the outcome.

**My own audit is a simulation, not a ground reading.** Same essay: "LLMs can quickly simulate an approximate answer/output... However, the truly testable output would need to be derived from the real architecture or working the formula yourself." So `01` and `09` are simulated derivations, checked for internal consistency against the figures. They cannot settle anything they report on, and no verdict in this folder is `grounded`.
