# The Tether's answer on democratic self-correction before trust collapse

**Mode: `specified` - not `grounded`.** This is a shape and a route, not a result. Simulated derivation checked against the figures (`05_what_shape_means.md` §6), not a ground reading.

## 1. The residual it answers

The question contains two loops joined into a union compound (Fig 9 :54-57):

- **Q-failure:** "did the institution correct the outcome it failed to deliver?" Anchor: the outcome.
- **Q-trust:** "is this answer good for these people, and do they still regard the body as answerable to them?" Anchor: their judgment, legitimate because the question is constitutively about them (Fig 2 :36).

Using Q-trust's anchor for Q-failure is the failure mode: the anchor becomes another frame, "everything drifts together, and the drift is coherent, which is why nobody notices" (Fig 2 :34).

## 2. The shape: four conditions, per domain, never averaged

1. **An anchor the institution cannot move.** Fig 2 :29 "An anchor is whatever settles the question and does not change its answer because of what any frame thinks. A measurement, an experiment, a counter, a market, a patient outcome." For Q-failure the outcome is the anchor; an approval number is an instrument and can be wrong on axes it does not measure (Fig 2 :37).

2. **A live, per-slot channel.** Fig 10 :24 "the ground does not decay, the channel does"; Fig 10 :26 "saturated metrics, pooled readings". Fig 1 :22 "Aggregating across slots is how a live signal disappears into an average." Trust is the reading of this channel, not a cause to be maximised. Correction fails silently when the channel dies: Fig 10 :27-28 "the output is not a verdict, it is you have lost touch."

3. **Outside frames with bought independence.** Fig 2 :35 "Alignment is triangulation, not negotiation. A mirror cannot correct you." Fig 10 :42 "A verifier that can reconstruct the claim cannot verify it." The institution is one frame among others; the others range against the anchor separately and are not authored by it. Fig 2 :42 "Buy independence in proportion to the cost of being wrong." Independence decays with use and is refreshed by rotation or separate experience, not by more calibration of the same seat. Fig 8 :48-49: two frames that overlap almost completely are "one frame wearing two names."

4. **A vocabulary that can refuse and renew its reach.** Fig 5 :53 "refuted: a term we hold is wrong; a term is owed that says so"; Operators :58 "a refutation is a negation, which is why a vocabulary without it can only accumulate." Fig 6 :23 "No frame escapes its own closure. The bound moves only on contact with a frame whose closure differs." Adding programs to a system that cannot refuse increases the library without changing the reach.

**Union, not checklist.** Fig 9 :55 "A bundled problem is usually a union of overlapping conditions, not a list of separate ones." A case in two conditions is categorically different (Fig 9 :57). The dependency order is channel → per-slot vocabulary → outside frames → refusal/reach (Fig 3 :19, :27). Effort above the break cannot rescue a link below it.

## 3. What it explicitly does not say

- Not that "self-correction" is literal. A frame cannot check itself; correction is a property of an arrangement (Fig 2 :35, Fig 10 :42).
- Not that public trust is the ground for the failure question. It is a legitimate anchor only for Q-trust (Fig 2 :36).
- Not that more civil society, more deliberation, or more transparency is sufficient. Correlated frames add vocabulary, not evidence (Fig 7 :12-13), and Berman's Weimar case is recorded as a real conflict in `01` §3 F7.
- Not a named reform, agency, law or electoral system. Fig 9 :32 "you get a kind of thing, never the answer itself." Federalist 51's checks, Janis's devil's advocate and V-Dem's indices are provenanced bindings from outside closures, not figure derivations.
- Not `grounded`. No measurement in this folder settles `left(R,φ)` for any institution.

## 4. What would settle it: the ground, not agreement

A discriminating instrument, not another argument. *The Hard Problem is About Source*: an instrument that reads green "cannot comment on the source," and "The answer is unreached until a better instrument exists." Here the corresponding readings are:

- A per-slot outcome reading that survives averaging, showing whether the live residual moved.
- A channel reading that is not saturated or pooled: a metric that still discriminates after being used as a target (Goodhart's law, Strathern 1997).
- A test of independence: can the checking frame produce a refusal the institution could not produce, from an evidence pool the institution did not author?
- A test of refusal: did any held term lose its standing to be composed over, with the reach falling because something was added (Fig 6 :42), rather than only a new program appearing?
- A union test: after one lever fires, is the residue categorically different, and can it now be described (Fig 9 :59)?
- The seven pre-registered falsifiers in `01` §4, any one of which withdraws the shape.

## 5. The honest stop

**Unreached, not unreachable.** The specific reform that fires all four conditions is a `gap` after re-census. Fig 9 :32 gives a kind of thing, never the answer. Fig 13 :134 "Unreachable is a verdict here, not a silence," and Fig 8 :39 "So a search that finds nothing is never a proof of absence." The lineage is recorded so the stop is checkable. "Indiscernibility is not identity."

What is claimed is `specified`: the shape any candidate answer must have, the falsifiers written in advance, and the instruments that would close it. What is not claimed is a verdict on any real institution, since none was measured here.

## 6. One-paragraph version for outreach

> The Tether does not say that a democracy can literally check itself, and it does not name a reform. It says the question splits into two loops: did the failure get fixed (anchor, the outcome) and is the answer good for these people (anchor, their judgment). Using one anchor for both is the collapse. Correction before trust collapse requires four conditions in a union: an anchor the institution cannot move, a live per-slot channel rather than a national average, outside frames that range against the anchor separately with independence bought in proportion to the cost of being wrong, and a vocabulary able to refuse a held false term and renew its reach from a differing closure. Approval numbers and democracy indices are filters for where to look, never witnesses for the verdict. The framework hands back that shape, seven refuters written in advance, and the instruments that would close it; the specific reform stays open until a ground settles it.
