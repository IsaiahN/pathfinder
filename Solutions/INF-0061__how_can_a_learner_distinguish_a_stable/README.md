## Restating the question in effect terms

A learner holds a prediction for each object slot and observes an outcome. The residual is the gap between them, per slot. "Stable pattern" and "coincidence" are not two readings of one observation. They are the outcomes of two different tests: a term-management test (does this candidate regularity pay for itself and settle against a ground that does not update?) and a regime test (does this contact persist while the environment changes, or did the channel simply carry one occasion?). One option leads to a composable term with a written refuter; the other leads to a fitted one-occasion recording that must be refused or re-fit. An answer would look like a separator with the tests, the anchor for each, and the failure conditions written before the run. That shape is bound as an ordinary term in `01` and `07`, so it can accrue a residual rather than hide as a preference.

## What is deliberately not here

- No personal emails of named individuals.
- No ranking of people by how likely they are to agree.
- No bulk-send plan.

Three reasons, in the figures' own words:

1. **A room does not upgrade a label.** `Fig 7 :35` calls a room's agreement *"variation, not a verdict"*, and `Fig 2 :35` says *"Alignment is triangulation, not negotiation. A mirror cannot correct you."*
2. **Selecting for receptiveness manufactures the disqualifying correlation.** `Fig 2 :39` says *"Correlation has valence"* and `Fig 2 :40` makes agreement *"disqualifying for a verdict"* when the frames share an evidence pool.
3. **Asking for trust before checking spends the reader's independence.** `Fig 10 :42`: *"A verifier that can reconstruct the claim cannot verify it."*

Legally, scraping personal emails for unsolicited bulk outreach breaches GDPR and CAN-SPAM style rules in many jurisdictions. Public institutional and opt-in channels avoid that failure as well as the structural one.

## Honest standing of this folder

Every substantive file is a **simulated derivation checked for internal consistency against the figures**, not a ground reading. *The Recursive Self-Improvement Trap* (carried quote, see `05` section 7) says LLM output is a simulacrum and *"the truly testable output would need to be derived from the real architecture or working the formula yourself."* This folder cannot settle the question it reports on. It returns a shape, a separator, and refuters; the numerical threshold that would answer the literal question is declared as a `gap`.
