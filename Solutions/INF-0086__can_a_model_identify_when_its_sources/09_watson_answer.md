## QUESTION

Decide, in effect terms, whether a model can register that a source is misleading rather than only reproducing its pattern. Option A: reproduction plus scale is sufficient, so reliability is a property of the model alone. Option B: reproduction and refusal are different quantities with different anchors, so detection needs a non-updating anchor, a differing closure, a bound per-source residual and a negation operator. What each leads to: A spends the budget on agent-side improvement and reads frame-internal scores as verdicts; B returns a checkable shape with five refuters and declares the empirical question a gap until a source-discriminating instrument settles it. The question is a union compound of overlapping conditions, not a partition (Fig 9 :54-57).

## DOMAIN CENSUS

- **R (the residual).** The gap between what the source pattern predicts and what a fixed anchor returns, indexed per source slot. Arity: three places, source, model frame, anchor. Symmetry: misleading-ness is directed and ordered, a comparison plus a negation, not the symmetric bond of two things holding at once. Scale: intra-closure at reproduction, cross-frame at detection. Fig 1 :19, *"The residual is indexed by object slot, not aggregated."*
- **D1, model internal.** Actors: library Gamma, atoms, bonds, belief b, weighting w. Currency: pattern reproduction and composition inside closure(Gamma). Anchor: none inside the frame; the ground enters as the only metric from outside. Question type: boundary of the vocabulary. Verdict: NO from the pattern alone.
- **D2, model plus source as one pool.** Actors: the source and the model drawing on the same corpus. Currency: agreement and mutual update. Anchor: none, because they share an evidence pool. Question type: a collapse panel, not a verdict domain. Verdict: NO, and the agreement is the pool talking.
- **D3, model plus independent frame.** Actors: model, anchor, second source or instrument, maintainer seat. Currency: residuals that register against the anchor, imported atoms with provenance. Anchor: the immovable ground, which does not update. Question type: triangulation. Verdict: YES in principle, as a shape.
- **D4, the empirical question.** Actors: researchers, benchmarks, deployed systems. Currency: measured accuracy on withheld-label tests. Anchor: ground truth labels, not agreement. Question type: a separate question with its own ground. Verdict: `gap`, unreached in this run.
- **Order by dependency.** Register source and anchor in different slots (Fig 3 :4-5), have terms for claim, source, ground and refusal (Fig 3 :6-7), state the objective as a persistent non-trending residual (Fig 3 :9-10), route by import with residual described before frame chosen (Fig 8 :53), then name, verify and carry the refusal (Fig 3 :14-15). The chain plausibly breaks between vocabulary and objective, the middle link with no instrument (Fig 3 :24-27).

## VERDICT (from the figures)

Reproduction and detection are different quantities, so the question's either/or is a union compound that must be unbundled. From the source pattern alone (D1) and from a shared pool (D2), the answer is no: *"A mirror cannot correct you."* With an anchor that does not update, a frame whose closure differs, a bound per-source residual and a negation operator (D3), the answer is yes in principle, as a shape that has never been measured here. Whether any deployed model implements it (D4) is a `gap`, reported as unreachable rather than silence. Verdicts are kept per domain and never averaged. Mode `specified` on D1 to D3, `general` on the structural shape, `gap` on D4, not `grounded`.

- **cost if wrong:** bounded and locatable. The shape claim carries five written refuters and a declared gap, so an error is findable rather than invisible. The real cost is treating a frame-internal score as a reliability verdict, which Fig 1 :27 forbids, and spending independence by sending a recording instead of a method (Fig 4 :25). Fig 2 :42 prices the remedy: *"Buy independence in proportion to the cost of being wrong."* That cost is avoidable by leading with the shape and its refuter.
- **undoable:** yes. The empirical gap can be closed by a source-discriminating instrument with withheld labels, injected misleading sources, refusal scoring, per-source residuals, a non-updating anchor and a low-overlap checking frame. The shape claim can be withdrawn if any of the five refuters fires. No primitive has to be invented and no record is deleted to do either.
- **reversed by:** a ground reading, not agreement. A properly instrumented experiment finding above-chance per-source refusal at a non-updating anchor would convert D4 from `gap` to `grounded` without refuting the shape. A model detecting misleading sources with its sole source as its only evidence, single-frame detection, an in-closure derivation, an accumulation-only correction, or scale-only contact change would each break one of the five refuters and withdraw the shape claim. Until one of these fires, the verdict stands as `candidate`.
