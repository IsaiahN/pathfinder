## Independent domain census

I redid the census from the residual line, holding only the question and the effect restatement, before reading the primary's conclusions. WATSON order: R by arity, symmetry and scale; one line per domain with actors, currency, anchor; partition or union; order by dependency.

- **R (arity / symmetry / scale).** The residual is the gap between a source's predicted pattern and an observation at a fixed anchor, indexed per source. Arity: three places, source, model frame, anchor; a one-place property cannot express "misleading". Symmetry: directed and ordered, a comparison plus a negation, not the symmetric bond of two things holding at once (Operators table :12 and :48). Scale: intra-closure for reproduction, cross-frame for detection. This matches Fig 1 :19's per-slot rule.
- **D1, model internal.** Actors: library, atoms, bonds, belief, weighting. Currency: reproduction and in-closure composition. Anchor: none inside; supplied from outside. Question type: boundary of the vocabulary. Verdict: NO from the pattern alone.
- **D2, shared pool.** Actors: source and model drawing on one corpus. Currency: agreement and mutual update. Anchor: none, since they share evidence. Question type: a collapse panel. Verdict: NO, agreement is the pool talking.
- **D3, independent frame.** Actors: model, anchor, differing source or instrument, maintainer seat. Currency: residuals at the anchor, imported atoms with provenance. Anchor: the immovable ground. Question type: triangulation. Verdict: YES in principle as a shape.
- **D4, empirical question.** Actors: researchers and deployed systems. Currency: measured accuracy. Anchor: withheld ground truth. Question type: separate question with its own ground. Verdict: `gap`, unreached.
- **Order by dependency.** Register source and anchor in distinct slots, bind claim/source/ground/refusal, state a residual objective, route by import with residual described first, carry the refusal. Break at the vocabulary/objective link (Fig 3 :24-27).

My census matches the primary's four domains. Where the primary derived D3 from Fig 6 :23 and Fig 8 :48, I derived it from Fig 2 :29-35 (anchor and mirror) and Fig 3 :25-27 (the uninstrumented middle link). Different starting figures, same boundaries: low overlap between routes inside the framework, so the agreement is weak but not circular at the figure level. It is still one evidence pool.

## Round trip gap R_T

The Symbols table defines R_T as *"The gap between what was sent up and what came back down"*, `gap(x, (T_E ∘ T_A)(x))`, measuring what the coarser description cannot hold. Fig 4 :30 requires it to be measured rather than assumed. I ran the round trip on the condensation this folder actually uses.

1. **Sent up (x):** the verbatim figure set, chiefly Fig 1 :24-27 (disqualified internal quantities), Fig 2 :29-35 (anchor and mirror), Fig 5 :61-66 (preference not term, make it ordinary), Fig 6 :23 (import) and Fig 8 :39/:48 (gap and independence).
2. **Abstraction (T_A):** the shorthand carried in `07` and `08`: reproduction is not detection; detection needs an anchor, a differing closure, a bound residual and ¬; the empirical claim is a gap.
3. **Concretisation (T_E):** a concrete experimental design: withhold labels, inject misleading sources, score refusal, keep per-source residuals, use a non-updating anchor and a low-overlap checking frame.

**R_T is nonzero.** Four things the shorthand holds do not survive the trip back down:

- the **disqualification of internal scores**, which a benchmark accuracy can quietly reinstate as a verdict (Fig 1 :27, Fig 9 :60);
- the **leakage and memorisation conditions**, which a naive implementation of the instrument would miss, so the anchor would not actually be independent (Glockner et al., Debate-to-Detect);
- the **refusal scoring requirement**, which collapses back into answer accuracy unless deliberately preserved (Azaria and Mitchell);
- the **per-source indexing**, which a single aggregate truthfulness rate destroys (Fig 1 :19, :22).

One thing survives cleanly: reproduction and refusal are different quantities, and a non-updating anchor plus a differing closure is the condition under which detection becomes possible. That is the residue worth sending. R_T was **not** measured numerically, because no ground reading exists; it is recorded qualitatively as the enumerated set of dropped constraints. Treating "the formula is the condensed version of the metalaws" as identity would set R_T to zero and would be the dead-reckoning error Fig 4 :30 names. The honest form is a lossy condensation with a measured gap, which is what `09_watson_answer.md` states.
