## What the wider literature bears on the question

1. **Reproduction dominates truthfulness.** TruthfulQA shows that false answers are learned from the training distribution and that larger models were *"generally the least truthful"* on misconception questions. This is the world-side analogue of F1 and F8: reproducing the pattern and judging the pattern are different jobs.
2. **A source can poison the instrument.** Model collapse shows that models trained on recursively generated content *"mis-perceive reality"* and that provenance of crawled content is the open practical problem. This is the common-cause collapse of Fig 2, observed at corpus scale.
3. **Internal detection exists but is not the same as refusal.** Azaria and Mitchell show that a trained classifier can read truthfulness from hidden activations, while the model's own zero-shot verdict fails, and that retrospectively knowing a statement is false *"does not entail that the LLM will not generate it in the first place."* This is F6 and F7 in world form: the discrimination can be extractable without being a bound, refusing term in the generating frame.
4. **More sources can make detection worse.** RAGuard shows misleading retrievals drive all tested RAG systems below their zero-shot baselines, and more documents raise the chance of harmful content. This is F4: independence, not count, is the variable (Fig 8 :48).
5. **Leaked evidence fakes detection.** Glockner et al. show all surveyed fact-checking datasets fail the sufficient-and-unleaked criteria and models rely on leaked evidence. This is why the stage-1 instrument must withhold ground labels, and why a high benchmark score is a `filter for the budget`, not a witness (Fig 9 :60).
6. **Low-overlap, independent checking can genuinely help.** Debate-to-Detect reports generalization to news published after a model's pretraining cutoff with *"absence of verbatim overlaps"*, and human source-monitoring work shows source confusions arise because *"activated information is incomplete or ambiguous and the evaluative processes responsible for attributing information to sources are imperfect."* The first is a weak corroboration of Fig 8 :45; the second is a genuinely low-overlap human-cognition witness for the difficulty of source discrimination.
7. **No world source conflicts with the figure verdict.** The conflict rule was not triggered. The AI/NLP sources partly share one evidence pool (factuality benchmarks and LLM evaluation), so their agreement counts as one witness with several names (Fig 2 :40). The human source-monitoring source is the only genuinely low-overlap corroborator.

## Distribution: offer, do not push

Figure-grounded reasons:

- **Fig 10 :52** — *"Bring a characterised residual, never the source."* A recipient who receives the full framework before being asked for a verdict has been handed the interior and has joined the frame being checked. Lead with the shape and its refuter.
- **Fig 10 :42** — *"A verifier that can reconstruct the claim cannot verify it."* This is the reason the first contact does not contain the derivation.
- **Fig 9 :27-28** — *"describe it before you go looking, or any frame you pick will seem to fit."* Ask what they would expect to see if the shape claim were true before offering any source.
- **Fig 2 :40** — *"disqualifying for a verdict: agreement about the world is worthless when the agreeing frames share an evidence pool."* Do not select recipients for likely agreement and do not pool later agreement as corroboration.
- **Fig 4 :22-25** — *"Only methods cross up. Only head starts come down."* What may be offered at stage 3 is the method, not a recording of one successful evaluation.

**Recommended order in a reply:** (1) the shape claim and its five refuters; (2) ask what they would count as a failure, and record their verdict; (3) only then the figure extracts, with the note that the figures, not this summary, are the authority; (4) only if they ask, the formula as a method to reuse with their own refuter and mode.

## The two-stage offer

- **Stage 1:** send the shape claim and the pre-registered refuter. Ask what would refute it. Record the answer before any theory is shown. This is opt-in by construction and keeps the recipient outside the interior (Fig 10 :52, Fig 2 :45).
- **Stage 2:** if they are curious, offer the three local figure extracts. Their later agreement is never pooled as corroboration (Fig 2 :40).
- **Stage 3:** offer the formula as a generator. Each user writes their own refuter and states their own mode, because a loop *"cannot check the direction of its own question"* (Formula, "Direction").

**Hard precondition:** stages 1 to 3 only work if the stage-1 shape survives. The empirical question about real models is explicitly a `gap` in this folder and must stay marked as such. Do not present the world literature as proof that models fail; present it as the reason an instrument is owed.
