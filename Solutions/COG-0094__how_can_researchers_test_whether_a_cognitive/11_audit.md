## Independent domain census

I redid the census from the residual line before deciding whether the primary's verdicts were right, using the WATSON order: R by arity, symmetry and scale, then one line per domain with actors, currency and anchor, partition or union, and order by dependency.

- **R (arity / symmetry / scale).** The residual is the gap between observing that a cognitive pattern varies and attributing it. Arity: four named candidates plus a compound, so a **union** (Fig 9 :57: *"a case in two conditions is not a harder case of either"*). Symmetry: attribution is ordered, so a production or sequence bond governs, not a symmetric `+`. Scale: person to population, so a lossy transform is present. This matches the primary.
- **Domain 1, measurement.** Actors: participants, tasks, operationalisations. Currency: responses and invariance checks. Anchor: performance, because the question is constitutively about people; the test itself is an instrument. Question type: channel maintenance, not attribution.
- **Domain 2, attribution.** Actors: the three habitat conditions and interventions. Currency: causal credit and per-slot residual. Anchor: an intervention that moves one condition and holds another. Question type: ordered bond, union compound.
- **Domain 3, verification.** Actors: research teams. Currency: agreement and residual. Anchor: the ground, never agreement. Independence in this simulated run is effectively zero because all seats read the same files.
- **Domain 4, compound and habitat.** Actors: intersections, cascades, substituted lab habitats. Currency: interaction terms. Anchor: an intervention that separates the intersections. Question type: union, enumerated habitat, substitution not removal.

Order by dependency: perceive the pattern, build effect-term vocabulary, state the objective, then plan the design, then carry the method (Fig 3). The vocabulary and objective link is the one without an instrument.

## Round trip gap R_T

The Symbols table defines R_T as the gap between what was sent up and what came back down, `gap(x, (T_E ∘ T_A)(x))`, measuring what the coarser description cannot hold. Fig 4 :30 requires it to be measured rather than assumed. I ran the round trip on the condensation the folder actually uses.

1. **Sent up (x):** the verbatim figure set, chiefly Fig 9 :54-58 (union), Fig 2 :29-38 (anchor and instrument), Fig 1 :19 and :22 (per-slot residual), Fig 11 :43 and :73 (habitat enumeration, habitat not ground).
2. **Abstraction (T_A):** the shorthand carried in `07`: unbundle the union, anchor in an intervention, buy independence, keep the residual per person, enumerate the habitat, measure the cross-scale gap.
3. **Concretisation (T_E):** a concrete multi-site cross-cultural design with invariance testing, mixed methods and a factorial or natural-experiment component.

**R_T is nonzero.** Three things the shorthand holds do not survive the trip back down without an explicit record:

- which bond the attribution uses (production or sequence rather than a symmetric `+`); this is settled in `06` Q1 but has to be restated in any concrete design;
- the distinction between performance as ground and the task as instrument; a design that treats a scale score as the ground has already collapsed the membrane;
- the substitution of a laboratory habitat for the natural habitat, which carries silently unless it is recorded (Fig 11 :70).

Conversely, one thing survives cleanly: a union must be unbundled before testing, and the residual is per person. That is the residue worth sending. R_T was **not** measured numerically, because no ground reading exists; it is recorded qualitatively as the enumerated set of dropped constraints. Treating "the formula is the condensed version of the figures" as identity would set R_T to zero and would be the dead-reckoning error Fig 4 :30 names. The honest form is a lossy condensation with a recorded gap, which is what `09_watson_answer.md` states.
