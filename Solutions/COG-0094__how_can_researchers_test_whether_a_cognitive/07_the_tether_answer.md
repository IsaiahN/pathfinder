# The Tether's answer on culture, ecology, institutions and their interaction

**Mode: `specified` on the design constraints, `general` on the structural shape. Not `grounded`.** This is a shape and a route, not a result. Simulated derivation checked against the figures (`05_what_shape_means.md` §6), not a ground reading.

## 1. The residual it answers

A cognitive pattern varies across populations; which contact produces it is unattributed. The four options are not four hypotheses. Culture, ecology, and institutions are overlapping habitat conditions, so the question is a **union compound** with its own loop (Fig 9 :57: *"a case in two conditions is not a harder case of either"*). Each attribution leads to a different intervention, so an error is not merely classificatory: it changes what is done to people.

## 2. The shape

1. **Unbundle before testing.** Fig 9 :54: *"And when the split lands on a union rather than a partition, unbundle before you search again."* Fig 9 :55-56: *"A single lever clears the region where only one condition holds and leaves the intersections standing, because the second condition still binds."* Test the single-condition regions and then the intersections. "Interaction" is a compound, not a fourth cause.

2. **Anchor the test in an intervention, not in a frame's reading.** Fig 2 :29: *"An anchor is whatever settles the question and does not change its answer because of what any frame thinks."* Because the question is constitutively about people's performance, that performance is the legitimate ground (Fig 2 :36), but a particular test is only an instrument and can be wrong on axes it does not measure (Fig 2 :37-38). Measurement invariance is a check on the channel: Fig 10 :24, *"the ground does not decay, the channel does."* An invariant-but-wrong instrument is still an instrument.

3. **Buy independence.** Fig 2 :31: *"Independence, between two frames, is how little they draw on the same evidence."* Culture, ecology, and institutions historically co-vary, so three readings from the same literature and the same sample are one evidence pool (Fig 2 :40). Use settings where the candidates move separately and instruments with low overlap. Fig 2 :42: *"Buy independence in proportion to the cost of being wrong."*

4. **Describe the pattern in effect terms first.** Fig 9 :27-28: *"describe it before you go looking, or any frame you pick will seem to fit."* Bind the sought-for design shape as an ordinary term and attach the pre-registered refuter from `01_figure_grounded_audit.md` §3 before the run.

5. **Keep the residual per person.** Fig 1 :19: *"The residual is indexed by object slot, not aggregated."* Fig 1 :22: *"Aggregating across slots is how a live signal disappears into an average."* A culture mean is a derivative, not the observation.

6. **Enumerate the habitat and treat person plus habitat as the unit.** Fig 11 :43: *"The habitat is enumerated, never composed."* Fig 11 :39: *"And capability is a property of agent-and-habitat, never of the agent alone."* A laboratory task is a substituted habitat (Fig 11 :70: *"A test harness is a substituted habitat. Which is why a synthetic solve proves wiring and never capability."*), so record the substitution and re-range the result against the original habitat.

7. **Measure the cross-scale gap.** Fig 4 :30: *"So take the round trip and measure the gap. Without it you are navigating by dead reckoning: confident about a position nobody checked."* `R_T = gap(x, (T_E ∘ T_A)(x))` is recorded qualitatively in `11_audit.md`; no numeric value is claimed.

8. **The framework does not name the cause.** Fig 9 :32: *"you get a kind of thing, never the answer itself."* A cause name is licensed only by a ground reading (an intervention that pays) or by a provenanced import with a shadow test. Until then the slots stay open.

## 3. What it explicitly does not say

- Not that culture, ecology, or institutions is the cause of any particular pattern. The slots stay open.
- Not a specific experimental protocol. The figures constrain a design; they do not write it. The factorial design and the natural experiment are world implementations, corroboration only.
- Not that measurement invariance establishes a culture. It establishes a channel condition at best.
- Not that a population mean is a person. It is a lossy derivative.
- Not `grounded`. No intervention, dataset, or participant was used in this derivation.

## 4. What would settle it — the ground

A source-discriminating intervention, not further agreement. The six refuters in `01` §3 are written to fire:

1. One lever clears the whole attribution, including all intersections (breaks the union constraint).
2. One instrument gives the same attribution under every framing and population, with no invariance failure (breaks the instrument-versus-ground constraint).
3. The three candidates have zero shared variance, so one reading is already three (breaks the independence constraint).
4. A population mean carries all per-person structure (breaks the per-slot constraint).
5. An attribution excluding all habitat conditions still picks the intervention that works (breaks the habitat constraint).
6. The individual to population transform is measured with `R_T = 0` (breaks the round-trip constraint).

Any one of these fires against this derivation's claim, not against the world's answer. The ground decides the question; agreement among researchers does not.

## 5. The honest stop

**Unreached, not unreachable.** The habitat is enumerated by reading the world, not composed from a list, and what cannot yet be perceived or measured is the residual (Fig 11 :48: *"You do not invent the list. You read it off the world, and what you cannot perceive or measure yet is the residual."*). This derivation stops at the limit of the available instruments and records the lineage so the stop is checkable. Fig 8 :39: *"So a search that finds nothing is never a proof of absence."* Indiscernibility is not identity. A second seat that shares these files is a mirror, not a witness.

## 6. One-paragraph version for outreach

> The Tether does not name culture, ecology, or institutions as the cause of a cognitive pattern, and it does not supply a ready-made experiment. It says that the four options are a union of overlapping conditions, so a single lever leaves the intersections standing and the bundle must be split before it is tested; that the test must range against an intervention that does not move when a theory pushes on it, with performance as the ground and the test as a fallible instrument; that independence must be bought because the three candidates share an evidence pool; that the residual belongs to the person and not to a culture mean; and that the habitat must be enumerated and carried into the unit of analysis. It offers the shape any valid test must have, six refuters written in advance, and the instruments that would close it. The slots stay open until the ground settles them. Everything here is `specified` and simulated, not grounded.
