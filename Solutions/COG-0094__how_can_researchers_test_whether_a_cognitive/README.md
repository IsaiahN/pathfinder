## What this is

A derive-first package. The question asks for a causal attribution among four overlapping candidates. The figures are read before any answer is chosen. The residual is stated in effect terms, the domains are censused, one loop is run per domain, and the verdicts are kept separate. The figures return the **shape of a valid test**, not the name of the cause, and not a single experimental recipe.

**Boundary and honesty.** This is a simulated derivation checked for internal consistency against the figures, not a ground reading. From *The Recursive Self-Improvement Trap* (opened 2026-11-22): *"the output is only as good as the validator. ## What is deliberately not here

- No personal emails of named individuals.
- No ranking of people by how likely they are to agree.
- No bulk-send plan.

Three reasons, in the figures' own words:

1. **Agreement is not an anchor.** *"Alignment is triangulation, not negotiation. A mirror cannot correct you."* (Fig 2 :35). A list selected for likely agreement is a mirror.
2. **Correlation disqualifies a verdict.** The Seat Map, line 163: *"High correlation is disqualifying for a verdict and efficient for a convention."* Cross-cultural researchers who share a method, a sample, and a literature are one evidence pool.
3. **Asking for trust before checking spends independence.** *"A verifier that can reconstruct the claim cannot verify it."* (Fig 10 :42).

Legally: public institutional and opt-in channels avoid the GDPR and CAN-SPAM failure of scraping personal addresses for unsolicited outreach.
