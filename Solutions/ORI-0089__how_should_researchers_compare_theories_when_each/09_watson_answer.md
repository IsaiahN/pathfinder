**Mode: `specified` on the substantive claims, `general` on the structural shape, not `grounded`.**

## QUESTION

Decide, in effect terms, what the Tether says when each partial theory explains a different subset of stages from prebiotic chemistry to an evolving system. Option A: rank the theories as rival answers to one question by one aggregate score of explanatory scope. Option B: unbundle the shared stage chain, index the residual per stage, range each theory against the ground separately, and compose only where closures genuinely differ and a residual has been described. What each leads to: A produces averaged scores that hide a live signal on one stage, a false partition of a union, and agreement mistaken for evidence; B produces a per-stage coverage map, a named unreachable where no theory covers a stage, and a checkable import path. The answer is bound as an ordinary term, a per-stage ground-ranged comparison with an open slot, so it can accrue a residual and be refused.

## DOMAIN CENSUS

R by arity, symmetry and scale, then one line per domain with actors / currency / anchor, partition or union, and order by dependency. No nameable anchor means the item is not a domain yet.

- **R (the residual).** The stage-by-stage disagreement. Arity: N stage slots plus a comparison at each slot, not one scalar. Symmetry: the stage links are ordered bonds, `→` sequence and `⇒` production, and the inter-theory comparison is `⋛`, an ordered threshold, not a symmetric merge. Scale: first order within a stage, second order across the chain where a settled stage becomes an atom for the next. This is a union compound, not one hard question, because each theory keeps working on a different subset.
- **Domain 1, framework-internal composition.** Actors: stage slots, the six-term set, bonds, negation. Currency: residual per slot `R_s`, description cost and leftover. Anchor: the figures as metalaws, plus the ground for any measurement. Question type: partition boundary of what composes inside a closure.
- **Domain 2, theory frames.** Actors: RNA-first, metabolism-first, lipid-first and coevolutionary accounts, with their models, experiments and instruments. Currency: predictions and the residual each leaves on a stage. Anchor: the experiment, the ground for this domain. Question type: union, since each frame covers a different subset of stages.
- **Domain 3, comparison and verification frame.** Actors: expert readers, reviewers, rival closures. Currency: agreement and residual. Anchor: the ground, never agreement. In this simulated run all seats draw on one evidence pool (the same figures, essays and folder files), so their agreement is one pool talking, not corroboration. Its independence is zero.
- **Order by dependency.** Stages are ordered by dependency, not importance. Describe the residual before choosing a frame; refuse before mint; settle each first-order stage before composing the second-order comparison.
- **Not a domain yet.** A single true theory of life's origin has no nameable anchor inside this derivation, so it is not admitted. Prestige and citation counts are absorbed into Domain 3 as contact-cost proxies and carry zero verdict weight.

Strain test applied before concluding: the acceptance bargain prices one new term by description length, while the bond figure prices an arrangement of already-held terms in two non-additive currencies (bits and actions). These look contradictory only if both are read as one score. Re-census: they govern different objects, so no strain remains.

## VERDICT (from the figures)

The shape survives; naming a winner is `gap`. The figures license splitting the stage disagreement into a union of conditions before search, indexing the residual per stage in a dependency-ordered table, ranging each theory against an experiment separately, treating the inter-theory comparison as an ordered threshold with scope and leftover both counted, and leaving uncovered stages open with an effect-term description of the missing instrument. A combination of theories earns a surplus only where closures differ and a residual has been described; a blend from one shared evidence pool is a mute union. The figures do not name a correct origin-of-life theory, and no measurement was taken, so every substantive claim is `specified` and the final slot is `candidate` rather than `grounded`.

- **cost if wrong:** bounded and locatable. The packet states its mode, writes its refuters before the conclusion and records the lineage, so an error is findable rather than invisible. The real cost is spending the independence of outside readers by sending a recording instead of a generator, which the figures price as buying independence in proportion to the cost of being wrong. That cost is avoidable by leading with the shape claim and its refuter, and by recording the verdict before showing the architecture.
- **undoable:** yes. The shape can be repaired or withdrawn by editing the coverage table and its falsifiers; no primitive has to be retracted and no term has to be deleted. A sent claim can be superseded by a corrected packet, and an open slot can be filled when an instrument settles it without erasing the record that it was once unreachable.
- **reversed by:** a ground reading, not agreement. A source-discriminating experiment showing one theory predicts a stage the others cannot would fill the open slot; a single mechanism that shrinks the stage disagreements without splitting the chain, an aggregate ranking unchanged when the worst stage is removed, permutable stages, a symmetric comparison, or a mute union that nonetheless extends reach would break the shape; a figure re-census that admits naming a primitive without import or ground would clear the shape-not-a-name limit. Until one of these fires, the verdict stands as `candidate`.
