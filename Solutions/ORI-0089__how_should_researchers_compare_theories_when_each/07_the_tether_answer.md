# The Tether's answer: comparing theories with partial stage coverage

**Mode: `specified` on the substantive claims, `general` on the structural shape, not `grounded`.** This is a shape and a route, not a result. It is a simulated derivation checked for internal consistency against the figures (`05_what_shape_means.md` §6), not a ground reading.

## 1. The residual it answers

Origin-of-life accounts disagree in a specific pattern: each explains some stages from prebiotic chemistry to an evolving system and not others. The decision is whether to rank them as rival answers to one question or to treat the disagreement as a union of stage conditions. Fig 9 :4-5 "Disagreements that do not shrink with effort, where each rule keeps working well on a different subset, are not one hard question but several well-formed ones. Split it rather than search." This is a union compound with its own loop and its own ground (Fig 9 :57), not one harder question.

## 2. The shape

1. **Split before you compare.** A bundled problem is a union of overlapping conditions, not a list of separate ones (Fig 9 :55). Fig 9 :54 "And when the split lands on a union rather than a partition, unbundle before you search again." The comparison artifact is a stage-by-theory table, not a league table of theories.

2. **Index the residual per stage, never aggregate.** Fig 1 :19 "The residual is indexed by object slot, not aggregated." Fig 1 :22 "Aggregating across slots is how a live signal disappears into an average." A theory can score well overall and leave one live stage unexplained; that cell is where the comparison must look. Fig 1 :13 "a global R near zero with one live slot residual is a legal state".

3. **Order the stages by dependency.** Fig 3 :20 "The chain is ordered by dependency, not by importance." `→` gives precedence and `⇒` gives production (Fig 12 :72, Operators :37). A stage with no instrument is not a refuted stage; it is a link nobody has looked at (Fig 3 :27).

4. **Range each theory against the ground separately.** Fig 2 :29 "An anchor is whatever settles the question and does not change its answer because of what any frame thinks. A measurement, an experiment, a counter, a market, a patient outcome." Fig 2 :35 "Alignment is triangulation, not negotiation. A mirror cannot correct you." Agreement among proponents is a convention, not a verdict (Fig 2 :40). A ground listed as one of the parties has already become a frame (Fig 2 :47).

5. **A blind union adds nothing.** Fig 8 :14 "their union adds nothing". A surplus exists only where closures differ and a residual has been described; without the residual, nothing was aimed and the union is mute (Fig 8 :19-21). Fig 8 :53 "Residual first, frame second." So a genuine combination must import from a frame with a different closure, pass a source gate and a shadow test (Fig 6 :23), and be debited against that frame's independence.

6. **Compare as a threshold, not as a blend.** Fig 12 :87 "a threshold relation between two quantities" names `⋛`, ordered and asymmetric. Both scope and leftover count in the bargain (Fig 5 :43). The phrase *both are partly right* is a conjunction (`+`), which carries no ordering information. The arrangement, not the ingredient list, is the substance (Fig 12 :94).

7. **Leave the winner slot open.** Fig 9 :32 "you get a kind of thing, never the answer itself". Fig 13 :134 "Unreachable is a verdict here, not a silence". A stage no theory covers gets an effect-term description of the missing instrument, not a forced name. Which named theory is correct is `gap` from the figures; it needs a ground reading (Fig 13 :135).

8. **Keep prestige and agreement out of the verdict.** Fig 7 :13 "agreement compounds, evidence does not". Fig 11 :51 "No standing list of good actors survives a change of residual." The public contact routes are there to get the shape refuted, not endorsed. Fig 2 :42 "Buy independence in proportion to the cost of being wrong."

## 3. What it explicitly does not say

- Not that RNA-first, metabolism-first or any other named theory is the answer. The figures return a shape, not a name.
- Not that the theories should simply be combined. A blend from one shared evidence pool is a mute union (Fig 8 :14).
- Not that the stage order is settled. It is ordered by dependency as a structural claim; the dependencies themselves need experiments.
- Not that expert consensus closes a stage. A room is not an anchor (Fig 2 :47).
- Not a theory of everything. There is no last room (Fig 7 :6), and the origin-of-life chain has no last route (Fig 13 :90).
- Not `grounded`. No measurement settles any cell of the table.

## 4. What would settle it: the ground

A source-discriminating instrument, not a further argument. From *The Hard Problem is About Source*: *If several paths create the same behavior, then behavior is a projection with lossy information*, and *The answer is unreached until a better instrument exists.* Here:

- **Stage coverage:** an experiment that makes one theory predict a stage that the others cannot, and that is independently reproduced under plausible conditions.
- **Order:** an observed intermediate state that can only be produced if stage B precedes stage A, distinguishing `→` from `⇒`.
- **Threshold:** a measured quantity on which one theory leaves a smaller residual than the other at the same stage, with the leftover and the cost both reported.
- **Open slot:** an instrument whose effect-term description matches the missing stage, moving it from `unreachable` to `candidate`.
- **Standing falsifiers from `01` §4:** one mechanism that shrinks the disagreements without splitting the chain; an aggregate ranking unchanged when the worst stage is removed; permutable stages; a symmetric comparison; a mute union that nonetheless extends reach.

## 5. The honest stop

**Unreached, not unreachable.** The shape is figure-derived and checkable. The cells are not filled by this derivation. Fig 8 :39 "So a search that finds nothing is never a proof of absence." Fig 13 :134 "Unreachable is a verdict here, not a silence". The lineage is recorded so the stop is checkable: which stage is uncovered, what an instrument would have to detect, and what would reverse the verdict. Indiscernibility is not identity. This package cannot settle which theory of life's origin is correct.

## 6. One-paragraph version for outreach

> The Tether does not pick a winning origin-of-life theory. It says the disagreement is a union of stage conditions, so split it before searching; index the residual per stage and never average it into one score; order the stages by dependency rather than importance; and range each theory against the experiment separately. Combining theories is not automatically progress: a blend from one shared evidence pool adds nothing, and a real surplus needs a described residual and a frame whose closure differs. The comparison at each stage is an ordered threshold, not a symmetric *both partly right*, and the slot no theory covers stays open with an effect-term description of what an instrument would have to detect. What is offered is the shape any checkable comparison must have, the falsifiers written in advance, and the route to the ground. Which theory is right is left to the experiments.
