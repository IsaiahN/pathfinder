## 1. A shape is a constraint, not a filler

The canonical usage rule states the limit: the framework returns "what a missing thing would have to look like (a shape, not a name)". Fig 9 :32 "you get a kind of thing, never the answer itself". Fig 13 :133-135: "A system with a closure can answer that before it searches, and what it returns is a shape rather than a name." and "It cannot say which primitive is missing, because naming it would mean already holding it."

From *The Library of Babel*: "you can cut your search short if the set of books returned is small and descriptive enough that it provides a general shape of the answer that satisfies your question."

So a **shape** is the narrowed form of an answer you can state before you can name it. It constrains; it does not fill.

For this question the shape is not "RNA-first wins" or "metabolism-first wins." It is:

- the disagreement is a **union** of stage conditions and must be split before search (Fig 9 :4-5);
- the residual is **indexed per stage**, never aggregated (Fig 1 :19);
- stages are **ordered by dependency**, not importance (Fig 3 :20);
- each theory is **ranged against the ground separately** (Fig 2 :29, :35);
- the inter-theory comparison is an ordered **threshold** `⋛`, not a blend (Fig 12 :87);
- where no theory covers a stage, the slot stays open and is recorded as `unreachable` (Fig 13 :134).

That is a shape with six constraints. A named winner would be a name.

## 2. How the shape is obtained

- *The Library of Babel* describes proximity bootstrapping: use the vocabulary you already have that is closest to the thing, look for recurring new terms that overlap or correlate with it, recurse. The same essay gives the decisive move for this question: "Perhaps your problem was a bundle or conflation of several problems masquerading as one? You will need to search all of those problems individually to find their answers and then combine them in a satisfactory way."
- Fig 6 :30 "outward: does another frame already hold it? The description travels." Fig 9 :30-31 "your words are borrowed too, so describe the effect rather than the cause."
- *The Library of Babel*: "The books that you are returning in your searches need to be tested or ranged against the problem you want answered in order to steer the direction of the next search." This is Fig 2's ranging discipline in essay form.
- Fig 9 :45 "Describing the residual is the expensive part. Leaving, looking up and recognising are one step each, and cheap only in that order."

Applied here: describe the uncovered stage in effect terms, for example "a compartments-and-energy coupling that yields heritable variation before template replication", then go looking for the frame that predicts that shape. Do not pick RNA-first or metabolism-first first and then interpret every stage through it (Fig 9 :27-28).

## 3. What licenses a name

Only two things, both outside the shape itself:

1. **The ground settles it.** Formula step 5: "the ground settles it. Verified only by what pays. A gate is not the ground." Here that means an experiment, not a review.
2. **A provenanced import from a frame whose closure differs** (Fig 6 :23, Fig 8 :51), with a source gate and a shadow test: "Convergent derivation and adopted import look identical in the contents."

*The Hard Problem is About Source* gives the reason a single observation cannot name the source: "If several paths create the same behavior, then behavior is a projection with lossy information". Several prebiotic pathways can yield the same ancient chemical signature. The reading does not determine the pathway. And: "The answer is unreached until a better instrument exists. It was proven wrong not by argument, but by the arrival of a better instrument." That is why the honest cell in the coverage table names the instrument, not the winner.

## 4. A correction to my own first pass

My first pass read "shape, not a name" as a blanket ban on naming any component of an answer. That was an overstatement, and I record the correction rather than erasing it.

The canonical phrase is "what a **missing** thing would have to look like (a shape, not a name)." It governs a primitive not held in the library. The chemical actors in an origin-of-life comparison (monomers, energy sources, mineral surfaces, compartments, replication) are not missing from the chemistry library; they are already held there. Naming them is a binding from the chemistry frame onto existing vocabulary, not the invention of a new primitive. What remains forbidden is deriving the winner from the figures alone, or presenting a binding as if the framework had named it.

Both readings are preserved: the blanket ban is retracted; the ban on deriving a name without import or ground stands.

## 5. Why a single winner is the specific forbidden error

*The Recursive Self-Improvement Trap*: "the output is only as good as the validator. The agent, or the tool, or the frame, or the theory, cannot validate itself. An external validator, or ground, is always needed". A framework that declared one origin-of-life theory the winner would be a frame certifying its own conclusion, which Fig 8 :37 forbids: "A frame cannot certify its own limit."

And a uniqueness claim over all theories would require a census of all possible chemistries, which has not been run. Fig 9 :44: "Only a sealed room can be searched to the end, and no frame is a sealed room."

## 6. My own audit is a simulation, not a ground reading

From *The Recursive Self-Improvement Trap*: "LLMs can quickly simulate an approximate answer/output with the formula found in the architecture post... However, the truly testable output would need to be derived from the real architecture or working the formula yourself." The essay calls LLM output "simulacra."

So `01_figure_grounded_audit.md`, `06_isomers_and_order.md`, `07_the_tether_answer.md` and `09_watson_answer.md` are simulated derivations checked for internal consistency against the figures. They are **not** tests of any origin-of-life claim and must be labelled as such. They cannot settle which theory is right.

## 7. Two consequences for this outreach

**Prestige is not action.** From *The Recursive Self-Improvement Trap*: "Prestige can fund an action. It can give an action a budget to be tried. But it cannot settle an action. Only the ground can settle it. The Ground Maintainer must be blind to prestige." A distinguished advocate gives the coverage table a budget to be checked; the table is not thereby true. This is why `03_public_contact_points.csv` asks for refutation rather than endorsement and carries no receptiveness rank.

**The stopping rule is honest.** The shape stops where the instruments stop. Fig 13 :134 "Unreachable is a verdict here, not a silence"; Fig 8 :39 "So a search that finds nothing is never a proof of absence." The record of which stage is uncovered, what shape an instrument would have to detect, and what would reverse the verdict is the deliverable.
