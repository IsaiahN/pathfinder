# Outreach draft: check the comparison first, then decide about the framework

**Use:** one-to-one, or as a cover letter attached to a submission through the public venues in `03_public_contact_points.csv`. **Not** for bulk send.

**Hard precondition:** do not send until the coverage table described in `01_figure_grounded_audit.md` §5 exists and its cells carry predictions, residuals, and named instruments. The output must survive validation or the two-stage offer collapses on contact.

---

**Subject:** One checkable output on comparing partial theories

> Hi [name],
>
> I have one specific output I would like you to check against what you already know. I am not asking you to read the framework first, and I am not asking you to agree with it. If the output does not survive, there is nothing else to look at.
>
> **The question in effect terms.** RNA-first, metabolism-first, lipid-first and coevolutionary accounts of life's origin each explain some stages and not others. The usual comparison ranks them by one score of explanatory scope. The figure-grounded reading says that is the wrong object. The disagreement is a union of stage conditions, not one hard question: "Disagreements that do not shrink with effort, where each rule keeps working well on a different subset, are not one hard question but several well-formed ones. Split it rather than search." (Fig 9 :4-5). So the comparison should be a table with one row per stage, ordered by dependency rather than importance, and one column per theory; each cell states the prediction, the residual that remains, and the instrument that would settle it.
>
> **The output (`specified`, not `grounded`).** Four claims:
> 1. Split before search. A bundled problem is "a union of overlapping conditions, not a list of separate ones" (Fig 9 :55), and "a case in two conditions is not a harder case of either" (Fig 9 :57).
> 2. Never aggregate. "The residual is indexed by object slot, not aggregated" (Fig 1 :19); "Aggregating across slots is how a live signal disappears into an average" (Fig 1 :22). A theory can score well overall and leave one live stage unexplained. That live cell is the one that matters.
> 3. Compare as a threshold, not a blend. The comparison operator is "a threshold relation between two quantities" (Fig 12 :87), ordered rather than symmetric. Saying two theories are "both partly right" is a conjunction and says nothing about which is larger on the axis in question.
> 4. Leave the winner slot open. A union of accounts that cite the same evidence adds nothing: "their union adds nothing" (Fig 8 :14). A surplus appears only "where the closures differ" and a residual has been described; otherwise "nothing was aimed; the union is mute" (Fig 8 :19-21).
>
> **What I am not claiming.** I am not claiming that any named theory is the answer. The framework returns a shape, not a name, and a stage no theory covers is recorded as `unreachable`, which is "a verdict here, not a silence" (Fig 13 :134). I am not claiming that combining the theories is progress; a blend from one shared evidence pool is not a new result. I am not claiming that a panel of experts settles this. "Alignment is triangulation, not negotiation. A mirror cannot correct you" (Fig 2 :35), and "agreement about the world is worthless when the agreeing frames share an evidence pool" (Fig 2 :40). The ground is the experiment, not the room.
>
> **What would refute the shape.** I wrote these down before sending this:
> 1. A single mechanism is measured that shrinks the stage disagreements without splitting the chain, and no partition of the stages improves prediction.
> 2. A comparison's ranking is unchanged after its worst-covered stage is removed.
> 3. The stages can be permuted with no change to any prediction, or the comparison is shown to be symmetric rather than a threshold on a shared axis.
> 4. A combined model built from one shared evidence pool, with no described residual, extends reach and makes a new prediction.
>
> If any holds, I will retract or repair the claim. A one-line "this fails because..." is more useful to me than agreement. If you would rather not engage, no reply is needed.
>
> **If it survives, and only then:** the sources are below. The figures are the authority, not my summary of them.
> - Full figure text with line numbers (local framework extract): `FRAMEWORK/reference/03-FIGURES_TEXT.md`, cite as `Fig N :line`.
> - Canonical usage rule: `FRAMEWORK/reference/FIGURES_README_canonical.md`.
> - The eight-step loop and the mode rule: `FRAMEWORK/figures/THE_FORMULA.md`.
> - Origins-of-life background I actually read: Robertson and Joyce, "The Origins of the RNA World", 2012, https://pmc.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/articles/PMC3331698/ ; Cech, "The RNA Worlds in Context", 2012, https://pmc.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/articles/PMC3385955/
>
> **If you want to use the machinery:** the formula is in the figures. Run it on a problem you know well and can check against a ground. Write down what would refute your result *before* you run it, and state the mode of what you get. The author's own warning is that outputs "could never be verified if the solutions presented were not contrived to fit the formula", and that LLM output is "simulacra" rather than a ground reading. A reported failure is more useful to me than a reported success.
>
> - [your name]

## Why this version is ordered this way

| the move | figure-grounded reason |
|---|---|
| Output and refuter first, theory second | Fig 10 :52 "Bring a characterised residual, never the source." Fig 2 :45 "Frames range against the ground. They do not negotiate with it." |
| Do not ask for agreement | Fig 2 :35 "Alignment is triangulation, not negotiation. A mirror cannot correct you." |
| Pre-registered refuter | Seat Map: "a falsifier written before the run is a design instrument, not only an honesty device." |
| Mode stated on the claim | `THE_FORMULA.md`: "Mode. general · specified · grounded. State which." |
| Per-stage cells, no aggregate score | Fig 1 :19 and Fig 1 :22. |
| Dependency order for stages | Fig 3 :20 "The chain is ordered by dependency, not by importance." |
| Threshold named, blend refused | Fig 12 :87 and Fig 8 :14. |
| Winner slot left open | Fig 13 :134 "Unreachable is a verdict here, not a silence"; Fig 9 :32 "you get a kind of thing, never the answer itself". |
| No request for prestige or trust | *The Recursive Self-Improvement Trap*: "Prestige can fund an action. It can give an action a budget to be tried. But it cannot settle an action. Only the ground can settle it." |
| Offer the formula as a method to reuse | Fig 4 :22 "Only methods cross up. Only head starts come down." |
| Ask users to write their own refuter | Formula "Direction": a frame cannot check the direction of its own question; commit the prediction before the run. |

**Stage 2 is opt-in by construction.** Once a recipient has absorbed the framework, their agreement stops being evidence because they hold its interiors (Fig 10 :42: "A verifier that can reconstruct the claim cannot verify it"). Their verdict on stage 1 must be recorded before any theory is shown, and later agreement is never pooled as corroboration.
