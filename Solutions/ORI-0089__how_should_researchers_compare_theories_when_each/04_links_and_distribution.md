## Two things in the literature that bear directly on this question

1. **Different stages have different evidence.** Robertson and Joyce distinguish strong evidence that an RNA world existed from much more tenuous arguments about whether life began with RNA. Cech records continuing disagreement about whether RNA was the first autonomous replicator. That is the phenomenon Fig 9 :4-5 names: each rule keeps working well on a different subset. The world sources corroborate the shape; they do not decide which theory is right.
2. **A multi-model union can beat a single model, but agreement is not evidence.** Ensemble forecasting improves on one model when the models differ, which corroborates Fig 8 :45. The same page warns that ensemble spread can be too small and make a forecaster overconfident. That is Fig 2 :35 in a different field: agreement inside a set is not contact with the ground. The analogy is a corroboration of shape only, not a derivation.

## Distribution: offer, do not push

- **Fig 4 :22** "Only methods cross up. Only head starts come down." Sending the whole framework before anyone has described a residual sends a recording, not a generator. Lead with the coverage table and its refuter.
- **Fig 10 :42** "A verifier that can reconstruct the claim cannot verify it." A recipient who has absorbed the architecture before giving a verdict has joined the frame being checked.
- **Fig 9 :27-28** "describe it before you go looking, or any frame you pick will seem to fit." Ask what they would expect to see in each stage cell before offering any source.
- **Fig 2 :40** "agreement about the world is worthless when the agreeing frames share an evidence pool." Do not pool later agreement as corroboration.

Recommended order in a reply: (1) the shape claim and its refuter; (2) ask what they would count as a failure in a specific stage cell; (3) only then the links, with the note that the figures, not the summary, are the authority.

## The two-stage offer

**Stage 1, before any theory.** Send the effect-term residual, the four-part shape, and the four falsifiers in `02_outreach_email.md`. Record the recipient's verdict before showing any framework material.

**Stage 2, opt-in only.** If they are curious, offer the figure text, the canonical README and the formula. Their later agreement is never counted as a witness.

**Stage 3, method only.** Offer the formula as a generator for other problems, with the instruction that each user writes down what would refute their result first and states the mode of what they get.
