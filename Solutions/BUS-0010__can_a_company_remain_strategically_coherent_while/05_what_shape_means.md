## 1. A shape is a constraint you can state before you can name it

The canonical statement in `FIGURES_README_canonical.md` is that the framework returns *"what a missing thing would have to look like (a shape, not a name)"*, then stops where instruments can no longer distinguish and records the lineage. Fig 9 :32: *"you get a kind of thing, never the answer itself."* Fig 13 :134: *"Unreachable is a verdict here, not a silence,"* and :135: *"It cannot say which primitive is missing, because naming it would mean already holding it."*

From the essay *The Library of Babel*, read directly:

> *"This means that you can cut your search short if the set of books returned is small and descriptive enough that it provides a general shape of the answer that satisfies your question."*

So for BUS-0010 a shape is: **the narrowed form of an answer about coherence and change that can be stated before any particular priority, mission, culture or capability is named.** It constrains. It does not fill.

- Shape: *a fixed anchor and kernel with arrangements selected against the residual; coherence and priority change are different quantities.*
- Name, withheld: *this company should keep its core and change its sales model, or hold its mission and rotate its product portfolio.*

The distinction matters here because the strategy and organization literature is almost entirely in the naming business. Ambidexterity names exploration and exploitation; dynamic capabilities name sensing, seizing and reconfiguring; the coherence literature names alignment and consistency. Those are bindings from the management frame onto atoms, not outputs of the loop.

**A correction carried forward from BUS-0001 and confirmed here.** The phrase "shape, not a name" governs a **missing** primitive, one not held in Γ. If a capability or commitment is already in the company's library, naming it is a binding from the management frame, not the invention of a new primitive. What remains forbidden is claiming the binding moves the wall (Fig 6 :21, *"MINT composes inside the closure and can never add an atom"*), or presenting the binding as the framework's own answer. Both the corrected and the withdrawn formulations are kept so the correction is visible rather than silent.

## 2. How the shape was obtained

The essay gives the bootstrapping procedure:

> *"If you don’t know the right terms to search for, you will have to use the existing vocabulary you do know that is closest to the words that you wish to describe and grasp the shape of."*
> *"With each new term gained, you are able to narrow the space by definition and deepen your vocabulary."*

Applied here: the closest available terms were "strategic coherence", "ambidexterity", "dynamic capabilities", "strategic persistence", "success trap" and "rigidity trap". None was used as the answer. Each was treated as a witness to be ranged against the residual, per Fig 8 :53: *"Residual first, frame second."* The description in effect terms (two quantities, one fixed and one changing, indexed per slot) came first; the frames were selected only after that description was fixed.

The essay also names the bundle stop:

> *"Perhaps your problem was a bundle or conflation of several problems masquerading as one? You will need to search all of those problems individually to find their answers and then combine them in a satisfactory way."*

That is the canonical warrant for treating BUS-0010 as a union (Fig 9 :54-57), not as one hard question.

The stop condition:

> *"You will know that you are done because the finish line will also be the starting place, as there is nowhere else to search and nothing else that can describe the answer better."*

In this solution the stop is reached when the two conditions C1 and C2 are stated, the instrument for each is named, and the falsifiers are written. Any further search that only renames the conditions is motion, not progress (Fig 7 :32, *"one long room: motion, no progress"*).

## 3. What licenses a name

Only two things, both outside the shape:

1. **The ground settles it.** Formula step 5: *"the ground settles it. Verified only by what pays. A gate is not the ground."* The README: the framework *"stops at the limit of what the available instruments can distinguish, and records the lineage so the stop is honest."*
2. **A provenanced import from a frame whose closure differs** (Fig 6 :23, Fig 8 :50-52), with a source gate and a shadow test. Fig 8 :51: *"Convergent derivation and adopted import look identical in the contents. Only the record of where each came from separates them."*

For BUS-0010, neither has happened for the specific priorities a company should change. There is no prospective measurement that pays for "this priority sequence was the coherent one", and the management-pool names were adopted without a shadow test. Hence the `gap` row in F11 of `01_figure_grounded_audit.md`.

## 4. The source error is the specific forbidden move here

From *The Hard Problem is About Source*, read directly:

> *"The Problem: You can have several underlying causes that create identical readings but the definition of the output would be read as the same."*
> *"If several paths create the same behavior, then behavior is a projection with lossy information"*
> *"What the Spectrometer Example Reveals: The answer is unreached until a better instrument exists."*

This is the exact error a strategy explanation makes when it reads "a coherent company" and names its source. A stable residual reading is consistent with a genuinely anchored kernel, with a monopoly that hides the residual, with a market that has not yet moved, with coherent drift, or with luck. The reading does not determine the source. The literature's habit of naming the source from the reading is metamerism, and no amount of agreement among readers fixes it. What is owed is an instrument that discriminates sources, which this solution does not have. Fig 6 :30: *"outward: does another frame already hold it? The description travels. Fails by being sharpened into terms only we can read. Cast it in effect terms."*

The essay closes: *"An instrument that can’t distinguish these cases demonstrates its own inadequacy, it cannot claim anything substantial about what is being measured or what causes it."* That is why the expert packet asks for a failure condition before a theory.

## 5. Prestige is not action, applied to strategic coherence research

From *The Recursive Self-Improvement Trap*, read directly:

> *"Prestige must never be allowed to validate an action directly."*
> *"Prestige can fund an action. It can give an action a budget to be tried. But it cannot settle an action. Only the ground can settle it. The Ground Maintainer must be blind to prestige."*
> *"If prestige could buy actions, the system would collapse into a monoculture. The highest-prestige agent would monopolize compute and repeat its past success, and the system would stop exploring."*
> *"You may all agree and be wrong. You may set your clock to someone else's, who was setting your clock to yours, and be off by many deviations over time."*

The third and fourth lines are direct instances, in the author's own agent system, of Fig 7 :36 (*"A chain of mirrors is one long room"*) and Fig 2 :34 (*"the drift is coherent, which is why nobody notices"*). Because it is the author's own design, it is framework-internal corroboration, not an independent witness; it is recorded here as an illustration of the rule, not as evidence that companies behave this way.

For outreach this means: a list of famous strategy professors who agree with the shape would be a monoculture, not a check. The ask gives the claim a budget to be tried by someone outside; it does not solicit endorsement.

## 6. The kernel instance

The same essay states the question's own metaphor in the author's terms:

> *"A virus rewrites its own code, hijacks the host, has no autonomy, no stability."*
> *"A human keeps a stable kernel (DNA) but changes as a person over time. The Tether is like a human. The kernel is fixed. The operating system grows."*

This is framework-internal corroboration of F1 and F3: the stable kernel is the set and the anchor, the growing operating system is the arrangements and rooms. It matters because it shows the framework's own design treats fixed-kernel-plus-changing-arrangement as the healthy case, and total-rewrite as the failure case. It is still one evidence pool with the figures, so it corroborates the shape without independently verifying the business application.

## 7. This derivation is a simulation, not a ground reading

From the same essay, read directly:

> *"the output is only as good as the validator. The agent, or the tool, or the frame, or the theory, cannot validate itself. An external validator, or ground, is always needed to tether the veracity of the output to reality. Anything else is drift."*
> *"LLMs can quickly simulate an approximate answer/output with the formula found in the architecture post. It will often be better than the generic outputs that you will usually get otherwise too (intelligence is essentially arrangement). However, the truly testable output would need to be derived from the real architecture or working the formula yourself."*

So this `05` file, like `01`, is a simulated derivation checked for internal consistency against the figures. It is labelled `specified` wherever it makes a claim about companies. It is not a test of the question and cannot settle it. The point of publishing it is to make the shape and its refuters easy to check by someone whose ground contact is real and whose closure differs.

## 8. The shape, stated once more

For BUS-0010 the canonical shape is: **two quantities kept distinct, one fixed (the anchor and kernel) and one changing (the arrangements and rooms), with the change selected against the surfaced residual and classified as re-arrangement or import.** It is not a coherence score, not a name of the right priorities, and not a claim that alignment is worthless. The names that appear in the strategy literature are bindings, provenance owed. The instruments that would convert the shape into a grounded answer are not held here, so the answer stops at `specified` and the stop is recorded.
