## 2. Independent re-census from the residual line

### 2.1 Arity, symmetry, scale

- **Arity:** a relation among company-library Γ, action a and market return o′, indexed by slot s and ordered by time t. Priority change is a second-order property of a across t. A two-place reading (company, coherence) would lose both the action and the market, so it is rejected.
- **Symmetry:** not symmetric. The market is an anchor and does not update on the company's opinion (Fig 2 :25). "Coherent" is a threshold comparison (Fig 12 :87); "change" is a sequence (Fig 12 :72). One operand is compared, the other is ordered, and neither is interchangeable.
- **Scale:** the slot is a commitment or business, but the ranking of "coherent" exists only against a market at the population scale (Fig 4 :5). The time index crosses the membrane between the company's step loop and the market's selection loop (Fig 4). The residual is intrinsically cross-scale.

## 4. Round-trip gap R_T

Fig 4 :30: *"So take the round trip and measure the gap. Without it you are navigating by dead reckoning: confident about a position nobody checked."*

- **T_A, going up:** carry the primary's two-condition shape to a one-sentence abstraction. Result: *strategic coherence is a stable relation to a market, and priorities should change when the market does.*
- **T_E, coming back:** put that abstraction into a specific company situation. Result: *keep the mission fixed and refresh the priorities regularly.*

**R_T = gap(x, (T_E ∘ T_A)(x)).** The gap is what the abstraction cannot hold:

1. The one-sentence form loses the anchor rule. "Stable relation" does not specify that the market, not internal agreement, settles the question (Fig 2 :29, :34, :35). Coming back down, it lets a company redefine the mission to match its plan and call that coherence, which is Fig 2 :34.
2. It loses the room-selection condition. "Priorities should change when the market does" has no place for the requirement that each room match the residual the last one surfaced (Fig 7 :36). Coming back down, any change counts.
3. It loses the import distinction. "Refresh the priorities" treats re-arrangement and import as one move, so reach can appear to grow when nothing new entered (Fig 8 :32).
4. It loses the valence flip. "Keep the mission fixed" has no place for the possibility that the mission's own good commitment changed sign (Fig 11 :50, Fig 12 :119).
5. It collapses the per-slot indexing into a company-wide gesture (Fig 1 :19).

This gap is not an error to eliminate; it is the measurement of what the coarser description cannot hold (Fig 4 :31). It is also the reason the outreach packet sends the generator, not the one-sentence version (Fig 4 :25). The one-paragraph version in `07` is a deliberately lossy artefact and is labelled as such.
