## QUESTION

Can a company remain strategically coherent while frequently changing its priorities?

Restated as an effect: how can R = |Γ(b,a) - o′| stay small across slots while the action a changes at high frequency over time? R is indexed per slot and per time, never aggregated (Fig 1 :19). Anchor: the market, named as an anchor in Fig 2 :29. The ordinary term "coherence" must first be refused as a named collapse (Fig 2 :34) and re-grounded as stable contact with a non-updating anchor.

## VERDICT (from the figures)

**The figure-derived verdict:** a company can remain strategically coherent while changing priorities frequently, because coherence and priority change govern different quantities. Coherence is stable contact with a non-updating market anchor and a fixed kernel; priority change is the rearrangement of held atoms and the selection of new rooms against the residual the last room surfaced. The pair fails together only when the anchor moves (coherent drift) or when rooms change without residual selection (motion, not progress). Re-shuffling preserves coherence but adds no atom; only contact with a differing closure expands reach, and it needs a provenance record. The formerly coherent commitment can change sign when the residual changes. The ordinary sense of coherence as internal agreement is refused as a named collapse, not adopted. The verdict is the union kept separate per domain, never averaged. Mode `general` for the structure, `specified` for the company application. Which priorities to change, and whether a given change is an import or a re-shuffle, is a declared `gap`, not a hidden answer.

- **cost if wrong:** misdirected diagnosis. A company failing C2 would be told to strengthen its mission and metrics, which is effort above the break (Fig 3 :29-30), while a company failing C1 would be told to change faster, which accelerates drift. In outreach terms, an expert's twenty minutes would be spent refuting a shape that was never the right shape. The cost is real but bounded and largely reversible, because the packet states its refuters before its conclusion.
- **undoable:** yes. No term is deleted if the claim fails; a refutation is added and the reach falls because *"something was added rather than because anything was deleted"* (Fig 6 :42-43). The shape and its six falsifiers are recorded in a provenance trail so a later reader can locate the error (Fig 10 :62-64).
- **reversed by:** any of the six pre-registered falsifiers, in particular (1) an arrangement change that necessarily changes the set, (2) high internal agreement with a moved market anchor and continued contact, (3) frequent selected change with R not trending down, (4) reach expanded purely by internal re-prioritisation, (5) invariant commitment valence across a change of residual, or (6) one intervention restoring both conditions with no categorically different residue. A source-discriminating instrument that separates anchored coherence from coherent drift with no access to the market verdict would narrow the `gap`. A figure re-census that collapses Fig 13 :115 would break the shape. Prestige or agreement is not such an instrument.
