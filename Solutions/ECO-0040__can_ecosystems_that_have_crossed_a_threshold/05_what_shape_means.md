## 1. A shape is a constraint, not a filler

`FRAMEWORK/reference/FIGURES_README_canonical.md`: the framework returns "what a missing thing would have to look like (a shape, not a name)". Fig 9 :32: "you get a kind of thing, never the answer itself". Fig 13 :135: "It cannot say which primitive is missing, because naming it would mean already holding it."

From *The Library of Babel* (opened 2026-11-23): "you can cut your search short if the set of books returned is small and descriptive enough that it provides a general shape of the answer that satisfies your question"

So a **shape** is the narrowed form of an answer that can be stated before it can be named. It constrains; it does not fill. For ECO-0040 the shape is: a bundle of conditions to unbundle, a measured anchor for "restored", an import-shaped intervention, and a non-invertible return. A specific claim such as "reintroduce grazer X to lake Y" is a name, and is not licensed by the figures alone.

## 2. How the shape is obtained

- *The Library of Babel*: when the right vocabulary is unavailable, use the words already held that are "closest" to the target and look for new terms that "overlap, correlate, or are even used interchangeably" with them; recurse. "With each new term gained, you narrow the space by definition and deepen your vocabulary." That is the bootstrapping described in the essay.
- Fig 9 :30-31: "your words are borrowed too, so describe the effect rather than the cause."
- From the exemplar's mapping of *Decoding Tacit Knowledge*: "When contact isn't possible, the best you can send is a plain description of what you'd expect to see, because anyone can check that against the world." (recorded via the exemplar, not re-fetched; quoted here as that record).
- *The Library of Babel* on stopping: "You will know that you are done because the finish line will also be the starting place." The paradox is that the searcher has "already composed the answer" through the process of locating it.

Applied here: describe the effect ("the target state and function do not return after the driver is removed, and a coarse reading cannot discriminate the cause") before adopting any frame (alternative stable states, resilience, restoration ecology). Otherwise any frame will seem to fit (Fig 9 :27-28).

## 3. What licenses a name

Only two things, both outside the shape itself:

1. **The ground settles it.** `THE_FORMULA.md` step 5: "the ground settles it. Verified only by what pays." The README says the framework "stops at the limit of what the available instruments can distinguish, and records the lineage so the stop is honest."
2. **A provenanced import from a frame whose closure differs** (Fig 6 :23, Fig 8), with a source gate and a shadow test. Fig 11 :65: "Introducing an actor for its cascade is ecosystem engineering" and the same move with an unenumerated cascade produces cane toads and kudzu.

From the exemplar's mapping of *They're Scanning Books When They Should*: "The real import is not a new term in the existing space. It is a new dimension of the space." Also: "Declaring the primitives does not make them neutral. It makes the choice visible." (recorded via the exemplar, not re-fetched).

So naming a missing actor is not forbidden. It is a binding from the ecology frame onto a held atom, and it is licensed only with provenance and a shadow test. What is forbidden is claiming that the binding was derived from the six-term set, or that it moves the wall in Fig 6's sense without adding an atom.

## 4. Why the endpoint reading is not enough

*The Hard Problem is About Source* (opened 2026-11-23) gives the mechanism. An instrument that reads green "cannot comment on the source." It could be one green light, or yellow plus blue, or RGB, or CMYK, or a scheme not yet discovered. "If several paths create the same behavior, then behavior is a projection with lossy information." The instrument reading does not determine the source; "the answer is unreached until a better instrument exists," and it was the spectrometer, not an argument, that resolved the case.

For ECO-0040: a reading of "coral dominated" or "clear water" does not say which feedback maintains it. A restoration that returns the reading without returning the source has not settled the question. What is owed is a source-discriminating instrument, which is why F2 in `01_figure_grounded_audit.md` demands a measured round-trip gap.

*The Recursive Self-Improvement Trap* (opened 2026-11-23): "the output is only as good as the validator. The agent, or the tool, or the frame, or the theory, cannot validate itself." This is why this folder cannot settle the question it reports on.

## 5. The answer to "what shape is the Tether's answer here"

**Nothing in the framework names a restorable ecosystem.** What it provides is:

- the unbundling of the question into overlapping conditions (Fig 9 :54-57);
- the anchor rule: the measured return, not the habitat's current look and not agreement (Fig 2 :29, Fig 11 :73);
- the import rule with provenance and a named cascade (Fig 11 :61, :65, Fig 6 :14);
- the non-invertible round trip and the demand that its gap be measured (Fig 4 :28-31);
- the honest stop: `unreachable` or `gap` is a verdict, not silence (Fig 13 :134-135, Fig 8 :39).

The particular species, intervention, and threshold value are names. They arrive from ecology with a source and a shadow test, and are settled by the ground. Until then they carry zero verdict weight.

## 5b. Correction to my own reading (see `06_isomers_and_order.md`, Q3)

I initially treated "shape, not a name" as a blanket ban on naming anything in this answer. That overstates it. The README's phrase governs a **missing** thing: "what a missing thing would have to look like (a shape, not a name)." Fig 13 :135 agrees: "It cannot say which primitive is missing, because naming it would mean already holding it." A grazer, a mutualist, or a hydrological connection is not a seventh primitive invented by the framework; it is an actor read off the habitat (Fig 11 :43-48: "The habitat is enumerated, never composed... You do not invent the list. You read it off the world"). So naming it is allowed as a provenanced import, not as a derivation. The correction is recorded here rather than silently applied.

## 6. Two consequences for outreach

**Prestige is not action.** *The Recursive Self-Improvement Trap* (opened 2026-11-23): "Prestige must never be allowed to validate an action directly." Full passage: "Prestige can fund an action. It can give an action a budget to be tried. But it cannot settle an action. Only the ground can settle it. The Ground Maintainer must be blind to prestige." A campaign that gathers respected ecologists to agree that restoration is possible (or impossible) is the named collapse, not corroboration. The legitimate ask is the opposite: give the claim a budget to be tried and let the measured outcome settle it.

**This audit is a simulation, not a ground reading.** Same essay: "LLMs can quickly simulate an approximate answer/output with the formula found in the architecture post... However, the truly testable output would need to be derived from the real architecture or working the formula yourself." So `01_figure_grounded_audit.md` is a simulated derivation checked for internal consistency against the figures. It is not a test of any ecosystem and must be labelled as such. It cannot settle anything it reports on.
