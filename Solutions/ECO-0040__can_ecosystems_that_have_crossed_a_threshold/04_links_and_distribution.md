## Wider literature that bears on the question

These are the primary sources cited by the Wikipedia pages above. They were confirmed to exist through Crossref and the Wikipedia reference lists; where a paper's body was not opened, it is not quoted as a world witness in `09_watson_answer.md`.

- Scheffer, Carpenter, Foley, Folke and Walker, "Catastrophic shifts in ecosystems", Nature 413, 2001. The canonical statement of alternative states and hysteresis in ecology.
- Scheffer and Carpenter, "Catastrophic regime shifts in ecosystems: linking theory to observation", Trends in Ecology and Evolution 18, 2003. Links the theory to observed cases.
- Biggs et al., "Turning back from the brink: Detecting an impending regime shift in time to avert it", PNAS 106, 2009. The source of the "prevent returning even when the driver is removed" definition quoted on the Regime shift page.
- Scheffer et al., "Early-warning signals for critical transitions", Nature 461, 2009. Basis of the critical slowing down literature.
- Dent, Cumming and Carpenter 2002 (open access, fetched above), the one primary paper quoted directly here.

Overlap note: the three Wikipedia pages and the Wikipedia reference lists share the same underlying ecology literature pool, so their agreement is one pool talking (Fig 2 :40), not four independent witnesses. The crossref record is bibliographic, not a second witness. The essays share an author with the figures. The one genuinely different evidence pool in this folder is the figure set itself, which is why the verdict comes from the figures and the ecology material is corroboration only.

## The two-stage offer

1. **Stage 1.** Send the residual in effect terms: what conditions are bundled, what counts as the measured return, and the three refuters. Ask what they would count as a failure. Record the verdict before any theory is shown.
2. **Stage 2, opt-in only.** If they are curious, offer the figure text link and the three source links with the note that the figures, not this summary, are the authority. Their later agreement is never pooled as corroboration (Fig 2 :40).
3. **Stage 3.** Offer the formula as a method to reuse, with the instruction that each user writes their own refuter and states their own mode. A frame "cannot check the direction of its own question" (`THE_FORMULA.md`, "Direction"), so the direction discipline is the user's, not the framework's.

**Hard precondition:** the two-stage offer only works if the stage-1 output survives. If any refuter in `01_figure_grounded_audit.md` §4 fires for the case in question, the shape is withdrawn for that case before any theory is offered.
