## What is deliberately not here

- No personal emails of named individuals.
- No ranking of people by how likely they are to agree that restoration is possible or impossible.
- No bulk-send plan.

Three figure-grounded reasons:

1. **Agreement is not an anchor.** Fig 2 :35: "Alignment is triangulation, not negotiation. A mirror cannot correct you." An anchor is "whatever settles the question and does not change its answer because of what any frame thinks" (Fig 2 :29). For this question the anchor is the measured ecological outcome, not the number of ecologists who agree.
2. **Selecting for receptiveness manufactures the disqualifying correlation.** Fig 2 :40: "agreement about the world is worthless when the agreeing frames share an evidence pool." A list of people chosen because they already believe restoration is possible (or impossible) is a list selected to agree.
3. **Showing the framework before taking the verdict spends independence.** Fig 10 :42: "A verifier that can reconstruct the claim cannot verify it." So stage 1 carries only the residual, the shape claim, and the refuter.

Legally: harvesting personal emails for unsolicited bulk outreach breaches GDPR and CAN-SPAM style rules in many jurisdictions. That is a practical failure on top of the structural one. All venues in `03_public_contact_points.csv` are public institutional submission or contact routes.

## Suggested next step

Take `08_expert_verification_packet.md` and `02_outreach_email.md` to the public venues in `03`. Ask what would refute the shape claim. Record that verdict before showing any framework material, and never pool later agreement as corroboration.
