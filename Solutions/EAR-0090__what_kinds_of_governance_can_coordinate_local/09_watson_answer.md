## DOMAIN CENSUS

R by arity, symmetry and scale, then one line per domain giving actors / currency / anchor, partition or union, and order by dependency.

- **R (the residual).** The gap between a compound, cascading Earth-system risk and scale-bounded governance authority. Arity: three levels in one compound, not three residuals. Symmetry: not symmetric, methods move up and head starts move down, currencies differ. Scale: first order inside each level, second order at the interfaces, third order at the cascade that shifts risk to a neighbour or a later generation. Fig 9 :55 "A bundled problem is usually a union of overlapping conditions, not a list of separate ones".
- **Domain 1, the Earth system (the ground).** Actors: atmosphere, ocean, cryosphere, land, ecosystems, hazards and their cascades. Currency: material flows, exposure, loss. Anchor: the physical measurement, which does not update on what frames think. Union: compound risk. Order: first, every other domain ranges against it.
- **Domain 2, governance frames at each level.** Actors: councils, agencies, ministries, treaty bodies, courts, markets, communities. Currency: rules, decisions, finance, enforcement, legitimacy. Anchor: must be Domain 1's measurement; if a frame's answer moves when pushed, it is a convention. One domain with three levels, not three domains. Order: second.
- **Domain 3, the membrane between levels.** Actors: methods travelling up, head starts travelling down, monitoring and review. Currency: reusable method, cheaper next search, round-trip gap. Anchor: the measured `R_T`. Union where two levels bind at once. Order: third.
- **Domain 4, verification and outside frames.** Actors: expert readers, research networks, public review. Currency: residuals and agreement. Anchor: the ground, never agreement. Order: fourth; sees the residual, not the interiors.

Dependency order: keep the ground reachable, then have terms for what each level affords, then state what counts as progress, then plan coordination (Fig 3 :20 "The chain is ordered by dependency, not by importance"). Strain test: three candidate strains (binding law versus method/recording, real exclusion versus contact asymmetry, markets as anchors) were re-censused and resolved in `01` §5. No hidden strain; one live ground question about method versus recording.

## VERDICT (from the figures)

The shape survives; the ranking of named kinds does not exist in this derivation. A compound cascading risk is one problem with three levels. Any coordinating arrangement must run one kernel at all three levels, pass methods up and head starts down with a measured `R_T`, keep the physical measurement as the anchor, buy independence between centres, enumerate the habitat to the third-order cascade, and hold a refusal move. Polycentric, adaptive, multilevel and subsidiarity-based kinds are provenanced imports that satisfy parts of the shape; none is derived or ranked. This is a `specified`, ungrounded result. The strongest single figure-grounded point is the union reading: a case in two levels is not a harder case of one, so a scheme that merely adds local, national and international action leaves the intersections standing.

- **cost if wrong:** bounded and locatable. The packet is mode labelled, states refuters before the conclusion and records the lineage, so an error is findable rather than invisible. The live cost is spending the independence of outside readers by sending a list of names instead of a shape and a refuter. Fig 2 :42 says "Buy independence in proportion to the cost of being wrong", and here the cost of being wrong is high, so that independence is expensive to replace.
- **undoable:** yes. The named imports are removable without touching the derivation, and `07` and `08` already remove them from the claim. A sent shape can be superseded by a corrected one. No figure primitive has to be withdrawn, and no institution is named as the final answer.
- **reversed by:** a ground reading, not agreement. A source-discriminating instrument that shows which arrangement actually reduced exposure rather than changing a boundary or a metric would name a kind; a demonstration that two levels have separable residuals and no intersections would break the union verdict; a zero measured `R_T` on a cross-scale transfer would remove the membrane caveat for that transfer; a nominal reorganisation that changed no contact yet reduced risk would break Fig 11's capability claim; and a figure re-census that produced a ranking of institutions would close the gap. Until one of these fires, the verdict stands as `candidate`.
