# The Tether's answer: what kinds of governance can coordinate across scales

**Mode: `specified` on the shape, `general` on the structural constraint, and explicitly not `grounded`.** This is a simulated derivation checked for internal consistency against the figures (`05` §7), not a ground reading. No governance arrangement was measured.

## 1. The residual it answers

The risk is compound and cascading, crossing local, national and international boundaries, while governance authority is scale-bounded. Fig 9 :55, "A bundled problem is usually a union of overlapping conditions, not a list of separate ones." IPCC AR6 SYR B.2: "Climatic and non-climatic risks will increasingly interact, creating compound and cascading risks that are more complex and difficult to manage." So the question is not which one institution is best. It is what shape any arrangement must have to close a residual that lives in the intersections.

The answer predicate was bound in `01` §0: `coordinates(R, G)` holds when `G` keeps the Earth-system anchor reachable at all three levels and closes the compound residual without substituting agreement for measurement.

## 2. The shape

1. **One kernel at three sizes.** The same perceive, decide, act, check-against-the-ground loop runs locally, nationally and internationally. Fig 4 :19-20, "Only the actors, the currency and the anchor change." Levels are not separate problems and not a hierarchy of copies.

2. **A two-legged directional membrane.** Fig 4 :22, "Only methods cross up. Only head starts come down." Local experiments yield reapplicable methods; higher levels send down finance, models and standards that make the next local search cheaper. A uniform blueprint sent downward is a recording, not a head start (Fig 4 :25). Every transfer carries a round-trip gap `R_T` that must be measured rather than assumed (Fig 4 :30).

3. **The anchor is measurement, at every level.** Fig 2 :29, "An anchor is whatever settles the question and does not change its answer because of what any frame thinks." Fig 10 :24, "the ground does not decay, the channel does." Success counted in agreements, frameworks or meetings is a convention deciding an outcome, which Fig 10 :47 says "has stopped being one."

4. **Independence is bought, not assumed.** Fig 2 :31-32, "Independence, between two frames, is how little they draw on the same evidence." and "Independence is what makes agreement mean something." Multiple centres that share one assessment are one witness with several names. IPCC AR6 WGII D.2 says decision-making "are integrated across governance levels, sectors and timeframes"; the figure adds that integration must not collapse into a single evidence pool.

5. **The habitat is enumerated per risk, out to the third-order cascade.** Fig 11 :43-44, "The habitat is enumerated, never composed." and "List everything in contact with the residual, then what is in contact with those, and outward until the cascade stops mattering." Fig 11 :52-53 gives the Earth-system case: "And contact cascades. Scoping the habitat to direct contact is how DDT reached the eagles: first-order enumeration, second-order effect, and the residual that mattered was three steps out." A standing sector list will not do.

6. **Capability belongs to the arrangement plus its habitat.** Fig 11 :40, "An improvement that does not change contact changes nothing, however much it improves." Renaming or merging agencies without changing measurement contact, decision paths to exposure or finance flows does not reduce the residual.

7. **A refusal move must exist.** Operators table :58, "a refutation is a negation, which is why a vocabulary without it can only accumulate." A system that can add frameworks but not withdraw a failing one cannot correct, only grow.

8. **The named kinds are imports, not results.** Fig 9 :32, "you get a kind of thing, never the answer itself." Polycentric governance, adaptive co-management, multilevel governance, subsidiarity: each passes a source gate and a shadow test in the world literature, and each is recorded as an import. The figures do not rank them. That part of the question is a **gap**.

## 3. What it explicitly does not say

- Not that one named kind is the answer, and not that polycentricity is figure-derived.
- Not that coordination is a single hierarchy or a flat partnership of equal peers. It is a two-legged cycle with different currencies in each direction.
- Not that more institutions help. More rooms that read the same report are one long room (Fig 7 :36, "A chain of mirrors is one long room. The next room must differ from the last, and match the residual it surfaced.").
- Not that this is settled by measurement. No instrument result is claimed.
- Not a theory of everything. There is no last governance recipe; Fig 7 names "the limit: no last room".

## 4. What would settle it, and what would settle a named kind

For the **shape**, the falsifiers are in `01` §6 and the packet in `08`. They are fired by a measurement against the Earth-system anchor, not by agreement.

For a **named kind**, what is owed is a source-discriminating measurement of the kind *The Hard Problem is About Source* describes. A governance process that reports "risk reduced" cannot comment on the source. Several arrangements give the same reading: real exposure reduction, a boundary change, or a metric that stopped registering a slow-onset hazard. An instrument that discriminates among those would settle which arrangement actually closed `R`. It was a spectrometer, not an argument, that resolved the colour case. The corresponding questions here are:

- Does each level's anchor move with the physical measurement, or with what the centres agree to report?
- Is a given cross-scale transfer a reapplicable method or a replayed recording? Measure `R_T`.
- Did the arrangement change contact with the risk (decision path, finance flow, enumerated cascade), or only its own structure?
- Can it refuse a failed term, and is the refusal recorded with provenance?

## 5. The honest stop

**The shape is reached; the ranking is a gap.** Fig 13 :134, "Unreachable is a verdict here, not a silence." The six-term set and the operators determine the constraints above, but no figure line names an institution, and no figure line ranks Ostrom against Folke against subsidiarity. The canonical rule is that the framework returns "a shape, not a name". The stop is at the limit of the available instruments, with the lineage recorded so it is checkable.

IPCC AR6 SYR C.6 says "Effective climate action is enabled by political commitment, well-aligned multilevel governance, institutional frameworks, laws, policies and strategies and enhanced access to finance and technology." That is the field's own convergence on the shape. It is corroboration, not the verdict, and it does not close the gap between shapes and kinds.

## 6. One-paragraph version for outreach

> The Tether does not name a best governance kind, and it does not claim the final institutional recipe. It says that a risk which is compound and cascading is one problem with three levels, not three problems added together; that the same decision loop must run locally, nationally and internationally with only actors, currency and anchor changing; that reusable methods travel up and head starts travel down, each with a measured round-trip gap; that the anchor at every level is the Earth-system measurement, never an agreement count; that decision centres must be genuinely independent rather than readers of one report; that the actor set must be enumerated out to the third-order cascade; and that a system which can only add frameworks cannot correct itself. Polycentric, adaptive and subsidiarity-based arrangements are named as provenanced imports from the literature, not as figure results. What the framework offers is the shape any coordinating kind must have, four refuters written in advance, and the instruments that would close the question. The ranking of named kinds stays open until the ground settles it.
