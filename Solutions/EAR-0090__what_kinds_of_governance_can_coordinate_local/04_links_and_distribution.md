## What the wider literature contributes, and what it does not

The world sources converge on four claims that match the figure-derived shape: multiple decision centres at multiple scales (Ostrom, Folke), integration across governance levels (IPCC), direction from the level best placed to act (EU subsidiarity), and scale as a crosscutting analytical problem (Biermann). This is corroboration only. It cannot decide the verdict, and the sources overlap: Ostrom is cited by the resilience and governance literatures, and the IPCC assessment draws on the same policy literature. Agreement from a shared pool is one witness, not two (Fig 2 :31-32).

No world source was found that establishes a unique best governance kind for this residual. That absence is consistent with F9 in `01_figure_grounded_audit.md`, but a search that finds nothing is never a proof of absence (Fig 8 :39). The gap is recorded, not closed.

## Distribution: offer, do not push

Figure-grounded rules:

- **Fig 4 :25**, "A recording carried upward looks like knowledge and is a description of one occasion." Sending the full framework before anyone has described a residual sends a recording. Lead with the shape claim and its refuter; offer links after.
- **Fig 10 :42**, "A verifier that can reconstruct the claim cannot verify it." A recipient who has absorbed the framework before giving a verdict has joined the frame being checked.
- **Fig 9 :27-28**, "describe it before you go looking, or any frame you pick will seem to fit." Ask what they would expect to see if the shape claim were true before giving them the article.
- **Fig 2 :35**, "Alignment is triangulation, not negotiation. A mirror cannot correct you." A list selected for likely agreement produces a convention, not corroboration.

**Recommended order in a reply:** (1) the residual, the shape claim and its refuter; (2) ask what they would count as a failure; (3) record that verdict; (4) only then offer the links, with the note that the figures, not the summary, are the authority.

## The two-stage offer

1. **Stage 1.** Validate the output against the ground. Send the residual, the shape claim and the four refuters. Ask what failure they would expect. Do not attach the theory.
2. **Stage 2, opt-in only.** If they are curious, offer the source links. Their stage-1 verdict must already be recorded. Later agreement is never pooled as corroboration, because Fig 2 :40 says "disqualifying for a verdict: agreement about the world is worthless when the agreeing frames share an evidence pool".
3. **Stage 3.** Offer the formula as a method to reuse. This is permitted because Fig 4 :22 says "Only methods cross up. Only head starts come down." Each user must name their own ground and write their own refuter before running it.

**Hard precondition:** stages 1 to 3 work only if the stage-1 output survives checking. This folder records a simulated derivation, not a ground reading, so stage 1 is where the real work begins.
