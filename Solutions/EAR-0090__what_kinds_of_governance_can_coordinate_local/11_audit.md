## Round trip gap R_T

The Symbols table defines `R_T` as the gap between what was sent up and what came back down, `gap(x, (T_E o T_A)(x))`, measuring what the coarser description cannot hold. Fig 4 :30 requires it to be measured rather than assumed. I ran the round trip on the condensation this folder actually uses.

1. **Sent up (x):** the verbatim figure set, chiefly Fig 4 :19-25 (one kernel, methods up, head starts down), Fig 9 :54-58 (union compound) and Fig 11 :43-53 (enumerated habitat and cascade).
2. **Abstraction (T_A):** the shorthand in `07`: compound risk, one kernel at three levels, methods up and head starts down, measurement anchor, independent centres, enumerated habitat, refusal move.
3. **Concretisation (T_E):** the concrete world names: polycentric governance, adaptive co-management, multilevel governance, subsidiarity, and the IPCC's "well-aligned multilevel governance".

**R_T is nonzero.** Three things the shorthand holds do not survive the trip back down:

- the **test of independence**, which the names do not contain. Ostrom's own lecture says whether centres "actually function independently... is an empirical question in particular cases". A label can be adopted without the independence.
- the **method versus recording distinction** (Fig 4 :23-25). A named arrangement can send down blueprints that replace local search while still calling itself multilevel governance.
- the **required refusal move** (Operators table :58). Most named governance vocabularies are accumulative; adding a withdrawal term is a separate design act.

Conversely, one thing survives cleanly: the union compound and the need for one kernel with a measurement anchor at each level. That is the residue worth sending. `R_T` was **not** measured numerically, because no ground reading exists; it is recorded qualitatively as the enumerated set of dropped constraints. Treating "the figures correspond to the governance literature" as identity would set `R_T` to zero and would be the dead-reckoning error Fig 4 :30 names. The honest form is a lossy condensation with a measured gap, which is what `09` states.
