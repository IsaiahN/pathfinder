# Outreach draft - check the output before deciding about the theory

**Use:** one to one, or as a cover letter attached to a submission through the public venues in `03_public_contact_points.csv`. **Not** for bulk send.

**Hard precondition:** do not send until the shape claim and its refuter in `01_figure_grounded_audit.md` are stated on the page exactly as they are here. The stage-1 output must survive checking or the two-stage offer collapses on contact.

---

**Subject:** One checkable claim about cross-scale climate governance, before any theory

> Hi [name],
>
> I have one specific output I would like you to check against what you already know about climate governance. I am not asking you to read a framework first, and I am not asking you to agree with anything. If the output does not survive, there is nothing else to look at.
>
> **The question in effect terms:** what would have to be true of a governance arrangement for it to coordinate local, national and international responses to a risk that is itself compound and cascading, rather than three separate problems at three administrative levels?
>
> **The output (`specified`, not `grounded`):** any such arrangement must have the same decision loop at each level, with only the actors, the currency and the anchor changing. Reusable methods must be able to travel upward from local practice, and head starts (finance, models, standards, information) must travel downward in a form that makes the next local search cheaper rather than replacing it. The anchor at every level must remain the Earth-system measurement, not an agreement count. Decision centres must be genuinely independent, meaning they do not all draw on one assessment and one evidence pool. The set of actors in scope must be enumerated for the specific risk out to the third-order cascade, not drawn from a standing list of sectors. And the arrangement needs a move that refuses a failing term, because a vocabulary that can only accumulate cannot correct.
>
> **What I am not claiming:** that any named kind, polycentric, adaptive, multilevel or subsidiarity-based, is the answer. Those are names from the literature, imported with provenance, not derived from the framework. The framework returns a shape, not a name. I am also not claiming that this output is settled by measurement. It is a `specified` prediction, not a `grounded` result.
>
> **What would refute the shape claim (written down before this email):**
> 1. one governance frame at one level can close the cross-scale residual while ranging against the measurement, with no method travelling up and no head start coming down;
> 2. a fixed sector list, assembled before the risk and without enumerating the cascade, handles a third-order risk shift as well as an enumerated habitat;
> 3. two bodies reading the same assessment count as two independent witnesses for a verdict about the world;
> 4. a nominal reorganisation that changes no measurement contact, decision path to exposure or finance flow measurably reduces the risk.
>
> If any holds, I withdraw the corresponding claim. A one-line "this fails because..." is more useful to me than agreement. If you would rather not engage, no reply is needed.
>
> **If it survives, and only then:** I can send the source links. The figures, not my summary of them, are the authority.
>
> **If you want to use the method:** the formula is public. Run it on a problem you know well and can check against a ground. Write down what would refute your result *before* you run it, and state the mode of what you get. A reported failure is more useful to me than a reported success.
>
> Thanks,
> [name]

## Why this version is ordered this way

| the move | figure-grounded reason |
|---|---|
| Output first, theory second | Fig 10 :52, "Bring a characterised residual, never the source." Frames range against the ground separately. |
| Do not ask for agreement | Fig 2 :35, "Alignment is triangulation, not negotiation. A mirror cannot correct you." |
| Pre-registered refuter | The standing rule that a falsifier written before the run is a design instrument, not only an honesty device. |
| Mode stated on every substantive claim | `THE_FORMULA.md`: "Mode. general · specified · grounded. State which. Accepted means the ground settled it; otherwise candidate." |
| Named kinds withheld | Fig 9 :32, "you get a kind of thing, never the answer itself." Names here are imports and carry provenance or no weight. |
| Method up, head start down | Fig 4 :22, "Only methods cross up. Only head starts come down." |
| Independence demanded, not assumed | Fig 2 :31-32, "Independence, between two frames, is how little they draw on the same evidence." and "Independence is what makes agreement mean something." |
| Habitat scoped by the risk | Fig 11 :43, "The habitat is enumerated, never composed." |
| No request for prestige or trust | *The Recursive Self-Improvement Trap* (essay read 2026-11-22): "Prestige can fund an action. It can give an action a budget to be tried. But it cannot settle an action. Only the ground can settle it." The ask gives the claim a budget to be tried, not an endorsement. |
| Offer the formula as a method to reuse | Fig 4 :22, "Only methods cross up. Only head starts come down." A generator can be given away without giving away an answer. |

## What is deliberately absent from the email

- No "do you agree", no "do you trust the framework", no "this is the only true theory".
- No named expert list, no receptiveness ranking, no statement that the recipient was chosen because they are likely to be sympathetic.
- No attachment of the full framework before the verdict on stage 1 is recorded. A recipient who has absorbed the interiors has joined the frame being checked, and Fig 10 :42 says "A verifier that can reconstruct the claim cannot verify it."
