## 1. A shape is a constraint you can state before you can name the thing that fills it

The canonical limit: the framework returns *"what a missing thing would have to look like (a shape, not a name)."* Fig 9 :32: *"you get a kind of thing, never the answer itself."* Fig 13 :134: *"Unreachable is a verdict here, not a silence"* (the source line continues with a comma).

From *The Library of Babel*: *"You can cut your search short if the set of books returned is small and descriptive enough that it provides a general shape of the answer that satisfies your question."*

So a **shape** narrows; it does not fill. For this question, the shape `E*` is the five-part evaluation protocol in `01_figure_grounded_audit.md` section 0: non-updating legitimate anchor, per-slot residual, enumerated habitat, low-overlap instruments, recorded round trip gap. Naming a particular benchmark or metric as *the* answer would be a name where only a shape is available.

**Correction carried forward, not erased.** The README phrase governs a *missing* primitive. In this question the outcome is not missing from the world; it is unmeasured. Naming a candidate anchor is a binding from the deployment frame and must be provenanced and dated (see `01`, section 5). It is not the forbidden act. The earlier, broader reading is retracted.

## 2. How a shape is obtained

- *Library of Babel*: use the vocabulary you already have that is *closest* to the thing, correlate it with recurring unfamiliar terms, recurse. *"With each new term gained, you are able to narrow the space by definition and deepen your vocabulary."*
- Fig 6 :30: *"The description travels. Fails by being sharpened into terms only we can read. Cast it in effect terms."* Fig 9 :30-31: *"your words are borrowed too, so describe the effect rather than the cause."*
- *Decoding Tacit Knowledge*: *"When contact isn't possible, the best you can send is a plain description of what you'd expect to see, because anyone can check that against the world."* This is exactly the outreach order in `02`: ask what failure would look like before sending theory.
- *Library of Babel* on stopping: *"You will know that you are done because the finish line will also be the starting place"* and the paradox is that by then you have *"composed the answer through the process of locating it."*
- *Arrival of the Fittest* on arrangement: *"The arrangement is the substance."* A shape is about how parts are bonded, not a list of parts. Five separate metrics are not a protocol until their relations are stated.

## 3. What licenses a name here

Only two things, both outside the shape:

1. **The ground settles it.** Formula step 5: *"the ground settles it. Verified only by what pays."* The README: the framework *"stops at the limit of what the available instruments can distinguish, and records the lineage so the stop is honest."* For this question, that means a field outcome measured after the delay, not an argument about what would happen.
2. **A provenanced import from a closure that differs.** Fig 6 :23: *"No frame escapes its own closure. The bound moves only on contact with a frame whose closure differs."* The relevant frames here are clinical surrogate endpoints, econometric policy evaluation, ecological validity and multi-metric evaluation design. Each is corroboration only.

*They're Scanning Books* sharpens what an import is: *"The real import is not a new term in the existing space. It is a new dimension of the space."* It also says: *"A minimum viable set of six terms is a human's careful guess at reality's compression axes; another sensorium's minimum set need not be those six."* So a five-part evaluation protocol is a visible design choice, not nature's own list. Declaring it makes the choice auditable; it does not make it neutral.

## 4. Why a single score is the specific forbidden error for this question

*The Hard Problem is About Source* gives the mechanism. An instrument reads green and *"cannot comment on the source"*: one green light, or yellow plus blue, or RGB, or CMYK, or a scheme not yet discovered. *"If several paths create the same behavior, then behavior is a projection with lossy information."* It was the **spectrometer**, not an argument, that resolved it: *"An instrument that can't distinguish these cases demonstrates its own inadequacy."*

A benchmark score is the same kind of reading. Many deployment states can produce the same score. Reading the source off the score is the metamerism error. What is owed is a discriminating field instrument, not a better argument and not another aggregated metric.

*The Recursive Self-Improvement Trap* states the validation limit: *"the output is only as good as the validator. The agent, or the tool, or the frame, or the theory, cannot validate itself."* This is why an evaluation protocol cannot be validated by the scores it produces.

*How Intelligence Actually Works* adds the distinction horizon: *"the point at which the observer cannot discern any difference between one generation method and another"* and the union horizon, beyond which no observer with any available instrument can discriminate origin. Both are limits of current instruments, not settled facts. *"Indiscernibility is not identity."*

## 5. Agreement, independence and the human part of the habitat

*How a Society of Agents Would Work* is direct: *"capability is a property of agent-and-habitat, never of the agent alone."* It warns that *"Ten copies watching the same thing are one opinion said ten times"* and that a group's *"agreement is never the verdict."* For real-world evaluation this means a panel of reviewers reading the same dashboard adds vocabulary, not evidence (Fig 7 :35: *"A room's agreement is variation, not a verdict. Selection between rooms is where the ground votes."*).

*Decoding Tacit Knowledge* explains why: high contact builds shared understanding, which makes communication work and makes agreement worthless as a check. The safeguards it names match the figures: a ground both sides are corrected by separately, separate experience, and claims in effect terms.

## 6. Simulation status and the stop

*The Recursive Self-Improvement Trap*: *"LLMs can quickly simulate an approximate answer/output with the formula found in the architecture post"* but *"the truly testable output would need to be derived from the real architecture or working the formula yourself."* LLM output is *"simulacra."*

*Arrival of the Fittest* is blunter: LLM outputs *"could never be verified if the solutions presented were not contrived to fit the formula"* and *"Because it is a metatheory, it cannot be validated, nor can it validate itself."*

So `01_figure_grounded_audit.md`, `06_isomers_and_order.md`, `07_the_tether_answer.md` and `09_watson_answer.md` are simulated derivations checked for internal consistency against the figures. They are **not** tests of any evaluation protocol and cannot settle this question. The honest stop is: here is the shape a defensible answer must have, here are its refuters, and here is the field reading that would close it.
