## What is deliberately not here

- No personal emails of named individuals.
- No ranking of people by how likely they are to agree.
- No bulk-send plan.

Figure-grounded reasons:

1. **A room does not upgrade the label.** Seat Map :283: *"Adding people raises the vocabulary and does not add an anchor."* Ten eval readers selected for agreement are one frame wearing ten names.
2. **Correlation disqualifies a verdict.** Seat Map :163: *"High correlation is disqualifying for a verdict and efficient for a convention."* Selecting recipients for receptiveness manufactures the very correlation that makes their agreement worthless.
3. **A verifier must not hold the interiors.** Fig 10 :42: *"A verifier that can reconstruct the claim cannot verify it."* The first contact therefore carries the residual and its refuter, not this folder's theory.

Legally: unsolicited bulk email to scraped personal addresses breaches GDPR or CAN-SPAM style rules in many jurisdictions. The venues in `03` are public submission or opt-in channels.
