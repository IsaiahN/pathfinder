## 0. The residual, in effect terms

Let the slot `s` be the cosmic expansion history, the scale factor `a(t)` and its second derivative. The prediction from GR with known matter is deceleration: `a''/a = -(4πG/3)(rho + 3p) < 0` for ordinary matter. The observation from Type Ia supernovae, the CMB and BAO is `a'' > 0`. The live residual is the gap between these.

Two responses are on the table.

| option | effect | what it adds to the library |
|---|---|---|
| A, dark energy or a cosmological constant | a stress-energy component with `w < -1/3` | a term built on the energy state already held |
| B, modified gravity on very large scales | a change in the relation between matter and geometry | a change in the arrangement of the gravitational structure |

The question asks whether the acceleration means B rather than A. Critical point, established before any figure line is chosen: **the acceleration reading does not discriminate A from B.** Nesseris (2013) writes that one *"may only hope to break this degeneracy by using dynamic probes like the growth rate data that track the matter density perturbations"*, and the Stanford Encyclopedia of Philosophy entry on underdetermination describes *"empirically equivalent theories: that is, alternative theories making the very same empirical predictions, and which therefore cannot be better or worse supported by any possible body of evidence."* Option A and option B are one evidence pool until a source-discriminating instrument is found. Fig 1 :19: *"The residual is indexed by object slot, not aggregated."* The slot here is the expansion history; a second slot, the growth of structure, is where the discrimination lives.

R by arity, symmetry and scale:

- **Arity:** one observed quantity (`a''`), two candidate causes that fit it. This is a **union compound**, not one hard question. Fig 9 :57: *"a case in two conditions is not a harder case of either."*
- **Symmetry:** the difference between A and B is not a symmetric join of two operands at one level. It is a difference of which quantity is changed (geometry-source coupling versus an added energy term). The comparison is a threshold test between two observables, not a symmetric bond.
- **Scale:** cosmological, near the horizon scale, far outside the Solar System regime where GR is tested. Fig 6 :27: *"Which is why some unreachability is depth and some is genuine absence, and from inside the frame the two look the same."*

## 3. Findings

### F1 - Gravity is a structure, and a direct state-state bond is refused (decisive for the framing)

Fig 13 :74: *"Everything else is a composition across the two kinds, never within one."*
Fig 13 :75: *"Two states never bond directly. It is not space-time but space, a structure, time; and two structures join only through a state."*

Gravity is not one of the six members. It is a structure in the slot between space and time, exactly as the calibrated exemplar derived for `SPACE(???)TIME`. Therefore a claim of the form "gravity behaves differently on large scales" cannot mean "a new primitive appears at large scales." It can only mean one of two things inside the framework: a different structure occupies the slot, or the same structure is arranged differently. Both are composition questions inside `closure(Γ)`.

**Mode:** `general`, structural. **Status:** triangulated across Fig 13 :74-75 and the exemplar's six-term derivation (low overlap: composition rule versus the imported QM/GR record). **Refuter:** derive one of the six from the other five; or show an admitted state-state or structure-structure composition with no intervening opposite-kind term; or exhibit a gravitational phenomenon that cannot be written as a structure between two states at any scale.

### F2 - A scale-dependent law is an arrangement, not a new member

Fig 12 :94: *"The arrangement is the substance."*
Fig 12 :117: *"isomer. Same atoms, different bond, different substance. The difference is invisible in the formula and decisive in the behaviour."*
Fig 12 :104: *"A settled molecule becomes an atom for whatever composes over it. So irreducible is a fact about a level and never about the world"*

Under the six-term set, the difference between GR on small scales and a different law on large scales is a difference of arrangement over the same atoms. Fig 12 :117 licenses two substances with identical ingredient sets. The popular framing "dark energy versus modified gravity" therefore does **not** map onto "new atom versus rearranged atom": dark energy is a term built on the *energy state already held*, and modified gravity is a re-arrangement of structure. Neither introduces a seventh member. Fig 13 :117: *"Six members, and no amount of arranging produces a seventh: a new member would have to be a new kind, and composition only ever rearranges the kinds already held."*

**Mode:** `specified`, ungrounded. **Status:** triangulated across Fig 12 (bond pricing and isomer definition) and Fig 13 (closed member set) with low overlap between the two figures. **Strain, named:** Fig 13 :76 is an analogue about which state is bonded, not a governing line about scale; see section 5. **Refuter:** exhibit a seventh member derived from a scale-dependent gravitational law, or show that the same held atoms under a different arrangement give identical behaviour at every accessible scale (which would collapse the isomer reading).

## 4. Pre-registered refuter (written before the conclusion)

Required by the exemplar's discipline: *"a falsifier written before the run is a design instrument, not only an honesty device."* The shape claim is: **a scale-dependent gravitational law, if true, is a re-arrangement of held structure, and the observation that would settle it is a source-discriminating growth-or-geometry measurement rather than a distance measurement.** It is refuted if any of the following holds:

1. A source-discriminating observable (growth index, slip, tensor speed, or an equivalent) shows consistency with GR plus an added energy term at the precision required to exclude the modified-law alternatives, at the relevant scales. This refutes the ground reading of the shape claim.
2. The six-term set cannot express any scale-dependent arrangement, that is, a structure bonded to a state fails to yield distinguishable terms at different scales even in principle. This refutes F2 and F7.
3. A seventh member is required to express the acceleration, with a valid source gate and shadow test. This refutes the "no new kind" claim and sends the census back to D1.
4. Existing tensor-speed or growth constraints rule out every specific modified-law term on offer, leaving no arrangement of the six that reproduces the acceleration. This refuses the specific terms at record level; the shape claim may survive but is no longer instantiated.
5. A purely geometric observable is found that entails a unique source among the candidates. This refutes F3 and dissolves the degeneracy without a growth measurement.

Until one of these is run, no part of this claim may be labelled `grounded`. A failure is more useful than an endorsement.
