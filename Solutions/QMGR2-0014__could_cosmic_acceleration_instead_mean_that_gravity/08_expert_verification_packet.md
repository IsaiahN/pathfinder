# Expert verification packet - cosmic acceleration and modified gravity

**What this is:** a claim in a form a modified-gravity researcher can check. Mode, derivation and refuters are stated before the conclusion, so a reader knows what to look for and what would settle it.

**Mode: `general` on the structural shape, `specified` on the sub-claims, not `grounded`.** No measurement is claimed to settle the choice between an added energy term and a changed gravitational law. This is a shape and a route, not a result.

## 1. The claim, in one paragraph

The measured acceleration of cosmic expansion is one reading and does not by itself identify its source. An added negative-pressure component and a change to the gravitational law on large scales both reproduce the same expansion history, so the question is a union of two conditions rather than one hard choice. Under the framework's six-term substrate, gravity is a structure between states, so a scale-dependent law, if real, is a re-arrangement of held atoms rather than a seventh primitive. What would discriminate the two options is not another distance measurement but an observable that responds to the growth of structure, to gravitational slip, or to tensor propagation. The framework does not name which option is correct; it returns the shape any answer must have, with falsifiers written in advance.

## 2. Three checkable sub-claims

### C1 - framework-internal; checkable by reading Figures 13 and 12

- **Statement:** gravity is a structure between states, not a primitive and not a direct state-state bond; a scale-dependent law is therefore a change of arrangement over held atoms, not a new member.
- **Source, verbatim:** Fig 13 :75 - *"Two states never bond directly. It is not space-time but space, a structure, time; and two structures join only through a state."* Also Fig 12 :94 - *"The arrangement is the substance."* And Fig 13 :117 - *"Six members, and no amount of arranging produces a seventh: a new member would have to be a new kind, and composition only ever rearranges the kinds already held."*
- **Refuter:** derive one of the six from the other five; show an admitted state-state or structure-structure bond with no intervening opposite-kind term; or exhibit gravitational behaviour that cannot be written as a structure between states at any scale.
- **Strain recorded, not hidden:** Fig 13 :76 (*"A gradient over space and one over time are different terms, because which state it is bonded to is part of what it is."*) governs which state is bonded, not the scale of the measurement. It is an analogue here, not a governing line.

### C2 - physics-facing; checkable against the data by a cosmologist

- **Statement:** the acceleration reading alone does not discriminate an added energy term from a modified gravitational law; the discriminator is growth or tensor propagation, not distance.
- **Source, verbatim:** Fig 9 :30-31 - *"your words are borrowed too, so describe the effect rather than the cause."* Fig 2 :35 - *"Alignment is triangulation, not negotiation. A mirror cannot correct you."* Fig 9 :54 - *"And when the split lands on a union rather than a partition, unbundle before you search again."*
- **What to check:** do the current SN Ia, CMB and BAO constraints indeed fit both classes at the background level? Nesseris (2013) states that one *"may only hope to break this degeneracy by using dynamic probes like the growth rate data that track the matter density perturbations."* Do the growth, slip and tensor-speed data separate them at the required precision?
- **Refuter:** produce a geometric observable that entails a unique source; or show that growth and tensor data already exclude every modified-law arrangement at the relevant scales.

### C3 - framework-internal derivation

- **Statement:** the honest first move when a held regularity is false is a refusal of that term, not a fresh explanation; and neither option adds a seventh primitive.
- **Source, verbatim:** Fig 5 :56 - *"refuted: a term we hold is wrong; a term is owed that says so"*; Fig 6 :21 - *"MINT composes inside the closure and can never add an atom."*; Fig 6 :22 - *"NOVEL means novel relative to atoms, not to the world. IMPORT adds an atom, and the added atom is what expands the closure."*
- **Refuter:** show that a false held term can be repaired by a mint with no negation in the record, or exhibit a cosmological term not composable from the six that passes a source gate and a shadow test.

## 5. What would settle it

1. **Growth test:** does `fσ8(z)`, the growth index `γ`, or the slip parameter depart from the GR prediction given the measured `H(z)`?
2. **Tensor test:** does tensor propagation match GR at the relevant scales? GW170817 already refused many alternative theories, which is a ground event, not an argument.
3. **Seventh-member test:** is there a cosmological term genuinely outside the six, with provenance and a shadow test?
4. **Unique geometric test:** is there a distance-based observable that identifies the source on its own? If yes, the degeneracy claim fails.

## 6. What an expert can check in twenty minutes

1. Read Fig 13 :75 and Fig 13 :117, plus Fig 12 :94: three short passages.
2. Check C2 against their own knowledge: do the geometric probes really leave the background level degenerate, and which current observable has the best chance of separating the two classes?
3. Name one observable, at any resolution, that responds differently to an added energy term and to a changed law, or say why none is possible.
4. Send the refutation. A failure is more useful than an endorsement.
