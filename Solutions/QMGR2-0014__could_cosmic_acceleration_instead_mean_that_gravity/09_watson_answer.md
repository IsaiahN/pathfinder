## VERDICT (from the figures)

The shape survives, the either/or framing does not. Gravity is a structure between states, so a scale-dependent law, if real, is a re-arrangement of held atoms rather than a seventh primitive; both dark energy and modified gravity are bindings onto the six. The acceleration reading underdetermines its source, so the question is a union compound that must be unbundled into background and growth before it is searched. The ground that would settle it is a source-discriminating observable, and such instruments already exist, so the stop is unreached rather than unreachable. A claim that the large-scale law is wrong owes a refusal of the held term before it owes a replacement. This is a `specified`, ungrounded, candidate result. The strongest figure verbatim in the case is Fig 13 :75, because it decides what gravity can be at all.

- **cost if wrong:** bounded and locatable. The packet states mode, five refuters and provenance before the conclusion, so an error is findable rather than invisible. The real cost is spending the independence of outside readers by sending a recording rather than a generator, which Fig 2 :42 prices: "Buy independence in proportion to the cost of being wrong." That cost is avoidable by leading with the union split and the refuter.
- **undoable:** yes. The either/or framing is removable without touching the derivation; `07_the_tether_answer.md` and `08_expert_verification_packet.md` already remove it. A sent claim can be superseded by the corrected packet, and no primitive has to be withdrawn.
- **reversed by:** a ground reading, not agreement. A growth, slip or tensor-speed measurement that excludes the modified-law alternatives at the required precision would settle the source question; a seventh member with a source gate and shadow test would break the closed-set claim; a derivation of one of the six from the other five would break the substrate; a purely geometric observable entailing a unique source would dissolve the degeneracy. Until one of these fires, the verdict stands as `candidate`.
