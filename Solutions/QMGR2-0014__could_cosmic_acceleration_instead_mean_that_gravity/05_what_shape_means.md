## 1. A shape is a constraint, not a filler

The canonical limit: a shape describes a missing thing, not a name for it. Fig 9 :32: *"you get a kind of thing, never the answer itself."* Fig 13 :134: *"Unreachable is a verdict here, not a silence."*

From *The Library of Babel*: *"you can cut your search short if the set of books returned is small and descriptive enough that it provides a general shape of the answer that satisfies your question."*

So a **shape** is the narrowed form of an answer you can state before you can name it. It constrains; it does not fill. For this question:

- `a'' > 0` is a reading, not a shape.
- "dark energy or modified gravity?" is a choice between two named candidates, not a shape.
- "gravity is a structure between states, so a scale-dependent law is a re-arrangement of held atoms, and the discriminator is growth rather than distance" **is** a shape. It constrains any admissible answer without selecting one.

## 2. How a shape is obtained

- *Library of Babel*: bootstrap vocabulary by proximity, look for recurring new terms that overlap the thing, recurse, narrow. The essay also insists on a tether: *"you need something to tether and anchor you to reality, the same way a mountaineer needs a tether when rappelling or climbing."* Here the tether is the residual in effect terms, not a preferred answer.
- Fig 9 :30-31: *"your words are borrowed too, so describe the effect rather than the cause."* The effect is acceleration; the cause is what is being asked about.
- *Decoding Tacit Knowledge*: *"When contact isn't possible, the best you can send is a plain description of what you'd expect to see, because anyone can check that against the world."* This is the form of the outreach claim in `02_outreach_email.md`.
- *How a Society of Agents Would Work*: the arrangement is *"separate frames, one ground, methods shared upward, head starts handed down, a room per problem, and a maintainer who sets conventions but never verdicts."* Different questions get different rooms; this one gets its own, not a pooled answer.
- *Library of Babel* on stopping: the answer is reached when the finish line is also the starting place, because the answer has been composed through the process of locating it. The lineage is what makes that checkable.

## 3. What licenses a name

Only two things, both outside the shape itself:

1. **The ground settles it.** `THE_FORMULA.md` step 5: *"the ground settles it. Verified only by what pays. A gate is not the ground."* For this question the ground is a source-discriminating observable, not an additional distance measurement.
2. **A provenanced import from a frame whose closure differs** (Fig 6 :23), with a source gate and a shadow test. *They're Scanning Books* sharpens this: *"The real import is not a new term in the existing space. It is a new dimension of the space."*

*They're Scanning Books* also warns: *"Declaring the primitives does not make them neutral. It makes the choice visible."* The six-term set is a visible guess, not nature's own list. Naming Lambda or a particular `f(R)` as *the* answer would turn a visible choice into an invisible one.

## 4. Why "which option is true" is the forbidden error here

*The Hard Problem is About Source* gives the mechanism. An instrument reads green and *"cannot comment on the source."* One green light, or yellow plus blue, or RGB, or a source not yet discovered, all give the same reading. The reading does not determine the source; only a discriminator does. Here the acceleration reading is green and the source is either an added energy term or a changed gravitational law. What is owed is a better discriminator, not a name.

This is corroborated in a low-overlap field by the Stanford Encyclopedia of Philosophy entry on underdetermination: *"empirically equivalent theories: that is, alternative theories making the very same empirical predictions, and which therefore cannot be better or worse supported by any possible body of evidence."* The philosophy source is secondary; the figure verdict comes from Fig 9 and Fig 2.

## 5. Q2 - what licenses a particular structure or law in the slot?

**Answer: nothing in the framework.** The framework returns a shape. A particular law is licensed only by the ground settling a minted term, or by a provenanced import with a shadow test. Until one of those, the name is the searcher's own contribution and must be recorded as such.

**Does any folder claim commit the forbidden error?** Not after the retraction log in `01_figure_grounded_audit.md` section 5. The early reading that dark energy "adds a new atom" was retracted: energy is already one of the six. The early reading that Fig 13 :76 governs scale-dependence was demoted to an analogue: it governs which state is bonded, not the scale of the measurement. Both retractions are visible.

## 6. Two consequences for outreach

**Prestige is not action.** *The Recursive Self-Improvement Trap*: *"Prestige can fund an action. It can give an action a budget to be tried. But it cannot settle an action. Only the ground can settle it. The Ground Maintainer must be blind to prestige."* A campaign that gathers prestigious names to agree with either option is the named collapse, not corroboration. The legitimate ask gives the claim a budget to be tried and lets an instrument settle it.

**My own audit is a simulation, not a ground reading.** *Arrival of the Fittest: The Tether* says LLM outputs must be seen as *"simulacra"*; *The Recursive Self-Improvement Trap* adds *"the truly testable output would need to be derived from the real architecture or working the formula yourself."* So `01` is a simulated derivation, checked for internal consistency against the figures. It is not a test of the claim and must be labelled as such. It cannot settle anything it reports on.
