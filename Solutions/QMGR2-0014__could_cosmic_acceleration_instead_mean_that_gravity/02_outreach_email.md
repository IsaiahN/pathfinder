**Subject:** Check one output before you decide whether the question is one question

> Hi [name],
>
> I have one specific output about the dark energy / modified gravity question that I would like you to check against what you already know. I am not asking you to read a theory first, and I am not asking you to agree with anything. If the output does not survive, there is nothing else to look at.
>
> **The output (`specified`, not `grounded`):** the measured acceleration of the expansion is one reading. It does not by itself say what produced it. Adding a negative-pressure component and changing the gravitational law on large scales both reproduce the same expansion history, so a distance measurement cannot separate them. What can separate them is an observable that responds to the growth of structure rather than to the background geometry. The output is therefore a shape: gravity is a structure between states in the framework's six-term set, so a scale-dependent law, if real, is a different arrangement of held atoms rather than a seventh primitive; and the question "dark energy or modified gravity" is a union of two conditions that must be unbundled before it is searched.
>
> **What I am not claiming:** that modified gravity is the correct explanation, that dark energy does not exist, that the framework derives general relativity or any specific `f(R)` model, or that this is settled by any measurement. The framework returns a shape, not a name. It does not select a particular law, and it does not overrule the data. This is a simulated derivation checked for internal consistency, not a ground reading.
>
> **What would refute the shape claim, written before this message:**
> 1. a growth, slip or tensor-speed measurement that shows consistency with GR plus an added energy term at the precision required to exclude the modified-law alternatives;
> 2. a demonstration that the six-term set cannot express any scale-dependent arrangement;
> 3. a genuine seventh primitive with a source gate and a shadow test;
> 4. existing tensor-speed or growth constraints that rule out every modified-law arrangement on offer;
> 5. a purely geometric observable that entails a unique source among the candidates.
>
> If any one holds, I retract the claim. A one-line "this fails because..." is more useful to me than agreement. If you would rather not engage, no reply is needed.
>
> **If it survives, and only then:** here are the sources. The figures are the authority, not my summary of them.
> - Figures and the eight-step formula, direct download (branch `general-agent`): https://github.com/IsaiahN/tether/tree/general-agent/figures

Write down what would refute your result *before* you run it, and state the mode of what you get. A reported failure is more useful to me than a reported success.
