## Wider literature that bears on the question

1. **The degeneracy is real and documented.** Nesseris (2013) states that modifying gravity can always reproduce the expansion history of any dark energy model and that geometric probes (SN Ia, CMB, BAO) cannot break the degeneracy because they measure distances and `H(z)`. This is the world-side corroboration of F3 and F4. It is secondary: the figure verdict comes from Fig 9 and Fig 2, not from this paper.
2. **The discriminator is growth, not more distance data.** Ferreira (2019) and the arXiv listing both show that the growth of structure, gravitational slip and tensor propagation are the live instruments. This corroborates F5. Note the overlap: Ferreira is a co-author of Clifton et al. (2012), so these two are one evidence pool for the review-level claims, not two independent witnesses.
3. **The philosophy-of-science source is genuinely low overlap.** The SEP entry gives a general form of the same structure: empirically equivalent theories cannot be separated by evidence that both fit, and a failed prediction does not say which held term to give up. It corroborates the figure shape from outside cosmology and outside the framework. It does not decide the physics.
4. **A live counterexample to premature settlement.** Wikipedia records that GW170817 ruled out many alternative theories of gravity as explanations for accelerated expansion. This is a ground event that refused specific terms. It refutes particular models, not the shape claim, and `01` records it as refuter item 4.
5. **Retrodiction warning.** The Clifton et al. review is a survey of work already done, not a prediction the framework produced. Do not present any of these sources as derived from the figures. Fig 8 :51-52: *"Convergent derivation and adopted import look identical in the contents. Only the record of where each came from separates them."*

## Distribution rules

Figure-grounded reasons:

- **Fig 4 :22-25** - *"Only methods cross up. Only head starts come down. A recording carried upward looks like knowledge and is a description of one occasion."* Sending the full architecture before anyone has described the residual sends a recording, not a generator. Lead with the two-condition split and the refuter.
- **Fig 10 :42** - *"A verifier that can reconstruct the claim cannot verify it."* Seat 4 must not hold the interiors. A recipient who has absorbed the framework before being asked for a verdict has joined the frame being checked.
- **Fig 9 :27-28** - *"describe it before you go looking, or any frame you pick will seem to fit."* Ask what they would expect to see if the shape claim were true before giving them the article.
- **Fig 2 :40** - *"disqualifying for a verdict: agreement about the world is worthless when the agreeing frames share an evidence pool"* A send selected for people who already prefer modified gravity produces a convention, not corroboration.
- **Fig 2 :42** - *"Buy independence in proportion to the cost of being wrong."* Choose venues by ground contact, not by likely agreement.

**Recommended order in a reply:** (1) the residual and the union split; (2) the five refuters, and the question of what observable would discriminate; (3) record the verdict; (4) only then the links, with the note that the figures, not the summary, are the authority.

## The two-stage offer

1. **Stage 1:** send the shape claim and its refuter. Ask what the recipient would count as a failure and what observable they would use to separate an added energy term from a changed law. Record their verdict before showing any theory.
2. **Stage 2, opt-in only:** if they are curious, offer the figures, the formula and the canonical README. Their later agreement is never pooled as corroboration.
3. **Stage 3:** offer the formula as a method to reuse. Each user must write their own refuter, state their own mode, and name their own ground.

**Hard precondition:** stages 1 to 3 only work if the stage-1 output survives. If a source-discriminating observable already settles the degeneracy in favour of one option, the shape claim is refuted and nothing should be sent. This is refuter item 1 in `01_figure_grounded_audit.md` section 4.
