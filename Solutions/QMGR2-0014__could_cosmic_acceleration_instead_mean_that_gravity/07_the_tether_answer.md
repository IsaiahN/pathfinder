# The Tether's answer on cosmic acceleration and modified gravity

**Mode: `general` on the structural shape, `specified` on the per-domain findings, not `grounded`.** This is a shape and a route, not a result. Simulated derivation checked against the figures (`05_what_shape_means.md` section 6), not a ground reading.

## 1. The residual it answers

The observed acceleration `a'' > 0` is a reading, not a cause. Under GR with known matter the prediction decelerates. An added negative-pressure component and a change to the gravitational law on large scales both fit the same expansion history, so the question "which one is it?" is underdetermined by the acceleration data. Fig 9 :57: *"a case in two conditions is not a harder case of either."* It is a union compound with its own loop and its own ground.

## 2. The shape, per domain

**D1, framework-internal composition.** Gravity is a structure in the slot between space and time; it is not a primitive and not a direct state-state bond. Fig 13 :75: *"Two states never bond directly. It is not space-time but space, a structure, time; and two structures join only through a state."* Therefore a scale-dependent law means a different arrangement over held atoms, not a seventh member. Fig 12 :94: *"The arrangement is the substance."* Fig 13 :117: *"Six members, and no amount of arranging produces a seventh: a new member would have to be a new kind, and composition only ever rearranges the kinds already held."*

**D2, the physics frame.** Both options are bindings onto the six. Lambda is built on the energy state already held; a modified law changes bonds over states and structures already held. Neither moves the wall (Fig 6 :21, *"MINT composes inside the closure and can never add an atom."*). The acceleration alone cannot discriminate them: Fig 9 :30-31, *"your words are borrowed too, so describe the effect rather than the cause."*

**D3, verification.** The ground is an observable that responds differently to the two options: growth of structure, gravitational slip, tensor propagation speed. Fig 6 :32: *"An instrument is not built from a description. It is improved from a worse instrument already returning something."* Such instruments are already returning readings. This is unreached, not unreachable.

**The four moves in order:** (1) describe the residual in effect terms before choosing a frame, Fig 9 :27-28, *"describe it before you go looking, or any frame you pick will seem to fit"*; (2) unbundle the union into background and growth, Fig 9 :54; (3) refuse the held term "GR holds on all scales" if it is false, before minting a replacement, Fig 5 :56, *"refuted: a term we hold is wrong; a term is owed that says so"*; (4) sharpen the existing discriminator, then let the ground settle it.

## 3. What it explicitly does not say

- Not that modified gravity is correct, and not that dark energy does not exist.
- Not that the framework derives general relativity, any `f(R)` model, or any screenable theory.
- Not a seventh primitive, and not a final theory. Fig 13 :115: *"The set is closed. The arrangements are not. That gap is why import exists."*
- Not grounded. No measurement at this scale is claimed to settle `left(R,φ)` between the two options.
- Not that the six-term set is nature's own list. It is a visible guess (see `05_what_shape_means.md` section 3).

## 4. What would settle it - the ground

A source-discriminating instrument, not a further argument.

- **Growth test:** does the growth index `γ`, the slip parameter, or `fσ8(z)` depart from the GR value given the measured `H(z)`? Ferreira (2019) notes that screening may leave *"distinctive signatures in the large-scale structure of the Universe."*
- **Tensor test:** does tensor propagation at cosmological speed match GR? Wikipedia records that GW170817 *"ruled out many alternative theories of gravity as explanations for the accelerated expansion."* This refuses specific terms at record level.
- **Geometric test:** a purely geometric observable entailing a unique source would refute the degeneracy (refuter item 5).
- **Standing falsifiers from `01` section 4:** source-discriminating consistency with GR plus an added term; inability of the six-term set to express scale-dependence; a genuine seventh member with a source gate and shadow test; exclusion of every modified-law arrangement; a unique geometric discriminator.

## 5. The honest stop

**Unreached, not unreachable.** The instruments exist and are returning readings that do not yet resolve the degeneracy. Fig 6 :39: *"instruments do not have to improve by small steps. A jump in resolution is fine"*; what is unavailable is a sensor for something that has never registered anywhere. Here something has registered: the acceleration and the growth data both exist. The answer stops at the limit of what those instruments distinguish, and records the lineage so the stop is checkable. The canonical rule: *"Indiscernibility is not identity."* Fig 13 :134: *"Unreachable is a verdict here, not a silence."*

## 6. One-paragraph version for outreach

> The Tether does not choose between dark energy and modified gravity from the acceleration data, because both fit the same reading. It says that gravity is a structure between states, so a law that changes on large scales would be a different arrangement of the same held atoms rather than a seventh primitive, and that the either/or question is a union of two conditions that must be unbundled before it is searched. The observation that would settle it is not another distance measurement but a source-discriminating one, the growth of structure, gravitational slip or tensor propagation. Such instruments already exist, so the stop is unreached rather than unreachable. What the framework offers is the shape any answer must have, five falsifiers written in advance, and the lineage of the route, not a verdict about which option is true.
