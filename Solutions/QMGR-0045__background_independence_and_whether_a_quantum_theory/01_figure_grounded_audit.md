## 0. The claim under audit

> **Question (QM & GR Questions.txt line 58):** Background independence and whether a quantum theory can be formulated without a fixed spacetime.
>
> **Restated as an effect:** the contact failure between a theory in which the geometry is solved for (GR) and a framework that needs a fixed background to define its states, its evolution and its inner product (QM and QFT). Decide two things: (a) whether the background can be non-fixed, and (b) whether the spacetime terms can be absent altogether. Each option leads somewhere different. Answering (a) yes licenses a search for a background-independent quantum theory; answering (b) yes removes a term the loop assumes throughout and leaves a system that cannot compose.
>
> **What an answer would look like, bound as an ordinary term (Formula step "Direction"):** a shape with two properties, *derived geometry* and *retained primitives*, plus a written refuter. The shape is the sought-for term; binding it makes it refusable by the same test as everything else.

**Question type before any figure line is chosen:** a **union compound**, not one hard question (Fig 9 :55 to :58). The two conditions, *no fixed background* and *no spacetime term*, overlap and bind in the intersections. A single lever that removes fixedness leaves the term-presence condition standing. They must be unbundled before a frame is searched (Fig 9 :54, *"And when the split lands on a union rather than a partition, unbundle before you search again."*).

## 3. Findings

### F1 — The question is a union compound, and the two conditions have opposite verdicts (governing finding, triangulated)

Fig 9 :55 to :58:

> **"a bundled problem is usually a union of overlapping conditions, not a list of separate ones."**
> **"A single lever clears the region where only one condition holds and leaves the intersections standing, because the second condition still binds."**
> **"a case in two conditions is not a harder case of either. It stops being a mixture and becomes a specific compound."**

Applied:

- Condition A, *the background is not fixed*. **Composable.** A fixed background is a particular settled arrangement promoted to an unupdatable given. The figures forbid that promotion.
- Condition B, *there is no spacetime term at all*. **Not composable.** Space and time are two of the six primitives the loop assumes throughout. Removing them removes a required term.

A single lever cannot clear both. Reporting one verdict for the whole question averages two different answers, which is the failure Fig 1 :22 names: *"Aggregating across slots is how a live signal disappears into an average."*

**Refuter:** show the two conditions are a partition rather than a union, so one lever clears both; or show that a composing system needs neither space nor time term. Either collapses the split.

### F2 — A fixed background is a confused anchor (triangulated across four figures)

Fig 2 :33: **"The anchor must not update."** Fig 2 :36: **"An anchor is legitimate when the question is constitutively about it."** The symbols table defines the ground as *"Whatever settles the question and does not change its answer because of what any frame thinks."*

A background that acts on everything but is never acted upon has the exact signature of the anchor in Fig 2 panel 3, except that it is being asked to do the work of a fixed given inside the theory rather than to settle a question from outside it. Kiefer and Peter (2022) quote Einstein in Ehlers' translation: *"It is contrary to the scientific mode of understanding to postulate a thing that acts, but which cannot be acted upon."* That is the definition of the thing GR removes.

Fig 13 :43 to :44: **"A continuum was the seventh and was removed for failing exactly this: it is a spectrum without gaps, or smoothness over space and time. A generating set must not contain what its own members produce."** Fig 13 :124 to :125: **"Smoothness, symmetry, chaos and discreteness are each what a particular composition leaves behind, and none is a seventh member."**

So a smooth fixed continuum is a derived remainder. Promoting it to a primitive is the category error. Fig 2 :34: *"If it does, panel 3 turns into panel 1: the anchor becomes another frame, everything drifts together, and the drift is coherent, which is why nobody notices."*

**Refuter:** derive the continuum from the other six and show it is indivisible, so it belongs in the minimum set; or show a ground that does update on frames and still settles the question.

### F3 — The background is a habitat, not a ground: removal is substitution (triangulated, Fig 11 plus Fig 2)

Fig 11 :73: **"The habitat is not the ground. The habitat is where actors come from and it updates; the ground is what settles, and it does not."** Fig 11 :61: **"Introduction is available; exclusion is not. You can add an actor and you cannot subtract the habitat, because contact is a fact about the world and does not consult you."** Fig 11 :66: **"Isolation is not removal of the habitat. It is substitution of one habitat for another."**

This is the sharpest figure-bearing answer to the literal wording "without a fixed spacetime." You can replace a fixed background with a dynamical one, a different topology, or a discrete causal order. You cannot subtract the field of contact. Fig 11 :70: *"A test harness is a substituted habitat. Which is why a synthetic solve proves wiring and never capability."*

**Refuter:** exhibit a composing quantum system whose habitat has been removed rather than substituted, and which still ranges against a ground. If contact is a fact about the world, this cannot be done; if it can, Fig 11's asymmetry is wrong.

### F4 — A quantum theory with literally no space or no time term fails the minimum viable set (triangulated, Fig 13 plus Symbols table plus Formula substrate)

Fig 13 :3: **"No system functions without any of them, none is built from the others, and everything else is a composition across the two kinds."** Fig 13 :41: **"Each is indivisible: no arrangement of the other five yields it."** The Symbols table lists space as *"Near, far, between, contained. One of the six the loop assumes throughout."* and time as *"The ordered succession of differences."*

A theory that removes both space and time is not a more radical background independence. It is a system with four of six terms, which the framework says does not function. Fig 13 :49: *"Read the established laws of thermodynamics as a test rather than a scorecard."* The point is the same: removing a member breaks a derivation, it does not deepen it.

The physics-facing form of this is Kiefer and Peter (2022): at the fundamental level *"spacetime (and with it, time) has disappeared, and only space remains."* Notice what survives: space. The continuum goes, the adjacency does not. That is exactly the figure prediction, arrived at in a different frame.

**Refuter:** demonstrate a composing system with no space and no time term that still settles against a ground; or derive space from the other five.

### F5 — Derived geometry is second order and legal, not an import (single-source for legality, specified)

Fig 12 :104: **"A settled molecule becomes an atom for whatever composes over it. So irreducible is a fact about a level and never about the world."** Fig 12 :103: **"Composition is recursive, and the level decides what is irreducible."**

GR is settled against the ground (decades of experiment), so its core can serve as an atom for whatever composes over it. A background-independent quantum theory is not required to import a new primitive; it is required to recompose an already settled one. Fig 6 :21: **"MINT composes inside the closure and can never add an atom."**

Anderson (2013) names the same move from the physics side: background independence includes *"the reconstruction of spacetime from space."* That is the figures' cross-kind composition stated in physics terms.

**Refuter:** show a settled molecule cannot serve as an atom at the next level; or show that reconstructing spacetime requires a seventh primitive.

### F6 — Naming a program is a binding, not a derivation (gap after re-census)

Fig 9 :27 to :28: **"describe it before you go looking, or any frame you pick will seem to fit."** Fig 9 :32: **"you get a kind of thing, never the answer itself."** Fig 6 :30: **"The description travels. Fails by being sharpened into terms only we can read. Cast it in effect terms."**

The figures license the shape (derived geometry, retained primitives). They do not select loop quantum gravity, causal dynamical triangulations, causal sets, or any other program. That selection is a binding from the physics frame onto held atoms. Fig 8 :50 to :52: *"Convergent derivation and adopted import look identical in the contents. Only the record of where each came from separates them."*

World corroboration is explicit: Rovelli (2008) says of LQG, *"Direct or indirect experimental support is lacking. This is the case, unfortunately, for all present approaches to quantum gravity."* The SEP (2024) says *"there is no solid, unproblematic definition of background structure (and therefore background independence and dependence) on the table."* Both make the point that the program-level verdict is not settled.

**Refuter:** a figure line that names a specific quantization program, or a ground reading that settles which program is correct. Until one appears, the program-level verdict is `gap`, stated plainly. Fig 13 :134: *"Unreachable is a verdict here, not a silence."*

### F7 — The mint is inert at the level of a specific answer (Fig 5, triangulated with Fig 6)

Fig 5 :41: **"Any one of them at zero stops the mint entirely. Which is why a system that is minting nothing may have nothing wrong with its minting."** `THE_FORMULA.md` step 3 names the guards: SUPPORT `|R⁺_s| > 0`, REACHABILITY `φ ∈ closure(Γ)`, NOVELTY `φ ∉ atoms(Γ)`.

For a claimed background-independent quantum theory:
- **SUPPORT:** the residual is real (the contact failure between GR and QM), so this factor passes at the shape level.
- **REACHABILITY:** the two conditions are in the closure of the six plus the bonds, so the shape passes.
- **NOVELTY:** a specific program is not novel relative to `atoms(Γ)`; it is a rearrangement of held terms. This factor is zero for the program-level claim.
- **SETTLEMENT:** no ground reading. Fig 5 :32, *"then check it against the ground, then carry it up."* No Planck-scale instrument pays for it.

So the absence of a program-level mint is not a defect in the machinery. Fig 8 :39: *"So a search that finds nothing is never a proof of absence."*

**Refuter:** supply a program-level term that adds a genuine atom to Γ and is settled at the ground. Then the mint fires and this finding is withdrawn.

## 4. Pre-registered refuter

Written before any outreach or conclusion, per Seat Map :287: *"a falsifier written before the run is a design instrument, not only an honesty device."*

The shape claim is refuted if any of the following holds:

1. One of the six terms is derived from the other five (breaks F4 and Fig 13 indivisibility).
2. A composing system is demonstrated with no space term and no time term that still settles against a ground (breaks F4 and the minimum viable set).
3. The continuum is derived from the six and then shown to be an indivisible seventh member (breaks F2 and Fig 13 :43 to :44).
4. A measurement shows that quantum theory requires a fixed background in principle, so non-fixedness is not composable (breaks F2).
5. The two conditions in F1 are shown to be a partition rather than a union, so one lever clears both.

If any fires, the shape is withdrawn. Until one is run, no part of this may be labelled `grounded`.
