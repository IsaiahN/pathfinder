### Q1 — Union or partition?

- Fig 9 :55: **"a bundled problem is usually a union of overlapping conditions, not a list of separate ones."**
- Fig 9 :56: **"A single lever clears the region where only one condition holds and leaves the intersections standing, because the second condition still binds."**

- The *Library of Babel* gives the same instruction in search terms: *"Perhaps your problem was a bundle or conflation of several problems masquerading as one? You will need to search all of those problems individually to find their answers and then combine them in a satisfactory way."*
- ⇒ **Union**, not partition. Verdicts stay separate and are never averaged. Fig 1 :22: *"Aggregating across slots is how a live signal disappears into an average."*

### Q3 — Does emergent spacetime contradict indivisible space and time? (re-census)

- The apparent conflict: Fig 13 :41 says **"Each is indivisible: no arrangement of the other five yields it."** Anderson (2013) and the LQG literature say spacetime is reconstructed or built by the states of the theory.
- Fig 13 :43 to :44: **"A continuum was the seventh and was removed for failing exactly this: it is a spectrum without gaps, or smoothness over space and time. A generating set must not contain what its own members produce."**
- Fig 13 :124 to :125: **"Smoothness, symmetry, chaos and discreteness are each what a particular composition leaves behind, and none is a seventh member."**
- Symbols table: space is *"Near, far, between, contained. One of the six the loop assumes throughout."* Time is *"The ordered succession of differences."*
- Kiefer and Peter (2022), from the physics frame: *"Therefore, at the fundamental level, spacetime (and with it, time) has disappeared, and only space remains."*
- ⇒ **No contradiction after re-census.** The indivisible primitive is space as adjacency and time as ordered succession. The thing that emerges is the four-dimensional smooth continuum, which is explicitly a removed seventh and a composed remainder. Different objects. The figures README's rule applies: a reading that makes figures contradict each other has misidentified its domain.
- This is the same shape as the exemplar's Fig 13 versus Rule 2 re-census in `QMGR-0037`: that one separated primitives from second-order derivatives; this one separates primitives from the continuum remainder.
- **Falsifier:** derive the continuum from the other five and then show it is indivisible, so it belongs in the minimum set. Then the re-census fails and the conflict is real.

### Q4 — Is the fixed background a ground, a habitat, or a molecule?

- Fig 2 :33: **"The anchor must not update."** A fixed background never updates. That is the anchor's signature.
- Fig 2 :36: **"An anchor is legitimate when the question is constitutively about it."** A background inside a theory is not the thing that settles the theory; experiment is. So the fixedness is illegitimate.
- Fig 11 :73: **"The habitat is not the ground. The habitat is where actors come from and it updates; the ground is what settles, and it does not."**
- Fig 12 :104: **"A settled molecule becomes an atom for whatever composes over it. So irreducible is a fact about a level and never about the world."**
- ⇒ A fixed background is a settled molecule wearing the anchor's clothes. It is a habitat that has been mistaken for a ground. The repair is not to delete it but to return it to its proper role: derived, updatable, substitutable. Fig 11 :66: **"Isolation is not removal of the habitat. It is substitution of one habitat for another."**
- Kiefer and Peter (2022) quote Einstein in Ehlers' translation: *"It is contrary to the scientific mode of understanding to postulate a thing that acts, but which cannot be acted upon."* Convergent derivation from a different frame, but still one evidence pool for this simulated run.
- **Falsifier:** show a background that updates on the theory's own states and still functions as the thing that settles the theory. Then it is a ground and the fixedness is legitimate.

## CONCLUSION

The question is a **union compound**. The two conditions have opposite verdicts: non-fixedness is composable, term-absence is not. Dependency order runs refuse, split, derive, then name provisionally. The apparent contradiction between emergent spacetime and indivisible space/time dissolves on re-census: the primitive is adjacency and succession, the continuum is a derived remainder and a removed seventh. The fixed background is a settled molecule that has taken the anchor's office, and the repair is substitution rather than removal.

This rescues what is defensible in the background-independence claim (geometry is derived, no unupdatable given) without the part that fails (the literal removal of the spacetime terms, or the promotion of a program to a figure-derived answer). The program slot stays open, and is recorded as a binding rather than a derivation.
