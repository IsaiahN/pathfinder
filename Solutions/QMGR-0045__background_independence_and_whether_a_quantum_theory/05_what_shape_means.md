## 1. A shape is a constraint, not a filler

The figures README states the limit: the framework returns *"what a missing thing would have to look like (**a shape, not a name**)."* Fig 9 :32: *"you get a kind of thing, never the answer itself."* Fig 13 :134: *"Unreachable is a verdict here, not a silence."*

From *The Library of Babel*: *"You can cut your search short if the set of books returned is small and descriptive enough that it provides a general shape of the answer that satisfies your question."*

So a **shape** is the narrowed form of an answer you can state before you can name it. It constrains; it does not fill. For this question the shape is:

> *derived geometry, retained primitives, no fixed anchor, program slot open*

That is a shape. *"Loop quantum gravity is the answer"* is a name, and a name the figures do not supply.

## 2. How a shape is obtained

- *Library of Babel*: use the vocabulary you already have that is *closest* to the thing, look for recurring new terms that overlap or correlate with it, recurse. *"With each new term gained, you narrow the space and deepen your vocabulary."*
- Fig 6 :30: *"The description travels. Fails by being sharpened into terms only we can read. Cast it in effect terms."* Fig 9 :30 to :31: *"your words are borrowed too, so describe the effect rather than the cause."*
- *How Intelligence Actually Works*: *"The framework if applied effectively provides us a tool that cuts down to the shape of a thing, problem or question based on the given information available at this point in time."*
- *Library of Babel* on stopping: *"You will know that you are done because the finish line will also be the starting place, as there is nowhere else to search and nothing else that can describe the answer better."*

Applied here: begin with the effect term *"a structure that acts but is not acted upon"* (background), find the closest held terms (anchor, habitat, continuum remainder), and recurse until only the union split remains.

## 3. What licenses a name

Only two things, both outside the shape itself:

1. **The ground settles it.** Formula step 5: *"the ground settles it. Verified only by what pays."* The README: the framework *"stops at the limit of what the available instruments can distinguish, and records the lineage so the stop is honest."*
2. **A provenanced import from a frame whose closure differs** (Fig 6 :23, Fig 8 :50), with a source gate and a shadow test.

*How Intelligence Actually Works* on the practitioner: *"If a practitioner traverses the framework and supplies measurements at each level, then the output will eventually converge toward a perceptibility that is indiscernible."* And the limit immediately after: *"The recursion converges toward indiscernibility, and indiscernibility is not identity."* Agreement with a program by experts in that program is not a ground reading.

The six are a visible choice, not nature's own list. From *How Intelligence Actually Works*: *"we can safely assume that if this is truly the minimum set, it should be able to compose everything we can think of and measure."* That is a conditional, not a discovery claim. Naming one of the six as *the* answer to a slot turns a visible choice into an invisible one.

## 4. Why the literal "no spacetime" reading is the specific forbidden error

*The Hard Problem is About Source* gives the mechanism. Metamerism: an instrument reads green and *"cannot comment on the source."* One reading, many possible sources. *"If several paths create the same behavior, then behavior is a projection with lossy information."* And: *"An instrument that can't distinguish these cases demonstrates its own inadequacy, it cannot claim anything substantial about what is being measured or what causes it."* It was the **spectrometer**, not an argument, that resolved it: *"It was proven wrong not by argument, but by the arrival of a better instrument."*

Applied here: "spacetime" is a single reading over several possible compositions. Reading it as one primitive is the metamerism error. The correct move is to split it. *How Intelligence Actually Works* states the split directly:

> *"SPACETIME would break rule 1 (they are both states). The only way SPACE and TIME could be joined via this framework is if one or more derivatives were chained in between their composition..."*
>
> *"SPACETIME must have some structure kind smuggled in between to make composition possible..."*
>
> *"it wouldn't be SPACETIME but SPACE(???)TIME (a testable theory) and we cannot say which state composed first, and we don't know if the order mattered..."*

And the continuum is explicitly second order: *"some second and third order concepts would naturally fall out of composition of the two kinds such as smoothness, discreteness, continuums and other things needed to fill in the gaps (???) of the previous chain."* That is the essay-level corroboration of F2 and F3.

*The Recursive Self-Improvement Trap*: *"the output is only as good as the validator. The agent, or the tool, or the frame, or the theory, cannot validate itself."*

## 5. The narrowed answer, and a correction to my own reading

**What licenses a particular background-independent program?** Nothing in the framework. The framework returns a shape: derived geometry, retained primitives, no fixed anchor. A particular program is licensed only by the ground settling a minted term, or by a provenanced import with a shadow test. Until one of those, the program name is the searcher's own contribution and must be recorded as such.

**Correction.** I initially read the README's *"shape, not a name"* as forbidding any selection among held terms. That overstates it. The phrase governs a **missing** primitive, one not held in Γ. Space and time are already among the six; selecting them as retained primitives is a binding onto held atoms, not the invention of a seventh. The genuine prohibition is on promoting a derived continuum (smoothness over space and time) to a primitive and on importing a program as if it added an atom (Fig 6 :21, *"MINT composes inside the closure and can never add an atom"*). Both the original claim and the correction are recorded so neither is quietly lost.

## 6. Two consequences for outreach

**Prestige is not action.** From *The Recursive Self-Improvement Trap*: *"Prestige must never be allowed to validate an action directly... Prestige can fund an action. It can give an action a budget to be tried. But it cannot settle an action. Only the ground can settle it. The Ground Maintainer must be blind to prestige."* A campaign that gathers prestigious names to agree that background independence is required is the named collapse, not corroboration. The legitimate ask is to give the claim a budget to be tried and let the ground settle it.

**My own audit is a simulation, not a ground reading.** Same essay: *"LLMs can quickly simulate an approximate answer/output with the formula found in the architecture post... However, the truly testable output would need to be derived from the real architecture or working the formula yourself."* So `01` is a simulated derivation, checked for internal consistency against the figures. It is **not** a test of the claim and must be labelled as such. It cannot settle anything it reports on.
