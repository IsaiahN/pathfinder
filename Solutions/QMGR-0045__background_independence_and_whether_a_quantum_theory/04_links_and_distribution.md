## Three things in the literature that bear directly on this question

1. **The definition is itself unsettled.** The SEP says there is *"no solid, unproblematic definition of background structure"* and that *"background independence cannot be helpfully used to distinguish the approaches"* until one is agreed. So a single yes/no answer to the question as phrased is not available from the world literature either. That supports the figure-derived union split in F1: first split the question, then answer each condition.
2. **Einstein's own formulation maps onto the figure anchor.** Kiefer and Peter quote *"a thing that acts, but which cannot be acted upon"* as the thing background independence removes. Fig 2 :33 says *"The anchor must not update."* A fixed background has the anchor's signature without the anchor's office. That is the precise structural error F2 names.
3. **The strongest world-level conflict is recorded, not smoothed.** Casadio et al conclude that *"diffeomorphism invariance and background independence, in the extended sense of the absence of any fixed metric structure at any level, cannot coexist."* That conflicts with the stronger rhetorical claims that background independence is simply required. Per the method, the conflict sends us back to the domain census. It sharpens the result: the figures also require six terms at every level (F4), so "no structure at any level" overreaches, while "no fixed background" remains composable. The verdict is unchanged.
