# The Tether's answer on background independence

**Mode: `specified` on the structural prediction, `general` on the shape, not `grounded`.** This is a shape and a route, not a result. Simulated derivation checked against the figures (`05_what_shape_means.md` §6), not a ground reading.

## 1. The residual it answers

GR is background independent: the metric is solved for, not given. QM and QFT need a fixed background to define states, evolution and the inner product. The contact failure is a **union compound**, not one hard question (Fig 9 :55 to :58). Two conditions are bound together: *the background is not fixed* and *the spacetime terms are absent*. They have opposite answers and must not be averaged.

## 2. The shape

1. **Non-fixedness is composable.** A fixed smooth continuum is a derived remainder, not a primitive. Fig 13 :43 to :44: *"A continuum was the seventh and was removed for failing exactly this: it is a spectrum without gaps, or smoothness over space and time."* Fig 13 :124 to :125: *"Smoothness, symmetry, chaos and discreteness are each what a particular composition leaves behind, and none is a seventh member."* A settled arrangement can become an atom for the next level (Fig 12 :104), but it cannot be promoted to an unchanging given (Fig 2 :33, *"The anchor must not update."*).
2. **Term-absence is not composable.** Space and time are two of the six indivisible primitives the loop assumes throughout. Fig 13 :41: *"Each is indivisible: no arrangement of the other five yields it."* A theory with neither term is a composing system with four of six terms, which the framework says does not function (Fig 13 :3). Kiefer and Peter (2022) reach the same place from physics: *"spacetime (and with it, time) has disappeared, and only space remains."*
3. **Removal is substitution.** Fig 11 :66: *"Isolation is not removal of the habitat. It is substitution of one habitat for another."* Fig 11 :61: *"Introduction is available; exclusion is not."* You can replace a fixed background with a dynamical one, a different topology or a discrete causal order. You cannot subtract the field of contact. The background is a habitat that has been mistaken for a ground (Fig 11 :73).
4. **No program is named.** The figures return a shape, not a name. Fig 9 :32: *"you get a kind of thing, never the answer itself."* Loop quantum gravity, causal dynamical triangulations and causal sets are bindings from the physics frame onto held atoms. They are neither figure-derived nor ground-settled here.
5. **Order runs refuse, split, derive, name.** Refuse the literal reading first (Formula step 2); split the union before searching (Fig 9 :54); settle each first-order molecule before the second-order comparison (Fig 12 :104).

## 3. What it explicitly does not say

- Not that spacetime is nothing. It is a derivative with a structure in the slot; the slot stays `???`.
- Not that a particular quantum gravity program is correct, and not that one is ruled out by these figures.
- Not that background independence has a settled definition. The SEP (2024) says *"there is no solid, unproblematic definition of background structure (and therefore background independence and dependence) on the table."*
- Not a derivation of quantum theory from GR, and no new primitive.
- Not `grounded`. Rovelli (2008): *"Direct or indirect experimental support is lacking. This is the case, unfortunately, for all present approaches to quantum gravity."*

## 4. What would settle it — the ground

An instrument that discriminates among the compositions that produce the same "background independence" reading. From *The Hard Problem is About Source*: an instrument that reads green *"cannot comment on the source"*; the spectrometer resolved it. Here:

- **Definition test:** does a single, formally correct predicate of background independence exist that all parties accept? The SEP says the definition is still open. A definition is a convention (Fig 10 :47, *"a convention that decides an outcome has stopped being one"*), so it cannot by itself settle a verdict.
- **Discriminator test:** is there an observable that distinguishes a derived geometry from a fixed one at the scale where both theories apply? That would name the slot.
- **Term test:** exhibit a composing quantum system with no space term and no time term that still settles against a ground. That would break F4.
- **Indivisibility test:** derive the continuum from the other five and show it is indivisible. That would break F2.
- **Standing falsifiers** from `01` §4: one of the six derived from the other five; the conditions shown to be a partition; a measurement showing quantum theory requires a fixed background in principle.

## 5. The honest stop

**Unreached, not unreachable.** The six-term set is a visible guess, not nature's own list. *How Intelligence Actually Works* puts it conditionally: *"we can safely assume that if this is truly the minimum set, it should be able to compose everything we can think of and measure."* The answer stops where the instruments stop and records the lineage so the stop is checkable. Fig 8 :39: *"So a search that finds nothing is never a proof of absence."* Fig 13 :134: *"Unreachable is a verdict here, not a silence."* *Indiscernibility is not identity.*

## 6. One-paragraph version for outreach

> The Tether does not pick a quantum gravity program and does not claim a final theory. It says the question is two questions in one bundle. A background that is not fixed is composable: the smooth continuum is a derived remainder, and a settled geometry can be reused without becoming an unchanging given. A theory with no spacetime term at all is not composable: space and time are two of the six primitives the loop assumes throughout, and the habitat can be substituted but not subtracted. The fixed background is a settled molecule wearing the anchor's clothes. What the framework offers is the shape any candidate must have, four falsifiers written in advance, and the instruments that would close it. The program slot stays open until the ground settles it.
