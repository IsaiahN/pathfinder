## 1. The claim, in one paragraph

The question "can a quantum theory be formulated without a fixed spacetime" is a union of two conditions with opposite answers. Non-fixedness is composable: a smooth fixed continuum is a derived remainder, and a settled geometry can become an atom for the next level without becoming an unchanging given. Term-absence is not composable: space and time are two of the six primitives the loop assumes throughout, and the habitat can be substituted but not subtracted. The fixed background is a settled molecule that has taken the anchor's office. No quantum gravity program is named by the figures; that slot stays open as a binding from the physics frame, provenanced as such.
