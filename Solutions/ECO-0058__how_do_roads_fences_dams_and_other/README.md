## Key verdict in one line

The question is a **union**, not one thing, so there is no single averaged effect of "infrastructure" on "movement." The figure that governs is Fig 11 (The Habitat): the habitat is enumerated rather than composed, an actor's valence is relative to the residual, contact cascades outward, and isolation is substitution rather than removal. A road is a directional filter (Fig 4), not a wall. Mitigation is addition, not subtraction, so connectivity is permanent work rather than a solved state. Verdicts are kept per barrier type, per taxon and per process, and the ground is measurement, never model agreement.

## What is deliberately not here

- No personal emails of named individuals.
- No ranking of people by how likely they are to agree.
- No bulk-send plan.

Three figure-grounded reasons:

1. **A room does not upgrade the label.** Seat Map :283: *"A room reaching a conclusion without the ground is agreement, and agreement among frames is a weighted average of influence. Adding people raises the vocabulary and does not add an anchor."* A list selected for receptiveness manufactures exactly the correlation that would make agreement worthless.
2. **Correlation has valence.** Seat Map :163: *"High correlation is disqualifying for a verdict and efficient for a convention."*
3. **A verifier that can reconstruct the claim cannot verify it.** Fig 10 :42. The first contact carries the residual and the refuter, never the framework's interiors.

Legally: scraping personal emails for unsolicited bulk outreach breaches GDPR/CAN-SPAM style rules in many jurisdictions. Public opt-in institutional channels avoid that failure and the structural one.
