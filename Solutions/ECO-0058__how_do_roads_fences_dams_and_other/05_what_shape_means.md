## 1. A shape is a constraint, not a filler

The canonical figures README (local) states the limit: the framework returns *"what a missing thing would have to look like (a shape, not a name)"*. Fig 9 :32: **"you get a kind of thing, never the answer itself"**. Fig 13 (local extract): *"It can say the answer is out of reach with the library as it stands... It cannot say which primitive is missing, because naming it would mean already holding it."*

From *The Library of Babel*: *"This means that you can cut your search short if the set of books returned is small and descriptive enough that it provides a general shape of the answer that satisfies your question."*

So a **shape** is the narrowed form of an answer that can be stated before it can be filled in. It constrains; it does not substitute. For this question:

- A shape: *for each barrier and each residual, enumerate the contact field outward, treat the barrier as a directional filter, name the substituted habitat, and report the per-slot residual.*
- Not a shape, a filler: *"roads reduce connectivity by 30 percent."* A number with no slot, no direction and no cascade.

## 2. The question is a bundle; the essay names the remedy

*The Library of Babel*: *"Perhaps your problem was a bundle or conflation of several problems masquerading as one? You will need to search all of those problems individually to find their answers and then combine them in a satisfactory way."*

This is the essay form of Fig 9 :54-55, which is why §1 of the audit refuses a single averaged effect of "infrastructure." The roads case, the fence case and the dam case are searched separately, then recombined. The intersections are a compound, not a harder road.

*The Library of Babel* also gives the stopping rule: you are done when *"the finish line will also be the starting place"* and *"the mystery of The Library of Babel is that you enter as a researcher looking for a book, and if successful, you will leave as the author."* Here that means: by the time you can describe the right shape of a connectivity study, you have composed the study design. What remains is to run it.

## 3. How a shape is obtained: effect terms, proximity, 1:1 contact

- *Library of Babel*: use the vocabulary you already have that is closest to the thing, look for recurring new terms that overlap or correlate with it, recurse. *"With each new term gained, you narrow the space by definition and deepen your vocabulary."*
- Fig 6 :30 (local extract): *"The description travels. Fails by being sharpened into terms only we can read. Cast it in effect terms."* Fig 9 :30-31: *"your words are borrowed too, so describe the effect rather than the cause."*
- *Decoding Tacit Knowledge*: *"When contact isn't possible, the best you can send is a plain description of what you'd expect to see, because anyone can check that against the world."* This is the reason the outreach email leads with what a reader would observe, not with the framework.
- *How a Society of Agents Would Work*: *"A group is only worth having while its members stay different."* and *"The group's agreement is never the verdict."* So the public venues in `03` are places to find refuters, not a panel to collect endorsements.

## 4. What licenses a name, and the metamerism limit

Two things, both outside the shape: the ground settles a term, or a provenanced import from a frame whose closure differs (Fig 6 :23; Fig 8).

*The Hard Problem is About Source* gives the mechanism: an instrument reads green but *"the instrument is speaking only about what it measured, it cannot comment on the source."* And: *"If several paths create the same behavior, then behavior is a projection with lossy information."* The resolution was not an argument: *"It was proven wrong not by argument, but by the arrival of a better instrument."*

Applied here: a crossing count reads "passage." It cannot comment on the source of that passage, that is, whether the barrier was a filter, a mortality sink or a relocated population. Several mechanisms give the same count. What is owed is a source-discriminating instrument (before/after control, genetics, telemetry), not another connectivity index.

## 5. Correction to my own first reading: "habitat" is NOT the ecological niche

This is the most important honesty point for this question, so it is recorded rather than smoothed.

My first pass treated Fig 11's **habitat** as if it were the ecologist's habitat. It is not. The canonical essay *Arrival of the Fittest: The Tether Architecture*, in its section **"Three mappings offered and declined,"** explicitly declines this mapping:

> *"The habitat as an ecological niche. A niche is defined by an organism's relation to its environment. A habitat here is defined by contact with a residual, and the residual moves so the same actors constitute a different habitat when the question changes. The resemblance is real and the definitions do not align, which is why the figure says enumerated, never composed rather than borrowing the ecological term outright."*

Consequences, recorded:

1. Fig 11 governs this question **structurally**, not by definitional identity with ecology. The shape transfers; the word does not.
2. The same road is a different habitat depending on which residual is tracked. That is not a metaphor; it is the declined mapping made explicit.
3. The figure's own DDT example and its Jones/Lawton/Shachak citation are *instances* in the essay's citation scheme, not borrowed definitions. They do not make Fig 11 an ecology paper.
4. The independent ecology sources in `09_watson_answer.md` are therefore genuine corroboration (different evidence pool), not restatements of the figure. But their agreement with Fig 11 must not be read as the figure citing them.
5. The canonical essay also notes that several Fig 11 claims carry **no citation** and that *"Absence of a citation is not a claim of originality. It means a search was made and returned nothing, and Rule 3 on Figure 8 says exactly what that is worth: a search that finds nothing is never a proof of absence."* So no priority claim is made here either.

This correction does **not** retract the figure verdict. It narrows what kind of verdict it is: a structural shape for any residual in contact with a field, applied to an ecological case.

## 6. Why this audit cannot settle the ecology question

From *The Recursive Self-Improvement Trap*: *"the output is only as good as the validator. The agent, or the tool, or the frame, or the theory, cannot validate itself."* And: *"You may all agree and be wrong."*

From the same essay: *"LLMs can quickly simulate an approximate answer/output with the formula found in the architecture post. It will often be better than the generic outputs that you will usually get otherwise too. However, the truly testable output would need to be derived from the real architecture or working the formula yourself."*

From *Arrival of the Fittest*: an LLM prompted with the figures gives *"a plausible simulated output to an issue in any given domain that the LLM has knowledge of,"* and the result *"could never be verified if the solutions presented were not contrived to fit the formula."*

Therefore `01_figure_grounded_audit.md`, `06` and `07` are **simulated derivations checked for internal consistency against the figures**. They state a shape and its refuters. They are not field measurements and cannot settle which barrier alters which process at which site.

## 7. Two consequences for outreach

**Prestige is not action.** *The Recursive Self-Improvement Trap*: *"Prestige can fund an action. It can give an action a budget to be tried. But it cannot settle an action. Only the ground can settle it. The Ground Maintainer must be blind to prestige."* A list of famous ecologists who agree with the shape is the named collapse. The legitimate ask is: give the claim a budget to be tried against a site, and let the ground settle it.

**Rates, not totals.** *How a Society of Agents Would Work*: *"Use the success rate per try, not the total"* and *"a method that is brilliant in one setting must not be drowned by an average across all of them. Figure 1: Aggregating across slots is how a live signal disappears into an average."* This is the essay's version of F7. Ask a checker for the per-slot result, never for an overall verdict on infrastructure.

## 8. A directly relevant sentence from the essays

*How Intelligence Actually Works*, on habitat alteration, states the ecology case almost verbatim:

> *"This is also why, when altering a habitat by unintentionally introducing a predatory threat into the ecosystem, or disturbing the balance by adding or removing vital resource points, you can inadvertently create major unpredictable consequences downstream."*

That is F3 and F5 in the essay's own words. It is framework-author text, so it is not an independent witness; it is the reason the derivation was run in this direction. The independent witnesses are the road-ecology, habitat-fragmentation and fish-passage pages in `09_watson_answer.md`.
