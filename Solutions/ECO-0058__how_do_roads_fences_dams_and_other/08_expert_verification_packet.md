# Expert verification packet — the Tether on infrastructure and ecological movement

**What this is:** a claim in a form a transportation ecologist, landscape ecologist, hydrologist or wildlife manager can check. Mode, derivation and refuters are stated before the conclusion, so a reader knows what to look for and what would settle it.

**Mode: `general` on the shape, `specified` on the ecology-facing predictions, not `grounded`.** No site measurement is claimed. This is a shape and a route, not a field result. It is a simulated derivation checked for internal consistency against the figures; it cannot settle the question it reports on (`05_what_shape_means.md` §6).

## 1. The claim, in one paragraph

For each barrier type and each residual, the habitat is enumerated rather than derived from the noun "infrastructure": list everything in contact with the movement or process, then what contacts those, outward until the cascade stops mattering. An actor's effect is relative to the residual, so there is no verdict averaged across species. A road, fence or dam is a directional filter whose zero-permeability limit is what the literature calls a barrier. First-order counts (roadkill, passage) can miss a third-order process outcome. A crossing or fishway substitutes a habitat; it does not restore the old one, and mitigation is addition rather than subtraction, which makes it permanent work. The question is a union of overlapping conditions, not one thing. The framework names this shape and refuses to name a design.

## 2. Three checkable sub-claims

### C1 — framework-internal; checkable by reading Figure 11 and Figure 4

- **Statement:** valence is relative to the residual and the barrier is a filter with a direction, not a wall. Therefore no single averaged infrastructure effect is licensed and no barrier is intrinsically "a barrier" for all taxa.
- **Source, verbatim:** Fig 11 :49 **"An actor's valence is relative to the residual, not a property it carries."** Fig 11 :50 **"The same actor is a benefactor to one residual and a malfactor to another, so mutualism and parasitism are relations rather than kinds."** Fig 4 :21 **"The membrane is the boundary between the sizes, and the rule about what may cross it. Not a wall: a filter with a direction."** Fig 1 :22 **"Aggregating across slots is how a live signal disappears into an average."**
- **What to check:** is the shape claim consistent with these four lines? Does the empirical record show a single structure with one sign of effect across taxa and both travel directions?
- **Refuter:** one barrier shown to have the same sign of effect for all taxa, life stages and directions, with no live slot residual; or a barrier equally permeable in both directions for all taxa; or a single averaged metric that predicts per-species outcomes on withheld sites.

### C2 — ecology-facing; checkable against the reader's own field data

- **Statement:** first-order scoping misses the residual that matters, and isolation is substitution, not removal.
- **Source, verbatim:** Fig 11 :52-53 **"And contact cascades. Scoping the habitat to direct contact is how DDT reached the eagles: first-order enumeration, second-order effect, and the residual that mattered was three steps out."** Fig 11 :66 **"Isolation is not removal of the habitat. It is substitution of one habitat for another."** Fig 11 :67 **"Move the residual and you must reproduce everything its goal required, and you inherit the new habitat's actors, which you did not choose."**
- **What to check:** take a crossing, fence or passage you know. Does direct mortality/passage count predict the population or process outcome, or is there a second or third-order residual? After mitigation, did you restore the old habitat or substitute a new one, and did you inherit actors you did not choose?
- **Refuter:** a measured case where direct counts fully predict the third-order outcome with no residual, using an instrument that would have detected the cascade; or a demonstrated restoration of full pre-barrier contact with no substitution, verified before and after.

### C3 — framework-internal, with an engineering consequence

- **Statement:** mitigation is introduction, not subtraction, so it is permanent work. And the union must be unbundled before it is searched.
- **Source, verbatim:** Fig 11 :61 **"The seat's power is asymmetric. Introduction is available; exclusion is not."** Fig 11 :63 **"Which is why a managed ecosystem is permanent work rather than a solved state."** Fig 9 :54 **"And when the split lands on a union rather than a partition, unbundle before you search again."** Fig 9 :55 **"A bundled problem is usually a union of overlapping conditions, not a list of separate ones. A single lever clears the region where only one condition holds and leaves the intersections standing, because the second condition still binds."**
- **What to check:** do the interventions you know persist as maintenance regimes? Does removing one barrier type clear the whole problem, or do road-plus-fence-plus-dam intersections remain?
- **Refuter:** a mitigation that subtracts contact cleanly and stays effective without maintenance over more than one season; or one lever shown to clear all regions of the union, including intersections, on measured outcomes.

## 3. What is explicitly not claimed

- Not a single averaged effect of infrastructure on movement.
- Not that Fig 11's "habitat" is the ecologist's habitat or niche. The architecture essay explicitly declines that mapping (see `05` §5). The figure is used structurally.
- Not that crossings, fishways, fences or corridors fail. Their effects are site-specific and are ground questions.
- Not that any particular design is licensed by the figures. The framework returns a shape, not a name (Fig 9 :32).
- Not that removing a barrier restores the pre-barrier habitat.
- Not `grounded`. Nothing here is asserted as settled by field measurement. The derivation is a simulation.

## 5. What would settle it

A source-discriminating instrument, not a further argument. From *The Hard Problem is About Source*: an instrument that reads green *"cannot comment on the source"*; the spectrometer resolved it. Here:

1. **Per-slot before/after control:** passage, survival and gene flow per taxon and per direction, not a pooled index.
2. **Source discrimination:** an instrument that separates a directional filter from a mortality sink from a relocated population when all three give the same crossing count.
3. **Cascade reach:** measure the third-order process and test whether first-order counts predict it with no residual.
4. **Substitution audit:** list what the mitigation failed to reproduce and what it unintentionally introduced; recheck after a full season.

## 6. What an expert can check in twenty minutes
