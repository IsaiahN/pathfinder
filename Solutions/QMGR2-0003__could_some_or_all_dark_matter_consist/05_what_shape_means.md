## 1. A shape is a constraint, not a filler

The figures README states the limit: the framework returns *"what a missing thing would have to look like (a shape, not a name)."* Fig 9 :32: *"you get a kind of thing, never the answer itself"*. Fig 13 :134: *"It can say the answer is out of reach with the library as it stands... Unreachable is a verdict here, not a silence,"*.

From *The Library of Babel*: *"you can cut your search short if the set of books returned is small and descriptive enough that it provides a general shape of the answer that satisfies your question."*

So a **shape** is the narrowed form of an answer you can state before you can name the thing that fills it. It constrains; it does not fill. For this question the shape is:

> a source term with a formation mechanism and a mass function, reproducing the gravitational readings in every claimed slot, and returning a reading at least one rival source does not predict.

The name "primordial black hole" is a binding from the physics frame. The shape does not produce that name.

## 3. What licenses a name

Only two things, both outside the shape itself:

1. **The ground settles it.** Formula step 5: *"SETTLE the ground settles it. Verified only by what pays. A gate is not the ground."* The README: the framework *"stops at the limit of what the available instruments can distinguish, and records the lineage so the stop is honest."*
2. **A provenanced import from a frame whose closure differs** (Fig 6 :23, Fig 8 :50 to :52), with a source gate and a shadow test.

For this question, Hawking evaporation, microlensing, the CMB and the merger population are the instruments that could pay for the name. Until one of them returns a source-specific reading, any name is the searcher's own contribution and must be recorded as such. This is exactly why the outreach packet asks what would refute the shape before showing the theory: a failure is a ground response, not an opinion.

## 4. Why source-discrimination is the governing error here

*The Hard Problem is About Source* gives the mechanism. Metamerism: an instrument reads green and *"cannot comment on the source"*. One green light, or yellow plus blue, or RGB, or CMYK, or a scheme not yet discovered, can all give the same reading. The reading does not determine the source. *"If several paths create the same behavior, then behavior is a projection with lossy information."* It was the spectrometer, not an argument, that resolved it.

Dark matter has the same structure. Rotation curves, lensing, the Bullet Cluster offset and the CMB return gravitational readings. PBHs, WIMPs, axions and modified gravity (in the systems where it applies) are candidate sources. Clowe et al. 2006 (*"8-sigma significance spatial offset"*) removed one class of reading, but the remaining candidates still give the same readings. What is owed is a source-discriminating instrument, not a name.

The Stanford Encyclopedia of Philosophy entry on underdetermination (2009, revised 2023) is a low-overlap corroboration from philosophy of science: *"the evidence available to us at a given time may be insufficient to determine what beliefs we should hold in response to it."* It does not decide the verdict; the figures do.

## 5. Q  -  what licenses a particular source for the residual?

**Nothing in the framework.** The framework returns a shape. A particular source is licensed only by the ground settling a minted term, or by a provenanced import with a shadow test. Until one of those, the name is a binding from the physics frame and must be recorded as such. The honest form for this question is: "a source term with a mass function and a discriminator, slot by slot", not "PBH".

## 5b. Correction to my own §3 and §4

I initially overstated the "shape, not a name" rule as if it forbade naming primordial black holes at all. It does not. The README's phrase is *"what a **missing** thing would have to look like (a shape, not a name)."* The subject is a missing primitive, one not held in Γ. PBH is not a missing primitive of the physics library; the candidate is already available as a composition of known physics (inflationary perturbations plus general relativity). Naming PBH is therefore a **binding** from the physics frame onto an available candidate, not the invention of a new primitive. What remains wrong is presenting that binding as a figure derivation, or claiming it moves the closure wall (Fig 6 :21: *"MINT composes inside the closure and can never add an atom."*).

My §4 metamerism point is **not** retracted. The readings still do not determine the source, and an instrument that cannot discriminate sources demonstrates its own inadequacy. Both the narrowed naming rule and the discrimination requirement are recorded so neither is quietly lost. Triangulated (README + Fig 6 + Fig 9).
