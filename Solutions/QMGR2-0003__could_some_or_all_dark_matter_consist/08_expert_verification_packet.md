# Expert verification packet  -  the Tether on primordial black hole dark matter

**What this is:** a claim in a form a cosmologist or astrophysicist can check. Mode, derivation and refuters are stated before the conclusion, so a reader knows what to look for and what would settle it.

**Mode: `specified`  -  not `grounded`.** No measurement is claimed to settle the identity of dark matter. This is a shape, an unbundling and a route, not a result.

## 1. The claim, in one paragraph

The dark-matter residual is a set of gravitational readings indexed by slot, not one global measurement. The readings do not determine the source, so the answer has the shape of a source term with a formation mechanism, a mass function and a discriminator, and the name "primordial black hole" is a binding from the physics frame, not a figure derivation. PBHs are a reachable candidate inside current physics and remain unsettled. "Some" and "all" are separate claims over separate slots: "all" must clear every slot, "some" needs one surviving mass window. Existing instruments (microlensing, gamma-ray background, CMB distortions, gravitational-wave mergers) already return partial readings, so the stop is unreached rather than unreachable, and every exclusion cut carries assumptions that must be recorded and reversible.

### C2  -  physics-facing; checkable against the constraint literature

- **Statement:** "some" and "all" must be verdicts kept apart, and every exclusion cut is assumption-dependent and reversible.
- **Source, verbatim:** Fig 1 :19  -  *"The residual is indexed by object slot, not aggregated."* Fig 9 :40  -  *"a wrong cut removes the answer and speeds up"*; Fig 9 :41  -  *"so keep every cut ranked and reversible"*.

- **Refuter:** a complete, assumption-free exclusion covering every mass window, or a demonstration that one global residual suffices so the slot split is spurious.

## 5. What would settle it

A source-discriminating instrument, not further argument. From *The Hard Problem is About Source*: an instrument that reads green *"cannot comment on the source"*; the spectrometer resolved it. Here:

1. **Mass-window test:** a positive, source-specific signal in microlensing, gamma-ray background or CMB rather than another upper limit.
2. **Merger-population test:** the mass spectrum, high ellipticities or stochastic background distinguishing primordial from astrophysical mergers (Bird et al. 2016).
3. **Assumption test:** which exclusion cuts survive an extended mass function and varied accretion and clustering assumptions.
