## Round trip gap R_T

The Symbols table defines R_T as the gap between what was sent up and what came back down, `gap(x, (T_E ∘ T_A)(x))`, measuring what the coarser description cannot hold. Fig 4 :30 requires it to be measured rather than assumed. I ran the round trip on the condensation the folder actually uses.

1. **Sent up (x):** the verbatim figure set, chiefly Fig 1 :19 (slot-indexed residual), Fig 2 :29 (anchor), Fig 9 :41 (reversible cuts), Fig 8 :39 (null is not absence).
2. **Abstraction (T_A):** the shorthand carried in `07`, "PBH is a reachable but unsettled candidate; some and all are separate; a discriminator is owed".
3. **Concretisation (T_E):** a concrete paper claim, "primordial black holes make up all the dark matter" or "primordial black holes are excluded as dark matter".

**R_T is nonzero.** Three things the shorthand holds do not survive the trip back down:

- the **slot index**, which a single global "fraction of dark matter in PBHs" erases;
- the **reversibility of cuts**, which a published exclusion table routinely drops when its assumptions move into a footnote;
- the **shape-not-a-name limit**, which a concrete claim breaches by binding the source name as if the framework derived it.

Conversely, one thing survives cleanly: the readings do not name the source, and a discriminator is required. That is the residue worth sending. R_T was **not** measured numerically, because no ground reading exists; it is recorded qualitatively as the enumerated set of dropped constraints. Treating "the formula is the condensed version of the metalaws" as identity would set R_T to zero and would be the dead-reckoning error Fig 4 :30 names.
