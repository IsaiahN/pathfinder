## VERDICT (from the figures)

The shape survives; both specific answers are refused as verdicts. The dark-matter residual is a slot-indexed source-identification problem, and the readings underdetermine the source. PBH is a reachable candidate inside current physics, still `candidate` and not `grounded`. "Some" and "all" are separate questions: "some" needs one surviving window, "all" must clear every slot, and neither is settled. Existing instruments already return partial readings, so this is unreached, not unreachable. Every exclusion cut carries assumptions that must remain visible and reversible. What is owed is a source-discriminating instrument, not another fit. This is a `specified`, ungrounded result.

- **cost if wrong:** bounded and locatable. The packet states mode, unbundles the question, attaches refuters and records its lineage, so an error is findable rather than invisible. The real cost is spending the independence of outside readers by sending a verdict instead of a generator, which Fig 2 :42 prices as "Buy independence in proportion to the cost of being wrong." That cost is avoidable by leading with the shape claim and its refuter.
- **undoable:** yes. Both refused verdicts can be reinstated without touching the derivation: a positive source-specific signal supports "some" or "all", and an assumption-free all-window exclusion supports "none". Excluded mass windows can reopen when an assumption changes. No primitive has to be withdrawn.
- **reversed by:** a ground reading, not agreement. A source-specific signal in microlensing, gamma-ray background, CMB or the merger population would move the candidate toward settled; a mass-spectrum, ellipticity or stochastic-background discriminator would separate primordial from astrophysical mergers; an assumption-free exclusion over every window would refuse it; an instrument mapping readings uniquely to a source would break the metamerism shape; a demonstration that one global residual suffices would collapse the "some" and "all" split. Until one of these fires, the verdict stands as `candidate`.
