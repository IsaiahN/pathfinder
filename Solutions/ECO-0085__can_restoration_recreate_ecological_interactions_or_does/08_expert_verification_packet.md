# Expert verification packet  -  the Tether on restoration and ecological interactions

**What this is:** a claim in a form a restoration ecologist can check. Mode, derivation and refuters are stated before the conclusion, so the reader knows what to look for and what would settle it.

**Mode: `specified` on the substantive sub-claims, `general` on structural rules, `gap` on site-specific detail, not `grounded`.** No measurement is claimed. This is a shape and a route, not a result. It is a simulated derivation checked for internal consistency against the figures, not a ground reading, and it cannot settle the ecological question it reports on.

## 1. The claim, in one paragraph

A site has three slots that must not be averaged: physical conditions, the species list, and the relations between the species. A species list plus a habitat description is an ingredients record; an interaction is a typed bond whose information is not carried by the ingredients. So rebuilding habitat and lists does not by itself close an interaction slot. Relations can be worked on by a maintained seat that introduces actors for their cascades and monitors the result, but exclusion of the habitat is unavailable and a managed ecosystem stays at work rather than becoming a solved state. The ground is the measured interaction rate, not the declaration "restored." Which relation, species and threshold apply at a given site are a declared gap. Falsifiers were written before this claim.

## 2. Three checkable sub-claims

### C1  -  structure, checkable by reading Figures 11 and 12

- **Statement:** a species list and a physical habitat description record the ingredients and not the arrangement; an interaction is a typed bond that is not recoverable from the ingredients.
- **Source, verbatim:** Fig 11 :47  -  *"relations: pollination, predation, competition, dependency"*; Fig 12 :46-47  -  *"A recipe that names only its ingredients has recorded half of itself."*; Fig 12 :5  -  *"A set of ingredients is not one. The bond is the rest of the information, and it is the half nothing was recording."*
- **Refuter:** a predictive model using only the species list and measured habitat conditions reproduces measured interaction rates for several ordered pairs at two sites out of sample, with no residual on the interaction slot. If that holds, the bond carries no information here.

### C2  -  restoration practice, checkable against a real project

- **Statement:** the maintained seat can work on arrival by introducing actors for their cascades, but it cannot subtract the habitat, a seed is a head start and not the grown thing, and managed restoration is permanent work.
- **Source, verbatim:** Fig 11 :58  -  *"Maintained. A seat exists and can act: choose the instruments, distil the impactful actors, introduce what was not there. That is the garden, and it is how arrival is worked on rather than waited for."*; Fig 11 :61  -  *"The seat's power is asymmetric. Introduction is available; exclusion is not."*; Fig 11 :63  -  *"Which is why a managed ecosystem is permanent work rather than a solved state."*; Fig 4 :34-35  -  *"A seed carries no small tree: it carries what will grow one, and the conditions it lands in decide what it becomes."*
- **What to check:** does the project name a relation-level objective and instrument, or does it stop at hectares and species counts? Does the monitoring contract outlive the planting? Can the project name an actor it deliberately removed from the habitat itself?
- **Refuter:** a site where the seat subtracted the habitat itself and reached a target state that persisted with no further contact and no new readings, or a project whose species list alone closes every measured interaction slot.

### C3  -  verification, checkable against the project's reporting

- **Statement:** the anchor for the claim is a measured interaction rate, not a checklist verdict; no actor carries a fixed valence across a change of residual; a plot or mesocosm result is a substituted habitat that proves wiring and not field capability.
- **Source, verbatim:** Fig 2 :29  -  *"An anchor is whatever settles the question and does not change its answer because of what any frame thinks."*; Fig 10 :49  -  *"Declaring a meaning does not ground it."*; Fig 11 :50  -  *"The same actor is a benefactor to one residual and a malfactor to another, so mutualism and parasitism are relations rather than kinds."*; Fig 11 :70  -  *"A test harness is a substituted habitat. Which is why a synthetic solve proves wiring and never capability."*
- **What to check:** is there a per-slot interaction measurement with a declared protocol? Is the actor list justified by a relation at this site rather than by a standing good-species list? Is a plot result being used as a landscape claim?
- **Refuter:** a checklist verdict treated as settling the question while interaction rates remain degraded for several seasons and not withdrawn; an actor with invariant valence across two different residuals; a plot result that transfers with no new ground reading and no interaction residual.

## 3. What is explicitly not claimed

- Not that restoration cannot recreate interactions. The maintained seat can work on arrival (Fig 11 :58, :65).
- Not that the historical baseline is an anchor. It is a convention selected by the project.
- Not that any particular species, technique, site or threshold is framework-derived. That is a `gap`.
- Not that a mesocosm or small plot is the field. Substitution is not removal (Fig 11 :66).
- Not that a cover or richness score is a verdict. It is a filter for the budget (Fig 9 :60).
- Not `grounded`. No measurement in this packet settles any sub-claim.
- Not a ground reading: this packet is a simulated derivation checked for internal consistency against the figures.

## 5. What would settle it

A source-discriminating instrument, not a further argument. From *The Hard Problem is About Source*: an instrument that reads green *"cannot comment on the source"*; the spectrometer resolved it.

1. **Per-slot interaction rates.** Measure pollination, predation, competition and dependency as ordered pairs with a declared anchor and a residual per slot.
2. **Ingredient sufficiency.** Test the species-list-plus-habitat model out of sample at two sites. A near-zero interaction residual refutes C1.
3. **Transfer.** Compare plot result with field result without new ground readings. A zero R_T refutes the substitution claim in C3.
4. **Cascade.** Enumerate outward until the cascade stops mattering (Fig 11 :44) and check both silent failure modes (Fig 11 :68-69).
5. **Valence.** Test one actor against two materially different residuals. Invariant valence refutes C3.
