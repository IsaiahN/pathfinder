## 1. A shape is a constraint, not a filler

The canonical figures README states the limit: the framework returns *"what a missing thing would have to look like (a shape, not a name)."* Fig 9 :32: *"you get a kind of thing, never the answer itself."* Fig 13: *"It cannot say which primitive is missing, because naming it would mean already holding it."*

From *The Library of Babel*, opened this session: *"you can cut your search short if the set of books returned is small and descriptive enough that it provides a general shape of the answer that satisfies your question."*

So a **shape** is the narrowed form of an answer you can state before you can name it. It constrains; it does not fill. For this question the shape is:

> A site has separate slots for conditions, species and relations. A species list plus a habitat description is an ingredients record, and the relation is a typed bond whose information is not carried by the ingredients. A maintained seat can work on arrival by introducing actors for their cascades, but it cannot subtract the habitat and the work does not end at a solved state. What settles the claim is a measured interaction rate, not a declaration that the site is restored. Which relation, species and threshold apply at a given site are not supplied by the framework.

That shape is checkable. "Use clutch size 4 and plant these five species" would be a name, not a shape, and the figures do not license it.

## 2. How a shape is obtained

- *Library of Babel*: use the vocabulary already held that is closest to the thing, look for recurring new terms that overlap with it, recurse, narrow. The essay says the answer is reached when *"the finish line will also be the starting place"*, because by then the searcher has *"composed the answer through the process of locating it."* Here the closest held terms are "habitat," "species" and "interaction"; the recursion narrows them into three separate slots.
- *Decoding Tacit Knowledge*: *"When contact isn't possible, the best you can send is a plain description of what you'd expect to see, because anyone can check that against the world."* This is why stage 1 of the outreach asks what a reader would expect to observe on an interaction slot, before showing the framework.
- Fig 6 :30: *"The description travels. Fails by being sharpened into terms only we can read. Cast it in effect terms."* Fig 9 :30-31: *"your words are borrowed too, so describe the effect rather than the cause."* The effect here is a measured interaction rate, not a philosophical definition of "restoration."
- *How Intelligence Actually Works*: *"repeat until the residual stops registering."* The loop stops when nothing registers a difference, which is either a perfect prediction or a closed channel. Formula step 8 says those look the same from inside. So "no more species losses observed" is not proof that the interaction slot closed; the channel may simply have stopped reporting it.

## 3. What licenses a name

Two things, both outside the shape itself.

1. **The ground settles it.** `THE_FORMULA.md` step 5: *"the ground settles it. Verified only by what pays. A gate is not the ground."* A measured interaction rate that persists under a declared protocol settles a term; a planting checklist does not.
2. **A provenanced import from a frame whose closure differs** (Fig 6 :23, Fig 8 :50-52), with a source gate and a shadow test (Fig 6 :12-13). A donor-frame species or technique can be brought in, but its origin and the gap it explains must be recorded.

*They're Scanning Books When They Should* sharpens the second point: *"The real import is not a new term in the existing space. It is a new dimension of the space."* And the essay warns that *"Declaring the primitives does not make them neutral."* Restoration's chosen baseline is such a declaration. It makes the choice visible; it does not make it the ground.

## 4. The metamerism error applied to this question

*The Hard Problem is About Source* gives the mechanism. A colour instrument detects green and *"cannot comment on the source"*; green can come from one green light, from yellow plus blue, from RGB, or from a scheme not yet discovered. *"If several paths create the same behavior, then behavior is a projection with lossy information."* The essay's conclusion: *"The answer is unreached until a better instrument exists."* It was the spectrometer, not an argument, that resolved it.

Applied here: a species-presence survey reads "the actors are there" and cannot comment on the source, which is the interaction that produced the recruitment or the trophic effect. A project can report a complete native list and still be reading only one projection. Naming "interactions restored" from that reading is the metamerism error. What is owed is a better instrument: an interaction rate per ordered pair, with a declared anchor (Fig 2 :29).

## 5. Prestige, funding and action

*The Recursive Self-Improvement Trap*: *"Prestige can fund an action. It can give an action a budget to be tried. But it cannot settle an action. Only the ground can settle it."* A large restoration budget, a high-profile planting or an endorsed protocol funds the action. None of them settles whether the interaction returned. This is why `03_public_contact_points.csv` contains institutional venues and no reputational ranking, and why the outreach asks for a refutation rather than an endorsement.

## 6. This derivation is a simulation, not a ground reading

Same essay: *"the truly testable output would need to be derived from the real architecture or working the formula yourself."* *Arrival of the Fittest: The Tether Architecture* says LLM outputs generated outside a framework-compliant architecture must be seen as *"simulacra."* So this entire solution folder is a simulated derivation checked for internal consistency against the figures. It is not a field study, not a meta-analysis and not a test of any real restoration project. It cannot settle the question it reports on. The label to use everywhere is `specified`, with `general` on structural rules and `gap` on the site-specific unknowns.

## 7. Correction to my own use of "shape, not a name"

I initially wrote that the rule forbids naming any restoration technique. That overstates it. The README's phrase governs a **missing** thing: *"what a missing thing would have to look like (a shape, not a name)."* Pollination, predation and introduction are categories already held in the figures (Fig 11 :47, :58) and in the world frame. Naming one as the candidate mechanism is a **binding** from the world frame onto a held category, not the invention of a new primitive. What is forbidden is treating that binding as figure-derived, or as an import that moves the wall when it adds no atom (Fig 6 :22, :25).

Both readings are kept: the corrected one governs, and the earlier overstatement is recorded here rather than erased. The metamerism point in section 4 is **not** retracted: it still says a source must not be named from a single projection. The reason is the missing discriminator, not a ban on naming held categories.

## 8. Consequences for how the answer is used

1. **The shape is per-slot and testable.** Do not average conditions, species and interactions into one restoration score (Fig 1 :19; Fig 9 :60).
2. **The name is owed by the ground or by provenance.** A technique, species or threshold is chosen by measurement or by a source-gated import, never by the framework alone (Fig 6 :12-13).
3. **The gap stays open.** Not knowing which relation matters at a particular site is a verdict, not a silence (Fig 13 :134; Fig 8 :39).
4. **The refusal is a design instrument.** The refuter in `01` section 4 was written before the conclusion (Fig 5 :65-66). If one fires, the shape is withdrawn.
5. **The external check must come first.** *A Society of Agents* describes *"a room per problem, and a maintainer who sets conventions but never verdicts."* The outside reader sees the residual and the refuter, not the framework's interiors (Fig 10 :42, :52).
6. **Decline near-miss mappings.** *Arrival of the Fittest* has a section called "Three mappings offered and declined" and says of one near miss that *"there is no prior, no likelihood and no posterior anywhere in the mechanism."* Likewise, "species list equals restored ecosystem" is a near miss that should be declined with a reason, not adopted for convenience.
