# The Tether's answer: restoration, habitat, species lists and interactions

**Mode: `specified` on the substantive verdicts, `general` on the structural rules, `gap` on the particular relation, species, site and threshold, not `grounded`.** This is a shape and a route, not a result. Simulated derivation checked against the figures (`05_what_shape_means.md` section 6), not a ground reading. No site was visited and no measurement was taken.

## 1. The residual it answers

Question 85 offers two options. Under the figures they are not two answers to one slot. They name different slots of the same site: physical conditions, species actors, and the relations between them. A site can have a near-zero residual on the first two and a live residual on the third (Fig 1 :13, :19). The question is therefore a **union compound**, not one hard question: a case with the ingredients present and the relation dead is categorically different from either missing-ingredient case (Fig 9 :54-57). The answer must be given per slot and never averaged into one restoration score.

## 2. The verdicts, kept separate and never averaged

**D1, physical habitat: rebuildable, `specified`, triangulated.** Conditions and structure can be restored by physical work. But a seed is a head start, not the grown thing: *"A seed carries no small tree: it carries what will grow one, and the conditions it lands in decide what it becomes."* (Fig 4 :34-35). The seat can add to a habitat it still did not author (Fig 11 :64). Falsifier: a site whose habitat target persists with no conditions maintained and no readings.

**D2, species list: rebuildable, `specified` at the figure level, single-source there.** Introduction is available: *"The seat's power is asymmetric. Introduction is available; exclusion is not."* (Fig 11 :61). A historically correct list is achievable in principle. It is not sufficient for D3, and no actor keeps a fixed valence across a change of residual (Fig 11 :49-51). Falsifier: an actor with invariant beneficial or harmful status across two materially different residuals.

**D3, ecological interactions: recreatable only as a maintained bond, `specified`, triangulated across Fig 11 and Fig 12 at low overlap.** The relation is on the habitat enumeration list alongside actors and conditions (Fig 11 :45-47), and *"a recipe that names only its ingredients has recorded half of itself"* (Fig 12 :46-47). So a project that stops at D1 and D2 has not worked on D3. A seat can work on arrival by introducing an actor for its cascade (Fig 11 :58, :65), but the relation needs its own objective and instrument: *"A link with no instrument is not a link that works. It is a link nobody has looked at."* (Fig 3 :27). The answer to the question's first option is therefore yes in principle and no by ingredient replacement. Falsifier: a model from the species list and conditions alone that predicts interaction rates at two sites out of sample.

**D4, verification ground: the measured interaction rate, `general` rule and `specified` application, triangulated across Fig 2, Fig 10 and Fig 1.** *"An anchor is whatever settles the question and does not change its answer because of what any frame thinks."* (Fig 2 :29). A checklist verdict is *"a declared meaning"* and declaring it *"does not ground it"* (Fig 10 :18, :49). Because *"the ground does not decay, the channel does"* (Fig 10 :24), monitoring is part of the intervention, not paperwork after it. Managed ecosystems are *"permanent work rather than a solved state"* (Fig 11 :63). Falsifier: a checklist verdict treated as settling while interaction slots remain degraded for several seasons.

**D5, the particular relation, species, site and threshold: `gap`.** No figure line names them. *"you get a kind of thing, never the answer itself"* (Fig 9 :32). *"Unreachable is a verdict here, not a silence,"* (Fig 13 :134) and *"So a search that finds nothing is never a proof of absence."* (Fig 8 :39). Any specific import needs a source gate and a shadow test (Fig 6 :12-13) and its provenance must be recorded (Fig 8 :50-52).

The D1/D2/D3 split is a partition **in notation** and a union **in effect**. A lever that adds species clears only the region where "actors missing" holds alone and leaves the intersection standing (Fig 9 :55-56). This is why the either/or framing is refused rather than answered.

## 3. What it explicitly does not say

- Not that restoration cannot recreate interactions. The maintained seat can work on arrival (Fig 11 :58, :65).
- Not that a species list is useless. It is the ingredients record, and ingredients are needed operands. It is just not the arrangement (Fig 12 :5).
- Not that any species, technique or threshold follows from the framework. That is a `gap`, not a hidden answer.
- Not that a historical baseline is an anchor. It is a convention selected by the project and it moves with climate and land use.
- Not that a mesocosm or plot result transfers to a landscape. *"A test harness is a substituted habitat. Which is why a synthetic solve proves wiring and never capability."* (Fig 11 :70).
- Not `grounded`. No measurement closes any of these verdicts.

## 4. What would settle it  -  the ground

A source-discriminating instrument, not a further argument. From *The Hard Problem is About Source*, opened this session: an instrument that reads green *"cannot comment on the source"*; the spectrometer resolved it. Here:

1. **Per-slot interaction rates.** Measure pollination, predation, competition and dependency as ordered pairs at the site, with a declared anchor and a per-slot residual (Fig 1 :19; Fig 2 :29). This is the INWARD question (Fig 6 :29): extend an instrument already returning something.
2. **Ingredient sufficiency test.** Fit a model from the species list plus habitat conditions alone and test it out of sample at two sites. If the interaction residual stays near zero, F1 fails and the bond argument falls.
3. **Transfer test.** Compare a plot or mesocosm result with the field site without new ground readings. If they match with no residual, R_T is zero for that case and the substitution rule fails.
4. **Cascade test.** Enumerate outward until the cascade stops mattering (Fig 11 :44). Check for the two silent failure modes: *"what you failed to reproduce is invisible until the goal fails"* and *"what you unintentionally introduced is invisible until it acts"* (Fig 11 :68-69).
5. **Standing falsifiers** from `01` section 4: invariant valence, reversible habitat subtraction, a label accepted as an anchor, one lever clearing the whole union, and a genuinely independent world source showing ingredient sufficiency.

## 5. The honest stop

**Unreached, not unreachable.** The interaction-slot instrument may exist and simply not have been applied (SEARCHED, cheapest first). If it does not exist, it has to be extended from one already returning something (INWARD); a sensor for something that has never registered anywhere is not available by description (Fig 6 :32-35). If another frame already predicts the shape, that frame can be imported (OUTWARD), with provenance and a shadow test. Fig 8 :45: *"Two frames together reach what neither reaches alone."* The round trip between a plot result and a landscape result is lossy and must be measured, not assumed (Fig 4 :30). The lineage is recorded so the stop is checkable. *"Indiscernibility is not identity."*

## 6. One-paragraph version for outreach

> The question sets up a false choice. A species list plus a physical habitat description is an ingredients record, while an ecological interaction is a typed bond whose information is not carried by the ingredients, so a site can score well on the first two and still have a dead interaction slot. The Tether says the three slots must be reported separately, never averaged. Habitat and species can be rebuilt by introduction, but exclusion is unavailable, a seed is a head start rather than the grown thing, and a managed ecosystem stays at work rather than becoming a solved state. Interactions can be recreated only when the relation itself is enumerated, given its own objective and instrument, and monitored as a maintained contact; introducing an actor for its cascade is one legitimate way to work on arrival. The label "restored" is a declared meaning, not a grounded one, so the anchor has to be the measured interaction rate in the field. Which relation, which species and which threshold apply at a given site are a declared gap. The package offers that shape, five falsifiers written in advance, and the instruments that would close it. Nothing here is settled by a measurement.
