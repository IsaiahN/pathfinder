## What this package answers, in effect terms

The question is not one hard question with two answers. It is a union compound (Fig 9 :54-57). Three things can be rebuilt separately: the physical conditions, the list of species present, and the relations between them. The figures allow the first two by introduction and physical work, and they say the third is a bond whose information is not carried by the operands (Fig 12 :46-47). So the honest answer is a conditional shape, not an either/or: interactions can be recreated, but only when the relation itself is enumerated, introduced and monitored as a maintained contact, not when a project stops at habitat structure and a species list. The label "restored" is a declared meaning, not a grounded one (Fig 10 :18, :49). The ground is the measured interaction rate in the field (Fig 2 :29).

This is a shape, not a prescription. Which relations, which species and which threshold count as recovered are a declared `gap` (Fig 9 :32; Fig 13 :134). "Unreachable is a verdict, not a silence."

## What is deliberately not here

- No personal emails of named individuals.
- No ranking of people by how likely they are to agree.
- No bulk-send plan.
- No claim that this derivation settles any real restoration question.

Three reasons, in the figures' own words. First, a room does not supply an anchor: *"Alignment is triangulation, not negotiation. A mirror cannot correct you."* (Fig 2 :35). Second, selecting for likely agreement manufactures the disqualifying correlation. Third, a verifier who has already absorbed the framework cannot check it: *"A verifier that can reconstruct the claim cannot verify it."* (Fig 10 :42). The invitation is to refute a shape, not to endorse a theory.
