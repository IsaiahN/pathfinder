## QUESTION

Decide, in effect terms, what the Tether says about the two options in Question 85. Option A: restoration recreates ecological interactions. Option B: restoration mainly rebuilds physical habitat and species lists. What each leads to: A is true only under a maintained seat with an interaction-level objective, instrument and cascade monitoring, because the relation is a bond that is not recoverable from its operands; B describes the default outcome of an ingredient-only programme, which records the ingredients and leaves the arrangement unrecorded. The two options are not one question with two answers. They are different slots of the same site, and the question is a union compound (Fig 9 :54-57).

## DOMAIN CENSUS

R by arity, symmetry and scale, then one line per domain with actors, currency and anchor. Partition or union, and order by dependency.

- **R (the residual).** Arity: three place and more, conditions, actors and relations; each relation joins an ordered pair. Symmetry: a species list is a symmetric conjunction, while an interaction is a typed, ordered bond (Fig 12 :59, :71-74, :117). Scale: first order at the site, second order at the cascade three steps out (Fig 11 :52), and across the lossy membrane from plot to landscape (Fig 4).
- **D1 Physical conditions.** Actors: substrate, light, water, terrain, season. Currency: condition values and area. Anchor: measured microclimate and substrate in the field. Admitted.
- **D2 Species list.** Actors: introduced, returning and resident species. Currency: presence and abundance. Anchor: field counts and detections. Admitted.
- **D3 Relations and cascades.** Actors: pollination, predation, competition, dependency, engineering. Currency: typed bonds, interaction rates, cascade reach. Anchor: measured interaction rates per ordered pair. Admitted.
- **D4 Verification channel.** Actors: instruments, monitors, reports, reviewers. Currency: contact, bracket fidelity and residual. Anchor: the ground, which does not decay (Fig 10 :24). Admitted.
- **X1 "The site is restored" as a checklist verdict.** Actors: the declaring seat. Currency: agreement. Anchor: none nameable that does not move when the seat pushes on it. Not a domain yet (Fig 2 :30).
- **X2 "The historical baseline" as an anchor.** Actors: a reference state. Currency: resemblance. Anchor: moves with climate, land use and the chosen reference. Absorbed into D1 and D2 as a convention, not an anchor.
- **X3 "Restoration success" as one score.** Actors: the scoring framework. Currency: aggregate index. Anchor: none at interaction-slot resolution. Absorbed into D4 as a filter, not a witness (Fig 9 :60).

D1/D2 versus D3 is a partition in notation and a union in effect: the bond needs its operands, so the compound case is categorically different from either single condition. Unbundle before searching (Fig 9 :54-57). Dependency order: perception, vocabulary, objective, planning, learn and carry (Fig 3 :4-15). The chain usually breaks between vocabulary and objective, where a species list supplies names but no measurable relation target (Fig 3 :24-27). Strain test: Fig 12 governs what a notation records and Fig 11 :65 governs what a seat can do, so there is no internal strain after re-census. The world phrase "self-regulating ecosystems" versus "permanent work" is resolved as system dynamics versus channel maintenance, recorded below as an apparent conflict that did not flip the verdict.

## VERDICT (from the figures)

The question's either/or is refused. The verdict is per slot and never averaged. Habitat conditions and species lists can be rebuilt by physical work and introduction, but a seed is a head start and the seat cannot subtract the habitat. Interactions can be recreated only when the relation itself is enumerated, given a measurable objective and instrument, and maintained as contact; introducing an actor for its cascade is a legitimate way to work on arrival, while ingredient replacement alone records half the story. The anchor is the measured interaction rate, not the declared label, and a managed ecosystem is permanent work rather than a solved state. Which relation, species, site and threshold apply is a `gap`. Mode `specified`, ungrounded, candidate. This simulated derivation cannot settle the question it reports on.

- **Cost if wrong:** misdirected effort. A project whose interaction slot is dead would be sold more planting, and a project whose channel has decayed would be told to import more species. The cost is a union mistake: a single lever applied where only one condition holds and the intersection left standing (Fig 9 :55-56). It is bounded because the shape and its eight refuters are recorded before the claim (Fig 10 :62-64).
- **Undoable:** yes. A refutation adds a term and removes nothing; the reach falls because *"something was added rather than because anything was deleted"* (Fig 6 :42-43). No primitive is withdrawn if this shape fails, and a corrected packet supersedes the old one with a visible lineage.
- **Reversed by:** any of the eight pre-registered refuters in `01` section 4, in particular a model from species list plus habitat conditions that predicts interaction rates at two sites out of sample, a site whose relations are fully derived with no field reading, a plot result that transfers with zero R_T, an actor with invariant valence across residuals, a checklist verdict accepted as an anchor, or one lever clearing the whole union with no residue. A genuinely independent world source from outside the ecology pool showing ingredient sufficiency would also reverse it. A declaration, a larger room or a prestigious endorsement would not (Fig 2 :35, :40; Fig 7 :35).
