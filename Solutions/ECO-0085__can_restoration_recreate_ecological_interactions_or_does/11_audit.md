## 1. Independent domain census, redone from the residual line alone

I redid the census from the question line before relying on the primary's conclusions, using the WATSON order: R by arity, symmetry and scale; one line per domain with actors, currency and anchor; partition or union; order by dependency.

- **R (arity / symmetry / scale).** The residual is the gap between what an intervention predicts for a site slot and what the field returns. Arity: three place and more, conditions, actors, relations; each relation is itself an ordered pair. Symmetry: a species list is symmetric under permutation of names, while an interaction is typed and ordered, so pollination and predation are different bonds over the same actors. Scale: first order at the site, second order at the cascade three steps out, and across the lossy plot-to-landscape membrane.
- **D1 Physical conditions.** Actors: substrate, light, water, terrain. Currency: condition values. Anchor: measured microclimate. Admitted.
- **D2 Species list.** Actors: planted, returning and resident species. Currency: presence. Anchor: field counts. Admitted.
- **D3 Relations.** Actors: pollination, predation, competition, dependency. Currency: typed bonds, interaction rates, cascade reach. Anchor: measured rates per ordered pair. Admitted.
- **D4 Verification channel.** Actors: instruments, monitors, reviewers. Currency: contact and residual. Anchor: the ground, which does not decay. Admitted.
- **X1 Checklist verdict.** No anchor that does not move when the declaring seat pushes on it. Not a domain.
- **X2 Historical baseline.** A convention that moves with climate and land use. Absorbed.
- **X3 One success score.** No anchor at interaction-slot resolution. A filter, not a witness.

Question type: D1/D2 versus D3 is a partition in notation and a union in effect, because the relation needs its operands. The case with ingredients present and relation dead is a compound, not a harder case of either single condition. Dependency order: perception, vocabulary, objective, planning, learn and carry. I derive the likely break between vocabulary and objective from Fig 3 :24-27 rather than from the primary's F2. Different starting figure, same domain boundary. Low overlap between routes, still one evidence pool.

## 4. Round trip gap R_T

The Symbols table defines R_T as `gap(x, (T_E ∘ T_A)(x))`, the difference between what was sent up and what came back down. Fig 4 :30 requires it to be measured rather than assumed. I ran the round trip on the condensation this folder actually uses.

1. **Sent up (x):** the verbatim figure set, chiefly Fig 11 :43-70 (habitat enumeration, introduction asymmetry, permanence, substitution) and Fig 12 :5, :46-47, :117 (bond information, isomer).
2. **Abstraction (T_A):** the shorthand in `07_the_tether_answer.md`: conditions and lists can be rebuilt, relations need a maintained bond and an instrument, the label is not the anchor, site specifics are a gap.
3. **Concretisation (T_E):** an actual project brief: restore substrate, plant a native list, monitor cover for two years, declare success.

**R_T is nonzero.** Four constraints held in the shorthand do not survive the trip back down:

- **Per-slot indexing.** A project brief with one success criterion drops Fig 1 :19. A live interaction slot disappears into a site average.
- **Valence relativity.** A fixed native-species list drops Fig 11 :49-51. An actor's value changes with the residual.
- **The two substitution failure modes.** A brief that treats a plot result as a field result drops Fig 11 :68-69 and :70.
- **Permanence and channel maintenance.** A two-year monitoring contract drops Fig 10 :24 and Fig 11 :63. The channel decays and the report says "restored."

What survives cleanly: the relation is a bond not a species, and the ground is the measured interaction rate rather than the declaration. That is the residue worth sending. R_T is **not** measured numerically, because no ground reading was taken; it is recorded qualitatively as the enumerated set of dropped constraints. Treating "the formula is the condensed version of the metalaws" as identity would set R_T to zero and commit the dead-reckoning error Fig 4 :30 names. The honest form is a lossy condensation with a measured gap, which is what `09_watson_answer.md` states.
