## QUESTION

Should fiscal policy aim to balance budgets over the business cycle, stabilize the debt ratio, or prioritize employment and price stability?

Restated as an effect: which quantity is permitted to settle `R = |Γ(b,a) - o′|` when a fiscal authority acts? R is indexed per state of the world and per period, never averaged over the cycle (Fig 1 :19). Anchor: realized measurement and market verdict, named in Fig 2 :29. The three options are not peer goals; the answer is a seat assignment plus an instrument that discriminates failures.

## VERDICT (from the figures)

The figure-derived verdict: the three candidates do not occupy one level. Realized employment, prices and solvency are the ground-facing aims and the question is constitutively about them, but they are a union of two comparisons with different instruments; the debt ratio is a keep-reachable constraint on an accumulated stock and must be met by a state-contingent surplus response; the balanced budget is an instrument, a convention that must not be allowed to decide the outcome, and balancing over the cycle is a pooled reading that hides the live state. The rule must renew from closures that differ. Verdicts are kept per domain and never averaged. Mode `specified`. Which numbers and which rule design are optimal is a declared `gap`, not a hidden answer.

- **Cost if wrong:** if the wrong seat is chosen, a government is sold a procyclical instrument during a bust, or told to import when what has failed is its contact with the ground. The ground cost can be large and is not easily undone in the short run: employment lost in a bust is not recovered by reversing the accounting. The figure-level error is also a spent independence: an expert's twenty minutes go to refuting a shape that was never the right shape, and Fig 2 :42 says *"Buy independence in proportion to the cost of being wrong."*
- **Undoable:** yes at the level of the claim. No term is deleted if a refutation lands; the refutation is added and the reach falls because *"something was added rather than because anything was deleted"* (Fig 6 :42-43). The shape and its five falsifiers are recorded with provenance so a later reader can locate the error (Fig 10 :62-64). The policy consequence of acting before the check is less reversible.
- **Reversed by:** a ground reading, not agreement. Any of the five pre-registered falsifiers, in particular (1) a balanced budget that leaves outcomes unchanged when the market stops paying, (2) one rule passing across two regimes with no import, (3) a fixed debt target dominating a responsive surplus with no stabilization loss, (4) one instrument hitting both targets in every state with no residue, or (5) a shared evidence pool prospectively finding a rule failure neither frame predicted alone. A source-discriminating instrument that names the numbers from realized data would move the gap toward a name. Until one of these fires, the verdict stands as `candidate`.
