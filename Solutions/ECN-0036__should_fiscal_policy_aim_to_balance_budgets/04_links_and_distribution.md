## 2. What the wider literature bears on the question

**One evidence pool, one witness.** Bohn, Auerbach and Gorodnichenko, Abad and coauthors, the IMF dataset and the IMF blog all sit in the broad macro-fiscal literature. They cite one another, use the same conferences and the same post-war fiscal datasets lineage. Per Fig 2 :31, *"Independence, between two frames, is how little they draw on the same evidence."* Their agreement is one witness, not six.

- Bohn 1998 reports a primary surplus that rises with the debt ratio and a debt ratio that is mean-reverting once wars and cycles are controlled. This is the state-contingent response, not a fixed target. It corroborates F4, the constraint reading.
- Auerbach and Gorodnichenko 2012 report that fiscal policy is *"considerably more effective in recessions than in expansions"*. That is the reason a cycle-average balance is the wrong instrument: the same action has different returns in the two states that the average erases. It corroborates F2.
- Abad, Lloyd-Braga and Modesto 2020 show that a balanced-budget rule with incompressible spending produces global indeterminacy and two steady states. That is a concrete instability result for a rule that cannot borrow. It corroborates F2 at the model level, still in the same pool.
- The IMF Fiscal Rules Dataset defines a rule as a long-lasting numerical constraint and says rules aim to contain overspend *"particularly in good times"*. That is an instrument description, not an aim. It corroborates F1's seat assignment after re-mapping, and on first reading it conflicts with treating the rule as an end; see F9 in `01_figure_grounded_audit.md`.
- The IMF PFM Blog subtitle, *"Prudent anchors, corrective mechanisms, and supportive institutions can help countries comply with their fiscal rules"*, uses the word anchors for a debt ratio or medium-term balance target. The figures would call that a convention used as an anchor, and Fig 2 :30 says an anchor that moves when pushed is not one. The blog is a policy frame, not a ground reading.

**Low-overlap witnesses**, each from a different field and each read directly:

1. **Resilience Alliance, adaptive cycle.** The model is proposed as *"a fundamental unit for understanding complex systems from cells to ecosystems to societies"* and alternates long accumulation with short windows for innovation. It corroborates the membrane rule (Fig 4) and the need for a differing closure (Fig 6) without sharing the macro-fiscal evidence pool.
2. **Allison and Hobbs 2004, ecology and society.** A rigidity trap is a state where a system is *"highly connected, rigid, and inflexible"*, and their Table 1 states *"Vulnerability increases as sources of novelty are eliminated and as functional diversity and cross-scale functional replication are reduced."* This corroborates F5 and F6 at the mechanism level. It is an analogue, not a fiscal measurement.
3. **Cognitive and source-discrimination framing.** The essay *The Hard Problem is About Source* is framework-internal, not an independent world witness. It supplies the metamerism argument used in F3: several underlying causes give the same reading, so a reading cannot identify its source without a better instrument. A deficit number is such a reading.

**Framework-internal essays** are used for the canonical reading of "shape" in `05_what_shape_means.md` only. Their author wrote the figures, so their agreement with the figures is maximal overlap and carries no independent corroboration weight. That overlap is recorded in the ledger.

## 3. Distribution rules

Figure-grounded reasons for how material is offered:

- **Fig 4 :22:** *"Only methods cross up. Only head starts come down."* Send the generator, the residual and the refuter, not the conclusion. A recording carried upward *"looks like knowledge and is a description of one occasion"* (Fig 4 :25).
- **Fig 10 :42:** *"A verifier that can reconstruct the claim cannot verify it."* A recipient who has absorbed the framework before being asked for a verdict has joined the frame being checked.
- **Fig 9 :28:** *"any frame you pick will seem to fit"* if the residual is not described first. Ask what they would expect to see if the seat-assignment claim were true before showing the source.
- **Fig 2 :40:** agreement from a shared pool is worthless as a verdict. Later agreement about the framework is never pooled as corroboration.
- **Seat Map :171:** *"an interest in the outcome is unavoidable, an interest in the reading is disqualifying."* Declare the stake; do not ask for belief.

**A hard practical constraint.** The AOM AI policy quoted in the BUS-0001 pilot states that submitted work must be the authors' own and not a product of AI tools unless appropriate to the research question and properly cited. Economics journals and associations have comparable rules, and the IIPF and SSRN pages fetched here do not waive them. This whole solution is an LLM-assisted simulated derivation and is labelled as such everywhere. It must **not** be submitted as an authored empirical or theoretical article. The legitimate use is as a private checking instrument: post the residual and the refuter, invite a human to run the check, and let that human author whatever they conclude, with disclosure as the venue requires.

**Order in any reply:** (1) the seat-assignment claim and the pre-registered refuter; (2) the question "what would you count as a failure here?"; (3) only after a verdict is recorded, the three source links with the note that the figures, not the summary, are the authority; (4) the formula as a reusable method, with the instruction that each user writes their own refuter and states their own mode.

## 4. The two-stage offer, compressed

**Stage 1.** Send `08_expert_verification_packet.md` sections 1 to 3 only: the one-paragraph claim, the three checkable sub-claims with their verbatim figure lines and refuters, and the non-claims. No provenance bundle, no essay links.

**Stage 2, opt-in only.** If the recipient is curious and has already recorded a verdict on stage 1, offer the three public links: the figure text, the formula and usage rule, and the essays. Their later agreement is a conversation, not corroboration (Fig 2 :40).

**Stage 3.** Offer the formula as a method, never as an answer (Fig 4 :22). Each user writes a refuter before running it and states the mode of what they get.

**Stop rule.** If stage 1 fails, the theory is not offered. A failure is more useful than an endorsement.

**Hosting.** Nothing here is hosted or sent by this agent. The human operator hosts the packet on an opt-in page and sends one to one. Prefer a download page that lets a recipient decline contact over an attachment pushed to a list.
