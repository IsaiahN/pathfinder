# The Tether's answer on the aims of fiscal policy

**Mode: `specified` - not `grounded`.** This is a shape and a route, not a result. Simulated derivation checked against the figures (`05_what_shape_means.md` section 7), not a ground reading. No prospective, per-state measurement pays for any claim here.

## 1. The residual it answers

An answer would say which quantity is permitted to settle `R = |Γ(b,a) - o′|` when a fiscal authority acts. R is indexed by state of the world and by period, never averaged over the cycle (Fig 1 :19, :22). The question is about **which seat each candidate occupies**, not about the level of the deficit.

The union must be unbundled before it is searched (Fig 9 :54). It is three conditions, not one hard question with three answers:

- **C1:** the channel to a ground that still pays stays reachable.
- **C2:** the accumulated debt stock stays reachable.
- **C3:** the rule keeps renewing its reach from a closure that differs from its own.

## 2. The shape

1. **The three candidates are not peers.** All three are comparisons (*"a goal is a comparison"*, Operators :51), but a threshold on realized outcomes, a threshold on an accumulated stock and a threshold on a government-produced ledger are different objects. *"a convention that decides an outcome has stopped being one"* (Fig 10 :47). The ledger is a convention; when it overrides the outcome it becomes an unpaid verdict.
2. **Realized outcomes are the anchor.** Fig 2 :29 names *"a measurement"* and *"a market"* as instances of an anchor, and :36 says an anchor is legitimate when the question is constitutively about it. A question about what fiscal policy should aim at is about employment, prices and solvency. The ledger is an instrument, and *"instruments can be wrong on axes they do not measure"* (Fig 2 :38).
3. **"Balance over the cycle" is a pooled reading.** Fig 1 :22: *"Aggregating across slots is how a live signal disappears into an average."* Netting the bust against the boom throws away the state that needed action. Fig 10 :26 names the failure mode: *"saturated metrics, pooled readings"*.
4. **The debt ratio is a keep-reachable constraint on a stock.** Fig 10 :24: *"the ground does not decay, the channel does"*; :29: *"→ keep the anchor reachable"*. Debt is conserved and finite (Fig 13 :13-14), so it is steered by a response, a sequence and a subtraction (Fig 12 :112), not by a fixed balance. Bohn's finding that the surplus rises with the debt ratio is the shape of that response.
5. **Employment and price stability are the ground-facing aims, and they are a union.** Fig 9 :57: *"a case in two conditions is not a harder case of either."* One lever clears the region where only one condition binds and leaves the intersection standing. A healthy scepticism about optimistic forecasts is a second condition with a second instrument, not a mood.
6. **The rule must meet differing closures.** Fig 6 :23: *"No frame escapes its own closure. The bound moves only on contact with a frame whose closure differs."* Fig 8 :32: closure is idempotent, so re-running the same rule at a larger scale adds no atom. Other countries' rules, independent councils and new data are not distractions; they are the only operators that move the wall, and they need provenance.
7. **The break is usually at the middle link.** Fig 3 :24 and :27: the vocabulary/objective link has no instrument, and a link with no instrument is a link nobody has looked at. "Does this authority have a term for a structural versus a cyclical deficit?" is a better diagnostic than "is this rule credible?".
8. **Internal agreement is not evidence.** Fig 7 :13: *"agreement compounds, evidence does not"*. Fig 2 :35: *"Alignment is triangulation, not negotiation. A mirror cannot correct you."* Fiscal economists who read the same papers and agree are one frame wearing many names.

## 3. What it explicitly does not say

- Not `grounded`. No prospective, per-state measurement has paid for this shape.
- Not a name. It does not say the inflation target, the natural rate, the debt threshold, or the optimal rule design. Fig 9 :32: *"you get a kind of thing, never the answer itself."*
- Not an average. It makes no claim about the percentage of variance attributable to fiscal policy, luck or the cycle. Such numbers are filters for budget, not witnesses (Fig 9 :60).
- Not that fiscal discipline is irrelevant. A rule is Γ; the claim is that Γ alone does not settle the outcome (Fig 10 :24, Fig 11 :40).
- Not that a balanced budget is always wrong. It is a useful instrument against overspend in good times; what is refused is promoting it to the aim.
- Not a theory of everything about public finance. It stops where the instrument stops.
- Not corroboration by agreement. The macro-fiscal sources share one evidence pool (Fig 2 :31, :40). The low-overlap resilience sources corroborate the mechanism, not the fiscal fact.

## 4. What would settle it - the ground

A source-discriminating instrument, not a further argument. The source essay's metamerism rule: a reading of a deficit *"cannot comment on the source"*, and *"the answer is unreached until a better instrument exists"*. Here the reading is the deficit or the debt ratio; the candidate sources are a recession-driven shortfall, structural overspend, a tax change, a reclassification, or luck.

The falsifiers, pre-registered in `01` and repeated in `08`:

1. a balanced budget leaves employment and prices unchanged when the market stops paying for what the government does;
2. the same rule passes the outcome test across two regimes with no import from a differing closure;
3. a fixed debt target dominates a responsive surplus with no loss of stabilization;
4. one instrument brings both employment and prices to target in every state with no residue;
5. two frames with a shared evidence pool prospectively find a rule failure neither predicted alone.

A prospective, per-state study that measures C1 and C2 separately, records the bond as well as the atoms, and reports which condition failed before the outcome worsened would turn this from `specified` toward `grounded`.

## 5. The honest stop

**Unreached, not unreachable.** Fig 13 :134: *"Unreachable is a verdict here, not a silence,"* and Fig 8 :39: *"So a search that finds nothing is never a proof of absence."* The shape stops at the limit of the available instruments and records the lineage so the stop is checkable. The missing thing is a discriminator of sources and a prospective measurement, characterized in effect terms but not held here. Naming a target from a reading would be the metamerism error the source essay forbids. The six points in section 2 are a shape; the seventh thing, which rule and which numbers pay next, is not held and is not pretended.

## 6. One-paragraph version for outreach

> The Tether does not recommend a fiscal charter or name the right debt ratio. It says that the familiar three options are not rival goals at one level. Realized employment, prices and solvency are what the question is about, so they settle it; the debt ratio is a threshold on an accumulated stock that has to be kept reachable by a surplus that responds to the state of the world; and a balanced budget is an instrument, a convention that must not be allowed to decide the outcome. Balancing over the cycle nets the bust against the boom and hides the state that needed action. Employment and price stability are two conditions, not one, so one lever clears only the easy region. A rule that never meets a way of working different from its own stops adding to what it can do. What is offered is that shape, five falsifiers written in advance, and the instruments that would close it. Which numbers and which rule design occupy the slots stays open until a source-discriminating instrument exists, and the stop is recorded rather than filled.
