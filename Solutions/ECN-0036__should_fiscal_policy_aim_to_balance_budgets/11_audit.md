## 2. Independent re-census from the residual line

### 2.1 Arity, symmetry, scale

- **Arity:** a relation among authority-library Γ, action a, measured outcome o′ and state s, ordered by time t. It has at least three operands and one ordering. A two-place reading (authority, deficit) loses the action, the state and the measurement, so it is rejected. Each candidate adds a comparison operand, so the full residual is multi-arity.
- **Symmetry:** not symmetric. The realized outcome is an anchor and does not update on the authority's opinion (Fig 2 :25). Each candidate is a threshold comparison (*"is it about which is larger, rather than both being present?"*, Operators :50-51), so one operand is compared against another or against a target, not merely conjoined with it.
- **Scale:** the slot is a period and a state of the world, but "over the cycle" exists only at the pooled scale. The debt stock exists at the intertemporal scale. The residual is intrinsically cross-scale, and the membrane is lossy (Fig 4).

### 2.3 The seat-assignment test

Taking the residual line alone, no candidate can be dismissed as false. What can be tested is whether each candidate *can* settle R:

- The balanced ledger moves when the authority changes its accounting boundary, so it fails Fig 2 :30 (*"you push on it, it is not an anchor"*). It is an instrument.
- The debt ratio is a state that responds to flows; it is settled by whether the sovereign pays, which is a market verdict. It is a constraint to be kept reachable (Fig 10 :29).
- Employment and prices are measurements and the question is constitutively about them (Fig 2 :36). They are the ground-facing aims, but two conditions (Fig 9 :57).

This seat reaches the same seat assignment as the primary by a different route: the primary began from the anchor definition and Fig 10 :47, this seat began from Fig 2 :30's push test and the union rule. Different starting figures, same boundaries: low overlap between routes, so the agreement is weak but not circular within the framework. It is still one evidence pool.

## 4. Round-trip gap R_T

Fig 4 :30: *"So take the round trip and measure the gap. Without it you are navigating by dead reckoning: confident about a position nobody checked."*

- **T_A, going up:** carry the primary's seat assignment to a one-sentence abstraction. Result: *fiscal policy should keep the books sustainable while supporting employment and prices*.
- **T_E, coming back:** put that abstraction into a specific recession. Result: *tighten to show sustainability, and hope employment and prices follow*.

**R_T = gap(x, (T_E ∘ T_A)(x)).** The gap is what the abstraction cannot hold:

1. The one-sentence form reconflates the seats. "Sustainable while supporting" makes the ledger and the outcomes co-equal, losing Fig 10 :47 (*"a convention that decides an outcome has stopped being one"*). Coming back down, it licenses a surplus that overrides the outcome.
2. It loses per-slot indexing. "The books" is a pooled object; the abstraction cannot say which state of the world is failing, so it permits Fig 1 :22's aggregation error.
3. It loses the response requirement. "Sustainable" does not say that the surplus must respond to the ratio and to the cycle, which is the entire content of the Bohn finding used in F4.
4. It loses the union. "Employment and prices" reads as one phrase, not Fig 9 :57's *"a case in two conditions is not a harder case of either."* Coming back down, it buys one lever for two failures.
5. It loses the closure requirement. Nothing in the abstraction says the rule must import from a differing closure, so it licenses a chain of mirrors (Fig 7 :36).
6. It loses the source-discriminator requirement. The abstraction cannot say what instrument would distinguish a recession shortfall from structural overspend, which is the actual `gap` in the answer.

This gap is not an error to eliminate; it is the measurement of what the coarser description cannot hold (Fig 4 :31). It is also the reason the outreach packet sends the generator, not the one-sentence version (Fig 4 :25). The one-paragraph version in `07` is a deliberately lossy artefact and is labelled as such.
