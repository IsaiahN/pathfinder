## QUESTION

Decide, in effect terms, what the Tether says about learning whom to trust when social networks and institutions differ. Option A: treat trust as one global level that networks and institutions scale up or down. Option B: treat the question as a union compound of per-slot learning, a reputation cascade, and an institutional habitat, and derive the shape each domain must have. What each leads to: A aggregates away the very signal "whom" asks for and mistakes a convention for a verdict; B yields a checkable shape, written refuters, and a stated gap where the mechanism cannot be named at this budget.

## DOMAIN CENSUS

R by arity, symmetry and scale, then one line per domain with actors / currency / anchor, partition or union, and order by dependency.

- **R (the residual).** For each candidate trustee s, the gap between the belief-formed prediction and what the trustee actually did. Arity: many, one slot per candidate. Symmetry: directed; A trusting B is not B trusting A. Scale: first order inside one interaction, second order when settled outcomes compose into reputation. Fig 9 names the whole a union compound: a case in two or more conditions is not a harder case of one.
- **Domain A, per-slot trust learning.** Actors: learner, candidate trustee. Currency: the per-slot residual, with w weighting private history against inherited prior. Anchor: revealed behaviour in a trust-diagnostic interaction. Question type: partition; do not aggregate slots.
- **Domain B, network and reputation.** Actors: peers, peers of peers, observers who inform others. Currency: reputation, a second-order atom built from settled outcomes. Anchor: the underlying behaviour reputation is a reading of, which must not update on reputation alone. Question type: union; many outcomes compose into one term.
- **Domain C, institutions and habitat.** Actors: courts, property rights, enforcement, norms, kinship structures. Currency: consequences that make checking cheap or costly. Anchor: the enforcement record, which must not change because a frame approves of it. Question type: habitat, enumerated and never composed (Fig 11), with contact cascades beyond direct partners.
- **Domain D, verification.** Actors: expert reader and the checking seats. Currency: agreement and residual. Anchor: the figures and the ground, never agreement. In this simulated run all seats draw on one evidence pool, so independence is zero.
- **Order by dependency (Fig 3).** Perceive the outcome, have terms for what the trustee affords, say what counts as progress here, choose the action, then name and carry what worked. Refuse before mint (Formula step 2). Describe the residual before choosing a frame (Fig 9 :27-28).

Strain test applied: the evolutionary-biology w (probability of another encounter) and the Tether w (weighting between private history and prior) share a glyph but govern different quantities. Re-census keeps them separate, so no strain survives. This is recorded as a retraction in `01_figure_grounded_audit.md` section 6.

## VERDICT (from the figures)

The shape survives; the mechanism name does not. Trust is learned per candidate, not as one global level. The anchor is revealed behaviour, so agreement from a shared evidence pool is a convention about meaning and not a verdict about trustworthiness. The weighting between private experience and inherited institutional prior is learnable and is where networks and institutions differ. Trustworthiness is a valence relative to the learner's residual and habitat, not a property carried by the person. Betrayal is a refusal, and reputation is a second-order atom that a closed frame cannot add to by search alone. Kinship and court-backed regimes are isomers over a shared atom set, with a stated falsifier. The specific cognitive algorithm is a `gap`, not a hidden failure. This is a `general` and `specified`, ungrounded result, not a `grounded` one.

- **cost if wrong:** bounded and locatable. The packet states mode, refuters and provenance before the conclusion, so an error is findable rather than invisible. The real cost is spending the independence of outside readers by sending a theory before a checkable output. Fig 2 :42 "Buy independence in proportion to the cost of being wrong." That cost is avoidable by leading with the shape claim and its refuter.
- **undoable:** yes. The three domain verdicts are recorded separately, and a falsifier in `01_figure_grounded_audit.md` section 4 removes only the domain it breaks. No primitive has to be withdrawn and no literature claim has to be retracted.
- **reversed by:** a ground reading, not agreement. A within-person design showing that per-target residuals predict nothing beyond a global score would break domain A. A design in which independent readings add nothing to a shared-pool agreement would break domain B. A habitat-shift design in which the same actor's rating never changes would break domain C. A source-discriminating instrument naming the mechanism would close the gap. Until one of these fires, the verdict stands as `candidate`.
