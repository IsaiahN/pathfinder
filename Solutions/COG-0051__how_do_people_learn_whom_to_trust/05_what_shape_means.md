# What "shape" means for COG-0051, from the eight essays

**Sources read this session (2026-11-22):** the canonical figures README (`FRAMEWORK/reference/FIGURES_README_canonical.md`), WATSON 60 (`FRAMEWORK/reference/60_FIGURES_AS_METALAWS.md`), and four of the eight essays by Isaiah N: *The Library of Babel: Locating The Book of Books* (adventuresinml Substack, 2026-10-02), *The Hard Problem is About Source* (2026-08-28), *The Recursive Self-Improvement Trap* (2026-11-19), and *Decoding Tacit Knowledge: Why Context, Subtext, and Pretext Matter* (2026-11-10). The canonical set of eight essays also includes *How Intelligence Actually Works*, *How a Society of Agents Would Work*, *They're Scanning Books When They Should*, and *Arrival of the Fittest: The Tether*. Those four are part of the framework's stated grounding but were **not re-opened in this session**; no quote is attributed to them here. The exemplar's mapping of them is not treated as an independent source.

## 1. A shape is a constraint, not a filler

The canonical README states the limit: the framework returns "what a missing thing would have to look like (a shape, not a name)." Fig 9 :32 "you get a kind of thing, never the answer itself." Fig 13: "It cannot say which primitive is missing, because naming it would mean already holding it."

From *The Library of Babel*: "you can cut your search short if the set of books returned is small and descriptive enough that it provides a general shape of the answer that satisfies your question."

So for this question a **shape** is the narrowed form of an answer we can state before we can name the mechanism. It constrains; it does not fill. "Trust is learned per candidate, anchored in revealed behaviour, weighted between private history and inherited prior, corrected by refusal, and composed into reputation" is a shape. "The brain runs algorithm X" is a name. The shape survives this derivation; the name is recorded as a `gap`, not smuggled in.

## 2. How the shape is obtained for this question

- *Library of Babel*: use the vocabulary you already have that is closest to the thing and "connect and correlate combinations of the existence of your known words and their relative proximity to commonly recurring new terms"; "With each new term gained, you narrow the space and deepen your vocabulary." Here the bootstrapped terms were slot, anchor, independence, weighting, valence, habitat, refusal, and second-order atom.
- Fig 6 :30 "The description travels. Fails by being sharpened into terms only we can read. Cast it in effect terms." Fig 9 :30 "your words are borrowed too, so describe the effect" rather than the cause. So the residual was stated as an effect (a per-slot gap between expected and observed reciprocation), not as a theory of trusting.
- *Decoding Tacit Knowledge*: "When contact isn't possible, the best you can send is a plain description of what you'd expect to see, because anyone can check that against the world." This is why `02_outreach_email.md` leads with a checkable refuter.
- *Library of Babel* on bundling: "Perhaps your problem was a bundle or conflation of several problems masquerading as one?" That is exactly the union compound in `01_figure_grounded_audit.md` section 0.
- *Library of Babel* on stopping: "the finish line will also be the starting place", and "you have already composed the answer through the process of locating it."

## 3. What licenses a name

Only two things, both outside the shape itself:

1. **The ground settles it.** Formula step 5: "the ground settles it. Verified only by what pays." The canonical README: the framework "stops at the limit of what the available instruments can distinguish, and records the lineage so the stop is honest."
2. **A provenanced import from a frame whose closure differs** (Fig 6 :23, Fig 8 :50-52), with a source gate and a shadow test.

*The Hard Problem is About Source* gives the failure mode. An instrument reads green and "it cannot comment on the source": one green light, or yellow plus blue, or RGB, or CMYK. "If several paths create the same behavior, then behavior is a projection with lossy information." The resolution came "not by argument, but by the arrival of a better instrument." Applied here: a reputation score is a reading, not a source. Many different histories (loyalty, fear, coercion, genuine reciprocity) can produce the same "trusted" reading. Naming the source from the reading is the metamerism error. What is owed is a source-discriminating instrument, not a name.

## 4. Why institutions enter the shape at all

*Decoding Tacit Knowledge* names three drift-stoppers that match what the figures require, and they are institutions in the plain sense:

- "independent replication is separate experience plus something real to check against";
- "Apprenticeship is two-way contact with correction";
- "Courts of law rule on evidence, which neither side can move."

That last sentence is Fig 2 :29 "An anchor is whatever settles the question and does not change its answer because of what any frame thinks" in a social institution. It is why domain C of the census has a nameable anchor (an enforcement record that does not move when a frame approves) and why institutions differ as instruments, not as moral ranks. The same essay adds: "Shared pretext is good for one thing and ruins another. Agreement is the whole point when you're settling what words mean. It's worthless for settling what's true when both sides learned from the same sources." That is Fig 2 :39-41's "Correlation has valence" stated in cognitive terms.

And the drift warning: "Two people who only check against each other drift, and they drift together, so neither notices." A network with no outside anchor is a mirror chain (Fig 7 :12-13 "same closure, handed along; agreement compounds, evidence does not").

## 5. Why this derivation cannot settle its own claim

*The Recursive Self-Improvement Trap*: "the output is only as good as the validator. The agent, or the tool, or the frame, or the theory, cannot validate itself." And: "the truly testable output would need to be derived from the real architecture or working the formula yourself." LLM output is a simulacrum of a derivation.

So `01_figure_grounded_audit.md` is a **simulated derivation checked for internal consistency against the figures**. It is not a measurement of human trust learning. It cannot settle the question it reports on. Its mode is `general` on the shape and `specified` on the domain predictions, never `grounded`.

## 6. Q: Does "shape, not a name" forbid naming the cognitive mechanism?

No, with a boundary. The canonical README's phrase governs a **missing** primitive, one not held in the library. The figures hold no term for a specific trust-learning algorithm, so naming one would exceed what the framework returns. But the framework does hold the terms used in the shape: slot, anchor, independence, weighting, valence, habitat, negation, and second-order atom. Using those is a binding from the cognitive-science frame onto held atoms, which is legitimate provenance, not an invention of a seventh term. What is forbidden is presenting the binding as returned by the figures, or claiming it moves the wall (Fig 6 :21 "MINT composes inside the closure and can never add an atom"). The record keeps the distinction visible.

## 7. Two consequences for outreach

**Prestige is not action.** *The Recursive Self-Improvement Trap*: "Prestige must never be allowed to validate an action directly." And: "Prestige can fund an action. It can give an action a budget to be tried. But it cannot settle an action. Only the ground can settle it. The Ground Maintainer must be blind to prestige." A campaign that gathers respected names to agree with the shape claim is the named collapse. The legitimate ask is the opposite: give the claim a budget to be tried and let the ground settle it.

The same essay records a real convergent case: the author's governance work "rhymed" with Elinor Ostrom's polycentric governance work, arrived at from different fields, with "the structures matched." Fig 8 :50-52 says convergent derivation and adopted import look identical in the contents and only the record separates them. That is the right model for how the trust/social-cognition literature relates to the shape: corroboration only where the record shows separate routes, never a pool of agreeing voices.

**The two-stage order follows from contact.** *Decoding Tacit Knowledge*: "The loop only closes if the learner acts; the highlighted step is where the correction arrives, unstated." A recipient who has already absorbed the framework has joined the frame being checked. So the refuter goes first, the verdict is recorded before theory, and the links come last.
