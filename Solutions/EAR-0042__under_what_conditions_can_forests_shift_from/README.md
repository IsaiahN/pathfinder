# Solution EAR-0042: when does a forest become a carbon source?

**Question:** Under what conditions can forests shift from absorbing carbon to releasing it?

**Source:** `earth systems.txt` line 58

**Section:** 5. Forests, ecosystems, and biodiversity

**Mode:** `general` on the structural shape, `specified` on the per-domain verdicts, `gap` on exact numerical thresholds. Nothing here is `grounded`. This is a simulated derivation checked for internal consistency against the figures, not a ground reading.

**Status:** done

**Registry status:** pending

## What the question decides, in effect terms

A forest has a net carbon balance: uptake by photosynthesis minus release by respiration, decomposition, fire and export. The question asks when the sign of that balance flips from net uptake (sink) to net release (source), and what would count as a genuine shift rather than an ordinary seasonal or interannual excursion. Bound the sought-for shape as an ordinary term before deriving: `SHIFT = (signed net flux crosses zero) AND (crossing persists beyond a stated window) AND (the standing stocks and return paths that produced the crossing are altered)`. The last clause separates a persistent shift from a drought year that reverses.

## What is deliberately not here

- No personal emails of named individuals, only public institutional submission and contact routes.
- No ranking of people by how likely they are to agree with the result.
- No bulk-send plan.
- No numerical tipping threshold claimed as settled by the figures. The figures return a shape, not a number.

Three reasons in the figures' own words: Fig 2 :35 "Alignment is triangulation, not negotiation. A mirror cannot correct you."; Fig 2 :40 "disqualifying for a verdict: agreement about the world is worthless when the agreeing frames share an evidence pool"; Fig 10 :42 "A verifier that can reconstruct the claim cannot verify it.". Selecting recipients for receptiveness manufactures the correlation that makes their agreement worthless, and asking them to trust the framework before they check the output spends the only independence they contribute.
