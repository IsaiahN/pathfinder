## Wider literature that bears on this question, kept distinct from the figures

- **Brando 2019 and Hubau 2020** corroborate the union shape: several drivers combine, and the threshold conditions are not yet specified. They also declare the gap. A gap stated by a world source is not a figure verdict; it is corroboration for the figure-derived `gap` on numbers.
- **Gatti 2021 and the 2024 Amazon flux synthesis** corroborate the per-slot finding: the southeast was a source while the wider region was closer to balance. Different instruments (aircraft profiles, bottom-up inventories, top-down inversions) are three attempts at the same anchor, and their disagreement is informative rather than pooled as agreement.
- **Clarke 2022 and Reinsel 2026** disagree about the role of fire in a specific year. Both were read. The disagreement is treated as a question for the census (interval, region, instrument), not as a reason to pick a winner. No verdict was flipped by it.
- **Campioli 2016** is the strongest witness for the anchor claim: the sign itself depends on method where the flux is small. This is why "measured flux" must be triangulated, not treated as one magic instrument.
- **Scheffer 2009** is a complex-systems source (mathematics and finance as well as ecology), so it is low overlap with the forest literature. It predicts that generic warning signals may exist, but it does not supply a forest threshold. It corroborates the shape and cannot decide the verdict.

The world sources are secondary. They agree with the figure-derived shape in part because the figure census predicted what to look for in them. That is expected and carries limited independent weight.

## Distribution: offer, do not push

Figure-grounded rules:

- **Fig 4 :23** "Only methods cross up. Only head starts come down." Send the generator (the shape, the refuter, the census method), not a recording of someone else's threshold number.
- **Fig 10 :42** "A verifier that can reconstruct the claim cannot verify it." A recipient who has absorbed the framework before being asked for a verdict has joined the frame being checked.
- **Fig 9 :27-28** "describe it before you go looking, or any frame you pick will seem to fit." Ask what they would expect to see if the shape claim were true before offering any source.
- **Fig 2 :40** "disqualifying for a verdict: agreement about the world is worthless when the agreeing frames share an evidence pool." Do not pool later agreement as corroboration.
- **Fig 9 :60** "Use filters for the budget and witnesses for the verdict. Never let a filter hand you a verdict." A tier or a journal name selects where to try, never whether the claim is true.

**Recommended order in a reply:** (1) the shape claim and its five refuters; (2) ask what they would count as a failure; (3) record their verdict; (4) only then the three links, with the note that the figures, not the summary, are the authority.

## Contact discipline

Use the public, opt-in venues in `03_public_contact_points.csv`: society contact pages, journal submission systems, research-network join routes, conference abstract portals. One-to-one only. No personal emails, no receptiveness ranking, no bulk send, no automation. Unsolicited bulk email is constrained by GDPR and CAN-SPAM-style rules in many jurisdictions; public opt-in routes avoid both that failure and the structural one.
