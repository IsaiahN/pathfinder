## QUESTION

Decide, in effect terms, what the Tether says about the conditions under which a forest's net carbon balance flips from net uptake to net release, and persists. Option A: report a single numerical threshold at which forests flip. Option B: keep the figure-derived shape, keep per-domain verdicts separate, and mark exact numbers as `gap`. ## DOMAIN CENSUS

R by arity, symmetry and scale, then one line per domain with actors / currency / anchor; partition or union; order by dependency.

- **R (the residual).** The signed net carbon flux of a forest and the event of its crossing zero persistently. Arity: a union of overlapping conditions (Fig 9 :55), not one threshold. Symmetry: a signed loop, not a one-way chain (Fig 12 :121). Scale: stand and region, with basin means hiding live slots (Fig 1 :22); intervals from diurnal to multi-decadal. Fig 3 :25-27 names the broken link: we can perceive flux and name its components, but "persistent shift" has no settled objective definition.
- **Domain 1, forest carbon flux.** Actors: GPP, autotrophic and heterotrophic respiration, deadwood, soil, fire emissions, harvest export. Currency: carbon mass per area per time. Anchor: eddy-covariance towers, biometric inventories, atmospheric inversions. Verdict `specified`, triangulated.
- **Domain 2, forest demography.** Actors: trees by size and age, mortality, recruitment, competition. Currency: biomass and stems. Anchor: permanent plots. Verdict `specified`, triangulated with Domain 1 data.
- **Domain 3, disturbance and hazard.** Actors: fire, VPD, drought, pests, edges. Currency: area burned, mortality, emissions. Anchor: burned-area satellites, fire records, reanalysis. Verdict `general` valence rule plus `specified` assignments, triangulated.
- **Domain 4, climate forcing.** Actors: CO2, temperature, precipitation, VPD, ENSO. Currency: concentration and moisture demand. Anchor: atmospheric records. Upstream of D1 to D3; no separate sink/source verdict.
- **Domain 5, measurement and accounting frame.** Actors: satellites, DGVMs, credits. Currency: proxies and modeled flux. **No independent anchor**: it borrows the flux anchor from Domain 1, and credit prices move when pushed. Not a ground domain. Verdict `gap` for any proxy-only claim.
- **Domain 6, management seat.** Actors: foresters, suppression, planting. Currency: interventions. Anchor: the forest's response through D1 to D3. Introduction available, exclusion not. Verdict `general`, single-source.
- **Order by dependency:** measure flux, name habitat items, define the crossing, choose a lever, carry what worked (Fig 3 :20). Union, not partition (Fig 9 :54-58).

## VERDICT (from the figures)

The shape survives; exact thresholds do not. A forest sink-to-source shift is the sign of a return loop, produced by a union of habitat conditions, occurring per stand, with actor valence relative to the residual and the measured signed flux as anchor. Management introduces and steers and cannot subtract the habitat. The figures return the shape of the missing thing, not its name; exact numerical thresholds are a `gap` at this budget because the cross-network convention for "persistent" is missing, not because the phenomenon is unobservable. The one live strain is fire's varying role across intervals and regions, named rather than hidden. Everything here is `specified` or `general`, candidate, ungrounded.

- **cost if wrong:** bounded and locatable. The packet states mode, refuters and provenance before its conclusion, so an error is findable rather than invisible. The real cost is spending the independence of outside readers by sending a threshold as if it were the ground. Fig 2 :42 "Buy independence in proportion to the cost of being wrong." That cost is avoidable by leading with the shape and the refuter.
- **undoable:** yes. No primitive is withdrawn and no number is retracted, because none was asserted. The shape can be superseded by a better enumerated habitat list or a stronger per-slot instrument without disturbing the rest of the folder.
- **reversed by:** a ground reading, not agreement. A cross-network operational definition of persistent shift plus tower, plot and inversion records showing altered stocks would name the threshold; a flip with no habitat change, a net-source forest with a net-uptake return loop and no lateral export, a single threshold partitioning everything, synchronous flips everywhere, or an invariant proxy would break the corresponding claim. Until one fires, the verdict stands as `candidate`.
