# Outreach draft: check one shape before deciding about the threshold claim

**Use:** one-to-one, or as a cover letter attached to a submission through the public venues in `03_public_contact_points.csv`. **Not** for bulk send. No personal emails are used anywhere in this folder.

**Hard precondition:** do not send until the shape and the pre-registered refuter in `01_figure_grounded_audit.md` are intact. This package contains no numerical tipping threshold, so nothing in it can collapse on contact the way a threshold claim would.

---

**Subject:** One checkable claim about forest sink-to-source shifts, before any theory

> Hi [name],
>
> I am writing about one specific output, not about a framework. I am not asking you to agree with it, and I am not asking you to read any theory first. If the output fails your check, there is nothing else to look at.
>
> **The output (`general` shape, `specified` per-domain verdicts, `gap` on exact numbers):** a forest switching from carbon sink to source is the sign change of a return loop, not the running down of a stock. Uptake becomes biomass, biomass becomes litter and deadwood, deadwood and soil respire back, and fire or harvest can short-circuit the return. When the release side exceeds the uptake side for long enough, the signed flux crosses zero. The conditions that produce that crossing are habitat items read off the world: water, temperature, season, terrain, fire, pests, stand age, soil stocks, and the relations between them, out to at least the second order (a drought that kills trees adds fuel; fuel plus ignition produces fire; fire opens the canopy and dries the stand further).
>
> Three things follow, and each is falsifiable:
>
> 1. This is a **union** of overlapping conditions, not one threshold. A single lever clears the region where only one condition holds and leaves the intersections standing. A drought-plus-fire-plus-edge case is a different object from a drought-only case, not a harder version of it.
> 2. The flip is **per stand or per region**, not per continental mean. A basin can be near neutral while one subregion is already a clear source. Averaging hides the live slot.
> 3. The **anchor is the measured signed flux** (tower, inventory, inversion), not greenness and not a model average. Where the flux is small, two independent instruments can disagree on the sign itself. Greenness does not see respiration or fire emissions.
>
> **What would refute it, written before this message:** (a) a flip with no change in any actor, condition or relation in the habitat; (b) a persistently net-source forest whose biological return loop still has an uptake sign and no lateral export to account for the difference; (c) one condition's threshold that partitions all stands and intervals with no interaction terms; (d) flips that are always spatially synchronous, so no slot crosses before the mean; (e) a proxy shown to map invariantly to net signed flux across biomes and disturbances. If any of these holds, I will retract the corresponding claim.
>
> **What I am not claiming:** no numerical tipping point, no single global threshold, no claim that fire is always a source condition, no claim that a managed forest is a solved state. I am not claiming the framework names the threshold. It returns a shape; the number is owed to an instrument and a stated convention for "persistent".
>
> A one-line "this fails because..." is more useful to me than agreement. If you would rather not engage, no reply is needed.
>
> **If it survives, and only then:** the sources are below. The figures, not my summary, are the authority.
> - Verbatim figure text with line numbers (local framework extract): `FRAMEWORK/reference/03-FIGURES_TEXT.md`, cited as `Fig N :line`.
> - Figures README and canonical usage rule (local): `FRAMEWORK/reference/FIGURES_README_canonical.md`.
> - The two-stage packet with three checkable sub-claims: `08_expert_verification_packet.md` in this folder.
>
> **If you want to reuse the machinery:** the formula is in the local figures folder. Run it on a problem you can check against a ground. Write down what would refute your result *before* you run it, and state your mode. A reported failure is more useful to me than a reported success.

## The two-stage offer

1. **Stage 1.** Send the shape claim and its five refuters. Ask what the recipient would count as a failure. Record their verdict before showing any framework material.
2. **Stage 2, opt-in only.** If they are curious, offer the three links and the verification packet. Their later agreement is never pooled as corroboration, because a recipient who has absorbed the theory has joined the frame being checked (Fig 2 :40 "disqualifying for a verdict: agreement about the world is worthless when the agreeing frames share an evidence pool").
3. **Stage 3.** Offer the formula as a method to reuse, with the instruction that each user writes their own refuter and states their own mode.
