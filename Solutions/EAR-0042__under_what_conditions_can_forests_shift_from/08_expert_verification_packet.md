# Expert verification packet: the Tether on forest sink-to-source shifts

**What this is:** a claim in a form a forest ecologist, fluxes researcher or resilience scientist can check. Mode, derivation and refuters are stated before the conclusion, so a reader knows what to look for and what would settle it.

**Mode: `general` for the shape, `specified` for the per-domain verdicts, `gap` for exact thresholds.** No measurement is claimed. This is a shape and a route, not a result.

## 1. The claim, in one paragraph

A forest becomes a carbon source when the sign of its return loop flips: release by respiration, decomposition, fire and export exceeds uptake for long enough that the stocks and return paths are altered, not merely for one season. The conditions producing the crossing form a union of overlapping habitat items read off the world (water, temperature, fire, pests, stand age, soil stocks and edges, with second-order cascades), not a single threshold. The flip is per stand, so a near-neutral basin can hide a source subregion. Driver valence is relative to the residual, so fire is not intrinsically a source condition. The anchor is the measured signed flux, triangulated across independent instruments rather than averaged. Management introduces and steers but cannot subtract the habitat, so a managed sink is maintained work. Exact numerical thresholds are not claimed; they are a `gap` owed to an instrument and a stated convention for persistence.

## 2. Three checkable sub-claims

### C1: framework-internal, checkable by reading Figures 11, 12 and 9

- **Statement:** the flip is the sign of a return loop, and the conditions are a union of habitat items, not a single threshold.
- **Source, verbatim:** Fig 12 :121 "cycle. The output re-enters as input. The sign of the loop decides whether it closes, and a chain cannot close by construction." Also Fig 11 :43 "The habitat is enumerated, never composed." and Fig 9 :55 "A bundled problem is usually a union of overlapping conditions, not a list of separate ones. A single lever clears the region where only one condition holds and leaves the intersections standing, because the second condition still binds."
- **Refuter:** a persistently net-source forest whose biological return loop still has an uptake sign and no lateral export; or a single condition's threshold that partitions all stands and intervals with no interaction terms and no compound residue.

### C2: physics and ecology facing, checkable against flux and plot data

- **Statement:** a sink/source claim must be anchored in measured signed flux, and the sign itself is method-sensitive where the flux is small; a basin mean can hide a source slot.
- **Source, verbatim:** Fig 2 :29 "An anchor is whatever settles the question and does not change its answer because of what any frame thinks. A measurement, an experiment, a counter, a market, a patient outcome. If it moves when you push on it, it is not an anchor." Also Fig 2 :37-38 "instruments can be wrong on axes they do not measure." and Fig 1 :22 "Aggregating across slots is how a live signal disappears into an average."
- **What to check:** at a boreal site with small net flux, do eddy covariance and biometric inventory agree on the sign? (Campioli 2016 reports they need not.) Does any subregion cross zero while the basin mean does not? (Gatti 2021 and Hubau 2020 report this.)
- **Refuter:** a proxy that maps invariantly to net signed flux across biomes, disturbances and intervals, so the measured-flux anchor is unnecessary; or evidence that flips are always spatially synchronous.

### C3: framework-internal derivation with a world-facing consequence

- **Statement:** driver valence is relative to the residual, management can introduce but not subtract, and exact thresholds are `gap` rather than settled.
- **Source, verbatim:** Fig 11 :50 "The same actor is a benefactor to one residual and a malfactor to another, so mutualism and parasitism are relations rather than kinds." Also Fig 11 :62 "You can add an actor and you cannot subtract the habitat, because contact is a fact about the world and does not consult you." and Fig 3 :27 "A link with no instrument is not a link that works. It is a link nobody has looked at."
- **What to check:** does the same fire event have opposite signs for different residuals (regeneration in a fire-adapted stand, emission pulse in a humid stand)? Is there any management action that removes a habitat condition rather than substituting a habitat?
- **Refuter:** an actor whose valence is invariant across all residuals, or a management action that changes the sign of the net flux without changing any contact. The threshold part is refuted by a cross-network operational definition of persistent shift.

## 3. What is explicitly not claimed

- No numerical tipping point, critical VPD, temperature or moisture threshold.
- No single global driver, and no claim that fire is always a source condition.
- No claim that a continental mean is the unit of a shift, or that greenness measures net flux.
- No claim that management can switch off decomposition, drought or the carbon cycle.
- No claim that the figures are a forest model. They constrain the shape of an answer and the conditions for settling it.
- Not `grounded`. Nothing here is asserted as settled by experiment, and this packet is a simulated derivation.

## 5. What would settle it

A source-discriminating instrument and a convention, not a further argument. From *The Hard Problem is About Source*: an instrument that reads green "cannot comment on the source"; the spectrometer resolved the case. Here:

1. Define a persistent shift: a crossing threshold, a duration, and a stock-change criterion, adopted across networks.
2. Apply towers, biometric plots and atmospheric inversions separately, then compare rather than pool them.
3. Check whether a proxy exists that discriminates uptake from release at all scales. If none exists, the anchor claim stands.

## 6. What an expert can check in twenty minutes

1. Read Fig 12 :121, Fig 11 :43 and :50, Fig 9 :55, Fig 2 :29 and Fig 1 :22. Six short passages.
2. Check C2 against one site you know: is the sink/source sign sensitive to method, and does the mean hide a slot?
3. Name one proxy you would trust to measure net signed flux across biomes, or say why none exists.
4. Name one condition that acts with opposite valence on two different residuals.
5. Send the refutation. A failure is more useful than an endorsement.
