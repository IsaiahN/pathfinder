## 1. A shape is a constraint, not a filler

The canonical README says the framework returns "what a missing thing would have to look like (a shape, not a name)". Fig 9 :32: "you get a kind of thing, never the answer itself".

*The Library of Babel*: "You can cut your search short if the set of books returned is small and descriptive enough that it provides a general shape of the answer that satisfies your question."

For EAR-0042 the missing thing is **the operational threshold for a persistent shift**. Its shape, bound in `01_figure_grounded_audit.md` section 0, is three clauses: a signed crossing, a persistence window, and an alteration of the stocks and return paths. That is a shape. "The Amazon tips at 1.8 kPa VPD" would be a name, and the figures do not license it.

## 2. How the shape was obtained

- *Library of Babel*: use the vocabulary closest to the thing, then "With each new term gained, you narrow the space and deepen your vocabulary." Here the starting vocabulary was GPP, Reco, NEP, NBP, mortality, burned area; the new terms were "union of conditions", "per-slot residual", "signed return loop", "measured flux anchor".
- Fig 9 :30-31: "your words are borrowed too, so describe the effect rather than the cause." The question was restated as an effect (a change in the sign of a flux), not as a cause (which single driver did it).
- *Decoding Tacit Knowledge*: "When contact isn't possible, the best you can send is a plain description of what you'd expect to see, because anyone can check that against the world." Hence the outreach packet asks what a checker would expect to see if the shape were true.
- *Library of Babel* on stopping: "the finish line will also be the starting place". The stop here is not an answer but a `gap`: the instruments exist, the convention is missing.

## 3. What would license a name

Two things, both outside the shape itself:

1. **The ground settles it.** `THE_FORMULA.md` step 5: "the ground settles it. Verified only by what pays. A gate is not the ground." For this question that means a stated, instrument-backed convention for persistence plus measurements that show the stocks changed. Then, and only then, a number can be named.
2. **A provenanced import with a shadow test** (Fig 6 :23, Fig 8 :50-52). A threshold definition from resilience science can be imported, but it must be recorded as borrowed, and it must explain the gap it was brought in to close.

*The Hard Problem is About Source* gives the mechanism. An instrument that reads green "cannot comment on the source"; several sources can produce the same reading; "An instrument that can't distinguish these cases demonstrates its own inadequacy". The spectrometer, not an argument, resolved the case. For forests: satellite greenness cannot distinguish a forest that is still accumulating from one whose respiration and fire emissions have overtaken uptake. What is owed is a better instrument and an explicit convention, not a name read off a proxy.

## 4. Correction to my own reading of section 1

I initially treated "shape, not a name" as if it forbade naming any particular condition, so that "drought" and "fire" would have to stay as blank slots. That is an overstatement. The README's phrase governs a **missing primitive**, one not held in the frame. The conditions here are not primitives of the framework at all. They are habitat items enumerated from the world under Fig 11 :43 "The habitat is enumerated, never composed." and Fig 11 :46 "conditions: light, water, temperature, season, terrain".

What remains forbidden is naming the **threshold** as if the framework produced it. The habitat list is read off the world; the threshold is the missing thing whose shape is given. Both statements are now recorded so neither is quietly lost. This is the same correction pattern the exemplar applies when it narrows "shape, not a name" to missing primitives.

## 5. Two consequences for outreach

**Prestige is not action.** *The Recursive Self-Improvement Trap*: "Prestige can fund an action. It can give an action a budget to be tried. But it cannot settle an action. Only the ground can settle it. The Ground Maintainer must be blind to prestige." A list of famous names agreeing that forests are tipping is the named collapse. The legitimate ask is a budget to try the shape against a ground.

**This audit is a simulation, not a ground reading.** Same essay: "LLMs can quickly simulate an approximate answer/output" but "the truly testable output would need to be derived from the real architecture or working the formula yourself", and LLM output is "simulacra". So `01_figure_grounded_audit.md` is a simulated derivation checked for internal consistency against the figures. It changes no contact and settles nothing it reports on.
