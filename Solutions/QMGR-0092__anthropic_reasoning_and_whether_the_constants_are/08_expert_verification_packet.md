**What this is:** a claim in a form a cosmologist, philosopher of science or philosopher of physics can check. Mode, derivation and refuters are stated before the conclusion, so a reader knows what to look for and what would settle it.

**Mode: `specified` on the per-domain verdicts, `general` on the shape, `gap` on the ground verdict. Not `grounded`.** No measurement is claimed. This is a shape and a route, not a result.

## 1. The claim, in one paragraph

"Are the constants fine-tuned for life?" is a union of three questions, not one: are the life-permitting ranges narrow; what does our existence license us to infer; and what fixes the values. The constants must be kept as separate slots, never averaged. Anthropic selection is a filter on which observations we can make, not a verdict about whether the values are fine-tuned. Turning it into a verdict requires an anchor over possible values that does not move when our existence is pushed, and no such anchor is reachable from inside our universe's closure. From here a deeper theory and a brute given look the same. The owed object is an ordered comparison between a value and a permitted range under an anchor the framework cannot name. The shape survives; any claim that the constants are, or are not, fine-tuned at the ground does not yet follow.

## 2. Three checkable sub-claims

### C1: framework-internal; checkable by reading Figures 9 and 2

- **Statement:** selection conditioning is a filter for the budget, never a witness for the verdict. The fact "we exist" cannot serve as the anchor for the fine-tuning claim.
- **Source, verbatim:** Fig 9 :60, "Use filters for the budget and witnesses for the verdict. Never let a filter hand you a verdict." Fig 2 :29, "An anchor is whatever settles the question and does not change its answer because of what any frame thinks. A measurement, an experiment, a counter, a market, a patient outcome. If it moves when you push on it, it is not an anchor."
- **What to check:** is the filter/witness distinction applied to the inference, not used to ban inference inside an imported ensemble? If it is used as a blanket ban, that is a misreading; the narrowing is recorded in `01` §8.
- **Refuter:** exhibit a case where conditioning on our existence yields a ground-settled verdict about the constants without an independently motivated measure, and where the conditioning does more than redirect where we look.

### C2: philosophy and physics facing; checkable against the published debate

- **Statement:** no anchor over possible constants is currently available that is independent of the observed values. The measure problem is open, and the life-permitting argument lacks a settled definition of "life". The question is therefore a union compound, not a single question with a single lever.
- **Source, verbatim:** Fig 9 :54, "And when the split lands on a union rather than a partition, unbundle before you search again." Fig 3 :27, "A link with no instrument is not a link that works. It is a link nobody has looked at."
- **What to check:** SEP *Fine-Tuning* §1.2 on the lack of a widely accepted definition of life, and §2.1 on the impossibility of a non-arbitrary uniform measure over the reals; Barnes (2021) §4 on the measure problem. Do these sources support "no settled anchor", and do they also support "no answer is possible"? They support the first, not the second. The claim is deliberately weaker than the literature's strong pessimism.
- **Refuter:** exhibit a measure that is fixed independently of the value, does not update on our existence, and yields a novel successful prediction; or show that a single lever does clear all three sub-questions.

### C3: framework-internal derivation

- **Statement:** "fine-tuned for life" is an ordered threshold comparison between a value and a permitted range, second order, under an anchor the framework cannot name. It is a comparison bond, not a mechanism and not a seventh primitive.
- **Source, verbatim:** Fig 12 :86, "a threshold relation between two quantities". Fig 12 :112, "A plan is a sequence. A goal is a comparison. Progress is a subtraction." Fig 12 :104, "A settled molecule becomes an atom for whatever composes over it. So irreducible is a fact about a level and never about the world". Fig 9 :32, "you get a kind of thing, never the answer itself".
- **What to check:** does the comparison survive when the operands are swapped? It should not: the Operators table identifies ⋛ by "is it about which is larger, rather than both being present? Yes." Is the second-order composition legal under Fig 13 :75, "Two states never bond directly. It is not space-time but space, a structure, time; and two structures join only through a state"? The re-census is in `06` Q3. The operand type of ⋛ at that level is a live strain, not a settled point.
- **Refuter:** write the fine-tuning claim without any comparison bond and lose nothing; or show that two bare orderings compose directly with no intervening state; or show a settled atom cannot be an operand of ⋛ at any level.

## 3. What is explicitly not claimed

- Not that the constants are fine-tuned, and not that they are not.
- Not that a multiverse, a designer or a brute given is the answer. These are bindings from frames, not derivations.
- Not that anthropic reasoning is useless. It is a legitimate within-ensemble inference when the measure was fixed independently and carries a pre-registered refuter.
- Not that no mechanism will ever be found. Fig 8 :39, "So a search that finds nothing is never a proof of absence."
- Not grounded. Nothing here is asserted as settled by experiment.
- Not that this derivation tests the claim. It is a simulation checked for internal consistency against the figures.

## 5. What would settle it

1. **Anchor test:** a measure over possible values fixed before the value is used, independent of our existence, with a pre-registered refuter that survives. Barnes's account of Weinberg's Λ bound is the candidate; it fails the gate if the distribution was fitted after.
2. **Breadth test:** life-permitting ranges measured under that measure. If broad, the residual dissolves.
3. **Dynamical test:** a derivation of the constants from principles, so alternative values are not possible.
4. The six standing refuters in `07` §3, any one of which withdraws the claim.

## 6. What an expert can check in twenty minutes

1. Read Fig 9 :60, Fig 2 :29, Fig 9 :54 and Fig 12 :86. Four short passages.
2. Check C2 against what you know of the measure problem and the definition of life. Is "no settled anchor" defensible, and is the claim's refusal to conclude stronger or weaker than the literature warrants?
3. Name one instrument or one gated import that would discriminate among the sources of a life-permitting reading, or say why none is possible at this budget.
4. Send the refutation. A failure is more useful than an endorsement, and later agreement is never pooled as corroboration.
