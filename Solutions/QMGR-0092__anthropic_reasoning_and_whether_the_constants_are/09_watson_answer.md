## VERDICT (from the figures)

The figures settle the shape of the reasoning and do not settle whether the constants are fine-tuned in the ground sense. The question is three loops bundled together. Keep the constants as separate slots. Anthropic selection is a filter for the budget, never a witness for the verdict. An anchor over possible values is owed and is unreachable from inside our universe's closure; from here a deeper theory and a brute given look the same. The owed object is an ordered threshold comparison between a value and a permitted range, at second order, under an anchor the framework cannot name. "Unreachable is a verdict here, not a silence" (Fig 13 :134). The naive claim the claim that the constants are fine-tuned for life does not survive without a measure, and neither does its denial. This is `specified` on structure, `general` on shape, `gap` on the ground verdict, `candidate` until an anchor pays.

- **cost if wrong:** bounded and locatable. If the filter/witness distinction is wrong, the cost is a stopped search, since a filter presented as a verdict removes the reason to look for a ground. If the refusal to name an anchor is wrong, the cost is a missed gated import such as a well motivated measure with a pre-registered refuter. Both are avoidable because the unbundling, the six refuters and the provenance gate are written down before the conclusion. The larger cost is spending the independence of outside readers by sending a recording instead of a residual, which Fig 2 :42 prices: "Buy independence in proportion to the cost of being wrong."
- **undoable:** yes. The solution asserts no value for any constant and no answer about their source. A gated measure, a demonstration that the ranges are broad, or a dynamical derivation of the constants can be added without retracting the shape. The verdict is about the form of the inference, so it survives changes to the content.
- **reversed by:** a ground reading, not agreement. A measure fixed independently of the observed value, with a pre-registered refuter that survives, would name the anchor; a measurement showing the life-permitting ranges to be broad would dissolve the residual; a derivation of the constants from principles would remove the comparison's reference. A figure re-census that admits a filter as a verdict, or one that derives one of the six terms from the other five, would break the shape. Until one of these fires, the verdict remains `candidate`.
