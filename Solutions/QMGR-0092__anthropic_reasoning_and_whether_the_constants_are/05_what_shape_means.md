## 1. A shape is a constraint, not a filler

From *The Library of Babel*: "you can cut your search short if the set of books returned is small and descriptive enough that it provides a general shape of the answer that satisfies your question." And: "With each new term gained, you are able to narrow the space by definition and deepen your vocabulary."

Fig 9 :32: "you get a kind of thing, never the answer itself." Fig 13 :135: "It cannot say which primitive is missing, because naming it would mean already holding it."

So a **shape** is the narrowed form of an answer you can state before you can name it. It constrains; it does not fill. For this question the shape is: *a comparison between a constant's value and its life-permitting range under an anchor that does not update on our existence*. Naming the anchor (multiverse measure, designer, brute given) is a name, not a shape.

**Falsifier:** if the shape constrains nothing, meaning any answer whatever satisfies it, the reading fails.

## 2. How the shape is obtained

- *Library of Babel*: use the vocabulary already held that is closest to the thing, then look for recurring new terms that overlap or correlate with it. "With each new term gained, you are able to narrow the space by definition and deepen your vocabulary."
- *Library of Babel* on the anchor: "This anchor is provided by your own question". The residual selects the search; it does not get chosen by the searcher.
- Fig 6 :30: "The description travels. Fails by being sharpened into terms only we can read. Cast it in effect terms." Fig 9 :30-31: "your words are borrowed too, so describe the effect rather than the cause."
- *Library of Babel* on bundling: "Perhaps your problem was a bundle or conflation of several problems masquerading as one? You will need to search all of those problems individually to find their answers and then combine them in a satisfactory way." This is the essay-level authority for the unbundling in `01` F5.
- *Library of Babel* on stopping: "you know whether to stop or continue and which direction to turn next." The stop is when the residual stops registering, not when a preferred answer arrives.

Applied here: describe the residual in effect terms (per-constant gap between observed value and permitted range, with no anchor available), then ask which frame predicts that shape. Do not begin with "multiverse" or "design" and then fit the residual to it. Fig 9 :27-28: "describe it before you go looking, or any frame you pick will seem to fit."

**Falsifier:** if the residual can be fully described only after a frame is chosen, the derive-first order fails.

## 3. What licenses a name

Only two things, both outside the shape itself:

1. **The ground settles it.** `THE_FORMULA.md` step 5: "SETTLE the ground settles it. Verified only by what pays." The canonical README says the framework "stops at the limit of what the available instruments can distinguish, and records the lineage so the stop is honest."
2. **A provenanced import from a frame whose closure differs** (Fig 6 :23, Fig 8 :50-51), with a source gate and a shadow test. *They're Scanning Books*: "The real import is not a new term in the existing space. It is a new dimension of the space." A measure over universes is a new dimension, not a new constant, so if it is imported it must pass the gate and carry its record.

For this question, neither condition is met. The measure is not settled by an instrument, and no measure currently on offer has both an independently fixed prior and a pre-registered refuter that survived.

**Falsifier:** exhibit an instrument or a gated import that fixes the measure, and the "name withheld" part of the shape is falsified.

## 4. The specific canonical error for this question: reading a source off a projection

*The Hard Problem is About Source* gives the mechanism. Metamerism: "the instrument is speaking only about what it measured, it cannot comment on the source." One green reading can come from one green light, or yellow plus blue, or RGB, or CMYK, or "a completely novel color scheme that humans have not yet discovered that can comprise the entire color set." The essay states: "If several paths create the same behavior, then behavior is a projection with lossy information". And: "It was proven wrong not by argument, but by the arrival of a better instrument."

Applied here: the observation "the constants are life-permitting" is a reading. It cannot comment on the source. Several sources can produce the same reading: a narrow distribution over constants, a broad distribution plus strong selection, a dynamical law that fixes the values, or no distribution at all. The reading does not discriminate among them. What is owed is a source-discriminating instrument, not a name for the source.

This is not a claim that the constants are not fine-tuned. It is a claim about what the observation can support. Fig 8 :39: "So a search that finds nothing is never a proof of absence."

**Falsifier:** produce an instrument that discriminates among those sources at the current ground, and the metamerism claim is withdrawn.

## 5. Why "fine-tuned for life" is a shape, not a mechanism

Fig 12 :112: "A plan is a sequence. A goal is a comparison. Progress is a subtraction." The comparison bond ⋛ is "a threshold relation between two quantities" (Fig 12 :86). "Fine-tuned for life" compares an observed value with a permitted range; it is ordered and goal-shaped. It does not say how the value got there.

*They're Scanning Books*: "Declaring the primitives does not make them neutral. It makes the choice visible". Declaring a reference class or a measure does the same: it makes the choice visible, not neutral. The choice remains authored until the ground pays.

**Falsifier:** write the fine-tuning claim so that it yields a mechanism for the values and loses no evidential content, and this section fails.

## 6. Correction to my own reading

I initially treated "shape, not a name" as a blanket ban on naming any element of the answer. That was too broad, and the same correction was made in the exemplar `QMGR-0037` `05` §5b. The canonical README's phrase governs a **missing** thing. A measure over possible constants is not among the six terms, so it is missing and the limit applies to it. But a comparison bond and a constant value are already available, so describing the comparison is permitted.

What remains wrong in the naive framing is not that it names a held atom, but that it reads the source off a projection (section 4) and that it treats an authored measure as a ground-settled anchor. Both claims are now recorded, neither quietly erased.

## 7. Two consequences for this solution

**Prestige is not action.** From *The Recursive Self-Improvement Trap*: "Prestige can fund an action. It can give an action a budget to be tried. But it cannot settle an action. Only the ground can settle it." A set of famous names agreeing that anthropic reasoning works is the named collapse, not corroboration. The legitimate ask is a budget for the inference to be tried and a ground to settle it.

**My own audit is a simulation, not a ground reading.** Same essay: "the output is only as good as the validator. The agent, or the tool, or the frame, or the theory, cannot validate itself." And: "the truly testable output would need to be derived from the real architecture or working the formula yourself." So `01`, `06`, `07`, `09` and `11` are simulated derivations checked for internal consistency against the figures. They are not tests of the fine-tuning question and must be labelled as such. They cannot settle anything they report on.
