## Two findings that bear directly on the question

1. **The anthropic objection is a live dispute, not a settled tool.** SEP §3.2 records that Sober's formulation makes the likelihoods trivially equal once observation selection is included, while Weisberg and Kotzen argue the selection effect should be represented differently. Wikipedia adds that no possible observational evidence bears on Carter's WAP. This corroborates Fig 9 :60, "Use filters for the budget and witnesses for the verdict. Never let a filter hand you a verdict." It does not by itself settle whether a filter may ever be promoted, which is why `01` narrows the reading rather than forbidding anthropic inference outright.

2. **Weinberg's cosmological constant bound is the strongest case for an anthropic prediction, and its status depends on the record.** Barnes reports that the bound was computed before observation and gives the explicit falsifier: a value orders of magnitude smaller would have meant an assumption was wrong. This corroborates Fig 8 :50-51, "Convergent derivation and adopted import look identical in the contents. Only the record of where each came from separates them." Send the order of operations and the refuter, not the numerical closeness.

Note on overlap: SEP Fine-Tuning and Barnes both draw on Carr and Rees 1979 and Weinberg 1987. Their agreement is partly one evidence pool. Their methods differ (philosophical census versus physics calculation), which is the only independence claimed.

## Distribution: offer, do not push

Figure-grounded reasons:

- **Fig 4 :22** - "Only methods cross up. Only head starts come down." Sending the full derivation before anyone has described a residual sends a recording, not a generator. Lead with the unbundling and the refuter.
- **Fig 10 :42** - "A verifier that can reconstruct the claim cannot verify it." A recipient who has absorbed the framework before giving a verdict has joined the frame being checked.
- **Fig 9 :27-28** - "describe it before you go looking, or any frame you pick will seem to fit." Ask what they would count as a failure before showing them any proposed measure or multiverse.
- **Fig 2 :35** - "Alignment is triangulation, not negotiation. A mirror cannot correct you." Do not ask whether they agree that anthropic reasoning is a filter. Ask what they would expect to see if it were a verdict.
- **Fig 2 :40** - "disqualifying for a verdict: agreement about the world is worthless when the agreeing frames share an evidence pool." Do not sort recipients by receptiveness.

**Recommended order in a reply:** (1) the unbundled residual and the filter/witness distinction; (2) the pre-registered refuter and the question "what would you count as a failure?"; (3) record their stage-1 verdict; (4) only if they are curious, the figure text and source links, with the note that the figures, not the summary, are the authority.

## The two-stage offer

**(1)** Check the stage-1 inference against the ground, with no framework material shown. **(2)** Only if curious, read the figures that produced it. **(3)** Optionally, reuse the eight-step loop on a problem the reader can check, with their own refuter written first.

- Stage 1 before stage 2 is Fig 10 :52, "Bring a characterised residual, never the source." The recipient checks the inference without holding the framework's interior.
- Stage 2 is opt-in by construction. Once a recipient has absorbed the theory, their agreement stops being evidence, so the stage-1 verdict must be recorded before anything else is shown (Fig 2 :40).
- Stage 3 sends a method, which is permitted: Fig 4 :22, "Only methods cross up. Only head starts come down."
- Stage 3 still needs a ground per problem. `THE_FORMULA.md` "Direction" says a frame "cannot check the direction of its own question". Each user must write down what would refute their result and state the mode of what they get.

**Hard precondition:** stages 1 to 3 only work if the stage-1 inference survives. As drafted, it survives only when the three loops are unbundled and the filter is not presented as a verdict (`01`, F2 and F5). Repair before sending.
