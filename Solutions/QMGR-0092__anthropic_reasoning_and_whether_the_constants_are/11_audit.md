## Independent domain census, written from the residual line alone

I redid the census before relying on the primary's conclusions. WATSON order: R by arity, symmetry and scale; one line per domain with actors, currency and anchor; partition or union; order by dependency.

- **R.** Arity: many slots, one per constant, explicitly not an aggregate. Symmetry: ordered threshold comparison between a value and a permitted range; swapping operands changes the assertion. Scale: first order inside one universe, second order across an ensemble or a deeper theory. The residual persists because the reference distribution is unavailable, so it does not trend down.
- **Domain 1, measurement.** Actors: dimensionless constants, Standard Model, GR. Currency: values and life-permitting ranges. Anchor: experiment. Real domain.
- **Domain 2, anthropic inference.** Actors: observers and reference classes. Currency: selection weights. Anchor: none, since "we exist" moves with the frame. Not yet a domain; a filter on domain 1.
- **Domain 3, ensemble and measure.** Actors: vacua, pockets, observer-moments, measure. Currency: N(X) P(X). Anchor: a measure that does not update on our observation. Import candidate whose gates have not passed.
- **Domain 4, framework.** Actors: six terms, seven bonds, negation. Currency: composition then settlement. Anchor: figures and ground.
- **Domain 5, verification.** Actors: expert readers. Currency: agreement and residual. Anchor: ground, never agreement. Its independence is zero in this simulation.
- **Union.** Three loops are bundled: measurement of ranges, inference under selection, explanation of values. A single lever cannot clear the intersections.
- **Order by dependency.** Perceive, acquire vocabulary for life-permitting and measure, state an objective, plan a lever, carry the lineage. The break is at vocabulary, confirmed by the lack of an instrument for "life" and for "measure".

My census matches the primary's five domains and its union reading. Where the primary derived from Fig 1 :19 and Fig 9 :54, I derived from Fig 3's dependency links and Fig 2's anchor test. Different starting figures, same domain boundaries. That is low overlap between **routes within one evidence pool**, so it is weak internal agreement, not corroboration.
