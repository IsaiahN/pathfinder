## 3. Pre-registered refuter (written before this synthesis and before the WATSON verdict)

Seat Map :287 (local: `FRAMEWORK/figures/THE_SEAT_MAP_Field_Guide_amended.md`): "And a falsifier written before the run is a design instrument, not only an honesty device."

The shape claim is refuted if any of the following holds:

1. A frame-independent anchor over the constants is exhibited, does not update on our existence, is not authored by the frame that needs it, and predicts a constant value not already known.
2. Under an independently motivated measure the life-permitting ranges are shown to be broad, so the residual does not register.
3. A theory derives the constants from principles, so the comparison has a fixed reference and "fine-tuning" dissolves.
4. A selection filter is shown to settle a ground verdict without an imported measure (breaks Fig 9 :60).
5. One of the six terms is derived from the other five, or the residual is shown to be genuinely aggregate (breaks the census).
6. The fine-tuning claim is written without a comparison bond and loses nothing.

If any fires, the claim is withdrawn. Until one is run, nothing here is `grounded`.

## 4. What it explicitly does not say

- Not that the constants are fine-tuned, and not that they are not. No ground verdict is asserted.
- Not that a multiverse exists, that a designer exists, or that the values are brute facts. Those are bindings from frames, not derivations from the six.
- Not that anthropic reasoning is useless. It is a legitimate inference inside an ensemble whose measure was fixed independently. It is a filter, and a filter has a budget use.
- Not that no mechanism will ever be found. Fig 8 :39: "So a search that finds nothing is never a proof of absence."
- Not that the comparison is a new primitive. It is a bond over held quantities (Fig 12 :86).
- Not grounded. No instrument settles `left(R,φ)` at this budget.

## 5. What would settle it: the ground

A source-discriminating instrument or a gated import, not a further argument. From *The Hard Problem is About Source* (read 2026-11-23): "It was proven wrong not by argument, but by the arrival of a better instrument." The reading "life-permitting constants" cannot comment on its source; several sources give the same reading.

- **Anchor test:** exhibit a measure over possible values that is fixed before the value is used, does not move when the observation is pushed, and survives a pre-registered refuter. Barnes's account of Weinberg's Λ bound is the candidate; it fails if the distribution was chosen after the value was known.
- **Breadth test:** measure the life-permitting ranges under that measure. If they are broad (the Adams 2019 direction), the residual dissolves.
- **Dynamical test:** derive the values from principles, as Einstein hoped, so that alternative values are not possible and the comparison loses its reference.
- **Standing falsifiers from section 3:** any of the six can fire.

## 7. One-paragraph version for outreach

> The Tether does not decide whether the constants are fine-tuned for life, and it does not claim the final answer. It says the question is three questions bundled together: are the life-permitting ranges narrow, what does our existence license us to infer, and what fixes the values. It keeps the constants as separate slots. It says anthropic selection is a filter on where we can look, not a witness for what is true; to turn it into a verdict we need an anchor that does not move when we push on it, and no such anchor is available from inside our universe. From here, a deeper theory and a brute given look the same. What the framework offers is the shape any answer must have: an ordered comparison between a value and a permitted range under an anchor it cannot name, with the refuters written in advance and the instrument that would close it. The center stays open until the ground settles it.
