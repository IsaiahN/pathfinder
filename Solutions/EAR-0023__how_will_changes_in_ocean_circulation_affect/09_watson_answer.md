## VERDICT (from the figures)

The shape survives; the empirical forecast does not exist in this run. A far-field ocean effect must be answered per region and per medium; the three media are a union with separate grounds; the effect travels through a network imported from a differing closure; the pathway is an ordered cascade three steps out; the middle dependency link is the one to instrument; and observations, not model agreement, settle each slot. The regional sign, size and timing is a declared `gap`, not a silence and not a forecast. Mode `specified` for the shape, `general` for the structure.

- **cost if wrong:** bounded and locatable for the claim, real for the decision. If the union is treated as one averaged problem, a single lever clears one condition and leaves the intersections standing; a region could be prepared for cooling while its rainfall tail and ecosystem threshold move the other way. Model agreement from a shared CMIP pool would be mistaken for corroboration (Fig 2 :40). For long-lived infrastructure the error is partly irreversible, which is the figures' reason to buy independence in proportion to the cost (Fig 2 :42).
- **undoable:** yes for the claim, no for the world. No term is deleted if the shape fails; a refutation is added and the reach falls because something was added, so the correction is reversible and recorded. A sent packet can be superseded. Physical ecosystem shifts and infrastructure lock-in are not undoable by a revision to the claim, which is why the falsifiers are written before use.
- **reversed by:** a ground reading that changes contact. A pathway-resolving attribution that separates circulation-driven from radiative-driven change in a named region; a sustained transport record long enough to settle the ocean-end trend; a third-order ecological series with a stated threshold; or any pre-registered refuter firing (one lever clears all three media, a local-only frame derives the remote response, a global mean predicts the per-slot residuals, the middle link measures high while predictions fail, or two pathway arrangements give identical outcomes). None is a vote or an endorsement. Until one fires, the verdict stands as `candidate`.
