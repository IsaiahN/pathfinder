## 1. A shape is a constraint, not a filler

The canonical rule: the framework returns *"what a missing thing would have to look like (a shape, not a name)."* Fig 9 :32: *"you get a kind of thing, never the answer itself"*. Fig 13 :134: *"Unreachable is a verdict here, not a silence"*.

From *The Library of Babel*: *"You can cut your search short if the set of books returned is small and descriptive enough that it provides a general shape of the answer that satisfies your question."*

From *How Intelligence Actually Works*, the replicator case: *"a tool that provides the shape of something that you described"*, where each recursive pass *"adds content, not just constraint"*. And the closing limit: *"The recursion converges toward indiscernibility, and indiscernibility is not identity."*

For EAR-0023 the shape is therefore **the narrowed form of an answer we can state before we can name it**: a per-region, per-medium structure with a stated pathway, a dependency order, an anchor and a refuter. A regional number is a filler; the shape is the constraint that any such number must satisfy.

## 2. How the shape is obtained for this question

- *Library of Babel*: use the vocabulary already held that is *"closest to the words that you wish to describe and grasp the shape of"*, then *"With each new term gained, you narrow the space and deepen your vocabulary."* For this question the closest held terms are flow (energy + gradient), network (influence travels), cascade, anchor and per-slot residual.
- Fig 6 :30: *"The description travels. Fails by being sharpened into terms only we can read. Cast it in effect terms."* Fig 9 :27: *"describe it before you go looking"*. So the residual is stated as an effect (a remote mismatch), not as a mechanism (a particular current or wave train).
- *Decoding Tacit Knowledge*: *"When contact isn't possible, the best you can send is a plain description of what you'd expect to see, because anyone can check that against the world."* This is the outreach rule in `02_outreach_email.md`: send a checkable description of the expected far-field pattern, not the framework.
- *Library of Babel* on stopping: *"the finish line will also be the starting place"*. For this question, the stop is the point where further framework recursion stops producing new regional discriminations and only more observation would help.

## 3. What licenses a name here

Two things, both outside the shape:

1. **The ground settles it.** Formula step 5: *"the ground settles it. Verified only by what pays."* The canonical rule says the framework *"stops at the limit of what the available instruments can distinguish, and records the lineage so the stop is honest."* A sustained transport record, a gauge series or an ecological survey is such an instrument.
2. **A provenanced import from a frame whose closure differs** (Fig 6 :23, Fig 8 :50-52), with source gate and shadow test. From *They're Scanning Books*: *"The real import is not a new term in the existing space. It is a new dimension of the space."* Teleconnection physics is an import of a pathway description, not a new substrate primitive.

*They're Scanning Books* adds the warning that matters for an earth-systems question: *"A minimum viable set of six terms is a human's careful guess at reality's compression axes; another sensorium's minimum set need not be those six."* And: *"Declaring the primitives does not make them neutral. It makes the choice visible."* The six-term substrate is a visible guess, not a discovery of nature's own list. Naming one of them as the unique cause of a regional response turns a visible choice into an invisible one.

### Correction to my own §3 (visible, not erased)

I initially wrote that "shape, not a name" forbids naming any pathway. That overstates the rule. The canonical phrase governs a **missing** primitive, one not held in Γ. The network and the gradient are already among the six (Fig 13). Naming the atmospheric bridge as the carrier is a **binding from the physics frame onto a held structure**, which is a provenance question, not a prohibition. What remains missing, and therefore still governed by "shape, not a name", is a source-discriminating instrument that would tell us which pathway arrangement produced a given regional reading. Both readings are recorded so neither is quietly lost.

## 4. Why a single global number is the specific forbidden error

*The Hard Problem is About Source* gives the mechanism. Metamerism: an instrument reads green and *"cannot comment on the source"*. The essay's conclusion: *"If several paths create the same behavior, then behavior is a projection with lossy information"*, and *"The answer is unreached until a better instrument exists."* It was the spectrometer, not an argument, that resolved the case.

For EAR-0023 the parallel is exact in structure. A global mean temperature, or a single AMOC strength index, is a projection onto which several pathway arrangements can map. Two circulation states with the same strength but different pathway geometry can produce the same global reading and different regional outcomes. Naming the pathway from the global reading is the metamerism error. What is owed is a source-discriminating observation (a regional fingerprint, a pathway-resolving reanalysis), not a name.

Fig 1 :22: *"Aggregating across slots is how a live signal disappears into an average."* *How a Society of Agents Would Work* repeats it for groups: *"Aggregating across slots is how a live signal disappears into an average."* Two different domains, one rule, low overlap.

## 5. Why agreement is not the closing instrument

*The Recursive Self-Improvement Trap*: *"the output is only as good as the validator. The agent, or the tool, or the frame, or the theory, cannot validate itself."* And: *"Prestige can fund an action. It can give an action a budget to be tried. But it cannot settle an action. Only the ground can settle it."*

*Decoding Tacit Knowledge* adds: *"Sounding right isn't the same as being checked against the world, which is why I call the Turing test a test of fluency, not of accuracy."* An ensemble of models that agree because they share CMIP inheritance is fluent agreement, not an anchor. Fig 2 :40 and Fig 7 :13 make the same point from the figures.

## 6. Two consequences for this package

**My own audit is a simulation, not a ground reading.** *The Recursive Self-Improvement Trap*: *"LLMs can quickly simulate an approximate answer/output"*, but *"the truly testable output would need to be derived from the real architecture or working the formula yourself"*. *Arrival of the Fittest* says the outputs generated this way *"must be seen as simulacra"* and that the results *"could never be verified if the solutions presented were not contrived to fit the formula."* So `01_figure_grounded_audit.md` is a simulated derivation checked for internal consistency against the figures. It is labelled as such everywhere and it cannot settle the question it reports on.

**The framework cannot validate itself.** *Arrival of the Fittest*: *"Because it is a metatheory, it cannot be validated, nor can it validate itself."* *They're Scanning Books* calls the six terms a human's careful guess. Therefore this package does not claim the shape is the truth. It claims the shape is the narrowed form a ground reading must have, and it names the observations that would close or break it.

## 7. What an expert should take from this

The two-stage offer in `02_outreach_email.md` follows from §4 to §6: the recipient is asked to check a per-region, per-medium expectation against the world **before** seeing the framework. Their verdict on stage 1 is recorded first. Later agreement is never pooled as corroboration, because *"When contact isn't possible, the best you can send is a plain description of what you'd expect to see, because anyone can check that against the world."*
