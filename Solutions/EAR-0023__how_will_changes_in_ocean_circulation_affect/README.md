## What this folder is, in effect terms

The residual is non-local: a change in a basin-scale ocean flow produces a mismatch in remote land slots that a frame predicting local conditions from local conditions cannot close. The question names three media, rainfall, temperature and ecosystems, which this package treats as a **union of overlapping conditions**, not as one hard question to be averaged (Fig 9 :55, :57). What a Tether derivation can return is the **shape** any answer must have, the dependency order of the links that carry the effect, the observations that could settle it, and a refuter written in advance. It cannot return the sign, size or timing of a regional change: that is ground work, and this package says so plainly.

## What is deliberately not here

- No personal emails of named individuals.
- No ranking of people by how likely they are to agree with the shape claim.
- No bulk-send plan.
- No regional number presented as a Tether result. The figures return a shape, not a name (Fig 9 :32); a regional number needs a ground reading this simulated run did not take.

Three reasons, in the figures' own words:

1. **A room does not upgrade the label.** Fig 7 :13: *"agreement compounds, evidence does not"* and Fig 7 :35: *"A room's agreement is variation, not a verdict. Selection between rooms is where the ground votes."* A list of people selected for receptiveness is a list selected to agree.
2. **Correlation disqualifies a verdict.** Fig 2 :40: *"disqualifying for a verdict: agreement about the world is worthless when the agreeing frames share an evidence pool"*. Selecting recipients for likely agreement manufactures the correlation that makes their agreement worthless.
3. **Asking for trust before checking spends independence.** Fig 10 :42: *"A verifier that can reconstruct the claim cannot verify it."* An outside reader who has absorbed the framework before giving a verdict has joined the frame being checked.

Legally: unsolicited bulk email to scraped personal addresses breaches GDPR and CAN-SPAM style rules in many jurisdictions and gets the sending domain blacklisted. That is a practical failure on top of the structural one. All channels in `03_public_contact_points.csv` are public institutional or opt-in routes.

## Honest standing

This is a simulated derivation checked for internal consistency against the figures, not a ground reading. The framework's own essay says the truly testable output *"would need to be derived from the real architecture or working the formula yourself"* (see `05_what_shape_means.md` §6). Consequently this package cannot settle the question it reports on. It narrows the answer's shape, states what would close it, and records where it stopped.
