# The Tether's answer: ocean circulation changes and far-off rainfall, temperature and ecosystems

**Mode: `specified` for the shape, `general` for the structural facts. The empirical forecast is a `gap`. Nothing is `grounded`.** This is a simulated derivation checked against the figures (`05_what_shape_means.md` §6), not a ground reading.

## 1. The residual it answers

A basin-scale ocean circulation change reorganises the atmospheric pathways that deliver heat and moisture to distant land. A frame predicting a region from local conditions alone misses. The miss is indexed by region and medium (Fig 1 :19), so there is no single global residual for this question. Rainfall, temperature and ecosystems are a union of overlapping conditions (Fig 9 :55), not one hard problem with three labels.

## 2. The shape

1. **Answer per slot, never averaged.** Fig 1 :19: *"The residual is indexed by object slot, not aggregated."* Fig 1 :22: *"Aggregating across slots is how a live signal disappears into an average."* The answer is a table over regions and media. A global mean can stay near zero while a remote region carries a live residual.
2. **The three media are a union.** Fig 9 :57: *"What remains is smaller and it is also categorically different: a case in two conditions is not a harder case of either."* Unbundle before searching; keep each verdict separate and never average them.
3. **The effect is carried by a network; a local frame cannot derive it.** Fig 13 :25: *"that entities relate, and influence travels"*. Fig 6 :23: *"No frame escapes its own closure. The bound moves only on contact with a frame whose closure differs."* The circulation is a flow (energy + gradient); the bridge is a network. The pathway frame is an import from a differing closure, found by describing the residual first (Fig 8 :53). It adds no seventh primitive.
4. **The pathway is an ordered cascade.** Fig 12 :117: *"isomer. Same atoms, different bond, different substance. The difference is invisible in the formula and decisive in the behaviour."* Ocean → atmosphere → land surface → biota. Same forcing under a different pathway arrangement is a different term. The ecosystem effect is three steps out (Fig 11 :52).
5. **The middle link is the one to instrument.** Fig 3 :27: *"A link with no instrument is not a link that works. It is a link nobody has looked at."* The ocean end and the gauge end have instruments; the vocabulary for what a circulation change affords a remote region is thinner. Effort above the break cannot rescue it.
6. **Observations settle; agreement does not.** Fig 2 :29: *"An anchor is whatever settles the question and does not change its answer because of what any frame thinks."* Fig 2 :35: *"Alignment is triangulation, not negotiation. A mirror cannot correct you."* Ensembles are rooms; their agreement is a shared-pool signal, not a verdict.
7. **The regional sign, size and timing is a `gap`.** Fig 9 :32: *"you get a kind of thing, never the answer itself"*. Fig 13 :134: *"Unreachable is a verdict here, not a silence"*. No figure line returns a named regional outcome. The instruments to close it exist but the records are short and the pathways contested.

## 3. What it explicitly does not say

- Not a regional forecast. No sign, magnitude or date is returned for any named place.
- Not "AMOC collapse will cool Europe" or any other single headline. That is a world-source claim, not a figure result, and the world sources dispute both timing and magnitude.
- Not a single lever. The three media are a union; one intervention cannot be assumed to clear all three.
- Not a derivation of teleconnection physics from the six-term set. The pathway is a provenanced import, recorded as such.
- Not agreement-based. Model consensus is not corroboration (Fig 2 :40).
- Not `grounded`. No measurement was taken in this simulated run.

## 4. What would settle it — the ground

The figures name the kind of instrument, not its reading. From *The Hard Problem is About Source* (quoted in `05_what_shape_means.md` §4): an instrument that reads green *"cannot comment on the source"*; the spectrometer resolved it. Here the corresponding instruments are:

- **Pathway-resolving attribution:** does a regional rainfall or temperature change separate into a circulation-driven component and a radiative-forcing-driven component? That would tell which pathway arrangement produced the reading.
- **Sustained transport observations:** RAPID at 26°N, OSNAP in the subpolar North Atlantic, Argo floats. A longer record moves the `gap` toward a settled sign for the ocean end.
- **Per-slot ecological series:** a long record at a remote forest or fishery that registers the third-order effect with a threshold, not just a mean shift.
- **Falsifiers from `01` §4:** one lever clearing all three residuals; a local-only derivation of the remote response; global mean predicting per-slot residuals; a zero-independence middle link; two pathway arrangements with identical outcomes. Any one firing breaks the shape.

## 5. The honest stop

**Unreached, not unreachable.** *They're Scanning Books* (quoted in `05_what_shape_means.md` §3) says the six terms are *"a human's careful guess at reality's compression axes"*; declaring them makes the choice visible, not neutral. The answer stops where the available instruments stop discriminating, and the lineage is recorded so the stop is checkable. *"Indiscernibility is not identity"* (essay, §1). A later ground reading can move a slot from `gap` to `grounded` without touching the shape.

## 6. One-paragraph version for outreach

> The Tether does not predict how much rain will fall in a named place, or by how much a region will warm. It says that a far-field ocean effect must be answered per region and per medium, because a global average throws away the signal; that rainfall, temperature and ecosystems are three overlapping conditions rather than one question; that the effect travels through a network from a basin-scale flow to the atmosphere and then to land, so a local-only frame cannot derive it and the pathway must be imported from a frame that already holds it; that the pathway is an ordered cascade, so the same circulation strength under a different arrangement is a different outcome, with the ecosystem effect three steps out; and that observations, not model agreement, settle each slot. The regional sign, size and timing is declared as a gap, with the instruments that would close it named. What is offered is the shape any answer must have, five falsifiers written in advance, and an honest stop.
