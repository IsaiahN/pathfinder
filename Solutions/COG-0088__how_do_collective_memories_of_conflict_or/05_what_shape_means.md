## 1. A shape is a constraint, not a filler

The figures README states the limit: the framework returns "what a missing thing would have to look like (**a shape, not a name**)." Fig 9 :32: "you get a kind of thing, never the answer itself". Fig 13 :134: "Unreachable is a verdict here, not a silence". The Library of Babel essay gives the operational definition: "you can cut your search short if the set of books returned is small and descriptive enough that it provides a general shape of the answer that satisfies your question."

Applied here, a shape is the narrowed form of an explanation you can state before you can name a mechanism. For this question the shape is:

> any answer must say whether the inherited memory occupies the prior slot or the ground slot; whether the people holding it are independent frames or one evidence pool; whether what crosses generations is a method or a recording; and whether a trust judgment can reach an anchor at all.

Those four constraints do not tell us which neural circuit, which narrative, or which policy works. They tell us what a candidate explanation must be made of. `SPACE(???)TIME` in the exemplar has its analogue here: `INHERITED(???)PRESENT`. The `???` is the carrier and the mechanism, and the figures explicitly do not fill it.

## 2. How the shape was obtained

- *Library of Babel*: use the vocabulary closest to the thing, look for recurring new terms that overlap or correlate with it, recurse. "With each new term gained, you are able to narrow the space by definition and deepen your vocabulary."
- The same essay names the union split directly, which is why §1 of `01_figure_grounded_audit.md` splits before searching: "Perhaps your problem was a bundle or conflation of several problems masquerading as one? You will need to search all of those problems individually to find their answers and then combine them in a satisfactory way."
- Fig 6 :30: "The description travels. Fails by being sharpened into terms only we can read. Cast it in effect terms." Fig 9 :30-31: "your words are borrowed too, so describe the effect rather than the cause".
- *Decoding Tacit Knowledge*: "When contact isn't possible, the best you can send is a plain description of what you'd expect to see, because anyone can check that against the world." This is exactly why the outreach artifact leads with testable expectations (a memory that moves with context, agreement that tracks a shared pool, trust that moves after an out-group signal).
- *Library of Babel* on stopping: "you will know that you are done because the finish line will also be the starting place". Here the stop is at the four constraints, not at a named cause.
- *How a Society of Agents Would Work* supplies the population reading: a society can hold what an individual cannot, but the agents remain separate frames. That is the structure of a "collective memory" under the figures: distributed, not a group mind (confirmed by Orianne and Eustache, 2023, and Figueiredo et al., 2017/2018).

## 3. What licenses a name

Only two things, both outside the shape itself:

1. **The ground settles a term.** Formula step 5: "the ground settles it. Verified only by what pays." An experiment that manipulates frame independence rather than memory content could name the effective carrier.
2. **A provenanced import from a frame whose closure differs** (Fig 6 :23, Fig 8 :51-52), with a source gate and a shadow test. The world sources in `09_watson_answer.md` are imports of this kind. Their agreement is reported with field, author, venue, year, and overlap so the reader can see which pool it comes from.

*They're Scanning Books When They Should* sharpens this: "The real import is not a new term in the existing space. It is a new dimension of the space." And: "Declaring the primitives does not make them neutral. It makes the choice visible." The six-term substrate and the frame vocabulary are a visible guess, not nature's own list; a hidden guess is what the provenance rule prevents.

## 4. The source problem is the live error here

*The Hard Problem is About Source* gives the mechanism. A spectrometer reads green, "the instrument is speaking only about what it measured, it cannot comment on the source": green light, or yellow plus blue, or RGB, or CMYK, or a scheme not yet discovered. "If several paths create the same behavior, then behavior is a projection with lossy information." And "An instrument that can't distinguish these cases demonstrates its own inadequacy." The resolution was not an argument: "It was proven wrong not by argument, but by the arrival of a better instrument."

Applied to this question: a population's uniform mistrust is a *reading*. It does not comment on its source. It could be a correct response to a genuinely untrustworthy present environment, an inherited prior, a shared pool correlating the frames, or a habitat substitution that changed what was reproduced. Those sources project to the same behavior. Naming "trauma" or "collective memory" as the source from the mistrust reading alone is the metamerism error. What is owed is a discriminator: an independence manipulation, an anchor test, or a cross-frame signal. Fig 13 :134 again: "Unreachable is a verdict here, not a silence".

The trap essay adds the self-check: "The catch is that the output is only as good as the validator. The agent, or the tool, or the frame, or the theory, cannot validate itself." This is why the second seat in `11_audit.md` declares that it shares the same evidence pool and therefore corroborates nothing.

## 5. Q: Does "shape, not a name" forbid naming the carrier? ANSWERED, with a correction to my own §1

**First reading (overstated):** the framework returns a shape, so I must not say whether family narrative, schooling, commemoration, or media is the operative carrier.

**Correction.** The README's phrase is "what a **missing** thing would have to look like (a shape, not a name)". The subject is a primitive not held in Γ. Family narrative, schooling, and commemoration are not missing primitives; they are bindings from the world frame onto held atoms (prior, recording, import, habitat). Naming one as a *candidate carrier* is allowed, provided it is provenanced as a binding and carries its own residual. What remains forbidden is inventing a seventh primitive, or claiming that the figure framework derives which carrier operates. This mirrors the exemplar's own correction on naming Gradient.

**Current position:** the shape stays `INHERITED(???)PRESENT`. Candidate carriers are admissible as bindings, never as figure-derived names. Both the first reading and the correction are recorded so neither is quietly lost. Triangulated (README + Fig 9 :32 + Fig 13 :134).

## 6. Two consequences for outreach and for method

**Prestige is not action.** From the trap essay, opened this run: "Prestige must never be allowed to validate an action directly." And: "Prestige can fund an action. It can give an action a budget to be tried. But it cannot settle an action. Only the ground can settle it. The Ground Maintainer must be blind to prestige." A campaign that gathers famous names to agree that collective memory distorts trust is the named collapse, not corroboration. The legitimate ask is the opposite: give the shape claim a budget to be tried, let an experiment or a checkable record settle it. "You may all agree and be wrong."

**My own audit is a simulation, not a ground reading.** Same essay, opened this run: "LLMs can quickly simulate an approximate answer/output with the formula found in the architecture post." And: "the truly testable output would need to be derived from the real architecture or working the formula yourself." So `01_figure_grounded_audit.md` and `09_watson_answer.md` are simulated derivations checked for internal consistency against the figures. They are not tests of the claim, and they cannot settle anything they report on. The essay also warns that outputs could be contrived to fit the formula; that is why the refuter is written before the run and sent before the theory.
