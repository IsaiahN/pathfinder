## The question restated as an effect

An inherited representation of a past conflict or displacement is carried across people and generations. The decision is between two readings. Option A: the memory is information about the past that simply conditions present judgment, so the effect should be a content effect, trackable by which events are recalled. Option B: the memory operates as a frame-internal inherited prior and as a shared evidence pool, so the effect is structural: it changes what counts as evidence, removes the anchor from the reasoning, and makes agreement between similarly socialised people worthless as a verdict. Option B predicts that trust is restored by contact with a frame whose closure differs, not by adding more of the same memory. What an answer would look like is bound in `01_figure_grounded_audit.md` §0 before the figures are used, per the "Direction" rule in `THE_FORMULA.md`.

## What is deliberately not here

- No personal emails of named individuals.
- No ranking of people by how likely they are to agree with the shape claim.
- No bulk-send plan.

The reasons are figure-grounded, not merely legal. Fig 2 :40 says "agreement about the world is worthless when the agreeing frames share an evidence pool"; selecting recipients for receptiveness constructs exactly such a pool (also Fig 2 :35, "Alignment is triangulation, not negotiation. A mirror cannot correct you."). Fig 10 :42 says "A verifier that can reconstruct the claim cannot verify it." A first contact that carries the whole framework before asking for a verdict spends the only independence the outside frame contributes. Public, opt-in channels are also the practical answer to GDPR and CAN-SPAM style constraints on unsolicited outreach.
