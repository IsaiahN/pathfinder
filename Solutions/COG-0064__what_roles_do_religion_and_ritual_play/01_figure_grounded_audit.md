## 0. The claim under audit

> Religion and ritual create shared beliefs, emotional experiences, and social obligations. The three are produced by one thing, religion-and-ritual, and they are beneficial to the group.

The audit asks what survives when that sentence is parsed into effect terms and measured against the figures. The sentence as written fails three tests at once: it averages across slots, it assumes one lever for three conditions, and it states "beneficial" as a verdict without naming an anchor. What survives is a union shape, stated in §5.

---

## 1. The residual in effect terms

Let each community member be a slot s with a library Γ, a belief b (weighted between private history and inherited prior by w, Symbols table :23), an action a, and an observation o′. The residual is R = |Γ(b,a) - o′|, indexed per slot.

> **Fig 1 :19** "The residual is indexed by object slot, not aggregated."
> **Fig 1 :22** "Aggregating across slots is how a live signal disappears into an average."

The question names three effects. Stated as residuals:

| effect | what is being reduced | what would have to be named |
|---|---|---|
| shared beliefs | disagreement between members' Γ and b | an anchor that does not move when frames move |
| emotional experiences | the gap between a felt state and the prediction of it | the felt state as ground, per slot |
| social obligations | the cost of coordinating action without a shared rule | observed behaviour and its consequences |

In effect terms the question is: do religion and ritual (i) supply an anchor, (ii) supply a structure that changes contact with a per-slot ground, (iii) install a convention that can be violated, or (iv) add an atom that moves the wall? The four options are not mutually exclusive, which is the first finding.

## 2. Pre-registered refuter (written before the derivation)

Required before any outreach, per Seat Map :287: a falsifier written before the run is a design instrument, not only an honesty device.

The shape claim recorded in §5 is refuted if any of the following holds:

1. **Union fails.** One intervention clears all three effects and leaves no categorically different residue (breaks Fig 9 :55-57).
2. **Convention reading fails.** A doctrine is found that governs belief and whose content does not change when the community changes its mind, that is, a genuine non-updating anchor (breaks Fig 2 :33).
3. **Per-slot reading fails.** Emotional alignment is shown to be purely the average of one shared evidence pool with no slot carrying positive live mass (breaks Fig 1 :19 and Fig 2 :40).
4. **Convention install fails.** An obligation is purely declared meaning with no possible violation, or it decides an outcome while still functioning as a mere convention (breaks Fig 10 :18 and Fig 10 :47).
5. **Valence relativity fails.** The same rite has invariant valence across a documented change of residual (breaks Fig 11 :49-51).
6. **Gap closes.** A figure re-census derives which specific structure fills a slot, or a source-discriminating instrument names it (closes F7).

If any fires, the shape is withdrawn or narrowed. Until one is run, no part of this may be labelled `grounded`.

## 3. Domain census

R described by arity, symmetry and scale, then one line per domain with actors / currency / anchor. No nameable anchor means it is not a domain yet.

- **R (arity / symmetry / scale).** Arity: three conditions, so a **union compound**, not one hard question (Fig 9 :55, Fig 9 :57). Symmetry: belief alignment is symmetric across frames, but the anchor relation is asymmetric (Fig 2 :33); affect is private per slot; obligation is ordered, a sequence and a threshold. Scale: individual loop, gathered assembly, and cross-generational transmission across a lossy membrane (Fig 4 :30).
- **D1 Belief and meaning.** Actors: believers, their Γ, their b and w. Currency: prediction residual, inherited prior. Anchor: the world, what actually happens, named as an anchor in Fig 2 :29. Admitted.
- **D2 Emotional experience.** Actors: bodies, nervous systems, the assembled crowd. Currency: a spendable directed difference (Fig 13 :22), self-transcendent affect. Anchor: the participant's own felt state. Fig 2 :36-37 makes human judgment the legitimate anchor when the question is constitutively about people. Admitted.
- **D3 Social obligation.** Actors: members, roles, institutions, norms. Currency: commitment, costliness, trust, sanction. Anchor: observed cooperation and defection, a counter or a market in the sense of Fig 2 :29. Admitted.
- **X1 Religion as a free-floating essence.** Actors: none nameable. Currency: none. Anchor: none nameable. **Not a domain yet.** Refused.
- **X2 Ritual is universally functional.** Absorbed into D1 to D3. Valence is relative to the residual, so "functional" is not a property carried by the rite (Fig 11 :49-51).

**Union, not partition.** The three effects overlap: a single rite synchronizes emotion, aligns belief, and binds obligation at once. Fig 9 :55 and Fig 9 :57 govern: a case in two conditions is not a harder case of either. The compound needs its own loop.

**Dependency order (Fig 3).** Perception of the ritual cues → vocabulary for what the rite affords → objective (what counts as a shared belief or a fulfilled obligation) → planning the coordinated performance → learn and carry across generations. The middle link is the one that usually lacks an instrument:

> **Fig 3 :27** "A link with no instrument is not a link that works. It is a link nobody has looked at."

**Strain test.** "Religion is an anchor" strains against Fig 2 :33 because doctrine changes with what communities think. A line that only fits with a strain governs another quantity, so re-census: religion-as-meaning is a **convention**, not a ground (see F2). Strict production of emotion (⇒) strains because baseline affect exists before the rite, so re-census to the gradient/structure reading (see F3).

## 5. Findings

### F1 - Three effects, one union compound (decisive)

> **Fig 9 :55** "A bundled problem is usually a union of overlapping conditions, not a list of separate ones."
> **Fig 9 :57** "What remains is smaller and it is also categorically different: a case in two conditions is not a harder case of either."

A single lever, "religion promotes cohesion," clears the region where only one condition holds and leaves the intersections standing. The three effects must be derived separately and then recombined as a compound with its own loop.

- **Mode:** `general` for the union rule, `specified` for this question.
- **Status:** triangulated (Fig 9 union split and Fig 11 valence relativity, low overlap).
- **Refuter:** finding 1 in §2.

### F2 - Shared belief is a convention, not an anchor

> **Fig 2 :40** "disqualifying for a verdict: agreement about the world is worthless when the agreeing frames share an evidence pool"
> **Fig 2 :41** "efficient for a convention: agreement about a shared meaning is the product, not a corruption of it"
> **Fig 2 :33** "The anchor must not update."

Shared doctrine and synchronized performance give members a shared library Γ and a shared weighting w. They therefore converge. But the convergence is exactly Fig 2's panel 1 or panel 2: mutual update, or one evidence pool. It is *efficient for a convention*, because a shared meaning is the product of a shared practice, and it is *disqualifying for a verdict* about the world, because the agreeing frames draw on the same pool.

The strain test is informative. Calling doctrine an anchor requires that the anchor not update on the frames, but doctrine reforms, splits, and is reinterpreted when communities change their minds. The line therefore does not govern religion-as-belief-content; it governs the ground that a belief is about. Re-census sends religion-as-meaning to the convention domain, where agreement is legitimate without being evidence.

- **Mode:** `specified`.
- **Status:** triangulated (Fig 2 :40-41 valence rule and Fig 10 :18 declared meaning, distinct figures).
- **Refuter:** finding 2 in §2.
- **World check:** Boyer and Liénard 2020 report that a prescribed performance "contributes to common knowledge by creating an accessible reference point that results in similar representations in participants." Legare and Nielsen 2020 define rituals as "socially stipulated, conventional behaviours." Both corroborate the convention reading. They share the same Royal Society theme issue, so they are one witness, not two.

### F3 - Emotional experience changes contact with a per-slot ground

> **Fig 13 :22** "a difference that can be spent"
> **Fig 1 :19** "The residual is indexed by object slot, not aggregated."
> **Fig 2 :36** "An anchor is legitimate when the question is constitutively about it."

For "is this experience meaningful for a person," the question is constitutively about people, so the participant's own felt state is the legitimate anchor. The rite supplies a structure over bodily states. Fig 13 :74 requires composition across the two kinds, never within one: a state (the assembled bodies) must be joined through a structure (a spectrum ordering the actions, a gradient directing arousal, a network wiring the participants).

> **Fig 5 :46** "When nothing is scoring, the drive manufactures a gradient."

Strict production (⇒, "A produces B; B did not exist before") strains here: baseline affect exists before the rite. The re-census replaces "the rite produces emotion" with "the rite is the structure that turns a distribution into a spendable directed difference." The felt state stays per slot and is never averaged into a group mood that would be mistaken for the ground.

- **Mode:** `specified`.
- **Status:** triangulated (Fig 13 :22 gradient and Fig 5 :46 manufactured gradient, low overlap between substrate and loop).
- **Refuter:** finding 3 in §2.
- **World check:** Pizarro et al. 2022 define collective effervescence as a "state of intense shared emotional activation and sense of unison" and report a metaanalysis of k = 50 studies, N = 182,738. This corroborates the per-slot intensity structure, but an aggregate correlation is a filter for budget, not a witness for the per-slot verdict (Fig 9 :60). The meta-analysis is silent on whether the live residual sits in a slot or only in the pool.

### F4 - Obligation is a convention installed so it can be violated

> **Fig 10 :18** "a declared meaning is not a grounded one"
> **Fig 10 :47** "a convention that decides an outcome has stopped being one"
> **Fig 12 :112** "A plan is a sequence. A goal is a comparison. Progress is a subtraction."

Obligations are conventions. They become checkable only when something can violate them: a taboo can be broken, a fast can be failed, an oath can be sworn falsely. Fig 10 :18 forces "install what can be violated" rather than "declare a meaning." When a convention decides an outcome, for example when a sanction determines who eats or who is expelled, it has stopped being a mere convention and is exercising an anchor-like force. That force must be declared as a stake (Fig 10 :59), not hidden inside the word "tradition."

The bonds are legible. The rite is a sequence:

> **Fig 12 :74** "A then B; B needs A to have happened"

The sacred/profane and in-group/out-group boundary is a comparison:

> **Fig 12 :87** "a threshold relation between two quantities"

And the goal is a comparison by the Operators table: "a goal is a comparison, which is why a vocabulary without it cannot state one."

- **Mode:** `specified`.
- **Status:** triangulated (Fig 10 convention governance and Fig 12 bond types, distinct objects).
- **Refuter:** finding 4 in §2.
- **World check:** Boyer and Liénard 2020 describe "normative scripting of actions," and Legare and Nielsen 2020 list "ensuring commitment to the group norm(s)" among the adaptive functions. Gelfand et al. 2020 supply the conflict case below (F5).

### F5 - Valence is relative to the residual (a real world conflict, handled)

> **Fig 11 :49** "An actor’s valence is relative to the residual, not a property it carries."
> **Fig 11 :50** "The same actor is a benefactor to one residual and a malfactor to another, so mutualism and parasitism are relations rather than kinds."

The claim "ritual is functional for the group" conflicts with Gelfand et al. 2020, who find that ritualistic synchrony also increases conformity, destructive obedience, and reduces creativity and healthy dissent. By the conflict rule, a conflict sends us back to the census; it never flips a figure verdict.

Re-census result: "functional" is not a property of the rite. It is a relation between the rite and a named residual. For a residual defined as "coordination under threat," synchrony is a benefactor; for a residual defined as "detecting a minority view that contradicts the majority," the same synchrony is a malfactor. The world conflict therefore corroborates Fig 11 :49-51 rather than overthrowing it, and it refutes the naive Option A verdict in §0.

- **Mode:** `specified`.
- **Status:** triangulated (Fig 11 valence rule and Fig 2 :39 correlation valence, distinct figures).
- **Refuter:** finding 5 in §2.
- **Overlap note:** Gelfand et al. is a social-psychology frame with its own experiments, the lowest-overlap witness in this folder.

### F6 - Transmission crosses the membrane as a method, not a recording

> **Fig 4 :22** "Only methods cross up. Only head starts come down."
> **Fig 11 :61** "The seat’s power is asymmetric. Introduction is available; exclusion is not."

What survives across generations is how to do the rite and how to coordinate, a method that can be reapplied. The recording of one successful performance is not what crosses; carried upward it looks like knowledge and is a description of one occasion. Introduction is available, exclusion is not: a community can add a rite and cannot subtract the habitat it lands in.

- **Mode:** `general` for the membrane rule, `specified` for ritual transmission.
- **Status:** single-source for the membrane rule (Fig 4), corroborated at the world level by Legare and Nielsen 2020, who note that rituals are transmitted "within communities (horizontal transmission) and across generations (vertical transmission)" and are cumulative. White et al. 2025 corroborate with the doctrinal versus imagistic distinction: frequently enacted rituals in diffuse communities versus rare, intense rites in small ones. Both world sources are corroboration only.
- **Refuter:** a tradition transmitted as a verbatim recording that still produces novel coordination in a changed habitat would break the membrane reading.

### F7 - Which specific structure fills each slot is a `gap`

> **Fig 9 :32** "you get a kind of thing, never the answer itself"
> **Fig 8 :39** "So a search that finds nothing is never a proof of absence."
> **Fig 13 :134** "Unreachable is a verdict here, not a silence,"

No figure line selects *which* of the three structures (spectrum, gradient, network) occupies the affect slot, or *which* doctrine is the true one. The canonical rule is a shape, not a name. The structures are already atoms in the six-term set, so naming one for a slot is a binding from the anthropology frame onto a held atom, provenanced as such. It is not the invention of a seventh term and not an import that moves the wall:

> **Fig 6 :23** "No frame escapes its own closure. The bound moves only on contact with a frame whose closure differs."

- **Mode:** `gap`, stated plainly.
- **Status:** gap after genuine re-census.
- **What is owed:** a source-discriminating instrument, characterized in effect terms but not held. For belief: an instrument that separates a shared-pool reading from a triangulated anchor reading. For affect: one that discriminates a per-slot residual from a pooled average. For obligation: one that shows whether the convention can be violated in fact.

### F8 - The outside frame must receive the residual, not the theory

> **Fig 10 :52** "Bring a characterised residual, never the source."
> **Fig 10 :42** "A verifier that can reconstruct the claim cannot verify it."
> **Fig 2 :35** "Alignment is triangulation, not negotiation. A mirror cannot correct you."

This governs `02`, `04` and `08`. The first contact carries the shape claim and the six falsifiers of §2, in effect terms. The framework is offered only after a verdict on stage 1 is recorded, and later agreement is never pooled as corroboration (Fig 2 :40).

- **Mode:** `general` for the seat rule, `specified` for this outreach.
- **Status:** triangulated (Fig 10 verifier non-access and Fig 2 mirror rule).

## 8. Updated verdict

The shape survives: religion and ritual are a union of three roles, installing a convention where agreement is the product, supplying a structure that changes contact with a per-slot felt ground, and binding obligations with ordered and threshold bonds that must be installable so they can be violated. None of the three is a world-anchor, "functional" is a relation to a named residual rather than a property of the rite, and which specific structure fills each slot is a declared `gap`. Mode `specified`, ungrounded, candidate. Nothing here is settled by experiment.
