## The question restated as an effect

What is being decided is not whether religion and ritual are "good" or "adaptive" in the abstract. It is what each does to a residual that is indexed per person, not averaged across a crowd. A community member's library predicts a belief or an outcome, Γ(b,a); the world or the felt state returns o′; R = |Γ(b,a) - o′| is measured per slot (Fig 1 :19). Three effects are claimed: shared beliefs, emotional experiences, social obligations. The question asks which loop moves each one.

- Option A, "religion and ritual are adaptive mechanisms that produce group benefit": leads to a `general`-mode verdict that averages slots, assumes a single lever, and treats "benefit" as settled without naming an anchor. The figures refuse this (Fig 1 :22, Fig 9 :55, Fig 11 :49).
- Option B, "religion and ritual are a union of three domain-specific roles in the loop, none of which is a world-anchor, and which specific forms fill the slots is a gap": leads to a checkable shape, per-domain verdicts kept separate, falsifiers written in advance, and a recorded stop. This is the verdict recorded in `09_watson_answer.md`.

What an answer would look like, written down and bound as an ordinary term before the run (Formula, "Direction"): a *shape* naming (i) which quantity each effect changes, (ii) which anchor can settle it, and (iii) an instrument that would discriminate a convention from a ground and a per-slot change from a pooled average. Until that shape is bound it predicts nothing and no check in the loop can see it.

## What is deliberately not here

- No personal emails of named individuals.
- No ranking of people by how likely they are to agree.
- No bulk-send plan.
- No claim that this derivation is `grounded`, no claim that it settles the question, and no claim that "religion is adaptive" or "ritual is functional" has been established.

Three figure-grounded reasons, plus one practical one:

1. A room is not an anchor. *"A room’s agreement is variation, not a verdict. Selection between rooms is where the ground votes."* (Fig 7 :35) and *"Alignment is triangulation, not negotiation. A mirror cannot correct you."* (Fig 2 :35). Gathering agreeable readers manufactures the correlation that disqualifies a verdict.
2. Correlation has valence. *"disqualifying for a verdict: agreement about the world is worthless when the agreeing frames share an evidence pool"* (Fig 2 :40). A list selected for receptiveness is one witness wearing many names.
3. A verifier must be outside the interior. *"A verifier that can reconstruct the claim cannot verify it."* (Fig 10 :42). The first contact therefore carries the residual and its refuter, not the framework.
4. Legally, unsolicited bulk email to scraped addresses breaches GDPR and CAN-SPAM style rules in many jurisdictions and gets the sending domain blacklisted. Public opt-in routes avoid that failure as well as the structural one.
