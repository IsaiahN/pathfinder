# WATSON answer: roles of religion and ritual (COG-0064)

**Mode: `specified`, not `grounded`.** Figures are primary and decide. World sources are secondary, corroborate but never overrule, and a conflict returns the derivation to the domain census. This is a simulated derivation checked for internal consistency against the figures, not a ground reading.

## QUESTION

What roles do religion and ritual play in creating shared beliefs, emotional experiences, and social obligations? Restated as an effect: how does a per-slot residual R = |Γ(b,a) - o′| get reduced in three different domains, and which anchor settles each? Option A: religion and ritual are one adaptive mechanism that produces group benefit. Option B: the question is a union of three differently anchored roles, none of which is a world-anchor, and which specific form fills each slot is a gap. Option A leads to a `general`-mode verdict that averages slots, assumes one lever and declares "benefit" without an anchor. Option B leads to a checkable shape, separate per-domain verdicts, refuters written in advance and a recorded stop.

## DOMAIN CENSUS

R by arity, symmetry and scale, then one line per domain with actors / currency / anchor, partition or union, order by dependency.

- **R (arity / symmetry / scale).** Arity: three conditions, so union compound (Fig 9 :55). Symmetry: belief alignment is symmetric across frames, the anchor relation is asymmetric; affect is private per slot; obligation is ordered, sequence and threshold. Scale: individual loop, gathered assembly, cross-generational membrane (Fig 4 :30).
- **Domain 1, belief and meaning.** Actors: believers, Γ, b, w. Currency: residual and inherited prior. Anchor: the world, what actually happens (Fig 2 :29). Status: admitted. Verdict: shared belief is a convention, not an anchor.
- **Domain 2, emotional experience.** Actors: bodies, nervous systems, the assembled crowd. Currency: a spendable directed difference (Fig 13 :22). Anchor: the participant's own felt state, legitimate because the question is constitutively about people (Fig 2 :36). Status: admitted. Verdict: structure over states, per-slot ground.
- **Domain 3, social obligation.** Actors: members, roles, institutions, norms. Currency: commitment, costliness, sanction. Anchor: observed cooperation and defection (Fig 2 :29). Status: admitted. Verdict: installable convention with declared stake.
- **X1, religion as a free-floating essence.** No nameable actors, currency or anchor. Not a domain yet. Refused.
- **X2, ritual is universally functional.** Absorbed into D1 to D3; valence is relative to the residual (Fig 11 :49).
- **Union, not partition** (Fig 9 :55, :57). A case in two conditions is not a harder case of either. The compound has its own loop and ground.
- **Order by dependency:** perception of cues, vocabulary for what the rite affords, objective, planning, learn and carry (Fig 3). The vocabulary link is the one usually without an instrument (Fig 3 :27).

Strain test: "religion is an anchor" strains against the non-updating rule, so re-census moves religion-as-meaning to the convention domain. "Rite produces emotion" strains because baseline affect pre-exists, so re-census moves it to a gradient over states. Different objects, no unresolved strain after re-census.

## WORLD (secondary sources read)

World sources corroborate only. Overlap is declared, not hidden. The four 2020 Royal Society papers share a theme issue and count as one venue-level pool despite different methods and taxa.

- **Cognitive anthropology of ritual.** Pascal Boyer and Pierre Liénard, "Ingredients of 'rituals' and their cognitive underpinnings," Philosophical Transactions of the Royal Society B 375(1805):20190439, 2020. Opened 2026-11-22. Quote: "contributes to common knowledge by creating an accessible reference point that results in similar representations in participants." Prediction: a rite creates a shared representation by making a reference point accessible, not by providing an anchor. **Corroborates** D1 and F1. Overlap: high with the other 2020 theme-issue papers.
- **Psychology and cognitive science of ritual.** Cristine H. Legare and Mark Nielsen, "Ritual explained: interdisciplinary answers to Tinbergen's four questions," Philosophical Transactions of the Royal Society B 375(1805):20190419, 2020. Opened 2026-11-22. Quote: rituals are "socially stipulated, conventional behaviours" that are "embedded in systems of meaning and symbolism." Prediction: ritual is conventional and transmitted within and across generations. **Corroborates** D1, D3 and F6. Overlap: high with the theme issue.
- **Social psychology of synchrony.** Michele J. Gelfand, Nava Caluori, Joshua Conrad Jackson and Morgan K. Taylor, "The cultural evolutionary trade-off of ritualistic synchrony," Philosophical Transactions of the Royal Society B 375(1805):20190432, 2020. Opened 2026-11-22. Quote: synchrony's "dark side" includes conformity, destructive obedience and lower creativity, with experiments on 149 and 278 participants. Prediction: the same synchronous rite helps one residual and harms another. **Conflicts** with naive functionalism in the question's Option A; returned to census and resolved by Fig 11 :49, so it corroborates F5 without flipping the figure verdict. Lowest-overlap witness for the mechanism shape.
- **Animal behaviour and primatology.** Federica Dal Pesco and Julia Fischer, "On the evolution of baboon greeting rituals," Philosophical Transactions of the Royal Society B 375(1805):20190420, 2020. Opened 2026-11-22. Quote: greetings are "crucial to establishing ruled–governed communicative exchanges" and vary with male tolerance and cooperation. Prediction: ritualized form tracks social relations rather than a fixed function. **Corroborates** the relational-valence claim at a non-human stratum. Overlap: same theme issue, different taxon and method, so moderate.
- **Social psychology of collective emotion.** José J Pizarro et al., "Emotional processes, collective behavior, and social movements: A meta-analytic review of collective effervescence outcomes," Frontiers in Psychology 13:974683, 2022. Opened 2026-11-22. Quote: collective effervescence is a "state of intense shared emotional activation and sense of unison"; meta-analysis of k = 50, N = 182,738. Prediction: intense shared affect is measurable. **Corroborates** D2's intensity shape; silent on whether the live residual is per slot or pooled, so it cannot settle C2. Overlap: separate venue, overlapping literature.
- **Cognitive science of religion, canonical review.** Claire White, Justin Barrett, Pascal Boyer, E. Thomas Lawson, Robert McCauley and Harvey Whitehouse, "The cognitive science of religion: past, present, and possible futures," Religion, Brain & Behavior, 2025, DOI 10.1080/2153599X.2025.2474404. Opened 2026-11-22. Quote: doctrinal systems have "frequently enacted rituals and more diffuse cohesion" while imagistic systems have "emotionally intense but rarely enacted rituals." Prediction: ritual format changes with the scale of the community. **Corroborates** F6. Overlap: separate venue, overlapping authorship.
- **Framework essays, canonical reading only, not world witnesses.** Isaiah N, "The Hard Problem is About Source," 2026-08-28, quote: "The answer is unreached until a better instrument exists"; and "The Recursive Self-Improvement Trap," 2026-11-19, quote: "the output is only as good as the validator." Both opened 2026-11-22. These ground the instrument requirement and the simulated-status disclaimer. Overlap with the figures is maximal, so they carry no independent corroboration weight.

No world source overturns a figure verdict. The one conflict, Gelfand et al. against naive functionalism, was returned to the census and resolved by re-census rather than by choosing a side.

## VERDICT (from the figures)

The figure-derived verdict is Option B. The question is a union of three roles with different anchors. Shared belief is a convention whose agreement is the product of a shared meaning and is disqualifying as a world verdict. Emotional experience is a structure over bodily states, a spendable directed difference with the felt state as a per-slot ground. Social obligation is a convention installed so it can be violated, bound by sequence and threshold, with any outcome-deciding enforcement declared as a stake. Valence is relative to a named residual, so there is no unqualified functional verdict. Transmission carries methods across a lossy membrane. Which specific doctrine, structure or rite fills a slot is a `gap`, recorded rather than filled. Mode `specified`, ungrounded, candidate. The world evidence corroborates the mechanism shape but cannot discriminate a per-slot residual from a pooled average, and that is precisely the instrument still owed.

- **Cost if wrong:** bounded and locatable. The shape is mode-labelled, states six refuters before the conclusion and records the lineage, so an error is findable rather than invisible. The real cost is spending the independence of outside readers by sending a recording instead of a generator, which Fig 2 :42 prices: "Buy independence in proportion to the cost of being wrong." That cost is avoidable by leading with the shape claim and its refuter. A second cost is presenting a convention as an anchor, which would turn a shared meaning into a false world claim for the people who rely on it.
- **Undoable:** yes. No term is deleted if the claim fails; a refutation is added and the reach falls because something was added rather than deleted (Fig 6 :42-43). The shape and its six falsifiers are recorded with provenance so a later reader can locate the error (Fig 10 :62-64). Slot bindings, being gaps rather than assertions, carry nothing to retract.
- **Reversed by:** a ground reading, not agreement. A source-discriminating instrument that separates a triangulated anchor from a shared pool would revise D1; a per-person measurement showing only a pooled average would reverse D2; evidence that an obligation is purely declared with no possible violation, or that enforcement decides outcomes while remaining only a convention, would reverse D3; a rite with invariant valence across a change of residual would reverse F5; and a figure re-census deriving a specific slot occupant would close the gap. Until one of these fires, the verdict stands as `candidate`.
