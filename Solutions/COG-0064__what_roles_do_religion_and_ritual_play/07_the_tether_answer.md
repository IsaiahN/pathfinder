# The Tether's answer: what roles do religion and ritual play?

**Mode: `specified` on the three roles, `general` on the structural constraints, `gap` on which specific form fills a slot. Not `grounded`.** This is a simulated derivation checked against the figures (`05_what_shape_means.md` §6), not a ground reading.

## 1. The residual it answers

The question bundles three effects into one phrase. In effect terms, each is a different way of reducing a per-person residual R = |Γ(b,a) - o′|. "Shared beliefs" reduces disagreement between members' libraries; "emotional experiences" reduces the gap between a felt state and its prediction; "social obligations" reduces the cost of coordinating action. They are a union compound, not one hard problem:

> **Fig 9 :55** "A bundled problem is usually a union of overlapping conditions, not a list of separate ones."

A single lever clears where only one condition holds and leaves the intersections standing. The verdicts are therefore kept separate and never averaged.

## 2. The shape

1. **Shared belief is a convention, not a world-anchor.** Shared doctrine and synchronized performance give members a shared library and a shared weighting, so they converge. The framework prices that agreement:

   > **Fig 2 :41** "efficient for a convention: agreement about a shared meaning is the product, not a corruption of it"

   > **Fig 2 :40** "disqualifying for a verdict: agreement about the world is worthless when the agreeing frames share an evidence pool"

   Doctrine changes when communities change their minds, so it is not a non-updating anchor (Fig 2 :33). Religion's role here is to manufacture a shared meaning, not to certify a world-claim. The slot for "which doctrine is true" stays open.

2. **Emotional experience is a structure over bodily states, and the felt state is a per-slot ground.**

   > **Fig 13 :22** "a difference that can be spent"

   The rite orders actions, directs arousal, and wires participants: a spectrum, a gradient and a network composed across bodily states, never within one kind (Fig 13 :74). For the question whether an experience is meaningful for a person, the question is constitutively about people, so the participant's own felt state is the legitimate anchor (Fig 2 :36-37). That state is never averaged:

   > **Fig 1 :22** "Aggregating across slots is how a live signal disappears into an average."

   The rite does not create affect ex nihilo; it turns a distribution into a directed, spendable difference (Fig 5 :46).

3. **Social obligation is a convention installed so it can be violated.**

   > **Fig 10 :18** "a declared meaning is not a grounded one"

   > **Fig 10 :47** "a convention that decides an outcome has stopped being one"

   The rite binds the rule with a sequence (Fig 12 :74) and marks the in-group boundary with a threshold comparison (Fig 12 :87). A goal is a comparison, so a vocabulary without ⋛ could not state the obligation at all. When enforcement actually decides an outcome, the convention has become an anchor-like force and that stake must be declared (Fig 10 :59), not hidden in the word "tradition."

4. **"Functional" is a relation, not a property.**

   > **Fig 11 :49** "An actor’s valence is relative to the residual, not a property it carries."

   The same rite is a benefactor to a residual defined as coordination under threat, and a malfactor to a residual defined as detecting a minority view that contradicts the majority. The world literature on synchrony's dark side corroborates this; it does not overturn it. There is no unqualified "religion is adaptive" verdict.

5. **What crosses generations is a method, not a recording.**

   > **Fig 4 :22** "Only methods cross up. Only head starts come down."

   The performance is reapplicable; the record of one successful occasion is not. Introduction is available and exclusion is not (Fig 11 :61), so a community can add a rite and cannot subtract the habitat it lands in.

6. **Which specific form fills each slot is a declared gap.**

   > **Fig 9 :32** "you get a kind of thing, never the answer itself"

   The framework returns the three-role shape and refuses to name which doctrine, which structure or which rite occupies a slot. Spectrum, gradient and network are held atoms, so a binding onto one is permitted but must be provenanced; it is not a derivation and not an import that moves the wall (Fig 6 :23).

## 3. What it explicitly does not say

- Not that religion or ritual is an anchor for world beliefs. Doctrine updates on the frames; the ground does not (Fig 2 :33).
- Not that agreement among believers is evidence that the belief is true. It is the product of a convention (Fig 2 :41).
- Not that a rite produces an emotion that did not exist before. Baseline affect precedes it; the rite is the structure that directs it.
- Not that ritual is universally functional. Valence is relative to a named residual (Fig 11 :49-51).
- Not a single group-benefit verdict. The residual is indexed per person (Fig 1 :19).
- Not `grounded`. No measurement settles which binding occupies a slot.

## 4. What would settle it - the ground

A source-discriminating instrument, not a further argument. From *The Hard Problem is About Source*: an instrument that reads green *"cannot comment on the source."* Here the corresponding questions are:

- **Belief:** does an instrument distinguish a triangulated anchor from a shared evidence pool, so that agreement with a non-updating anchor predicts a belief better than agreement with a co-believer?
- **Affect:** does a per-person measurement show live residual on some slots after the rite, or only a pooled average? An aggregate correlation cannot answer this (Fig 1 :19, Fig 9 :60).
- **Obligation:** can the convention be violated in fact, and does enforcement actually decide an outcome? A rule no real occupant could break is a prohibition stated wrong (Fig 10 :53).
- **Standing refuters from `01` §2:** one lever clearing all three effects; a non-updating doctrine; a pure pooled average with no per-slot mass; a purely declared obligation; invariant rite valence across a change of residual; or a figure re-census deriving a specific slot occupant.

## 5. The honest stop

**Unreached, not unreachable.** The six-term set is a visible guess at compression axes, not nature's own list, and declaring it makes the choice visible rather than neutral. The answer stops at the limit of the available instruments and records the lineage so the stop is checkable:

> **Fig 8 :39** "So a search that finds nothing is never a proof of absence."

> **Fig 13 :134** "Unreachable is a verdict here, not a silence,"

Indiscernibility is not identity. A gap is a verdict, not a failure to try.

## 6. One-paragraph version for outreach

> The Tether does not say that religion is an anchor or that ritual is a single adaptation. It says the question is three conditions in a union, each with a different anchor. Shared belief is a convention: the agreement a rite produces is the product of a shared meaning, not evidence that the belief is about the world. Emotional experience is a structure over bodily states, a directed and spendable difference, and the person's own felt state is the ground for whether it is meaningful, measured per person and never averaged. Social obligation is a convention installed so it can be violated; when a rule actually decides an outcome it has stopped being a mere convention and that stake must be declared. The same rite is benefactor or malfactor depending on which residual is named, so "functional" is a relation, not a property. What crosses generations is the method, not the recording. Which specific doctrine, structure or rite fills a slot is left open: the framework returns a shape, not a name, and an instrument that discriminates the alternatives is what is owed. What it offers is that shape, six falsifiers written in advance, and a recorded stop.
