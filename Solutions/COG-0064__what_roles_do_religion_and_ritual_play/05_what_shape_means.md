## 1. A shape is a constraint, not a filler

The canonical README states the limit: the framework returns *"what a missing thing would have to look like (a shape, not a name)."*

> **Fig 9 :32** "you get a kind of thing, never the answer itself"

> **Fig 13 :134** "Unreachable is a verdict here, not a silence,"

A **shape** is the narrowed form of an answer you can state before you can name the occupant. It constrains; it does not fill. For this question the shape is:

- three roles, not one, because the question is a union compound (Fig 9 :55);
- each role has a different anchor, so the verdicts are kept separate;
- the quantity each role changes is named in effect terms, not in the vocabulary of folk psychology;
- the occupant of each slot is left open until a ground or a provenanced binding settles it.

"The rite produces group benefit" is not a shape. "A shared meaning is the product of a shared practice, and agreement about it is efficient for a convention but disqualifying for a verdict about the world" is a shape.

## 3. What licenses a name

Only two things, both outside the shape itself:

1. **The ground settles it.** `THE_FORMULA.md` step 5: *"the ground settles it. Verified only by what pays. A gate is not the ground."* For this question, a source-discriminating instrument would be needed to tell a per-slot residual from a pooled average, or to tell an anchor from a convention.
2. **A provenanced import from a frame whose closure differs**, with a source gate and a shadow test (Fig 6 :23, Fig 8 :50-52).

From *The Hard Problem is About Source*: an instrument that detects green *"cannot comment on the source."* Several sources can give the same reading, and the reading does not determine which. *"If several paths create the same behavior, then behavior is a projection with lossy information."* The resolution came from a spectrometer, not an argument: *"The answer is unreached until a better instrument exists."*

Applied here: the observation "members agree after the rite" is a reading that does not discriminate among a shared anchor, a shared pool, and a shared convention. Naming one of them from that reading alone is the metamerism error. What is owed is a discriminator, not a name. This is why F2 in `01_figure_grounded_audit.md` refuses "religion is an anchor" and why F7 marks the slot binding as a gap.

## 4. The narrow point about naming held atoms

The rule *"a shape, not a name"* governs a **missing** primitive, one not held in Γ. Spectrum, gradient and network are already among the six terms (Fig 13). So saying "the affect slot is a gradient" is not the invention of a seventh term. It is a **binding** from the anthropology frame onto an atom already held.

What remains forbidden is treating that binding as a derivation from the six alone, or as an import that moves the wall:

> **Fig 6 :23** "No frame escapes its own closure. The bound moves only on contact with a frame whose closure differs."

> **Fig 6 :25** "An import adds no atom to the world."

Both claims are recorded so neither is quietly lost: the binding is permitted but unproven, and the gap is about *which* binding, not about whether naming is allowed at all.

## 5. Q2 - what licenses a particular doctrine or rite in a slot?

Nothing in the framework. The framework returns a shape. A particular doctrine, structure or rite is licensed only by the ground settling a minted term, or by a provenanced import with a shadow test. Until one of those, the name is the searcher's own contribution and must be recorded as such.

The world literature makes the same point in its own vocabulary. Boyer and Liénard 2020 refuse a single theory of "ritual" and instead list four mechanisms in combination. Legare and Nielsen 2020 use a "fractionating strategy." A shape that names one mechanism as *the* role of ritual would be doing exactly what those sources warn against.

## 6. My own derivation is a simulation, not a ground reading

From *The Recursive Self-Improvement Trap*: *"the output is only as good as the validator. The agent, or the tool, or the frame, or the theory, cannot validate itself."* And: *"LLMs can quickly simulate an approximate answer/output... However, the truly testable output would need to be derived from the real architecture or working the formula yourself."* The essay calls such output *"simulacra."*

So `01_figure_grounded_audit.md`, `06_isomers_and_order.md`, `07_the_tether_answer.md` and `09_watson_answer.md` are simulated derivations, checked for internal consistency against the figures. They are **not** a test of the claim and must be labelled as such. They cannot settle anything they report on.

## 7. Prestige is not action

From the same essay: *"Prestige must never be allowed to validate an action directly... Someone let prestige stand in for proof... Prestige can fund an action. It can give an action a budget to be tried. But it cannot settle an action. Only the ground can settle it. The Ground Maintainer must be blind to prestige."*

For this question, a famous theorist of ritual (Durkheim, Geertz, Turner, Bell, Whitehouse) can fund the attempt by making it legible. That name cannot settle whether the shape is right. The first contact therefore asks for a refutation in effect terms, not for an endorsement by authority, and the public contact points in `03_public_contact_points.csv` are venues, not names.
