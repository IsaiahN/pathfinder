## What is deliberately not here

- No personal emails of named individuals.
- No ranking of people by how likely they are to agree with the answer.
- No bulk-send plan.

Three reasons, in the figures' own words:

1. **Agreement is not a verdict.** Seat Map :163: "High correlation is disqualifying for a verdict and efficient for a convention." Selecting reviewers for receptiveness manufactures the correlation that would make their agreement worthless (Fig 2 :40).
2. **A room cannot anchor a claim.** Fig 7 :35: "A room’s agreement is variation, not a verdict. Selection between rooms is where the ground votes." Adding more human judges raises the vocabulary, not the anchor.
3. **Sending the theory first spends the reader's independence.** Fig 10 :42: "A verifier that can reconstruct the claim cannot verify it." The outside frame must not hold the interiors.

Legally: scraping personal emails for unsolicited bulk outreach breaches GDPR and CAN-SPAM-style rules in many jurisdictions. That is a practical failure on top of the structural one.

## The answer in one line

The offered either/or is a union masquerading as one question. Novelty is a frame-relative membership test, so an AI can mint terms new to its own library, but no frame, human or AI, adds an atom to the world, and human knowledge is the legitimate anchor only for the question that is constitutively about human knowledge.
