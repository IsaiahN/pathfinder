## Five findings from the wider literature that bear on the claim

1. **P-creativity versus H-creativity is the figure distinction in a different vocabulary.** Paul and Stokes (SEP): a student's compatibilism can be P-creative without being H-creative. "creativity always exhibits psychological novelty, though it doesn't always exhibit historical novelty." This is Fig 6 :22 ("NOVEL means novel relative to atoms, not to the world") from cognitive science and philosophy. Use it as corroboration, never as the authority.
2. **Novelty scopes are explicit in business.** Wikipedia Innovation: "new to the firm, new to the market, new to the industry, or new to the world." This makes the bundle/split visible outside the framework. A single unqualified "novel" hides which scope is being claimed.
3. **Law refuses the absolute claim.** Wikipedia Novelty (patent): no search can prove newness. Combined with Fig 8 :39, this is a low-overlap corroboration of the "no proof of absence" verdict. The two sources share no author or evidence pool.
4. **Mathematics refuses self-certification.** Chaitin's incompleteness theorem, via Wikipedia Kolmogorov complexity: a system cannot prove that a specific string exceeds its own complexity beyond a fixed constant. This is the mathematical cousin of Fig 8 :37, "A frame cannot certify its own limit." Different field, different proof, same shape.
5. **Animal behaviour refuses the human-only framing.** Wikipedia g factor in non-humans: innovation is observed in animals as novel solutions to everyday problems, drawn from a meta-analysis of 4,000 primate behaviour papers (Reader, Hager and Laland 2011). A novelty standard need not be human to be a standard.

## The two-stage offer (and the optional third stage)

1. **Stage 1.** Send the residual, the split, the `specified` output and the pre-registered refuter. Record the verdict before any theory is shown.
2. **Stage 2, opt in only.** If the recipient is curious after recording a verdict, offer the three links: the local figure text, the canonical README and How Intelligence Actually Works. State that the figures, not the summary, are the authority.
3. **Stage 3, method reuse.** Offer the formula as a tool, with the instruction that each user writes their own refuter and states their own mode. A frame cannot check the direction of its own question (formula "Direction"), so the user must name the ground for their own problem.

**Hard precondition.** Stages 1 to 3 only work if the stage-1 output survives. The agency open item in `01_figure_grounded_audit.md` §5 must be declared, not glossed. Do not claim the framework answers whether an AI "really creates" in the agency sense; it does not test that.
