## Round trip gap R_T

The Symbols table defines R_T as `gap(x, (T_E ∘ T_A)(x))`, measuring what the coarser description cannot hold. Fig 4 :30 requires it to be measured rather than assumed. I ran the round trip on the condensation this folder actually uses.

1. **Sent up (x):** the verbatim figure set, chiefly Fig 6 :21-26 (mint and import), Fig 2 :36-37 (anchor legitimacy), Fig 8 :39 and Fig 9 :44 (no proof of absence), Fig 9 :54-55 (union split).
2. **Abstraction (T_A):** the shorthand in `07_the_tether_answer.md`: novelty is frame-relative, an AI can mint new terms but no frame adds a primitive to the world, and human knowledge is the anchor only for human-relative questions.
3. **Concretisation (T_E):** the question's concrete terms: "can an AI create genuinely novel ideas?" and "is novelty only judgeable relative to human knowledge?"

**R_T is nonzero.** Four things the shorthand holds do not survive the trip back down:

- the **word "create"**: the shorthand says "mint", which is composition, while the question's "create" carries an agency condition the figures do not test. The primary declares this as an open item; a condensation that drops the declaration would set this part of R_T to zero and be a dead-reckoning error;
- the **word "genuinely"**: the shorthand says "world-atom-added", a binding made by the searcher. Returning it to "genuinely" without the binding reintroduces the preference the formula says a frame cannot see (formula "Direction");
- the **scope of "relative to human knowledge"**: the shorthand distinguishes anchor from instrument; the original phrase collapses them. That distinction is lost on the way down and must be re-stated explicitly;
- the **two sub-questions**: the shorthand splits a union. The original bundles it. A reader who hears only the shorthand may hear one answer to one question and miss that two verdicts are required.

Two things survive cleanly: NOVEL is relative to atoms(Γ), and no frame adds a primitive to the world. Those are the residue worth sending. R_T was **not** measured numerically because no ground reading exists; it is recorded qualitatively as the enumerated set of dropped constraints. Treating "the answer is just novelty is relative" as identical to the full derivation would set R_T to zero and would be the dead-reckoning error Fig 4 :30 names.
