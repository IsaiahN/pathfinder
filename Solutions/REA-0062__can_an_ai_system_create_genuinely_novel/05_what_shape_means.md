## 1. A shape is a constraint, not a filler

The canonical README states the limit: the framework returns *"what a missing thing would have to look like (**a shape, not a name**)."* Fig 9 :32: *"you get a kind of thing, never the answer itself"*. Fig 13 :135: *"It cannot say which primitive is missing, because naming it would mean already holding it."*

From *The Library of Babel*: *"You can cut your search short if the set of books returned is small and descriptive enough that it provides a general shape of the answer that satisfies your question."*

So a **shape** is the narrowed form of an answer you can state before you can name it. It constrains; it does not fill. For this question the shape is:

> Novelty is a membership test against the atoms of a frame, declared from outside that frame, with an anchor that is legitimate only for the question actually asked.

That is a shape. *"This generated idea is genuinely new in the world"* is a name, and the framework does not hand it out.

## 2. How a shape is obtained

- *Library of Babel*: use the vocabulary you already have that is *closest* to the thing, look for recurring new terms that overlap or correlate with it, recurse. *"With each new term gained, you are able to narrow the space by definition and deepen your vocabulary."*
- Fig 6 :30: *"The description travels. Fails by being sharpened into terms only we can read. Cast it in effect terms."* Fig 9 :30-31: *"your words are borrowed too, so describe the effect rather than the cause."*
- *Decoding Tacit Knowledge*: *"When contact isn't possible, the best you can send is a plain description of what you'd expect to see, because anyone can check that against the world."*
- *Arrival of the Fittest*: *"a frame searching for an answer of a given SHAPE holds that shape as a PREFERENCE, not a term. Unbound, predicting nothing, no residual - so nothing above can see it."* The remedy is a discipline: make the shape an ordinary term. That is why `01_figure_grounded_audit.md` binds "genuinely novel" as `world-atom-added` before reasoning about it.
- *Library of Babel* on stopping: the answer is reached when *"the finish line will also be the starting place"*, and the paradox is that by then you have *"composed the answer through the process of locating it."*

For this question, "novelty" is a borrowed word with at least three scopes. *Wikipedia Innovation* (read 2026-11-23) makes them explicit: "new to the firm, new to the market, new to the industry, or new to the world." Describing the effect rather than the word is what lets the two sub-questions separate.

## 3. What licenses a name

Only two things, both outside the shape itself:

1. **The ground settles it.** Formula step 5: *"the ground settles it. Verified only by what pays. A gate is not the ground."* The README: the framework *"stops at the limit of what the available instruments can distinguish, and records the lineage so the stop is honest."*
2. **A provenanced import from a frame whose closure differs** (Fig 6 :23, Fig 8 :50-52), with a source gate and a shadow test.

*They're Scanning Books* sharpens the second: *"The real import is not a new term in the existing space. It is a new dimension of the space."* And: *"A minimum viable set of six terms is a human's careful guess at reality's compression axes; another sensorium's minimum set need not be those six."* Also: *"Declaring the primitives does not make them neutral. It makes the choice visible."*

The human record is a visible choice of anchor, not a discovery of nature's own standard. Calling it *the* standard for all novelty turns a visible choice into an invisible one.

## 4. Why "genuinely" is the specific forbidden move

*The Hard Problem is About Source* gives the mechanism. Metamerism: an instrument reads green and *"the instrument is speaking only about what it measured, it cannot comment on the source."* One green light, or yellow plus blue, or RGB, or CMYK, or a scheme not yet discovered. *"If several paths create the same behavior, then behavior is a projection with lossy information (it cannot define consciousness in full)."* It was the **spectrometer**, not an argument, that resolved it: *"The answer is unreached until a better instrument exists."*

A novelty detector reads "not found in the corpus" and names the source as "new in the world." That is the metamerism error: a source named from a reading, where several sources could give the same reading. What is owed is a discriminator, not a name.

*How Intelligence Actually Works* makes the same point about artefacts: *"The recursion converges toward indiscernibility, and indiscernibility is not identity."* Its provenance experiments show that the frame alone cannot tell which route produced an output. So an output that looks novel is not thereby novel.

*They're Scanning Books* puts the load-bearing sentence plainly: *"A corpus is the compressed output of human minds; the capacity that produced it is a generator, and outputs do not contain their generator."* Training on that output reproduces the traces, not the generator. This is the strongest essay-level corroboration of the figure rule that mint cannot add an atom.

## 5. Q1  -  ANSWERED, no further question needed

**What licenses a particular novelty judgment?** Nothing in the framework. The framework returns a shape: a membership test over a declared frame's atoms, judged from outside that frame. A particular judgment is licensed by the ground settling a term, or by a provenanced import with a shadow test. Until one of those, the name is the searcher's own contribution and must be recorded as such.

**Does the common reading commit the error the README forbids?** Yes, in two ways. It reads "new to the record" as "new in the world" (metamerism), and it treats the human record as the only possible outside frame. Both are names where a shape is what is available.

## 6. Two consequences for outreach

**Prestige is not action.** From *The Recursive Self-Improvement Trap*: *"Prestige must never be allowed to validate an action directly... Prestige can fund an action. It can give an action a budget to be tried. But it cannot settle an action. Only the ground can settle it. The Ground Maintainer must be blind to prestige."*

A campaign that gathers famous names to agree that AI novelty is real is the named collapse, not corroboration. The legitimate ask gives the claim a budget to be tried and lets the ground settle it.

**Agreement is not a verdict.** From *How a Society of Agents Would Work*: *"A group is only worth having while its members stay different."* And: *"The group's agreement is never the verdict."* From *Decoding Tacit Knowledge*: *"Sounding right isn't the same as being checked against the world, which is why I call the Turing test a test of fluency, not accuracy."* So a fluent human judgment that "this is novel" is a fluency result until it is ranged against a ground.

## 7. Correction to my own §3-§4

I first wrote this section as if "shape, not a name" forbade naming any particular structure or standard. That overstates the rule. The README's phrase is *"what a **missing** thing would have to look like (a shape, not a name)."* It governs a primitive not held in Γ. The human record **is** held, and so is the membership test. Naming them is a binding from the verification frame onto held atoms, not the invention of a new primitive.

What remains wrong in the common reading is not the naming. It is (a) calling a binding an import that moves the wall (Fig 6 :26: *"The world does not gain primitives; a frame gains access."*) and (b) reading a projection as a source. Both claims are now recorded so neither is quietly lost.

## 8. My own audit is a simulation, not a ground reading

Same essay: *"LLMs can quickly simulate an approximate answer/output with the formula found in the architecture post... However, the truly testable output would need to be derived from the real architecture or working the formula yourself."* The architecture article is blunter: the outputs *"must be seen as simulacra"* and *"could never be verified if the solutions presented were not contrived to fit the formula."*

So `01` is a simulated derivation, checked for internal consistency against the figures. It is **not** a test of the question and must be labelled as such. It cannot settle anything it reports on.
