## What is being decided, in effect terms

The question asks for two different things under one "or": a frequency (how often a predicate called "Earth-like" is instantiated) and a discrimination (which of Earth's properties are load-bearing for a named outcome). The two are not alternatives. Both can hold at once. The residual is a union compound, so the verdicts are kept per domain and never averaged. An answer would look like a declared predicate set, an occurrence rate with a confidence bound over a declared sample, and an enumerated habitat with each Earth property tagged relative to an outcome, each tag settled by measurement. Binding that shape as an ordinary term lets it accrue a residual and be refused (Formula "Direction"; Fig 5 :65).
