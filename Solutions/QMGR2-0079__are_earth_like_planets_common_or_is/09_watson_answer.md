## QUESTION

Decide, in effect terms, what the Tether says about the question "Are Earth-like planets common, or is Earth unusual in important ways?" Option A: read the "or" as a forced choice and answer one half. Option B: recognise a union compound, unbundle it, and give a separate verdict per half with the predicate declared. What each leads to: A produces a single number or a single uniqueness claim whose meaning depends on an undeclared predicate and an unnamed outcome, and it spends the independence of the people checking it. B produces a checkable shape, a declared `gap` for the count, five written refuters, and an open feature ranking until a ground settles it. The line "Unreachable is a verdict here, not a silence" (Fig 13 :134) governs the choice.

## DOMAIN CENSUS

R by arity, symmetry and scale, then one line per domain giving actors, currency and anchor, partition or union, and order by dependency.

- **R (the residual).** A requested verdict on a frequency and a feature discrimination bundled under one "or". Arity: two conditions, so a **union compound** (Fig 9 :54, :55), not one hard question. Symmetry: the common versus unusual judgment is a threshold comparison (⋛) against a reference distribution, not a symmetric combine; the important-ways judgment is an isomer and functional-group question. Scale: population count, single-object feature list, and deep-time habitability.
- **Domain 1, occurrence.** Actors: Kepler and TESS photometry, injected synthetic transits, host-star radii, the surveyed sample. Currency: detections, completeness corrections, occurrence rate with confidence interval. Anchor: the observed sample, a counter (Fig 2 :29). Nameable: yes.
- **Domain 2, discrimination.** Actors: Earth's feature list (mass, Moon, obliquity, plate tectonics, magnetic field, oxygen, gas giants, galactic location), plus the outcome to be explained. Currency: which features change the outcome, enumerated from contact. Anchor: a measurement of a feature's effect, of which there is one inhabited instance. Nameable: yes, but thin.
- **Domain 3, verification.** Actors: outside readers, referees, re-census seat. Currency: agreement and residual. Anchor: the ground, never agreement. Nameable: yes, but zero independence in this simulated run.
- **Order by dependency.** Declare the predicate before counting (Fig 9 :27); enumerate the habitat before ranking actors (Fig 11 :43); bind the outcome as an ordinary term before judging importance (Fig 5 :65); bring a characterised residual, not the source, to D3 (Fig 10 :52).

## VERDICT (from the figures)

The forced "or" is invalid. The question is a union of a count and a feature discrimination, and the two verdicts are kept separate and never averaged. For D1, the frequency is a counter and therefore anchor-owned; the figure library yields a shape and a predicate requirement, not a number, so the honest verdict is a `gap` with an import instruction: measure over a declared box, publish completeness and confidence, and treat non-detections as bounds. For D2, the shape survives: declare the predicate, enumerate the habitat, bind the outcome that "important" refers to, and treat Earth and its rocky peers as isomers over a shared atom set. Which features are load-bearing stays unreached with one inhabited instance and no source-discriminating instrument for Earth twins. D3 holds that agreement is not a verdict and non-detections bound rather than settle. This is `specified` and `candidate`, never `grounded`.

- **cost if wrong:** bounded and locatable. The packet states its mode, declares the predicate problem, writes five refuters before the conclusion and records the lineage, so an error is findable. The real cost is spending the independence of outside readers by sending a single number or a uniqueness claim before the shape has been checked. Fig 2 :42 prices that: "Buy independence in proportion to the cost of being wrong." That cost is avoidable by leading with the union split, the gap and the refuters.
- **undoable:** yes. The count can be imported with provenance when a declared measurement exists, and the feature ranking can be filled when a discriminating instrument and a second inhabited case exist. No primitive has to be withdrawn, and no figure line has to be revised. A sent claim can be superseded by a corrected packet.
- **reversed by:** a ground reading, not agreement. A strain-free figure line yielding the rate would close D1; one stable predicate across instrument revisions would collapse the union; a feature shown important independent of the named outcome would break valence relativity; radius plus insolation shown to determine habitability would break the source limit; two same-atom planets with different arrangements shown to have identical outcomes would collapse the isomer claim. Until one of these fires, the verdict stands as `candidate`.
