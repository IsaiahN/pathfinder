# The Tether's answer: what makes a system robust to unfamiliar inputs and adversarial pressure

**Mode: `general` on the shape, `specified` where it is applied to AI systems, nothing `grounded`.** This is a shape and a route, not a result. It is a simulated derivation checked against the figures (`05_what_shape_means.md` section 6), not a ground reading.

## 1. The residual it answers

`R = |Γ(b,a) − o′|` per slot `s`. Why does it stay small in some systems when `o′` is unfamiliar, or when an actor in the habitat is working to widen it? The two pressures are a **union compound**, not one hard question: a case in two conditions is not a harder case of either (Fig 9 :57). They have to be unbundled, each with its own loop and its own failure. An aggregate accuracy figure is a filter for the budget, not a witness for the verdict (Fig 9 :60).

## 2. The shape, per domain, kept separate

**D1, the per-slot loop.** Fig 1 :19: **"The residual is indexed by object slot, not aggregated."** Fig 1 :22: **"Aggregating across slots is how a live signal disappears into an average."** A global score near zero with one live slot is a legal state and not an inert one. The first requirement is therefore a slot-indexed monitor that keeps `R⁺_s` visible. The same figure adds that a frame cannot score itself with a quantity it also produces (Fig 1 :28), so the monitor cannot be the same quantity the system optimises.

**D1, the route and the refusal.** Unfamiliarity is not one bin. Fig 5 :11-14 separates *"held: the model was right, nothing owed"*, **"new: extend perception, aim the probe"**, *"mis-attached: refit what you have, do not invent"* and *"mechanism broken: a new term is owed"*. Fig 5 :53 adds the fifth: **"refuted: a term we hold is wrong; a term is owed that says so"**. Adversarial pressure typically makes a held regularity false, so the operator needed is negation, not another added term. Operators table :58: **"a refutation is a negation, which is why a vocabulary without it can only accumulate"**. A system with `−` but not `¬` can say what is missing and cannot say what is wrong.

**D2, the reach.** Fig 8 :32: **"Closure is idempotent. Composing inside a closed set never leaves it, so no search, however long, adds an atom. This one is definitional: it is what closure means."** A genuinely new input class is not in `closure(Γ)`. Three questions, not one: SEARCHED (in here and unfound, asked first), INWARD (extend an instrument already returning something), OUTWARD (characterise `R` in effect terms and find the frame whose closure predicts its shape, with source gate, shadow test and a debit against independence). Underivable in principle and unreachable at this budget are different facts (Fig 8 :40-42). The wall moves only on contact with a differing closure (Fig 6 :23).

**D3, the ground channel and independence.** Fig 10 :24: **"the ground does not decay, the channel does"**. When the error signal stops arriving, that is a perfect prediction or a dead channel, and from inside they look the same. A separate seat must be able to return *"you have lost touch"* (Fig 10 :28) without reading the interior it checks; Fig 10 :42: **"A verifier that can reconstruct the claim cannot verify it."** Independence is the quantity that makes a check mean something: Fig 2 :31 **"Independence, between two frames, is how little they draw on the same evidence."** Fig 2 :35 **"Alignment is triangulation, not negotiation. A mirror cannot correct you."** Fig 2 :40 **"agreement about the world is worthless when the agreeing frames share an evidence pool"**. Adding evaluators that share one benchmark is a mirror chain, motion without progress (Fig 7 :12-13, :36).

**D4, the habitat.** Fig 11 :39: **"So the answerable question is not what intelligence is, but what makes a system capable. And capability is a property of agent-and-habitat, never of the agent alone."** Fig 11 :40: **"A system cannot be made more capable by improving the agent in isolation. An improvement that does not change contact changes nothing, however much it improves."** The habitat is enumerated, never composed (Fig 11 :43), and an adversary is an actor whose valence is relative to `R` (Fig 11 :49), so no standing list of defensive components survives a change of residual. Fig 11 :70: **"A test harness is a substituted habitat. Which is why a synthetic solve proves wiring and never capability."**

**D5, the round trip and the price.** Fig 4 :30: **"So take the round trip and measure the gap. Without it you are navigating by dead reckoning: confident about a position nobody checked."** Any reduction of these conditions to a single robustness score is lossy, and the gap `R_T` has to be measured rather than assumed zero. Fig 12 :125: **"A composition costs in two currencies, and they do not add."** Bits price the description and actions price finding out whether it holds; one accounting will not notice the shortfall.

**The union, not an average.** These five verdicts stay separate. A strong per-slot monitor cannot repair a dead channel (Fig 3 :19: *"Optimising a link that already works cannot rescue one that does not."*), and more internal agreement cannot substitute for a differing closure. The result is a union of conditions, not a ranked list.

## 3. What it explicitly does not say

- Not an architecture. The figures return a shape, not a name (Fig 9 :32: **"you get a kind of thing, never the answer itself"**). Which ensemble, regulariser, verifier or monitor wins is a declared `gap` (F9 in `01_figure_grounded_audit.md`).
- Not that robustness is a property of the model alone. The question's phrase "internal mechanisms" was corrected against Fig 11 :39-40 and the strain is recorded, not hidden.
- Not that more data or more search closes a missing class. Composition inside a closure adds no atom.
- Not that agreement among evaluators is evidence. Fig 7 :35: **"A room's agreement is variation, not a verdict. Selection between rooms is where the ground votes."**
- Not `grounded`. No measurement was taken and no instrument discriminated among candidate mechanisms.

## 4. What would settle it - the ground

A source-discriminating instrument, not a further argument. *The Hard Problem is About Source*: an instrument that reads green cannot comment on the source; the spectrometer resolved it. Here:

1. **Channel test:** can a dead channel be distinguished from a perfect prediction from inside the loop? If yes, F3 falls.
2. **Independence test:** does a system with one evidence pool and a moveable reference stay robust against an adversary able to move its frames? If yes, F4 falls.
3. **Novelty test:** does internal composition alone produce robustness to a genuinely new atom-class, with no import and no instrument extension? If yes, F5 falls.
4. **Isomer test:** do two systems with the same components but different bonds behave identically under both a novel input and an adversarial input? If yes, the isomer part of `06_isomers_and_order.md` falls.
5. **Capability test:** does an agent-only change move the ground while habitat and contact are held fixed? If yes, Fig 11 :40 is refuted.
6. **Round trip test:** does a scalar robustness metric preserve every per-slot refusal, channel state and bond arrangement? If yes, `R_T = 0` and F8 falls.

All six were written in `01_figure_grounded_audit.md` before the conclusion was drafted. A failure is more useful than an endorsement.

## 5. The honest stop

**Unreached, not unreachable.** Fig 13 :134: **"Unreachable is a verdict here, not a silence"**. Fig 8 :39: **"So a search that finds nothing is never a proof of absence."** The shape stops at the limit of what the available instruments can distinguish, and records the lineage so the stop is checkable. The canonical rule adds *"Indiscernibility is not identity"*. Which specific mechanism fills the shape needs an instrument that can tell candidates apart, and none has been applied here. Status: `candidate`, not `accepted`.

## 6. One-paragraph version for outreach

> The Tether does not name a robustness module. It says the error signal has to stay indexed per object slot, because an average hides the one live slot an adversary created; that unfamiliarity splits into distinct bins, and a system that cannot say "a held regularity is false" can only accumulate; that a silent error signal is either a perfect prediction or a dead channel, so a check from outside the loop is load bearing; that independent frames ranged against a reference nobody can move are the only thing that makes agreement mean something, since ten copies of one evaluator are one frame wearing many names; that genuinely new input classes cannot be composed from inside a fixed vocabulary and need an instrument or a differing frame; and that capability belongs to the system and its habitat, not the system alone, so a fixed synthetic harness proves wiring and not capability. What it offers is that shape, six falsifiers written in advance, and the instruments that would close it. Which specific mechanism wins is left open until a ground settles it.
