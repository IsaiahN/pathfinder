## QUESTION

What kinds of internal mechanisms make a system robust to unfamiliar inputs and adversarial pressure? Restated in effect terms: why does R = |Γ(b,a) - o'| stay small per slot s when o' is unfamiliar, or when an actor in the habitat is working to widen it? Option A: robustness is a component inside the model, so the search is for that component. Option B: robustness is a union of conditions spanning the per-slot loop, the reach, the ground channel, independence between frames, the habitat and the round trip, so the search is for the shape those conditions must satisfy. A leads to a single gadget that can be defeated by a different pressure and to an aggregate score that hides the live slot. B leads to a checkable shape with six refuters written in advance and an open slot where a name is not licensed. The sought-for shape was bound as an ordinary term before the run (Fig 5 :66), because a frame cannot check its own direction otherwise.

Quote: *"accidents in machine learning systems, defined as unintended and harmful behavior that may emerge from poor design of real-world AI systems"*; the abstract names distributional shift. Prediction: failures come from design and shift, not from one missing gadget. **Corroborates** the multi-condition reading. Overlap: low with the framework, but overlapping with the other AI papers below (one AI-safety witness).
- **Adversarial ML.** Christian Szegedy et al., "Intriguing properties of neural networks", arXiv:1312.6199, 2013 to 2014, opened 2026-11-24. Quote: *"deep neural networks learn input-output mappings that are fairly discontinuous to a significant extend"*; the same perturbation transfers across networks trained on different subsets. Prediction: worst-case perturbation is a distinct condition with cross-model reach. **Corroborates** the REFUTED-bin reading. Overlap: same broad AI pool as the papers above.
- **Adversarial ML.** Ian Goodfellow, Jonathon Shlens and Christian Szegedy, "Explaining and Harnessing Adversarial Examples", arXiv:1412.6572, 2014 to 2015, opened 2026-11-24. Quote: *"the primary cause of neural networks' vulnerability to adversarial perturbation is their linear nature"* and generalisation across architectures; adversarial training reduces error. Prediction: one lever helps one condition. **Corroborates** the union reading rather than a single mechanism. Overlap: same AI pool.
- **Robustness evaluation.** Dan Hendrycks and Thomas Dietterich, "Benchmarking Neural Network Robustness to Common Corruptions and Perturbations", ICLR 2019, arXiv:1903.12261, opened 2026-11-24. Quote: *"this benchmark evaluates performance on common corruptions and perturbations not worst-case adversarial perturbations"*; a bypassed adversarial defence gives common-perturbation robustness. Prediction: corruption and worst-case adversarial robustness are different conditions. **Corroborates** F6. Overlap: same AI pool.
- **Machine learning, human cognition overlap.** Robert Geirhos, Jorn-Henrik Jacobsen, Claudio Michaelis, Richard Zemel, Wieland Brendel, Matthias Bethge and Felix Wichmann, "Shortcut learning in deep neural networks", Nature Machine Intelligence 2, 665 to 673, 2020, opened 2026-11-24. Quote: *"Shortcuts are decision rules that perform well on standard benchmarks but fail to transfer to more challenging testing conditions"*; related issues in comparative psychology, education and linguistics. Prediction: a rule can pay on a benchmark and fail on transfer, which is a wrong binding. **Corroborates** the BROKEN/refusal reading from a different evidence pool (comparative psychology and education), so it is a genuine second witness for the shape.
- **Ecology and resilience.** Morgan MathisonSlee, Steven Lade, Conor Barnes et al., "Fourteen propositions for resilience, fourteen years later", Ecology and Society 27(3):8, 2022, opened 2026-11-24. Quote: a *"rigidity trap"* in which a system becomes *"highly connected, rigid, and inflexible"* and *"System capacity for novelty is diminished"*; also an inverted-U caution about too much redundancy. Prediction: more internal connectedness and agreement can reduce adaptive capacity. **Corroborates** F4 and F7 from a genuinely low-overlap field.

## VERDICT (from the figures)

Robustness is a union of five conditions, not one internal component: per-slot residual with a refusal operator; a reach that admits imports for genuine novelty; a non-updating ground with a channel monitor and independent frames; a habitat in which contact is enumerated; and a measured round trip gap with bits and actions read separately. The verdicts stay separate and are never averaged. The question's "internal mechanisms" framing is narrowed to "conditions that may cross the model boundary", with the strain recorded. Which specific mechanism fills the shape is a declared `gap`. Mode `general` for the shape, `specified` for the AI mapping, `candidate`, not `grounded`. The world sources corroborate the shape, especially the multi-condition and independence claims, but they do not decide it.

- **cost if wrong:** misdirected diagnosis and a spent independent checker. A dead channel would be met with more internal capacity; a one-pool evaluation would be read as corroboration; a single aggregate score would hide the live slot. The cost is bounded and locatable because each condition has its own refuter, but an expert's twenty minutes could be spent refuting a shape that was never the right one. Fig 2 :42 prices the remedy: "Buy independence in proportion to the cost of being wrong."
- **undoable:** yes. Nothing is deleted if a condition fails; a refusal is added and the reach falls because *"something was added rather than because anything was deleted"* (Fig 6 amendment). The shape and its six falsifiers are recorded with provenance, so a later reader can locate the error (Fig 10 :62-64). A published claim can be superseded by a corrected one.
- **reversed by:** one of six ground tests: a dead channel distinguishable from perfect prediction from inside; a one-pool system staying robust against a frame able to move its reference; internal composition reaching a new atom-class with no import or instrument extension; identical behaviour under the same components but different bonds; an agent-only change moving the ground with contacts held fixed; or a scalar metric preserving all slot refusals, channel states and bond arrangements with R_T = 0. A low-overlap source showing that per-slot indexing or independence does not predict robustness under shift would also return the relevant condition to the census. Agreement by itself is not such a source (Fig 2 :40).
