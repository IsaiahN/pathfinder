# Expert verification packet - the Tether on robustness to unfamiliar and adversarial input

**What this is:** a claim in a form a robustness researcher can check. Mode, derivation and refuters are stated before the conclusion, so a reader knows what to look for and what would settle it.

**Mode: `general` on the shape, `specified` where applied to AI systems, not `grounded`.** No measurement is claimed. This is a shape and a route, not a result. It is a simulated derivation checked for internal consistency against the figures, not a ground reading.

## 1. The claim, in one paragraph

Robustness is a union of conditions, not one internal component. The prediction error must stay indexed per object slot, because an average hides the live slot an adversary created. The system must be able to distinguish "this is new", "this binding is broken" and "this held regularity is false", because the last needs a refusal operator and the first two do not. The error channel must be checked by a seat outside the loop, since a dead channel and a perfect prediction look the same from inside. Checking frames must be independent and ranged against a reference they cannot move, since agreement inside one evidence pool is a mirror. Genuine novelty needs an instrument extension or an import from a differing closure, since composition inside a fixed vocabulary adds no new class. And capability belongs to the system and its habitat, so a fixed synthetic harness proves wiring rather than capability. The specific mechanism that fills this shape is not named by the figures.

## 2. Three checkable sub-claims

### C1 - framework-internal, checkable by reading Figures 1, 5 and the Operators table

- **Statement:** the signal is per-slot and the route needs a refusal operator. "Unfamiliar" is at least three conditions, not one.
- **Source, verbatim:**
  - Fig 1 :19 **"The residual is indexed by object slot, not aggregated."**
  - Fig 1 :22 **"Aggregating across slots is how a live signal disappears into an average."**
  - Fig 5 :53 **"refuted: a term we hold is wrong; a term is owed that says so"**
  - Operators table :58 **"a refutation is a negation, which is why a vocabulary without it can only accumulate"**
- **Refuter:** demonstrate a system whose worst-case per-slot residual is large under a shift while an unaggregated monitor shows no live slot; or exhibit a system with no negation operator that recovers from a refutation of a held term with no new refusing term and no fall in reach; or exhibit a single intervention clearing NOVEL, BROKEN and REFUTED cases with no categorically different residue.

### C2 - framework-internal and evaluation-facing, checkable by reading Figures 2, 10 and 11

- **Statement:** the channel check must sit outside the loop, and the checking frames must be independent and ranged against an anchor they cannot move. Capability is agent-and-habitat, not agent alone.
- **Source, verbatim:**
  - Fig 10 :24 **"the ground does not decay, the channel does"**
  - Fig 10 :28 **"you have lost touch"**
  - Fig 10 :42 **"A verifier that can reconstruct the claim cannot verify it."**
  - Fig 2 :31 **"Independence, between two frames, is how little they draw on the same evidence."**
  - Fig 2 :35 **"Alignment is triangulation, not negotiation. A mirror cannot correct you."**
  - Fig 11 :40 **"A system cannot be made more capable by improving the agent in isolation. An improvement that does not change contact changes nothing, however much it improves."**
- **What to check:** is a dead error channel distinguishable from perfect prediction from inside the loop? Does the arrangement actually put a checker in a different closure, or is it a copy of the same evaluator? Does the reported result survive a habitat substitution (different harness, different red team, different budget)?
- **Refuter:** a dead channel that is distinguishable from perfect prediction from inside the system; or a system with one evidence pool and a moveable reference staying robust against an adversary able to move its frames; or an agent-only change moving the ground with habitat and contacts held fixed; or a synthetic harness proving capability rather than wiring.

### C3 - framework-internal derivation about novelty and measurement

- **Statement:** a genuinely new input class is not reachable by internal composition alone, and any scalar summary of these conditions carries a round trip gap that must be measured.
- **Source, verbatim:**
  - Fig 8 :32 **"Closure is idempotent. Composing inside a closed set never leaves it, so no search, however long, adds an atom. This one is definitional: it is what closure means."**
  - Fig 6 :23 **"No frame escapes its own closure. The bound moves only on contact with a frame whose closure differs."**
  - Fig 8 :39 **"So a search that finds nothing is never a proof of absence."**
  - Fig 4 :30 **"So take the round trip and measure the gap. Without it you are navigating by dead reckoning: confident about a position nobody checked."**
- **Refuter:** internal composition producing robustness to a genuinely new atom-class with no import and no instrument extension; or conflating underivable-in-principle with unreachable-at-budget and getting the same instruction; or a scalar robustness metric that recovers every per-slot refusal, channel state and bond arrangement exactly after abstraction and concretisation, giving `R_T = 0`.

## 3. What is explicitly not claimed

- Not a named architecture. Which ensemble, regulariser, verifier or monitor wins is a `gap`, and the figures forbid naming it from the shape alone (Fig 9 :32 **"you get a kind of thing, never the answer itself"**).
- Not that robustness is a property of the model in isolation. The question's phrase "internal mechanisms" was corrected against Fig 11 :39-40 and the strain is recorded.
- Not that more data or more adversarial training closes a missing class. Both may help SEARCHED cases; neither adds an atom (Fig 8 :32).
- Not that agreement among evaluators is evidence. Fig 7 :35 **"A room's agreement is variation, not a verdict. Selection between rooms is where the ground votes."**
- Not `grounded`. No measurement was taken and no instrument discriminated among candidate mechanisms.
- Not a claim of novelty for the framework. Its own article says the triangulation claim *"fits four cases already known and has not been tested forward."*

## 5. What would settle it

A source-discriminating instrument, not a further argument. Six tests, all pre-registered in `01_figure_grounded_audit.md` section 4:

1. Channel test: dead channel distinguishable from perfect prediction from inside.
2. Independence test: one-pool system staying robust against a frame able to move its reference.
3. Novelty test: internal composition reaching a genuinely new atom-class with no import and no instrument extension.
4. Isomer test: same components and different bonds giving identical behaviour under both novel and adversarial input.
5. Capability test: agent-only change moving the ground with habitat and contacts held fixed.
6. Round trip test: a scalar robustness metric preserving every slot refusal, channel state and bond arrangement, so `R_T = 0`.

A refutation of any one returns the relevant condition to the census; it does not flip the verdict by argument alone (Fig 2 :34 would be the coherent-drift failure if it did).
