## Independent domain census

I redid the census from the residual line alone before relying on the primary's conclusions: a wage floor is imposed, and two outcomes are requested of it, one about household income against a line and one about the employment of some workers.

- **R (arity / symmetry / scale).** Three, one lever and two outcomes. Symmetry: the floor is a ⋛ threshold, and "reduce" is an ordered comparison with a subtraction, not a symmetric join. The two outcomes have different currencies. Scale: per worker-firm slot, per household slot, per sector, per regime. Fig 1 :19 "The residual is indexed by object slot, not aggregated." Fig 9 :4-5 "are not one hard question but several well-formed ones. Split it rather than search." This is a **union**, not a partition, because the low-skilled worker in a poor household satisfies both conditions.
- **Domain 1, labour-market frame.** Actors: workers, firms, hours, the floor. Currency: jobs and wage rate. Anchor: payroll records, a counter that does not update on what a frame thinks. Question type: one condition of the union.
- **Domain 2, household-distribution frame.** Actors: households, heads, secondary earners, transfers. Currency: disposable income against a line. Anchor: the household income survey. Question type: the second condition, with a targeting seam because a low wage and a poor household are not the same set.
- **Domain 3, research / identification frame.** Actors: researchers, estimators, data sets. Currency: estimates and residuals. Anchor: the measured outcomes, never agreement. In this simulated run all frames draw on one pool, so independence is zero.
- **Domain 4, habitat / coverage frame.** Actors: covered and uncovered sectors, compliance, informal work, household sharing. Currency: coverage and spillover. Anchor: measured formal and informal outcomes. Question type: the compound at the overlap.

Order by dependency: refuse the compromise answer before minting anything (Formula step 2, attributed to `THE_FORMULA.md`); settle the employment slot before the household slot, because a job must precede its wage; describe the residual before naming a frame (Fig 9 :27-28 "describe it before you go looking, or any frame you pick will seem to fit").

My census matches the primary's four domains and its union reading. ## Round trip gap R_T

Fig 4 :30 requires the round trip to be measured rather than assumed. The Symbols table defines R_T as `gap(x, (T_E ∘ T_A)(x))`, the gap between what was sent up and what came back down.

1. **Sent up (x):** the figure set, chiefly Fig 1 :19, Fig 9 :4-5 and :54-57, Fig 11 :49-51, Fig 12 :87 and :125, Fig 13 :134.
2. **Abstraction (T_A):** the shorthand actually carried in `07` and `09`: split first, read each slot against its own anchor, do not add the currencies, the overlap is a compound, the pooled sign is a `gap`.
3. **Concretisation (T_E):** an ordinary policy answer, for example "a moderate floor reduces poverty with little job loss", or "a floor costs low-skill jobs so it cannot reduce poverty".

**R_T is nonzero.** Four things the shorthand holds do not survive the trip back down to an ordinary answer:

- the **per-slot indexing** is lost when a national rate is quoted (Fig 1 :19, :22);
- the **non-updating anchor** is replaced by a published estimate that moves with the literature (Fig 2 :29, :33);
- the **two-currency separation** is collapsed into a net welfare sentence (Fig 12 :125);
- the **union compound** is flattened into a single conditional, so the intersection where both conditions bind disappears (Fig 9 :57).

Conversely, one thing survives cleanly: the floor is a threshold and its valence is relative to the residual, so both effects can be true at once. That is the residue worth sending. R_T was **not** measured numerically, because no ground reading exists; it is recorded qualitatively as the enumerated set of dropped constraints. Treating the formula as identical to the figures would set R_T to zero and would be the dead-reckoning error Fig 4 :30 names. The honest form is a lossy condensation with a recorded gap, which is what `09_watson_answer.md` states.
