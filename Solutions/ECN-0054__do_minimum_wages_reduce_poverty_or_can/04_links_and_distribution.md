## Wider literature that bears on the question

1. **The disagreement is not shrinking with effort.** Neumark and Wascher (NBER, IZA) report a majority of studies pointing to negative employment effects, strongest for the least skilled. Dube (GOV.UK) reports a muted effect and significant earnings gains. Both were opened. Because they draw on overlapping studies and often the same surveys, their disagreement is treated as evidence for a union of conditions, not as two independent frames.
2. **The targeting seam.** Neumark (IZA) and Gindling (IZA) both state that a low wage and a poor household are not the same thing. That is the household condition in D2 and the overlap in D4, corroborated from two articles in one economics pool.
3. **The instrument condition.** *The Hard Problem is About Source* says an instrument that reads green "cannot comment on the source". A national poverty headcount cannot say which household moved, and a national employment rate cannot say which worker lost a job. The essay is a framework source with maximal overlap, so it shapes the design rather than adding independent evidence.
4. **The false-dichotomy check.** The Wikipedia logic source is the genuinely low-overlap corroborator for the claim that the question's "or" excludes viable alternatives. It is secondary and does not decide anything.
5. **The independence arithmetic.** Kish's design effect, via the opened statistics page and the opened society-of-agents essay, is the genuinely low-overlap corroborator for Fig 2 :31 and :40. Agreement among studies that share a survey counts as fewer witnesses than the number of papers.

## Distribution: offer, do not push

Figure-grounded reasons:

- **Fig 4 :22 "Only methods cross up. Only head starts come down."** Sending a pooled conclusion before a reader has described the residual sends a recording, not a generator. Lead with the split and the refuter.
- **Fig 10 :42 "A verifier that can reconstruct the claim cannot verify it."** A reader who has absorbed the framework before giving a Stage 1 verdict has joined the frame being checked.
- **Fig 9 :27-28 "describe it before you go looking, or any frame you pick will seem to fit"** Ask what they would expect to see in payroll and household data before giving them the packet.
- **Fig 2 :40 "disqualifying for a verdict: agreement about the world is worthless when the agreeing frames share an evidence pool"** Do not ask for agreement and do not pool later agreement as corroboration.
- **Fig 2 :42 "Buy independence in proportion to the cost of being wrong."** Prefer one reader with an independent dataset over ten readers with the same dataset.

**Recommended order in a reply:** (1) the residual and the split; (2) ask what they would count as a failure and which anchor they would trust; (3) record the verdict; (4) only then offer the links, with the note that the figures, not the summary, are the authority.

## The two-stage offer

**Stage 1:** residual, split, mode and five refuters, no framework. The reader's verdict is recorded first.

**Stage 2, opt-in:** the three local figure links and the essay link above.

**Stage 3:** the formula as a method to reuse. Each user writes their own refuter, states their own mode, and names their own ground.

**Hard precondition:** stages 1 to 3 only work if the Stage 1 output survives. The output here is a split and a `gap`, not a number. A reader who expects a number is owed the honest statement that no figure line supplies one (Fig 13 :134 "Unreachable is a verdict here, not a silence.").
