## 1. A shape is a constraint, not a filler

The figures README states the limit: the framework returns "what a missing thing would have to look like (a shape, not a name)". Fig 9 :32 "you get a kind of thing, never the answer itself". Fig 13 :134 "Unreachable is a verdict here, not a silence."

From *The Library of Babel*: "you can cut your search short if the set of books returned is small and descriptive enough that it provides a general shape of the answer that satisfies your question."

For this question a **shape** is the form an answer must have before anyone supplies a sign or a number. It says: a verdict per named slot, an anchor that does not move when a frame pushes on it, two currencies kept apart, a compound where the conditions overlap, and a failure condition written before the reading. It does **not** say "yes" or "no" to the question as posed. `per-slot verdict with a named anchor` is a shape. `the minimum wage cuts poverty by 2.5 percentage points` is a name, and a name that belongs to a particular country, year and instrument.

## 2. How the shape is obtained

- *Library of Babel*: use the vocabulary already closest to the thing, look for recurring new terms that overlap or correlate with it, recurse. "With each new term gained, you narrow the space and deepen your vocabulary."
- *Library of Babel* on bundles: "Perhaps your problem was a bundle or conflation of several problems masquerading as one?" This is the exact move Fig 9 :4-5 makes for the question's "or".
- Fig 6 :30 "The description travels. Fails by being sharpened into terms only we can read. Cast it in effect terms." Fig 9 :30-31 "your words are borrowed too, so describe the effect rather than the cause."
- *Decoding Tacit Knowledge*: "When contact isn't possible, the best you can send is a plain description of what you'd expect to see, because anyone can check that against the world."
- *Library of Babel* on stopping: the answer is reached when the finish line is also the starting place, and the paradox is that by then the searcher has composed the answer through locating it.

Applied here: the first vocabulary is "jobs" and "poor households". The closest recurring terms in the opened economics sources are "employment elasticity", "poverty headcount", "poverty gap", "informal sector", "household head" and "design effect". Adding them narrows the search from "does it work" to "which slot, which anchor, which currency".

## 3. What licenses a name, and what is only a binding

Only two things, both outside the shape itself:

1. **The ground settles it.** Formula step 5: "the ground settles it. Verified only by what pays." The README: the framework "stops at the limit of what the available instruments can distinguish, and records the lineage so the stop is honest."
2. **A provenanced import from a frame whose closure differs** (Fig 6 :23, Fig 8 :50-52), with a source gate and a shadow test.

*They're Scanning Books* sharpens this: "A minimum viable set of six terms is a human's careful guess at reality's compression axes; another sensorium's minimum set need not be those six." Also: "Declaring the primitives does not make them neutral. It makes the choice visible."

Payroll records and household income surveys are instruments from the D1 and D2 frames. Naming them in the shape is a binding from those frames, provenanced in the ledger. It is not the invention of a new primitive and it does not move a wall. The correction to my own earlier overstatement is kept in section 6.

## 4. The source-separation error is the one this question invites

*The Hard Problem is About Source* gives the mechanism. An instrument that reads green "cannot comment on the source" because one green light, or yellow plus blue, or RGB, or CMYK, can produce the same reading. "If several paths create the same behavior, then behavior is a projection with lossy information." The resolution came "not by argument, but by the arrival of a better instrument."

Here the projection is a national statistic. A poverty headcount cannot say which household crossed the line. An employment rate cannot say which worker lost a job. An employment elasticity cannot say whether the losses fell on household heads or secondary earners. Gindling's opened IZA article says exactly why this matters: the effect on poverty depends on whether the affected worker is a household head or a secondary earner, and on whether the sector is covered at all. Several distinct household configurations produce the same national reading. What is owed is a better instrument, not a single number named as the answer.

*How Intelligence Actually Works* adds the provenance version: a video frame and a photograph "are indistinguishable as artifacts, and they carry different warrants" and "nothing in the image records which". Two studies returning the same elasticity can have reached it by convergent derivation or by adopting the same estimate. Only the record of where each came from separates them (Fig 8 :50-52).

## 5. Q: what shape does the framework return here?

**Answer:** the separator, not the sign.

1. Split before answering (Fig 9 :4-5, :54-57). The two outcomes are a union of overlapping conditions, not contraries.
2. Keep each verdict indexed to its slot (Fig 1 :19, :22).
3. Range each slot against an anchor that does not update on the frame's opinion (Fig 2 :29, :33).
4. Refuse the addition of the two currencies (Fig 12 :125).
5. Enumerate the habitat outward, including the uncovered sector and the household composition (Fig 11 :43-44, :52-54).
6. Report the universal sign as `gap`, with the instrument it would take to close it (Fig 13 :134, :135).
7. Write the refuter before the run (local Seat Map :287).

No step returns "yes" or "no" to the literal question. That absence is the honest result, not an evasion.

## 6. Correction to my own use of "shape, not a name"

I initially wrote as if "shape, not a name" forbade naming the payroll and household instruments. It does not. The README's phrase governs a **missing** primitive, one not held in Γ. Payroll records, household income surveys and poverty lines are already available instruments in the D1 and D2 frames. Naming them is a provenanced binding, the same move the exemplar's `05` section 5b records for Gradient and Spectrum. What remains forbidden is inventing a seventh primitive, or claiming that the binding moves the wall (Fig 6 :21 "MINT composes inside the closure and can never add an atom."). My §4 metamerism point is **not** retracted: the national statistic still cannot name its source, and the reason is the missing discriminator, not a ban on naming held instruments. Both readings are kept visible.

## 7. Two consequences for this package

**Prestige is not action.** From *The Recursive Self-Improvement Trap*: "Prestige must never be allowed to validate an action directly". Also: "Prestige can fund an action. It can give an action a budget to be tried. But it cannot settle an action. Only the ground can settle it. The Ground Maintainer must be blind to prestige." A list of well known economists who agree with a sign is the named collapse, not corroboration. The legitimate ask gives the claim a budget to be tried against payroll and household data. *How a Society of Agents Would Work* states the arithmetic in plain words: "Ten copies watching the same thing are one opinion said ten times."

**This audit is a simulation, not a ground reading.** Same essay: LLMs "can quickly simulate an approximate answer/output with the formula", but "the truly testable output would need to be derived from the real architecture or working the formula yourself." So `01` is a simulated derivation checked for internal consistency against the figures. It is not a measurement and must be labelled as such. It cannot settle anything it reports on. No payroll or household survey was read for this solution; the economics sources are summaries and reviews of such readings, opened and recorded as secondary material.
