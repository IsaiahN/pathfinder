## Restating the question in effect terms

A legislature sets a wage floor. Two outcomes are then asked about in one breath: does the floor lift households above a poverty line, and can it remove jobs from some workers? Each option leads to a different measurement. The first needs household disposable income against a threshold. The second needs a per-worker employment reading against payroll. They are not two answers to one question. They are two quantities in two currencies with two anchors, and the policy lever is a comparison, not an atom. An answer would look like a verdict per slot, with the slot named, the anchor named, and a failure condition written down before the reading is taken. That ordinary description of an answer is bound as a term in `01_figure_grounded_audit.md` section 9 so it can accrue a residual like anything else.

## What is deliberately not here

- No personal emails of named individuals.
- No ranking of people by how likely they are to believe a verdict.
- No bulk-send plan.
- No pooled number that pretends to settle both outcomes at once.

Three reasons in the figures' own words:

1. **A room does not upgrade the label.** Fig 7 :35: "A room's agreement is variation, not a verdict. Selection between rooms is where the ground votes." Adding agreeable readers raises the vocabulary and does not add an anchor.
2. **Selecting for receptiveness manufactures the disqualifying correlation.** Fig 2 :40: "disqualifying for a verdict: agreement about the world is worthless when the agreeing frames share an evidence pool." Fig 2 :42: "Buy independence in proportion to the cost of being wrong."
3. **Asking a reader to trust before checking spends their independence.** Fig 10 :42: "A verifier that can reconstruct the claim cannot verify it." Fig 10 :52: "Bring a characterised residual, never the source."

Legally: scraping personal emails for unsolicited bulk outreach breaches GDPR and CAN-SPAM style rules in many jurisdictions. Every venue in `03_public_contact_points.csv` is an institutional page or an opt-in submission route, verified on 2026-11-23.
