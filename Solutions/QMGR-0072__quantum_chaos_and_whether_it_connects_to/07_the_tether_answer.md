The chaos bound `λ_L ≤ 2π k_B T / ħ` belongs to the second, and Einstein-gravity black holes and SYK saturate it. The residual is whether the bound governs chaos itself and whether black-hole saturation is a composition fact or an extra law. ## 2. The shape

1. **Chaos is a derived quantity, not a primitive.** Fig 13 :31-32: *"a remainder compounding through a chain that amplifies is chaos"*. Fig 13 :124-125: *"Smoothness, symmetry, chaos and discreteness are each what a particular composition leaves behind, and none is a seventh member."* So a bound on chaos is a threshold on the growth of a derived remainder, not a law about a substance.
2. **The question is a union compound and the verdicts stay separate.** The spectral and dynamical diagnoses share almost no atoms and are not isomers (`06` Q2). Xu et al. and Trunin show that an OTOC can grow exponentially in an integrable system with an isolated saddle point, so scrambling is not identical to chaos. The bound constrains the dynamical condition's early-time growth rate and says nothing direct about the spectral condition. Fig 13 :103: *"Composition is recursive, and the level decides what is irreducible."*
3. **The specific number is an import with provenance, not a derivation.** Fig 6 :23: *"No frame escapes its own closure. The bound moves only on contact with a frame whose closure differs."* The factor `2π` arrives from MSS 2015 with a source gate (paper, date, assumptions) and a shadow test (it explains why scrambling cannot be faster without breaking unitarity, analyticity or no-cloning). Black-hole saturation is a **retrodiction**, an instance the frame describes that was arrived at independently. Fig 8 :56-57 lists Bekenstein and holography among cases the triangulation claim *"fits four cases already known and has not been tested forward"*.
4. **The bound is not the comparison bond ⋛.** It has a threshold shape (Operators table: *"a threshold relation between two quantities"*), but the operands are a derived rate and a temperature at different levels. Fig 8 :32: *"no search, however long, adds an atom"* - no amount of composing inside the six-term set produces `2π`. The strain is named, not smoothed.
5. **What would name the slot is an instrument, not an argument.** *The Hard Problem is About Source*: a reading *"cannot comment on the source"*, and the spectrometer, not a debate, resolved the color case. Here the corresponding discriminator is an OTOC protocol that separates a genuine positive Lyapunov exponent from a saddle-point artifact. Trunin's logarithmic OTOC is a candidate from the world literature. Fig 6 :34-35: *"the search for a proto-instrument is a search for one that is already there."*
