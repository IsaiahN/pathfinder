## The question restated as an effect

Two literatures answer to the word "chaos." One diagnoses quantum chaos from spectra and level statistics. The other diagnoses it from the early exponential growth of out-of-time-order correlators (OTOCs), and places a bound on that growth, `λ_L ≤ 2π k_B T / ħ`, which Einstein-gravity black holes and the SYK model saturate. The residual is not "is the bound true." It is: what is the relation between the label *quantum chaos* and the threshold called the *chaos bound*, and is black-hole saturation a fact about how the system composes or an additional law imported from the black-hole frame?

Option A asserts they are one phenomenon and the bound is a fundamental limit on chaos itself. Option B keeps the figure-derived shape, splits the two conditions, and treats the specific bound and its saturation as provenanced imports. What each leads to is written out in `01` §0 and decided per domain in `09`.

What an answer would look like, bound as an ordinary term before the run (Formula "Direction"): a statement of which quantity the bound constrains, what kind of thing that quantity is under the six-term substrate, which frame supplies the number `2π`, and which ground reading would move the result from `specified` to `grounded`. That statement is committed in `01` §3 before any verdict is delivered.

## What is deliberately not here

- No personal emails of named individuals.
- No ranking of people by how likely they are to believe the claim.
- No bulk-send plan.

Three reasons, in the figures' own words:

1. **A room does not upgrade the label.** Seat Map :283: *"A room does not upgrade the label. A room reaching a conclusion without the ground is agreement, and agreement among frames is a weighted average of influence. Adding people raises the vocabulary and does not add an anchor."*
2. **Selecting for receptiveness manufactures the disqualifying correlation.** Seat Map :163: *"High correlation is disqualifying for a verdict and efficient for a convention."* Fig 2 :42: *"Buy independence in proportion to the cost of being wrong."*
3. **Asking for trust before a check spends independence.** Fig 10 :42: *"A verifier that can reconstruct the claim cannot verify it."* Fig 10 :52: *"Bring a characterised residual, never the source."*

Legally: unsolicited bulk email to scraped addresses breaches GDPR and CAN-SPAM style rules in many jurisdictions and gets a sending domain blacklisted. Public opt-in and submission routes avoid that failure as well as the structural one.
