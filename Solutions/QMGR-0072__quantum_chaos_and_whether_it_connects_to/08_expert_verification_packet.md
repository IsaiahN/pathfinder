**What this is:** a claim in a form a chaos, condensed-matter or high-energy researcher can check. Mode, derivation and refuters are stated before the conclusion, so a reader knows what to look for and what would settle it.

**Mode: `specified` - not `grounded`.** No measurement at a black-hole horizon is claimed, and no derivation of `2π` is claimed. This is a shape and a route, not a result.

## 1. The claim, in one paragraph

Chaos is not a primitive in the Tether's six-term substrate. Fig 13 describes it as a remainder compounding through a chain that amplifies, and explicitly lists chaos among the things a composition leaves behind rather than a seventh member. A bound on chaos therefore has the shape of a threshold on the growth rate of that derived remainder. The question splits into two conditions: spectral quantum chaos (level statistics, spectral form factor, ETH) and dynamical scrambling (OTOC growth), measured by different instruments and sharing almost no atoms. The chaos bound `λ_L ≤ 2π k_B T / ħ` constrains the second condition; the number and black-hole saturation are imports with provenance from the QFT and holography frame, not outputs of the six-term set. The mapping of the bound onto the comparison bond ⋛ is refused because the operand type strains.

### C2 - physics-facing; checkable by a researcher against the two conditions

- **Statement:** spectral chaos and OTOC scrambling are two conditions of a union, not isomers and not one phenomenon. The MSS bound constrains the dynamical condition's early-time growth rate only.
- **Sources, verbatim:** Xu, Scaffidi and Cao (PRL 124, 140602, 2020): *"scrambling can simply result from the presence of unstable fixed points in phase space, even in a classically integrable model... ## 5. What would settle it

A source-discriminating instrument and a threshold measurement, not a further argument. From *The Hard Problem is About Source*: an instrument that reads a value *"cannot comment on the source"*; the spectrometer resolved the color case.

1. Does an observed exponential OTOC growth come from a positive phase-space-averaged Lyapunov exponent, or from an isolated saddle point? Trunin's logarithmic OTOC is the published candidate discriminator.
2. **Threshold Test:** measure `λ_L` inside the MSS assumption set and check `λ_L ≤ 2π k_B T / ħ`.
3. **Spectral Check:** in the same system, do the level statistics move independently of the OTOC rate when a parameter is changed? If yes, the union reading is confirmed at the ground.
4. **Standing falsifiers:** derive one of the six from the other five; show ⋛ accepts the rate and the temperature at one level; derive `2π` from the six-term set; find one instrument reading both diagnoses as the same quantity.
