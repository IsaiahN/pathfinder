## 1. A shape is a constraint, not a filler

The canonical figures README (local, read) states the limit: the framework returns *"what a missing thing would have to look like (a shape, not a name)."* Fig 9 :32: *"you get a kind of thing, never the answer itself."* Fig 13 :132-135: *"A system with a closure can answer that before it searches, and what it returns is a shape rather than a name... It cannot say which primitive is missing, because naming it would mean already holding it."*

*The Library of Babel*: *"you can cut your search short if the set of books returned is small and descriptive enough that it provides a general shape of the answer that satisfies your question."*

So a **shape** is the narrowed form of an answer you can state before you can name it. It constrains; it does not fill. For this question:

- `chaos = derived remainder of a cross-kind chain that amplifies` is a shape (from Fig 13 :31-32).
- `the chaos bound is a threshold on the growth rate of that remainder` is a shape (from Fig 13 plus Operators :50).
- `black holes are maximally chaotic because the framework says so` is a name and a verdict, neither of which the framework supplies.

## 2. How a shape is obtained

- *Library of Babel* on proximity: use the vocabulary already held that is closest to the thing, then recurse on newly found terms. *"With each new term gained, you narrow the space by definition and deepen your vocabulary."*
- Fig 6 :30: *"The description travels. Fails by being sharpened into terms only we can read. Cast it in effect terms."* Fig 9 :30-31: *"your words are borrowed too, so describe the effect rather than the cause."*
- *Decoding Tacit Knowledge*: *"When contact isn't possible, the best you can send is a plain description of what you'd expect to see, because anyone can check that against the world."* This is why stage 1 of the outreach is an effect description, not a theory summary.
- *Library of Babel* on stopping: the answer is reached when *"the finish line will also be the starting place"*, and the paradox is that by then you have *"composed the answer through the process of locating it."*
- Fig 9 :60: *"Use filters for the budget and witnesses for the verdict. Never let a filter hand you a verdict."* A journal tier, a famous author, or a large OTOC simulation is a filter for what gets tried, not a witness for what is true.

## 3. What licenses a name

Only two things, both outside the shape itself:

1. **The ground settles it.** Formula step 5: *"the ground settles it. Verified only by what pays."* Here: a measured OTOC growth rate, a measured level-spacing distribution, or (not available to us) a horizon measurement.
2. **A provenanced import from a frame whose closure differs** (Fig 6 :23, Fig 8 :50-52), with a source gate and a shadow test. Here: the MSS factor `2π`, imported with its paper, date and assumptions.

*They're Scanning Books* sharpens the second point: *"The real import is not a new term in the existing space. It is a new dimension of the space."* And: *"A minimum viable set of six terms is a human's careful guess at reality's compression axes; another sensorium's minimum set need not be those six."* Also: *"Declaring the primitives does not make them neutral. It makes the choice visible."*

The six are a visible guess, not a discovery of nature's own list. That matters here: "chaos is a derived remainder" is a claim about how this chosen set composes, not a report of what the world's own primitives are.

### 3b. Scope correction to the "shape, not a name" rule

The README's phrase governs a **missing** primitive, one not held in Γ. Gradient, Spectrum and the other four members are already in the six. So naming one as a binding from the physics frame is not inventing a seventh. What is forbidden is (a) presenting the binding as a derivation, (b) claiming it moves the wall (Fig 6: *"IMPORT adds an atom"* - a binding adds none), and (c) naming the source of a reading without an instrument that discriminates among sources. This correction was learned from the exemplar's `05` §5b and `06` Q1 and is applied here to the chaos case: saying "the remainder is the OTOC commutator growth" is a binding, not a derivation.

## 4. The source-discriminator point applies directly here

*The Hard Problem is About Source* gives the mechanism. A color instrument reads green and *"cannot comment on the source"*: one green light, or yellow plus blue, or RGB, or CMYK, or something not yet discovered. *"If several paths create the same behavior, then behavior is a projection with lossy information."* The spectrometer, not an argument, resolved it: *"The answer is unreached until a better instrument exists."*

For this question the projection is the exponential growth of an OTOC. Two sources can produce it: genuine chaos (a positive Lyapunov exponent over the phase-space average) or an isolated saddle point in an integrable system. Xu et al. and Trunin show exactly this. The reading does not determine the source. Trunin's proposed LOTOC is the analogue of the spectrometer: a better instrument, arrived at from outside the frame, that separates the two readings. This is a world-source corroboration of the figure rule, not a figure line, and it is marked as such.

*How Intelligence Actually Works*: the recursion converges toward indiscernibility, and *"indiscernibility is not identity."* An OTOC that looks exponential and comes from a saddle point is indiscernible from one that comes from chaos at the resolution of the plain OTOC. The lineage, not the reading, records the difference.

## 5. Why this question's "shape" has two parts

The union census in `01` F2 gives a shape with two limbs, and the essays explain why a single-limb answer would be worse than useless:

- *How a Society of Agents Would Work*: *"capability is a property of agent-and-habitat, never of the agent alone."* An OTOC's meaning is a property of the diagnostic and the system, not of the correlator alone.
- *They're Scanning Books*: *"General intelligence is not reliable success on the novel... It is the right kind of failure: making progress through search-and-adaptation rather than confabulation."* A package that answered "yes, quantum chaos connects to the chaos bound" without splitting the union would be confabulating a shape, not narrowing one.
- *Library of Babel*: a bundled question must be split into its conditions and searched separately. *"Perhaps your problem was a bundle or conflation of several problems masquerading as one?"*

So the canonical shape for QMGR-0072 is: **a derived remainder whose growth is bounded, split into the spectral and dynamical conditions, with the bound's number imported and its source recorded.** Anything beyond that is a name, and names need a ground.

## 6. Two consequences for outreach

**Prestige is not action.** *The Recursive Self-Improvement Trap*: *"Prestige must never be allowed to validate an action directly... Prestige can fund an action. It can give an action a budget to be tried. But it cannot settle an action. Only the ground can settle it. The Ground Maintainer must be blind to prestige."* A list of famous chaos researchers who agree would be the named collapse, not corroboration. The legitimate ask is a budget to be tried and a refuter to be checked.

**My own audit is a simulation, not a ground reading.** Same essay: *"LLMs can quickly simulate an approximate answer/output with the formula... However, the truly testable output would need to be derived from the real architecture or working the formula yourself."* So `01` is a simulated derivation, checked for internal consistency against the figures. It is **not** a test of the claim and must be labelled as such. It cannot settle anything it reports on.
