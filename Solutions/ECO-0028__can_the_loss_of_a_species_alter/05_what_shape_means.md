## 1. A shape is a constraint, not a filler

The canonical README states the limit: the framework returns `what a missing thing would have to look like (a shape, not a name)`. Fig 9 :32: `you get a kind of thing, never the answer itself`. From *The Library of Babel*: `you can cut your search short if the set of books returned is small and descriptive enough that it provides a general shape of the answer that satisfies your question`.

For this question, a **shape** is the form a settled answer must have before we can name the species, the site, or the threshold. It is:

> a loss removes an actor, the removal changes a field of contact, the change compounds through relations that carry influence, and where the compounded remainder crosses a threshold a second loss follows; the threshold is ground-decided, not figure-decided.

That is a shape. `The loss of the sea otter will cause the extinction of X by 2030` is a name plus a number, and the figures do not supply either. Fig 1 :18: `support evaluated per slot`. The answer must be per species and per relation.

## 2. How the shape is obtained

- *Library of Babel*: use the vocabulary you already have that is closest to the thing, then look for recurring new terms that overlap with it. `With each new term gained, you narrow the space by definition and deepen your vocabulary.` For this question the bootstrap vocabulary is `loss`, `contact`, `relation`, `remainder`, `trajectory`, `threshold`. Each new term narrows: `relation` replaces `importance`, `remainder` replaces `impact`, `threshold` replaces `enough`.
- *Library of Babel* on bundling: `Perhaps your problem was a bundle or conflation of several problems masquerading as one? You will need to search all of those problems individually`. This is Fig 9 :55 in different words: `A bundled problem is usually a union of overlapping conditions, not a list of separate ones.` ECO-0028 is such a bundle (ecological contact, evolutionary trajectory, managed response), so the shape must be three verdicts.
- Fig 9 :27-28: `describe it before you go looking, or any frame you pick will seem to fit`. The residual is described in effect terms before choosing coextinction models, early-warning statistics, or restoration practice. Otherwise any of them will seem to fit.
- Fig 11 :43: `The habitat is enumerated, never composed.` *Library of Babel* stops when `the finish line will also be the starting place`; for this question that means when the list of actors, conditions and relations in contact with the loss stops growing. Enumeration is the stopping rule.

## 3. What licenses a name or a number

Two things, both outside the shape:

1. **The ground settles it.** Formula step 5: `the ground settles it. Verified only by what pays. A gate is not the ground.` Fig 2 :29: `An anchor is whatever settles the question and does not change its answer because of what any frame thinks.` Here the anchor is a site, a removal experiment, a long-term time series, a measured second extinction. Not a model fit, not a citation count.
2. **A provenanced import from a frame whose closure differs**, with a source gate and a shadow test (Fig 6, Fig 8). An early-warning statistic imported from dynamical systems is allowed as a `specified` instrument if its provenance and the gap it explains are recorded. It is not a figure derivation.

Fig 9 :32 again: `you get a kind of thing, never the answer itself`. The shape licenses a check; it does not replace the check.

## 4. The two mappings to refuse here

Two near-misses are worth stating because this is an ecology question and both are tempting.

1. **The figure's `habitat` is not an ecological niche.** *The Tether Architecture*, section `Three mappings offered and declined`: `A niche is defined by an organism's relation to its environment. A habitat here is defined by contact with a residual, and the residual moves so the same actors constitute a different habitat when the question changes.` The essay adds `the resemblance is real and the definitions do not align`. So Fig 11 :43 `The habitat is enumerated, never composed` is a rule about a moving residual, not a synonym for niche theory. The ecological case is still figure-governed through contact and enumeration; the terms are not identified.
2. **`Natural selection` is an instance of the loop, not the whole framework.** *The Recursive Self-Improvement Trap*: `Natural selection is not the whole answer. It is a special case. The full answer is the loop itself.` And: `Natural selection is what the loop looks like when there is no one maintaining the ground.` So the question's phrase `evolutionary trajectories` is read as a non-invertible, ground-contacted transform (Fig 10 :70), not as a request for a population-genetics model the figures do not contain.

## 5. Why irreversibility is part of the shape

*The Recursive Self-Improvement Trap*: `You cannot recreate humanity. The exact lineage of events is lost. You would need to know every natural event and interaction with every other species, all the way back, and even then you would need the exact sequence.` Fig 10 :70: `The tree of life is what a non-invertible transform looks like from outside.` Fig 11 :66: `Isolation is not removal of the habitat. It is substitution of one habitat for another.`

So reintroducing a species is not playback of the unloss trajectory. The answer's shape must include a one-way condition: after the loss, the system is in a different habitat, and restoring the actor restores an addition to a changed field, not a return to the prior state. This is a structural claim (`general`), not a claim about any particular reintroduction project (`grounded`).

## 6. Prestige is not action, and agreement is not a verdict

*The Recursive Self-Improvement Trap*: `Prestige must never be allowed to validate an action directly.` `Prestige can fund an action. It can give an action a budget to be tried. But it cannot settle an action. Only the ground can settle it. The Ground Maintainer must be blind to prestige.` Fig 2 :35: `Alignment is triangulation, not negotiation. A mirror cannot correct you.` Fig 2 :40: `agreement about the world is worthless when the agreeing frames share an evidence pool`.

Consequence for this question: a list of famous ecologists who agree that cascades matter is a convention, not a corroboration. The outreach material asks what would refute the shape, and records that answer before any theory is shown.

## 7. My own audit is a simulation, not a ground reading

*The Recursive Self-Improvement Trap*, verbatim: `LLMs can quickly simulate an approximate answer/output with the formula found in the architecture post. It will often be better than the generic outputs that you will usually get otherwise too (intelligence is essentially arrangement). However, the truly testable output would need to be derived from the real architecture or working the formula yourself.` And: `The catch is that the output is only as good as the validator. The agent, or the tool, or the frame, or the theory, cannot validate itself. An external validator, or ground, is always needed to tether the veracity of the output to reality. Anything else is drift.`

Therefore `01_figure_grounded_audit.md`, `06_isomers_and_order.md`, `07_the_tether_answer.md` and `09_watson_answer.md` are **simulated derivations checked for internal consistency against the figures.** They are not field measurements, not meta-analyses, and not a settlement of ECO-0028. Every mode label in the folder is to be read under that constraint.
