## 1. A shape is a constraint, not a filler

The canonical figures README states the limit: the framework returns *"what a missing thing would have to look like (a shape, not a name)."* Fig 9 :32: *"you get a kind of thing, never the answer itself."* Fig 13 :134: *"Unreachable is a verdict here, not a silence"*.

From *The Library of Babel* (opened 2026-11-23): *"you can cut your search short if the set of books returned is small and descriptive enough that it provides a general shape of the answer that satisfies your question."* Also: *"you will have to use the existing vocabulary you do know that is closest to the words that you wish to describe and grasp the shape of."*

So a **shape** is the narrowed form of an answer you can state before you can name it. It constrains; it does not fill. For INS-0066 the shape is:

> a durable informal elite bargain is a checkable convention ranged against an anchor the bargainers cannot move, composed lossily from the elite scale to the order scale, and broken by an added refusal rather than by a removed term.

That is a shape. Naming the military faction, the party, the court, or the pact that fills it is a name.

## 2. How the shape was obtained

- *Library of Babel*: bootstrap vocabulary by proximity. *"With each new term gained, you are able to narrow the space by definition and deepen your vocabulary."*
- Fig 6 :30: *"The description travels. Fails by being sharpened into terms only we can read. Cast it in effect terms."* Fig 9 :30-31: *"your words are borrowed too, so describe the effect rather than the cause."* The residual was described as an effect (restraint observed where power predicts grabbing; collapse observed where cooperation predicts persistence) before any political-science frame was chosen.
- Fig 9 :55, quoted in `01`: the question was re-censused as a union of overlapping conditions after the first single-cause reading failed the strain test.
- *Library of Babel* on stopping: *"you will know that you are done because the finish line will also be the starting place, as there is nowhere else to search and nothing else that can describe the answer better."* The stop here is not completion; it is the limit of what the instruments distinguish, with the lineage recorded.

## 3. What licenses a name

Only two things, both outside the shape itself:

1. **The ground settles it.** `THE_FORMULA.md` step 5: *"the ground settles it. Verified only by what pays. A gate is not the ground."* The figures README: the framework *"stops at the limit of what the available instruments can distinguish, and records the lineage so the stop is honest."*
2. **A provenanced import from a frame whose closure differs** (Fig 6 :23, Fig 8 :50-52), with a source gate and a shadow test.

For INS-0066, a claim such as "the party arbitration committee is the anchor" is a binding from the political-science frame onto a role characterized in effect terms. It is allowed, but it is provenance, not derivation, and it moves no wall: Fig 6 :21, *"MINT composes inside the closure and can never add an atom."*

## 4. Why the room answer is the forbidden error

Fig 7 :35: *"A room's agreement is variation, not a verdict. Selection between rooms is where the ground votes."* A claim that "the elites all agree, therefore order is stable" reads an internal room agreement as a verdict. Fig 2 :35: *"Alignment is triangulation, not negotiation. A mirror cannot correct you."*

*The Recursive Self-Improvement Trap* gives the mechanism: *"You may all agree and be wrong."* And: *"The catch is that the output is only as good as the validator. The agent, or the tool, or the frame, or the theory, cannot validate itself. An external validator, or ground, is always needed to tether the veracity of the output to reality. Anything else is drift."*

So the shape is not a consensus description. It is a set of conditions that would have to hold for an outside ground to vote.

## 5. Q for this question: does "shape, not a name" forbid naming any institution? ANSWERED with a correction

**No.** The canonical phrase governs a **missing** primitive, one not held in Γ. An institution already present in the frame is not missing. What remains forbidden is claiming that the framework derives which institution fills the role, or claiming that binding one moves the wall (Fig 6 :21).

**Correction to my own reading.** In §1 I first wrote that a name is always outside what the framework can do. That overstates it. The corrected form is: the framework returns a shape for a missing primitive; it can bind a role onto an already-held atom, but the binding needs provenance and a shadow test and adds no atom. Both readings are recorded here so neither is quietly lost.

Applied here: "the anchor must not update on the bargainers" is a shape. "The constitutional court is the anchor" is a binding that would have to be shown to be non-updating in the relevant sense. The second is not delivered by the figures.

## 6. Prestige is not action

From *The Recursive Self-Improvement Trap* (opened 2026-11-23):

> *"Prestige can fund an action. It can give an action a budget to be tried. But it cannot settle an action. Only the ground can settle it. The Ground Maintainer must be blind to prestige."*

A famous theorist's agreement with this derivation is the named collapse, not corroboration. The legitimate ask is the opposite: give the claim a budget to be tried, let an instrument or a documented case settle it, and ask for a refutation. Fig 2 :42: *"Buy independence in proportion to the cost of being wrong."*
