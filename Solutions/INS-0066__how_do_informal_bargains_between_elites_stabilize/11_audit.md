## Round trip gap `R_T`

The Symbols table defines `R_T` as `gap(x, (T_E ∘ T_A)(x))`, measuring what the coarser description cannot hold. Fig 4 :30 requires it to be measured, not assumed. I ran the round trip on the condensation the folder actually ships.

1. **Sent up (x):** the figure lines, chiefly Fig 10 :46-48 (convention status), Fig 2 :33-35 (anchor), Fig 4 :30 (round trip), Fig 5 :55 (refusal).
2. **Abstraction (T_A):** the shorthand in `07` and `09`: checkable convention, independent anchor, lossy transform, refusal adds and removes standing.
3. **Concretisation (T_E):** a concrete claim about a real bargain, for example "the coalition's arbitration committee is the anchor and the deal held until a general defected."

**`R_T` is nonzero.** Three things the shorthand holds do not survive the trip back down:

- the requirement that the anchor be independent rather than merely shared, which a named committee may fail;
- the prohibition on naming the specific bargain, which the concrete claim breaches unless the name is provenanced as a binding;
- the operand type of a negation applied to a group belief rather than a single term, which the figures leave open.

One thing survives cleanly: a convention that decides an outcome has stopped being one, and a refusal removes standing rather than content. That is the residue worth sending. `R_T` was **not** measured numerically, because no ground reading exists. It is recorded qualitatively as the enumerated set of dropped constraints. Treating the formula as identical to the figures would set `R_T` to zero and commit the dead-reckoning error Fig 4 :30 names.
