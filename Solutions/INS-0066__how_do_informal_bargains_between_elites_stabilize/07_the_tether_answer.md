# The Tether's answer: informal elite bargains

**Mode: `specified` on the shape, `general` on the structural rules, not `grounded`.** A simulated derivation checked against the figures (`05_what_shape_means.md` §7), not a ground reading. No political settlement or dataset was measured.

## 1. The residual it answers

We observe restraint where a power frame predicts grabbing, and collapse where a cooperation frame predicts persistence. These are two conditions of one union compound, not one hard question (Fig 9 :55: *"A bundled problem is usually a union of overlapping conditions, not a list of separate ones."*). The residual is indexed by elite, not aggregated into "the society is stable" (Fig 1 :19: *"The residual is indexed by object slot, not aggregated."*).

## 2. The shape, per domain (verdicts kept separate)

**D1, the bargain as convention. Verdict `specified`, triangulated.** An informal bargain is a convention: *"A convention makes no claim about the world; a verdict and an atom both do"* (Fig 10 :46), and *"a convention that decides an outcome has stopped being one"* (Fig 10 :47). It stabilizes while it is checkable and does not itself distribute power. It breaks down when it becomes the distributor, or when it becomes *"a convention nothing can check"* (Fig 10 :48). Fig 10 :18: *"a declared meaning is not a grounded one"*, then *"→ install what can be violated"* (Fig 10 :19).

**D2, the ground. Verdict `specified`, triangulated.** Stability is triangulation, not negotiation: *"Alignment is triangulation, not negotiation. A mirror cannot correct you."* (Fig 2 :35). The anchor is *"whatever settles the question and does not change its answer because of what any frame thinks"* (Fig 2 :29), and *"The anchor must not update."* (Fig 2 :33). With no anchor, *"they converge, and what they converge on is whoever had the most influence"* (Fig 2 :14-15), and the drift is coherent so nobody notices (Fig 2 :34). Where the parties share one evidence pool, *"the agreement is the pool talking"* (Fig 2 :20), which is one witness, not two. What decays is the channel, not the ground: *"the ground does not decay, the channel does"* (Fig 10 :24).

**D3, the bargain to order transform. Verdict `specified` on the gap, `general` on the rule, triangulated with Fig 12.** The transform is lossy and must be bracketed: *"So take the round trip and measure the gap."* (Fig 4 :30). A settled bargain can become an atom at the next level, *"irreducible is a fact about a level and never about the world"* (Fig 12 :104), but that is a recursion, not a new primitive. Fig 4 :22: *"Only methods cross up. Only head starts come down."*

**D4, verification. Verdict `general`, triangulated.** *"A room's agreement is variation, not a verdict. Selection between rooms is where the ground votes."* (Fig 7 :35). This simulated second seat shares the primary's evidence pool, so its agreement is not corroboration (Fig 2 :40). *"Use filters for the budget and witnesses for the verdict."* (Fig 9 :60).

**Breakdown, spanning D1 and D2. Verdict `specified`, triangulated.** A refused term is not deleted; its standing is removed and the reach falls because something was added. Fig 5 :55: *"What it removes is not a term but a term's standing to be composed over. Nothing leaves the library. What falls is the reach."* Operators table :58: *"a refutation is a negation, which is why a vocabulary without it can only accumulate."*

**Which specific bargain, country, or institution. Verdict `gap`.** Fig 9 :32: *"you get a kind of thing, never the answer itself"*; Fig 13 :134: *"Unreachable is a verdict here, not a silence"*. The figures return a shape, not a name.

These verdicts are not averaged. A high score on checkability does not compensate for a moving anchor; the conditions overlap as a union (Fig 9 :56).

## 3. What it explicitly does not say

- Not that any named institution is the anchor. That is a binding needing provenance, not a derivation.
- Not that more elites agreeing means more stability. Agreement inside a room is variation, not a verdict (Fig 7 :35).
- Not that a bargain composes losslessly into public order. `R_T` is unmeasured and is not zero by assumption.
- Not a single-cause theory. One lever leaves the intersections standing (Fig 9 :56).
- Not `grounded`. No measurement settles any of it.
- Not a theory of everything. There is no last room (Fig 7 :6).

## 4. What would settle it - the ground

An instrument or a documented case that discriminates the conditions, not a further argument:

1. **Checkability test:** is there a visible violation signal that the parties themselves can audit? Ostrom's monitoring and graduated sanctions design principles are a world-side instance, not a figure line.
2. **Anchor test:** is there something that settles the bargain and does not change its answer when the bargainers push on it? A balance of power measured independently of elite statements would qualify; their own consensus about it would not.
3. **Round-trip test:** describe the bargain at the elite scale, compose it up to the population order, bring it back down, and measure `R_T` as the set of dropped constraints.
4. **Refusal test:** after a defection, did the record gain a refusing term, and did the arrangements built on the old belief lose standing? If the order simply re-explained itself, the refusal claim fails.

## 5. Standing falsifiers

The pre-registered set in `01_figure_grounded_audit.md` §4 fires if: a bargain both decides outcomes and stays checkably stable ungrounded; a no-anchor bargain does not drift to the strongest; a stable bargain has no monitoring at all; `R_T` is zero; one lever clears all four conditions at once; or a durable breakdown rebuilds without any negation. Any one withdraws the affected claim.

## 6. The honest stop

**Unreached, not unreachable.** The four conditions are stateable in effect terms, and the instruments that would close them are named. What is not available is a ground reading of a real bargain. Fig 8 :39: *"So a search that finds nothing is never a proof of absence."* Fig 4 :30 requires the round trip to be measured rather than assumed. The stop is recorded with its lineage so a later reader can locate the error.

## 7. One-paragraph version for outreach

> The Tether does not name the bargain that keeps a country orderly, and it does not claim a single cause. It says that an informal elite bargain is a convention, and a convention that decides outcomes has stopped being one; so it stabilizes order only while it is checkable and does not itself distribute power. Stability is triangulation, not negotiation: each elite must range against an anchor it cannot move by agreeing, or the group simply converges on whoever is strongest and calls the drift consensus. Agreement among elites is variation inside a room, not a verdict from the ground. Breakdown is an addition, not a subtraction: a defection adds a refusal, and every arrangement built on the belief that the others would restrain themselves loses standing at once. The transform from an elite bargain to a population order is lossy, and its round-trip gap has to be measured. What the framework offers is that shape, four refuters written in advance, and the instruments that would close it. The name of the particular bargain stays open until the ground settles it.
