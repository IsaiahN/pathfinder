# Expert verification packet: the Tether on informal elite bargains

**What this is:** a claim in a form a scholar of elite bargains, political economy, or institutional change can check. Mode, derivation and refuters are stated before the conclusion.

**Mode: `specified` on the shape, `general` on structural rules, not `grounded`.** No real political settlement was measured. This is a simulated derivation checked for internal consistency against the figures.

## 1. The claim, in one paragraph

An informal elite bargain is a convention, not a verdict. It stabilizes political order while it is checkable and while it does not itself decide who gets what. The moment it becomes the mechanism distributing power it has stopped being a convention and must be grounded; if it cannot be checked it is simply the authored constant of whoever holds the seat. Stability requires an anchor the bargainers cannot move by agreeing among themselves, because without one the group converges on whoever had the most influence and calls the drift consensus. Agreement inside the elite room is variation, not a verdict from the ground. Breakdown is an addition to the record, not a subtraction: a defection adds a refusal, and the standing of every arrangement composed over the old belief falls without anything being deleted. The transform from the elite scale to the population order is lossy, and its round-trip gap `R_T` has to be measured. The framework returns this shape, not the name of a particular bargain.

## 5. What would settle it

A ground reading, not agreement. The discriminating instruments are:

1. **Checkability:** is there a visible violation signal the parties themselves audit? Ostrom's monitoring and graduated sanctions design principles are a world-side instance.
2. **Anchor independence:** is there something that settles the bargain and does not change when the bargainers push on it? Measured balance of power qualifies; their own consensus about it does not.
3. **Round trip:** describe the bargain at the elite scale, compose up to public order, bring it back down, and enumerate `R_T`.
4. **Refusal:** after a defection, did the record gain a refusing term and did the old arrangements lose standing?
