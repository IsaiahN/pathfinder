## 0. The residual in effect terms

The observation to be explained has two halves that do not reduce to one another:

1. Actors who each hold the power to take more instead repeatedly restrain themselves and produce an orderly, predictable political arrangement.
2. The same arrangement sometimes collapses, quickly, into defection, violence, or institutional breakdown.

State it as a prediction gap. `Γ(b,a)` is what a frame predicts given what it believes about the others and what it does; `o'` is what actually happens; `R = |Γ(b,a) − o'|`. Two frames fail here in opposite directions. A frame that predicts *power will be exercised without restraint* fails on observation 1. A frame that predicts *cooperation once established persists* fails on observation 2. The residual is the gap between them.

`R` is a **slice, never a global number**. Symbols table :38-40: *"The gap between prediction and observation. The central quantity: everything the system does is driven by it."* Fig 1 :19: *"The residual is indexed by object slot, not aggregated."* Each elite is a slot. A global reading of "the order is stable" with one live slot (one elite about to defect) is a legal state, and aggregating across slots throws away the only signal that says where to look (Fig 1 :22). So the unit of analysis is **each elite's standing relative to the bargain**, not "society".

**Bound the sought-for shape as an ordinary term now, before the run.** The answer being searched for is: *the set of conditions that make a checkable convention stable against defection, the conditions that remove its standing, and an instrument that discriminates them*. Binding this lets it accrue a residual like any other term. Fig 5 :65-66: *"The remedy is a discipline rather than a branch: make the sought-for shape an ORDINARY TERM. Write down what an answer would look like, bind it, and let it accrue a residual like anything else."*

### R by arity, symmetry, scale

- **Arity:** k-ary, k at least 2. The unit is the relation among elites, not any one elite. A bargain is a bond, and *"A set of ingredients is not one. The bond is the rest of the information"* (Fig 12 :5).
- **Symmetry:** the *form* is symmetric (mutual restraint, a convention), the *terms* are asymmetric (unequal power, unequal payoffs). This mismatch is where the strain lives and is not smoothed over.
- **Scale:** three scales. Individual elite (micro), the bargain (meso), political order (macro). This is a membrane question, not a single-level one. Fig 4 :30: *"So take the round trip and measure the gap. Without it you are navigating by dead reckoning: confident about a position nobody checked."*

## 3. Findings

### F1 - A convention that decides an outcome has stopped being one (decisive, `general`)

Fig 10 :45-48:

> **"It does author conventions, because nothing else can see across a seam."**
> **"A convention makes no claim about the world; a verdict and an atom both do."**
> **"a convention that decides an outcome has stopped being one"**
> **"a convention nothing can check is a constant the seat authored, carrying the seat's authority"**

An informal elite bargain is a convention: an unwritten coordination that makes no claim about the world, only an arrangement among the parties. It stabilizes order while it stays a convention, that is, while it is checkable and does not itself distribute power. The moment it becomes the mechanism that actually decides who gets what, it has become a claim about the world and must be grounded. If it cannot be checked, it is simply the authored constant of whoever holds the seat.

This is the single most useful line in the whole derivation because it explains both halves of the question with one move. Stabilization is the maintenance of a checkable convention. Breakdown is the loss of that status.

**Strain noticed and re-censused.** The observation "elite bargains allocate resources" at first looks like a contradiction of *"A convention makes no claim about the world"* (Fig 10 :46). Re-census: Fig 10 :46 describes what a convention *is*; Fig 10 :47 describes what it *becomes* when it decides an outcome. Different states of the same object, not contradictory rules. No strain after re-census. This correction is preserved here rather than silently erased.

**Refuter:** a durable informal elite bargain found in which the agreement itself decides outcomes and yet remains checkable and stable with no grounding. If one exists, F1 fails.

### F2 - Without an anchor, the bargain converges on whoever had the most influence (`specified`, triangulated)

Fig 2 :13-15 and :24-25:

> **"no anchor; each updates on the others"**
> **"they converge, and what they converge on is whoever had the most influence"**
> **"the anchor"**
> **"pinned: it does not update on A or B"**

Fig 2 :33-35:

> **"The anchor must not update."**
> **"If it does, panel 3 turns into panel 1: the anchor becomes another frame, everything drifts together, and the drift is coherent, which is why nobody notices."**
> **"Alignment is triangulation, not negotiation. A mirror cannot correct you."**

A bargain among elites that is pure mutual updating is Figure 2's first collapse. Each elite adjusts to the others, the group converges on whoever has the most influence, and the result looks like consensus while being domination renamed. The drift is coherent, so nobody notices. Stability of this kind is not triangulation. Triangulation requires each elite to range separately against an anchor it cannot move. In effect terms the anchor is the actual material balance of power and outside options, not anyone's belief about it.

The second collapse also bites. Fig 2 :19-21: *"they never talk, and still agree"* and *"so the agreement is the pool talking, not two frames confirming each other."* Elites who share class, education, and interests can agree without any independent check. That agreement is the shared evidence pool talking, so it carries no more information than one witness.

**Refuter:** a durable bargain with no anchor independent of the bargainers that does not drift toward the strongest party, plus an instrument showing this is not just an unmeasured shared pool. If found, F2 fails.

### F3 - Elite agreement is variation, not a verdict (`general`, triangulated)

Fig 7 :13: *"agreement compounds, evidence does not"*. Fig 7 :35:

> **"A room's agreement is variation, not a verdict. Selection between rooms is where the ground votes."**

Fig 7 :36: *"A chain of mirrors is one long room. The next room must differ from the last, and match the residual it surfaced."*

A political order that rests only on the elites continuing to agree is an internal room agreement. Ten more elites agreeing is a bigger room, not a ground. The ground votes only when the arrangement is selected against something that can refuse it, such as an actual defection, an election, a court, or a material shock. This is why "all the elites are on board" is a weak stability claim. It reports variation inside a room.

Fig 2 :42: *"Buy independence in proportion to the cost of being wrong."* The cost of being wrong about political order is high, so independent checks are worth buying. Agreement among insiders is the cheapest and least informative of them.

**Refuter:** a case where elite agreement alone, with no ground pressure of any kind, settled a dispute durably across a generation. If documented, the room claim narrows to the cases where a ground was present but unrecognized.

### F4 - Checkability is the load-bearing link (`specified`, triangulated)

Fig 10 :18-19:

> **"a declared meaning is not a grounded one"**
> **"→ install what can be violated"**

Fig 10 :24-28:

> **"the ground does not decay, the channel does"**
> **"stale percepts, dropped transients, saturated metrics, pooled readings"**
> **"the output is not a verdict, it is"**
> **"you have lost touch"**

Fig 3 :27: *"A link with no instrument is not a link that works. It is a link nobody has looked at."*

An informal bargain is stable in proportion to how visibly a breach can be registered. What decays is not the material balance of power (the ground) but the channel through which the elites read it: stale information about each other's strength, saturated signals, pooled readings that blur one elite's move into the group's average. When the channel fails, the output is not a verdict about stability, it is a report that the seat has lost touch.

This is the dependency link that attracts the least attention. Fig 3 :25-26: perception and planning have obvious failures and obvious tests; the middle link, having terms for what a situation affords and saying what counts as progress, has neither. In elite bargains the middle link is "what would count as a breach, and who would see it". That is where the instrument is missing.

**Refuter:** a stable bargain with no monitoring of any kind and no violation signal, where a breach would be invisible to the other parties and yet the arrangement endures across repeated opportunities to defect. If found, F4 fails.

### F5 - The bargain to order transform is lossy, and `R_T` must be measured (`specified`, triangulated with Fig 12)

Fig 4 :22 and :27-30:

> **"Only methods cross up. Only head starts come down."**
> **"The transform between scales loses information, and that is the point."**
> **"Going up throws away detail; that is what makes the result reapplicable."**
> **"So take the round trip and measure the gap."**

Fig 4 :34-35: *"A seed carries no small tree: it carries what will grow one, and the conditions it lands in decide what it becomes."*

A bargain that holds among five elites does not carry the population order inside it. What crosses up is a method (a way of coordinating restraint), and what crosses down is a head start (a cheaper search for the next arrangement), never a recording of one success. The round-trip gap `R_T` measures what the elite-scale description cannot hold about the population-scale order. Without measuring it, any claim that "the bargain stabilized the country" is dead reckoning.

Fig 12 :104 supplies the low-overlap second witness: *"A settled molecule becomes an atom for whatever composes over it. So irreducible is a fact about a level and never about the world."* A bargain that has settled against the ground can serve as an atom at the next level. That is a recursion, not an import of a new primitive.

**Refuter:** a demonstration that the elite-scale description of a bargain reproduces the population-scale order losslessly, so `R_T` is zero. If shown, the membrane claim is wrong for this case.

### F6 - The question is a union, not one hard problem (`general`, triangulated)

Fig 9 :55-58:

> **"A bundled problem is usually a union of overlapping conditions, not a list of separate ones."**
> **"A single lever clears the region where only one condition holds and leaves the intersections standing, because the second condition still binds."**
> **"What remains is smaller and it is also categorically different: a case in two conditions is not a harder case of either."**

The four conditions in the census overlap. Checkability without an independent anchor gives coherent drift. An anchor without checkability gives a convention nobody can verify. Either without the cross-scale transform gives elite calm and population disorder. A reform that only adds transparency clears the first condition and leaves the others binding, which is why single-cause reform stories fail. The residue after one lever is not a smaller version of the original problem; it is a specific compound.

Fig 9 :60: *"Use filters for the budget and witnesses for the verdict. Never let a filter hand you a verdict."* Any score for how stable a bargain looks is a budget filter, not a witness.

**Refuter:** a single intervention observed to clear all four conditions at once across several cases, with each condition separately measured. If found, the union reading collapses to a single lever.

### F7 - Breakdown is a refusal, and the reach falls because something was added (`specified`, triangulated with the Operators and `THE_FORMULA`)

`THE_FORMULA.md` step 2 names a fifth bin that the four missing-kind bins cannot sort: *"a term held is false; what is owed is a refusal, not an explanation"*. Fig 5 :53: *"refuted: a term we hold is wrong; a term is owed that says so"*. Fig 5 :54: *"A term that refuses another pays the same bargain."* Fig 5 :55: *"What it removes is not a term but a term's standing to be composed over. Nothing leaves the library. What falls is the reach."*

Operators table :58: *"a refutation is a negation, which is why a vocabulary without it can only accumulate."*

Applied here: the held term is "the other elites will restrain themselves". When an elite acts on the contrary, the correct move is not to re-explain the bargain but to mint a refusing term. Nothing is deleted from the record. What falls is the standing of every arrangement composed over the refused belief. That is why breakdown looks abrupt after a long calm: the library is unchanged, the reach is not.

`THE_FORMULA.md` step 3: *"NOTHING IS EVER DELETED, and the reach falls because something was ADDED."* A defection is an addition to the record, not a subtraction.

**Refuter:** a documented durable breakdown in which no held term was refused, only re-weighted or re-explained, and the order rebuilt without adding a refusing term. If common, the refusal account is wrong about this domain.

### F8 - Arrangement is the substance: same elites, different bond, different order (`general`, single-source for the figure definition)

Fig 12 :94: *"The arrangement is the substance."* Fig 12 :117: *"isomer. Same atoms, different bond, different substance. The difference is invisible in the formula and decisive in the behaviour."* Fig 12 :119: *"catalyst. A part that survives its own use."*

Two elite sets with the same members and the same payoffs can stand under different bonds. A symmetric bond of mutual restraint plus monitoring (Fig 12 :55-59, conjunction) and a hierarchical patron-client bond (Fig 12 :80-83, subtraction) are not the same substance even though the ingredient list is identical. A durable institution is Fig 12's catalyst: a part that survives its own use, spent and regenerating, and *"nothing records which a term is"* (Fig 12 :119). A personalist deal is the spent part: it works once and is gone.

Fig 12 :5: *"A set of ingredients is not one. The bond is the rest of the information, and it is the half nothing was recording."* Counting elites and their assets records half the bargain.

**Status: single-source.** The isomer and catalyst definitions are figure-internal. The political-science analogue (clientelism as hierarchy and iteration) is secondary and does not decide the figure reading.

**Refuter:** show that the bond between elites is fully recoverable from the actors and payoffs alone, so the arrangement carries no extra information. That breaks Fig 12 :5 for this domain.

### F9 - No figure line names which bargain stabilizes which order (`general`, gap at the level of names)

Fig 9 :32: *"you get a kind of thing, never the answer itself."* Fig 13 :134: *"Unreachable is a verdict here, not a silence"*. Fig 8 :39: *"So a search that finds nothing is never a proof of absence."*

The figures license a shape (checkable convention, independent anchor, lossy cross-scale composition, refusal before mint). They do not name a country, a pact, a date, or an institution. That is not a failure to try harder; it is a barrier. A frame cannot name a primitive it does not hold, and naming a particular bargain would mean already holding it.

The essays sharpen the same limit. *The Library of Babel* (read 2026-11-23): *"you can cut your search short if the set of books returned is small and descriptive enough that it provides a general shape of the answer that satisfies your question."* The canonical figures README: *"what a missing thing would have to look like (a shape, not a name)."*

**Verdict at this level: gap.** Honest and recorded, not silence.

**Refuter:** a figure-derived derivation that names a specific elite bargain and whose name follows from the figures rather than from an import with a source gate and shadow test. If produced, F9 is withdrawn.

## 4. Pre-registered refuter

Written before the conclusion, per the standing rule that a falsifier written before the run is a design instrument. The shape claim fails if any of the following holds:

1. A durable informal elite bargain is found in which the agreement itself decides outcomes and yet remains checkable and stable with no grounding (breaks F1).
2. A durable bargain is found with no anchor independent of the bargainers that does not drift toward the strongest party, with an instrument showing the drift is absent rather than unmeasured (breaks F2).
3. A stable bargain is found across repeated defection opportunities with no monitoring and no violation signal (breaks F4).
4. `R_T` from the elite-scale description to the population-scale order is shown to be zero for a case where the two differ (breaks F5).
5. A single intervention is observed to clear all four conditions at once across several cases, each separately measured (breaks F6).
6. Breakdown is documented in which no held term was refused, the order rebuilt without a negation, and the rebuild was durable (breaks F7).

If any fires, withdraw the affected claim, not the whole derivation. Until one is run, no part of this may be labelled `grounded`.
