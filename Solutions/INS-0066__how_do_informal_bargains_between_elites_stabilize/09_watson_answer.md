## QUESTION

Decide, in effect terms, what the Tether says about how informal bargains between elites stabilize political order and what makes them break down. Option A: treat the bargain as a self-enforcing agreement that directly produces order, and treat breakdown as a failure of enforcement. Option B: treat the bargain as a checkable convention ranged against an anchor the bargainers cannot move, composed lossily to the order scale, and treat breakdown as the addition of a refusal that removes standing. Option A leads to a verdict stated without a ground, a single-cause story, and a room agreement mistaken for a ground vote. Option B leads to a shape with separate domain verdicts, four refuters written in advance, and named instruments that would close it.

## DOMAIN CENSUS

R by arity, symmetry and scale, then one line per domain with actors, currency and anchor. A domain with no nameable anchor is not a domain yet.

- **R (the residual).** Restraint is observed where a power frame predicts grabbing, and collapse is observed where a cooperation frame predicts persistence. Arity: k-ary, k at least 2, the unit being the relation among elites. Symmetry: the form is symmetric, a convention, while the terms are asymmetric, unequal power and payoffs. Scale: individual elite, bargain, political order. It is a union of overlapping conditions, not one hard question (Fig 9, a case in two conditions is not a harder case of either). The residual is indexed by elite, not aggregated (Fig 1).
- **D1, the bargain as convention.** Actors: elites and their breaches. Currency: agreements, expectations, checkability. Anchor: yes, a visible violation. Question type: convention versus claim about the world.
- **D2, the ground: material balance of power.** Actors: elites as power holders and their outside options. Currency: material payoffs, credible threats, capacity to impose. Anchor: yes, the actual distribution of power, which does not change because they agreed. Question type: triangulation versus coherent drift.
- **D3, the bargain to order transform.** Actors: the elite bargain, the population order, other institutions. Currency: methods up, head starts down, the round-trip gap. Anchor: yes, the gap itself. Question type: lossy composition across a membrane.
- **D4, verification of this claim.** Actors: this simulated derivation and outside readers. Currency: agreement and residual. Anchor: yes, the ground, never agreement. Question type: witness versus filter. In this run all seats draw on one evidence pool, so independence is zero.

Order by dependency: perceive a breach, have terms for what it affords, say what counts as progress, plan under incomplete information, then learn and carry (Fig 3). Refuse before mint (`THE_FORMULA.md` step 2). Describe the effect before choosing a frame (Fig 9).

## VERDICT (from the figures)

The shape survives; the name does not. An informal elite bargain stabilizes order while it remains a checkable convention ranged against an anchor the bargainers cannot move, and while it does not itself distribute power. It breaks down when it decides an outcome, when it becomes uncheckable, when the anchor moves, or when a defection adds a refusal that strips the standing of every arrangement built on the old belief. The elite to order transform is lossy and its round trip is unmeasured. Which bargain, country or institution fills the shape is unreachable with the figures as they stand, and that is recorded as `gap`, not as silence. This is a `specified`, ungrounded result. Domain verdicts are kept separate and never averaged: D1 triangulated, D2 triangulated, D3 triangulated, D4 triangulated, names gap.

- **cost if wrong:** bounded and locatable. Mode is stated, refuters are pre-registered, and the domain verdicts are separate, so an error is findable rather than invisible. The real cost is spending the independence of outside readers by sending a recording instead of a generator, which Fig 2 :42 prices as *"Buy independence in proportion to the cost of being wrong."* That cost is avoidable by leading with the shape claim and its refuter.
- **undoable:** yes. The four conditions and the name claim can be revised independently; `07_the_tether_answer.md` and `08_expert_verification_packet.md` already withhold the name. No figure primitive has to be withdrawn, and a superseding packet can replace the sent one.
- **reversed by:** a ground reading, not agreement. An instrument showing a visible violation signal that the parties audit, a balance-of-power measure independent of elite statements, a measured round-trip gap, or a documented refusal after a defection would move the verdict. A derivation naming a specific bargain from the figures with a source gate and shadow test would close the gap. Prestige or consensus is not such an instrument.
