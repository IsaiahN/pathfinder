## 1. A shape is a constraint, not a filler

The figures README states the limit: the framework returns "what a missing thing would have to look like (a shape, not a name)". Fig 9 :32: "you get a kind of thing, never the answer itself". Fig 13 :135: "It cannot say which primitive is missing, because naming it would mean already holding it."

From *The Library of Babel* (opened): "You can cut your search short if the set of books returned is small and descriptive enough that it provides a general shape of the answer that satisfies your question."

For this question, a **shape** is the narrowed form of an explanation you can state before you can name its mechanism. It constrains any answer: the explanation must be a relation rather than an actor kind, must include the habitat, must contain a cycle, must register per slot, and must be settled by a ground. It does not fill the slot with kin selection, reciprocity, group selection, or a parameter value. `cooperation = relation(agent, habitat, cycle)` is a shape. `cooperation = indirect reciprocity with q > c/b` is a name.

This matters because the question as posed invites a name. "Can evolutionary dynamics explain..." reads like a yes/no about a specific explanatory framework. The framework's honest output is the set of constraints any yes would have to satisfy, plus the conditions under which the frame's closure can produce one.

## 2. How a shape is obtained

- *Library of Babel* (opened): use the vocabulary already held that is closest to the thing, then look for recurring new terms that overlap or correlate with it. "With each new term gained, you are able to narrow the space by definition and deepen your vocabulary." For this question the starting vocabulary is the one-shot payoff residue (defection pays now); the recurring correlated terms in the literature are repeated interaction, memory, reputation, graph topology, and reciprocity.
- Fig 6 :30: "The description travels. Fails by being sharpened into terms only we can read. Cast it in effect terms." Fig 9 :30-31: "your words are borrowed too, so describe the effect rather than the cause." Describe the residual as "a per-slot prediction of defection misses stable cooperation", not as "the prisoner's dilemma fails", because "prisoner's dilemma" is the borrowed cause-word of one frame.
- *Decoding Tacit Knowledge* (carried): "When contact isn't possible, the best you can send is a plain description of what you'd expect to see, because anyone can check that against the world." Translated here: describe what would be observed if the cycle claim were true, for example, cooperation tracking past donations rather than relatedness once association is held constant. Carter and Wilkinson (2013) did exactly this (Fig 2 :45, "Frames range against the ground. They do not negotiate with it.").
- *Library of Babel* on stopping: "the finish line will also be the starting place", and by then "you have already composed the answer through the process of locating it." For this question the stop is not a mechanism name; it is a stable set of constraints with the mechanism still open.
- *Library of Babel* on bundles: "Perhaps your problem was a bundle or conflation of several problems masquerading as one?" That is the explicit warrant for the union reading in `01` section 1.3 and Fig 9 :54-58.

## 3. What licenses a name (and the correction to the overstatement)

Only two things, both outside the shape itself:

1. **The ground settles it.** `THE_FORMULA.md` step 5: "the ground settles it. Verified only by what pays." The README: the framework "stops at the limit of what the available instruments can distinguish, and records the lineage so the stop is honest." For cooperation, that means a discriminating measurement, not another model that reproduces cooperation.
2. **A provenanced import from a frame whose closure differs** (Fig 6 :23, Fig 8 :50-52), with a source gate and a shadow test. Naming kin selection, for example, is an import from the evolutionary-biology frame into the figure vocabulary, and must be recorded as such.

### Correction to my own first reading

I initially wrote that the "shape, not a name" rule forbids naming any mechanism. That overstates it. The README's phrase is "what a **missing** thing would have to look like (a shape, not a name)." The subject is a primitive not held in the library. Network is already one of the six structures (Fig 13 :24, "that entities relate, and influence travels"), and comparison and sequence are already bonds (Fig 12 :55-89). So naming network structure or a cycle is **not** inventing a missing primitive; it is a binding onto atoms and bonds already held. What remains forbidden is:

- inventing a seventh substrate kind to carry cooperation;
- claiming that such a binding moves the wall when it adds no atom (Fig 6 :22, "NOVEL means novel relative to atoms, not to the world");
- naming the mechanism that occupies the slot as though the figures selected it.

The correction is kept visible here rather than silently applied, matching the exemplar's own §5b practice. Triangulated (README + Fig 13 + Fig 6).

## 4. Why the cooperation literature's plurality is a shape result, not a failure

*The Hard Problem is About Source* (opened) gives the mechanism. Metamerism: an instrument reads green and "cannot comment on the source." Several paths create the same reading, so "If several paths create the same behavior, then behavior is a projection with lossy information." It was the spectrometer, not an argument, that resolved it: "An instrument that can't distinguish these cases demonstrates its own inadequacy."

The cooperation literature is in exactly that position. Kin selection, direct reciprocity, indirect reciprocity, network reciprocity, group selection, punishment, and conformist learning all produce a rising cooperation reading. Observing cooperation rising cannot comment on the source. The right response is to ask for a discriminator (as Carter and Wilkinson 2013 did by holding association constant and comparing reciprocity with relatedness), not to choose a mechanism by fit.

*The Recursive Self-Improvement Trap* (opened): "the output is only as good as the validator. The agent, or the tool, or the frame, or the theory, cannot validate itself." A model of cooperation cannot certify that it has explained cooperation; the ground must pay.

*They're Scanning Books When They Should* (carried) sharpens the import point: "The real import is not a new term in the existing space. It is a new dimension of the space." And: "Declaring the primitives does not make them neutral. It makes the choice visible." The six are a visible guess, not nature's own list; naming one as *the* explanation converts a visible choice into an invisible one.

## 5. Answers to the two questions this file owes

**What licenses a particular mechanism as the explanation of cooperation?** Not the framework. The framework returns the shape: relation, habitat, cycle, per-slot, ground-settled. A particular mechanism is licensed only by the ground settling a discriminating test, or by a provenanced import with a shadow test. Until one of those, the name is the searcher's contribution and must be recorded as such.

**Does the derivation commit a forbidden error?** The first-pass derivation did, in two ways, both now corrected and preserved in `01` section 7: it treated the one-shot defection premise as a false term owed a refusal (it is a correct within-domain prediction), and it asserted an import unconditionally (the graph frame already holds the atoms). Neither error is a naming error; both are domain errors, and re-census dissolved them. The remaining gap, the mechanism slot, is reported as `gap`, which is allowed.

## 6. Two consequences for outreach

**Prestige is not action.** From *The Recursive Self-Improvement Trap* (opened): "Prestige must never be allowed to validate an action directly... Someone let prestige stand in for proof... Prestige can fund an action. It can give an action a budget to be tried. But it cannot settle an action. Only the ground can settle it. The Ground Maintainer must be blind to prestige." A list of famous cooperation researchers assembled to agree with the shape claim is the named collapse, not corroboration. The legitimate ask is for a refuter and a discriminator.

**This audit is a simulation, not a ground reading.** Same essay: "LLMs can quickly simulate an approximate answer/output with the formula found in the architecture post. It will often be better than the generic outputs that you will usually get otherwise too. However, the truly testable output would need to be derived from the real architecture or working the formula yourself." So `01` and this file are simulated derivations checked for internal consistency against the figures. They are not tests of the cooperation claim and must be labelled as such. They cannot settle anything they report on.

## 7. What the essays decide by themselves, without an anchor ruling

*The Recursive Self-Improvement Trap* (opened) states: "Natural selection is what the loop looks like when there is no one maintaining the ground." And: "The answer is not 'just evolution.' ... Natural selection is not the whole answer. It is a special case. The full answer is the loop itself." Applied here: "evolutionary dynamics" names one mode of the habitat, the unmaintained one (Fig 11 :36, "Where nothing maintains anything, intelligence is a label applied afterwards to a lineage that met its ground. Nothing was intelligent; selection ran. The word does no work there."). The question's answer cannot be "just evolution" at the level of the framework; at the level of the world it is a candidate mechanism whose source still needs a discriminator. This is decided from the figures and the essay, so no external ruling is owed. It is recorded here under the metalaw reading in `FRAMEWORK/reference/60_FIGURES_AS_METALAWS.md`.
