## 4. Distribution rules: offer, do not push

The figure-grounded reasons are stated first so the rule is checkable rather than habitual.

- **Fig 10 :52, "Bring a characterised residual, never the source."** Send the residual, the shape claim, and the refuter first. The architecture goes only in stage 2, after the recipient has recorded a verdict on stage 1.
- **Fig 4 :22, "Only methods cross up. Only head starts come down."** What crosses between people is a reusable method (the derivation and its refuter), not a recording of one literature result. A recording carried upward "looks like knowledge and is a description of one occasion" (Fig 4 :25).
- **Fig 9 :27-28, "describe it before you go looking, or any frame you pick will seem to fit."** Ask what shape a failing cooperation explanation would have before showing any source.
- **Fig 2 :35, "Alignment is triangulation, not negotiation. A mirror cannot correct you."** Do not ask for agreement or trust.
- **Fig 2 :40, "disqualifying for a verdict: agreement about the world is worthless when the agreeing frames share an evidence pool."** A list selected for receptiveness manufactures the disqualifying correlation. There is no receptiveness rank in this folder.
- **Fig 10 :42, "A verifier that can reconstruct the claim cannot verify it."** A recipient who has absorbed the derivation before giving a verdict has joined the frame being checked.
- **Seat Map: "an interest in the outcome is unavoidable, an interest in the reading is disqualifying."** The author's interest in the framework's usefulness is declared here and in `08_expert_verification_packet.md`; it is not concealed behind a claim of neutrality.

2. **Stage 2, opt-in only.** If the recipient is curious, offer the local figure extract and the two world links. State that the figures are the authority and the world sources are secondary corroboration.
3. **Stage 3, opt-in only.** Offer the formula as a method. Each user writes their own refuter, names their own ground, and states their own mode. Report failures, not endorsements.

## 6. Legal and venue constraints

Unsolicited bulk email breaches GDPR and CAN-SPAM style rules in many jurisdictions and will get a sending domain blacklisted. Public institutional contact and submission routes avoid that failure as well as the structural one. Use them one-to-one.

Several journals in evolutionary biology and complex systems restrict or require disclosure of AI-assisted submissions. Because this derivation is a simulated result checked for internal consistency rather than a ground reading, check the current AI policy of any venue before submitting; disclose the method rather than presenting the output as a measurement. This constraint is repeated in `08_expert_verification_packet.md`.
