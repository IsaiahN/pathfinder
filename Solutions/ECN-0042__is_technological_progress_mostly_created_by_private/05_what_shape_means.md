## 1. A shape is a constraint, not a filler

The canonical figures README states the limit: the framework returns *"what a missing thing would have to look like (a shape, not a name)."* Fig 9 :32: *"you get a kind of thing, never the answer itself."* Fig 13 :135: *"It cannot say which primitive is missing, because naming it would mean already holding it."*

From *The Library of Babel*: *"You can cut your search short if the set of books returned is small and descriptive enough that it provides a general shape of the answer that satisfies your question."*

So a **shape** is the narrowed form of an answer you can state before you can name it. It constrains; it does not fill. For ECN-0042:

- A shape: "the three options form a union of overlapping conditions; a new atom enters a closure only on contact with a differing closure; the surplus requires low overlap and a described residual; each domain has its own non-updating anchor."
- A name: "private firms create 55%, public research 30%, collaboration 15%."

The first is a shape that can be checked. The second is a number the framework does not return and no measurement in this folder settles.

## 2. How a shape is obtained

- *Library of Babel*: use the vocabulary already closest to the thing, look for recurring new terms that overlap with it, recurse. *"With each new term gained, you narrow the space and deepen your vocabulary."*
- Fig 6 :30: *"The description travels. Fails by being sharpened into terms only we can read. Cast it in effect terms."* Fig 9 :30-31: *"your words are borrowed too, so describe the effect rather than the cause."* For this question, "who creates progress" is a cause-shaped phrase. The effect-shaped version is: which closure held the primitive that closed a slot's residual, and how would we know derivation from import?
- *Decoding Tacit Knowledge*: *"When contact isn't possible, the best you can send is a plain description of what you'd expect to see, because anyone can check that against the world."* This is why the outreach email asks what the recipient would expect to see if the union shape were true.
- *Library of Babel* on stopping: the answer is reached when *"the finish line will also be the starting place"*, with the paradox that you have *"composed the answer through the process of locating it."* Here the stop is the bound shape plus its refuter, not the percentage.

## 3. What licenses a name

Only two things, both outside the shape itself:

1. **The ground settles it.** Formula step 5: *"the ground settles it. Verified only by what pays."* The README says the framework *"stops at the limit of what the available instruments can distinguish, and records the lineage so the stop is honest."* For ECN-0042 that would be a per-slot, source-discriminating instrument: provenance plus a ground that pays.
2. **A provenanced import from a frame whose closure differs** (Fig 6 :23, Fig 8 :50-52), with a source gate and a shadow test. The Government Patent Register's funding/ownership classification (CEPR column) is such an import from the economics frame. It is a record of origin, not a reading of the primitive's source.

*They're Scanning Books* sharpens this: *"The real import is not a new term in the existing space. It is a new dimension of the space."* And: *"A minimum viable set of six terms is a human's careful guess at reality's compression axes; another sensorium's minimum set need not be those six."* Also: *"Declaring the primitives does not make them neutral. It makes the choice visible."*

## 4. The ground and the source problem

*The Hard Problem is About Source* gives the mechanism. Metamerism: an instrument reads green and *"cannot comment on the source"*, whether the source is one green light, yellow plus blue, RGB, CMYK, or a scheme not yet discovered. *"If several paths create the same behavior, then behavior is a projection with lossy information."* And: *"An instrument that can't distinguish these cases demonstrates its own inadequacy."* It was the **spectrometer**, not an argument, that resolved it.

For ECN-0042 the artifact is a patent, a paper, a product or a productivity series. Several institutional paths can produce the same artifact. Reading "private", "public" or "collaboration" off the artifact is the metamerism error. What is owed is a source-discriminating instrument plus a provenance record, not an attribution argument. Fig 8 :50-52 supplies the figure-level version: convergent derivation and adopted import look identical in the contents and only the record separates them.

*The Recursive Self-Improvement Trap*, opened directly this session, gives the validator limit: *"the output is only as good as the validator. The agent, or the tool, or the frame, or the theory, cannot validate itself."* Applied here: a funding agency's own accounting of its contribution is the frame scoring itself with a quantity it also produces (Fig 1 :24-27).

## 5. Answer to the shape question

**What does "shape" license us to say here?** The union separator, the per-slot indexing rule, the closure/import boundary condition, the independence condition on collaboration, the per-domain anchor rule, and the honest `gap` on the percentage. It does not license a winner or a share.

**Does naming "private", "public" or "collaboration" commit the error the README forbids?** Not by itself. Here is the correction, kept visible.

> **Correction to my own §3 and §4.** I initially read "shape, not a name" as forbidding the naming of any institutional source. That overstates it. The README's phrase is *"what a **missing** thing would have to look like (a shape, not a name)."* It governs a primitive not held in Γ. Private firms, public research and cross-closure contact are bindings from the economics frame onto structures already in view, not invented seventh primitives. Naming them is provenance. What remains forbidden is claiming that naming one moves the wall (Fig 6 :22: *"IMPORT adds an atom, and the added atom is what expands the closure"*, which these bindings do not), or turning a binding into a standing list of good actors (Fig 11 :51). My §4 metamerism point is **not** retracted: reading a source off a single artifact is still an error when several sources give the same reading. The reason is the missing discriminator, not a ban on naming held bindings. Both claims are recorded so neither is quietly lost.

## 6. Consequences for contact

**Prestige is not action.** From *The Recursive Self-Improvement Trap*, opened this session: *"Prestige must never be allowed to validate an action directly... Someone let prestige stand in for proof... Prestige can fund an action. It can give an action a budget to be tried. But it cannot settle an action. Only the ground can settle it. The Ground Maintainer must be blind to prestige."* A campaign that gathers famous economists to agree with the union framing is the named collapse, not corroboration. The legitimate ask gives the shape a budget to be tried and lets a ground settle it.

**My own audit is a simulation, not a ground reading.** Same essay: *"LLMs can quickly simulate an approximate answer/output with the formula found in the architecture post... However, the truly testable output would need to be derived from the real architecture or working the formula yourself."* So `01_figure_grounded_audit.md` is a simulated derivation, checked for internal consistency against the figures. It is **not** a test of the economics question and must be labelled as such. It cannot settle anything it reports on.
