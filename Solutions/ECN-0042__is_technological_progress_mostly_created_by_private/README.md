## The question restated as an effect

The loop writes R = |Γ(b,a) - o′| per slot s (Formula step 1; Symbols table, `R`). Here:

- **o′** is a realised change in what can be done, a new settled method or atom entering the productive library. "Technological progress" is the name we give o′ after the fact.
- **b** is what an institutional actor takes to be the case before it acts, formed from its own history and inherited priors.
- **a** is what it does: fund, publish, patent, commercialise, licence, collaborate.
- **Γ(b,a)** is what that actor's library predicts it can produce.
- **s** is the technology or domain slot. R is indexed per technology and per domain, never averaged into one global rate (Fig 1 :19, Fig 1 :22).

The question asks which actor *mostly* closes R. In effect terms it asks for a source attribution over a set of per-slot residuals.

- Option A, "private firms mostly": predicts a single standing source wins across slots, settled by market payment.
- Option B, "public research mostly": predicts a single standing source wins across slots, settled by peer or reproduction settlement.
- Option C, "collaboration mostly": predicts a single standing arrangement wins across slots.

- What an answer would look like, written down and bound as an ordinary term before the run (Formula, "Direction"): a **separator plus a shape**, the conditions under which each institutional closure contributes an atom to a slot, an instrument that settles each contribution against a ground that does not update on the contributor's own claim, and a provenance record that distinguishes convergent derivation from adopted import. Until that shape is bound it predicts nothing and no check in the loop can see it. It is bound in `01_figure_grounded_audit.md` section 0, so the pre-registered refuter in section 3 can fire on it.

The verdict recorded in `09_watson_answer.md` refuses A, B and C as stated. The three options form a **union of overlapping conditions**, not a partition with an assignable majority share. Verdicts are kept per domain and never averaged. The literal "mostly" percentage is a declared `gap`, reported as unreachable rather than silence.

## What is deliberately not here

- No personal emails of named individuals.
- No ranking of people by how likely they are to agree.
- No bulk-send plan.
- No claim that this derivation is `grounded`, no claim that it settles the economics question, and no claim that the framework is the only true account of innovation.

Three figure-grounded reasons, plus one practical one:

1. A room is not an anchor. *"A room's agreement is variation, not a verdict. Selection between rooms is where the ground votes."* (Fig 7 :35) and *"Alignment is triangulation, not negotiation. A mirror cannot correct you."* (Fig 2 :35). Gathering agreeable economists manufactures the correlation that disqualifies a verdict.
2. Correlation has valence. *"disqualifying for a verdict: agreement about the world is worthless when the agreeing frames share an evidence pool"* (Fig 2 :40). A list selected for receptiveness is one witness wearing many names.
3. A verifier must be outside the interior. *"A verifier that can reconstruct the claim cannot verify it."* (Fig 10 :42). The first contact therefore carries the residual and its refuter, not the framework.
4. Legally, unsolicited bulk email to scraped addresses breaches GDPR and CAN-SPAM style rules in many jurisdictions and gets the sending domain blacklisted. Public opt-in routes avoid that failure as well as the structural one.
