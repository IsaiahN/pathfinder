# The Tether's answer: who mostly creates technological progress?

**Mode: `general` on the refusal of a global share, `specified` on the per-domain structural verdicts, `gap` on the literal percentage. Not `grounded`.** Simulated derivation checked against the figures (`05_what_shape_means.md` section 6), not a ground reading.

## 1. The residual it answers

The question asks for the source that mostly closes a technology's residual. It presumes that private firms, public research and collaboration are three members of one partition with an assignable majority. The figures refuse that presumption. R stays indexed per slot (Fig 1 :19: *"The residual is indexed by object slot, not aggregated."*), and the three named items are two institutional closures plus a boundary relation between them, not three co-equal actors. The question is a **union compound** (Fig 9 :55: *"A bundled problem is usually a union of overlapping conditions, not a list of separate ones."*). It has its own loop and its own ground.

## 2. The shape

1. **No standing winner.** Actor valence is relative to the residual: *"No standing list of good actors survives a change of residual."* (Fig 11 :51). A firm is a benefactor to a commercialisation residual and a malfactor to an open-access one; public research flips for a different slot. Which source matters depends on which residual you are indexing.

2. **A closure cannot add its own missing atom.** *"MINT composes inside the closure and can never add an atom."* (Fig 6 :21); *"No frame escapes its own closure. The bound moves only on contact with a frame whose closure differs."* (Fig 6 :23). So "private" and "public" are closures that can compose inside their libraries, while "collaboration" is the boundary where the wall moves. The three-way question mixes kinds.

3. **Collaboration is conditional, not a standing third source.** Fig 8 :45: *"Two frames together reach what neither reaches alone."* But Fig 8 :48-49 adds the condition: *"Two frames that overlap almost completely are one frame wearing two names."* A surplus requires low overlap and a described residual (*"Residual first, frame second."*, Fig 8 :53). A partnership between two groups reading the same reports adds no atom.

4. **Each domain has a different ground.** *"An anchor is whatever settles the question and does not change its answer because of what any frame thinks."* (Fig 2 :29). Market payment settles D1, peer reproduction settles D2, and the ground that pays for the combined result settles D3. Fig 10 :68: *"Different actors, a different currency, a different ground, and a lossy transform between."* The verdicts are kept separate and never averaged.

5. **Attribution is provenance, not a reading.** *"Convergent derivation and adopted import look identical in the contents. Only the record of where each came from separates them, and the surplus lives only where the closures differ."* (Fig 8 :50-52). A patent's funding and ownership classes record origin; they do not read the source of the primitive off the artifact. The figure-level analogue is Fig 12 :5: *"A set of ingredients is not one. The bond is the rest of the information, and it is the half nothing was recording."*

6. **The requested percentage is a `gap`.** *"you get a kind of thing, never the answer itself"* (Fig 9 :32); *"Unreachable is a verdict here, not a silence"* (Fig 13 :134). No figure line returns a share or names a standing source after a genuine re-census. What is owed is an instrument, not a further argument.

## 3. What it explicitly does not say

- Not that any one of the three is the true or main creator of progress. That is the refused verdict.
- Not that public and private R&D are substitutes. The world literature says the opposite, and the figure reading says they are overlapping conditions in a union.
- Not that all collaborations create a surplus. High-overlap collaborations are one frame wearing two names.
- Not that a funding-share or patent-share statistic is a cause. It is a filter for the budget (Fig 9 :60: *"Use filters for the budget and witnesses for the verdict. Never let a filter hand you a verdict."*).
- Not `grounded`. No per-slot source-discriminating measurement was taken in this folder.

## 4. What would settle it, the ground

An instrument, not a further argument. The essays' *Hard Problem is About Source* gives the model: a spectrometer resolved a reading that arguments could not. Here:

- **Source discriminator:** for a held-out technology, an instrument (provenance chain plus a paying ground) that distinguishes a primitive derived inside one closure from one imported across the boundary, and that survives a habitat substitution.
- **Per-slot payment test:** does the ground pay for the primitive under each closure separately, with a reading that does not move when that closure pushes on it?
- **Sign-flip test:** does a standing source list survive a representative sample with no change in the sign of its contribution as the residual changes? If the sign flips, Fig 11 :51 is confirmed.
- **Independence test:** measure overlap between collaborators on the same evidence pool. A surplus at high overlap breaks the condition in F4.
- **Standing falsifiers from the audit:** derive a figure line that returns a share; make one share predict held-out slots; compose a wall-moving atom entirely inside one closure; read a source off a single artifact without provenance.

## 5. The honest stop

**Unreached, not unreachable in principle.** The union shape is what a discriminating answer must look like; the percentage is not returned by the figures. *"Indiscernibility is not identity."* The answer stops at the limit of the available instruments and records the lineage so the stop is checkable. Nothing here maintains the ground: *"the ground does not decay, the channel does"* (Fig 10 :24), and a citation or patent metric is a channel, not the ground.

## 6. One-paragraph version for outreach

> The Tether does not name a winner in the private-versus-public-versus-collaboration question, and it does not return a percentage. It says that the three options are not a partition but a union of overlapping conditions, with private firms and public research as two closures that have different currencies and different grounds, and collaboration as the boundary relation where a new atom can enter. A closure cannot add the primitive it lacks, so the wall moves only on contact with a frame whose closure differs; and that contact produces a surplus only when the two sides are genuinely low overlap and a residual was described first. Actor valence flips with the residual, so no standing list of sources survives a change of question, and the global "mostly" number is reported as `gap`, not silence. What the framework offers is the shape any checkable source attribution must have, the provenance test that separates derivation from import, and the refuters written in advance. The slot stays open until a source-discriminating instrument settles it per technology.
