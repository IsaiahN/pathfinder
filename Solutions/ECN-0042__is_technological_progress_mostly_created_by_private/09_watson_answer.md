## QUESTION

Decide, in effect terms, what the Tether says about the claim that technological progress is mostly created by private firms, public research, or collaboration between them. Option A: accept the three-way partition and report a majority source. Option B: refuse the partition, keep the union of conditions, and report the percentage as a `gap` with a source-discriminating instrument owed. What each leads to: A sends a `general`-mode verdict built by aggregating per-slot residuals, spends the independence of the people checking it, and mistakes a provenance record for a source reading; B sends a checkable union shape, separate per-domain verdicts, written refuters, and an open slot until a ground settles it.

## DOMAIN CENSUS

R by arity, symmetry and scale, then one line per domain with actors / currency / anchor, partition or union, and order by dependency.

- **R (the residual).** The source of the atom that closes a technology slot's gap between prediction and realised capability. Arity: multi-source over three institutional forms plus conditions. Symmetry: *"created by"* is an ordered production bond (⇒), not a symmetric disjunction; "mostly" is a threshold over an attribution distribution. Scale: single research act, institutional closure, economy-wide productivity, connected by a lossy membrane. Fig 9 names this a union compound, not one hard question: a case in two conditions is not a harder case of either.
- **Domain 1, private firm closure.** Actors: firms, in-house R&D, investors, customers. Currency: appropriable return and market share. Anchor: realised market payment per technology, a market named in Fig 2 :29. Question type: one closure among several, settled by a ground that does not update on the firm's own claim.
- **Domain 2, public research closure.** Actors: universities, public labs, funding agencies, peer reviewers. Currency: publications, citations, openly published knowledge. Anchor: reproducibility and settlement by a peer frame. Question type: a second closure with a different currency and a lossy transform to D1.
- **Domain 3, cross-closure contact (collaboration).** Actors: licences, joint projects, public-private patents, spillovers. Currency: the surplus atom neither closure could compose alone. Anchor: the ground that pays for the combined result, per slot. Question type: a boundary relation, not a third standing source; a compound with its own loop.
- **Domain 4, the channel.** Actors: instruments, patent classifications, metrics, percepts. Currency: contact with the ground over time. Anchor: the ground, which does not decay while the channel does (Fig 10 :24).
- **Order by dependency.** 1 perception of the capability change, 2 vocabulary for derivation versus import, 3 objective (what counts as a settled source), 4 planning of a discriminating design, 5 learn and carry as provenance. Refuse the partition before minting the union shape (Formula step 2); describe the residual before choosing a collaborator (Fig 9 :27-28).

Strain test applied: an early attempt to map Fig 13's six physical terms onto institutions only fit by calling a patent a kind of energy. That is a strain, so Fig 13 governs the honest stop only. Re-census recorded in `01` section 7 strain S1.

## VERDICT (from the figures)

The three-way question is refused as stated. Private firms and public research are two institutional closures with different currencies and different grounds, and collaboration is the boundary relation where the wall moves, not a third standing source. The three form a union of overlapping conditions with per-slot residuals and separate verdicts that are never averaged. A new atom enters a closure only on contact with a differing closure, and the cross-closure surplus requires low evidence overlap plus a described residual. Actor valence flips with the residual, so no source wins across all technologies. The global "mostly" percentage is a `gap`: unreachable from the figures and owed to a source-discriminating instrument, not silence and not an invitation to invent a number. Mode `general` on the structure, `specified` on the per-domain claims, not `grounded`. Whole folder is a simulated derivation checked for internal consistency against the figures, not a ground reading.

- **cost if wrong:** bounded and locatable. The packet states mode, separates the domain verdicts and writes refuters before the conclusion, so an error is findable rather than invisible. The real cost is spending the independence of outside readers by sending an aggregated share or a list of agreeable economists instead of a checkable shape. Fig 2 :42 prices that: *"Buy independence in proportion to the cost of being wrong."* That cost is avoidable by leading with the union claim and its refuter.
- **undoable:** yes. The refused partition and the standing-list reading are removable without touching the figures, and `07_the_tether_answer.md` and `08_expert_verification_packet.md` already remove them. No primitive has to be withdrawn; the union shape is added to a vocabulary that keeps the old terms, and the reach falls because the refused terms lose their standing to be composed over.
- **reversed by:** a ground reading, not agreement. A source-discriminating instrument that names the primitive's closure per technology and survives a habitat substitution would settle the slot; one share that predicts held-out per-technology residuals would break the union verdict; a standing source list with no sign flip would break Fig 11 :51; a high-overlap collaboration producing a surplus atom neither side held would break the independence condition; a figure line returning a global share after re-census would break the `gap`. Until one of these fires, the verdict stands as `candidate`.
