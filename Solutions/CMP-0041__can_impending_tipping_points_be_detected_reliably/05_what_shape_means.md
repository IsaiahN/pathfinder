## 1. A shape is a constraint, not a filler

The canonical rule from `FRAMEWORK/reference/FIGURES_README_canonical.md` is that the framework returns *"what a missing thing would have to look like (a shape, not a name)"*. Fig 9 :32 says *"you get a kind of thing, never the answer itself"*. Fig 13 :134 says *"Unreachable is a verdict here, not a silence,"* and the same passage adds that it *"cannot say which primitive is missing, because naming it would mean already holding it."*

From *The Library of Babel*: *"you can cut your search short if the set of books returned is small and descriptive enough that it provides a general shape of the answer that satisfies your question."*

For this question a **shape** is the form any adequate answer must have: a four-link chain (threshold, channel, indicator, decision), a threshold treated as a comparison and a warning as a sequence, a refusal of pooled agreement as corroboration, and a `gap` where only a ground reading can give a number. A **name** would be "variance with a 30-day window reliably detects lake eutrophication at 90 percent accuracy". The framework cannot produce that name. A measurement can.

## 2. How the shape is obtained

- *Library of Babel*: use the vocabulary already held that is closest to the thing, then *"With each new term gained, you are able to narrow the space by definition and deepen your vocabulary."* Bootstrapping by proximity, not by authoritative definition.
- Fig 6 :30: *"The description travels. Fails by being sharpened into terms only we can read. Cast it in effect terms."* Fig 9 :30-31: *"your words are borrowed too, so describe the effect rather than the cause."* So state the residual as "a rising statistic before a crossing", not as "critical slowing down", because the cause-word imports a theory.
- *Decoding Tacit Knowledge*: *"When contact isn't possible, the best you can send is a plain description of what you'd expect to see, because anyone can check that against the world."* That is exactly the stage-1 outreach rule: describe what a detector should show if it had real skill, and let a researcher check that against their own data.
- *Library of Babel* on stopping: *"the finish line will also be the starting place"*. Here the shape is reached when the four domains can no longer be reduced into one another and the `gap` is stated rather than filled.

## 3. What licenses a name

Only two things, both outside the shape:

1. **The ground settles it.** Formula step 5: *"the ground settles it. Verified only by what pays. A gate is not the ground."* The canonical README: the framework *"stops at the limit of what the available instruments can distinguish, and records the lineage so the stop is honest."*
2. **A provenanced import from a frame whose closure differs** (Fig 6 :23, Fig 8 :50-52), with a source gate and a shadow test. A new indicator is such an import only if its provenance and the gap it explains are recorded.

This is why the solution treats Scheffer et al. (2009) and van Beers et al. (2026) as boundary reports from other closures, not as figure-derived names. They are secondary witnesses, read and recorded with provenance.

## 4. The source problem, which is the core difficulty here

*The Hard Problem is About Source* gives the mechanism. An instrument reads green but *"it cannot comment on the source"*; the same reading can come from one green light, yellow plus blue, RGB, CMYK, or a scheme not yet discovered. The essay states: *"If several paths create the same behavior, then behavior is a projection with lossy information"* and *"An instrument that can't distinguish these cases demonstrates its own inadequacy."* What resolved the case was *"not by argument, but by the arrival of a better instrument."*

For tipping points, a rising variance followed by a crossing is the same kind of projection. It can come from a fold bifurcation with critical slowing down, from an externally forced transition with no loss of local stability, from a change in sampling or aggregation, or from a noise-induced jump. The reading does not determine the source. Chekroun and Lucarini (2026) state the same thing in dynamical-systems terms: *"An early-warning signal is therefore a property of a triple: RP block, observable, and perturbation direction."* What is owed is a source-discriminating instrument, not another argument that the reading means slowing down.

This is the deepest reason the answer is conditional: the indicator is a reading, and the question asks about the source and the future event. The instrument that would close it is not yet held.

## 5. Q for this question: does "shape, not a name" forbid naming an indicator?

**No, not as a blanket rule.** The README's phrase governs a *missing* primitive, one not held in `Γ`. Variance, autocorrelation and skewness are already in the vocabulary of the EWS frame. Selecting one for a named system is a binding from the world frame onto a held term, not the invention of a new primitive.

What is forbidden is claiming that the figure framework *derives* the selection, or that a binding moves the wall (Fig 6 :22: *"NOVEL means novel relative to atoms, not to the world. IMPORT adds an atom, and the added atom is what expands the closure."*). The binding needs ground and provenance. Until then it is a candidate, not a name the framework produced.

The correction above is kept visible rather than silently substituted. The substantive conclusion of the solution is unchanged: the universal rate and the system-specific indicator remain a `gap` because no ground reading was taken here, not because naming is categorically forbidden.

## 6. Prestige is not action

*The Recursive Self-Improvement Trap*: *"Prestige must never be allowed to validate an action directly."* And: *"Prestige can fund an action. It can give an action a budget to be tried. But it cannot settle an action. Only the ground can settle it."* In this field that means the authority of a well-known reviewer, or the citation count of the 2009 review, can get a detector tried. It cannot decide whether the detector works. The ground is a prospective crossing in a real system.

## 7. My own audit is a simulation, not a ground reading

Same essay: *"LLMs can quickly simulate an approximate answer/output with the formula found in the architecture post."* And: *"the truly testable output would need to be derived from the real architecture or working the formula yourself."* Also: *"the output is only as good as the validator. The agent, or the tool, or the frame, or the theory, cannot validate itself."* Therefore `01_figure_grounded_audit.md` is a simulated derivation checked for internal consistency against the figures. It is **not** a test of whether early warnings are reliable and must be labelled as such. It cannot settle anything it reports on.

## 8. Consequence for this solution

The shape survives: a union of four links, a comparison at the threshold, a sequence for the warning, one evidence pool behind the indicators, and a `gap` for the rate and the name. What does not survive is any claim that this derivation measured reliability. That claim would require the ground, and the ground is a prospective crossing that this agent did not observe.
