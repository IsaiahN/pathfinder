# The Tether's answer: can impending tipping points be detected reliably?

**Mode: `specified` on the conditional shape, `general` on the two refusals, `gap` on a universal false-alarm rate and on naming the indicator for a given system. Not `grounded`.** Simulated derivation checked against the figures (`05_what_shape_means.md` section 7), not a ground reading.

## 1. The residual it answers

A detector reads early-warning statistics from a real system and is asked to say whether a regime crossing is impending, with a stated false-alarm rate, miss rate and lead time. The two options lead to different actions: if reliable, intervene against the warning; if not, the warning is a bet with declared error costs. The answer is bound in advance as an ordinary term: a conditional shape with four per-domain verdicts, two refusals, and a `gap`.

## 2. The shape

1. **The threshold and the warning are different bonds.** The crossing is Fig 12 :87, *"a threshold relation between two quantities"*; the warning is Fig 12 :72, *"A then B; B needs A to have happened"*. Score the comparison and the sequence separately. A detector fitted after the event has silently swapped the sequence.

2. **The precursor and its disguise share a source.** Fig 13 :31-32: *"a remainder compounding through a chain that amplifies is chaos, and the detail a crossing discards reappears above it as noise."* Rising variance is both informative and ambiguous, because the feedback that makes the crossing possible is the same process that puts variation into the observable. The reading cannot by itself determine the source.

3. **The channel is the part that decays.** Fig 10 :24: *"the ground does not decay, the channel does."* Fig 10 :25-26 names the failure modes: *"stale percepts, dropped transients, saturated metrics, pooled readings"*. Fig 1 :22: *"Aggregating across slots is how a live signal disappears into an average."* A null reading from a pooled, coarsely sampled channel is not evidence of safety; the honest output is *"you have lost touch"* (Fig 10 :27-28).

4. **The indicators are one evidence pool, not four witnesses.** Fig 2 :40: *"disqualifying for a verdict: agreement about the world is worthless when the agreeing frames share an evidence pool"*. Fig 7 :35: *"A room's agreement is variation, not a verdict. Selection between rooms is where the ground votes."* Variance, autocorrelation, skewness and flickering descend from the same slowing-down mechanism (06 Q3). Their agreement is expected whether or not a crossing is near.

5. **Retrospective success is not prospective reach.** Fig 5 :43-44: *"A term that explains everything by saying nothing fails this, and so does a term that explains one occasion perfectly."* Fig 5 :71-72: *"and a library that grows is not a reach that grows."* A hindcast-selected indicator has grown the library. Whether it composes over into a real system is a separate fact about that level.

6. **Absence of warning proves nothing.** Fig 8 :39: *"So a search that finds nothing is never a proof of absence."* Fig 9 :44: *"Only a sealed room can be searched to the end, and no frame is a sealed room. So searching and finding nothing proves nothing."* A real system is not a sealed room.

7. **A universal rate and a named indicator are a `gap`.** Fig 9 :32: *"you get a kind of thing, never the answer itself"*. Fig 13 :134: *"Unreachable is a verdict here, not a silence,"*. No figure line supplies a number or selects a statistic. The derivation stops there and records the lineage.

8. **Conditional success is expected, not denied.** Fig 6 :34: *"The question is not whether a sensor could exist. It is whether anything, at any resolution, is already returning something that fails to resolve."* Where the system tracks a slowly changing steady state, the observable is aligned, the channel preserves the transient and the decision declares its costs, the canonical indicators can work with a short lead time. The engineering case in `10_source_ledger.csv` is a boundary report from another closure, not a contradiction.

## 3. What it explicitly does not say

- Not that early warnings never work. The framework refuses blanket certification, not the possibility of detection.
- Not a universal false-alarm or miss rate, not a lead time, not a named best indicator for a named system. Those are ground readings, recorded as `gap`.
- Not that a flat indicator record means safety (finding 6).
- Not that multiple agreeing indicators corroborate each other (finding 4).
- Not that this derivation is a measurement. It is a simulation checked for internal consistency.
- Not that the world sources decide the verdict. They corroborate or bound; the figures decide.

## 4. What would settle it, the ground

Fig 2 :29-30 gives the anchor test: *"An anchor is whatever settles the question and does not change its answer because of what any frame thinks. A measurement, an experiment, a counter, a market, a patient outcome. If it moves when you push on it, it is not an anchor."* Here the anchor is the recorded crossing under a definition fixed in advance. The instruments that would close the `gap`:

1. **A source-discriminating instrument.** From *The Hard Problem is About Source*: a color meter reads green *"cannot comment on the source"*; the spectrometer resolved it, and the essay says it was *"not by argument, but by the arrival of a better instrument."* Here: can the rising variance be attributed to loss of local stability rather than to external forcing, misaligned observation or aggregation? Chekroun and Lucarini (2026) say an early-warning signal is a property of a triple (block, observable, perturbation direction); an instrument that resolves the triple would name the source.
2. **A prospective, pre-registered trial.** Declare the event definition, window, lead time, false-alarm cost and miss-alarm cost before the run, on real systems with independent channels. Retrospective fits do not count (finding 5).
3. **An independence test for indicators.** Show two indicators drawing on distinct evidence and distinct generating mechanisms; their prospective joint agreement would break finding 4.
4. **Standing figure-level refuters.** Produce a figure line governing a universal rate, or select an indicator; exhibit a closed system where no warning proves no tipping; measure a precursor whose variation is not the discarded detail of a crossing. These are written before the conclusion in `01_figure_grounded_audit.md` section 4.

## 5. The honest stop

**Unreached, not unreachable.** Fig 6 :27: *"Which is why some unreachability is depth and some is genuine absence, and from inside the frame the two look the same."* The framework can say what a reliable detector would have to satisfy and what would refute the shape. It cannot say which statistic will work in a named lake or ice sheet, because that is a ground reading. The canonical README: the framework *"stops at the limit of what the available instruments can distinguish, and records the lineage so the stop is honest. Indiscernibility is not identity."*

## 6. One-paragraph version for outreach

> The figures do not give a yes-or-no reliability number. They say the warning is a sequence and the crossing is a threshold comparison, so the two must be scored separately; that the feedback which makes a tipping possible also puts variation into the record, so rising variance is informative and ambiguous at source; and that the monitoring channel, not the ground, is the part that decays, so pooled and coarsely sampled readings can erase a local warning. They also refuse the usual comfort of agreement: variance, autocorrelation, skewness and flickering are one functional group sharing a slowing-down mechanism, so their concordance is not independent corroboration, and hindcast success grows the library without growing the reach. Absence of a warning proves nothing in a system that is not a sealed room. The result is a conditional shape with four separate domain verdicts and pre-registered refuters, plus an honest `gap` where only a prospective ground reading can supply a false-alarm rate or name the right indicator. Where all four links hold, short-lead detection does work, and that boundary is recorded rather than denied.

**Use filters for the budget and witnesses for the verdict.** Fig 9 :60: *"Use filters for the budget and witnesses for the verdict. Never let a filter hand you a verdict."*
