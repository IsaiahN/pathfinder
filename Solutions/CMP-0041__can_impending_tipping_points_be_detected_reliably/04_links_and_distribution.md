## Distribution: offer, do not push

Figure-grounded reasons:

- **Fig 10 :52** - *"Bring a characterised residual, never the source."* Sending the whole architecture before anyone has described an expected observation sends a source, not a residual.
- **Fig 9 :27-28** - *"describe it before you go looking, or any frame you pick will seem to fit"* - ask what they would expect to see if the shape claim were true before giving them the field literature.
- **Fig 2 :35** - *"Alignment is triangulation, not negotiation. A mirror cannot correct you."* Do not ask for agreement.
- **Fig 7 :35** - *"A room's agreement is variation, not a verdict. Selection between rooms is where the ground votes."* Do not pool later agreement as corroboration.
- **Fig 2 :42** - *"Buy independence in proportion to the cost of being wrong."* The cost of being wrong here is a missed crossing or a false alarm that spends institutional credibility, so independent checks are worth real effort.

Recommended order in a reply: (1) the shape claim and its four refuters; (2) ask what they would count as a failure and what ground they would use; (3) record their verdict on stage 1; (4) only then the three links, with the note that the figures, not this summary, are the authority.

## The two-stage offer

**Stage 1.** Send the residual, the conditional shape and the pre-registered refuter. Ask for the researcher's own failure criterion and their proposed ground. Record the answer before any theory is shown. A verdict is admissible here only if the person is outside the frame being checked and is not drawing on the same evidence pool.

**Stage 2, opt-in only.** If they are curious, offer the three source links. The world sources are secondary; the figures decide. Agreement after stage 2 is never added to the stage-1 verdict as a second witness.

**Stage 3, method only.** Offer the formula as a generator that others can run on a problem they can check. Require each user to write their own refuter before the run and to state the mode of the output. The Recursive Self-Improvement Trap: *"the output is only as good as the validator. The agent, or the tool, or the frame, or the theory, cannot validate itself."*

**Hard precondition.** Stages 1-3 only work if the stage-1 output survives. `01_figure_grounded_audit.md` section 4 carries the refuter that decides this. Keep the shape and the refuter in front of every link.
