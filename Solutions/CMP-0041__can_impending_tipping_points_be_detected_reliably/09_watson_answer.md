## QUESTION

Decide, in effect terms, whether a detector reading early-warning statistics from a real system can announce an impending regime crossing with acceptable false-alarm rate, miss rate and lead time. Option A = yes, reliability can be asserted from the generic behaviour of the indicators. Option B = reliability cannot be certified from inside the detection frame; it is a union of four links to be checked separately, with a `gap` where only the ground can decide. What each leads to: A licenses intervention on a warning and reassurance on its absence, with an unstated error budget; B requires an explicitly defined event, a prospective trial and a declared error budget before any detector is trusted.

## DOMAIN CENSUS

R by arity, symmetry and scale; one line per domain with actors / currency / anchor; partition or union; order by dependency.

- **R (the residual).** The gap between a warning reading and a future crossing at the ground. Arity: binary at minimum, k-indicator in practice, with independent witness arity probably 1. Symmetry: the crossing is a threshold comparison, not a symmetric join; the warning is a directed sequence; the path back after crossing differs from the outward path. Scale: fast sampling of a state variable versus slow drift of the control parameter and a change of regime.
- **Domain 1, the ground.** Actors: state variables, control parameter, basin, noise. Currency: distance to threshold. Anchor: the recorded crossing under a definition fixed in advance. Nameable, so this is a domain.
- **Domain 2, the channel.** Actors: sensors, sampling interval, spatial aggregation, observer. Currency: fidelity of the time series. Anchor: the same ground, which the channel cannot move. Nameable.
- **Domain 3, the indicator frame.** Actors: variance, AR(1), skewness, flickering, models, analysts. Currency: predictive skill estimated inside models and hindcasts. Anchor: should be prospective crossing, but in practice a retrospective fit is substituted; the substitution is the defect under audit, not a reason to drop the domain.
- **Domain 4, the decision frame.** Actors: managers, action thresholds, governance. Currency: false-alarm cost versus missed-alarm cost, lead time. Anchor: outcomes of acting or refraining, available only prospectively. Nameable.
- **Partition or union.** Union. All four links must hold at once; one lever clears only the region where one condition binds. Per-domain verdicts are never averaged.
- **Order by dependency.** D1 then D2 then D3 then D4. Settle the event definition before scoring; preserve the signal before refining the statistic; declare the costs before acting. Fig 3 :20: *"The chain is ordered by dependency, not by importance. Each link consumes what the one above produces, so effort spent above the break arrives at a step that cannot use it."*

## VERDICT (from the figures)

Reliability cannot be certified from inside the detection frame. The framework gives a conditional shape: a threshold comparison and a warning sequence scored separately; a channel that must preserve the local signal; indicators that are one functional group rather than independent witnesses; hindcasts that grow the library but not the reach; and no proof of safety from a flat record. A universal false-alarm or miss rate and a named indicator for a named system are a `gap`, because no ground reading was taken here. Narrow, short-lead success is expected where all four links hold and is recorded as a boundary, not as a contradiction. Verdict mode: `specified` and `general`, ungrounded, candidate.

- **cost if wrong:** if the shape is wrong, a real detector could be dismissed because someone applied the one-pool rule to genuinely independent indicators, or a good channel could be undervalued. The figures price the opposite error higher: treating pooled agreement as corroboration manufactures the correlation that disqualifies a verdict (Fig 2 :40), and a false alarm or a missed crossing is paid at the ground. Fig 2 :42: *"Buy independence in proportion to the cost of being wrong."* The cost is bounded and locatable because the shape states its four links and its refuters before any claim is acted on.
- **undoable:** yes. The `gap` is recorded rather than filled, so a prospective trial with a fixed event definition can supply the rate and the name without withdrawing any part of the shape. If C1, C2 or C3 fires, the affected finding is narrowed or withdrawn; the four-link census itself survives as a method. No primitive has to be invented.
- **reversed by:** a ground reading, not agreement. A source-discriminating instrument that attributes a rising statistic to loss of local stability rather than to forcing, aggregation or misaligned observation; a pre-registered prospective trial with declared error rates on two low-overlap real systems; two indicators proven independent with prospective joint skill; a genuinely closed system in which no warning proves no tipping; or a figure line from a fresh re-census that governs a universal rate or selects an indicator. Until one of these fires, the verdict stands as `candidate`.
