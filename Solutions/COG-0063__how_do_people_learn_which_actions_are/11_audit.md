## Independent domain census

I redid the census from the residual line alone, before reading the primary's conclusions, in the WATSON order: R by arity, symmetry and scale; one line per domain with actors, currency and anchor; partition or union; order by dependency.

- **R (arity / symmetry / scale).** The residual is the gap, for one action slot, between a predicted category and the category the environment returns. Arity: three conditions, so a **union compound** (Fig 9 :5), not one hard question. Symmetry: asymmetric; the act-to-category relation has different grounds in the three conditions, so the loops are not interchangeable. Scale: first order inside a learner, second order when a settled classification becomes an atom for the next learner (Fig 12 :104).
- **Domain 1, the individual learner.** Actors: learner, act, observed reaction. Currency: per-slot residual. Anchor: a consequence record that does not move with opinion. Nameable.
- **Domain 2, peer agreement.** Actors: peers, majority, authority. Currency: agreement and imitation. Anchor: none inside peer opinion. Not a verdict domain; a convention generator.
- **Domain 3, ritual and maintained habitat.** Actors: ritual specialists, participants, practices. Currency: introduction of actors, high-fidelity transmission. Anchor: the ground the ritual keeps reachable. Nameable.
- **Domain 4, cultural transmission.** Actors: generations, methods, head starts. Currency: methods up, head starts down. Anchor: the ground, which does not decay. Nameable.
- **Union.** An act can be in two conditions at once, so the intersection is a compound. Fig 9 :55 "A bundled problem is usually a union of overlapping conditions, not a list of separate ones."
- **Order by dependency.** Perception and vocabulary before objective before planning before carry (Fig 3); refusal before mint; describe the residual before picking a frame; settle before transmit.

My census matches the primary's four domains and its union reading. **R_T is nonzero.** Four constraints held by the shorthand do not survive the trip down without being made explicit:

- the **non-updating** requirement on the anchor (Fig 2 :29-30), which a study must build into its design rather than assume;
- the **refusal operator** (Fig 5 :53, Fig 12 :140), which a vocabulary audit must look for rather than treat as universal;
- the **provenance** requirement (Fig 8 :51), which distinguishes convergent derivation from copied adoption.

Conversely, one thing survives cleanly: three conditions with different grounds cannot be averaged into one learning mechanism without losing the signal that says where to look. That is the residue worth sending. R_T was **not** measured numerically, because no ground reading exists; it is recorded qualitatively as the enumerated set of dropped constraints. Treating "the formula is the condensed version of the figures" as identity would set R_T to zero, the dead-reckoning error Fig 4 :30 names.
