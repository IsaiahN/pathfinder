## Wider literature that bears on the question

1. **The three-category split is empirically discriminable.** The SEP entry reports that children judge prototypical moral transgressions to be wrong, authority independent and generalisable, and conventional transgressions to be wrong, authority dependent and not generalisable. It also reports the limitation: these criterion judgments were *hypothesised* to distinguish the categories, and the cross-cultural challenge is live. This corroborates the figure claim that the three conditions have different grounds, and it does **not** settle universality.
2. **Copying is selective, not indiscriminate.** Laland 2004 gives named strategies (copy when uncertain, copy the majority, copy if better) and places them hierarchically before innovation. This corroborates F5 and F6: what is transmitted is a policy for when to copy, which is a method, not a recorded verdict.
3. **Ritual transmits under cost and opacity.** Legare and Nielsen 2020 and McNamara 2024 independently describe high-fidelity transmission, causal opacity, costly signalling and group cohesion. Neither attributes a verdict function to ritual. This is consistent with Fig 11's maintained habitat and with Fig 10 :47's warning about a convention that decides an outcome.
4. **Agreement is a dynamical state, not a ground.** Peralta, Kertesz and Iniguez 2022 review consensus and polarisation as outcomes of exchange. This is a low-overlap corroboration of Fig 2 :35 and Fig 7 :35, from physics and network science rather than moral psychology.
5. **Simulacra warning.** The Recursive Self-Improvement Trap (opened this session) says "the truly testable output would need to be derived from the real architecture or working the formula yourself". This derivation is therefore labelled a simulation throughout.

**Overlap declaration.** The framework essays and figures share an author and an evidence pool. The six world sources above have different authors, venues and fields, and several of them cite each other only indirectly. Agreement between two of them that merely repeats a shared literature review is one witness, not two. The ledger records overlap per row.

- **Seat Map :163** "High correlation is disqualifying for a verdict and efficient for a convention." Do not select recipients by likely agreement, and do not pool later agreement as corroboration.

**Recommended order in a reply:** (1) the residual and the three refuters; (2) ask what they would count as a failure for each of the three categories; (3) record that verdict; (4) only then offer the figure text and the six source links, noting that the figures, not this summary, are the authority.

## The two-stage offer

**Stage 1.** Send the residual and the pre-registered refuter. The recipient checks the shape without holding the framework's interior.

**Stage 2, opt-in only.** If curious, offer the three figure links. Their later agreement is never pooled as corroboration, because by then they hold the frame that produced the claim.

**Stage 3.** Offer the eight-step formula as a method to reuse, with the instruction that each user writes their own refuter before running it and states the mode of what they get.

**Hard precondition:** stages 1 to 3 only work if the stage-1 output survives. No experiment in this session tested it, so the status remains `specified` and `candidate`.
