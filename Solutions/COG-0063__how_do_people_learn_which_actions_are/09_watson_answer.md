## QUESTION

Decide, in effect terms, what the Tether says about the claim that people learn which actions are wrong, disapproved or unusual by one mechanism of social copying. Option A: accept the one-loop claim. Option B: unbundle into three loops with three grounds and keep the verdict channel separate from the convention channel. What each leads to: A produces a global rating in which agreement does the work of a ground, so the coherence drift is invisible; B produces a checkable shape, written refuters, and a statement of what only a ground can settle.

## DOMAIN CENSUS

R by arity, symmetry and scale, then one line per domain with actors, currency and anchor; partition or union; order by dependency.

- **R (the residual).** Per action slot, the gap between a predicted category and the category the environment returns. Arity: three conditions, so a union compound. Symmetry: the act-to-category relation is asymmetric and the three grounds are not interchangeable. Scale: first order inside a learner, second order across generations when a settled classification becomes an atom for the next learner.
- **Domain 1, the individual learner.** Actors: learner, act, observed reaction. Currency: per-slot residual. Anchor: the consequence record that does not update on opinion. Nameable, so this is a domain.
- **Domain 2, peer agreement.** Actors: peers, majority, authority, prestige. Currency: agreement and imitation. No independent anchor inside peer opinion. It is a convention generator, not a verdict domain.
- **Domain 3, ritual and maintained habitat.** Actors: ritual specialists, participants, transmitted practices. Currency: introduction of actors, high-fidelity transmission. Anchor: the ground the ritual keeps reachable. Nameable.
- **Domain 4, cultural transmission.** Actors: generations, methods, head starts. Currency: methods up, head starts down. Anchor: the ground, which does not decay. Nameable.
- **Union, not partition.** An act can satisfy two conditions at once, so the intersection is a compound with its own ground question.
- **Order by dependency.** Perception and vocabulary first, then objective, then planning and carry. Refuse before mint. Describe the residual before choosing a frame. Settle the first-order classification before transmitting the method.

Strain test applied: the initial reading put "wrong" and "disapproved" in one domain with a strength parameter. That strained the opposite valences of agreement in Fig 2 and Fig 7. Re-census split them. No figure line was forced.

## VERDICT (from the figures)

The shape survives, the one-loop claim does not. Wrongness, disapproval and unusualness are three conditions with three grounds and must be unbundled before they are searched. Wrongness needs an anchor that does not update on opinion; disapproval is a convention for which agreement is the product, not a corruption; unusualness is a count and a filter. Valence is relative to the residual, so no standing list of wrong acts is licensed. Transmission carries methods rather than recordings, ritual maintains the habitat, and a convention that decides an outcome has stopped being a convention. Revision requires a refusal operator. Which specific actions fall in which category, and whether the split is universal, are `gap`. This is a `specified`, ungrounded, candidate result.

- **cost if wrong:** if the ground for wrongness is misidentified as peer agreement, the checking arrangement becomes a mirror chain: agreement compounds and evidence does not, and nobody can notice the drift from inside. That cost is bounded and locatable, because `08_expert_verification_packet.md` states the anchor test and its refuter in advance. It is avoidable by keeping the consequence and agreement channels separate in any study.
- **undoable:** yes. The three bindings are removable without touching the loop derivation. `07_the_tether_answer.md` and `08_expert_verification_packet.md` already withhold every act-specific name. No primitive has to be withdrawn.
- **reversed by:** a ground reading, not agreement. A two-by-two study holding consequences fixed and varying agreement that moves authority-independent wrongness judgments would break the anchor claim; a frequency-only condition producing a stable wrongness verdict would break the filter claim; a community retracting a rule without a refusal operator would break the negation claim; a re-census showing one ground for all three conditions would break the union claim. Until one fires, the verdict stands as `candidate`.
