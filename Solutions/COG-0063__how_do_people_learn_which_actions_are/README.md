## What is deliberately not here

- No personal emails of named individuals.
- No ranking of people by how likely they are to agree.
- No bulk-send plan.
- No claim that the figures settle which actions are in fact wrong in any particular society. That is a ground question and it is recorded as a `gap`.

Three reasons, in the figures' own words:

1. **A room does not upgrade a label.** Seat Map :283: "A room does not upgrade the label. A room reaching a conclusion without the ground is agreement, and agreement among frames is a weighted average of influence. Adding people raises the vocabulary and does not add an anchor." A list of receptive readers cannot corroborate this derivation.
2. **Correlation disqualifies a verdict.** Seat Map :163: "High correlation is disqualifying for a verdict and efficient for a convention." Selecting recipients for likely agreement would manufacture exactly the correlation that makes their agreement worthless.
3. **A verifier must not hold the interiors.** Fig 10 :42 "A verifier that can reconstruct the claim cannot verify it." The first contact therefore carries the residual and its refuter, never the framework.
