## Two things in the wider literature that bear directly on the residual

1. **Translation history is a two-way circulation fact, not a one-way loss.** The history of science page records that Hellenistic knowledge was preserved and absorbed into Arabic through translations, then recovered into Western Europe from the tenth to the thirteenth century. If the figure were only "translation loses," this could not be described. The correct figure pair is Fig 4 :27 (the transform loses information) and Fig 8 :45 *"Two frames together reach what neither reaches alone."* Loss at the transform and surplus at the union are different quantities in different domains.
2. **The field itself denies neutrality.** Translation studies records the shift from equivalence to Skopos theory, which gives priority to the purpose of the translation over equivalence, and treats translations as facts of the target culture. That is the world-side corroboration of the figure claim that translation is a typed transform with a receiving closure, not a copy operation.

## Distribution: offer, do not push

Figure-grounded reasons:

- **Fig 10 :52** *"Bring a characterised residual, never the source."* Sending the framework before anyone has described a slot sends a recording, not a generator.
- **Fig 4 :22** *"Only methods cross up. Only head starts come down."* The formula is a method and may be offered; a finished conclusion is a recording and should not be pushed.
- **Fig 9 :27** *"describe it before you go looking"* Ask what the recipient would expect to see if the lossy-transform claim were true at their own language pair, before giving them the article.
- **Fig 2 :41** *"efficient for a convention: agreement about a shared meaning is the product, not a corruption of it"* A shared glossary is a success. Do not present multilingual terminological agreement as a failure of translation.
- **Fig 2 :40** *"agreement about the world is worthless when the agreeing frames share an evidence pool"* Do not select recipients for likely agreement, and do not pool later agreement as corroboration.

**Recommended order in a reply:** (1) the slot and its refuter; (2) ask what the recipient would count as a failure; (3) record that verdict; (4) only then offer the three links, with the note that the figures and not the summary are the authority.

## The two-stage offer

**Stage 1.** The residual, the bound shape, and the five refuters, with the mode label. No theory, no source links. Ask one question: what would you count as a failure at your language pair?

**Stage 2, opt-in only.** If the recipient is curious, send the figure text extract, the canonical usage rule and the formula. Their stage 1 verdict stays on record and their later agreement is never used as evidence.

**Stage 3.** Offer the formula as a method to reuse. Each user names their own ground, writes their own refuter before the run, and states the mode of what they get.

## Legal and practical note

No bulk send, no scraped personal emails, no list ranked by receptiveness. Use public submission systems, partner and volunteer routes, and public contact forms only. Unsolicited bulk outreach breaches GDPR and CAN-SPAM style rules in many jurisdictions, and the sending domain gets blacklisted. The structural failure comes first: a mass send selected for agreement produces a convention, not a check.
