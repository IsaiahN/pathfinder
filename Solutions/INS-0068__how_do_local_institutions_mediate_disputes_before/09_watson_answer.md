## QUESTION

Decide, in effect terms, what a local institution must do so that a dispute closes before it becomes a broader conflict. Option A: name an institution that works best and describe its techniques. Option B: return the shape of a seat that can close such disputes and declare the naming question open. What each leads to: A hands over a name the figures cannot derive, adds an unearned atom, and risks prestige standing in for a ground; B hands over four checkable properties, five falsifiers written in advance, and an instrument that could close the empirical part. Option B is taken here. The sought-for shape was bound as an ordinary term in `01` §0 before any conclusion was drawn.

## DOMAIN CENSUS

R by arity, symmetry and scale, then one line per domain with actors, currency and anchor, partition or union, and order by dependency.

- **R (the residual).** A claim or grievance between parties that fails to close locally. Arity: at least two parties plus a third seat; without the third there is no anchor. Symmetry: the party-to-party relation is symmetric in form and asymmetric in power, and what is contested is a threshold, the comparison bond (Fig 12 :87). Scale: one dispute in one venue to many disputes in a broader arena, a membrane crossing. Fig 9 names it a union compound, not one hard problem: a case in two conditions is not a harder case of either.
- **Domain 1, the dispute frame.** Actors: the parties. Currency: claims, threats, concessions, retaliation. Anchor: none of its own, since each party's reference is its own position and moves under pressure. Verdict: not a settling domain, it is a mirror configuration.
- **Domain 2, the local institution.** Actors: mediator, judge, council, elders, ADR or customary body. Currency: procedure, evidence, precedent, ruling, record. Anchor: the rule or fact that does not move when a party pushes. Verdict: settles R if the anchor stays reachable and the reading is not owned by a participant.
- **Domain 3, the broader conflict.** Actors: wider community, higher courts, outside actors, media. Currency: alignment, mobilisation, aggregated grievance. Anchor: none; reciprocal response is the engine. Verdict: the thing to avoid crossing into, not a settling domain.
- **Domain 4, the habitat.** Actors: kinship, markets, schools, police, overlapping identities. Currency: contact and cascade. Anchor: the ground that settles the underlying resource question. Verdict: changes what a seat can do only by changing contact.
- **Partition or union.** Union, not partition. Moore's five sources of conflict (data, relationship, value, structure, interests) can coexist in one dispute. Fig 9 :55 says a bundled problem is a union of overlapping conditions, not a list of separate ones. A single lever leaves the intersections standing.
- **Order by dependency (Fig 3).** Perception, then vocabulary, then the objective (what counts as resolution), then planning, then learn and carry. Effort spent above a broken middle link cannot be used below it.

Strain test applied: Fig 2 :31 independence versus Ostrom's embedded monitors. Re-census assigns independence to evidence and embeddedness to social proximity, so they are different axes. A live capture sub-strain remains (Fig 10 :47) and is recorded in `01` §6.3, not smoothed over.

## VERDICT (from the figures)

The shape survives; the naming question does not. An effective local seat ranges parties against a reference neither can move, keeps each dispute as its own slot, draws on evidence that does not largely overlap with the disputants', and filters at the boundary between local and broader scales while leaving a provenanced ruling. It survives its own use. The dispute frame alone, the broader conflict, and a like-venue referral chain are not settling domains and must not be averaged with the seat. Which named institution occupies the seat is a declared `gap` of the unreachable-at-this-budget kind. Capture is a live strain: an embedded monitor can turn the rule into a convention that decides outcomes. Mode `general` on the shape and `specified` on the mechanisms, ungrounded, candidate.

- **cost if wrong:** bounded and locatable if the packet is used as designed. The shape claim is mode-labelled, its refuters precede its conclusion, and its lineage is recorded, so an error is findable rather than invisible. The real cost is spending the independence of outside readers by sending a name and a conclusion instead of a shape and a refuter. Fig 2 :42 prices that: "Buy independence in proportion to the cost of being wrong." That cost is avoidable by leading with the shape claim and the five refuters. If the `gap` is quietly filled with a favourite institution, the cost is an unearned atom and a wrong verdict presented as a result.
- **undoable:** yes. No institution has been elected and no primitive withdrawn. The four properties can each be refused independently by one of the five refuters; the capture strain can be closed by a documented anchor test; the naming gap can be closed by a comparative multi-frame study. A sent claim can be superseded by a corrected packet.
- **reversed by:** a ground reading, not agreement. A source-discriminating comparison of matched disputes, across frames with no shared evidence pool, that shows whether local handling changes recurrence would settle the empirical part. A case of durable settlement with no non-moving reference would break C1. Evidence that aggregation improves per-dispute settlement would break C2. A mediator sharing the parties' evidence doing as well as an independent one would break C3. A ruling carried down from a higher level with no loss would break the membrane reading. A figure re-census that selects a named institution for a given union of conditions would close the gap. Until one of these fires, the verdict stands as candidate.
