# Outreach draft: check the shape before you decide about the theory

**Use:** one-to-one, or as a cover letter attached to a submission through the public venues in `03_public_contact_points.csv`. **Not** for bulk send.

**Hard precondition:** do not send until you have read the refuter in `08_expert_verification_packet.md` §2 and can state it in your own words. The offer collapses on contact if the stage-1 output cannot survive checking.

---

**Subject:** One checkable claim about local dispute resolution, before any theory

> Hi [name],
>
> I have one specific output I would like you to check against what you already know. I am not asking you to read a theory first, and I am not asking you to agree with anything. If the output does not survive, there is nothing else to look at.
>
> **The output (`general` on the shape, `specified` on the mechanisms, not `grounded`):** a local institution closes a dispute before it escalates only if the seat has four properties. (1) It ranges the parties against a reference neither of them can move, such as evidence, a rule, a precedent, or an impartial procedure. The figures put it plainly: *"Alignment is triangulation, not negotiation. A mirror cannot correct you."* (2) It keeps each dispute as its own slot. Aggregating grievances into "the conflict" throws away the signal about which dispute is live. (3) It is a third frame whose evidence does not largely overlap with the disputants' evidence. Two frames together reach what neither reaches alone, but two people reading the same report are one frame wearing two names. (4) It acts as a filter with a direction: what should cross upward to a higher venue is the method that settled the dispute, not a recording of that one occasion.
>
> **What this is not.** It is not a ranking of mediation, arbitration, customary councils, or restorative circles. The framework returns a shape, not a name: *"you get a kind of thing, never the answer itself."* Which named institution fits a given community is left open and declared as a gap. It is not a claim that a respected mediator's authority settles anything. Prestige can give a process a budget to be tried; it cannot close a dispute. It is also not a result of this email. This is a simulated derivation checked for internal consistency against the figures, not a ground reading, so it cannot settle the question it reports on.
>
> **What would refute the shape claim:**
> 1. a local institution that settles disputes durably by negotiation alone with no external reference the parties cannot move;
> 2. evidence that aggregating disputes into one conflict index improves the settlement of individual disputes across independent sites;
> 3. a mediator drawing on the same evidence as both parties who reduces escalation as much as an independent one;
> 4. a ruling carried back down from a higher level that reproduces new local settlements with no loss;
> 5. a reform that leaves contact with the parties unchanged yet reliably reduces escalation.
>
> I wrote these before sending this. If any one holds, I will withdraw the corresponding part. A one-line "this fails because..." is more useful to me than agreement. If you would rather not engage, no reply is needed.
>
> **If it survives, and only then:** the sources are below. The figures are the authority, not my summary of them.

Write down what would refute your result *before* you run it. ## Two-stage offer, stated explicitly

1. **Stage 1.** Send the residual, the four-property shape claim, and the five refuters. Ask one question: what would you count as a failure? Record the answer before showing anything else.
2. **Stage 2, opt-in only.** If the recipient is curious, offer the figure text and canonical rule. Their later agreement is never pooled as corroboration (Fig 2 :40).
3. **Stage 3, on request.** Offer the formula as a method to reuse, with the instruction that each user states their own ground and writes their own refuter first.

No reply is requested from anyone who declines. One message per person, sent by the human who hosts this folder. This agent sends nothing.
