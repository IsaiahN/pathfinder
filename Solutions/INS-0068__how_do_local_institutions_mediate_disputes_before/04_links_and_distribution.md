## What the wider literature contributes, and what it does not

The world sources corroborate the *shape* of the answer, not the framework's authority:

1. **An anchor the parties cannot move** is independently described in conflict studies (objective criteria, procedures outside the parties' control), law (impartial decisions on evidence), and the framework's own cognition essay (courts rule on evidence neither side can move). Three fields, one shape.
2. **Per-slot handling** is corroborated by the dispute pyramid, but the pyramid is an aggregate and so is a filter, not a witness.
3. **A low-overlap third frame** is corroborated by Ostrom's independent monitoring principles and by restorative justice's use of community circles, with the embeddedness/independence strain named in `01` §6.3.
4. **A directional membrane** is corroborated by the systems-theory description of escalation as positive feedback and by Ostrom's nested enterprises.
5. **Source discrimination, not further argument**, is what would settle the empirical question. The Hard Problem essay's spectrometer is the model: an instrument that can tell mediation from selection effects would name what argument cannot.

No world source conflicts with the figure verdict. The conflict rule was therefore not triggered. But the four Wikipedia pages form one broad conflict-studies pool on a single host, and the framework's own essay cites Ostrom, so parts of the apparent corroboration share an evidence pool. `10_source_ledger.csv` declares this per row.

## Distribution: offer, do not push

Figure-grounded reasons:

- **Fig 10 :52**: *"Bring a characterised residual, never the source."* Sending the full architecture before anyone has described a residual sends a recording rather than a generator. Lead with the four-property shape and the five refuters.
- **Fig 9 :27-28**: *"describe it before you go looking, or any frame you pick will seem to fit."* Ask what they would expect to see if the shape claim were true, before offering the institution typology.
- **Fig 10 :42**: *"A verifier that can reconstruct the claim cannot verify it."* A recipient who has absorbed the framework before giving a stage-1 verdict has joined the frame being checked.
- **Fig 2 :40**: *"agreement about the world is worthless when the agreeing frames share an evidence pool"*; later agreement is never pooled as corroboration.
- **Fig 9 :60**: *"Use filters for the budget and witnesses for the verdict. Never let a filter hand you a verdict."* A venue's size, prestige, or accessibility decides where to send, never whether the claim is true.
- **Fig 4 :22**: *"Only methods cross up. Only head starts come down."* Stage 3 sends the formula as a method, not the folder's conclusion as an answer.

**Recommended order in a reply:** (1) the shape claim and its five refuters; (2) the question "what would you count as a failure?" and their verdict, recorded before anything else; (3) only if they are curious, the local figure text and canonical rule, with the note that the figures, not the folder, are the authority; (4) on request, the formula as a reusable method with an instruction to write a fresh refuter.

## The two-stage offer, with its honest limit

1. **Stage 1:** four-property shape plus refuters. Checkable against a researcher's own knowledge of dispute resolution without holding the framework's interior.
2. **Stage 2, opt-in:** the figure text and canonical rule. Stage-1 verdict must already be recorded.
3. **Stage 3, on request:** the formula as method. Each user states their own ground and writes their own refuter.

**Hard precondition:** stages 1 to 3 only work if the stage-1 output survives. The output here is `specified` and ungrounded, with F6 left as a declared `gap`. If a recipient shows that any of the five refuters holds, the corresponding property is withdrawn before stage 2 is offered.

## Hosting and sending

`03_public_contact_points.csv` contains institutional pages only. There is no archive to upload and no list to send. The human who hosts this folder decides whether to host the packet behind an opt-in download page and sends any messages one to one. This agent hosts, uploads and sends nothing. An opt-in page lets a recipient decline contact, which is closer to the figures' rule than an attachment pushed to a list.
