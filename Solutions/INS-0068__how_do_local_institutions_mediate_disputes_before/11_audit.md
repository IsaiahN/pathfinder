Symmetry: the form is symmetric (each can update on the other) while power is asymmetric, and what is contested is a threshold, so the comparison bond is the relevant one. - **Domain 2, the local seat.** Actors: mediator, judge, council, elders, customary or ADR body. Currency: procedure, evidence, ruling, record. Anchor: a rule or fact neither party can move. Settles R if the anchor stays reachable.
- **Domain 3, the broader conflict.** Actors: wider community, higher venues, outside actors. Currency: alignment and aggregation. Anchor: none. Avoid crossing into; a like-venue chain is one long room.
- **Domain 4, the habitat.** Actors: kinship, markets, police, schools, overlapping identities. Currency: contact and cascade. Anchor: the ground settling the underlying resource question.

Order by dependency: establish what happened, make affordances nameable, bind what counts as resolution, then plan, then carry. Refuse a captured rule before minting a new procedure (Formula step 2). Conversely, one thing survives cleanly: a structure must stand between the parties and something they cannot move, and that structure must not be a mirror of the parties. That is the residue worth sending. R_T was **not** measured numerically, because no ground reading exists; it is recorded qualitatively as the enumerated set of dropped constraints.
