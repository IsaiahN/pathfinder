## 1. A shape is a constraint, not a filler

The canonical local rule: the framework returns *"what a missing thing would have to look like (a shape, not a name)"*. Fig 9 :32: *"you get a kind of thing, never the answer itself"*. Fig 13 on what it can and cannot return: it can report that something is out of reach, but *"It cannot say which primitive is missing, because naming it would mean already holding it."*

From *The Library of Babel*: *"you can cut your search short if the set of books returned is small and descriptive enough that it provides a general shape of the answer that satisfies your question."*

For this question, a **shape** is the narrowed form of a working institution that we can state before we can pick a winner. It constrains; it does not fill. The shape is: a seat holding a non-updating anchor, indexing R per dispute, drawing on a low-overlap evidence pool, filtering at the membrane, and leaving a provenanced rule. "Mediation", "arbitration", "customary council" and "restorative circle" are names. The four properties are the shape.

## 2. How the shape is obtained

- *Library of Babel*: use the vocabulary you already have that is closest to the thing, then look for recurring new terms that overlap or correlate with it; *"With each new term gained, you narrow the space by definition and deepen your vocabulary."* For this question the starting vocabulary was "dispute", "mediator", "escalation", "local"; the figure text supplied "anchor", "residual by slot", "membrane", "independent frame".
- Fig 6 :30: *"The description travels. Fails by being sharpened into terms only we can read. Cast it in effect terms."* Fig 9 :30-31: *"your words are borrowed too, so describe the effect rather than the cause."* So the residual was described as an effect (a claim that fails to close and crosses a scale boundary) before any institution was chosen (Fig 8 :53: *"Residual first, frame second."*).
- *Decoding Tacit Knowledge*: *"When contact isn't possible, the best you can send is a plain description of what you'd expect to see, because anyone can check that against the world."* This is why §4 of the outreach packet states observable failure conditions rather than theoretical claims.
- *Library of Babel* on bundling: *"Perhaps your problem was a bundle or conflation of several problems masquerading as one?"* That is exactly what the union census in `01` §1 found: legal, relational, and structural conditions overlap in one dispute.

## 3. What licenses a name

Two things, both outside the shape itself:

1. **The ground settles it.** Formula step 5: *"the ground settles it. Verified only by what pays. A gate is not the ground."* Here that would be a source-discriminating measurement of recurrence or escalation across matched disputes with and without a given institution.
2. **A provenanced import from a frame whose closure differs** (Fig 6 :23, Fig 8 :50-52), with a source gate and a shadow test. A comparative institutional study could supply this.

The *Hard Problem is About Source* essay gives the reason an argument is not enough. An instrument that detects green *"cannot comment on the source"*: one green light, or yellow plus blue, or RGB, or CMYK, or a scheme not yet discovered. *"If several paths create the same behavior, then behavior is a projection with lossy information."* It was *"the spectrometer, not an argument"* that resolved it. Applied here: "no escalation" is a reading, not a source. It does not by itself tell you whether the institution caused it or whether the dispute would have cooled anyway. What is owed is a discriminator, not another name.

## 4. Correction to my own reading of §1 (kept visible)

I initially read *"shape, not a name"* as forbidding the mention of any specific institution at all. That is too strong. The canonical phrase governs a **missing** thing, a primitive not held in Γ. Naming an institution that is already in the frame's vocabulary is a **binding** from the world onto held terms, not the invention of a new primitive. What remains forbidden is (a) claiming the framework *derives* which institution wins, (b) claiming a binding moves the wall when it adds no atom (Fig 6: *"IMPORT adds an atom"*), and (c) presenting the name as settled by the ground when it is not. So `01` F6 is a `gap` about *selection*, not a ban on *mentioning* mediation or arbitration. Both the original overstatement and this correction remain on record.

## 5. "Local" and "broader" are scales, not kinds

Fig 4 :19-20: *"Only the actors, the currency and the anchor change."* The same loop runs at the scale of one dispute and at the scale of a region; what changes is the cast and the anchor. Fig 10 :68: *"Different actors, a different currency, a different ground, and a lossy transform between."*

This matters for the question because "local institution" is not a natural kind. It is whatever seat happens to hold the anchor at the lower scale. Ostrom's eighth design principle, *"multiple layers of nested enterprises, with small, local CPRs at their bases"*, is a world-source description of the same scale rule. Fig 12 :104: *"A settled molecule becomes an atom for whatever composes over it. So irreducible is a fact about a level and never about the world."* A small local body that has settled a dispute becomes an operand for the next level precisely because it settled; an unsettled one does not.

## 6. Prestige is not action

*The Recursive Self-Improvement Trap*: *"Prestige must never be allowed to validate an action directly"*; *"Prestige can fund an action. It can give an action a budget to be tried. But it cannot settle an action. Only the ground can settle it. The Ground Maintainer must be blind to prestige."*

A well-regarded mediator, a famous court, or a long list of endorsees gives a process a budget to be tried. It is not evidence that a dispute closed. The corresponding drift-stoppers in *Decoding Tacit Knowledge* are: *"A ground that both are corrected by, separately"*, *"Separate experience"*, and claims in effect terms. The essay notes that *"Courts of law rule on evidence, which neither side can move"*, which is the same anchor as Fig 2 :29 under a different name.

This governs the outreach order in `02_outreach_email.md`: the refuter comes first, agreement is never requested, and later agreement is never pooled as corroboration (Fig 2 :40).

## 7. This file, and the whole folder, is a simulation

*The Recursive Self-Improvement Trap*: *"LLMs can quickly simulate an approximate answer/output with the formula... However, the truly testable output would need to be derived from the real architecture or working the formula yourself."* Also: *"Metatheories cannot validate themselves"* and *"You need the seed and the ground."*

So `01_figure_grounded_audit.md` is a simulated derivation checked for internal consistency against the figures. It is **not** a test of whether local institutions actually prevent escalation, and it must be labelled as such. By its own rule it cannot settle the question it reports on. The honest stop is in `07_the_tether_answer.md` §6.
