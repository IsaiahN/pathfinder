## What is deliberately not here

- No personal emails of named individuals.
- No ranking of people by how likely they are to agree with the claim.
- No bulk-send plan.

Three reasons, in the figures' own words:

1. **A room does not upgrade the label.** "A room reaching a conclusion without the ground is agreement, and agreement among frames is a weighted average of influence. Adding people raises the vocabulary and does not add an anchor." (Seat Map :283) Selecting recipients for receptiveness manufactures the correlation that would make their agreement worthless (Seat Map :163).
2. **Asking people to trust before they check spends their independence.** "A verifier that can reconstruct the claim cannot verify it." (Fig 10 :42) The outside frame must not hold the framework's interior.
3. **Prestige is not action.** "Prestige can fund an action. It can give an action a budget to be tried. But it cannot settle an action. Only the ground can settle it." (*The Recursive Self-Improvement Trap*, opened 2026-11-23) A campaign that gathers prestigious names to agree is the named collapse, not corroboration.

Legally: collecting personal emails for unsolicited bulk outreach breaches GDPR and CAN-SPAM style rules in many jurisdictions. Public opt-in channels avoid that failure as well as the structural one.
