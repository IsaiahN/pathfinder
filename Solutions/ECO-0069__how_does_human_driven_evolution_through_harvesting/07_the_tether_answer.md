# The Tether's answer: how human-driven evolution alters ecosystem relationships

**Mode: `general` on the shape, `specified` on the three named pressures. Not `grounded`.** This is a shape and a route, not a result. Simulated derivation checked against the figures (`05_what_shape_means.md` §7), not a ground reading.

## 1. The residual it answers

A human seat acts on a habitat (harvest, contaminant, substitute habitat). The residual `R` is the gap between what the current library predicts about relationships and what actually persists and reproduces, indexed per species, per interaction and per place (Fig 1 :19). The question asks what that gap is made of and where it propagates. It is a union of three overlapping conditions (Fig 9 :54-57), not one hard problem and not three independent ones.

## 2. The shape

1. **This is a maintained habitat, not bare selection.** A seat exists and can act: "Maintained. A seat exists and can act: choose the instruments, distil the impactful actors, introduce what was not there." (Fig 11 :58) The unmaintained panel, where "nothing selects, and what survives is what the ground kept" (Fig 11 :56), does not govern a question that names a human driver. The framework's own essay calls this "unnatural selection" or "the steering of evolution."
2. **What alters first is the relation, not the actor list.** "An actor's valence is relative to the residual, not a property it carries." (Fig 11 :49) "The same actor is a benefactor to one residual and a malfactor to another, so mutualism and parasitism are relations rather than kinds." (Fig 11 :50) Two states with the same species and different relations are **isomers** (Fig 12 :117): "Same atoms, different bond, different substance." A sign flip is not asserted in every case; magnitude-only shifts occur (Six and Klepzig 2021, opened 2026-11-23), and which happens is a measurement question.
3. **The change cascades past direct contact.** "List everything in contact with the residual, then what is in contact with those, and outward until the cascade stops mattering." (Fig 11 :44) The figure's own case: "Scoping the habitat to direct contact is how DDT reached the eagles: first-order enumeration, second-order effect, and the residual that mattered was three steps out." (Fig 11 :52) The published record corroborates the eggshell-thinning case (Bowerman et al. 1995).
4. **Habitat change is substitution, not removal.** "The seat's power is asymmetric. Introduction is available; exclusion is not." (Fig 11 :61) "Isolation is not removal of the habitat. It is substitution of one habitat for another." (Fig 11 :66) A moved, restored or fenced system inherits actors it did not choose, and fails in two silent ways: "what you failed to reproduce, and what you brought with you" (Fig 11 :29-30). Management is "permanent work rather than a solved state" (Fig 11 :63).
5. **The three pressures are a union and must be unbundled.** "A bundled problem is usually a union of overlapping conditions, not a list of separate ones." (Fig 9 :55) A single lever clears where one condition holds and leaves the intersections standing. The compound is "categorically different" (Fig 9 :57), not an average.
6. **The ground, not the target, settles it.** "a convention that decides an outcome has stopped being one" (Fig 10 :47). A yield target or recovery index is a convention. "An anchor is whatever settles the question and does not change its answer because of what any frame thinks." (Fig 2 :29) The biological ground is that anchor.
7. **Per-slot, never averaged.** "The residual is indexed by object slot, not aggregated." (Fig 1 :19) A harvest experiment found heritable change toward earlier maturation and higher fecundity in populations exposed to size-dependent harvest (Diaz Pauli et al. 2026), a per-population reading. An "average ecosystem response" would erase it.
8. **The monitoring channel can fail.** "the ground does not decay, the channel does" (Fig 10 :24); "saturated metrics, pooled readings" (Fig 10 :26). Consecutive years of the same index are one room handed along: "agreement compounds, evidence does not" (Fig 7 :13). Selection between rooms is where the ground votes (Fig 7 :35).

## 3. What it explicitly does not say

- Not a prediction of which trait, species or interaction changes in a named system. That is a `gap` (F7 in `01`), not a hidden answer. "you get a kind of thing, never the answer itself" (Fig 9 :32); "Unreachable is a verdict here, not a silence," (Fig 13 :134).
- Not that a relationship must flip sign. Fig 11 :50 governs valence across residuals; a magnitude-only shift is possible and the discriminator is owed.
- Not that the framework's habitat is an ecological niche. The canonical article refuses that mapping explicitly.
- Not that harvesting is a subtraction from the habitat. Exclusion is not available to the seat (Fig 11 :61), so the earlier reading was retracted in `01` §6.
- Not that the six-term set derives ecology. It returns a shape; a pressure is a binding from the ecology frame with provenance, not a new primitive and not an import that moves the wall.
- Not `grounded`. No measurement was taken by this derivation.

## 4. What would settle it: the ground

A source-discriminating instrument, not a further argument. From *The Hard Problem is About Source*: an instrument reading green "cannot comment on the source," and "The answer is unreached until a better instrument exists." Here the corresponding tests are:

1. **Valence test:** does the same species pair change interaction sign, or only magnitude, under a documented change of residual? Six and Klepzig (2021) found magnitude only in their system, and a different system could differ. That is a measurement, not an argument.
2. **Cascade test:** does the measured residual three links out from the point of use appear before a first-order-only enumeration predicts it? The DDT case is the template.
3. **Substitution test:** after a habitat intervention, enumerate what was failed to reproduce and what was unintentionally introduced; a substituted habitat with neither is the refuter.
4. **Compound test:** does one lever clear harvesting, pollution and habitat change with no categorically different residue? If yes, the union claim fails.
5. **Ground test:** does a declared recovery survive a reading in which the biological relations did not recover? If yes, the convention has wrongly decided the outcome.
6. **Per-slot test:** does an ecosystem-level aggregate predict every slot with no residual? If yes, F8 fails.

Standing falsifiers from `01` §4 apply in full.

## 5. The honest stop

**Unreached, not unreachable.** The specific local answer requires instruments that are "already returning something" and can be sharpened from a worse instrument (Fig 6 :34-35). Where nothing is returning anything, there is no edge to extend from. The framework can characterise the missing thing in effect terms and say it is owed, but it cannot name it, because "naming it would mean already holding it" (Fig 13 :135). The canonical figures README adds: "Indiscernibility is not identity." The lineage is recorded so the stop is checkable: which lines governed, which were strained, and what would reverse them.

## 6. One-paragraph version for outreach

> The Tether does not say which species or trait will change under harvesting, pollution or habitat change, and it does not claim the framework derives ecology. It says this is a maintained habitat, so a seat is acting and what alters first is the relation, not the species list: two states with the same actors and different bonds are isomers. The change propagates outward through contact, past the point of use, as DDT reached the eagles three steps away. Habitat change is substitution rather than removal, and the seat can introduce but cannot subtract. The three pressures form a union, so a single lever leaves the intersections standing. A yield target is a convention, not the ground; the ground is what actually persists and reproduces, read per slot rather than averaged. What the framework offers is that shape, four refuters written in advance, and the instruments that would close it. The local answer stays open until the ground settles it.
