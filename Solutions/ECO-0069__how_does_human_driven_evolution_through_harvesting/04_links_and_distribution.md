## Distribution: offer, do not push

Figure-grounded reasons:

- **Fig 4 :22**: "Only methods cross up. Only head starts come down. A recording carried upward looks like knowledge and is a description of one occasion." Sending the full architecture before anyone has described a residual sends a recording. Lead with the shape claim and its refuter.
- **Fig 10 :42**: "A verifier that can reconstruct the claim cannot verify it." A reader who has absorbed the framework before being asked for a verdict has joined the frame being checked.
- **Fig 9 :27-28**: "describe it before you go looking, or any frame you pick will seem to fit." Ask what they would expect to see before giving them the article.
- **Seat Map :163**: "High correlation is disqualifying for a verdict and efficient for a convention." A send selected for people who already agree produces a convention, not corroboration.

**Recommended order in a reply:** (1) the shape claim and its four refuters; (2) ask what they would count as a failure in their system; (3) record their verdict; (4) only then the framework links, with the note that the figures, not the summary, are the authority.

## The two-stage offer (and an optional third)

1. **Stage 1, before any theory:** send the shape claim, the mode labels and the pre-registered refuter. Ask for a refutation, not agreement.
2. **Stage 2, opt-in only:** if the recipient is curious after recording a verdict, offer the figure links. Agreement expressed after seeing the framework is never pooled as corroboration (Fig 2 :40: "agreement about the world is worthless when the agreeing frames share an evidence pool").
3. **Stage 3, a method to reuse:** the formula is a generator, so giving it away does not give away an answer (Fig 4 :22). Each user must state their own ground, their own mode and their own refuter before running it, because a frame "cannot check the direction of its own question" (`THE_FORMULA.md`, Direction).

**Hard precondition:** stages 1 to 3 only work if the stage-1 shape survives. The four refuters in `02_outreach_email.md` were written before this distribution plan, and the corrections in `01` §6 are applied, so the shape is in its checkable form. If a refuter fires, stop sending.
