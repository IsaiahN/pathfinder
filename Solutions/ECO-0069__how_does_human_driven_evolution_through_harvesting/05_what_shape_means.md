## 1. A shape is a constraint, not a filler

The canonical figures README states the limit: the framework returns "what a missing thing would have to look like (a shape, not a name)." Fig 9 :32: "you get a kind of thing, never the answer itself." Fig 13 :134: "Unreachable is a verdict here, not a silence,"

From *The Library of Babel*: "you can cut your search short if the set of books returned is small and descriptive enough that it provides a general shape of the answer that satisfies your question."

So a **shape** is the narrowed form of an answer you can state before you can name it. It constrains; it does not fill. For this question:

- shape: "a seat acts on a habitat, the residual moves, valences are re-typed relative to it, the change cascades outward through contact"
- name: "in the Cod stock, size-selective harvest selected for maturation at smaller size by 1984, with a causal estimate of X"

The first is returned by the figures. The second requires a ground reading the figures do not contain.

## 2. How a shape is obtained

- *Library of Babel*: use the vocabulary you already have that is closest to the thing and look for recurring new terms that overlap or correlate with it. "With each new term gained, you narrow the space and deepen your vocabulary."
- *Library of Babel* on bundles, directly relevant here: "Perhaps your problem was a bundle or conflation of several problems masquerading as one? You will need to search all of those problems individually to find their answers and then combine them in a satisfactory way." This is Fig 9 :54-57 in narrative form, and it is why harvesting, pollution and habitat change are unbundled in `01` §1.
- Fig 6 :30: "The description travels. Fails by being sharpened into terms only we can read. Cast it in effect terms." Fig 9 :30-31: "your words are borrowed too, so describe the effect rather than the cause."
- *Decoding Tacit Knowledge*: "When contact isn't possible, the best you can send is a plain description of what you'd expect to see, because anyone can check that against the world."
- *Library of Babel* on stopping: "You will know that you are done because the finish line will also be the starting place, as there is nowhere else to search and nothing else that can describe the answer better."

## 3. What licenses a name

Only two things, both outside the shape itself:

1. **The ground settles it.** Formula step 5: "the ground settles it. Verified only by what pays. A gate is not the ground." The canonical README says the framework "stops at the limit of what the available instruments can distinguish, and records the lineage so the stop is honest."
2. **A provenanced import from a frame whose closure differs** (Fig 6 :23, Fig 8 :51-52), with a source gate and a shadow test.

*They're Scanning Books* sharpens the import case: "The real import is not a new term in the existing space. It is a new dimension of the space." And: "A minimum viable set of six terms is a human's careful guess at reality's compression axes; another sensorium's minimum set need not be those six." Also: "Declaring the primitives does not make them neutral. It makes the choice visible."

For ecology this matters directly. A "three-pressure model" of human-driven evolution is a visible choice of axes, not nature's own list. Naming one pressure as *the* cause of a relationship change turns a visible choice into an invisible one.

## 4. Why a reading of the artifact is not a reading of the source

*The Hard Problem is About Source* gives the mechanism. An instrument reads green and "cannot comment on the source." It could be one green light, or yellow plus blue, or RGB, or CMYK, or a scheme not yet discovered. "If several paths create the same behavior, then behavior is a projection with lossy information." And "The answer is unreached until a better instrument exists." It was "proven wrong not by argument, but by the arrival of a better instrument."

Applied here: a decline in a predator population could be caused by harvesting of its prey, by a contaminant biomagnifying up the food chain, by substitution of its habitat, or by migration. The abundance reading cannot comment on which source acted. What is owed is a source-discriminating instrument, not a confident name. This is the reason F7 in `01` is a declared gap.

*They're Scanning Books* adds the limits of instruments themselves: "Every instrument is a crystallized hypothesis about what is worth measuring, and the hypothesis comes from human awareness." So even the time series that would close the gap is built along chosen axes.

## 5. What the essays say specifically about human-driven evolution

This is unusual: two of the eight essays speak to the question almost directly.

- *The Recursive Self-Improvement Trap*: "Natural selection is what the loop looks like when there is no one maintaining the ground. When there is no one in the Ground Maintainer seat, the environment is the only selector." It continues: "The same code runs when there is a Ground Maintainer. It runs in cultivation, when humans select which plants to breed. It runs in habitat formation, when beavers build dams and change the selection pressures."
- *How Intelligence Actually Works*: "In order to steer evolution of a thing, you need to control what becomes the ground for the composer to range against. The 'Steering of Evolution' is unnatural selection." And the caution: "when altering a habitat by unintentionally introducing a predatory threat into the ecosystem, or disturbing the balance by adding or removing vital resource points, you can inadvertently create major unpredictable consequences downstream."
- *How Intelligence Actually Works* on why the record matters: "indiscernibility is not identity. The projection that no instrument distinguishes from the thing is still a projection, which is why the lineage has to be kept even when the copy is perfect."

These are framework-internal, same author as the figures, so they carry no independent corroboration weight. They establish the intended canonical reading, not a second witness.

## 6. Correction to my own §3 and §4

I initially wrote that "shape, not a name" forbids naming any specific pressure or actor as the cause here. That overstates the rule. The canonical phrase is "what a **missing** thing would have to look like (a shape, not a name)." Its subject is a primitive or atom not held in the library.

Harvesting, pollution and habitat change are not missing primitives in the framework's sense. They are bindings from the ecology frame onto actors and actions already available to the habitat description, and the figure's habitat rule explicitly permits introduction. So naming them is a **binding with provenance**, not the invention of a new atom and not a breach of the shape rule. What remains wrong is claiming that such a binding is *derived* from the six-term set, or that it moves the wall (Fig 6: IMPORT "adds an atom"; this one adds none).

My §4 metamerism point is **not retracted**. The reason is the missing discriminator, not a ban on naming held things. Both claims are kept so neither is quietly lost. A second correction is recorded in `01` §6: the canonical article refuses the habitat-as-niche mapping, so "habitat" here always means the field of contact with R, enumerated, never the ecological niche.

## 7. Two consequences for this solution

**Prestige is not action.** From *The Recursive Self-Improvement Trap*: "Prestige can fund an action. It can give an action a budget to be tried. But it cannot settle an action. Only the ground can settle it. The Ground Maintainer must be blind to prestige." A campaign that gathers authoritative names to agree with the shape is the named collapse, not corroboration. The legitimate ask gives the claim a budget to be tried and asks what would refute it.

**This audit is a simulation, not a ground reading.** Same essay: "LLMs can quickly simulate an approximate answer/output with the formula found in the architecture post... However, the truly testable output would need to be derived from the real architecture or working the formula yourself." The canonical article's demo section calls such outputs "simulacra" and warns they "could never be verified if the solutions presented were not contrived to fit the formula." So `01`, `06`, `07` and `09` are simulated derivations checked for internal consistency against the figures. They are **not** tests of the claim and must be labelled as such. They cannot settle anything they report on.
