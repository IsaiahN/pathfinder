## QUESTION

Decide, in effect terms, what the Tether says about the distribution of risk, credit and economic power across society. Option A: treat the question as one aggregate quantity and name a policy as the answer. Option B: unbundle it into three overlapping domains, keep their verdicts separate, return the shape each must have, and declare the numerical distribution a gap. What each leads to: A produces a pooled reading that erases the live signal, a named binding with no source gate, and agreement selected for receptiveness; B produces per-slot predictions, six refuters written in advance, and an honest characterisation of the instrument that would close the gap.

## DOMAIN CENSUS

R by arity, symmetry and scale, then one line per domain with actors, currency and anchor, union or partition, and dependency order.

- **R (the residual).** The gap between the neutral-pipe prediction (savers' funds pass to borrowers; risk stays with the saver) and the observed allocation. Arity: three overlapping conditions, so a union compound, not one hard question. Symmetry: transfers are asymmetric (creditor versus debtor, seller versus bearer of risk, setter versus taker of terms), so the carrying bonds are ordered and comparative rather than symmetric. Scale: single contract to system-wide distribution, separated by a lossy membrane.
- **D1 Credit creation and allocation.** Actors: commercial banks, borrowers, depositors, central bank. Currency: credit and debt, a claim on future repayment. Anchor: the balance sheet and repayment record, a counter or market. Admitted.
- **D2 Risk transfer and concentration.** Actors: banks, insurers, investors, rating agencies, taxpayers. Currency: risk as probability times loss. Anchor: defaults, realised losses, bailout payments, market prices. Admitted.
- **D3 Economic power and concentration.** Actors: financial institutions, regulators, households, firms. Currency: capacity to set or refuse terms. Anchor: measurable concentrations of market share, lending by neighbourhood, wealth and political contributions. Admitted, with a proxied and weaker anchor.
- **D4 Verification and outreach.** Actors: expert readers. Currency: residual versus agreement. Anchor: the ground, never agreement. In this simulated run all seats draw on one evidence pool, so independence is zero.
- **X1 Power as a free-floating property.** No nameable anchor apart from its effects, so not a domain yet; absorbed into D3 relations.
- **Union, not partition.** D1, D2 and D3 overlap. A disclosure lever clears the region where only one condition holds and leaves the compounds standing. Dependency order: D1, then D2, then D3. Describe the residual before choosing a frame; refuse before mint.

Strain test applied: Fig 11 :61 "The seat's power is asymmetric. Introduction is available; exclusion is not" first appeared to conflict with credit denial. Re-census resolved it: the line governs the habitat, not access to a ledger product. No strain remains. Recorded in `01_figure_grounded_audit.md` section 1 and section 6.

## VERDICT (from the figures)

The shape survives, the numbers and the normative ranking do not. Credit is a minted arrangement over a closed generator, so a new instrument is a new bond rather than a new atom. Loss reaches third-order slots through cascading contact and settled claims becoming operands. A rating that decides an outcome and is paid for by the party it rates is a disqualified keeper, not an anchor. Standing to set terms accumulates through closure contact, and an institution's valence flips with the residual. Every allocation is per slot; the aggregate average erases the live signal. These are separate domain verdicts, `general` on the structural shape and `specified` on the institutional mapping, with no `grounded` claim. The empirical distribution is a declared gap.

- **cost if wrong:** a misdiagnosed distribution. A single lever, for example more disclosure, would be applied to a union that needs three loops, leaving the compounds standing while appearing to act. The second cost is spending the independence of outside readers by sending a recording and a policy name before the shape and its refuters. Fig 2 :42 "Buy independence in proportion to the cost of being wrong." Both costs are bounded and avoidable by leading with the refuters.
- **undoable:** yes. No term is deleted if a finding fails; the refutation is added and the reach falls because something was added rather than taken away. The retraction and confirmation log in `01_figure_grounded_audit.md` section 6 keeps each earlier reading visible so a later reader can locate the error. A sent packet can be superseded by a corrected one.
- **reversed by:** any of the six pre-registered falsifiers, in particular (1) one aggregate statistic capturing the whole distribution with no live per-slot residual, (2) losses stopping at first-order counterparties, (3) a rating that keeps its discriminating power under issuer-pays, (4) an instrument needing a genuinely new primitive, (5) standing growing with no change in closure contact, or (6) tranching changing no loss allocation. A source-discriminating per-slot instrument that returned stable readings under pressure would close the gap and move the empirical part toward `grounded`. Agreement, prestige or a larger sample of like-minded readers reverses nothing.
