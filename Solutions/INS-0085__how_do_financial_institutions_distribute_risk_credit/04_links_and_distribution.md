## Two things in the world sources that bear directly on this answer

1. **The money-creation result is a mint, not a new primitive.** The Bank of England article says banks create the deposit when they lend. Under Fig 6 :21 "MINT composes inside the closure and can never add an atom," this is a composition inside the bank's library. Do not present it as an import that moves the wall; that reading was retracted in `01_figure_grounded_audit.md` section 6.
2. **The rating agency is a keeper with a stake.** The credit rating agency article's issuer-pays finding is an instance of Fig 10 :93 "A ground with a reputation to protect is a ground someone benefits from, and its keeper is thereby disqualified." Because that source and Goodhart's law draw on the same broad economics pool, they are one witness for the mechanism, not two (Fig 2 :40).

## Distribution: offer, do not push

Figure-grounded reasons:

- **Fig 10 :52 "Bring a characterised residual, never the source."** Sending the full framework before anyone has described the per-slot residual sends a recording, not a generator. Lead with the shape claim and its refuter; offer links after.
- **Fig 4 :25 "A recording carried upward looks like knowledge and is a description of one occasion."** A catalogue of finance anecdotes is a description of occasions. The generator is the census plus the falsifiers.
- **Fig 2 :35 "Alignment is triangulation, not negotiation. A mirror cannot correct you."** Do not ask for agreement. Ask what the recipient would count as a failure.
- **Fig 2 :42 "Buy independence in proportion to the cost of being wrong."** The cost here is a misdiagnosed distribution, so checkers should come from at least two different closures (for example, a financial economist and a data regulator), and their later agreement is never pooled as corroboration.

**Recommended order in a reply:** (1) the shape claim and its six refuters; (2) ask what they would count as a failure and which instrument would resolve the per-slot allocation; (3) only then the public figure link and the Bank of England article, with the note that the figures, not this summary, are the authority.

## The two-stage offer

- **Stage 1:** the residual, the shape and the pre-registered refuter. The recipient's verdict is recorded before any theory is shown.
- **Stage 2, opt in only:** the three public links (figure text, money-creation article, one institutional venue relevant to their field).
- **Stage 3:** the formula as a method to reuse, with the instruction that each user writes their own refuter and states their own mode.

**Hard precondition:** stages 1 to 3 only work if the stage-1 shape survives. The six refuters in `01_figure_grounded_audit.md` section 4 are the test. If one fires, the corresponding domain finding is withdrawn before anything is sent.
