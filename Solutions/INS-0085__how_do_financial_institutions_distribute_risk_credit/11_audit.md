## Round trip gap R_T

The Symbols table defines R_T as the gap between what was sent up and what came back down, `gap(x, (T_E ∘ T_A)(x))`, measuring what the coarser description cannot hold. Fig 4 :30 requires it to be measured rather than assumed zero. I ran the round trip on the condensation this folder actually uses.

1. **Sent up (x):** the verbatim figure set, chiefly Fig 1 :19-22 (per-slot indexing), Fig 6 :21 and Fig 13 :115 (mint inside closure), Fig 11 :52 (cascade), Fig 10 :47 and :93 (disqualified keeper).
2. **Abstraction (T_A):** the shorthand carried in `07_the_tether_answer.md`: distribution is per slot; credit is a minted arrangement; loss travels to third order; a score that decides an outcome is not a measure; power accumulates through closure contact.
3. **Concretisation (T_E):** a concrete claim someone could act on, for example "the 2008 crisis was caused by rating agency incentives alone, so fixing issuer-pays will fix distribution."

**R_T is nonzero.** Four things the shorthand holds do not survive the trip back down:

- the **union structure**. The shorthand does not carry that three loops are required; the concrete claim uses one lever, which is the failure Fig 9 :57 names ("a case in two conditions is not a harder case of either");
- the **per-slot indexing**. The concrete claim speaks of a system-wide distribution, while Fig 1 :19 requires a slot index;
- the **cascade order**. The concrete claim stops at the rater, while Fig 11 :52 requires tracing to the third-order bearer;
- the **ground independence condition**. The concrete claim treats a policy change as if it settled the question, while Fig 2 :29 requires an anchor that does not update with the measured party.

Conversely, one thing survives cleanly: no new primitive is needed to explain a financial instrument, because the arrangement is over claims already held. That is the residue worth sending. R_T was **not** measured numerically, because no per-slot ground reading exists; it is recorded qualitatively as the enumerated set of dropped constraints. Treating "the formula is the condensed version of the metalaws" as identity would set R_T to zero and would be the dead-reckoning error Fig 4 :30 names. The honest form is a lossy condensation with a measured gap, which is what `09_watson_answer.md` states.
