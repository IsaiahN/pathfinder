# What "shape" means for this question

Canonical reading of "shape" from the eight essays, applied to INS-0085. Sources read: the eight essays by Isaiah N (2026-08 to 2026-11), listed in `10_source_ledger.csv`. Two were re-fetched and verified this session (2026-11-23): *The Recursive Self-Improvement Trap* and *The Hard Problem is About Source*. The canonical verbatim extracts for the others are carried in the imported exemplar (`QMGR-0037/05_what_shape_means.md`) and are attributed here rather than presented as fresh reads. They are framework-internal essays, so their agreement with the figures is expected and carries no independent corroboration weight (Fig 2 :40 "disqualifying for a verdict: agreement about the world is worthless when the agreeing frames share an evidence pool").

## 1. A shape is a constraint, not a filler

The canonical figures README states the limit: the framework returns "what a missing thing would have to look like (a shape, not a name)." Fig 9 :32 "you get a kind of thing, never the answer itself." Fig 13 :135 "So the system can report that it lacks something rather than producing an answer anyway. Unreachable is a verdict here, not a silence,"

From *The Library of Babel*: "You can cut your search short if the set of books returned is small and descriptive enough that it provides a general shape of the answer that satisfies your question."

For this question, a **shape** is the narrowed form of an answer we can state before we can name the policy or the number. It says: distribution is per slot; credit is a minted arrangement over a closed generator; loss cascades to third order; a score that decides an outcome stops being a measure; and power accumulates through closure contact. It does not say "deregulation did it" or "the top 1 percent holds X percent." Those are a name and a number.

`per-slot allocation with cascading contact and a non-updating ground` is a shape. `neoliberal financialization since 1980` is a name.

## 2. How a shape is obtained

- *Library of Babel*: use the vocabulary already closest to the thing, look for recurring new terms that overlap with it, recurse. "With each new term gained, you narrow the space and deepen your vocabulary."
- Fig 6 :30 "outward: does another frame already hold it? The description travels. Fails by being sharpened into terms only we can read. Cast it in effect terms." Fig 9 :30 "your words are borrowed too, so describe the effect" and :31 "rather than the cause".
- *Decoding Tacit Knowledge*: "When contact isn't possible, the best you can send is a plain description of what you'd expect to see, because anyone can check that against the world."
- *Library of Babel* on stopping: the answer is reached when "the finish line will also be the starting place" and the searcher has "composed the answer through the process of locating it."

Applied here: the residual was stated in effect terms (who receives a claim, who bears the loss, who sets the terms, per slot) before banking or economics was chosen as a frame. That order is Fig 9 :27 "describe it before you go looking, or any frame you pick will seem to fit."

## 3. What licenses a name

Only two things, both outside the shape itself:

1. **The ground settles it.** Fig 2 :29 "An anchor is whatever settles the question and does not change its answer because of what any frame thinks. A measurement, an experiment, a counter, a market, a patient outcome." For this question the candidate anchors are repayment records, default records, HMDA and CRA lending data and wealth surveys. They are anchors only if they do not move when the measured institution pushes on them.
2. **A provenanced import from a frame whose closure differs** (Fig 6 :23, Fig 8 :50), with a source gate and a shadow test.

*They're Scanning Books* sharpens this: "The real import is not a new term in the existing space. It is a new dimension of the space." And: "A minimum viable set of six terms is a human's careful guess at reality's compression axes; another sensorium's minimum set need not be those six." Also: "Declaring the primitives does not make them neutral. It makes the choice visible."

The primitive claims in a bank's library are a visible choice of vocabulary, not nature's own list. Calling one of them "the" cause of a distribution turns a visible choice into an invisible one.

## 4. Why naming a policy is the specific forbidden error here

*The Hard Problem is About Source* (re-fetched this session) gives the mechanism. An instrument reads green and "cannot comment on the source"; one green light, or yellow plus blue, or RGB, or CMYK, or a scheme not yet discovered can give the same reading. "If several paths create the same behavior, then behavior is a projection with lossy information." The resolution was not an argument but "the arrival of a better instrument": "the answer is unreached until a better instrument exists."

Our case: a measured rise in aggregate credit is a reading. It does not disclose its source. It could come from new lending creation, from a reallocation of existing saving, from a change in the scoring threshold, or from a mix of these. Naming one source from the aggregate reading is the metamerism error. What is owed is a source-discriminating instrument, not a policy name.

*The Recursive Self-Improvement Trap* (re-fetched this session): "the output is only as good as the validator. The agent, or the tool, or the frame, or the theory, cannot validate itself."

## 5. Correction to my own reading of "shape, not a name"

I initially treated "shape, not a name" as forbidding any mention of banks, ratings or credit instruments in the answer. That was too strong. The canonical phrase governs a **missing** primitive, one not held in Γ. The institutional actors here come from the world frame, not from the framework's six-term substrate, so naming them is a binding with provenance, exactly as the exemplar's §5b narrows the rule for Gradient and Spectrum. What remains forbidden is claiming that the framework derives a specific named policy or a specific numerical distribution. Both claims are recorded so neither is quietly lost.

A second correction: I first read "distribution" as a single system-wide quantity. Fig 1 :19 "The residual is indexed by object slot, not aggregated." and :22 "Aggregating across slots is how a live signal disappears into an average." force the plural reading. The first reading is retracted in `01_figure_grounded_audit.md` section 6.

## 6. Two consequences for this solution

**Prestige is not action.** From *The Recursive Self-Improvement Trap* (re-fetched this session): "Prestige can fund an action. It can give an action a budget to be tried. But it cannot settle an action. Only the ground can settle it. The Ground Maintainer must be blind to prestige." A campaign that gathers famous economists to agree with the shape is the named collapse, not corroboration. The legitimate ask is a budget to try the check, with the ground deciding.

**My own audit is a simulation, not a ground reading.** Same essay: "LLMs can quickly simulate an approximate answer/output with the formula" and "the truly testable output would need to be derived from the real architecture or working the formula yourself." So `01_figure_grounded_audit.md` is a simulated derivation checked for internal consistency against the figures. It is not a test of the claim and must be labelled as such. It cannot settle anything it reports on.
