# The Tether's answer on the distribution of risk, credit and economic power

**Mode: `general` on the structural shape, `specified` on the institutional mapping, not `grounded`.** This is a shape and a route, not a result. Simulated derivation checked against the figures (`05_what_shape_means.md` section 6), not a ground reading.

## 1. The residual it answers

The neutral-pipe picture predicts that banks pass savers' funds to borrowers and that risk stays with the saver. What is observed is different: lending creates the deposit, loss cascades through interlinkages and the capacity to set terms concentrates. R is the gap between prediction and observation, indexed per household, firm, neighbourhood and nation. Fig 1 :19 "The residual is indexed by object slot, not aggregated." The question is a union of three overlapping conditions, not one hard question (Fig 9 :55 "A bundled problem is usually a union of overlapping conditions, not a list of separate ones."), so each domain keeps its own verdict.

## 2. The shape, per domain, never averaged

**D1, credit creation and allocation.** A loan composes primitive claims inside the bank's library and the deposit appears with it. This is a mint, not an import that moves the wall. Fig 6 :21 "MINT composes inside the closure and can never add an atom." Fig 13 :115 "The set is closed. The arrangements are not. That gap is why import exists." Derivatives and securitisations are further arrangements over the same claims; Fig 8 :32 "Closure is idempotent. Composing inside a closed set never leaves it, so no search, however long, adds an atom. This one is definitional: it is what closure means." Triangulated across Fig 6 and Fig 13, with the Bank of England money-creation record as a secondary institutional fact. Verdict `general` on closure idempotence, `specified` on the banking binding.

**D2, risk transfer and concentration.** The loss that matters lands beyond the direct counterparty. Fig 11 :52 "And contact cascades. Scoping the habitat to direct contact is how DDT reached the eagles: first-order enumeration, second-order effect, and the residual that mattered was three steps out." Fig 12 :104 "A settled molecule becomes an atom for whatever composes over it. So irreducible is a fact about a level and never about the world, and there is no bottom" explains why a repaid or defaulted claim becomes an operand at the next level. And a score that decides an outcome stops being a measure: Fig 10 :47 "a convention that decides an outcome has stopped being one." Fig 10 :93 "A ground with a reputation to protect is a ground someone benefits from, and its keeper is thereby disqualified." Triangulated across Fig 11 and Fig 10, and corroborated by the systemic-risk and rating-agency records. Verdict `specified`.

**D3, economic power and concentration.** Standing to set terms grows through contact with a differing closure, not by improving an isolated institution. Fig 6 :23 "No frame escapes its own closure. The bound moves only on contact with a frame whose closure differs." The same institution changes valence with the residual: Fig 11 :49 "An actor's valence is relative to the residual, not a property it carries." Corroborated by the financialization and redlining records. Verdict `specified`.

**D4, verification.** Fig 2 :35 "Alignment is triangulation, not negotiation. A mirror cannot correct you." Fig 2 :42 "Buy independence in proportion to the cost of being wrong." Agreement among institutions is not evidence; a check is. In this simulated run all seats draw on one evidence pool, so any internal agreement is one pool talking. Verdict `general`.

**Structure across domains.** Fig 1 :22 "Aggregating across slots is how a live signal disappears into an average." A system-wide average is not the distribution. Fig 12 :117 "isomer. Same atoms, different bond, different substance. The difference is invisible in the formula and decisive in the behaviour." A held pool and a tranched pool share atoms and differ in substance; worked out in `06_isomers_and_order.md`.

## 3. What it explicitly does not say

- Not a policy name. Fig 9 :32 "you get a kind of thing, never the answer itself." The framework does not derive deregulation, financialization or any administration as the answer. Such a name is a binding from the world frame with its own provenance.
- Not a numerical distribution. No figure line yields the share of credit, loss or power in any household, firm or nation.
- Not a normative verdict. The figures constrain the shape of an empirical answer; they do not rank that answer as just or unjust.
- Not `grounded`. No measurement at the per-slot, per-step level has been taken in this derivation.
- Not a claim that institutional actors are members of the six-term physical substrate. That mapping would be a strong binding with no source gate, and it is not made here.

## 4. What would settle it

An instrument that resolves, per slot and per step of the cascade, who received a claim, who bore the loss and who could set the terms, and whose reading does not move when the measured institution pushes on it. Fig 2 :29 "An anchor is whatever settles the question and does not change its answer because of what any frame thinks. A measurement, an experiment, a counter, a market, a patient outcome." Candidate instruments are the public HMDA and CRA records, balance-sheet data, default records and rating histories; they become anchors only when shown independent of the measured party.

Falsifiers, pre-registered in `01_figure_grounded_audit.md` section 4: no live per-slot residual; no cascade beyond first order; a rating that keeps its discriminating power under issuer-pays; an instrument needing a new primitive; standing that grows with no change in closure contact; tranching that changes no loss allocation.

## 5. The honest stop

The empirical distribution is unreached at this budget and with these instruments, not underivable in principle. Fig 8 :39 "So a search that finds nothing is never a proof of absence." Fig 13 :135 "So the system can report that it lacks something rather than producing an answer anyway. Unreachable is a verdict here, not a silence," The missing instrument is characterised in effect terms in `01_figure_grounded_audit.md` F9. Importing a new theoretical primitive against a budget limit would add an atom that was not needed (Fig 4 :30 "So take the round trip and measure the gap. Without it you are navigating by dead reckoning: confident about a position nobody checked."). The lineage is recorded so the stop is checkable.

## 6. One-paragraph version for outreach

> The Tether does not name a policy and does not produce the numbers. It says that the question is three overlapping questions, not one: credit is created inside a bank's library as a new arrangement over old claims, so no new primitive is needed to explain a derivative; loss travels beyond the direct counterparty and lands three steps out, so a first-order map of lenders and borrowers misses the loss that matters; and the capacity to set terms accumulates through contact between a regulator's closure and an industry's closure, while a rating that decides an outcome and is paid for by the party it rates stops being a measure. Each of these has to be read per household, firm and neighbourhood, because an aggregate average erases the live signal. What the framework offers is that shape, six refuters written in advance, and a characterisation of the source-discriminating instrument that would close it. The empirical distribution stays open until the ground settles it.
