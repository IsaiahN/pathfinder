# Expert verification packet: the Tether on financial institutions and distribution

**What this is:** a claim in a form a financial economist, regulator or data specialist can check. Mode, derivation and refuters are stated before the conclusion, so a reader knows what to look for and what would settle it.

**Mode: `general` on the structural shape, `specified` on the institutional mapping, not `grounded`.** No per-slot measurement is claimed. This is a shape and a route, not a result. It is a simulated derivation checked for internal consistency against the figures (`05_what_shape_means.md` section 6), not a ground reading.

## 1. The claim, in one paragraph

The question is a union of three overlapping conditions, not one hard problem. Credit is a minted arrangement over a closed generator of primitive claims, so a new financial instrument is a new bond, not a new atom. Risk reaches third-order slots through cascading contact; a settled claim becomes an operand at the next level, which is why a first-order enumeration of counterparties misses the final loss. A rating or score stops being a measure once it decides an outcome and its keeper has a stake. Standing to set terms accumulates through contact between differing closures. Each allocation must be read per household, firm, neighbourhood and nation, because an aggregate average erases the live signal. The numerical distribution and any normative ranking are a declared gap.

## 2. Three checkable sub-claims

### C1. Credit is a mint inside closure, not a new primitive
- **Statement:** a loan composes primitive claims and creates the deposit; a derivative is a further arrangement over the same claims. No new atom is required.
- **Source, verbatim:** Fig 6 :21 "MINT composes inside the closure and can never add an atom." Also Fig 13 :115 "The set is closed. The arrangements are not. That gap is why import exists."
- **What to check:** is there any financial instrument in current practice that cannot be recomposed from promise, dated repayment, collateral, priority and issuer identity? If yes, the claim fails.
- **Refuter:** exhibit such an instrument, or show that deposits must precede loans (which also breaks the production reading in `06_isomers_and_order.md` Q1).

### C2. Loss reaches third order, and the score that decides an outcome is disqualified
- **Statement:** a loss on a loan can reach a pension beneficiary three steps away; a rating used as a regulatory threshold and paid for by the issuer is not an anchor.
- **Source, verbatim:** Fig 11 :52 "And contact cascades. Scoping the habitat to direct contact is how DDT reached the eagles: first-order enumeration, second-order effect, and the residual that mattered was three steps out." Also Fig 10 :47 "a convention that decides an outcome has stopped being one." and Fig 10 :93 "A ground with a reputation to protect is a ground someone benefits from, and its keeper is thereby disqualified."
- **What to check:** in a documented episode, trace the loss from borrower to pool to tranche holder to final bearer. Separately, ask whether the rating changed with who paid for it and whether that changed allocation.
- **Refuter:** losses demonstrably stop at first-order counterparties; or the rating keeps its discriminating power after becoming a regulatory threshold and is unaffected by commercial pressure.

### C3. The distribution is per slot, and power accumulates through closure contact
- **Statement:** a system-wide average hides the live slot; a financial institution's standing to set terms grows only through contact with another closure.
- **Source, verbatim:** Fig 1 :19 "The residual is indexed by object slot, not aggregated." Also Fig 1 :22 "Aggregating across slots is how a live signal disappears into an average." and Fig 6 :23 "No frame escapes its own closure. The bound moves only on contact with a frame whose closure differs."
- **What to check:** does a single aggregate statistic predict who got credit and who bore loss across neighbourhoods? Does political or regulatory standing rise with no lending relation, regulatory contact or dependency?
- **Refuter:** one aggregate statistic fully captures the distribution with no live per-slot residual; or standing grows with no change in contact with any other closure.

## 3. What is explicitly not claimed

- Not a policy name, a numerical distribution or a normative verdict. Fig 9 :32 "you get a kind of thing, never the answer itself."

## 5. What would settle it

A source-discriminating instrument, not a further argument. From *The Hard Problem is About Source*: an instrument that reads green "cannot comment on the source"; the spectrometer resolved it. Here:

1. A per-slot, per-step record of who received a claim, who bore the loss and who could set the terms, with a check that its reading is stable when the measured institution pushes on it.
2. A documented cascade showing whether loss reaches third-order bearers.
3. A test of whether a rating's change under issuer pressure changed any allocation.
4. An inventory test for a financial instrument that needs a new primitive.

## 6. What an expert can check in twenty minutes

1. Read Fig 1 :19-22, Fig 6 :21 and :23, Fig 10 :47 and :93, Fig 11 :52, Fig 13 :115. Eight short passages.
2. Choose one episode you know (a mortgage pool, a credit default swap, a redlining settlement) and trace one loss through it. Does it stop at first order or travel?
3. Name one instrument that would resolve the per-slot allocation and say whether it updates when the measured party pushes on it.
4. Send the refutation. A failure is more useful than an endorsement.
