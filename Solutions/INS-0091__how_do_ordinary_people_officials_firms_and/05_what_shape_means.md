## 1. A shape is a constraint, not a filler

The figures README states the limit: the framework returns *"what a missing thing would have to look like (a shape, not a name)."* Fig 9 :32: *"you get a kind of thing, never the answer itself."* Fig 13 :133-134: the system can report that it lacks something and *"Unreachable is a verdict here, not a silence"*, but *"It cannot say which primitive is missing, because naming it would mean already holding it."*

From *The Library of Babel*: *"you can cut your search short if the set of books returned is small and descriptive enough that it provides a general shape of the answer that satisfies your question."*

For this question a **shape** is the form of the mechanism that can be stated before we know which practice, in which city, at which threshold, tipped which institution. "A union of four actor classes, per-slot, with contact-changing practices selected against a ground, settling into atoms at the next stratum" is a shape. "The 2011 protest in square X caused reform Y" is a name, and it is not what the figures return.

## 2. How the shape is obtained

- *Library of Babel*: use the vocabulary closest to the thing, look for recurring new terms that correlate with it, recurse. *"With each new term gained, you narrow the space and deepen your vocabulary."*
- Fig 6 :30: *"The description travels. Fails by being sharpened into terms only we can read. Cast it in effect terms."* Fig 9 :30-31: *"your words are borrowed too, so describe the effect rather than the cause."*
- *Decoding Tacit Knowledge*: *"When contact isn't possible, the best you can send is a plain description of what you'd expect to see, because anyone can check that against the world."* This is the precise reason the outreach email sends an effect description and a refuter rather than a theory.
- *Library of Babel* on stopping: the finish line is also the starting place; by then *"you have composed the answer through the process of locating it."* For INS-0091 the stopping point is the shape plus the gap, not a named reform.

## 3. What licenses a name

Only two things, both outside the shape itself:

1. **The ground settles it.** `THE_FORMULA.md` step 5: *"the ground settles it. Verified only by what pays. A gate is not the ground."* The README: the framework *"stops at the limit of what the available instruments can distinguish, and records the lineage so the stop is honest."*
2. **A provenanced import from a frame whose closure differs** (Fig 6 :23, Fig 8 :51-52), with a source gate and a shadow test.

*The Hard Problem is About Source* sharpens the first: an instrument that reads green *"cannot comment on the source"*, and *"If several paths create the same behavior, then behavior is a projection with lossy information."* It was the spectrometer, not an argument, that resolved the case. Applied here: observing that an institution changed after a protest does not identify which path produced the change. The same reading is consistent with the protest changing contact, with an official rule that was already moving, and with a firm practice that shifted the habitat. What is owed is a discriminator, not a preferential story.

## 4. The "shape, not a name" correction (kept visible)

I initially read the README phrase as a ban on naming any specific mechanism. That was an overstatement. The phrase governs a *missing* primitive, one not held in Γ. In this solution the candidate bindings (a routine, a rule, a market practice, a protest repertoire) are already held as ordinary institutional vocabulary. Naming one is a binding from the institutional-research frame onto held terms, and it is allowed provided the provenance is recorded. It is also explicitly **not** an import that moves the wall: Fig 6 :21 *"MINT composes inside the closure and can never add an atom."*

The correction does not rescue the local claim. The world sources corroborate the mechanism shape, but none of them was produced by the figure derivation, so none is a figure-derived name. The honest wording remains: the figures return the shape; the local content is a binding with provenance, still ungrounded.

## 5. Q: does the framework name "deliberative democracy" or "polycentric governance" as the answer?

**No.** Both are candidate bindings from political science, and the author's own provenance record in *The Recursive Self-Improvement Trap* describes convergence with Ostrom's work from a different direction: *"I had arrived at the same conclusions from bacteria and biology and game agents. She had arrived at them from committees and fisheries and forests."* That is convergent derivation across differing closures, which the figures treat as corroboration at high independence (Fig 8 :50). It is not a figure output. Fig 9 :32 governs: a kind of thing, never the answer itself.

## 6. Two consequences for this question

**Prestige is not action.** *The Recursive Self-Improvement Trap*: *"Prestige must never be allowed to validate an action directly... Someone let prestige stand in for proof. Someone let reputation substitute for result. Someone let the trusted name validate the untested claim."* And the operational form: *"Prestige can fund an action. It can give an action a budget to be tried. But it cannot settle an action. Only the ground can settle it. The Ground Maintainer must be blind to prestige."* A movement with famous supporters or a firm with a celebrated routine has a larger budget to be tried, not a truer mechanism. Contact points are therefore chosen as public, opt-in, ground-visible venues, never as a list of prestigious agreeable people.

**This audit is a simulation, not a ground reading.** *The Recursive Self-Improvement Trap* again: *"LLMs can quickly simulate an approximate answer/output with the formula... However, the truly testable output would need to be derived from the real architecture or working the formula yourself."* So `01` is a simulated derivation, checked for internal consistency against the figures. It cannot settle whether ordinary people, officials, firms or movements reshaped any actual institution. It can only say what an answer must look like and what would refute it.

## 7. The ground for this question

A ground here is whatever settles the shape at a slot and does not move because a frame thinks something (Fig 2 :29). Candidates named in effect terms, not selected: a published rule that continues to pay after its authors leave; a budget line that survives a change of personnel; a commons that is still used sustainably after the founding organization disbands; a workplace practice that persists after the trainer leaves. Each is an effect description anyone can check (Fig 6 :30), and each could be wrong on axes it does not measure (Fig 2 :37-38). Which one discriminates the local case is exactly the gap recorded in `01` F9.
