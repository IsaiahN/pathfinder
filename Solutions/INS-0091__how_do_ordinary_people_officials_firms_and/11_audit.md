## Round trip gap R_T

The Symbols table defines R_T as the gap between what was sent up and what came back down, `gap(x, (T_E ∘ T_A)(x))`, measuring what the coarser description cannot hold. Fig 4 :30 requires it to be measured rather than assumed. I ran the round trip on the condensation the folder actually uses.

2. **Abstraction (T_A):** the shorthand carried in `01` §9 and `07` §2: practice changes contact, the ground selects, what settles becomes an atom, four classes form a union, addition not subtraction.
3. **Concretisation (T_E):** the local story a reader will hear: "this protest plus this rule plus this firm practice reshaped this institution."

**R_T is nonzero.** Three things the shorthand holds do not survive the trip back down:

- the **per-slot indexing**, which a local story collapses into a single before-and-after narrative;
- the **method versus recording distinction**, which a local story collapses into "the practice was copied";
- the **non-updating anchor requirement**, which a local story replaces with agreement among the actors that change happened.

A fourth thing is partly lost: the **fixed-atom condition** on the isomer claim, since real institutional change also changes the habitat, so the before and after are rarely over the same atom set. This is recorded as a live strain in `06` and `09`, so it is partly preserved.

Conversely, one thing survives cleanly: the union reading and the refusal of a standing list of good changemakers. That is the residue worth sending. R_T was **not** measured numerically, because no institutional measurement was taken; it is recorded qualitatively as the enumerated set of dropped constraints. Treating "the formula is the condensed version of the metalaws" as identity would set R_T to zero and would be the dead-reckoning error Fig 4 :30 names.
