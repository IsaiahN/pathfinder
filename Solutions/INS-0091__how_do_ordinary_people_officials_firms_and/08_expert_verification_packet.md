# Expert verification packet — the Tether on everyday practice and institutional change

**What this is:** a claim in a form a researcher of grassroots institutional change can check. Mode, derivation and refuters are stated before the conclusion, so a reader knows what to look for and what would settle it.

**Mode: `specified` on the sub-claims, `general` on the union structure, with a declared `gap`. Not `grounded`.** No institution was measured. This is a simulated derivation checked for internal consistency against the figures, not a ground reading.

## 1. The claim, in one paragraph

The residual is per institution-site, not an average called "social change." Four actor classes, ordinary people, officials, firms and movements, form a union of overlapping conditions at a site, not a list of separate causes, and no standing list of them predicts who is a benefactor after the residual changes. A practice changes an institution only where it changes contact at the site; the ground then selects between differing rooms rather than the rooms negotiating agreement. What settles crosses the scale membrane as a reapplicable method and becomes an atom at the institutional level; two institutions built from the same atoms with different bonds are isomers. Additions and re-arrangements are available, subtraction of a habitat is not. Which specific practice wins, where, at what threshold is a declared `gap`, and seven refuters are written below in advance.

## 2. Three checkable sub-claims

### C1 — Union and no standing list (framework-internal, checkable by reading Fig 9 and Fig 11)

- **Statement:** the four classes are a union of overlapping conditions; there is no standing list of good changemakers.
- **Source, verbatim:** Fig 9 :55 — *"A bundled problem is usually a union of overlapping conditions, not a list of separate ones."* Fig 9 :56 — *"A single lever clears the region where only one condition holds and leaves the intersections standing, because the second condition still binds."* Fig 11 :51 — *"No standing list of good actors survives a change of residual."* Fig 11 :49 — *"An actor's valence is relative to the residual, not a property it carries."*
- **What to check:** do the four classes actually overlap at the same sites in the literature you know, so that a single lever leaves a residue? Can you name a class that is a benefactor in one setting and a malfactor in another after the residual changed?
- **Refuter:** a single lever clearing all four conditions with no intersection residue across three sites; or a fixed ranking of the classes predicting valences across a documented change of residual.

### C2 — Practice changes contact or changes nothing (framework-internal plus field-facing)

- **Statement:** an everyday practice reshapes an institution only where it changes contact at the site; otherwise it changes nothing however much it improves. Formal rules can codify a settled method, but a rule that fixes an outcome stops being a convention and an anchor that updates on the institution's own report produces coherent drift.
- **Source, verbatim:** Fig 11 :40 — *"A system cannot be made more capable by improving the agent in isolation. An improvement that does not change contact changes nothing, however much it improves."* Fig 11 :39 — *"capability is a property of agent-and-habitat, never of the agent alone."* Fig 7 :35 — *"A room's agreement is variation, not a verdict. Selection between rooms is where the ground votes."* Fig 10 :47 — *"a convention that decides an outcome has stopped being one"* Fig 2 :34 — *"If it does, panel 3 turns into panel 1: the anchor becomes another frame, everything drifts together, and the drift is coherent, which is why nobody notices."*
- **What to check:** in a case you know, did the effective practice change who pays, what is counted, or which rule is invoked? If a rule mattered, was its reference outside the institution, or did the institution grade its own report?
- **Refuter:** an institution changes reliably at a site while no practice changes contact there; or a rule keeps paying while its reference is the institution's own report and no outside reading is taken; or agreement within one evidence pool changes the trajectory with no independent reading.

### C3 — Recursion, method crossing, and asymmetry (framework-internal derivation)

- **Statement:** what settles locally becomes an atom at the institutional level; what crosses up is a method, not a recording of one occasion; addition and re-arrangement are available, subtraction of a habitat is not. Over a fixed atom set, two differently bonded institutions are isomers.
- **Source, verbatim:** Fig 12 :104 — *"A settled molecule becomes an atom for whatever composes over it."* Fig 4 :22 — *"Only methods cross up. Only head starts come down."* Fig 4 :25 — *"A recording carried upward looks like knowledge and is a description of one occasion."* Fig 11 :61 — *"Introduction is available; exclusion is not."* Fig 12 :117 — *"Same atoms, different bond, different substance."*
- **What to check:** does the institution you know survive the departure of the person who built the practice, and if so was a method recorded or only an occasion? Did the change add or re-arrange practices rather than remove a habitat condition?
- **Refuter:** a recording of one occasion functioning as a method without re-derivation across a habitat boundary; a change produced by subtracting a habitat condition; or two institutions with identical atoms and bonds behaving identically at every slot (which would make the isomer claim empty for the case).

## 3. Pre-registered refuter, all seven conditions

Written before the conclusion. The shape claim is refuted or narrowed if any holds at the ground:

1. an institution changes at a slot while no practice changes contact there;
2. one lever clears all four actor-class conditions with no intersection residue across three slots;
3. a recording of one occasion crosses upward and works as a method without re-derivation;
4. a standing list of actor classes predicts benefactor versus malfactor across a change of residual;
5. a convention that fixes an outcome still behaves as a convention when checked against an outside reference;
6. a movement changes an institution by subtracting a habitat condition rather than adding or re-arranging;
7. agreement among frames sharing one evidence pool changes the institution's trajectory with no independent reading and no non-updating ground.

## 4. What is explicitly not claimed

- Not a theory of everything; no final room. Institutional change is permanent work, not a solved state (Fig 11 :63).
- Not a local mechanism name: no named protest, rule, standard or routine is asserted as the cause.
- Not a ranking of the four actor classes.
- Not that agreement is corroboration (Fig 2 :40, Fig 7 :35).
- Not that a habitat can be subtracted (Fig 11 :61).
- Not that the isomer claim holds without a fixed atom set; that condition is a live strain, named in `06`.
- Not `grounded`, and not a claim that the figures settled any actual case.

## 6. What would settle it

A source-discriminating instrument at a slot, not further argument. From *The Hard Problem is About Source*: an instrument that reads green *"cannot comment on the source"*; *"If several paths create the same behavior, then behavior is a projection with lossy information."* Here that means an observation design that distinguishes a contact-changing practice from an already-moving rule and from a habitat shift. Which observation discriminates in a given case is the declared gap, not something this packet decides.

## 7. What an expert can check in twenty minutes

1. Read Fig 9 :55-57 and Fig 11 :49-51, :61 — five short passages.
2. Name one institution you know where the four actor classes overlap at the same site. Does a single lever leave a residue? (C1)
3. Identify the contact that changed: who paid, what was counted, which rule was invoked. Did the practice reach it? (C2)
4. Say whether what persisted was a method or a recording of one occasion, and whether the change added or removed habitat conditions. (C3)
5. Send the refutation. A failure is more useful than an endorsement.
