# The Tether's answer on everyday practice and institutional change

**Mode: `specified` on the per-domain claims, `general` on the structural shape, with one `gap`. Not `grounded`.** This is a shape and a route, not a local result. Simulated derivation checked against the figures (`05_what_shape_means.md` §6), not a ground reading.

## 1. The residual it answers

Institutions at t+1 differ from t in ways not fully explained by formal decisions. The residual is per institution-site, never a global "social change" average: Fig 1 :19 *"The residual is indexed by object slot, not aggregated."* The question "how do they reshape" asks for a route, and a vocabulary of ingredients alone *"can name a state and cannot name a route to one"* (Fig 12 :111). The actor classes form a union (Fig 9 :55), so the answer is a union of conditions with separate verdicts, not a single lever.

## 2. The shape, per domain

**D1, everyday practice (`specified`, triangulated).** A practice changes an institution only where it changes contact at the slot. Fig 11 :40: *"An improvement that does not change contact changes nothing, however much it improves."* Unmaintained change is selection, not design: Fig 11 :36 *"Where nothing maintains anything, intelligence is a label applied afterwards to a lineage that met its ground. Nothing was intelligent; selection ran."* Many small, low-coordination acts are therefore sufficient where they reach the ground, and many sincere acts are inert where they do not.

**D2, official rule-making (`specified`, triangulated).** Official codification can preserve a method that has settled. Its failure mode is specific: a convention that decides an outcome *"has stopped being one"* (Fig 10 :47), and an anchor that updates on the institution's own reports turns the arrangement into coherent drift (Fig 2 :34). A rule is a legitimate coordinator of shared meaning and an illegitimate substitute for a ground.

**D3, firm and market practice (`specified`, triangulated).** The market is an anchor (Fig 2 :29), and a firm's capability is a property of the firm and its habitat (Fig 11 :39). A procurement standard, hiring norm or reporting metric can be a head start in one habitat and a recording of one occasion in another (Fig 4 :25). Its valence is relative to the residual (Fig 11 :49).

**D4, movements and contention (`specified`, triangulated).** A movement is a differing closure; it moves the wall only through import, with provenance and a shadow test (Fig 6 :23, Fig 8 :51-52). Its power is asymmetric: *"Introduction is available; exclusion is not."* (Fig 11 :61). So it adds and re-arranges; it does not subtract habitat conditions, and the work is permanent (Fig 11 :63).

**D5, the institutional stratum (`specified`, triangulated).** A settled practice becomes an atom at the next level: Fig 12 :104 *"A settled molecule becomes an atom for whatever composes over it."* What crosses up is a method, not a recording of one occasion (Fig 4 :22, :25). Two institutions over the same atoms but different bonds are isomers (Fig 12 :117), conditional on a fixed atom set, a live strain named in `06`.

**D6, the temporal channel (`specified`, triangulated).** The ground does not decay; the channel does (Fig 10 :24). Persistence and quick loss are read on the same two factors: bracket fidelity across the handoff and ground pressure on the room (Fig 7 :37-39). Zero either and the chain runs in place.

**Union (`general`, triangulated).** The four classes overlap at the same slots. Fig 9 :55 *"A bundled problem is usually a union of overlapping conditions, not a list of separate ones."* Verdicts are kept separate and never averaged. There is no standing list of good changemakers: Fig 11 :51 *"No standing list of good actors survives a change of residual."*

**Mechanism summary.** Practice changes contact → the ground selects between differing rooms, not by negotiation (Fig 7 :35) → what settles crosses the membrane as a method → it becomes an atom at the institutional stratum → addition and re-arrangement only, exclusion unavailable → the cycle's sign decides whether the result reproduces or transforms.

## 3. What it explicitly does not say

- Not which protest, rule, standard or routine tipped any actual institution. That is a name, and the figures return a shape (Fig 9 :32).
- Not a standing ranking of the four actor classes.
- Not that agreement among agreeable people is evidence (Fig 2 :40).
- Not that an institution can be reset by removing a habitat condition (Fig 11 :61).
- Not that any part of this is `grounded`. No institution was measured.
- Not that a named institutional theory is the figure's answer. Such a name is a provenanced binding from a different frame, not a figure output.

## 4. What would settle it — the ground

A source-discriminating instrument, not another argument. *The Hard Problem is About Source*: an instrument that reads green *"cannot comment on the source"*, and *"If several paths create the same behavior, then behavior is a projection with lossy information."* Here that means an observation design that separates, at one slot, a practice that changed contact from a formal rule that was already moving and from a habitat shift that did the work. Candidate effect descriptions, none selected by the figures:

- Does the routine survive the departure of its author and keep paying? (Fig 12 :108-110 on re-derivation across a boundary.)
- Does the rule still pay when its reference is external to the institution? (Fig 2 :29, Fig 10 :47.)
- Does the change persist after the founding organization disbands? (Fig 11 :66 on substitution of habitat.)
- Does a partner with a genuinely different evidence base read the same contact change? (Fig 2 :31, Fig 8 :47-49.)

The seven standing falsifiers are in `01` §6 and `08` §2. Any one firing narrows or withdraws the shape.

## 5. The honest stop

**Unreached, not unreachable in principle.** The six-term substrate analogy and the figure set are a visible guess about what composes a system; declaring them makes the choice visible, not neutral. The answer stops where the available instruments cannot distinguish a method from a recording or a convention from a verdict, and it records the lineage so the stop is checkable. Fig 8 :39: *"So a search that finds nothing is never a proof of absence."* Fig 13 :134: *"Unreachable is a verdict here, not a silence"* The local mechanism stays open until the ground settles it.

## 6. One-paragraph version for outreach

> The Tether does not name which protest, rule or routine changed which institution, and it does not rank people, officials, firms and movements as causes. It says that the residual must be read per institution-site, not averaged into "social change"; that those four actor classes form a union of overlapping conditions with no standing list of good changemakers; that a practice changes an institution only where it changes contact and is selected against a ground that does not update on the institution's own reports; that what settles crosses the scale membrane as a reapplicable method and becomes a building block for the next level; and that addition and re-arrangement are available while subtraction of a habitat is not. Two institutions over the same atoms but arranged differently are isomers. Which specific practice wins, where and at what threshold is a declared gap. What the offer carries is the shape any answer must have, seven refuters written in advance, and the kinds of instrument that would close it. The site stays open until the ground settles it.
