## VERDICT (from the figures)

The shape survives; the local name does not. The residual is per institution-site. Four actor classes form a union of overlapping conditions with no standing list of good changemakers. A practice changes an institution only where it changes contact and is selected against a ground that does not update on the institution's own reports; what settles crosses the membrane as a method and becomes an atom at the next stratum. Addition and re-arrangement are available, subtraction is not. Over a fixed atom set the compared institutions are isomers. Which specific practice wins, where, at what threshold is a declared `gap`, reported as unreached rather than silence. Mode `specified` on per-domain claims, `general` on structure, not `grounded`, candidate. Verdicts are kept separate per domain and never averaged.

- **cost if wrong:** bounded and locatable. The packet states its mode, seven refuters and a declared gap, so an error is findable rather than invisible. The real cost is spending an outside reader's independence by sending a recorded theory before the shape has been checked, which Fig 2 :42 prices: "Buy independence in proportion to the cost of being wrong." That cost is avoidable by leading with the shape claim and the refuter. A second cost is the average: treating "social change" as one number hides the live slot (Fig 1 :22).
- **undoable:** yes. No term is deleted if the claim fails; a refutation is added and the reach falls because something was added rather than subtracted (Fig 6 :42-43). The shape and its refuters are recorded with provenance so a later reader can locate the error (Fig 10 :62-64). No institutional primitive has to be withdrawn to correct it.
- **reversed by:** a ground reading, not agreement. A source-discriminating observation at a slot that separates a contact-changing practice from an already-moving rule or a habitat shift; any of the seven pre-registered falsifiers (an institution changing with no contact change, one lever clearing the union, a recording functioning as a method, a standing valence list, a convention that still decides outcomes, subtraction of a habitat condition, or agreement within one evidence pool changing the trajectory); or a figure re-census that admits a new primitive. Until one of these fires, the verdict stands as `candidate`.
