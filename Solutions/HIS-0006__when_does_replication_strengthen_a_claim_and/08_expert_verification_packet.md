# Expert verification packet: the Tether on replication and method difference

**What this is:** a claim in a form a replication researcher or methodologist can check. Mode, derivation and refuters are stated before the conclusion, so a reader knows what to look for and what would settle it.

**Mode: `general` on the structural conditions, `specified` on any field application. Not `grounded`.** No historical episode is asserted to satisfy these conditions. This is a shape and a route, not a result.

**Status:** a simulated derivation checked for internal consistency against the figures, not a ground reading. It cannot settle the claim it reports on.

## 1. The claim, in one paragraph

Repeated observation strengthens a claim only as triangulation, never as a mirror chain. Three conditions must hold together: the anchor must not change its answer when a frame pushes on it; the repeat must be a separate frame, since two people reading the same report are not two frames; and the two attempts must read the same quantity. Different methods make the *direct* form of repetition impossible when there is no shared pose and hence no frame transform between them, so the available routes are indirect, through effect terms and a stated shape of what is missing. Different methods are not thereby weaker: convergence from independently chosen methods is corroboration at maximum independence, provided provenance shows which frame produced which result. A count of replications is a budget filter, never a witness for the verdict.

## 2. Three checkable sub-claims

### C1 : framework-internal; checkable by reading Figures 2 and 7

- **Statement:** repetition adds evidential weight only when it is triangulation between separate frames ranging against one anchor that does not update. A chain of same-closure repeats adds agreement, not evidence.
- **Source, verbatim:** Fig 2 :35 "Alignment is triangulation, not negotiation. A mirror cannot correct you." Fig 2 :31 "Independence, between two frames, is how little they draw on the same evidence." Fig 7 :13 "agreement compounds, evidence does not". Fig 7 :36 "A chain of mirrors is one long room. The next room must differ from the last, and match the residual it surfaced."
- **Refuter:** exhibit two frames that draw on the same evidence pool and show their agreement changes the warrant beyond what the pool already carried; or show a chain of same-closure rooms whose evidence increases with length.

### C2 : framework-internal plus a pose test; checkable against any two actual methods

- **Statement:** direct repetition requires a shared pose between the two methods. Where the methods differ enough that no frame transform exists, direct repetition is unavailable and the honest report is the shape of what is missing, not a verdict of false.
- **Source, verbatim:** Symbols table :82 "Cannot be run without a pose." Fig 10 :68 "Different actors, a different currency, a different ground, and a lossy transform between." Fig 8 :39 "So a search that finds nothing is never a proof of absence." Fig 6 :31 "Neither answer tells you much about the other. Run them as two tracks, and never read one against the other."
- **What to check:** for any named pair of methods, can a pose be exhibited, that is a shared setup in which both read the same quantity at the same scale? If yes, a direct repeat may be meaningful. If no, the two readings are different terms and comparing them as one repeat is a category error.
- **Refuter:** demonstrate a frame transform between two methods with no shared pose, or show a direct repeat across different instruments preserving the same quantity at the same scale.

### C3 : framework-internal; checkable by reading Figures 1, 8 and 9

- **Statement:** replication count is a filter for the budget, not a witness for the verdict. Convergence from independent frames is corroboration at maximum independence, but only with recorded provenance; otherwise it is one pool wearing two names.
- **Source, verbatim:** Fig 1 :23 "The ground enters as the only metric." Fig 9 :60 "Use filters for the budget and witnesses for the verdict. Never let a filter hand you a verdict." Fig 8 :50 "Two frames can hold the same atom by convergence, reaching it separately, which is corroboration at maximum independence." Fig 8 :48 "How much two frames draw on the same evidence decides both whether their agreement means anything and whether their union has any surplus."
- **Refuter:** a ground metric that is simply the count of repetitions; or a case where convergence from independently chosen methods adds no more warrant than one frame, or provenance does not change the reading.

## 3. What is explicitly not claimed

- Not that any named historical case did or did not satisfy these conditions. That requires protocols, registrations, instrument histories and ground readings this folder did not inspect.
- Not that a failed direct repeat refutes a claim.
- Not that more methods always help. High-overlap frames are one frame wearing two names.
- Not that replication count is a verdict.
- Not that the three repetition types (direct, systematic, conceptual) are interchangeable. Under Fig 12 :117 "isomer. Same atoms, different bond, different substance. The difference is invisible in the formula and decisive in the behaviour." they are different terms.
- Not `grounded`. Nothing here was settled by a measurement.

## 5. What would settle it

A ground reading, not agreement. Concretely: a protocol lodged before the repeat that fixes the endpoint; a demonstration that the repeat drew on a separate instrument rather than the same report; a shown pose between the two methods, or a demonstrated absence of one; and a recorded provenance distinguishing convergence from adoption. Until those are checked on an actual case, the verdict stays `candidate`.
