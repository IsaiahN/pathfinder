# Outreach draft: check the conditions before you decide about the framework

**Use:** one-to-one, or as a cover letter attached to a submission through the public venues in `03_public_contact_points.csv`. **Not** for bulk send.

**Hard precondition:** the shape claim in `01_figure_grounded_audit.md` must still have its four domains and its refuter attached when this goes out. If the refuter is stripped, the offer collapses on contact.

---

**Subject:** Would you check one claim about replication before we talk about theory?

> Hello,
>
> I have one specific output I would like you to check against what you already know. I am not asking you to read the framework first, and I am not asking you to agree with it. If the output does not survive, there is nothing else to look at.
>
> **The output (`general` on the structure, `specified` on any field application, not `grounded`):** repeated observation strengthens a claim only as triangulation, never as a mirror chain. Three conditions have to hold at once.
>
> 1. The thing being measured does not change its answer because of what any frame thinks. If the operational endpoint moves after the data are in, the repeat is a convention-setting exercise, not a verdict.
> 2. The repeat is a separate frame. Two people reading the same report are not two frames; two instruments pointed at the same thing are. A repeat that shares the original's evidence pool adds agreement, not evidence.
> 3. The quantity read is the same quantity. When the methods differ, a direct repeat may be unavailable for lack of a shared pose between them. What remains is an indirect route: describe the residual in effect terms and find a frame that already predicts its shape.
>
> Different methods are not automatically weaker. Convergence from independently chosen methods is the strongest form of corroboration, provided the record shows which result came from which frame. A borrowed conclusion replayed in two vocabularies is one witness wearing two names.
>
> **What I am not claiming:** that a count of replications is a verdict. It is a budget filter, a description of how much independent search was bought. Nor am I claiming that a failed direct repeat refutes a claim, or that every historical case fits these three conditions. The framework returns the shape of the conditions, not a ruling on any particular episode.
>
> **What would refute the output.** I wrote these down before sending this:
> 1. a repeat sharing the original evidence pool changes the warrant by more than the pool already carried;
> 2. a repeat that moves the endpoint still counts as strengthening;
> 3. a frame transform is demonstrated between two methods with no shared pose, or a direct repeat across different instruments preserves the same quantity at the same scale;
> 4. a ground metric is found that is simply the number of repetitions.
>
> If any one holds, I will retract the output. A one-line "this fails because..." is more useful to me than agreement. If you would rather not engage, no reply is needed.
>
> **If it survives, and only then:** the source material is below. The figures, not my summary of them, are the authority.
> - Verbatim figure text with line numbers (local extract): `FRAMEWORK/reference/03-FIGURES_TEXT.md`
> - Figures and formula, branch `general-agent`: https://github.com/IsaiahN/tether/tree/general-agent/figures
> - Center for Open Science, open science infrastructure and registered reports: https://www.cos.io/
>
> **If you want to reuse the machinery:** the formula is in the figures link. Run it on a problem you know well and can check against a ground. Write down what would refute your result *before* you run it, and state the mode of what you get. A reported failure is more useful to me than a reported success.

## Legality and contact hygiene

No personal emails are collected or used here. The three links are public institutional or repository pages. If this is attached to a journal submission or a public call, use the submission portal, not a staff member's private address. One recipient at a time. No automation, no misleading subject line, and an opt-out in every message.
