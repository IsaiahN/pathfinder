# The Tether's answer: when replication strengthens, and when methods block a direct repeat

**Mode: `general` on the structural conditions, `specified` on any application to a particular field or historical episode. Not `grounded`.** Simulated derivation checked against the figures (`05_what_shape_means.md` §7), not a ground reading.

## 1. The residual it answers

People use "replication" for several different operations and then argue about whether the claim got stronger. The residual is: what does repetition actually buy, and when is the direct form of repetition unavailable? The conditions for strengthening and the conditions for method-based impossibility form a union, not one hard question (Fig 9 :54: *"And when the split lands on a union rather than a partition, unbundle before you search again."*). Each needs its own loop and its own verdict.

## 2. The answer, per domain, kept separate

**D1, measurement and ground.** The thing measured must not change its answer because of what any frame thinks. Fig 2 :29: *"An anchor is whatever settles the question and does not change its answer because of what any frame thinks."* Fig 2 :33: *"The anchor must not update."* If the operational endpoint moves after the data are in, the repeat has become another frame and the drift is coherent, which is why nobody notices (Fig 2 :34). *Verdict: triangulated* (Fig 2 + Fig 10 + Fig 3 dependency order).

**D2, replication and agreement.** A repeat strengthens a claim only as triangulation. Fig 2 :35: *"Alignment is triangulation, not negotiation. A mirror cannot correct you."* Fig 7 :13: *"agreement compounds, evidence does not"*. Fig 6 :53: *"searchers drawing on the same evidence are one searcher wearing many names."* Independence, not headcount, is the parameter: *"Two people reading the same report are not two frames; two instruments pointed at the same thing are"* (Fig 2 :31). *Verdict: triangulated* (Fig 2 + Fig 7 + Fig 6, three low-overlap figures).

**D3, method comparison.** When methods differ, a direct repeat needs a shared pose and a transform that does not exist without one. Symbols table :82: *"Cannot be run without a pose."* Fig 10 :68: *"Different actors, a different currency, a different ground, and a lossy transform between."* The two tracks, inward sharpening and outward casting into effect terms, *"never read one against the other"* (Fig 6 :31). Where the pose is absent, direct repetition is not merely hard, it is a different operation; what remains is indirect triangulation and a stated shape of what is missing. *Verdict: triangulated* (Symbols table + Fig 6 + Fig 10).

**D4, standards and the record.** A count of replications is a budget filter, not a witness. Fig 1 :23: *"The ground enters as the only metric."* Fig 9 :60: *"Use filters for the budget and witnesses for the verdict. Never let a filter hand you a verdict."* Agreement is disqualifying for a verdict about the world and efficient for a convention about shared meaning (Fig 2 :39-41). *Verdict: single-source for the valence claim* (Fig 2), triangulated for the filter claim (Fig 1 + Fig 9).

**Across domains:** different methods are not automatically weaker. Convergence from independently chosen methods is *"corroboration at maximum independence"* (Fig 8 :50), provided provenance records which frame produced which result. Same atoms, different bond, different substance (Fig 12 :117).

## 3. What it explicitly does not say

- Not that any particular historical claim was or was not strengthened by replication. That is a name, and names need protocols and ground readings.
- Not that a failed direct repeat refutes a claim. Fig 8 :39: *"So a search that finds nothing is never a proof of absence."*
- Not that more methods always help. High-overlap frames are *"one frame wearing two names"* (Fig 8 :49) and their union *"adds nothing"*.
- Not that the count of replications is the verdict. It is a description of effort.
- Not a new operator called "replication strength." The local operators table :68 says: *"A relation that fails all seven is not an operator you are missing; it is a claim that has not been made yet."*
- Not `grounded`. No experiment, dataset or instrument was consulted to settle the historical question.

## 4. What would settle it, and the pre-registered falsifiers

The ground, not agreement. Written before the conclusion, so a failure is findable:

1. A repeat sharing the original evidence pool changes the warrant by more than the pool already carried.
2. A repeat that moves the operational endpoint after seeing data still counts as strengthening.
3. A frame transform is demonstrated between two methods with no shared pose, or a direct repeat across different instruments preserves the same quantity at the same scale.
4. Convergence from independently chosen methods adds no more warrant than a single frame, or provenance does not change the reading.
5. A ground metric is found that is simply the number of repetitions.
6. A sealed frame is exhibited in which exhaustive search proves absence.

If any fires, the shape claim is withdrawn. Anything short of these is a call for more agreement and does not move the verdict.

## 5. The honest stop

**Reached in shape, unreached in name.** Fig 13 :134: *"Unreachable is a verdict here, not a silence"*. The shape is the set of conditions a repeat must meet. Whether any given episode met them is a matter of protocols, preregistrations, instrument histories and ground readings, none of which this derivation inspected. Fig 4 :30: *"So take the round trip and measure the gap. Without it you are navigating by dead reckoning: confident about a position nobody checked."* The round-trip gap here is enumerated qualitatively in `11_audit.md`, not measured numerically, because there is no ground reading in this folder. Indiscernibility is not identity; the lineage is the proof.

## 6. One-paragraph version for outreach

> Replication strengthens a claim only as triangulation, not as a mirror chain. The repeat must range against an anchor that does not move when a frame pushes on it; it must be a separate instrument rather than a second reader of the same report; and it must read the same quantity. When methods differ, a direct repeat can be unavailable because there is no frame transform without a shared pose, so what remains is an indirect route through effect terms and a stated shape of what is missing. Different methods are not automatically weaker: convergence from independently chosen methods is the strongest corroboration, provided the record shows which frame produced which result. A count of replications is a budget filter, never a verdict. This is a shape of conditions, not a ruling on any historical case, and it carries four refuters written in advance.
