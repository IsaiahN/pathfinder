Order by dependency: anchor fixed, independence established, agreement interpreted, commensurability checked (Fig 3 :20-21). A reading below a broken link is a reading of nothing (Fig 3 :22-23).

## 4. Round-trip gap R_T

The Symbols table defines R_T as the gap between what was sent up and what came back down, gap(x, (T_E composed with T_A)(x)), measuring what the coarser description cannot hold. 2. **Abstraction (T_A):** the shorthand carried in `07` and `09`: anchor fixed, separate frame, same quantity; no pose means no direct repeat; count is a filter; convergence needs provenance.

**R_T is nonzero.** Three things the shorthand holds do not survive the trip back down:

- the **union structure** (Fig 9 :54-58). The shorthand lists three conditions as if they were a checklist; the figure requires the strengthening question and the method question to be unbundled, because a case in both conditions is a specific compound, not a longer checklist.
- the **pose rule's scope** (Symbols :82 and Fig 6 :29-31). The shorthand says "no pose means no direct repeat"; it drops the warning that inward sharpening and outward framing are two tracks that must never be read against one another, so a user can try to solve the method problem by refining one instrument and mistake it for progress.

Conversely, two things survive cleanly: a repeat that shares the evidence pool adds agreement rather than evidence, and the anchor must not update. Those are the residue worth sending.
