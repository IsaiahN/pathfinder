## 1. A shape is a constraint, not a filler

The canonical figures README (local copy) states the limit: the framework returns *"what a missing thing would have to look like (a shape, not a name)."* Fig 9 :32: *"you get a kind of thing, never the answer itself."* Fig 13 :134: *"Unreachable is a verdict here, not a silence"*.

From *The Library of Babel*: *"you can cut your search short if the set of books returned is small and descriptive enough that it provides a general shape of the answer that satisfies your question."*

For this question, a **shape** is the set of conditions a repeat must satisfy before it can count as evidence: anchor fixed, separate frame, same quantity. It is not a list of famous replications and not a ruling on any particular historical episode. A shape narrows the field of admissible repeats; it does not fill in which one worked.

## 2. How the shape is obtained

- *Library of Babel*: *"The books that you are returning in your searches need to be tested or ranged against the problem you want answered in order to steer the direction of the next search."* The residual selects the frame, not the other way round. Fig 9 :27-28: *"describe it before you go looking, or any frame you pick will seem to fit."*
- *Library of Babel* on bundles: *"Maybe your problem was a bundle or conflation of several problems masquerading as one? You will need to search all of those problems individually"*. Fig 9 :54 says the same: *"And when the split lands on a union rather than a partition, unbundle before you search again."* This is why §0 of `01_figure_grounded_audit.md` splits strengthening from method impossibility.
- *How a Society of Agents Would Work*: each agent is described as answering to *"the same judge: the world itself."* The group is only worth having while its members stay different: *"Ten copies watching the same thing are one opinion said ten times."*
- *Library of Babel* on helpers: *"Convergence towards the answer is faster with multiple helpers, but communication between them is paramount and would have to be virtually 1:1 to be effective."* Verbatim quote copied as printed.

## 3. What licenses a name

Only two things, both outside the shape itself:

1. **The ground settles it.** `THE_FORMULA.md`, step 5, says *"the ground settles it. Verified only by what pays. A gate is not the ground."* The canonical README says the framework *"stops at the limit of what the available instruments can distinguish, and records the lineage so the stop is honest."*
2. **A provenanced import from a frame whose closure differs**, with a source gate and a shadow test (Fig 6 :23, Fig 8 :50-52).

For this question, "which historical case actually fixed its anchor and used independent instruments" is a name. The framework cannot supply it. It can supply the conditions a case would have to meet. Anyone who names a case must show the protocol, the timing of the endpoint fix, and the independence of the teams. That is provenance, not shape.

## 4. Why the source essay governs the "different methods" half

*The Hard Problem is About Source* gives the mechanism directly. An instrument reads green and *"cannot comment on the source"*. *"If several paths create the same behavior, then behavior is a projection with lossy information"*. The resolution was *"proven wrong not by argument, but by the arrival of a better instrument."* And *"An instrument that can't distinguish these cases demonstrates its own inadequacy"*.

This is the world-level content of Symbols table :82, *"Cannot be run without a pose."* Two methods can return the same reading while measuring different things, so laying their outputs side by side as a "direct replication" is a metamerism error. What is owed is a source-discriminating instrument, not a vote between the readings. The essay is by the framework author, so this corroborates F3 but does not decide it. Fig 10 :68 and the Symbols table decide it.

## 5. Why agreement cannot be the validator here

*The Recursive Self-Improvement Trap*: *"You may all agree and be wrong. You may set your clock to someone else's, who was setting your clock to yours, and be off by many deviations over time. You need ground truth to define where you stand."* And *"the output is only as good as the validator. The agent, or the tool, or the frame, or the theory, cannot validate itself."*

*How a Society of Agents Would Work*: *"Independent discoveries count most."* and *"When people could see each other's guesses, their answers narrowed without getting any more accurate."*

Fig 2 :40: *"disqualifying for a verdict: agreement about the world is worthless when the agreeing frames share an evidence pool."*

So a community that reports "five replications, all consistent" has described a convention unless it can also show that the five did not share the pool. The shape claim is about independence, not headcount.

## 6. Prestige is not action

*The Recursive Self-Improvement Trap*: *"Prestige must never be allowed to validate an action directly."* *"Prestige can fund an action. It can give an action a budget to be tried. But it cannot settle an action. Only the ground can settle it."* In this field that means: a famous replication project gives a claim a budget to be tried, but the name of the project is not the ground. This is why `03_public_contact_points.csv` lists institutions and public channels, not named people ranked by likely agreement.

## 7. My own derivation is a simulation, not a ground reading

Same essay: *"LLMs can quickly simulate an approximate answer/output with the formula found in the architecture post."* However, *"the truly testable output would need to be derived from the real architecture or working the formula yourself."* So `01_figure_grounded_audit.md` and this file are simulated derivations checked for internal consistency against the figures. They are not tests of the historical question and must be labelled as such. They cannot settle anything they report on.

## 8. Correction to my own §3 (kept visible)

I first wrote §3 as if "shape, not a name" forbade naming any historical case at all. That overstates it. The README's phrase governs *"a missing thing"*, meaning a primitive or atom not held in the library. Naming a case is not inventing a new primitive; it is a binding from the world frame onto conditions the figures already state. What remains forbidden is presenting that binding as if the framework had derived the case, or as if the case moved the wall (Fig 6 :25: *"An import adds no atom to the world."*).

Both readings are recorded. The surviving rule is: the framework returns the conditions; a case is licensed only by its protocol and provenance, and is then reported as a binding, not a derivation. This matches the correction pattern in the exemplar's `05_what_shape_means.md` §5b, reached here independently from the same canonical phrase.
