## QUESTION

Decide, in effect terms, what repeated observation buys and when the direct form of repetition becomes unavailable. Option A: repetition itself is evidence, so more replications give a stronger claim. Option B: repetition is evidence only when the attempts are independent frames ranging against an anchor that does not update and reading the same quantity, and when methods differ the direct form can be unavailable for lack of a shared pose, leaving indirect triangulation. Option A licenses a replication count as a verdict proxy and, if the replicators are chosen for likely agreement, manufactures the disqualifying correlation. Option B yields checkable conditions, named failure modes and an honest stop where no common frame exists.

## DOMAIN CENSUS

R by arity, symmetry and scale, then one line per domain with actors / currency / anchor, partition or union, and order by dependency.

- **R (the residual).** The evidential value of repetition and its limit under method change. Arity: two conditions that can bind at once, so a union compound, not one hard question. Symmetry: repeats are not interchangeable; a second reader of the same report and a second instrument are different objects. Scale: within one frame (same method) versus across frames (different methods and a lossy transform).
- **Domain 1, measurement and ground.** Actors: the claim, the instrument, the observation, the thing measured. Currency: observations. Anchor: the ground, which does not change its answer because a frame thinks otherwise. Question type: partition (does this observation range against an anchor?).
- **Domain 2, replication and agreement.** Actors: original study, repeat attempt, shared pool. Currency: agreement and residual. Anchor: the ground, never the agreement. Question type: the independence sub-loop of the union.
- **Domain 3, method comparison and transfer.** Actors: two methods, the frame transform F, the instrument history. Currency: readings and the lossy transform between them. Anchor: the ground, approached through effect terms. Question type: the commensurability sub-loop of the union; a direct repeat needs a pose.
- **Domain 4, standards and the historical record.** Actors: historians, methodologists, journals, registrations. Currency: recorded standards of proof. Anchor: the ground, never consensus about standards. Question type: partition (filter versus witness).
- **Order by dependency.** Anchor fixed, then independence established, then agreement interpreted, then method commensurability checked. A reading below a broken link is not weak, it is a reading of nothing (Fig 3 :22-23).

## VERDICT (from the figures)

Replication strengthens a claim only as triangulation between separate frames ranging against an anchor that does not update and reading the same quantity. When methods differ enough that no shared pose exists, direct repetition is unavailable and the available routes are indirect, through effect terms and a stated shape of what is missing. Different methods are not thereby weaker: convergence from independently chosen methods is corroboration at maximum independence if provenance records the routes. Replication count is a budget filter, never a witness, and agreement is disqualifying for a verdict about the world while being efficient for a convention about shared meaning. This verdict is `general` in structure, `specified` in application, ungrounded, and `candidate` until a ground reading of an actual case checks it. Verdicts were not averaged across domains.

- **cost if wrong:** bounded and locatable. The packet states mode, three checkable sub-claims, verbatim lines, refuters and provenance before the conclusion, so an error is findable rather than invisible. The real cost is spending the independence of outside readers by sending a count or a conclusion instead of conditions and a refuter. Fig 2 :42 prices that cost: "Buy independence in proportion to the cost of being wrong." The cost is avoidable by leading with the conditions and the refuter.
- **undoable:** yes. The three-condition shape can be corrected or withdrawn without touching the figure text. No primitive was minted and no atom was added, so there is nothing to retract at the level of the framework. A sent packet can be superseded by a corrected one, and the refuter list tells a later reader exactly what fired.
- **reversed by:** a ground reading, not agreement. A demonstrated frame transform between two methods with no shared pose, or a direct repeat across different instruments preserving the same quantity, breaks the impossibility condition. A repeat that changes the endpoint and still strengthens a claim breaks the anchor condition. A repeat sharing one evidence pool that adds warrant beyond the pool breaks the independence condition. A ground metric that is simply the count of repetitions breaks the filter claim. A sealed frame in which search proves absence breaks the unreachable verdict. Until one of these fires, the verdict stands as `candidate`.
