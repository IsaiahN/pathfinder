## 1. A shape is a constraint, not a filler

The figures README states the limit: the framework returns *"what a missing thing would have to look like (**a shape, not a name**)."* Fig 9 :32: *"you get a kind of thing, never the answer itself"*. Fig 13: *"It can say the answer is out of reach with the library as it stands... It cannot say which primitive is missing, because naming it would mean already holding it."*

From *The Library of Babel* (carried quote): *"You can cut your search short if the set of books returned is small and descriptive enough that it provides a general shape of the answer that satisfies your question."*

For this question a **shape** is the narrowed form of the answer we can state before we can name any particular threshold value. It says: a threshold is an ordered comparison relation; the question splits into two uses; a moving threshold cannot be ground; the decision is a re-struck bargain. A shape does **not** say that the AMOC tips at +2 C or that Greenland tips at +3 C. Those are names, and they belong to the world sources, provenanced as such.

## 2. How the shape was obtained

- *Library of Babel* (carried quote): use the vocabulary already closest to the thing, look for recurring new terms that overlap with it, recurse. *"With each new term gained, you narrow the space and deepen your vocabulary."*
- Fig 6 :30: *"The description travels. Fails by being sharpened into terms only we can read. Cast it in effect terms."* Fig 9 :30-31: *"your words are borrowed too, so describe the effect rather than the cause."* Here the effect is stated in §0 of `01`: the number a decision leans on either stays put or slides with the pressures it judges.
- *Decoding Tacit Knowledge* (carried quote): *"When contact isn't possible, the best you can send is a plain description of what you'd expect to see, because anyone can check that against the world."* This is exactly the two-stage offer in `02`: send what would count as a moving threshold before sending the framework.
- *Library of Babel* on stopping: the answer is reached when *"the finish line will also be the starting place"*; by then you have *"composed the answer through the process of locating it."*

## 3. What licenses a name

Only two things, both outside the shape itself:

1. **The ground settles it.** Formula step 5: *"the ground settles it. Verified only by what pays."* The README: the framework *"stops at the limit of what the available instruments can distinguish, and records the lineage so the stop is honest."* A numerical threshold becomes a name only when a measurement or a well-specified model pays for it.
2. **A provenanced import from a frame whose closure differs** (Fig 6 :23, Fig 8 :50-52), with a source gate and a shadow test. The rate-induced-tipping literature is such a frame: it holds the comparison quantity "rate of forcing", which the climate-threshold literature did not hold as the deciding variable.

*They're Scanning Books* (carried quote): *"The real import is not a new term in the existing space. It is a new dimension of the space."* And: *"A minimum viable set of six terms is a human's careful guess at reality's compression axes; another sensorium's minimum set need not be those six."* Also: *"Declaring the primitives does not make them neutral. It makes the choice visible."*

The six are a visible guess, not nature's own list. Declaring that a threshold lives in temperature rather than in warming rate is a choice; the figures make it visible, they do not make it neutral.

## 4. The metamerism error is live for this question

*The Hard Problem is About Source* (carried quotes) gives the mechanism. A spectrometer reads green and *"cannot comment on the source"*: one green light, or yellow plus blue, or RGB, or CMYK, or a scheme not yet discovered. *"If several paths create the same behavior, then behavior is a projection with lossy information."* And: *"An instrument that can't distinguish these cases demonstrates its own inadequacy."* It was the spectrometer, not an argument, that resolved it.

Applied here: a time series showing rising variance and autocorrelation before a transition reads *"a threshold is approaching"* without commenting on the source. The same reading is compatible with a bifurcation approaching, with noise pushing a system already near a boundary, with a rate-induced departure, and with a gradual drift that merely looks like one (Dakos et al. 2015; Kefi et al. 2013). Naming the mechanism from the warning signal is the metamerism error. What is owed is a source-discriminating instrument, not a name.

This is also why S3 in `01` is a live strain and not a correction: van Westen et al. report that the threshold moves when the comparison is recast as a rate. Which quantity the threshold lives in is a source question, and the warning signal alone cannot settle it.

## 5. Q: Does "shape, not a name" forbid naming the comparison quantity?

**No, and this is a correction to the first pass of my own reading, kept visible.**

- The README's phrase is *"what a **missing** thing would have to look like (a shape, not a name)."* The subject is a primitive not held in the library.
- The comparison bond ⋛ is already held: Fig 12 :87 *"a threshold relation between two quantities"*, Operators :49. Temperature, rate, forcing and state variables are quantities already available to the world frames. Neither is a missing primitive.
- So saying "the threshold is a comparison between a state and a rate" is a **binding** from the rate-induced-tipping frame onto quantities already in play. It is provenance, not the invention of a seventh primitive and not an import that moves the wall in the sense of Fig 6 :21 (*"MINT composes inside the closure and can never add an atom"*).
- What remains forbidden is asserting a **numerical** value as if the framework returned it. That is a name where the framework returns a shape, and it is provenanced to a world model, not derived from the figures.

I first overstated the rule as a blanket ban on naming anything. Narrowed here and in `06` Q1. Both readings are recorded so neither is quietly lost.

## 6. Two consequences for outreach and for the verdict

**Prestige is not action.** From *The Recursive Self-Improvement Trap* (carried quote): *"Prestige must never be allowed to validate an action directly... Prestige can fund an action. It can give an action a budget to be tried. But it cannot settle an action. Only the ground can settle it. The Ground Maintainer must be blind to prestige."* A list of famous climatologists who agree that thresholds move is not a second witness; it is a convention. Fig 2 :42: *"Buy independence in proportion to the cost of being wrong."*

**My own audit is a simulation, not a ground reading.** Same essay (carried quote): *"LLMs can quickly simulate an approximate answer/output... However, the truly testable output would need to be derived from the real architecture or working the formula yourself."* LLM output is *"simulacra."* So `01` is a simulated derivation checked for internal consistency against the figures. It is not a measurement, it is not a test of any tipping element, and it cannot settle the question it reports on.

## 7. What "shape" concretely is for EAR-0075

The shape returned by this derivation is a four-part constraint:

1. A threshold is an **ordered comparison relation** between two quantities (Fig 12 :87, :89).
2. The question is a **union of two uses** of that relation: system property and decision ground (Fig 9 :57).
3. As a system property it **can move**, because the habitat updates while the ground does not (Fig 11 :73); as a decision anchor it **must not move**, or it stops being an anchor (Fig 2 :29-34).
4. The decision rule is a **bargain re-struck against a non-updating measurement**, not a fixed cut (Fig 5 :42), and the numerical threshold remains a declared gap (Fig 13 :134).

That is a shape any answer to this question must have. The number, the tipping element and the mechanism are names that a ground must pay for.
