## What is deliberately not here

- No personal emails of named individuals, no scraped contact list.
- No ranking of people by how likely they are to agree with the shape claim.
- No bulk-send plan, no request for trust, no request for endorsement.

Three figure-grounded reasons: Fig 2 :40 "disqualifying for a verdict: agreement about the world is worthless when the agreeing frames share an evidence pool"; Fig 2 :35 "Alignment is triangulation, not negotiation. A mirror cannot correct you."; Fig 10 :42 "A verifier that can reconstruct the claim cannot verify it." Selecting recipients for receptiveness manufactures exactly the correlation that would make their agreement worthless, and asking them to absorb the framework before they deliver a verdict spends the independence they contribute.

## Key verdict in one line

The three-way choice is not one dial with three settings. It is a union of five conditions with different anchors, so the figures return a decision procedure and a per-slot verdict discipline, not a list of which risks to put in which bin and not a percentage (Fig 9 :54 "And when the split lands on a union rather than a partition, unbundle before you search again."; Fig 9 :32 "you get a kind of thing, never the answer itself").
