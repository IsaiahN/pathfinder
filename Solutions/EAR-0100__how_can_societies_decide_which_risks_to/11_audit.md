## Independent domain census

I redid the census before relying on the primary's conclusions, using the WATSON order: R by arity, symmetry and scale; one line per domain with actors, currency and anchor; partition or union; order by dependency.

- **R (arity / symmetry / scale).** The residual is the per-slot gap between a predicted outcome under an action and the later observation, under a binding budget. Arity: N slots, so it is indexed, not global. Symmetry: the three actions are ordered and differently bonded; "which is worse" is a comparison, an ordered threshold relation, not a symmetric join. Scale: individual slot and society portfolio, with a membrane. A near-zero global average with one live slot is a legal state (Fig 1 :20), so aggregation destroys the only actionable signal (Fig 1 :22).
- **D1, physical hazard.** Actors: hazards, exposure, vulnerability. Currency: physical magnitude and realised outcome. Anchor: measurement that does not update on a frame's opinion (Fig 2 :29). Union member.
- **D2, forecast frame.** Actors: models, ensembles, scenarios. Currency: predictions and residuals. Anchor: an instrument that can fail a projection; model agreement is one pool (Fig 2 :40). Union member.
- **D3, allocation.** Actors: decision makers, budgets, a refusal seat. Currency: actions and resources. Anchor: realised outcome, not the ranking score (Fig 9 :60). Union member.
- **D4, acceptability.** Actors: affected people and communities. Currency: welfare, equity, tolerable loss. Anchor: human judgment, because the question is constitutively about people (Fig 2 :37). Union member.
- **D5, verification.** Actors: auditors, independent frames, a ground maintainer. Currency: refutations, residuals, provenance. Anchor: the ground, never agreement. Union member.

Order by dependency: perceive, vocabulary, objective, plan, learn and carry (Fig 3 :20). My census matches the primary's five domains and its union reading. Where the primary derived the union from Fig 9 :54-57 and the different anchors from Fig 2 :36-37, I derived the same boundaries from Fig 3 :25-27 (the broken objective link, which only makes sense if the value objective is a distinct domain) and Fig 12 :125-126 (two non-adding currencies, which only makes sense if description and action are different domains' currencies). ## Round trip gap R_T

The Symbols table defines R_T as the gap between what was sent up and what came back down, `gap(x, (T_E ∘ T_A)(x))`, measuring what the coarser description cannot hold. Fig 4 :30 requires it to be measured rather than assumed. I ran the round trip on the condensation this folder actually uses.

1. **Sent up (x):** the verbatim figure set, chiefly Fig 1 :19-22 (per-slot residual), Fig 2 :36-38 (anchor changes kind with the question), Fig 9 :54-60 (union, filters not verdicts), Fig 3 :25-27 (objective link), Fig 8 :39 (no proof of absence).
2. **Abstraction (T_A):** the shorthand carried in `07_the_tether_answer.md` and `09_watson_answer.md`: keep residual per slot, split into five anchored domains, never average, repair the objective link, scores filter, accept is a verdict with a reason, allocation stays a gap.
3. **Concretisation (T_E):** a national risk register with a composite score, a treatment band mapping, and a sign-off by a committee.

**R_T is nonzero.** Four things the shorthand holds do not survive the trip back down:

- the **per-slot indexing**, which a composite score erases by construction (Fig 1 :22);
- the **anchor split**, because a single committee sign-off tends to make the physical score decide acceptability too, which Fig 2 :36-38 forbids;
- the **objective link**, because a band mapping presupposes a goal and typically supplies no instrument for "success at this slot" (Fig 3 :27);
- **the accept subcases**, because a register records "accepted" without saying whether the case was underivable, unreachable at budget, or genuinely absent (Fig 8 :39, Fig 9 :44).

Conversely, two things survive cleanly: the requirement to read the residual per slot, and the rule that scores are filters rather than verdicts (the DCCEEW practitioner guidance independently preserves the second). R_T was **not** measured numerically, because no ground reading exists; it is recorded qualitatively as the enumerated set of dropped constraints. Treating "the folder's procedure is just the figures in fewer words" would set R_T to zero and would be the dead-reckoning error Fig 4 :30 names. The honest form is a lossy condensation with a recorded gap, which is what `09_watson_answer.md` states.
