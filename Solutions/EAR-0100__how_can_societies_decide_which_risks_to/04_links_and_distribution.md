## 3. Distribution: offer, do not push

Figure-grounded rules:

- Fig 4 :22 "Only methods cross up. Only head starts come down." Sending the full architecture before anyone has described a residual sends a recording, not a generator. Lead with the per-slot residual and the refuter; offer links after.
- Fig 10 :42 "A verifier that can reconstruct the claim cannot verify it." A recipient who has absorbed the whole framework before giving a verdict has joined the frame being checked.
- Fig 9 :27-28 "describe it before you go looking, or any frame you pick will seem to fit." Ask what they would count as a failure before giving them the article.
- Fig 2 :42 "Buy independence in proportion to the cost of being wrong." Spend more scrutiny on high-consequence slots and on voices from a different evidence pool.
- Fig 2 :40 "disqualifying for a verdict: agreement about the world is worthless when the agreeing frames share an evidence pool." Later agreement from people who read the theory is never pooled as corroboration.

**Recommended order in a reply:** (1) the per-slot residual and the five-anchor split; (2) ask what they would count as a failure and record it; (3) only then the three links, with the note that the figures, not the summary, are the authority.

## 4. The two-stage offer

1. **Stage 1 (verdict first):** send the residual, the shape claim and the seven pre-registered refuters. Ask what they would count as a failure. Record their answer before showing any theory.
2. **Stage 2 (opt-in):** if the output survives and they are curious, offer the source links. Stage 2 is opt-in by construction because Seat 4 must not hold the interiors.
3. **Stage 3 (opt-in):** offer the formula as a method to reuse. Fig 4 :22 permits a method to cross; each user writes their own refuter and states their own mode, because a frame cannot check the direction of its own question.

**Hard precondition:** stages 1 to 3 work only if the stage-1 output survives. `01_figure_grounded_audit.md` records what survives (the per-slot procedure, the union reading, the five anchors, the refuters) and what does not (any claim to a specific allocation ratio or named risk list). Send only what survives.
