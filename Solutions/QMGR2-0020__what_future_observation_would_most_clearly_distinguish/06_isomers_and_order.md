## DERIVATION

### Q1 - Partition or union? Union, with the intersections recorded

- Fig 9 :55: "A bundled problem is usually a union of overlapping conditions, not a list of separate ones." Fig 9 :57: "What remains is smaller and it is also categorically different: a case in two conditions is not a harder case of either."
- Zhang 2026 lists the overlapping conditions explicitly: "late-time dynamical dark energy, dark-sector interactions, modified gravity, early-Universe sound-horizon calibration, neutrino-mass inference and residual systematics."
- Gu et al. 2025 note that because dark energy chiefly affects the background and "modelling its perturbations requires extra assumptions, we focus here on background observables." That is a scoped choice, not a closure of the union.
- ⇒ A detection of w not equal to -1 in the background clears the region where only one condition holds and leaves the intersections standing. The residue is a specific compound: a source that varies AND a gravity sector that behaves as assumed AND a calibration that is correct AND systematics that are under control.
- **Falsifier, written before the run:** if the Lambda-versus-changing binary is shown to be a true partition with no overlapping confounders, so one background detection closes the entire question, Q1 fails and the simpler reading stands. Status: `triangulated` (Fig 9 and the review).

### Q2 - Where does the dependency chain break? At vocabulary and objective for "source"

- Fig 3 :19: "Optimising a link that already works cannot rescue one that does not." Fig 3 :27: "A link with no instrument is not a link that works." Fig 3 :29: "Before asking whether a system is good, ask which link it currently stops at, and whether the answer was measured or assumed."
- Link 1 perception works: SNe, BAO and CMB already read distances with percent-level precision. Link 2 vocabulary is partial: we have w0, wa, rho(z), eigenmodes, but no direct term for "source" in the data vector. Link 3 objective is where the chain stops: "distinguish" is stated as a sigma target, which is a budget filter, not a measurement of the source (Fig 9 :60 "Use filters for the budget and witnesses for the verdict. Never let a filter hand you a verdict.").
- Lee 2026 supplies the mechanism: distance observables integrate over H(z), so their Fisher spectrum is dominated by one mode. Adding precision above the break cannot rescue the missing objective. Growth, which responds differentially, can activate a second mode, but in Euclid-like configurations only at percent-level precision.
- ⇒ Most effort currently lands above the break (more distance points). The lever that matters is below it: a term for the source in the data vector, instantiated as a redshift-dependent function or a second channel.
- **Falsifier:** if a source term can be read directly from a single integrated distance at one epoch, with no differentiation and no second channel, the chain is unbroken at links 2-3 and Q2 fails. Status: `triangulated` for the chain rule (Fig 3 plus Fig 9), `single-source` for the Fisher mechanism (Lee 2026).

### Q3 - Are rho_DE(z) and w(z) isomers? Yes, and the bond changes the answer

- Fig 12 :117: "Same atoms, different bond, different substance. The difference is invisible in the formula and decisive in the behaviour."
- The relation is rho_DE(z) / rho_DE,0 = exp[3 integral of (1 + w(z')) / (1 + z') dz'] (DESI DR2 analysis, Eq. 8). The atoms, distance measurements, are the same. The bond differs: rho(z) is an integral of w(z) plus the expansion history; w(z) is the local ratio of pressure to density. Under comparison (⋛) a threshold on one is not a threshold on the other in the same representation.
- Wang and Freese 2026 report that measuring rho_X(z_i) is preferred to measuring w_X(z_i) by both AIC and BIC, that the free-function reconstruction is "consistent with a cosmological constant," and that the collaboration's 3.1 sigma result is "primarily the consequence of their assuming parametrization w_X(z)=w_0+w_a(1-a)." They add: "assuming a linear w_X(z) could be misleading."
- ⇒ The two are isomers in the figure's sense: same atoms, different arrangement, different substance. The observed "detection" is a property of the molecule, not of the atoms. This is why the discriminator shape requires reporting both representations.
- Note the distinction from the exemplar's use of "isomer": there GR and QM were said to be isomers over a shared atom set. Here the shared atoms are the distance data and the two observables are alternative bondings of them. The word is used in the figure's strict sense in both cases (same atoms, different bond, different substance).
- **Falsifier:** if rho(z) and w(z) reconstructions give the same significance and the same model preference under AIC and BIC for every prior and sample, the bond carries no information and the isomer claim fails. Status: `single-source` for the empirical preference (one paper), triangulated for the figure definition (Fig 12 and Fig 13 :121 "Different routes can arrive at the same substance.").

### Q4 - The order of the union: refuse before mint, describe before looking

- Fig 9 :27-28: "describe it before you go looking, or any frame you pick will seem to fit." Formula step 2: REFUTED routes before a mint is owed; ¬ is not a bond.
- Concretely: the frame that says "use mission X" is chosen only after the residual has been described as a source-discrimination failure. The present folder does that: F1 establishes the metamerism, F2 and F3 identify the two channels, and only F5 names candidate surveys as instruments that could close the gap. If the order were reversed, any mission would seem to fit (echo without shadow → apophenia, per the exemplar's philosophy mapping).
- Fig 4 :30: "So take the round trip and measure the gap. Without it you are navigating by dead reckoning: confident about a position nobody checked." A forecast significance (a recording of a simulated fit) carried outward is not the same as the method that would settle the source. The gap must be measured, not assumed zero.
- **Falsifier:** if the choice of instrument is shown to be independent of how the residual is described (any description selects the same survey with the same confidence), the "describe before looking" discipline adds nothing here. Status: `triangulated` (Fig 9 and Fig 4).

### Q5 - Analogue: the spectrometer, with its limit

- *The Hard Problem is About Source*, opened directly: the eye reads green and cannot comment on the source; "It was proven wrong not by argument, but by the arrival of a better instrument."
- The analogue transfers a method, not a name. The better instrument here has a stated shape (differentiate across epoch, or add a low-overlap channel), not a part number.
- **Anchor-renaming caveat.** Fig 4 :20: "Only the actors, the currency and the anchor change." In the cognition domain the anchor is a spectrometer reading; in cosmology it is a measured expansion and growth history with systematics audited. The analogue licenses the *form* of the solution (a discriminator instrument), not the claim that a familiar laboratory device exists for this case. Where no proto-instrument is already returning something that fails to resolve, the search is for one that is already there (Fig 6 :34-35).
- **Falsifier:** if no channel at any resolution already returns a source-dependent reading that fails to resolve, the analogue fails to transfer and the verdict becomes `unreachable`. Status: `single-source` analogue, corroborating not deciding.

## CONCLUSION

The question is a union of overlapping conditions (Q1); the current chain breaks at the vocabulary and objective for "source", so extra distance precision is effort above the break (Q2); rho(z) and w(z) are isomers of the same distance atoms under different bonds, and the bond changes the reported answer (Q3); refutation and description must come before naming a mission (Q4); and the spectrometer analogue transfers the shape of the instrument, not its identity (Q5). Every claim carries its own falsifier. No figure line returns a single best observation, which is recorded as a `gap` in `01` F8 and `09`.
