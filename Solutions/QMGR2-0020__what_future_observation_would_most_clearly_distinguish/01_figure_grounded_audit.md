**The residual in effect terms.** A constant term and a changing component are metamers under the current instrument: they yield the same integrated distance reading but from different sources. Both fit the data; only the source differs. The residual is the instrument's inability to comment on the source. The question asks which future observation would make the source register. This is a source-discrimination question, not a question about who fits the curve best.

**Direction bound as an ordinary term (Formula "Direction"; Fig 5 :65).** Let DISCRIMINATOR be the ordinary term: "an observation whose reading is different under a constant source and a changing source at the ground, with the difference attributable to the source rather than to the representation or a shared systematic." Written down before the run, it can accrue a residual and be refused. It is not left as an unbound preference for a favourite telescope.

**Two options and what each leads to.** Option A: name one mission or one significance threshold as "the" answer. This returns a name where the framework returns a shape (Fig 9 :32), and it treats a filter as a witness (Fig 9 :60). Option B: return the shape the discriminator must have, the two channels that can instantiate it, the refuters written in advance, and the instruments that could close it, with the single-name question declared `gap`. Option B is the honest form and is what this folder does.

## 2. Findings

### F1 - This is a source-discrimination problem, not a fit-quality problem (frame-setting, triangulated)

The instrument reads an integrated distance curve. A cosmological constant and a changing component can give the same curve from different sources. From *The Hard Problem is About Source*: "If several paths create the same behavior, then behavior is a projection with lossy information"; the color instrument "cannot comment on the source"; the answer "was proven wrong not by argument, but by the arrival of a better instrument." Fig 6 :34: "already returning something that fails to resolve." Fig 6 :29: "Fails by refining forever on an axis that cannot carry it."

**Consequence.** The discriminator must register the source. Two structural routes do this: differentiate the reading across epoch (the source leaves a redshift-dependent trace), or range a second, low-overlap channel against the same ground (the source affects growth differently from geometry). A third route, more precision on the same integrated channel without differentiation, is the refinement Fig 6 :29 forbids.

**Refuter.** A single-epoch, integrated distance measurement under fixed assumptions conclusively selects one hypothesis at the ground, with no reliance on redshift differentiation or a second channel. If that happens the metamerism claim is false and this folder withdraws F1. Status: `triangulated`, low overlap between the framework instrument rule (Fig 6) and the cognition essay (metamerism).

### F2 - Geometry and growth are a union of channels, not two names for one reading (triangulated)

Fig 2 :31: "Independence, between two frames, is how little they draw on the same evidence." Fig 2 :35: "Alignment is triangulation, not negotiation. A mirror cannot correct you." Fig 9 :54-57: a bundled problem is a union of overlapping conditions and a case in two conditions is not a harder case of either.

World corroboration: Zhang 2026 (*Science China Physics, Mechanics & Astronomy*) states that "Future progress will require orthogonal measurements of geometry, growth, and absolute distance" and that "The key advance will not be precision alone, but the ability of these probes to break degeneracies." Gu et al. 2025 (*Nature Astronomy*) show the DESI BAO signal is modest alone and strengthens only with independent supernova compilations and a CMB prior, and that adding supernova data "produces further constrained modes" in w(z).

**Refuter.** Geometry and growth are demonstrated to be the same information channel for w(z) at every achievable precision, so their agreement is one pool talking. Status: `triangulated` for the union reading (Fig 2 and Fig 9 have low overlap: one is about anchors, one about splitting compounds). The physics identification of which channels are low-overlap is `single-source` on the world side and is allowed to fail without flipping the figure verdict.

### F3 - Distance data have one dominant information direction; growth can open a second (single-source on the world side)

Lee 2026 (*arXiv:2602.08207*) finds that "distance and expansion-rate data generically produce a strongly hierarchical Fisher spectrum dominated by a single information mode, reflecting an irreducible loss of sensitivity to temporal variations in dark energy" and that "Growth measurements, by contrast, respond through differential dynamics and can introduce additional independent information directions." The same paper adds the honest limit: in Euclid-like configurations "the information remains effectively one-dimensional until growth precision reaches the percent level."

**Figure reading.** Fig 11 :40: "An improvement that does not change contact changes nothing, however much it improves." Making distances more precise without adding a channel does not change contact with the source. Fig 10 :26 names the failure: "saturated metrics, pooled readings."

**Refuter.** A proof that the Fisher spectrum stays one-dimensional for w(z) even at sub-percent growth precision, for all survey configurations. That would kill the growth route but not the redshift-differentiation route of F1. Status: `single-source` (one methodology paper); corroborates F1 and F2, does not decide the verdict.

### F4 - The signal is representation-dependent: rho(z) versus w(z) are isomers (conflict returned to census)

The DESI DR2 cosmological analysis reports a preference for evolving dark energy of 2.8 to 4.2 sigma depending on the supernova sample, with the favoured quadrant w0 > -1 and wa < 0. Gu et al. 2025 find "moderate support for departures from Lambda cold dark matter" and an SNR of 3.9 for DESI BAO + CMB + DESY5. But Wang and Freese 2026 (*JCAP 02 (2026) 023*) analyze the same DESI DR2 BAO data and find both rho_X(z) and w_X(z) "consistent with a cosmological constant," and state that the DESI collaboration's 3.1 sigma result is "primarily the consequence of their assuming parametrization w_X(z)=w_0+w_a(1-a)." They conclude "assuming a linear w_X(z) could be misleading."

**Census return.** The conflict is not a contradiction to be resolved by picking a side. It shows the object called "the signal" is a union compound of data, representation (w versus rho, linear versus free), prior, and sample. Fig 12 :117: "Same atoms, different bond, different substance." The atoms (distances) are shared; the bond differs; the substance, a detection or a null, differs. Fig 9 :54 requires unbundling before searching again. The verdict is not flipped; the discriminator shape is sharpened: the observation must be reported in a representation that does not bake in the answer.

**Refuter.** A demonstration that the rho(z) and w(z) reconstructions give the same significance and the same model choice under AIC and BIC for every prior and sample, so the bond carries no information. Status: `single-source` on the world side for the conflict itself; the figure reading of isomerism is triangulated with Fig 12 and Fig 13 :121 ("Different routes can arrive at the same substance.").

### F5 - The residual is unreached, not unreachable: there is an edge to extend from (triangulated)

Fig 6 :32: "An instrument is not built from a description. It is improved from a worse instrument already returning something." Fig 6 :34: "The question is not whether a sensor could exist. It is whether anything, at any resolution, is already returning something that fails to resolve." DESI DR2, together with SNe and CMB, is already returning something that fails to resolve: a 2.8 to 4.2 sigma hint that is sample- and parametrization-dependent. There is a proto-instrument to extend from. Euclid forecasts in *Astronomy & Astrophysics* (2026) find Euclid spectroscopic growth data plus external products will improve the precision of current null tests "on average by a factor of eight," and Wang and Freese note "Future galaxy redshift surveys by Euclid and Roman at higher redshifts will significantly advance our understanding of dark energy."

**Refuter.** A demonstration that no channel at any resolution registers any source-dependent difference, so there is no edge to extend from. That would move the verdict from `unreached` to `unreachable`, which is still a verdict (Fig 8 :39: "So a search that finds nothing is never a proof of absence"). Status: `triangulated` (Fig 6 instrument rule plus current measured hints).

### F6 - A significance threshold is a filter for the budget, never a witness for the verdict (triangulated)

Fig 9 :60: "Use filters for the budget and witnesses for the verdict. Never let a filter hand you a verdict." A 5 sigma convention decides which result gets published; it does not decide whether the source is constant. Fig 10 :47: "a convention that decides an outcome has stopped being one." The DESI numbers (1.7 sigma BAO alone, 3.1 sigma with CMB, 2.8 to 4.2 sigma with SNe) are filters that rank how hard to look, not the verdict about the world. The same holds for Fisher eigenmodes.

**Refuter.** A demonstration that a fixed sigma threshold, applied to a single pipeline, settles the source question without independent channels or systematics audits. Status: `triangulated` across Fig 9 and Fig 10, two figures with different objects (search economics versus seat conventions).

### F7 - The binary is bundled with confounders; one lever leaves the intersections standing (triangulated)

Zhang 2026 lists the competing explanations: "late-time dynamical dark energy, dark-sector interactions, modified gravity, early-Universe sound-horizon calibration, neutrino-mass inference and residual systematics." Gu et al. note that "Because DE chiefly affects the expansion history of the background of the Universe and modelling its perturbations requires extra assumptions, we focus here on background observables." A detection of w not equal to -1 in the background does not by itself separate a changing fluid from modified gravity. Fig 9 :55-57: a single lever clears the region where only one condition holds and leaves the intersections standing, and the residue becomes a specific compound. Fig 8 :53: "Residual first, frame second."

**Refuter.** A demonstration that the Lambda-versus-changing binary is a genuine partition with no overlapping confounders, so a single background detection closes the whole question. Status: `triangulated` for the union reading (Fig 9 plus the review). The specific confounder list is `single-source` world corroboration.

### F8 - No figure line selects an instrument or a number: the single-name question is a gap (triangulated)

Fig 9 :32: "you get a kind of thing, never the answer itself." Fig 13 :135: "It cannot say which primitive is missing, because naming it would mean already holding it." ## 3. Pre-registered refuter

Required before any outreach, per the seat map local guide line 287 (opened and checked this run): "a falsifier written before the run is a design instrument, not only an honesty device." The shape claim S is: *a discriminator must register the source by redshift differentiation and/or by a low-overlap cross-channel comparison; no refinement of one integrated reading at one epoch can do it.* S is refused if any of the following holds at the ground:

1. **R1.** A systematics-free, single-epoch, integrated distance measurement under fixed assumptions conclusively selects one hypothesis, with no redshift differentiation and no second channel.
2. **R2.** Geometry and growth observables are proven to carry identical information about w(z) at every achievable precision, so no second information direction can ever be opened.
3. **R3.** A source-dependent signal of the required kind is shown to be entirely a shared systematic of the SN, BAO and CMB pipelines (one evidence pool), with no residual source signal in any independent channel. This moves the verdict to `unreachable` for those instruments, not to a false shape.
4. **R4.** A figure re-census yields a line that names a specific instrument, redshift or significance threshold as the discriminator. F8 is then withdrawn.
5. **R5.** The rho(z) and w(z) reconstructions are shown to have identical model preference under AIC and BIC for all priors and samples, so F4's isomer claim fails.

If any fires, the relevant finding is withdrawn or narrowed and the retraction is recorded in section 6 rather than silently applied. Until one is run, nothing in this folder may be labelled `grounded`.

## 4. Recommendation

1. Lead with the residual and its refuter, not with a mission name (Fig 10 :52, "Bring a characterised residual, never the source").
2. Say the mode on every substantive sentence. The correct form is: "a source-discriminating differential measurement is required; this is a `specified` shape, not a measurement."
3. Present the two routes (redshift differentiation, low-overlap cross-channel) as separate verdicts and never average them into one sigma.
4. Report any future detection in a representation that does not bake in the answer: rho_DE(z) as a free function as well as w0-wa, with independent SN samples and a growth-channel consistency check (F4, F7).
5. Do not use the public contact list as an endorsement list. Ask what would refute the shape (Fig 2 :40).
