# Expert verification packet - the Tether on Lambda versus changing dark energy

**What this is:** a claim in a form a cosmologist can check. Mode, derivation and refuters are stated before the conclusion, so a reader knows what to look for and what would settle it.

**Mode: `specified` on the shape and per-domain verdicts, `general` on the structural claim, `gap` on the name of a single best instrument. Not `grounded`.** No cosmological measurement is claimed by this packet. It is a shape and a route.

**Status:** a simulated derivation checked for internal consistency against the figures, not a ground reading. It cannot settle the question it reports on.

## 1. The claim, in one paragraph

A cosmological constant and changing dark energy are metamers under an integrated distance reading: they give the same fitted curve from different sources. The discriminating observation must make the source register, and there are two structural routes. Route A differentiates the reading across epoch, measuring rho_DE(z) as a free function (not only linear w(a)) so the time variation is the signal, targeting the weakly constrained z greater than 0.5 window. Route B adds a low-overlap channel, growth of structure (RSD f sigma8, weak lensing, clusters, CMB lensing), which can open a second information direction that distance data lack. Significance thresholds are filters for the budget, never witnesses for the verdict. Current DESI DR2 hints (roughly 2.8 to 4.2 sigma, sample- and representation-dependent) are an edge to extend from, not a result. No figure line names a single best mission or threshold, so that part is declared `gap`.

## 2. Three checkable sub-claims

### C1 - framework metatheory; checkable by reading Figures 6 and 9 plus the metamerism essay

- **Statement:** a discriminator must register the source; refining one integrated reading cannot. The two legitimate routes are redshift differentiation and a low-overlap second channel.
- **Source, verbatim:** Fig 6 :29 "Fails by refining forever on an axis that cannot carry it." Fig 6 :32 "An instrument is not built from a description. It is improved from a worse instrument already returning something." Fig 9 :55 "A bundled problem is usually a union of overlapping conditions, not a list of separate ones." World corroboration: *The Hard Problem is About Source* (Isaiah N, adventuresinml Substack, 2026-08-28), "The answer is unreached until a better instrument exists."
- **Refuter:** a systematics-free, single-epoch integrated distance measurement under fixed assumptions conclusively selects one hypothesis with no redshift differentiation and no second channel. If that holds, C1 is false.

### C2 - physics-facing; checkable by a cosmologist against survey forecasts

- **Statement:** geometry and growth carry different information about w(z). Distance and expansion-rate data have one dominant Fisher mode; growth can open a second, but in Euclid-like configurations only when growth precision reaches the percent level. A background-only detection does not separate a changing fluid from modified gravity.
- **What to check:** is Lee 2026's Fisher-structure result correct for the relevant kernels and covariances? Does the percent-level condition hold for Euclid, Rubin and Roman configurations? Does the growth-channel comparison (f sigma8 versus weak lensing) actually test gravitational slip, and therefore the confounders named by Zhang 2026?
- **Source, verbatim (world, secondary):** Seokcheon Lee, *Complementary Roles of Distance and Growth Probes in Testing Time-Varying Dark Energy*, arXiv:2602.08207 (2026): "distance and expansion-rate data generically produce a strongly hierarchical Fisher spectrum dominated by a single information mode." Figure bearing: Fig 2 :31 "Independence, between two frames, is how little they draw on the same evidence." Fig 11 :40 "An improvement that does not change contact changes nothing, however much it improves."
- **Refuter:** a proof that the Fisher spectrum stays one-dimensional for w(z) at every achievable growth precision, for all survey configurations. That kills Route B but not Route A, so C2 must state explicitly that it is a claim about the growth route only. A second refuter: a background detection alone is shown to close the entire confounder union, in which case the union reading of C1 fails.

### C3 - model-selection facing; checkable by anyone with the DESI DR2 chains

- **Statement:** the reported preference for evolution depends on the representation and the data pool. rho_DE(z) and linear w(a) are isomers of the same distance atoms under different bonds; the bond changes the answer.
- **Source, verbatim (world, secondary):** Yun Wang and Katherine Freese, *Model-Independent Dark Energy Measurements from DESI DR2 and Planck 2015 Data*, JCAP 02 (2026) 023: "assuming a linear w_X(z) could be misleading"; measuring rho_X(z_i) is "preferred to measuring {w_X(z_i)} by model selection using the Akaike Information Criterion (AIC) as well as the Bayesian Information Criterion (BIC)". Gu et al., Nature Astronomy 9, 1879 (2025): "Baryon acoustic oscillation data alone yield modest constraints, but in combination with independent supernova compilations and the prior for the cosmic microwave background, they strengthen the evidence for dynamics." Figure bearing: Fig 12 :117 "Same atoms, different bond, different substance." Fig 9 :60 "Use filters for the budget and witnesses for the verdict. Never let a filter hand you a verdict."
- **Refuter:** a demonstration that rho(z) and w(z) reconstructions give the same significance and model preference under AIC and BIC for every prior and sample, and that the preference is invariant across independent SN and BAO pools. Then the bond carries no information and C3 fails.

## 3. What is explicitly not claimed

- Not that any existing survey has settled the question. The DESI hint is tentative and representation-dependent.
- Not the name of a single best mission, redshift or sigma threshold. That is a declared `gap` (Fig 9 :32).
- Not that w not equal to -1 in the background closes the union: modified gravity, interacting dark sectors, sound-horizon calibration, neutrino mass and systematics remain (Fig 9 :55-57).
- Not a derivation of a cosmological model from the figures. rho(z) and w(z) are bindings from the physics frame, provenanced as such (JCAP 2026), not new primitives.
- Not `grounded`. Nothing here is asserted as settled by a measurement made in this derivation.
- Not a claim that the spectrometer analogue supplies a literal device for cosmology. It transfers the form of the solution, with the anchor renamed (Fig 4 :20).

## 5. What would settle it

A source-discriminating detection that survives four refusals: representation (rho(z) free function, not only linear w(a)); sample (Pantheon+, Union3, DESY5 and independent BAO); channel (geometry plus percent-level growth, with an f sigma8 versus lensing check for gravitational slip); systematics (smooth offsets cannot mimic a redshift-dependent feature). The anchor is the measured history, not the collaboration's sigma value and not agreement among pipelines that share a pool (Fig 2 :29, :40).
