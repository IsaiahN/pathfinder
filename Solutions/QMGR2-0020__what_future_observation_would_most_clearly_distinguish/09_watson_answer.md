## VERDICT (from the figures)

The discriminator is a source-registering measurement, not a tighter fit to the same curve. Two routes instantiate the shape and stay separate: differentiate the reading across redshift, reporting rho_DE(z) as a free function so the time variation is the signal; or add a low-overlap growth channel so geometry and growth can be ranged against one non-updating ground. Sigmas and Fisher modes are filters for the budget, never witnesses. The binary is a union bundled with modified gravity, interacting sectors, calibration, neutrino mass and systematics, so one background detection leaves intersections standing. The single-name question, which mission or threshold is "best", is a `gap` of the shape-not-a-name kind. Current DESI hints give an edge to extend from, so the status is `unreached`, not `unreachable`. Mode `specified` on the shape, `general` on structure, not `grounded`.

- **cost if wrong:** bounded and locatable. The packet states mode, refuters and lineage before the conclusion, so an error is findable. The real cost is spending the independence of outside readers by sending a recorded fit or a mission name instead of a discriminator method, which Fig 2 :42 prices: "Buy independence in proportion to the cost of being wrong." That cost is avoidable by leading with the shape claim and its refuters.
- **undoable:** yes. The single-name temptation is removable without touching the derivation; `07_the_tether_answer.md` and `08_expert_verification_packet.md` already remove it. No primitive has to be withdrawn. A sent claim can be superseded by the corrected packet.
- **reversed by:** a ground reading, not agreement. A source-discriminating detection of rho_DE(z) variation or w(z) not equal to -1 that survives free-function reconstruction, multiple SN and BAO samples, a percent-level growth channel with an f sigma8 versus lensing check, and systematics audits would settle the source. A proof that geometry and growth carry identical information at all precisions would kill Route B. A proof that no channel at any resolution registers a source-dependent difference would move the verdict to `unreachable`, which is a verdict. Until one of these fires, the verdict stands as `candidate`.
