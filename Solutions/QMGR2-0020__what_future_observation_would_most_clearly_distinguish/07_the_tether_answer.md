# The Tether's answer: what observation would distinguish Lambda from changing dark energy?

**Mode: `specified` on the shape and on per-domain verdicts, `general` on the structural claim, `gap` on the name of a single best instrument. Not `grounded`.** This is a shape and a route, not a measurement. Simulated derivation checked against the figures (`05_what_shape_means.md` section 6), not a ground reading.

## 1. The residual it answers

A constant cosmological term and a changing dark-energy component are metamers under the current instrument. They yield the same integrated distance reading from different sources. Every extra distance point tightens the same curve; the source stays invisible. The residual is the instrument's inability to comment on its source, not a shortage of fit quality. It is a **union compound** of background geometry, growth, absolute distance and confounders (Fig 9 :55-57), not one hard question with two opposite answers. Each condition has its own anchor and its own loop.

## 2. The shape of the discriminator

1. **The discriminator must make the source register.** Fig 6 :29: "Fails by refining forever on an axis that cannot carry it." Fig 6 :32: "An instrument is not built from a description. It is improved from a worse instrument already returning something." From *The Hard Problem is About Source*, the eye reads green and "cannot comment on the source"; the spectrometer, not a better argument, settled it. The answer has the form of an instrument capability, not a source name.

2. **There are two structural routes, and they are separate verdicts.**  
   **Route A, inward (differentiate across epoch):** measure rho_DE(z) or w(z) as a function of redshift, especially the z greater than 0.5 window where current reconstruction is weak, so that the time variation itself is the signal. Report rho_DE(z) as a free function as well as w0-wa, because the bond changes the answer (Fig 12 :117: "Same atoms, different bond, different substance."; Wang and Freese 2026).  
   **Route B, outward (add a low-overlap channel):** combine geometry with growth (RSD f sigma8, weak lensing, clusters, CMB lensing). Fig 2 :31: "Independence, between two frames, is how little they draw on the same evidence." Lee 2026 finds distance data have one dominant information mode and growth can open a second, but in Euclid-like forecasts only at percent-level growth precision. Fig 2 :35: "Alignment is triangulation, not negotiation. A mirror cannot correct you."

3. **Sigmas are filters, not witnesses.** Fig 9 :60: "Use filters for the budget and witnesses for the verdict. Never let a filter hand you a verdict." The current DESI DR2 numbers (1.7 sigma BAO alone, 3.1 sigma with CMB, 2.8 to 4.2 sigma with SNe) rank how hard to look. They do not decide the source. A future 5 sigma threshold is a convention, and "a convention that decides an outcome has stopped being one" (Fig 10 :47).

4. **One detection does not close the union.** A background w not equal to -1 leaves the intersections standing: modified gravity, interacting dark sectors, sound-horizon calibration, neutrino mass and systematics (Fig 9 :55-57). Route B is also what separates a changing fluid from modified gravity: comparing f sigma8 with weak lensing tests gravitational slip, because the two respond to different potentials.

5. **The single-name question is a gap, reported as unreached.** No figure line names a mission, a redshift or a significance threshold (Fig 9 :32: "you get a kind of thing, never the answer itself"; Fig 13 :135). What is already returning something that fails to resolve (the DESI hint) gives an edge to extend from (Fig 6 :34), so this is `unreached`, not `unreachable`. Fig 8 :39: "So a search that finds nothing is never a proof of absence."

## 3. What it explicitly does not say

- Not "DESI DR2 discovered evolving dark energy." The hint is sample- and representation-dependent, and one evidence pool is not a witness (Fig 2 :40).
- Not "Euclid or Roman will settle it." They are instruments that *could* close the gap; no forecast is a ground reading.
- Not a source name. The framework returns a shape, not a name. Choosing rho(z) over w(z) is a provenanced binding from the physics frame (JCAP 2026), not a derivation.
- Not that a fixed sigma threshold decides the question. A gate is not the ground.
- Not a closed binary. The union's intersections are named and left open.
- Not `grounded`. No measurement made by this derivation settles anything.

## 4. What would settle it - the ground

A source-discriminating detection, not another argument. Concretely, a detection of rho_DE(z) variation or w(z) not equal to -1 that survives all of the following, each of which is a different refusal seat:

1. **Representation refusal:** the signal persists when rho_DE(z) is reconstructed as a free function, not only under linear w(a) (Wang and Freese 2026).
2. **Sample refusal:** the signal persists across independent supernova compilations (Pantheon+, Union3, DESY5) and BAO datasets; if it appears only in one pool it is the pool talking (Fig 2 :40).
3. **Channel refusal:** growth and geometry agree, or their disagreement is itself the discovered structure; a growth channel at percent-level precision plus weak lensing tests for gravitational slip and separates the confounders of F7.
4. **Systematics refusal:** known smooth systematics cannot mimic the redshift-dependent feature; Gu et al. 2025 report that the oscillatory w(z) feature survives photometric-offset and BAO-shift checks because those vary smoothly with redshift.
5. **Anchor refusal:** the result is recorded with its lineage so a later reader can locate the error rather than only feel it (Fig 10 :62-64).

Nothing in this list is a request for agreement. A failure is more useful than an endorsement.

## 5. The honest stop

**Unreached, not unreachable.** Current instruments are already returning a reading that fails to resolve: a 2.8 to 4.2 sigma, representation-dependent preference for evolution. There is an edge to extend from, so the search is for a better discriminator, not for a hypothetical sensor. The framework stops at the limit of what the available instruments distinguish and records the lineage so the stop is checkable. Fig 13 :134: "It can say the answer is out of reach with the library as it stands, so something has to be imported before it is asked again." *Indiscernibility is not identity.* If no channel at any resolution ever registers a source-dependent difference, the honest verdict changes to `unreachable`, which is a verdict and not silence.

## 6. One-paragraph version for outreach

> A constant cosmological term and changing dark energy currently give the same integrated distance reading from different sources, so the reading cannot comment on the source. The observation that would most clearly distinguish them is therefore not a tighter fit to the same curve. It is a measurement that makes the source register, and it has two structural routes: differentiate the expansion history across redshift, measuring the dark-energy density as a free function so the time variation is the signal; or add a genuinely independent channel, such as growth of structure, so geometry and growth can be ranged against the same ground. A significance number is a filter for how hard to look, never the verdict. The current DESI hint is an edge to extend from, not a discovery. The framework returns that shape and four refuters written in advance, not the name of a telescope or a threshold. The center stays open until a source-discriminating instrument, checked across representations, samples and channels, settles it.
