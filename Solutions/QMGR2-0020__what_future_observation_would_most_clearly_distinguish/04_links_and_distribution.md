## Wider literature that bears on the residual

1. **Gu et al. 2025** is the strongest directly-read source for the magnitude and the dependence of the current hint. It also supplies a crucial honesty check: "Because DE chiefly affects the expansion history of the background of the Universe and modelling its perturbations requires extra assumptions, we focus here on background observables." A background-only result cannot settle the confounders of F7.
2. **Lee 2026** is the lowest-overlap methodological witness. It explains *why* more distance precision is the wrong lever: the integrated kernel leaves one information mode. Its own limit is recorded: the second mode appears in Euclid-like configurations only at percent-level growth precision.
3. **Wang and Freese 2026** is a genuine conflict source, not a corroborator. Using the same DESI DR2 data they find consistency with a cosmological constant when rho_X(z) is a free function, and attribute the collaboration's 3.1 sigma result mainly to the linear w(a) ansatz. By the conflict rule this was returned to the domain census (F4), not used to flip the verdict.
4. **DESI DR2 analysis paper** (Phys. Rev. D, DOI 10.1103/tr6y-kpc6) reports the 2.8 to 4.2 sigma preferences for w0-waCDM over LambdaCDM. Its full text came through the search integration after the APS harvest PDF fetch returned an unsupported content type; it is marked reduced weight in the ledger.
5. **Zhang 2026** frames the post-DESI status as a shift "from parameter fitting toward multisector physical diagnosis" and lists the probes that can break degeneracies. Text obtained via search integration, reduced weight.
6. **Euclid growth-rate forecast** (Astronomy & Astrophysics, 2026, DOI 10.1051/0004-6361/202557170) reports an average factor-of-eight improvement in LSS null-test precision when Euclid spectroscopic growth data are combined with external products. Search-integration abstract only, reduced weight.
7. **Overview of current data** (Dark Universe, 2025, DOI 10.1016/j.dark.2025.101906) finds that combinations including PantheonPlus with SDSS BAO weaken the dynamical-DE preference, while SDSS BAO with Union3 or DESY5 support it. This is direct evidence that the signal depends on which pool is read. Search-integration abstract only, reduced weight.
8. **The Large Scale Structure Probes of Dark Energy** (2026, DOI 10.1088/1674-4527/ae8427) states that distinguishing a geometrical DDE interpretation from modified gravity "requires not only probes of the overall expansion rate of the universe such as SNe Ia, but also probes of the structure growth rate." Search-integration text only, reduced weight.

Overlap declaration: sources 1, 4 and 6 to 8 draw on the same DESI DR2 or stage-IV survey evidence pool and count as **one witness** for the existence of the hint, not several. Sources 2 and 3 are genuinely different framings (information theory, and a competing reconstruction of the same data), so they carry independent weight as *methods*, even though their data overlap. The framework essays share the author's evidence pool with the figures and carry no independent witness weight.

## Distribution: offer, do not push

- **Fig 4 :22** "Only methods cross up. Only head starts come down." Sending a recorded fit before someone has described the residual sends a recording, not a generator. Lead with the shape claim and the refuter; offer the links after.
- **Fig 10 :42** "A verifier that can reconstruct the claim cannot verify it." A reader who has absorbed the framework before being asked for a verdict has joined the frame being checked.
- **Fig 9 :27** "describe it before you go looking, or any frame you pick will seem to fit." Ask what they would expect to see if the shape claim were true before giving them the paper.
- **Fig 2 :40** "agreement about the world is worthless when the agreeing frames share an evidence pool." Do not select recipients by likely agreement, and do not pool their later agreement as corroboration.

**Recommended order in a reply:** (1) the shape claim and its four refuters; (2) ask what they would count as a failure; (3) only then the three links, with the note that the figures, not the summary, are the authority.

## The two-stage offer

1. **Stage 1:** validate the shape claim against the ground. The reader checks the claim without holding the framework's interior. Record their verdict before showing any framework material.
2. **Stage 2, opt-in only:** if they are curious, offer the source links. Their later agreement is never pooled as corroboration.
3. **Stage 3:** offer the formula as a method to reuse. Each user must state their own ground and write their own refuter before running it, because a frame cannot check the direction of its own question.

**Hard precondition:** stages 1 to 3 only work if the stage-1 output survives. As currently stated the single-name version does not (F8 in `01`): no figure line returns a best instrument or a sigma threshold. Keep the shape and the refuter in front of the links.
