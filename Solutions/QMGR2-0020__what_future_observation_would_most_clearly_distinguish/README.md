## Restating the question as an effect

Two hypotheses, a constant cosmological term (w = -1, constant dark-energy density) and changing dark energy (w(z) varying, hence rho_DE(z) varying), currently produce the same observed reading of the expansion history. Each leads to the same fitted distance curve; only their source differs. The question asks for the observation that would make that hidden source register. In effect terms: what instrument closes a metamerism, where a reading cannot comment on its source. What an answer would look like is bound in `01` section 0 as the ordinary term DISCRIMINATOR, so it can accrue a residual and be refused rather than left as a preference (Formula "Direction"; Fig 5 :65).

## What is deliberately not here

- No personal emails of named individuals.
- No ranking of people by how likely they are to agree.
- No bulk-send plan.

Reasons, in the figures' own words:

1. **Agreement is not an anchor.** Fig 2 :35: "Alignment is triangulation, not negotiation. A mirror cannot correct you." Fig 2 :29: "An anchor is whatever settles the question and does not change its answer because of what any frame thinks."
2. **Correlation disqualifies a verdict.** Fig 2 :40: "agreement about the world is worthless when the agreeing frames share an evidence pool." A contact list selected for receptiveness manufactures the correlation that would make its agreement worthless.
3. **The checker must not hold the interiors.** Fig 10 :42: "A verifier that can reconstruct the claim cannot verify it." Fig 10 :52: "Bring a characterised residual, never the source."

Legally: scraping personal emails for unsolicited bulk outreach breaches GDPR and CAN-SPAM style rules in many jurisdictions. Public opt-in and institutional channels avoid that failure as well as the structural one.
