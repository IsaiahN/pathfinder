# Expert verification packet: the Tether on LQG area and volume quantization

**What this is:** a claim in a form a physicist can check. Mode, derivation and refuters are stated before the conclusion, so a reader knows what to look for and what would settle it.

**Mode: `specified`, not `grounded`.** No Planck-scale measurement is claimed. This is a shape and a route, not a result.

## 1. The claim, in one paragraph

Discreteness is not a primitive added to the six-term set. It is what a composition leaves behind, and the smooth continuum is likewise a derivative that must be earned as a low-energy limit. A discrete geometry spectrum is a threshold on an ordered set, so a gap is a structural relation rather than a new operator. Area and volume are isomers over the same held atoms, arranged differently. The framework licenses that shape but does not license the numerical value of the minimum eigenvalue or its dependence on the Immirzi parameter; those are bindings from the LQG frame, provenanced and still ungrounded. The live ground question is whether the kinematic discreteness survives the move to a gauge-invariant observable.

## 2. Three checkable sub-claims

### C1 - framework-internal; checkable by reading Figure 13

- **Statement:** the continuum is not a primitive, and discreteness is a composition remainder. The correct question is which composition leaves which remainder at which scale, not which of the two is fundamental.
- **Source, verbatim:** Fig 13 :43-44 *"A continuum was the seventh and was removed for failing exactly this: it is a spectrum without gaps, or smoothness over space and time. A generating set must not contain what its own members produce."* Fig 13 :124 *"Smoothness, symmetry, chaos and discreteness are each what a particular composition leaves behind, and none is a seventh member."*
- **Refuter:** derive discreteness or the continuum as an irreducible seventh term, or show a composition that leaves neither. If either holds, this finding falls.

### C2 - framework-internal; checkable by reading the Operators table and Figure 13

- **Statement:** a gap in a geometry spectrum is a threshold relation on an ordered set, not a new operator and not a uniform lattice.
- **Source, verbatim:** Fig 13 :19-20 *"ordering, so more and less can be said"* and the Operators table :47-51 *"a threshold relation between two quantities"* with the test *"is it about which is larger, rather than both being present? Yes."*
- **What to check:** is the LQG area/volume spectrum an ordered set with a threshold between successive values? Does any claim of equal spacing come from the LQG frame rather than from the figures?
- **Refuter:** show that the gap is not a threshold on an ordering, or exhibit a figure line that requires a regular lattice. The pixel-grid analogue is declined in `06_isomers_and_order.md` Q4 until a source pays for it.

### C3 - physics-facing; checkable against the published LQG literature

- **Statement:** the discrete spectra are kinematic results; whether they survive gauge-invariant completion is unsettled. The figures do not adjudicate this and do not need to, because it is a ground question in the physics frame.
- **Source, verbatim:** Fig 5 amendment in the figure text: *"settling is necessary for a term to be composed over, and it is not sufficient."* Fig 2 :29 *"An anchor is whatever settles the question and does not change its answer because of what any frame thinks."*
- **What to check:** Dittrich and Thiemann (arXiv:0708.1721) find that *"kinematical discreteness of the spectrum does not necessarily survive at the gauge invariant level."* Rovelli (arXiv:0708.2481) replies that *"within one of these, the derivation of physical discreteness is immediate, and not in contradiction with gauge invariance."* Which frame has paid, and on what instrument?
- **Refuter:** a settled, gauge-invariant area or volume observable with a continuous spectrum removes the LQG binding. It does not remove C1 or C2, which are figure claims.

## 3. What is explicitly not claimed

- Not a theory of everything; no final room. The compound at the contact keeps its own loop.
- Not a confirmation of LQG. Nothing here is asserted as settled by experiment.
- Not a numerical minimum area or volume, not a value of γ, and not a formula for the gap. No source opened in this run supplies a verified coefficient.
- Not that discreteness is fundamental. It is a composition remainder.
- Not that the continuum is false. It is a derivative that has to be derived.
- Not that Lorentz bounds refute the shape. Fermi GRB 090510 constrains a linear energy dependence of photon speed, which is a different question.
- Not that the kinematic spectrum is a physical observable. That is the live dispute in C3.
