## 1. The residual it answers

A finite region of space has two measured slots: the area of its boundary and the volume of its interior. Classical general relativity treats both as continuum values on a smooth manifold. Loop quantum gravity derives operators with discrete spectra. The residual is not "which theory wins"; it is the category question: what kind of thing is a gap in a geometry spectrum? Fig 9 :54-58 gives the discipline for a bundled question: a case in two conditions is not a harder case of either. The continuum limit and the discrete microstructure are a **union compound**, each with its own loop and its own ground.

## 2. The shape
