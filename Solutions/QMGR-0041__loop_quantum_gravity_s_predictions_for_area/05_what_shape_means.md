## 1. A shape is a constraint, not a filler

The canonical README states the limit: the framework returns *"what a missing thing would have to look like (a shape, not a name)."* Fig 9 :32: *"you get a kind of thing, never the answer itself."* Fig 13 :134: *"Unreachable is a verdict here, not a silence."*

From The Library of Babel (opened this session):

> *"you can cut your search short if the set of books returned is small and descriptive enough that it provides a general shape of the answer that satisfies your question."*

So a **shape** is the form of an answer you can state before you can name its coefficient. It constrains; it does not fill. For this question:

- **Shape:** a gapped ordering over a finite region, with a smooth continuum that is a derivative and must be earned.
- **Name:** the lowest nonzero area eigenvalue and its dependence on γ and the Planck area.

The first is what the figures license. The second belongs to the LQG frame.

## 2. How a shape is obtained

From The Library of Babel (opened this session):

> *"If you don't know the right terms to search for, you will have to use the existing vocabulary you do know that is closest to the words that you wish to describe and grasp the shape of."*

> *"With each new term gained, you are able to narrow the space by definition and deepen your vocabulary."*

Applied here: start from the effect (a finite region returns two values, and the question is whether the ordering has gaps), not from the name "loop quantum gravity." The Figures' own rule agrees. Fig 6 :30: *"The description travels. Fails by being sharpened into terms only we can read. Cast it in effect terms."* Fig 9 :30-31: *"your words are borrowed too, so describe the effect rather than the cause."*

The essay's stopping rule is also the framework's: *"you will know that you are done because the finish line will also be the starting place."* For this question the stop is not the coefficient. It is the point at which the category of the gap is fixed and the remaining work is clearly an instrument question.

## 3. What licenses a name

Two things, both outside the shape:

1. **The ground settles it.** `THE_FORMULA.md` step 5: *"the ground settles it. Verified only by what pays. A gate is not the ground."* No Planck-scale measurement pays here.
2. **A provenanced import from a frame whose closure differs**, with a source gate and a shadow test (Fig 6 :23, Fig 8 :50-52). The Rovelli-Smolin derivation satisfies both: it is dated, published, and it explains the residual rather than merely resembling it.

**Correction carried forward.** The exemplar QMGR-0037 retracted an over-broad reading of "shape, not a name": the limit governs a *missing* primitive, not the selection among atoms already in Γ. Here `spectrum`, `space` and `network` are held atoms, so naming them is a legal binding. What is *not* licensed is the numerical coefficient, which is not a framework term at all. Both readings are kept visible so neither is quietly lost.

## 4. Why the coefficient cannot be named from a reading

From The Hard Problem is About Source (opened this session):

> *"If several paths create the same behavior, then behavior is a projection with lossy information (it cannot define consciousness in full)."*

> *"It was proven wrong not by argument, but by the arrival of a better instrument."*

The essay's metamerism case: one green reading can come from one green light, or yellow plus blue, or RGB, or CMYK, or a scheme not yet discovered. The instrument cannot comment on the source. The spectrometer resolved it, not an argument.

For this question: a discrete spectrum reading does not by itself identify the source of the gap. Several frames can produce a gapped ordering. Naming the Immirzi-dependent coefficient from the reading alone repeats the metamerism error. What is owed is a source-discriminating instrument, which does not yet exist at the Planck scale. Fig 6 :32: *"An instrument is not built from a description. It is improved from a worse instrument already returning something."*

## 5. The honesty rule for this run

From The Recursive Self-Improvement Trap (opened this session):

> *"the output is only as good as the validator. The agent, or the tool, or the frame, or the theory, cannot validate itself."*

> *"LLMs can quickly simulate an approximate answer/output with the formula found in the architecture post. It will often be better than the generic outputs that you will usually get otherwise too... However, the truly testable output would need to be derived from the real architecture or working the formula yourself."*

So this file, and every other file in the folder, is a simulated derivation checked for internal consistency against the figures. It is not a test of LQG, and it cannot settle the question it reports on.

## 6. Prestige is not action

Same essay (opened this session):

> *"Prestige must never be allowed to validate an action directly."*

> *"Prestige can fund an action. It can give an action a budget to be tried. But it cannot settle an action. Only the ground can settle it. The Ground Maintainer must be blind to prestige."*

A campaign that gathers famous names to agree that area is discrete is the named collapse, not corroboration. The legitimate ask is the opposite: give the claim a budget to be tried, ask what would refute it, and let an instrument settle it. This is why `03_public_contact_points.csv` contains venues and opt-in channels, not a list of believers.
