## 0. The residual, stated in effect terms

The contact problem is not "is LQG right." It is this: classical general relativity uses a smooth spatial manifold on which area and volume take continuum values, while loop quantum gravity derives operators whose spectra are discrete. What is being decided is the microstructure of a finite region of space.

- **Arity.** One quantity under two readings (a finite region's geometry). Area and volume are two distinct slots, not two instances of one slot, because they scale differently and are measured by different operators.
- **Symmetry.** A spectrum is an ordering: "ordering, so more and less can be said" (Fig 13 :19-20, Symbols table). The question is whether the ordering has gaps. Gaps are not a symmetric relation between two equal operands; they are a threshold fact about successive values.
- **Scale.** Planck scale, roughly 10^-35 m. No current instrument returns a geometry reading at that resolution.

**What an answer would look like.** A shape statement (a gapped ordering whose smooth continuum is a derivative) plus a frame-specific coefficient (the lowest nonzero eigenvalue and its dependence on the Immirzi parameter and the Planck area). The first is a figure claim. The second is a binding from the physics frame, provenance-bearing, and ungrounded.

## 3. Findings

### F1 - Discreteness is a derivative, not a primitive (decisive for the shape)

Fig 13 :43-44:

> **"A continuum was the seventh and was removed for failing exactly this: it is a spectrum without gaps, or smoothness over space and time. A generating set must not contain what its own members produce."**

Fig 13 :124-125:

> **"Smoothness, symmetry, chaos and discreteness are each what a particular composition leaves behind, and none is a seventh member."**

`THE_FORMULA.md` substrate section adds that the derived quantities (*"symmetry, smoothness, chaos, a continuum"*) are what a turn leaves rather than members of the set. Three independent figure passages, low overlap between them, so the shape is **triangulated**.

Consequence: LQG's discrete area and volume are not a new primitive added to the six. They are the remainder a composition leaves. The smooth continuum is likewise not a primitive. So the question "continuum or discrete?" is posed wrongly if it asks which one is in the floor. Both are what a turn leaves at different scales. The framework's shape prediction is: **a gapped ordering at one scale with a smooth limit that must be derived**, not assumed.

**Refuter:** derive discreteness or the continuum from the other five members as an irreducible sixth or seventh, or show a composition that leaves neither. If either holds, this finding falls.

### F2 - The residual is a spectrum question, and a gap is a threshold

Symbols table and Fig 13 :19-20: *"ordering, so more and less can be said."* Operators table :47-51 defines ⋛ as *"a threshold relation between two quantities"* with the test *"is it about which is larger, rather than both being present? Yes."*

A discrete spectrum is an ordering in which the next value sits beyond a threshold. That is the framework's comparison bond applied to successive eigenvalues, not a new operator and not a new term. Mode: `general` for the structural reading, `specified` when applied to area and volume specifically.

**Refuter:** show that the gap in the LQG spectrum is not a threshold relation between ordered values (for example, that it is a label with no ordering), and this mapping fails.

### F3 - What the figures return is a shape, not the coefficient

Fig 9 :32: *"you get a kind of thing, never the answer itself."* Fig 6 :30: *"The description travels. Fails by being sharpened into terms only we can read. Cast it in effect terms."*

The canonical figures README (local copy) states the limit as *"what a missing thing would have to look like (a shape, not a name)."* The coefficient in LQG, the minimum nonzero eigenvalue and its dependence on γ and the Planck area, is a binding from the physics frame. It is not derivable from the six term set. It needs a source gate and a shadow test: Fig 8 :50-52 *"Convergent derivation and adopted import look identical in the contents. Only the record of where each came from separates them."*

**Correction carried forward from the exemplar, not silently erased.** In QMGR-0037 §5b and `06_isomers_and_order.md` Q1 the author retracted an over-broad reading: "shape, not a name" governs a *missing* primitive, not the selection among atoms already held. Here the correction is applied rather than ignored. `spectrum`, `space`, `gradient` and `network` are all held atoms, so naming them is a legal binding, not a forbidden invention of a seventh. What the limit does forbid is naming the *coefficient*, which is not a framework term at all and carries no figure provenance. Naming it as if the figures produced it repeats the metamerism error described in The Hard Problem is About Source (opened 2026-11-23): an instrument reads a value and cannot comment on the source.

**Refuter:** exhibit a derivation of the coefficient from the six term set and the seven bonds alone. Until one exists, the coefficient is `specified`, ungrounded, and attributed to the LQG frame.

### F4 - The smooth limit is owed, and it is an inverse problem

World corroboration (secondary, low overlap with the figures: philosophy of science and methodology). The Stanford Encyclopedia of Philosophy entry on Quantum Gravity (first published 2005, substantive revision 2024, opened 2026-11-23) states:

> *"The possibility that a continuous structure (with continuous symmetries) could emerge from a fundamentally discrete structure is a problem with a clear philosophical flavour"*

and, in §4.1:

> *"it is a major challenge of loop quantum gravity to show that it indeed has general relativity as a low-energy limit"*

This corroborates F1 from a different field: continuity is a limit to be derived, not a floor to be declared. Fig 13 :43 already says the continuum was removed because a generating set must not contain what its members produce. The world source predicts the same direction of work. **Triangulated** across one figure passage and one low-overlap world source. The world source is not a witness for the figure verdict; it is corroboration only.

**Refuter:** a demonstration that the smooth limit is a primitive and not derivable, or a completed low-energy derivation that makes the continuum exact at all scales.

### F5 - A genuine world conflict, handled by re-census rather than by flipping the verdict

Two world sources opened 2026-11-23 disagree on the central physics claim.

- Bianca Dittrich and Thomas Thiemann, "Are the spectra of geometrical operators in Loop Quantum Gravity really discrete?" (arXiv:0708.1721, J. Math. Phys. 50:012503, 2009): *"we find, even in those simpler cases, that kinematical discreteness of the spectrum does not necessarily survive at the gauge invariant level... This indicates that ``fundamental discreteness at Planck scale in LQG'' is far from established."*
- Carlo Rovelli, "Comment on ..." (arXiv:0708.2481, 2007): *"the prediction of physical discreteness at the Planck scale in loop gravity is a reasonable conclusion that derives from a sensible ensemble of hypotheses, in spite of some contrary arguments"* and *"within one of these, the derivation of physical discreteness is immediate, and not in contradiction with gauge invariance."*

The framework rule is that a conflict sends the reader back to the census and never flips the verdict. Re-census result: this is not a figure conflict. It is a D2 settlement question. Fig 5's amendment (opened this session in the figure text) says settling is necessary for composition and not sufficient; here the dispute is whether the term is settled at all. Fig 2 :29-30: *"An anchor is whatever settles the question and does not change its answer because of what any frame thinks."* The two papers are two frames ranging against the same ground, and the ground has not paid. The figures classify the dispute; they do not adjudicate it. Verdict unchanged: the shape stands, the binding stays `candidate`, and the conflict is recorded as a live strain in D2.

**Refuter:** a settled, gauge-invariant area or volume observable that returns a continuous spectrum would remove the LQG binding. It would not remove F1, because F1 is about the category of discreteness, not about which frame pays for it.

### F6 - Reachability: SEARCHED first, then INWARD, then OUTWARD

The formula's step 7 separates three questions. They are kept distinct.

Where nothing is, there is no edge to extend from, and the search for a proto-instrument is a search for one that is already there."* Verdict: **unreached at this budget.**
- **OUTWARD (import, with source gate and shadow test).** The LQG results are a legitimate import from a frame whose closure differs. **Do not conflate.** Underivable-in-principle and unreachable-at-this-budget are different facts with different instructions (Fig 8 :40-43). Importing against a budget limit would add an atom that was not needed. This run imports one binding with provenance and refuses the rest.

### F7 - What LQG actually predicts, reported without upgrading its mode

From Rovelli and Smolin (1994/1995), opened 2026-11-23:

> *"We find that the spectrum of the volume of any physical region is discrete."*

> *"Furthermore, we show that the eigenstates of the volume diagonalize also the area operator."*

From SEP §3.2 (opened 2026-11-23):

> *"In the loop quantum gravity program, the area and volume operators have discrete spectra. Thus, like electron spins, they can only take certain values. This suggests (but does not imply) that space itself has a discrete nature, and perhaps time as well"*

Reported in `specified` mode: discrete spectra for area and volume over any finite region, a family of eigenstates in one-to-one correspondence with spin networks, and a shared eigenbasis for volume and area. The lowest nonzero value depends on the Immirzi parameter; SEP §4.2 notes that LQG gets black-hole entropy right *"only up to an overall constant."* No source opened here supplies a verified numerical coefficient, so none is asserted. Mode stays `specified`. **Single-source for the coefficient question**, triangulated for the qualitative discreteness (Rovelli-Smolin plus SEP plus the figure shape).

## 4. Pre-registered refuter (written before the conclusion)

Required before any outreach, per the seat map: a falsifier written before the run is a design instrument. The shape claim is refuted if any of the following holds:

1. A seventh primitive is derived from the six, or discreteness or the continuum is shown to be indivisible and not a composition remainder (breaks F1).
2. A settled, gauge-invariant area or volume observable is demonstrated to have a continuous spectrum in the relevant regime (removes the LQG binding; does not remove F1).
3. A direct Planck-scale geometry measurement returns a smooth continuum at every resolution where the shape predicts a gap.
4. A figure line is found to require strain to fit after re-census (the line governs another quantity; the census is redone).

If any fires, the affected claim is withdrawn and the earlier reading stays visible in this log. Until one is run, no part of this may be labelled `grounded`.
