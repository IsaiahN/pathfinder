# The Tether's answer: what would distinguish reasoning from its imitation

**Mode: `specified` on the separator and the per-domain verdicts, `general` on the structural shape, `gap` on a single output-internal detector or a numeric threshold. Not `grounded`.** This is a simulated derivation checked for internal consistency against the figures, not a ground reading. It does not assert that any named system does or does not reason.

## 1. The residual it answers

We are handed an output: an answer, a chain of steps, a confidence. The same output could come from a loop that bet, observed a per-slot residual and settled its terms against a ground, or from a replay that has learned the surface of such a chain. The question asks for evidence that tells them apart. The figures answer that the evidence is not in the output. Their agreement is exact, from three independent directions:

- Fig 8 :51-52: "Convergent derivation and adopted import look identical in the contents. Only the record of where each came from separates them, and the surplus lives only where the closures differ."
- Fig 13 :128-129: the figure point that a composed term does not reveal its route of composition by inspection
- *How Intelligence Actually Works*: "The frame and the photograph are indistinguishable as artifacts, and they carry different warrants. One was taken; one was extracted. And nothing in the image records which."

So the literal request fails against the figures, and the failure is the answer. The question is a union of five conditions, not one detector (Fig 9 :54-58). Verdicts are kept per domain and never averaged into a reasoning score (Fig 1 :19, :22; Fig 9 :60).

## 2. The shape: five domains, five separators

**D1, the output.** No anchor is nameable from inside. Fluency, coherence, step count and confidence are quantities the frame produces (Fig 1 :24-27: "They are frame-internal. A frame cannot score itself with a quantity it also produces"). Verdict: `general`, disqualified as a witness. It may be used as a filter for the budget, never as a witness for the verdict (Fig 9 :60).

**D2, the loop.** Actor: Γ and the eight operators. Currency: a residual indexed per slot, settled by what pays. Anchor: the ground as the only metric (Fig 1 :23). Separator: is there a live per-slot residual that drives a mint, and was the term settled by a gate that pays? A system whose own output is its only success measure fails here (Fig 5 :48: "Its own output is never a measure of success"). Verdict: `specified`, triangulated with the mint guards.

**D3, the checking arrangement.** Actors: maker, interior auditor, ground maintainer, outside frame. Currency: independence, provenance, refuters. Anchor: a non-updating anchor (Fig 2 :29). Separator: did the answer survive a frame that could not have produced it, ranging separately against an anchor it could not move? Agreement among frames sharing an evidence pool is worthless as a verdict (Fig 2 :40), and a verifier that can reconstruct the claim cannot verify it (Fig 10 :42). Verdict: `specified`, triangulated across Fig 2 and Fig 10.

**D4, the habitat.** Actors: agent and habitat. Currency: contact and cascade. Anchor: the ground, which decides and does not update. Separator: break the cue channel and watch performance collapse; or substitute the habitat and ask whether the capability survives. "A test harness is a substituted habitat. Which is why a synthetic solve proves wiring and never capability" (Fig 11 :70). Verdict: `specified`, triangulated with the shortcut-learning literature.

**D5, the lineage.** Actors: the record with origin stamps and seq, the refusal. Currency: derivability and decidability. Anchor: whether the route was available and settled. Separator: the record of where the answer came from, including the term that could have been refused. But provenance is necessary and not sufficient: "Provenance shows a ruling was reached legitimately. It never shows it was right: the ground does that" (Fig 10 :65). Verdict: `specified`, single-source for the figure, decisive.

## 3. The practical separator, in probe order

1. Write down what would count as a failure before running anything. Fig 5 :62-66 requires the sought shape to be bound as an ordinary term so it can be refused; the Seat Map calls a pre-registered falsifier a design instrument.
2. Ask the cheapest question first: is the discriminator already in the evaluation we hold? Fig 1 :24-27 says no.
3. Find an existing reading that already fails to resolve and sharpen it by cutting the cue channel. Fig 6 :34: "Where nothing is, there is no edge to extend from." The Clever Hans result is the shape: 89 percent correct when the questioner was visible and knew the answer, 6 percent when the horse could not see the questioner. The discriminator was an intervention on the channel, not a rating of the output.
4. Demand the record: origins, seq, the per-slot residual that drove each mint, and the refusal that could have been written. A refusal is ¬ (Operators :58), not a subtraction; an output vocabulary without it can only accumulate.
5. Check whether failure accumulates traction. From *They're Scanning Books*: "The human fails by searching; the decompressor fails by guessing... The bar is whether failure accumulates traction, not whether the answer arrives." A sequence of attempts that moves against a residual is evidence of a loop; a fluent one-shot answer is not.
6. Only then eliminate at the ground, with cuts kept ranked and reversible (Fig 9 :40).

## 4. What it explicitly does not say

- Not that any named current system reasons or fails to reason. No measurement was taken.
- Not that fluent answers are always imitations. Fluency is a budget filter, not a verdict.
- Not that imitation is impossible to distinguish ever. The distinction horizon is reported, not declared final. A source-discriminating instrument could change the verdict, as spectrometry changed the metamerism case.
- Not that provenance establishes correctness. A perfectly provenanced wrong ruling is an unimpeachable record of nothing (Fig 10 :66).
- Not that a bigger panel settles it. Ten readers of one transcript are one frame wearing ten names (Fig 8 :48-49).
- Not a numeric threshold. Fig 9 :32 returns a kind of thing, not a number.

## 5. What would settle it: the ground

A discriminator would have to be an instrument, not a further argument. *The Hard Problem is About Source*: an instrument that reads green "cannot comment on the source"; the question was resolved "not by argument, but by the arrival of a better instrument." Here the candidates are:

- a channel-break protocol that reliably collapses one class of systems and not the other, pre-registered before the run;
- a record format in which convergent derivation and adopted import are distinguishable by provenance, with the record verified against an independent source (Fig 8 :50-52);
- a source-discriminating measure that separates "taken" from "extracted" answers, with known-positive and known-negative fixtures, because a known-positive fixture proves sensitivity and never specificity (Seat Map, failure genera);
- an anchor that does not update on the evaluation itself: an outcome, a proof checker, a market, a patient outcome (Fig 2 :29).

Standing falsifiers from `01` §4: an output-only route reader; an agent-only capability gain at fixed contact; sound verification by a checker that can reconstruct the claim; evidence created by agreement inside one pool; identical behaviour under every contact-changing intervention for systems with different routes; a figure line selecting a detector.

## 6. The honest stop

**Unreached, not unreachable.** Fig 8 :39: "So a search that finds nothing is never a proof of absence." Fig 13 :134: "Unreachable is a verdict here, not a silence,". Fig 13 :135: "It cannot say which primitive is missing, because naming it would mean already holding it." The figures return the shape of the evidence and the order of the probe. They do not return a detector, and the essays add that the horizon is always "a report that nobody has reached it yet," never a settled verdict. Indiscernibility is not identity. The lineage is kept so the stop is checkable.

## 7. One-paragraph version for outreach

> The evidence that distinguishes reasoning from its imitation is not in the answer. A video frame and a photograph taken from it can be pixel-identical while carrying different warrants, and nothing in the image records which. The same is true of a fluent chain of steps. What can distinguish them is outside the output: a residual indexed per slot with the ground as the only metric, a record from which the route is recoverable, independent checkers ranging against an anchor that does not move, and a cue channel that can be broken to see whether performance collapses. The right kind of failure matters more than the right answer; a system that revises a partial model against a ground fails by searching, while a decompressor of human output fails by guessing. The figures do not return a reasoning detector or a numeric threshold. They return the shape the evidence must have, the order in which to probe for it, and the refuters written before the run. Anything less is a convention about what to call reasoning, not a verdict about what happened.
