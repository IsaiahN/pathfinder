## 1. The governing sentence for this question

*How Intelligence Actually Works* gives the exact case, in a thought experiment about a video frame and a photograph:

> "The frame and the photograph are indistinguishable as artifacts, and they carry different warrants. One was taken; one was extracted. And nothing in the image records which."

The same essay states the general law:

> "often, very little about a composed term tells you, from looking at it alone, which route produced it."

And the provenance consequence:

> "The recursion converges toward indiscernibility, and indiscernibility is not identity."

A **shape** here is the statement of which warrant an answer would need, written before the answer is available. It is not a list of surface tells. For REA-0030 the shape is: an answer that was "taken" by a loop in contact with a ground, rather than "extracted" as a plausible trace, would carry a record of the route, would survive the breaking of the cue channel, and would fail in the search-like way rather than the guessing-like way. None of those is readable off the artefact.

## 2. A shape is a constraint, not a filler

The figures README states the limit: the framework returns "what a missing thing would have to look like (a shape, not a name)". Fig 9 :32: "you get a kind of thing, never the answer itself". Fig 13 :135: "It cannot say which primitive is missing, because naming it would mean already holding it."

*The Library of Babel* gives the operational form:

> "You can cut your search short if the set of books returned is small and descriptive enough that it provides a general shape of the answer that satisfies your question."

> "With each new term gained, you are able to narrow the space by definition and deepen your vocabulary."

Applied here: the eight essays and the figures narrow the space from "find a reasoning detector" to "state the conditions under which a verdict about a route could be settled." That narrowing is the shape. It does not name a detector.

## 3. The distinction horizon: why the answer is not a detector

*How Intelligence Actually Works* supplies the vocabulary the figures imply but do not name:

- **Distinction horizon:** "There exists a 'distinction horizon' for every individual observer, the point at which the observer cannot discern any difference between one generation method and another."
- **Union horizon:** "A projection sinks below the union horizon when no observer, with any instrument any of them can build, can discriminate its origin."
- **Its status:** "Being 'below the union horizon' is never a settled verdict, only a report that nobody has reached it yet."

This converts cleanly into Fig 8 :39 ("So a search that finds nothing is never a proof of absence") and Fig 13 :134 ("Unreachable is a verdict here, not a silence,"). A failed attempt to distinguish reasoning from imitation is not proof that no distinction exists, and it is not proof that one does. It is a report about the current union horizon. The honest answer to the question is therefore a shape plus a recorded horizon, not a Yes/No about a detector.

The essay also prevents an overstatement. It distinguishes cases where no trace remains from cases where a trace exists but no instrument yet reads it. The diamond case: isotope ratios genuinely differed, so when the instrument arrived the distinction was recovered. So "imitation and reasoning are forever indistinguishable" is **not** a claim this solution makes. The claim is narrower: from the output alone, with the record and the ground withheld, the figures and the essays give no discriminator; where a trace exists, a better instrument could change the verdict, and that is exactly why the horizon is reported rather than declared final.

## 4. How a shape is obtained

- *Library of Babel*: use the vocabulary already held that is closest to the thing, look for new terms that overlap or correlate with it, recurse. "With each new term gained, you are able to narrow the space and deepen your vocabulary."
- *Decoding Tacit Knowledge*: "When contact isn't possible, the best you can send is a plain description of what you'd expect to see, because anyone can check that against the world." Also: "your words are borrowed too, so describe the effect rather than the cause" (Fig 9 :30-31, quoted in that essay).
- *Library of Babel* on the bundle: "Perhaps your problem was a bundle or conflation of several problems masquerading as one?" This is the essay's own version of Fig 9 :54-58, and it is exactly the move made in `01` §1.3: REA-0030 is a union of five conditions, not one detector question.
- *Library of Babel* on stopping: "You will know that you are done because the finish line will also be the starting place, as there is nowhere else to search and nothing else that can describe the answer better." The paradox: "By the time you learn the EXACT search terms to find the CORRECT book, you won't need to read it anymore, because you have already composed the answer through the process of locating it."

## 5. What licenses naming, and what this question is owed

Only two things license a name, both outside the shape: the ground settles a minted term (formula step 5: "the ground settles it. Verified only by what pays"), or a provenanced import with a source gate and a shadow test (Fig 6 :23, Fig 8 :50-52).

For REA-0030 the sought shape is "an output-internal discriminator exists." Writing it down as an ordinary term lets it accrue a residual and be refused by F1 and F11 of `01` rather than being smuggled in as a preference (Fig 5 :62-66, formula "Direction"). What is owed instead is a per-domain description of the evidence that would settle the route. Naming a specific current system as "genuinely reasoning" or "only imitating" would be a name without a ground reading, and this solution does neither.

### 5b. Correction to my own first reading

I initially wrote that the framework "cannot name the structure in the slot" as if it forbade naming any held atom. That overstates the rule. The README's phrase governs a **missing** primitive, one not held in Γ. Here the atoms are domains and conditions, and naming an already-available binding is provenance, not the invention of a new primitive. What remains forbidden is claiming that such a binding moves the wall (Fig 6 :21: "MINT composes inside the closure and can never add an atom"). This correction is kept visible rather than silently applied; `06` Q1 and `11` record it again.

## 6. Outputs, generators, and the failure that counts

*They're Scanning Books When They Should Be Studying Nature* is the closest world essay to this question:

> "A written sentence is a lossy compression of the reasoning that produced it."

> "When a model trains on text, it is not learning to think; it is learning the compressed traces of many minds' outputs and reconstructing the distribution that generated them."

> "The corpus contains the problems evolution already solved and someone wrote down. It does not contain the capacity to solve new ones, because that capacity lives in the living process, the mind generating, not in the text it generated."

Most importantly, it gives a behavioural separator that is *not* an output feature:

> "A human facing novel structure builds a partial model, tests it, notices it is wrong, and revises, even in failure, the mind moves; the failure is a search. A corpus model facing novel structure produces a fluent, confident, wrong answer and gains no traction over attempts, because there is no search, one forward pass pattern-matches to the nearest familiar thing and confabulates. The human fails by searching; the decompressor fails by guessing."

> "General intelligence is not reliable success on the novel... It is the right kind of failure: making progress through search-and-adaptation rather than confabulation. The bar is whether failure accumulates traction, not whether the answer arrives."

This is the strongest positive content in the answer. The discriminator is not whether the answer is right or fluent. It is whether *failure accumulates traction*: whether successive attempts move against a residual rather than re-pattern-matching. That is an output-observable-in-time property, but it requires a sequence of attempts against a ground, not a single answer. It fits F4 and F10 of `01`.

The essay also states the limit: "The real import is not a new term in the existing space. It is a new dimension of the space," and "Declaring the primitives does not make them neutral. It makes the choice visible." A test built on human priors is a visible choice, not a neutral instrument.

## 7. What would settle it, from the essays

- *The Hard Problem is About Source*: "If several paths create the same behavior, then behavior is a projection with lossy information." And the key experimental claim: "It was proven wrong not by argument, but by the arrival of a better instrument." For REA-0030, the instrument is a channel-break or a source-discriminating measure, not a further argument about fluency.
- *The Recursive Self-Improvement Trap*: "The catch is that the output is only as good as the validator. The agent, or the tool, or the frame, or the theory, cannot validate itself." Also: "You may all agree and be wrong." This is F3, F5 and F6.
- *Decoding Tacit Knowledge*: "Sounding right isn't the same as being checked against the world, which is why I call the Turing test a test of fluency, not of accuracy." This is the essay's own resolution of the apparent conflict with the world source on the Turing test, and it is why the conflict was returned to census rather than allowed to flip the verdict.
- *How a Society of Agents Would Work*: "Ten copies watching the same thing are one opinion said ten times," and the ground-voting rule from Fig 7. This is why a panel of raters cannot substitute for an anchor.
- *Arrival of the Fittest: The Tether Architecture*: "Because it is a metatheory, it cannot be validated, nor can it validate itself," and the demo caveat that outputs "could never be verified if the solutions presented were not contrived to fit the formula." This is the label on this very document.

## 8. Prestige is not action

From *The Recursive Self-Improvement Trap*: "Prestige must never be allowed to validate an action directly." And: "Prestige can fund an action. It can give an action a budget to be tried. But it cannot settle an action. Only the ground can settle it. The Ground Maintainer must be blind to prestige."

For this question: citing a famous endorser, a well-known benchmark, or a large number of agreement responses gives a claim a budget to be tried. It does not settle whether reasoning occurred. The evidence that settles it is the channel break, the record, and the non-updating anchor. This is why `03_public_contact_points.csv` contains institutional opt-in venues and no named individuals, and why the outreach offer asks for a refutation rather than agreement.
