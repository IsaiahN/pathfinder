## QUESTION

Decide, in effect terms, what evidence could separate a process that ranges against a ground from one that produces the same surface without that contact. Option A: a body of evidence obtainable from the output or behaviour alone, a detector. Option B: evidence in the contact, the record, the checking arrangement and the habitat. What each leads to: A treats a fluent answer as sufficient and collapses convergent derivation into adopted import; B returns a per-domain separator, a probe order and a pre-registered refuter, and reports the missing instrument as `unreached` rather than inventing one.

## DOMAIN CENSUS

R by arity, symmetry and scale, then one line per domain with actors, currency and anchor; partition or union; order by dependency.

- **R (the residual).** Gap between an output O and what would be observed if O came from a loop that ranged against a ground. Arity: one artefact, five overlapping conditions that could explain it, so a **union compound**, not a partition. Symmetry: "genuine" is an ordered relation to a non-updating anchor, a threshold and production question, not a symmetric `+` property of the text. Scale: loop inside a frame, verification between seats, capability in agent-and-habitat; the crossing is lossy (Fig 4 :27-30), so the artefact cannot carry the verdict.
- **D1, output.** Actors: answer text, confidence, step count. Currency: fluency, coherence, compression, credibility. Anchor: none nameable from inside. Union member, no verdict power. Verdict `general`, disqualified.
- **D2, loop.** Actors: Γ, operators, per-slot R. Currency: residuals, MDL bargain, settlement. Anchor: ground as only metric. Verdict `specified`, triangulated.
- **D3, seats.** Actors: maker, auditor, maintainer, outside frame. Currency: independence, provenance, refuters. Anchor: anchor that does not update. Verdict `specified`, triangulated.
- **D4, habitat.** Actors: agent and habitat, actors, conditions, relations. Currency: contact and cascade. Anchor: ground. Verdict `specified`, triangulated.
- **D5, lineage.** Actors: record, origin stamps, seq, refusal. Currency: derivability and decidability. Anchor: whether the route was available and settled. Verdict `specified`, single-source, necessary and not sufficient.
- **Order by dependency (Fig 3).** Describe R before naming a frame; establish anchor and channel before scoring a process; settle first-order terms before composing; allow refusal before mint; only then read the output. Probe order: SEARCHED first (cheapest), then channel-break INWARD, then provenanced OUTWARD import, then ground elimination (formula step 7; Fig 9 :43).

Re-census resolves it: Fig 10 governs reconstruction of the *claim* (content); the record is the *route* (origins, dates, receipts), which Seat 2 reads and Seat 3 must not. ## VERDICT (from the figures)

The literal question fails against the figures, and that failure is the result. No figure line governs an output-internal discriminator. Convergent derivation and adopted import are identical in the contents; only the record separates them; nothing about a composed term tells you the route by looking at it. Genuine reasoning is not a property of the output and not a property of the agent in isolation. It is a three-part relation: a loop with a per-slot residual and the ground as the only metric; a record from which the route is recoverable and a refusal can be written; and maintained contact with a habitat in which independent frames range against an anchor that does not update. Over the full shared atom set, a reasoning process and an imitation producing the same output are isomers: same atoms, different bond, invisible in the formula and decisive under intervention. The practical separator is a channel break, not a rating, and the positive behavioural signature is that failure accumulates traction rather than repeating a confident guess. This is `specified` on the separator, `general` on the shape, and a declared `gap` on any detector or numeric threshold. It is ungrounded.

- **cost if wrong:** bounded and locatable. The separator is stated per domain with a refuter before the conclusion, so an error is findable rather than invisible. The real cost is spending the independence of outside readers by sending a detector pitch or a list of surface tells instead of a residual and a refuter. That cost is priced by Fig 2 :42: "Buy independence in proportion to the cost of being wrong." It is avoidable by leading with the shape and the refuter.
- **undoable:** yes. The claimed detector and the numeric threshold are removable without touching the domain census; `07` and `08` already remove them. A sent claim can be superseded by the corrected packet, and no primitive has to be withdrawn. A refutation adds a term and removes nothing in the record; the reach falls because something was added (Fig 6 :42-43).
- **reversed by:** a ground reading, not agreement. A pre-registered channel-break protocol that reliably separates two classes would move D4; a source-discriminating instrument that separates "taken" from "extracted" answers would move D5; a record format that distinguishes convergence from import against an independent source would move D3; an agent-only improvement that moved the ground would refute Fig 11 :40; a verifier that can reconstruct the claim and still verifies it soundly would refute Fig 10 :42; a figure line selecting a detector would close the `gap`. Until one of these fires, the verdict stands as `candidate`.
