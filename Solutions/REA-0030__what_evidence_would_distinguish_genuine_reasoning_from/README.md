## The residual in effect terms

We are shown an output O: an answer, a chain of steps, a confident explanation. Two processes could have produced it. Process G ranges against a ground: it bets, observes a per-slot residual, mints and settles terms by what pays, and keeps a record of the route. Process I produces the same surface without that contact, by pattern completion, cue-reading, or replay of a recording. The residual R is the gap between what O claims and what would be observed under G. The difficulty is that O is the same under both. The question asks for evidence that separates them, and the figures answer that the evidence is not in O. It is in the contact, the record, and the checking arrangement. So "evidence" is itself re-grounded rather than taken as a standing list of tests.

## What an answer looks like (bound as an ordinary term)

A candidate answer must state, for each domain: (a) the actor that does the work, (b) the currency in which it pays, (c) the anchor that settles it, (d) the observable whose absence or presence would move the verdict, and (e) the failure that would retract it. The sought shape "an output-internal discriminator exists" is written down, bound, and then refused by the figures rather than smuggled in as a preference (Fig 5 :62-66; formula step "Direction").

## What is deliberately not here
