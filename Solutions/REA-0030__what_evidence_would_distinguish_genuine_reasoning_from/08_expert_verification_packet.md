# Expert verification packet: the Tether on reasoning versus imitation

**What this is:** a claim in a form a researcher can check. Mode, derivation and refuters are stated before the conclusion, so a reader knows what to look for and what would settle it.

**Mode: `specified` on the separator, `general` on the structural shape, `gap` on a single output-internal detector. Not `grounded`.** No measurement of any system is claimed. This is a shape and a route, not a result.

**Standing: simulated derivation checked for internal consistency against the figures, not a ground reading.** It cannot settle the claim it reports on. It does not say that any named system reasons or fails to reason.

## 1. The claim, in one paragraph

The evidence that would distinguish genuine reasoning from a convincing imitation is not in the output. Convergent derivation and adopted import look identical in the contents; only the record separates them; nothing about a composed term tells you, from looking at it, which route produced it. What survives is a five-domain separator. An output alone has no anchor and is disqualified as a witness. A loop is checkable by a per-slot residual with the ground as the only metric. A checking arrangement is sound only when independent frames range against an anchor they cannot move and the verifier cannot reconstruct the claim. Capability is a property of agent and habitat, so a benchmark is a substituted habitat and a harness solve proves wiring, not capability. The lineage carries the route, as provenance that is necessary and not sufficient. Over the full shared atom set, a reasoning process and an imitation producing the same output are isomers: same atoms, different bond, invisible in the formula and decisive under intervention. No detector or numeric threshold is returned; that is a declared `gap`.

## 2. Three checkable sub-claims

### C1 : Framework-internal; checkable by reading Figures 8, 13 and 1

- **Statement:** output-only evidence cannot discriminate a ground-settled derivation from an imitation that reproduces its surface. The separator is the record and the contact.
- **Source, verbatim:** Fig 8 :51, "Convergent derivation and adopted import look identical in the contents. Only the record of where each came from separates them, and the surplus lives only where the closures differ." Fig 13 :128, "Nothing about a composed term tells you, from looking at it, which route produced it or whether that route was available." Fig 1 :27, "They are frame-internal. A frame cannot score itself with a quantity it also produces."
- **What to check:** is there any figure line that assigns verdict weight to an output-internal quantity? If so, C1 fails.
- **Refuter:** demonstrate an instrument that reads the route, the provenance or the ground contact off the artefact alone, with no record and no anchor. Or produce the figure line C1 says does not exist.

### C2 : Empirical and methodological; checkable by an experiment designer

- **Statement:** the practical separator is an intervention on the channel to the cue source, not a rating of the output. The predicted signature is a performance collapse when the channel is cut, with the criterion written before the run.
- **Source, verbatim:** Fig 10 :24, "the ground does not decay, the channel does". Fig 6 :34, "Where nothing is, there is no edge to extend from, and the search for a proto-instrument is a search for one that is already there." Fig 11 :70, "A test harness is a substituted habitat. Which is why a synthetic solve proves wiring and never capability."
- **What to check:** does the Clever Hans protocol (questioner visible or not, knowing or not) have the predicted shape? In the opened source, 89 percent correct fell to 6 percent when the horse could not see the questioner. Does a known-positive and a known-negative fixture distinguish sensitivity from specificity? A known-positive fixture alone proves sensitivity, never specificity.
- **Refuter:** a case where removing the candidate cue channel leaves performance intact and a second channel cannot be found; or a channel-break result that a pre-registered criterion later fails to classify.

### C3 : Framework-internal derivation with an empirical limb; checkable by a researcher in AI evaluation

- **Statement:** two systems with the same output and different routes are isomers over the shared atom set, separable under intervention; the positive signature of the ground-ranging route is that failure accumulates traction, while the replay route fails by guessing.
- **Source, verbatim:** Fig 12 :117, "isomer. Same atoms, different bond, different substance. The difference is invisible in the formula and decisive in the behaviour." Fig 12 :111, "A vocabulary without bonds can name a state and cannot name a route to one." Operators :58, "a refutation is a negation, which is why a vocabulary without it can only accumulate".
- **What to check:** can the same output atoms be held fixed while the route bonds differ? Is the comparison made under an intervention that changes contact, not under a descriptive rating? Does the evaluated system ever emit a refusal that changes the reach, rather than a sentence shaped like one?
- **Refuter:** two systems with identical output and different internal routes that behave identically under every contact-changing intervention; or a demonstration that the refusal sentence is a replay with no effect on what can subsequently be composed.

## 3. What is explicitly not claimed

- Not that any named current system reasons, or that any named current system merely imitates. No measurement was taken.
- Not that a fluent or correct answer is evidence of imitation. Fluency is a budget filter, never a witness (Fig 9 :60).
- Not that imitation and reasoning are forever indistinguishable. The distinction horizon is a report about the current union horizon, not a final verdict; a source-discriminating instrument could change it.
- Not that provenance establishes correctness. Fig 10 :65: "Provenance shows a ruling was reached legitimately. It never shows it was right: the ground does that, and a wrong ruling stays wrong however clean its record."
- Not that a larger panel helps. Ten readers of one transcript share an evidence pool and are one frame wearing ten names (Fig 8 :48-49).
- Not a numeric threshold, detector, benchmark ranking or standing list of surface tells. That is the declared `gap`.
- Not `grounded`. Nothing here is asserted as settled by experiment.

## 5. What would settle it

A discriminator would have to be an instrument, not an argument. The candidates, each with its failure condition:

1. A pre-registered channel-break protocol that separates two classes of systems by a collapse in performance.
2. A record format in which convergent derivation and adopted import are distinguishable by provenance, verified against an independent source.
3. A source-discriminating measure that separates "taken" from "extracted" answers, with known-positive and known-negative fixtures.
4. An anchor that does not update on the evaluation itself: an outcome, a proof checker, a market, a patient outcome.
5. A figure line selecting a detector, which would close the declared `gap`.

A refutation is more useful than an endorsement. A one-line "this fails because..." is the preferred reply.
