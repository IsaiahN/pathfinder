## 2. Wider literature that bears on the separator

1. **Clever Hans is the methodological ancestor, not an analogy.** Pfungst did not rate the horse's answers. He varied who was in the room, whether the horse could see the questioner, and whether the questioner knew the answer. The 89-to-6 percent collapse is the shape any channel-break probe should have. The Wikipedia entry notes that removing the trainer is not always sufficient, because social relationship can matter; what is required is that nobody present knows the expected response. That caution is carried into the protocol in `08`.
2. **Morgan's canon is a prior, not a verdict.** It says prefer the lower-faculty explanation until contact evidence forces the higher one. Its own entry records that modern researchers dispute its operational hierarchy. It corroborates the shape of the default, not a conclusion about any system.
3. **Shortcut learning names the failure class.** A rule can work on a benchmark and fail on transfer; the essay calls this "the human fails by searching; the decompressor fails by guessing." Both point to the same instrument: out-of-distribution or channel-change testing with a pre-registered collapse criterion.
4. **Grokking is the caution.** Late generalization can appear well past overfitting. So an early failure is not a final verdict either; the trajectory, not one snapshot, is the reading. This is why `08` asks for a sequence of attempts and for both a known-positive and a known-negative fixture.
5. **The Turing/Chinese Room pair is the philosophical frame, and is high overlap with the framework's own discussion.** The framework essay on tacit knowledge calls the Turing test a test of fluency, not of accuracy. That is why the world "conflict" with Fig 1 :27 was returned to census and dissolved as a convention/verdict distinction in `01` §6 rather than allowed to flip the figure verdict.

Overlap declaration: the two philosophy-of-AI sources are one witness; the two ML sources are one witness; the eight essays share the framework author's evidence pool completely. Low-overlap corroboration comes from animal behaviour, comparative psychology and ML benchmark transfer. No world source overrode a figure.

## 4. Venue constraints found while verifying

- **Minds and Machines** states that LLMs do not satisfy its authorship criteria and that use of an LLM must be documented in the Methods section. AI-assisted copy editing is exempt; generative editorial work is not. This packet is a simulated derivation, so any submission must declare that rather than present it as a human ground reading.
- **JAIR** requires a completed reproducibility checklist and states that AI systems cannot be authors or independent sources. Its current call also pauses survey submissions from 2026-12-01; a methods paper is not a survey, but check scope before submitting.
- **AAAI-26** had an AI-assisted peer-review pilot and a strict author submission limit. The conference dates and deadlines visible on the page had already passed by 2026-11-23; verify the current cycle before using these routes.
- **ICLR 2026** uses double-blind review and OpenReview public discussion. A public discussion record is closer to a ground-visible verdict than a private endorsement.
