## Round trip gap R_T

The Symbols table defines R_T as the gap between what was sent up and what came back down, gap(x, (T_E ∘ T_A)(x)), measuring what the coarser description cannot hold. Fig 4 :30 requires it to be measured rather than assumed. I ran the round trip on the condensation this folder actually uses.

1. **Sent up (x):** the verbatim figure set, chiefly Fig 2 :29 (anchor), Fig 4 :22 (membrane), Fig 8 :50-51 (convergence versus import), Fig 10 :24-26 (channel decay), Fig 11 :36 (lineage and ground).
2. **Abstraction (T_A):** the shorthand carried in `07` and `09`: dependability needs an anchor that does not move, a maintained channel, and a low-overlap checker; proof forms are conventions.
3. **Concretisation (T_E):** a concrete verdict about a named case, for example "the navigational guild's knowledge is dependable and the institution's rejection of it is an error".

**R_T is nonzero.** Four things the shorthand holds do not survive the trip back down:

- the **per-slot index** (Fig 1 :19). The shorthand says "dependable knowledge"; the concrete case is always this route, this season, this practitioner. A global verdict over a tradition aggregates away the live slot.
- the **provenance requirement** (Fig 8 :51). The shorthand says independent agreement is corroboration; the concrete case needs a record proving the two frames did not import from one another.
- the **channel check** (Fig 10 :25-26). The shorthand says maintained contact; the concrete case needs an instrument still returning fresh readings, not a recording.
- the **specific-form gap** (F9). The shorthand is silent on which form is necessary; any concrete naming of a form is a binding from the institutional frame, not a figure result.

Conversely, one thing survives cleanly: the outcome is the anchor and a movable convention is not. That is the residue worth sending. R_T was **not** measured numerically, because no ground reading exists; it is recorded qualitatively as the enumerated set of dropped constraints. Treating "the formula is the condensed version of the metalaws" as identity would set R_T to zero and would be the dead-reckoning error Fig 4 :30 names. The honest form is a lossy condensation with a measured gap, which is what `09_watson_answer.md` states.
