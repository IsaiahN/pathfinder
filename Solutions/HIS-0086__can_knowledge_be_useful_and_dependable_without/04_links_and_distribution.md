## What the wider literature adds, and its limits

- The four low-overlap empirical fields (human cognition, ecology and Indigenous knowledge, animal behaviour, navigation) all describe dependable know-how that is transmitted without a codified proof form. They corroborate the *mechanism shape* in `01` F5: what passes down is a head start and a method, rebuilt through contact. They do not decide the figure verdict.
- The two philosophy-of-science sources (Feyerabend, Lakatos) corroborate the narrower point that a proof form is a historical convention, not a single fixed method. Feyerabend's "anything goes" is declined after re-census in `01` F10; Lakatos supports the idea that even mathematics grows by "proofs and refutations". They are one evidence pool on the question, not two independent witnesses.
- The epistemic-injustice source names a mechanism by which dependable knowers get excluded: credibility deficits are evidence about the maker, not about ground contact. It corroborates F11 at the social level. It shares no evidence pool with the figures, so it is admissible as a witness for shape only.
- The essays by the framework author share the maximal evidence pool with the figures. Their agreement is expected and carries no independent corroboration weight. They are used only for the canonical reading of "shape" in `05`.

## Distribution: offer, do not push

Figure-grounded reasons:

- **Fig 4 :22**: *"Only methods cross up. Only head starts come down."* Sending the full architecture before anyone has described a residual sends a recording, not a generator. Lead with the shape claim and its refuter.
- **Fig 10 :42**: *"A verifier that can reconstruct the claim cannot verify it."* Seat 4 must not hold the interiors. A recipient who has absorbed the framework before being asked for a verdict has joined the frame being checked.
- **Fig 9 :27-28**: *"describe it before you go looking, or any frame you pick will seem to fit"*. Ask what they would expect to see if the shape claim were true before giving them any links.
- **Fig 2 :40**: *"agreement about the world is worthless when the agreeing frames share an evidence pool"*. A send selected for people likely to agree produces a convention, not corroboration (Seat Map :163).
- **Fig 2 :42**: *"Buy independence in proportion to the cost of being wrong."* The cost here is declaring a practice dependable when its channel is dead, so the checking frame should be chosen for its different evidence, not its friendliness.

**Recommended order in a reply:** (1) the shape claim and its four refuters; (2) ask what they would count as a failure and record that verdict; (3) only then the three links, with the note that the figures, not this summary, are the authority; (4) if they ask, offer the formula as a method with the instruction that they write their own refuter and state their own mode.

## The two-stage offer

1. **Stage 1 before stage 2** is Fig 10 :52, *"Bring a characterised residual, never the source"*, and Fig 2 :45, *"Frames range against the ground. They do not negotiate with it."* The recipient checks the output without holding the framework interior.
2. **Stage 2 is opt in by construction.** Once a recipient has absorbed the theory, their agreement stops being evidence, so the stage-1 verdict is recorded before any theory is shown and is never pooled as corroboration later.
3. **Stage 3 sends a method, which is permitted.** Fig 4 :22: methods cross up. The formula is a generator, so giving it away does not give away an answer.
4. **Stage 3 still needs a ground per problem.** `THE_FORMULA.md` "Direction": a frame *"cannot check the direction of its own question, because the shape a frame is searching for is not a term it holds"*. Each user must write down what would refute their result before running it.

## Hosting

No archive is created by this agent and nothing is sent. If you host the packet, prefer a link that lets a recipient decline contact, an opt-in download page rather than an attachment pushed to a list. That is closer to the figure rule than a mass send, and it avoids the legal failure that comes with unsolicited bulk email.
