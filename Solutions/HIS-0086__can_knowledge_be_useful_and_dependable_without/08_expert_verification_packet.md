## 1. The claim, in one paragraph

Dependability is not membership in a proof convention. It is a three-part relation: a claim is settled by a ground that does not change its answer because of what any frame thinks (an outcome, a measurement, a patient result, a landfall); the channel to that ground is maintained rather than stale, pooled or saturated; and a second frame with a different evidence pool can check it. The proof forms favored by scientific institutions are a grammar for shared meaning inside one frame, efficient for coordination and not the discriminator of dependability. Practical, craft and Indigenous knowledge can satisfy the three conditions, and institutional science can too. Whether any *named* form is necessary is a declared `gap`. "Anything goes" is explicitly refused.

## 2. Three checkable sub-claims

### C1: framework-internal; checkable by reading Figures 2 and 10

- **Statement:** the anchor is the outcome, not the proof form. A convention that decides an outcome has stopped being a convention.
- **Source, verbatim:** Fig 2 :29, *"An anchor is whatever settles the question and does not change its answer because of what any frame thinks. - **Refuter:** a figure line that makes a named proof convention necessary for settlement by the ground; or an anchor that updates on institutional opinion (R1 and R4 in `01` §3). Either reverses the verdict.

### C2: framework-internal plus field-facing; checkable against the case record

- **Statement:** dependability is indexed per slot and ground-paid; a global credential aggregates away the live signal. What transmits across generations is a method and a head start, not a recording.
- **Source, verbatim:** Fig 1 :19, *"The residual is indexed by object slot, not aggregated."* Fig 4 :22, *"Only methods cross up. Only head starts come down."* Fig 11 :36, *"Where nothing maintains anything, intelligence is a label applied afterwards to a lineage that met its ground. Nothing was intelligent; selection ran."*
- **What to check:** find one documented case where a credential predicted a fresh slot with no new ground reading, or one case where a written recording alone produced a new correct performance with no contact and no correction. Either would falsify the claim as written.
- **Refuter:** R2 in `01` §3, a practical system losing dependability when the institutional form is removed with ground contact held constant.

### C3: framework-internal, load-bearing caveat; checkable by reading Figures 10, 2 and 7

- **Statement:** the answer is not "anything goes". A dead channel is invisible from inside, and agreement within one evidence pool is not corroboration.
- **Source, verbatim:** Fig 10 :24, *"the ground does not decay, the channel does"*; Fig 10 :25-26, *"stale percepts, dropped transients, saturated metrics, pooled readings"*. Fig 2 :40, *"agreement about the world is worthless when the agreeing frames share an evidence pool"*. Fig 7 :35, *"A room's agreement is variation, not a verdict. Selection between rooms is where the ground votes."*
- **Refuter:** R3 in `01` §3, a knowledge system with a demonstrably dead channel whose claims still pay at a fresh ground measurement. That breaks the contact requirement and forces re-census.

## 3. What is explicitly not claimed

- Not that anything goes. Ground contact, a maintained channel and a low-overlap checker are required.
- Not that old or local practices are dependable by virtue of being old or local.
- Not that scientific institutions are worthless. They are one ground-contacting frame.
- Not that a specific institutional form is unnecessary. That is a declared `gap` (F9 in `01`), not a result.
- Not that the two frames are isomers as encountered. `06` leaves the **functional-group** reading standing until a shared atom set is enumerated.
- Not `grounded`. No measurement was taken and no community outcome was audited.
