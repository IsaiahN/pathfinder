## 0. The residual, stated in effect terms

For a practical slot s (a wound, a landfall, a harvest, a bridge), write

```
R_s = | what the situation requires  -  what the held vocabulary predicts |
```

The question asks whether *fitting the forms of proof favored by scientific institutions* (call it P) is a necessary condition for R_s to stay dependably small.

- **Option A, P is necessary.** Only knowledge certified by institutional proof forms counts as dependable. Leads to: practical, craft, Indigenous, and apprenticeship knowledge is discarded whenever it cannot be expressed in P, even when it keeps paying at the ground. Verification is concentrated in one closure. Fig 8 :32 makes the cost of that explicit.
- **Option B, P is a convention.** Dependability is a maintained relation between a frame and a ground that settles and does not update. Leads to: multiple closures can hold dependable knowledge; agreement between them is corroboration only when they are independent (Fig 2 :40); and a permissive anything-goes reading is still refused because a frame with a dead channel is not dependable (Fig 10 :24-26).

What an answer would look like, bound as an ordinary term before the run (formula "Direction"): a claim is dependable iff its terms are settled by a ground that does not move when frames push on it, the channel to that ground is maintained, and at least one low-overlap frame can check it. This is the sought-for shape, written down so it can accrue a residual and be refused. It is not assumed true.

## 1. Domain census (before choosing any figure line)

**R by arity, symmetry, scale.** Arity: one index per practical slot, many slots, never one global score (Fig 1 :19). Symmetry: dependability is a threshold comparison, not a symmetric join of two operands; the question is "does it pay at the ground", a ⋛-like comparison, not a blending. Scale: (i) the single practitioner's loop, (ii) the transmission habitat across generations, (iii) cross-system verification between institutions and local practitioners.

| domain | actors | currency | anchor | status |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| D1 Practitioner loop | practitioner, tools, observations, held library Γ | residual reduction per slot: patient recovers, landfall, harvest | the practical outcome (Fig 2 :29 names patient outcome, market) | admitted |
| D2 Transmission habitat | apprentice, master, practice, conditions, relations | survival of the practice; contact maintained | the ground through repeated practice (Fig 11 :36) | admitted |
| D3 Institutional science frame | scientists, journals, peer review, statistical methods | publications, proofs, replications | experiment and measurement (Fig 2 :29); the proof *form* is a convention (Fig 10 :47) | admitted |
| D4 Cross-system verification | two or more knowledge systems with differing closures | independence, triangulation | the ground, never agreement (Fig 2 :35) | admitted |
| X1 "proof form itself is necessary" | none nameable | none | none: a convention cannot settle (Fig 10 :47) | not a domain yet |

**Partition or union.** Union, not partition. D1 and D2 overlap (the practitioner is inside a habitat), D3 and D4 overlap (institutions are frames subject to outside checking), and the intersection of institutional and local knowledge is a compound with its own loop. Fig 9 :55: *"A bundled problem is usually a union of overlapping conditions, not a list of separate ones."* A single lever (accept only P) clears the region where only one condition holds and leaves the intersections standing (Fig 9 :57).

**Dependency order (Fig 3).** perception -> vocabulary -> objective -> planning -> learn and carry. A system that cannot name what a situation affords cannot say what would count as progress in its own terms (Fig 3 :9-10). Effort spent "above the break" (more statistics, more credentials) arrives at a step that cannot use it (Fig 3 :20). This is why the middle link is where institutional and practical frames most often disagree: the institution may lack terms for what the practitioner sees.

Strain test: a figure line that only fits with a strain governs another quantity. Applied below in F6 (Fig 3 vs Fig 11) and F10 (Feyerabend). Both re-censuses are recorded, not smoothed.

## 2. Findings

### F1: The anchor is the outcome, not the proof form (decisive, `general`)

Fig 2 :29:

> **"An anchor is whatever settles the question and does not change its answer because of what any frame thinks. A measurement, an experiment, a counter, a market, a patient outcome."**

Fig 2 :30 (the sentence continues from :29): "If it moves when you push on it, it is not an anchor." Fig 10 :47: *"a convention that decides an outcome has stopped being one"*. Fig 10 :49: *"Declaring a meaning does not ground it."*

The anchor named by the figure is a patient outcome, a market, a measurement. A proof form is a shared rule about what counts as a demonstration. It is a convention, and the figure says a convention that decides an outcome has stopped being one. So P cannot be the anchor for "is this dependable": pushing on P (changing the convention) would change the verdict, which disqualifies it by Fig 2 :30.

**Status: triangulated** across Fig 2 (anchor definition) and Fig 10 (convention rule), two figures with low overlap: one defines what settles, the other diagnoses a seat failure. Mode `general`, with the application to institutions `specified`.

**Refuter:** produce a figure line that makes membership in a named proof convention a necessary condition for a term to be settled by the ground. If one is found, this finding falls and the answer to the question changes to "no" as written.

### F2: Dependability is per-slot and ground-paid, not a global credential (`specified`)

Fig 1 :19: **"The residual is indexed by object slot, not aggregated."** Fig 1 :22: *"Aggregating across slots is how a live signal disappears into an average."* Formula step 5: *"SETTLE: the ground settles it. Verified only by what pays. A gate is not the ground."*

A single certificate "this knowledge system is dependable" aggregates across slots and throws away the signal that says which slot is failing. Practical systems track per-slot residuals: this route, this season, this patient. Institutional credentials are frame-internal quantities in the Fig 1 sense: they cannot score the frame with a quantity the frame also produces.

**Status: triangulated** across Fig 1 (per-slot residual) and the formula's settlement step. Mode `specified` for knowledge systems.

**Refuter:** a global reliability index that predicts a never-before-seen slot correctly with no fresh ground reading. That would make aggregation a witness rather than a filter (Fig 9 :60).

### F3: No single closure can certify its own completeness (`general`)

Fig 6 :23: *"No frame escapes its own closure. The bound moves only on contact with a frame whose closure differs."* Fig 8 :32: *"Closure is idempotent. Composing inside a closed set never leaves it, so no search, however long, adds an atom. This one is definitional: it is what closure means."* Fig 8 :39: **"So a search that finds nothing is never a proof of absence."** Fig 9 :44: *"Only a sealed room can be searched to the end, and no frame is a sealed room. So searching and finding nothing proves nothing."*

If option A were true, institutional science would have to certify, from inside its own closure, that no dependable knowledge exists outside it. Fig 8 rules this out definitionally. The honest report is "unreachable at this budget" or "we lack a frame with that atom", never "absent".

**Status: triangulated** across Fig 6 (what moves the wall) and Fig 8 (why nothing else can), low overlap: boundary mechanics versus metalaw proof. Mode `general`.

**Refuter:** a derivation from inside one closure that proves dependability requires P, with no imported atom. That would make closure non-idempotent and break the figure.

### F4: Convergent derivation and adopted import look identical (`general`)

Fig 8 :50-51:

> **"Two frames can hold the same atom by convergence, reaching it separately, which is corroboration at maximum independence."**
> **"Convergent derivation and adopted import look identical in the contents. Only the record of where each came from separates them, and the surplus lives only where the closures differ."**

This is the key to the history-of-science case. When an oral tradition and a laboratory arrive at the same containment practice or the same season rule, the contents look the same. Without the record, a reader cannot tell whether the two frames derived independently (maximum-independence corroboration) or one imported the other (one witness wearing two names). The provenance, not the content, decides. This is exactly the work of the historian in this question's section.

**Status: triangulated** across Fig 8 (surplus) and Fig 2 :31-32 (independence defined as low shared evidence). Mode `general`.

**Refuter:** a content-only test that reliably separates convergence from import without a record. No such test is named in the figures; one would collapse the provenance rule.

### F5: What crosses generations is a method or a head start, not a recording (`specified`)

Fig 4 :22: **"Only methods cross up. Only head starts come down."** Fig 4 :25: *"A recording carried upward looks like knowledge and is a description of one occasion."* Fig 11 :36: *"Where nothing maintains anything, intelligence is a label applied afterwards to a lineage that met its ground. Nothing was intelligent; selection ran."* Fig 11 :40: *"A system cannot be made more capable by improving the agent in isolation. An improvement that does not change contact changes nothing, however much it improves."*

Apprenticeship and oral tradition are not deficient copies of a written proof. They are a maintained habitat in which a lineage keeps meeting its ground. What the novice receives is a head start and a method, and the knowledge is rebuilt through contact (essay *Decoding Tacit Knowledge*, read 2026-11-23: "Writing carries the context well and the subtext partly, but it almost never carries the pretext").

**Status: triangulated** across Fig 4 (membrane) and Fig 11 (habitat/capability), two different figures with different actors. Mode `specified` for human knowledge transmission.

**Refuter:** a case where a written recording alone, with no contact, no correction, and no ground reading, produces a new correct performance in a fresh learner on a novel slot. That would make the recording a generator rather than a description of one occasion.

### F6: The habitat is enumerated, not composed; the middle link is where readings part (`specified`)

Fig 11 :43: *"The habitat is enumerated, never composed."* Fig 11 :48: *"You do not invent the list. You read it off the world, and what you cannot perceive or measure yet is the residual."* Fig 11 :49: *"An actor's valence is relative to the residual, not a property it carries."* Fig 3 :27: *"A link with no instrument is not a link that works. It is a link nobody has looked at."*

An institution reading a local practice sees "no instrument" where the practice has an instrument the institution cannot read (a swell pattern, a soil colour, a face). That is not a broken link in the knowledge; it is a broken link in the observer's vocabulary. Fig 3 governs link order, not which frame holds the vocabulary.

**Strain and re-census.** First reading: Fig 3 declares the practical chain broken below the vocabulary link, so the knowledge would not be assessable. That strained against the world sources, which show the knowledge paying at the ground. Re-census: Fig 3 is about a chain inside a frame; Fig 11 is about the habitat a frame is embedded in. They govern different objects. The practical frame has terms for what the situation affords; the institutional frame does not. No contradiction after re-census, matching the README rule that a reading which makes figures contradict has misidentified its domain.

**Status:** `single-source` for the enumeration rule (Fig 11 only; the seat map repeats the framework's own rule and shares the evidence pool, so it is not a second witness), `triangulated` for the link-order reading (Fig 3 plus the re-census against Fig 11). Mode `specified`.

**Refuter:** demonstrate that a frame with no term for what a situation affords nonetheless ranks residuals in that situation as well as a frame with the term.

### F7: "Anything goes" is not licensed; the channel can die (`general`, load-bearing caveat)

Fig 10 :24: *"the ground does not decay, the channel does"*. Fig 10 :25-26: *"stale percepts, dropped transients, saturated metrics, pooled readings"*. Fig 2 :40: *"agreement about the world is worthless when the agreeing frames share an evidence pool"*. Fig 7 :12-13: *"same closure, handed along;"* *"agreement compounds, evidence does not"*. Fig 2 :35: **"Alignment is triangulation, not negotiation. A mirror cannot correct you."**

This is the constraint that stops the answer from collapsing into relativism. A knowledge system whose channel to the ground is dead keeps producing readings, and internal instruments stay green because they read through the dead channel (Seat Map :285). Such a system is not dependable however much its members agree. Dependability requires maintained contact plus low-overlap checking, not mere persistence.

**Status: triangulated** across Fig 10 (channel failure), Fig 2 (correlation valence) and Fig 7 (mirror chain). Mode `general`.

**Refuter:** a knowledge system whose channel to ground is demonstrably dead (stale percepts, saturated metrics, pooled readings) and whose practical claims still pay at a fresh ground measurement. That would break the contact requirement and force a full re-census.

### F8: Institutional proof is one frame's grammar, not the ground (`specified`)

Fig 2 :36: *"An anchor is legitimate when the question is constitutively about it."* Fig 2 :37-38: human judgment is the right anchor for "is this a good answer for a person" but only an instrument for "is this code correct", and instruments can be wrong on axes they do not measure. Seat Map :163: *"High correlation is disqualifying for a verdict and efficient for a convention."*

Institutional science is a genuine ground-contacting frame. It is not dismissed by F1. What is refused is the upgrade of its *grammar* (what counts as a proof inside it) into the thing that settles. A convention is efficient for coordinating shared meaning, and that is a real product. It becomes a defect only when it decides an outcome (Fig 10 :47).

**Status:** `single-source` for "a convention that decides an outcome has stopped being one" (Fig 10), triangulated with Seat Map :163 only at the cost of shared framework pool, so the corroboration is weak. Honest label: `single-source` on the figure set. Mode `specified`.

**Refuter:** a measurement showing that a claim's dependability changes when the proof convention is held constant and ground contact is varied, with no other change. That would make the convention load-bearing rather than grammatical.

### F9: Which named form is necessary: `gap`, stated plainly

No figure line names a randomized controlled trial, a p-value threshold, double blinding, or any other institutional form as a necessary condition of dependable knowledge. Fig 9 :32: *"you get a kind of thing, never the answer itself"*. Fig 13 :134: *"Unreachable is a verdict here, not a silence,"*. Fig 8 :39: *"So a search that finds nothing is never a proof of absence."*

After a genuine census, the specific-form claim remains a `gap`. The framework returns the shape of the discriminator (an anchor that does not move, a maintained channel, a low-overlap check), not its institutional name. Naming a form is a binding from the institutional frame, provenanced as such, and it has no figure line behind it.

**Status: `gap`.** This is a verdict, not a silence (Fig 13 :134). Refuter: produce the figure line, or an instrument that discriminates dependability from proof-form compliance at a new slot.

### F10: A world-source conflict, returned to census (not a verdict flip)

*Against Method* (Feyerabend, 1975; Wikipedia article read 2026-11-23) is reported as holding "that there is no such thing as the scientific method and that it is not appropriate to impose a single methodological rule upon scientific practices." The article also notes that the German title "translates more directly to 'Against the Forced Constraint of Method' emphasizing that it is the imposition of methodological rules that is rejected rather than the uses of methods altogether." A common reading of "anything goes" is described as "the terrified exclamation of the rationalist who takes a closer look at history", that is, a reductio, not a prescription.

First reading: this conflicts with F7, because "anything goes" looks like a license to drop ground contact. Conflict rule says it goes back to census, never flips the verdict. Re-census: Feyerabend's target is the *imposition of a single rule*, which is exactly the Fig 10 :47 convention-deciding-an-outcome failure. It is not a claim about maintaining contact with a ground. After re-census the apparent conflict dissolves; the slogan is declined, not adopted. The corroboration is genuine but from a field (philosophy of science) with high overlap on the question itself, so it is one witness for the grammar-not-ground point.

**Status:** conflict resolved by re-census; verdict unchanged. Recorded visibly here and in §5.

### F11: Credibility deficits are a stake channel, not evidence

*Epistemic injustice* (Wikipedia article on Fricker, read 2026-11-23): the term names a wrong done to someone "in their 'capacity as a knower'", and includes a "credibility deficit" where a group is "regarded as unqualified to describe even their own experiences". Fig 9 :51 states the framework rule: *"what the verifier may see: evidence bearing on the residual is admissible; evidence bearing only on its maker is a stake-inducing channel, and the verifier is denied it"*. Seat Map :171: *"an interest in the reading is disqualifying"*.

A credibility deficit is evidence about the maker's standing, not about the claim's contact with the ground. This corroborates F1 and F8 at the social level, but it is a world source and shares no evidence pool with the figures, so it can be admitted as a witness for the *mechanism shape* only. It does not decide the verdict.

**Status:** world corroboration only, not triangulated with the figure set on the verdict.

## 3. Pre-registered refuter (written before the conclusion)

Required by Seat Map :287: *"a falsifier written before the run is a design instrument, not only an honesty device."*

The shape claim "dependability does not require institutional proof forms" is refused if any of the following holds:

1. **R1, figure refuter.** A verbatim figure line is found that makes a named proof convention necessary for a term to be settled by the ground. The answer then flips to "no" as written.
2. **R2, case refuter.** A documented case shows a practical knowledge system losing dependability when the institutional form is removed while ground contact is held constant. That makes P load-bearing rather than grammatical.
3. **R3, channel refuter.** A knowledge system is shown with a dead channel (stale percepts, saturated metrics, pooled readings, Fig 10 :25-26) whose practical claims still pay at a fresh ground measurement. That breaks the contact requirement and forces re-census.
4. **R4, anchor refuter.** An anchor is shown that updates on institutional opinion, so that "scientific consensus" is itself the thing that settles. By Fig 2 :29-30 the anchor has become a frame, panel 3 collapses into panel 1, and the verdict reverses.

A fifth, the standing `gap`: a source with a genuinely different evidence pool that names the necessary form and demonstrates it at a new slot would move F9 from `gap` to `grounded`. Until one of these fires, no part of this may be labelled `grounded`.

## 4. Recommendation

1. Lead with the shape and the refuter, not with a theory of knowledge. State that dependability requires an anchor that does not move, a maintained channel, and a low-overlap check (F1, F7).
2. Do not answer "yes, anything goes". The answer is "yes, conditional on ground contact and independence" (F7).
3. Keep the specific-form question as a declared `gap` (F9). Do not smuggle in a preferred institution as the missing primitive.
4. When comparing systems, require provenance before counting agreement as corroboration (F4). Convergent derivation and adopted import are indistinguishable in content.
5. Route through public, opt-in venues in practical epistemology, history of science and STS (`03_public_contact_points.csv`). Ask what would refute the shape; do not ask for belief.

### Seven-question recursive derivation (summary)

1. **Is the proof form the anchor?** No. Fig 2 :29-30 names outcomes and measurements; a movable convention is disqualified. *Legitimate anchor*, not a matter of preference.
2. **Can a single frame certify that no dependable knowledge lies outside it?** No. Fig 8 :32, :39; Fig 9 :44. Closure is idempotent and no frame is sealed. `general`.
3. **What makes the knowledge dependable then?** Ground payment per slot (Fig 1 :19; formula step 5) plus a maintained channel (Fig 10 :24). `specified`.
4. **How do two systems that agree get read?** Either convergence at maximum independence or adopted import; only the record separates them (Fig 8 :50-51). `general`.
5. **Is anything imported here?** No new atom is licensed. The binding "institutional form is a convention" is a reading of Fig 10, not an import from Feyerabend. Feyerabend corroborates; he does not supply the atom (Fig 6 :25: *"An import adds no atom to the world."*).
6. **Does the answer settle anything?** No. Fig 5 :69: *"settling is necessary for a term to be composed over, and it is not sufficient"*; no measurement was taken. Status `candidate`.
7. **Where is the stop recorded?** Here, in `07`, `09`, and `11`, with `R_T` measured qualitatively in `11`. The sought-for shape was written down in §0 before the run, so it is refusable (formula "Direction").

### Updated verdict

The conditional yes survives: dependability is a relation to an anchor that does not update on frames' opinions, maintained through a channel, and checked by a low-overlap frame. Institutional proof forms are a convention inside one frame, not the anchor. What does not survive is any claim that the specific form is named by the framework (`gap`), any claim that "anything goes" is licensed (F7), and the strong claim that science as a whole is merely a convention (retracted in F1). Strains after re-census: **none hidden**, one world-source conflict recorded and dissolved (F10), one declared `gap` (F9).
