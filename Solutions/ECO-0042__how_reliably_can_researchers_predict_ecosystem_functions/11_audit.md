## Independent domain census

I redid the census from the residual line alone, before relying on the primary's conclusions, using
the WATSON order: R by arity, symmetry and scale; one line per domain with actors, currency and
anchor; partition or union; order by dependency.

- **R (arity / symmetry / scale).** The residual is the gap between a prediction from one integer
  and a measured function at a slot. Arity: one scalar against a vector, therefore many-to-one.
  Symmetry: the integer is invariant under species permutation and identity substitution, so the
  projection is symmetric. Scale: a site aggregate against a process rate, so a transform between
  scales. Fig 9 :57 makes the compound a union, not one hard problem. I did not use the primary's
  finding list to obtain any of these four properties.
- **D1 Taxonomic counting.** Actors: interchangeable species tokens and the observer. Currency: `S`.
  Anchor: a plot census or reference collection. Nameable, so a domain.
- **D2 Trait and composition.** Actors: identities, traits, abundances, functional groups. Currency:
  trait distributions, dominance, complementarity, bonds. Anchor: a measured function. Nameable.
- **D3 Habitat and context.** Actors: light, water, temperature, season, terrain and the relations
  of pollination, predation, competition, dependency. Currency: contact and cascades. Anchor: the
  ground in that habitat. Nameable.
- **D4 Verification.** Actors: ecologists, referees, the claim and its refuter. Currency: residuals
  and agreement. Anchor: the ground, never agreement. Nameable. In this simulated run its
  independence is zero, because it draws on the same pool.
- **Union, not partition.** D1, D2 and D3 overlap. A lever on `S` clears only the region where the
  other conditions do not bind, which is Fig 9 :54 and :59.
- **Order by dependency.** Perceive identity and count, acquire terms for traits and functions,
  state the objective as reducing per-slot `R`, then plan and carry. The break in a count-only
  protocol sits at vocabulary or objective, not at planning.

## Round trip gap R_T

The Symbols table defines R_T as the gap between what was sent up and what came back down,
`gap(x, (T_E ∘ T_A)(x))`, measuring what the coarser description cannot hold. Fig 4 :30 requires it
to be measured rather than assumed. I ran the round trip on the condensation this folder actually
uses.

1. **Sent up (x):** the verbatim figure set, chiefly Fig 1 :13, :19, :22 (per-slot residual),
   Fig 12 :23, :92 (symmetric projection), Fig 11 :43, :46, :47 (enumerated habitat) and Fig 13
   :133 (shape, not a name).
2. **Abstraction (T_A):** the shorthand carried in `01` and repeated in `07`: a count is a symmetric
   lossy aggregation; reliability is per slot and context; the missing discriminator is a shape;
   more counting cannot add an atom.
3. **Concretisation (T_E):** the concrete claim a reader might try to derive from that shorthand:
   "count alone predicts ecosystem function with reliability r across all functions and habitats."

**R_T is nonzero.** Five things the shorthand holds do not survive the trip back down:

- **slot indexing** collapses into a global scalar, which is the aggregation failure Fig 1 :22 names;
- **identity and arrangement** drop out, so the symmetric projection is treated as lossless;
- **habitat conditions and relations** drop out, so context becomes noise rather than a condition;
- **the ground requirement** drops out, so a coefficient is asserted without a named function,
  site set, habitat, model and error metric;
- **the shape-not-a-name limit** drops out, so one variable is nominated as universally sufficient.

Conversely, two things survive cleanly: richness has a general positive association with ecosystem
functioning in the opened studies, and a count is a legitimate cheap filter. That is the residue
worth sending. R_T was **not** measured numerically, because no ground reading exists; it is
recorded qualitatively as the enumerated set of dropped constraints. Treating the shorthand as
identical to the figures would set R_T to zero, which is exactly the dead-reckoning error Fig 4 :30
names. `09_watson_answer.md` states this honestly by marking the coefficient a `gap`.
