## 1. A shape is a constraint, not a filler

The canonical figures README states the limit: the framework returns *"what a missing thing would have to look like (a shape, not a name)."* Fig 9 :32: *"you get a kind of thing, never the answer itself."* Fig 13 :133: *"A system with a closure can answer that before it searches, and what it returns is a shape rather than a name."*

From *The Library of Babel*: *"you can cut your search short if the set of books returned is small and descriptive enough that it provides a general shape of the answer that satisfies your question."*

For ECO-0042 the shape is: **a predictor that is indexed by function slot, cross-kind, and embedded in an enumerated habitat, settled per function against measured data.** What it is not: a single reliability coefficient, and a nomination of one magic variable. `S = 30` is a reading, not a shape. "Per-function error bounded under named habitat conditions, with the worst live slot reported" is a shape.

## 2. How a shape is obtained

- *Library of Babel*: *"With each new term gained, you are able to narrow the space by definition and deepen your vocabulary."* Here the new terms are trait identity, dominance, complementarity, habitat conditions and relations, each read from the world sources in `04_links_and_distribution.md`.
- Fig 9 :30: *"your words are borrowed too, so describe the effect rather than the cause."* Describe the loss in effect terms: swapping species at fixed count changes function; adding a condition changes the coefficient. Do not start by naming "functional diversity" as the answer.
- *Decoding Tacit Knowledge*: *"When contact isn't possible, the best you can send is a plain description of what you'd expect to see, because anyone can check that against the world."* This is the outreach rule in `02_outreach_email.md`.
- *How a Society of Agents Would Work* quotes Fig 1 directly: *"Aggregating across slots is how a live signal disappears into an average."* The essay adds that independent discoveries count most, which is why the count-only frame and the trait/habitat frames must be checked separately against the ground.

## 3. What licenses a name

Only two things, both outside the shape itself:

1. **The ground settles it.** Formula step 5: the ground settles, and a gate is not the ground. Hong et al. (2022) and Gagic et al. (2015) are ground readings for their scopes; their results are `specified`, not a framework decree.
2. **A provenanced import from a frame whose closure differs**, with a source gate and a shadow test (Fig 6 :12, Fig 6 :13). Trait ecology and habitat ecology are such frames.

*They're Scanning Books When They Should Be Studying Nature* sharpens this: *"The real import is not a new term in the existing space. It is a new dimension of the space."* It also warns: *"Declaring the primitives does not make them neutral. It makes the choice visible."* A decision to report function per slot and per habitat is a visible choice, not a discovery of nature's own reporting format.

## 4. Why "count alone" is the specific forbidden error

*The Hard Problem is About Source* gives the mechanism. A spectrometer reads green and *"cannot comment on the source"*: one green light, or yellow plus blue, or RGB, or a scheme not yet discovered. The essay states: *"If several paths create the same behavior, then behavior is a projection with lossy information."* It was the instrument, not an argument, that resolved the case.

A species count is the green reading. Many communities (yellow plus blue, RGB, and so on) produce the same integer. The source, which species and in what arrangement and habitat, is not in the reading. What is owed is a better instrument, not a confidence interval on the wrong one. The independent colour-science source in `10_source_ledger.csv` confirms the phenomenon outside the framework: *"a perceived matching of colors with different (nonmatching) spectral power distributions."*

*The Recursive Self-Improvement Trap*: *"the output is only as good as the validator. The agent, or the tool, or the frame, or the theory, cannot validate itself."* A count-only protocol cannot certify that the count is enough.

## 5. Q: does "shape, not a name" forbid naming traits or habitat conditions?

**Answer: no, with a recorded narrowing.** The README phrase governs a *missing* primitive, one not held in Γ. Traits, identities, abundances, conditions and relations are held atoms in the trait and habitat frames, and they are independently attested in the opened world sources. Naming them is a binding from those frames, provenanced as such. What the framework returns as a shape is the *discriminator a count-only frame lacks*: the arrangement and contact structure that must be present for per-slot residuals to fall. It does not nominate one of those atoms as universally sufficient.

I initially overstated this rule in the direction of "never name a predictor." That is retracted in section 6. The correct constraint is that a name needs provenance and a shadow test, and that a universal single-variable answer is still refused.

## 7. Two consequences for outreach

**Prestige is not action.** From *The Recursive Self-Improvement Trap*: *"Prestige can fund an action. It can give an action a budget to be tried. But it cannot settle an action. Only the ground can settle it."* A list of prestigious ecologists who agree with a count-only metric is the named collapse, not corroboration.

**My own audit is a simulation, not a ground reading.** Same essay: LLM output is described as *"simulacra."* So `01_figure_grounded_audit.md` is a simulated derivation checked against the figures and against opened sources. It is not a test of the claim and must be labelled as such. It cannot settle anything it reports on.
