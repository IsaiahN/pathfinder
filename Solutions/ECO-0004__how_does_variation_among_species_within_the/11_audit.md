## Round trip gap R_T

The Symbols table defines R_T as `gap(x, (T_E ∘ T_A)(x))`, the difference between what was sent up and what came back down. Fig 4 :30 requires it to be measured rather than assumed. I ran the round trip on the condensation this folder actually uses.

1. **Sent up (x).** The verbatim figure set, chiefly Fig 12 :123 (group versus qualifier), Fig 1 :19/:22 (per-slot residual), Fig 7 :32-35 (variation inside, selection between), Fig 8 :32/:50 (closure and convergence), Fig 11 :49-51 (valence relative to residual) and Fig 12 :125 (two currencies).
2. **Abstraction (T_A).** The shorthand carried in `07` and `09`: the group fixes kind, the qualifier fixes member, variation is per-slot signal, the perturbation selects, identical responses are one room, independence is the quantity, valence flips, currencies do not add, no number is returned.
3. **Concretisation (T_E).** The operational ecological claim: maintain response diversity among functionally equivalent species; do not manage by richness alone; measure per-member responses under named perturbations; report function and composition separately.

**R_T is nonzero.** Four things the shorthand holds do not survive the trip back down without an external instrument:

- the **source of a response difference** (Fig 8 :51-52). The abstraction says "independent responses"; the concrete claim needs an instrument that distinguishes separate mechanisms from one mechanism at different magnitudes. A trait table cannot do it.
- the **identity of the perturbation and the anchor** (Fig 2 :29/:36). The abstraction says "the perturbation selects"; the concrete claim must name a condition that does not update on group composition. "Disturbance" in general is not an anchor.
- the **currency split** (Fig 12 :125-130). The abstraction says "two currencies"; the concrete claim must decide which function, which turnover measure, and why they are not summed. The figure does not supply units.
- the **echo risk** (strain S1). The abstraction uses "functional group" and "habitat"; the concrete ecological reading is licensed only because the effect was described before the figures were quoted. A reader who skips that order will read the figure's words as ecological evidence, which they are not.

One thing survives cleanly: the group fixes what kind of function is at stake while the qualifier says which member performs, and an averaged member list loses the slot-level signal. That is the residue worth sending. R_T was **not** measured numerically, because no ground reading exists; it is recorded qualitatively as the enumerated set of dropped constraints. Treating "the formula is the condensed version of the metalaws" as identity would set R_T to zero and would be the dead-reckoning error Fig 4 :30 names.
