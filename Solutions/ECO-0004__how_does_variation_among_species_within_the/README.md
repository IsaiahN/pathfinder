## What is deliberately not here

- No personal emails of named individuals.
- No ranking of people by how likely they are to agree.
- No bulk-send plan.
- No assertion that this derivation settles the ecological question. It is a simulated derivation checked for internal consistency against the figures, not a ground reading.

Three reasons, in the figures' own words:

1. **Agreement is not an anchor.** Fig 2 :35: *"Alignment is triangulation, not negotiation. A mirror cannot correct you."* A list of people selected for receptiveness manufactures the correlation that would make their agreement worthless.
2. **Correlation disqualifies a verdict.** Fig 2 :40: *"disqualifying for a verdict: agreement about the world is worthless when the agreeing frames share an evidence pool"*; Fig 2 :41: *"efficient for a convention: agreement about a shared meaning is the product, not a corruption of it"*.
3. **Sending the interiors spends independence.** Fig 10 :42: *"A verifier that can reconstruct the claim cannot verify it."* The first contact carries the residual and the refuter, never the framework.

## The answer in one line

Variation among species within one functional group is not noise to be averaged away: it is the per-slot residual and the independence between member responses that decides whether the perturbation has a different room to select when the current performer fails. The figures return that shape and its refuters, not a species list or a numerical threshold.
