## QUESTION

Decide, in effect terms, what the figures say about the effect of within-functional-group species variation on ecosystem resilience. Option A: treat the group as one averaged actor, so variation is noise on a single response and resilience is a property of group composition. Option B: keep the variation per member, so the group fixes the kind of function while the members supply independent responses that a perturbation can select between. What each leads to: A produces a frame-internal richness or mean-trait score that cannot identify the failing member and cannot say why one system persisted and another did not; B produces a checkable shape, per-member residuals, a source-discriminating instrument requirement, and five refuters written before the conclusion. The figures decide B for the shape, and refuse to name a species, a variance or a universal sign.

## VERDICT (from the figures)

The question as posed is a union, not a dial. The functional group fixes what kind of function is at stake, and the within-group variation is the qualifier that says which member is performing. That variation is the live per-member residual; aggregating it into a richness or mean-trait score discards the signal that says where the failure is. The perturbation is a selector between member rooms: identical responses are one long room with no second response to offer, while independent responses let the ground select the member that matches the surfaced residual. Independence, not member count, carries verdict weight; convergence is the strong case and a shared mechanism is a mirror. Member valence is relative to the perturbation, so no standing list of good or redundant species survives a change of condition, and capability belongs to group-and-habitat. Function persistence and compositional turnover are different currencies and are not pooled. No figure returns a species, a variance, a threshold or a universal sign; that is a declared `gap`. Mode `specified` on the domain verdicts, `general` on the structural shape, ungrounded, candidate.

- **cost if wrong:** bounded and locatable. The packet is mode-labelled, carries refuters before the conclusion and records the lineage, so an error is findable rather than invisible. The real cost is spending the independence of outside readers by sending a pooled richness story instead of a generator. Fig 2 :42 prices that: *"Buy independence in proportion to the cost of being wrong."* A second cost is a wrong restoration decision, since managing for count rather than response independence can leave a group that fails together.
- **undoable:** yes. The shape, the per-slot instruction and the refuters can be sent without any species name or number, and a later ground reading can change the verdict without withdrawing the derivation. No primitive has to be invented or withdrawn. The two live strains (ecological-sounding vocabulary; response as atom or bond) are recorded rather than built into the conclusion.
- **reversed by:** a ground reading, not agreement. A replicated, anchored experiment showing correlated-response groups persist as well as response-diverse groups would fire refuter 1; flat per-member residuals across perturbations would fire refuter 2; a source-discriminating instrument showing one shared response mechanism would fire refuter 3; a systematic sign reversal would fire refuter 4; an unresolvable domain mismatch would fire refuter 5. A single accepted resilience measure that legitimately combines function and composition would withdraw the two-currency split. Prestige, citation count or pooled agreement are not such instruments (Fig 2 :40; *The Recursive Self-Improvement Trap*: prestige can fund an action but cannot settle it). Until something fires, the verdict stands as `candidate`.
