## 1. A shape is a constraint, not a filler

The figures README states the limit: the framework returns *"what a missing thing would have to look like (**a shape, not a name**)."* Fig 9 :32: *"you get a kind of thing, never the answer itself."* Fig 13 :134: *"Unreachable is a verdict here, not a silence,"*.

From *The Library of Babel*, opened this session:

> *"You can cut your search short if the set of books returned is small and descriptive enough that it provides a general shape of the answer that satisfies your question."*

For this question a **shape** is the form an answer must have before anyone can name the species, the variance, or the perturbation. The derived shape is:

> the group fixes what kind of function is at stake; the qualifier says which member performs; within-kind distinctness makes members separate frames; the perturbation selects between those frames; the ground votes on the function's persistence; identical responses are one long room and cannot supply a second response at need.

That is a shape. "Keep the warm-tolerant whelk and the sea star" is a name. "Maintain 12 species per functional group" is a number. Neither is derivable here.

## 2. How the shape was obtained

- *Library of Babel*: start with the vocabulary closest to the thing and look for recurring terms that overlap with it: *"With each new term gained, you are able to narrow the space by definition and deepen your vocabulary."* Here the starting vocabulary was the question's own words: functional group, variation, resilience. The recurring new terms that overlapped were response diversity, response traits, effect traits, asynchrony, insurance, common-cause failure, covariance. Each narrowed the search.
- Fig 6 :30: *"outward: does another frame already hold it? The description travels. Fails by being sharpened into terms only we can read. Cast it in effect terms."* The residual was cast as "functionally equivalent actors whose responses differ under a changing condition" before ecology or reliability engineering was searched.
- Fig 9 :30-31: *"your words are borrowed too, so describe the effect rather than the cause."* This rule is active twice here: once because ecology uses the words "functional group" and "habitat" that also appear in the figures (see section 6), and once because "resilience" is itself a bundle.
- *Decoding Tacit Knowledge*: *"When contact isn't possible, the best you can send is a plain description of what you'd expect to see, because anyone can check that against the world."* The outreach email in `02` therefore asks what a failure would look like, rather than asking for agreement.
- *Library of Babel* on stopping: the answer is reached when *"the finish line will also be the starting place"*, and the paradox is that by then you have *"composed the answer through the process of locating it."* Here the starting description (group fixes kind, variation fixes member) is also the final shape; the intervening work supplied the mechanism and the refuters.

## 3. What licenses a name

Only two things, both outside the shape itself:

1. **The ground settles it.** `THE_FORMULA.md` step 5: *"the ground settles it. Verified only by what pays. A gate is not the ground."* In this case, a replicated perturbation experiment with a non-updating outcome measure. Fig 2 :29: *"An anchor is whatever settles the question and does not change its answer because of what any frame thinks."*
2. **A provenanced import from a frame whose closure differs** (Fig 6 :23, Fig 8 :50-52), with a source gate and a shadow test. Naming response traits or functional groups is a binding from the ecological frame onto an effect description already held; it is provenance, not a new primitive.

*The Hard Problem is About Source*, opened this session, gives the instrument rule. A colour instrument that reads green *"cannot comment on the source"*: the same reading can come from one green light, yellow plus blue, RGB, CMYK, or a scheme not yet discovered. *"If several paths create the same behavior, then behavior is a projection with lossy information."* It was *"the spectrometer, not an argument"* that resolved it.

Applied here: a trait table that says two species are "functionally redundant" reads the group-level output and cannot comment on the source. They may share one response mechanism (one pool) or have converged on the same function by independent responses (maximum independence). Fig 8 :51-52: *"Convergent derivation and adopted import look identical in the contents. Only the record of where each came from separates them."* What is owed is a source-discriminating measurement, not a name.

## 4. Why the pooled resilience number is the specific forbidden error

The question's word "resilience" invites one score. Fig 9 :4-5 says what to do with a disagreement that does not shrink with effort:

> *"are not one hard question but several well-formed ones. Split it rather than search."*

Fig 12 :125-130 adds the two-currency rule:

> *"A composition costs in two currencies, and they do not add."*
> *"Summing them into one figure is the error this names, not the fix for it."*

Pitzer et al. 2026 (opened through the search index) report exactly this split: response diversity increases functional stability and decreases compositional stability. A single score would erase the finding. The shape therefore includes the split; it does not choose one of the two as "real resilience".

## 5. Q: does the framework name which species matter? ANSWERED, no further question needed

**Nothing in the framework names the species or sets the variance.** The framework returns the shape. A particular species is licensed only by the ground settling a claim under a stated perturbation, or by a provenanced import with a shadow test. Until one of those, the species name is the searcher's own contribution and must be recorded as such. Fig 11 :51: *"No standing list of good actors survives a change of residual."*

## 6. Correction to my own reading of the ecological vocabulary (kept visible)

My first pass treated the appearance of "functional group" in Fig 12 and "habitat" in Fig 11 as evidence that the figures were describing ecology directly. That is an **echo**, not corroboration. The canonical article's own "Three mappings offered and declined" section declines the habitat-as-ecological-niche mapping, and *Decoding Tacit Knowledge* warns that shared pretext makes two frames *"one frame wearing two names"* (Fig 8 :48-49).

The correction: the figures are domain-agnostic metalaws. They apply to an ecosystem only because the effect was described first (section 0 of `01`) and the figure lines were then quoted from the verbatim extract. The shared vocabulary is not evidence; it is a hazard that makes an effect description feel unnecessary. The hazard is recorded as strain S1 in `01` section 7 and carried into `11_audit.md`.

This is not a retraction of the shape. It is a retraction of the *reason* I first thought the shape fit: not "the figures use ecological words", but "the effect description selects these lines, and the lines independently constrain the answer".

## 7. Two consequences for outreach

**Prestige is not action.** From *The Recursive Self-Improvement Trap*, opened this session:

> *"Prestige must never be allowed to validate an action directly... Someone let prestige stand in for proof. Someone let reputation substitute for result. Someone let the trusted name validate the untested claim. This is the recursive rot at the helm of many ills of human society."*

A campaign that gathers famous ecologists to agree with the shape is the named collapse, not corroboration. The legitimate ask gives the claim a budget to be tried and lets the ground, an experiment with a non-updating outcome, settle it. Fig 2 :42: *"Buy independence in proportion to the cost of being wrong."*

**My own audit is a simulation, not a ground reading.** Same essay:

> *"LLMs can quickly simulate an approximate answer/output with the formula found in the architecture post. It will often be better than the generic outputs that you will usually get otherwise too (intelligence is essentially arrangement). However, the truly testable output would need to be derived from the real architecture or working the formula yourself."*

So `01`, `06`, `07`, `08`, `09` and `11` are simulated derivations checked for internal consistency against the figures. They are **not** a test of the ecological claim and must be labelled as such. They cannot settle anything they report on.
