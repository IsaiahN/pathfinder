## Three things in the wider literature that bear directly on this pitch

1. **The mechanism was derived independently.** Yachi and Loreau's 1999 model reaches the mirror-chain result mathematically: with perfect positive correlation among species responses, diversity gives no buffering. Fig 7 :32-33 reaches the same shape from the room chain. Fig 8 :50 calls this *"corroboration at maximum independence"* when two frames hold the same atom by convergence. This is the strongest world corroboration available, but it is one witness, not two agreeing witnesses.
2. **The variable is response diversity, not richness.** Elmqvist et al. 2003 define response diversity among species contributing to the same function; Chico-Ortiz et al. 2026 show a case with high functional redundancy and low response diversity where richness is a poor predictor. That matches Fig 1 :22 (*"Aggregating across slots is how a live signal disappears into an average"*) and Fig 9 :60 (filters are not witnesses).
3. **The opposing evidence is real and belongs in the packet.** Pitzer et al. 2026 find functional stability up and compositional stability down; Ross et al. 2024 argue the evidence base is limited. These are not reasons to hide the claim. They are the reason the packet asks for a discriminator and keeps the currencies separate (Fig 12 :125-130).

## Distribution: offer, do not push

- **Fig 4 :22:** *"Only methods cross up. Only head starts come down."* Sending the whole architecture before anyone has described a residual sends a recording, not a generator. Lead with the shape claim and its refuter.
- **Fig 10 :42:** *"A verifier that can reconstruct the claim cannot verify it."* A recipient who has absorbed the theory before giving a verdict has joined the frame being checked.
- **Fig 9 :27-28:** *"describe it before you go looking, or any frame you pick will seem to fit."* Ask what they would expect to see if the shape claim were true, before giving them the article.
- **Fig 2 :40:** *"disqualifying for a verdict: agreement about the world is worthless when the agreeing frames share an evidence pool"*; Fig 2 :41: *"efficient for a convention: agreement about a shared meaning is the product, not a corruption of it"*. Do not select or rank recipients by likely agreement.

**Recommended order in a reply:** (1) the shape and the refuter; (2) ask what they would count as a failure and which function / perturbation / measurement would discriminate; (3) record their verdict; (4) only then, if curious, offer the links, with the note that the figures, not the summary, are the authority.

## What is not done here

No packets were sent, no files were hosted, and no recipient was contacted. Hosting and sending are the owner's actions. If the material is hosted, prefer a link that lets a recipient decline contact, an opt-in download page being closer to the figures' rule than an attachment pushed to a list.
