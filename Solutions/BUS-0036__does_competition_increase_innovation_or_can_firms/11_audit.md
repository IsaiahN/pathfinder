## 1. Independent domain census

I redid the census from the residual line alone, before reading the primary's conclusions, using the WATSON order: R by arity, symmetry and scale; one line per domain with actors / currency / anchor; union or partition; order by dependency.

- **R (arity / symmetry / scale).** The residual is the gap between the research library's prediction and the market's payment. Arity: four-place (firm Γ, competitive structure, research action, market return), indexed by firm and technology slot and ordered in time. It carries an ordered bond (`→`, investment then payment), a subtraction (`−`, profits above baseline) and a comparison (`⋛`, "increase"). Symmetry: not symmetric. Firm and market are not interchangeable; a competition-innovation association is symmetric and cannot carry the arrow. Scale: single firm loop, market population ranking, and the time channel across technology cycles; the between-scale transform is lossy.
- **D1 Firm research loop.** Actors: research staff, routines, Γ. Currency: investment and residual reduction. Anchor: market payment for the product. Admitted.
- **D2 Competition habitat.** Actors: incumbents, entrants, rivals, customers, regulators. Currency: entry, share, survival. Anchor: customer choice and market return; the market is a named anchor. Admitted. Competition is itself a habitat relation.
- **D3 Protection and appropriability.** Actors: patent office, legal system, firm. Currency: granted claims, rents. Anchor: the grant as a counter; value settled later by the market. Admitted, overlaps D2.
- **D4 Temporal channel.** Actors: instruments, metrics, patent counts. Currency: contact. Anchor: the ground, which does not decay while the channel does. Admitted.
- **X1 Innovation as free-floating quantity.** No anchor independent of a product; counts are instruments. Not a domain yet.
- **X2 Market power as sole cause.** Absorbed into D2 and D3.
- **X3 Universal number for competition.** Bound as a sought-for shape, not held as a term.

**Union, not partition.** D1/D3 overlap at appropriability; D2/D4 overlap at market contact over time. The union is C1 (channel to a paying ground) and C2 (reach renewed by a differing closure). Order by dependency: split the question first, settle the gate and the product before comparing regimes, describe R before choosing a frame.

My census matches the primary's four domains and its union reading. - the **gap itself** is filled by an invented peak, which is the shape-not-a-name breach (Fig 9 :32).

What survives cleanly: the question is a union; competition's sign is relative to the residual; a gate is not the ground; patents and market payments are two currencies. That is the residue worth sending. R_T was **not** measured numerically, because no ground reading exists; it is recorded qualitatively as the enumerated set of dropped constraints.
