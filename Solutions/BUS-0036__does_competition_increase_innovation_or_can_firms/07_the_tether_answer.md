# The Tether's answer: competition versus protection for innovation

**Mode: `specified` on the per-domain claims, `general` on the structural shape, `gap` on the numerical balance point. Not `grounded`.** This is a shape and a route, not a result. Simulated derivation checked against the figures (`05_what_shape_means.md` section 6), not a ground reading.

## 1. The residual it answers

The gap between what a firm's research library predicts the market will pay and what the market actually pays, indexed per firm and per technology slot, never averaged into an industry innovation rate:

> **"The residual is indexed by object slot, not aggregated."** (Fig 1 :19)
> **"Aggregating across slots is how a live signal disappears into an average."** (Fig 1 :22)

Two anchors are named: the market pays or does not pay, and the patent grant is counted (Fig 2 :29). R&D spend, patent citations and analyst sentiment are instruments, and *"instruments can be wrong on axes they do not measure"* (Fig 2 :38).

The question as posed is a union, not a partition:

> **"First: is there a fact here? Disagreements that do not shrink with effort, where each rule keeps working well on a different subset, are not one hard question but several well-formed ones. Split it rather than search."** (Fig 9 :4)
> **"A bundled problem is usually a union of overlapping conditions, not a list of separate ones."** (Fig 9 :55)

## 2. The shape

1. **Competition and protection are two conditions, not opposite ends of one dial.** Competition supplies contact with a firm's differing closure and can renew its reach; protection keeps a gate open so the market can settle a research project whose fixed cost needs time. The relevant operator lines are:
   > **"No frame escapes its own closure. The bound moves only on contact with a frame whose closure differs."** (Fig 6 :23)
   > **"Import from a live peer is debited against that frame’s independence. A wrong atom costs more than starting from primitives."** (Fig 6 :24)

   Renewal and independence are one quantity read twice (Fig 8 :47). A policy that maximizes imports can spend the independence that made them useful.

2. **High profits are a gate, not the ground.** `THE_FORMULA.md` step 5 states the settlement rule: *"A gate is not the ground."* The market and the patent counter settle (Fig 2 :29). Protection changes the conditions under which a firm may keep the return; it cannot make the return real. Fig 10 :47: *"a convention that decides an outcome has stopped being one."*

3. **The sign of competition is relative to the residual.** Competition is named as a habitat relation (Fig 11 :47), and:
   > **"An actor’s valence is relative to the residual, not a property it carries."** (Fig 11 :49)
   > **"The same actor is a benefactor to one residual and a malfactor to another, so mutualism and parasitism are relations rather than kinds."** (Fig 11 :50)
   > **"No standing list of good actors survives a change of residual."** (Fig 11 :51)

   The same rivalry encourages a neck-and-neck race and discourages a laggard catch-up. That is the figure-grounded reading of the world's inverted-U.

4. **The balance point has a threshold form and no number here.**
   > **"a threshold relation between two quantities"** (Fig 12 :87)
   > **"you get a kind of thing, never the answer itself"** (Fig 9 :32)
   > **"Unreachable is a verdict here, not a silence,"** (Fig 13 :134)

   The `⋛` form is licensed; its location is a `gap` of the shape-not-a-name kind, reported as unreached rather than silence (Fig 8 :39).

5. **Patent counts and market payments do not add.**
   > **"A composition costs in two currencies, and they do not add."** (Fig 12 :125)
   > **"Summing them into one figure is the error this names, not the fix for it."** (Fig 12 :130)

6. **Research is a mint with a product of three guards.**
   > **"The three guards are a product, not a checklist."** (Fig 5 :33)
   > **"A new term is worth having when stating it, plus what remains unexplained after it, costs less than what was unexplained before."** (Fig 5 :43)

   SUPPORT, REACHABILITY and NOVELTY must all pass; high profits enter at settlement, not as a guard. Fig 5 :48: *"Its own output is never a measure of success."*

7. **Long protection can hand the same room along.**
   > **"same closure, handed along;"** (Fig 7 :12)
   > **"agreement compounds, evidence does not"** (Fig 7 :13)
   > **"A chain of mirrors is one long room. The next room must differ from the last, and match the residual it surfaced."** (Fig 7 :36)

   Progress per handoff needs both bracket fidelity and ground pressure (Fig 7 :37-39). Either at zero and the chain runs in place.

## 3. What it explicitly does not say

- Not "competition always increases innovation" and not "monopoly is always better for R&D." Both are names where the framework returns a per-slot sign.
- Not a competition index, an optimal patent term, or an industry percentage. Those are the number the framework does not hold (Fig 9 :32).
- Not that patents measure innovation. A patent is a counted legal event, an instrument, not the ground (Fig 2 :29-30, :38).
- Not a derivation of Schumpeter from Arrow or of Arrow from Schumpeter. Their disagreement is a union of subsets, not a chain (Fig 9 :4).
- Not a claim that protection does nothing. A gate can be necessary for a mint to be settled even though it cannot settle it.
- Not `grounded`. No market payment for a named product was measured here, so no claim is accepted.

## 4. What would settle it, the ground

A per-slot market measurement, not a further argument. The questions an instrument must answer:

- **Sign flip.** Does the same competition event have opposite signs for a frontier race and a catch-up problem in the same industry, measured per slot? A monotone sign across both would break F6.
- **Gate versus ground.** Does research keep paying after the market stops paying for the product and the channel is severed? If it does, protection has become the ground, contrary to Formula step 5.
- **Threshold.** At what competition level does the sign flip for a named technology? No figure returns this; an instrument is owed. The ecology analogue supplies only the form, with its own contested record.
- **Two currencies.** Can patent counts and market payments be converted into one additive unit that predicts prospectively? Fig 12 :130 says no; a working conversion would reverse F11.
- **Standing falsifiers** from `01` section 4: a monotone predictor across all slots, reach renewed with no differing closure, payment after the channel is severed, a patent that adds an atom, invariant valence across residuals, one lever restoring both conditions, or a failure of Fig 6 idempotence.

## 5. The honest stop

**Unreached, not unreachable.** The decomposition into "competition" and "protection" is a visible choice made by the question, not nature's own list. Fig 13 :134: *"Unreachable is a verdict here, not a silence,"* and Fig 8 :39: *"So a search that finds nothing is never a proof of absence."* The answer stops where the instruments can no longer distinguish a patent count from a market payment, and records the lineage so the stop is checkable. *"Indiscernibility is not identity"* (figures README). The world literature supplies an inverted-U and a sign flip as corroboration, but it does not move the figure verdict and it is not a ground reading of this derivation.

## 6. One-paragraph version for outreach

> The Tether does not answer "competition or protection?" with one winner. It says the question is a union of two conditions: rivalry is the contact with a differing closure that can renew a firm's research reach, at a debit against its independence, while temporary protection is a gate that keeps a paying market reachable long enough to settle a research project with a fixed cost. Neither is the ground; the market and the count of granted patents are the anchors, and only the market pays. The sign of rivalry is relative to the residual, so it encourages a neck-and-neck race and discourages a laggard catch-up, which is the figure's reading of the inverted-U the economics literature observes. Patent counts and market payments are two currencies that do not add. What the framework returns is this shape, an instrument owed to name the source of a reading, and falsifiers written in advance. The balance point stays open until a per-slot measurement settles it.
