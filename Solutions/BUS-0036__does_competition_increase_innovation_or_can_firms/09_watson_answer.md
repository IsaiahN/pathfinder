# WATSON answer: BUS-0036, does competition increase innovation or can firms need temporary protection to justify research?

**Mode: `specified` on the per-domain claims, `general` on the structural shape, `gap` on the numerical balance point. Not `grounded`.** Figures are primary and decide. World sources are secondary, corroborate but never overrule, and a conflict returns the derivation to the domain census. This is a simulated derivation checked for internal consistency against the figures, not a ground reading.

## QUESTION

Decide, in effect terms, what the Tether says about the choice between promoting competition and granting temporary protection plus high profits to justify research. Option A: competition increases innovation. Option B: firms need protection and high profits to justify research. What each leads to: A sends a single lever to every firm slot and destroys the return in the slots where rivalry is a malfactor; B keeps a gate open where the market still pays and hands the same room along where it does not. The shape claims there is no single winner: the two options are conditions in a union with different anchors, and the sign of competition is relative to the residual. Restated as the residual, `R = |Γ(b,a) − o′|` per slot, where Γ is the firm's research library, a is the research or entry action, o′ is the market payment, and the slot is the firm and technology (Fig 1 :19).

## DOMAIN CENSUS

R by arity, symmetry and scale, then one line per domain with actors, currency and anchor; partition or union; order by dependency.

- **R (arity / symmetry / scale).** A four-place relation: firm library, competitive structure, research action, market payment, indexed by slot and ordered in time. It carries an ordered bond (`→`, investment precedes payment), a subtraction for profits above a competitive baseline (`−`), and a comparison for "increase" (`⋛`). It is not symmetric: firm and market are not interchangeable, and a competition-innovation correlation is symmetric while "increases" is ordered. Scale: single firm loop, market population ranking, time channel across technology cycles.
- **D1 Firm research loop.** Actors: research staff, routines, Γ, prior art. Currency: investment actions and residual reduction. Anchor: realized market payment for the product. Admitted.
- **D2 Competition habitat.** Actors: incumbents, entrants, rivals, customers, regulators; competition is named as a habitat relation in Fig 11 :47. Currency: entry, share, price, survival. Anchor: customer choice and market return; the market is a named anchor in Fig 2 :29. Admitted.
- **D3 Protection and appropriability frame.** Actors: patent office, legal system, firm. Currency: granted claims, excluded imitators, appropriable rents. Anchor: the grant as a counted legal event (Fig 2 :29, "a counter"); the value is settled later by the market. Admitted, overlaps D2.
- **D4 Temporal channel.** Actors: instruments, metrics, patent counts, percepts. Currency: contact with the ground. Anchor: the ground, which does not decay while the channel does (Fig 10 :24). Admitted.
- **X1 Innovation as a free-floating quantity.** No anchor independent of a product; R&D spend and patent counts are instruments that can be wrong on axes they do not measure (Fig 2 :38). Not a domain yet; handled as an instrument.
- **X2 Market power as sole cause.** Already inside D2 and D3. Absorbed.
- **X3 The right amount of competition as a standing number.** No nameable anchor; the number is a sought-for shape, bound as a term in `01` section 0.

**Union, not partition.** D1/D3 overlap because appropriability enters Γ; D2/D4 overlap because market contact is exercised in time. The union is C1 (a maintained channel to a paying ground) and C2 (renewal of reach through contact with a differing closure), with different instruments. Fig 9 :4 says a disagreement that does not shrink, with each rule working well on a different subset, is several questions; split rather than search. **Dependency order:** refuse the partition before splitting (Fig 9 :4); settle each first-order molecule before the second-order regime comparison (Fig 12 :104); describe the residual before choosing a frame (Fig 9 :27-28).

**Strain test:** applying Fig 13's six-term physical substrate to market structure only fit by renaming rivalry as a gradient and protection as a spectrum. Re-census sent Fig 13 to the honest-stop role only. No unresolved strain after re-census.

## VERDICT (from the figures)

The question as posed has no single winner. It is a union of two conditions with different anchors. Competition is the contact with a differing closure that can renew a firm's reach, at a debit against independence; protection is a gate that keeps a paying market reachable long enough to settle a research mint with a fixed cost. Neither is the ground: the market and the patent counter are the anchors, and only the market pays. Competition's sign is relative to the residual, so it encourages a neck-and-neck race and discourages a laggard catch-up. Patent counts and market payments are two currencies that do not add. The balance point has a threshold form but no number here. Verdicts are kept separate per domain and never averaged. Mode `specified` on the per-domain claims, `general` on the structural shape, `gap` on the number. The world sources corroborate the union and the sign flip; the one conflict was re-censused, not used to flip the verdict.

- **cost if wrong:** a single lever is applied to a union. Promoting rivalry in a catch-up slot destroys the return before the research pays; extending protection in a frontier-race slot hands the same room along and compounds agreement without evidence. The cost is also spent on outside readers: sending a recording instead of a generator spends their independence, which Fig 2 :42 prices (*"Buy independence in proportion to the cost of being wrong."*). The error is bounded and locatable because the seven refuters are written down in advance.
- **undoable:** yes. No term is deleted if the claim fails; a refutation is added and the reach falls because *"something was added rather than because anything was deleted"* (Fig 6 :42). The shape and its refuters are recorded with provenance in `01` and `08`, so a later reader can locate the error rather than only feel it (Fig 10 :62-64).
- **reversed by:** a ground reading, not agreement. A per-slot measurement showing a monotone sign across all residuals would break F1 and F6; reach renewed with no differing closure would break F5; payment after the channel is severed would break F3 and F7; a patent that adds an atom would break the gate-versus-ground distinction; one lever restoring both conditions with no categorically different residue would collapse the union; a working one-currency conversion would break F11; or a failure of Fig 6 idempotence would break the figures themselves. Until one of these fires, the verdict stands as `candidate`.
