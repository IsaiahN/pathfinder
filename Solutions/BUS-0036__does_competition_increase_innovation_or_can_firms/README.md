# Solution BUS-0036

**Question:** Does competition increase innovation, or can firms need temporary protection and high profits to justify research?

**Source:** business.txt line 49

**Section:** 4. Innovation, research, and disruption

**Mode:** `specified` on the per-domain claims, `general` on the structural split, `gap` on the location of the balance point. Nothing is `grounded`.

**Status:** done

**Registry status:** pending (the registry is outside this folder and was not modified)

**Question restated as an effect.** The decision is between two levers: promote entry and rivalry, or grant temporary protection and allow high profits. Option A, competition, contacts a firm with closures that differ from its own. Option B, protection, keeps a paying ground reachable long enough to pay for a fixed research cost. Each leads to a different failure if applied to the wrong slot. The answer that would bind as an ordinary term is: a pair of conditions with different anchors, plus an instrument that says which one is failing in a given slot. Until bound, that shape predicts nothing and no loop check can see it (Formula, "Direction").
