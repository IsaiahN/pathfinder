## 1. A shape is a constraint, not a filler

The figures README states the limit: the framework returns *"what a missing thing would have to look like (a shape, not a name)."* Fig 9 :32: *"you get a kind of thing, never the answer itself."* Fig 13 :134: *"Unreachable is a verdict here, not a silence,"* and :135: *"It cannot say which primitive is missing, because naming it would mean already holding it."*

From *The Library of Babel*, opened this session: *"you can cut your search short if the set of books returned is small and descriptive enough that it provides a general shape of the answer that satisfies your question."*

So a **shape** is the narrowed form of an answer you can state before you can name it. It constrains; it does not fill. For this question:

- A shape: competition and protection are two conditions in a union with different anchors, and competition's sign is relative to the residual.
- A name: "the optimal competition index is X" or "patent term length Y is the answer."
- A shape also includes the *instrument* owed: something that discriminates a paying-channel failure from a reach-renewal failure per slot.

The question as posed asks for a choice between two names. The framework can only return the shape of the choice, plus what would settle it.

## 2. How a shape is obtained

- *Library of Babel*, opened this session: use the vocabulary already held that is closest to the thing, then recurse. *"With each new term gained, you are able to narrow the space by definition and deepen your vocabulary."* The bundle stop: *"Perhaps your problem was a bundle or conflation of several problems masquerading as one?"* That is exactly the response to the either/or in BUS-0036.
- Fig 6 :30: *"outward: does another frame already hold it? The description travels. Fails by being sharpened into terms only we can read. Cast it in effect terms."* Fig 9 :30-31: *"your words are borrowed too, so describe the effect"* and *"rather than the cause."*
- *Decoding Tacit Knowledge* (carried from the exemplar's canonical mapping): *"When contact isn't possible, the best you can send is a plain description of what you'd expect to see, because anyone can check that against the world."* For this question: what would you expect to see if rivalry were renewing reach rather than destroying the return?
- *Library of Babel* on stopping: *"You will know that you are done because the finish line will also be the starting place."* The paradox is that by then you have *"composed the answer through the process of locating it."*

The derivation in `01` follows this order: describe R in effect terms, census the domains by actor, currency and anchor, split the union, then read figure lines. It did not start by choosing Schumpeter or Arrow.

## 3. What licenses a name

Only two things, both outside the shape itself:

1. **The ground settles it.** Formula step 5: *"the ground settles it. Verified only by what pays. A gate is not the ground."* The README: the framework *"stops at the limit of what the available instruments can distinguish, and records the lineage so the stop is honest."* For this question, a market payment per named product is the settlement. A patent grant is a count, not that settlement.
2. **A provenanced import from a frame whose closure differs** (Fig 6 :23, Fig 8 :51), with a source gate and a shadow test. The economics literature is such a frame: Aghion et al. bring an inverted-U that the figures do not contain, but it is admitted only with provenance and only as world corroboration, never as a figure-derived verdict.

*They're Scanning Books* (carried from the exemplar's canonical mapping) sharpens this: *"The real import is not a new term in the existing space. It is a new dimension of the space."* And: *"Declaring the primitives does not make them neutral. It makes the choice visible."* The choice to treat competition as a habitat relation (Fig 11 :47) is a visible choice, not a discovery of the market's own list.

**Correction to an overstatement.** The README's phrase is *"what a **missing** thing would have to look like (a shape, not a name)."* It governs a primitive not held in Γ. Competition, protection and patents are already in the firm's and the market's library; naming them is a **binding** from the economics frame onto held objects, not the invention of a new primitive. What remains forbidden is returning a number the framework does not hold. This correction is kept visible rather than silently applied, and it matches the correction recorded in the exemplar's `05` section 5b and in BUS-0001, BUS-0009 and BUS-0015.

## 4. The ground and the source problem

*The Hard Problem is About Source* (carried from the exemplar's canonical mapping) gives the mechanism. Metamerism: an instrument reads green and *"cannot comment on the source"* one green light, or yellow plus blue, or RGB, or CMYK. The reading does not determine the source. *"If several paths create the same behavior, then behavior is a projection with lossy information."* It was the spectrometer, not an argument, that resolved it.

The question's version: patent counts, R&D spend and productivity are readings. Several different sources can give the same reading: genuine new products, patent shelving, incremental improvements, a monopoly rent with no new product, or a spillover captured by a rival. Naming "innovation" from a patent reading is the metamerism error. What is owed is a discriminator, not a name. Fig 2 :38: *"instruments can be wrong on axes they do not measure."* Fig 12 :130: *"Summing them into one figure is the error this names, not the fix for it."*

*The Recursive Self-Improvement Trap*, opened this session: *"the output is only as good as the validator. The agent, or the tool, or the frame, or the theory, cannot validate itself."* And: *"You may all agree and be wrong. You may set your clock to someone else's, who was setting your clock to yours, and be off by many deviations over time."* That is Fig 2 :13-15 (mutual update) stated in the essay's own words, and it is why a survey of economists agreeing with the framework would not settle BUS-0036.

## 5. Answer to the shape question

**What does the framework return for "does competition increase innovation, or can firms need temporary protection and high profits to justify research?"**

It returns: a union of two conditions with different anchors, a per-residual sign rule for competition, a gate-versus-ground distinction for high profits, a threshold form for the balance point, a two-currency rule that refuses to add patents to market value, and an owed instrument that would name the source of a reading. It does not return a single direction, a competition index, a patent term, or a `grounded` verdict.

**Does this solution commit the forbidden error?** No, provided the gap at F9 stays a gap. The moment a number is asserted as *the* balance point, or patent count is treated as the ground, the shape has been filled and the framework's own limit breached. The one place where the first draft came close was F1, which treated the question as a two-answer partition; that was retracted in `01` section 6 and kept visible.

## 6. Consequences for contact

**Prestige is not action.** From *The Recursive Self-Improvement Trap*, opened this session: *"Prestige must never be allowed to validate an action directly... Someone let prestige stand in for proof... Prestige can fund an action. It can give an action a budget to be tried. But it cannot settle an action. Only the ground can settle it. The Ground Maintainer must be blind to prestige."* A list of famous economists who agree with the shape is the named collapse, not corroboration. The legitimate ask gives the claim a budget to be tried and lets the market, not a name, settle it.

**My own audit is a simulation, not a ground reading.** The same essay: *"LLMs can quickly simulate an approximate answer/output with the formula found in the architecture post... However, the truly testable output would need to be derived from the real architecture or working the formula yourself."* So `01`, `06`, `07`, `09` and `11` are simulated derivations checked for internal consistency against the figures. They are **not** a test of the industrial-organization claim and must be labelled as such. They cannot settle it.

**The sign, the shape and the number.** A shape can say that competition's sign flips and that a threshold exists (Fig 11 :50, Fig 12 :87). It cannot say where, for whom, or by how much. That is the honest stop, and it is a verdict, not a silence (Fig 13 :134).
