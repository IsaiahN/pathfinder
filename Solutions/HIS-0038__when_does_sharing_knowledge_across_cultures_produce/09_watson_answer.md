## QUESTION

Decide, in effect terms, when a transfer of knowledge between cultures is genuine exchange and when it is extraction. Option A: treat the two as kinds of transfer distinguishable by content, partner type or stated intention. Option B: treat them as two relations to a residual and a ground, distinguishable only by lineage, reach, independence and habitat. What each leads to: A produces a global label on a partnership, spends the independence of the people asked to check it, and mistakes a convention for a verdict; B produces a per-slot discriminator with written refuters and an open case until a ground settles it.

## DOMAIN CENSUS

R by arity, symmetry and scale, then one line per domain with actors, currency and anchor. A candidate with no nameable anchor is not admitted.

- **R (the residual).** The gap between a transfer event as described by its participants and the state of the receiving frame's reach and ground afterwards. Arity: three (source frame, item, receiving frame), with a lineage and a habitat as conditions. Symmetry: not symmetric, because introduction is available and exclusion is not (Fig 11 :61). Scale: first order at the contact, second order when a settled transfer becomes an atom for a further composition. Fig 9 names a union compound: a case failing both lineage and settlement is a specific compound, not a harder case of either.
- **Domain 1, what crosses the membrane.** Actors: two frames with libraries and the item moved. Currency: methods and head starts versus recordings and labels. Anchor: the receiving ground and whether its reach grows. Partition.
- **Domain 2, transfer structure.** Actors: two closures, importer and source. Currency: atom crossing back, blend vocabulary, mute union. Anchor: the wall in the receiving closure moves. Partition with overlap compound.
- **Domain 3, verification.** Actors: frames, expert readers, instruments. Currency: agreement versus evidence. Anchor: the ground, never agreement. Partition.
- **Domain 4, habitat and valence.** Actors: people, institutions, artifacts, conditions, relations in contact with R. Currency: contact (introduction versus substitution). Anchor: whether the goal still works after the habitat changes. Union compound because contact cascades.
- **Order by dependency.** Describe the residual before choosing a frame; enumerate the habitat; refuse before mint; check the record; settle at the ground; measure the round trip (Fig 3 :20, Fig 9 :27 to :28, formula steps 2 and 5).

Strain test: Fig 8 :32 says no search adds an atom to a closed set. Read as "no work inside a culture helps", it strains. Re-census shows :32 governs closure of a fixed library, while the transfer event is the import that moves the wall (Fig 6 :14). No strain after re-census.

## VERDICT (from the figures)

The two verdicts are relations to a residual, not kinds of transfer or kinds of actor. Exchange: a method crosses between frames whose closures differ, the item's route is recorded, the receiving closure expands, and the new term settles against the receiving frame's own ground while the source frame's reach is undiminished. Extraction: a recording crosses without its lineage, agreement comes from one evidence pool, the wall does not move, the receiving ground is repointed to the item so it cannot be refused, or the habitat is substituted by withdrawal. Verdicts are per slot. Consent, benefit sharing, documentation and institutional prestige are budget filters, never verdict witnesses. Bits and actions are two currencies and do not add. This is a `specified`, ungrounded result, not a settlement of any real case.

- **cost if wrong:** bounded and locatable if the packet is sent as written, because it states mode, three checkable sub-claims and refuters before the conclusion, so an error is findable rather than invisible. The real cost is spending the independence of outside readers by sending a finished verdict instead of a discriminator, which Fig 2 :42 prices: "Buy independence in proportion to the cost of being wrong." That cost is avoidable by leading with the shape claim and its refuter. A second cost is practical: a global label of "extraction" applied to a real community could redirect funding and trust on no ground reading at all.
- **undoable:** yes. The global-label reading is removable without touching the derivation; `07_the_tether_answer.md` and `08_expert_verification_packet.md` already remove it. A sent claim can be superseded by the corrected packet, and no atom is withdrawn. The field material can be replaced with other sources without changing the figure verdict, because the figures decide.
- **reversed by:** a ground reading, not agreement. A source-discriminating instrument that settles whether an imported term was derived or adopted would close the lineage test; a receiving frame settling a new term with no lineage and one shared evidence pool would break D1 and D2; an actor parasitic relative to every residual would break D4; a single measure predicting documentation and settlement with both measured would break the two-currency claim; a removal leaving the receiving habitat unchanged and the ground reachable would break the substitution claim. Until one of these fires, the verdict stands as `candidate`.
