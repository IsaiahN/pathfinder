## Overlap warning about the world sources

The six field articles are independent subject-matter articles, but five of them are hosted by Wikipedia and share its editorial pool. Agreement among them is weak corroboration for that reason alone. The four essays share one author with the figures, so their agreement is expected and carries little independent weight (Fig 2 :40 *"agreement about the world is worthless when the agreeing frames share an evidence pool"*). The genuinely low-overlap frames here are: cognitive science and animal behaviour (Cultural learning), organisational studies (Knowledge transfer), critical data studies (Data colonialism), law (Biopiracy), and ecology (Traditional ecological knowledge). None of them uses the Tether vocabulary, and none was written to corroborate this answer.

## Distribution: offer, do not push

- **Fig 4 :22 to :25** *"Only methods cross up. Only head starts come down."* Sending the full framework before a recipient has described a residual sends a recording, not a generator. Lead with the discriminator and its refuter; offer links after.
- **Fig 10 :42** *"A verifier that can reconstruct the claim cannot verify it."* A recipient who has absorbed the framework before giving a verdict has joined the frame being checked.
- **Fig 9 :27 to :28** *"describe it before you go looking, or any frame you pick will seem to fit."* Ask what they would expect to see if the discriminator were true before giving them the architecture.
- **Fig 2 :39** *"Correlation has valence."* Selecting recipients by likelihood of agreement manufactures the correlation that makes their agreement worthless.
- **Fig 6 :24** *"Import from a live peer is debited against that frame's independence. A wrong atom costs more than starting from primitives."* Do not hand over the atom early.
- **Fig 9 :60** *"Use filters for the budget and witnesses for the verdict. Never let a filter hand you a verdict."* Open-access status, journal impact and institutional prestige are filters.

**Recommended order in a reply:** (1) the discriminator stated in effect terms; (2) ask what the recipient would count as a failure; (3) record that verdict; (4) only then the links, with the note that the figures, not this summary, are the authority.

## The two-stage offer, restated

1. **Stage 1.** Send the residual and the pre-registered refuter. Ask for a failure, not an endorsement. Record the verdict before any theory is shown.
2. **Stage 2, opt-in only.** If the recipient is curious, offer the figure text and the canonical README. Their later agreement is never pooled as corroboration (Fig 2 :40).
3. **Stage 3.** Offer the formula as a method to reuse. Each user writes their own refuter and states their own mode first.

**Hard precondition:** stages 1 to 3 only work if the stage-1 discriminator survives. It is currently `specified` and ungrounded. It has not been run against a real case in this folder.
