## 0. The residual, stated in effect terms

A transfer event: an item (practice, datum, method, classification, artifact, or agreement) moves between frame A and frame B, where a frame is *"any bounded system with a library: an agent, a person, a discipline, a model"* (Symbols table :111). The same surface event can end in two different states:

- **E, genuine exchange.** B's closure expands. B can reach a term φ* it could not derive from Γ_B alone, the imported primitive settles against B's ground, and A's reach is not consumed by the transfer. Both frames gain contact.
- **X, extraction.** An item crosses and agreement is produced, but B's wall is unmoved, or the lineage of the item is absent, or the item is installed as a convention that decides outcomes it cannot settle, or B's habitat is substituted so that the goal silently changes.

The residual R is the gap between the transfer event as described by its participants and the state of B's reach and ground after it. Crucially, **R is indexed per slot, not aggregated**: Fig 1 :19 *"The residual is indexed by object slot, not aggregated."* A transfer can be genuine for one practice in one domain and extractive for another practice in the same partnership. Fig 1 :22 *"Aggregating across slots is how a live signal disappears into an average."* Any verdict of the form "the whole collaboration was exchange" or "the whole collaboration was extraction" is an aggregation error before it is a moral claim.

An answer would look like a discriminator that returns E or X for one slot of one transfer, with a written refuter. This is bound as an ordinary term here, before the conclusion, so it can accrue a residual like any other (formula "Direction").

## 1. Domain census, before any figure line is chosen

R by arity, symmetry and scale:

- **Arity:** binary at minimum (two frames), but with a third term, the item moved, so the minimal composition is a three-operand relation, not a simple A to B edge.
- **Symmetry:** not symmetric in general. Introduction is available to the receiving seat and exclusion is not (Fig 11 :61). The transform is lossy (Fig 4 :27), so carrying a method up and replaying it down does not return the original.
- **Scale:** first order at the contact between two frames, second order when a settled transfer becomes an atom for a further composition (Fig 12 :104).

One line per domain, with actors, currency and anchor. A candidate with no nameable anchor is not yet a domain and is not admitted.

| domain | actors | currency | anchor (nameable?) | question type |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| **D1, what crosses** | frames A and B with libraries Γ_A, Γ_B; the membrane; the item | methods and head starts versus recordings and labels | yes: whether a term settles against B's ground and B's reach grows | partition (method / recording) |
| **D2, transfer structure** | two closures, the importing frame, the source frame | an atom crossing back versus vocabulary versus a mute union | yes: the wall in B moves (Fig 8 :9, :45) | partition with overlap compound |
| **D3, verification** | frames, expert readers, instruments | agreement versus evidence | yes: the ground, never agreement (Fig 2 :45) | partition (triangulation / mirror) |
| **D4, habitat and valence** | people, institutions, artifacts, conditions, relations in contact with R | contact (introduction versus substitution) | yes: whether the goal still works after the habitat changes | union compound (contact cascades) |

**No fifth domain admitted.** "Intentions of the participants" has no nameable anchor in the figures: only the reading of the ground and the record of lineage are checkable, and the seat map says a frame cannot check its own direction (Seat Map :190 to :192). Intention is declared as a stake, not used as a verdict axis (Fig 10 :59).

**Partition or union.** D1 and D3 are partitions. D2 looks like a partition but a case can satisfy both import and blend conditions, so it is a union compound with its own loop; a single lever clearing one condition leaves the intersection standing (Fig 9 :54 to :58). D4 is a union compound because contact cascades: first-order enumeration misses the third-order effect (Fig 11 :52 to :53).

**Order by dependency (Fig 3).** The chain is ordered by dependency, not importance (Fig 3 :20). The usable order here is: name the residual and enumerate the habitat (D4) → decide what crosses (D1) → decide whether the wall moves (D2) → verify by triangulation against the ground (D3). Refuse before mint (formula step 2), settle before composing (step 5), describe the residual before choosing a frame (Fig 9 :27 to :28).

## 3. Findings F1 to F9

### F1 - The discriminator is the record and the ground, not the content (decisive, triangulated)

Fig 8 :51 *"Convergent derivation and adopted import look identical in the contents. Only the record of where each came from separates them"*. Fig 6 :23 *"No frame escapes its own closure. The bound moves only on contact with a frame whose closure differs."*

A transferred practice, a patent, a translated text, or a dataset looks the same in the receiving library whether it was converged on independently or adopted. The contents cannot decide E versus X. What decides is (a) whether the lineage is recorded, and (b) whether the atom settles against B's ground and B's reach grows. Fig 10 :65 separates the two: clean provenance is not correctness; the ground is correctness.

This is why "fair benefit sharing" cannot be the verdict witness. It is a filter (Fig 9 :60). A contract can be perfectly documented and the imported term still unsettled.

**Refuter:** exhibit a transfer in which provenance is absent, the receiving closure does not expand, yet the item settles against the receiving ground and the source frame's reach is undiminished. That breaks the necessity of both the record and the wall-movement condition.

### F2 - Methods cross, recordings are replayed (triangulated)

Fig 4 :22 *"Only methods cross up. Only head starts come down."* Fig 4 :25 *"A recording carried upward looks like knowledge and is a description of one occasion."*

Genuine exchange carries a generator: a method that can be reapplied in the receiving frame, or a head start that makes its next search cheaper. Extraction carries a recording: the artifact of one occasion, presented as the method. Replayed downward it produces *"a system that repeats a past success and cannot produce a new one"* (Fig 4 :25 to :26, same passage).

The cross-cultural literature corroborates the surface form without deciding the verdict. Wikipedia, Knowledge transfer: *"Knowledge transfer is the transfer of facts or practical skills from one entity to another."* The same article distinguishes an object-view of knowledge from a context-view, and notes that transfer *"raises ethical considerations particularly where there is an imbalance in power relationships."* These are two different frames on the same event, which is exactly Fig 8 :47.

**Refuter:** show a recording that, replayed in the receiving frame without its lineage or its history, generates a new term that settles at that frame's ground. Then the recording was a generator in disguise and the method/recording distinction collapses.

### F3 - Independence is the single parameter behind both halves (triangulated)

Fig 8 :47 *"Independence is one quantity read twice."* Fig 2 :40 *"disqualifying for a verdict: agreement about the world is worthless when the agreeing frames share an evidence pool"*. Fig 2 :41 *"efficient for a convention: agreement about a shared meaning is the product, not a corruption of it."*

Extraction commonly presents as agreement: two institutions endorse the same account, cite the same source, and announce a successful exchange. If they draw on one evidence pool, the agreement is the pool talking (Fig 2 :19 to :21), so it is evidence for a convention and worthless for a verdict. Conversely, real convergence from separate experience is corroboration at maximum independence (Fig 8 :50): *"Two frames can hold the same atom by convergence, reaching it separately, which is corroboration at maximum independence."*

The cultural-learning literature supplies a low-overlap corroboration. Wikipedia, Cultural learning, citing van Schaik and Burkart: *"Opportunities for social learning allow an individual to acquire many learned skills during development that it could not acquire on its own."* The same article records how a story passed person to person loses what does not fit and becomes more familiar until each report mirrors its culture (Bartlett). The gain and the loss are the same mechanism, separated only by what the receiving frame checks against.

**Refuter:** exhibit two frames with measurably shared evidence whose agreement still settles a new term neither held, with no third ground consulted. That breaks the independence condition.

### F4 - A chain of agreeing frames compounds agreement without adding evidence (triangulated)

Fig 7 :13 *"agreement compounds, evidence does not"*. Fig 7 :36 *"A chain of mirrors is one long room. The next room must differ from the last, and match the residual it surfaced."*

A practice passed through a sequence of willing partners, each endorsing the last, is a mirror chain. It looks like diffusion and is motion, not progress. The genuine handoff needs two factors: the next frame must differ from the last, and it must match the residual the last one surfaced (Fig 7 :36). Fig 7 :37 to :39 say zero either factor and the chain runs in place.

**Refuter:** show a chain of frames with no closure difference between successive members in which the residual closed at the ground after the handoff. That breaks the requirement that the next room differ.

### F5 - Exchange and extraction are relations to a residual, not kinds of actor (triangulated)

Fig 11 :50 *"The same actor is a benefactor to one residual and a malfactor to another, so mutualism and parasitism are relations rather than kinds."* Fig 11 :51 *"No standing list of good actors survives a change of residual."*

A university, a foundation, a collector, an archive, or a state agency cannot be labelled extractive in itself. It can be extractive relative to one residual and beneficent relative to another. This kills the person-based verdict before it starts: the verdict attaches to a transfer slot, not to an identity. It also kills the reverse move of certifying a partner as "community-led" and treating that label as a verdict.

Fig 11 :39 and :40 add the capability claim: *"capability is a property of agent-and-habitat, never of the agent alone"* and *"An improvement that does not change contact changes nothing, however much it improves."* A partnership that improves the paperwork without changing contact has not changed capability.

**Refuter:** find an actor that is parasitic relative to every residual in every habitat. Fig 11 :51 says no such list survives; if one is produced, the figure is wrong.

### F6 - Withdrawal is substitution, not removal (single-source for the figure, corroborated by field cases)

Fig 11 :61 *"Introduction is available; exclusion is not."* Fig 11 :66 *"Isolation is not removal of the habitat. It is substitution of one habitat for another."* Fig 11 :68 to :69 name the two silent failure modes: *"what you failed to reproduce is invisible until the goal fails"* and *"what you unintentionally introduced is invisible until it acts."*

The common remedy for extraction is to withdraw. The figures say withdrawal cannot restore the prior habitat; it replaces it with another one and imports actors not chosen by anyone. So "we stopped the project" is not evidence that the extraction ended. The residual must be reproduced in the new habitat, and the inherited actors will act.

Field corroboration: Wikipedia, Data colonialism, describes a practice in which governments, organizations and corporations *"extract, control, or profit from data produced by individuals"* and notes that *"resources are appropriated first, while questions of equity are addressed only afterward."* The sequence matters: remediation after appropriation is a new habitat, not the old one restored.

**Refuter:** show a removal of a transferred practice that leaves the receiving habitat unchanged and the ground reachable, with no inherited actors and nothing unreproduced. Then isolation is removal and the law fails.

### F7 - A repointed ground is the deepest extraction failure (triangulated)

Fig 10 :49 *"Declaring a meaning does not ground it."* Fig 10 :93 *"A ground with a reputation to protect is a ground someone benefits from, and its keeper is thereby disqualified."* Fig 2 :45 *"Frames range against the ground. They do not negotiate with it."*

Extraction is not only taking an item. It is installing the item as the thing that decides outcomes, so that the receiving frame can no longer refuse it. The seat map (:163) says a convention that decides an outcome has stopped being a convention. Once the imported item is unfalsifiable inside the receiving frame, that frame has lost contact with its ground, and Fig 10 :28 names the output: *"you have lost touch."*

The Traditional knowledge article reports the Tulalip Tribes' position that open sharing does not automatically confer a right to use knowledge, and frames the problem as failure to recognise customary law. That is a claim about a ground (what the customary law settles), not about whether documents exist. Treating the document as the ground is the F7 failure.

**Refuter:** exhibit an extraction case in which the receiving frame's anchor was never repointed, the item is refusable there, and still no settlement occurs. Then ground repointing is not the discriminator.

### F8 - Two currencies, and they do not add (single-source, named strain)

Fig 12 :125 *"A composition costs in two currencies, and they do not add."* Fig 12 :126 *"Bits price the description. Actions price finding out whether it holds."*

A transfer can be richly documented, consented and benefit-shared (bits) while no action was ever spent checking whether the imported term settles in the receiving frame (actions). Summing the two into one "ethical score" is the exact error the figure names. Neither figure predicts the other, so both must be read.

This is the point at which the field literature is most useful and most dangerous. Biopiracy (Wikipedia) defines the term as *"the unauthorized appropriation of knowledge and genetic resources of farming and indigenous communities by individuals or institutions seeking exclusive monopoly control through patents or intellectual property."* That is a statement about the authorization currency, not the settlement currency. It corroborates F1 and F2 and is silent on F8.

**Refuter:** produce a single measure that predicts both documentation quality and settlement, with both axes measured. Until then the two currencies are held apart.

## 4. Pre-registered refuter

Written before the conclusion and before any outreach, per Seat Map :287 *"a falsifier written before the run is a design instrument, not only an honesty device."*

The shape claim is refuted if any of the following holds:

1. A transfer is shown in which the receiving closure expands and the new term settles at the receiving ground, with no recorded lineage and with high evidence overlap with the source frame. This breaks F1 and F3.
2. A recording replayed in the receiving frame, without its history, generates a new term that settles at that frame's ground. This breaks F2.
3. Two frames drawing on one evidence pool settle a new term neither held, with no third ground. This breaks F3.
4. A chain of frames with no closure difference between successive members closes the residual at the ground. This breaks F4.
5. An actor is parasitic relative to every residual in every habitat. This breaks F5.
6. Removing a transferred practice leaves the receiving habitat unchanged and the ground reachable, with no inherited actors. This breaks F6.
7. A single measure predicts both documentation quality and settlement, with both axes measured. This breaks F8.

If any fires, the discriminator is withdrawn or narrowed. Until one is run, no part of this may be labelled `grounded`.

## 5. Recommendation

1. Return a verdict per slot, never per partnership (F9, Fig 1 :19).
2. Ask for lineage before content. *"Convergent derivation and adopted import look identical in the contents"* (Fig 8 :51), so the record is the first thing to request.
3. Carry a method, not an artifact (Fig 4 :22 to :25). A head start that makes the next search cheaper is legitimate; a replay of one success is not.
4. Price independence explicitly (Fig 6 :24, Fig 8 :47). Two reviewers who read the same report are one witness.
5. Treat benefit sharing, consent language and documentation as filters for the budget, never witnesses for the verdict (Fig 9 :60).
6. Do not offer withdrawal as repair. It substitutes a habitat (Fig 11 :66).
7. Send the generator and the refuter, not the conclusion (Fig 4 :23). The first contact carries the residual only (Fig 10 :52 *"Bring a characterised residual, never the source."*).
