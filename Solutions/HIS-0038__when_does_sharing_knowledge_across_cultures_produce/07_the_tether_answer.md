# The Tether's answer: exchange versus extraction

**Mode: `specified` - not `grounded`.** This is a shape and a route, not a result. Simulated derivation checked against the figures (`05_what_shape_means.md` §7), not a ground reading. Verdicts are kept per domain and never averaged.

## 1. The residual it answers

A transfer of knowledge between cultural frames can end in two states that look the same in their contents. The residual is the gap between the transfer as described by its participants and the state of the receiving frame's reach and ground afterwards. It is a **union compound**, not one hard question: a clean lineage and a settled import are separate conditions, and a case failing both is a specific compound, not a harder case of either (Fig 9 :54 to :58). The residual is indexed per object slot (Fig 1 :19), so a partnership can be exchange for one practice and extraction for another.

## 2. The answer, one loop per domain

**D1, what crosses (`specified`, ungrounded, triangulated across Fig 4 and Fig 6).** Genuine exchange carries a method or a head start that can be reapplied in the receiving frame. Fig 4 :22 *"Only methods cross up. Only head starts come down."* Extraction carries a recording of one occasion: Fig 4 :25 *"A recording carried upward looks like knowledge and is a description of one occasion."* A recording replayed downward produces a system that repeats a past success and cannot produce a new one. Refuter: a recording replayed without its history generates a new term that settles at the receiving ground.

**D2, transfer structure (`specified`, ungrounded, triangulated across Fig 6 and Fig 8).** Import is *"the only operator that moves the wall"* (Fig 6 :14). It requires contact with a frame whose closure differs (Fig 6 :23). Two frames with the same atoms and different names add nothing (Fig 8 :13); an undirected union is mute (Fig 8 :21). Genuine exchange is the triangulation case in which *"Two frames together reach what neither reaches alone"* (Fig 8 :45), and the atom crosses back (Fig 8 :26). Extraction is the blend or mirror form in which vocabulary increases and the wall does not move. Refuter: a well-documented transfer that adds no atom yet settles a new term at the receiving ground.

**D3, verification (`general` structural, triangulated across Fig 2, Fig 7 and Fig 9).** *"Independence is one quantity read twice"* (Fig 8 :47). It decides both whether agreement means anything and whether the union has a surplus. Agreement among frames sharing one evidence pool is *"disqualifying for a verdict"* (Fig 2 :40) and *"efficient for a convention"* (Fig 2 :41). A chain of agreeing frames is one long room in which *"agreement compounds, evidence does not"* (Fig 7 :13). Refuter: two frames with measurably shared evidence settle a new term neither held, with no third ground.

**D4, habitat and valence (`specified`, ungrounded, single-source for the figure, corroborated by field cases).** *"The same actor is a benefactor to one residual and a malfactor to another, so mutualism and parasitism are relations rather than kinds"* (Fig 11 :50). Exchange and extraction are therefore properties of a transfer slot relative to a residual, not properties of an institution or a culture. The maintainer's power is asymmetric: *"Introduction is available; exclusion is not"* (Fig 11 :61). Withdrawal is not repair: *"Isolation is not removal of the habitat. It is substitution of one habitat for another"* (Fig 11 :66), with two silent failure modes, what you failed to reproduce and what you unintentionally introduced (Fig 11 :68 to :69). Refuter: an actor extractive relative to every residual in every habitat, or a removal that leaves the habitat unchanged and the ground reachable.

**The connecting claim (`general` structural, triangulated across Fig 8, Fig 10 and the essays).** The two states are indistinguishable in content. Fig 8 :51 *"Convergent derivation and adopted import look identical in the contents. Only the record of where each came from separates them"*. *How Intelligence Actually Works* puts the question's own word on it: *"One was taken; one was extracted. And nothing in the image records which."* Provenance shows a route was legitimate, never that it was right (Fig 10 :65). So the discriminator is the record plus the ground, in that order, with the ground having the last word.

**The pricing (`general` structural, single-source).** Fig 12 :125 *"A composition costs in two currencies, and they do not add."* Bits price the description, actions price finding out whether it holds (Fig 12 :126). Consent documents, benefit-sharing schedules and impact assessments are filters for the budget (Fig 9 :60), never witnesses for the verdict. Refuter: a single measure predicting both axes with both measured.

## 3. What it explicitly does not say

- Not that any actual historical case has been decided here. The folder offers a discriminator and its refuters, not a verdict on a culture, a state or an institution.
- Not that a person, community, university or corporation is extractive. Valence is relative to the residual (Fig 11 :50).
- Not that agreement, partnership longevity or the absence of complaint is evidence of exchange (Fig 2 :40, Fig 7 :13).
- Not that a signed consent form or a benefit-sharing clause settles the ground question (Fig 10 :49, Fig 9 :60).
- Not that withdrawing a practice restores the prior habitat (Fig 11 :66).
- Not that the framework can name a missing primitive or a real case as *the* answer. It returns a shape (Fig 9 :32).
- Not `grounded`. No measurement in this folder settles `left(R,φ)` for any transfer.

## 4. What would settle it - the ground

A source-discriminating instrument, not a further argument. *The Hard Problem is About Source*: an instrument that reads green *"cannot comment on the source"*; the spectrometer resolved it. Here the corresponding tests are:

1. **Lineage test.** Can the route by which the item entered the receiving library be reconstructed from a record, or is the artifact indiscernible from one derived independently? A trace may exist, as isotope ratios did for diamonds; if none exists, only the record can decide.
2. **Reach test.** Did the receiving closure expand, and did the new term settle against the receiving frame's own ground? A growing library is not a growing reach (`THE_FORMULA.md` step 5).
3. **Independence test.** Did the two frames draw on separate evidence, and does their agreement survive contact with a third ground? If not, the agreement is the pool talking.
4. **Habitat test.** After the transfer and after any withdrawal, can the goal still be reached, and which actors were inherited? Both failure modes are invisible until they act (Fig 11 :68 to :69).
5. **Standing refuters** from `01` §4: a lineage-free settling import; a replayed recording that generates a new term; a standing parasitic actor; a single measure predicting bits and actions.

## 5. The honest stop

**Unreached, not unreachable.** The field sources opened for this folder are real and checkable, but five of the six Wikipedia articles share one host and the four framework essays share one author with the figures, so agreement among them is one evidence pool talking (Fig 2 :40). No world source uses the Tether vocabulary, which is good for independence and bad for direct coverage. The decision about a real case is owed to a ground, not to this folder. *"Indiscernibility is not identity."* The record of the route is what keeps the stop checkable.

## 6. One-paragraph version for outreach

> The Tether does not say which cultures exchange well or which institutions are extractive. It says that a transfer can be described in two ways that look the same in their contents, and that the difference is carried by the record and by the ground. Genuine exchange carries a method that can be reapplied, between frames whose closures actually differ, so that a new term settles in the receiving frame and both sides gain reach. Extraction carries a recording of one occasion, arrives without a lineage, leans on agreement among frames sharing one evidence pool, or installs the imported item where the receiving ground used to be, so that it can no longer be refused. Because valence is a relation to a residual, the verdict attaches to one practice in one partnership, never to a whole culture. Consent documents and benefit sharing are filters for the budget, never witnesses for the verdict, and withdrawing a practice substitutes a habitat rather than restoring the old one. What this offers is the shape any answer must have, falsifiers written in advance, and the instruments that would close it. The case stays open until a ground settles it.
