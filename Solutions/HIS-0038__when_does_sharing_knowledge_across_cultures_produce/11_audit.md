## Round trip gap R_T

The Symbols table defines R_T as `gap(x, (T_E ∘ T_A)(x))`, what the coarser description cannot hold. Fig 4 :30 requires it to be measured rather than assumed. I ran the round trip on the condensation this folder actually uses.

1. **Sent up (x):** the verbatim figure set, chiefly Fig 4 :22 and :25, Fig 6 :14 and :23, Fig 8 :51, Fig 11 :50 and :66, Fig 1 :19.
2. **Abstraction (T_A):** the shorthand carried in `07` and `08`: exchange equals method plus lineage plus settlement plus independence; extraction equals recording plus absent lineage plus repointed ground; verdict per slot.
3. **Concretisation (T_E):** a real case, for example a conventional benefit-sharing agreement in which a community's classification system is recorded by an outside institution.

**R_T is nonzero.** Three things the shorthand holds do not survive the trip back down:

- **Which ground.** The shorthand says "settles at the receiving ground". In a real case there are at least two grounds (the institution's and the community's), and the figures give no rule for choosing between them. The seat map says an anchor listed as one of the parties has already become a frame (:22). This is the largest dropped constraint.
- **What counts as a method.** Fig 4 :23 says a method is a generator that can be reapplied. In a real case a practice can be a method for one residual and a recording for another (Fig 11 :50). The shorthand flattens that relation into a property of the item.
- **The action currency.** Fig 12 :126 says actions price finding out whether a composition holds. The shorthand mentions the bits side (consent, documentation) and names the action side but never measures it.

Conversely, two things survive cleanly: content alone cannot separate the two verdicts, and valence attaches to a slot rather than an identity. That is the residue worth sending. R_T was **not** measured numerically, because no ground reading exists; it is recorded qualitatively as the enumerated set of dropped constraints. Treating "the formula is the condensed version of the figures" as identity would set R_T to zero and would be the dead reckoning error Fig 4 :30 names.
