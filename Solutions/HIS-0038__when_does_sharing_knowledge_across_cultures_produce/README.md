## What is deliberately not here

- No personal emails of named individuals.
- No ranking of people by how likely they are to agree with the answer.
- No bulk-send plan.
- No claim that this folder settles the question. It is a simulated derivation checked for internal consistency against the figures, not a ground reading. The Recursive Self-Improvement Trap says LLM output is *"simulacra"* and that *"the truly testable output would need to be derived from the real architecture or working the formula yourself."* See `05_what_shape_means.md` §7.

Three figure-grounded reasons for the refusals:

1. **Agreement is not a verdict.** Fig 2 :35 *"Alignment is triangulation, not negotiation. A mirror cannot correct you."* Seat Map :283 *"A room does not upgrade the label."*
2. **Selection for receptiveness manufactures the disqualifying correlation.** Fig 2 :39 *"Correlation has valence."* Fig 2 :40 *"disqualifying for a verdict: agreement about the world is worthless when the agreeing frames share an evidence pool"* and Fig 2 :41 *"efficient for a convention: agreement about a shared meaning is the product, not a corruption of it."*
3. **A verifier must not hold the interiors.** Fig 10 :42 *"A verifier that can reconstruct the claim cannot verify it."*

Legally, unsolicited bulk email to scraped personal addresses breaches GDPR and CAN-SPAM style rules in many jurisdictions and gets the sending domain blacklisted. Public opt-in channels avoid that practical failure as well as the structural one.
