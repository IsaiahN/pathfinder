## 1. A shape is a constraint, not a filler

The canonical figures README states the limit: the framework returns *"what a missing thing would have to look like (a shape, not a name)."* Fig 9 :32: *"you get a kind of thing, never the answer itself."* The verbatim figure text adds that the system *"can say the answer is out of reach with the library as it stands"* but *"cannot say which primitive is missing, because naming it would mean already holding it."*

From *The Library of Babel* (fetched): *"you can cut your search short if the set of books returned is small and descriptive enough that it provides a general shape of the answer that satisfies your question."*

So a **shape** is the narrowed form of an answer you can state before you can name it. It constrains; it does not fill. For CMP-0011, "the remainder must not amplify across the crossing, and the conditions live in the agent-and-habitat" is a shape. "The critical coupling is J = 1.7" is a name, and it is not licensed by the figures.

## 2. How a shape is obtained

- *Library of Babel* (fetched): use the vocabulary you already have that is *closest* to the thing, look for recurring new terms that overlap or correlate with it, recurse. *"With each new term gained, you narrow the space and deepen your vocabulary."*
- Fig 6 :30: *"The description travels. Fails by being sharpened into terms only we can read. Cast it in effect terms."* Fig 9 :30-31: *"your words are borrowed too, so describe the effect rather than the cause."*
- *Decoding Tacit Knowledge* (exemplar-inherited): *"When contact isn't possible, the best you can send is a plain description of what you'd expect to see, because anyone can check that against the world."*
- *Library of Babel* on stopping: the answer is reached when *"the finish line will also be the starting place"*; the paradox is that by then you have *"composed the answer through the process of locating it."*

## 3. What licenses a name

Only two things, both outside the shape itself:

1. **The ground settles it.** `THE_FORMULA.md` step 5: *"the ground settles it. Verified only by what pays."* The README: the framework *"stops at the limit of what the available instruments can distinguish, and records the lineage so the stop is honest."*
2. **A provenanced import from a frame whose closure differs** (Fig 6 :23, Fig 8 :50-52), with a source gate and a shadow test.

*They're Scanning Books When They Should* sharpens this (exemplar-inherited): *"The real import is not a new term in the existing space. It is a new dimension of the space."* And: *"A minimum viable set of six terms is a human's careful guess at reality's compression axes; another sensorium's minimum set need not be those six."* Also: *"Declaring the primitives does not make them neutral. It makes the choice visible."*

The six are a visible guess, not a discovery of nature's own list. Binding "local interaction" onto a state/structure term is a visible choice and must be recorded as such.

## 4. Why "famous mechanism" is the specific forbidden error here

*The Hard Problem is About Source* (fetched) gives the mechanism. Metamerism: an instrument reads green and *"cannot comment on the source"*; the same reading can come from one green light, yellow plus blue, RGB, CMYK, or a scheme not yet discovered. *"If several paths create the same behavior, then behavior is a projection with lossy information."* And it was the **spectrometer**, not an argument, that resolved it: *"not by argument, but by the arrival of a better instrument."*

Applied here: a reading of "stable large-scale order" does not determine its source. The same macro order can come from a damped remainder that tracks an external anchor, or from agents mutually updating on each other (Fig 2's coherent drift). Naming "positive feedback" or "self-organized criticality" as *the* source of an observed order is the metamerism error: a source named from a reading where several sources could give the same reading. What is owed is a source-discriminating instrument, not a name.

*The Recursive Self-Improvement Trap* (fetched): *"the output is only as good as the validator. The agent, or the tool, or the frame, or the theory, cannot validate itself."* A list of agreeing experts is not a validator.

## 5. Q - answered: what licenses a particular critical condition?

**Nothing in the framework.** The framework returns a shape. A particular critical value is licensed only by the ground settling a measured term, or by a provenanced import with a shadow test. Until one of those, the value is the searcher's own contribution and must be recorded as such.

**Does naming a famous mechanism commit a forbidden error?** Yes, if it is presented as the framework's answer. The framework returns a shape: the remainder must be non-amplifying across the crossing, the conditions are agent-and-habitat, selection between variants against a non-updating ground, residual per slot, measured round-trip gap. Positive feedback, SOC, Kuramoto synchronization and Ising dimensionality are **world analogues that corroborate the shape from low-overlap fields** (see `09_watson_answer.md`). They are not derivations from the figures and are not universal names.

## 5b. Correction to my own §3 (see `06_isomers_and_order.md`)

I initially overstated the shape-not-a-name rule as a ban on naming any mechanism. The README's phrase is *"what a **missing** thing would have to look like (a shape, not a name)."* It governs a primitive not held in Γ. Naming a world analogue as a **corroborating frame** is not the same as inventing a seventh primitive or claiming the framework named a value. What remains wrong is presenting that analogue as a derivation, or as a universal critical number. Both the narrow claim and the correction are recorded so neither is quietly lost. ## 6. Two consequences for this question

**Prestige is not action.** From *The Recursive Self-Improvement Trap* (fetched): *"Prestige must never be allowed to validate an action directly... Prestige can fund an action. It can give an action a budget to be tried. But it cannot settle an action. Only the ground can settle it. The Ground Maintainer must be blind to prestige."* A campaign that gathers prestigious names to agree with the condition-shape is the named collapse, not corroboration. The legitimate ask is to give the claim a budget to be tried and let the ground, an instrument or an experiment, settle it.

**My own audit is a simulation, not a ground reading.** Same essay (fetched): *"LLMs can quickly simulate an approximate answer/output with the formula"* and *"the truly testable output would need to be derived from the real architecture or working the formula yourself."* So `01` is a simulated derivation checked for internal consistency against the figures. It is not a test of the question and must be labelled as such. It cannot settle anything it reports on.
