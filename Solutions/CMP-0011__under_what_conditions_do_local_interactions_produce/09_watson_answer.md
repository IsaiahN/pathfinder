## DOMAIN CENSUS

R by arity, symmetry and scale; one line per domain with actors / currency / anchor; partition or union; order by dependency.

- **R (the residual).** Two regimes of the same local interactions: macro residual trending down and staying down (order) versus persisting or growing (fluctuation). Arity: many-to-one, N local interactions composing into one or a few macro order parameters. Symmetry: local rules are typically exchange- and translation-symmetric, so an ordered state is a symmetry breaking of many spellings into one term. Scale: at least two, the local step and the macro level, with a membrane between them.
- **Domain 1, local interaction (the board).** Actors: agents, local rules, neighbours (the habitat). Currency: local state changes, signals, a gradient. Anchor: running the local rules and measuring the micro dynamics. Question type: partition, which compositions are legal.
- **Domain 2, cross-scale composition (the membrane).** Actors: local interactions, bonds, the membrane. Currency: what crosses up (method/order) and down (head start), and the round-trip gap R_T. Anchor: the macro order parameter measured across the crossing. Question type: partition, what may cross.
- **Domain 3, persistence and selection (the pattern).** Actors: competing macro patterns, order parameter, habitat conditions (coupling, noise, density, topology, dimensionality). Currency: stability versus fluctuation; variation inside patterns and selection between patterns. Anchor: what persists under the conditions, measured over time. Question type: selection between rooms.
- **Domain 4, description and verification (the observer).** Actors: the researcher, the pre-registered refuter. Currency: residual per slot, never averaged. Anchor: the ground, never agreement. Question type: reading below the break.
- **Union.** D2 composes and D3 persists; they overlap at the macro pattern. Their intersection is a compound with its own loop (Fig 9 :54: *"And when the split lands on a union rather than a partition, unbundle before you search again."*). The compound is unbundled, each loop searched, then recombined.
- **Order by dependency (Fig 3).** Local contact and legal composition first; name the macro slot and say what counts as order before the search; cross the membrane; select and persist; carry and verify. Fig 3 :20: *"The chain is ordered by dependency, not by importance."*

Strain test applied: the six-term substrate governs a composing system's kinds, not the complexity frame's word "local interaction". Binding one onto the other is provenance, not a derivation. Named in `01` §2, kept here, not smoothed.

## VERDICT (from the figures)

The condition-shape survives; the universal name does not. Stable large-scale order is the regime in which a cross-kind composition carries a non-amplifying remainder across the membrane, under habitat conditions that support variation inside competing patterns and selection between them against an anchor that does not move, with the residual read per slot and the round-trip gap measured. Fluctuation is the same alternation with an amplifying remainder and an open, non-closing loop. No universal critical value, single mechanism or agent-only explanation is licensed. The binding of generic local interactions onto the six-term substrate is a named strain and a provenance item, not a derivation. This is a `specified`, ungrounded, candidate result.

- **cost if wrong:** bounded and locatable. The packet states mode, four refuters and provenance before the conclusion, so an error is findable. The real cost is spending the independence of outside readers by sending a famous-mechanism list instead of a generator, which Fig 2 :42 prices: *"Buy independence in proportion to the cost of being wrong."* That cost is avoidable by leading with the shape claim and its refuter.
- **undoable:** yes. No primitive is withdrawn and no literature is overturned. A universal binding, if proposed by a world source, is added with provenance as a corroborating frame; a refutation narrows the shape. A sent claim can be superseded by a corrected packet.
- **reversed by:** a ground reading, not agreement. A per-slot residual measurement showing the macro slot trending down while the loop gain is demonstrably amplifying would break F1; a habitat intervention that moves the regime with local rules fixed would confirm the agent-and-habitat claim, while an agent-only change that moves it would break it; a source-discriminating instrument showing the order tracks no external anchor would break the grounded-order claim; a derivation of a universal critical value from the figures with no measurement would break the shape-not-a-name limit. Until one of these fires, the verdict stands as `candidate`.
