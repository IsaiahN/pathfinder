## What is deliberately not here

- No personal emails of named individuals.
- No ranking of people by how likely they are to agree.
- No bulk-send plan.

Three reasons, in the figures' own words:

1. **A room does not upgrade the label.** Fig 7 :13: *"agreement compounds, evidence does not."* Adding agreeable readers raises the vocabulary and does not add an anchor.
2. **Correlation disqualifies a verdict.** Fig 2 :40: *"agreement about the world is worthless when the agreeing frames share an evidence pool."* Selecting recipients for receptiveness manufactures the correlation that makes their agreement worthless.
3. **Asking people to trust before they check spends their independence.** Fig 10 :42: *"A verifier that can reconstruct the claim cannot verify it."*

Legally: scraping personal emails for unsolicited bulk outreach breaches GDPR and CAN-SPAM style rules in many jurisdictions. That is a practical failure on top of the structural one.
