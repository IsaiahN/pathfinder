## Round trip gap R_T

The Symbols table defines R_T as the gap between what was sent up and what came back down, `gap(x, (T_E ∘ T_A)(x))`, measuring what the coarser description cannot hold. Fig 4 :30 requires it to be measured, not assumed. I ran the round trip on the condensation the folder actually uses.

2. **Abstraction (T_A):** the shorthand used in `07` and `09`: order when the remainder is damped and the ground selects; fluctuation when it amplifies; conditions live in the habitat; read per slot; measure the gap.
3. **Concretisation (T_E):** a claimed universal list of critical parameter values, or a single named mechanism, for all self-organizing systems.

**R_T is nonzero.** Three things the shorthand holds do not survive the trip back down:

- the **critical values**: the shorthand holds no temperature, coupling or density number, and none is derivable without a ground reading;
- the **source discriminator**: the shorthand says an anchor that does not move is required, but does not say which instrument in a given system distinguishes grounded order from coherent drift;
- the **per-slot measurement protocol**: the shorthand says read per slot, but does not specify the slot grid or the residual estimator for a given system.

Conversely, one thing survives cleanly: a structure must sit between local states for a macro order to compose, and the regime depends on the gain on the remainder and on habitat conditions. That is the residue worth sending. R_T was **not** measured numerically, because no ground reading exists; it is recorded qualitatively as the enumerated set of dropped constraints. Treating "the formula is the condensed version of the metalaws" as identity would set R_T to zero and would be the dead-reckoning error Fig 4 :30 names.
