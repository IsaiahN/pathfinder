# The Tether's answer: conditions for stable large-scale order

**Mode: `specified` - not `grounded`.** This is a shape and a route, not a result. Simulated derivation checked against the figures (`05_what_shape_means.md` §6), not a ground reading.

## 1. The residual it answers

We observe many local interactions. Sometimes they settle into a stable large-scale order; sometimes they produce continual fluctuations. The residual is the question of what separates the regimes: does the macro-slot residual trend down and stay down, or does the remainder re-enter and amplify? It is a **union compound**, not one hard problem: "compose" and "persist" overlap at the macro pattern and have separate grounds (Fig 9 :54: *"And when the split lands on a union rather than a partition, unbundle before you search again."*).

## 2. The shape

1. **Order and fluctuation are two regimes of one cross-kind alternation.** Fig 13 :28: *"They alternate, and the alternation is where both the recursion and the randomness come from."* Fig 13 :31-32: *"a remainder compounding through a chain that amplifies is chaos, and the detail a crossing discards reappears above it as noise."* The discriminator is the gain on the remainder across the crossing, not a substance.
2. **The loop must close, and its sign decides the regime.** Fig 12 :121: *"cycle. The output re-enters as input. The sign of the loop decides whether it closes, and a chain cannot close by construction."* Positive feedback amplifies a fluctuation into order; negative feedback damps it. A catalyst, a part that survives its own use, lets a small local trace stabilise the loop (Fig 12 :119).
3. **The conditions live in agent-and-habitat, not in the agents alone.** Fig 11 :39: *"capability is a property of agent-and-habitat, never of the agent alone."* Fig 11 :40: *"An improvement that does not change contact changes nothing, however much it improves."* Coupling, noise, density, topology and dimensionality are the habitat conditions that decide the regime.
4. **Persistence needs variation inside and selection between against a non-updating ground.** Fig 7 :33: *"variation inside, selection between"*; Fig 7 :35: *"Selection between rooms is where the ground votes."* Fig 2 :33: *"The anchor must not update."* Mutual alignment with no anchor is coherent drift, not grounded order.
5. **The macro order is a derivative, and the crossing owes a measured round-trip gap.** Fig 4 :22: *"Only methods cross up. Only head starts come down."* Fig 4 :30: *"So take the round trip and measure the gap."* Treating the macro pattern as the sum of local rules sets R_T to zero and is dead reckoning.
6. **The residual must be read per slot, never averaged.** Fig 1 :22: *"Aggregating across slots is how a live signal disappears into an average."* A global order parameter can hide the one live slot that decides whether order holds.
7. **The framework returns a shape, not a critical number.** Fig 9 :32: *"you get a kind of thing, never the answer itself."* No universal temperature or coupling constant is derived.

## 3. What it explicitly does not say

- Not a universal critical value, and not a single mechanism that explains every self-organizing system. Named analogues (positive feedback, SOC, Kuramoto, Ising) are world corroboration from low-overlap fields, not framework derivations.
- Not that local rules alone determine the regime. The opposite is the claim: the conditions are agent-and-habitat.
- Not that agreement among researchers, or prestige, settles anything. Fig 7 :13: *"agreement compounds, evidence does not."*
- Not `grounded`. No measurement at the macro slot or of the round-trip gap was taken here.

## 4. What would settle it - the ground

A source-discriminating instrument, not a further argument. From *The Hard Problem is About Source*: an instrument that reads green *"cannot comment on the source"*; the spectrometer resolved it *"not by argument, but by the arrival of a better instrument."* Here the corresponding questions are:

- **Per-slot residual over time:** does the macro slot's residual trend down and stay down, or persist? Measured per slot (Fig 1 :19).
- **Amplification test:** does the remainder grow across the scale crossing, or is it damped? A measurement of the loop gain, not an argument about feedback (Fig 13 :31-32).
- **Source discrimination:** does the measured order track an external anchor that does not move, or is it agents updating on each other? (Fig 2 :33.)
- **Habitat intervention:** does changing the habitat conditions (coupling, density, dimensionality, topology) move the regime while the local rules are held fixed? (Fig 11 :40.) The Ising result is the world precedent.
- **Standing falsifiers from `01`:** a same-kind aggregation that yields stable order; an amplifying remainder that still settles; coherent drift that counts as grounded order; an agent-only change that moves the regime.

## 6. One-paragraph version for outreach

> The Tether does not give a universal critical temperature for self-organization, and it does not name a single mechanism. It says that order and fluctuation are two regimes of the same cross-kind alternation, distinguished by what happens to the remainder at each scale crossing: damped, the macro residual settles; amplified, the same alternation becomes chaos, and what the crossing discards comes back as noise. The loop must close and its sign decides the regime. The conditions are not in the agents alone but in the agent-and-habitat: coupling, noise, density, topology, dimensionality. Persistence needs variation inside competing patterns and selection between them against an anchor that does not move; mutual agreement without that anchor is coherent drift, not order. The residual must be read per slot, never averaged, and the round trip from local method to macro order carries a gap that must be measured. What it offers is the shape any answer must have, four falsifiers written in advance, and the instruments that would close it. The binding of that shape to a number stays open until the ground settles it.
