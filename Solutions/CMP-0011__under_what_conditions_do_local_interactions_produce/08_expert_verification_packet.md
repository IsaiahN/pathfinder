# Expert verification packet - the Tether on local interactions and large-scale order

**What this is:** a claim in a form a complexity researcher can check. Mode, derivation and refuters are stated before the conclusion, so a reader knows what to look for and what would settle it. One-to-one use only.

**Mode: `specified` - not `grounded`.** No universal critical value or measurement is claimed. This is a shape and a route, not a result.

## 1. The claim, in one paragraph

Order and fluctuation are two regimes of one cross-kind alternation, distinguished by the gain on the remainder at each scale crossing: damped, the macro residual trends down and order settles; amplified, the same alternation becomes chaos and the discarded detail reappears as noise. The feedback loop must close and its sign decides the regime. The deciding conditions are properties of the agent-and-habitat, not of the agents alone. Persistence needs variation inside competing patterns and selection between them against an anchor that does not move; mutual alignment without such an anchor is coherent drift, not grounded order. The residual must be read per slot, and the local-to-macro crossing owes a measured round-trip gap. The framework returns this shape, not a critical number.

## 2. Three checkable sub-claims

### C1 - framework-internal; checkable by reading Figures 13 and 12
- **Statement:** order and fluctuation are one alternation distinguished by the remainder's gain, and the loop sign decides whether order closes.
- **Source, verbatim:** Fig 13 :28 - *"They alternate, and the alternation is where both the recursion and the randomness come from."* Fig 13 :31-32 - *"a remainder compounding through a chain that amplifies is chaos, and the detail a crossing discards reappears above it as noise."* Fig 12 :121 - *"cycle. The output re-enters as input. The sign of the loop decides whether it closes, and a chain cannot close by construction."*
- **Refuter:** show a stable large-scale order from a pure same-kind aggregation with no structure in the slot and no re-entrant loop; or show the remainder amplifying while the macro residual still settles durably; or reverse the loop sign with no change of regime.

### C2 - complexity-science-facing; checkable against the literature
- **Statement:** the regime is a property of agent-and-habitat, and the same local rules can yield different regimes by arrangement alone. The framework's isomer definition (Fig 12 :117: *"Same atoms, different bond, different substance. The difference is invisible in the formula and decisive in the behaviour."*) applies to regimes: same local rule, different topology or dimensionality, different outcome.
- **What to check:** Is the Ising contrast faithful? Wikipedia's *Ising model* reports *"no phase transition in one dimension"* yet order *"in 2 dimensions or more"* under the same nearest-neighbour rule. Is the Kuramoto threshold faithful? Wikipedia's *Synchronization* reports an abrupt transition *"when the coupling strength exceeds a critical threshold."* Are these bindings provenanced as world analogues, not presented as framework derivations?
- **Refuter:** show that a fixed local rule gives the same regime under all topologies and dimensionalities; or show that changing only the agents with habitat contact unchanged moves the regime (breaks Fig 11 :40 - *"An improvement that does not change contact changes nothing, however much it improves."*).

### C3 - framework-internal plus a ground discriminator
- **Statement:** an apparent order that is only agents updating on each other is coherent drift, not grounded order; it needs an anchor that does not move, and the macro residual must be read per slot with a measured round-trip gap.
- **Source, verbatim:** Fig 2 :33 - *"The anchor must not update."* Fig 2 :35 - *"Alignment is triangulation, not negotiation. A mirror cannot correct you."* Fig 1 :22 - *"Aggregating across slots is how a live signal disappears into an average."* Fig 4 :30 - *"So take the round trip and measure the gap. Without it you are navigating by dead reckoning: confident about a position nobody checked."*
- **Refuter:** show a stable order that persists with no selection between competing patterns and no non-updating ground, where coherent drift counts as grounded order; or show that a global average of the macro residual is sufficient, with no live slot hidden.

## 3. What is explicitly not claimed

- Not a universal critical parameter value, and not a single mechanism for all self-organizing systems.
- Not that positive feedback, SOC, Kuramoto synchronization or the Ising model are framework derivations. They are world corroboration from low-overlap fields, provenanced as such.
- Not that local rules alone decide the regime. The opposite is claimed.
- Not that agreement or prestige settles the question. Fig 7 :13 - *"agreement compounds, evidence does not."*
- Not `grounded`. Nothing here was measured. The binding of generic local interactions onto the six-term substrate is a named strain, recorded in `01` §2.

## 5. What would settle it

A source-discriminating instrument, not a further argument. From *The Hard Problem is About Source*: an instrument that reads green *"cannot comment on the source"*; the spectrometer resolved it *"not by argument, but by the arrival of a better instrument."*

1. **Per-slot residual over time:** trending down and staying down versus persisting, measured per slot.
2. **Amplification test:** a measured loop gain across the scale crossing, damped versus amplifying.
3. **Source discrimination:** does the order track an external non-updating anchor, or only mutual updates among agents?
4. **Habitat intervention:** change coupling, density, dimensionality or topology with local rules fixed; does the regime move?
