## 3. Wider literature that bears on the mechanisms

These were observed in the fetched working-papers index or the fetched pages above. They are listed as further reading, **not** as sources read in depth this session, and no quote is taken from them.

- "How WTO Commitments Tame Uncertainty" (Jakubik and Piermartini, 2019). Direct published treatment of F2: what an added commitment does to uncertainty and hence to the reachable policy set. https://www.wto.org/english/res_e/reser_e/ersd201906_e.htm
- "Committing to grow: The full impact of WTO accessions" (Brotto, Jakubik, Piermartini, Silvy, 2024). Empirical work on what accession changes domestically. https://www.wto.org/english/res_e/reser_e/ersd202406_e.htm
- "Distance, Formal and Informal Institutions in International Trade" (Lanz, Lee, Stolzenburg, 2019). A bridge between institutional economics and trade, useful for a cross-field checker on F5. https://www.wto.org/english/res_e/reser_e/ersd201903_e.htm
- "A Quantitative Analysis of Trade Policy Cooperation Scenarios" (Bekkers, Kalachyhin, Humphreys, 2026). A modelling approach to the surplus in F2. https://www.wto.org/english/res_e/reser_e/ersd202606_e.htm
- "Welfare and cross-border spillover effects of industrial subsidies in general equilibrium" (Bekkers, Jhunjhunwala, 2026). A concrete subsidy slot for F1. https://www.wto.org/english/res_e/reser_e/ersd202604_e.htm

The eight framework essays are the philosophical grounding. Four were re-read this session and are quoted in `05_what_shape_means.md`: *The Recursive Self-Improvement Trap*, *The Library of Babel*, *The Hard Problem is About Source*, and *They're Scanning Books*. The other four are named in that file but no new quote is claimed from them here.

## 4. Distribution: offer, do not push

Fig 4 :22: "Only methods cross up. Only head starts come down." Sending the full framework before anyone has described a residual sends a recording, not a generator. Lead with the per-slot claim and the five refuters; offer the links after a verdict is recorded.

Fig 10 :42: "A verifier that can reconstruct the claim cannot verify it." A recipient who has absorbed the architecture before being asked for a verdict has joined the frame being checked.

Fig 9 :27 (with :28): "describe it before you go looking, or any frame you pick will seem to fit." Ask what the recipient would expect to see if the claim were true before giving them the article.

Fig 2 :40: "agreement about the world is worthless when the agreeing frames share an evidence pool." A send selected for people already inclined to agree produces a convention, not corroboration (Fig 7 :35: "A room's agreement is variation, not a verdict").

**Recommended order in a reply:** (1) the per-slot shape claim; (2) the five refuters and the question "what would you count as a failure?"; (3) record the verdict; (4) only then the figure links, with the note that the figures, not the summary, are the authority.

## 5. The two-stage offer

**Stage 1.** Residual and refuter only. No framework, no links, no request for agreement or trust.

**Stage 2, opt-in.** If curious, send the three framework links. Later agreement is never pooled as corroboration.

**Stage 3, opt-in.** Offer the formula as a method to reuse. Each user writes their own refuter and states their own mode. THE_FORMULA.md's "Direction" section is the reason: a frame cannot check the direction of its own question, so the sought-for shape must be made an ordinary term.
