## VERDICT (from the figures)

The unqualified question is refused as a bundled one. The shape survives: constrain is a fall in reach by addition of a refusing term; enable is a rise in reach by import of a primitive; valence is relative to the policy slot; capability belongs to government and habitat; a declared rule needs an enforcement ground. The compound is ordered and productive, so regimes with identical instruments can be isomers when the bonds differ. Which policy, which institution, and by how much is a `gap`, not a silence. This is a `general`/`specified`, ungrounded, candidate result. The strongest single point is methodological: the residual is per slot, so a global "sovereignty constrained" or "sovereignty preserved" verdict has already thrown away the signal.

- **cost if wrong:** bounded and locatable. The packet states mode, mechanisms and refuters before the conclusion, so an error is findable rather than invisible. The real cost is spending the independence of outside readers by sending a framework before a residual, which Fig 2 :42 prices ("Buy independence in proportion to the cost of being wrong."). That cost is avoidable by leading with the per-slot claim and its refuters.
- **undoable:** yes. The mechanisms are stated separately, so a failed mechanism can be withdrawn without disturbing the others or any primitive. No institution has to be retracted; only its valence claim for a slot. A sent claim can be superseded by the corrected packet.
- **reversed by:** a ground reading, not agreement. A final ruling plus compliance record showing reliance ending while the statute stayed would settle the constrain mechanism; a documented case of a policy becoming composable only after an external primitive arrived would settle the enable mechanism; a comparison of agency-only reform against contact-changing reform would settle the habitat claim. A figure re-census that admits a symmetric domestic/external bond would break F1, and a figure line predicting a magnitude or a permanent institutional valence would clear the `gap`. Until one of these fires, the verdict stands as `candidate`. Fig 7 :35: "A room's agreement is variation, not a verdict. Selection between rooms is where the ground votes."
