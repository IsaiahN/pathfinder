## 1. A shape is a constraint you can state before you can name the fill

The canonical figures README (read locally) says the framework returns "a shape, not a name" and that "Indiscernibility is not identity." Fig 9 :32: "you get a kind of thing, never the answer itself."

From *The Library of Babel*: "you can cut your search short if the set of books returned is small and descriptive enough that it provides a general shape of the answer that satisfies your question."

For INS-0089 the shape is therefore **not** "the WTO constrains sovereignty" or "trade enables growth." It is the narrower, checkable claim:

> constrain = reach falls because a refusing term was added;
> enable = reach rises because a primitive was imported;
> valence is relative to the policy slot;
> capability belongs to government and habitat, not government alone.

That is a set of constraints on any acceptable answer. It does not fill in a country, a treaty, or a magnitude. `SPACE(???)TIME` in the exemplar is the same kind of object: the slot is marked, not filled.

## 2. How the shape was obtained here

- *Library of Babel* on bootstrapping: "With each new term gained, you narrow the space and deepen your vocabulary."
- *Library of Babel* on steering: returned books must be "tested or ranged against the problem you want answered." In this solution that test is the five refuters in `01` §4, written before the conclusion.
- *Library of Babel* on stopping: "the finish line will also be the starting place." The run stopped at F8, where the figures give the shape and declare the specifics `gap`.
- Fig 9 :27 (with :28): "describe it before you go looking, or any frame you pick will seem to fit." The residual was described before WTO, IMF, ISDS or North was chosen.

The vocabulary was bootstrapped from the residual itself (reach, refusal, import, slot, habitat), not from a preferred institution. That order is the discipline the essay describes.

## 3. Bundling is a shape error, not a search problem

*The Library of Babel* asks: "Perhaps your problem was a bundle or conflation of several problems masquerading as one?" Fig 9 :55 names the same structure: "A bundled problem is usually a union of overlapping conditions, not a list of separate ones."

"Constrain or enable?" is that error. It asks for one verdict over a union of two operations (F1 and F2 in `01`) applied to different slots. The correct move is the one Fig 9 :54 requires: "when the split lands on a union rather than a partition, unbundle before you search again."

## 4. Why the shape cannot name the fill

*They're Scanning Books*: "A minimum viable set of six terms is a human's careful guess at reality's compression axes; another sensorium's minimum set need not be those six." And: "Declaring the primitives does not make them neutral. It makes the choice visible."

Applied here: the census of domains (domestic frame, institution frame, verification frame) is a declared choice, not nature's list. It makes the framing visible, not neutral. Naming "the WTO" as *the* actor that constrains would turn that visible choice into an invisible one. The shape permits naming an actor already in the habitat, but only as a provenanced binding, never as a derivation (see `06_isomers_and_order.md` Q1 and the correction below).

*They're Scanning Books* on imports: "The real import is not a new term in the existing space. It is a new dimension of the space." F2 is exactly this: the useful external contribution is a new policy dimension (access, a commitment device, a coordination axis), not merely another label for an instrument the domestic frame already holds.

## 5. Source discrimination, or why the ground must decide

*The Hard Problem is About Source* gives the mechanism. An instrument that reads green "cannot comment on the source"; several sources can produce the same reading. "If several paths create the same behavior, then behavior is a projection with lossy information." The example "was proven wrong not by argument, but by the arrival of a better instrument," and "An instrument that can't distinguish these cases demonstrates its own inadequacy."

For INS-0089: observing that a subsidy was withdrawn does not identify the source of the withdrawal. It could be a panel ruling (F1), a domestic election, a budget cut, or a market condition. A reading of "policy changed" is a projection with lossy information. What is owed is a discriminator (the ruling record, the damages award, the conditionality decision), not a name. This is why `07_the_tether_answer.md` asks for an instrument and not a narrative.

Fig 2 :29: "An anchor is whatever settles the question and does not change its answer because of what any frame thinks." A market price, a ruling, a disbursement decision: these are anchors. Agreement among commentators is not.

## 6. Prestige is not action, and this audit is a simulation

*The Recursive Self-Improvement Trap*: "Prestige can fund an action. It can give an action a budget to be tried. But it cannot settle an action. Only the ground can settle it. The Ground Maintainer must be blind to prestige."

A list of famous economists who agree that "institutions matter" is a convention, not corroboration. The ask in `02_outreach_email.md` gives the claim a budget to be tried; it does not ask anyone to endorse it.

Same essay: "the output is only as good as the validator. The agent, or the tool, or the frame, or the theory, cannot validate itself." And: "LLMs can quickly simulate an approximate answer/output with the formula" while "the truly testable output would need to be derived from the real architecture or working the formula yourself." "Metatheories cannot validate themselves."

So `01_figure_grounded_audit.md` and `11_audit.md` are simulated derivations checked for internal consistency against the figures. They are not tests of the claim and must be labelled as such. They cannot settle anything they report on.

## 7. Correction to my own reading of §4

I initially wrote that "shape, not a name" forbids naming an institution. That overstates the rule. The canonical phrase governs a **missing** primitive, one not held in the frame. An institution already in the habitat (a WTO panel, an IMF programme, an ISDS tribunal) is an actor that may be named as a provenanced binding. What remains forbidden is treating that binding as a figure-derived necessity, or claiming it moves the wall when it only occupies a slot already available. Both statements are recorded so neither is quietly lost.
