# Expert verification packet: the Tether on trade rules and domestic policy choice

**What this is:** a claim in a form a researcher on global economic institutions can check. Mode, derivation and refuters are stated before the conclusion, so a reader knows what to look for and what would settle it.

**Mode: `general` on the shape, `specified` on the mechanisms, not `grounded`.** No dataset, ruling series or market observation was gathered for this run.

## 1. The claim, in one paragraph

"Constrain or enable?" is a bundled question, not one question. Per policy slot, **constrain** is a fall in the reachable policy set caused by an added term (a ruling, a damages exposure, a condition) that removes a measure's standing without deleting it; **enable** is a rise in reach caused by an imported primitive (market access, finance, a commitment device, a coordination axis) from a frame whose closure differs. The valence of an institution is relative to the slot, so the same agreement can enable one policy and constrain another. Capability belongs to the government and its institutional habitat, not to the government alone. A declared rule is a declared meaning until an enforcement channel grounds it. The framework reports this shape, keeps the mechanisms separate, and declares `gap` on which policy, which institution, and by how much.

## 2. Three checkable sub-claims

### C1. Framework-internal plus trade-law facing; checkable against a ruling record

- **Statement:** an adverse external term removes the standing of a domestic instrument without erasing the instrument; the reach falls by addition.
- **Source, verbatim:** Fig 5 :55 "What it removes is not a term but a term's standing to be composed over." Fig 6 :43 "something was added rather than because anything was deleted."
- **What to check:** the WTO dispute gateway states: "A dispute arises when a member government believes another member government is violating an agreement or a commitment that it has made in the WTO." Find one final ruling with a documented compliance response. Did the measure stay on the books while its use became unreliable, or was it withdrawn in direct response to the ruling?
- **Refuter:** a domestic instrument whose reach falls with no term added from another frame; or reach unchanged after a final adverse ruling that was actually complied with. Either refutes C1.

### C2. Framework-internal plus political-economy facing; checkable against the trilemma literature

- **Statement:** enable is an import that expands the closure; valence is relative to the residual, so a single global verdict on an institution is a category error.
- **Source, verbatim:** Fig 6 :22 "IMPORT adds an atom, and the added atom is what expands the closure." Fig 11 :49 "An actor's valence is relative to the residual, not a property it carries."
- **What to check:** the political-trilemma source quotes Rodrik: "democracy, national sovereignty and global economic integration are mutually incompatible: we can combine any two of the three, but never have all three simultaneously and in full." The same page records Hirschman's rebuttal: diversification of trade "could bolster national sovereignty by making it harder for a state to be coerced by any other single state." These are not two conclusions to average. They are the same institution read against two different residuals. Does that reading preserve both without forcing one to be wrong?
- **Refuter:** a policy becoming reachable with no imported primitive; or the same institution showing identical valence across two slots that differ in the relevant respect. Either refutes C2.

### C3. Institutional-economics facing; checkable against institutional theory

- **Statement:** the domestic choice set is a property of government and habitat; a signed rule is a declared meaning until enforcement grounds it.
- **Source, verbatim:** Fig 11 :39 "capability is a property of agent-and-habitat, never of the agent alone." Fig 10 :49 "Declaring a meaning does not ground it."
- **What to check:** North (Nobel press release 1993): "Institutions provide the basic structure by which human beings throughout history have created order and attempted to reduce uncertainty in exchange. Together with the technology employed, they determine transaction and transformation costs and hence the profitability and feasibility of engaging in economic activity." Is the choice set better read as a property of the administration alone, or of the administration in its institutional habitat? Name the enforcement channel for any rule you count as a real constraint.
- **Refuter:** agency-only reform that changes the reachable policy set with no change in contact; or a declaratory commitment with no enforcement channel that reliably determines outcomes. Either refutes C3.

## 3. What is explicitly not claimed

- Not that any named institution is always constraining or always enabling.
- Not that sovereignty has been globally lost or preserved. That aggregates over slots.
- Not a magnitude, a country forecast, or a causal estimate.
- Not a mapping of the six-term physical substrate onto legislatures, panels or markets. That mapping was tried and refused as a strain in `06_isomers_and_order.md`.
- Not `grounded`. No measurement was taken; this is a simulated derivation.
- Not a theory of everything, and not the only framework. "A room's agreement is variation, not a verdict" (Fig 7 :35).

## 5. What would settle it

A ground reading, not more agreement. The discriminating instruments are: a final ruling plus compliance record (C1); a documented case of a policy becoming composable only after an external primitive arrived (C2); and a comparison of agency-only reform against contact-changing reform (C3). Observing only that "policy changed" is a lossy projection: several sources can produce the same reading. As Fig 2 :29 puts it, "An anchor is whatever settles the question and does not change its answer because of what any frame thinks."

## 6. What an expert can check in twenty minutes

1. Read `01` §0 and §1 (the residual and the census), then Fig 1 :19, Fig 6 :22 and :43, Fig 11 :39 and :49 (five short passages).
2. Pick one policy slot you know well. Say which added term removes its standing and which imported primitive, if any, expanded it. If neither can be named, the claim fails for that slot.
3. Name the enforcement channel for one rule you treat as binding. If you cannot, the rule is declared, not grounded (C3).
4. Check whether Rodrik and Hirschman can both be right on different slots. If one must be wrong, say which residual you held fixed.
5. Send the refutation. A failure is more useful than an endorsement.
