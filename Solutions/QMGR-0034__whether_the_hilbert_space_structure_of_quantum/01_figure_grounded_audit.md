## 0. The residual, stated in effect terms

`R = |Γ(b,a) − o′|` per slot `s` (Fig 1 :11, Fig 1 :19). The question puts one bundle into the slot: "the Hilbert space structure of quantum theory." The residual is not one signal. It is at least five, and the figures require them to be kept apart:

| slot | structural feature | what fails to resolve | candidate "more basic" frame |
|---|---|---|---|
| s1 | linearity and superposition | why vector addition rather than a nonlinear state space | collapse models modify the dynamics outside unitarity |
| s2 | inner product and Born rule | where the probability rule comes from | Gleason and Solèr type reconstruction theorems |
| s3 | dimension | why three spatial dimensions, why this Hilbert dimension | operational and categorical axiomatisations |
| s4 | tensor product and entanglement | why composition is a tensor product | entanglement-first and holography frames |
| s5 | unitarity and time evolution | exact validity at all mass scales | objective-collapse experiments |

Fig 1 :19: *"The residual is indexed by object slot, not aggregated."* Fig 1 :22: *"Aggregating across slots is how a live signal disappears into an average."* A single verdict on "the Hilbert space structure" would throw away exactly the per-slot signal that says where to look.

Effect statement of the two options:

- **Fundamental.** Hilbert space is in `atoms(Γ)` of the physics frame. It is a floor. What it leads to: you stop deriving there, but you cannot show that the stopping point is a floor rather than an unvisited region of the closure (Fig 8 :39, Fig 9 :44).
- **Emergent.** Hilbert space is a molecule composed from atoms held in a frame with a different closure and imported. What it leads to: an import is owed with a source gate (provenance) and a shadow test (it explains a gap), plus a discriminator that returns its distinct prediction (Fig 6 :23, Fig 8). Without those, "emergent" is a name where an import shape is all that is held.

A third option the binary suppresses: a structure that is neither atom nor imported atom, but a regularity a composition leaves behind. Fig 13 :124-125: *"Smoothness, symmetry, chaos and discreteness are each what a particular composition leaves behind, and none is a seventh member."* Hilbert space may be such a left-over. The binary has no slot for it.

## 3. Findings

### F1 - The binary is a union of at least five slots, not a partition (governing)

From the census and Fig 1 :19 and :22 above, and Fig 9 :54 and :57. Each slot admits a different status:

| slot | figure-governed status today | why |
|---|---|---|
| s1 linearity | `specified`, ungrounded | collapse models posit non-linear stochastic modifications; Fig 2 :45 requires the ground to decide |
| s2 inner product and Born rule | `specified`, triangulated for reachability | several distinct axiom sets derive the probability rule; reachability is not physical emergence |
| s3 dimension | `gap` | no figure line returns a dimension; the shape is "a parameter of the arrangement, not a seventh member" |
| s4 tensor product and entanglement | `specified`, single-source for the analogue | entanglement-to-geometry work shows a structure can be derivative of another frame |
| s5 unitarity | `gap` at the ground | no experiment to date has fired a deviation; unreached, not silence |

Refuter: show that one figure line governs the whole bundle as one term (for example, that Hilbert space is one of the six, or that one of the six is derived from the other five). Then F1 fails and a single verdict may be attempted.

### F2 - "Fundamental" is a level-relative claim, not a world verdict (decisive against the literal option A)

Fig 12 :103: *"Composition is recursive, and the level decides what is irreducible."* Fig 12 :104: *"A settled molecule becomes an atom for whatever composes over it. So irreducible is a fact about a level and never about the world"* (full line continues *"and there is no bottom"*). Fig 13 :41: *"Each is indivisible: no arrangement of the other five yields it."* Fig 13 :143-144: *"Indivisibility and minimality are claims and both are refutable: derive a member from the other five, or derive one of the established laws without a member the table says it needs."*

So "Hilbert space is fundamental" can only mean "primitive relative to the current physics closure, pending an import." Stated as a world verdict it claims a bottom, which Fig 12 rules out, and a certified limit, which Fig 8 rule 2 forbids: *"A frame cannot certify its own limit."*

The precedent is Fig 13 :43-44: *"A continuum was the seventh and was removed for failing exactly this: it is a spectrum without gaps, or smoothness over space and time. A generating set must not contain what its own members produce."* Something once treated as fundamental was reclassified as derivative. That is an existence proof that a floor can fall, not evidence that Hilbert space will fall.

Refuter: derive one of the six from the other five, or show a seventh member that cannot be produced by the six and their bonds. Either breaks the figure's own minimality and with it this finding.

### F3 - "Emergent" is an import claim and owes a source gate and a shadow test (decisive against the literal option B)

Fig 6 :23: *"No frame escapes its own closure. The bound moves only on contact with a frame whose closure differs."* Fig 6 :21: *"MINT composes inside the closure and can never add an atom."* Fig 6 :22: *"NOVEL means novel relative to atoms, not to the world. IMPORT adds an atom, and the added atom is what expands the closure."* Fig 6 :25: *"An import adds no atom to the world."*

A claimed substrate (qubits, entanglement, information, category, relational events, geometry) is an import into the physics closure. The figures price it: Fig 6 :24 *"A wrong atom costs more than starting from primitives."* Fig 8 gives the two gates, *"source gate: provenance"* and *"shadow test: explains a gap"* (Fig 8 :12-13), and the record rule: Fig 8 :51 *"Convergent derivation and adopted import look identical in the contents. Only the record of where each came from separates them"*.

World check: Hardy 2011 (arXiv quant-ph/0101012, opened 2026-11-23) says *"the usual formulation of quantum theory is based on rather obscure axioms (employing complex Hilbert spaces, Hermitean operators, and the trace rule for calculating probabilities)"* and derives it from five axioms. Solèr's theorem (opened 2026-11-23) forces the scalar field to be real, complex, or quaternionic from purely algebraic hypotheses. These are derivations inside a mathematical closure. They show reachability, not that nature imported those axioms.

Refuter: produce a substrate import with both gates and an instrument returning its distinct prediction. Then "emergent" is licensed for that slot and F3 is narrowed, not erased.

### F4 - Many reconstructions are one witness, not many (strains named)

Fig 7 :13: *"agreement compounds, evidence does not"*. Fig 8 :50: *"Two frames can hold the same atom by convergence, reaching it separately, which is corroboration at maximum independence."* But reconstructions of quantum theory (Hardy, Solèr and Gleason routes, Heunen and Kornell's categorical axioms) all aim at the same target formalism and cite substantial portions of the same mathematical literature. They are high-overlap. Fig 2 :31: *"Independence, between two frames, is how little they draw on the same evidence."* Fig 2 :40: *"agreement about the world is worthless when the agreeing frames share an evidence pool"*.

So the number of axiomatisations is a filter for the budget (which route is cheapest to try), never a witness for the verdict (which substrate nature uses). Fig 9 :60: *"Use filters for the budget and witnesses for the verdict. Never let a filter hand you a verdict."*

Strain kept visible: the reconstructions differ genuinely in their starting axioms, so they are not literally one document. I call them one witness because they share the target and much of the evidence pool, and I record the degree of overlap in `10_source_ledger.csv` rather than hiding it.

Refuter: two reconstructions with provably disjoint evidence pools and distinct novel predictions would be two witnesses, and would upgrade this finding.

### F5 - The emergence analogue runs in the reverse direction (world/figure strain)

Van Raamsdonk 2010 (arXiv:1005.3035, opened 2026-11-23) argues that *"the emergence of classically connected spacetimes is intimately related to the quantum entanglement of degrees of freedom in a non-perturbative description of quantum gravity."* This is a real case of a structure (classical spacetime connectivity) being derivative of another frame's currency (entanglement). It corroborates the figure's general claim that structures can be composed, and Fig 13 :124-125 that some things are what a composition leaves behind.

But it is the reverse of this question: it derives spacetime from Hilbert-space entanglement, not Hilbert space from something more basic. Using it as evidence that Hilbert space is emergent would be a category mistake. Fig 9 :27-28: *"describe it before you go looking, or any frame you pick will seem to fit"*. The residual here is Hilbert space, so a frame whose closure predicts Hilbert space is wanted, not one that presupposes it.

Refuter: an entanglement-first derivation that reconstructs the Hilbert space itself, with a novel prediction, would flip this from analogue to corroboration for option B.

### F6 - The ground has not fired, so no slot is `grounded`

Fig 2 :29: *"An anchor is whatever settles the question and does not change its answer because of what any frame thinks."* Fig 2 :45: *"Frames range against the ground. They do not negotiate with it."* Formula step 5: *"SETTLE - the ground settles it. Verified only by what pays. A gate is not the ground."*

The objective-collapse review reproduced on Wikipedia (opened 2026-11-23) states that modifications to the Schrödinger equation *"make predictions that differ from standard quantum mechanical predictions"* and that *"such deviations can potentially be detected in dedicated experiments, and efforts are increasing worldwide towards testing them."* That is exactly an owed instrument: an experiment that would distinguish a non-linear substrate from exact Hilbert linearity. No confirmed deviation is reported there. So s1 and s5 stay `candidate`, not `grounded`.

Fig 6 :27: *"Which is why some unreachability is depth and some is genuine absence, and from inside the frame the two look the same."* Fig 8 :39: *"So a search that finds nothing is never a proof of absence."*

Refuter: a confirmed, independently replicated deviation from linearity or unitarity in the mass regime where candidate substrates differ, or a demonstration that no such deviation is possible in principle. The first settles for emergence-for-that-slot; the second would be a real underivability result, not a budget failure.

### Seven-question recursive derivation (summary)

1. **Is the residual one thing?** No. Five slots, Fig 1 :19 and Fig 9 :54.
2. **Does a figure line return either option?** No line returns "fundamental" or "emergent" as a world verdict. Fig 12 :103-104 makes irreducibility level-relative; Fig 8 rule 2 forbids self-certification.
3. **Can reconstitutions settle it?** No. They show reachability inside a mathematical closure (Fig 6 :21); convergent derivation looks like adopted import (Fig 8 :51).
4. **Is there an import with provenance and shadow test?** No. Candidates are enumerated from the world, not composed (Fig 11 :48); without gates they are wrong atoms at a cost above primitives (Fig 6 :24).
5. **Has the ground fired?** No. Collapse-model deviations are predicted but unreported (Fig 2 :45).
6. **SEARCHED / INWARD / OUTWARD.** SEARCHED: the closure of current axiomatisations has been extensively visited and yields reachability, not nature's choice. INWARD: sharpen the instrument, from interferometric and non-interferometric collapse tests already returning something (Fig 6 :32-35). OUTWARD: describe the residual in effect terms and look for a frame whose closure predicts the per-slot shape. These two are opposed and are never read against each other (Fig 6 :31).
7. **Route.** The question's ordinary term "fundamental" is refused as a named collapse and re-grounded as level-relative irreducibility. A refusal precedes any mint (Formula step 2: a term held is false, what is owed is a refusal, not an explanation).
