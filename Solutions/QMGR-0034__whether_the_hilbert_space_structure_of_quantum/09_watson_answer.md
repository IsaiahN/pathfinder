## QUESTION

Decide, in effect terms, whether the Hilbert space structure of quantum theory belongs in atoms(Γ) of physics or is a molecule composed from more basic atoms imported from another frame. Option A: assert "fundamental". Option B: assert "emergent from X". What each leads to: A declares a certified floor no frame can certify; B declares an import with no provenance, no shadow test, and no discriminator. Neither literal option survives. A third structural type, a regularity a composition leaves behind, and a per-feature split of the question also remain. The honest output is a shape, a pre-registered refuter, and a named `gap`, not a verdict between A and B.

## VERDICT (from the figures)

Both literal options fail as stated. "Fundamental" is retained only as level-relative irreducibility: Hilbert space is a settled molecule of the physics closure, and no frame can certify a bottom. "Emergent" is retained only as a gated import: a substrate needs provenance, a shadow test and a discriminating instrument, none of which is supplied. The question is a union of five slots, not one binary, and the reconstructions are convergent routes to one target formalism, one witness rather than several. A third structural type (a left-over of composition) is live and is the one the figures most directly license. Which substrate (if any) is real is a `gap`, unreached at this budget, reported as a verdict with the instruments that would close it. Mode `specified` / `general`, ungrounded, candidate.

- **cost if wrong:** bounded by the per-slot split and the pre-registered refuter, so an error is locatable rather than invisible. The real cost is spent independence: presenting a substrate name as a result, or a reconstruction count as corroboration, converts outside checkers into members of the frame (Fig 2 :42, "Buy independence in proportion to the cost of being wrong."). That cost is avoidable by leading with the five slots and the refuter.
- **undoable:** yes. The bindings to substrates are records, not primitives; no atom of the physics closure and no member of the six-term set has to be withdrawn. A superseded packet can replace a sent one.
- **reversed by:** a ground reading, not agreement. A confirmed deviation from linearity or unitarity in the mass regime where candidate substrates differ would settle s1 and s5; a substrate derivation with a distinguishable novel prediction confirmed would license an import for s2 or s4; a derivation of one of the six from the other five, a seventh member, or an operator failing all seven bond tests that genuinely joins two operands would break the shape; an instrument fixing Hilbert-space dimension as derived would close s3. Until one of these fires, the verdict stands as `candidate` and the substrate question as `gap`.
