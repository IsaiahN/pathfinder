## 1. A shape is a constraint, not a filler

The canonical figures README states the limit: the framework returns *"what a missing thing would have to look like (a shape, not a name)."* Fig 9 :32: *"you get a kind of thing, never the answer itself."* Fig 13: *"It can say the answer is out of reach with the library as it stands... It cannot say which primitive is missing, because naming it would mean already holding it."*

From *The Library of Babel* (opened): *"you can cut your search short if the set of books returned is small and descriptive enough that it provides a general shape of the answer that satisfies your question."*

So a **shape** here is the narrowed form of an answer you can state before you can name it. For QMGR-0034 the shape is:

> A frame whose closure predicts, per structural feature, the linearity, the probability rule, the dimension, the composition rule, and the dynamics of the quantum formalism; whose atoms are not the Hilbert-space atoms of the current physics frame; whose import carries provenance and a shadow test; and for which an instrument exists that returns a prediction distinguishing it from the current formalism.

That is a shape. "Qubits", "entanglement", "information", "geometry", or "consciousness" are names. None is licensed by the figures without a shadow test and a discriminator.

## 2. How the shape is obtained

- *Library of Babel* (opened): use the vocabulary already closest to the thing, look for recurring new terms that overlap or correlate with it, recurse. *"With each new term gained, you narrow the space and deepen your vocabulary."*
- Fig 6 :30: *"The description travels. Fails by being sharpened into terms only we can read. Cast it in effect terms."* Fig 9 :30-31: *"your words are borrowed too, so describe the effect rather than the cause."*
- *Decoding Tacit Knowledge* (carried): *"When contact isn't possible, the best you can send is a plain description of what you'd expect to see, because anyone can check that against the world."*
- *Library of Babel* on stopping: the answer is reached when *"the finish line will also be the starting place"*, and the paradox is that by then you have *"composed the answer through the process of locating it."*

Applied here: start from the borrowed word "structure" and split it into the five features in `01` section 0. Each split narrows the search. That is the bootstrapping convergence the essay describes.

## 3. What licenses a name

Only two things, both outside the shape itself:

1. **The ground settles it.** Formula step 5: *"the ground settles it. Verified only by what pays. A gate is not the ground."* The README: the framework *"stops at the limit of what the available instruments can distinguish, and records the lineage so the stop is honest."*
2. **A provenanced import from a frame whose closure differs** (Fig 6 :23, Fig 8 :50-52), with a source gate and a shadow test.

*They're Scanning Books* (carried) sharpens this: *"The real import is not a new term in the existing space. It is a new dimension of the space."* And: *"A minimum viable set of six terms is a human's careful guess at reality's compression axes; another sensorium's minimum set need not be those six."* Also: *"Declaring the primitives does not make them neutral. It makes the choice visible."*

The six are a visible guess, not a discovery of nature's own list. Promoting a candidate substrate to the answer would turn a visible choice into an invisible one.

## 4. The record-level answer is the specific forbidden error

*The Hard Problem is About Source* (opened) gives the mechanism. Metamerism: an instrument reads green and *"cannot comment on the source"*; one green light, or yellow plus blue, or RGB, or CMYK, or a scheme not yet discovered can produce the same reading. *"If several paths create the same behavior, then behavior is a projection with lossy information."* And: *"An instrument that can't distinguish these cases demonstrates its own inadequacy."* It was the **spectrometer**, not an argument, that resolved it.

Here: quantum experiments read the formalism's predictions, and different substrates (axiom sets, entanglement-first reconstructions, collapse modifications, relational states) can give the same readings in the regimes already measured. Naming one of them as the source of the Hilbert formalism is the metamerism error. What is owed is a discriminator, not a name.

*The Recursive Self-Improvement Trap* (opened): *"The catch is that the output is only as good as the validator. The agent, or the tool, or the frame, or the theory, cannot validate itself."*

## 5. Q: what licenses a particular substrate for the Hilbert slot? ANSWERED

**Nothing in the framework.** The framework returns a shape. A particular substrate is licensed only by the ground settling a minted term, or by a provenanced import with a shadow test plus a discriminating instrument. Until one of those, the name is the searcher's own contribution and must be recorded as such.

### 5b. Correction to my own §3 and §4

I initially wrote this as if "shape, not a name" forbade naming any substrate at all. That overstates the rule. The README's phrase is *"what a **missing** thing would have to look like (a shape, not a name)."* The subject is a primitive not held in Γ. Qubits, entanglement, category and geometry are not missing primitives of this derivation; they are candidates from other frames. Naming one is a **binding**, not the invention of a seventh primitive. What remains wrong is presenting the binding as an import that moves the wall without provenance or a shadow test (Fig 6 :25 *"An import adds no atom to the world."*) and sourcing it from a projection the instrument cannot discriminate (the metamerism point). Both claims are now recorded so neither is quietly lost. This is the same correction the exemplar makes in its `05` section 5b.

## 6. Two consequences for this question

**Prestige is not action.** From *The Recursive Self-Improvement Trap* (opened): *"Prestige must never be allowed to validate an action directly... Prestige can fund an action. It can give an action a budget to be tried. But it cannot settle an action. Only the ground can settle it."* A list of famous physicists who agree that Hilbert space is emergent is the named collapse, not corroboration. The legitimate ask gives the shape claim a budget to be tried and lets an instrument settle it.

**My own audit is a simulation, not a ground reading.** Same essay (opened): *"LLMs can quickly simulate an approximate answer/output with the formula,"* but *"the truly testable output would need to be derived from the real architecture or working the formula yourself."* So `01` is a simulated derivation, checked for internal consistency against the figures. It is **not** a test of the claim and must be labelled as such. It cannot settle anything it reports on.

**Stop rule.** Fig 13 :134: *"Unreachable is a verdict here, not a silence"*. The substrate question is reported as `gap` / unreached at this budget, with the discriminator named, not as an silence and not as an invitation to invent a substrate. Fig 8 :39: *"So a search that finds nothing is never a proof of absence."*
