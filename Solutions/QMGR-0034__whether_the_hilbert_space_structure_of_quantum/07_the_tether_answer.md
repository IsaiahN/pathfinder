# The Tether's answer: fundamental or emergent Hilbert space?

**Mode: `specified` on the per-domain findings, `general` on the structural shape, `gap` on which substrate (if any) is real, NOT `grounded`.** Simulated derivation checked against the figures (`05_what_shape_means.md` section 6), not a ground reading.

## 1. The residual it answers

The question bundles five structural features of the quantum formalism into one slot: linearity and superposition, the inner product and Born rule, dimension, tensor product and entanglement, and unitarity. Fig 1 :19: *"The residual is indexed by object slot, not aggregated."* Fig 1 :22: *"Aggregating across slots is how a live signal disappears into an average."* The residual is the failure to say, for each feature, whether it is a primitive of the physics frame or a derivative of another frame. It persists because quantum predictions read the formalism without disclosing its source (the metamerism point in *The Hard Problem is About Source*).

## 2. The shape, per domain

1. **The literal binary fails at both ends.** "Fundamental" claims a certified floor, which is a completeness claim no frame can make (Fig 8 rule 2: *"A frame cannot certify its own limit."*). "Emergent" claims an import, which needs a source gate, a shadow test, and a discriminator (Fig 6 :23, Fig 8 :12-13). Neither is available. Both literal options are refused as stated, and each is retained only in a narrowed form.
2. **"Fundamental" survives only as level-relative irreducibility.** Fig 12 :103: *"Composition is recursive, and the level decides what is irreducible."* Fig 12 :104: *"A settled molecule becomes an atom for whatever composes over it. So irreducible is a fact about a level and never about the world"*. Hilbert space is a settled atom at the level of current physics; calling it the world's floor adds a claim the figures forbid.
3. **"Emergent" survives only as a gated import.** Fig 6 :25: *"An import adds no atom to the world."* A candidate substrate becomes a licensed atom of our frame only with provenance, a shadow test, and an instrument returning its distinct prediction. Until then it is a binding presented as a result, which the figures refuse.
4. **The reconstructions show reachability, not nature's choice.** Hardy, Solèr and the categorical axioms are convergent routes to the same substance: Fig 13 :121 *"Different routes can arrive at the same substance."* Fig 8 :51 *"Convergent derivation and adopted import look identical in the contents. Only the record of where each came from separates them"*. Fig 7 :13 *"agreement compounds, evidence does not"*. One witness, not five.
5. **A third structural type is live.** Fig 13 :124-125: *"Smoothness, symmetry, chaos and discreteness are each what a particular composition leaves behind, and none is a seventh member."* Hilbert space may be a left-over of a composition rather than either an atom or an imported atom. The binary has no slot for this.
6. **Which substrate is real is a `gap`.** Fig 9 :32 *"you get a kind of thing, never the answer itself"*; Fig 13 :134 *"Unreachable is a verdict here, not a silence"*. This is reached-not-unreachable at this budget: the instrument owed is a deviation test for linearity/unitarity and a discriminator among substrates that currently give the same readings.

## 3. What it explicitly does not say

- Not that Hilbert space is definitely emergent from qubits, entanglement, information, category theory, or relational events. Those are bindings without a shadow test.
- Not that one axiom set is the true one. Reachability from axioms is not a measurement.
- Not that quantum theory is wrong, or that unitarity has been observed to fail. No such result is in the sources read.
- Not a derivation of one theory from another. The entanglement-to-geometry result runs in the reverse direction and is an analogue only.
- Not grounded. No instrument result settles any slot.
- Not a theory of everything. There is no last room; the question stays open at the instrument limit.

## 4. What would settle it - the ground

An instrument, not a further argument. From *The Hard Problem is About Source*: a color instrument *"cannot comment on the source"*; it was the spectrometer that resolved the ambiguity. Here:

- **Deviation test (s1, s5):** does a non-linear, stochastic modification of the Schrödinger dynamics appear in macroscopic-superposition or precision-interferometry experiments in the mass regime where collapse models differ from exact Hilbert linearity? The objective-collapse review states such deviations *"can potentially be detected in dedicated experiments"*. None is confirmed in the page read.
- **Source-discriminating test (s2, s4):** a prediction of the Born rule or tensor composition from a substrate that differs from standard quantum theory in some accessible regime, so the source can be read off rather than inferred from the same old predictions.
- **Dimension test (s3):** any observation that fixes Hilbert-space dimension as a derived parameter rather than an input. No figure line governs a number, so this is a `gap`, reported rather than filled.
- **Standing falsifiers from `01`:** one figure line shown to govern the whole bundle as one term; a gated import with a firing instrument; linearity and unitarity shown exact with a unique Born-rule derivation, collapsing the five slots; a seventh member of the minimum set.

## 5. The honest stop

**Unreached, not unreachable in principle.** Fig 6 :27: *"Which is why some unreachability is depth and some is genuine absence, and from inside the frame the two look the same."* Fig 8 :39: *"So a search that finds nothing is never a proof of absence."* The six (or here, the physics closure's atoms and bonds) are a visible guess at compression axes; declaring them makes the choice visible, not neutral. The answer stops at the limit of available instruments and records the lineage so the stop is checkable. Indiscernibility is not identity.

## 6. One-paragraph version for outreach

> The Tether does not decide that Hilbert space is fundamental, and it does not decide that it emerges from qubits, entanglement, or geometry. It says the question is bundled: linearity, the Born rule, dimension, tensor composition and unitarity can have different answers, so one verdict would throw away the signal. "Fundamental" can only mean irreducible at a level, never a certified floor; "emergent" is an import claim that needs provenance, a shadow test and a discriminating instrument, none of which is currently supplied. The many axiomatisations of quantum theory show the formalism is reachable from several starting points, not which one nature uses; the figures call that convergent derivation, one witness rather than five. A third option survives that the binary has no slot for: Hilbert space may be a regularity a composition leaves behind. The substrate stays a named `gap` until an instrument returns something that distinguishes the candidates. What the framework offers is the shape any answer must have, refuters written in advance, and the instruments that would close it.
