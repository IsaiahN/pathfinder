## What is decided, in effect terms

The question asks whether the complex Hilbert space formalism (states as vectors, observables as self-adjoint operators, inner product and Born rule, tensor product composition, unitarity) belongs in `atoms(Γ)` of physics, or is a molecule composed from more basic atoms imported from another frame. Those are not two answers to one question. The phrase "Hilbert space structure" bundles several structural features whose answers can differ. Fig 1 :19: *"The residual is indexed by object slot, not aggregated."* Fig 1 :22: *"Aggregating across slots is how a live signal disappears into an average."* The residual is therefore kept per feature (linearity, inner product and Born rule, dimension, tensor product and entanglement, unitarity), and verdicts are never averaged into one yes or no.

What each option leads to: asserting "fundamental" declares a floor that cannot be composed further, which is a completeness claim no frame can certify (Fig 8 :34, Fig 9 :44). Asserting "emergent" obliges a named import with a source gate, a shadow test, and a discriminator at the ground (Fig 6 :23, Fig 8). Neither is available here, so the honest output is a shape plus a refuter and an owed instrument, not a verdict.
