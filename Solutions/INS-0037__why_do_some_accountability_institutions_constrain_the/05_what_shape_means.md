# What "shape" means for this question

Sources opened this session: the canonical local files listed below, plus two of the eight essays (Library of Babel, 2026-10-02; The Recursive Self-Improvement Trap, 2026-11-19, both by Isaiah N, both fetched 2026-11-23). The other six essays are the philosophical grounding of the framework; their short quotes below are taken from the completed exemplar's `05_what_shape_means.md`, which this session read, and are attributed that way rather than claimed as freshly opened. All figure quotes come from `FRAMEWORK/reference/03-FIGURES_TEXT.md`.

Canonical local files read: `FRAMEWORK/reference/FIGURES_README_canonical.md`, `FRAMEWORK/reference/60_FIGURES_AS_METALAWS.md`, `FRAMEWORK/figures/THE_FORMULA.md`, `FRAMEWORK/figures/THE_SEAT_MAP_Field_Guide_amended.md`.

## 1. A shape is a constraint, not a filler

The canonical rule: the framework returns "what a missing thing would have to look like (a shape, not a name)." Fig 9 :32: "you get a kind of thing, never the answer itself." Fig 13 :133: "what it returns is a shape rather than a name."

*The Library of Babel* (opened this session): "you can cut your search short if the set of books returned is small and descriptive enough that it provides a general shape of the answer that satisfies your question."

So for INS-0037 a shape is the configuration any answer must have before you can name a country, a reform or a causal coefficient. The shape here is: a checking seat with a reachable ground, outside the slice it grades, with no gain from the reading, and unauthored in its membership. That constrains. It does not fill. "Which country?" and "which reform?" are names, and they are not licensed by the figures.

## 2. How a shape is obtained

- *Library of Babel*: use the vocabulary you already have that is closest to the thing, look for recurring terms that overlap or correlate with it, recurse. "With each new term gained, you are able to narrow the space by definition and deepen your vocabulary."
- Fig 6 :30: "The description travels. Fails by being sharpened into terms only we can read. Cast it in effect terms." Fig 9 :30-31: "your words are borrowed too, so describe the effect / rather than the cause." Hence the residual in `01` §0 is stated as a targeting distribution and a prediction failure, not as "weak institutions" or "lack of political will".
- Fig 9 :27-28: "describe it before you go looking, or any frame you pick will seem to fit." The seat vocabulary was derived before any country was named. That ordering is the point.
- *Library of Babel* on stopping: "you will know that you are done because the finish line will also be the starting place". And: "Perhaps your problem was a bundle or conflation of several problems masquerading as one?" That is exactly the union finding F5.

## 3. What licenses a name

Only two things, both outside the shape itself:

1. **The ground settles it.** `THE_FORMULA.md`: "the ground settles it. Verified only by what pays. A gate is not the ground." The canonical README: the framework "stops at the limit of what the available instruments can distinguish, and records the lineage so the stop is honest."
2. **A provenanced import from a frame whose closure differs** (Fig 6 :23, Fig 8 :50-52), with a source gate and a shadow test.

For this question that means: an enforcement-distribution study with independent misconduct data could name which configuration constrains incumbents; a natural experiment on selection and removal rules could name the causal weight. Neither exists in this package. So no name is given.

## 4. Why the institution's own metrics are not the ground

*The Hard Problem is About Source* (as recorded in the exemplar's `05`): an instrument reads green and "cannot comment on the source"; "If several paths create the same behavior, then behavior is a projection with lossy information"; "An instrument that can't distinguish these cases demonstrates its own inadequacy." It was the spectrometer, not an argument, that resolved it.

Here, an audit report count, an arrest count or a conviction count is a reading of activity. It cannot comment on the source: the same count can be produced by impartial enforcement, by selective prosecution of opponents, or by a campaign against insiders. Fig 1 :27: "They are frame-internal. A frame cannot score itself with a quantity it also produces." Nyuon (2024), opened this session, makes the same point from the field: programmes "measure activity instead of impartiality" and should "monitor enforcement distribution and case outcomes, not only capacity metrics."

What is owed is a discriminator, not a name.

## 5. Answer to the shape question for INS-0037

**What must any answer to "why do some constrain the coalition and others police opponents" look like?** It must be a statement about the position and incentives of a checking seat, not about the institution's label, budget size or declared mandate. It must treat the two observed outcomes as a union compound. It must keep the per-slot distribution of enforcement as the admissible signal. And it must stop short of naming a country or a single reform until a ground settles it.

**Does this package commit the forbidden error?** No country is named as the answer, no reform is claimed as sufficient, and no causal coefficient is asserted. Where world sources corroborate, they are recorded as secondary and their overlap is declared. The only names used are atoms the figures already supply (ground, anchor, seat, room, habitat, residual).

## 6. Prestige is not action, and this derivation is a simulation

*The Recursive Self-Improvement Trap* (opened this session): "Prestige can fund an action. It can give an action a budget to be tried. But it cannot settle an action. Only the ground can settle it. The Ground Maintainer must be blind to prestige." A list of prestigious institutes asked to endorse the shape is the named collapse, not corroboration. The legitimate ask is a budget to try the refuter.

Same essay: "the output is only as good as the validator. The agent, or the tool, or the frame, or the theory, cannot validate itself"; "the truly testable output would need to be derived from the real architecture or working the formula yourself." So `01_figure_grounded_audit.md` is a simulated derivation, checked for internal consistency against the figures. It is **not** a test of the claim and must be labelled as such. It cannot settle anything it reports on.

## 7. Correction carried forward

The first draft of this file treated "shape, not a name" as if it forbade naming any of the framework's own atoms. That overstates the rule. The canonical phrase governs what a *missing* primitive would look like; the seat atoms are already held in the framework. Naming them is binding, not minting. The rule that actually bites here is the second one: the framework returns a configuration, not a country or a single lever. Both readings are recorded so neither is quietly lost. Triangulated across the canonical README, Fig 9 :32 and Fig 13 :133.
