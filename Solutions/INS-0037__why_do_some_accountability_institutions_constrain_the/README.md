## What is deliberately not here

- No personal emails of named individuals.
- No ranking of people by how likely they are to agree with the shape claim.
- No bulk-send plan.

Three reasons, in the framework's own words:

1. **A room does not upgrade the label.** *"A room does not upgrade the label. A room reaching a conclusion without the ground is agreement, and agreement among frames is a weighted average of influence. Adding people raises the vocabulary and does not add an anchor."* (Seat Map :283)
2. **Correlation disqualifies a verdict.** *"High correlation is disqualifying for a verdict and efficient for a convention."* (Seat Map :163) Selecting recipients for receptiveness manufactures the correlation that makes their agreement worthless.
3. **A verifier must not hold the interior.** *"A verifier that can reconstruct the claim cannot verify it."* (Fig 10 :42) The outside frame *"must not hold the interiors, the records, the anchor, any stake, or any authority"* (Seat Map :139).

Legally, scraping personal emails for unsolicited bulk outreach breaches GDPR and CAN-SPAM style rules in many jurisdictions. Public opt-in channels avoid that failure as well as the structural one.
