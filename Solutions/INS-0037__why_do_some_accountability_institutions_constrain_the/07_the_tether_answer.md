# The Tether's answer: why accountability bodies differ in whom they constrain

**Mode: `specified`, not `grounded`.** This is a shape and a route, not a result. Simulated derivation checked against the figures (`05_what_shape_means.md` §6), not a ground reading.

## 1. The residual it answers

Two bodies with the same formal mandate differ in targeting: one sanctions the coalition that appointed and funds it; the other mainly sanctions that coalition's opponents. The prediction that the mandate determines targeting fails. The residual is per institution and per target slot, not an average: Fig 1 :19: "The residual is indexed by object slot, not aggregated." Fig 1 :22: "Aggregating across slots is how a live signal disappears into an average."

The question is a union compound (Fig 9 :55: "A bundled problem is usually a union of overlapping conditions, not a list of separate ones"), so it needs two loops and a name for their intersection, not one grand cause.

## 2. The shape

1. **The institution is a checking seat, and the ground must not move when the coalition pushes.** Fig 2 :29: "An anchor is whatever settles the question and does not change its answer because of what any frame thinks." Fig 2 :33: "The anchor must not update." When a verdict moves with coalition pressure, the anchor has become another frame and the drift is coherent, which is why observers call it a functioning institution.

2. **Position is decisive.** Fig 10 :86: "Outside the slice being graded. Whoever is measured by a reading cannot own it." An auditor removable by the agency it audits, or a commission staffed from the coalition it must investigate, owns the reading of the slice it is graded on. It cannot range against it. Independence is a fact about position, not a virtue of occupants (Seat Map :304).

3. **The disqualifying stake is a stake in the reading, not in the outcome.** Fig 10 :59: "an interest in the outcome is unavoidable, an interest in the reading is disqualifying." A body may care about stability and still be a checker. It becomes a weapon when it gains from which reading the ground returns: rivals prosecuted, insiders spared. Fig 9 :51: "evidence bearing only on its maker is a stake-inducing channel, and the verifier is denied it." Loyalty is not evidence about misconduct.

4. **The room must be unauthored.** Fig 10 :73-74: "A candidate set chosen by a participant is an authored room however well the selection is reasoned, so the pool must be unauthored first and examined second." Melton and Ginsburg (2014), a genuinely lower-overlap world source, find that selection and removal rules are the only formal protections that work, and only conjunctively. Mundane as it sounds, who appoints and who can fire the checker is the sharpest observable handle on the shape.

5. **The two outcomes are isomers, and the compound is categorically different.** Over the same formal atom set, the difference is the bond joining checker to target (Fig 12 :117: "isomer. Same atoms, different bond, different substance"). Insider impunity is a channel-starvation loop; opponent targeting is an authored-room and stake loop; their intersection is case choreography. Fig 9 :57: "a case in two conditions is not a harder case of either."

6. **A declared mandate is a convention, not a settlement.** Fig 10 :49: "Declaring a meaning does not ground it." Fig 10 :16-17: "the convention does not fix the bug", "it makes the bug statable." Rewriting a law to say "independent" makes the defect visible; it does not move the checker outside the slice.

7. **Independence decays and must be refreshed by a different closure.** Fig 10 :81: "no amount of use makes them less alike and replacement is the only refresh." Fig 7 :36: "A chain of mirrors is one long room. The next room must differ from the last, and match the residual it surfaced." Adding another copy of the same oversight body is motion, not progress.

## 3. What it explicitly does not say

- Not a country name, and not a single reform that works everywhere. Fig 9 :32: "you get a kind of thing, never the answer itself."
- Not that formal rules are irrelevant. Selection and removal rules matter, conjunctively with budget and information access.
- Not that this institution is "captured" in a moral sense. Valence is relative to the residual (Fig 11 :49), and a seat with no stake at all does not exist. The question is structural.
- Not that more watchdogs help. Seat Map :283: "Adding people raises the vocabulary and does not add an anchor."
- Not `grounded`. No enforcement-distribution study or natural experiment was run here.

## 4. What would settle it: the ground

A discriminator, not a further argument. *The Hard Problem is About Source* records that a spectrometer, not a debate, resolved metamerism; an instrument that reads green "cannot comment on the source."

1. **Distribution test.** Compare the targeting distribution of each body against an independent measure of misconduct, slot by slot (insiders and opponents separately). If targeting tracks the ground and not coalition connection, the shape claim fails.
2. **Selection test.** A natural experiment on appointment and removal rules, with budgets held comparable. If selection and removal do not change targeting, F4 fails.
3. **Channel test.** Measure whether information about insider misconduct reaches the checker at all. A dead channel reports as a low case count, not as a wrong verdict (Fig 10 :93: "the ground does not decay, the channel does").
4. **Union test.** Check whether a lever that clears insider impunity leaves opponent targeting standing. If one lever clears both with no residue, the union reading fails.

Standing refuters from `01` §4: the channel, position, union, formal-rule and stake tests. Until one is run, the verdict is `candidate`.

## 5. The honest stop

**Unreached, not unreachable.** Fig 13 :133: "what it returns is a shape rather than a name." Fig 13 :134: "Unreachable is a verdict here, not a silence." The six-term substrate and the seat rules are a human's careful compression of what any checking arrangement needs; declaring them makes the choice visible, not neutral. The stop is recorded with its lineage so a later reader can locate the error rather than only feel it. Agreement from a list of agreeable readers is not such a record.

## 6. One-paragraph version for outreach

> The Tether does not name a country or a single reform. It says that an accountability body constrains the ruling coalition when it occupies a checking seat outside the slice it grades, ranges against misconduct and law that do not move when the coalition pushes, gains nothing from which reading the ground returns, and draws its occupants from a pool the party it checks did not author. It mainly polices opponents when those conditions are inverted: the checker sits inside the slice, shares the coalition's evidence, gains from a particular reading, and is staffed from an authored room. The two outcomes are isomers over the same formal atoms, differing in the bond that joins checker to target; insider impunity and opponent targeting form a union compound, not one problem, so their intersection must be named separately. A declared mandate is a convention, not a settlement, and a stack of lookalike watchdogs is a mirror chain, not a check. What is offered is the shape any answer must have, five refuter tests written in advance, and the instruments that would close it. The ground decides.
