## DOMAIN CENSUS

R by arity, symmetry and scale, then one line per domain with actors, currency and anchor; partition or union; order by dependency.

- **R (the residual).** Arity: two target slots per institution (coalition insiders, opponents) plus the ground (actual misconduct). Symmetry: asymmetric, because the coalition controls appointment, budget and removal and the opponent does not; valence is relative to this residual (Fig 11 :49: "An actor's valence is relative to the residual, not a property it carries"). Scale: meso, the institution as a checking seat, with micro incentives and macro settlement as adjacent orders. Fig 1 :19: "The residual is indexed by object slot, not aggregated." Question type: union compound, not one hard problem.
- **Domain 1, the checking seat.** Actors: checker occupants, the coalition and its agencies, the ground. Currency: verdicts (open, prosecute, sanction, drop, delay, report). Anchor: the facts of misconduct and the law, which must not move when the coalition pushes. Question type: partition, which configurations keep the checker outside the slice it grades.
- **Domain 2, the constitution of the checker.** Actors: appointing authority, selector, candidate pool, occupants. Currency: selection rules, removal rules, funding, information access. Anchor: an unauthored pool, for example "a set assembled before the question existed" (Fig 10 :78). Question type: partition, authored room versus unauthored pool.
- **Domain 3, the habitat.** Actors: coalition, opponents, donors, other oversight bodies, civil society. Currency: power, resources, standing. Anchor: none of its own; it updates and supplies actors to D1 and D2. "The habitat is not the ground." (Fig 11 :73) Question type: enumeration, read off the world, never composed.
- **Order by dependency (Fig 3).** Ground reachable, then checker outside the slice, then unauthored membership with protected removal, then habitat enumeration. Fig 3 :20: "The chain is ordered by dependency, not by importance."

## VERDICT (from the figures)

The shape survives; names and coefficients do not. An accountability body constrains the ruling coalition when its checking seat is outside the slice it grades, its ground does not move when the coalition pushes, it gains nothing from which reading the ground returns, and its occupants come from a pool the party it checks did not author. It mainly polices opponents when those conditions invert. Insider impunity and opponent targeting form a union compound with a third, categorically different region (case choreography). The bodies are isomers over the same formal atoms, differing in the bond that joins checker to target. A declared mandate is a convention that makes the defect statable, not a settlement. Independence is positional and decays with use. The causal weight of each condition, and which named reform works where, are `gap` after re-census. This is a `specified`, ungrounded result; no part of it is `grounded`.

- **cost if wrong:** bounded and locatable if the shape and its refuters go first. The record states mode, carries five pre-registered tests and keeps the lineage, so an error is findable rather than invisible. The real cost is spending outside checkers' independence by sending a theory before a residual, which Fig 2 :42 prices: "Buy independence in proportion to the cost of being wrong." That cost is avoidable.
- **undoable:** yes. The seat conditions can be changed without rewriting the mandate: selection, removal, budget and information access are separately adjustable. But a checker whose independence was fixed at origin cannot be repaired by more use; Seat Map :237 says such frames "can only be rotated". The correction is replacement plus provenance, not a retraction of the shape.
- **reversed by:** a ground reading, not agreement. An enforcement-distribution study with independent misconduct measures could break the per-slot claim; a natural experiment on selection and removal could break the authored-room claim; a case showing one lever clears the whole union could break the compound claim; a checker whose independence rises with use against the same target could break the decay claim. Fig 9 :44: "Only a sealed room can be searched to the end, and no frame is a sealed room." Until one of these fires, the verdict stands as `candidate`.
