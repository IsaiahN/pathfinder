## QUESTION

How much redundancy is worth paying for when the probability and cost of future disruptions are uncertain? Restated as an effect: a firm's operating model Gamma predicts continuity Gamma(b,a); a future disruption returns o-prime; R = |Gamma(b,a) - o'| is indexed per slot (supplier, component, route, region, tier), never aggregated (Fig 1 :19). Option A: a computable optimal number from p and C, a `general`-mode verdict with no ground reading. Option B: a shape and decision rule, independence counted by differing closures, sized to the cost of being wrong, staged as reversible cuts, held as `candidate`. What each leads to: A spends a premium against an anchor that has not spoken; B gives a checkable shape with refuters, open until the ground settles it.

## VERDICT (from the figures)

The figure-derived verdict is Option B, per domain and never averaged: the gap is per slot; redundancy buys independence, counted by differing closures and sized to the cost of being wrong, not to a point estimate of p; probability and cost are two readings and do not form one expected-loss verdict; the question is a union of four conditions staged as ranked reversible cuts; the habitat is mapped outward because the material failure is often three tiers out; and resilience is maintained work. No specific number survives as `grounded`. The ground, a future disruption, has not spoken, so the verdict is `specified` and `candidate`. The strongest outreach point is canonical: the framework returns a shape, not a name, and a clean history is not proof of absence.

- **Cost if wrong:** a firm buys "redundancy" that shares a common cause and pays twice for one failure mode, or it under-buys on a catastrophic low-probability slot by reading a clean history as p=0. Both are costly; the first is invisible until the disruption acts. Fig 2 :42 prices the remedy: "Buy independence in proportion to the cost of being wrong." That cost is avoidable by leading with the shape claim and its refuter.
- **Undoable:** largely yes, with a residual commitment. Cuts are staged as ranked and reversible (Fig 9 :41), but an introduced source brings a habitat that cannot be subtracted (Fig 11 :61: "Introduction is available; exclusion is not"). A superseding packet can replace the claim, and nothing is deleted from the record when it is refuted.
- **Reversed by:** a ground reading, not agreement. A real event in which common-cause twins fail independently (refuter 1), or in which an identical duplicate expands the closure (refuter 2), or a proof that the two currencies are addable (refuter 4), or a source with a genuinely different evidence pool showing that a clean history is a sufficient statistic for p (refuter 5), or a figure re-census showing one lever clears all four union conditions with a non-categorical residue (refuter 6). Until one of these fires, the verdict stands as `candidate`.
