# The Tether's answer on efficiency versus spare capacity

**Mode: `specified`, not `grounded`.** This is a shape and a decision order, not a result. Simulated derivation checked for internal consistency against the figures (`05_what_shape_means.md` §5), not a ground reading.

## 1. The residual it answers

A firm's library predicts service, unit cost and time to recover for each component slot; a disruption arrives; `R = |Γ(b,a) − o′|` is measured per slot, never as one aggregate. Fig 1 :19: "The residual is indexed by object slot, not aggregated." Fig 1 :22: "Aggregating across slots is how a live signal disappears into an average."

The question's premise, one dial between cheap and safe, is refused. The situation is a **union compound**: routine cost and disruption survival are two conditions with different grounds, overlapping at the same atoms. Fig 9 :57: "What remains is smaller and it is also categorically different: a case in two conditions is not a harder case of either."

## 2. The shape, per domain

1. **Keep two ledgers, never sum them.** Cost and recovery are different readings. Fig 12 :125: "A composition costs in two currencies, and they do not add." Fig 12 :130: "Summing them into one figure is the error this names, not the fix for it." Fig 9 :55: "A bundled problem is usually a union of overlapping conditions, not a list of separate ones." Unbundle before searching; one lever clears where only one condition binds and leaves the overlap standing.

2. **The unit of analysis is the firm-and-habitat.** Fig 11 :40: "A system cannot be made more capable by improving the agent in isolation. An improvement that does not change contact changes nothing, however much it improves." Slack added inside the firm without changing its contact with the disruption ground changes the firm, not its resilience to that ground.

3. **Enumerate the habitat outward through tiers.** Fig 11 :52: "And contact cascades. Scoping the habitat to direct contact is how DDT reached the eagles: first-order enumeration, second-order effect, and the residual that mattered was three steps out." A tier-1 map is not the habitat.

4. **Choose bonds, not just atoms.** Fig 12 :117: "isomer. Same atoms, different bond, different substance. The difference is invisible in the formula and decisive in the behaviour." Two firms with the same supplier list can be isomers: one bonds it `supplier → component → assembly`, the other adds `∥` (either suffices) with a qualified alternate. A bill of materials records the atoms and hides the bond. Fig 12 :124: "the group says what kind; the qualifier says which member."

5. **Resilience to a new failure needs an import, not more optimisation.** Fig 8 :32: "Closure is idempotent. Composing inside a closed set never leaves it, so no search, however long, adds an atom. This one is definitional: it is what closure means." Fig 6 :23: "No frame escapes its own closure. The bound moves only on contact with a frame whose closure differs." Qualifying a never-used source or region is an import and owes a source gate and a shadow test; adding a second supplier of an already-held type is only an isomer bond change. Both are legitimate; the record must say which.

6. **Buy independence in proportion to the cost of being wrong.** Fig 2 :42: "Buy independence in proportion to the cost of being wrong." Fig 2 :40: "agreement about the world is worthless when the agreeing frames share an evidence pool." Supplier and route diversity is independence, not automatic waste; for a low-consequence component the right amount may be zero. Fig 9 :60: "Use filters for the budget and witnesses for the verdict. Never let a filter hand you a verdict."

7. **Keep the channel to the anchor reachable.** Fig 10 :24: "the ground does not decay, the channel does" Fig 10 :26: "saturated metrics, pooled readings" An aggregate service percentage can be high while one slot is live and failing. The anchor is realized recovery. Fig 2 :35: "Alignment is triangulation, not negotiation. A mirror cannot correct you."

8. **A stress test is a substituted habitat.** Fig 11 :70: "A test harness is a substituted habitat. Which is why a synthetic solve proves wiring and never capability." Fig 11 :66: "Isolation is not removal of the habitat. It is substitution of one habitat for another." Reshoring substitutes a habitat and inherits actors the firm did not choose. Fig 11 :63: "Which is why a managed ecosystem is permanent work rather than a solved state."

9. **The early warning is improved from one already returning something.** Fig 6 :34: "Where nothing is, there is no edge to extend from, and the search for a proto-instrument is a search for one that is already there." Near misses, tier-k deviations and quality signals are the proto-instruments. Fig 8 :39: "So a search that finds nothing is never a proof of absence."

## 3. What it explicitly does not say

- Not a universal number of days of inventory, safety stock, or suppliers. Fig 9 :32: "you get a kind of thing, never the answer itself." Fig 13 :134: "Unreachable is a verdict here, not a silence"
- Not that slack is worthless, that stress tests are useless, or that every firm should dual source. Independence is proportional (Fig 2 :42), and a stress test proves wiring (Fig 11 :70).
- Not a derivation of one ledger from the other, and no new primitive is claimed.
- Not `grounded`. No firm, disruption or recovery measurement was taken.
- Not a claim that a familiar framework or a prestigious author settles anything. *The Recursive Self-Improvement Trap*: "Prestige can fund an action. It can give an action a budget to be tried. But it cannot settle an action. Only the ground can settle it."

## 4. What would settle it, the ground

A source-discriminating reading, not a further argument. From *The Hard Problem is About Source*: an instrument that reads green *"cannot comment on the source"*, and *"The answer is unreached until a better instrument exists."* Here:

- **Discriminator test:** does a per-slot recovery signal distinguish a genuinely robust configuration from one that merely has not been hit? An instrument that does would name the source of the aggregate reading.
- **Tier-k test:** a documented disruption whose outcome is traced through tier 2 and beyond, with measured effect sizes, would settle whether the cascade reaches the residual that mattered.
- **Isomer test:** configurations identical in atoms and bond record compared under a representative disruption set; or a demonstrated case where removing one of two bonded suppliers never matters.
- **Import test:** a new capability reached with and without contact with a differing closure, showing whether an atom was added or only a bond changed.
- **Standing falsifiers from `01` §4:** the eight pre-registered conditions, in particular a single scalar index predicting both conditions with no per-slot residual, and a habitat substitution leaving outcome unchanged while policy is held fixed.

## 5. The honest stop

**Unreached, not unreachable.** The relevant quantity is `gap`: what buffer, what count of suppliers, which region. No figure line sets it, and the framework says it cannot name what it does not hold. *They're Scanning Books*: "Declaring the primitives does not make them neutral. It makes the choice visible." The answer stops at the limit of the available instruments and records the lineage so the stop is checkable. *"Indiscernibility is not identity."*

## 6. One-paragraph version for outreach

> The Tether does not give a number of days of inventory. It says the question is bundled: routine cost and disruption survival are two conditions with two grounds, so they must be kept in two ledgers rather than traded on one curve. Capability is a property of the firm and its supply habitat, not of its slack policy, and the habitat has to be enumerated outward because the residual that matters can be three tiers away. The configuration you choose is an arrangement of atoms under bonds: the same suppliers under a different bond are a different firm, which a bill of materials cannot show. A capability that needs a supplier or region the current set lacks requires an import from a differing closure, with provenance and a check that it explains a real gap; independence is bought in proportion to the cost of being wrong. Stress tests prove wiring, not capability. What the framework returns is the shape any answer must have, eight falsifiers written in advance, and a `gap` where the number should be until a disruption, not a meeting, settles it.
