## QUESTION

Restated in effect: how does a firm keep `R = |Γ(b,a) − o′|` small per component slot both under routine operation and when a disruption arrives? Option B: unbundle the union, choose bonds over atoms in the firm-and-habitat, import when the closure lacks a capability, and leave the number open until the ground settles it. A sends an unsummed pair of readings into one figure and hides the bond; B sends a checkable shape with eight pre-registered refuters and an honest `gap`.

## VERDICT (from the figures)

The premise is refused. Efficiency and resilience are not two ends of one dial. The figure-derived shape has nine parts: two ledgers unsummed; capability in firm-and-habitat; habitat enumerated outward through tiers; a bond choice that makes same-atom configurations isomers; an import from a differing closure when a new capability is needed; independence bought in proportion to the cost of being wrong; a per-slot channel to realized recovery; stress tests as wiring checks, not capability proofs; and early warning improved from a signal already returning something. The numeric balance is `gap`, settled only by a disruption or a source-discriminating instrument. Mode `specified`, ungrounded, candidate. World sources corroborate the mechanism shape; none settles the firm-level quantity.

- **Cost if wrong:** a firm failing the bond or import condition is sold more cost precision or a thicker generic buffer, and a single lever is applied to a union that needs two. The cost is real but bounded: the packet states mode, refuters and lineage, so the error is findable. The larger cost is spending an outside reader's independence by sending a recording instead of a characterised residual, which Fig 2 :42 prices: "Buy independence in proportion to the cost of being wrong."
- **Undoable:** yes. Nothing is deleted if the claim fails; a refutation is added and the reach falls because something was added. Fig 6 :42 "What is recorded only grows. What is reachable is derived, and it can fall." The shape and its eight falsifiers are recorded in a provenance trail so a later reader can locate the error (Fig 10 :62-64).
- **Reversed by:** a source-discriminating per-slot recovery instrument; a tier-k disruption trace with zero measured effect beyond tier 1; an isomer test showing bond records carry nothing; a reach renewed by internal optimisation with no differing closure; or any of the eight refuters in `01` §4, in particular a single scalar index predicting both conditions with no per-slot residual, and a habitat substitution that leaves the outcome unchanged while internal policy is fixed. Agreement, prestige and a stress-test pass are not such instruments.
