## Wider literature that bears on the question

The business and operations pool (Zhang and Yu; lean manufacturing; healthcare operations) treats the trade-off as a real multi-objective design problem. It corroborates the figure's instruction to keep two readings apart, but it is one broad pool, so it counts as one witness, not several. The low-overlap pools supply the mechanism shape: ecological resilience on specialisation and the loss of adaptive range, high reliability organizations and normal accident theory on tight coupling, limited slack, and looking across system boundaries, and healthcare operations on multi-tier supplier reviews, buffer reserves and time-to-recover metrics. None of these settles the firm-level question. They are corroboration only; Fig 9 :60 forbids letting them hand over the verdict.
