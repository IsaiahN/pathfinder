## QUESTION

Is disruptive innovation a general pattern, or a useful label applied selectively after the fact? Option A: assert a law-like pattern that predicts ahead of the outcome. Option B: a retrospective name fitted to completed cases. What each leads to: A commits a verdict that must be settled at a ground and currently is not; B gives an efficient convention with zero verdict weight about the world. The derived answer refuses the either/or: the question is a union of mechanism, instrument, name and convention, and the figures license a shape while refusing the name as a settled ex ante term.

## VERDICT (from the figures)

The either/or is a union, and the two halves govern different objects. In ordinary use "disruptive innovation" is a label applied after the market ground settled: an efficient convention, disqualified as a verdict about the world while its users share one case pool. A general pattern is licensed only as a shape, per case: an entrant from a differing closure enters where the incumbent's channel to a non-updating ground has decayed, with residual-relative valence and no standing list. The figures do not name a winner, do not equate the 1995 and 2003 terms, and do not return an ex ante discriminator. That is a `gap`, reported as unreached, not silence. Mode `specified` on the per-domain verdicts, `general` on the structural shape, not `grounded`.

- **cost if wrong:** bounded and locatable. The packet states mode, writes refuters before the conclusion and records the lineage, so an error is findable rather than invisible. The real cost is promoting a convention into a verdict, where it decides which firms get studied or where capital goes, and then cannot be refused. Fig 10 :47 prices that cost; Fig 2 :42 says "Buy independence in proportion to the cost of being wrong."
- **undoable:** yes. The convention can be relabelled as a convention, the standing list can be withdrawn, and the shape claim can be sent without the name. No primitive has to be abandoned and no case record has to be deleted. A sent claim can be superseded by a corrected packet.
- **reversed by:** a ground reading, not agreement. A blinded ex ante classification, written before the outcomes and scored on held-out cases against a market outcome that does not update on the score, would close the `gap` and check the label as a generator. A source-discriminating instrument separating channel decay from scale economies, legacy costs, regulation and entrant count would name the source. A standing list keeping its valence across a documented residual change, or a substitution test equating the 1995 and 2003 terms, would break the shape claim. Until one of these fires, the verdict stands as `candidate`.
