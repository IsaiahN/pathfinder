## Relevant wider literature

Two independent empirical checks matter more than the many opinion pieces:

1. **King and Baatartogtokh 2015** is the only source opened that tests the theory against experts on the cases the theory itself claims. Its 9% result is a ground-visible reading, not a preference. It corroborates the union verdict (the four elements are separate conditions) and the ex ante gap (the theory is a warning, not a prediction). It does **not** establish that no mechanism exists.
2. **Budd and Mann 2018** is the genuinely low-overlap witness. In an homogeneous birth-death model, conditioning on survival produces an apparent early burst followed by slowdown, without any change in the underlying process. That is the shape of the retrospective-label claim stated in a different currency: the pattern in the observed set is produced by who survived to be observed. It corroborates Fig 11 :36 and Fig 9 :27-28 without using any business evidence.

The other business sources (Wikipedia's disruption article, Lepore 2014, the Christensen quotations carried inside them) are **one evidence pool**, not four witnesses. The four framework essays share the framework author's pool completely and carry no independent witness weight.

## Distribution rules

Figure-grounded reasons for the order and the tone:

- **Fig 10 :52** *"Bring a characterised residual, never the source."* Lead with the residual and the refuter. Do not send the architecture before the check.
- **Fig 2 :45** *"Frames range against the ground. They do not negotiate with it."* Ask what the market would show, not whether the reader finds the framing plausible.
- **Fig 2 :40** *"disqualifying for a verdict: agreement about the world is worthless when the agreeing frames share an evidence pool"* A reader who has absorbed the framework first has joined the frame being checked. Their later agreement is never pooled as corroboration.
- **Fig 4 :25** *"A recording carried upward looks like knowledge and is a description of one occasion."* Send the generator (shape plus refuter), not the case history.
- **Fig 2 :42** *"Buy independence in proportion to the cost of being wrong."* The cost here is a convention being promoted into a capital-allocation verdict, so the check is worth buying.

**Recommended order in a reply:** (1) the residual and the union framing; (2) the four refuter conditions; (3) ask what the reader would count as a failure and record it; (4) only then the three links, with the note that the figure text, not the summary, is the authority; (5) offer the method for reuse, with the instruction that each user writes their own refuter and states their own mode.

## The two-stage offer (and the optional third)

**(1)** Validate the separator against the ground. **(2)** If curious, read the source that produced it. **(3)** If useful, reuse the formula on a problem with a real ground.

- Stage 1 before stage 2 is Fig 10 :52 and Fig 2 :45. The recipient checks without holding the framework interior.
- Stage 2 is opt-in. Seat 4 must not hold the interiors; once a recipient has absorbed the theory, their agreement stops being evidence (Fig 2 :40).
- Stage 3 sends a method, which is permitted: Fig 4 :22 *"Only methods cross up. Only head starts come down."*
- Stage 3 still needs a ground per problem: a frame *"cannot check the direction of its own question, because the shape a frame is searching for is not a term it holds"* (`THE_FORMULA.md`, Direction). Each user writes the refuter before the run.

**Hard precondition:** the stage-1 output must survive. As currently stated it is a shape plus a gap, not a prediction; that is the honest form. Do not convert it into a prediction to make the email sound stronger.
