# Outreach draft: check the shape before you decide about the framework

**Use:** one-to-one, or as a cover letter attached to a submission through the public venues in `03_public_contact_points.csv`. **Not** for bulk send.

**Hard precondition:** do not send until the shape claim and its refuter in `08_expert_verification_packet.md` are stable. If the stage-1 output does not survive, there is nothing else to look at.

---

**Subject:** One structural claim about language, culture and categories: would this fail for you?

> Hi [name],
>
> I have one narrow structural claim I would like you to check against what you already know. I am not asking you to read a theory first, and I am not asking you to agree with anything. If the claim does not survive, there is nothing else to look at.
>
> **The claim (`specified`, not `grounded`).** Cross-language category differences are better described as differences of *relation over a shared inventory* than as differences of *which words exist*. The shape says: a perceptual and bodily kernel is shared; what varies is the bonding installed by language and cultural practice; the carrier is a convention; and the comparison has to be made in effect terms, because the category names we borrow already carry the frame we are trying to check.
>
> Three consequences follow, and these are the part I would like checked:
>
> 1. Strong determinism is the wrong target. If a naming convention decided the outcome, it would have stopped being a convention. So the interesting question is not whether language "determines" thought, but which discriminations change under which uncertainty, and by what instrument we would know.
> 2. "Language and culture jointly" is a union of overlapping conditions, not one lever. An averaged "language effect" is the wrong object. Verdicts have to be kept per domain.
> 3. A category name is not a portable unit of comparison. Whorf's Hopi example is often said to fail partly because he had to translate Hopi terms into English to explain that they were untranslatable. What travels is a description of effects: what would you expect to see if the category boundary shifted?
>
> **What would refute it.** I wrote these down before sending this:
>
> 1. Matched nonverbal tasks showing zero relational difference between communities whose languages differ maximally in the relevant domain, with translation equivalence controlled and uncertainty manipulated.
> 2. A demonstration that one lever, holding the other conditions constant, accounts for all observed category differences across at least three semantic domains.
> 3. A translation that preserves every discrimination the source term licenses, verified in matched nonverbal tasks.
>
> If any of these holds, the shape is wrong and I will withdraw it. A one-line "this fails because..." is much more useful to me than agreement. If you would rather not engage, no reply is needed.
>
> **What I am not claiming:** that any particular language has any particular category, that the framework names a specific category system, that I have measured anything, or that this settles the Sapir-Whorf debate. The framework returns a shape and a route, not a list and not a verdict. Agreement among speakers is the convention working, not evidence about the world.
>
> **If it survives, and only then:** the sources are below. The figures are the authority, not my summary of them.
>
> - Figures text, verbatim with line numbers (local path in the packet)
> - Canonical figures README: https://github.com/IsaiahN/tether/blob/general-agent/figures/README.md
> - Figures folder, branch `general-agent`: https://github.com/IsaiahN/tether/tree/general-agent/figures
>

Write down what would refute your result *before* you run it, and state the mode of what you get. A reported failure is more useful to me than a reported success.

## Sequence for a reply

1. Send the shape claim and the three refuters.
2. Ask what they would count as a failure. Record their verdict *before* showing any framework material.
3. Only if they are curious, send the three links, noting that the figures and not the summary are the authority.
4. Only after stage 2, offer the formula as a reusable method with the instruction that each person writes their own refuter and states their own mode.

A stage-2 agreement is never pooled as corroboration. Fig 2 :40 applies: a shared evidence pool is one witness talking, not two confirming.
