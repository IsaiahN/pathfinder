## DOMAIN CENSUS

R by arity, symmetry and scale, then one line per domain with actors / currency / anchor, partition or union, and order by dependency.

- **R (the residual).** A continuous, variable stream of experience yields different, partly overlapping category systems across communities. Arity: three conditions (language, cultural practice, shared perceptual substrate), so a **union compound**, not one lever (Fig 9 :54). Symmetry: language and practice are not interchangeable operators; their feedback is a cycle whose sign decides whether it closes (Fig 12 :121). Scale: one lifetime (acquisition), many lifetimes (transmission), many communities (comparison).
- **Domain 1, perceptual / conceptual substrate.** Actors: bodily and perceptual capacities, attention, core knowledge. Currency: discrimination and salience. Anchor: the shared sensorium and the world for "what is discriminable." Kernel, not a variable.
- **Domain 2, language.** Actors: labels, generics, grammar, colour / number / spatial terms. Currency: conventional shared meaning. Anchor: the speech community convention, legitimate for "what does this word mean here", an instrument only for "what is the world like" (Fig 2 :37). One leg of a union.
- **Domain 3, cultural practice.** Actors: rituals, tool use, child-rearing, norms. Currency: transmission fidelity plus innovation. Anchor: outcomes practices produce in the world. One leg of a union.
- **Domain 4, verification / comparison.** Actors: cross-linguistic experimenters, nonverbal tasks. Currency: residuals in nonverbal behaviour. Anchor: the ground, never agreement. The only domain that can settle a verdict.
- **Question type:** union, not partition. D1, D2 and D3 overlap; D4 ranges against them separately.
- **Order by dependency (Fig 3).** Perception, then vocabulary, then objective, then planning, then carry. The chain breaks at the objective in this field: having terms for what a situation offers versus saying what would count as a category difference "has neither, so it attracts the least attention and the fewest instruments" (Fig 3 :26). Refuse before mint (Formula step 2). Describe the residual before choosing a frame (Fig 9 :27-28).

Strain test applied: Fig 10 :47 versus Fig 12 :121 was a domain error on first reading. Re-census: Fig 10 governs conventions, Fig 12 governs cycles and names the sign condition. Different objects. One strain remains genuinely open, the human-kind looping effect (F7 in `01`), because no instrument reads the sign.

## VERDICT (from the figures)

Category differences are differences of bonding over a shared inventory, not word-list differences. Strong determinism is refused because a convention that decided the outcome would have stopped being one. "Jointly" is a union compound, so verdicts stay per domain and are never averaged. Cross-language comparison must use effect descriptions because names carry their frame. The specific carrier (labels, generics, the looping effect) is a `single-source` world finding, not a figure derivation. The human-kind case is an open strain. Mode: `specified` on structure, `general` on union and dependency, ungrounded, candidate. The figures decide the shape; they do not report how much any language or practice shapes any category.

- **cost if wrong:** bounded and locatable. The packet states mode, refuters and provenance before the conclusion, so an error is findable rather than invisible. The larger cost is spending outside readers' independence by sending the framework before the shape claim; Fig 2 :42 "Buy independence in proportion to the cost of being wrong." That cost is avoidable by leading with the refuter.
- **undoable:** yes. The carrier binding and the functional-group reading are removable without touching the shape. No primitive has to be withdrawn, and a sent claim can be superseded by a corrected packet.
- **reversed by:** a ground reading, never agreement. A nonverbal instrument that discriminates bonds from names, a demonstration that the compound is a partition, a translation preserving all discriminations, a convention changing its own ground, or an instrument that reads the sign of the human-kind loop would each reverse the relevant finding. Until one fires, the verdict stands as `candidate`.
