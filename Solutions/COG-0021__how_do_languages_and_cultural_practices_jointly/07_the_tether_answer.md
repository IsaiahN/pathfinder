# The Tether's answer on language, culture and categories

**Mode: `specified` on the structure, `general` on the dependency and union rules. Not `grounded`.** This is a shape and a route, not a result. Simulated derivation checked against the figures (`05_what_shape_means.md` section 7), not a ground reading.

## 1. The residual it answers

A continuous stream of experience arrives. Communities end up with different, partly overlapping category systems. What fails to resolve is whether the difference is in the arrangement, in the names, or in the substrate. It is a **union compound**, not one hard question: Fig 9 :54 "And when the split lands on a union rather than a partition, unbundle before you search again." A case in three conditions is not a harder case of one. It is a specific compound with its own loop.

## 2. The shape

1. **An invariant kernel is shared; what varies is the binding.** Fig 4 :20 "Only the actors, the currency and the anchor change." The perceptual and bodily capacities are the kernel; the relations installed by language and practice are the variable. Fig 13 :37 "If two members drew on the same underlying fact, their agreement would carry no information, and one of them would be redundant rather than minimal." So the kernel claim and the variation claim must be kept as separate domains with separate anchors, not blended into one story.

2. **Category differences are differences of bonding, not of word inventories.** Fig 12 :5 "A set of ingredients is not one. The bond is the rest of the information, and it is the half nothing was recording." Fig 12 :94 "The arrangement is the substance." Over a shared basic inventory, languages stand as Fig 12 :117 "isomer. Same atoms, different bond, different substance. The difference is invisible in the formula and decisive in the behaviour." Wang et al. (2026) measure exactly this: shared meanings, different relations.

3. **Strong determinism is refused, not merely unmeasured.** Fig 10 :47 "a convention that decides an outcome has stopped being one". A category system is a convention; if it settled the world it would cease to be one. Seat Map :163 "High correlation is disqualifying for a verdict and efficient for a convention." Whorf's own formulation calls the category system "an agreement that holds throughout our speech community" (quoted in the *Linguistic relativity* entry). The interesting question is therefore not whether language determines thought, but which discriminations change under which conditions.

4. **There is no single "language effect".** Fig 1 :19 "The residual is indexed by object slot, not aggregated." Verdicts stay per domain. Cibelli et al. show the effect is conditional on uncertainty and disappears under verbal interference but not visual interference, which is a per-slot, instrument-level result rather than a population average.

5. **The carrier is a convention with recorded provenance.** Fig 10 :45 "It does author conventions, because nothing else can see across a seam." The specific carrier, labels and generics plus the looping effect for human kinds, is a world finding from Gelman and Roberts (2017), `single-source` and ungrounded. Fig 8 :50-52 "Convergent derivation and adopted import look identical in the contents. Only the record of where each came from separates them" is why the provenance, not the identity of the carrier, is the figure-level claim.

6. **Cross-language comparison must use effect descriptions.** Fig 9 :30-31 "your words are borrowed too, so describe the effect rather than the cause". Fig 6 :30 "The description travels. Fails by being sharpened into terms only we can read. Cast it in effect terms." Whorf's Hopi example is reported as self-refuting because he had to translate Hopi terms into English to explain that they were untranslatable. What travels is a description of what you would expect to see.

7. **The human-kinds case stays open.** Fig 12 :121 "cycle. The output re-enters as input. The sign of the loop decides whether it closes, and a chain cannot close by construction." A human-kind classification can loop back and change the classified, so the convention/verdict boundary is genuinely strained. The figures give the sign condition but no instrument to read it. This is `gap` at this budget, not a settled result.

## 3. What it explicitly does not say

- Not that any language has any particular category. The framework returns a shape, not a name.
- Not a percentage, a ranking of languages, or a "how much" number. There is no anchor for such a number.
- Not that the six-term substrate answers a cross-cultural question. It is used only as the kernel/variable distinction; it is "a human's careful guess at reality's compression axes" (*They're Scanning Books*).
- Not that the Sapir-Whorf debate is over. The figures refuse the strong version; they do not settle the weak version's magnitude.
- Not grounded. No nonverbal measurement was taken, and the second seat shares this seat's evidence pool (`11_audit.md`).

## 4. What would settle it: the ground

A source-discriminating instrument, not a further argument. From *The Hard Problem is About Source*: "the instrument is speaking only about what it measured, it cannot comment on the source", and the colour case was resolved "not by argument, but by the arrival of a better instrument."

The concrete questions:

1. **Bond test.** Do matched nonverbal tasks show relational differences across communities whose languages differ maximally, with translation equivalence controlled and uncertainty manipulated? If no, F1 fails.
2. **Union test.** Can one lever, holding the other conditions constant, account for differences across three semantic domains? If yes, F3 fails and the compound is a partition.
3. **Effect-term test.** Does a translation preserve every discrimination the source term licenses, verified in matched nonverbal tasks? If yes, F4 fails.
4. **Convention test.** Does a naming convention change the physical facts its category picks out, with an independent ground registering the change? If yes, F2 fails.
5. **Human-kind sign test.** Is there an instrument that distinguishes a classification tracking an independent property from one manufacturing it? That would close the F7 strain.

The five full refuters, written before the conclusion, are in `01_figure_grounded_audit.md` section 5.

## 5. The honest stop

**Unreached, not unreachable.** Fig 2 :45 "Frames range against the ground. They do not negotiate with it." The figures give the shape and the direction of the instrument. They do not give the measurement. The substrate is a visible guess, not a discovery: "Declaring the primitives does not make them neutral. It makes the choice visible" (*They're Scanning Books*). "Indiscernibility is not identity" (figures README). The lineage is recorded so the stop is checkable.

## 6. One-paragraph version for outreach

> The Tether does not rank languages or say how much each one shapes thought. It says that category differences are differences of *relation over a shared inventory*, not differences of word lists: languages stand to each other as isomers, same atoms arranged differently. It says that strong linguistic determinism is the wrong target, because a convention that decided the outcome would have stopped being a convention. It says that "language and culture jointly" is a union of overlapping conditions, so an averaged language effect is the wrong object and verdicts must be kept per domain. It says that a category name cannot be carried across languages at all; what travels is a description of the discriminations it licenses. What it offers is the shape any answer must have, five refuters written in advance, and the kind of nonverbal, source-discriminating instrument that would close it. The human-kinds case stays open until an instrument can read the sign of the loop.

## 7. Verdict fields (carried into `09_watson_answer.md`)

- **Cost if wrong:** bounded and locatable, because the packet states mode, refuters and provenance before the conclusion. The larger cost is spending the independence of outside readers by sending the framework before the shape claim; Fig 2 :42 "Buy independence in proportion to the cost of being wrong."
- **Undoable:** yes. The specific carrier binding and the functional-group claim are removable without touching the shape. No primitive has to be withdrawn.
- **Reversed by:** a ground reading, never agreement. A nonverbal instrument that discriminates bonds from names, a demonstration that the compound is a partition, a translation that preserves all discriminations, or a convention that changes its own ground would each reverse the relevant finding.
