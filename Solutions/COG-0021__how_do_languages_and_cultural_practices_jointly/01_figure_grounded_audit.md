## 1. The residual, stated in effect terms

**R:** A continuous, variable stream of perceptual and social experience arrives. Different linguistic and cultural communities end up with different, partly overlapping systems of categories. What fails to resolve is *what kind of difference* this is. Three candidate answers:

| option | what it asserts | what it leads to |
|---|---|---|
| A: arrangement differs | the relations among a shared set of basic discriminations are bonded differently | a structural claim, testable by non-verbal tasks (Fig 12 :117) |
| B: labels differ only | the same arrangement is named differently | a convention; agreement inside a speech community is the product (Fig 2 :41) |
| C: substrate differs | the communities operate with different primitives | a new primitive is owed, and it cannot be named from inside (Fig 9 :32) |

**Direction discipline.** The Formula says a frame cannot check the direction of its own question: "a frame cannot check the direction of its own question, because the shape a frame is searching for is not a term it holds." So we bind "an answer" as an ordinary term before searching:

> **BOUND TERM (answer-shape):** a decomposition of category structure into (i) an invariant kernel supplied by shared perceptual and bodily capacities, (ii) a variable binding installed by language and cultural practice, (iii) a carrier that transmits the binding, and (iv) a ground that discriminates whether the binding changed the categories or only the names.

This term now accrues a residual and is refusable like anything else. It is deliberately not a list of categories.

**R by arity, symmetry, scale** (Fig 9 :22, "arity · symmetry · scale"):

- **Arity:** three operands, not two. Language, cultural practice, and a shared perceptual substrate. The question says "jointly", which is literally a union with overlap, not a single lever (Fig 9 :54).
- **Symmetry:** language and practice are not interchangeable operators. A label and a practice do different work, so this is not a symmetric bond. The feedback between them is a cycle, and the figures note that "The sign of the loop decides whether it closes, and a chain cannot close by construction" (Fig 12 :121).
- **Scale:** three scales are in play. One lifetime (acquisition), many lifetimes (transmission), and many communities (comparison). The membrane governs what crosses between them (Fig 4 :19).

## 4. Findings F1 to F8

### F1. Categories are arrangements, not ingredient lists (decisive for option A)

Fig 12 :5: "A set of ingredients is not one. The bond is the rest of the information, and it is the half nothing was recording."
Fig 12 :94: "The arrangement is the substance."
Fig 12 :117: "isomer. Same atoms, different bond, different substance. The difference is invisible in the formula and decisive in the behaviour."

Wang, Qin and Liu (arXiv:2610.30778, 2026-10-22), a computational comparison of 196 basic concepts across 81 languages, report: "Shared basic meanings, however, need not imply shared relations among them." In the Discussion: "languages may express corresponding basic meanings while differing in the relations connecting them."

This is a direct, low-overlap match to Fig 12 :117. The question's "categories" are therefore not a list of names to be compared for translation equivalence. They are bonded arrangements. A language difference that leaves the atoms alone but changes the relations is an isomer difference, and it is "decisive in the behaviour" even though it is "invisible in the formula."

**Refuter:** if cross-language relational dissimilarity falls to zero once translation equivalence is controlled, the isomer claim fails and the difference is a pure naming difference (Fig 8 :13, "same atoms, different names").

**Mode:** `specified`, triangulated across Fig 12 and Wang et al. (different instruments, different evidence pools).

### F2. Strong determinism is refused, not merely unsupported (decisive for option C)

Fig 10 :18: "a declared meaning is not a grounded one"
Fig 10 :47: "a convention that decides an outcome has stopped being one"
Seat Map :163: "High correlation is disqualifying for a verdict and efficient for a convention."

Whorf's own canonical statement, quoted in the *Linguistic relativity* entry (Wikipedia, accessed 2026-11-22), contains the following verbatim excerpts (ellipses mark cuts; the original also contains em dashes, which this file's style rule forbids reproducing):

- "We dissect nature along lines laid down by our native language."
- "We cut nature up, organize it into concepts, and ascribe significances as we do, largely because we are parties to an agreement to organize it in this way"
- "an agreement that holds throughout our speech community and is codified in the patterns of our language"

Note what Whorf calls it: an **agreement**. The figures' rule for an agreement is Fig 2 :41, "efficient for a convention." The strong reading turns that agreement into a verdict about the world, which is exactly the transition Fig 10 :47 forbids. Wikipedia records that "The strong hypothesis of linguistic relativity, now referred to as linguistic determinism, is that language determines thought and that linguistic categories limit and restrict cognitive categories. This was a claim by some earlier linguists pre-World War II; since then it has fallen out of acceptance by contemporary linguists."

So option C is not left open as an untested possibility. It is **refused by the figures**, and the world literature independently moved the same way. This is a refusal, which the Formula distinguishes from an explanation: a "REFUTED" route owes "a refusal, not an explanation."

**Refuter:** a demonstrated case in which a naming convention, with no mediating instrument or practice, changes the physical facts that the category picks out and the change is registered by an independent ground. That would falsify Fig 10 :47 as applied here. (The human-kinds looping effect is a genuine partial case and is recorded as a live strain in F7.)

**Mode:** `specified`, triangulated across Fig 10, Seat Map :163 and the Whorf primary quote. The Wikipedia editorial line is a secondary summary and is not counted as a separate witness.

### F3. "Jointly" is a union compound, so it must be unbundled before search

Fig 9 :54: "And when the split lands on a union rather than a partition, unbundle before you search again."
Fig 9 :55: "A bundled problem is usually a union of overlapping conditions, not a list of separate ones."
Fig 1 :19: "The residual is indexed by object slot, not aggregated."
Fig 1 :22: "Aggregating across slots is how a live signal disappears into an average."

Asking for the single effect of "language and culture" is asking one lever to clear a region where three conditions overlap. Fig 9 :57: "a case in two conditions is not a harder case of either. It stops being a mixture and becomes a specific compound." So the honest answer has separate verdicts per domain, never an averaged "language contributes X percent."

Cibelli, Xu, Austerweil, Griffiths and Regier (PLoS ONE, 2016) give the instrument-level version: "language-specific effects on memory can emerge from a largely universal substrate when one critical component of that substrate is language-specific: the category distribution." And the effect is conditional: "The model predicts that categories will most clearly affect color memory when perceptual information is uncertain." That is a per-slot, conditional effect, not an average over a population.

**Refuter:** a single manipulation that holds culture constant and fully accounts for all observed category differences across all semantic domains, replicated with nonverbal materials. That would show a partition, not a union, and F3 would fail.

**Mode:** `specified`, triangulated across Fig 9, Fig 1 and Cibelli et al.

### F4. The comparison must travel in effect terms, because the words are borrowed

Fig 9 :30-31: "your words are borrowed too, so describe the effect rather than the cause"
Fig 6 :30: "The description travels. Fails by being sharpened into terms only we can read. Cast it in effect terms."

The *Linguistic relativity* entry reports the self-refutation problem directly: "philosophers including Donald Davidson and Jason Josephson Storm have argued that Whorf's Hopi examples are self-refuting, as Whorf had to translate Hopi terms into English in order to explain how they are untranslatable." Sapir is quoted there as saying: "No two languages are ever sufficiently similar to be considered as representing the same social reality. The worlds in which different societies live are distinct worlds, not merely the same world with different labels attached."

From *Decoding Tacit Knowledge* (2026-11-10): "When contact isn't possible, the best you can send is a plain description of what you'd expect to see, because anyone can check that against the world."

So a category name cannot be the unit of cross-language comparison. The unit is the effect: which discriminations the category licenses, under which uncertainty, in which task. The Whorf example fails not because Whorf was wrong about everything but because he sent a name through a translation that already carried the receiving frame.

**Refuter:** a demonstrated translation between two languages that preserves all discriminations the original term licenses, verified by matched nonverbal tasks. Then names do travel and F4 fails.

**Mode:** `general` on "describe effects", `specified` on the Whorf application. Triangulated across Fig 9, Fig 6, the Wikipedia/Davidson report and *Decoding Tacit Knowledge*.

### F5. Kernel and variable: the substrate is invariant, the binding is not

Fig 4 :20: "Only the actors, the currency and the anchor change."
Fig 13 :37: "If two members drew on the same underlying fact, their agreement would carry no information, and one of them would be redundant rather than minimal."
Fig 13 :39: "Six members that overlapped would be fewer members wearing six names."

Cibelli et al.: "taking that hypothesis seriously need not entail a wholesale rejection of important universal components of human cognition." Gelman and Roberts (PNAS, 2017): "language is a technology that enhances and expands the categorization capacities that we share with other animals." Wang et al.: "These findings suggest that universality and relativity are interwoven across conceptual domains."

Three independent literatures converge on kernel plus variable. The figures license the same shape: an invariant loop with a variable cast. Note the Fig 13 warning: if the "universal" and "relative" accounts were the same fact under two names, their agreement would carry no information. So they must be kept as separate domains with separate anchors (D1 and D4), not blended.

**Refuter:** a demonstration that the shared substrate claim and the variation claim are generated by the same measurement, so their agreement is one evidence pool wearing two names. Then the triangulation collapses to one witness.

**Mode:** `general`, triangulated across Fig 4, Fig 13 and three world sources with different methods (developmental, experimental, corpus-computational).

### F6. The carrier is a convention with a source gate, not an import that moves the wall

Fig 10 :45: "It does author conventions, because nothing else can see across a seam."
Fig 10 :49: "Declaring a meaning does not ground it."
Fig 8 :50-52: "Convergent derivation and adopted import look identical in the contents. Only the record of where each came from separates them"

Gelman and Roberts identify the carrier precisely: "words are conventional and prescriptive, and provide a stable representation that is easily shared with great fidelity, but on the other hand, words have an open-ended 'placeholder' structure that invites innovation." They call the mechanism a "looping effect" and quote Hacking: "To create new ways of classifying people is also to change how we can think of ourselves, to change our sense of self-worth, even how we remember our own past."

The figure contribution is only the *shape* of the carrier: something must cross a seam, it must be a convention rather than a verdict, and its provenance must be recorded because convergent derivation and adopted import look the same in the contents. The specific identity of the carrier (labels, generics, practices) is a world finding, not a figure line. It is `single-source` for the mechanism and must not be promoted to a Tether result.

**Refuter:** a case where the category binding is transmitted with no convention of any kind, by pure individual perception with zero contact. That would remove the seam and F6 would fail.

**Mode:** `specified` for the shape, `single-source` (Gelman and Roberts 2017) for the specific carrier.

### F7. Live strain: the looping effect for human kinds versus "a convention that decides an outcome"

Fig 12 :121: "cycle. The output re-enters as input. The sign of the loop decides whether it closes, and a chain cannot close by construction."
Fig 10 :47: "a convention that decides an outcome has stopped being one"

For natural kinds, naming a category does not change what the category picks out, so the convention stays a convention. For **human kinds**, Gelman and Roberts report that classification feeds back into the classified: people "behave differently and so are different". The category label then helps make the category true. That looks like a convention deciding an outcome, which Fig 10 :47 forbids.

This is a genuine strain, not a defect to hide. Re-census resolves part of it: Fig 12 :121 says the sign of the loop decides whether it closes, so the figures already distinguish a loop that stays a convention from one whose sign flips into a verdict. But *reading the sign* still needs an instrument: does the classification track an independent ground, or did it manufacture the ground it now reads? *The Hard Problem is About Source* states the general requirement: "the instrument is speaking only about what it measured, it cannot comment on the source." For social kinds, we generally lack the spectrometer.

**Recorded as:** one live strain, named, not smoothed. It does not flip the verdict on F2 (strong determinism still refused for the natural-kind and perceptual domains), but it blocks any claim that the figures have already settled the human-kinds case.

**Refuter for the strain resolution:** an instrument that discriminates, for a human kind, between a classification that tracks an independent property and one that manufactures it. Then the sign could be read and the strain would close.

**Mode:** `specified`, single-source on the world side (Gelman and Roberts / Hacking), figure strain explicitly open.

## 5. Pre-registered refuter (written before the conclusion)

Per Seat Map :287: "And a falsifier written before the run is a design instrument, not only an honesty device. Stating what failure looks like surfaces constraints the design does not, and the constraint arrives as a change to the experiment rather than as a note about it."

The answer-shape is refuted if any of the following holds:

1. **Isomer refuter.** Matched nonverbal tasks show zero relational difference between communities whose languages differ maximally in the relevant domain, with translation equivalence controlled and uncertainty manipulated. This kills F1.
2. **Convention refuter.** A naming convention is shown, with an independent ground, to change the physical facts that its category picks out with no instrument or practice mediating. This kills F2.
3. **Union refuter.** One lever, holding the other two conditions strictly constant, accounts for all observed category differences across at least three semantic domains. This kills F3 and turns the compound into a partition.
4. **Effect-term refuter.** A translation preserves every discrimination the source term licenses, verified in matched nonverbal tasks. This kills F4.
5. **Kernel refuter.** The shared-substrate claim and the variation claim are shown to be the same measurement under two descriptions. This kills F5's triangulation.

If any fires, the affected finding is withdrawn, not softened. Until one is run, no part of this is labelled `grounded`.

## 8. Strains and honest stop

- **Strain F7:** human-kinds looping effect versus "a convention that decides an outcome has stopped being one." Named, partially dissolved by Fig 12 :121, sign-reading left to a ground that does not yet exist. Not hidden.
- **No other strain after re-census.** The apparent Fig 10 :47 versus Fig 12 :121 conflict was a domain error: one governs conventions, the other governs cycles. Different objects.
- **Unreached, not unreachable.** The figures return a shape and a route. They do not report how much any given language or practice shapes any given category. That number needs a ground reading, and this document is a simulated derivation. "Unreachable is a verdict, not a silence" applies to the specific measurement, not to the whole question.
- **The six-term set is a visible guess.** From *They're Scanning Books When They Should Be Studying Nature*: "A minimum viable set of six terms is a human's careful guess at reality's compression axes; another sensorium's minimum set need not be those six." This solution does not smuggle the six in as an answer to a cross-cultural question; Fig 4 :20 is used only as the kernel/variable distinction.
