## Question restated as an effect

The residual is not "which language has which words." It is this: a continuous, variable stream of experience arrives, and different communities end up with different but overlapping systems of categories. What is being decided is whether that variation is a difference in the *arrangement* of a shared substrate, a difference in *labels* over a shared arrangement, or a difference in the substrate itself. Each option leads somewhere different:

- Arrangement differs: the claim is about bonds and relations, testable by non-verbal tasks (Fig 12 :117).
- Labels differ only: the claim is a convention, and agreement inside a speech community is the product, not evidence about the world (Fig 2 :41).
- Substrate differs: a new primitive would be owed, which the figures say cannot be named from inside the frame (Fig 9 :32).

An answer is bound as an ordinary term in `01_figure_grounded_audit.md` section 1, per the Formula's Direction rule, so it can accrue a residual like anything else.

## What is deliberately not here

- No ranking of languages, cultures, or communities by "how much" they shape thought. There is no anchor for such a number.
- No name of a specific category system as "the Tether answer." The framework returns a shape, not a name.
- No claim that any world source corroborates the figures independently where it shares an author or an evidence pool. Overlap is recorded in `10_source_ledger.csv`.
