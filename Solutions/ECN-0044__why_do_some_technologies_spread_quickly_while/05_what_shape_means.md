## 1. A shape is a constraint, not a filler

The canonical README states the limit: the framework returns "what a missing thing would have to look like (a shape, not a name)". Fig 9 :32: "you get a kind of thing, never the answer itself." Fig 13 :134: "Unreachable is a verdict here, not a silence,".

From *The Library of Babel*: "you can cut your search short if the set of books returned is small and descriptive enough that it provides a general shape of the answer that satisfies your question."

So a **shape** is the narrowed form of an answer you can state before you can name it. It constrains; it does not fill. For this question, "two conditions of a union, per-slot residuals, and a missing atom or a decayed channel" is a shape. "The missing thing is firm-specific tacit knowledge" or "the missing thing is complementary organisational capital" would be names, and the framework does not license either without a ground or a provenanced import.

## 2. How a shape is obtained

- *Library of Babel*: use the vocabulary already held that is closest to the thing, look for recurring new terms that overlap or correlate with it, recurse. "With each new term gained, you narrow the space and deepen your vocabulary." The essay also names the failure mode when a bundle is treated as one question: "Perhaps your problem was a bundle or conflation of several problems masquerading as one?" This is exactly the union split in F2 of `01_figure_grounded_audit.md`.
- Fig 6 :30: "The description travels. Fails by being sharpened into terms only we can read. Cast it in effect terms." Fig 9 :30-31: "your words are borrowed too, so describe the effect rather than the cause".
- *Decoding Tacit Knowledge*: "When contact isn't possible, the best you can send is a plain description of what you'd expect to see, because anyone can check that against the world." An effect description is the one message that survives without shared history.
- *Library of Babel* on stopping: "you will know that you are done because the finish line will also be the starting place". The shape is converged when further search returns nothing that can describe the answer better.

For this question the effect description is deliberately empty of technology names: what you would expect to see is a per-slot residual that stays live where a complement is absent or contact is lost, and a per-slot residual that trends down where the technology composes inside existing reach.

## 3. What licenses a name

Only two things, both outside the shape itself:

1. **The ground settles it.** `THE_FORMULA.md` step 5: "the ground settles it. Verified only by what pays. A gate is not the ground." A measured technology-context slot whose lag closes on a specific imported complement would license naming that complement.
2. **A provenanced import from a frame whose closure differs**, with a source gate and a shadow test (Fig 6 :23, Fig 8 provenance rule). A complement imported from the engineering or organisation frame is a name only if its provenance is recorded and it explains a gap the using frame could not close.

*They're Scanning Books When They Should Be Studying Nature* sharpens the point: "The real import is not a new term in the existing space. It is a new dimension of the space." Importing a term extends what can be said; importing an axis extends what can be perceived at all. A technology that needs a new axis, not a new term, is the hard case of "decades".

*Arrival of the Fittest* adds the honest caveat: because a metatheory "cannot be validated, nor can it validate itself", the framework's claim to return a shape is itself a candidate, not a verdict.

## 4. Why the aggregate reading is a lossy projection

*The Hard Problem is About Source* gives the mechanism. An instrument reads green and "cannot comment on the source": one green light, or yellow plus blue, or RGB, or CMYK, or a scheme not yet discovered. The reading does not determine the source. "If several paths create the same behavior, then behavior is a projection with lossy information". It was the spectrometer, not an argument, that resolved it.

The economy-wide productivity series is exactly such a projection. A flat aggregate can be produced by fast adoption with slow complementarity, by slow adoption with fast local payoff, by a decayed measurement channel, and by combinations of all three. Fig 1 :22: "Aggregating across slots is how a live signal disappears into an average." What is owed is a source-discriminating instrument at the per-slot level, not a better argument about the aggregate.

*How Intelligence Actually Works* restates the same discipline in terms of provenance: "indiscernibility is not identity". Two diffusion paths that read the same in an aggregate series are not the same path; the lineage has to be kept.

## 5. Capability, habitat and contact

*How a Society of Agents Would Work* states the figure line directly: "capability is a property of agent-and-habitat, never of the agent alone." Applied here, a technology's speed is a property of technology and using habitat, not of the technology alone. The same essay notes that "Only methods cross up. Only head starts come down", which is why this package sends the derivation and the refuter, not the conclusion.

*How Intelligence Actually Works* describes learning as "the emergent result of extensive mapping of a complex system against unknown and unforgiving environments", and names contact as the distinction resolver. The diffusion question is a contact question before it is a technology question.

## 6. Correction to my own reading

I initially read "shape, not a name" as a ban on describing any mechanism. That overstates it. The canonical phrase governs "what a **missing** thing would have to look like", that is, a primitive not held in the frame. The two conditions in this solution (a complement outside the adopter's closure, a channel that has decayed) are effect descriptions of what is missing or broken. They do not name a specific atom. They are inside what the framework may say.

What remains forbidden is naming the complement before a ground or a provenanced import licenses it, and treating the 45-year lag as figure-derived. Both earlier statements are preserved here rather than silently erased.

## 7. Prestige and the status of this derivation

From *The Recursive Self-Improvement Trap*: "Prestige can fund an action. It can give an action a budget to be tried. But it cannot settle an action. Only the ground can settle it." A campaign that gathers well-known diffusion economists to endorse the shape is the named collapse. The legitimate ask is a refutation against a ground the reader controls.

Same essay: "the truly testable output would need to be derived from the real architecture or working the formula yourself", and LLM outputs are "simulacra". Therefore `01_figure_grounded_audit.md`, `07_the_tether_answer.md` and `09_watson_answer.md` are simulated derivations checked for internal consistency against the figures. They are not tests of the claim and must be labelled as such. They cannot settle anything they report on.

## 8. What shape means, in one sentence for this question

A shape is the per-slot, two-condition constraint that any answer about diffusion speed and productivity lag must satisfy, stated before any complement is named, narrow enough to be refuted, and open at the slot where the framework cannot see the missing atom.
