## What an answer would look like (bound as an ordinary term)

Per `THE_FORMULA.md` step "Direction", the sought-for shape is written down and bound so it can accrue a residual like any other term. An answer here is a rule that, for a technology-in-context slot, says which of two conditions holds (the technology composes inside the adopter's existing reach with a live per-slot payoff and an intact channel, or it requires a new atom or a rebuilt channel), and states what would refute that classification. It returns a shape and a route, not a name and not a date.

## What is deliberately not here

- No personal emails of named individuals.
- No ranking of people by how likely they are to agree with the shape claim.
- No bulk-send plan.
- No claim that this run settled the question it reports on.

Three reasons, in the figures' own words:

1. **Agreement is not a verdict.** Fig 2 :35: "Alignment is triangulation, not negotiation. A mirror cannot correct you." Fig 7 :13: "agreement compounds, evidence does not".
2. **Selecting for receptiveness manufactures the disqualifying correlation.** Fig 2 :40: "disqualifying for a verdict: agreement about the world is worthless when the agreeing frames share an evidence pool". A list chosen for likely agreement is a list chosen to produce a convention.
3. **Sending the interior spends the reader's independence.** Fig 10 :42: "A verifier that can reconstruct the claim cannot verify it." The first contact carries the residual and its refuter, never the source.

Legally: scraping personal emails for unsolicited bulk outreach breaches GDPR and CAN-SPAM style rules in many jurisdictions and gets the sending domain blacklisted. That is a practical failure on top of the structural one.
