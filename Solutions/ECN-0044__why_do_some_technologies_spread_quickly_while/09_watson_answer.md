# WATSON answer: why do some technologies spread quickly while others take decades to affect economy-wide productivity?

**Mode: `specified` on the application, `general` on the structural rules, not `grounded`.** Figures are primary and decide. World sources are secondary, corroborate but never overrule, and a conflict returns the derivation to the domain census. This is a simulated derivation checked for internal consistency against the figures, not a ground reading. No measurement was taken by this run.

## QUESTION

Decide, in effect terms, what the Tether says about the pace of technology diffusion and the lag before economy-wide productivity responds. Option A: treat this as one hard question and answer with a single cause (technology quality, profitability, networks, or measurement). Option B: treat it as a union of two conditions with per-slot residuals, and answer with a shape that states which condition binds and what would refute it. What each leads to: A produces a single lever that clears the region where one condition holds and leaves the intersection standing, and it invites a room of agreeable readers whose agreement is not a verdict; B produces a checkable two-condition shape with written refuters and an open slot until a ground settles it.

Restated as an effect: R = |Γ(b,a) − o′| per technology-in-context slot s. The prediction is that an available technology with a positive advantage will be taken up and show in measured productivity; the observation is a mean adoption lag near 45 years (Comin and Hobijn 2010, world source) and a long aggregate visibility puzzle (Solow via Triplett 1999, world source). The residual is indexed per slot, never aggregated.

## DOMAIN CENSUS

R by arity, symmetry and scale, then one line per domain giving actors, currency and anchor, with partition or union and order by dependency.

- **R (arity / symmetry / scale).** Arity at least three: technology, using context, time, plus an instrument reading the ground. Carries an ordered bond (sequence), a production bond, a subtraction (the gap) and a comparison (advantage relative to the incumbent); not symmetric. Scale: single adopter loop, habitat, economy-wide measurement, and a lossy membrane between scales (Fig 4).
- **D1 Adopter decision loop.** Actors: using unit, its library of routines and skills, perceived advantage. Currency: reduction of per-slot R; the adoption action. Anchor: the using unit's own realized payoff. Admitted.
- **D2 Habitat and transmission field.** Actors: peers, channels, education, trade openness, standards, institutions. Currency: contact and reinforcement. Anchor: the ground, never agreement; the habitat supplies actors. Admitted.
- **D3 The technology's own reach.** Actors: artifact atoms and bonds, complements, infrastructure, skills. Currency: closure and the cost of stating a term. Anchor: settlement against the ground. Admitted.
- **D4 Economy-wide measurement channel.** Actors: agencies, metrics, sector boundaries, instruments. Currency: contact with the ground; the channel decays. Anchor: the ground, which does not decay. Admitted.
- **X1 Technology quality as free-floating property.** No anchor nameable (good relative to what, read where). Not a domain yet; absorbed into D1 and D3.
- **X2 Time itself as cause.** Time is a state, not an agent. Not a domain; absorbed into ordered bonds and D4.
- **X3 More research as a lever.** No anchor; agreement is not an anchor. Admitted only as a budget filter, never a verdict.

Union, not partition: D1/D3 and D2/D4 form overlapping compounds, and the whole is a union of C1 (spread) and C2 (visibility) (Fig 9). Dependency order: perception, vocabulary, objective, planning, learn and carry (Fig 3). The middle link, terms for what a technology affords and what counts as progress, is the one with no instrument.

Strain test applied: Fig 11 capability (adopter and habitat) and Fig 6 reach (what composes from what) govern different objects, so no strain after re-census. One live strain remains: the figures do not order a decayed channel relative to a genuinely absent complement inside the union. It is named, not smoothed.

## VERDICT (from the figures)

The figure-derived verdict: the question is a union of two conditions kept separate and never averaged. Spread is quick when the technology composes inside the adopter's existing reach, the per-slot advantage has positive discriminable mass, and the habitat and channel are intact; it is slow when a complement must be imported from a differing closure. Economy-wide visibility is a second condition: the ground does not decay but the channel does, so a long measured lag can be a channel failure, a missing complement, or both as a compound. Which complement and how many years is a declared `gap`. Mode `specified`, ungrounded, candidate. The world sources corroborate the shape from low-overlap fields; the economics pool of sources 1 to 3 counts as one witness.

- **Cost if wrong:** misdirected diagnosis and a wasted refuter. A slow slot failing D3 would be sold channel instruments, or a slot failing D4 would be told to import complements, and a single lever would be applied to a union that needs two. Fig 2 :42 "Buy independence in proportion to the cost of being wrong." The cost is bounded and measurable per slot, and is avoidable by leading with the union split and the refuters.
- **Undoable:** yes. No term is deleted if the claim fails; a refutation is added and the reach falls because "something was added rather than because anything was deleted" (Fig 6 amendment). The shape and its falsifiers are recorded with provenance so a later reader can locate the error (Fig 10 :62-64) rather than only feel it.
- **Reversed by:** any of the seven pre-registered refuters in `01_figure_grounded_audit.md` §4, in particular (1) an averaged statistic beating a per-slot residual on the same ground, (2) one lever clearing both conditions with no categorically different residue, (3) isolated adopters matching embedded adopters, (4) the ground moving with channel instruments unchanged and no import, or (5) a figure line naming the missing complement. A genuinely disjoint world evidence pool showing that per-slot persistence is fully predicted by averaged industry effects would also reverse it. Until one fires, the verdict stands as `candidate`.
