## What the wider literature actually says

1. **The lag is real and large.** Comin and Hobijn (2010): "on average, countries have adopted technologies 45 years after their invention", with at least 25 percent of per-capita income differences accounted for by adoption variation. This is a measurement of the question, not a derivation from the figures.
2. **The lag is habitat-shaped.** Comin and Hobijn (NBER 12314): adoption lags are "largely determined by lack of educational attainment and lack of trade openness". Centola and Macy: "Complex contagions require social affirmation from multiple sources", with unproven technologies as the example. Beck et al. show at the animal stratum that the learning rule, not just network position, decides who adopts first.
3. **The advantage has to pay locally.** Griliches (1957) explains regional differences in origins and slopes by profitability tied to market density and cost. The Rogers review reports that relative advantage, compatibility, trialability and observability raise adoption speed and complexity lowers it.
4. **Visibility at the aggregate is a separate problem.** Triplett's Brookings review lists the electricity-lag analogy as one of seven live explanations for the Solow paradox. Fig 10 governs that reading; the figures do not decide which of the seven is true in a given dataset.
5. **The logistic is a filter, not a law.** Comin, Hobijn and Rovito find technologies "do not diffuse in a logistic way" once the intensive margin is measured. Use the S-curve to budget attention, never to hand a verdict.

## The two-stage offer

- **Stage 1 before stage 2.** The reader checks the classification against their own ground without holding the framework interior. Their agreement is not collected as evidence and is never pooled with other replies.
- **Stage 2 is opt-in by construction.** Once a reader has absorbed the theory, their agreement stops being evidence. Fig 2 :40: "disqualifying for a verdict: agreement about the world is worthless when the agreeing frames share an evidence pool".
- **Stage 3 sends a method, which is permitted.** The formula is reusable; each user must write their own ground and refuter before running it.
- **Hard precondition.** Stages 1 to 3 only work if the stage-1 output survives. F1 and F2 in `01_figure_grounded_audit.md` are the load-bearing findings. If the union split fails, stop.
