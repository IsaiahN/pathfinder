## 1. The claim, in one paragraph

The question "could apparent acceleration arise partly from how cosmic structure affects observations?" is a union of at least three overlapping conditions: the dynamical averaging (backreaction) channel, the light-propagation and observer-location channel, and the inference and modeling channel. They must be unbundled before any one is searched, and the dimming residual must be tracked per slot (per averaging domain, per line of sight, per probe) before it is aggregated, because aggregation is how a live signal disappears. The rival explanations are isomers of one observational reading: the same dimming arranged as global dynamics, as line-of-sight propagation, or as a pipeline artifact. No figure line determines the magnitude, so the figure-level verdict is `gap`; a source-discriminating instrument must settle it. What is offered is the shape any answer must have, falsifiers written in advance, and the instruments that would close it.

## 2. Three checkable sub-claims

### C1 - framework-internal; checkable by reading Figure 9

- **Statement:** the question is a union compound, not a single hard question, and must be unbundled.
- **Source, verbatim:** Fig 9 :54 - "And when the split lands on a union rather than a partition, unbundle before you search again." - **World-facing check:** is the compound claim faithful? Kainulainen and Marra (2009, arXiv:0906.3871) combine a local void with a meatball lensing model so that "Together these two elements ... allow the model to mimic the concordance model." That is one object with overlapping conditions, not a list.
- **Refuter:** demonstrate a single mechanism that clears all structure-related residuals with no categorical remainder; or show the three channels draw on the same evidence by construction, so the union is one frame wearing three names.

### C2 - framework-internal; checkable by reading Figure 1

- **Statement:** the residual is per slot, and aggregation is how a live signal disappears. Applied here, the averaging domain and the line of sight are part of the measured quantity.
- **Source, verbatim:** Fig 1 :19 - "The residual is indexed by object slot, not aggregated." Fig 1 :22 - "Aggregating across slots is how a live signal disappears into an average." Fig 1 :20 - "a global residual near zero with one live slot is a legal state and not an inert one."
- **Important limit:** these lines govern a learning system's per-object residual indexing. Their use here is a `general` structural analogue, not a derivation of the Einstein equations. Reading them as physics is the strain the audit names and re-censes in F2.
- **World-facing check:** Paranjape and Singh (2008, arXiv:0801.1546) find that averaging can give acceleration "via a selective choice of averaging domains" but "the acceleration is not present when more generic domains are used for averaging." Does the domain dependence survive a gauge-invariant treatment? Verweg, Jones and van de Weygaert (2023, arXiv:2310.19451): "spatial averaging can induce artificial, i.e. gauge dependent, backreaction terms that arise from any specific choice of gauge."
- **Refuter:** show the relevant residual is exactly additive across slots and domains, so aggregation is information preserving; or show that domain and gauge choices can be removed without changing the deceleration parameter.
