# Outreach draft: check a shape claim before deciding about the theory

**Use:** one to one, or as a cover letter attached to a submission through the public venues in `03_public_contact_points.csv`. **Not** for bulk send.

**Hard precondition:** do not send until the union claim and the per-slot analogue in `01_figure_grounded_audit.md` F1 and F2 are understood as `specified` and `general` respectively. If the first-stage output does not survive, there is nothing else to look at.

---

**Subject:** One shape claim about structure and the supernova dimming, with the refuter written in advance

> Hi [name],
>
> I have one specific output I would like you to check against what you already know. I am not
> asking you to read a theory first, and I am not asking you to agree with anything. If the
> output does not survive, there is nothing else to look at.
>
> **The residual (`specified`):** distant Type Ia supernovae appear dimmer than a smooth
> matter-only prediction allows. That dimming is read as accelerated expansion. The observation
> passes through a clumpy universe. The effect-level question is whether part of the gap is
> produced by structure rather than by a smooth component, and whether that part is a real
> change in the global expansion or a change in the inference. I am not assuming the answer.
>
> **The shape claim:** this is a union of at least three overlapping conditions, not one hard
> question. They are the dynamical averaging or backreaction channel, the light-propagation and
> observer-location channel, and the inference or modeling channel. A single lever that clears
> one leaves the intersections standing. Separately, aggregation can hide a live per-slot signal:
> the choice of averaging domain and the choice of line of sight are not bookkeeping; they are
> part of the quantity being measured. The figures say "And when the split lands on a union
> rather than a partition, unbundle before you search again" and "Aggregating across slots is
> how a live signal disappears into an average." Those are statements about the shape of the
> inquiry, not a cosmology result.
>
> **What I am explicitly not claiming:** that backreaction is large enough to remove the need
> for dark energy; that any particular averaging scheme is gauge invariant; that lensing changes
> the global expansion; that the figures derive a number. No figure line governs the magnitude.
> I record that as `gap`, not as an answer. The literature is genuinely split on it. A figure
> returns a kind of thing, never the answer itself.
>
> **What would refute the shape claim (written before this message):**
> 1. A single mechanism clears all structure-related residuals with no categorical remainder,
>    so the union unbundling is unnecessary.
> 2. The relevant residual is exactly additive across slots and domains, so aggregation is
>    information preserving and the per-slot warning does not apply.
> 3. A figure line is produced that directly governs the magnitude, or a ground reading settles
>    it, which would end the `gap` and move the claim out of this shape.
>
> If any holds, I retract the shape. A one-line "this fails because..." is more useful to me
> than agreement. If you would rather not engage, no reply is needed.
>
> **Stage 2, only if you are curious and only after your verdict on stage 1 is recorded:** the
> sources are below. The figures, not my summary of them, are the authority.
> - Verbatim figure text with line numbers (local extract): `FRAMEWORK/reference/03-FIGURES_TEXT.md`
> - Canonical usage rule (local copy): `FRAMEWORK/reference/FIGURES_README_canonical.md`
> - The union and invariance papers that bear on this: arXiv:1109.2314, arXiv:2310.19451,
>   arXiv:1002.1232, arXiv:0801.1546
>
> **Stage 3, if you want to reuse the machinery:** the formula is in the figures. Run it on a
> problem you know well and can check against a ground. Write down what would refute your result
> *before* you run it, and state the mode of what you get. A reported failure is more useful to
> me than a reported success.
>
> Thanks,
> [name]

## Distribution constraints

- One to one. No bulk send, no automation, no misleading subject line, an opt-out line in every message.
- Record the stage-1 verdict before any theory is shown. Later agreement is never pooled as corroboration (Fig 2 :40 "agreement about the world is worthless when the agreeing frames share an evidence pool").
- Selection of recipients is by ground-visible public work and a public opt-in channel, never by receptiveness. Fig 9 :60: "Use filters for the budget and witnesses for the verdict. Never let a filter hand you a verdict."
