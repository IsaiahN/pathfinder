## 2. Where this literature agrees and where it does not

- **Agreement, one pool.** Amendola et al. and Kainulainen/Marra both use supernova likelihoods and overlap in authors. Their agreement that lensing and voids shift fitted parameters is one witness, not two (Fig 2 :40: "agreement about the world is worthless when the agreeing frames share an evidence pool").
- **Conflict, real.** Buchert and Räsänen keep the backreaction channel open; Kasai et al. and Rosenthal/Flanagan constrain or oppose it; Clarkson et al. call it unanswered; Verweg et al. show gauge dependence in existing averaging schemes. Per the WATSON rule a conflict sends us back to the census, never flips a figure verdict. The census predicted a union of live conditions, so the conflict is expected.
- **Shared ground.** Every one of these papers ranges against observations (CMB, BAO, supernovae, sims), which is why disagreement is informative rather than mere noise. The ground does not update on which frame is right (Fig 2 :29, "An anchor is whatever settles the question and does not change its answer because of what any frame thinks").

## 3. Distribution: offer, do not push

Figure-grounded reasons:

- **Fig 4 :22-25** "Only methods cross up. Only head starts come down. A recording carried upward looks like knowledge and is a description of one occasion." Send the shape claim and its refuter first; offer the links after a verdict is recorded.
- **Fig 10 :42** "A verifier that can reconstruct the claim cannot verify it." A reader who has absorbed the full argument before being asked for a stage-1 verdict has joined the frame being checked.
- **Fig 9 :27-28** "describe it before you go looking, or any frame you pick will seem to fit." Ask what they would expect to see if a structure contribution were present before giving them the papers.
- **Fig 9 :60** "Use filters for the budget and witnesses for the verdict. Never let a filter hand you a verdict." Recipient selection is by public ground-visible work and opt-in channels, never by likely agreement.
- **Fig 2 :35** "Alignment is triangulation, not negotiation. A mirror cannot correct you." Do not ask for endorsement.

**Order in a reply:** (1) the residual and the shape claim; (2) the pre-registered refuter and the question "what would you count as a failure?"; (3) only after their stage-1 verdict is recorded, the links, with the note that the figures, not the summary, are the authority; (4) only if they ask, the formula as a method with their own ground and refuter.

## 4. The two-stage offer (three-stage with the method)

1. **Stage 1:** validate the output against the ground. The recipient checks the shape claim without holding the framework's interior.
2. **Stage 2, opt-in:** if curious, read the theory that produced it. Their later agreement is never pooled as corroboration.
3. **Stage 3:** reuse the formula on other problems, each with a ground and a refuter written in advance.

This matches the exemplar's two-stage offer plus its method stage, adapted to a question where the figure-level verdict is `gap` and the physics is contested.
