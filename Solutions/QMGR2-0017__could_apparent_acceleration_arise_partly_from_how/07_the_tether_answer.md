# The Tether's answer on QMGR2-0017

**Mode: `general` on structural shape, `specified` on the union and per-slot predictions, `gap` on the physics magnitude. Not `grounded`.** Simulated derivation checked against the figures (`05_what_shape_means.md` section 7), not a ground reading.

## 1. The residual it answers

Distant Type Ia supernovae appear dimmer than a smooth matter-only prediction allows. That dimming is read as accelerated expansion. The observation passes through a clumpy universe. The question asks whether part of the gap arises from how structure affects observations.

The residual is **per slot**, not global: each supernova, line of sight, averaging domain and probe carries its own gap. Fig 1 :19: "The residual is indexed by object slot, not aggregated." Fig 1 :22: "Aggregating across slots is how a live signal disappears into an average."

The question is a **union compound**, not one hard problem. Fig 9 :54: "And when the split lands on a union rather than a partition, unbundle before you search again." Fig 9 :57: "a case in two conditions is not a harder case of either. It stops being a mixture and becomes a specific compound." The overlapping conditions are the dynamical averaging channel, the light-propagation and observer-location channel, and the inference and modeling channel.

## 2. The shape

1. **Unbundle before answering.** A local void is simultaneously a dynamical, a propagation and an observer-location effect. Kainulainen and Marra (2009) model the compound: a local void plus a meatball lensing model "allow the model to mimic the concordance model." A single lever that clears one channel leaves the intersections standing.
2. **Track the residual per slot before aggregating.** The averaging domain and the line of sight are not bookkeeping; they are part of the measured quantity. Paranjape and Singh (2008) find that averaging can yield acceleration "via a selective choice of averaging domains" but not "when more generic domains are used for averaging." That is the per-slot warning at the ground.
3. **The anchor is observation, not agreement among modelers.** Fig 2 :29: "An anchor is whatever settles the question and does not change its answer because of what any frame thinks." Fig 2 :40: "agreement about the world is worthless when the agreeing frames share an evidence pool." Supernova groups sharing a compilation and pipeline are one witness.
4. **The rival explanations are isomers of one reading.** Same observational atoms (dimming, redshift, structure), different arrangement, different substance: global dynamics, line-of-sight propagation, or pipeline artifact. Fig 12 :117: "isomer. Same atoms, different bond, different substance. The difference is invisible in the formula and decisive in the behaviour." The Hubble-diagram reading alone cannot discriminate, exactly as a green reading cannot comment on its source (*The Hard Problem is About Source*).
5. **The magnitude is a `gap` at the figure level.** After a genuine re-census, no figure line determines the size of a backreaction term or a lensing bias. Fig 13 :134: "Unreachable is a verdict here, not a silence," and Fig 8 :39: "So a search that finds nothing is never a proof of absence." The framework returns a shape, never the answer itself (Fig 9 :32).

## 3. What it explicitly does not say

- Not that backreaction is large enough to remove dark energy, and not that it is negligible. The figures do not decide magnitude.
- Not that any particular averaging scheme (Buchert's, Verweg's gauge-invariant proposal, or another) is correct. Those are ground questions.
- Not that lensing changes the global expansion. It changes the inference, which is a different substance.
- Not a derivation from the six-term substrate. Fig 1's per-slot line is an epistemic analogue, `general`, not a GR result.
- Not `grounded`. No measurement settled any claim here.

## 4. What would settle it, the ground

A source-discriminating instrument, not a further argument. From *The Hard Problem is About Source*: "The answer is unreached until a better instrument exists"; the spectrometer, not an argument, resolved the metamerism.

Concrete instruments already in play:

- **Gauge and domain robustness.** Does an averaged backreaction term survive a gauge-invariant, domain-generic calculation? Verweg, Jones and van de Weygaert (2023) show that "spatial averaging can induce artificial, i.e. gauge dependent, backreaction terms." Kasai, Asada and Futamase (2006) state "No cosmic acceleration occurs as a result of the nonlinear backreaction via averaging." A settled result either way closes part of the union.
- **Cross-probe curvature constraint.** Rosenthal and Flanagan (2008) require an average curvature "difficult to reconcile with observations of the location of the first Doppler peak of the CMBR." A measurement that moves the first peak independently of the averaging scheme is a discriminator.
- **Magnification in the likelihood.** Amendola et al. (2010): lensing "can move the best-fit model significantly towards the cosmic concordance of the flat LCDM model." Putting the magnification distribution into the likelihood and checking whether the shift persists with an independent supernova set tests the observational channel.
- **Observer-location tests.** A local void predicts specific sky-dependent signatures; kinematic SZ and BAO can test them. If they are absent, that condition is cleared and the union shrinks.
- **Standing falsifiers from the audit:** show the union fails (one mechanism clears all residuals); show aggregation is information preserving; produce a figure line that governs magnitude.

## 5. The honest stop

**Unreached, not unreachable.** The figure-level verdict is `gap`; the ground-level literature is genuinely split (Buchert and Räsänen keep the channel open, Kasai et al. and Rosenthal/Flanagan constrain it, Clarkson et al. call it unanswered). The answer stops at the limit of the available instruments and records the lineage so the stop is checkable. "Indiscernibility is not identity" (canonical README, local). A failure to find a figure line is not proof that the magnitude is zero, and a `gap` is not a silence.
