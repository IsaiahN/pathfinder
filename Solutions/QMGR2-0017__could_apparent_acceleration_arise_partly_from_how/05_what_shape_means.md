## 4. Why observation is the hard part, not the theory

*The Hard Problem is About Source* (read 2026-11-23) gives the mechanism that matters most for a question phrased as "how cosmic structure affects observations":

> "If several paths create the same behavior, then behavior is a projection with lossy information (it cannot define consciousness in full)."

The metamerism case: an instrument reads green and "it cannot comment on the source." Several sources (one green light, yellow plus blue, RGB, CMYK, a scheme not yet discovered) give the same reading. The spectrometer, not an argument, resolved it. The essay's rule: "The answer is unreached until a better instrument exists." And: "An instrument that can't distinguish these cases demonstrates its own inadequacy, it cannot claim anything substantial about what is being measured or what causes it."

This is exactly the structure of QMGR2-0017. The supernova Hubble diagram is a projection. A smooth accelerating component, a backreaction term, a local void and a lensing bias can in principle produce overlapping readings. The reading does not determine the source. What is owed is a discriminator, not a name. Rosenthal and Flanagan's use of the CMB first Doppler peak (04, arXiv:0809.2107) is an example of a discriminator: it ranges one candidate against a different anchor.

Fig 11 :48: "You do not invent the list. You read it off the world, and what you cannot perceive or measure yet is the residual." The list of structure effects is enumerated from the world, not composed from a preferred theory.

## 6. Why the magnitude stays `gap`

*They're Scanning Books*: "The real import is not a new term in the existing space. It is a new dimension of the space." Importing a new axis extends what can be perceived at all; importing a term only extends what can be said. No figure line here supplies a new term for the magnitude, so no import has occurred. The magnitude is owed to the instruments.

*The Recursive Self-Improvement Trap*: "You may all agree and be wrong. You may set your clock to someone else's, who was setting your clock to yours, and be off by many deviations over time. You need ground truth to define where you stand." This is why agreement among groups using the same supernova compilation is not an answer to a `gap`.

## 7. Prestige is not action, and this audit is a simulation

*The Recursive Self-Improvement Trap*: "Prestige can fund an action. It can give an action a budget to be tried. But it cannot settle an action. Only the ground can settle it. The Ground Maintainer must be blind to prestige." A campaign that gathers well-known names to endorse a preferred cosmology is the named collapse, not corroboration. The legitimate ask is a budget to try the shape against a ground.

Same essay, on standing: "LLMs can quickly simulate an approximate answer/output ... However, the truly testable output would need to be derived from the real architecture or working the formula yourself." So `01_figure_grounded_audit.md` and this file are simulated derivations checked for internal consistency against the figures. They are not tests of the cosmology and must be labelled as such. They cannot settle anything they report on.
