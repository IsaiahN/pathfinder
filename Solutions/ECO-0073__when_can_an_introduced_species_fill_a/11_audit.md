## Round trip gap R_T

The Symbols table defines R_T as `gap(x, (T_E ∘ T_A)(x))`, the difference between what was sent up and what came back down. Fig 4 :30 requires measuring it rather than assuming it zero. I ran the round trip on the condensation this folder actually uses.

1. **Sent up (x):** the verbatim figure set, chiefly Fig 11 :39-40/:43-54/:61-73, Fig 9 :54-57, Fig 1 :19/:22 and Fig 8 :39.
2. **Abstraction (T_A):** the shorthand in `07` and `09`: union compound, valence relative to residual, capability agent-and-habitat, cascades third order, asymmetry, ground settles it.
3. **Concretisation (T_E):** a concrete decision, for example "introduce species X into reserve Y to restore pollination Z."

**R_T is nonzero.** Three things the shorthand holds do not survive the trip back down:

- **which slot is measured.** The shorthand says "per slot"; a concrete release invites a single net-impact score. Fig 1 :19/:22 is dropped.
- **which residual the valence is relative to.** The shorthand says "relative to the residual"; a concrete release invites naming the species as good or bad. Fig 11 :49-50 is dropped.
- **the order of enumeration before introduction.** The shorthand requires the habitat to be read first; a concrete release invites choosing the species first and checking impacts afterwards. Fig 11 :43-48 and Fig 9 :27-28 are dropped.

Conversely, one thing survives cleanly: the act with the cascade enumerated is a different act from the same addition with the cascade unenumerated (Fig 11 :65). That is the residue worth sending. R_T was **not** measured numerically, because no ground reading exists; it is recorded qualitatively as the enumerated set of dropped constraints. Treating "the summary is the figures" would set R_T to zero and would be the dead-reckoning error Fig 4 :30 names. The honest form is a lossy condensation with a measured gap, which is what `09_watson_answer.md` states.
