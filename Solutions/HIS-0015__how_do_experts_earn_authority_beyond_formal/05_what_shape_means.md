## 1. A shape is a constraint, not a filler

The figures README states the limit: the framework returns "what a missing thing would have to look
like (a shape, not a name)." Fig 9 :32: "you get a kind of thing, never the answer itself"
Fig 13 :134: "Unreachable is a verdict here, not a silence," and the figure adds that it cannot say
which primitive is missing, because naming it would mean already holding it.

From *The Library of Babel*: "You can cut your search short if the set of books returned is small and
descriptive enough that it provides a general shape of the answer that satisfies your question."

So a **shape** is the narrowed form of an answer you can state before you can name the answer. It
constrains; it does not fill. For this question:

- Shape: "authority is a per-slot, provenance-bearing, independently checkable record of contact with
  a non-updating ground, recognized retrospectively."
- Name: "this person is an authority." The framework does not return the name.
- Anti-shape: "authority is whatever the institution grants." That is a frame-internal score, not a
  shape, because it has no anchor outside the granting frame (Fig 1 :27).

The shape also refuses a tempting shortcut. It does not say training is useless. It says training and
titles are not the settling quantity; contact and its record are (Fig 11 :40).

## 2. How the shape is obtained

- *Library of Babel*: use the vocabulary you already have that is closest to the thing, look for
  recurring new terms that overlap with it, recurse. "With each new term gained, you narrow the space
  and deepen your vocabulary." The essay calls this a "bootstrapping convergence process."
- *Library of Babel* on steering: the returned books "need to be tested or ranged against the problem
  you want answered in order to steer the direction of the next search." The question is the tether.
- Fig 9 :27-28: "describe it before you go looking, or any frame you pick will seem to fit." Fig 6
  :30: "The description travels. Fails by being sharpened into terms only we can read. Cast it in
  effect terms."
- *Decoding Tacit Knowledge*: "When contact isn't possible, the best you can send is a plain
  description of what you'd expect to see, because anyone can check that against the world." This is
  the reason the outreach packet asks what the recipient would expect to see if the shape claim were
  true, rather than sending the framework first.
- *Library of Babel* on stopping: "you will know that you are done because the finish line will also
  be the starting place", and the paradox is that by then you have "composed the answer through the
  process of locating it."

Applied here: the residual was described in effect terms (standing versus ground contact) before any
field was chosen. If world sources had been picked first, any discipline would have seemed to fit.

## 3. What licenses a name

Only two things, both outside the shape itself:

1. **The ground settles it.** Formula step 5: "the ground settles it. Verified only by what pays."
   The README: the framework "stops at the limit of what the available instruments can distinguish,
   and records the lineage so the stop is honest."
2. **A provenanced import from a frame whose closure differs** (Fig 6 :23; Fig 8 :51), with a source
   gate and a shadow test. Even then it is an import with a provenance record, not a derivation from
   nothing.

*The Hard Problem is About Source* sharpens why an instrument, not an argument, is required. An
instrument that reads green "cannot comment on the source": one green light, or yellow plus blue, or
RGB, or CMYK. "If several paths create the same behavior, then behavior is a projection with lossy
information." "An instrument that can't distinguish these cases demonstrates its own inadequacy."
The resolution came "not by argument, but by the arrival of a better instrument", the spectrometer.

Applied here: a title, a citation count and a well-earned reputation can produce the same reading,
"this person is trusted". The reading cannot comment on the source. What is owed is a
source-discriminating instrument: a pre-registered prediction scored at a non-updating ground. Until
that instrument exists, naming the source of the reading is the metamerism error.

## 4. Why provenance is part of the shape, not decoration

Fig 8 :51: "Convergent derivation and adopted import look identical in the contents. Only the record
of where each came from separates them"
Fig 10 :62: "A ruling carries provenance like an atom."
Fig 10 :65: "Provenance shows a ruling was reached legitimately. It never shows it was right: the
ground does that"

Fig 12 :107 adds the cost side: "Settling compresses the description and not what it cost to
establish." A title compresses a lineage into a word. The work that built it does not travel with the
word. *Decoding Tacit Knowledge* makes the same point in human terms: Polanyi's "we can know more than
we can tell", and Harry Collins's three kinds of tacit knowledge (relational, somatic, collective).
A compressed judgment from an expert unpacks only for a listener with the right history. That is why
the shape includes a record and an effect description: the record travels, the history does not.

## 5. Q — Does "shape, not a name" forbid saying anything about authority at all?

**No.** The README's phrase governs a *missing* thing: "what a missing thing would have to look like
(a shape, not a name)." Here the missing thing is the check that would distinguish earned standing
from credentialled standing. What is owed is a description of that check. Saying "a title is not the
settling quantity" is a constraint, not a name. Naming a particular person as authoritative would be
a name, and is refused.

This is a correction to a possible overreading. Fig 1 :27 does not ban the word "authority"; it bans
a frame from scoring itself with a quantity it also produces. The word is usable when it is bound to
a ground and a slot.

## 6. Two consequences for outreach

**Prestige is not action.** From *The Recursive Self-Improvement Trap*: "Prestige is not equal to
actions." and "Prestige must never be allowed to validate an action directly." And: "Prestige can
fund an action. It can give an action a budget to be tried. But it cannot settle an action. Only the
ground can settle it. The Ground Maintainer must be blind to prestige." The essay names the exact
failure this question is about: "someone let prestige stand in for proof. Someone let reputation
substitute for result. Someone let the trusted name validate the untested claim." Therefore the ask
gives the shape a budget to be tried. It does not ask for endorsement, and it never selects readers
for likely agreement.

**Drift is the standing hazard.** *Decoding Tacit Knowledge*: "Long, close contact pulls the frames
together, so the process that makes communication easy slowly uses up that extra reach." A community
of experts who only check against each other drift together and do not notice. The safeguards named
there match the figures: independent replication, two-way contact with correction, and courts that
rule on evidence neither side can move. Fig 2 :45: "Frames range against the ground. They do not
negotiate with it."
