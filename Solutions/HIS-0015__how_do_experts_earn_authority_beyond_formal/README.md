## What is deliberately not here

- No personal emails of named individuals.
- No ranking of people by how likely they are to agree.
- No bulk-send plan.
- No claim that this derivation has settled the question.

Three reasons, in the figures' own words:

1. **A room does not become an anchor.** Fig 2 :35: "Alignment is triangulation, not negotiation. A mirror cannot correct you." A list of people selected for likely agreement manufactures the correlation that would make their agreement worthless.
2. **Agreement is not evidence when the pool is shared.** Fig 2 :40: "disqualifying for a verdict: agreement about the world is worthless when the agreeing frames share an evidence pool." This whole folder is one simulated seat reading one evidence pool.
3. **Do not show the interior before the verdict.** Fig 10 :42: "A verifier that can reconstruct the claim cannot verify it." Stage 1 therefore carries the residual and its refuter, not the framework.

The direct institutional addresses on society pages are generic office mailboxes, not personal emails of
named experts. The CSV records only public contact and submission pages and tells the reader to use the
public route. No message is sent by this agent.
