## QUESTION

Decide, in effect terms, what earns a person authority beyond formal training and titles. Option A:
authority is a possession conferred in advance by credentials and titles, so checking it means
checking the record. Option B: authority is a per-slot relation among a judgment, a ground that does
not move with opinion, independent checkers and a recorded lineage, recognized retrospectively.
What each leads to: A gives a frame-internal score with no anchor and a prestige loop in which a name
validates an untested claim. B gives a checkable shape, written refuters, and an open slot until a
ground settles it.

## DOMAIN CENSUS

R by arity, symmetry and scale, then one line per domain with actors / currency / anchor, partition
or union, and order by dependency.

- **R (the residual).** The gap between standing accorded and judgments that actually pay at a
  non-updating ground. Arity: four conditions bundled (credential, ground, check, lineage), so a
  union compound, not one hard question. Symmetry: a comparison of a claim against a ground, not a
  symmetric join between two peers. Scale: first order in one slot, second order in a recognized
  lineage. Fig 9 :56: a case in several conditions stops being a mixture and becomes a specific
  compound.
- **Domain 1, credential and institution.** Actors: institutions, departments, licensing boards.
  Currency: credentials, titles, prestige. Anchor: the issuer's own standard, which moves when the
  frame moves, so it fails the anchor test and is a currency, not an anchor. Verdict: cannot settle.
- **Domain 2, subject-matter ground.** Actors: measurements, experiments, datasets, markets, patients,
  boards. Currency: residuals settled or not. Anchor: the ground, pinned. Verdict: the only
  legitimate anchor.
- **Domain 3, verification community.** Actors: independent peer frames. Currency: agreement and
  residuals. Anchor: the ground, never agreement. Verdict: corroborates only at low overlap.
- **Domain 4, lineage and history.** Actors: lineages of work, provenance records. Currency: standing
  and provenance. Anchor: the ground, retrospectively. Verdict: authority is a retrospective label
  and standing is a declared stake.
- **Partition or union.** Union compound. The four domains overlap in one biography, so each gets its
  own verdict; they are never averaged into one authority score.
- **Order by dependency.** Ground and residual, then claim, then independent check, then recognized
  lineage, then credential. Effort spent on a credential cannot repair a break upstream.

Strain test: Fig 1 :27 governs quantities a frame produces about itself, not retrospective
recognition at a ground. Fig 10 :61 governs the checker's stake, not the legitimacy of the person
checked. Both were re-censed; no verdict flipped.

## VERDICT (from the figures)

Authority beyond training and titles is not a possession and not a global score. It is a per-slot
relation: a judgment checked at a ground that does not move with opinion, by checkers whose evidence
pools differ from the claim's, with a recorded route and standing declared as a stake. Recognition is
retrospective and attached to a lineage. Credentials fund a trial but cannot settle a claim. The
framework returns this shape, not a name, and the claim is `specified`, ungrounded and candidate
until a pre-registered prediction is scored against a fixed ground.

- **cost if wrong:** bounded and locatable. The packet states mode, refuters and provenance before the
  conclusion, so an error is findable. The real avoidable cost is spending a reader's independence by
  sending a recording instead of a generator. Fig 2 :42 "Buy independence in proportion to the cost
  of being wrong." That cost is avoided by leading with the claim and its refuter.
- **undoable:** yes. The framing is removable without touching the derivation. A wrong claim about
  authority can be superseded by a corrected packet, and no primitive is withdrawn. A public claim
  that a title settles a question can be retracted cleanly because the refuter is already written.
- **reversed by:** a ground reading, not agreement. A pre-registered prediction test showing that
  title-holders and non-holders with ground-contact records settle equally would break F1 and F3. A
  demonstration that the anchor moves with community opinion would break F2. A global authority score
  that works across unrelated slots would break F4. Evidence that a declared stake does not change
  refusal rates would break F7. Until one of these fires, the verdict stands as `candidate`.
