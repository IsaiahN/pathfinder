| Offer the formula as a method | Fig 12 :104: "A settled molecule becomes an atom for whatever composes over it." The generator can be given away without giving away an answer. |

## Stage order

1. **Stage 1:** send the claim, its slot, and the four refuters. Record the verdict before showing any
   theory. Ask what the recipient would count as a failure.
2. **Stage 2, opt-in only:** if curious, offer the three links. Do not pool later agreement as
   corroboration.
3. **Stage 3:** offer the formula as a method to reuse, with the instruction that each user writes
   their own refuter and states their own mode.

Nothing in this file asks anyone to agree, to trust, or to lend a name. A name lent to a claim is a
prestige transfer, which the figures price as a fund for a trial, never as a settlement.
