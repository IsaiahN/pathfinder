## Two things in the wider literature that bear directly on this question

1. **An apparent conflict that survives reading.** Elor, Houtz, Ipek and Ulloa (2024) is titled "The Standard Model CP Violation is Enough". A reader stopping at the title would conclude that new physics is not needed. The abstract then introduces a morphon field and dark sector dynamics, which are new degrees of freedom. So the paper contests the specific claim that CKM CP violation is too small, while corroborating the shape claim that an import is owed. Cite it as a correction to any simplistic "the Standard Model cannot do it" sentence, not as a refutation of the import verdict.
2. **Two independent grounds for the number.** The Snowmass white paper quotes eta = (6.10 +/- 0.4) x 10^-10 and notes BBN and CMB agreement. Steigman and Scherrer (2018) note that eta is diluted by photon production during cooling, so eta and n_B/s are related by a fixed factor in the late universe. The residual is a joint particle-physics and cosmology product, not a single-experiment number (Fig 9 :54-58).
