## VERDICT

The shape survives; the specific option A does not. Eta is one live slot in an otherwise successful frame, no mechanism predicting it is in the Standard Model closure at this budget, and an import is therefore owed to particle physics. That import must have a five part shape whose first three conditions bind simultaneously as one union compound. The framework does not name the import, and the import it speaks of is frame access, not the creation of a world primitive. Whether new physics exists in nature is not settled here: that is an instrument question. The specific claim that new physics explains the asymmetry is a `specified`, ungrounded inference about a frame's reach, not a `grounded` answer. C and CP violation and non-equilibrium are bindings onto held vocabulary, and broken baryon-number symmetry is a declared figure gap. This is a `candidate` verdict. The strongest outreach point is the stop: no last room, so no candidate is final, and agreement is never a substitute for the ground.

- **cost if wrong:** bounded and locatable, not invisible. The packet is mode labeled, states refuters before the conclusion and records the lineage, so an error can be found rather than absorbed. The real cost is spending the independence of outside readers by sending the framework interior before the shape claim, which Fig 2 :42 prices (Buy independence in proportion to the cost of being wrong.). That cost is avoidable by leading with the residual and its refuter.
- **undoable:** yes. The bindings can be withdrawn without touching the derivation, and the specific model names were never asserted. A sent claim can be superseded by a corrected packet, and no primitive has to be withdrawn. If a Standard Model only calculation of eta were confirmed by BBN and the CMB, the import verdict would be withdrawn and the shape claim restated for a different residual.
- **reversed by:** a ground reading, not agreement. A nonzero EDM, a Higgs coupling deviation, a gravitational-wave spectrum from a first order transition, a collider signature for a new state or a primordial non-Gaussianity signal would name or kill a frame. A Standard Model only calculation of eta confirmed by both BBN and the CMB would kill the import verdict outright. A confirmed mechanism with one Sakharov condition absent would kill the union compound. A figure re-census that derives a term for broken baryon-number symmetry, or a demonstrated frame with no outside left to import from, would break the structural claims. Until one of these fires, the verdict stands as `candidate`.
