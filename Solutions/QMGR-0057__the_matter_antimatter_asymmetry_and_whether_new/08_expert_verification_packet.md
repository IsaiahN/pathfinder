## 1. The claim, in one paragraph

The observed baryon asymmetry, eta about 6.1 x 10^-10 measured independently by BBN and the CMB, is one live slot in an otherwise spectacularly successful frame. The Standard Model plus the standard hot thermal history does not produce it. A closed frame cannot generate a quantity its atoms do not reach, and only an import from a frame whose closure differs moves that boundary, so the framework owes an import to particle physics. It does not name the import and does not claim that the framework has discovered new physics. The import must have a five part shape: baryon number violation, C and CP violation, operation out of thermal equilibrium, eta inside the measured band, and at least one signature an instrument can refuse. The first three are the Sakharov conditions and bind simultaneously as one compound condition, not three independent problems. Whether such a primitive exists in nature is for an instrument to decide.
