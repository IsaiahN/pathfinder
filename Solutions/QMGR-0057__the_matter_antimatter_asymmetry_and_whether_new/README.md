## Restatement in effect terms

The observed universe carries roughly one extra baryon for every 1.6 billion photons, eta about 6.1 x 10^-10, measured independently by Big Bang nucleosynthesis and by the cosmic microwave background. The Standard Model plus the standard hot thermal history predicts essentially a symmetric universe after annihilation. Option A says: new physics explains the asymmetry, full stop. Option B says: the Standard Model frame is closed with respect to this quantity, so an import is owed; the framework can state the shape that import must have and the instruments that would settle it, but it cannot name the responsible model or assert that new physics exists in the world. This solution derives B and records precisely where it stops.

## What is deliberately not here

- No personal emails of named individuals.
- No ranking of people by how likely they are to agree that new physics is the answer.
- No bulk-send plan.

Three reasons, in the framework's own words:

1. **A room does not upgrade the label.** "A room does not upgrade the label. A room reaching a conclusion without the ground is agreement, and agreement among frames is a weighted average of influence. Adding people raises the vocabulary and does not add an anchor." (Seat Map :283). A list of people selected for believing in a favourite baryogenesis model produces a convention, not corroboration.
2. **Correlation disqualifies a verdict.** "High correlation is disqualifying for a verdict and efficient for a convention." (Seat Map :163). Agreement among reviews that share one evidence pool is one witness, not several (Fig 2 :40).
3. **Asking for trust before checking spends independence.** "A verifier that can reconstruct the claim cannot verify it." (Fig 10 :42). The first contact carries the residual and its refuter, never the framework's interiors.

Legally, scraping personal emails for unsolicited bulk outreach breaches GDPR and CAN-SPAM style rules in many jurisdictions. All venues in `03` are public, institutional and opt-in.
