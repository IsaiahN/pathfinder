## 4. What would settle it: the ground, not a further argument

A source-discriminating instrument, not a preference among elegant models. Observing eta alone reads one number and cannot name its source, the way a reading of green cannot say whether it came from blue plus yellow, RGB or CMYK (Isaiah N, How Intelligence Actually Works, 2026, quoted in `10_source_ledger.csv`). The discriminators are:

- **BBN and CMB** for the value of eta, the anchor both instruments already hold;
- **electric dipole moments** for new CP violation, already returning null results that constrain low scale frameworks;
- **Higgs coupling fits** for an extended Higgs sector;
- **gravitational-wave backgrounds** for a first-order phase transition or for dark sector dynamics;
- **collider searches** for new heavy states and mesogenesis signatures;
- **primordial non-Gaussianity and neutrino properties** for the CPT symmetric frame.
