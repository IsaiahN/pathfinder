## 1. A shape is a constraint, not a filler

The canonical README states the limit: the framework returns "what a missing thing would have to look like (a shape, not a name)". Fig 9 :32: "you get a kind of thing, never the answer itself". Fig 13: "It cannot say which primitive is missing, because naming it would mean already holding it."

From *The Library of Babel*: "You can cut your search short if the set of books returned is small and descriptive enough that it provides a general shape of the answer that satisfies your question."

For this question a **shape** is the five-part bound, not a model:

- a process that changes baryon number;
- a process that violates C and CP;
- a process that runs out of thermal equilibrium;
- a predicted eta in the measured band;
- at least one signature a real instrument can refuse.

Each item is a constraint. None names a heavy neutrino, a new Higgs doublet, an X boson or a morphon field. "A mechanism with these five properties" is a shape. "Leptogenesis with M_N = 10^10 GeV" is a name.

## 2. How a shape is obtained

- *Library of Babel*: use the vocabulary already held that is closest to the thing, then look for recurring new terms that overlap or correlate with it. "With each new term gained, you narrow the space and deepen your vocabulary." Here the held vocabulary is the six substrate terms and the eight operators, and the recurring physics terms are Sakharov's conditions, sphalerons, the Jarlskog invariant, the phase transition order and EDM limits.
- Fig 9 :30-31: "your words are borrowed too, so describe the effect rather than the cause". So describe "one extra baryon per 1.6 billion photons, a process that treats matter and antimatter differently while out of equilibrium", not "a right-handed neutrino decays CP asymmetrically".
- *Decoding Tacit Knowledge*: "When contact isn't possible, the best you can send is a plain description of what you'd expect to see, because anyone can check that against the world." This is exactly why the outreach in `02` leads with refuters rather than with the framework.
- *Library of Babel* on stopping: the answer is reached when "the finish line will also be the starting place", and the paradox is that by then you have "composed the answer through the process of locating it."

## 3. What licenses a name

Only two things, both outside the shape itself:

1. **The ground settles it.** Formula step 5: "the ground settles it. Verified only by what pays. A gate is not the ground." The README says the framework "stops at the limit of what the available instruments can distinguish, and records the lineage so the stop is honest." Here the ground is BBN plus CMB for eta, and EDM, Higgs, gravitational-wave and collider outcomes for the mechanism.
2. **A provenanced import from a frame whose closure differs** (Fig 6 :23, Fig 8 :50-52), with a source gate and a shadow test. A candidate baryogenesis frame imports new degrees of freedom; its provenance is the chain of measurements and calculations that led to it, not its elegance.

*They're Scanning Books When They Should Be Studying Nature* sharpens this: "The real import is not a new term in the existing space. It is a new dimension of the space." And: "A minimum viable set of six terms is a human's careful guess at reality's compression axes; another sensorium's minimum set need not be those six." Also: "Declaring the primitives does not make them neutral. It makes the choice visible."

So the six are a visible guess. In this question they do not themselves contain baryon number, lepton number or a gauge symmetry, so the framework cannot derive the Sakharov conditions from them. It can only say that a successful answer has a shape and that the frame must reach it.

## 4. Why a single reading cannot name its source

*The Hard Problem is About Source* gives the mechanism. A color instrument reads green and "cannot comment on the source": one green light, or yellow plus blue, or RGB, or CMYK, or a scheme not yet discovered. "If several paths create the same behavior, then behavior is a projection with lossy information." It was the spectrometer, not an argument, that resolved it. "An instrument that can't distinguish these cases demonstrates its own inadequacy."

Applied here: observing eta = 6.1 x 10^-10 is a reading of one number. Many mechanisms can produce that same reading, including leptogenesis, electroweak baryogenesis, GUT decays, mesogenesis and a CPT-symmetric initial condition. The reading does not determine the source. What is owed is a source-discriminating instrument (EDM, Higgs coupling, gravitational waves, collider signatures, primordial non-Gaussianity), not a preferred name.

*The Recursive Self-Improvement Trap*: "the output is only as good as the validator. The agent, or the tool, or the frame, or the theory, cannot validate itself." A baryogenesis model that fits eta by construction is not validated by fitting eta.

## 5. Q: Can the framework say which new physics explains the asymmetry? ANSWERED

**No.** The framework can say:

- that eta is a live slot the Standard Model frame does not close (Fig 1 :13, :19);
- that a closing term must have the five-part shape (Fig 9 :32);
- that the three Sakharov conditions bind as a union compound (Fig 9 :54-58);
- that the frame must import from a closure that differs (Fig 6 :23);
- that only an instrument can choose among the frames (Fig 9 :42).

It cannot say which frame is right. A name is licensed by the ground or by a provenanced import with a shadow test, and neither has fired for this question. The claim "new physics explains it" is therefore a `specified`, ungrounded inference about a frame's reach, not a `grounded` answer about nature.

## 5b. Correction to my own §1 to §4

I initially wrote the gap in §4 as if the framework's inability to name a source implied that no source could be named. That overstates *The Hard Problem is About Source*, which says "The answer is unreached until a better instrument exists", and explicitly allows that a trace may be recoverable when the right instrument appears (the mined versus lab-grown diamond case). The correct claim is narrower: eta is below the current union horizon as a **source discriminator**, not permanently unknowable. "Being below the union horizon is never a settled verdict, only a report that nobody has reached it yet." So the gap is `unreached`, not `unreachable`, and the instruments in §3 are how it closes.

A second narrowing: the README's "shape, not a name" governs a **missing** primitive. Naming an atom already held by the six (for example Gradient for a directedness) is a binding with provenance, not the invention of a new primitive. What remains forbidden here is asserting that the figures select a specific baryogenesis model, because every such model imports degrees of freedom genuinely outside the Standard Model closure.

Both the original and the narrowed reading are kept here so the correction is visible.

## 6. Two consequences for this question

**Prestige is not action.** From *The Recursive Self-Improvement Trap*: "Prestige must never be allowed to validate an action directly... Prestige can fund an action. It can give an action a budget to be tried. But it cannot settle an action. Only the ground can settle it. The Ground Maintainer must be blind to prestige." The fact that the Snowmass white paper has many distinguished authors, or that a model is widely discussed, gives it a budget to be tried. It does not make the model true. A failed prediction is worth more than an endorsement.

**My own audit is a simulation, not a ground reading.** Same essay: "LLMs can quickly simulate an approximate answer/output with the formula found in the architecture post" and "the truly testable output would need to be derived from the real architecture or working the formula yourself." *Arrival of the Fittest: The Tether Architecture* says outputs "could never be verified if the solutions presented were not contrived to fit the formula." So `01_figure_grounded_audit.md` is a simulated derivation checked for internal consistency against the figures. It is **not** a test of baryogenesis and must be labelled as such. It cannot settle anything it reports on.
