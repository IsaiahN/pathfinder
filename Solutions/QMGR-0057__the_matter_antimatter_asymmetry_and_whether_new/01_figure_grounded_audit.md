## 0. The question under audit, in effect terms

The question as written: "The matter-antimatter asymmetry and whether new physics explains it" (source line 73).

Stated as an effect so it can be ranged rather than believed:

- **The observation `o'`.** eta = (n_B - n_antiB)/n_gamma is about 6.1 x 10^-10. Two independent instruments return it: Big Bang nucleosynthesis (deuterium abundance in metal-poor absorbers) and the cosmic microwave background (odd/even acoustic peak heights, Planck 2018). Gilly Elor et al., *New Ideas in Baryogenesis: A Snowmass White Paper* (2022), write: "The Standard Model of Particle Physics cannot explain the observed baryon asymmetry of the Universe. This observation is a clear sign of new physics beyond the Standard Model."
- **The prediction `Gamma_SM(b,a)`.** Standard Model particle content plus the standard hot thermal history gives a nearly symmetric universe. Gary Steigman and Robert J. Scherrer (2018) put the symmetric relic at about 10^-18, nine to ten orders below the observed value.
- **Option A:** assert "new physics explains it" as a settled answer and name a mechanism.
- **Option B:** keep the residual, show that the Standard Model closure cannot close it, state the shape any successful mechanism must have, name no mechanism without a ground settlement, and point to the instruments that would decide.

What an answer would look like, bound as an ordinary term before the run (Formula "Direction"): a mechanism that (i) violates baryon number, (ii) violates C and CP, (iii) operates out of thermal equilibrium, (iv) predicts eta within the measured band, and (v) carries at least one signature an existing or upcoming instrument can refuse. Conditions (i) to (iii) are the Sakharov conditions (Sakharov 1967; van de Vis, de Vries and Postma 2025/2026, section 1.1.1).

The shape claim is written down now so it can accrue a residual like anything else. Fig 5 :65: "make the sought-for shape an ORDINARY TERM".

## 1. The residual, described before any figure line is chosen

`R_s = |Gamma(b,a) - o'|` on the slot `baryon asymmetry`. Fig 1 :19: "The residual is indexed by object slot, not aggregated." Fig 1 :13: "a global R near zero with one live slot residual is a legal state." Symbols table :38: R "is the aim, not only the error signal, and it is always a slice: what cannot be perceived yet is residual and simply cannot be read."

- **Arity.** One live slot inside an otherwise spectacularly successful global frame. The Standard Model predicts many observables to high precision and fails this one by nine to ten orders of magnitude. Aggregating would hide it. Fig 1 :22: "Aggregating across slots is how a live signal disappears into an average."
- **Symmetry.** The observable is a comparison between two quantities, baryons and antibaryons, measured against a photon count. Operators table :51 defines comparison as "a threshold relation between two quantities." The comparison is about which is larger, not about both being present. The theoretical condition that lets a difference appear is the violation of a symmetry (C and CP), not the presence of an ingredient.
- **Scale.** First order inside a particle-physics frame (a decay, a phase transition, a sphaleron process in the early plasma). Second order at the union with the cosmological thermal history: the expansion rate sets whether equilibrium holds. Steigman and Scherrer (2018) note that eta is diluted by photon production as the universe cools, so eta and entropy-per-baryon are related by a known factor; the residual is a joint particle-physics and cosmology product.
- **Partition or union.** The three Sakharov conditions are not three independent questions. A candidate mechanism must satisfy all three at once. Fig 9 :54: "And when the split lands on a union rather than a partition, unbundle before you search again." Fig 9 :57: "a case in two conditions is not a harder case of either." They form a **union compound** with its own loop and its own ground. Clearing one condition does not close the residual.
- **Dependency order (Fig 3).** (1) Settle eta at BBN and CMB (perception). (2) Have terms for what early-universe processes afford (vocabulary). (3) Say what would count as progress, namely a mechanism predicting eta in the measured band (objective). (4) Route: verdict on closure, lookup of a frame, narrowing by instruments (planning). (5) Carry the settled term, with provenance, for the next question (learn and carry). Fig 3 :19: "Optimising a link that already works cannot rescue one that does not." More collider hours do not repair a missing vocabulary link.

## 5. Pre-registered refuter (written before the conclusion)

Required before any conclusion is drawn. Seat Map :287: "And a falsifier written before the run is a design instrument, not only an honesty device." Fig 5 :65: "make the sought-for shape an ORDINARY TERM".

The shape claim of section 0 is refuted, or moved, if any of the following fires:

1. **Closure refuter.** A calculation using only Standard Model degrees of freedom produces eta in the measured band, confirmed by both BBN and CMB. This kills F2 and the import verdict. Note the check that was actually run: Elor, Houtz, Ipek and Ulloa (2024), "The Standard Model CP Violation is Enough," claims the observed asymmetry can be generated using only Standard Model CP violation, but adds "a morphon field generates present day mass contributions" and dark sector dynamics. It therefore does **not** fire this refuter; it imports an atom under another name. The paper is a genuine apparent conflict with the consensus and is treated as such in `09_watson_answer.md`.
2. **Union refuter.** A confirmed mechanism produces the observed eta with one Sakharov condition demonstrably absent (for example a CPT-violating channel). This kills F3.
3. **Ground refuter.** eta is measured as zero, symmetric universe, within BBN and CMB precision. The residual goes to zero and the whole question dissolves. It has not happened: both instruments return about 6.1 x 10^-10.
4. **Instrument refuter.** All frames that predict the five-part shape are excluded by EDM, Higgs, gravitational-wave and collider results to the point where no candidate remains reachable at this budget. That is `unreachable-at-this-budget`, not `underivable`; the remedy is more search or a different frame, not a new atom (Fig 8 :40-43).
5. **Figure refuter.** A figure re-census derives a term for broken baryon-number symmetry from the six without import, closing the F7 gap, or shows that import adds a world primitive, falsifying F6.

Until one of these is run, no part of this solution may be labelled `grounded`.
