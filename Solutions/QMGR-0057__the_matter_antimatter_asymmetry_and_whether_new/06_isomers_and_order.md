# Isomers and order: baryogenesis candidates as bonded molecules

**Mode: `specified` on the structural claims, `general` on the composition rules, not `grounded`.** This is a simulated derivation checked for internal consistency against the figures, not a ground reading (see `05_what_shape_means.md` section 6). No measurement of a baryogenesis signature was taken in this run.

## RESIDUAL

Files `01` to `05` fix a five-part shape: a process that changes baryon number, a process that violates C and CP, operation out of thermal equilibrium, a prediction of eta inside the measured band, and at least one signature an instrument can refuse. This file asks three further questions about that shape.

1. Can a candidate mechanism be written as a bonded molecule over an atom set, so that the figure vocabulary for structure applies to it?
2. Are the candidate mechanisms **isomers** under the figure definition, or only members of one functional group?
3. What is the **order**, both inside a candidate (how the conditions bind) and in the inquiry (which question is asked first)?

A falsifier is registered in the section before the conclusion, as required by Seat Map :287: an interest in an outcome is unavoidable, but a falsifier written before the run is a design instrument.

## DOMAIN CENSUS (short form, full version in 01 section 2)

- **Molecule frame.** Actors: atoms and the eight operators. Currency: composition cost in bits and settlement cost in actions. Anchor: the figures for legality, the ground for settlement.
- **Particle frame.** Actors: quarks, leptons, Higgs and gauge fields, the CKM phase, sphalerons, plus imported heavy neutrinos, extended Higgs sectors, GUT bosons, a dark meson sector or a CPT anti-universe. Currency: rates and cross sections. Anchor: experiment.
- **Ground frame.** Actors: BBN, CMB, EDM, Higgs coupling fits, gravitational-wave detectors, colliders. Currency: exclusion limits and signals. Anchor: the instrument outcome, which does not move with persuasion.
- **Union, not partition.** A candidate can occupy more than one frame at once, so the Sakharov conditions are one union compound with three binding conditions, not three independent problems (Fig 9 :54 "And when the split lands on a union rather than a partition, unbundle before you search again.").

## DERIVATION

### Q1. The three conditions are bond requirements, not atoms

Fig 9 :55 "A bundled problem is usually a union of overlapping conditions, not a list of separate ones." Fig 9 :57 "a case in two conditions is not a harder case of either."

A candidate is a molecule. The three Sakharov conditions constrain the **typed relations** in that molecule, not the ingredient list:

- **Baryon number violation.** Fig 12 :77 "A produces B; B did not exist before" (production, ⇒, ordered). A process creates a B carrying state that was absent before it fired. This is why adding a new decay channel is not the same as revealing an existing one.
- **C and CP violation.** Operators :55 "this does not hold", Operators :58 "a refutation is a negation, which is why a vocabulary without it can only accumulate". CP violation resembles the refusal of an invariance claim. It is a **binding** from the physics frame onto a held operator, not a derivation: negation is unary and joins nothing (Fig 12 :49 "Negation is the eighth operator and is not a bond").
- **Departure from thermal equilibrium.** Fig 13 :22 "a difference that can be spent". An expanding plasma supplies directedness. Also a **binding**, with the strain named in `01` F7: a structure (gradient) is not a state of a system (non-equilibrium plasma).

All three hold at once. Clearing one leaves the residual live. **Mode:** `general` for the union structure, `specified` for the binding to these three conditions. **Status:** triangulated at low overlap between Fig 9's union rule and the physics derivation (Sakharov 1967; van de Vis, de Vries and Postma 2025/2026, section 1.1.1, as recorded in `01` and `04`). **Declared gap:** no figure line governs broken baryon-number symmetry. Fig 8 :39 "So a search that finds nothing is never a proof of absence." The gap is unavailability in the figure vocabulary, not a physical verdict.

### Q2. Isomers or a functional group?

Fig 12 :117 "isomer. Same atoms, different bond, different substance. The difference is invisible in the formula and decisive in the behaviour." Fig 12 :124 "functional group. A substructure whose presence fixes the outcome. The group says what kind; the qualifier says which member." Fig 12 :95-96 "Two entries with an identical ingredient set are not variants of one thing. They are different things whose notation cannot tell them apart, and the difference is carried entirely by the bond."

Write each candidate over the atom set it imports. The atoms differ by frame: a heavy Majorana neutrino for leptogenesis, an extended Higgs sector for electroweak baryogenesis, a GUT boson for GUT baryogenesis, a dark meson sector for mesogenesis, an anti-universe for the CPT symmetric model. Each candidate imports genuinely new degrees of freedom, so none is a mere rebinding inside the Standard Model closure.

- **As the candidates are normally written,** they share only the Sakharov bond pattern and differ in atoms. By Fig 12's definition they are **not isomers**; they are members of one **functional group**. The group says what kind (baryogenesis), the qualifier says which member (leptogenesis, mesogenesis, and so on).
- **The stronger, checkable claim:** hold the **full shared atom set** (the six substrate terms plus the imported particle frame atoms) and vary only which structure mediates which state pair and which operator carries the refusal. Then the candidates are isomers: same atoms, different bond, different substance. Fig 13 :121 "Different routes can arrive at the same substance." The converse also holds, per Fig 12 :95-96, so two candidates with the same written ingredients can be inverses in behaviour.

**Mode:** `specified`, ungrounded. **Status:** single-source for the figure definition (Fig 12), with the physics-facing check unmeasured. **Falsifier, written before this run:** if one candidate requires an atom that no other candidate can express, the isomer claim fails and the functional-group reading stands. A second falsifier: if two candidates are shown to have identical bond typings over the same atoms, they are one term wearing two names, not isomers (Fig 8 :50-52 on convergent derivation and adopted import looking identical in the contents).

### Q3. Order: inside a candidate, then in the inquiry

**Inside a candidate**, the three conditions are simultaneous, not a sequence of stages in time. Reading them as a chain (first B violation, then CP, then freeze out) is the error of treating a union as a chain: a mechanism with two of the three does not close the residual. The figure order is one of **composition**, not of chronology. Where a true sequence appears, it is a bond: Fig 12 :77 "A produces B; B did not exist before" (⇒); Operators :49 "a threshold relation between two quantities" (⋛, ordered); Fig 12 :49 negation is not a bond at all.

**In the inquiry**, the order is fixed by dependency, not by interest. Fig 3 :19 "Optimising a link that already works cannot rescue one that does not." Fig 9 :6 "the verdict: is φ* in here at all?" Fig 9 :7 "the lookup: which frame holds it?" Fig 9 :8 "the narrowing: which one is it?"

1. Settle eta at BBN and CMB (perception).
2. Close the Standard Model frame: no mechanism predicting eta is in the closure (verdict).
3. Describe the residual in effect terms **before** looking for a frame: Fig 9 :27-28 "describe it before you go looking, or any frame you pick will seem to fit." Otherwise every candidate from leptogenesis to the CPT symmetric universe will seem to fit.
4. Look up frames whose closure predicts the five part shape (lookup), then narrow by EDM, Higgs, gravitational wave and collider cuts, keeping each cut ranked and reversible: Fig 9 :41 "so keep every cut ranked and reversible".
5. Let the ground decide between the survivors: Fig 9 :42 "narrowing gets you close, the ground decides". Fig 9 :60 "Use filters for the budget and witnesses for the verdict. Never let a filter hand you a verdict."

### Q4. Two currencies, not one score

Fig 12 :125 "A composition costs in two currencies, and they do not add." Fig 12 :126 "Bits price the description. Actions price finding out whether it holds."

A high scale leptogenesis model can be cheap to state and prohibitively expensive to test. A low scale electroweak baryogenesis model with a sharp EDM prediction is more expensive to describe and cheaper to refute. Neither price predicts the other (Fig 12 :128-129). Summing them into one figure of merit is the error the figure names, not the fix. Budget filters belong at the search stage; witnesses belong at the verdict stage (Fig 9 :60).

### Q5. Settling makes an atom, not a final room

Fig 12 :103 "Composition is recursive, and the level decides what is irreducible." Fig 12 :104 "A settled molecule becomes an atom for whatever composes over it. So irreducible is a fact about a level and never about the world." Fig 7 :6 "the limit: no last room".

If one candidate were confirmed, it would explain eta and immediately become an atom for the next residual: why that CP phase, why that mass scale, how it composes with neutrino masses and dark matter. The framework cannot license the word "final". A claim that some model is the answer, full stop, is the terminal room error (Fig 7 :13 "agreement compounds, evidence does not").

## FALSIFIER (pre-registered, written here before the conclusion)

This file's structural claims are refuted or moved if:

1. a confirmed mechanism produces the observed eta with one Sakharov condition demonstrably absent, which kills the union compound (Q1);
2. every candidate can be written over the same atom set with an identical bond typing, which kills the isomer claim and leaves one term with two names (Q2);
3. one candidate provably requires an atom no other candidate can express, which kills the isomer claim and confirms only the functional-group claim (Q2);
4. a single instrument closes the residual without any ground settlement of eta, which would make narrowing the verdict (Q3);
5. the two currencies are shown to be one figure in this domain, which would falsify Q4;
6. a frame with no outside left to import from is demonstrated, which would falsify Q5.

Until one of these fires, no structural claim above is `grounded`.
