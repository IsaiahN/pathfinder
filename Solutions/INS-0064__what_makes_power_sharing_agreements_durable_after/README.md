## Restating the question as an effect

The ordinary wording asks for a list of design features that make an agreement last. Restated in effect terms: a signed allocation of power (the prediction, "this settlement will hold") is compared against the continued distribution of power and the absence or return of organized violence (the observation). The residual is the per-group, per-slot gap between the written allocation and what the ground pays. What is being decided is whether durability is a property of the text, of the credibility of future conduct, or of the habitat into which the agreement is dropped. Each option leads to a different check: read the constitution, test the anchor, or enumerate the habitat. An answer would look like a shape with a refuter, not a ranked list of institutions, and that sought-for shape is bound as an ordinary term in `01` section 0 so it can accrue a residual like anything else.

## What is deliberately not here

- No personal emails of named scholars.
- No ranking of people by how likely they are to agree with the claim.
- No bulk-send plan.

Three reasons, in the figures' own words:

1. **A room does not upgrade the label.** A room reaching a conclusion without the ground is agreement, and agreement among frames is a weighted average of influence. Adding people raises the vocabulary and does not add an anchor (Seat Map :283).
2. **Selecting for receptiveness manufactures the disqualifying correlation.** "High correlation is disqualifying for a verdict and efficient for a convention." (Seat Map :163) A list of readers chosen because they already accept the shape claim is a convention, not corroboration.
3. **Asking for trust before a check spends the only thing an outside frame contributes.** "A verifier that can reconstruct the claim cannot verify it." (Fig 10 :42) The first contact therefore carries the residual and its refuter, never the framework's interiors.

Legally, unsolicited bulk email to harvested addresses breaches GDPR and CAN-SPAM style rules in many jurisdictions. That is a practical failure on top of the structural one.
