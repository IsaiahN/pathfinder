## 1. A shape is a constraint, not a filler

The canonical rule: the framework returns "what a missing thing would have to look like (a shape, not a name)". Fig 9 :32: "you get a kind of thing, never the answer itself." Fig 13: "It can say the answer is out of reach with the library as it stands... It cannot say which primitive is missing, because naming it would mean already holding it."

From *The Library of Babel* (carried): "You can cut your search short if the set of books returned is small and descriptive enough that it provides a general shape of the answer that satisfies your question."

So a **shape** is the narrowed form of an answer you can state before you can name it. It constrains; it does not fill. For this question, "durability needs a non-updating anchor, a residual-relative habitat enumeration, and separate per-condition verdicts" is a shape. "Article 7(b) of the constitution guarantees the vice-presidency to the second group" is a name.

## 2. How a shape is obtained

- *Library of Babel* (carried): use the vocabulary already closest to the thing, look for recurring new terms that overlap with it, recurse. "With each new term gained, you narrow the space and deepen your vocabulary."
- Fig 6 :30: "The description travels. Fails by being sharpened into terms only we can read. Cast it in effect terms." Fig 9 :30: "your words are borrowed too, so describe the effect rather than the cause."
- *Decoding Tacit Knowledge* (carried): "When contact isn't possible, the best you can send is a plain description of what you'd expect to see, because anyone can check that against the world."
- *Library of Babel* (carried) on stopping: the answer is reached when "the finish line will also be the starting place", and the paradox is that by then you have "composed the answer through the process of locating it."

Here the bootstrap vocabulary is the political-science one actually found in the opened sources: institution versus practice, credible commitment, costly signal, shadow of the future, external guarantee, regime shift. The shape is obtained by describing the residual in those terms and then checking which figure lines govern them. The vocabulary is borrowed; the figure lines decide.

## 3. What licenses a name

Only two things, both outside the shape itself:

1. **The ground settles it.** Formula step 5: "the ground settles it. Verified only by what pays." The canonical README: the framework "stops at the limit of what the available instruments can distinguish, and records the lineage so the stop is honest."
2. **A provenanced import from a frame whose closure differs** (Fig 6 :23, Fig 8 :50-52), with a source gate and a shadow test.

*They're Scanning Books* (carried) sharpens this: "The real import is not a new term in the existing space. It is a new dimension of the space." Also: "A minimum viable set of six terms is a human's careful guess at reality's compression axes; another sensorium's minimum set need not be those six." And: "Declaring the primitives does not make them neutral. It makes the choice visible."

For this question, the list of possible institutions (veto, rotation, proportionality, autonomy, monitoring) is a visible human guess drawn from a long literature, not a discovery of nature's own list. Naming one as *the* durability mechanism turns a visible choice into an invisible one unless the binding is recorded as provenance.

## 4. Why a single durability coefficient commits the named error

*The Hard Problem is About Source* (opened 2026-11-23) gives the mechanism. Metamerism: an instrument reads green and "cannot comment on the source". One green light, or yellow plus blue, or RGB, or CMYK, or a scheme not yet discovered. The reading does not determine the source. "If several paths create the same behavior, then behavior is a projection with lossy information." And: "An instrument that can't distinguish these cases demonstrates its own inadequacy." It was the spectrometer, not an argument, that resolved it.

A country-level "peace lasted ten years" reading is the green reading here. Several sources could produce it: a genuinely credible commitment, a well-maintained external guarantee, sheer exhaustion of the parties, or a census that simply did not record the recurrence. Fig 1 :19: "The residual is indexed by object slot, not aggregated." Fig 1 :22: "Aggregating across slots is how a live signal disappears into an average." A single coefficient names the source from the projection. What is owed is a discriminator, not a number.

*The Recursive Self-Improvement Trap* (opened): "the output is only as good as the validator. The agent, or the tool, or the frame, or the theory, cannot validate itself." A durability study that codes both the institutions and the outcome from the same government report is a frame validating itself.

## 5. Q: What would license a particular institution in the slot?

**Answer: the same two things as anywhere else.** The ground settling a minted term, or a provenanced import with a shadow test. Nothing in the framework returns "veto" or "rotation" as the answer to a durability slot. A particular provision is licensed only by a measurement that pays for it under a declared code, or by an import whose origin is recorded. Until one of those, the name is the searcher's own contribution and must be recorded as such.

**Does the ordinary question commit the forbidden error?** Yes, in two ways. First, it asks for a standing list, which Fig 11 :51 structurally refuses: "No standing list of good actors survives a change of residual." Second, if the list is validated by the same source that wrote the agreement, it names the source from a lossy reading (section 4). The correct form remains a separator with a refuter, not a provision list.

## 5b. Correction to my own §3 and the first-pass audit (see `01` section 4 R2)

I overstated the "shape, not a name" rule. The canonical phrase governs a primitive **missing** from the library. Vetoes, rotation, proportionality and monitoring are already in the political-science library; naming one is a **binding** from that frame onto an available atom. It is provenance, not the invention of a new primitive and not an import that moves the wall (Fig 6 :21: "MINT composes inside the closure and can never add an atom"). The earlier claim that naming provisions was "forbidden" is retracted as applied to available atoms.

The §4 metamerism point is **not** retracted. The reason a single coefficient cannot license a provision is the missing discriminator, not a ban on naming. Both claims are recorded so neither is quietly lost.

## 6. What "shape" delivers here

The shape is a four-part separator with a falsifier, not a list:

1. an anchor that does not update on the parties' statements (Fig 2 :29, :33);
2. a residual-relative enumeration of the habitat rather than a standing list (Fig 11 :43, :49-51);
3. a union of conditions with per-domain verdicts, never averaged (Fig 9 :54-57, Fig 1 :19);
4. an import with a source gate and shadow test whenever an outside guarantor is invoked (Fig 6 :23, Fig 8 :50-52).

It also delivers what the figures **do not**: no named provision, no numerical durability threshold, no standing list. That is a declared `gap`, not an omission. Fig 13 :134: "Unreachable is a verdict here, not a silence."

## 7. Two consequences for outreach and for this document

**Prestige is not action.** From *The Recursive Self-Improvement Trap* (opened): "Prestige must never be allowed to validate an action directly... Prestige can fund an action. It can give an action a budget to be tried. But it cannot settle an action. Only the ground can settle it. The Ground Maintainer must be blind to prestige." A list of famous signatories or high-impact journals endorsing the shape is the named collapse, not corroboration. The legitimate ask is a budget to try the refuter against a ground.

**My own audit is a simulation, not a ground reading.** Same essay: "LLMs can quickly simulate an approximate answer/output with the formula... However, the truly testable output would need to be derived from the real architecture or working the formula yourself." LLM output is a simulacrum. So `01` is a simulated derivation checked for internal consistency against the figures. It is **not** a test of the claim and must be labelled as such. It cannot settle anything it reports on.
