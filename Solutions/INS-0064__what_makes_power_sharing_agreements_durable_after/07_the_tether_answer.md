# The Tether's answer: what makes power-sharing agreements durable after civil conflict

**Mode: `specified` on the three per-domain verdicts, `general` on the union shape, `gap` on the standing list and the numerical threshold. Not `grounded`.** This is a shape and a route, not a result. Simulated derivation checked against the figures (`05` section 7), not a ground reading.

## 1. The residual it answers

A signed power-sharing agreement predicts that an allocation will hold. The observation is what the ground pays: continued joint exercise of power or a return to organized violence. The gap is indexed per group and per condition, not pooled. Fig 1 :19: "The residual is indexed by object slot, not aggregated." Fig 1 :22: "Aggregating across slots is how a live signal disappears into an average." A global durability rate discards the live slot.

The question as posed is a **union compound**, not one hard question with opposite answers. Fig 9 :55-56: "A bundled problem is usually a union of overlapping conditions, not a list of separate ones. A single lever clears the region where only one condition holds and leaves the intersections standing, because the second condition still binds."

## 2. The shape, per domain, kept separate and never averaged

**D1, the agreement text.** A written allocation is a convention, not an anchor. Fig 10 :49: "Declaring a meaning does not ground it." Fig 10 :47: "a convention that decides an outcome has stopped being one." The text is useful because it can be violated (Fig 10 :19), which makes a failure statable. It cannot be the thing that settles whether the failure was repaired. Verdict: `specified`, triangulated (Fig 2 + Fig 10). Naming this as a verdict is not naming the text irrelevant.

**D2, credibility.** Durability needs an anchor that does not update on what the signatories say. Fig 2 :33: "The anchor must not update." Fig 2 :35: "Alignment is triangulation, not negotiation. A mirror cannot correct you." Observed recurrence and the post-settlement distribution of capacity are anchors; the parties' own compliance reports are readings through a channel. Fig 10 :24: "the ground does not decay, the channel does." Where the parties share a closure, the wall moves only through a frame whose closure differs (Fig 6 :23), imported with provenance and a shadow test. Verdict: `specified`, triangulated (Fig 2 + Fig 6 + Fig 10); world corroboration from strategic studies and game theory is secondary and shares one evidence pool.

**D3, the habitat.** Capability is agent-and-habitat. Fig 11 :39: "capability is a property of agent-and-habitat, never of the agent alone." Fig 11 :43: "The habitat is enumerated, never composed." Introduction is available and exclusion is not (Fig 11 :61), so a managed peace is permanent work rather than a solved state (Fig 11 :63-64). Verdict: `specified`, triangulated (Fig 11 + Fig 10 + Fig 6).

**Across domains.** No standing list of durability institutions is residual-independent. Fig 11 :51: "No standing list of good actors survives a change of residual." A mutual veto helps the group that fears exclusion and hurts the settlement that cannot decide. Valence flips with the residual. Over a fixed atom set, two settlements bonded by sequence versus by threshold are isomers, Fig 12 :117: "isomer. Same atoms, different bond, different substance. The difference is invisible in the formula and decisive in the behaviour." Verdict: `general` structural on the refusal, `specified` unmeasured on the isomer claim.

**The literal request.** Which specific provisions occupy the slots, and what numerical durability threshold counts as success, are a declared `gap`. Fig 9 :32: "you get a kind of thing, never the answer itself." Fig 13 :134: "Unreachable is a verdict here, not a silence." Fig 8 :39: "So a search that finds nothing is never a proof of absence."

## 3. What it explicitly does not say

- Not a best-practice checklist. A checklist is a recording of occasions, not a generator (Fig 4 :25).
- Not a pooled durability coefficient. The residual is per slot (Fig 1 :19).
- Not a claim that vetoes, rotation, proportionality or monitoring are *the* causes. Naming available atoms is a binding from the political-science frame, recorded as provenance; it is not a figure-derived name and not a wall-moving import.
- Not a theory that any one lever clears the union. One lever leaves the intersections standing (Fig 9 :55-56).
- Not a sign for the sharing-feedback cycle. Fig 12 :121 says the sign decides whether the loop closes; the figures do not supply it.
- Not `grounded`. No measurement at the ground was taken in this derivation.

## 4. What would settle it - the ground, not agreement

A source-discriminating instrument, not a further argument. *The Hard Problem is About Source* (opened): an instrument that reads green "cannot comment on the source"; the spectrometer resolved the case, not an argument. Here the corresponding questions are:

- **Anchor test:** does the parties' compliance reading move when a party pushes on it? If yes, it is not an anchor (Fig 2 :30).
- **Independence test:** are institution coding and recurrence read by frames that do not share an evidence pool? Fig 2 :31-32: "Independence, between two frames, is how little they draw on the same evidence. Two people reading the same report are not two frames; two instruments pointed at the same thing are."
- **Import test:** does an outside guarantor carry provenance and explain a gap the parties' own terms could not, or is it an undeclared second voice? Fig 8 :50-52.
- **Per-slot test:** does the failure show up in a particular group's residual before the country average moves (Fig 1 :19-22)?
- **Cycle-sign test:** does sharing lower fear and raise credibility in the measured case, or entrench identity and raise fear? The sign of the loop (Fig 12 :121) decides.
- **Isomer test:** do two settlements with the same atoms but sequence versus threshold bonding differ at the ground under independent readings? If not, the behavioural clause of Fig 12 :117 fails.

## 5. Standing falsifiers

The five written before the run in `01` section 5 are repeated here without editing: (1) text alone with no ground contact determines recurrence out of sample; (2) a purely verbal promise with no signal, no repetition and no outside reading prevents recurrence; (3) removing every monitor and guarantor leaves capability unchanged; (4) a provision list whose valence never flips with the residual; (5) an institution/outcome study coded from one source with no independent reading still predicts out of sample. Any firing narrows or withdraws the shape, in a dated entry.

## 6. The honest stop

**Unreached, not unreachable.** The political-science atom list is "a human's careful guess at reality's compression axes"; declaring it makes the choice visible, not neutral. The framework stops at the limit of what the available instruments distinguish and records the lineage. "Indiscernibility is not identity." The cycle sign and the provision slots stay open until a ground reading closes them. Agreement among readers of this packet is one evidence pool, not a witness (Fig 2 :40).

## 7. One-paragraph version for outreach

> The Tether does not return a ranked list of institutions that make power sharing last. It says the question is a union of three conditions with different anchors, not one lever. A signed allocation is a convention that makes violations statable but cannot ground its own durability; durability needs an anchor that does not move when a party pushes on it, and an imported outside reading with provenance; and capability lives in the habitat of monitors, guarantors and actors who did not sign, which can be added to but not subtracted from. No standing list of provisions survives a change of residual, because a veto helps the group that fears exclusion and hurts the state that cannot decide. Two settlements holding the same institutional atoms but bonded by sequence rather than by threshold are isomers, which is why atom-level coding finds mixed results. The specific provisions and the numerical threshold are a declared gap. What the framework offers is the shape any answer must have, five failure conditions written in advance, and the instruments that would close it.
