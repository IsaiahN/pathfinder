## 0. The residual in effect terms, and the shape bound as an ordinary term

**R (the residual).** A signed power-sharing agreement is a prediction: "the allocation written here will hold." The observation is what the ground pays: continued joint exercise of power, or a return to organized violence. The gap between them, per agreement and per signatory group, is R. It is not a single global residual. Fig 1 :19: "The residual is indexed by object slot, not aggregated." Fig 1 :22: "Aggregating across slots is how a live signal disappears into an average." A one-group fear of exclusion can be live while the country-level "durability score" looks fine.

**Arity.** At least three, one per binding condition found in the census below (text, credibility, habitat). Each has its own slot structure and its own ground contact.

**Symmetry.** "Durable" is an ordered threshold (a comparison ⋛ over time), not a symmetric join. The Operand test in Fig 12 :87 asks "is it about which is larger, rather than both being present? Yes." A durability figure is a threshold on elapsed peace, not a co-present ingredient.

**Scale.** First order inside one group's calculation (a guarantee lowers this group's fear); second order at the regime where groups' expectations feed one another (Fig 12 :104: "A settled molecule becomes an atom for whatever composes over it. So irreducible is a fact about a level and never about the world, and there is no bottom").

**The direction limit and its remedy.** A frame searching for "the list of durability institutions" holds that list as a preference, not as a term: it predicts nothing and accrues no residual, so no internal check can see it (Fig 5 :61-64; Formula "Direction"). The remedy is a discipline rather than a step: write down what an answer would look like, bind it, and let it be refused. The sought-for shape bound here is:

> **SHAPE (bound before search):** any answer must (i) name at least one anchor that does not update on what the signatories say, (ii) enumerate the habitat actors relative to a stated residual rather than carry a standing list, (iii) keep the verdicts for text, credibility and habitat separate, and (iv) state what measurement would reverse each verdict. Anything that returns only a list of provisions, or a single pooled durability coefficient, fails this shape.

That bound is an ordinary term now. It can be refused by the pre-registered refuter in section 5.

Fig 2 :30: "If it moves when you push on it, it is not an anchor." A signature moves when the signatories push on it. Fig 2 :34 names the failure: "If it does, panel 3 turns into panel 1: the anchor becomes another frame, everything drifts together, and the drift is coherent, which is why nobody notices." Seat Map :163: "A convention that decides an outcome has stopped being one." The written allocation is a convention that makes a bug statable; it is not the ground that settles whether the bug was fixed.

**Partition or union? Union.** Fig 9 :54: "And when the split lands on a union rather than a partition, unbundle before you search again." Fig 9 :55-56: "A bundled problem is usually a union of overlapping conditions, not a list of separate ones. A single lever clears the region where only one condition holds and leaves the intersections standing, because the second condition still binds." Fig 9 :57-58: "a case in two conditions is not a harder case of either. It stops being a mixture and becomes a specific compound." Text, credibility and habitat overlap at different cases. Adding a veto clears the case where only the text condition binds and leaves the case where credibility also binds standing. So there is no single "power-sharing works" verdict to average toward.

**Order by dependency (Fig 3).** Fig 3 :20: "The chain is ordered by dependency, not by importance. Each link consumes what the one above produces, so effort spent above the break arrives at a step that cannot use it." The order here is: (1) perception, can each group read the other's capacity and intent at all; (2) vocabulary, does the settlement have terms for what it affords (veto, rotation, monitoring); (3) objective, can it say what counts as progress (no recurrence, not merely signature); (4) planning; (5) learn and carry. The recurring break is between (2) and (3): ingredients are nameable while the progress measure is missing or is the agreement's own text. Fig 3 :27: "A link with no instrument is not a link that works. It is a link nobody has looked at." Seat Map :271 names the output: "Produced, recorded, and not read. A stream with a writer and no consumer" is "the one found last, because the evidence exists and so nothing looks missing."

## 3. Findings

### F1 - The agreement text is a convention, not an anchor (decisive for D1)

Fig 10 :49: "Declaring a meaning does not ground it." Fig 10 :47: "a convention that decides an outcome has stopped being one." Fig 2 :43: "Guard, from Figure 10: a convention that decides an outcome has stopped being one."

A constitution that allocates vetoes, ministries and police posts is a declaration of meaning about who may decide. It can be violated, which is what makes it a useful convention (Fig 10 :19: "→ install what can be violated"). But if the convention is treated as the thing that settles whether peace will hold, it has decided an outcome and stopped being a convention. The agreement's own monitoring report is a reading through the channel, not the ground (Fig 10 :24: "the ground does not decay, the channel does").

**Refuter:** show a written allocation that, with no contact with any non-updating ground and no external frame, determines recurrence out of sample. Equivalently, show a case where the signatories' own compliance report is a non-updating anchor. If either holds, F1 is refused.

**Status:** `specified`, triangulated across Fig 2 (anchor rule) and Fig 10 (convention/channel rule), two figures with low overlap between them.

### F2 - Credibility requires an anchor that does not update (governs D2)

Fig 2 :33: "The anchor must not update." Fig 2 :35: "Alignment is triangulation, not negotiation. A mirror cannot correct you." Fig 2 :45: "Frames range against the ground. They do not negotiate with it."

The ordinary vocabulary here is "credible commitment". Restated in effect terms: each party's expectation about future exclusion or retaliation must range against something that is not regenerated by the parties' own statements. A promise between former belligerents is two frames negotiating; it converges on whoever had the most influence at the table unless a third reference is present. Fig 2 :31-32: "Independence, between two frames, is how little they draw on the same evidence. Two people reading the same report are not two frames; two instruments pointed at the same thing are." Independence "is what makes agreement mean something."

World corroboration (secondary, not verdict-bearing): the deterrence literature states that "costly signals are required to communicate the credibility of a defending state's resolve" and that "all defending states have an incentive to act as if they are determined to resist an attack"; the iterated-game literature states that "for cooperation to emerge between rational players, the number of rounds must be unknown or infinite" and lists Axelrod's conditions for a successful strategy as "Nice", "Retaliating", "Forgiving", "Non-envious". Both predict that the written promise is weak and the repeated, signal-bearing contact is strong. They share a broad strategic-studies pool with each other and with the political-science literature, so they count as one witness pool, not two.

**Refuter:** exhibit a case where a purely verbal, unverifiable promise, with no costly signal, no repeated contact and no external reading, reliably prevents recurrence. Or exhibit an anchor that demonstrably moves when a party pushes on it and still settles the question. If either holds, F2 is refused.

**Status:** `specified`, figure side triangulated (Fig 2 + Fig 6 + Fig 10). World side single pool.

### F3 - The habitat is enumerated, never composed, and capability is relational (governs D3)

Fig 11 :43: "The habitat is enumerated, never composed." Fig 11 :39: "capability is a property of agent-and-habitat, never of the agent alone." Fig 11 :40: "An improvement that does not change contact changes nothing, however much it improves."

A peace agreement dropped into a different habitat is not the same agreement. The actors to enumerate are everything in contact with the residual: monitors, neighbouring states, armed groups that did not sign, the security forces being reformed, economic interests, media, diasporas, then what is in contact with those. Fig 11 :48: "You do not invent the list. You read it off the world, and what you cannot perceive or measure yet is the residual." Fig 11 :73: "The habitat is not the ground. The habitat is where actors come from and it updates; the ground is what settles, and it does not."

Fig 11 :61: "The seat's power is asymmetric. Introduction is available; exclusion is not." Fig 11 :63-64: "Which is why a managed ecosystem is permanent work rather than a solved state." A durable settlement is maintained contact, not a solved constitutional state. Dayton is the case in point in the opened world record: "The agreement mandated a wide range of international organizations to monitor, oversight and implement components of the agreement", and the same record notes the agreement was "Praised for establishing lasting peace" and also "criticized for creating an unduly complex political governance system", with "entrenching regional ethnic cleansing". Introduction worked; the habitat was inherited and not authored.

**Refuter:** show that a settlement's capability is unchanged when every monitoring, guaranteeing and second-order contact actor is removed, with the same signatories and the same text. Or show that the habitat can be subtracted rather than substituted. If either holds, F3 is refused.

**Status:** `specified`, triangulated (Fig 11 + Fig 10 channel + Fig 6 import with source gate).

### F4 - No standing list of durability institutions survives a change of residual

Fig 11 :51: "No standing list of good actors survives a change of residual." Fig 11 :49: "An actor's valence is relative to the residual, not a property it carries." Fig 11 :50: "The same actor is a benefactor to one residual and a malfactor to another, so mutualism and parasitism are relations rather than kinds."

A mutual veto is a benefactor to the residual "one group will be excluded" and a malfactor to the residual "the state cannot decide and deliver". A foreign guarantor is a benefactor to "a party will renege" and a malfactor to "the settlement must be owned locally". Rotation of office is a benefactor to "permanent capture" and a malfactor to "accountability at the ballot". The same institution, differently valued, because the residual changed. So the question's ordinary form, "which provisions make agreements durable", asks for an object the figures structurally refuse. Fig 9 :30: "your words are borrowed too, so describe the effect rather than the cause."

This is the same refusal the completed BUS pilots recorded for standing lists of leadership behaviors and org-chart layers. It is not an excuse for silence: the separator is delivered instead of the list (see F5 and `07`).

**Refuter:** exhibit a list of provisions whose valence does not flip under any plausible change of residual in post-conflict settings, with the residual named for each. If one is found, F4 is refused for that provision.

**Status:** `general` structural, triangulated (Fig 11 alone among the figures, plus the re-census in `11_audit.md` which reaches it from Fig 9's union split). Figure-side single-source on the exact sentence; the method is corroborated by the exemplar's use of the same line. Marked `single-source` for the precise claim, not upgraded.

### F5 - The union cannot be cleared by one lever; verdicts stay separate

Fig 9 :55-56: "A bundled problem is usually a union of overlapping conditions, not a list of separate ones. A single lever clears the region where only one condition holds and leaves the intersections standing, because the second condition still binds." Fig 9 :57-58: "a case in two conditions is not a harder case of either. It stops being a mixture and becomes a specific compound." Fig 1 :19: "The residual is indexed by object slot, not aggregated."

The three conditions bind in different combinations. A settlement where only the text is weak is repaired by drafting. A settlement where text and credibility are both weak is not repaired by drafting alone. A settlement where credibility and habitat are both weak needs a wall-moving import, not more text. Fig 9 :60: "Use filters for the budget and witnesses for the verdict. Never let a filter hand you a verdict." Literature effect sizes (how much PR reduces recurrence, how much a veto helps) are filters that decide where to spend search budget. They are not witnesses for the structural verdict.

**Refuter:** show that a single institution clears recurrence across all three condition combinations with the residual indexed per group, or show that the three conditions are a partition with an assignable shared fraction.

**Status:** `specified`, triangulated (Fig 9 union rule + Fig 1 per-slot rule, independent quantities).

### F6 - Import is the only wall-moving operator, and it needs a source gate

Fig 6 :14: "the only operator that moves the wall". Fig 6 :21: "MINT composes inside the closure and can never add an atom." Fig 6 :23: "No frame escapes its own closure. The bound moves only on contact with a frame whose closure differs." Fig 6 :24: "Import from a live peer is debited against that frame's independence. A wrong atom costs more than starting from primitives."

The parties to a civil war usually share a closure: the same history of grievance, the same reading of past accords, the same evidence pool. Refining that shared text is composition inside the closure, and Fig 8 :32: "Closure is idempotent. Composing inside a closed set never leaves it, so no search, however long, adds an atom." What can move the wall is a frame whose closure differs: an outside monitor whose reading does not come from the parties' reports, a guarantor that can impose a cost neither party controls, an election observation mission. Fig 8 :12-13 (source gate) and "shadow test: explains a gap" (Fig 8, the grounded triangulation panel) must both be supplied. Fig 8 :50-52: "Convergent derivation and adopted import look identical in the contents. Only the record of where each came from separates them, and the surplus lives only where the closures differ."

World corroboration: the opened record on Cederman, Hug and Wucherpfennig (Cambridge, 2022) reports that the book reaches "a mostly positive conclusion by focusing on practices rather than merely formal institutions", and a reviewer on that page states that the book shows "the peace-inducing effects of the practice - rather than merely the promise - of sharing political power, while also noting that power-sharing institutions are no panacea." That is a direct corroboration of the practice/promise and no-panaea distinction, from a source with no connection to the framework.

**Refuter:** show a new atom (a genuinely new guarantee or actor) produced by composing the parties' own shared terms, with provenance showing no outside frame. Or show an import that carries no provenance and no shadow test and still reliably moves the wall. If either holds, F6 is refused.

**Status:** `specified`, triangulated (Fig 6 + Fig 8, different figures and different quantities).

### F7 - The round trip from this framework to the field loses constraints

Fig 4 :30: "So take the round trip and measure the gap. Without it you are navigating by dead reckoning: confident about a position nobody checked." Fig 4 :22: "Only methods cross up. Only head starts come down." Fig 4 :25: "A recording carried upward looks like knowledge and is a description of one occasion."

Sending a list of "durability institutions" is carrying a recording of occasions upward. What can cross legitimately is the method: describe the residual, bind the shape, enumerate the habitat, write the refuter. The condensation of the figures into that method is lossy, and the loss is recorded qualitatively in `11_audit.md` as a nonzero `R_T`. Treat the method as identical to the figures and the gap is set to zero, which is the dead-reckoning error Fig 4 :30 names.

**Refuter:** demonstrate a numeric round trip with a measured gap of zero on a real post-conflict case, under a declared code. Until then this is `specified`.

**Status:** `general`, single-source on the exact line (Fig 4), confirmed by the canonical README in section 7.

## 4. Two corrections and a re-census (kept visible)

**R1, retracted first-pass reading.** I first read "what makes agreements durable" as a property of the agreement text and began assembling an institutional list with effect-size weights. Re-census: D1 has no anchor (F1), so the list is a convention describing a frame, not a verdict about the ground. The list is withdrawn as a verdict. **R2, narrowed overstatement of "shape, not a name."** The canonical README says the framework returns "what a missing thing would have to look like (a shape, not a name)". I initially wrote that this forbids naming vetoes, rotation, proportionality or monitoring. Re-census: the phrase governs a primitive **not held** in the library. Those institutional atoms are already available in the political-science frame. Naming one is a **binding** from that frame onto an available atom, which is provenance to be recorded, not a prohibition and not a wall-moving import (Fig 6 :21: "MINT composes inside the closure and can never add an atom"). The refusal in F4 is about *standing lists whose valence never changes*, not about naming atoms. Both readings are preserved: naming is allowed with provenance; a standing list is refused by Fig 11 :51.

The group says what kind; the qualifier says which member."). Re-census: Fig 11 governs actor valence relative to a changing residual, while Fig 12 :123 governs arrangement kinds at a fixed level. Different objects. ## 5. Pre-registered refuter (written before the conclusion)

Required before any outreach: Seat Map :287, "a falsifier written before the run is a design instrument, not only an honesty device." The SHAPE claim in section 0 is refused if any of the following holds:

1. A written allocation of power, with no contact with any non-updating ground and no external frame, determines recurrence out of sample. (Refuses F1.)
2. A promised future conduct with no costly signal, no repeated interaction and no outside reading reliably prevents recurrence. (Refuses F2.)
3. A settlement's capability is unchanged after every monitoring, guaranteeing and second-order contact actor is removed, holding text and signatories fixed. (Refuses F3.)
4. A list of provisions is exhibited whose valence does not flip under any plausible change of post-conflict residual, with the residual named for each case. (Refuses F4.)
5. Two settlements over the same atom set with different bond arrangements do **not** differ in durability after the ground is read independently by frames with low overlap. (Refuses the isomer reading in `06`.)
6. A durability study codes institutions and recurrence from the same source, publishes no outside reading, and still predicts out-of-sample recurrence. (Refuses the independence condition in F2 and F6.)

If any fires, the shape is withdrawn or narrowed in a dated entry here. Until one is run, no part of this is labelled `grounded`.

## 8. Seven-question recursive derivation (summary)

1. **Is durability a property of the text?** No. The text is a frame with no anchor; Fig 10 :49 and Fig 2 :29. The first-pass list is retracted as a verdict (R1).
2. **What is the anchor?** Whatever settles recurrence and does not update on the parties' statements: observed recurrence and the post-settlement distribution of capacity. Fig 2 :29-30, :33.
3. **What closes the gap between text and anchor?** A frame with a different closure, imported with a source gate and shadow test. Fig 6 :23, Fig 8 :50-52. Its independence is debited.
4. **Can the habitat be composed from the institution list?** No. It is enumerated from contact outward. Introduction is available, subtraction is not. Fig 11 :43, :61, :63-64.
5. **Is there a standing list?** No. Valence flips with residual. Fig 11 :49-51. What survives is a separator: anchor, unbundled union, enumerated habitat, recorded import, refuter.
6. **Do identical ingredients always behave alike?** Not necessarily. Same atoms with a different bond are isomers (Fig 12 :117); a part that survives its own use is a catalyst (Fig 12 :119); a loop whose output re-enters as input is a cycle whose sign decides whether it closes (Fig 12 :121). The sign is unmeasured here.
7. **Route at ROUTE.** The standing list is REFUTED as a verdict (the fifth bin, Fig 5 :50-56; negation is not a bond, Operators :58). We refuse it rather than minting another institution. Then SEARCHED is asked first (Fig 8 :49-50): the provisions are already in the political-science closure and were not found to be residual-independent. INWARD would sharpen the case coding; OUTWARD would import a frame whose closure differs. They are not read against one another.
