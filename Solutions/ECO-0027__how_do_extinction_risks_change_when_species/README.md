## What is deliberately not here

- No personal emails of named individuals.
- No ranking of people by how likely they are to agree.
- No bulk-send plan.

Three reasons, in the figures' own words:

1. A room does not upgrade the label. Fig 2 :35: "Alignment is triangulation, not negotiation. A mirror cannot correct you."
2. Selecting for likely agreement manufactures the disqualifying correlation. Fig 9 :60: "Use filters for the budget and witnesses for the verdict. Never let a filter hand you a verdict."
3. Asking for trust before a check spends the reader's independence. Fig 10 :42: "A verifier that can reconstruct the claim cannot verify it."

Legally: scraping personal emails for unsolicited bulk outreach breaches GDPR and CAN-SPAM style rules in many jurisdictions. That is a practical failure on top of the structural one.
