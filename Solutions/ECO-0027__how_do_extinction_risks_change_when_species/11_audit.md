## Independent domain census

I redid the census from the residual line alone, before relying on the primary's conclusions. The
residual line I used was: change in a species' failure probability when its contact relations change.

- **R (arity / symmetry / scale).** Arity: relational, two or more species, with higher-order cases when a partner's partner matters. Symmetry: mixed, since food and habitat provision are directed while pollination and reproduction can be mutual; a symmetric-only graph loses cases. Scale: first order at direct partners, second and third order along cascades. Fig 9 makes this a union compound because a case in two conditions is not a harder case of either.
- **Domain 1, habitat and contact.** Actors: species, conditions, relations. Currency: contact and cascade. Anchor: survival and extinction. Question type: enumeration of a field of contact, not composition.
- **Domain 2, bond and arrangement.** Actors: species as operands, dependency relations as typed bonds. Currency: arrangement. Anchor: observed survival. Question type: union, one species in several relations, so unbundling before search.
- **Domain 3, assessment and verification.** Actors: assessors, models, instruments. Currency: per-slot residual and agreement. Anchor: the ground, never agreement. In this simulated run its independence is zero.

Order by dependency: enumerate contacts before assigning bonds; describe the residual before choosing a frame; settle first order before second order; refuse before mint (Formula step 2).

## Round trip gap R_T

The Symbols table defines R_T as the gap between what was sent up and what came back down,
`gap(x, (T_E ∘ T_A)(x))`, measuring what the coarser description cannot hold. Fig 4 :30 requires it to
be measured rather than assumed. I ran the round trip on the condensation this folder actually uses.

1. **Sent up (x):** the verbatim figure set, chiefly Fig 11 :43-47, :49-50, :61-66, :73; Fig 1 :19; Fig 3 :20-21; Fig 12 :117.
2. **Abstraction (T_A):** the shorthand in `07_the_tether_answer.md`: risk is species plus contact web; enumerate outward; per species; sign is relative; same species different bonds is different risk; provider-to-dependent order; management asymmetric; the ground decides.
3. **Concretisation (T_E):** a concrete claim about an actual community, for example that losing a pollinator raises risk by a given amount in a given site.

**R_T is nonzero.** Three things the shorthand holds do not survive the trip back down:

- the **scope of Fig 11's capability line**, which is about agents, not extinction probabilities; the shorthand uses it as a general rule, so any ecological application must be marked `specified` and tested by refuter R1;
- the **identity of the bonds**, since Fig 12's formal operators are not ecological relations; the shorthand says "arrangement matters", but which ecological relation maps to which bond needs a source gate and a shadow test;
- the **ground**, since the shorthand says "the ground decides" but does not name a discriminating instrument; without one, buffer and transmission path remain indiscernible in a given case.

One thing survives cleanly: a species' risk is not a function of the species alone, and the contact
web must be enumerated outward and indexed per species. That is the residue worth sending. R_T was
**not** measured numerically, because no ground reading exists; it is recorded qualitatively as the
enumerated set of dropped constraints. Treating the formula as identical to the figures would set R_T
to zero and would be the dead-reckoning error Fig 4 :30 names.
