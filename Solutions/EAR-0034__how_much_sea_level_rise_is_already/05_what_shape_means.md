## 1. A shape is a constraint, not a filler

The figures README states the limit: the framework returns "what a missing thing would have to look like (a shape, not a name)". Fig 9 :32: "you get a kind of thing, never the answer itself." Fig 13 :134: "Unreachable is a verdict here, not a silence". Fig 13 :138-139: "narrowing gets you close while the ground decides".

From *The Library of Babel* (opened directly): "You can cut your search short if the set of books returned is small and descriptive enough that it provides a general shape of the answer that satisfies your question."

So a **shape** is the narrowed form of an answer you can state before you can name the magnitude. It constrains; it does not fill. For EAR-0034 the shape is: a per-slot, union-compounded, horizon- and forcing-conditioned residual with an enumerated contact web and a named instrument. "One global number in metres" is a magnitude. "A per-contributor structure whose slot identity and definition must be bound before it can be measured" is a shape.

## 2. How a shape is obtained

- *Library of Babel* (opened directly): use the vocabulary you already have that is closest to the thing, look for recurring new terms that overlap or correlate with it, recurse. "With each new term gained, you narrow the space and deepen your vocabulary." The essay also names the failure mode relevant here: a malformed question may be "a bundle or conflation of several problems masquerading as one", to be searched individually and then combined. That is exactly the union diagnosis in `01` F2.
- Fig 9 :27-28: "describe it before you go looking, or any frame you pick will seem to fit". Fig 9 :30-31: "your words are borrowed too, so describe the effect rather than the cause." For this question the effect is "a committed but not yet realised rise", not the name "marine ice sheet instability".
- *Decoding Tacit Knowledge* (carried): "When contact isn't possible, the best you can send is a plain description of what you'd expect to see, because anyone can check that against the world."
- Fig 6 :30: "Cast it in effect terms." The description that travels to another frame is the effect, not the model parameter.
- *Library of Babel* on stopping: the answer is reached when "the finish line will also be the starting place" and the paradox is that by then you have "composed the answer through the process of locating it."

## 3. What licenses a magnitude

Only two things, both outside the shape itself:

1. **The ground settles it.** Formula step 5: "the ground settles it. Verified only by what pays." The figures README: the framework "stops at the limit of what the available instruments can distinguish, and records the lineage so the stop is honest". For this question that means the future observed sea level, the observed per-ice-sheet mass series and the paleo highstands. The committed amount is not settled until those pay.
2. **A provenanced import from a frame whose closure differs** (Fig 6 :23: "No frame escapes its own closure. The bound moves only on contact with a frame whose closure differs."), with a source gate and a shadow test. The paleo archive is such a frame; another CMIP ensemble is not.

*They're Scanning Books When They Should* (carried): "The real import is not a new term in the existing space. It is a new dimension of the space." And: "A minimum viable set of six terms is a human's careful guess at reality's compression axes; another sensorium's minimum set need not be those six." Also: "Declaring the primitives does not make them neutral. It makes the choice visible."

The six are a visible guess, not a discovery of nature's own list. Naming one as *the* answer to a slot turns a visible choice into an invisible one. Likewise, adopting one published committed-slope value as *the* answer turns a horizon-conditional, provenanced import into an invisible convention.

## 4. Why a single number is the specific forbidden move here

*The Hard Problem is About Source* (carried) gives the mechanism. Metamerism: an instrument reads green and "cannot comment on the source" - one green light, or yellow plus blue, or RGB, or CMYK, or a scheme not yet discovered. "If several paths create the same behavior, then behavior is a projection with lossy information." And: "An instrument that can't distinguish these cases demonstrates its own inadequacy." It was the spectrometer, not an argument, that resolved it.

A global committed-sea-level number is the reading. Thermal expansion, Greenland surface mass balance, Antarctic marine dynamics and glaciers are the possible sources of the same reading. The reading does not determine the source. What is owed is a source-discriminating instrument and a per-slot report, not a more precise scalar. NOAA's public distinction between global and relative sea level (opened 2026-11-22) and IPCC AR6 Box 9.1's near-field/far-field sign reversal are the instrument-level evidence that the scalar loses information.

*The Recursive Self-Improvement Trap* (opened directly): "the output is only as good as the validator. The agent, or the tool, or the frame, or the theory, cannot validate itself." And: "You may all agree and be wrong." A pool of agreeing models is not a validator.

## 5. CORRECTION TO MY OWN §3 AND §4

I initially drafted §3 and §4 as if "shape, not a name" forbade reporting any published number at all. That overstates the rule.

- The README's phrase is "what a **missing** thing would have to look like (a shape, not a name)". The subject is a missing primitive, one not held in Γ.
- The published committed amounts are not missing primitives. They are provenanced measurements and model products from the earth-science frame, bound onto atoms the framework already holds (mass, a gradient over space, a gradient over time, a threshold comparison).
- What is refused is **deriving** a magnitude from a figure line, or presenting an import as a derivation. What is permitted is reporting the magnitude with author, venue, year, horizon, forcing definition and provenance, exactly as `01` F9 and `10_source_ledger.csv` do.

My §4 metamerism point is **not** retracted: the scalar reading still fails to determine its source, and that is a real reason to report per slot. The reason is the missing discriminator, not a ban on citing numbers. Both claims are now recorded so neither is quietly lost. This correction mirrors the one kept visible in the exemplar's `05` section 5b and in `EAR-0027/05` section 5b.

## 6. Two consequences for outreach

**Prestige is not action.** From *The Recursive Self-Improvement Trap* (opened directly): "Prestige must never be allowed to validate an action directly... Prestige can fund an action. It can give an action a budget to be tried. But it cannot settle an action. Only the ground can settle it. The Ground Maintainer must be blind to prestige." A campaign that gathers famous names to agree with a committed-SLR number is the named collapse, not corroboration. The legitimate ask is the opposite: give the shape claim a budget to be tried, let the tide gauge and the paleo archive settle it.

**My own audit is a simulation, not a ground reading.** Same essay (opened directly): "LLMs can quickly simulate an approximate answer/output with the formula found in the architecture post... However, the truly testable output would need to be derived from the real architecture or working the formula yourself." So `01` is a simulated derivation, checked for internal consistency against the figures. It is **not** a test of the claim, it cannot settle the committed amount, and it must be labelled as such everywhere it appears.
