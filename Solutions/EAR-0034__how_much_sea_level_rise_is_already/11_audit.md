## Independent domain census

I redid the census from the residual line alone, using the WATSON order: R by arity, symmetry and scale; one line per domain with actors / currency / anchor; partition or union; order by dependency. I did this before adopting the primary's wording.

- **R (arity / symmetry / scale).** The residual is the gap between projected and observed sea-level change. Arity: five or more overlapping conditions (thermal, glaciers, Greenland, Antarctica, land water) plus a coastal receiver, so a **union compound**, not one hard question (Fig 9 :57-58: "a case in two conditions is not a harder case of either. It stops being a mixture and becomes a specific compound."). Symmetry: asymmetric, because ice loss has hysteresis and a size-linked irreversibility threshold; a symmetric join would misrepresent a one-way commitment. Scale: years to tens of millennia, from one grounding line to the global mean and the regional fingerprint.
- **Domain 1, ice-and-ocean ground.** Actors: two ice sheets, marine sectors, ice shelves, grounding lines, ocean heat, bedrock, gravity, rotation, deformation, isostasy. Currency: Gt, m SLE, response time, threshold size. Anchor: altimetry, gravimetry, tide gauges, paleo archives and ice-flow physics. Question type: a union of slow responses.
- **Domain 2, projection and assessment frame.** Actors: ice-sheet models, emulators, assessment chapters, paleo-constrained studies. Currency: committed GMSL per degree, conditional ranges, supplied horizon. Anchor: the ground in domain 1. Question type: a frame, not an anchor.
- **Domain 3, definition and convention frame.** Actors: the words "commitment", "zero emissions", "constant warming", "horizon", "irreversible". Currency: meaning. Anchor: an operational definition, a convention with no truth value. Question type: the missing link 3 of Fig 3.
- **Domain 4, verification and outreach frame.** Actors: expert readers and editors. Currency: agreement and residual. Anchor: the ground, never agreement. In this simulated run all four domains draw on one evidence pool, so this domain's independence is zero.

Order by dependency: bind the objective (horizon, forcing definition, contributor) before observing the per-slot residual; settle each contributor before composing the union at second order (Fig 12 :104 "A settled molecule becomes an atom for whatever composes over it."); describe the residual before naming a frame (Fig 9 :27-28 "describe it before you go looking, or any frame you pick will seem to fit").

## Round trip gap R_T

The Symbols table defines R_T as the gap between what was sent up and what came back down, gap(x, (T_E ∘ T_A)(x)) (Symbols table :77). Fig 4 :30 requires it to be measured rather than assumed: "So take the round trip and measure the gap. Without it you are navigating by dead reckoning: confident about a position nobody checked." I ran the round trip on the condensation this folder actually uses.

1. **Sent up (x):** the verbatim figure set, chiefly Fig 1 :19 and :22 (per-slot residual), Fig 9 :54 and :57-58 (union compound), Fig 3 :19 (objective before amount), Fig 13 :76 (spatial versus temporal gradients), Fig 12 :119 and :121 (persisting part and loop sign), Fig 2 :29 and :40 (anchor and pool warning).
2. **Abstraction (T_A):** the shorthand carried in `07_the_tether_answer.md`: committed rise is per slot, a union, definition-conditioned, with separate spatial and temporal terms, an observation anchor, and an enumerated habitat; the number is a gap.
3. **Concretisation (T_E):** the concrete literature answer: about 2.3 m per °C over 2000 years (Levermann et al. 2013); 0.7 to 1.1 m from historical emissions to 2300 and 2 to 6 m at 2 °C over 2000 years (IPCC AR6); 2 to 3 m at 2000 years and 6 to 7 m at 10,000 years at 1.5 °C (Palmer 2024).

**R_T is nonzero.** Four things the shorthand holds do not survive the trip back down:

- **slot identity and datum:** the shorthand says "per slot" but does not carry the near-field fall / far-field rise sign structure or the tide-gauge versus satellite reference frames. A local relative sea-level datum is not the global mean, and the condensation drops that.
- **horizon and forcing definition:** the shorthand says "definition-conditioned" but does not carry which horizon or which forcing definition produced a given number. The literature numbers differ for that reason, and a naive retelling presents them as a disagreement.
- **source provenance:** the shorthand says "import" but does not carry author, venue, year and the paleo pairing that makes Levermann et al. (2013) a low-overlap witness rather than another model vote.
- **the comparison-bond operand type at second order:** the shorthand permits a threshold over settled contributors; the Operators table restricts ⋛ to two quantities, and whether settled contributors count as quantities at that level needs an instrument.

One thing survives cleanly: a structure must mediate a composition, the residual is per slot, and the union must be unbundled before search. That is the residue worth sending. R_T was **not** measured numerically, because no ground reading exists for the committed amount; it is recorded qualitatively as the enumerated set of dropped constraints. Treating "the formula is the condensed version of the metalaws" as identity would set R_T to zero and would be the dead-reckoning error Fig 4 :30 names. The honest form is a lossy condensation with a recorded gap, which is what `09_watson_answer.md` states.
