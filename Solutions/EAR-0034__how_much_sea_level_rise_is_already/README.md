## What is deliberately not here

- No personal emails of named individuals.
- No ranking of people by how likely they are to agree.
- No bulk-send plan.
- No single global committed-sea-level number presented as a Tether verdict. The number belongs to the instruments; the figures return the shape of the answer and the refuter.

Three figure-grounded reasons, in the figures' own words:

1. **A room does not upgrade the label.** Fig 2 :35: "Alignment is triangulation, not negotiation. A mirror cannot correct you." Selecting recipients for likely agreement manufactures exactly the correlation that makes their agreement worthless.
2. **Correlation disqualifies a verdict.** Fig 2 :40: "agreement about the world is worthless when the agreeing frames share an evidence pool". CMIP and ISMIP ensembles share forcing, model ancestry and assessment pools, so their agreement is one witness with several names.
3. **Asking people to trust before they check spends their independence.** Fig 10 :42: "A verifier that can reconstruct the claim cannot verify it." The first contact carries the residual and its refuter, never the framework.

Legally: scraping personal emails for unsolicited bulk outreach breaches GDPR and CAN-SPAM style rules in many jurisdictions. That is a practical failure on top of the structural one. All channels listed in `03_public_contact_points.csv` are institutional or opt-in.
