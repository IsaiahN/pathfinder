# The Tether's answer on committed sea-level rise

**Mode: `specified` on the structural verdicts, `general` on the union/per-slot form, explicit `gap` on the numerical amount, not `grounded`.** This is a shape and a route, not a result. Simulated derivation checked against the figures (`05_what_shape_means.md` section 6), not a ground reading.

## 1. The residual it answers

"How much sea-level rise is already committed because ice sheets respond slowly?" is a request for a number. The figures refuse the container. The residual between what projection frames predict and what altimeters, gravimeters, tide gauges and the paleo archive return is indexed per contributor and per coast: Greenland, Antarctica, thermal expansion, glaciers, land water, and the regional fingerprint. Fig 1 :19: "The residual is indexed by object slot, not aggregated." Fig 1 :22: "Aggregating across slots is how a live signal disappears into an average." The union is a compound with its own loop and its own ground, not one hard question (Fig 9 :57-58: "a case in two conditions is not a harder case of either. It stops being a mixture and becomes a specific compound.").

## 2. The shape

1. **It is a union, unbundle before searching.** Fig 9 :54: "And when the split lands on a union rather than a partition, unbundle before you search again." Thermal expansion, glaciers, Greenland surface mass balance, Antarctic marine dynamics and land water overlap and compound. One lever does not clear the compound: a zero-emissions pathway still leaves the deep-ocean and marine-ice slots bound.
2. **The amount is not defined until the objective is bound.** "Committed" needs a forcing definition (zero emissions, constant present warming, pledged emissions), a horizon (2100, 2300, 2000 years, 10,000 years) and a contributor. Fig 3 :19: "Optimising a link that already works cannot rescue one that does not." The chain breaks at the objective if these are left implicit. The published numbers differ because the questions differ, not because the science is confused.
3. **Slowness is a cross-kind structure, and spatial and temporal gradients are different terms.** Fig 13 :76: "A gradient over space and one over time are different terms, because which state it is bonded to is part of what it is." The regional fingerprint (a near-field fall, a far-field rise above the global mean, IPCC AR6 Ch9 Box 9.1) and the committed multidecadal-to-millennial trend are two terms. A single rise number collapses them.
4. **Irreversibility is a loop sign and a persisting part, not a seventh primitive.** Fig 12 :121: "cycle. The output re-enters as input. The sign of the loop decides whether it closes, and a chain cannot close by construction." Fig 12 :119: "catalyst. A part that survives its own use." The ice sheets are persisting parts; a size-linked threshold in Greenland (about 3 to 3.5 m SLE lost, beyond which regrowth fails) is a world binding onto that structure. Fig 13 :115: "The set is closed. The arrangements are not. That gap is why import exists."
5. **The anchor is measurement and paleo, never model agreement.** Fig 2 :29: "An anchor is whatever settles the question and does not change its answer because of what any frame thinks." Fig 2 :40: "agreement about the world is worthless when the agreeing frames share an evidence pool". CMIP and ISMIP models share ancestry and forcing, so their agreement is one witness. The paleo archive is the low-overlap witness, which is why the commitment literature pairs it with process models.
6. **The instruments already exist and already return something.** Fig 6 :32: "An instrument is not built from a description. It is improved from a worse instrument already returning something." Extend altimetry, GRACE and GRACE-FO, the tide-gauge network and paleo reconstruction; do not imagine a perfect future sensor. Fig 10 :24: "the ground does not decay, the channel does" - datum drift and short records are channel failures.
7. **The habitat must be enumerated out to second order.** Fig 11 :43: "The habitat is enumerated, never composed." Fig 11 :52-53: "first-order enumeration, second-order effect, and the residual that mattered was three steps out." Ice loss changes gravity and rotation, which changes regional relative sea level, which changes extreme-water-level frequency, which changes exposure. A first-order-only list misses the coastal result.
8. **The number is a `gap`, reported as a verdict, not as silence.** Fig 9 :32: "you get a kind of thing, never the answer itself." Fig 8 :39: "So a search that finds nothing is never a proof of absence." Fig 13 :134: "Unreachable is a verdict here, not a silence". The figures return the form of the answer and its refuters; they yield no metres.

## 3. What it explicitly does not say

- Not a committed value in metres derived from the figures. The published values are world imports with provenance, not loop outputs.
- Not that all ice-sheet change is irreversible. The record reports thresholds for specific sectors and sizes, and distinguishes them from continuing trends.
- Not a date for a collapse, and not a scenario forecast.
- Not that the ice sheets are Spectrum, the ocean is Gradient, or any other one-to-one bond mapping. The habitat is enumerated, not composed (Fig 11 :43).
- Not that a larger model ensemble would settle it (Fig 8 :32: "Closure is idempotent. Composing inside a closed set never leaves it, so no search, however long, adds an atom.").
- Not `grounded`. No measurement taken by this author settles any part of it.

## 4. What would settle it : the ground

A source-discriminating instrument and an operational definition, not a further argument. *The Hard Problem is About Source* (carried in `05`): an instrument that reads green "cannot comment on the source"; the spectrometer resolved it. Here:

- **Definition test:** state the horizon, forcing definition and contributor, then show the amount is stable under that definition. If it is not, the object was never bound.
- **Source discrimination:** separate the thermal, Greenland, Antarctic-dynamic and glacier contributions to the observed regional trend at decadal scale, so the scalar source question can be answered by an instrument.
- **Fingerprint test:** measure the near-field and far-field relative sea-level pattern against the mass-loss pattern, so the spatial and temporal terms can be distinguished at the ground.
- **Threshold test:** locate the irreversibility threshold for a named ice-sheet sector and show whether the loop sign closes.
- **Standing falsifiers from `01` section 5:** a single scalar that preserves all per-slot trends; one monotonic law across all horizons; a definition-invariant amount; an ensemble that moves the measurement; a first-order-only contact list that predicts the regional pattern; a numeric amount derived from a figure line.

## 5. The honest stop

**Unreached, not unreachable.** The six-term set is "a human's careful guess at reality's compression axes"; declaring it makes the choice visible, not neutral. The committed amount stops at the limit of what the available instruments can distinguish, and the lineage is recorded so the stop is checkable. "Indiscernibility is not identity." Fig 13 :138-139: "narrowing gets you close while the ground decides". The live strain is the operand type of the threshold comparison at second order, named in `06` Q4 and `11_audit.md`, not hidden.

## 6. One-paragraph version for outreach

> The Tether does not give a number for how much sea level is already committed, and it does not claim the figures can. It says the residual is indexed per contributor and per coast, so a single global scalar hides which slot is live (Fig 1 :19, :22); that "committed" is a union of overlapping slow responses that must be unbundled before it is searched (Fig 9 :54); that the amount is undefined until a horizon and a forcing definition are bound (Fig 3 :19); that the regional fingerprint and the multidecadal trend are different terms (Fig 13 :76); and that irreversibility is a loop sign and a persisting part, not a new primitive (Fig 12 :119, :121). The anchor is the tide gauge, the gravimeter and the paleo archive, never model agreement (Fig 2 :29, :40). What it offers is the shape any admissible answer must have, seven refuters written in advance, and the instruments that would close it. The number stays with the ground until the ground pays for it.
