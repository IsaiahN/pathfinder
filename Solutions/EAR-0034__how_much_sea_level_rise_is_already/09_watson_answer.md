## QUESTION

Decide, in effect terms, what the Tether says about the question "How much sea-level rise is already committed because ice sheets respond slowly?" (earth systems.txt line 47). Option A: answer with one global number in metres. Option B: refuse the scalar, return a per-slot union shape with a bound definition, seven refuters and an instrument specification. What each leads to: A gives a decision-maker a number that cannot say which contributor is live, which coast receives the fingerprint, or which horizon and forcing definition the number belongs to, and it spends the independence of the people asked to check it; B gives a checkable shape, keeps verdicts per domain, and leaves the number to the instruments. This answer takes B.

## DOMAIN CENSUS

R by arity, symmetry and scale, then one line per domain giving actors / currency / anchor, partition or union, and order by dependency.

- **R (the residual).** The gap between what projection frames predict per contributor and per coast and what altimetry, gravimetry, tide gauges and the paleo archive return. Arity: five or more overlapping conditions, so a union compound (Fig 9 :57-58: "a case in two conditions is not a harder case of either. It stops being a mixture and becomes a specific compound."). Symmetry: asymmetric, because ice loss has hysteresis and a size-linked irreversibility threshold; a symmetric join would misrepresent a one-way commitment. Scale: years to tens of millennia, from one grounding line to the global mean and the regional fingerprint.
- **Domain 1, ice-and-ocean ground.** Actors: Greenland and Antarctic ice sheets, marine sectors, ice shelves, grounding lines, ocean heat, bedrock, gravity, rotation and deformation, isostasy. Currency: ice mass (Gt), sea-level equivalent (m SLE), response time, irreversible threshold. Anchor: observations and paleo reconstructions plus the physics of ice flow and ocean heat uptake. Type: union compound of slow responses.
- **Domain 2, projection and assessment frame.** Actors: ice-sheet models of the ISMIP6 lineage, emulators, IPCC AR6 WG1 Ch9, paleo-constrained commitment studies. Currency: committed GMSL per degree, conditional ranges, supplied horizon. Anchor: the ground in domain 1, which this frame must range against. Type: a frame, not an anchor.
- **Domain 3, definition and convention frame.** Actors: "commitment", "zero emissions commitment", "constant warming commitment", "irreversible", "threshold", horizon, reference period. Currency: the meaning of the quantity. Anchor: an operational definition, which is a convention with no truth value. Type: convention.
- **Domain 4, verification and outreach frame.** Actors: expert readers, venue editors, reviewers. Currency: agreement and residual. Anchor: the ground, never agreement. Type: checking arrangement. In this simulated run all four domains draw on one evidence pool, so this domain's independent standing is zero.

**Partition or union:** union (Fig 9 :54: "And when the split lands on a union rather than a partition, unbundle before you search again."; Fig 9 :55: "A bundled problem is usually a union of overlapping conditions, not a list of separate ones.").

**Order by dependency (Fig 3):** bind the objective first (horizon, forcing definition, contributor); then perceive the per-slot residual; then acquire vocabulary for what each slot affords; then plan and carry. Fig 3 :19: "Optimising a link that already works cannot rescue one that does not."

Strain test applied: Fig 13 :76 governs the spatial fingerprint and the temporal commitment as different terms; world studies govern the magnitude of each. Different objects, so no strain after re-census. The one live strain is the comparison-bond operand type at second order, recorded in the TETHER section and in `11_audit.md`.

## VERDICT (from the figures)

The question's form is refused and the shape survives, with the numeric field declared `gap`. The residual of committed sea-level rise is per contributor and per coast, so aggregation loses the live signal (Fig 1 :19, :22). It is a union compound of overlapping slow responses that must be unbundled (Fig 9 :54, :55, :57-58), not one lever. The amount is undefined until a horizon and a forcing definition are bound (Fig 3 :19, :27), which is why the published magnitudes are conditional rather than contradictory. The spatial fingerprint and the temporal commitment are different terms (Fig 13 :76), and irreversibility is a loop sign and a persisting part rather than a seventh primitive (Fig 12 :119, :121; Fig 13 :115). The anchor is observation and paleo, never the agreement of models that share an evidence pool (Fig 2 :29, :35, :40). The framework returns this shape, seven refuters written before the run and an instrument specification; it returns no metres. Verdicts are kept per domain and never averaged. This is a `specified`, ungrounded result with an explicit `gap` on the number.

- **cost if wrong:** bounded and locatable. The packet states mode, refuters and provenance before the conclusion, so an error is findable rather than invisible. The real cost is giving a coastal decision-maker a scalar that hides which contributor is live, which coast receives the fingerprint and which horizon the number belongs to, and spending the independence of outside readers by sending a recording instead of a generator. Fig 2 :42 "Buy independence in proportion to the cost of being wrong." prices that; it is avoidable by leading with the shape and the refuter.
- **undoable:** yes. The definition can be made explicit, the union unbundled, the spatial and temporal terms separated, and the imported magnitudes reported with author, year, horizon and forcing definition. No primitive is withdrawn and no sent claim has to be defended as final; a corrected packet can supersede it.
- **reversed by:** a ground reading, not agreement. A source-discriminating instrument that separates thermal, Greenland, Antarctic-dynamic and glacier contributions at decadal scale, a fingerprint measurement that fixes the near-field and far-field pattern, or a threshold measurement that locates the loop closure for a named sector would close the gap. A single scalar that preserves all per-slot trends, one monotonic law across all horizons, a definition-invariant amount, an ensemble that moves the measurement, a first-order-only contact list that predicts the regional pattern, or a derivation of one of the six from the other five would break the shape claim. A re-census that admits the comparison bond over settled contributors as quantities at second order would clear the live strain. Until one of these fires, the verdict stands as `candidate`.
