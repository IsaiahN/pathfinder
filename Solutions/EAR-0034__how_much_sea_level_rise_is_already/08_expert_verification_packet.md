## 1. The claim, in one paragraph

The residual between projected and observed sea-level change is indexed per contributor and per coast, so a single global committed-SLR scalar is the wrong container: Fig 1 :19 "The residual is indexed by object slot, not aggregated." and Fig 1 :22 "Aggregating across slots is how a live signal disappears into an average." "Committed rise" is a union of overlapping slow responses (thermal expansion, glaciers, Greenland surface mass balance, Antarctic marine dynamics, land water) that must be unbundled before it is searched, Fig 9 :54 "And when the split lands on a union rather than a partition, unbundle before you search again." The amount is undefined until a horizon and a forcing definition are bound, since 0.7 to 1.1 m to 2300 from historical emissions and 2 to 6 m over 2000 years at 2 °C are different questions. The framework returns this shape, seven refuters written in advance, and the instruments that would close it. It returns no number in metres.

## 2. Three checkable sub-claims

### C1 : framework-internal; checkable by reading Figures 1 and 9

- **Statement:** the committed-SLR residual is per slot, and the contributors form a union compound, not one quantity. A global scalar is not an admissible answer container.
- **Source, verbatim:** Fig 1 :19 "The residual is indexed by object slot, not aggregated."; Fig 1 :22 "Aggregating across slots is how a live signal disappears into an average."; Fig 9 :54 "And when the split lands on a union rather than a partition, unbundle before you search again."; Fig 9 :57-58 "a case in two conditions is not a harder case of either. It stops being a mixture and becomes a specific compound." The Symbols table adds "R is measured per slot, never as a single global number." (Symbols table :43).
- **World check:** IPCC AR6 WG1 Ch9 reports Greenland losing 4890 Gt and Antarctica 2670 Gt over 1992 to 2020, with the ice-sheet contribution four times larger in 2010 to 2019 than in 1992 to 1999; and Box 9.1 reports a near-field relative sea-level fall within about 2000 km of a mass source, rising above the global mean beyond about 7000 km. NOAA's public page distinguishes global from relative sea level.
- **Refuter:** exhibit a single global committed-SLR scalar that predicts per-contributor and per-region residual trends with no loss of sign or rank; or show the contributors reduce to one variable under one monotonic law across all horizons and forcing definitions.

### C2 : physics-facing; checkable by a cryosphere or sea-level researcher

- **Statement:** the spatial fingerprint and the temporal commitment are different terms, and irreversibility is a loop sign and a persisting part rather than a new primitive. Fig 13 :76 "A gradient over space and one over time are different terms, because which state it is bonded to is part of what it is."; Fig 12 :121 "cycle. The output re-enters as input. - **Refuter:** show a regional sea-level pattern is a direct state-state bond, or that spatial and temporal gradients are one term under the six-term set; or show the irreversibility requires a new primitive.
