## Round trip gap R_T

The Symbols table defines R_T as the gap between what was sent up and what came back down, `gap(x, (T_E ∘ T_A)(x))`. Fig 4 :30 requires it to be measured rather than assumed. I ran the round trip on the condensation this folder actually uses.

1. **Sent up (x):** the national balance and the accounting identity, plus the shape claim that the balance is an instrument.
2. **Abstraction (T_A):** "a deficit is saving below investment, mirrored by capital inflow, so it is not a loss in itself."
3. **Concretisation (T_E):** the local labour market where the import-competing plant closes, and the balance sheet with foreign-currency short-term debt.

**R_T is nonzero.** The coarse description drops at least four constraints that matter at the ground:

- which local slots carry the adjustment and for how long (Fig 1 :19; Autor, Dorn and Hanson 2013);
- whether the borrowing finances investment or consumption (World Bank 2018);
- the currency, maturity and gross-exposure composition that decides rollover risk (BIS 2015);
- the sign-independence of the contest frame, which the national description never tests.

Conversely, one thing survives cleanly: a deficit is matched by net capital inflow by definition, and the balance is not an anchor for a question about outcomes. That is the residue worth sending. R_T was **not** measured numerically, because no ground reading for a named country was run; it is recorded qualitatively as the enumerated set of dropped constraints. Treating "national net welfare" as identical to "every local slot is fine" would set R_T to zero and would be the dead-reckoning error Fig 4 :30 names. `09_watson_answer.md` now states the shape rather than a verdict, which is the honest form.
