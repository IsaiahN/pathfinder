## Wider literature that bears on the question, grouped by overlap

**One macro-accounting pool (one witness, not six).** Dallas Fed 2025, IMF 2026, CGD 2025, CRS 2020, OpenStax 2022, PIIE 2024. All restate CA = S - I and its mirror in the financial account. Their convergence is internal to one closure. Their legitimate use is as a *filter for the budget* (Fig 9 :60: "Use filters for the budget and witnesses for the verdict. Never let a filter hand you a verdict."), not as independent confirmation of a welfare verdict.

**Low-overlap witness 1, labour economics.** Autor, Dorn and Hanson 2013 and the NBER Digest 2025 summary of Autor, Dorn, Hanson, Jones and Setzler. These speak to D3 (per-slot adjustment), not to the identity. They corroborate that a national reading cannot be played back down to a local slot without a measured gap.

**Low-overlap witness 2, international finance and sustainability.** BIS WP 525 (2015), World Bank brief 17 (2018), IMF Finance and Development (2017). These speak to D4 (financing composition), and explicitly warn that the net balance does not carry the gross-exposure information that decides reversal risk.

**Low-overlap witness 3, cognitive and social psychology.** Davidai and Tepper 2023; Brutger and Rathbun 2025; International Organization 2017 on in-group favouritism. These speak to D5 (why the contest frame persists). They do not corroborate the economics claims.

**Low-overlap witness 4, economic history.** Oxford Public International Law on mercantilism; OpenStax World History volume 2. These record the zero-sum, balance-as-score board frame as a historical construct, which supports the claim that it is a frame rather than a law.

## Distribution: offer, do not push

Figure-grounded reasons:

- Fig 4 :22-24: "Only methods cross up. Only head starts come down. A recording carried upward looks like knowledge and is a description of one occasion." Sending the full derivation before anyone has described a residual sends a recording. Lead with the shape claim and its refuter; offer the links after.
- Fig 10 :42: "A verifier that can reconstruct the claim cannot verify it." A recipient who has absorbed the whole framework before being asked for a verdict has joined the frame being checked.
- Fig 9 :27-28: "describe it before you go looking, or any frame you pick will seem to fit." Ask what they would expect to see if the shape claim were true before giving them the sources.
- Fig 2 :35: "Alignment is triangulation, not negotiation. A mirror cannot correct you." A list selected for people who already agree produces a convention, not corroboration.

**Recommended order in a reply:** (1) the shape claim and its five refuters; (2) ask what they would count as a failure, and record the answer; (3) only then the links, with the note that the figures and the data, not this summary, are the authority.

## The two-stage offer

1. **Stage 1.** Send the residual, the shape claim and the pre-registered refuter. Ask what they would count as a failure. Record their verdict **before** any theory is shown (Fig 10 :52, "Bring a characterised residual, never the source"; Fig 2 :45, "Frames range against the ground. They do not negotiate with it.").
2. **Stage 2, opt-in only.** If they are curious, offer the three source links or the relevant paper. Their later agreement is never pooled as corroboration (Fig 2 :40: "agreement about the world is worthless when the agreeing frames share an evidence pool").
3. **Stage 3.** Offer the formula as a method to reuse. Fig 4 :22 permits sending a method. Each user must state their own ground and write their own refuter before running it, because THE_FORMULA "Direction" says a frame cannot check the direction of its own question.

**Hard precondition:** stages 1 to 3 work only if the stage-1 output survives. It is a shape, not a verdict on a named country, and it is labelled as such. Do not quietly upgrade it to "trade deficits are harmless" in the email or in a follow-up.
