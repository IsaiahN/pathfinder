## 1. A shape is a constraint you can state before you can name the instance

The canonical usage rule (`FRAMEWORK/reference/FIGURES_README_canonical.md`, read 2026-11-23) gives the limit directly: the framework returns *"what a missing thing would have to look like (a shape, not a name)"*, then stops *"at the limit of what the available instruments can distinguish, and records the lineage so the stop is honest. Indiscernibility is not identity."* Fig 9 :32: "you get a kind of thing, never the answer itself." Fig 13 :134: "Unreachable is a verdict here, not a silence,".

From *The Library of Babel* (fetched 2026-11-23): "you can cut your search short if the set of books returned is small and descriptive enough that it provides a general shape of the answer that satisfies your question."

So for ECN-0079 a **shape** is the set of conditions an answer must satisfy before we can say which real deficit we mean: the union of accounting, distributional and financing conditions, per slot, read against outcomes rather than against the sign of the balance. "Trade deficits are harmful" is a name for a verdict. "Harm is conditional on per-slot adjustment, use of borrowing and financing composition, with the balance as an instrument" is a shape.

## 2. How the shape is obtained: proximity, then recurrence

*Library of Babel*: "If you don't know the right terms to search for, you will have to use the existing vocabulary you do know that is closest to the words that you wish to describe and grasp the shape of." And: "With each new term gained, you are able to narrow the space by definition and deepen your vocabulary."

Applied here: the residual is first described in effect terms (a gap between imports and exports that registers as a loss in one frame and as borrowing in another). Only then were frames selected: national accounting, labour economics, international finance, cognitive psychology, economic history. Had the frames been named first, any of them would have seemed to fit (Fig 9 :27-28). This is the residual-first rule, and it is the reason the domain census in `01` precedes the figure quotes.

## 3. The bundle test

*Library of Babel*: "Perhaps your problem was a bundle or conflation of several problems masquerading as one? You will need to search all of those problems individually to find their answers and then combine them in a satisfactory way."

This is the single most directly applicable essay line for this question. The either/or in ECN-0079 is such a bundle. Fig 9 :55 says the same in figure terms: "A bundled problem is usually a union of overlapping conditions, not a list of separate ones." The answer is not to choose a horn but to split and then recombine, keeping the verdicts separate per domain (Fig 1 :19).

## 4. What licenses a name: the source-discriminating instrument

*The Hard Problem is About Source* (fetched 2026-11-23): "If several paths create the same behavior, then behavior is a projection with lossy information"; the instrument "cannot comment on the source"; and "The answer is unreached until a better instrument exists."

For this question the balance is a projection. A deficit can be produced by low saving, by high investment, by fiscal expansion, by a reserve-currency role, or by a global savingglut. The balance reading cannot comment on which source. Naming one source for a particular deficit from the balance alone is the metamerism error. What is owed is a source-discriminating instrument: data on saving and investment, on the composition and currency of external liabilities, and on the local adjustment path. When those discriminators exist, a name is licensed; until then, only the shape.

The same essay also supplies the honesty rule used throughout this folder: an instrument that cannot distinguish these cases "demonstrates its own inadequacy". Reporting "the balance says deficit, therefore harm" would be that failure.

## 5. Naming imported atoms versus deriving them

I correct my own earlier phrasing, consistent with the exemplar's correction. The canonical "shape, not a name" limit governs a **missing** primitive. Saving, investment, current account and capital inflow are ordinary terms already held in the economics frame. Using them is an import with provenance, not a derivation from the figures and not a new primitive. Fig 6 :23: "No frame escapes its own closure. The bound moves only on contact with a frame whose closure differs." Claiming the figures derived CA = S - I would be an import with no source gate. The honest record is that the identity comes from the national accounts frame with provenance (Dallas Fed, IMF, CRS, OpenStax), and the figures govern how to *use* it: as an instrument, per slot, in a union, never as a verdict.

## 6. Prestige is not action

*The Recursive Self-Improvement Trap* (fetched 2026-11-23): "Prestige can fund an action. It can give an action a budget to be tried. But it cannot settle an action. Only the ground can settle it. The Ground Maintainer must be blind to prestige."

A survey showing that most economists accept the accounting identity is not a ground reading of harm; it is agreement inside a frame. The legitimate ask is to give the shape a budget to be tried against data, and let the ground settle it. This is why `02_outreach_email.md` asks for a refutation, not endorsement.

## 7. My own audit is a simulation, not a ground reading

Same essay: "LLMs can quickly simulate an approximate answer/output with the formula found in the architecture post. It will often be better than the generic outputs that you will usually get otherwise too (intelligence is essentially arrangement). However, the truly testable output would need to be derived from the real architecture or working the formula yourself."

Therefore `01_figure_grounded_audit.md` and this entire package are simulated derivations checked for internal consistency against the figures and the sources actually opened. They are not tests of the economic claim. They cannot settle whether any real trade deficit is harmful. The verdict on a named country waits for a ground reading.

## 8. Consequence for this question

The shape returned here is: a four-condition union, evaluated per slot, with the balance as an instrument, the contest term refused, harm conditional on use and financing composition, and a declared `gap` for the empirical verdict. Eight essays were consulted for the canonical reading; three were opened directly this session and five are recorded from the exemplar. That difference is stated rather than hidden, and none of the eight is counted as an independent world witness.
