## 0. The residual in effect terms

The observation is a persistent gap between imports and exports in a country-slot. The competitiveness frame predicts that this gap registers as a loss, a failure to compete, a national harm. What actually arrives is a deficit coexisting with capital inflows, high investment, and in some cases strong growth. The residual R = |Γ(b,a) - o'| is the mismatch between the contest frame's prediction (deficit equals losing) and the observation (deficit equals net borrowing from the rest of the world, with outcomes that vary by slot).

**R by arity, symmetry and scale.** Arity: a comparison between two quantities, imports against exports, and saving against investment (the comparison bond ⋛, "a threshold relation between two quantities", Operators table :48 and Fig 12 :87). Symmetry: a deficit and a surplus are inverses under the accounting identity, but they are *not* symmetric under the contest frame, which assigns valence to the sign. That asymmetry is the thing under audit, not an assumption. Scale: national accounts (one slot), sector and local labour markets (sub-slots), and the global capital market (the habitat that finances the slot). The transformation between the national account and the local labour market is lossy, so a national reading cannot be played back down to a factory town without measuring the gap (Fig 4).

**Per-slot warning, before any conclusion.** Fig 1 :19: "The residual is indexed by object slot, not aggregated." Fig 1 :22: "Aggregating across slots is how a live signal disappears into an average." A national deficit number averaged over all sectors is exactly the aggregation the figures forbid as a verdict.

| domain | actors | currency | anchor | status |
|---|---|---|---|---|

| D3 Distribution and adjustment | specific industries, local labour markets, workers | jobs, wages, participation, transfer payments | observed local employment and wage data | admitted |
| D4 Financing and sustainability | foreign creditors, domestic borrowers, reserve authorities | gross and net external positions, currency and maturity of debt, exchange rate | rollover outcomes, spreads, reversal events, solvency | admitted |

| X1 "The deficit itself is the national loss" | the balance number | a score | none nameable when pushed: the number moves when exchange rates, saving or investment move | not a ground; refunded to D2 |

**Partition or union.** This is a **union**, not a partition (Fig 9 :55: "A bundled problem is usually a union of overlapping conditions, not a list of separate ones."). The either/or in the question bundles at least four conditions: (a) an accounting fact, (b) a causal competitiveness claim, (c) a distributional claim, (d) a sustainability claim. A single lever clears the region where only one condition holds and leaves the intersections standing (Fig 9 :57: "What remains is smaller and it is also categorically different: a case in two conditions is not a harder case of either."). Therefore the question's own either/or is not a well-formed partition and must be unbundled before a verdict is attempted.

**Order by dependency (Fig 3).** perception of the measured flow, then vocabulary (S - I, financial account, per-slot), then objective (what counts as harm: a ground outcome, not a score), then planning (which lever), then learn and carry. The middle link is the one that fails in D2: it has terms for winning and losing but no instrument for what would count as progress (Fig 3 :25-26: "The link between them, having terms for what a situation offers and being able to say what would count as progress, has neither, so it attracts the least attention and the fewest instruments.").

## 3. Findings

### F1 The question is a union compound, not one hard question (decisive for the framing)

Fig 9 :55: "A bundled problem is usually a union of overlapping conditions, not a list of separate ones." Fig 9 :57: "What remains is smaller and it is also categorically different: a case in two conditions is not a harder case of either." Fig 9 :54: "And when the split lands on a union rather than a partition, unbundle before you search again."

The accounting fact (deficit equals capital inflow) can be true while a local harm is also true and a sustainability risk is also true. Answering only the accounting part with "not harmful" clears the region where the other conditions do not hold and leaves the intersections standing. Conversely, answering only the distributional part with "harmful" mistakes a sub-slot for the national slot. The question as posed ("harmful, or rather saving and flows") is therefore a false either/or.

- Refuter: a single mechanism that predicts the national balance, the local employment outcome and the reversal risk with one variable and no compound would collapse the union and make this finding false.

### F2 The trade balance is an instrument, not the anchor (decisive for the contest frame)

Fig 2 :29: "An anchor is whatever settles the question and does not change its answer because of what any frame thinks. A measurement, an experiment, a counter, a market, a patient outcome." Fig 2 :36: "An anchor is legitimate when the question is constitutively about it." Fig 2 :37-38: "instruments can be wrong on axes they do not measure."

The question "is this deficit harmful?" is constitutively about real outcomes: consumption, employment, income, and the ability to service external liabilities. The balance is a measurement of net flows, so it is an instrument. It moves when exchange rates, saving and investment move; it does not sit outside the frames. Using it as the scoreboard is using an instrument as an anchor, exactly the failure Fig 2 names. Fig 1 :24-27 disqualifies a frame scoring itself with a quantity it also produces: "They are frame-internal. A frame cannot score itself with a quantity it also produces."

- Refuter: a ground reading in which real outcomes track the *sign* of the balance one-for-one across all slots, and the balance does not move when exchange rates or saving are pushed, would promote it to an anchor and refute this finding.

### F3 A refusal is owed, not a new theory (the "deficit equals loss" term)

Fig 5 :51: "The four kinds sort a gap by what is missing. None of them sorts a gap by what is wrong." Fig 5 :53: "refuted: a term we hold is wrong; a term is owed that says so." `THE_FORMULA.md` step 2 (REFUTED): "a term held is false; what is owed is a refusal, not an explanation."

The term "a trade deficit is a national loss of competitiveness" is not missing content to be minted. It is a wrong binding of the balance to a score. What is owed is a refusal that removes its standing to be composed over. Nothing is deleted; the reach falls because something is added (Fig 6 :42-43: "What is recorded only grows. What is reachable is derived, and it can fall."). This is why the answer to the question's first horn is not a new theory of trade but a negation.

- Refuter: a derivation showing the contest term is not wrong but merely incomplete, and that adding capital-flow content leaves its standing intact, would turn this back into a mint and refute the refusal.

### F4 Per-slot indexing: national "not harmful" must not be averaged over local harm

Fig 1 :19: "The residual is indexed by object slot, not aggregated." Fig 1 :22: "Aggregating across slots is how a live signal disappears into an average." Fig 11 :49: "An actor's valence is relative to the residual, not a property it carries." Fig 4 :30: "So take the round trip and measure the gap. Without it you are navigating by dead reckoning: confident about a position nobody checked."

World evidence (Autor, Dorn and Hanson, AER 2013; NBER Digest 2025) shows that rising import competition raises unemployment and lowers wages in *local* labour markets, explaining about one quarter of the contemporaneous aggregate manufacturing decline. This does not refute the accounting identity; it occupies D3. The same inflow that is a benefactor to the national investment slot can be a malfactor to a specific factory town. No standing list of good actors survives a change of residual (Fig 11 :51). Carrying the national "not harmful" verdict down to the local slot without measuring the round-trip gap is dead reckoning.

- Refuter: a source with a genuinely different evidence pool showing import-competition harms uniformly distributed across slots, with no local concentration and no slow adjustment, would break the per-slot claim.

### F5 The identity is definitional, not causal, and it is one witness, not two

Fig 6 :23: "No frame escapes its own closure. The bound moves only on contact with a frame whose closure differs." Fig 8 :50: "Convergent derivation and adopted import look identical in the contents. Only the record of where each came from separates them."

The identity CA = S - I and the balance-of-payments mirror CA = net capital inflow are two readings of the same national accounts (Dallas Fed; IMF 2026; CGD 2025; CRS 2020; OpenStax 2022; PIIE 2024). They are internally convergent, which is corroboration at maximum independence *within* the accounting frame, but they are all restatements of one evidence pool. They cannot, by themselves, settle causation: a deficit can be read as trade-driven or capital-driven. The sources explicitly decline to settle direction from the identity alone. The D3 and D5 sources (labour economics, psychology, economic history) are genuinely low-overlap witnesses for the *mechanism shape*, not for the national-accounting fact.

- Refuter: two sources from non-overlapping closures that derive the direction of causality from independent instruments would move this from `general` (identity) toward `specified` (direction), and would change the witness count.

### F6 Financing composition carries what the net balance discards (D4)

Fig 6 :25: "An import adds no atom to the world." Fig 12 :125-127: "A composition costs in two currencies, and they do not add. Bits price the description. Actions price finding out whether it holds." Fig 2 :40: "disqualifying for a verdict: agreement about the world is worthless when the agreeing frames share an evidence pool."

The sign of the net balance is a coarse description. World sources (World Bank 2018; BIS WP 525, 2015; IMF 2017) report that a deficit financing productive investment with stable, long-horizon liabilities has a different reversal and growth profile from one financing consumption with short-term foreign-currency debt, and that sudden stops are driven by *gross* exposures, not by the net current account alone. The fine structure is in the financing composition, an atom the net-balance description does not hold. This is the round-trip gap in miniature: sign up, composition down, and they do not match.

- Refuter: cross-country evidence that deficits financing investment and deficits financing consumption have identical reversal probabilities and growth outcomes, conditional on size, would make composition idle and refute this finding.

### F7 The MDL bargain favours the accounting description over the contest description

Fig 5 :43: "A new term is worth having when stating it, plus what remains unexplained after it, costs less than what was unexplained before." Fig 5 :44: "A term that explains everything by saying nothing". Fig 9 :60: "Use filters for the budget and witnesses for the verdict. Never let a filter hand you a verdict."

"Deficit equals loss of competitiveness" explains the observation by renaming it with a scoreboard, and leaves the capital inflow unexplained. "Deficit equals S below I, mirrored by net capital inflow" states the same observation in fewer terms and predicts where the financing must show up. The contest term loses the bargain. Note the discipline: the accounting identity is a *filter for the budget*, a cheap description of the flow, never itself a witness for the welfare verdict. The verdict waits on the ground (Fig 9 :42: "narrowing gets you close, the ground decides").

- Refuter: a competitiveness term that predicts the local harm and the reversal risk, not merely the sign, at lower total description cost would win the bargain and refute this finding.

### F8 The cognitive and historical frame explains persistence, not the economics

Fig 2 :35: "Alignment is triangulation, not negotiation. A mirror cannot correct you." Fig 7 :13: "agreement compounds, evidence does not." Davidai and Tepper (Nature Reviews Psychology 2023) define zero-sum beliefs as the view that "one party's gains are inevitably accrued at other parties' expense" across "interpersonal, intergroup and international relations". Brutger and Rathbun (2025) find that the term trade deficit itself acts as a pejorative heuristic. Oxford's entry on mercantilism records that prosperity was read as "a zero-sum game" and that "one reason for this thinking was the use of trade balances."

These are low-overlap witnesses for *why the contest frame is sticky*. They corroborate the frame diagnosis, not the accounting verdict, and pooling them with the economics sources would be a category error. Their agreement with the figures is expected and carries bounded weight.

- Refuter: evidence that zero-sum framing is uncorrelated with support for the "deficit equals loss" reading, or that mercantilist balance thinking was not tied to a static stock of wealth, would remove this corroboration without touching F1 to F6.

## 4. Pre-registered refuter (written before the conclusion)

Required by the discipline that a falsifier written before the run is a design instrument. The shape claim is refuted if any of the following holds:

1. A ground reading shows a persistent current-account deficit with no matching net capital inflow and no change in foreign claims (breaks the accounting description in D1).
2. The balance behaves as a true anchor: it does not move when exchange rates, saving or investment are pushed, and real outcomes track its sign one-for-one across all slots (breaks F2).
3. A low-overlap source shows import-competition harms uniformly distributed across slots, with no local concentration and no slow adjustment (breaks F4).
4. Cross-country data show that deficits financing productive investment and deficits financing consumption have the same reversal risk and growth outcome, with no dependence on financing composition (breaks F6).
5. A re-census shows the "deficit equals loss" term is a ground-discriminating measurement rather than a frame-internal self-score (breaks F3).
6. The conditions in F1 are shown to be a partition with a single governing variable, not a union of overlapping conditions (breaks F1).

If any fires, the corresponding finding is withdrawn and the relevant domain returns to census. Until one is run, nothing here may be labelled `grounded`.

## 7. Seven-question recursive derivation (summary)

1. **Is the balance itself the ground?** No. Fig 2 :29, :36-38. It is an instrument that moves; the question is about outcomes.
2. **Is the either/or a partition?** No. Fig 9 :54-57. It is a union of accounting, competitiveness, distribution and financing conditions.
3. **What is owed for "deficit equals loss"?** A refusal. Fig 5 :51, :53. Not a new theory of trade.
4. **Does the national verdict play back down to local slots?** Not without measuring the round-trip gap. Fig 1 :19, :22; Fig 4 :30.
5. **Does the sign of the balance hold the financing information?** No. Gross composition is a different atom. BIS 2015; World Bank 2018; Fig 6 :26.
6. **Does composition add an atom to the world?** No. It adds access to a frame whose closure differs (Fig 6 :25-26). The world's accounts are unchanged by our description.
7. **Route for the empirical question?** SEARCHED is not enough (no dataset was run here). INWARD would sharpen the national-account instrument; OUTWARD is what the labour, finance, psychology and history frames already supply. Both are distinct and neither was read against the other. Status `unreached`, candidate, `specified`.

### Updated verdict

The figure-derived shape survives: the question is a union, the balance is an instrument, the contest term is refused, per-slot verdicts stay separate, and harm is conditional on use and financing composition. What does not survive is any single-sentence answer on either horn. The specific empirical claim about any named country is a declared `gap`. Strains after re-census: none live.
