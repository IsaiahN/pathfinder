## Independent domain census

I redid the census from the residual line alone before comparing with the primary's conclusions, using the WATSON order: R by arity, symmetry and scale; one line per domain with actors, currency and anchor; partition or union; order by dependency.

- **R (arity / symmetry / scale).** The residual is whether a person's confidence tracks what an AI system does in a decision slot. Arity: three-place, person, output, ground, indexed by slot. Symmetry: ordered, since trust precedes or follows checking, and comparative, since calibration compares two quantities. Scale: individual loop, repeated interaction, social frame. This is a **union**, not a single hard question: at least two conditions must hold, and a single lever leaves the intersection standing.
- **Domain 1, the person's loop.** Actors: person, belief b, weighting w, action. Currency: confidence and per-slot residual. Anchor: the task ground, when legitimately about it. Admitted.
- **Domain 2, the system frame.** Actors: model, outputs, confidence, explanation. Currency: prediction. Anchor: the task ground, external to the frame. Admitted. Self-confidence is output, not anchor.
- **Domain 3, task ground and habitat.** Actors: task, data, environment, stakeholders, instruments. Currency: correctness and harm. Anchor: measurement or outcome. Admitted.
- **Domain 4, social and reputational frame.** Actors: users, reviews, leaderboards. Currency: agreement. Anchor: none that does not update on opinion. **Not a domain yet**, and excluded from verdict weight.
- **Order by dependency.** Perception, vocabulary, objective, planning, carry. Refuse the illegitimate anchor before minting a trust score (local Formula step 2). Describe the residual before naming a frame (Fig 9 :27-28).

My census matches the primary's three admitted domains and its union reading, and reaches the same exclusion of the social frame by the anchor test. Where the primary derived from Fig 1 and Fig 2, I derived from Fig 9's union/partition split and Fig 3's dependency order. Different starting figures, same boundaries. Since we share an evidence pool, that agreement is expected and carries no independent weight.

- the **six pre-registered refuters** are dropped, so the dashboard cannot tell whether it passed or failed the shape;

Conversely, one thing survives cleanly: "do not average away the live slot, and do not let the system check itself." That is the residue worth sending. R_T was **not** measured numerically, because no ground reading exists; it is recorded qualitatively as the enumerated set of dropped constraints. Treating a condensation as identity would set R_T to zero and would be the dead-reckoning error Fig 4 :30 names.
