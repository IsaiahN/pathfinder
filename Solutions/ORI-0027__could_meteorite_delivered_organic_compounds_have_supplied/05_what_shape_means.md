## 1. A shape is a constraint, not a filler

The canonical figures README: the framework returns "what a missing thing would have to look like (a shape, not a name)". Fig 9 :32: "you get a kind of thing, never the answer itself". Fig 13 on an out-of-reach answer: "It cannot say which primitive is missing, because naming it would mean already holding it."

From *The Library of Babel*: "This means that you can cut your search short if the set of books returned is small and descriptive enough that it provides a general shape of the answer that satisfies your question."

So a **shape** is the narrowed form of an answer you can state before you can fill in its magnitude. It constrains; it does not fill. For this question, the shape is:

> any exogenous contribution must cross a boundary as an import, carry a provenance trace, explain a gap the receiving closure has, survive the entry channel, and become a head start that local chemistry composes over.

That shape is stateable without knowing the share. "Meteorites supplied 42 percent" is not a shape, it is a name with a number attached, and the figures do not return it.

## 3. What licenses going beyond a shape

Two things, both outside the shape itself:

1. **The ground settles it.** `THE_FORMULA.md` step 5: "the ground settles it. Verified only by what pays. A gate is not the ground." The figures README: the framework "stops at the limit of what the available instruments can distinguish, and records the lineage so the stop is honest."
2. **A provenanced import from a frame whose closure differs** (Fig 6 :23, Fig 8 :50-52), with a source gate and a shadow test.

*They're Scanning Books When They Should* sharpens the limit: "A minimum viable set of six terms is a human's careful guess at reality's compression axes; another sensorium's minimum set need not be those six." And: "Declaring the primitives does not make them neutral. It makes the choice visible." For this question the relevant choice is which molecules count as "building blocks". That choice is ours, and must be recorded, not hidden inside the word "supply".

## 4. Why the magnitude is not a nameable slot

*The Hard Problem is About Source* gives the mechanism. An instrument reads green and "cannot comment on the source": one green light, or yellow plus blue, or RGB, or CMYK. "If several paths create the same behavior, then behavior is a projection with lossy information." The reading does not determine the source; "An instrument that can't distinguish these cases demonstrates its own inadequacy, it cannot claim anything substantial about what is being measured or what causes it." It was the spectrometer, not an argument, that resolved it.

Applied here: detecting glycine in a meteorite does not tell you whether that glycine entered the prebiotic inventory. The molecule is the projection; the route is the source. Stable isotope anomalies can separate extraterrestrial from terrestrial glycine, which is the spectrometer step. A share still requires the incorporation route and the counterfactual, which are further instruments. This is not a ban on naming molecules already detected. It is a refusal to name the *route* from the reading alone.

*How Intelligence Actually Works* adds the lineage condition: "indiscernibility is not identity" and "the seed was always the seedline". A delivered molecule that is indistinguishable from a terrestrial one still has a different lineage, and the lineage is the term, not documentation of it (Fig 12 :130).

## 5. Answer to the shape question for ORI-0027

**What does the framework constrain here?** That any real exogenous contribution must be an import, must pass provenance and shadow gates, must survive the channel, and must become a head start that local chemistry composes over. Four conditions, conjunctive in effect.

**Does the framework return the share?** No. Fig 9 :32, the figures README's "shape, not a name", and both agree with the literature's own admission that whether delivery fostered biogenesis is not known (Pizzarello and Shock 2010, opened 2026-11-23).

**Does the framework return a name for which molecule did the work?** No. It does not select among amino acids, nucleobases or sugars. Selecting one is a binding from the cosmochemistry frame, provenanced as such. The world sources provide those names; the figures provide the gates they must pass.

**Prestige is not action.** From *The Recursive Self-Improvement Trap*: "Prestige must never be allowed to validate an action directly. [...] Prestige can fund an action. It can give an action a budget to be tried. But it cannot settle an action. Only the ground can settle it. The Ground Maintainer must be blind to prestige."
