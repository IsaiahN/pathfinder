# The Tether's answer: could meteorite-delivered organics have supplied a meaningful share?

**Mode: `general` on reachability, `specified` on the four-condition shape, NOT `grounded` on magnitude.** Simulated derivation checked against the figures (`05_what_shape_means.md` §6), not a ground reading.

## 1. The residual it answers

"Could" asks reachability. "Meaningful share" asks magnitude. These are different questions with different anchors. Held together, they let a structural result about what is possible masquerade as a result about what happened, and they let a number be demanded from a framework that returns a kind of thing.

## 2. The shape

1. **An external import can expand a closure's reach; internal composition cannot.** Fig 6 :21-23: "MINT composes inside the closure and can never add an atom." "IMPORT adds an atom, and the added atom is what expands the closure." "No frame escapes its own closure. The bound moves only on contact with a frame whose closure differs." Meteorite delivery is such a contact. **Answer to "could": yes, structurally, with no strain.** This is `general` and triangulated across Fig 6, Fig 8 :45-46 and Fig 13 :115.
2. **What crosses is a head start, not an answer.** Fig 4 :34-35: "A seed carries no small tree: it carries what will grow one, and the conditions it lands in decide what it becomes." The mixture is a flat formula: Fig 12 :5, "A set of ingredients is not one. The bond is the rest of the information."
3. **Four conditions must all hold for a material contribution.** A source with a provenance trace (Fig 6 :12), a shadow test showing it explains a gap the receiving closure has (Fig 6 :13, Fig 8 :50-52), survival of the channel (Fig 10 :24, "the ground does not decay, the channel does"), and incorporation into local chemistry as a usable head start (Fig 4 :11). Verdict per condition is `specified` and unmeasured here. The conditions are conjunctive; a zero at any one stops the claim.
4. **The comparison is ⋛, not a name.** Operators table: "a threshold relation between two quantities". A "meaningful share" compares exogenous and endogenous supply against a threshold. Fig 12 :117 reminds us that same atoms in different arrangements are different substances, so "a sugar was delivered" is a family of claims, not one.
5. **The framework does not return the share.** Fig 9 :32: "you get a kind of thing, never the answer itself." Fig 9 :42: "narrowing gets you close, the ground decides." The canonical usage rule says a shape, not a name.

## 3. What it explicitly does not say

- Not that meteorites did supply a meaningful share. That is a `grounded` claim and nothing here is grounded.
- Not a percentage, a flux estimate, or a ranking of sources. Those are measurements.
- Not that endogenous synthesis was insufficient. Fig 6 :28-31 distinguishes "is it in here and unfound?" from "does another frame hold it?". Asking SEARCHED first is cheaper; this solution does not establish that endogenous chemistry failed.
- Not that delivered molecules were more important than terrestrial ones, or that one class of molecule did the work.
- Not that the six-term substrate names amino acids, sugars or nucleobases. Fig 4 :19-20 says only actors, currency and anchor change; the figures govern the closure law at `general` mode.
- Not a verdict from the agreement of the papers cited. Most share samples, laboratories and authors (Fig 2 :40).

## 4. What would settle it - the ground

A measurement, not a further reading of the figures. From *The Hard Problem is About Source*: an instrument that reads green "cannot comment on the source"; the spectrometer resolved the case, not an argument.

1. **Source gate:** independent isotope and chiral measurements that separate extraterrestrial organics from terrestrial contamination for the specific compounds invoked. Furukawa 2019 and Callahan 2011 show this can be done and also show where it remains ambiguous.
2. **Channel:** measured survival fractions for the relevant molecules through atmospheric entry and parent-body alteration. Fig 10 :24 predicts this is where loss occurs.
3. **Incorporation:** a laboratory or field demonstration that the delivered distribution lowers the cost of reaching a functional building block under early-Earth conditions. This is the head start made checkable.
4. **Counterfactual:** an estimate of the inventory with and without exogenous delivery, under the same local chemistry. This is the ⋛ comparison. Without it, "meaningful" has no threshold.
5. **Standing refuters** from `01` §4: a closure that adds an atom by composition alone; a figure line returning the numeric share; delivered organics unusable as a head start under any habitat.

## 5. The honest stop

**Reached on shape, unreached on magnitude.** Fig 8 :39: "So a search that finds nothing is never a proof of absence." Fig 13 :134: "Unreachable is a verdict here, not a silence." The absence of a figure line for the share is not an admission that meteorites were unimportant; it is a report that the instrument for the magnitude is the ground, not the framework. The literature itself records the same stop: Pizzarello and Shock (2010, opened 2026-11-23) write that whether cosmochemical evolution resumed on Earth to foster biogenesis after delivery is not known. Indiscernibility is not identity, and a stop is not a silence.

## 6. One-paragraph version for outreach

> The Tether does not say meteorites supplied a meaningful share of life's building blocks, and it does not say they did not. It says that a closure cannot add an atom to itself, so if the prebiotic Earth lacked the needed inventory, only an import across its boundary could expand what was reachable, and meteorite delivery is exactly that kind of import. What crossed was a head start, not a finished answer: a flat mixture of ingredients whose bonds had to be supplied by local chemistry. Four conditions decide whether the contribution was material, a source with a provenance trace, a gap that source explains, survival of the entry channel, and incorporation into the local chemistry. All four are ground questions, not figure questions. "Meaningful share" is a comparison between exogenous and endogenous supply, and the figures return the shape of that comparison, not a number. This answer is a simulated derivation checked for internal consistency against the figures, not a measurement, and a refuter was written before the run.
