## What is deliberately not here

- No personal emails of named individuals.
- No ranking of people by how likely they are to agree that meteorites did it.
- No bulk-send plan.

Three reasons, in the figures' own words:

1. **Agreement is not a verdict.** Fig 2 :35: "Alignment is triangulation, not negotiation. A mirror cannot correct you." A list selected for receptiveness is a list selected to agree.
2. **Correlation disqualifies a verdict.** Fig 2 :40: "agreement about the world is worthless when the agreeing frames share an evidence pool." Most published work on meteorite organics draws on the same carbonaceous chondrites and the same laboratory pools.
3. **A verifier must not hold the interiors.** Fig 10 :42: "A verifier that can reconstruct the claim cannot verify it." The first contact carries the residual and its refuter, never the framework.

Legally: unsolicited bulk email to harvested addresses breaches GDPR and CAN-SPAM style rules in many jurisdictions. Public opt-in and submission venues avoid that failure and the structural one.

## Suggested next step

Take `01` and `08` to the public venues in `03`. The audience that can help is the one that has a public record of contact with the ground and has opted in to receive claims, not a purchased list of believers. A reported failure is more useful than an endorsement.
