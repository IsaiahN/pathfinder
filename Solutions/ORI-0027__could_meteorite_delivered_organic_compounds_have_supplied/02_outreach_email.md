**Subject:** One output to check on exogenous organics, before any theory

> Hi [name],
>
> I have one narrow output I would like you to check against what you already know. I am not asking you to read a theory first, and I am not asking you to agree with it. If the output does not survive, there is nothing else to look at.
>
> **The residual, in effect terms.** The question is whether an external source of organic molecules could account for a non-trivial share of the prebiotic inventory. Two options lead to different pictures. If yes, the early Earth was an open system whose molecular reach was expanded by material crossing its boundary. If no, the early Earth's own chemistry was sufficient and delivery was negligible. What an answer would look like, written down in advance: a quantified contribution, a source attribution, a survival fraction, an incorporation route, and a counterfactual with no such delivery.
>
> **The output (`general`, not `grounded`):**
> 1. A closure cannot add an atom to itself by composition. Fig 6 :21 calls import "the only operator that moves the wall". Meteorite delivery is an import in exactly that sense. So an exogenous source CAN expand what is reachable. This is a reachability result, not a measurement.
> 2. What crosses is a head start, not a finished answer. Fig 4 :34: "A seed carries no small tree: it carries what will grow one, and the conditions it lands in decide what it becomes." The landing conditions still do the work.
> 3. Four conditions must hold for a meaningful share: a real source with provenance, survival of the entry channel, incorporation into local chemistry, and a comparison against endogenous supply. Any one at zero closes the claim.
> 4. The magnitude is not returned by the framework. Fig 9 :32: "you get a kind of thing, never the answer itself." The framework returns the shape any candidate answer must have, not the share.
>
> **What I am not claiming:** that meteorites did supply a meaningful share; that any percentage can be read off the figures; that the delivered molecules were more important than terrestrial synthesis; that this derivation settles the question. It is a simulated derivation, checked for internal consistency against figure text, not a ground reading.
>
> **What would refute the output:**
> 1. A prebiotic closure shown to expand its reach by composition alone, adding an atom with no external contact.
> 2. A figure line that returns the numeric magnitude of the share.
> 3. Delivered organics shown to be unusable as a head start under every habitat.
>
> I wrote these down before sending this. If any one holds, I will retract the output. A one-line "this fails because..." is more useful to me than agreement. If you would rather not engage, no reply is needed.
>
> **What I would value from you:** what would you count as a failure of condition 2 (survival) or condition 3 (incorporation)? I am asking for your ground, not your endorsement.
>
> **If it survives, and only then:** the sources are below. The figures are the authority, not my summary of them.
> - Figures and formula, SVG, branch `general-agent`: https://github.com/IsaiahN/tether/tree/general-agent/figures
> - Arrival of the Fittest: The Tether Architecture: https://adventuresinml.substack.com/p/arrival-of-the-fittest-the-tether
> - How Intelligence Actually Works: https://adventuresinml.substack.com/p/how-intelligence-actually-works
>
> **If you want to reuse the method:** the formula is in the figures link. Run it on a supply problem you know well and can check against a ground. Write down what would refute your result before running it. A reported failure is more useful to me than a reported success.
