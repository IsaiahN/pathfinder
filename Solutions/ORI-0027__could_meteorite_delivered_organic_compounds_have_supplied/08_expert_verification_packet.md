# Expert verification packet - the Tether on meteorite-delivered organics

**What this is:** a claim in a form a cosmochemist, prebiotic chemist or planetary scientist can check. Mode, derivation and refuters are stated before the conclusion, so a reader knows what to look for and what would settle it.

**Mode: `general` on reachability, `specified` on the four-condition shape, NOT `grounded` on magnitude.** No percentage is asserted and no measurement is claimed by this packet.

**Status:** a simulated derivation checked for internal consistency against the figures, not a ground reading. It cannot settle the question it reports on.

## 1. The claim, in one paragraph

A closure cannot add an atom to itself by composition; only an import across its boundary can expand what is reachable. Meteorite delivery is such an import, so an exogenous source CAN expand the prebiotic inventory. That is a reachability result, not a claim that it happened at a material level. What crossed was a head start, not a finished answer: a flat mixture of compounds whose bonds still had to be supplied by local chemistry. For the contribution to be "meaningful", four conditions must hold: a source with a provenance trace, a shadow test showing it explains a gap the receiving chemistry has, survival of the entry channel, and incorporation into the local chemistry. "Meaningful share" is a comparison between two quantities, and the figures return the shape of that comparison, not a number. The magnitude is ground-owed.

## 2. Three checkable sub-claims

### C1 - framework-internal; checkable by reading Figure 6

- **Statement:** a closure cannot add an atom to itself; import is the only operator that moves the wall. Delivery is an import in this sense, so the "could" part of the question has a yes answer at `general` mode.
- **Source, verbatim:** Fig 6 :21 "MINT composes inside the closure and can never add an atom." Fig 6 :22 "IMPORT adds an atom, and the added atom is what expands the closure." Fig 6 :14 "the only operator that moves the wall". Fig 6 :23 "No frame escapes its own closure. The bound moves only on contact with a frame whose closure differs."
- **Refuter:** show a prebiotic closure expanding its reach by composition alone, adding an atom with no external contact, or show the operator does not apply across a physical membrane.

### C2 - cosmochemistry-facing; checkable against published measurements

- **Statement:** the provenance and channel conditions are empirically checkable and are partly checked, but they do not by themselves yield a share. Isotope anomalies separate extraterrestrial organics from contamination; parent-body alteration and entry can destroy the compounds; measured abundances (parts per billion for sugars) are inputs to a comparison, not the comparison.
- **What to check:** Fig 6 :12 "source gate: provenance"; Fig 10 :24 "the ground does not decay, the channel does". Against your own knowledge: do the isotope and chiral discriminators used in the cited papers actually separate source for the compounds invoked? Is the survival fraction known, or assumed? Does a reported concentration tell you anything about incorporation?
- **Refuter:** exhibit a case where provenance is fully resolved and survival is 100 percent, and the supply conclusion is unchanged; or show that concentration measurements alone settle the incorporation condition.

### C3 - framework-internal derivation about order and comparisons

- **Statement:** the gates have a dependency order; magnitude comes last. "Meaningful share" is a ⋛ comparison, which the figures define as a threshold relation between two quantities, and the framework returns a shape rather than a threshold value.
- **Source, verbatim:** Fig 3 :20 "The chain is ordered by dependency, not by importance." Fig 8 :53 "Residual first, frame second." Fig 9 :32 "you get a kind of thing, never the answer itself". Fig 9 :42 "narrowing gets you close, the ground decides".
- **Refuter:** show that magnitude can be decided before provenance, survival and incorporation are settled, because those conditions cannot change it; or produce a figure line that returns the numeric share.

## 3. What is explicitly not claimed

- Not that meteorites did supply a meaningful share. That would be `grounded`, and nothing here is.
- Not a percentage, flux, or ranking of exogenous versus endogenous sources.
- Not that endogenous synthesis failed. Fig 6 :28-31 asks SEARCHED first; this packet does not establish a closed endogenous reach.
- Not that all delivered organics are useful. Fig 11 :49: "An actor's valence is relative to the residual, not a property it carries." A mixture can be a benefactor to a supply residual and a malfactor to a mixture-dominance residual.
- Not that delivered glycine and terrestrial glycine are different substances. They are one referent with two names (Operators table :24-26); only the lineage distinguishes them (Fig 12 :130).
- Not a verdict from agreement among the cited papers. Most share samples, laboratories and authors (Fig 2 :40).
- Not a claim that the six-term substrate names amino acids, nucleobases or sugars.

## 5. What would settle it

1. Independent isotope and chiral source attribution for the specific compounds invoked.
2. Measured survival fractions through atmospheric entry and parent-body alteration.
3. A demonstration that the delivered distribution lowers the cost of reaching a functional building block under early-Earth conditions.
4. An inventory comparison with and without exogenous delivery under the same local chemistry, with an explicit threshold for "meaningful".

## 6. What an expert can check in twenty minutes

1. Read Fig 6 :21-23 and Fig 10 :24. Two short passages. Decide whether the reachability claim is sound and whether the channel condition is real.
2. Check C2 against one paper you know well: does the provenance discriminator actually discriminate, and is the concentration a contribution or just an input?
3. Name one measurement that would give the share, or say why none is possible with current instruments.
4. Send the refutation. A failure is more useful than an endorsement.
