## 0. The residual, in effect terms

The question asks whether an external source of organic molecules could account for a non-trivial fraction of the inventory from which life's building blocks were drawn.

- **Option A:** yes. The prebiotic Earth was an open system whose molecular reach was expanded by material crossing its boundary. This leads to a supply story with four live conditions: a real extraterrestrial source, survival of the entry channel, incorporation into local chemistry, and a Comparison against endogenous supply.
- **Option B:** no. The prebiotic Earth's own closure was sufficient, and delivery was negligible. This leads to a closed-system story in which the building blocks must be composed from what was already on Earth.

What an answer would look like, written down and bound before the run (Direction discipline, `THE_FORMULA.md`): a quantified contribution of exogenous organics to the prebiotic inventory, with a source attribution, a survival fraction, an incorporation route, and a stated counterfactual for an Earth with no such delivery. That is an ordinary term. It accrues a residual like anything else and is refusable.

The residual splits immediately. "Could" is a reachability question (Fig 6). "Meaningful share" is a Comparison question (Fig 12 ⋛) that requires an anchor (Fig 2 :29). Keeping these together is how a reachability verdict gets mistaken for a magnitude verdict.

### R by arity, symmetry, scale

- **Arity:** at least two quantities compared (exogenous supply, endogenous supply) under a threshold ("meaningful"). This is a ⋛ comparison, not a single magnitude. The Operators table defines ⋛ as "a threshold relation between two quantities".
- **Symmetry:** the two sources are not interchangeable. Import and mint-in-closure are different operators (Fig 6 :21-22). The comparison is ordered and threshold-like, not a symmetric join.
- **Scale:** the boundary is the prebiotic Earth's closure, a membrane (Fig 4). The actors are molecules at one scale, while the habitat spans parent asteroids, the entry channel and the surface (Fig 10 :68: "Different actors, a different currency, a different ground, and a lossy transform between").

## 3. Findings

### F1 - Delivery is the import operator, and import is the only operator that moves the wall (decisive for reachability)

Fig 6 :14: "the only operator that moves the wall"

Fig 6 :21-22: "MINT composes inside the closure and can never add an atom." "NOVEL means novel relative to atoms, not to the world. IMPORT adds an atom, and the added atom is what expands the closure."

Fig 6 :23: "No frame escapes its own closure. The bound moves only on contact with a frame whose closure differs."

Fig 13 :115: "The set is closed. The arrangements are not. That gap is why import exists."

If the prebiotic Earth's Γ lacked the atoms needed for its building blocks, no amount of internal composition could add them. Contact with a frame whose closure differs is required. Meteorite delivery is exactly such contact: an external source (Fig 6 :10, "external source", :11 names "nature · corpus · peer frame") carrying molecules across a membrane. **Verdict: `general`, triangulated** across Fig 6, Fig 8 and Fig 13 (low overlap between the reachability law, the union-surplus law and the substrate-level statement). It says an import CAN expand the reach. It does not say this import DID, or by how much.

**Refuter:** show a prebiotic closure expanding its reach by pure composition with no external contact, that is, a mint that adds an atom to the closure. Or show that the Fig 6 operator does not apply across a physical membrane. If either holds, F1 is withdrawn.

### F2 - Delivered molecules are a head start, not a finished answer

Fig 4 :11: "a head start, never a recording"

Fig 4 :34-35: "A seed carries no small tree: it carries what will grow one, and the conditions it lands in decide what it becomes."

Fig 4 :22: "Only methods cross up. Only head starts come down."

The delivered inventory lowers the cost of later chemistry; it does not contain the chemistry. The landing conditions decide what the seed becomes. This is `general` (structural) and triangulated with F1.

**Refuter:** demonstrate that delivered organics alone, with no terrestrial chemistry, constitute a living system, or that landing-site conditions are irrelevant to what the delivered molecules become. Either would break the head-start/seed reading.

### F3 - The supply question must be enumerated as a habitat, not composed

Fig 11 :43-44: "The habitat is enumerated, never composed." "List everything in contact with the residual, then what is in contact with those, and outward until the cascade stops mattering."

Fig 11 :46: "conditions: light, water, temperature, season, terrain"

Fig 11 :49: "An actor's valence is relative to the residual, not a property it carries."

Fig 11 :51: "No standing list of good actors survives a change of residual."

A meteorite is not intrinsically a benefactor or a malfactor. Relative to a supply residual it may be a benefactor; relative to a mixture-dominance residual (the next question in the source file) it may be a malfactor. The habitat includes parent bodies, dust, entry, water, surfaces, concentration cycles, and the endogenous chemistry already present. Contact cascades: "And contact cascades" (Fig 11 :52). **Verdict: `general`, triangulated** across Fig 11 and Fig 6 (the habitat supplies the frames whose closures differ).

**Refuter:** show that supply is a single-variable question in which only total delivered mass matters, so enumeration changes nothing. Then the habitat census is unnecessary and F3 fails.

### F4 - An import has two gates: provenance and shadow test

Fig 6 :12-13: "source gate: provenance" and "shadow test: explains a gap"

Fig 8 :50-52: "Two frames can hold the same atom by convergence, reaching it separately, which is corroboration at maximum independence." "Convergent derivation and adopted import look identical in the contents. Only the record of where each came from separates them, and the surplus lives only where the closures differ."

This is where the re-census from §1 pays. The mint guards of Fig 5 were the wrong instrument. For an import the question is not "is φ novel relative to atoms(Γ)" but "does this source claim carry a provenance trace, and does it explain a gap the receiving frame has". Isotopic enrichment in meteoritic sugars and nucleobases is a provenance trace (world sources, §4 of `09_watson_answer.md`); the claim that delivery explains inventory that endogenous synthesis cannot is the shadow test. **Verdict: `general` for the gate structure, triangulated** (Fig 6 + Fig 8); `specified`, unmeasured for whether each gate is passed on the early Earth.

**Refuter:** exhibit a source claim for which no discriminator can separate terrestrial contamination from extraterrestrial origin, and show that the supply conclusion is unchanged. Then the provenance gate is not load-bearing and F4 is weakened. A second refuter: show the endogenous closure already reaches the same inventory, so the import explains no gap.

### F5 - Survival is a live guard: the ground does not decay, the channel does

Fig 10 :24: "the ground does not decay, the channel does"

Fig 10 :25-26 lists the failure modes: "stale percepts, dropped transients, saturated metrics, pooled readings"

Delivery is a channel. Atmospheric entry, parent-body aqueous alteration and terrestrial weathering can drop the very molecules whose delivery is claimed. The world literature records this directly: ribose is "a molecule as fragile as ribose" and was still detected, while degradation in parent bodies is documented. **Verdict: `general`, single-source for the figure line (Fig 10), corroborated by world sources.** Triangulation status is honest: the figure and the world sources are different pools, so the agreement is weak corroboration, not a shared-pool effect.

**Refuter:** measure 100 percent survival of all relevant organics through entry and alteration for all plausible delivery routes. Then the channel guard is zero-cost and F5 is spent.

### F6 - "Meaningful share" is a comparison, and the figure returns a shape, not the share

Fig 9 :27-28: "describe it before you go looking, or any frame you pick will seem to fit"

Fig 9 :32: "you get a kind of thing, never the answer itself"

Fig 9 :42: "narrowing gets you close, the ground decides"

Fig 12 :117: "isomer. Same atoms, different bond, different substance. The difference is invisible in the formula and decisive in the behaviour."

Operators table: ⋛ is "a threshold relation between two quantities"; "a goal is a comparison, which is why a vocabulary without it cannot state one".

The phrase "meaningful share" needs a threshold and a comparison between two quantities. The figures supply the bond (⋛) and the rule that the ground decides, but no figure line returns a number, a percentage, or a named winner. Asking the framework for the share is asking it for an answer where it returns a kind of thing. **Verdict: `general` (structural limit), triangulated** across Fig 9 and the canonical figures README's "a shape, not a name".

**Refuter:** produce a figure line that yields the numeric magnitude of the share. Until one exists, the magnitude is ground-owed, not figure-owed.

### F7 - Independence decides whether the corroboration means anything

Fig 2 :31-32: "Independence, between two frames, is how little they draw on the same evidence." "Independence is what makes agreement mean something."

Fig 2 :40: "agreement about the world is worthless when the agreeing frames share an evidence pool"

Fig 8 :45: "Two frames together reach what neither reaches alone."

Cosmochemistry (isotopic measurement of meteorite organics), prebiotic chemistry (laboratory pathways) and geology (early Earth record) are three low-overlap frames in principle. In practice, most of the sources read for this solution draw on the same carbonaceous chondrites and several share authors and laboratories. Their agreement is one pool talking. The absorptive-capacity source from business studies is genuinely independent, but it is an analogue, not evidence for the chemistry. **Verdict: `general`, triangulated** across Fig 2 and Fig 8, with the practical independence of this run declared as low.

**Refuter:** demonstrate that the three frames draw on disjoint instruments and records and that their agreement survives a source-gate check. Then the corroboration gains independent weight.

## 4. Pre-registered refuter

Written before the conclusion, per Fig 5 :65-66: "make the sought-for shape an ORDINARY TERM" and "Committing a prediction before a run is this, already working."

The shape claim is refuted if any of the following holds:

1. A prebiotic closure is shown to expand its reach by composition alone, so import is not the only operator that moves the wall (breaks Fig 6 :21-23).
2. A figure line is produced that returns the numeric magnitude of a "meaningful share" (breaks Fig 9 :32).
3. Delivered organics are shown to be unusable as a head start under every habitat (breaks Fig 4 :34-35).

Until one of these is run, no part of this answer may be labelled `grounded`. The refuter was written before the world sources were read; a conflict sends the derivation back to the census, it does not flip the verdict.

## 5. Recommendation

1. Keep the reachability verdict and the magnitude verdict separate in every sentence (F1, F6).
2. Enumerate the habitat before naming a frame (F3, Fig 8 :53).
3. Gate the import with provenance and shadow test, and state when a gate is unmeasured (F4).
4. Treat survival as a guard, not an assumption (F5).
5. Refuse to name a percentage. Say what would settle it (F6).
6. Lead outreach with the shape claim and the refuter, not with a conclusion about what meteorites did (Fig 10 :52: "Bring a characterised residual, never the source").
