## Wider literature that bears on the question, from the sources above

1. **The source gate is empirical, not rhetorical.** Carbon and nitrogen isotope anomalies distinguish meteoritic organics from terrestrial contamination (Martins 2008, Furukawa 2019, Callahan 2011). This is how a provenance claim becomes checkable, and it is why F4 does not collapse into "trust the sample".
2. **Abundance is not the same as contribution.** Furukawa 2019 reports sugars at parts per billion and three orders of magnitude below amino acids. Pizzarello and Shock 2010 report an estimated 1 to 3 percent of meteorite weight as carbon during the early impact period. Neither number is a share of the prebiotic inventory. They are inputs to the comparison, not the comparison.
3. **The channel is lossy.** Parent-body aqueous alteration can degrade sugars (Furukawa 2019), and HMT is invoked as a stable carrier precisely because formaldehyde and ammonia are easily lost (NASA 2020). This is the figure line "the ground does not decay, the channel does" meeting a measured mechanism.
4. **The honest stop is already in the literature.** Pizzarello and Shock write that whether cosmochemical evolution resumed on Earth to foster biogenesis after delivery "is not known". A figure-grounded answer that declines to name the share is aligned with the literature's own uncertainty, not behind it.
5. **Low-overlap analogue.** Absorptive capacity in business studies says external knowledge helps only when the receiver has prior related knowledge to recognise and assimilate it. As an analogue this matches the shadow test: an import explains a gap only where the receiving closure can register it. It is not evidence about meteorites.

## Distribution: offer, do not push

Figure-grounded reasons:

- **Fig 4 :22** - "Only methods cross up. Only head starts come down." Sending the full framework before a recipient has described a residual sends a recording, not a generator. Lead with the shape claim and the refuter; offer links after.
- **Fig 10 :42** - "A verifier that can reconstruct the claim cannot verify it." A reader who has absorbed the framework before giving a verdict has joined the frame being checked.
- **Fig 9 :27-28** - "describe it before you go looking, or any frame you pick will seem to fit." Ask what they would count as a failure of the survival or incorporation conditions before giving them the article.
- **Fig 2 :40** - "agreement about the world is worthless when the agreeing frames share an evidence pool." Most meteorite-organics papers share samples and laboratories. Do not pool later agreement as corroboration.
- **Fig 9 :60** - "Use filters for the budget and witnesses for the verdict. Never let a filter hand you a verdict." A venue's prestige or an author's fame is a contact-cost filter, never a witness.

**Recommended order in a reply:** (1) the residual in effect terms; (2) the four-condition shape claim; (3) the pre-registered refuter; (4) ask what they would count as a failure; (5) only then the links, with the note that the figures, not the summary, are the authority.

## The two-stage offer

- **Stage 1 before stage 2.** Fig 10 :52, "Bring a characterised residual, never the source." Fig 2 :45, "Frames range against the ground. They do not negotiate with it." The recipient checks the shape claim without holding the framework's interiors.
- **Stage 2 is opt-in by construction.** Once a reader has absorbed the theory, their agreement stops being evidence. Their verdict on stage 1 must be recorded before any theory is shown, and later agreement is never pooled as corroboration (Fig 2 :40).
- **Stage 3 sends a method, which is permitted.** Fig 4 :22. The formula is a generator, so giving it away does not give away an answer.
- **Stage 3 still needs a ground per problem.** `THE_FORMULA.md` "Direction": a frame cannot check the direction of its own question, because the shape it seeks is not a term it holds. Each user writes down what would refute their result before running it and labels the mode.
- **Hard precondition.** Stages 1-3 only work if the stage-1 output survives. As written, the answer is a shape with an open magnitude; if a recipient shows that the reachability or gate claim strains, repair before sending anything further.
