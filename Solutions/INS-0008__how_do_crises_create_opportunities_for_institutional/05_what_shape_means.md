## 1. A shape is a constraint, not a filler

The canonical figures README states the limit: the framework returns *"what a missing thing would have to look like (**a shape, not a name**)."* Fig 9 :32: *"you get a kind of thing, never the answer itself."* Fig 13 :134: *"Unreachable is a verdict here, not a silence"*.

From *The Library of Babel*: *"You can cut your search short if the set of books returned is small and descriptive enough that it provides a general shape of the answer that satisfies your question."*

So a **shape** here is the narrowed form of an answer that can be stated before it can be filled in. It constrains the branch conditions; it does not name the policy. For INS-0008, "a registered live-slot residual, a closure-different import with provenance, a channel that survives the change" is a shape. "Adopt a transparency law" is a name.

## 2. How a shape is obtained

- *Library of Babel*: use the vocabulary already held that is closest to the thing, then recurse on recurring terms that overlap with it. *"With each new term gained, you are able to narrow the space by definition and deepen your vocabulary."*
- Fig 6 :30: *"The description travels. Fails by being sharpened into terms only we can read. Cast it in effect terms."* Fig 9 :30-31: *"your words are borrowed too, so describe the effect rather than the cause."*
- *Decoding Tacit Knowledge*: *"When contact isn't possible, the best you can send is a plain description of what you'd expect to see, because anyone can check that against the world."*
- *Library of Babel* on stopping: the answer is reached when *"the finish line will also be the starting place"*, and the paradox is that by then you have *"composed the answer through the process of locating it."*

Applied here: the residual is described in effect terms (a slot fails against a ground the institution cannot move) before any frame is chosen. Fig 9 :27-28: *"describe it before you go looking, or any frame you pick will seem to fit."* If "crisis" is named first and the theory fitted afterwards, any crisis will seem to fit, which is the apophenia failure the exemplar names.

## 3. What licenses a name

Only two things, both outside the shape itself:

1. **The ground settles it.** THE_FORMULA.md step 5: *"the ground settles it. Verified only by what pays. A gate is not the ground."* The canonical README: the framework *"stops at the limit of what the available instruments can distinguish, and records the lineage so the stop is honest."*
2. **A provenanced import from a frame whose closure differs** (Fig 6 :23, Fig 8 :51), with a source gate and a shadow test.

*They're Scanning Books* sharpens the distinction: *"The real import is not a new term in the existing space. It is a new dimension of the space."* And: *"A minimum viable set of six terms is a human's careful guess at reality's compression axes; another sensorium's minimum set need not be those six."* Also: *"Declaring the primitives does not make them neutral. It makes the choice visible."*

For this question, the six physical terms are the framework's visible guess and are **not** mapped onto political atoms. Naming "the reform that works" without a ground reading turns a visible choice into an invisible one.

## 4. Why a source-discriminating instrument is owed, not a better argument

*The Hard Problem is About Source* gives the mechanism. Metamerism: an instrument reads green and *"cannot comment on the source"*. One green light, or yellow plus blue, or RGB, or CMYK, or a scheme not yet discovered, all read the same. *"If several paths create the same behavior, then behavior is a projection with lossy information."* And: *"The answer is unreached until a better instrument exists."* It was the spectrometer, not an argument, that resolved it.

For INS-0008, "reform followed the crisis" is a reading of the projection. Several sources can produce it: a genuine import, a coherent drift of the incumbent frame, a pooled metric that changed definition, or a re-equilibration that merely looked like change. Naming which one from the outcome is the metamerism error. What is owed is an instrument that discriminates among sources, not a finer story about the outcome.

*The Recursive Self-Improvement Trap*: *"the output is only as good as the validator. The agent, or the tool, or the frame, or the theory, cannot validate itself."*

## 6. Two consequences for this answer

**Prestige is not action.** From *The Recursive Self-Improvement Trap*: *"Prestige must never be allowed to validate an action directly... Prestige can fund an action. It can give an action a budget to be tried. But it cannot settle an action. Only the ground can settle it. The Ground Maintainer must be blind to prestige."* A list of famous crisis scholars who agree with the shape is the named collapse, not corroboration. The legitimate ask gives the claim a budget to be tried and lets the ground settle it.

**My own audit is a simulation, not a ground reading.** Same essay: *"LLMs can quickly simulate an approximate answer/output with the formula found in the architecture post. It will often be better than the generic outputs that you will usually get otherwise too... However, the truly testable output would need to be derived from the real architecture or working the formula yourself."* So `01` and `09` are simulated derivations, checked for internal consistency against the figures. They are **not** tests of the claim and must be labelled as such. They cannot settle anything they report on.

## 7. Society-of-agents addendum

*How a Society of Agents Would Work* makes the outreach rule concrete: *"The group shares methods, never recordings"* and *"What comes down to a newborn agent is a head start: candidates that make its search cheaper. It still has to test each one in its own world."* Applied here: send the method (describe the residual, find the differing closure, keep the channel) and the refuter, never the recorded conclusion. *How Intelligence Actually Works* names the same limit for the searcher: intelligence is a mapping built against an unforgiving environment, and *"Contact Is the Distinction Resolver"*. A crisis is precisely a contact event; whether it resolves anything depends on whether a ground that cannot be moved is present on the other side.
