# The Tether's answer: crisis, reform and entrenchment

Mode: `specified` on the institutional claims, `general` on the structural shape. Not `grounded`. Simulated derivation checked for internal consistency against the figures (`05_what_shape_means.md` section 6), not a ground reading.

## 1. The residual it answers

A crisis is a registered failure on a specific slot: `R = |Γ(b,a) − o′|` for that slot. A global picture can be near zero while one slot is live, which is a legal state (Fig 1 :13, :19). The residual is the branch difference: why does the same class of event widen constraint on power in one case and concentrate it in another?

## 2. The shape, one loop per domain, kept separate

D1, the incumbent frame. A crisis can be an opportunity only where the institution has a nameable ground it is constitutively about. If the mandate has been replaced by self-preservation, the institution is a convention that decides an outcome (Fig 10 :47) and its keeper is disqualified (Fig 10 :93). Standing earned on an earlier crisis survives that crisis as a stake (Fig 10 :61). Verdict, `specified`: an institution that cannot show which ground it answers to will route the crisis through itself. Triangulated (Fig 10 channel, Fig 2 anchor). Refuter: a ground-verified widening of constraint while the keeper's standing was untouched and the mandate unmeasured.

D2, the crisis as contact. The event has no fixed valence. Fig 11 :49-51: "An actor's valence is relative to the residual, not a property it carries" and "No standing list of good actors survives a change of residual" The same shock can break concentrated power or intensify it, depending on the residual and ground it meets. Verdict, `specified`: asking "is this crisis a reform opportunity" without naming the residual is unanswerable. Triangulated (Fig 11 valence, Acemoglu and Robinson secondary). Refuter: invariant event valence across a documented change of residual.

D3, the reform process. Reform is an import, not more search. Fig 6 :14: "the only operator that moves the wall". Fig 6 :23: "No frame escapes its own closure. The bound moves only on contact with a frame whose closure differs." Fig 8 :32: "Closure is idempotent. Composing inside a closed set never leaves it, so no search, however long, adds an atom." The imported atom needs provenance and a shadow test, because Fig 8 :51 says convergent derivation and adopted import look identical in the contents. The new arrangement must change contact (Fig 11 :40), be a method rather than a recording (Fig 4 :22-25), and be selected as a room that differs from the last and matches the residual it surfaced (Fig 7 :36). Verdict, `specified`: a window that closes without an import is one long room, motion without progress (Fig 7 :32). Triangulated (Fig 6/8 vs Fig 7/4, low overlap). Refuter: reach expanded with no differing closure crossing.

D4, entrenchment. This is not a separate force. It is D1 to D3 with the channel degraded. Fig 10 :24: "the ground does not decay, the channel does"; Fig 10 :25-26: "stale percepts, dropped transients, saturated metrics, pooled readings"; Fig 10 :23: "R never arrives". The anchor then becomes another frame and the drift is coherent, which is why nobody notices (Fig 2 :34). Agreement compounds while evidence does not (Fig 7 :12-13). An emergency actor can be introduced whose cascade was unenumerated (Fig 11 :65), and because exclusion is unavailable (Fig 11 :61-62) the emergency framework persists after the event. Verdict, `specified`: entrenchment is the failure mode of the same loop, not its opposite. Triangulated (Fig 2/7 vs Fig 10/11). Refuter: ground-verified reform with a measurably dead channel.

Verdicts are not averaged. The four domain verdicts stand side by side. The shape is a union of three conditions (Fig 9 :55-57): C1 a registered live-slot residual, C2 a closure-different import with provenance, C3 a channel to the ground that survives the change. Reform appears where they conjoin; entrenchment appears where C3 fails or C2 is replaced by a blend.

## 3. What it explicitly does not say

- Not that a crisis is necessary for reform. Arrangements over a closed set can change incrementally (Fig 13 :115, :116-118).
- Not that "critical juncture" is a cause. It is a filter for the budget, never a witness (Fig 9 :60).
- Not which policy works. The framework returns a shape, not a name. Fig 9 :32: "you get a kind of thing, never the answer itself."
- Not that the six physical terms map onto political atoms. That mapping was refused in `01_figure_grounded_audit.md` F10 and section 3 strains.
- Not that repeal subtracts an actor. Refusal is negation added; nothing is deleted (THE_FORMULA.md step 3).
- Not `grounded`. No institutional measurement was taken.

## 4. What would settle it: the ground, not agreement

A source-discriminating instrument, not a better argument. From The Hard Problem is About Source: a reading of green "cannot comment on the source"; the spectrometer resolved it. Here the discriminating questions are:

1. Slot test. Is there a live slot residual before the branch, hidden by an aggregate near zero? (Fig 1 :13, :19)
2. Provenance test. What crossed from a closure that differs, with a source gate and a shadow test? (Fig 8 :51)
3. Channel test. Is the channel to the ground still returning readings after the change, with no saturated metric or pooled reading? (Fig 10 :24-26)
4. Room test. Did the next room differ from the last and match the residual? (Fig 7 :36)
5. Contact test. Did the change alter contact with the residual, or only the vocabulary describing it? (Fig 11 :40)
6. Cycle test. Did the oversight part survive its own use across instances? (Fig 12 :119)

The standing falsifiers are the six in `01_figure_grounded_audit.md` section 4. If any fires, the claim is withdrawn or sent back to census.

## 5. The honest stop

Unreached, not unreachable. The figure set narrows the shape of the answer and stops. They're Scanning Books: "A minimum viable set of six terms is a human's careful guess at reality's compression axes; another sensorium's minimum set need not be those six." Declaring the primitives makes the choice visible, not neutral. Fig 13 :134: "Unreachable is a verdict here, not a silence"; Fig 8 :39: "So a search that finds nothing is never a proof of absence."

Which specific reform works in a specific crisis is a declared `gap`. No figure line governs it after a genuine re-census. What is owed is an instrument that discriminates among sources, not another argument about the outcome. Indiscernibility is not identity: the lineage must be kept even when the story looks complete.

## 6. One-paragraph version for outreach

> The Tether does not name the reform that will work, and it does not treat a crisis as a cause with a fixed valence. It says that a crisis matters only as a registered failure on a specific slot, hidden easily by an aggregate headline; that the branch after it is a union of three conditions, namely a failure registered against a ground the institution cannot move, an import from a closure that differs with a record of where it came from, and a channel to that ground that survives the change. Where the third condition fails, the same event is processed inside the incumbent's own vocabulary, the anchor quietly becomes another frame, and power is entrenched by the very measure meant to restrain it. Reform and entrenchment use the same actors under different bonds, so they are isomers whose difference is invisible in a list of reforms passed and decisive in behaviour. The framework offers the shape any candidate answer must have, five or six falsifiers written in advance, and the instruments that would close it. Which policy works stays open until a ground settles it.
