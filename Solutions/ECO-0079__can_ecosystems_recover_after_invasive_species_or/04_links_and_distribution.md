## Two cautions about the wider literature

1. **Resilience is a borrowed word, not a shared evidence pool.** Holling (ecology), May (mathematics), Scheffer (dynamical systems), Hollnagel (engineering) and Su and Junge (business) converge on similar shapes from different evidence. Fig 8 :50 says "Two frames can hold the same atom by convergence, reaching it separately, which is corroboration at maximum independence", but the shared word alone is not proof of independence. Each is read directly and its field is recorded in the ledger.
2. **Jones et al. 1994 is already inside the figure's citation list.** Fig 11 :65 names it. It corroborates the figure's maintained-mode reading, but it is not an independent witness against the framework. Overlap is marked accordingly.

## Distribution: offer, do not push

Figure-grounded reasons:

- **Fig 10 :52** "Bring a characterised residual, never the source." Sending the full framework before anyone has described a residual sends a recording, not a generator. Lead with the shape claim and its refuter.
- **Fig 10 :42** "A verifier that can reconstruct the claim cannot verify it." A recipient who has absorbed the interiors before giving a verdict has joined the frame being checked.
- **Fig 9 :27-28** "describe it before you go looking, or any frame you pick will seem to fit." Ask what they would expect to see before giving them the source links.
- **Fig 2 :40** "disqualifying for a verdict: agreement about the world is worthless when the agreeing frames share an evidence pool". Do not pool the replies of people who all read the same review.
- **Fig 7 :13** "same closure, handed along; agreement compounds, evidence does not". No mail list.
- **Fig 4 :22** "Only methods cross up. Only head starts come down." Stage 3 sends a method, not an answer.

**Recommended order in a reply:** (1) the shape claim and its pre-registered refuters; (2) ask what they would count as a failure and record it; (3) only then the four framework links, with the note that the figures, not the summary, are the authority; (4) if asked, the formula as a reusable method with the instruction that each user writes their own refuter and states their own mode.

## Two-stage offer, restated

1. **Stage 1:** validate the output against a ground the recipient names. No theory shown.
2. **Stage 2, opt-in only:** if curious, the four framework links. Their stage-1 verdict was already recorded, and later agreement is never pooled as corroboration.
3. **Stage 3:** the formula as a method to reuse, with a fresh refuter and mode per problem.

**Hard precondition:** stages 1 to 3 only work if the stage-1 output survives. The output is a shape and five refuters, not a universal claim. If a recipient fires one of the refuters, stop and record the failure rather than offering the theory.
