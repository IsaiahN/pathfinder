# Expert verification packet: the Tether on ecosystem recovery

**What this is:** a claim in a form an ecosystem-recovery researcher can check. Mode, derivation and refuters are stated before the conclusion, so a reader knows what to look for and what would settle it. One-to-one use only, never a bulk send.

**Mode: `specified` on the shape, `general` on structural rules, `gap` on the universal yes/no. Not `grounded`.** No field measurement is claimed. This is a simulated derivation checked for internal consistency against the figures, not a ground reading.

## 1. The claim, in one paragraph

"Recovery" after an invasive species or pathogen rewires a food web is not a property of an ecosystem and is not a universal yes/no. It is a union of two questions. In an unmaintained habitat nothing selects and what survives is what the ground kept, so "recovered" is a retrospective label. Where a seat acts, introduction is available and exclusion is not: removal substitutes a habitat, and the work is permanent. In both cases the state variable is the bond, not the species list, so two webs with identical species can be different substances, and the residual can be three steps out. The shape any answer must have is a stated reference configuration, a per-slot comparison of interaction strengths and signs, an enumerated contact cascade, the sign of the feedback loop, and an explicit seat statement. The figures give that shape and five refuters. They give no universal recovery threshold, which is recorded as `gap`.

## 2. Three checkable sub-claims

### C1: framework-internal and ecology-facing; checkable against Fig 12

- **Statement:** two food webs with the same species set but different interaction-strength matrices are different substances. A species census cannot distinguish them.
- **Source, verbatim:** Fig 12 :117 "isomer. Same atoms, different bond, different substance. The difference is invisible in the formula and decisive in the behaviour." Also Fig 12 :5 "A set of ingredients is not one. The bond is the rest of the information, and it is the half nothing was recording." And Fig 12 :95 "Two entries with an identical ingredient set are not variants of one thing."
- **World corroboration, independent:** Holling 1973 defines resilience as persistence of relationships; Ings et al. 2009 say "it is the distribution of interaction strengths and the configuration of complexity, rather than just its magnitude, that governs network stability and structure."
- **Refuter:** a controlled pair of webs sharing every species and every measured interaction strength and sign, yet behaving as different substances on every response variable. If shown, the bond claim buys nothing and is withdrawn.

### C2: ecology-facing; checkable against Fig 11 and a field programme

- **Statement:** scoping the habitat to direct contact misses residuals three steps out, and a seat's removal substitutes a habitat rather than removing one; valence is relative to the stated reference.
- **Source, verbatim:** Fig 11 :52-53 "And contact cascades. Scoping the habitat to direct contact is how DDT reached the eagles: first-order enumeration, second-order effect, and the residual that mattered was three steps out." Fig 11 :66 "Isolation is not removal of the habitat. It is substitution of one habitat for another." Fig 11 :50 "The same actor is a benefactor to one residual and a malfactor to another, so mutualism and parasitism are relations rather than kinds."
- **World corroboration:** Jones, Lawton and Shachak 1994 on ecosystem engineering; Simberloff et al. 2013, "Species are neither and, furthermore, invasion pertains to the population level, not the species level." Note that Jones et al. is already cited by Fig 11 :65, so it is an instance inside the framework, not an independent witness.
- **Refuter:** first-order enumeration predicting third-order outcomes as well as full enumeration across repeated events, or a case where removal truly removes a contact with no substitution and the reference is regained with no introduction.

### C3: measurement-facing; checkable against Fig 1, Fig 3, Fig 12

- **Statement:** recovery is an ordered comparison against a stated reference, measured per slot, and the feedback sign decides closure or flip. An aggregate index can hide a live slot.
- **Source, verbatim:** Fig 1 :19 "The residual is indexed by object slot, not aggregated." Fig 3 :10 "it can say what would count as progress here, in its own terms". Fig 12 :121 "cycle. The output re-enters as input. The sign of the loop decides whether it closes, and a chain cannot close by construction."
- **World corroboration:** Carpenter et al. 2001, "To assess a system's resilience, one must specify which system configuration and which disturbances are of interest." Dakos and Kéfi 2022 report that measuring ecological resilience "has remained elusive". Scheffer et al. 2009 give the early-warning instrument; May 1972 gives the sharp stability transition.
- **Refuter:** an aggregate index predicting all per-slot failures as well as the per-slot series, or a web with unchanged sign that flips, or reversed sign that recloses.

## 3. What is explicitly not claimed

- Not "ecosystems recover" and not "they never recover". No universal verdict is figure-derived; the universal question is `gap`.
- Not that eradication restores an interaction network. It substitutes a habitat and creates two invisible failure modes.
- Not that an invasive species is a malfactor by kind. Valence is relative to the reference and the slot.
- Not that the framework's habitat is the ecological niche. The source article's "Three mappings offered and declined" says "The resemblance is real and the definitions do not align".
- Not that diversity indices or ecosystem-health scores are the state variable. They are projections with loss.
- Not `grounded`. No site, time series or experiment settles the claim here.
- Not corroborated by agreement among people who read the same literature (Fig 2 :40).

## 5. What would settle it

A source-discriminating field instrument, not more argument. From *The Hard Problem is About Source*: a reading produced by several paths "cannot comment on the source"; the spectrometer resolved the analogy.

1. State the reference configuration and disturbance before the season. Does the return land on that reference or on a different basin with the same cast?
2. Measure interaction strengths and signs per slot. Can the instrument distinguish two isomer webs?
3. Enumerate contacts to the order where the cascade stops mattering. Was a third-order effect missed by first-order scoping?
4. Estimate the loop sign before and after. Does the web reclose or flip?
5. If a seat acts, record every introduction and substitution and the continuing cost. Is a "solved state" claim sustainable?
6. Run the five pre-registered refuters in `01_figure_grounded_audit.md` §5. Any one firing retracts the corresponding finding.

## 6. What an expert can check in twenty minutes

1. Read Fig 12 :117, :5, :121 and Fig 11 :52-53, :50, :61, :66. Seven short passages.
2. Check C1 against a web you know: do two webs with the same species and different interaction strengths behave differently? If not, say so.
3. Check C2 against a restoration or eradication you know: was the removed actor's role substituted, and was the effect first order or three steps out?
4. Name one instrument that distinguishes a restored web from a different web with the same cast, or say why none is available.
5. Send the refutation. A failure is more useful than an endorsement, and later agreement will not be pooled as corroboration.
