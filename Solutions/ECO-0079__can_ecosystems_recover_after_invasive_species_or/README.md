## Restatement as an effect

What is being decided is whether a changed interaction network returns to a stated reference configuration after an introduced actor (an invasive species or a pathogen) rewires it. Option A treats recovery as a property of the ecosystem, so a species count returning to baseline counts as recovery. Option B treats recovery as an ordered comparison against a reference, measured per object slot, at the level of bonds (interaction strengths and signs), with cascades beyond direct contact and a named seat whose power is asymmetric. A leads to removal being read as restoration and to live slot residuals being averaged away. B leads to a stated reference, an enumerated habitat, a measured loop sign, and an honest price for permanent management work.

What an answer would look like, bound as an ordinary term before the run: *a checkable shape consisting of a named reference configuration, a per-slot interaction-strength comparison, an enumerated contact cascade to the order where the residual stops mattering, the sign of the feedback loop that either recloses or flips the web, and an explicit statement of which seat (if any) is doing the work.* That shape is the pre-registered commitment in `01_figure_grounded_audit.md` §3.

## What is deliberately not here

- No personal emails of named individuals.
- No ranking of people by how likely they are to agree that ecosystems recover.
- No bulk-send plan.
- No claim that the figures settle the empirical question for any actual ecosystem.

Three reasons, in the figures' own words:

1. **A room does not upgrade the label.** Fig 7 :13: "same closure, handed along; agreement compounds, evidence does not". Adding agreeable recipients raises the vocabulary and does not add an anchor.
2. **Correlation disqualifies a verdict.** Fig 2 :40: "disqualifying for a verdict: agreement about the world is worthless when the agreeing frames share an evidence pool". Selecting recipients for receptiveness manufactures the correlation that makes their agreement worthless.
3. **Asking for trust before checking spends independence.** Fig 10 :42: "A verifier that can reconstruct the claim cannot verify it." The first contact carries the residual and its refuter, never the framework's interiors.

Legally: unsolicited bulk email to scraped addresses breaches GDPR and CAN-SPAM style rules in many jurisdictions. That is a practical failure on top of the structural one.

## Suggested next step

Take `01` and `08` to the public submission and contact venues in `03`. Ask what the recipient would count as a failure of the shape claim. Record that verdict before showing any theory. A reported failure is more useful than an endorsement, and later agreement is never pooled as corroboration.
