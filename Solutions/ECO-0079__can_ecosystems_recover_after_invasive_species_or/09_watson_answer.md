## QUESTION

Decide, in effect terms, what the Tether says about an interaction network after an introduced actor rewires it. Option A: recovery is a property of the ecosystem, so return of the species list counts as recovery and eradication counts as restoration. Option B: recovery is an ordered comparison against a stated reference, measured per slot at the level of bonds, over an enumerated contact cascade, with the loop sign deciding closure or flip and an explicit seat statement. What each leads to: A hides live slot residuals in an average and treats removal as restoration; B names the reference, enumerates the habitat, measures bonds, prices permanent management and records where a universal predictor is absent. No universal yes/no is asserted.

Symmetry: ordered, a comparison rather than a symmetric join; return and flip are different results. Scale: direct contact first order, cascade second and third order; per slot, never aggregate. Fig 9 names a union compound when two conditions bind, so the question must be unbundled.
- **Domain 1, unmaintained habitat.** Actors: native species, invader or pathogen, resources, light, water, season, terrain. Currency: survival and contact, what persists. Anchor: what the ground kept, which does not update when a frame changes its mind. Not a partition; it runs beneath any seat.
- **Domain 2, maintained habitat.** Actors: managers, control tools, reintroductions, engineers. Currency: introduction, never subtraction. Anchor: the field outcome. The seat adds to a habitat it did not author, so D1 and D2 form a union, not a partition.
- **Domain 3, measurement and verification.** Actors: ecologists, plots, time series, interaction-strength estimates. Currency: residuals. Anchor: a field measurement, not agreement. In this simulated run all seats draw on one evidence pool, so agreement is one pool talking, not corroboration.

## VERDICT (from the figures)

The shape survives; the universal verdict does not exist in the figures. Recovery is a union of an unmaintained selection process and a maintained seat, never a single yes/no. It is an ordered comparison against a stated reference, per object slot, at the level of bonds, over a habitat enumerated until the cascade stops mattering, with the loop sign deciding closure or flip. Eradication substitutes a habitat, exclusion is unavailable to a seat, and managed recovery is permanent work priced in two currencies. An actor's valence is relative to the reference, not fixed by the label "invasive". What is absent is any figure line giving a universal recovery threshold; that is reported as `gap`, with the SEARCHED / INWARD / OUTWARD route recorded. This is `specified` and ungrounded, not a field result. The strongest single figure point for outreach is Fig 11 :63: a managed ecosystem is permanent work rather than a solved state.

- **cost if wrong:** bounded and locatable. The packet states its mode, binds the reference as an ordinary term and writes five refuters before the conclusion, so an error is findable rather than invisible. The real cost is spending the independence of outside readers by sending a universal claim instead of a checkable shape. Fig 2 :42 "Buy independence in proportion to the cost of being wrong." That cost is avoidable by leading with the shape claim and its refuter.
- **undoable:** yes. The universal claim is withdrawn rather than repaired, and no primitive is removed. A field frame can add a reference, a slot instrument and a cascade cutoff by declaration. A sent claim can be superseded by a corrected packet. Nothing in the habitat has to be undone for the shape to be re-tested.
- **reversed by:** a ground reading, not agreement. A field time series that distinguishes a restored web from a different web with the same cast would settle the bond claim; a first-order scoping that predicts third-order outcomes across repeated events would relax the cascade requirement; an aggregate index matching every per-slot failure would remove the per-slot requirement; a removal that truly subtracts a contact with no substitution would break the asymmetry law; a web with unchanged sign that flips, or reversed sign that recloses, would break the sign claim. A verbatim figure line stating a universal recovery threshold would retract the `gap`. Until one of these fires, the verdict stands as `candidate`.
