## 1. A shape is a constraint, not a filler

The canonical figures README states the limit: the framework returns "what a missing thing would have to look like (a shape, not a name)". Fig 9 :32: "you get a kind of thing, never the answer itself". Fig 13 :135: "It cannot say which primitive is missing, because naming it would mean already holding it."

From *The Library of Babel*, opened: "you can cut your search short if the set of books returned is small and descriptive enough that it provides a general shape of the answer that satisfies your question."

So a **shape** is the narrowed form of an answer you can state before you can settle it. It constrains; it does not fill. For this question:

- **Shape:** recovery is an ordered, per-slot, bond-level comparison against a named reference, over an enumerated cascade, with a loop sign and a seat statement.
- **Not a shape (a name or a verdict):** "this lake recovered", "invasive species always reduce recovery", "eradication restores the web". Those are names and verdicts, not constraints.

The shape is returnable because all its terms are already in the vocabulary: bonds (Fig 12), slots (Fig 1), comparison (Fig 3 and Operators), habitat (Fig 11). No missing primitive is required.

## 2. How the shape is obtained

- *Library of Babel*: use the vocabulary closest to the thing and bootstrap. "With each new term gained, you narrow the space by definition and deepen your vocabulary."
- Fig 6 :30: "The description travels. Fails by being sharpened into terms only we can read. Cast it in effect terms." Fig 9 :31: "your words are borrowed too, so describe the effect rather than the cause". This is why the outreach material says "returns to a reference configuration" rather than "recovers" alone, and "bond strength and sign" rather than "health".
- *The Hard Problem is About Source*, opened: "If several paths create the same behavior, then behavior is a projection with lossy information". A species census that reads "recovered" cannot comment on the source (the restored interaction network). What is owed is a better instrument, not a name.
- *Library of Babel* on stopping: "the finish line will also be the starting place". Here the finish is the point where the reference, slots, bonds, cascade order and seat are all stated and the refuters can fire.

## 3. What licenses a name

Two things, both outside the shape itself:

1. **The ground settles it.** Formula step 5: "the ground settles it. Verified only by what pays. A gate is not the ground." Here: a field time series of interaction strengths against a stated reference.
2. **A provenanced import from a frame whose closure differs** (Fig 6 :23, Fig 8 :50-52), with a source gate and a shadow test.

*Arrival of the Fittest*, opened, has a section "Three mappings offered and declined". One of the declined mappings is exactly the temptation in this question: "The habitat as an ecological niche... The resemblance is real and the definitions do not align". So the framework's habitat is **not** the niche, and importing niche theory as if it were the same term would be an unprovenanced binding. Likewise, the article's citation list marks Jones, Lawton and Shachak 1994 as an *instance*, not as machinery borrowed from ecology. That is the correct status for the ecosystem-engineering sources used here.

## 4. Why a species-list reading is the forbidden error

*The Hard Problem is About Source* gives the mechanism. A reading that several sources produce identically does not identify which source produced it. Here, a native species list returning can be produced by at least three different histories: genuine bond restoration, a different web with the same cast, or a seat maintaining a substitute habitat. The census cannot distinguish them.

Fig 12 :5: "A set of ingredients is not one. The bond is the rest of the information, and it is the half nothing was recording." The species list is the projection; the interaction-strength matrix is the source. The spectrometer case in the essay is resolved by an instrument, not by an argument.

## 5. Q: does "shape, not a name" forbid naming the reference configuration?

**No, with a provenance obligation.** The phrase governs a missing primitive. The reference configuration is not a primitive; it is a binding from the measurement frame onto an observation. Naming it is required by F1, because without it "recovery" is an unbound preference (Formula "Direction"). What is forbidden is presenting the binding as if the framework derived which reference to choose. The ecologist chooses it and records the choice. The same applies to naming a focal slot or a cascade cutoff: those are declared, not derived.

**Correction to my own reading.** I initially treated "shape, not a name" as a blanket ban on naming anything concrete, which would have made F1 self-contradictory. Narrowed above: the ban applies to missing primitives and to turning a search preference into a term without a residual. The exemplar makes the same correction in its `05` §5b. Both readings are kept visible so the correction is auditable.

## 6. Two consequences for this solution

**Prestige is not action.** From *The Recursive Self-Improvement Trap*, opened: "Prestige must never be allowed to validate an action directly... Prestige can fund an action. It can give an action a budget to be tried. But it cannot settle an action. Only the ground can settle it." A list of distinguished ecologists who agree that ecosystems can recover is the named collapse, not corroboration. The legitimate ask is a budget to try the shape against a field ground.

**This audit is a simulation, not a ground reading.** Same essay: "LLMs can quickly simulate an approximate answer/output with the formula found in the architecture post", and "the truly testable output would need to be derived from the real architecture or working the formula yourself." The article labels such output "simulacra" and warns that solutions "could never be verified if the solutions presented were not contrived to fit the formula". So `01`, `06`, `07`, `08` and `09` are simulated derivations checked for internal consistency against the figures. They are **not** tests of ecological recovery and must be labelled as such. They cannot settle anything they report on.
