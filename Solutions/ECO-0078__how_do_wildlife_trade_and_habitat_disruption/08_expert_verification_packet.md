# Expert verification packet: the Tether on wildlife trade and habitat disruption

**What this is:** a claim in a form a disease ecologist, veterinary epidemiologist or wildlife-trade researcher can check. Mode, derivation and refuters are stated before the conclusion, so a reader knows what to look for and what would settle it.

**Mode: `specified`, not `grounded`.** No particular outbreak is claimed to be explained. This is a shape and a route, not a result. Simulated derivation checked for internal consistency against the figures, not a ground reading.

## 1. The claim, in one paragraph

Wildlife trade and habitat disruption are different operators on the contact field, not two strengths of one pressure. Disruption re-enumerates the actors, conditions and relations in contact with the residual; trade imports an actor and its pathogens into a frame whose closure differs, and the union's surplus is where new transmission routes become reachable. Valence is relative to the residual being tracked, so the same host can dilute one pathogen and source another. Emergence is a mint under guards and sometimes a refusal of a held claim; spread is a routed process with reservoirs as catalysts and establishment as a closed cycle, not a species list. Detection is a maintained channel with an anchor, not agreement among overlapping reviews. The framework offers a shape, written falsifiers and the instruments that would close it, and it does not name a culprit.

## 2. Three checkable sub-claims

### C1: framework-internal; checkable by reading Figures 11 and 6

- **Statement:** the habitat is enumerated, never composed, while import is the only operator that moves the wall. These are different operations on the contact field.
- **Source, verbatim:** Fig 11 :43: *"The habitat is enumerated, never composed."* Fig 11 :44: *"List everything in contact with the residual, then what is in contact with those, and outward until the cascade stops mattering."* Fig 6 :14: *"the only operator that moves the wall"*. Fig 6 :25: *"An import adds no atom to the world."*
- **Refuter:** show a re-enumeration of contacts that adds an atom to closure(Γ), or an import that changes nothing reachable in either frame at the scale where contact occurs. Either collapses the operator distinction.

### C2: ecology-facing; checkable by a disease ecologist against their own system

- **Statement:** the same actor can have opposite valence for different residuals, and this is observed in the biodiversity-disease literature; therefore no standing dangerous-species list is licensed by the derivation.
- **Source, verbatim (figure):** Fig 11 :49: *"An actor's valence is relative to the residual, not a property it carries."* Fig 11 :50: *"The same actor is a benefactor to one residual and a malfactor to another, so mutualism and parasitism are relations rather than kinds."*
- **World check:** Keesing et al. 2010, Nature, *"In principle, loss of biodiversity could either increase or decrease disease transmission. However, mounting evidence indicates that biodiversity loss frequently increases disease transmission. In contrast, areas of naturally high biodiversity may serve as a source pool for new pathogens."* Rohr et al. 2020, Nature Ecology and Evolution, records dilution, amplification, null and context-dependent findings and scale dependence. These are not independent witnesses of the figure; they corroborate from a different field.
- **Refuter:** a host or pathway shown to be hazardous for every pathogen in every receiving community at every scale, independent of the residual tracked.

### C3: provenance-facing; checkable by a genomic epidemiologist or trade-data researcher

- **Statement:** a "new" pathogen in a new place is a reading, not a source. Genomic and documentary records are the discriminating instrument; without them, emergence is a metamerism error.
- **Source, verbatim (figure):** Fig 8 :51-52: *"Convergent derivation and adopted import look identical in the contents. Only the record of where each came from separates them"*. Fig 9 :27-28: *"describe it before you go looking, or any frame you pick will seem to fit"*.
- **World check:** Gippet et al. 2026, Science, via the University of Lausanne release, *"the length of time an animal has been present in trade plays a key role: on average, a species shares one additional pathogen with humans for every ten-year period spent on the market."* This is a record-based, cumulative-contact result, not an observation of a single emergence.
- **Refuter:** two independent provenance records giving incompatible routes with no third instrument able to discriminate them. Then record a gap at provenance rather than forcing a verdict.

## 3. What is explicitly not claimed

- Not a theory of everything, and no final answer. The question stays open until the ground settles it.
- Not "biodiversity loss always increases disease", not "high biodiversity is always protective", and not a universal dilution effect.
- Not a named culprit species, market, pathogen or country. Fig 9 :32: *"you get a kind of thing, never the answer itself"*.
- Not a single risk index. Fig 1 :19: *"The residual is indexed by object slot, not aggregated."*
- Not a claim that a trade ban subtracts a habitat. Fig 11 :61: *"The seat's power is asymmetric. Introduction is available; exclusion is not. You can add an actor and you cannot subtract the habitat, because contact is a fact about the world and does not consult you."*
- Not `grounded`. Nothing here is asserted as settled by experiment.

## 5. What would settle it

A provenance-discriminating instrument, not a further argument. From *The Hard Problem is About Source*: an instrument that reads green *"cannot comment on the source"*; the spectrometer resolved it. Here:

1. **Operator separation:** matched interventions, one re-enumerating contacts and one importing an actor, compared per slot and scale.
2. **Surplus:** does the import add a reachable transmission route neither frame held alone, measured by host-range expansion plus a recorded contact?
3. **Provenance:** do genomic and documentary records independently name the same route?
4. **Union:** does one continuous predictor beat crossing, competent habitat and detection with no interaction and no loss of resolution?
5. **Channel:** does improved surveillance change detection only, leaving sequence-confirmed transmission unchanged (Fig 10 :24)?
