## Wider literature that bears on the residual

1. **Dual role of biodiversity.** Keesing et al. 2010 and Rohr et al. 2020 together show that the sign of the biodiversity-disease relation depends on scale, pathogen type and community composition. This corroborates the figure rule that valence is relative to the residual (Fig 11 :49-51) and the union split (Fig 9 :54-57). The two papers draw partly on overlapping syntheses, so their agreement is one pool talking to the extent that they cite the same meta-analyses.
2. **Trade as a cumulative contact process.** Gippet et al. 2026 adds a time dimension: one extra shared pathogen per decade in trade. This corroborates the import identification (Fig 6 :14, :23) and the need for provenance (Fig 8 :51-52). It is a different field, method and dataset from the biodiversity literature, so this is genuinely low overlap.
3. **Governance and channel maintenance.** Karesh et al. 2025 describes a risk-analysis process with monitoring and evaluation. It corroborates Fig 10 :24, *"the ground does not decay, the channel does"*, from a veterinary-governance frame rather than an ecology frame.

No world source was found that directly tests the figure-derived operator distinction (enumeration versus import) as such. That is the gap the outreach is meant to close. "Unreachable is a verdict, not a silence": the absence of a direct test is recorded, not papered over.

## Distribution rules: offer, do not push

Figure-grounded reasons:

- **Fig 4 :22**: *"Only methods cross up. Only head starts come down."* Sending the full audit before anyone has described a residual sends a recording, not a generator. Lead with the shape and the refuter; offer links after a verdict is recorded.
- **Fig 10 :42**: *"A verifier that can reconstruct the claim cannot verify it."* A recipient who has absorbed the whole framework before being asked for a judgment has joined the frame being checked.
- **Fig 9 :27-28**: *"describe it before you go looking, or any frame you pick will seem to fit"*. Ask what they would expect to see if the operator distinction were true before giving them the ecology review.
- **Fig 2 :40**: *"disqualifying for a verdict: agreement about the world is worthless when the agreeing frames share an evidence pool"* Overlapping reviews are one witness. Do not present a stack of agreeing papers as corroboration.
- **Fig 2 :42**: *"Buy independence in proportion to the cost of being wrong."* The cost here is a wrong pandemic-prevention policy, so independent frames are worth real effort.

**Recommended order in a reply:** (1) the shape claim and its four refuters; (2) ask what they would count as a failure, and record the answer; (3) only then the three links, with the note that the figures, not the summary, are the authority; (4) offer the method as a reusable generator, with the instruction that each user write their own refuter and state their own mode.
