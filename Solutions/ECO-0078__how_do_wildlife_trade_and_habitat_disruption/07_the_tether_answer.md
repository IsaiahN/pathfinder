# The Tether's answer on wildlife trade, habitat disruption and disease emergence

**Mode: `specified`, not `grounded`.** This is a shape and a route, not a result. Simulated derivation checked against the figures (`05_what_shape_means.md` §7), not a ground reading.

## 1. The residual it answers

A host-pathogen system predicts which hosts, vectors and contacts will produce infections. An intervention changes the contact field, and the observed return no longer matches the prediction: a new slot becomes live, an old slot goes quiet, or a cascade appears three steps out. `R` is indexed per host, pathogen, place and time. Fig 1 :19: *"The residual is indexed by object slot, not aggregated."*

The two named drivers are a **union of overlapping conditions**, not two degrees of one pressure (Fig 9 :54-57). Crossing a boundary, receiving that crossing in a competent habitat, and registering the cascade are categorically different.

## 2. The shape

1. **Habitat disruption changes the enumeration of the habitat.** Fig 11 :43: *"The habitat is enumerated, never composed."* It alters which actors, conditions and relations are in contact with the residual, without adding an atom to any frame's closure. Contact cascades make a first-order intervention a third-order effect: Fig 11 :52, *"And contact cascades. Scoping the habitat to direct contact is how DDT reached the eagles: first-order enumeration, second-order effect, and the residual that mattered was three steps out."*
2. **Wildlife trade is an import.** Fig 6 :14 calls import *"the only operator that moves the wall"*. It carries an actor and its pathogens into a frame whose closure differs. The union of the two closures has a surplus: Fig 8 :45-46, *"Two frames together reach what neither reaches alone."* *"Composition crosses the boundary between them, and that surplus is where imports live."* The world gains no new primitive (Fig 6 :25: *"An import adds no atom to the world."*); a frame gains access.
3. **Valence is relative to the residual.** Fig 11 :49-51: *"An actor's valence is relative to the residual, not a property it carries."* *"The same actor is a benefactor to one residual and a malfactor to another, so mutualism and parasitism are relations rather than kinds."* The same community can dilute one pathogen and source another; the literature records both signs and their scale dependence.
4. **Emergence is a mint; spread is a route.** A new dominant lineage must have support on a slot, compose from what the pathogen holds or be imported, and be new relative to the library (Formula step 3). Sometimes the owed term is a refusal of a held host association, not an explanation (Fig 5 :53: *"refuted: a term we hold is wrong; a term is owed that says so"*). Transmission is a sequence and can close into a cycle with a reservoir as catalyst (Fig 12 :119, :121).
5. **Detection is an office, not a loop property.** Fig 10 :24: *"the ground does not decay, the channel does"*. Anchors are confirmed cases, sequences, serology and trade records (Fig 2 :29). Agreement among overlapping reviews is one pool talking (Fig 2 :40). Independence must be bought in proportion to the cost of being wrong (Fig 2 :42).
6. **The framework does not name the culprit.** It returns a shape, not a name. Fig 9 :32: *"you get a kind of thing, never the answer itself"*. Which species, which pathogen and which place is a matter for the ground, recorded as provenance.

## 3. What it explicitly does not say

- Not "biodiversity loss always increases disease", and not "high biodiversity is always protective". The sign depends on scale, pathogen type and community composition (Rohr et al. 2020; Keesing et al. 2010).
- Not "this species is dangerous". Valence is a relation to a tracked residual (Fig 11 :49-51).
- Not a single global risk score. Aggregation across slots throws away the live signal (Fig 1 :19, :22).
- Not a derivation of any particular outbreak, and no new ecological primitive.
- Not `grounded`. No sequence, outbreak record or intervention trial in this folder settles `left(R,φ)`.
- Not a claim that a trade ban subtracts a habitat. Introduction is available; exclusion is not (Fig 11 :61). Isolation substitutes a habitat rather than removing one (Fig 11 :66).

## 4. What would settle it: the ground

A provenance-discriminating instrument, not a further argument. *The Hard Problem is About Source*: an instrument that reads green *"cannot comment on the source"*; the spectrometer resolved it. Here:

- **Operator separation test:** for a matched pair of interventions, does re-enumeration of contacts and import across a boundary change `R` differently at the same slot and scale? If not, F1 falls.
- **Surplus test:** does the import event add a reachable transmission route that neither the source frame nor the receiving frame held alone? Measure by host-range expansion plus a recorded contact, not by incidence alone.
- **Provenance test:** do genomic phylogeny and documentary trade records independently name the same route for the same lineage? If they conflict and no third instrument discriminates, record a gap at provenance.
- **Union test:** does a single continuous predictor beat the three unbundled conditions with no interaction and no loss of per-slot resolution? If yes, the union split is unnecessary.
- **Channel test:** does improved surveillance change only detection, leaving sequence-confirmed transmission unchanged? That distinguishes a channel effect from a ground effect (Fig 10 :24).

## 5. The honest stop

**Unreached, not unreachable.** The ecology frame's primitives are a visible choice, not nature's own list (*They're Scanning Books*: *"Declaring the primitives does not make them neutral. It makes the choice visible."*). The framework stops at the limit of what the available instruments can distinguish and records the lineage so the stop is checkable. *How Intelligence Actually Works*: *"The recursion converges toward indiscernibility, and indiscernibility is not identity."* For any particular outbreak, the answer to "which pathogen, from where, by which route" remains a **gap** after genuine census. That is a verdict, not a silence (Fig 8 :39: *"So a search that finds nothing is never a proof of absence."*).

## 6. One-paragraph version for outreach

> The Tether does not name a dangerous species or predict the next outbreak. It says that wildlife trade and habitat disruption are two different operators, not two strengths of one pressure: disruption re-enumerates the actors, conditions and relations in contact with a pathogen, so a first-order change can surface three steps out, while trade is an import that carries an actor and its pathogens into a host community whose closure differs, and the surplus of those two closures is where new transmission routes become reachable. Valence is relative to what you are tracking: the same host can dilute one pathogen and source another. Emergence is a term minting event, and sometimes the refusal of a held host-association claim; spread is a route with reservoirs as catalysts and establishment as a closed cycle, not a list of species. Detection is a maintained channel with an anchor, not a vote among overlapping reviews. What the framework offers is the shape any answer must have, four falsifiers written in advance, and the instruments that would close it. The identity of the next outbreak stays open until the ground settles it.
