## 1. A shape is a constraint, not a filler

The canonical figures README states the limit: the framework returns *"what a missing thing would have to look like (a shape, not a name)"*. Fig 9 :32: *"you get a kind of thing, never the answer itself"*. Fig 13 :134: *"Unreachable is a verdict here, not a silence"*.

From *The Library of Babel*: *"You can cut your search short if the set of books returned is small and descriptive enough that it provides a general shape of the answer that satisfies your question."*

For this question a **shape** is therefore: two distinct operators on the contact field, each with a scale, plus the instrument that discriminates them. It is not a named list of species, markets, pathogens or policies. `"trade volume"`, `"habitat integrity"` and `"biodiversity loss"` are candidate names and are withheld until the census admits them as conditions rather than verdicts.

*How Intelligence Actually Works* supplies the exact precedent: the article writes `SPACE(???)TIME` and calls it *"a testable theory"*, adding that *"we cannot say which state composed first, and we don't know if the order mattered."* The `(???)` is the shape. Deleting it is the error.

## 2. How a shape is obtained

- *Library of Babel*: use the vocabulary you already have that is *closest* to the thing, then look for new terms that overlap or correlate with it. *"With each new term gained, you narrow the space and deepen your vocabulary."*
- Fig 6 :30: *"The description travels. Fails by being sharpened into terms only we can read. Cast it in effect terms."* Fig 9 :30-31: *"your words are borrowed too, so describe the effect rather than the cause"*. This is why the residual in `01` §0 is stated as a gap between prediction and return, not as "zoonotic risk".
- *Decoding Tacit Knowledge*: *"When contact isn't possible, the best you can send is a plain description of what you'd expect to see, because anyone can check that against the world."*
- *Library of Babel* on a bundled question: *"Perhaps your problem was a bundle or conflation of several problems masquerading as one?"* This is the essay-level statement of the union split in `01` §1.3.
- *Library of Babel* on stopping: *"the finish line will also be the starting place"*, and the paradox that by then you have *"composed the answer through the process of locating it."*

## 3. What licenses a name

Only two things, both outside the shape itself:

1. **The ground settles it.** Formula step 5: *"the ground settles it. Verified only by what pays."* Here: a confirmed outbreak with a sequence and a traceable contact record. *The Recursive Self-Improvement Trap*: *"Prestige can fund an action. It can give an action a budget to be tried. But it cannot settle an action. Only the ground can settle it."* A well-cited review funds a line of inquiry; it cannot settle a causal claim.
2. **A provenanced import from a frame whose closure differs** (Fig 6 :23, Fig 8 :45-46), with a source gate and a shadow test. *They're Scanning Books*: *"The real import is not a new term in the existing space. It is a new dimension of the space."*

That essay also warns: *"A minimum viable set of six terms is a human's careful guess at reality's compression axes; another sensorium's minimum set need not be those six."* And: *"Declaring the primitives does not make them neutral. It makes the choice visible."* So naming a reservoir as *the* reservoir is a choice made visible, not a discovery of nature's own list.

## 4. Source discrimination is the operative limit here

*The Hard Problem is About Source* gives the mechanism that governs this question most directly. Metamerism: an instrument reads green and *"cannot comment on the source"*; the same reading can come from one green light, yellow plus blue, RGB, CMYK or an undiscovered scheme. *"If several paths create the same behavior, then behavior is a projection with lossy information (it cannot define consciousness in full)."*

Translated: a pathogen found in a new place is a reading. It can come from a genuinely new lineage, from an old lineage newly detected, or from a lineage whose host association was mis-specified. The reading does not determine the source. *"An instrument that can't distinguish these cases demonstrates its own inadequacy, it cannot claim anything substantial about what is being measured or what causes it."* It was the spectrometer, not an argument, that resolved the colour case: *"The answer is unreached until a better instrument exists."*

Here the corresponding instruments are genomic phylogeny, trade manifests, seizure records and field serology. Without them, "emergence" is a metamerism error: a source named from a projection.

## 5. Capability and habitat: the essay that bears directly on this question

*How Intelligence Actually Works* contains an unusually direct statement: *"when altering a habitat by unintentionally introducing a predatory threat into the ecosystem, or disturbing the balance by adding or removing vital resource points, you can inadvertently create major unpredictable consequences downstream."* This is Fig 11 :52 in narrative form, and it is the strongest essay-level corroboration for F2.

*How a Society of Agents Would Work* adds: *"capability is a property of agent-and-habitat, never of the agent alone."* For disease ecology the counterpart is that spillover potential is a property of pathogen-and-habitat, never of the pathogen alone. A "dangerous pathogen" list without a receiving habitat is the same category error. The same essay: *"Aggregating across slots is how a live signal disappears into an average."* and *"Deadlines are filters, never verdicts."*

## 6. Why the record's answer would be the forbidden error

If this folder asserted "wildlife trade raises disease risk by X percent, therefore ban route Y", it would commit three named errors:

1. **Naming where a shape is owed** (Fig 9 :32).
2. **Turning a filter into a witness** (Fig 9 :60): an average risk score cannot say which slot is live.
3. **Settling by agreement** (Fig 2 :35, :40): overlapping reviews agreeing is one pool talking.

*Arrival of the Fittest* records three mappings already offered and declined, and defines an *"instance"* as *"a case the framework describes, arrived at independently by someone else"*. Its triangulation claim *"fits four cases already known and has not been tested forward"*. So a review citing the framework's agreement with ecology is a retrodiction, not a forward result. This folder makes no such claim.

## 7. Two consequences for this package

**Prestige is not action.** *The Recursive Self-Improvement Trap*: *"The prestige loop confirms itself. The high-prestige person is believed because they are high-prestige."* The correct ask of a checker is a budget to try the claim, not endorsement. This is why `02_outreach_email.md` asks for a failure condition, not agreement.

**This audit is a simulation, not a ground reading.** Same essay: *"LLMs can quickly simulate an approximate answer/output with the formula found in the architecture post. It will often be better than the generic outputs that you will usually get otherwise too (intelligence is essentially arrangement). However, the truly testable output would need to be derived from the real architecture or working the formula yourself."* The article's caveat on the demo is blunter: because the library is a black box, *"the outputs generated must be seen as simulacra"*, and *"could never be verified if the solutions presented were not contrived to fit the formula."* So `01` is a simulated derivation checked for internal consistency against the figures. It is not a test of the ecological claim and must be labelled as such. It cannot settle anything it reports on.

## 8. Correction to my own §3

I initially wrote that "the framework returns a shape, not a name" forbids naming any actor or pathway in this question. That overstates the rule. The README's phrase governs a **missing** primitive, one not held in Γ. Trade, habitat and network are all already available as ordinary ecological descriptions; naming a reservoir role is a binding from the ecology frame onto a held category, not the invention of a new primitive.

What remains wrong in a naive answer is different: treating that binding as a derivation, or as an import that moves the wall. Fig 6 :25: *"An import adds no atom to the world."* The binding adds none. Both the corrected and the overstated readings are recorded here so neither is quietly lost.
