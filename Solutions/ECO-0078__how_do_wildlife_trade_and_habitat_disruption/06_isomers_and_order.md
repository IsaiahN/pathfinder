## DERIVATION

### Q1: Are the drivers isomers? CORRECTION TO MY OWN FIRST READING IN `01`

- Fig 12 :117: **"isomer. Same atoms, different bond, different substance. The difference is invisible in the formula and decisive in the behaviour."**
- The two drivers do not share an atom set. One re-enumerates actors, conditions and relations; the other moves an atom across a closure boundary. An operator and a molecule are different grammatical categories.

The correct typing is: **two different operators, one acting on the enumeration, one acting on the reach**. Fig 6 :21 distinguishes them directly: *"MINT composes inside the closure and can never add an atom."* Import adds one; re-enumeration adds none.

### Q2: What is the molecule in this question?

The molecule is the **transmission route**, not the species list. Fig 12 :111-112:

> **"A vocabulary without bonds can name a state and cannot name a route to one."**
> **"A plan is a sequence. A goal is a comparison. Progress is a subtraction. A refutation is a negation, and that one is not a bond."**

- `→` sequence: host A then host B, with B needing A to have happened (Fig 12 :71-72). A chain of spillovers is a sequence.
- `⇒` production: the pathogen replicates in a host that did not carry it before. The second operand is absent until the first fires (Operators table :38-40).
- `⋛` comparison: a contact rate or host density crosses a threshold. Fig 12 :87: *"a threshold relation between two quantities"*.
- `¬` refusal: a held host-association claim is refused. Operators table :57: *"a refutation is a negation, which is why a vocabulary without it can only accumulate"*.

A species list written without these bonds is a flat formula. It names ingredients and cannot name the route, which is exactly the failure Fig 12 diagnoses.

### Q3: Reservoir as catalyst, establishment as cycle

- Fig 12 :119: **"catalyst. A part that survives its own use. Spent and regenerating parts behave differently across uses, and nothing records which a term is."** A reservoir host fits: it supports the pathogen without being consumed by the transmission event. A dead-end host does not fit: it is spent. The distinction is a bond-level fact, not a property of the species name.
- Fig 12 :121: **"cycle. The output re-enters as input. The sign of the loop decides whether it closes, and a chain cannot close by construction."** Establishment in a reservoir closes the loop; a chain of one-off spillovers cannot close, because a chain has no return edge.
- ⇒ "Spillover" and "emergence" are not the same term. Spillover is a `→` event; sustained transmission is a `cycle` with a `catalyst`. Conflating them is the flat-formula error at the population scale.
- **Refuter:** a documented sustained transmission chain with no part that survives its own use, or a closure of the loop that a chain can express. Either breaks the catalyst/cycle typing.

### Q4: What order is the union?

- Fig 12 :103: **"Composition is recursive, and the level decides what is irreducible."**
- Import must be assessed before enumeration of the receiving habitat, because what crosses the boundary is an atom, and the habitat is then enumerated *with* that atom present. This is the dependency order from Fig 3 carried into the ecological case.
- Detection is last in dependency, not first: a channel that fails cannot tell you whether C1 and C2 held (Fig 10 :24).
- ⇒ Order: **import → re-enumeration → route composition → detection**. Reversing it, by naming a risky species before describing the residual, is the apophenia Fig 9 :27-28 warns against: *"describe it before you go looking, or any frame you pick will seem to fit"*.
- A stable strain on the typing: `⋛` is defined as a threshold between two quantities, but in surveillance it is often applied to a model score and a policy threshold, where one operand is a convention rather than a quantity (Fig 10 :47: *"a convention that decides an outcome has stopped being one"*). Named, not hidden.

## CONCLUSION

The drivers are **not isomers**; they are different operators. The molecule worth testing is the transmission route, typed with `→`, `⇒`, `⋛` and `¬`. Reservoirs are catalysts and establishment is a cycle, so spillover and emergence are structurally different terms. The union is ordered import, then re-enumeration, then route composition, then detection. This rescues what was defensible in the first reading without the part that failed: no new primitive, no naming of an intrinsically dangerous species, and a clear falsifier for the typing.
