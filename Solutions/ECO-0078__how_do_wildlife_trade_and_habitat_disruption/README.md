## The question restated as an effect

Two interventions are proposed as explanations: wildlife trade, and habitat disruption. The effect to be explained is not "disease" in general. It is a change in the residual `R = |Γ(b,a) − o′|`, indexed per host, pathogen, place and time: the gap between what a system's library of expectations predicts about hosts, vectors and transmission, and what a market, a forest, a clinic or a sequence actually returns. The question asks which operator on the contact field changes that gap, at which scale, and by what route.

An answer would look like a pair of distinct operators on the habitat, plus the scale at which each operates, plus an instrument that discriminates a failure of one from a failure of the other. That sought-for shape is bound as an ordinary term in `01` §0 before the run, so the pre-registered refuter can fire on it.

## What is deliberately not here

- No personal emails of named individuals, and no bulk-send plan.
- No ranking of people by how likely they are to agree with the claim.
- No claim that this derivation settles the ecological question. It is a simulated derivation checked for internal consistency against the figures, not a ground reading. The essays call LLM output *"simulacra"* and say the testable output *"would need to be derived from the real architecture or working the formula yourself"* (The Recursive Self-Improvement Trap, 2026, accessed 2026-11-23). See `05_what_shape_means.md` §7.

Three figure-grounded reasons for the exclusions:

1. **A room does not upgrade the label.** Fig 7 :12-13: *"same closure, handed along; agreement compounds, evidence does not"*. A list selected for agreement adds votes, not an anchor.
2. **Correlation disqualifies a verdict.** Fig 2 :40: *"disqualifying for a verdict: agreement about the world is worthless when the agreeing frames share an evidence pool"*. Selecting recipients for receptiveness manufactures the disqualifying correlation.
3. **A verifier must not hold the interiors.** Fig 10 :42: *"A verifier that can reconstruct the claim cannot verify it."* The first contact carries the residual and its refuter, never the framework.
