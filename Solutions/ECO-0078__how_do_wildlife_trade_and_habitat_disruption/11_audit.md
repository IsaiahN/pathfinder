## Independent domain census

I redid the census before reading the primary's conclusions, using the WATSON order: R by arity, symmetry and scale; one line per domain with actors / currency / anchor; partition or union; order by dependency.

- **R (arity / symmetry / scale).** The residual is the change in the gap between what a host-pathogen system predicts about hosts, vectors and transmission and what a market, forest or clinic returns. Arity: multi-place, indexed per host / pathogen / place / time, so per-slot reporting is mandatory. Symmetry: asymmetric; trade has a direction and emergence has a before and after. Scale: four nested scales with a lossy transform between them.
- **Domain 1, pathogen closure.** Actors: genotypes, hosts, vectors. Currency: reach inside closure(Γ). Anchor: sequence, culture, infection assay. Partition boundary question.
- **Domain 2, habitat contact field.** Actors: wild and domestic hosts, people, vectors, conditions, relations. Currency: contact and cascade. Anchor: serology, field surveillance. Enumeration question, not composition.
- **Domain 3, trade system.** Actors: traders, markets, transport, source and destination communities. Currency: movement across closures. Anchor: trade records, seizures, phylogeny. Import question.
- **Domain 4, verification.** Actors: agencies, databases, researchers. Currency: reports and residuals. Anchor: the ground, never agreement. In this simulated run all four domains draw on one evidence pool, so independence between them is effectively zero.
- **Excluded X1.** "Intrinsically harmful species" has no anchor independent of a residual. Absorbed into Domain 2.

Order by dependency: import, then re-enumeration of the receiving field, then route composition, then detection. Refuse a held host-association claim before minting a new one (Formula step 2); describe the residual before naming a frame (Fig 9 :27-28).

My census matches the primary's four domains, its exclusion of X1 and its union reading. ## Round trip gap `R_T`

The Symbols table defines `R_T` as the gap between what was sent up and what came back down, `gap(x, (T_E ∘ T_A)(x))`, measuring what the coarser description cannot hold. Fig 4 :30 requires it to be measured rather than assumed. I ran the round trip on the condensation this folder actually uses.

1. **Sent up (x):** the verbatim figure set, chiefly Fig 11 :43-52 (enumeration, cascade, relative valence), Fig 6 :14, :22, :25 (import), Fig 8 :45-46, :51-52 (surplus and provenance), Fig 10 :24 (channel).
2. **Abstraction (`T_A`):** the shorthand carried in `07`: two different operators; disruption re-enumerates, trade imports; valence is relative; emergence is a mint or a refusal; spread is a route with catalyst and cycle; detection is a maintained channel.
3. **Concretisation (`T_E`):** the concrete field claim a reader would take away: ban this trade route, restore this habitat, add this species to a watchlist.

**`R_T` is nonzero.** Four things the shorthand holds do not survive the trip back down:

- **Which slot is live.** The shorthand says "per slot"; the concrete policy wants a national risk score. Fig 1 :19 and :22 are dropped.
- **The union split.** The shorthand says crossing, competent habitat and detection are different compounds; a single intervention targets only one and leaves the intersections standing (Fig 9 :55-57).
- **Provenance.** The shorthand says a new observation is not a new source; the concrete claim reads an observed lineage as a freshly emerged one (Fig 8 :51-52).
- **The channel/ground distinction.** The shorthand says surveillance effort changes the channel; the concrete reading treats an incidence rise as a transmission rise (Fig 10 :24).

Conversely, two things survive cleanly: the habitat is enumerated rather than composed, and import is the only operator that adds an atom to a frame's reach. That is the residue worth sending. `R_T` was **not** measured numerically, because no ground reading exists; it is recorded qualitatively as the enumerated set of dropped constraints. Treating "the formula is the condensed version of the figures" as identity would set `R_T` to zero and would be the dead-reckoning error Fig 4 :30 names. The honest form is a lossy condensation with a measured gap, which is what `09_watson_answer.md` states.
