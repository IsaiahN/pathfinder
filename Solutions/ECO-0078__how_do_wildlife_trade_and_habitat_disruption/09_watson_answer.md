## QUESTION

Decide, in effect terms, how two named interventions change the emergence and spread of infectious disease. Option A: treat wildlife trade and habitat disruption as two strengths of one pressure, "human disturbance", and rank slots by a single risk index. Option B: treat them as categorically different operators on the contact field, keep verdicts per domain, and withhold any name of a culprit until a provenance-discriminating instrument settles it. What each leads to: A aggregates the live slot into an average and hands a filter a verdict (Fig 1 :22; Fig 9 :60); B sends a checkable shape, four written refuters and an open slot for any particular outbreak.

## DOMAIN CENSUS

R by arity, symmetry and scale, then one line per domain with actors / currency / anchor, partition or union, and order by dependency.

- **R (the residual).** The gap between what a host-pathogen system's library predicts and what a forest, a market or a clinic returns, indexed per host, pathogen, place and time. Arity: multi-place, indexed per slot. Symmetry: asymmetric in time and direction (trade has source and destination; a spillover chain is a `→` sequence). Scale: within-host, population, landscape, global trade network, with a lossy transform between scales. Fig 1 :19 "The residual is indexed by object slot, not aggregated."
- **Domain 1, the pathogen frame.** Actors: genotypes, competent hosts, vectors. Currency: host range and reach inside closure(Γ). Anchor: culture, sequence, infection assay. Question type: partition boundary (what composes inside the closure).
- **Domain 2, the habitat contact field.** Actors: wild and domestic hosts, people, vectors, conditions, relations. Currency: contact rate and cascade distance. Anchor: field surveillance and serology. Question type: enumeration of actors, conditions and relations, not composition.
- **Domain 3, the trade system.** Actors: traders, markets, transport, source and destination communities. Currency: movement of animals and pathogens across closures. Anchor: CITES and LEMIS records, seizures, genomic tracing. Question type: import, the only operator that moves the wall.
- **Domain 4, verification and surveillance.** Actors: public health, veterinary and wildlife agencies, sequence databases. Currency: reports, residuals, agreement versus ground. Anchor: the ground, never agreement. In this simulated run all four domains draw on one evidence pool, so independence between them is low.
- **Excluded X1: "invasive species are harmful per se".** No anchor independent of a named residual; absorbed into Domain 2 as a relation.
- **Partition or union.** D1 and D2 overlap where a pathogen meets a new host; D2 and D3 overlap where an import lands; D4 observes all. It is a union. Order by dependency: import, then re-enumeration, then route composition, then detection. Fig 9 :54 "And when the split lands on a union rather than a partition, unbundle before you search again."

Strain test applied: Fig 11 :61 (introduction available, exclusion not) and Fig 11 :66 (isolation substitutes a habitat) govern different acts, so re-census resolves the apparent contradiction. The live strain is the operand type of the comparison bond when applied to a model score and a policy threshold; named, not hidden.

## VERDICT (from the figures)

The shape survives; a culprit does not. Habitat disruption re-enumerates the contact field, so a first-order change can appear three steps out. Wildlife trade imports an actor across a closure boundary, and the union's surplus is where new transmission routes become reachable. Valence is relative to the residual, so no standing dangerous-species list is licensed. Emergence is a mint or a refusal; spread is a route with reservoirs as catalysts and establishment as a cycle. Detection is a maintained channel with an anchor, not agreement among overlapping reviews. Which species, pathogen and place is a `gap` after genuine census, and is recorded as such. The verdict is `specified` and ungrounded.

- **cost if wrong:** bounded and locatable. The packet states mode, writes refuters before the conclusion and records the lineage, so an error is findable. The real cost would be handing a single risk index a verdict, which spends the independence of outside readers and could misdirect surveillance or trade policy. Fig 2 :42 "Buy independence in proportion to the cost of being wrong." That cost is avoidable by leading with the shape and its refuter.
- **undoable:** yes. The operator distinction and the union split are removable without touching the world sources; `07_the_tether_answer.md` and `08_expert_verification_packet.md` already state the narrower form. No ecological primitive has to be withdrawn.
- **reversed by:** a ground reading, not agreement. A matched-pair experiment showing re-enumeration and import have the same effect on R at the same slot and scale would break the operator distinction; a documented import adding no reachable route would break the trade verdict; a single continuous predictor beating the three conditions with no interaction would break the union split; genomic and documentary records giving irreconcilable routes with no discriminating instrument would force a gap at provenance. Until one of these fires, the verdict stands as `candidate`.
