## 1. A shape is a constraint, not a filler

The canonical figures README states the limit: the framework returns *"what a missing thing would have to look like (a shape, not a name)."* Fig 9 :32 *"you get a kind of thing, never the answer itself"*. Fig 13 :133 *"what it returns is a shape rather than a name."* Fig 13 :134 *"Unreachable is a verdict here, not a silence"*

From *The Library of Babel* (carried): *"You can cut your search short if the set of books returned is small and descriptive enough that it provides a general shape of the answer that satisfies your question."*

For EAR-0071, a **shape** is the discriminating structure of the evidence, before any numeric threshold is known. The shape is: threshold comparison, new regime, closing loop, persistent residual, per slot, triangulated against a fixed ground. A specific number, for example "the AMOC tips at 0.1 Sv of North Atlantic freshwater input", is a name obtained by measurement or by a pushed model, not by the framework. Lenton et al. 2008 (world source) list such values with wide ranges and several entries marked *"Unidentified"*, which is exactly the shape/not-a-name boundary in domain vocabulary.

## 2. How a shape is obtained

## 3. What licenses a name

Only two things, both outside the shape itself:

1. **The ground settles it.** Formula step 5 (THE_FORMULA): *"the ground settles it. Verified only by what pays. A gate is not the ground."* For a tipping point this means a process experiment, a palaeo reconstruction with discriminating proxies, or an Earth-system model pushed through the crossing and checked against data.
2. **A provenanced import from a frame whose closure differs** (Fig 6 :23 *"No frame escapes its own closure. The bound moves only on contact with a frame whose closure differs."*), with source gate and shadow test.

*They're Scanning Books* (carried): *"The real import is not a new term in the existing space. It is a new dimension of the space."* and *"Declaring the primitives does not make them neutral. It makes the choice visible."* A universal tipping threshold declared from the figures alone would be such an invisible choice.

## 4. The source problem is the real problem for tipping-point evidence

*The Hard Problem is About Source* (re-opened 2026-11-22): an instrument that detects green *"cannot comment on the source"*, because one green light, yellow plus blue, RGB, or CMYK can produce the same reading. *"If several paths create the same behavior, then behavior is a projection with lossy information."* And: *"An instrument that can't distinguish these cases demonstrates its own inadequacy."* It was the spectrometer, not an argument, that resolved the case: *"The answer is unreached until a better instrument exists."*

This is the exact structure of the EAR-0071 question. A time series showing a fast change is a **reading**. It cannot comment on the source. Three sources can produce that reading: a threshold crossing with internal amplification (tipping point), proportional forcing (gradual trend), or an external shock (abrupt event). A magnitude statistic is the eye, not the spectrometer. What is owed is a source-discriminating instrument: the conjunction of F2 to F5 in `01_figure_grounded_audit.md`, plus process attribution. This is why the answer is a conjunction and not a single early-warning number.

## 5. Correction to my own reading of "shape, not a name"

I initially read Fig 13 :133 and the README phrase as forbidding any numeric threshold in a Pathfinder answer. **Corrected.** The phrase governs a missing primitive or a universal name. A measured critical value for a specific system is a ground reading, not a framework name. Fig 13 :134-135 *"narrowing gets you close while the ground decides"*. So this solution may legitimately say what form the evidence must take (shape) while declining to supply a number (name), and that decline is a verdict rather than a silence. The earlier over-reading is preserved here rather than erased.

## 6. Two consequences for outreach

**Prestige is not action.** *The Recursive Self-Improvement Trap* (re-opened 2026-11-22): *"Prestige must never be allowed to validate an action directly"*; *"Prestige can fund an action. It can give an action a budget to be tried. But it cannot settle an action. Only the ground can settle it. The Ground Maintainer must be blind to prestige."* A list of famous climatologists who agree with an evidence rule is a convention, not corroboration (Fig 2 :40 *"agreement about the world is worthless when the agreeing frames share an evidence pool"*).

**My own audit is a simulation, not a ground reading.** Same essay: *"the output is only as good as the validator. The agent, or the tool, or the frame, or the theory, cannot validate itself."* and *"LLMs can quickly simulate an approximate answer/output with the formula ... However, the truly testable output would need to be derived from the real architecture or working the formula yourself."* So `01_figure_grounded_audit.md` and this file are simulated derivations checked for internal consistency against the figures. They are **not** a test of any earth system and must be labelled as such. They cannot settle anything they report on.
