## What is deliberately not here

- No personal emails of named individuals.
- No ranking of people by how likely they are to believe the answer.
- No bulk-send plan.

Three reasons, in the figures' own words:

1. **A room does not upgrade the label.** Fig 2 :35 *"Alignment is triangulation, not negotiation. A mirror cannot correct you."*
2. **Correlation disqualifies a verdict.** Fig 2 :40 *"agreement about the world is worthless when the agreeing frames share an evidence pool"* — selecting recipients for receptiveness manufactures the correlation that makes their agreement worthless.
3. **Asking people to trust before they check spends their independence.** Fig 10 :42 *"A verifier that can reconstruct the claim cannot verify it."*

Legally: scraping personal emails for unsolicited bulk outreach breaches GDPR and CAN-SPAM style rules in many jurisdictions. Public opt-in institutional channels avoid that failure as well as the structural one.

## Headline verdict

The distinction lives in the bond, not the variables (Fig 12 :94 *"The arrangement is the substance."*). A change qualifies as a tipping point when a threshold comparison, a new regime and a closing self-amplification loop all hold, read per slot and verified against a fixed ground. A persistent, non-trending residual is a filter that directs monitoring, not a witness for the verdict (Fig 9 :60). The framework returns the shape of the qualifying evidence, not a universal number (Fig 13 :133), and does not declare any present-day element tipped.
