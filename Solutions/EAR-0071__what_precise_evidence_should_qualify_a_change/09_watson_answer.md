## QUESTION

Decide, in effect terms, what evidence should qualify a change in an Earth system as a tipping point rather than a gradual trend or an abrupt external event. Option A: the label is earned by a single discriminating statistic, such as a large jump or a strong early-warning signal. Option B: the label is earned by a conjunction of structural evidence (threshold comparison, new regime, closing internal loop, persistent residual used only as a filter), read per slot and verified against a fixed ground. What each leads to: A spends the verdict on a filter and mislabels proportional or shock-driven changes; B sends a checkable rule with refuters and leaves the numeric threshold open until a ground settles it.

## VERDICT (from the figures)

The conjunction survives; the single-statistic option does not. A change qualifies as a tipping point when a threshold comparison (E1), a new regime (E2) and a closing self-amplifying loop (E3) all hold, verified per slot against a fixed ground with recorded provenance. A persistent, non-trending residual (E4) directs the search and raises the prior but cannot carry the verdict, because it is not specific. A gradual trend is an ordered chain whose residual trends down; an abrupt event is an imposed jump with no internal threshold and no closing loop. The framework returns the shape of the qualifying evidence, not a universal number, and it does not declare any present-day element tipped. This is a `specified`, ungrounded result.

- **cost if wrong:** bounded and locatable, but not trivial. Mislabelling a shock-driven jump as a tipping point sends monitoring effort to a nonexistent threshold; mislabelling a genuine threshold as a trend removes the chance to avoid a crossing. Lenton et al. 2008 price the error through the political and ethical time horizons, and IPCC attribution practice prices it through confidence levels. The framework's own price is Fig 2 :42 "Buy independence in proportion to the cost of being wrong." The packet carries refuters so an error is findable rather than invisible.
- **undoable:** yes. The rule states its own falsifiers (same bond different verdict; proportional response with no rankable control; relaxation with no internal loop; non-threshold slow-down; moving anchor). None of them requires withdrawing a primitive. A sent claim can be superseded by a corrected packet, and the lineage makes the correction traceable (Fig 10 :62-66, provenance of rulings).
- **reversed by:** a ground reading, not agreement. A pushed model or process experiment locating ρcrit and showing non-proportional response would settle E1 for one element; proxy evidence discriminating alternative stable states and hysteresis would settle E2; a diagnosed loop with a closing sign would settle E3; a slow-down record with noise-colour and forcing controls would either validate or demote E4; a figure re-census that admits a universal numeric threshold would reverse the shape-not-a-name limit. Until one of these fires, the verdict stands as candidate.
