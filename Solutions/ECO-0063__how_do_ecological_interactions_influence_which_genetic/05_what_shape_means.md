## 1. A shape is a constraint, not a filler

The canonical figures README states the limit: the framework returns "what a missing thing would have to look like (a shape, not a name)". Fig 9 :32 "you get a kind of thing, never the answer itself". Fig 13 :135 "It cannot say which primitive is missing, because naming it would mean already holding it."

From *The Library of Babel*, opened this session: "This means that you can cut your search short if the set of books returned is small and descriptive enough that it provides a general shape of the answer that satisfies your question."

So a **shape** is the narrowed form of an answer you can state before you can name it. For ECO-0063 the shape is: interactions are residual-relative relations in an enumerated habitat; they sort per-slot heritable variation; atoms arrive by mutation, recombination or gene flow; the ground votes through survival and reproduction; contact cascades; the feedback is a cycle. A **name** would be "the allele at locus X wins under predator Y in lake Z". The first constrains, the second fills. The first is derivable here; the second is a measurement.

## 2. How the shape is obtained

- *Library of Babel*: use the vocabulary closest to the thing and recurse on recurring new terms. "With each new term gained, you are able to narrow the space by definition and deepen your vocabulary." Here the first vocabulary is predation, competition, pollination, dependency (Fig 11 :47), and the recursion adds per-locus frequency, standing variation, import, cascade and reverse edge.
- Fig 6 :30 "The description travels. Fails by being sharpened into terms only we can read. Cast it in effect terms." Fig 9 :30-31 "your words are borrowed too, so describe the effect rather than the cause". So say "a frequency rise conditional on contact configuration", not a gene name imported from a lab vocabulary that the field ecologist does not use.
- *Decoding Tacit Knowledge* (carried, 2026-11-22): "When contact isn't possible, the best you can send is a plain description of what you'd expect to see, because anyone can check that against the world." This is the basis of the outreach order in `02` and `04`: describe what a refutation would look like before sending theory.
- *Library of Babel* on stopping: "You will know that you are done because the finish line will also be the starting place" and "you have already composed the answer through the process of locating it." The stop in `07` is not a claim of final truth; it is the point where the shape stops registering against the ground.

## 3. What licenses a name

Only two things, both outside the shape itself:

1. **The ground settles it.** `THE_FORMULA.md` step 5: "the ground settles it. Verified only by what pays. A gate is not the ground." The README: the framework stops "at the limit of what the available instruments can distinguish, and records the lineage so the stop is honest". For this question that means an allele-frequency time series with the interactor measured, or a manipulative experiment that identifies the source of the frequency change (selection versus drift versus gene flow).
2. **A provenanced import from a frame whose closure differs**, with a source gate and a shadow test (Fig 6 :23-24 "Import from a live peer is debited against that frame's independence. A wrong atom costs more than starting from primitives."). In ecology the genetics frame can import an allele-frequency instrument into the field-ecology frame, but the import must be declared.

*They're Scanning Books* (carried, 2026-11-22) sharpens the import rule: "The real import is not a new term in the existing space. It is a new dimension of the space." Also: "Declaring the primitives does not make them neutral. It makes the choice visible." Deciding that the relevant units are alleles rather than phenotypes or individuals is a visible choice, not a discovery of nature's own list.

## 4. Why the source of a reading is the hard part here

*The Hard Problem is About Source*, opened this session, gives the mechanism. Metamerism: an instrument reads green and "it cannot comment on the source"; the same reading could come from one green light, yellow plus blue, RGB, CMYK, or a scheme not yet discovered. "If several paths create the same behavior, then behavior is a projection with lossy information." And: "An instrument that can't distinguish these cases demonstrates its own inadequacy, it cannot claim anything substantial about what is being measured or what causes it." It was the spectrometer, not an argument, that resolved it: "The answer is unreached until a better instrument exists."

For ECO-0063 the reading is an allele-frequency change. Its source might be selection by a measured interactor, drift in a small population, gene flow from a neighbouring population, a change in sampling, or several at once. The frequency curve alone does not name the source. Calling it "adaptation to predation" from the curve alone is the metamerism error. What is owed is a discriminator: a replicated interactor manipulation with per-locus frequencies and a drift null. This is why F8 is a declared `gap` and not a guess.

## 5. Natural selection as an instance, not the framework's claim

*The Recursive Self-Improvement Trap*, opened this session: "Natural selection is what the loop looks like when there is no one maintaining the ground. When there is no one in the Ground Maintainer seat, the environment is the only selector." And: "The answer is not 'just evolution.' ... Natural selection is not the whole answer. It is a special case."

This maps directly onto the habitat figure. Fig 11 :56 "Unmaintained. No maintainer and no live frame in the seat. The habitat is pure contact, nothing selects, and what survives is what the ground kept." Fig 11 :36 "Where nothing maintains anything, intelligence is a label applied afterwards to a lineage that met its ground. Nothing was intelligent; selection ran." The same loop runs with a maintainer in a breeding programme (Fig 11 :61 introduction without exclusion), so this solution keeps the two branches separate rather than treating ecology as a special case of management or management as a special case of ecology.

## 6. Correction to my own reading of sections 1 and 3

I initially wrote the "shape, not a name" rule as if it forbade naming predation, competition or a specific allele. That overstates it. The README phrase is "what a **missing** thing would have to look like (a shape, not a name)". The subject is a primitive not held in Γ. Predation, competition and dependency are already named as relations in Fig 11 :47, and alleles are already the held atoms of the genetics frame. Naming one is a **binding** from a world frame onto something already available, not the invention of a missing primitive. What remains forbidden is two different moves: inventing a new seventh kind where the set is closed (Fig 13 :115 "The set is closed. The arrangements are not. That gap is why import exists."), and claiming that a binding moves the wall when it adds no atom (Fig 6 :21).

My section 4 metamerism point is **not** retracted. It still says that naming the *source* of a frequency reading without a discriminator is an error. The corrected reason is the missing instrument, not a ban on naming held atoms. Both claims are recorded so neither is quietly lost. This is the same correction the exemplar made at its own `05` section 5b and `06` Q1, reached here independently from the wording of the README.

## 7. Two consequences for this folder

**Prestige is not action.** *The Recursive Self-Improvement Trap*: "Prestige must never be allowed to validate an action directly" and "Prestige can fund an action. It can give an action a budget to be tried. But it cannot settle an action. Only the ground can settle it. The Ground Maintainer must be blind to prestige." A list of famous ecologists who agree with the shape is the named collapse. The legitimate ask gives the claim a budget to be tried and lets the ground, an allele-frequency series or an interactor manipulation, settle it.

**This audit is a simulation, not a ground reading.** Same essay: "LLMs can quickly simulate an approximate answer/output with the formula found in the architecture post" and "the truly testable output would need to be derived from the real architecture or working the formula yourself." Also: "Metatheories cannot validate themselves." So `01`, `06`, `07`, `09` and `11` are simulated derivations checked for internal consistency against the figures. They are **not** tests of the ecological claim and must be labelled as such. They cannot settle anything they report on.

## 8. What shape means for the verdict in one sentence

A shape here is the set of constraints any answer about trait spread must satisfy, per trait slot and per habitat condition, with the identity of the winning allele left to an instrument that can discriminate among the sources of a frequency reading.
