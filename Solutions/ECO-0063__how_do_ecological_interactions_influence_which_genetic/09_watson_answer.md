## QUESTION

Decide, in effect terms, what the Tether says when ecological interactions are asked to determine which genetic traits spread. Option A: interactions generate the adaptive variation that then spreads, so adaptive change can occur with no segregating variation and no import. Option B: interactions are the habitat's relations through which the ground votes on standing heritable variation, indexed per trait slot, with new atoms arriving by mutation, recombination and gene flow. What each leads to: A predicts directional change without variation and makes a universal adaptive story cheap; B predicts that the sign of an effect flips with the community, that the habitat must be enumerated outward, that the unit is the per-locus frequency, and that a specific winner is unreachable without a discriminator. The shape is bound as an ordinary term in `01` section 0 and made refutable in section 4.

## DOMAIN CENSUS

- **R (the residual).** Arity: many, a population's trait vector against a community of interactors, so not a two-reader seam. Symmetry: asymmetric, because the same interactor changes sign with the residual (Fig 11 :50). Scale: generational for frequency change, community for the interaction web, lineage for the feedback. Fig 11 :44 "List everything in contact with the residual, then what is in contact with those, and outward until the cascade stops mattering."
- **Domain 1, the population-genetic frame.** Actors: alleles, genotypes, recombination. Currency: allele frequency, heritability, fitness covariance. Anchor: measured frequencies across generations, a counter. Type: partition of composition inside closure(Γ) versus import.
- **Domain 2, the ecological habitat.** Actors: species, individuals, relations. Currency: interaction effects on survival and reproduction. Anchor: a manipulative field or mesocosm measurement of an interaction's fitness effect. Type: union of overlapping conditions, not a partition; each overlap is a compound with its own loop.
- **Domain 3, the ground of selection.** Actors: environments and descendants. Currency: survival and reproductive outcomes. Anchor: the actual outcome, which does not update on what any frame thinks. Type: unmaintained in the field, maintained in breeding or assisted gene flow.
- **Domain 4, the feedback compound.** Actors: the evolved population and the web it changes. Currency: the reverse edge from trait change to interaction strength. Anchor: a measured change in interaction strength after trait change. Type: compounding cycle when the reverse edge is present, chain when absent.
- **Order by dependency (Fig 3 :20).** Refuse option A before minting; then standing variation or an awaited import; then a vocabulary for trait and interactor; then enumerate the habitat outward; then measure per locus with a non-updating anchor. Effort at the last step cannot rescue a break at the second.

## VERDICT (from the figures)

Ecological interactions are the habitat's relations through which the ground votes on standing heritable variation, indexed per trait slot. Their valence is relative to the current residual, so no standing list of winning traits survives a community change. They reweight variation already present or imported by mutation, recombination and gene flow; they do not mint it. Contact cascades outward, the population mean is the wrong unit, and the feedback is a cycle only when the reverse edge from evolved trait to interaction strength is measured. Over the same allele set, two populations under different interactions are isomers; when the atoms differ, the functional-group reading applies. In managed cases the seat introduces but cannot exclude. No figure line names the winning allele in a particular community; that is a declared `gap` of the unreached kind, not silence. This is `general` on the shape, `specified` on the per-domain verdicts, `gap` on the identity, and not `grounded`.

- **cost if wrong:** bounded and locatable. The packet states mode, three checkable sub-claims and seven refuters before the conclusion, so an error is findable rather than invisible, and the arrangement claim is reversible without touching the census method. The real cost is spending an outside reader's independence by sending a story about adaptation instead of a discriminating measurement. Fig 2 :42 "Buy independence in proportion to the cost of being wrong." That cost is avoidable by leading with the shape and its refuter.
- **undoable:** yes at the level of the claim, costly at the level of the field case. A wrong arrangement reading can be withdrawn or narrowed to the functional-group form without inventing a new primitive. A published adaptive story is harder to withdraw, which is why the stage-1 refuter is written before outreach. A habitat substitution that introduced an unwanted actor cannot be undone by exclusion (Fig 11 :61), so that part is not undoable in practice.
- **reversed by:** a ground reading, not agreement. An allele-frequency time series with the interactor manipulated and a drift null, or any instrument that discriminates selection from drift and gene flow for the same reading, would move a specific case to `grounded`. A documented adaptive rise with zero variation and no import would break the sorting verdict. A measured forward edge with a zero reverse edge would turn the cycle into a chain for that system. A figure re-census that admits a universal winning allele would close the `gap`. Until one of these fires, the verdict stands as `candidate`.
