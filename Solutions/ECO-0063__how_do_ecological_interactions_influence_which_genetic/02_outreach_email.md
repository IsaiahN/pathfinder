**Use:** one-to-one, or as a cover note attached to a submission through the public venues in `03_public_contact_points.csv`. **Not** for bulk send.

**Hard precondition:** do not send until the three checkable sub-claims in `08_expert_verification_packet.md` survive a knowledgeable reader's check. The offer collapses on contact if the stage-1 output does not hold.

**Subject:** One checkable claim about selection in a community, before any theory

> Hi [name],
>
> I have one specific output I would like you to check against work you already know. I am not asking
> you to read the framework first, and I am not asking you to agree with it. If this output does not
> survive, there is nothing else to look at.
>
> **The output (`general` on the shape, `specified` on the mechanism, not `grounded`):** ecological
> interactions are the habitat's relations (predation, competition, pollination, dependency), and an
> interaction's effect on a trait is relative to the current residual, not carried by the actor. So
> there is no standing list of traits that "win". Selection reweights heritable variation that is
> already in the population or arrives by mutation and gene flow; an interaction does not manufacture
> the variant it favours. The effect must be read per trait slot, because a population mean can stay
> flat while individual loci move in opposite directions. Contact cascades outward, so a second-order
> interactor can set the pressure on a trait it never touches.
>
> **What this is not.** It is not a claim that I can name the allele that wins in your study system.
> It is not a universal interaction strength. It is not a claim that this derivation is grounded: I
> took no measurement. It is a shape, written so that it can fail.
>
> **What would refute it, written before this message:**
> 1. a heritable trait rises at a locus with zero segregating variation and no mutation, immigration
>    or introgression in the measurement window;
> 2. a population mean of a heritable trait shifts while every locus frequency at that trait stays
>    unchanged;
> 3. the same interactor has the same sign on the same trait in two communities under opposite
>    selection regimes, with interaction strength measured in both;
> 4. manipulating a second-order interactor never changes the selection gradient on the focal trait
>    in two independent systems.
>
> If any one holds, I will retract the corresponding part. A one-line "this fails because..." is more
> useful to me than agreement. If you would rather not engage, no reply is needed.
>
> **If it survives, and only then:** here are the sources. The figures are the authority, not my
> summary of them.
> - Canonical figures text with line citations: local copy at `FRAMEWORK/reference/03-FIGURES_TEXT.md`, public figures at https://github.com/IsaiahN/tether/tree/general-agent/figures
> - Full architecture (Medium): https://medium.com/@IsaiahNwukor/arrival-of-the-fittest-the-tether-architecture-1de7edf641ec
> - Worked narrative: https://adventuresinml.substack.com/p/how-intelligence-actually-works
>
> **If you want to use the machinery:** the formula is in the figures link. Run it on a problem you
> know and can check against a ground. Write down what would refute your result *before* you run it,
> and state the mode of what you get. A reported failure is more useful to me than a reported success.
>
> — [your name]

| Per slot, not averaged | Fig 1 :19 "The residual is indexed by object slot, not aggregated." |

| One-to-one and opt-in only | Fig 2 :42 "Buy independence in proportion to the cost of being wrong." Selecting recipients for likely agreement would manufacture the disqualifying correlation. |

**Stage 1:** the shape claim and the four refuters, with the request for a failure. **Stage 2, opt-in:** the three links, with the note that the figures, not the summary, are the authority. **Stage 3:** the formula as a method to reuse, with each user writing their own refuter and mode. A reply to stage 1 is recorded before any theory is shown and is never pooled as corroboration.
