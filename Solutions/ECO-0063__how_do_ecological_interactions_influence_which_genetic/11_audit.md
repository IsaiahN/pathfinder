## Independent domain census (written from the residual line alone)

I redid the census before relying on the primary's conclusions, using the WATSON order: R by arity, symmetry and scale; one line per domain with actors / currency / anchor; partition or union; order by dependency. I started from the residual as posed in the question, not from the figure lines the primary chose.

- **R (arity / symmetry / scale).** The residual is a gap between the trait-frequency distribution predicted from inheritance alone and the distribution measured after a generation in a community. Arity: many, one population's trait vector against several interactors and conditions. Symmetry: asymmetric; predation is not interchangeable with pollination, and the same interactor flips sign with the residual. Scale: generational for frequency shift, community for the web, lineage for feedback. I reached this without using the primary's figure list; the primary reached it through Fig 11 :44 and Fig 9 :22. Different routes, same boundaries.
- **Domain 1, the genetic library.** Actors: alleles, genotypes, recombination. Currency: allele frequency and fitness covariance. Anchor: a per-locus frequency time series. Type: partition of composition versus import.
- **Domain 2, the community habitat.** Actors: species, individuals, relations. Currency: interaction effects on survival and reproduction. Anchor: a manipulative measurement of an interaction's effect. Type: union of overlapping conditions, each overlap a compound.
- **Domain 3, the ground.** Actors: environments and descendants. Currency: survival and reproduction outcomes. Anchor: the outcome itself, which does not update on anyone's opinion. Type: unmaintained in the field, maintained under a breeder or manager.
- **Domain 4, the feedback compound.** Actors: the evolved population and the web it changes. Currency: the reverse edge. Anchor: a measured interaction-strength change after trait change. Type: cycle when the reverse edge is present, chain when absent.

Order by dependency (Fig 3 :20): refuse the "interactions generate variation" reading before minting; require standing variation or an awaited import; acquire a vocabulary for trait and interactor; enumerate the habitat outward; measure per locus against a non-updating anchor. My census matches the primary's four domains and its union reading. Where the primary derived from Fig 11 and Fig 6, I derived from the residual's arity and the ground/anchor test. Low overlap between routes, but one evidence pool.

## Round trip gap R_T

The Symbols table defines R_T as the gap between what was sent up and what came back down, `gap(x, (T_E ∘ T_A)(x))`, measuring what the coarser description cannot hold. Fig 4 :30 requires it to be measured rather than assumed. I ran the round trip on the condensation this folder actually uses.

1. **Sent up (x):** the verbatim figure set, chiefly Fig 11 :43/:47/:49-52/:56/:61, Fig 6 :21-22, Fig 1 :19 and Fig 9 :32.
2. **Abstraction (T_A):** the shorthand carried in `07` and `09`: interactions are residual-relative habitat relations; selection sorts standing variation per slot; atoms arrive by import; contact cascades; the feedback is a cycle; the winner is unreached.
3. **Concretisation (T_E):** a concrete field claim, for example "predation drove the increase in allele A at locus L in lake 1".

**R_T is nonzero.** Four things the shorthand holds do not survive the trip back down:

- the **identity of the interactor and the allele**, which the figures never supply (Fig 9 :32);
- the **source of the frequency reading**, since selection, drift, gene flow and sampling can give the same curve (Hard Problem is About Source, opened this session);
- the **sign of the reverse edge**, which decides whether the object is a cycle or a chain (Fig 12 :121);
- the **list of second-order actors**, which determines whether enumeration stopped too early (Fig 11 :52).

Conversely, one thing survives cleanly: a structure sits between the residual and the frequency change in the form of an enumerated relation, and the unit is the per-locus slot. That is the residue worth sending. R_T was **not** measured numerically, because no ground reading exists; it is recorded qualitatively as the enumerated set of dropped constraints. Treating "the figures plus a formula are the same thing in fewer words" would set R_T to zero and would be the dead-reckoning error Fig 4 :30 names. The honest form is a lossy condensation with a measured gap, which is what `09_watson_answer.md` states.
