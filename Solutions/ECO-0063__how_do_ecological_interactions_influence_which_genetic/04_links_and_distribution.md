## Wider literature that bears on the residual

1. **The feedback is real but hard to demonstrate.** Post and Palkovacs write that "not a single system where the evidence of dynamic feedbacks is without gaps" exists among their five cases. This does not conflict with the figure verdict; it is the budget side of the claim. Fig 9 :60 says "Use filters for the budget and witnesses for the verdict. Never let a filter hand you a verdict." The cycle is figure-licensed; its strength in any particular system is a measurement.
2. **Variation is the precondition, not the product.** The single-clone algal control and the "lack of genetic variation" constraint in Post and Palkovacs corroborate F3: selection sorts what is there. The figure line is Fig 6 :21 "MINT composes inside the closure and can never add an atom."
3. **A source discriminator is the instrument that is owed.** The salmon MHC time series in Pelletier et al. is the model: allele frequencies measured through time, pathogen exposure characterized, a specific allele implicated. It is the closest world analogue to the ground reading that would close F8. From the Hard Problem essay: "An instrument that can't distinguish these cases demonstrates its own inadequacy."
4. **Managers are inside the loop but cannot subtract contact.** "Assisted gene flow" in the gene-flow source is a maintained import: move individuals or gametes, introduce pre-adapted genotypes, and inherit whatever the new habitat brings. Fig 11 :61: "Introduction is available; exclusion is not."

None of these sources conflicts with the figure verdict, so the conflict rule (return to census) was not triggered. The ecology sources share a broad review pool (Post and Palkovacs is cited by the Wikipedia synthesis and by Pelletier et al.), so they are counted as a partial pool rather than three independent witnesses. The Price equation and the DDT record are genuinely lower overlap. The three essays share the framework author's evidence pool completely and carry no independent witness weight.
