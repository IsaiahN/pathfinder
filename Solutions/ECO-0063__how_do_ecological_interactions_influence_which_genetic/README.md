## The residual in effect terms

What failed to resolve is why allele and trait frequencies in a population move in directions that track the surrounding community of predators, prey, competitors, mutualists, pathogens and pollinators, rather than drifting without direction. The effect to explain is a change in the frequency of a heritable variant conditional on which other organisms are in contact with the population. Option A, interactions create the variation that then spreads, leads to the prediction that adaptive change can occur without segregating variation or import. Option B, interactions sort variation that is already present or arrives by mutation and gene flow, leads to the prediction that adaptive change requires standing variation or an imported atom and that the sign of selection flips when the community changes. The answer is bound as an ordinary term: "ecological interactions are the habitat's relations through which the ground votes on standing heritable variation, indexed per trait slot".

## What is deliberately not here

- No personal emails of named individuals, and no scraped contact list.
- No ranking of people by how likely they are to agree with the claim.
- No bulk-send plan.

The reason is structural, not politeness. Fig 2 :35: "Alignment is triangulation, not negotiation. A mirror cannot correct you." Asking them to accept the framework before they check the output spends the only thing they contribute (Fig 10 :42, "A verifier that can reconstruct the claim cannot verify it.").
