## QUESTION

Decide, in effect terms, whether information flow or energy flow is the more fundamental account of physical processes. Restated as an effect: a descriptor is more fundamental if it (1) sits closer to primitives, (2) needs less import from other descriptors, and (3) bears more derivation at the same level. Option A: information wins. Option B: energy wins. Option C: the comparison is level-relative and the question is a union that must be split. What each leads to: A promotes a compound to a primitive without a source gate; B defends "energy alone" although energy is directionless alone and a flow needs a gradient; C returns a level-indexed shape, a lossy crossing, and a declared instrument gap. R is indexed per slot and per level, never aggregated (Fig 1 :19). Anchor candidates: the six-term set for the substrate, the Landauer erasure experiment for the crossing, and a same-level same-ground residual comparison for the verdict.

## VERDICT (from the figures)

The shape survives; a single winner does not. At the substrate, energy is one of six held terms and information is not, and a flow of either kind is a compound of a state and a structure. At the controller, information in the MDL sense is the price of a description, so it is primary there in the only sense the framework licenses. The levels are in lockstep with a lossy crossing, not in a rank order, so there is no level at which one descriptor finally wins. Semantic information is a frame-relative relation to a residual. The world literature conflicts, and the conflicts were re-censused rather than averaged: Wheeler claims the information-first name, the Landauer tradition ties information to an energy bill, and Cárdenas-García et al. deny that information is fundamental at all. The verdict is the union kept separate per domain, never averaged. The missing thing is a source-discriminating instrument, declared `gap`, not a hidden answer. Mode: `general` on the level-refusal, `specified` on each domain.

- **Cost if wrong:** a wrong split sends researchers to the wrong lever. If the two conditions form a partition rather than a union, an instrument aimed at the ground misses the controller condition, and a metatheory claim is refuted at the wrong level. The cost is bounded and locatable because the five refuters are written in advance and level-indexed.
- **Undoable:** yes. No term is deleted if the claim fails; a refutation is added and the reach falls because something was added rather than deleted (Fig 6 :42-43). The lineage in `01_figure_grounded_audit.md` and `11_audit.md` lets a later reader locate the error.
- **Reversed by:** any of R1 to R5, in particular a same-level same-ground residual comparison in which one descriptor persists and does not trend down while the other trends down; a zero-energy information transfer; a derived seventh primitive load-bearing in a law; a level-independent rank order surviving no-bottom and no-top; or one lever clearing both union conditions. A world source with a genuinely different evidence pool showing that all controller pricing reduces to joules with no residual would also reverse it. None has been run. Until one fires, the verdict stands as `candidate`.
