# Outreach email, two-stage offer

**Use:** one to one, or as a cover letter to a public submission venue in `03_public_contact_points.csv`. **Not** for bulk send. No trust request, no agreement request, no ranking of recipients by likely assent.

---

**Subject:** One check on a claim that "more fundamental" is level-relative

> Hi [name],
>
> I have one specific output I would like you to check against what you already know. I am not asking you to read any theory first, and I am not asking you to agree with anything. If the output does not survive, there is nothing else to look at.
>
> **The output (`specified`, not `grounded`).** A common framing asks whether information flow or energy flow is the more fundamental account of physical processes. The residual, stated in effect terms, is that "more fundamental" has to mean closer to primitives, cheaper in imports, and more productive of derivation. When the comparison is indexed by level, the two descriptors trade places: at the substrate, energy is a held primitive and information is a compound; at the controller, information is the native currency for pricing descriptions and energy is not. The framework's rule is that the two levels are in lockstep, with a lossy transform between them, so there is no total rank and the question is really two well-formed questions that must be split.
>
> **What I am not claiming:** that information has no physical purchase. Erasure and transmission have measured energy bills. Nor that energy flows "alone": a flow is energy plus a gradient, a compound, not a bare primitive. Nor that Shannon information carries semantic content; the founding measure explicitly excluded semantics.
>
> **What would refute the shape claim.** I wrote these down before sending this:
> 1. a seventh primitive derived from inside the six-term set, load-bearing in an established law, with no import;
> 2. a same-level, same-ground measurement in which one descriptor's residual persists and does not trend down while the other's trends down;
> 3. information transfer with zero energy and zero structural exchange at any resolution;
> 4. a level-independent rank order of descriptors that survives both no-bottom and no-top;
> 5. one lever that resolves the substrate and controller conditions with no categorically different residue.
>
> If any one holds, I will retract the shape claim. A one-line "this fails because..." is more useful to me than agreement. If you would rather not engage, no reply is needed.
>
> **If it survives, and only then:** the sources are below. The figures are the authority, not my summary of them.
> - Verbatim figure text with line numbers (local framework extract): `FRAMEWORK/reference/03-FIGURES_TEXT.md`
> - Canonical usage rule (local): `FRAMEWORK/reference/FIGURES_README_canonical.md`
> - The eight-step loop (local): `FRAMEWORK/figures/THE_FORMULA.md`
>
> **If you want to use the method:** write down what would refute your own result before you run it, and state whether your claim is general, specified or grounded. The framework's own limit is that a loop cannot check the direction of its own question, so a pre-registered refuter is the only discipline available here. A reported failure is more useful to me than a reported success.
>
> - [name]
