## Distribution rules

Figure-grounded reasons for offering rather than pushing:

- **Fig 10 :52** - "Bring a characterised residual, never the source." Lead with the split question and its refuter.
- **Fig 10 :42** - "A verifier that can reconstruct the claim cannot verify it." A recipient who has absorbed the whole framework before giving a verdict has joined the frame being checked.
- **Fig 2 :35** - "Alignment is triangulation, not negotiation. A mirror cannot correct you." Do not ask for agreement.
- **Fig 9 :30-31** - "your words are borrowed too, so describe the effect / rather than the cause." Describe the residual in effect terms before naming a field.
- **Fig 4 :22** - "Only methods cross up. Only head starts come down." The formula is a method and may be given away; a conclusion is a recording.
- **Fig 2 :40** - "agreement about the world is worthless when the agreeing frames share an evidence pool." Do not pool later agreement as corroboration.
- **Recursive Self-Improvement Trap essay** - "Prestige can fund an action. It can give an action a budget to be tried. But it cannot settle an action. Only the ground can settle it." A famous reader's endorsement is a budget, not a verdict.

**Recommended order in a reply:** (1) the split question and the shape claim; (2) the five refuters, with the request "what would you count as a failure?"; (3) record the verdict; (4) only then, if the recipient is curious, the framework links, with the note that the figures, not this summary, are the authority.

## The two-stage offer

- **Stage 1.** The residual, the shape claim and the pre-registered refuters, with no theory. The recipient checks the shape without holding the framework interior. Verdict recorded first.
- **Stage 2, opt-in only.** The framework links, after the stage-1 verdict. Later agreement is never pooled as corroboration.
- **Stage 3.** The formula as a reusable method, with the instruction that each user state their own mode and write their own refuter before running it. The Formula's "Direction" section says a frame "cannot check the direction of its own question, because the shape a frame is searching for is not a term it holds."

**Hard precondition.** Stages 1 to 3 only work if the stage-1 output survives. Its refuters are written and level-indexed, so a failure is locatable rather than invisible. If R1 to R5 in `01_figure_grounded_audit.md` are not yet run, say so explicitly rather than implying settlement.
