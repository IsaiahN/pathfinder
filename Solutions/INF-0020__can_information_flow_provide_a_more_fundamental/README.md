## What is deliberately not here

- No personal emails of named individuals.
- No ranking of people by how likely they are to agree that information is fundamental.
- No bulk-send plan, and no request for endorsement.

Three figure-grounded reasons:

1. **Agreement is not an anchor.** Fig 2 :35: "Alignment is triangulation, not negotiation. A mirror cannot correct you." A list selected for people already inclined to agree manufactures the correlation that would disqualify their agreement as a verdict (Fig 2 :40, Fig 9 :60).
2. **A verifier must not hold the interior.** Fig 10 :42: "A verifier that can reconstruct the claim cannot verify it." The Seat Map keeps the outside frame ignorant of the framework's interior for this reason.
3. **The ground, not prestige, settles.** The Recursive Self-Improvement Trap essay: "Prestige can fund an action. It can give an action a budget to be tried. But it cannot settle an action. Only the ground can settle it." A famous name is a budget, not a witness.

Legally: unsolicited bulk email to harvested addresses breaches GDPR and CAN-SPAM style rules in many jurisdictions. Public opt-in submission routes avoid that failure as well as the structural one.
