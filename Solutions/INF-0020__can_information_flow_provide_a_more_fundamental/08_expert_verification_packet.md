# Expert verification packet: information flow versus energy flow

**What this is:** a claim in a form a physicist, information theorist or philosopher of science can check. Mode, derivation and refuters are stated before the conclusion.

**Mode: `general` on the level-refusal, `specified` on each domain reading. Not `grounded`.** No same-level, same-ground residual comparison is claimed. This is a shape and a split, not a result.

**Status:** simulated derivation checked for internal consistency against the figures, not a ground reading. It cannot settle the question it reports on.

## 1. The claim, in one paragraph

"More fundamental than" is an ordered comparison indexed by level, not a total rank of descriptors. At the substrate, energy is a held term of the six-term set and information is not, and a flow of either kind is a compound of a state and a structure. At the controller, information in the Shannon and Kolmogorov sense is the currency the loop charges for a description, so it is primary there in the only sense the framework licenses. The levels are in lockstep with a lossy crossing, so there is no level at which one descriptor wins globally. Semantic information is a frame-relative relation to a residual. The missing thing is a source-discriminating instrument, not a further argument.

## 2. Three checkable sub-claims

### C1 - framework internal; checkable by reading Figure 13

- **Statement:** energy is a held primitive; information is not one of the six; a flow is a compound of energy and a gradient.
- **Source, verbatim:** Fig 13 :14: "conserved, finite, directionless alone" (energy as a state). Fig 13 :116: "Six members, and no amount of arranging produces a seventh: a new member would have to be a new kind, and composition only ever rearranges the kinds already held." - **Mode:** `specified` for the census claim, `general` for the no-seventh rule.
- **Refuter:** derive a seventh member from inside the six and show it load-bearing in an established law with no import; or exhibit a flow composed from energy with no structure.

### C2 - framework internal, checkable by reading Figures 12 and 5

- **Statement:** the two currencies do not add, and irreducibility is level-relative. Bits price descriptions at the controller; actions price settling at the ground. There is no global rank.
- **Source, verbatim:** Fig 12 :125: "A composition costs in two currencies, and they do not add." Fig 12 :126: "Bits price the description. Actions price finding out whether it holds." Fig 12 :104: "A settled molecule becomes an atom for whatever composes over it. So irreducible is a fact about a level and never about the world, and there is no bottom". Fig 5 :45: "This is minimum description length, Rissanen 1978, which is itself a composition of Kolmogorov complexity and Shannon coding put to work on model selection."
- **Mode:** `general`.
- **Refuter:** give an instrument that converts a bit-price and an action-price into one lossless unit, and show a case where their sum, rather than either alone, decides; or state a rank order of descriptors independent of level that survives both no-bottom and no-top.

### C3 - world-facing, checkable against the published record

- **Statement:** the physical literature does not settle a single winner. Landauer and Bérut show an energy bill for information processing; Wheeler claims the information-first name from a different closure; Shannon excludes semantics from his measure; ecological and neural work treat information and energy as two layers of one process.
- **Sources actually read:** R. Landauer, IBM J. Res. Dev. 5 (1961): "logical irreversibility is associated with physical irreversibility and requires a minimal heat generation." A. Bérut et al., Nature 483 (2012): "This result demonstrates the intimate link between information theory and thermodynamics." J. A. Wheeler (1990): "every it - every particle, every field of force, even the spacetime continuum itself - derives its function, its meaning, its very existence... from the apparatus-elicited answers to yes or no questions." C. E. Shannon (1948): "These semantic aspects of communication are irrelevant to the engineering problem." U. Brose et al., Nature Ecology & Evolution 9 (2025): "Traditionally, ecological network research has focused on interactions involving the flow of matter and energy... species also interact by... exchanging information signals and cues."
- **What to check:** is each characterization faithful? Do the Landauer experiments establish a *bill* for information, not the *identity* of information with energy? Does Wheeler's thesis belong to the same closure as the six-term census?
- **Refuter:** a same-level, same-ground residual comparison in which one descriptor's residual persists and does not trend down while the other's trends down; or a measured information transfer with zero energy and zero structural exchange at any resolution.

## 3. What is explicitly not claimed

- Not that energy wins and information loses, nor the reverse. No global rank is asserted.
- Not that "energy flow alone" is a primitive. A flow is energy plus a gradient.
- Not that information has no physical purchase. Erasure has a measured energy bill.
- Not that Shannon information carries semantic content. The founding measure excluded semantics; semantic valence is frame-relative.
- Not a theory of everything and not a sealed room. No last descriptor ends the question.
- Not `grounded`. Nothing here is asserted as settled by experiment.

## 5. What would settle it

1. **Crossing test:** same level, same ground, compare the residuals of an energy plus gradient descriptor and an information plus network descriptor. The one that persists and does not trend down is the term to refuse (Formula step 2).
2. **Zero-bill test:** information transfer with zero energy and zero structural exchange at any resolution. A positive result breaks C3 and the flow-as-compound reading.
3. **Seventh-member test:** derive a seventh primitive from inside the six, load-bearing in an established law. Breaks C1.
4. **Level-independence test:** a rank order surviving no-bottom and no-top. Breaks C2.
5. **Union test:** one lever clears the substrate and controller conditions with no categorically different residue. Breaks the split.
