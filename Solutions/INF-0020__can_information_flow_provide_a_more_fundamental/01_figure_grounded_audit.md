## 0. The residual, stated in effect terms

The question asks which of two descriptors is "more fundamental": information flow or energy flow "alone." In effect terms, a claim to be more fundamental must do three things: (1) sit closer to the primitives, (2) require less import from other descriptors, and (3) bear more derivation at the same level. The two options are:

- **Option A:** information flow is the more primitive account, and energy flow is a derivative of it.
- **Option B:** energy flow is the more primitive account, and information is a derivative description of energy arrangements.
- **Option C (the shape derived below):** "more fundamental" is not a total rank over descriptors. It is a comparison indexed by level and by currency. At the substrate, energy is a held primitive and information is a compound; at the controller, information is the native pricing currency and energy is not. Because the levels are in lockstep and the transform between them is lossy, the question is a **union** of two well-formed questions and must be split before it is searched.

What an answer would look like, bound as an ordinary term before the run (Formula "Direction"): a claim that names, for each level, the primitives, the currency, the anchor, and the instrument that would discriminate between A, B and C. If no such discriminating instrument is nameable, the honest output is a shape plus a declared gap, not a winner.

Anchor candidates: the six-term set as a framework-internal metalaw; experiments such as the measured Landauer bound (Bérut et al., Nature 2012, read 2026-11-23) for the physical crossing; Shannon's own exclusion of semantics for the syntactic/semantic split. An anchor is defined at Fig 2 :29 as "An anchor is whatever settles the question and does not change its answer because of what any frame thinks. A measurement, an experiment, a counter, a market, a patient outcome."

## 3. Findings F1 to F10

### F1 - Energy is a held primitive; information is not among the six (decisive for D1)

Fig 13 :3: "No system functions without any of them, none is built from the others, and everything else is a composition across the two kinds."

Fig 13 :14: "conserved, finite, directionless alone" (the entry for energy as a state).

Fig 13 :43-44: "A continuum was the seventh and was removed for failing exactly this: it is a spectrum without gaps, or smoothness over space and time. A generating set must not contain what its own members produce."

Fig 13 :116: "Six members, and no amount of arranging produces a seventh: a new member would have to be a new kind, and composition only ever rearranges the kinds already held."

Energy is one of the six. Information is not one of the six; like the removed continuum, it is something the members produce. Promoting it to a primitive would require an import with a source gate and a shadow test (Fig 6 :14: "the only operator that moves the wall"), and no such import is supplied by the question.

**Mode:** `specified` for the framework claim that energy is held and information is not; `general` for the no-seventh rule. **Status: triangulated** across Fig 13 (the six-term set) and Fig 4 :20 ("Only the actors, the currency and the anchor change"), two figures with different jobs that agree on what a level's primitives are. **Refuter:** derive a seventh member from inside the six and show it is load-bearing in an established law, with no import. If that succeeds, F1 falls.

### F2 - "Energy flow alone" is not a primitive object; a flow is already a compound

Fig 13 :76: "Energy and a gradient compose a flow. A gradient over space and one over time are different terms, because which state it is bonded to is part of what it is."

Fig 13 :22: "a difference that can be spent" (the entry for gradient as a structure).

Energy is "directionless alone" (Fig 13 :14); a gradient supplies the directedness. So the question's "energy flow alone" is a **state plus a structure**, not a bare primitive. Option B as stated is therefore not available: nobody is defending energy alone, because energy alone does not flow.

**Mode:** `general` for the composition rule, `specified` for the energy-flow reading. **Status: triangulated** (Fig 13 :14 and :76, plus Fig 13 :45-47's argument that a gradient carries directedness neither part has). **Refuter:** exhibit a flow composed from energy with no structure and show the framework admits it. Until then the word "alone" in the question is a strain, named here.

### F3 - Information is the controller's pricing currency, already inside Γ

Fig 5 :45: "This is minimum description length, Rissanen 1978, which is itself a composition of Kolmogorov complexity and Shannon coding put to work on model selection."

Fig 12 :125: "A composition costs in two currencies, and they do not add."

Fig 12 :126: "Bits price the description. Actions price finding out whether it holds."

Inside the framework, "information" in the Shannon/Kolmogorov sense is not a physical primitive at all. It is the price of a description, used for model selection, and it lives on the controller side of the membrane. The framework also refuses to let this internal price certify itself: Fig 1 :27 says "They are frame-internal. A frame cannot score itself with a quantity it also produces."

**Mode:** `general` (structural). **Status: triangulated** across Fig 5 (MDL composition) and Fig 12 (two currencies), with Fig 1 :27 as a third, low-overlap check. **Refuter:** show that MDL is not a composition of Shannon coding and Kolmogorov complexity, or show the loop has no description-cost term. Then D2 dissolves.

### F4 - The two levels are in lockstep, not in a rank order

Fig 13 :4-5: "There is no order of rank here. The two kinds are in lockstep, a crossing is a change of role rather than a step upward, and no member of the pair is senior to the other."

Fig 12 :103: "Composition is recursive, and the level decides what is irreducible."

Fig 12 :104: "A settled molecule becomes an atom for whatever composes over it. So irreducible is a fact about a level and never about the world, and there is no bottom"

Fig 4 :33: "Which is why there is no top and no infinite regress."

"More fundamental" is a level-relative predicate. A settled controller-level molecule can become an atom at the next level, so information can become primitive *at a level* without being a substrate primitive. There is no bottom and no top, so there is no total order in which one descriptor wins globally.

**Mode:** `general`. **Status: triangulated** across Fig 13 (lockstep), Fig 12 (recursion and irreducibility) and Fig 4 (no top). **Refuter:** exhibit a consistent rank order of descriptors that is independent of level and survives both the no-bottom and no-top rules. That would break the framework, not merely the answer.

### F5 - The membrane between levels is lossy, so the comparison needs a measured round trip

Fig 4 :27: "The transform between scales loses information, and that is the point."

Fig 4 :30: "So take the round trip and measure the gap. Without it you are navigating by dead reckoning: confident about a position nobody checked."

A claim that "information explains energy" typically smuggles a controller-level measure down to the substrate without measuring what the descent dropped. **Mode:** `general`. **Status: single-source for the explicit round-trip requirement (Fig 4), corroborated by the formula's "PROMOTE" step.** **Refuter:** show a transform between the substrate and controller descriptions that is information-preserving in both directions, with a measured zero gap.

## 4. Pre-registered refuter (written before the conclusion)

Required before any conclusion or outreach: "a falsifier written before the run is a design instrument, not only an honesty device." The shape claim is refuted if any of the following holds:

1. **R1 (substrate).** A seventh member of the six-term set is derived from inside the set, with no import, and is load-bearing in an established law. This breaks F1 and Fig 13 :116.
2. **R2 (ground).** At the same level and against the same ground, an information-flow descriptor yields a residual that persists and does not trend down while an energy plus gradient descriptor's residual trends down (or the reverse). The term to refuse is the one with the persistent flat residual (Formula step 2; Fig 5 :59).
3. **R3 (currency).** A measurement shows information transfer with zero energy and zero structural exchange, that is, an erasure or transmission with no heat bill at any resolution. This breaks F2 and the Landauer crossing.
4. **R4 (level).** A rank order of descriptors that is independent of level is stated and survives both "there is no bottom" (Fig 12 :104) and "there is no top" (Fig 4 :33). This breaks F4.
5. **R5 (union).** One lever resolves both the substrate condition and the controller condition with no categorically different residue. This breaks F6.

If any fires, the shape claim is withdrawn. Until one is run, no part of this may be labelled `grounded`.

## 7. Updated verdict

The shape survives; a single winner does not. At the substrate, energy is a held term of the six, information is not, and a flow of either kind is a compound (F1, F2). At the controller, information in the MDL sense is the native price of a description and energy is not, so information is "more fundamental" there in the only sense the framework licenses: it is the currency the loop uses to decide what to mint (F3). The two levels are in lockstep and the crossing is lossy, so the question is a union that must be split (F4, F5, F6). Semantic information is a frame-relative relation to a residual, not a substrate primitive (F7). No sealed room and no last room end the argument in favour of one descriptor (F9). What remains is a `gap`: a source-discriminating instrument, characterised in effect terms but not held (F10).

Strains after re-census: one, named. The phrase "energy flow alone" has no figure-licensed referent, because a flow requires a gradient. It is treated as a strain in the question, not repaired by silently rewriting it. Conflict with Wheeler's "it from bit" was returned to the census and did not flip the verdict. Mode: `general` on the level-refusal, `specified` on each domain. No claim is `grounded`.
