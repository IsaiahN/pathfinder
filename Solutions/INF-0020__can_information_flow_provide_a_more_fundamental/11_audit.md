## Round trip gap R_T

The Symbols table defines R_T as `gap(x, (T_E ∘ T_A)(x))`, the gap between what was sent up and what came back down. Fig 4 :30 requires it measured rather than assumed. I ran the round trip on the condensation the folder actually uses.

2. **Abstraction (T_A):** the shorthand carried in `07_the_tether_answer.md` §2: energy held at substrate, information priced at controller, levels in lockstep, no global winner, missing instrument.
3. **Concretisation (T_E):** a concrete claim of the kind the question invites: "information is the truly fundamental substance, and energy is a mode of information," as in Wheeler's it-from-bit thesis.

**R_T is nonzero.** Three things the shorthand holds do not survive the trip back down:

- the **no-seventh constraint** (Fig 13 :116): the concrete claim promotes a compound to a primitive without a source gate, so it adds an atom the shorthand forbids;
- the **two-currency separation** (Fig 12 :125-126): the concrete claim prices joules and bits in one accounting, which Fig 12 :130 names as the error;
- the **lossy crossing** (Fig 4 :30): the concrete claim reads a controller-level price down into the substrate without measuring what the descent dropped.

Conversely, two things survive cleanly: energy is a held term at the substrate and information is not, and a flow is a compound of a state and a structure. That is the residue worth sending. R_T was **not** measured numerically, because no ground reading exists; it is recorded qualitatively as the enumerated set of dropped constraints. Treating "the formula is the condensed version of the metalaws" as identity would set R_T to zero and would be the dead-reckoning error Fig 4 :30 names. The honest form is a lossy condensation with a recorded gap, which is what `09_watson_answer.md` states.
