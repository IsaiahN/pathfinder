# The Tether's answer: is information flow more fundamental than energy flow?

**Mode: `general` on the level-refusal, `specified` on each domain reading. Not `grounded`.** This is a shape and a route, not a result. Simulated derivation checked against the figures (`05_what_shape_means.md` §6), not a ground reading.

## 1. The residual it answers

The question asks which descriptor is "more fundamental." In effect terms, a fundamental descriptor must sit closer to primitives, require less import, and bear more derivation. The comparison is a two-place, ordered relation indexed by level. R is per slot and per level, never aggregated (Fig 1 :19: "The residual is indexed by object slot, not aggregated."). The question is a **union**, not one hard problem (Fig 9 :55).

## 2. The shape

1. **At the substrate, energy is a held term and information is not.** Fig 13 :14 gives energy as "conserved, finite, directionless alone," one of the six indivisible terms. Fig 13 :116: "Six members, and no amount of arranging produces a seventh." Information, like the removed continuum, is something the members produce, so promoting it to a primitive needs an import the question does not supply.
2. **"Energy flow alone" is not a primitive object.** Fig 13 :76: "Energy and a gradient compose a flow." Energy is directionless alone; the gradient supplies directedness. The comparison is between two compounds, not a primitive and a derivative.
3. **At the controller, information is the native currency.** Fig 5 :45: MDL "is itself a composition of Kolmogorov complexity and Shannon coding put to work on model selection." Fig 12 :126: "Bits price the description. Actions price finding out whether it holds." Information is "more fundamental" here only in the sense that it is the price the loop charges for a term.
4. **Levels are in lockstep, not ranked.** Fig 13 :4-5: "There is no order of rank here. The two kinds are in lockstep, a crossing is a change of role rather than a step upward, and no member of the pair is senior to the other." Fig 12 :104: "irreducible is a fact about a level and never about the world, and there is no bottom." Fig 4 :33: "Which is why there is no top and no infinite regress." A settled controller molecule can become an atom at the next level, so information can be primitive at a level without being a substrate primitive.
5. **The crossing is lossy and must be measured.** Fig 4 :30: "So take the round trip and measure the gap. Without it you are navigating by dead reckoning." Treating the bit-price and the energy-price as one accounting commits the error Fig 12 :130 names.
6. **Semantic information is frame-relative.** Shannon excluded semantics from the engineering problem; Fig 11 :49 says "An actor's valence is relative to the residual, not a property it carries." Meaning is a relation between a frame and a residual, not a substrate primitive.
7. **The missing thing is an instrument, not a winner.** Fig 9 :32: "you get a kind of thing, never the answer itself." Fig 13 :134: "Unreachable is a verdict here, not a silence." After genuine re-census, no figure ranks one descriptor above the other across all levels. What is owed is a source-discriminating instrument.

## 3. What it explicitly does not say

- Not that energy wins and information loses. The framework does not rank descriptors across levels.
- Not that information has no physical purchase. Landauer's bound and its experimental verification show an energy bill for erasure (Bérut et al. 2012, read 2026-11-23).
- Not that "energy alone" flows. A flow is energy plus a gradient.
- Not that Shannon information carries semantic content. The founding measure excluded it.
- Not a theory of everything. There is no last room (Fig 7 :13: "agreement compounds, evidence does not") and no sealed room (Fig 9 :44).
- Not `grounded`. No same-level, same-ground residual comparison has been run here.

## 4. What would settle it, the ground

A source-discriminating instrument, not a further argument. From *The Hard Problem is About Source*: an instrument that reads green "cannot comment on the source"; the spectrometer resolved it. Here:

- **The crossing test.** At one level and against one ground, does an energy plus gradient descriptor or an information plus network descriptor produce a residual that persists and does not trend down while the other's trends down? That identifies the term to refuse (Formula step 2).
- **The zero-bill test.** Is there information transfer with zero energy and zero structural exchange at any resolution? A positive result breaks the Landauer crossing and the flow-as-compound reading.
- **The seventh-member test.** Can a seventh primitive be derived from inside the six and shown load-bearing in an established law?
- **The level-independence test.** Can a rank order of descriptors be stated that survives no-bottom and no-top?
- **The union test.** Can one lever resolve substrate and controller conditions with no categorically different residue?

## 5. The honest stop

**Unreached, not unreachable.** The six-term set is "a human's careful guess at reality's compression axes" (*They're Scanning Books*, read 2026-11-23); declaring it makes the choice visible, not neutral. Fig 13 :143 says indivisibility and minimality "are claims and both are refutable." The world sources conflict: Wheeler's "it from bit" claims the information-first name, Landauer and Bérut tie information to energy, Cárdenas-García et al. deny information fundamental status. The conflict was returned to the census and did not flip the verdict. Fig 8 :39: "So a search that finds nothing is never a proof of absence." "Indiscernibility is not identity."

## 6. One-paragraph version for outreach

> The Tether does not name a winner between information flow and energy flow. It splits the question by level. At the substrate, energy is one of six held terms and information is not, and a flow of either kind is already a compound, energy bonded to a gradient. At the controller, information in the Shannon and Kolmogorov sense is the currency the loop charges for a description, so it is primary there in the only sense the framework licenses. The levels are in lockstep with a lossy crossing, not in a rank order, so there is no bottom and no top at which one descriptor finally wins. The physical energy bill for erasure and the bee's measurable bits of dance are two readings of one process, not rivals. What is missing is not a better argument but a source-discriminating instrument, characterized in effect terms and named with a falsifier written in advance. Until one exists, the answer is a shape and a declared gap, not a verdict.

## 7. Cost, undoability, reversal

- **Cost if wrong:** a wrong split would send researchers to the wrong lever. If the two conditions really form a partition rather than a union, a source-discriminating instrument aimed at the ground would miss the controller condition, and a metatheory claim would be refuted at the wrong level. The cost is bounded and locatable because the refuters are written in advance and the level of each is named.
- **Undoable:** yes. No term is deleted if the claim fails; a refutation is added and the reach falls because "something was added rather than because anything was deleted" (Fig 6 :42-43). The lineage in `01_figure_grounded_audit.md` and `11_audit.md` lets a later reader locate the error.
- **Reversed by:** any of R1 to R5 in `01_figure_grounded_audit.md`, in particular a same-level, same-ground residual comparison that trends one way; a zero-energy information transfer; a derived seventh primitive; a level-independent rank order surviving no-bottom and no-top; or one lever clearing both union conditions. None has been run. Until one fires, the verdict stands as `candidate`.
