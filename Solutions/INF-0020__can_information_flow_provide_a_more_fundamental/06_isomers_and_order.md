### Q3 - Are energy flow and information flow isomers?

- Fig 12 :117: "isomer. Same atoms, different bond, different substance. The difference is invisible in the formula and decisive in the behaviour."
- Fig 12 :124: "functional group. A substructure whose presence fixes the outcome. The group says what kind; the qualifier says which member."

As the question frames them:

- "Energy flow alone" is not a well-formed atom set. Energy is directionless alone (Fig 13 :14) and a flow is energy bonded to a gradient (Fig 13 :76). Its atoms are {Energy, Gradient}.
- "Information flow" has no held term "information." The nearest figure-held composition is Network between frames, since Fig 13 :24 says of network "that entities relate, and influence travels," and Fig 5 :45 prices descriptions in Shannon bits. Its atoms are {Network, Spectrum, Gradient} depending on the reading, with energy still paid at the ground.

The two do not share an atom set, so under Fig 12 :117 they are **not isomers as named**. What they share is the substructure **flow**: a state bonded to a structure that carries directedness. Under Fig 12 :124 that makes them members of the same **functional group**, with different qualifiers. The honest structural claim is: a flow is a functional group realised by different atoms in different frames, and the difference is decided at the level where the ground pays.

A stronger isomer reading is available if both are written over the full shared six-term set, differing only in which structure mediates which state pair. Then they are "same atoms, different bond, different substance," but that version requires the import that neither the question nor the world sources supply, so it is marked `specified`, ungrounded.

**Falsifier, written before the run:** if either description can be written without an atom the other requires, and no shared flow substructure survives, the functional-group claim fails and the two are simply unrelated compounds. Triangulated for the definition (Fig 12), single-source for the physics binding to "information."

### Q4 - Is there a route from one description to the other? Yes, across the membrane, and it is lossy

- Fig 4 :21: "The membrane is the boundary between the sizes, and the rule about what may cross it. Not a wall: a filter with a direction."

The route from an energy-plus-gradient description to a bit-priced description is a controller crossing: the method for pricing descriptions crosses up, a head start crosses down, and the recording of any particular success does neither. No energy reading becomes a bit by composition; a new currency enters at the membrane. **Falsifier:** show a composition inside one closure that changes the currency from action-price to bit-price with no import. Triangulated across Fig 4 and Fig 8.

### Q5 - What does the bee dance analogue say?

- Schürch and Ratnieks 2015, read 2026-11-23: "the direction component conveys 2.9 bits and the distance component 4.5 bits of information," and information is "a reduction in uncertainty."
- Fig 11 :40: "A system cannot be made more capable by improving the agent in isolation. An improvement that does not change contact changes nothing, however much it improves."

A bee's dance is an information flow that is physically a movement and a sound, paid for by the forager's energy, and whose value is relative to the follower's residual. It corroborates the union: the bit measure and the energy bill are two readings of one process at different levels, and neither displaces the other. The dance also shows the capability is agent-and-habitat, not information alone. This is a low-overlap world witness, not a figure line.

**Falsifier:** a dance whose information content is unchanged when the followers' residual changes, with a measured contact unchanged. Single-source for the bee case.

### Q6 - What does the neural analogue say?

- Zhang, Chitic and Bohté 2025, read 2026-11-23: "predictive-coding-like behaviour might be an emergent property of energy optimisation."
- Millidge, Seth and Buckley 2021, read 2026-11-23: predictive coding "postulat[es] that the core function of the brain is to minimize prediction errors with respect to a generative model of the world."

This is the reverse-direction case. An energy objective produced an information-processing structure, which shows that "information first" is not a level-free truth even in the controller. It corroborates F4 (level relativity) and conflicts with a naive substrate reading of Option A. Returned to census; it governs D2 and D3, not D1.

**Falsifier:** a network trained with no energy objective that shows the same predictive-coding properties under the same task. Single-source for the neural case.

## CONCLUSION

"More fundamental than" is an ordered comparison (⋛) whose threshold is set by level, not by a single currency. Energy flow and information flow are not isomers as named: they share a functional group, the flow, with different atoms. The route between their descriptions crosses the membrane, is lossy, and moves a method or a head start rather than a recording. Two low-overlap analogues, the waggle dance and energy-optimised predictive coding, corroborate the union and level shape from animal behaviour and computational neuroscience. The isomer reading over the full six-term set is a real but ungrounded variant with the stated falsifier above.
