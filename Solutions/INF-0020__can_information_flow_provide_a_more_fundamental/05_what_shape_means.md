## 1. A shape is a constraint, not a filler

The canonical figures README states the limit: the framework returns "what a missing thing would have to look like (a shape, not a name)". Fig 9 :32: "you get a kind of thing, never the answer itself". Fig 13 :134: "It cannot say which primitive is missing, because naming it would mean already holding it."

From *The Library of Babel*: "you can cut your search short if the set of books returned is small and descriptive enough that it provides a general shape of the answer that satisfies your question."

For this question a **shape** is: a level-indexed comparison in which the substrate's primitives and the controller's pricing currency are named, but no global winner is named. A name would be "information is fundamental" or "energy is fundamental." The shape is "fundamental is a predicate of a level, and the two descriptors win at different levels because the levels are in lockstep and not ranked."

## 2. How the shape is obtained

- *Library of Babel*: use the vocabulary already held that is closest to the thing, look for recurring new terms that overlap or correlate with it, recurse. "With each new term gained, you are able to narrow the space by definition and deepen your vocabulary."
- Fig 6 :30: "The description travels. Fails by being sharpened into terms only we can read. Cast it in effect terms." Fig 9 :30-31: "your words are borrowed too, so describe the effect / rather than the cause." The effect here is: one descriptor pays in joules at the ground, the other pays in bits for descriptions, and the two prices do not add (Fig 12 :125-126).
- *Decoding Tacit Knowledge*: when contact is not possible, the best that can be sent is a plain description of what one would expect to see, because anyone can check that against the world. For this question the expected observation is a same-level, same-ground residual comparison (refuter R2 in `01_figure_grounded_audit.md`).
- *Library of Babel* on stopping: "you will know that you are done because the finish line will also be the starting place, as there is nowhere else to search and nothing else that can describe the answer better." Here the stop is not a winner but a declared gap, because no source-discriminating instrument is held.

## 3. What licenses a name

Only two things, both outside the shape itself:

1. **The ground settles it.** Formula step 5: "the ground settles it. Verified only by what pays. A gate is not the ground." The README: the framework "stops at the limit of what the available instruments can distinguish, and records the lineage so the stop is honest."
2. **A provenanced import from a frame whose closure differs** (Fig 6 :23, Fig 8 :50-52), with a source gate and a shadow test.

Wheeler's "it from bit" is a name offered by a physics-metaphysical frame. It is admitted as a world source with a source gate: it predicts that physical entities derive from bits. It conflicts with the substrate reading of Fig 13, and the conflict was returned to the census rather than allowed to flip the verdict. The name is not accepted.

Cárdenas-García et al. offer the opposite name from a different closure, biosemiotics and philosophy of nature. It corroborates the shape of the refusal but does not name a missing primitive either. Both names are held as candidates with provenance, not as settled terms.

## 4. Why the "fundamental" question shows the metamerism error

*The Hard Problem is About Source* gives the mechanism. An instrument reads green and "it cannot comment on the source": one green light, or yellow plus blue, or RGB, or CMYK, or a scheme not yet discovered. "If several paths create the same behavior, then behavior is a projection with lossy information." "An instrument that can't distinguish these cases demonstrates its own inadequacy." It was the spectrometer, not an argument, that resolved it.

Here, a process can return the same observable reading under an energy plus gradient description and under an information plus network description. Naming one as "the fundamental source" from the shared reading is exactly the metamerism error. What is owed is a source-discriminating instrument (F10 and refuter R2), not a verdict about which word wins.

*The Recursive Self-Improvement Trap*: "the output is only as good as the validator. The agent, or the tool, or the frame, or the theory, cannot validate itself." The two competing literatures cannot settle this by mutual agreement; each is a frame.

## 5. Q: Does "shape, not a name" forbid saying energy is held at the substrate? ANSWERED, no further question needed

No. The README's phrase governs a **missing** primitive, one not held in Γ. Energy is one of the six terms (Fig 13 :13-14), so naming it is a report about the held set, not the invention of a seventh. Information is not one of the six. Saying so is a census result, not an answer to which descriptor is metaphysically fundamental.

What remains forbidden is promoting information to a seventh primitive on the strength of a shared reading. That would be an import with no source gate, and Fig 6 :24 prices a wrong atom above starting from primitives.

Two consequences, both recorded so neither is silently lost:

- The framework can say energy is a held term and information is a compound. That is a figure-licensed census claim.
- The framework cannot say which descriptor is fundamental *simpliciter*. That is a name where only a shape is available, and the missing thing is an instrument, not a better argument.

## 6. Two further consequences for outreach

**Prestige is not action.** From *The Recursive Self-Improvement Trap*: "Prestige must never be allowed to validate an action directly... Someone let prestige stand in for proof... Prestige can fund an action. It can give an action a budget to be tried. But it cannot settle an action. Only the ground can settle it. The Ground Maintainer must be blind to prestige." A long list of famous information theorists who agree with Option A is the named collapse, not corroboration. The legitimate ask is a budget to try the shape against a ground.

**This audit is a simulation, not a ground reading.** Same essay: "LLMs can quickly simulate an approximate answer/output with the formula found in the architecture post. It will often be better than the generic outputs that you will usually get otherwise too... However, the truly testable output would need to be derived from the real architecture or working the formula yourself." So `01_figure_grounded_audit.md` and this file are simulated derivations, checked for internal consistency against the figures. They are not tests of the question and must be labelled as such. They cannot settle anything they report on.

**Declared primitives are a visible guess.** *They're Scanning Books When They Should*: "A minimum viable set of six terms is a human's careful guess at reality's compression axes; another sensorium's minimum set need not be those six." And: "Declaring the primitives does not make them neutral. It makes the choice visible." Fig 13 :143 echoes this: indivisibility and minimality "are claims and both are refutable." The answer therefore stops at the figure's own limit, with the lineage recorded rather than hidden.
