## Wider literature that bears on the question

1. **The conformist-transmission pool is not one witness.** Henrich and Boyd (1998) argue that conformism maintains within-group similarity and between-group differences; Denton et al. (2020) and Morgan and Thompson (2020) qualify or reverse that claim. They are not independent corroboration of each other, and two of them are modelling papers in the same modern pool. The figures never name a mechanism, so the conflict does not move the verdict. Cite the conflict, not a winner.
2. **Niche construction and the habitat.** Laland et al. (2000) gives the world analogue of Fig 11: practices modify the selection pressures that keep them. This corroborates the habitat domain but is in the same broad evolutionary-evolutionary pool as item 1.
3. **Animal culture, genuinely low overlap.** Whiten et al. (1999) documents 39 behavioural traditions in chimpanzees that vary between communities where ecological explanations were discounted. This corroborates the per-slot and valence-relative findings from a different evidence pool.
4. **Economics and resilience, lower overlap again.** Arthur (1989) shows persistence without current efficiency; Fath et al. (2015) shows long persistence as a rigidity trap with little room for novelty. Both corroborate Fig 7 :12-13 and Fig 8 :32 from outside anthropology.

These are secondary. None of them decides anything the figures have already decided.

## Distribution: offer, do not push

Figure-grounded reasons:

- **Fig 4 :22** - *"Only methods cross up. Only head starts come down."* Sending the full framework before anyone has described a residual sends a recording, not a generator. Lead with the shape and its refuter; offer links after.
- **Fig 10 :42** - *"A verifier that can reconstruct the claim cannot verify it."* A recipient who has absorbed the architecture before giving a verdict has joined the frame being checked. Stage 1 must be answerable without it.
- **Fig 9 :27-28** - *"describe it before you go looking, or any frame you pick will seem to fit."* Ask what they would expect to see if the two-condition shape were true before sending the literature.
- **Fig 2 :40** - *"disqualifying for a verdict: agreement about the world is worthless when the agreeing frames share an evidence pool"*; Fig 2 :42 - *"Buy independence in proportion to the cost of being wrong."* A mass send selected for receptiveness produces a convention, not corroboration.

**Recommended order in a reply:** (1) the per-slot residual, the two-condition shape and the five refuters; (2) ask what they would count as a failure and record it; (3) only then the links, with the note that the figure text, not the summary, is the authority; (4) offer the formula as a method for their own problem, with their own refuter written first.

## Practical constraints

- The `Human Nature` guidelines read on 2026-11-22 state that LLMs do not satisfy authorship criteria and that LLM use must be documented in Methods. This folder is explicitly a simulated derivation. Do not present it as a human-authored manuscript; offer it as a checkable shape and let the recipient write the paper.
- CES abstracts are reviewed anonymously, so a CES submission is a legitimate ground-visible route that does not depend on a personal network. Check current deadlines on the conference site.
- No archive was created and nothing was uploaded. If you host the packet, prefer an opt-in download page with a clear decline option over an attachment pushed to a list.

## The two-stage offer (restated)

1. **Stage 1:** residual, shape, refuter. Ask what would count as a failure. Record the verdict before any theory is shown (Fig 10 :52, Fig 2 :45).
2. **Stage 2, opt-in:** the sources above. Later agreement is never pooled as corroboration (Fig 2 :40).
3. **Stage 3:** the formula as a reusable generator, with each user writing their own refuter and stating their own mode (`THE_FORMULA.md` "Direction").

**Hard precondition:** stages 1 to 3 only work if stage 1 survives. The five refuters in `01_figure_grounded_audit.md` §3 and `08_expert_verification_packet.md` are the test.
