## What is deliberately not here

- No personal emails of named individuals.
- No ranking of people by how likely they are to agree that persistence has a shape.
- No bulk-send plan.
- No claim that conformist transmission, niche construction, or any single mechanism is "the" answer. Those are world sources, secondary to the figures, and two of them conflict with each other on 2026-11-22 reading.

Three reasons from the figures' own words:

1. **Agreement is not an anchor.** *"Alignment is triangulation, not negotiation. A mirror cannot correct you."* (Fig 2 :35). A list assembled from people who already agree about cultural persistence manufactures the correlation that would make their agreement worthless.
2. **Correlation disqualifies a verdict.** *"disqualifying for a verdict: agreement about the world is worthless when the agreeing frames share an evidence pool"* (Fig 2 :40). This simulated derivation and every essay it cites share one author and one evidence pool.
3. **A verifier must not be handed the interior.** *"A verifier that can reconstruct the claim cannot verify it."* (Fig 10 :42). The first contact carries the residual and its refuter, not the framework.

Legally: public institutional submission routes and opt-in channels avoid the practical failure of unsolicited bulk outreach as well as the structural one. The `Human Nature` submission guidelines read for this folder state that large language models do not currently satisfy authorship criteria and that LLM use must be documented in the Methods section, which is one more reason this packet is offered as a checkable shape and never as a manuscript to submit as-is.
