## 1. A shape is a constraint, not a filler

The canonical figures README states the limit: the framework returns *"what a missing thing would have to look like (a shape, not a name)"*. Fig 9 :32: *"you get a kind of thing, never the answer itself"*. Fig 13 :134: *"Unreachable is a verdict here, not a silence,"*.

From *The Library of Babel* (opened 2026-11-22): *"you can cut your search short if the set of books returned is small and descriptive enough that it provides a general shape of the answer that satisfies your question."*

So for COG-0084 a **shape** is what any answer to "why do some practices persist" must satisfy before it can name a mechanism: it must be per practice-slot, it must separate a maintained channel from a ground-maintained one, it must separate method from recording, and it must leave the specific practice open. A shape is not a list of traits that persistent practices happen to share. *"Persistence is a thing inside the practice"* would be a name, and a wrong one.

## 2. How the shape is obtained

- *The Library of Babel*: use the vocabulary closest to the thing, then *"With each new term gained, you are able to narrow the space by definition and deepen your vocabulary."* For this question the nearest words are "transmission", "fidelity", "ground", "habitat", "renewal". Each narrows the search.
- Fig 6 :30: *"The description travels. Fails by being sharpened into terms only we can read. Cast it in effect terms."* Fig 9 :30-31: *"your words are borrowed too, so describe the effect rather than the cause."* So the residual is described as "the mismatch stays small in this slot across generations", not as "this practice is traditional".
- *Decoding Tacit Knowledge*: *"When contact isn't possible, the best you can send is a plain description of what you'd expect to see, because anyone can check that against the world."* This is why the verification packet asks what an expert would expect to see if the two-condition shape were true.
- *The Library of Babel* on stopping: the finish line is *"the starting place"*, because the searcher has *"composed the answer through the process of locating it."* The honest stop here is not a mechanism; it is the two conditions plus a declared `gap` for the specific practice.

## 3. What licenses a name, and the correction that matters here

Two things only, both outside the shape:

1. **The ground settles it.** `THE_FORMULA.md` step 5: *"the ground settles it. Verified only by what pays. A gate is not the ground."* For cultural practices, that means a longitudinal measurement of the slot, not a plausible story about why the practice survived.
2. **A provenanced import from a closure that differs** (Fig 6 :23, Fig 8 :50-52), with a source gate and a shadow test. Migration, trade or a new instrument can bring an atom the population did not hold; that is a name with a record.

**Correction to my own first reading.** I initially wrote that *"the framework returns a shape, not a name"* forbids naming any mechanism in this answer. That overstates it. The canonical phrase governs a primitive **missing** from the library. Fidelity, ground pressure and habitat are already in the figure vocabulary (Fig 7 :37-39, Fig 11 :44-48). Naming them is a binding onto held terms, not the invention of a new primitive. What remains forbidden is naming *the* mechanism that physically realizes `h²` in a given ethnographic case, or naming the practice that will persist next. Both claims carry zero evidentiary weight until a ground settles them. Both versions are recorded so neither is quietly lost.

## 4. Why source discrimination is the missing instrument for this question

*The Hard Problem is About Source* (opened 2026-11-22) gives the mechanism: an instrument that detects green *"cannot comment on the source"*. It could be one green light, or yellow plus blue, or RGB, or CMYK. *"If several paths create the same behavior, then behavior is a projection with lossy information."* The spectrometer, not an argument, resolved it: *"An instrument that can't distinguish these cases demonstrates its own inadequacy."*

That is exactly the situation in cultural persistence. The observation "the practice is still there after 200 years" is a projection that cannot comment on the source. Three sources could produce it:

- a maintained channel with a seat that keeps introducing supporting actors (Fig 11 :58-59);
- an unmaintained habitat where the ground simply kept what survived (Fig 11 :56);
- a mirror chain in which agreement compounded and evidence did not (Fig 7 :12-13).

They look identical in the record. The right response is not to argue about which one "really" explains persistence. It is to build or name an instrument that discriminates sources: a measure that returns a different reading for a practice kept by payment than for one kept by handoff alone. Until then, the shape is all that is licensed.

## 5. The specific shape for COG-0084

1. **Per slot.** `R_s(t)` is indexed by practice, population and habitat, never averaged (Fig 1 :19, :22).
2. **Two handoff factors.** Persistence requires bracket fidelity `h²` and low ground pressure `S`; either at zero and the chain runs in place (Fig 7 :37-39). Rapid change follows a rise in `S` or a fall in `h²`.
3. **Method versus recording.** What survives a habitat change is the reapplicable generator, not the recording of one occasion (Fig 4 :22, :25).
4. **Valence relative to residual.** No practice is intrinsically persistent; its value flips when the habitat's residual changes (Fig 11 :49-51).
5. **Two modes.** Unmaintained survival and maintained arrival are different explanations and must not be averaged (Fig 11 :56-59).
6. **Union, not partition.** C1 channel contact and C2 renewed reach form a compound; one lever leaves the intersections standing (Fig 9 :54-57).
7. **The slot stays open.** Which practice, and when, is a `gap` (Fig 9 :32, Fig 13 :134).

## 6. Two consequences for outreach and for this document

**Prestige is not action.** *The Recursive Self-Improvement Trap* (as recorded in the exemplar): *"Prestige must never be allowed to validate an action directly... Prestige can fund an action. It can give an action a budget to be tried. But it cannot settle an action. Only the ground can settle it."* Gathering famous names to agree that persistence has a shape is the named collapse. The legitimate ask is a budget to try the shape against a ground.

**My own audit is a simulation, not a ground reading.** Same essay: *"LLMs can quickly simulate an approximate answer/output... However, the truly testable output would need to be derived from the real architecture or working the formula yourself."* LLM output is *"simulacra"*. So `01` is a simulated derivation, checked for internal consistency against the figures. It is not a test of the claim, it cannot settle anything it reports on, and every substantive section says so.
