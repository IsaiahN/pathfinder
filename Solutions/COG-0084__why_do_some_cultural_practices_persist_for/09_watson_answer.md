## QUESTION

Decide, in effect terms, what governs the difference between practice-slots whose mismatch `R_s(t) = |Γ(b,a) - o′|` stays small across many generations and those where it grows within one or two. Option A: persistence is an intrinsic property of a practice, so the answer is a list of traits that persistent practices have. Option B: persistence and rapid change are two regimes of one handoff, decided per slot by a maintained channel and a renewed reach, with the specific practice left open. What each leads to: A searches for a kind of thing and tends to select informants who already agree that tradition is real; B asks for two per-slot measurements and records a `gap` where no measurement exists.

## DOMAIN CENSUS

R by arity, symmetry and scale, then one line per domain with actors / currency / anchor, partition or union, and order by dependency.

- **R (arity / symmetry / scale).** Arity at least three: practice `Γ`, action `a`, observation `o′`, indexed by slot `s` and generation `t`. Symmetry: not time symmetric, and "persists longer than X" is a threshold comparison, not a symmetric join (Operators table :48). Scale: step, cycle, level, lineage, with a lossy membrane between (Fig 4 :27-29).
- **D1 Learner's loop.** Actors: bearer, `Γ`, belief `b` with weighting `w`, action, observation. Currency: per-slot residual reduction; inherited prior versus private history. Anchor: the practice as actually observed. Admitted.
- **D2 Transmission chain.** Actors: teachers, learners, successive rooms. Currency: bracket fidelity `h²`, ground pressure `S`, agreement. Anchor: selection between rooms, where the ground votes. Admitted.
- **D3 Practice composition and reach.** Actors: acts, tools and rules as atoms, plus bonds. Currency: description length, reach, method versus recording. Anchor: settlement against the ground. Admitted.
- **D4 Habitat and ground.** Actors: conditions, relations, other practices. Currency: survival of what the ground kept; valence relative to the residual. Anchor: the ground, which does not decay. Admitted.
- **D5 Maintainers and seat.** Actors: elders, institutions, ritual, enforcement. Currency: contact maintained; introduction available, exclusion not. Anchor: ground kept reachable. Admitted.
- **X1 Intrinsic conservatism of a practice.** No nameable anchor, because valence is relative to the residual. Not a domain yet; refused. X2 "traditional versus modern" is absorbed into D4 as a habitat change.
- **Union, not partition.** D1∩D2 is the handoff; D2∩D4 the maintained transmission channel; D4∩D5 the managed habitat. Each overlap is a compound with its own loop (Fig 9 :55). The whole is a union of C1 (channel contact maintained) and C2 (reach renewed by contact with a differing closure).
- **Order by dependency (Fig 3 :20).** perception → vocabulary → objective → planning → learn and carry. C1 is upstream of C2: a new atom cannot cross a channel with zero fidelity.

Strain test: the earlier reading that "survival of the fittest" governs all persistence strained against the mirror chain; re-census assigned it to the unmaintained panel only (Fig 11 :56). The one remaining light strain is ⋛ applied to a duration; named in `01` and `06`, not hidden.

## VERDICT (from the figures)

The figure-derived verdict: persistence and rapid change are two regimes of one handoff, decided per practice-slot by (C1) a maintained channel with bracket fidelity above zero and low ground pressure, and (C2) renewal of reach through contact with a differing closure. A practice can persist by handed-along agreement with no new evidence, so agreement among bearers is not an anchor. What crosses generations differs by kind: a method can be rebuilt where a recording cannot. Valence is relative to the residual, so no practice is intrinsically persistent. There are two modes, unmaintained survival and maintained arrival, and they must never be averaged. The conditions form a union with C1 upstream of C2. Which practice will change, and when, is a declared `gap`. Mode `general` on the laws, `specified` on the application, nothing `grounded`. The world pool corroborates the mechanism shape from low-overlap fields but conflicts internally on whether conformist transmission stabilizes traditions; that conflict changes no figure verdict.

- **cost if wrong:** misdirected diagnosis and a wasted expert's twenty minutes. A practice failing C2 would be given better communication channels, or a practice failing C1 would be told to import more novelties, and a single lever would be applied to a union that needs two. The avoidable part is spending an outside reader's independence by sending a recording instead of a generator (Fig 2 :42, "Buy independence in proportion to the cost of being wrong."). The cost is real but bounded and largely reversible.
- **undoable:** yes. No term is deleted if the claim fails; a refutation is added and the reach falls because something was added rather than struck out (Fig 6 :41-43). The shape and its five falsifiers are recorded with provenance so a later reader can locate the error (Fig 10 :62-64).
- **reversed by:** any of the five pre-registered falsifiers: (1) both `h²` and `S` high with rapid change, or both near zero with rapid change; (2) invariant valence across an instrument-verified residual change; (3) new atoms in a documented closed habitat with no import; (4) one intervention clearing both conditions with no categorically different residue; (5) a culture-level average predicting per-practice persistence as well as per-slot measures. A source with a genuinely different evidence pool showing that per-practice persistence is fully predicted by a single intrinsic trait would also reverse it. Agreement and prestige are not such evidence.
