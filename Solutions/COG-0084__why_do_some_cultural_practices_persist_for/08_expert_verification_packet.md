# Expert verification packet: the Tether on cultural persistence

**What this is:** a claim in a form a cultural-evolution researcher or anthropologist can check. Mode, derivation and refuters are stated before the conclusion, so a reader knows what to look for and what would settle it.

**Mode: `general` on the structural laws, `specified` on the application, not `grounded`.** No ethnographic measurement is claimed. This is a shape and a route, not a result.

**Status:** simulated derivation checked for internal consistency against the figures, not a ground reading. It cannot settle the claim it reports on.

## 1. The claim, in one paragraph

Persistence and rapid change are not two kinds of cultural practice. They are two regimes of one handoff, decided per practice-slot by two factors: the fidelity with which the bracket survives transfer to the next generation, and the pressure the ground puts on the receiving generation. Persistence requires the first to be nonzero and the second to be low; rapid change follows when pressure rises or fidelity falls. With either at zero the chain runs in place, so agreement can compound for generations without added evidence. A second condition sits alongside it: genuinely new atoms require contact with a differing closure. The two conditions form a union, and the specific practice that will persist is deliberately left open.

## 2. Three checkable sub-claims

### C1 - Framework-internal; checkable by reading Figure 7 and Figure 1

- **Statement:** the handoff has two factors, `h²` (bracket fidelity) and `S` (ground pressure), and persistence must be measured per slot, never as a culture-level average.
- **Source, verbatim:** Fig 7 :37 - *"Progress per handoff has two factors. Zero either and the chain runs in place:"* Fig 7 :38 - *"the bracket's fidelity across the handoff"* Fig 7 :39 - *"the ground's pressure on the room"*. Fig 7 :12-13 - *"same closure, handed along;"* *"agreement compounds, evidence does not"*. Fig 1 :19 - *"The residual is indexed by object slot, not aggregated."*
- **What to check:** are those the actual governing quantities in the figure text, and does the per-slot requirement follow rather than being imposed?
- **Refuter:** a practice with both `h²` and `S` measured high that changes rapidly, or with both near zero that changes; or a culture-level average that predicts per-practice persistence as well as per-slot measures on the same data.

### C2 - Field-facing; checkable by a cultural-evolution researcher against transmission data

- **Statement:** two practices with the same observable record can be kept by different sources: a maintained channel, unmaintained ground survival, or a mirror chain of handed-along agreement. The observation "still present after N generations" cannot discriminate among them.
- **Source, verbatim:** Fig 11 :56-59 - *"Unmaintained. No maintainer and no live frame in the seat. The habitat is pure contact, nothing selects, and what survives is what the ground kept."* and *"Maintained. A seat exists and can act: choose the instruments, distil the impactful actors, introduce what was not there."* Fig 7 :35 - *"A room's agreement is variation, not a verdict. Selection between rooms is where the ground votes."* Fig 11 :49 - *"An actor's valence is relative to the residual, not a property it carries."*
- **What to check:** can you name an observation, available in your own data, that returns a different reading for a maintained practice than for a handed-along one? If not, this is a metamerism result (one reading, several sources), not a mechanism claim.
- **Refuter:** a practice whose measured valence is invariant across an instrument-verified change of its habitat; or an instrument that distinguishes the three sources from the observable record alone, which would collapse the source-discrimination limit for that case.

### C3 - Framework-internal derivation with a field-facing consequence

- **Statement:** genuinely new atoms require contact with a differing closure; a closed population can rearrange held practices indefinitely without producing a new one. What crosses a generation also differs by kind: a method survives a habitat substitution that kills a recording.
- **Source, verbatim:** Fig 6 :23 - *"No frame escapes its own closure. The bound moves only on contact with a frame whose closure differs."* Fig 8 :32 - *"Closure is idempotent. Composing inside a closed set never leaves it, so no search, however long, adds an atom."* Fig 4 :22 - *"Only methods cross up. Only head starts come down."* Fig 4 :25 - *"A recording carried upward looks like knowledge and is a description of one occasion."*
- **What to check:** is there a documented case of a genuinely new practice arising in a closed habitat with no import? Does the method/recording distinction survive a habitat substitution in your data?
- **Refuter:** new atoms arising generation after generation in a documented closed habitat with no external contact; or a verbatim recording that remains reapplicable across every habitat change with no re-derivation by any bearer.

### Additional refuter on the union

- **Statement:** the two conditions are a union, not a partition (Fig 9 :55 - *"A bundled problem is usually a union of overlapping conditions, not a list of separate ones."*).
- **Refuter:** one intervention that clears both conditions with no categorically different residue left standing.

## 3. What is explicitly not claimed

- Not that "tradition" is a property inside a practice. Valence is relative to a residual (Fig 11 :49-51).
- Not that conformist transmission, niche construction, lock-in, or adaptive-cycle rigidity is *the* mechanism. These are secondary world sources; two of them conflict with a third on 2026-11-22 reading, and the figures name no mechanism.
- Not that a persistent practice is currently useful.
- Not a forecast of which practice will change or when. That is a declared `gap`: *"you get a kind of thing, never the answer itself"* (Fig 9 :32) and *"Unreachable is a verdict here, not a silence,"* (Fig 13 :134).
- Not a ground reading. No measurement was taken and no manuscript is offered for submission.
- Not a claim that the formula is identical to the figure set. The condensation is lossy and the round-trip gap `R_T` is enumerated in `11_audit.md`.

## 5. What would settle it

A per-slot instrument, not a further argument. An instrument that reads green *"cannot comment on the source"*; a spectrometer resolved that case (*The Hard Problem is About Source*, 2026). Here:

1. Measure `h²` and `S` separately per practice-slot.
2. Build an observation that discriminates maintained survival from handed-along agreement without assuming the answer.
3. Substitute the habitat (Fig 11 :70, "a test harness is a substituted habitat") and see whether the practice is rebuilt or replayed.
4. Document a closed habitat and check whether new atoms appear without import.
5. Apply one intervention at a time and record the residue.

## 6. What an expert can check in twenty minutes
