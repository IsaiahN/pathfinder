## The residual in effect terms

A climate frame predicts a regional trajectory. With well-mixed greenhouse-gas forcing alone it
gets the global mean roughly right and the regional pattern wrong. The live signal is the gap
between prediction and observation per region and per season. It is indexed by slot, not by a
global average. The question asks what a second forcing, short-lived and spatially patchy aerosol
pollution, does to that slot-indexed residual when it is composed with the long-lived, well-mixed
greenhouse forcing.

Restated in the framework's notation: `R = |Γ(b,a) − o′|` per slot `s`, with `Γ` the model's
library, `b` its forcing belief, `a` the emissions action, and `o′` the observed regional climate
(Fig 1 :19, Fig 1 :23; Symbols table). An answer to this question is a **shape** the interaction
must have, not a number and not a name for a mechanism.

## What an answer would look like (bound as an ordinary term)

Before the run I bind the sought-for shape as an ordinary term, per `THE_FORMULA.md` "Direction":
an answer is a claim that (i) keeps `R` indexed by region and season, (ii) refuses to treat aerosol
and greenhouse forcing as interchangeable symmetric ingredients, (iii) treats their combination as
a union compound with its own loop rather than an arithmetic sum, (iv) carries the lossy crossing
from particle microphysics to regional circulation with a measured round-trip gap `R_T`, and
(v) leaves the dominant-mechanism slot open until a source-discriminating instrument closes it.
This binding accrues a residual like anything else and is refusable by the criteria in `01` §3.

## What is deliberately not here

- No personal emails of named individuals.
- No ranking of people by how likely they are to agree.
- No bulk-send plan.
- No claim that the figures settle a radiative-forcing magnitude. They do not, and the ground
  does.

Three reasons, in the figures' own words:

1. **Agreement is not an anchor.** Fig 2 :35: *"Alignment is triangulation, not negotiation. A mirror cannot correct you."* Fig 2 :40: *"disqualifying for a verdict: agreement about the world is worthless when the agreeing frames share an evidence pool."* A climate-model ensemble whose members share aerosol parametrizations is one evidence pool wearing many names.
2. **Selecting for receptiveness manufactures the disqualifying correlation.** Fig 9 :60: *"Use filters for the budget and witnesses for the verdict. Never let a filter hand you a verdict."*
3. **Asking for trust before checking spends independence.** Fig 10 :42: *"A verifier that can reconstruct the claim cannot verify it."*

Legally: public institutional submission and opt-in channels avoid the GDPR/CAN-SPAM failure of
scraped personal addresses. One-to-one contact only; a reply is a conversation, never corroboration.
