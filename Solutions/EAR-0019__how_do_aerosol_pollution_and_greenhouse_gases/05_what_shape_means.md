## 1. A shape is a constraint, not a filler

The canonical usage rule says the framework returns *"what a missing thing would have to look like
(a shape, not a name)."* Fig 9 :32: *"you get a kind of thing, never the answer itself."* Fig 13 :134:
*"Unreachable is a verdict here, not a silence"*.

From *The Library of Babel*, opened this session: *"you can cut your search short if the set of books
returned is small and descriptive enough that it provides a general shape of the answer that
satisfies your question."*

So a **shape** is the narrowed form of an answer you can state before you can name it. It constrains;
it does not fill. For this question:

- the shape is: slot-indexed residual, non-symmetric operands, union compound, lossy cross-scale
  crossing, sign relative to the residual, open mechanism slot;
- a name would be: "sulfate aerosol dominates the South Asian monsoon response", or any number in
  W m⁻². Neither is given by the figures.

## 2. How a shape is obtained

*The Library of Babel*: use the vocabulary already held that is closest to the thing, look for
recurring new terms that overlap or correlate, and recurse. The essay describes a *"bootstrapping
convergence process"* in which *"With each new term gained, you narrow the space and deepen your
vocabulary."*

- Fig 6 :30: *"The description travels. Fails by being sharpened into terms only we can read. Cast it
  in effect terms."*
- Fig 9 :30: *"your words are borrowed too, so describe the effect"*; Fig 9 :31: *"rather than the
  cause"*.
- This is why the audit describes the interaction as a sign-opposed, slot-indexed coupling before it
  chooses a bond. Starting from "aerosol forcing" and "greenhouse forcing" and then looking for a
  frame to fit would be the reverse order.

## 3. What licenses a name

Two things, both outside the shape itself:

1. **The ground settles it.** `THE_FORMULA.md` step 5: the ground settles a term, verified only by
   what pays. The canonical README: the framework *"stops at the limit of what the available
   instruments can distinguish, and records the lineage so the stop is honest."*
2. **A provenanced import from a frame whose closure differs** (Fig 6 :23), with a source gate and a
   shadow test (Fig 6 :12, :13).

For this question, naming sulfate, black carbon, or a particular aerosol-cloud process as *the*
answer to the regional residual is a binding from the climate frame onto the shape. It is allowed
as a binding, but it must be recorded as such and it carries no figure authority. The canonical
phrase governs a missing primitive; here the relevant primitives are held, so the error would be
claiming the framework selected one, not merely mentioning one.

## 4. The ground and the source problem

*The Hard Problem is About Source*, opened this session, gives the mechanism. An instrument reads
green and *"cannot comment on the source"*. Several sources can produce the same reading. The essay
concludes: *"The answer is unreached until a better instrument exists."* It was the spectrometer,
not an argument, that resolved the case.

Applied here: a top-of-atmosphere shortwave flux anomaly is a reading. It does not by itself say
whether the source was a change in aerosol scattering, cloud droplet number, surface albedo, or a
combination. Several paths give the same reading. What is owed is a source-discriminating
instrument (spectral, polarimetric, in situ, or a controlled perturbation), not a name assigned
from the projection. This is the reason F6 records the dominant-mechanism slot as a `gap`.

Fig 10 :24: *"the ground does not decay, the channel does"*. A model ensemble can lose contact
through *"saturated metrics, pooled readings"* (Fig 10 :26) long before it notices that it has.

## 5. Answer to the shape question

**What shape must an answer to EAR-0019 have?** It must:

- keep the residual indexed by region and season (Fig 1 :19, :22);
- treat the two forcings as role-differentiated operands whose bond carries the coupling (Fig 12
  :5, :59);
- treat their combination as a union compound with its own loop, not a list (Fig 9 :55, :57);
- carry the microphysics-to-region transform with a measured round-trip gap `R_T` (Fig 4 :27, :30);
- recognise that an actor's sign is relative to the named residual (Fig 11 :49, :50);
- leave the dominant-mechanism slot open until an instrument closes it (Fig 9 :32).

**Does the common "net forcing = aerosol + greenhouse" reading commit a figure error?** Yes, but the
error is aggregation and notation, not a figure conflict. It aggregates slots (Fig 1 :22) and uses a
flat formula (Fig 12 :5). Re-census resolves the apparent strain: a forcing budget is a
frame-internal accounting quantity (Fig 1 :27: *"They are frame-internal. A frame cannot score
itself with a quantity it also produces."*), while Fig 12 governs typed composition. Different
objects.

## 6. Consequences for contact

**A simulation, not a ground reading.** LLM output is *"simulacra"*; the testable output would have
to be derived from the real architecture or by working the formula yourself. This audit is a
simulated derivation checked for internal consistency against the figures. It is not a measurement
of any regional climate response and must be labelled as such. It cannot settle the question it
reports on.

**Do not ask for trust.** Fig 10 :42: *"A verifier that can reconstruct the claim cannot verify
it."* The first contact carries the residual, the shape and the refuter. The framework, if offered
at all, comes only after the recipient has recorded a verdict.

**Prestige is not action, and neither is a large ensemble.** Fig 2 :40 disqualifies agreement from a
shared evidence pool; Fig 7 :13 says *"agreement compounds, evidence does not"*. The legitimate ask
is for a source-discriminating check, not for endorsement.
