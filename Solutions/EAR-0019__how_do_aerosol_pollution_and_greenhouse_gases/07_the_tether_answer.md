# The Tether's answer: aerosol pollution and greenhouse gases on regional climate

**Mode: `specified` for the applied shape, `general` for the framework-internal laws, `gap` for the
dominant mechanism and all magnitudes. Not `grounded`.** Simulated derivation checked for internal
consistency against the figures (`05_what_shape_means.md` §6), not a ground reading.

## 1. The residual it answers

A climate model with greenhouse forcing alone gets the global mean approximately right and leaves a
live residual in particular regions and seasons. The question is what a second, short-lived and
spatially patchy forcing does to that slot-indexed residual when it is composed with the first. This
is a **union compound**, not one hard problem: the two conditions overlap through clouds,
atmospheric stability and the interhemispheric gradient, and the compound has its own loop and its
own ground. Fig 9 :57: *"a case in two conditions is not a harder case of either"*.

## 2. The shape

1. **Keep the residual per region and per season.** Fig 1 :19: *"The residual is indexed by object
   slot, not aggregated."* Fig 1 :13: *"a global R near zero with one live slot residual is a legal
   state"*. A global forcing budget that matches observation while a monsoon region drifts is the
   legal state, not a contradiction. Aggregating is how the signal disappears (Fig 1 :22).
2. **The two operands are not interchangeable.** They fail the symmetric-bond test (Fig 12 :59:
   *"swap the operands. Does the meaning change? No."*) and pass the ordered one (Fig 12 :74:
   *"swap them. Does it become a different thing? Yes."*). Their identity includes the state each is
   bonded to: Fig 13 :76: *"A gradient over space and one over time are different terms, because
   which state it is bonded to is part of what it is."* So the coupling is carried by a typed
   relation, not by a flat sum.
3. **The combination is a union compound, not an arithmetic sum.** Fig 9 :55: *"A bundled problem is
   usually a union of overlapping conditions, not a list of separate ones."* Fig 12 :5: *"A set of
   ingredients is not one. The bond is the rest of the information, and it is the half nothing was
   recording."* The bond is the aerosol-cloud interaction, the stability change and the gradient
   that moves the ITCZ.
4. **The cross-scale transform is lossy.** Fig 4 :27: *"The transform between scales loses
   information, and that is the point."* Fig 4 :30: *"So take the round trip and measure the gap.
   Without it you are navigating by dead reckoning: confident about a position nobody checked."*
   Any parameter-to-region claim carries a measured `R_T`.
5. **Actor sign is relative to the residual.** Fig 11 :49: *"An actor's valence is relative to the
   residual, not a property it carries."* Fig 11 :50: *"The same actor is a benefactor to one
   residual and a malfactor to another"*. Sulfate cools a warming residual and harms a monsoon or
   health residual. There is no standing list of good or bad species.
6. **The mechanism slot stays open.** Fig 9 :32: *"you get a kind of thing, never the answer
   itself."* Which species or process dominates a named regional residual is a `gap`, not a figure
   verdict. Fig 8 :39: *"So a search that finds nothing is never a proof of absence."*
7. **The anchor is measurement, not agreement.** Fig 2 :29: *"An anchor is whatever settles the
   question and does not change its answer because of what any frame thinks."* Fig 2 :40:
   *"disqualifying for a verdict: agreement about the world is worthless when the agreeing frames
   share an evidence pool"*. Fig 7 :13: *"agreement compounds, evidence does not"*.

## 3. What it explicitly does not say

- Not a value for aerosol effective radiative forcing, and not a ranking of sulfate, black carbon
  or organic carbon. Those are ground questions.
- Not that the figures settle the sign or magnitude of any regional temperature or precipitation
  response. They do not.
- Not that ⋛ is the first-order bond between the two forcings. That reading was retracted in
  `01` §5 and `06` Q1; it is kept only as a single-source observation about sign-opposed
  competition.
- Not that emission policy can subtract committed warming. Fig 11 :61: *"The seat's power is
  asymmetric. Introduction is available; exclusion is not."*
- Not that a multi-model consensus corroborates the shape. Shared parametrisations are one
  evidence pool.
- Not `grounded`. No measurement was taken.

## 4. What would settle it, the ground

A source-discriminating instrument, not a further argument. The framework essay *The Hard Problem
is About Source* (opened this session) states: *"The answer is unreached until a better instrument
exists."* Here the corresponding tests are:

- **Slot test:** does a slot-indexed regional residual persist while the global mean is near zero?
  Check against station data and reanalysis before invoking any framework claim.
- **Swap test:** do two forcing histories with the same final totals but opposite order produce
  different regional trajectories? If not, the ordered bond is wrong.
- **Sum test:** does the combined regional response equal the sum of the separate responses in
  every slot, with no interaction term? If yes, the union compound claim is wrong.
- **Round-trip test:** measure `R_T` for a microphysics-to-region parameterisation against
  independent datasets. A zero gap would break F4.
- **Source test:** can spectral, polarimetric or in-situ observations discriminate among aerosol
  scattering, cloud-droplet number and surface-albedo contributions to a TOA shortwave anomaly?
  This is what would name the mechanism slot.
- **Threshold test:** is there a threshold in interhemispheric aerosol optical depth difference for
  ITCZ displacement, and at what value? The number comes from the ground, not from Fig ⋛.

## 5. The honest stop

**Unreached, not unreachable.** Fig 9 :32 gives a kind of thing, never the answer itself. Fig 6 :34:
*"the search for a proto-instrument is a search for one that is already there."* There are already
instruments returning partial readings, satellites, AOD networks, station rainfall, reanalysis, so
the stop is about what they can currently discriminate, not about an impossible sensor. The lineage
is recorded so the stop is checkable. A ground reading, not agreement, would move any part of this
from `candidate` to settled.

## 6. One-paragraph version for outreach

> The Tether does not give a number for how aerosol pollution and greenhouse gases affect regional
> climate. It gives the shape any answer must have. The residual is indexed by region and season,
> not by a global average, so a matching global mean can coexist with a live monsoon residual. The
> two forcings are not interchangeable ingredients that can be added under a flat plus: their
> identity includes the timescale and the state each is bonded to, and their coupling lives in
> clouds, stability and the interhemispheric gradient. Their combination is a union compound with
> its own loop, the crossing from microphysics to region is lossy and must carry a measured round
> trip gap, and an actor's sign depends on which residual you name. The framework stops before
> naming the dominant species or giving a magnitude, because it returns a shape, not a name. What
> it offers is that shape, four refuters written in advance, and the instruments that would close
> the open slot.
