## QUESTION

Decide, in effect terms, what the Tether says about the interaction of aerosol pollution and
greenhouse gases on regional climate. Option A: accept the common reading that their forcings are
additive ingredients in one global budget and that this budget answers the regional question.
Option B: keep the figure-derived shape and refuse the aggregation and the flat bond. What each
leads to: A sends a frame-internal accounting quantity where a slot-indexed ground measurement is
owed, treats non-interchangeable operands as symmetric, and spends the independence of the people
checking it; B sends a checkable shape, written refuters, and an open mechanism slot until a
source-discriminating instrument closes it.

## DOMAIN CENSUS

R by arity, symmetry and scale, then one line per domain with actors, currency and anchor;
partition or union; order by dependency.

- **R (the residual).** The gap between predicted and observed regional climate with greenhouse
  forcing alone, indexed by region and season. Arity: at least three indices (agent, slot, time).
  Symmetry: non-commutative, because the two agents have different lifetimes and spatial patterns.
  Scale: microphysical to regional to global, with lossy crossings. Fig 9 names this a union
  compound, not one hard question: a case in two conditions is not a harder case of either.
- **D1 Radiative forcing frame.** Actors: sulfate, black carbon, organic carbon, CO₂, CH₄, clouds.
  Currency: signed W m⁻² effective radiative forcing. Anchor: top-of-atmosphere flux measured by
  satellite and aircraft. Admitted.
- **D2 Regional response frame.** Actors: land, ocean, monsoon circulation, ITCZ, snow and ice.
  Currency: temperature, precipitation, circulation anomaly. Anchor: station data, reanalysis and
  observed rainfall. Admitted.
- **D3 Emissions and policy habitat.** Actors: emitters, clean-air regulation, geoengineering
  proposals. Currency: emission trajectories, aerosol optical depth difference. Anchor: emissions
  inventories and AOD records. Admitted.
- **D4 Model closure frame.** Actors: parametrizations, Γ, candidate terms, instruments. Currency:
  `R` per slot, description length `|φ|`, `R_T`. Anchor: the ground, against which the model is
  settled. Admitted.
- **X1 The global offset as one scalar.** Actors: none nameable at regional scale. Anchor: none.
  Not a domain yet until the slot is named.
- **Union, not partition.** D1 and D2 overlap through clouds and stability; D3 acts on both; D4
  contains all of them. Dependency order: perceive the slot residual, then vocabulary, then
  objective, then plan, then carry (Fig 3 :20).

Strain test: Fig 12's ordered-bond rule governs typed composition; a forcing budget is a
frame-internal accounting convention, which Fig 1 :27 disqualifies as a ground metric. Different
objects, so no strain after re-census. The single-source ⋛ role was re-census-refused and is not
verdict-bearing.

## WORLD (secondary, each source read; field, author/venue/year, quote, prediction, corroborates|conflicts)

Climate sources below form largely one evidence pool (the same assessment literature and the same
CMIP generation), so they are counted as one witness for the shape, not several.

- **Climate assessment, global budget.** IPCC, Summary for Policymakers, AR6 WGI, 2021. Quote:
  "increases in monsoon precipitation due to warming from GHG emissions were counteracted by
  decreases in monsoon precipitation due to cooling from human-caused aerosol emissions over the
  20th century". Prediction: the regional response is a sign-opposed coupling, not a sum.
  **Corroborates** F2 and F3. Overlap: high with the other climate sources.
- **Aerosol physics, forcing bounds.** N. Bellouin et al., Reviews of Geophysics 58(1), 2020.
  Quote: "Tropospheric aerosols, the aerosols of main concern here, remain in the atmosphere for
  days to weeks". Prediction: short lifetime and heterogeneous sources make aerosols spatially
  nonuniform, unlike well-mixed greenhouse gases. **Corroborates** F2. Overlap: high with the
  climate pool; independent of the framework essays.
- **Aerosol modelling, spatial pattern.** A. Kalisoras et al., Atmospheric Chemistry and Physics
  24, 2024. Quote: "sulfates dominate ERF spatial patterns, exerting a strongly negative ERF
  especially over industrialized regions of the Northern Hemisphere". Prediction: region and
  pattern matter, not just the global scalar. **Corroborates** F1 and F3. Overlap: high with the
  climate pool.
- **Climate dynamics, monsoon response.** P.-A. Monerie, L. J. Wilcox and A. G. Turner, Journal of
  Climate 35(8), 2022. Quote: "the drying trends are likely to be mainly due to increased AA
  emissions, which cause shifts of the atmospheric circulation and a decrease in moisture
  advection." Prediction: aerosols act through circulation and moisture, greenhouse gases through
  moisture advection; the operands play different roles. **Corroborates** F2 and F3. Overlap: high
  with the climate pool.
- **Earth system boundaries, threshold.** J. Rockström et al., Nature 619, 2023. Quote: "a rising

## VERDICT (from the figures)

The shape survives; the aggregation and the flat bond do not. The residual is indexed by region and
season, the two forcings are non-interchangeable operands joined by a typed relation that carries
the coupling, their combination is a union compound, the cross-scale transform has a measurable
round-trip gap, actor sign is relative to the residual, and the anchor is measurement rather than
pooled model agreement. The dominant regional mechanism and every numerical magnitude are a
declared `gap`. The first-order ⋛ reading was re-census-refused and is not verdict-bearing. Mode
`specified` for the application, `general` for the framework laws, never `grounded`. The world
sources corroborate the shape but do not decide it; the figures do.

- **cost if wrong:** bounded and locatable. The packet is mode-labelled, states its refuters before
  the conclusion and records its lineage, so an error is findable rather than invisible. The real
  cost is spending the independence of outside readers by sending a settled-sounding number or a
  consensus claim instead of a shape with refuters. Fig 2 :42 "Buy independence in proportion to the
  cost of being wrong." That cost is avoidable by leading with the slot claim and its refuters.
- **undoable:** yes. No finding deletes a term; a refutation adds one and the reach falls because
  something was added (Fig 6). The affected finding is withdrawn and the shape re-derived, with the
  earlier reading preserved in `01` §5. A sent packet can be superseded.
- **reversed by:** a ground reading, not agreement. A slot test showing global forcing predicts all
  regional trajectories; a swap test giving identical histories; a sum test with no interaction
  term; a zero-gap round trip on independent datasets; a source-discriminating instrument naming the
  mechanism; or a genuine figure re-census that admits ⋛ at first order. Until one of these fires,
  the verdict stands as `candidate`.
