# Outreach draft - check the shape before deciding about the framework

**Use:** one-to-one, or as a cover note attached to a submission through the public venues in
`03_public_contact_points.csv`. **Not** for bulk send.

**Hard precondition:** do not send until the audit findings F1 to F5 are repaired in the text you
actually circulate. The offer collapses on contact if the shape claim does not survive its own
refuter.

---

**Subject:** One checkable claim about regional aerosol and greenhouse forcing, before any theory

> Hi [name],
>
> I have one specific output I would like you to check against what you already know. I am not
> asking you to read a framework first, and I am not asking you to agree with anything. If the
> output does not survive, there is nothing else to look at.
>
> **The output (`specified`, not `grounded`).** Aerosol pollution and greenhouse gases cannot be
> treated as two interchangeable ingredients added under a single global W m⁻² budget when the
> question is regional climate. Three things follow from the composition rule:
>
> 1. The quantity that matters is the gap between prediction and observation **per region and per
>    season**, not the global mean. A global mean that matches observation while a monsoon region
>    drifts is a legal state, not a contradiction.
> 2. The two forcings are not symmetric operands. Swapping a short-lived, patchy scattering aerosol
>    for a long-lived, well-mixed gas changes the regional outcome and the timescale. So their
>    coupling is carried by a typed relation, not by a flat sum.
> 3. The combination is a **union compound**, not a list of two independent effects. The coupling
>    lives in clouds, atmospheric stability and the interhemispheric gradient that moves the ITCZ.
>
> This is a prediction of a **shape**, not a measurement and not a number. It deliberately does not
> say which aerosol species or process dominates in a given region. That slot stays open until a
> source-discriminating instrument closes it.
>
> **What I am not claiming:** not a value for aerosol effective radiative forcing, not that the
> figures can rank sulfate against black carbon, not that a model ensemble agrees and therefore the
> answer is settled, and not that clean-air policy can subtract warming that is already committed.
>
> **What would refute the shape claim:**
> 1. a case where global-mean forcing predicts every regional and seasonal trajectory with no live
>    slot residual left;
> 2. a case where swapping the two forcing histories yields the same regional trajectories at every
>    slot;
> 3. a case where the combined regional response equals the sum of the separate responses in every
>    slot, with no interaction term;
> 4. a microphysics-to-region map that round-trips with zero measured gap on independent datasets.
>
> I wrote these down before sending this. If any one holds, the corresponding part is wrong and I
> will retract it. A one-line "this fails because..." is more useful to me than agreement. If you
> would rather not engage, no reply is needed.
>
> **If it survives, and only then:** the sources are below. The figures are the authority, not my
> summary of them.
> - Canonical figures text with line numbers (local extract): see the folder's `10_source_ledger.csv`
>   for the exact path.
> - IPCC AR6 WGI Summary for Policymakers, section A.3.3: https://www.ipcc.ch/report/ar6/wg1/chapter/summary-for-policymakers
> - Bellouin et al. 2020, Reviews of Geophysics: https://pmc.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/articles/PMC7384191
> - Monerie, Wilcox and Turner 2022, Journal of Climate: https://journals.ametsoc.org/view/journals/clim/35/8/JCLI-D-21-0412.1.xml
>
> **If you want to use the method:** the eight-step formula is public. Run it on a problem you know
> well and can check against a ground. Write down what would refute your result *before* you run it,
> and label the mode of what you get. A reported failure is more useful to me than a reported
> success.
>
> - [name]

## The two-stage offer

1. **Stage 1.** Send the shape claim and its four refuters. Ask what they would count as a failure.
   Record their verdict **before** showing any framework material.
2. **Stage 2, opt-in only.** If they are curious, offer the links. The figures, not this summary,
   are the authority. Their later agreement is never pooled as corroboration.
3. **Stage 3.** Offer the formula as a method to reuse, with the instruction that each person writes
   their own refuter and states their mode.

A reply is a conversation, never corroboration. One-to-one only. No bulk send.
