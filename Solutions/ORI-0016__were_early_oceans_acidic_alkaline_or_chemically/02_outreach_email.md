# Outreach draft: check one output before deciding whether the framing is worth reading

**Use:** one to one, or as a cover letter attached to a submission through the public venues in `03_public_contact_points.csv`. **Not** for bulk send.

**Hard precondition:** do not send until the five refuters in `01_figure_grounded_audit.md` section 4 are stated in the recipient's own terms. The output must survive a check or the two-stage offer collapses on contact.

**Subject:** One testable claim about early ocean pH, checked before any theory

> Hi [name],
>
> I work on a figure-grounded method for taking hard questions apart. I have one output about early ocean chemistry I would like you to check against what you already know. I am not asking you to read the method first, and I am not asking you to agree. If the output does not survive, there is nothing else to look at.
>
> **The output (`specified` for the shape, `gap` for the numbers):** the question "were early oceans acidic, alkaline, or varied from place to place?" is not a three-way choice. The readings it asks about are indexed by slot (basin, depth, setting, time window). The figure rule I am testing says a residual is indexed by object slot and never aggregated. So "varied from place to place" is not a third label beside acidic and alkaline; it is the form the other two presuppose. The bulk acidic or alkaline values are abstractions across slots, and the gap between the abstraction and the slots is measurable, not zero.
>
> **What I am not claiming:** that early oceans were globally acidic, that they were globally alkaline at pH 10, that any published number is the ground, or that one setting (vents, ponds, or anything else) was the cause. Those are frame-bound results with provenance, and I keep them as slot values, not as verdicts. I also do not claim this method settles the pH question. It returns a shape, not a number.
>
> **What would refute the shape claim, written down before this message:**
> 1. one pH value predicts every slot's speciation within instrument error;
> 2. one mechanism under one condition accounts for all settings and leaves no categorically different residue;
> 3. a source-discriminating measurement of bulk Archean seawater gives one unambiguous value and shows local variation is below detection;
> 4. a re-census admits a global-pH primitive or derives one of the six substrate terms from the other five;
> 5. a measured round-trip gap of zero between the bulk abstraction and slot readings.
>
> I wrote these before contacting you. If any holds, I retract the shape claim. A one-line "this fails because..." is more useful to me than agreement. If you would rather not engage, no reply is needed.
>
> **If it survives, and only then:** the sources are below. The figures, not my summary of them, are the authority.
> - Verbatim figure text with line numbers (local): `FRAMEWORK/reference/03-FIGURES_TEXT.md`
> - Canonical figures README: https://raw.githubusercontent.com/IsaiahN/tether/general-agent/figures/README.md
> - Eight-step formula (local): `FRAMEWORK/figures/THE_FORMULA.md`
>
> **If you want to use the machinery:** run it on a problem you know well and can check against a ground. Write down what would refute your result before you run it, and state the mode of what you get. A reported failure is more useful to me than a reported success.
>
> Thanks,
> [name]

## Stage order

1. **Stage 1.** Send the per-slot output and the five refuters. Ask what the recipient would count as a failure. Record the verdict before any method is shown.
2. **Stage 2, opt in only.** If curious, offer the three links. Tell them the figures are the authority.
3. **Stage 3.** Offer the formula as a method to reuse, with the instruction that each user writes their own refuter and states their own mode.

Stage 1 before stage 2 is Fig 10 :52 and Fig 2 :45 ("Frames range against the ground. They do not negotiate with it."). Stage 2 is opt in by construction because Seat 4 must not hold the interiors; once a recipient has absorbed the method, their agreement stops being evidence (Fig 2 :40). Stage 3 sends a method, which is permitted by Fig 4 :22, but it still needs a ground per problem.
