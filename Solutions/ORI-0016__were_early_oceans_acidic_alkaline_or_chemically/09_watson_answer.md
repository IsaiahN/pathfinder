## QUESTION

## DOMAIN CENSUS

R by arity, symmetry and scale, then one line per domain with actors, currency and anchor; partition or union; order by dependency.

- **R, the residual.** Arity: the question bundles at least three settings and two time windows, so R has the arity of a union, not a scalar. Symmetry: pH is an ordering and acidic versus alkaline is a threshold comparison, not a symmetric join. Scale: molecular speciation, vent chimney, basin, globe. The transform between these scales loses information.
- **D1, bulk ocean and atmosphere frame.** Actors: pCO2, silicate and seafloor weathering, carbonate burial, dissolved inorganic carbon. Currency: alkalinity and bulk pH. Anchor: paleosols, carbonates, banded iron formations and isotopes, which do not update on the models.
- **D2, local setting and habitat frame.** Actors: hydrothermal fluids, serpentinization, vent chimneys, pore waters, shallow ponds, impact ejecta. Currency: local pH, proton motive force, redox disequilibrium. Anchor: direct fluid measurement and mineral assemblages.
- **D3, archive and instrument frame.** Actors: boron and oxygen isotopes, carbonate rarity, greenalite, zircons. Currency: contact with a past state. Anchor: the rock, which does not update.
- **D4, question frame.** Actors: the three offered labels. Currency: class labels. Anchor: none until the third option is bound to a per-slot distribution; after binding it is the union of D1 to D3.
- **X1, a single global pH for all early oceans.** No nameable anchor and no instrument returning a global average of a 4 Ga ocean. Not a domain; this is the aggregation error.
- **Partition or union.** Union, because D1 and D2 overlap in the carbon cycle and D3 reads both. A single lever clears the region where one condition holds and leaves the intersections standing.
- **Order by dependency.** D3 archive first, then vocabulary for what a slot affords, then the objective, then D1 and D2, then the label. Effort spent above a broken archive link arrives at a step that cannot use it.

Strain test applied before conclusions: a per-slot residual and global pH curves strain only if both are read as claims about the same quantity. Re-census: the curve is an abstraction across slots, up a scale, so the loss is licensed and the round-trip gap is owed. No hidden strain after re-census. The live alkaline versus weakly acidic conflict is a world-source conflict inside one evidence pool, not a figure strain.

## VERDICT (from the figures)

The shape survives; the aggregation does not. The answer must be indexed by slot, with bulk acidic and alkaline values kept as lossy abstractions inside a per-slot distribution, settings kept as a union, and pH gradients treated as spendable structures. The numeric pH of a named slot is a gap, and the round-trip gap between the global curve and the slots is nonzero. This is a `specified`, ungrounded, candidate result from a simulated derivation. The strongest outreach point is the shape plus the five refuters, not a pH number.

- **cost if wrong:** bounded and locatable. Mode, five pre-registered refuters and provenance are stated before the conclusion, so an error is findable rather than invisible. The real cost is spending the independence of outside readers by sending a recording instead of a generator. That cost is priced by Fig 2 :42 "Buy independence in proportion to the cost of being wrong." It is avoidable by leading with the shape claim and the refuter.
- **undoable:** yes. The shape claim can be withdrawn or narrowed without withdrawing any primitive. Files 07 and 08 already remove the failed readings. A claim already sent can be superseded by a corrected packet, and no term has to be deleted from the record.
- **reversed by:** a ground reading, not agreement. A source-discriminating measurement of bulk Archean seawater with local variation below detection; a measured round-trip gap of zero at the resolution in question; a figure re-census that admits a global-pH primitive or derives one of the six substrate terms from the other five; or a provenanced import with a shadow test that predicts the observed slot distribution and narrows the envelope of settings. Prestige, repeated models and selected lists of agreeable readers are not such instruments.
