## 1. The sought-for shape, bound before the run

The question offers three answers: acidic, alkaline, or chemically varied from place to place. File `README.md` bound three candidate shapes before any conclusion:

1. one global class label for all early oceans;
2. a per-slot distribution of classes, indexed by basin, depth, setting and time window;
3. a `gap`, where the framework returns the kind of thing an answer must be rather than the number.

The run selects shape 2 as the figure-licensed form and keeps shape 3 attached to the numeric content. Shape 1 is refused as an aggregation error. A shape here is therefore the narrowed form of an answer that can be stated before it can be filled. It constrains; it does not supply values.

## 2. What the figures say a shape is

Fig 9 :32: "you get a kind of thing, never the answer itself"

Fig 13 :133: "A system with a closure can answer that before it searches, and what it returns is a shape rather than a name."

Fig 13 :134: "Unreachable is a verdict here, not a silence,"

The canonical README states the limit as what a missing thing would have to look like, a shape and not a name. Fig 9 :42 adds "narrowing gets you close, the ground decides". So a shape is a constraint derived from what is already held, plus an honest record of what is still owed from the ground. It is not a guess dressed as a result.

## 3. The shape this question returns

Four constraints survive the figure census in file 01:

1. **Index by slot.** Fig 1 :19: "The residual is indexed by object slot, not aggregated." The readings in the opened literature are already slot indexed: a vent fluid, a mineral assemblage in one formation, a paleosol at one horizon, a modeled global curve. Asking for one label over all of them is the named failure.
2. **Treat the options as a union.** Fig 9 :55: "A bundled problem is usually a union of overlapping conditions, not a list of separate ones." Acidic and alkaline are bulk-frame labels. Varied is a statement about slot structure that remains after the bulk conditions are accounted for. It is not a third label on the same scale.
3. **Keep gradients as structures.** Fig 13 :22: "a difference that can be spent" . A pH difference across a vent wall or a pore-water boundary is not merely a distribution. It has directedness and can do chemical work. Altair et al. 2025 report that pH separation across vent walls could have been the forerunner to pH separation across cell membranes, which is this structure in a low-overlap field.
4. **Report the pooled curve as an abstraction with a measured gap.** Fig 10 :26: "saturated metrics, pooled readings" . Fig 4 :27: "The transform between scales loses information, and that is the point." The global pH curve is a coarse description of slots, not the ground. Fig 4 :30 requires the round trip: "So take the round trip and measure the gap. Without it you are navigating by dead reckoning: confident about a position nobody checked."

The resulting answer form is: a slot-indexed distribution of pH classes and gradients, with the bulk acidic or alkaline numbers retained as abstractions inside it, and the numeric value of any named slot marked `gap` until a source-discriminating instrument settles it.

## 4. What licenses a number inside the shape

Two things, both outside the shape itself:

1. **The ground settles it.** Formula step 5 says the ground settles a term and that a gate is not the ground. A measured pH from a rock or a fluid is a ground contact, provided the channel is reachable and the reading is not pooled.
2. **A provenanced import from a frame whose closure differs.** Fig 6 :23: "No frame escapes its own closure. The bound moves only on contact with a frame whose closure differs." Fig 8 :50: "Convergent derivation and adopted import look identical in the contents." A published pH value is an import. It needs a source gate, a shadow test and a debit against independence before it can be used as more than a slot value.

The bulk model papers here all lean on the same sparse proxy families, so their agreement is one pool talking. A number from one of them is not promoted to the ground by being repeated.

## 5. Correction to an earlier reading in this folder

An earlier reading, recorded in file 01 section 5, said that the framework forbids naming any pH number. That overstates the canonical limit. The README phrase governs a **missing** primitive, one not held in the library. A measured pH is not a missing primitive. Naming one is permitted as a provenanced import with a source gate and a shadow test, which is why file 01 keeps Guo and Korenaga 2025 and Ohmoto and Ferry 2026 as slot values rather than banning them. Both the original overstatement and this narrowing are kept visible; neither is silently erased.

The metamerism point is not retracted. When a frame reads a proxy and names the source of that reading without an instrument that discriminates among sources, it makes the error described in *The Hard Problem is About Source*. What is owed is the discriminator, not a refusal to name held quantities.

## 6. The derivation is a simulation, not a ground reading

*The Recursive Self-Improvement Trap*, opened for this folder on 2026-11-23, says LLM output can simulate an approximate answer but "the truly testable output would need to be derived from the real architecture or working the formula yourself." Every file in this package is therefore a simulated derivation checked for internal consistency against the figures. It cannot settle the question it reports on. It is labelled `specified` for the shape and `gap` for the number, never `grounded`.

## 7. Why this matters for outreach

Fig 10 :52: "Bring a characterised residual, never the source." The first contact carries the shape claim and the refuter, not the framework interiors. A recipient who has already absorbed the method has joined the frame being checked, so their later agreement cannot be counted as evidence (Fig 2 :40). Prestige is not a substitute for an instrument: the trap essay says prestige can fund an action and cannot settle one, and only the ground can settle it.

## 8. One-sentence answer to the heading question

For this question, shape means the per-slot distributional form of the answer, with bulk acidic and alkaline values kept as lossy abstractions inside it and with the actual numeric pH of any named slot left as an honest `gap` until a source-discriminating measurement closes it.
