## What an answer would look like (bind the sought-for shape as an ordinary term)

Before the run, the sought-for answer shape is written down so it can accrue a residual like any other term (Formula, "Direction"). Three candidate shapes were bound:

1. A single class label for all early oceans (acidic, alkaline, or neutral), predicted if the residual can be aggregated into one global slot.
2. A per-slot distribution of classes (open ocean, vent interface, pore water, shallow pond, by time window), predicted if the residual is indexed by object slot.
3. A `gap`: the framework returns the kind of thing an answer must be, not the number, because no instrument held by this derivation discriminates the source of the proxy readings.

The run selects shape 2 as the figure-licensed form, with shape 3 attached to the numeric content. Shape 1 is refused as an aggregation error.

## What is deliberately not here

- No personal emails of named individuals.
- No ranking of people by how likely they are to agree with the verdict.
- No bulk-send plan.

Three reasons, in the figures' own words:

1. **A room does not upgrade the label.** Fig 7 :13: "agreement compounds, evidence does not". Adding readers raises the vocabulary and does not add an anchor.
2. **Selecting for receptiveness manufactures the disqualifying correlation.** Seat Map :163: "High correlation is disqualifying for a verdict and efficient for a convention." A list chosen for likely agreement is a list chosen to agree.
3. **Asking for trust before a check spends the reader's independence.** Fig 10 :42: "A verifier that can reconstruct the claim cannot verify it." The first contact carries the residual and its refuter, never the framework's interiors.

Legally: scraping personal emails for unsolicited bulk outreach breaches GDPR and CAN-SPAM style rules in many jurisdictions. Public opt-in channels avoid that practical failure as well as the structural one.
