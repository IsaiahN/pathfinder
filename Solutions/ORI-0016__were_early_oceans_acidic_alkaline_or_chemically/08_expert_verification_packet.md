**Mode: `specified` for the shape, `general` for the per-slot rule, `gap` for the numeric pH. Not `grounded`.** No measurement is claimed. This is a shape and a route, not a result, and it is a simulated derivation checked for internal consistency against the figures rather than a ground reading.

## 1. The claim in one paragraph

The question "were early oceans acidic, alkaline, or chemically varied from place to place?" is not a three-way choice. The readings it asks about are indexed by slot, that is by basin, depth, setting and time window. The figure rule says a residual is indexed by object slot and never aggregated, so varied is not a third label beside acidic and alkaline; it is the form the other two presuppose. Bulk acidic or alkaline values are abstractions across slots, and the gap between the abstraction and the slots is measurable and nonzero. Numeric pH values are kept as provenanced slot values, not promoted to the ground.
