## 4. Wider literature that bears on the claim

These were not all used as ledger sources, but each was checked for relevance while locating the six above.

- Krissansen-Totton, Arney and Catling 2018, PNAS: https://www.pnas.org/doi/10.1073/pnas.1721296115 . A geological carbon cycle model returning circumneutral Precambrian pH. It is the source of the curve in Hao et al. Fig 2a, so it is the same evidence pool, not a second witness.
- Halevy and Bachan 2017, Science: https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/28280204 . Early Archean pH between about 6.5 and 7.0. Also inside the Hao et al. Fig 2a pool.
- Kadoya, Krissansen-Totton and Catling 2020, Geochemistry Geophysics Geosystems: https://faculty.washington.edu/dcatling/Kadoya2020_Cold_Moderate_pCO2_Hadean.pdf . Impact ejecta weathering giving neutral to weakly alkaline Hadean seawater. A different mechanism in the same bulk-model family.
- Lin and Catling 2024, American Journal of Science: https://ajsonline.org/article/118215-the-rare-earth-element-distribution-in-marine-carbonates-as-a-potential-proxy-for-seawater-ph-on-early-earth.pdf . A proxy calibration that states published ideas span about 7 pH units. Useful for the gap claim, not for a global number.
- PNAS 2020, CO2 reduction driven by a pH gradient: https://www.pnas.org/doi/10.1073/pnas.2002659117 . Independent electrochemical work on whether a vent gradient can drive the first energy bottleneck. Low overlap with the carbon-cycle modellers.
- PNAS 2026, Rethinking the origin of life at seafloor hydrothermal vents: https://www.pnas.org/doi/10.1073/pnas.2613108123 . Argues that ancient serpentinizing systems would not have held strong pH gradients at hydrothermal temperatures. A genuine refuter of the simple vent-gradient story, recorded so the claim does not look safer than it is.

## 5. Distribution rules

Figure-grounded reasons for offering rather than pushing:

- Fig 4 :22: "Only methods cross up. Only head starts come down." Sending the whole framework before anyone has stated a residual sends a recording, not a generator. Lead with the per-slot claim and its five refuters.
- Fig 10 :42: "A verifier that can reconstruct the claim cannot verify it." A recipient who has absorbed the interiors has joined the frame being checked.
- Fig 9 :27-28: "describe it before you go looking, or any frame you pick will seem to fit." Ask what the recipient would expect to see before offering the theory.
- Fig 2 :40: "disqualifying for a verdict: agreement about the world is worthless when the agreeing frames share an evidence pool" . Later agreement from a recipient who has read the method is not corroboration.
- Seat Map :163: "High correlation is disqualifying for a verdict and efficient for a convention." A list chosen for likely agreement produces a convention, not a check. This is why file 03 has no ranking by receptiveness and no personal addresses.

Recommended order in a reply:

1. The shape claim and the five refuters from file 01 section 4.
2. Ask what the recipient would count as a failure. Record that verdict before any method is shown.
3. Only then, and only if asked, offer the three framework links, with the note that the figures and not the summary are the authority.

## 6. The two-stage offer

Stage 1 is check the output against the ground. Stage 2 is opt in, read the theory that produced it. Stage 3 is reuse the formula on a problem the reader knows and can check. This is Fig 10 :52, "Bring a characterised residual, never the source," and Fig 2 :45, "Frames range against the ground. They do not negotiate with it."

**Hard precondition:** stages 1 to 3 only work if the stage-1 output survives. The five refuters in file 01 section 4 are the test. A reported failure is more useful than an endorsement. If the claim fails, file 07 is withdrawn or narrowed and the links are not sent.

## 7. What this package cannot do

No link here was used to take a measurement. The world literature conflicts inside one evidence pool, and this derivation is a simulation checked for internal consistency against the figures. It can state the shape an answer must have and where the gap is. It cannot settle the pH of a named slot.
