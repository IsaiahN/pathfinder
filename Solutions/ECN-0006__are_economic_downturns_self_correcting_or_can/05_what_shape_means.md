## 1. A shape is a constraint you can state before you can name the answer

The canonical README says the framework returns "what a missing thing would have to look like (a shape, not a name)." Fig 9 :32: "you get a kind of thing, never the answer itself." Fig 13 :134: "Unreachable is a verdict here, not a silence".

From *The Library of Babel*: "you can cut your search short if the set of books returned is small and descriptive enough that it provides a general shape of the answer that satisfies your question."

For this question, a **shape** is the narrowed form of an answer that can be stated before empirical work decides which regime holds. The shape delivered in `01` is: the residual is per slot; the question is a union compound; depth and absence look the same from inside; internal adjustment cannot add an atom once the closure is fixed; the answerable form is a per-slot recovery-rate trend. The names "liquidity trap", "balance sheet recession", "hysteresis" and "real business cycle" are candidates from the world frame, not things the figures name. They are bindings, and must be provenanced as such.

`DEPRESSED(???)DEMAND` is a shape. `DEPRESSED(balance sheet recession)DEMAND` is a name.

## 2. How the shape is obtained

- *Library of Babel*: use the vocabulary already held that is *closest* to the thing, then bootstrap. "With each new term gained, you are able to narrow the space by definition and deepen your vocabulary." Here the closest held terms are the figure terms: residual, closure, atom, import, anchor, union, depth and absence. The economic terms are the new vocabulary found by proximity.
- Fig 6 :30: "The description travels. Fails by being sharpened into terms only we can read. Cast it in effect terms." Fig 9 :30: "your words are borrowed too, so describe the effect rather than the cause." So describe the effect: a gap that does not trend down, not "a failure of market psychology".
- *Decoding Tacit Knowledge*: "When contact isn't possible, the best you can send is a plain description of what you'd expect to see, because anyone can check that against the world." This is why the outreach packet asks what a reader would expect to see in each regime before offering the literature.
- *Library of Babel* on stopping: "you will know that you are done because the finish line will also be the starting place." Here the stop is reached when the residual stops registering against data, not when a preferred school wins.

## 3. What licenses a name

Only two things, both outside the shape:

1. **The ground settles it.** Formula step 5: "the ground settles it. Verified only by what pays. A gate is not the ground." For this question, that means per-slot outcome data and a discriminator that works before the policy decision.
2. **A provenanced import from a frame whose closure differs** (Fig 6 :23, Fig 8 :50 to 52), with a source gate and a shadow test. Naming "balance sheet recession" is an import from the economics frame. It is licensed only when the empirical record of the episode pays for it.

*They're Scanning Books* sharpens the limit: "A minimum viable set of six terms is a human's careful guess at reality's compression axes; another sensorium's minimum set need not be those six." And: "Declaring the primitives does not make them neutral. It makes the choice visible." Applied here, declaring the private closure `Γ_1` is a visible choice, not a discovery. This is the reason F8 in the audit requires the horizon and boundary to be written down.

## 4. Why a source-named regime is the classic forbidden error

*The Hard Problem is About Source* gives the mechanism. Metamerism: an instrument reads green and "it cannot comment on the source." One green light, or yellow plus blue, or RGB, or CMYK could give the same reading. "If several paths create the same behavior, then behavior is a projection with lossy information." It was "the spectrometer, not an argument" that resolved it.

The downturn case is a direct instance. The same observed persistent gap (falling output, rising unemployment) is produced by at least two sources: a reachability failure and a genuine absence. Naming one from the aggregate reading is the metamerism error, exactly what Fig 6 :27 warns: "Which is why some unreachability is depth and some is genuine absence, and from inside the frame the two look the same." What is owed is a discriminating instrument, not a name chosen from the reader's preferred school.

*How Intelligence Actually Works* adds the distinction horizon and union horizon: a projection can pass one observer's horizon and fail against a group's, and "Being 'below the union horizon' is never a settled verdict, only a report that nobody has reached it yet." So the honest statement of this solution's empirical limit is: the regime discriminator is below the union horizon at this budget. That is a report of where the search stopped, not a proof of absence (Fig 8 :39).

## 5. Q: does "shape, not a name" forbid naming a regime here? ANSWERED

No, not as a blanket prohibition. The canonical README's phrase has a subject: "what a **missing** thing would have to look like (a shape, not a name)." It governs a primitive not held in the library. "Liquidity trap" and "balance sheet recession" are not missing primitives of the framework; they are candidate bindings from the economics frame onto the held terms 'absence' and 'asymmetric introduction'. Naming them is permitted as a provenanced binding, not a derivation, and not an import that moves the framework's wall (Fig 6 :14, "the only operator that moves the wall").

What remains wrong is naming a regime *from the aggregate reading alone*. The metamerism point is retained: several sources give the same observed gap, and a source named without a discriminating instrument is a guess recorded as a fact.

## 6. Two consequences for this folder

**Prestige is not action.** From *The Recursive Self-Improvement Trap*: "Prestige can fund an action. It can give an action a budget to be tried. But it cannot settle an action. Only the ground can settle it. The Ground Maintainer must be blind to prestige." A list of famous economists who agree with one camp is the named collapse, not corroboration. The legitimate ask is the opposite: give the split a budget to be tried against data, and let the ground decide which condition holds.

**This audit is a simulation, not a ground reading.** Same essay: "LLMs can quickly simulate an approximate answer/output with the formula found in the architecture post." And: "the truly testable output would need to be derived from the real architecture or working the formula yourself." The essay also says "You may all agree and be wrong." So `01` is a simulated derivation checked for internal consistency against the figures. It is not a test of the economic claim and must be labelled as such. It cannot settle anything it reports on.

My first reading of the README phrase treated it as a ban on naming any regime. That was too strong, and §5 narrows it to missing primitives. The earlier, stronger reading is preserved here because the retraction is part of the record: a reader can see that the error was made and where it was repaired.
