# Outreach draft: check the split before you decide which side of the debate you are on

**Use:** one-to-one, or as a cover note attached to a submission through a public venue in `03_public_contact_points.csv`. **Not** for bulk send.

**Hard precondition:** the shapes and refuters below must survive the reader's check before any theory is offered. If the stage-1 output fails, there is nothing else to look at.

---

**Subject:** One check on a downturn question, before any theory

> Hi [name],
>
> I have one specific output I would like you to check against what you already know about recessions and recoveries. I am not asking you to read a theory first, and I am not asking you to agree with it. If the output does not survive, there is nothing else to look at.
>
> **The output (`specified`, not `grounded`).** The question "are downturns self-correcting, or can weak demand persist?" does not shrink with effort because each side's rule works on a different subset. It is a union compound of two conditions that look the same from inside the private frame:
>
> 1. **Depth.** The demand exists inside the private adjustment frame but is not arriving, through sticky prices, wages, a credit channel that has stopped transmitting, or a savings-investment coordination failure. The move here is to sharpen the measurement, not to inject demand.
> 2. **Absence.** There is no source of final demand at any resolution inside that frame, for example a liquidity trap or a balance sheet recession where the whole private sector saves and no one borrows. The move here is to import a spender from a frame whose closure differs.
>
> The figure-grounded claim is narrow: once the closure is fixed, adjustments inside it cannot create a source of demand it does not contain. The two conditions are indistinguishable from inside that frame with current instruments, which is why both literatures can be coherent and still disagree.
>
> **What I am not claiming:** that markets never self-correct; that intervention is always needed; that any named mechanism (liquidity trap, debt overhang, hysteresis) is the true one in a given episode; or that this derivation has measured anything. The framework returns a shape, not a name. Naming a regime is a job for data, not for this derivation.
>
> **What would refute the output:** (1) a single within-frame statistic that discriminates depth from absence in real time, before the policy decision; (2) a private adjustment mechanism that creates a new source of final demand after nationwide balance-sheet damage at the zero lower bound, with no public or foreign spender; (3) per-slot residuals all trending down together through a downturn while only the aggregate looks flat; (4) an outcome-blind agreeing group that predicts recovery paths reliably without measuring realised spending or employment. I wrote these down before sending this. If any holds, I will retract the corresponding part. A one-line "this fails because..." is more useful to me than agreement. If you would rather not engage, no reply is needed.
>
> **If it survives, and only then:** here are the sources. The figures are the authority, not my summary of them.
> - Figures and formula, verbatim extract (local): `FRAMEWORK/reference/03-FIGURES_TEXT.md`
> - Canonical usage rule (local): `FRAMEWORK/reference/FIGURES_README_canonical.md`
> - Public figures repository (SVG originals, branch `general-agent`): https://github.com/IsaiahN/tether/tree/general-agent/figures
>
> **If you want to use the machinery:** run the loop on a downturn you know well and can check against data. Write down what would refute your result before you run it. A reported failure is more useful to me than a reported success.
>
> Thanks,
> [name]

## Two-stage offer, explicit

1. **Stage 1:** the split, the bound term and the refuter. Ask what the reader would count as a failure. Record their verdict **before** showing any framework material.
2. **Stage 2, opt-in only:** if they are curious, offer the three links. The figures, not the summary, are the authority.
3. **Stage 3:** offer the formula as a method to reuse, with the instruction that each person writes their own refuter and states their own mode.

Nothing here asks for trust, membership, belief, or a public endorsement. One-to-one only. No follow-up to someone who does not reply.
