**Mode:** `general` on the structural shape, `specified` on the two-regime split and its refuters. Nothing here is `grounded`: no econometric result was computed in this folder, and the figures do not report which regime the world is in.

**Status:** done

**Field for public contact points:** recession and recovery research.

## What this folder is

A derive-first package. It states the residual in effect terms, runs a domain census, quotes the figures verbatim before concluding, keeps verdicts per domain, and records a pre-registered refuter. The figures are primary and decide the shape. World sources (economics, complexity science, the framework essays) are secondary corroboration only and never overrule a figure line.

**Honest standing.** This is a simulated derivation checked for internal consistency against the figures, not a ground reading. *The Recursive Self-Improvement Trap* says LLM output is simulacra and that the truly testable output would need to be derived from the real architecture or worked by hand. This folder therefore cannot settle the question it reports on. It reports the shape an answer must have and what would settle it.

## What is deliberately not here

- No personal emails of named individuals. Only public institutional venues and role addresses published on the institution's own site.
- No ranking of people by how likely they are to believe a verdict. Selecting for receptiveness manufactures the disqualifying correlation (Fig 2 :40).
- No bulk-send plan.
- No claim that this derivation has settled whether downturns self-correct. That is an empirical question for the ground.

## The one-line result

The figures do not pick a side in the classical versus Keynesian dispute. They split the question into two regime conditions that look identical from inside the private frame, specify what would discriminate them, and leave the empirical verdict to data. A shape, not an answer.
