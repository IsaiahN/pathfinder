## QUESTION

Decide, in effect terms, what the Tether says about the choice between "downturns self-correct" and "weak demand can persist without intervention". Option A: assert self-correction as a general property of markets. Option B: assert persistence as a general property and prescribe intervention. Option C, derived below: refuse A and B as `general` verdicts, split the question into two conditions that look the same from inside the private frame, bind the term "self-correcting" so it can accrue a residual, and leave the empirical verdict to the ground. What each leads to: A sends an unmeasured verdict and misses the absence regime; B sends an unmeasured verdict and imports an atom into the depth regime; C sends a checkable shape, written refuters and an instrument requirement.

## DOMAIN CENSUS

R by arity, symmetry and scale, then one line per domain with actors / currency / anchor, partition or union, and order by dependency.

- **R (the residual).** The gap between the predicted path of spending, output and employment and what is observed, indexed by slot (sector, region, worker cohort, balance sheet). Arity: two conditions, so a union compound, not one hard question. Symmetry: broken; a representative-agent symmetry is imposed by the model, not read from the residual. Scale: the private adjustment loop closes at one scale; the policy frame is a different stratum with different actors, currency and anchor.
- **Domain 1, private adjustment frame.** Actors: firms, households, workers, banks. Currency: prices, wages, interest rates, inventories, quantities. Anchor: realised transactions, output and employment. This is a genuine domain because it has a nameable anchor. Its closure contains relative prices, wage flexibility, interest-rate adjustment and intertemporal substitution.
- **Domain 2, policy and import frame.** Actors: treasury, legislature, central bank, foreign sector. Currency: fiscal spending, taxes, money, credit, guarantees. Anchor: the same outcomes, reached from a different closure containing a spender whose demand does not depend on private profitability. Different closure from Domain 1, so it can serve as an import source.
- **Domain 3, measurement and verification frame.** Actors: statistical agencies, econometricians, economic historians. Currency: estimates, revisions, confidence intervals. Anchor: the data series, with the caveat that the anchor is partly a maintained convention and is revised. Independence is low because most macro datasets are one evidence pool.
- **Partition or union.** Union. The reachability and absence conditions overlap; a balance sheet recession is both a broken interest-rate channel and an absence of borrowers. A single lever clears only its own region.
- **Order by dependency.** Per-slot perception, then vocabulary for what the situation affords, then the objective that counts as recovery, then which frame holds the missing term, then carry the method. The vocabulary-to-objective link has no instrument of its own and is where the debate stalls. Describe the residual before choosing a frame; refuse a broken term before minting a new one.

Strain test: Fig 13's six physical terms were re-censused and not applied to macro categories; the forcing would strain, so it governs another quantity here. Three strains are recorded openly in `01` §6.

## VERDICT (from the figures)

Neither "self-correcting" nor "weak demand persists" survives as a `general` verdict. The figures deliver a conditional shape. Demand is per slot; once the private closure is fixed, internal adjustment cannot add a spender the closure lacks; reachability failure and genuine absence look the same from inside and issue opposite instructions; the question is a union compound that must be split; "self-correcting" becomes answerable only when bound as a per-slot trend over a declared horizon and closure; a flat residual points a refusal at the held term; intervention is asymmetric introduction; persistence is a negative-sign cycle. The empirical regime is `gap` at this budget, not silence. All of this is `specified` or `general` and ungrounded.

- **cost if wrong:** bounded and locatable. The packet states mode, refuters and lineage before the conclusion, so an error is findable rather than invisible. The real cost is spending the independence of outside readers by sending a conclusion instead of a split, which Fig 2 :42 prices: "Buy independence in proportion to the cost of being wrong." That cost is avoidable by leading with the shape and its refuter.
- **undoable:** yes. The empirical regime is left open, so no atom has to be withdrawn. A later dataset or discriminator can be added to `01` §4 and the verdict revised without disturbing the shape. No claim here depends on a particular named regime being true.
- **reversed by:** a ground reading, not agreement. A within-frame statistic that separates depth from absence before the policy decision would dissolve the indistinguishability claim; a private atom-creating mechanism observed at the zero lower bound after nationwide balance-sheet damage would break the closure claim; a single mechanism closing both conditions across downturns would collapse the union; a derivation showing the horizon and boundary are given by the figures rather than declared would break F8. Until one of these fires, the verdict stands as `candidate`.
