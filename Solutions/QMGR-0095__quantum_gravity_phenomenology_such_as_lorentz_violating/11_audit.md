**R_T is nonzero.** Three things the shorthand holds do not survive the trip back down:

- the **emission-assumption dependence of a bound**, which the shorthand compresses into "null result bounds a class" and which in the concrete paper spans limits from about 1.19 to greater than 100 Planck depending on `t_start`.

One thing survives cleanly: a structure must sit between the energy modification and the space and time it composes with, and a null result is not proof of absence. That is the residue worth sending.
