# Expert verification packet — the Tether on Lorentz-violating effects in GRBs

**What this is:** a claim in a form a GRB phenomenologist or quantum-gravity theorist can check. Mode, derivation and refuters are stated before the conclusion, so a reader knows what to look for and what would settle it.

**Mode: `specified` on the per-domain claims, `general` on the cross-scale shape, `gap` on which QG model is correct, not `grounded`.** No registration of new physics is claimed. This is a shape and a route, not a result.

**Status of this packet: a simulated derivation checked for internal consistency against the figures, not a ground reading.** It cannot settle the claim it reports on.

## 1. The claim, in one paragraph

Quantum-gravity phenomenology in GRBs is a ground-access problem: the GR/QM contact at the Planck scale cannot be reached directly, so the field looks for a per-unit-length modification of photon dispersion that accumulates over cosmological distance. Under the figures this is a cross-scale, cross-kind composition: a structure over energy bonded to the states time and distance. Any detection must have a threefold shape, an energy- and redshift-growing delay, independence from the source's emission state, and registration by low-overlap instruments. A measured lag is a projection that cannot comment on its source, so establishing LIV requires a source-discriminating instrument, not a better fit to one burst. Null results bound model classes; they are not proof of absence. The framework returns this shape, five refuters written in advance, and the instruments that would close it. It does not name a QG theory.

## 2. Three checkable sub-claims

### C1 — framework-internal; checkable by reading Figures 13 and 4

- **Statement:** a GRB LIV signature is a cross-scale composition across the two kinds, not a direct state-state bond and not a new primitive.
- **Source, verbatim:** Fig 13 :75 — "Two states never bond directly. It is not space-time but space, a structure, time; and two structures join only through a state." Also Fig 13 :74 — "Everything else is a composition across the two kinds, never within one." And Fig 4 :20 — "Only the actors, the currency and the anchor change."
- **What to check:** parse the claimed object as `energy structure, over space and time, gives delay`. Does any step join two states directly, or two structures directly? Does the binding add a seventh primitive to the six-term set?
- **Refuter:** exhibit an energy-dependent lag that does not grow with distance, or a per-unit modification that cannot compose with the propagation baseline (breaks F2); or derive a new primitive from the other five.

### C2 — observation-facing; checkable against the GRB record

- **Statement:** an arrival-time measurement is a non-updating anchor for a model's reach, but a measured lag is a projection with several possible sources; separation requires low-overlap instruments and a source-discriminating observable.
- **Source, verbatim:** Fig 2 :29 — "An anchor is whatever settles the question and does not change its answer because of what any frame thinks. A measurement, an experiment, a counter, a market, a patient outcome." Fig 2 :31 — "Independence, between two frames, is how little they draw on the same evidence." Fig 2 :40 — "disqualifying for a verdict: agreement about the world is worthless when the agreeing frames share an evidence pool". Fig 10 :26 — "stale percepts, dropped transients, saturated metrics, pooled readings".
- **What to check:** do the cited analyses use independent photons and independent emission assumptions, or are they re-analyses of one photon list? Does any single burst's lag determine its own source, given the `t_start` freedom documented in the 2009 Nature paper (seven choices, limits from about 1.19 to greater than 100 Planck)?
- **Refuter:** show that arrival times or redshifts move with the model or the pipeline; or show zero independence between two alleged corroborating frames; or exhibit a discriminator that shows the lag is wholly source-intrinsic.

### C3 — framework-internal inference from the null record

- **Statement:** a non-detection bounds a model class under stated emission assumptions; it does not prove absence, and an upper limit is a filter for the budget, not a witness for the verdict.
- **Source, verbatim:** Fig 8 :39 — "So a search that finds nothing is never a proof of absence." Fig 9 :60 — "Use filters for the budget and witnesses for the verdict. Never let a filter hand you a verdict." Fig 6 :27 — "Which is why some unreachability is depth and some is genuine absence, and from inside the frame the two look the same."
- **What to check:** is the published Fermi result a bound with a confidence interval and stated `t_start` assumptions ("no evidence for the violation of Lorentz invariance", lower limit near 1.2 Planck), or is it presented as the verification of Lorentz invariance? Does the 2025 3.1 sigma dispersion claim survive low-overlap triangulation, or is it a candidate inside one analysis pool?
- **Refuter:** produce a sealed room, a closed model space plus a complete search, such that a null result entails absence. Then Fig 8 :39 no longer applies.

## 3. What is explicitly not claimed

- Not that Lorentz invariance is violated, and not that it is exact.
- Not a theory of everything; no final room (Fig 7 :6 — "the limit: no last room").
- Not a specific value of `E_QG`, and no derivation of string theory, loop quantum gravity or any named model from the six-term set. Which model is correct is a `gap`.
- Not that the 2025 dispersion claim is correct, and not that it is refuted. It is recorded as a low-confidence candidate inside one evidence pool, with a live strain.
- Not `grounded`. Nothing here is asserted as settled by experiment.
- Not that a numeric bound on `E_QG` is forbidden by "shape, not a name". That rule governs a missing primitive; a bound is a measurement about a model's reach. See `05_what_shape_means.md` §6.

## 5. What would settle it

A source-discriminating instrument plus low-overlap triangulation, not a further argument.

1. **Threshold Test:** is the measured delay larger than the maximum source-intrinsic delay the emission model permits, across multiple bursts and instruments?
2. **Accumulation Test:** does the lag grow with redshift at the predicted rate? A robust lag that does not grow breaks the shape claim.
3. **Discriminator:** is there an observable that separates emission from propagation for a single burst, equivalent to the spectrometer that resolved the metamerism case in *The Hard Problem is About Source*?
