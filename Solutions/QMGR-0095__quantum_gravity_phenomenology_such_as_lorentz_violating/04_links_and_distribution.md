## Wider literature that bears on the answer

These are physics-facing sources opened through the search integration. They are recorded in the ledger with author, venue, year and quote. The 2009 and 2010 Fermi papers all analyse the same GRB 090510 event and count as **one evidence pool**, not several witnesses.

1. **Wei and Wu 2021/2022** (Handbook of X-ray and Gamma-ray Astrophysics, arXiv:2111.02029): the review that frames the whole field as a ground-access problem solved by accumulation over long baselines, and that states the source-intrinsic degeneracy explicitly. This is the best single orientation source for a checker.
2. **Abdo et al. 2009** (Nature, GRB 090510): the canonical null result with a limit near the Planck scale. Read it for the honesty of "no evidence for the violation", not as a verdict on quantum gravity.
3. **Vasileiou et al. 2010** (Fermi Symposium proceedings): the accumulation sentence and the statement that affected linear models are rendered highly implausible.
4. **Vasileiou et al. 2013** (Phys Rev D 87, 122001, gained via the published record in the review): three complementary dispersion estimators over four Fermi GRBs, with limits `E_QG,1 > 7.6 E_Pl` subluminal. Source-intrinsic dispersion is treated as a separate, explicitly conservative assumption.
5. **The 2023 PRD study on intrinsic lags** (Phys Rev D 108, 123023, abstract via search integration, author not captured): source contributions "can strongly impact resulting LIV tests, modifying their limits by up to a factor of a few." This is the central objection to a single-burst detection claim.
6. **The 2025 ApJ claim of a dispersion-free rejection at 3.1 sigma** (doi:10.3847/1538-4357/adb8d4, abstract via search integration): a candidate registration using multiple instruments and photons up to 12.2 TeV. It is recorded as an unresolved low-confidence claim inside one analysis pool, not as a flipped verdict.
7. **Altman and Bland / BMJ editorial 1995-style null-result statistics** and a 2025 Biology Letters paper: genuinely low-overlap corroboration for F7 (a non-detection is not a proof of absence).
8. **Kalderon 2008** and the metamerism literature (colour input devices, 2007 CIC): low-overlap corroboration for F5 (one reading, several possible sources).

Overlap declaration: sources 2 to 6 share the GRB timing evidence pool and count as one witness with internal disagreement. Sources 7 and 8 are low-overlap corroborators. The three framework essays share the framework author's pool completely and carry no independent witness weight.

## Distribution: offer, do not push

Figure-grounded reasons:

- **Fig 4 :22** — *"Only methods cross up. Only head starts come down."* Sending the full architecture before anyone has described a residual sends a recording, not a generator. Lead with the shape and its refuter; offer links after.
- **Fig 10 :42** — *"A verifier that can reconstruct the claim cannot verify it."* Seat 4 must not hold the interiors. A recipient who has absorbed the framework before being asked for a verdict has joined the frame being checked.
- **Fig 9 :27-28** — *"describe it before you go looking, or any frame you pick will seem to fit."* Ask what they would expect to see if the shape claim were true before giving them the article.
- **Fig 2 :40** — *"disqualifying for a verdict: agreement about the world is worthless when the agreeing frames share an evidence pool"*. A mass send selected for people who already expect a detection produces a convention, not corroboration.
- **Fig 2 :42** — *"Buy independence in proportion to the cost of being wrong."* The cost here is announcing a detection of new physics that is actually a source-intrinsic lag, so independence is worth real effort.

**Recommended order in a reply:** (1) the threefold shape claim and the five refuters; (2) ask which failure condition they would fire first and what they would count as a detection; (3) record that verdict; (4) only then the three links, with the note that the figures, not the summary, are the authority.
