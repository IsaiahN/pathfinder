## 1. A shape is a constraint, not a filler

The figures README states the limit: the framework returns *"what a missing thing would have to look like (a shape, not a name)."* Fig 9 :32: *"you get a kind of thing, never the answer itself"*. Fig 13: *"It can say the answer is out of reach with the library as it stands... It cannot say which primitive is missing, because naming it would mean already holding it."*

From *The Library of Babel* (opened): *"you can cut your search short if the set of books returned is small and descriptive enough that it provides a general shape of the answer that satisfies your question."* The same essay: *"With each new term gained, you narrow the space by definition and deepen your vocabulary."*

So a **shape** is the narrowed form of an answer you can state before you can name it. It constrains; it does not fill. For this question the shape is: *an energy-ordered delay, growing with distance, source-independent, registered by low-overlap instruments, with a refusal condition written in advance*. "Lorentz invariance is violated at scale X by model Y" is a name, not a shape.

## 2. How a shape is obtained

- *Library of Babel* (opened): use the vocabulary you already have that is *closest* to the thing, look for recurring new terms that overlap or correlate with it, recurse. The essay calls this proximity and describes a bootstrapping convergence: *"you will find these new terms that overlap, correlate, or are even used interchangeably with your known terms."*
- Fig 6 :30: *"The description travels. Fails by being sharpened into terms only we can read. Cast it in effect terms."* Fig 9 :30-31: *"your words are borrowed too, so describe the effect rather than the cause."*
- *Decoding Tacit Knowledge* (carried): *"When contact isn't possible, the best you can send is a plain description of what you'd expect to see, because anyone can check that against the world."* This is exactly the two-stage offer: describe the expected delay, let the reader check it.
- *Library of Babel* on stopping (opened): the answer is reached when *"the finish line will also be the starting place"* and the paradox is that by then you have *"composed the answer through the process of locating it."*

For this question the proximity vocabulary came from the essays and figures, and the new terms came from the world: `Delta t`, `E_QG`, spectral lag, `t_start`, birefringence, redshift. Each narrowed the shape. The residual that will not close is which physical mechanism produced the delay.

## 3. What licenses a name

Only two things, both outside the shape itself:

1. **The ground settles it.** Formula step 5: *"the ground settles it. Verified only by what pays."* The README: the framework *"stops at the limit of what the available instruments can distinguish, and records the lineage so the stop is honest."* A registered, source-independent, distance-growing delay that survives low-overlap triangulation would settle the shape; it still would not select a unique quantum-gravity theory.
2. **A provenanced import from a frame whose closure differs** (Fig 6 :23, Fig 8 :50-52), with a source gate and a shadow test. A binding to a specific LIV model is such an import and must be recorded as such.

*They're Scanning Books* (carried) sharpens this: *"The real import is not a new term in the existing space. It is a new dimension of the space."* And: *"A minimum viable set of six terms is a human's careful guess at reality's compression axes; another sensorium's minimum set need not be those six."* Also: *"Declaring the primitives does not make them neutral. It makes the choice visible."*

The six are a visible guess, not a discovery of nature's own list. Naming one as *the* explanation of a delay turns a visible choice into an invisible one.

## 4. The metamerism problem is the governing error for this question

*The Hard Problem is About Source* (opened) gives the mechanism in the source's own words:

> *"You can have several underlying causes that create identical readings but the definition of the output would be read as the same."*
>
> *"If several paths create the same behavior, then behavior is a projection with lossy information"*
>
> "the instrument is speaking only about what it measured, it cannot comment on the source."

A detector that reads an energy-dependent arrival lag *cannot comment on the source* of that lag. It could be emission physics at the burst; it could be modified propagation; it could be a selection choice for `t_start`. The essay's resolution is the important part: *"It was proven wrong not by argument, but by the arrival of a better instrument."* And: *"An instrument that can't distinguish these cases demonstrates its own inadequacy."*

Kalderon 2008 (opened, Mind) gives the epistemic form from a genuinely different field: "In an environment known to be populated by metameric pairs, a perceiver does not know which colour he is perceiving." Colour constancy, the varying of illumination, is the solution there. The analogue here is varying the redshift, the energy band and the instrument, so the source and propagation causes separate.

So the honest statement is: **the shape is registerable, the source of a registration is not determined by the reading alone.** This is F5 in `01`.

## 5. Q1 — What does the framework actually answer here?

**Nothing about which model is true.** The framework returns the shape a detection must have and the conditions under which a null result binds a model's reach. It does not return a quantum-gravity theory, a unique `E_QG` value, or a verdict that Lorentz invariance is exact. Those require the ground.

**Does a published limit close the residual?** No. Fig 8 :39: *"So a search that finds nothing is never a proof of absence."* The limit closes the reach of a model class under stated assumptions; it leaves the contact residual open. *How Intelligence Actually Works* (carried) is careful on the analogous point: *"we cannot say which state composed first, and we don't know if the order mattered."* The same discipline applies here: we cannot say which cause produced the lag.

## 6. Correction to my own §3 and §5 (the shape-not-a-name overstatement)

I initially applied *"shape, not a name"* to forbid stating a numerical bound on `E_QG`. That was an overstatement. The README's phrase is *"what a **missing** thing would have to look like (a shape, not a name)."* It governs a primitive not held in the library. `E_QG` is a parameter of a model already in the physics library, and a bound on it is a ground result about that model's reach, not the invention of a new primitive. What remains genuinely forbidden is naming the correct quantum-gravity theory from the framework alone, because no figure line selects it.

Both claims are now recorded so neither is quietly lost:

- A numeric bound is a legitimate measurement statement, provided its emission assumptions are stated (Fig 2 :29, the counter).
- A specific QG model named as *the* explanation is a binding from a world frame, provenanced as such, and carries no framework authority.

Triangulated for the correction: canonical README + Fig 13 :115 (+ F10 of `01`).

## 7. Two consequences for this solution

**Prestige is not action.** From *The Recursive Self-Improvement Trap* (opened): *"Prestige must never be allowed to validate an action directly... Someone let prestige stand in for proof... Prestige can fund an action. It can give an action a budget to be tried. But it cannot settle an action. Only the ground can settle it. The Ground Maintainer must be blind to prestige."*

A campaign that gathers prestigious names to agree that LIV has been detected is the named collapse, not corroboration. The legitimate ask is the opposite: give the shape a budget to be tried, and let the arrival-time counter, not a reputation, settle it.

**This audit is a simulation, not a ground reading.** Same essay (opened): *"LLMs can quickly simulate an approximate answer/output with the formula... However, the truly testable output would need to be derived from the real architecture or working the formula yourself."* So `01` is a simulated derivation, checked for internal consistency against the figures. It is **not** a test of any LIV model and must be labelled as such. It cannot settle anything it reports on.
