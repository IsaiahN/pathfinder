## 6. One-paragraph version for outreach

> The Tether does not tell you whether Lorentz invariance is violated, and it does not name a quantum-gravity theory. It says that a GRB Lorentz-violation test is a cross-scale composition: a structure over photon energy bonded to the states time and distance, so a tiny per-unit effect accumulates over a cosmological baseline. Any detection must have three features at once: the delay grows with energy and redshift, it does not track the burst's own emission state, and it is seen by instruments that do not share the same photons and the same assumptions. What the framework offers is that shape, five failure conditions written in advance, and the instruments that would close it. The center stays open until the ground settles it.
