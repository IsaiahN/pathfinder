## What is deliberately not here

- No personal emails of named individuals.
- No ranking of people by how likely they are to believe a Lorentz-violation claim or a quantum-gravity model.
- No bulk-send plan.

Three reasons, in the figures' own words:

1. **A room does not upgrade the label.** Fig 7 :35: *"A room's agreement is variation, not a verdict. Selection between rooms is where the ground votes."* A hundred analyses of the same Fermi photons are one pool talking, not a hundred witnesses.
2. **Correlation disqualifies a verdict.** Fig 2 :40: *"disqualifying for a verdict: agreement about the world is worthless when the agreeing frames share an evidence pool"*. Selecting recipients for receptiveness manufactures the correlation that makes their agreement worthless.
3. **Asking for trust before checking spends the only thing the reader contributes.** Fig 10 :52: *"Bring a characterised residual, never the source."* Fig 10 :42: *"A verifier that can reconstruct the claim cannot verify it."*

Legally: unsolicited bulk outreach breaches GDPR and CAN-SPAM style rules in many jurisdictions and will get a sending domain blacklisted. That is a practical failure on top of the structural one. The Fermi Guest Investigator page also records a live constraint on generative-tool use in proposals (see `04`), which is recorded rather than hidden.
