## 1. A shape is a constraint, not a filler

From *The Library of Babel*: *"You can cut your search short if the set of books returned is small and descriptive enough that it provides a general shape of the answer that satisfies your question."*

So a **shape** is the narrowed form of an answer you can state before you can name it. It constrains; it does not fill. For this question the shape is: "two separable conditions, contact change and percept change, with a discriminator between them." It is not "brand X creates value" or "all branding is manipulation." Fig 9 :32: *"you get a kind of thing, never the answer itself."*

## 2. How a shape is obtained

- *Library of Babel*: use the vocabulary you already have that is *closest* to the thing, look for recurring new terms that overlap or correlate with it, recurse. *"With each new term gained, you are able to narrow the space by definition and deepen your vocabulary."* Here the closest terms were residual, anchor, channel, ground, catalyst and settlement, borrowed from the figures.
- Fig 6 :30: *"The description travels. Fails by being sharpened into terms only we can read. Cast it in effect terms."* Fig 9 :30-31: *"your words are borrowed too, so describe the effect rather than the cause."*
- *Decoding Tacit Knowledge*: *"When contact isn't possible, the best you can send is a plain description of what you'd expect to see, because anyone can check that against the world."* This is why the outreach asks what a refuter would expect to see under C1 versus C2, before showing any theory.
- *Library of Babel* on stopping: the answer is reached when *"the finish line will also be the starting place"*, and the paradox is that by then you have *"composed the answer through the process of locating it."*

## 3. What licenses a name

Only two things, both outside the shape itself:

1. **The ground settles it.** Formula step 5: *"the ground settles it. Verified only by what pays."* The README: the framework *"stops at the limit of what the available instruments can distinguish, and records the lineage so the stop is honest."*
2. **A provenanced import from a frame whose closure differs** (Fig 6 :23, Fig 8 :50-52), with a source gate and a shadow test.

*They're Scanning Books* sharpens this: *"The real import is not a new term in the existing space. It is a new dimension of the space."* Also: *"Declaring the primitives does not make them neutral. It makes the choice visible."* So importing "brand trust" into the framework must be recorded as a binding from the marketing frame, not as a discovery of a new primitive.

## 4. Why the premium is the specific forbidden error

*The Hard Problem is About Source* gives the mechanism. Metamerism: an instrument reads green and *"cannot comment on the source"*; one green light, or yellow plus blue, or RGB, or CMYK, or a scheme not yet discovered. The reading does not determine the source. *"If several paths create the same behavior, then behavior is a projection with lossy information."* And: *"An instrument that can't distinguish these cases demonstrates its own inadequacy."* It was the **spectrometer**, not an argument, that resolved it.

A willingness-to-pay reading is a metamerism error when it is used to name the source of value. Several paths (genuine quality, a head start, a stale percept, habit, switching cost) can create the same premium. What is owed is a better instrument, not a name. Fig 1 :27: *"They are frame-internal. A frame cannot score itself with a quantity it also produces."*

*The Recursive Self-Improvement Trap*: *"the output is only as good as the validator. The agent, or the tool, or the frame, or the theory, cannot validate itself."*

I first wrote as if every consumer reading were an untrusted instrument. That overstates the case. Fig 2 :37: *"Human judgment is the right anchor for is this a good answer for a person, because the question is about people."* For the sub-question "is this brand trustworthy for this person," human judgment is the anchor, not merely an instrument. ## 6. Two consequences for outreach

**Prestige is not action.** From *The Recursive Self-Improvement Trap*: *"Prestige must never be allowed to validate an action directly... Prestige can fund an action. It can give an action a budget to be tried. But it cannot settle an action. Only the ground can settle it. The Ground Maintainer must be blind to prestige."*

A campaign that gathers famous brand experts to agree with the claim is the named collapse, not corroboration. The legitimate ask is the opposite: give the shape a budget to be tried, and let the ground, an instrument, a blind test, a long-horizon outcome record, settle it.

**A brand is a head start, not a recording.** *Decoding Tacit Knowledge*: *"Only methods cross up. Only head starts come down"* (Fig 4 :22). A brand that makes a consumer's next search cheaper is a head start. A brand that is replayed as proof of a past success is a recording, and *"A recording carried upward looks like knowledge and is a description of one occasion."* (Fig 4 :25).

## 7. My own audit is a simulation, not a ground reading

Same essay: *"LLMs can quickly simulate an approximate answer/output with the formula found in the architecture post... However, the truly testable output would need to be derived from the real architecture or working the formula yourself."* LLM output is *"simulacra."* So `01` is a simulated derivation, checked for internal consistency against the figures. It is **not** a test of the question and must be labelled as such. It cannot settle anything it reports on.
