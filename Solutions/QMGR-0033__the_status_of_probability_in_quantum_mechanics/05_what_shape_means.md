## 1. A shape is a constraint, not a filler

The figures README states the limit: the framework returns *"what a missing thing would have to look like (a shape, not a name)"*. Fig 9 :32: *"you get a kind of thing, never the answer itself"*. Fig 13 :135: *"It cannot say which primitive is missing, because naming it would mean already holding it."*

From *The Library of Babel*: *"you can cut your search short if the set of books returned is small and descriptive enough that it provides a general shape of the answer that satisfies your question."*

So a **shape** is the narrowed form of an answer you can state before you can name it. It constrains; it does not fill. For this question:

- The shape is: *probability has two structurally distinct roles, a frame-internal controller role and a structural remainder role, and neither role is a name for the physical status.*
- The name would be: *"probability is fundamental"* or *"probability is epistemic."* The derivation does not supply either name.

## 2. How a shape is obtained

- *Library of Babel*: use the vocabulary you already have that is *closest* to the thing, look for recurring new terms that overlap or correlate with it, recurse. *"With each new term gained, you narrow the space and deepen your vocabulary."* It also warns about *"a bundle or conflation of several problems masquerading as one"*, which is exactly the state of the fundamental/epistemic binary before it is split.
- Fig 6 :30: *"The description travels. Fails by being sharpened into terms only we can read. Cast it in effect terms."* Fig 9 :30-31: *"your words are borrowed too, so describe the effect rather than the cause."*
- *Decoding Tacit Knowledge*: *"When contact isn't possible, the best you can send is a plain description of what you'd expect to see, because anyone can check that against the world."* The same essay gives the sharpest formulation for a measurement question: *"A measurement with no question is noise, and a reading with no encounter is projection."*
- *Library of Babel* on stopping: the answer is reached when *"the finish line will also be the starting place"*, and the paradox is that by then you have *"composed the answer through the process of locating it."*

## 3. What licenses a name

Only two things, both outside the shape itself:

1. **The ground settles it.** Formula step 5: *"the ground settles it. Verified only by what pays. A gate is not the ground."* The README: the framework *"stops at the limit of what the available instruments can distinguish, and records the lineage so the stop is honest."* For QM probability, the ground settles the frequencies. It does not settle the single-event source here.
2. **A provenanced import from a frame whose closure differs** (Fig 6 :23, Fig 8 :50-52), with a source gate and a shadow test.

*They're Scanning Books* sharpens the second: *"The real import is not a new term in the existing space. It is a new dimension of the space."* And: *"A minimum viable set of six terms is a human's careful guess at reality's compression axes; another sensorium's minimum set need not be those six."* Also: *"Declaring the primitives does not make them neutral. It makes the choice visible."*

The six are a visible guess, not a discovery of nature's own list. Naming one as *the* status of probability turns a visible choice into an invisible one.

## 4. The metamerism point is the load-bearing one here

*The Hard Problem is About Source* gives the mechanism. An instrument reads green and *"cannot comment on the source"*: one green light, or yellow plus blue, or RGB, or CMYK, or a scheme not yet discovered. *"If several paths create the same behavior, then behavior is a projection with lossy information."* And: *"An instrument that can't distinguish these cases demonstrates its own inadequacy, it cannot claim anything substantial about what is being measured or what causes it."* It was the **spectrometer**, not an argument, that resolved it.

*How Intelligence Actually Works* repeats the case: *"The projection of green can't tell you whether it was RGB, CMYK or something else that composed it."* And it names the limit precisely: *"The recursion converges toward indiscernibility, and indiscernibility is not identity."*

Applied here: the Born-frequency reading cannot comment on whether its source is objective chance or ignorance. The two readings are metamerically equivalent at that reading. What is owed is a source-discriminating instrument, not a further argument.

The same essay supplies the direct statement about the quantum case: *"Superposition isn't ignorance: it's the state where the distinction hasn't been made yet."* And: *"Measurement doesn't reveal the value. It creates the value by collapsing the space of values."* This is a framework reading of measurement, not settled physics, and it is recorded as such. It does not settle the physics question.

## 5. The honest stop is a named category, not a failure

From *How Intelligence Actually Works*: *"Being 'below the union horizon' is never a settled verdict, only a report that nobody has reached it yet."* And: *"Case one is not learnable in the same way. 'No trace exists' can never be confirmed by better instruments. A bigger and better instrument finding nothing is indistinguishable from a trace it still can't reach. You can only ever say nothing has been found yet."*

So the stop on QM probability is **unreached at this instrument set**, with the lineage recorded. Fig 13 :134: *"Unreachable is a verdict here, not a silence"*. Fig 8 :39: *"So a search that finds nothing is never a proof of absence."*

## 5b. Correction to my own §3 and §4

I initially wrote that *"shape, not a name"* forbids saying anything about probability's status. That overstates it. The README's phrase governs *"a missing thing"*, a primitive not held in Γ. Two of the things discussed here are already held: the belief/weighting couple (`b`, `w`) is in the Symbols table, and randomness is a derived quantity described by Fig 13 :28-32. Naming those roles is a **binding onto held terms**, not the invention of a new primitive.

What remains forbidden is promoting either role to *the* physical status of quantum probability. That status is not a held atom, and no instrument in this derivation settles it. My §4 metamerism point is **not** retracted: the frequency reading still cannot comment on its source. The reason is the missing discriminator, not a blanket ban on describing held terms. Both claims are now recorded so neither is quietly lost. Same correction pattern as exemplar `05` §5b, applied to a status claim instead of to Gradient.

## 6. Two consequences for this question

**Prestige is not action.** From *The Recursive Self-Improvement Trap*: *"Prestige must never be allowed to validate an action directly... Someone let prestige stand in for proof... Prestige can fund an action. It can give an action a budget to be tried. But it cannot settle an action. Only the ground can settle it. The Ground Maintainer must be blind to prestige."* A campaign that gathers famous names to agree with a reading of probability is the named collapse, not corroboration. The legitimate ask is the opposite: give the claim a budget to be tried, and let an instrument settle it.

**My own audit is a simulation, not a ground reading.** Same essay: *"LLMs can quickly simulate an approximate answer/output with the formula found in the architecture post."* And: *"the truly testable output would need to be derived from the real architecture or working the formula yourself."* The canonical article says such outputs *"must be seen as simulacra"* and *"could never be verified if the solutions presented were not contrived to fit the formula."* So `01_figure_grounded_audit.md` is a simulated derivation, checked for internal consistency against the figures. It is **not** a test of the claim and must be labelled as such. It cannot settle anything it reports on.
