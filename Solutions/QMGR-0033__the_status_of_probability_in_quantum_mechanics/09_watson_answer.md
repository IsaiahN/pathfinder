## QUESTION

Decide, in effect terms, the status of probability in quantum mechanics. Option A: probability is fundamental, an objective single-case chance that no further information could remove. Option B: probability is epistemic, a measure of an observer's ignorance that a complete specification would remove. What each leads to: A makes the Born rule a law about the world and requires a real collapse or real branch weights; B makes the state a representation of knowledge and requires a story about what the missing information is. The figures are asked whether either option is nameable from what is held, or whether the binary itself is the wrong object. This is a `specified` structural question, not a measurement.

## VERDICT (from the figures)

The binary as posed is refused. The shape survives. The counter is an anchor for frequencies, but aggregation across slots discards the live single-event signal, so the same Born frequencies fit both readings. In the framework the epistemic side is `b` and `w`, frame-internal and "not the observation", so a frame cannot settle its own status with it; the ontic-looking side is randomness as a remainder compounding through the loop, with genuine indeterminacy explicitly recorded as unresolved. Over the shared atom set the two readings are isomers, same atoms arranged differently, invisible in the Born formula and decisive in which instrument would separate them. This is a `specified`, ungrounded, `candidate` result. The binary verdict is `gap` or `unreached`, not a silence and not a false name.

- **cost if wrong:** bounded and locatable. The packet states its mode, lists refuters before the conclusion and records the lineage, so an error is findable rather than invisible. The real cost is spending the independence of outside readers by sending a conclusion instead of a generator. Fig 2 :42 says "Buy independence in proportion to the cost of being wrong." That cost is avoidable by leading with the residual, the shape claim and its refuter.
- **undoable:** yes. The refused binary is removable without touching the derivation; `07_the_tether_answer.md` already removes it. A sent claim can be superseded by the corrected packet, and no primitive has to be withdrawn. The unbundled sub-questions can be answered one at a time as instruments improve.
- **reversed by:** a ground reading, not agreement. A source-discriminating instrument whose readings differ between an objective-chance model and an ignorance model while agreeing on all Born frequencies would name the status. A derivation of one of the six from the other five, a demonstration that `b` or `w` is not frame-internal, or a derivation of the loop's randomness from a single primitive with no crossing would break the shape. A barrier theorem proving observational equivalence for every possible instrument would change the verdict from `unreached` to `underivable`, a different fact with a different instruction. Until one of these fires, the verdict stands as `candidate`.
