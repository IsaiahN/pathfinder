## 0. The question under audit, stated in effect terms

> Should economics favor unified mathematical models, or use many models tailored to different questions?

**Option U (unified):** one mathematical framework intended to cover all economic questions, with one closure and one vocabulary. What it leads to: compression, cross-domain inference, a shared set of terms; and a terminal frame whose residual is supposed to be global.

**Option P (many tailored):** a model per question or domain, each with its own closure, each settled against its own ground. What it leads to: per-slot residuals stay visible, models can be compared, and no single frame claims the whole field; and a permanent risk that the models overlap so much that their union adds nothing.

**The residual R.** A methodological prescription has no single measurement, so state R in effect terms: the live per-question gaps between what an economic model predicts and what data, markets, experiments or outcomes return. Fig 1 :19: *"The residual is indexed by object slot, not aggregated."* Symbols table :38-40: R is *"the aim, not only the error signal, and it is always a slice: what cannot be perceived yet is residual and simply cannot be read."* The 12-model Brookings comparison in the world literature is the concrete shape: the same shock, different answers, no convergence.

**Bind the sought-for shape as an ordinary term before the run** (formula step "Direction"; Fig 5 :62-63, discussed at F6). The shape to be tested is written down now, in advance:

> A defensible answer will keep the loop and the substrate unified across models, keep models separate and independently grounded per residual, split the question into its distinct conditions, keep verdicts per domain, and refuse any claim to a final model. It will not be expressible as "U" or "P" alone.

That sentence is bound as a term so it can accrue a residual and be refused by the same test as everything else. If the pre-registered refuter in section 4 fires, this shape is withdrawn, not explained away.

## 1. Domain census (before choosing any figure line)

**R by arity, symmetry, scale.** Arity: many, one slot per economic question, not one global question. Symmetry: models are not interchangeable. A model built for labour supply and one built for monetary transmission answer different questions and do not commute into one another. The comparison bond between them is ordered or comparative (→ or ⋛), not the symmetric + that would flatten them into one list (Operators table :14-15, :49-51). Scale: first order inside a model, second order when models are compared.

**One line per domain, actors / currency / anchor.** A domain is admitted only if a nameable anchor exists.

| domain | actors | currency | anchor | question type |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| D1, the model-and-question frame | models as bounded closures, variables, assumptions, agents; the phenomena asked about | prediction, description length, falsifiability | data, markets, experiments, outcomes (Fig 2 :29) | union: models overlap in phenomena and share data |
| D2, the methodology frame | methodologists, criteria (generality, simplicity, elegance, rigor), schools | standards of theory choice | the success of models at settling slots, never preference for a style (Fig 2 :36) | partition of criteria into budget filters and verdict witnesses |
| D3, the verification frame | independent research groups, replication studies, model comparison exercises | agreement versus residual | the real economy, never agreement among modellers (Fig 2 :45) | union only if closures differ; otherwise one frame twice |

All three have nameable anchors, so all three are domains. There is no fourth domain of "truth about unification itself": a preference for unity has no anchor and therefore is not a domain yet. It is handled at F6.

**Partition or union.** The question is a **union compound**, not a partition. It bundles at least three conditions: (a) is there one shared substrate or primitive set all models use, (b) can one model close every slot at acceptable cost, (c) does adding models add evidence or only vocabulary? They overlap: a school can share primitives with another and still close different slots. Fig 9 :54: *"And when the split lands on a union rather than a partition, unbundle before you search again."* Library of Babel: *"Perhaps your problem was a bundle or conflation of several problems masquerading as one? You will need to search all of those problems individually to find their answers and then combine them in a satisfactory way."*

**Order by dependency (Fig 3).** Settle each first-order model against its own ground before comparing models (Fig 12 :104). Refuse the terminal frame before minting a unified one (formula step 2). Describe the residual before naming a frame (Fig 9 :27-28). Fig 3 :20-21: *"The chain is ordered by dependency, not by importance."*

**Strain test applied early.** Fig 13 :115 says *"The set is closed. The arrangements are not. That gap is why import exists."* Read naively, "closed set" seems to support unification. That reading strains, because a closed set of primitives is not a terminal model. Re-census: the closed-set line governs primitives; Fig 7 :6 governs final rooms. Different quantities. The strain is named at F2 and resolved by re-census, not smoothed over.

## 3. Findings

### F1 : The question is a union compound, so answer options must not be averaged (decisive for the re-census)

Fig 9 :4-5:

> **"Disagreements that do not shrink with effort, where each rule keeps working well on a different subset, are not one hard question but several well-formed ones. Split it rather than search."**

Fig 9 :57: *"a case in two conditions is not a harder case of either"*. Fig 9 :54: *"And when the split lands on a union rather than a partition, unbundle before you search again."*

The economics literature supplies the matching symptom. The Wikipedia *Economic model* entry, read 2026-11-23, reports that the Brookings Institution compared 12 leading macroeconometric models under identical assumptions and obtained significantly different answers to the same shock. That is the figure's "each rule keeps working well on a different subset" made empirical: not one hard question, several well-formed ones. The question "U or P?" therefore does not have one verdict. D1, D2 and D3 are answered separately in section 8, and the answers are never averaged into a single "economics should do X".

**Refuter.** A demonstration that the disagreement shrinks monotonically with effort inside one model, so that all slots close on one residual and the per-slot index is redundant, breaks the union reading. Equivalently, a single frame that closes every slot with no positive live residual remaining. If that is shown, split is the wrong move and F1 is withdrawn.

### F2 : A unified final model is forbidden; the closed set is not the final frame

Fig 7 :6 names **"the limit: no last room"**. Fig 7 :30-31:

> **"every room closes some of the gap; no finite number of them ever arrives"**

Fig 13 :90: *"One chain is one route through a space that has no last route."* Fig 13 :115: *"The set is closed. The arrangements are not. That gap is why import exists."* Fig 13 :116-117: *"Six members, and no amount of arranging produces a seventh: a new member would have to be a new kind, and composition only ever rearranges the kinds already held."* Fig 13 :117-118: *"So a frame can search without end and still not arrive: infinite effort inside a finite reach."*

The canonical figures README has a section titled *"This is not a Theory of Everything - and that is the point"*: a final frame with no outside left to import from is forbidden by Fig 4 (no top) and Fig 12 (no bottom). A single unified mathematical model of all economic questions, offered as the frame after which nothing further can be asked, is exactly that final room.

**Re-census recorded.** The first reading of Fig 13 :115 took "closed set" as an argument for unification. That is a strain. Re-census: Fig 13 governs the six primitives, not the terminal model; a discipline can share a closed substrate and still need unboundedly many models over it. Fig 7 independently forbids the last room. Different objects, so no contradiction after re-census.

**Refuter.** Exhibit a terminal frame in economics from which no further question can be asked, or derive a seventh primitive from the six (breaking Fig 13 :41, *"Each is indivisible: no arrangement of the other five yields it"*). Either fires F2.

### F3 : Per-slot residuals survive only under a regime of tailored models (single-source for the figure)

Fig 1 :19: **"The residual is indexed by object slot, not aggregated."** Fig 1 :22: **"Aggregating across slots is how a live signal disappears into an average."**

A unified model that reports one global goodness-of-fit has performed exactly the aggregation the figure forbids. *How a Society of Agents Would Work* applies this line directly to model choice: *"Keep the rate per kind of situation. A method that is brilliant in one setting must not be drowned by an average across all of them. Figure 1: 'Aggregating across slots is how a live signal disappears into an average.'"*

**Triangulation status.** `single-source` for the figure (Fig 1 only), with world corroboration from the Brookings divergence and from Hayek's dispersed-knowledge argument as reported in the *Economic model* entry: *"many of the true forces shaping the economy can never be captured in a single plan."* Corroboration, not a second witness for the figure.

**Refuter.** Show that per-slot residuals are a bookkeeping convenience with no causal role, and that a global residual preserves the same information under a sufficient statistic. Then aggregation loses nothing and F3 fails.

### F4 : "Many models" earns no verdict from count; independence is the operative quantity (the central re-census)

Two figure lines pull in opposite directions until the quantity is re-censused.

Fig 8 :45: **"Two frames together reach what neither reaches alone."** This seems to endorse many models.

Fig 8 :48-49: **"Two frames that overlap almost completely are one frame wearing two names."** Fig 8 :12-14 records the other collapse: **"high overlap / same atoms, different names / their union adds nothing"**. Fig 8 :21: *"nothing was aimed; the union is mute"*.

**Re-census.** The two lines govern different quantities. Fig 8 :45 governs *low-overlap* frames; :48-49 governs *high-overlap* frames. The count of models governs neither. Fig 9 :60 supplies the discipline: **"Use filters for the budget and witnesses for the verdict. Never let a filter hand you a verdict."** A large model count is a filter for budget (how much search to fund); low overlap is a witness for the verdict (whether the union has surplus). This is why the question's own framing, unified versus many, is a false dichotomy at the level the figures govern.

The same conclusion from D3: Fig 2 :31-32: *"Independence, between two frames, is how little they draw on the same evidence."* Fig 2 :40: agreement about the world is worthless when the agreeing frames share an evidence pool. A pile of models trained on the same dataset and calibrated to the same prior is one witness wearing many names.

**Triangulation status.** `triangulated` across Fig 8 :45/:48-49 and Fig 2 :31-32/:40. Low overlap between the union-surplus figure and the ground/alignment figure: different diagrams, different vocabulary, same boundary.

**Refuter.** Show two high-overlap models whose agreement is independently corroborating (contradicts Fig 2 :40), or that union surplus is a function of model count after overlap is held constant (contradicts Fig 8 :48-49). Either breaks F4.

### F5 : The unity is in the method and the substrate, not in one model (resolves the dichotomy)

Fig 4 :19-20:

> **"The kernel is the part that does not change with size: the same loop and the same rule for naming what worked, whether one agent is running it or a thousand. Only the actors, the currency and the anchor change."**

Fig 4 :22: **"Only methods cross up. Only head starts come down."** Fig 4 :25: *"A recording carried upward looks like knowledge and is a description of one occasion."*

*How a Society of Agents Would Work* makes the binding explicit for a community of frames: a "room" is opened per problem and closed with it, and the "commons, across problems... holds only methods distilled from finished rooms." Fig 10 :71-72: *"The room composition is a function of the question. When the question changes, the room should change."*

So the question "unified or many" answers differently at different strata. At the level of the kernel and the substrate, unity is mandatory and figure-derived. At the level of rooms, models, casts and closures, plurality is mandatory because each question needs its own ground contact. Fig 13 :3: *"No system functions without any of them, none is built from the others, and everything else is a composition across the two kinds."* Fig 13 :74: *"Everything else is a composition across the two kinds, never within one."*

**Triangulation status.** `triangulated` across Fig 4 :19-22 and Fig 13 :3/:74, with Fig 12 :103-104 (recursion) as a third, lower-overlap route.

**Refuter.** Derive a different kernel for a different economic question such that no common loop holds across models, while both models still settle against the ground. That breaks unity of method. Alternatively, show that one model can occupy the room for every question without any change of room composition (breaks Fig 10 :71-72).

### F6 : The preference for unification is unbound, predicts nothing, and is invisible to the frame's own checks (limit, not a step)

Fig 5 :62-63:

> **"A frame searching for an answer of a particular shape holds that shape as a PREFERENCE rather than as a term. It is not bound, it predicts nothing, and it accrues no residual, so nothing here can see it."**

The formula's "Direction" section names the remedy as a discipline rather than a step: make the sought-for shape an ordinary term, write down what an answer would look like, bind it, and let it accrue a residual. Section 0 does exactly that. *Decoding Tacit Knowledge* (2026-11-10) restates the limit: a frame holds its preferred answer *"as a PREFERENCE rather than as a term", so "nothing here can see it"*.

The professional preference for elegant unified models is such a preference. Until it is bound, a discipline's internal checks run on the mechanism and not on the direction of the question. Binding it means asking what a unified model would predict that tailored models do not, and letting that prediction fail.

**Refuter.** Show that the unification preference already accrues a residual in normal practice (it predicts a specific cross-domain result that is then tested and can fail). If it does, it is already an ordinary term and the limit does not apply.

### F7 : Model comparison is second order, and its operand type is strained

Fig 12 :104: **"A settled molecule becomes an atom for whatever composes over it. So irreducible is a fact about a level and never about the world, and there is no bottom"**. Fig 12 :5: *"A set of ingredients is not one. The bond is the rest of the information, and it is the half nothing was recording."* Fig 12 :94: **"The arrangement is the substance."**

Each model, settled against its own ground, can serve as an atom at the next level, so comparing models is legal second-order composition and needs no new primitive. But the Operators table :49 defines ⋛ as **"a threshold relation between two quantities"**, and :51 adds *"a goal is a comparison, which is why a vocabulary without it cannot state one"*. Whether two settled models-now-atoms count as quantities at that level is an open type question. It needs an instrument, not an argument.

**This strain is named, not hidden.** It does not change the verdict at F5, because the recursion is licensed by Fig 12 :104 regardless of the operand type of the particular bond used to compare.

**Refuter.** Show that settled models cannot serve as atoms, or that ⋛'s operands must be bare quantities at every level. Then the second-order comparison is not licensed.

### F8 : A single model owned by one school destroys the outside check

Fig 10 :42: **"A verifier that can reconstruct the claim cannot verify it."** Fig 10 :52: **"Bring a characterised residual, never the source."** Fig 2 :35: *"Alignment is triangulation, not negotiation. A mirror cannot correct you."* Seat Map :163: **"High correlation is disqualifying for a verdict and efficient for a convention."**

A field with one dominant model has one frame checking itself through its own practitioners. A field with several independently grounded models has frames that can range against the same ground separately. Fig 2 :45: *"Frames range against the ground. They do not negotiate with it."* The practical consequence for outreach is in `02_outreach_email.md`: send the residual and its refuter first, never the framework's interiors.

**Refuter.** Demonstrate a single-model regime with a genuine outside checker that cannot reconstruct the model and still checks it against the ground. That would show multiplicity is not required for soundness.

### F9 : "Unification by importing assumptions" must be recorded as import, not derivation

Fig 8 :51-52:

> **"Convergent derivation and adopted import look identical in the contents. Only the record of where each came from separates them, and the surplus lives only where the closures differ."**

Fig 13 :127: *"The array cannot be checked by inspection, so the record is what makes it verifiable."* Fig 13 :130: *"A term carries the atoms it was built from and the bonds that joined them. That record is not documentation of the term. It is the term."*

When a model or a school absorbs another school's assumptions and presents the combined model as a single derivation, the contents look the same as convergence. Only provenance separates them. Fig 6 :23: *"No frame escapes its own closure. The bound moves only on contact with a frame whose closure differs."* The source gate is provenance and the shadow test is that the import explains a gap (Fig 6 :11-13).

**Refuter.** Show that content alone determines whether an atom was converged upon or imported, with no record needed. That contradicts Fig 8 :51-52.

### F10 : Generality, simplicity and elegance are budget filters, never verdicts; mode must be stated

Fig 9 :60: **"Use filters for the budget and witnesses for the verdict. Never let a filter hand you a verdict."** Fig 5 :42: *"The acceptance test is a bargain, not a threshold."* Fig 5 :49: *"Machinery firing is not the milestone."*

Seat Map, failure genera, output layer: *"an unmeasured quantity is a specification of what to measure and is worth having, it is not a verdict. The modes are general, specified, and grounded; each is legitimate and the mode must be stated."* A claim that economics "should" unify, delivered as a settled verdict with no measurement, commits the named output-layer defect. The honest form is the mode-tagged shape in section 8.

**Refuter.** Produce a ground reading that settles a disciplinary prescription directly, so the claim can be labelled `grounded`. Until then the verdict stays `candidate`.

## 4. Pre-registered refuter (written before the conclusion)

Required by Seat Map :287: *"a falsifier written before the run is a design instrument, not only an honesty device."*

The shape claim in section 0 is refuted if any of the following holds:

1. **Global residual fires.** A single model closes every economic slot with no positive live residual left, so per-slot indexing is redundant and Fig 1 :19 is not a law here.
2. **Last room fires.** A terminal economic frame is exhibited from which no further question can be asked, or a seventh primitive is derived from the six (Fig 7 :6; Fig 13 :41, :115).
3. **Correlation fires.** Two models drawing on the same evidence and the same assumptions are shown to give independent corroboration, or model count alone is shown to buy surplus with overlap held constant (Fig 2 :40; Fig 8 :48-49).
4. **Kernel fires.** Two economic questions are shown to require different kernels with no common loop, while both models still settle against the ground (Fig 4 :19-20).
5. **World conflict.** A general-purpose economic model is demonstrated to dominate across all problems at acceptable cost, with published ground results. Per WATSON discipline this does not flip the verdict by itself; it sends us back to the domain census. If the re-census still holds, the conflict is recorded, not smoothed.

If any of 1 to 4 fires, the shape is withdrawn. Until one is run, no part of this may be labelled `grounded`.

## 5. Recommendation

1. Do not make "one unified model of economics" the goal of method. It is the last room, and Fig 7 forbids it. Keep the ambition of a shared kernel and substrate; drop the ambition of a terminal frame.
2. Split the question before answering it (F1). Ask separately about shared primitives, per-slot closure, and model independence. Keep the three verdicts separate.
3. Report residuals per slot (F3). If a unified model is proposed, demand its per-slot residual table, not its global fit statistic. Fig 1 :22 says an average throws away the only signal that says where to look.
4. Make independence measurable before calling a plurality productive (F4). Record each model's evidence sources and calibration data. Models sharing a pool are one witness.
5. Share methods upward and head starts downward, not conclusions (F5, Fig 4 :22). A commons of methods with a room per question is the figure-licensed institutional form of the answer.
6. Bind the unification preference as a term with a stated prediction (F6). Ask what a unified model would get right that tailored models get wrong, and let the prediction fail.
7. Keep second-order model comparison, but record the operand type strain at F7 and the provenance of every import at F9.
8. State the mode on every claim (F10). This is `general` / `specified`, candidate, awaiting ground.

Fig 4 :25: *"A recording carried upward looks like knowledge and is a description of one occasion."* Send the generator, the residual and the refuter, not a conclusion.

## 7. Strain test and round trip

**Strains after re-census: one, named.** ⋛'s operand type at second order (F7). All other apparent conflicts dissolved by re-censusing the quantity: closed set versus last room (F2), union surplus versus overlapping blend (F4), unity versus plurality (F5).

**Round trip R_T.** `gap(x, (T_E ∘ T_A)(x))` (Symbols table :77-78). Sent up (x): the verbatim figure set, chiefly Fig 1 :19/:22, Fig 4 :19-22, Fig 7 :6, Fig 8 :45/:48-49, Fig 9 :4-5/:60, Fig 13 :115. Abstracted (T_A): the shorthand "unity of method, plurality of independently grounded models, no last room, split the union". Concretised (T_E): the concrete institutional prescription in section 5. **R_T is nonzero** because the shorthand drops (a) which independence measure is meant, (b) the operand type of the second-order comparison, (c) the requirement that each model be settled before comparison, and (d) the provenance record that distinguishes import from convergence. One thing survives cleanly: per-slot residuals and a shared kernel. R_T is recorded qualitatively as the enumerated set of dropped constraints; it is not measured numerically because no ground reading exists. Treating the formula as identical to the metalaws would set R_T to zero, which is the dead-reckoning error Fig 4 :30 names.

## 8. Verdicts per domain (separate, never averaged)

- **D1, model and question. `general`.** A final unified model is refused (F2). Residuals must remain per slot (F3). *"One chain is one route through a space that has no last route."* Verdict: no terminal frame; split the union; per-slot reporting. `triangulated` (Fig 7, Fig 9, Fig 13) for the no-final-frame part; `single-source` (Fig 1) for the per-slot part, with world corroboration.
- **D2, methodology. `general`.** The kernel and substrate are unified; models, casts and rooms are plural (F5). The preference for unification is an unbound shape until written down as a term (F6). Generality and elegance are filters for the budget (F10). `triangulated` across Fig 4, Fig 13 and the *Society of Agents* essay's room/commons split.
- **D3, verification. `specified`.** Many models buy evidence only at low overlap with separate ground contact (F4, F8). A single dominant model collapses the checking arrangement. Provenance is the only discriminator between convergence and import (F9). `triangulated` across Fig 8, Fig 2 and Fig 10.
- **Overall.** Neither option as posed survives. The figure-licensed shape is: unity of method and substrate, plurality of independently grounded models split by residual, second-order comparison of settled models, an import channel with source gate and shadow test, and no last room. Mode `general` / `specified`, candidate, not `grounded`. Cost if wrong, undoability, and reversal conditions are in `09_watson_answer.md`.
