## 1. A shape is a constraint, not a filler

The canonical figures README states the limit: the framework returns *"what a missing thing would have to look like (a shape, not a name)"*. Fig 9 :32: *"you get a kind of thing, never the answer itself."* Fig 13 :133: *"what it returns is a shape rather than a name."*

From *The Library of Babel* (2026-10-02): *"You can cut your search short if the set of books returned is small and descriptive enough that it provides a general shape of the answer that satisfies your question."*

So a **shape** is the narrowed form of an answer you can state before you can name the particular object that fills it. It constrains; it does not fill. For this question the shape is:

> Unity of method and substrate; plurality of independently grounded models; residuals per slot; split the union; no last room; second-order comparison of settled models; provenance for every import.

That is a shape. Naming a specific unified model, or a specific list of models that economics should keep, would be a name and is not licensed.

## 2. How the shape is obtained

- *Library of Babel*: use the vocabulary already closest to the thing, then *"With each new term gained, you are able to narrow the space by definition and deepen your vocabulary."* Applied here: the closest available terms are "unified" and "plural", and the first recursive move is to notice that they name a bundle.
- *Library of Babel* on bundles: *"Perhaps your problem was a bundle or conflation of several problems masquerading as one? You will need to search all of those problems individually to find their answers and then combine them in a satisfactory way."* This is the essay's own version of Fig 9 :4-5 and it decides the structure of `01`.
- *Library of Babel* on stopping: *"You will know that you are done because the finish line will also be the starting place, as there is nowhere else to search and nothing else that can describe the answer better."* Here the stop arrives when the question has been split into domains that each have a ground and a figure line.
- *Decoding Tacit Knowledge* (2026-11-10): *"When contact isn't possible, the best you can send is a plain description of what you'd expect to see, because anyone can check that against the world."* This is why the outreach material sends the refuter before the theory: a failure condition can be checked without sharing the framework's interior.
- *How a Society of Agents Would Work* (2026-10-22) carries the decisive application to this exact question: *"Keep the rate per kind of situation. A method that is brilliant in one setting must not be drowned by an average across all of them."* It then quotes Fig 1 :19 directly. The essay has already made the move from per-slot residuals to a methodological prescription.

## 3. What licenses a name

Only two things, both outside the shape itself:

1. **The ground settles it.** Formula step 5: *"the ground settles it. Verified only by what pays."* The canonical README: the framework *"stops at the limit of what the available instruments can distinguish, and records the lineage so the stop is honest."* In this question the ground would be a published model comparison showing that one regime closes the slots the other leaves open.
2. **A provenanced import from a frame whose closure differs** (Fig 6 :23, Fig 8 :51-52), with a source gate and a shadow test. Importing "polycentric governance" from Ostrom into economics methodology would be such a move, and it must be labelled as import, not derivation. *The Recursive Self-Improvement Trap* records the convergence honestly: the author arrived at a polycentric structure from agents and biology, Ostrom arrived at it *"from committees and fisheries and forests"*, and *"the structures matched"*. Because the fields differ, that is a real low-overlap corroboration of the shape; it is still not a name for a specific economic model.

*They're Scanning Books When They Should Be Studying Nature* (2026-09-06) sharpens the limit: *"A minimum viable set of six terms is a human's careful guess at reality's compression axes; another sensorium's minimum set need not be those six."* And: *"Declaring the primitives does not make them neutral. It makes the choice visible."* So the shared substrate proposed at D2 is a visible choice, not a discovery of nature's own list. Naming one model as *the* economic model would turn that visible choice into an invisible one.

## 4. Why "the unified model" is the specific forbidden error here

*How Intelligence Actually Works* (2026-09-16) gives the mechanism with the replicator and the video-frame examples. The closing line is the one that matters: *"The recursion converges toward indiscernibility, and indiscernibility is not identity. The projection that no instrument distinguishes from the thing is still a projection, which is why the lineage has to be kept even when the copy is perfect."*

A single unified model that reproduces every other model's outputs would be indiscernible from the plurality at the level of outputs. That would not make it the same thing. The lineage, the per-slot residuals and the provenance of its assumptions are what discriminate. Fig 8 :51-52 says the same in the figures' own voice: *"Convergent derivation and adopted import look identical in the contents. Only the record of where each came from separates them."*

*The Hard Problem is About Source* (2026-08-28) supplies the instrument argument. Metamerism: a colorimeter reads green and *"cannot comment on the source"*. *"If several paths create the same behavior, then behavior is a projection with lossy information (it cannot define consciousness in full)."* And: *"An instrument that can't distinguish these cases demonstrates its own inadequacy, it cannot claim anything substantial about what is being measured or what causes it."* Applied here: agreement between a unified model's outputs and the outputs of many tailored models does not show they are the same model, and *"It was proven wrong not by argument, but by the arrival of a better instrument."* What is owed is a source-discriminating comparison, not a verdict on elegance.

## 5. The preference for unity is a shape held as a PREFERENCE

Fig 5 :62-63: *"A frame searching for an answer of a particular shape holds that shape as a PREFERENCE rather than as a term. It is not bound, it predicts nothing, and it accrues no residual, so nothing here can see it."*

*Decoding Tacit Knowledge* restates it: a frame holds its preferred answer *"as a PREFERENCE rather than as a term", so "nothing here can see it"*. The remedy is a discipline, not a step: write the shape down, bind it, let it accrue a residual. `01` section 0 does this. The professional preference for elegant unified models is therefore not evidence against the shape claim; until it is bound it is not even a claim. It becomes one only when someone states what the unified model would predict that a tailored model would not.

## 6. Unity of method, plurality of rooms: the essays' own answer

*How a Society of Agents Would Work* gives the institutional form directly. A "room" is opened for one problem and closed with it: *"The room composition is a function of the question. When the question changes, the room should change."* Across problems there are two stores, and the second is described as: *"The commons, across problems. It holds only methods distilled from finished rooms."* The ground sits under all of them.

That is the answer to ECN-0092 in the essays' vocabulary: the unity is in the commons (methods), the plurality is in the rooms (models per question), and neither replaces the ground. The source article's caveat is attached: *"Outside research is supporting evidence from other fields. It does not prove the Tether. Where those fields and the figures disagree, the figures govern."*

## 7. Prestige is not action

From *The Recursive Self-Improvement Trap* (2026-11-19): *"Prestige can fund an action. It can give an action a budget to be tried. But it cannot settle an action. Only the ground can settle it."* The same essay: *"The high-prestige person is believed because they are high-prestige. The action is assumed to work because the person is believed. The ground is never consulted."*

For this question, a list of famous economists who endorse unification or pluralism would be the named collapse. The legitimate ask is the opposite: give each claim a budget to be tried against a ground, and record the failure. This is why `03_public_contact_points.csv` contains institutional and opt-in venues, not personal addresses, and why no recipient is ranked by receptiveness.

## 8. My own audit is a simulation, not a ground reading

Same essay: *"the output is only as good as the validator. The agent, or the tool, or the frame, or the theory, cannot validate itself."* And: *"LLMs can quickly simulate an approximate answer/output with the formula found in the architecture post. It will often be better than the generic outputs that you will usually get otherwise too (intelligence is essentially arrangement). However, the truly testable output would need to be derived from the real architecture or working the formula yourself."*

*Arrival of the Fittest: The Tether Architecture* (2026-08-19) adds: *"Because it is a metatheory, it cannot be validated, nor can it validate itself."* And in its demo note: outputs *"could never be verified if the solutions presented were not contrived to fit the formula."*

So `01_figure_grounded_audit.md` is a simulated derivation checked for internal consistency against the figures. It is **not** a test of the methodological claim and must be labelled as such. It cannot settle the question it reports on.
