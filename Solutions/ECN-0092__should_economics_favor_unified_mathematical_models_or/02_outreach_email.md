# Outreach draft: check the output before you decide about the theory

**Use:** one to one, or as a cover note attached to a submission through a public venue in `03_public_contact_points.csv`. **Not** for bulk send.

**Hard precondition:** the stage-1 claim below must survive the recipient's own check before any theory is shown. If it does not, the two-stage offer collapses on contact and nothing further should be sent.

---

**Subject:** One checkable claim about unified versus tailored economic models

> Hello,
>
> I have one specific output I would like you to check against what you already know about economic methodology. I am not asking you to read any framework first, and I am not asking you to agree with anything. If the output fails your check, there is nothing else to look at.
>
> **The output (`general` on the structural shape, `specified` on the economic prediction; not `grounded`):**
>
> The question "one unified model or many tailored models" is a bundle of three different questions that have been treated as one: do models share a substrate, can one model close every question at acceptable cost, and does adding models add evidence or only vocabulary. Because those conditions overlap, the question should be split before it is searched, and the three answers should be kept separate rather than averaged.
>
> What survives after the split: a shared kernel and a shared substrate are mandatory, but a final all-purpose model is not available. Residuals have to be reported per question, because averaging across questions throws away the signal that says where to look. Many models buy real surplus only when they are independently grounded and draw on different evidence. A pile of models calibrated on the same data is one witness stated many times. What should cross between models is methods and head starts, not conclusions.
>
> So the honest answer is neither "unify" nor "multiply". It is: unity of method, plurality of independently grounded models, no last room.
>
> **What I am not claiming:** that any particular unified model is wrong because it is unified; that mathematical unity has no value; that pluralism is automatically productive; that this derivation settles economics methodology. The preference for unity is a shape I have written down as a term so it can be tested, not a verdict I have earned.
>
> **What would refute this, written down before I wrote the paragraph above:**
> 1. A single model closes every economic question with no positive residual left on any one of them.
> 2. A terminal economic frame is produced from which no further question can be asked.
> 3. Two models using the same evidence and the same assumptions are shown to give independent corroboration, or model count alone buys surplus once overlap is held constant.
> 4. Two economic questions require different kernels with no common loop, while both models still settle against the ground.
>
> I wrote those four down before running the derivation. If any holds, I withdraw the claim. A one-line failure is more useful to me than agreement. If you would rather not engage, no reply is needed.
>
> **If it survives, and only then:** here are the public sources. The figures, not my summary of them, are the authority.
> - Verbatim figure text with line numbers (local framework extract): `FRAMEWORK/reference/03-FIGURES_TEXT.md`
> - Figures repository, branch `general-agent`: https://github.com/IsaiahN/tether/tree/general-agent/figures
> - Canonical figures README: https://github.com/IsaiahN/tether/blob/general-agent/figures/README.md
>
> **If you want to reuse the machinery:** the formula is in the figures link. Run it on a methodological problem you know well and can check against a ground. Write down what would refute your result before you run it. The source material itself warns that outputs can be contrived to fit the formula, so a reported failure is more useful to me than a reported success.

## Note on the audience

ICAPE's 2027 call for papers explicitly asks whether heterodox economists are moving toward a unified approach or whether unity would reduce the rich variation of each school. That is the live form of this question, and a public submission route is available. The right first contact is a person or a venue that can refute the shape claim, not one selected for likely agreement. Send the residual and the four refuters; record the verdict before sending anything else.
