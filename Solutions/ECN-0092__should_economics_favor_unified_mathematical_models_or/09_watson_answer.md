## QUESTION

Decide, in effect terms, which modelling regime a discipline should favor. Option U, one unified mathematical framework intended to cover all economic questions. Option P, many models each tailored to a question. What each leads to: U promises compression and cross-domain inference but requires a final closure and averages residuals across slots; P preserves per-slot residuals and independent grounding but can decay into a pile of overlapping models whose union adds nothing. The answer is bound in advance as an ordinary term in `01` section 0: unity of method, plurality of independently grounded models, split the union, no last room. That shape is refutable by the four conditions in `01` section 4.

## DOMAIN CENSUS

R by arity, symmetry and scale, then one line per domain with actors / currency / anchor, partition or union, and order by dependency.

- **R (the residual).** The live per-question gaps between model prediction and data, markets, experiments or outcomes. Arity: many, one slot per question, never one global number. Symmetry: ordered, because a labour model and a monetary model answer different questions and do not commute. Scale: first order inside a model, second order when settled models are compared. Fig 9 names the question a union compound, not one hard problem: a case in two conditions is not a harder case of either.
- **Domain 1, model and question.** Actors: models as bounded closures, variables, assumptions, phenomena. Currency: prediction, description length, falsifiability. Anchor: data, markets, experiments, outcomes. Question type: union, because models overlap in phenomena and share data.
- **Domain 2, methodology.** Actors: methodologists, criteria such as generality, simplicity, elegance, schools. Currency: standards of theory choice. Anchor: the success of models at settling slots, never preference for a style. Question type: partition of criteria into budget filters and verdict witnesses. The preference for unity has no anchor and is not a domain; it is handled as a bound term.
- **Domain 3, verification.** Actors: independent research groups, replications, model comparison exercises. Currency: agreement versus residual. Anchor: the real economy, never agreement among modellers. Question type: union only where closures differ; otherwise one frame wearing two names.
- **Order by dependency.** Refuse the terminal frame before minting a unified one; settle each first-order model against its own ground before the second-order comparison; describe the residual before naming a frame.

Predicts structured pluralism rather than monism; corroborates, but the sentence is repeated from ICAPE, so overlap with the ICAPE row is high and the two count as one witness.
- **Mathematics / computation.** Wolpert and Macready as reported by Wikipedia contributors, "No free lunch in search and optimization" (2026): "any two algorithms are equivalent when their performance is averaged across all possible problems." Also "improvement of performance in problem-solving hinges on using prior information to match procedures to problems." Predicts that no one regime dominates across all problems; corroborates by analogy. Caveat recorded from the same page: the result does not apply where the search space has structure to exploit, so this is not a proof against unification in every field. Overlap: low.
- **Philosophy of science.** Roman Frigg and Stephan Hartmann, "Models in Science", Stanford Encyclopedia of Philosophy (first published 2006, substantive revision 2025): "there is no such thing as a perfectly faithful scale model; faithfulness is always restricted to some respects." Predicts that no single model is faithful in all respects; corroborates. - **Physics.** Wikipedia contributors, "Effective field theory" (2026): an effective theory "includes the appropriate degrees of freedom to describe physical phenomena occurring at a chosen length scale or energy scale, while ignoring substructure and degrees of freedom at shorter distances." Predicts scale-relative models in the most unified science; corroborates the no-final-frame shape. Overlap: low.
- **Mathematics / economics.** Focardi and Fabozzi and Velupillai as reported by Wikipedia contributors, "Unreasonable ineffectiveness of mathematics" (2026): "The science of economics does not study immutable laws of nature but the complex human artefacts that are our economies and our financial markets" and "models can only be moderately accurate." ## VERDICT (from the figures)

Neither option as posed survives. The question is a union compound and must be split. At D1, no final unified economic model is available and residuals must stay per slot. At D2, the kernel and substrate are unified while models and rooms are plural; the preference for unity is an unbound term until written down as a prediction; generality and elegance are budget filters, never verdicts. At D3, many models buy evidence only at low overlap with separate ground contact, and provenance alone discriminates convergence from import. Comparing settled models is legal second-order composition, with one live strain over ⋛'s operand type. The result is `general` / `specified`, candidate, not `grounded`. The strongest single outreach point is canonical: there is no last room, so no final unified economic model.

- **cost if wrong:** bounded and locatable. The packet states its mode, registers four refuters before the conclusion and records the lineage, so an error is findable rather than invisible. The real cost is spending the independence of outside readers by sending a conclusion instead of a generator; Fig 2 :42 says *"Buy independence in proportion to the cost of being wrong."* That cost is avoidable by leading with the split, the shape claim and the refuters.
- **undoable:** yes. The shape can be withdrawn without touching the figure text or the world sources. No primitive has to be removed, and a sent packet can be superseded by a corrected one. The functional-group reading in `06_isomers_and_order.md` already replaces the stronger isomer claim where the evidence does not support it.
- **reversed by:** a ground reading, not agreement. A single model with no positive live residual on any slot would break the per-slot claim; a terminal economic frame or a seventh primitive would break the no-final-room claim; a controlled comparison in which model count buys surplus at fixed overlap, or two same-evidence models giving independent corroboration, would break the independence claim; two economic questions with no common loop would break unity of method. A source-discriminating comparison that separates convergent derivation from imported assumptions would clear the provenance question. Until one of these fires, the verdict stands as `candidate`.
