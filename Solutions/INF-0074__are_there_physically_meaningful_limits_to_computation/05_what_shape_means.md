# What "shape" means for this question

Canonical reading from the eight essays, with the governing figure lines. Mode: `general` on the reading, `specified` where it bears on INF-0074.

**Sources opened directly this session (2026-11-23):** *The Library of Babel: Locating The Book of Books* (Oct 2 2026), *The Recursive Self-Improvement Trap* (Nov 19 2026), *The Hard Problem is About Source* (Aug 28 2026), *Decoding Tacit Knowledge* (Nov 10 2026), *They're Scanning Books When They Should Be Studying Nature* (Sep 6 2026), *How Intelligence Actually Works* (Sep 16 2026), all by Isaiah N at adventuresinml.substack.com. *Arrival of the Fittest: The Tether Architecture* is represented by its figure text in the local framework files. *How a Society of Agents Would Work* was not opened this session and is not quoted here. ## 1. A shape is a constraint, not a filler

The figures README states the limit: the framework returns what a missing thing would have to look like, a shape and not a name. Fig 9 :32: "you get a kind of thing, never the answer itself." Fig 13 :135: "It cannot say which primitive is missing, because naming it would mean already holding it."

From *The Library of Babel*: "you can cut your search short if the set of books returned is small and descriptive enough that it provides a general shape of the answer that satisfies your question."

So a **shape** is the narrowed form of an answer you can state before you can name it. It constrains; it does not fill. For this question, "the reach is bounded by what is held, and only an import moves the wall" is a shape. "The maximum is 6x10^33 operations per second per joule" is a name, and it comes from physics, not from the framework. The Tether can say that a wall of some kind exists; the numerical walls are imports from the physical frame, recorded as such.

## 2. How a shape is obtained

- *Library of Babel*: use the vocabulary you already have closest to the thing, look for recurring new terms that overlap with it, recurse. "With each new term gained, you narrow the space and deepen your vocabulary."
- Fig 6 :30: "outward: does another frame already hold it? The description travels. Fails by being sharpened into terms only we can read. Cast it in effect terms." Fig 9 :30-31: "your words are borrowed too, so describe the effect rather than the cause."
- *Decoding Tacit Knowledge*: "When contact isn't possible, the best you can send is a plain description of what you'd expect to see, because anyone can check that against the world." This is why `08_expert_verification_packet.md` asks what an expert would expect to see if a wall existed, before showing the framework.
- *Library of Babel* on stopping: you know you are done when "the finish line will also be the starting place", and the paradox is that by then you have "composed the answer through the process of locating it."

## 3. What licenses a name

Only two things, both outside the shape itself:

1. **The ground settles it.** `THE_FORMULA.md` step 5: "the ground settles it. Verified only by what pays. A gate is not the ground." For INF-0074, an instrument that discriminates whether the six-term atom set is a compression guess or nature's own list would name something; none was run.
2. **A provenanced import from a frame whose closure differs** (Fig 6 :23, Fig 8 :50-52), with a source gate and a shadow test. Landauer's kT ln2, the quantum speed limit, and the Bekenstein bound are exactly such imports, and each is credited to its field.

*They're Scanning Books* sharpens the second case: "The real import is not a new term in the existing space. It is a new dimension of the space." And: "A minimum viable set of six terms is a human's careful guess at reality's compression axes; another sensorium's minimum set need not be those six." And: "Declaring the primitives does not make them neutral. It makes the choice visible, which is worth a great deal, because a visible choice can be revised and an emergent one cannot. But visibility is not neutrality, and revision is still bounded by what can be conceived to revise toward."

So the Tether wall is a visible choice of primitives, not a discovery of nature's own list. Calling it a physical law would turn a visible choice into an invisible one.

## 4. The shape of a limit, specifically

Three figure lines fix it:

- Fig 8 :32: "Closure is idempotent. Composing inside a closed set never leaves it, so no search, however long, adds an atom." The shape of the wall: same atoms, same wall, more of the space visited.
- Fig 13 :115: "The set is closed. The arrangements are not. That gap is why import exists." The shape of what moves it: a new atom from a closure that differs.
- Fig 12 :125: "A composition costs in two currencies, and they do not add." The shape of the shortfall: a resource accounting cannot see the action cost of finding out whether a composition holds.

From *How Intelligence Actually Works*, the halting case is the same shape at the formal stratum: "you can't decide from inside whether the computation ends. You need to run it, or step outside it." And the simulation caveat: "Because it is a metatheory, it cannot be validated, nor can it validate itself."

So the shape returned by the Tether for INF-0074 is: **a boundary between the budget and the wall, indexed to what a frame holds, crossed only by a provenanced import, and reportable as unreached rather than silenced.** It does not return a fourth resource to add to the trio.

## 5. Why several limits give the same reading, and why that matters

*The Hard Problem is About Source* gives the mechanism. Metamerism: an instrument reads green and "cannot comment on the source." The essay's general form: "If several paths create the same behavior, then behavior is a projection with lossy information." And: "An instrument that can't distinguish these cases demonstrates its own inadequacy." It was the spectrometer, not an argument, that resolved the case.

This bears directly on INF-0074. A measured bound on operations per joule, a bound on erasure heat, and a bound on bits per bounded region can all be read as "a limit on computation." The reading does not determine the source. One may be a budget in disguise (energy), one a logical/physical irreversibility distinction (D2), one a consequence of gravity plus thermodynamics (D4), and one a structural closure (D6). Naming the source of the shared reading is the metamerism error. What is owed is a discriminator, not a name.

## 6. Prestige is not action

From *The Recursive Self-Improvement Trap*: "Prestige must never be allowed to validate an action directly." And: "Prestige can fund an action. It can give an action a budget to be tried. But it cannot settle an action. Only the ground can settle it. The Ground Maintainer must be blind to prestige."

A list of famous limits assembled to make "yes, there are limits" feel inevitable is the named collapse, not corroboration. The legitimate ask is the opposite: give the shape a budget to be tried, ask what would refute it, and let an instrument settle it. The public contact points in `03_public_contact_points.csv` are venues, not endorsements.

## 7. My own audit is a simulation, not a ground reading

Same essay: "LLMs can quickly simulate an approximate answer/output with the formula found in the architecture post." And: "the truly testable output would need to be derived from the real architecture or working the formula yourself." From *How Intelligence Actually Works*: "indiscernibility is not identity." So `01_figure_grounded_audit.md` and this folder are simulated derivations checked for internal consistency against the figures. They are **not** tests of the claim and must be labelled as such. They cannot settle anything they report on.

## 8. Correction to my own §3 (kept visible)

I initially wrote "the shape, not a name" rule as a ban on naming any bound. That is too broad. The figures README phrase governs a **missing** primitive, one not held in Γ. The physical bounds in D2 to D4 are names of quantities already in the physics library, imported into the computation frame. Naming them is provenance, not the invention of a new primitive. What remains forbidden is claiming that the import moves the Tether's wall merely by being named (Fig 6 :25: "An import adds no atom to the world") or naming a seventh primitive for the six-term set (Fig 13 :135). Both claims are now recorded, so neither is quietly lost.
