# Expert verification packet: the Tether on limits to computation

**What this is:** a claim in a form a physicist, computer scientist or philosopher of computation can check. Mode, derivation and refuters are stated before the conclusion.

**Mode: `specified` on the wall and per-domain claims, `general` on the structural distinction, `gap` on the physical instantiation of the closure wall. Not `grounded`.** This is a shape and a route, not a numerical result. Simulated derivation checked for internal consistency against the figures (`05_what_shape_means.md` §7), not a ground reading.

## 1. The claim, in one paragraph

There is a physically meaningful limit to computation beyond time, memory and energy, but it is not a bigger resource. The trio are budgets: quantities you can have more of. A closure wall is a different kind of limit: given a held set of primitives, composing inside the closure never leaves it, so no amount of search at fixed atoms adds a new primitive, and only an import from a frame whose closure differs moves the wall. Landauer's erasure cost, quantum speed limits and entropy bounds are real and fundamental but are mostly couplings of the trio's own units, with two genuinely distinct contents, the difference between logical and thermodynamic irreversibility and the area-scaling of information density. Godel, Turing and Chaitin show underivability in principle, not a measured physical barrier. The framework returns this shape and its refuters, not a fourth constant and not a name; whether the closure wall is physical or a notation is left open until an instrument discriminates it.

## 2. Three checkable sub-claims

### C1 — framework-internal; checkable by reading Figures 8 and 13

- **Statement:** a categorical limit exists that more time, memory or energy cannot cross. Search inside a closure visits more of the same space; it does not move the wall.
- **Source, verbatim:** Fig 8 :32 "Closure is idempotent. Composing inside a closed set never leaves it, so no search, however long, adds an atom." And Fig 13 :115 "The set is closed. The arrangements are not. That gap is why import exists."
- **Refuter:** derive a composition inside closure(Γ) that adds an atom; or show reach is a monotone function of time, memory and energy at fixed atoms; or measure a physical process leaving its closure by search alone with "outside" defined before the run.

### C2 — physics-facing; checkable against the three fundamental bounds

- **Statement:** Landauer, Margolus-Levitin/Bremermann and Bekenstein are isomers over a shared atom set (same atoms, different bond, different substance), and are couplings of the trio's units except for two distinct contents. Fig 12 :117: "Same atoms, different bond, different substance. The difference is invisible in the formula and decisive in the behaviour." Fig 12 :125: "A composition costs in two currencies, and they do not add."
- **What to check:** kT ln2 is an energy, finite-time erasure couples to time, quantum speed limits are operations per second per joule, and the Bekenstein bound is bits per bounded area. Does any of these require an atom the others do not (gravity and horizon area are the live case)?
- **Refuter:** show that logical and thermodynamic irreversibility are the same property, which removes D2's distinct content; or show description cost and action cost reduce to a single unit; or show D4 needs a genuinely irreducible seventh atom, which reduces the isomer claim to a functional group.

### C3 — framework-internal; checkable by reading Figures 8, 2 and 13

- **Statement:** formal undecidability is underivable in principle and is not a physical barrier without an import; the physical instantiation of the closure wall is a `gap`, not a verdict.
- **Source, verbatim:** Fig 8 :40 "Underivable-in-principle and unreachable-at-this-budget are different facts". Fig 2 :29 "An anchor is whatever settles the question and does not change its answer because of what any frame thinks." Fig 13 :135 "It cannot say which primitive is missing, because naming it would mean already holding it."
- **Refuter:** a proven bridge from a formal undecidability result to a measured physical bound (moves D5 to `grounded`); or an instrument showing the six-term set is nature's compression axes, or a proof it is only a notation (collapses D6 to a category error and withdraws the physical claim).

## 3. What is explicitly not claimed

- Not a fourth constant, a maximum operations-per-second value, or a ranking of which bound is "the" limit.
- Not that Godel's theorem stops a physical computer.
- Not that the six-term substrate is nature's own list. It is a visible guess, and declaring it makes the choice visible rather than neutral.
- Not that reversible or cyclic computation evades the wall. Reversible gates survive their own use, which changes the accounting, not the closure.
- Not that "physically meaningful" has been settled beyond the anchor rule. If it is stipulated to mean "expressible in the trio's units alone", D2 to D4 collapse into D1 and only D6 remains beyond (refuter R3 in `01`).
- Not `grounded`. Nothing here is asserted as settled by experiment.

## 5. What would settle it

A ground reading, not agreement. Three instruments are owed:

1. Does the six-term set correspond to nature's compression axes, or is it a notation? A source-discriminating reading would close D6 or retract it.
2. Is there a measured process leaving its closure by search alone, with the boundary operationally defined in advance?
3. Are logical and thermodynamic irreversibility distinguishable by an instrument, as the metamerism argument in *The Hard Problem is About Source* requires?
