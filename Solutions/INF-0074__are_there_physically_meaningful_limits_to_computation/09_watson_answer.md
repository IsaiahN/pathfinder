## QUESTION

Decide, in effect terms, whether there are physically meaningful limits to computation beyond time, memory and energy. Option A: every genuine limit is a resource budget, so the answer is no and Landauer, quantum speed limits and entropy bounds are relabellings of the trio. Option B: there is a categorically different limit, a closure boundary that more time, memory and energy cannot cross, so the answer is yes but not as a bigger resource. What each leads to: A leaves undecidability as a formal curiosity and flattens six different anchors into one; B keeps a wall, records the missing thing as an effect-shaped gap, and owes a ground reading before calling it physical.

## VERDICT (from the figures)

Yes, with the verdicts kept separate. The trio are budgets. Landauer, quantum speed limits and entropy bounds are fundamental but largely couplings of the trio's units, with two genuinely distinct contents: logical versus thermodynamic irreversibility, and area-scaling information density. Formal undecidability is underivable in principle, not a measured physical barrier. The figure-governed limit that is categorically beyond the trio is closure: no search, however long, adds an atom, and only a provenanced import from a closure that differs moves the wall. Its physical instantiation is `gap`, not `grounded`. This is a `specified` shape, not a constant and not a name.

- **cost if wrong:** bounded and locatable if the refuters are kept visible. The real cost is presenting a frame-internal closure as a physical law, which Fig 1 :27 disqualifies as a frame scoring itself with a quantity it produces. That cost is avoidable by leading with the budget/wall distinction and stating that the six-term set is a visible guess. Fig 2 :42: "Buy independence in proportion to the cost of being wrong." The question's cost is high, so the wall claim must not rest on the framework author's essays alone.
- **undoable:** yes. The wall claim is a shape and can be withdrawn or narrowed without touching any physical result. Refuters R1 to R6 in `01` name exactly which part falls, and R3 to R6 leave the others standing. No physical bound has to be retracted to revise D6.
- **reversed by:** a ground reading, not agreement. A source-discriminating instrument showing whether the six-term set is nature's compression axes (D6), or a measured process leaving its closure by search alone (R2), or a proof that reach is monotone in the trio at fixed atoms (R1), or a proven formal-to-physical bridge (R6). Until one fires, the verdict stands as `candidate`.
