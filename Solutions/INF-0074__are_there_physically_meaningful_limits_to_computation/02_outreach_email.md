> **The output (`specified`, not `grounded`):** there are two kinds of limit on computation, and the question "beyond time, memory, and energy" asks which kind the candidate limits are. Time, memory, and energy are budgets, quantities you can have more of. A closure wall is the other kind: given a held set of primitives, composing inside the closure never leaves it, so more search at fixed atoms does not add a primitive. The figures put it plainly: "no search, however long, adds an atom" (Fig 8 :32). Only an import from a frame whose closure differs moves the wall. This is a prediction of a **shape**, not a measurement.
>
> What I am **not** claiming: that this returns a fourth constant, that Godel's theorem stops a physical computer, or that the six-term atom set is nature's own list. Landauer's kT ln2, quantum speed limits and entropy bounds are imports from physics, credited as such. Their genuinely distinct content is narrower than is usually said: logical and thermodynamic irreversibility are different properties, and information density scales with area rather than volume.
>
> **What would refute the shape claim:**
> 1. a composition inside the closure that adds an atom, or a demonstration that reach is monotone in time, memory and energy at fixed atoms;
> 2. a measured process that leaves its closure by search alone, with "outside" defined in advance;
> 3. an instrument showing the six-term set is nature's compression axes, or a proof that it is only a notation.
>
> I wrote these down before sending this. If any one holds, I will retract the claim. A one-line "this fails because..." is more useful to me than agreement. If you would rather not engage, no reply is needed.

## Two-stage offer, stated explicitly

1. **Stage 1:** send the residual, the shape claim and the three refuters. Ask what the reader would count as a failure. Record their verdict **before** showing any theory.
2. **Stage 2, opt-in only:** if they are curious, offer the three links. The figures, not the summary, are the authority.
3. **Stage 3:** offer the formula as a method to reuse, with the instruction that each user writes their own refuter and states their own mode.

Stage 2 is opt-in by construction. Fig 2 :40: "agreement about the world is worthless when the agreeing frames share an evidence pool." Once a recipient has absorbed the theory, their agreement stops being evidence. Their stage-1 verdict is never pooled as corroboration.
