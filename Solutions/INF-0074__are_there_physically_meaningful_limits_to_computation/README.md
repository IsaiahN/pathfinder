## The question restated as an effect

What we are deciding is what class of thing a "limit on computation" is. Option A: every genuine limit is a resource budget, so the answer to "beyond time, memory, energy" is no. Option B: there is a categorically different limit, a closure boundary that more time, memory, or energy cannot cross, so the answer is yes but not in the form of a bigger resource. What each leads to: A reduces Landauer, quantum speed limits, and the Bekenstein bound to relabellings of the trio and leaves undecidability as a merely formal curiosity. B keeps a wall that only an import from a differing closure moves, records the missing thing as an effect-shaped gap, and owes a ground reading before calling it physical. The verdict below is B, stated as `specified`, with the physical instantiation of the wall left as `gap`.
