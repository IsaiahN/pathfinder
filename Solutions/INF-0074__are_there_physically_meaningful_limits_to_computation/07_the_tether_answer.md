# The Tether's answer: are there physically meaningful limits beyond time, memory, energy?

**Mode: `specified` on the wall and per-domain claims, `general` on the structural distinction, `gap` on the physical instantiation of the six-term atom set. Not `grounded`.** Simulated derivation checked for internal consistency against the figures, not a ground reading (`05_what_shape_means.md` §7).

## 1. The residual it answers

The question asks what class of thing a limit on computation is. The residual is the unstated difference between a budget and a wall, indexed per candidate limit rather than averaged (Fig 1 :19: "The residual is indexed by object slot, not aggregated."). Six conditions share the word "limit": resource budgets, thermodynamic cost, quantum rate bounds, information density, formal undecidability, and reachability closure. They do not share an anchor, so they are a union compound, not one hard question.

## 2. The answer, domain by domain, kept separate

1. **Familiar resources (D1): budgets, and by definition not "beyond".** Time, memory, and energy are quantities you can have more of. They filter what gets attempted. Fig 9 :60: "Use filters for the budget and witnesses for the verdict. Never let a filter hand you a verdict."
2. **Thermodynamic cost (D2): a real physical limit, but not cleanly beyond the trio.** Landauer's kT ln2 is an energy, and finite-time erasure couples to time. What is genuinely distinct is that logical irreversibility and thermodynamic irreversibility are different properties, so a raw joule accounting misses the erasure cost. `specified`, corroborated by experiment, single-source on the figure side (Fig 12 :125).
3. **Quantum rate bounds (D3): fundamental but dimensionally the trio.** Margolus-Levitin and Bremermann give operations per second per joule. They are constant-derived, not engineering caps, so they are "beyond" in provenance and not in units. `specified`.
4. **Information density (D4): a categorical memory limit.** The Bekenstein bound scales with area, not volume, so it is not ordinary memory scaling. It is a fundamental limit derived from gravity plus thermodynamics. `specified`.
5. **Formal limits (D5): underivable in principle, not physical grounds.** Godel, Turing, and Chaitin prove a frame cannot reach its own metaframe. Fig 8 :40-41: "Underivable-in-principle and unreachable-at-this-budget are different facts". Whether that bears on a physical machine is an open import. `general` structural, `gap` on physical instantiation.
6. **Reachability closure (D6): the figure-governed "yes, beyond".** Fig 8 :32: "Closure is idempotent. Composing inside a closed set never leaves it, so no search, however long, adds an atom." Fig 13 :115: "The set is closed. The arrangements are not. That gap is why import exists." This is not a shortage of time, memory, or energy. It is a wall that more of all three cannot cross. The only operator that moves it is an import from a frame whose closure differs (Fig 6 :14, :23).

**So the answer is: yes, but the strongest member of "beyond" is not a bigger resource. It is a closure boundary.** The trio are budgets. The fundamental physical bounds are real but mostly couplings of the trio's units, with two genuinely distinct contents (logical versus thermodynamic irreversibility, and area-scaling information density). The formal limits are underivable rather than physically grounded. The closure wall is the limit that is categorically not a resource. Its physical instantiation is `gap`, because the six-term atom set is "a human's careful guess at reality's compression axes", not a ground-settled list.

## 3. What it explicitly does not say

- Not a new numerical bound, a fourth constant, or a ranking of which limit is "the" limit. Fig 9 :32: "you get a kind of thing, never the answer itself."
- Not that Godel's theorem stops a physical computer. That bridge is an unrun import.
- Not that the six-term substrate is nature's own list. Naming a missing primitive would mean already holding it (Fig 13 :135).
- Not that reversible or cyclic computation evades the wall. Rearrangement adds no atom.
- Not a verdict on what "physically meaningful" must mean beyond the anchor rule of Fig 2 :29. If the asker stipulates a units-only definition, D2 through D4 collapse into D1 and only D6 remains "beyond" (refuter R3 in `01`).
- Not `grounded`. No measurement was taken and no figure line was treated as a substitute for one.

## 4. What would settle it

A ground reading, not agreement. Fig 2 :29: "An anchor is whatever settles the question and does not change its answer because of what any frame thinks."

1. A source-discriminating instrument showing whether the six-term set is a compression guess or nature's own primitives. That would move the D6 instantiation from `gap` to `grounded` or retracted.
2. A measured physical process producing a result outside its held closure without an import, with "outside" operationally defined before the run (refuter R2).
3. A demonstration that description cost and action cost reduce to one unit, which would narrow F7.
4. A proven bridge from a formal undecidability result to a measured physical bound, which would move D5 (refuter R6).
5. A figure re-census admitting that search inside a closure can add an atom, which would kill the wall verdict (refuter R1).

## 5. The honest stop

**Unreached, not unreachable.** The answer stops where instruments cannot distinguish whether the closure wall is a physical fact or a visible choice of primitives. The lineage is recorded so the stop is checkable. Indiscernibility is not identity. A search that finds no figure line naming a physical atom set is not proof of absence (Fig 8 :39).

## 6. One-paragraph version for outreach

> The Tether does not offer a fourth resource to add to time, memory, and energy. It draws a harder line: some limits are budgets you can have more of, and some are walls that more of the same budget cannot cross. Time, memory, and energy are budgets. Landauer's erasure cost, quantum speed limits, and entropy bounds are real and fundamental, but they are mostly couplings of those same three units; what is genuinely new in them is the difference between logical and physical irreversibility and the area-scaling of information. Godel and Chaitin show a frame cannot certify its own limit, which is underivability rather than a physical barrier. The one limit that is categorically beyond the trio is closure: the set of primitives a frame holds is closed while the arrangements over it are not, so no search, however long, adds an atom, and only a provenanced import from a differing frame moves the wall. The framework returns that shape and a refuter, not a constant and not a name. Whether the wall is physical or a notation is left open until an instrument can discriminate it, and that is where the answer honestly stops.
