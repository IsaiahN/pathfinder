## QUESTION

Decide, in effect terms, what the Tether says about the claim that scientific research sometimes challenges unequal power and sometimes justifies or strengthens it. Option A: treat this as one question and answer with a balanced list of historical cases, so that "science is a tool that can be used either way" becomes the verdict. Option B: treat it as a union of two conditions, locate the sign in the relation between an actor, a residual and a reachable ground, and keep the two verdicts separate. What each leads to: A produces a list with no discriminating condition, averages the two valences and spends the independence of anyone asked to endorse it; B produces a checkable shape, written refuters and an honest `gap` on chronology, with named cases admitted as provenanced imports.

## DOMAIN CENSUS

R by arity, symmetry and scale, then one line per domain with actors / currency / anchor, partition or union, and order by dependency.

- **R (the residual).** The bivalent effect of one actor, research, on unequal power. Arity: two valences, so a union compound (Fig 9 :54, :57). Symmetry: asymmetric, because challenge is a unary refusal and justification is a bonded accumulation (Fig 12 :112), so the two cannot be averaged. Scale: recursive across individual, institutional and societal strata with a lossy transform between (Fig 10 :68).
- **Domain 1, the research act / claim frame.** Actors: researchers, instruments, methods, claims, refuters. Currency: residuals and settlement at a ground. Anchor: measurement, experiment, patient outcome (Fig 2 :29). Question type: which operator is in play, refusal or accumulation.
- **Domain 2, the institutional / power frame.** Actors: funders, states, regulators, journals, patent holders, gatekeepers. Currency: budgets, access, standing, enforcement. Anchor: material consequences are nameable but can be moved by the actors in the frame (Fig 2 :33-34), so the non-updating test is the finding. Question type: a moved ground, not a bad intention.
- **Domain 3, the verification / reception frame.** Actors: historians, affected communities, expert outside frames. Currency: agreement and residuals. Anchor: the ground, never agreement (Fig 7 :35). In this simulated run every seat reads the same folder and the same figure extract, so independence is zero and agreement is one pool talking.
- **Order by dependency.** Unbundle the union before searching (Fig 9 :54); describe the residual before naming a frame (Fig 9 :27-28); refuse before minting (Formula step 2); settle at the ground before promoting (Fig 12 :104); add a low-overlap frame before accepting a verdict (Fig 7 :35).

Strain test applied: Bautista's "tool to either oppress or push against injustice" strains against Fig 11 :49 if read as a property carried by an actor. Re-census shows two different objects, a human reading of outcomes and the quantity the figure governs, so the strain is named rather than smoothed (see `01` F2 and `06` Q4). After re-census: two named strains, none hidden.

## VERDICT (from the figures)

The sign is not carried by research. It is set by the residual and by whether the ground can be moved. Challenge is a refusal (¬, not a bond) paid for by a reachable ground and checked by a low-overlap frame. Justification is accumulation in a shared evidence pool where the categories rank people and the anchor has been pushed by the interest it appears to check. The two halves are a union and are never averaged. D1 and D2 are `triangulated`; D3 is `triangulated` but weak in this simulation because all seats share an evidence pool; the chronology is `gap` at the figure level and is supplied by world sources as provenanced imports. This is `general` and `specified`, ungrounded, candidate.

- **cost if wrong:** bounded but real. If valence is in fact carried by a stable method property, the whole residual-dependent shape collapses. The larger practical cost is spending the independence of outside readers by sending a list of famous cases and asking for endorsement rather than sending a refutable shape (Fig 2 :42 "Buy independence in proportion to the cost of being wrong."). Both costs are avoidable: the first is refutable by the tests in `01` §4, the second by leading with the refuter.
- **undoable:** yes. The shape can be withdrawn without touching the world-source ledger or the domain census. No primitive and no archive has to be discarded. A sent packet can be superseded by a corrected one, and the retraction table in `01` §6 keeps the earlier readings visible.
- **reversed by:** a ground reading, not agreement. A method property that predicts valence across all residuals (breaks Fig 11 :49); open records showing an anchor that did not move when pushed (breaks Fig 2 :33-34 and Fig 10 :93); a single closed room settling a high-stakes case correctly without an outside frame (breaks Fig 7 :35); a source-discriminating instrument that survives changing funder and venue (settles the instrument question). Until one of these fires, the verdict stands as `candidate`.
