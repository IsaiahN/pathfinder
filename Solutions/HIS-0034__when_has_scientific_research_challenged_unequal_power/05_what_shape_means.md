## 1. A shape is a constraint on an answer, not the answer

The canonical usage rule says the framework returns "what a missing thing would have to look like (a shape, not a name)." Fig 9 :32: "you get a kind of thing, never the answer itself". Fig 13 :134-135 says a system with a closure can answer decidability before it searches, "and what it returns is a shape rather than a name. So the system can report that it lacks something rather than producing an answer anyway."

From *The Library of Babel*, read directly: "you can cut your search short if the set of books returned is small and descriptive enough that it provides a general shape of the answer that satisfies your question."

For HIS-0034 this means the deliverable is **not** a list of periods, institutions or famous controversies. It is the constraint any such list must satisfy: *the sign of the research effect is set by the residual and by whether the ground can be moved, not by a standing property of research.* A chronology is a name-like object; the figure returns the shape.

## 2. How a shape is obtained: proximity, then recursion

*The Library of Babel*, read directly: "With each new term gained, you are able to narrow the space by definition and deepen your vocabulary." The method is to start from vocabulary you already hold that is closest to the thing you are trying to describe, find recurring new terms that overlap or correlate with it, and recurse.

Fig 6 :30: "The description travels. Fails by being sharpened into terms only we can read. Cast it in effect terms." Fig 9 :30-31: "your words are borrowed too, so describe the effect rather than the cause."

For this question, the borrowed words are "science", "power", "bias", "objectivity", "impact". Cast into effect terms they become: *a research act, a residual burden, a ground that can or cannot be moved, and a refusal that is or is not admitted.* That is the vocabulary shift the audit performs in §1 before any figure line is chosen.

## 3. What licenses a name, and why the chronology is `gap`

Only two things license naming, both outside the shape itself:

1. **The ground settles it.** Formula step 5: "the ground settles it. Verified only by what pays. A gate is not the ground." The canonical README says the framework "stops at the limit of what the available instruments can distinguish, and records the lineage so the stop is honest."
2. **A provenanced import from a frame whose closure differs** (Fig 6 :23, Fig 8 :50-52), with a source gate and a shadow test.

The dated "when" of this question is supplied by historians and by the public-health and environmental-justice literature in `10_source_ledger.csv`. Those are imports from other frames. They are admitted with provenance and overlap recorded; they are not derived from the figures. At the figure level the chronology is `gap`. "Unreachable is a verdict here, not a silence" (Fig 13 :135).

## 4. The instrument question: source versus reading

*The Hard Problem is About Source*, read directly: "If several paths create the same behavior, then behavior is a projection with lossy information (it cannot define consciousness in full)." And: "An instrument that can't distinguish these cases demonstrates its own inadequacy, it cannot claim anything substantial about what is being measured or what causes it."

This is the sharpest essay for HIS-0034. A journal impact factor, a citation count, a consensus statement and a peer-reviewed stamp are all **readings**. They do not comment on the source. A result can be published by a prestigious venue and still have been produced under a funding arrangement that moved the ground. What is owed is a source-discriminating instrument: open funding records, replicability, and an outside frame that did not share the evidence pool. The spectrometer, not an argument, resolved the colour case; the equivalent here is an instrument that discriminates "this result would have been reached the same way under a different funder" from "it would not."

## 5. Contact, and why agreement inside one room cannot check this question

*Decoding Tacit Knowledge*, read directly: "When contact isn't possible, the best you can send is a plain description of what you'd expect to see, because anyone can check that against the world." The same essay warns that shared pretext makes agreement worthless as a check, and that two people who only check against each other drift together without noticing.

That is exactly Fig 2 :20-21 ("so the agreement is the pool talking, not two frames confirming each other") and Fig 7 :35 ("A room's agreement is variation, not a verdict. Selection between rooms is where the ground votes."). For this question the outside frame is the affected community, not another historian of the same archive. Chowkwanyun's review of community-based participatory research, read this session, is the world witness for that structural claim.

## 6. My own derivation is a simulation, not a ground reading

*The Recursive Self-Improvement Trap*, read directly: "the truly testable output would need to be derived from the real architecture or working the formula yourself." The same essay states the prestige rule: "Prestige can fund an action. It can give an action a budget to be tried. But it cannot settle an action. Only the ground can settle it."

So `01_figure_grounded_audit.md` is a simulated derivation, checked for internal consistency against the figures. It is not a test of the historical claim and cannot settle it. A list of prestigious historians agreeing with the shape would be the named prestige collapse, not corroboration. What gives the claim a budget to be tried is the refuter in §4 of `01`, not the reputation of anyone who endorses it.

## 7. Why this question is a bundle, and why that matters for the answer

*The Library of Babel*, read directly: "Perhaps your problem was a bundle or conflation of several problems masquerading as one?" This matches Fig 9 :54, "And when the split lands on a union rather than a partition, unbundle before you search again," and gives the reason the two halves of HIS-0034 are kept in separate loops in `07_the_tether_answer.md`. A single answer to "when does research challenge power, and when does it strengthen it" would be a flat formula covering a specific compound, and the compound would not actually be answered.

## 9. Consequence for the answer

The shape that survives is relational and instrumental: name the residual, test whether the ground can be moved, check whether a refusal was admitted, and require a low-overlap frame. Anything more specific, in particular a dated list of "when this happened", is a name where the framework returns a shape and is recorded as a package supplied by world sources, not by the figures.
