## 3. Wider literature that bears on the question

Three findings in the fetched sources change what an answer to this question can honestly say.

1. **The bivalence is a published finding, not our invention.** Bautista's abstract states that science can be "a tool to either oppress or push against injustice." The figure-level result in `01` does not contradict this; it refines it by locating the sign in the operator and the residual rather than in the tool.
2. **The justification half has a named mechanism in another field.** Eisenberg-Guyot and Eisenberg-Guyot describe false theories gaining currency "not because sound evidence supports them, but because they are useful in maintaining inequities." Read against Fig 2's common-cause collapse, this is a low-overlap witness for the same structural claim. It comes from political epidemiology, not from the framework, so it is genuine corroboration rather than an author quoting herself.
3. **The challenge half is usually a coalition, not a lone scientist.** Chowkwanyun's review shows EJ activists setting research agendas and shaping study design, while the academic partner holds the funding. Fig 11 :61-62 ("The seat's power is asymmetric. Introduction is available; exclusion is not.") governs that asymmetry. A story about heroic individuals misses the habitat.

None of these sources was cited without being opened. Overlap with the figures is recorded per row in `10_source_ledger.csv`.

## 4. Distribution: offer, do not push

Figure-grounded rules:

- **Fig 10 :52** "Bring a characterised residual, never the source." Send the residual and refuter first; the framework interior can only spend the reader's independence.
- **Fig 9 :27-28** "describe it before you go looking, or any frame you pick will seem to fit." Ask what they would expect to see if the shape were true before offering the links.
- **Fig 2 :35** "Alignment is triangulation, not negotiation. A mirror cannot correct you." Do not ask for agreement.
- **Fig 2 :40** "disqualifying for a verdict: agreement about the world is worthless when the agreeing frames share an evidence pool." Their later agreement is never pooled as corroboration.
- **Fig 4 :22** "Only methods cross up. Only head starts come down." Stage 3 gives away a method, not an answer, which is permitted.
- **Fig 2 :42** "Buy independence in proportion to the cost of being wrong." One-to-one contact, no bulk send, no receptiveness ranking.

**Recommended order in a reply:** (1) the shape claim and its four refuters; (2) ask what they would count as a failure and record the answer; (3) only then the world links, with the note that the figures and the published sources, not our summary, are the authorities.

## 5. The two-stage offer

**Stage 1.** Validate the output against the ground. The recipient checks the shape claim without holding the framework interior. Their verdict is recorded before anything else is shown.

**Stage 2, opt-in only.** If curious, read the derivation and the figure text. A recipient who has absorbed the theory before giving a verdict has joined the frame being checked, so stage 1 must already be on record.

**Stage 3, opt-in only.** Offer the eight-step loop as a method to reuse. Each user writes their own refuter and states their own mode before running it, because a frame cannot check the direction of its own question.

**Hard precondition:** stages 1-3 only work if the stage-1 output survives. Nothing in this folder asserts that it has been checked against a historical ground. It has been checked for internal consistency against the figures only.
