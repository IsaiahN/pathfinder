# The Tether's answer: when research challenges power, and when it strengthens it

**Mode: `general` on the shape, `specified` on the per-domain verdicts, `grounded` nowhere.** Simulated derivation checked for internal consistency against the figures, not a ground reading. It cannot settle the question it reports on.

## 1. The residual it answers

The question is a union of two conditions, not one hard problem. Fig 9 :54: "And when the split lands on a union rather than a partition, unbundle before you search again." Fig 9 :57: "a case in two conditions is not a harder case of either." A research act is one actor that appears at two residuals with opposite signs. The question asks for the condition that flips the sign.

## 2. The shape (one, with two loops)

**The sign is not carried by the research.** Fig 11 :49: "An actor's valence is relative to the residual, not a property it carries." Fig 11 :50: "The same actor is a benefactor to one residual and a malfactor to another, so mutualism and parasitism are relations rather than kinds." Fig 2 :36: "An anchor is legitimate when the question is constitutively about it." So "when" is answered by naming the residual and the ground, never by labelling research good or bad.

### Loop A: when research challenges unequal power

1. A held regularity that the unequal-power arrangement composes over is **refused**, not merely replaced. Fig 5 :53: "refuted: a term we hold is wrong; a term is owed that says so." Fig 12 :112: "A refutation is a negation, and that one is not a bond." This is a unary operator, which is why challenge and justification are not isomers (`06` Q1).
2. The refusal is **paid for by a reachable ground**. Formula step 5: "the ground settles it. Verified only by what pays. A gate is not the ground." A published refusal with a moved anchor is a gate, not a settlement.
3. The ground must be one the interested party **could not move**. Fig 2 :30: "If it moves when you push on it, it is not an anchor." Fig 2 :33: "The anchor must not update."
4. A **low-overlap frame** is required before the verdict counts. Fig 7 :35: "A room's agreement is variation, not a verdict. Selection between rooms is where the ground votes." Fig 2 :40: "disqualifying for a verdict: agreement about the world is worthless when the agreeing frames share an evidence pool." In the world literature this is the environmental-justice coalition: affected communities pointing scientists at problems experts had missed (Chowkwanyun 2023).
5. The cast is **enumerated, not composed**. Fig 11 :44: "List everything in contact with the residual, then what is in contact with those, and outward until the cascade stops mattering." "Science" is not a single actor.

### Loop B: when research justifies or strengthens unequal power

1. The same actor **accumulates** instead of refusing. Fig 12 :140: "A vocabulary that cannot refuse can only accumulate." No REFUTED bin is entered.
2. The **evidence pool is shared** with the interest the research appears to check. Fig 2 :20-21: "so the agreement is the pool talking, not two frames confirming each other." Eisenberg-Guyot and Eisenberg-Guyot (2022) state the same structure in another field: false group-difference theories "gain currency not because sound evidence supports them, but because they are useful in maintaining inequities."
3. The **ground has been moved**. Fig 10 :93: "A ground with a reputation to protect is a ground someone benefits from, and its keeper is thereby disqualified." Fig 2 :34: "If it does, panel 3 turns into panel 1: the anchor becomes another frame, everything drifts together, and the drift is coherent, which is why nobody notices." The journal that publishes, the funder that pays and the regulator that enforces are not outside the arrangement when they can be pushed by it. Nature's 2022 audit of its own eugenics publishing is a documented instance of a ground keeper examining its own capture.
4. The **categories themselves carry the power**. Dawson and Lock (2025) show how biomedical research built a normative body "against which everyone else is measured and often found wanting." Fig 11 :51: "No standing list of good actors survives a change of residual." A classification that ranked people was a research product before it was a policy.

### The loops are separate verdicts

Kept apart, never averaged:

| loop | figure verdict | status |
|---|---|---|
| A, challenge | a refusal paid for by an unmoved ground, with a low-overlap frame | `triangulated` (Fig 5 + Fig 7 + Fig 2 + Fig 11; Chowkwanyun as world witness) |
| B, justification | accumulation inside a shared evidence pool with a moved ground | `triangulated` (Fig 2 + Fig 10; Eisenberg-Guyot, Dawson and Lock, Nature as world witnesses) |
| chronology (named years and cases) | not derivable from the figures | `gap` at the figure level, supplied by world sources with provenance |

Fig 1 :22: "Aggregating across slots is how a live signal disappears into an average." A single sentence combining A and B would erase the operator difference.

## 3. What it explicitly does not say

- Not that any named discipline, institution or country is uniformly oppressive or uniformly liberating. Valence is relative to a residual (Fig 11 :49).
- Not that intentions decide the sign. Fig 10 :59: "an interest in the outcome is unavoidable, an interest in the reading is disqualifying." Eisenberg-Guyot and Eisenberg-Guyot make the same point: power distorts science "scientists' intentions notwithstanding."
- Not that more scientists, or more prestigious scientists, settle a case. Fig 2 :35: "Alignment is triangulation, not negotiation. A mirror cannot correct you." Fig 10 :93 disqualifies a keeper with a reputation to protect.
- Not that a published consensus is a ground. It is a reading; *The Hard Problem is About Source* warns that an instrument "cannot comment on the source."
- Not a chronology from the figures. Fig 9 :32: "you get a kind of thing, never the answer itself".
- Not `grounded`. No measurement in this folder settles a historical "when".

## 4. What would settle it: the ground

An instrument that discriminates **source from reading**, not another argument. Concretely:

1. Open funding, publication and enforcement records that let a reader test whether the anchor moved when the interested party pushed it. This fires the F3 refuter in `01`.
2. A case where a high-stakes verdict was correctly reached with two genuinely low-overlap frames, one of them in contact with the affected community, with the verdict recorded before any theory was shown. This fires the F5 refuter.
3. A source-discriminating measure of a research effect that survives changing the funder and the venue. This is the spectrometer analogue from *The Hard Problem is About Source*.
4. Standing falsifiers from `01` §4: a method property that predicts valence across all residuals; one condition predicting both signs without residual-specific grounding.

## 5. The honest stop

**Unreached, not unreachable.** The figures supply the constraint on any answer and the route to checking it. They do not supply dates, names or archival judgements. The world sources supply those, and each is recorded with author, venue, year, quote, field and overlap in `10_source_ledger.csv`. Where sources agree with the figure shape they are witnesses, not derivations, and where several draw on the same evidence pool they are one witness with several names. Fig 2 :40 applies to this folder too: the second seat in `11_audit.md` shares the primary's pool, so its agreement is internal consistency only. Fig 13 :135: "Unreachable is a verdict here, not a silence."

## 6. One-paragraph version for outreach

> The Tether does not give a list of good and bad episodes in the history of science, and it does not say that science is simply a tool that can be used either way. It says that the sign is not carried by the research at all: the same actor is a benefactor at one residual and a malfactor at another, so the "when" is answered by naming the residual and asking whether the ground can be moved. Research challenges unequal power when it refuses a regularity the arrangement composes over and a reachable ground pays for the refusal, with an outside frame that does not share the institution's evidence pool. It strengthens unequal power when it accumulates inside a shared evidence pool, the categories it produces rank people, and the anchor it appears to check moves when the interested party pushes it. The two halves are separate loops, never averaged. The figures return that shape and stop; the dates and the cases come from historians and from public-health and environmental-justice research, recorded with provenance. What would close the answer is a source-discriminating instrument, not another agreement.
