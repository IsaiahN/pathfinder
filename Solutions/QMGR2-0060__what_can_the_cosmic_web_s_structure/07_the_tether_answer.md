# The Tether's answer: what the cosmic web can reveal about dark matter, dark energy and gravity

**Mode: `general` on the structural shape, `specified` on the per-domain verdicts, not `grounded`.** This is a shape and a route, not a new result. Simulated derivation checked for internal consistency against the figures (`05_what_shape_means.md` §7), not a ground reading.

## 1. The residual it answers

One pattern is observed, the web of voids, walls, filaments and nodes. Three hidden things are asked about through it: unseen mass, the expansion driver, and the gravitational law. These are not one question. Fig 9 :57: **"What remains is smaller and it is also categorically different: a case in two conditions is not a harder case of either."** It is a union compound with overlapping conditions, so it must be unbundled before it is searched (Fig 9 :54). Each target has its own anchor, and the verdicts are kept separate and never averaged.

## 2. The shape

1. **The web is a network structure, and its arrangement is the information.** Fig 13 :23-24 names network as a held structure: *"that entities relate, and influence travels"*. Fig 12 :94: **"The arrangement is the substance."** A catalog of galaxy positions is the flat ingredient list; the connectivity, geometry, topology and tidal field are what carry cosmological information. The web is a legal cross-kind composition over space, not a seventh primitive (Fig 13 :74-75).

2. **It constrains the shape of dark matter's distribution and of dark energy's footprint.** Libeskind et al. 2018: *"each of the relevant cosmological variables will leave its imprint on the structure, geometry and topology of the cosmic web"*; voids are *"one of the cleanest probes and measures of dark energy as well as tests of gravity and general relativity."* Van de Weygaert 2014: void shapes are *"highly sensitive to the nature of dark energy"* while their substructure is *"a direct key to the nature of dark matter."*

3. **It does not name a source.** Fig 9 :32: **"you get a kind of thing, never the answer itself."** The web is a many-to-one projection. The inverse-problems and colour-metamerism witnesses show, in low-overlap fields, that identical readings can arise from different sources. *The Hard Problem is About Source*: an instrument *"cannot comment on the source"* and *"An instrument that can't distinguish these cases demonstrates its own inadequacy."* So dark-matter particle identity is a declared `gap`.

4. **The web alone cannot split dark energy from modified gravity.** Kunz 2009: gravity probes only the total energy-momentum tensor, so *"Omega_m cannot be measured"* from that reading alone. Nesseris 2013: geometric probes (supernovae, CMB, BAO) *"cannot"* break the degeneracy; growth data with roughly a threefold improvement is needed. Verdict: the web gives the footprint, a second anchor gives the discrimination.

5. **It supplies a real consistency test of gravity.** Fig 2 :29: **"An anchor is whatever settles the question and does not change its answer because of what any frame thinks."** Lensing (light) and growth (matter) are two frames with different closures ranged against one ground. Fig 2 :35: **"Alignment is triangulation, not negotiation. A mirror cannot correct you."** A divergence between them, if it survives systematics, would be a positive signal of modified gravity, not a silence.

6. **Verdicts by domain:** dark matter existence and distribution = `triangulated`; dark matter particle identity = `gap`; dark energy geometric footprint = `single-source` from the web, `triangulated` only with a second anchor; the DE/modified-gravity split = `gap` from the web alone; gravity = `triangulated` across lensing, growth and local tests.

## 3. What it explicitly does not say

- Not that the web determines what dark matter is made of, or what dark energy is. Naming a source from a reading is the metamerism error.
- Not that void shapes alone distinguish dark energy from modified gravity. They do not; the degeneracy is live.
- Not that "unseen" means "unfindable". Fig 8 :39: **"So a search that finds nothing is never a proof of absence."**
- Not a single "dark sector" answer. Three verdicts, different statuses, never averaged (Fig 9 :60).
- Not `grounded`. No new measurement was taken. The lensing and survey results cited are other people's ground readings, used as secondary corroboration.
- Not a new primitive. Network is already in the six (Fig 13).

## 4. What would settle it - the ground

A source-discriminating instrument, not a further argument. *The Hard Problem is About Source*: the green-reading instrument *"cannot comment on the source"*, and the spectrometer, not an argument, resolved that case.

- **Dark matter identity:** a laboratory detection would move the `gap` to `grounded`; a lensing configuration in which the potential tracks the baryons would remove the need for unseen mass. The Bullet Cluster (Clowe et al. 2006) already does the discrimination for *existence*: *"by deriving a map of the gravitational potential, one can discriminate between these possibilities."*
- **Dark energy versus modified gravity:** growth-rate data at roughly threefold current precision (Nesseris 2013), ideally with independent anchors, would break the degeneracy.
- **Gravity:** a systematics-surviving divergence between lensing-derived and dynamics-derived potentials would be a real signal.
- **Standing refuters from the record:** a web observable that uniquely names a source; a web-only degeneracy break; a genuinely flat arrangement; one observable with one anchor that settles all three.

## 5. The honest stop

**Unreached, not unreachable.** *They're Scanning Books*: *"A minimum viable set of six terms is a human's careful guess at reality's compression axes; another sensorium's minimum set need not be those six."* The six terms and the figure set are a visible guess, declared so it can be revised. The answer stops at the limit of what the available instruments can distinguish, and records the lineage so the stop is checkable. *"Indiscernibility is not identity."* By Fig 2 :42, *"Buy independence in proportion to the cost of being wrong"*: three separate verdicts are worth more than one agreed answer drawn from one evidence pool.

## 6. One-paragraph version for outreach

> The cosmic web does not deliver a single answer about the dark sector, because it is not one question. It is a network structure whose arrangement carries information. From that arrangement we can read the shape of where unseen mass sits and how structure grew, and we can test gravity by checking whether lensing and growth agree. What we cannot do from a many-to-one reading is name the source: the particle identity of dark matter stays open, and the web alone cannot separate dark energy from modified gravity. Those are declared gaps with named instruments, not hidden answers. What the framework offers is the shape any candidate answer must have, five falsifiers written in advance, and the instruments that would close the gaps. The center stays open until the ground settles it.
