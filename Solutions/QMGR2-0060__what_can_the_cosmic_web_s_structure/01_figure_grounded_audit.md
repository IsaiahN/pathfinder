## 0. The residual, in effect terms

Observed: one pattern, the cosmic web (nodes, filaments, walls, voids; the network of galaxies and matter on scales of a few to hundreds of megaparsecs). Asked: what does this pattern decide about (a) unseen mass, (b) the expansion driver, (c) the gravitational law?

R is the gap between what a candidate model predicts for the pattern and the pattern actually observed. It is indexed by slot, never averaged (Fig 1 :19 *"The residual is indexed by object slot, not aggregated."*). The slots here are the three targets, and each has a different anchor.

Stated as an effect: **given a reading of the web, which hidden source does the reading discriminate, which does it only constrain in shape, and which remains unreachable until a different instrument exists?** An answer would look like a per-domain verdict with its own anchor and its own refuter, not one blended answer.

Binding the sought-for shape as an ordinary term before the run (Formula step "Direction"): the shape is *"a network structure over space whose arrangement carries cosmological information, constraining distribution and growth while leaving source identity open."* This is written down here, before the findings, so it can be refuted rather than being an invisible preference.

## 1. Domain census (before choosing any figure line)

**R by arity, symmetry, scale.** Arity: three targets inferred from one pattern, so a **union compound** of overlapping conditions, not one hard question (Fig 9 :57 *"a case in two conditions is not a harder case of either"*; :55 *"A bundled problem is usually a union of overlapping conditions, not a list of separate ones."*). Symmetry: the pattern is statistically homogeneous and isotropic, and the map from sources to pattern is **many-to-one**, so it is lossy and non-invertible; it is not a symmetric join of the three targets. Scale: the web is the largest scale; halos are sub-web; dark energy is background/global; gravity is the law at all scales. The residual crosses scales, so the membrane and round trip govern (Fig 4).

**Partition or union.** Union, not partition. The conditions overlap: dark matter and gravity (unseen mass is inferred through gravity), dark energy and gravity (modified gravity mimics dark energy), dark matter and the web (baryons trace the mass). An overlap is a compound with its own loop, so the targets must be unbundled before searching (Fig 9 :54 *"And when the split lands on a union rather than a partition, unbundle before you search again."*). The web is the shared reading across all three, which is why a single question appears to bundle them.

**Order by dependency (Fig 3).** Perception (we can see the web) → vocabulary (we hold network, gradient, spectrum) → objective (which target is being decided) → planning → learn and carry. The chain characteristically breaks at the middle link, the one with no obvious instrument: *"having terms for what a situation offers and being able to say what would count as progress"* (Fig 3 :25-26). Here that is the missing term mapping a web pattern to one specific source. A reading taken below the break is a reading of nothing (Fig 3 :22-23).

**Habitat, enumerated not composed (Fig 11 :43-44):** *"The habitat is enumerated, never composed. List everything in contact with the residual, then what is in contact with those, and outward until the cascade stops mattering."* In contact with this residual: surveys, lensing maps, supernova distances, BAO, CMB, simulations, modified-gravity models, laboratory detectors. The last is second-order contact and is exactly the missing source-discriminating instrument.

**Strain test.** If I try to make Fig 13's cross-kind composition rule govern "which source produced this pattern," it strains: Fig 13 governs composition of the six substrate terms, not inference from a reading to a source. Re-census: source discrimination is governed by Fig 9 (shape, not the answer), Fig 6 (instruments built from prior readings), and the source/provenance rule. With that re-census, no strain remains. ## 3. Findings

### F1 - The web is a network structure, and its arrangement is the information (general, triangulated)

Fig 13 :23-24 names the structure:

> **"network"** and **"that entities relate, and influence travels"**

Fig 12 :94: **"The arrangement is the substance."** Fig 12 :5: **"A set of ingredients is not one. The bond is the rest of the information, and it is the half nothing was recording."**

So what the web reveals is carried by the *arrangement*: connectivity, geometry, topology, the tidal field. A catalog of galaxy positions alone is the flat formula; the web is the bonded molecule. Fig 13 :74: **"Everything else is a composition across the two kinds, never within one."** The web is a structure (network, plus gradient flows and a spectrum of fluctuations) composed over the state space, not a new seventh primitive. Fig 13 :124-125: *"Smoothness, symmetry, chaos and discreteness are each what a particular composition leaves behind, and none is a seventh member."*

- Mode: `general` (structural), low-overlap triangulation between Fig 13 (substrate kinds) and Fig 12 (bond pricing).
- Refuter: exhibit a pattern in which the arrangement carries no information beyond the ingredient catalog (a genuinely flat case). If that holds for the web, F1 fails.
- World corroboration: Libeskind et al. 2018 and Tojeiro & Kraljic 2025 both describe the web as a classification into environments whose structure, geometry and topology respond to cosmological parameters (see §8). Same field pool, one witness.

### F2 - A reading constrains shape but cannot name its source (general, triangulated, low overlap)

Fig 9 :32: **"you get a kind of thing, never the answer itself."** Fig 9 :30-31: **"your words are borrowed too, so describe the effect rather than the cause."** The canonical usage rule returns *"what a missing thing would have to look like (a shape, not a name)."*

The web is a projection with lossy information. This is not a figure metaphor: it is the standard structure of an inverse problem. Carreira-Perpiñán (inverse problems / machine learning, low overlap) writes that *"Gauss' theorem shows that there are infinitely many different distributions of matter density that give rise to identical exterior gravitational fields."* Drew and Funt (vision science / computer graphics, low overlap) write that *"The problem of metamerism arises because the mapping from SPD to RGB is many-to-one: there are usually a great many possible color signals that could have resulted in exactly the same set of sensor responses."*

So a web reading can narrow the *class* of sources consistent with it, but several sources can give the same reading. The framework essay *The Hard Problem is About Source* states the same rule in the canonical reading: an instrument that reads green *"cannot comment on the source"* and *"An instrument that can't distinguish these cases demonstrates its own inadequacy."*

- Mode: `general` for the many-to-one claim; `specified` when applied to dark matter and dark energy.
- Triangulated across three genuinely low-overlap frames (Fig 9, inverse-problem mathematics, colour vision). The essays share the framework author and carry no independent weight.
- Refuter: produce a web observable that uniquely determines the particle identity of dark matter or the microphysical nature of dark energy, with no auxiliary assumption. That would break the many-to-one claim.

### F3 - Dark matter: the web reveals distribution and influence, and a lensing instrument settles existence, but not identity (mixed verdicts)

Fig 6 :33-34: **"Nobody specified a telescope from an account of Mars. A wandering point in the sky was already a reading, and every sharper instrument was built on the residual the last one produced."** Fig 6 :34-35: **"The question is not whether a sensor could exist. It is whether anything, at any resolution, is already returning something that fails to resolve."**

Weak lensing is exactly such a proto-instrument: it is already returning a map of the potential that fails to coincide with the baryons. Clowe et al. 2006 (Bullet Cluster) state that *"by deriving a map of the gravitational potential, one can discriminate between these possibilities"* and that an *"8σ significance spatial offset"* *"cannot be explained with an alteration of the gravitational force law, and thus proves that the majority of the matter in the system is unseen."*

Verdicts kept separate:

| DM sub-claim | verdict | mode | basis |
|---|---|---|---|
| Unseen mass exists | triangulated | specified (ground-backed by lensing) | lensing + CMB peaks + growth, low overlap between anchors |
| Its distribution and influence are traced by the web | triangulated | specified | web connectivity and lensing convergence, Libeskind 2018 |
| Its particle identity | **gap** | none | no instrument in this reading discriminates among candidate identities |

The gap is a verdict, not a silence (Fig 13 :134: *"Unreachable is a verdict here, not a silence"*; Fig 8 :39: **"So a search that finds nothing is never a proof of absence."**). Clowe et al. also say the result *"does not preclude non-standard gravity"*, so even the existence verdict is about unseen mass, not about the particle.

- Refuter: a configuration in which the lensing potential traces the baryons with no offset, or a laboratory detection (which would move the identity sub-claim from `gap` to `grounded`, not refute the shape).

### F4 - Dark energy: the web gives a geometric footprint, but the web alone cannot break the degeneracy with modified gravity (single-source via the web)

Kunz 2009 (*The dark degeneracy*) states the structural reason: *"gravity probes only the total energy momentum tensor to show how this leads to a degeneracy for generalised dark energy models. Because of this degeneracy, Omega_m cannot be measured."* Nesseris 2013: *"This degeneracy cannot be broken with geometric probes like the Type Ia supernovae, the cosmic microwave background or the baryon acoustic oscillations"*; a *"threefold improvement"* in growth data would be needed.

The web does contribute real probes: BAO is a standard ruler carried by the web, and void shapes respond to dark energy. Van de Weygaert 2014: *"The shape and evolution of voids are highly sensitive to the nature of dark energy, while their substructure and galaxy population provides a direct key to the nature of dark matter."* Libeskind et al. 2018: *"void regions of the cosmic web offer one of the cleanest probes and measures of dark energy as well as tests of gravity and general relativity."*

So the web narrows the shape (geometry and growth footprint) but, without a second anchor, cannot decide whether the cause is dark energy or modified gravity. Fig 9 :43: *"The verdict tells you to leave. The lookup tells you where to go. Elimination is what you do after you arrive."* The verdict here says leave, toward growth-rate measurements.

- Mode: `specified`. Status: single-source for the web-only reading; triangulated only after the second anchor (growth data) is added.
- Refuter: a web-only observable that separates dark energy from modified gravity with no additional anchor and no fine-tuned model.

### F5 - Gravity: the web tests the law by cross-probe consistency (specified, triangulated)

Fig 2 :29: **"An anchor is whatever settles the question and does not change its answer because of what any frame thinks. A measurement, an experiment, a counter, a market, a patient outcome."** Fig 2 :35: **"Alignment is triangulation, not negotiation. A mirror cannot correct you."**

Lensing (light deflection) and growth (matter motion) are two frames with different closures ranged against one ground. Libeskind et al. 2018: *"The evolution, structure and dynamics of the cosmic web are to a large extent dependent on the nature of dark matter and dark energy. As the evolution of the cosmic web is directly dependent on the rules of gravity, each of the relevant cosmological variables will leave its imprint on the structure, geometry and topology of the cosmic web."* Testing GR in cosmology (2018 review): *"we can also contrast them for consistency"* between expansion/geometry probes and growth probes.

- Mode: `specified`. Status: triangulated across lensing, growth and local tests (low overlap between anchors).
- Refuter: a divergence between lensing-derived and dynamics-derived potentials that survives every systematic. That would not refute the method; it would be a real signal of modified gravity, and would replace the verdict rather than silently bend it.

### F6 - Keep the three verdicts separate; never average (general, triangulated)

Fig 9 :57: **"What remains is smaller and it is also categorically different: a case in two conditions is not a harder case of either."** Fig 2 :40: **"disqualifying for a verdict: agreement about the world is worthless when the agreeing frames share an evidence pool."** Fig 9 :60: **"Use filters for the budget and witnesses for the verdict. Never let a filter hand you a verdict."**

A single number like "the web tells us about the dark sector" averages three different anchors and manufactures an agreement that is really one evidence pool. The correct output is three verdicts with different statuses (triangulated, single-source, gap).

- Refuter: a single observable with one anchor that simultaneously settles all three targets with no auxiliary assumption.

### F7 - The break is at the vocabulary/objective link; the missing thing is an instrument, not more data (general)

Fig 3 :25-27: the middle link *"has neither, so it attracts the least attention and the fewest instruments"* and **"A link with no instrument is not a link that works. It is a link nobody has looked at."** Fig 6 :27: *"Which is why some unreachability is depth and some is genuine absence, and from inside the frame the two look the same."*

We already perceive the web and already hold the vocabulary. What we lack is the term mapping pattern to source, and the instrument that would settle it. Fig 6 :29-30 distinguishes the two tracks: *"inward: is our representation adequate to hold it? The instrument extends access"* and *"outward: does another frame already hold it? The description travels."* Here the inward track is a source-discriminating measurement (a lab detection, a higher-precision growth-vs-geometry comparison); the outward track imports a candidate identity from particle physics with a source gate and a shadow test (Fig 8 :9-13).

- Mode: `general`. Refuter: exhibit the mapping term as a settled term in the library and show what it composes over.

## 4. Pre-registered refuter (written before the conclusion)

Required before any outreach, per the seat-map rule that a falsifier written before the run is a design instrument. The shape claim is refuted if any of the following holds:

1. A web observable uniquely determines the particle identity of dark matter or the microphysical nature of dark energy, with no auxiliary assumption (breaks F2).
2. A web-only measurement breaks the dark-energy versus modified-gravity degeneracy without a second anchor (breaks F4).
3. The web's arrangement carries no information beyond its ingredient catalog, that is, a genuinely flat case (breaks F1).
4. A systematic, systematics-surviving divergence is found between lensing and dynamical potentials that cannot be recovered by one potential (breaks the single-law reading in F5 and would be a positive signal, not a silence).
5. The three targets are settled simultaneously by one observable with one anchor (breaks F6).

A laboratory detection of a dark-matter particle is **not** a refuter of the shape; it moves the identity sub-claim from `gap` to `grounded`, which is the intended way for a gap to close. Until one of 1-5 fires, nothing here may be labelled `grounded`.

## 7. Strains and round-trip gap

- **Fig 13 versus source discrimination.** Dissolved by re-census (§6). No strain remains.
- **The word "structure" is used in two senses.** In cosmology it means the web's pattern; in Fig 13 it means one of two kinds (spectrum, gradient, network). These are the same notion at different levels: the web's pattern *is* a network structure. Recorded as a naming caution, not a strain.
- **The DE/modified-gravity degeneracy is live.** Named, not hidden (F4). It is a genuine absence of a discriminating instrument at this budget, not a proof of unfindability (Fig 8 :39).
- **Round-trip gap `R_T` (measured qualitatively in `11_audit.md`).** The abstraction "the web constrains the dark sector and gravity" loses, on the way back down, the many-to-one limit, the need for a second anchor, the union-not-partition rule, and the requirement of a nameable anchor per domain. Treating the shorthand as identical to the figure set would set `R_T` to zero and would be the dead-reckoning error Fig 4 :30 names.
