## 1. A shape is a constraint, not a filler

The canonical figures README states the limit: the framework returns *"what a missing thing would have to look like (a shape, not a name)."* Fig 9 :32: *"you get a kind of thing, never the answer itself."* Fig 13 :134: *"Unreachable is a verdict here, not a silence"* and :135 *"It cannot say which primitive is missing, because naming it would mean already holding it."*

From *The Library of Babel*: *"you can cut your search short if the set of books returned is small and descriptive enough that it provides a general shape of the answer that satisfies your question."*

So for this question a **shape** is the narrowed form of what the web could tell us, statable before we can name a specific source. *"A network over space whose arrangement carries information about distribution and growth, with the identity of the source left open"* is a shape. *"The web proves particle X"* is a name. The first is checkable; the second is not licensed by a many-to-one reading.

## 2. How the shape is obtained

- *Library of Babel*: use the vocabulary you already have that is *closest* to the thing, then recurse. *"With each new term gained, you narrow the space and deepen your vocabulary."* For the web: the closest held terms are network, gradient and spectrum (Fig 13), plus the survey instruments already returning readings.
- *Decoding Tacit Knowledge*: *"When contact isn't possible, the best you can send is a plain description of what you'd expect to see, because anyone can check that against the world."* This is why the outreach material leads with effects (lensing peaks offset from baryons, void shapes responding to dark energy) rather than with the framework.
- Fig 6 :30: *"The description travels. Fails by being sharpened into terms only we can read. Cast it in effect terms."* Fig 9 :30-31: *"your words are borrowed too, so describe the effect rather than the cause."*
- *Library of Babel* on stopping: *"you will know that you are done because the finish line will also be the starting place"*, and the paradox that by then you have *"composed the answer through the process of locating it."* Here: the shape stops when the available instruments can no longer distinguish among the sources consistent with the web pattern.

## 3. What licenses a name

Only two things, both outside the shape:

1. **The ground settles it.** Formula step 5: *"the ground settles it. Verified only by what pays. A gate is not the ground."* The Clowe et al. lensing map is a ground reading for "unseen mass exists", not for "the particle is X".
2. **A provenanced import from a frame whose closure differs** (Fig 6 :23, Fig 8 :50-52), with a source gate and a shadow test. A dark-matter particle candidate imported from particle physics is an import; it must be recorded as such and debited against independence (Fig 6 :24).

*They're Scanning Books When They Should Be Studying Nature* sharpens what an import can be: *"The real import is not a new term in the existing space. It is a new dimension of the space."* And: *"A minimum viable set of six terms is a human's careful guess at reality's compression axes; another sensorium's minimum set need not be those six."* Also: *"Declaring the primitives does not make them neutral. It makes the choice visible."*

So naming a source from a web reading turns a visible choice into an invisible one. It is the error §4 names.

## 4. Why naming a source from the pattern is the specific forbidden error

*The Hard Problem is About Source* gives the mechanism, and it is the canonical reason for the `gap` verdicts in `01_figure_grounded_audit.md`:

> *"the instrument is speaking only about what it measured, it cannot comment on the source."*

> *"If several paths create the same behavior, then behavior is a projection with lossy information"*

> *"An instrument that can't distinguish these cases demonstrates its own inadequacy, it cannot claim anything substantial about what is being measured or what causes it."*

> *"The answer is unreached until a better instrument exists."*

Applied here: the web (via lensing, clustering, void shapes) is a reading. Dark matter, dark energy and modified gravity are candidate sources of that reading. Several sources give the same reading. An instrument that cannot discriminate them demonstrates its own inadequacy; the correct response is a better instrument, not a more confident interpretation. The two low-overlap world witnesses (inverse problems and colour metamerism in `10_source_ledger.csv`) establish the same many-to-one structure independently of the framework.

This is also why the Clowe et al. result matters: it is not another pattern-match, it is a *source-discriminating* configuration (the baryons and the collisionless component separate during a merger), so it does real work that an ordinary web pattern cannot.

## 5. Q answered: what does "shape" permit us to say about the three targets?

**What licenses a particular hidden source?** Nothing in the framework, from a reading alone. A source is licensed by the ground settling a minted term, or by a provenanced import with a shadow test. Until one of those, the name is the searcher's own contribution and must be recorded as such.

**Does this mean the web tells us nothing?** No. This is the correction I have to make to my own first reading:

- I first wrote "the web cannot reveal dark energy." **Retracted and narrowed.** Van de Weygaert 2014 and Libeskind et al. 2018 show void shapes and BAO are genuine web-based probes of dark energy. The correct claim is that the web alone cannot break the dark-energy versus modified-gravity degeneracy (Kunz 2009, Nesseris 2013).
- I first wrote "network is a new kind revealed by the web." **Refused.** Fig 13 already lists network as one of the six structures. Naming it as a new primitive would break minimality. The web is a composition over space, not a seventh kind.
- I first treated "a better instrument would refute the shape." **Corrected.** A better instrument closes a `gap`; it is not a refuter of the many-to-one claim. The two are recorded separately in `01_figure_grounded_audit.md` §4.

## 6. Two consequences for outreach

**Prestige is not action.** *The Recursive Self-Improvement Trap*: *"Prestige must never be allowed to validate an action directly."* And: *"Prestige can fund an action. It can give an action a budget to be tried. But it cannot settle an action. Only the ground can settle it. The Ground Maintainer must be blind to prestige."* A campaign that gathers famous names to agree with the claim is the named collapse, not corroboration. The legitimate ask is the opposite: give the claim a budget to be tried, and let the ground (lensing, a survey, a laboratory detector) settle it.

**Agreement from a shared pool is worthless as a verdict.** *Decoding Tacit Knowledge*: *"A shared mindset lets two people drift together without either one noticing"* and *"Agreement is the whole point when you're settling what words mean. It's worthless for settling what's true when both sides learned from the same sources."* *How a Society of Agents Would Work* (essay 4): *"Ten copies watching the same thing are one opinion said ten times."* This governs the three-verdicts-never-averaged rule and the no-bulk-send rule.

## 7. My own audit is a simulation, not a ground reading

*The Recursive Self-Improvement Trap*: *"LLMs can quickly simulate an approximate answer/output with the formula found in the architecture post. It will often be better than the generic outputs that you will usually get otherwise too"*; however, *"the truly testable output would need to be derived from the real architecture or working the formula yourself."* LLM output is *"simulacra."*

So `01_figure_grounded_audit.md` and this file are simulated derivations checked for internal consistency against the figures. They are **not** tests of the claim and must be labelled as such. They cannot settle anything they report on.
