## VERDICT (from the figures)

The web's structure reveals the shape of the dark sector and tests the law, but does not uniquely name its sources. Its arrangement carries cosmological information, constrains where unseen mass sits and how structure grew, and supplies a lensing-versus-growth consistency test of gravity. Dark-matter particle identity and the dark-energy versus modified-gravity split are declared `gap` verdicts with named instruments, not hidden answers. Verdicts are kept separate per domain and never averaged. Mode `general` for the shape, `specified` for the per-domain verdicts, ungrounded, candidate. The strongest figure-grounded point is the many-to-one limit: a reading narrows a class of sources and cannot comment on which one produced it.

- **cost if wrong:** bounded and locatable. The corrected packet states modes, refuters and per-domain statuses before the conclusion, so an error is findable rather than invisible. The real cost is spending the independence of outside readers by sending a blended answer instead of three checkable verdicts, which Fig 2 :42 prices ("Buy independence in proportion to the cost of being wrong."). That cost is avoidable by leading with the shape and the five refuters.
- **undoable:** yes. A `gap` is closed by an instrument, not by deleting a term; a refutation is added and the reach falls because "something was added rather than because anything was deleted" (Fig 6 :42-43). The three verdicts and their provenance are recorded so a later reader can locate the error (Fig 10 :62-64).
- **reversed by:** a ground reading, not agreement. A laboratory dark-matter detection closes the identity gap; growth data at roughly threefold current precision breaks the DE/modified-gravity degeneracy; a systematics-surviving lensing/dynamics divergence is a positive modified-gravity signal; a web observable that uniquely names a source, a web-only degeneracy break, a genuinely flat arrangement, or one observable with one anchor that settles all three would each fire one of the five pre-registered refuters and reverse the shape. Until one of these fires, the verdict stands as `candidate`.
