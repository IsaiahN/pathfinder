## DERIVATION

### Q1 - Is the web a composition of the six, or a seventh kind?

- Fig 13 :23-24 names **network** as one of the six structures: *"that entities relate, and influence travels"*.
- Fig 13 :74: **"Everything else is a composition across the two kinds, never within one."**
- Fig 13 :75: **"Two states never bond directly. It is not space-time but space, a structure, time; and two structures join only through a state."**
- Fig 13 :43-44: *"A continuum was the seventh and was removed for failing exactly this: it is a spectrum without gaps, or smoothness over space and time. A generating set must not contain what its own members produce."*

So the web is a **composition over space using network (and gradient flows, and the spectrum of density fluctuations), not a seventh primitive**. To add "web" as a primitive would be to add what the six already produce, which the minimality rule forbids. The observable pattern is a *molecule* of held atoms, settled by surveys, and therefore itself eligible to become an atom for the next level (Fig 12 :104: *"A settled molecule becomes an atom for whatever composes over it"*).

**Verdict:** legal composition. Mode `general`. Refuter: derive a web property that cannot be composed from the six, with no import. Triangulated (Fig 13 minimality + Fig 12 settled-molecule rule).

### Q2 - What is the bond structure inside the web?

Fig 12 :5: **"A set of ingredients is not one. The bond is the rest of the information, and it is the half nothing was recording."** Fig 12 :94: **"The arrangement is the substance."**

The cosmologists describe exactly this. Libeskind et al. 2018: filaments are *"the highways of the Universe, the transport channels along which mass and galaxies get channelled into the higher density cluster regions"*; the web's morphology depends on *"the eigenvalues of the deformation tensor"* (sheets, filaments, clusters, voids for one, two, three, zero positive eigenvalues). Tojeiro & Kraljic 2025: the web is a *"multi-scale classification of the complex and anisotropic distribution of matter"* whose anisotropic nature is *"lost if we focus on the central regions of the cluster alone."*

In figure terms: the **spectrum** (an ordering, so more and less can be said) is the density field; the **gradient** (a difference that can be spent) is the tidal/deformation field driving flows; the **network** (entities relate, influence travels) is the connectivity of nodes and filaments. The two states bonded are space (the region) and, at the next turn, time (the growth history). The web is a chain of cross-kind compositions, not an ingredient list.

**Verdict:** the arrangement, not the galaxy catalog, is the bond-bearing object. Mode `general` for the structure, `specified` for the cosmological reading. Refuter: show a web classifier in which connectivity adds nothing beyond local density (the flat-form failure). The Tojeiro & Kraljic chapter records that this is an open, measured question, not an assumed one: the anisotropic signal is *"a second order effect"* that must be disentangled from density.

### Q3 - Are dark matter and dark energy isomers?

Fig 12 :117: **"isomer. Same atoms, different bond, different substance. The difference is invisible in the formula and decisive in the behaviour."**

As inferred sources, dark matter and dark energy do **not** share the same atoms arranged differently. Dark matter is inferred from mass/lensing/growth; dark energy from expansion geometry. They are different molecules, not different arrangements of one molecule. So the isomer claim fails for them.

The interesting analogue is their **modified-gravity rivals**. Kunz 2009: *"gravity probes only the total energy momentum tensor"*, producing a degeneracy in which *"Omega_m cannot be measured."* Nesseris 2013: modified-gravity models can be constructed that *"mimic exactly the LCDM model at the background level"* so that *"no geometric test, like the SnIa, the CMB or the BAO, can discriminate them."*

That is **not** an isomer case either. Isomers are same atoms, different bond, different substance, and the difference is invisible in the *formula* but decisive in behaviour. Here the two models are different substances that produce the **same reading** on a whole class of instruments. That is the source-discrimination / metamerism case of `05_what_shape_means.md` §4, not Fig 12's isomerism. Confusing the two would be a category error: the remedy for isomers is a better notation that prices the bond, while the remedy for a many-to-one reading is a source-discriminating instrument.

**Verdict:** DM and DE are not isomers; DE versus modified gravity is a metamer-type many-to-one case, not an isomer case. Mode `specified`. Refuter: exhibit two dark-sector models built from the same held atoms under different bonds that behave differently and are distinguishable by a web observable alone; that would restore the isomer reading and break F4.

### Q4 - What order governs the whole derivation?

Fig 3 :20: **"The chain is ordered by dependency, not by importance."** The order for this question:

1. **Perceive** the web (surveys, lensing maps). This link works; it has obvious instruments.
2. **Vocabulary** (network, gradient, spectrum; lensing convergence, BAO, void shape). This link works.
3. **Objective**: which target is being decided, and what would count as progress for it. **This is the break** (Fig 3 :25-27), because no held term maps a pattern to one source. Fig 3 :27: **"A link with no instrument is not a link that works. It is a link nobody has looked at."**
4. **Planning**: choose the instrument for the specific target (lensing for mass, growth rates for the DE/MG split, cross-probe consistency for gravity).
5. **Learn and carry**: record the provenance of each verdict, and the gap, so the next search starts from the residual.

Dependency order also settles gravity before the dark-sector parameters in practice: the inference of unseen mass and of dark energy both presume a gravity model, so a change in gravity moves both. This is why the gravity verdict (F5) is stated separately but is logically upstream.

**Verdict:** the chain breaks at the objective/vocabulary link, so no amount of effort at steps 4-5 rescues it. Mode `general`. Refuter: demonstrate a settled term that maps web pattern to a unique source, moving the break downstream.

## CONCLUSION

The web is a legal first-order composition of the six-term substrate (space crossed with network, gradient and spectrum), not a seventh primitive. Its bond-bearing object is the arrangement, which is why the cosmological literature's geometry/topology/connectivity results are the structural content of the answer. Dark matter and dark energy are not isomers, and their modified-gravity rivals are a metamer-type many-to-one case, not an isomer case. The derivation breaks at the objective/vocabulary link because no term maps a pattern to one source; the remedy is an instrument, not more composition. This rescues the defensible structural reading without naming a source or minting a primitive.

## FALSIFIER (written before the conclusion)

If either (a) a web property is found that cannot be composed from the six with no import, or (b) two dark-sector models are shown to be genuine isomers under Fig 12 with a web-only observable that distinguishes them, then the isomer/section conclusions fail and `01_figure_grounded_audit.md` must be re-censused. A source-discriminating instrument closing a `gap` is not a falsifier; it is the intended way the gap ends.
