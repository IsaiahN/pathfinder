## Headline verdict (from the figures, not from the world sources)

The web is a network structure whose arrangement carries cosmological information. It constrains the *shape* of dark matter's distribution and dark energy's geometric footprint, and it supplies a real consistency test of gravity. It does not uniquely name the particle identity of dark matter or the microphysical nature of dark energy, and the web alone cannot break the dark-energy versus modified-gravity degeneracy.
