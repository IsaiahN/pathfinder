## Two things in the literature that bear directly on this question

1. **The web is an arrangement, not a catalog.** Libeskind et al. and Tojeiro & Kraljic both classify the web by environment (nodes, filaments, walls, voids) and report that the geometry, topology and connectivity respond to cosmological parameters. That is the figure-12 point (arrangement carries information, the ingredient list does not) arrived at independently in cosmology. Cite this to explain why "what the web reveals" is a structural claim.

2. **A many-to-one reading is a standing result, not a pessimism.** The inverse-problems and metamerism sources establish, in fields with no stake in this framework, that identical readings can arise from different sources. Kunz and Nesseris establish the specific cosmological version: geometry alone cannot split dark energy from modified gravity. Cite these together to justify the `gap` verdicts, and keep the corpus note that they are six papers in one broad cosmology pool plus two genuinely low-overlap witnesses.
