# Expert verification packet: the Tether on the cosmic web, dark matter, dark energy and gravity

**What this is:** a claim in a form a cosmologist or large-scale-structure researcher can check. Mode, derivation and refuters are stated before the conclusion, so a reader knows what to look for and what would settle it.

**Mode: `general` on the structural shape, `specified` on the per-domain verdicts, not `grounded`.** No new measurement is claimed. This is a shape and a route, not a result.

## 1. The claim, in one paragraph

The cosmic web is a network structure whose *arrangement*, not its galaxy catalog, carries cosmological information. From that arrangement one can constrain the distribution and growth history of unseen mass, the geometric footprint of dark energy, and the consistency of gravity between lensing and structure growth. One cannot uniquely name a source from a many-to-one reading: the particle identity of dark matter is a declared `gap`, and the web alone cannot separate dark energy from modified gravity. The three targets are separate verdicts with different statuses, never averaged. What is offered is the shape any candidate answer must have, falsifiers written in advance, and the instruments that would close the gaps.

## 2. Three checkable sub-claims

### C1 - the arrangement carries the information (framework-internal + web-structure check)

- **Statement:** the web is a legal composition of the six-term substrate using network, gradient and spectrum over space; its connectivity and geometry carry information that the ingredient list does not. No seventh primitive is minted.
- **Source, verbatim:** Fig 13 :23-24 *"network / that entities relate, and influence travels"*; Fig 12 :94 *"The arrangement is the substance."*; Fig 12 :5 *"A set of ingredients is not one. The bond is the rest of the information, and it is the half nothing was recording."*; Fig 13 :74 *"Everything else is a composition across the two kinds, never within one."*
- **What to check:** is the web a composition over space (network, tidal gradient, density spectrum) rather than a new primitive? Do the observed geometry/topology/connectivity effects survive controlling for local density? The Tojeiro & Kraljic chapter calls that *"a second order effect"* that must be disentangled from density, so it is an open, checkable question.
- **Refuter:** derive a web property that cannot be composed from the six without import; or show a classifier in which connectivity adds nothing beyond local density (the flat-form failure).

### C2 - a reading cannot name its source (framework-internal + inverse-problem check)

- **Statement:** the web is a many-to-one projection. It narrows the class of sources consistent with it but does not uniquely fix dark-matter particle identity or the nature of dark energy.
- **Source, verbatim:** Fig 9 :32 *"you get a kind of thing, never the answer itself"*; Fig 9 :30-31 *"your words are borrowed too, so describe the effect rather than the cause"*; Fig 8 :39 *"So a search that finds nothing is never a proof of absence."*
- **World witness (low overlap):** Carreira-Perpinan (inverse problems) - *"Gauss' theorem shows that there are infinitely many different distributions of matter density that give rise to identical exterior gravitational fields"*; Drew & Funt (vision science) - *"The problem of metamerism arises because the mapping from SPD to RGB is many-to-one."*
- **What to check:** is there a web observable that uniquely determines a dark-sector source with no auxiliary assumption? If not, the `gap` verdict stands.
- **Refuter:** produce such an observable. That breaks the many-to-one claim and closes the gap in the other direction.

### C3 - the three verdicts are separate and the degeneracy needs a second anchor (physics-facing)

- **Statement:** dark matter existence and distribution are triangulated across lensing and surveys; the dark energy footprint is single-source from the web until growth data is added; gravity is triangulated across lensing, growth and local tests. The DE/modified-gravity split is a `gap` from the web alone.
- **Source, verbatim:** Fig 2 :29 *"An anchor is whatever settles the question and does not change its answer because of what any frame thinks. A measurement, an experiment, a counter, a market, a patient outcome."*; Fig 2 :35 *"Alignment is triangulation, not negotiation. A mirror cannot correct you."*; Fig 9 :60 *"Use filters for the budget and witnesses for the verdict. Never let a filter hand you a verdict."*
- **World witness (cosmology pool, one witness):** Kunz 2009 - *"gravity probes only the total energy momentum tensor to show how this leads to a degeneracy for generalised dark energy models"*; Nesseris 2013 - *"This degeneracy cannot be broken with geometric probes like the Type Ia supernovae, the cosmic microwave background or the baryon acoustic oscillations."* Clowe et al. 2006 for the DM existence discriminator: *"by deriving a map of the gravitational potential, one can discriminate between these possibilities."*
- **What to check:** is the degradation of the DE/modified-gravity split with web-only data at least roughly what these papers report? Is the lensing-versus-growth consistency reading faithful?
- **Refuter:** a web-only observable that breaks the degeneracy without a second anchor; or a systematics-surviving lensing/dynamics divergence that one potential cannot recover (this is a positive signal of modified gravity, not a refutation of the method).

## 3. What is explicitly not claimed

- Not that the web determines the particle identity of dark matter or the nature of dark energy.
- Not that void shapes or BAO alone distinguish dark energy from modified gravity.
