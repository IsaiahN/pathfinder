## Round trip gap R_T

The Symbols table defines R_T as the gap between what was sent up and what came back down, `gap(x, (T_E ∘ T_A)(x))`, measuring what the coarser description cannot hold. Fig 4 :30 requires it to be measured rather than assumed. I ran the round trip on the condensation the folder actually uses.

1. **Sent up (x):** the verbatim figure set, chiefly Fig 11 :43-53 and :66-67 (enumeration, cascade, substitution), Fig 9 :55 (union), Fig 1 :19 (per slot).
2. **Abstraction (T_A):** the shorthand carried in `07` §2: a range shift substitutes a habitat, novel interactions are inherited contact relations that cascade, valence is relative to the residual, three outcomes are a union compound, report per slot.
3. **Concretisation (T_E):** a field case, for example an ungulate shifting upslope, a tick vector expanding, a novel predator-prey link.

**R_T is nonzero.** Three things the shorthand holds do not survive the trip back down to a particular case:

- the **identity of the live bond** in that case (Fig 12 :117 and the Operators table require comparison, production or transmission to be distinguished by a test; the shorthand only says "contact relation");
- the **source of the observed decline**, which the bare reading cannot discriminate (the metamerism point of `05` §4); a spectrometer-equivalent instrument is required;
- the **order of refusal before mint**, which a field report that names "no risk because none detected" reverses (Formula step 2; Fig 8 :39).

Conversely, one thing survives cleanly: the recipient habitat must be enumerated per slot outward until the cascade stops, and the three outcomes overlap. That is the residue worth sending. R_T was **not** measured numerically, because no ground reading exists; it is recorded qualitatively as the enumerated set of dropped constraints. Treating "the formula is the condensed version of the metalaws" as identity would set R_T to zero and would be the dead-reckoning error Fig 4 :30 names. The honest form is a lossy condensation with a measured gap, which `09_watson_answer.md` states.
