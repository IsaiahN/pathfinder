**Question:** How do climate-driven shifts in species ranges create new competition, disease risks, or food-web connections?

**Source:** ecology.txt line 48

**Section:** 4. Climate change, disturbance, and ecological thresholds

**Mode:** `specified` on the substantive claims, `general` on the structural shape. No field measurement settles the binding here, so nothing is `grounded`.

**Status:** done

**Registry status:** pending

**Boundary.** Nothing in this folder edits anything outside it. Primary authority is the local figure text at `FRAMEWORK/reference/03-FIGURES_TEXT.md`, cited as `Fig N :line`. World sources are secondary corroboration only and every URL cited was actually fetched on 2026-11-23.

**Restatement as an effect.** A species range edge moves. The old prediction of that species' relations (Γ(b,a)) no longer matches what the new location returns (o′). The residual is the set of relations the old picture missed: a competitor not formerly met, a pathogen not formerly encountered, a predator or prey not formerly connected. R is indexed per species-slot and per interaction-slot, never averaged into one biodiversity number (Fig 1 :19). What is being decided is *by what structure* a range shift generates novel interactions. Option A: treat competition, disease risks and food-web connections as three separate impact categories predicted independently from species traits. Option B: treat them as one contact mechanism over a substituted habitat, enumerated outward, with each outcome's sign relative to the residual. A loses the per-slot signal and leaves the intersections standing; B keeps the slot-level residual and names a checkable shape.

## What is deliberately not here

- No personal emails of named individuals.
- No ranking of people by how likely they are to agree.
- No bulk-send plan.

Three reasons in the figures' own words:

1. **A room does not supply an anchor.** *"Alignment is triangulation, not negotiation. A mirror cannot correct you."* (Fig 2 :35). A list selected for receptiveness is a mirror.
2. **Correlation disqualifies a verdict.** The agreement of models fed the same occurrence records is one evidence pool talking, not two witnesses (Fig 2 :40).
3. **Asking for trust before checking spends independence.** *"A verifier that can reconstruct the claim cannot verify it."* (Fig 10 :42).

Legally, scraping personal emails for unsolicited bulk outreach breaches GDPR and CAN-SPAM style rules in many jurisdictions. That is a practical failure on top of the structural one.

## Honest standing

Every substantive artifact here is a **simulated derivation checked for internal consistency against the figures, not a ground reading.** By the framework's own rule (the Recursive Self-Improvement Trap, fetched 2026-11-23): *"the truly testable output would need to be derived from the real architecture or working the formula yourself"* and LLM output is *"simulacra."* This package cannot settle the ecology question it reports on. It offers a shape, written refuters, and the instruments that would close it.
