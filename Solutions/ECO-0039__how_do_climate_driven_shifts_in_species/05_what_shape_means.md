## 1. A shape is a constraint, not a filler

The figures README states the limit: the framework returns *"what a missing thing would have to look like (a shape, not a name)."* Fig 9 :32: *"you get a kind of thing, never the answer itself."* Fig 13: *"It can say the answer is out of reach with the library as it stands... It cannot say which primitive is missing, because naming it would mean already holding it."*

From *The Library of Babel* (fetched 2026-11-23): *"You can cut your search short if the set of books returned is small and descriptive enough that it provides a general shape of the answer that satisfies your question."*

So a **shape** is the narrowed form of an answer you can state before you can name the particular case. It constrains; it does not fill. For ECO-0039:

- shape: a range shift substitutes a habitat; new interactions are inherited contact relations that cascade outward; each outcome's sign is relative to the residual; report per slot; the three named outcomes are a union compound.
- name: *this* mountain ungulate acquires *this* tick-borne pathogen from *this* resident rodent in *this* valley. That is a case for the ground, not a product of the derivation.

## 2. How a shape is obtained

- *Library of Babel*: use the vocabulary you already have that is *closest* to the thing, look for recurring new terms that overlap or correlate with it, recurse. *"With each new term gained, you narrow the space and deepen your vocabulary."*
- Fig 6 :30: *"The description travels. Fails by being sharpened into terms only we can read. Cast it in effect terms."* Fig 9 :30-31: *"your words are borrowed too, so describe the effect rather than the cause."*
- *Decoding Tacit Knowledge*: *"When contact isn't possible, the best you can send is a plain description of what you'd expect to see, because anyone can check that against the world."*
- *Library of Babel* on stopping: *"You will know that you are done because the finish line will also be the starting place"*, and the paradox is that by then you have *"composed the answer through the process of locating it."*

For this question the bootstrapping went: "new species interaction" (given) -> "novel combination" -> "contact" -> "habitat substitution" -> "cascade" -> "valence relative to the residual" -> "union compound." At each step the vocabulary narrowed until the shape was stateable. The process stopped at the limit where only a field measurement could name a particular link.

## 3. What licenses a name

Only two things, both outside the shape itself:

1. **The ground settles it.** Formula step 5: *"the ground settles it. Verified only by what pays. A gate is not the ground."* The README: the framework *"stops at the limit of what the available instruments can distinguish, and records the lineage so the stop is honest."* In ecology: an observed transmission event, a recorded trophic link, a measured competitive displacement.
2. **A provenanced import from a frame whose closure differs** (Fig 6 :23, Fig 8 :50-52), with a source gate and a shadow test. In this solution the invasion-biology and disease-ecology literatures are imports with a stated source gate (they are cited, dated and quoted) and a shadow test (they explain why trait-only prediction fails for novel interactions). Nuttall's caution that the tick evidence is *"generally lacking"* is recorded rather than hidden, so the import is not oversold.

*They're Scanning Books* sharpens the point: *"The real import is not a new term in the existing space. It is a new dimension of the space."* And: *"A minimum viable set of six terms is a human's careful guess at reality's compression axes; another sensorium's minimum set need not be those six."* The six are a visible guess, not nature's own list.

## 4. The metamerism warning applied here

*The Hard Problem is About Source* gives the mechanism. An instrument reads green and *"cannot comment on the source"*; one green light, or yellow+blue, or RGB, or CMYK can give the same reading. *"If several paths create the same behavior, then behavior is a projection with lossy information."* It was the **spectrometer**, not an argument, that resolved it.

The ecological analogue: observing "a new interaction" does not identify which path produced it. The same observed decline in a resident species could be competition, apparent competition through a shared predator, or a pathogen vectored by the arriver. A reading of "new competition" is a source named from a projection. What is owed is a discriminator (a pathogen screen, a trophic-tracer or stable-isotope measurement, an exclusion experiment), not a label. *The Recursive Self-Improvement Trap*: *"the output is only as good as the validator. The agent, or the tool, or the frame, or the theory, cannot validate itself."*

This is why `08_expert_verification_packet.md` asks an expert to name the instrument that would distinguish the three mechanisms, rather than asking them to agree with the shape.

## 5. Q: Does "shape, not a name" forbid naming the mechanism here? ANSWERED

**No, with a boundary.** The figures README phrase governs a *missing* primitive, one not held in Γ. The mechanism claimed here (habitat substitution, contact cascade, relative valence, union compound) is not a new primitive; it is derived from Fig 11's enumeration and valence rules, Fig 9's union rule and Fig 1's slot rule. What remains forbidden is naming the *particular* species, pathogen or link in a particular range shift, and claiming the derivation settled it. That is a case, and cases are settled by the ground.

**Does the record commit the error the README forbids?** Not as written. `01` §3 F10 and `07` §3 keep the slot for the particular case open. The one place the first draft did commit it was naming "network" as *the* structure of food-web connections; Fig 13 :24 names network as one of six structures but does not select which observed link is the network. That was retracted in the `01` §5 log and is preserved there.

## 5b. Correction to my own reading of Fig 11

I first read Fig 11 as a claim about *intelligence* and about a maintainer seat, and nearly used its capability claim (Fig 11 :39-40, *"capability is a property of agent-and-habitat, never of the agent alone"*) as if it governed a species range shift. Re-census: that claim governs a system with a maintainer. An unmaintained range shift is Fig 11's first panel, *"pure contact, nothing selects, and what survives is what the ground kept."* The parts of Fig 11 that govern this question are the enumeration and valence rules (Fig 11 :43-51), the cascade rule (Fig 11 :52-53) and the substitution/asymmetry rules (Fig 11 :61-70). The correction is kept visible in `01` §5 and `11_audit.md`; it is not silently erased.

## 6. Two consequences for outreach

**Prestige is not action.** From *The Recursive Self-Improvement Trap* (fetched 2026-11-23): *"Prestige must never be allowed to validate an action directly... Prestige can fund an action. It can give an action a budget to be tried. But it cannot settle an action. Only the ground can settle it. The Ground Maintainer must be blind to prestige."* A campaign that gathers respected names to agree with the claim is the named collapse, not corroboration. The legitimate ask gives the claim a budget to be tried and lets the ground settle it.

**You may all agree and be wrong.** Same essay: *"You may all agree and be wrong. You may set your clock to someone else's, who was setting your clock to yours, and be off by many deviations over time. You need ground truth to define where you stand."* This is Fig 2 :35 and :40 in prose, and it is why the outreach file never asks for agreement.

**My own audit is a simulation, not a ground reading.** Same essay: *"LLMs can quickly simulate an approximate answer/output... However, the truly testable output would need to be derived from the real architecture or working the formula yourself"*; LLM output is *"simulacra."* So `01` is a simulated derivation checked for internal consistency against the figures. It is **not** a test of the ecology claim and must be labelled as such. It cannot settle anything it reports on.
