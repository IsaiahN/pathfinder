## Distribution: offer, do not push

Figure-grounded reasons:

- **Fig 4 :22-25** - *"Only methods cross up. Only head starts come down. A recording carried upward looks like knowledge and is a description of one occasion."* Sending the full architecture before anyone has described a residual sends a recording, not a generator. Lead with the shape claim and its refuter; offer links after.
- **Fig 10 :42** - *"A verifier that can reconstruct the claim cannot verify it."* Seat 4 must not hold the interiors. A recipient who has absorbed the framework before being asked for a verdict has joined the frame being checked.
- **Fig 9 :27-28** - *"describe it before you go looking, or any frame you pick will seem to fit."* Ask what they would expect to see if the shape claim were true before giving them the article.
- **Fig 2 :40** - *"agreement about the world is worthless when the agreeing frames share an evidence pool."* A mass send selected for people who already agree produces a convention, not corroboration.
- **Fig 8 :39** - *"So a search that finds nothing is never a proof of absence."* If a reader reports seeing no new interaction in their system, that is a question about their instrument's resolution, not a refutation or a confirmation.

**Recommended order in a reply:** (1) the shape claim and its pre-registered refuter; (2) ask what they would count as a failure and what instrument would resolve it; (3) only then the three links, with the note that the figures, not the summary, are the authority.

## The two-stage offer

1. **Stage 1:** validate the output against the ground. The recipient checks the shape claim without holding the framework's interior.
2. **Stage 2, opt-in only:** if curious, read the theory that produced it. Their verdict on stage 1 must be recorded *before* any theory is shown, and later agreement is never pooled as corroboration.
3. **Stage 3:** use the formula recursively on other range shifts. The formula is a generator, so giving it away does not give away an answer. Each user must write down what would refute their result before running it and label the mode of what they get.

- Stage 1 before stage 2 is Fig 10 :52 - *"Bring a characterised residual, never the source"* - and Fig 2 :45 - *"Frames range against the ground. They do not negotiate with it."*
- Stage 2 is opt-in by construction because Seat 4 must not hold the interiors; agreement after exposure is not evidence (Fig 2 :40).
- Stage 3 sends a method, which Fig 4 :22 permits, but the Formula "Direction" section notes a frame *"cannot check the direction of its own question."* The Library of Babel essay (fetched 2026-11-23) adds: *"You can cut your search short if the set of books returned is small and descriptive enough that it provides a general shape of the answer that satisfies your question."*

**Hard precondition:** the offer only works if the stage-1 output survives. If a refuter in `01_figure_grounded_audit.md` §4 fires, repair or withdraw before sending.
