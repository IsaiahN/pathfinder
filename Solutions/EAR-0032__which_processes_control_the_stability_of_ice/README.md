## What is deliberately not here

- No personal emails of named individuals.
- No ranking of people by how likely they are to agree.
- No bulk-send plan.
- No single global "ice shelf stability" number.
- No claim that the figures name basal melting, hydrofracture, calving, cliff failure or wave forcing as "the" control. The framework returns a shape, not a process name.

Three reasons, in the figures' own words:

1. **Agreement is not an anchor.** Fig 2 :35 "Alignment is triangulation, not negotiation. A mirror cannot correct you." Fig 7 :35 "A room's agreement is variation, not a verdict. Selection between rooms is where the ground votes."
2. **Shared evidence disqualifies a verdict.** Fig 2 :40 "disqualifying for a verdict: agreement about the world is worthless when the agreeing frames share an evidence pool". A list selected for receptiveness manufactures exactly the correlation that makes agreement worthless (Fig 2 :31).
3. **Send the residual, not the interiors.** Fig 10 :52 "Bring a characterised residual, never the source." Fig 10 :42 "A verifier that can reconstruct the claim cannot verify it."

Legally: scraping personal emails for unsolicited bulk outreach breaches GDPR and CAN-SPAM style rules in many jurisdictions. That is a practical failure on top of the structural one.

## Result in one line

The figures settle the **shape** of the answer, not the **list of processes**. They require a per-shelf, per-slot residual over an enumerated contact habitat, a union of overlapping conditions unbundled before search, measurement as the only anchor, low-overlap triangulation, and a declared `gap` where a source-discriminating instrument is owed. The process names that earth science supplies are genuine ground reports, but they are imports into the framework, recorded with provenance, never figure-derived verdicts.
