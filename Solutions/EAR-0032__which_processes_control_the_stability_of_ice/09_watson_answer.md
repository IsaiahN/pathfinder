## DOMAIN CENSUS

- **R (the residual).** For shelf `s` and time `t`, `R_s,t = | Gamma(b,a) - o' |` over thickness, velocity, rift state and back stress. Arity: at least five condition families, so a union compound rather than one hard question (Fig 9 :4 "Split it rather than search."). Symmetry: not symmetric, processes are ordered and coupled; two different processes can yield the same thinning. Scale: shelf-local and hours to weeks for disintegration, regional and years to decades for melt and grounding-line retreat, millennial for committed loss.
- **Domain 1, subsurface ocean forcing.** Actors: warm deep water, cavity circulation, basal melt, grounding line. Currency: heat flux, melt rate, thinning. Anchor: measured melt rates, moorings, altimetry. Ground-visible.
- **Domain 2, surface and atmospheric forcing.** Actors: air temperature, firn permeability, melt ponds, crevasse water. Currency: surface energy balance, meltwater volume. Anchor: melt pond area and observed fracture propagation. Ground-visible.
- **Domain 3, ice mechanical state.** Actors: rifts, shear margins, pinning points, back stress, calving front, ice cliffs. Currency: stress, strain rate, velocity. Anchor: InSAR velocity and grounding-line position. Ground-visible, with the cliff-failure link contested.
- **Domain 4, sea ice and waves.** Actors: sea-ice cover, swell, storms. Currency: wave energy, flexural stress. Anchor: observed sea-ice retreat before breakups; magnitude contested. Weakest anchor, instrument owed.
- **Domain 5, verification and modelling.** Actors: ice-ocean models, remote sensing, field campaigns, paleo archives. Currency: prediction minus observation. Anchor: observed glacier response. Models share a parameter pool, so they are one witness with several views.
- **Order by dependency.** Perception of shelf state, then vocabulary for what processes afford, then objective (thinning, retreat, acceleration or disintegration), then planning, then learn and carry (Fig 3). Refuse a single scalar before minting any ranking; describe the shape before choosing a discipline (Fig 8 :53 "Residual first, frame second.").

## VERDICT (from the figures)

**Option B.** The figure-derived shape stands; the specific process ranking is a `gap`. No single scalar describes shelf stability, no single process list is valid across shelves, the five condition families form a union that must be unbundled, contact must be enumerated outward to third order, measurement is the only anchor, and a source-discriminating instrument is owed before any process is named as control. The functional group shared by the response modes is loss of back reaction; the process names are qualifiers imported from the earth-science frame with provenance.
