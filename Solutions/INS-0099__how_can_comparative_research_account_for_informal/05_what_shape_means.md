## 1. A shape is a constraint, not a filler

From *The Library of Babel*: *"You can cut your search short if the set of books returned is small and descriptive enough that it provides a general shape of the answer that satisfies your question."*

So a **shape** is the narrowed form of an answer you can state before you can name it. It constrains; it does not fill. For this question the shape is:

> At the failing slot there is an operative rule in contact with the behaviour, carrying a sanction and a community, which the official measure cannot represent; the account is checkable only if at least two low-overlap readings of that slot are ranged against an outcome that does not move when the measure is pushed.

That is a shape. "The informal rule is *blat* in this ministry" is a name. "Informal institutions explain 30 percent of the variance" is a number that no figure line returns.

Fig 13 :134-135: *"Unreachable is a verdict here, not a silence,"* and *"It cannot say which primitive is missing, because naming it would mean already holding it."* The honest report is the shape plus the refuter, not a guessed rule or a guessed magnitude.

## 2. How a shape is obtained

- *Library of Babel*: use the vocabulary you already have closest to the thing, look for recurring new terms that overlap or correlate with it, recurse. *"With each new term gained, you narrow the space and deepen your vocabulary."*
- *Library of Babel* on a bundled question: *"Perhaps your problem was a bundle or conflation of several problems masquerading as one?"* This is exactly the union finding F5 of `01_figure_grounded_audit.md`.
- Fig 6 :30: *"outward: does another frame already hold it? The description travels. Fails by being sharpened into terms only we can read. Cast it in effect terms."* Fig 9 :30-31: *"your words are borrowed too, so describe the effect rather than the cause."*
- *Decoding Tacit Knowledge*: *"When contact isn't possible, the best you can send is a plain description of what you'd expect to see, because anyone can check that against the world."* This is the outreach rule in `02` and `04`.
- The stop: *Library of Babel* says you are done when *"the finish line will also be the starting place"*, and the paradox is that by then you have *"composed the answer through the process of locating it."*

## 3. What licenses a name

Only two things, both outside the shape itself:

1. **The ground settles it.** Formula step 5: *"the ground settles it. Verified only by what pays. A gate is not the ground."* Here: a source-discriminating observation at the slot that shows which practice produced the behaviour.
2. **A provenanced import from a frame whose closure differs** (Fig 6 :23, Fig 8 :50-52), with a source gate and a shadow test. Here: a concept such as legal pluralism or street-level discretion, recorded as coming from that frame and shown to explain a gap already described.

*They're Scanning Books*: *"The real import is not a new term in the existing space. It is a new dimension of the space."* Importing "blat" extends what can be said; importing the frame that can perceive a sanction enforced outside official channels extends what can be perceived at all. The second is the valuable one.

## 4. The source problem: why official measures overlook the operative rule

*The Hard Problem is About Source* gives the mechanism, and it is the strongest essay-level support for this question:

> *"What if you have metrics that do not discriminate about its inputs?"*
> *"If several paths create the same behavior, then behavior is a projection with lossy information (it cannot define consciousness in full)."*
> *"An instrument that can't distinguish these cases demonstrates its own inadequacy, it cannot claim anything substantial about what is being measured or what causes it."*

The official measure reads a score. The score does not say which rule produced the behaviour. Two countries with the same score can be governed by a functioning written rule in one and a functioning unwritten rule in the other. The reading is a projection with lossy information; naming the source from the reading is the metamerism error. The colour-science source opened for this folder makes the same point independently: *"metamerism is a perceived matching of colors with different (nonmatching) spectral power distributions."*

*How Intelligence Actually Works* states the same thing in its first thought experiment: *"The projection of green can't tell you whether it was RGB, CMYK or something else that composed it."* It then gives the resolution rule that governs this question: *"the recursion converges toward indiscernibility, and indiscernibility is not identity. The projection that no instrument distinguishes from the thing is still a projection, which is why the lineage has to be kept even when the copy is perfect."*

So what is owed is a **source-discriminating instrument at the slot**, not a better argument and not a larger index. And the lineage of the reading must be recorded, because the score will not carry it.

## 5. Q2 - What licenses a particular informal rule as the answer?

**Nothing in the framework.** The framework returns a shape. A particular rule is licensed only by (a) the ground settling an observation at the slot, or (b) a provenanced import with a shadow test. Until one of those, the name is the researcher's own contribution and must be recorded as such.

**Does the standard "add an informal-institutions variable" move commit the forbidden error?** Yes, in a specific sense. It takes a reading (the official score), attributes a source to it (the missing informal variable) without an instrument that discriminates among sources, and then reports the attribution as if the measure had named it. That is the metamerism error applied to comparative research. The correct form is the slot-level residual with its lineage open.

The *Hard Problem* essay is careful about the size of this claim: *"It was proven wrong not by argument, but by the arrival of a better instrument."* So do not claim the source is unknowable. Claim it is **unreached at this budget** for this case, and name the instrument that would close it.

## 6. Correction to my own §3 and §5 (kept visible)

I initially stated the "shape, not a name" rule as if it forbade naming any informal practice. That overstates it. The README's phrase is *"what a **missing** thing would have to look like (a shape, not a name)."* The subject is a primitive not held in Γ.

The concepts used in this folder (broker, sanction, customary forum, discretion, clientelism, legal pluralism) are **already in the researcher's library** as ordinary terms. Naming one is a **binding** from a frame that supplies it, provenanced as such. What remains forbidden is:

- inventing a new primitive to fill the slot without a source gate (Fig 6 :24);
- claiming the binding moves the wall (Fig 6 :21: *"MINT composes inside the closure and can never add an atom"*);
- reporting the binding as a derivation from the residual alone.

*They're Scanning Books*: *"Declaring the primitives does not make them neutral. It makes the choice visible."* A declared, provenanced binding is auditable; an undeclared one runs unchallenged. Both my earlier overstatement and this narrower reading are recorded so neither is quietly lost.

## 7. Two consequences for this derivation

**Prestige is not action.** From *The Recursive Self-Improvement Trap*: *"Prestige must never be allowed to validate an action directly."* And: *"Prestige can fund an action. It can give an action a budget to be tried. But it cannot settle an action. Only the ground can settle it. The Ground Maintainer must be blind to prestige."* A list of famous authors on informal institutions cannot settle which rule governs a slot. It can only fund the attempt to check. The public contact points in `03` are chosen for ground contact and an opt-in route, never for receptiveness.

**This audit is a simulation, not a ground reading.** Same essay: *"LLMs can quickly simulate an approximate answer/output with the formula found in the architecture post. It will often be better than the generic outputs that you will usually get otherwise too (intelligence is essentially arrangement). However, the truly testable output would need to be derived from the real architecture or working the formula yourself."* And *Arrival of the Fittest* (Substack) is blunter: outputs *"could never be verified if the solutions presented were not contrived to fit the formula."* So `01` and this file are simulated derivations checked for internal consistency against the figures. They are not a test of any institution and cannot settle the question they report on. That is stated on every substantive file.

## 8. Two mappings declined (from *Arrival of the Fittest*)

The article's own section "Three mappings offered and declined" declines Bayesian updating because *"there is no prior, no likelihood and no posterior anywhere in the mechanism."* Two nearby mappings are declined here for the same reason:

- **Do not read the residual as a Bayesian prior update.** The framework sorts the failure into kinds before deciding what to do; it does not compute a posterior.
- **Do not read the coding convention as a Popperian critic.** The article notes the seat's defining property is that it cannot reconstruct the claim, *"the opposite of the critical-rationalist picture rather than a version of it."* A convention that decides which rule counts has stopped being a convention (Fig 10 :47).
