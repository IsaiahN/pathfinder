# Expert verification packet - the Tether on informal rules and comparative measurement

**What this is:** a claim in a form a comparative-institutions scholar, sociolegal researcher or measurement specialist can check. Mode, derivation and refuters are stated before the conclusion.

**Mode: `specified` on the three sub-claims, `general` on the structural shape, `gap` on the identity and magnitude of any concrete informal rule, not `grounded`.** No slot-level measurement was taken. This is a shape and a checking discipline, not a result.

**Standing status:** a simulated derivation checked for internal consistency against the figures, not a ground reading. It cannot settle the question it reports on.

---

## 1. The claim, in one paragraph

A comparative study predicts behaviour from official institutional measures and fails in a structured way: the misses concentrate at slots (a licensing desk, a land registry, a police stop, a customary forum), while the country average looks healthy. The Tether's answer is to keep the residual indexed per slot, to range at least two readings with different evidence pools against an outcome that does not move when the measure is pushed, and to treat the written rule and the operative rule as a union of overlapping conditions whose intersections have their own loop. The coding convention is made violable and the case pool unauthored. What survives structurally is a functional group (a rule with a credible sanction, qualifier stated) and a cycle (compliance re-enters as expectation). The framework returns this shape and a falsifier written in advance; it does not name the winning practice or return a magnitude.

## 2. Three checkable sub-claims

### C1 - framework-internal, checkable by reading Figures 1, 2 and 9

- **Statement:** the residual must be kept per slot, and any anchor that moves when pushed is disqualified. The formal and the informal are a union, so a single added variable leaves the intersections standing.
- **Source, verbatim:** Fig 1 :19 - **"The residual is indexed by object slot, not aggregated."** Fig 1 :22 - **"Aggregating across slots is how a live signal disappears into an average."** Fig 2 :29-30 - **"An anchor is whatever settles the question and does not change its answer because of what any frame thinks."** and **"you push on it, it is not an anchor."** Fig 9 :55-56 - **"A bundled problem is usually a union of overlapping conditions, not a list of separate ones. A single lever clears the region where only one condition holds and leaves the intersections standing, because the second condition still binds."**
- **Refuter:** show that the prediction failure is uniformly distributed across slots with no positive mass anywhere, that a candidate anchor holds fixed under a change of beliefs while the outcome is unchanged, or that the formal and informal patterns form a zero-overlap partition cleared by one lever.

### C2 - measurement-facing, checkable by a researcher against their own comparison

- **Statement:** two readings count as independent only if they draw on different evidence; a coding rule is a convention until it decides an outcome; a case pool selected on the dependent variable is an authored room.
- **Source, verbatim:** Fig 2 :31 - **"Independence, between two frames, is how little they draw on the same evidence. Two people reading the same report are not two frames; two instruments pointed at the same thing are."** Fig 2 :40 - **"disqualifying for a verdict: agreement about the world is worthless when the agreeing frames share an evidence pool"** Fig 10 :16-17 - **"the convention does not fix the bug"** and **"it makes the bug statable"** Fig 10 :73-74 - **"The residual selects the members, but only from what is available. A candidate set chosen by a participant is an authored room however well the selection is reasoned, so the pool must be unauthored first and examined second."**
- **What to check:** for one comparison you know, compute the overlap of the two readings (shared source, shared respondent pool, shared codebook). Check whether the outcome was used to select the cases. Check whether the coding rule decides which rule counts.
- **Refuter:** show that two readings with documented overlap still corroborate at the ground, that the convention survives an independently checkable redraw, or that a conclusion from dependent-variable selection survives an unauthored pool. Then C2 is withdrawn for that comparison.

### C3 - framework-internal derivation, checkable by reading Figures 11 and 12

- **Statement:** a standing list of good informal practices is structurally refused; what a locally enumerated habitat yields is a functional group and a cycle, and the broker is a catalyst that must be distinguished from spent parts by observation.
- **Source, verbatim:** Fig 11 :51 - **"No standing list of good actors survives a change of residual."** Fig 11 :43 - **"The habitat is enumerated, never composed."** Fig 12 :123 - **"functional group. A substructure whose presence fixes the outcome. The group says what kind; the qualifier says which member."** Fig 12 :121 - **"cycle. The output re-enters as input. The sign of the loop decides whether it closes, and a chain cannot close by construction."** Fig 12 :119 - **"catalyst. A part that survives its own use. Spent and regenerating parts behave differently across uses, and nothing records which a term is."**
- **Refuter:** show that a standing catalogue predicts the residual as well as a local enumeration across several residuals with no valence flip, or exhibit a norm that persists without compliance re-entering as expectation. Then C3 is narrowed to repeated-interaction norms and the enumeration instruction is weakened accordingly.

## 3. What is explicitly not claimed

- Not a list of informal institutions, not a universal percentage, not a new composite index.
- Not that any named practice (blat, guanxi, clientelism, legal pluralism, street-level discretion) is the answer. Such names are provenanced bindings from the frame that supplies them.
- Not that the written rule is irrelevant. The claim is that it is not the only thing in contact with the slot.
- Not a causal identification. Correlation is symmetric and the accounting relation is ordered; the ground work in C2 is what chooses the arrow.
- Not `grounded`. The three sub-claims are `specified`; the identity of the operative rule is a declared `gap`.
- Not a vindication of the framework. This packet is a simulated derivation and cannot validate the source it derives from.

## 5. What would settle it

1. A slot-level residual decomposition with reported positive mass (tests the unit of analysis).
2. An anchor that survives an intervention on beliefs while the outcome is held fixed, or an honest `unreached` for that slot.
3. Two readings with documented low overlap, and the overlap reported beside the agreement.
4. A violable coding convention and an unauthored case pool whose conclusion survives a redraw.
5. A source-discriminating observation that names which practice produced the behaviour. Only this moves the `gap` from `unreached` to a name.

## 6. What an expert can check in twenty minutes

1. Read Fig 1 :19, :22; Fig 2 :29-31, :40; Fig 9 :55-56 (six short passages).
2. Check C2 against one comparison you know: list the two readings, their shared sources, and whether the cases were selected on the outcome.
3. Name one outcome for a slot that would not move if the researchers' beliefs changed, or say why none is available.
4. Send the refutation. A failure is more useful than an endorsement.
