### Overlap declaration

The comparative-politics, institutional-economics, public-administration and measurement sources all draw on a broad social-science literature about rules and institutions; they count as **one witness**, not four. The genuinely low-overlap corroborators are the color-science source (metamerism) and the tacit-knowledge source (cognition). The four essays share the framework author's evidence pool completely. No world source conflicts with the figure verdict, so the conflict rule was not triggered; the Goodhart scope limit was recorded rather than treated as a conflict, because it concerns physical measurement and this question concerns measures that human respondents can game.

## 3. Distribution: offer, do not push

Figure-grounded reasons:

- **Fig 10 :52** - *"Bring a characterised residual, never the source."* Send the slot-level failure and its refuter; offer the figures after a verdict is recorded.
- **Fig 4 :22 and :25** - *"Only methods cross up. Only head starts come down."* and *"A recording carried upward looks like knowledge and is a description of one occasion."* A field report from one country is a recording; the method for locating the operative rule is a generator. Send the latter.
- **Fig 9 :27-28** - *"describe it before you go looking, or any frame you pick will seem to fit."* Ask what the recipient would expect to observe at the slot before mentioning legal pluralism or street-level bureaucracy.
- **Fig 2 :40** - *"disqualifying for a verdict: agreement about the world is worthless when the agreeing frames share an evidence pool"* - never pool later agreement as corroboration.
- **Fig 2 :42** - *"Buy independence in proportion to the cost of being wrong."* The cost here is a mistaken reform built on a measure that never saw the operative rule, so the reading budget should be high.

**Recommended order in a reply:** (1) the slot-level residual and the four refuters; (2) ask what they would count as a failure, and record the answer before showing any theory; (3) only then the three framework links, with the note that the figures, not the summary, are the authority; (4) offer the formula as a method to reuse, with the instruction that each user writes their own refuter and states their own mode.

## 4. The two-stage offer

- **Stage 1 before stage 2** is Fig 10 :52 and Fig 2 :45. The recipient checks the separator without holding the framework's interior.
- **Stage 2 is opt-in by construction.** Once a recipient has absorbed the theory, their agreement stops being evidence. Their stage-1 verdict must be on record first.
- **Stage 3 sends a method**, which Fig 4 :22 permits. The generator can be given away without giving away an answer.
- **Stage 3 still needs a ground per comparison.** `THE_FORMULA.md` "Direction" says a frame *"cannot check the direction of its own question, because the shape a frame is searching for is not a term it holds."* Each user must write down what would refute their result and label the mode of what they get.
