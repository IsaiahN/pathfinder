# The Tether's answer: agency independence without unresponsiveness

**Mode: `specified` - not `grounded`.** This is a shape and a design order, not a result. Simulated derivation checked against the figures (`05_what_shape_means.md` §6), not a ground reading.

## 1. The residual it answers

An agency must satisfy two conditions at once: political pressure must not move its decisions, and legitimate elected direction must move them. As effects these are not a contradiction unless both arrive on the same channel. The residual is therefore: what separates the channel that must be closed from the channel that must stay open? Fig 9 :54-58 makes this a **union with a compound**, not one hard question: *"What remains is smaller and it is also categorically different: a case in two conditions is not a harder case of either. It stops being a mixture and becomes a specific compound."*

## 2. The shape

1. **The standard must not move when pushed.** Fig 2 :29-30: *"An anchor is whatever settles the question and does not change its answer because of what any frame thinks. A measurement, an experiment, a counter, a market, a patient outcome. If it moves when you push on it, it is not an anchor."* Fig 2 :33: *"The anchor must not update."* An agency whose correctness standard is the current preference of a political actor has no anchor, and Fig 2 :34 warns that the drift is coherent, "which is why nobody notices."

2. **Both sides are corrected by the same ground, separately.** Fig 2 :45-46: *"Frames range against the ground. They do not negotiate with it."* and *"Each frame is corrected by it separately, and what makes the correction sound is that neither frame can move it."* The legitimate elected will enters as the standing law and the mandate that define what the anchor is for this question, not as an instruction in a particular case. This is what "responsive without being controlled" means in figure terms.

3. **Resistance and responsiveness are different operators, not two weights on one scale.** Resistance is a refusal of a particular channel; responsiveness is a comparison against a goal. Fig 12 :112: *"A vocabulary without bonds can name a state and cannot name a route to one. A plan is a sequence. A goal is a comparison. Progress is a subtraction."* An index that averages "independence" and "accountability" has thrown away the bond.

4. **The channel decays even when the ground does not.** Fig 10 :24: *"the ground does not decay, the channel does"*; :25-26 names the failure: *"stale percepts, dropped transients, saturated metrics, pooled readings"*; :28: *"you have lost touch"*. Interference usually works by degrading the channel, not by repealing the statute. The repair is Fig 10 :29: *"→ keep the anchor reachable"*.

5. **Independence is a property of the channel and habitat, not a virtue of the agent.** Fig 11 :40: *"A system cannot be made more capable by improving the agent in isolation. An improvement that does not change contact changes nothing, however much it improves."* Training, pay and exhortation cannot produce either property unless they change what the agency is in contact with.

6. **The checking seat must be outside what it grades and must declare its stake.** Fig 10 :86: *"Outside the slice being graded. Whoever is measured by a reading cannot own it."* Fig 10 :59: *"an interest in the outcome is unavoidable, an interest in the reading is disqualifying"*; :60: *"Where a stake cannot be removed, declare it."* Oversight frames that share a pool are one room (Fig 7 :36: *"A chain of mirrors is one long room."*).

7. **Design order:** state what counts as a correct outcome; separate the two channels; maintain the channel from an outside seat. Fig 3 :19: *"Optimising a link that already works cannot rescue one that does not."*

## 3. What it explicitly does not say

- Not a trade-off to be balanced. It is a union with a compound that needs its own loop.
- Not that the agency should agree with elected leaders. Fig 2 :35: *"Alignment is triangulation, not negotiation. A mirror cannot correct you."* Agreement is a convention when the product is shared meaning, and disqualifying when it is used as a verdict about the world (Fig 2 :39-41).
- Not a name. Merit recruitment, fixed terms, independent budgets, statutory mandates and instrument independence are candidate bindings from other fields with their own provenance. Fig 9 :32: *"you get a kind of thing, never the answer itself."*
- Not that written rules are neutral. Fig 10 :47: *"a convention that decides an outcome has stopped being one"*.
- Not `grounded`. No measurement in this folder settles `left(R,φ)`.

## 4. What would settle it - the ground

A source-discriminating instrument, not another argument. *The Hard Problem is About Source*: a colour instrument reads green and "cannot comment on the source"; the spectrometer, not an argument, resolved it. Here the equivalent test is: in the same decision, can we tell whether the cause of the outcome was the law or the preference? Candidate readings already exist in the world literature (a merit-recruitment mediation study, central-bank independence studies, cross-national quality-of-government indices), but each measures a proxy and they partly share an evidence pool. They corroborate; they do not settle. A real ground reading would have to vary insulation exogenously and measure both properties against an outcome neither the agency nor the politicians control.

Standing falsifiers from `01` §4: an anchor that moves when pushed yet still yields correct outcomes; a shared channel where both properties hold; an agent-only improvement that moves the ground; an outcome-deciding rule that still works as a checkable convention; a single lever that clears the intersection.

## 5. The honest stop

**Unreached, not unreachable.** Two things remain open. First, the political frame has two grounds, elections and public outcomes; when they diverge, "responsiveness" is underdetermined by the figures. That is a `gap` at this budget, and "unreachable is a verdict, not a silence" (Fig 13 :134). Second, the specific institution that occupies each slot in the shape is a binding, not a derivation, and its validity is an empirical question. Fig 4 :30 requires the round trip: *"So take the round trip and measure the gap. Without it you are navigating by dead reckoning: confident about a position nobody checked."* The gap is enumerated in `11_audit.md`. This answer stops at the limit of the available instruments and records the lineage so the stop is checkable.

## 6. One-paragraph version for outreach

> The Tether does not give a recipe for civil service reform. It says that an agency's standard of correctness must be something that does not change when a politician pushes on it, and that the agency and the elected leaders should be corrected by that same public outcome separately rather than by each other. The two wanted properties are a union with a specific compound, so no single lever clears both: insulation clears only the case where resistance is missing, and oversight clears only the case where responsiveness is missing. Resistance is a refusal of a channel, responsiveness is a comparison against a goal, and an index that averages them loses the difference. The channel, not the ground, is what decays under interference, so the design task is to keep the anchor reachable and put its maintenance in a seat outside what it grades, with declared stakes. Independence is a property of that channel and habitat, not a virtue of the agency. What the framework returns is this shape and its falsifiers written in advance; which institution occupies each slot is an empirical binding that the framework does not name. The middle ground between the two conditions stays open until a source-discriminating instrument settles it.
