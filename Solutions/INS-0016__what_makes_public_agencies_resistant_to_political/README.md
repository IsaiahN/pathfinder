# Tether outreach - figure-grounded package

**Question:** What makes public agencies resistant to political interference without making them unresponsive to elected leaders?

**Source:** `Questions/institutional questions.txt` line 21 (ID 16), section 2. State capacity and public administration.

**Mode:** `specified` on the substantive claims, `general` on the structural shape. No measurement settles the binding in this folder, so nothing here is `grounded`.

**Status:** done

**Registry status:** pending

## What is being decided, in effect terms

Two properties are wanted at once: (1) political pressure does not move an agency's decisions; (2) legitimate elected direction does move them. They look contradictory only if both arrive on the same channel. The derivation below asks which channel must be closed and which must stay open, and answers in terms of an anchor and the channel that ranges against it. The answer is a shape, not an institutional recipe.

An answer would look like this: a specification of the anchor, the correction channel, and the seat that maintains that channel, such that a reader can say what would refute each part before seeing the theory. That sought-for shape is bound as an ordinary term at the start of `01_figure_grounded_audit.md` (formula step "Direction"), so it can accrue a residual and be refused like anything else.

## Boundary and standing

This is a simulated derivation checked for internal consistency against the figures, not a ground reading. *The Recursive Self-Improvement Trap* says LLM output is "simulacra" and that "the truly testable output would need to be derived from the real architecture or working the formula yourself." So this package cannot settle the question it reports on. Every substantive claim carries its mode.

All figure quotes are verbatim from `FRAMEWORK/reference/03-FIGURES_TEXT.md` and cited as `Fig N :line`. Local framework files are used as authority for usage rules, never as world witnesses. World sources were opened and are recorded in `10_source_ledger.csv`; their agreement is corroboration only and sources from one evidence pool are declared as one witness.

## What is deliberately not here

- No personal emails of named individuals.
- No ranking of people by how likely they are to agree with the claim.
- No bulk-send plan.

Three reasons, in the figures' own words:

1. **Agreement is not an anchor.** Fig 2 :35: *"Alignment is triangulation, not negotiation. A mirror cannot correct you."* A list selected for receptiveness is a list of mirrors.
2. **Correlation disqualifies a verdict.** Fig 2 :40: *"disqualifying for a verdict: agreement about the world is worthless when the agreeing frames share an evidence pool."* Selecting for likely agreement manufactures the disqualifying correlation.
3. **Asking for trust before a check spends independence.** Fig 10 :42: *"A verifier that can reconstruct the claim cannot verify it."*

Legally, scraping personal emails for unsolicited bulk outreach breaches GDPR or CAN-SPAM style rules in many jurisdictions. Public opt-in venues avoid that failure as well as the structural one.
