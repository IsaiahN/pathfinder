## QUESTION

Decide, in effect terms, what the Tether says about a public agency that must be both resistant to political interference and responsive to elected leaders. Option A: treat this as a trade-off to be balanced with an independence score and more oversight. Option B: treat it as a union with a compound, pin an anchor that does not move when pushed, keep the correction channel separate and intact, and put its maintenance in an outside seat. What each leads to: A sends a filter as a verdict and leaves the intersection standing, because a single lever cannot clear two binding conditions; B sends a checkable shape, five written refuters and an honest gap where the institution would be named.

## DOMAIN CENSUS

R by arity, symmetry and scale, then one line per domain with actors / currency / anchor, partition or union, and order by dependency.

- **R (the residual).** Two conditions that must hold at once: political pressure does not move the decision, and legitimate elected direction does move it. Arity: two conditions joined by a shared channel, so a union with a compound, not one hard problem. Symmetry: asymmetric, since resistance is a refusal (¬) and responsiveness is a comparison (⋛). Scale: first order inside a decision, second order in the design of the checking seat. Fig 9 :55: "A single lever clears the region where only one condition holds and leaves the intersections standing."
- **Domain 1, the decision frame.** Actors: caseworker, citizen or firm, statute, case facts. Currency: correctness of the decision. Anchor: the public outcome the law is constitutively about. Union or partition: one side of the union. Dependency: first.
- **Domain 2, the political frame.** Actors: elected leaders, appointees, party and interest networks. Currency: votes, appointments, influence. Anchor: the same public outcome, plus elections. Union or partition: the other side of the union; the overlap with D1 is D4. Dependency: second, because the channels cannot be separated until the anchor is stated.
- **Domain 3, the checking arrangement.** Actors: legislature, courts, auditors, inspectors general, professional peers. Currency: conventions, verdicts, provenance. Anchor: the reachable ground, which the seat maintains but never authors. Union or partition: a separate domain at the next scale. Dependency: third.
- **Domain 4, the compound D1 ∩ D2.** Actors: the case where the same person is both legitimate direction and pressure. Currency: ambiguous input. Anchor: present but unable to correct both frames separately. Union or partition: a specific compound with its own loop. Dependency: resolved by channel separation after D1 and D2 are specified.

Strain test applied: the "anchor must not update" rule governs the reference of a decision; law changes at the level of the question, not the anchor's updating on a frame. Different objects, so no internal strain after re-census. The divergence between elections and public outcomes is a gap, recorded as such.

## VERDICT (from the figures)

The shape survives; the institution is not named. Two conditions, resistance and responsiveness, are a union with a compound, not a trade-off. They can share one anchor if the agency and the elected leaders are corrected by it separately and neither can move it. Resistance is a refusal of a channel, responsiveness is a comparison against a goal, and the channel, not the ground, is what decays. Independence is a property of channel and habitat, not of the agent. Design order: state the outcome, separate the channels, maintain the channel from an outside seat with declared stakes. The framework does not select merit recruitment, fixed terms, mandates or budgets; those are candidate bindings with provenance and an empirical check still owed. The divergence between elections and public outcomes is an open gap. Mode `specified`, ungrounded, candidate.

- **cost if wrong:** bounded and locatable. The packet states mode and refuters before the conclusion and records the lineage, so an error is findable rather than invisible. The real cost is spending the independence of outside readers by sending a recipe instead of a shape. Fig 2 :42 prices that: "Buy independence in proportion to the cost of being wrong." That cost is avoidable by leading with the shape claim and its five refuters.
- **undoable:** yes. The candidate bindings are separable from the shape in `07_the_tether_answer.md`. A defective binding can be withdrawn without touching the derivation, and no primitive has to be removed. A sent claim can be superseded by a corrected packet.
- **reversed by:** a ground reading, not agreement. A source-discriminating instrument that shows whether a decision followed the law or the preference would settle the source question; exogenous variation in insulation that measures both properties against an outcome neither side controls would settle the design claim; a single lever that clears the compound, or an agent-only improvement that moves the ground, would break the shape; and a figure re-census that allows the two grounds of the political frame to be one would close the gap. Until one of these fires, the verdict stands as `candidate`.
