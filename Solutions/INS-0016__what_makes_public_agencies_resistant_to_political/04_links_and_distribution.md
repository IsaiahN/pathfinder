## Three things in the wider literature that bear on this question

1. **The Meyer distinction is a world instance of the channel distinction.** Goal independence means the elected government sets the target; instrument independence means the agency chooses the means without day-to-day interference. That is the closest real-world approximation of "resistant without unresponsive" that I found, and it is a **binding from the economics frame**, not a figure derivation. Its provenance is stated so a reader can see which came first.
2. **The Kolvani and Nistotskaya mechanism is the closest thing to a ground reading in this folder.** It tests a causal path from merit recruitment through competence and impartiality to outcomes using 14 years of cross-national data. It is still a secondary source: it measures proxies, and its measures of impartiality come partly from democracy experts, which the authors themselves flag as a limitation. It corroborates the figure verdict but cannot settle it.
3. **Two conflicts are worth sending to a checker.** Eijffinger and de Haan find that legal independence is not sufficient; Walters and Bolger find the procedural justice effect weak in longitudinal data. Both conflicts send the derivation back to the union census rather than flipping the verdict: if a single lever cleared the intersection, both findings would look different. Neither conflict is hidden.

## Distribution: offer, do not push

Figure-grounded reasons:

- **Fig 10 :52** - *"Bring a characterised residual, never the source."* Sending the full framework before anyone has described the residual sends a source, not a characterisation. Lead with the shape claim and the refuter.
- **Fig 2 :35** - *"Alignment is triangulation, not negotiation. A mirror cannot correct you."* Do not select recipients for likely agreement and do not ask for endorsement.
- **Fig 9 :27-28** - *"describe it before you go looking, or any frame you pick will seem to fit."* Ask what the recipient would expect to see if the shape claim were true before giving them the article.
- **Fig 7 :36** - *"A chain of mirrors is one long room. The next room must differ from the last, and match the residual it surfaced."* A second venue is useful only if its readers draw on a different evidence pool. A governance journal and an administrative law journal and a reliability-safety venue are three different rooms; three public-administration lists are one room.
- **Fig 2 :40** - *"disqualifying for a verdict: agreement about the world is worthless when the agreeing frames share an evidence pool"* - a stage 2 agreement is never pooled as corroboration.

**Recommended order in a reply:** (1) the shape claim and its five refuters; (2) ask what the recipient would count as a failure and record it; (3) only then the three canonical links, with the note that the figures, not the summary, are the authority.

## The two-stage offer

1. **Stage 1 before stage 2** is Fig 10 :52 and Fig 2 :45 - *"Frames range against the ground. They do not negotiate with it."* The recipient checks the output without holding the framework's interior.
2. **Stage 2 is opt in by construction.** Once a recipient has absorbed the theory, their agreement stops being evidence. The stage 1 verdict must be recorded first.
3. **Stage 3 sends a method, which is permitted.** Fig 4 :22 - *"Only methods cross up. Only head starts come down."* The formula is a generator, so giving it away does not give away an answer.
4. **Stage 3 still needs a ground per problem.** `THE_FORMULA.md` "Direction": a frame *"cannot check the direction of its own question, because the shape a frame is searching for is not a term it holds."* Each user must write down what would refute their result before running it.

**Hard precondition:** stages 1 to 3 only work if the stage 1 output survives. The five refuters in `01_figure_grounded_audit.md` are the check. If they are not decidable by the recipient, the packet is not ready to send.
