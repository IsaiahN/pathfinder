## Independent domain census (reconstructed from the residual line alone)

I redid the census using the residual as an effect before reading the primary's conclusions, using the WATSON order: R by arity, symmetry and scale; one line per domain with actors / currency / anchor; partition or union; order by dependency.

- **R (arity / symmetry / scale).** The residual is: what separates the channel that must be closed from the channel that must stay open? Arity: two influencers on one decision, so a seam with a shared case, which makes it a union with a compound rather than a partition. Symmetry: broken, because closing a channel is a refusal (¬) while setting a direction is a comparison (⋛); treating them as symmetric produces the familiar "balance independence with accountability" platitude. Scale: the single decision (first order) and the design of the checking seat (second order).
- **Domain 1, the decision frame.** Actors: the decider, the case, the standing rule. Currency: correctness. Anchor: the outcome the rule is constitutively about. Nameable, so a domain.
- **Domain 2, the political frame.** Actors: elected leaders, appointees, networks. Currency: influence and electoral reward. Anchor: the same public outcome, plus elections. Nameable.
- **Domain 3, the checking arrangement.** Actors: oversight seats. Currency: conventions and verdicts. Anchor: the ground kept reachable by a seat that does not author it. Nameable.
- **Domain 4, the compound.** Actors: one person occupying both the legitimate-direction role and the pressure role. Currency: an input that cannot be classified by content. Anchor: present but unable to correct both frames separately. Nameable as a failure class, so a compound with its own loop.

Partition or union: **union**, with D1 ∩ D2 = D4. Order by dependency (Fig 3): anchor, then channel separation, then seat. A lever applied at step 3 when step 1 is broken is effort arriving at a step that cannot use it.

My census matches the primary's four domains, its union reading and its dependency order. Where the primary derived the union from Fig 9 :54-58 and the order from Fig 3 :19-21, I derived the union from the arity of the residual itself and the order from the dependency of one domain's anchor on the next. Different starting points, same boundaries. Low overlap between routes within the framework, but still one evidence pool.

## Round trip gap R_T

The Symbols table defines R_T as the gap between what was sent up and what came back down, `gap(x, (T_E ∘ T_A)(x))`, measuring what the coarser description cannot hold. Fig 4 :30 requires it to be measured rather than assumed zero. I ran the round trip on the condensation the folder actually uses.

2. **Abstraction (T_A):** the shorthand carried in `07_the_tether_answer.md`: pin the anchor, separate the channels, maintain the seat.
3. **Concretisation (T_E):** a concrete institutional recipe, for example "merit recruitment plus fixed terms plus an independent budget plus an inspector general".

**R_T is nonzero.** Four things the shorthand holds do not survive the trip back down:

- the **union condition**: a single lever in the concrete recipe clears only the region where one condition is missing, so the recipe leaves D4 standing;
- the **¬ versus ⋛ distinction**: the recipe treats insulation and accountability as weights on one scale, which the shorthand forbids;
- the **source-discrimination requirement**: the recipe reads an implemented policy as responsiveness without an instrument that distinguishes law from preference, the metamerism error of *The Hard Problem is About Source*;
- the **two-grounds gap**: the recipe assumes elections and public outcomes are one ground, which the figures do not establish.

Conversely, two things survive cleanly: the standard must not move when a frame pushes, and the checking seat must be outside what it grades. That is the residue worth sending. R_T was **not** measured numerically, because no ground reading exists; it is recorded qualitatively as the enumerated set of dropped constraints. Treating "the formula is the condensed version of the metalaws" as identity would set R_T to zero and would be the dead-reckoning error Fig 4 :30 names.
