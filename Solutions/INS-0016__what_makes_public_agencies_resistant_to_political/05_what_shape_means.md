## 1. A shape is a constraint, not a filler

The figures README states the limit: the framework returns *"what a missing thing would have to look like (a shape, not a name)."* Fig 9 :32: *"you get a kind of thing, never the answer itself."* Fig 13 :134: *"Unreachable is a verdict here, not a silence,"* and :135: *"It cannot say which primitive is missing, because naming it would mean already holding it."*

From *The Library of Babel*: *"you can cut your search short if the set of books returned is small and descriptive enough that it provides a general shape of the answer that satisfies your question."*

So a **shape** is the narrowed form of an answer you can state before you can name it. It constrains; it does not fill. For this question:

- **Shape:** a standard that does not move when a frame pushes; a correction channel that ranges against it; a seat that maintains the channel from outside what it grades.
- **Name:** "merit-based civil service", "fixed term with for-cause removal", "instrument independence with an elected mandate", "inspector general plus judicial review". Each is a binding from a specific field, not a figure output.

A shape can be checked by anyone. A name smuggles in a field's vocabulary and its evidence pool.

## 2. How a shape is obtained

- *Library of Babel*: *"If you don't know the right terms to search for, you will have to use the existing vocabulary you do know that is closest to the words that you wish to describe and grasp the shape of."* And: *"With each new term gained, you narrow the space by definition and deepen your vocabulary."* This is why §1 of `01_figure_grounded_audit.md` describes the effect before naming any institution.
- Fig 6 :30: *"The description travels. Fails by being sharpened into terms only we can read. Cast it in effect terms."* Fig 9 :30-31: *"your words are borrowed too, so describe the effect rather than the cause."*
- *Decoding Tacit Knowledge*: *"When contact isn't possible, the best you can send is a plain description of what you'd expect to see, because anyone can check that against the world."* This is the reason the outreach email in `02` sends a refuter rather than a theory: a refuter is an effect description.
- *Library of Babel* on stopping: *"You will know that you are done because the finish line will also be the starting place, as there is nowhere else to search and nothing else that can describe the answer better."* The answer stops when the question-as-tether has no further purchase, not when a preferred institution has been found.
- *Library of Babel* on bundled questions: *"Perhaps your problem was a bundle or conflation of several problems masquerading as one? You will need to search all of those problems individually to find their answers and then combine them in a satisfactory way."* This is the essay-level statement of the union finding in `01` §1c.

## 3. What licenses a name

Only two things, both outside the shape itself:

1. **The ground settles it.** Formula step 5: *"the ground settles it. Verified only by what pays. A gate is not the ground."* The README: the framework *"stops at the limit of what the available instruments can distinguish, and records the lineage so the stop is honest."*
2. **A provenanced import from a frame whose closure differs** (Fig 6 :23, Fig 8 :50-52), with a source gate and a shadow test.

Here the candidate names arrive from public administration, institutional economics, administrative law, reliability science and political philosophy. Each was opened this session and recorded in `10_source_ledger.csv`, with field, overlap and a quote. That provenance is what makes them admissible as *candidate bindings*. It does not turn them into figure derivations. Fig 6 :22: *"NOVEL means novel relative to atoms, not to the world. IMPORT adds an atom, and the added atom is what expands the closure."* A binding of an existing term onto a described slot adds no atom and does not move the wall.

## 4. The source discrimination problem in this question

*The Hard Problem is About Source* gives the mechanism that matters most here. Metamerism: an instrument reads green and *"the instrument is speaking only about what it measured, it cannot comment on the source."* The source might be one green light, or yellow plus blue, or RGB, or CMYK, or a scheme not yet discovered. *"If several paths create the same behavior, then behavior is a projection with lossy information."*

Applied here: an agency implements a policy. The observed output is the same whether it followed the statute (legitimate responsiveness) or the minister's phone call (illegitimate pressure). A single reading of "policy implemented" cannot comment on the source. *The Hard Problem is About Source*: *"The answer is unreached until a better instrument exists"* and *"It was proven wrong not by argument, but by the arrival of a better instrument."*

This is why the ground that would settle this question is a **source-discriminating instrument**, not another governance index. The right test is: in the same decision, can we tell whether the cause was the law or the preference? If not, the instrument is inadequate, not the question unanswerable. The source ledger's two conflicts (legal independence not sufficient; procedural justice effect weak longitudinally) are both instances of one reading with several possible sources.

## 5. Prestige, and why the verdict must come first

*The Recursive Self-Improvement Trap*: *"Prestige must never be allowed to validate an action directly."* And: *"Prestige can fund an action. It can give an action a budget to be tried. But it cannot settle an action. Only the ground can settle it. The Ground Maintainer must be blind to prestige."*

In this field, prestige arrives as the senior economist, the famous public-administration scholar, the former agency head. Their agreement gives the claim a budget to be tried. It does not make the shape true. And: *"You may all agree and be wrong. You may set your clock to someone else's, who was setting your clock to yours, and be off by many deviations over time. You need ground truth to define where you stand."*

*Decoding Tacit Knowledge* states the check directly: *"two people who only check against each other drift, and they drift together, so neither notices."* And the remedy: *"A ground that both are corrected by, separately... An experiment, an outcome, a measurement."* So the outreach protocol records the checker's verdict before showing any theory (Fig 10 :52).

## 6. My own audit is a simulation, not a ground reading

*The Recursive Self-Improvement Trap*: *"the output is only as good as the validator. The agent, or the tool, or the frame, or the theory, cannot validate itself. An external validator, or ground, is always needed to tether the veracity of the output to reality. Anything else is drift."* And: *"LLMs can quickly simulate an approximate answer/output with the formula found in the architecture post... However, the truly testable output would need to be derived from the real architecture or working the formula yourself."*

So `01_figure_grounded_audit.md` is a simulated derivation checked for internal consistency against the figures. It is **not** a test of the claim and must be labelled as such. It cannot settle anything it reports on. The two conflicts in the world literature are presented as conflicts, not overruled, and the final verdict is `candidate`.

## 7. Correction to my own §3 (kept visible)

I initially wrote that "the framework returns a shape, not a name" forbids naming any institution at all. That overstates it. The README's phrase governs *"what a missing thing would have to look like"*, a primitive not held in Γ. Here nothing in the framework is missing: the shape is built from held concepts (anchor, channel, seat). Naming a candidate institution is a **binding from a world frame onto a described slot**, which is allowed once provenance and a shadow test are supplied. What remains forbidden is presenting that binding as figure-derived. Both readings are kept: the binding is admissible as a candidate with provenance, and inadmissible as a framework output. This matches the correction recorded in the exemplar's `05` §5b, reached independently here.
