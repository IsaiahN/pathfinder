## QUESTION

Is it better for a firm to specialize in a narrow market or diversify across products and industries?

Restated as an effect: for which shape of scope does the per-slot residual R = |Γ(b,a) - o′| stay small across the slots a firm occupies and over time? R is indexed per product-market slot, never aggregated (Fig 1 :19 "The residual is indexed by object slot, not aggregated."). Anchor: the market, named as an anchor in Fig 2 :29 "An anchor is whatever settles the question and does not change its answer because of what any frame thinks." Both the strategy literature and industrial organization supply candidate answers, but the verdict comes from the figures.

## VERDICT (from the figures)

**The figure-derived verdict:** specialization and diversification are not rival universal answers. The question is a union of two conditions that must both hold: C1, a maintained channel to a paying ground, and C2, renewed reach through a differing closure. Related breadth is legal rearrangement of held atoms (an isomer); unrelated breadth is a priced import requiring a source gate. What travels between units must be a method, not a recording; capability valence is relative to the residual, so no standing list of good capabilities survives a market change. Measured per slot, not by a pooled average. Which numerical breadth wins is a declared `gap`. Mode `specified`. The strategy and IO evidence pools corroborate the related/unrelated and shared-input distinctions after re-mapping them to held atoms; the discount/premium conflict was returned to the census and resolved by the relatedness distinction, not used to flip the verdict. Low-overlap ecology witnesses corroborate the habitat-dependence of the shape.

- **Cost if wrong:** misdirected diagnosis. A firm failing C2 would be told to listen harder to its existing market, or a firm failing C1 in one unit would be told to import more businesses; a single lever would be applied to a union that needs two. An expert's twenty minutes would be spent refuting a shape that was never the right shape. The cost is real but bounded and largely reversible.
- **Undoable:** yes. No term is deleted if the claim fails; a refutation is added and the reach falls because *"something was added rather than because anything was deleted"* (Fig 6 :42). The shape and its five falsifiers are recorded with provenance so a later reader can locate the error (Fig 10 :62-64).
- **Reversed by:** any of the five pre-registered falsifiers, in particular (1) one scope lever restoring both conditions with no categorically different residue, (2) pooled return shown sufficient for per-slot residuals, (3) related breadth reaching a new capability with no rearrangement and no import, (4) invariant capability valence across a market change, or (5) an instrument fixing one optimal breadth. A source with a genuinely different evidence pool showing that per-slot persistence is fully predicted by averaged scope categories would also reverse it.
