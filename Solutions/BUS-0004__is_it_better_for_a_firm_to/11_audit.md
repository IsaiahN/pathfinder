## 2. Independent re-census from the residual line

### 2.1 Arity, symmetry, scale

- **Arity:** a relation among firm-library Γ, scope action a and market return o′, indexed by slot s and ordered by time t. At least three operands plus an ordering and a threshold comparison. A two-place reading (firm, performance) loses both the action and the prediction, so it is rejected.
- **Symmetry:** not symmetric. The market is an anchor and does not update on the firm's opinion (Fig 2 :25). "Better" is a comparison against competitors (Operators table :51), so one operand is a quantity compared to a second, not merely conjoined with it.
- **Scale:** the slot is a product-market; "superior" exists only at the population/market scale (Fig 4 :5). The scope decision crosses the membrane between the firm's step loop and the market's selection loop (Fig 4). So the residual is intrinsically cross-scale.

## 4. Round-trip gap R_T

Fig 4 :30: *"So take the round trip and measure the gap. Without it you are navigating by dead reckoning: confident about a position nobody checked."*

- **T_A, going up:** carry the primary's two-condition shape to a one-sentence abstraction. Result: *scope pays when the firm keeps a live relation to the market and keeps renewing what it can do.*
- **T_E, coming back:** put that abstraction into a specific firm situation. Result: *stay close to customers and keep adding new businesses.*

**R_T = gap(x, (T_E ∘ T_A)(x)).** The gap is what the abstraction cannot hold:

1. It collapses the union into one lever. "Keep a live relation" does not say that C1 and C2 are separate conditions, so coming back down it tells a firm failing C2 merely to listen harder, which is effort above the break (Fig 3 :29-30), and loses Fig 9 :57.
2. It loses the anchor rule. "Live relation" does not say the market, not internal agreement, settles the question (Fig 2 :29, :35). Coming back down, it permits a firm to score itself with a quantity it produces (Fig 1 :27).
3. It loses per-slot indexing. "Adding new businesses" can be done while one unit's residual grows unseen in a pooled average (Fig 1 :22).
4. It loses the related/unrelated distinction. "Adding new businesses" treats an isomer rearrangement and a priced import as the same move (Fig 6 :24, Fig 13 :115).
5. It loses the valence flip and the source-discriminator requirement, which are the actual `gap` in the answer (Fig 11 :49-51).

This gap is not an error to eliminate; it is the measurement of what the coarser description cannot hold (Fig 4 :31). It is why the outreach packet sends the generator, not the one-sentence version (Fig 4 :25). The one-paragraph version in `07` is a deliberately lossy artefact and is labelled as such.
