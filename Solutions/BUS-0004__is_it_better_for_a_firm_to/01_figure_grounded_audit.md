## 0. Residual in effect terms

The question parses as a scope choice whose "better" must be anchored. A firm holds a library Γ of routines and atoms. For each product-market slot s it forms a belief b, takes a scope action a (enter a narrow slot, or enter many slots), predicts a return Γ(b,a), and the market returns o′. The residual is R = |Γ(b,a) - o′|, indexed per slot:

> Fig 1 :19 "The residual is indexed by object slot, not aggregated."
> Fig 1 :22 "Aggregating across slots is how a live signal disappears into an average."

"Better" is not the level of R at one moment. It is the persistence of a low R across the slots a firm occupies and across time. Two options are on the table:

- **Specialize (narrow):** deep Γ in one slot, small R there, but one channel to one ground.
- **Diversify (broad):** Γ spread over many slots, several channels to several grounds, shared atoms reused, but coordination cost and import risk when a new slot's closure differs.

The sought-for shape, bound before the run as an ordinary term (Formula, "Direction"): the conditions under which either scope keeps the union of slot-residuals small, plus an instrument that separates related scope (reuse inside one closure) from unrelated scope (an import across closures). Until bound, this shape is a preference that predicts nothing and that no check in the loop can see.

## 1. Domain census (before any figure line is chosen)

**Arity, symmetry, scale.** Arity is three-place (Γ, scope action, market return), indexed by slot s and ordered by time t; the relation carries an ordered bond (a route from capability to market) and a comparison bond ("better" is a threshold), not symmetric ones. Symmetry is broken: the market is an anchor and does not update on the firm's own opinion (Fig 2 :29). Scale is intrinsically cross-scale: the slot is a product-market, but "better" is selected only at the population/market scale (Fig 4 :5), across the lossy membrane between a single firm's loop and the market's selection loop.

One line per candidate domain. A nameable anchor is required for admission.

| X2 "industry attractiveness" as sole cause | an industry category | structural rents | already inside D2 | absorbed |

**Partition or union.** Union, not partition. The question is a union of two conditions: C1, a maintained channel to a paying ground; C2, renewed reach through contact with a differing closure. Their overlap is a compound with its own loop:

> Fig 9 :55 "A bundled problem is usually a union of overlapping conditions, not a list of separate ones."
> Fig 9 :57 "a case in two conditions is not a harder case of either."

**Dependency order (Fig 3).** perception → vocabulary → objective → planning → learn and carry. The middle link is vocabulary/objective, what a new slot affords and what would count as progress there. It is the link where diversification most often breaks, and it has no instrument (Fig 3 :27 "A link with no instrument is not a link that works. It is a link nobody has looked at.").

## 3. Findings F1 to F9

### F1 - The question is a union, so a single lever cannot clear it

> Fig 9 :55 "A bundled problem is usually a union of overlapping conditions, not a list of separate ones."
> Fig 9 :57 "a case in two conditions is not a harder case of either."

Specialization and diversification each clear one condition. Narrow scope can keep the channel to a known ground, but does nothing to renew reach when that ground's residual changes. Broad scope can renew reach, but does nothing to keep each new channel reachable. A firm failing C2 that is told to "focus harder" is receiving effort above the break (Fig 3). **Mode `general` for the union structure, `specified` for firms. Refuter:** one intervention that restores both conditions and leaves no categorically different residue.

### F2 - "Better" must range against the ground, not internal agreement

> Fig 2 :29 "An anchor is whatever settles the question and does not change its answer because of what any frame thinks."
> Fig 2 :35 "Alignment is triangulation, not negotiation. A mirror cannot correct you."

Both options generate internal agreement: a focused firm's routines agree with each other, and a diversified firm's boardroom can agree that diversification is good. Neither is the anchor. The market, as realized return and survival, settles it. **Mode `specified`. Refuter:** a scope choice that persists while realized return declines and customers stop paying, with no correction from the market registering.

### F3 - Scope is measured per slot; pooling hides the live signal

> Fig 1 :19 "The residual is indexed by object slot, not aggregated."
> Fig 1 :22 "Aggregating across slots is how a live signal disappears into an average."

A diversified firm can post a healthy pooled return while one business unit's residual grows, masked by the other units. This is the same arithmetic as the mirror chain: *"agreement compounds, evidence does not"* (Fig 7 :13). The correct object is the vector of slot-residuals, not its mean. **Mode `general`, `specified` for the firm application. Refuter:** a diversification decision shown to reduce the mean return while no individual slot-residual improved.

### F4 - Related scope is rearrangement inside a closure; unrelated scope is an import with a price

> Fig 12 :117 "isomer. Same atoms, different bond, different substance. The difference is invisible in the formula and decisive in the behaviour."
> Fig 13 :115 "The set is closed. The arrangements are not. That gap is why import exists."
> Fig 6 :23 "No frame escapes its own closure. The bound moves only on contact with a frame whose closure differs."

A firm that enters a new product made from the capabilities it already holds is rearranging the same atoms under a different bond, an isomer in the figure's sense. No new atom is needed. A firm that enters an industry whose capability set it does not hold must import an atom from a frame whose closure differs, and import carries a source gate and a shadow test (Fig 8). *"A wrong atom costs more than starting from primitives"* (Fig 6 :24). This is why related diversification and unrelated diversification must not be averaged into one verdict. **Mode `general` for the composition rule, `specified` for firms. Refuter:** a genuinely new industry entered with no new atom and no source gate, or reach renewed by pure internal composition with no new atom (which would break Fig 6).

### F5 - What crosses between slots must be a method, not a recording

> Fig 4 :22 "Only methods cross up. Only head starts come down."
> Fig 4 :23 "upward: a method that can be reapplied. Never the recording of a particular success."

The claimed payoff of diversification is that a capability learned in one market helps in another. That only holds if what travels is a reusable method. A recording of a past success, carried into a new market, *"looks like knowledge and is a description of one occasion"* (Fig 4 :25) and replays an old win instead of producing a new one. This gives the mechanism behind the relatedness result in the corporate-finance evidence: shared competences travel; shared products do not. **Mode `specified`. Refuter:** a new unit that reproduces a past product success but cannot generate a new one in the new market from the transferred capability.

### F6 - Capability valence is relative to the residual; a strength can flip sign

> Fig 11 :49 "An actor’s valence is relative to the residual, not a property it carries."
> Fig 11 :50 "The same actor is a benefactor to one residual and a malfactor to another, so mutualism and parasitism are relations rather than kinds."
> Fig 11 :51 "No standing list of good actors survives a change of residual."

The capability that made a specialist excellent can become the rigidity that loses the market when the residual changes. This is the mechanism by which a narrow scope fails, and it is why "deep capability" cannot be entered as a standing advantage. There is no permanent list of good capabilities. **Mode `specified`. Refuter:** a capability shown to keep identical valence across a documented change of residual and market.

### F7 - More slots means more channels to keep reachable

> Fig 10 :24 "the ground does not decay, the channel does"
> Fig 10 :26 "saturated metrics, pooled readings"

Diversification multiplies the number of channels between the firm and its grounds. Each channel can go stale independently. The ground itself does not decay, so a diversified firm's failure is usually a channel failure in one unit that pooled reporting (Fig 1 :22, Fig 11 :26) hides. **Mode `specified`. Refuter:** advantage lost across slots while every channel measures high and non-declining.

### F8 - Diversification most often breaks at the vocabulary/objective link

> Fig 3 :27 "A link with no instrument is not a link that works. It is a link nobody has looked at."
> Fig 3 :29 "Before asking whether a system is good, ask which link it currently stops at, and whether the answer was measured or assumed."

Entering a new slot requires terms for what that slot affords and a statement of what counts as progress there. A firm that has only ingredients (a capability list) can name what a market contains but cannot name a route to winning in it. Effort spent on planning above the break cannot rescue the missing vocabulary (Fig 3 :19). **Mode `specified`. Refuter:** a diversified firm that has explicit terms for what each new market affords and a stated objective in each, yet still fails for a reason traceable to perception rather than to the middle link.

### F9 - Naming the optimal breadth is a `gap`

> Fig 9 :32 "you get a kind of thing, never the answer itself."
> Fig 13 :134 "Unreachable is a verdict here, not a silence,"
> Fig 8 :39 "So a search that finds nothing is never a proof of absence."

No figure line governs the numerical scope optimum (how many markets, how broad the SIC spread). After a genuine re-census the figures yield conditions and a shape, not a number. The missing thing is a source-discriminating instrument that would tell a protected related capability from a mere shared label, and a ground reading of the firm's slot-residual vector. **Verdict `gap` for the number, `triangulated` for the conditions. Refuter:** a figure line, or a ground instrument, that directly fixes an optimal breadth. Until then, "two or three related businesses" is a name where the framework returns a shape.

## 4. Pre-registered refuter (written before the conclusion)

Required before any outreach, per the Formula direction rule and the exemplar's discipline: a falsifier written before the run is a design instrument. The shape claim is refuted if any of the following holds:

1. A single scope lever (focus or diversify) restores both C1 (a maintained paying channel) and C2 (renewed reach) with no categorically different residue, collapsing the union into one condition.
2. A firm's pooled return is shown to be a sufficient statistic for its per-slot residuals, so that per-slot indexing (Fig 1 :19) is unnecessary.
3. Related scope is shown to reach a genuinely new capability without any import and without rearranging held atoms (breaks Fig 6 and Fig 13).
4. A capability is shown to keep identical valence across a documented change of market residual (breaks Fig 11 :49-51).
5. A source-discriminating instrument, or a ground reading, directly fixes one optimal breadth across firms and markets (closes F9).

If any fires, the shape is withdrawn or narrowed. Until one is run, no part of this may be labelled `grounded`.

## 5. Recommendation

1. Do not answer "specialize" or "diversify" as a universal verdict. The figure-derived answer is the shape of the conditions (F1, F9).
2. Choose narrow scope when the single ground is rich and stable and the capability can be deepened without a valence flip (F6, Fig 11), and record that this is a `specified` bet on the habitat, not a property of the firm.
3. Choose broad scope when the same atoms can be rearranged into new slots (related diversification, Fig 12 :117, Fig 13 :115), and treat what travels as a method, not a recording (F5).
4. Treat unrelated diversification as an import: require a source gate and a shadow test before paying for a new atom, and default to refusal without one (F4, Fig 6 :24).
5. Measure the slot-residual vector, not the pooled average (F3), and keep every channel reachable (F7).
6. Put the pre-registered refuter in front of any expert before showing the framework. Ask for a failure, not agreement.
