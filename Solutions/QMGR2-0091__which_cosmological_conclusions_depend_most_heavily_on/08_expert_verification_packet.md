# Expert verification packet: model and calibration dependence in cosmology

**What this is:** a claim in a form a cosmologist, metrologist or philosopher of science can check. Mode, derivation and refuters are stated before the conclusion.

**Mode: `specified` on C1 to C3, `general` on the structural shape, `gap` on the numeric ordering, not `grounded`.** This is a simulated derivation checked for internal consistency against the figures. No cosmological measurement was taken in this run.

## 1. The claim, in one paragraph

A cosmological conclusion depends heavily on a model or calibration exactly when the item its inference terminates in fails the anchor test: it changes when the calibration or model is swapped. Dependence is indexed per conclusion, never averaged. In a dependency chain of anchor, rung, ruler, model and conclusion, leverage is upstream, so conclusions downstream of a moving calibration carry its movement. H0 has two such legs (a local stellar calibration feeding the supernova absolute magnitude, and a sound horizon that is itself computed from the assumed pre-recombination energy budget), so it ranks first by mechanism count. Base CMB parameters are only weakly model-dependent and are a different class of object from the derived parameters built over them. Apparent corroboration among probes is worth its independence, and shared calibration priors make agreement a convention rather than a verdict. The numeric per-conclusion sensitivity ranking is not returned; it is a declared gap owed to a ground-level sensitivity analysis.

## 2. Three checkable sub-claims

### C1 - framework-internal; checkable by reading Figures 1, 2 and 3

- **Statement:** dependence is a per-conclusion anchor property, and leverage is upstream in the calibration chain.
- **Source, verbatim:** Fig 1 :19 "The residual is indexed by object slot, not aggregated." Fig 2 :29 "An anchor is whatever settles the question and does not change its answer because of what any frame thinks." Fig 2 :30 "If it moves when you push on it, it is not an anchor." Fig 3 :20 "The chain is ordered by dependency, not by importance."
- **What to check:** does the anchor test, applied to a named conclusion, actually separate moving calibrations from non-updating anchors? Is the claimed chain order the one cosmologists use?
- **Refuter:** a conclusion that stays fixed under every admissible model and calibration while its anchor still fails the test; or a downstream lever that repairs an upstream break without changing contact.

### C2 - cosmology-facing; checkable against the parameter literature

- **Statement:** base CMB parameters are weakly model-dependent; derived late-Universe parameters are model-dependent; H0 carries both a calibration leg and a model leg; the sound horizon is a model output used as a ruler.
- **Source, verbatim:** Fig 9 :32 "you get a kind of thing, never the answer itself." Fig 12 :104 "A settled molecule becomes an atom for whatever composes over it. So irreducible is a fact about a level and never about the world, and there is no bottom"
- **World record to check:** Planck 2018 VI states that primary results are "only weakly dependent on the cosmological model" while labelling the inferred late-Universe parameters model-dependent; Cyr-Racine states that H0 inference is "only as good as our knowledge of the baryon-photon sound horizon"; Knox and Millea show that CMB inference of H0 requires adjusting a free density so the angular-diameter distance matches; Cai and Wang state that "A specific cosmological model is therefore needed to extrapolate the early-Universe information to the late Universe" and that "what really matters is the calibration of the SNe Ia absolute magnitude".
- **Refuter:** a base parameter that moves materially under a standard extension, a derived parameter invariant under an extension that changes its defining relation, or a cross-calibration sensitivity analysis that reverses the mechanism ranking.

### C3 - corroboration and provenance; checkable against any multi-probe claim

- **Statement:** agreement among probes is worth its independence; probes sharing calibration priors or model assumptions are one witness, not several.
- **Source, verbatim:** Fig 2 :35 "Alignment is triangulation, not negotiation. A mirror cannot correct you." Fig 7 :12-13 "same closure, handed along; agreement compounds, evidence does not" Fig 8 :48-49 "Two frames that overlap almost completely are one frame wearing two names."
- **What to check:** for two probes cited as independent confirmations of H0, list shared calibration, shared priors, shared data and shared model. The decade review explicitly records that some non-Planck CMB results rely on Planck-derived optical-depth priors or Planck-based absolute calibration.
- **Refuter:** two probes with disjoint instruments, disjoint calibrations and disjoint model assumptions that agree, while the claim still predicts high dependence; or a figure line that returns the numeric ordering, which would close the gap.

## 3. What is explicitly not claimed

- Not that all cosmology is model-dependent. Primary CMB quantities are explicitly weakly model-dependent.
- Not a numeric H0 value, an uncertainty budget, or a sigma level for any tension. Sigma levels filter the budget; they do not witness the verdict.
- Not that ΛCDM is false, that the sound horizon is wrong, or that new physics is required. A tension does not identify the faulty background hypothesis.
- Not that any named probes are independent. Overlap is measured from the calibration and prior record.
- Not a theory of everything, and not a final ranking.
- Not `grounded`. The numeric ordering is a `gap`; reaching it needs a ground reading.
