## DOMAIN CENSUS

R by arity, symmetry and scale, then one line per domain with actors / currency / anchor, partition or union, and order by dependency.

- **R (the residual).** For each conclusion c_i, the movement of c_i under a swap of model or calibration. Arity: one per conclusion, so per slot (Fig 1 :19); the question itself is a union of at least three overlapping conditions, not a single ranking problem. Symmetry: ordered, model/calibration to conclusion, not reversible. Scale: from local geometric anchors through rung indicators to a global model extrapolation.
- **Domain 1, inference chain and calibration.** Actors: geometric anchors, Cepheid / TRGB / SBF / Mira rungs, SN Ia absolute magnitude, BBN abundances, the sound horizon, fiducial cosmologies. Currency: propagated scale and accumulated uncertainty. Anchor: whatever does not move when pushed (Fig 2 :29). Question type: dependency chain, a union of conditions along the chain.
- **Domain 2, the model world.** Actors: base parameters (θ*, ωb, ωc, A_s, n_s, τ) and derived parameters (H0, Ωm, σ8, age, w, ΩK, Neff), ΛCDM and extensions. Currency: likelihood mass over parameter space. Anchor: the data, but the step to a derived parameter consumes the model. Question type: a partition of base versus derived parameters, with the boundary itself the object of interest.
- **Domain 3, corroboration and probe independence.** Actors: CMB experiments, BAO, SNe, lensing, BBN, cosmic chronometers, megamasers, inverse ladders. Currency: agreement versus residual. Anchor: the ground, never agreement. Question type: union of overlapping conditions when calibration priors are shared, partition only when pools are demonstrably disjoint.
- **Order by dependency (Fig 3).** Geometric anchor → calibration rung → model inference → conclusion. Upstream levers reach everything downstream; downstream levers do not reach upstream.
- **Partition or union overall.** Union. The sound horizon is simultaneously a D1 calibration, a D2 derived quantity and a D3 shared pool, so one lever does not clear the intersections (Fig 9 :54).

Strain test: the "weakly model-dependent" versus "model-dependent" readings of the Planck abstract are not a figure conflict. Re-census assigns them to D2 base and D2 derived parameters, different objects.

## VERDICT (from the figures)

The answer is the separator plus a mechanism ranking, not a measured table. Dependence is per conclusion and is distance from a non-updating anchor, amplified by chain length and by evidence-pool overlap. H0 ranks first by mechanism count because it has both a moving local calibration leg and a moving model-extrapolation leg. The age, matter density, fluctuation amplitude, curvature, relativistic degree count and dark-energy equation of state are derived through a model bond and rank next; the sound horizon is the deepest case of a model output used as a ruler. Base CMB quantities are only weakly model-dependent, so the answer is not that everything is uncertain. Expansion, the hot Big Bang and the dark-matter inference are comparatively robust only where triangulation is genuinely low-overlap. The literal numeric ordering is a `gap` of the unreached-at-this-budget kind. Mode `specified` on the ranking, `general` on the shape, not `grounded`.

- **cost if wrong:** bounded and locatable, because the verdict is mode-labelled, per slot and carries refuters before the conclusion. The real cost is spending the independence of outside readers by sending a ranking instead of the separator. Fig 2 :42 "Buy independence in proportion to the cost of being wrong." That cost is avoidable by leading with the anchor test and its refuter.
- **undoable:** yes. The mechanism ranking can be revised without touching the separator or removing any term: a published cross-model, cross-calibration sensitivity analysis simply pays for the numeric ordering. No primitive has to be withdrawn, and a sent claim can be superseded by the corrected packet.
- **reversed by:** a ground reading, not agreement. A sensitivity analysis reversing the mechanism ranking; a conclusion invariant under all admissible swaps whose anchor still fails the test; zero-overlap probes agreeing while dependence stays high; or a figure line that returns the numeric ordering. Until one fires, the verdict stands as `candidate`.
