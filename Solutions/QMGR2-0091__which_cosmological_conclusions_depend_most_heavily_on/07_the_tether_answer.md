# The Tether's answer: which cosmological conclusions depend most heavily on a model or calibration

**Mode: `specified` on the per-domain verdicts and the mechanism ranking, `general` on the structural shape, `gap` on the literal numeric ordering, not `grounded`.** Simulated derivation checked against the figures, not a ground reading (`05` section 6). No cosmological measurement was taken in this folder.

## 1. The residual it answers

Each cosmological conclusion is a slot with its own movement under a swap of model or calibration. The question asks for the ordering of those movements. The answer is a separator, a mechanism ranking of the conclusions, a list of weakly dependent conclusions, and a declared gap where the numbers would go.

## 2. The shape, with verbatim ground

1. **Dependence is distance from a non-updating anchor, per conclusion.** Fig 2 :29 "An anchor is whatever settles the question and does not change its answer because of what any frame thinks." Fig 2 :30 "If it moves when you push on it, it is not an anchor." A calibration that moves under swap is a frame wearing the anchor's label. Fig 1 :19 "The residual is indexed by object slot, not aggregated." So the ranking is per conclusion and is never collapsed into one number for cosmology.
2. **Leverage is upstream in a dependency chain.** Fig 3 :20 "The chain is ordered by dependency, not by importance." The chain runs geometric anchor → calibration rung → model inference → conclusion. Fig 3 :19 "Optimising a link that already works cannot rescue one that does not." Metrology corroborates independently: traceability is an unbroken chain of calibrations in which uncertainty increases at each link.
3. **Base parameters and derived parameters are different objects.** Planck's primary CMB quantities are explicitly only weakly model-dependent, while H0, Ωm and σ8 are explicitly inferred, model-dependent late-Universe parameters. Fig 12 :104 "A settled molecule becomes an atom for whatever composes over it. So irreducible is a fact about a level and never about the world, and there is no bottom" explains why: the derived terms are molecules composed over settled atoms, and the bond is the model.
4. **Agreement among probes is worth only its independence.** Fig 2 :35 "Alignment is triangulation, not negotiation. A mirror cannot correct you." Fig 7 :12-13 "same closure, handed along; agreement compounds, evidence does not". Probes that share calibration priors or model assumptions are one long room; their agreement is variation, not a verdict.
5. **The numeric ordering is a gap, and that is a verdict.** Fig 9 :32 "you get a kind of thing, never the answer itself". Fig 13 :134 "Unreachable is a verdict here, not a silence". What is owed is a cross-model, cross-calibration sensitivity analysis with the overlap matrix recorded. Importing a new primitive would be the wrong remedy (Fig 6 :24).

## 3. The mechanism ranking (`specified`, kept separate, never averaged)

| rank by number of independent moving legs | conclusion | moving legs | status |
|---|---|---|---|
| 1 | **H0** | local calibration rung plus SN Ia absolute magnitude **and** sound-horizon plus model extrapolation | `specified`, triangulated across figures; world sources are one pool |
| 2 | **age of the universe, Ωm, σ8 / S8, w, ΩK, Neff** | downstream of the base parameters through a model bond, and downstream of H0 in most global fits | `specified`, single cosmology pool empirically |
| 3 | **the sound horizon r_s / r_d itself** | a calibration computed from ωb, ωm, radiation and recombination; a model output used as a ruler | `specified`, structurally triangulated |
| 4 | **primordial light-element abundances** | low model dependence in the standard BBN chain, but sensitive to burning rates and the adopted BBN code | `specified`, comparative only |
| 5 | **expansion, the hot Big Bang, the dark-matter inference** | each triangulated across genuinely low-overlap probes; residual sensitivity, not immunity | `general` structural, world corroboration mixed |

Ranks 1 to 3 are mechanism counts, not measured coefficients. The ordering within ranks 2 to 4 is not settled by anything in this folder and is part of the gap.

## 4. What it explicitly does not say

- Not that all cosmology is model-dependent. Base CMB quantities are weakly model-dependent, and that distinction is load-bearing.
- Not a number for H0, its uncertainty budget, or its tension significance. A 5σ or 6σ tension is a filter for the budget, not a witness for the verdict (Fig 9 :60).
- Not that the sound horizon is wrong, that ΛCDM is false, or that new physics is required. A tension does not name the faulty background hypothesis.
- Not that any two probes are independent. Overlap is measured from the record of calibrations and priors, not assumed.
- Not a theory of everything. The ranking is a method output with a stop, not a final room.
- Not `grounded`. Nothing here was settled by a measurement in this run.

## 5. What would settle it, the ground

A real sensitivity analysis, not a further argument. Concretely:

1. **Cross-calibration sweep.** Recompute H0 with Cepheid, TRGB, SBF, Mira and inverse-ladder calibrations under a recorded covariance matrix; report the spread attributable to the calibrator.
2. **Cross-model sweep.** Recompute the inferred parameters under ΛCDM and the extensions that change the sound horizon or the late-time distance relation, recording which base terms are held fixed.
3. **Overlap matrix.** For every pair of probes cited as corroborating a conclusion, record shared calibration, shared prior, shared data and shared model. Two frames with no shared entry are genuinely independent; others are one witness.
4. **Discriminating instrument.** A measurement that distinguishes a moving calibration from a non-updating anchor without presupposing the model that produced the calibration. This is the hard one; it is the analogue of the spectrometer in *The Hard Problem is About Source*.
5. **Standing refuters from `01`:** a conclusion invariant under all swaps while its anchor fails the test; zero-overlap probes agreeing while dependence stays high; a figure line that returns the numeric ordering; a ground analysis that reverses the mechanism ranking.

## 6. The honest stop

**Unreached, not unreachable.** The framework returns a shape plus a refuter and stops at the limit of the instruments. The base/derived split, the anchor test and the overlap test are available now; the numeric ordering is not. The six-term substrate is a visible guess, and declaring it does not make it neutral. Indiscernibility is not identity: two routes that give the same posterior are not thereby the same measurement. The record of which calibration and which model produced each number is what makes the result checkable.

## 7. One-paragraph version for outreach

> The question asks which cosmological results move most when you change the ruler. The Tether answers with a separator rather than a guessed list. A conclusion depends heavily on a model or calibration exactly when what it terminates in fails the anchor test: swap the calibration or the model and the conclusion moves. Applied to cosmology, the Hubble constant has two moving legs, a local stellar calibration feeding the supernova absolute magnitude and an early-Universe sound horizon that is itself a model output, which is why it ranks first by number of independent moving legs. The age, matter density, fluctuation amplitude, curvature and dark-energy equation of state are derived through a model bond and rank next; the sound horizon is the deepest case of a model output used as a ruler. Primary CMB quantities, by contrast, are only weakly model-dependent, so the answer is not that everything is uncertain. What the framework cannot return is a numeric per-conclusion sensitivity ranking, and it says so: that is a gap owed to a real cross-model, cross-calibration analysis, with the overlap of apparently independent probes recorded. The shape, the mechanism ranking and the refuters written in advance are checkable now; the numbers are not claimed.
