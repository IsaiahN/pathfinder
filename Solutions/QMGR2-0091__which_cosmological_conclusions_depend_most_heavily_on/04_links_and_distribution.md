## Wider literature that bears on the question

Three clusters, with overlap declared. The five cosmology records are **one evidence pool**, not five witnesses: they cite the same Planck, SH0ES, BAO and distance-ladder results. The metrology and philosophy-of-science records are genuinely lower overlap and corroborate the mechanism shape, not the cosmological facts.

1. **Cosmology, one pool.** Planck 2018 supplies the base-versus-derived distinction. Cyr-Racine supplies the calibrator-scale and sound-horizon mechanism. Knox and Millea supply the model-extrapolation mechanism and the "largely independent of assumed model" contrast. Cai and Wang supply the overlap caveat and the calibrator-dependence of H0. PDG supplies the H0 degeneracy statement.
2. **Metrology, low overlap.** VIM defines calibration, calibration hierarchy and traceability as an unbroken chain in which uncertainty increases at each link. This is the same dependency structure as Fig 3, arrived at in a field that does not study cosmology.
3. **Philosophy of science, low overlap.** SEP's measurement and underdetermination entries state that measurement is theory-laden, that the trouble is sharpest when the hypothesis under test is presupposed in the model of the measuring instrument, and that a failed prediction does not identify which background belief was wrong. This corroborates the anchor-test separator and the reason provenance matters, without touching any cosmological datum.

No world source conflicts with the figure verdict, so the conflict rule was not triggered. Agreement among the cosmology sources is expected because they share data, and it is recorded as one witness.

## Distribution: offer, do not push

Figure-grounded reasons:

- **Fig 10 :52** "Bring a characterised residual, never the source." Sending a polished ranking before anyone has checked the separator sends a recording, not a method.
- **Fig 2 :35** "Alignment is triangulation, not negotiation. A mirror cannot correct you." A list selected for people likely to agree produces a convention.
- **Fig 9 :27-28** "describe it before you go looking, or any frame you pick will seem to fit." Ask a recipient what they would expect to see if the separator were true before giving them the literature.
- **Fig 8 :50-52** convergent derivation and adopted import look identical in the contents; only the record separates them. Ask for the provenance of any calibration a cited paper uses.

**Recommended order in a reply:** (1) the separator and its refuter; (2) ask what the recipient would count as a failure, and record the answer; (3) only then the links, with the note that the figures, not the summary, are the authority. Later agreement is never pooled as corroboration (Fig 2 :40).

## The two-stage offer (with the optional third stage)

1. **Stage 1.** Validate the separator against the ground. The recipient checks whether a named conclusion moves when its anchor is pushed, without holding the framework interior.
2. **Stage 2, opt-in only.** If curious, read the derivation and the links. Their verdict on stage 1 is already recorded, so their later agreement cannot be counted as a second witness.
3. **Stage 3.** Offer the eight-step loop as a method to reuse. Each user writes their own refuter and states their own mode. The loop cannot check the direction of its own question, so a pre-registered failure condition is part of the method, not an optional courtesy.
4. **Hard precondition.** Stages 1 to 3 only work if the stage-1 separator survives. It is `specified`, not `grounded`, and the numeric ranking is an admitted `gap`. Present it that way or the two-stage offer collapses on contact.

## Venue constraint on simulated work

Living Reviews in Relativity states that large language models do not satisfy its authorship criteria, that use of an LLM must be documented in the Methods section, and that copying of wording by generative AI is treated as plagiarism. Other venues in `03` have comparable policies. This folder is a checkable derivation packet, not a manuscript. Do not submit it as independent human work, and state the simulated status of the derivation in any cover note.
