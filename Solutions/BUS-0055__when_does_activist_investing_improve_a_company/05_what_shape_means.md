## 1. A shape is a constraint, not a filler

The canonical README states the limit: the framework returns "what a missing thing would have to look like (a shape, not a name)," then stops at "the limit of what the available instruments can distinguish, and records the lineage so the stop is honest." Fig 9 :32: "you get a kind of thing, never the answer itself."

For this question a **shape** is the narrowed form of the answer we can state before we can name the winning intervention. "Two conditions, per firm, per horizon" is a shape. "Buybacks always help" and "activism always destroys value" are names, and both are out of scope.

## 2. How the shape is obtained: describe the effect, not the cause

Fig 6 :30: "The description travels. Fails by being sharpened into terms only we can read. Cast it in effect terms." Fig 9 :30-31: "your words are borrowed too, so describe the effect rather than the cause."

The effect terms here are: contact with a paying ground, reach or capacity, metric fidelity, and the horizon at which each is read. Those four are stated before any study is named. If the studies had been named first, any of them would have seemed to fit.

## 3. What licenses a name

Only two things, both outside the shape:

1. **The ground settles it.** THE_FORMULA.md step 5: "the ground settles it. Verified only by what pays. A gate is not the ground." For this question that means a firm-level instrument that discriminates a metric change from a capacity change.
2. **A provenanced import from a frame whose closure differs** (Fig 6 :23, Fig 8 :50-52), with a source gate and a shadow test.

The three finance studies are imports from the same frame. Their agreement or disagreement does not license a name.

## 4. The source-discrimination problem is the precise failure mode

"The Hard Problem is About Source" gives the mechanism. An instrument reads green and "it cannot comment on the source"; one green light, yellow plus blue, RGB or CMYK can produce the same reading. "If several paths create the same behavior, then behavior is a projection with lossy information." The essay's conclusion: "The answer is unreached until a better instrument exists," and that instrument, the spectrometer, not an argument, resolved the case.

For BUS-0055 the reading is the announcement return plus the payout ratio plus ROA. The source is either a real capacity change or a channel rearrangement. Those produce the same reading. The literature is measuring the green and naming the source. What is owed is a discriminator, not another pooled regression.

## 5. Why the pooled verdict is the specific forbidden error

Fig 1 :22: "Aggregating across slots is how a live signal disappears into an average." Fig 1 :27: "They are frame-internal. A frame cannot score itself with a quantity it also produces."

A pooled "activism creates value" or "activism destroys long-term value" is a frame scoring itself with a quantity it also produces. The campaign's own metric cannot be the anchor (Fig 2 :30: "If it moves when you push on it, it is not an anchor.").

## 6. Prestige is not action

"The Recursive Self-Improvement Trap" (opened 2026-11-22): "Prestige is not equal to actions. (prestige =/= actions)"; "Prestige must never be allowed to validate an action directly"; "Prestige can fund an action. It can give an action a budget to be tried. But it cannot settle an action. Only the ground can settle it."

A list of famous activist funds or famous critics does not settle the shape. Fame funds the attempt; the ground settles it. This is why `03_public_contact_points.csv` contains venues, not people, and why no receptiveness rank appears anywhere in this folder.

## 7. This audit is a simulation, not a ground reading

Same essay: "LLMs can quickly simulate an approximate answer/output with the formula found in the architecture post" and "the truly testable output would need to be derived from the real architecture or working the formula yourself." So `01_figure_grounded_audit.md` is a simulated derivation checked for internal consistency against the figures. It is not a test of activism and must be labelled as such. It cannot settle anything it reports on.

## 8. Consequence for this question

The honest deliverable of this folder is the shape plus its refuters plus the instrument question:

- shape: two conditions (contact changed, reach renewed), per firm, per horizon, with an activist whose valence is relative to the residual;
- non-name: which specific campaign wins in which specific firm stays an open slot;
- instrument owed: something at the firm that discriminates a moved metric from a changed capacity.

That is a shape, not an answer. The gap is recorded deliberately, not hidden.
