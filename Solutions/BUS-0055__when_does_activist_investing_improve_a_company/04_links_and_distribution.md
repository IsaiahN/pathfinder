## What the wider literature bears on the question

1. **A genuine within-pool conflict, not a consensus.** Bebchuk, Brav and Jiang (2015) test the "myopic-activists" claim over five years and do not find it supported. Cremers, Giambona, Sepe and Wang (2016) replicate the raw result, then show it is affected by selection bias: activists target poorly performing firms, and matched controls improve more. Both use Schedule 13D based samples and the Brav et al. dataset. By Fig 2 :40 this is one pool arguing with itself. The conflict is returned to the census, not resolved by picking a side.
2. **Horizon indexing is the live dispute.** Brav et al. (2008) note that operating improvement "takes longer to happen" and that EBITDA/Assets rises by two years after intervention, while the 7% announcement return has "no reversal during the subsequent year". This is exactly the per-horizon residual in Fig 1 :19. A pooled figure erases it.
3. **The short-term mechanism is a measurement failure.** The Goodhart quote and Fig 10 :47 ("a convention that decides an outcome has stopped being one") name the same structure: a metric becomes a target and stops discriminating. This is the low-overlap witness for F4.
4. **The capacity tradeoff appears in ecology.** The resilience literature's Proposition 11 and the "rigidity trap" describe a system that becomes highly connected and inflexible, with diminished capacity for novelty. This is a separate evidence pool corroborating F5 and F6 at the mechanism level, not a firm-level measurement.

## The two-stage offer

- **Stage 1 before stage 2** is Fig 10 :52 and Fig 2 :45, "Frames range against the ground. They do not negotiate with it." The recipient checks the output without holding the framework's interior.
- **Stage 2 is opt-in by construction.** Once a recipient has absorbed the theory, their agreement stops being evidence. Their stage-1 verdict must be recorded before any theory is shown, and later agreement is never pooled as corroboration (Fig 2 :40).
- **Stage 3 sends a method, which is permitted.** Fig 4 :22: only methods cross up. The formula is a generator, so giving it away does not give away an answer.
- **Stage 3 still needs a ground per problem.** Each user writes down what would refute their result before running it and labels the mode of what they get.
