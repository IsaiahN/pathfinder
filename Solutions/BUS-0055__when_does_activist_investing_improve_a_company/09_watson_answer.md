## QUESTION

When does activist investing improve a company, and when does it push firms toward short-term gains at the expense of long-term capacity? Restated as an effect: when does R = |Γ(b,a) - o'| shrink because the campaign changed contact with a paying ground and renewed reach, and when does it shrink only at the measured horizon while capacity is spent? R is indexed per firm and per horizon, never aggregated (Fig 1 :19). Option A asserts one averaged verdict. Option B keeps two conditions separate and names an instrument for each. Option A loses the signal in an average (Fig 1 :22); Option B preserves it and leaves the winning intervention open.

**Union, not partition.** The whole is a union of C1 (contact changed) and C2 (reach renewed), crossed to four cells (Fig 9 :55, :57). ## VERDICT (from the figures)

The figure-derived verdict: activist valence is relative to the residual, so the same campaign can improve one firm and harm another; the announcement metric is not the anchor because it can be pushed; improvement that leaves contact unchanged changes the reading, not the capability; short-termism is a channel failure in which a measurement becomes a target; extraction and reinvestment are isomers over the same atoms; and which campaign wins in which firm is a `gap` at this budget. The verdicts are the per-domain union kept separate, never averaged. Mode `specified`. The finance pool's internal conflict is a union problem, not a verdict to be chosen. Low-overlap fields corroborate the mechanism, not the firm-level fact.

- **cost if wrong:** misdirected diagnosis and a spent independent reader. A firm whose broken link is perception would be handed a planning lever, and a firm whose channel is saturated would be told to import more; a single lever would be applied to a union that needs two. Fig 2 :42 says "Buy independence in proportion to the cost of being wrong." The cost is bounded and is avoided by leading with the shape claim and its refuter.
- **undoable:** yes. No term is deleted if the shape fails; a refutation is added and the reach falls because something was added rather than anything struck out (Fig 6 :42-43). The shape and its seven falsifiers are recorded with provenance so a later reader can locate the error (Fig 10 :62-64).
- **reversed by:** any of the seven pre-registered falsifiers, in particular a capability gain with no contact change, per-firm horizons that trend together with no cross-over, capacity loss with the channel intact, or one intervention restoring both conditions across all four cells with no categorically different residue. A genuinely independent instrument at the firm showing that the payment stream follows the pushed metric would also reverse it. Until one fires, the verdict stands as `candidate`.
