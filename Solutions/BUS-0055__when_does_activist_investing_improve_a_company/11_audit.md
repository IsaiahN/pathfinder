## Round trip gap R_T

The Symbols table defines R_T as gap(x, (T_E ∘ T_A)(x)), the gap between what was sent up and what came back down. Fig 4 :30 requires it be measured rather than assumed zero. I ran the round trip on the condensation this folder actually uses.

**R_T is nonzero.** Three things the shorthand holds do not survive the trip back down:

- **Third-order cascade.** The shorthand implies capacity loss can be indirect; the concretisation needs full habitat enumeration to the cascade stopping point, which none of the opened sources supplies.
- **Catalyst versus spent part.** Fig 12 :119 names the distinction, but the shorthand carries no test for it; the test (post-exit persistence) had to be added from the finance sources.

One thing survives cleanly: the announcement metric is not the anchor because it can be pushed, and improvement without contact change is not capability change. That is the residue worth sending. R_T is recorded qualitatively, not numerically, because no firm-level ground reading exists. Treating the formula as identical to the figures would set R_T to zero and commit the dead-reckoning error Fig 4 :30 names.
