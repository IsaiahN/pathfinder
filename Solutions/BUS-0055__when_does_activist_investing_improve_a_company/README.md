# Solution BUS-0055: when activist investing improves a company

**Question:** When does activist investing improve a company, and when does it push firms toward short-term gains at the expense of long-term capacity?

**Source:** business.txt line 74

**Section:** 6. Corporate governance, ownership, and finance

**Mode:** `specified` on the substantive verdict, `general` on the structural shape, not `grounded`. No measurement in this run settles which intervention improves which firm.

**Status:** done

**Registry status:** pending (the registry is not written by this subagent)

**Restated as an effect.** The decision is whether a campaign changes the firm's contact with a paying ground and its reachable capacity (improvement), or changes only the reported reading while the underlying capacity is spent (the short-termism charge). Option A treats campaigns as one kind of thing and averages across firms and horizons. Option B sorts campaigns by two conditions kept separate: did contact with a paying ground change, and did reach renew. A loses the signal in an average (Fig 1 :22). B keeps the residual indexed per firm and per horizon (Fig 1 :19).

## What is deliberately not here

- No personal emails of named individuals.
- No ranking of people by how likely they are to agree with the verdict.
- No bulk-send plan.

Three reasons, in the figures' own words:

1. **A room is not an anchor.** "A room's agreement is variation, not a verdict. Selection between rooms is where the ground votes." (Fig 7 :35). Selecting recipients by receptiveness manufactures the correlation that makes their agreement worthless (Fig 2 :40).
2. **Agreement from one pool is one witness.** "agreement about the world is worthless when the agreeing frames share an evidence pool" (Fig 2 :40). The activist-investing literature in this folder is one pool; the ecology and measurement sources are separate pools.
3. **Asking for trust before checking spends independence.** "A verifier that can reconstruct the claim cannot verify it." (Fig 10 :42). The first contact carries the residual and its refuter, never the framework's interiors.

Legally: unsolicited bulk email to scraped personal addresses breaches GDPR and CAN-SPAM style rules in many jurisdictions. That is a practical failure on top of the structural one.
