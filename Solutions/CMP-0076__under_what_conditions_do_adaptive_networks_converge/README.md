# Solution CMP-0076: figure-grounded package

**Question:** Under what conditions do adaptive networks converge to stable structures rather than
continually reorganize?

**Source:** `Questions/complexity science.txt` line 93 (question 76), section 8 "Adaptation,
learning, and evolution".

**Mode:** `specified` on the substantive conditions, `general` on the structural shape. Nothing here
is `grounded`: no measurement of a particular adaptive network was taken in this derivation.

**Status:** done.

**Registry status:** pending.

## What this package answers, in effect terms

An adaptive network is a cycle: node states change, links rewire in response, and the rewired
topology becomes the substrate for the next state update. Two long-run regimes are observed. In one
the wiring settles; in the other one or more slots keep producing positive residual against any
candidate reference. The residual is per node or per link, never a global average (Fig 1 :19).

The Tether's answer is not a single threshold in coupling strength, noise, or rewiring rate. It is a
union of conditions that must hold together, plus an honest limit: the loop has no convergence
criterion of its own. Per `THE_FORMULA.md` (Closure): "No convergence criterion: the loop stops only
when R stops arriving, which is either a perfect prediction or a closed channel, and from inside
those look the same." So "converged" is a `candidate` verdict until an instrument outside the frame
shows that the channel to the ground is still open. That limit is stated as `gap` where it applies.

## Boundary and honest standing

This is a simulated derivation checked for internal consistency against the figures, NOT a ground
reading. The essays state the rule: LLM output is "simulacra" and "the truly testable output would
need to be derived from the real architecture or working the formula yourself" (*The Recursive
Self-Improvement Trap*, Isaiah N, 2026-11-19). It cannot settle the question it reports on. No
adaptive-network experiment, dataset, or theorem was run here. World sources were opened only as
secondary corroboration; they never overrule a figure.

Nothing in this folder is in the Tether repository and no repository file was modified. Sources were
the local canonical figures in `FRAMEWORK/`, the eight essays where cited, and the public URLs in

## What is deliberately not here

- No personal emails of named researchers, and no contact details scraped from private posts.
- No ranking of people by how likely they are to agree with the claim.
- No bulk-send plan.

Reason from the figures: "Alignment is triangulation, not negotiation. A mirror cannot correct you."
(Fig 2 :35), "Correlation has valence." (Fig 2 :39), and agreement that shares an evidence pool is
"disqualifying for a verdict" (Fig 2 :40). A list selected for receptiveness manufactures the
correlation that would make its agreement worthless.
