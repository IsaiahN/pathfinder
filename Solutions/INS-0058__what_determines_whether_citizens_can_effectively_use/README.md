## The answer in one line

Formal existence is a necessary admission, not the determinant. The determinant is composability: whether the right is reachable inside the citizen's closure, within a habitat that supplies the atoms the citizen lacks, measured per citizen against the ground rather than counted on the books.

## What is deliberately not here

- No personal emails of named individuals.
- No ranking of people by how likely they are to agree with the claim.
- No bulk-send plan.

Three reasons in the figures' own words:

1. **Agreement is not an anchor.** Fig 2 :35: "Alignment is triangulation, not negotiation. A mirror cannot correct you." A list selected for receptiveness manufactures the very correlation that would make its agreement worthless.
2. **A shared pool is one witness.** Fig 2 :40: "agreement about the world is worthless when the agreeing frames share an evidence pool." The simulated second seat in `11_audit.md` shares this folder's evidence pool, so its agreement is internal consistency, not corroboration.
3. **Sending the interior spends independence.** Fig 10 :42: "A verifier that can reconstruct the claim cannot verify it." The first contact carries the residual and its refuter, never the framework's interior.

Legally: scraping personal emails for unsolicited bulk outreach breaches GDPR and CAN-SPAM style rules in many jurisdictions. Public opt-in venues avoid that failure as well as the structural one.
