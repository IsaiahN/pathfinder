# Outreach draft — check the residual before you decide about the frame

**Use:** one-to-one, or as a cover note attached to a submission through a public venue in `03_public_contact_points.csv`. **Not** for bulk send.

**Hard precondition:** the three paragraphs before the links are the offer. If you move the links above the residual and refuter, the two-stage structure collapses on contact.

---

**Subject:** One claim about paper rights, and the test that would break it

> Hello,
>
> I have one claim about why a right can exist on the books and still be unusable. I am not asking you to read a theory first, and I am not asking you to agree with me. If the claim fails the test below, there is nothing else to look at.
>
> **The residual (`specified`, not `grounded`).** In many legal systems a right is formally admitted, sometimes after litigation, and citizens still cannot turn it into a remedy. The gap is not between two theories. It is between a term in the legal library and a person's ability to compose over it. In the framework's terms, settling a term and making it usable are two conditions, and only one is named by the law: "A term is settled when the ground pays for it. A term is usable when something can compose over it." A growing catalogue of rights is a growing library, and "a library that grows is not a reach that grows." (Fig 5 :70, :72)
>
> **The claim.** Formal existence is necessary and not sufficient. What determines effective use is composability: whether the right is reachable inside the citizen's own closure, with the habitat supplying the atoms the citizen lacks, measured per citizen at the ground rather than counted on the books. The framework also says capability "is a property of agent-and-habitat, never of the agent alone" (Fig 11 :39), and that "An improvement that does not change contact changes nothing, however much it improves" (Fig 11 :40). So the determinant is neither the citizen's virtue nor the right's wording. It is the contact.
>
> **What I am not claiming.** That any particular institution is *the* answer. The framework narrows a shape, not a name: "you get a kind of thing, never the answer itself" (Fig 9 :32). Legal aid, legal awareness, anti-retaliation protection, identity documents and court access are bindings from the world frame onto that shape. They carry provenance, not derivation. I am also not claiming that a right which fails in practice is a false right, or that courts or laws are illegitimate. A formal right that never registers is a channel that has lost touch with the ground, and the framework says plainly: "the ground does not decay, the channel does" (Fig 10 :24).
>
> **What would refute it, written before this message:**
> 1. a formally admitted right shown to be reliably effective across citizens and conditions with no habitat or closure change;
> 2. an improvement of the citizen alone, with no change in contact, that reliably moves the outcome;
> 3. effective use arising by search alone inside an unchanged closure, with no new atom entering it;
> 4. an aggregate rights count shown to preserve the per-citizen signal at the ground;
> 5. usability settled by professional agreement alone, with no outcome reading, and that agreement predicting outcomes.
>
> If any holds, I retract the claim. A one-line "this fails because..." is more useful to me than agreement. If you would rather not engage, no reply is needed.
>
> **If it survives, and only then:** the sources are below. The figures are the authority, not my summary of them.
> - Verbatim figure text with line numbers (local extract): `FRAMEWORK/reference/03-FIGURES_TEXT.md`
> - Canonical figures README: https://github.com/IsaiahN/tether/blob/general-agent/figures/README.md
> - The figures as SVG: https://github.com/IsaiahN/tether/tree/general-agent/figures
>
> **If you want to reuse the method:** run it on a problem you know well and can check against a ground. Write down what would refute your result *before* you run it. The framework's own limit is that a frame cannot check the direction of its own question, so the write-up warns that outputs can be contrived to fit the formula. A reported failure is more useful to me than a reported success.
>
> With thanks for your time,

## Why this version is ordered this way

| the move | figure-grounded reason |
|---|---|
| Residual and refuter first, theory last | Fig 10 :52, "Bring a characterised residual, never the source." Frames range against the ground separately (Fig 2 :45). |
| Do not ask for agreement | Fig 2 :35, "Alignment is triangulation, not negotiation. A mirror cannot correct you." |
| Five pre-registered refuters | "A falsifier written before the run is a design instrument." |
| Mode stated on the substantive claim | Formula: "Mode. general · specified · grounded. State which." |
| No institution named as the answer | Fig 9 :32, "you get a kind of thing, never the answer itself." |
| No claim that paper rights are false rights | Fig 10 :24, "the ground does not decay, the channel does." A channel failure is not a verdict about the law. |
| No request for trust or prestige | *The Recursive Self-Improvement Trap*: "Prestige can fund an action. It can give an action a budget to be tried. But it cannot settle an action. Only the ground can settle it." |
| Offer the method only at stage 3 | Fig 4 :22, "Only methods cross up. Only head starts come down." A generator can be given away without giving away an answer. |
| Ask users to write their own refuter | Formula "Direction": a frame cannot check what it is looking for; the sought-for shape must be bound as an ordinary term. |
| One-to-one, no bulk send | Fig 2 :40, "agreement about the world is worthless when the agreeing frames share an evidence pool." A list selected for receptiveness manufactures the disqualifying correlation. |

## Stage order

1. **Stage 1:** residual, shape claim, five refuters. Ask what the recipient would count as a failure. Record that answer before any theory is shown.
2. **Stage 2 (opt-in only):** the three links, with the note that the figures, not the summary, are the authority.
3. **Stage 3:** the formula as a reusable method, with the instruction that each user states a mode and writes a refuter before running it.

A stage 1 verdict is a conversation with an outside frame, never corroboration. Its later agreement is not pooled (Fig 2 :40). A refutation is copied into `11_audit.md` with provenance and re-evaluated by the auditor seat as if it came from any other source.
