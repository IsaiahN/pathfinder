# Expert verification packet — the Tether on formal rights and effective use

**What this is:** a claim in a form a legal-access researcher, institutional scholar or development practitioner can check. Mode, derivation and refuters are stated before the conclusion, so a reader knows what to look for and what would settle it.

**Mode: `specified` on the mapping and verdict, `general` on the structural shape, not `grounded`.** No controlled ground reading is claimed. This is a shape and a route, not a result.

**Status of this packet:** a simulated derivation checked for internal consistency against the figures, not a ground reading. By its own rule (*The Recursive Self-Improvement Trap*: "the truly testable output would need to be derived from the real architecture or working the formula yourself"), it cannot settle the question it reports on.

## 1. The claim, in one paragraph

A right that formally exists is a term admitted to the legal library. Using it is a separate matter: whether a person can compose over that term to reach a remedy. Formal existence is necessary and not sufficient, so a growing catalogue of rights is a growing library and not a growing reach. What determines effective use is composability: whether the right is reachable inside the citizen's closure, within a habitat that supplies the atoms the citizen lacks, measured per citizen against an outcome rather than counted on the books. Capability is a property of citizen-and-habitat, never of the citizen alone, and an improvement that does not change contact changes nothing. The framework does not name which institution supplies the missing atoms; it narrows the shape any such institution must have.

## 2. Three checkable sub-claims

### C1 — framework internal; checkable by reading Figure 5

- **Statement:** settling a term and making it usable are two conditions; the law names one.
- **Source, verbatim:** Fig 5 :70 "A term is settled when the ground pays for it. A term is usable when something can compose over it. Those are two conditions and this figure names one." Also Fig 5 :72 "a library that grows is not a reach that grows." Necessity is supplied by Fig 12 :106 "Nothing composes over what was never admitted as a part."
- **What to check:** is the mapping from "right on the books" to "admitted term", and from "citizen can use it" to "compose over it", faithful? The mapping is a `specified` claim, not a figure line.
- **Refuter:** a formally admitted right shown to be reliably effective across citizens and conditions with no habitat or closure change; or effective use of a right never formally admitted, with no enactment. The first collapses the two conditions; the second breaks necessity.

### C2 — framework internal and world facing; checkable by a researcher in access to justice or development

- **Statement:** the determinant of effective use is citizen-and-habitat, not the citizen alone and not the right alone. Reform that changes only the wording changes nothing.
- **Source, verbatim:** Fig 11 :39 "capability is a property of agent-and-habitat, never of the agent alone." Also Fig 11 :40 "An improvement that does not change contact changes nothing, however much it improves." The asymmetry that makes adding possible and removing impossible: Fig 11 :61 "Introduction is available; exclusion is not."
- **What to check:** does the literature support the agent-and-habitat reading? Two independent fields do. *Capability approach* (Wikipedia, development economics): "concentrates on the actual capability of persons to achieve lives they value rather than solely having a right or freedom to do so", with Nussbaum's "combined capabilities" defined as "internal capabilities together with the social/political/economic conditions in which functioning can actually be chosen". *World Justice Project, Measuring the Justice Gap*: "4.5 billion people who are excluded from the opportunities the law provides. These are people who lack legal tools - including identity documents, land or housing tenure, and formal work arrangements". These are two different fields, so their agreement is weak corroboration, not two witnesses to the same figure.
- **Refuter:** a controlled case in which improving the citizen in isolation (legal literacy or motivation only), with no change in habitat or contact, reliably moves the outcome at the ground.

### C3 — measurement claim; checkable by anyone with per-case data

- **Statement:** the verdict must be read per citizen or per case. Rights-on-books and similar totals are frame-internal quantities and cannot witness effective use.
- **Source, verbatim:** Fig 1 :19 "The residual is indexed by object slot, not aggregated." Fig 1 :22 "Aggregating across slots is how a live signal disappears into an average." Fig 1 :27 "A frame cannot score itself with a quantity it also produces." Fig 9 :60 "Use filters for the budget and witnesses for the verdict. Never let a filter hand you a verdict."
- **What to check:** does the available data preserve the per-slot signal, or does reporting at the level of statutes, offices and training sessions erase it? The UN and WJP sources declare the size of the justice gap; LSC reports that low-income Americans received no or insufficient help for 92% of the problems that substantially affected them. Those are per-problem readings, and they are the right shape of evidence.
- **Refuter:** an aggregate rights or resource count shown to predict per-person remedy outcomes as well as a per-person reading does.

## 3. What is explicitly not claimed

- Not that a formal right is false, illusory or illegitimate. It is a necessary term.
- Not that any named institution (legal aid, legal awareness, identity documents, court access, anti-retaliation rules) is figure-derived. Each is an imported binding with provenance in `10_source_ledger.csv` and a shadow test against the gap it claims to explain.
- Not that the citizen is at fault. Capability is agent-and-habitat.
- Not that legislation is useless. The claim is about what determines use, not about whether to legislate.
- Not that the study fields are independent witnesses. Access to justice, legal consciousness, the UN and WJP share a law-and-society pool.
- Not grounded. Nothing here was settled by a controlled measurement.

## 5. What would settle it

An instrument at the middle link, not more agreement. Three tests, restated so they can fire:

1. **Reach test:** for a named right and a named citizen, does the citizen's closure contain the atoms needed to run the route to a remedy, and does an instrument distinguish missing information from retaliation risk from distance? A statute cannot discriminate these, which is the metamerism error from *The Hard Problem is About Source*: "If several paths create the same behavior, then behavior is a projection with lossy information."
2. **Import test:** does supplying one atom change the ground reading, and does it explain the observed gap (Fig 6 :12 to :13, source gate and shadow test)?
3. **Per-slot test:** does the measure track the individual case, or does it average the live signal away (Fig 1 :19, :22)?

The five refuters in `01_figure_grounded_audit.md` §4 and the isomer falsifier in `06_isomers_and_order.md` are copies of the same instruments in different words.

1. Read Fig 5 :69 to :72, Fig 11 :39 to :40 and Fig 1 :19, :22, :27. Four short passages, about three minutes.
2. Check C2 against your own field: does the agent-and-habitat claim match what the data actually show? Name one dataset that would test it (about five minutes).
3. Name one instrument that would distinguish missing information from missing safety at the individual case level, or say why none is possible (about five minutes).
