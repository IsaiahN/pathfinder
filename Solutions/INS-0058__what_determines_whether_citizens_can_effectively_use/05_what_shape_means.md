## 1. A shape is a constraint, not a filler

The figures README states the limit: the framework returns "what a missing thing would have to look like (a shape, not a name)". Fig 9 :32: "you get a kind of thing, never the answer itself." Fig 13 says it "can say the answer is out of reach with the library as it stands... It cannot say which primitive is missing, because naming it would mean already holding it."

From *The Library of Babel*, re-opened this session: "you can cut your search short if the set of books returned is small and descriptive enough that it provides a general shape of the answer that satisfies your question."

So a **shape** is the narrowed form of an answer you can state before you can name it. It constrains; it does not fill. For this question:

- `formal right on the books` is a name we already hold;
- `effective use by this citizen` is an outcome at the ground;
- **the shape of the determinant** is: composability of the admitted term in the citizen's closure, within a habitat, measured per slot.

That shape does not say *legal aid*, *legal awareness*, *court access* or *anti-retaliation protection*. Those are names. It says what any name must do: supply the missing atom that lets the citizen compose over the right.

## 2. How a shape is obtained

- *Library of Babel*: use the vocabulary you already have that is closest to the thing, look for recurring new terms that overlap or correlate with it, recurse. "With each new term gained, you narrow the space and deepen your vocabulary."
- Fig 6 :30: "The description travels. Fails by being sharpened into terms only we can read. Cast it in effect terms." Fig 9 :30 to :31: "your words are borrowed too, so describe the effect rather than the cause."
- *Decoding Tacit Knowledge*, re-opened this session: "When contact isn't possible, the best you can send is a plain description of what you'd expect to see, because anyone can check that against the world."
- *Library of Babel* on stopping: "the finish line will also be the starting place". The paradox is that by then you have "composed the answer through the process of locating it."

Applied here: the effect description is "a right exists and this person still cannot obtain the remedy". That description travels across law, development economics, sociology and practice, which is why the WATSON world section can use low-overlap fields. Naming "legal aid" before describing the effect would be picking a frame first, which Fig 8 :53 forbids: "Residual first, frame second."

## 3. What licenses a name

Only two things, both outside the shape itself:

1. **The ground settles it.** Formula step 5: "the ground settles it. Verified only by what pays. A gate is not the ground." The README: the framework "stops at the limit of what the available instruments can distinguish, and records the lineage so the stop is honest."
2. **A provenanced import from a frame whose closure differs** (Fig 6 :23, Fig 8 :50 to :52), with a source gate and a shadow test.

*They're Scanning Books* sharpens this: "The real import is not a new term in the existing space. It is a new dimension of the space." Also: "Declaring the primitives does not make them neutral. It makes the choice visible."

For this question the institutions named in `01` and `09` are imports from the world frame. Each has a source gate (a real URL and a quote in `10_source_ledger.csv`) and a shadow test (it explains the observed reach failure). None is claimed as a figure-derived primitive. The World Justice Project's missing identity documents and tenure are a particularly clean import: they are atoms absent from a citizen's closure, and their absence explains why a formally available service cannot be reached.

## 4. Why the "paper right" diagnosis is not a source error

*The Hard Problem is About Source*, re-opened this session, gives the mechanism. An instrument reads green and "cannot comment on the source". One green light, or yellow plus blue, or RGB, or CMYK, or a scheme not yet discovered can all give the same reading. "If several paths create the same behavior, then behavior is a projection with lossy information." And: "An instrument that can't distinguish these cases demonstrates its own inadequacy." It was the spectrometer, not an argument, that resolved it.

Applied here: a statute book is an instrument that reads "right exists". It cannot comment on the source of effective use. Counting rights is reading the projection and naming the source off it. The metamerism error is exactly the error this question warns against: the same formal right can be effective for one citizen and inert for another, so the reading does not determine the source. What is owed is a better instrument at the ground, per slot, not a new proclamation.

## 5. The specific correction

**I initially treated the world sources' agreement as if it licensed a name.** It does not. Namati, the World Justice Project, LSC, the UN and the capability literature all point toward access, resources and empowerment, but their agreement is either a shared law-and-society evidence pool or, in the capability case, a low-overlap corroboration of a figure verdict that already stands. Under Fig 2 :40, "agreement about the world is worthless when the agreeing frames share an evidence pool." Under Fig 8 :50 to :52, convergent derivation and adopted import look identical in the contents and only the record separates them. The names are therefore recorded as bindings with provenance, and the shape stays open.

This correction is kept visible rather than erased. The earlier reading was not silly: it is what the practice literature itself says. It was just a name where the framework returns a shape.

## 6. Two consequences for outreach

**Prestige is not action.** From *The Recursive Self-Improvement Trap*, re-opened this session: "Prestige must never be allowed to validate an action directly" and "Prestige can fund an action. It can give an action a budget to be tried. But it cannot settle an action. Only the ground can settle it." A campaign that gathers eminent names to agree that paper rights are a problem is the named collapse, not corroboration. The legitimate ask is the opposite: give the shape claim a budget to be tried and let an outcome settle it.

**My own audit is a simulation, not a ground reading.** Same essay: "LLMs can quickly simulate an approximate answer/output with the formula" and "the truly testable output would need to be derived from the real architecture or working the formula yourself." So `01_figure_grounded_audit.md` is a simulated derivation checked for internal consistency against the figures. It is not a test of the claim and must be labelled as such. It cannot settle anything it reports on.

## 7. Honest stop

The determinant's shape is reachable from the figures. The determinant's name is unreached at this budget, and an honest `unreached` is a verdict, not a silence: Fig 8 :39, "So a search that finds nothing is never a proof of absence." What would close it is an instrument at the middle of Fig 3's chain that reads, for a named right and a named citizen, what would count as progress and whether it was obtained. Until such an instrument is run, the answer stays `specified` and `candidate`.
