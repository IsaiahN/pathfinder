## QUESTION

Decide in effect terms what determines whether a citizen can effectively use a legal right that formally exists. Option A: formal existence is the determinant, so the measure of success is rights on the books. Option B: formal existence is necessary and not sufficient, so the determinant is composability of the right in the citizen's closure within a habitat, measured per citizen at the ground. What each leads to: A counts predicates minted and mistakes a growing library for a growing reach; B instruments the middle link, records the lineage, and states five refuters before any theory is shown.

## DOMAIN CENSUS

R by arity, symmetry and scale, then one line per domain with actors / currency / anchor, partition or union, and order by dependency.

- **R (the residual).** The gap between a right admitted into the legal library and a citizen's ability to turn it into a remedy. Arity: two conditions, so a **union compound** (Fig 9 :55, "a case in two conditions is not a harder case of either"), not one hard question. Symmetry: asymmetric; a written right is a record and a used right is a capability, so a generator crosses where an instance does not. Scale: the individual case (one citizen, one slot) and the institution. R is indexed per slot (Fig 1 :19), never aggregated.
- **Domain 1, formal law.** Actors: legislatures, statutes, constitutions, the enrolled text. Currency: formal admission and legal validity. Anchor: the authoritative legal record. This anchor can settle "does the right exist?" and cannot settle "can it be used?". Question type: one condition of the union.
- **Domain 2, effective use.** Actors: the citizen, the adjudicator, the remedy. Currency: realized outcomes. Anchor: the actual outcome for a person (Fig 2 :29, "An anchor is whatever settles the question and does not change its answer because of what any frame thinks"). Question type: the second condition of the union; the ground for the question.
- **Domain 3, habitat.** Actors: legal aid, awareness, anti-retaliation rules, court access, documents, time, money, kin and community, employers, state. Currency: contact relations and cascades. Anchor: still the ground; the seat may introduce but not subtract. Question type: the region where the union's intersection lives.
- **Domain 4, verification.** Actors: researchers, courts, oversight bodies, the second seat. Currency: residuals and agreement. Anchor: the ground, never agreement. In this simulated run all seats draw on one evidence pool, so this domain's independence is zero.
- **Order by dependency (Fig 3).** Admission before use; affordance before diagnosis; measurement last. Fig 3 :20: "The chain is ordered by dependency, not by importance. Each link consumes what the one above produces". Describe the residual before choosing a frame (Fig 8 :53).

Strain test applied: Fig 5's "settling is not sufficient" and Fig 12 :107's "Settling compresses the description" concern different quantities (composability versus what is recorded), so re-census dissolves the apparent contradiction. One strain remains and is named in the verdict.

## VERDICT (from the figures)

Formal existence is a necessary admission and not the determinant. What determines effective use is composability of the right inside the citizen's closure, within a habitat that supplies the missing atoms, measured per citizen at the ground. Capability belongs to citizen-and-habitat; reform that changes only the statute does not change contact and therefore changes nothing. The determinant is a shape, not a name: the specific institutions are imported bindings with provenance and a shadow test, not figure-derived primitives. Two systems with identical formal rights and different access arrangements are isomers under Fig 12 :117, same atoms, different bond, different substance. This is a `specified`, ungrounded, candidate result. The one live strain: the figures narrow a shape while the assigned research field asks for named institutions, and that gap is recorded as `R_T` in `11_audit.md` rather than filled by assertion.

- **cost if wrong:** bounded and locatable. The packet states mode, sub-claims and refuters before the conclusion, so an error is findable rather than invisible. The real cost is spending the independence of outside readers by sending a count or a theory instead of a generator, which Fig 2 :42 prices: "Buy independence in proportion to the cost of being wrong." That cost is avoidable by leading with the shape claim and its refuters. A second cost is political: calling a paper right a false right would overreach the evidence and could discredit legitimate reform.
- **undoable:** yes. No primitive is withdrawn. The institutional bindings are removable without touching the derivation; `07_the_tether_answer.md` and `08_expert_verification_packet.md` already state them as bindings. A sent claim can be superseded by a corrected packet, and a refutation adds a term that refuses without deleting the record.
- **reversed by:** a ground reading, not agreement. A per-slot instrument at the middle link that discriminates missing information from missing safety would name the slot; a controlled citizen-only improvement that reliably moves outcomes would break Fig 11 :40; an aggregate count that preserves the per-slot signal would break Fig 1 :19; a formally admitted right that works with no habitat change would break Fig 5 :70-72; a systematic absence of difference between isomeric systems would break the Fig 12 :117 claim. Until one fires, the verdict stands as `candidate`.
