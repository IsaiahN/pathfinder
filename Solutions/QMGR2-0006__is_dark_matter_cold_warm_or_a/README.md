## What an answer would look like (Direction)

Before running the loop I bind the sought-for shape as an ordinary term, per `THE_FORMULA.md` "Direction": a frame cannot check the direction of its own question, so write down what an answer would look like and let it accrue a residual.

An answer here would be: (1) a statement of the residual per observational slot, never a single averaged verdict; (2) a shape, namely the existence, location and redshift evolution of a scale-dependent suppression in the matter power spectrum, rather than the name of a particle; (3) three domain-specific verdicts kept separate (particle frame, structure frame, instrument frame); (4) the unbundling of "mixture" into a specific union compound with its own loop; (5) a pre-registered refuter naming what the ground would have to return; and (6) an honest stop at the limit of the instruments available. Anything that simply picks one of the three labels and averages the probes is *not* an answer in this sense.

## What is deliberately not here

- No personal emails of named individuals, and no purchased or scraped contact list.
- No ranking of people by how likely they are to agree that the answer is cold, warm or mixed.
- No bulk-send plan.
- No claim that this derivation settles the physics.

Three reasons, in the figures' own words:

1. **Frames must be corrected by the ground, not by each other.** Fig 2 :35 "Alignment is triangulation, not negotiation. A mirror cannot correct you." A list selected for receptiveness manufactures the correlation that would make its agreement worthless (Fig 2 :39-40 "disqualifying for a verdict: agreement about the world is worthless when the agreeing frames share an evidence pool").
2. **The verifier must not hold the interiors.** Fig 10 :42 "A verifier that can reconstruct the claim cannot verify it." The first contact carries the residual and its refuter, never the framework.
3. **Declared interest is disqualifying, undeclared interest runs unchallenged.** Fig 10 :59 "an interest in the outcome is unavoidable, an interest in the reading is disqualifying." This audit declares its stake: it is a simulated derivation produced by the same model that wrote the packet, so its agreement with the packet is internal consistency, not corroboration.
