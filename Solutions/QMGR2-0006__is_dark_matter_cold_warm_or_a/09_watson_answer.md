## VERDICT (from the figures)

The shape survives; every averaged label and every particle name from a projection does not. The residuals are per slot, so no single *cold*, *warm* or *mixed* verdict is licensed. Cold and warm are threshold regions over one ordering and are isomers over the six-term set. A mixture is a union compound that must be unbundled before it is searched. The best discriminators are probes with distinct systematics, with the cutoff's redshift evolution at z greater than 5 and percent-level 21 cm at z greater than 15 as the instruments still to come. This is a `specified`, ungrounded, candidate result. What would not survive: a claim that a lower limit is a detection, a claim that warmth solves the small-scale problems on its own, or a single global fit that averaged the live slots away.

- **cost if wrong:** bounded and locatable. The packet is mode-labelled, states refuters before the conclusion and records the lineage, so an error is findable rather than invisible. The real cost is spending the independence of outside readers by sending a particle name or an averaged verdict instead of a shape and a refuter; Fig 2 :42 "Buy independence in proportion to the cost of being wrong" prices that cost, and it is avoidable by leading with the shape claim.
- **undoable:** yes. The three averaged readings (one label, one name, one fits-all fit) are removable without touching the derivation; `07_the_tether_answer.md` and `08_expert_verification_packet.md` already remove them. No primitive has to be withdrawn, and a sent claim can be superseded by a corrected packet.
- **reversed by:** a ground reading, not agreement. A source-discriminating measurement at z greater than 5 showing the cutoff's thermal scaling with no warm component, or percent-level `P(k)` at z greater than 15 separating free-streaming from interacting DM, would name the slot. All small-scale slots resolving to baryonic feedback alone would answer *cold* by the ground. A seventh primitive would break the six-term grammar. Until one of these fires, the verdict stands as `candidate`.
