## 1. A shape is a constraint, not a filler

The canonical local rule: the framework returns "what a missing thing would have to look like (a shape, not a name)." Fig 9 :32 "you get a kind of thing, never the answer itself." Fig 13 :134 "Unreachable is a verdict here, not a silence". From *The Library of Babel*: "You can cut your search short if the set of books returned is small and descriptive enough that it provides a general shape of the answer that satisfies your question."

For this question the shape is **not** "a 7 keV sterile neutrino" and not "cold dark matter is right." It is:

> a scale-dependent suppression in the matter power spectrum, located at a cutoff `k_fs` (or half-mode mass `M_hm`), whose redshift evolution can in principle discriminate a thermal IGM source from free-streaming dark matter.

That constrains; it does not fill. It says what any answer must look like before saying which answer is true. The three labels in the question are regions over this shape: "cold" is suppression below all probed scales, "warm" is suppression at a dwarf-galaxy scale, "mixed" is two components with potentially different cutoffs. None of them is the shape itself.

## 2. How a shape is obtained

- *Library of Babel* on vocabulary by proximity: use "the existing vocabulary you do know that is closest to the words that you wish to describe and grasp the shape of." Then: "With each new term gained, you are able to narrow the space by definition and deepen your vocabulary."
- Fig 6 :30 "The description travels. Fails by being sharpened into terms only we can read. Cast it in effect terms." Fig 9 :30-31 "your words are borrowed too, so describe the effect rather than the cause."
- The effect terms here are: cutoff location, cutoff redshift scaling, subhalo mass-function suppression, and the instrument that could see each. The cause terms to avoid are particle names and production mechanisms until a ground settles them.
- *Decoding Tacit Knowledge*: "When contact isn't possible, the best you can send is a plain description of what you'd expect to see, because anyone can check that against the world." For outreach this means stating the expected suppression and asking what would count as its absence.
- *Library of Babel* on stopping: "the finish line will also be the starting place" and "you have already composed the answer through the process of locating it." For this question, the stop is reached when independent probes with distinct systematics all return the same cutoff scaling, not when one more model is fitted.

## 3. What licenses a name

Two things, both outside the shape itself:

1. **The ground settles it.** `THE_FORMULA.md` step 5: "the ground settles it. Verified only by what pays." For dark matter this means a source-discriminating measurement (cutoff redshift evolution, 21 cm at high z) or a direct detection. A lower limit is not such a measurement (Fig 9 :60 "Use filters for the budget and witnesses for the verdict. Never let a filter hand you a verdict").
2. **A provenanced import from a frame whose closure differs** (Fig 6 :23, Fig 8 :50-52), with a source gate and shadow test. Fig 8 :50-52: "Convergent derivation and adopted import look identical in the contents. Only the record of where each came from separates them." Naming a sterile neutrino from a power-spectrum fit is an adopted import with no source gate; it must be recorded as such.

"They're Scanning Books" (item 7) sharpens the distinction between a new term and a new dimension of the space; the local canonical rule is that naming a primitive from a reading is what the shape-not-a-name limit forbids. The six-term set is a human guess at the compression axes rather than nature's own list, and declaring it makes that choice visible, not neutral (item 7, as carried by the exemplar mapping; not re-fetched this session).

## 4. The ground and the source problem

*The Hard Problem is About Source* gives the mechanism. Metamerism: an instrument reads green and "the instrument is speaking only about what it measured, it cannot comment on the source." Several paths, one reading. "If several paths create the same behavior, then behavior is a projection with lossy information (it cannot define consciousness in full)." The color case "was proven wrong not by argument, but by the arrival of a better instrument." And: "The answer is unreached until a better instrument exists."

Applied here: the Lyman-alpha flux power-spectrum cutoff is a projection. Doppler broadening, Jeans smoothing and DM free-streaming all produce it. The 2018 MNRAS paper read this session says the cutoff fits CDM with a thermal history and also fits roughly 7 keV sterile neutrinos. Naming the source from that reading is the metamerism error. What is owed is a source-discriminating instrument, not another fit. Fig 2 :37-38 "instruments can be wrong on axes they do not measure."

## 5. Answer to the shape question

**What is the canonical shape here?** A scale-dependent suppression with a redshift-dependent signature, per slot, with the three labels treated as regions and unions over it.

**What is refused?** Any answer that (a) averages the probes, (b) names a particle from a projection, (c) converts a lower bound into a detection, or (d) treats "mixture" as a third label instead of a union compound requiring unbundling (Fig 9 :54 "And when the split lands on a union rather than a partition, unbundle before you search again").

**Does this derivation commit the error the README forbids?** It did in an earlier draft, which asserted warmth from the cutoff. That reading is retracted in `01_figure_grounded_audit.md` section 5. ## 6. Consequences for contact

**Prestige is not action.** From *The Recursive Self-Improvement Trap*: "Prestige can fund an action. It can give an action a budget to be tried. But it cannot settle an action. Only the ground can settle it. The Ground Maintainer must be blind to prestige." A campaign that gathers famous names to agree that the answer is cold or warm is the named collapse, not corroboration. The legitimate ask is the opposite: give the shape claim a budget to be tried and let an instrument settle it.

**My own audit is a simulation, not a ground reading.** Same essay: "LLMs can quickly simulate an approximate answer/output with the formula found in the architecture post" and "the truly testable output would need to be derived from the real architecture or working the formula yourself". So `01` and this file are simulated derivations, checked for internal consistency against the figures. They are not tests of the physics and must be labelled as such. They cannot settle the question they report on.

**Contact follows the shape, not the crowd.** Fig 10 :52 "Bring a characterised residual, never the source." Send the suppression claim and its refuter first. Record the reader's verdict before showing any framework. Never pool later agreement as corroboration (Fig 2 :39-40).
