# Expert verification packet: the Tether on cold, warm and mixed dark matter

**What this is:** a claim in a form a cosmologist or particle astrophysicist can check. Mode, derivation and refuters are stated before the conclusion, so a reader knows what to look for and what would settle it.

**Mode: `specified` on the shape and the per-domain verdicts, `general` on the structural grammar. Not `grounded`.** No measurement in this simulated derivation is claimed to settle which option nature realised.

**Status:** a simulated derivation checked for internal consistency against the figures, not a ground reading. It cannot settle the claim it reports on.

## 1. The claim, in one paragraph

The residuals in this question are indexed by probe, so averaging them into a single "cold versus warm" verdict discards the signal. The figure-governed output is a shape: a scale-dependent suppression in the matter power spectrum at a cutoff `k_fs` or half-mode mass `M_hm`, with a redshift evolution that can in principle separate a thermal IGM source from free-streaming dark matter. Cold and warm are threshold regions over that ordering and are isomers over the same six-term substrate; a mixture is a union compound of two conditions that must be unbundled before it is searched again. The framework returns a shape, not a particle name. The observations that best distinguish the options are those with distinct systematics, with the cutoff's redshift evolution and high-redshift 21 cm as the discriminators still to come.

### C2 — physics-facing; checkable by a cosmologist against the literature

- **Refuter:** a source-discriminating measurement showing no cutoff where the shape predicts one, or a proof that redshift scaling cannot separate thermal from free-streaming sources even in principle. - **Refuter:** demonstrate that the mapping from particle mass to observables is invertible and independent of baryonic history, so `R_T` is zero, or produce a ground reading that discriminates sources without any instrument extension.

## 3. What is explicitly not claimed

- Not a verdict that dark matter is cold, warm or mixed. The shape is claimed; the occupation of the slot is not.
- Not a particle name. Sterile neutrino, thermal relic, axion and fuzzy DM are bindings from the particle frame, provenanced as such.
- Not that a lower limit is a detection. Bounds are filters (C3).
- Not that the small-scale problems are solved by warmth; the 2015 PNAS review says WDM on its own does not fix rotation curves across galaxy masses.
- Not that a mixture is excluded. Mixed models are constrained, not ruled out.
- Not `grounded`. This is a simulated derivation, and this packet states so on its face.

## 5. What would settle it

1. The cutoff's redshift evolution measured across z greater than 5 against the thermal scaling.
2. Percent-level `P(k)` at z greater than 15 from 21 cm, separating free-streaming from collisional damping.
3. Independent probes with distinct systematics returning the same cutoff scale.
4. Any of the four falsifiers in `01_figure_grounded_audit.md` section 3 firing, in which case the shape is withdrawn or narrowed.
