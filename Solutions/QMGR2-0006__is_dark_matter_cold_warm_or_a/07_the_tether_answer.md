# The Tether's answer: Is dark matter cold, warm, or a mixture, and what observations best distinguish these possibilities?

**Mode: `specified` on the shape and on each per-domain verdict, `general` on the structural grammar. Not `grounded`.** This is a simulated derivation checked against the figures (`05_what_shape_means.md` section 6), not a ground reading. It cannot settle which option nature realised.

## 1. The residual it answers

The residual is not one number. It is indexed by slot (Fig 1 :19 "The residual is indexed by object slot, not aggregated"): large-scale structure is fit by cold collisionless dark matter, while dwarf satellites, inner halo profiles and the Lyman-alpha cutoff leave live residuals, and strong lensing, stellar streams and future 21 cm probe different scales with different systematics. Fig 9 :4-5 "Disagreements that do not shrink with effort, where each rule keeps working well on a different subset, are not one hard question but several well-formed ones. Split it rather than search."

So the question as posed is a **union compound**, not one hard problem. Cold, warm and mixed name regions and combinations of one ordering (a suppression scale), and "mixture" is two conditions binding at once, which Fig 9 :57-58 "a case in two conditions is not a harder case of either. It stops being a mixture and becomes a specific compound" requires us to unbundle.

## 2. The shape

1. **The figure-governed output is a shape, not a name.** Fig 9 :32 "you get a kind of thing, never the answer itself." The shape is a scale-dependent suppression in the matter power spectrum, located at a cutoff `k_fs` or half-mode mass `M_hm`, with a redshift evolution that could separate a thermal IGM source from free-streaming DM. Fig 13 :20 "ordering, so more and less can be said" and Fig 13 :22 "a difference that can be spent" name the spectrum and the gradient that write it.
2. **Cold and warm are threshold regions over one ordering, hence isomers.** Fig 12 :117 "isomer. Same atoms, different bond, different substance. The difference is invisible in the formula and decisive in the behaviour." Both are composed over the same six-term set; what differs is the scale of the gradient and which structure mediates the state pair. No seventh primitive is needed (Fig 13 :115 "The set is closed. The arrangements are not. That gap is why import exists").
3. **A mixture is a union compound, not a third same-kind label.** Unbundle into: is there a suppression (U1); is its source free-streaming or baryonic / in-situ physics (U2); if a warm component exists, what fraction and cutoff (U3). Each has its own loop and its own ground. `06_isomers_and_order.md` carries the full derivation and the falsifiers.
4. **The per-domain verdicts, kept separate and never averaged:**
   - **D1 particle / early universe:** ab initio physics sets a transfer function `T(k)`. Large-scale structure already rules out hot dark matter and constrains the warm tail. Verdict: the particle frame supplies the *shape vocabulary*, not the answer. `specified`.
   - **D2 nonlinear structure:** baryonic feedback and DM temperature are overlapping conditions. The small-scale residuals do not by themselves discriminate them. Verdict: union, unbundle required. `specified`.
   - **D3 instruments:** the cutoff is a projection with several sources (Doppler, Jeans, free-streaming). Lyman-alpha at high z is the current best discriminator; 21 cm above z 15 is the next, and distinguishing WDM from interacting DM needs percent-level `P(k)` there. Verdict: **unreached at this budget, not unreachable in principle** (Fig 8 :39 "So a search that finds nothing is never a proof of absence").
   - **D4 verification:** in this run all frames share one evidence pool, so agreement is internal consistency, not corroboration (Fig 2 :39-40 "disqualifying for a verdict: agreement about the world is worthless when the agreeing frames share an evidence pool").
5. **Limits are filters, not witnesses.** Fig 9 :60 "Use filters for the budget and witnesses for the verdict. Never let a filter hand you a verdict." The 2013, 2021, 2023 and 2026 mass bounds say which models get tried with which instrument. None is a detection of cold or warm dark matter.

## 3. What it explicitly does not say

- Not "dark matter is cold." The large-scale fit is real, but it does not see the cutoff slots.
- Not "dark matter is warm." The cutoff has multiple sources, and the same flux power spectrum fits CDM with a thermal history and roughly 7 keV sterile neutrinos.
- Not "a mixture is ruled out." Mixed models are constrained, not excluded; the mixture question is a union that must be unbundled before it is searched again.
- Not a particle name. Sterile neutrino, thermal relic, axion and fuzzy DM are bindings from the particle frame onto the shape, provenanced as such, not derivations.
- Not that the small-scale problems are solved by any of the three options. WDM alone does not fix rotation-curve shapes across galaxy masses (PNAS 2015 review).
- Not `grounded`. No measurement in this simulated derivation settles `left(R,φ)` for any option.
- Not a theory of everything. No final room; the high-redshift instrument question stays open.

## 4. What would settle it: the ground

A source-discriminating instrument, not another fit. From *The Hard Problem is About Source*: an instrument that reads green "cannot comment on the source"; the color case was resolved by a spectrometer. Here the corresponding questions are:

- **Cutoff redshift scaling.** The 2018 MNRAS paper reports the DM free-streaming cutoff scales with redshift while the thermal cutoffs do not. An instrument that measures the scaling across z greater than 5 would name the source.
- **21 cm at z greater than 15.** Percent-level `P(k)` there would separate warm free-streaming from collisional damping (Mosbech et al. 2023). Fig 3 :27 "A link with no instrument is not a link that works. It is a link nobody has looked at."
- **Probe independence.** Two probes with distinct systematics returning the same cutoff scaling would be triangulation; two probes sharing a simulation pool would be one witness. Fig 2 :31-32 "Independence, between two frames, is how little they draw on the same evidence."
- **Standing falsifiers from `01`, written before the run:** independent probes whose discrepancy does not shrink; a source-discriminating measurement showing no cutoff; all slots resolving to baryons alone; or a required seventh primitive.

## 5. The honest stop

**Unreached, not unreachable.** The six-term set is a visible guess at compression axes, not nature's own list. The instrument that would close the high-redshift link is being built (SKA), and the 2026 filament study shows which instrument axis cannot carry the signal, so the stop is at the limit of available instruments rather than at a claimed absence. "Unreachable is a verdict here, not a silence" (Fig 13 :134). The lineage is recorded so the stop is checkable; indiscernibility is not identity.
