**Subject:** A checkable shape for when diversity destabilises, and the one thing that would break it

> Hi [name],
>
> I have one specific output about the diversity-stability question that I would like you to check against what you already know. I am not asking you to read the framework first, and I am not asking you to agree with it. If the output does not survive, there is nothing else to look at.
>
> **The output (`specified`, not `grounded`):** "diversity" is not the governing quantity. What governs the sign of a perturbation response is (1) whether the interaction web contains a signed loop whose output re-enters as input, (2) whether species responses are independent or read the same environmental signal, and (3) whether the stability metric hides one live species slot inside an aggregate. The figures put it this way: "same atoms, different bond, different substance" (Fig 12 :117), "the sign of the loop decides whether it closes" (Fig 12 :121), and "agreement compounds, evidence does not" (Fig 7 :13). So a diverse ecosystem becomes less stable through interaction-amplified fluctuations when its web has an amplifying loop, its species respond in overlapping ways, and its aggregate metric cannot show the single species that is running away. Diversity count only matters through those three.
>
> **What this does not claim:** it is not a threshold diversity, it does not say that diversity is generally bad, and it does not derive a critical connectance or a leading eigenvalue from the framework. The framework returns a shape, not a number. The number has to come from your ground, not from me.
>
> **What would refute the shape:**
> 1. a web with all loops damping and all response correlations near zero that still shows growing per-species fluctuations after a small perturbation, with external forcing ruled out;
> 2. holding the interaction web and response correlations fixed, stability changing monotonically with species count alone;
> 3. an aggregate stability metric that provably tracks every species slot one-to-one while one slot is still amplifying.
>
> I wrote these down before sending this. If any one holds, the shape is wrong and I will withdraw it. A one-line "this fails because..." is more useful to me than agreement. If you would rather not engage, no reply is needed.
>
