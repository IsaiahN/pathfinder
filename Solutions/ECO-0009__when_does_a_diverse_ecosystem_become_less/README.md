## What is being decided, in effect terms

The effect to be explained: a system with more tracked object slots (species) shows *larger*, not smaller, per-slot fluctuation residuals after a perturbation, and the pathway runs through species-to-species interaction rather than through an external forcing or the loss of a single key actor. R is indexed per species and per time, never as one ecosystem-wide average (Fig 1 :19, Fig 1 :22).

Two answers are being compared:

- **A:** species count is itself the governing quantity, so more diversity means less stability as a direct law.
- **B:** the governing quantities are the *arrangement* of interaction bonds and the *correlation* of species responses; diversity only enters through those, so a diverse ecosystem can be stable when its interactions are coherently arranged and its responses are independent.

What an answer would look like, bound as an ordinary term before the run: a set of conditions on the interaction structure and response structure that decide the sign of the response to a perturbation, with a stated instrument that could fire against each condition. That binding is the sought-for shape made refusable (Formula, "Direction"; Fig 5 :65-66).

## What survives

Condition B. The figures do not support a diversity-count law. They support a union of three conditions whose coincidence is what the question describes: (1) an amplifying, signed interaction cycle; (2) overlapping, correlated species responses, so that diversity supplies no independent insurance; (3) an aggregated stability metric that hides the live per-slot residual. Each keeps its own verdict and its own ground. The numeric threshold at which a particular ecosystem crosses over is a declared `gap`, not a hidden answer.

## What is deliberately not here

- No personal emails of named individuals.
- No ranking of people by how likely they are to agree.
- No bulk-send plan.

The reason is structural, not just legal. "High correlation is disqualifying for a verdict and efficient for a convention" (Seat Map :163, see local framework), and "A verifier that can reconstruct the claim cannot verify it" (Fig 10 :42). A list selected for receptiveness manufactures the very correlation that would make its agreement worthless. Public, opt-in venues in `03` are contact routes, not witnesses.
