# The Tether's answer: when does diversity destabilise through amplified fluctuations?

**Mode: `specified` on the per-domain verdicts, `general` on the structural shape, not `grounded`.** Simulated derivation checked for internal consistency against the figures (`05_what_shape_means.md` §6), not a ground reading. No ecosystem was measured.

## 1. The residual it answers

An ecosystem with more species slots shows growing per-slot fluctuation residuals after a perturbation, and the growth is carried by species-to-species interaction. The residual is indexed per species and per time, never aggregated (Fig 1 :19, Fig 1 :22). The question is a union compound, not one hard law (Fig 9 :55-57): it bundles a dynamical claim, a statistical claim and a measurement claim.

## 2. The shape, one verdict per domain

**D1, habitat and valence.** A community is enumerated into actors, conditions and relations (Fig 11 :43-47), not counted. "An actor's valence is relative to the residual, not a property it carries" (Fig 11 :49); "the same actor is a benefactor to one residual and a malfactor to another" (Fig 11 :50). Verdict: species number is not the governing quantity. Mode `general` structurally, `specified` here. Triangulated (Fig 11 plus Fig 4 :20).

**D2, interaction-bond structure.** Two webs over the same species list are isomers: "same atoms, different bond, different substance. The difference is invisible in the formula and decisive in the behaviour" (Fig 12 :117). "The arrangement is the substance" (Fig 12 :94). Verdict: stability differences at fixed diversity are carried by the arrangement. Mode `general` for the isomer rule, `specified` for the ecological binding. Triangulated (Fig 12 plus Fig 13 :124).

**D3, amplification.** "a remainder compounding through a chain that amplifies is chaos" (Fig 13 :31); "the sign of the loop decides whether it closes, and a chain cannot close by construction" (Fig 12 :121). Verdict: the mechanism named in the question requires a signed return loop; a long chain is not enough. Mode `general` for the sign rule. Triangulated (Fig 13 plus Fig 12).

**D4, response correlation.** Independence is how little two responders draw on the same evidence (Fig 2 :31); "agreement compounds, evidence does not" (Fig 7 :13); "two frames that overlap almost completely are one frame wearing two names" (Fig 8 :49). Verdict: when species read the same environmental signal, their fluctuations compound and diversity buys no independent insurance; when responses are independent, the union adds reach (Fig 8 :45). Mode `specified`, with the cross-kind mapping declared and its strain named in `01` section 4. Triangulated (Fig 2, Fig 7, Fig 8).

**D5, detection.** "The residual is indexed by object slot, not aggregated" (Fig 1 :19); "Aggregating across slots is how a live signal disappears into an average" (Fig 1 :22); pooled readings are a channel failure (Fig 10 :26). Verdict: part of the observed "became less stable" can be a metric effect; a single species slot can run away while an aggregate stays flat. Mode `specified`. Triangulated (Fig 1 plus Fig 10).

**The union.** The three conditions are not averaged. A case in two conditions is "not a harder case of either" (Fig 9 :57) but a specific compound with its own loop and its own ground.

**The gap.** No figure line yields a threshold diversity, a critical connectance or an eigenvalue bound. "you get a kind of thing, never the answer itself" (Fig 9 :32); "Unreachable is a verdict here, not a silence" (Fig 13 :134). The number is a world import or it is not stated.

## 3. What it explicitly does not say

- Not a diversity threshold, not "diversity is bad", not a critical connectance, not a particular species.
- Not that every diverse ecosystem is unstable. The conditions can be absent; the shape is conditional.
- Not that interaction strength alone governs. Strength without a return sign does not close a loop (Fig 12 :121).
- Not a replacement for community matrix theory or consumer-resource models. Those frames hold the number the figures do not.
- Not grounded. Nothing here was settled by measurement.

## 4. What would settle it, and what would reverse it

The ground, not agreement (Fig 2 :45). The instruments that would close the shape:

1. **Per-slot time series** after a standardised perturbation, so a live slot cannot hide in an average (D5).
2. **An estimated interaction matrix** that discriminates signed return loops from chains and from reciprocal, damping structure (D2, D3). The transition to chaos in non-reciprocal consumer-resource models (Blumenthal et al. 2024) and the coherence result of Johnson et al. 2014 are candidate frames to import the number from, with provenance.
3. **A response-correlation matrix** showing whether species read independent signals or the same one (D4).
4. **Reversal:** any of refuters 1 to 4 in `01` section 5. In particular, monotonic stability change with species count at fixed bonds and fixed correlations would break the whole shape and reinstall the count as an anchor.

## 5. The honest stop

**Unreached, not unreachable for ecology.** The shape is derived; the threshold is not. The framework stops where the available instruments stop distinguishing and records the lineage (canonical README). "Indiscernibility is not identity." An ecosystem stability researcher with a real interaction matrix and a perturbation series can do what this document cannot: take the ground reading and name the quantity for a specific system. Until then the verdict is `candidate`, `specified`.

## 6. One-paragraph version for outreach

> The Tether does not give a diversity threshold for instability, and it does not say diversity is generally bad. It says that the question is a bundle of three conditions. Stability at a given species count is carried by the arrangement of interactions, so two communities with the same species list can differ as isomers; amplification needs a signed return loop, not merely many links; and diversity buys independent insurance only while species responses are independent, since overlapping responses compound instead. A fourth condition is detection: an aggregate stability metric can hide the one species slot that is running away. The framework returns that conditional shape and stops. The threshold must come from an interaction matrix and a perturbation experiment, not from the figures. What is offered is the shape any answer must have, the refuters written in advance, and the instruments that would close it.
