| May 1972, "Will a Large Complex System be Stable?" | https://www.nature.com/articles/238413a0 | Robert M. May, *Nature* 238, 413-414, 1972. Abstract: "large complex systems which are assembled (connected) at random may be expected to be stable up to a certain critical level of connectance, and then, as this increases, to suddenly become unstable." | Ecological stability (reference overview) | https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Ecological_stability | Tertiary but useful: "dynamical stability is limited by diversity, and the strictness of this tradeoff is related to the magnitude of fluctuations in interactions." Also: "The effect of diversity on stability in food-web models can be either positive or negative, depending on the trophic coherence of the network." And: Grimm and Wissel inventoried 167 definitions and found 70 stability concepts in 1997. |
| Blumenthal, Rocks and Mehta 2024, PRL | https://www.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/pmc/articles/PMC11780484 | "such ecosystems generically undergo a phase transition to chaotic dynamics as the amount of non-reciprocity is increased." The phase boundary is set by the species-packing ratio, the ratio of surviving species to non-depleted resources. Maximal Lyapunov exponent positive in the dynamic phase. Statistical physics, low overlap with field ecology. |
| Johnson, Dominguez-Garcia, Donetti and Munoz 2014, PNAS | https://www.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/pmc/articles/PMC4273378 | "trophic coherence ... is a better statistical predictor of linear stability than size or complexity." Food-web structure, not species count, predicts stability across 46 webs. Complexity science and network theory, moderate overlap with theoretical ecology. |
| Positive feedback (reference overview) | https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Positive_feedback | Systems and control theory, genuinely low overlap with ecology: "the effects of a perturbation on a system include an increase in the magnitude of the perturbation"; "Positive feedback tends to cause system instability. ## The two-stage offer

**Stage 1.** Send the residual plus the refuter. Ask one question: under what interaction structure and response correlation would you expect a diverse community to amplify, rather than damp, a small perturbation? Record the answer before showing anything else.

**Stage 2, opt-in only.** If they are curious, offer the verbatim figure extract, the canonical usage rule and the public repo. Their agreement at this stage is never pooled as corroboration (Fig 2 :40).

**Stage 3.** Offer the formula as a method to reuse. Each user states the ground and writes their own pre-registered refuter. A failure report is more useful than an endorsement.
