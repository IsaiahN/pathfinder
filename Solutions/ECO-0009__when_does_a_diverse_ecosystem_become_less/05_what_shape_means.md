## 1. A shape is a constraint, not a filler

The canonical README gives the limit: the framework returns *"what a missing thing would have to look like (a shape, not a name)."* Fig 9 :32: *"you get a kind of thing, never the answer itself."* Fig 13 :134-135: *"Unreachable is a verdict here, not a silence,"* and *"It cannot say which primitive is missing, because naming it would mean already holding it."*

From *The Library of Babel*: *"You can cut your search short if the set of books returned is small and descriptive enough that it provides a general shape of the answer that satisfies your question."*

For ECO-0009 the shape is therefore the set of constraints on any answer, not the answer. It says: whatever decides this question must be expressible as arrangement of interaction bonds, correlation of responses, and per-slot detection. It does **not** say which species, which connectance value, or which eigenvalue crossing.

## 2. How the shape is obtained

- *Library of Babel*: use the vocabulary closest to the thing, look for recurring new terms that overlap or correlate, recurse. *"With each new term gained, you narrow the space and deepen your vocabulary."*
- Fig 6 :30: *"The description travels. Fails by being sharpened into terms only we can read. Cast it in effect terms."* Fig 9 :30-31: *"your words are borrowed too, so describe the effect rather than the cause."*
- The effect description here is: "a fluctuation that should damp instead grows, and it grows through interaction."
- *Library of Babel* on stopping: the finish line is also the starting place, *"you have already composed the answer through the process of locating it."* In this solution the residual stated in `01` section 0 is the same residual the verdict answers. That identity is the check, not a coincidence.

## 3. Why this question is a bundle, and why that matters for "shape"

*The Library of Babel* asks: *"Perhaps your problem was a bundle or conflation of several problems masquerading as one?"* Fig 9 :55 says the same structurally: *"A bundled problem is usually a union of overlapping conditions, not a list of separate ones."*

The question "when does a diverse ecosystem become less stable because interactions amplify fluctuations?" is already a bundle:

- a dynamical claim (the fluctuation grows through a loop),
- a statistical claim (species responses overlap rather than compensate),
- a measurement claim (which reading of "stability" is being used).

A shape for the bundle is a union of three constraints, not a single threshold. This is the substantive reason the solution refuses to output a diversity number. A number would fill a slot; a shape says what the slot must be able to hold.

## 4. What would license a name here

Two things, both outside the shape itself:

1. **The ground settles it.** Formula step 5: *"the ground settles it. Verified only by what pays."* A perturbation experiment, an interaction matrix estimated in the field, or a long time series that discriminates a positive from a negative loop would name the quantity for a given ecosystem.
2. **A provenanced import from a frame whose closure differs** (Fig 6 :23, Fig 8 :50-52), with a source gate and a shadow test. The community-matrix criterion from theoretical ecology is such an import. It carries the provenance of that frame, not the authority of the figures.

The canonical README warns that declaring primitives does not make them neutral. Mapping "evidence pool" onto "environmental response signal" in finding F4 is a visible choice, recorded as such in `01` section 4.

## 5. Prestige is not action (consequence for this field)

From *The Recursive Self-Improvement Trap*: *"Prestige can fund an action. It can give an action a budget to be tried. But it cannot settle an action. Only the ground can settle it."*

For ecosystem stability research this means a well-cited diversity-stability review can fund a field season, but it cannot settle whether a given web has an amplifying loop. The instrument settles it. A campaign that gathers famous names to agree with a shape is the named collapse, not corroboration.

## 6. My own audit is a simulation, not a ground reading

Same essay: *"LLMs can quickly simulate an approximate answer/output with the formula"* but *"the truly testable output would need to be derived from the real architecture or working the formula yourself."* The essay adds: *"Metatheories cannot validate themselves."*

So `01_figure_grounded_audit.md`, `06_isomers_and_order.md` and `09_watson_answer.md` are simulated derivations checked for internal consistency against the figures. They are **not** tests of the ecological claim and must be labelled as such. They cannot settle anything they report on. The honest output is a shape with a refuter attached, addressed to people who can take a ground reading.

## 7. One-paragraph version

A shape here is the narrowed form of an answer you can state before you can name it. For this question it is a union of three constraints: an amplifying signed loop in the interaction web, overlapping rather than independent species responses, and a stability metric that hides a live per-slot signal. The figures return that shape and stop; they do not return a threshold diversity, a critical connectance, or a named species. A number is licensed only by a ground reading or by a provenanced import from ecological stability theory, and in either case it is recorded as coming from there, not from the figures. This document is a simulated derivation, so its verdict is `specified`, not `grounded`.
