# Expert verification packet: the Tether on diversity and stability

**What this is:** a claim in a form an ecosystem stability researcher can check. Mode, derivation and refuters are stated before the conclusion, so a reader knows what to look for and what would settle it.

**Mode: `specified` on the ecological application, `general` on the structural shape, not `grounded`.** No measurement at a field site or in a model ecosystem is claimed. This is a shape and a route, not a result.

**Status:** simulated derivation checked for internal consistency against the figures, not a ground reading. It cannot settle the claim it reports on.

## 1. The claim, in one paragraph

Species number does not govern the sign of a perturbation response at fixed interactions. What governs it is a union of three conditions: (1) the interaction web contains a signed return loop whose output re-enters as input; (2) species responses are overlapping rather than independent, so diversity supplies no insurance; (3) the stability metric hides a live per-species slot inside an aggregate. Two communities with the same species list but different bonds stand to each other as isomers, with the stability difference carried by the arrangement. The framework does not return a threshold diversity, a critical connectance or an eigenvalue bound; those must come from the ground or from a provenanced import.

## 2. Three checkable sub-claims

### C1: ecological, checkable against an interaction matrix

- **Statement:** at fixed species count, stability tracks interaction arrangement, not the ingredient list. Two webs over the same species set are isomers under Fig 12's definition.
- **Source, verbatim:** Fig 12 :117, *"isomer. Same atoms, different bond, different substance. The difference is invisible in the formula and decisive in the behaviour."* Also Fig 12 :94, *"The arrangement is the substance."* And Fig 11 :50, *"The same actor is a benefactor to one residual and a malfactor to another, so mutualism and parasitism are relations rather than kinds."*
- **What to check:** given two webs with identical species sets and different interaction signs or topologies, does the measured stability differ in the direction the bond difference predicts? Is the species list alone non-predictive once bonds are held?
- **Refuter:** identical stability across parameter ranges for webs whose bonds demonstrably differ, so the bond carries no information; or monotonic stability change with species count at fixed bonds and fixed correlations.

### C2: dynamical, checkable against a perturbation time series

- **Statement:** amplification requires a signed return loop. A long chain without a return edge cannot close the loop; the sign decides damping versus growth.
- **Source, verbatim:** Fig 12 :121, *"cycle. The output re-enters as input. The sign of the loop decides whether it closes, and a chain cannot close by construction."* Also Fig 13 :31, *"a remainder compounding through a chain that amplifies is chaos, and the detail a crossing discards reappears above it as noise."*
- **What to check:** in a real or model ecosystem, does the leading response change sign when the reciprocity or coherence of interactions is varied while diversity is held? Does a chain-only web fail to amplify?
- **Refuter:** exponential growth of a small perturbation in a system with no return edge anywhere, no external driver and no hidden slot.

### C3: statistical and measurement, checkable against response data

- **Statement:** overlapping responses compound; independent responses are the insurance diversity is supposed to buy. An aggregate metric can hide a single amplifying slot.
- **Source, verbatim:** Fig 7 :13, *"agreement compounds, evidence does not"*; Fig 8 :49, *"Two frames that overlap almost completely are one frame wearing two names."*; Fig 1 :22, *"Aggregating across slots is how a live signal disappears into an average."*
- **What to check:** do correlated species responses remove the stabilising effect of species number? Does a per-slot reading reveal amplification that the ecosystem aggregate conceals?
- **Refuter:** full independent-response stabilisation in a community whose responses are near-identical, or a provably non-masking aggregate that stays flat while one slot amplifies without bound.

## 3. What is explicitly not claimed

- Not a diversity threshold, not a critical connectance, not an eigenvalue bound. The figures return a shape, not a number (Fig 9 :32).
- Not "diversity is destabilising" as an unconditional verdict. The claim is conditional on the three listed conditions.
- Not that interaction strength alone governs. Strength without a return sign does not close a loop.
- Not that this packet replaces community matrix theory, random-matrix stability analysis or consumer-resource modelling. Those frames are where the number lives.
- Not `grounded`. Nothing here was settled by experiment.

## 5. What would settle it

An instrument, not a further argument: per-species perturbation time series, an estimated signed interaction matrix, and a response-correlation matrix. Candidate source frames for the numeric threshold, to be imported with provenance and a shadow test: May's community-matrix criterion, trophic coherence (Johnson et al. 2014), and the species-packing phase boundary in non-reciprocal consumer-resource models (Blumenthal et al. 2024).

## 6. What an expert can check in twenty minutes

1. Read Fig 12 :117, :121 and Fig 1 :22. Three short passages.
2. Say whether C1's isomer characterisation is faithful to their system, or name the bond that carries the difference.
3. Say whether the leading response in their data changes sign with reciprocity or coherence at fixed diversity (C2), or say why not.
4. Name one case where an aggregate stability metric hid a per-species amplification (C3), or say why none applies.
5. Send the refutation. A failure is more useful than an endorsement.
