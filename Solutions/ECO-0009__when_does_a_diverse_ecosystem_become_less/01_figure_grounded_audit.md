## 0. The residual, in effect terms

> An ecosystem with more tracked species slots shows a *growing* per-slot fluctuation residual after a perturbation, and the growth is carried by species-to-species interaction.

- **R (effect):** the gap between the expected damping of a perturbation in a diverse community and the observed amplification. It is indexed per species slot and per time, never aggregated (Fig 1 :19, Fig 1 :22).
- **Arity:** many-body. A single species cannot amplify its own fluctuation through a relation; the residual needs at least an interacting pair and usually a loop. This is not a two-body symmetric relation. It carries an ordered relation (who affects whom) and a signed comparison (grows versus decays).
- **Symmetry:** signed and directed. "Amplify" is a positive loop gain, not a symmetric join. The identifying test is the sign of the loop (Fig 12 :121).
- **Scale:** per-species slot versus ecosystem-level aggregate, with a lossy membrane between them (Fig 4 :30). A global stability number and a single species in bloom are different readings of the same system.
- **Anchor:** an instrument, an experiment, a long-term time series or an interaction matrix that pays or does not (Fig 2 :29). Agreement among ecologists is not the anchor.

What an answer would look like, written down before the search: a small set of conditions on bond arrangement and response correlation such that amplification appears exactly when the conditions hold, each with a refuter. Without this binding the sought-for shape would be an unrefusable preference (Fig 5 :61-66).

## 3. Findings, each with a refuter

### F1: "Diverse" misnames the governing quantity (D1)

Fig 11 :49-51 says an actor's valence is relative to the residual, that the same actor can be benefactor or malfactor depending on the residual, and that no standing list of good actors survives a change of residual. The figures' own ecology vocabulary names *relations* (Fig 11 :47), not a headcount. A community is enumerated (Fig 11 :43) into actors, conditions and relations; a count is a projection of that enumeration onto one axis.

Reading: a species count is a cheap description of the cast, not an anchor. In a union question, the count can change while the relations that decide stability stay the same, and the relations can change while the count stays the same. So a law of the form "diversity above N causes instability" is a filter on the budget, not a witness for the verdict (Fig 9 :60). Mode `general` for the structural claim, `specified` for ecology.

**Refuter:** hold the interaction matrix and response-correlation matrix fixed, change only the species count by a neutral addition, and find stability moving monotonically with the count. That would make the count an anchor and would break this finding.

Status: **triangulated** (Fig 11 valence/habitat plus Fig 4 :20 actors-currency-anchor rule; low overlap between the habitat figure and the membrane figure).

### F2: Amplification is a signed cycle, not a generic consequence of having many parts (D3)

Fig 13 :31 names the mechanism in the question's own words: *"a remainder compounding through a chain that amplifies is chaos."* Fig 12 :121 gives the structural condition: a cycle whose output re-enters as input, with the sign of the loop deciding whether it closes. A chain cannot close by construction, so a merely long food chain is not the same thing as an amplifying loop. Fig 13 :124 adds that chaos is what a composition leaves behind, not a seventh primitive.

Reading: "interactions amplify fluctuations" means at least one effective positive loop in the interaction structure. A negative (damping) loop does the opposite work. The sign, not the number of links, governs the quantity the question asks about. Mode `general` for the sign rule, `specified` for its ecological reading.

**Refuter:** a system with all interaction loops demonstrably damping and all response correlations near zero that still shows growing per-slot residuals after a small perturbation, with external forcing ruled out and no hidden slot. That would break the necessity of the signed loop.

Status: **triangulated** (Fig 13 chaos line plus Fig 12 cycle line; distinct figures, different diagrams, genuinely low overlap).

### F3: Same species, different web, different stability (D2)

Fig 12 :5 says the bond is the information the ingredients do not carry. Fig 12 :94: *"The arrangement is the substance."* Fig 12 :117: *"isomer. Same atoms, different bond, different substance."* Fig 12 :123 names the weaker case: a functional group where a substructure fixes the kind and a qualifier fixes the member. Fig 13 :124 repeats the same rule at the substrate level.

Reading: two communities with the same species set but differently wired interactions are isomers under the figure's own definition. Their stability difference is carried entirely by the arrangement, which is invisible in a species list. This is the strongest single figure-grounded point in the answer: it explains *why* a count cannot decide the question. Mode `general` for the isomer rule, `specified` for the ecological binding.

**Refuter:** a pair of communities with identical species sets and demonstrably different interaction bonds whose stability is nevertheless identical across perturbations and parameter ranges, so the bond carries no information about the outcome. That would collapse the isomer claim.

Status: **triangulated** (Fig 12 definition plus Fig 13 :124 re-statement; low overlap).

### F4: Correlated responses remove the insurance diversity would otherwise buy (D4)

Fig 2 :31 defines independence as how little two frames draw on the same evidence. Fig 7 :12-13: *"same closure, handed along; agreement compounds, evidence does not."* Fig 8 :48 says overlap decides both whether agreement means anything and whether the union has a surplus; Fig 8 :49 says frames that overlap almost completely are one frame wearing two names. Fig 8 :45 gives the opposite case: two frames together reach what neither reaches alone.

Reading: diversity stabilises through the union only when responses are independent, that is, when different species read different parts of the environmental signal. When they read the same signal in the same way, their fluctuations compound and the insurance term is zero. In that regime extra species add spelling, not reach, which is exactly the "amplify fluctuations" outcome. Mode `specified`; the mapping of "evidence" onto "shared environmental response signal" is declared, not hidden.

**Refuter:** a community whose species responses to a perturbation are demonstrably near-identical (one frame under many names) that nevertheless gains the full independent-response stabilising effect, or a community with fully independent responses that shows compounded fluctuations with no signed amplifying loop. Either would break this finding.

Status: **triangulated** (Fig 2 independence, Fig 7 compounding, Fig 8 surplus; three distinct figures with low overlap). **Strain declared:** "evidence pool" is a frame term. Mapping it onto species responses is a cross-kind application allowed by Fig 4 :20 (only actors, currency and anchor change). If the mapping needs forcing, the line governs correlation of responses and not species count, so re-census leaves the domain boundary intact. This is the weakest link and is labelled as such.

### F5: An aggregate stability metric can hide the live slot (D5)

Fig 1 :19: *"The residual is indexed by object slot, not aggregated."* Fig 1 :22: *"Aggregating across slots is how a live signal disappears into an average."* Fig 1 :23: *"The ground enters as the only metric."* Fig 10 :24 and :26 name pooled readings and saturated metrics as channel failures in which the output is not a verdict but "you have lost touch".

Reading: "the ecosystem became less stable" is often read off one aggregate (total biomass, mean abundance, variance of a composite index). That is precisely the reading that can stay flat while one species slot amplifies. The question's observation may be partly a detection effect, not only a dynamical one. Detection is a separate domain with its own ground and must not be averaged into D2 or D3.

**Refuter:** demonstrate an aggregate metric that tracks every per-slot residual one-to-one with no masking, on a system where a single slot is amplifying. That would break the claim that aggregation is the hiding mechanism here.

Status: **triangulated** (Fig 1 slot rule plus Fig 10 channel-failure rule).

### F6: The question is a union compound, not one hard law

Fig 9 :55-57 (quoted in section 1). The Library of Babel essay (read 2026-11-23) asks: *"Perhaps your problem was a bundle or conflation of several problems masquerading as one?"* The phrase "because interactions among species amplify fluctuations" already contains two conditions: signed amplification (D3) and correlated response (D4), observed through a detector (D5). A single lever that clears one condition leaves the intersections standing.

**Refuter:** find one intervention that restores stability across all three conditions at once with no categorically different residue left over. That would show the conditions were one variable, not a union.

Status: **triangulated** (Fig 9 union rule plus the essay's bundle test; framework-internal, so low independent weight).

### F7: The budget is spent on the cheap count, not the costly arrangement

Fig 12 :125-126: *"A composition costs in two currencies, and they do not add."* *"Bits price the description. Actions price finding out whether it holds."* A species list is cheap in bits and expensive to settle only if you try to measure the interactions; a short interaction claim can be cheap to state and take a whole field season to settle. Neither price predicts the other.

Reading: this explains the *shape of the literature* rather than the ecology. Studies that can be done from presence-absence lists will be done first, and they will report an effect of diversity because that is the axis they can see. It is a filter for the budget, never a witness for the verdict (Fig 9 :60). Mode `general`.

**Refuter:** show that measuring interaction bonds costs no more actions than counting species across the relevant systems, so the two currencies collapse.

Status: **single-source** (Fig 12 pricing rule), framework-internal.

### F8: The numeric crossover is a `gap`, not a hidden answer

No figure line yields a threshold diversity, a critical connectance, an eigenvalue bound or a species-packing ratio. Fig 9 :32: *"you get a kind of thing, never the answer itself."* Fig 13 :134: *"Unreachable is a verdict here, not a silence"* and Fig 8 :39: *"So a search that finds nothing is never a proof of absence."* The figures return the shape of the crossover (signed loop plus response overlap plus detection), not its number.

Honest stop: the number must be imported from a source whose closure differs, with a source gate and a shadow test (Fig 6 :23, Fig 8 :50-52). The world literature supplies candidates in section `09_watson_answer.md`, but those are world results, not figure derivations, and they carry no figure authority.

**Refuter:** derive a numeric diversity-stability threshold using only figure lines, with no ecological import. That would remove the gap.

Status: **gap**, declared after genuine re-census.

Re-census: Fig 13 describes a compounding remainder through alternating kinds (chaos as a left-over); Fig 12 describes a bond topology in which a chain has no feedback edge. Different objects. The ecological reading needs a *loop*, which is why an amplifying interaction web is not just a long food chain. **Not strained after re-census.**

- **Fig 11 "mutualism and parasitism are relations rather than kinds" versus the question's "interactions among species".** Re-census: confirms D1; no strain.

## 5. Pre-registered refuter

Written before the conclusion and before outreach, per the seat rule: a falsifier written before the run is a design instrument.

The shape claim is refuted if any of the following holds:

1. A community with all interaction loops damping and all response correlations near zero still shows growing per-slot residuals after a small perturbation, with external forcing ruled out and no hidden slot (breaks F2 and F4).
2. Holding interaction bonds and response correlation fixed, stability changes monotonically with species count alone (breaks F1 and F3 and reinstalls the count as an anchor).
3. A single species slot amplifies without bound while an aggregate metric that provably tracks every slot stays flat with no pooling or saturation involved (breaks F5).
4. A source with a genuinely different evidence pool from the ecology and statistical-physics pool shows that the sign of the leading response is independent of interaction reciprocity, coherence and response correlation (breaks the cross-domain corroboration of F2 to F4).
5. A numeric diversity-stability threshold is derived from figure lines alone (removes F8; would strengthen, not refute, the solution).

If 1 to 4 fires, the corresponding verdict is withdrawn. Until one is run, nothing here is `grounded`.

## 6. Recommendation

1. Lead with F3 and F2: same species, different arrangement, different stability; the sign of the loop decides. Do not lead with a diversity threshold, because there is none in the figures.
2. Keep the three conditions (signed loop, response overlap, detection) as separate verdicts with separate instruments. Do not average them into a single "biodiversity effect".
3. Ask a checker for a refutation, never for agreement. The first contact carries the residual and the refuter; the framework comes later and only if asked (Fig 10 :52).
