## Wider literature that bears on this question

The moral-identity and moral-licensing literatures are the nearest pool. Three things make them a **single pool, not three witnesses**: the licensing reviews cite the same core experiments, the meta-analyses overlap in primary studies, and most of the relevant work draws on the same social-cognitive tradition (Aquino and Reed, Bandura, Monin and Miller). The ledger records overlap explicitly so a pooled number is not mistaken for independent corroboration.

Lower-overlap fields that would be worth a second frame if this were taken further: comparative animal behavior (status and self-regard analogues), development across cultures (whether the slot structure is learned), measurement theory (whether a global score can ever carry a per-slot signal), and computational implementations of belief weighting (whether `w` is learnable in practice). None was cited here without being read.

## Distribution: offer, do not push

- **Fig 4 :22** *"Only methods cross up. Only head starts come down."* Sending the whole framework before anyone has described a residual sends a recording, not a generator. Lead with the four-part shape and its refuter; offer links after stage 1 is recorded.
- **Fig 10 :42** *"A verifier that can reconstruct the claim cannot verify it."* A recipient who has absorbed the framework before being asked for a verdict has joined the frame being checked.
- **Fig 8 :53** *"Residual first, frame second."* Ask what they would expect to see if the shape were true before naming any theory.
- **Fig 2 :40** *"agreement about the world is worthless when the agreeing frames share an evidence pool"* and Fig 2 :42 *"Buy independence in proportion to the cost of being wrong."* One-to-one contact; later agreement is never pooled.
- **Fig 9 :60** *"Use filters for the budget and witnesses for the verdict. Never let a filter hand you a verdict."* A high citation count or an impressive venue is a way to decide what to read, not whether the shape is right.

**Recommended reply order:** (1) the residual and the four parts; (2) the pre-registered refuter; (3) the question "what would count as a failure here?"; (4) record the answer; (5) only then the three local framework links, with the note that the figures, not the summary, are the authority.

## Two-stage offer, restated

Stage 1: the reader checks the output against their own knowledge, with no framework interior. Stage 2, opt-in only: if curious, they read the framework. Stage 3: offer the formula as a method to reuse, with the instruction to write a fresh refuter and state a fresh mode for each new problem. This is the right order because stage 2 agreement is worthless as evidence once the reader holds the interior, while stage 3 sends a generator, which Fig 4 permits. This agent does not host or send anything; the user does.
