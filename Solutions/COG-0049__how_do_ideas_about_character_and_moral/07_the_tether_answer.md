# The Tether's answer: how character and moral-worth ideas shape self-interpretation

**Mode: `specified` for the four-part shape; `general` for the structural skeleton; `gap` for the specific magnitude. Not `grounded`.** This is a simulated derivation checked for internal consistency against the figures (`05_what_shape_means.md` §7), not a ground reading.

## 1. The residual it answers

A person acts and then interprets their own act. They carry a self-theory (character) and a worth metric (moral worth) into the act. The residual is the gap between what that self-theory predicted and what the person observed themselves doing. The question asks how the two ideas alter the reading of that gap. The figures answer with a shape and a route, not with a psychological construct or a number.

## 2. The shape (four parts, kept separate)

1. **The gap is per slot, not a global score.** Fig 1 :19 *"The residual is indexed by object slot, not aggregated."* Fig 1 :22 *"Aggregating across slots is how a live signal disappears into an average."* The act is read as a local deviation on a specific self-slot. A global "how moral am I" average can be near zero while one slot carries all the live mass. This is why a person can hold positive overall self-regard and still be troubled by one specific act.

2. **The ideas enter the prediction, not the caption.** Symbols table: `b` is *"What the system takes to be the case before it acts. Formed from its own history and from what it inherited, mixed by w."* The character ideas are the inherited part; the person's own record is the private part; `w` is learnable. So the ideas influence interpretation by helping generate the prediction that the act then violates or confirms, not by relabeling the act afterwards.

3. **The vocabulary supplies bonds, and the fifth bin is a refusal.** Fig 12 :111 *"A vocabulary without bonds can name a state and cannot name a route to one."* Bonding the act to the self-term and the worth spectrum turns a state into a route (what counts as getting somewhere). Fig 5 :53 *"a term is owed that says so"* names the fifth routing bin: the self-attribution can be refused. Fig 12 :112 *"A refutation is a negation, and that one is not a bond."* The act stays in the record; what falls is its standing as evidence about the self.

4. **Valence is relative to the residual.** Fig 11 :49 *"An actor's valence is relative to the residual, not a property it carries."* Fig 11 :51 *"No standing list of good actors survives a change of residual."* There is no standing list of good or bad character ideas. The same idea can restore consistency for one residual and close down enquiry for another, which is why the literature's licensing and consistency results are not a single effect.

## 3. What it explicitly does not say

- No specific trait, scale, or construct is named. The framework returns a shape; naming a construct is a provenanced import, not a derivation (Fig 8 :50-52).
- No direction is asserted: it does not say that thinking about character always increases moral behavior, or always licenses immoral behavior. Each outcome occupies a different condition (Fig 9 :55).
- No magnitude is given. A pooled effect size (`d = 0.31` in one meta-analysis) is a budget filter, not a verdict (Fig 9 :60).
- No claim that the six-term substrate is a set of psychological primitives. It is a visible guess, not nature's list.
- Not `grounded`. No measurement was taken, and a simulated derivation cannot validate itself.

## 4. What would settle it, and the honest stop

Four instruments, one per part:

1. A per-slot design in which the same act is judged under different character primes, testing whether the slot profile predicts interpretation better than a global score.
2. A learning design that estimates `w` from repeated acts and tests whether inherited priors plus private history predict the interpretation.
3. A manipulation of bond framing (sequence, comparison, subtraction) and of the availability of refusal vocabulary, testing whether interpretation changes accordingly.
4. A crossed design over at least two residual conditions (self-regard threat versus unfinished self-knowledge, and prescriptive versus proscriptive domain), testing the interaction that the union reading predicts.

The honest stop is a `gap`, not silence. Fig 8 :39 *"So a search that finds nothing is never a proof of absence."* Fig 13 :134 *"Unreachable is a verdict here, not a silence"*. What is unreachable at this budget is the specific construct and the magnitude; what is reachable is the four-part shape and its refuters. The line is recorded so a later reader can see where the derivation stopped.

## 5. Falsifiers (pre-registered in `01_figure_grounded_audit.md` §5)

1. A global worth score predicts self-interpretation as well as the per-slot profile.
2. No learnable `w` maps priors and history onto interpretation.
3. Interpretation is invariant under changes of bond type and under removal of the refusal vocabulary.
4. A character idea has the same effect across all measured residuals, with no interaction.
5. One clean condition explains both licensing and consistency, with no surviving subset.

Any one fires: withdraw the affected part, keep the record.

## 6. One-paragraph version for outreach

> The Tether does not name a psychological mechanism for how ideas about character and moral worth shape self-interpretation. It gives the shape any such mechanism must have. The gap between what you thought you were and what you just did is tracked per self-slot, not as one global moral score, so a near-zero overall picture can hide one live slot. The character ideas are an inherited prior mixed with your own behavioral history by a learnable weighting, so they enter before the act and help generate the prediction the act then confirms or violates. Your vocabulary supplies the bonds that turn an isolated act into a route, and it also supplies a separate negation that can refuse the self-attribution while leaving the act in the record. The valence of any character idea depends on what residual you are trying to close, which is why the literature's licensing and consistency findings are two conditions rather than one effect. The framework stops there: the construct and the magnitude are a gap until an instrument settles them, and the refuters that would break this shape were written before this paragraph.
