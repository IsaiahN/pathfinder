## Round trip gap R_T

The Symbols table defines R_T as the gap between what was sent up and what came back down, `gap(x, (T_E ∘ T_A)(x))`, measuring what the coarser description cannot hold. Fig 4 :30 requires it to be measured rather than assumed. I ran the round trip on the condensation this folder actually uses.

1. **Sent up (x):** the figure set, chiefly Fig 1 :19-22 (per slot), the Symbols `b`/`w` entries, Fig 12 :111-112 (bond and negation), Fig 5 :53 (fifth bin), Fig 11 :49-51 (valence), Fig 9 :55 (union).

3. **Concretisation (T_E):** a concrete psychological claim: *moral identity is the mechanism; priming it produces moral licensing with d = 0.31.*

**R_T is nonzero.** Three things the shorthand holds do not survive the trip down:

- the **name**: a bound construct is substituted for a slot, and the framework's *"shape, not a name"* limit is breached;
- the **object**: a global effect size replaces a per-slot residual, which Fig 1 :22 says is how the live signal disappears;
- the **condition structure**: one pooled effect replaces the union of conditions that Fig 9 :55 requires to be unbundled.

Conversely, one thing survives cleanly: a structure must mediate between act and self, and the valence of the character idea depends on the residual. That is the residue worth sending. R_T was **not** measured numerically, because no ground reading exists; it is recorded qualitatively as the enumerated set of dropped constraints. Treating the formula as identical to the figures would set R_T to zero and would be the dead-reckoning error Fig 4 :30 names. The honest form is a lossy condensation with a measured gap, which `09_watson_answer.md` states.
