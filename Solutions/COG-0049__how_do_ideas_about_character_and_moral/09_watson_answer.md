## DOMAIN CENSUS

R by arity, symmetry and scale, then one line per domain with actors / currency / anchor, partition or union, and order by dependency.

- **R (the residual).** The gap between what a person's self-theory predicted and what they observed themselves doing. Arity: two or more (act, self-theory, and usually a social audience). Symmetry: broken, since the person infers internal states from their own overt behavior the way an outside observer would. Scale: one per-slot loop inside the person plus one habitat loop across person and culture. Fig 9 :4-5 establishes that *"Disagreements that do not shrink with effort, where each rule keeps working well on a different subset, are not one hard question but several well-formed ones. Split it rather than search."* This is a union compound.
- **Domain 1, the person as interpreting frame.** Actors: belief `b`, weighting `w`, action `a`, observation `o'`, residual `R` per slot. Currency: prediction versus observation. Anchor: the behavioral record plus human judgment, legitimately, because the question is constitutively about people (Fig 2 :36-38). Partition boundary: what counts as a slot. Order: 1.
- **Domain 2, the character and moral-worth vocabulary as library Γ.** Actors: trait concepts, worth metric as a spectrum, inherited priors, seven bonds plus negation. Currency: description length and what composes in `closure(Γ)`. Anchor: ground settlement, never frame-internal credibility (Fig 1 :27). Union with D1: the vocabulary lives inside the person's Γ and has its own loop. Order: 2.
- **Domain 3, the social habitat.** Actors: other people, norms, audiences, reputation. Currency: agreement, convention, prestige. Anchor: ground contact; the habitat is where actors come from, not what settles (Fig 11 :39). Union: imports cross into D2. Order: 3.
- **Domain 4, the research frame.** Actors: researchers, instruments, scales. Currency: evidence and residuals. Anchor: the empirical ground; human judgment is legitimate here because the question is constitutively about people. Separate closure; low overlap if independent instruments, high overlap if the same review. Order: 4.
- **Order by dependency.** Perceive the act, name what it affords, state what would count as a good interpretation, refuse the persistently wrong self-term, then re-describe (Fig 3 and Formula step 2). The objective is the middle link with the least instrumentation.

## VERDICT (from the figures)

The four-part shape survives; the name and the magnitude do not. The gap is per slot, so a global moral score is the wrong object; the ideas enter the prediction as a weighted mixture of history and prior; the vocabulary supplies bonds that make an act into a route and a separate negation that can refuse the attribution; and the valence of any character idea is relative to the residual, which is why licensing and consistency are two conditions rather than one effect. The framework does not name the construct, and no figure line yields an effect size. This is a `specified`, ungrounded result with a declared `gap`.

- **cost if wrong:** bounded and locatable, because the four parts and their refuters are stated before the conclusion. The real cost is spending an outside reader's independence by sending the framework interior before their stage-1 verdict; Fig 2 :42 says *"Buy independence in proportion to the cost of being wrong."* That cost is avoidable by leading with the shape and its refuter.
- **undoable:** yes. No primitive is withdrawn; the specific bindings and the magnitude are simply withheld. A corrected packet can supersede an earlier one, and the record keeps both. Fig 6 principle: what is recorded only grows, so a correction adds a term rather than erasing one.
- **reversed by:** a ground reading, not agreement. A per-slot design that beats a global score, a learnable weighting that predicts interpretation, a framing or refusal manipulation that changes it, an interaction that shows two residual conditions, or an instrument that discriminates the sources of a self-report reading would each move a part of the claim. A global score that predicts as well as the slot profile, or a single condition that explains both literatures, would break it. Until one of these fires, the verdict stands as `candidate`.
