# The Tether's answer: eternal inflation and the multiverse

**Mode: `specified`, not `grounded`.** This is a shape and a route, not a result. It is a simulated derivation checked for internal consistency against the figures (`05_what_shape_means.md` section 9), not a ground reading. No measurement was taken by this run. World measurements made by others are reported, not produced.

## 1. The residual it answers

The question arrives as one sentence: can eternal inflation and the multiverse be tested, or are they unfalsifiable? In effect terms the residual is: a family of theories reproduces our observed cosmos with high precision, yet the same dynamics generically produce a causally disconnected ensemble whose members vary, so which observation would count for or against the claim, and which part of the claim is the unfalsifiable charge actually aimed at?

Fig 1 :23 "The ground enters as the only metric." The question is therefore not whether the ideas are coherent or explanatory. It is whether anything can return against them. Fig 9 :4-5 "Disagreements that do not shrink with effort, where each rule keeps working well on a different subset, are not one hard question but several well-formed ones. Split it rather than search." One verdict over the package is a category error.

## 2. The shape: four domains, four verdicts, no average

**D1, inflation in our patch.** It has an anchor: the cosmic microwave background and BAO returned numbers. Fig 2 :29 "An anchor is whatever settles the question and does not change its answer because of what any frame thinks." Planck 2018 X reports n_s = 0.9649 +- 0.0042 at 68% CL, flatness confirmed at 0.4% with BAO, and r_0.002 < 0.056 after BK15. Models that predict larger tensors lose against that number. Verdict: testable and partly tested, not unfalsifiable.

**D2a, other pockets generically.** It has no nameable anchor. Fig 6 :40 "What is unavailable is a sensor for something that has never registered anywhere, at any resolution. That is the whole of it." Fig 8 :39 "So a search that finds nothing is never a proof of absence." Fig 13 :134 "Unreachable is a verdict here, not a silence". Verdict: unreachable at this budget, `gap`, neither refuted nor confirmed. The word unfalsifiable is defensible here only in the narrow sense that no observation from a causally disconnected region has ever registered.

**D2b, bubble collisions with our bubble.** This subclass does have an edge. Fig 6 :34 "The question is not whether a sensor could exist. It is whether anything, at any resolution, is already returning something that fails to resolve." CMB maps return localized anomalies and upper limits. Feeney et al. (2013) constrained the expected number of bubble collisions to fewer than 4.0 at 95% confidence using WMAP 7-year data. Verdict: testable, constrained in one signature class, not excluded in general.

**D3, measure and typicality.** It has no anchor and sits at the broken objective link. Fig 3 :27 "A link with no instrument is not a link that works. It is a link nobody has looked at." Fig 10 :18 "a declared meaning is not a grounded one." Fig 10 :47 "a convention that decides an outcome has stopped being one." Linde and Noorbala (2010) found that different measures give strongly discontinuous predictions on the same dynamics. Verdict: `gap`, a convention conflict, not a measurement. This is the domain where the unfalsifiable charge is strongest.

**D4, the verification frame.** Proponents and critics are rooms, not anchors. Fig 2 :35 "Alignment is triangulation, not negotiation. A mirror cannot correct you." Fig 2 :40 "disqualifying for a verdict: agreement about the world is worthless when the agreeing frames share an evidence pool". Guth and Linde agreeing is one pool; Steinhardt and Ijjas agreeing is another; both pools use the same Planck data, so their disagreement is not two independent witnesses either. This seat has no independent verdict.

The one-line answer is: not one verdict. Unfalsifiable is the right word for D3 and generic D2a, and the wrong word for D1 and D2b. Tested is the right word for D1 and part of D2b, and the wrong word for D2a and D3.

## 3. What it explicitly does not say

- Not that the multiverse exists, and not that it does not.
- Not that eternal inflation is established. D1's partial grounding does not transfer upward.
- Not that a null CMB search disproves other bubbles. Fig 8 :39 and Fig 9 :44 "Only a sealed room can be searched to the end, and no frame is a sealed room. So searching and finding nothing proves nothing."
- Not that a measure is a physical parameter. Fig 10 :18.
- Not that agreement between camps settles anything. Fig 2 :35 and Fig 2 :40.
- Not `grounded`. Everything here is `specified` or `general`, ungrounded and candidate.

## 4. What would settle it, and the pre-registered refuter

Written before the conclusion above, per the rule that a falsifier written before the run is a design instrument. The shape claim is that testability splits by anchor availability and no domain's verdict transfers. It is refused if any of the following holds:

1. A spatially localized CMB signature is detected, survives model selection against ordinary fluctuations, and is independently attributed to a bubble collision. That closes D2b.
2. A prediction of eternal inflation is derived that does not depend on any measure, typicality prior or observer proxy. That closes D3.
3. A ground reading shows the primordial perturbation spectrum is incompatible with every slow-roll inflation model. That refutes D1's partial grounding.
4. It is proven that no possible observation could discriminate eternal inflation from a single-universe model even in principle, not merely at this budget. That moves D2a from unreachable-at-budget to underivable-in-principle, a different fact with different instructions (Fig 8 :39-43 "Underivable-in-principle and unreachable-at-this-budget are different facts, and they issue different instructions.").
5. An anchor is specified for the ensemble claim whose answer does not move when a proponent or critic pushes on it. That dissolves D2a and D3 as gaps.
6. The anchor rule of Fig 2 :29 is shown not to apply to cosmological ensemble claims. That refutes the whole derivation.

If any fires, the affected finding is withdrawn in the retraction log of `01_figure_grounded_audit.md` with the reason and the date. Until one fires, nothing here is labelled `grounded`.

## 5. The honest stop

Fig 9 :32 "you get a kind of thing, never the answer itself." The framework returns a shape, not which vacuum we occupy, and not which measure is correct. Fig 4 :30 "So take the round trip and measure the gap. Without it you are navigating by dead reckoning: confident about a position nobody checked." The popular slogan inflated is testable, brought back down as the multiverse has been tested, carries a nonzero round-trip gap equal to the four-domain split. Fig 6 :27 "Which is why some unreachability is depth and some is genuine absence, and from inside the frame the two look the same." From inside our horizon we cannot tell there are other pockets but we cannot see them from there is nothing beyond to see. That is a limit on the instrument, not a fact about the world. The stop is recorded with its lineage so it is checkable, rather than filled with a decision about existence.

## 6. One-paragraph version for outreach

> The Tether does not say the multiverse exists or that it does not. It says the question is a bundle of four with different grounds. Inflation in our patch is testable and partly tested: the cosmic microwave background returned numbers that models must meet. Other pocket universes in general have no anchor, so they are unreachable at this budget, which is a verdict and not a silence and not a refutation. One subclass, a bubble collision with our bubble, has an edge onto the ensemble and has been constrained without being excluded. The measure or typicality rule that would turn the ensemble into predictions has no ground at all, so different rules give different answers and that part is a convention conflict rather than a measurement. The honest answer is therefore not one verdict. What is offered is the four-way split, the refuters written in advance, and the instruments that would close each gap. The center stays open until a ground settles it.
