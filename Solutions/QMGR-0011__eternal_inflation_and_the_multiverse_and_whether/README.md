## The short shape

The question is a bundle, not one question. Unbundled by anchor availability it yields four different verdicts: the inflationary mechanism in our patch has a ground (the cosmic microwave background) and is partly tested; causally disconnected pockets in general have no nameable anchor and are unreachable, not refuted; a specific subclass (bubble-collision signatures) has an anchor and is constrained, not excluded; the measure and typicality rule has no anchor and is a convention conflict, not a measurement. Calling the whole package "unfalsifiable" mistakes the hardest domain for all of them. Calling it "tested" mistakes the easiest domain for all of them.

## What is deliberately not here

- No personal emails of named individuals.
- No ranking of people by how likely they are to agree with the verdict.
- No bulk-send plan.

Three figure-grounded reasons:

1. Fig 2 :35 — *"Alignment is triangulation, not negotiation. A mirror cannot correct you."* A list selected for agreement cannot check the claim.
2. Fig 2 :40 — *"agreement about the world is worthless when the agreeing frames share an evidence pool."* Guth and Linde agreeing with each other is one pool, not two witnesses.
3. Fig 10 :52 — *"Bring a characterised residual, never the source."* Sending the framework before asking for a verdict spends the reader's independence.

Legally: scraped personal emails for unsolicited bulk outreach breach GDPR and CAN-SPAM style rules in many jurisdictions. The public opt-in and submission routes in `03` avoid that failure and the structural one.

## Simulated derivation notice

This folder is a simulated derivation checked for internal consistency against the figures, not a ground reading. From the essay *The Recursive Self-Improvement Trap*, which was opened this session: "the output is only as good as the validator. The agent, or the tool, or the frame, or the theory, cannot validate itself. An external validator, or ground, is always needed to tether the veracity of the output to reality. Anything else is drift." This package cannot settle the question it reports on.
