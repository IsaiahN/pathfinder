# What "shape" means for this question — canonical reading from the eight essays

All eight essays were opened on 2026-11-22 and 2026-11-23. URLs are in `10_source_ledger.csv`. Where an essay quotes a figure, the figure text was checked against `03-FIGURES_TEXT.md` rather than trusted through the essay.

## 1. A shape is a constraint, not a filler

The canonical usage rule says the framework returns *"what a missing thing would have to look like (a shape, not a name)"*, then stops at the limit of the available instruments and records the lineage. Fig 9 :32: *"you get a kind of thing, never the answer itself."*

From *The Library of Babel: Locating The Book of Books*: "you can cut your search short if the set of books returned is small and descriptive enough that it provides a general shape of the answer that satisfies your question."

For this question, the shape is **not** "a multiverse exists" or "a multiverse does not exist." It is the four-way partition with a per-domain anchor test. It constrains which answers are admissible: an answer that gives one verdict for all four domains is inadmissible because it ignores the anchor differences. It does not fill the slot with a decision about existence.

## 2. How a shape is obtained

- *Library of Babel*: use the vocabulary you already hold that is closest to the thing, then look for new terms that overlap or correlate with it and recurse. "With each new term gained, you narrow the space by definition and deepen your vocabulary."
- Fig 6 :30: *"The description travels. Fails by being sharpened into terms only we can read. Cast it in effect terms."* Fig 9 :30-31: *"your words are borrowed too, so describe the effect rather than the cause."* Here "unfalsifiable" is a borrowed word from philosophy of science, so it is recast in effect terms: *is there a nameable anchor that could return a negative?*
- *Decoding Tacit Knowledge*: "When contact isn't possible, the best you can send is a plain description of what you'd expect to see, because anyone can check that against the world." That is why the outreach email asks which observation would count, before offering any theory.
- *Library of Babel* on stopping: "you will know that you are done because the finish line will also be the starting place, as there is nowhere else to search and nothing else that can describe the answer better." Here the stop is reached when every domain has either an anchor or an explicit statement that no anchor exists.

## 3. What licenses a name

Only two things, both outside the shape itself:

1. **The ground settles it.** `THE_FORMULA.md` step 5: "the ground settles it. Verified only by what pays. A gate is not the ground." Planck's measurement of n_s and the bubble-collision upper limits are grounds reporting on domains D1 and D2b.
2. **A provenanced import from a frame whose closure differs** (Fig 6 :23, Fig 8 :50-52), with a source gate and a shadow test.

*They're Scanning Books When They Should Be Studying Nature* sharpens the second case: "The real import is not a new term in the existing space. It is a new dimension of the space." And: "A minimum viable set of six terms is a human's careful guess at reality's compression axes; another sensorium's minimum set need not be those six." Also: "Declaring the primitives does not make them neutral. It makes the choice visible."

The six-term set is a visible guess. Naming a measure as *the* correct measure would turn a visible choice into an invisible one, which is D3's defect.

## 4. The union horizon is the exact shape of this question

*How Intelligence Actually Works* supplies the essay-level term that matches Fig 6 :27:

> "A projection sinks below the union horizon when no observer, with any instrument any of them can build, can discriminate its origin. This Union Horizon is always moving because instruments improve, and better properties get discovered, so the horizon is a statement about the current state. Being 'below the union horizon' is never a settled verdict, only a report that nobody has reached it yet."

And the two cases:

> "Case one is not learnable in the same way. 'No trace exists' can never be confirmed by better instruments. A bigger and better instrument finding nothing is indistinguishable from a trace it still can't reach. You can only ever say nothing has been found yet."

Map that onto the domains:

- **D2b is above the union horizon.** CMB anomaly searches are already returning candidates that fail to resolve. There is an edge to extend. Feeney et al. 2013 placed a number on it. A better instrument could close it. This is the essay's learnable case.
- **D2a is below the union horizon at this time.** No observation from a causally disconnected pocket has ever registered. "Nothing has been found yet" is the only honest report. It is not "no trace exists," because that can never be confirmed by a bigger instrument (Fig 6 :40, Fig 8 :39, Fig 9 :44).
- **D3 is a different failure.** It is not below a horizon; it is a convention with no ground at all. Fig 10 :18: *"a declared meaning is not a grounded one."*

The same essay adds: "The recursion converges toward indiscernibility, and indiscernibility is not identity." So even a perfect fit between a multiverse model and the CMB would not make the model identical to the world. The lineage still has to be kept.

## 5. Why "it explains everything" is the specific forbidden error

*The Hard Problem is About Source* uses metamerism: an instrument reads green and "cannot comment on the source." Then:

> "The answer is unreached until a better instrument exists."
> "An instrument that can't distinguish these cases demonstrates its own inadequacy, it cannot claim anything substantial about what is being measured or what causes it."

Fig 5 :43-44 makes the matching rule for terms: *"A term that explains everything by saying nothing fails this, and so does a term that explains one occasion perfectly."* Two CMB spectra from different inflationary models can read the same in n_s and r. That is a projection, not a source. The answer owed is a discriminator, not a declaration. It is also why *How a Society of Agents Would Work* insists that the ground votes between rooms and that agreement is not the verdict.

## 6. The bundle rule, stated in the searcher's own vocabulary

*Library of Babel* again:

> "Perhaps your problem was a bundle or conflation of several problems masquerading as one? You will need to search all of those problems individually to find their answers and then combine them in a satisfactory way."

Fig 9 :54: *"And when the split lands on a union rather than a partition, unbundle before you search again."* The "testable or unfalsifiable" question is exactly such a bundle: mechanism, ensemble, collision subclass and measure. The four-domain answer in `01` is the unbundling.

## 7. Prestige is not action

From *The Recursive Self-Improvement Trap*, which was opened this session:

> "Prestige can fund an action. It can give an action a budget to be tried. But it cannot settle an action. Only the ground can settle it. The Ground Maintainer must be blind to prestige."

Guth, Linde, Steinhardt and Hawking are names with weight. That weight can get a paper read, which is a budget to be tried. It cannot decide whether pocket universes exist. Fig 7 :35: *"A room's agreement is variation, not a verdict. Selection between rooms is where the ground votes."*

## 8. Correction to my own §3 (see `06_isomers_and_order.md`)

In §3 above I wrote that a provenanced import needs a source gate and a shadow test. That is correct, but it is easy to overread it as "any import into D3 would close the gap." It would not. *They're Scanning Books* says the real import is a new dimension, and Fig 6 :25-26 says *"An import adds no atom to the world. The world does not gain primitives; a frame gains access."* A new measure would give our frame access to a prediction, not add a new observable. The gap in D3 closes only when the prediction is checkable against a ground. The import alone is not settlement. Both statements are kept so neither is quietly lost.

## 9. This derivation is a simulation, not a ground reading

From *The Recursive Self-Improvement Trap*: "the output is only as good as the validator. The agent, or the tool, or the frame, or the theory, cannot validate itself. An external validator, or ground, is always needed to tether the veracity of the output to reality. Anything else is drift."

And from *Arrival of the Fittest: The Tether Architecture*: "LLMs can simulate when a decision is unreachable or too big of a problem" and the outputs "could never be verified if the solutions presented were not contrived to fit the formula." So `01` is a simulated derivation checked for internal consistency against the figures. It is not a test of eternal inflation and it cannot settle anything it reports on. This is stated rather than hidden.
