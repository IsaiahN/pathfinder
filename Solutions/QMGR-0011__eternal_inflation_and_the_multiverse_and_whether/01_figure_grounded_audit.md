## 0. The residual, in effect terms

The question arrives as one sentence: can eternal inflation and the multiverse be tested, or are they unfalsifiable? Stated as an effect, the residual is this:

> A family of theories reproduces our observed cosmos with high precision, yet the same dynamics generically produce a causally disconnected ensemble whose members vary. What observation would count for or against the claim, and which part of the claim is the "unfalsifiable" charge actually aimed at?

Fig 1 :23: **"The ground enters as the only metric."** So the question is not whether the ideas are popular, coherent, or explanatory. It is whether anything can return against them.

The disagreement does not shrink with effort. One camp reads the same Planck data as eliminating simple inflation and says the multiverse makes inflation unpredictable (Ijjas, Steinhardt and Loeb 2013, abstract). Another reads the same data as support for inflation and says evidence for inflation pushes us toward taking the multiverse seriously (Guth 2007, abstract; Linde, as reported in the Wikipedia summary). Fig 9 :4-5 says what to do with a disagreement of that shape:

> **"Disagreements that do not shrink with effort, where each rule keeps working well on a different subset, are not one hard question but several well-formed ones. Split it rather than search."**

Fig 5 :65-66 supplies the discipline for the thing being searched for: **"Write down what an answer would look like, bind it, and let it accrue a residual like anything else."** The sought-for shape here is: *a testability verdict that transfers from one domain only where an anchor exists*. That shape is written down now so it can be refused.

**What an answer would look like (bound term).** A claim is testable in a domain exactly when there is a nameable anchor in that domain that could return a negative. A claim is unfalsifiable in a domain exactly when no such anchor exists even in principle. A claim is unreachable-at-this-budget when an anchor exists but has not yet returned. These are three different verdicts, and no verdict transfers across domains. This is the ordinary term the derivations below test.

## 1. Domain census

### R by arity, symmetry and scale

- **Arity.** Three conditions are bundled: (i) inflation occurred in our past, (ii) inflation is future-eternal and produces other pockets, (iii) a measure or typicality rule selects what observers should expect. A fourth condition is often smuggled in: (iv) the claim "explains why the constants are right for life." By Fig 9 :54: **"And when the split lands on a union rather than a partition, unbundle before you search again."** This is a union compound. It is not one hard question.
- **Symmetry.** Local inflationary dynamics inside one pocket is approximately homogeneous and isotropic, so many different pockets are rearrangements of the same atom set rather than new kinds of thing (Fig 12 :117, developed in `06_isomers_and_order.md`). The global claim breaks the symmetry that the local dynamics has: our pocket is not exchangeable with other pockets for observation purposes, because we are inside ours. The measure problem is the shadow of that broken symmetry, since a choice of time-slicing or observer weighting must be made before a prediction can be stated.
- **Scale.** First order: our observable patch and its CMB. Second order: the ensemble of pockets. Third order: the rule that assigns probabilities across the ensemble. Fig 13 :104: **"A settled molecule becomes an atom for whatever composes over it. So irreducible is a fact about a level and never about the world."** Each level needs its own anchor. A level with no anchor cannot borrow one from the level below.

### One line per domain: actors, currency, anchor

| domain | actors | currency | anchor (nameable?) | question type |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| **D1. Inflation in our patch** | inflaton, false vacuum, quantum fluctuations, CMB photons and polarization | measured spectra: `n_s`, `r`, non-Gaussianity, flatness | **yes**: Planck satellite and CMB/BAO data. A physical instrument returned numbers. | partition: which inflation models survive |
| **D2a. Other pockets, generically** | other bubbles, false vacuum, bubble walls beyond our horizon | causal contact, any signal | **no**: nothing has ever registered from a causally disconnected region at any resolution | unreachable / gap, not a partition question |
| **D2b. Bubble collisions with our bubble** | colliding bubble walls, CMB temperature and polarization maps | spatially localized CMB signatures | **yes (conditional)**: the CMB is an instrument already returning candidates that fail to resolve | partition: are the signatures there? |
| **D3. Measure and typicality** | probability measures, observer proxies, vacua | predicted probabilities | **no**: competing measures are not decided by a counter, market, or experiment | split into several well-formed questions (Fig 9 :4-5) |
| **D4. The verification frame** | proponents, critics, reviewers | agreement and residual | the ground only, never agreement; in this simulated run all seats share one evidence pool | no domain of its own until a ground is named |

"No nameable anchor means it is not a domain yet." D2a and D3 fail that test in the grounding sense. They remain well-formed theoretical claims; they just do not have a ground that could settle them today.

**Partition or union.** Union, four conditions with overlaps. The overlap between D2a and D2b matters: D2b is not a separate theory, it is the one part of the ensemble claim that has an anchor. The overlap between D1 and D3 matters too: D3 only becomes testable if D1's measured quantities are combined with a measure, and the measure is the ungrounded part.

**Dependency order (Fig 3).** Settle D1 before D2b, because the collision search uses the inflationary background model to define its signal. Run D2b before D2a, because it is the only edge we have onto the ensemble. D3 is last and is blocked: by Fig 3 :27, **"A link with no instrument is not a link that works. It is a link nobody has looked at."** A probability rule with no instrument sits at the objective link of the chain, and a verdict read below that break is a reading of nothing.

## 3. Findings

### F1 — The question is a union, so a single verdict would be a category error

Fig 9 :4-5 and :54 are quoted in §2. The four conditions of §1 share atoms but not anchors. The *Library of Babel* essay (opened this session) states the same failure in search terms: "Perhaps your problem was a bundle or conflation of several problems masquerading as one? You will need to search all of those problems individually to find their answers and then combine them in a satisfactory way."

**Mode:** `general` for the structural claim, `specified` for the four-way split. **Verdict for D1 to D4:** split before testing. **Refuter:** a single observation that simultaneously settles the mechanism, the ensemble, the collision subclass and the measure would show the split is unnecessary. No such observation is named in any source read.

### F2 — The anchor test divides the domains, and the division does not average

Fig 2 :29: **"An anchor is whatever settles the question and does not change its answer because of what any frame thinks."** D1 has Planck and BAO. D2b has CMB anomaly searches. D2a and D3 have no nameable anchor. Fig 1 :23: **"The ground enters as the only metric."**

World corroboration (secondary): Planck 2018 X reports "Planck temperature, polarization, and lensing data determine the spectral index of scalar perturbations to be n_s = 0.9649 ± 0.0042 at 68% CL" and "Spatial flatness is confirmed at a precision of 0.4% at 95% CL with the combination with BAO data." That is a ground returning numbers in D1. No corresponding number exists in D3.

**Mode:** `specified`. **Verdict:** testability is domain-local; the verdicts must not be averaged. **Refuter:** name an anchor for D3 whose answer does not move when a cosmologist changes their typicality prior. If one exists, the D3 gap closes.

### F3 — For generic other pockets, unreachable is the verdict, not false and not silence

Fig 6 :40: **"What is unavailable is a sensor for something that has never registered anywhere, at any resolution. That is the whole of it."** Fig 8 :39: **"So a search that finds nothing is never a proof of absence."** Fig 9 :44: **"Only a sealed room can be searched to the end, and no frame is a sealed room. So searching and finding nothing proves nothing."** Fig 13 :134: **"Unreachable is a verdict here, not a silence"**

Guth 2007 states the Physics-facing version in the abstract: "Although the infinity of pocket universes produced by eternal inflation are unobservable, it is argued that eternal inflation has real consequences in terms of the way that predictions are extracted from theoretical models." So even a leading proponent concedes the pockets are unobservable while claiming the theory has observable consequences. Both halves of that sentence are needed; quoting only one is how the debate gets stuck.

Fig 6 :27: **"Which is why some unreachability is depth and some is genuine absence, and from inside the frame the two look the same."** From inside our horizon we cannot tell "there are other pockets but we cannot see them" from "there is nothing beyond to see." That is a limit on the instrument, not a fact about the world.

**Mode:** `specified`. **Verdict D2a:** `gap`, unreachable at this budget, not refuted, not confirmed. **Triangulation:** three figures with different roles (instrument rule, closure theorem, search verdict), low overlap between the argument forms, all inside one framework. Marked `triangulated` within the figures; no independent world witness for a gap. **Refuter:** any signal at any resolution registering from outside our horizon closes D2a.

### F4 — There is one subclass with an edge to extend, and it has already returned a constraint

Fig 6 :34: **"The question is not whether a sensor could exist. It is whether anything, at any resolution, is already returning something that fails to resolve."** CMB maps return localized anomalies and upper limits. That is a proto-instrument. Fig 6 :33: **"Nobody specified a telescope from an account of Mars. A wandering point in the sky was already a reading, and every sharper instrument was built on the residual the last one produced."**

World corroboration (secondary, low overlap with the figures): Feeney, Johnson, McEwen, Mortlock and Peiris (2013) write that "the WMAP 7-year data do not favor the addition of either cosmic textures or bubble collisions to the standard cosmological model" and constrain the expected numbers to "fewer than 4.0 and 5.2 at 95% confidence, respectively." Feeney, Elsner, Johnson and Peiris (2015) forecast that "very near-future data will have the final word on bubble collisions in the CMB." Hawking and Hertog (2017) conjecture that "the exit from eternal inflation does not produce an infinite fractal-like multiverse, but is finite and reasonably smooth," which changes what a multiverse theory predicts and therefore what an instrument should look for.

**Mode:** `specified`. **Verdict D2b:** testable, currently constrained in a specific signature class, not excluded in general. **Triangulation:** framework rule plus two independent CMB data analyses, low overlap between a metatheory and a likelihood pipeline. **Refuter:** a detected signature whose statistics survive model selection against standard fluctuations moves D2b to grounded. A proof that no CMB signature could discriminate a collision at any resolution would move D2b into D2a and collapse this distinction.

### F5 — The measure problem is a link-three break and a convention doing the work of a measurement

Fig 3 :27: **"A link with no instrument is not a link that works. It is a link nobody has looked at."** Fig 10 :18: **"a declared meaning is not a grounded one."** Fig 10 :47: **"a convention that decides an outcome has stopped being one."**

World corroboration (secondary): Linde and Noorbala (2010) find that of four measures applied to a toy model, "only for the stationary measure the predictions change continuously" while "for the proper-time and the scale factor cutoff, the predictions are strongly discontinuous." So different rules give different answers on the same dynamics. The *Stanford Encyclopedia of Philosophy* entry on Fine-Tuning (§4.4, opened this session) says "concrete multiverse scenarios such as the landscape multiverse are extremely difficult to test" and that typicality principles "have the benefit of making multiverse theories at least in principle testable," while adding that "the freedom to choose, for example, an observer proxy and a cosmic measure makes typicality-based predictions from multiverse theories untrustworthy and susceptible to confirmation bias." SEP calls the regularization difficulty "the measure problem of cosmology" and reports that some cosmologists call it "the greatest crisis in physics today."

**Mode:** `specified`. **Verdict D3:** `gap`. A convention conflict, not an empirical question as currently posed. **Triangulation:** framework rule plus two independent literatures (a measure comparison in physics and a philosophical assessment), low overlap. **Refuter:** a measure-independent prediction, derived from eternal inflation without choosing a typicality prior or observer proxy, would close D3 and refute this finding.

### F6 — "It explains everything" is the named failure, not a virtue

Fig 5 :43-44: **"A term that explains everything by saying nothing fails this, and so does a term that explains one occasion perfectly."** Fig 1 :27: **"They are frame-internal. A frame cannot score itself with a quantity it also produces."**

The accusation recorded in the Wikipedia summary of Steinhardt's position is that "in a multiverse, any outcome is equally possible, so inflation makes no predictions and, hence, is untestable." If that were the whole theory, the figure verdict would agree with the critic: a claim that accommodates every observation pays no bargain. But the criticism targets the measure-augmented claim (D3), not the CMB-facing mechanism (D1). Fig 5's rule applies to the former. Applying it to the latter is the category error this audit keeps separating.

**Mode:** `specified`. **Verdict:** the charge is valid against a specific construction, not against the package. **Refuter:** exhibit a measure-augmented multiverse claim that states a restrictive, risky prediction and pays the description-length bargain. It would then survive this finding.

### F7 — Even the strongest critic appeals to a ground, so the debate is not unfalsifiable in D1

Ijjas, Steinhardt and Loeb (2013) conclude their abstract: "Forthcoming searches for B-modes, non-Gaussianity and new particles should be decisive." A critic who names decisive searches is treated by the figures as ranging against a ground, not negotiating. Fig 7 :35: **"A room's agreement is variation, not a verdict. Selection between rooms is where the ground votes."**

Planck 2018 X also supplies a live constraint: "The Planck 95% CL upper limit on the tensor-to-scalar ratio, r_0.002 < 0.10, is further tightened by combining with the BICEP2/Keck Array BK15 data to obtain r_0.002 < 0.056." Models that predict larger tensors lose against that number. That is falsification in the ordinary sense, happening now in D1.

**Mode:** `specified`. **Verdict D1:** testable and partly tested; not unfalsifiable. **Triangulation:** a critic's own stated method plus an actual upper limit plus Fig 7. Low overlap between the critic's rhetoric and the satellite constraint. **Refuter:** show that the critic's "decisive searches" are rhetorical rather than an appeal to an instrument that could return a negative.

### F8 — Proponents and critics are rooms, not anchors

Fig 2 :35 and :40 are quoted in §2. Fig 2 :42: **"Buy independence in proportion to the cost of being wrong."** Guth and Linde agreeing is one evidence pool. Steinhardt and Ijjas agreeing is another. If the two pools use the same Planck data set, they overlap on the data even where they differ on the inference, so their disagreement is not two fully independent witnesses either. Fig 8 :39-43: **"Underivable-in-principle and unreachable-at-this-budget are different facts, and they issue different instructions."**

**Mode:** `general` (a rule about verification, not a physics result). **Verdict:** no verdict from agreement. **Refuter:** none available from inside the figures; this is a rule the framework imposes on itself, and a rule is not refuted by a single contrary conversation.

### F9 — The ensemble is arrangements over a closed set, not new primitives

Fig 13 :115: **"The set is closed. The arrangements are not."** Fig 12 :117: **"isomer. Same atoms, different bond, different substance. The difference is invisible in the formula and decisive in the behaviour."** An infinite multiverse does not require an infinite inventory of new kinds. It requires many arrangements of one set. Hawking and Hertog's conjecture that the exit is "finite and reasonably smooth" changes the arrangement count without adding a primitive. Worked out in `06_isomers_and_order.md`.

**Mode:** `general`. **Verdict:** shape only. **Refuter:** a pocket whose description requires a primitive outside the six-term set breaks the isomer reading and would be a genuine import.

### F10 — The popular slogan has a nonzero round-trip gap

Fig 4 :30: **"So take the round trip and measure the gap. Without it you are navigating by dead reckoning: confident about a position nobody checked."** Sent up: "inflation is testable." Brought back down as: "the multiverse has been tested." What the coarser description cannot hold is the anchor restriction: the first sentence is about D1, the second silently claims D2a and D3. The gap `R_T` is exactly the four-domain split of §1. It is recorded qualitatively in `11_audit.md`.

**Mode:** `specified`. **Verdict:** do not use the slogan. **Refuter:** a demonstration that D1's anchor transfers to D2a and D3 without remainder would set `R_T` to zero.

## 4. Pre-registered refuter

Written before the verdict in §5 was drafted. Per the exemplar's rule, a falsifier written before the run is a design instrument. The shape claim is: *testability splits by anchor availability, and no domain's verdict transfers to another*. It is refused if any of the following holds:

1. **Collision detection.** A spatially localized CMB signature is detected, survives model-selection against standard fluctuations, and is independently attributed to a bubble collision. That closes D2b and weakens the "no anchor anywhere beyond our patch" claim.
2. **Measure-independent prediction.** A prediction of eternal inflation is derived that does not depend on the choice of measure, typicality prior or observer proxy. That closes D3 and refutes F5.
3. **Mechanism falsified.** A ground reading shows the primordial perturbation spectrum is incompatible with every slow-roll inflation model, for example a non-Gaussianity shape none of them produces. That refutes F7 and D1's partial grounding.
4. **Genuine underivability shown.** It is proven that no possible observation could discriminate eternal inflation from a single-universe model even in principle, not merely at this budget. That moves D2a from `unreachable at this budget` to `underivable in principle`, which is a different fact with different instructions (Fig 8 :39-43).
5. **A multiverse anchor is defined.** An anchor is specified for the ensemble claim whose answer does not move when a proponent or critic pushes on it. That dissolves D2a and D3 as gaps.
6. **Framework-level failure.** The anchor rule of Fig 2 :29 is shown not to apply to cosmological ensemble claims. That refutes the whole derivation, not one domain.

If any fires, the affected finding is withdrawn in the retraction log below with the reason and the date. Until one fires, nothing in this folder is labelled `grounded`.

## 5. Verdict (the conclusion, after the refuter above)

The four domains keep separate verdicts and are never averaged.

| domain | verdict | mode | status |
|---|---|---|---|
| D1 inflation in our patch | testable, partly tested by CMB and BAO, not unfalsifiable | `specified` (world measurements reported, not produced) | triangulated |
| D2a other pockets, generically | unreachable, `gap`, not refuted and not confirmed | `specified` | triangulated within figures |
| D2b bubble collisions with our bubble | testable, constrained in one signature class, not excluded | `specified` | triangulated (figures plus CMB analyses) |
| D3 measure and typicality | `gap`, a convention conflict, not a measurement | `specified` | triangulated (figures plus two literatures) |
| D4 verification frame | no independent verdict; this seat shares the evidence pool | `general` | single-source, simulated |

The one-line answer: **not one verdict**. The package is a union of an empirically tested mechanism, an instrument-limited ensemble claim, a testable collision subclass, and a measure convention with no anchor. "Unfalsifiable" is correct only about D3 and generic D2a. "Tested" is correct only about D1 and part of D2b. The framework's own words for the honest shape are Fig 9 :32, **"you get a kind of thing, never the answer itself."**

### Strain summary after re-census

One live strain, named and not hidden: Fig 6 permits importing a frame whose closure differs (Fig 6 :23), but neither the figures nor any world source read here supplies a source gate for an anchor on D3. The measure problem may be solved by a future mathematical result that makes a prediction measure-independent. That would close D3 without new observational contact, which is exactly the case the figures do not price. It is recorded as open, not smoothed into a verdict. No other strain remains.

**Final status:** `specified`, ungrounded, candidate, with four domain-local verdicts and six pre-registered refuters. The strongest single figure-grounded point is not that either side is right, but that the question was bundled (Fig 9 :54) and must be unbundled before it can be tested at all.
