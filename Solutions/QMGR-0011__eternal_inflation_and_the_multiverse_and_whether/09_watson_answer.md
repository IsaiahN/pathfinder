## DOMAIN CENSUS

R by arity, symmetry and scale, then one line per domain with actors, currency and anchor, then partition or union, then order by dependency.

- **R, the residual.** A family of theories reproduces our observed cosmos with high precision, yet the same dynamics generically produce a causally disconnected ensemble whose members vary. Arity: four conditions in a union, with overlaps. Symmetry: local dynamics inside one pocket is approximately homogeneous and isotropic, so pockets are rearrangements of one closed set; the global claim breaks that symmetry because we are inside our pocket. Scale: first order our patch and its CMB, second order the ensemble, third order the rule assigning probabilities across the ensemble.
- **D1, inflation in our patch.** Actors: inflaton, false vacuum, quantum fluctuations, CMB photons and polarization. Currency: measured spectra n_s, r, non-Gaussianity, flatness. Anchor: Planck and CMB/BAO data, which returned numbers. Question type: partition of inflation models.
- **D2a, other pockets generically.** Actors: other bubbles, false vacuum, walls beyond our horizon. Currency: causal contact, any signal. Anchor: none nameable; nothing has registered from a causally disconnected region at any resolution. Question type: unreachable, a gap, not a partition question.
- **D2b, bubble collisions with our bubble.** Actors: colliding walls, CMB temperature and polarization maps. Currency: spatially localized CMB signatures. Anchor: conditional and present, the CMB anomaly searches returning candidates that fail to resolve. Question type: partition of signature classes.
- **D3, measure and typicality.** Actors: probability measures, observer proxies, vacua. Currency: predicted probabilities. Anchor: none; competing measures are not decided by a counter, market or experiment. Question type: split into well-formed questions, and blocked at the objective link.
- **D4, the verification frame.** Actors: proponents, critics, reviewers. Currency: agreement and residual. Anchor: the ground only, never agreement. In this simulated run all seats share one evidence pool, so this frame has no independent verdict.
- **Partition or union.** Union, four conditions with overlaps. D2b is the one anchored part of the D2a ensemble claim; D3 only becomes predictive when D1 quantities are combined with a rule. Fig 9 :54 "And when the split lands on a union rather than a partition, unbundle before you search again."
- **Order by dependency.** Settle D1 before D2b, because the collision search uses the inflationary background to define its signal. Run D2b before D2a, because it is our only edge onto the ensemble. D3 is last and blocked at the objective link.

Strain test before selection: Fig 6 :33 governs a quantity already returning something, which is D2b; Fig 6 :40 governs one that never registered, which is D2a. Different objects, so no strain after re-census.

## VERDICT (from the figures)

The question has four verdicts, not one. D1 inflation in our patch is testable and partly tested by CMB and BAO, not unfalsifiable. D2a other pockets generically is a gap, unreachable at this budget, not refuted and not confirmed. D2b bubble collisions with our bubble is testable and constrained in one signature class, not excluded. D3 measure and typicality is a gap, a convention conflict rather than a measurement. D4 has no independent verdict in this simulated run. The word unfalsifiable is correct only for D3 and generic D2a; the word tested is correct only for D1 and part of D2b. This is a `specified`, ungrounded, candidate result. The strongest figure-grounded point is not that either side is right but that the question was bundled and must be unbundled before it can be tested at all.

- **reversed by:** a ground reading, not agreement.
