## RESIDUAL

The source question bundles four conditions: inflation happened in our patch; inflation is future-eternal and produces other pockets; a measure or typicality rule selects what observers should expect; and the package is said to explain why the constants are right for life. `01_figure_grounded_audit.md` splits these into D1, D2a, D2b and D3. This file owes three pieces of supporting work that the split alone does not supply:

1. **Structure.** Show that an unbounded ensemble of pockets is arrangements over a closed set, not an inventory of new primitives. If it needed new primitives, the union claim would be a different kind of claim from the tested mechanism.
2. **Order.** Show the dependency order among the domains, and identify which link is broken by having no instrument.
3. **Analogue.** Say precisely what the measure problem is an analogue of, and why an import into D3 would not by itself close D3.

Each is stated as an effect term and made refusable before any conclusion is drawn.

## DERIVATION

### Q1. Pockets are isomers over a closed set, not new primitives

Fig 13 :115 "The set is closed. The arrangements are not." Fig 12 :117 "isomer. Same atoms, different bond, different substance. The difference is invisible in the formula and decisive in the behaviour."

The ensemble claim does not require an infinite inventory of new kinds. It requires many arrangements of one closed set, where which state a structure is bonded to is part of what it is. Different vacua differ in the bond and therefore in the substance, while the atoms are the ones already held. Hawking and Hertog (2017) conjecture that the exit from eternal inflation is "finite and reasonably smooth" rather than an infinite fractal-like multiverse. That conjecture changes the arrangement count, and therefore what an instrument should look for, without adding a primitive. It is a conjecture reported by a world source, not a measurement produced here.

**Falsifier, written before the result.** If a pocket were described that requires a primitive outside the closed set, the isomer reading fails. That would be a genuine import, not an arrangement. Until such a description is produced, the ensemble is a combinatorics of the held set.

**Mode:** `general` for the structural claim; `specified` for the mapping onto pockets. **Status:** single-source for the figure definition of isomer, with the physics-facing check unmeasured.

### Q2. Dependency order and the broken link

The chain is D1 settled -> D2b edge -> D2a blocked -> D3 blocked. Fig 12 :104 "A settled molecule becomes an atom for whatever composes over it. So irreducible is a fact about a level and never about the world." D1 is settled against a ground (the CMB returned n_s and r limits), so it can serve as an atom for the collision search. D2b has a proto-instrument: Fig 6 :34 "The question is not whether a sensor could exist. It is whether anything, at any resolution, is already returning something that fails to resolve." D2a has none: Fig 6 :40 "What is unavailable is a sensor for something that has never registered anywhere, at any resolution. That is the whole of it."

Fig 3 :27 governs the objective link where D3 sits. A probability rule with no instrument is a link nobody has looked at. A verdict read below that break is a reading of nothing, not a negative result. Fig 11 :40 "An improvement that does not change contact changes nothing, however much it improves." Adding vacua or refining a prior improves the agent's description without changing contact.

**Falsifier.** Name an instrument for D3 whose answer does not move when an observer proxy or time-slicing is changed. The break closes. Failing that, the order stands.

**Mode:** `specified`. **Status:** triangulated within the figures (level rule, instrument rule, contact rule), with no independent witness for the break.

### Q3. The measure problem as a convention doing the work of a measurement

Fig 10 :18 "a declared meaning is not a grounded one." Fig 10 :47 "a convention that decides an outcome has stopped being one." These two lines locate D3. A measure is a declared meaning until a ground settles it. When the choice of measure changes the predicted observation, the convention has stopped being a convention and is deciding an outcome without a ground.

Linde and Noorbala (2010) report that of four measures applied to a toy model, only for the stationary measure the predictions change continuously, while the proper-time and scale-factor cutoff predictions are strongly discontinuous. The Stanford Encyclopedia of Philosophy (Fine-Tuning, section 4.4) reports that typicality principles make multiverse theories "at least in principle testable" while the freedom to choose an observer proxy and a cosmic measure makes the predictions "untrustworthy and susceptible to confirmation bias." Both halves are needed: the first says a test is possible, the second says no specific test is yet licensed.

This is the analogue, stated exactly: D3 is not a fifth domain and not a harder version of D2a. It is the link-three break of Fig 3, a convention occupying the objective link. The honest verdict is `gap`, not `false` and not `silence`.

**Falsifier.** A measure-independent prediction derived from eternal inflation without choosing a typicality prior or observer proxy would close D3 and refute this finding.

**Mode:** `specified`. **Status:** triangulated (framework rule plus a physics measure-comparison plus a philosophical assessment, low overlap).

### Q4. Strain test: a new measure is access, not settlement

Two apparent figure conflicts were re-censused rather than smoothed.

- Fig 6 :33 "Nobody specified a telescope from an account of Mars. A wandering point in the sky was already a reading, and every sharper instrument was built on the residual the last one produced." appears to pull against Fig 6 :40 (no sensor for something that never registered). Re-census: :33 governs a quantity already returning something, which is D2b; :40 governs one that has never registered, which is D2a. Different objects, so no strain.
- `05_what_shape_means.md` section 3 says a provenanced import needs a source gate and a shadow test, which could be overread as "any import into D3 closes the gap." Fig 6 :25-26 "An import adds no atom to the world. The world does not gain primitives; a frame gains access." A new measure gives our frame access to a prediction. It does not add an observable. D3 closes only when the prediction is checkable against a ground. Both statements are kept.

After re-census one live strain remains, named and not hidden: Fig 6 :23 "No frame escapes its own closure. The bound moves only on contact with a frame whose closure differs." permits importing a frame whose closure differs, but neither the figures nor any world source read here supplies a source gate for an anchor on D3. A future mathematical result could make a prediction measure-independent and close D3 without new observational contact. That is precisely the case the figures do not price. It is recorded as open.

## CONCLUSION

Pockets are rearrangements of a closed set, so an unbounded ensemble needs no new primitives (Q1). The dependency order is D1 then D2b then D2a, with D3 blocked at the objective link (Q2). The measure problem is that break, a convention deciding outcomes without a ground (Q3). The instrument rule and the no-sensor rule govern different domains, and a new measure is access rather than settlement (Q4). Each claim carries its falsifier above.
