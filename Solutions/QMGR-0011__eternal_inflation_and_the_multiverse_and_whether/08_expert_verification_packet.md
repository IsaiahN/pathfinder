# Expert verification packet: testability of eternal inflation and the multiverse

**What this is:** a claim in a form a cosmologist or philosopher of science can check. Mode, derivation and refuters are stated before the conclusion, so a reader knows what to look for and what would settle it.

**Mode: `specified`, not `grounded`.** No measurement is claimed by this run. This is a shape and a route, not a result. Some world measurements are reported from sources that were opened; they are attributed, not produced.

## 1. The claim, in one paragraph

The question "is eternal inflation and the multiverse testable or unfalsifiable" is a union of four conditions with different anchors, so it has four verdicts that must not be averaged. Inflation in our patch has a ground, the cosmic microwave background, and is testable and partly tested. Other pocket universes in general have no nameable anchor and are unreachable at this budget, which is a verdict and not a silence and not a refutation. One subclass, a bubble collision with our bubble, has an edge onto the ensemble through CMB maps and is constrained in a specific signature class without being excluded in general. The measure or typicality rule that would turn the ensemble into predictions has no ground, so different rules give different answers on the same dynamics and that part is a convention conflict rather than a measurement. Therefore "unfalsifiable" is the right word for the measure domain and generic other pockets, and the wrong word for the tested mechanism and the collision subclass. The framework returns this shape, not a decision about existence.

## 2. Three checkable sub-claims

### C1. The union split by anchor availability

- **Statement:** the package splits into domains with different grounds, and a verdict from one does not transfer to another.
- **Source, verbatim:** Fig 9 :4-5 "Disagreements that do not shrink with effort, where each rule keeps working well on a different subset, are not one hard question but several well-formed ones. Split it rather than search." Fig 9 :54 "And when the split lands on a union rather than a partition, unbundle before you search again." Fig 2 :29 "An anchor is whatever settles the question and does not change its answer because of what any frame thinks." Fig 1 :23 "The ground enters as the only metric."
- **What to check:** do the four conditions actually have different anchors, and is any claim made about D2a or D3 borrowing D1's CMB ground?
- **Refuter:** a single observation that simultaneously settles the mechanism, the generic ensemble, the collision subclass and the measure would show the split is unnecessary. None is named in any source read.

### C2. Generic pockets are unreachable; the collision subclass has an edge

- **Statement:** "no anchor anywhere" is false because D2b has a proto-instrument, but that edge reaches only the collision subclass, not the generic ensemble.
- **Source, verbatim:** Fig 6 :34 "The question is not whether a sensor could exist. It is whether anything, at any resolution, is already returning something that fails to resolve." Fig 6 :40 "What is unavailable is a sensor for something that has never registered anywhere, at any resolution. That is the whole of it." Fig 8 :39 "So a search that finds nothing is never a proof of absence." Fig 13 :134 "Unreachable is a verdict here, not a silence".
- **World check:** Feeney et al. (2013) report that WMAP 7-year data do not favor bubble collisions and constrain the expected numbers to fewer than 4.0 at 95% confidence. Guth (2007) concedes in his abstract that the infinity of pocket universes is unobservable while arguing that eternal inflation has real consequences for how predictions are extracted. Both halves are needed.
- **Refuter:** any signal at any resolution registering from outside our horizon closes D2a. A detected collision signature surviving model selection against standard fluctuations moves D2b to grounded. A proof that no CMB signature could discriminate a collision at any resolution would collapse D2b into D2a and remove this distinction.

### C3. The measure problem is a broken objective link, not a fifth domain

- **Statement:** the measure rule occupies the objective link of the chain and has no instrument. A convention that decides an outcome is illegitimate, and a null result here is not a negative measurement.
- **Source, verbatim:** Fig 3 :27 "A link with no instrument is not a link that works. It is a link nobody has looked at." Fig 10 :18 "a declared meaning is not a grounded one." Fig 10 :47 "a convention that decides an outcome has stopped being one." Fig 11 :40 "An improvement that does not change contact changes nothing, however much it improves."
- **World check:** Linde and Noorbala (2010) find that only the stationary measure changes predictions continuously, while proper-time and scale-factor cutoffs are strongly discontinuous. The Stanford Encyclopedia of Philosophy (Fine-Tuning, section 4.4) reports both that typicality principles make multiverse theories at least in principle testable and that the freedom to choose an observer proxy and a cosmic measure makes such predictions untrustworthy and susceptible to confirmation bias.
- **Refuter:** a measure-independent prediction derived from eternal inflation without choosing a typicality prior or observer proxy would close D3 and refute this sub-claim.

## 3. What is explicitly not claimed

- Not that the multiverse exists or does not exist, and not that eternal inflation is established.
- Not that a null CMB search disproves other bubbles. Fig 8 :39.

## 5. What would settle it

A ground reading, not agreement. The six pre-registered refuters are in `01` section 4 and `07` section 4. In compact form:

1. A collision signature that survives model selection and is independently attributed (closes D2b).
2. A measure-independent prediction (closes D3).
3. A perturbation spectrum incompatible with every slow-roll model (refutes D1).
4. A proof of underivability in principle, not just at this budget (changes D2a's instruction).
5. An anchor for the ensemble whose answer does not move under pushing (dissolves D2a and D3).
6. A demonstration that the anchor rule does not apply to cosmological ensembles (refutes the whole derivation).
