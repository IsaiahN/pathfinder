## What is deliberately not here

- No personal emails of named individuals.
- No ranking of people by how likely they are to believe the claim.
- No bulk-send plan, no purchased list, no harvesting from private posts.

Reasons, in the figures' own words, adapted to this question:

1. **Agreement is not a ground.** Fig 2 :35, "Alignment is triangulation, not negotiation. A mirror cannot correct you." A list selected for receptiveness manufactures the correlation that would make its agreement worthless (Fig 2 :40).
2. **The verifier must not hold the interiors.** Fig 10 :42, "A verifier that can reconstruct the claim cannot verify it." The first contact carries the residual and its refuter, never the framework.
3. **Prestige cannot settle an evaluation.** *The Recursive Self-Improvement Trap* (essay, opened 2026-11-24): "Prestige can fund an action. It can give an action a budget to be tried. But it cannot settle an action. Only the ground can settle it."

Legally: public institutional submission forms and opt-in channels avoid the GDPR or CAN-SPAM failure that unsolicited bulk email creates, and a form that lets a recipient decline is closer to the figure's rule than an attachment pushed to a list.
