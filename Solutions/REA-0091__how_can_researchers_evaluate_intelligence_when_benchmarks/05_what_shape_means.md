## 1. A shape is a constraint you can state before you can name the answer

The canonical usage rule (`FRAMEWORK/reference/FIGURES_README_canonical.md`, local copy read 2026-11-24) says the framework returns "what a missing thing would have to look like (a shape, not a name)", then "stops at the limit of what the available instruments can distinguish, and records the lineage so the stop is honest." Fig 9 :32: "you get a kind of thing, never the answer itself."

From *The Library of Babel*: "you can cut your search short if the set of books returned is small and descriptive enough that it provides a general shape of the answer that satisfies your question." And the method: "With each new term gained, you narrow the space by definition and deepen your vocabulary."

So for REA-0091 a **shape** is the form any acceptable evaluation must have, stated before we pick a benchmark: a private ground the model cannot reach, per-slot residuals with a trend, independent instruments, a maintainer who refreshes without authoring, and a provenance record. "Use benchmark X" is a name. "Keep a ground that does not move when the system is optimized against it" is a shape.

## 2. How the shape is obtained here

- *Library of Babel*: use the vocabulary you already have that is closest to the thing, look for recurring terms that overlap or correlate with it, and recurse. "This is a bootstrapping convergence process."
- *Library of Babel* on bundling: "Perhaps your problem was a bundle or conflation of several problems masquerading as one? You will need to search all of those problems individually." This is the essay's own version of the Fig 9 union finding (F1).
- Fig 6 :30: "The description travels. Fails by being sharpened into terms only we can read. Cast it in effect terms." Fig 9 :30-31: "your words are borrowed too, so describe the effect rather than the cause."
- *Decoding Tacit Knowledge*: "When contact isn't possible, the best you can send is a plain description of what you'd expect to see, because anyone can check that against the world." A shape is exactly that: an effect description that a researcher can range against the world before accepting a framework.

## 3. What licenses a name

Only two things, both outside the shape itself:

1. **The ground settles it.** Formula step 5: "the ground settles it. Verified only by what pays. A gate is not the ground." The canonical README: the framework stops where instruments cannot distinguish and records the lineage.
2. **A provenanced import from a closure that differs** (Fig 6 :23, Fig 8 :50-52), with a source gate and a shadow test.

*They're Scanning Books* sharpens the second: "The real import is not a new term in the existing space. It is a new dimension of the space." And: "A minimum viable set of six terms is a human's careful guess at reality's compression axes; another sensorium's minimum set need not be those six." Also: "Declaring the primitives does not make them neutral. It makes the choice visible."

So a benchmark is a visible choice of compression axis, not a neutral window onto capability. Naming one as *the* measure turns a visible choice into an invisible one. That is why this solution returns a protocol shape and refuses to recommend a single benchmark.

## 4. The metamerism error in benchmark evaluation

*The Hard Problem is About Source* gives the mechanism. A colorimeter reads green and "cannot comment on the source": one green light, yellow plus blue, RGB, CMYK, or a scheme not yet discovered. "If several paths create the same behavior, then behavior is a projection with lossy information." And: "An instrument that can't distinguish these cases demonstrates its own inadequacy, it cannot claim anything substantial about what is being measured or what causes it." It was the **spectrometer**, not an argument, that resolved it.

Applied here: a benchmark score of 90 percent is a projection. It can come from capability, from memorized items, from metric optimization, or from a substituted habitat that omitted the hard parts. The score does not determine the source. The essay's own rule: "the answer is unreached until a better instrument exists", and "It was proven wrong not by argument, but by the arrival of a better instrument." So the remedy for memorization and gaming is a discriminating instrument (a canaried held-out set, a perturbation suite, a real habitat), not a better argument about what the score "really means."

*How Intelligence Actually Works* adds the same point as provenance loss: a video frame and a photograph "are indistinguishable as artifacts, and they carry different warrants." "Indiscernibility is not identity." A high score and a capable system can be indiscernible to the instrument while remaining different things.

## 5. A benchmark score is a test of fluency until it is checked

*Decoding Tacit Knowledge*: "Sounding right isn't the same as being checked against the world, which is why I call the Turing test a test of fluency, not of accuracy." The same applies to a benchmark trained into the model. Memorization and gaming produce fluent answers with no new contact. Fig 11 :40: "An improvement that does not change contact changes nothing, however much it improves."

*How a Society of Agents Would Work* gives the check: the ground is "the world itself", which "does not change its mind because of what any agent thinks." It also warns that "Ten copies watching the same thing are one opinion said ten times." Multiple benchmarks trained on the same corpus are ten copies.

## 6. Prestige is not action

*The Recursive Self-Improvement Trap*: "Prestige must never be allowed to validate an action directly." "Prestige can fund an action. It can give an action a budget to be tried. But it cannot settle an action. Only the ground can settle it. The Ground Maintainer must be blind to prestige." A prestigious leaderboard, a prestigious lab or a prestigious reviewer can give an evaluation a budget to be tried. It cannot make the score true.

## 7. Simulation status

Same essay: "LLMs can quickly simulate an approximate answer/output with the formula found in the architecture post." But "the truly testable output would need to be derived from the real architecture or working the formula yourself." The *Arrival of the Fittest* article adds: simulated outputs "could never be verified if the solutions presented were not contrived to fit the formula", and the outputs are "simulacra."

Therefore `01_figure_grounded_audit.md`, this file and every other file in this folder are a simulated derivation checked for internal consistency against the figures. They are not a ground reading. They cannot settle REA-0091 or validate any benchmark.

## 8. Correction to my own §4 (kept visible)

I first wrote §4 as "a score cannot tell you its source, so a benchmark can never validate capability." That overstates the rule. The essay's claim is conditional: an instrument that **cannot distinguish** sources demonstrates its own inadequacy, and the case is resolved when a **better instrument** arrives. A canaried private held-out set plus perturbation tests plus real-habitat outcomes is exactly such a better instrument, at least for the memorization and gaming cases. The weaker, correct form stands: a single score does not determine its source until a discriminating instrument is shown, and the burden is on the evaluator to produce that instrument. The earlier sentence is retracted, not silently erased. Triangulated with Fig 6 :32 and Fig 8 :39.

## 9. One-paragraph summary

For this question, a shape is the set of constraints an evaluation must satisfy before any number is trusted: a ground that does not move when the system pushes on it, residuals kept per capability rather than averaged into one score, a verifier that cannot see the items before acting, instruments whose independence is recorded rather than assumed, a maintainer who refreshes or retires on a schedule, and a lineage that says what was held out and what was trained on. The framework does not name a benchmark. It says what any acceptable benchmark protocol must look like, states what would refute that shape in advance, and stops where the available instruments can no longer discriminate.
