# The Tether's answer on evaluating intelligence when benchmarks fail

**Mode: `specified`, not `grounded`.** This is a shape and a route, not a result. Simulated derivation checked against the figures (`05_what_shape_means.md` §7), not a ground reading.

## 1. The residual it answers

A benchmark score is a reading built to stand in for a capability. It comes loose in three ways: the items enter the training set (memorization), the system optimizes the metric (gaming), or the instrument saturates (obsolescence). Fig 9 :55: "A bundled problem is usually a union of overlapping conditions, not a list of separate ones." This is a union compound with its own loops, not one hard question. Its central defect is that the reading moves when the frame pushes on it, which by Fig 2 :30 means "it is not an anchor."

## 2. The shape

1. **One lever does not clear the compound.** Memorization needs an unreachable ground; gaming needs a verifier that the scored party cannot author; obsolescence needs a refresh or retirement rule. Intersections are compounds with their own loops (Fig 9 :55-58). Three loops, then one per nonempty intersection.
2. **An evaluation anchor must not update on the system being evaluated.** Fig 2 :29: "An anchor is whatever settles the question and does not change its answer because of what any frame thinks." Fig 1 :27: "A frame cannot score itself with a quantity it also produces." A canary-marked, private, held-out set whose generation code is not released is the operational form of this. LiveBench and HLE both implement it; the figures give the reason, not the vendor.
3. **The per-slot residual must not be averaged away.** Fig 1 :19: "The residual is indexed by object slot, not aggregated." Fig 1 :22: "Aggregating across slots is how a live signal disappears into an average." Report capability-by-capability residuals and their trend, not one leaderboard number. Two benchmarks with the same score and different access conditions are isomers in Fig 12's sense: same atoms, different bond, different warrant.
4. **Saturation is a dead channel, not a capability verdict.** Fig 10 :24: "the ground does not decay, the channel does"; Fig 10 :27-28: "the output is not a verdict, it is you have lost touch." Fig 8 :39: "So a search that finds nothing is never a proof of absence." Retire or refill the instrument; do not conclude that intelligence has been attained.
5. **A benchmark solve is a substituted habitat.** Fig 11 :70: "A test harness is a substituted habitat. Which is why a synthetic solve proves wiring and never capability." Fig 11 :39: "capability is a property of agent-and-habitat, never of the agent alone." What you failed to reproduce is invisible until the goal fails (Fig 11 :68). Where the question is constitutively about people and delayed consequences, the real habitat is the ground and human judgment can be the anchor; for a code-correctness question it is only an instrument with axes it does not measure (Fig 2 :36-38).
6. **Independence is provenance, not count.** Fig 2 :40: "disqualifying for a verdict: agreement about the world is worthless when the agreeing frames share an evidence pool." Ten benchmarks trained on the same corpus are one witness. Buy independence with different habitats, modalities and closures (Fig 2 :42).
7. **The verifier must not hold the interiors.** Fig 10 :42: "A verifier that can reconstruct the claim cannot verify it." Fig 10 :84 gives the zero-knowledge form. The first contact carries the residual and the refuter, never the framework.
8. **The framework names no benchmark.** Fig 9 :32: "you get a kind of thing, never the answer itself." It returns the constraints any acceptable evaluation must satisfy, plus the refuter written in advance. Which instrument to build is an import from a closure that differs, with provenance and a shadow test.

## 3. What it explicitly does not say

- Not that any particular benchmark, lab, leaderboard or number is invalid or false. No vendor is accused of gaming here.
- Not that a benchmark score can never be useful. It is an instrument. The claim is about what it can settle, not whether it can measure.
- Not "intelligence is undefinable." Fig 11 :36 reframes: where nothing maintains anything, intelligence is a label applied afterwards; the answerable question is what makes a system capable.
- Not that human judgment is always the ground. Fig 2 :36-38: it is the right anchor only when the question is constitutively about people.
- Not a derivation of one evaluation from another, and no new primitive.
- Not `grounded`. No held-out result, contamination test or deployment outcome was taken for this derivation.
- Not a claim that the figure set has been validated. It is a metatheory and cannot validate itself (*Arrival of the Fittest*, essay 2026-08-19: "Because it is a metatheory, it cannot be validated, nor can it validate itself.").

## 4. What would settle it — the ground

A discriminating instrument, not a further argument. *The Hard Problem is About Source*: an instrument that reads green "cannot comment on the source", and the spectrometer, not an argument, resolved it. Here:

- **Memorization test:** does a canary-marked private set withheld from training separate two systems that tie on the public set? A failure to separate, with the budget sufficient, is a `gap` to report, not silence (Fig 8 :39).
- **Gaming test:** does raising the reported score ever lower the outcome in a reproduced habitat? Reward hacking is defined exactly this way (Skalse et al., NeurIPS 2022).
- **Saturation test:** does the residual keep a direction on harder fresh items after the public set is exhausted? If not, record the channel as lost (Fig 10 :24-29).
- **Habitat test:** does a benchmark solve transfer to the real goal with all required conditions reproduced and no unenumerated actors (Fig 11 :67-70)?
- **Standing refuters from §4 of `01_figure_grounded_audit.md`:** a scalar that determines all per-slot residuals; an unhackable general proxy; a fully informed verifier that is still sound; a private set that fails to separate; a solve that transfers to a fully reproduced habitat.

## 5. The honest stop

**Unreached, not unreachable.** The framework stops where the available instruments can no longer discriminate, and records the lineage so the stop is checkable. "Indiscernibility is not identity." A private set, a perturbation suite and a deployed-habitat outcome are instruments that extend what can be represented; they are not new primitives. Underivable in principle and unreachable at this budget are different facts with different instructions (Fig 8 :40-43). Refreshing a private set fixes a budget-limited channel. An axis no instrument can represent needs an import from a different closure, and the import is owed, not silently assumed.

## 6. Pre-registered refuter (same five, written before the conclusion)

1. A single scalar determines every per-slot residual exactly.
2. A general metric is an anchor that does not move under optimization.
3. A benchmark solve transfers to capability in a fully reproduced habitat.
4. A fresh private held-out instrument fails to discriminate between systems that differ in capability for reasons other than budget.
5. A verifier with full access to the item pool and selection rule is still sound against a party optimizing against it.

If any fires, this answer is retracted or narrowed. Until one is run, nothing here is `grounded`.

## 7. One-paragraph version for outreach

> The Tether does not offer a better benchmark and does not claim intelligence has been measured. It says that "memorized, gamed, obsolete" is a union of three overlapping conditions, so one lever cannot clear it; that an evaluation anchor must not move when the system is optimized against it, which a public training-exposed item set does; that a single score hides the per-capability signal that matters, because residuals are indexed by slot and not aggregated; that saturation is a lost channel rather than a capability verdict, since the ground does not decay and the channel does; and that a benchmark solve is a substituted habitat, so it proves wiring and not capability in the real habitat. What it offers is the shape any acceptable evaluation must have: a private unreachable ground, per-slot residuals with a trend, independent instruments whose overlap is recorded, a verifier that cannot see the items before acting, a maintainer who refreshes or retires with provenance, and five refuters written in advance. The question stays open where the instruments can no longer discriminate, and the framework records why rather than filling the gap with a number.
