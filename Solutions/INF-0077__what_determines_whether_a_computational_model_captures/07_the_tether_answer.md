# The Tether's answer: what determines causal capture

**Mode: `general` on the shape, `specified` on the checks, NOT `grounded`.** This is a shape and a route, not a result. Simulated derivation checked for internal consistency against the figures (`01_figure_grounded_audit.md` header); not a ground reading.

## 1. The residual it answers

The question bundles two conditions that do not clear together: a model can fit observations well and still hold the wrong directed arrangement. Fig 9 :55: *"A bundled problem is usually a union of overlapping conditions, not a list of separate ones."* The intersection, good fit plus wrong edges, is Fig 12's isomer case: same ingredients, different bond, decisive difference under intervention. Fig 1 :19: *"The residual is indexed by object slot, not aggregated."* The signal is on a variable or a link, not in a global score.

## 2. The shape

1. **A model's own predictive score cannot settle the question.** Fig 1 :27: *"They are frame-internal. A frame cannot score itself with a quantity it also produces."* Accuracy, likelihood and compression are budget filters (Fig 9 :60), never witnesses. This is what rules out the default reading.

2. **Causal structure is the arrangement, and the arrangement is not in the ingredients.** Fig 12 :94: *"The arrangement is the substance."* Fig 12 :46: *"A bond is the typed relation between two operands. There are seven, they are not interchangeable, and which one holds is not recoverable from the operands."* Two models with the same observational implications are isomers, same atoms different bond, and only intervention separates them.

3. **The vocabulary must be able to write direction and refuse it.** Fig 12 :111: *"A vocabulary without bonds can name a state and cannot name a route to one."* Operators table :58: *"a refutation is a negation, which is why a vocabulary without it can only accumulate."* An ordered bond (`→` or `⇒`) is needed for "A produces B"; negation is needed for "that direction is false." Without both, no amount of fitting produces causal capture.

4. **What settles the arrangement is a non-updating anchor from a closure that differs.** Fig 2 :29: *"An anchor is whatever settles the question and does not change its answer because of what any frame thinks."* Fig 6 :23: *"No frame escapes its own closure. The bound moves only on contact with a frame whose closure differs."* Fig 8 :32: *"Closure is idempotent. Composing inside a closed set never leaves it, so no search, however long, adds an atom."* The canonical case is an intervention, an instrument or an outcome the model did not author. This is a **shape**, not a named procedure.

5. **The residual is per variable and per link.** Fig 1 :22: *"Aggregating across slots is how a live signal disappears into an average."* A global accuracy near zero can coexist with one wrong causal link. The operational question is: changing which variable, by how much, fails to move the prediction as it should?

6. **A synthetic test harness is a substituted habitat.** Fig 11 :70: *"A test harness is a substituted habitat. Which is why a synthetic solve proves wiring and never capability."* Held-out data from the same observational pool tests the wiring, not the contact. Fig 11 :40: *"An improvement that does not change contact changes nothing, however much it improves."*

7. **The check must be independent and the checker must not author the claim.** Fig 2 :40: *"disqualifying for a verdict: agreement about the world is worthless when the agreeing frames share an evidence pool."* Fig 10 :42: *"A verifier that can reconstruct the claim cannot verify it."*

8. **Identifiability is decidable before searching.** Fig 13 :133: *"A system with a closure can answer that before it searches, and what it returns is a shape rather than a name."* Fig 13 :134: *"Unreachable is a verdict here, not a silence."* Report "not identifiable from this graph and data" as an answer, not a failure to answer.

## 3. What it explicitly does not say

- It does not name an estimator. Do-calculus, randomised trials, instrumental variables and additive-noise models are bindings from the world frame, recorded as corroboration in `09_watson_answer.md`, not figure-derived names.
- It does not say that a well-fitting model is useless. Fit is legitimate for prediction and is a valid budget filter. It is not a witness to causal structure.
- It does not say that correlation is always misleading. It says correlation cannot by itself carry the directed arrangement (Fig 12 :46).
- It does not say all causal questions are answerable. Some are non-identifiable from the available closure; that is a verdict (F7).
- It is not `grounded`. No model was run, no intervention performed, no instrument read.

## 4. What would settle it: the ground

A source-discriminating instrument, not a further argument. The framework essay *The Hard Problem is About Source*, as recorded in the exemplar's source set, gives the metamerism case: a reading of green *"cannot comment on the source,"* and the spectrometer, not an argument, resolved it. Here the corresponding questions are:

- Does the model's response to a change in X track the system's response to the same change, on each variable and link? (the intervention test)
- Do two observationally equivalent models come apart under that change? (the isomer test)
- Is the effect identifiable from the graph and data at all? (the reachability test, decidable before collecting)
- Can the checker give a verdict without being able to reconstruct the model from what it was shown? (the independence test)

A failure on any of these is more informative than an endorsement. The refuters are pre-registered in `01_figure_grounded_audit.md` section 5.

## 5. The honest stop

**Unreached, not unreachable.** The figures say *"you get a kind of thing, never the answer itself"* (Fig 9 :32) and *"narrowing gets you close, the ground decides"* (Fig 9 :42). The shape stops at the limit of what the available instruments can distinguish and records the lineage so the stop is checkable. *"Indiscernibility is not identity."* The six-term substrate and the bond vocabulary are a visible choice, not nature's own list; declaring them makes the choice visible, not neutral. A frame that cannot certify its own direction binds the sought-for shape as a refusable term and lets the ground decide.

## 6. One-paragraph version for outreach

> The Tether does not name an estimator, and it does not treat a high accuracy score as proof of causal understanding. It says that a model's own fit is frame-internal and can only filter what to try next; that causal structure is the directed arrangement between variables, which is not recoverable from the observational ingredients, so two models can fit identically and differ decisively under intervention; that writing and correcting that difference needs an ordered bond and a refusal operator; that what settles it is contact with a ground that does not update when the model changes, reached from a frame whose closure differs; and that the failure must be read per variable and per link, by a checker who did not author the claim. A test set from the same observational pool is a substituted habitat: it proves wiring, not causal contact. Identifiability can be checked before the search, and "not identifiable from this graph and data" is a verdict, not a silence. What is offered is the shape any candidate answer must have, four refuters written in advance, and the instruments that would close it. The slot stays open until the ground settles it.
