## 1. Independent domain census (written from the residual line alone)

I redid the census before comparing with the primary's conclusion. The residual line is the question itself: what determines whether a computational model captures the causal structure of the system it describes? Stated in effect terms before any figure was chosen: a gap, on a variable or link, between what a model represents and what the system does when that variable or link changes.

- **R (arity / symmetry / scale).** Arity is per slot, because the failure can be local to one link while every other link is right. Symmetry is asymmetric, because the question asks after a relation whose reverse is a different claim. Scale spans two levels: fitting happens inside the model; settling happens at the system. This is a union compound, not one hard question, because a model can satisfy the fit condition while failing the structure condition.
- **Domain 1, the model frame.** Actors: variables, parameters, candidate graphs. Currency: prediction error, likelihood, compressed description. Anchor: none for the target quantity. A score this frame produces cannot settle what this frame represents. So this domain cannot decide the question, though it can optimize inside it.
- **Domain 2, the system and interventions.** Actors: mechanisms, interventions, instruments, the act of changing something. Currency: response under change. Anchor: the ground, which does not move when a frame pushes on it. This is the deciding domain.
- **Domain 3, the verification frame.** Actors: checkers, other instruments, other closures. Currency: refutations. Anchor: the ground, never agreement. In this simulation its independence from domains 1 and 2 is zero.

**Order by dependency.** Perception, vocabulary, objective, planning, learn and carry. The vocabulary/objective link is where causal directedness is either nameable or not, and it is the link most likely to lack an instrument.

**My census matches the primary's three domains, its union reading and its break location.** I reached them from different starting points: the primary from Fig 1's per-slot residual and Fig 12's bond, I from Fig 9's union/partition split and Fig 3's dependency order. Low overlap between routes within the same framework, so the agreement is weak but not mechanically circular. It is still one evidence pool.

## 4. Round trip gap R_T

The Symbols table defines R_T as the gap between what was sent up and what came back down, `gap(x, (T_E ∘ T_A)(x))`, measuring what the coarser description cannot hold. Fig 4 :30 requires it to be measured rather than assumed zero. I ran the round trip on the condensation this folder actually uses.

1. **Sent up (x):** the verbatim figure set, chiefly Fig 1 :27 (frame-internal scores), Fig 12 :5, :46, :94 (arrangement is the substance), Fig 2 :29 (anchor), Fig 6 :23 (differing closure), Fig 1 :19 (per-slot), Fig 10 :42 (non-access).
2. **Abstraction (T_A):** the shorthand in `07_the_tether_answer.md` and `09_watson_answer.md`: fit is not a witness; structure is the bond; an anchor from a differing closure settles it; check per variable; independent checker; non-identifiability is a verdict.
3. **Concretisation (T_E):** a concrete protocol a practitioner would write: use do-calculus or a randomised trial, report an average treatment effect with a confidence interval, validate on a held-out set.

**R_T is nonzero.** Four things the shorthand holds do not survive the trip back down:

- the prohibition on naming an estimator, which a concrete protocol almost always breaks (the framework returns a shape, Fig 9 :32);
- the requirement that the checker not reconstruct the claim, which a standard peer-review protocol does not enforce (Fig 10 :42);
- the per-slot residual, which an average treatment effect re-aggregates (Fig 1 :22);
- the union reading, which a single estimator silently treats as one condition (Fig 9 :55-57).

One thing survives cleanly: a model's own fit cannot settle causal fidelity, and the arrangement carries information the ingredients do not. That is the residue worth sending. R_T was not measured numerically because no ground reading exists; it is recorded qualitatively as the enumerated set of dropped constraints. Treating "the formula is the condensed version of the figures" as identity would set R_T to zero and commit the dead-reckoning error Fig 4 :30 names. The honest form is a lossy condensation with a measured gap, which is what `09_watson_answer.md` states.
