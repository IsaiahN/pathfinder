# Outreach draft: check the output first, then decide whether the frame is worth reading

**Use:** one-to-one, or as a cover letter attached to a submission through the public venues in `03_public_contact_points.csv`. **Not** for bulk send.

**Hard precondition:** the residual must be described in effect terms before any frame is named. If the first message names a theory, the recipient is being asked to join the frame before they have checked the output (Fig 10 :52).

---

**Subject:** One checkable claim about what makes a model causal, before any theory

> Hi [name],
>
> I have one specific output I would like you to check against what you already know. I am not asking you to read the framework first, and I am not asking you to agree with it. If the output does not survive, there is nothing else to look at.
>
> **The output (`general` shape, `specified` checks, not `grounded`).** A computational model's own predictive score is produced by the frame that is being graded. Fig 1 :27 puts it plainly: *"They are frame-internal. A frame cannot score itself with a quantity it also produces."* So accuracy, likelihood and compression can decide what to try next, but they cannot witness that the model has the causal structure.
>
> The shape that survives is this: causal structure is the **arrangement** between variables, not the list of variables. Fig 12 :94: *"The arrangement is the substance."* Two models can hold the same ingredients and differ only in which directed bond joins them; under an intervention they come apart. What settles which arrangement is right is contact with an anchor that does not update when the model changes. Fig 2 :29: *"An anchor is whatever settles the question and does not change its answer because of what any frame thinks."* In practice that contact is usually an intervention, an instrument or an outcome the model did not author. A test set drawn from the same observational pool is not that anchor; it is a substituted habitat.
>
> Three operational consequences: read the failure per variable and per link, not as one accuracy number; check whether the model's vocabulary can even express a directed bond and the refusal of a wrong direction; and check identifiability before collecting more data, reporting *"not identifiable from this graph and data"* as a verdict rather than silence.
>
> **What I am not claiming:** that any particular estimator is the answer. The framework returns a shape, not a name. I am not claiming do-calculus, randomised trials or instrumental variables are the unique route; those are bindings from another frame. I am not claiming this is a theory of everything or that it is the only true one. Nothing here is asserted as settled by experiment.
>
> **What would refute the output**, written down before sending this:
> 1. show that a model's own fit settles causal fidelity without any external anchor;
> 2. show that the directed arrangement is recoverable from observational ingredients alone;
> 3. show that two models with identical observational implications and different edge directions agree under every intervention;
> 4. show that a substituted test habitat transfers to the real causal ground with measured, not assumed, success.
>
> If any holds, I retract the shape claim. A one-line "this fails because..." is more useful to me than agreement. If you would rather not engage, no reply is needed.
>
> **If it survives, and only then:** here are the sources. The figures are the authority, not my summary of them.
> - Verbatim figures text with line numbers (local extract): `FRAMEWORK/reference/03-FIGURES_TEXT.md`
> - Canonical usage rule (local): `FRAMEWORK/reference/FIGURES_README_canonical.md`
> - The eight-step loop and mode rule (local): `FRAMEWORK/figures/THE_FORMULA.md`
>
> **If you want to use the machinery:** the formula is in the last link. Run it on a problem you know well and can check against a ground. Write down what would refute your result *before* you run it, and state the mode of what you get. A reported failure is more useful to me than a reported success.

## Stage order for any reply

1. **Stage 1:** the residual, the shape claim and the four refuters. Ask what the recipient would count as a failure. Record their verdict **before** showing any theory.
2. **Stage 2, opt-in only:** if they are curious, offer the three local source paths. The figures, not the summary, are the authority.
3. **Stage 3:** offer the formula as a method to reuse, with the instruction that each user states their own ground, mode and refuter.

Later agreement is never pooled as corroboration. Fig 2 :40: *"disqualifying for a verdict: agreement about the world is worthless when the agreeing frames share an evidence pool."*
