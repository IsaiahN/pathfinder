# Expert verification packet - the Tether on startup durability

**What this is:** a claim in a form an entrepreneurship researcher can check. Mode, derivation and refuters are stated before the conclusion, so a reader knows what to look for and what would settle it.

**Mode: `specified` - not `grounded`.** No firm-level measurement is claimed. This is a shape and a route, not a result.

**Status of this packet:** a simulated derivation checked for internal consistency against the figures, not a ground reading. It cannot settle the question it reports on.

## 1. The claim, in one paragraph

Durability is not a stock a startup owns. It is a property of venture-and-habitat. Two conditions must both hold and have distinct failure modes: a maintained channel to a paying ground (C1), and reach renewed by contact with a frame whose closure differs from the startup's own (C2). A case in two conditions is not a harder case of either, so one lever does not clear both. Success is a label applied after selection ran, not a property read off the survivor; a strength can flip sign when the residual changes; and isolating a startup in a sandbox substitutes a habitat rather than removing one. The framework does not name the winning move, returns a shape, and leaves that slot open until a source-discriminating instrument settles it.

## 2. Three checkable sub-claims

### C1 - framework-internal; checkable by reading Figure 11

- **Statement:** capability is agent-and-habitat. Holding ideas and resources equal does not hold the habitat equal, so an agent-only explanation of divergence is refused.
- **Source, verbatim:** Fig 11 :39 - "capability is a property of agent-and-habitat, never of the agent alone." Also Fig 11 :40 - "A system cannot be made more capable by improving the agent in isolation. An improvement that does not change contact changes nothing, however much it improves."
- **Refuter:** a venture whose durability is unchanged across a habitat substitution that removes the market's payment, with internal Γ held fixed. Or: a prospectively measured property, fixed before outcome, that predicts durability with a source-discriminating instrument (which would also break the post-selection-label part).

### C2 - frameworks-internal; checkable by reading Figures 6, 9 and 12

- **Statement:** the two conditions form a union with two failure modes; reach must be renewed from a differing closure; two ventures with the same starting atoms but different bonds are isomers, while two sharing only a pattern are a functional group.
- **Source, verbatim:** Fig 6 :23 - "No frame escapes its own closure. The bound moves only on contact with a frame whose closure differs." Fig 9 :57 - "What remains is smaller and it is also categorically different: a case in two conditions is not a harder case of either." Fig 12 :117 - "Same atoms, different bond, different substance. The difference is invisible in the formula and decisive in the behaviour." Fig 12 :124 - "the group says what kind; the qualifier says which member."
- **What to check:** is the two-condition split real, or is one condition a renaming of the other? Is the "same atoms, different bond" reading faithful to the two startups under discussion?
- **Refuter:** one intervention that restores both conditions with no categorically different residue; or reach renewed purely by internal composition with no new atom and no differing closure while payment continues.

### C3 - framework-internal; checkable by reading Figures 10 and 11

- **Statement:** quick collapse is channel failure; success is a post-selection label; a strength flips sign with the residual; a sandbox is habitat substitution.
- **Source, verbatim:** Fig 10 :24 - "the ground does not decay, the channel does." Fig 11 :36 - "Where nothing maintains anything, intelligence is a label applied afterwards to a lineage that met its ground. Nothing was intelligent; selection ran." Fig 11 :49 - "An actor's valence is relative to the residual, not a property it carries." Fig 11 :66 - "Isolation is not removal of the habitat. It is substitution of one habitat for another."
- **Refuter:** a failure fully explained by direct first-order contact with no higher-order actor; invariant valence of a named capability across a documented residual change; capability preserved across a substitution with all goal-required conditions reproduced.

## 3. What is explicitly not claimed

- Not a list of success factors, and not a scoring model for startups.
- Not "the founder is irrelevant." The founder is part of Γ and of the loop; the claim is only that Γ alone cannot carry durability.
- Not a derivation of effectuation, product-market fit, timing, or lean methods. Those are bindings from one business evidence pool, provenance owed, and they carry zero evidentiary weight until provenance and a ground test are shown.
- Not a claim that this framework is the only true account of startup success. Fig 8 :39: "So a search that finds nothing is never a proof of absence."
- Not `grounded`. Nothing here is asserted as settled by experiment.
- Not a claim that the specific winning move can be named. That slot is a declared `gap`.

## 5. What would settle it

A source-discriminating instrument, not further agreement. The six falsifiers are stated in section 4 of `07_the_tether_answer.md`. In short: a payment-removing habitat substitution, a prospective discriminator, a single-lever two-condition fix, an internal-only reach renewal, a first-order-only failure, or invariant valence across a residual change. Any one firing narrows or withdraws the shape.

## 6. What an expert can check in twenty minutes

1. Read Fig 11 :39-40 and :66, Fig 9 :57, and Fig 12 :117 (five short passages).
2. Check C1 against your own knowledge: is the divergence between two similar startups ever purely inside Γ? Name a case if so.
3. Check C2: are C1 and C2 genuinely two failure modes, or one renamed? A one-line answer is enough.
4. Name one instrument that would prospectively discriminate the sources of a high reading, or say why none is possible.
5. Send the refutation. A failure is more useful than an endorsement. Later agreement is never pooled as corroboration (Fig 2 :40).
