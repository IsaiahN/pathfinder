## What the wider literature bears on the question

1. **The failure literature refuses a single cause.** CB Insights reports that capital running out is the final cause in 70% of cases "but it's almost always the final cause of death, not the root problem," and names product-market fit, timing and unit economics as the deeper causes. That is the figure's union finding in business vocabulary: several conditions bind, and "a case in two conditions is not a harder case of either" (Fig 9 :57). The list is not a derivation; it is one evidence pool ranged against the residual after the shape was fixed.
2. **The means-driven literature corroborates reach renewal but also strains against the agent-only reading.** Sarasvathy's "Causation rests on a logic of prediction, effectuation on the logic of control" and the bird-in-hand means ("Who I am / What I know / Who I know") look like an agent-only account. Returned to the census: "Who I know" is a network tie, which is habitat contact (D2), and the crazy-quilt rule ("take the product to the nearest potential customer") is contact with a paying ground (C1). So it corroborates the shape only after re-mapping; it is not an independent witness for the verdict, and it carries zero evidentiary weight until provenance is shown.
3. **The lean-startup loop is the perceive-act-observe loop at the venture scale.** "Build-Measure-Learn" matches Fig 1's "perceive · plan · act · predict" (Fig 1 :9), but the source itself reports weak early validation and a critique that the method yields incremental outcomes. So it corroborates the mechanism at `specified` mode with a caveat, not as a `grounded` law of startup success. This is the one source that partly conflicts with a strong reading; the conflict was returned to the census and did not flip the verdict.
4. **Survivorship bias is the low-overlap witness for F2.** "Advice about commercial success distorts perceptions of it by ignoring all of the businesses and college dropouts that failed" is the business-pool version of Fig 11 :36, where a "label [is] applied afterwards to a lineage that met its ground." Different field, same mechanism shape, so it is the strongest independent corroboration here, not another restatement of the same pool.
5. **Ecology is the low-overlap witness for multi-scale habitat.** The adaptive cycle and the requirement to analyse "simultaneously at different scales" corroborate Fig 11 :44-54 (contact cascades outward) and Fig 4's lossy membrane. It is an analogue, not a firm-level measurement, and it is recorded as corroboration of mechanism only.

No source was discarded by prestige or familiarity. Each was read after the residual was described in effect terms.

## The two-stage offer

**(1)** validate the output against the ground, **(2)** only if curious, read the theory that produced it, **(3)** use the formula recursively on other problems. Stage 2 is opt-in by construction: once a recipient has absorbed the theory, their agreement stops being evidence, so the stage-1 verdict is recorded first and later agreement is never pooled as corroboration. Stage 3 sends a method, which is permitted (Fig 4 :22), but each user still needs a ground and a pre-written refuter for their own problem, because "a frame cannot check the direction of its own question."

**AI-policy caveat.** AOM, Springer and SSRN routes all require disclosure or restrict AI authorship. This whole folder is a simulated derivation. Any submission must disclose AI assistance and must carry a human author who takes responsibility for the claims; several venues bar LLM authorship outright. This is a practical constraint on distribution, not evidence about startups.

**Hard precondition:** stages 1 to 3 only work if the stage-1 output survives. `01_figure_grounded_audit.md` states the output and its refuters before any theory. Do not skip stage 1.
