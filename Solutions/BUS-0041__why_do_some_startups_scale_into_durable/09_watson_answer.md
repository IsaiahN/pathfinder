**Mode: `specified`, not `grounded`.** Figures are primary and decide. World sources are secondary, corroborate but never overrule, and a conflict returns the derivation to the domain census. This is a simulated derivation checked for internal consistency against the figures, not a ground reading.

## QUESTION

Why do some startups scale into durable businesses while others with similar ideas and resources fail?

Restated as an effect: why does R = |Γ(b,a) - o′|, the gap between what a venture predicts and what the market returns, stay small across years of growth in some startup-slots and grow until the venture dies in others, when the two began with similar Γ? R is indexed per startup and per time, never aggregated (Fig 1 :19 "The residual is indexed by object slot, not aggregated."). Anchor: the market, named as an anchor in Fig 2 :29. Option A, durability is a stock the venture owns, predicts the difference is inside the venture alone. Option B, durability is a maintained relation, predicts the difference is a property of venture-and-habitat and fails when contact or reach fails. Each leads to a different instrument and a different refuter.

## DOMAIN CENSUS

Arity: a four-place relation among venture Γ, action, habitat conditions and time; it carries an ordered bond and a comparison bond, not a symmetric join. Symmetry: asymmetric; the "successful" label is applied after selection runs (Fig 11 :36). Scale: the single venture loop, the population selected against the market, and the time channel across a venture's life; the membrane between scales is lossy (Fig 4).

## VERDICT (from the figures)

The figure-derived verdict is Option B in `specified` mode. Durability is agent-and-habitat, a two-condition union (a maintained paying channel plus reach renewed from a differing closure), with a valve that flips sign when the residual changes and success as a label applied after selection ran. The specific bindings offered by entrepreneurship research are business-pool names, provenance owed, and none moves the wall. Which move wins next is a declared `gap`, not a hidden answer. Verdicts are kept per domain and never averaged.

- **cost if wrong:** misdirected diagnosis. A venture failing C2 would be sold funnel instruments, or a venture failing C1 would be told to import more, and a single lever would be applied to a union that needs two. In outreach terms, an expert's twenty minutes would be spent refuting a shape that was never the right shape. The cost is real but bounded and largely reversible. Fig 2 :42: "Buy independence in proportion to the cost of being wrong."
- **undoable:** yes. No term is deleted if the claim fails; a refutation is added, and the reach falls because "something was added rather than because anything was deleted" (Fig 6 :42-43). The shape and its six falsifiers are recorded in a provenance trail so a later reader can locate the error (Fig 10 :62-64).
- **reversed by:** any of the six pre-registered falsifiers, in particular (1) sustained durability after a payment-removing habitat substitution with Γ fixed, (2) a prospective source-discriminating instrument that names the cause before outcome, (3) one intervention restoring both conditions with no categorically different residue, (4) reach renewed purely internally with no new atom while payment continues, (5) a failure fully explained by first-order contact, or (6) invariant capability valence across a documented residual change. A source with a genuinely different evidence pool showing that per-venture persistence is fully predicted by the founder's Γ alone would also reverse it. Until one of these fires, the verdict stands as `candidate`.
