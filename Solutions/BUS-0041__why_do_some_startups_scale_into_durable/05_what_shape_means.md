## 1. A shape is a constraint you can state before you can name it

The canonical statement in `FIGURES_README_canonical.md` is that the framework returns "what a missing thing would have to look like (a shape, not a name)," then stops where instruments can no longer distinguish and records the lineage. Fig 9 :32: "you get a kind of thing, never the answer itself." Fig 13 :134: "Unreachable is a verdict here, not a silence," and :135: "It cannot say which primitive is missing, because naming it would mean already holding it."

From *The Library of Babel*, read directly:

> "you can cut your search short if the set of books returned is small and descriptive enough that it provides a general shape of the answer that satisfies your question."

So for BUS-0041 a shape is: **the narrowed form of an answer about durability that can be stated before any particular founder trait, practice, or market condition is named.** It constrains. It does not fill.

- Shape: *durability holds only while a paying channel is maintained and reach is renewed from a differing closure; when the residual changes, a strength can flip sign; success is a label applied after selection.*
- Name, withheld: *this startup wins because of its founder, its team, its timing, its product-market fit, or its effectual method.*

The distinction matters because entrepreneurship research is almost entirely in the naming business. Sarasvathy names means-driven reasoning and affordable loss; CB Insights names product-market fit and timing; the lean-startup literature names the build-measure-learn loop. Those are bindings from the entrepreneurship frame onto atoms, not outputs of the loop.

## 2. How the shape was obtained

The essay gives the bootstrapping procedure, read directly:

> "If you don't know the right terms to search for, you will have to use the existing vocabulary you do know that is closest to the words that you wish to describe and grasp the shape of."
> "With each new term gained, you are able to narrow the space by definition and deepen your vocabulary."

Applied here: the closest available terms were "durable business," "product-market fit," "effectuation," "timing," "survivorship bias," "adaptive cycle" and "dynamic capabilities." None was used as the answer. Each was treated as a witness to be ranged against the residual; the description in effect terms (duration of a low-residual slot, indexed per venture) came first, and frames were selected only after that description was fixed (Fig 8 :53 "Residual first, frame second.").

The essay also names the bundle stop, read directly:

> "Perhaps your problem was a bundle or conflation of several problems masquerading as one? You will need to search all of those problems individually to find their answers and then combine them in a satisfactory way."

That is the canonical warrant for treating BUS-0041 as a union of two conditions (Fig 9 :55-57), not as one hard question. The stop condition: "You will know that you are done because the finish line will also be the starting place." In this solution the stop is reached when the two conditions, their instruments and their falsifiers are stated. Any further search that only renames the conditions is motion, not progress (Fig 7 :32 "one long room: motion, no progress").

## 3. What licenses a name

Only two things, both outside the shape:

1. **The ground settles it.** Formula step 5: "the ground settles it. Verified only by what pays. A gate is not the ground." The README: the framework "stops at the limit of what the available instruments can distinguish, and records the lineage so the stop is honest."
2. **A provenanced import from a frame whose closure differs** (Fig 6 :23, Fig 8 :50-52), with a source gate and a shadow test. Fig 8 :50: "But shared atoms are not the signature. Two frames can hold the same atom by convergence, reaching it separately, which is corroboration at maximum independence."

For BUS-0041 neither has happened. No prospective instrument here pays for "this specific trait caused the decade," and the business-pool names were adopted without a shadow test. Hence the `gap` at F10 of `01_figure_grounded_audit.md`.

## 4. The source error is the specific forbidden move here

Via the canonical mapping in the exemplar, *The Hard Problem is About Source* gives the mechanism: metamerism. An instrument reads green and "cannot comment on the source"; if several paths create the same behaviour, "behavior is a projection with lossy information," and "the answer is unreached until a better instrument exists." I did not open that essay directly this session, so this is recorded as a mapping, not a fresh reading.

The error it names is exactly what a startup explanation makes when it reads durable profit and names its source. A high return is a reading. It is consistent with a protected resource, a favourable habitat, a temporary information asymmetry, an accounting choice, or a monopoly granted by regulation. The reading does not determine the source. Naming the source from the reading is the error, and no amount of agreement among readers fixes it. Fig 6 :30: "outward: does another frame already hold it? The description travels. Fails by being sharpened into terms only we can read. Cast it in effect terms." The survivorship-bias source fetched this session corroborates the same mechanism at low overlap: "advice about commercial success distorts perceptions of it by ignoring all of the businesses and college dropouts that failed."

## 5. Prestige is not action, applied to entrepreneurship research

From *The Recursive Self-Improvement Trap*, read directly:

> "Prestige must never be allowed to validate an action directly."
> "Prestige can fund an action. It can give an action a budget to be tried. But it cannot settle an action. Only the ground can settle it. The Ground Maintainer must be blind to prestige."
> "If prestige could buy actions, the system would collapse into a monoculture. The highest-prestige agent would monopolize compute and repeat its past success, and the system would stop exploring."
> "You may all agree and be wrong."

The last two lines are an instance, in the author's own agent system, of Fig 7 :36 ("A chain of mirrors is one long room") and Fig 10 :24 ("the ground does not decay, the channel does"). Because it is framework-internal, it illustrates the rule; it is not evidence that startups behave this way.

For outreach this means: a list of famous founders or famous entrepreneurship professors who agree with the shape would be a monoculture, not a check. The ask gives the claim a budget to be tried by someone outside; it does not solicit endorsement.

## 6. Correction to my own section 3 (an overstatement, retained as evidence)

I initially wrote that naming any particular practice is forbidden by "shape, not a name." That is too broad and is withdrawn here. The canonical phrase governs a **missing** primitive, one not held in Γ. If a practice is already in a venture's library, naming it is a binding from the entrepreneurship frame, not the invention of a new primitive. What remains forbidden is claiming the binding moves the wall (Fig 6 :21, "MINT composes inside the closure and can never add an atom"). The binding still needs provenance and a ground test, but it is not a category error. Both the corrected and the withdrawn formulations are kept so the correction is visible rather than silent.

## 7. This derivation is a simulation, not a ground reading

From *The Recursive Self-Improvement Trap*, read directly:

> "the output is only as good as the validator. The agent, or the tool, or the frame, or the theory, cannot validate itself. An external validator, or ground, is always needed to tether the veracity of the output to reality. Anything else is drift."
> "LLMs can quickly simulate an approximate answer/output with the formula found in the architecture post. It will often be better than the generic outputs that you will usually get otherwise too ... However, the truly testable output would need to be derived from the real architecture or working the formula yourself."

So this `05` file, like `01`, is a simulated derivation checked for internal consistency against the figures. It is labelled `specified` wherever it makes a claim about startups. It is not a test of the question and cannot settle it. The point of publishing it is to make the shape and its refuters easy to check by someone whose ground contact is real and whose closure differs.

## 8. The shape, stated once more

For BUS-0041 the canonical shape is: **a two-condition conjunction with two distinct failure modes and a valve that changes sign when the residual changes, with success as a post-selection label.** It is not a list of success factors, not the name of a founder trait, and not a ranking of startups. The names in the entrepreneurship literature are bindings, provenance owed. The instruments that would convert the shape into a grounded answer are not held here, so the answer stops at `specified` and the stop is recorded.
