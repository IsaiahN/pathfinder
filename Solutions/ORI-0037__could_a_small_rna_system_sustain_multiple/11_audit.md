## Independent domain census

I redid the census from the residual line alone, before reading the primary's conclusions. The method was the WATSON order: R by arity, symmetry and scale; one line per domain with actors, currency and anchor; partition or union; order by dependency.

- **R, the residual.** Per molecule slot and per handoff, the gap between prediction (Γ(b,a)) and observation (o'). Arity: three-place (replicator, habitat, generation index), with the slot and the handoff indexed. Symmetry: the production bond is ordered and productive, so parent and offspring are not interchangeable; fidelity is a comparison. Scale: single-molecule loop, population in habitat, cross-generation channel. Fig 1 :19 "The residual is indexed by object slot, not aggregated." locks the unit of analysis.
- **Domain 1, replication channel.** Actors: template, complement, activated monomers, any catalyst. Currency: copied bases, fidelity q, residual per slot. Anchor: the actual offspring sequence. Admitted.
- **Domain 2, habitat and contact.** Actors: monomers, surfaces, cycles, parasites, conditions. Currency: contact, introduction versus subtraction. Anchor: the ground, natural chemistry and differential survival. Admitted.
- **Domain 3, reach and variation.** Actors: atoms and bonds in Γ, imports. Currency: atom count, size of reach. Anchor: the closure test. Admitted.
- **Domain 4, observation and verification.** Actors: instruments, serial-transfer experiments, measurers. Currency: readings, bracket fidelity, ground pressure. Anchor: the ground, never agreement. Admitted.
- **Rejected candidates.** Intrinsic vitality of a small RNA has no nameable anchor; the RNA world as a single object is already inside domains 2 and 3. Neither is a domain.

Question type: **union**, not partition. The channel condition, the habitat condition and the no-author condition overlap. Dependency order: enumerate contact, acquire terms for fidelity and strand separation, state the objective as keeping R small across n handoffs, design the transfer, name what worked and carry it.

- The **shape-not-a-number limit**. The shorthand says the threshold is a gap; converting that into a model estimate would breach Fig 3 :27 and Fig 9 :60.

One thing survives cleanly: a laboratory transfer series with a supplied replicase and a defined buffer is a substituted habitat, and that proves wiring rather than natural capability. That is the residue worth sending outward.
