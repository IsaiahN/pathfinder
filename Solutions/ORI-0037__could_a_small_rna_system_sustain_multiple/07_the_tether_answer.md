## 1. The residual it answers

The question bundles three things that the phrase "a small RNA system sustaining itself" tends to merge: a bounded replicating library, a habitat that supplies atoms and pressure, and a chain of handoffs across which information must survive. Fig 9 :55 "A bundled problem is usually a union of overlapping conditions, not a list of separate ones." Clearing the channel condition leaves the habitat condition binding. The answer term bound in `README.md` is `SUSTAIN(R, n, H)`: R stays inside the band the ground pays for, across n handoffs, in a habitat no maintainer authors.

## 2. The shape returned

1. **Capability is not in the molecule.** Fig 11 :39 "capability is a property of agent-and-habitat, never of the agent alone." Fig 11 :40 "A system cannot be made more capable by improving the agent in isolation. An improvement that does not change contact changes nothing, however much it improves." The answerable question is what contact keeps R small, not whether an isolated RNA is capable.

2. **Natural conditions is a habitat clause.** Fig 11 :56 "Unmaintained. No maintainer and no live frame in the seat. The habitat is pure contact, nothing selects, and what survives is what the ground kept." The clause removes the seat that could supply monomers, choose a temperature, or exclude a parasite. It does not change the chemistry. Contact cascades, so second-order effects such as parasite arrival can decide persistence.

3. **A laboratory transfer series is a substituted habitat.** Fig 11 :66 "Isolation is not removal of the habitat. It is substitution of one habitat for another." Fig 11 :70 "A test harness is a substituted habitat. Which is why a synthetic solve proves wiring and never capability." A supplied replicase, a defined buffer and a feedstock of activated monomers show that the wiring of copying, variation and selection works. They do not show that the system sustains itself where no seat supplies those things. Fig 11 :61 "The seat's power is asymmetric. Introduction is available; exclusion is not." You may add an enzyme; you cannot subtract the natural habitat's lack of one.

4. **Multiple generations is a two-factor channel problem.** Fig 10 :24 "the ground does not decay, the channel does." Fig 7 :37 "Progress per handoff has two factors. Zero either and the chain runs in place:" Fig 7 :38 "the bracket's fidelity across the handoff", Fig 7 :39 "the ground's pressure on the room". Chemistry does not get tired; the transmission channel does. Fidelity alone or selection alone is one lever applied to a union.

5. **Novelty needs a differing closure and a catalyst.** Fig 8 :32 "Closure is idempotent. Composing inside a closed set never leaves it, so no search, however long, adds an atom." A small Γ cannot manufacture new atoms by internal composition. Renewal arrives from a differing closure, and persistence requires a part that survives its own use (Fig 12 :119). Fig 7 :36 "A chain of mirrors is one long room. The next room must differ from the last, and match the residual it surfaced."

6. **The figures return a shape, not the number or the yes.** Fig 9 :32 "you get a kind of thing, never the answer itself." The numeric error threshold under natural conditions is a `gap`: Fig 3 :27 "A link with no instrument is not a link that works. It is a link nobody has looked at." The empirical yes/no is also a `gap`, handed to measurement. This is unreached at this budget, not shown unreachable in principle: Fig 8 :39 "So a search that finds nothing is never a proof of absence." Fig 13 :134 "Unreachable is a verdict here, not a silence," licenses saying so directly.

## 3. What this answer explicitly does not say

- Not that small RNA replication is impossible. No figure asserts absence, and the empirical claim stays open.
- Not that the RNA world hypothesis is wrong, or that any published experiment failed. The Salk 2024 result and Spiegelman-type serial transfers are real and are treated as wiring results.
- Not that a lab result is worthless. It proves wiring. It does not prove natural capability.
- Not that field consensus decides anything. Fig 9 :60 "Use filters for the budget and witnesses for the verdict. Never let a filter hand you a verdict."
- Not a numeric error threshold. Robertson and Joyce (2012) and Szostak (2012) give conflicting model estimates; no figure arbitrates them.
- Not `grounded`. Nothing here was settled by measurement in an unmaintained habitat.
- Not an import that moves the wall. No new primitive is claimed, and the shape stays inside the closure already held.

## 4. Falsifiers, written before the conclusion

The shape claim consisting of items 1 to 5 is withdrawn or narrowed if any of the following is measured:

1. Sustained replication invariant across a habitat substitution that removes feedstock, surfaces and cycling, with no introduced compensating actor. (Breaks items 1 and 3.)
2. A lab demonstration transfers to an unmaintained natural habitat with no substitution cost, no introduced enzyme and no scheduled activation, under the same per-slot residual accounting. (Breaks item 3.)
3. Multi-generation persistence measured while both bracket fidelity and ground pressure are at or near zero and non-declining. (Breaks item 4.)
4. Open-ended adaptation across many handoffs in a closed Γ with no import and no part surviving its own use. (Breaks item 5.)

Finding a numeric threshold is not a falsifier of the shape; it fills an empty slot. A falsifier written before the run is a design instrument. These four were fixed in `01` section 4 before the conclusion was drawn and are repeated here unchanged.

## 5. What would settle it: the ground

What is owed is an instrument, not a further reading of the figures. The measurement would be a serial-transfer protocol in an unmaintained habitat: no introduced catalyst, no scheduled activated monomers, no artificial compartment, no excluded competitor, and the same per-slot residual accounting used here. It must report bracket fidelity and ground pressure per handoff, and it must record what was brought with the setup (Fig 11 :67, the inherited actors you did not choose). A result that lands while the four falsifiers remain unfired would move the empirical question from `gap` toward `grounded`. Fig 4 :30 "So take the round trip and measure the gap. Without it you are navigating by dead reckoning: confident about a position nobody checked." The round-trip gap R_T is elaborated in `11_audit.md`.

World sources currently report no demonstration of robust, continuous RNA self-replication from a realistic feedstock, and the Salk 2024 result is described as a step toward, not an arrival at, autonomous RNA life in the laboratory. That is a report about the state of evidence. It is not a figure verdict and is not promoted to one.
