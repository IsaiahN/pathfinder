## 1. The answer term was bound before the run

Formula step Direction requires the sought-for shape to be written down and bound before the run so it can accrue a residual. The term from `README.md` is:

> `SUSTAIN(R, n, H)` = the residual R for a replicating RNA system stays inside the band the ground pays for, across n handoffs, in habitat H where no maintainer authors H.

`SUSTAIN` is the ordinary term that carries the preference. Fig 5 :62 says a frame searching for an answer of a particular shape otherwise "holds that shape as a PREFERENCE rather than as a term. It is not bound, it predicts nothing, and it accrues no residual, so nothing here can see it." Fig 5 :65 gives the remedy: "make the sought-for shape an ORDINARY TERM. Write down what an answer would look like, bind it, and let it accrue a residual like anything else." Binding `SUSTAIN` is that remedy. It is why the question is checkable rather than a preference disguised as a result.

Two options were open, and both were recorded before the derivation:

- Option A: assert that `SUSTAIN` holds for small RNA under natural conditions.
- Option B: return the conditions `SUSTAIN` must satisfy, refuse the assertion that they are met, and hand the measurement to the ground.

This package took Option B. Each option leads to a different next instrument, so the choice is a design decision with consequences, not a stylistic preference.

## 2. A shape is a constraint, not a filler

The canonical README states the limit: the framework reports "what a missing thing would have to look like (a shape, not a name)." Fig 9 :32: "you get a kind of thing, never the answer itself." Fig 13 :132-133: "A system with a closure can answer that before it searches, and what it returns is a shape rather than a name."

A shape here is therefore the narrowed form of an answer that can be stated before it can be settled. It constrains; it does not fill. `SUSTAIN(R, n, H)` is a shape. A numerical q_crit for a given RNA length in an unmaintained habitat would be a name, and no figure supplies one.

Fig 13 :134: "Unreachable is a verdict here, not a silence," licenses reporting the absence of the number as a result rather than as a gap in the prose. Fig 8 :39: "So a search that finds nothing is never a proof of absence," keeps that report from being read as a claim that the empirical answer is no.

## 3. How the shape is obtained for this question

The order is describe, then census, then frame, then conclude.

- **Describe the residual first.** Fig 9 :27-28: "describe it before you go looking, or any frame you pick will seem to fit." The residual was described in `01` section 0 in effect terms (per slot, per handoff, ordered productive bond, comparison bond) before any figure line was selected.
- **Cast in effect terms, not borrowed cause words.** Fig 6 :30: "The description travels. Fails by being sharpened into terms only we can read. Cast it in effect terms." Hence "sustain" was restated as a channel staying inside the band the ground pays for, not as a property of a molecule.
- **Enumerate the habitat rather than compose it.** Fig 11 :43: "The habitat is enumerated, never composed." Fig 11 :44: "List everything in contact with the residual, then what is in contact with those, and outward until the cascade stops mattering." This is why the natural-conditions clause was treated as a habitat clause and why contact cascades (parasites, surfaces, feedstock) belong in the answer.
- **Keep the union unbundled.** Fig 9 :55: "A bundled problem is usually a union of overlapping conditions, not a list of separate ones." Fig 9 :57: "What remains is smaller and it is also categorically different: a case in two conditions is not a harder case of either." Keeping the channel condition separate from the habitat condition is what prevents a single lever from being mistaken for a solution.

## 4. What would license a name instead of a shape

Only two things, both outside the shape itself:

1. **The ground settles it.** Fig 2 :29: "An anchor is whatever settles the question and does not change its answer because of what any frame thinks." For this question the anchor is measurement in an unmaintained habitat, not field consensus and not a model estimate.
2. **A provenanced import from a frame whose closure differs.** Fig 6 :23: "No frame escapes its own closure. The bound moves only on contact with a frame whose closure differs." An import adds a primitive under a source gate and a shadow test. No import was made here.

A number produced by a model is not the second thing and is not the first. Fig 3 :27: "A link with no instrument is not a link that works. It is a link nobody has looked at." The numeric threshold is a specification of what to measure. Declaring it settled would be a filter handing us a verdict, against Fig 9 :60: "Use filters for the budget and witnesses for the verdict. Never let a filter hand you a verdict."

## 5. The shape actually returned

The figures return five conditions and two gaps. They never return the yes/no.

| item | shape returned | status |
|---|---|---|
| Capability location | capability is a property of replicator and habitat, never of the molecule alone (Fig 11 :39) | `general`, triangulated |
| Natural conditions | no maintainer, no live frame, pure contact; what survives is what the ground kept (Fig 11 :56) | `general` rule, `specified` application |
| Lab to nature | a test harness is a substituted habitat; a synthetic solve proves wiring and never capability (Fig 11 :70) | `general` rule, `specified` application |
| Multiple generations | two factors per handoff, bracket fidelity and ground pressure; zero either and the chain runs in place (Fig 7 :37-39) | `general` rule, `specified` application |
| Novelty | closure is idempotent and composition adds no atom, so renewal needs a differing closure and a catalyst that survives its own use (Fig 8 :32; Fig 12 :119) | `general`, triangulated |
| Numeric threshold | no figure line yields q_crit under natural conditions | `gap`, unreached |
| Empirical yes/no | not returned by any figure | `gap`, handed to the ground |

The shape is the conjunction of the first five rows. A candidate answer that violates any one of them has not answered `SUSTAIN(R, n, H)` as bound; it has answered a different question.
