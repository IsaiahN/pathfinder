## What in the wider literature bears on the question

1. **The gap is between cycles, not single reactions.** Szostak's eight problems are problems of *repeated* replication: strand separation, reactivation, copying rate and reannealing all fail on the second and later cycles even when a single copying step works. Robertson and Joyce make the same point from the theoretical side: self-replication requires fidelity sufficient to produce viable progeny at a rate exceeding parent decomposition. This supports `01` F4 (channel maintenance) and is only world corroboration; the figure authority for the distinction is Fig 10 :24 ("the ground does not decay, the channel does").
2. **The lab result is a substituted habitat, exactly as the figures say.** The Salk experiments use a laboratory-evolved polymerase ribozyme, a defined hammerhead substrate and controlled test-tube conditions. The release says the work brings researchers "one step closer" to autonomous RNA life and describes the goal as an RNA polymerase that can replicate itself "within the next decade." Spiegelman's monster was produced with a supplied Qβ replicase and feedstock. These are the empirical instances of Fig 11 :70 ("A test harness is a substituted habitat. Which is why a synthetic solve proves wiring and never capability"), not counterexamples to it.
3. **Parasites and compartmentalization are habitat conditions, not molecular details.** Furubayashi and Ichihashi report that their translation-coupled system declined below detection after 17 rounds in bulk solution, survived over 100-fold improvement when compartmentalized, then reached stasis at about 600 generations, and resumed diversification only when coevolving parasites made the environment dynamic. That is the world-level version of Fig 11 :43-52 (enumerate the habitat, contact cascades) and Fig 7 :36 ("The next room must differ from the last"). It also bears on question 36 in the same section (parasite sequences), but it is not used here as a figure line.
4. **A genuine conflict, not a pooled agreement.** Summers and Litwin dispute the sharp error threshold under realistic lethal-mutation spectra. We did not smooth this into the RNA story. It was returned to the domain census and re-mapped to the model-assumptions domain (D3), where it supports `01` F6: the threshold is instrument-and-assumption dependent, so no figure or single model supplies the number. Fig 2 :31-32: "Independence is how little they draw on the same evidence."
