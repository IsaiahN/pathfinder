**Hard precondition:** do not send until F1, F2 and F4 in `01_figure_grounded_audit.md` survive your own check. The output must survive validation or the two-stage offer collapses on contact.

**Subject:** Check one output about measurement and objectives before you decide whether the framework is worth reading

> Hi [name],
>
> I am sending one specific output to check against what you already know. I am not asking you to read the theory first, and I am not asking you to agree with it. If the output does not survive, there is nothing else to look at.
>
> **The output (`specified`, not `grounded`):** an economic model cannot compute a residual until three things are fixed: which slot is tracked, what observation counts, and which direction of change counts as progress. The first two are measurement choices. The third is the objective, and in the figure vocabulary an objective is a comparison bond, not a quantity. That matters because a comparison needs a quantity and a threshold that someone selected. The framework's own sentence is: *"a convention that decides an outcome has stopped being one"* (Fig 10 :47). So the claim is not that economics is worthless or arbitrary. It is that **neutral execution of a declared measure is possible, while neutral selection of the objective is not**, and the two must be reported separately.
>
> **What I am not claiming:** that any particular welfare function is the right one, that all social science is value-impregnated, or that a panel of agreeable economists could settle the matter. Which values ought to be adopted is a declared `gap` here, not a hidden answer. Fig 9 :32 puts the limit as *"you get a kind of thing, never the answer itself."*
>
> **What would refute the shape claim:**
> 1. a measure that settles a welfare policy choice while ranging against a legitimate ground and containing no constant that decides an outcome;
> 2. a frame-internal procedure that checks the direction of its own question without first binding that direction as an ordinary term;
> 3. a figure line that governs a particular welfare weight, discount rate, or distributional index;
> 4. an aggregate that preserves every per-slot residual without a further declaration of whose slots are weighted how.
>
> I wrote these down before sending this. If any one holds, the shape is wrong and I will retract it. A one-line "this fails because..." is more useful to me than agreement. If you would rather not engage, no reply is needed.
>
> **If it survives, and only then:** the sources are below. The figures are the authority, not my summary of them.
> - Figures and formula, verbatim with line numbers (local framework copy): `FRAMEWORK/reference/03-FIGURES_TEXT.md`
> - Canonical usage rule: `FRAMEWORK/reference/FIGURES_README_canonical.md`
> - Public figures repository (branch `general-agent`): https://github.com/IsaiahN/tether/tree/general-agent/figures
>
> **If you want to use the machinery:** the formula is in the figures link. Run it on a problem you know well and can check against a ground. Write down what would refute your result *before* you run it, and label the mode of what you get. The framework's own warning is that a frame cannot check the direction of its own question, so a pre-registered refuter is a design instrument, not a courtesy.

## Why this version is ordered this way

| the move | figure-grounded reason |
|---|---|
| Output first, theory second | Fig 10 :52 - *"Bring a characterised residual, never the source."* Frames range against the ground separately. |
| Do not ask for agreement | Fig 2 :35 - *"Alignment is triangulation, not negotiation. A mirror cannot correct you."* |
| Pre-registered refuter | Seat Map, failure genera: a falsifier written before the run is a design instrument, not only an honesty device. |
| Mode stated on every claim | `THE_FORMULA.md` mode rule: `general`, `specified`, `grounded`. State which. |
| The two-stage order | Stage 1 is Fig 10 :52 plus Fig 2 :45, *"Frames range against the ground. They do not negotiate with it."* Stage 2 is opt-in because once a recipient holds the interiors, their agreement stops being evidence (Fig 2 :40). |
| Neutrality narrowed, not denied wholesale | Fig 2 :36, *"An anchor is legitimate when the question is constitutively about it."* The claim about prediction survives; the claim about welfare does not. |
| No request for agreement or prestige | *The Recursive Self-Improvement Trap*: *"Prestige can fund an action. It can give an action a budget to be tried. But it cannot settle an action. Only the ground can settle it."* |
| The gap is named | Fig 8 :39, *"So a search that finds nothing is never a proof of absence."* |
| Offer the formula as a method to reuse | Fig 4 :22, *"Only methods cross up. Only head starts come down."* |
| Do not pool later agreement as corroboration | Fig 2 :40, *"disqualifying for a verdict: agreement about the world is worthless when the agreeing frames share an evidence pool."* |

**Hard precondition:** stages 1 and 2 only work if the stage-1 output survives. As drafted in the literature, the strong version of "economics is value-neutral" does not (F4), and the strong version of "all economics is value-impregnated" does not either (F7 and the Mongin source conflict). Send the narrowed shape, with its refuter, or send nothing.
