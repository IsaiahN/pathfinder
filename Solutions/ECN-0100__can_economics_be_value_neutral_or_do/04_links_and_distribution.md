## What the wider literature bears on the question

1. **Two defensible positions, not one.** Friedman defends neutrality for prediction; Mongin rebuts both strong neutrality and strong non-neutrality. Together they support the narrowed verdict: neutrality survives in execution, not in selection of the objective. This is a **conflict handled by re-census**, not a flip of the figure verdict (see `01` section 7).
2. **Measures change what they measure.** Goodhart (economics) and Campbell (social psychology) independently describe the same phenomenon in low-overlap fields. Both corroborate Fig 10 :47, *"a convention that decides an outcome has stopped being one"*, at the ground. They are **one mechanism witnessed twice**, not two independent confirmations of the whole figure verdict, because both are about the use of measures, not about the choice of objective.
3. **Engineering has a method for this.** Value Sensitive Design is a low-overlap witness for the remedy, not for the verdict: names value trade-offs explicitly, identify direct and indirect stakeholders, and build the value conflict into the design record. That is the practical analogue of binding the objective as an ordinary term.
4. **Sen and Schroeder differ in scope.** Sen argues the object of assessment should be freedoms rather than GNP, which is a binding from a philosophical frame. Schroeder argues measures are already structured to reflect ethical values and that an intermediate position is correct. Neither is a figure line; both are corroboration with declared overlap.
5. **Aggregation is not a theorem of value neutrality.** Arrow's theorem, as reported by SEP, shows that the aggregation conditions themselves are value judgments. This corroborates F2 at F2's level only. It does not by itself say which welfare function to choose, which is why F7 remains a `gap`.

## Distribution: offer, do not push

- **Fig 10 :52** - *"Bring a characterised residual, never the source."* Send the shape and its refuter first. Sending the whole framework before a verdict is recorded turns a potential outside frame into a participant (Fig 10 :42, *"A verifier that can reconstruct the claim cannot verify it."*).
- **Fig 9 :27-28** - *"describe it before you go looking, or any frame you pick will seem to fit."* Ask what the recipient would expect to see if the shape were true before giving them any figure text.
- **Fig 2 :40** - *"disqualifying for a verdict: agreement about the world is worthless when the agreeing frames share an evidence pool."* Do not select venues by likely agreement, and do not pool later agreement as corroboration.
- **Fig 4 :22** - *"Only methods cross up. Only head starts come down."* Stage 3 offers the formula as a method, never as a conclusion.
- **Fig 2 :42** - *"Buy independence in proportion to the cost of being wrong."* One economist and one philosopher or affected-community venue is worth more than a long list of friendly economists.

**Recommended order in a reply:** (1) the shape claim and its four refuters; (2) ask what the recipient would count as a failure; (3) record their verdict before showing anything theoretical; (4) only then the three links, with the note that the figures, not this summary, are the authority.

## The two-stage offer

1. **Stage 1:** the narrowed claim, the objective-as-comparison finding, and the pre-registered refuter. No framework, no links, no request for agreement.
2. **Stage 2, opt-in only:** after a verdict on stage 1 is recorded, offer the figures, the formula and the canonical usage rule. Their later agreement is never counted as corroboration.
3. **Stage 3:** offer the formula as a reusable method, with the instruction that each user writes their own refuter and states their own mode.

## Practical caveat

Several venues listed in `03_public_contact_points.csv` have journal article processing charges or require membership. That is a contact cost, not evidence about the claim. Some sites block automated page fetching; where that happened it is recorded in the CSV notes and the venue was verified through its publisher or sponsoring society instead. No venue requires a personal email, and no message in this pipeline is bulk-sent by this agent.
