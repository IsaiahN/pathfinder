## 1. A shape is a constraint, not a filler

The figures README states the limit: the framework returns *"what a missing thing would have to look like (a shape, not a name)."* Fig 9 :32: *"you get a kind of thing, never the answer itself."* Fig 13 :134: *"Unreachable is a verdict here, not a silence,"* and the framework *"cannot say which primitive is missing, because naming it would mean already holding it."*

From *The Library of Babel*: *"You can cut your search short if the set of books returned is small and descriptive enough that it provides a general shape of the answer that satisfies your question."*

For this question a **shape** is the statement of which links carry a value choice and which can be neutral after declaration. It is not a welfare function and not a list of values. The shape says: the objective is a comparison, a comparison needs a threshold, a threshold that decides an outcome is a verdict, and which threshold ought to be chosen is not reachable from the figures. That is a filled shape with an open slot, not an empty answer.

## 2. How a shape is obtained

- *Library of Babel*: use the vocabulary you already have that is closest to the thing, look for recurring new terms that overlap or correlate with it, recurse. *"With each new term gained, you are able to narrow the space by definition and deepen your vocabulary."*
- Fig 6 :30: *"The description travels. Fails by being sharpened into terms only we can read. Cast it in effect terms."* Fig 9 :30-31: *"your words are borrowed too, so describe the effect rather than the cause."* This is why the residual in `01` is stated as "what fails to register about welfare" rather than as "utility" or "happiness", which are borrowed terms with disputed meanings.
- *Decoding Tacit Knowledge*: *"When contact isn't possible, the best you can send is a plain description of what you'd expect to see, because anyone can check that against the world."* This is the reason `02_outreach_email.md` leads with refuters instead of theory.
- *Library of Babel* on stopping: *"You will know that you are done because the finish line will also be the starting place, as there is nowhere else to search and nothing else that can describe the answer better."*

## 3. The essay that most directly governs this question

*They're Scanning Books When They Should Be Studying Nature* names the exact distinction this solution defends:

> *"Declaring the primitives does not make them neutral. It makes the choice visible, which is worth a great deal, because a visible choice can be revised and an emergent one cannot. But visibility is not neutrality, and revision is still bounded by what can be conceived to revise toward."*

And, in the same section:

> *"A minimum viable set of six terms is a human's careful guess at reality's compression axes; another sensorium's minimum set need not be those six."*

Earlier in the essay: *"there is no neutral representation to aim at"*, and at the end: *"The real import is not a new term in the existing space. It is a new dimension of the space."*

This is decisive for the mode of the answer. Declaring the objective makes the value choice visible and revisable, which is the most the method can deliver. It does **not** make it neutral. So Option A fails in its strong form, while the practical remedy survives. This is a `general`-mode structural claim from the essay, corroborating Fig 10 :47 (*"a convention that decides an outcome has stopped being one"*) and Fig 10 :49 (*"Declaring a meaning does not ground it."*). The two sources share an author, so this is internal consistency, not two witnesses.

## 4. What licenses a name

Only two things, both outside the shape itself:

1. **The ground settles it.** Formula step 5: *"the ground settles it. Verified only by what pays. A gate is not the ground."* The README: the framework *"stops at the limit of what the available instruments can distinguish, and records the lineage so the stop is honest."*
2. **A provenanced import from a frame whose closure differs** (Fig 6 :23, Fig 8 :50-52), with a source gate and a shadow test. *How Intelligence Actually Works* calls the pattern *"indiscernibility is not identity"* and insists the lineage must be kept even when a copy is perfect.

For this question, naming a particular welfare function or discount rate is a binding from a human frame, provenance owed. It is not a derivation, and claiming it is one is the metamerism error described next.

## 5. Source, not reading

*The Hard Problem is About Source* gives the mechanism. A color instrument reads green and *"cannot comment on the source"*: one green light, or yellow plus blue, or RGB, or CMYK, or a scheme not yet discovered. *"If several paths create the same behavior, then behavior is a projection with lossy information."* And: *"The answer is unreached until a better instrument exists."* It was the spectrometer, not an argument, that resolved it.

Applied here: a single national aggregate reads "welfare went up." It cannot say which people gained, which lost, or which objective produced the reading. Naming the source from the reading is the error. What is owed is a source-discriminating instrument (distribution by slot, sensitivity to thresholds), not a new assertion. *How Intelligence Actually Works* makes the same point about artifacts: *"often, very little about a composed term tells you, from looking at it alone, which route produced it."*

## 6. Two consequences for outreach

**Prestige is not action.** From *The Recursive Self-Improvement Trap*: *"Prestige must never be allowed to validate an action directly... Someone let prestige stand in for proof... Prestige can fund an action. It can give an action a budget to be tried. But it cannot settle an action. Only the ground can settle it."* A campaign gathering famous economists to agree with the shape is the named collapse. *How a Society of Agents Would Work* adds: *"The group’s agreement is never the verdict."*

**My own audit is a simulation, not a ground reading.** Same essay: *"LLMs can quickly simulate an approximate answer/output... However, the truly testable output would need to be derived from the real architecture or working the formula yourself."* *Arrival of the Fittest* says the outputs produced by prompting with the formula *"must be seen as simulacra"* and warns that results *"could never be verified if the solutions presented were not contrived to fit the formula."* So `01_figure_grounded_audit.md` is a simulated derivation, checked for internal consistency against the figures. It is **not** a test of the claim and must be labelled as such. It cannot settle anything it reports on.

## 7. Honest standing of this "shape" reading

It does not independently confirm that the framework is right about economics.
