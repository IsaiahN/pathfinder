## 1. A shape is a constraint, not a filler

The canonical README states the limit: the framework returns *"what a missing thing would have to look like (a shape, not a name)"*. Fig 9 :32: *"you get a kind of thing, never the answer itself"*. Fig 13 :134: *"Unreachable is a verdict here, not a silence,"* and the following line: *"It cannot say which primitive is missing, because naming it would mean already holding it."*

From *The Library of Babel*, fetched this session: *"you can cut your search short if the set of books returned is small and descriptive enough that it provides a general shape of the answer that satisfies your question."*

So a **shape** is the narrowed form of an answer that can be stated before it can be named. It constrains; it does not fill. For BUS-0016:

- A **name** would be: "standardize 80 percent of processes" or "centralize all quality checks" or "adopt the X production system everywhere".
- A **shape** is: "an element is standardized if agreement about it is the product; it is localized if the local ground settles its correctness". That constrains any answer without naming which elements fall where.

The shape here is a **split rule with an anchor test**. It is not a percentage, not a process list, and not a diagram of an organization chart.

## 2. How a shape is obtained

- *Library of Babel*, fetched: use the vocabulary already closest to the thing, look for recurring new terms that overlap or correlate with it, and recurse. *"With each new term gained, you narrow the space by definition and deepen your vocabulary."*
- Fig 6 :30: *"The description travels. Fails by being sharpened into terms only we can read. Cast it in effect terms."* Fig 9 :30-31: *"your words are borrowed too, so describe the effect"* / *"rather than the cause"*.
- *Decoding Tacit Knowledge* (exemplar record): *"When contact isn't possible, the best you can send is a plain description of what you'd expect to see, because anyone can check that against the world."* This is exactly why the stage-1 offer asks what a failure would look like before showing any theory.
- *Library of Babel* on stopping, fetched: *"You will know that you are done because the finish line will also be the starting place, as there is nowhere else to search and nothing else that can describe the answer better."* And the paradox: *"by the time you learn the EXACT search terms to find the CORRECT book, you won't need to read it anymore, because you have already composed the answer through the process of locating it."*

Applied here: the effect terms are "agreement is the product" and "the local ground decides". Those were not borrowed from operations management; they were read off Fig 2 :39-43. The business vocabulary (required versus optional practices, template core versus augmentation, interface versus module) arrived later as corroboration, exactly in the order Fig 9 :53 requires: *"Residual first, frame second."*

## 3. What licenses a name

Only two things, both outside the shape itself:

1. **The ground settles it.** Formula step 5: *"the ground settles it. Verified only by what pays. A gate is not the ground."* The README: the framework *"stops at the limit of what the available instruments can distinguish, and records the lineage so the stop is honest."* For this question the instrument is a per-slot measurement: defect rate, customer outcome, safety finding, survival. It would name which elements belong in which column.
2. **A provenanced import from a frame whose closure differs** (Fig 6 :23, Fig 8 :50-52), with a source gate and shadow test. A practice imported from another site or another firm can name an element, but only if the record shows where it came from and which gap it explains.

*They're Scanning Books* (exemplar record) sharpens this: *"The real import is not a new term in the existing space. It is a new dimension of the space."* And: *"Declaring the primitives does not make them neutral. It makes the choice visible."* A list of "standard processes" is a visible choice, not a discovery of nature's list. Turning it into a universal requirement makes the choice invisible.

## 4. The specific forbidden error for this question

*The Hard Problem is About Source* (exemplar record) gives the mechanism. Metamerism: an instrument reads green and *"cannot comment on the source"*; one green light or yellow plus blue or RGB can give the same reading. *"If several paths create the same behavior, then behavior is a projection with lossy information."* It was the spectrometer, not an argument, that resolved it.

Here the corresponding error is reading a high compliance score (the artifact) and naming its source as "good process design" or "good local adaptation". Several different arrangements of bonds can produce the same compliance reading. Fig 12 :94: *"The arrangement is the substance."* The reading does not determine the arrangement. What is owed is an instrument that discriminates sources, not a name. Fig 2 :41 gives the sharper version: a shared meaning creates agreement as its product, so the agreement reading is about the convention, not about the world. Mistaking a convention reading for a verdict is the metamerism error in this question.

## 5. Q: Does the framework name which processes to standardize?

**Answered, no further question needed.** Nothing in the framework names them. The framework returns a shape: standardize the layer where agreement is the product, localize the layer the ground settles. A particular process element is licensed only by a local ground measurement settling it, or by a provenanced import with a shadow test. Until one of those, the assignment is the searcher's own contribution and must be recorded as such.

This is a `gap` in the framework's vocabulary, not a defect in the derivation. Fig 8 :39: *"So a search that finds nothing is never a proof of absence."* Fig 13 :134: *"Unreachable is a verdict here, not a silence."*

## 6. Prestige is not action, and agreement is not correction

From *The Recursive Self-Improvement Trap*, fetched this session: *"Prestige must never be allowed to validate an action directly."* And: *"Prestige can fund an action. It can give an action a budget to be tried. But it cannot settle an action. Only the ground can settle it. The Ground Maintainer must be blind to prestige."* Also: *"You may all agree and be wrong. You may set your clock to someone else's, who was setting your clock to yours, and be off by many deviations over time. You need ground truth to define where you stand."*

A famous operations author, a widely adopted production system, or a long list of agreeing teams gives this claim a budget to be tried. It does not settle it. Standardization itself is a prestige-shaped object in organizations: the standard has standing because it was approved, and approval is not a ground reading. Fig 10 :47: *"a convention that decides an outcome has stopped being one"*.

## 7. My own audit is a simulation, not a ground reading

Same essay, fetched: *"the output is only as good as the validator. The agent, or the tool, or the frame, or the theory, cannot validate itself."* And: *"LLMs can quickly simulate an approximate answer/output with the formula found in the architecture post... However, the truly testable output would need to be derived from the real architecture or working the formula yourself."*

So `01_figure_grounded_audit.md` is a simulated derivation checked for internal consistency against the figures. It is **not** a test of the business claim and must be labelled as such. It cannot settle anything it reports on. The mode everywhere is `specified`, with the structural parts marked `general`.
