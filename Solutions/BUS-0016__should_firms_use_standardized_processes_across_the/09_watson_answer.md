## QUESTION

Decide, in effect terms, what the Tether says about the choice between organization-wide standardized processes and local adaptation. Option A: standardize everything across the firm. Option B: let every team adapt processes locally. What each leads to: A makes agreement efficient where a shared meaning is the product but disqualifying where a local ground must settle correctness, and it sends recordings instead of methods across the membrane; B keeps contact with the local ground but can drop the convention that made coordination possible and can mistake a re-bonding for noise. The residual is per team and per slot, never an organization-wide average.

## DOMAIN CENSUS

R by arity, symmetry and scale, then one line per domain with actors / currency / anchor, partition or union, and order by dependency.

- **R (the residual).** When a process settled in one slot is carried to another, what may be replayed and what must be re-settled against the new slot's ground? Arity: three-place, template, local habitat, outcome, indexed by slot and ordered by time. Symmetry: not symmetric; "standard" asserts an identity bond across slots and is a threshold comparison, not a join. Scale: one team, the population of teams with a shared library, and the time channel across revisions. Fig 9 names this a union compound: a case in two conditions is not a harder case of either.
- **Domain 1, the single team loop.** Actors: team members, local routines, the team's Γ. Currency: actions and residual reduction at that slot. Anchor: the local ground return, defect count, customer outcome, safety finding or regulator decision. Nameable, admitted.
- **Domain 2, the replication membrane.** Actors: central process owners, templates, local adapters, the process document. Currency: methods, head starts or recordings crossing between scales. Anchor: the round-trip gap R_T (Fig 4 :30) and whether the ground still pays after the crossing. Nameable, admitted.
- **Domain 3, the population of teams.** Actors: teams, sites, customers, suppliers, regulators, conditions. Currency: network-scale benefits of a shared template versus fit to the local ground. Anchor: the market as ground (Fig 2 :29). Nameable, admitted.
- **Domain 4, the convention layer.** Actors: shared vocabulary, interfaces, metric definitions, safety rules, handoff protocols. Currency: coordination and agreement. Anchor: agreement itself is the product here (Fig 2 :41). Nameable with a special rule, admitted.
- **Excluded or absorbed.** "One best way" as a free-floating property has no anchor independent of each slot's ground (Fig 11 :51), so it is not a domain. "Local knowledge" as a property of the team alone shares D1's anchor and is capability of team-and-habitat (Fig 11 :39), so it is absorbed into D1 and D3.
- **Union, not partition.** The convention layer and the verdict layer overlap in the same document. Unbundle before choosing a lever (Fig 9 :54-57).
- **Order by dependency.** Describe the residual before naming a frame (Fig 9 :27-28); settle the template before composing over it (Fig 12 :104); perception, vocabulary, objective, planning, learn and carry (Fig 3 :20); refuse before mint (Formula step 2).

## VERDICT (from the figures)

The question as posed is a union, so a global either/or is a filter masquerading as a verdict. **Standardize the convention and method layer, because agreement about a shared meaning is the product there; localize the layer whose correctness is settled by the local ground, because uniformity there is disqualifying. Carry methods and head starts, never recordings; measure the round-trip gap; treat local variants as isomers until a bond difference is ruled out; keep residuals per slot.** Capability is team-and-habitat, so a copied process document is a substituted habitat with invisible losses. Which exact elements belong in which column is a `gap`: the instrument that would name them is a local measurement, not an argument. Mode `specified`, ungrounded, candidate.

- **cost if wrong:** bounded and locatable. The packet states mode, refuters and provenance before the conclusion, so an error is findable. The real cost is spending the independence of outside readers by sending a recording instead of a generator, or by handing every team the same room and calling the resulting agreement evidence. Fig 2 :42: *"Buy independence in proportion to the cost of being wrong."* Both costs are avoidable by leading with the split rule and its refuter.
- **undoable:** yes. A wrong assignment of an element to a layer is corrected by a local ground measurement without withdrawing the split rule. A sent claim can be superseded by a corrected packet. No organizational primitive has to be discarded.
- **reversed by:** a ground reading, not agreement. An instrument that measures coordination failure separately from local-ground failure in a named slot would assign the elements; a replay with `R_T` measured as zero on an unvisited slot would break the method/recording claim; identical outcomes after a habitat substitution with no conditions reproduced would break the capability claim; a single lever that clears both union conditions with no different residue would collapse the union. Until one fires, the verdict stands as `candidate`.
