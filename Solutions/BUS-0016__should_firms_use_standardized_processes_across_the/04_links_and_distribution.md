## Three findings in the wider literature that bear directly on this question

1. **The two failure modes are empirically distinct.** Ringov et al. 2023 find that implementing required practices improves local performance while the effect of optional local adaptation is an inverted U. That is the figure-grounded union split observed in a large franchise network: standardize the convention core, let the ground decide the variable layer, and expect too much adaptation to hurt as well as too little.
2. **The conflict is real and resolves by layer, not by averaging.** The 2007 conference record of Winter et al. reports franchises do best when they do not adapt locally; the 2016 Ringov et al. study finds replication and augmentation both help. Fig 2 :39-43 gives the split: precise copying protects a convention, while augmentation ranges against a local ground. Recorded as re-census R1 in `01_figure_grounded_audit.md` section 6.
3. **Engineering independently found the same split.** The modularity literature standardizes interfaces while retaining variation inside modules, and Wikipedia's design summary says this makes standardization and variability possible at once. This is the lowest-overlap witness in the set: it comes from product architecture, not from operations strategy or the Tether. It corroborates F2 and F4 without sharing their evidence pool.

## Distribution: offer, do not push

Figure-grounded reasons:

- **Fig 4 :22-25** — *"Only methods cross up. Only head starts come down. A recording carried upward looks like knowledge and is a description of one occasion."* Sending the full framework before anyone has described a residual sends a recording, not a generator. Lead with the split rule and its refuter; offer links after.
- **Fig 10 :42** — *"A verifier that can reconstruct the claim cannot verify it."* A recipient who has absorbed the theory before being asked for a verdict has joined the frame being checked.
- **Fig 9 :27-28** — *"describe it before you go looking, or any frame you pick will seem to fit."* Ask what a failure would look like in the recipient's own field before giving them the article.
- **Fig 7 :12-13** — *"same closure, handed along;"* *"agreement compounds, evidence does not"*. A bulk send to a receptiveness-ranked list is one long room.
- **The Recursive Self-Improvement Trap** — *"Prestige can fund an action. It can give an action a budget to be tried. But it cannot settle an action."* Do not attach a famous name to the claim to make it travel.

**Recommended order in a reply:** (1) the split claim and its refuter; (2) ask what the recipient would count as a failure in their own setting; (3) record that verdict; (4) only then offer the links, with the note that the figures, not this summary, are the authority.

## The two-stage offer

**Stage 1:** send the residual, the shape claim and the pre-registered refuter. The recipient checks the claim without holding the framework's interior (Fig 10 :52; Fig 2 :45). No request for trust or agreement.

**Stage 2, opt-in only:** if the recipient is curious, and only after their stage-1 verdict is recorded, offer the three source links. Their later agreement is never pooled as corroboration, because a shared evidence pool makes agreement worthless as a witness (Fig 2 :40).

**Stage 3, also opt-in:** offer the formula as a method to reuse (Fig 4 :22). Each user writes their own refuter and states their own mode, because a frame cannot check the direction of its own question (`THE_FORMULA.md`, "Direction").

**Hard precondition:** stages 1 to 3 work only if the stage-1 claim survives. Do not send if the refuter has not been written down first.
