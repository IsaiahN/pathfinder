## What is deliberately not here

- No personal emails of named individuals. Institutional role addresses published publicly on a venue's own site are recorded only where the venue itself invites enquiries.
- No ranking of people by how likely they are to agree with the claim.
- No bulk-send plan.

Three reasons, in the figures' own words:

1. **A room does not add an anchor.** *"same closure, handed along;"* and *"agreement compounds, evidence does not"* (Fig 7 :12-13). A list of recipients selected for receptiveness is one room repeated.
2. **Correlation disqualifies a verdict.** *"disqualifying for a verdict: agreement about the world is worthless when the agreeing frames share an evidence pool"* (Fig 2 :40). Selecting for likely agreement manufactures exactly the correlation that makes agreement worthless.
3. **Asking for trust before a check spends independence.** *"A verifier that can reconstruct the claim cannot verify it."* (Fig 10 :42). The first contact carries the residual and its refuter, never the theory.

Legally: scraping personal emails for unsolicited bulk outreach breaches GDPR and CAN-SPAM style rules in many jurisdictions, on top of the structural failure. Public opt-in channels avoid both.
