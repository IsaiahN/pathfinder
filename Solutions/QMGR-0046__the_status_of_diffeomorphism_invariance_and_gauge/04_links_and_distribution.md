## Wider literature that bears on the question

1. **The hole argument as a template for identifying surplus structure.** Norton's SEP entry gives two physical grounds for calling structure redundant: verifiability (changes make no difference to what can be verified) and determinism (the laws cannot fix the candidate structure). These are the external counterparts of the figure rules that a convention must not decide an outcome and that declaring a meaning does not ground it. The same entry notes that general relativity is "in this respect a gauge theory," which is the bridge from D1 to D2.
2. **Constraints and the problem of time.** Weinstein and Rickles give the formal bridge: diffeomorphism invariance becomes the momentum and Hamiltonian constraints in the canonical theory, and in constraint quantization physical states satisfy `A psi = 0`. They also record the open worry that the quantized theory "may well not be independent of the choice of gauge." That is precisely the failure mode the identity-bond and convention rules forbid, and it is why D3 stays a gap rather than a verdict.
3. **Equivalence classes and observables.** Brading and Castellani connect group actions to partitions into equivalence classes and to the rule that only gauge-invariant quantities are observables. They also flag the Aharonov-Bohm dilemma, which prevents an easy claim that gauge-dependent structure is always physically idle. That is an honest complication, not a conflict with the shape claim: the figure mapping is to the referent of an equivalence, not to a blanket denial of gauge structure.
4. **Source discrimination as the ground question.** *The Hard Problem is About Source* (essay, fetched 2026-11-23): "If several paths create the same behavior, then behavior is a projection with lossy information." In this question the analogous instrument would have to distinguish whether two gauge-related descriptions are one source or two. No such instrument exists at the Planck scale, so the stop is honest.

- *The Recursive Self-Improvement Trap* (essay): "Prestige can fund an action. It can give an action a budget to be tried. But it cannot settle an action. Only the ground can settle it."
