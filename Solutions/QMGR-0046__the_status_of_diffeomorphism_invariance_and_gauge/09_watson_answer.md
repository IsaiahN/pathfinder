## VERDICT (from the figures)

The shape survives; the specific physics does not follow from it. Diffeomorphism invariance and gauge redundancy have the figure shape of a symmetric identity over descriptions: many names, one referent, where the referent is the invariant or relational content. Diffeomorphism-related configurations are not two physical states, and counting them as two is counting k spellings as k terms. Two descriptions sharing all invariants are one frame wearing two names, not two witnesses. A gauge choice is a convention and may not decide an outcome, so only invariant or relational quantities are admissible as observables. Symmetry is derived, irreducibility is level-relative, and quantization adds no primitive. The framework returns this shape, not a name: it selects no gauge group, quantization or observable algebra. The quantum-gravity contact has no anchor, so that part is `unreached`, not `refuted`. Overall mode: `specified`, ungrounded, candidate. D1 and D2 are settled at their own anchors; D3 is a gap.
