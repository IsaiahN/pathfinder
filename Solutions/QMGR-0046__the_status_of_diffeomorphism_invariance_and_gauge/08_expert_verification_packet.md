**Mode: `specified` on C1 to C3, `general` on the structural limits, not `grounded`.** No measurement at the Planck scale is claimed. This is a shape and a route, not a result. It is a simulated derivation checked for internal consistency against the figures, not a ground reading.

## 1. The claim, in one paragraph

Symmetry is a derived quantity, not a primitive. Gauge equivalence has the shape of an identity bond: many names, one referent, where the referent is the invariant or relational content. Diffeomorphism-related configurations are therefore not two distinct physical states, and counting them as two is counting k spellings as k terms. Two descriptions that share all invariants are one frame wearing two names, not two independent witnesses. A gauge choice is a convention and may not decide an outcome, so only invariant or relational quantities are admissible as observables. What counts as irreducible is level-relative, and quantization adds no physical primitive. The framework returns this shape, not a name: it selects no gauge group, no quantization, and no observable algebra, and the quantum-gravity contact stops as unreached rather than answered.

- **Statement:** gauge equivalence maps to the symmetric identity bond, not to an ordered comparison. k representatives, one term.

### C2. Physics-facing, checkable against the structure of GR and gauge theory

- **Statement:** diffeomorphism-related configurations share all invariant content; they are mathematically distinct but physically equivalent. The classical frame (invariants as currency) and the quantum gauge frame (gauge-invariant observables as currency) are the same bond kind at two levels.
- **What to check:** is it true that a diffeomorphism-dragged solution preserves all invariant properties and all observables? Does the equivalence class, not the representative, carry the physical content? Does the canonical constraint structure implement the same many-to-one map?
- **Verbatim figure line:** Fig 8 :48-49: "Two frames that overlap almost completely are one frame wearing two names." And Fig 13 :103-104: "A settled molecule becomes an atom for whatever composes over it. So irreducible is a fact about a level and never about the world."
- **Refuter:** show that two diffeomorphism-related configurations disagree on an invariant, or that their agreement carries information beyond the shared structure. Then they are two frames, not one.

### C3. Framework-internal plus physics-facing, the convention rule

- **Statement:** a gauge choice is a convention; if it enters a prediction it has stopped being one. Only invariant or relational quantities are admissible as observables.
- **Source, verbatim:** Fig 10 :47: "a convention that decides an outcome has stopped being one." Fig 10 :49: "Declaring a meaning does not ground it." Fig 9 :51: "what the verifier may see: evidence bearing on the residual is admissible; evidence bearing only on its maker is a stake-inducing channel, and the verifier is denied it."

- **Refuter:** a measurement whose outcome changes under a pure gauge transformation while all invariants are held fixed. Then the convention does decide an outcome and C3 is false.
