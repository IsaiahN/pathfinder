## 1. A shape is a constraint, not a filler

The canonical figures README states the limit: the framework returns "what a missing thing would have to look like (a shape, not a name)." Fig 9 :32: "you get a kind of thing, never the answer itself." Fig 13 :134-135 says it "can say the answer is out of reach with the library as it stands" and "cannot say which primitive is missing, because naming it would mean already holding it."

*The Library of Babel* (fetched this session): "you can cut your search short if the set of books returned is small and descriptive enough that it provides a general shape of the answer that satisfies your question."

So a **shape** is the narrowed form of an answer you can state before you can name the particular instance. It constrains; it does not fill. For this question:

- **Shape:** a gauge symmetry is an identity relation over descriptions; the referent is the invariant content; a convention may not decide an outcome.
- **Name (withheld):** this specific gauge group, this specific quantization, this specific observable algebra.

The distinction matters because the question asks for a "status," and a status is a kind of thing, not a label for one theory.

## 2. How the shape is obtained

From *The Library of Babel* (fetched this session): "use the existing vocabulary you do know that is closest to the words that you wish to describe and grasp the shape of." And: "With each new term gained, you are able to narrow the space by definition and deepen your vocabulary." The same essay gives the stopping rule: "the finish line will also be the starting place," and "you have already composed the answer through the process of locating it."

Fig 6 :30 (local): "The description travels. Fails by being sharpened into terms only we can read. Cast it in effect terms." Fig 9 :30-31: "your words are borrowed too, so describe the effect rather than the cause."

Applied here: the effect is "many descriptions, one physical referent, no convention-dependent prediction." The cause-level vocabulary (BRST, Dirac constraints, diffeomorphism group, refoliation invariance) is the physics frame's, used only as provenanced corroboration in section 4 of `01`, never as a figure derivation.

*Decoding Tacit Knowledge* (quoted in the exemplar, not fetched this session): "When contact isn't possible, the best you can send is a plain description of what you'd expect to see, because anyone can check that against the world." That is why the outreach packet in `08` asks what would refute the shape before offering any theory.

## 3. What licenses a name

Two things, both outside the shape:

1. **The ground settles it.** Formula step 5 (local): "the ground settles it. Verified only by what pays. A gate is not the ground." For D3 no such ground exists, so no name is licensed.
2. **A provenanced import from a frame whose closure differs**, with a source gate and a shadow test. Fig 6 :23: "No frame escapes its own closure. The bound moves only on contact with a frame whose closure differs."

*They're Scanning Books When They Should* (quoted in the exemplar, not fetched this session): "The real import is not a new term in the existing space. It is a new dimension of the space." And: "Declaring the primitives does not make them neutral. It makes the choice visible."

The six terms are a visible guess, not nature's own list. Saying "the identity bond is the status of gauge redundancy" is a binding onto a held bond, not the discovery of a new one. Fig 6 :25: "An import adds no atom to the world."

## 4. Why the metamerism warning bites here

*The Hard Problem is About Source* (fetched this session) gives the mechanism. A color instrument reads green but "the instrument is speaking only about what it measured, it cannot comment on the source." Yellow plus blue, RGB, CMYK, or a scheme not yet discovered can all read green. The essay's conclusion: "If several paths create the same behavior, then behavior is a projection with lossy information," and the case "was proven wrong not by argument, but by the arrival of a better instrument."

For this question: a gauge-invariant reading does not by itself tell us which underlying descriptions are physically distinct, any more than a green reading tells us which lights were on. That is exactly why the shape claim refuses to name a referent beyond the equivalence class and why it stops at D3. What is owed is an instrument, not a further argument. The essay also says "An instrument that can't distinguish these cases demonstrates its own inadequacy," which is the honest status of the current theoretical apparatus at the quantum-gravity contact.

## 5. Does "shape, not a name" forbid naming the identity bond? Answered

**No, for the same reason the exemplar narrowed it.** The README's phrase governs "a **missing** thing," a primitive not held in the library. The identity bond ≡ is already in the Operators table (local), and frame overlap is already a figure rule (Fig 8 :48-49). Naming them is selecting held atoms, not inventing a seventh. What remains forbidden is claiming the selection moves the wall (Fig 6 :25) or that it settles which quantization is correct (Fig 9 :32).

**What the record would get wrong:** saying "the framework derives that gauge symmetry is a redundancy of description X" with X a specific group or constraint algorithm. The figures license the shape; the particular X is a binding from the physics frame, and must be recorded as such. Triangulated across Fig 9, Fig 12, and the local canonical README.

## 6. Prestige is not action, and this derivation is a simulation

*The Recursive Self-Improvement Trap* (fetched this session): "Prestige must never be allowed to validate an action directly." And: "Prestige can fund an action. It can give an action a budget to be tried. But it cannot settle an action. Only the ground can settle it." A list of famous names who agree with the shape claim is the named collapse, not corroboration.

The same essay: LLMs "can quickly simulate an approximate answer/output," but "the truly testable output would need to be derived from the real architecture or working the formula yourself." So this entire folder, including `01`, is a **simulated derivation** checked for internal consistency against the figures. It is **not** a test of the claim, it is not a ground reading, and it cannot settle anything it reports on. It is labelled `specified` / `general` throughout for that reason.

## 7. Summary for this question

- Shape: identity over descriptions, invariant referent, convention-bound.
- Not a name: no gauge group, no quantization, no observable algebra.
- How obtained: proximity vocabulary, effect terms, refuse before mint.
- What would license a name: a ground reading or a provenanced import with a shadow test. Neither exists at D3.
- Honest stop: unreached, not refuted (Fig 8 :39).
