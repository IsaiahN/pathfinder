# The Tether's answer on climate sensitivity (EAR-0011)

**Mode: `specified` on the shape; the numeric range is world-sourced and reported, not derived here.** This is a shape and a route, not a new climate result. Simulated derivation checked against the figures (`05_what_shape_means.md` §6), not a ground reading.

## 1. The residual it answers

Given an increase in greenhouse gases, the predicted warming is compared with measured warming. The residual is not a single global number. Fig 1 :19: *"The residual is indexed by object slot, not aggregated."* The live slots are forcing agents, feedbacks, time scales and climate states. Fig 1 :22: *"Aggregating across slots is how a live signal disappears into an average."*

## 2. The shape

1. **The question is a union and must be unbundled.** Fig 9 :54: *"And when the split lands on a union rather than a partition, unbundle before you search again."* Fig 9 :57: *"a case in two conditions is not a harder case of either."* Forcing conversion, feedback response, time scale and forcing identity are four searches, not one.
2. **The answer is a constrained range per slot, not a point and not one global scalar.** The range is triangulated across lines of evidence that are genuinely independent. Fig 2 :35: *"Alignment is triangulation, not negotiation. A mirror cannot correct you."* Fig 2 :31: *"Independence, between two frames, is how little they draw on the same evidence."*
3. **The anchor is measurement, not model agreement.** Fig 2 :29: *"An anchor is whatever settles the question and does not change its answer because of what any frame thinks."* Spectroscopy, satellite flux, the instrumental record and paleoclimate proxies do not move when a model's prior changes. A GCM is a frame that ranges against them.
4. **Model agreement is room variation.** Fig 7 :13: *"agreement compounds, evidence does not."* CMIP models share code and evaluation data, so a large ensemble is closer to one witness wearing many names than to many independent witnesses. The IPCC AR6 assessment follows the figure-compliant route: it does not use model estimates of sensitivity directly as a line of evidence, but combines process understanding, historical warming, paleoclimate and emergent constraints.
5. **Forcing identity changes the response per unit forcing.** Fig 12 :117: *"isomer. Same atoms, different bond, different substance. The difference is invisible in the formula and decisive in the behaviour."* Methane and black carbon are isomers over a shared forcing atom set; a single degrees-per-watt scalar is their functional group (Fig 12 :124).
6. **The number is not figure-derived.** Fig 9 :32: *"you get a kind of thing, never the answer itself."* The world supplies: equilibrium climate sensitivity best estimate 3 °C, likely range 2.5 °C to 4 °C, very likely range 2 °C to 5 °C (IPCC AR6); effective sensitivity 66 percent range 2.6 K to 3.9 K, robust within 2.3 K to 4.5 K (Sherwood et al. 2020). These are imported with provenance, not minted from the figures.
7. **The residual is unreached, not unreachable.** Fig 6 :32: *"An instrument is not built from a description. It is improved from a worse instrument already returning something."* Spectrometers, flux satellites, Argo floats and paleoclimate proxies already register, so instruments can be sharpened from things already returning readings. There is no `gap` verdict here.

## 3. What it explicitly does not say

- Not a new climate number, not a replacement for IPCC AR6 or Sherwood et al. 2020.
- Not a claim that the figures compute 3 °C, or that any figure line contains a temperature value.
- Not a claim that climate models are useless. They are rooms: search inside a closure with the instruments they hold. They are not the anchor and their agreement is not a verdict.
- Not a claim that efficacy differences are settled beyond instrument resolution. The isomer reading has a pre-registered falsifier (`06_isomers_and_order.md`).
- Not `grounded` by this run. No measurement was taken here.

## 4. What would settle it: the ground

A source-discriminating instrument, not more agreement. From *The Hard Problem is About Source*: an instrument that reads green *"cannot comment on the source"*; the spectrometer resolved it. For this question:

- **Slot discriminator:** can an instrument distinguish a response produced by CO2 with one feedback set from the same response produced by a different aerosol, methane and ocean-heat-uptake mix? If yes, the per-slot residual is settled; if no, the global mean remains a projection with lossy information.
- **Efficacy test:** measure all forcing-agent efficacies against a common forcing definition. If they collapse to 1 within resolution, the isomer claim is refused and the scalar functional-group claim stands.
- **Order test:** settle the transient molecule first, then measure the increment to equilibrium and to Earth-system sensitivity. Sherwood et al. 2020 and Tierney et al. 2020 supply the current readings; a new instrument can change them.
- **Standing falsifiers from `01`:** produce a figure line that yields a number; show that the evidence lines share a single hidden error source; move the observational anchor with a model; show that the four sub-questions share one anchor and one currency.

## 5. The honest stop

**Unreached, not unreachable.** Fig 13 :134: *"Unreachable is a verdict here, not a silence."* The six-term set is *"a human's careful guess at reality's compression axes"* and declaring it makes the choice visible, not neutral. The same is true of the forcing-feedback vocabulary. The answer stops at the limit of the available instruments and records the lineage so the stop is checkable. What is recorded only grows; what is reachable can fall (Fig 6 amendment). *They're Scanning Books* reminds that a declared primitive set is a visible guess, not nature's own list.

## 6. One-paragraph version for outreach

> The Tether does not produce a new number for climate sensitivity. It says that the question bundles at least four searches, forcing conversion, feedback response, time scale and forcing identity, and that asking for one number before unbundling them hides the live residuals in an average. The answer any number must satisfy is a constrained range per slot, triangulated against a ground that does not move when models change their priors: spectroscopy, satellite flux, the instrumental record and paleoclimate. Climate models are useful rooms, but they share code and evaluation data, so their agreement is not independent corroboration. Different forcing agents give different warming per Watt, so a single degrees-per-watt scalar is a functional group rather than the whole substance. The figure set returns that shape; the number comes from the ground, currently 3 °C best estimate with a likely range of 2.5 °C to 4 °C (IPCC AR6, corroborated independently by Sherwood et al. 2020 at 2.6 K to 3.9 K). What is offered is the shape, the independence requirement, the refuters written in advance, and the instruments that would close each slot. The slots stay open until the ground settles them.
