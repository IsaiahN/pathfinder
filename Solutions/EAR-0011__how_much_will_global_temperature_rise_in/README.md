**Field for public contact points:** climate projection research.

**Mode:** `specified` on the figure-derived shape. The numeric range is reported from world sources as ground-settled within climate science; this simulated run does not itself settle it. Nothing here is a new measurement.

**Status:** done.

**Registry status:** pending (set by the registry owner, not by this author).

## Restatement as an effect

Given an increase in greenhouse gases, what global mean surface temperature rise follows, and what would count as a failure of any proposed answer? The two options are: (A) read the question as asking for one number that the framework should produce, or (B) read the question as a union of slots, forcing conversion, feedback response, time scale and forcing identity, each with its own anchor, and report the shape that any number must satisfy. Option A makes the framework answer a question it does not govern and hides live slot residuals in an average. Option B keeps the residual per slot, triangulates low-overlap frames against a ground that does not update, and hands the number over to the instruments that actually measure it.

## What an answer would look like (Direction, THE_FORMULA.md)

Write the sought-for shape as an ordinary term before the run, so it can accrue a residual and be refused: a constrained range of global temperature rise per unit forcing, stated per forcing agent, per feedback, per time scale and per climate state, with a named anchor, a declared independence structure and a written refuter. A single global number with no slot structure is explicitly not an acceptable answer shape.

## Boundary and standing

This package is a simulated derivation checked for internal consistency against the figures, not a ground reading. *The Recursive Self-Improvement Trap* states that LLM output is *"simulacra"* and that *"the truly testable output would need to be derived from the real architecture or working the formula yourself."* By its own rule this solution cannot settle the question it reports on. No measurement was taken here. World sources were opened directly; local framework files were read from `FRAMEWORK/` in this workspace.

## What is deliberately not here

- No personal emails of named individuals.
- No ranking of people by how likely they are to believe the result.
- No bulk-send plan.
- No claim that the figures compute a temperature value.

Three reasons, in the figures' own words:

1. **A room is not an anchor.** Fig 7 :13: *"agreement compounds, evidence does not."* A list of climate models or reviewers selected for agreement cannot manufacture evidence.
2. **Correlation disqualifies a verdict.** Fig 2 :40: *"agreement about the world is worthless when the agreeing frames share an evidence pool."* CMIP models share code and evaluation data, so their agreement is partly one pool talking.
3. **Asking for trust before a check spends independence.** Fig 10 :42: *"A verifier that can reconstruct the claim cannot verify it."*

Legally: public opt-in or institutional submission routes avoid the GDPR and CAN-SPAM style failures that personal-email harvesting creates. The two-stage offer asks for a refutation, never an endorsement.
