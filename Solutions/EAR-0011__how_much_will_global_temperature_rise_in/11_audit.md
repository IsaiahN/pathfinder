## Independent domain census

- **R (arity / symmetry / scale).** Predicted minus observed warming, indexed by slot. Arity at least three: forcing, response, agent identity and placement. Symmetry: forcing to response is ordered (`→` in the Operators table), and a global mean does not commute back to a unique forcing-feedback composition. Scale: first order inside a time scale; second order across feedbacks and assessment; cross scale from decades to millennia.
- **Domain 1, forcing.** Actors: CO2, CH4, aerosols, solar. Currency: W m-2. Anchor: spectroscopy and TOA flux, which do not move when a model changes. Partition.
- **Domain 2, feedback response.** Actors: clouds, albedo, water vapour and lapse rate, ocean heat uptake, carbon cycle. Currency: W m-2 K-1 and K. Anchor: instrumental record, flux satellites, paleoclimate proxies. Union of slots.
- **Domain 3, model and assessment.** Actors: GCMs, assessors. Currency: ensemble spread and ranges. Anchor: observed warming; the models themselves are frames. Abramowitz et al. 2019 and Knutti et al. 2013 document shared code and data. Frame, not anchor.
- **Domain 4, forcing identity.** Actors: agent and placement. Currency: efficacy. Anchor: single-forcing experiments. Isomer or functional-group question.
- **Domain 5, time and state.** Actors: transient, equilibrium, Earth-system; glacial, warm. Currency: time and K. Anchor: long simulations and paleoclimate. Ordered chain.
