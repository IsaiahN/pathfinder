# Outreach draft: check the shape before you decide whether the framework matters

**Use:** one-to-one, or as a cover letter attached to a submission through the public venues in `03_public_contact_points.csv`. **Not** for bulk send.

**Hard precondition:** do not send until the three sub-claims in `08_expert_verification_packet.md` have been checked locally against the verbatim figure text. The stage-1 output must survive or the two-stage offer collapses on contact.

---

**Subject:** Can one shape claim about climate sensitivity survive your check?

> Hi [name],
>
> I have one specific, checkable claim about how the climate-sensitivity question is structured. I am not sending a new temperature number, I am not asking you to read a theory first, and I am not asking you to agree. If the claim does not survive, there is nothing else to look at.
>
> **The residual (stated in effect terms):** given an increase in greenhouse gases, the prediction of the warming response is measured against what the instruments report. That residual is indexed by slot: by forcing agent (CO2, methane, aerosol, solar), by feedback (clouds, albedo, water vapour and lapse rate, ocean heat uptake), by time scale (transient, equilibrium, Earth-system) and by climate state. A single global number is a legitimate headline, but it is not a diagnosis of which slot is still wrong. Fig 1 :19: *"The residual is indexed by object slot, not aggregated."* Fig 1 :22: *"Aggregating across slots is how a live signal disappears into an average."*
>
> **The shape claim (`specified`, not `grounded`):** the question as usually asked is a union of at least four sub-questions, not one hard question. It must be unbundled before a number is searched for (Fig 9 :54: *"And when the split lands on a union rather than a partition, unbundle before you search again."*). Once unbundled, the answer any number must satisfy is a constrained range per slot, triangulated across evidence lines that are genuinely independent, with a named anchor that does not move when models change their priors. Fig 2 :35: *"Alignment is triangulation, not negotiation. A mirror cannot correct you."*
>
> **Where the number comes from:** not from the figures. The figures return a kind of thing, not the answer itself (Fig 9 :32: *"you get a kind of thing, never the answer itself"*). The world does supply a number: the IPCC AR6 assessed best estimate of 3 °C for a doubling of CO2, with a likely range of 2.5 °C to 4 °C and a very likely range of 2 °C to 5 °C, from process understanding, the instrumental record and paleoclimate (Sherwood et al. 2020 gives an effective-sensitivity 66 percent range of 2.6 K to 3.9 K). I am reporting that, not deriving it.
>
> **What I am not claiming:** that a model ensemble is an anchor. Fig 7 :13: *"agreement compounds, evidence does not."* Climate models share code, parameterisations and evaluation datasets (Abramowitz et al. 2019; Knutti et al. 2013), so their agreement is partly one evidence pool talking. I am also not claiming that a single universal degrees-per-watt figure captures every forcing: Hansen et al. 2005 found efficacies from roughly 58 percent for biomass black carbon to about 110 percent for methane, so agent identity changes the response per unit forcing. In the framework's vocabulary those are isomers, not one substance.
>
> **What would refute the shape claim (written down before this message):**
> 1. A verbatim figure line that yields a numeric temperature response. Then the figures do settle the number and my boundary claim is false.
> 2. All forcing efficacies equal to 1 within instrument resolution. Then forcing identity carries no information and the isomer reading fails.
> 3. The process, historical and paleo evidence lines shown to share one hidden source that determines their errors together. Then my triangulation is one pool wearing three names.
> 4. A measurement move in which a model or assessment changed the observational anchor.
> 5. The four sub-questions shown to share one anchor and one currency at one scale. Then the union reading fails.
>
> I wrote these before sending this. A one-line "this fails because..." is more useful to me than agreement. If you would rather not engage, no reply is needed. If you have a stake in this framing, please say so, since an undeclared interest is modelled anyway (Fig 10 :59: *"an interest in the outcome is unavoidable, an interest in the reading is disqualifying."*).
>
> **If it survives, and only then:** the sources are below. The figures, not my summary of them, are the authority.
> - Figure text with line numbers (local workspace): `FRAMEWORK/reference/03-FIGURES_TEXT.md`
> - Canonical usage rule (local workspace): `FRAMEWORK/reference/FIGURES_README_canonical.md`
> - IPCC AR6 WG1 Summary for Policymakers: https://www.ipcc.ch/report/ar6/wg1/chapter/summary-for-policymakers
> - Sherwood et al. 2020, Reviews of Geophysics: https://pmc.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/articles/PMC7524012/
>
> **If you want to use the machinery:** the formula is in the figures. Run it on a problem you know well and can check against a ground. Write down what would refute your result *before* you run it and state the mode of what you get. A reported failure is more useful to me than a reported success.

## Why this version is ordered this way

| the move | figure-grounded reason |
|---|---|
| Residual and refuter before theory | Fig 10 :52, *"Bring a characterised residual, never the source."* The recipient ranges the claim against their own ground before holding the framework's interior. |
| Do not ask for agreement | Fig 2 :35, *"Alignment is triangulation, not negotiation. A mirror cannot correct you."* |
| Verdict recorded before any theory is shown | Fig 2 :40, agreement from a shared evidence pool is worthless as a verdict. The stage-1 verdict is sealed before stage 2. |
| Mode stated on the claim | `THE_FORMULA.md` mode rule: `general | specified | grounded`. The claim is `specified`. |
| The number is reported, not derived | Fig 9 :32, *"you get a kind of thing, never the answer itself."* |
| Model ensemble is not called an anchor | Fig 2 :29, the anchor does not change its answer because of what a frame thinks. |
| Efficacy is admitted before a scalar | Fig 12 :117, *"isomer. Same atoms, different bond, different substance. The difference is invisible in the formula and decisive in the behaviour."* |
| Stake declared | Fig 10 :59, *"an interest in the outcome is unavoidable, an interest in the reading is disqualifying."* |
| No request for trust or prestige | *The Recursive Self-Improvement Trap*: *"Prestige can fund an action. It can give an action a budget to be tried. But it cannot settle an action. Only the ground can settle it."* |
| Offer the formula as a method only in stage 3 | Fig 4 :22, *"Only methods cross up. Only head starts come down."* |
