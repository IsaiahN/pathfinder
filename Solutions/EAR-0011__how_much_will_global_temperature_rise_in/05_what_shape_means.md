## 1. A shape is a constraint, not a filler

From *The Library of Babel*: *"You can cut your search short if the set of books returned is small and descriptive enough that it provides a general shape of the answer that satisfies your question."* And on the method: *"narrow the space by definition and deepen your vocabulary."*

For this question a **shape** is the narrowed form of the answer you can state before you can name a single number. It constrains: per-slot residuals, a named anchor, independence between lines, a union that has been unbundled, a range rather than a point, and a refuter. It does not fill: it does not return 3 °C, and it does not select which feedback slot is currently live.

`SPACE(???)TIME` is the exemplar's shape. The analogous shape here is `ΔT(WHICH FORCING, WHICH FEEDBACK, WHICH TIME SCALE, WHICH STATE) = a constrained range against a named ground`. The single number `3 °C per doubling` is the name, supplied by the world, not by the shape.

## 2. How a shape is obtained

- *Library of Babel*: use the vocabulary already held that is closest to the thing, look for recurring terms that overlap or correlate with it, recurse. The searcher must become a better searcher: the obstacle is *"your ignorance, of not knowing what you don't know."*
- *Library of Babel* on a bundled question: *"Perhaps your problem was a bundle or conflation of several problems masquerading as one? You will need to search all of those problems individually to find their answers and then combine them in a satisfactory way."* This is the direct essay-level warrant for F1 in `01_figure_grounded_audit.md`.
- Fig 6 :30: *"outward: does another frame already hold it? The description travels."* Fig 9 :30-31: *"your words are borrowed too, so describe the effect rather than the cause."* Describe the residual as an effect, for example "warming per unit forcing with the forcing identity left open", then look for the frame that predicts it.
- *Decoding Tacit Knowledge*: *"When contact isn't possible, the best you can send is a plain description of what you'd expect to see, because anyone can check that against the world."* This is why the outreach packet asks the checker what they would expect to see before showing the framework.
- *Library of Babel* on stopping: the answer is reached when *"the finish line will also be the starting place"*, because by then you have *"composed the answer through the process of locating it."* For climate sensitivity the stop is not the number; it is the point at which every slot has a live instrument and a declared residual.

## 3. What licenses a name, and what the world actually named

Only two things license filling a shape, both outside the shape itself (Fig 6 :23, Fig 8 :50-52):

1. **The ground settles it.** Formula step 5: *"the ground settles it. Verified only by what pays."* For this question the ground is the instrumental record, satellite flux, process experiments and paleoclimate proxies.
2. **A provenanced import from a frame whose closure differs**, with a source gate and a shadow test.

The world did settle a name: IPCC AR6's 3 °C best estimate and 2.5 °C to 4 °C likely range. Sherwood et al. 2020 arrived independently at a 2.6 K to 3.9 K 66 percent range. Those are reports from the ground, not derivations from the shape. *They're Scanning Books* warns: *"Declaring the primitives does not make them neutral. It makes the choice visible."* Declaring the shape does not make the number either; the number is earned by instruments.

## 4. The source-discrimination point matters here

*The Hard Problem is About Source* gives the mechanism. An instrument that reads green *"cannot comment on the source"*, because one green light, yellow plus blue, RGB, CMYK or a scheme not yet discovered all give the same reading. *"If several paths create the same behavior, then behavior is a projection with lossy information."* The resolution came from a better instrument, the spectrometer, not from an argument.

For climate sensitivity the analogue is exact in structure: a measured global temperature rise does not by itself name its source. A rise can be produced by CO2 forcing with one feedback set, or by a different mix of aerosol, methane and ocean heat uptake with another. *"An instrument that can't distinguish these cases demonstrates its own inadequacy."* What is owed is a source-discriminating instrument per slot, not a single number asserted as the source. Hansen et al. 2005's efficacy measurements are one such discriminator between forcing agents.

*How Intelligence Actually Works* restates the limit operationally: the recursion converges toward indiscernibility, and *"indiscernibility is not identity"*, which is why the lineage has to be kept even when the copy is perfect. A global mean matches many compositions; only the recorded lineage tells you which one you are looking at.

## 5. Q1: does "shape, not a name" forbid reporting the IPCC number?

**No.** Answered after reading the essays in full. The canonical phrase in the README governs a **missing** thing, a primitive not held in the library, and the framework's inability to name which primitive is missing. A ground-settled range reported with its evidence lines is not an invented primitive; it is an import with provenance from the climate-science frame, which Fig 6 :23 permits on contact with a frame whose closure differs. The range is itself a shape in the library-of-Babel sense: it narrows the search without pretending to be a point answer.

What would be forbidden is asserting that the figures *produce* 3 °C, or that the framework selects which feedback slot is currently dominant without a source-discriminating instrument. So the correct form in this folder is: *figure-governed shape, world-sourced number, provenance recorded*.

## 5b. Correction to my own earlier reading

I initially wrote that "the framework returns a shape, not a name" meant no numeric range could be reported at all. That overstates the rule. The README's wording is about a **missing** thing. Nothing in the figures forbids reporting a number the ground has settled elsewhere, provided the number is labelled as an import with provenance rather than a figure output. I have retracted the overstatement here rather than removing it silently. The §4 metamerism point is **not** retracted: a global temperature reading still does not determine which forcing-feedback composition produced it, and that is a missing-discriminator claim, not a ban on numbers.

## 6. Prestige is not action, and this audit is a simulation

**Prestige is not action.** From *The Recursive Self-Improvement Trap*: *"Prestige can fund an action. It can give an action a budget to be tried. But it cannot settle an action. Only the ground can settle it. The Ground Maintainer must be blind to prestige."* A list of famous climate institutes to agree with this shape claim would be the named collapse, not corroboration. The legitimate ask is to give the claim a budget to be tried and let the ground, an instrument or an experiment, settle it. IPCC authority is used here as a source for the number, never as a reason a shape claim is true.

**My own audit is a simulation, not a ground reading.** Same essay: *"the output is only as good as the validator. The agent, or the tool, or the frame, or the theory, cannot validate itself."* And: *"the truly testable output would need to be derived from the real architecture or working the formula yourself."* The architecture essay warns that outputs *"could never be verified if the solutions presented were not contrived to fit the formula."* So `01_figure_grounded_audit.md` is a simulated derivation checked for internal consistency against the figures. It is **not** a test of any climate claim and must be labelled as such. It cannot settle anything it reports on.
