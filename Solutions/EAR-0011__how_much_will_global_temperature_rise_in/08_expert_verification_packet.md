# Expert verification packet: the Tether on climate sensitivity (EAR-0011)

**What this is:** a claim in a form a climate researcher can check. Mode, derivation and refuters are stated before the conclusion, so a reader knows what to look for and what would settle it.

**Mode: `specified`, not `grounded`.** This packet makes no new measurement and supplies no new temperature value. It is a shape claim about the structure of the question and the admissibility of witnesses. This is a simulated derivation checked for internal consistency against the figures; it cannot settle the question it reports on.

## 1. The claim, in one paragraph

The question "how much will global temperature rise in response to a given increase in greenhouse gases?" bundles at least four sub-questions: forcing conversion, feedback response, time scale and forcing identity. It should be unbundled before a number is searched for. The answer any number must satisfy is a constrained range per slot, triangulated against a ground that does not move when models change their priors (spectroscopy, satellite flux, the instrumental record, paleoclimate proxies). Climate models are useful rooms for search but not independent witnesses when they share code and evaluation data. Different forcing agents give different warming per unit forcing, so a single degrees-per-watt scalar is a functional group, not the full substance. The figures return this shape; the number is world-sourced and reported with provenance, not derived from the figures.

## 2. Three checkable sub-claims

### C1: framework-internal, checkable by reading the figure text

- **Statement:** the question is a union and must be split; the residual is per slot, never a single average.
- **Source, verbatim:** Fig 9 :54, *"And when the split lands on a union rather than a partition, unbundle before you search again."* Fig 9 :57, *"a case in two conditions is not a harder case of either."* Fig 1 :19, *"The residual is indexed by object slot, not aggregated."* Fig 1 :22, *"Aggregating across slots is how a live signal disappears into an average."*
- **Refuter:** show that forcing conversion, feedback response, time scale and forcing identity share one anchor and one currency at one scale, so that answering one answers all. Or produce a case where a single global residual correctly identifies the responsible slot without decomposition.

### C2: climate-science-facing, checkable against the assessment literature

- **Statement:** the evidence lines are independent witnesses only to the extent their overlap is declared; a correlated model ensemble is not equivalent to an equal number of independent lines.
- **Source, verbatim:** Fig 2 :31, *"Independence, between two frames, is how little they draw on the same evidence."* Fig 2 :40, *"agreement about the world is worthless when the agreeing frames share an evidence pool."* Fig 7 :13, *"agreement compounds, evidence does not."*
- **World check:** Abramowitz et al. 2019 (Earth System Dynamics) report that models *"share literature, ideas for representations of processes, parameterisations, evaluation data sets and even sections of model code."* Knutti et al. 2013 report that CMIP5 models are *"neither independent of each other nor independent of the earlier generation."* Sherwood et al. 2020 report that their three lines *"agree and are judged to be largely independent."* IPCC AR6 states that its assessed range does not directly use model estimates of ECS and TCR.
- **Refuter:** demonstrate that the process, historical and paleo lines share a single hidden source that determines their errors together, so their agreement is one pool wearing three names. If so, the triangulation here is downgraded to single-source.

### C3: physics-facing, checkable against forcing experiments

- **Statement:** forcing identity carries information that Watts alone do not; the agents are isomers over a shared forcing atom set, and the universal scalar is their functional group.
- **Source, verbatim:** Fig 12 :117, *"isomer. Same atoms, different bond, different substance. The difference is invisible in the formula and decisive in the behaviour."* Fig 12 :124, *"functional group. A substructure whose presence fixes the outcome. The group says what kind; the qualifier says which member."*
- **World check:** Hansen et al. 2005 define efficacy as temperature response per unit forcing relative to CO2 and find roughly 110 percent for CH4 (145 percent with indirect effects) and 58 to 78 percent for black carbon depending on source. If those differences survive a source-discriminating instrument, the isomer reading governs.
- **Refuter:** show that all efficacies equal 1 within the resolution of the instruments, so that agent and placement carry no information. Then the isomer claim is refused and only the functional-group claim stands.

## 3. What is explicitly not claimed

- Not a new climate sensitivity number. The number reported here is IPCC AR6 (3 °C best estimate, likely 2.5 °C to 4 °C, very likely 2 °C to 5 °C) and Sherwood et al. 2020 (66 percent range 2.6 K to 3.9 K), cited as world sources.
- Not that the figures compute a temperature value. Fig 9 :32: *"you get a kind of thing, never the answer itself."*
- Not that climate models are invalid or useless. They are frames and rooms, and the framework's own rule is that frames range against the ground rather than negotiate with it.
- Not that forcing efficacies are settled beyond instrument resolution. That is an open instrument question with a pre-registered falsifier.
- Not `grounded` by this run. No measurement was taken here.

## 5. What would settle it

A source-discriminating instrument, not further agreement. Three concrete tests:

1. **Slot discriminator:** can an instrument tell a warming produced by CO2 with one feedback set from the same warming produced by an aerosol, methane and ocean-heat-uptake mixture? If not, the global mean is a projection with lossy information and the per-slot residual stays live.
2. **Efficacy test:** measure all forcing-agent efficacies against a common forcing definition and see whether they collapse to 1 within resolution. This fires the C3 refuter either way.
3. **Order test:** settle the transient molecule first, then measure the increment to equilibrium and to Earth-system sensitivity. Sherwood et al. 2020 and Tierney et al. 2020 provide the current readings; a better instrument can change them.

## 6. What an expert can check in twenty minutes

1. Read Fig 1 :19, :22; Fig 2 :31, :40; Fig 7 :13; Fig 9 :32, :54; Fig 12 :117, :124. Eight short passages.
2. Check C2 against your own knowledge: are CMIP models independent enough that their spread is an evidence line rather than one pool resampled? Name the dependence mechanism you would use.
3. Check C3: is the efficacy spread real at current instrument resolution, and does it survive the effective-radiative-forcing definition?
4. State the four sub-questions of C1. If you can collapse them to one anchor and one currency, say so.
5. Send the refutation. A failure is more useful than an endorsement. Record your verdict before reading any framework material beyond the quoted lines.

## 7. Status

`specified`, ungrounded, candidate. The shape survives only while the refuters in C1, C2 and C3 remain unfired. Prestige or the size of a model ensemble is not a refuter and is not a witness.
