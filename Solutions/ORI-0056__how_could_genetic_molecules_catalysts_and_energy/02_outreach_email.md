# Outreach draft: check the shape before you decide about the framework

**Use:** one to one, or as a cover note attached to a submission through a public venue in `03_public_contact_points.csv`. **Not** for bulk send.

**Hard precondition:** do not send until the refuter below has been checked by the sender and the mode labels are intact. The output has to survive validation or the two-stage offer collapses on contact.

---

**Subject:** One checkable shape claim about protocell integration, before any theory

> Hi [name],
>
> I am writing from Project Pathfinder. I have one specific output I would like you to check against what you already know about protocell assembly. I am not asking you to read the framework first, and I am not asking you to agree with it. If the output does not survive, there is nothing else to look at.
>
> **The question in effect terms.** Heritable molecules, catalysts and energy-producing chemistry each exist on their own. In every known cell they sit inside one boundary. What has to be true for them to be gathered in the same compartment?
>
> **The output (`specified`, not `grounded`).** Three things follow from the figure set before any chemistry is named:
>
> 1. This is a union of overlapping conditions, not one hard problem. Replication needs retention, catalysis needs concentration and flux, energy capture needs a boundary and a spendable difference. A single lever that clears one condition leaves the intersections standing. Fig 9 :54: "And when the split lands on a union rather than a partition, unbundle before you search again."
> 2. The boundary is a containing state, and the three functions must compose with it across kinds, never directly with each other. Fig 13 :10 lists "near, far, between, contained" as a state, and Fig 13 :74 says "Everything else is a composition across the two kinds, never within one."
> 3. The energy part is not a bare source. It is a spendable difference bonded to a conserved state, so it is a flow, and the catalytic part must survive its own use, with the whole compound closing a cycle. Fig 13 :22 calls a gradient "a difference that can be spent"; Fig 12 :119 calls a catalyst "a part that survives its own use."
>
> That is the whole claim: a **shape**, not a route recipe and not a named molecule. Fig 9 :32: "you get a kind of thing, never the answer itself."
>
> **What I am not claiming.** Not that wet-dry cycles, fatty-acid vesicles, montmorillonite or hydrothermal gradients are *the* answer. Those are results from the chemistry literature. They enter here as imports with a source and a provenance, not as derivations from the figures. I am also not claiming this settles how life began. No experiment I ran settles it.
>
> **What would refute the shape.** I wrote these down before sending this:
>
> 1. A self-maintaining co-location with no containing state.
> 2. A state-to-state or structure-to-structure composition with no opposite-kind intermediary that the framework must admit and cannot refuse.
> 3. Evidence that the three conditions form a partition, so one lever closes all three without touching their intersections.
> 4. A persistent compartment with heredity and catalysis but no spendable difference coupled to a conserved state, or a catalyst consumed on first use and never regenerated.
>
> If any one holds, I withdraw the claim. A one-line "this fails because..." is more useful to me than agreement. If you would rather not engage, no reply is needed.
>
> **If it survives, and only then:** here are three public sources. The figures are the authority, not my summary of them.
> - The verbatim figure text with line numbers (local extract): `FRAMEWORK/reference/03-FIGURES_TEXT.md`
> - The canonical usage rule: https://github.com/IsaiahN/tether/blob/general-agent/figures/README.md
> - The architecture article, including the section "Three mappings offered and declined": https://medium.com/@IsaiahNwukor/arrival-of-the-fittest-the-tether-architecture-1de7edf641ec
>
> **If you want to use the machinery:** the formula is in the figures link. Run it on a problem you know well and can check against a ground. Write down what would refute your result *before* you run it, and state the mode of what you get. The architecture article warns that outputs "could never be verified if the solutions presented were not contrived to fit the formula." A reported failure is more useful to me than a reported success.
>
> Thanks for reading,
> [name / project contact]

## Why this version is ordered this way

| the move | figure-grounded reason |
|---|---|
| Output first, framework second | Fig 10 :52: "Bring a characterised residual, never the source." Frames range against the ground separately (Fig 2 :45). |
| Do not ask for agreement | Fig 2 :35: "Alignment is triangulation, not negotiation. A mirror cannot correct you." |
| Refuter before conclusion | A falsifier written before the run is a design instrument. See `01_figure_grounded_audit.md` §4. |
| Mode stated on every claim | `THE_FORMULA.md`: "Mode. general · specified · grounded. State which." |
| Mechanism names withheld until provenanced | Fig 8 :50: "Convergent derivation and adopted import look identical in the contents." |
| Union claim before boundary claim | Fig 9 :54: unbundle before you search. A single lever on one condition leaves the intersections. |
| No "bombshell", no "true answer", no request for trust | Fig 10 :42: "A verifier that can reconstruct the claim cannot verify it." Prestige is not ground. |
| The framework returns a shape, not a name | Canonical figures README: "a shape, not a name". The email never names the molecular route. |
| Offer the formula as a method to reuse | Fig 4 :22: "Only methods cross up. Only head starts come down." |
| Ask users to state their own ground and refuter | `THE_FORMULA.md` "Direction": a frame cannot check the direction of its own question. |

## Distribution limits

- One to one only. No list, no automation, no bulk.
- Record the recipient's verdict on stage 1 **before** sending any framework link.
- Do not pool later agreement as corroboration. Fig 2 :40: "agreement about the world is worthless when the agreeing frames share an evidence pool".
- A Track B reply, if one is ever made under the separate direct-outreach track, is a conversation with zero verdict weight, never a witness.
