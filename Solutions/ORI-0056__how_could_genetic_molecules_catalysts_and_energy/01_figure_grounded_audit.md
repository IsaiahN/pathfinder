## 0. The residual stated in effect terms

Three capacities are found separately in prebiotic chemistry: a polymer that can carry heritable variation (genetic), a surface or molecule that lowers an activation barrier and survives its own use (catalyst), and a flow of energy that can be captured and spent (energy-producing chemistry). In every known cell the three sit inside one boundary. The residual is the gap between "each exists" and "they compose into one unit that can be settled by a ground."

Effect terms, not names:

- **Arity:** four, three functions plus a containing state. This is a union compound, not one hard question (Fig 9 :54-55). It has its own loop and its own ground.
- **Symmetry:** the roles are not interchangeable. Joining them requires ordered bonds, because a route is needed and a list of ingredients is not a route (Fig 12 :111).
- **Scale:** molecular reactions compose into a protocell at one level; the protocell composes into a population at the next. A lossy transform lies between (Fig 4 :19-21).
- **What an answer would look like (bound as an ordinary term, Formula step "Direction"):** a containing state that retains the three functions while remaining open to flux; a cross-kind composition among them; an enumerated habitat that supplies conditions and actors; a candidate mechanism imported with provenance; and an experiment that could refuse the whole compound. Call this shape **CO-LOCATION**. It is bound now so it can accrue a residual like any other term.

## 3. Findings F1 to F10

### F1 : The residual is a union compound, not one hard question

Fig 9 :54: "And when the split lands on a union rather than a partition, unbundle before you search again." Fig 9 :55: "A bundled problem is usually a union of overlapping conditions, not a list of separate ones." Fig 9 :57: "a case in two conditions is not a harder case of either."

The three functions are overlapping conditions: replication needs a boundary to keep template and replicase together; catalysis needs concentration and a flux of substrate; energy capture needs a membrane or interface and a gradient. A single lever that clears the region where only one condition holds leaves the intersections standing. So the question must be searched as a union of three loops plus their intersections, not as one linear problem.

Fig 1 :19: "The residual is indexed by object slot, not aggregated." Averaging the three into a single "becoming alive" residual would throw away the only signal that says which slot is live.

**Refuter:** show that a single lever, applied to one condition, closes the residual in all three at once without touching their intersections. Then it is a partition and this finding fails.
**Status:** `triangulated` across Fig 9 (union/partition split) and Fig 1 (per-slot residual), two figures with low overlap between them.

### F2 : The containing boundary is a state (space), not Fig 4's membrane

Fig 13 :10: "near, far, between, contained". Containment is listed as a state, one of the six. Fig 13 :74: "Everything else is a composition across the two kinds, never within one." Fig 13 :75: "Two states never bond directly. It is not space-time but space, a structure, time; and two structures join only through a state."

So a compartment that gathers a genetic polymer, a catalyst and an energy flow is a containing **state** with structures mediated through it. The functions cannot bond directly to each other as bare functions. A structure must sit between each pair. This is a shape claim, not a chemical name.

**Strain test on Fig 4.** Fig 4 :21 calls the membrane "the boundary between the sizes, and the rule about what may cross it. Not a wall: a filter with a direction." Read as the lipid bilayer of a protocell, this strains: Fig 4's membrane separates one agent from a population and carries methods upward and head starts downward. It governs the crossing from molecules to protocells to a population of protocells, not containment inside one protocell. Re-census: the literal compartment is Fig 13's space/containment state; Fig 4 governs the scale transition. Both are kept, with different domains.

**Refuter:** derive containment from the other five terms, or show a self-maintaining co-location with no containing state at all. Either breaks the finding.
**Status:** `triangulated` across Fig 13 (the six-term census) and Fig 4 (the scale membrane, after re-census). The re-census is recorded rather than hidden.

### F3 : Energy-producing chemistry is the gradient, bonded to energy as a flow

Fig 13 :22: "a difference that can be spent". Fig 13 :76: "Energy and a gradient compose a flow. A gradient over space and one over time are different terms, because which state it is bonded to is part of what it is." Fig 13 :74 forbids a gradient bonding directly to another structure without a state.

An energy-producing chemistry is not a bare energy source. It is a spendable difference bonded to a conserved state, so it is a flow, and which state it is bonded to changes the term. A proton gradient across a membrane and a thermal cycle in a wet-dry sequence are different terms, not two spellings of one.

World corroboration (secondary): Deamer and Weber, "Bioenergetics and Life's Origins" (Cold Spring Harbor Perspectives in Biology, 2010): "The origin of life was marked when a rare few protocells happened to have the ability to capture energy from the environment to initiate catalyzed heterotrophic growth directed by heritable genetic information in the polymers." This is a near-verbatim statement of the three slots co-located. It corroborates the shape but supplies no figure authority.

**Refuter:** demonstrate a self-maintaining compartment with heredity and catalysis in which no spendable difference is coupled to any conserved state. That would break the gradient claim at the ground.
**Status:** `single-source` for the figure mapping (Fig 13), with independent world corroboration from Deamer and Weber 2010.

### F4 : Catalysis is a part that survives its own use; the compound must close a cycle

Fig 12 :119: "catalyst. A part that survives its own use. Spent and regenerating parts behave differently across uses, and nothing records which a term is." Fig 12 :121: "cycle. The output re-enters as input. The sign of the loop decides whether it closes, and a chain cannot close by construction."

A catalyst that is consumed on first use is not a catalyst under this definition. A mineral surface, a ribozyme or a metal ion qualifies because it persists across uses. But persistence alone is not enough: the whole compound must close a cycle, because a chain cannot close by construction. Genetic replication, metabolic regeneration and boundary growth are three loops that must re-enter as input to each other.

The "nothing records which a term is" clause is load bearing. In chemistry, a spent catalyst and a regenerated catalyst can leave the same end product; the difference is in the lineage, not the artifact. This is provenance at the molecular level.

World corroboration: Shirt-Ediss, Murillo-Sánchez and Ruiz-Mirazo, Beilstein Journal of Organic Chemistry (2017): "everything must come 'in the same package'". They argue for "dynamic, functionally integrated protocell systems ... as the proper units of prebiotic evolution" and name "Challenge 2: finding conditions and mechanisms for minimal functional integration."

**Refuter:** show a persistent protocell whose catalytic part is consumed on first use and not regenerated, or whose three functions form a chain that never re-enters as input.
**Status:** `single-source` for the figure definitions (Fig 12), corroborated independently by Shirt-Ediss et al. 2017.

### F5 : The three functions are each settled before the compound; recursion needs no new primitive

Fig 12 :104: "A settled molecule becomes an atom for whatever composes over it. So irreducible is a fact about a level and never about the world, and there is no bottom". Fig 13 :110: "And each composition becomes an atom for whatever composes over it."

Heredity, catalysis and energy flow are each observable and settled separately in the world. They can therefore serve as atoms at the protocell level. The protocell, once it persists, becomes an atom at the population level. No seventh primitive is needed to compose the compound; only the six and their bonds. That is the recursion Fig 12 names, not an import.

World corroboration: Schrum, Zhu and Szostak, "The Origins of Cellular Life" (Cold Spring Harbor Perspectives in Biology, 2010): "The integration of a dynamic fatty-acid compartment with robust, generalized genetic polymer replication would yield a laboratory model of a protocell with the potential for classical Darwinian biological evolution." Note the verb "integration": the two parts are already held and are being composed.

**Refuter:** show that one of the three functions requires a primitive outside the six-term set in order to be composed at the protocell level. Then this is an import, not a recursion, and the finding falls.
**Status:** `triangulated` across Fig 12 and the metalaws note (different statements of the same structural rule), with world corroboration from Schrum et al. 2010.

### F6 : The habitat is enumerated, never composed

Fig 11 :43: "The habitat is enumerated, never composed." Fig 11 :44: "List everything in contact with the residual, then what is in contact with those, and outward until the cascade stops mattering." Fig 11 :46: "conditions: light, water, temperature, season, terrain".

For this question the habitat includes wet-dry cycles, UV flux, mineral surfaces, hydrothermal settings, fatty acids, activated nucleotides, osmotic gradients and competing vesicle populations. These are read off the world, not derived from the figures. Fig 11 :49: "An actor's valence is relative to the residual, not a property it carries." A mineral surface helps replication and hinders release; the same actor changes sign with the residual.

Formula step 7 says SEARCHED first. Before importing a new mechanism, ask whether it is already in the closure and unfound. The habitat list is the habitat, not an imported atom.

**Refuter:** derive a habitat actor from the six terms alone rather than reading it off the world, or show that contact need not be enumerated to settle the residual.
**Status:** `single-source` for the figure rule (Fig 11), corroborated by Deamer 2024 (below) and by Shirt-Ediss et al. 2017.

### F7 : The framework returns a shape, not a mechanism name

Fig 9 :32: "you get a kind of thing, never the answer itself". Fig 6 :23: "No frame escapes its own closure. The bound moves only on contact with a frame whose closure differs." Fig 8 :50: "Convergent derivation and adopted import look identical in the contents."

Fig 13 :115: "The set is closed. The arrangements are not. That gap is why import exists." So the figures can say that a containing state with cross-kind composition is required, and that a spendable difference, a persistent catalyst and a heritable ordering must be present. They cannot say "wet-dry cycling did it" or "fatty acid vesicles did it." Those are names from the chemistry frame. They enter as imports with a source gate (provenance) and a shadow test (do they explain a gap we actually have?), and they are debited against independence.

World corroboration: Deamer, "Perspective: Protocells and the Path to Minimal Life" (Journal of Molecular Evolution, 2024): "The answer to this question is surprisingly simple. The same wet-dry cycles that drive polymerization of monomers also promote encapsulation if lipids are present." This is exactly the kind of candidate mechanism that must be provenanced, not derived. The figure verdict is that a mechanism of this shape is owed; it does not name it.

**Refuter:** derive a specific prebiotic mechanism from the six-term set with no contact with a chemistry frame. If that succeeds, the shape-not-a-name limit is wrong here.
**Status:** `triangulated` across Fig 9 and the canonical figures README ("a shape, not a name"), with world sources corroborating that candidate mechanisms exist but are external.

### F8 : The compound is settled only by an experiment, never by agreement

Fig 2 :29: "An anchor is whatever settles the question and does not change its answer because of what any frame thinks." Fig 2 :35: "Alignment is triangulation, not negotiation. A mirror cannot correct you." Fig 2 :45: "Frames range against the ground. They do not negotiate with it." Fig 10 :42: "A verifier that can reconstruct the claim cannot verify it."

The relevant anchor for ORI-0056 is a laboratory or field result: a protocell that shows heritable variation, catalytic persistence and energy capture in one compartment, with a measured failure mode. Agreement among origins-of-life researchers about the plausibility of the shape is a convention, not a verdict. Two reviews quoting the same experiments are one evidence pool talking (Fig 2 :40).

World corroboration: Szostak, Bartel and Luisi, "Synthesizing life" (Nature, 2001): "Advances in directed evolution and membrane biophysics make the synthesis of simple living cells, if not yet foreseeable reality, an imaginable goal." The words "imaginable goal" mark it as a target for the ground, not a settled result.

**Refuter:** settle the compound by vote or by prestige. If that works, Fig 2 is wrong. It does not.
**Status:** `triangulated` across Fig 2 (anchor) and Fig 10 (verifier non-access), two figures with distinct roles.

### F9 : Two currencies: bits to state the compound, actions to test it

Fig 12 :125: "A composition costs in two currencies, and they do not add." Fig 12 :126: "Bits price the description. Actions price finding out whether it holds."

A statement of CO-LOCATION is cheap: a containing state, three cross-kind bonds, an enumerated habitat. Testing it is expensive: years of laboratory work on vesicle dynamics, template copying and energy coupling. A system that holds one accounting will propose arrangements it cannot afford to test. The budget decides how much of the space is reached, and a budget is a filter for what gets tried, never a witness for whether the claim is true (Fig 9 :60).

**Refuter:** produce a single unit in which description cost and test cost are the same quantity. Then the two-currency claim fails.
**Status:** `single-source` (Fig 12).

## 4. Pre-registered refuter

Written before the conclusion, per the standing rule that a falsifier written before the run is a design instrument.

The CO-LOCATION shape is refuted if any of the following holds:

1. A self-maintaining co-location is demonstrated with no containing state (breaks Fig 13 :10 and F2).
2. A composition of a state directly with a state, or a structure directly with a structure, with no opposite-kind intermediary, is admitted by the framework and cannot be refused (breaks Fig 13 :74-75).
3. The three conditions turn out to be a partition, so one lever closes all three without touching their intersections (breaks Fig 9 :54-57 and F1).
4. An experiment shows a persistent compartment with heredity and catalysis but no spendable difference coupled to a conserved state (breaks F3).
5. An experiment shows a persistent compartment whose catalytic part is consumed on first use and never regenerated (breaks F4).
6. The compound is settled by agreement among overlapping frames with no independent ground contact (breaks Fig 2 :35, :40 and F8).

If any fires, the shape is withdrawn or narrowed. Until one is run, no part of this may be labelled `grounded`. Condition 6 is a standing discipline, not an empirical prediction.
