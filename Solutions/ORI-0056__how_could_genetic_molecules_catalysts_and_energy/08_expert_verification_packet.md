## 1. The claim, in one paragraph

The question of how genetic molecules, catalysts and energy-producing chemistry came to sit in one compartment is a union of three overlapping conditions, not one linear problem. The framework's minimum set requires a containing state, cross-kind composition among the three functions with a structure between any pair, a spendable difference bonded to a conserved state (a flow), a catalytic part that survives its own use, and a cycle whose output re-enters as input. The habitat is enumerated from the world rather than composed. Genetics-first, metabolism-first and compartment-first are isomers of this shape, same atoms arranged differently, and the figures do not select among them. Candidate mechanisms from the chemistry literature are provenanced imports, not derivations. What is offered is the shape any answer must have, refuters written in advance, and the instruments that would close it.

### C2 : chemistry-facing; checkable by an origins-of-life researcher against the laboratory record

- **Statement:** the energy slot is a flow (a spendable difference bonded to a conserved state), the catalytic slot is a part that survives its use, and the whole compound must close a cycle.
- **Source, verbatim:** Fig 13 :22 "a difference that can be spent". Fig 13 :76 "Energy and a gradient compose a flow. A gradient over space and one over time are different terms, because which state it is bonded to is part of what it is." Fig 12 :119 "catalyst. A part that survives its own use. Spent and regenerating parts behave differently across uses, and nothing records which a term is." Fig 12 :121 "cycle. The output re-enters as input. The sign of the loop decides whether it closes, and a chain cannot close by construction."
- **What to check:** is the figure definition of catalyst faithful to how you use the word? Does any current protocell system close a loop whose output re-enters as input, and does any system that fails the cycle test still persist? Are the proton-gradient and wet-dry-thermal cases genuinely different terms under :76, rather than two names for one?
- **Refuter:** a persistent compartment with heredity and catalysis but no spendable difference coupled to a conserved state; or a catalyst consumed on first use and never regenerated; or a chain that persists across generations without re-entering as input.
