# The Tether's answer on ORI-0056

**Mode: `specified` on the substantive claims, `general` on the structural shape, not `grounded`.** This is a shape and a route, not a result. Simulated derivation checked against the figures (`05_what_shape_means.md` §6), not a ground reading.

## 1. The residual it answers

Heritable molecules, catalysts and energy-producing chemistry each exist on their own in prebiotic chemistry. In every known cell they sit inside one boundary. The residual is the gap between "each exists" and "they compose into one unit that can be settled by a ground."

Fig 9 :54: "And when the split lands on a union rather than a partition, unbundle before you search again." This is a **union compound**: replication, catalysis and energy capture are overlapping conditions, and a case in two conditions is not a harder case of one. It has its own loop and its own ground. The three function slots are kept separate (Fig 1 :19: "The residual is indexed by object slot, not aggregated."), because averaging them into one "life emerged" residual would discard the only signal that says which slot is live.

## 2. The shape

1. **A containing state is required.** Fig 13 :10 lists "near, far, between, contained" as a state. Fig 13 :74: "Everything else is a composition across the two kinds, never within one." The boundary that gathers the functions is containment, not a bare list of molecules. Fig 4's "membrane" is a different thing: it is the filter between scales, and the two must not be conflated (strain and re-census in `01` F2).
2. **The three functions must compose across kinds, never bond directly to each other.** Fig 13 :75: "Two states never bond directly. It is not space-time but space, a structure, time; and two structures join only through a state." A genetic function and an energy function cannot compose as bare functions; a structure mediated by a state must sit between them.
3. **Energy-producing chemistry is a flow, not a source.** Fig 13 :22 defines a gradient as "a difference that can be spent", and :76 says "Energy and a gradient compose a flow." Which state the gradient is bonded to is part of the term, so a proton gradient across a boundary and a thermal wet-dry cycle are different molecules, not two spellings of one.
4. **Catalysis is persistence across uses.** Fig 12 :119: "catalyst. A part that survives its own use." A consumed accelerator is out. The "nothing records which a term is" clause means the difference between spent and regenerated lives in the lineage, not in the end product.
5. **The compound must close a cycle.** Fig 12 :121: "cycle. The output re-enters as input. The sign of the loop decides whether it closes, and a chain cannot close by construction." Growth alone is a chain. Growth plus template copying plus division, where the product re-enters as input, is a cycle.
6. **The habitat is enumerated, not composed.** Fig 11 :43: "The habitat is enumerated, never composed." Conditions and actors are read off the world, and an actor's valence is relative to the residual. Wet-dry cycles, UV flux, mineral surfaces, fatty acids and competing vesicles are habitat items, not derivations from the six-term set.
7. **The historical stories are isomers of the shape.** Fig 12 :117: "Same atoms, different bond, different substance. The difference is invisible in the formula and decisive in the behaviour." Genetics-first, metabolism-first and compartment-first share a broad atom set but differ in bond placement and dependency order. The dependency order licensed by the figures is partial, not total (see `06_isomers_and_order.md`).
8. **The framework returns a shape, not a name.** Fig 9 :32: "you get a kind of thing, never the answer itself." It does not say which polymer came first, which boundary chemistry won, or which setting hosted the transition. Names from the chemistry literature are imports with a source gate and a shadow test, carrying zero figure authority until an experiment settles them.

## 3. What it explicitly does not say

- Not a claim that any particular experiment has produced a living protocell. The literature itself says the gap remains; Deamer 2024 writes that "none of the laboratory simulations have faced the ultimate challenge of being demonstrated to function in natural conditions."
- Not a claim that fatty acids, RNA, montmorillonite, hydrothermal vents or wet-dry cycles are the answer. Those are world imports, provenanced in `10_source_ledger.csv`, not figure derivations.
- Not a total order. The figures do not select genetics-first, metabolism-first or compartment-first.
- Not a new primitive. The six-term set is closed; the arrangements over it are not (Fig 13 :115).
- Not `grounded`. No measurement was taken and no experiment was run.

## 4. What would settle it: the ground

An instrument or experiment that can discriminate among the candidate sources, not a further argument. From *The Hard Problem is About Source*: an instrument that reads green "cannot comment on the source"; the spectrometer resolved it. Here:

1. **Containment test:** is there a self-maintaining co-location with no containing state? If yes, F2 breaks.
2. **Cross-kind test:** can a state bond directly to a state, or a structure to a structure, with no opposite-kind intermediary, in a way the framework must admit and cannot refuse? If yes, F2 breaks.
3. **Union test:** does one lever close all three conditions without touching their intersections? If yes, F1 breaks and the residual is a partition.
4. **Flow test:** is there a persistent compartment with heredity and catalysis but no spendable difference coupled to a conserved state? If yes, F3 breaks.
5. **Cycle test:** is there a persistent compartment whose catalytic part is consumed on first use and never regenerated, or whose functions form a chain that never re-enters as input? If yes, F4 breaks.
6. **Discriminating instrument:** an experiment in natural conditions that distinguishes encapsulation-after-polymerisation from co-assembly on mineral surfaces from coupled bottom-up chemistry. That would name a route. Until then, the route stays below the union horizon.

## 5. The honest stop

**Unreached, not unreachable.** The six-term set is "a human's careful guess at reality's compression axes; another sensorium's minimum set need not be those six" (*They're Scanning Books When They Should Be Studying Nature*). Declaring the primitives makes the choice visible, not neutral. The answer stops at the limit of the available instruments and records the lineage so the stop is checkable. *How Intelligence Actually Works*: "The recursion converges toward indiscernibility, and indiscernibility is not identity." A candidate route matching every current observation is still a projection until something can refuse it.

The round trip carries a nonzero gap `R_T`: what is sent up is the verbatim figure set, what comes down is a claim about a wet laboratory system, and the coarser description cannot hold the bond types, the refusal ordering and the provenance of the imported mechanism details. That gap is the measurement of what was given up, not an error to erase.

## 6. One-paragraph version for outreach

> The Tether does not say how life began and does not name a molecule or a setting. It says that the question is a union of three overlapping conditions, not one problem, so a single lever on replication, catalysis or energy capture leaves the intersections standing. It says that a containing state is required, that the three functions must compose across kinds with a structure between any pair, that the energy part is a spendable difference bonded to a conserved state rather than a bare source, that a catalyst must survive its own use, and that the whole compound must close a cycle whose output re-enters as input. It enumerates the habitat instead of composing it, reads genetics-first, metabolism-first and compartment-first as isomers of one shape, and refuses to choose among them. The chemistry literature supplies candidate mechanisms, but those are imports with provenance, not derivations. What is offered is the shape any answer must have, four refuters written in advance, and the instruments that would close it. The route stays open until the ground settles it.
