## Wider literature read for this question

These are secondary, world-frame sources. They corroborate the figure-derived shape but never overrule it. Their agreement with one another is partly a shared evidence pool, so it is recorded as such, not counted as independent witnesses.

1. **Deamer, D. and Weber, A. L., "Bioenergetics and Life's Origins", Cold Spring Harbor Perspectives in Biology, 2010.** Opened at https://pmc.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/articles/PMC2828274. Key line: "The origin of life was marked when a rare few protocells happened to have the ability to capture energy from the environment to initiate catalyzed heterotrophic growth directed by heritable genetic information in the polymers." Also: "there were no protein enzymes on the prebiotic Earth, so simpler catalysts like mineral surfaces, metal ions, and small polymers presumably acted as catalysts." Predicts the three-slot co-location shape. Corroborates F1, F3 and F4.
2. **Schrum, J. P., Zhu, T. F. and Szostak, J. W., "The Origins of Cellular Life", Cold Spring Harbor Perspectives in Biology, 2010.** Opened at https://pmc.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/articles/PMC2926753. Key line: "The integration of a dynamic fatty-acid compartment with robust, generalized genetic polymer replication would yield a laboratory model of a protocell with the potential for classical Darwinian biological evolution." Also notes that the fatty-acid membrane is permeable to small polar molecules and that competition among vesicles can couple replication to growth. Predicts that integration is the residual, not any single polymer. Corroborates F1 and F5.
3. **Shirt-Ediss, B., Murillo-Sánchez, S. and Ruiz-Mirazo, K., "Framing major prebiotic transitions as stages of protocell development", Beilstein Journal of Organic Chemistry, 2017.** Opened at https://www.beilstein-journals.org/bjoc/articles/13/135. Key line: "everything must come 'in the same package'". Also: "dynamic, functionally integrated protocell systems (rather than complex reaction networks in bulk solution, sets of artificially evolvable replicating molecules, or even these same replicating molecules encapsulated in passive compartments) as the proper units of prebiotic evolution." Predicts a union compound with a functional-integration bottleneck. Corroborates F1, F4 and F6.
4. **Deamer, D., "Perspective: Protocells and the Path to Minimal Life", Journal of Molecular Evolution, 2024.** Opened at https://pmc.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/articles/PMC11458682/. Key line: "The same wet-dry cycles that drive polymerization of monomers also promote encapsulation if lipids are present." Lists six open questions including "How did a primitive metabolism become established in protocell populations?" Predicts a candidate mechanism. This is a world import, not a figure derivation, so it is provenanced rather than asserted.
5. **Szostak, J. W., Bartel, D. P. and Luisi, P. L., "Synthesizing life", Nature, 2001.** Opened at https://www.nature.com/articles/35053176. Abstract: "Advances in directed evolution and membrane biophysics make the synthesis of simple living cells, if not yet foreseeable reality, an imaginable goal." The phrase "imaginable goal" is the honest status: a target for the ground, not a settled result. Corroborates F8.

**Overlap note.** Sources 1 and 4 share Deamer as an author; sources 2 and 5 share Szostak and the same research tradition. Their agreement about protocell integration is expected from a common evidence pool and carries less independent weight than their different venues suggest (Fig 2 :40). They are still useful as a check on the shape, because they come from a different field from the figures.

This follows Fig 10 :52 "Bring a characterised residual, never the source" and Fig 2 :45 "Frames range against the ground. They do not negotiate with it." **Stage 2, opt-in only.** If they are curious after recording a stage-1 verdict, offer the three framework links. Their later agreement is never pooled as corroboration, because outside-frame agreement after reading the source is one evidence pool talking (Fig 2 :40).

The architecture article's warning that outputs "could never be verified if the solutions presented were not contrived to fit the formula" applies to them as much as to this folder.

**Hard precondition.** Stages 1 to 3 only work if the stage-1 output survives. The four refuters in `02_outreach_email.md` are live. If one fires, stop and narrow the claim before any contact.
