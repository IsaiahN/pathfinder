## VERDICT (from the figures)

The shape survives, the route names do not. The residual is a union of three overlapping conditions inside a containing state. The three functions must compose across kinds with a structure between any pair; the energy part is a spendable difference bonded to a conserved state, hence a flow; the catalytic part survives its own use; and the compound must close a cycle whose output re-enters as input. The habitat is enumerated from the world. Genetics-first, metabolism-first and compartment-first are isomers of this shape with a shared functional group, and the figures select none of them. Candidate mechanisms from the chemistry literature are provenanced imports with zero figure authority until an experiment settles them. This is a `specified`, ungrounded result, not a historical reconstruction. The strongest figure-grounded point is the union claim: a single lever on one condition leaves the intersections standing.

- **cost if wrong:** bounded and locatable. The packet is mode-labelled, states four refuters before the conclusion and records the lineage, so an error is findable rather than invisible. The real cost is spending the independence of outside readers by sending a named route instead of a shape, which Fig 2 :42 prices: "Buy independence in proportion to the cost of being wrong." That cost is avoidable by leading with the union claim and the refuters.
- **undoable:** yes. The route names are withheld rather than asserted, so no claim about a specific mechanism has to be withdrawn. A future experiment can add a route without touching the shape. A sent claim can be superseded by a corrected packet, and no primitive has to be withdrawn.
- **reversed by:** a ground reading, not agreement. A demonstration of self-maintaining co-location with no containing state; an admissible direct state-state or structure-structure bond; a single lever that closes all three conditions without touching their intersections; a persistent compartment with no coupled spendable difference or with a consumed catalyst that is never regenerated; or a source-discriminating experiment in natural conditions that names a route. Until one of these fires, the verdict stands as `candidate`.
