**Question:** How could genetic molecules, catalysts, and energy-producing chemistry become gathered in the same compartment?

## What is being decided

The residual is a co-location failure. Heritable information, catalysis, and energy capture are each observable on their own. In every known cell they sit inside one boundary. The question asks what composition brings them together and keeps them there. Each candidate answer leads somewhere different: a genetics-first route, a metabolism-first route, and a compartment-first route each price a different dependency chain and imply a different first experiment. An answer would look like a shape plus a route: which kinds must be present, which bonds may join them, which boundary holds them, what must be enumerated in the habitat, and what an instrument would have to show to settle it.

## What is deliberately not here

- No personal emails of named individuals.
- No ranking of people by how likely they are to agree.
- No bulk-send plan.
- No claim that the figures name a chemical mechanism. They return a shape, not a name.

Three reasons, in the figures' own words:

1. **Agreement is not a verdict.** Fig 2 :35: "Alignment is triangulation, not negotiation. A mirror cannot correct you." A list selected for receptiveness manufactures the correlation that disqualifies its agreement.
2. **The outside checker must not hold the interiors.** Fig 10 :42: "A verifier that can reconstruct the claim cannot verify it." The first contact carries the residual and its refuter, never the framework.
3. **Prestige is not ground.** *The Recursive Self-Improvement Trap*: "Prestige can fund an action. It can give an action a budget to be tried. But it cannot settle an action. Only the ground can settle it."

Legally: unsolicited bulk email selected by receptiveness breaches GDPR and CAN-SPAM style rules in many jurisdictions. Public opt-in submission venues avoid that failure as well as the structural one.
