## 1. A shape is a constraint you can state before you can name it

The canonical figures README states the limit: the framework returns "what a missing thing would have to look like (a shape, not a name)". Fig 9 :32: "you get a kind of thing, never the answer itself". Fig 13: "It cannot say which primitive is missing, because naming it would mean already holding it."

From *The Library of Babel*: "You can cut your search short if the set of books returned is small and descriptive enough that it provides a general shape of the answer that satisfies your question."

For ORI-0056, a **shape** is the form of the required co-location that can be stated without naming a molecule, a setting or a historical route. The shape is: a union of three conditions, a containing state, cross-kind composition, a spendable difference bonded to a conserved state, a catalyst that survives its use, a closed cycle, and an enumerated habitat. "Fatty-acid vesicle with an encapsulated RNA replicase in a wet-dry hot spring" is a name, not a shape.

## 2. How the shape is obtained

- *Library of Babel* uses proximity: "With each new term gained, you narrow the space by definition and deepen your vocabulary." The question acts as the anchor: "This anchor is provided by your own question".
- *Library of Babel* on bundles: "Perhaps your problem was a bundle or conflation of several problems masquerading as one?" That is exactly the union reading of F1, reached from an essay rather than from Fig 9. Two independent routes to the same domain boundary, but both are inside the same framework evidence pool.
- Fig 6 :30: "The description travels. Fails by being sharpened into terms only we can read. Cast it in effect terms." Fig 9 :30-31: "your words are borrowed too, so describe the effect rather than the cause". Hence the residual was stated as co-location failure, not as "how did RNA first replicate".
- *Decoding Tacit Knowledge*: "When contact isn't possible, the best you can send is a plain description of what you'd expect to see, because anyone can check that against the world." This is why `02_outreach_email.md` sends the four refuters before the links.

## 3. What licenses a name

Only two things, both outside the shape:

1. **The ground settles it.** Formula step 5: "the ground settles it. Verified only by what pays." The canonical README says the framework "stops at the limit of what the available instruments can distinguish, and records the lineage so the stop is honest."
2. **A provenanced import from a frame whose closure differs** (Fig 6 :23, Fig 8 :50), with a source gate and a shadow test.

For this question, the candidate names come from Deamer and Weber 2010, Schrum et al. 2010, Shirt-Ediss et al. 2017 and Deamer 2024: wet-dry cycling, fatty-acid vesicles, montmorillonite clay, proton gradients, hydrothermal fields. Each is a real result from a chemistry frame. None is derived from the six-term set, so each is an import and must be recorded with provenance.

*They're Scanning Books When They Should Be Studying Nature* sharpens the point: "The real import is not a new term in the existing space. It is a new dimension of the space." And: "A minimum viable set of six terms is a human's careful guess at reality's compression axes; another sensorium's minimum set need not be those six." The six are a visible guess, not nature's own list. Turning one of the chemistry names into *the* answer would make a visible choice invisible.

## 4. Why the source question matters for protocells

*The Hard Problem is About Source* gives the mechanism. Metamerism: an instrument reads green and "cannot comment on the source" - one green light, or yellow plus blue, or RGB, or CMYK, or a scheme not yet discovered. "If several paths create the same behavior, then behavior is a projection with lossy information". It was the spectrometer, not an argument, that resolved it: "It was proven wrong not by argument, but by the arrival of a better instrument."

The protocell case has the same structure. A vesicle that grows, divides and copies an internal template is a reading. Several different source histories can produce it: encapsulation after polymer formation, co-assembly on clay, growth inside mineral pores, or a fully bottom-up coupled chemistry. The artifact does not determine the source. Deamer 2024 explicitly notes the ambiguities: the site is uncertain, nucleotides are not present in meteorites, and "none of the laboratory simulations have faced the ultimate challenge of being demonstrated to function in natural conditions." What is owed is a discriminating instrument, not a name.

*How Intelligence Actually Works* adds the stopping rule: "The recursion converges toward indiscernibility, and indiscernibility is not identity." A candidate route that matches every current observation is still a projection until something can refuse it.

## 5. Answer to the shape question for ORI-0056

**What does the framework say the answer must look like?** It must be a union compound of three conditions inside a containing state, with cross-kind composition among them, a spendable difference bonded to a conserved state, a catalytic part that survives its use, a cycle that re-enters as input, and an enumerated habitat. It must be stated in effect terms so an outside checker can test it without holding the framework's interior.

**What does the framework not say?** Which polymer came first, which boundary chemistry is correct, which environment hosted it, and whether any particular laboratory system is the historical route. Those are world-frame names. They are admitted only as provenanced imports with a shadow test, and they carry zero figure authority until an experiment settles them.

**Does this folder commit the forbidden error?** No, with one standing caveat. It never names a mechanism as the answer. The caveat is that the six-term vocabulary itself is a human guess, and the whole shape inherits that guess, so the verdict is `specified` and revisable, not `grounded`.

## 6. Two consequences for the package

**Prestige is not action.** *The Recursive Self-Improvement Trap*: "Prestige can fund an action. It can give an action a budget to be tried. But it cannot settle an action. Only the ground can settle it. The Ground Maintainer must be blind to prestige." A list of famous origins-of-life names who agree with the shape is the named collapse. The legitimate ask is to give the claim a budget to be tried and let an experiment settle it.

**This audit is a simulation, not a ground reading.** Same essay: "LLMs can quickly simulate an approximate answer/output with the formula found in the architecture post... However, the truly testable output would need to be derived from the real architecture or working the formula yourself." The canonical article is blunter: outputs "could never be verified if the solutions presented were not contrived to fit the formula." So `01_figure_grounded_audit.md` and this file are simulated derivations checked for internal consistency. They are not tests of the claim and must be labelled as such.

## 7. Correction kept visible

An earlier draft of this file said the shape-not-a-name rule forbad naming any of the six terms. That was overstated. The README's phrase governs a *missing* primitive, one not held in Γ. Gradient, Spectrum and Network are already among the six. Naming one as the slot occupant would be a binding from the chemistry frame, not the invention of a seventh term. The real error would be claiming that binding moves the wall (Fig 6: "IMPORT adds an atom" - this one adds none). The wet-dry and lipid names are different: they are not among the six, so they really are imports and really do need a source gate. Both readings are recorded, neither silently erased.
