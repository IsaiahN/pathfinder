## What is deliberately not here

- No personal emails of named individuals.
- No ranking of people by how likely they are to agree.
- No bulk-send plan.

Three figure-grounded reasons:

1. A room does not supply an anchor. "*same closure, handed along; agreement compounds, evidence does not*" (Fig 7 :12-13). A list selected for receptiveness is a room, not a ground.
2. Correlation disqualifies a verdict. "*agreement about the world is worthless when the agreeing frames share an evidence pool*" (Fig 2 :40). Selecting recipients for likely agreement manufactures exactly the correlation that makes their agreement worthless.
3. Asking for trust before a check spends the only thing an outside frame contributes. "*A verifier that can reconstruct the claim cannot verify it*" (Fig 10 :42).

Legally: scraping personal emails for unsolicited bulk outreach breaches GDPR and CAN-SPAM style rules in many jurisdictions. That is a practical failure on top of the structural one.
