## Independent domain census

I redid the census from the residual line alone, before using the primary's conclusions, in the WATSON order: R by arity, symmetry and scale; one line per domain with actors, currency and anchor; partition or union; order by dependency.

- **R (arity / symmetry / scale).** A behaviour change in one actor class plus a network-level reshaping. Arity: two conditions, so a **union compound**, not one lever (Fig 9 :55-58). Symmetry: a predation or pollination interaction is directed, so operand order matters; the actor classes are not interchangeable. Scale: individual behaviour, interaction graph, ecosystem state. Anchor: measured interaction turnover or state change. Agreement is not an anchor.
- **Domain 1, habitat / contact.** Actors: predators, prey, pollinators, plants, competitors, conditions. Currency: contact, cascade mass, valence relative to R. Anchor: measured interaction frequencies and outcomes. Question type: enumeration, not composition.
- **Domain 2, structural / substrate.** Actors: the six terms, network as structure, states as space, time and energy, plus the bonds. Currency: composition across kinds and isomerism. Anchor: the six-term set as metalaw. Question type: composition, and an overlap with domain 1 at the network, hence a compound with its own loop.
- **Domain 3, empirical ecology.** Actors: experiments and censuses. Currency: observations and residuals. Anchor: each experiment in its own system. Multiple distinct systems give genuinely low overlap between sources.
- **Domain 4, verification / management.** Actors: reviewers, managers, maintainers. Currency: agreement versus ground. Anchor: the ground. In this simulated run all four domains draw on one evidence pool, so this domain's independence is zero.

Order by dependency: name R before enumerating (Fig 9 :27-28); enumerate before composing (Fig 11 :43-44); name states before measuring (Fig 13 :29); refuse before mint where a refusal is owed (`THE_FORMULA.md` step 2).

My census matches the primary's four domains and its union reading. Where the primary derived from Fig 11's habitat laws, I derived from Fig 9's union/partition split and Fig 12's bond definition. Different starting figures, same domain boundaries: low overlap between routes, so the agreement is weak but not circular within the framework. It is still one evidence pool.

## Round trip gap R_T

The Symbols table defines R_T as `gap(x, (T_E ∘ T_A)(x))`, the gap between what was sent up and what came back down. Fig 4 :30 requires it to be measured rather than assumed. I ran the round trip on the condensation the folder actually uses.

1. **Sent up (x):** the verbatim figure set, chiefly Fig 11 :43-54 (enumeration, cascade, valence relative to R), Fig 12 :117 (isomer), Fig 13 :74-75 (cross-kind composition).
2. **Abstraction (T_A):** the shorthand carried in `01` F10 and `07` section 2: a behaviour change is a contact change; the cascade can run several steps out; enumerate outward; actor classes are roles; network is a structure; same species with different wiring is a different substance.
3. **Concretisation (T_E):** a concrete study claim of the form "behaviour change X in system S reshaped network Y with cascade length Z".

**R_T is nonzero.** Three things the shorthand holds do not survive the trip back down:

- the **dependence of valence on the chosen residual**. The shorthand is easily read as reifying "predator" or "pollinator" as a fixed sign, which Fig 11 :49-51 forbids;
- the **requirement to name states**, where, when and what is spent, which the shorthand compresses into "network reshaped";
- the **niche/habitat refusal** and the **shape-versus-coefficient gap**. Neither is carried by the shorthand, so a reader can take the condensation as promising a coefficient and a niche equivalence that the figures do not supply.

One thing survives cleanly: a behaviour change reshapes a network through a cascade of changed contacts, and the consequential contact can sit several steps from the actor that moved. That is the residue worth sending.

R_T was **not** measured numerically, because no ground reading exists in this derivation. It is recorded qualitatively as the enumerated set of dropped constraints. Treating the formula as identical to the figures would set R_T to zero and would be the dead-reckoning error Fig 4 :30 names. The honest form is a lossy condensation with a recorded gap, which is what `09_watson_answer.md` states.
