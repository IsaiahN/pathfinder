## Two things in the wider literature that bear directly on this question

1. **Rewiring dominates turnover.** CaraDonna et al. found week-to-week turnover of plant-pollinator interactions was high and dominated by *"interaction rewiring (the reassembly of interactions among species)"*, with phenology and relative abundance constraining it. This is the bond-change picture of F6, arrived at independently in a subalpine system. It is corroboration, not a derivation from the figures.
2. **Behaviour change can move ecosystem state without a density change.** Sheridan and Randell found that a damage cue, not predator presence, reduced urchin exposure, grazing and kelp mortality, and that adding drift kelp overrode the cue. This is the valence-relative and contact-dependent claim of F1 and F4, measured in a kelp forest. It is a different evidence pool from the plant-pollinator work, so the two together are worth more than either alone, though still only one witness per system.

## Distribution: offer, do not push

Figure-grounded reasons:

- **Fig 4 :22-25** - *"Only methods cross up. Only head starts come down."* and *"A recording carried upward looks like knowledge and is a description of one occasion."* Sending the full architecture before anyone has described a residual sends a recording. Lead with the shape claim and its refuter, and offer links after.
- **Fig 10 :42** - *"A verifier that can reconstruct the claim cannot verify it."* A recipient who has absorbed the framework before the check has joined the frame being checked.
- **Fig 9 :27-28** - *"describe it before you go looking, or any frame you pick will seem to fit."* Ask what they would expect to see if the cascade claim were true before giving them the article.
- **Fig 2 :40** - *"agreement about the world is worthless when the agreeing frames share an evidence pool."* A send selected for people who already agree produces a convention, not corroboration.

**Recommended order in a reply:** (1) the shape claim and its refuter, (2) the question of what the recipient would count as a failure, (3) only then the links, with the note that the figures and not the summary are the authority. Record the stage-1 verdict before step 3.

## The two-stage offer

1. **Stage 1:** validate the output against the ground. This is Fig 10 :52, *"Bring a characterised residual, never the source,"* and Fig 2 :45, *"Frames range against the ground. They do not negotiate with it."*
2. **Stage 2, opt-in only:** if curious, read the theory that produced it. The stage-1 verdict must be recorded first, and later agreement is never pooled as corroboration.
3. **Stage 3:** offer the formula as a method to reuse. This is permitted because a generator can be given away without giving away an answer (Fig 4 :22). Each user still has to state a ground and write a refuter before running it, because `THE_FORMULA.md` "Direction" says a frame cannot check the direction of its own question.

**Hard precondition:** stages 1 to 3 only work if the stage-1 output survives. The output as drafted in `01` does not survive all five refuters yet: none of them has been run against a ground. That is why this folder states mode and non-claims rather than presenting a result.
