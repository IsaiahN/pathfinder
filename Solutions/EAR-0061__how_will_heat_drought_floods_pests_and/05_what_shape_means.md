## 4. Why "which stressor wins" is the specific forbidden error

*The Hard Problem is About Source* (carried) gives the mechanism. An instrument reads green and *"cannot comment on the source"*; several sources can give the same reading. *"If several paths create the same behavior, then behavior is a projection with lossy information."* It was the spectrometer, not an argument, that resolved it.

Here the analogy is exact. A yield loss is a reading. Drought, heat, waterlogging, pest herbivory and degraded soil can each produce a low yield; a single yield reading cannot name which path produced it. Ribeiro et al. (2020) found that in compound dry and hot events *"the likelihood of crop loss is driven primarily by drought stress rather than by heat stress"*, while Romero et al. (2019) found hydrological stress dominated a biofilm response but the interaction sign changed with exposure duration. Those are source claims from projections, and they are context-specific. What is owed is a factorial discriminator, not a universal ranking.

*The Recursive Self-Improvement Trap* (opened this session): *"the output is only as good as the validator. The agent, or the tool, or the frame, or the theory, cannot validate itself."* A single-cause story read off an aggregate yield is a self-validation.
