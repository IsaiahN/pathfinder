## QUESTION

## DOMAIN CENSUS

R by arity, symmetry and scale, then one line per domain with actors / currency / anchor, partition or union, and order by dependency.

- **R (the residual).** The combined yield deviation that a single-stressor model fails to predict when the five conditions co-occur. Arity: five operands, so a multi-operand composition and a bond-type question. Symmetry: additivity would make `+` symmetric with 120 spellings and one term (Fig 12 :92); reported path dependence on timing, severity and stage means the governing bonds are ordered or comparative. Scale: first order at the crop slot and field, second order at the region, third order at the food system.
- **D1 Crop physiology.** Actors: crop genotype, heat, water deficit, excess water, soil biota, pests and pathogens. Currency: biomass, yield, harvest index. Anchor: a measured harvest in a factorial field trial. Union of conditions, per slot.
- **D2 Agroecosystem habitat.** Actors: conditions (temperature, water, soil, season, terrain) and relations (pollination, predation, competition, dependency). Currency: ecosystem services and disservices. Anchor: the same non-updating harvest ground. Union with a contact cascade; the residual can be three steps out.
- **D3 Climate and atmosphere frame.** Actors: CO2 concentration, temperature trend, precipitation distribution, extreme-event frequency. Currency: forcing, concentration, probability. Anchor: the instrumental record, which does not update on crop models. A different closure; its binding into D1 needs a source gate and shadow test.
- **D4 Verification and model frame.** Actors: crop models, FACE and T-FACE trials, remote sensing, statistical yield records. Currency: residuals and agreement. Anchor: measured yield, never agreement. Models sharing one FACE corpus are one evidence pool.
- **D5 The intersections.** Actors: heatÃ-drought, droughtÃ-flood, droughtÃ-pest, soilÃ-water, pestÃ-heat. Currency: the joint yield deviation. Anchor: a factorial interaction term measured at the ground. Not a list; each intersection is a specific compound.
- **Order by dependency.** Perceive each condition at the slot; acquire terms for what it affords; state what counts as a joint yield loss; plan an intervention; carry what worked. The break is between vocabulary and objective, where no instrument exists for the joint case. Describe the residual before choosing a frame (Fig 9 :27-28).

Strain test applied: the apparent conflict between Fig 11 :51 (no standing list of good actors) and practical adaptation advice is dissolved by re-census. Fig 11 governs valence relative to a residual; the advice is a binding relative to a yield-stability residual at one site, not a standing category. The apparent conflict between Fig 13's cross-kind composition rule and heat plus drought as two states is dissolved by re-census: Fig 13 governs the six physical states and structures, while Fig 11 enumerates habitat conditions and relations. Different objects, no contradiction.

## VERDICT (from the figures)

The shape survives; the single number does not. The combined effect is indexed per crop, region and growth stage (Fig 1 :19, :22). The five conditions form a union with binding intersections, not a partition (Fig 9 :55-58), and the joining bond is typed: `â†’` where timing or duration carries the result and `â‹›` where severity crosses a threshold, with `+` as a testable null (Fig 12 :5, :92, :87). Same conditions in a different order or at a different severity are a different substance over a shared atom set (Fig 12 :117). The path is a contact cascade in which the crop-state-to-pest link can be three steps out (Fig 11 :52-53), and no condition carries a standing valence (Fig 11 :49, :51). The anchor is a factorial harvest measurement with a reachable channel (Fig 2 :29; Fig 10 :24), and adaptation is permanent work, not a solved state (Fig 11 :63). The coefficient for a named crop is a declared `gap` of the unreachable-at-this-budget kind. Mode `specified` on the domains, `general` on the shape, not `grounded`; the whole folder is a simulated derivation checked for internal consistency, not a ground reading.

- **cost if wrong:** bounded and locatable. The packet states mode, five refuters and provenance before the conclusion, so an error is findable. The larger cost is handing a global number or a standing ranking to a recipient who could have checked a shape, which spends the independence Fig 2 :42 prices: "Buy independence in proportion to the cost of being wrong." That cost is avoidable by leading with the shape claim and the refuter.
- **undoable:** yes. The global ranking and the additive default are removable without touching the derivation; `07_the_tether_answer.md` and `08_expert_verification_packet.md` already remove them. A sent claim can be superseded by the corrected packet, and no habitat condition has to be withdrawn, only its standing valence.
- **reversed by:** a ground reading, not agreement. A stage-resolved full-factorial experiment showing exact additivity across all five for all crops would break the typed-bond claim; one lever clearing every intersection would break the union claim; one yield number predicting all five without per-slot structure would break the indexing claim; a standing harmful or beneficial list established at the ground would break the valence claim; and a measurement showing the channel still reachable after the calibration climate was exceeded would settle the channel question. Until one fires, the verdict stands as `candidate`.
