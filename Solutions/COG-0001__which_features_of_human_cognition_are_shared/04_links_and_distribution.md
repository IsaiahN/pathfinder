## What in the wider literature bears on the question

The world sources are secondary. They corroborate the shape or reveal a conflict; they never decide a figure verdict.

1. **Universals lists exist, but lists are not mechanisms.** Brown's hundreds of items and Christakis's social universals give candidate content. The same article records the non-nativist explanation: repeated presence does not establish a common mechanism. This is exactly Fig 8 :51 territory: *"Convergent derivation and adopted import look identical in the contents. Only the record of where each came from separates them"* A list cannot tell us which.
2. **Population variation reaches basic domains.** Henrich, Heine and Norenzayan 2010 review visual perception, fairness, cooperation, spatial reasoning, categorization, moral reasoning, reasoning styles and self-concepts, and conclude there are *"no obvious a priori grounds for claiming that a particular behavioral phenomenon is universal based on sampling from a single subpopulation."* This corroborates F1 and F9 (per slot, no pooled shortcut). It does not refute the figure verdict, because the figure verdict names no feature.
3. **A contested domain is still one evidence pool.** Ekman's cross-cultural recognition work supports a universality reading; Russell 1994, Nelson and Russell 2013 and Jack et al. 2012 criticize the method (posed expressions, forced choice, relative judgment). Under Fig 2 :31 these are not two independent frames just because they disagree. They are recorded as one contested pool, one witness.
4. **Language gives the clean functional-group case.** Universal grammar proposes shared constraints with language-specific settings; Evans and Levinson 2009 argue the universals are a *"myth"* and that typology finds *"a bewildering range of diverse languages"* from which *"generalizations are really quite hard to extract."* Sampson goes further and calls the theory unfalsifiable. The conflict sends the census back to section 1; it does not flip the figure verdict, which is only that a constraint kind and a variable member are distinguishable.
5. **Ethnography supports the union directly.** Berlin and Kay find a repeatable order of color-term acquisition, while individual languages sit at different stages. Ethnomathematics finds counting everywhere but bases, number words and finger systems that differ sharply. These are the two halves of the union, present in the same domain.
6. **Ethology supplies the low-overlap frame.** Fixed action patterns are species-characteristic and *"independent of experience"*, while number sense appears across mammals and birds. This corroborates Fig 11 :39 that capability is a property of agent and habitat. It is a separate evidence pool from the human cross-cultural literature, so it buys independence.

## Distribution rules

- **Fig 4 :22**, *"Only methods cross up. Only head starts come down."* Send the residual, the shape claim and the refuter. The full framework is offered only after stage 1 is recorded.
- **Fig 10 :42**, *"A verifier that can reconstruct the claim cannot verify it."* A recipient who has absorbed the whole framework before judging has joined the frame being checked.
- **Fig 9 :54**, *"And when the split lands on a union rather than a partition, unbundle before you search again."* Do not send one grand "universal versus variation" claim. Send the domain-specific shape.
- **Fig 2 :40**, *"disqualifying for a verdict: agreement about the world is worthless when the agreeing frames share an evidence pool"* No list ranked by likely agreement, no bulk send, no pooled endorsements.
- **Fig 10 :59**, *"an interest in the outcome is unavoidable, an interest in the reading is disqualifying."* Declare the stake in one line; do not ask for trust.
- **Fig 9 :60**, *"Use filters for the budget and witnesses for the verdict."* Journal scope and audience size choose where to spend a submission budget; they do not make the claim true.

## The two-stage offer

1. **Stage 1.** Send the residual in effect terms, the shape claim with its mode, and the pre-registered refuter. Ask: what would you count as a failure here? Record the answer before any framework material is shown.
2. **Stage 2, opt-in only.** If the recipient is curious, then and only then send the figure links and the four essays. The figures are the authority; these notes are not.
3. **Stage 3, opt-in again.** Offer the formula as a method to reuse. Each user writes their own refuter and states their own mode. A reported failure is more useful than a reported success.

Nothing is sent by this agent. Hosting and sending are the user's to do. Prefer a public submission route or an opt-in download page over an attachment pushed to a list.
