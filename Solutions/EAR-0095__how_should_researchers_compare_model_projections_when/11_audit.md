## Independent domain census (written from the residual line alone)

I redid the census before reading the primary's conclusions, starting only from the question and the effect terms: a set of models disagrees about probability, timing and severity; what procedure should a researcher use to compare them?

- **R (arity / symmetry / scale).** The gap between projections and observations. Arity: three named conditions, so a **union compound** (Fig 9 :4-5, :57), not one hard question. Symmetry: asymmetric; the frames are distinct and the three axes are not interchangeable. Scale: per slot, meaning region, variable and horizon. A global number is a description, not an error signal.
- **Domain 1, the models as frames.** Actors: models, ensemble members, shared code and tuning history. Currency: projections. Anchor: none of their own; must range against D2. Union of frames with unequal independence.
- **Domain 2, the observational ground.** Actors: instruments, reanalyses, proxies. Currency: observations. Anchor: the ground, which must not update. Partition boundary between measured and predicted.
- **Domain 3, the comparison and decision frame.** Actors: researchers, assessment bodies. Currency: agreement and residuals. Anchor: the ground, never agreement. In this simulated run all three domains draw on one evidence pool, so this domain's independence is zero.

Order by dependency: describe R before choosing a frame; split the union before searching; range each frame against the ground before reading agreement; establish independence before discounting agreement; let the ground decide last. My census matches the primary's three domains and union reading. I reached it from Fig 9's union/partition split and Fig 2's anchor rule rather than from Fig 1's slot indexing. Different starting figures, same boundaries: low overlap between routes, so the agreement is weak but not circular within the framework. It is still one pool.

## Round trip gap R_T

The Symbols table defines R_T as the gap between what was sent up and what came back down, `gap(x, (T_E ∘ T_A)(x))`, measuring what the coarser description cannot hold. Fig 4 :30 requires it to be measured rather than assumed. I ran the round trip on the condensation this folder actually uses.

1. **Sent up (x):** the verbatim figure set, chiefly Fig 1 :19/:22/:28 (slot-indexed residual), Fig 2 :31-32/:40/:46 (independence and separate ranging), Fig 9 :4-5/:57 (split the union), Fig 12 :125/:130 (two currencies), Fig 13 :134 (unreachable is a verdict).
2. **Abstraction (T_A):** the shorthand in `07_the_tether_answer.md` §2: index by slot, unbundle three axes, range each model against a fixed ground, discount dependence, keep separate accounts, report unreachable.
3. **Concretisation (T_E):** an actual comparison of a real CMIP ensemble with a chosen weighting, a chosen independence metric and a decision about one region and one horizon.

**R_T is nonzero.** Three things the shorthand holds do not survive the trip back down:

- the **metric for model independence**. Fig 2 :44 invokes standard arithmetic, but Abramowitz et al. 2019 report no universally accepted methodology. A concretisation must choose one, and that choice is an added atom with a provenance and a stake.
- the **definition of a slot**. The figure says object slot; a real comparison must decide region, variable, horizon and baseline, and those choices are not given by the figure (IPCC AR6 documents how baselines and regional definitions change the perceived result).
- the **commensuration claim**. The figure forbids summing bits and actions; the transfer to probability, timing and severity is a `specified` analogy. If a concretisation treats the three axes as the figure's literal currencies, it has drifted.

Conversely, one thing survives cleanly: a global average cannot tell you where to look, and dependent agreement is not a verdict. That is the residue worth sending. R_T was **not** measured numerically, because no ground reading exists; it is recorded qualitatively as the enumerated set of dropped constraints. Treating "the formula is the condensed version of the figures" as identity would set R_T to zero and would be the dead-reckoning error Fig 4 :30 names.
