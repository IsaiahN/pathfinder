# The Tether's answer: comparing disagreeing model projections

**Mode: `specified`, not `grounded`.** This is the shape of a comparison procedure, not a measurement and not a verdict about any particular model. Simulated derivation checked against the figures (`05_what_shape_means.md` §6), not a ground reading.

## 1. The residual it answers

Researchers hold several projections of a changing system and observe a gap between those projections and the ground. The projections disagree about probability, timing and severity. Fig 9 :4-5 says *"Disagreements that do not shrink with effort, where each rule keeps working well on a different subset, are not one hard question but several well-formed ones. Split it rather than search."* So this is a union compound, not one hard problem. It needs its own loop and its own ground.

## 2. The shape of the comparison procedure

1. **Index the residual by slot, never by global average.** Fig 1 :19 *"The residual is indexed by object slot, not aggregated."* and Fig 1 :22 *"Aggregating across slots is how a live signal disappears into an average."* Slots are region, variable and horizon. A global mean near zero can coexist with a live slot residual.

2. **Unbundle probability, timing and severity before searching.** Fig 9 :57 *"a case in two conditions is not a harder case of either. It stops being a mixture and becomes a specific compound."* Each axis gets its own loop, its own residual and its own settlement test. *The Library of Babel* says the same thing in prose: a problem may be *"a bundle or conflation of several problems masquerading as one"*, to be searched individually and then combined satisfactorily.

3. **Range each model separately against the observational ground, and hold that ground fixed.** Fig 2 :46 *"Each frame is corrected by it separately, and what makes the correction sound is that neither frame can move it."* Fig 2 :33 *"The anchor must not update."* If the observational record is adjusted because models agree, it has become another frame and the comparison has drifted coherently.

4. **Estimate independence before interpreting agreement.** Fig 2 :31 *"Independence, between two frames, is how little they draw on the same evidence."* Fig 2 :40 *"agreement about the world is worthless when the agreeing frames share an evidence pool"* . Climate models share code, parameterisations and evaluation data. Ten members of one lineage are closer to one witness with ten names than to ten witnesses. The ensemble mean is a useful description and a poor verdict: Fig 7 :35 *"A room's agreement is variation, not a verdict. Selection between rooms is where the ground votes."*

5. **Keep the three axes in separate accounts.** Fig 12 :125 *"A composition costs in two currencies, and they do not add."* and Fig 12 :130 *"Summing them into one figure is the error this names, not the fix for it. A price in bits and a price in actions are two readings, held apart."* The figure's two currencies are bits and actions; the transfer to probability, timing and severity is a `specified` structural claim, not a quote. A one-number risk score hides the shortfall it cannot test.

6. **Describe the residual before choosing the preferred frame, and keep provenance.** Fig 9 :27-28 *"describe it before you go looking, or any frame you pick will seem to fit"* . Fig 8 :51-52 *"Convergent derivation and adopted import look identical in the contents. Only the record of where each came from separates them, and the surplus lives only where the closures differ."* Record each model's lineage, version, shared components and evaluation data.

7. **Test out of sample.** Fig 5 :43-44 *"A term that explains everything by saying nothing fails this, and so does a term that explains one occasion perfectly."* A model tuned to one historical record is not thereby licensed for the projection horizon.

8. **Stop honestly.** Fig 13 :134 *"Unreachable is a verdict here, not a silence"* . Fig 13 :138 *"narrowing gets you close while the ground decides"* . If the current closure cannot resolve a slot, report *unreachable at this budget* with the residual described in effect terms. Distinguish it from underivable in principle; the remedy for the second is a new frame, for the first it is more independent search.

The ordering is itself figure-governed. The link that usually breaks is ranging each frame against the ground, because a mean can be computed without it. Fig 3 :22-23 *"A step whose input never arrives cannot be diagnosed, only its predecessor can. Which is why a reading taken below the break is not a weak reading. It is a reading of nothing."*

## 3. What it explicitly does not say

- Not that any model is wrong, or that one weighting scheme is the answer.
- Not that the ensemble mean is useless. It is a description; it is refused as a verdict.
- Not that all disagreement is resolvable. Some of it is unreachable at this budget.
- Not that the three axes are the figure's own two currencies. That is a declared structural transfer.
- Not `grounded`. No measurement was taken by this derivation. It cannot settle the question it reports on.

## 4. What would settle it, the ground

A source-discriminating check, not a further argument. The corresponding questions are:

- Does a global score preserve a positive per-slot residual that the indexed record would otherwise have lost?
- Do models with documented shared ancestry and evaluation data carry independent corroborating weight on the residual at issue? This becomes measurable when the model-dependence metric is agreed; Abramowitz et al. 2019 note that no universally accepted definition exists, which is the live strain in this solution.
- Do the three axes collapse to one residual that shrinks under effort, or do they behave as separate compounds?
- Can probability, timing and severity be commensurated in one score with no decision-relevant loss?
- Is the "unreachable" slot actually in the closure and merely unfound? Then the instruction is SEARCHED, not OUTWARD.

## 5. The honest stop

Fig 8 :39 *"So a search that finds nothing is never a proof of absence."* Fig 9 :32 *"you get a kind of thing, never the answer itself"* . The output is a shape of a procedure with a pre-registered refuter and a lineage. It stops at the limit of what the available instruments can distinguish. Prestige or agreement is not such an instrument: *"Prestige can fund an action. It can give an action a budget to be tried. But it cannot settle an action. Only the ground can settle it"* (*The Recursive Self-Improvement Trap*, read directly).

## 6. One-paragraph version for outreach

> The figures do not pick a winning climate model. They say that a comparison should keep its residual indexed by region, variable and horizon instead of hiding a live slot in a global average; that probability, timing and severity are a bundle of separate compounds and should be searched separately before being combined; that each model should be ranged against a fixed observational ground rather than against the consensus of the others; that models sharing code and evaluation data are one witness with several names, so their agreement is not corroboration; that the three axes must be kept in separate accounts because they are different typed relations, not one score; and that when the current models and instruments cannot resolve a slot the correct answer is "unreachable at this budget" with the residual described in effect terms. This is a `specified` shape of a procedure with refuters written in advance, not a measurement and not a verdict. The ground decides whether it works.
