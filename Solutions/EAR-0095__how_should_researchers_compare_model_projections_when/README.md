## What is deliberately not here

- No personal emails of named individuals.
- No ranking of people by how likely they are to agree with the protocol.
- No bulk-send plan.

Three figure-grounded reasons:

1. **Agreement is not a verdict.** Fig 7 :35 *"A room's agreement is variation, not a verdict. Selection between rooms is where the ground votes."* A list of readers chosen for likely assent is a room, not an anchor.
2. **Correlation disqualifies a verdict.** Fig 2 :40 *"agreement about the world is worthless when the agreeing frames share an evidence pool"* and Fig 2 :42 *"Buy independence in proportion to the cost of being wrong."*
3. **A verifier must not hold the interiors.** Fig 10 :42 *"A verifier that can reconstruct the claim cannot verify it."* The first contact carries the residual and its refuter, never the framework.

Legally, unsolicited bulk email to scraped personal addresses breaches GDPR and CAN-SPAM style rules in many jurisdictions. That is a practical failure on top of the structural one.
