## Five things in the wider literature that bear directly on this question

1. **Credibility is assigned, not just read.** Fricker's *credibility deficit* and *credibility excess* (IEP, 2026-11-22) show that a hearer's attribution of trust can be driven by identity power rather than by the claim. This is the human-face version of Fig 1 :26, where *"credibility accrued"* is a frame-internal quantity.
2. **More evidence need not produce more agreement.** Kahan's cultural-cognition work finds that the same scientist is rated an expert differently depending on whether their position fits the reader's cultural group, and that both sides believe the consensus is on their side. That is a channel failure in the language of Fig 10 :24.
3. **The cultural-cognition magnitude is contested.** Van der Linden (2016) and the replication literature he cites report that consensus messages often reduce polarisation. The figures govern the structural condition, not the size of the effect, so this conflict narrows the claim rather than flipping it.
4. **Independence is the parameter, not headcount.** Simons (2004) and Mann et al. (2024) give the animal-behaviour and mathematical version of Fig 2 :31 and Fig 6 :53: pooling helps only while errors are not perfectly correlated, and the correlations that emerge between agents reduce the effective number of independent samples.
5. **Standards change, and the change is contested.** Kuhn's account of changing standards for admissible solutions is corroborated in shape by Fig 7 :35-36 and Fig 10 :72, but conflicts with the figures' requirement of a non-updating ground and with the 2024 cumulative-progress paper. The conflict is recorded in `01` section 5 and is not resolved by assertion.

## Distribution: offer, do not push

Figure-grounded reasons:

- **Fig 4 :22** *"Only methods cross up. Only head starts come down."* Sending the whole architecture before anyone has described a residual sends a recording, not a generator. Lead with the shape claim and its five refuters; offer links after.
- **Fig 10 :42** *"A verifier that can reconstruct the claim cannot verify it."* A reader who has absorbed the framework before being asked for a verdict has joined the frame being checked.
- **Fig 9 :27-28** *"describe it before you go looking, or any frame you pick will seem to fit"* Ask what the recipient would expect to see if the shape claim were true before giving them the article.
- **Seat Map :163** *"High correlation is disqualifying for a verdict and efficient for a convention."* A mass send selected for people who already agree produces a convention, not corroboration.
- **Fig 10 :60** *"Where a stake cannot be removed, declare it."* Any institutional self-interest in the answer must be declared in the first message, not discovered later.

**Recommended order in a reply:** (1) the shape claim and its five refuters; (2) ask what they would count as a failure and record the answer; (3) only then the three links, with the note that the figures, not the summary, are the authority; (4) offer the formula as a method with the instruction to write a fresh refuter per problem.

## The two-stage offer

1. **Stage 1 before Stage 2** is Fig 10 :52 *"Bring a characterised residual, never the source."* The recipient checks the output without holding the framework's interior.
2. **Stage 2 is opt-in by construction.** Once a recipient has absorbed the theory, their agreement stops being evidence. The Stage 1 verdict must be recorded before any theory is shown, and later agreement is never pooled as corroboration, Fig 2 :40.
3. **Stage 3 sends a method, which is permitted.** Fig 4 :22. The formula is a generator, so giving it away does not give away an answer.
4. **Stage 3 still needs a ground per problem.** Formula "Direction": a frame *"cannot check the direction of its own question"*. Each user must write what would refute their result before running it and label the mode of what they get.

**Hard precondition:** stages 1-3 only work if the Stage 1 output survives. The two live type questions (S4, S5) must be declared as open in the text sent. No quantity here is reported as measured.
