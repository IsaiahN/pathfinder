## 1. A shape is a constraint, not a filler

The figures README states the limit: the framework returns *"what a missing thing would have to look like (a shape, not a name)."* Fig 9 :32: *"you get a kind of thing, never the answer itself"*. Fig 13 :134: *"Unreachable is a verdict here, not a silence,"*.

From *The Library of Babel* (opened 2026-11-22): *"you can cut your search short if the set of books returned is small and descriptive enough that it provides a general shape of the answer that satisfies your question."*

For this question, a **shape** is the set of properties any credibility standard must have before we can name the institution that implements it:

- an anchor that does not change its answer because of what the community thinks (Fig 2 :29);
- at least one checking frame that does not share the claim makers' evidence pool (Fig 2 :31);
- agreement classified by quantity, shared meaning as product and world-verdict as contaminant (Fig 2 :39-41);
- a residual indexed per claim, not averaged into a community trust score (Fig 1 :19, :22);
- an operator that can withdraw the standing of a held claim (Fig 12 :140);
- a lineage that distinguishes convergent derivation from adopted import (Fig 8 :51-52).

That is a constraint set. It does not name a journal, a committee, a replication threshold or a credential.

## 2. How the shape is obtained

- *Library of Babel* (opened 2026-11-22): *"If you don't know the right terms to search for, you will have to use the existing vocabulary you do know that is closest to the words that you wish to describe and grasp the shape of."* Here the near vocabulary is "credibility", "consensus", "trust", "standard", "evidence". The figures sharpen them into anchor, independence, valence, per-slot residual and refusal.
- Fig 6 :30: *"outward: does another frame already hold it? The description travels. Fails by being sharpened into terms only we can read. Cast it in effect terms."* Fig 9 :30-31: *"your words are borrowed too, so describe the effect rather than the cause"*.
- *Decoding Tacit Knowledge* (carried from the exemplar mapping, **not** re-opened this session; correction recorded below): *"When contact isn't possible, the best you can send is a plain description of what you'd expect to see, because anyone can check that against the world."*
- *The Hard Problem is About Source* (opened 2026-11-22): *"An instrument that can't distinguish these cases demonstrates its own inadequacy, it cannot claim anything substantial about what is being measured or what causes it."* That is the governing point for a community trust score: an instrument that returns "credible" for several different sources has not measured credibility, it has demonstrated its own coarseness.

## 3. What licenses a name

Only two things, both outside the shape itself:

1. **The ground settles it.** Formula step 5: *"SETTLE the ground settles it. Verified only by what pays. A gate is not the ground."* For this question: an institution earns the name "credible standard" only by paying against outcomes the community cannot move.
2. **A provenanced import from a frame whose closure differs** (Fig 6 :23, Fig 8 :51-52), with a source gate and a shadow test. A community can import a practice (preregistration, replication, adversarial review) from another closure, but the import must be recorded and must explain a gap the community already had.

*They're Scanning Books When They Should* (carried from the exemplar mapping, not re-opened): *"The real import is not a new term in the existing space. It is a new dimension of the space."* Also: *"Declaring the primitives does not make them neutral. It makes the choice visible."*

The six-term substrate is a visible guess, not nature's own list. The same applies to a community's chosen credibility criteria: declaring them makes the choice visible, not neutral.

## 4. The specific error this question invites

*The Hard Problem is About Source* (opened 2026-11-22) gives the mechanism. A color instrument reads green and cannot say whether the source was one green light, yellow plus blue, RGB, CMYK, or a scheme not yet discovered. *"If several paths create the same behavior, then behavior is a projection with lossy information"*. The spectrometer, not an argument, resolved it.

A community credibility score is exactly this kind of projection. The same score can be produced by an independent ground check, by a shared evidence pool, by identity power in the hearer, or by prestige accumulating around a name. Fig 2 :31 says two people reading the same report are not two frames. The score cannot comment on the source. What is owed is a discriminator, not a larger score.

*The Recursive Self-Improvement Trap* (opened 2026-11-22): *"the output is only as good as the validator. The agent, or the tool, or the frame, or the theory, cannot validate itself."* A community cannot certify its own credibility by its own agreement. It needs a ground and a frame that can see what the agreement hides.

## 5. Q: Does "shape, not a name" forbid describing what a standard must be?

**No, and this is a correction to my own first reading.** The README's phrase is *"what a missing thing would have to look like (a shape, not a name)."* The subject is a thing **not held** in Γ. A credibility standard that a community is trying to build may be such a missing thing, and then only its shape can be stated. But the properties an anchor must have are already given by the figures (Fig 2 :29, :36: *"An anchor is legitimate when the question is constitutively about it."*). Describing those properties is not naming a primitive; it is applying one.

Naming a **specific institution** as the answer is a binding from the community's frame onto an available form, with provenance. It is not a derivation and it is not an import that moves the wall (Fig 6 :25: *"An import adds no atom to the world."*). As in the completed exemplar's correction, the rule governs missing primitives, not the selection of already-available bindings.

Both readings are recorded so neither is quietly lost.

## 6. Two consequences for the outreach and the answer

**Prestige is not action.** From *The Recursive Self-Improvement Trap* (opened 2026-11-22): *"Prestige must never be allowed to validate an action directly."* And: *"Prestige can fund an action. It can give an action a budget to be tried. But it cannot settle an action. Only the ground can settle it. The Ground Maintainer must be blind to prestige."* A letter signed by famous names is a budget for a check, not the check. Seat Map :283: *"Adding people raises the vocabulary and does not add an anchor."*

**Drift is invisible from inside.** Same essay (opened 2026-11-22): *"You may all agree and be wrong. You may set your clock to someone else's, who was setting your clock to yours, and be off by many deviations over time. You need ground truth to define where you stand."* That is Fig 2 :34 in plain language, and it is the clearest one-paragraph statement of why a community's own agreement cannot be its own credibility standard.

**My own audit is a simulation, not a ground reading.** Same essay: *"LLMs can quickly simulate an approximate answer/output with the formula found in the architecture post. It will often be better than the generic outputs that you will usually get otherwise too (intelligence is essentially arrangement). However, the truly testable output would need to be derived from the real architecture or working the formula yourself."* So `01` is a simulated derivation, checked for internal consistency against the figures. It is not a test of any community and must be labelled as such. It cannot settle anything it reports on.
