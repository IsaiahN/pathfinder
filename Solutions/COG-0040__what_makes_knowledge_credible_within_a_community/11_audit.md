## 1. Independent domain census, from the residual line alone

I redid the census before reading the primary's conclusions, using the WATSON order: R by arity, symmetry and scale; one line per domain with actors / currency / anchor; partition or union; order by dependency.

- **R (arity / symmetry / scale).** The residual is: which claim failed to resolve for this community, and why the rule for resolving it moved. Arity at least two, because a shared-meaning convention and a world verdict have different anchors. It is a **union compound** (Fig 9 :54, :57), not one hard question with two answers. Symmetry: the community's agreement is a symmetric relation (`+`, Fig 12 :59), while the ground-to-claim relation is ordered; a score made of the former cannot represent the latter. Scale: one frame, a population of frames (a room), and a chain of rooms over time (Fig 7).
- **Domain 1, the world verdict.** Actors: frames and a ground. Currency: residuals ranged against the ground, agreement discounted by evidence overlap. Anchor: a thing that does not change its answer when pushed (Fig 2 :29, :33). Question type: verdict. Without a nameable anchor, no credibility verdict exists yet.
- **Domain 2, the shared-meaning convention.** Actors: members co-authoring a convention. Currency: coordination. Anchor: none in the world, which is legitimate because a convention makes no world claim (Fig 10 :46). Question type: convention. Agreement here is the product, not a contaminant.
- **Domain 3, the change of standards.** Actors: rooms and the ground. Currency: handoff progress with two factors (Fig 7 :26, :37-39). Anchor: the ground that selects between rooms (Fig 7 :35). Question type: dynamics of D1 and D2.
- **Cross-cutting refusal condition.** Without an operator that withdraws standing, D1 cannot correct and D3 cannot move (Fig 5 :53, Fig 12 :140). I treat it as a condition rather than a fourth domain because it has no separate anchor.

Order by dependency: describe the residual before naming a frame (Fig 9 :27-28); refuse before mint (Formula step 2); keep the anchor non-updating before comparing frames (Fig 2 :33); buy independence before reading agreement (Fig 2 :42); require rooms to differ before selection (Fig 7 :36).

**My census matches the primary's three domains, the union reading and the cross-cutting refusal condition.** Where the primary derived from Fig 1 :26/:27 (the frame-internal metric) and Fig 2 :29 (the anchor definition), I derived from Fig 9 :54/:57 (union) and Fig 7 :35 (selection). Different starting figures, same domain boundaries. Low overlap between routes, so the agreement is weak but not circular within the framework. It is still one evidence pool.

## 4. Round trip gap R_T

The Symbols table defines R_T as the gap between what was sent up and what came back down, `gap(x, (T_E ∘ T_A)(x))`, measuring what the coarser description cannot hold. Fig 4 :30 requires it to be measured rather than assumed: *"So take the round trip and measure the gap. Without it you are navigating by dead reckoning: confident about a position nobody checked."* I ran the round trip on the condensation this folder actually uses.

1. **Sent up (x):** the verbatim figure set, chiefly Fig 1 :26-27 (frame-internal metric), Fig 2 :29/:31/:39-41 (anchor, independence, valence), Fig 7 :35-36 (selection between rooms), Fig 12 :140 (refusal) and Fig 6 :23 (import).
2. **Abstraction (T_A):** the shorthand carried in `07` and `09`: credibility is independent ranging against a non-updating ground; agreement has valence; standards change by selection, refusal and import; the record grows while the reach can fall.
3. **Concretisation (T_E):** a concrete community credibility standard, for example a journal's acceptance rule, a replication threshold, or a professional roster of trusted sources.

**R_T is nonzero.** Four things the shorthand holds do not survive the trip back down:

- the **identity of the anchor at the social scale**. Fig 2 :29 defines it abstractly; a concrete institution that claims the role may have a reputation to protect and be disqualified by Fig 10 :93. The abstraction does not tell you which institution passes.
- the **measurement of independence**. Fig 2 :31 defines it as evidence overlap; a concrete field rarely records enough provenance to compute it, so a claimed "independent replication" may reuse the same data or instrument.
- the **institutional form of negation**. Fig 12 :140 and Operators :58 require a refusal operator; a concrete retraction or failed replication may not remove the standing of the claims composed over the retracted result.
- the **empirical magnitude of standard change**. Fig 7 :35 says the ground votes between rooms; the world literature (Kuhn 1962 versus the 2024 cumulative-progress result) disagrees about how far standards actually replace rather than accumulate.

Conversely, two things survive cleanly: a community cannot certify itself with its own credit score, and agreement from one pool is not a second witness. That is the residue worth sending. R_T was **not** measured numerically, because no community ground reading exists; it is recorded qualitatively as the enumerated set of dropped constraints. Treating the formula as identical to the law set would set R_T to zero and would be the dead-reckoning error Fig 4 :30 names. The honest form is a lossy condensation with a measured gap, which is what `09_watson_answer.md` states.
