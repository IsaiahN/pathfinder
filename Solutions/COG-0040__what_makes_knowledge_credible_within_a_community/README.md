## Effect term for the question

Two readings are being decided. A: credibility is what the community agrees on, and standards move by negotiation and generational turnover. B: credibility is contact with a ground that does not update on what the community thinks, checked by frames that do not share an evidence pool, and standards move when rooms that differ are selected against a surfaced residual. A ends in a frame-internal quantity, Fig 1 :26 *"credibility accrued"*, and in coherent drift, Fig 2 :34. B keeps an anchor, an independence parameter, a refusal operator, and an unauthored pool. ## What is deliberately not here

- No personal emails of named individuals.
- No ranking of people by how likely they are to agree with the claim.
- No bulk-send plan.

Three figure-grounded reasons:

1. A room does not manufacture an anchor. Seat Map :283: *"A room does not upgrade the label. A room reaching a conclusion without the ground is agreement, and agreement among frames is a weighted average of influence. Adding people raises the vocabulary and does not add an anchor."*
2. Selecting for receptiveness produces the disqualifying correlation. Seat Map :163: *"High correlation is disqualifying for a verdict and efficient for a convention."*
3. Showing the framework before asking for a verdict spends the reader's independence. Fig 10 :42: *"A verifier that can reconstruct the claim cannot verify it."*

Legally, scraped personal emails for unsolicited bulk outreach breach GDPR and CAN-SPAM style rules in many jurisdictions. That is a practical failure on top of the structural one.
