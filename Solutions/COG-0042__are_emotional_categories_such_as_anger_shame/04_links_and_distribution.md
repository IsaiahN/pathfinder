## Wider literature that bears on the question

- **Universalist side.** Ekman's two-culture matching studies and the six-basic-emotion tradition; the Cowen and Keltner large-scale context-mapping programme. These supply the shared-substrate and shared-context evidence.
- **Constructionist and variation side.** Russell's core affect and the psychological construction approach; Barrett and colleagues' reviews; the Gendron and Wierzbicka lines; Mesquita and colleagues' cultural construction of emotion and emotional acculturation.
- **The bridge that matters here.** Jackson 2019 finds both variation and universal structure in the same dataset. That is the empirical analogue of the figure verdict: not a compromise average, but two different strata reported separately.
- **Measurement caution.** Gendron 2014 shows that what looks like universal recognition in constrained forced-choice tasks weakens under unconstrained sorting. This is a direct instance of Fig 2 :37-38, where an instrument is *"wrong on axes they do not measure."*

## Distribution: offer, do not push

Figure-grounded reasons:

- **Fig 10 :52** *"Bring a characterised residual, never the source."* Sending the whole framework before someone has described the residual sends a recording, not a generator.
- **Fig 10 :42** *"A verifier that can reconstruct the claim cannot verify it."* A recipient who has absorbed the interior before giving a verdict has joined the frame being checked.
- **Fig 9 :27-28** *"describe it before you go looking, or any frame you pick will seem to fit."* Ask what they would expect to see if the shape claim were true before showing the derivation.
- **Fig 2 :40** *"disqualifying for a verdict: agreement about the world is worthless when the agreeing frames share an evidence pool."* A mass send to people likely to agree manufactures a convention, not evidence.

**Recommended order in a reply:** (1) the union claim and its refuter; (2) ask what they would count as a failure and what anchor they would use; (3) record that verdict; (4) only then, if invited, the three local source files, with the note that the figures and not the summary are the authority.

## The two-stage offer

**Stage 1.** Send the residual, the shape claim and the pre-registered refuter. Ask which of the six refuters they think is most likely to fire and what they would count as a failure. Do not ask for agreement and do not ask for trust.

**Stage 2, opt in only.** If they are curious, offer the verbatim figure text, the canonical usage rule and the formula. Their stage 1 verdict is already recorded; later agreement is never pooled as corroboration.

**Stage 3.** Offer the formula as a method to reuse, with the instruction that each user writes their own refuter and states their own mode before running it.

**Hard precondition.** Stages 1 through 3 only work if the stage 1 claim survives. This is why `01_figure_grounded_audit.md` keeps the strains and the refuters visible rather than polishing them away.
