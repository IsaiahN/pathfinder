## What this solution decides, in effect terms

The question offers two options: categories are shared, or categories vary. The residual is the gap between what a shared-category prediction projects onto a society and what that society's own reports, concepts and practices return. The figures say to index that gap per society and per category slot, not to average it (Fig 1 :19, Fig 1 :22). Under the dependency and union rules (Fig 9 :4-5, Fig 9 :54-55), the binary is the wrong form: the shared part and the varying part live at different strata, so the honest answer is a bounded union with a separate verdict per domain.

## What is deliberately not here

- No personal emails of named individuals and no receptiveness ranking.
- No bulk send plan.
- No claim that this simulated derivation is a ground reading or that it settles whether anger, shame or grief are universal.

Three reasons, in the figures' own words. First, agreement is not an anchor: Fig 2 :35 says *"Alignment is triangulation, not negotiation. A mirror cannot correct you."* Second, correlation disqualifies a verdict: Fig 2 :40 says *"disqualifying for a verdict: agreement about the world is worthless when the agreeing frames share an evidence pool"*, while Fig 2 :41 says agreement about a shared meaning is *"efficient for a convention: agreement about a shared meaning is the product, not a corruption of it."* A list selected for people likely to agree would manufacture exactly the convention that cannot corroborate. Third, a verifier must not hold the interiors: Fig 10 :42 says *"A verifier that can reconstruct the claim cannot verify it."* Lead with the residual and its refuter, then offer the links only if asked.
