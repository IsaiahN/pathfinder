## The question restated as an effect

What is being decided is not which culture scores higher on a test. It is whether a comparison can be built at all without one culture's instrument silently becoming the anchor. In effect terms (Formula step 1; Symbols table, `R`):

- **b** is what a researcher believes a cognitive process is, formed from their own training and inherited disciplinary priors.
- **a** is the act of testing: choose items, choose a scoring rule, choose a sample, choose a criterion.
- **Γ(b,a)** is what the researcher's library of constructs and instruments predicts will be observed.
- **o′** is what participants actually do in their habitat.
- **s** is the cultural slot. R = |Γ(b,a) − o′| is indexed per cultural slot, never averaged into one "human cognition" number (Fig 1 :19, Fig 1 :22).

The defect the question names is specific: the instrument is authored inside frame A, validated on frame A, and then applied to frame B, so the "ground" it ranges against is actually frame A's convention wearing the costume of a measurement. A frame is scoring itself with a quantity it also produces (Fig 1 :27).

- Option A, "use one culture-free test everywhere": leads to the prediction that a single construct is invariant and that differences in score are differences in the trait. This imposes the very assumption the question forbids, because a declared meaning is not a grounded one (Fig 10 :18).
- Option B, "range each frame against its own ground and triangulate": leads to the prediction that a comparison is legitimate only when the construct is described in effect terms before any culture's instrument is selected, each habitat's own outcomes settle the reading, and the comparing frames are independent. This is the verdict recorded in `09_watson_answer.md`, kept per domain and never averaged.

What an answer would look like, written down and bound as an ordinary term before the run (Formula, "Direction"): a conjunction of conditions for a legitimate cross-cultural comparison, plus an instrument that would discriminate a failure of one condition from a failure of another. Until bound, that shape predicts nothing and no loop check can see it. It is now bound in `01_figure_grounded_audit.md` section 2, so the pre-registered refuter in section 3 can fire on it.

## What is deliberately not here

- No personal emails of named individuals. The IACCP public pages inspected for `03` contain many published individual addresses; none is reproduced here.
- No ranking of people by how likely they are to agree.
- No bulk-send plan.
- No claim that this derivation is `grounded`, no claim that it settles the cross-cultural question, and no claim that the framework is the only true account of cognition.

Three figure-grounded reasons, plus one practical one:

1. A room is not an anchor. *"A room's agreement is variation, not a verdict. Selection between rooms is where the ground votes."* (Fig 7 :35) and *"Alignment is triangulation, not negotiation. A mirror cannot correct you."* (Fig 2 :35). Gathering agreeable readers manufactures the correlation that disqualifies a verdict.
2. Correlation has valence. *"disqualifying for a verdict: agreement about the world is worthless when the agreeing frames share an evidence pool"* (Fig 2 :40). A list selected for receptiveness is one witness wearing many names. The same rule applies to a study that samples many participants from one cultural evidence pool and calls them independent frames.
3. A verifier must be outside the interior. *"A verifier that can reconstruct the claim cannot verify it."* (Fig 10 :42). The first contact therefore carries the residual and its refuter, not the framework.
4. Legally, unsolicited bulk email to scraped addresses breaches GDPR and CAN-SPAM style rules in many jurisdictions and gets the sending domain blacklisted. Public opt-in routes avoid that failure as well as the structural one.
