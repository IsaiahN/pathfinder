## 1. A shape is a constraint you can state before you can name it

The canonical statement in `FIGURES_README_canonical.md` is that the framework returns *"what a missing thing would have to look like (a shape, not a name)"*, then stops where instruments can no longer distinguish and records the lineage. Fig 9 :32: *"you get a kind of thing, never the answer itself."* Fig 13 :134: *"Unreachable is a verdict here, not a silence,"* and :135: *"It cannot say which primitive is missing, because naming it would mean already holding it."*

From *The Library of Babel*, read directly:

> *"you can cut your search short if the set of books returned is small and descriptive enough that it provides a general shape of the answer that satisfies your question."*

So for COG-0091 a shape is: **the narrowed form of a legitimate comparison that can be stated before any culture, any test, and any construct is named.** It constrains; it does not fill.

- **Shape:** *a conjunction of conditions on anchor legitimacy, frame independence and effect-first description, kept per cultural slot, with a declared stake and a refuter.*
- **Name, withheld:** *this is the culture with more analytical intelligence; this test is culture-free; rationality is universal construct X.*

The distinction matters here because the entire applied literature is in the naming business. A test is named, a score is named, a culture is named, a rank is produced. The shape says what any of those would have to satisfy before the rank means anything.

## 2. How the shape was obtained

*The Library of Babel* gives the bootstrapping procedure:

> *"If you don't know the right terms to search for, you will have to use the existing vocabulary you do know that is closest to the words that you wish to describe and grasp the shape of."*
> *"With each new term gained, you are able to narrow the space by definition and deepen your vocabulary."*

Applied here: the closest available terms were "etic and emic", "pseudoetic", "construct equivalence", "measurement invariance", "WEIRD bias", "anthropomorphism" and "cultural relativity". None was used as the answer. Each was treated as a witness to be ranged against the residual, per Fig 8 :53: *"Residual first, frame second."* The effect description (a gap between an instrument built in one frame and behaviour in another habitat) came first; the disciplines were selected only after that description was fixed.

The essay also names the bundle stop:

> *"Perhaps your problem was a bundle or conflation of several problems masquerading as one? You will need to search all of those problems individually to find their answers and then combine them in a satisfactory way."*

That is the canonical warrant for treating COG-0091 as a union (Fig 9 :54-57), not as one hard question. The three conditions C1, C2 and C3 are the unbundled pieces.

The stop condition:

> *"You will know that you are done because the finish line will also be the starting place, as there is nowhere else to search and nothing else that can describe the answer better."*

In this solution the stop is reached when C1, C2 and C3 are stated, each has an instrument and a failure mode, and the refuters are written. Any further search that only renames "culture-fairness" is motion, not progress (Fig 7 :32, *"one long room: motion, no progress"*).

## 3. The source error is the specific forbidden move here

From *The Hard Problem is About Source*, read directly:

> *"The Problem: You can have several underlying causes that create identical readings but the definition of the output would be read as the same."*
> *"If several paths create the same behavior, then behavior is a projection with lossy information"*
> *"the instrument detects green, and says that it's green. However, the instrument is speaking only about what it measured, it cannot comment on the source."*
> *"What the Spectrometer Example Reveals: The answer is unreached until a better instrument exists."*
> *"An instrument that can't distinguish these cases demonstrates its own inadequacy, it cannot claim anything substantial about what is being measured or what causes it."*

This is the exact error a cross-cultural score makes when its source is named. A score is a reading. It is consistent with a locally valued capability, a test-taking convention, a translation artifact, a familiar item format, a motivation to perform for the tester, or a habitat that rewards a different strategy entirely. The reading does not determine the source. Naming the source "intelligence" from the score is metamerism, and no amount of agreement among readers fixes it. What is owed is an instrument that discriminates sources, which this solution does not have. Fig 6 :30: *"outward: does another frame already hold it? The description travels. Fails by being sharpened into terms only we can read. Cast it in effect terms."*

This is also why the answer is a `gap` at the point of naming a construct. The framework can say a source must be discriminated; it cannot say which construct is the source until an instrument discriminates it.

## 4. Tacit knowledge makes the "culture-free" claim impossible

From *Decoding Tacit Knowledge*, read directly, the essay most directly on this question:

> *"every message is understood through three things the listener brings: context (what's going on around the words), subtext (what the words are for, though they don't say it) and pretext (the history and assumptions the listener had before the words arrived)."*
> *"Writing carries the context well and the subtext partly, but it almost never carries the pretext, the 'you had to be there' part."*
> *"When contact isn't possible, the best you can send is a plain description of what you'd expect to see, because anyone can check that against the world."*
> *"Culture is a shared backdrop, Collins's collective tacit knowledge. Its members can hold very different mindsets; what they share isn't a set of beliefs but the backdrop they read each other against."*
> *"Two people who only check against each other drift, and they drift together, so neither notices."*
> *"two people who share one evidence pool"* agreement is *"worthless for settling what's true"* and *"Two frames that overlap almost completely are 'one frame wearing two names'"*.

A test item is a compressed message. It carries context and subtext; it almost never carries the pretext, the shared history that makes the item mean what the author intended. A participant who lacks that history can answer correctly for a different reason, and the score cannot comment on the source. This is the metamerism of section 3 in a different vocabulary.

The essay names three safeguards that match the figures exactly:

1. *"A ground that both are corrected by, separately"* : Fig 2 :45-46.
2. *"Separate experience"* : Fig 10 :80, *"Frames whose independence is renewed by separate experience can be maintained"*.
3. *"Claims in effect terms. An effect description can be checked against the ground by anyone"* : Fig 6 :30, Fig 9 :30-31.

These are the three conditions C1, C2 and C3 restated by the framework's own author in an essay written independently of this question. That is framework-internal corroboration, not independent evidence, and it is recorded as such.

## 5. What licenses a name

Only two things, both outside the shape:

1. **The ground settles it.** Formula step 5: *"the ground settles it. Verified only by what pays. A gate is not the ground."* The README: the framework *"stops at the limit of what the available instruments can distinguish, and records the lineage so the stop is honest."*
2. **A provenanced import from a frame whose closure differs** (Fig 6 :23, Fig 8 :50-51), with a source gate and a shadow test.

For COG-0091 neither has happened. There is no instrument that discriminates among the possible sources of a cross-cultural score, and the constructs in common use were adopted from a single disciplinary pool without a shadow test. Hence the `gap` row in F12 of `01_figure_grounded_audit.md`.

## 6. Prestige is not action, applied to cross-cultural method

From *The Recursive Self-Improvement Trap*, read directly:

> *"Prestige must never be allowed to validate an action directly."*
> *"Someone let prestige stand in for proof. Someone let reputation substitute for result. Someone let the trusted name validate the untested claim."*
> *"Prestige can fund an action. It can give an action a budget to be tried. But it cannot settle an action. Only the ground can settle it. The Ground Maintainer must be blind to prestige."*
> *"the output is only as good as the validator. The agent, or the tool, or the frame, or the theory, cannot validate itself."*

A campaign that gathers famous cross-cultural psychologists to endorse the shape is the named collapse, not corroboration. Fig 2 :40 disqualifies agreement from a shared evidence pool, and the framework's pool is the same one that produced the problem being diagnosed. The legitimate ask gives the claim a budget to be tried by someone outside that pool and lets a local ground settle it. This is why `03_public_contact_points.csv` contains no personal emails and no receptiveness ranking.

## 7. This derivation is a simulation, not a ground reading

From the same essay, read directly:

> *"LLMs can quickly simulate an approximate answer/output with the formula found in the architecture post. It will often be better than the generic outputs that you will usually get otherwise too. However, the truly testable output would need to be derived from the real architecture or working the formula yourself."*

So this `05` file, like `01`, is a simulated derivation checked for internal consistency against the figures. It is labelled `general` where it states the reading and `specified` where it applies the reading to this question. It is not a test of the cross-cultural question and cannot settle it. The point of publishing it is to make the shape and its refuters easy to check by someone whose ground contact is real and whose closure differs.

## 8. Correction to my own §3 (an overstatement, retained as evidence)

I initially wrote that naming any construct in a cross-cultural comparison is forbidden by "shape, not a name". That statement is too broad and is withdrawn here. The canonical phrase governs a **missing** primitive, one not held in Γ. If a construct is already in the researcher's library, naming it is a binding from that research frame, not the invention of a new primitive. What remains forbidden is claiming the binding moves the wall (Fig 6 :21, *"MINT composes inside the closure and can never add an atom"*). The binding still needs provenance and a ground test, but it is not a category error in the way naming a seventh physical primitive would be. Both the corrected and the withdrawn formulations are kept so the correction is visible rather than silent.

## 9. The shape, stated once more

For COG-0091 the canonical shape is: **a three-condition conjunction with three distinct failure modes, a per-slot verdict, a declared stake, and a refuter written before the run.** It is not a culture ranking, not a universal construct, not a claimed culture-free instrument, and not a list of cognitive domains. The names that appear in the applied literature are bindings, provenance owed. The instruments that would convert the shape into a grounded answer are not held here, so the answer stops at `general` plus `specified` and the stop is recorded.
