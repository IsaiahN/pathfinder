## QUESTION

Decide, in effect terms, what a legitimate cross-cultural comparison of cognitive processes requires. Option A: use one culture-neutral instrument everywhere and treat score differences as differences in intelligence or rationality. Option B: reframe the question from a substance to a relation, then require three conditions, an effect described in travelling terms before any instrument is named, a local anchor each instrument author cannot move, and frames with low-overlap evidence. What each leads to: A imposes the assumption the question forbids, because a declared meaning is not a grounded one and a frame then scores itself with a quantity it also produces; B yields a checkable shape, three failure modes, per-site verdicts and refuters written before the run. The framework returns a shape, not a name, so neither option selects a construct.

## DOMAIN CENSUS

R by arity, symmetry and scale, then one line per domain with actors / currency / anchor, partition or union, and order by dependency.

- **R (the residual).** Arity: three conditions in a union, anchor legitimacy C1, frame independence C2, effect-first description C3. Symmetry: the comparison bond is asymmetric, tester and tested are not interchangeable, and a score changes meaning when the reference group changes (Fig 12 :87). Scale: first order inside each habitat's local ground, second order at the cross-cultural comparison. Fig 9 names it a union compound: a case in two conditions is not a harder case of either.
- **Domain 1, the measuring frame.** Actors: test designers, items, rubric, the discipline's library. Currency: internal scores and construct validity within the frame. Anchor: scope-limited local outcomes inside that culture, a genuine ground for the local question only (Fig 2 :36-38). Admitted with a scope restriction.
- **Domain 2, the measured habitat.** Actors: participants, local practices, conditions and relations read off the world. Currency: locally valued outcomes. Anchor: the local ground, enumerated rather than composed (Fig 11 :43-48). Admitted.
- **Domain 3, the comparison frame.** Actors: researchers, translated instruments, multi-site samples. Currency: equivalence claims and group differences. Anchor: not yet legitimate, because the anchor in common practice is Domain 1's test, a convention that decides an outcome (Fig 10 :47). Admitted as the domain under repair.
- **Domain 4, the verification frame.** Actors: methodologists, indigenous psychologists, reviewers. Currency: agreement and residual. Anchor: the ground, never agreement. In this simulated run all four domains draw on one evidence pool, so independence is low and agreement is one pool talking (Fig 2 :31-32).
- **Excluded, X1 "culture-free universal intelligence".** No anchor independent of the test that produces it (Fig 1 :27). Not a domain. This excludes it structurally, not by denying that some general capacity exists.
- **Order by dependency.** C3 first, then C1, then C2 (Fig 3 :20, Fig 9 :27-28). Stake declaration and habitat enumeration cross-cut all three. Carry the method, not the recording, to the next comparison (Fig 4 :22).

Strain test applied: an early attempt to apply Fig 13's six-term physical substrate to cognitive constructs required renaming practices as gradients and items as networks. Re-census sent Fig 13 to the honest-stop role only. A second reading of Fig 2 :36-38 as banning human judgment as an anchor was re-censused: the question IS constitutively about human cognition, so human outcomes are the right anchor, but one culture's judgment is only an instrument and instruments can be wrong on axes they do not measure. See `01_figure_grounded_audit.md` section 6.

## VERDICT (from the figures)

The shape survives; no construct, culture or test is named. "What counts as intelligence or rationality" dissolves into "what makes a system capable in a habitat" (Fig 11 :39). A comparison is legitimate only when the effect is described in travelling terms before any instrument is selected, each site's anchor is a local outcome the instrument authors cannot move, and the frames compared draw on low-overlap evidence so their agreement is triangulation rather than the pool talking. The habitat is enumerated rather than composed, the observer's stake is declared rather than hidden behind culture-freedom, strategy valence is relative to the local residual, and verdicts are kept per site and task rather than averaged. Which construct occupies the slot is a `gap`: the instrument that would discriminate among the sources of a score is not held here. Mode `general` on the shape, `specified` on the method consequences, ungrounded.

- **cost if wrong:** bounded and locatable. The packet states mode, three checkable sub-claims and refuters before the conclusion, so an error is findable rather than invisible. The real cost is spending the independence of outside readers by sending a finished theory instead of a checkable shape, which Fig 2 :42 prices as buying independence in proportion to the cost of being wrong. That cost is avoidable by leading with the shape claim and its refuter. A second cost is misdescribing a population through a rank that answers a dissolved question; the per-site reporting requirement limits that.
- **undoable:** yes. The named bindings are removable without touching the derivation: no culture is ranked, no test is endorsed, and no construct is selected. A sent claim can be superseded by a corrected packet, and no primitive has to be withdrawn. The three conditions can be applied to a new comparison without changing the framework.
- **reversed by:** a ground reading, not agreement. A source-discriminating instrument that separates a locally valued capability from a test-taking convention, a translation artifact and a motivation to perform would begin to name the slot; a culture-invariant construct predicting local outcomes with the same mapping would break the dissolution; a study in which sample size with evidence pool held constant changes the verdict would break the independence condition; a residual-free universal domain list would break the enumeration requirement; a genuinely culture-free instrument with a construction record would weaken the stake declaration. Until one of these fires, the verdict stands as candidate.
