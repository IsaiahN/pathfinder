# The Tether's answer on comparing cognitive processes across cultures

**Mode: `general` on the shape, `specified` on its consequences for method. Not `grounded`.** This is a shape and a route, not a result. Simulated derivation checked against the figures (`05_what_shape_means.md` section 7), not a ground reading.

## 1. The residual it answers

A comparison instrument is built inside one frame, validated on that frame, and applied to another. The resulting score is a projection of the instrument as much as a reading of the participant, yet it is used as if it were a measurement of a culture's intelligence or rationality. Fig 11 :35: *"The wrong question, and this figure is the replacement for it."* The contact failure is a **union compound**, three overlapping conditions C1 (anchor legitimacy), C2 (frame independence), C3 (effect-first description), not one hard question (Fig 9 :55, :57). It has its own loop and its own ground.

## 2. The shape

1. **The question is reframed from a substance to a relation.** "What counts as intelligence or rationality?" dissolves. Fig 11 :39: *"So the answerable question is not what intelligence is, but what makes a system capable. And capability is a property of agent-and-habitat, never of the agent alone."* Fig 11 :41: *"Which is the same move as asking what generates lift rather than whether a thing is a bird."* A culture ranking answers a question the framework has already dissolved.
2. **Describe the effect before choosing any frame.** Fig 9 :27-28: *"describe it before you go looking, or any frame you pick will seem to fit."* Fig 6 :30: *"outward: does another frame already hold it? The description travels. Fails by being sharpened into terms only we can read. Cast it in effect terms."* The pre-registration should contain a form of words anyone can check against the world, written before any instrument is named.
3. **Each slot needs its own anchor, and the instrument's authors cannot move it.** Fig 2 :36: *"An anchor is legitimate when the question is constitutively about it."* The question is about human cognition, so human outcomes qualify. One culture's rubric is only an instrument, and *"instruments can be wrong on axes they do not measure"* (Fig 2 :37-38). A test validated only on itself is a frame scoring itself with a quantity it also produces (Fig 1 :27).
4. **Independence is bought, not assumed.** Fig 2 :40: *"disqualifying for a verdict: agreement about the world is worthless when the agreeing frames share an evidence pool"*; Fig 2 :42: *"Buy independence in proportion to the cost of being wrong."* Fig 7 :36: *"A chain of mirrors is one long room. The next room must differ from the last, and match the residual it surfaced."* Many participants from one pool is one witness wearing many names (Fig 8 :48-49).
5. **Convergence at maximum independence is the evidence.** Fig 8 :50: *"But shared atoms are not the signature. Two frames can hold the same atom by convergence, reaching it separately, which is corroboration at maximum independence."* Fig 8 :51: *"Convergent derivation and adopted import look identical in the contents. Only the record of where each came from separates them,"* So each site must record how it arrived at its construct. A translated and shipped instrument is an adopted import, not corroboration.
6. **Enumerate the habitat; do not fill it from a universal list.** Fig 11 :43: *"The habitat is enumerated, never composed."* Fig 11 :48: *"You do not invent the list. You read it off the world, and what you cannot perceive or measure yet is the residual."* Fig 11 :52 extends the cascade to second order. A capability absent from the battery remains a live residual, not evidence of absence (Fig 8 :39).
7. **Valence is relative to the residual.** Fig 11 :49: *"An actor's valence is relative to the residual, not a property it carries."* Fig 11 :51: *"No standing list of good actors survives a change of residual."* A strategy can belong to a shared functional group while its member, the thing that actually works here, is habitat-specific (Fig 12 :124).
8. **Declare the stake; do not claim culture-freedom.** Fig 10 :54: *"A rule no real occupant could meet describes an idealisation, not a position."* Fig 10 :60: *"Where a stake cannot be removed, declare it. An undeclared interest is modelled anyway and an unnamed model runs unchallenged."* Install definitions that can be violated (Fig 10 :19: *"→ install what can be violated"*).
9. **Keep verdicts per slot.** Fig 1 :22: *"Aggregating across slots is how a live signal disappears into an average."* Fig 9 :60: *"Use filters for the budget and witnesses for the verdict. Never let a filter hand you a verdict."* A pooled fairness index is a filter for where to look, never the verdict.

## 3. What it explicitly does not say

- Not a culture ranking. No culture is more or less intelligent or rational in this answer.
- Not a claim that a universal cognitive capacity does not exist. It says that a construct with no independent ground cannot anchor a comparison. X1 in the census is not a domain because it has no anchor, not because it is false.
- Not a claim that any particular test is culture-free, and not an endorsement of measurement invariance as *the* answer. That is a binding from the psychometrics pool, provenance owed.
- Not a derivation of one culture's cognition from another, and no new primitive.
- Not `grounded`. No local-ground measurement pays for any of this here.
- Not a name for the construct in the slot. Fig 9 :32: *"you get a kind of thing, never the answer itself."*

## 4. What would settle it - the ground

A source-discriminating instrument, not a further argument. From *The Hard Problem is About Source*: an instrument that reads green *"cannot comment on the source"*; the spectrometer resolved it. Here the corresponding questions are:

- **Effect discrimination.** Can an instrument distinguish a locally valued capability from a test-taking convention, a translation artifact and a motivation to perform, all of which can produce the same score? If yes, the metamerism in F3 of `05` is resolved.
- **Anchor test per site.** Name the outcome in each habitat that the test designer cannot move, and show that it does not move when the instrument is revised (Fig 2 :29-30).
- **Independence audit.** Show that the sites' evidence pools are disjoint by contact history, not merely by national label, and that agreement survives against separate grounds.
- **Violability.** State which observation would show the construct does not apply in a given site, and show that the study would record that observation rather than exclude the site (Fig 10 :19).
- **Standing falsifiers** from `01` section 4: an invariant single test with full equivalence, repairable disagreement across disjoint pools, a residual-free universal list, habitat-invariant strategy ranking, a working aggregate diagnostic, or an actually culture-free instrument.

## 5. The honest stop

**Unreached, not unreachable.** The three conditions and their failure modes are stated; the instrument that would discriminate among the sources of a score is not held here. Fig 13 :134: *"Unreachable is a verdict here, not a silence,"* and Fig 8 :39: *"So a search that finds nothing is never a proof of absence."* There is also a real limit the figures do not close: they say the anchor must not update and must be legitimate for the question, but they do not specify what counts as a ground for a claim about a whole culture. That is left open and recorded in `11_audit.md` rather than filled in. *"Indiscernibility is not identity."*

## 6. One-paragraph version for outreach

> The Tether does not rank cultures by intelligence or rationality, and it does not claim a culture-free test. It reframes the question: what makes a system capable in a habitat, rather than how much of a substance a culture has. It then gives the shape any legitimate comparison must have. Describe the cognitive effect in words anyone can check against the world before naming any instrument. Name an anchor in each site that the test designer cannot move. Buy independence between sites, because agreement from one evidence pool is the pool talking, not corroboration. Enumerate the habitat instead of filling it from a universal list. Declare the investigator's stake instead of hiding behind culture-freedom. Keep verdicts per site and per task, never averaged. What it offers is that shape, three failure modes and refuters written in advance. It does not say which construct is the right one; the instrument that would settle that is not in hand. The comparison stays open until a ground discriminates the sources of the score.
