## What the wider literature bears on the question

1. **The pseudoetic failure is the named defect, not a hypothetical.** The cross-cultural psychology article reports that an intelligence test was brought into another culture without checking whether it measured what it was intended to measure, and that this is pseudoetic because *"various cultures have their own concepts for intelligence."* That is F2 and F3 observed from inside the field. It corroborates the figure verdict without supplying it.

2. **The emic/etic literature independently reaches the same constraint on anchors.** Mostowlansky and Rota report that for Pike, etic categories devised by scholars *"had no special status, but amounted to nothing more than the emic perspective of a scientific community."* That is Fig 1 :27 (*"A frame cannot score itself with a quantity it also produces"*) and Fig 10 :47 (*"a convention that decides an outcome has stopped being one"*) stated by an anthropologist working from linguistics and theology, with no access to the framework. This is the lowest-overlap corroboration in the set.

3. **A different field shows the same habitat dependence.** The animal cognition article reports that urban and rural Barbados bullfinches differ in innovative problem solving but not in colour discrimination learning, and that cognitive abilities are shaped by the structure of environmental change. That is Fig 11 :49-51 (valence relative to the residual) in comparative cognition, with no shared evidence pool with cross-cultural psychology.

4. **Measurement theory supplies the violability test.** The psychometrics article notes that validity is about the interpretations of scores entailed by proposed uses, and that *"a test is not valid unless it is interpreted and used in the manner for which it was designed."* This is Fig 10 :19 (*"install what can be violated"*) in the technical vocabulary of the field. It is a binding from a different pool, not a figure-derived name, and it is recorded as such.

5. **A shared evidence pool is the recurring failure across all of these.** The WEIRD critique, the coherent-drift passage in *Decoding Tacit Knowledge*, and Fig 2 :40 all name the same arithmetic: agreement among frames that draw on one pool is one witness wearing many names. Agreement from a shared pool is one witness, not two. No accumulated number of similar articles changes that.

## Distribution: offer, do not push

Figure-grounded reasons:

- **Fig 10 :52** : *"Bring a characterised residual, never the source."* Sending the full framework before anyone has described the comparison failure sends a recording, not a generator. Lead with the three conditions and the refuter; offer the links after a verdict is recorded.
- **Fig 10 :42** : *"A verifier that can reconstruct the claim cannot verify it."* A recipient who has absorbed the whole architecture before being asked for a verdict has joined the frame being checked. Their later agreement is never pooled as corroboration (Fig 2 :40).
- **Fig 9 :27-28** : *"describe it before you go looking, or any frame you pick will seem to fit."* Ask what they would expect to see if the shape claim were true before giving them the article or the repository.
- **Fig 8 :50-51** : *"But shared atoms are not the signature. Two frames can hold the same atom by convergence, reaching it separately, which is corroboration at maximum independence."* A recipient who already agrees with the framework because they read its author is an adopted import, not convergence. Record the route.
- **Fig 2 :42** : *"Buy independence in proportion to the cost of being wrong."* The cost here is a bad cross-cultural comparison, which can misdescribe a whole population. Spend real effort contacting methodologists outside the framework's pool.

**Recommended order in a reply:** (1) the three conditions and the refuter; (2) ask what they would count as a failure and what their local ground would be; (3) only after their verdict, the links, with the note that the figures, not the summary, are the authority.

## The two-stage offer

1. **Stage 1.** Send the shape claim and the pre-registered refuter. Ask what would refute it in the recipient's own field. Record their verdict before showing any theory. This is Fig 10 :52 and Fig 2 :45.
2. **Stage 2, opt-in only.** If they are curious, offer the three links above. Seat 4 must not hold the interiors; once a recipient has absorbed the theory, their agreement stops being evidence (Fig 2 :40).
3. **Stage 3.** Offer the formula as a method to reuse, with the instruction that each user names their own ground, writes their own refuter and states their own mode (Fig 4 :22; Formula "Direction").

**Hard precondition:** stages 1 to 3 only work if the stage-1 shape survives. As currently drafted it does not claim to settle anything, which is the point; it is a checkable conjunction with a named refuter. Do not upgrade it to a verdict about any culture at any stage.
