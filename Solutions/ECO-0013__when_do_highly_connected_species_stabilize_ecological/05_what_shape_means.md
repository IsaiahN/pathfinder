## 1. A shape is a constraint, not a filler

The figures README states the limit: the framework returns "what a missing thing would have to look like (a shape, not a name)". Fig 9 :32: "you get a kind of thing, never the answer itself." Fig 13 :134: "Unreachable is a verdict here, not a silence".

From *The Library of Babel*: "You can cut your search short if the set of books returned is small and descriptive enough that it provides a general shape of the answer that satisfies your question."

So a **shape** is the narrowed form of an answer you can state before you can name it. It constrains; it does not fill. For this question, `CONNECTANCE_VALENCE(R)` is a shape: it says the sign of a hub's contribution is a function of the residual and of link overlap, without naming which species in a given web will be stabilizing. "Protect all generalists" is a name, and a roster.

## 2. How a shape is obtained

- *Library of Babel*: use the vocabulary you already have that is closest to the thing, look for recurring new terms that overlap or correlate with it, recurse. "With each new term gained, you narrow the space and deepen your vocabulary."
- Fig 6 :30: "The description travels. Fails by being sharpened into terms only we can read. Cast it in effect terms." Fig 9 :30-31: "your words are borrowed too, so describe the effect rather than the cause."
- *Decoding Tacit Knowledge*: "When contact isn't possible, the best you can send is a plain description of what you'd expect to see, because anyone can check that against the world."
- *Library of Babel* on stopping: the answer is reached when "the finish line will also be the starting place" - the paradox is that by then you have "composed the answer through the process of locating it."

Applied here: the effect description is "a hub's sign flips with the correlation of its partners' responses". That is checkable by anyone with a perturbation experiment, whatever vocabulary they use for connectance.

## 3. What licenses a name

Only two things, both outside the shape itself:

1. **The ground settles it.** Formula step 5: "the ground settles it. Verified only by what pays." The README: the framework "stops at the limit of what the available instruments can distinguish, and records the lineage so the stop is honest."
2. **A provenanced import from a frame whose closure differs** (Fig 6 :23, Fig 8 :50-52), with a source gate and a shadow test.

*They're Scanning Books* sharpens this: "The real import is not a new term in the existing space. It is a new dimension of the space." Also: "A minimum viable set of six terms is a human's careful guess at reality's compression axes; another sensorium's minimum set need not be those six." And: "Declaring the primitives does not make them neutral. It makes the choice visible."

For ecology: "network" is already one of the six structures, so we are not importing a new primitive. What is owed is an instrument for response asynchrony and interaction strength.

## 4. Why a roster of "good hubs" is the specific forbidden error

*The Hard Problem is About Source* gives the mechanism. Metamerism: an instrument reads green and "cannot comment on the source" - one green light, or yellow+blue, or RGB, or CMYK, or a scheme not yet discovered. The reading does not determine the source. "If several paths create the same behavior, then behavior is a projection with lossy information." And: "An instrument that can't distinguish these cases demonstrates its own inadequacy." It was the **spectrometer**, not an argument, that resolved it.

A connectance value or an observed persistence outcome is a reading. Naming the source of that reading as "this hub is stabilizing" is the metamerism error: several hub configurations could give the same persistence reading, and the reading does not discriminate among them. Fig 8 :49: "Two frames that overlap almost completely are one frame wearing two names." Yachi and Loreau (1999) derived the same discrimination result in population biology: with perfectly correlated responses, "the ecosystem behaves exactly as a single species".

*How a Society of Agents Would Work* makes the ecological link explicit. It glosses Fig 11 as: "nothing is bad in itself, only bad for a given goal, the way one species is both predator and prey." That is the valence rule in ecology's own vocabulary, and the reason a standing list of good hubs cannot survive a change of residual.

## 5. Q - what licenses a particular hub in a particular web?

**Nothing in the framework.** The framework returns the conditions under which valence flips. A particular hub's sign is licensed only by the ground settling a measurement (interaction strengths and response asynchrony under perturbation), or by a provenanced import with a shadow test. Until then, the name is the searcher's own contribution and must be recorded as such.

This is triangulated: Fig 11 :49-51 (valence relative to residual), Fig 2 :31-32 and Fig 8 :48-49 (overlap condition), and the ecology sources actually read. The figure verdict is single-source per line but the two figure families have low overlap.

## 5b. Correction to my own §3-§4

I initially wrote the "shape, not a name" rule as if it forbade naming any hub at all. It does not. The README's phrase is "what a **missing** thing would have to look like (a shape, not a name)". Its subject is a primitive not held in Γ. Network, overlap and correlation are already held (Fig 13, Fig 2, Fig 8), so reasoning with them is composition, not the invention of a new primitive. What remains forbidden is (i) naming a seventh primitive, (ii) claiming a binding moves the wall when it adds no atom (Fig 6: "IMPORT adds an atom"), and (iii) reading a source off a projection without a discriminating instrument. My §4 metamerism point is **not retracted**: the reason the roster fails is the missing discriminator, not a blanket ban on naming held atoms. Both claims are recorded so neither is quietly lost.

## 6. Two consequences for outreach

**Prestige is not action.** From *The Recursive Self-Improvement Trap*: "Prestige must never be allowed to validate an action directly." And: "Prestige can fund an action. It can give an action a budget to be tried. But it cannot settle an action. Only the ground can settle it. The Ground Maintainer must be blind to prestige." A campaign that gathers famous ecologists to agree with the valence claim is the named collapse, not corroboration. The legitimate ask is the opposite: give the claim a budget to be tried and let the ground, a perturbation experiment, settle it.

**My own audit is a simulation, not a ground reading.** Same essay: "LLMs can quickly simulate an approximate answer/output" and "the truly testable output would need to be derived from the real architecture or working the formula yourself." The architecture article is blunter: the outputs of an LLM prompted with the framework "must be seen as simulacra", and the solutions "could never be verified if the solutions presented were not contrived to fit the formula." So `01_figure_grounded_audit.md` is a simulated derivation, checked for internal consistency against the figures. It is **not** a test of the ecological claim and must be labelled as such. It cannot settle anything it reports on.

## 7. The honest stop

Fig 13 :134: "Unreachable is a verdict here, not a silence". Fig 8 :39: "a search that finds nothing is never a proof of absence". The figures give the condition but no numeric threshold for how much asynchrony is enough to flip the sign. That is an instrument question, not an argument. The lineage is recorded so a later reader can locate the stop rather than only feel it.
