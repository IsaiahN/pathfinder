## QUESTION

Decide, in effect terms, what the Tether says about a highly connected species (a hub or generalist) in an ecological network. Option A: high degree is stabilizing because extra links damp perturbations. Option B: high degree is destabilizing because it concentrates failure. What each leads to: A recommends protecting hubs and reads connectance as a predictor; B recommends treating hubs as liabilities. The figures decide neither A nor B as a fixed verdict. They give a shape whose sign flips with the residual and the overlap of partner responses. State the mode on each claim.

- **R (the residual).** The gap between the connectance prediction and what a web does under perturbation. Arity: one node property (degree) mapped to a system outcome through the typed relations incident on that slot. Symmetry: degree is symmetric only if links are lumped; real links are typed and often directed, so the effect is not one scalar. Scale: first order is direct partners, second and third order are the cascade. Fig 9 names this a union compound, not one hard question: a case in two conditions is not a harder case of either.

Currency: contact and persistence under perturbation. Anchor: the ground, what the web actually does after a perturbation, measured as persistence or observed extinction cascade. Question type: union of overlapping conditions.

## VERDICT (from the figures)

A highly connected species has no fixed sign. It stabilizes when the responses of the partners it links are independent and weak, so the union has a surplus; it destabilizes when those responses are correlated and concentrated, so the hub is one point of failure. Links are typed, the cascade is second or third order, stability is per slot, and the ground settles which regime a web is in. No roster of good hubs survives Fig 11 :51, and no global connectance preserves the per-slot signal. The verdict is `specified`, ungrounded, candidate. The structural shape is `general`.

- **cost if wrong:** a management decision about a hub (protect it or cull it) is inverted. That cost is bounded if the answer is delivered as a conditional with refuters rather than as a conclusion. The avoidable extra cost is spending an outside checker's independence by sending a recording instead of a generator. Fig 2 :42 "Buy independence in proportion to the cost of being wrong." is the governing price.
- **undoable:** yes. The shape can be refined without withdrawing any figure line. The regime classification is reversible as instruments improve, and a sent packet can be superseded by a corrected one. No primitive has to be withdrawn.
- **reversed by:** a ground reading, not agreement. A perturbation experiment showing that perfectly correlated hub partners still buffer as well as independent ones would break the flip condition; a direct-only model predicting first extinctions as well as a cascade model would break the order claim; a global connectance that preserves the live-slot signal would break the per-slot claim; a hub roster that keeps its sign across a change of residual would break Fig 11 :49-51. A figure re-census that made bond type carry no information would break Fig 12 :46-47. Until one of these fires, the verdict stands as `candidate`.
