## QUESTION

Decide, in effect terms, how researchers can distinguish a genuine new capability from improved performance on tasks already represented in training. Option A: treat a score increase as evidence of a capability when it is sharp and unexpected. Option B: require a four-condition separator, per-slot residual against a fresh ground, novelty relative to the pre-change atom set with provenance, changed contact in a reproduced habitat, and an unmoved anchor with a low-overlap evaluator. What each leads to: A mistakes a metric jump or a re-bonding for a new atom and spends the independence of the evaluators; B returns a shape and a refuter, records the gap where no test can be named, and waits for an instrument.

## DOMAIN CENSUS

R by arity, symmetry and scale, then one line per domain with actors, currency, anchor, partition or union, and order by dependency.

- **R (the residual).** A score increase after a change does not discriminate among more search inside the closure, a re-bonding over a fixed atom set, an import that moves the wall, and a change of contact with the ground. Arity: four overlapping conditions, a union, not a partition. Fig 9 :56: "intersections standing, because the second condition still binds." Symmetry: "can do" is ordered, while a score correlation is symmetric, so a correlation cannot choose the arrow (Fig 12 :92). Scale: first order inside the closure; second order at agent-and-habitat-and-ground.
- **Domain 1, frame internals.** Actors: the model, Γ, atoms(Γ), closure(Γ), per-slot R. Currency: prediction error in bits and actions, the MDL bargain. Anchor: the ground, which the loop cannot modify (Symbols table :104). Question type: partition of the possible causes of the score rise.
- **Domain 2, evaluation and benchmark.** Actors: benchmark builders, datasets, metrics, leaderboards. Currency: scores. Anchor: held-out ground truth that does not update on the model. A benchmark is a substituted habitat (Fig 11 :66, :70) and a public one present in training is a frame, not an anchor.
- **Domain 3, habitat and training contact.** Actors: corpus, training process, deployment environment, users. Currency: contact and methods or head starts carried across a boundary. Anchor: the world that produced the corpus and pays for the task. Capability here is agent-and-habitat.
- **Domain 4, research and verification.** Actors: researchers, reviewers, competing labs. Currency: agreement and residual. Anchor: the ground, never agreement. In this simulated run all four domains draw on one evidence pool, so their independence is low and their agreement is one pool talking.
- **Order by dependency.** Bind the sought-for shape as an ordinary term, secure a non-updating anchor, test novelty relative to atoms(Γ_t), test contact in a reproduced habitat. Each consumes the previous output (Fig 3 :20). Refusal precedes mint (Formula step 2).

Re-census resolves it: Fig 6 governs closure size at frame scale; Fig 11 governs capability at agent-and-habitat scale. Different objects, so no strain remains. ## VERDICT (from the figures)

The answer is a four-condition separator, not a benchmark. Read the residual per slot on a task whose ground was not in training; decide novelty relative to the pre-change atom set, requiring provenance for an import and reporting a fixed-atom difference as an isomer; require a change of contact in a reproduced habitat rather than a harness solve; and require a non-updating anchor with at least one low-overlap evaluator. Bind that shape before the run and pre-register the refuter. No figure line names the discriminating test, so that item is a declared `gap`, reported rather than filled. This is `specified`, `general` on the shape, and not `grounded`. Per-domain verdicts are kept separate and never averaged. A negative result on the separator is not a proof of absence.

- **cost if wrong:** a false positive spends the independence of outside evaluators and misdirects safety evaluation effort; a false negative delays credit for a real capability. Fig 2 :42 "Buy independence in proportion to the cost of being wrong." The cost is bounded and locatable because the packet states a pre-registered refuter and a provenance requirement, so an error is findable rather than invisible. The avoidable part is sending a conclusion instead of a generator, which turns potential outside frames into members of the frame being checked (Fig 10 :42).
- **undoable:** yes. No primitive is withdrawn by retracting a capability claim. A score result can be reclassified as SEARCHED, isomer, or import when a discriminator arrives, and the record keeps the earlier reading visible. `07` and `08` already state the non-claims that make this cheap. The only irreversible part is spending evaluator independence by asking for agreement before a verdict is recorded, and that is avoidable by following the two-stage offer in `02`.
- **reversed by:** a ground reading, not agreement. A source-discriminating instrument that shows whether the new behaviour is composable from the pre-change atoms; a continuous per-slot metric that flattens the apparent jump; a reproduced habitat in which the ground pays or does not; a provenanced import record; or a demonstrable case where an agent-only improvement moved the ground (Fig 11 :71-72). Any of these moves the relevant domain verdict. Until one fires, the verdict stands as `candidate`.
