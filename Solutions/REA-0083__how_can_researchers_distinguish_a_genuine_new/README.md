## What is deliberately not here

- No personal emails of named individuals.
- No ranking of researchers by how likely they are to agree with the shape claim.
- No bulk-send plan, and no claim that a benchmark score is a witness for a capability.

Three reasons, in the figures' own words:

1. **A benchmark in the training corpus is frame-internal.** Fig 1 :27: "They are frame-internal. A frame cannot score itself with a quantity it also produces." The score is a quantity the model's own frame helps produce.
2. **Agreement inside one evidence pool is one witness.** Fig 2 :40: "disqualifying for a verdict: agreement about the world is worthless when the agreeing frames share an evidence pool." Two models trained on the same corpus are not two frames.
3. **A room does not upgrade the label.** Fig 7 :13: "agreement compounds, evidence does not." Gathering more labs that agree with a claim adds votes, not ground contact.

Legally: unsolicited bulk email to harvested addresses breaches GDPR and CAN-SPAM style rules in many jurisdictions and gets the sending domain blacklisted. That is a practical failure on top of the structural one.
