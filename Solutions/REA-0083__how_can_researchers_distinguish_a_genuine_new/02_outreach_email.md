# Outreach draft: check the separator before you decide about the theory

**Use:** one to one, or as a cover letter attached to a submission through the public venues in `03_public_contact_points.csv`. **Not** for bulk send.

**Hard precondition:** do not send until the refuter in `01_figure_grounded_audit.md` section 4 has been written down and dated. The whole offer rests on stage 1 surviving without the recipient holding the framework's interior.

---

**Subject:** Would these four conditions separate a new capability from a benchmark gain?

> Hi [name],
>
> I have one specific output I would like you to check against what you already know about capability evaluation. I am not asking you to read the theory first, and I am not asking you to agree with it. I am asking what you would count as a failure. If the output does not survive, there is nothing else to look at.
>
> **The residual.** A model changes and scores better on tasks whose form was in its training data. A score increase does not tell us whether anything new became reachable or whether the model visited more of the same space. Those two readings predict the same number, so the number cannot decide between them.
>
> **The output, stated before the theory (`specified`, not `grounded`).** Four conditions separate the readings. (1) Read the residual per task slot, not as an aggregate, and use a task whose ground was not in training. (2) Ask whether the new behaviour is composable from the pre-change atom set. If it is not, an import from a differing frame is owed, with a provenance record. If it is, the before and after states are a real difference of arrangement, an isomer, not a new primitive. (3) Ask whether contact with a non-updating ground changed. A benchmark is a substituted habitat, so a harness solve proves wiring and never capability. (4) Require an anchor that does not update on the model's own outputs and at least one evaluator whose evidence does not come from the same corpus.
>
> **What this is not claiming.** It is not claiming that any named model has or lacks a capability. It is not claiming that scaling cannot produce something new. It is not claiming a benchmark, a threshold, or a percentage that decides the case. The framework returns a shape and a refuter, not a name for the test. There is no ground reading behind this, so it is `specified`, not `grounded`.
>
> **What would refute it.** I wrote these down before sending this:
> 1. A behaviour appears that is not composable from the old atom set, yet no differing frame or import can be found.
> 2. A continuous per-slot metric shows the same smooth trend across scales that the discontinuous benchmark showed as a jump, with no new term required.
> 3. The behaviour is derived from the old atom set under an existing bond, so the difference is an isomer rather than a new primitive.
> 4. The capability disappears when contaminated items are removed or the task is moved into a reproduced habitat with an independent ground.
> 5. The score moves while a non-updating ground shows no change in what the system can do.
> 6. An agent-only improvement moves the ground. That one is the framework's own standing refuter, and I am recording it against myself.
>
> A one-line "this fails because..." is more useful to me than agreement. If you'd rather not engage, no reply is needed. Please tell me your verdict on these four conditions **before** I show you any theory, so the verdict is not shaped by it.
>
> **If it survives, and only then:** the figures are below. They are the authority, not my summary of them.
> - Verbatim figure text with line numbers (local extract): `FRAMEWORK/reference/03-FIGURES_TEXT.md`
> - Canonical usage rule: `FRAMEWORK/reference/FIGURES_README_canonical.md`
> - The eight-step loop: `FRAMEWORK/figures/THE_FORMULA.md`
>
> **If you want to use the machinery:** the formula is in the last link. Run it on a problem you know well and can check against a ground. Write down what would refute your result *before* you run it, and state the mode of what you get. A reported failure is more useful to me than a reported success.
>
> — [name]

## Why this version is ordered this way

| the move | figure-grounded reason |
|---|---|
| Residual and refuter before theory | Fig 10 :52: "Bring a characterised residual, never the source." Frames range against the ground separately (Fig 2 :45). |
| Ask for a failure, not agreement | Fig 2 :35: "Alignment is triangulation, not negotiation. A mirror cannot correct you." |
| Verdict recorded before any theory is shown | Seat 4 "must not hold the interiors"; Fig 10 :42: "A verifier that can reconstruct the claim cannot verify it." |
| Pre-registered refuter | Operators :58: "a refutation is a negation, which is why a vocabulary without it can only accumulate." A falsifier written before the run is a design instrument. |
| Mode stated on the output | `THE_FORMULA.md` mode rule. Nothing here is settled by measurement. |
| No named benchmark or percentage | Fig 9 :32: "you get a kind of thing, never the answer itself." |
| Score as per-slot, not aggregate | Fig 1 :19: "The residual is indexed by object slot, not aggregated." |
| Capability as contact, not agent-only | Fig 11 :40: "An improvement that does not change contact changes nothing, however much it improves." |
| Harness solve not promoted to capability | Fig 11 :70: "A test harness is a substituted habitat. Which is why a synthetic solve proves wiring and never capability." |
| Independence from a separate evidence pool | Fig 2 :40: "disqualifying for a verdict: agreement about the world is worthless when the agreeing frames share an evidence pool." |
| Links after the verdict | Fig 4 :25: "A recording carried upward looks like knowledge and is a description of one occasion." |
| Offer the formula as a method, not an answer | Fig 4 :22: "Only methods cross up. Only head starts come down." |
| No request for trust or prestige | *The Recursive Self-Improvement Trap*: "Prestige can fund an action. It can give an action a budget to be tried. But it cannot settle an action. Only the ground can settle it." |

## What is deliberately absent

No statement that the recipient is likely to agree. No invitation to endorse. No mention of how well known anyone is. Prestige is a contact-cost estimate at most and never an evidence weight. Fig 7 :13: "agreement compounds, evidence does not."

## Stage order

1. **Stage 1:** send the residual, the four conditions, and the refuter. Record the verdict.
2. **Stage 2, opt in only:** if they are curious, offer the three links. Their later agreement is never pooled as corroboration (Fig 2 :40).
3. **Stage 3:** offer the formula as a reusable method, with the instruction that each person writes their own refuter and states their own mode.
