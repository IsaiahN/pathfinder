## 0. The residual in effect terms

R = |Γ(b,a) - o'| per slot s (Formula step 1; Symbols table, `R`, `s`):

- **b** is the inherited framework: GR, FLRW symmetry, the ΛCDM standard model, and the extrapolative habit that carries a well-tested model past its tested domain.
- **a** is running that model backwards, computing a scale factor, a temperature, a light-element yield, a last-scattering surface, and an extrapolated boundary at `t = 0`.
- **Γ(b,a)** is the model's prediction at each epoch.
- **o'** is what instruments return: CMB temperature and polarization anisotropies, primordial light-element abundances, redshift and distance measurements, the Hubble flow.
- **s** is the epoch under test. R is indexed by epoch, never averaged into one global "age" number (Fig 1 :19, Fig 1 :22).

> **"The residual is indexed by object slot, not aggregated."** (Fig 1 :19)
> **"Aggregating across slots is how a live signal disappears into an average."** (Fig 1 :22)

The phenomenon: the same extrapolation pays for a set of epochs and then produces a boundary term for which nothing is returned. The question bundles the well-paid epochs and the unpaid boundary into one phrase, "the beginning of time." State the residual as: *why does a model that is settled by instruments at nucleosynthesis and last scattering also license a claim about a first event for which no instrument returns anything?* This is an effect description. It names no theory of quantum gravity, no bounce, no no-boundary proposal. Those are candidate names and are withheld until the census.

**Bind the sought-for shape as an ordinary term** (Formula, "Direction"), before the run: the answer would be a boundary shape `BEGINNING(???)`, plus an anchor that would decide whether the boundary is a first event or a limit of the theory's applicability, plus an instrument that discriminates a genuine first event from a theory's own breakdown. Until bound, that shape predicts nothing and no loop check can see it. It is now bound, so section 4's refuter can fire on it.

**Anchor test.** Fig 2 :29: *"An anchor is whatever settles the question and does not change its answer because of what any frame thinks."* Fig 2 :30: *"you push on it, it is not an anchor."* The anchor candidates are the CMB, BBN abundances and the distance ladder. They do not move when a philosopher or a cosmologist changes their interpretation. The `t = 0` boundary does move when the theory changes: add quantum gravity, add a bounce, change the energy condition, and the boundary changes or disappears. It is therefore frame-internal, not an anchor. Separate anchors per slot, not one anchor for the bundled question.

## 1. Domain census (before choosing any figure line)

### 1.1 Arity, symmetry, scale of R

- **Arity.** The phrase under test is a three-place bundle: (C1) the earliest epoch the standard model explains and instruments confirm; (C2) the extrapolated boundary of that model; (C3) the claim that time itself begins there. C1 and C2 share a theory but not an anchor. C3 shares neither. The bundle therefore has three conditions, not one hard question (Fig 9 :4-5).
- **Symmetry.** "Earlier than" is an **ordered** bond, sequence `→`: *"A then B; B needs A to have happened"* (Fig 12 :72). It is not symmetric. "Beginning" is a **threshold** against a boundary, comparison `⋛`: *"a threshold relation between two quantities"* (Fig 12 :87). A boundary is not a member of the series it bounds, which is the source of the trouble in section 3 F6.
- **Scale.** Three scales are in view: the single epoch slot (BBN at about one second, last scattering at about 380,000 years), the domain of applicability of the model (the closure of GR plus the standard model), and the boundary of that domain. The membrane between them is *"Not a wall: a filter with a direction"* (Fig 4 :21) and the transform is lossy, so the round-trip gap R_T must be measured, not assumed (Fig 4 :30).

### 1.3 Partition or union

D1 and D2 overlap: the same extrapolation that fits the CMB produces the boundary term. D2 and D3 overlap: past-incompleteness is precisely the residual the candidate boundary frames address. D3 and D4 overlap: the choice of which candidate to test is a verification decision. The whole question is a **union**, not a partition.

> **"And when the split lands on a union rather than a partition, unbundle before you search again."** (Fig 9 :54)
> **"A bundled problem is usually a union of overlapping conditions, not a list of separate ones."** (Fig 9 :55)
> **"What remains is smaller and it is also categorically different: a case in two conditions is not a harder case of either."** (Fig 9 :57)

The unbundled conditions and their anchors:

- **C1, the earliest explained era.** Anchor: instruments. Settled from nucleosynthesis onward, unsettled before it in the sense that no instrument returns a reading there.
- **C2, the theory's own boundary.** Anchor: mathematical proof, which settles incompleteness and does not settle origination.
- **C3, a first event in time.** Anchor: none held. This is where `BEGINNING(???)` is written.

C1 and C2 have different instruments. A lever that clears C1, for example a better fit to the CMB, leaves C2 standing. A lever that clears C2, for example a nonsingular model, leaves the question of whether C1's fitted era is all that is being asked. That is why the verdicts in `09` are kept separate and never averaged.

### 1.4 Dependency order (Fig 3)

> **"The chain is ordered by dependency, not by importance."** (Fig 3 :20)
> **"A link with no instrument is not a link that works. It is a link nobody has looked at."** (Fig 3 :27)

The break is at link 2 to 3. Most cosmological effort lands above the break because that is where the measurements already exist (Fig 3 :29-30).

## 3. Findings

Mode tag on each: `general` if structural, `specified` if a falsifiable prediction, `grounded` only if settled by a measurement. None is `grounded`.

### F1: The Big Bang earns its keep as the earliest empirically explained era

**Mode: `specified`. Domain D1. Status: triangulated (Fig 1 and Fig 2, low topical overlap: per-slot metric vs anchor).**

> **"The ground enters as the only metric."** (Fig 1 :23)
> **"An anchor is whatever settles the question and does not change its answer because of what any frame thinks."** (Fig 2 :29)
> **"Only the actors, the currency and the anchor change."** (Fig 4 :20)

The standard model is confirmed by independent lines of evidence from nucleosynthesis onward. The world source *Philosophy of Cosmology* states: *"The SM from nucleosynthesis on can be regarded as well supported by many lines of evidence."* NASA's public history states: *"It is the oldest light we can observe in the universe."* These are two witnesses from different strata (a reference work and an institutional communicator), but both are secondary: the ground is the instrument, not the sentence.

**Refuter:** a well-measured early-universe observation after nucleosynthesis that the model cannot fit at any parameter choice, or a demonstration that BBN abundances and the CMB are not independent anchors (shared systematics that move together under a push). Either breaks F1 as written.

### F2: The extrapolated boundary is frame-internal, not an anchor

**Mode: `specified`. Domains D1 and D2. Status: triangulated (Fig 1 and Fig 2, low overlap).**

> **"They are frame-internal. A frame cannot score itself with a quantity it also produces."** (Fig 1 :27)
> **"you push on it, it is not an anchor."** (Fig 2 :30)

The age and the boundary are outputs of the same model that is being tested. Change the model and they change. The singularity in the FLRW extrapolation is a placeholder where the theory reports its own invalidity; the SEP entry *Cosmology and Theology* says: *"In fact, relativistic cosmology predicts its own invalidity for times close to a dynamic singularity, such as the big bang."* The same source states the limit plainly: *"Hence, it is an empirical, rather than a metaphysical, question whether the big bang was the beginning of the universe."*

**Refuter:** an observation that fixes a first event independently of any model extrapolation, that is, an anchor for `t = 0` that does not move when the theory changes. If such an anchor exists and returns a stable first event, F2 is false.

### F3: Incompleteness settles a boundary of the theory, not a first event in the world

**Mode: `specified`. Domain D2. Status: single-source on the proof (Fig 6 :37 plus the BGV and SEP world sources; one evidence pool in the cosmology-and-singularity literature).**

> **"It tells a question that nothing could ever answer apart from one that is merely hard. It is not a rule about theory."** (Fig 6 :37)
> **"you get a kind of thing, never the answer itself"** (Fig 9 :32)

The Borde-Guth-Vilenkin result states: *"Thus inflationary models require physics other than inflation to describe the past boundary of the inflating region of spacetime."* That is a mathematical claim about past incompleteness. It licenses a shape: a past boundary is owed. It does not name what occupies it. Fig 9 :32 returns a kind of thing, never the answer. The SEP entry *Singularities and Black Holes* sharpens the point: *"Cosmic time 'zero' is a label attached to no event."*

**Refuter:** a derivation that turns geodesic incompleteness into a physical first event without adding a premise. Such a derivation would be an import in disguise and would owe a source gate and a shadow test (Fig 6 :14, Fig 8 :50).

### F4: The question is a union of three conditions, not one question

**Mode: `general`. Domains all. Status: figure-derived (Fig 9).**

The governing lines Fig 9 :54, :55 and :57 are quoted in section 1.3. The three conditions have different anchors: instruments for C1, a proof for C2, nothing held for C3. A philosopher who answers only C1 and a cosmologist who answers only C2 are not disagreeing about one hard question; they are answering different well-formed ones. This is why the exemplar's union rule applies before any theory is chosen.

**Refuter:** a single instrument that determines both the explained era and the absolute origin at once, leaving no categorically different residue. That would collapse the union into a partition.

### F5: Candidate boundary frames are imports with no source gate, and they stand as isomers where they share an atom set

**Mode: `specified`. Domains D2 and D3. Status: triangulated for the import rule (Fig 6 and Fig 8, distinct roles), single-source for the isomer claim (Fig 12).**

> **"the only operator that moves the wall"** (Fig 6 :14)
> **"No frame escapes its own closure. The bound moves only on contact with a frame whose closure differs."** (Fig 6 :23)
> **"Convergent derivation and adopted import look identical in the contents."** (Fig 8 :50)
> **"isomer. Same atoms, different bond, different substance. The difference is invisible in the formula and decisive in the behaviour."** (Fig 12 :117)

The no-boundary proposal, the loop bounce, the past-eternal NEC-violating construction and the cyclic conformal model are different arrangements offered for the same slot. Lehners's review says: *"the big bang singularity is avoided."* Easson and Lesnefsky report: *"we present a simple, explicit class of inflationary solutions that are smooth, nonsingular, and geodesically complete for all time, including into the past."* These are candidate imports, not settled terms. They have provenance (each is published and dated) but no shadow test against a discriminating observation, so they carry zero verdict weight (Fig 6 :23, Fig 8 :50).

**Refuter:** an observation at large angular scales that discriminates two of them, or a demonstration that two candidates do not share the atom set, in which case the isomer claim falls back to the functional-group claim (Fig 12 :124): *"the group says what kind; the qualifier says which member."*

### F6: "Beginning" is a bond that presupposes the substrate it is asked to explain

**Mode: `general`. Domains D2 and X1. Status: figure-derived (Fig 12 and Fig 13).**

> **"A then B; B needs A to have happened"** (Fig 12 :72)
> **"the ordered succession of differences"** (Fig 13 :12)

"Beginning" is a sequence boundary: it needs events ordered by time. But the claim "time began" puts the ordering itself at the boundary, so the term is asked to explain the substrate it runs on. Time is one of the six held states (Fig 13 :12), not a missing primitive. What is missing is the referent of the boundary ordering, not the term time. This is the narrowed form of the shape-not-a-name rule recorded as correction R2 in section 6.

**Refuter:** a model in which sequence and comparison operate without an ordered time substrate. Then the reification charge fails and C3 becomes a well-formed condition with an anchor to be named.

### F7: The CMB is an artifact and does not carry the route that produced it

**Mode: `specified`. Domains D1 and D3. Status: triangulated (Fig 13 and the essay *The Hard Problem is About Source*, low overlap in subject matter but same framework author, so one pool).**

> **"Nothing about a composed term tells you, from looking at it, which route produced it or whether that route was available."** (Fig 13 :129)
> **"describe it before you go looking, or any frame you pick will seem to fit"** (Fig 9 :27-28)

The essay *The Hard Problem is About Source* gives the mechanism with metamerism: an instrument reads green and *"it cannot comment on the source."* The spectrometer, not an argument, resolved the source. Here the CMB reads a nearly scale-invariant spectrum, and the source (inflation, bounce, no-boundary, something else) is not in the reading. Naming the source from the reading is the metamerism error.

**Refuter:** a trace in the large-scale CMB or in primordial non-Gaussianity that carries its own provenance and uniquely fixes the boundary route. The essay's own distinction matters: where traces exist, depth recovers them; where they do not, no depth helps (lab-grown versus mined diamonds).

### F8: Agreement and prestige cannot settle any of the three conditions

## 4. Pre-registered refuter (written before the conclusion)

Per the design-instrument rule: *"a falsifier written before the run is a design instrument, not only an honesty device."* The shape claim is refuted if any of the following is observed:

1. An anchor for `t = 0` that does not move when the theory changes, and that returns a stable first event (breaks F2, Fig 2 :29-30).
2. A derivation that yields a physical first event from geodesic incompleteness with no added premise (breaks F3, Fig 9 :32).
3. A single instrument that settles the explained era and the absolute origin together, leaving no categorically different residue (breaks F4, Fig 9 :57).
4. An observation that discriminates two candidate boundary frames at large angular scales, or a demonstration that their atom sets do not overlap (breaks F5, Fig 12 :117).
5. A model in which sequence and comparison operate with no ordered time substrate (breaks F6, Fig 12 :72, Fig 13 :12).
6. A trace in the CMB that uniquely identifies the boundary route (breaks F7, Fig 13 :129).

No part of this solution may be upgraded to `grounded` until at least one of these has been run and has failed to fire, and until an instrument pays for the shape.
