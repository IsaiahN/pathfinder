## The question restated as an effect

What is being decided is not how old the universe is, but what the phrase "beginning of time" would have to refer to for it to be settled. In effect terms, R = |Γ(b,a) - o'| is measured per slot s (Formula step 1; Symbols table, `R`):

- **b** is the inherited framework of GR, FLRW symmetry and the standard model of cosmology.
- **a** is the act of running that model backwards toward early times.
- **Γ(b,a)** is what the model predicts at each epoch: a hot dense state, light-element abundances at about one second, a last-scattering surface, a scale factor tending to zero at the extrapolated boundary.
- **o'** is what instruments actually return: the CMB, BBN abundances, Hubble-flow and distance measurements.
- **s** is the epoch being tracked. R is indexed by epoch, never averaged into a single "age of the universe" story (Fig 1 :19, Fig 1 :22).

The residual is this: the model pays for slots from nucleosynthesis onward, where instruments return something, and it produces a boundary term `t = 0` for which no instrument returns anything. The question asks whether one anchor settles both.

- Option A, "the Big Bang is the beginning of time": leads to the prediction that a first event is settled by the same extrapolation that fits the CMB and BBN.
- Option B, "the Big Bang is the earliest era current theories can explain": leads to the prediction that the fitted era is anchored by instruments, while the extrapolated boundary is frame-internal and moves when the theory changes.

What an answer would look like, written down and bound as an ordinary term before the run (Formula, "Direction"): a boundary shape `BEGINNING(???)`, a stated anchor that would decide whether the boundary is a first event or a limit of the theory's applicability, and an instrument that discriminates a genuine first event from a theory's own breakdown. Until bound, that shape predicts nothing and no loop check can see it. It is now bound, so the refuter in `01` section 4 can fire on it.

The verdict in `09_watson_answer.md` is Option B in `specified` mode, kept per domain and never averaged.

## What is deliberately not here

- No personal emails of named individuals.
- No ranking of people by how likely they are to agree.
- No bulk-send plan.
- No claim that this derivation is `grounded`, no claim that it settles whether time began, and no claim that the framework is the only true account of cosmic origins.

Three figure-grounded reasons, plus one practical one:

1. A room is not an anchor. *"A room's agreement is variation, not a verdict. Selection between rooms is where the ground votes."* (Fig 7 :35) and *"Alignment is triangulation, not negotiation. A mirror cannot correct you."* (Fig 2 :35). Gathering agreeable readers manufactures the correlation that disqualifies a verdict.
2. Correlation has valence. *"disqualifying for a verdict: agreement about the world is worthless when the agreeing frames share an evidence pool"* (Fig 2 :40). A list selected for receptiveness is one witness wearing many names.
3. A verifier must be outside the interior. *"A verifier that can reconstruct the claim cannot verify it."* (Fig 10 :42). The first contact therefore carries the residual and its refuter, not the framework.
4. Legally, unsolicited bulk email to scraped addresses breaches GDPR and CAN-SPAM style rules in many jurisdictions and gets the sending domain blacklisted. Public opt-in routes avoid that failure as well as the structural one.
