## Two things in the wider literature that bear directly on this question

1. **The singularity result is self-limiting.** *Cosmology and Theology* states that relativistic cosmology *"predicts its own invalidity for times close to a dynamic singularity, such as the big bang."* That is an instance of Fig 6 :37: a rule that tells you a question is hard is not a rule that nothing could ever answer it, and it is not a rule about theory. The same entry records the conventionality of the finite/infinite time distinction (Milne, Misner) and the order-theoretic problem that FRW models *"have no first moment of time."* This corroborates F2 and F6 but is secondary: the figures govern.
2. **The candidate boundary frames genuinely conflict.** BGV establishes past-incompleteness under its hypotheses; Easson and Lesnefsky construct a past-complete class with localized NEC violation; Lehners's no-boundary review removes the singularity by summing over geometries with no past boundary. These are not three reports of one fact. Per Fig 2 :40, papers drawing on the same CMB pool are one witness, so the conflict cannot be resolved here by counting papers. It returns the derivation to the census: D3 is a candidate domain with no discriminating anchor (see F5 and retraction R4).
