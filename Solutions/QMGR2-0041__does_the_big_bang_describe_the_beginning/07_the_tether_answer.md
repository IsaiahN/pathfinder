# The Tether's answer: does the Big Bang describe the beginning of time?

**Mode: `specified` on the boundary shape and the union reading, `general` on the structural rules. Not `grounded`.** This is a shape and a route, not a cosmological result. Simulated derivation checked against the figures (`05_what_shape_means.md` section 6), not a ground reading.

## 2. The shape

1. **The Big Bang names an epoch, not a first event.** The standard model earns its keep from nucleosynthesis onward, where instruments pay. Before that, the model's domain is uncertain. SEP *Philosophy of Cosmology*: *"The SM from nucleosynthesis on can be regarded as well supported by many lines of evidence."* NASA: *"Scientists aren't sure what came before inflation or what powered it."* Both are secondary; the ground is the instrument.
2. **The extrapolated boundary is frame-internal.** Fig 2 :30: *"you push on it, it is not an anchor."* The `t = 0` term moves when the theory changes: add quantum gravity, a bounce or a no-boundary proposal and the boundary changes or disappears. Fig 1 :27: *"They are frame-internal. A frame cannot score itself with a quantity it also produces."*
3. **Incompleteness settles a boundary of the theory, not a first event.** BGV establishes past-incompleteness under its hypotheses: *"Thus inflationary models require physics other than inflation to describe the past boundary of the inflating region of spacetime."* That licenses a shape, not a name. Fig 9 :32: *"you get a kind of thing, never the answer itself."* SEP *Singularities and Black Holes*: *"Cosmic time 'zero' is a label attached to no event."*
4. **"Beginning" is a bond that presupposes its substrate.** Sequence, *"A then B; B needs A to have happened"* (Fig 12 :72), and comparison, *"a threshold relation between two quantities"* (Fig 12 :87), both run on the ordered succession of differences that is time (Fig 13 :12). The claim that time began asks the bond to explain the thing it runs on. The slot is marked `BEGINNING(???)`.
5. **Candidate boundary frames are imports with no shadow test.** Lehners's no-boundary review, the loop bounce, the past-eternal construction and the conformal cyclic model are provenanced publications (Fig 6 :23) but none is discriminated by an instrument. Fig 8 :50: *"Convergent derivation and adopted import look identical in the contents."* At best they stand as isomers over a shared atom set, Fig 12 :117: *"isomer. Same atoms, different bond, different substance."* The weaker functional-group reading stands where the atom sets are not shared.
6. **The CMB does not carry its own route.** Fig 13 :129: *"Nothing about a composed term tells you, from looking at it, which route produced it or whether that route was available."* *The Hard Problem is About Source*: an instrument that reads green *"cannot comment on the source."* Naming a boundary frame from the CMB is that metamerism error.

## 3. What it explicitly does not say

- Not that the universe is infinitely old, and not that it is finitely old. The framework returns neither verdict from these instruments.
- Not that the singularity theorems prove creation. They establish incompleteness under stated hypotheses; the SEP *Cosmology and Theology* entry notes that *"relativistic cosmology predicts its own invalidity for times close to a dynamic singularity, such as the big bang."*
- Not that inflation occurred, that there was a bounce, or that any named boundary proposal is correct. Those are candidate bindings recorded with provenance, not derivations and not wall-moving imports.
- Not a theory of everything and not a final room (Fig 7 :6, *"the limit: no last room"*).
- Not `grounded`. Nothing here is asserted as settled by an experiment at the boundary.
- Not that "before the Big Bang" is meaningful in the ordinary temporal sense. The sequence bond presupposes the substrate; see item 4.

## 4. What would settle it - the ground

A discriminator, not a further argument. The essay *The Hard Problem is About Source*: *"It was proven wrong not by argument, but by the arrival of a better instrument."* Here:

- **Anchor for `t = 0`:** an observation that fixes a first event independently of any model extrapolation and does not move when the theory changes (breaks F2).
- **Derivation test:** a proof that yields a first event from geodesic incompleteness with no added premise (breaks F3).
- **Union test:** one instrument that settles the explained era and the origin together with no categorically different residue (breaks F4).
- **Discriminator:** a large-angular-scale or non-Gaussianity trace that separates two candidate boundary frames, or uniquely identifies the route (breaks F5 and F7).
- **Grammar test:** a model in which sequence and comparison operate with no ordered time substrate (breaks F6).
