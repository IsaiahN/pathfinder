## 1. A shape is a constraint, not a filler

From *The Library of Babel*: *"you can cut your search short if the set of books returned is small and descriptive enough that it provides a general shape of the answer that satisfies your question."* From *How Intelligence Actually Works*, the replicator passage: at some point *"the recursion of the 'shape' will have carved away at so many aspects, that it will fall out of perceptible range and be indistinguishable, in your frame, from the answer."*

So a **shape** is the narrowed form of an answer you can state before you can name it. It constrains; it does not fill. `BEGINNING(???)` is a shape. `BEGINNING(a first singularity event)` is a name. The distinction matters here because the phrase "the beginning of time" already sounds like a name. In the framework it is only a slot with a boundary condition.

## 2. How a shape is obtained

- *Library of Babel*: use the vocabulary you already have that is *closest* to the thing; look for recurring new terms that overlap or correlate with it; recurse. *"With each new term gained, you narrow the space and deepen your vocabulary. This is a bootstrapping convergence process."*
- Fig 6 :30: *"The description travels. Fails by being sharpened into terms only we can read. Cast it in effect terms."* Fig 9 :30-31: *"your words are borrowed too, so describe the effect rather than the cause."*
- *Decoding Tacit Knowledge*: *"When contact isn't possible, the best you can send is a plain description of what you'd expect to see, because anyone can check that against the world."* This is why the first email asks for a failure description rather than assent.
- *Library of Babel* on stopping: *"You will know that you are done because the finish line will also be the starting place, as there is nowhere else to search and nothing else that can describe the answer better."* The paradox is that by then *"you have already composed the answer through the process of locating it."* For this question the finish line is not an answer; it is the honest boundary shape where instruments stop returning a difference.
- *How a Society of Agents Would Work* adds the room rule: *"The room composition is a function of the question. When the question changes, the room should change."* A boundary question needs a room of cosmologists and philosophers of physics who read different evidence, not a room of people who already agree.

## 3. What licenses a name

Only two things, both outside the shape itself:

1. **The ground settles it.** Formula step 5: *"the ground settles it. Verified only by what pays. A gate is not the ground."* For this question, an instrument that discriminates a first event from a theory breakdown would settle the referent of the boundary.
2. **A provenanced import from a frame whose closure differs** (Fig 6 :23, Fig 8 :50), with a source gate and a shadow test.

*They're Scanning Books When They Should Be Studying Nature* sharpens this. *"The real import is not a new term in the existing space. It is a new dimension of the space."* And: *"A minimum viable set of six terms is a human's careful guess at reality's compression axes; another sensorium's minimum set need not be those six."* Also: *"Declaring the primitives does not make them neutral. It makes the choice visible."*

The six are a visible guess, not a discovery of nature's own list. Turning a boundary proposal into *the* answer turns a visible choice into an invisible one.

## 4. Why naming a boundary frame from the CMB is the specific forbidden error

*The Hard Problem is About Source* gives the mechanism. Metamerism: an instrument reads green and *"it cannot comment on the source."* One green light, or yellow plus blue, or RGB, or CMYK, or a scheme not yet discovered can produce the same reading. *"If several paths create the same behavior, then behavior is a projection with lossy information (it cannot define consciousness in full)."* It was the spectrometer, not an argument, that resolved the source: *"It was proven wrong not by argument, but by the arrival of a better instrument."*

The CMB is the artifact. Its near scale invariance does not record which boundary proposal produced it. Naming no-boundary, bounce, or past-eternal from that reading is the metamerism error: a source named from a reading where several sources give the same reading. What is owed is a discriminator, not a name.

*How Intelligence Actually Works* supplies the second half: *"often, very little about a composed term tells you, from looking at it alone, which route produced it."* The video-frame versus photograph case is the exact shape: *"the frame and the photograph are indistinguishable as artifacts, and they carry different warrants."*

**A correction that matters here.** The same essay draws a limit in its own favour: *"Being 'below the union horizon' is never a settled verdict, only a report that nobody has reached it yet."* So the `gap` in `01` F9 is a report about the current instruments, not an assertion that no boundary could ever be settled. Fig 8 :39 says the same: *"So a search that finds nothing is never a proof of absence."*

## 5. Q: what licenses the phrase "the beginning of time"?

**Answered.** Nothing in the framework does. The framework returns the boundary shape `BEGINNING(???)`, an anchor test showing that the extrapolated boundary is frame-internal, and a list of refuters. A particular boundary frame is licensed only by an instrument that discriminates it, or by a provenanced import with a shadow test. Until one of those, the name is the searcher's own contribution and must be recorded as such.

**Does the folder commit the error the README forbids?** No, and the reason is recorded: the framework returns a shape for a *missing* thing. What is missing here is the referent of the boundary ordering, not the term "time" and not the observed early universe. See correction R2 in `01` section 6.

## 5b. Correction to my own earlier reading

I first wrote that "shape, not a name" forbids saying anything about time. That overstates the rule. The README's phrase governs *"what a **missing** thing would have to look like."* Time is a held state (Fig 13 :12: *"the ordered succession of differences"*). What is not held is the referent of a boundary to ordered succession. Naming that referent is forbidden only in the sense that naming any missing primitive is forbidden; naming a held state, or naming a well-published candidate boundary frame as a provenanced binding, is allowed if the provenance is recorded. The claim that survives is the narrower one, and both the first reading and its narrowing are kept visible here and in `11_audit.md`.

## 6. Two consequences for outreach

**Prestige is not action.** From *The Recursive Self-Improvement Trap*: *"Prestige must never be allowed to validate an action directly."* And: *"Prestige can fund an action. It can give an action a budget to be tried. But it cannot settle an action. Only the ground can settle it. The Ground Maintainer must be blind to prestige."* A campaign that gathers famous names to agree that the universe began is the named collapse, not corroboration. The legitimate ask is the opposite: give the boundary claim a budget to be tried, let the ground settle it.

**My own audit is a simulation, not a ground reading.** Same essay: *"the output is only as good as the validator. The agent, or the tool, or the frame, or the theory, cannot validate itself."* And: *"LLMs can quickly simulate an approximate answer/output... However, the truly testable output would need to be derived from the real architecture or working the formula yourself."* So `01` is a simulated derivation checked for internal consistency against the figures. It is **not** a test of the cosmological claim and must be labelled as such. It cannot settle anything it reports on.

**Provenance is a discipline, not a decoration.** *They're Scanning Books* states the discipline that makes this folder checkable: *"where a task is itself built on human priors, the priors are labeled and their provenance recorded, added because the task required them, marked as what they are, so a later reader can see exactly what was assumed and what was earned."* That is what `10_source_ledger.csv` does for every source used here.
