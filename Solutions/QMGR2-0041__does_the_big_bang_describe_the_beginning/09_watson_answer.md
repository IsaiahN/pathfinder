## VERDICT (from the figures)

Option B in `specified` mode. The Big Bang describes the earliest era current theories can explain, confirmed epoch by epoch by instruments; it does not describe an anchored beginning of time. The extrapolated `t = 0` boundary is a limit of the theory's applicability, frame-internal and movable, so it is not an anchor. Past-incompleteness settles the shape of a boundary, not what occupies it. "Beginning" is a sequence threshold and a comparison that presupposes the ordered time it is asked to explain, so the slot stays `BEGINNING(???)`. Candidate boundary frames are provenanced imports with no shadow test and stand at best as isomers over a shared atom set. The three conditions keep separate verdicts, never averaged. Which boundary frame, if any, is correct is a `gap`: unreached at this library and budget, not absent and not unknowable in principle.

- **cost if wrong:** bounded and locatable. The packet is mode-labeled, states refuters before the conclusion and records provenance, so an error is findable. The real avoidable cost is spending the independence of outside readers by sending a named boundary frame or a "the universe began" verdict before the shape has been checked. Fig 2 :42 prices that: *"Buy independence in proportion to the cost of being wrong."*
- **undoable:** yes. The named-frame binding is removable without touching the derivation; `07_the_tether_answer.md` and `08_expert_verification_packet.md` already remove it. A sent claim can be superseded by the corrected packet, and no primitive has to be withdrawn.
- **reversed by:** a ground reading, not agreement. An anchor for `t = 0` that does not move with the theory, a derivation of a first event from incompleteness with no added premise, one instrument that settles era and origin together, a trace that discriminates boundary frames, or a model without an ordered time substrate would each move the verdict.
