## Round trip gap R_T

The Symbols table defines R_T as the gap between what was sent up and what came back down, `gap(x, (T_E ∘ T_A)(x))`, measuring what the coarser description cannot hold. **R_T is nonzero.** Four things the shorthand holds do not survive the trip back down cleanly:

- **The process-discriminating instrument.** The shorthand says "observations anchor"; it does not carry which instrument separates surface mass balance from dynamical discharge at the slot. That is a discriminator still owed.
- **The threshold operand type.** The shorthand says "tail unreached"; the concrete case applies a comparison bond ⋛ whose operands (a warming level and a geometric limit) are typed differently in Operators :49. The figures do not settle the type.
- **The round-trip gap itself.** Converting a 2100 rate into a committed multi-century total loses information the coarser description cannot hold. Treating "rate plus commitment" as a single time derivative would set R_T to zero and be the dead-reckoning error Fig 4 :30 names.
