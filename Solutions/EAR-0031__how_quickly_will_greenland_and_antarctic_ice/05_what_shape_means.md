## 1. A shape is a constraint, not a filler or a number

The canonical README gives the limit: *"what a missing thing would have to look like (a shape, not a name)."* Fig 9 :32: *"you get a kind of thing, never the answer itself"* Fig 13 :135: *"It cannot say which primitive is missing, because naming it would mean already holding it."*

From *The Library of Babel*: *"you can cut your search short if the set of books returned is small and descriptive enough that it provides a general shape of the answer that satisfies your question."* And: *"With each new term gained, you are able to narrow the space by definition and deepen your vocabulary."*

So a **shape** is the narrowed form of an answer you can state before you can name it. It constrains; it does not fill. For this question the shape is not a millimetre-per-year value. It is: the residual is indexed per sheet and per process, the union is unbundled before search, rate and committed total are separate quantities, the deep-uncertainty tail is stated separately, and the anchor is observation. That is enough to tell a wrong answer from a right-shaped one without supplying the number.

## 2. How the shape is obtained here

- *Library of Babel*: use the vocabulary you already have closest to the thing, look for recurring new terms that overlap or correlate with it, recurse. *"With each new term gained, you narrow the space and deepen your vocabulary."*
- Fig 9 :27-28: *"describe it before you go looking, or any frame you pick will seem to fit."* Fig 9 :30-31: *"your words are borrowed too, so describe the effect rather than the cause."*
- *Decoding Tacit Knowledge*: *"When contact isn't possible, the best you can send is a plain description of what you'd expect to see, because anyone can check that against the world."*
- *How Intelligence Actually Works*: the recursion converges toward indiscernibility, and *"indiscernibility is not identity."* The lineage must be kept even when the copy is perfect.
- *Library of Babel* on stopping: *"you will know that you are done because the finish line will also be the starting place"*, because by then you have *"composed the answer through the process of locating it."* Here the stopping place is the shape, and the remaining gap is owed to the ground.

The practical consequence: I wrote the shape down as an ordinary term in `01` section 0 and bound it before deriving, so the frame's preference for a single number can accrue a residual and be refused. That is the formula's "Direction" discipline applied to this question.

## 3. What licenses a number (not a shape) here

Only two things, both outside the shape itself:

1. **The ground settles it.** Formula step 5: *"the ground settles it. Verified only by what pays."* The README says the framework *"stops at the limit of what the available instruments can distinguish, and records the lineage so the stop is honest."* Here that means satellite altimetry, gravimetry, tide gauges, and process observations.
2. **A provenanced import from a frame whose closure differs** (Fig 6 :23, Fig 8 :51), with a source gate and a shadow test. The observational record is such an import, but it is supplied by the world sources, not generated by the figures.

*They're Scanning Books*: *"The real import is not a new term in the existing space. It is a new dimension of the space."* And: *"A minimum viable set of six terms is a human's careful guess at reality's compression axes; another sensorium's minimum set need not be those six."* Also: *"Declaring the primitives does not make them neutral. It makes the choice visible."*

So the six-term set is a visible guess at composition, not a parts list for a glacier. Mapping "ice equals a state" would turn a visible choice into an invisible one. That is why I retracted my first-pass inventory mapping in `01` section 6.

## 4. Why the assessed ranges are not the ground

*The Hard Problem is About Source* gives the mechanism. Metamerism: an instrument reads green and *"cannot comment on the source."* One green light, or yellow plus blue, or RGB, or CMYK, or a scheme not yet discovered. *"If several paths create the same behavior, then behavior is a projection with lossy information."* It was the spectrometer, not an argument, that resolved it. *"An instrument that can't distinguish these cases demonstrates its own inadequacy."*

Applied here: an assessed "likely range" is a reading of many models and observations. It does not by itself comment on which process produced the spread. Where surface mass balance, ice dynamics, ocean forcing, and deep-uncertainty instabilities can produce overlapping readings, the range is a projection with lossy information. The instrument owed is a process-discriminating observation, not another argument about the range.

*The Recursive Self-Improvement Trap*: *"the output is only as good as the validator. The agent, or the tool, or the frame, or the theory, cannot validate itself."* A forecast that validates itself by agreement with other forecasts is exactly the named collapse.

## 5. Q for this question: does the six-term set name the ice-sheet mechanism?

**No.** The set governs composition at any level; it does not contain ice, ocean heat, grounding lines, or ice cliffs. The framework returns a shape. A specific physical binding (for example, ocean heat as the gradient driving ice-shelf basal melt) is a binding from the earth-systems frame onto held atoms, supplied by provenance, not a derivation from the six. Until a source-discriminating instrument settles it, the binding carries `specified` mode and no more.

## 5b. Correction to my own §3 and §4 (kept visible)

I initially overstated "shape, not a name" as a blanket ban on naming any component. The README's phrase is *"what a **missing** thing would have to look like (a shape, not a name)."* The subject is a missing primitive, one not held in Γ. The physical components here are not candidates for the six-term set at all; they belong to the world frame. So the correct statement is narrower: the figures cannot name a missing atom or a numerical rate, but they also do not forbid naming frame-supplied bindings, provided the provenance is recorded. What remains wrong in a naive reading is treating the assessed range as if it identified its own source. That error is retained in §4; the reason is the missing discriminator, not a ban on naming.

## 6. Prestige, agreement, and the cost of being wrong

From *The Recursive Self-Improvement Trap*: *"Prestige must never be allowed to validate an action directly."* And: *"Prestige can fund an action. It can give an action a budget to be tried. But it cannot settle an action. Only the ground can settle it. The Ground Maintainer must be blind to prestige."*

For this question the prestige proxy is the number of agreeing models or the authority of an assessment. That gives a projection a budget to be tried; it does not settle the rate. The cost if wrong is high (coastal capital committed for decades), so Fig 2 :42 requires buying independence in proportion to that cost.

*How a Society of Agents Would Work* gives the operational rule: *"A group is only worth having while its members stay different."* And: *"The group's agreement is never the verdict."* Ten model ensembles watching the same forcing data are one opinion said ten times.

## 7. My own audit is a simulation, not a ground reading

Same essay, verbatim: *"LLMs can quickly simulate an approximate answer/output with the formula"* and *"the truly testable output would need to be derived from the real architecture or working the formula yourself."* The architecture article is blunter: outputs generated by an LLM prompt are *"simulacra"* and *"could never be verified if the solutions presented were not contrived to fit the formula."*

So `01`, `06`, `07`, `08`, `09` and `11` are simulated derivations checked for internal consistency against the figures. They are **not** tests of any ice-sheet claim and must be labelled as such. They cannot settle the question they report on. The number, when wanted, must come from the ground.

## 8. One-paragraph canonical statement

A shape here is the narrowed form of a checkable answer that can be stated before any number is available. It says the ice-sheet sea-level residual is indexed per sheet and per process, that the question is a union to be unbundled, that a 2100 rate and a committed millennial total are different quantities across a lossy transform, that the instability tail is unreached at this budget rather than absent, and that the anchor is observation and not model agreement. It does not say how many millimetres per year. That is owed to a ground that does not update on what any frame thinks, and to a provenanced import with a shadow test. Indiscernibility is not identity: a forecast that fits every model is still a forecast.
