# The Tether's answer on ice-sheet sea-level rise

**Mode: `general` on the structural shape, `specified` on the per-domain physical verdicts, `gap` on the literal number. Not `grounded` as a forecast.** Simulated derivation checked against the figures (`05_what_shape_means.md` §7), not a ground reading. The measured historical losses are observations; the forecast is not.

## 1. The residual it answers

"How quickly will Greenland and Antarctic ice loss raise sea levels under different warming pathways?" asks for a rate. The figures decide that the object asked for is a union, not a scalar. Figure 9 :54: *"And when the split lands on a union rather than a partition, unbundle before you search again."* Figure 9 :57: *"a case in two conditions is not a harder case of either."* The conditions are sheet, process, horizon and pathway. They overlap, so one lever leaves the intersections standing.

## 2. The shape, per domain

1. **Index the residual per slot, never average the two sheets.** Fig 1 :19: *"The residual is indexed by object slot, not aggregated."* Fig 1 :22: *"Aggregating across slots is how a live signal disappears into an average."* Greenland's recent loss is roughly half surface melt and half dynamic imbalance; Antarctica's is dominated by ocean-forced West Antarctic ice-shelf thinning and grounding-line retreat. Different arrangement, different behaviour, different lever. Over the shared atom set they are isomers (Fig 12 :117).

2. **Separate rate from commitment.** A 2100 rate and a multi-century committed total are different quantities across a lossy transform. Fig 12 :112: *"A plan is a sequence. A goal is a comparison. Progress is a subtraction. A refutation is a negation, and that one is not a bond."* "How quickly" is a subtraction over a named horizon. Operators :46: *"progress is a subtraction, which is why a vocabulary without it cannot say how far along it is."* Fig 4 :30 requires the round trip: *"So take the round trip and measure the gap. Without it you are navigating by dead reckoning: confident about a position nobody checked."*

3. **The anchor is observation, not consensus.** Fig 1 :23: *"The ground enters as the only metric."* Fig 2 :29: *"An anchor is whatever settles the question and does not change its answer because of what any frame thinks."* Fig 2 :35: *"Alignment is triangulation, not negotiation. A mirror cannot correct you."* The ground here is altimetry, gravimetry and tide gauges. A multi-model *likely* range is a calibrated statement about a pool, not a new instrument. Fig 2 :40: *"agreement about the world is worthless when the agreeing frames share an evidence pool."*

4. **The deep-uncertainty tail is unreached, not absent.** Fig 3 :27: *"A link with no instrument is not a link that works. It is a link nobody has looked at."* Fig 8 :39: *"So a search that finds nothing is never a proof of absence."* Marine ice sheet instability, hydrofracture and ice cliff failure have registered in paleo constraints and observed grounding-line retreat, so there is an edge to extend from. The verdict is `gap` of the unreachable-at-this-budget kind, not underivable in principle.

5. **Independence is debited, and the cost if wrong is high.** Fig 7 :35: *"A room's agreement is variation, not a verdict. Selection between rooms is where the ground votes."* Fig 2 :42: *"Buy independence in proportion to the cost of being wrong."* Coastal capital is committed for decades, so a cheap consensus is not licensed here.

6. **Committed loss is not subtractable on the planning horizon.** Fig 11 :61: *"You can add an actor and you cannot subtract the habitat, because contact is a fact about the world and does not consult you."* Stabilising temperature does not undo mass already committed.

7. **The number is a declared gap.** Fig 9 :32: *"you get a kind of thing, never the answer itself"* Fig 13 :134: *"So the system can report that it lacks something rather than producing an answer anyway. Unreachable is a verdict here, not a silence,"* No figure line contains a Gt or a millimetre. This run does not manufacture one; the ground supplies it.

## 4. What it explicitly does not say

- Not a single global millimetre-per-year rate, and not a single number for all pathways. The shape refuses both.
- Not a claim that model agreement is the anchor. Agreement is a frame's property; the ground settles.
- Not a claim that the high-end storyline is the true tail, and not a claim that its exclusion from *likely* ranges proves it cannot happen.
- Not a derivation of ice physics from the six-term set. That mapping was retracted in `01` §6 as a strain.
- Not `grounded`. No measurement was taken in this run, and no forecast here is asserted as settled by experiment.
- Not an assertion that stabilising temperatures rapidly reverses committed loss.

## 5. What would settle it : the ground

A source-discriminating instrument, not a further argument. From *The Hard Problem is About Source*: an instrument that reads green *"cannot comment on the source"*; the spectrometer resolved it. Here the corresponding questions are:

- Does a process-discriminating observation separate surface-mass-balance loss from dynamical discharge at the slot, so the two functional-group members stop sharing one reading?
- Does a grounding-line or ice-shelf observation resolve the threshold tail into a settled band, closing the link that Fig 3 :27 says is unworked? If so, the `gap` retires to `specified`.
- Do new altimetry and gravimetry time series move the measured rate, and do independent instruments agree separately against the same anchor?
- Standing falsifiers from `01` §4: aggregate predicts each sheet as well as per-sheet; consensus moves the observed anchor; committed total is a lossless derivative of the 2100 rate; disjoint frames add no surplus; a numerical rate is derived from the six-term set alone.

## 6. The honest stop

**Unreached, not unreachable.** Fig 8 :39 again: a search that finds nothing is never a proof of absence. Fig 13 :134: unreachable is a verdict, not a silence. The six-term set is *"a human's careful guess at reality's compression axes"* (from *They're Scanning Books*); declaring it makes the choice visible, not neutral. The answer stops at the limit of what the available instruments distinguish and records the lineage so the stop is checkable. *"Indiscernibility is not identity."*

## 7. Cost if wrong, undoable, reversed by

- **Cost if wrong:** high and locatable. Coastal defence, zoning and infrastructure lifetimes commit capital for decades. Fig 2 :42 prices the required independence. The avoidable part is spending an outside reader's independence by sending a projection instead of a refuter (Fig 10 :42).
- **Undoable:** largely no for committed mass loss (Fig 11 :61). The shape claim itself is undoable: a single fired falsifier in `01` §4 withdraws the corresponding finding.
- **Reversed by:** a ground reading, not agreement. See §5. Until one fires, every claim here is `candidate`.

## 8. One-paragraph version for outreach

> The Tether does not return a millimetre-per-year number for ice-sheet sea-level rise, and it does not claim one. It says the question is a union that must be unbundled: Greenland and Antarctica are indexed per slot and are isomers over a shared atom set, so averaging them discards the arrangement that decides the behaviour. It says a 2100 rate and a committed multi-century total are different quantities across a lossy transform, so every rate must name its horizon. It says the anchor is observation, never model agreement, and that agreement among frames sharing forcing and ancestry is one witness, not many. It says the marine-instability tail is unreached at this budget, not absent, and that committed loss cannot be subtracted on a planning horizon. What it offers is the shape any checkable answer must have, six refuters written in advance, and the instruments that would close the remaining gap. The number stays owed to the ground.
