## VERDICT (from the figures)

The shape survives; the number is owed to the ground. The question is a union of sheet, process, horizon and pathway, so per-sheet verdicts stay separate and are never averaged. Greenland and Antarctica are isomers over a shared atom set, so averaging discards the arrangement that decides behaviour. Surface mass balance and dynamical discharge form a functional group with different instruments and anchors. "How quickly" is a subtraction over a named horizon, so the 2100 rate and the committed multi-century total are different quantities across a lossy transform. The anchor is observation, not model agreement; shared forcing and ancestry debit independence. The marine-instability tail is unreached at this budget, not absent, and committed loss is not subtractable on a planning horizon. No figure line returns a millimetre per year; that is a declared `gap`, which is a verdict and not a silence. This is a `general` shape with `specified` per-domain verdicts, ungrounded, candidate.

The generated number itself is not from the Tether. It comes from the observing system and the assessed literature reported in the WORLD section. The figures only say what a checkable answer must look like and what would refute it.

- **cost if wrong:** high and locatable. Coastal defence, zoning and infrastructure lifetimes commit capital for decades, so the cost of a wrong rate is paid by physical systems and budgets, not by the reputation of a forecast. Fig 2 :42 prices the required independence. The avoidable part is spending an outside reader's independence by sending a projection before a refuter, which Fig 10 :42 forbids.
- **undoable:** mostly no for the physical loss. Once mass is committed and grounding lines retreat past a threshold, the habitat cannot be subtracted on a planning horizon (Fig 11 :61). The shape claim itself is undoable: any one of the six pre-registered refuters withdraws its corresponding finding, and the files already show which one fires on what.
- **reversed by:** a ground reading, not agreement. A process-discriminating observation separating surface mass balance from dynamical discharge at the slot; a grounding-line or ice-shelf observation that closes the threshold tail into a settled band; new independent altimetry and gravimetry series that move the measured rate; or any of the six refuters in `08` section 3 firing. Until one fires, every claim here remains `candidate`. Prestige and agreement are not such instruments.
