# The Tether's answer: board oversight of emerging strategic risk

**Mode: `general` on the structural shape, `specified` on the four conditions and the per-domain verdicts, not `grounded`.** No board, incident, or control data was measured. This is a simulated derivation checked against the figures (`05_what_shape_means.md` §7), not a ground reading. Verdicts are kept per domain and never averaged.

## 1. The residual it answers

Directors and executives must decide on AI, cyber, and geopolitical exposure with limited expertise and limited information. In effect terms, the board's prediction about a risk fails against what happens on a particular slot, and the reporting channel has gone stale, pooled, or saturated while the underlying incidents and near misses kept occurring. The three classes are a union of overlapping conditions with compounds at the intersections (Fig 9 :54-58), not three instances of one question. "Limited expertise" is a closure bound; "limited information" is a channel bound.

## 2. The shape

1. **Keep the residual per slot.** Fig 1 :19: *"The residual is indexed by object slot, not aggregated."* Fig 1 :22: *"Aggregating across slots is how a live signal disappears into an average."* One enterprise risk posture is the forbidden aggregate. A global reading near zero with one live slot is a legal state, not a clean bill of health.

2. **Range against an anchor that does not update, and keep the channel to it reachable.** Fig 2 :29: *"An anchor is whatever settles the question and does not change its answer because of what any frame thinks."* Fig 2 :35: *"Alignment is triangulation, not negotiation. A mirror cannot correct you."* The anchor is an incident, near miss, loss, control failure, or regulatory finding, never management's own rating. Fig 10 :24: *"the ground does not decay, the channel does"*, and Fig 10 :27-28: *"the output is not a verdict, it is you have lost touch"*. When the channel dies, the honest report is loss of contact.

3. **Make the checker unable to produce what it checks.** Fig 10 :42: *"A verifier that can reconstruct the claim cannot verify it."* Fig 10 :43: *"Soundness requires the checker to be unable to produce what it is checking."* Another committee reading the same management pack is one long room: Fig 7 :36: *"A chain of mirrors is one long room. The next room must differ from the last, and match the residual it surfaced."*

4. **Import a differing closure only after the residual is described in effect terms.** Fig 6 :23: *"No frame escapes its own closure. The bound moves only on contact with a frame whose closure differs."* Fig 9 :27-28: *"describe it before you go looking, or any frame you pick will seem to fit"*. Fig 10 :52: *"Bring a characterised residual, never the source."* Ask SEARCHED first (is the expertise already in the room and unfound?), then INWARD (sharpen an instrument already returning something), then OUTWARD (import, with provenance and a shadow test). Fig 8 :53: *"Residual first, frame second."*

5. **Enumerate the habitat outward, and declare the stakes.** Fig 11 :40: *"A system cannot be made more capable by improving the agent in isolation. An improvement that does not change contact changes nothing, however much it improves."* Fig 11 :44: *"List everything in contact with the residual, then what is in contact with those, and outward until the cascade stops mattering."* Fig 11 :52-53: *"contact cascades. Scoping the habitat to direct contact is how DDT reached the eagles: first-order enumeration, second-order effect, and the residual that mattered was three steps out."* Fig 10 :59: *"an interest in the outcome is unavoidable, an interest in the reading is disqualifying"*, and Fig 10 :60: *"Where a stake cannot be removed, declare it. An undeclared interest is modelled anyway and an unnamed model runs unchallenged."*

6. **Report `unreached` where no instrument discriminates.** Fig 8 :39: *"So a search that finds nothing is never a proof of absence."* Fig 9 :60: *"Use filters for the budget and witnesses for the verdict. Never let a filter hand you a verdict."* A clean quarter and a heat map rank the budget; they do not witness the risk.

7. **No recipe.** Fig 9 :32: *"you get a kind of thing, never the answer itself"*. The committee, cadence, skills matrix, and time allocation are bindings from the governance frame, not figure outputs. That is a `gap`, and Fig 13 :134: *"Unreachable is a verdict here, not a silence"* makes reporting it mandatory.

## 4. What it explicitly does not say

- Not which committee, meeting cadence, reporting line, or percentage of board time. Those are `gap`.
- Not that a cyber expert director is required, or that one is sufficient. The NACD handbook itself calls adding a cyber expert *"an open one for many boards"* (NACD/ISA, 2026).
- Not that more information is useless. It is useless at the broken link; at a working perception link it is exactly the remedy (Fig 3 :19).
- Not that AI, cyber, and geopolitical risk are three independent questions. They are a union with compounds.
- Not that absence of incidents proves safety (Fig 8 :39).
- Not `grounded`. Nothing here is asserted as settled by a board measurement.

## 5. What would settle it - the ground

A source-discriminating instrument at the named slot, read by a frame that does not author the decisions it grades. The corresponding questions are:

1. **Slot test.** Does a specific control change an incident, near-miss, or loss outcome at that slot, with established sensitivity and specificity? If yes, that binding moves toward `grounded`.
2. **Channel test.** Does the board's risk reading track an independently verified change in the ground at every material slot? If yes, the channel is live there (F3, F9).
3. **Soundness test.** Can the verifying body independently generate at least one reading it checks, from a source the maker did not supply? If yes, the checker condition is met at that seat.
4. **Import test.** Does an external adviser supply a term that was provably not in the board's closure, and does the risk outcome move when that term is used? If no, the import added no atom (Fig 6 :25).
5. **Standing falsifiers from `01` §4.** A per-slot readout from a non-authoring source; a non-moving rating that changes the verdict; an independent checker; a partition result; a live channel with a discriminating instrument. Any of them withdraws the corresponding condition.

## 6. The honest stop

**Unreached, not unreachable.** The four-condition separator is a shape, and the framework's minimum sets are visible choices rather than nature's own list (*"a human's careful guess at reality's compression axes"*, carried from *They're Scanning Books* in the exemplar's mapping). Another board, another regulator, or another sensorium may bind the same shape differently. The answer stops at the limit of the available instruments and records the lineage so the stop is checkable. *"Indiscernibility is not identity"* (figures README). No measurement was taken here, so the verdict remains `candidate`.

## 7. One-paragraph version for outreach

> The Tether does not give boards a committee chart or a risk percentage. It says that an oversight arrangement has a shape: keep each live risk separate instead of averaging it into one posture; range against incidents, near misses, and control failures that do not move when management pushes on them, and treat a dead reporting channel as loss of contact rather than as safety; make the checking body unable to produce the risk claim it checks, so another review of the same pack does not count as verification; and bring in independent technical expertise only after the risk has been described in effect terms, with the source and its independence declared. "Limited expertise" is a closure bound moved by contact, not by training alone, and "limited information" is usually a decayed channel, not absent ground. AI, cyber, and geopolitical exposure are a union with compounds, so per-slot verdicts are never averaged. What the framework returns is that shape, the refuters written in advance, and the instruments that would close it. The recipe stays open until a ground settles it.
