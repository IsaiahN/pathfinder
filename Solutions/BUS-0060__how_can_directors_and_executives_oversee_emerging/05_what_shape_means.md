## 1. A shape is a constraint, not a filler

The figures README states the limit: the framework returns *"what a missing thing would have to look like (a shape, not a name)."* Fig 9 :32: *"you get a kind of thing, never the answer itself"*. Fig 13: *"It can say the answer is out of reach with the library as it stands... It cannot say which primitive is missing, because naming it would mean already holding it."*

From *The Library of Babel* (opened this session): *"You can cut your search short if the set of books returned is small and descriptive enough that it provides a general shape of the answer that satisfies your question."*

So a **shape** is the narrowed form of an answer you can state before you can name it. It constrains; it does not fill. For this question the shape is the four-condition separator in `01` §0: per-slot residual, non-updating anchor with a live channel, a checker unable to produce what it checks, and imports of differing closures with provenance. The committee name, meeting cadence, reporting line, and risk-time allocation are names, not shape, and are withheld.

## 2. How a shape is obtained, and why the board's ignorance is load-bearing

*The Library of Babel* relocates the problem from the size of the search to the searcher: *"Your ignorance, of not knowing what you don't know, is the actual obstacle between you and the answers, not the vastness of the library."* The method is proximity: use the vocabulary you already have that is closest to the thing, look for recurring new terms that overlap or correlate with it, and recurse. *"With each new term gained, you are able to narrow the space by definition and deepen your vocabulary."*

That is exactly the board's position. It does not lack a catalogue of risks; it lacks the terms for what a given risk affords. The essay also names the union error directly: *"Perhaps your problem was a bundle or conflation of several problems masquerading as one?"* which is Fig 9 :54-58 in narrative form. And on stopping: *"You will know that you are done because the finish line will also be the starting place, as there is nowhere else to search and nothing else that can describe the answer better."*

Fig 6 :30 governs the wording of the description: *"The description travels. Fails by being sharpened into terms only we can read. Cast it in effect terms."* Fig 9 :30-31: *"your words are borrowed too, so describe the effect rather than the cause"*. So the board writes "a vendor's access path to production data was not visible to us until an incident," not "we need AI governance." The first can travel to an outside frame; the second can only be read inside the board's own vocabulary.

## 3. What licenses a name

Only two things, both outside the shape itself:

1. **The ground settles it.** Formula step 5: *"the ground settles it. Verified only by what pays."* The README: the framework *"stops at the limit of what the available instruments can distinguish, and records the lineage so the stop is honest."* For governance, that means a measured change in incident, near-miss, loss, or control-failure outcome at the named slot.
2. **A provenanced import from a frame whose closure differs** (Fig 6 :23, Fig 8 :50-52), with a source gate and a shadow test. An independent assessor's finding is a name only after its provenance and independence are recorded.

*Decoding Tacit Knowledge* (opened this session) supplies the sharpest version of the first: *"When contact isn't possible, the best you can send is a plain description of what you'd expect to see, because anyone can check that against the world."* It also explains why a committee that shares management's briefing cannot check management: *"Long, close contact pulls the frames together, so the process that makes communication easy slowly uses up that extra reach."* And: *"Two frames that overlap almost completely are 'one frame wearing two names'."* The three drift-stoppers it names are all figure lines: *"A ground that both are corrected by, separately"*, *"Separate experience"*, and *"Claims in effect terms."*

This is the essay-level reason for requiring an anchor that does not update (Fig 2 :29), independent frames (Fig 2 :31), and residual-first framing (Fig 9 :27-28).

## 4. Why a single enterprise risk posture is the forbidden error

*The Hard Problem is About Source* (carried from the exemplar's canonical mapping, not re-opened this session) gives the mechanism. Metamerism: an instrument reads green and *"cannot comment on the source"*; one green light, or yellow plus blue, or RGB, or CMYK, or a scheme not yet discovered. The reading does not determine the source. *"If several paths create the same behavior, then behavior is a projection with lossy information."* And: *"An instrument that can't distinguish these cases demonstrates its own inadequacy."* It was the spectrometer, not an argument, that resolved it.

A single "amber" risk posture is that green reading. It is produced by several different underlying sources, a live AI failure, a dormant cyber exposure, and a geopolitical dependency, and it cannot comment on which. Naming one of them as the source from the aggregate reading is the metamerism error. What is owed is a source-discriminating instrument at the slot, not a better sentence in the annual report.

Fig 1 :19 and :22 make the same point in the framework's own notation: the residual is per slot, and aggregating is how a live signal disappears. The HRO literature corroborates from a different field: *"The absence of errors or accidents leads not to complacency but to a heightened sense of vigilance for the next possible failure. Near misses are viewed as opportunities to learn about systems issues and potential improvements, rather than as evidence of safety"* (AHRQ PSNet, 2024, opened this session).

## 5. What shape means for this question, answered

**The shape is a four-condition separator, not a board design.** An acceptable answer must (i) keep R per slot, (ii) keep a ground that does not update on the organization's own report and keep the channel to it reachable, (iii) make the checker unable to produce what it checks, and (iv) add differing closures only after the residual is described in effect terms, with provenance and a shadow test. Anything that satisfies those four and names a specific committee, cadence, or allocation has added a binding from the governance frame and must record it as such.

**What would settle the bindings?** A ground reading at the slot. A source-discriminating instrument at the AI, cyber, or geopolitical slot that shows whether a specific control actually changes an incident or near-miss outcome. Until then, the recipe is a `gap`, of the shape-not-a-name kind, reported as unreachable at this budget rather than as silence (Fig 13 :134; Fig 8 :39).

## 6. Correction to my own §3 (kept visible)

I initially read "shape, not a name" as forbidding any named board mechanism. That overstates the rule. The README's phrase is *"what a **missing** thing would have to look like (a shape, not a name)."* The subject is a primitive not held in Γ. Independent audit, red teams, near-miss reporting, and external assessors are already available in the governance frame. Naming one of them is a **binding onto a held atom**, with provenance, not the invention of a new primitive. What remains wrong when a board names a mechanism without declaring where it came from is the missing provenance, not the naming itself. The metamerism objection in §4 is not retracted: it still targets a source named from an aggregate reading without a discriminator. Both claims are recorded so neither is quietly lost.

## 7. Two consequences for outreach

**Prestige is not action.** From *The Recursive Self-Improvement Trap* (opened this session): *"Prestige must never be allowed to validate an action directly."* And: *"Prestige can fund an action. It can give an action a budget to be tried. But it cannot settle an action. Only the ground can settle it. The Ground Maintainer must be blind to prestige."* A governance campaign that gathers famous boards or well-known governance gurus to agree with the separator is the named collapse. The legitimate ask gives the claim a budget to be tried and lets an incident record, a near-miss series, or an independent assessment settle it.

**This audit is a simulation, not a ground reading.** Same essay: *"LLMs can quickly simulate an approximate answer/output with the formula found in the architecture post."* However, *"the truly testable output would need to be derived from the real architecture or working the formula yourself."* The essay also says plainly: *"The catch is that the output is only as good as the validator. The agent, or the tool, or the frame, or the theory, cannot validate itself."* So `01` is a simulated derivation, checked for internal consistency against the figures. It is not a test of board oversight and must be labelled as such. It cannot settle anything it reports on.
