## QUESTION

Decide, in effect terms, whether computation can occur with (condition 1) no discrete symbols and (condition 2) no central processor. Option A: treat this as one question and answer yes or no globally. Option B: treat it as a union compound of two slots, derive a shape for each against the figures, and declare the physical existence claim a gap. What each leads to: A averages a live residual on one half into a verdict that hides where the failure is (Fig 1 :19); B delivers two checkable shapes, four refuters and a named instrument, and stops honestly where the instruments stop.

## DOMAIN CENSUS

R by arity, symmetry and scale, then one line per domain with actors / currency / anchor, partition or union, and dependency order.

- **R (the residual).** The gap between predicting a process P by its own dynamics and predicting it by the attribution "P computes f". Arity: two conditions, so a union compound (Fig 9 :57 "a case in two conditions is not a harder case of either"), not one hard question. Symmetry: neither absence is a symmetric join; rank is denied, sequence is retained (Fig 13 :4-5). Scale: substrate, single loop, population of attributing frames (Fig 4 :20).
- **D1 Substrate / symbols.** Actors: six terms, bonds, molecules. Currency: composition in `closure(Gamma)`; discreteness as derivative. Anchor: a source-discriminating instrument. Status: admitted. Question type: partition boundary (which terms are primitives).
- **D2 Control / architecture.** Actors: frames, seats, loop, membrane. Currency: methods up, head starts down, imports. Anchor: the ground; no top rung. Status: admitted. Question type: rank denial.
- **D3 Attribution / observer.** Actors: outside frame, ground, provenance record. Currency: residual, agreement, source gate. Anchor: the ground (Fig 2 :29 names "a counter"), never agreement. Status: admitted. Question type: mapping and settlement.
- **X1 Pure symbolic computation as a primitive.** No nameable anchor; discreteness is explicitly a derivative. Not a domain yet.
- **X2 Single universal controller as a primitive.** Forbidden by no top rung / no last room. Refused term, absorbed into D2.

Union, not partition: D1/D2/D3 form a compound of overlapping conditions with separate grounds (Fig 9 :54-57). Dependency order: perception -> vocabulary -> objective -> planning -> learn and carry (Fig 3).

Strain test applied: "no symbols" was initially read as "continuous"; re-census after Maley showed this was a misreading of the compositional claim. Corrected and recorded.

## VERDICT (from the figures)

The figure verdict: yes at shape level for both conditions, kept separate. Composition does not require discrete symbol atoms; discreteness is what a composition leaves behind (D1). The loop has no top rung, last room, rank or bottom, so a central processor is forbidden while ordered routes remain required (D2). "Computation" is a label applied after the fact and settled by an instrument, not by agreement (D3). Continuous CRNs, cellular automata and swarms are structural isomers over the shared atom set, corroborating the shape without deriving it. Physical existence of a process meeting both absences is a declared `gap` awaiting a source-discriminating instrument. Mode `specified` / `general` / `gap`, never `grounded`.

- **cost if wrong:** bounded and locatable. The packet states mode, separates the two conditions and records four refuters before the conclusion, so an error is findable rather than invisible. The real cost is a reader spending their independence on a recording of the framework instead of a generator (Fig 2 :42 "Buy independence in proportion to the cost of being wrong."). That cost is avoidable by leading with the shape and its refuter.
- **undoable:** yes. No term is deleted if a refuter fires; a refusal is added and the reach falls because "something was added rather than because anything was deleted" (Fig 6 :42-43). The shape, the four refuters and the strain record are provenanced, so a later reader can locate the error (Fig 10 :62-64).
- **reversed by:** a ground reading, not agreement. Deriving discreteness as a seventh member breaks D1; exhibiting a top room that issues verdicts, or showing an ordered bond needs an executive, breaks D2; showing a non-instrument anchor for "computation" breaks D3; producing a frame-free instrument that measures computation arrival closes the gap. Until one of these fires, the verdict stands as `candidate`.
