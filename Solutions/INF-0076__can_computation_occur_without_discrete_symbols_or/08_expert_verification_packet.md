**What this is:** a claim in a form a researcher in computation, complexity, or philosophy of computation can check. Mode, derivation and refuters are stated before the conclusion.

**Mode: `specified` on the two shape verdicts, `general` on the structural no-top claim, `gap` on physical existence. Not `grounded`.** This is a shape and a route, not a result. Simulated derivation checked for internal consistency against the figures, not a ground reading.

## 1. The claim, in one paragraph

Discreteness is not a primitive of composition, so a route from input to output does not require discrete symbol tokens; finite physical precision at an instrument is a separate constraint. The loop has no top rung, no last room and no rank among frames, so a central processor is forbidden while ordered routes inside a process are required. "Computation" is a label applied after the fact to a process that met its ground, settled by an instrument rather than by agreement. The conclusion is a shape, not an existence proof: no particular substrate is named, and the existence of a physical process satisfying both absences is a declared `gap` until a source-discriminating instrument exists.

## 2. Three checkable sub-claims

### C1. Framework-internal, checkable by reading Figure 13 and Figure 12

- **Statement:** discreteness is a derivative of composition, not a seventh primitive; a route needs bonds, not tokens.
- **Source, verbatim:** Fig 13 :124-125 *"Smoothness, symmetry, chaos and discreteness are each what a particular composition leaves behind, and none is a seventh member."* Fig 12 :111 *"A vocabulary without bonds can name a state and cannot name a route to one."*
- **Refuter:** derive discreteness as a seventh member of the minimum set, or show that no input/output route can be composed without a discrete token. Either breaks C1.
- **Status:** triangulated (two distinct figures, low overlap).

### C2. Framework-internal and architecture-facing, checkable by reading Figures 10, 7, 12, 13

- **Statement:** a central processor is a top room, and the framework has no top rung, no last room, no rank and no bottom. Ordered routes inside a process remain legal.
- **Source, verbatim:** Fig 10 :68 *"Different actors, a different currency, a different ground, and a lossy transform between. That is why there is no top rung and no regress: a generator crosses, an instance does not."* Fig 7 :6 *"the limit: no last room"*; Fig 13 :4-5 *"There is no order of rank here. The two kinds are in lockstep, a crossing is a change of role rather than a step upward, and no member of the pair is senior to the other."* Fig 12 :104 *"A settled molecule becomes an atom for whatever composes over it. So irreducible is a fact about a level and never about the world, and there is no bottom"*
- **What to check:** is "no rank" being confused with "no sequence"? The claim denies the former and requires the latter. Operators table :34: *"the only bond a flat chain can express, so every term today is a sequence whether or not it should be"*.
- **Refuter:** exhibit a figure-sanctioned top room that issues verdicts to subordinate frames, or show that an ordered bond requires an executive state. Either breaks C2.
- **Status:** triangulated across four figures.

### C3. Attribution-facing, checkable by a researcher in philosophy of computation

- **Statement:** the attribution "P computes f" is a label applied after the fact, settled by an instrument, not a primitive read off P and not a consensus of frames. The three world analogues (continuous CRNs, cellular automata, swarms) are structural isomers over a shared atom set, not derivations.
- **Source, verbatim:** Fig 11 :36 *"Where nothing maintains anything, intelligence is a label applied afterwards to a lineage that met its ground. Nothing was intelligent; selection ran."* Fig 2 :29 *"An anchor is whatever settles the question and does not change its answer because of what any frame thinks. A measurement, an experiment, a counter, a market, a patient outcome."* Fig 9 :27-28 *"describe it before you go looking, or any frame you pick will seem to fit."* Fig 12 :117 *"isomer. Same atoms, different bond, different substance. The difference is invisible in the formula and decisive in the behaviour."*
- **What to check:** does the mapping account of computation (a physical system computes when its microphysical states mirror computational state transitions) support the claim that the attribution requires an observer-supplied mapping? Wikipedia "Computation" states the mapping account and warns of *"pancomputationalism ... the idea that everything can be said to be computing everything"*.
- **Refuter:** show that "computation" has a nameable, non-instrument anchor inside the figures, or that two disjoint-evidence frames must agree on the attribution with no ground reading. Also: show that one analogue requires an atom the others do not, which breaks the isomer claim and leaves the functional-group claim standing (Fig 12 :124 *"the group says what kind; the qualifier says which member"*).
- **Status:** triangulated (Fig 11, Fig 2, Fig 9, Fig 12).

## 3. What is explicitly not claimed

- Not that any particular physical system is a computer. No substrate is named.
- Not that absence of discrete symbols means continuity. Maley (BJPS) shows that is wrong.
- Not that discreteness is impossible at the ground. Finite precision is real physics; it is a constraint on instruments, not a seventh atom (Diamantini et al., Phys Rev E 94, 012139, 2016).
- Not that a route needs no order. Ordered bonds are required; only rank is forbidden.
- Not that expert agreement settles the question (Fig 2 :35).
- Not an existence proof. A failed search is not proof of absence (Fig 8 :39).
- Not `grounded`. Nothing here is asserted as settled by experiment.

## 5. What would settle it

A source-discriminating instrument, not a further argument. Three concrete tests:

1. **Instrument Test:** can a reading of P distinguish a bond-licensed route from a bare state sequence without the observer supplying the input/output mapping? If not, the attribution is observer-supplied and the `gap` stands.
2. **Precision Test:** does the ground reading carry finite distinguishable states imposed by physics or by the observer's frame? This locates the strain; it does not add a primitive.
3. **Refusal Test:** can P carry a negation, a correction that removes a term's standing to be composed over (Operators :57-58 *"a refutation is a negation, which is why a vocabulary without it can only accumulate"*)?
