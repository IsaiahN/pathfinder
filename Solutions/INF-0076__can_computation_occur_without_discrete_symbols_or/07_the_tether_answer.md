# The Tether answer: can computation occur without discrete symbols or a central processor?

**Mode: `specified` on both shape verdicts, `general` on the structural no-top claim, `gap` on physical existence. Not `grounded`.** Simulated derivation checked against the figures (`05_what_shape_means.md` section 6), not a ground reading.

## 1. The residual it answers

Two conditions, two slots, never averaged (Fig 1 :19). R is the gap between predicting a process P by its own dynamics and predicting it by the attribution "P computes f". The question asks whether that attribution can be warranted when P has no discrete symbol tokens and no single executive state. Fig 9 :57: *"a case in two conditions is not a harder case of either."*

## 2. The shape

**Condition 1, no discrete symbols: yes, in shape terms.** Fig 13 :124-125: *"Smoothness, symmetry, chaos and discreteness are each what a particular composition leaves behind, and none is a seventh member."* Discreteness is a derivative of composition, not a primitive. A route from input to output needs bonds, not tokens: Fig 12 :111 *"A vocabulary without bonds can name a state and cannot name a route to one."* So composition can carry a computation without discretised symbols. A finite-precision measuring instrument can still impose discretisation at the ground (Diamantini et al.), but that is a constraint on instruments, not a seventh atom. Strain recorded, verdict not flipped.

**Condition 2, no central processor: yes, in shape terms.** Fig 10 :68: *"That is why there is no top rung and no regress: a generator crosses, an instance does not."* Fig 7 :6 *"the limit: no last room"*; Fig 12 :104 *"there is no bottom"*; Fig 13 :4-5 *"There is no order of rank here."* A central processor is a top room, and the framework forbids a top rung, a last room, and a bottom. The checking seat is not a controller: Fig 10 :43-44 *"It transmits verdicts and issues none."* Frames range against the ground separately (Fig 2 :45). Computation here is a distributed composition, with ordered routes inside it and no executive above it (`06_isomers_and_order.md` Q2).

**The attribution itself.** "Computation" is a label applied after the fact to a process that met its ground, in the same way Fig 11 :36 treats "intelligence": *"intelligence is a label applied afterwards to a lineage that met its ground."* Picking the digital frame first makes any process look computational (Fig 9 :27-28). The anchor is an instrument: Fig 2 :29 names *"a counter"* as an anchor. Agreement is not the anchor (Fig 2 :35).

**The analogues.** Continuous chemical reaction networks, cellular automata, and swarms are structural isomers over the shared six-term atom set, with the common functional group `state → structure → state` (Fig 12 :117, :124). They corroborate the shape as instances, not as derivations.

## 3. What it explicitly does not say

- Not that any particular physical system (slime mould, chemical network, analogue circuit) **is** a computer. That is a binding requiring an instrument and provenance, not a derivation.
- Not that "no symbols" means "continuous". Maley's result rules that out; the audit retracts that reading.
- Not that discreteness is impossible at the ground. Finite precision is real physics; the claim is only that discreteness is not a compositional primitive.
- Not that a route needs no order. Ordered bonds are required; only rank is forbidden.
- Not that a consensus of experts settles the attribution. Fig 2 :35 forbids it.
- Not `grounded`. No measurement in this derivation discriminates computing from merely evolving.
- Not an existence proof. Search finding nothing is not proof of absence (Fig 8 :39).

## 4. What would settle it, the ground

A source-discriminating instrument, not a further argument. Its shape: a probe that separates "P computes f" from "P merely evolves under rules" without first importing a digital frame. Concretely:

- **Instrument Test:** can a reading of P distinguish a bond-licensed route from a bare state sequence without the observer supplying the input/output mapping? If not, the attribution is observer-supplied and the gap stands.
- **Precision Test:** does the process's ground reading carry finite distinguishable states imposed by physics (Diamantini et al.) or by the observer's frame? This locates the strain, not the primitive.
- **Refusal Test:** can the process carry a negation, a correction that removes standing to compose over a term (Operators :57-58)? Without it, a system can only accumulate.
- **Standing refuters:** derive discreteness as a seventh member; exhibit a top room that issues verdicts; show a non-instrument anchor for "computation"; or produce a frame-free computation detector.

## 5. The honest stop

**Unreached, not unreachable.** The six-term set is a visible guess, not nature's own list. The shape is delivered; the physical existence claim is a declared `gap`. Fig 13 :134 *"Unreachable is a verdict here, not a silence."* Fig 9 :32 *"you get a kind of thing, never the answer itself."* Fig 13 :135: *"It cannot say which primitive is missing, because naming it would mean already holding it."* The derivation stops at the limit of the available instruments and records the lineage so the stop is checkable. *"Indiscernibility is not identity."*

## 6. One-paragraph version for outreach

> The Tether does not name a substrate and does not claim to settle whether a symbol-free, processor-free computer exists. It says two things. First, discreteness is not a primitive of composition, so a route from input to output does not need discrete symbol tokens; a finite-precision ground can still impose discretisation on an instrument without changing that. Second, the loop has no top rung, no last room and no rank among frames, so a central processor is forbidden while ordered routes inside a process are required. "Computation" is a label applied after the fact to a process that met its ground, settled by an instrument such as a counter, not by agreement. Continuous chemical networks, cellular automata and swarms stand to one another as isomers over a shared atom set, which corroborates the shape without deriving it. What is owed is a source-discriminating instrument and four refuters written in advance. The existence claim stays open until the ground settles it.
