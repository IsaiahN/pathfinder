## 0. The question under audit

> Can computation occur without discrete symbols or a central processor?

Two absences are named. Effect terms:

1. **Can computation occur without discrete symbols?** Effect: a process with no discrete symbol tokens (no alphabet, no tape squares, no digitised state) may still support an input/output mapping that an outside frame would call a computation.
2. **Can computation occur without a central processor?** Effect: a process with no single controlling state or privileged executive may still support that mapping.

The residual R is the gap between predicting the process under its own dynamical rules and predicting it under the attribution "P computes f". Fig 1 :19 *"The residual is indexed by object slot, not aggregated."* So the two conditions are two slots and are never merged into one averaged verdict. Fig 9 :57 *"a case in two conditions is not a harder case of either."*

What an answer would look like, bound as an ordinary term before the run (formula step "Direction"): a **shape** (the minimum structural constraints any candidate process must satisfy for the attribution to be warranted) plus an **instrument** characterized in effect terms that would settle the attribution at the ground, and a **refuter** written in advance.

## 1. Residual described by arity, symmetry, scale

- **Arity:** two conditions (no symbols, no central processor). The residual is a **union compound** (Fig 9 :54), not a single hard question.
- **Symmetry:** neither absence is a symmetric join of two operands. The relation between the two conditions is not `+` (both hold) in the sense of one frame; they are two slots with different actors and different anchors. The ordered bond `→` is not licensed here because the question names no sequence between them (see `06_isomers_and_order.md`).
- **Scale:** three scales register: the physical substrate (the symbol question), the single loop or agent (the control question), and the population of frames that does the attributing. Fig 4 :19-20 *"Only the actors, the currency and the anchor change."*

## 4. Findings F1 to F6

### F1. Discreteness is a derivative, not a primitive (decisive for condition 1)

Fig 13 :124-125, verbatim:

> **"Smoothness, symmetry, chaos and discreteness are each what a particular composition leaves behind, and none is a seventh member."**

Fig 12 :111, verbatim:

> **"A vocabulary without bonds can name a state and cannot name a route to one."**

`THE_FORMULA.md` substrate section: *"the derived quantities (symmetry, smoothness, chaos, a continuum) are what a turn leaves rather than members of the set."*

The six terms are space, time, energy (states) and spectrum, gradient, network (structures). Discreteness is not among them and is explicitly placed among the things a composition leaves behind. So "without discrete symbols" does not mean "without composition". It means composition whose leaves are not discretised tokens. The spectrum bond (ordering, so more and less can be said) can leave discreteness as a derivative; a network or gradient composition need not.

**Reading:** yes, in shape terms. A process needs composition and bonds to support a route from input to output, but it does not need discrete symbol atoms. Mode `specified`.

**Refuter:** derive discreteness as a seventh member of the minimum set (contradicts Fig 13 indivisibility), or show that no route from input to output can be composed without a discrete token. If either fires, F1 fails.

**Status: triangulated** (Fig 13 :124-125 and Fig 12 :111, two distinct constructions, low overlap). A physical strain is recorded in section 6 and did not flip the verdict.

### F2. No central processor follows from no top rung, no last room, no bottom (decisive for condition 2)

Fig 10 :68, verbatim:

> **"Different actors, a different currency, a different ground, and a lossy transform between. That is why there is no top rung and no regress: a generator crosses, an instance does not."**

Fig 7 :6, verbatim:

> **"the limit: no last room"**

Fig 12 :104, verbatim:

> **"A settled molecule becomes an atom for whatever composes over it. So irreducible is a fact about a level and never about the world, and there is no bottom"**

Fig 4 :33: **"Which is why there is no top and no infinite regress."** Fig 10 :43-44: **"It transmits verdicts and issues none."**

A "central processor" in this frame set is a top room that issues verdicts to all lower frames. The framework forbids a top rung, a last room, and a bottom. The one checking seat that stands in a second frame (the ground maintainer) is forbidden from authoring or issuing: it *"transmits verdicts and issues none"* (Fig 10 :43-44), it is not a controller. Control is distributed: frames range against the ground separately (Fig 2 :45 *"Frames range against the ground. They do not negotiate with it."*).

**Reading:** yes, in shape terms. The loop has no privileged executive; a computation in this frame set is a distributed composition of frames, each ranging against the ground. Mode `general` for the structural no-top claim, `specified` for the physical-computation application.

**Refuter:** exhibit a figure-sanctioned top room or final rung that issues verdicts to subordinate frames, or show a computation whose input/output mapping is definable only relative to one central state that admits no frame decomposition. If either fires, F2 fails.

**Status: triangulated** across Fig 10, Fig 7, Fig 12, Fig 4 (four distinct figures, low overlap).

### F3. "Computation" is a label applied afterwards, and the anchor is an instrument

Fig 11 :36, verbatim:

> **"Where nothing maintains anything, intelligence is a label applied afterwards to a lineage that met its ground. Nothing was intelligent; selection ran."**

Fig 9 :27-28, verbatim:

> **"describe it before you go looking, or any frame you pick will seem to fit"**

Fig 2 :29: **"An anchor is whatever settles the question and does not change its answer because of what any frame thinks. A measurement, an experiment, a counter, a market, a patient outcome."** Fig 2 :45: **"Frames range against the ground. They do not negotiate with it."**

The word "intelligence" in Fig 11 does no work where nothing maintains anything; the answerable question is what makes a system capable, and capability is agent-and-habitat (Fig 11 :39). The same move applies to "computation": it is an attribution applied after the fact to a process that met its ground, not a primitive property read off the process. If you first pick the digital-computer frame and then look at P, any process will seem to compute (Fig 9 :27-28). The anchor that settles the attribution is an instrument, and Fig 2 :29 explicitly names *"a counter"* as such an anchor. Agreement between frames is not that anchor (Fig 2 :35).

**Reading:** the attribution is observer-relative in the weak sense of Fig 9, but not arbitrary: it is settled by an instrument, not by a vote. Mode `specified`.

**Refuter:** show that "computation" has a nameable, non-instrument anchor inside the figures, or exhibit two frames with disjoint evidence pools that necessarily agree on the attribution with no ground reading and no import. If either fires, F3 fails.

**Status: triangulated** (Fig 11, Fig 9, Fig 2).

### F4. The "does it compute?" question sits at the uninstrumented middle link

Fig 3 :19, verbatim:

> **"Optimising a link that already works cannot rescue one that does not."**

Fig 3 :27, verbatim:

> **"A link with no instrument is not a link that works. It is a link nobody has looked at."**

Fig 3 :25-27 names the middle link: having terms for what a situation affords and saying what would count as progress, with *"neither"* an obvious test. The attribution "does P compute?" is exactly this middle link (vocabulary plus objective), not a perception question and not a planning question. Effort lands on perception and planning because those have measurements. Fig 9 :53: *"Nobody chooses. The gap does, and the gap is only as good as its description."*

**Reading:** the reason this question stays open is not lack of intelligence in the observers; it is a missing instrument at the objective link. Mode `specified`.

**Refuter:** produce an instrument that directly measures "computation arriving" without first importing a computational frame. If produced, F4's diagnosis is falsified.

**Status: triangulated** (Fig 3 and Fig 9).

### F5. Naming a computational frame is an import and needs a source gate

Fig 6 :23, verbatim:

> **"No frame escapes its own closure. The bound moves only on contact with a frame whose closure differs."**

Fig 6 :30, verbatim:

> **"The description travels. Fails by being sharpened into terms only we can read. Cast it in effect terms."**

Fig 8 :50-52: *"Convergent derivation and adopted import look identical in the contents. Only the record of where each came from separates them, and the surplus lives only where the closures differ."* Fig 9 :30-31: *"your words are borrowed too, so describe the effect rather than the cause."*

To call P a computation one imports a frame (Turing machine, continuous chemical reaction network, cellular automaton, dynamical system). That import must carry a provenance record and a shadow test (Fig 6 :12-13: *"source gate: provenance"*, *"shadow test: explains a gap"*). Without the record, a convergent derivation and an adopted import are indistinguishable.

**Reading:** the frame choice is where error enters, not the composition itself. Mode `specified`.

**Refuter:** a case where the computational description is proven to be a convergent derivation from P's own dynamics with no import, and the record demonstrates this before the frame is named. If shown, the import warning is satisfied rather than violated, so F5 survives; it fails only if provenance is shown to be unnecessary for a correct attribution.

**Status: triangulated** (Fig 6, Fig 8, Fig 9).

### F6. What survives, and the honest gap

The **conceptual shape** survives: yes to both absences at the level of composition.

The **physical existence claim** ("there exists a physical process with neither discrete symbols nor a central processor that computes f") is a **gap**, not a hidden answer. Fig 9 :32 *"you get a kind of thing, never the answer itself."* Fig 8 :39 *"So a search that finds nothing is never a proof of absence."* Fig 13 :134 *"Unreachable is a verdict here, not a silence."* Fig 13 :135: *"It cannot say which primitive is missing, because naming it would mean already holding it."*

What is owed is a source-discriminating instrument described in effect terms: a probe that separates "P computes f" from "P merely evolves under rules" without first importing the digital frame. Its shape is characterisable; its name is not held.

**Status: triangulated** (Fig 9, Fig 8, Fig 13). Verdict `gap` for physical existence, `specified` for the shape.

## 5. Pre-registered refuter (written before the conclusion)

Per the exemplar's discipline, *"a falsifier written before the run is a design instrument."* The shape claim is refuted if any of the following holds:

1. Discreteness is derived as a seventh member of the minimum set, or six-term composition is shown to require a discrete token (breaks F1).
2. A figure-sanctioned top room is exhibited that issues verdicts to subordinate frames, or a computation is shown whose mapping is definable only relative to one central state with no frame decomposition (breaks F2).
3. "Computation" is shown to have a nameable, non-instrument anchor inside the figures, or two disjoint-evidence frames necessarily agree on the attribution with no ground reading (breaks F3).
4. An instrument is produced that directly measures computation arrival without importing a frame (breaks F4's diagnosis), or provenance is shown unnecessary for a correct attribution (breaks F5).

None of these was fired in this simulated run. Until one is run, no part of the shape claim may be labelled `grounded`.

## 6. Strains, re-census, and what was retracted

**Strain A, world source versus F1.** Diamantini, Gammaitoni and Trugenberger (Phys Rev E 94, 012139, 2016) state: *"every computation has to be carried on with a finite number of bits and infinite precision is forbidden by the fundamental laws of physics."* Read naively this says computation needs discreteness at the ground and conflicts with F1. Re-census: Fig 13 :124-125 governs which terms are **compositional primitives**; the physics result governs **physical precision at a ground instrument**. Different objects, so no verdict flip. The strain is recorded as real: a finite-precision ground can impose discretisation as a derivative even though discreteness is not a primitive. F1 stands; the physical existence verdict remains `gap`.

**Strain B, world source versus the naive reading of "no symbols".** Maley (BJPS, arXiv 2012.05965) states: *"The received view -- that analog computation has to do essentially with continuity -- is simply wrong."* An earlier draft of this audit equated "no discrete symbols" with "continuous". That was a strain and a misreading: absence of discrete symbols is not the same as continuity. Retracted and replaced: the figure claim is about atoms and bonds, not about the real line.

**Strain C, Fig 12 :104 operand scope.** The claim that a settled molecule becomes an atom is structural; whether a physical process is "settled" against the ground needs an instrument. Named as open, not hidden. Matches the exemplar's handling of the same line.

**Retraction log.** (1) Earlier draft said "the framework answers yes outright". Retracted: the answer is a shape-level yes; physical existence is a declared `gap`. (2) Earlier draft treated Strain A as a flip. Retracted: different objects after re-census. (3) Earlier draft equated no-symbol with continuous. Retracted: see Strain B. (4) No figure line was found to drift after verbatim re-check.
