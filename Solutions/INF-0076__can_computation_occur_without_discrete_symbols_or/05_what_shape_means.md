## 1. A shape is a constraint, not a filler

From *The Library of Babel* (OPENED): *"you can cut your search short if the set of books returned is small and descriptive enough that it provides a general shape of the answer that satisfies your question."*

A **shape** is the narrowed form of an answer that can be stated before it can be named. It constrains; it does not fill. For this question the shape is: composition without discrete atoms, and a loop without a top rung. Naming a particular substrate (a chemical network, a cellular automaton, a slime mould) would be a name, not a shape.

Fig 9 :26: *"R's own predicates name the habitat that holds φ*" and :27-28 *"describe it before you go looking, or any frame you pick will seem to fit."* The residual's predicates here are "no discrete tokens" and "no executive state". Those are effect descriptions, not substrate names.

## 2. How a shape is obtained

From *The Library of Babel* (OPENED): *"With each new term gained, you are able to narrow the space by definition and deepen your vocabulary."* And: the searcher must *"use the existing vocabulary you do know that is closest to the words that you wish to describe and grasp the shape of."*

Fig 6 :30: *"The description travels. Fails by being sharpened into terms only we can read. Cast it in effect terms."* Fig 9 :30-31: *"your words are borrowed too, so describe the effect rather than the cause."*

For this question the bootstrap vocabulary came from the residual itself: "route", "atom", "central state", "instrument". Each was borrowed in effect terms, then narrowed: route becomes a bond-licensed composition (Fig 12 :111), atom becomes a member of the six (Fig 13), central state becomes a forbidden top room (Fig 7 :6, Fig 10 :68), instrument becomes the missing middle link (Fig 3 :27).

## 3. What licenses a name

Only two things, both outside the shape:

1. **The ground settles it.** `THE_FORMULA.md` step 5: *"the ground settles it. Verified only by what pays. A gate is not the ground."*
2. **A provenanced import from a differing closure** (Fig 6 :23, Fig 8 :50-52) with a source gate and a shadow test.

So physical existence of a symbol-free, processor-free computing process is not settled by this derivation. The instrument that would discriminate "computing f" from "merely evolving" is owed and is characterized in effect terms only. That is the `gap` in `07_the_tether_answer.md`.

## 4. A correction to my own first reading

I initially wrote that the shape rule forbids naming any substrate at all. That was too broad. The canonical phrase is about a **missing** thing: *"what a missing thing would have to look like (a shape, not a name)."* Here the missing thing is the discriminating instrument, not a computational substrate. Naming an existing frame (a continuous CRN, a cellular automaton) is a binding onto concepts we already hold, which Fig 13 :75 and Fig 12 permit as compositions. What is forbidden is presenting that binding as a derivation of the shape from the six alone, or as an import that moves the wall when it adds no atom (Fig 6 :21 *"MINT composes inside the closure and can never add an atom."*).

Both readings are kept visible. The corrected one governs the files.

## 5. Prestige is not action

From *The Recursive Self-Improvement Trap* (OPENED): *"Prestige can fund an action. It can give an action a budget to be tried. But it cannot settle an action. Only the ground can settle it. The Ground Maintainer must be blind to prestige."*

A list of famous computing pioneers assembled to agree with the shape claim is the named collapse. The legitimate ask is the opposite: give the claim a budget to be tried, let an instrument settle it, and ask for a refutation.

## 6. My own audit is a simulation, not a ground reading

Same essay (OPENED): *"LLMs can quickly simulate an approximate answer/output with the formula found in the architecture post"* and *"the truly testable output would need to be derived from the real architecture or working the formula yourself."* Also: *"The output is only as good as the validator. The agent, or the tool, or the frame, or the theory, cannot validate itself."*

So `01_figure_grounded_audit.md`, `06_isomers_and_order.md`, `07_the_tether_answer.md` and `09_watson_answer.md` are simulated derivations checked for internal consistency against the figures. They are **not** tests of the question and must be labelled as such. They cannot settle anything they report on.

## 7. Why the union matters for the shape

From *The Library of Babel* (OPENED): *"Perhaps your problem was a bundle or conflation of several problems masquerading as one?"* That is exactly Fig 9 :54-57. The two absences in this question are two slots with different actors and different anchors. A single averaged answer would hide the live residual on whichever half actually fails. The shape is therefore delivered as two separate constraints, never as one blended claim.
