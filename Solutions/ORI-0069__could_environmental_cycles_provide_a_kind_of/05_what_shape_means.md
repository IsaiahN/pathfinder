# What "shape" means for ORI-0069

## 1. A shape is a constraint, not a filler

The figures README states the canonical limit: the framework reports "what a missing thing would have to look like (a shape, not a name)." Fig 9 :32: "you get a kind of thing, never the answer itself." Fig 13 :133: "A system with a closure can answer that before it searches, and what it returns is a shape rather than a name."

So a shape is the narrowed form of an answer that can be stated before it can be named. It constrains; it does not fill.

For this question the shape is **four slots that any answer must bind**: (a) a recurring condition, (b) a state carried across cycles, (c) a cross-turn mechanism, (d) a settling ground. The shape is not "wet-dry cycling did it." That is a name for one geochemical setting, and naming it would be the forbidden move.

## 2. How the shape is obtained

- *The Library of Babel*: start from the vocabulary you already have that is nearest the thing, then "With each new term gained, you are able to narrow the space by definition and deepen your vocabulary." The essay calls this a "bootstrapping convergence process."
- Fig 9 :30-31 requires describing "the effect rather than the cause" because "your words are borrowed too." Fig 6 :30: "The description travels. Fails by being sharpened into terms only we can read. Cast it in effect terms."
- Here the effect terms are deliberately plain: something returns, something is carried, the next turn starts differently, something decides what persists. None of these says "memory" yet, which is the point.

The essay's stopping rule also applies: "you can cut your search short if the set of books returned is small and descriptive enough that it provides a general shape of the answer that satisfies your question." Four slots are small and descriptive enough. Stop there.

## 3. What would license a name

Only two things, both outside the shape:

1. **The ground settles it.** Formula step 5: "the ground settles it. Verified only by what pays. A gate is not the ground." For ORI-0069 that means a cycling-versus-continuous control that isolates the cross-turn variable.
2. **A provenanced import from a frame whose closure differs** (Fig 6 :23), with source gate and shadow test. A geochemical setting imported from prebiotic chemistry into the four slots must carry its provenance; it does not become a figure-derived primitive.

*The Hard Problem is About Source* supplies the discriminator argument: an instrument that reads green "cannot comment on the source," and several paths can produce the same reading. "If several paths create the same behavior, then behavior is a projection with lossy information." Applied here: observing ordered products after wet-dry cycling cannot by itself tell you whether the environment carried the state or a persisting polymer did. The spectrometer, not an argument, resolved the colour case. A source-discriminating cycling experiment is what is owed here.

## 4. The specific shape returned for this question

| slot | constraint the figures impose | figure |
|---|---|---|
| recurring condition | must be enumerated from the habitat, not composed into the library | Fig 11 :43 "The habitat is enumerated, never composed." |
| carried state | is the residual of the last turn, not a stored snapshot | Fig 13 :88 "The residual of the turn just closed is what the next turn composes against, so no two passes begin from the same place." |
| cross-turn mechanism | must close a chain from outside; a recording cannot do it | Fig 12 :121 "cycle. The output re-enters as input. The sign of the loop decides whether it closes, and a chain cannot close by construction." |
| settling ground | must not move when a frame pushes on it; the cycling pool does move | Fig 10 :24 "the ground does not decay, the channel does" |

The word "memory" is not one of the four slots. It is a label applied to slot (b) and (c) jointly. Fig 12 names catalyst and cycle, and Fig 13 names residual carry; no figure names memory. So the canonical output is: **a head start from a habitat condition, with the record internal to a structure that survives its use.** Fig 4 :24: "downward: something that makes the next search cheaper. Never a replay of what worked once."

## 5. Correction to my own earlier reading (kept visible)

I first wrote "environmental cycles are an external memory store" as the headline. That is now **retracted**, for two separate reasons, kept distinct:

1. **Wrong object.** The canonical phrase governs a *missing* primitive. The environment is not missing: Fig 11 :46 lists "conditions: light, water, temperature, season, terrain" as habitat. Describing it is allowed. Calling it a new primitive called memory is not.
2. **Wrong role.** Fig 10 :24 makes the cycling medium a channel, and channels decay. The ground is what settles. A decaying channel is not a store in the settling sense.

What survives the correction: residual carry across turns (Fig 13 :88) and cycle-as-closing-bond (Fig 12 :121). Both are figure-derived. The label "memory" remains a question-facing word, not a primitive. I also initially treated the environmental recurrence as literally a Fig 12 bond inside Γ; that is narrowed to "a habitat condition that plays the cycle role from outside," a binding with provenance, not a derivation.

## 6. Two consequences for outreach and standing

**Prestige is not action.** From *The Recursive Self-Improvement Trap*: "Prestige can fund an action. It can give an action a budget to be tried. But it cannot settle an action. Only the ground can settle it." A list of people selected for their enthusiasm about external memory would fund the claim with agreement, not settle it. Fig 2 :35: "Alignment is triangulation, not negotiation. A mirror cannot correct you."

**This audit is a simulation, not a ground reading.** Same essay: "LLMs can quickly simulate an approximate answer/output with the formula found in the architecture post... However, the truly testable output would need to be derived from the real architecture or working the formula yourself." So `01_figure_grounded_audit.md`, `06`, `07` and `09` are simulated derivations checked for internal consistency against the figures. They are not tests of whether cycles did this at the origin of life. They cannot settle the question they report on.
