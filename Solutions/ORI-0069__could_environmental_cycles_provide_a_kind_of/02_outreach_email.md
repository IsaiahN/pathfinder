**Subject:** One checkable shape about cycles and prebiotic organization

> Hi [name],

> I have one specific output I would like you to check against what you already know. I am not asking you to read a theory first, and I am not asking you to agree with anything. If the output does not survive, there is nothing else to look at.
>
> **The output (`specified` on the shape, `general` on the structural claim, not `grounded`):** a set of ingredients cannot name a route through those ingredients; a bond is required. A recurrence in which conditions return is the one structural relation that closes a chain, because its output re-enters as input. So before any genetic polymer exists, an environmental cycle can do two things that matter: it can close the chain of reactions from outside, and it can make the next cycle's search cheaper. What crosses the cycle is the residue of the last turn, not a stored snapshot of it.
>
> **What I am not claiming:** that environmental cycles literally stored sequence information, or that they were the ground that settled which molecules persisted. The cycling pool decays; it is a channel and a habitat condition, not the anchor. In this framework the persisting record is a structure that survives its own use, such as a catalyst or a settled molecule. The framework returns a shape, not a name, so I am not filling the slots with a particular geochemical setting.
>
> **The four slots an answer has to name:** (1) the recurring condition, (2) the state carried across cycles, (3) the mechanism by which that state changes the next cycle's reactions, (4) the ground that would show it happened. I wrote these down before this run.
>
> **What would refute it:**
> 1. an ordered reaction route composed from ingredients with no closing cycle and no bond;
> 2. a cross-turn case in which no residual is handed forward and the next pass starts from exactly the same place;
> 3. a habitat condition that changes its answer because a frame thinks something, yet still settles the question;
> 4. a cycling experiment in which all cross-turn organization is accounted for by an environmental state variable and no persisting internal structure.
>
> If any one holds, I withdraw the external-memory shape claim. A one-line "this fails because" is more useful to me than agreement. If you would rather not engage, no reply is needed.

## Stage 1 before stage 2

Stage 1: send the four slots, the shape claim and the four refuters. Ask what the recipient would count as a failure. Record the verdict before showing any framework material.

Stage 2, opt-in only: if they are curious, offer the three links. Later agreement is never pooled as corroboration (Fig 2 :40: agreement from a shared evidence pool is the pool talking).

Stage 3: offer the formula as a method, with the instruction that each user writes a refuter and states a mode before running it.

No stage works if the refuter does not. Send the generator, not a conclusion.
