## QUESTION

Decide, in effect terms, whether recurring environmental conditions can do cross-turn work for prebiotic chemistry: carry a state across cycles so reactions become an ordered route rather than one-off events. Option A: cycles literally were an external memory store, so the origin of order is located in habitat structure. Option B: cycles were a head start and a closing bond, with the record internal to a persisting structure, and "memory" is a label the framework does not license as a primitive. What each leads to: A requires an instrument able to read a state held by the environment alone; B returns a four-slot shape, written refuters, and an open ground question.

## VERDICT (from the figures)

Environmental recurrence can close a reaction chain from outside and act as a head start, making each turn's search cheaper; the residual of the last turn is what the next turn composes against. The persisting record is a structure that survives its use, not the recurrence itself. The cycling pool is habitat and channel, part of capability but not the settling ground. So: **yes as a head start and a closing bond; no as a literal external memory store; "memory" is a label, not a primitive.** The origin-of-life history claim stays `candidate`, unmeasured. This is a `specified` shape, not a `grounded` result.

- **cost if wrong:** bounded and locatable. The packet states mode, four refuters and provenance before the conclusion, so an error is findable rather than invisible. The avoidable cost is spending outside readers' independence by sending a conclusion instead of a generator, which Fig 2 :42 prices: "Buy independence in proportion to the cost of being wrong."
- **undoable:** yes. The literal memory label can be withdrawn without touching the derivation. Keep the cycle, residual-carry and habitat-condition findings; drop the store reading. No primitive has to be retracted.
- **reversed by:** a ground reading, not agreement. A cycling-versus-continuous experiment with a measurement that separates environment-carried from structure-carried state would move the shape toward or away from the literal reading. A derivation of an ordered route with no closing bond, or a cross-turn case with no residual handed forward, would break Domains 1 and 3. A habitat condition shown to be both frame-dependent and settling would break Domain 2. Until one fires, `candidate`.
