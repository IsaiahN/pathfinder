# Solution ORI-0069: environmental cycles as "external memory"

**Question:** Could environmental cycles provide a kind of "external memory" that helped organize reactions?
**Source:** origins of life.txt line 95
**Section:** 7. Energy, environment, and chemical cycles
**Mode:** `specified` on the shape, `general` on the structural claim that a cycle is the bond that closes a chain and that capability lives in agent-and-habitat. Nothing here is `grounded`: no experiment settles whether cycles actually played this role.
**Status:** done
**Registry status:** pending

## Question restated in effect terms

What is being decided is whether a recurring environmental condition (wet/dry, freeze/thaw, heating/cooling, day/night, tidal) can do the cross-turn work that an internal record (a genetic polymer, an enzyme, a persisting product) does later in life. "Memory" is doing a specific job here: a state that survives the occasion and makes the next occasion's search cheaper. Two options and what each leads to:

- **Option A, yes literally.** The environment itself holds the state; the origin of reaction order is located in habitat structure rather than in a new molecule. Leads to a claim that would need an instrument able to read a state held by the environment alone.
- **Option B, no, it is a metaphor.** Cycles supply conditions and gradients only; the record must be internal to a persisting structure. Leads to the ordinary origin-of-life position that the memory is the polymer or the catalyst.

An answer would look like this, written down as an ordinary term so it can accrue a residual (formula step "Direction"): it must name (a) the recurring condition, (b) the state carried across cycles, (c) the mechanism by which that state changes the next cycle's reactions, and (d) the ground that would show it happened. This package returns the shape those four slots must have. It does not fill them with a name.

**Simulated derivation.** Everything in this folder is a simulated derivation checked for internal consistency against the figures. It is not a ground reading and cannot settle the question it reports on. See `05_what_shape_means.md` section 6.

## What is deliberately not here

- No personal emails of named individuals.
- No ranking of people by how likely they are to agree that cycles were a memory.
- No bulk-send plan.

Three figure-grounded reasons. Fig 2 :35: "Alignment is triangulation, not negotiation. A mirror cannot correct you." Fig 2 :42: "Buy independence in proportion to the cost of being wrong." Fig 10 :42: "A verifier that can reconstruct the claim cannot verify it." Selecting recipients for receptiveness manufactures the correlation that would make their agreement worthless, and sending the full framework before asking for a verdict spends the reader's independence.
