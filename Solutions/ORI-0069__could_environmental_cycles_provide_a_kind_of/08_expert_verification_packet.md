# Expert verification packet: the Tether on ORI-0069

**What this is:** a claim in a form a prebiotic chemist or origins researcher can check. Mode, derivation and refuters are stated before the conclusion.

**Mode: `specified` on the shape, `general` on the structural claims, not `grounded`.** No measurement is claimed. This is a shape and a route, not a result.

## 1. The claim, in one paragraph

Environmental recurrence is the one structure whose output re-enters as input, so it can close a reaction chain from outside and make the next cycle's search cheaper. What crosses the cycle is the residual of the last turn, not a stored snapshot. The persisting record is a structure that survives its own use, such as a catalyst or a settled molecule. The cycling pool is a habitat condition and a channel that decays, not the ground that settles which structures persist. Therefore environmental cycles can be an external head start and a closing bond, but not a literal external memory store; the framework returns that shape and does not name a geochemical setting.

## 2. Three checkable sub-claims

### C1, framework-internal, checkable by reading Figures 12 and 13
- **Statement:** a route needs a bond; a cycle is the bond that closes a chain; cross-turn carry is a residual, not a recording.
- **Source, verbatim:** Fig 12 :121 "cycle. The output re-enters as input. The sign of the loop decides whether it closes, and a chain cannot close by construction." Fig 12 :111 "A vocabulary without bonds can name a state and cannot name a route to one." Fig 13 :88 "The residual of the turn just closed is what the next turn composes against, so no two passes begin from the same place."
- **Refuter:** compose an ordered reaction route with no closing cycle and no bond; or show a cross-turn case in which the next pass begins from exactly the same place with no residual handed forward.

### C2, environment versus record, checkable by reading Figures 10 and 11
- **Statement:** the environment is part of the capable system and is enumerated as habitat conditions, but it is a decaying channel, not the settling anchor. The record is internal to a survivor.
- **Source, verbatim:** Fig 11 :39 "capability is a property of agent-and-habitat, never of the agent alone." Fig 10 :24 "the ground does not decay, the channel does." Fig 11 :73 "The habitat is not the ground. The habitat is where actors come from and it updates; the ground is what settles, and it does not." Fig 12 :119 "catalyst. A part that survives its own use."
- **What to check:** is a wet-dry or thermal recurrence a habitat condition rather than a stored state? Does anything in the claim require the environment to settle which molecules persist? If it does, the claim breaks.
- **Refuter:** show a habitat condition that changes its answer because a frame thinks something and still settles the question; or show a cross-turn record with no persisting structure at all.

### C3, literature-facing, checkable against the cycling experiments
- **Statement:** the shape predicts that cycling acts by selection and by making later search cheaper, not by storing a sequence. A cycling-versus-continuous control should show architectural enrichment, with the source of cross-turn state still unresolved by product data alone.
- **What the read sources say:** Mamajanov (Life, 2019) found that intermittent wetting enriched branched polyesters while continuous drying gave crosslinked products, and wrote that "environmental conditions have the ability to exert a rudimentary pressure to selectively enrich the polyesterification products." Orgel (PNAS, 2000) warned that cycle theories on mineral surfaces "make unreasonable assumptions about the catalytic properties of minerals and the ability of minerals to organize sequences of disparate reactions." The two conflict about the chemistry, not about the shape: that conflict is recorded, not hidden.
- **Refuter:** a cycling experiment in which all cross-turn organization is accounted for by an environmental state variable and no persisting internal structure, with a measurement that discriminates the two sources.

## 3. What is explicitly not claimed

- Not that wet-dry cycling, or any setting, is where life began. No setting is named.
- Not that the environment literally stored sequence information. A channel is not an anchor.
- Not that isomerism applies between a cycle and a polymer. It does not: they share no atom set.
- Not a seventh primitive, and not a derivation of one theory from another.
- Not `grounded`. No cycling experiment was run for this packet.
- Not that the cognition literature (Clark and Chalmers 1998; Michaelian and Sutton 2013) is evidence for prebiotic chemistry. It is a low-overlap analogue only.
