## 0. The claim under audit and the residual

> "Could environmental cycles provide a kind of 'external memory' that helped organize reactions?"

Restated as an effect before any figure line was chosen: the question asks whether a recurring condition outside the chemistry can carry a state across turns so that reactions become an ordered route rather than a set of one-off events. The residual is the gap between (a) a mixture of ingredients and (b) a system whose next turn begins from the residue of the last one. Four slots must be named by any answer: the recurring condition, the carried state, the cross-turn mechanism, the settling ground.

R by arity, symmetry, scale:
- **Arity:** two terms in composition (environmental recurrence and chemical reaction set), evaluated at a cross-scale boundary. It is a "could" possibility claim, not a measurement.
- **Symmetry:** recurrence is ordered and non-symmetric. Output re-enters as input only if the loop closes; this is the sign question in Fig 12 :121, not a symmetric join.
- **Scale:** the residual crosses from molecular scale to habitat scale. That is a membrane crossing with a lossy transform (Fig 4), so a claim made at one scale does not transfer to the other without a round-trip gap.

## 3. Findings

### F1: A cycle is the one bond that closes, and that is what "organize" requires (triangulated)

Fig 12 :121: "cycle. The output re-enters as input. The sign of the loop decides whether it closes, and a chain cannot close by construction."
Fig 12 :5: "A set of ingredients is not one. The bond is the rest of the information, and it is the half nothing was recording."
Fig 12 :111: "A vocabulary without bonds can name a state and cannot name a route to one."

A chemical mixture is a set of ingredients. To "organize reactions" is to name a route through them, which the figures say a vocabulary of ingredients cannot do. A recurrence in which conditions return is structurally the cycle bond: output re-enters as input, and the chain that cannot close by itself is closed from outside. This is the strongest positive result in the audit, and it is triangulated across Fig 12 (bond taxonomy) and Fig 13 (cross-turn residual, F2) with low overlap between the two routes.

**Refuter.** Show that an ordered route can be named from ingredients with no bond that closes, or that a wet-dry recurrence is not a case of output re-entering as input. Either breaks F1.

### F2: What crosses a cycle is the residual, not a stored snapshot (triangulated)

Fig 13 :88: "The residual of the turn just closed is what the next turn composes against, so no two passes begin from the same place."
Fig 13 :12: "the ordered succession of differences."
Fig 13 :22: "a difference that can be spent."
Fig 13 :28: "They alternate, and the alternation is where both the recursion and the randomness come from."

The framework's own account of cross-turn carry is the residual of the last turn becoming the operand of the next, with time as ordered succession and gradient as a spendable difference. That is a real cross-turn state, so the phenomenon the question calls "memory" is not denied by the figures. But what is carried is a remainder, not a recording of a previous answer. Fig 12 :107: "Settling compresses the description and not what it cost to establish." Fig 12 :108: "recorded, re-deriving it is a lookup and the actions were spent once."

**Refuter.** Show a cross-turn composition in which no residual is handed forward and the next pass begins from exactly the same place. That breaks Fig 13 :88 and F2.

### F3: The environment is part of the capable system, but it is not the anchor (triangulated)

Fig 11 :46: "conditions: light, water, temperature, season, terrain"
Fig 11 :43: "The habitat is enumerated, never composed."
Fig 11 :39: "capability is a property of agent-and-habitat, never of the agent alone."
Fig 11 :40: "An improvement that does not change contact changes nothing, however much it improves."

Environmental cycles are habitat conditions, enumerated from the world, not members of the chemical library. The framework therefore takes the question seriously: capability is not located in the molecules alone, so the environment is not background. But this is where the "memory" label starts to strain. Fig 10 :24: "the ground does not decay, the channel does." Fig 11 :73: "The habitat is not the ground. The habitat is where actors come from and it updates; the ground is what settles, and it does not." A cycling pool is a channel and a habitat condition. It decays, it is enumerated, and it is not what settles which structures persist.

**Refuter.** Show that a habitat condition changes its answer because of what a frame thinks and still settles the question. That would make the environment an updating anchor and break Fig 2 :29 and Fig 10 :24.

### F4: The persisting record is internal to a structure that survives its use (single-source for the figure definition, world-corroborated)

Fig 12 :119: "catalyst. A part that survives its own use. Spent and regenerating parts behave differently across uses, and nothing records which a term is."
Fig 12 :104: "A settled molecule becomes an atom for whatever composes over it. So irreducible is a fact about a level and never about the world."
Fig 12 :112: "A plan is a sequence. A goal is a comparison. Progress is a subtraction."

If "memory" means a record that survives the occasion and can be composed over, the figure names the catalyst as the thing with that property, and the settled molecule as the thing that becomes an operand at the next level. The cycle provides the recurrence; the surviving structure provides the record. Conflating the two is the specific error the question invites.

World corroboration, secondary only. Dill and Agozzino (Open Biology, 2021) write that genomes implement memory for precise heritability, and that chemistry harnessed "daily cycles" for energy. That is exactly the distinction: cycles for driving, genome for memory. Lehman and Kauffman (Entropy, 2021) write that "work itself can construct the constraints needed for closure" (their figure, not ours), which supports the cycle-as-closing-bond reading rather than the cycle-as-store reading.

**Refuter.** Show a persisting record that is entirely in the environmental recurrence, with no structure surviving its own use and no settled molecule becoming an operand. That breaks F4.

### F5: A head start is allowed; a recording is not (triangulated)

Fig 4 :24: "downward: something that makes the next search cheaper. Never a replay of what worked once."
Fig 4 :25: "A recording carried upward looks like knowledge and is a description of one occasion."
Fig 4 :34: "A seed carries no small tree: it carries what will grow one, and the conditions it lands in decide what it becomes."

This is the figure-level resolution of the word "memory." An external condition that makes each cycle's search cheaper is a head start and is licensed. An external condition that stores one success and replays it produces repetition, not new organization. The seed does not contain the tree; the conditions decide what it becomes. So "external memory" is defensible only in the head-start sense. In the recording sense it is a figure-forbidden reading.

**Refuter.** Show a case where a replayed environmental recording generates new organization rather than repetition. That breaks Fig 4 :25 and F5.

### F6: This is a union, and "memory" is the naming break (single-source for the chain structure)

Fig 9 :54: "And when the split lands on a union rather than a partition, unbundle before you search again."
Fig 3 :27: "A link with no instrument is not a link that works. It is a link nobody has looked at."
Fig 9 :32: "you get a kind of thing, never the answer itself"

The chemistry link (does cycling change products?) has instruments and many papers. The naming link (is the word "memory" licensed?) has none. By Fig 3, effort spent on the well-instrumented link cannot rescue the uninstrumented one. The framework returns a kind of thing, never the name.

**Refuter.** Produce an instrument that decides whether a cross-turn state is "memory" rather than "residual carry" without importing a definition from outside the figures. Until then the naming link is the break.

### F7: World sources conflict in the right way, and the conflict does not flip the verdict

Mamajanov (Life, 2019) reports that cycling selectively enriches polymer architectures, writing that "environmental conditions have the ability to exert a rudimentary pressure to selectively enrich the polyesterification products" (her paper, not a figure line). That corroborates F1 and F5: selection pressure, not a store. Orgel (PNAS, 2000) warns that cycles on mineral surfaces make "unreasonable assumptions about the catalytic properties of minerals and the ability of minerals to organize sequences of disparate reactions" (his paper). That is a genuine conflict with an optimistic reading of F1, but it is a conflict about whether the chemistry closes, not about the figure shape. By the WATSON rule, a conflict sends us back to the census, not to a flipped verdict. Re-census: Orgel governs domain 1 (chemistry, ground-visible), while F1 governs domain 2 (composition shape). Different objects. The verdict from the figures stands; the chemistry remains `candidate`.

## 4. Pre-registered refuter (written before the conclusion)

Required before any outreach. A falsifier written before the run is a design instrument.

The shape claim is refuted if any of the following holds:

1. An ordered reaction route can be composed with no closing cycle and no bond, that is, ingredients alone name a route (breaks Fig 12 :111, :121).
2. A cross-turn composition is shown in which no residual is handed forward and the next pass begins from the same place (breaks Fig 13 :88).
3. A habitat condition is shown to change its answer because a frame thinks something, yet still settle the question (breaks Fig 2 :29, Fig 10 :24).
4. A cycling experiment accounts for all cross-turn organization with an environmental state variable and no persisting internal structure (breaks the "external" part at the ground).

If any fires, the external-memory shape claim is withdrawn. The weaker claim that cycles are an enabling habitat condition and a closing bond survives unless (1) or (2) fires. No finding here may be labelled `grounded` until a cycling-versus-continuous control settles it.

## 7. Strains and re-census

- **"Cycle" as bond versus "cycle" as habitat condition.** The same word names a composition relation in Fig 12 and a recurrence in Fig 11. Re-census: different objects. Fig 12 governs bonds inside a library; Fig 11 governs conditions enumerated from the world. We do not claim the environment is a member of Γ. Marked as a binding, not a derivation.
- **"Memory" versus "residual carry."** Real conceptual strain, not smoothed. The figures license carry (Fig 13 :88) and survivor records (Fig 12 :119) but contain no term named memory. Operationally we keep both readings visible: carry is figure-derived, the label "memory" is a question-facing word, not a primitive.
- **After re-census: two strains, both named, none hidden.**

## 8. Conclusion

The figures return a shape. Environmental recurrence is a habitat condition that can play the role of the closing cycle bond, making a route nameable and making each turn's search cheaper, while the residual of the last turn becomes the operand of the next. The actual record, in the figure's sense of something that survives its use and can be composed over, is internal to a persisting structure such as a catalyst or settled molecule. So "could environmental cycles provide a kind of external memory?" is answered as follows: **yes as a head start and a closing condition, no as a literal external store, and the word "memory" is a label the framework does not license as a primitive.** Whether this is what happened at the origin of life is a ground question for cycling experiments, and is `unreachable` at this budget rather than answered. Fig 8 :39: "So a search that finds nothing is never a proof of absence."
