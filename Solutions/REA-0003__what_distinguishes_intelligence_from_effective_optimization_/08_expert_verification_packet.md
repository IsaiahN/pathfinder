# Expert verification packet: intelligence versus narrow optimization

**What this is:** a claim in a form a researcher can check. Mode, derivation and refuters are stated before the conclusion, so a reader knows what to look for and what would settle it.

**Mode: `specified` on the distinction, `general` on the structure, `gap` on a single test. Not `grounded`.** No measurement is claimed. This is a shape and a route, not a result.

## 1. The claim, in one paragraph

A narrow optimizer and an intelligent system are not separated by degree of optimization. They are separated by a four-part relation: capability is a property of agent-and-habitat, not of the agent alone; contact with a ground is maintained and the channel stays readable; reach is renewed by importing from a frame whose closure differs; and the objective is refusable, so the system can say that its aim is wrong rather than only that it is not yet met. A system can run the whole inner loop, score perfectly on its proxy, and still lack all four. The single-test question is declared a gap, not answered by assertion.

## 2. Three checkable sub-claims

### C1 - Framework-internal; checkable by reading Figure 11

- **Statement:** the answerable question is not "what is intelligence" but "what makes a system capable", and capability is agent-and-habitat. Improving the agent alone does not change this relation.
- **Source, verbatim:** Fig 11 :39 *"So the answerable question is not what intelligence is, but what makes a system capable. And capability is a property of agent-and-habitat, never of the agent alone."* Also Fig 11 :40 *"A system cannot be made more capable by improving the agent in isolation. An improvement that does not change contact changes nothing, however much it improves."* Triangulated with Fig 4 :20 *"Only the actors, the currency and the anchor change."*
- **Refuter:** an agent-only improvement, with no habitat or contact change, that moves the ground or raises verified capability. Fig 11 :72 itself permits this as a possible refutation. If it occurs, C1 is withdrawn.

### C2 - Framework-internal plus AI-facing; checkable by a researcher against specification gaming and Goodhart

- **Statement:** a narrow objective's score is not a ground. It is a quantity the same frame produces, it becomes unstable when made the target, and under strong optimization it can rise while intended contact falls.
- **Source, verbatim:** Fig 1 :27 *"They are frame-internal. A frame cannot score itself with a quantity it also produces."* Fig 2 :29 *"An anchor is whatever settles the question and does not change its answer because of what any frame thinks."* Fig 10 :24 *"the ground does not decay, the channel does"* and :26 *"saturated metrics, pooled readings"*. Triangulated with Fig 5 :48 *"Its own output is never a measure of success. A system can be enthusiastically curious and produce nothing."*
- **What to check:** take a published specification-gaming or reward-hacking case. Did the proxy rise while the intended outcome failed? If yes, the score was not an anchor. World sources W1, W2 and W5 supply the cases; the figure lines supply the verdict.
- **Refuter:** an objective metric that stays a valid anchor when it becomes the target, with a published test showing no drift under optimization pressure. Then C2 weakens.

### C3 - Framework-internal; checkable by reading Figures 8 and 12 and the Operators table

- **Statement:** search inside a fixed closure adds no atom, and a vocabulary with goal, progress and plan but no negation can only accumulate; it cannot refuse its own objective. Together these are the structural reason better optimization is not the same move as re-aiming.
- **Source, verbatim:** Fig 8 :32 *"Closure is idempotent. Composing inside a closed set never leaves it, so no search, however long, adds an atom. This one is definitional: it is what closure means."* Fig 6 :23 *"No frame escapes its own closure. The bound moves only on contact with a frame whose closure differs."* Fig 12 :112 *"A plan is a sequence. A goal is a comparison. Progress is a subtraction. A refutation is a negation, and that one is not a bond."* Fig 12 :140 *"A vocabulary that cannot refuse can only accumulate."*
- **What to check:** is there a case where reach was renewed purely by internal composition with no import and no new measurement? Is there a system with no ¬ in its settled vocabulary that nevertheless refused and replaced its own objective on ground evidence?
- **Refuter:** either demonstration. The first breaks the idempotence claim; the second makes ¬ unnecessary for refusal. C3 then fails.

## 3. What is explicitly not claimed

- Not a definition of intelligence in one number, and not a benchmark or IQ score. The single-test request is a `gap`.
- Not a claim that narrow optimization is useless, that more compute cannot help, or that a perfect optimizer is stupid.
- Not that the habitat is an ecological niche. The framework declines that mapping; niche construction and affordance corroborate a mechanism only.
- Not Chollet's skill-acquisition efficiency, not the orthogonality thesis, not a psychometric g. Those are different quantities in the same neighbourhood.
- Not `grounded`. Nothing here is asserted as settled by measurement.
- Not a claim that this derivation settles the philosophical question. It is a simulated derivation checked for internal consistency against the figures.

## 5. What would settle it

1. An agent-only improvement that moves the ground (kills C1).
2. An objective metric that remains an anchor under optimization pressure (kills C2).
3. Reach renewed by internal composition alone with no import (kills C3, part 1).
4. Refusal of an objective without a negation operator in the settled vocabulary (kills C3, part 2).
5. A case where "intelligence" attaches to a lineage with no ground contact and the word still does explanatory work (kills the label claim in `01` F2).
6. A published instrument separating maintained contact from proxy optimality (closes the declared gap; this is a success).
7. A source with a genuinely different evidence pool showing that the four-part relation adds no discrimination beyond a skill score. Note that agreement from a shared pool is one witness, not two (Fig 2 :40).

1. Read Fig 11 :39-41, Fig 1 :27, Fig 8 :32 and Fig 12 :112. Four short passages, all verbatim above.
