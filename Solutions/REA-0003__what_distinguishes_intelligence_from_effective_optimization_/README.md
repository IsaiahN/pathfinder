## Residual in effect terms

Two systems can produce the same observable competence on a narrow task. One is called intelligent, the other is called a good optimizer. The residual is the missing difference in **contact structure**, not in score: what has to be present for the label to attach, such that maximizing a fixed objective can be excellent and still not be it. Option A: intelligence is more of the same (more tasks, more compute, higher score). Option B: intelligence is a different relation to a ground and a habitat (maintained contact, cross-closure import, a re-aimable objective, and the capacity to refuse). What an answer would look like is bound in `01_figure_grounded_audit.md` §0 and carried as an ordinary term, per Formula step "Direction".
