## 1. A shape is a constraint you can state before you can name it

The canonical usage rule says the framework returns *"what a missing thing would have to look like (a shape, not a name)"*. Fig 9 :32 says *"you get a kind of thing, never the answer itself."*

From *The Library of Babel*: *"You can cut your search short if the set of books returned is small and descriptive enough that it provides a general shape of the answer that satisfies your question."* And: *"With each new term gained, you are able to narrow the space by definition and deepen your vocabulary."*

For this question the shape is **not** a threshold on a benchmark. It is the four-part relation in `01` §2: capability as agent-and-habitat; maintained ground contact; live reach through import; a refusable objective. A number would be a name where only a shape is owed.

## 2. The shape is obtained by proximity, not by guessing a definition

*The Library of Babel*: *"If you don't know the right terms to search for, you will have to use the existing vocabulary you do know that is closest to the words that you wish to describe and grasp the shape of."* The question is the tether: *"This anchor is provided by your own question: If the question is answered by your present collection of books from your search you know whether to stop or continue and which direction to turn next."*

Applied here: starting from the words we have ("skill", "objective", "score"), the nearest figure vocabulary is "ground", "habitat", "closure", "refusal". The census in `01` §1 was built by that proximity, and the figure lines were quoted before the verdict was written.

## 3. What licenses a name

Only two things:

1. **The ground settles it.** Formula step 5: *"the ground settles it. Verified only by what pays. A gate is not the ground."* From *The Hard Problem is About Source*: *"The answer is unreached until a better instrument exists."* The spectrometer, not an argument, resolved the green-light case.
2. **A provenanced import from a differing closure**, with a source gate and a shadow test (Fig 6 :23; Fig 8).

*The Hard Problem is About Source* gives the discrimination test that matters here: *"If several paths create the same behavior, then behavior is a projection with lossy information"* and *"An instrument that can't distinguish these cases demonstrates its own inadequacy, it cannot claim anything substantial about what is being measured or what causes it."* This is exactly the situation in the question: a narrow objective and an intelligent system can produce the same behavior on the task, so the behavior cannot discriminate them. What is owed is an instrument, not a definition by fiat. That is why F6 in `01` is a `gap` rather than a reluctance.

## 4. Contact is the load-bearing word

*How Intelligence Actually Works*: *"The intelligence we experience is the emergent result of extensive mapping of a complex system against unknown and unforgiving environments."* And: *"The mapping is not a means to intelligence; the mapping is the intelligence. And that mapping is not a tree with roots and branches, it is a web, with no canonical order and no single path through."*

The same essay gives the direct answer to this question's neighbourhood: *"The definition of what we call General Intelligence is that a system can answer questions to problems it's never seen before and in environments it has no training data for."* That is a **parallel definition**, not the Tether's. The Tether's move is Fig 11 :39, which replaces "what is intelligence" with "what makes a system capable" and locates capability in agent-and-habitat. I keep both visible and do not merge them.

From *Decoding Tacit Knowledge*: *"If intelligence is something each mind rebuilds through contact, then text alone can't hand it over."* And: *"When contact isn't possible, the best you can send is a plain description of what you'd expect to see, because anyone can check that against the world."* That is the rule this folder follows in `02_outreach_email.md`: send the effect description and the refuter, not the interior.

## 5. Why the narrow objective fails the shape

*How Intelligence Actually Works*: *"Alignment asks what the thing is answerable to. A ground, something that refuses, a bill that gets paid. Without it you have a system that can generate things and nothing that can tell it's wrong."* A narrow objective supplies a score, not a thing that refuses. The same essay: *"A seed with no ground gives you unbounded generation with no resilience to harsh conditions and constraints."*

*The Recursive Self-Improvement Trap*: *"Prestige can fund an action. It can give an action a budget to be tried. But it cannot settle an action. Only the ground can settle it."* A benchmark result has exactly this prestige role: it buys a hearing for a system, it does not settle what the system is.

## 6. This derivation is a simulation, not a ground reading

*The Recursive Self-Improvement Trap*, verbatim: *"LLMs can quickly simulate an approximate answer/output with the formula found in the architecture post. It will often be better than the generic outputs that you will usually get otherwise too (intelligence is essentially arrangement). However, the truly testable output would need to be derived from the real architecture or working the formula yourself."*

So this file, like `01`, is a simulated derivation checked for internal consistency against the figures. It is not a test of the claim. It cannot settle anything it reports on. The one-paragraph version in `07` is a shape offered for checking, not a result.

## 7. What the framework explicitly declines, relevant to the world sources

*Arrival of the Fittest: The Tether Architecture*, in the section *"Three mappings offered and declined"*, declines *"The habitat as an ecological niche"* with the reason: *"A niche is defined by an organism's relation to its environment. A habitat here is defined by contact with a residual, and the residual moves so the same actors constitute a different habitat when the question changes."* It also declines the Popperian-critic mapping and the Bayesian-updating mapping.

Consequences for this solution:

- The affordance and niche-construction world sources (W4, W6) corroborate the **mechanism shape** at low overlap, not an identity with Fig 11's habitat. Claiming identity would be the exact mapping the framework refuses.
- Chollet's *"skill-acquisition efficiency"* is named by the same article as *"a parallel"*: *"Chollet measures skill-acquisition efficiency against stated priors; Figure 11 argues capability is a property of agent-and-habitat rather than of the agent, which is a different quantity in the same neighbourhood."* So it is not the Tether answer to the measurement question and it cannot be used to fill F6's gap.
- The framework's own "A note on what is not cited" records that the claim *"capability is a property of agent-and-habitat, never of the agent alone"* carries no borrowed citation. That is an honest limit, not evidence of originality. Our world sources corroborate the neighbourhood, not the claim itself.

## 8. Summary statement of shape

For this question, "shape" means: a discriminating relation stated in effect terms, with its refuters written first, that any reader can check against a ground without first accepting the framework. It stops at the limit of the available instruments, declares the single-test request a `gap`, and records the lineage so the stop is checkable. *"Indiscernibility is not identity."*
