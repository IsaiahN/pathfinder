# Outreach draft: check the output before deciding about the theory

**Use:** one-to-one, or as a cover letter attached to a submission through the public venues in `03_public_contact_points.csv`. **Not** for bulk send.

**Hard precondition:** do not send until the six refuters in `01_figure_grounded_audit.md` §3 are in your own words and you can say which one you would run first. The offer collapses on contact if the stage-1 output cannot survive.

---

**Subject:** One distinction to check: is "intelligent" the same claim as "good at the objective"?

> Hi [name],
>
> I have one specific output I would like you to check against what you already know. I am not asking you to read a theory first, and I am not asking you to agree with anything. If the output does not survive, there is nothing else to look at.
>
> **The question in effect terms.** Two systems can score the same on a narrow objective. One gets called intelligent, the other a good optimizer. What has to be present for the first word to apply? My own answer is written down below as a prediction, before I show you where it came from.
>
> **The output (`specified`, not `grounded`).** The distinction is not a higher score. It is a four-part relation:
> 1. capability belongs to the agent **and its habitat**, not to the agent alone;
> 2. contact with a ground is maintained, and the channel that reads it stays uncluttered;
> 3. reach is kept live by importing from a frame whose assumptions differ, because search inside a fixed set cannot add a new primitive;
> 4. the objective itself is refusable, so the system can say "this aim is wrong" and not only "this aim is not yet met."
>
> A narrow optimizer can run the inner loop perfectly while lacking all four. In that reading, a benchmark score is a way to choose what to try next, not a witness for what the word means. This is a **shape**, not a measurement, and I am not claiming it settles the philosophical question.
>
> **What I am not claiming.** Not that there is a single test that separates the two cases. I record that as a gap, not a hidden answer. Not that more compute cannot help; it can, but improvement that does not change contact changes nothing about this distinction. Not that the word "intelligence" names a property of the agent alone.
>
> **What would refute the shape claim.** I wrote these down before this email:
> 1. an agent-only improvement with no habitat or contact change that moves the ground or raises verified capability;
> 2. an objective metric that stays a valid anchor when it becomes the target;
> 3. reach renewed by internal search alone with no import;
> 4. a system with no way to refuse a claim that nevertheless correctly refuses its own objective;
> 5. a case where "intelligence" attaches to a lineage with no ground contact and the word still does explanatory work.
>
> If any one holds, I withdraw the claim. A one-line "this fails because..." is more useful to me than agreement. If you would rather not engage, no reply is needed.
>
> **If it survives, and only then**, here are the sources. The figures are the authority, not my summary of them:
> - [The Tether Architecture: Arrival of the Fittest](https://adventuresinml.substack.com/p/arrival-of-the-fittest-the-tether)
> - [How Intelligence Actually Works](https://adventuresinml.substack.com/p/how-intelligence-actually-works)
> - [Figures and formula, branch general-agent](https://github.com/IsaiahN/tether/tree/general-agent/figures)
>
> **If you want to use the machinery:** the formula is in the figures link. Run it on a problem you know well and can check against a ground. Write down what would refute your result *before* you run it. A reported failure is more useful to me than a reported success.
>
> Thanks,
> [name]

## Contact discipline

- One recipient at a time. No bulk send, no automation, no misleading subject line.
- Public institutional or opt-in channels only (see `03_public_contact_points.csv`). No personal emails.
- Record the stage-1 verdict before any theory is shown, and never pool later agreement as corroboration (Fig 2 :40, the same-evidence-pool rule).
- If a referee returns a refutation, copy it into `11_audit.md` with provenance and re-evaluate it as if it came from any other source.
