# The Tether's answer: intelligence versus narrow optimization

**Mode: `specified` on the distinction, `general` on the structural shape, `gap` on the single-test request. Not `grounded`.** Simulated derivation checked against the figures (`05_what_shape_means.md` §6), not a ground reading.

## 1. The residual it answers

Two systems can score the same on a narrow objective and still be called different things. The residual is the missing contact structure: what has to be present for the word "intelligent" to apply, such that a perfect proxy score need not qualify and an imperfect score on a live ground can. This is a union compound, not one hard question (Fig 9 :57), so the verdicts are kept separate per domain.

## 2. The verdict, by domain

**D1, the narrow-optimizer loop.** It is a loop whose metric is not an anchor. Fig 1 :27: *"They are frame-internal. A frame cannot score itself with a quantity it also produces."* A reward the same frame produces cannot score that frame, and a proxy that becomes a target stops settling the question (Fig 2 :29-30). Fig 5 :48: *"Its own output is never a measure of success. A system can be enthusiastically curious and produce nothing."* Mode `general` for the disqualification, `specified` for the AI case.

**D2, ground contact and habitat.** This is where the main distinction lives. Fig 11 :39: *"So the answerable question is not what intelligence is, but what makes a system capable. And capability is a property of agent-and-habitat, never of the agent alone."* Fig 11 :40: *"A system cannot be made more capable by improving the agent in isolation. An improvement that does not change contact changes nothing, however much it improves."* Fig 11 :41 makes the lift move: *"Which is the same move as asking what generates lift rather than whether a thing is a bird. The substrate question dissolves; the contact question does not."* Mode `specified`.

**D2b, the label.** Fig 11 :36: *"Where nothing maintains anything, intelligence is a label applied afterwards to a lineage that met its ground. Nothing was intelligent; selection ran. The word does no work there."* Where a seat exists, the same figure says the word names a practice: *"enumerate the habitat, rank by cascade, introduce actors, build instruments, read the ground"* (Fig 11 :37). In neither case is it a scalar carried by the agent. Mode `general`.

**D3, closure and reach.** Fig 8 :32: *"Closure is idempotent. Composing inside a closed set never leaves it, so no search, however long, adds an atom. This one is definitional: it is what closure means."* Fig 6 :23: *"No frame escapes its own closure. The bound moves only on contact with a frame whose closure differs."* So more optimization is more search inside the same wall; the distinguishing move is an import, not a better optimizer. Mode `general`.

**D4, direction and the objective.** Fig 12 :112: *"A plan is a sequence. A goal is a comparison. Progress is a subtraction. A refutation is a negation, and that one is not a bond."* The narrow objective has goal, progress and plan; what its vocabulary lacks is the refusal of its own aim. Fig 12 :140: *"A vocabulary that cannot refuse can only accumulate."* Order matters: refuse before mint (`THE_FORMULA.md` step 2). Mode `general` for the operator claim.

**Union.** A narrow optimizer can run D1 perfectly while D2 through D4 are absent. Fig 9 :54: *"And when the split lands on a union rather than a partition, unbundle before you search again."* There is no single lever: adding compute improves D1 and leaves the compound standing.

## 3. What it explicitly does not say

- **Not a definition of intelligence in one number.** Fig 9 :32: *"you get a kind of thing, never the answer itself."*
- **Not that narrow optimization is useless.** It is excellent at what it is aimed at. The claim is that excellence at the aim does not settle what the aim should be, and does not maintain contact when the proxy drifts.
- **Not that more compute cannot help.** It can; but an improvement that does not change contact changes nothing about this distinction (Fig 11 :40).
- **Not a claim that the habitat is an ecological niche.** The framework declines that mapping; the world sources on affordance and niche construction corroborate a mechanism shape only.
- **Not `grounded`.** No measurement settles the four-part claim. It is a candidate with written falsifiers.
- **Not a claim that Fig 11 gives a psychometric g, an IQ definition, or Chollet's skill-acquisition efficiency.** Those are different quantities in the same neighbourhood.

## 6. One-paragraph version for outreach

> The Tether does not define intelligence as a score, and it does not claim that a good optimizer is stupid. It says the two differ in arrangement, not ingredients: capability belongs to an agent and its habitat, not to the agent alone; contact with a ground has to be maintained, because a score the same frame produces cannot score that frame; reach has to be kept live by importing from a frame whose assumptions differ, since no amount of search inside a fixed set adds a new atom; and the objective itself has to be refusable, because a vocabulary that cannot refuse can only accumulate. A narrow optimizer can run the whole inner loop and still lack all four. Where nothing maintains anything, "intelligence" is just a name we give a lineage after it met its ground. I am not claiming a single test separates the cases; that is a declared gap. What I am offering is the shape any answer must have, six refuters written in advance, and the instruments that would close it. Nothing here is settled until a ground pays for it.
