## VERDICT (from the figures)

The shape survives; the existence question does not. The residual is a union compound of tensor, lensing and foreground conditions, so a single-frequency claim cannot assign the source. The instrument is a proto-instrument already returning readings that fail to resolve, which makes this `unreached` rather than `unreachable`. A legitimate detection would be a source-discriminated spectrum confirmed against a non-updating sky by a frame whose closure differs; the recombination and reionization windows are two members of one functional group, not competing signals. No figure line returns r, a pivot scale or a two-bump form. Those are held physics terms, provenanced as such. This is a `specified`, ungrounded result with a `general` structural shape and a `gap` on existence. The strongest outreach point is the 2014 instance: a frame named a source before its anchor had been ranged (Fig 2 :35).

- **cost if wrong:** bounded and locatable. The packet is mode-labelled, states five refuters before the conclusion and records the lineage, so an error is findable rather than invisible. The real cost is spending the independence of outside readers by sending a detection narrative instead of a shape claim, which Fig 2 :42 prices: *"Buy independence in proportion to the cost of being wrong."* That cost is avoidable by leading with the residual and its refuter. The opposite error, declaring detection prematurely as in 2014, costs a public retraction and is avoided by the same discipline.
- **undoable:** yes. The shape claim and the per-domain verdicts can be superseded by a ground reading without withdrawing any primitive. A premature detection claim is retractable, and was retracted. No term has to be deleted; the refusal is added and the reach falls safely.
- **reversed by:** a ground reading, not agreement. A multi-frequency, delensed, independently confirmed spectrum at more than 5 sigma showing the predicted two-window shape would move existence from `gap` toward `grounded`; a demonstration that a non-tensor source with the same shape accounts for the signal would break the discriminator claim; a clean spectrum consistent with r below 10^-3 everywhere would break the proto-instrument premise and move the status toward `unreachable`; a derivation of one of the six from the other five would break the framework claim. Until one of these fires, the verdict stands as `candidate`.
