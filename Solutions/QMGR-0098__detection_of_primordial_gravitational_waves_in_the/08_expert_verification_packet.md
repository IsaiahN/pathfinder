# Expert verification packet: the Tether on primordial gravitational waves in the CMB

**What this is:** a claim in a form a CMB or gravitational-wave physicist can check. Mode, derivation and refuters are stated before the conclusion, so a reader knows what to look for and what would settle it.

**Mode: `specified` on the per-domain claims, `general` on the structural shape, `gap` on existence and amplitude, not `grounded`.** No measurement at the ground is claimed. This is a shape and a route, not a result. It is a simulated derivation checked for internal consistency against the figures, not a ground reading.

## 1. The claim, in one paragraph

The measured degree-scale B-mode polarization pattern is a union of at least three overlapping conditions (primordial tensor perturbations, lensing of scalar E-modes, and polarized Galactic foregrounds), so a single-frequency map cannot assign the source. The instrument lineage is a proto-instrument already returning readings that fail to resolve, which makes the tensor question `unreached` rather than `unreachable`. A legitimate detection would be a source-discriminated spectrum, confirmed against a non-updating sky by an instrument whose closure differs from the claiming instrument; the recombination and reionization windows are two members of one functional group, not competing signals. The framework returns that shape and a pre-registered refuter. It does not return a value of r and does not say whether the tensor background exists.

## 2. Three checkable sub-claims

### C1: union versus single cause; checkable against the BK18 likelihood structure and Fig 9

- **Statement:** the residual is a union of overlapping conditions, not one signal with a yes or no answer. A single frequency or sky patch cannot clear the intersections.
- **Source, verbatim:** Fig 9 :54, *"And when the split lands on a union rather than a partition, unbundle before you search again."* Fig 9 :55, *"A bundled problem is usually a union of overlapping conditions, not a list of separate ones."* Fig 9 :57, *"a case in two conditions is not a harder case of either"*
- **What to check:** is the published likelihood a joint model of lensed ΛCDM + r + dust + synchrotron + noise, with a dust-synchrotron correlation parameter? BK18 (arXiv:2110.00483) says yes: *"a multicomponent model of lensed-ΛCDM+r+dust+synchrotron+noise"* with *"seven parameters."* If the components did not overlap in multipole and frequency, the union reading would be unnecessary.
- **Refuter:** show that one observable separates the tensor component from all others without multi-frequency or external-tracer information; or show the published components do not overlap. Then the residual is a partition and C1 fails.

### C2: reachability; checkable against Fig 6 and the instrument record

- **Statement:** the status is `unreached`, not `unreachable`, because an instrument already returns readings that fail to resolve and every sharper instrument was built on that residual.
- **Source, verbatim:** Fig 6 :32, *"An instrument is not built from a description. It is improved from a worse instrument already returning something."* Fig 6 :33, *"Nobody specified a telescope from an account of Mars. A wandering point in the sky was already a reading, and every sharper instrument was built on the residual the last one produced."* Fig 6 :34, *"The question is not whether a sensor could exist. It is whether anything, at any resolution, is already returning something that fails to resolve."*
- **What to check:** did the 2014 excess over the lensing expectation drive the 95/150/220 GHz expansion, the addition of WMAP and Planck maps, and the delensing programme? BK18's own history says yes. Is there any frequency, sky fraction or multipole range at which nothing has ever returned a failing reading? If not, the edge exists.
- **Refuter:** show that no reading at any resolution fails to resolve into known components. Then Fig 6 :34 applies and the status flips to `unreachable`. This is the cheapest and most important failure to test.

### C3: verification condition; checkable against Fig 2 and the 2014 episode

- **Statement:** a detection counts only as triangulation against a non-updating anchor by a frame whose closure differs. Agreement between analysts reading the same likelihood is not corroboration.
- **Source, verbatim:** Fig 2 :29, *"An anchor is whatever settles the question and does not change its answer because of what any frame thinks."* Fig 2 :35, *"Alignment is triangulation, not negotiation. A mirror cannot correct you."* Fig 2 :40, *"agreement about the world is worthless when the agreeing frames share an evidence pool"* Fig 10 :42, *"A verifier that can reconstruct the claim cannot verify it."*
- **What to check:** in 2014, did the claim rest on a single frequency plus a dust model derived from an unreleased Planck slide (Science 2014, JCAP 2014), and did the multi-frequency joint analysis (NASA JPL 2015; ESA 2014/2015) supply the differing frame? If the decisive evidence came from one evidence pool read two ways, C3 is confirmed as a condition and as an instance. The independent-teams rule in CMS blind analysis (https://cms.cern/physics/cms-higgs-search/blinding-and-unblinding-analyses) corroborates the *shape* of the condition from a low-overlap field.
- **Refuter:** show that all supposedly independent instruments share a calibration, foreground model or pipeline, so their agreement is one pool talking; or show that the 2014 claim was settled by a genuine differing frame at the time it was made. Then C3 fails or its instance is withdrawn.

## 3. Pre-registered refuters (written before the conclusion)

The packet's shape claim is refuted if any of the following holds:

1. A matching two-bump spectrum is measured at greater than 5 sigma after multi-frequency cleaning and delensing, and confirmed independently. This moves existence from `gap` toward `grounded` and refutes the non-detection status.
2. The cleaned spectrum is fully accounted for by a non-tensor source with a matching two-window shape (for example patchy reionization at ℓ ≈ 100), so no tensor component is needed.
3. The cleaned spectrum is consistent with r < 10^-3 with no residual bump across the full multipole range, so nothing is failing to resolve.
4. One of the six substrate terms is derived from the other five, or the cross-kind composition rule does not govern the CMB polarization field.
5. All independent measurements share a calibration, foreground model or pipeline, so their agreement is one evidence pool talking.

If any fires, withdraw the relevant sub-claim. Until one is run, nothing here is `grounded`.

## 4. What is explicitly not claimed

- Not that primordial gravitational waves exist or do not exist; existence is a ground reading no figure line supplies.
- Not that any experiment has detected them; the published result is an upper limit, r < 0.036 at 95% confidence (BK18), σ(r) = 0.009.
- Not that the Tether returns r, a pivot scale, or the two-bump functional form. Those are held physics terms, provenanced as such.
- Not that the 2014 retraction was a forward prediction of the framework. It is a retrodiction, an instance of Fig 2's collapse (Fig 8 :56-57).
- Not that the recombination and reionization windows are isomers. Over the fixed atom set they are two members of one functional group (Fig 12 :124, Fig 13 :76).
- Not that the CMB-S4 cancellation changes the verdict. It changes the budget filter, not the ground (Fig 9 :60).
- Not grounded. Simulated derivation, checked for internal consistency only.
