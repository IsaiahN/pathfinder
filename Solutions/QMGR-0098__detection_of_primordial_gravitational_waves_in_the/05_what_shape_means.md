## 1. A shape is a constraint, not a filler

The canonical figures README states the limit: the framework returns *"what a missing thing would have to look like (**a shape, not a name**)."* Fig 9 :32: *"you get a kind of thing, never the answer itself."* Fig 13 :134: *"Unreachable is a verdict here, not a silence."*

From *The Library of Babel* (opened 2026-11-23): *"you can cut your search short if the set of books returned is small and descriptive enough that it provides a general shape of the answer that satisfies your question."*

So a **shape** is the narrowed form of an answer you can state before you can name it. It constrains; it does not fill. For this question:

- `B-mode power whose source is unseparated` is a reading.
- `a source-discriminated tensor component confirmed against a non-updating sky by a differing instrument` is a shape.
- `r = 0.01 at pivot 0.05 per Mpc` is a name, and the framework does not return it.

## 2. How the shape is obtained

- *Library of Babel*: use the vocabulary you already hold that is closest to the thing, look for recurring new terms that overlap or correlate with it, recurse. *"With each new term gained, you narrow the space by definition and deepen your vocabulary."* Here the near vocabulary is B-mode power, E/B parity, frequency spectra, lensing, delensing; the recurring new terms are the spectral indices, the correlation parameter, the two bumps.
- Fig 6 :30: *"The description travels. Fails by being sharpened into terms only we can read. Cast it in effect terms."* Fig 9 :30-31: *"your words are borrowed too, so describe the effect rather than the cause."* The outreach email therefore describes what a detection would look like, not the framework's private vocabulary.
- *Decoding Tacit Knowledge* (opened 2026-11-23): *"When contact isn't possible, the best you can send is a plain description of what you'd expect to see, because anyone can check that against the world."* This is the governing sentence for stage 1 of the offer.
- *Library of Babel* on stopping: the answer is reached when *"the finish line will also be the starting place"*; the paradox is that by then you have *"composed the answer through the process of locating it."* For this question the loop has not reached that point: σ(r) is still shrinking, so the search is live.

## 3. What licenses a name

Only two things, both outside the shape itself:

1. **The ground settles it.** Formula step 5: *"the ground settles it. Verified only by what pays."* The README: the framework *"stops at the limit of what the available instruments can distinguish, and records the lineage so the stop is honest."* For PGWs this means a measured, multi-frequency-confirmed spectrum, not a forecast.
2. **A provenanced import from a frame whose closure differs** (Fig 6 :23, Fig 8 :50-52), with a source gate and shadow test. The multi-frequency foreground frame and the external mass-tracer frame are such imports: they add access, not a physical primitive (Fig 6 :21, *"MINT composes inside the closure and can never add an atom."*).

*They're Scanning Books When They Should* (carried): *"The real import is not a new term in the existing space. It is a new dimension of the space."* And: *"A minimum viable set of six terms is a human's careful guess at reality's compression axes; another sensorium's minimum set need not be those six."* The six are a visible guess, not nature's own list.

## 4. Why the source question governs this physics question

*The Hard Problem is About Source* (opened 2026-11-23) gives the mechanism. Metamerism: an instrument reads green and *"cannot comment on the source."* One green light, or yellow plus blue, or RGB, or CMYK, or a scheme not yet discovered can produce the same reading. *"If several paths create the same behavior, then behavior is a projection with lossy information."* And: *"An instrument that can't distinguish these cases demonstrates its own inadequacy."* It was the **spectrometer**, not an argument, that resolved it: *"It was proven wrong not by argument, but by the arrival of a better instrument."*

This is precisely the PGW situation. A single-frequency B-mode map is the colour-matching instrument: it reads B-mode power and cannot comment on the source. The 2014 claim named the source (tensor perturbations) from a projection that several sources could produce. The multi-frequency instrument (Planck plus BICEP/Keck) was the spectrometer. What is owed when sources are indistinguishable is a better instrument, not a name.

The BICEP2 region reanalysis (JCAP 2014, opened) makes it quantitative: the data were *"consistent with a cosmology with r =0.2 and negligible foregrounds, but also with a cosmology with r =0 and a significant dust polarization signal."* Two sources, one reading. This is metamerism in the CMB.

## 5. Correction to my own §3 and §4

I initially overstated the "shape, not a name" rule. The README's phrase is *"what a **missing** thing would have to look like (a shape, not a name)."* The subject is a primitive not held in the library. Neither the tensor-to-scalar ratio nor the recombination and reionization bumps are missing primitives: they are already terms in the physics frame. Refusing to name them is not what the rule requires.

What remains wrong in the 2014 record is the metamerism error: it named the source of a reading without an instrument that discriminates among sources. The reason is the missing discriminator, not a ban on naming held terms. Both claims are now recorded so neither is quietly lost. This mirrors the correction in the exemplar's `05` section 5b, reached independently for a different question.

## 6. Two consequences for this solution

**Prestige is not action.** From *The Recursive Self-Improvement Trap* (opened 2026-11-23): *"Prestige must never be allowed to validate an action directly... Someone let prestige stand in for proof... Prestige can fund an action. It can give an action a budget to be tried. But it cannot settle an action. Only the ground can settle it. The Ground Maintainer must be blind to prestige."*

The 2014 "discovery of the century" framing is the named collapse: a press conference let reputation stand in for a multi-frequency result that did not yet exist. The legitimate ask is the opposite: give the shape claim a budget to be tried, and let the sky, an instrument that does not update on preference, settle it.

**This audit is a simulation, not a ground reading.** Same essay: *"LLMs can quickly simulate an approximate answer/output... However, the truly testable output would need to be derived from the real architecture or working the formula yourself."* LLM output is "simulacra." So `01` is a simulated derivation, checked for internal consistency against the figures. It is not a test of whether primordial gravitational waves exist and must be labelled as such. It cannot settle anything it reports on.

## 7. What "shape" means here, in one line

The shape is the separator: source-discriminating observables measured against a non-updating sky by instruments whose closures differ, with a refuter written before the run. It says what a detection would have to look like; it does not say whether the tensor background is there, and it cannot name r. That last gap is a verdict (Fig 13 :134), not a silence.
