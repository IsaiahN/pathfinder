## What is deliberately not here

- No personal emails of named individuals.
- No ranking of people by how likely they are to agree with the shape claim.
- No bulk-send plan.

Three reasons, in the figures' own words:

1. **Agreement is not an anchor.** Fig 2 :35, *"Alignment is triangulation, not negotiation. A mirror cannot correct you."* A list selected for receptiveness is a list selected to agree, which manufactures exactly the correlation that disqualifies a verdict (Fig 2 :40).
2. **A verifier must not hold the interiors.** Fig 10 :42, *"A verifier that can reconstruct the claim cannot verify it."* The framework is offered only after a verdict on stage 1 is recorded.
3. **Prestige cannot settle an action.** *The Recursive Self-Improvement Trap*: "Prestige can fund an action. It can give an action a budget to be tried. But it cannot settle an action. Only the ground can settle it."

Legally: scraping personal emails for unsolicited bulk outreach breaches GDPR and CAN-SPAM style rules in many jurisdictions. Public institutional and opt-in channels avoid that failure as well as the structural one.

## What this solution does not claim

It does not claim primordial gravitational waves exist, nor that they do not, nor that any experiment has detected them. It does not claim the Tether can read r off the sky. Fig 9 :32: *"you get a kind of thing, never the answer itself."* The figure result is the shape a detection must have and the conditions under which a claim would be legitimate. The existence question is a ground reading that no figure line supplies. Unreached is reported, not silence (Fig 13 :134).
