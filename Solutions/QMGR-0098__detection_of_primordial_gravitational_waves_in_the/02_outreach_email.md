# Outreach draft: check the output first, then decide about the theory

**Use:** one-to-one, or as a cover letter attached to a submission through the public venues in `03_public_contact_points.csv`. **Not** for bulk send.

**Hard precondition:** do not send until the shape claim in `01` has survived the check below. If the residual is wrong, the two-stage offer collapses on contact.

---

**Subject:** One check on a primordial-gravitational-wave claim, before any theory

> Hi [name],
>
> I have one specific output I would like you to check against what you already know. I am not asking you to read a theory first, and I am not asking you to agree with anything. If the output does not survive, there is nothing else to look at.
>
> **The residual (`specified`, not `grounded`):** a degree-scale B-mode polarization pattern is measured in the CMB, but the same reading can be produced by primordial tensor perturbations, by gravitational lensing of E-modes, and by polarized Galactic dust and synchrotron. The current non-detection is a source-separation problem, indexed per multipole band, sky patch and frequency channel. An aggregate tensor-to-scalar ratio hides where the live signal is. This is not a measurement claim, it is a shape claim about what any answer would have to look like.
>
> **The shape a legitimate detection would have:** a B-mode power spectrum whose source-discriminating observables (frequency dependence, spatial shape, and correlation with an external mass tracer) separate a tensor component at the recombination and reionization windows from the foreground and lensing components, where the separation is measured against a sky that does not change because we prefer an answer, and is confirmed by an instrument whose systematics differ from the claiming instrument. Two instruments pointed at the same thing are two frames; two analysts reading the same likelihood are one frame with two names.
>
> **What would refute it:** I wrote these down before writing this message.
> 1. A matching two-bump spectrum measured at more than 5 sigma after multi-frequency cleaning and delensing, confirmed independently. That would move the existence question from open to settled and refute the non-detection status.
> 2. The cleaned spectrum fully accounted for by a non-tensor source with the same two-window shape, so no tensor component is needed.
> 3. The cleaned spectrum consistent with r below 10^-3 and no residual bump, so nothing is failing to resolve any more.
> 4. A demonstration that all supposedly independent measurements share a calibration, foreground model or pipeline, so their agreement is one evidence pool talking.
>
> If any holds, I will retract the shape claim. A one-line "this fails because..." is more useful to me than agreement. If you would rather not engage, no reply is needed.
>

## Two-stage discipline

1. **Stage 1:** send the residual, the shape claim and the four refuters. Ask what the recipient would count as a failure. Record the verdict before any theory is shown.
2. **Stage 2, opt-in only:** if they are curious, offer the three source links, with the note that the figures, not this summary, are the authority.
3. **Stage 3:** offer the formula as a method to reuse, with the instruction that each user writes their own refuter and states their own mode.

A stage 2 or stage 3 reply is a conversation, never corroboration. Later agreement is never pooled as a witness (Fig 2 :40).
