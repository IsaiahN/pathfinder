# The Tether's answer on detecting primordial gravitational waves in the CMB

**Mode: `specified` on the per-domain claims, `general` on the structural shape, `gap` on existence and amplitude, not `grounded`.** This is a shape and a route, not a result. Simulated derivation checked against the figures (`05_what_shape_means.md` section 6), not a ground reading. No measurement was taken by this author.

## 1. The residual it answers

A degree-scale B-mode polarization pattern is measured in the CMB, but the same reading can be produced by primordial tensor perturbations, by gravitational lensing of scalar E-modes, and by polarized Galactic dust and synchrotron. The 2014 BICEP2 claim named the tensor source before the multi-frequency anchor could discriminate; the joint Planck/BICEP analysis showed that much of the signal was dust while leaving a low-level tensor signal possible. The residual is therefore a **union compound** of overlapping conditions, indexed per multipole band, sky patch and frequency channel (Fig 9 :54-57, Fig 1 :19/:22). Verdicts are kept per domain and never averaged into a single "detection likely" line.

## 3. What it explicitly does not say

- Not that primordial gravitational waves exist, and not that they do not. The existence question is a ground reading the figures do not supply.
- Not that any experiment has detected a tensor signal. The current published result is an upper limit, r < 0.036 at 95% confidence (BK18), with σ(r) = 0.009.
- Not that the Tether can read r off the sky, or that it can derive the recombination bump at ℓ ≈ 80 or the reionization bump below ℓ ≈ 10. Those come from the world literature, marked single-source.
- Not that more sensitivity at one frequency would settle it. Fig 3 :19: *"Optimising a link that already works cannot rescue one that does not."*
- Not that the 2014 retraction was predicted by the framework. It is a retrodiction, an instance of Fig 2's collapse (Fig 8 :56-57).
- Not grounded. No measurement at the ground was taken in producing this answer.

## 4. What would settle it: the ground

A source-discriminating instrument, not a further argument. From *The Hard Problem is About Source*: an instrument that reads green *"cannot comment on the source"*; it was the spectrometer, not an argument, that resolved the case. Here the corresponding tests are:

1. **Positive firing:** a degree-scale B-mode spectrum matching the tensor shape, measured at more than 5 sigma after multi-frequency foreground removal and delensing, and confirmed by an independent instrument or pipeline. This moves the existence question from `gap` toward `grounded`.
2. **Source substitution:** the cleaned spectrum fully accounted for by a non-tensor source with the same two-window shape, for example patchy reionization at ℓ ≈ 100. This would break the discriminator claim without proving tensors absent.
3. **Empty reach:** the cleaned spectrum consistent with r below 10^-3 with no residual bump, across the full allowed multipole range. This would break the "proto-instrument already returning something" premise and move the status toward `unreachable`.
4. **Framework firing:** derive one of the six substrate terms from the other five, or show the cross-kind composition rule does not govern this field.
5. **Independence firing:** show that all supposedly independent measurements share a calibration, foreground model or pipeline, so their agreement is one evidence pool talking.

These five are written before the conclusion and repeated in `01` section 4 and `08` section 3.

## 5. The honest stop

A single sky is a single realisation, so cosmic variance on the largest scales cannot be reduced by building a second universe. Planck's TT-only r constraint is cosmic-variance limited at σ(r) = 0.093. Independence here is bought by frequency and external tracers, not by re-observing the ground. That is a live type limit, recorded as S3 in `01` section 7. The answer stops at the limit of what the instruments can distinguish and records the lineage so the stop is checkable. *"Indiscernibility is not identity"* (figures README). The CMB-S4 cancellation of July 2025 changes which instrument gets built, not whether the tensor background exists (Fig 9 :60: *"Use filters for the budget and witnesses for the verdict."*).
