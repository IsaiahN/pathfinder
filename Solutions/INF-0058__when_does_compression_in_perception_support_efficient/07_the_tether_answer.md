# The Tether's answer: when compression helps and when it distorts

**Mode: `specified` on the per-domain separators, `general` on the structural shape, not `grounded`.** This is a shape and a route, not a result. Simulated derivation checked against the figures (`05_what_shape_means.md` section 7), not a ground reading.

## 1. The residual it answers

A perceptual system throws away detail. The same act is called efficient in one setting and distorting in another. The question asks where the boundary lies. The figures refuse the question as posed, because its two outcomes are a bundled union with different anchors, not two settings of one dial (Fig 9 :54 to :57). They also refuse the obvious score: Fig 1 :25 names *"coverage, and compression achieved"* among the frame-internal quantities, and Fig 1 :27 says *"They are frame-internal. A frame cannot score itself with a quantity it also produces."*

The answer is therefore not a ratio. It is a four-condition separator, stated in effect terms, with per-slot verdicts kept separate and never averaged.

## 2. The separator

1. **Keep the residual per slot.** Fig 1 :19: *"The residual is indexed by object slot, not aggregated."* Fig 1 :22: *"Aggregating across slots is how a live signal disappears into an average."* A global reconstruction error is a budget filter, not a witness. The failure is located at a slot, and the slot must remain readable after compression.

2. **Measure the round trip.** Fig 4 :27: *"The transform between scales loses information, and that is the point."* Fig 4 :30: *"So take the round trip and measure the gap. Without it you are navigating by dead reckoning: confident about a position nobody checked."* Fig 4 :31 and :32: *"The gap is not an error to eliminate. It is the measurement of what the coarser description cannot hold, and it is the only honest report of how much was given up."* Compression is efficient when the gap is measured and paid; it distorts when the gap is assumed to be zero or is never measured.

3. **Pay the bargain, and refuse the one-occasion fit.** Fig 5 :43 and :44: *"A new term is worth having when stating it, plus what remains unexplained after it, costs less than what was unexplained before."* *"A term that explains everything by saying nothing fails this, and so does a term that explains one occasion perfectly."* Cost plus leftover must be less than the uncompressed residual. A compression that fits the training scene perfectly and fails on the next one is the named failure.

4. **Keep the bonds or record their loss.** Fig 12 :5: *"A set of ingredients is not one. The bond is the rest of the information, and it is the half nothing was recording."* Fig 12 :117: *"isomer. Same atoms, different bond, different substance. The difference is invisible in the formula and decisive in the behaviour."* Two compressed percepts with the same features under different relations are different substances. A feature list with no relations has recorded half of itself, and Fig 12 :16 says *"nothing signals that anything was lost"*.

## 3. Why the failure is systematic

The distortion is not noise that averages out. It has three mechanisms, all figure-derived.

- **Atom loss.** Fig 6 :21: *"MINT composes inside the closure and can never add an atom."* Fig 8 :39: *"So a search that finds nothing is never a proof of absence."* If the thrown-away detail was an atom, re-reading the compressed description cannot recover it. An import or a better instrument is owed.
- **Channel decay.** Fig 10 :24: *"the ground does not decay, the channel does"*; Fig 10 :25 and :26: *"stale percepts, dropped transients, saturated metrics, pooled readings"*; Fig 10 :27 and :28: *"the output is not a verdict, it is you have lost touch"*. The world stays unchanged; the report stops tracking it and still looks like a verdict.
- **Coherent drift.** Fig 2 :34: *"everything drifts together, and the drift is coherent, which is why nobody notices."* Fig 2 :35: *"Alignment is triangulation, not negotiation. A mirror cannot correct you."* A compressed frame is self-consistent. Agreement inside it is one evidence pool talking (Fig 2 :40).

The world sources supply the artifacts: color metamerism (one reading, several sources), change blindness (a missed change unless a local transient survives), rate coding (two spike strings, same mean rate), and abstract interpretation (a formal round trip). They corroborate the mechanism shape; they do not decide it.

## 4. What it explicitly does not say

- Not a universal compression ratio, threshold or bit budget. No figure line returns one.
- Not that compression is bad, or that less compression is always better. Fig 4 :27 says the loss is the point; Barlow's bandwidth numbers make compression necessary.
- Not a named optimal code. Sparse coding, predictive coding and rate codes are bindings from the neuroscience frame, provenance owed, not derivations.
- Not a fixed feature list. Fig 11 :51: *"No standing list of good actors survives a change of residual."*
- Not that pooled observer agreement is evidence. Fig 7 :13: *"agreement compounds, evidence does not"*.
- Not `grounded`. No perceptual measurement was taken and no instrument was run.

## 5. What would settle it

A source-discriminating instrument at the slot, not a further argument. The survey asks three ground questions:

1. Does a per-slot readout locate a failing region that a pooled reconstruction error misses, at the same compression ratio?
2. Is `R_T` measurably nonzero at that slot, and does its magnitude predict later behavioral failure on a task where the dropped relation matters?
3. Does a bond-preserving compression outperform a feature-only compression at equal bit cost on a second scene, with an anchor that did not update between scenes?

The instrument need not be invented from a description. Fig 6 :32: *"An instrument is not built from a description. It is improved from a worse instrument already returning something."* Change detection, metamerism matching and abstract-interpretation round trips are already returning something that fails to resolve; they are the proto-instruments.

Standing falsifiers are the seven in `01_figure_grounded_audit.md` section 4. Any one firing withdraws the separator as written.

## 6. The honest stop

**Unreached, not unreachable.** The framework returns a shape, not a name (Fig 9 :32, Fig 13 :133). It cannot say which coding scheme a given brain uses, which features are worth keeping in a given task, or what the correct numerical budget is. It can say what any answer must satisfy and what would refute it. It stops at the limit of the available instruments and records the lineage. *How Intelligence Actually Works*: *"The recursion converges toward indiscernibility, and indiscernibility is not identity."* A compressed percept no instrument currently distinguishes from the original is still a projection.

No ground was consulted in producing this folder. Its status is `candidate`, not accepted.

## 7. One-paragraph version for outreach

> The Tether does not say that compression is good or bad, and it does not name a coding scheme. It says that compression achieved cannot be the verdict, because a frame cannot score itself with a quantity it produces. What survives is a four-condition separator: keep the readout indexed per object slot, measure the round-trip gap between the signal and the compressed-then-reconstructed signal, require the description cost plus what is left unexplained to be less than the uncompressed residual and refuse a one-occasion fit, and keep the typed relations between features rather than only the feature list. Compression supports cognition when those hold and a ground that does not move pays for the result. It produces systematic distortion when the description shrinks while one of them is lost without a signal that it was lost, which happens through dropped atoms that no amount of search in the same closure recovers, through dropped transients in a decaying channel, and through coherent drift that mirrors cannot correct. What is offered is the shape, seven refuters written in advance, and the instruments that would close it. The slot stays open until the ground settles it.
