## 1. Independent domain census

I redid the census before reading the primary's conclusions, using the WATSON order: R by arity, symmetry and scale; one line per domain with actors, currency and anchor; partition or union; order by dependency.

- **R (arity / symmetry / scale).** The residual is the per-slot gap between a prediction and an observation after a lossy transform. Arity: at least three conditions act at once (a slot with positive mass, a retained typed relation, an affordable action budget), so it is a **union compound** (Fig 9 :55 *"A bundled problem is usually a union of overlapping conditions, not a list of separate ones."*). Symmetry: the pair "efficient / distorting" is not symmetric. One is ground-paid success; the other is a persisting residual combined with a channel failure. Scale: first order within a perceptual frame, second order across the abstraction membrane, third order in the verification arrangement.
- **Domain 1, per-slot readout.** Actors: slots, belief, weighting, action, observation. Currency: the residual at a slot. Anchor: the observation at the slot. Union: mass, gap kind and goal weighting overlap.
- **Domain 2, abstraction membrane.** Actors: one agent, a population, `T_A` and `T_E`. Currency: methods up, head starts down, the round-trip gap. Anchor: the measured round trip. Union: method and recording are different bonds over the same atoms.
- **Domain 3, bonds and currencies.** Actors: atoms and typed bonds. Currency: bits and actions, separately. Anchor: a behavior that changes when the bond changes. Union: isomers over a fixed atom set, composition plus import when the atom set changes.
- **Domain 4, channel and anchor.** Actors: the ground, the channel, stale percepts and pooled readings. Currency: contact. Anchor: a measurement that does not update. Union: channel decay and anchor movement are different failures.
- **Domain 5, verification.** Actors: independent frames, one evidence pool. Currency: agreement versus independence. Anchor: the ground, never agreement. Union: triangulation and mirror agreement are different objects.

Order by dependency: read the slot residual before compressing; keep bonds before stating the objective; measure the round trip as the objective; refuse or import before minting; verify last. The middle link has no instrument in most work (Fig 3 :27).

## 4. Round trip gap R_T

Conversely, one thing survives cleanly: a lossy transform owes a measured gap, and the readout that detects failure must stay at the slot. That is the residue worth sending.
