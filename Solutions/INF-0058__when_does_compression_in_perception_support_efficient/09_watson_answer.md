## QUESTION

Decide, in effect terms, what the Tether says about the boundary between efficient and distorting compression in perception. Option A: accept the ordinary framing, in which efficiency is high compression with small reconstruction error, so the boundary is a ratio or a threshold. Option B: refuse the ratio as a verdict, unbundle the two outcomes into a union of conditions with different anchors, and return a four-condition separator with refuters written in advance. What each leads to: A hands the frame a quantity it produces and uses to score itself, hides the failing slot in an average, and spends the independence of any outside reader; B returns a ground-paid shape, a measured round-trip gap, a per-slot readout, a bond requirement and seven refuters, with the slot left open until an instrument settles it.

## DOMAIN CENSUS

R by arity, symmetry and scale, then one line per domain with actors, currency and anchor. Partition or union and order by dependency (Fig 3 :20 *"The chain is ordered by dependency, not by importance."*).

- **R (the residual).** The gap between a prediction and an observation at an object slot after a lossy transform. Arity: at least three conditions (slot mass, bond retention, action budget), so a union compound (Fig 9 :55 *"A bundled problem is usually a union of overlapping conditions, not a list of separate ones."*). Symmetry: "efficient" and "distorting" are not opposite settings of one scalar; one is ground-paid success and the other is a persisting residual plus a decayed channel. Scale: first order inside one perceptual frame, second order across the abstraction membrane, third order in the verification arrangement.
- **Domain 1, per-slot perceptual readout.** Actors: object slots, belief `b`, weighting `w`, action `a`, observation `o'`. Currency: the per-slot residual. Anchor: the observation at the slot, because Fig 1 :23 *"The ground enters as the only metric."* Union: mass, gap kind and goal weighting overlap.
- **Domain 2, the abstraction membrane.** Actors: one agent, a population, `T_A` and `T_E`. Currency: a method carried up, a head start sent down, the round-trip gap `R_T`. Anchor: the measured round trip. Union: method transfer and recording transfer are different bonds over the same atoms.
- **Domain 3, composition and bonds.** Actors: atoms, the seven typed bonds and negation. Currency: description cost in bits and settling cost in actions. Anchor: a behavior that differs when the bond differs. Union: isomers over a fixed atom set, composition plus import when the atom set changes.
- **Domain 4, ground-channel maintenance.** Actors: the anchor, the channel, stale percepts and pooled readings. Currency: contact with a ground that does not update. Anchor: a measurement or outcome that cannot be moved by a frame's opinion. Union: channel decay and anchor movement are different failures.
- **Domain 5, verification by other frames.** Actors: independent instruments or frames, one evidence pool. Currency: agreement versus independence. Anchor: the ground, never agreement. Union: triangulation and mirror agreement are different objects.
- **Order by dependency.** Read the per-slot residual before compressing; keep bonds before stating an objective; measure `R_T` as the objective; refuse or import before minting; verify with a second frame last. The middle link has no instrument in most work, which is where the argument lands (Fig 3 :27 *"A link with no instrument is not a link that works. It is a link nobody has looked at."*).

**Strain test.** Fig 1 :25 disqualifies compression as a self-score while Fig 5 :45 puts description length inside the MDL bargain. Re-census: one governs a metric the frame produces, the other a cost term in a bargain settled by the ground. Different objects, no contradiction. The six-term physical substrate was tried on perceptual atoms and withdrawn (see `01` F11 S2); only the general state/structure and discarded-detail-as-noise claims survive.

## VERDICT (from the figures)

The ordinary framing is refused. Compression achieved is a self-produced score and cannot be the verdict. What survives is a four-condition separator: keep the residual per slot, measure the round-trip gap and report it as what the coarser description cannot hold, pay the description-length bargain with a one-occasion fit refused, and retain the typed bonds or record their loss. Compression supports cognition when a ground that does not move pays under those conditions and the channel keeps contact. It distorts systematically when the description shrinks while one condition is silently lost. The mechanisms are atom loss inside a closed reach, a decayed channel with dropped transients, and coherent drift that a same-pool mirror cannot correct. Over a fixed atom set, the two outcomes are isomers, same features under a different bond. The framework returns that shape, not a coding scheme, a ratio or a feature list. Mode `specified` on the separators, `general` on the shape, not `grounded`.

- **cost if wrong:** bounded and locatable. The packet states mode, refuters and provenance before the conclusion, so an error is findable rather than invisible. The larger cost is spending the independence of outside readers by sending an architecture instead of a checkable residual, which Fig 2 :42 prices: *"Buy independence in proportion to the cost of being wrong."* That cost is avoidable by leading with the shape and its refuters.
- **undoable:** yes. The four conditions can be corrected without importing a new primitive, and a sent packet can be superseded. A false separator can be withdrawn through the pre-registered refuter path, which adds a term and removes standing rather than deleting a record.
- **reversed by:** a ground reading, not agreement. A per-slot experiment showing that a pooled score locates the failing region as well; a measurement that the round-trip gap is zero at the slot; a bond-preserving compression that fails to beat a feature-only one at equal bit cost on a relation-sensitive task; or a search inside the same compressed closure that recovers the dropped atom and pays. Until one fires, the verdict stands as `candidate`.
