## 1. A shape is a constraint you can state before you can name the answer

The canonical figures README says the framework reports *"what a missing thing would have to look like (a shape, not a name)"*, then stops at the limit of the instruments and records the lineage. Fig 9 :32: *"you get a kind of thing, never the answer itself."* Fig 13 :133: *"A system with a closure can answer that before it searches, and what it returns is a shape rather than a name."* Fig 13 :134: *"Unreachable is a verdict here, not a silence"*.

From *The Library of Babel*: *"You can cut your search short if the set of books returned is small and descriptive enough that it provides a general shape of the answer that satisfies your question."*

So for this question a **shape** is the set of conditions any answer must satisfy before anyone has picked a coding scheme. The four-condition separator in `01_figure_grounded_audit.md` section 0 is a shape. "Use sparse coding", "use predictive coding", "keep the low spatial frequencies" are names. A shape constrains; it does not fill.

## 2. How the shape is obtained

- *Library of Babel*: use the vocabulary already held that is closest to the thing, look for recurring new terms that overlap or correlate with it, and recurse. *"With each new term gained, you are able to narrow the space by definition and deepen your vocabulary."*
- *Decoding Tacit Knowledge*: *"When contact isn't possible, the best you can send is a plain description of what you'd expect to see, because anyone can check that against the world."*
- Fig 6 :30: *"The description travels. Fails by being sharpened into terms only we can read. Cast it in effect terms."* Fig 9 :30 and :31: *"your words are borrowed too, so describe the effect rather than the cause."*
- *The Hard Problem is About Source*: an instrument that reads green *"cannot comment on the source"*. The shape of the missing thing is a discriminator, not a name for the source. The spectrometer supplied it.

For INF-0058, describing the effect rather than the cause means saying what would be observed if compression were harmful: a specific slot fails, the round trip holds a nonzero gap, a bond is missing from the description while the ingredient list is intact, or a transient was dropped while the channel still reports confidence. None of those statements mentions a brain area or a coding scheme.

## 3. What licenses a name

Only two things, both outside the shape itself:

1. **The ground settles it.** `THE_FORMULA.md` step 5: *"the ground settles it. Verified only by what pays. A gate is not the ground."* The canonical README: the framework *"stops at the limit of what the available instruments can distinguish, and records the lineage so the stop is honest."*
2. **A provenanced import from a frame whose closure differs**, with a source gate and a shadow test (Fig 6 :23, Fig 8 :50 to :52).

**Correction to an overstatement.** The phrase *"a shape, not a name"* governs a **missing** primitive, one not held in the library. Sparse coding, predictive coding, rate codes and feature lists are already in the neuroscience library. Naming one of them as the compression scheme is a **binding** from the neuroscience frame onto something already held, not the invention of a seventh primitive. What remains owed is provenance: which measurement, in which preparation, paid for the binding. This correction is kept visible rather than quietly applying the narrower rule.

## 4. Compression has a special status in four of the essays

### 4.1 How Intelligence Actually Works: projection, provenance and the distinction horizon

The essay defines projection as an expression of an inner state through a medium in a form the source did not have, and says the crossing is lossy. Its video-frame example is directly about this question: a single frame extracted from a video *"would look the same to the untrained eye"* as a photograph, *"and nothing in the image records which"*. That is provenance loss, and it is the canonical case of a compressed artifact that looks complete.

Two sentences govern the boundary:

- *"The distinguishing structure might be exactly what the next rational restriction discards in order to transform."* This is Fig 12 :5 and :94 in narrative form: the bond is the half nothing was recording, and the arrangement is the substance. The very act of compressing can delete the property that would have identified the source.
- *"The recursion converges toward indiscernibility, and indiscernibility is not identity."* A compressed percept that no available instrument distinguishes from the original is still a projection. The lineage has to be kept. The essay's **distinction horizon** (one observer) and **union horizon** (all observers with any instrument they can build) give the honest stop: being below the union horizon *"is never a settled verdict, only a report that nobody has reached it yet."*

### 4.2 They're Scanning Books When They Should Be Studying Nature: compression is not neutral

The essay's central claim for this question: *"compression is shaped by the sensory priors of the compressor"*, so *"there is no neutral representation to aim at, and every reasoner, biological, artificial, or composed, can only be surprised along axes its primitives can already represent."* It adds: *"A minimum viable set of six terms is a human's careful guess at reality's compression axes; another sensorium's minimum set need not be those six."* And: *"Declaring the primitives does not make them neutral. It makes the choice visible."*

Narrowing for this solution: this does **not** ban compression, and it does **not** say the framework's six terms govern perceptual features (that mapping was withdrawn in `01_figure_grounded_audit.md` F11 S2). It says the choice of what to compress is a visible choice with a provenance obligation. It supports F10: no standing feature list survives a change of residual. The essay's final move, *"The real import is not a new term in the existing space. It is a new dimension of the space"*, is the boundary case: sometimes the fix for a distortion is not keeping more of the same features but importing a new sensor axis.

### 4.3 Decoding Tacit Knowledge: ultra-compression needs context the text does not carry

An expert's few words carry paragraphs because the listener brings context, subtext and pretext. Writing carries context, subtext partly and pretext almost never. *"Without the right one, a compressed message can't be unpacked, however clearly it was said."* The essay quotes the relevant figures directly: Fig 13 :32, *"the detail a crossing discards reappears above it as noise"*; Fig 4 :31, *"The gap is not an error to eliminate. It is the measurement of what the coarser description cannot hold"*; Fig 12 :107, *"Settling compresses the description and not what it cost to establish"*; Fig 11 :40, *"An improvement that does not change contact changes nothing, however much it improves."*

Applied here: a compressed percept can travel between people, but the work that built it does not travel with it. What travels is context and effect descriptions. That is why the outreach asks what the recipient would expect to see, rather than sending the architecture.

### 4.4 How a Society of Agents Would Work: methods, recordings and pooled slots

The essay distinguishes two stores: a room for one problem where everything can be shared, and a commons across problems that holds only methods. *"Only methods cross up. Only head starts come down."* It quotes Fig 1 :22, *"Aggregating across slots is how a live signal disappears into an average"*, and explains why a success rate must be kept per kind of situation: a method that is brilliant in one setting must not be drowned by an average across all of them. It also quotes Fig 12's point that inside a run the arrangement persists and nothing is re-earned, while across a boundary that drops it everything is. This is the social-scale version of F2 and F3.

## 5. The shape for this question, stated once

Any answer to INF-0058 must say all of the following and may not use a bits-saved score as the verdict:

1. **Per-slot.** The readout that detects harm is indexed by object or region, not pooled into a global quality number.
2. **Round-tripped.** The loss across the compression is measured as a gap, and reported as what was given up rather than treated as noise to be zeroed.
3. **Bargained.** The description cost plus the leftover must be less than the uncompressed residual, and a one-occasion fit is refused.
4. **Bonded.** What is retained is the typed arrangement, not just the feature list; if a bond is dropped, that is a recorded loss.
5. **Grounded, not self-scored.** An anchor that does not move settles it, and an independent frame with separate contact checks it.
6. **Two currencies.** Bits and actions are priced separately.
7. **Provenanced.** Naming a particular scheme is a binding with a source, not a derivation.

The shape does **not** return a universal compression ratio, a named optimal code, a fixed feature list, or a numeric budget. Those are `gap` items of the shape-not-a-name kind, reported as unreached rather than silence (Fig 9 :32, Fig 13 :134).

## 6. Prestige is not action

From *The Recursive Self-Improvement Trap*: *"Prestige must never be allowed to validate an action directly"*; *"Prestige can fund an action. It can give an action a budget to be tried. But it cannot settle an action. Only the ground can settle it. The Ground Maintainer must be blind to prestige."*

A list of famous perceivers who agree that a compressed image looks fine is the named collapse, not evidence. The legitimate ask is a budget to try the separator against a ground, with a failure condition written first.

## 7. My own audit is a simulation, not a ground reading

Same essay: *"the output is only as good as the validator. The agent, or the tool, or the frame, or the theory, cannot validate itself."* And: *"LLMs can quickly simulate an approximate answer/output with the formula found in the architecture post... However, the truly testable output would need to be derived from the real architecture or working the formula yourself."* So `01_figure_grounded_audit.md` is a simulated derivation checked for internal consistency against the figures. It is **not** a test of any perceptual claim and must be labelled as such. It cannot settle anything it reports on.
