## QUESTION

Decide, in effect terms, what the Tether says about the origins of water on Earth, given that the present reading (D/H, N, O isotopes and noble gases) can be measured precisely but does not by itself name a route. Option A: assert a single origin (comets, or asteroids, or the nebula) as the answer the framework returns. Option B: keep the figure-derived shape, name no single reservoir, and mark the literal single-origin list as a declared gap. What each leads to: A sends a `general`-mode verdict disguised as a ground result, spends the independence of the people checking it, and commits the metamerism error of naming a source from a reading; B sends a checkable shape, per-reservoir verdicts, written refuters and a discriminator owed, and leaves the contribution of each route to the ground.

## VERDICT (from the figures)

The shape survives, the literal single-origin answer does not. The origins of terrestrial water are a union of production histories whose present isotope reading cannot name its own route. What is owed is a source-discriminating instrument, per-reservoir provenance for every sample and model, and an explicit record of which part of the residual remains unreached. The verdict is `specified` on D1 and D2, `general` on the structural shape, `gap` on the single-origin list, and nothing in this folder is `grounded`. One strain remains live and is named: the lineage-isomer use of the bond language is a level-relative analogy, not a molecular claim. Status of every verdict: candidate.

- **cost if wrong:** bounded and locatable. The packet is mode-labelled, states its refuters before the conclusion and records its lineage, so an error can be found rather than invisible. The real cost is spending the independence of outside readers by sending a recording instead of a generator, which Fig 2 :42 prices ("Buy independence in proportion to the cost of being wrong."). That cost is avoidable by leading with the shape and its refuter. A second cost is scientific: if the union rule does not govern this residual, the folder has imposed a shape on a partition and obscured a single live source. Both costs are recoverable because no term was deleted.
- **undoable:** yes. The bindings to particular reservoirs are removable without touching the derivation, and `07_the_tether_answer.md` and `08_expert_verification_packet.md` already keep them separate from the shape. A sent claim can be superseded by a corrected packet. No primitive has to be withdrawn and no evidence is discarded; a refusal adds a term and lowers the reach safely. The `gap` can be closed by an instrument without rewriting the shape.
- **reversed by:** a ground reading, not agreement. A multi-isotope and noble-gas discriminator on independent samples that assigns the reservoirs with no leftover residual would collapse the union to a partition (fires refuter 1). An instrument mapping a present bulk reading uniquely to one route would close the shape-not-a-name gap (fires refuter 2). A reorganization showing one source reproduces all ratios and the water mass budget would do the same. A figure re-census showing a line that directly names one reservoir, or that Fig 9 :55-57 does not govern this residual, would break the structural claim. A demonstration that a route is pure sequence with water already present would misname the `⇒` claim. A returned bulk cometary sample matching Earth's H, N and noble gases would re-open the exclusion of most comets. Until one of these fires, the verdict stands as candidate.
