# Outreach draft: check the shape before you decide about the source

**Use:** one-to-one, or as a cover letter attached to a submission through the public venues in `03_public_contact_points.csv`. **Not** for bulk send.

**Hard precondition:** do not send until the shape claim and its refuter in `01_figure_grounded_audit.md` survive the reader's own check. The offer collapses on contact if the stage-1 output does not hold.

---

**Subject:** One checkable claim about the origin of Earth's water, before any theory

> Hi [name],
>
> I have one specific output I would like you to check against what you already know. I am not asking you to read a framework first, and I am not asking you to agree with anything. If the output does not survive, there is nothing else to look at.
>
> **The output (`specified` on the per-domain verdicts, `general` on the structural shape, `gap` on the literal single-origin list, not `grounded`):** the present Earth gives us a reading, not a route. D/H, nitrogen and noble-gas ratios in ocean water, mantle plumes, meteorites and cometary samples can be measured precisely, but several distinct source histories produce overlapping values. The reading cannot comment on its own source. What the figures license is a shape: the origins of terrestrial water are a union of production histories (nebular hydrogen on grains or in a magma ocean, enstatite-like inner-disk material, carbonaceous chondrite material, cometary and dust input, mantle storage and volcanic return), with the contribution indexed per reservoir, and with a provenance record for every sample and model. No single origin is named by the framework. That absence is a declared gap, not a silence.
>
> **What I am not claiming:** that the figures select comets, or asteroids, or the solar nebula as the one source. That is a binding from the planetary-science record, with its own provenance, and it is still contested in the literature. The framework returns a shape, not a name. Convergent derivation and adopted import look identical in the contents; only the record of where each came from separates them. Until that record is shown, a binding carries zero evidentiary weight from the framework.
>
> **What would refute the shape claim:** I wrote these down before sending this.
> 1. One source reproduces all discriminating H, N, O and noble-gas ratios and the water mass budget, with no residual left, so the union collapses to a partition.
> 2. An instrument maps a present-day bulk reading uniquely to one route, on independent samples, so the shape-not-a-name gap closes.
> 3. The route turns out to be pure sequence (water already present and merely moved), not production, so the origin claim for that route is misnamed.
>
> If any one holds, the claim is withdrawn or rewritten. A one-line "this fails because..." is more useful to me than agreement. If you would rather not engage, no reply is needed.
>
> **If it survives, and only then:** here are the sources. The figures are the authority, not my summary of them.
> - Canonical figure text with line numbers (local framework extract): `FRAMEWORK/reference/03-FIGURES_TEXT.md`
> - Figures README and the eight-step formula: `https://github.com/IsaiahN/tether/tree/general-agent/figures`
> - The peer-reviewed reviews this shape was checked against: Russell, Ballentine and Grady 2017, `https://pmc.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/articles/PMC5394259/`; Alexander 2017, `https://pmc.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/articles/PMC5394251/`
>
> **If you want to use the machinery:** the formula is in the figures link. Run it on a problem you know well and can check against a ground. Write down what would refute your result *before* you run it, and label the mode of what you get. A reported failure is more useful to me than a reported success.
>
> [your name]
