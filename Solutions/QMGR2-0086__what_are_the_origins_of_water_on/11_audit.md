## Round trip gap R_T

The Symbols table defines R_T as the gap between what was sent up and what came back down, `gap(x, (T_E ∘ T_A)(x))`, measuring what the coarser description cannot hold. Fig 4 :30 requires it to be measured rather than assumed zero. I ran the round trip on the condensation the folder actually uses.

1. **Sent up (x):** the verbatim figure set, chiefly Fig 9 :55 (union), Fig 8 :51-52 (contents versus provenance), Fig 1 :19 (per-slot residual) and Fig 13 :134 (unreachable is a verdict).
2. **Abstraction (T_A):** the shorthand carried in `07_the_tether_answer.md` and `09_watson_answer.md`: present reading cannot name its route; sources are a union of production histories; per-reservoir verdicts; provenance on every binding; single name is a gap.
3. **Concretisation (T_E):** the concrete world claim: comets, asteroids, nebular material or enstatite chondrites supplied Earth's water, with a specific percentage and a named source.

**R_T is nonzero.** Three things the shorthand holds do not survive the trip back down:

- the **union structure**, which a single-source answer silently converts into a partition;
- the **provenance condition**, which a bare isotope ratio cannot carry;
- the **gap status**, which a concrete name replaces with a verdict the framework does not return.

Conversely, two things survive cleanly: the reading cannot comment on its own source, and the residual must be indexed per reservoir rather than averaged. Those are the residue worth sending. R_T was **not** measured numerically, because no ground reading exists; it is recorded qualitatively as the enumerated set of dropped constraints. Treating the shorthand as identical to the figures would set R_T to zero and would be the dead-reckoning error Fig 4 :30 names. The honest form is a lossy condensation with a measured gap, which is what `09_watson_answer.md` now states.
