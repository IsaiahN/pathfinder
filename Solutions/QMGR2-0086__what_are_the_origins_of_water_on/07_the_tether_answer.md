# The Tether's answer on the origins of water on Earth

**Mode: `specified` on the per-domain verdicts, `general` on the structural shape, `gap` on the literal single-origin list. Not `grounded`.** This is a shape and a route, not a result. Simulated derivation checked against the figures (`05_what_shape_means.md` section 7), not a ground reading.

## 1. The residual it answers

Earth has a large water inventory with a measurable isotope signature. The present reading cannot name the route that produced it: several source histories yield overlapping D/H, N and noble-gas values, and the chemical fingerprints are smeared by mixing and parent-body processing (Meech 2019; Alexander 2017). The residual is the gap between what the reading can discriminate and what the question asks. Fig 9 :54-57 makes it a **union compound**, not one hard question with two opposite answers. It has its own loop and its own ground, the physical sample record.

## 2. The shape

1. **No single origin is returned.** Fig 13 :133-135: the system *"can report that it lacks something rather than producing an answer anyway"* and *"Unreachable is a verdict here, not a silence."* Fig 9 :32: *"you get a kind of thing, never the answer itself."* The literal answer is a declared `gap` of the shape-not-a-name kind, not silence and not underivable in principle.
2. **The sources are a union, not a partition.** Fig 9 :55-57: *"A bundled problem is usually a union of overlapping conditions, not a list of separate ones."* The live members are nebular hydrogen on grains or in a magma ocean, enstatite-like inner-disk material, CI/CM-like asteroidal material, cometary and dust input, and mantle storage with volcanic return. Russell et al. (2017) report the same union from the sample record: volatiles incorporated both during accretion and by later asteroidal impacts, with a separate cometary contribution to noble gases. Verdicts are kept per reservoir and never averaged (Fig 1 :19).
3. **The reading cannot comment on its source.** Fig 8 :50-52: *"Convergent derivation and adopted import look identical in the contents. Only the record of where each came from separates them."* A D/H value is the same number from several routes. What separates them is provenance: which sample, which mission, which activity state, which model assumption. This is the metamerism point of *The Hard Problem is About Source*.
4. **What is owed is a discriminator, not another argument.** Fig 6 :34-35: *"Where nothing is, there is no edge to extend from, and the search for a proto-instrument is a search for one that is already there."* The edge already exists: isotope mass spectrometry on meteorites, plumes and cometary samples. Meech (2019): *"Multiple Fingerprints are Needed"*; Alexander (2017): *"D/H alone is not sufficient to determine a formation distance to compare to dynamical models."*
5. **An origin is production, not sequence.** Operators table :37: *"A produces B; B did not exist before."* A stage list is a recording; `⇒` is the claim. Over the same H2O substance the routes are lineage isomers, same substance, different route, difference carried by provenance (Fig 12 :117, Fig 13 :121, :127-129). A cycle returns water to the mantle, so a one-way chain is one pass of a loop (Fig 12 :121).
6. **Bindings need a source gate and a shadow test.** Fig 8 :9-13, Fig 6 :23. Naming EC material, carbonaceous chondrites or comets is a binding from the planetary frame onto an available reservoir, not a new primitive and not a wall-moving import. The framework neither licenses nor forbids the binding; the evidence does.

## 3. What it explicitly does not say

- Not that water came from comets, asteroids, the nebula or enstatite chondrites as the one source. Those are world claims with contested contributions; the figures return no name.
- Not a single origin percentage, and not an averaged global verdict. The residual is indexed per reservoir (Fig 1 :19, :22).
- Not that the present reading is sufficient to settle a route. It is a projection with lossy information (Fig 8 :50-52; metamerism).
- Not `grounded`. No measurement at the delivery event exists; only its surviving record is read (Fig 5 :41).
- Not a claim that the six-term substrate governs an isotope ratio. That was a first-pass strain, dissolved by re-census: Fig 13 governs the primitives; the D/H comparison lives in the measurement frame.

## 4. What would settle it, the ground

A source-discriminating instrument, not a further argument on one ratio. Concretely:

- A multi-isotope and noble-gas discriminator (H, N, O, Xe, Kr, Ne) tied to a dynamical disk model, applied to independently recovered samples, that assigns the reservoirs with a stated leftover residual. Meech (2019) and Alexander (2017) both say this is what is needed.
- A bulk cometary measurement, not only the water fraction, matched to Earth's bulk H, N and noble gases. Alexander (2017) warns that measured cometary H2O and HCN/NH3 are *"probably not their bulk compositions."*
- A measurement showing one route reproduces all ratios and the mass budget with nothing left, which would collapse the union to a partition and fire refuter 1.
- Standing falsifiers: a figure line that names one source directly; a reading that maps uniquely to one route; a demonstration that the route is pure `→` and not `⇒`; a returned sample that re-opens the exclusion of most comets. Full list in `01` section 4.

## 5. The honest stop

**Unreached, not unreachable.** The candidate sources are available in the world record; the missing thing is a discriminator that maps the overlapping readings to routes. Fig 8 :39: *"So a search that finds nothing is never a proof of absence."* Fig 6 :27: *"Which is why some unreachability is depth and some is genuine absence, and from inside the frame the two look the same."* More measurements at the same read are SEARCHED; a wider multi-isotope instrument is INWARD; dynamical models and sample returns and the equifinality literature are OUTWARD. They are two tracks and must not be read against each other (Fig 6 :31). The answer stops at the limit of the available instruments and records the lineage so the stop is checkable.

## 6. One-paragraph version for outreach

> The Tether does not name a single origin for Earth's water and does not claim to have measured one. It says the present reading (D/H, nitrogen, noble gases) cannot comment on its own source, because several source histories produce overlapping fingerprints, so what is owed is a discriminator across several isotopes and reservoirs, not another argument on one ratio. The candidate sources form a union of production histories, nebular and asteroidal and cometary and mantle, indexed per reservoir and kept apart in the verdict, with provenance attached to every sample and model. Over the same water molecule the routes are lineage isomers: same substance, different route, the difference carried by the record rather than by the molecule. The literal single origin is a declared gap, not a silence, and the framework stops where the instruments stop, leaving the contribution of each route to the ground.
