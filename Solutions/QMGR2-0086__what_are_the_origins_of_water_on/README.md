## What is deliberately not here

- No personal emails of named individuals.
- No ranking of people by how likely they are to agree with the claim.
- No bulk-send plan.

Three reasons, in the figures' own words:

1. **A room does not upgrade the label.** Seat Map :283: *"A room reaching a conclusion without the ground is agreement, and agreement among frames is a weighted average of influence. Adding people raises the vocabulary and does not add an anchor."*
2. **Correlation disqualifies a verdict.** Seat Map :163: *"High correlation is disqualifying for a verdict and efficient for a convention."* Selecting recipients for receptiveness manufactures the correlation that makes their agreement worthless.
3. **Asking for trust before checking spends independence.** Fig 10 :42: *"A verifier that can reconstruct the claim cannot verify it."* The first contact carries the residual and its refuter, never the framework's interiors.

Legally: unsolicited bulk email to scraped personal addresses breaches GDPR and CAN-SPAM style rules in many jurisdictions. Every venue below is public, institutional and opt-in.

## Honest standing

Every substantive file is a **simulated derivation checked for internal consistency against the figures, not a ground reading.** No meteorite was measured, no comet sample was read, no isotope ratio was recomputed. The framework's own source says LLM output is an approximate simulation and *"the truly testable output would need to be derived from the real architecture or working the formula yourself"* (Recursive Self-Improvement Trap, 2026-11-19). This folder cannot settle the scientific question it reports on. It can only state the shape an answer must have, the refuters written in advance, and the instruments that would close it.
