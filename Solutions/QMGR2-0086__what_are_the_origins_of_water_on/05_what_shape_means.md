## 1. A shape is a constraint, not a filler

The canonical figures README states the limit: the framework returns *"what a missing thing would have to look like (a shape, not a name)."* Fig 9 :32: *"you get a kind of thing, never the answer itself."* Fig 13 :134-135: *"Unreachable is a verdict here, not a silence, It cannot say which primitive is missing, because naming it would mean already holding it."*

From *The Library of Babel* (opened): *"you can cut your search short if the set of books returned is small and descriptive enough that it provides a general shape of the answer that satisfies your question."*

So a **shape** is the narrowed form of an answer you can state before you can name it. It constrains; it does not fill. For this question, *"the origins of Earth's water are a union of production histories whose present reading cannot name its own route"* is a shape. *"Water came from comet Hartley 2"* is a name. The question as posed asks for a name; the framework returns the shape and marks the name as owed.

## 2. How a shape is obtained

- *The Library of Babel* (opened): *"you will have to use the existing vocabulary you do know that is closest to the words that you wish to describe and grasp the shape of."* And: *"With each new term gained, you narrow the space and deepen your vocabulary."* Here the closest available vocabulary is isotope ratio, reservoir, impact, degassing, fractionation, provenance and discrimination. Those terms narrow the search without selecting a winner.
- Fig 6 :30: *"The description travels. Fails by being sharpened into terms only we can read. Cast it in effect terms."* Fig 9 :30-31: *"your words are borrowed too, so describe the effect rather than the cause."*
- *Decoding Tacit Knowledge* (opened): *"When contact isn't possible, the best you can send is a plain description of what you'd expect to see, because anyone can check that against the world."* The effect description here: several sources produce overlapping D/H and N values; a discriminator across several isotopes and reservoirs would separate them; no such single-route discriminator has been returned.
- *The Library of Babel* on bundles (opened): *"Perhaps your problem was a bundle or conflation of several problems masquerading as one?"* That is Fig 9 :54-58 applied to the origin question: nebular incorporation, impact delivery and mantle recycling are overlapping conditions, not one problem with one answer.
- *The Library of Babel* on stopping: the answer is reached when *"the finish line will also be the starting place"*, and the paradox is that by then you have *"composed the answer through the process of locating it."*

## 3. What licenses a name

Only two things, both outside the shape itself:

1. **The ground settles it.** `THE_FORMULA.md` step 5: *"SETTLE the ground settles it. Verified only by what pays. A gate is not the ground."* Here the ground is the physical sample record and the measured isotope ratios, which do not update on our theory.
2. **A provenanced import from a frame whose closure differs** (Fig 6 :23, Fig 8 :50-52), with a source gate and a shadow test. A returned comet sample or a dynamical disk model can supply a binding, but only with its recovery history, its assumptions and the gap it explains recorded beside it.

*They're Scanning Books When They Should* (carried from the exemplar mapping, not re-opened) sharpens this: *"The real import is not a new term in the existing space. It is a new dimension of the space."* And: *"Declaring the primitives does not make them neutral. It makes the choice visible."* The candidate reservoirs in this question are a visible choice from the planetary record, not a discovery of nature's own list.

## 4. Why the record's single origin is the forbidden error

*The Hard Problem is About Source* (opened) gives the mechanism. Metamerism: an instrument reads green and *"the instrument is speaking only about what it measured, it cannot comment on the source."* The source could be one green light, yellow plus blue, RGB, CMYK, or a scheme not yet discovered. *"If several paths create the same behavior, then behavior is a projection with lossy information."* And: *"The answer is unreached until a better instrument exists."* It was the spectrometer, not an argument, that resolved it.

Applied here: a D/H reading of terrestrial water is the green reading. Enstatite-like inner-disk hydrogen, carbonaceous chondrite water, cometary water that has been fractionated, and solar-wind-implanted hydrogen can overlap in that reading. Naming one source from D/H alone is the metamerism error. What is owed is a better instrument, not a name. Meech (2019) and Alexander (2017) independently say the same thing in planetary terms: multiple fingerprints are needed, and D/H alone cannot determine formation distance.

*The Recursive Self-Improvement Trap* (opened): *"the output is only as good as the validator. The agent, or the tool, or the frame, or the theory, cannot validate itself."* A figure-derived shape cannot validate its own binding to one reservoir.

## 5. Q2: ANSWERED: what licenses a particular source in the slot?

**Nothing in the framework.** The framework returns a shape. A particular source is licensed only by the ground settling a measured term, or by a provenanced import with a shadow test. Until one of those, the name is the searcher's own contribution and must be recorded as such. The reviews disagree about how much each route contributed (Russell et al. favour accretion plus asteroidal impacts; Piani et al. favour a large EC contribution; Alexander requires CI/CM plus a solar component). That disagreement is a live residual, not a license to pick one.

## 5b. Correction to my own §3 and §4

I overstated the "shape, not a name" rule in the first pass. The README's phrase is *"what a **missing** thing would have to look like (a shape, not a name)."* Its subject is a primitive not held in the library. The candidate sources here (nebular hydrogen, chondritic material, cometary ice, mantle water) are already available in the world record; they are not missing primitives. Naming one is a **binding** from the planetary frame onto an available reservoir, not the invention of a seventh term.

What remains wrong in the first-pass reading is calling that binding an import that moves the wall. Fig 6 :22: *"NOVEL means novel relative to atoms, not to the world. IMPORT adds an atom, and the added atom is what expands the closure."* A binding to an already-available reservoir adds no atom to the world record. The real error is the metamerism error of section 4: a source named from a reading when several sources could give that reading. Both claims are now recorded so neither is quietly lost.

## 6. Prestige is not action

From *The Recursive Self-Improvement Trap* (opened): *"Prestige can fund an action. It can give an action a budget to be tried. But it cannot settle an action. Only the ground can settle it. The Ground Maintainer must be blind to prestige."* Also: *"You may all agree and be wrong."*

A campaign that gathers famous names to agree that water came from one source is the named collapse, not corroboration. Seat Map :283: *"A room reaching a conclusion without the ground is agreement, and agreement among frames is a weighted average of influence. Adding people raises the vocabulary and does not add an anchor."* The legitimate ask gives the claim a budget to be tried and lets the ground (a sample, an isotope measurement, a discriminator) settle it.

## 7. My own audit is a simulation, not a ground reading

Same essay (opened): *"LLMs can quickly simulate an approximate answer/output with the formula found in the architecture post."* And: *"the truly testable output would need to be derived from the real architecture or working the formula yourself."* So `01_figure_grounded_audit.md` and every other substantive file here are simulated derivations checked for internal consistency against the figures. They are **not** tests of the origin claim and must be labelled as such. They cannot settle anything they report on.

## 8. Consequences for the shape claim

1. The shape survives: union of production histories, per-reservoir index, provenance for bindings, discriminator owed, literal single name refused.
2. A specific binding (comets, asteroids, nebula, EC material) is a world claim with its own evidence and provenance. It is neither licensed nor forbidden by the framework. It cannot be upgraded to `grounded` by this folder.
3. The one place the framework does decide is the form of the question: it is a union, not a partition, and the present reading cannot name its route. That is a figure verdict, stated before any world source in `01` and `09`.
