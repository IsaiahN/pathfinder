## RESIDUAL

The question asks for an origin, which is a production claim, but the easy reading of an origin story is a list of stages, which is a sequence claim. Two questions are owed: (1) which operator actually carries an origin, and (2) under Fig 12's definitions, what is the relation between water delivered by different routes: isomer, functional-group member, or simply the same substance reached by different paths?

## DERIVATION

### Q1: Does "shape, not a name" forbid naming a candidate source? CORRECTION TO MY OWN FIRST PASS

- The figures README: *"what a missing thing would have to look like (a shape, not a name)."* The subject is a missing primitive, one not held in the library.
- Nebular hydrogen, enstatite-like material, carbonaceous chondrite water, cometary ice and mantle water are all available in the world record. None is a missing primitive of the framework.
- ⇒ Naming one of them is **not** forbidden. It is a **binding** from the planetary frame onto an available reservoir. What is forbidden is inventing a seventh term, or claiming the binding moves the wall (Fig 6 :22: *"IMPORT adds an atom, and the added atom is what expands the closure."* This one adds none).
- **I overstated this in the first pass.** Narrowed and kept visible, as in `05` §5b. Triangulated (README + Fig 6).

### Q2: Is an origin a sequence (→) or a production (⇒)?

- Operators table :37: *"A produces B; B did not exist before."* :39: *"is there a B before A fires? No."*
- Operators table :31: *"A then B; B needs A to have happened"* for `→`, with the test *"swap them. Does it become a different thing? Yes."*
- An origin claim asserts that the water **did not exist in its present reservoir before the route fired**. That is `⇒`, not `→`. A stage list (nebula, planetesimals, impacts, mantle) is a recording of an ordering; it cannot by itself assert that anything was produced.
- Fig 12 :111: *"A vocabulary without bonds can name a state and cannot name a route to one."* The route needs the ordered, productive bond, not an ingredient list.
- Fig 12 :112: *"A plan is a sequence. A goal is a comparison. Progress is a subtraction. A refutation is a negation, and that one is not a bond."*
- **Verdict:** the origin of water is a production history. A stage list is a method of describing it, not the claim itself. `general` on the operator distinction, `specified` on its application here. **Falsifier:** show water already present and merely relocated, so no `⇒` fires, and the origin claim for that route is misnamed as pure `→`.

### Q3: Are the delivered waters isomers?

- Fig 12 :117: **"isomer. Same atoms, different bond, different substance. The difference is invisible in the formula and decisive in the behaviour."**
- Fig 13 :121: **"Different routes can arrive at the same substance."**
- Fig 13 :122-123: **"In chemistry this has been established since Liebig and Wöhler found two substances with the same composition and different properties, 1824 to 1826, and Berzelius named it isomerism in 1830."**
- Water from a comet and water from a chondrite parent body have the same formula, H2O, and overlapping isotope ratios. Their difference is the **production route**, which is the bond in the lineage, not a bond between atoms in the molecule. Fig 13 :127-129: **"Nothing about a composed term tells you, from looking at it, which route produced it or whether that route was available. The space has no last route and every turn begins from a remainder the last one left, so the order is not recoverable from the artefact. It is recoverable from the lineage."**
- ⇒ The literal molecular isomer claim is **refused**. The defensible claim is a **lineage isomer**: same substance, different route, difference carried by provenance. Fig 12 :95-96: **"Two entries with an identical ingredient set are not variants of one thing. They are different things whose notation cannot tell them apart, and the difference is carried entirely by the bond."**
- **Falsifier:** if the route were recoverable from the water molecule alone (a unique isotopic or structural marker per source), the lineage-isomer claim would collapse into an ordinary identity (`≡`) and the shape-not-a-name gap would close.

### Q4: Functional group rather than a single substance

- Fig 12 :123: **"functional group. A substructure whose presence fixes the outcome. The group says what kind; the qualifier says which member."**
- Each proposed source is a member of the group `source ⇒ water`: EC-like inner-disk hydrogen, CI/CM-like asteroidal material, cometary ice plus dust, nebular adsorption or magma-ocean dissolution, mantle storage and volcanic return. The group fixes "can deliver water"; the qualifier selects the member and its measured contribution.
- Russell et al. (2017) explicitly hold several members at once: incorporation during accretion, later asteroidal impacts, and a cometary contribution to noble gases. That is a functional group under Fig 12 :123, not a competition with one winner.

### Q5: What is the order of the union?

- Fig 3 :20: **"The chain is ordered by dependency, not by importance."** Fig 3 :19: **"Optimising a link that already works cannot rescue one that does not."**
- The dependency order of the union is: isotope vocabulary and what it affords, then the residual description and the objective (a discriminator), then the choice of frames and settlement. The chain breaks between vocabulary and objective when a reader has ratios but no term for what would count as a route. Fig 3 :27: **"A link with no instrument is not a link that works. It is a link nobody has looked at."**
- The geological order (disk chemistry, accretion, impacts, mantle processing) is a property of the habitat, not of the six-term substrate. Fig 13 :75: **"Two states never bond directly. It is not space-time but space, a structure, time; and two structures join only through a state."** Re-census: that rule governs the primitives, not the temporal order of delivery events. No strain after assignment.
- **Falsifier:** show that the framework selects one delivery order as the only composable one. It does not; Fig 13 permits many routes to one substance.

### Q6: The cycle that defeats a one-way origin story

- Fig 12 :121: **"cycle. The output re-enters as input. The sign of the loop decides whether it closes, and a chain cannot close by construction."**
- Water enters the mantle by subduction and returns by volcanism. Russell et al. (2017) note that *"the Earth's oceans are not a closed system; they interact with the atmosphere, and water is mixed into the mantle via subduction."* Alexander (2017) separates water from organics within the same chondrite, another loop.
- ⇒ A single linear chain from source to ocean is a recording of one pass. The real habitat is a cycle, and the origin residual is the current state of that loop, not its first term.
- **Falsifier:** show that terrestrial water is a one-pass system with no return flow, so the cycle structure is void here.

### Q7: Catalyst and spent parts

- Fig 12 :119: **"catalyst. A part that survives its own use. Spent and regenerating parts behave differently across uses, and nothing records which a term is."**
- The mantle and the disk are persistent processing reservoirs; an impactor is spent on delivery. Treating both as the same kind of "source" loses the distinction the figure prices. Russell et al. (2017) report ancient fracture fluids stable on planetary timescales, a maintained reservoir rather than a one-shot delivery.
- **Falsifier:** show that delivery reservoirs and processing reservoirs behave identically across uses. Then the catalyst distinction is idle here and is withdrawn.

## CONCLUSION

The origin of Earth's water is a `⇒` production history, not a `→` stage list. Over the shared substance H2O, the candidate routes are not literal molecular isomers; they are **lineage isomers**, same substance, different route, with the difference carried entirely by provenance. They are members of one functional group (`source ⇒ water`), and the union has a dependency order set by the missing discriminator, not by the order of geological events. A cycle returns water to the mantle, so a one-way origin chain is a recording of one pass, not the generator. Every one of these claims has a named falsifier above.

This framing rescues what is defensible in an origin question without selecting a source: the operator type, the union, the provenance condition and the cycle need no new primitive and no single-origin name. Which reservoir contributed how much stays a world claim with its own evidence.
