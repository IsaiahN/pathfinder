# Expert verification packet: what the Tether says about the origins of water on Earth

**What this is:** a claim in a form a planetary scientist, meteoriticist or cosmochemist can check. Mode,
derivation and refuters are stated before the conclusion, so a reader knows what to look for and what
would settle it. This is the packet to send, ahead of any framework material.

**Mode: `specified` on the per-domain verdicts, `general` on the structural shape, `gap` on the literal
single-origin list. Not `grounded`.** No delivery event was measured. This is a shape and a route, not
a result.

**Status of this packet:** a simulated derivation checked for internal consistency against the
figures, not a ground reading (see `05_what_shape_means.md` section 7). It cannot settle the scientific
question it reports on. No meteorite, comet or mantle sample was measured here.

---

## 1. The claim, in one paragraph

The present Earth gives us a reading, not a route. D/H, nitrogen, oxygen and noble-gas ratios in
ocean water, mantle plumes, meteorites and cometary samples can be measured precisely, but several
distinct source histories produce overlapping values. The reading cannot comment on its own source.
What the figures license is a shape: the origins of terrestrial water are a **union of production
histories** (nebular hydrogen on grains or in a magma ocean, enstatite-like inner-disk material,
CI/CM-like asteroidal material, cometary and interplanetary dust input, mantle storage and volcanic
return), with the contribution indexed per reservoir and with provenance attached to every sample and
model. No figure returns a single origin name. That absence is a declared `gap`, not silence and not
a claim that the question is unanswerable in principle. An origin is a production history, not a
stage list, and across the same water molecule the routes differ by lineage, not by chemistry.

## 2. Three checkable sub-claims

### C1: framework-internal; checkable by reading Figures 9 and 13

- **Statement:** the candidate sources form a union of overlapping conditions, not a partition with
  one winner. The literal single-origin answer is a `gap` of the shape-not-a-name kind.
- **Source, verbatim:** Fig 9 :55 "A bundled problem is usually a union of overlapping conditions, not a list of separate ones." Fig 13 :134 "Unreachable is a verdict here, not a silence," Fig 9 :32 "you get a kind of thing, never the answer itself"
- **What to check:** does the figure's union rule govern this residual, or is the origin question a
  partition with a single live condition? Read the residual description in `01_figure_grounded_audit.md`
  section 0 before deciding.
- **Refuter:** show a figure line that directly names one reservoir as the source of Earth's water,
  or produce an instrument that maps a present bulk reading uniquely to one route. Then the union
  collapses to a partition, the `gap` closes, and the claim is withdrawn.

### C2: measurement-facing; checkable against the isotope record

- **Statement:** a D/H value (or any single ratio) is the same number whichever route produced it.
  Contents do not carry provenance, so the record of where a sample or model came from is the only
  separator. More argument on one ratio is not the remedy; a multi-isotope discriminator is owed.
- **Source, verbatim:** Fig 8 :51 "Convergent derivation and adopted import look identical in the contents." Fig 8 :51-52 "Only the record of where each came from separates them, and the surplus lives only where the closures differ." Fig 6 :26 "The world does not gain primitives; a frame gains access. Composition explores what the operators reach; instruments extend what can be represented at all."
- **What to check:** the world-facing part is not from the figures. Meech (2019) writes that multiple
  reservoirs can have the same fingerprint and that multiple fingerprints are needed. Alexander
  (2017) writes that D/H alone is not sufficient to determine a formation distance. Russell,
  Ballentine and Grady (2017) rule out most comets as the source of most water by C, N and O
  isotopes while retaining a cometary contribution to the noble gases. Are those readings fair?
- **Refuter:** a measurement in which one route yields a signature that no other route can yield,
  with the discriminating quantity named and read on independently recovered samples. That would
  break the convergence and restore a name.

### C3: structural; checkable by reading the Operators table, Figures 12 and 13

- **Statement:** an origin is a production claim (`⇒`, B did not exist before), not a stage list
  (`→`, A then B). Over the same substance H2O, the candidate routes are **lineage isomers**: same
  substance, different route, with the difference carried by provenance rather than by the molecule.
  A cycle (subduction and volcanic return) makes a one-way delivery chain a recording of one pass.
- **Source, verbatim:** Operators table :37 "A produces B; B did not exist before." Fig 13 :121 "Different routes can arrive at the same substance." Fig 13 :128-129 "Nothing about a composed term tells you, from looking at it, which route produced it or whether that route was available." Fig 12 :117 "isomer. Same atoms, different bond, different substance. The difference is invisible in the formula and decisive in the behaviour." Fig 12 :121 "cycle. The output re-enters as input. The sign of the loop decides whether it closes, and a chain cannot close by construction."
- **Caveat, kept visible:** the figure's isomer definition is about typed bonds between operands.
  Here the "bond" is a production route in a lineage. This is a level-relative use, an analogue for
  provenance, not a claim that isotopologues are chemical isomers. It is recorded as `single-source`
  and strained, not upgraded. See `06_isomers_and_order.md` Q3 and `01_figure_grounded_audit.md` F6.
- **Refuter:** show that the route is recoverable from the water molecule alone (a unique marker per
  source), so the lineage collapses into identity (`≡`) and the gap closes; or show that the water
  was already present and merely relocated, so no `⇒` fires and the origin claim for that route is
  misnamed as pure `→`.

## 3. What is explicitly not claimed

- Not that water came from comets, asteroids, the solar nebula or enstatite chondrites as **the** one
  source. Those are world claims with contested contributions. The figures return no name.
- Not a single origin percentage and not an averaged global verdict. The residual is indexed per
  reservoir: ocean, deep mantle, plume samples, chondrite water, cometary water, adsorbed nebular
  hydrogen (Fig 1 :19, :22).
- Not that D/H alone selects a formation location. Multiple fingerprints are needed.
- Not that the six-term substrate governs an isotope ratio. That was a first-pass strain, dissolved
  by re-census: Figure 13 governs the primitives; the D/H comparison lives in the measurement frame.
- Not that naming a candidate reservoir is forbidden. It is a binding from the planetary frame onto
  an available reservoir, with its own provenance. What is forbidden is claiming the framework made
  the binding or that it moves the wall.
- Not `grounded`. No measurement at the 4.5 Ga delivery event exists; only its surviving record is read.
- Not a claim that this packet, or the derivation behind it, is a ground reading. It is a simulation.

## 5. What would settle it

A source-discriminating instrument, not a further argument on one ratio. Concretely:

1. A multi-isotope and noble-gas discriminator (H, N, O, Xe, Kr, Ne) tied to a dynamical disk model,
   applied to independently recovered samples, assigning reservoirs with a stated leftover residual.
   Meech (2019) and Alexander (2017) both say this is what is needed.
2. A bulk cometary measurement, not only the water fraction, matched to Earth's bulk H, N and noble
   gases. Alexander (2017) warns that measured cometary compositions in the figure are "probably not
   their bulk compositions".
3. A single source that reproduces all discriminating ratios and the mass budget with no leftover
   residual, which would collapse the union to a partition and fire refuter 1 in C1.
4. An instrument that maps a present reading uniquely to one route on independent samples, firing
   refuter 2 in C1 and closing the shape-not-a-name gap.
