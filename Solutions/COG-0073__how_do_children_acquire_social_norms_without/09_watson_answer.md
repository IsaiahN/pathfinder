## VERDICT (from the figures)

The shape survives; the name does not. A norm reaches a child as a head start across a generation membrane, grows in an enumerated habitat, is corrected against a convention that is constitutively about shared meaning, is tracked per slot, and is selected from variation inside the child. Caregiver, peer and institution must be low-overlap frames. An explicit rule makes a norm statable without installing it. The framework returns a shape, not a name, so "imitation," "theory of mind," "natural pedagogy" and "norm psychology" are bindings from the science frame, provenanced as such, and the mechanism slot is `gap`. This is `general` and `specified`, ungrounded, candidate. The strongest single point is canonical: the non-explicit channel is not a defect in the account but the documented form of a head start.

- **cost if wrong:** bounded and locatable. The packet states mode, refuters and provenance before the conclusion, so an error is findable rather than invisible. The real cost is spending the independence of outside readers by sending a name or a recording instead of a generator. Fig 2 :42 prices it: *"Buy independence in proportion to the cost of being wrong."* That cost is avoidable by leading with the shape claim and its refuter, and by marking the mechanism `gap`.
- **undoable:** yes. The mechanism name, if anyone adds one, is removable without touching the derivation. `07_the_tether_answer.md` and `08_expert_verification_packet.md` already withhold it. A sent shape can be superseded by a corrected packet, and no term has to be withdrawn from the record.
- **reversed by:** a ground reading, not agreement. A contact intervention that changes norm capability (Fig 11 :40), an explicit rule that installs a novel norm with no contact change (Fig 4 :22-26), a single isolated frame producing the full repertoire (Fig 2 :31), or a community practice that moves with the child's judgement (Fig 2 :33-34) would each break the corresponding shape claim. A source-discriminating instrument that separates imitation from pedagogy from reinforcement while holding contact fixed would close the `gap` by naming the source. Until one of these fires, the verdict stands as `candidate`.
