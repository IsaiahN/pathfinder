## Distribution: offer, do not push

Figure-grounded reasons:

- **Fig 4 :22-25**  -  *"Only methods cross up. Only head starts come down. A recording carried upward looks like knowledge and is a description of one occasion."* Sending the full framework before a reader has described the residual sends a recording, not a generator. Lead with the shape claim and the refuter; offer links after.
- **Fig 10 :42**  -  *"A verifier that can reconstruct the claim cannot verify it."* A recipient who has absorbed the framework before giving a verdict has joined the frame being checked.
- **Fig 9 :27-28**  -  *"describe it before you go looking, or any frame you pick will seem to fit."* Ask what the reader would expect to see if the shape claim were true before sending the article.
- **Fig 2 :40**  -  *"agreement about the world is worthless when the agreeing frames share an evidence pool"*; **Fig 7 :35**  -  *"A room's agreement is variation, not a verdict."* A send selected for people already likely to agree produces a convention, not corroboration.
- **Fig 11 :43**  -  *"The habitat is enumerated, never composed."* The first question to a researcher is what is actually in contact with this residual in the population they study, not whether they like the framework.

**Recommended order in a reply:** (1) the residual, shape claim and refuter; (2) ask what they would count as a failure and record it; (3) only then the three links, with the note that the figures, not the summary, are the authority.

## Legal and channel rule

Unsolicited bulk email to scraped addresses breaches GDPR and CAN-SPAM style rules in many jurisdictions and will get a sending domain blacklisted. Use only the public, opt-in channels in `03_public_contact_points.csv`. One to one, no automation, no misleading subject lines, and an opt-out line in every message. Nothing is sent by this agent: hosting and sending is the operator's job. Prefer a download or submission page that lets a reader decline contact over an attachment pushed to a list.
