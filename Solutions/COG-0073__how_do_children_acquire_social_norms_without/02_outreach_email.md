# Outreach draft  -  check the shape before you decide about the theory

**Use:** one to one, or as a cover letter attached to a submission through the public venues in `03_public_contact_points.csv`. **Not** for bulk send.

**Hard precondition:** do not send until a reader can answer stage 1 without seeing the framework. If the shape claim cannot be checked on its own, the two-stage offer collapses on contact.

**Subject:** One figure-grounded shape claim about implicit norm acquisition, with its refuter

> Hi [name],
>
> I am writing about a narrow output of a figure-grounded derivation, not about the theory that produced it. I am not asking you to read the theory first, and I am not asking you to agree with the output. If the output does not survive, there is nothing else to look at.
>
> **The residual, in effect terms.** A child begins without the ambient regularities of turn-taking, sharing, personal space, politeness and obligation, and later behaves in line with them, with no rule sentence handed over as the cause. The gap is between the child's behaviour and the community's practice, indexed per context. The question is what closes it.
>
> **The shape claim (`general` on the structure, `specified` on one prediction).** The absence of explicit instruction is not a hole in the account. It is the required shape of transmission across a generation boundary. What crosses into the next child is a head start, never a recording of a particular success: *"Only methods cross up. Only head starts come down."* A seed carries no small tree; the conditions it lands in decide what it becomes. So a norm is grown by contact with an enumerated habitat (caregivers, siblings, peers, artifacts, institutions), not installed in an isolated child. Capability is a property of agent and habitat, never of the agent alone. A convention is settled by the community's actual practice, which does not move because of what the child thinks; agreement about shared meaning is the product here, not a corruption. Behaviour is tracked per slot, not as one global "has norms" score. I also predict that a single isolated frame cannot supply the full novel-context repertoire: caregiver, peer and institution must be low-overlap frames.
>
> **What I am not claiming.** I am not naming a cognitive mechanism. The framework returns a kind of thing, never the answer, so "imitation," "theory of mind," "natural pedagogy" and "norm psychology" are bindings from your field, recorded as such, not results of the derivation. I am not claiming explicit teaching never happens: an explicit rule makes a norm statable, but a declared meaning is not a grounded one. Nothing here is `grounded`, and this is a simulated derivation checked for internal consistency, not a measurement.
>
> **What would refute the shape claim (written down before this message):**
> 1. a norm installed as an intact explicit rule, with no change in habitat contact, that produces correct behaviour in a novel case the rule never covered;
> 2. identical contact producing different norm capability accounted for only by a change inside the child that did not alter contact;
> 3. one frame, isolated from peers and institutions, producing the full novel-context repertoire;
> 4. the community's practice changing because of what the child thinks, so the anchor updates and the group drifts coherently.
>
> If any holds, I will retract the claim. A one-line "this fails because..." is more useful to me than agreement. If you would rather not engage, no reply is needed.
>
> **If it survives, and only then:** here are the sources. The figures are the authority, not my summary of them.
> - Canonical figures text with line numbers (local extract): `FRAMEWORK/reference/03-FIGURES_TEXT.md`
> - Figures and formula for direct download, branch `general-agent`: https://github.com/IsaiahN/tether/tree/general-agent/figures
> - Worked architecture and the shape-not-a-name limit: https://medium.com/@IsaiahNwukor/arrival-of-the-fittest-the-tether-architecture-1de7edf641ec
>
> **If you want to use the machinery:** the formula is in the figures link. Run it on a norm-acquisition problem you know well and can check against a ground. Write down what would refute your result before you run it, and label the mode of what you get. A reported failure is more useful to me than a reported success.

## Two-stage offer, staged explicitly

1. **Stage 1.** Send the residual, the shape claim and the four refuters. Ask what the reader would count as a failure. Record their verdict before any theory is shown.
2. **Stage 2, opt in only.** If they are curious, offer the three links with the note that the figures, not the summary, are the authority. Their later agreement is never pooled as corroboration (Fig 2 :40  -  *"agreement about the world is worthless when the agreeing frames share an evidence pool"*).
3. **Stage 3.** Offer the formula as a method to reuse, with the instruction that each reader writes their own refuter and states their own mode.

**Hard precondition:** stages 1 to 3 only work if stage 1 survives. This packet is a simulation, not a ground reading. It cannot settle the question it reports on.
