## Independent domain census

I redid the census from the residual line alone, using the WATSON order: R by arity, symmetry and scale; one line per domain with actors / currency / anchor; partition or union; order by dependency.

Arity: a vector of norm slots, not a scalar. Symmetry: one-way at acquisition, community to child; recursive at the population level because the acquired child becomes a carrier. Scale: one agent and generations, so a membrane is in play. Fig 1 :19 indexes the residual per object slot, not in aggregate.
- **Domain 1, the child as one agent.** Actors: the child, perception, action, a per-slot residual. Currency: prediction against observed reaction. Anchor: the community's practice, which does not move when the child changes its mind. Type: partition boundary within one life. Order: terms for what a social situation affords before any plan (Fig 3).
- **Domain 2, the social habitat.** Actors: caregivers, siblings, peers, artifacts, institutions. Currency: contact and influence. Anchor: the norm as a convention. Type: a **union** of overlapping conditions, so unbundle before search (Fig 9).
- **Domain 3, the generation membrane.** Actors: adults and arriving children. Currency: what passes between sizes. Anchor: the ground and the round-trip gap. Type: a filter with a direction, not a wall.
- **Domain 4, verification.** Actors: this seat and outside readers. Currency: residuals and agreement. Anchor: the ground, never agreement. Independence in this run is low because we share the pool.

Order by dependency: membrane first (what the child starts with), habitat second (who is in contact), child loop third, verification last. Refuse the mechanism name before minting it (Formula step 2); describe the residual before picking a frame (Fig 9 :27-28).
