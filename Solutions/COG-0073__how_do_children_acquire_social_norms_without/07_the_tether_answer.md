# The Tether's answer: how children acquire social norms without explicit instruction

**Mode: `general` on the structural shape, `specified` on the falsifiable prediction, `gap` on the name of the mechanism. Not `grounded`.** This is a simulated derivation checked against the figures (`05_what_shape_means.md` §6), not a ground reading.

## 1. The residual it answers

A child starts without the ambient regularities of turn-taking, sharing, personal space, politeness and obligation, and later behaves in line with them without a rule sentence being the cause. The residual is the gap between the child's behaviour and the community's practice, indexed per slot. It is a union of conditions (family, peers, institutions), not one hard question. Fig 9 :54: *"And when the split lands on a union rather than a partition, unbundle before you search again."*

## 2. The shape, per domain, verdicts kept separate

**D3, the intergenerational membrane.** What crosses into the child is a head start, not a recording. Fig 4 :22: **"Only methods cross up. Only head starts come down."** Fig 4 :34: **"A seed carries no small tree: it carries what will grow one, and the conditions it lands in decide what it becomes."** A rule replayed would produce *"a system that repeats a past success and cannot produce a new one"* (Fig 4 :26). So the absence of explicit instruction is the expected transmission shape, not a missing piece. Verdict: `general`, triangulated (Fig 4 plus Fig 11, low overlap).

**D2, the social habitat.** The answer has to be an enumeration of contact, not a list of innate modules. Fig 11 :39: **"capability is a property of agent-and-habitat, never of the agent alone"**; Fig 11 :43: **"The habitat is enumerated, never composed."** Caregivers, siblings, peers, artifacts and institutions are the actors; influence and reaction are the currency; the norm as a convention is the anchor. The seat can introduce but not subtract (Fig 11 :61: *"The seat's power is asymmetric. Introduction is available; exclusion is not."*). Verdict: `general`, triangulated (Fig 11 plus two independent developmental reviews).

**D1, the child's loop.** Behaviour is tracked per slot. Fig 1 :19: **"The residual is indexed by object slot, not aggregated."** Fig 1 :22: **"Aggregating across slots is how a live signal disappears into an average."** The child ranges its behaviour against the community's practice, which does not move because of what the child thinks: Fig 2 :45, **"Frames range against the ground. They do not negotiate with it."** Because the object of study is a convention, agreement is the product: Fig 2 :41, **"efficient for a convention: agreement about a shared meaning is the product, not a corruption of it."** Verdict: `general` for the ranging and convention reading, `specified` for the low-overlap-frames prediction below.

**D4, verification.** One frame is not enough. Fig 2 :31: **"Independence, between two frames, is how little they draw on the same evidence."** Fig 8 :48: **"How much two frames draw on the same evidence decides both whether their agreement means anything and whether their union has any surplus."** Caregiver, peer and institution must differ in closure; if they all deliver the same lesson they are one frame wearing three names. Verdict: `specified`, falsifiable.

**The order and the bond.** The head start produces the specific convention (production, ⇒), as derived in `06`. The norm persists as a catalyst and re-enters as a cycle (Fig 12 :119, :121). An explicit rule is cheap to state while the contact that settles it is expensive: Fig 12 :126, **"Bits price the description. Actions price finding out whether it holds."** Verdict: `general` structural, with the generic-versus-specific strain recorded in `06` Q1.

**The mechanism name.** `gap`. Fig 9 :32: **"you get a kind of thing, never the answer itself."** The figures return a shape, not a name. Imitation, theory of mind, natural pedagogy and norm psychology are bindings from the science frame (Fig 8 :50-52), and the literature conflicts about them. Verdict: `gap`, unreachable at this budget and by this instrument, not silence.

## 3. What it explicitly does not say

- Not that children are blank slates. The framework's own author writes *"The child is a lineage."* The head start is inherited; the blank slate was *"a half-truth."*
- Not that explicit teaching never happens. A rule *"makes the bug statable"* (Fig 10 :17), but *"a declared meaning is not a grounded one"* (Fig 10 :18).
- Not that any one cognitive mechanism is the answer. That is the `gap` above, and a world source's name for it is a binding, not a derivation.
- Not that all cultures acquire norms the same way. The shape is claimed universal at the structural level; its content and the balance of observation, participation and teaching vary by population in the world literature.
- Not `grounded`. No measurement in this packet settles left(R,φ).

## 4. What would settle it  -  the ground

The ground here is an instrument that discriminates among mechanisms while holding contact fixed, or a controlled contact change that moves capability. Concretely:

1. **Contact test:** hold the child and the head start fixed, change only the habitat contact, and see whether norm capability moves. Fig 11 :40 predicts it does; a null result with a real contact change would break the capability claim.
2. **Recording test:** deliver an explicit rule with no habitat change and test a novel case the rule did not cover. Fig 4 :22-26 predicts failure; success would break F1.
3. **Single-frame test:** isolate one frame from peers and institutions and test the full novel-context repertoire. Fig 2 :31 and Fig 8 :48 predict failure.
4. **Anchor test:** make the community's practice move because of what the child thinks. If it moves, it was not an anchor (Fig 2 :33-34) and the convention reading collapses.
5. **Mechanism test:** an instrument that separates imitation from pedagogy from reinforcement while contact is held fixed would close the F8 `gap` by naming the source instead of reading it off the behaviour. From *The Hard Problem is About Source*: *"The answer is unreached until a better instrument exists"* and it was *"proven wrong not by argument, but by the arrival of a better instrument."*

## 5. The honest stop

**Unreached, not unreachable.** The world literature is real external evidence with low overlap with the framework, so it genuinely corroborates the shape. But it does not name the mechanism, it disagrees with itself about pedagogy, and this derivation is a simulation. The answer stops at the limit of what the available instruments distinguish and records the lineage so the stop is checkable. *"Indiscernibility is not identity."* No agreement among reviewers is a substitute for a ground.

## 6. One-paragraph version for outreach

> The Tether does not supply a named mechanism for norm acquisition, and it does not claim children are blank slates. It says that what crosses a generation into a child is a head start, not a replayed rule, and that the norm grows in an enumerated habitat of caregivers, peers and institutions: capability is agent-and-habitat, never the child alone. The child ranges its behaviour against a convention whose whole point is shared meaning, so agreement here is the product, not a corrupted verdict; behaviour is tracked per slot, and overlapping family, peer and institutional conditions form a compound rather than one averaged problem. An explicit rule makes a norm statable but does not install it, because a description is cheap while the contact that settles it costs actions. The framework refuses to name the cognitive mechanism, so "imitation" and "natural pedagogy" are recorded as bindings from the science frame, not results. What it offers is the shape any candidate answer must have, refuters written in advance, and the contact and mechanism tests that would close the open slot.
