## 1. A shape is a constraint, not a filler

The figures README states the limit: the framework returns *"what a missing thing would have to look like (a shape, not a name)"*. Fig 9 :32: *"you get a kind of thing, never the answer itself."* Fig 13 :134: *"Unreachable is a verdict here, not a silence."*

From *The Library of Babel*: *"You can cut your search short if the set of books returned is small and descriptive enough that it provides a general shape of the answer that satisfies your question."*

For this question a **shape** is the narrowed form of the transmission mechanism that can be stated before it can be named. It says what any answer must be like: a head start crossing a generation boundary, grown in an enumerated habitat, corrected against a convention, tracked per slot, selected from variation. It does not say "imitation" or "theory of mind." Those are names.

## 2. How a shape is obtained

- *Library of Babel*: use the vocabulary already held that is closest to the thing, correlate it with recurring new terms, recurse. *"With each new term gained, you narrow the space by definition and deepen your vocabulary."* The searcher's obstacle is not the size of the library: *"your ignorance, of not knowing what you don't know, is the actual obstacle between you and the answers, not the vastness of the library."*
- Fig 6 :30: *"outward: does another frame already hold it? The description travels. Fails by being sharpened into terms only we can read. Cast it in effect terms."* Fig 9 :30-31: *"your words are borrowed too, so describe the effect rather than the cause."*
- The exemplar's record of *Decoding Tacit Knowledge*: *"When contact isn't possible, the best you can send is a plain description of what you'd expect to see, because anyone can check that against the world."* This is why the outreach email sends the residual and the refuter, not the theory.
- *Library of Babel* on stopping: *"You will know that you are done because the finish line will also be the starting place"*, and the paradox: *"you have already composed the answer through the process of locating it."* Here the stopping shape is the head start and the habitat, with the mechanism name left open.

## 3. What licenses a name

Only two things, both outside the shape:

1. **The ground settles it.** Formula step 5: *"the ground settles it. Verified only by what pays. A gate is not the ground."* For this question the ground would be an instrument or experiment that discriminates among candidate mechanisms while holding contact fixed.
2. **A provenanced import from a frame whose closure differs** (Fig 6 :23, Fig 8 :50-52), with a source gate and a shadow test.

*The Hard Problem is About Source* gives the reason names from readings are unsafe: *"If several paths create the same behavior, then behavior is a projection with lossy information (it cannot define consciousness in full)."* The color instrument *"is speaking only about what it measured, it cannot comment on the source."* Several sources (imitation, pedagogy, reinforcement, ToM) could produce the same observed norm-conformant behaviour. Observing the behaviour does not name the source. And *"The answer is unreached until a better instrument exists"*; it was *"proven wrong not by argument, but by the arrival of a better instrument."*

So naming "imitation" from watching a child copy is the metamerism error. What is owed is a discriminating instrument, not a name.

## 4. Why this question is unusually exposed to the name error

*The Recursive Self-Improvement Trap* contains a sentence directly about this topic: *"The cognitive development I was thinking about was not actually a blank slate. Children had parents who taught them, nurtured them, corrected them. The blank slate was a half-truth. The child is a lineage."* The child is a lineage, which is exactly the membrane claim in Fig 4 and the capability claim in Fig 11. What crosses is a head start in a lineage, not a recording into a blank slate.

The same essay warns: *"the output is only as good as the validator. The agent, or the tool, or the frame, or the theory, cannot validate itself."* A figure-grounded shape about norm acquisition cannot certify its own mechanism name; the name must come from an outside frame and be provenanced.

## 5. Q for this question  -  is the mechanism name a shape or a name?

**ANSWERED: a name, therefore a `gap`.** The framework returns a head start and a habitat. It does not return the cognitive process that carries the head start. Csibra and Gergely's *natural pedagogy* and Heyes's *cultural pedagogy* are two candidate names from the science frame, and they conflict. Per WATSON, a conflict sends the derivation back to the census; the correct outcome here is the recorded `gap` in `01` F8, not a picked winner.

No correction is needed to the "shape, not a name" rule itself. In the QMGR exemplar that rule had to be narrowed because Gradient and Spectrum were already among the six primitives, so naming one was a binding onto a held atom. Here no figure primitive holds "imitation" or "pedagogy" at all, so the rule applies without narrowing: the slot is open and no figure fills it.

## 6. Two consequences for outreach

**Prestige is not action.** From *The Recursive Self-Improvement Trap*: *"Prestige can fund an action. It can give an action a budget to be tried. But it cannot settle an action. Only the ground can settle it. The Ground Maintainer must be blind to prestige."* A campaign that gathers well known developmental scientists to agree with the shape is the named collapse, not corroboration. The legitimate ask is the opposite: give the claim a budget to be tried and let the ground settle it. Note the special case of this question: the norm under study *is* a convention, so agreement among community members is the product (Fig 2 :41). That does not make agreement among the *reviewers* evidence.

**My own audit is a simulation, not a ground reading.** Same essay: *"LLMs can quickly simulate an approximate answer/output with the formula found in the architecture post."* and *"the truly testable output would need to be derived from the real architecture or working the formula yourself."* So `01` is a simulated derivation checked for internal consistency against the figures. It is not a test of the claim and must be labelled as such. It cannot settle anything it reports on.
