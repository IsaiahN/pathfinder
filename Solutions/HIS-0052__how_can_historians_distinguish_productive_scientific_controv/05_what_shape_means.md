## 1. A shape is a constraint, not a filler

The canonical limit, from the local figures README: "what a missing thing would have to look like (a shape, not a name)." Fig 9 :32: "you get a kind of thing, never the answer itself."

From *The Library of Babel*: "you can cut your search short if the set of books returned is small and descriptive enough that it provides a general shape of the answer that satisfies your question."

So a **shape** here is the narrowed form of an answer that can be stated before it can be assigned to a case. It constrains; it does not fill. For this question the shape is: *an anchor that does not update on the parties, separate ranging against it, refusals and a portable residue, with the claim-level and standing-level verdicts kept apart*. Saying "the X controversy was really about status" is a name and a case verdict. Saying "in this episode the invoked ground moved when pushed" is a shape claim that can be checked.

*How Intelligence Actually Works* gives the stop rule: "the recursion converges toward indiscernibility, and indiscernibility is not identity." And: "This process isn't about reaching the truth. It stops when nothing you have registers a difference from it." A discriminator that no longer registers a difference against the documentary record is the stop, not a proof that the episode is understood.

## 2. How the shape is obtained

- *Library of Babel*: "With each new term gained, you are able to narrow the space by definition and deepen your vocabulary." The historian's first vocabulary is effect terms (what moved, what was tested, what crossed out), not labels like "visionary" or "crank".
- Fig 6 :30: "The description travels. Fails by being sharpened into terms only we can read. Cast it in effect terms." Fig 9 :30 to :31: "your words are borrowed too, so describe the effect rather than the cause." So describe "the arbiter changed its ruling when the parties' standing changed", not "the arbiter was corrupt".
- *Decoding Tacit Knowledge*: "When contact isn't possible, the best you can send is a plain description of what you'd expect to see, because anyone can check that against the world."
- *Library of Babel* on the endpoint: "You will know that you are done because the finish line will also be the starting place, as there is nowhere else to search and nothing else that can describe the answer better."

## 3. What licenses a name, and why this question mostly refuses one

Two things, both outside the shape: the ground settles a minted term, or a provenanced import with a shadow test arrives from a frame whose closure differs (Fig 6 :23; Fig 8 :50).

*They're Scanning Books* sharpens the limit on imported vocabulary: "A minimum viable set of six terms is a human's careful guess at reality's compression axes; another sensorium's minimum set need not be those six." And: "Declaring the primitives does not make them neutral. It makes the choice visible." A historian's period categories and standards of evidence are declared primitives of that frame. Making them visible is the honest move; pretending they are nature's list is not.

*The Hard Problem is About Source* gives the error this question is most prone to: "If several paths create the same behavior, then behavior is a projection with lossy information". Reading a motive off a victory is the metamerism error. The essay's remedy is instrumental: "It was proven wrong not by argument, but by the arrival of a better instrument." For the historian the corresponding "better instrument" is a document or dataset that discriminates between the two trajectories (who cited whom, what changed when the arbiter was pushed, what method left the episode), not a sharper narrative.

## 4. Why agreement about a "productive dispute" is not a verdict

*How a Society of Agents Would Work*: "The group's agreement is never the verdict." And: "Ten copies watching the same thing are one opinion said ten times." A modern consensus that a past controversy was productive is a room's agreement. Fig 7 :35: "A room's agreement is variation, not a verdict. Selection between rooms is where the ground votes." The ground here is the documentary trace, not today's approval.

Fig 2 :41 adds the needed nuance: "efficient for a convention: agreement about a shared meaning is the product, not a corruption of it". When the dispute was partly about what counts as evidence, agreement may legitimately have produced a convention. The historian must unbundle that product from any claim about the world (Fig 9 :54).

## 5. Prestige is not action, applied to this question

*The Recursive Self-Improvement Trap*: "Prestige can fund an action. It can give an action a budget to be tried. But it cannot settle an action. Only the ground can settle it. The Ground Maintainer must be blind to prestige." So eminence buys the historian an audience and a hearing for a claim, never a vote on whether the claim is true. Fig 1 :27: "They are frame-internal. A frame cannot score itself with a quantity it also produces."

*Arrival of the Fittest* warns that the framework "cannot be validated, nor can it validate itself", and that its triangulation claim "fits four cases already known and has not been tested forward". Both are reasons to offer the discriminator and its refuter rather than a completed historiography.

## 6. Correction to my own §3 (and why the limit bites differently here)

I initially wrote that the shape-not-a-name rule forbids saying anything about a particular controversy. That overstates it. The README's phrase governs a **missing** thing, a primitive not held in Γ. Here nothing primitive is missing. The three discriminators are assembled from figure lines already held, and the "thing" being sought is a classification of an episode, which is a binding from the documentary frame onto held structure.

What remains forbidden is this: naming the source of a reading without an instrument that discriminates among sources. Calling a controversy "status-driven" because its winners were high-status is the metamerism error, not a shape claim. The correct form is the observable discriminator (which ground moved, which currency was paid, what crossed out). Both the original limit and this narrowing are kept visible so neither is quietly lost.

## 7. My audit is a simulation, not a ground reading

*The Recursive Self-Improvement Trap*: "the truly testable output would need to be derived from the real architecture or working the formula yourself." The outputs of a language model simulating the framework "must be seen as simulacra." So `01_figure_grounded_audit.md` and this file are simulated derivations checked for internal consistency against the figures. They are not a test of any historical case and cannot settle the question they report on.
