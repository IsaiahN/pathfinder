## What is deliberately not here

- No personal emails of named individuals.
- No ranking of people by how likely they are to agree that AI helps or harms human cognition.
- No bulk-send plan.

Three figure-grounded reasons:

1. **Agreement is not corroboration when the pool is shared.** Fig 2 :40: "disqualifying for a verdict: agreement about the world is worthless when the agreeing frames share an evidence pool." AI systems are trained on human output, so human and AI judgments usually draw on one pool.
2. **A room does not upgrade the label.** Seat Map :283: "A room does not upgrade the label. A room reaching a conclusion without the ground is agreement, and agreement among frames is a weighted average of influence. Adding people raises the vocabulary and does not add an anchor."
3. **A verifier must not hold the interiors.** Fig 10 :42: "A verifier that can reconstruct the claim cannot verify it." Send the shape claim and its refuter, not the framework.

Legally: public institutional submission portals and opt-in channels avoid the GDPR and CAN-SPAM style failures that attach to unsolicited bulk email. Nothing here is sent by this agent.
