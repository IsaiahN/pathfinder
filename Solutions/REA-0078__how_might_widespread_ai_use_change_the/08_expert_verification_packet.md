# Expert verification packet: AI use and human memory, expertise, decision-making

**What this is:** a claim in a form a researcher in AI effects on human expertise can check. Mode, derivation and refuters are stated before the conclusion, so a reader knows what to look for and what would settle it.

**Mode: `specified` on the three sub-claims, `general` on the shape, not `grounded`.** No longitudinal ground reading is claimed. This is a shape and a route, not a result.

**Standing:** a simulated derivation checked for internal consistency against the figures, not a ground reading. It cannot settle the question it reports on.

## 1. The claim, in one paragraph

Memory, expertise and decision-making are three slots with different anchors, not one effect size, and averaging them destroys the signal. Widespread AI use changes contact and what crosses the boundary, not an intrinsic human capacity in isolation. A recording of a past success replayed gives repetition; a method gives a new success. Capability lives in agent-and-habitat, so the direction of the expertise effect turns on closure overlap and provenance rather than on the tool: a high-overlap pair is one frame wearing two names and adds nothing, while a genuinely different closure creates a surplus. In decisions the AI is an instrument or a peer closure depending on what the question is constitutively about, and human and AI agreement is not corroboration when they share an evidence pool. The channel to the ground decays silently while internal metrics stay green. The framework offers the shape any answer must have, refuters written in advance, and the tests that would close it.

## 2. Three checkable sub-claims

### C1 - Memory: recording versus method, checkable by a transfer test

- **Statement:** an AI answer consumed as a head start can make the next search cheaper, but replayed as a recording it produces repetition of a past success rather than a new capability. A no-AI test is a *substituted* habitat, not the old habitat minus an actor.
- **Source, verbatim:** Fig 4 :22 "Only methods cross up. Only head starts come down." Fig 4 :25 "A recording carried upward looks like knowledge and is a description of one occasion." Fig 4 :26 "Replayed downward, it produces a system that repeats a past success and cannot produce a new one." Fig 11 :66 "Isolation is not removal of the habitat. It is substitution of one habitat for another." Fig 11 :70 "A test harness is a substituted habitat. Which is why a synthetic solve proves wiring and never capability."
- **What to check:** do a recording-led and a method-led group differ on unaided transfer to a novel task of the same class? Are the two silent failures stated? "what you failed to reproduce is invisible until the goal fails" (Fig 11 :68) and "what you unintentionally introduced is invisible until it acts" (Fig 11 :69).
- **Refuter (pre-registered):** the two groups are indistinguishable on that transfer test, or a recording-led group matches a method-trained control with the aid removed. Then C1 falls: the recording-method distinction did not bite in that slot.

### C2 - Expertise: agent-and-habitat plus closure overlap, checkable against study design

- **Statement:** improving the AI in isolation, with human contact held fixed, cannot move human capability. The sign of the AI effect depends on closure overlap and on the residual, not on the tool.
- **Source, verbatim:** Fig 11 :39 "And capability is a property of agent-and-habitat, never of the agent alone." Fig 11 :40 "A system cannot be made more capable by improving the agent in isolation. An improvement that does not change contact changes nothing, however much it improves." Fig 11 :49-50 "The same actor is a benefactor to one residual and a malfactor to another, so mutualism and parasitism are relations rather than kinds." Fig 8 :49 "Two frames that overlap almost completely are one frame wearing two names." Fig 8 :51-52 "Convergent derivation and adopted import look identical in the contents. Only the record of where each came from separates them, and the surplus lives only where the closures differ."
- **What to check:** does the study measure contact or only output quality? Was the overlap between the person's and the aid's evidence audited, or assumed? Does the paper report an interaction with prior knowledge instead of a single average? The external literature predicts exactly such an interaction (Tetzlaff et al. 2025, low prior knowledge d = 0.505 for high assistance; Brynjolfsson et al. 2025, novice gains and small expert quality declines). These are corroboration only.
- **Refuter (pre-registered):** an agent-only improvement that leaves contact unchanged moves the ground on unaided capability; the figure's own standing falsifier applies (Fig 11 :71-72 "an agent-only improvement that moved the ground would refute the capability claim"). Or a high-overlap pair produces a surplus that survives an independence audit. C2 is then withdrawn.

### C3 - Decision-making: instrument versus anchor and the shared pool, checkable against the decision task

- **Statement:** the AI is an instrument, not an anchor, for a decision it was not constituted for; human judgment is the legitimate anchor only when the question is constitutively about people. Agreement between human and AI is not corroboration when both draw on one evidence pool, and the channel to the ground decays silently.
- **Source, verbatim:** Fig 2 :36 "An anchor is legitimate when the question is constitutively about it." Fig 2 :37-38 "Human judgment is the right anchor for is this a good answer for a person, because the question is about people. It is only an instrument for is this code correct, and instruments can be wrong on axes they do not measure." Fig 2 :40 "disqualifying for a verdict: agreement about the world is worthless when the agreeing frames share an evidence pool." Fig 10 :24 "the ground does not decay, the channel does." Fig 10 :27-28 "the output is not a verdict, it is you have lost touch."
- **What to check:** what is the decision question about? Was independence between the human and the aid measured rather than assumed? Is the channel to the ground measured directly (seeded-error detection, unaided contact) rather than by self-reported confidence?
- **Refuter (pre-registered):** an independence audit shows the two parties drawing on different evidence pools on that task, and their agreement still predicts the ground better than either alone; or direct contact with the ground does not decline as AI use rises and seeded-error detection is unchanged. C3 is then narrowed or withdrawn.

## 3. What is explicitly not claimed

- Not that AI necessarily degrades memory, expertise or judgment. Valence is relative to the residual (Fig 11 :49-51); the sign is a measurement.
- Not that AI necessarily improves them. High overlap is the default, and a high-overlap union adds nothing.
- Not a single "human plus AI" score. Fig 1 :19 "The residual is indexed by object slot, not aggregated."
- Not that a no-AI test is a clean removal of the aid. It is a substituted habitat.
- Not that human oversight always helps. For technical questions human judgment is an instrument that can be wrong on axes it does not measure.
- Not `grounded`. Nothing here is asserted as settled by a longitudinal ground reading.
- Not a claim about a named person or a famous lab. Prestige is not action: "Prestige can fund an action. It can give an action a budget to be tried. But it cannot settle an action. Only the ground can settle it" (*The Recursive Self-Improvement Trap*, 2026).

## 5. What would settle it

A discriminating instrument, not a further argument. From *The Hard Problem is About Source*: a color instrument "cannot comment on the source"; the spectrometer resolved it. Here:

1. **Membrane test:** unaided transfer to a novel task, recording-led versus method-led, with the aid removed.
2. **Contact test:** agent-only improvement with human contact and habitat held fixed.
3. **Overlap audit:** source gate and shadow test before calling any AI contribution an import.
4. **Anchor test:** state what the decision is about, then identify which party the question is constitutively about.
5. **Channel test:** direct longitudinal contact and seeded-error detection, not confidence ratings.
6. **Disaggregation test:** the three slots reported separately before any composite.
