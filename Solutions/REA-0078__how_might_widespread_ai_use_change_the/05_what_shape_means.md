## 1. A shape is a constraint, not a filler

The figures README states the limit: the framework returns *"what a missing thing would have to look like (a shape, not a name)."* Fig 9 :32: "you get a kind of thing, never the answer itself." Fig 13 :134-135: "Unreachable is a verdict here, not a silence" and "It cannot say which primitive is missing, because naming it would mean already holding it."

From *The Library of Babel*: "You can cut your search short if the set of books returned is small and descriptive enough that it provides a general shape of the answer that satisfies your question."

So for this question a **shape** is the set of constraints any answer about AI's effects must satisfy, stated before the direction is known. It does not say "AI improves expertise" or "AI destroys memory". It says:

- the answer must be given **per slot**, with an anchor per slot (Fig 1 :19);
- it must be about **contact and what crosses**, not an isolated agent (Fig 11 :39-40);
- it must distinguish **recording from method** (Fig 4 :22-26);
- it must state **closure overlap** before asserting complementarity (Fig 8 :49-52);
- it must name what the question is **constitutively about** before calling human judgment an anchor or an instrument (Fig 2 :36-38).

`AI changes contact, with a per-slot flip condition` is a shape. `AI makes humans forgetful` is a name applied before the ground has settled it.

## 2. How a shape is obtained

- *Library of Babel*: use the vocabulary already held that is closest to the thing, then look for new terms that overlap or correlate with it. "With each new term gained, you are able to narrow the space by definition and deepen your vocabulary."
- Fig 9 :30-31: "your words are borrowed too, so describe the effect rather than the cause." Fig 6 :30: "The description travels. Fails by being sharpened into terms only we can read. Cast it in effect terms."
- *Decoding Tacit Knowledge*: "When contact isn't possible, the best you can send is a plain description of what you'd expect to see, because anyone can check that against the world." This is the reason the verification packet asks what a failure would look like rather than asking for endorsement.
- *Library of Babel* on stopping: "You will know that you are done because the finish line will also be the starting place, as there is nowhere else to search and nothing else that can describe the answer better." And the paradox: "by the time you learn the EXACT search terms to find the CORRECT book, you won't need to read it anymore, because you have already composed the answer through the process of locating it."
- *How Intelligence Actually Works*: "The recursion converges toward indiscernibility, and indiscernibility is not identity." A shape can become indistinguishable from an answer operationally without becoming identical to it, so the lineage is still owed.

For this question the vocabulary bootstrap is visible in the domain census: "recording", "method", "overlap", "channel", "weighting" are terms already held in the figures and the world literature. The residual narrowed the search to those five. No new primitive was invented.

## 3. What licenses a name

Only two things, both outside the shape itself:

1. **The ground settles it.** Formula step 5: "the ground settles it. Verified only by what pays." The README: the framework "stops at the limit of what the available instruments can distinguish, and records the lineage so the stop is honest." Here that means a longitudinal, per-slot study with a substituted habitat and an independence audit, not a cross-sectional survey of attitudes.
2. **A provenanced import from a frame whose closure differs** (Fig 6 :23, Fig 8 :50-52), with a source gate and a shadow test.

*They're Scanning Books* sharpens the second point: "The real import is not a new term in the existing space. It is a new dimension of the space." And: "A minimum viable set of six terms is a human's careful guess at reality's compression axes; another sensorium's minimum set need not be those six." Also: "Declaring the primitives does not make them neutral. It makes the choice visible."

The six terms are a visible guess, not nature's own list. So a figure-shaped answer to a question about human cognition is a deliberate binding of a general grammar onto a particular habitat, and the binding must be recorded as such.

## 4. The ground and the source problem

*The Hard Problem is About Source* gives the mechanism. A color instrument reads green and "cannot comment on the source": one green light, yellow plus blue, RGB, CMYK, or a scheme not yet discovered. The reading does not determine the source. The essay's version: "If several paths create the same behavior, then behavior is a projection with lossy information (it cannot define consciousness in full)." And: "It was proven wrong not by argument, but by the arrival of a better instrument."

Applied here: a fluent AI answer is a projection. Its quality does not tell you whether it came from a method the person can re-derive or from a recording of one occasion. Self-reported "I learned a lot from AI" is the same metamerism error: several sources give that reading. What is owed is a discriminating instrument (a novel task with the aid removed, plus provenance of what crossed), not a name.

*The Recursive Self-Improvement Trap*: "the output is only as good as the validator. The agent, or the tool, or the frame, or the theory, cannot validate itself." The same essay: "You may all agree and be wrong. You may set your clock to someone else's, who was setting your clock to yours, and be off by many deviations over time. You need ground truth to define where you stand."

## 5. Answer to the shape question

**What licenses a direction in this question? Nothing in the framework alone.** The framework returns the constraints above. A direction requires either a ground reading (per-slot measurement with a substituted habitat and an independence audit) or a provenanced import with a shadow test. Until one of those, the direction is the researcher's own contribution and must be recorded as such.

*Decoding Tacit Knowledge* explains why the distinction survives even with perfect text: "we can know more than we can tell" (Polanyi, *The Tacit Dimension*, 1966, as quoted in the essay). Writing "carries the context well and the subtext partly, but it almost never carries the pretext, the 'you had to be there' part." Expertise is the part that does not travel. The essay quotes Fig 4: "Only methods cross up. Only head starts come down." That is why the memory finding about remembering where to find information is not a paradox: the location travelled, the route did not.

*How a Society of Agents Would Work* states the habitat claim in plain terms: "capability is a property of agent-and-habitat, never of the agent alone", and "A newborn agent is made capable by the surroundings it is born into, and the shared knowledge store is part of those surroundings." Substitute "a person new to a field" for "a newborn agent" and the D2 census follows.

## 6. Consequences for contact

**Prestige is not action.** From *The Recursive Self-Improvement Trap*: "Prestige can fund an action. It can give an action a budget to be tried. But it cannot settle an action. Only the ground can settle it. The Ground Maintainer must be blind to prestige." A study cited because a famous lab ran it, or a critic dismissed because they are not famous, is the named collapse. The legitimate ask is the opposite: give the claim a budget to be tried and let the ground settle it.

**Aid and expertise are a joint property.** *How Intelligence Actually Works*: "It can be inherited or taught, so the recipient need not pay the bill themselves, but something, somewhere, already paid it." Widespread AI makes that inheritance cheap. The cost did not disappear; it moved to whoever or whatever paid it earlier. That is the honest shape of the "deskilling" worry: not that the person becomes empty, but that the bill for a capability was paid somewhere the person can no longer inspect.

**My own audit is a simulation, not a ground reading.** Same essay: "LLMs can quickly simulate an approximate answer/output with the formula found in the architecture post." And: "the truly testable output would need to be derived from the real architecture or working the formula yourself." The architecture essay adds that outputs "could never be verified if the solutions presented were not contrived to fit the formula." So `01` is a simulated derivation checked for internal consistency against the figures. It is **not** a test of the question and must be labelled as such. It cannot settle anything it reports on.
