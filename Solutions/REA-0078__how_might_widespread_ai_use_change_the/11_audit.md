## 1. Independent domain census

I redid the census from the residual line alone, before comparing with the primary's conclusions. WATSON order: R by arity, symmetry and scale; one line per domain with actors / currency / anchor; partition or union; order by dependency.

- **R (arity / symmetry / scale).** The residual is the gap between what a person predicts about their own capability and what happens when an AI output is permanently available. Arity three: memory, expertise, decision-making. That is a **union compound**, not a scale (Fig 9 :57: "a case in two conditions is not a harder case of either"), and the three must be indexed by slot (Fig 1 :19: "The residual is indexed by object slot, not aggregated."). Symmetry is broken: in one slot the aid is an instrument, in another it can be a peer closure. Scale: learner, agent-and-habitat, checking arrangement.
- **Domain 1, memory.** Actors: learner, output, method. Currency: what crosses the membrane. Anchor: unaided novel transfer in a substituted habitat. Partition: recording versus method.
- **Domain 2, expertise.** Actors: practitioner, AI peer, problem habitat. Currency: capability in agent-and-habitat. Anchor: real problems and unaided transfer. Union where closures differ, one frame where they overlap.
- **Domain 3, decision-making.** Actors: judge, aid, ground. Currency: agreement versus residual; `w` between private history and inherited prior. Anchor: the ground; human judgment legitimate only where the question is constitutively about people. Partition by question type.
- **Order by dependency.** Refuse before mint (Formula step 2); settle the membrane crossing before judging capability; describe the residual before choosing a frame (Fig 9 :27-28); a settled molecule becomes an atom for the next level (Fig 12 :104).
