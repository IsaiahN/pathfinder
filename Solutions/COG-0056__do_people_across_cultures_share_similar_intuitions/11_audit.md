## Round trip gap R_T

The Symbols table defines R_T as the gap between what was sent up and what came back down, `gap(x, (T_E ∘ T_A)(x))`, measuring what the coarser description cannot hold. Fig 4 :30 requires it to be measured rather than assumed. I ran the round trip on the condensation this folder actually uses.

2. **Abstraction (T_A):** the shorthand in `07_the_tether_answer.md`: shared comparison structure plus relationship-indexed qualifier, per-slot, union not partition.
3. **Concretisation (T_E):** a concrete claim a reader might take away, for example "all cultures use fairness/cheating and care/harm as the shared foundations, with local thresholds".

Conversely, one thing survives cleanly: the residual is per relationship slot and the question is a union of a shared kind and a variable qualifier. That is the residue worth sending.
