# Expert verification packet: the Tether on the fundamental constants

**What this is:** a claim in a form a physicist, metrologist or philosopher of science can check. Mode, derivation and refuters are stated before the conclusion.

**Mode: `specified` on the structural verdicts, `general` on the shape, none `grounded`.** Simulated derivation checked for internal consistency against the figures, not a ground reading. It cannot settle the question it reports on.

## 1. The claim, in one paragraph

The values question and the invariance question are a union and must be reported separately. Inside the six-term generative set the numerical values are underivable, so the value half is a figure-level gap rather than a failure to search hard enough. The invariance half is a ground question: only dimensionless ratios can be tested for variation, invariance requires an anchor that does not change its answer because of what any frame thinks, and agreement between instruments sharing calibration models and reduction pipelines is one witness rather than two. Current measurements give no clear positive detection, while the tentative alpha dipole remains live because known systematics are comparable to the signal. Defining constants by fixed SI values is a convention, not a settlement. Four refuters are written below.

## 2. Three checkable sub-claims

### C1. Framework-internal; checkable by reading Figures 13, 6 and 8

- **Statement:** the numerical values are not derivable from the closed generative set; this is a gap verdict, not silence.
- **Source, verbatim:** Fig 13 :115 "The set is closed. The arrangements are not. That gap is why import exists." Also Fig 6 :21 "MINT composes inside the closure and can never add an atom." Also Fig 8 :39 "So a search that finds nothing is never a proof of absence."
- **Refuter:** derive a numerical value for a parameter from the six terms and seven bonds under a declared code, or derive one of the six from the other five (Fig 13 :41). Either breaks the gap verdict.

### C2. Measurement-facing; checkable by an astronomer or metrologist against the published literature

- **Statement:** an invariance claim needs an unmoved anchor and independent frames. A spatial dipole in a dimensionless ratio measured with shared wavelength calibration and shared reduction models does not by itself meet that condition.
- **Source, verbatim:** Fig 2 :29-30 "An anchor is whatever settles the question and does not change its answer because of what any frame thinks. A measurement, an experiment, a counter, a market, a patient outcome." Also Fig 2 :31-32 "Independence, between two frames, is how little they draw on the same evidence."
- **What to check:** does the claimed alpha dipole survive separate calibration (for example astrocombs rather than the standard lamp model) and separate modelling pipelines, with the common-cause alternative explicitly tested? Milakovic 2023 reports wavelength calibration as the dominant systematic; Wilczynska et al. 2015 analyses modelling errors; Bambi 2022 reports no clear observational evidence of variation. The figure rule predicts that shared-systematics agreement carries no verdict weight.
- **Refuter:** two genuinely low-overlap instruments return a trending residual in a dimensionless ratio that survives a common-cause test. That would refute the structural application and require revision of the invariance verdict.

### C3. Framework-internal plus metrology; checkable in ten minutes

- **Statement:** a declared constant is not a settled constant. SI-fixed values are conventions; the physics question concerns dimensionless ratios and their possible spacetime dependence.
- **Source, verbatim:** Fig 10 :49 "Declaring a meaning does not ground it." Also Fig 10 :18 "a declared meaning is not a grounded one."
- **What to check:** NIST publishes CODATA recommended 2022 values and a 2026 adjustment cycle with a data closing date; Uzan 2011 s2.1.2 states that only the variation of dimensionless constants can be measured. Is any step in the argument smuggling a change of units into a claim about the universe?
- **Refuter:** exhibit a variation in a dimensional constant with all dimensionless ratios fixed and the unit system held fixed. Under the standard metrological argument this is not possible; a demonstrated case would break C3.

## 3. What is explicitly not claimed

- Not a numerical value for alpha, G, Lambda or any other parameter.
- Not that constants are proved fixed; a current null report is not a theorem.
- Not that varying constants are impossible; the anchor rule governs measurement, not ontology (retraction R1 in `01`).
- Not that a new field has been derived; any such field is an import and owes provenance and a shadow test.
- Not a theory of everything, not a final frame, not `grounded`.
- Not a claim that a figure line settles a physics measurement. Figures decide the shape; the instrument decides the reading.

## 5. What would settle it

1. A low-overlap, source-discriminating measurement of a dimensionless ratio across space or epoch.
2. A derivation of a numerical value inside a larger closure with its own anchor.
3. An evolution law and new atom for any parameter shown to be a dynamical field.
4. A demonstration that the apparent variation is entirely a unit convention with no dimensionless residual, which would dissolve the question.
