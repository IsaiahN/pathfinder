## Residual, stated as an effect

Two questions are bundled in one sentence and must be split before either is searched (Fig 9 :54, :57). One asks where the numbers come from (why this value of alpha, of G, of the cosmological constant). The other asks whether those numbers are invariant under displacement in space and time. Option A (fixed) leads to a metric-coupled, equivalence-principled closed system with brute contingency in the parameters. Option B (varying) leads to a new dynamical field, a violation of local position invariance and of universality of free fall, and an instrument-level residual that could in principle be detected. The answer is bound in advance as an ordinary term: a settled value or a settled variation law, plus the instrument that discriminates the two. Without that binding, the search chases a preference rather than a term (THE_FORMULA.md, "Direction").
