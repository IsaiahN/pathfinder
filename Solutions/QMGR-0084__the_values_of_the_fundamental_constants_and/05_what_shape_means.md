## 1. A shape is a constraint you can state before you can name it

The canonical figures README states the limit: the framework returns "what a missing thing would have to look like (a shape, not a name)", then stops at the limit of the instruments and records the lineage so the stop is honest. "Indiscernibility is not identity." Fig 9 :32: "you get a kind of thing, never the answer itself". Fig 13 :134: "Unreachable is a verdict here, not a silence". Fig 13 :135: "It cannot say which primitive is missing, because naming it would mean already holding it."

From *The Library of Babel*: "This means that you can cut your search short if the set of books returned is small and descriptive enough that it provides a general shape of the answer that satisfies your question."

For this question a **shape** is therefore: a statement of what any acceptable answer must satisfy, without supplying the value or the name of the field. It has four constraints:

1. it separates the value question from the invariance question (Fig 9 :54, :57);
2. it says which dimensionless ratio is at issue and which instrument reads it (Fig 1 :19, per-slot residual);
3. it says what anchor would settle it (Fig 2 :29-30);
4. it says what would reverse it (Fig 13 :143-144, refutability as a design requirement).

A number such as "alpha equals 1/137" is a value, not a shape. A phrase such as "alpha is a function of a light scalar field" is a name, not a shape. The shape is: a dimensionless ratio, a ground that does not update, an independence condition between frames, and a stated refuter.

## 2. How the shape is obtained

- *The Library of Babel* describes bootstrapping by proximity: "With each new term gained, you are able to narrow the space by definition and deepen your vocabulary." The same essay names the stop: "you will know that you are done because the finish line will also be the starting place, as there is nowhere else to search and nothing else that can describe the answer better."
- Fig 6 :30: "outward: does another frame already hold it? The description travels. Fails by being sharpened into terms only we can read. Cast it in effect terms." Fig 9 :30-31: "your words are borrowed too, so describe the effect rather than the cause." We describe "a dimensionless ratio that fails to resolve across two independent instruments", not "the fine-structure constant is oscillating", because the latter already assumes the ontology.
- Fig 9 :26: "R's own predicates name the habitat that holds φ*". For this question the habitat is enumerated, not composed: clocks, combs, absorption systems, Oklo, meteorites, CMB, BBN, unit definitions. Fig 11 :43-44: "The habitat is enumerated, never composed. List everything in contact with the residual, then what is in contact with those, and outward until the cascade stops mattering."
- The question itself is the tether. *The Library of Babel*: "This anchor is provided by your own question: If the question is answered by your present collection of books from your search you know whether to stop or continue and which direction to turn next."

## 3. What would license an actual number or an actual field

Two things, both outside the shape itself.

1. **The ground settles it.** THE_FORMULA.md step 5: "the ground settles it. Verified only by what pays. A gate is not the ground." For this question: an instrument returning a dimensionless ratio that survives a common-cause test.
2. **A provenanced import from a frame whose closure differs.** Fig 6 :25-26: "An import adds no atom to the world. The world does not gain primitives; a frame gains access." Fig 8 :50-52 requires the record of where each claim came from, since convergent derivation and adopted import look identical in the contents.

*They're Scanning Books When They Should*: "The real import is not a new term in the existing space. It is a new dimension of the space." Also: "Declaring the primitives does not make them neutral. It makes the choice visible." A proposed varying field is a new dimension; it owes its provenance and its own ground. Uzan's later review says the same in physics language: a varying constant would have to be replaced by a dynamical field whose evolution law introduces new parameters.

## 4. The specific error this question invites: naming the source from a reading

*The Hard Problem is About Source* uses metamerism: an instrument reads green and "cannot comment on the source"; several physically different sources can give the same reading. "If several paths create the same behavior, then behavior is a projection with lossy information." The spectrometer, not an argument, resolved the case.

Applied here: a fitted shift in a quasar absorption spectrum is a projection. It is consistent with a change in alpha, with a wavelength-calibration distortion, with an incomplete absorption model, and with a combination of these. Naming the source as "varying alpha" from the reading alone repeats the metamerism error. Milakovic 2023 and Wilczynska et al. 2015 identify exactly these competing sources in the measurement literature. What is owed is a source-discriminating instrument, not an interpretation. Fig 6 :34-35: "The question is not whether a sensor could exist. It is whether anything, at any resolution, is already returning something that fails to resolve."

The same point arrives independently from human vision. Morimoto 2024 reports that "countless combinations of lighting and surfaces can yield the same responses from L-, M-, and S-cones. Thus, computing the illuminant color from the cone response to one surface is an ill-posed problem". Visual constancy is achieved with anchors and multiple cues, not by a single reading. Low overlap with physics, same shape.

## 5. Two consequences for this answer

**Prestige is not action.** *The Recursive Self-Improvement Trap*: "Prestige can fund an action. It can give an action a budget to be tried. But it cannot settle an action. Only the ground can settle it. The Ground Maintainer must be blind to prestige." A list of famous physicists who already believe in fixed constants is the named collapse, not corroboration. The legitimate ask is a budget to try the refuter against a ground.

**The audit is a simulation, not a ground reading.** Same essay: "LLMs can quickly simulate an approximate answer/output with the formula found in the architecture post. It will often be better than the generic outputs that you will usually get otherwise too (intelligence is essentially arrangement). However, the truly testable output would need to be derived from the real architecture or working the formula yourself." So `01_figure_grounded_audit.md` and this file are simulated derivations checked for internal consistency against the figures. They cannot settle the values question or the invariance question. Fig 2 :45: "Frames range against the ground. They do not negotiate with it."

## 6. Correction to my own section 1 (kept visible)

I first wrote that "the framework cannot name the missing primitive" forbids naming a candidate field such as a dilaton. That overstates the rule. The canonical phrase governs a primitive **not held in Γ**. A proposed dynamical field is an import from a physics frame, and imports are permitted when provenance and a shadow test are supplied (Fig 6, Fig 8). What remains forbidden is pretending the framework derived the field, or treating a declared SI convention as a settled value (Fig 10 :49: "Declaring a meaning does not ground it."). Both readings are recorded here so neither is quietly lost.
