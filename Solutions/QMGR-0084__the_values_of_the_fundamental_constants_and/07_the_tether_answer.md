# The Tether's answer on the constants and their variation

**Mode: `specified` on the structural verdicts, `general` on the shape, none `grounded`.** Simulated derivation checked against the figures (`05_what_shape_means.md` §5), not a ground reading.

## 1. The residual it answers

The question bundles two conditions. Fig 9 :57: "a case in two conditions is not a harder case of either". The residual is per constant, not global: Fig 1 :19 "The residual is indexed by object slot, not aggregated." A single averaged "are the constants constant" statistic would hide a live alpha slot behind a null G slot. The answer is a shape with an anchor and a refuter, not a number.

## 2. The per-domain verdicts, kept separate

1. **D4 convention.** Definitional fixity is not ground settlement. Fig 10 :49: "Declaring a meaning does not ground it." Only dimensionless ratios can be tested for variation. *Verdict: `general`, single-source for the figure rule, corroborated by metrology.*
2. **D1 ground.** An invariance claim needs an anchor that does not move when frames disagree. Fig 2 :29-30: "An anchor is whatever settles the question and does not change its answer because of what any frame thinks. A measurement, an experiment, a counter, a market, a patient outcome." Instruments are improved from worse instruments already returning something (Fig 6 :32). Present readings give no clear positive detection in the world literature. *Verdict: `specified`, triangulated between figures and the measurement literature.*
3. **D3 comparison.** Agreement between frames that share calibration models and reduction pipelines is one witness, not two. Fig 2 :31-32: "Independence, between two frames, is how little they draw on the same evidence. Two people reading the same report are not two frames; two instruments pointed at the same thing are." *Verdict: `general` structural, single-source from the figures, corroborated by a low-overlap cognition field.*
4. **D2 value.** The numerical values are not derivable from the six-term closed set: Fig 13 :115 "The set is closed. The arrangements are not. That gap is why import exists." Fig 13 :134: "Unreachable is a verdict here, not a silence". *Verdict: `specified` gap at figure level, triangulated across Fig 13, Fig 6 and Fig 8.* More search inside the closure will not close it; a new field would be an import with provenance and a shadow test, not a result of the loop.

The verdicts are not averaged. A figure-level gap in the value half does not weaken the ground-visible invariance half, and a null in the measurement half does not close the value half.

## 3. What it explicitly does not say

- Not a numerical value for alpha, G, Lambda or any other parameter. The framework returns a shape, not a name (Fig 9 :32).
- Not that constants are proved fixed. The world literature reports no clear positive detection, which is a current ground report, not a theorem.
- Not that varying constants are impossible. The anchor rule governs the measurement, not the ontology (retraction R1 in `01`).
- Not a derivation of a new field. A varying parameter would have to be imported from a physics frame with a source gate and a shadow test.
- Not grounded. No measurement was taken. This is a simulated derivation.

## 4. What would settle it, the ground

A source-discriminating instrument, not a further argument. The four refuters are stated in `01` §3. Concretely:

- A dimensionless ratio measured by two low-overlap instruments (separate calibration, separate reduction, different sky coverage or different system class) that trends with position or epoch and survives a common-cause test.
- A source-discriminating measurement in a quasar absorption system that distinguishes a genuine shift from a wavelength-calibration distortion and an incomplete absorption model. Fig 6 :34-35: "The question is not whether a sensor could exist. It is whether anything, at any resolution, is already returning something that fails to resolve."
- An evolution law and a new atom for any parameter shown to be a dynamical field, imported with provenance. Fig 13 :143-144: "Indivisibility and minimality are claims and both are refutable: derive a member from the other five, or derive one of the established laws without a member the table says it needs."
- For the value half, a derivation inside a larger closure with its own anchor. Until then the honest word is gap.

## 5. The honest stop

**Unreached, not unreachable.** The value half is underivable inside this closure; the invariance half is reachable only by sharpening instruments that already return unresolved residuals. The round trip from a local null bound to a cosmological claim is lossy and its gap R_T is enumerated qualitatively in `11_audit.md`, not assumed to be zero. Fig 4 :30: "So take the round trip and measure the gap. Without it you are navigating by dead reckoning: confident about a position nobody checked." Fig 9 :39: "So a search that finds nothing is never a proof of absence." The lineage is recorded so the stop is checkable. "Indiscernibility is not identity."
