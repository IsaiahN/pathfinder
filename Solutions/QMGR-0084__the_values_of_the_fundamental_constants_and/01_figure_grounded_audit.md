## 0. The claim under audit

> The values of the fundamental constants, and whether they are fixed or vary across the universe.

Stated in effect terms, this is a **union compound** of two different questions (Fig 9 :54, Fig 9 :57). It must be unbundled before a frame is chosen.

- **Value question.** Why do the parameters take these numbers rather than others? Candidate answers: brute contingency; a derivation from a deeper closed set; an anthropic selection effect. Each leads somewhere different. Brute contingency closes the question by convention. A deeper derivation owes new atoms. An anthropic answer owes a population of universes and a selection instrument.
- **Invariance question.** Are the dimensionless ratios of those parameters the same here and there, now and then? Candidate answers: fixed by metric coupling and local position invariance; varying through a new dynamical field; or the apparent variation is a shared systematic in the measuring frames. Each leads to a different instrument and a different revision of the constant list.

An answer would look like this, written down before the run as an ordinary term: a settled number or a settled variation law, with the anchor that settles it and the residual that would reverse it. As Fig 9 :26 puts it, "R's own predicates name the habitat that holds φ*". Without that written binding, the sought-for shape stays an invisible preference (THE_FORMULA.md, step 2 and "Direction").

## 2. Findings

### F1. The value question is a gap at figure level, and that is a verdict (D2)

Figure 13 :115: "The set is closed. The arrangements are not. That gap is why import exists." Figure 13 :116-118: "So a frame can search without end and still not arrive: infinite effort inside a finite reach." Figure 6 :21, quoted verbatim: "MINT composes inside the closure and can never add an atom." Figure 8 :39: "So a search that finds nothing is never a proof of absence."

No figure line returns a numerical value for alpha or G, and no figure line derives one of the six terms from the other five (Fig 13 :41: "Each is indivisible: no arrangement of the other five yields it."). Constants are not among the six terms and are not produced by the seven bonds. They are parameters of the models that compose those terms. A search over arrangements of a closed set cannot manufacture a number the set does not contain. Figure 13 :134: "Unreachable is a verdict here, not a silence". Figure 13 :135: "It cannot say which primitive is missing, because naming it would mean already holding it."

**Verdict D2: gap (figure level), specified, triangulated** across Fig 13 (closed set), Fig 6 (mint cannot add an atom) and Fig 8 (underivable is not the same as unfound). These three have low overlap: one is a substrate claim, one a closure claim, one a metaframe theorem. The gap concerns the derivation of values inside this closure, not whether the physical question is meaningful. World corroboration from Uzan and from the Stanford Encyclopedia of Philosophy (below) is secondary and shares no method with the figures.

Strain test, applied rather than smoothed: Figure 8 :40-43 says "Underivable-in-principle and unreachable-at-this-budget are different facts, and they issue different instructions." For the value question the correct classification is *underivable inside this closure*, not *unreachable at this budget*. More computing on the same six terms would not help. Importing an atom to get a number would be a new physical postulate, and Fig 8 prices a wrong atom above starting from primitives.

### F2. "Fixed or varying" is settled only by an anchor, never by agreement (D1 and D3)

Figure 2 :29-30: "An anchor is whatever settles the question and does not change its answer because of what any frame thinks. A measurement, an experiment, a counter, a market, a patient outcome." Symbols table, lines 102-106: "Whatever settles the question and does not change its answer because of what any frame thinks. It is not a frame and has no vantage to update from."

The constant itself is not the anchor. The constant is a term in Γ whose value is inferred through models; the anchor is the instrument reading and the measurement procedure that does not move when two frames disagree. **Re-census consequence:** a varying constant is not ruled out a priori by the anchor rule (see retraction R1). What is ruled out is a variation claim that cannot be ranged against an unmoved instrument.

Figure 2 :31-32: "Independence, between two frames, is how little they draw on the same evidence. Two people reading the same report are not two frames; two instruments pointed at the same thing are." Figure 2 :42: "Buy independence in proportion to the cost of being wrong." A claimed spatial dipole in alpha measured by instruments sharing wavelength-calibration models and reduction pipelines is closer to one frame with two names than to two witnesses. Figure 2 collapse 1 is "collapse 1: mutual update (coherent drift)"; collapse 2 is "collapse 2: common cause (hidden shared source)", elaborated at Fig 2 :18-21: "they never talk, and still agree so the agreement is the pool talking, not two frames confirming each other."

**Verdict D1: triangulated, specified.** Figures D1 (anchor, per-slot residual, instruments improved from worse instruments) plus the world measurement literature (Milakovic 2023; Wilczynska et al. 2015; Bambi 2022) point the same way with low method overlap: the ground decides and present readings do not return a clear detection. **Verdict D3: single-source for the structural rule** that shared evidence disqualifies a verdict (Fig 2), corroborated but not established by the cognition literature in `09_watson_answer.md`.

### F3. Instruments are extended from worse instruments already returning something (D1)

Figure 6 :32: "An instrument is not built from a description. It is improved from a worse instrument already returning something." Figure 6 :34-35: "The question is not whether a sensor could exist. It is whether anything, at any resolution, is already returning something that fails to resolve." Figure 3 :27: "A link with no instrument is not a link that works. It is a link nobody has looked at."

The real programme matches. Quasar absorption spectroscopy already returned unresolved shifts; laser frequency combs improved the wavelength calibration from that existing reading, and AI-based fitting was used to expose modelling assumptions (Milakovic 2023). That is INWARD in the formula's step 7, not an import of a new primitive. The reframing owed is "which residual fails to resolve in the present spectrograph", not "could a sensor in principle detect varying constants". Figure 4 :30: "So take the round trip and measure the gap. Without it you are navigating by dead reckoning: confident about a position nobody checked." A local null bound and a cosmological claim are not the same proposition; the round-trip gap R_T between them must be enumerated (see `11_audit.md`).

### F4. A declared constant is not a settled constant (D4)

Figure 10 :18: "a declared meaning is not a grounded one." Figure 10 :49: "Declaring a meaning does not ground it." The SI fixes the numerical values of c, h and e by definition; NIST lists the current CODATA recommended values and the 2026 adjustment cycle. Those are conventions for units, not ground readings about the universe. Only dimensionless ratios can be measured for variation (Uzan 2011, section 2.1.2). Figure 10 :47: "a convention that decides an outcome has stopped being one." The SI convention must not be allowed to decide the physics question.

**Verdict D4: single-source for the convention/ground distinction** (Fig 10), corroborated by the independent metrology field (NIST/CODATA, Uzan's metrology section). Definitional fixity is declared, not settled.

### F5. The invariance question presupposes a union, not a single lever

Fig 9 :54: "And when the split lands on a union rather than a partition, unbundle before you search again." The Library of Babel essay: "Perhaps your problem was a bundle or conflation of several problems masquerading as one? You will need to search all of those problems individually to find their answers and then combine them in a satisfactory way." A single lever that clears "constants might vary" leaves two intersections standing: the value of a given dimensionless ratio, and the independence of the frames that claim to measure its variation. They are separate compounds with separate grounds. Fig 9 :60: "Use filters for the budget and witnesses for the verdict. Never let a filter hand you a verdict." A tier, a crowd of agreeable readers, or an authoritative review is a filter for the budget, not a witness.
