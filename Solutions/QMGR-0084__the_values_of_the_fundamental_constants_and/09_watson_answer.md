## VERDICT (from the figures)

Split the question. The value half ends in a figure-level gap: the six-term set is closed, its arrangements are unbounded, and no figure line returns a numerical parameter; this is `specified`, triangulated, and underivable inside this closure rather than unreachable at this budget. The invariance half is `specified`: it requires a dimensionless ratio, an anchor that does not update, and independence between instruments; present sources report no clear detection while a dipole hint remains live because the systematics are comparable to the signal. The convention half is `general`: SI-fixed values are declared meanings, not ground readings. The three verdicts are not averaged. The whole package is a simulated derivation checked for internal consistency against the figures, not a ground reading.

- **cost if wrong:** bounded and locatable. Each half carries its own refuter and provenance, so an error is findable rather than invisible. The real cost is spending the independence of outside checkers by sending a recording or a numerical claim instead of a generator and a refuter. Fig 2 :42 "Buy independence in proportion to the cost of being wrong." That cost is avoidable by leading with the shape claim and the four refuters.
- **undoable:** yes. The convention and invariance verdicts can be revised by a single source-discriminating measurement without touching the value-gap derivation. A new field, if demonstrated, is added as a provenanced import; nothing in the six-term set has to be withdrawn. The figure-level gap would be closed only by a new closure with its own anchor.
- **reversed by:** a ground reading, not agreement. Two genuinely low-overlap instruments returning a trending dimensionless ratio that survives a common-cause test would reverse the invariance verdict; a derivation of a numerical value inside a larger closure would close the value gap; a demonstration that the apparent variation is a pure unit artefact would dissolve the invariance question into the convention half. Until one of these fires, every verdict stands as `candidate`.
