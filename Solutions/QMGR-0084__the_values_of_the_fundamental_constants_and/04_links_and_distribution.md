## What these sources do and do not establish

1. **The value half stays open in the literature.** Uzan 2011 and Uzan 2025 are the same author and one evidence pool, so their agreement is one witness. Uzan 2025 says that demonstrating a constant is varying would mean replacing it with a field whose dynamics introduce new parameters. That is an import in the figures' sense, not a derivation inside a closure.
2. **The invariance half is an instrument question.** Milakovic 2023 reports a tentative spatial dipole with wavelength calibration the dominant systematic; Wilczynska et al. 2015 reports an analysis of modelling errors; Bambi 2022 reports no clear observational evidence of variation. Fig 6 :32: "An instrument is not built from a description. It is improved from a worse instrument already returning something."
3. **Philosophy corroborates the gap, from a different method.** The Stanford Encyclopedia of Philosophy notes that under the standard view of physical possibility, "alternative physical laws and constants are physically impossible by the definition of physical possibility". The "why these values" question therefore lacks a physical probability measure without an additional population hypothesis.
4. **Cognition corroborates the anchor requirement, from a low-overlap field.** Morimoto 2024: "countless combinations of lighting and surfaces can yield the same responses from L-, M-, and S-cones. Thus, computing the illuminant color from the cone response to one surface is an ill-posed problem". Constancy is achieved by multiple cues and by references that act as anchors, not by reading a single signal. That is the same shape as Fig 2's independence and anchor rules, arrived at in visual science.

## Distribution: offer, do not push

- **Fig 10 :52:** "Bring a characterised residual, never the source." A recipient who has absorbed the whole framework before being asked for a verdict has joined the frame being checked.
- **Fig 4 :22:** "Only methods cross up. Only head starts come down." Send the generator (the residual, the shape and the refuter), not a recording of a conclusion.
- **Fig 9 :27-28:** "describe it before you go looking, or any frame you pick will seem to fit." Ask what they would expect to see if the claim were true before offering a frame.
- **Fig 2 :40:** agreement among frames sharing an evidence pool is disqualifying for a verdict. A mailout selected for people likely to agree would manufacture the pool that invalidates their agreement.

Recommended order in a reply: (1) the shape claim and its four refuters; (2) ask what they would count as a failure; (3) record their verdict; (4) only then offer these links, with the note that the figures and the published measurements, not this summary, are the authority.

## The two-stage offer, and the honest stop

Stage 1 is the residual and the refuter. Stage 2 is opt-in only: the sources, after a verdict is on record. Stage 3 is the formula as a method to reuse, with a request that each user write their own refuter and state their mode. Fig 4 :30: "So take the round trip and measure the gap. Without it you are navigating by dead reckoning: confident about a position nobody checked." The round-trip gap between a local null bound and a cosmological variation claim is enumerated in `11_audit.md`, not assumed to be zero.
