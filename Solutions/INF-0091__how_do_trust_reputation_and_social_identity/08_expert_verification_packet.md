# Expert verification packet - the Tether on trust, reputation, and identity

**What this is:** a claim in a form a social epistemologist, cognitive scientist, or information scientist can check. Mode, derivation and refuters are stated before the conclusion, so a reader knows what to look for and what would settle it.

**Mode: `specified` on the per-domain separators, `general` on the structural shape, not `grounded`.** No measurement was taken. Simulated derivation checked for internal consistency against the figures, not a ground reading.

## 1. The claim, in one paragraph

The question is a union of conditions, not one dial. Trust, reputation and social identity enter belief through the inherited weighting of the belief rather than through the observation, so they can change confidence without changing ground contact. Reputation is a metric only when it records contact with an anchor that does not update on the believing frame's own reports; otherwise it is a frame-internal quantity. Social identity is the legitimate anchor for conventions about belonging and shared meaning, and is structurally disqualified from settling truth, because the same agreement is efficient for a convention and worthless for a verdict. Repetition inside one closure compounds agreement and does not add evidence; only a differing closure can supply a new atom, and only a method with its provenance intact crosses as a generator rather than a recording. No figure line returns a universal coefficient, and that is a declared gap.

## 2. Three checkable sub-claims

### C1 - framework-internal; checkable by reading the Symbols table and Figure 1

- **Statement:** the three cues move the inherited side of belief, not the observation. Reputation accrued inside the believing frame is a frame-internal quantity and cannot serve as the ground metric.
- **Source, verbatim:** Symbols table :19-21: "What the system takes to be the case before it acts. Formed from its own history and from what it inherited, mixed by w. Not the observation." Fig 1 :26-27: "predicates minted, and credibility accrued" and "They are frame-internal. A frame cannot score itself with a quantity it also produces." Fig 1 :23: "The ground enters as the only metric."
- **Refuter:** show a cue changing belief by changing the observation term with ground contact held constant; or exhibit a reputation measure produced entirely inside the believing frame that still predicts out-of-sample ground readings. Either narrows C1.

### C2 - the domain split; checkable by anyone working on misinformation or expertise

- **Statement:** identity legitimately anchors questions of belonging and shared meaning and is disqualified from truth verdicts; the same agreement is efficient for a convention and disqualifying for a verdict. Repetition in one closure raises confidence without raising evidence.
- **Source, verbatim:** Fig 2 :36: "An anchor is legitimate when the question is constitutively about it." Fig 2 :39-41: "Correlation has valence." "disqualifying for a verdict: agreement about the world is worthless when the agreeing frames share an evidence pool" and "efficient for a convention: agreement about a shared meaning is the product, not a corruption of it." Fig 7 :12-13: "same closure, handed along; agreement compounds, evidence does not." Fig 7 :35: "A room's agreement is variation, not a verdict. Selection between rooms is where the ground votes."
- **What to check:** is the truth/convention split preserved in your data, or does identity move truth judgments in your paradigm? Does repeated exposure increase accuracy or only confidence?
- **Refuter:** show group identity changing the truth value of a ground-constitutive proposition; or show a closed group improving ground accuracy without any import from a differing closure. Either breaks C2.

### C3 - framework-internal derivation about crossing boundaries

- **Statement:** transmission that carries a recording produces repetition and not a generator; a claim crossing into a differing closure is an import with a source gate and a shadow test. Trust-as-method and trust-as-standing are isomers over the same atom set.
- **Source, verbatim:** Fig 4 :22-25: "Only methods cross up. Only head starts come down." "A recording carried upward looks like knowledge and is a description of one occasion." Fig 8 :48-49: "How much two frames draw on the same evidence decides both whether their agreement means anything and whether their union has any surplus. Two frames that overlap almost completely are one frame wearing two names." Fig 12 :117: "isomer. Same atoms, different bond, different substance. The difference is invisible in the formula and decisive in the behaviour."
- **Refuter:** show a transmitted claim whose receiver can reproduce the original ground contact from the message alone; or show the two trust cases using the same bond type with the same operands, which would make them one term rather than isomers.
- **Live strain, not hidden:** the world distinction between an echo chamber and an epistemic bubble (Nguyen 2020) is mapped onto figure structures as an analogue only. The figures do not mention either term.

## 3. What is explicitly not claimed

- Not a coefficient, a percentage, or a universal ranking of the three cues.
- Not that trust or identity is irrational. Each is an instrument for a different question.
- Not that credentials or reputation are useless. They pay when they track a non-updating anchor out of sample.
- Not a claim that all echo effects are the same, or that social identity is the identity operator (≡). That mapping is refused in `06`.
- Not `grounded`. Nothing here was settled by experiment, and the derivation is simulated.

## 5. What would settle it

1. A design that separates the weighting w from the observation o-prime and measures which one a cue moved.
2. An out-of-sample comparison of a ground-contact reputation record against a measure of in-group repetition.
3. A paradigm that varies identity while holding the ground fixed, checking whether the truth verdict moves with the group or with the ground.
4. A measurement of evidence-pool overlap between source and receiver, testing whether new atoms enter at low overlap.
