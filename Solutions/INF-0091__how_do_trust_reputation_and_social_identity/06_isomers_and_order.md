# Isomers, bonds, and order in the trust / belief question

**Mode: `specified` on the structural claims, `general` on the ordering, not `grounded`.** Simulated derivation checked against the figures, not a ground reading (see `05_what_shape_means.md` section 7).

## RESIDUAL

The primary audit separates trust-as-a-ground-contact-record from trust-as-accrued-standing, and separates an echo chamber from an epistemic bubble. Two questions are owed: (1) are the two trust cases **isomers** under Fig 12's definition, and (2) what is the **order** of the conditions, that is, which link is dependency-first and which bond holds between the cues?

## DOMAIN CENSUS (supporting derivation)

Atoms held for this question: a source, an audience, a claim, a history of past claims, and a ground that returns or withholds an outcome. Bonds available: the seven typed binary bonds plus negation. No new primitive is imported. The standing-versus-record distinction is a bond difference, not an atom difference.

## DERIVATION

### Q1 - Are trust-as-method and trust-as-standing isomers?

- Fig 12 :117: "isomer. Same atoms, different bond, different substance. The difference is invisible in the formula and decisive in the behaviour."
- Fig 12 :46: "A bond is the typed relation between two operands. There are seven, they are not interchangeable, and which one holds is not recoverable from the operands."
- As method, the relation source-to-claim is production (⇒): the receiver re-runs a check and a result comes into existence for them that was not there before. Operators table :36-37: "A produces B; B did not exist before."
- As standing, the relation source-to-claim is identity or comparison: the claim is taken as the source's claim (≡, "two names, one referent") or its standing is compared against a threshold (⋛). The receiver does not reproduce the check.
- Same atoms, different bond, different behaviour: one case produces a new check in the receiver, the other transfers a label. By Fig 12 :117 they are **isomers**, not degrees of one quantity. Over a shared atom set `{source, audience, claim, history, ground}` with different bonds, the isomer claim is well formed.
- **Falsifier, written before the run:** if the two cases can be written with the same bond type and the same operands, they are one term, not isomers, and the standing-versus-method distinction collapses into a degree. The claim would then be withdrawn.

### Q2 - What bond holds between the three cues?

- Fig 12 :92: "Symmetry is what decides whether an arrangement is a new thing."
- Trust and reputation can plausibly compose under conjunction (+): "both hold at once", symmetric. Swapping them does not change the meaning. Where they both bind, the case is a compound with its own loop, not a harder case of either (Fig 9 :57: "a case in two conditions is not a harder case of either").
- Identity and credibility compose under sequence (→) when the group's recognition comes before the source isheard as credible: Operators :29-31 "A then B; B needs A to have happened." Swap them and you get a different thing, so the bond is ordered and the order carries information.
- The standing that a claim acquires after propagation is a **cycle** (Fig 12 :121: "The output re-enters as input."): a claim is shared because it is credible, and it becomes credible because it is shared. Whether the loop closes depends on the sign. A chain cannot close by construction, so modelling this as a one-way chain loses the mechanism.
- **Where the amount of trust is not a quantity.** Fig 12 :125-126: "A composition costs in two currencies, and they do not add." "Bits price the description. Actions price finding out whether it holds." The description cost of a trusted claim and the action cost of checking it are different units. A system holding one accounting will not notice the shortfall. This is why "more trust" is not a single axis.

### Q3 - Echo chamber and epistemic bubble: a union, a mute union, and a mirror chain

- World reading (Nguyen 2020, opened): a bubble leaves voices out; a chamber actively discredits them. Fig 8 :19-21: "closures differ, so a surplus exists but no residual is described; nothing was aimed; the union is mute." An epistemic bubble is close to the undirected union: voices are absent, and no residual has been aimed at what they would have supplied.
- An echo chamber is close to Fig 7 :36: "A chain of mirrors is one long room. The next room must differ from the last, and match the residual it surfaced." Outside voices are heard and dismissed, so the closure is actively maintained.
- These are different structures requiring different interventions (Nguyen: mere exposure can shatter a bubble but may reinforce a chamber). The figure reason: a bubble lacks an atom; a chamber's channel to a differing closure has been saturated. Fig 10 :24: "the ground does not decay, the channel does."
- **Strain named:** the mapping from the world distinction to the figure distinction is an analogue, not a derivation. The figures do not mention bubbles or chambers. It is used for ordering only.

### Q4 - The identity bond as an analogue, refused as a derivation

- Operators table :25: "two names, one referent." This is ≡ (identity), symmetric, with the test "substitute one for the other. Is anything lost? No." (Operators :27).
- It is tempting to say social identity is the ≡ bond: the group and the member are two names for one referent. **Refused.** A social group is not a symbol with two names, and substituting one for the other does change things (the member can be wrong while the group persists). The bond is an analogue for the compression of difference inside a group, not a derivation of identity politics.
- Fig 8 :49: "Two frames that overlap almost completely are one frame wearing two names." This is the legitimate figure line for the compression claim. It governs overlap of evidence, not the identity operator. Using it as a derivation would be echo without shadow: a structure found elsewhere and given somewhere to live. Kept visible as a refused temptation.

### Q5 - What is the order of the union (dependency, not importance)?

- Fig 3 :20: "The chain is ordered by dependency, not by importance. Each link consumes what the one above produces, so effort spent above the break arrives at a step that cannot use it."
- Order: perceive the claim -> have terms for what a source affords -> state the objective separately for belief and for sharing -> route -> carry and update standing.
- The likely break is between "terms for what a source affords" and "what would count as progress," because Fig 3 :25-27 says that link "has neither, so it attracts the least attention and the fewest instruments" and "A link with no instrument is not a link that works. It is a link nobody has looked at." In this question: people can name "credible" and "one of us," but rarely have an instrument for "true independent of who said it."
- Fig 9 :60: "Use filters for the budget and witnesses for the verdict. Never let a filter hand you a verdict." Effect sizes and engagement metrics are filters for where to spend attention, not witnesses for whether a claim is true.

## CONCLUSION

Trust-as-method and trust-as-standing are **isomers over a shared atom set** with a stated falsifier. The bonds between cues are mixed: conjunction where both hold, sequence where group recognition precedes credibility, and a cycle where propagation feeds standing. Identity ≡ is a refused derivation, retained as an analogue for within-group compression only. The union's dependency order puts the objective link (what counts as truth versus what counts as belonging) first, and that is where the missing instrument lies. No bond type or order is claimed to be measured; this is a `specified` structural result.
