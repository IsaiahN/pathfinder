# The Tether's answer: trust, reputation, and social identity in belief and sharing

**Mode: `specified` on the per-domain separators, `general` on the structural shape, not `grounded`.** Simulated derivation checked for internal consistency against the figures (`05_what_shape_means.md` section 7), not a ground reading. Verdicts are kept per domain and never averaged.

## 1. The residual it answers

People believe and share the same claim differently depending on who said it, that source's standing, and their own group. The question asks how trust, reputation, and identity produce that difference. The residual is per claim and per receiver (Fig 1 :19: "The residual is indexed by object slot, not aggregated."). It is a union of conditions, not one dial (Fig 9 :54: "And when the split lands on a union rather than a partition, unbundle before you search again."), so one lever clears only the region where one condition holds and leaves the intersections standing.

## 2. The shape, domain by domain

**D1, the claim-and-ground domain (`specified`, triangulated).** The ground is the only metric that settles whether a claim is true. Fig 1 :23: "The ground enters as the only metric." The Symbols table defines the belief as "Formed from its own history and from what it inherited, mixed by w. Not the observation." So the three cues affect belief by moving the inherited prior, not by supplying the observation. A confidence change produced this way is not automatically a ground-accuracy change. That is the mechanism, stated as an effect.

**D2, the source-trust domain (`specified`, triangulated for the frame-internal charge; single-source for the instrument condition).** Reputation is a metric only when it is a record of contact with an anchor that does not update on the believing community's own reports. Fig 1 :26-27: "predicates minted, and credibility accrued" are "frame-internal. A frame cannot score itself with a quantity it also produces." Fig 10 :59-61 declares the stake: "an interest in the outcome is unavoidable, an interest in the reading is disqualifying." "And standing is a stake that survives the outcome it was earned on." A keeper of a reputation with an interest in the outcome is disqualified as its own verifier (Fig 10 :93).

Repetition inside one closure compounds agreement and adds no evidence (Fig 7 :12-13: "same closure, handed along; agreement compounds, evidence does not"). To add evidence, the next source must differ from the last and match the residual it surfaced (Fig 7 :36).

**D3, the social-identity domain (`specified`, triangulated).** Correlation has valence. Fig 2 :39-41: "disqualifying for a verdict: agreement about the world is worthless when the agreeing frames share an evidence pool" and "efficient for a convention: agreement about a shared meaning is the product, not a corruption of it." Identity is the legitimate anchor for "is this one of us / is this meaningful here" because Fig 2 :36 says "An anchor is legitimate when the question is constitutively about it." It is not an anchor for "is P true." The two verdicts are different quantities and cannot be averaged. No standing list of identity markers or trusted sources survives a change of claim (Fig 11 :51).

**D4, the propagation domain (`specified`, triangulated for the channel; single-source for the isomer reading).** What crosses a group boundary matters. Fig 4 :22-25: "Only methods cross up. Only head starts come down." "A recording carried upward looks like knowledge and is a description of one occasion." A slogan spreads as a recording and carries no generator. A method with its provenance intact can be re-run. Fig 8 :48-49: "How much two frames draw on the same evidence decides both whether their agreement means anything and whether their union has any surplus. Two frames that overlap almost completely are one frame wearing two names." High overlap gives the echo effect; low overlap gives a surplus, but only when a residual has been described and aimed (Fig 8 :19-21).

Trust-as-method and trust-as-standing are isomers over the same atoms, same atoms different bond (Fig 12 :117). The cycle in which a claim is credible because it is shared and shared because it is credible is a real bond structure (Fig 12 :121), with a falsifier in `06`.

**D5, the verification domain (`general` structural; the simulated seat has zero independence).** The checker ranges against the ground and does not negotiate with it (Fig 2 :45: "Frames range against the ground. They do not negotiate with it."). A verifier that already holds the framework cannot verify it (Fig 10 :42). In this folder all seats draw on one evidence pool, so their agreement is one pool talking, not a second witness.

## 3. What it explicitly does not say

- Not a coefficient. No figure line returns "trust raises belief by X," and no universal ranking of the three cues is derivable (Fig 9 :32 "you get a kind of thing, never the answer itself").
- Not that trust or identity is irrational. Each is a legitimate instrument for a different question. The error is using a convention's agreement as a truth verdict.
- Not that credentials or reputation are useless. They are instruments when they pay against a non-updating anchor out of sample.
- Not that all echo effects are the same. A bubble omits voices; a chamber discredits them; the remedies differ.
- Not a measurement. Nothing here is `grounded`, and a simulated derivation cannot settle the claim it reports on.

## 4. What would settle it - the ground

An instrument is owed, not a further argument. From *The Hard Problem is About Source* (carried in `05`): an instrument that reads green "cannot comment on the source"; the spectrometer resolved it. Here the discriminations needed are:

1. **Prior versus observation:** a design that separates the weighting w from the observation o-prime and measures whether a cue moved one or the other.
2. **Ground-contact record versus standing:** a reputation measure whose out-of-sample prediction of ground outcomes is compared with a measure of in-group repetition. If only the former predicts, F2 is confirmed.
3. **Truth versus convention:** a case where group identity is varied while the ground is held fixed, to see whether the truth verdict moves with the group or with the ground.
4. **Differing closure test:** a measurement of evidence-pool overlap between source and receiver, checking whether new ground-contact atoms enter when the overlap is low.
5. Standing falsifiers from the audit: a closed group improving ground accuracy with no import; a fixed invariant list of trustworthy sources; a cue that changes the observation with ground contact held fixed.

## 5. The honest stop

**Unreached, not unreachable.** Fig 13 says the system "can say the answer is out of reach with the library as it stands," and "Unreachable is a verdict here, not a silence,". What is unreached is the numerical share and the community-specific ranking. That needs an instrument that does not yet exist in this derivation. The answer stops at the limit of the available distinctions and records the lineage so the stop is checkable. "Indiscernibility is not identity."

## 6. One-paragraph version for outreach

> The Tether does not give a number for how much trust, reputation or identity move belief. It gives a separator. The three cues enter the belief through its inherited weighting rather than through the observation, so they can raise confidence without changing contact with the ground. Reputation is a real metric only when it records contact with an anchor that does not update on the community's own reports; otherwise it is a frame scoring itself, and the keeper of a reputation has a disqualifying stake. Social identity is the right anchor for questions of belonging and shared meaning and is structurally disqualified from deciding truth, because the same agreement is efficient for a convention and worthless for a verdict. Repetition inside one closure compounds agreement and does not add evidence; only a source from a differing closure can bring a new atom, and only a method with its provenance intact crosses as a generator rather than a recording. No standing list of trustworthy sources survives a change of claim. The coefficient and the per-community ranking are a declared gap until an instrument can separate the prior from the observation. What is offered is the shape, the refuters written in advance, and the measurements that would close it.
