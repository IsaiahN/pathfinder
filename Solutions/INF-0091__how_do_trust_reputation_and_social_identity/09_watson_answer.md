## QUESTION

Decide, in effect terms, what the Tether says about the claim that trust, reputation, and social identity affect whether information is believed or shared. Option A: treat the three cues as evidence about the claim and report a single effect size. Option B: treat the question as a union of conditions and return a per-domain separator with the failure conditions written in advance. What each leads to: A averages a prior shift with a ground contact and a convention with a truth verdict, and it cannot say where the signal is. B keeps each cue in its domain, states which anchor it ranges against, and leaves the coefficient as a declared gap until an instrument exists.

## DOMAIN CENSUS

R by arity, symmetry and scale, then one line per domain with actors, currency and anchor; partition or union; order by dependency.

- **R (the residual).** The difference between what a receiver believes or shares and what the ground returns for that claim. Arity: at least claim times source times receiver, so R is indexed per slot and never aggregated. Symmetry: believing and sharing are not symmetric operations. Scale: individual cognition, dyad, group, network, institution. Fig 1 :19 "The residual is indexed by object slot, not aggregated." Fig 1 :22 "Aggregating across slots is how a live signal disappears into an average."
- **D1, claim and ground.** Actors: proposition, evidence, instruments. Currency: the residual between prediction and observation. Anchor: the ground, which does not update on any frame's opinion. Nameable: yes.
- **D2, source trust.** Actors: speaker, audience, trust relation. Currency: credibility and reputation. Anchor: conditional. A verified predictive track record is a valid anchor; standing accrued from in-group repetition has none. This is a union of two conditions with different anchors.
- **D3, social identity and convention.** Actors: in-group, out-group, community standards. Currency: shared meaning, belonging, standing. Anchor: the group's judgment is legitimate for questions constitutively about people, and has no standing for truth questions. This is a convention domain, not an epistemic one.
- **D4, propagation.** Actors: routers, bridges, feeds, communities. Currency: attention and reachability. Anchor: the ground, reached through a channel that can decay while the ground does not.
- **D5, verification.** Actors: outside checker, anchor, seat. Currency: residual and provenance. Anchor: the ground, never agreement. In this simulated run the seat shares the author's evidence pool, so its independence is zero.
- **Partition or union.** Union of overlapping conditions, not a partition. Fig 9 :54 "And when the split lands on a union rather than a partition, unbundle before you search again." Fig 9 :57 "a case in two conditions is not a harder case of either."
- **Order by dependency.** Perceive the claim, gain terms for what a source affords, state the objective separately for belief and sharing, route, then carry and update standing. The middle link has no instrument in most of this literature. Fig 3 :27 "A link with no instrument is not a link that works. It is a link nobody has looked at."

Strain test applied: the claim that identity is a legitimate anchor for conventions (Fig 2 :36) and the claim that identity agreement is worthless for truth (Fig 2 :40) govern different questions, so there is no contradiction after re-census. One live analogue strain remains: the identity operator versus social identity, named in `06`.

## VERDICT (from the figures)

The separator survives; the demand for a single effect size does not. The three cues enter belief through the inherited weighting, reputation is a metric only when it pays against a non-updating anchor, identity legitimately settles conventions and is disqualified from truth, repetition inside one closure compounds agreement and adds no evidence, the surplus needs differing closures, and no standing list of trusted sources survives a change of claim. Trust-as-method and trust-as-standing are isomers over a shared atom set, a `specified` and unmeasured structural result. The coefficient and the per-community ranking are a declared `gap`, reported as unreached rather than as silence. This is `specified` on the domain separators, `general` on the shape, not `grounded`; the whole folder is a simulated derivation checked for internal consistency, not a ground reading.

- **cost if wrong:** bounded and locatable. Every separator carries its refuter, so an error is findable rather than invisible. The real cost is spending the independence of outside readers by sending a recording instead of a generator, which Fig 2 :42 prices: "Buy independence in proportion to the cost of being wrong." That cost is avoidable by leading with the separator and its refuters.
- **undoable:** yes. The gap and the analogue strain are removable without withdrawing the separator; `06` and `07` already narrow them. No primitive has to be given up, and a superseding packet can replace this one.
- **reversed by:** a ground reading, not agreement. A design that separates the weighting from the observation, an out-of-sample comparison of a reputation record against in-group repetition, a paradigm that varies identity while holding the ground fixed, or a measurement of overlap showing new atoms entering at low overlap would move a separator to `grounded` or break it. A figure re-census admitting a universal coefficient would close the gap. Until one of these fires, the verdict stands as candidate.
