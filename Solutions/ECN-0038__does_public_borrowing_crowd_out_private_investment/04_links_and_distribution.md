## 4. Distribution: offer, do not push

Figure-grounded reasons:

- **Fig 10 :42** - "A verifier that can reconstruct the claim cannot verify it." A recipient who has absorbed the framework before being asked for a stage-1 verdict has joined the frame being checked.
- **Fig 9 :27** - "describe it before you go looking, or any frame you pick will seem to fit." Ask what they would expect to see in each regime slot before giving them the theory.
- **Fig 2 :35** - "Alignment is triangulation, not negotiation. A mirror cannot correct you." Do not ask for agreement.
- **Fig 2 :40** - "agreement about the world is worthless when the agreeing frames share an evidence pool." Never pool later agreement as corroboration.
- **Fig 9 :60** - "Use filters for the budget and witnesses for the verdict. Never let a filter hand you a verdict." Prestige and citation count decide what to read first, not what is true.

**Recommended order in a reply:** (1) the regime-indexed shape claim and its three refuters; (2) the question "what would you count as a failure, and in which slot?"; (3) only then the three public links, with the note that the figures, not the summary, are the authority.

## 5. The two-stage offer

**Stage 1.** Send the shape claim, the regime index and the pre-registered refuter. Ask for a verdict on that alone. Record it before any framework material is shown.

**Stage 2, opt-in only.** If they are curious, offer the figure text, the canonical README and the formula. Their later agreement is a conversation, never corroboration.

**Stage 3.** Offer the loop as a method to reuse. Each user writes their own refuter and states their own mode, because a frame cannot check the direction of its own question.
