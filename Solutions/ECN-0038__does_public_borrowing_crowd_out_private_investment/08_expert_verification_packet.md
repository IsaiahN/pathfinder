# Expert verification packet - the Tether on public borrowing and private investment

**What this is:** a claim in a form an economist can check. Mode, derivation and refuters are stated before the conclusion, so a reader knows what to look for and what would settle it.

**Mode: `specified` on the shape, `general` on the structural rule. Not `grounded`.** No dataset was opened and no estimate was recomputed for this packet. It is a simulated derivation checked for internal consistency against the figures, not a ground reading, and it cannot settle the question it reports on.

## 1. The claim, in one paragraph

The response of private investment to deficit-financed public borrowing is a regime-indexed sign and magnitude, not a single number. The residual must be read per economic state (full employment, slack, bound policy rate, normal policy, open versus closed economy, high versus low debt), because averaging across slots discards the signal that says where the effect lives. Two routes share almost the same ingredients and differ in the bond: a cost-of-credit route and an income-and-demand route. The sign of the response flips with the regime, and the crossing is a threshold comparison, not a smooth average. The framework returns this shape and the refuters; it does not supply a magnitude.

## 2. Three checkable sub-claims

### C1 - framework-internal; checkable by reading Figure 1

- **Statement:** the residual is per slot, so no single sign governs all regimes.
- **Source, verbatim:** Fig 1 :19 "The residual is indexed by object slot, not aggregated." Fig 1 :22 "Aggregating across slots is how a live signal disappears into an average." Fig 1 :13 "a global R near zero with one live slot residual is a legal state."
- **Refuter:** an identified reading returns the same sign and statistically indistinguishable magnitude in every enumerated slot; or a derivation shows that averaging preserves the live signal that these lines say is lost.

### C2 - economics-facing; checkable against the two routes

- **Statement:** the credit route and the income route are distinct bonds over a nearly shared atom set, and the borrowing's valence changes with the regime.
- **Source, verbatim:** Fig 12 :111 "A vocabulary without bonds can name a state and cannot name a route to one." Fig 11 :50 "The same actor is a benefactor to one residual and a malfactor to another, so mutualism and parasitism are relations rather than kinds."
- **What to check:** is the credit route (borrowing, cost of credit, investment) distinguishable from the income route (spending, income, capacity utilisation, investment) in the data? At capacity or with an offsetting central bank, does the credit route dominate? In slack with a bound policy rate, does the income route dominate? The opened Fiscal multiplier page reports an IMF admission that multipliers were "in the 0.9 to 1.7 range since the Great Recession" under "substantial economic slack, monetary policy constrained by the zero lower bound", which is a reading inside one slot, not a universal law.
- **Refuter:** a single bond reproduces both routes without loss (for example, the interest rate carries no independent information once income is conditioned on); or a reading shows the same sign and route in every regime slot.

### C3 - framework-internal derivation with an empirical hook

- **Statement:** the question is a union compound that must be unbundled before search, and the regime acts as a threshold comparison rather than a gradual average.
- **Source, verbatim:** Fig 9 :54 "And when the split lands on a union rather than a partition, unbundle before you search again." Fig 9 :57 "a case in two conditions is not a harder case of either." Operators table: "a threshold relation between two quantities".
- **What to check:** do the crowding-out and crowding-in conditions overlap in actual borrowings, so that a compound residue exists? Does a threshold specification (slack versus no slack, bound versus free policy rate) fit better than a single linear average?
- **Refuter:** the two conditions form a partition in the data, so no compound residue exists; or a linear average fits as well as the threshold across all slots. The operand type of ⋛ at macro scale is a named strain, not a claimed settlement.

## 5. What would settle it

A ground reading, not agreement. Specifically: an identified, per-slot estimate of `dI/dG`; an instrument that separates the cost-of-credit route from the income route; a policy-regime change that tests whether the estimated relation is invariant (Lucas, 1976). Until one of these fires, the shape stands as `candidate`.

## 6. What an expert can check in twenty minutes

1. Read Fig 1 :13, :19, :22 and Fig 11 :50 (four short passages).
2. Check C2 against your own knowledge: can you name a setting where the credit route dominates and another where the income route dominates? If not, say so.
3. Name one instrument or identification strategy that would separate the two routes in a given slot, or say why none exists.
4. Send the refutation. A failure is more useful than an endorsement.
