## The question restated as an effect

Two frames predict opposite signs for the same quantity, the response of private investment to deficit-financed public borrowing. One route runs through the price of credit and real resource use (crowding out, private investment falls). The other runs through aggregate demand and income (crowding in, private investment rises). An answer is a statement of the sign and magnitude of that response, indexed by the state of the economy in which the borrowing occurs. Bind that statement as an ordinary term before running any search, so the sought-for shape can accrue a residual like anything else (formula step "Direction").

## What is deliberately not here

- No personal emails of named individuals.
- No ranking of people by how likely they are to agree with the shape claim.
- No bulk-send plan.

Three reasons, in the figures' own words:

1. **Agreement is not an anchor.** Fig 2 :35: "Alignment is triangulation, not negotiation. A mirror cannot correct you." A list selected for receptiveness manufactures the disqualifying correlation (Fig 2 :40).
2. **Independence is bought, not assumed.** Fig 2 :42: "Buy independence in proportion to the cost of being wrong." Fiscal policy errors are costly, so the verdict must range against data, not against a school's prior.
3. **Showing the interior spends independence.** Fig 10 :42: "A verifier that can reconstruct the claim cannot verify it." Stage 1 carries the residual and its refuter, not the framework.

Legally: public institutional and opt-in channels avoid the GDPR and CAN-SPAM failure that comes with scraping personal emails for unsolicited bulk outreach.
