# Outreach draft: check the output first, then decide about the theory

**Use:** one to one, or as a cover note attached to a submission through a public venue in `03_public_contact_points.csv`. **Not** for bulk send.

**Hard precondition:** do not send until the shape claim, its regime index and the three refuters in `08_expert_verification_packet.md` are stated exactly as written there. The two-stage offer collapses on contact if stage 1 cannot survive.

**Subject:** Check one output on public borrowing and private investment before you decide whether the framing is worth reading

> Hi [name],
>
> I have one specific output I would like you to check against what you already know. I am not asking you to read the framework first, and I am not asking you to agree with it. If the output does not survive, there is nothing else to look at.
>
> **The output (`specified`, not `grounded`):** the effect of deficit-financed public borrowing on private investment is not one number. It is indexed by the state of the economy. The framework's ground rule puts the residual per object slot, not in an average, and says that aggregating across slots is how a live signal disappears into an average. Applied here, the slots are full employment, slack, a bound policy rate, a normal policy rate, open versus closed economy, and high versus low public debt. The shape claim is that the sign of the response is negative in some slots and positive in others, and that the channels are distinct routes (a cost-of-credit route and an income-and-demand route), not competing ingredients in one regression.
>
> This is a shape and a route, not a measurement. I am not claiming a particular magnitude for any slot, and I am not claiming that "it depends" is an answer on its own. The regime index has to be stated for the claim to say anything.
>
> **What I am not claiming:** that public borrowing always crowds out private investment, that it always crowds in, that a single average estimate is the net effect, or that a consensus of economists settles any of this. School agreement drawn from the same evidence pool is one witness, not two.
>
> **What would refute the shape claim.**
> 1. An identified reading returns the same sign and statistically indistinguishable magnitude across all the enumerated regime slots.
> 2. A derivation shows that averaging across slots preserves the signal that the per-slot rule says is lost.
> 3. A ground reading shows the credit route and the income route are the same chain, so the distinction between them carries no information.
>
> I wrote these down before sending this. If any one holds, I will retract the shape. A one-line "this fails because..." is more useful to me than agreement. If you would rather not engage, no reply is needed.
>
> **If it survives, and only then:** the public sources behind this are below. The figures are the authority, not my summary of them.
> - Figure text with line numbers (local framework extract): `FRAMEWORK/reference/03-FIGURES_TEXT.md`
> - Canonical usage rule (local framework copy): `FRAMEWORK/reference/FIGURES_README_canonical.md`
> - The eight-step loop and its mode rule (local framework file): `FRAMEWORK/figures/THE_FORMULA.md`
>
> **If you want to use the machinery:** the figures are public. Run the loop on a problem you know well and can check against a ground. Write down what would refute your result *before* you run it, and state the mode of what you get. A reported failure is more useful to me than a reported success.

## Why this version is ordered this way

| the move | figure-grounded reason |
|---|---|
| Output first, theory second | Fig 10 :52: "Bring a characterised residual, never the source." The recipient checks the shape without holding the framework's interior. |
| Do not ask for agreement | Fig 2 :35: "Alignment is triangulation, not negotiation. A mirror cannot correct you." |
| Pre-registered refuter | A falsifier written before the run is a design instrument, not only an honesty device. |
| Mode stated on every claim | `THE_FORMULA.md` mode rule: general, specified, grounded. State which. |
| Regime index stated explicitly | Fig 1 :19: "The residual is indexed by object slot, not aggregated." |
| Channels kept as separate routes | Fig 12 :111: "A vocabulary without bonds can name a state and cannot name a route to one." |
| No prestige request, no "true answer", no "trust" | Fig 2 :42: "Buy independence in proportion to the cost of being wrong." A reply is a conversation, never corroboration. |
| Verdict comes from a ground, not from a school | Fig 2 :29: "An anchor is whatever settles the question and does not change its answer because of what any frame thinks." |
| Links after the verdict | Fig 4 :22: only methods cross up, and a recording sent first looks like knowledge without being one. |
| Offer the formula as a method to reuse | A generator can be given away without giving away an answer. Each user still needs a ground and their own refuter. |

## Distribution rule

One to one only. Record the stage-1 verdict before any framework material is shown. Never pool a later agreement as corroboration, because a shared evidence pool is one witness (Fig 2 :40).
