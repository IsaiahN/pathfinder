## 1. A shape is a constraint, not a filler

The canonical README says the framework returns "what a missing thing would have to look like (a shape, not a name)". Fig 9 :32: "you get a kind of thing, never the answer itself".

From *The Library of Babel*: "you can cut your search short if the set of books returned is small and descriptive enough that it provides a general shape of the answer that satisfies your question."

For this question the shape is the constraint that any candidate answer must name the regime slot and the route. "Public borrowing crowds out private investment" is not a shape of that kind. It fills the slot with a single sign and discards the index. "The effect is regime-indexed through two distinct routes" is a shape: it narrows what an acceptable answer can look like without supplying the magnitude.

## 2. How the shape is obtained

From *The Library of Babel*, the search uses vocabulary already held that is closest to the thing, then recurses on overlapping new terms: "With each new term gained, you are able to narrow the space by definition and deepen your vocabulary." And on malformed questions: "Perhaps your problem was a bundle or conflation of several problems masquerading as one? You will need to search all of those problems individually."

That is exactly the move in `01_figure_grounded_audit.md` sections 1 and 2b. The bundled either/or was split into a union of channels indexed by regime before any frame was chosen. Fig 8 :53: "Residual first, frame second."

Fig 6 :30: "The description travels. Fails by being sharpened into terms only we can read. Cast it in effect terms." So the slots are described in effect terms (slack, bound policy rate, openness, debt level), not in the jargon of one school.

## 3. What licenses a name (a magnitude, a sign)

Two things, both outside the shape itself:

1. **The ground settles it.** A state-contingent estimate becomes a number only when an identified reading pays for it. From *The Hard Problem is About Source*: "The answer is unreached until a better instrument exists."
2. **A provenanced import from a frame whose closure differs**, with a source gate and a shadow test (Fig 6 :23, Fig 8 :50).

Until one of those, the magnitude belongs to whoever ran the regression and must be recorded as such.

## 4. The metamerism warning applies directly to an average estimate

*The Hard Problem is About Source*: "If several paths create the same behavior, then behavior is a projection with lossy information." An instrument that reads green "cannot comment on the source"; the spectrometer, not an argument, resolved which mixture was present.

A single estimated coefficient on `G` is the same kind of reading. It can be produced by the credit route, by the income route, or by both in different proportions, and the reading does not determine the source. What the question needs is a discriminator that separates the routes and the slots, not a more precise average. Fig 1 :22: "Aggregating across slots is how a live signal disappears into an average."

This warning is **not** retracted. It survives the correction in section 5.

## 5. Correction to an overstatement (kept visible)

An earlier draft of this reading treated "shape, not a name" as a ban on saying anything specific. That was too strong. The canonical phrase governs a *missing* thing, a primitive not held in the library. Regime slots and routes are not missing primitives; they are distinctions the world supplies and that the world literature already names. The ban applies to inventing a new mechanism to fill a slot, not to indexing the residual by known states. So this file now says: name the slots, keep the routes distinct, and refuse to supply a magnitude that no ground paid for. The earlier overstatement is preserved here rather than erased.

A related correction: the Fig 13 substrate is an analogue for this question, not a derivation. "Shape" here does not license mapping macro quantities onto space, time, energy, spectrum, gradient and network. That strain is recorded as F10 in `01_figure_grounded_audit.md`.

## 6. Two consequences for outreach

**Prestige is not action.** From *The Recursive Self-Improvement Trap*: "Prestige can fund an action. It can give an action a budget to be tried. But it cannot settle an action." Listing famous economists who agree with a crowding-out or crowding-in position is the named collapse. The legitimate ask is a ground reading in a stated slot.

**This audit is a simulation, not a ground reading.** Same essay: "the output is only as good as the validator. The agent, or the tool, or the frame, or the theory, cannot validate itself." And: "the truly testable output would need to be derived from the real architecture or working the formula yourself." So `01`, `06`, `07`, `08` and `09` are simulated derivations checked for internal consistency against the figures. They cannot settle the question. No dataset was opened and no estimate was recomputed.

## 7. One-sentence answer to "what shape is the answer here"

A regime-indexed sign and magnitude over two distinct routes, with the slots named and the magnitude left open until an identified reading pays for it.
