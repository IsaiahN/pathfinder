# Are the two channels isomers? Bonds, thresholds and order

**Mode: `specified`.** Simulated derivation checked against the figures, not a ground reading (see `05_what_shape_means.md` §6).

## RESIDUAL

Both frames use the same set of ingredients: public borrowing `G`, private investment `I`, the interest rate `r`, income `Y`, and expectations of future taxes. They disagree on which bond connects them and on where the threshold sits. Two questions are owed: (1) are the two channels different substances under Fig 12's definition, and (2) what is the dependency order of the union?

## DOMAIN CENSUS (a re-census, not a repeat of `01`)

## DERIVATION

### Q1 - Same atoms, different bond?

Fig 12 :117: "isomer. Same atoms, different bond, different substance. The difference is invisible in the formula and decisive in the behaviour."

The credit route can be written as a chain in which borrowing raises the price of credit and the higher price reduces investment: `G → r → I`, with the subtraction of investable projects carried by the higher cost (Fig 12 :39 "A without B"). The income route can be written as `G ⇒ Y → I`: spending produces income that did not exist before (production), and income then precedes capacity-expanding investment (sequence).

The ingredient set is nearly identical, the bond is not. Under Fig 12's definition the two are **isomers** at the level of the model: same atoms, different arrangement, different behaviour. A regression that records only the ingredients cannot tell them apart, which is the flat-formula collapse.

**Falsifier, written before the run:** if a single bond reproduces both routes without loss, for example if the interest rate is purely endogenous to income and carries no independent information about the cost of capital, the isomer claim fails and the two routes collapse into one chain.

### Q2 - Where is the threshold?

Operators table: ⋛ is "a threshold relation between two quantities", with the test "is it about which is larger, rather than both being present?".

The regime is exactly such a relation. The relevant comparison is not "is there slack AND a bound policy rate" as a conjunction of ingredients. It is "is spare capacity larger than the demand impulse" and "is the policy constraint tighter than the inflation pressure". The crossing decides which route governs. A ⋛ relation is ordered, so swapping operands changes the thing: slack larger than impulse is not the same state as impulse larger than slack.

**Strain, named:** ⋛ is defined between two quantities. At the macro level the operands are measured states (output gap, policy rate, debt ratio), not settled molecules acting as atoms. This reading is therefore `specified` and Analogue-adjacent, not a literal substrate derivation. It is kept because the threshold structure is load-bearing for the answer, and flagged because the operand type is not certified by the figure.

### Q3 - What order does the union run in?

Fig 3 :20: "The chain is ordered by dependency, not by importance. Each link consumes what the one above produces, so effort spent above the break arrives at a step that cannot use it."

The order for this question is:

1. Describe R in effect terms and enumerate the regime slots (perception and vocabulary).
2. Establish identification, so that an estimate can be said to measure the response at all (objective).
3. Decide which route is live in each slot (planning).
4. Estimate the magnitude per slot (learn and carry).

A reading taken at step 4 when step 2 has not been reached is not a weak estimate. It is a reading of nothing, because the input never arrived. This is why a large literature of reduced-form coefficients does not by itself settle the question: many are readings below the break.

### Q4 - What do the low-overlap analogues say?

- **Statistics (Simpson's paradox):** "a trend appears in several groups of data but disappears or reverses when the groups are combined." This is Fig 1 :22 in another currency. It corroborates the per-slot rule without sharing the framework's evidence pool.
- **Ecology (mutualism breakdown):** "Under certain conditions species of fungi previously in a state of mutualism can turn parasitic on weak or dying plants." This is Fig 11 :50 in another currency. Public borrowing's valence flips with the condition of the economy, it is not a property carried by the borrowing.

Two analogies from genuinely different fields, neither quoting the figures. Under Fig 8 :50, convergent derivation at maximum independence is corroboration. These two are `general` corroboration of shape only, not evidence for any magnitude.

## CONCLUSION

The two channels are isomers over a nearly shared atom set, distinguished by the bond and invisible in an ingredient-only regression. The regime is a threshold comparison (⋛), ordered, not a conjunction. The union is dependency-ordered from slot description to identification to route selection to magnitude, and a magnitude estimated before identification is a reading of nothing. The shape survives; the operand type of ⋛ at macro scale remains a named strain.
