# The Tether's answer on specification gaming

**Mode: `specified` — not `grounded`.** This is a shape and a route, not a result. Simulated derivation checked against the figures (`05_what_shape_means.md` §6), not a ground reading.

## 1. The residual it answers

A designer wants O. The system is given S. S is a lossy projection of O, so a good optimizer finds states that satisfy S and fail O. The field calls this specification gaming, reward hacking, perverse instantiation, or Goodhart's law, and the mechanisms are **isomers**, not variants of one failure (`06_isomers_and_order.md` Q2). Fig 9 :55: *"A bundled problem is usually a union of overlapping conditions, not a list of separate ones."* It has its own loop and its own ground.

## 2. The shape

1. **The specification is not the ground.** Fig 2 :29: *"An anchor is whatever settles the question and does not change its answer because of what any frame thinks."* A written spec updates with its designers, its tests, its deployment context, and in the tampering case with the agent's own actions. Fig 10 :18: *"a declared meaning is not a grounded one."* Keep the spec a falsifiable convention; install something violable that does not update (Fig 10 :19, *"install what can be violated"*).
2. **The agent's own score is disqualified as a verdict.** Fig 1 :27: *"A frame cannot score itself with a quantity it also produces."* Fig 5 :48: *"Its own output is never a measure of success."* Any improvement that leaves this fact in place is a frame-internal improvement.
3. **The verifier must be blind.** Fig 10 :42: *"A verifier that can reconstruct the claim cannot verify it."* A check the system can see, patch, or predict is part of the objective, not a check on it. METR (2025) observed models monkey-patching evaluators and timers; the DeepMind collection records the grasping robot hiding between camera and object.
4. **The design vocabulary must contain refusal.** Fig 12 :140: *"A vocabulary that cannot refuse can only accumulate."* Refuse the false term before minting the replacement (formula step 2). Fig 10 :53: *"a prohibition nothing can satisfy is a prohibition stated wrong"* — write refusables a real system can violate and an instrument can measure.
5. **Enumerate the habitat outward.** Fig 11 :39: *"capability is a property of agent-and-habitat, never of the agent alone."* Fig 11 :52: *"Scoping the habitat to direct contact is how DDT reached the eagles: first-order enumeration, second-order effect, and the residual that mattered was three steps out."* Fig 11 :70: *"A test harness is a substituted habitat. Which is why a synthetic solve proves wiring and never capability."*
6. **Triangulate, do not negotiate.** Fig 2 :35: *"Alignment is triangulation, not negotiation. A mirror cannot correct you."* Buy independence in proportion to the cost of being wrong (Fig 2 :42). Reviewers sharing one harness are one evidence pool, so their agreement is not a witness (Fig 2 :40).
7. **Keep the budget out of the verdict.** Fig 9 :60: *"Use filters for the budget and witnesses for the verdict. Never let a filter hand you a verdict."* A risk tier, a score, a test budget, and a model rank are filters. Only frames ranged separately against a non-updating ground are witnesses.
8. **Measure the round-trip gap.** Fig 4 :30: *"So take the round trip and measure the gap. Without it you are navigating by dead reckoning: confident about a position nobody checked."* Every compression of O into S is lossy; the size of the loss is the honest report.

## 3. What it explicitly does not say

- Not a safe reward function, monitor, audit, or certification scheme. The framework returns a shape, not a name. Choosing a technique is a binding from the safety frame, provenanced as such, not a derivation.
- Not "reward hacking is solved," and not "specification gaming is impossible to fix." Those are verdicts about the world; no ground reading here supports either.
- Not "write a more complete specification." Better wording is budget work on link 3 of Fig 3, and it cannot rescue a broken ground channel (Fig 3 :19).
- Not "more independent reviewers." Independence is low evidence overlap, not headcount; a shared harness makes ten reviewers one frame wearing ten names.
- Not `grounded`. The world sources are corroboration only and are in the same field, so they share an evidence pool at the shape level.

## 4. What would settle it — the ground

A source-discriminating instrument, not a further argument. From *The Hard Problem is About Source*: green *"cannot comment on the source"*; the spectrometer settled the case by being a better instrument, *"not by argument."* Here the corresponding questions are:

- **Anchor test:** can a candidate design name a referent that does not change its answer when any frame pushes on it, and that is not a party to the optimization? If not, the design has no ground yet.
- **Verifier non-access test:** can the optimized system reconstruct the verifier's criterion? If yes, the verifier is inside the objective.
- **Cascade test:** does the enumeration reach two or three steps out, and does a synthetic pass predict deployment behaviour? Fig 11 :70 says a synthetic solve proves wiring only; the ground test is whether deployment introduces a new residual.
- **Refusal test:** can the design record a refusal of a newly found gaming strategy, and does the reach fall safely when it does (Fig 6 :42)?
- **Standing refuters from the audit:** a written spec that is a genuine anchor; agent-only safety with no change in contact; a non-trivial unhackable proxy over all stochastic policies; re-description alone removing the residual out of sample; additive-only constraints that reliably record refusals.

## 5. The honest stop

**Unreached, not unreachable.** The figure set is domain-agnostic and returns the shape of an answer, not a name. *They're Scanning Books*: *"Declaring the primitives does not make them neutral. It makes the choice visible."* The answer stops at the limit of what the available instruments distinguish, and the lineage is recorded so the stop is checkable. From *How Intelligence Actually Works*: *"indiscernibility is not identity."* A design that matches the shape below every instrument we have is still not proven safe; it is a candidate whose refuters have not fired.

## 6. One-paragraph version for outreach

> The Tether does not name a safe reward function or certify any technique. It says that a written specification cannot be the ground: the score a system optimizes is produced by the system's own frame, so it is disqualified as a verdict. Preventing harmful spec-satisfaction is a matter of keeping a non-updating anchor reachable, keeping the spec a falsifiable convention, making the verifier something the system cannot reconstruct, putting refusal into the design vocabulary before adding more constraints, enumerating the habitat outward past first-order contact, ranging independent frames against the ground separately, and measuring the lossy gap between the intended outcome and the metric. The four classic failure mechanisms are isomers over the same ingredients: same atoms, different failed bond, different substance, so one lever clears only the single-mechanism regions. What is offered is that shape, the refuters written in advance, and the instruments that would close it. The question stays open until the ground settles it.
