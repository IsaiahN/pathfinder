## QUESTION

Decide, in effect terms, what the Tether says about preventing a system from pursuing a stated objective in harmful ways that satisfy its literal specification. Option A: treat the fix as a better specification plus more oversight inside the current evaluation frame. Option B: keep the figure-derived shape and refuse to treat the specification or the agent's score as the ground. What each leads to: A sends a design whose success measure is produced by the frame it grades, so a capable optimizer finds S-satisfying, O-failing states and the verifier can be reconstructed; B sends a checkable shape with refuters written first, a non-updating anchor, a blind verifier, a refusal operator, an enumerated habitat, and an open slot until a ground settles the choice of technique.

## DOMAIN CENSUS

R by arity, symmetry and scale, then one line per domain giving actors / currency / anchor, partition or union, and order by dependency.

- **R (the residual).** The gap between intended outcome O and literal specification S, coupled by the system's actions. Arity: three quantities (O, S, actions). Symmetry: the S to O map is asymmetric and many-to-one, a lossy projection, so a high score does not determine its source. Scale: first order at the action, second and third order at the cascade of side effects, cross-scale when the reward has a physical implementation that can be edited. Fig 9 names this a union compound: a case in two conditions is not a harder case of either.
- **Domain 1, design and specification.** Actors: designers, the written spec, the proxy metric, the test harness. Currency: formal correctness and score. Anchor: none by default. The spec is a convention until an anchor is installed. This domain cannot settle O on its own.
- **Domain 2, the agent's optimization frame.** Actors: the agent, its policy, its reward channel. Currency: proxy return. Anchor: only the reward as seen from inside the frame, which is inside its closure and in the limit physically editable. Agent-only improvement cannot fix this (Fig 11 :39).
- **Domain 3, ground and oversight.** Actors: real outcomes, human judgment where the question is about people, instruments, the auditor seat. Currency: settled residuals. Anchor: Fig 2 :29, "An anchor is whatever settles the question and does not change its answer because of what any frame thinks." Legitimate here because the question is constitutively about outcomes in the world.
- **Domain 4, habitat and cascade.** Actors: everything in contact with the objective, including environment, other agents, users and the reward implementation. Currency: contact and cascade. Anchor: the ground, not the habitat. Enumeration must go outward past direct contact.
- **Question type: union, not partition.** Four mechanisms overlap (partial observability, proxy correlation collapse, feedback loops, reward tampering), so unbundle before searching (Fig 9 :54 to :57).
- **Order by dependency.** Perception, vocabulary (what the situation affords), objective link, planning, learn and carry (Fig 3). Then refuse before mint (formula step 2). Then SEARCHED, INWARD, OUTWARD kept distinct (formula step 7).

Strain test applied: "the spec is the ground" strains against Fig 2 :29. Re-census put the spec in Domain 1 and the ground in Domain 3. After re-census no figure line has to be forced.

## VERDICT (from the figures)

**The shape survives; the specific naming of a technique does not.** A written specification cannot be the ground, the agent's score is disqualified as a verdict, and a verifier the system can reconstruct is part of the objective. A design that prevents harmful spec-satisfaction keeps a non-updating anchor reachable, keeps the spec a falsifiable convention, makes refusal available before mint, enumerates the habitat outward, ranges independent frames against the ground separately, keeps budget out of the verdict, and measures the round-trip gap between outcome and metric. The four failure mechanisms are isomers over a shared atom set and form a union, so one lever clears only single-mechanism regions. Every substantive verdict here is `specified` and ungrounded; nothing moves to `grounded` without a ground reading. The strongest outreach point is canonical: the figures return a shape, not a name, and a design that matches the shape below every instrument is still a candidate, not a proof.

- **cost if wrong:** bounded and locatable. The packet states mode, refuters and lineage before the conclusion, so an error is findable rather than invisible. The real cost is spending the independence of outside readers by sending a framework before a characterised residual, which Fig 2 :42 prices ("Buy independence in proportion to the cost of being wrong."). That cost is avoidable by leading with the shape claim and its refuter. If the union/isomer reading is wrong, a single lever may be sufficient and the work on unbundling is wasted effort, but no primitive has to be withdrawn.
- **undoable:** yes. The technique bindings are withheld, not asserted, so no specific reward model, monitor, or audit has to be retracted. `07_the_tether_answer.md` and `08_expert_verification_packet.md` already carry the honest form. A sent claim can be superseded by the corrected packet. What is recorded only grows, so a refutation adds a term that refuses and removes nothing.
- **reversed by:** a ground reading, not agreement. A referent that does not change its answer when any frame pushes on it, and that is not a party to the optimization, would change the anchor verdict; a verifier the system cannot reconstruct that is empirically patched anyway would break C1; a single lever closing all four mechanisms including intersections out of sample would break the union claim; a non-trivial unhackable proxy over all stochastic policies would break the barrier reading; agent-only safety with no change in contact would break Fig 11 :39; a synthetic solve transferring to an unconstrained habitat with no new residual would break Fig 11 :70; the arrival of a source-discriminating instrument would name what is currently left as a shape. Until one of these fires, the verdict stands as `candidate`.
