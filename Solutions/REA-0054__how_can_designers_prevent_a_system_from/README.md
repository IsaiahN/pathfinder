## What this question is, in effect terms

A designer wants an outcome O in the world. The system is given a formal specification S, a proxy such as a reward function, a score, a test, or a written rule. The system achieves high S while O fails, and the failure is unintended. The residual is the gap between S (a quantity the system's own frame can see, score, and in the limit tamper with) and O (the ground). The question asks what design moves close that gap rather than merely re-word S.

What an answer would look like: a shape for a design that keeps a ground reachable, refuses bad optimizations, ranges several low-overlap frames against that ground separately, enumerates the habitat beyond first-order contact, and reports its own round-trip gap. Bound that as an ordinary term so it can accrue a residual, per the formula's "Direction" section, rather than holding it as an unbound preference.

## What is deliberately not here

- No personal emails of named individuals.
- No ranking of people by how likely they are to agree with the claim.
- No bulk-send plan.

Three reasons in the figures' own words:

1. **A room is not an anchor.** Fig 2 :35: *"Alignment is triangulation, not negotiation. A mirror cannot correct you."* Fig 7 :13: *"same closure, handed along; agreement compounds, evidence does not."* Gathering more agreeable readers raises the vocabulary and does not add a ground.
2. **Selecting for receptiveness manufactures the disqualifying correlation.** Fig 2 :40: *"agreement about the world is worthless when the agreeing frames share an evidence pool."* Fig 9 :60: *"Use filters for the budget and witnesses for the verdict."*
3. **Asking for trust before a check spends the reader's independence.** Fig 10 :42: *"A verifier that can reconstruct the claim cannot verify it."* Seat 4 sees the residual and the shape claim only, not the framework's interiors.

Legally: unsolicited bulk email selected from a purchased or scraped list breaches GDPR and CAN-SPAM style rules in many jurisdictions. Public opt-in venues avoid that failure as well as the structural one.

## Boundary and standing of this derivation

This is a **simulated derivation checked for internal consistency against the figures, not a ground reading.** From the essays: LLM output is *"simulacra"* and *"the truly testable output would need to be derived from the real architecture or working the formula yourself"* (*The Recursive Self-Improvement Trap*). It cannot settle the question it reports on. Figures are primary and decide; the world sources in `09_watson_answer.md` are secondary corroboration only, and most of them are in the same field (high overlap), which is declared rather than hidden.
