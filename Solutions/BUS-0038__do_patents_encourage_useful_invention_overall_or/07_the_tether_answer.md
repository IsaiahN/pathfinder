# The Tether's answer on patents, invention and follow-on research

**Mode: `specified` on the shape, `general` on the structural rules, `gap` on the numeric net effect. Not `grounded`.** This is a shape and a route, not a result. Simulated derivation checked against the figures (`05_what_shape_means.md` section 7), not a ground reading.

## 1. The residual it answers

"Encourage useful invention overall" and "impede follow-on research and market entry" are not two answers to one question. They name two loops with different actors, currencies and anchors. Fig 1 :19: **"The residual is indexed by object slot, not aggregated."** Fig 9 :57: **"What remains is smaller and it is also categorically different: a case in two conditions is not a harder case of either."** The word "overall" asks for the aggregate the figures forbid.

## 2. The shape, per domain, never averaged

1. **The first loop (the inventor).** A patent can change whether a costly search gets funded. In effect terms it can supply the support gate, make the disclosure worth paying for, and thereby admit a term that otherwise would not be minted. But a term that is *granted* is not a term that *settled*: Formula step 5, *"the ground settles it. Verified only by what pays."* Whether the net effect on useful invention is positive is a measurement question. Verdict: `specified` for the mechanism, `gap` for the sign.
2. **The follow-on loop (everyone else).** Two operators, opposite directions. Fig 6 :22: **"IMPORT adds an atom, and the added atom is what expands the closure."** The published teaching is such an import into followers' closure. Fig 11 :61: **"Introduction is available; exclusion is not. You can add an actor and you cannot subtract the habitat, because contact is a fact about the world and does not consult you."** The exclusion right changes who may compose over the teaching. Both effects are real and are in different currencies, so they are not netted before each is measured. Verdict: `specified`, triangulated across Fig 6 and Fig 11 and Fig 8.
3. **The institution.** Fig 10 :47: **"a convention that decides an outcome has stopped being one."** A right whose enforcement decides market entry has crossed from convention into verdict. That is a named failure condition, not a legal conclusion. The institution's stake is declared: an interest in the outcome is unavoidable, an interest in the reading is disqualifying. Verdict: `specified`, single-source for the figure line.
4. **Valence.** Fig 11 :50: **"The same actor is a benefactor to one residual and a malfactor to another, so mutualism and parasitism are relations rather than kinds."** The same patent can help a small inventor fund a search and hinder an entrant who must compose over the claim. Neither "patents are good" nor "patents are bad" is a figure-licensed verdict. Verdict: `general`.
5. **The number.** No figure line returns a numeric net effect. Fig 9 :32: **"you get a kind of thing, never the answer itself."** Fig 13 :135: **"Unreachable is a verdict here, not a silence,"** Fig 8 :39: **"So a search that finds nothing is never a proof of absence."** The number is a `gap`, owed to an instrument, not to a further reading.

## 3. What it explicitly does not say

- Not "patents encourage invention" and not "patents impede entry" as single verdicts. Each may hold per slot; neither is licensed overall.
- Not a call to abolish or strengthen patents. That is a policy choice that would require the ground, the two measured domains, and an account of the distribution of the effects.
- Not that patent counts measure invention. Fig 1 :27: **"They are frame-internal. A frame cannot score itself with a quantity it also produces."**
- Not that the institution is neutral, and not that the economics literature has reached a verdict. The sources opened for this solution are divided, and most share one evidence pool.
- Not `grounded`. No measurement of the two domains was taken here.

## 4. What would settle it - the ground

Fig 2 :29: **"An anchor is whatever settles the question and does not change its answer because of what any frame thinks. A measurement, an experiment, a counter, a market, a patient outcome."** The corresponding instruments are:

- **Disclosure/distribution instrument:** does the published teaching actually enter follower closure, and how fast, measured by what downstream frames can compose rather than by filing counts?
- **Contact instrument:** who is excluded from composing over the claim, measured by entry and by the cost of designing around, not by the text of the claims alone.
- **Source-discriminating instrument:** can we tell a real increase in minting from a change in filing behaviour, a subsidy, or a strategic thicket? This is the spectrometer case from *The Hard Problem is About Source*; patent counts read the projection and *"cannot comment on the source."*
- **Standing falsifiers from `01`:** a single per-slot residual that closes both loops; a regime that changes follow-on reach without changing either operator; a figure line returning the number; invariant valence across a change of residual; an enforcement regime that decides no outcome.

## 5. The honest stop

**Unreached, not unreachable.** Fig 4 :30: **"So take the round trip and measure the gap. Without it you are navigating by dead reckoning: confident about a position nobody checked."** The condensation from the figures into "union / two operators / relative valence / convention risk" is lossy; what it drops is exactly the instrument that would separate the loops. A shape that fits every fact we have is still a shape: *"indiscernibility is not identity"* (*How Intelligence Actually Works*). The answer stops where the instruments stop and records the lineage so the stop is checkable.

## 6. One-paragraph version for outreach

> The Tether does not answer "do patents encourage invention overall?" with a yes or a no, because that question bundles two loops that must be kept separate. For the first inventor, a temporary right can supply the support that makes a costly search worth funding and can buy disclosure, but a granted right is not a settled invention; whether the net effect is positive is a measurement question. For everyone downstream, the same patent does two opposite things: the published teaching is an import that expands what other frames can reach, while the exclusion right changes who may compose over it. These act in different currencies and cannot be added into one welfare number. The right's valence is relative to the residual, so it can help one inventor and hinder one entrant, and the enforcing institution has a stake that must be declared because a convention that decides an outcome has stopped being a convention. What the framework offers is the shape any answer must have, six falsifiers written in advance, and a clear statement that the numeric net effect is a gap that only a source-discriminating instrument can close.
