## 0. The residual, stated in effect terms

A patent is a legal operator that changes two loops at once:

1. **The first loop.** It changes the payoff to funding the search that produces a candidate term φ. The term must still pay the bargain *"The acceptance test is a bargain, not a threshold"* (Fig 5 :42) and *"A new term is worth having when stating it, plus what remains unexplained after it, costs less than what was unexplained before"* (Fig 5 :43). What the exclusion can change is the SUPPORT gate, `|R⁺_s| > 0 for some s` (Fig 5 :29), by making it worth betting at all.
2. **The follow-on loop.** The published application puts the teaching into other frames' reach; the exclusion right restricts which of those frames may compose over it. Fig 6 :22: *"IMPORT adds an atom, and the added atom is what expands the closure."* Fig 11 :61: *"Introduction is available; exclusion is not. You can add an actor and you cannot subtract the habitat, because contact is a fact about the world and does not consult you."*

The question asks for an "overall" verdict. That word is the defect. *"The residual is indexed by object slot, not aggregated"* (Fig 1 :19); *"Aggregating across slots is how a live signal disappears into an average"* (Fig 1 :22). Fig 9 :54: *"And when the split lands on a union rather than a partition, unbundle before you search again."* The residual is a union compound with its own two loops, and a single welfare scalar is not a term the figures license.

**What an answer would look like, bound before the run (Formula, "Direction"):** a shape that names the two conditions separately, with an actor, a currency and an anchor for each, and an instrument that would show a change in one without the other. The numeric net sign is not part of the shape. That is a `gap`, not a withheld answer: Fig 13 :135, *"Unreachable is a verdict here, not a silence,"* and Fig 8 :39, *"So a search that finds nothing is never a proof of absence."*

## 1. Domain census (before choosing any figure line)

**R by arity, symmetry and scale.** Arity: at least four-place (granting institution, first inventor, follow-on frame, paying market), indexed per patent-slot and per follow-on slot, never one scalar. Symmetry: asymmetric. Disclosure acts on all frames at once; exclusion acts directionally on the follow-on frame. The two effects are in different currencies, so no symmetric bond can join them. Scale: the single inventor loop, the population of inventors, the legal institution above the frames, and the market habitat that settles outcomes. The transform between scales is lossy (Fig 4 :30).

| line | domain | actors | currency | anchor | status |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| D1 | the first mint | inventor, research team, patent examiner | expected return against `\|φ\| + left(R,φ)`; disclosure event | an invention that actually pays and is disclosed (a counter, a market, a published specification) | admitted |
| D2 | the follow-on reach | subsequent inventors, entrants, research consortia | reach, `closure(Γ)`, independence | a new term composed and settled downstream (a follow-on patent, a product, a paper) | admitted |
| D3 | the institution | patent office, courts, legislature | rights, conventions, exclusion, fees | the market and the counter, which the convention must not become | admitted, stake declared |
| D4 | the verification frame | economists, economic historians, datasets | residuals and evidence | measurement, never agreement | admitted |
| X1 | a single welfare scalar "overall" | none nameable | none | none | **not a domain yet**; absorbed into the aggregation error |

**Partition or union.** Union, not partition. D1 and D2 overlap at the patent document: the disclosure is an output of the first loop and an input to the second. That overlap is a compound with its own loop (Fig 9 :55-57). Inside D2 there is a further union of two opposing effects: the teaching adds an atom to follower closures, while the exclusion removes contact. They cannot be added.

**Dependency order (Fig 3).** Describe R in effect terms before picking any frame or literature (Fig 9 :27-28: *"describe it before you go looking, or any frame you pick will seem to fit"*). Then D1 must settle before D2 can compose over the settled term (Fig 12 :104). D3 sets the conditions both loops run under. D4 checks each domain separately against its own anchor. *"Optimising a link that already works cannot rescue one that does not"* (Fig 3 :19).

**Strain test applied before conclusion.** Reading Fig 11 :61 as a ban on exclusion as such was strained; see the retraction log in section 7. Fig 8 :32 was initially read as a claim that patents add no knowledge; also re-censused. No strain remains after the re-census recorded there.

## 3. Findings, each with a verbatim line and a refuter

### F1 - "Overall" is a forbidden aggregation (decisive methodological finding)

Fig 1 :19: **"The residual is indexed by object slot, not aggregated."** Fig 1 :22: **"Aggregating across slots is how a live signal disappears into an average."** Fig 9 :57: **"What remains is smaller and it is also categorically different: a case in two conditions is not a harder case of either."** Fig 1 :27: **"They are frame-internal. A frame cannot score itself with a quantity it also produces."**

A patent count is a quantity the patent system produces. Using it as the score for whether the patent system works is the named frame-internal error. The two loops must be reported per domain. **Mode: `general`.** Status: triangulated across Fig 1 and Fig 9, two figures with different subjects (slot indexing and union splitting), low overlap.

**Refuter:** an instrument that returns one residual per patent-slot which jointly closes D1 and D2 with no leftover residual in either sub-slot. Until such an instrument exists, the aggregate is a convention, not a verdict.

### F2 - A patent is two operators, not one

Fig 6 :22: **"NOVEL means novel relative to atoms, not to the world. IMPORT adds an atom, and the added atom is what expands the closure."** Fig 11 :61: **"Introduction is available; exclusion is not. You can add an actor and you cannot subtract the habitat, because contact is a fact about the world and does not consult you."**

The enabling disclosure is an import into the follower's closure: the teaching becomes prior art and is available for open research (corroborated by the opened Wikipedia "Patent" page, which states that on publication the disclosed invention becomes prior art). The exclusion right is an attempted restriction of contact. These are opposite directions and must not be netted before each is measured. World corroboration: Heller and Eisenberg's anticommons argument, as rendered in the opened Wikipedia article, is that competing patent rights can block reaching useful products. **Mode: `specified` for the legal mapping, `general` for the two-operator rule.** Status: triangulated (Fig 6 plus Fig 11).

**Refuter:** a patent regime that changes follow-on reach while changing neither the disclosure (no atom imported into follower closures) nor the exclusion (no change in who may compose). Or a demonstration that the right adds an atom to the world's primitives, which Fig 6 :25 forbids: **"An import adds no atom to the world."**

### F3 - Valence is relative to the residual, not a property of patents

Fig 11 :49: **"An actor's valence is relative to the residual, not a property it carries."** Fig 11 :50: **"The same actor is a benefactor to one residual and a malfactor to another, so mutualism and parasitism are relations rather than kinds."** Fig 11 :51: **"No standing list of good actors survives a change of residual."**

The same patent is a benefactor to a small inventor deciding whether to fund a costly search (it can supply the SUPPORT gate) and a malfactor to an entrant who must compose over the same claim. This is why "patents encourage invention" and "patents impede entry" can both be true reports of different slots. A stable finding across fields would be a surprise under this rule, and the world sources are divided exactly along field lines (pharmaceuticals versus software in the opened Wikipedia "Patent troll" and "Patent" pages). **Mode: `general`.** Status: triangulated (Fig 11 plus Fig 5's support gate).

**Refuter:** an invariant valence for the same patent right across a documented change of residual in the same habitat.

### F4 - Independence decides whether any literature consensus means anything

Fig 2 :31: **"Independence, between two frames, is how little they draw on the same evidence."** Fig 2 :40: **"agreement about the world is worthless when the agreeing frames share an evidence pool"** (the full line begins *"disqualifying for a verdict:"*). Fig 8 :48-49: **"Two frames that overlap almost completely are one frame wearing two names."** Fig 2 :42: **"Buy independence in proportion to the cost of being wrong."**

Most of the opened sources quote the same small set of studies (Boldrin and Levine, Heller and Eisenberg, patent-count correlations). Agreement among them is one pool talking. Boldrin and Levine's own abstract makes the same point in the economics register: the missing evidence is anything other than the patent count itself. This is a filter on which sources to seek, not a witness for the verdict (Fig 9 :60: **"Use filters for the budget and witnesses for the verdict. Never let a filter hand you a verdict."**). **Mode: `general`.** Status: triangulated (Fig 2 plus Fig 8).

**Refuter:** a source with a genuinely different evidence pool and a different closure, such as historical world-fair data or litigation-cost microdata, that returns the same per-domain result. Note in the ledger which sources share a pool; do not count them twice.

### F5 - The anchor is a measurement; the net number is a `gap`

Fig 2 :29: **"An anchor is whatever settles the question and does not change its answer because of what any frame thinks. A measurement, an experiment, a counter, a market, a patient outcome."** Fig 9 :32: **"you get a kind of thing, never the answer itself."** Fig 13 :135: **"Unreachable is a verdict here, not a silence,"** Fig 8 :39: **"So a search that finds nothing is never a proof of absence."**

After a genuine re-census no figure line returns a numeric net effect of patents on invention. The figures decide the *shape* of the answer (a union, two currencies, relative valence, a convention that must not decide outcomes) and stop there. The numeric sign is owed to an instrument, not to a further reading. Declaring it `gap` is the honest verdict, not a failure to finish. **Mode: `general` for the rule, `gap` for the number.** Status: single-source for the shape; `gap` after re-census for the number.

**Refuter:** a figure line that returns the numeric net effect, or a measurement that settles it and is reproducible across independent anchors.

### F6 - The institution is a convention that decides outcomes

Fig 10 :47: **"a convention that decides an outcome has stopped being one."** Fig 10 :52: **"Bring a characterised residual, never the source."** Fig 2 :29 (above) names the market as the anchor.

A patent right is a legal convention. When enforcement determines who may enter a market, the convention has crossed into deciding an outcome, which is the named failure condition. This is a `specified` mapping of a figure line onto a legal institution, not a claim that patent law is invalid. It is also a reason to declare the institution's stake: an interest in the outcome is unavoidable and an interest in the reading is disqualifying (Seat Map :171); a rights office that benefits from its rules being used cannot be the verifier of those rules. **Mode: `specified`.** Status: single-source (Fig 10) plus the seat map rule.

**Refuter:** an enforcement regime in which the right changes no market outcome and is still a functioning patent right, that is, a pure coordination convention with zero decision content.

### F7 - The two currencies cannot be added

Fig 12 :125: **"A composition costs in two currencies, and they do not add."** Fig 12 :126: **"Bits price the description. Actions price finding out whether it holds."** Fig 9 :60 (above).

The first loop is priced in expected return and disclosure cost. The follow-on loop is priced in reach, independence and entry. They are in different units; a single "overall" score requires an extra convention that the figures do not supply. This is the structural reason Option A and Option B in the question cannot both be right as single verdicts, even though each can be right per slot. **Mode: `general`.** Status: triangulated (Fig 12 plus Fig 9).

**Refuter:** a common unit already measured in both domains, with no new convention introduced to create it.

### F8 - The empirical literature is itself a union with live conflicts

The opened sources do not converge. Boldrin and Levine report no evidence of net innovation gains and note that patent counts do not correlate with measured productivity. Henderson's Econlib entry calls the incentive argument *"airtight"* as a partial argument and then says it is insufficient, citing the diversion of creative energy into patentable improvements. The thicket and anticommons sources document blocking. The Wright brothers case is explicitly contested: many historians say the war stalled US aviation, while Katznelson and Howells say manufacturers faced no patent barriers. Patent-troll sources report both large direct costs and a defensible secondary market for licensing.

Per the WATSON conflict rule, a conflict sends the derivation back to the census; it never flips the figure verdict. Here it returns a confirmation: the world data behave like a union of conditions measured at different slots, not like one question with one sign. **Mode: `specified` for the reading of the sources.** Status: corroborates the union shape, but most sources share a literature pool, so this is one witness, not several.

**Refuter:** a single study that measures both sub-domains with independent anchors and finds one stable sign across industries and time.

## 4. Pre-registered refuter

Written before the WORLD sources were opened and before the conclusion, per the seat map's rule that *"a falsifier written before the run is a design instrument, not only an honesty device"* (Seat Map :287).

The shape claim is refuted if any of the following holds:

1. One instrument returns a per-slot residual that jointly closes D1 and D2 with no leftover residual in either when aggregated (breaks Fig 1 :19, :22 and Fig 9 :54).
2. A patent regime is shown that changes follow-on reach while changing neither the disclosure that imports an atom nor the exclusion that changes contact (breaks F2).
3. A figure line is found that returns a numeric net effect of patents on useful invention (breaks F5's `gap`).
4. The same patent right has invariant valence across a documented change of residual in the same habitat (breaks Fig 11 :49-51).
5. The enforcing institution can decide no market outcome and still function as a patent right (breaks Fig 10 :47).
6. A genuinely independent evidence pool, not sharing the studies in the ledger, returns a single stable sign for both domains across industries and time (breaks F8's union reading).

If any fires, the shape is withdrawn or narrowed. Until one is run, no part of this may be labelled `grounded`.

## 5. Recommendation

1. Do not answer "overall". Split the question into D1 and D2 and report each against its own anchor (F1, F7).
2. Keep the two operators of a patent separate in any instrumentation: measure disclosure as an import into follower closures and measure exclusion as a change in contact (F2).
3. Treat the institution as a convention that must not decide the outcome, and declare its stake (F6).
4. Buy independence in proportion to the cost of being wrong: seek sources whose evidence pool differs from the standard patent-count literature and record overlap in the ledger (F4).
5. State `gap` on the numeric net effect and name what would settle it (F5). Fig 4 :25: **"A recording carried upward looks like knowledge and is a description of one occasion."** Send the generator, the shape and the refuter, not a welfare number.
6. Lead outreach with the residual and the refusal criteria. Fig 10 :52: **"Bring a characterised residual, never the source."**
