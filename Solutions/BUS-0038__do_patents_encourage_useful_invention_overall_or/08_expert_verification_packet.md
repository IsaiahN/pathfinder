# Expert verification packet - the Tether on patents, invention and follow-on research

**What this is:** a claim in a form an intellectual-property or innovation economist can check. Mode, derivation and refuters are stated before the conclusion, so a reader knows what to look for and what would settle it. One to one. Not a bulk send.

**Mode: `specified` on the shape, `general` on the structural rules, `gap` on the numeric net effect. Not `grounded`.** No measurement of patent effects is claimed. This is a shape and a route, not a result. It is also a simulated derivation, not a ground reading.

## 1. The claim, in one paragraph

The question "do patents encourage useful invention overall, or impede follow-on research and market entry?" bundles two loops with different actors, currencies and anchors, and the figures forbid averaging them. For the first inventor, a temporary right can supply the support that makes a costly search worth funding and can buy disclosure, but a granted right is not a settled invention. For everyone downstream, a patent is two operators in opposite directions: the published teaching is an import that expands what other frames can reach, and the exclusion right changes who may compose over it. These act in two currencies that do not add, so no single "overall" welfare number is licensed. Valence is relative to the residual, and the enforcing institution's stake must be declared, because a convention that decides an outcome has stopped being a convention. The numeric net effect is a declared `gap`, owed to an instrument, not to a further reading.

## 2. Three checkable sub-claims

### C1 - framework-internal; checkable by reading Figures 1, 9 and 12
- **Statement:** the residual is per slot; a two-condition problem is categorically different from either condition; two currencies do not add. Therefore "overall" is a bundling error, not a question with one scalar answer.
- **Source, verbatim:** Fig 1 :19 - **"The residual is indexed by object slot, not aggregated."** Fig 9 :57 - **"What remains is smaller and it is also categorically different: a case in two conditions is not a harder case of either."** Fig 12 :125 - **"A composition costs in two currencies, and they do not add."**
- **Refuter:** an instrument that returns a per-slot residual jointly closing both loops with no leftover residual in either when aggregated; or a common unit in which both effects are already measured without a new convention.

### C2 - economics-facing; checkable by an innovation economist against the two loops
- **Statement:** a patent is two operators, not one. The teaching adds an atom to the follower's closure; the exclusion changes contact. They are separate and opposite, which is why both the pro-invention and the anti-entry literature can be reporting real effects at different slots.
- **Source, verbatim:** Fig 6 :22 - **"IMPORT adds an atom, and the added atom is what expands the closure."** Fig 11 :61 - **"Introduction is available; exclusion is not. You can add an actor and you cannot subtract the habitat, because contact is a fact about the world and does not consult you."**
- **What to check:** is the published specification an import into follower closure in the regimes you know? Does the exclusion right change who may compose in a way that is measurable separately from the disclosure? Look at defensive publication and trade secrecy as the two limiting cases.
- **Refuter:** a regime in which changing the disclosure without changing the exclusion, or the exclusion without the disclosure, leaves follow-on behaviour unchanged; or a single act that adds the atom and removes the contact with no separable instrument for each.

### C3 - framework-internal derivation with an institutional stake
- **Statement:** the enforcing institution is a convention that decides outcomes, so its interest must be declared and it cannot be its own verifier. Valence is relative to the residual, so "patents are good or bad by kind" is not licensed.
- **Source, verbatim:** Fig 10 :47 - **"a convention that decides an outcome has stopped being one."** Fig 11 :50 - **"The same actor is a benefactor to one residual and a malfactor to another, so mutualism and parasitism are relations rather than kinds."**
- **Refuter:** an enforcement regime in which the right changes no market outcome and still functions as a patent right; or an invariant valence for the same right across a documented change of residual in the same habitat.

## 3. What is explicitly not claimed

- Not "patents encourage invention" and not "patents impede entry" as whole answers. Each may hold per slot.
- Not a policy recommendation to abolish or strengthen patents.
- Not that patent counts measure invention. The figures disqualify a quantity a frame produces itself from scoring that frame.
- Not that the economics literature has settled the question. The sources opened for this packet disagree, and most draw on one evidence pool.
- Not that the enforcing institution is neutral. Its interest is declared here.
- Not `grounded`. No measurement was taken by this derivation.
- Not a human-authored research result. This packet was produced by an AI-assisted simulated derivation and must be disclosed as such if submitted anywhere.

## 5. What would settle it

A source-discriminating instrument, not a further argument. From *The Hard Problem is About Source*: an instrument that reads green *"cannot comment on the source"*; the spectrometer resolved it. Here:

1. Can we distinguish a real increase in useful minting from a change in filing behaviour, a subsidy, or a strategic thicket, using a measure that is not a patent count?
2. Can we measure, separately, the teaching's import into follower closure and the exclusion's change in contact?
3. Can a per-slot residual be returned for each loop so that the two verdicts stay separate?
