## QUESTION

Decide, in effect terms, what the Tether says about the three offered answers to the dark-matter residual. Option A: it is a particle (an unseen atom added to the library). Option B: it is a modification of gravity (a changed bond between mass and curvature). Option C: it is something else (an import from a frame with a different closure). What each leads to: A sends a name before a discriminator and treats null detections as a budget filter; B changes the law but must still fit cluster and cosmological slots; C requires a source gate and a shadow test before any candidate can stand. The figures are asked which move closes the per-slot residual, and whether they can name the winner at all.

## DOMAIN CENSUS

R by arity, symmetry and scale, then one line per domain with actors / currency / anchor, partition or union, and order by dependency.

- **R (the residual).** The mismatch between gravitational effects predicted from the baryonic inventory under GR plus the Standard Model and the effects actually measured. Arity: at least three overlapping conditions (missing source, changed law, unheld frame), so a **union compound** (Fig 9 :54-57), not one hard question. Symmetry: the three moves are different operators, not symmetric values of one variable (adding an atom, changing a bond, moving the closure wall). Scale: galaxy, cluster, horizon, and light-element slots; a lever at one scale leaves the intersections at the others standing.
- **Domain 1, gravitational observation.** Actors: photons, galaxies, hot X-ray gas, primordial plasma. Currency: lensing convergence, rotation velocity, acoustic peak positions and heights. Anchor: the measured sky, legitimate because the question is constitutively about gravity (Fig 2 :36). Union member.
- **Domain 2, particle frame.** Actors: WIMPs, axions, sterile neutrinos, primordial black holes. Currency: cross-section and relic abundance Omega_c h^2. Anchor: a detector recoil or collider missing-energy event. Currently returns background only. Union member.
- **Domain 3, modified-gravity frame.** Actors: acceleration, interpolation function, a_0. Currency: the acceleration law. Anchor: rotation-curve and cluster-lensing residuals; a_0 is fit, not derived. Union member.
- **Domain 4, verification / outreach.** Actors: expert readers in disjoint subfields. Currency: agreement and residual. Anchor: the ground, never the agreement. In this simulated run all seats draw on one evidence pool, so this domain's independence is zero.
- **Order by dependency.** Describe R per slot before naming a frame (Fig 8 :53, Fig 9 :27-28); refuse the flat-residual held term before minting (Formula step 2, Fig 5 :59); build the discriminator before ranking the moves (Fig 3 :27, Fig 6 :32); import only with source gate and shadow test (Fig 6 :30, Fig 8 :50-52).

Strain test applied: "dark matter as a seventh primitive" was sent back to census and dissolved. Dark matter is energy with an unregistered source (Fig 13 :13-14); a particle is an atom in the library, not a new substrate member. The "gravity is the Gradient" binding from the exemplar is unproven and is not used here. See `01` F10 and section 7.

## VERDICT (from the figures)

The three options are a union of overlapping conditions, not a partition. The residual is per slot. The sky and the detector are the ground, and neither frame may move them. The figures license the shape (close the gravitational residual at every slot where baryons are insufficient, with one account that survives an independent discriminator) and refuse the name. The canonical WIMP term has a flat residual and is the candidate for refusal before a new mint, but a null result is a budget filter and never a witness for absence. The rival models are a functional group as written and a checkable isomer claim over a full shared atom set. "Something else" is an import requiring provenance and a shadow test. The specific name is a `gap`, reported as unreached at this budget. This is a `specified`, ungrounded result, not an answer to what dark matter is.

- **cost if wrong:** bounded and locatable. The folder states mode, keeps per-slot verdicts separate, and writes the refuter before the conclusion, so an error is findable rather than invisible. The real cost is spending the independence of outside readers by sending a name instead of the shape, and Fig 2 :42 prices that: "Buy independence in proportion to the cost of being wrong." That cost is avoidable by leading with the shape and its refuter.
- **undoable:** yes. The union framing, the refusal-before-mint step and the gap report are separable from any future name. A positive detection or a successful single-law fit can be added without withdrawing the shape; if the union or per-slot split fails, it can be corrected without discarding the figure lines. Nothing primitive has to be withdrawn.
- **reversed by:** a ground reading, not agreement. A detector or collider signature closing the same residual at galaxy, cluster and CMB scales would move the particle name toward `grounded`; one law fitting all four slots with no unseen source would do the same for the modified-gravity name; an instrument that separates missing-source from changed-law would close the discriminator gap; a demonstration that aggregation preserves the signal, or a figure re-census in which the six-term set selects the name, would break the shape claim itself. Prestige or consensus is not such an instrument.
