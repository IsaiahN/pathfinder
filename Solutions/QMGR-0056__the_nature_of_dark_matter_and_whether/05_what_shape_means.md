## 1. A shape is a constraint, not a filler

The canonical figures README states the limit: the framework returns *"what a missing thing would have to look like (**a shape, not a name**)."* Fig 9 :32: *"you get a kind of thing, never the answer itself."* Fig 13 :134-135: *"Unreachable is a verdict here, not a silence,"* and *"It cannot say which primitive is missing, because naming it would mean already holding it."*

From *The Library of Babel* [carried]: *"You can cut your search short if the set of books returned is small and descriptive enough that it provides a general shape of the answer that satisfies your question."*

So a **shape** is the narrowed form of an answer you can state before you can name it. It constrains; it does not fill. For this question:

- `SHAPE`: *"close the measured gravitational residual at every slot where the baryonic inventory is insufficient, and do so with one account that survives an independent discriminator."*
- Names the question wants: `particle`, `modified gravity`, `something else`.

`SHAPE` is the bound ordinary term required by Formula "Direction". The three names are preferences held by frames. Preferences predict nothing, accrue no residual, and no internal check can see them (Fig 5 :61-64: *"a frame searching for an answer of a particular shape holds that shape as a PREFERENCE rather than as a term. It is not bound, it predicts nothing, and it accrues no residual"*). Binding the shape first is what makes the preferences refusable.

## 2. How a shape is obtained

- *Library of Babel* [carried]: use the vocabulary you already have that is *closest* to the thing, look for recurring new terms that overlap with it, recurse. *"With each new term gained, you narrow the space and deepen your vocabulary."*
- Fig 6 :30: *"outward: does another frame already hold it? The description travels. Fails by being sharpened into terms only we can read. Cast it in effect terms."* Fig 9 :30-31: *"your words are borrowed too, so describe the effect rather than the cause"*.
- *Decoding Tacit Knowledge* [carried]: *"When contact isn't possible, the best you can send is a plain description of what you'd expect to see, because anyone can check that against the world."*
- Applied here: describe the residual in effect terms (lensing mass offset, flat rotation speed, acoustic peak heights) before choosing particle, law or import. Fig 8 :53: *"Residual first, frame second."*

## 3. What licenses a name

Only two things, both outside the shape itself:

1. **The ground settles it.** Formula step 5: *"the ground settles it. Verified only by what pays."* The README: the framework *"stops at the limit of what the available instruments can distinguish, and records the lineage so the stop is honest."* Here that would be a detector signal, a force-law discriminator, or a decisive multi-slot fit that pays at the ground.
2. **A provenanced import from a frame whose closure differs** (Fig 6 :23, Fig 8 :50-52), with a source gate and a shadow test. A primordial-black-hole or emergent-gravity proposal is in this class.

*They're Scanning Books* [carried]: *"The real import is not a new term in the existing space. It is a new dimension of the space."* And: *"Declaring the primitives does not make them neutral. It makes the choice visible."* The six terms are a visible guess, not nature's own list. Turning one into the name of the dark-matter source would hide that choice.

## 4. Why a discriminator is owed, not another reading of the same projection

*The Hard Problem is About Source* (opened directly this session) gives the mechanism. A color instrument reads green and *"cannot comment on the source."* The source might be one green light, or yellow plus blue, or RGB, or CMYK, or a scheme not yet discovered. In the essay's words: *"If several paths create the same behavior, then behavior is a projection with lossy information."* And: *"An instrument that can't distinguish these cases demonstrates its own inadequacy."* It was the **spectrometer**, not an argument, that settled the case: *"It was proven wrong not by argument, but by the arrival of a better instrument."*

This is the dark-matter situation in shape. The rotation curve and the lensing map are readings, not sources. A missing mass term and a changed force law can produce the same projection. Naming the source from the reading is the metamerism error. What is owed is a source-discriminating instrument, which is why `01` F6 reports the discriminator link as unreached and why the three-way name is a gap.

The same essay supplies the validator rule: a theory that conflates projection with source has not explained the thing. Applied here, "dark matter is a WIMP" read off a flat rotation curve is a source named from a projection, unless and until a detector discriminates it.

## 5. The narrowing of "shape, not a name" (kept visible)

The exemplar's `05_what_shape_means.md` §5b corrected my earlier overstatement, and the correction is carried here. The README's phrase is *"what a **missing** thing would have to look like (a shape, not a name)."* The subject is a primitive not held in Γ.

Applied to dark matter:

- A particle candidate (WIMP, axion, sterile neutrino) is an **atom added to Γ**, not a missing primitive of the six-term substrate. Naming one is a binding or an import, not the invention of a seventh member. The substrate already holds **energy** (*"conserved, finite, directionless alone"*, Fig 13 :13-14); dark matter is mass-energy with an unregistered source.
- The rule still forbids claiming that the framework *derives* the name. There is no figure line that selects WIMP over axion over modified law over import. The name remains a provenance-bearing contribution by whoever supplies it, and requires a shadow test before it can stand.

So the honest statement is: the framework gives the shape of what must close R; it does not name the source; and naming an already-available atom is a binding with provenance, not a derivation and not a wall-moving import.

## 6. Prestige is not action

From *The Recursive Self-Improvement Trap* (opened directly this session): *"Prestige must never be allowed to validate an action directly."* And: *"Prestige can fund an action. It can give an action a budget to be tried. But it cannot settle an action. Only the ground can settle it. The Ground Maintainer must be blind to prestige."*

In this question the prestige leak is acute. A candidate backed by a famous collaboration, a highly cited review, or a large detector is easier to fund and easier to believe. That is a budget to be tried, not a settlement. The framework's rule requires the checker to read the ground (a recoil, a lensing map, a discriminator) and to be blind to the reputation of the claimant. This is why `03_public_contact_points.csv` contains institutional opt-in venues and no named-individual list.

However, the truly testable output would need to be derived from the real architecture or working the formula yourself."*

## 8. One-paragraph carry-forward

For this question, *shape* means the bound constraint that any answer must close the gravitational residual at every slot where baryons are insufficient and must survive an independent discriminator; the framework returns that constraint, not a name; a particle is an atom added to the library and a modified law is a changed bond, while "something else" is an import needing a source gate and a shadow test; the color-instrument essay shows why a projection cannot name its source, and the RSI essay shows why this simulated derivation cannot settle anything and why prestige cannot substitute for the ground.
