# Expert verification packet - the Tether on the dark-matter residual

**What this is:** a claim in a form an astroparticle physicist or cosmologist can check. Mode, derivation and refuters are stated before the conclusion, so a reader knows what to look for and what would settle it.

**Mode: `specified` on the per-domain claims, `general` on the structural shape, `gap` on the name. Not `grounded`.** No measurement in this simulated derivation is claimed to settle what dark matter is. This is a shape and a route, not a result.

## 1. The claim, in one paragraph

The residual between observed gravitational effects and those predicted from the baryonic inventory is measured per slot, not pooled. The three offered answers (particle, modified gravity, something else) are a union of overlapping conditions with different anchors, not a partition. The figures license a shape: whatever closes the residual must source the measured gravitational potential at every slot where baryons are insufficient and must survive an independent discriminator. They do not select the name. The canonical WIMP term has a flat residual and is the candidate for refusal before a new mint, while a null detection remains a budget filter and never a verdict for absence. The specific name is a `gap`, reported as unreached, with a discriminator owed.

## 2. Three checkable sub-claims

### C1 - framework-internal; checkable by reading Figures 9 and 1
- **Statement:** the three options are a union of overlapping conditions, and the residual must stay indexed per slot. One lever does not clear the intersections.
- **Source, verbatim:** Fig 9 :54 - *"And when the split lands on a union rather than a partition, unbundle before you search again."* Fig 9 :55 - *"A bundled problem is usually a union of overlapping conditions, not a list of separate ones."* Fig 1 :19 - *"The residual is indexed by object slot, not aggregated."* Fig 1 :22 - *"Aggregating across slots is how a live signal disappears into an average."*
- **Refuter:** show the three options are mutually exclusive and jointly exhaustive at every slot, with one lever clearing all three, or show that aggregation preserves the signal.

### C2 - physics-facing; checkable against the published record
- **Statement:** there are at least four distinct slots with different best reads at this budget (galaxy rotation, cluster lensing and gas separation, CMB acoustic peaks, light-element abundances), and no single name currently closes all four. Null direct detections constrain parameter space at increasing exposure but do not entail absence.
- **What to check:** the Clowe et al. 2006 lensing offset versus the Moffat 2007 fit to the same convergence map; the Planck Ω_c h² = 0.120 ± 0.001 result and acoustic-scale precision; the LZ 4.2 tonne-year and XENONnT 3.1 tonne-year null results. Are these four slots, or one residual measured four ways?
- **Source, verbatim (figure side):** Fig 9 :57 - *"a case in two conditions is not a harder case of either."* Fig 8 :39 - *"So a search that finds nothing is never a proof of absence."* Fig 9 :60 - *"Use filters for the budget and witnesses for the verdict. Never let a filter hand you a verdict."*
- **Refuter:** one model that closes every slot with one parameter and no residual anywhere, confirmed by a discriminator; or a positive recoil that closes the same residual at all four scales.

### C3 - framework-internal derivation
- **Statement:** a refusal precedes a new mint, and an instrument is owed at the broken link. The flat-residual WIMP term is the candidate for refusal; the discriminator is the missing link, not another fit.
- **Source, verbatim:** Fig 5 :59 - *"a term whose residual PERSISTS AND DOES NOT TREND DOWN is the one to point the refusal at"*; Operators :58 - *"a refutation is a negation, which is why a vocabulary without it can only accumulate"*; Fig 3 :27 - *"A link with no instrument is not a link that works. - **Refuter:** a positive detection that makes the WIMP term pay at the ground; or an existing instrument that already separates missing-source from changed-law across the slots and whose reading was decisive at the time of this derivation.

## 3. What is explicitly not claimed

- Not a particle verdict, not a modified-gravity verdict, and not a name for "something else."
- Not that the WIMP is disproved, and not that a null detection proves absence.
- Not that the Bullet Cluster settles the law question. The mass/gas separation is anchor-grade; its interpretation is contested.
- Not that dark matter is a seventh substrate member, and not that gravity is the Gradient.
- Not grounded. Nothing here is asserted as settled by experiment.
- Not a claim that the framework is the only true account of the dark sector.

## 5. What would settle it

1. A detector or collider signature with the required modulation and background rejection that closes the same per-slot residual at galaxy, cluster and CMB scales.
2. One modified law fitting all four slots with no unseen source and no slot-dependent fitted parameter.
3. A source-discriminating instrument that separates the missing-source projection from the changed-law projection where they currently coincide.
4. Any of the six refuters in `01_figure_grounded_audit.md` section 4 firing.
