## What the wider literature bears on the question

1. **The sky is a real, non-updating anchor, but its interpretation is contested.** Clowe et al. read the Bullet Cluster mass/gas separation as a direct proof of unseen matter; Moffat 2007 fits the same convergence map without non-baryonic matter. The figure verdict does not flip: the *observation* is anchor-grade, the *interpretation* is frame-dependent, and a discriminator is still owed (`01` F2, F6, S3).
2. **The null detections are budget filters.** LZ and XENONnT constrain parameter space at increasing exposure. Under Fig 8 :39 and Fig 9 :60 they cannot hand the verdict "there is no particle" (`01` F5).
3. **Each frame is strong on its own slots.** MOND fits galaxies with one parameter per galaxy and hits sharp challenges at clusters and cosmology; Planck fits the CMB within a six-parameter Lambda-CDM model with an extra non-baryonic density. That is a union with per-slot verdicts, not one winner (`01` F9).
4. **The philosophy-of-science source is the genuinely low-overlap corroborator.** The Neptune versus Vulcan pair is a historical instance of the same shape: adding an unseen object and changing the law are both live moves against one residual until a discriminator arrives. It corroborates the union and discriminator shape, not any dark-matter candidate.

No world source conflicts with the figure verdict in a way that sends the derivation back to census; the one local conflict (Clowe versus Moffat) was kept as evidence for F6 rather than used to pick a name.
