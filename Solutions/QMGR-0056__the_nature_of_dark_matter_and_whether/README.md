## The question restated as an effect

What is being decided is not a vote among three named candidates but how a persistent residual is to be closed. The current library Γ (general relativity plus the Standard Model plus the observed baryon inventory) predicts a gravitational effect Γ(b,a) from visible mass. The sky returns o′: galaxy rotation curves, cluster weak-lensing convergence maps, the cosmic microwave background acoustic peaks, large-scale structure, and light-element abundances. The residual R = |Γ(b,a) - o′| is measured per slot, not pooled into one global number (Fig 1 :19, Fig 1 :22). Aggregating the slots is how a live signal disappears.

- Option A, "it is a particle": leads to the prediction that the missing gravity is sourced by an unseen atom added to Γ. Its ground is a detector recoil or a collider missing-energy signature.
- Option B, "it is a modification of gravity": leads to the prediction that the law connecting mass to curvature changes at low acceleration or large scale. Its ground is a force-law discriminator, for example the separation of lensing mass from baryonic gas in a cluster collision.
- Option C, "something else": leads to the prediction that neither a new atom in Γ nor a changed bond suffices, and that an import from a frame with a different closure is owed. Its ground is a shadow test plus provenance.

What an answer would look like, written down and bound as an ordinary term before the run (Formula, "Direction"): a *shape* stating what any candidate must do (source the observed gravitational potential where the baryonic inventory cannot, across all slots, with per-slot verdicts kept separate) plus a discriminator that would tell a missing-source reading from a changed-law reading. Until that shape is bound it predicts nothing, and no internal check can see it (Fig 5 :61-64).

The verdict recorded in `09_watson_answer.md` is: the three options are a **union of overlapping conditions** with different anchors, not a partition; the figures license the shape and refuse the name; the specific held WIMP term has a flat residual and is the candidate for refusal first; and the number and identity of what closes R is a declared `gap`, reported as unreachable rather than silence. The verdicts are kept per domain and never averaged.

## What is deliberately not here

- No personal emails of named individuals.
- No ranking of people by how likely they are to agree with any of the three options.
- No bulk-send plan.
- No claim that this derivation is `grounded`, no claim that it settles what dark matter is, and no claim that the framework is the only true account.

Three figure-grounded reasons, plus one practical one:

1. A room is not an anchor. *"A room's agreement is variation, not a verdict. Selection between rooms is where the ground votes."* (Fig 7 :35) and *"Alignment is triangulation, not negotiation. A mirror cannot correct you."* (Fig 2 :35). A list selected for receptiveness manufactures the correlation that disqualifies a verdict.
2. Correlation has valence. *"agreement about the world is worthless when the agreeing frames share an evidence pool"* (Fig 2 :40). The particle and modified-gravity communities largely fit the same lensing and CMB data; their convergence is one pool talking, not two witnesses.
3. A verifier must be outside the interior. *"A verifier that can reconstruct the claim cannot verify it."* (Fig 10 :42). The first contact therefore carries the residual and its refuter, not the framework.
4. Legally, unsolicited bulk email to scraped addresses breaches GDPR and CAN-SPAM style rules in many jurisdictions and gets the sending domain blacklisted. Public opt-in routes avoid that failure as well as the structural one.
