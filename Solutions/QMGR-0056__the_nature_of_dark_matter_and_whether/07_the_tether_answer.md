# The Tether's answer on the nature of dark matter

**Mode: `specified` on the per-domain findings, `general` on the structural shape, `gap` on the name. Not `grounded`.** This is a shape and a route, not a result. Simulated derivation checked against the figures (`05_what_shape_means.md` section 7), not a ground reading.

## 1. The residual it answers

GR plus the Standard Model plus the observed baryon inventory predicts a gravitational signature from visible matter. The sky returns a different one, indexed per slot: galaxy rotation curves, cluster weak-lensing convergence versus X-ray gas, the CMB acoustic peaks, and light-element abundances. The gap is measured per slot (Fig 1 :19 *"The residual is indexed by object slot, not aggregated."*; :22 *"Aggregating across slots is how a live signal disappears into an average."*). The three candidate answers are three different operators against that residual, not three values of one variable.

## 2. The shape

1. **The three options are a union of overlapping conditions with different anchors, not a partition.** Fig 9 :54 *"when the split lands on a union rather than a partition, unbundle before you search again."* A modified-law fit can reach into the cluster discriminator; a particle shape spans different atoms; "something else" is the remainder after unbundling. Verdicts stay per slot and are never averaged.
2. **The anchor is gravitational measurement, and it is legitimate.** Fig 2 :29 *"An anchor is whatever settles the question and does not change its answer because of what any frame thinks."* Fig 2 :36 *"An anchor is legitimate when the question is constitutively about it."* The question is constitutively about gravity. The sky and the underground detector are two non-updating readings; neither frame may move them (Fig 2 :45 *"Frames range against the ground. They do not negotiate with it."*).
3. **The framework returns a shape, not a name.** Fig 9 :32 *"you get a kind of thing, never the answer itself"*; Fig 13 :134 *"Unreachable is a verdict here, not a silence,"*. What must close R is: *source the measured gravitational potential at every slot where the baryonic inventory is insufficient, and do so with one account that survives an independent discriminator.* Which atom, which bond change, or which import does that is not returned.
4. **The flat-residual held term is owed a refusal before a new mint.** Fig 5 :59 *"a term whose residual PERSISTS AND DOES NOT TREND DOWN is the one to point the refusal at"*; Operators :58 *"a refutation is a negation, which is why a vocabulary without it can only accumulate"*. Multiple tonne-year null results make the canonical WIMP the term to point at. This is a method verdict: it does not assert that no particle exists (Fig 8 :39 *"a search that finds nothing is never a proof of absence."*).
5. **A discriminator is owed, not another fit.** Fig 3 :27 *"A link with no instrument is not a link that works. It is a link nobody has looked at."* Fig 6 :32 *"An instrument is not built from a description. It is improved from a worse instrument already returning something."* The chain breaks between vocabulary and a source-discriminating instrument; fitting more above the break cannot rescue it (Fig 3 :19).
6. **The rival models are a functional group as usually written, a checkable isomer claim over a full shared atom set.** Fig 12 :117 *"Same atoms, different bond, different substance"*; :123 *"the group says what kind; the qualifier says which member."*
7. **"Something else" is an import with gates, not a catch-all.** Fig 6 :23 *"No frame escapes its own closure. The bound moves only on contact with a frame whose closure differs."* Fig 8 :50-52 requires provenance and a shadow test. A primordial-black-hole or emergent-gravity proposal is legitimate only if it explains a gap the held atoms cannot and carries its origin record.

## 3. What it explicitly does not say

- Not that dark matter is a particle, a modification of gravity, or any named candidate. The name is a `gap`.
- Not that the WIMP is disproved. A null result is a filter for the budget, not a witness for absence (Fig 9 :60).
- Not that the Bullet Cluster settles the law question. The observation is anchor-grade; the interpretation is contested, so a discriminator is still owed.
- Not that dark matter is a seventh substrate member. It is mass-energy with an unregistered source; a particle is an atom in Γ.
- Not that gravity is the Gradient. That is an unproven binding in the exemplar and is not used here.
- Not grounded. No measurement in this simulated derivation settles the name.

## 4. What would settle it - the ground

A source-discriminating instrument, not a further argument. From *The Hard Problem is About Source* (opened this session): a color instrument *"cannot comment on the source"*; several paths can create the same reading; *"an instrument that can't distinguish these cases demonstrates its own inadequacy."* The spectrometer settled that case, and it was *"proven wrong not by argument, but by the arrival of a better instrument."* Here the discriminating questions are:

- Does a recoil or missing-energy signature appear with the required modulation and survive independent background rejection, and does it close the same residual at galaxy, cluster and CMB scales?
- Can one modified law fit rotation curves, cluster lensing, CMB peaks and light-element abundances simultaneously with no unseen source and no slot-dependent fitted parameter?
- Is there an instrument that separates missing-source from changed-law where the two projections currently coincide?
- Standing refuters from `01` section 4: legitimate aggregation, name-capable figures, a decisive existing discriminator, or a failure of the non-updating anchor.

## 5. The honest stop

**Unreached, not unreachable.** The acoustic scale is measured to 0.03 percent and LZ has spent 4.2 tonne-years; these are real instruments that return something and are already failing to resolve the source. Fig 6 :34: *"The question is not whether a sensor could exist. It is whether anything, at any resolution, is already returning something that fails to resolve."* The stop is at the limit of what the available instruments distinguish. The lineage is recorded so the stop is checkable. *"Indiscernibility is not identity."* The framework cannot name which primitive is missing (Fig 13 :135), so the name stays open.
