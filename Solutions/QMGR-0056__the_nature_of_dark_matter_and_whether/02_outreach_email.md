**Subject:** Check one structural output on the dark-matter residual before you decide whether the framing is worth reading

> Hi [name],
>
> I have one specific output I would like you to check against what you already know. I am not asking you to read a theory first, and I am not asking you to agree with anything. If the output does not survive, there is nothing else to look at.
>
> **The residual, in effect terms.** General relativity plus the Standard Model plus the observed baryon inventory predicts a gravitational signature from visible mass. The sky returns a different one: galaxy rotation curves, cluster weak-lensing maps, the CMB acoustic peaks, and light-element abundances. The gap is measured per slot, not pooled into one number. A lever that clears one slot leaves the others standing.
>
> **The output (`specified` on the structure, `general` on the shape, not `grounded`):** the menu "particle, modification of gravity, or something else" is a union of overlapping conditions with different anchors, not a three-way partition. A modified-gravity model can reach into the cluster discriminator; an axion and a WIMP share the particle shape but differ as atoms; "something else" is the remainder after the two named closures are unbundled. The figures license the *shape* any answer must have (it must source the measured gravitational potential where the baryons cannot, at every slot), and they refuse to select the name. No figure line decides which of the three closes the residual. That is a declared gap, not a silence.
>
> A second output concerns method: a held term whose residual persists and does not trend down is the term to point a refusal at before minting another. In this field that points at the canonical WIMP, given multiple tonne-year null results, without claiming that no particle exists. A null result is a filter for the budget, never a witness for a verdict.
>
> **What I am not claiming:** that dark matter is a particle, that it is modified gravity, that the WIMP is disproved, that a null detection proves absence, that the Bullet Cluster settles the law question, or that this note is grounded. No measurement settles the name here, and I do not have one.
>
> **What would refute the shape claim (written before this message):**
> 1. a recoil or missing-energy signal that closes the same per-slot residual at galaxy, cluster and CMB scales;
> 2. one modified law that fits all four slots with no unseen source and no slot-dependent fitted parameter;
> 3. a demonstration that the per-slot residuals are one residual and that aggregation preserves the signal;
> 4. a figure re-census in which the six-term set plus its bonds selects which of the three moves closes the residual;
> 5. an existing instrument that already separates missing-source from changed-law across the slots and whose reading was decisive at the time of this derivation;
> 6. a demonstration that the lensing or CMB reading changes with the interpreter's preferred theory.
>
> If any one holds, I will withdraw or narrow the claim. A one-line "this fails because..." is more useful to me than agreement. If you would rather not engage, no reply is needed.
