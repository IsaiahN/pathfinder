## The residual in effect terms

A person observes a misfortune, an outcome that fails to match what their current beliefs and habits predicted at one tracked slot. A residual R registers there. The person must bind some cause term into their library so the residual stops arriving. The question asks which of four borrowed labels they choose. The derivation asks the different, prior question: what was missing or wrong in the observer's library that made one binding rather than another payable. Option A, treat the four labels as a partition and pick the right one, leads to a debate over words that does not shrink with effort. Option B, re-describe the decision as a routing of R and keep the four labels as bindings onto routing outcomes, leads to checkable sub-claims, refuters, and a ground that can settle them. This package takes B and records why.

What an answer would look like is written down and bound as an ordinary term before the run, per `THE_FORMULA.md` "Direction": an answer is a re-description of the four-way choice as (i) a routing of R by what is missing or wrong, (ii) a union of overlapping conditions rather than a partition, (iii) habitat-relative labels, and (iv) an import where a closure differs. That shape is refusable by the same test as everything else; the refusers are stated in `01_figure_grounded_audit.md` §4 and were written before the conclusion.

## What is deliberately not here

- No personal emails of named individuals.
- No ranking of people by how likely they are to agree with the claim.
- No bulk-send plan.

Three reasons in the figures' own words:

1. **Agreement is not an anchor when the frames share a pool.** Fig 2 :40: *"disqualifying for a verdict: agreement about the world is worthless when the agreeing frames share an evidence pool"*.
2. **Selecting for receptiveness manufactures the disqualifying correlation.** Fig 9 :60: *"Use filters for the budget and witnesses for the verdict. Never let a filter hand you a verdict."*
3. **Sending the theory before the verdict spends the reader's independence.** Fig 10 :42: *"A verifier that can reconstruct the claim cannot verify it."*

Legally: scraping personal emails for unsolicited bulk outreach breaches GDPR and CAN-SPAM style rules in many jurisdictions. Public opt-in channels avoid that failure as well as the structural one.
