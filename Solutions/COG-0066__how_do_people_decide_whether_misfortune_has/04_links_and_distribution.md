## Wider literature that bears on the re-description

1. **Attribution theory, human cognition.** The internal/external and stability/locus/controllability dimensions are a different partition from social/natural/moral/supernatural. They corroborate the claim that the four labels are a convention laid over a routing process, while their own criticism, that the theory *"fails to address the social, cultural, and historical factors"*, corroborates the habitat-relativity finding F6.
2. **Cognitive science of religion, human belief.** Hyperactive agency detection predicts an agentic term where no agent is present, matching F3's import reading. Its own cost asymmetry is a budget fact, not a verdict on the attribution.
3. **Anthropology of misfortune, cross-cultural.** The Azande granary is a direct case of overlapping natural and supernatural conditions with the moral condition excluded, matching F5. One evidence pool, two Wikipedia pages.
4. **Just-world and defensive attribution, social psychology.** Victim derogation and character reinterpretation match F4's mint-over-refusal move.
5. **Mathematics and engineering, signal detection.** The hit/miss/false-positive matrix on the Agent detection page predicts that a cheap false positive will dominate when the cost of a miss is high. This is a low-overlap analogue supporting the asymmetry in F3.
