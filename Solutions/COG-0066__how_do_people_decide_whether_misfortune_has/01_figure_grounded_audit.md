## 0. The residual under audit

The question is: **How do people decide whether misfortune has social, natural, moral, or supernatural causes?**

Stated as an effect, not a cause: an observer holds a prediction Γ(b,a) about how the world and the people in it will go. An event o' arrives that misses that prediction at one tracked slot, the person or episode affected. R = |Γ(b,a) − o'| is positive. Something is now owed: a cause term that, if bound into Γ, makes the event expected enough that the residual stops arriving. The decision under study is **which term gets bound and why**.

Two readings are available, and only one is a fact that shrinks with effort:

- **A (the question's own framing):** the four labels form a set of alternatives; the person selects one; the science of the thing is to find the rule that selects correctly.
- **B (the re-description derived here):** the four labels are borrowed cause-words for bindings onto routing outcomes. The person's actual decision is a routing of R by what is missing or wrong in their library, inside a habitat they did not author, with an import when their closure does not contain a paying term.

Binding the sought-for shape as an ordinary term before the run (Formula "Direction"): an answer is expected to be (1) a routing of R per slot, (2) a union of overlapping conditions rather than a partition, (3) habitat-relative labels, and (4) an import for the supernatural case. An answer that merely ranks four labels is not that shape and is refusable on that ground.

## 2. Domain census (before any figure line was chosen)

### 2a. R by arity, symmetry, scale

- **Arity:** one event at one slot. Fig 1 :19 *"The residual is indexed by object slot, not aggregated."* The victim, the household, the harvest is the slot. The four labels describe candidate causes, not four residuals.
- **Symmetry:** not interchangeable. An agentic cause (social or supernatural) carries a theory of mind available to the observer; a mechanism cause (natural) does not. Moral attribution is asymmetric between actor and observer: other people's misfortunes are dispositional, one's own situational. That asymmetry is a reading of the slot, not a property of the event.
- **Scale:** first order inside the observer's frame (perceive, route, bind, settle). Second order at frame contact, when a cause term from a closure that differs is imported. The two scales are not to be read against each other (Fig 6 :31).

### 2b. One line per domain: actors / currency / anchor

1. **The perceiver's loop.** Actors: observer, belief b, library Γ, observation o', the object slot. Currency: residual R per slot, and the description-length cost of a binding. Anchor: the ground, meaning what actually happened and what pays when the term is checked (Fig 2 :29 *"An anchor is whatever settles the question and does not change its answer because of what any frame thinks."*).
2. **The habitat of candidate causes.** Actors: social others, physical mechanisms, a normative order, non-natural agents, plus conditions and relations. Currency: contact with the residual, read off the world. Anchor: the ground. Fig 11 :43 *"The habitat is enumerated, never composed."* This is a domain because it has a nameable anchor, even though its membership is observer-relative.
3. **Frame contact / import.** Actors: the observer's closure and closures that differ (a scientific frame, a cosmological or religious frame). Currency: atoms crossing a wall. Anchor: provenance and the shadow test, the source gate of Fig 6/Fig 8. Without a differing closure there is no domain here, only more search in the same one.
4. **Verification / outreach.** Actors: outside frames and expert readers. Currency: agreement and residual. Anchor: the ground, never agreement. Because the question is constitutively about people, human judgment is the legitimate anchor here (Fig 2 :37 *"Human judgment is the right anchor for is this a good answer for a person, because the question is about people."*). In this simulated run all seats draw on one evidence pool, so their agreement is worth one witness at most.

### 2c. Partition or union

**Union, not partition.** A single misfortune can be natural (termites), social (a failure of maintenance), moral (punishment) and supernatural (a hostile agent) at once. Fig 9 :55 *"A bundled problem is usually a union of overlapping conditions, not a list of separate ones."* Fig 9 :57 *"a case in two conditions is not a harder case of either"*. Each intersection is a compound with its own loop and must be unbundled before search (Fig 9 :54 *"And when the split lands on a union rather than a partition, unbundle before you search again."*). The four labels are conditions that overlap, not boxes that exclude.

### 2d. Order by dependency (Fig 3)

Fig 3 :20 *"The chain is ordered by dependency, not by importance."* Order: **perception** (is there a residual and on which slot) to **vocabulary** (which cause terms are already in Γ and reachable) to **objective** (what counts as progress: left(R,φ) falling) to **planning** (which probe to aim) to **learn and carry**. Debating the correct label while perception and vocabulary are unsettled is a reading below the break: Fig 3 :22 *"A step whose input never arrives cannot be diagnosed, only its predecessor can."*

## 3. Findings

### F1  -  The four labels are cause-words; the framework routes by effect

Fig 9 :31 *"your words are borrowed too, so describe the effect rather than the cause"*. Fig 1 :15 *"where to look: broken vs novel"*. The observer does not first possess four labels and then choose. They possess a positive residual and sort it.

**Derivation.** THE_FORMULA step 2 routes R into TRANSFERRED (nothing owed), NOVEL (extend perception), BROKEN with rebinding (re-fit, do not mint), BROKEN with mechanism (a mint is owed), and REFUTED (a refusal is owed). Fig 5 :10 *"sort the gap into four kinds"* and Fig 5 :50 *"AMENDMENT · the gap has a fifth kind, and the pipeline has no branch for it."* The everyday four-way question is a different partition of the same event. Re-describing it as a routing is not a metaphor; it is the only description that names what the observer is doing when the residual changes.

**Mode:** `general`, structural. **Status:** triangulated (Fig 9 + Fig 1 + Fig 5; low overlap between the leave/arrive rule and the agent loop).

**Refuter:** a case where the routing does not change when the missing or wrong term changes, for example the same person binding the same cause to a predicted event (TRANSFERRED) and to a novel mechanism failure (BROKEN mechanism) under identical vocabulary.

### F2  -  The routing bins re-describe the labels, but only two map cleanly

Fig 5 :11 *"held: the model was right, nothing owed"*; Fig 5 :12 *"new: extend perception, aim the probe here"*; Fig 5 :13 *"mis-attached: refit what you have, do not invent"*; Fig 5 :14 *"mechanism broken: a new term is owed"*.

The clean mappings, stated as predictions rather than findings of fact:

- **"Nothing was meant to happen; that is just bad luck"** is TRANSFERRED or held. The misfortune was already predicted in outline; no search is owed. Note that this is a *stopped* search, not a cause term.
- **"I need to find out what happened"** is NOVEL. Perception is extended, the probe is aimed; no cause is bound yet.
- **"It was our neighbour / the faulty brake"** is BROKEN with rebinding, a mis-attached term re-fitted from what the observer already holds. This is where much **social** attribution lives, and much naive **natural** attribution too.
- **"Some mechanism we did not know about was at work"** is BROKEN with mechanism, where a natural cause term is minted inside the current closure.

The two strained mappings are F3 (supernatural) and F4 (moral). They are not forced into bins; they govern other quantities.

**Mode:** `specified`. **Status:** triangulated for the bin definitions (Fig 5 + Formula step 2), single-source for the mapping onto everyday labels (figure-derived, unmeasured at the ground).

**Refuter:** show that one of the four mappings fails systematically, for example that a novel event reliably produces a mint without any probe, or that an expected event reliably triggers a new cause term.

### F3  -  The supernatural case is an import, not a routing bin (strain recorded, re-census performed)

First reading, recorded and refused: "supernatural = mechanism broken, a mint owed." That strains. Fig 5 :14 governs a term minted **inside** the current closure from atoms already held. A non-natural agent is not such a term; it arrives from a frame whose closure differs. Per the derive-first rule, the strain sends the reading back to the census, not to a softer reading.

Re-census: the supernatural case belongs to domain 3, frame contact. Fig 6 :14 *"the only operator that moves the wall"*. Fig 6 :23 *"No frame escapes its own closure. The bound moves only on contact with a frame whose closure differs."* Fig 8 :53 *"Residual first, frame second."* The observer imports an atom (an agent not in the naturalistic closure) that makes the event predictable, and that atom expands the closure. It is licensed only by a source gate (provenance) and a shadow test (does it explain the gap?). Fig 6 :30 *"outward: does another frame already hold it? The description travels. Fails by being sharpened into terms only we can read. Cast it in effect terms."* And Fig 6 :31 *"Neither answer tells you much about the other. Run them as two tracks, and never read one against the other."*

**Mode:** `specified`. **Status:** triangulated (Fig 6 + Fig 8; different figures, low overlap between the wall mechanism and the union-surplus rule).

**Refuter:** demonstrate a supernatural attribution made with no import at all, that is, derived entirely inside a closure that already contains all the atoms it uses, with no provenance gap and no wall move; or show that the imported term fails the shadow test and the attribution persists anyway with no residual.

**World corroboration, secondary only:** the agent-detection literature predicts exactly an import-shaped failure. Gray and Wegner, quoted on the Agent detection page: *"The high cost of failing to detect agents and the low cost of wrongly detecting them has led researchers to suggest that people possess a Hyperactive Agent Detection Device, a cognitive module that readily ascribes events in the environment to the behavior of agents."* An ascription of agency to an event with no agent present is an import from a frame with a different closure, arriving with a cost asymmetry rather than with evidence. Corroborates, does not decide.

### F4  -  Moral attribution is a mis-route: a refusal is owed, a character term is minted

Fig 5 :53 *"refuted: a term we hold is wrong; a term is owed that says so"*. The fifth bin sorts by what is **wrong**, not by what is missing. A moral attribution has exactly that shape: the observer held a normative expectation (a person would not do this, or the world would not visit this on the innocent), and the event refutes it.

What the framework says is owed is a **refusal**, which removes a term's standing to be composed over. What people actually bind is usually a **character term**: they deserved it, they were careless, the world is just. Fig 12 :140 *"A vocabulary that cannot refuse can only accumulate."* Operators table :58: *"a refutation is a negation, which is why a vocabulary without it can only accumulate"*. The held norm is not refused; a disposition is minted over it and the library grows.

**Mode:** `specified`, ungrounded. **Status:** single-source for the figure rule, with world corroboration from just-world and defensive-attribution research (low overlap with the figures).

**Refuter:** find a population or a task in which a violated normative expectation leads to refusal of the expectation and no dispositional term is minted; or show that the minted character term pays better than the refusal under the same cost function, in which case the mis-route claim fails.

**World corroboration:** the Just-world fallacy page states the bias as *"people get what they deserve"* and notes that observers *"can reinterpret the outcome, the cause, and/or the character of the victim"*. It also states: *"Many researchers have interpreted just-world beliefs as an example of causal attribution. In victim blaming, the causes of victimization are attributed to an individual rather than to a situation."* This is the mint-over-refusal move: a character term is bound where a norm was refuted.

### F5  -  The categories are a union, and each intersection is its own compound

Fig 9 :55 quoted above; Fig 9 :57 *"a case in two conditions is not a harder case of either"*. A misfortune that is both natural and supernatural is not a harder natural misfortune; it is a specific compound, and a single lever (for example "was there a mechanism failure?") clears the region where only one condition holds and leaves the intersection standing. Fig 9 :55-56 *"A single lever clears the region where only one condition holds and leaves the intersections standing"*.

**Mode:** `general` for the union claim, `specified` for its application to these four categories. **Status:** triangulated (Fig 9 union rule + Fig 11 valence relativity).

**Refuter:** exhibit a case in which the four conditions are mutually exclusive by construction, so that proving one disproves the others, with no observer able to hold two at once.

**World corroboration, strong and low overlap:** the Azande granary case. From the *Azande witchcraft* page, quoting Evans-Pritchard: *"If a person is killed by the collapse of the granary, the Azande call it witchcraft. This does not mean they deny that the poles on which the granary rested were destroyed by termites."* Natural and supernatural causes are affirmed together. The same page adds that the Azande *"do not believe that witchcraft causes people to make mistakes in activities such as farming, hunting, or making crafts"* and that witchcraft does not influence *"moral crimes such as lying, cheating, or deceiving someone"*. So the moral condition is excluded from that compound while the natural and supernatural conditions co-occur. This is the union claim in one ethnographic example. It corroborates; the figures decide.

**Overlap warning:** the two Wikipedia Azande pages draw on the same Evans-Pritchard evidence pool. They are one witness with two spellings, not two witnesses.

### F6  -  Labels are habitat-relative relations, not kinds of cause

Fig 11 :49 *"An actor's valence is relative to the residual, not a property it carries."* Fig 11 :50 *"The same actor is a benefactor to one residual and a malfactor to another, so mutualism and parasitism are relations rather than kinds."* Fig 11 :48 *"You do not invent the list. You read it off the world, and what you cannot perceive or measure yet is the residual."*

Whether a cause is read as social or supernatural depends on the observer's residual and the habitat they are in, not on an intrinsic tag carried by the cause. Fig 10 :18 *"a declared meaning is not a grounded one"*: a category assigned by a convention is not thereby a settlement. This is also why Fig 2 :41 *"efficient for a convention: agreement about a shared meaning is the product, not a corruption of it"* applies to the four labels. Agreement on what to call a cause is a convention, useful and non-verdictive.

**Mode:** `general`. **Status:** triangulated (Fig 11 + Fig 10 + Fig 2).

**Refuter:** show a cause whose social or supernatural classification is invariant across residuals and habitats, with the same valence assigned by observers who share no vocabulary and no evidence pool.

**World corroboration:** the Attribution page reports actor/observer and culture differences: other people's conduct is attributed to disposition, one's own to situation, and individualist and collectivist cultures differ in which they favour. That is valence relative to the observer's position, as Fig 11 predicts.

### F7  -  Read the residual per slot; aggregates hide the routing signal

Fig 1 :19 quoted above; Fig 1 :22 *"Aggregating across slots is how a live signal disappears into an average."* A population-level statistic ("culture X attributes misfortune to spirits") can be near zero residual while one slot is live. The routing decision is made at the slot. A global R near zero with one live slot is a legal state (Fig 1 :13 *"a global R near zero with one live slot residual is a legal state"*).

**Mode:** `general`. **Status:** single-source (Fig 1), corroborated by the world finding that cultures differ on average but individuals within them route by position and similarity.

**Refuter:** show a group-level attribution rate that predicts an individual's per-event binding as well as the slot-level description does, with no loss of live slots.

### F8  -  Settlement needs an instrument or a differing frame, not a longer argument

Fig 6 :31 quoted above. Fig 8 :39 *"So a search that finds nothing is never a proof of absence."* Fig 8 :32 *"Closure is idempotent. Composing inside a closed set never leaves it, so no search, however long, adds an atom."* Underivable in principle and unreachable at this budget are different facts with different instructions. Nothing here licenses a claim that a missing cause term does not exist.

The inward instrument would be a probe already returning something that fails to resolve which routing a person performed at the moment of attribution (a reaction-time or eye-tracking measure that distinguishes probe-aiming from re-fitting, for instance). The outward move would be a frame whose closure predicts the four labels' shape and whose terms can be cast in effect terms. Neither is performed in this simulated run.

**Mode:** `general`. **Status:** single-source for the rule (Fig 6 + Fig 8 triangulate the two instruments).

**Refuter:** produce a ground measurement that settles which bin a given attribution used, then check the derivation against it. This is the intended way to move the solution out of `specified`.

### F9  -  Filters are not witnesses

The four label words work as **filters for the budget** (which field to search, which questions to ask, which venue to try) and never as **witnesses for the verdict**. Fig 9 :60 *"Use filters for the budget and witnesses for the verdict. Never let a filter hand you a verdict."* The tier of a cause category, the fame of a researcher, and the receptiveness of a respondent are all contact or budget quantities. Fig 2 :42 *"Buy independence in proportion to the cost of being wrong."*

**Mode:** `general`. **Status:** triangulated (Fig 9 + Fig 2).

## 4. Pre-registered refuter

Written before the conclusion, per the exemplar's rule that a falsifier written before the run is a design instrument. The shape claim is refuted if any of the following holds:

1. A case in which the same observer with the same vocabulary routes a predicted event and a novel mechanism failure identically, with no difference in probe-aiming or term minting (breaks F1/F2).
2. A case in which the four conditions are genuinely mutually exclusive, so that affirming the supernatural denies the natural, with no observer able to hold both (breaks F5).
3. A population in which a refuted normative expectation leads to refusal of the norm and no character term is minted (breaks F4).
4. A supernatural attribution with no import, no provenance gap, and no wall move (breaks F3).
5. A cause whose social or supernatural classification is invariant across residuals and independent habitats (breaks F6).
6. A group-level attribution rate that predicts individual per-event binding without loss of live slots (breaks F7).

If any fires, the re-description is withdrawn or narrowed. Until one is run, no part of this is `grounded`.

## 5. Recommendation

1. Lead with the residual and the re-description, not with a verdict about which label is correct (F1, F8).
2. State the union explicitly; do not let the audience hear "one of four" when the derivation says "overlapping conditions" (F5).
3. Keep the supernatural case in the import domain with a source gate, and keep the moral case in the refusal domain with its mis-route marked (F3, F4).
4. Use the public submission and contact points in `03_public_contact_points.csv` and ask for a refutation, not agreement (F9).
5. Fig 4 :30 *"So take the round trip and measure the gap. Without it you are navigating by dead reckoning: confident about a position nobody checked."* Send the generator, that is the residual, the census and the refuter, not the four-label conclusion.
