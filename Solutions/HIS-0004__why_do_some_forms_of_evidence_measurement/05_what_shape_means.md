## 1. A shape is a constraint, not a ranking

The figures README states the limit: the framework returns "what a missing thing would have to look like (a shape, not a name)." Fig 9 :32: "you get a kind of thing, never the answer itself." Fig 13 :134: "It can say the answer is out of reach with the library as it stands, so something has to be imported before it is asked again." Fig 13 :135: "It cannot say which primitive is missing, because naming it would mean already holding it."

From *The Library of Babel* (2026-10-02): "you can cut your search short if the set of books returned is small and descriptive enough that it provides a general shape of the answer that satisfies your question." And: "The problem you have determines what makes a book relevant to you or not."

For HIS-0004 a **shape** is therefore not a league table of evidence forms. It is the narrowed form of the answer we can state before we can rank anything: authority follows non-updatable, independent contact with the ground that the context's question is constitutively about. That constrains every candidate answer without supplying the historical case list. A global rank ("measurement > testimony > tradition") is a name, not a shape, and it is the specific thing the method refuses.

## 2. How the shape was obtained here

- *Library of Babel*: use the vocabulary closest to the thing and recurse. "With each new term gained, you are able to narrow the space by definition and deepen your vocabulary."
- Fig 6 :30: "The description travels. Fails by being sharpened into terms only we can read. Cast it in effect terms." Fig 9 :30-31: "your words are borrowed too, so describe the effect rather than the cause." Hence the residual is stated as variation of authority, not as a theory of knowledge.
- *Decoding Tacit Knowledge* (2026-11-10): "When contact isn't possible, the best you can send is a plain description of what you'd expect to see, because anyone can check that against the world." The refuters in `01` are that plain description.
- *How Intelligence Actually Works* (2026-09-16): "The recursion converges toward indiscernibility, and indiscernibility is not identity." A shape can get good enough to check; it does not become the truth by getting sharp.
- The essays also name the stop condition. *Library of Babel*: "You will know that you are done because the finish line will also be the starting place, as there is nowhere else to search and nothing else that can describe the answer better." Here that happens when every further historical example returns to the same anchor test.

## 3. What licenses a "name" in this question

The tempting name is a winner: "measurement wins because it is objective." The essays give three reasons to refuse that move.

1. **The reading does not determine the source.** *The Hard Problem is About Source* (2026-08-28): "the instrument is speaking only about what it measured, it cannot comment on the source." Several sources can produce the same reading. So "this form is authoritative" read off an outcome is a metamerism error; what is owed is a discriminator, not a label. The same essay: "It was proven wrong not by argument, but by the arrival of a better instrument."
2. **Outputs are not generators.** *They're Scanning Books When They Should Be Studying Nature* (2026-09-06): "A pile of human reasoning's outputs is not an engine." A list of authoritative forms is an output of past practice, not the generator of authority.
3. **Declared terms are visible choices, not neutral facts.** Same essay: "Declaring the primitives does not make them neutral. It makes the choice visible." Fig 13 :16 "it names what is being measured" and :26 "it decides what the measurement would mean": a measurement needs both a question and an interpretation, so no form carries authority by itself.

Correctly licensed names here are the *conditions*: non-updating reference, independent contact, question-appropriate anchor, re-ranged inheritance. Those are structural, checkable and independent of any particular historical case.

## 4. Why "context" is not a social fog but a selection of ground

*Decoding Tacit Knowledge*: "Two people who only check against each other drift, and they drift together, so neither notices." And: "Agreement is the whole point when you're settling what words mean. It's worthless for settling what's true when both sides learned from the same sources." Fig 2 :36: "An anchor is legitimate when the question is constitutively about it." So the word "context" in the question earns its keep: it names which ground is being asked about, and that selection decides which form can be an anchor.

*How a Society of Agents Would Work* (2026-10-22): "The group's agreement is never the verdict." And: "Ten copies watching the same thing are one opinion said ten times." That is the mechanism behind testimony's context sensitivity, from a different essay in the same set.

*Arrival of the Fittest: The Tether Architecture* (2026-08-19) records the warning directly: outputs "could never be verified if the solutions presented were not contrived to fit the formula." That is why the refuter was written before the conclusion and why no historical case list is presented as confirmation.

## 5. Prestige is not action

From *The Recursive Self-Improvement Trap* (2026-11-19): "Prestige can fund an action. It can give an action a budget to be tried. But it cannot settle an action. Only the ground can settle it. The Ground Maintainer must be blind to prestige." Same essay: "You may all agree and be wrong. You may set your clock to someone else's, who was setting your clock to yours, and be off by many deviations over time."

Applied here: a form of evidence that becomes authoritative because respected people endorse it is the named collapse. Historical "great man" accounts of evidential standards mistake prestige for ground. The refuter F7 in `01` is written to catch exactly that.

## 6. My own audit is a simulation, not a ground reading

Same essay: "LLMs can quickly simulate an approximate answer/output with the formula found in the architecture post" and "the truly testable output would need to be derived from the real architecture or working the formula yourself." *Arrival of the Fittest* says LLM outputs "must be seen as simulacra."

So `01_figure_grounded_audit.md` is a simulated derivation, checked for internal consistency against the figures. It is **not** a test of the historical claim, not a survey of actual societies, and not a ground reading. It cannot settle anything it reports on. Every world source in `10_source_ledger.csv` was opened, but the five essays all share an author and evidence pool with the figures, so their agreement is one pool talking rather than independent corroboration (Fig 2 :40).

## 7. Correction to my own §3 (see `06_isomers_and_order.md`)

I initially read "shape, not a name" as forbidding the identification of *any* form. That is too broad. The canonical phrase governs "a missing thing", a primitive not held in Γ. Measurement, experiment, testimony and tradition are already held as ordinary terms in the historian's vocabulary; naming them is not minting a primitive. What the framework forbids is promoting one of them to a universal anchor or claiming the derivation produces a historical ranking. The narrower reading is kept in both sections, and the earlier over-broad reading is recorded here rather than erased.
