## 4. Round trip gap R_T

The Symbols table defines R_T as the gap between what was sent up and what came back down, `gap(x, (T_E ∘ T_A)(x))`, measuring what the coarser description cannot hold. Fig 4 :30 requires it to be measured rather than assumed zero. I ran the round trip on the condensation the folder actually sends.

1. **Sent up (x):** the verbatim figure set, chiefly Fig 2 :29, :36-38, Fig 4 :22, Fig 9 :54 and Fig 10 :24.
2. **Abstraction (T_A):** the shorthand carried in `07_the_tether_answer.md` and `08_expert_verification_packet.md`: authority follows non-updatable, independent contact with the ground the question is about; tradition is a head start; no global rank.
3. **Concretisation (T_E):** a concrete historical verdict, for example "measurement won in this dispute because it is objective."

**R_T is nonzero.** Three things the shorthand holds do not survive the trip back down:

- the **hidden third term** G. Compressing to a two-term slogan invites the reader to treat authority as a property of E alone, which is the error Fig 2 :36 blocks;
- the **independence test** (Fig 2 :31-32, :40). A slogan about contact can be satisfied by ten witnesses reading one report, which the figure disqualifies;
- the **channel condition** (Fig 10 :24-26) and the **tradition-as-head-start limit** (Fig 4 :22). Both drop out of a one-line rule, and both are where the historical variation actually lives.

Conversely, one thing survives cleanly: no global ranking of forms, and the selection of anchor by what the question is constitutively about. That is the residue worth sending. R_T was **not** measured numerically, because no ground reading exists; it is recorded qualitatively as the enumerated set of dropped constraints. Treating the slogan as identical to the derivation would set R_T to zero and would be the dead-reckoning error Fig 4 :30 names. The honest form is a lossy condensation with a measured gap, which is what `09_watson_answer.md` now carries.
