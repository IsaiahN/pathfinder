## QUESTION

- **Domain 1, measurement.** Actors: instruments, standards, operators, the measured system. Currency: readings, calibrations, uncertainties. Anchor: yes, the non-updating reference named in Fig 2 :29. Question type: which reading settles the quantity.
- **Domain 2, experiment.** Actors: hypotheses, interventions, anomalies. Currency: refusals and bracketed residuals. Anchor: yes, the outcome that can violate a prediction (Fig 9 :42). Question type: which held term must be refused.
- **Domain 3, testimony.** Actors: speakers, hearers, experts, witnesses. Currency: assurance, credibility, independent corroboration. Anchor: conditionally, human judgment for questions about people (Fig 2 :37). Question type: is the claim about persons or about a quantity.
- **Domain 4, tradition.** Actors: lineages, apprentices, institutions, inherited priors (w in the Symbols table). Currency: head starts, methods, conventions. Anchor: none of its own; authority borrowed from earlier contact (Fig 4 :11). Question type: is the inheritance being re-ranged or obeyed.
- **Order by dependency.** Perception → vocabulary → objective → planning → learn and carry (Fig 3 :4-15). Fig 3 :20 "The chain is ordered by dependency, not by importance." Refuse before mint (Formula step 2); describe the residual before naming a frame (Fig 9 :27-28); settle each first-order contact before composing compound contexts.

Strain test applied: Fig 2 :29 defines an anchor (non-updating) while Fig 2 :36 selects which anchor is legitimate for a question. Different quantities, so no strain after re-census. Tal's finding that standardization corrects tradition is not a contradiction of the tradition-as-head-start rule; the two govern the making of a standard and the use of an inherited verdict respectively.

## VERDICT (from the figures)

The shape survives; the global ranking does not. Authority follows non-updatable, independent contact with the ground that the context's question is constitutively about. Measurement and experiment can be anchors; testimony is the right anchor for questions about persons with separate contact; tradition is a head start that must be re-ranged. The four are a union of contact relations, not a taxonomy and not isomers. Prestige, credibility accrual and agreement from a shared pool are disqualified. Two sub-claims are single-source within the framework and recorded as such: the Fig 3 dependency order and tradition's lack of an anchor of its own. This result is `specified`, ungrounded and a candidate.

- **cost if wrong:** bounded and locatable. The packet states mode, pre-registered refuters and provenance before the conclusion, so an error is findable rather than invisible. The real cost is spending an outside reader's independence by sending a historical conclusion instead of a generator and a refuter. Fig 2 :42 "Buy independence in proportion to the cost of being wrong." That cost is avoidable by leading with the shape and its refuter.
- **undoable:** yes. The global ranking and the community-acceptance reading of authority are removable without touching the derivation. `07_the_tether_answer.md` and `08_expert_verification_packet.md` already remove them. No primitive has to be withdrawn, and a sent claim can be superseded by a corrected one.
- **reversed by:** a discriminating case, not agreement. A form whose answer moves under pressure yet keeps authority with no alternative anchor would break F1; maximally independent frames gaining nothing over a shared pool would break F2; a wrong-kind anchor settling finally with no loss would break F3; a dead channel still settling would break F4; a tradition deciding empirically as a convention would break F5; in-group credibility tracking authority with no contact change would break F7; a missing objective link reliably selecting the ground-ranged form would break F8. A new form with a clear, non-composed anchor would require a fresh census. Until one of these fires, the verdict remains `candidate`.
