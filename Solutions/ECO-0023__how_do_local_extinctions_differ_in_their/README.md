## The residual, as an effect

A species disappears. Two treatments are compared: (L) it vanishes from one habitat while remaining elsewhere, and (G) it vanishes everywhere. The question decides whether these two events differ only by degree or categorically, and in what the difference consists. An answer is useful if it names, in effect terms, what recolonization can and cannot repair, and which ecological consequences are at stake. The sought-for shape is bound as an ordinary term in `01_figure_grounded_audit.md` and `06_isomers_and_order.md`, so it can accrue a residual and be refused.

## What is deliberately not here

- No personal emails of named individuals.
- No ranking of people by how likely they are to agree with the claim.
- No bulk-send plan.

Three reasons, in the figures' own words:

1. **A room is not an anchor.** Fig 2 :35, *"Alignment is triangulation, not negotiation. A mirror cannot correct you."* Adding readers raises the vocabulary, not the ground.
2. **Selecting for receptiveness manufactures the disqualifying correlation.** Fig 2 :40, *"disqualifying for a verdict: agreement about the world is worthless when the agreeing frames share an evidence pool"*; Fig 2 :42, *"Buy independence in proportion to the cost of being wrong."*
3. **Asking for trust before a check spends the reader's independence.** Fig 10 :42, *"A verifier that can reconstruct the claim cannot verify it."*

Legally: scraping personal emails for unsolicited bulk outreach breaches GDPR and CAN-SPAM style rules in many jurisdictions. That is a practical failure on top of the structural one.

## Honest standing of this whole folder

This is a **simulated derivation checked for internal consistency against the figures**, not a ground reading. It cannot settle the ecological question it reports on. The figures decide the *shape* of the difference; the ecological content comes from world sources and from the ground. See `05_what_shape_means.md` §6 and the status line of every file.
