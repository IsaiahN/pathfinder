## 1. A shape is a constraint, not a filler

The canonical Figures README states the limit: the framework returns *"what a missing thing would have to look like (a shape, not a name)."* Fig 9 :32: *"you get a kind of thing, never the answer itself."*

From *The Library of Babel*, opened this session: *"You can cut your search short if the set of books returned is small and descriptive enough that it provides a general shape of the answer that satisfies your question."*

So a **shape** is the narrowed form of an answer you can state before you can name its contents. It constrains; it does not fill. For ECO-0023:

- **Shape:** the difference between local and global extinction is a categorical difference in source availability, and consequences are a vector indexed by habitat, function and time.
- **Name (not given by the shape):** which function declines, in which habitat, by how much, after how long.

## 2. How the shape is obtained

- *The Library of Babel*: use the vocabulary you already have that is *closest* to what you seek, look for recurring new terms that overlap or correlate with it, recurse. *"With each new term gained, you narrow the space and deepen your vocabulary."*
- Fig 9 :27-28: *"describe it before you go looking, or any frame you pick will seem to fit."* Fig 9 :30-31: *"your words are borrowed too, so describe the effect rather than the cause."*
- The carried reading of *Decoding Tacit Knowledge*: when contact is not possible, send a plain description of what you would expect to see, because anyone can check that against the world.
- *The Library of Babel* on bundling: *"Perhaps your problem was a bundle or conflation of several problems masquerading as one?"* That is the figure's union/partition rule (Fig 9 :54) stated in essay form, and it is why `06_isomers_and_order.md` unbundles local versus global before deriving.
- *The Library of Babel* on stopping: *"You can cut your search short"* once the returned set is small and descriptive enough. The paradox recorded there is that by the time you have the terms, you have *"already composed the answer through the process of locating it."*

## 3. What licenses a name here

Only the ground, or a provenanced import from a frame whose closure differs (Fig 6 :23). For this question:

- A **function-level** name (pollination, seed set, predation rate) is licensed by measurement in a named habitat.
- A **global status** name ("extinct") is always `candidate`, because no survey is a sealed room (Fig 9 :44) and the rediscovery record is real (Smith-Patten et al. 2015).
- The framework itself licenses neither. It returns a shape.

*The Hard Problem is About Source*, opened this session, gives the mechanism for why a reading is not a name: *"If several paths create the same behavior, then behavior is a projection with lossy information."* An instrument reading "function intact" *"cannot comment on the source"*; several underlying causes can produce the same reading. And: *"The answer is unreached until a better instrument exists."* Applied here: observing that a habitat still "functions" after a loss does not identify which source (which species, which interaction) is doing the work. The right response is a better instrument, not a name.

## 4. Why the union reading matters for "shape"

Fig 9 :54: *"And when the split lands on a union rather than a partition, unbundle before you search again."* A bundled question ("how much worse is global than local?") hides two conditions: the removal, and the availability of a source. A single lever on removal leaves the source condition standing. The shape that survives unbundling is: **local = removal with source; global = removal without source.** That is a kind of thing, not an answer about any particular species.

## 5. The consequence vector is a shape, not an average

Fig 1 :19: *"The residual is indexed by object slot, not aggregated."* The object slots for this question are habitats, functions and times (Fig 11 :46-47 names conditions and relations as the habitat's read-off list). The shape therefore requires a vector. Anyone who reports a single "ecological consequence score" for an extinction has thrown away the signal (Fig 1 :22). *Functional extinction* as a category (opened this session) confirms the function slot: *"the reduced population no longer plays a significant role in ecosystem function."*

## 6. Prestige is not action, and this audit is a simulation

From the carried reading of *The Recursive Self-Improvement Trap*: *"Prestige must never be allowed to validate an action directly... Prestige can fund an action. It can give an action a budget to be tried. But it cannot settle an action. Only the ground can settle it."* A list of famous ecologists who agree with the shape is the named collapse, not corroboration.

Same essay: *"LLMs can quickly simulate an approximate answer/output... However, the truly testable output would need to be derived from the real architecture or working the formula yourself."* LLM output is *"simulacra."* So `01_figure_grounded_audit.md` is a simulated derivation, checked for internal consistency against the figures. It is **not** a test of the ecological claim and must be labelled as such. It cannot settle anything it reports on.
