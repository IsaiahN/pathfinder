## 1. A shape is a constraint, not a filler

`FRAMEWORK/reference/FIGURES_README_canonical.md` states the limit: the framework returns "what a missing thing would have to look like (a shape, not a name)". Fig 9 :32: "you get a kind of thing, never the answer itself". Fig 13 :132 to :135: "A system with a closure can answer that before it searches, and what it returns is a shape rather than a name", and "Unreachable is a verdict here, not a silence".

*The Library of Babel*: "This means that you can cut your search short if the set of books returned is small and descriptive enough that it provides a general shape of the answer that satisfies your question."

For EAR-0029 the shape is not a map of sea level. It is the set of constraints any correct regional answer must satisfy: per-slot, union, sign relative to the slot, cascade outward, and settled by instruments rather than agreement. It narrows; it does not fill.

## 2. How the shape was obtained here

- *Library of Babel* on vocabulary by proximity: "If you don't know the right terms to search for, you will have to use the existing vocabulary you do know that is closest to the words that you wish to describe and grasp the shape of." and "With each new term gained, you narrow the space by definition and deepen your vocabulary."
- *Library of Babel* on a bundling: "Perhaps your problem was a bundle or conflation of several problems masquerading as one? You will need to search all of those problems individually to find their answers and then combine them in a satisfactory way." This is the essay-level source for the union finding F2.
- Fig 9 :27-28: "describe it before you go looking, or any frame you pick will seem to fit." The residual was described in effect terms before oceanography was searched. The world frame was then asked to corroborate the shape, not to supply it.
- *Decoding Tacit Knowledge*: "When contact isn't possible, the best you can send is a plain description of what you'd expect to see, because anyone can check that against the world." This is why the outreach email leads with what you would expect to observe at a coast, not with the framework.
- *Library of Babel* on stopping: "You will know that you are done because the finish line will also be the starting place", and "you have already composed the answer through the process of locating it." Here the stop is the shape plus the measurement plan, not a regional map.

## 3. What licenses a name in this solution

Two things, both outside the shape itself:

1. **The ground settles it.** `THE_FORMULA.md` step 5: "the ground settles it. Verified only by what pays. A gate is not the ground." The figures README: the framework "stops at the limit of what the available instruments can distinguish, and records the lineage so the stop is honest."
2. **A provenanced import from a frame whose closure differs** (Fig 6 :23, Fig 8 :50 to :52), with a source gate and a shadow test.

*They're Scanning Books When They Should Be Studying Nature*: "The real import is not a new term in the existing space. It is a new dimension of the space." And: "A minimum viable set of six terms is a human's careful guess at reality's compression axes; another sensorium's minimum set need not be those six." Also: "Declaring the primitives does not make them neutral. It makes the choice visible, which is worth a great deal".

So the six are a visible guess, not nature's own list. Treating the per-slot shape as a law of the ocean rather than as a figure consequence would turn a visible choice into an invisible one.

### Correction to my own §3 (kept visible)

I initially wrote that "shape, not a name" forbids naming any mechanism in this solution. That was overstated, and the same correction was made in the exemplar's `05` §5b. The README's phrase governs a **missing primitive**, one not held in the library. Ocean circulation, gravitational/rotational/deformational response and vertical land motion are **world-frame mechanisms**, not candidate seventh primitives of the six-term set. Naming them is a provenanced binding from the Earth-science frame, explicitly marked `specified`, not an invention of a new atom and not an import that moves the figure wall. What the figures forbid is claiming that the framework *derives* which mechanisms operate, or that it produces their regional coefficients. Both the original statement and the correction are recorded so neither is quietly lost.

## 4. The specific error this question invites: a global mean read as a source

*The Hard Problem is About Source* gives the mechanism. An instrument reads green and "cannot comment on the source": one green light, or yellow plus blue, or RGB, or CMYK, or a scheme not yet discovered. "If several paths create the same behavior, then behavior is a projection with lossy information". And: "The answer is unreached until a better instrument exists." It was the spectrometer, not an argument, that resolved it.

Applied here: a global mean sea-level number is a projection. Circulation changes, gravitational fingerprints, land subsidence and thermal expansion can sum to the same global value while producing entirely different regional patterns. Reading the source (which mechanism, which coast) off the global number is the metamerism error. What is owed is a source-discriminating instrument at the slot, not a better average. Fig 6 :32: "An instrument is not built from a description. It is improved from a worse instrument already returning something." Tide gauges, altimeters, GNSS and GRACE are the worse instruments already returning something (see F7 in `01_figure_grounded_audit.md`).

*How Intelligence Actually Works* on provenance loss: "often, very little about a composed term tells you, from looking at it alone, which route produced it." The global number records almost none of the route. The lineage, meaning the per-slot series and the instrument provenance, is the thing that makes the claim checkable.

## 5. Shape answered for EAR-0029

**What is the shape of the answer the Tether returns?** A vector of per-slot residuals with these properties:

- indexed by coastal slot, never replaced by a scalar (Fig 1);
- a union of overlapping conditions, unbundled before being searched (Fig 9);
- each driver's sign relative to the slot, with a near-field reversal and a far-field excess for ice loss (Fig 11);
- a causal chain that reaches the coast three steps out (Fig 11);
- a spatial gradient and a temporal rate kept as different terms (Fig 13);
- settled, slot by slot, by low-overlap instruments ranging against the ground (Fig 2, Fig 8).

**What is not in the shape?** The numerical field, the dominant driver at a named coast, and any confidence interval. Those are ground results. The framework returns the kind of thing, not the thing (Fig 9 :32).

**Does the framework produce a name?** No. When it returns a shape to an outside frame, the mechanisms are supplied by that frame with provenance and declared `specified`. Naming them is the world frame's contribution, not the framework's.

## 6. Two consequences for outreach and status

**Prestige is not action.** *The Recursive Self-Improvement Trap*: "Prestige can fund an action. It can give an action a budget to be tried. But it cannot settle an action. Only the ground can settle it. The Ground Maintainer must be blind to prestige." A list of authoritative sea-level researchers who agree with the shape is a convention, not an anchor. The legitimate ask gives the shape a budget to be checked, and lets the ground, a measurement at a named coast, settle it.

**My own audit is a simulation, not a ground reading.** Same essay: "LLMs can quickly simulate an approximate answer/output with the formula found in the architecture post. It will often be better than the generic outputs that you will usually get otherwise too (intelligence is essentially arrangement). However, the truly testable output would need to be derived from the real architecture or working the formula yourself." *Arrival of the Fittest* adds the demo caveat: outputs "could never be verified if the solutions presented were not contrived to fit the formula." So `01_figure_grounded_audit.md` is a simulated derivation checked for internal consistency against the figures. It is **not** a test of the shape and must be labelled as such. It cannot settle anything it reports on.
