## What the wider literature bears on

1. **The three named mechanisms are assessed, not figure-derived.** IPCC AR6 names exactly ocean dynamics, gravity/rotation/deformation, and vertical land motion as the sources of regional difference. This is treated as `specified` world corroboration of the figure shape, never as a figure verdict (Fig 9 :60: "Use filters for the budget and witnesses for the verdict. Never let a filter hand you a verdict.").
2. **The dynamic part has zero global mean.** That is the per-slot finding in physical form: a driver can carry all the regional structure while adding nothing to the global total (Fig 1 :19).
3. **The fingerprint reversal is observed, not assumed.** Harvard Gazette reports a one-to-one match between predicted and satellite-observed pattern with more than 99.9 percent confidence. Mitrovica is quoted: "Ocean level projections, urban and coastal planning - all of it - has been built on the idea of fingerprints." Note that this is a single result touching one ice sheet; it corroborates the valence-relative shape, it does not settle every coast.
4. **The instrument split is the triangulation.** NASA describes tide gauges and altimeters as measuring different reference frames, and explicitly uses their difference to infer land motion. This is low overlap in the sense of Fig 2 :31, so their agreement carries information.
5. **Deep uncertainty remains.** IPCC notes low confidence in quantitative AMOC projections and deep uncertainty in ice-sheet instabilities. The figure verdict does not depend on those numbers; the honest stop in F7 does.

- **Fig 10 :52** "Bring a characterised residual, never the source." Send the per-slot claim and its five refuters first. Do not attach the framework.
- **Fig 10 :42** "A verifier that can reconstruct the claim cannot verify it." A recipient who has absorbed the framework before giving a verdict has joined the frame being checked.
- **Fig 9 :27-28** "describe it before you go looking, or any frame you pick will seem to fit." Ask what they would expect to see at a coast if the claim were true before showing any diagram.
- **Fig 2 :40** "agreement about the world is worthless when the agreeing frames share an evidence pool". Record the stage-1 verdict before any theory is shown, and never pool later agreement as corroboration.
- **Fig 7 :13** "agreement compounds, evidence does not". A room of colleagues who share the same IPCC assessment is a convention, not an anchor.
- **Seat map:** "High correlation is disqualifying for a verdict and efficient for a convention." Do not rank recipients by how likely they are to agree.

**Recommended order in any reply:** (1) the per-slot shape claim and its refuter; (2) ask what they would count as a failure at a named coast; (3) record that verdict; (4) only then offer the four or five links, with the note that the figures, not this summary, are the authority.

## The two-stage offer, with a third optional stage

1. **Stage 1.** Send the residual in effect terms and the pre-registered refuter. Ask for a refutation of the shape. No framework material.
2. **Stage 2, opt-in only.** If they are curious, offer the figure text, the canonical usage rule and the four verified physical-science links. Their stage-1 verdict stays recorded separately, because after stage 2 their agreement is no longer independent.
3. **Stage 3, optional.** Offer the formula as a method to reuse. Fig 4 :22: "Only methods cross up. Only head starts come down." Each user writes their own refuter and states their own mode. *The Recursive Self-Improvement Trap*: "Metatheories cannot validate themselves." A method is not a verdict.

**Hard precondition:** stages 1 to 3 only work if the stage-1 output survives. The global-mean confusion, the union-versus-partition question and the valence reversal in `01_figure_grounded_audit.md` F1 to F3 must be stated cleanly before anything is sent.
