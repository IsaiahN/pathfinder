# Expert verification packet : the Tether on regional sea-level rise

**What this is:** a claim in a form a sea-level researcher can check. Mode, derivation and refuters are stated before the conclusion, so a reader knows what to look for and what would settle it.

**Mode: `general` in shape, `specified` in the world binding of the three mechanisms, not `grounded`.** No regional map or coefficient is asserted. This is a shape and a measurement plan.

## 1. The claim, in one paragraph

Sea-level rise is not one number with local corrections. The live residual is indexed by coastal slot, so a near-zero global residual and a live coastal residual are simultaneously legal states. The three named drivers, ocean circulation, gravitational/rotational/deformational response, and vertical land motion, form a union of overlapping conditions, not a partition and not one hard problem. The sign of each driver is relative to the slot: ice loss can lower relative sea level near the ice sheet and raise it above the global average far away, and the chain reaches the coast three steps out. The spatial pattern and the time rate are distinct terms. Two low-overlap instruments, tide gauges and satellite altimeters, triangulate the slot; their disagreement is information about land motion. The framework returns this shape and the measurement plan, not a numerical field.

## 2. Three checkable sub-claims

### C1 : framework-internal; checkable by reading Figure 1
- **Statement:** the residual is per slot; aggregation destroys the live signal. A global mean near zero with one live coastal slot is a legal state, not an inert one.
- **Source, verbatim:** Fig 1 :19 "The residual is indexed by object slot, not aggregated." Fig 1 :22 "Aggregating across slots is how a live signal disappears into an average." Fig 1 :28 "The gap is measured per object, not per step. A system that averages across objects has thrown away the only signal that says where to look."
- **What to check:** is 'coastal slot' a legitimate instantiation of 'object slot' in this figure, or does the figure govern only an agent's internal bookkeeping? If the latter, the shape claim loses its primary figure line.
- **Refuter:** a global mean plus fixed local offsets predicts observed per-slot sea level within instrument error at every coast.

### C2 : physically facing; checkable against assessed sea-level science
- **Statement:** the drivers form a union whose intersections are real, and each driver's sign is relative to the slot. Ice loss produces a near-field fall and a far-field excess. The chain reaches extreme water levels three steps out.
- **Source, figure for the structure:** Fig 9 :54 "And when the split lands on a union rather than a partition, unbundle before you search again." Fig 11 :49 "An actor's valence is relative to the residual, not a property it carries." Fig 11 :52 "And contact cascades. Scoping the habitat to direct contact is how DDT reached the eagles: first-order enumeration, second-order effect, and the residual that mattered was three steps out."
- **Physically facing source (secondary, `specified`):** IPCC AR6 WG1 Ch9: "Regional differences in sea level arise from: ocean dynamics; changes in Earth gravity, rotation and deformation due to land ice and land-water changes; and vertical land motion." NASA: "This causes sea levels to lower near the ice sheet and rise in more distant locations." Harvard Gazette 2022: "Logic might suggest that nearby levels would rise, but instead they fall. Thousands of miles away, however, they do go up in a kind of seesaw effect."
- **What to check:** are the intersections genuinely nonempty (GRD contains deformation; sterodynamic contains steric and dynamic), and is the near-field reversal actually observed rather than only modelled?
- **Refuter:** disjoint slot sets with empty intersections; or an ice-loss event that raises relative sea level equally everywhere.

### C3 : measurement facing; checkable against the observing systems
- **Statement:** tide gauges and satellite altimeters are low-overlap frames, so their comparison is triangulation rather than two versions of one reading. Their difference locates vertical land motion. The source-discriminating instrument for separating drivers at one slot is still owed.
- **Source, verbatim:** Fig 2 :31 "Independence, between two frames, is how little they draw on the same evidence." Fig 8 :45 "Two frames together reach what neither reaches alone." Fig 6 :32 "An instrument is not built from a description. It is improved from a worse instrument already returning something."
- **Physically facing source (secondary, `specified`):** NASA, *How Do We Measure Sea Level Change?*: "Tide gauges measure relative sea level, while satellites provide a measurement of absolute or geocentric sea level. By comparing the two, we can get a better understanding of where coastal subsidence or upflift is playing a big role."
- **What to check:** is the overlap genuinely low? If the altimeter product is calibrated or corrected using the same tide-gauge network, the independence claim weakens. Also: does any existing product already separate the three contributions at one slot?
- **Refuter:** a demonstrated shared evidence pool, or a per-slot discriminator that already exists (which would move the `gap` in C4, not break the shape).

### C4 : the honest stop (not a sub-claim to be proved, but a limit to be tested)
- **Statement:** the numerical regional field is not derivable from the figure closure. The framework returns a shape and a measurement plan. "Unreachable is a verdict here, not a silence" (Fig 13 :134).
- **Refuter:** a per-slot field derived from the six-term set alone, with no imported Earth state and no instrument.

## 3. What is explicitly not claimed

- Not a theory of everything and not a final frame. The figures README forbids a last room; Fig 7 names "the limit: no last room".
- Not a regional map, not a coefficient per coast, not a dominant-driver ranking.
- Not that the three mechanisms are figure-derived. IPCC supplies them; the figures supply the shape.
- Not that the global mean is wrong. It is a legitimate aggregate indicator and a useful input.
- Not `grounded`. No measurement was taken by this derivation.
- Not a claim that agreement among researchers corroborates the shape. Shared evidence is one witness (Fig 2 :40).
