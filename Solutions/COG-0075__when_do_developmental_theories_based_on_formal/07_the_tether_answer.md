# The Tether's answer: when does a schooling-based developmental theory generalize?

**Mode: `specified` on the four conditions and on the per-domain verdicts, `general` on the structural shape, not `grounded`.** Simulated derivation checked for internal consistency against the figures (`05_what_shape_means.md` §6, §7). No target-setting measurement was taken, so nothing here is settled.

## 1. The residual it answers

A theory abstracted from children in formal schooling is used to predict a child raised in another setting. The prediction misses in some settings and pays in others. The residual is indexed per setting, never averaged: Fig 1 :19: "The residual is indexed by object slot, not aggregated." Fig 1 :22: "Aggregating across slots is how a live signal disappears into an average." The question "when do they generalize" is a union of overlapping conditions (Fig 9 :55), so one lever does not clear it.

## 2. The shape

1. **What crosses the membrane decides the case.** Fig 4 :22: "Only methods cross up. Only head starts come down." Fig 4 :23: "upward: a method that can be reapplied. Never the recording of a particular success." A theory generalizes when what is carried is the re-applicable kernel and its binding arrangement, not the age at which schooled children passed a test. Fig 4 :25: "A recording carried upward looks like knowledge and is a description of one occasion."
2. **The target habitat must reproduce what the theory's goal required.** Fig 11 :67: "Move the residual and you must reproduce everything its goal required, and you inherit the new habitat's actors, which you did not choose." Fig 11 :68: "what you failed to reproduce is invisible until the goal fails." Fig 4 :34-35: "The level a method lands on remakes it. A seed carries no small tree: it carries what will grow one, and the conditions it lands in decide what it becomes." Where the conditions are absent, the theory has no ground against which to pay.
3. **Terms must travel as effects, not as schooling readings.** Fig 9 :30-31: "your words are borrowed too, so describe the effect rather than the cause." Fig 6 :30: "The description travels. Fails by being sharpened into terms only we can read. Cast it in effect terms." A test score is a reading of a projection, not a source; several habitat paths can produce it.
4. **The ground is the target observation, and the frames must be independent.** Fig 2 :29: "An anchor is whatever settles the question and does not change its answer because of what any frame thinks." Fig 2 :35: "Alignment is triangulation, not negotiation. A mirror cannot correct you." Fig 2 :40: "agreement about the world is worthless when the agreeing frames share an evidence pool." More schooled samples are one long room: Fig 7 :12-13, "same closure, handed along; agreement compounds, evidence does not."
5. **Which theories and which cultures is a `gap`.** Fig 9 :32: "you get a kind of thing, never the answer itself." Fig 13 :134: "Unreachable is a verdict here, not a silence." The framework returns the separator and the instruments that would close each condition; it does not name a universal milestone table.
6. **A failed theory owes a refusal for that slot.** Fig 5 :53: "refuted: a term we hold is wrong; a term is owed that says so." Operators table :58: "a refutation is a negation, which is why a vocabulary without it can only accumulate." A moderator that does not change contact is not a correction (Fig 11 :40: "An improvement that does not change contact changes nothing, however much it improves.").

All six are kept separate. None is averaged into a single generalization verdict.

## 3. What it explicitly does not say

- Not that any named developmental theory is universal, and not that any named culture lacks a competence. The name is a binding from the developmental-science frame, provenanced as such (Fig 8 :50-52).
- Not that measurement equivalence decides the question. Fig 9 :60: "Use filters for the budget and witnesses for the verdict. Never let a filter hand you a verdict." Measurement equivalence is an INWARD instrument question (Formula step 7), not the verdict.
- Not that a group average tracks the per-setting residuals. Fig 1 :13 makes a global residual near zero with one live slot a legal state.
- Not that "generalizes" is symmetric. Operators table :33: "swap them. Does it become a different thing? Yes." The source-to-target crossing differs from the reverse.
- Not `grounded`. No source-discriminating instrument in a target setting was run.

## 4. What would settle it, the ground

A source-discriminating instrument, not a further argument. From *The Hard Problem is About Source* (opened 2026-11-22): an instrument that reads green "cannot comment on the source"; it was "the spectrometer, not an argument," that resolved the case. Here:

- **Kernel test (C1).** Show that a named theory's cross-habitat prediction is a re-applicable method with its arrangement intact, and specify what would count as a replay of one occasion in that theory. This determines whether the theory carries a kernel or a recording.
- **Habitat test (C2).** Enumerate settings, customs and caretaker psychology in the target habitat (Super and Harkness's three subsystems, imported with provenance) and check which of the theory's goal-required conditions are present. The instrument is a habitat census, not a new argument.
- **Effect-term test (C3).** Restate the theory's prediction as what these children do under these routine circumstances, and check it there. A term that can only be read inside the classroom is sharpened into terms only we can read (Fig 6 :30).
- **Independence test (C4).** Use a frame that did not draw on the schooling evidence pool; record the provenance of any import (Fig 8 :50-52) and debit independence accordingly.
- **Per-setting verdict (all).** Read R for each setting separately (Fig 1 :19). Report the live slots, not a universality percentage.
- **Refuter fired.** If the target observation shows the prediction pays with the goal-required conditions absent, withdraw C2; if two frames sharing one evidence pool are shown to corroborate independently, withdraw C4. Standing refuters are in `01` §4 and `08` §2.

## 5. The honest stop

**Unreached, not unreachable.** The developmental theory's declared universals are "a human's careful guess at reality's compression axes; another sensorium's minimum set need not be those six" (*They're Scanning Books*, opened 2026-11-22). Recording the choice makes it visible, not neutral. The answer stops at the limit of the available instruments, and the lineage is recorded so the stop is checkable. "The recursion converges toward indiscernibility, and indiscernibility is not identity" (*How Intelligence Actually Works*, opened 2026-11-22). The round-trip gap R_T is nonzero and enumerated in `01` §7: which part of a theory is kernel, which conditions its goal required, whether the checking frame is independent, and the per-slot residual do not survive the condensation into a universal claim.

## 6. One-paragraph version for outreach

> The Tether does not name a universal developmental theory and does not say that children in one setting are behind children in another. It says that a schooled-child theory generalizes only when what crosses into the new setting is a re-applicable method, not a recording of a classroom occasion; when the settings, customs and caretaker psychology the theory's goal required are reproduced there; when its terms describe the effect a child produces rather than the test that read it; and when the observation in that setting is an independent ground read per setting, not an average over cultures. It refuses to name which theory or which culture wins, because the framework returns a shape, not a name. What it offers is the shape any answer must have, the refuters written in advance, and the instruments that would close each condition. The center stays open until a source-discriminating instrument in that setting settles it. This is a `specified`, ungrounded candidate from a simulated derivation, not a ground reading.
