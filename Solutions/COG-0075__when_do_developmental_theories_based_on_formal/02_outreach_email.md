# Outreach draft: check the separator before you decide whether the framing is worth reading

**Use:** one to one, or as a cover letter attached to a submission through the public venues in `03_public_contact_points.csv`. **Not** for bulk send.

**Hard precondition:** do not send until the shape claim in `01_figure_grounded_audit.md` §3 is stated in the recipient's own terms and the refuter in §4 is written down where they can see it. The two-stage offer collapses on contact if the stage-1 object does not survive.

---

**Subject:** One checkable claim about when a schooled-child theory travels

> Hi [name],
>
> I have one specific output I would like you to check against what you already know. I am not asking you to read the framing first and I am not asking you to agree with it. If the output does not survive, there is nothing else to look at.
>
> **The output (`specified`, not `grounded`):** "a developmental theory generalizes from formal schooling to another setting" is not one question with a yes or no answer. It is a union of four conditions, and they have to be checked separately:
>
> 1. What crosses into the new setting is a re-applicable method, not a recording of what schooled children did on one occasion.
> 2. The new habitat reproduces whatever the theory's goal required. Otherwise the failure is silent until the goal fails.
> 3. The terms describe the effect the child produces, not the schooling-specific instrument that read it.
> 4. The observation in the new setting is an independent reading, and the residual is indexed per setting rather than averaged into a universality rate.
>
> This is a shape, not a name. It does not pick a theory, a culture or an age. It says what any answer of that kind would have to look like and what would settle each condition.
>
> **What I am not claiming:** that any named developmental theory is universal, that there is a culture-free milestone table, that a measurement-equivalence test decides whether a theory generalizes, or that this note is a result. No target-setting measurement was taken.
>
> **What would refute the shape claim:**
> 1. a cross-habitat prediction shown to be a re-applicable method with its arrangement intact and none of the source habitat's goal-required conditions reproduced;
> 2. a group average shown to track every per-setting residual with no live slot hidden;
> 3. a schooling-bound term shown to pay against a different setting's ground without an effect-term description;
> 4. two frames drawing on one schooled evidence pool shown to give independent corroboration;
> 5. a target-setting measurement showing the prediction pays with the goal-required conditions absent.
>
> I wrote these before sending this. If any one holds, I withdraw the condition it touches. A one-line "this fails because..." is more useful to me than agreement. If you would rather not engage, no reply is needed.
>
> **If it survives, and only then:** the sources are below. The figure text is the authority, not my summary of it.
> - Canonical figures and formula (local): `FRAMEWORK/reference/03-FIGURES_TEXT.md` and `FRAMEWORK/figures/THE_FORMULA.md`
> - Canonical usage rule (local): `FRAMEWORK/reference/FIGURES_README_canonical.md`
> - Public figures repository: https://github.com/IsaiahN/tether/tree/general-agent/figures
>
> **If you want to use the machinery:** the formula is in the figures link. Run it on a problem you know well and can check against a ground. Write down what would refute your result *before* you run it, and state the mode of what you get. A reported failure is more useful to me than a reported success.
>
> Thanks,
> [name]

## Why this version is ordered this way

| the move | figure-grounded reason |
|---|---|
| Output first, framing second | Fig 10 :52 - "Bring a characterised residual, never the source." Frames range against the ground separately (Fig 2 :45). |
| Do not ask for agreement | Fig 2 :35 - "Alignment is triangulation, not negotiation. A mirror cannot correct you." |
| Pre-registered refuter | A falsifier written before the run is a design instrument, not an honesty device. |
| Mode stated on every claim | `THE_FORMULA.md` :107 - "Mode. general · specified · grounded. State which." |
| Four conditions kept separate | Fig 9 :57 - "A case in two conditions is not a harder case of either." Never average the verdicts. |
| No universal culture list | Fig 9 :32 - "you get a kind of thing, never the answer itself." The canonical rule is a shape, not a name. |
| Effect terms, not schooling readings | Fig 9 :30-31 - "your words are borrowed too, so describe the effect rather than the cause." |
| Refusal offered, not a moderator | Operators table :58 - "a refutation is a negation, which is why a vocabulary without it can only accumulate." |
| Measurement equivalence after the verdict | Fig 9 :60 - "Use filters for the budget and witnesses for the verdict. Never let a filter hand you a verdict." |
| No request for trust or prestige | The framework's own rule: prestige can fund an action, it cannot settle one. Only the ground settles it. |
| Offer the formula as a method to reuse | Fig 4 :22 - "Only methods cross up. Only head starts come down." |
| Ask users to name their own ground and refuter | `THE_FORMULA.md` "Direction": a frame cannot check the direction of its own question. |
