## 1. A shape is a constraint, not a filler

The canonical README states the limit: the framework returns "what a missing thing would have to look like (a shape, not a name)." Fig 9 :32: "you get a kind of thing, never the answer itself." Fig 13 :134-135: "Unreachable is a verdict here, not a silence," and "It cannot say which primitive is missing, because naming it would mean already holding it."

From *The Library of Babel* (opened 2026-11-22): "you will need to use the existing vocabulary you do know that is closest to the words that you wish to describe and grasp the shape of." And the stopping rule: "you can cut your search short if the set of books returned is small and descriptive enough that it provides a general shape of the answer that satisfies your question."

So a **shape** is the form an answer must have, statable before you can name the answer. It constrains; it does not fill. For this question:

- A shape is the four-condition separator in `01` §3: kernel versus recording, habitat reproduction, effect terms, independent per-setting ground.
- A name would be "theory X generalizes to culture Y at age Z." The figures return the first, not the second.

## 2. How a shape is obtained

- *Library of Babel*: bootstrapping by proximity. "With each new term gained, you are able to narrow the space by definition and deepen your vocabulary. It's this recursive process that will help you converge on the answer." Here the known terms are "setting," "caregiver," "task," "milestone"; the recurring terms reached by proximity are "habitat," "settings and customs," "effect description," "independent frame."
- Fig 6 :30: "The description travels. Fails by being sharpened into terms only we can read. Cast it in effect terms." Fig 9 :30-31: "your words are borrowed too, so describe the effect rather than the cause."
- *Decoding Tacit Knowledge* (opened 2026-11-22) is the governing essay for this question: "When contact isn't possible, the best you can send is a plain description of what you'd expect to see, because anyone can check that against the world." That is exactly the cross-cultural method problem. A schooled-child theory carries context and subtext but almost no pretext; the target setting's caregivers and work patterns are the missing pretext. The essay adds: "Writing carries the context well and the subtext partly, but it almost never carries the pretext, the 'you had to be there' part." A theory abstracted from classroom behavior is a compressed message whose pretext is the classroom, and the pretext does not travel.
- *Library of Babel* on stopping: "You will know that you are done because the finish line will also be the starting place, as there is nowhere else to search and nothing else that can describe the answer better."

## 3. What licenses a name

Only two things, both outside the shape itself:

1. **The ground settles it.** `THE_FORMULA.md` step 5: "SETTLE the ground settles it. Verified only by what pays. A gate is not the ground." The README: the framework "stops at the limit of what the available instruments can distinguish, and records the lineage so the stop is honest." Here that would be a measurement in the target setting showing a named theory's prediction pays under a named habitat.
2. **A provenanced import from a frame whose closure differs** (Fig 6 :23, Fig 8 :50-52), with a source gate and a shadow test. Importing the target habitat's own frame (caregiver psychology, local work, play, storytelling) is legitimate, but the record must show where it came from and what gap it explains.

*They're Scanning Books When They Should Be Studying Nature* (opened 2026-11-22) sharpens this: "The real import is not a new term in the existing space. It is a new dimension of the space." And: "A minimum viable set of six terms is a human's careful guess at reality's compression axes; another sensorium's minimum set need not be those six." Also: "Declaring the primitives does not make them neutral. It makes the choice visible." A developmental theory's declared universals are a visible guess from one sensorium, not nature's own list.

## 4. Why the answer here is a separator and not a theory list

*The Hard Problem is About Source* (opened 2026-11-22) gives the mechanism. An instrument reads green and "cannot comment on the source." "If several paths create the same behavior, then behavior is a projection with lossy information." "An instrument that can't distinguish these cases demonstrates its own inadequacy." It was "the spectrometer, not an argument," that resolved it.

A schooled-child test score is a reading, not a source. Several habitat paths can produce the same score. Naming one as "the developmental stage the child has reached" is the metamerism error: a source named from a projection. What is owed is a source-discriminating instrument in the target setting, not a universal stage table.

*How Intelligence Actually Works* (opened 2026-11-22) gives the same point as provenance loss: "very little about a composed term tells you, from looking at it alone, which route produced it." And the seed example, which is the exact analogue for this question: "The seed is not the recording of a tree, it is instead the encoding of something that will grow one, wherever it lands in conditions that are hospitable, and the fate of its lineage changes based on where that happens." Fig 4 :34-35: "The level a method lands on remakes it. A seed carries no small tree: it carries what will grow one, and the conditions it lands in decide what it becomes."

*The Recursive Self-Improvement Trap* (opened 2026-11-22): "the output is only as good as the validator. The agent, or the tool, or the frame, or the theory, cannot validate itself." A theory validated only by the schooling frame that produced it is self-validating in the forbidden sense.

## 5. Q: What licenses a particular developmental theory as universal?

**Nothing in the framework.** The framework returns a shape. A particular theory is licensed only by (a) a ground reading in the target setting settling a minted term, or (b) a provenanced import with a shadow test. Until one of those, calling a theory universal is the searcher's own contribution and must be recorded as such. This matches `01` F8: the question "which theories and which cultures" is a declared `gap`, not a name.

Correction kept visible, as in the exemplar: the phrase "shape, not a name" governs a **missing** primitive. It does not forbid naming a reproduced habitat condition or a frame binding that is already available. So naming "caregiver psychology" or "local work" as conditions to reproduce is provenance, not an invented primitive. What is forbidden is claiming the framework derived a universal stage table from its own terms.

## 6. Two consequences for outreach

**Prestige is not action.** From *The Recursive Self-Improvement Trap*: "Prestige must never be allowed to validate an action directly." And: "Prestige can fund an action. It can give an action a budget to be tried. But it cannot settle an action. Only the ground can settle it. The Ground Maintainer must be blind to prestige." A campaign gathering famous developmental psychologists to endorse the shape is the named collapse. The legitimate ask is the opposite: give the claim a budget to be tried in a target setting and let that setting's observation settle it.

**My own audit is a simulation, not a ground reading.** Same essay: "LLMs can quickly simulate an approximate answer/output with the formula found in the architecture post... However, the truly testable output would need to be derived from the real architecture or working the formula yourself." So `01` is a simulated derivation, checked for internal consistency against the figures. It is not a test of any developmental theory and must be labelled as such. It cannot settle anything it reports on.

## 7. What would settle it, in the essay's own terms

From *Decoding Tacit Knowledge*: "The description travels. Fails by being sharpened into terms only we can read. Cast it in effect terms" (Fig 6 :30 quoted there). A checkable claim in this question is therefore an effect description: "under these settings, these customs and this caretaker psychology, these children produced this competence at this time." Anyone can check that against the world in that setting. The universal claim cannot be checked that way until the per-setting residuals are actually read, which this simulation did not do.
