## Why these sources, in figure terms

- **Developmental theory (D1).** Rogoff and Super and Harkness both name the same defect the figures name at the membrane: a theory built from one setting is a recording of one occasion unless the invariant is separated from the setting. Fig 4 :25.
- **Cross-cultural methods (D3).** The measurement invariance entry is the instrument track (INWARD), not the verdict track. Fig 9 :60 and Formula step 7.
- **Low-overlap corroboration.** The reaction norm comes from ecology and genetics and shares no evidence pool with developmental psychology. It corroborates the mechanism shape: the same starting structure yields different outcomes across environments, so "the child develops as the theory says" is a function of habitat, not of the theory alone. Fig 11 :39.
- **Bias and sampling.** Henrich et al. is a single influential review, not three witnesses; the three URLs that reproduce it are one paper. It corroborates F3 (per-slot residual) but does not decide the figure verdict.
- **Framework essays.** All are by the framework author, so their agreement with the figures is expected and carries no independent weight. They are used only for the canonical reading of "shape" and for the simulation/status warning.

No world source conflicts with the figure verdict. The one real disagreement, about invariance tests, was returned to the census and re-mapped to the instrument track (F7); it did not flip a verdict.

## Distribution: offer, do not push

- **Fig 4 :22-25** - "Only methods cross up. Only head starts come down. A recording carried upward looks like knowledge and is a description of one occasion." Sending the framing before anyone has described a residual sends a recording. Lead with the four conditions and the refuter.
- **Fig 10 :42** - "A verifier that can reconstruct the claim cannot verify it." A recipient who has absorbed the whole framework before being asked for a verdict has joined the frame being checked.
- **Fig 9 :27-28** - "describe it before you go looking, or any frame you pick will seem to fit." Ask what they would expect to see in a target setting before giving them any framework material.
- **Fig 2 :40** - "agreement about the world is worthless when the agreeing frames share an evidence pool." Do not select recipients for likely agreement and do not pool later agreement as corroboration.

**Recommended order in a reply:** (1) the residual and the four conditions; (2) the pre-registered refuter and the question "what would you count as a failure here?"; (3) record that verdict; (4) only then, if asked, the three framework links, with the note that the figure text, not the summary, is the authority.

## The two-stage offer

1. **Stage 1, before any theory.** Validate the output against the recipient's own ground. Fig 10 :52: "Bring a characterised residual, never the source." Fig 2 :45: "Frames range against the ground. They do not negotiate with it."
2. **Stage 2, opt-in only.** If they are curious after recording a verdict, offer the links. Once a recipient has absorbed the framework, their agreement stops being evidence, so the stage-1 verdict must be recorded first and later agreement is never pooled.
3. **Stage 3, method reuse.** The formula is a generator, so giving it away does not give away an answer (Fig 4 :22). Each user must name their own ground, write their own refuter before the run and state the mode of what they get (`THE_FORMULA.md` "Direction").

**Hard precondition:** stages 1 to 3 work only if the stage-1 object survives. The honest stage-1 object here is a four-condition separator with a declared gap, not a claim that any named theory generalizes. Send that, not a conclusion.
