## The question as an effect

A theory T is abstracted from children in formal schooling and used to predict a child raised in another setting. The prediction misses the observation in some settings and pays in others. The question asks for the conditions under which the cross-habitat transform preserves what matters. The answer bound as an ordinary term (Formula, "Direction") is: **generalization is contact-preserving transfer of a re-applicable kernel into a reproduced habitat, checked per setting against the ground, never a nameable list of cultures or a universal milestone table.** That term is written down here so it can accrue a residual like anything else and be refused.

## What is deliberately not here

- No ranking of people by how likely they are to agree with the shape claim.

Three reasons, in the figures' own words:

1. A room does not upgrade the label. Fig 7 :12-13: "same closure, handed along; agreement compounds, evidence does not." A list of sympathetic developmental psychologists is one room.
