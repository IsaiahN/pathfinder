## 1. A shape is a constraint, not a filler

The figures README states the limit: the framework returns *"what a missing thing would have to look like (a shape, not a name)."* Fig 9 :32: *"you get a kind of thing, never the answer itself."* Fig 13: *"It can say the answer is out of reach with the library as it stands... It cannot say which primitive is missing, because naming it would mean already holding it."*

From *The Library of Babel*: *"you can cut your search short if the set of books returned is small and descriptive enough that it provides a general shape of the answer that satisfies your question."*

So a **shape** is the narrowed form of an answer you can state before you can name it. It constrains; it does not fill. For INF-0027 the shape is the conjunction of conditions a residual must satisfy before it may be called a signal: per-slot liveness, compressible structure, an intact channel, and source corroboration across differing closures. Naming a particular source (a pulsar, a gene, a fault) is a name, not a shape.

## 2. How a shape is obtained

- *Library of Babel*: use the vocabulary you already have that is *closest* to the thing, look for recurring new terms that overlap or correlate with it, recurse. *"With each new term gained, you narrow the space and deepen your vocabulary."* For detection: start with the residual per slot, then add the terms compressibility, transient, saturation, pooling, independence, until the search space narrows.
- *Library of Babel* on bundles: *"Perhaps your problem was a bundle or conflation of several problems masquerading as one?"* This is Fig 9 :54-57 in essay form and it produced the C1/C2/C3 unbundling in `01_figure_grounded_audit.md` section 1.3.
- Fig 6 :30: *"The description travels. Fails by being sharpened into terms only we can read. Cast it in effect terms."* Fig 9 :30-31: *"your words are borrowed too, so describe the effect rather than the cause."* So we describe a live slot and a compressible residual, not "a matched filter output" or "a p-value".
- *Decoding Tacit Knowledge*: *"When contact isn't possible, the best you can send is a plain description of what you'd expect to see, because anyone can check that against the world."* This is why the outreach email carries an effect description and a refuter before any theory.
- *Library of Babel* on stopping: the answer is reached when *"the finish line will also be the starting place"* - the paradox is that by then you have *"composed the answer through the process of locating it."*

## 3. What licenses a name

Only two things, both outside the shape itself:

1. **The ground settles it.** Formula step 5: *"the ground settles it. Verified only by what pays."* The README: the framework *"stops at the limit of what the available instruments can distinguish, and records the lineage so the stop is honest."* For INF-0027, a source-confirming measurement that does not move when the criterion moves.
2. **A provenanced import from a frame whose closure differs** (Fig 6 :23, Fig 8 :50-52), with a source gate and a shadow test. A detection concept imported from radar or psychophysics into the framework is such an import and carries no verdict weight until provenance is shown.

*They're Scanning Books* sharpens the second point: *"The real import is not a new term in the existing space. It is a new dimension of the space."* And: *"A minimum viable set of six terms is a human's careful guess at reality's compression axes; another sensorium's minimum set need not be those six."* Also: *"Declaring the primitives does not make them neutral. It makes the choice visible."* The six are a visible guess, not a discovery of nature's own list. The same essay adds a limit directly relevant here: a recognizer *"can only register an anomaly it has the primitives to represent"*, so a receiver cannot be surprised along an axis its code cannot express. That is Fig 6 :34-35 at the level of representation.

## 4. Why the source question is the specific forbidden error

*The Hard Problem is About Source* gives the mechanism. Metamerism: an instrument reads green and *"cannot comment on the source"* - one green light, or yellow plus blue, or RGB, or CMYK, or a scheme not yet discovered. The reading does not determine the source. *"If several paths create the same behavior, then behavior is a projection with lossy information."* And: *"An instrument that can't distinguish these cases demonstrates its own inadequacy."* It was the **spectrometer**, not an argument, that resolved it.

Applied here: a positive detection reading cannot name its source. Calling the source from the reading is the metamerism error. What is owed is a better instrument or an independent frame, not a name (F4 and F7 of `01_figure_grounded_audit.md`).

The same essay identifies the noise term with the framework: *"Noise is Fig 13 :32: 'the detail a crossing discards reappears above it as noise.'"* So the question "is this noise or a signal?" is a question about the receiver's representation and its crossing, not a question about a substance in the world. This is the canonical grounding for F7.

*The Recursive Self-Improvement Trap*: *"the output is only as good as the validator. The agent, or the tool, or the frame, or the theory, cannot validate itself."* A detection score produced by a frame cannot be validated by that same frame.

## 5. Q1 - does "shape, not a name" forbid naming the detection mechanism?

**Answer: no, with a boundary.**

The README's phrase is *"what a **missing** thing would have to look like (a shape, not a name)."* The subject is a primitive not held in Γ. The four conditions in the shape are not missing primitives: they are figure-derived constraints on composition, contact and corroboration, each already in the framework's vocabulary. Naming them is deriving, not inventing.

What remains forbidden is naming the **specific source** of a particular reading, because that is exactly what a reading cannot determine (metamerism, F4), and it is not held in Γ until a source-discriminating instrument or a provenanced import supplies it. The correct form is a shape plus a stated instrument gap, not a named source. Both claims are now recorded so neither is quietly lost.

## 6. Two consequences for outreach

**Prestige is not action.** From *The Recursive Self-Improvement Trap*: *"Prestige must never be allowed to validate an action directly... Someone let prestige stand in for proof... Prestige can fund an action. It can give an action a budget to be tried. But it cannot settle an action. Only the ground can settle it. The Ground Maintainer must be blind to prestige."*

A campaign that gathers prestigious names to agree with the shape is the named collapse, not corroboration. The legitimate ask is the opposite: give the claim a budget to be tried, and let the ground, a source-confirming measurement, settle it.

**My own audit is a simulation, not a ground reading.** Same essay: *"LLMs can quickly simulate an approximate answer/output... However, the truly testable output would need to be derived from the real architecture or working the formula yourself."* LLM output is *"simulacra."* So `01` is a simulated derivation, checked for internal consistency against the figures. It is **not** a test of the shape and must be labelled as such. It cannot settle anything it reports on.
