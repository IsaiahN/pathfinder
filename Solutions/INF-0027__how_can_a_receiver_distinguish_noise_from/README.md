## The question restated as an effect

What is being decided is not the amplitude of a reading but the class of its source: is a given residual the detail a representation discarded, or a live, compressible, ground-confirmed event? In effect terms:

- **b** is the receiver's belief about what the channel will deliver, formed from its code, its model of the channel and its priors.
- **a** is what the receiver does: attend, probe, integrate, set a criterion, or pass the reading onward.
- **Γ(b,a)** is what the receiver's library predicts will be observed.
- **o'** is what the channel actually returns.
- **s** is the slot: one time step, one frequency bin, one spatial cell, one source hypothesis. R is indexed per slot and per time, never averaged into a global energy figure (Fig 1 :19, Fig 1 :22).

Option A, "the residual's magnitude tells you which it is": leads to the prediction that a threshold on amplitude alone separates the two, and that raising sensitivity is the whole problem.

Option B, "noise and signal are distinguished by structure, contact and provenance, not by magnitude": leads to the prediction that the discriminator is a conjunction of a live slot, a compressible pattern, an intact channel to the ground, and corroboration from a frame with a different evidence pool, while the criterion only allocates a budget.

What an answer would look like, written down and bound as an ordinary term before the run (Formula, "Direction"): a shape naming the conditions that must all hold before a residual may be called a signal, plus an instrument that would discriminate the source rather than merely the reading. Until bound, that shape predicts nothing and no loop check can see it. It is now bound, so the refuter in `01_figure_grounded_audit.md` section 5 can fire on it.

The verdict recorded in `09_watson_answer.md` is Option B in `specified` mode, kept per domain and never averaged. The criterion/threshold is explicitly a budget filter, never a verdict witness.

## What is deliberately not here

- No personal emails of named individuals.
- No ranking of people by how likely they are to agree.
- No bulk-send plan.
- No claim that this derivation is `grounded`, no claim that it settles the information-theory question, and no claim that the framework is the only true account of detection.

Three figure-grounded reasons, plus one practical one:

1. A room is not an anchor. *"A room's agreement is variation, not a verdict. Selection between rooms is where the ground votes."* (Fig 7 :35) and *"Alignment is triangulation, not negotiation. A mirror cannot correct you."* (Fig 2 :35). Gathering agreeable readers manufactures the correlation that disqualifies a verdict.
2. Correlation has valence. *"disqualifying for a verdict: agreement about the world is worthless when the agreeing frames share an evidence pool"* (Fig 2 :40). A list selected for receptiveness is one witness wearing many names.
3. A verifier must be outside the interior. *"A verifier that can reconstruct the claim cannot verify it."* (Fig 10 :42). The first contact therefore carries the residual and its refuter, not the framework.
4. Legally, unsolicited bulk email to scraped addresses breaches GDPR and CAN-SPAM style rules in many jurisdictions and gets the sending domain blacklisted. Public opt-in routes avoid that failure as well as the structural one.
