## Independent domain census

I redid the census from that residual, using the WATSON order: R by arity, symmetry and scale; one line per domain with actors, currency and anchor; partition or union; order by dependency.

- **R (arity / symmetry / scale).** Two or more conditions, so a **union compound** (Fig 9 :55), not a binary choice. The observation is symmetric across its sources, which is the metamer or equifinality shape; the underlying chain is ordered, so correlation cannot choose the arrow. Scale is per document and genre, with a frame-wide condition and a material condition at different levels.
- **Domain 1, the producing frame.** Actors: inquirers and their admission rules. Currency: reasons accepted as proof. Anchor: the rule actually in force, read from internal argument structure. Question type: convention or frame change.
- **Domain 2, the surviving sample.** Actors: media, archives, destruction and curation. Currency: what persisted. Anchor: material facts that do not update on the historian's reading. Question type: channel decay, a habitat selection process.
- **Domain 3, the present checking frame.** Actors: historians and their vocabularies. Currency: agreement and residual. Anchor: the ground, never agreement. In this simulated run its independence is zero, because it shares the primary's pool.

Order by dependency: describe the effect before choosing a frame; index per slot; refuse the naive identification; keep the two tracks separate; import instruments with provenance; range both against a non-updating anchor; record the lineage.

## Round trip gap R_T

The Symbols table defines R_T as `gap(x, (T_E ∘ T_A)(x))`, what the coarser description cannot hold. Fig 4 :30 requires it to be measured rather than assumed. I ran the round trip on the condensation this folder actually uses.

1. **Sent up (x):** the verbatim figure set, chiefly Fig 8 :32 (idempotence), Fig 9 :55-58 (union), Fig 10 :24 (channel), Fig 2 :29/:31-32 (anchor and independence).
2. **Abstraction (T_A):** the shorthand carried in `07`: index per slot, keep two tracks separate, use low-overlap readings and a non-updating anchor, import with provenance, report the case as a gap.
3. **Concretisation (T_E):** a claim about a named period, for example "the apparent increase in experimental reports in this archive reflects a real change in standards."

**R_T is nonzero.** Four things the shorthand holds do not survive the trip back down:

- the **source-discrimination instrument** is not named by the figures; the case needs a preservation model, a reference class and actual dating, none of which the abstraction contains;
- the **overlap condition** of the union, where a standards change altered what was kept, is flattened by the shorthand into two clean tracks;
- the **refusal operator**, a negation, is not a composed quantity, so the shorthand's "refuse first" can be misread as a statistical step rather than a change of standing;
- the **anchor's legitimacy**, a material fact that does not update on interpretation, can be replaced in concretisation by "what the documents say," which is a frame and not a ground.

One thing survives cleanly: the observation does not determine the source, so a discriminator with independent readings is required. That is the residue worth sending. R_T was not measured numerically, because no ground reading exists for a named case; it is recorded qualitatively as the enumerated set of dropped constraints. Treating the folder's shorthand as identical to the figures would set R_T to zero and commit the dead-reckoning error Fig 4 :30 names.
