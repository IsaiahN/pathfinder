## Wider literature that bears on the question

- **Palaeontology.** The Signor-Lipps effect and the confidence-interval literature are the closest worked analogue of this question: an abrupt reality can produce a gradual record, and a preservation model plus a reference class is what discriminates them. The Wang and Marshall review sets out first-, second- and third-generation methods, the last of which "attempts to infer the process, a causal model or mechanism, rather than just the pattern of fossil occurrences." That is the same move as separating the survival track from the standards track.
- **Archaeology.** Taphonomic loss is age-biased; younger material is over-represented. The field's own corrective is comparative: well-preserved against poorly preserved traditions, and datasets assembled under different analytical and classification standards. This is a concrete instance of the independent-readings requirement, imported with provenance.
- **Earth systems and hydrology.** Equifinality is the formal name for "same end state, different paths." It supplies the low-overlap corroboration that the observation underdetermines the source.
- **Historiography of science.** Daston's historical epistemology and the *Questions of Evidence* volume establish that the standards side of the question is real and historically variable. They do not supply a discriminator, which is why the figure derivation is still needed.

Figure-grounded reasons:

- **Fig 4 :22**, "Only methods cross up. Only head starts come down." Sending a concluded historical narrative before anyone has described a residual sends a recording, not a generator. Lead with the shape claim and the refuter.
- **Fig 10 :42**, "A verifier that can reconstruct the claim cannot verify it." A recipient who has absorbed the whole framework before being asked for a verdict has joined the frame being checked. Their verdict on stage 1 must be recorded first.
- **Fig 9 :27-28**, "describe it before you go looking, or any frame you pick will seem to fit." Ask what they would expect to see under each candidate source before giving them the derivation.
- **Fig 2 :40**, "agreement about the world is worthless when the agreeing frames share an evidence pool." Selecting recipients for likely agreement produces a convention, not corroboration.
- **Fig 9 :60**, "Use filters for the budget and witnesses for the verdict. Never let a filter hand you a verdict." A venue's prestige is a filter for where to try, never a reason the separator is right.

**Recommended order in a reply:** (1) the residual described in effect terms; (2) the shape claim and its refuter; (3) ask what they would count as a failure, and record the answer; (4) only then the three links, with the note that the figures, not the summary, are the authority.

## The two-stage offer

1. **Stage 1 before stage 2** is Fig 10 :52, "Bring a characterised residual, never the source," and Fig 2 :45, "Frames range against the ground. They do not negotiate with it." The recipient checks the output without holding the framework's interior.
2. **Stage 2 is opt-in by construction.** Once a recipient has absorbed the theory, their agreement stops being evidence (Fig 2 :40). Do not pool later agreement as corroboration.
3. **Stage 3 sends a method, which is permitted.** Fig 4 :22. The formula is a generator, so giving it away does not give away an answer. Each user writes their own refuter and states their own mode (Formula, "Direction").
4. **Hard precondition.** Stages 1 to 3 work only if the stage-1 output survives. The case verdict is a declared `gap` (Fig 13 :134), and the separator is `specified`, not `grounded`. Do not let a recipient read "we have identified what happened" into the offer.
