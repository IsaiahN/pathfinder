## What is deliberately not here

- No personal emails of named individuals.
- No ranking of people by how likely they are to agree with the derivation.
- No bulk-send plan.
- No verdict on any particular historical episode. The framework returns the shape of a valid discriminator, and the specific historical answer is a declared `gap`.

Three figure-grounded reasons for the refusals:

1. **Agreement is not an anchor.** Fig 2 :35, "Alignment is triangulation, not negotiation. A mirror cannot correct you." A list selected for receptiveness manufactures the correlation that would make its agreement worthless (Fig 2 :40).
2. **The source cannot be read off the artifact.** A single surviving record can be produced by a standards shift or by a survival change. This is the metamerism or equifinality shape, and it is the residual itself.
3. **A verifier must not hold the interiors.** Fig 10 :42, "A verifier that can reconstruct the claim cannot verify it." The first contact carries the residual and its refuter, never the framework.
