## 1. A shape is a constraint you can state before you can name the thing

The figures README gives the canonical limit: the framework returns *"what a missing thing would have to look like (a shape, not a name)"* (local README). Fig 9 :32: *"you get a kind of thing, never the answer itself"*. Fig 13 :134: *"Unreachable is a verdict here, not a silence"*.

From *The Library of Babel* (essay, opened): *"you can cut your search short if the set of books returned is small and descriptive enough that it provides a general shape of the answer that satisfies your question."* Also: *"With each new term gained, you are able to narrow the space by definition and deepen your vocabulary."*

For ORI-0060, the shape is: a discriminating result must make four different failures impossible, each on its own ground. The four slots (boundary coupling, heritable variation, differential fitness, cumulative change) are the constraints. The name would be a specific protocol with a specific round count and a specific threshold. The figures give the first, not the second.

## 3. What licenses a name

Only two things, both outside the shape itself:

1. **The ground settles it.** THE_FORMULA step 5: *"the ground settles it. Verified only by what pays. A gate is not the ground."* The figures README: the framework *"stops at the limit of what the available instruments can distinguish, and records the lineage so the stop is honest."*
2. **A provenanced import from a frame whose closure differs** (Fig 6 :23, Fig 8 :50-52), with a source gate and a shadow test. Fig 8 :50-52: *"Convergent derivation and adopted import look identical in the contents. Only the record of where each came from separates them"*.

For this question, a named assay is licensed only by a world source that actually used it and by a ground reading that pays. qPCR and next-generation sequencing come from Abil et al. 2024; fluorescence-marked membrane inheritance and conveyer-vesicle fusion come from Kurihara et al. 2015; flow setups and functional catalytic selection come from Liu et al. 2025. Each is a binding, recorded with provenance in `10_source_ledger.csv`. None is a figure-derived requirement.

*They're Scanning Books* (carried from the exemplar): *"The real import is not a new term in the existing space. It is a new dimension of the space."* And: *"Declaring the primitives does not make them neutral. It makes the choice visible."* The six-term substrate is a visible guess about physical composition, not nature's own list, and it is not being used here to name a biological assay.

## 4. The source question: why a single impressive reading is not enough

*The Hard Problem is About Source* (essay, opened) gives the mechanism. Metamerism: an instrument reads green and *"cannot comment on the source"*. Several paths can produce the same reading: *"If several paths create the same behavior, then behavior is a projection with lossy information"*. One green light, or yellow plus blue, or RGB, or CMYK. The reading does not determine the source. *"An instrument that can't distinguish these cases demonstrates its own inadequacy, it cannot claim anything substantial about what is being measured or what causes it."* It was the spectrometer, not an argument, that resolved it: *"The answer is unreached until a better instrument exists."*

Applied here: a rising DNA copy number inside liposomes is a projection. Its sources include heritable improvement, PCR bias toward short variants, a mutational hotspot, contamination, and selection in the external solution. Abil et al. report an actual contamination event that changed the course of one campaign. Without a source-discriminating instrument (bulk control, independent fitness assay, sequencing with lineage records, no-pressure control), the reading cannot name its source. What is owed is the discriminator, not a verdict.

## 5. Q1 - Does "shape, not a name" forbid naming an assay? CORRECTION TO MY OWN FIRST PASS

- The figures README's phrase is *"what a **missing** thing would have to look like (a shape, not a name)."* The subject is a thing missing from Γ, a primitive the frame does not hold.
- An assay such as qPCR or a bulk control is **not** a missing primitive in the Tether's six-term substrate. It is a binding from a world frame onto a capability the framework already describes as an instrument extending contact (Fig 6 :34: *"Where nothing is, there is no edge to extend from"*).
- ⇒ Naming an assay is **not** forbidden. What is forbidden is claiming the figure derives it, or claiming a named protocol is the only one that can settle the question. Fig 6 :21: *"MINT composes inside the closure and can never add an atom."*
- **I overstated this on a first pass.** "The framework returns a shape, not a name" was applied to the choice of assay. It governs missing primitives. Retracted as applied to assays, and kept visible here. Triangulated (figures README + Fig 6 :21). The same correction is recorded in `01` section 8 (R2) and in `06` Q1.

## 6. Two consequences for this solution

**Prestige is not action.** From *The Recursive Self-Improvement Trap* (essay, opened): *"Prestige must never be allowed to validate an action directly... Someone let prestige stand in for proof... Prestige can fund an action. It can give an action a budget to be tried. But it cannot settle an action. Only the ground can settle it. The Ground Maintainer must be blind to prestige."* A list of famous labs whose names agree with the shape would be the named collapse, not corroboration. The legitimate ask gives the discriminator a budget to be tried and lets the ground, an assay and a control, settle it.

**My own audit is a simulation, not a ground reading.** Same essay: *"LLMs can quickly simulate an approximate answer/output"* and *"the truly testable output would need to be derived from the real architecture or working the formula yourself"*. LLM output is *"simulacra"* (as recorded in the exemplar's mapping). So `01`, `06`, `07` and `09` are simulated derivations checked for internal consistency against the figures. They are not tests of a protocell and must be labelled as such. They cannot settle anything they report on. The ground here would be an actual lineage, an actual control and a pre-registered failure condition.

## 7. What "shape" is NOT in this question

- Not a protocol. No figure line returns a round count, lipid composition or PCR cycle (Fig 9 :32; Fig 13 :134).
- Not a single number. Fig 1 :19: *"The residual is indexed by object slot, not aggregated."*
- Not a definition of life. Chen and Walde note that *"the difficulties encountered in defining life suggest that life is a quantitative, not qualitative, trait"*. This question asks for a discriminating result, not a boundary in a continuum.
- Not a claim that compartments are necessary for evolution in general. Bunin and Rivoire's model exhibits evolutionary features without compartments. The figures require a boundary for the protocell question specifically (Fig 4 :21), which is a narrower claim (see `01` strain S5).
