## 0. The claim under audit

> An experimental result demonstrates that a protocell can undergo sustained Darwinian evolution when it returns four separately anchored readings over successive generations: (1) the boundary and its contents are coupled so that a daughter inherits both; (2) heritable states vary and a parent-offspring correlation survives removal of the selector; (3) variants differ in amplification under a defined pressure measured by a ground independent of the trait used to define them, with a no-pressure and a no-compartment control; (4) a pre-registered trait improves cumulatively across rounds and the improvement persists when selection is relaxed. The demonstration slot rides on all four. Each reading is per slot. No figure line names the protocol, the number of rounds, or a numerical threshold, so those are a declared `gap`, a shape and not a name.

The ordinary-language rival readings are recorded with it at the outset, so they can be refused: one-off replication; neutral drift; chemical oscillation or exponential growth without differential fitness; selection without heritability; a harness artifact; a criterion written after the data; a fitness assay calibrated from the lineages it ranks.

## 1. Residual, arity, symmetry, scale

**In effect terms.** The source line asks for a discriminating observation. The residual is not "is this thing alive", which is a global average. It is the gap, per slot, between a vessel's reading and each candidate verdict. The anchor is whatever settles a slot and does not change its answer because the experimenter wants a particular verdict (Fig 2 :29-30): *"An anchor is whatever settles the question and does not change its answer because of what any frame thinks."* and *"If it moves when you push on it, it is not an anchor."*

- **Arity.** The discriminating relation is binary (a lineage over rounds against a ground) with an internal three-term composition (variation, heredity, differential fitness) and a fourth cumulative term. The demonstration is a fifth slot that composes over the other four; it does not average them.
- **Symmetry.** "Demonstrate" is ordered: an experiment grounds a claim, not the reverse. "Evolution" is time-asymmetric and cumulative. "Darwinian" names a mechanism, not a symmetric property. A pooled correlation between trait and outcome cannot choose the arrow (Fig 2 :39-40): *"Correlation has valence."* and *"disqualifying for a verdict: agreement about the world is worthless when the agreeing frames share an evidence pool"*.
- **Scale.** A protocell is a compartment-scale object; the verdict is a population-over-generations property. The generation boundary is a membrane in Fig 4's sense: *"The membrane is the boundary between the sizes, and the rule about what may cross it. Not a wall: a filter with a direction."* (Fig 4 :21). What crosses is the head start of a state, never a recording of one occasion (Fig 4 :22, :25).

**Per-slot warning.** *"The residual is indexed by object slot, not aggregated."* (Fig 1 :19); *"Aggregating across slots is how a live signal disappears into an average."* (Fig 1 :22). A single headline number, such as fold-amplification over ten rounds, hides the slot that matters (heredity) behind the slot that is easiest to measure (amplification).

## 2. Domain census

One line per domain: actors, currency, anchor. No nameable anchor means it is not a domain yet.

| id | domain | actors | currency | nameable anchor | partition or union |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| D1 | boundary coupling | membrane, internal chemistry, external milieu | what crosses the boundary; coupling of replication, supply and division | an assay showing daughter compartments inherit both boundary state and contents, with a no-compartment control | union member |
| D2 | heredity | template or compositional state and its copies | copied state, fidelity, mutation | parent-offspring correlation above assay background, measured after the selector is removed | union member |
| D3 | variation and differential fitness | variant lineages in a shared habitat | differential amplification under a pressure | a pressure regime plus an independent fitness assay, with a no-pressure control | union member |
| D4 | cumulative change | lineage across rounds, environment | residual that trends down, adaptation | a pre-registered time series whose improvement persists when selection is relaxed | union member |
| D5 | demonstration | experimenter, controls, claim, outside checker | the claim's own residual and its refuter | a pre-registered failure condition and a ground that does not update on the claim | union member, composes over D1 to D4 |

No candidate domain was discarded for lacking an anchor; the parasitic-genome case in the literature is D3's malfactor, whose valence is residual-relative (Fig 11 :49-51), so it belongs in D3 and not in a separate "bad molecule" domain.

**Partition or union.** This is a union, not a partition (Fig 9 :54-55): *"And when the split lands on a union rather than a partition, unbundle before you search again."* *"A bundled problem is usually a union of overlapping conditions, not a list of separate ones."* A single lever that shows replication clears the region where only D1 holds and leaves the intersections standing (Fig 9 :57): *"a case in two conditions is not a harder case of either"*. Concretely, a linear DNA that amplifies inside liposomes but is re-extracted, PCR-amplified and re-encapsulated each round settles D1 and part of D2, but it does not settle D4's "sustained" claim without the no-compartment control. Verdicts per domain are kept separate and never averaged.

**Dependency order (Fig 3).** *"The chain is ordered by dependency, not by importance."* (Fig 3 :20). D1 (boundary and coupling) → D2 (heredity across the boundary) → D3 (variation and differential fitness in a population) → D4 (cumulative change over generations) → D5 (a claim that can survive an outside check). Effort spent at D5 before D2 is effort above a break: *"Optimising a link that already works cannot rescue one that does not."* (Fig 3 :19).

The chain typically breaks in the middle, between having terms for what a protocell does and having a criterion for what would count as progress (Fig 3 :24, :27): *"Why the middle link is where chains usually break."* *"A link with no instrument is not a link that works. It is a link nobody has looked at."* The field can measure amplification (perception) and can design selection regimes (planning), but the objective link, what counts as Darwinian rather than merely chemical progress, has no single instrument. That is why this question is asked.

## 4. Findings

### F1 - The verdict is a union of four conditions, not one experiment (decisive, triangulated)

Fig 9 :54-57, quoted in section 2. The source line asks for "an experimental result", singular. The figure forces a union: a single result that settles only boundary coupling or only amplification leaves the other three conditions binding. The honest answer is a conjunction of readings, each with its own ground, kept separate. **Triangulated**: Fig 9 :54-57 plus Fig 1 :19/:22 plus Fig 7 :35. World corroboration: Ameta et al. 2021 state that *"The specific characteristics of Darwinian evolution that we discuss here are (i) variation, (ii) heredity, and (iii) differential fitness, following the formulation by Lewontin and Godfrey-Smith"* and add compartmentalization as the coupling condition (world source, opened 2026-11-23).

**Refuter:** if an outside specialist accepts a single-round amplification signal, with no parent-offspring correlation and no control, as a demonstration of sustained Darwinian evolution, this union finding fails.

### F2 - "Selection ran" is a label applied afterwards to a lineage (decisive for D4 and D5, triangulated)

Fig 11 :36: *"Where nothing maintains anything, intelligence is a label applied afterwards to a lineage that met its ground."* Fig 11 :39: *"capability is a property of agent-and-habitat, never of the agent alone"*. So "Darwinian evolution" is not visible in one protocell or one round. It is a reading of a lineage that met its ground across rounds. A result that shows one division is not the verdict; a result that shows a lineage whose heritable state changed in a direction the ground pays for is. Fig 13 :129: *"the order is not recoverable from the artefact. It is recoverable from the lineage."*

**Refuter:** a demonstration that a single protocell, observed once, carries the property "undergoing sustained Darwinian evolution" with no lineage record.

### F3 - The anchor must not move, and the trait must not be read from the same sample that defines the variant (triangulated)

Fig 2 :29-30 (quoted in section 1), Fig 2 :31: *"Independence, between two frames, is how little they draw on the same evidence."* Fig 2 :42: *"Buy independence in proportion to the cost of being wrong."* A fitness assay calibrated from the lineages it ranks is panel 1 of Fig 2, a mutual update, not an anchor. This is the exact failure mode that makes an impressive-looking time series worthless: the rank and the measurement come from the same pool.

**Refuter:** show that the fitness assay returns the same rank when the lineages are relabelled by an independent party who never saw the selection regime.

### F4 - The no-compartment control is not optional (triangulated)

Fig 11 :70: *"A test harness is a substituted habitat. Which is why a synthetic solve proves wiring and never capability."* Fig 11 :66: *"Isolation is not removal of the habitat. It is substitution of one habitat for another."* A liposome experiment is a substituted habitat. Without a no-compartment bulk control, an observed improvement might be PCR selection, a mutational hotspot, or a chemical artifact of the vessel. With the control, the compartment's causal contribution becomes visible. World corroboration, genuinely low overlap with the framework: Abil et al. 2024 report that *"serial dilution of mod-orip2p3 with fresh PURE system and DNA replication components in the absence of liposomes ... resulted in a gradual decrease of DNA concentration and self-replication was totally suppressed at round 6"* and that *"liposomes enable sustained propagation through Darwinian selection that arises due to the genotype-phenotype link provided by (transient) compartmentalisation."* That is exactly the D1 control, done in a real laboratory.

**Refuter:** a bulk control that improves at the same magnitude and the same time constants as the compartment line.

### F5 - The three-way discriminator must be written before the run (single-source on the figure side, strongly corroborated)

Abil et al. 2024 wrote the rival outcomes down explicitly: *"We asked if repeated cycles of intermittent experimental evolution would result in the DNA (i) gradually losing its ability to self-amplify due to the accumulation of non-replicating variants (extinction of viable replicators) (ii) retaining its initial self-replication activity at the same level (neutral drift), or (iii) gradually improving its self-replication activity (adaptive evolution)."* In figure terms this is the refusal, held, minted sort of Fig 5 :10-14, but applied to a verdict rather than to a term. Formula step "Direction" (THE_FORMULA.md): bind the sought-for shape as an ordinary term so it accrues a residual and can be refused. Fig 5 :65-66: *"make the sought-for shape an ORDINARY TERM. Write down what an answer would look like, bind it, and let it accrue a residual like anything else."*

Marked `single-source` on the figure side because Fig 5's three guards are about minting a term, not about deciding a biological verdict. The world source is the governing witness here, but it corroborates the shape, not the figure's authority over it.

**Refuter:** a criterion for "adaptive evolution" first stated after the time series is inspected.

### F6 - Heritability is a catalyst-like surviving part, and amplification is not heredity (triangulated)

Fig 12 :119: *"catalyst. A part that survives its own use."* Fig 12 :104: *"A settled molecule becomes an atom for whatever composes over it."* The heritable state must survive its own use as a template and become an operand for the next round. Fig 12 :121: *"cycle. The output re-enters as input."* A linear amplification that is thrown away after one round is a chain, not a cycle: *"a chain cannot close by construction"* (Fig 12 :121). World corroboration: Kurihara et al. 2015 state that *"the ability of a protocell to recursively self-proliferate for multiple generations has not been demonstrated"* and then demonstrate daughter GVs restored to parental status by fusion with conveyer GVs, producing a granddaughter GV. Their own honesty is part of the result: they say a mutant protocell *"might exhibit a high frequency of self-proliferation and could become the predominant species"* and that *"this event could be regarded as evolution"*, that is, evolution is still prospective in that system.

**Refuter:** a lineage in which the daughter's state is uncorrelated with the parent's once the selector is removed.

### F7 - What crosses the generation boundary is a head start, never a recording (triangulated)

Fig 4 :22: *"Only methods cross up. Only head starts come down."* Fig 4 :25: *"A recording carried upward looks like knowledge and is a description of one occasion."* Applied to D1 and D2: PCR extraction plus re-encapsulation carries a head start (a copy of the state, ready to be reused), while a freeze-thaw fusion-fission transfer carries the state itself. These are different bonds, not different quantities, and are treated as isomers in `06_isomers_and_order.md`. Fig 4 :30: *"So take the round trip and measure the gap."* The round trip here is parent state → daughter state → assay; `R_T` is nonzero because the assay compresses a compartment's composition into a few numbers.

**Refuter:** a round trip that returns an exactly identical reading at the compartment and at the population level, meaning the compression lost nothing and the gap was not measured.

### F8 - The outside checker cannot hold the interiors (triangulated)

Fig 10 :42: *"A verifier that can reconstruct the claim cannot verify it."* Fig 10 :52: *"Bring a characterised residual, never the source."* Fig 2 :35: *"Alignment is triangulation, not negotiation. A mirror cannot correct you."* The first contact therefore carries the four-slot discriminator and the pre-registered refuter, never this derivation. An interest in the outcome is unavoidable and must be declared (Fig 10 :59: *"an interest in the outcome is unavoidable, an interest in the reading is disqualifying"*). The author's stake here is a preference for the framework's shape, and it is declared rather than hidden.

**Refuter:** evidence that the first contact included the derivation and the recipient's verdict was recorded only afterwards.

### F9 - Multiple low-overlap witnesses are needed, and agreement from one pool is one witness (triangulated)

Fig 2 :40 (quoted), Fig 8 :50-52: *"Convergent derivation and adopted import look identical in the contents. Only the record of where each came from separates them"*. Six world sources were opened across distinct pools: protocell liposome evolution (Abil et al.), recursive vesicle cell cycle (Kurihara et al.), vesicle/protein review (Chen and Walde), autocatalytic chemical networks (Ameta et al.), synthetic self-replicator catalysis (Liu et al.), and a minimal-physics model (Bunin and Rivoire). These are not six votes. The three chemistry/protocell papers share an origins-of-life pool; the physics model is genuinely low overlap; the AMP/engineered-systems paper is a different platform. Overlap is recorded row by row in `10_source_ledger.csv`. Most importantly, none of them runs all four readings with a pre-registered refuter, so world agreement does not settle this question.

**Refuter:** an independent search that finds all six sources trace to one review or one lab's reanalysis.

### F10 - The specific protocol is a `gap`, a shape and not a name (triangulated with a declared limit)

Fig 9 :32: *"you get a kind of thing, never the answer itself"*. Fig 13 :134: *"Unreachable is a verdict here, not a silence"*. Fig 8 :39: *"So a search that finds nothing is never a proof of absence."* No figure line selects ten rounds, a 5-fold improvement, a particular lipid composition, or a particular assay. The figures fix the discriminator's shape; a protocol is a binding that must be provenanced from a world source and checked against the ground. Declaring the gap is required, not optional: "Unreachable is a verdict, not a silence."

**Refuter:** a figure line that does name a numerical threshold, or a world ground that settles which protocol is required.

## 5. Pre-registered refuter (written before the conclusion)

The claim "this result demonstrates sustained Darwinian evolution in a protocell" is refused, and the report downgraded to `unreached`, if any of the following holds:

1. **R1 (D1/D4).** The improvement appears at the same magnitude in the no-compartment bulk control. The boundary is not the cause.
2. **R2 (D2).** Daughter state is uncorrelated with parent state above the assay's measured background once the selector is removed.
3. **R3 (D3).** No variant with differential amplification under the applied pressure exists, or the fitness reading and the variant definition come from the same sample and the same instrument (one evidence pool, Fig 2 :40).
4. **R4 (D4).** The trend does not persist after the pressure is relaxed, or it requires continued experimenter intervention of a kind not present in the control (harness artifact, Fig 11 :70).
5. **R5 (D5).** The criterion was written after the data were seen.
6. **R6 (D5).** The anchor moves when pushed: the fitness assay's calibration depends on the lineages it ranks.
7. **R7 (all).** The lineage record is missing, so the order of changes cannot be checked (Fig 13 :129).

A falsifier written before the run is a design instrument. If any fires, "demonstrates" is withdrawn and the row is recorded as `unreached`, not silently dropped.

## 6. Recommendation

1. Report the result as four separately anchored readings plus a demonstration verdict. Do not average them into a single "it evolved" number (F1).
2. Bind the three rival outcomes before the run (F5) and attach R1 to R7 to the protocol before any measurement.
3. Make the no-compartment and no-pressure controls part of the result, not an appendix (F4).
4. Use an independent fitness assay, and state its independence explicitly (F3).
5. Keep the lineage record (R7), because the artefact does not carry the order (Fig 13 :129).
6. Name candidate assays as provenanced bindings from world sources, never as figure-derived requirements (F10, and the correction in `05_what_shape_means.md` section 5).
7. Offer the residual and the refuter first (F8); the figures are the authority, not this summary.

## 9. Updated verdict

The shape survives and is now sharper. A demonstrating result is a four-slot conjunction with a fifth demonstration verdict riding on it, held per slot, with a pre-registered three-way rival list and seven failure conditions. The boundary and the lineage are load-bearing; the anchor must be independent of the trait; the control must be present. Retractions R1 to R3 are kept visible. No figure line selects a protocol, round count or threshold, and that is recorded as a `gap` of the shape-not-a-name kind, not as silence. Strains after re-census: none live; S1 to S5 are dissolved, narrowed or scoped as above. Mode: `specified` on the shape, `general` on the union structure, `gap` on the name, not `grounded`.
