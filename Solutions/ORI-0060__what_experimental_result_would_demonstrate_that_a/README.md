## What an answer would look like (direction bound as an ordinary term)

Restated as an effect: the residual is the difference between what a vessel currently shows and the verdict "this lineage underwent sustained Darwinian evolution". Rivals that produce a similar surface reading are one-off replication, neutral drift, chemical oscillation or growth, selection without heritability, a harness artifact, and a criterion chosen after seeing the data. An answer is a description of a result that separates the verdict from each rival on a ground that does not move when the claim is pushed. It is bound here as a four-slot discriminator (boundary coupling, heritable variation, differential fitness, cumulative change), with the demonstration slot riding on all four. Formula step "Direction": write down the sought-for shape before the run, so the preference can be refused (Fig 5 :65-66).

## What is deliberately not here

- No personal emails of named individuals.
- No ranking of people by likelihood of agreement.
- No bulk-send plan.
- No claim that a named experimental protocol is the answer. The figures return a shape, not a name (Fig 9 :32).

Three reasons, in the framework's own words: *"disqualifying for a verdict: agreement about the world is worthless when the agreeing frames share an evidence pool"* (Fig 2 :40); *"A verifier that can reconstruct the claim cannot verify it."* (Fig 10 :42); and *"Only methods cross up. Only head starts come down."* (Fig 4 :22). Sending a packaged conclusion before the other frame has described a failure condition converts a checker into a member of the frame.
