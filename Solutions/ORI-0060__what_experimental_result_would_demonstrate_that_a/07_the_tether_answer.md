# The Tether's answer: what result would demonstrate sustained Darwinian evolution in a protocell?

**Mode: `specified` on the four-slot shape and the discriminator conditions, `general` on the union structure, `gap` on the specific protocol, round count and threshold. Not `grounded`.** Simulated derivation checked for internal consistency against the figures (`05_what_shape_means.md` section 6), not a ground reading. No protocell experiment was run here.

## 1. The residual it answers

The question asks for one result, but the residual is a union of four overlapping conditions plus a demonstration slot that composes over them (Fig 9 :54-55: *"And when the split lands on a union rather than a partition, unbundle before you search again."* *"A bundled problem is usually a union of overlapping conditions, not a list of separate ones."*). The rival readings are one-off replication, neutral drift, chemical growth without differential fitness, selection without heritability, a vessel or harness artifact, a criterion chosen after the data, and an assay calibrated from the lineages it ranks. The effect asked for is a result that separates the verdict from each rival, per slot (Fig 1 :19), on a ground that does not update (Fig 2 :29-30).

## 2. The shape

1. **Boundary coupling (D1).** The daughter compartment inherits both boundary state and contents, and the no-compartment bulk control does not improve at the same magnitude. Fig 4 :21: *"The membrane is the boundary between the sizes, and the rule about what may cross it. Not a wall: a filter with a direction."* Fig 11 :70: *"A test harness is a substituted habitat. Which is why a synthetic solve proves wiring and never capability."*
2. **Heritable variation (D2).** States are copied with fidelity sufficient for a parent-offspring correlation to survive removal of the selector. Fig 12 :119: *"catalyst. A part that survives its own use."* Fig 12 :121: *"cycle. The output re-enters as input."* Amplification that is discarded after one round is a chain, not a cycle.
3. **Differential fitness under a defined pressure (D3).** Variants differ in amplification, and the fitness reading comes from an instrument independent of the trait used to define the variant. Fig 2 :31: *"Independence, between two frames, is how little they draw on the same evidence."* A fitness assay calibrated from the ranked lineages is a mutual update, not an anchor. Fig 11 :49: *"An actor's valence is relative to the residual, not a property it carries."* A parasite is a malfactor for one residual and neutral for another.
4. **Cumulative change over generations (D4).** A pre-registered trait improves across rounds and the improvement persists when the pressure is relaxed. Fig 11 :36: *"Where nothing maintains anything, intelligence is a label applied afterwards to a lineage that met its ground."* The verdict is a reading of a lineage, not of one protocell. Fig 13 :129: *"the order is not recoverable from the artefact. It is recoverable from the lineage."*
5. **Demonstration (D5).** The claim rides on all four and carries a pre-registered refuter. Fig 10 :42: *"A verifier that can reconstruct the claim cannot verify it."* Fig 9 :27-28: *"describe it before you go looking, or any frame you pick will seem to fit"*. Fig 2 :35: *"Alignment is triangulation, not negotiation. A mirror cannot correct you."*

The dependency order is D1 → D2 → D3 → D4 → D5 (Fig 3 :20: *"The chain is ordered by dependency, not by importance."*). Verdicts are kept per domain and never averaged into a single "it evolved" number.

## 3. What it explicitly does not say

- Not a protocol. No figure line returns a round count, lipid composition, PCR regime or numerical improvement threshold (Fig 9 :32: *"you get a kind of thing, never the answer itself"*). That is a declared `gap`, a verdict and not silence (Fig 13 :134: *"Unreachable is a verdict here, not a silence"*).
- Not a definition of life. Chen and Walde note that *"the difficulties encountered in defining life suggest that life is a quantitative, not qualitative, trait"*. The question asks for a discriminator, not a boundary in a continuum.
- Not that compartments are necessary for evolution in every physical system. Bunin and Rivoire's model shows evolutionary features *"without separate mechanisms for metabolism, replication and compartmentalization"*. The figures require a boundary for the protocell question specifically (Fig 4 :21), a narrower claim recorded as strain S5 in `01`.
- Not that any named assay is figure-derived. qPCR, sequencing, flow cytometry and freeze-thaw transfer are provenanced bindings from world sources (Fig 8 :50-52: *"Convergent derivation and adopted import look identical in the contents. Only the record of where each came from separates them"*). The first-pass overstatement of "shape, not a name" is corrected in `05` section 5.
- Not `grounded`. No measurement was made by this agent. Status: `candidate` under the mode rule.

## 4. What would settle it - the ground

A source-discriminating instrument, not a further argument. *The Hard Problem is About Source* (essay, opened 2026-11-23): an instrument reads green and *"cannot comment on the source"*; *"If several paths create the same behavior, then behavior is a projection with lossy information"*; *"The answer is unreached until a better instrument exists."* A rising copy number inside vesicles is a projection whose sources include heritable improvement, primer bias, mutational hotspots, contamination and external amplification. The discriminator that separates those sources is the ground.

Concretely, the checks to run, each already used somewhere in the opened literature:

- **Bulk no-compartment control at the same dilution and the same time constants.** Abil et al. 2024 found *"self-replication was totally suppressed at round 6"* in the absence of liposomes. That is the D1 ground.
- **Parent-offspring correlation after selector removal.** Kurihara et al. 2015 produced a granddaughter GV from a labelled mother line, but their adaptation claim remains prospective. The D2 ground is still owed.
- **Independent fitness assay and no-pressure control.** Abil et al.'s three-way rival list (extinction, neutral drift, adaptive evolution) is the right pre-registration shape; the D3 ground needs the independence condition added.
- **Lineage record with mutation frequencies across rounds.** Abil et al. supply next-generation sequencing data and report that S79G reached *"67%"* frequency by round 11 in one campaign, alongside an honest contamination event in another. That is part of the D4 ground.
- **Pre-registered refuter R1 to R7 from `01`.** The D5 ground.

None of the opened sources supplies all four readings for one lineage with a pre-registered refuter. The question is therefore **unreached, not unreachable**: the instruments exist, the discriminators exist, and combining them is a design task, not a proof of impossibility (Fig 8 :39: *"So a search that finds nothing is never a proof of absence."*).

## 5. The honest stop

The framework returns the shape of a demonstrating result and the refuters that would break it. It does not return the result. The six-term substrate is *"a human's careful guess at reality's compression axes"* (carried from the exemplar's mapping of *They're Scanning Books*); declaring it makes the choice visible, not neutral. Fig 6 :21: *"MINT composes inside the closure and can never add an atom."* A better instrument extends contact (Fig 6 :34: *"Where nothing is, there is no edge to extend from"*); it does not add a biological primitive. The record stops at the limit of the available discriminators and keeps the lineage. *"Indiscernibility is not identity"* (figures README).

## 6. One-paragraph version for outreach

> The Tether does not name the experiment that proves a protocell evolved, and it does not claim any lab has settled it. It says the question is a union of four conditions, not one number. A demonstrating result must show that a daughter compartment inherits both boundary and contents while a no-compartment control fails to improve; that heritable states vary and a parent-offspring correlation survives removal of the selector; that variants differ in amplification under a defined pressure measured by an assay independent of the trait that defined them; and that a pre-registered trait improves across rounds and keeps the improvement when selection is relaxed. The verdict about the lineage rides on all four, kept separate, with seven failure conditions written in advance. What the framework returns is this shape, plus a source-discriminating instrument to close it. The specific protocol, round count and threshold are a declared gap, not a hidden assumption. The center stays open until an actual lineage, an actual control and a pre-registered refuter settle it.
