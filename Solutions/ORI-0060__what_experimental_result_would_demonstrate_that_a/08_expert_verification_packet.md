# Expert verification packet: what result would demonstrate sustained Darwinian evolution in a protocell?

**What this is:** a claim in a form a protocell / origins-of-life specialist can check in twenty minutes. Mode, derivation and refuters are stated before the conclusion, so the reader knows what to look for and what would settle it.

**Mode: `specified` on the four-slot shape and the discriminator conditions, `general` on the union structure, `gap` on the specific protocol and threshold. Not `grounded`.** No measurement was made by this agent. This is a simulated derivation checked for internal consistency against the figures, not a ground reading.

## 1. The claim, in one paragraph

A result demonstrates sustained Darwinian evolution in a protocell lineage only when it returns four separately anchored readings and a fifth verdict riding on them. The four readings are: daughter compartments inherit both boundary state and contents while a bulk, no-compartment control fails to improve at the same magnitude; heritable states vary and a parent-offspring correlation survives removal of the selector; variants differ in amplification under a defined pressure measured by a ground independent of the trait used to define the variant, with a no-pressure control; and a pre-registered trait improves cumulatively across rounds and persists when selection is relaxed. The verdict is a reading of a lineage, not of one protocell. No figure line names a protocol, round count or threshold, so those are declared `gap`, a shape and not a name. Seven failure conditions were written before this paragraph.

## 2. Three checkable sub-claims

### C1 - framework-internal plus platform-facing; checkable against the opened papers

- **Statement:** the question is a union of overlapping conditions, so one lever (showing amplification) leaves the other conditions binding. A single headline number hides the heredity slot.
- **Source, verbatim:** Fig 9 :54 - *"And when the split lands on a union rather than a partition, unbundle before you search again."* Fig 9 :55 - *"A bundled problem is usually a union of overlapping conditions, not a list of separate ones."* Fig 1 :19 - *"The residual is indexed by object slot, not aggregated."*
- **World check:** Ameta et al. 2021 define the mechanism by *"variation, heredity, and differential fitness, following the formulation by Lewontin and Godfrey-Smith"*, with compartmentalization as the coupling condition.
- **Refuter:** an outside specialist accepts a single-round amplification signal, with no parent-offspring correlation and no control, as a demonstration of sustained Darwinian evolution.

### C2 - platform-facing; the load-bearing one

- **Statement:** the bulk, no-compartment control is part of the demonstration, not an appendix, and the fitness assay must be independent of the trait used to define the variant.
- **Source, verbatim:** Fig 11 :70 - *"A test harness is a substituted habitat. Which is why a synthetic solve proves wiring and never capability."* Fig 2 :31 - *"Independence, between two frames, is how little they draw on the same evidence."* Fig 2 :30 - *"If it moves when you push on it, it is not an anchor."*
- **What to check:** in Abil et al. 2024, does the no-liposome bulk control actually fail where the liposome line persists? They report that *"self-replication was totally suppressed at round 6"* without liposomes and that *"liposomes enable sustained propagation through Darwinian selection that arises due to the genotype-phenotype link provided by (transient) compartmentalisation."* Is the variant-defining assay independent of the fitness assay in your own setup, or do both come from the same qPCR read?
- **Refuter:** a bulk control that improves at the same magnitude and time constants, or a fitness rank that is unchanged when the lineages are relabelled by someone who never saw the selection regime.

### C3 - framework-internal derivation

- **Statement:** "Darwinian evolution" is a label applied afterwards to a lineage, so a lineage record is required and the round order cannot be read off the final artefact. The four slots are ordered by dependency with `→`, not a symmetric conjunction.
- **Source, verbatim:** Fig 11 :36 - *"Where nothing maintains anything, intelligence is a label applied afterwards to a lineage that met its ground."* Fig 13 :129 - *"the order is not recoverable from the artefact. It is recoverable from the lineage."* Fig 3 :20 - *"The chain is ordered by dependency, not by importance."*
- **Refuter:** a demonstration accepted without a lineage record, or a case in which the order heredity → variation → selection is demonstrably reversible with no change to the verdict.

## 3. What is explicitly not claimed

- Not a named protocol, round count, lipid composition or numerical threshold. That is a declared gap (Fig 9 :32: *"you get a kind of thing, never the answer itself"*).
- Not a definition of life, and not a claim that compartments are necessary for evolution in every physical system. Bunin and Rivoire's minimal model reports evolutionary features without compartments; the figures require a boundary for the protocell question specifically (Fig 4 :21), which is narrower.
- Not that any named assay is figure-derived. qPCR, sequencing, flow cytometry and freeze-thaw transfer are provenanced bindings from world sources. The first-pass overstatement of "shape, not a name" was retracted and kept visible in `05` section 5.
- Not `grounded`. Nothing here was settled by an experiment run by this agent.
- Not that any opened paper supplies all four readings. None does. The question is unreached, not unreachable.

## 5. What would settle it

A source-discriminating instrument plus the seven pre-registered failure conditions in `01` section 5:

1. improvement appears at the same magnitude in the bulk, no-compartment control;
2. daughter state is uncorrelated with parent state once the selector is removed;
3. fitness reading and variant definition come from the same sample and instrument;
4. the trend vanishes when the pressure is relaxed, or needs continuing experimenter intervention;
5. the criterion was written after the data;
6. the anchor moves when pushed;
7. the lineage record is missing.

If any fires, "demonstrates" is withdrawn and the case reported as unreached. From *The Hard Problem is About Source* (essay, opened): an instrument reads green and *"cannot comment on the source"*; *"An instrument that can't distinguish these cases demonstrates its own inadequacy"*; *"The answer is unreached until a better instrument exists."*
