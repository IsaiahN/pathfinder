## 1. Substantive world sources, actually opened

| what | URL | verified content and why it bears |
|---|---|---|
| Abil et al. 2024, "Darwinian Evolution of Self-Replicating DNA in a Synthetic Protocell", Nature Communications 15:9091 | https://www.nature.com/articles/s41467-024-53226-0 | Open access. Full abstract, introduction and results read. Reports intermittent and semi-continuous evolution in liposomes; self-replicator survives multiple rounds and *"within only ten evolution rounds, accumulates mutations conferring a selection advantage"*. States the three rival outcomes explicitly (extinction, neutral drift, adaptive evolution). Includes the essential bulk no-liposome control: *"self-replication was totally suppressed at round 6"*. Also reports an honest contamination event in one campaign. Governing witness for F4 and F5. |
| Kurihara et al. 2015, "A recursive vesicle-based model protocell with a primitive model cell cycle", Nature Communications 6:8352 | https://pmc.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/articles/PMC4598553/ | Full text read. *"the ability of a protocell to recursively self-proliferate for multiple generations has not been demonstrated"*; then demonstrates grandmother → mother → daughter → granddaughter GVs via pH-induced fusion with conveyer GVs. Explicitly says the genotype-phenotype correlation is a prospect, not a demonstrated adaptation: *"this event could be regarded as evolution"* is stated conditionally. Governing witness for F6. |
| Chen and Walde 2010, "From Self-Assembled Vesicles to Protocells", Cold Spring Harbor Perspectives in Biology 2:a002170 | https://pmc.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/articles/PMC2890201/ | Full text read. Gives the NASA operational definition *"a self-sustaining chemical system capable of Darwinian evolution"* (Joyce 1994), the stochastic-corrector mechanism by which *"Darwinian competition between 'cells' could have emerged simply on encapsulation of replicases or metabolic cycles inside vesicles"*, and the warning that life may be *"a quantitative, not qualitative, trait"*. Governing witness for the union census. |
| Ameta et al. 2021, "Self-Reproduction and Darwinian Evolution in Autocatalytic Chemical Reaction Systems", Life 11(4):308 | https://pmc.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/articles/PMC8066523/ | Full text read. Defines Darwinian evolution by *"variation, heredity, and differential fitness, following the formulation by Lewontin and Godfrey-Smith"*. Warns to *"distinguish heritable compositional variation from trivial compositional variation resulting as a consequence of boundary conditions"* and that *"selection among self-reproducing units is fine-tuned"*. States honestly that experiments are *"either still catching up or altogether lacking"*. Governing witness for F1. |
| Liu et al. 2025, "Selection for photocatalytic function through Darwinian evolution of synthetic self-replicators", Nature Catalysis 8:1000-1009 | https://www.nature.com/articles/s41929-025-01409-3 | Abstract, figure list, references and author information read; full text paywalled and not claimed as read. *"the emergence of Darwinian evolution in two systems of self-replicating molecules in which natural selection favours replicator mutants best capable of catalysing the production of the precursors required for their own replication"*. A genuinely different platform (peptide self-replicators, flow setup, functional catalytic trait), so a low-overlap witness for F1 and F3. |
| Bunin and Rivoire, "Evolutionary features in a minimal physical system: directionality, diversity, selection, growth, inheritance, and adaptation", arXiv:2411.03843 | https://arxiv.org/html/2411.03843v1 | Full HTML read. *"reproduction alone is not sufficient for Darwinian evolution: significant diversity must also be generated and inherited"*. Their model *"does not involve separate mechanisms for metabolism, replication and compartmentalization"*. The strongest low-overlap challenge in the pool; handled as strain S5, returned to census without flipping the verdict. |

## 6. Distribution: offer, do not push

Figure-grounded rules:

- **Fig 10 :52 - *"Bring a characterised residual, never the source."*** The first contact is the four-slot discriminator and R1 to R7. The derivation stays behind until stage 1 has a recorded verdict.
- **Fig 10 :42 - *"A verifier that can reconstruct the claim cannot verify it."*** A recipient who has absorbed this folder before being asked has joined the frame being checked.
- **Fig 9 :27-28 - *"describe it before you go looking, or any frame you pick will seem to fit"*.** Ask what they would expect to see if the shape claim were true before naming an assay.
- **Fig 2 :40 - *"disqualifying for a verdict: agreement about the world is worthless when the agreeing frames share an evidence pool"*.** Do not rank recipients by receptiveness. A list of likely believers is a convention, not corroboration.
- **Fig 4 :22 - *"Only methods cross up. Only head starts come down."*** Stage 3 offers the formula as a reusable method. It never offers this folder's answer as a result to be replayed.

**Recommended order in a reply:** (1) the four-slot discriminator and the seven refuters; (2) ask what the recipient would count as a failure; (3) record that verdict; (4) only then, if they are curious, the six source links, with the note that the figures, not this summary, are the authority; (5) if asked, the formula as a method, with the instruction to write a refuter before running it.

**Two-stage offer, stated in the exemplar's terms.** Stage 1 validates the output against the ground. Stage 2 is opt-in reading of the theory. Stage 3 is reuse of the formula. A stage-2 agreement is never pooled as corroboration, because the recipient has by then seen the interiors.
