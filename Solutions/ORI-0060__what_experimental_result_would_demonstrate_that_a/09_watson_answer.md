## VERDICT (from the figures)

The demonstrating result is a four-slot conjunction with a fifth demonstration verdict riding on it: boundary coupling with a failing bulk control, heritable variation that survives selector removal, differential fitness measured by an independent ground, and cumulative pre-registered improvement that persists when selection is relaxed, all recorded as a lineage. Verdicts are kept per domain and never averaged. The figures return the shape and the refuters. The protocol, round count and threshold are a declared `gap`. Status `specified`, `candidate`, not `grounded`; unreached rather than unreachable.

- cost if wrong: a genuine protocell lineage could be dismissed, or a harness artifact could be celebrated as evolution, because the discriminator was built to the wrong slot.
