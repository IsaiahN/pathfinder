# Outreach draft - check the discriminator before you decide about the framework

**Use:** one-to-one, or as a cover note attached to a submission through a public venue in `03_public_contact_points.csv`. **Not** for bulk send.

**Hard precondition:** do not send until the three rival outcomes and R1 to R7 in `01_figure_grounded_audit.md` are attached to the protocol. The offer collapses on contact if the stage-1 object cannot survive a check.

---

**Subject:** What would you count as a failure of this discriminator?

> Hi [name],
>
> I have one specific question I would like you to check. I am not asking you to read a theory first, and I am not asking you to agree with anything. If the object below does not survive, there is nothing else to look at.
>
> **The question (`specified`, not `grounded`):** what result would demonstrate that a protocell lineage undergoes sustained Darwinian evolution? The shape I get is four separately anchored readings, not one number.
>
> 1. The daughter compartment inherits both boundary state and contents, and the no-compartment control does not improve the same way.
> 2. Heritable states vary, and a parent-offspring correlation survives removal of the selector.
> 3. Variants differ in amplification under a defined pressure, measured by an assay that does not use the trait that defined the variant, with a no-pressure control.
> 4. A pre-registered trait improves cumulatively across rounds, and the improvement persists when selection is relaxed.
>
> The verdict about the lineage rides on all four. A single fold-change number is not the verdict; it can hide a live heredity slot inside an average.
>
> **What I am not claiming:** that any particular assay is required, that ten rounds is the right number, or that a 5-fold improvement is a threshold. The framework returns a shape, not a name. qPCR, sequencing, flow cytometry and freeze-thaw transfer are bindings from published work, not figure-derived requirements, and I can show where each came from.
>
> **The rival outcomes I wrote down before this:** one-off replication; neutral drift; chemical growth without differential fitness; selection without heritability; a vessel or harness artifact; a criterion written after seeing the data; a fitness assay calibrated from the lineages it ranks.
>
> **What would refute the shape:**
> 1. the improvement appears at the same magnitude in the bulk, no-compartment control;
> 2. daughter state is uncorrelated with parent state once the selector is removed;
> 3. the fitness reading and the variant definition come from the same sample and the same instrument;
> 4. the trend vanishes when the pressure is relaxed or needs continuing experimenter intervention;
> 5. the criterion was written after the data;
> 6. the anchor moves when pushed;
> 7. the lineage record is missing, so the order of changes cannot be checked.
>
> These were written before this message. If any holds, I withdraw "demonstrates" and report "unreached". A one-line "this fails because..." is more useful to me than agreement. If you would rather not engage, no reply is needed.
>
> **If it survives, and only then:** I will send the sources and the figures. The figures are the authority, not my summary of them.
>
> **If you want to reuse the method:** the formula is public. Run it on a problem you know well and can check against a ground. Write down what would refute your result *before* you run it. A reported failure is more useful to me than a reported success.
