## The residual in effect terms

The premise is conditional: *if* the dark-energy equation of state w(z) changes, what shape does that change have? The three offered answers are not three values of one variable. They are three predicates over one live residual: the **shape** of the change (smooth), the **duration or closure** of the change (temporary), and the **source** of the change (a new field). A single model can satisfy more than one at once: thawing quintessence is smooth and involves a new field; early dark energy is temporary and involves a new field. The Tether's first move is therefore to unbundle the question before searching (Fig 9 :54), and to keep each probe's residual separate rather than averaging it (Fig 1 :19).

The answer does not name a field. Under the figures, smoothness is a derivative that a composition leaves behind, not a primitive (Fig 13 :43-44, Fig 13 :124-125); a temporary phase is a loop whose sign decides whether it closes (Fig 12 :121); and a new field is a mint or import governed by guards, not a name the framework can supply (Fig 5 :41, Fig 6 :14, Fig 13 :135). Whether the change is real at all is a ground question, and the current world sources conflict on it.
