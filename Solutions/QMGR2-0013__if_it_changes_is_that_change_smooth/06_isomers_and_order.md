## DERIVATION

### Q1 - What structure does a smooth change require?

- Fig 13 :43-44: *"A continuum was the seventh and was removed for failing exactly this: it is a spectrum without gaps, or smoothness over space and time."*
- Fig 13 :124-125: *"Smoothness, symmetry, chaos and discreteness are each what a particular composition leaves behind, and none is a seventh member."*

- ⇒ A change over cosmic time is a gradient bonded to the time state, and smoothness is the ordering (spectrum-like) that the composition leaves. Two structures are involved: gradient for directedness, spectrum for more and less. Neither is a new primitive.
- **Falsifier, written before the run:** an instrument that resolves w(z) and shows a discontinuity not derivable from any composition of the six, or a derivation of smoothness as a seventh member. Either breaks this reading.

### Q2 - What structure does a temporary change require?

- Fig 12 :121: **"cycle. The output re-enters as input. The sign of the loop decides whether it closes, and a chain cannot close by construction."**
- Fig 12 :119: **"catalyst. A part that survives its own use."**
- ⇒ A phase that peaks and returns to baseline is a cycle that closes. The sign of the loop decides. A monotonic roll is a chain, which cannot close by construction. These are different bond arrangements over the same held atoms, so no new primitive is needed to express either.
- The world analogue (Poulin, Smith and Karwal 2023): early dark energy is frozen, becomes dynamical at a critical redshift, then dilutes faster than radiation, so f_EDE returns to zero. That is a closing loop in physical terms. Thawing quintessence (Tsujikawa 2013) is the chain case: w starts near -1 and rises at late times.
- **Falsifier:** a temporary phase whose residual does not re-enter as input, or a chain that demonstrably closes. Either breaks Fig 12 :121.

It is owed only if the residual persists and does not trend down under the held atoms, the candidate composes from what is held, it is novel relative to atoms (Fig 6 :22), and stating it plus the leftover costs less than the confusion it removes. No amount of fitting w(z) inside the current frame manufactures the atom.
- **Falsifier:** a field admitted with a guard at zero that settles at the ground, or a composition inside the closure that adds an atom.

### Q4 - Are smooth and new field (or temporary and new field) isomers?

- Fig 12 :117: **"isomer. Same atoms, different bond, different substance. The difference is invisible in the formula and decisive in the behaviour."**
- Fig 12 :123: **"functional group. A substructure whose presence fixes the outcome. The group says what kind; the qualifier says which member."**
- As the options are written, they share no atom set at all. "Smooth" and "temporary" are shapes of the change; "new field" is a source. They are not isomers of each other because isomerism requires the same atoms under different bonds.
- The real isomer claim is **within** the field-model frame: a rolling field and a transient field share the atom set {field, potential, kinetic energy, Hubble friction} and differ in the arrangement (Fig 12 :117). That is a checkable structural claim in the physics frame, and the world literature confirms it: Tsujikawa's thawing class and Poulin et al.'s early dark energy class use the same scalar-field equation with different potentials and transition mechanisms.
- The weaker framework claim is a **functional group**: both are compositions of a state with a structure and re-enter as input. Fig 12 :123: *"the group says what kind; the qualifier says which member."*
- **Falsifier:** show that one of the two models requires an atom the other cannot express. Then the isomer claim fails and the functional-group claim stands.

### Q5 - What order is the union?

- Fig 3 :20: **"The chain is ordered by dependency, not by importance."**
- Fig 12 :104: **"A settled molecule becomes an atom for whatever composes over it. So irreducible is a fact about a level and never about the world"**

- Order: (1) settle whether the residual is in the world or in the channel, otherwise every shape claim is a reading of nothing (Fig 3); (2) describe shape and duration separately, per probe; (3) decide whether a new atom is owed, with a source gate and shadow test. A settled first-order description can become an atom for the next level, but only after settlement.
- **Falsifier:** a shape claim that settles at the ground while the premise remains contested; this would reverse the dependency order.

## CONCLUSION

The three predicates need three different bond arrangements, none of which requires a seventh primitive. Smoothness is a remainder over a spectrum and a gradient over time; temporary is a closing cycle whose sign decides; a new field is an import that moves the wall and is priced by the mint guards. The two physical model classes are isomers within the field-model atom set, not alternatives to one another, which is why the original question's multiple-choice form fails. The order of inquiry runs from the ground to the shape to the atom, and cannot be reversed without reading a step whose input never arrived.
