## QUESTION

Decide, in effect terms, what the Tether says about a residual in the dark-energy equation of state w(z), conditional on that residual being real. Option A: the change is smooth. Option B: the change is temporary. Option C: the change is connected to a new field. What each leads to: A commits to a shape of the time evolution; B commits to the closure of a loop; C commits to a new atom in the closure. These are not exclusive: a thawing field is smooth and new; an early dark energy field is temporary and new. So the real question is whether these are three predicates over one residual, and what shape each predicate has under the figures.

Currency: composition inside closure(Gamma) and settlement against the ground. Anchor: the figures as metalaws, plus the ground for any measurement. Question type: a legality question about what may compose, not an existence question.

Strain test applied: the claim that smoothness is not a primitive governs a generating set of six terms; the existence of smooth field models in the world governs imports and their compositions. Different objects, so no strain after re-census.

## VERDICT (from the figures)

The shape survives; the premise and the field name do not. The question is a union of three predicates and must be unbundled before search. Smoothness is a remainder a cross-kind composition leaves behind, not a seventh primitive. A temporary phase is a closing loop whose sign decides; a monotonic roll is a chain that cannot close. A new field is an atom that only import can add, under a product of guards with a source gate, shadow test and independence debit. The framework returns these shapes and cannot name a field. Whether the change exists at all is contested at the ground, so the verdict is `specified`, ungrounded and `candidate`. No sigma level settles it.

- **cost if wrong:** bounded and locatable. The packet states modes, three checkable sub-claims, verbatim lines and refuters before the conclusion, so an error is findable. The real cost is spending the independence of outside readers by sending a field name or a conclusion instead of a generator and a refuter, which Fig 2 :42 prices as "Buy independence in proportion to the cost of being wrong." That cost is avoidable by leading with the unbundling and the refuter.
- **undoable:** yes. The field name is withheld, the premise is conditional, and the shape claims are separate, so any one can be withdrawn without touching the others. No primitive has to be removed from the record. A sent packet can be superseded by a corrected one.
- **reversed by:** a ground reading, not agreement. An instrument that resolves w(z) across the transition would settle smooth versus discontinuous; a measurement of f_EDE across the critical epoch would settle closure; cross-probe consistency tests would separate a physical field from a channel artifact; a derivation of a seventh primitive or a field admitted with a guard at zero that still settles would break the shape claim. Until one of these fires, the verdict stands as `candidate`. Prestige or agreement is not such an instrument.
