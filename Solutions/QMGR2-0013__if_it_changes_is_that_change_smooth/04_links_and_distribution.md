## Wider literature that bears on the residual

These are read sources, not a reading list to hand over before stage one. Their role is corroboration only.

1. **The premise is contested.** DESI DR2 reports evolving dark energy preferred over ΛCDM; Afroz and Mukherjee (2026) find the same preference is mimicked by an unaccounted inconsistency between supernovae and BAO. Under the figures this is a channel-versus-ground question, not a vote to be settled by counting papers (Fig 10 :24, Fig 2 :35).
2. **Smooth is an assumption about the potential, not a measurement.** Crittenden, Majerotto and Piazza (2007) write that once variation in time is allowed, w(z) "appears to be virtually a free function", and impose "some level of smoothness in the configuration space of the field" to make models constrainable. That is exactly the figure's move: smoothness is what a composition leaves, not a primitive handed down.
3. **New field and smooth are compatible.** Tsujikawa (2013) describes thawing quintessence: the field is nearly frozen by Hubble friction, w is about -1, and deviation occurs only recently. Smoothness and a new field are not exclusive.
4. **New field and temporary are compatible.** Poulin, Smith and Karwal (2023) describe early dark energy: a scalar field active before recombination that "quickly dilutes away at a redshift close to matter-radiation equality". The phase closes; the loop's sign decides.
5. **Naming needs an instrument.** The Hard Problem is About Source: a reading "cannot comment on the source", and the color case was settled "not by argument, but by the arrival of a better instrument". Applying that here: resolving which field, if any, needs a source-discriminating observation, not a preference over parametrizations.

## Distribution: offer, do not push

Figure-grounded reasons:

- **Fig 4 :22** *"Only methods cross up. Only head starts come down."* Sending the full architecture before a recipient has described a residual sends a recording, not a generator. Lead with the unbundling and the refuter; offer links after.
- **Fig 10 :42** *"A verifier that can reconstruct the claim cannot verify it."* Seat 4 must not hold the interiors. A recipient who has absorbed the framework before being asked for a verdict has joined the frame being checked.
- **Fig 9 :27-28** *"describe it before you go looking, or any frame you pick will seem to fit."* Ask what they would expect to see if w(z) were smooth or closing before giving them the articles.
- **Fig 2 :40** *"agreement about the world is worthless when the agreeing frames share an evidence pool"* A send selected for people who already accept evolving dark energy produces a convention, not corroboration.
- **Fig 9 :60** *"Use filters for the budget and witnesses for the verdict. Never let a filter hand you a verdict."* Significance levels are filters for how much data to gather, not witnesses for which model is true.

**Recommended order in a reply:** (1) the shape claim and its refuter; (2) ask what they would count as a failure, and record the answer; (3) only then the links, with the note that the figures, not the summary, are the authority; (4) offer the formula as a method to reuse, with the instruction that each user writes their own refuter and states their own mode.
