## 0. The residual under audit

> **Question:** If it changes, is that change smooth, temporary, or connected to a new field?
>
> **Restated as an effect:** Given a live residual in the expansion history H(z) and the dark-energy equation of state w(z), decide what form a departure from w = -1 has. Option A: the change is smooth. Option B: the change is temporary. Option C: the change is connected to a new field. What each leads to: A commits to a shape of the time evolution; B commits to the closure of a loop; C commits to a new atom in the frame's closure. These are not exclusive: a new field can roll smoothly (thawing quintessence) or appear briefly and dilute (early dark energy). The question is a bundled union, not a three-way partition.

**Bound ordinary term (Formula, "Direction"):** an answer would look like a predicate over one residual along three axes, with a stated shape for each axis and a stated ground that would settle it. It would not look like a name of a field, because naming is not available to the framework (Fig 13 :135).

## 1. Domain census (before any figure line is chosen)

R by arity, symmetry and scale, then one line per domain with actors, currency and anchor. No nameable anchor means it is not a domain yet.

- **R, the residual.** The departure of w(z) from -1, if any. Arity: three predicates over one residual (shape, duration, source), indexed per observation slot, not one global number (Fig 1 :19). Symmetry: w(z) is an ordering over time (spectrum-like, more and less) combined with a directedness over time (a gradient over time is a distinct term from a gradient over space, Fig 13 :76). It is not a symmetric bond. Scale: first order at the level of the dark-energy component, second order if that component is settled and composed over (Fig 12 :104).
- **Domain 1, the observation frame.** Actors: Type Ia supernovae (candles), BAO standard ruler, CMB acoustic scale and lensing, growth and weak lensing. Currency: distance-redshift measurements and H(z). Anchor: the instruments, which do not change their answer because a model thinks something (Fig 2 :29-30). This is a live domain: the residual is reported at 3.1σ to 4.2σ by DESI DR2, and contested by a distance-duality reanalysis.
- **Domain 2, the field-model frame.** Actors: the scalar field, its potential, its mass, the smoothness scale M, and w itself. Currency: potential and kinetic energy, and the shape of V(phi). Anchor: an instrument that discriminates a smooth roll from a transition. Not yet available, so this domain returns a shape, not a name. Question type: union with Domain 1, not a partition (the predicates overlap).
- **Domain 3, the framework-internal composition frame.** Actors: six terms (space, time, energy as states; spectrum, gradient, network as structures), seven bonds and negation. Currency: composition inside closure(Gamma), then settlement. Anchor: the figures as metalaws, plus the ground for any measurement. This is a legality question (what may compose), not an existence question.
- **Dependency order.** First settle whether the residual is in the world or in the channel (Domain 1, Fig 10 :24). Then characterize shape and duration (Domain 2). Then decide whether a new atom is owed (Domain 3, Fig 5, Fig 6). Fig 3 :20: *"The chain is ordered by dependency, not by importance."* A step whose input never arrives cannot be diagnosed.

**Partition or union:** union. A single lever cannot clear all three conditions because a new field can be smooth or temporary, and smoothness does not settle duration. Fig 9 :55: *"A bundled problem is usually a union of overlapping conditions, not a list of separate ones."* Unbundle, then run one loop per predicate.

## 3. Findings

### F1 - Smoothness is a remainder, not a primitive (decisive for option A)

Fig 13 :43-44:

> **"A continuum was the seventh and was removed for failing exactly this: it is a spectrum without gaps, or smoothness over space and time."**

Fig 13 :124-125:

> **"Smoothness, symmetry, chaos and discreteness are each what a particular composition leaves behind, and none is a seventh member."**

Smoothness was explicitly removed from the generating set for failing minimality. It is what a turn leaves behind, not a member of the six. So "is the change smooth" is not a question about adding a primitive; it is a question about what the composition of a state (time) with a structure (spectrum for ordering, gradient for directedness) leaves at this level. The framework can say what smoothness would have to be (a derived shape). It cannot, without a ground reading, say whether this residual has it.

**Mode:** `general` for the structural claim, `specified` for its application to w(z). **Status:** single-source for the minimality claim (both lines are in Fig 13, so overlap is high, not triangulated). The level-relativity that makes a remainder level-dependent is separately triangulated by Fig 12 :104. **Refuter:** derive smoothness from the other five terms of the minimum set, or show a seventh member is required by an established law; either breaks Fig 13. An instrument that resolves w(z) and shows a discontinuity where the composition predicts a remainder would break the application.

### F2 - The three options are a union, not a partition (decisive for the question as posed)

Fig 9 :54:

> **"And when the split lands on a union rather than a partition, unbundle before you search again."**

Fig 9 :55:

> **"A bundled problem is usually a union of overlapping conditions, not a list of separate ones."**

Fig 1 :19:

> **"The residual is indexed by object slot, not aggregated."**

Smooth (shape of w(z)), temporary (whether the phase closes) and new field (source of the component) are predicates over one residual, not exclusive values of one variable. The world confirms the overlap: Tsujikawa's thawing quintessence is a new field whose w approaches -1 smoothly and changes only recently; Poulin, Smith and Karwal's early dark energy is a new field that is temporary, peaking near recombination and diluting away. Neither is a counter-example to the other. A multiple-choice reading of the question would discard exactly the intersections that are physically populated.

**Mode:** `general` for the unbundling rule, `specified` for its application here. **Status:** triangulated across Fig 9 and Fig 1, two figures with low overlap (union/partition rule versus per-slot residual rule), and corroborated by two independent cosmology sources (Tsujikawa 2013; Poulin, Smith and Karwal 2023). **Refuter:** show one lever that clears all three predicates simultaneously, that is, a single measurement that decides shape, duration and source without remainder; this would break Fig 9 :54-56.

A scalar field is not among the six. "Connected to a new field" therefore means adding an atom, which only import can do (Fig 6 :14, :21). The mint guards are a product: SUPPORT (a live positive residual somewhere), REACHABILITY (the candidate composes from held atoms), NOVELTY (it is not already in the library) and the MDL bargain (stating it plus what remains costs less than the confusion it removes). ### F5 - Temporary maps to a cycle, not a chain

Fig 12 :121:

> **"cycle. The output re-enters as input. The sign of the loop decides whether it closes, and a chain cannot close by construction."**

Fig 12 :119:

> **"catalyst. A part that survives its own use."**

Fig 12 :82:

> **"A with B removed or absent"**

A change that returns to baseline is a loop that closes; the sign of the loop decides. A monotonic roll that never returns is a chain, and a chain cannot close by construction. So "temporary" is a structural claim about closure, not about the magnitude of w or the size of the energy density. In the world, early dark energy is exactly a closing loop: the field is frozen, becomes dynamical at a critical redshift, then dilutes faster than radiation, so f_EDE returns to zero. Thawing quintessence is the non-closing case. Operators table :53-58 adds that a phase that is refused rather than merely absent requires negation (*"a refutation is a negation, which is why a vocabulary without it can only accumulate"*), which is a unary operator, not a bond, and is not counted in the bond price.

**Mode:** `specified`. **Status:** single-source for the bond definition (Fig 12), corroborated by an independent cosmology review (Poulin, Smith and Karwal 2023) that describes the same closing dynamics in physical terms. **Refuter:** exhibit a temporary dark-energy phase whose residual does not re-enter as input, or a chain that closes; either breaks Fig 12 :121.

## 4. Pre-registered refuter (written before the conclusion)

Required before any outreach. Seat Map :287: *"a falsifier written before the run is a design instrument, not only an honesty device."*

The shape claim is refuted if any of the following holds:

1. A seventh primitive is derived from the six, or smoothness is admitted as a primitive (breaks Fig 13 :43-44 and the minimality claim).
2. An instrument resolves w(z) across the claimed transition and shows a discontinuity where the cross-kind composition predicts a smooth remainder, or shows a closing phase with no sign change (breaks F1 and Fig 12 :121).
3. A new field is imported with a guard at zero, no source gate, no shadow test, or no independence debit, and still settles at the ground (breaks Fig 5 :41, Fig 6 :24, Fig 8).
4. A single lever is shown to clear shape, duration and source at once, so the union reading is false (breaks Fig 9 :54-56).
5. The distance-duality disagreement is settled in favour of a real evolving component by a ground reading that both analyses accept; then F6's caution is withdrawn and the premise becomes `grounded`.

Until one is run, no part of this may be labelled `grounded`. A failure is more useful than an endorsement.

### Strains after re-census

- **Fig 13 minimality versus the existence of a smooth field in the world.** Not strained after re-census: Fig 13 governs the six primitives of a generating set; a scalar field is an import from the physics frame, and smoothness is what its composition leaves behind. Different objects.
- **Fig 12 cycle versus a monotonic roll.** Not strained: a cycle and a chain are different bond arrangements; the sign of the loop decides which applies. Both are figure-licensed; only one fits a given residual.

### Final audit verdict

The shape survives; the premise and the field name do not. Smoothness is a remainder, the three options are a union to be unbundled, a temporary phase is a closing loop, and a new field is an atom owed only under a product of guards with provenance. The change itself is `specified`, ungrounded and contested at the ground. No figure line selects a specific field. Status: `candidate`, to be settled by an instrument that resolves w(z) across the transition and by a source gate on any proposed field.
