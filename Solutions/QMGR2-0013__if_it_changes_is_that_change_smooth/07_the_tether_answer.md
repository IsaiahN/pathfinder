# The Tether's answer: if dark energy changes, what shape is the change?

**Mode: `specified` on the three shape claims, `general` on the structural rules, not `grounded`.** This is a shape and a route, not a result. Simulated derivation checked against the figures (`05_what_shape_means.md` §6), not a ground reading.

## 1. The residual it answers

The premise is conditional: *if* w(z) departs from -1, what form does the departure have? The residual is indexed per probe (supernovae, BAO, CMB lensing, growth), not aggregated (Fig 1 :19). The offered options are not three values of one variable but three predicates over one residual: shape, duration, source. DESI DR2 reports a preference for a time-evolving equation of state; a 2026 distance-duality analysis contests it. So even the premise is unsettled.

## 2. The shape

1. **The question must be unbundled before it is searched.** Fig 9 :54: *"And when the split lands on a union rather than a partition, unbundle before you search again."* Smoothness, duration and source overlap: a thawing field is smooth and new; an early dark energy field is temporary and new. A multiple choice discards the populated intersections. Run one loop per predicate.

2. **Smooth is a remainder, not a primitive.** Fig 13 :43-44: *"A continuum was the seventh and was removed for failing exactly this: it is a spectrum without gaps, or smoothness over space and time."* Fig 13 :124-125: *"Smoothness, symmetry, chaos and discreteness are each what a particular composition leaves behind, and none is a seventh member."* A change over time is a gradient bonded to the time state, and smoothness is the ordering the composition leaves. The framework can state what smoothness would be; without a ground reading it cannot say that this residual has it.

3. **Temporary is a closing cycle, sign decides.** Fig 12 :121: *"cycle. The output re-enters as input. The sign of the loop decides whether it closes, and a chain cannot close by construction."* A phase that peaks and returns to baseline is a loop that closes. A monotonic roll is a chain and cannot close. "Temporary" is a structural claim about closure, not a small value of w.

4. **A new field is an atom owed under a product of guards.** Fig 5 :41: *"Any one of them at zero stops the mint entirely."* Fig 6 :14: *"the only operator that moves the wall"* Fig 6 :21: *"MINT composes inside the closure and can never add an atom."* A scalar field is not among the six, so adding it is an import, gated by support, reachability, novelty and the description-length bargain, with a source gate and shadow test. Fig 6 :24: *"Import from a live peer is debited against that frame's independence. A wrong atom costs more than starting from primitives."*

5. **The framework does not name the field.** Fig 13 :135: *"It cannot say which primitive is missing, because naming it would mean already holding it."* Fig 9 :32: *"you get a kind of thing, never the answer itself."* Quintessence, phantom energy and axion-like early dark energy are candidates from the physics frame, provenanced as imports. None is a figure output.

## 3. What it explicitly does not say

- Not that dark energy changes. The premise is conditional and contested at the ground (Fig 10 :24).
- Not which field is responsible. No figure line selects one, and no instrument has discriminated among them.
- Not that "smooth", "temporary" and "new field" are exclusive. They are a union of predicates (Fig 9 :54-56).
- Not that smoothness is a fundamental property of space or of the vacuum. It was removed from the minimum set (Fig 13 :43-44).
- Not a significance verdict. Fig 9 :60: *"Use filters for the budget and witnesses for the verdict. Never let a filter hand you a verdict."* The 3.1 to 4.2 sigma preference is a budget filter.
- Not `grounded`. No measurement settles left(R, phi) here.

## 4. What would settle it — the ground

A source-discriminating instrument, not a further argument. *The Hard Problem is About Source*: a reading *"cannot comment on the source"*, and the color case was resolved *"not by argument, but by the arrival of a better instrument."* Here:

- **Shape test:** does w(z) resolve as a smooth remainder across the claimed transition, or as a discontinuity? An instrument that distinguishes these would settle option A.
- **Closure test:** does the residual re-enter as input and return to baseline, with the sign of the loop deciding? A measurement of f_EDE across the critical epoch would settle option B.
- **Source test:** which model, if any, produces the same w(z) reading? Cross-probe consistency (distance duality, CMB lensing, growth, BBN) is the instrument that can discriminate a physical field from a channel artifact (Fig 10 :24).
- **Standing falsifiers from `01`:** derive a seventh primitive; show a guard at zero still settling; show one lever clearing all three predicates; settle the channel disagreement in favour of a real component with a ground both analyses accept.

## 5. The honest stop

**Unreached, not unreachable.** Fig 8 :39: *"So a search that finds nothing is never a proof of absence."* The six are a visible guess, not nature's own list; another sensorium's minimum set need not be these six. The answer stops at the limit of the available instruments and records the lineage so the stop is checkable. Indiscernibility is not identity. The field, if any, stays unnamed until a ground settles it.

## 6. One-paragraph version for outreach

It says the question is a bundle, not a multiple choice: smoothness is the shape a cross-kind composition leaves behind, not a seventh primitive; a temporary phase is a loop whose sign decides whether it closes, and a chain cannot close by construction; and a new field is an atom that only import can add, under a product of guards with a provenance record. The two physical model classes, thawing quintessence and transient early dark energy, are not rivals but share a scalar-field atom set arranged differently, which is why both "smooth" and "temporary" can coexist with "new field". What is offered is the shape any answer must have, four refuters written in advance, and the instrument that would close it. The premise stays conditional until the ground settles whether there is a change at all.
