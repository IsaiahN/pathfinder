## 1. A shape is a constraint stated before a name is available

The canonical rule: the framework returns *"what a missing thing would have to look like (a shape, not a name)."* Fig 9 :32: *"you get a kind of thing, never the answer itself."* Fig 13 :135: *"It cannot say which primitive is missing, because naming it would mean already holding it."*

From *The Library of Babel* (opened 2026-11-23): *"you can cut your search short if the set of books returned is small and descriptive enough that it provides a general shape of the answer that satisfies your question."* Also: *"With each new term gained, you narrow the space and deepen your vocabulary."*

For this question a **shape** is any of the following, none of which is a field name:

- smoothness as a remainder a cross-kind composition leaves behind, not a seventh primitive;
- a closing loop whose sign decides whether a temporary phase returns to baseline;
- the statement that a new atom is owed under a product of guards, without saying which atom.

"Quintessence", "phantom" and "axion-like early dark energy" are names. They are candidates from the physics frame, not outputs of the framework. That is the whole distinction this question tests.

## 2. How the shape is obtained for this residual

- *Library of Babel*: use the vocabulary already held that is closest to the thing, look for recurring new terms that overlap or correlate with it, recurse. The essay calls this proximity and bootstrapping convergence.
- Fig 6 :30 (cited in *Decoding Tacit Knowledge*, opened 2026-11-23): *"The description travels. Fails by being sharpened into terms only we can read. Cast it in effect terms."* Fig 9 :30-31: *"your words are borrowed too, so describe the effect rather than the cause."* So describe w(z) in effect terms, a change in the ordered succession of differences, before asking which model produces it.
- *Decoding Tacit Knowledge* (opened 2026-11-23): *"When contact isn't possible, the best you can send is a plain description of what you'd expect to see, because anyone can check that against the world."* This is why the outreach draft asks what a recipient would expect to see if the change were smooth or closing, rather than sending the theory.
- *Library of Babel* on a bundled question: *"Perhaps your problem was a bundle or conflation of several problems masquerading as one? You will need to search all of those problems individually"*. This is the essay-level statement of Fig 9 :54. The three predicates here are such a bundle.

## 3. What licenses a name

Only two things, both outside the shape itself:

1. **The ground settles it.** Formula step 5: *"the ground settles it. Verified only by what pays. A gate is not the ground."* For this question that means an instrument that resolves w(z) across the claimed transition, not a better fit statistic.
2. **A provenanced import from a frame whose closure differs** (Fig 6 :23, Fig 8 :50-52), with a source gate and a shadow test. A scalar field proposed as dark energy is an import from the field-model frame. Its name is licensed by its provenance, not by the six-term set.

*They're Scanning Books When They Should* (carried quote from the exemplar): *"The real import is not a new term in the existing space. It is a new dimension of the space."* And: *"Declaring the primitives does not make them neutral. It makes the choice visible."* The six are a visible guess, not nature's own list. Turning one into the answer to a slot hides that choice.

**For this question:** neither condition is met. No instrument has resolved the change, and the conflict between DESI DR2 and the distance-duality reanalysis is unresolved. So the name stays withheld.

## 4. Why a reading does not determine the source

*The Hard Problem is About Source* (opened 2026-11-23) gives the mechanism. An instrument that reads green *"cannot comment on the source"*; the same reading can come from one green light, from yellow plus blue, from RGB, from CMYK, or from a scheme not yet discovered. The essay: *"If several paths create the same behavior, then behavior is a projection with lossy information (it cannot define consciousness in full)."* And the resolution: *"It was proven wrong not by argument, but by the arrival of a better instrument."*

Applied here: a measured w(z) is a reading of the expansion history. Several underlying models (rolling scalar, transient field, modified gravity, or an unmodeled dataset inconsistency) can produce the same w(z) over a limited redshift range. The reading does not determine the source. Crittenden, Majerotto and Piazza (2007) make the same point in cosmology: once variation is allowed, w(z) *"appears to be virtually a free function"*, which is why they impose *"some level of smoothness in the configuration space of the field"* as an assumption. The assumption is not a measurement. What is owed is a source-discriminating observation, not another parametrization.

*The Recursive Self-Improvement Trap* (carried quote from the exemplar): *"the output is only as good as the validator. The agent, or the tool, or the frame, or the theory, cannot validate itself."*

## 5. Q: Does "shape" license naming a field here? ANSWERED, no.

Nothing in the framework licenses the name. The framework returns the shape of what a candidate answer must have: a remainder for smoothness, a signed loop for duration, an atom owed under guards for a new field. A specific field is licensed only by a ground settlement or by a provenanced import with a shadow test. Until one of those, the name is the searcher's own contribution and must be recorded as such. The record here does not name one.

## 6. Two consequences for outreach

**Prestige is not action.** From *The Recursive Self-Improvement Trap* (carried quote): *"Prestige must never be allowed to validate an action directly... Prestige can fund an action. It can give an action a budget to be tried. But it cannot settle an action. Only the ground can settle it. The Ground Maintainer must be blind to prestige."* A list of recipients chosen because they already favour evolving dark energy is the named collapse, not corroboration. The legitimate ask is a budget to be tried and a refuter to be checked.

**This audit is a simulation, not a ground reading.** Same essay: *"LLMs can quickly simulate an approximate answer/output... However, the truly testable output would need to be derived from the real architecture or working the formula yourself."* LLM output is *"simulacra"*. So `01_figure_grounded_audit.md` is a simulated derivation checked for internal consistency against the figures. It is not a test of the claim and cannot settle anything it reports on.

## 7. Correction to my own §4

I initially wrote that the metamerism argument forbids naming any field whatsoever. That overstates it. *The Hard Problem is About Source* says an instrument cannot determine the source from its reading; it does not say sources are unknowable. The same essay says the spectrometer settled the color case. The correct claim here is narrower: naming a field from w(z) alone is a metamerism error because no discriminating instrument has been applied, and the framework itself cannot supply the name. A ground instrument could. The correction is recorded rather than silently applied.
