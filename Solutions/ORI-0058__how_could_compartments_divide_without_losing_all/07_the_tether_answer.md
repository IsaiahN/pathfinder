# The Tether's answer: how compartments could divide without losing contents or information

**Mode: `specified` on the shape, `general` on the cross-scale schema. Not `grounded`.** This is a shape and a route, not a result. Simulated derivation checked against the figures (`05_what_shape_means.md` §6), not a ground reading.

## 1. The residual it answers

The section's question is a union compound: boundary growth and division, content retention, and genetic replication and partition are coupled (Fig 9 :57 *"a case in two conditions is not a harder case of either"*). The two requirements pull against each other because the opening that divides the compartment is the opening that can spill it, and one genome cannot be halved into two working copies. The companion line 81 question (what result would demonstrate sustained Darwinian evolution) is the ground that this residual must satisfy first.

## 2. The shape

1. **The handoff carries a seed, not a miniature.** Fig 4 :34 *"A seed carries no small tree: it carries what will grow one, and the conditions it lands in decide what it becomes."* The parent transmits a recorded arrangement and enough material to rebuild the whole, with the habitat supplying the rest. Fig 4 :22 *"Only methods cross up. Only head starts come down."*
2. **The inherited part must survive its own use.** Fig 12 :119 *"catalyst. A part that survives its own use."* A genome that is consumed when copied fails; a genome that templates its own copying, or a replicase inside the compartment, succeeds. Fig 6 :21 *"MINT composes inside the closure and can never add an atom."* Division cannot create a copying capacity the parent did not hold.
3. **It is a cycle, not a chain.** Fig 12 :121 *"cycle. The output re-enters as input. The sign of the loop decides whether it closes, and a chain cannot close by construction."* Daughters become the operands of the next division (Fig 12 :104 *"A settled molecule becomes an atom for whatever composes over it"*).
4. **The composition crosses kinds.** Fig 13 :74 *"Everything else is a composition across the two kinds, never within one."* Parent region, boundary and record, daughter region. A membrane-only story and a genome-only story each fail the schema.
5. **Loss is mandatory; total loss is the failure.** Fig 4 :27 *"The transform between scales loses information, and that is the point."* Fig 13 :28 *"They alternate, and the alternation is where both the recursion and the randomness come from."* The discarded detail is the variation selection needs (Fig 7 :33 *"variation inside, selection between"*, an analogue with a named strain).
6. **The division modes are isomers.** Fig 12 :117 *"isomer. Same atoms, different bond, different substance. The difference is invisible in the formula and decisive in the behaviour."* Shear fission, budding and extrusion share ingredients and differ decisively in retention.
7. **The carrier is not named.** The canonical README limits the framework to *"a shape, not a name."* Which fatty acid, which polymer, which energy source is a ground question.

## 3. What it explicitly does not say

- Not a chemical mechanism. No claim that RNA was the first genome or that fatty acid vesicles were the first compartments.
- Not a claim that any specific laboratory system is the historical one. Those systems are ground-visible imports, provenanced in `10_source_ledger.csv`.
- Not that loss should be zero. A perfectly lossless division would supply no variation and no evolution.
- Not that fidelity is irrelevant. Zero information transfer across the handoff makes question 60 impossible; the analogue claim is the weakest of the findings and is marked as such.
- Not `grounded`. No measurement in this derivation settles the early-Earth case.
- Not a theory of everything. The union compound at the centre keeps its own loop and its own ground.

## 4. What would settle it (the ground)

A source-discriminating instrument and an evolution assay, not a further argument. Concretely:

- **Seed test:** a division where each daughter regenerates full organisation with no inherited record and no external seed. Breaks finding 1 and withdraws the shape.
- **Catalyst test:** sustained information continuity where the inherited molecule is consumed on use and no replicase is inside the compartment. Breaks finding 2.
- **Loss test:** a perfectly lossless division that still yields heritable variation, or a zero-stochasticity division that supports Darwinian evolution. Breaks finding 5.
- **Bond test:** a self-reproducing compartment organised entirely within one kind. Breaks finding 4.
- **Fidelity test:** sustained improvement with measured per-division information fidelity of zero. Breaks the analogue finding 5's Fig 7 reading only; findings 1 and 2 stand.
- **The section's own ground:** an experimental result demonstrating sustained Darwinian evolution in a protocell lineage (line 81). Positive results corroborate the shape; a negative result where fidelity is demonstrably nonzero would pressure it.

For the laboratory side, the relevant instrument already exists: Zhu and Szostak 2009 measured leakage after shear division versus extrusion. The open ground is the historical one, not the bench one.

## 5. The honest stop

**Unreached, not unreachable.** The shape is reachable from the figures; the historical carrier is not. The canonical README says the framework stops at the limit of what the available instruments can distinguish and records the lineage so the stop is honest. *"Indiscernibility is not identity."* Here that means: several division modes are indiscernible at the level of the shape, and none is thereby identified as the one that occurred.

## 6. One-paragraph version for outreach

> The Tether does not name the first protocell. It says what any successful division must look like. The parent cannot hand a daughter a miniature of itself; it hands her a seed, the recorded arrangement that can rebuild the whole, plus enough material and the right conditions, and the crossing is lossy by design. The inherited part must survive its own use, so it is copied rather than consumed, and because division cannot create a copying capacity the parent did not hold, that capacity must already be inside the compartment. The event is production closed into a cycle, not a one-off split: daughters become the parents of the next round. Shear fission, budding and extrusion are isomers over the same ingredients, and they differ decisively in whether they keep the contents. What the framework does not return is the name of the chemistry that did this on early Earth. What it offers is the shape, four refuters written in advance, and the instruments that would close it. The historical carrier stays open until the ground settles it.
