## What is being decided, in effect terms

A boundary grows and must split. The two options are: (A) treat the split as a container problem, where what matters is keeping the enclosure intact, or (B) treat the split as a union compound of boundary dynamics, content retention, and information replication, where what matters is that each daughter receives a seed able to regenerate the whole. Option A predicts a membrane that never opens and therefore never divides. Option B predicts a lossy transform that preserves a head start with nonzero fidelity and lets variation plus selection act between daughters. The answer binds what an answer must look like, not which chemistry did it.
