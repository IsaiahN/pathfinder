## 0. The residual, stated in effect terms

A compartment is a boundary that encloses a share of the world. It grows by taking in material, and it must split into daughters. The residual is the tension between two requirements that pull against each other:

1. **Retention.** The same event that opens the boundary to split it is the event that can spill the contents. A container that never opens never divides; a container that opens too far loses everything.
2. **Continuity of information.** A single genetic or replicating object cannot be literally halved into two functional copies. Each daughter needs at least one copy, or a head start from which a copy can be rebuilt, plus enough non-genetic machinery to use it.

Option A (a pure container story) leads to a membrane that cannot divide. Option B (a coupled story) leads to a lossy transform in which the parent hands each daughter a seed: a recorded arrangement and enough material to regenerate the whole, with nonzero fidelity, after which variation and selection between daughters do the rest.

The companion question in the same section, "What experimental result would demonstrate that a protocell can undergo sustained Darwinian evolution?" (line 81), is the ground for this residual. A lineage that divides without retaining information cannot evolve, so retention is a precondition for the ground to fire at all.

## 3. Findings, each with verbatim figure lines, mode, and refuter

### F1: A daughter receives a seed, not a miniature of the parent (decisive, triangulated)

Fig 4 :34–35, quoted before any conclusion:

> **"A seed carries no small tree: it carries what will grow one, and the conditions it lands in decide what it becomes."**

Fig 4 :22:

> **"Only methods cross up. Only head starts come down."**

The shape of a division that loses nothing essential is therefore not "the parent is preserved intact in two copies." It is: the parent transmits the capacity to regenerate the whole, plus conditions. The daughter need not contain the parent's full membrane or full complement of molecules at the instant of split. It must contain a head start that can rebuild them given the habitat.

Triangulation: the seed rule (Fig 4, a scale-transform figure) is corroborated by a different figure about composition cost. Fig 12 :107–109:

> **"where the arrangement is recorded, re-deriving it is a lookup and the actions were spent once."**

The recorded arrangement is the genome. A daughter that inherits the record does not re-evolve the arrangement; she looks it up. Low overlap between the scale figure and the composition-cost figure, so this is a genuine triangulation, not one pool speaking twice.

Mode: `general` on the schema, `specified` on the claim that the genome is the recorded head start. **Refuter:** show a dividing system in which each daughter regenerates the parent's organisation with no inherited record and no external seed, so the crossing carries no head start. That would break both Fig 4 :34–35 and Fig 12 :107–109.

### F2: The genetic part must survive its own use (triangulated)

Fig 12 :119:

> **"catalyst. A part that survives its own use. Spent and regenerating parts behave differently across uses, and nothing records which a term is."**

A genome that is consumed when copied is a spent part; a genome that templates its own copying is a regenerating part. Division without information loss requires the latter, or an external supply of copies. This is the figure-level reason a self-replicating or replicase-assisted polymer, not an inert information store, is the required shape.

Fig 6 :21:

> **"MINT composes inside the closure and can never add an atom."**

Fig 6 :23:

> **"No frame escapes its own closure. The bound moves only on contact with a frame whose closure differs."**

A daughter cannot be handed an atom the parent's closure does not already contain. If the parent lacks a copying mechanism inside its boundary, division cannot manufacture one; it can only lose information. So the replication capacity must be internal to the compartment before the split.

Mode: `specified`. **Refuter:** demonstrate sustained information continuity across divisions where the inherited molecule is consumed on first use and no replicase is present inside the compartment. That breaks Fig 12 :119 and Fig 6 :21.

### F3: The loop must close; a one-way chain cannot (single-source for the figure, world-corroborated)

Fig 12 :121:

> **"cycle. The output re-enters as input. The sign of the loop decides whether it closes, and a chain cannot close by construction."**

The protocell cycle is genome replication, membrane growth, division, genome partition, then replication again. A linear account that ends at division is a chain, and the figure says a chain cannot close by construction. The residual is therefore a cycle, which is why the coupling of growth and division is not optional.

Fig 12 :104:

> **"A settled molecule becomes an atom for whatever composes over it. So irreducible is a fact about a level and never about the world."**

Each generation's settled daughters become the atoms of the next generation. Division is recursion, not a one-off event.

Mode: `specified`. **Refuter:** exhibit a compartment system with repeated division in which daughters never become operands for a further division (the loop fails at the second generation). That breaks Fig 12 :121 and :104. Triangulated with world source W2 and W3 below (they measured the coupling of growth to division), but the figure claim itself is single-source.

### F4: Cross-kind composition, never within one kind (structural, specified)

Fig 13 :74:

> **"Everything else is a composition across the two kinds, never within one."**

Fig 13 :75:

> **"Two states never bond directly. It is not space-time but space, a structure, time; and two structures join only through a state."**

The compartment is a state (the enclosed region, `space`), its boundary is a structure (`network`, entities related and influence travelling), and the genetic record is an ordering (`spectrum`, so more and less can be said about a sequence). The composition runs state, structure, state across a generation: parent compartment, boundary and record, daughter compartment. No two states bond directly and no two structures bond directly, so a membrane-only story and a genome-only story each fail the schema; the compound is required.

Strain test: applying :75 to "space-time" is the exemplar's physics use. Here the two states are parent region and daughter region, with structures in between, so the rule is used on the same quantity it was written for (composition across kinds). No strain. The binding of membrane to `network` and genome to `spectrum` is a mapping from the world frame, marked `specified`, not a derivation of the six-term set.

Mode: `general` on the cross-kind rule, `specified` on the specific bindings. **Refuter:** show a self-reproducing compartment whose organisation is composed entirely within one kind (only states bonding to states, or only structures to structures) with no intervening opposite-kind term. That breaks Fig 13 :74–75.

### F5: Randomness and loss are the point, not a defect (triangulated)

Fig 13 :28:

> **"They alternate, and the alternation is where both the recursion and the randomness come from."**

Fig 13 :31–32, on the remainder re-entering the next turn:

> **"a remainder compounding through a chain that amplifies is chaos, and the detail a crossing discards reappears above it as noise."**

Fig 4 :27:

> **"The transform between scales loses information, and that is the point."**

The question asks how to divide "without losing all." The figures answer: loss is mandatory, total loss is the failure. The detail discarded at the crossing reappears as variation, which is what selection needs. A division that retained everything perfectly would supply no variation and no evolution.

Triangulation: Fig 13 (substrate, randomness from the crossing) and Fig 4 (scale transform, lossy by design) are independent figures with different subjects. Low overlap.

Mode: `general`. **Refuter:** a division event that is both perfectly lossless and still yields heritable variation, or a proven division mechanism with zero stochastic component that nonetheless supports Darwinian evolution. That breaks Fig 13 :28, :31–32 and Fig 4 :27.

### F6: Fidelity times selection sets progress per handoff (analogue, strain named)

Fig 7 :33:

> **"variation inside, selection between"**

Fig 7 :26:

> **"progress per handoff: R = h² × S"**

In Fig 7 the bracket's fidelity across a handoff (`h²`) and the ground's pressure (`S`) multiply; zero either and the chain runs in place. Read at the biological stratum, a daughter lineage with zero information-transfer fidelity cannot improve however strong selection is. This is why "not losing all genetic information" is a precondition for the ground (question 60) to register at all.

**Strain, named and not hidden.** Fig 7 is calibrated to rooms and frames handing a question along; its use for molecular inheritance is a cross-stratum application that the framework's domain-agnostic recursion permits but no experiment here measures. I record it as an analogue with a strain, not as a direct law. The unstrained version of the same point is F1 and F2 (the head start must arrive; the record must be copied).

Mode: `specified`, analogue. **Refuter:** sustained improvement in a lineage with measured per-division information fidelity of zero. That would break the product form or show the figure governs only frame handoffs, in which case I withdraw this finding and keep F1 and F2.

### F7: Division is habitat substitution, with two silent failure modes (single-source)

Fig 11 :66:

> **"Isolation is not removal of the habitat. It is substitution of one habitat for another."**

Fig 11 :68–69:

> **"what you failed to reproduce, and what you brought with you"**

Fig 11 :32:

> **"→ introduce, never subtract"**

A daughter is a substituted habitat, not a smaller copy of the parent's habitat. Two failures are invisible at the split and only show when the goal fails: the daughter failed to reproduce something essential (no genome, no catalyst), or the daughter unintentionally brought something (a parasite, a defective template). The figure also says you can only introduce actors; you cannot subtract the habitat. A division story that speaks of "removing waste" as if the compartment could subtract its environment is misstated.

Mode: `general`. **Refuter:** a division in which the daughter's habitat is a strict subtraction of the parent's rather than a substitution, with no new conditions and no inherited actors. That breaks Fig 11 :66 and :68–69.

## 4. Pre-registered refuter (written before the conclusion)

Per the discipline that a falsifier written before the run is a design instrument, I commit to withdrawing the shape claim if any of the following holds:

1. A dividing system transmits no head start and no record, yet each daughter regenerates the parent's full organisation (breaks Fig 4 :34–35 and Fig 12 :107–109).
2. Sustained information continuity is demonstrated where the inherited molecule is consumed on use and no replicase is inside the compartment (breaks Fig 12 :119 and Fig 6 :21).
3. A division event is perfectly lossless and still yields heritable variation, or a zero-stochasticity division supports Darwinian evolution (breaks Fig 4 :27 and Fig 13 :28).
4. A self-reproducing compartment is composed entirely within one kind (breaks Fig 13 :74–75).
5. Sustained improvement is measured with per-division information fidelity of zero (breaks the analogue F6; F6 alone is withdrawn, F1 and F2 stand).

None of these was run in this simulated derivation. Until one is run, no part of the shape is `grounded`.

### Round trip note

The condensation used in outreach is: division is a lossy cross-scale handoff that must carry a seed with nonzero fidelity, then variation and selection act between daughters. Sent up (the figure set) includes catalyst, cycle, cross-kind composition, seed, and recorded arrangement. Brought back down (a concrete lab or early-Earth claim) drops at least: which molecule is the catalyst, whether the partition is symmetric, and what the habitat supplies. That gap is nonzero and must not be treated as zero (Fig 4 :30 *"So take the round trip and measure the gap. Without it you are navigating by dead reckoning: confident about a position nobody checked."*). `11_audit.md` enumerates the dropped constraints.

### Final verdict of this audit

The shape survives; the chemistry is not derived. A compartment can divide without losing everything essential only if the crossing carries a head start (the recorded arrangement plus enough material) with nonzero fidelity, the inherited part survives its own use, the process closes as a cycle whose daughters become operands for the next division, and loss at the crossing supplies the variation selection needs. The specific prebiotic instantiation is a `gap` after a genuine census, which is a verdict, not a silence. World sources in `09` corroborate the laboratory shape but cannot ground the early-Earth claim. Strains after re-census: one (F6, analogue), named, none hidden.
