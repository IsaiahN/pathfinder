# Expert verification packet: the Tether on protocell division

**What this is:** a claim in a form a protocell researcher can check. Mode, derivation and refuters are stated before the conclusion, so a reader knows what to look for and what would settle it.

**Mode: `specified` on the shape, `general` on the cross-scale schema. Not `grounded`.** No measurement at the origin of life is claimed. This is a shape and a route, not a result. It is a simulated derivation checked for internal consistency against the figures, not a ground reading.

## 1. The claim, in one paragraph

A compartment can divide without losing everything essential only if the crossing carries a seed rather than a miniature: the recorded arrangement that can regenerate the whole, plus enough material and the right conditions. The inherited part must survive its own use, so it is copied rather than consumed, and because division cannot create a copying capacity the parent did not hold, that capacity must already be inside the compartment. The event is production closed into a cycle, not a one-off split, so daughters become the parents of the next round. Loss at the crossing is mandatory and supplies the variation selection needs. Shear fission, budding and extrusion are isomers over a shared atom set and differ decisively in retention. The framework does not name the chemistry that did this on early Earth.

## 2. Three checkable sub-claims

### C1: framework-internal, checkable by reading the figures

- **Statement:** the handoff carries a head start, and the inherited part must survive its own use; division cannot create a copying capacity absent from the parent.
- **Source, verbatim:** Fig 4 :34 *"A seed carries no small tree: it carries what will grow one, and the conditions it lands in decide what it becomes."* Fig 12 :119 *"catalyst. A part that survives its own use. Spent and regenerating parts behave differently across uses, and nothing records which a term is."* Fig 6 :21 *"MINT composes inside the closure and can never add an atom."*
- **Refuter:** a dividing system where each daughter regenerates the parent's full organisation with no inherited record and no external seed, or sustained information continuity where the inherited molecule is consumed on use and no replicase is inside the compartment.

### C2: biology-facing, checkable against vesicle and coacervate data

- **Statement:** the division event is a cycle whose daughters become operands for the next division, and candidate physical modes are isomers over a shared atom set with retention as the decisive difference.
- **Source, verbatim:** Fig 12 :121 *"cycle. The output re-enters as input. The sign of the loop decides whether it closes, and a chain cannot close by construction."* Fig 12 :104 *"A settled molecule becomes an atom for whatever composes over it. So irreducible is a fact about a level and never about the world."* Fig 12 :117 *"isomer. Same atoms, different bond, different substance. The difference is invisible in the formula and decisive in the behaviour."*
- **What to check:** do the daughters in the cited experiments actually re-enter as parents (multiple cycles were measured by Zhu and Szostak 2009), and do shear fission, budding and extrusion differ in retention as claimed? The opened sources report trace RNA leakage for shear division versus about 40 percent for extrusion, and content-retaining but externally triggered and heterogeneous budding.
- **Refuter:** repeated division in which daughters never serve as parents for a further division, or two modes over the same atom set with indistinguishable retention and heredity distributions.

### C3: structural, checkable by reading the figures

- **Statement:** the compartment composes across kinds (parent region, boundary and record, daughter region), and loss at the crossing is mandatory; the discarded detail is the variation selection needs.
- **Source, verbatim:** Fig 13 :74 *"Everything else is a composition across the two kinds, never within one."* Fig 4 :27 *"The transform between scales loses information, and that is the point."* Fig 13 :28 *"They alternate, and the alternation is where both the recursion and the randomness come from."*
- **Refuter:** a self-reproducing compartment organised entirely within one kind, or a perfectly lossless division that still yields heritable variation.

## 3. What is explicitly not claimed

- Not a chemical mechanism. No claim about which polymer, lipid or energy source operated on early Earth; the canonical figures README limits the framework to *"a shape, not a name."*

- Not that retention should be perfect. A lossless division would supply no variation and no evolution.
