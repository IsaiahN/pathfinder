## 1. A shape is a constraint, not a filler

The canonical figures README states the limit: the framework returns *"what a missing thing would have to look like (a shape, not a name)"*, then *"stops at the limit of what the available instruments can distinguish, and records the lineage so the stop is honest."* Fig 9 :32 *"you get a kind of thing, never the answer itself."*

From *The Library of Babel* (opened this session): *"You can cut your search short if the set of books returned is small and descriptive enough that it provides a general shape of the answer that satisfies your question."*

So a **shape** is the narrowed form of an answer you can state before you can name it. It constrains; it does not fill. For this question:

- Shape: a lossy cross-scale handoff that carries a seed with nonzero fidelity, closes as a cycle, and discards enough detail to supply variation.
- Name: oleate vesicles budding under EDC fuel, or an RNA replicase inside a fatty acid vesicle. The framework does not return either name.

## 2. How the shape was obtained

From *The Library of Babel*: *"With each new term gained, you narrow the space and deepen your vocabulary."* And the steering rule: the returned books must be *"tested or ranged against the problem you want answered in order to steer the direction of the next search."*

The same essay names the move that governs the union census in `01`: *"Perhaps your problem was a bundle or conflation of several problems masquerading as one? You will need to search all of those problems individually to find their answers and then combine them in a satisfactory way."* That is why the audit refuses to answer "how do compartments divide" as a single mechanism question.

Fig 9 :30–31: *"your words are borrowed too, so describe the effect rather than the cause."* The residual was therefore described in effect terms (opening spills, a single genome cannot be halved) before any frame was chosen.

## 3. What licenses a name

Only two things, both outside the shape itself:

1. **The ground settles it.** Formula step 5: *"the ground settles it. Verified only by what pays. A gate is not the ground."* For this question the ground is a measurement or an evolution assay (question 60), not an argument.
2. **A provenanced import from a frame whose closure differs**, with a source gate and a shadow test (Fig 6 :23, Fig 8 provenance language). Laboratory results are such imports: they are recorded with author, venue, year and quote in `10_source_ledger.csv`.

The six-term set is a visible guess, not nature's own list. Naming RNA as *the* genome or a fatty acid vesicle as *the* compartment turns a visible choice into an invisible one. Section 6 of `01` records the narrowing.

## 4. Why "shape, not a name" matters specifically here

The protocell field already has candidate names: fatty acid vesicles, coacervates, emulsion droplets, mineral pores. Choosing among them by how well each fits a verbally stated desire is the inversion Fig 9 :27–28 forbids: *"describe it before you go looking, or any frame you pick will seem to fit."*

The shape survives whichever carrier is chosen. A coacervate that divides and loses its replicator fails the same way a vesicle that leaks its RNA fails. That is the value of stopping at the shape: it is checkable across carriers.

## 5. Prestige is not action

From *The Recursive Self-Improvement Trap* (opened this session): *"Prestige must never be allowed to validate an action directly."* And: *"Prestige can fund an action. It can give an action a budget to be tried. But it cannot settle an action. Only the ground can settle it. The Ground Maintainer must be blind to prestige."*

For outreach this means the venues in `03_public_contact_points.csv` are used to give the claim a budget to be tried by people who can refute it, not to collect famous names that endorse it. The essay also says *"You may all agree and be wrong"*, which is the Fig 2 warning in authorial form.

## 6. This audit is a simulation, not a ground reading

Same essay: *"LLMs can quickly simulate an approximate answer/output with the formula found in the architecture post... However, the truly testable output would need to be derived from the real architecture or working the formula yourself."* And: *"the output is only as good as the validator. The agent, or the tool, or the frame, or the theory, cannot validate itself. An external validator, or ground, is always needed."*

So `01`, `06`, `07` and `09` are simulated derivations checked for internal consistency against the figures. They are **not** tests of the claim and must be labelled as such. The world sources corroborate the laboratory shape; they do not ground the early-Earth claim.
