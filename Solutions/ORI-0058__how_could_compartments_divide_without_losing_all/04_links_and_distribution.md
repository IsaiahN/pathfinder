## Wider literature that bears on the residual

From the five opened papers, the points a reader could check against the shape claim:

1. **The coupling is real and physical.** Szostak 2011 reports that division is coupled to growth because filaments divide and spheres do not, which addresses the coordination problem in compartments lacking internal machinery. This is a ground-level instance of the cycle claim (Fig 12 :121).
2. **Retention is a measured quantity, not an assumption.** Zhu and Szostak 2009 measured RNA leakage after shear division against extrusion division. The frame-level claim that the crossing loses detail (Fig 4 :27) has a numerical handle here.
3. **Information partition is stochastic.** Chen and Walde 2010 describe the stochastic corrector: compartments separate genomes, so a parasite in one daughter cannot poison the population. This is the world-side counterpart of Fig 7 :33 *"variation inside, selection between"*, though the figure's own use remains an analogue.
4. **Chemical energy can drive budding, with limits.** Zambrano et al. 2024 retained contents during fuel-driven budding but state that division is externally triggered, heterogeneous, and that vesicles shrink each cycle. Do not present this as a solved self-division cycle.
