## What in the wider literature bears directly on this question

1. **The strongest corroborator of the "miss" reading is the field's own self-description.** Mearman, Berger and Guizzo 2023 report a "methodological blindness": mainstream economics "does not study things that are very relevant in economic reality because it does not have the method to do it." That is the figure claim that composition inside a closure cannot add an atom (Fig 8 :32, "no search, however long, adds an atom"), stated in economics vocabulary. It is corroboration only; the figures decide.

2. **The overlap is real, which blocks the easy "six blocs" reading.** Lee 2008 says the multiple heterodox critiques of one core proposition overlap and that school boundaries are sometimes "not there at all." ICAPE lists neoclassical economics as one tradition among many and insists pluralism is not "anything goes." Together these support the union-of-overlapping-conditions verdict (F1) and the warning that the schools are not three independent witnesses merely because there are six of them (F5).

3. **The level distinction is already made in the field.** Dow 2008 separates plurality of theory and evidence, of method, and of methodologies. Gräbner and Strunk add a seven-dimension table. This matches the figure distinction between composition inside a closure (Fig 6 :21), import between closures (Fig 6 :23), and the habitat around a residual (Fig 11). A claim about methodological plurality does not automatically become a claim about explanatory surplus, which is why F2 refuses to average them.

4. **The anchor problem is documented, not merely asserted.** Gräbner and Strunk show that the "market test of ideas" presupposes a level playing field that does not exist, citing path dependence, asymmetric citations (under 3 percent) and monistic curricula. Under Fig 2 :29 and :33-34, an anchor that updates on the frames' own influence is not an anchor. This is the one place where a world source materially sharpened a figure finding: it turned "consensus is not an anchor" into "the specific proposed anchor is a mirror."

5. **A genuine contradiction was returned to the census, not used to flip the verdict.** The Economics Network handbook reports Tetlock 2005, where pluralistic thinking styles predicted better forecasting, while Gräbner and Strunk's "agonist pluralists" note the communication and quality-control costs of too much plurality. Read as a single "pluralism is good/bad" dispute, these conflict. Re-census dissolves it: Fig 8 :47 says "Independence is one quantity read twice," so the same parameter that creates the surplus also raises the cost of agreement. The trade-off is one quantity, not a refutation of either result.

## Distribution: offer, do not push

Figure-grounded reasons:

- **Fig 10 :52, "Bring a characterised residual, never the source."** Sending the full framework before anyone has described a residual sends a recording, not a generator. Lead with the four-part separator and its refuters; offer links after.
- **Fig 10 :42, "A verifier that can reconstruct the claim cannot verify it."** A recipient who has absorbed the framework before being asked for a verdict has joined the frame being checked.
- **Fig 9 :27-28, "describe it before you go looking, or any frame you pick will seem to fit."** Ask what they would expect to see if the surplus claim were true before giving them the source list.
- **Fig 2 :40, "disqualifying for a verdict: agreement about the world is worthless when the agreeing frames share an evidence pool."** A send selected for people already sympathetic to pluralism produces a convention, not corroboration.

**Recommended order in a reply:** (1) the separator and its four refuters; (2) ask what they would count as a failure for a specific slot, for example "gendered care work" or "persistent unemployment"; (3) only then the links, with the note that the figures, not the summary, are the authority; (4) record the stage-1 verdict before showing any theory.

## The two-stage offer

1. **Stage 1: validate the output.** Send the residual, the four-part separator and the pre-registered refuter. Ask what the recipient would count as a failure. Record the verdict before any theory is shown.
2. **Stage 2, opt-in only:** if they are curious, offer the local figure text and the four open sources above. Their later agreement is never pooled as corroboration, because once they hold the framework they are no longer outside it (Fig 2 :40).
3. **Stage 3: offer the formula as a method.** Fig 4 :22: "Only methods cross up. Only head starts come down." Each user must write their own refuter, state their own ground, and label their mode. `THE_FORMULA.md` "Direction" says a frame cannot check the direction of its own question.

**Hard precondition.** Stages 1 to 3 work only if stage 1 survives. F1 to F4 in `01_figure_grounded_audit.md` must be repaired in the sending copy, and the folder must be presented as a simulated derivation, not a ground reading.

## Submission constraint specific to this field

The Erasmus Journal for Philosophy and Economics states: "The EJPE will not consider submissions whose text has been substantially written by AI. The arguments and prose must be substantially the author's own." This folder is an AI-assisted simulated derivation. It must not be submitted as-is to that venue or to any journal with a comparable policy. The legitimate use is as a private check list: a human author writes their own analysis, states their own mode and their own refuter, and uses this folder only to locate the failure conditions. The same caution is recorded in `08_expert_verification_packet.md`.
