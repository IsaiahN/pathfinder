## 0. The claim under audit

> Question: Do Austrian, Post-Keynesian, Marxian, institutional, feminist, and complexity approaches offer distinct explanations that mainstream models miss?
>
> Plain reading: a list of six approaches is compared with a single mainstream, and the question asks for a yes or a no on "distinct explanations" and on "miss."
>
> Effect reading used here: R is the set of phenomenon slots s for which the mainstream frame returns no term while a heterodox frame returns a prediction. The question then has three parts that must not be averaged: (a) does a closure difference exist at the evidence level, (b) is a specific residual described so the union is aimed rather than mute, and (c) is there a non-updating ground that could settle which frame reaches the slot.

Two ordinary terms are refused before the derivation, following Formula step "Direction," which says to make the sought-for shape an ordinary term so it accrues a residual:

- **"distinct"** is a symmetric comparison. Under the Operators table, `+` conjunction is symmetric: "swap the operands. Does the meaning change? No." Distinctness carries no direction. "miss" is ordered and threshold-like, closer to `⋛` comparison ("a threshold relation between two quantities") or `⇒` production ("A produces B; B did not exist before"). A single verdict cannot answer a symmetric question and an ordered question at once. They are different bonds and must be separated, not averaged.
- **"mainstream models"** is treated as one frame for the census because the question treats it as one, but the figures license a frame as "any bounded system with a library: an agent, a person, a discipline, a model" (Symbols table :111). A research programme is a frame. Calling six schools "heterodox" is a name for a set of frames, not a single frame with a single closure. Treating them as one speaker is the aggregation error Fig 1 :22 names.

## 2. Findings

### F1 The question is a union of conditions, not a partition (decisive for the framing)

Fig 9 :4-5: "Disagreements that do not shrink with effort, where each rule keeps working well on a different subset, are not one hard question but several well-formed ones. Split it rather than search."

Fig 9 :57: "a case in two conditions is not a harder case of either. It stops being a mixture and becomes a specific compound."

Fig 9 :54: "When the split lands on a union rather than a partition, unbundle before you search again."

The six approaches do not answer the same question with six different answers. They hold different primitives and compose them differently. Lee 2008 (New Palgrave entry, reproduced by the Association for Heterodox Economics) records what each contributes: Marxists bring theories of class and the economic surplus, feminists a holistic account of gender, class and ethnicity, post-Keynesians effective demand, uncertainty and a monetary theory of production in real time, institutionalists a pragmatic approach to change, social economists ethics and justice. Those are different atoms, not different labels on one atom. The same source says the heterodox critiques of any single mainstream core proposition "overlap in argumentation" and that by the 1990s the boundaries of the approaches "do not simply overlap - they are, in some cases, not there at all." ICAPE's statement of purpose likewise says "each tradition of thought (Austrian, feminist, old and new institutionalist, Marxian, neoclassical, Post Keynesian, Sraffian, etc.) adds something unique and valuable to economic scholarship" and adds "This is not to say 'anything goes.'"

So the correct object is a union of overlapping conditions with a per-slot residual. One lever, such as "teach more pluralism" or "add complexity economics," clears the region where only one condition holds and leaves the intersections standing. Verdict: the yes/no framing is refused; the union framing stands. Status `general`, triangulated (Fig 9 union rule plus two independent world descriptions of overlapping school boundaries).

**Refuter.** If a single mechanism is shown to generate all six schools' distinct claims as special cases of one set of atoms under one bond, with no residual left, the union claim falls and a partition becomes searchable.

### F2 "Distinct" is not surplus; an undirected union is mute

Fig 8 :19: "closures differ, so a surplus exists" but :20 "but no residual is described" and :21 "nothing was aimed; the union is mute." Fig 8 :7 names the collapse: "interdisciplinarity, no mint."

Fig 8 :48-49: "Two frames that overlap almost completely are one frame wearing two names."

Fig 13 :36-37: "If two members drew on the same underlying fact, their agreement would carry no information, and one of them would be redundant rather than minimal."

It is not enough that a heterodox school uses different words, a different empirical style, or a different professional association. If it draws on the same evidence and composes the same atoms, it is a second name for one frame. Gräbner and Strunk (Journal of Economic Methodology) make the same point from the philosophy-of-science side: plurality must be stated by dimension (topical, methodological, epistemological, ontological) and by degree, and they note that adding a new topic inside the standard optimization-cum-equilibrium frame yields only complementary perspectives, not a stronger claim on truth. The Economics Network handbook records the same asymmetry: heterodox economists employ a wide range of methods in their official discourse while orthodoxy privileges mathematical formalism, and "Austrian economics, for example, is weak on principle 10" (power). So a blanket "all six distinct in the same way" is false even on the heterodox side.

Verdict: distinctness is a necessary but not sufficient condition for surplus. The surplus exists only where the closures differ at the evidence level and a residual is described. Status `specified`, triangulated (Fig 8 and Fig 13 are different figures with different objects; the world sources are corroboration only).

**Refuter.** Exhibit one heterodox school whose claims are strictly a re-labelling of mainstream atoms with the same evidence source and no live residual slot. That school drops out of the surplus set. The union claim for the others is untouched.

### F3 "Miss" is structural unreachability, not a failure of effort

Fig 6 :21: "MINT composes inside the closure and can never add an atom." Fig 6 :23: "No frame escapes its own closure. The bound moves only on contact with a frame whose closure differs." Fig 8 :32: "no search, however long, adds an atom." Fig 13 :115: "The set is closed. The arrangements are not."

Fig 6 :27: "Which is why some unreachability is depth and some is genuine absence, and from inside the frame the two look the same."

A mainstream model that searches forever inside its closure will not add class, gendered power, fundamental uncertainty, or an institutional habit as a primitive, because composition rearranges atoms and never adds a kind. That is a barrier-theorem result, not a sociological complaint. It is why the word "miss" has a literal reading here: the atom is unreachable from inside that closure, and something would have to be imported.

The strongest world corroborator is Mearman, Berger and Guizzo 2023 (Journal of Economic Issues 57(4)), reporting interviews with leading heterodox economists. They write that "mainstream economics cannot see decisive aspects of the economy because of a methodological blindness" and quote Dequech: "mainstream economics chooses the method at the expense of the subject-matter; it does not study things that are very relevant in economic reality because it does not have the method to do it, or at least to do it in what is considered an adequate way." That is the figure's closure claim stated in the field's own vocabulary. The direct publisher fetch returned 403; the text was read through the public search index and an open White Rose eprint record, and is marked as such in the ledger.

Gräbner and Strunk corroborate from a different direction: dynamical systems theory and agent-based modelling, which are based on disequilibrium and non-optimizing agents, "remain marginalized" in top journals; non-formalized and qualitative approaches "are marginalized even more." The 2017/2020 paper also cites that "less than 3% of total citations refer to heterodox publications" in mainstream outlays. A closure that cannot represent an atom will not cite the frame that holds it.

Verdict: for the specific atoms named above, "miss" is genuine unreachability relative to the mainstream closure, `specified` and triangulated. It is **not** a claim that mainstream economists are unable or unwilling; it is a claim about what the closure can compose. Status `specified`.

**Refuter.** Show a mainstream model that derives one of these atoms from inside its existing closure without import, with the derivation preceding any contact with a heterodox frame. The unreachability claim for that atom is refuted.

### F4 The residual must stay indexed per slot; there is no folder-wide "heterodox surplus"

Fig 1 :19: "The residual is indexed by object slot, not aggregated." Fig 1 :22: "Aggregating across slots is how a live signal disappears into an average." Fig 1 :23: "The ground enters as the only metric."

The slots are phenomena: financial crisis, long-run unemployment, income distribution, gendered care work, inflation as a conflict process, market process under dispersed knowledge, emergence from agent interaction. Each school lights up different slots. Austrian economics reaches the knowledge and process slot (Hayek), Post-Keynesian reaches effective demand and fundamental uncertainty (Colander, Holt and Rosser 2007 explicitly note that mainstream histories of macroeconomics such as Blanchard 2000 and Woodford 1999 fail to mention Post Keynesians on the openness of the economy), Marxian reaches class and accumulation, institutional reaches habit and routine, feminist reaches gendered power and social reproduction (IAFFE's stated purpose: "advancing gender-aware and inclusive economic research and policy analysis"), complexity reaches emergence and non-equilibrium. An average over slots would tell you "heterodox explains about 40 percent more," which is exactly the number no figure returns and which destroys the signal about where to look.

Verdict: verdicts are kept per school and per slot, never averaged into a single yes. This is the structural reason the question cannot be answered as written. Status `general` on the indexing rule, `specified` per slot.

**Refuter.** Provide an instrument that reads a single global surplus scalar across all phenomenon slots without losing which slot is live. Fig 1 :19-22 says such a scalar is not a residual at all.

### F5 Independence, not agreement, is what makes the comparison mean anything

Fig 2 :31: "Independence, between two frames, is how little they draw on the same evidence." Fig 2 :42: "Buy independence in proportion to the cost of being wrong." Fig 8 :47: "Independence is one quantity read twice." Fig 8 :50: "two frames can hold the same atom by convergence, reaching it separately, which is corroboration at maximum independence."

So the value of the six schools is not that they disagree with the mainstream, nor that they agree with each other. It is that some of them range against a different ground with a different closure, so a convergent result would be corroboration at high independence, while a shared-pool agreement is one witness wearing many names. The philosophy-of-science literature says the same: the "independence assumption" is what makes multi-method triangulation probative, and it is exactly the assumption that is hard to secure ("scientists are almost never in a position to know that the pieces of evidence available to them are independent"). Gräbner and Strunk say directly that "the triangulation of distinct approaches becomes mandatory to avoid intellectual lock-ins."

A caution recorded here rather than smoothed: the six schools are not automatically independent of one another. Lee 2008 records extensive cross-citation and integration among heterodox journals and associations, so they share an evidence pool among themselves. That is why their mutual agreement is weak evidence, and why the useful comparison is each school against a ground, not heterodoxy against mainstream as two blocs.

Verdict: the minimal legitimate test is per-school independence from the mainstream pool plus a non-updating anchor, not a head count of schools. Status `general` with a `specified` per-school check.

**Refuter.** Show that two schools' apparently different predictions are derived from the same dataset, the same assumptions, and the same yardstick, so their independence is zero. The corroboration value drops to zero, and this finding's per-school verdict follows.

### F6 The proposed anchor ("the market test of ideas") updates on what the frames think

Fig 2 :29: "An anchor is whatever settles the question and does not change its answer because of what any frame thinks." Fig 2 :33: "The anchor must not update." Fig 2 :34: "If it does, panel 3 turns into panel 1: the anchor becomes another frame, everything drifts together, and the drift is coherent, which is why nobody notices."

A common defence of the mainstream position is that if heterodox ideas were useful, they would pass the market test of ideas and be adopted. Gräbner and Strunk examine this argument and find the level playing field absent: research programmes accumulate power through "a path dependent and self-reinforcing process," editorial boards and promotion criteria favour the dominant paradigm, and citation flows are asymmetric. That makes the "market test" a mirror of the profession's own weighting, not an anchor. Under Fig 2, it is panel 1 (mutual update, coherent drift), not panel 3 (range against a pinned anchor).

Fig 2 :35: "Alignment is triangulation, not negotiation. A mirror cannot correct you." A consensus formed by citation counts cannot settle whether a school reaches the ground; it can only settle what the profession currently rewards. Fig 2 :41 allows that such agreement is "efficient for a convention," so it is not worthless, but it is worthless as a verdict about the world.

Verdict: the anchor for "does this school reach this slot" must be an empirical discriminator (a dataset, an experiment, a prediction that can fail in historical time), not a vote, a citation count, or a curriculum share. Status `specified`, triangulated (Fig 2 and the methodology literature on path dependence).

**Refuter.** Exhibit a case where the scientific community's adoption decision was demonstrably independent of its own prior influence structure, so that the market test did not update on what the frames thought. The anchor objection weakens for that case.

### F7 "Distinct from mainstream" is frame-relative; the ground question survives the renaming

Fig 2 :45: "Frames range against the ground. They do not negotiate with it." Fig 2 :46: "Each frame is corrected by it separately, and what makes the correction sound is that neither frame can move it."

The question defines the schools by their relation to one frame ("non-mainstream," "alternative," "heterodox"). That is a frame-relative definition. Lee 2008 notes that the term arose as a "big-tent feel" label and that its content is a family of theories, not a single alternativeto-one-theory. The Economics Network handbook says heterodox theories are "considerably more than reactions to orthodox theories" and root them in Marx, Keynes, Veblen, Hayek and Schumpeter. ICAPE lists neoclassical economics as one tradition among many, not as the reference against which all others are defined. So the figure-grounded re-grounding is: the schools are frames with closures, and the question is which frame's closure reaches a given ground at a given slot. "Different from mainstream" is a useful social fact about journals and curricula (domain D3), but it is not an evidence verdict.

Verdict: the question's own vocabulary is retained as provenance and refused as a verdict. Status `general`, single-source for the figures' anchor rule but corroborated by three independent descriptions of the labels' provenance.

**Refuter.** Show a decision procedure that ranks explanatory quality purely by distance from a designated mainstream frame, with a non-updating ground irrelevant to the ranking. The re-grounding is then unnecessary.

### F8 No figure line returns a folder-wide yes or a school-level winner

Fig 9 :32: "you get a kind of thing, never the answer itself." Fig 9 :42: "narrowing gets you close, the ground decides." Fig 13 :134: "Unreachable is a verdict here, not a silence." Fig 8 :39: "So a search that finds nothing is never a proof of absence."

The framework returns the shape of the check, not the answer to the question. It cannot say "Austrian economics wins" or "all six have surplus" or "none do." It can say that a genuine surplus would require differing closures, a described residual, an unmoved anchor, and independence; and that absent those, the honest report is `unreached`, not a silence and not an invented number. This is the declared `gap`: the literal yes/no answer. It is a shape-not-a-name gap, not an unreachable-at-this-budget gap, because the obstacle is not search effort but the absence of a single ground that settles seven frames at once.

Verdict: literal question `gap`; structural separator `specified`. Status `general` on the gap.

**Refuter.** Produce a figure line that governs the ranking of intellectual schools by explanatory surplus. None was found after a genuine census of all 13 figures and both tables; if one is produced, this gap closes.

### F9 Imports are the only wall-moving operator, and a wrong atom costs more than primitives

Fig 6 :14: "the only operator that moves the wall." Fig 6 :24: "Import from a live peer is debited against that frame's independence. A wrong atom costs more than starting from primitives." Fig 8 :42-43: "Importing against a budget limit adds an atom that was not needed, and Figure 6 prices a wrong atom above starting from primitives."

If a mainstream model imports "power" or "fundamental uncertainty" as an atom, its closure genuinely expands (Fig 6 :20 "closure expands; φ* now reachable"). But that import needs a source gate and a shadow test (Fig 8 :12-13) and must be recorded, because "Convergent derivation and adopted import look identical in the contents" and "Only the record of where each came from separates them" (Fig 8 :50-52). Casual integration of heterodox terms into mainstream models, with no provenance and no test of whether the term explains a described gap, buys a false sense of reach. Gräbner and Strunk's "sins of omission" point (Akerlof 2020) is a world-side warning in the same direction: what is excluded by the method is not the same as what has been refuted.

Verdict: import is available, cheap only with provenance, and always debited against independence. Status `specified`, triangulated.

**Refuter.** Show a model that absorbs a heterodox atom, shows no record of where it came from, and still demonstrates a surplus against a non-updating ground. The provenance requirement is then decorative and should be dropped.

### F10 Strain: the six-term physical substrate is not a taxonomy of schools (resolved by re-census)

A first pass tried to map the six approaches onto Fig 13's six terms (space, time, energy; spectrum, gradient, network). That reading strains: Fig 13 :74 says "Everything else is a composition across the two kinds, never within one," which governs physical composition inside a frame, not the classification of intellectual traditions. The Symbols table defines a frame as "any bounded system with a library: an agent, a person, a discipline, a model." A school of thought is a frame, not a molecule of the physical substrate.

Re-census per the canonical rule (a reading that makes figures contradict each other has misidentified its domain): Fig 13 governs composition inside a frame; Fig 6 and Fig 8 govern relations between frames; Fig 11 governs the habitat of actors around a residual. The schools belong to the between-frames census. No figure was distorted to fit. The strain is dissolved, not deleted; it is recorded here.

Verdict: no strain remains after re-census. Status `general`.

**Refuter.** Derive a mapping from the six physical terms to the six named economic schools that is load-bearing in a prediction rather than an analogy. If such a derivation pays, the re-census was wrong and F10 is retracted.

### F11 The author's interest in the outcome is structural and is declared

Fig 10 :59: "an interest in the outcome is unavoidable, an interest in the reading is disqualifying." Fig 10 :60: "Where a stake cannot be removed, declare it." The stake here is a preference for a pluralist answer, because the field itself (IAFFE, ICAPE, AHE, the heterodox methodology literature) is organised around the value of pluralism. That is an interest in the reading if it is allowed to decide which figures govern. The pre-registered refuter below and the separation of verdicts per domain are the controls. The author has no institutional stake in any particular school winning a slot.

Verdict: declared, not removed. Status `general`.

## 3. Pre-registered refuter

Written before the conclusion and before any outreach, per the seat-map rule that "a falsifier written before the run is a design instrument, not only an honesty device." The shape claim in this folder is refuted if any of the following holds:

1. **Rename failure.** A heterodox school's apparently distinct explanation is a pure renaming of a mainstream atom with the same evidence source and no live residual slot (breaks F2).
2. **Reachability failure.** A mainstream model derives one of the contested atoms from inside its own closure, with the derivation preceding any contact with the heterodox frame (breaks F3).
3. **Independence failure.** The apparent surplus comes from two frames drawing on the same dataset, assumptions, and yardstick, so independence is zero (breaks F5).
4. **Anchor failure.** A non-updating ground is produced that settles the surplus for all seven frames with a single reading, so no per-slot verdict is needed (breaks F6 and F8).
5. **Figure failure.** A figure line is found that ranks schools by explanatory surplus, or that returns the literal yes/no the question asks for (breaks F8).
6. **Union failure.** A single mechanism generates all six schools' claims as special cases with no residual left (breaks F1).
7. **Instrument failure.** An instrument is shown that reads a global surplus scalar across all phenomenon slots without losing which slot is live (breaks F4).

If any fires, the affected finding is withdrawn and the folder is re-censused. Until one is run, no part of this is labelled `grounded`.

## 4. Recommendation

1. Refuse the yes/no framing and present the union separator of F1 and F4. The right output is a per-slot, per-school verdict table, not a slogan about pluralism.
2. Replace "distinct" with "closure differs at the evidence level plus a described residual," and replace "miss" with "unreachable from that closure without import" (F2, F3).
3. Install a non-updating anchor per slot: a prediction that can fail in historical time. Do not accept citation share, curriculum share, or professional consensus as that anchor (F6).
4. Keep the six schools' verdicts separate. They are not a bloc, and their mutual agreement is one evidence pool (F5).
5. Record every import with a source gate and a shadow test, debited against independence (F9).
6. Report the literal question as `gap`: a verdict, not a silence (F8).
7. Send the generator, the separator and the refuter, not a recording of a conclusion. Fig 4 :25: "A recording carried upward looks like knowledge and is a description of one occasion." Fig 10 :52: "Bring a characterised residual, never the source."

### Round trip note

The condensation from the figures into the separator "different closures plus described residual plus unmoved anchor plus independence" loses at least three constraints, so the round-trip gap R_T is nonzero: the type difference between symmetric "distinct" and ordered "miss" (F1), the provenance requirement on imports (F9), and the per-slot indexing (F4). Treating the formula as identical to the figure set would set R_T to zero and be the dead-reckoning error Fig 4 :30 names. `11_audit.md` enumerates the gap in full.

### Updated verdict

A genuine explanatory surplus is possible and its shape is fixed by the figures: differing closures at the evidence level, a described residual per phenomenon slot, a non-updating ground, and independence. The folder does **not** assert which of the six schools has surplus, because no figure line governs that ranking and no non-updating ground was brought to this simulated run. Strains after re-census: none live; one retracted mapping (F10) and one declared stake (F11) are visible above. The whole remains `specified`/`general`, ungrounded, candidate.
