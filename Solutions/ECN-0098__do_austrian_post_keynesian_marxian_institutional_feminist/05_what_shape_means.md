# What "shape" means for this question

Sources opened directly this session (2026-11-23): *The Recursive Self-Improvement Trap*, *The Library of Babel: Locating The Book of Books*, and *The Hard Problem is About Source*, all by Isaiah N at adventuresinml.substack.com. ## 1. A shape is a constraint, not a filler

The figures README gives the canonical limit: the framework returns "what a missing thing would have to look like (a shape, not a name)." Fig 9 :32: "you get a kind of thing, never the answer itself." Fig 13 :134: "Unreachable is a verdict here, not a silence."

From *The Library of Babel* (opened directly): "you can cut your search short if the set of books returned is small and descriptive enough that it provides a general shape of the answer that satisfies your question." And on stopping: "You will know that you are done because the finish line will also be the starting place."

So a **shape** is the narrowed form of an answer you can state before you can name it. It constrains; it does not fill. For this question, the shape is the four-part separator: differing closure at the evidence level, a described residual per slot, a non-updating ground, and independence across frames. "Heterodox economics has surplus" is not a shape; it is a name applied before any slot has been described. "The mainstream is wrong" is also a name, and a symmetric complaint with no ordered prediction.

The same essay gives the exact reason the question must be split: "Perhaps your problem was a bundle or conflation of several problems masquerading as one? You will need to search all of those problems individually to find their answers and then combine them in a satisfactory way." That is Fig 9 :54-57 stated in the Essays' vocabulary, and it is the governing shape reading for ECN-0098.

## 2. How a shape is obtained

- *Library of Babel* (opened directly): use the vocabulary already held that is *closest* to the thing, look for new terms that overlap or recur near it, and recurse. "With each new term gained, you narrow the space and deepen your vocabulary." The anchor is the question itself: "This anchor is provided by your own question."
- Fig 6 :30: "The description travels. Fails by being sharpened into terms only we can read. Cast it in effect terms." Fig 9 :30-31: "your words are borrowed too, so describe the effect rather than the cause."
- *Decoding Tacit Knowledge* (carried from the exemplar's canonical mapping): "When contact isn't possible, the best you can send is a plain description of what you'd expect to see, because anyone can check that against the world."
- Applied here: describe the failure slot in effect terms first ("persistent unemployment after a demand shock," "unpriced care work," "emergent coordination failure") before selecting a school. If the school is picked first, any school will seem to fit (Fig 9 :27-28).

## 3. What licenses a name

Only two things, both outside the shape itself:

1. **The ground settles it.** Formula step 5: "the ground settles it. Verified only by what pays." The figures README says the framework "stops at the limit of what the available instruments can distinguish, and records the lineage so the stop is honest."
2. **A provenanced import from a frame whose closure differs** (Fig 6 :23, Fig 8 :50-52), with a source gate and a shadow test.

*They're Scanning Books* (carried): "The real import is not a new term in the existing space. It is a new dimension of the space." And: "A minimum viable set of six terms is a human's careful guess at reality's compression axes; another sensorium's minimum set need not be those six." Also: "Declaring the primitives does not make them neutral. It makes the choice visible."

In this question the nine names (six schools plus mainstream plus the terms "distinct" and "miss") are all visible choices. Naming a school as *the* source of a surplus would turn a visible choice into an invisible one. The correct form is a binding recorded with provenance: "Post-Keynesian frame supplies the fundamental-uncertainty atom, bound from that frame, not derived by the figures."

### Correction to my own §1 and §3 (kept visible)

I first wrote that "shape, not a name" forbids naming any school. It does not. The canonical phrase governs a *missing primitive*, one not held in Γ. The schools here are frames already available in the world, so naming one is a binding with provenance, not the invention of a seventh kind. What remains forbidden is claiming that a figure line ranks them, or that a binding moves the wall without adding an atom (Fig 6 :25 "An import adds no atom to the world"). Both readings are recorded so neither is quietly lost.

## 4. The ground and the source problem

*The Hard Problem is About Source* (opened directly) gives the mechanism that matters most here: metamerism. An instrument that reads green "cannot comment on the source." Several underlying causes can give the same reading, so "If several paths create the same behavior, then behavior is a projection with lossy information." The essay's conclusion: "The answer is unreached until a better instrument exists" and "An instrument that can't distinguish these cases demonstrates its own inadequacy."

Applied to this question: a citation count, a curriculum share, and a survey of economists' opinions are all readings of the same projection, the profession's current weighting. They cannot comment on the source, which is whether a frame reaches a ground at a slot. The essay's remedy was the spectrometer, not another argument. Here the corresponding instrument is a discriminating test at the slot: a prediction that can fail in historical time, independent of which school produced it. Fig 2 :29: "An anchor is whatever settles the question and does not change its answer because of what any frame thinks."

*How a Society of Agents Would Work* and *Arrival of the Fittest* (carried) supply the parallel claim that capability is a property of agent-and-habitat, never of the agent alone, which is why a school cannot be assessed apart from the ground it ranges against and the habitat of institutions around it.

## 5. Answer to the shape question

**What does "shape" require here?** Not a verdict on pluralism. It requires a separator that any candidate answer must satisfy:

1. Split the question into phenomenon slots; it is a bundle, not one question (*Library of Babel*, Fig 9 :54-57).
2. For each slot, describe the residual in effect terms before choosing a frame (Fig 9 :27-28).
3. Show a closure difference at the evidence level, not only a vocabulary difference (Fig 8 :48-49).
4. Name a non-updating ground that would settle the slot, and refuse the market test of ideas because it updates on the profession's own influence (Fig 2 :29/:33-34).
5. Keep frames independent, so agreement means something and a convergent derivation counts as corroboration at maximum independence (Fig 2 :31-32; Fig 8 :50).
6. Record every import with provenance and a shadow test (Fig 8 :50-52).

**Does any figure line name which school wins?** No. After a genuine census of all 13 figures and both tables, no line ranks schools by explanatory surplus. That is a `gap`, reported as a verdict. "Unreachable is a verdict here, not a silence" (Fig 13 :134).

## 6. Consequences for contact

**Prestige is not action.** From *The Recursive Self-Improvement Trap* (opened directly): "Prestige can fund an action. It can give an action a budget to be tried. But it cannot settle an action. Only the ground can settle it. The Ground Maintainer must be blind to prestige." A campaign that gathers prestigious pluralist names to agree with the separator is the named collapse, not corroboration. The legitimate ask is the opposite: give the separator a budget to be tried by someone who can refuse it.

**My own audit is a simulation, not a ground reading.** Same essay: "LLMs can quickly simulate an approximate answer/output with the formula found in the architecture post" and "the truly testable output would need to be derived from the real architecture or working the formula yourself." The essay also says "the output is only as good as the validator. The agent, or the tool, or the frame, or the theory, cannot validate itself." So `01`, `06`, `07`, `09` and `11` are simulated derivations checked for internal consistency against the figures. They are **not** tests of the question and must be labelled as such. They cannot settle anything they report on.
