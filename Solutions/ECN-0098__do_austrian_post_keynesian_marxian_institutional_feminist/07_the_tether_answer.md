# The Tether's answer: Do Austrian, Post-Keynesian, Marxian, institutional, feminist, and complexity approaches offer distinct explanations that mainstream models miss?

**Mode: `specified` on the per-school and per-slot claims, `general` on the structural shape, not `grounded`.** The literal yes/no answer is a declared `gap` of the shape-not-a-name kind. This is a shape and a route, not a result. Simulated derivation checked against the figures (`05_what_shape_means.md` section 6), not a ground reading.

## 1. The residual it answers

The question asks whether six heterodox frames explain what one mainstream frame leaves unregistered. In effect terms, R is the set of phenomenon slots s for which the mainstream returns no term while a heterodox frame returns a prediction. The question as posed bundles two incompatible bond types: "distinct" is symmetric (`+`, swap the operands and the meaning does not change), while "miss" is ordered and threshold-like (`⋛` or `⇒`). Fig 9 :4-5: "Disagreements that do not shrink with effort, where each rule keeps working well on a different subset, are not one hard question but several well-formed ones. Split it rather than search."

It is a **union of overlapping conditions**, not a partition. Lee 2008 records that heterodox critiques of any one mainstream core proposition overlap in argumentation and that school boundaries are sometimes "not there at all." ICAPE counts neoclassical economics as one tradition among many. One lever cannot clear a union; it leaves the intersections standing (Fig 9 :55-57).

## 2. The shape

1. **The question must be split per slot, never averaged.** Fig 1 :19: "The residual is indexed by object slot, not aggregated." Fig 1 :22: "Aggregating across slots is how a live signal disappears into an average." Financial crisis, persistent unemployment, distribution, gendered care work, inflation as conflict, dispersed knowledge, and emergence are different slots with different verdicts. There is no folder-wide "heterodox surplus" number.
2. **"Distinct" is necessary but not sufficient.** An un-aimed union is mute: Fig 8 :21 "nothing was aimed; the union is mute"; Fig 8 :7 "interdisciplinarity, no mint." If two frames draw on the same evidence and compose the same atoms, "Two frames that overlap almost completely are one frame wearing two names" (Fig 8 :49). They share a functional group, not a surplus.
3. **"Miss" has a literal structural reading.** Fig 6 :21: "MINT composes inside the closure and can never add an atom." Fig 8 :32: "no search, however long, adds an atom." A class atom, a gendered-power atom, a fundamental-uncertainty atom, a habit atom, or an emergence atom cannot be composed from an optimizing-agent-and-equilibrium closure. It requires an import from a frame whose closure differs (Fig 6 :23). Mearman, Berger and Guizzo 2023 report the field's own version: mainstream economics "does not study things that are very relevant in economic reality because it does not have the method to do it." That is unreachability, not negligence.
4. **The anchor must not be the profession's own weighting.** Fig 2 :29: "An anchor is whatever settles the question and does not change its answer because of what any frame thinks." The "market test of ideas" updates on citation shares, editorial boards and curricula, which Gräbner and Strunk show are path-dependent and asymmetric; under Fig 2 :33-34 that makes it a mirror, not an anchor. The legitimate anchor is a prediction that can fail in historical time.
5. **Independence decides what agreement means.** Fig 2 :31: "Independence, between two frames, is how little they draw on the same evidence." Fig 8 :50: "two frames can hold the same atom by convergence, reaching it separately, which is corroboration at maximum independence." The six schools are not automatically independent of each other; Lee 2008 describes dense cross-citation among them, so their mutual agreement is one pool. Each school must be ranged against the ground separately.
6. **Imports are possible but priced.** Fig 6 :14: "the only operator that moves the wall." Fig 6 :24: "Import from a live peer is debited against that frame's independence. A wrong atom costs more than starting from primitives." An import needs a source gate and a shadow test (Fig 8 :50-52); otherwise a vocabulary change is mistaken for a reach change.
7. **No figure ranks the schools.** Fig 9 :32: "you get a kind of thing, never the answer itself." The literal question receives `gap`, which "is a verdict here, not a silence" (Fig 13 :134).

## 3. What it explicitly does not say

- Not that all six approaches have surplus, or that any one is "right."
- Not that mainstream economics is failed scholarship. Unreachability is a property of a closure, not a verdict on the people inside it.
- Not that more schools automatically means more evidence. Six frames sharing one pool are one witness (Fig 2 :40).
- Not that citation counts, curriculum share or professional consensus settle the matter. Those are "efficient for a convention" (Fig 2 :41) and "disqualifying for a verdict" (Fig 2 :40).
- Not that the schools are isomers as currently written. They share a functional group (social provisioning in historical time), not an atom list. The strong isomer claim needs a fixed atom set and has a falsifier (`06` Q2).
- Not `grounded`. No economic measurement was taken in this simulated derivation.
- Not that the framework settles the question it reports on. "The output is only as good as the validator" (*The Recursive Self-Improvement Trap*).

## 4. What would settle it, the ground

A discriminator per slot, not a further argument. From *The Hard Problem is About Source*: an instrument that reads green "cannot comment on the source"; the spectrometer, not an argument, resolved it. Here:

- **Per slot:** state a prediction in effect terms that fails if the heterodox atom is not needed, and test it with data independent of the school's own community. For example: does a demand-contraction prediction that includes fundamental uncertainty outperform one that does not, on a dataset neither camp used to build its model? Does a model with a gendered-care atom predict a different labor-supply response than one without, on an independently collected panel?
- **Threshold test (`⋛`):** does a comparison hold between two frames' predictions at a specified magnitude, rather than merely both being present? Operators table :49: "a threshold relation between two quantities."
- **Convergence test:** do two frames reach the same atom from non-overlapping evidence with derivations recorded before contact (Fig 8 :50-52)?
- **Import test:** when a mainstream model adopts a heterodox atom, is the provenance and shadow test recorded, so a reach change is not confused with a vocabulary change (Fig 8 :12-13)?
- **Standing falsifiers:** a pure rename with no live slot; a mainstream derivation of a contested atom before any heterodox contact; zero independence; a single anchor for all frames; a figure line ranking schools; a single mechanism generating all six with no residual. All seven are written in `01` section 3 before this conclusion.

## 5. The honest stop

**Unreached, not unreachable in principle.** The separator is stateable and checkable; the ranking is not. The figure set gives the shape any answer must have and stops at the limit of the instruments available in this simulated run. "Indiscernibility is not identity": the six schools' agreement with each other is not evidence that they have found the same ground. The folder records the lineage so the stop is checkable rather than comfortable. Prestige and agreement are not instruments: "Prestige can fund an action. It can give an action a budget to be tried. But it cannot settle an action. Only the ground can settle it" (*The Recursive Self-Improvement Trap*).

## 6. One-paragraph version for outreach

> The Tether does not answer yes or no to whether Austrian, Post-Keynesian, Marxian, institutional, feminist and complexity economics offer distinct explanations that mainstream models miss, because no figure line ranks schools and no single ground settles seven frames at once. It returns the shape any answer must have. The question is a union of overlapping conditions, so the verdict must be taken per phenomenon slot and never averaged. "Distinct" is not enough: different names for the same atom are one frame wearing two names, and an un-aimed union is mute. "Miss" is structural: composition inside a closure never adds a primitive, so a genuinely new atom such as class, gendered power, fundamental uncertainty or emergence is unreachable from the mainstream closure without an import from a frame whose closure differs. But the import needs provenance and a shadow test, the anchor cannot be the profession's own citation-weighted consensus, and the schools must be kept independent because agreement from one evidence pool is one witness. What the framework offers is that four-part separator, the per-school verdicts kept separate, the falsifiers written in advance, and the instruments that would close each slot. The ranking stays open until a ground settles it.
