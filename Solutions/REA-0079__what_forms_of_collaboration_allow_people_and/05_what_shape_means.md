## 1. A shape is a constraint, not a filler

The canonical rule: the framework returns *"what a missing thing would have to look like (a shape, not a name)"*. Fig 9 :32 says *"you get a kind of thing, never the answer itself"*. From *The Library of Babel*: *"you can cut your search short if the set of books returned is small and descriptive enough that it provides a general shape of the answer that satisfies your question."*

For this question a shape is the set of constraints any collaboration must satisfy before it can count as correcting a blind spot. It is not a list of product features and not a name of a tool. The shape says: there must be an anchor that does not move, the two frames must not share one evidence pool, the residual must be described before a collaborator is selected, what crosses between them must be methods and head starts, the checker must not hold the generative interior, and successive collaborations must be selected against the residual. A specific arrangement, for example "two models debate and a third judges", is a binding onto that shape, not the shape.

## 2. How the shape is obtained

*The Library of Babel* gives the search discipline: use the vocabulary already held that is closest to the thing, look for new terms that overlap or correlate with it, and recurse. *"With each new term gained, you are able to narrow the space by definition and deepen your vocabulary."* It also gives the steering rule: *"The books that you are returning in your searches need to be tested or ranged against the problem you want answered"*.

Fig 9 :27 (*"describe it before you go looking, or any frame you pick will seem to fit"*) and Fig 9 :31 (*"your words are borrowed too, so describe the effect rather than the cause"*) are the figure-level version. So the residual in REA-0079 is described in effect terms: a per-slot error a frame cannot read, not "a human-AI disagreement".

The same essay contains a direct line about collaboration: *"Convergence towards the answer is faster with multiple helpers, but communication between them is paramount and would have to be virtually 1:1 to be effective."* Note the constraint. It is one to one communication, not a crowd. That is why `02_outreach_email.md` is one to one and `03_public_contact_points.csv` has no bulk channel.

## 3. What licenses a name

Two things, both outside the shape itself:

1. **The ground settles it.** `THE_FORMULA.md` step 5: "the ground settles it. Verified only by what pays. A gate is not the ground." In this question, a name like "blind review" or "red teaming" or "debate" is licensed only when an experiment shows that it reduces the per-slot residual.
2. **A provenanced import from a frame whose closure differs** (Fig 6 :23 *"No frame escapes its own closure. The bound moves only on contact with a frame whose closure differs."*), with a source gate and a shadow test. An imported practice must explain a gap described in advance.

*The Hard Problem is About Source* gives the reason an agreement reading is not enough: *"If several paths create the same behavior, then behavior is a projection with lossy information"*. A human and an AI that agree may have reached agreement from a shared corpus, from mutual copying, or from separate contact with the same ground. The agreement reading cannot discriminate. The essay adds: *"An instrument that can't distinguish these cases demonstrates its own inadequacy."* So a source-discriminating check is owed, not a vote count.

## 4. Correction to my own overstatement

I initially wrote this section as if "shape, not a name" forbade naming any form of collaboration. That is too strong. The canonical phrase governs a **missing** primitive, one not held in the library. "Naming" a practice that is already in the habitat, such as a blind test or a two-instrument comparison, is a binding from the world frame onto a held pattern, and it must be provenanced as such. What remains forbidden is inventing a new primitive to fill the slot, or claiming that a binding moves the closure wall when it adds no atom (Fig 6 :22: *"IMPORT adds an atom, and the added atom is what expands the closure."*). Both the original claim and the narrowing are recorded here so the correction is visible.

## 5. Validation and self-validation

*The Recursive Self-Improvement Trap*: *"the output is only as good as the validator. The agent, or the tool, or the frame, or the theory, cannot validate itself."* The same essay, on the failure this question is really about: *"You may all agree and be wrong. You may set your clock to someone else's, who was setting your clock to yours, and be off by many deviations over time. You need ground truth to define where you stand."*

That sentence is the figure-level mirror collapse stated in prose. It is why the answer to "what forms of collaboration allow correction" cannot be "the ones where they agree most".

The essay also gives the status of this file: "LLMs can quickly simulate an approximate answer/output with the formula", but "the truly testable output would need to be derived from the real architecture or working the formula yourself." So `01_figure_grounded_audit.md` and this file are simulations checked for internal consistency against the figures. They are not tests of any collaboration protocol.

## 6. Prestige is not a check

From the same essay: "Prestige can fund an action. It can give an action a budget to be tried. But it cannot settle an action. Only the ground can settle it. The Ground Maintainer must be blind to prestige." A collaboration form that borrows authority from a famous lab or a well known reviewer has been given a budget to be tried, not a verdict. Fig 2 :42 (*"Buy independence in proportion to the cost of being wrong."*) says what to spend instead.

## 7. Consequences for the answer file

`07_the_tether_answer.md` states the shape and marks each named practice as an instance with provenance. It does not assert that any named practice has been shown to work here. The honest stop is: the shape is derivable, a particular protocol is not, and what would close it is an instrument that discriminates among the paths that produce agreement.
