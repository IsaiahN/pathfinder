# The Tether's answer: which collaborations correct blind spots

**Mode: `specified` on the forms, `general` on the shape. Not `grounded`.** Simulated derivation checked against the figures, not a ground reading. No human-AI pair was measured in producing this answer.

## 1. The residual it answers

A blind spot is a per-slot residual a frame cannot read because its library lacks the term (Fig 1 :11, :19). The question asks which collaboration forms let a person and an AI system reduce each other's residuals. The residual of the question itself is a union of conditions, not one lever: anchor, independence, residual description, exchange rule, verification seat, and selection between rounds. A single lever clears only the region where one condition holds and leaves the intersections standing.

Two readings were available. Reading A: they correct each other by exchanging information and agreeing. Reading B: each is corrected separately by an anchor that does not move, with low evidence overlap between them. The figures decide B.

## 2. The shape, one verdict per domain

**D1, frame and ground contact. Triangulated.** Fig 2 :35: **"Alignment is triangulation, not negotiation. A mirror cannot correct you."** Fig 2 :46: **"Each frame is corrected by it separately, and what makes the correction sound is that neither frame can move it."** So a form counts only if a test, measurement or outcome that neither party may revise changes each party separately. Agreement between the pair is variation, not a verdict (Fig 7 :35).

**D2, coupling between frames. Triangulated.** Fig 2 :31: **"Independence, between two frames, is how little they draw on the same evidence."** Fig 8 :49: **"Two frames that overlap almost completely are one frame wearing two names."** The form is defined by what evidence each side holds that the other does not, not by how many participants or models are present. Two instances of the same model on the same prompt are one frame with two names.

**D3, verification seat. Single-source on the strong form, narrowed after a world conflict.** Fig 10 :42: **"A verifier that can reconstruct the claim cannot verify it."** The checker gets the characterised residual and the observed failure, plus any stated assumption that can be tested. It does not get the generative interior that lets it reconstruct the answer. The narrowing is recorded in `01_figure_grounded_audit.md` section 7.

**D4, habitat and team composition. Triangulated.** The relevant actors are enumerated from contact with the residual, not assigned from a role chart. The same AI instance is a benefactor for one residual and a malfactor for another (Fig 11 :50), so no standing list of good collaborators survives a change of residual. Changing the model without changing contact changes nothing, however much it improves (Fig 11 :40).

**D5, chain over rounds. Single-source, corroborated at low overlap.** Fig 7 :35: **"A room's agreement is variation, not a verdict. Selection between rooms is where the ground votes."** The next collaboration must differ from the last and match the residual the last one surfaced. Otherwise the chain is one long room: motion without progress.

**Ordering condition, all domains.** Fig 9 :27: **"describe it before you go looking, or any frame you pick will seem to fit"** The residual is stated in effect terms and kept per slot before a collaborator is chosen. Averaging into one satisfaction score destroys the signal (Fig 1 :22).

**Exchange condition, all domains.** Fig 4 :22: **"Only methods cross up. Only head starts come down."** What travels from the pair to the organisation is a reapplicable method; what comes back is a head start, never a recording of one success.

## 3. What it explicitly does not say

- Not that agreement between the person and the AI is evidence of correction. It is the thing that needs explaining.
- Not that mutual updating corrects anything. It is the coherent-drift collapse (Fig 2 :13-15).
- Not that a shared rubric is an anchor if the pair may revise it. An anchor that updates is another frame (Fig 2 :34).
- Not a named protocol. The framework returns a shape, not a name. "Blind review", "debate" and "red teaming" are bindings from the world frame and are licensed only by a ground or a provenanced import (Fig 6 :23).
- Not a verdict on which layer wins: for a question about what is good for people, human judgment is the right anchor because the question is constitutively about people (Fig 2 :37 gives the constitutive test). For a question about whether the per-slot residual fell against a test, that test is the anchor and human judgment is an instrument that can be wrong on axes it does not measure.
- Not `grounded`. Nothing here has been settled by an experiment, and this derivation cannot validate itself.

## 4. What would settle it, the ground

What is owed is a source-discriminating instrument, not more agreement: a check that distinguishes a pair ranging against a test from a pair sharing a pool, and a pair with an anchor from a pair drifting coherently. Concretely:

1. **Per-slot outcome test.** Does the anchored, low-overlap pair reduce held-out per-slot residuals more than a high-overlap pair and more than either frame alone? This fires refuter 1.
2. **Drift test.** Does a no-anchor pair that continuously updates on each other fail to beat the anchored pair? This fires refuter 2.
3. **Order test.** Does describing the residual before choosing the collaborator beat "just use AI as a collaborator"? This fires refuter 3.
4. **Access test.** With the same ground available, does withholding the generative interior do at least as well as handing it over? This fires refuter 4.
5. **Channel test.** Does keeping residuals per slot beat pooling them into one score? This fires F7.

Agreement, enthusiasm, reputation and the number of participants are none of these. Fig 2 :42: **"Buy independence in proportion to the cost of being wrong."**

## 5. The honest stop

**Unreached, not unreachable.** Fig 13 :134: **"Unreachable is a verdict here, not a silence"** and Fig 8 :39: **"So a search that finds nothing is never a proof of absence."** The shape stops at the limit of what these instruments can distinguish, and the lineage is recorded so the stop is checkable. Indiscernibility is not identity: a pair that agrees has not thereby been shown to correct.

## 6. One-paragraph version for outreach

> A blind spot is an error on a slot a frame cannot yet read. The Tether says that a person and an AI system do not correct each other by converging: a mirror cannot correct you. They correct only if each ranges separately against something neither may move, draw on different evidence rather than one shared pool, describe the blind spot before choosing the collaborator, exchange methods and head starts rather than recordings of past successes, give the checker the residual and the testable assumption rather than the model's generative interior, and select the next collaboration against the residual the last one surfaced. The same three participants under a mutual-update or shared-pool bond are a different substance with the same org chart. What is not claimed is that any named protocol has been shown to work here, or that this derivation is a test: it is a shape with four refuters written in advance, waiting for a ground.
