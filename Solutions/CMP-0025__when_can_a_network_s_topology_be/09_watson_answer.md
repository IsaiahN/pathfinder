## QUESTION

When can a network's topology be treated as fixed, and when must link formation and removal be modeled as part of the dynamics? Restated as an effect: a model predicts a process outcome from a belief about adjacency and an intervention; R = |Γ(b,a) - o'| is indexed per node trajectory and per candidate link (Fig 1 :19). Option A, fixed topology, predicts a held link-slot residual. Option B, link dynamics, predicts a persistent non-trending link residual while the node residual is small. What each leads to: A spends budget on process detail that cannot compose a missing link; B requires an edge-revealing anchor and a measured round-trip gap before it is owed.

## DOMAIN CENSUS

R by arity, symmetry and scale, then one line per domain giving actors, currency and anchor, partition or union, and order by dependency.

- **R (arity / symmetry / scale).** Three-place relation (adjacency belief, action, observation), indexed per slot. Symmetry: formation and removal are ordered bonds (⇒ production, − subtraction, ¬ refusal); the link bond may be symmetric (+) or ordered (→). Scale: first order is the process on a graph, second order is graph evolution, with a lossy membrane between. Two conditions are entangled, so R is a **union compound**, not one hard question (Fig 9 :57).
- **D1 Process on a fixed graph (quenched).** Actors: nodes, held links, the process. Currency: node-state residual. Anchor: measured node states over the window. Status: admitted.
- **D2 Topology evolution.** Actors: nodes, potential links, formation and removal rules. Currency: link residual, new arrangements of held atoms. Anchor: time-resolved edge observations or an edge-revealing intervention. Status: admitted.
- **D3 Observation and inference (the named field, network topology inference research).** Actors: observer, partial data, hidden edges. Currency: information about adjacency. Anchor: ground-truth adjacency when obtainable, otherwise an edge-revealing experiment. Status: admitted.
- **D4 Timescale membrane.** Actors: process timescale, topology timescale, instruments. Currency: what crosses between scales, round-trip gap R_T. Anchor: measured timescales and the measured gap. Status: admitted.
- **X "topology as intrinsic, independent of observation."** No nameable anchor once measurement and timescale are removed. Not a domain yet; the question is constitutively about the observation (Fig 2 :36), so it is absorbed into D3.

**Partition or union:** the regimes partition by observed timescale ratio, but the residual is a union compound of process mis-specification and topology mis-specification. Unbundle before searching (Fig 9 :54-57). **Order by dependency:** perception, vocabulary, objective, planning, learn and carry (Fig 3). The chain usually breaks between vocabulary and objective: having terms for what a graph affords and saying what counts as progress is the least-instrumented link (Fig 3 :27).

Strain test: Fig 13 :36 separates adjacency from duration as distinct structures; it does not forbid their composition. Re-census with Fig 13 :74 resolves the apparent contradiction. See `01_figure_grounded_audit.md` section 7.

## VERDICT (from the figures)

**The figure-derived verdict, kept per domain and never averaged:** fixed topology is the correct treatment when the per-link residual is held and the node residual trends down under a fixed adjacency; link formation and removal must enter the dynamics when the per-link residual persists and does not trend down while the node residual is small and the round-trip gap closes when edge dynamics are admitted. The two cases are a union compound, so more process detail clears only one region. A formed link is an arrangement of held atoms, not a new primitive, and what is owed is a refusal of constant adjacency. With timescale separation the fast process is a catalyst for slow rewiring; with comparable timescales the coupling is a cycle. The anchor is a time-resolved edge observation. No universal switching number is licensed: that is a declared `gap`. Mode `specified`, ungrounded, candidate. The world sources predict this shape in the network-science vocabulary but do not decide it, and several share one evidence pool.

- **cost if wrong:** a misdirected modelling budget. A union case is treated as a process-only case, so effort lands on sharper process parameters while the live link residual keeps persisting; or edge dynamics are added where a fixed graph would have done, paying a description cost for an atom that was not owed. For outreach, an expert's twenty minutes would be spent checking a claim whose refuter was never stated. The cost is bounded and locatable because the shape, the four refuters and the lineage are recorded. Fig 2 :42 "Buy independence in proportion to the cost of being wrong." The avoidable part is spending reader independence by leading with a recording rather than a generator.
- **undoable:** yes. No term is deleted if the claim fails; a refusal is added and the reach falls because *"Nothing leaves the library. What falls is the reach."* (Fig 5 :55). The defective parts, if any, are removable without touching the census. A sent claim can be superseded by a corrected packet. Nothing here commits a network to a regime.
- **reversed by:** a ground reading, not agreement. A time-resolved edge observation showing link turnover with a fixed-graph model still at held link residual reverses Option B for that system; a round-trip gap near zero under demonstrable outcome-changing turnover breaks the membrane rule; one intervention clearing both residuals with no categorically different residue dissolves the union; a figure line giving a universal threshold after genuine re-census would close the gap; no edge instrument at any resolution changes the verdict to `unreachable` for that system rather than "dynamic topology required." Prestige or agreement is not such an instrument.
