## 4. Round trip gap R_T

The Symbols table defines R_T as the gap between what was sent up and what came back down, `gap(x, (T_E ∘ T_A)(x))`, measuring what the coarser description cannot hold. Fig 4 :30 requires it to be measured rather than assumed. I ran the round trip on the condensation this folder actually uses.

1. **Sent up (x):** the verbatim figure set, chiefly Fig 1 :19, Fig 5 :59, Fig 6 :21, Fig 4 :30, Fig 9 :54, Fig 13 :36 and :115.
2. **Abstraction (T_A):** the shorthand in `07_the_tether_answer.md`: index per slot; refuse constant adjacency when the link residual persists; unbundle the union; measure the round-trip gap; let an edge anchor decide; no universal number.
3. **Concretisation (T_E):** a concrete claim about a named system: "in this contact network the process is faster than tie turnover, so use a static graph and interpret the remaining link residual as process noise."

**R_T is nonzero.** Three things the shorthand holds do not survive the trip back down:

- the **instrument requirement**: the shorthand says "let an edge anchor decide," but a concrete system may have no edge observation at any resolution (Fig 6 :34-35). The shortcut silently converts an `unreachable` verdict into a fixed-topology verdict.
- the **union structure**: the shorthand says "unbundle," but a single fitted process model can make the two conditions look like one. The intersection is categorically different and is lost in the concrete reading.
- the **refusal order**: Formula step 2 requires the refusal of constant adjacency before a mint. The concrete reading typically mints a process parameter first, which is exactly the inversion F2 names.

Conversely, one thing survives cleanly: the residual must be indexed per node and per link, and the decision is made on a persistent non-trending link residual. That is the residue worth sending. R_T was **not** measured numerically, because no ground reading exists; it is recorded qualitatively as the enumerated set of dropped constraints. Treating "the formula is the condensed version of the metalaws" as identity would set R_T to zero and would be the dead-reckoning error Fig 4 :30 names. The honest form is a lossy condensation with a recorded gap, which is what `09_watson_answer.md` states.
