## QUESTION

Decide, in effect terms, what distinguishes two outcomes of targeted state support. Option A: the supported sector gains productive capacity that survives withdrawal of support. Option B: the support becomes a durable transfer to politically connected firms with no such surviving gain. What each leads to: A requires a described residual, a change in the firm's contact with a competitive ground, an anchor metric with a real refusal clause, and low-overlap evaluation; B is the regime where selection runs by influence and the would-be ground updates on the frames. The shape is `CAPACITY := (described residual) AND (support changes contact) AND (grounded metric with refusal) AND (low-overlap evaluation)`.

## DOMAIN CENSUS

- **R (the residual).** The gap between the promise of industrial policy and the observed distribution of outcomes. Arity: two conditions, so a union compound (Fig 9 :54, :58), not one hard question. Symmetry: not symmetric; capacity and capture differ by the bond between support and the ground (Fig 12 :94). Scale: first order at the firm slot, second order at the evaluating institution, with a lossy round trip between measurement and policy (Fig 4).
- **D1 The firm and its habitat.** Actors: supported firm, competitors, suppliers, domestic and foreign buyers. Currency: productivity, exports, survival without support. Anchor: the market test at world prices after support ends. Nameable, so a domain.
- **D2 The implementing state.** Actors: ministry, monitoring agency, politicians, firms. Currency: performance conditions, monitoring, withdrawal. Anchor: an external metric that does not move when the allocator is lobbied, state-representable and ground-checkable. Nameable, so a domain.
- **D3 The political distribution frame.** Actors: connected firms, interest groups, electoral incentives. Currency: influence, rents. Anchor for this question: none. Its would-be ground updates on the frames, so it is the failure mode rather than an independent domain. Recorded as the collapse to be explained.
- **D4 The evaluation frame.** Actors: auditors, competitors, export data, researchers. Currency: residuals versus agreement. Anchor: an independent measurement the firm and ministry cannot move, with low overlap to D2. Nameable, so a domain.
- **Partition or union.** D1 and D2 form a union compound; D3 is the collapse that removes the anchor; D4 checks the result. Two conditions, two loops, separate verdicts.
- **Order by dependency (Fig 3).** Describe the residual with an instrument already returning something; state a state-representable and ground-checkable metric; bond support to it; verify against the ground; refuse the persistent non-performer before minting new support.
- **Strain test.** Fig 11's "exclusion is not" and the policy's withdrawal clause govern different quantities (habitat contact versus the state's own introduced term). Fig 3's "in its own terms" and Fig 10's ban on outcome-deciding conventions govern representation versus authorship. Both strains re-censused and resolved in `01` §6. One live strain remains: the identity of the anchor measure is figure-silent.

## VERDICT (from the figures)

The shape survives; the specific bindings do not. Capacity and capture are a union compound distinguished by the bond between support and the ground. Support builds capacity when it changes the firm's contact with a competitive ground, when the metric is an anchor that does not move with political pressure, when the program can refuse a persistent non-performer, and when evaluation uses low-overlap independent evidence. Support protects connected firms when selection runs by influence and the metric has stopped being an anchor. The same subsidy under different bonds is a different substance. The figures do not name the winning sector, the performance measure, or the subsidy amount; those are imports from economics, provenanced as such and still unproven. This is a `specified`, ungrounded candidate.

- **cost if wrong:** a wrong cut removes a real infant industry and speeds up the decline it was meant to prevent (Fig 9 :40 "a wrong cut removes the answer and speeds up so keep every cut ranked and reversible"). The second cost is spending the independence of outside readers by sending a list of successes instead of a refuter; Fig 2 :42 "Buy independence in proportion to the cost of being wrong." Both are reduced by keeping cuts ranked and reversible and by leading with the refuter.
- **undoable:** yes. Support, metrics and withdrawal clauses are institutional arrangements, not primitives; they can be revised without withdrawing the shape. Fig 9 :41 "so keep every cut ranked and reversible" is the operating rule. No figure term has to be retracted to change a program.
- **reversed by:** a ground reading, not agreement. A credible counterfactual showing surviving productivity after withdrawal would settle the metric and move the binding toward `grounded`; a demonstration that the allocation metric moves with political pressure would confirm the capture regime; a matched comparison showing no difference between programs with and without refusal clauses would break C2 and the bond claim; a figure re-census that licenses an updating metric as an anchor for this question would break D3. Until one of these fires, the verdict stands as `candidate`.
