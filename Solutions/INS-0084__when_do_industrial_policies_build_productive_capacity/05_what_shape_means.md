## 1. A shape is a constraint, not a filler

The figures README states the limit: the framework returns *"what a missing thing would have to look like (**a shape, not a name**)."* Fig 9 :32 *"you get a kind of thing, never the answer itself"*. Fig 13 :134 *"It cannot say which primitive is missing, because naming it would mean already holding it."*

From *The Library of Babel* (read directly): *"you can cut your search short if the set of books returned is small and descriptive enough that it provides a general shape of the answer that satisfies your question."*

So a **shape** is the narrowed form of an answer you can state before you can name it. It constrains; it does not fill. For this question the shape is:

`CAPACITY := (described residual) AND (support changes contact) AND (grounded metric with refusal) AND (low-overlap evaluation)`

That is a shape. "Subsidize semiconductor fabrication in province X" is a name. "Use export survival as the metric" is also a name, and the figures do not license it.

## 2. How the shape was obtained

- *Library of Babel*: use the vocabulary you already have closest to the thing, look for recurring new terms that overlap or correlate with it, recurse. *"With each new term gained, you are able to narrow the space by definition and deepen your vocabulary."*
- Fig 6 :30 *"The description travels. Fails by being sharpened into terms only we can read. Cast it in effect terms."* Fig 9 :31 *"your words are borrowed too, so describe the effect rather than the cause"*. So the residual was described as "support changes contact with a competitive ground," not as "improve firm-level dynamism," a phrase that already assumes the answer.
- *Decoding Tacit Knowledge*: *"When contact isn't possible, the best you can send is a plain description of what you'd expect to see, because anyone can check that against the world."* This is why the outreach packet leads with refuters stated as things a reader could observe.
- *Library of Babel* on stopping: the answer is reached when *"the finish line will also be the starting place"* and the paradox is that you have *"already composed the answer through the process of locating it"*. Here the starting place was "what would I count as a failure," which became the pre-registered refuter in `01` §4.

## 3. What licenses a name

Only two things, both outside the shape itself:

1. **The ground settles it.** Formula step 5: *"the ground settles it. Verified only by what pays."* The README: the framework *"stops at the limit of what the available instruments can distinguish, and records the lineage so the stop is honest."* For this question: a credible counterfactual showing surviving productivity after withdrawal would settle the metric choice.
2. **A provenanced import from a frame whose closure differs** (Fig 6 :23, Fig 8 :50-52), with a source gate and a shadow test. "Export survival" would be an import from development economics; it must be recorded as such, with provenance, not smuggled in as a figure-derived term.

From *They're Scanning Books When They Should* (mapped from the exemplar's local reading, not re-fetched): *"The real import is not a new term in the existing space. It is a new dimension of the space."* And: *"Declaring the primitives does not make them neutral. It makes the choice visible."* The six-term substrate and the figure vocabulary are a visible guess, not nature's own list.

## 4. Why naming the metric from the reading is the forbidden error

*The Hard Problem is About Source* (read directly) gives the mechanism. Metamerism: an instrument reads green and *"cannot comment on the source"*; one green light, or yellow plus blue, or RGB, or CMYK can produce the same reading. *"If several paths create the same behavior, then behavior is a projection with lossy information (it cannot define consciousness in full)."* And: *"An instrument that can't distinguish these cases demonstrates its own inadequacy."* It was the **spectrometer**, not an argument, that resolved it: *"It was proven wrong not by argument, but by the arrival of a better instrument."*

Applied here: a rise in measured sector output is a reading. Its source could be genuine productivity, or a subsidy-driven expansion of low-productivity entrants (the shipbuilding case in the fetched Industrial policy page), or pure rent capture with accounting changes. From the reading alone the source cannot be named. What is owed is a source-discriminating instrument (a counterfactual with firm-level data), not a confident label.

## 5. Q - Does the shape forbid naming a sector or a metric? ANSWERED

**No more than it forbids naming Gradient in the exemplar.** The README's phrase is *"what a **missing** thing would have to look like (a shape, not a name)."* It governs a primitive not held in the frame. In this question the figure vocabulary already holds comparison, subtraction, negation, contact, habitat, and ground (Fig 13 substrate, Operators table, Fig 11). What is not held is the *binding* of "productive capacity" to a particular measurable (TFP, export survival, patents). That binding is an import from the economics frame, licensed only by provenance and a shadow test, or by the ground settling it. Until then the slot stays marked `???`.

For the record, the exemplar's `05` §5b made the same correction about naming Gradient/Spectrum: they were already among the six, so naming one was a binding, not the invention of a missing primitive. This solution applies that correction to the economics case.

## 6. Two consequences for this solution

**Prestige is not action.** From *The Recursive Self-Improvement Trap* (read directly): *"Prestige can fund an action. It can give an action a budget to be tried. But it cannot settle an action. Only the ground can settle it. The Ground Maintainer must be blind to prestige."* A list of famous economists who agree with the shape claim is the named collapse, not corroboration. The legitimate ask is a budget to try the claim against a ground.

**This audit is a simulation, not a ground reading.** Same essay: *"the output is only as good as the validator. The agent, or the tool, or the frame, or the theory, cannot validate itself."* And: *"the truly testable output would need to be derived from the real architecture or working the formula yourself."* So `01` is a simulated derivation checked for internal consistency against the figures. It is **not** a test of the claim and must be labelled as such. It cannot settle anything it reports on.

## 7. Contact and drift, from *Decoding Tacit Knowledge*

The essay makes the outreach rule explicit: *"Two people who only check against each other drift, and they drift together, so neither notices."* Three things stop it: a ground both are corrected by separately (*"Frames range against the ground. They do not negotiate with it"*, Fig 2 :45); separate experience; and claims in effect terms. Long, close collaboration *"uses up that extra reach"* because two frames that overlap almost completely are *"one frame wearing two names"*. That is the figure-grounded reason the first contact is a single checkable output, not a conversation about the framework.
