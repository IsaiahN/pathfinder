# The Tether's answer: when industrial policy builds capacity

**Mode: `specified` - not `grounded`.** This is a shape and a route, not a result. Simulated derivation checked against the figures (`05_what_shape_means.md` §6), not a ground reading.

## 1. The residual it answers

A state targets a sector. Sometimes the sector becomes more productive in ways that survive withdrawal of support; sometimes the support becomes a permanent transfer to connected insiders. The question asks what distinguishes the regimes. Fig 9 :54 *"When the split lands on a union rather than a partition, unbundle before you search again."* The split is a union of a described residual and a discipline channel, not one scale of policy quality.

## 2. The shape

1. **Describe the failure before picking the instrument.** Fig 9 :28 *"describe it before you go looking, or any frame you pick will seem to fit"*. Naming a strategic sector first makes any intervention look right afterwards. What is owed is the specific coordination problem or learning spillover, plus an instrument already returning a weak signal (Fig 6 :32 *"An instrument is not built from a description. It is improved from a worse instrument already returning something."*).

2. **Support must change the firm's contact, not just its balance sheet.** Fig 11 :39 *"capability is a property of agent-and-habitat, never of the agent alone."* Fig 11 :40 *"An improvement that does not change contact changes nothing, however much it improves."* A subsidy that does not expose the firm to a competitive test leaves the habitat substituted; a protected habitat proves wiring and never capability (Fig 11 :70).

3. **The metric must be an anchor, not a frame.** Fig 2 :29 *"An anchor is whatever settles the question and does not change its answer because of what any frame thinks."* If the allocation metric moves when the allocator is lobbied, the ground has become another frame and the policy converges on whoever had the most influence (Fig 2 :14). Correlation with connection is disqualifying for a verdict about capacity (Fig 2 :40).

4. **The program must be able to refuse.** Operators :74 *"A vocabulary with − and without ¬ can say what is missing from an arrangement and cannot say that an arrangement is wrong, so it can only ever accumulate."* Fig 5 :59 *"a term whose residual PERSISTS AND DOES NOT TREND DOWN is the one to point the refusal at"*. A program that cannot withdraw from a persistent non-performer accumulates rents.

5. **The difference is the bond, not the amount.** Fig 12 :95 *"Two entries with an identical ingredient set are not variants of one thing. They are different things whose notation cannot tell them apart, and the difference is carried entirely by the bond."* Conditional and unconditional support with identical budgets are different substances. Fig 12 :94 *"The arrangement is the substance."*

6. **Evaluation must be independent and low-overlap.** Fig 6 :53 *"searchers drawing on the same evidence are one searcher wearing many names."* Fig 8 :45 *"Two frames together reach what neither reaches alone."* A ministry grading its own supported firms using the firms' data is one frame with three labels.

7. **Measure per slot, not in aggregate.** Fig 1 :19 *"The residual is indexed by object slot, not aggregated."* Fig 1 :22 *"Aggregating across slots is how a live signal disappears into an average."* Section totals are quantities the policy also produces and cannot score it (Fig 1 :27).

## 3. What it explicitly does not say

- Not that industrial policy always works, or always fails. The two outcomes are a union compound with its own loop.
- Not the winning sector, not the correct metric, and not the subsidy amount. Fig 9 :32 *"you get a kind of thing, never the answer itself"*; the framework returns a shape, not a name.
- Not that a protected market can never produce output. It can produce output for the protected market; that is a test harness, not world-market capability.
- Not a derivation of economics from the six-term substrate. The binding of "capacity" to TFP or export survival is an import from economics, provenanced as such, and still unproven.
- Not `grounded`. No counterfactual measurement was taken.

## 4. What would settle it - the ground

A source-discriminating instrument, not a further argument. *The Hard Problem is About Source*: an instrument that reads green *"cannot comment on the source"*; the spectrometer resolved it. Here:

- **Counterfactual test:** matched firms or a natural experiment; does measured productivity survive withdrawal of support, and does it hold when selection into support is controlled? This is the instrument that would discriminate genuine capacity from a subsidy-driven expansion of low-productivity entrants.
- **Anchor-invariance test:** does the allocation metric move with political contributions, lobby contacts, or connected-board membership? If yes, Fig 2 :34 applies and the metric is not an anchor.
- **Withdrawal test:** do programs with a pre-registered metric and a real sunset/refusal clause outperform those without, after selection is controlled?
- Standing falsifiers from `01` §4: any of the three that break the shape claim.

## 5. The honest stop

**Unreached, not unreachable.** The figures govern the role of the anchor and the shape of the bond, not the identity of the measure. The README's phrase is about *"a missing thing"*: what a missing thing would look like, not which thing it is. Naming export survival or TFP is a binding from the economics frame. The answer stops at the limit of the available instruments and records the lineage so the stop is checkable. *"Indiscernibility is not identity."*

## 6. One-paragraph version for outreach

> The Tether does not say that industrial policy works or that it fails. It says that the question is a union of two conditions: a described market-failure residual and a discipline channel that keeps support aimed at it. Support builds capacity when it changes the firm's contact with a competitive ground, when the metric used to allocate it cannot be moved by the people allocating it, when the program can refuse a persistent non-performer, and when evaluation draws on independent, low-overlap evidence. Support protects connected firms when those conditions are absent and selection runs by influence, because then the measurement has stopped being an anchor and the policy converges on whoever had the most influence. The same subsidy instrument under those two different bonds is a different substance; the arrangement is the substance. The framework names neither the winning sector nor the correct metric. It offers the shape any answer must have, three refuters written in advance, and the instruments that would close it. The slot stays open until a ground settles it.
