## Restating the question as an effect

The question decides which of two outcomes a targeted state intervention leads to. Option A: the supported sector becomes more productive in ways that survive the withdrawal of support (productive capacity). Option B: the support becomes a durable transfer to connected insiders with no such surviving gain (protection of politically connected firms). What an answer would look like: a set of conditions that are present in A and absent in B, stated as a shape that can be checked before the outcome, plus a refuter that would break the shape. Bound as an ordinary term per formula step "Direction": `CAPACITY := (described residual) AND (support changes contact) AND (grounded metric with refusal) AND (low-overlap evaluation)`.

## What is deliberately not here

- No personal emails of named individuals.
- No ranking of people by how likely they are to agree with the claim.
- No bulk-send plan.

Three reasons, in the figures' own words:

1. **A room does not upgrade the label.** Fig 7 :35 *"A room's agreement is variation, not a verdict. Selection between rooms is where the ground votes."* A list of receptive readers is a room, not an anchor.
2. **Correlation disqualifies a verdict.** Fig 2 :40 *"agreement about the world is worthless when the agreeing frames share an evidence pool"* ; Fig 2 :42 *"Buy independence in proportion to the cost of being wrong."* Selecting recipients for likely agreement manufactures exactly the correlation that makes their agreement worthless.
3. **Asking for trust before checking spends independence.** Fig 10 :42 *"A verifier that can reconstruct the claim cannot verify it."* The first contact carries the residual and its refuter, never the framework's interior.

Legally: scraping personal emails for unsolicited bulk outreach breaches GDPR and CAN-SPAM style rules in many jurisdictions. Public institutional and opt-in channels avoid that failure as well as the structural one.

## Honest standing of this package

This is a simulated derivation checked for internal consistency against the figures, not a ground reading. It cannot settle the question it reports on.
