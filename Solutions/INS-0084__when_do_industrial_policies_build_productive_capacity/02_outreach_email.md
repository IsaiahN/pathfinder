# Outreach draft - check the output before deciding about the theory

**Use:** one-to-one, or as a cover letter attached to a submission through the public venues in `03_public_contact_points.csv`. **Not** for bulk send.

**Hard precondition:** do not send until the three sub-claims in `08_expert_verification_packet.md` survive a first reader's check. The output must survive validation or the two-stage offer collapses on contact.

---

**Subject:** One checkable claim about when industrial policy works

> Hi [name],
>
> I am writing with one specific output to check against what you already know. I am not asking you to read a framework first, and I am not asking you to agree with anything. If the output fails, there is nothing else to look at.
>
> **The output (`specified`, not `grounded`).** Two things decide whether targeted state support builds productive capacity or protects connected firms: (1) the market-failure residual is described before an instrument is picked, and (2) support is bonded to a measurable ground with a real withdrawal clause and is graded by evaluators who do not share the ministry's evidence pool. Same subsidy instrument under those two different bonds is a different substance. Where the metric is authored by the allocators and moves when they are lobbied, the measurement has stopped being an anchor; the policy converges on whoever had the most influence. Where support never changes the firm's contact with a competitive market, it substitutes a protected habitat, and a protected habitat proves that the wiring works, not that the firm is competitive.
>
> **What I am not claiming.** That the framework names the winning sector or the right metric. It returns a shape, not a name. The choice between total factor productivity, export survival, and patent quality is a binding from your field, and I would ask you to supply it, not me. I also make no claim that industrial policy always works or always fails. The question is a union of two conditions, not one scale.
>
> **What would refute the shape claim (written before this message):**
> 1. A credible counterfactual showing no productivity or export gain that survives withdrawal of support, after selection into support is controlled.
> 2. A regime with no progress metric and no withdrawal clause performing indistinguishably from one with both.
> 3. The allocation metric shown to move systematically with political contributions or connections, with outcomes tracking that movement rather than the stated target.
>
> If any one holds, I withdraw the claim. A one-line "this fails because..." is more useful to me than agreement. If you would rather not engage, no reply is needed.
>
> **If it survives, and only then:** the sources are below. The figures are the authority, not my summary of them.
> - Verbatim figure text with line numbers (local extract): `FRAMEWORK/reference/03-FIGURES_TEXT.md`
> - Canonical figures README: https://github.com/IsaiahN/tether/blob/general-agent/figures/README.md
> - Figures and formula for direct download (branch `general-agent`): https://github.com/IsaiahN/tether/tree/general-agent/figures
>
> **If you want to reuse the machinery:** the formula is in the figures link. Run it on a problem you know well and can check against a ground. Write down what would refute your result *before* you run it, and state the mode of what you get. A reported failure is more useful to me than a reported success.
>
> - [your name]
