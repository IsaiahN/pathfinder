## Distribution rules: offer, do not push

Figure-grounded reasons:

- **Fig 4 :22** *"Only methods cross up. Only head starts come down."* Sending the full framework before a reader has described a residual sends a recording, not a generator. Lead with the shape claim and its refuter; offer the links after.
- **Fig 10 :42** *"A verifier that can reconstruct the claim cannot verify it."* A recipient who has absorbed the framework before giving a verdict has joined the frame being checked.
- **Fig 9 :28** *"describe it before you go looking, or any frame you pick will seem to fit"*. Ask what they would expect to see if the shape claim were true before showing the article.
- **Fig 2 :40** *"agreement about the world is worthless when the agreeing frames share an evidence pool"* and **Fig 2 :42** *"Buy independence in proportion to the cost of being wrong."* A list selected for receptiveness manufactures the disqualifying correlation. Use public, opt-in venues, never a purchased or scraped list.
- **Fig 9 :60** *"Use filters for the budget and witnesses for the verdict. Never let a filter hand you a verdict."* Venue tier is a budget filter; it says nothing about whether the claim is true.

**Recommended order in a reply:** (1) the shape claim and its three refuters; (2) ask what they would count as a failure and what ground they would use; (3) record that verdict before showing any theory; (4) only if curious, the three framework links, with the note that the figures, not the summary, are the authority.

## The two-stage offer (and the optional third stage)

1. **Stage 1:** validate the output against the ground. This is Fig 10 :52 *"Bring a characterised residual, never the source"* and Fig 2 :45 *"Frames range against the ground. They do not negotiate with it."*
2. **Stage 2, opt-in only:** if curious, read the framework that produced the output. Their stage-1 verdict must be recorded *before* this; later agreement is never pooled as corroboration.
3. **Stage 3, method only:** offer the formula as a generator. A generator crossing is permitted (Fig 4 :22); each user writes their own refuter and states their own mode.

**Hard precondition:** stages 1 to 3 work only if the stage-1 output survives. That is what `08_expert_verification_packet.md` is for. Ask for a failure, not an endorsement.
