I redid the census from the residual line alone, before relying on the primary's findings, using the WATSON order: R by arity, symmetry and scale; one line per domain with actors, currency and anchor; partition or union; order by dependency.

Arity: two conditions, therefore a **union compound**, not one hard question (Fig 9 :54, :58). Symmetry: not symmetric; the two regimes differ by the bond between support and ground, not by an amount (Fig 12 :94). Scale: first order at the firm slot, second order at the evaluating institution, with a lossy round trip between measurement and policy.
- **Domain 1, firm and habitat.** Actors: supported firm, competitors, suppliers, buyers. Currency: productivity, exports, survival without support. Anchor: the market test after support ends. Nameable, so a domain.
- **Domain 2, implementing state.** Actors: ministry, monitoring agency, politicians, firms. Currency: conditions, monitoring, withdrawal. Anchor: an external metric that does not move when the allocator is lobbied. Nameable, so a domain.
- **Domain 3, political distribution.** Actors: connected firms, interest groups. Currency: influence and rents. Anchor for this question: none. Its would-be ground updates on the frames, so this is the failure mode, not an independent domain. The absence of a legitimate anchor is the finding, not a gap in the audit.
- **Domain 4, evaluation.** Actors: auditors, competitors, data providers, researchers. Currency: residuals versus agreement. Anchor: an independent measurement with low overlap to Domain 2. Nameable, so a domain.
- **Partition or union.** Union of Domains 1 and 2 for capacity; Domain 3 removes the anchor; Domain 4 checks. Two loops, two verdicts.
- **Order by dependency.** Describe the residual before choosing an instrument (Fig 9 :28); state a checkable metric before planning (Fig 3 :27); refuse the persistent non-performer before minting new support (formula step 2); settle each first-order firm-level claim before the second-order institutional claim (Fig 12 :104 *"A settled molecule becomes an atom for whatever composes over it. ## Round trip gap R_T

The Symbols table defines R_T as the gap between what was sent up and what came back down, `gap(x, (T_E ∘ T_A)(x))`, measuring what the coarser description cannot hold. Fig 4 :30 requires it to be measured rather than assumed. I ran the round trip on the condensation the folder actually uses.

1. **Sent up (x):** the verbatim figure set, chiefly Fig 9 :54 and :58 (union compound), Fig 11 :39, :40 and :70 (contact and substituted habitat), Fig 2 :29 and :34 (anchor must not update), Operators :74 and Fig 5 :59 (refusal), Fig 6 :53 and Fig 8 :45 (low overlap).
2. **Abstraction (T_A):** the shorthand carried in `07` and `09`, "describe the failure, bond support to a metric you can withdraw on, and evaluate independently".
3. **Concretisation (T_E):** a concrete policy brief, "condition subsidies on export targets with sunset clauses and hire an external auditor".

**R_T is nonzero.** Four things the shorthand holds do not survive the trip back down:

- the **per-slot residual** (Fig 1 :19): the concrete brief can report sector totals and still hide a failing supported firm;
- the **invariance requirement on the metric** (Fig 2 :29, :34): an export target can itself be lobbied over, gamed by under-invoicing, or renegotiated, so naming "export targets" does not guarantee an anchor;
- the **actual use of ¬**: a sunset clause on paper is not a withdrawal in practice; the program must demonstrate that it refuses a persistent non-performer (Fig 5 :59);
- the **independence of the evaluator**: an "external" auditor paid and supplied data by the ministry shares its evidence pool (Fig 6 :53).

Conversely, one thing survives cleanly: support must change the firm's contact with a competitive ground, and the two regimes are a union compound under different bonds. That is the residue worth sending. R_T was **not** measured numerically, because no ground reading exists; it is recorded qualitatively as the enumerated set of dropped constraints. Treating "the formula is the condensed version of the metalaws" as identity would set R_T to zero and would be the dead-reckoning error Fig 4 :30 names. The honest form is a lossy condensation with a measured gap, which is what `09_watson_answer.md` states.
