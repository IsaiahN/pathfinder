## QUESTION

Decide, in effect terms, what the Tether says about choosing conservation strategies that protect ecological relationships as well as individual species. Option A: rank named strategies by a single biodiversity score that weights species and interactions together. Option B: refuse the single score as a flat formula, enumerate the habitat by contact, score species and relationships as separate slots, and return the shape any best strategy must have while leaving the name open. What each leads to: A averages away the relationship signal, assumes a partition where there is a union, and lets cost act as a verdict; B preserves the per-slot residual, scores the intersection as a compound, and stops at the limit of the available instruments.

## DOMAIN CENSUS

R by arity, symmetry and scale, then one line per domain with actors / currency / anchor, partition or union, and order by dependency.

- **R (the residual).** The gap between what a strategy predicts for each slot and what the field returns. Arity: two kinds of slot, species atoms and relationship bonds. Symmetry: bonds are typed and directional in effect; valence is relative to the residual, so a predator is a benefactor to one residual and a malfactor to another. Scale: first order (direct contact), second and third order (cascades such as DDT to eagles), and across scales from site to landscape. It is a **union compound**, not one hard question: species persistence and relationship persistence overlap, and a single lever clears only the region where one condition holds.
- **Domain 1, the habitat and field.** Actors: species, abiotic conditions, interactions. Currency: contact and cascade. Anchor: field measurement of persistence, a nameable anchor, so this is a domain. Governed by Fig 11 and Fig 1.
- **Domain 2, the management seat.** Actors: agency, monitoring instruments, introduced actors. Currency: asymmetric power, introduction available and exclusion not. Anchor: the stated goal, persistence of the residual. Governed by Fig 11 and Fig 3.
- **Domain 3, the verification and evidence frame.** Actors: researchers, reviewers, monitoring instruments. Currency: agreement and residual. Anchor: the ground, never agreement. In this simulated run all three domains draw on one evidence pool for the figure reading, so this domain's independence is zero for the internal check. Governed by Fig 2, Fig 4 and Fig 10.
- **Partition or union.** Union. A species-only strategy and a relationship-only strategy each clear their own region; the intersection is a specific compound with its own loop.
- **Order by dependency.** Perceive actors and relations, acquire terms for what a situation affords, state the objective, then plan, then learn and carry (Fig 3). Describe the residual before choosing a strategy (Fig 9 :27-28). Refuse before mint (Formula step 2).

Strain test applied: Fig 11 :61 says introduction is available; Fig 11 :65 calls cane toads and kudzu the same move with the cascade unenumerated. These do not strain, because one states a power and the other states its unenumerated use. Re-census resolves it: enumerate first, then introduce. No hidden strain remains.

## VERDICT (from the figures)

The shape survives; the name does not. A best conservation strategy is one that minimises per-slot residual over an enumerated habitat, treats species as atoms and relationships as typed bonds, preserves in-situ contact, budgets permanent maintenance, scores the species and relationship intersection as its own compound, uses cost as a budget filter, and settles at a measured ground with independent frames. Two strategies with the same species set and different interaction wiring are a real isomer claim with a stated falsifier. A single biodiversity score is a flat formula that has dropped the arrangement. Isolation is substitution, with two silent failure modes. No figure line names a winning strategy, and the slot for that name stays open until a ground reading or a provenanced import fills it. This is a `general` and `specified`, ungrounded result.

- **cost if wrong:** bounded and locatable. The packet is mode-labelled, states refuters before the conclusion and records the lineage, so an error is findable rather than invisible. The real cost is spending the independence of outside readers by sending a conclusion instead of a generator, which Fig 2 :42 prices as buying independence in proportion to the cost of being wrong. That cost is avoidable by leading with the shape claim and its refuter.
- **undoable:** yes. A published ranking can be superseded by a corrected shape and a refuter without withdrawing any primitive. No actor or habitat has been removed; the framework's own rule is that exclusion is not available, so corrections are additions to the record. The substitution failure modes are declared so they can be found later.
- **reversed by:** a ground reading, not agreement. A source-discriminating interaction instrument that resolves the relationship slots; a field measurement showing a named strategy dominating on both slots across habitats with low-overlap replication; a demonstration that a species list plus abundances fully predicts interaction persistence; a demonstration that a substituted habitat maintains wild capability and its interaction set; or a figure re-census that admits a single averaged metric without losing a live slot signal. Until one of these fires, the verdict stands as candidate.
