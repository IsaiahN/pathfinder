## 1. A shape is a constraint you can state before you can name the winner

The canonical README gives the limit: the framework returns "what a missing thing would have to look like (a shape, not a name)." Fig 9 :32 "you get a kind of thing, never the answer itself". From *The Library of Babel*: "You can cut your search short if the set of books returned is small and descriptive enough that it provides a general shape of the answer that satisfies your question."

For ECO-0081 this means the deliverable is the form of a best strategy, not a name such as "protected areas" or "ecosystem-based management". A shape here is:

> a rule that scores each strategy by its per-slot residual over an enumerated habitat, counts relationships as bonds alongside species as atoms, preserves in-situ contact, budgets permanent maintenance, scores the species and relationship intersection as a compound, and settles at a measured ground.

That is a shape. "Designate 30 percent of the landscape as strictly protected area" is a name. The one constrains; the other fills.

## 2. How the shape is obtained

- *The Library of Babel*: use the vocabulary you already have that is closest to the thing, then "With each new term gained, you are able to narrow the space by definition and deepen your vocabulary." For this question the bootstrap vocabulary is atom, bond, slot, contact, cascade, anchor.
- Fig 6 :30 "The description travels. Fails by being sharpened into terms only we can read. Cast it in effect terms." Fig 9 :30-31 "your words are borrowed too, so describe the effect rather than the cause". So describe the residual as "the target population and its interactions fail to persist under this strategy", not as "the strategy lacks a systems approach".
- *Decoding Tacit Knowledge*: "When contact isn't possible, the best you can send is a plain description of what you'd expect to see, because anyone can check that against the world." That is how the outreach email in `02` is written.
- *The Library of Babel* on stopping: "You will know that you are done because the finish line will also be the starting place." A shape is complete when no further term narrows it.

## 3. The bundle warning is the governing essay point for this question

The question bundles two goals. *The Library of Babel* names the failure directly: "Perhaps your problem was a bundle or conflation of several problems masquerading as one? You will need to search all of those problems individually to find their answers and then combine them in a satisfactory way." Fig 9 :54 "And when the split lands on a union rather than a partition, unbundle before you search again." So "protect species and relationships" is not one hard ranking problem. It is a union of overlapping conditions with its own intersection, and the intersection must be scored separately.

## 4. What licenses a name

Only two things, both outside the shape:

1. **The ground settles it.** Formula step 5: "the ground settles it. Verified only by what pays. A gate is not the ground." The canonical README: the framework "stops at the limit of what the available instruments can distinguish, and records the lineage so the stop is honest."
2. **A provenanced import from a frame whose closure differs**, with a source gate and a shadow test (Fig 6 :23, Fig 8 :50-52). A world result such as the 20-lake habitat experiment would be such an import only once its provenance and its shadow (which gap it explains) are recorded.

*They're Scanning Books When They Should Be Studying Nature*: "The real import is not a new term in the existing space. It is a new dimension of the space." And: "A minimum viable set of six terms is a human's careful guess at reality's compression axes; another sensorium's minimum set need not be those six." Declaring the six makes the choice visible, not neutral. Likewise, declaring that species and interactions are the two slots is a visible choice, and must be recorded as such.

## 5. Why a species-only reading is the specific forbidden error

*The Hard Problem is About Source* gives the mechanism. An instrument reads green and "cannot comment on the source". The reading does not determine the source. "If several paths create the same behavior, then behavior is a projection with lossy information". And: "An instrument that can't distinguish these cases demonstrates its own inadequacy, it cannot claim anything substantial about what is being measured or what causes it." It was the spectrometer, not an argument, that resolved it.

A conservation index that reads species persistence cannot comment on whether the relationships that generated it are intact. Several very different interaction structures could give the same species count. The count is a projection with lossy information. What is owed is a better instrument (interaction monitoring), not a claim that the count settles the question. This grounds F1 and F10 in `01`.

*How Intelligence Actually Works* restates it as provenance loss: "often, very little about a composed term tells you, from looking at it alone, which route produced it or whether that route was available." A recovered species list does not record the interaction routes that produced it.

## 6. Correction to my own §4

I initially wrote that the shape-not-a-name rule forbids naming any strategy at all. That overstates it. The README's phrase governs "a **missing** thing", one not held in the library. Here the missing thing is a ranking validated at the ground, not a new primitive. The honest form is therefore: no figure line names a winning strategy, so the figures cannot supply the name; a name would have to come from a provenanced import or a ground measurement. It is a reach limit under the current instruments, not a categorical ban on ever naming a strategy. This correction is kept visible rather than silently applied.

## 7. Two consequences for outreach and for this solution's status

**Prestige is not action.** From *The Recursive Self-Improvement Trap*: "Prestige must never be allowed to validate an action directly... Prestige can fund an action. It can give an action a budget to be tried. But it cannot settle an action. Only the ground can settle it. The Ground Maintainer must be blind to prestige." A campaign that gathers well known names to agree with a strategy ranking is the named collapse, not corroboration. The legitimate ask gives the claim a budget to be tried and lets the ground, a field measurement, settle it.

**My own audit is a simulation, not a ground reading.** Same essay: "the output is only as good as the validator. The agent, or the tool, or the frame, or the theory, cannot validate itself." And: "LLMs can quickly simulate an approximate answer/output... However, the truly testable output would need to be derived from the real architecture or working the formula yourself." The framework's own demo note calls such outputs "simulacra" and says they "could never be verified if the solutions presented were not contrived to fit the formula." So `01` is a simulated derivation checked for internal consistency against the figures. It is not a test of the conservation claim and must be labelled as such.

## 8. The society-of-agents point

*How a Society of Agents Would Work*: "A group is only worth having while its members stay different." Ten copies watching the same thing are one opinion said ten times. Fig 2 :40 "agreement about the world is worthless when the agreeing frames share an evidence pool". So the expert check on this shape must draw on independent ground contact (field data, experiments, different taxa and habitats), not on agreement among reviewers who read the same sources. The world sources in `10_source_ledger.csv` are declared with overlap for exactly this reason.
