## Distribution: offer, do not push

Figure-grounded rules:

- **Fig 10 :52** "Bring a characterised residual, never the source." Send the residual and its refuter before the theory. A recipient who has absorbed the whole framework before being asked for a verdict has joined the frame being checked.
- **Fig 2 :45** "Frames range against the ground. They do not negotiate with it." Ask what they would expect to see if the shape claim were true, before giving them the sources.
- **Fig 9 :27-28** "describe it before you go looking, or any frame you pick will seem to fit." The residual first, the venue second.
- **Fig 4 :22** "Only methods cross up. Only head starts come down." What crosses between sites is the monitoring method, not a recording of one successful site.
- **Fig 7 :13** "same closure, handed along; agreement compounds, evidence does not". A bulk send selected for likely agreement produces a convention, not corroboration.

**Recommended order in a reply:** (1) the shape claim and its five refuters; (2) ask what they would count as a failure; (3) record their verdict; (4) only then the links, with the note that the figures, not the summary, are the authority.

## The two-stage offer

1. **Stage 1 before stage 2** is Fig 10 :52 with Fig 2 :45. The recipient checks the output without holding the framework's interior.
2. **Stage 2 is opt-in by construction.** Once a recipient has absorbed the theory, their agreement stops being evidence (Fig 2 :40 "agreement about the world is worthless when the agreeing frames share an evidence pool"). Their stage-1 verdict must be recorded before any theory is shown.
3. **Stage 3 sends a method, which is permitted.** Fig 4 :22. The formula is a generator, so giving it away does not give away an answer. Each user still needs a ground per problem and must write a refuter before running it.
4. **Declare stakes.** Fig 10 :59 "an interest in the outcome is unavoidable, an interest in the reading is disqualifying". A reviewer who works for an organisation tied to a strategy declares that interest.

## Hard precondition

Stages 1 to 3 only work if the stage-1 output survives. That is why `08_expert_verification_packet.md` puts mode, three checkable sub-claims, verbatim lines and refuters before the conclusion. Send the generator, not the conclusion.
