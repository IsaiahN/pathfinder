## QUESTION

Decide, in effect terms, what the Tether says about the claim that output can rise while resource use and environmental damage fall. Option A: assert that separation is achievable and being achieved, so the answer is a practical yes. Option B: treat the question as a union compound and return the shape an admissible answer must have, leaving the empirical rate to measurement. What each leads to: A licenses efficiency and innovation alone as a sufficient policy response, and can score the frame with its own ratio; B requires an absolute, boundary-complete, slot-resolved, two-condition reading at the ground, and records the empirical question as a `gap` at this budget.

## VERDICT (from the figures)

The figures do not return a bare yes or no. They return the shape an admissible answer must have, kept separate per domain and never averaged. Separation must be absolute rather than a falling intensity (D1), measured at the ground rather than scored with the frame's own metric (D2), boundary-complete and slot-resolved across both conditions (D3), and paired with demand measures as maintained work (D4). Whether any economy has achieved this at global scale is a `gap` at this budget, owed to instruments, not to further derivation. Mode `specified`, ungrounded, candidate. World sources corroborate the shape from genuinely different evidence pools, but they do not decide it.

- **cost if wrong:** bounded and locatable. If the shape is wrong, a checker will name which of the four domains fails and the pre-registered refuter tells us which claim falls. The larger cost is spending an outside reader's independence by sending a finished theory before the residual, which Fig 2 :42 prices as "Buy independence in proportion to the cost of being wrong." That cost is avoidable by leading with the shape and its refuter.
- **undoable:** yes. A refutation adds a term rather than deleting one; reach falls because something was added, not because anything was struck out. No substrate term is withdrawn if the decoupling claim fails. The lineage is recorded so a later reader can locate the error.
- **reversed by:** a ground reading, not agreement. A sustained, multi-decade, global, absolute fall in throughput and damage across consumption-based and cascaded boundaries would move the empirical `gap` toward `grounded`; a demonstration that an intensity gain with unchanged absolute flow is accepted at the ground as separation would break D1; a frame-independent physical quantity that is also the frame's own optimisation target would break D2; a complete production-based account containing all third-order effects would break D3; a policy that subtracts the habitat rather than introducing actors would break D4. Mere agreement among agreeable readers reverses nothing.
