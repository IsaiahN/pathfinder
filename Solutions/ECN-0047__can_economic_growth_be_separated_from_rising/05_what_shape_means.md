## 1. A shape is a constraint, not a filler

The canonical usage rule (`FRAMEWORK/reference/FIGURES_README_canonical.md`) states the limit: the framework returns *"what a missing thing would have to look like (a shape, not a name)"*. Fig 9 :32: *"you get a kind of thing, never the answer itself"*. Fig 13 :134: *"Unreachable is a verdict here, not a silence,"*.

From *The Library of Babel*: "You can cut your search short if the set of books returned is small and descriptive enough that it provides a general shape of the answer that satisfies your question."

For ECN-0047 this means the framework can return the constraints an admissible answer must satisfy: absolute, boundary-complete, slot-resolved, two-condition, grounded. It cannot return a global decoupling rate or a date by which separation occurred. "Separation is possible" is a name for an outcome. "Separation would have to register as an absolute decline at the ground, with both conditions kept separate and all cascades enumerated" is a shape.

## 2. How the shape is obtained

- *Library of Babel*: use the vocabulary already held that is closest to the target and recurse on the new terms that overlap it. "With each new term gained, you narrow the space and deepen your vocabulary." Here the starting vocabulary is intensity, throughput, boundary, cascade, slot and anchor.
- Fig 9 :30-31: describe the effect rather than the cause, because the words are borrowed. Applied here: describe "a fall in physical contact" before naming a policy or a technology that might produce it.
- *The Hard Problem is About Source*: "If several paths create the same behavior, then behavior is a projection with lossy information (it cannot define consciousness in full)." An intensity ratio is a projection that cannot comment on its source: the same ratio can come from lower physical throughput or from higher throughput with even higher output. Naming the source from the reading is the metamerism error.
- *Library of Babel* on stopping: "the finish line will also be the starting place". For this question, the search stops when the residual stops registering at the ground, not when a satisfying narrative is found.

## 3. Why this question is a bundle

*Library of Babel* names the failure directly: "Perhaps your problem was a bundle or conflation of several problems masquerading as one?" The right move is to search the component problems individually and then combine them. Fig 9 :54-57 gives the figure version. This is why `01_figure_grounded_audit.md` F1 leads: resource use and environmental damage are two conditions, and production versus consumption boundary is a second, orthogonal split.

## 4. What licenses a name

Two things, both outside the shape itself:

1. **The ground settles it.** THE_FORMULA step 5: *"the ground settles it. Verified only by what pays. A gate is not the ground."* A measured, sustained absolute decline would name the outcome.
2. **A provenanced import from a frame whose closure differs** (Fig 6 :23), with a source gate and a shadow test. A world result such as IPCC B.5 is such an import, and its provenance is recorded in `10_source_ledger.csv`.

*The Hard Problem is About Source* gives the instrument rule: "The answer is unreached until a better instrument exists." The corresponding instrument here is a boundary-complete physical account, not a further argument about whether growth is good or bad.

## 5. Prestige is not action

*The Recursive Self-Improvement Trap*: "Prestige must never be allowed to validate an action directly... Someone let prestige stand in for proof. Someone let reputation substitute for result. Someone let the trusted name validate the untested claim." And: "Prestige can fund an action. It can give an action a budget to be tried. But it cannot settle an action. Only the ground can settle it."

A campaign that gathers well known economists to agree that green growth is possible is the named collapse, not corroboration. The legitimate ask gives the shape a budget to be tried and lets a physical measurement settle it. This is why `03_public_contact_points.csv` contains no personal emails and no receptiveness ranking.

## 6. My own audit is a simulation, not a ground reading

Same essay: "LLMs can quickly simulate an approximate answer/output with the formula found in the architecture post" and "the truly testable output would need to be derived from the real architecture or working the formula yourself." So `01_figure_grounded_audit.md` is a simulated derivation checked for internal consistency against the figures. It is **not** a test of whether decoupling has happened, and it cannot settle anything it reports on. Every substantive claim in this folder is labelled `general`, `specified` or (where a measurement is reported by a world source) attributed to that source. Nothing here is `grounded` by this derivation.
