## QUESTION

Does psychological safety increase learning and innovation, and when might it make teams less willing to challenge poor performance? Restated as an effect: why does a reduction in the felt interpersonal cost of speaking up sometimes shrink the gap between what a team predicts and what its ground returns, and sometimes grow that gap by removing the challenge that would have corrected a poor decision? `R = |Γ(b,a) - o′|` is indexed per team and per member, never averaged (Fig 1 :19). Option A: treat safety as a main effect that always improves learning. Option B: keep the conditional shape and state the two failure modes. A sends a `general`-mode verdict with a survey as its own anchor and spends the independence of the people checking it. B sends a checkable shape, written refuters and an open instrument until a ground settles it.

## VERDICT (from the figures)

**The figure-derived verdict:** psychological safety increases learning and innovation when C1 (a maintained channel to a paying ground) and C2 (renewed reach through contact with a differing closure) both hold, and it can make a team less willing to challenge poor performance when safety becomes an internal agreement room that hands itself the same closure, or when the valence of voice flips with the residual and the task phase. The verdict is the union kept separate per domain, never averaged. Mode `specified`. Pool A corroborates the two-condition shape only after re-mapping its learning variable to contact; the apparent conflict was returned to the census and did not flip the verdict. Low-overlap fields corroborate the mechanism, not the team-level fact. Which number or intervention occupies the slots is a declared `gap`, not a hidden answer.

- **cost if wrong:** misdirected diagnosis. A team failing C2 would be sold survey instruments, or a team failing C1 would be told to import more, and one lever would be applied to a union that needs two. The larger cost is spending an outside frame's independence by sending a conclusion instead of a residual, which Fig 2 :42 prices: *"Buy independence in proportion to the cost of being wrong."* That cost is avoidable by leading with the shape claim and its refuter.
- **undoable:** yes. No term is deleted if the claim fails; a refutation is added and the reach falls because *"something was added rather than because anything was deleted"* (Fig 6 :42-43). The shape and its falsifiers are recorded in a provenance trail so a later reader can locate the error (Fig 10 :62).
- **reversed by:** any of the five pre-registered falsifiers, in particular (1) individual safety changing reporting while contact measures as unchanged, (2) safety rising while fidelity and pressure both measure high and error correction declines, (3) high agreement with continuing ground verified readings and no import, (4) new atoms with no differing closure, or (5) invariant voice valence across a change of residual and phase. A study with a genuinely different evidence pool showing that per team learning is fully predicted by a safety score alone would also reverse it. Until one of these fires, the verdict stands as `candidate`.
