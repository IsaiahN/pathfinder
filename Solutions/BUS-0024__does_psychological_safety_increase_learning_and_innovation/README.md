## What is deliberately not here

- No personal emails of named individuals.
- No ranking of people by how likely they are to agree with the claim.
- No bulk-send plan.

Three figure-grounded reasons:

1. **Agreement is not a verdict.** Fig 2 :35: *"Alignment is triangulation, not negotiation. A mirror cannot correct you."* Fig 2 :40: *"agreement about the world is worthless when the agreeing frames share an evidence pool."*
2. **A verifier must not hold the interior.** Fig 10 :42: *"A verifier that can reconstruct the claim cannot verify it."* An interest in the outcome is unavoidable, but *"an interest in the reading is disqualifying"* (Fig 10 :59).
3. **Filters are not witnesses.** Fig 9 :60: *"Use filters for the budget and witnesses for the verdict. Never let a filter hand you a verdict."* Receptiveness is a contact estimate, never an evidence score.

Legally: unsolicited bulk email breaches GDPR and CAN-SPAM style rules in many jurisdictions. Public opt-in venues in `03_public_contact_points.csv` avoid that practical failure as well as the structural one.
