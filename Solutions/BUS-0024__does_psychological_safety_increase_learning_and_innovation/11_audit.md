## 2. Independent re-census from the residual line

### 2.1 Arity, symmetry, scale

- **Arity:** a relation among belief b, action a and ground outcome o′, indexed by slot and ordered by time. A two-place reading (safety, learning) would lose the action and the prediction, so it is rejected.
- **Symmetry:** not symmetric. The ground is pinned and does not update on the team's opinion (Fig 2 :25). "Increase" is a threshold comparison (Fig 12 :87), so one operand is a quantity compared against a second, not merely conjoined with it.
- **Scale:** the slot is a member or a team, but the ranking of "learning" and "innovation" exists only against a ground outside the team (Fig 4 :5). Time crosses the membrane between the team's step loop and the ground's selection loop (Fig 4). The residual is intrinsically cross scale.

## 4. Round trip gap `R_T`

Fig 4 :30: *"So take the round trip and measure the gap. Without it you are navigating by dead reckoning: confident about a position nobody checked."*

- **T_A, going up:** carry the primary's two-condition shape to a one-sentence abstraction. Result: *learning and innovation require a team to keep talking to the thing that pays it and to keep bringing in people who work differently.*
- **T_E, coming back:** put that abstraction into a specific team situation. Result: *run frequent retrospectives and invite outside speakers.*

**`R_T = gap(x, (T_E ∘ T_A)(x))`.** The gap is what the abstraction cannot hold:

1. The one-sentence form collapses the union into one lever. It loses Fig 9 :57, *"a case in two conditions is not a harder case of either."* Coming back down, it tells a team failing C2 merely to listen harder, which is effort above the break (Fig 3 :29-30).
2. It loses the anchor rule. "The thing that pays it" does not specify that error correction and adopted innovation, not a safety survey and not internal agreement, settle the question (Fig 2 :29, :35). Coming back down, it permits a team to score itself with a quantity it produces (Fig 1 :27).
3. It loses the idempotence rule. "Invite outside speakers" sounds like an import, but a speaker whose closure matches the team's adds no atom (Fig 6 :21, Fig 8 :32). The abstraction cannot tell a differing closure from a familiar one.
4. It loses the valence flip. "Keep talking" has no place for the possibility that the same voice changes sign when the work moves from deliberation to execution (Fig 11 :49-51, Fig 12 :119).
5. It loses the source discriminator. The abstraction cannot say what instrument would distinguish a reporting channel from an agreement room from a survey artifact, which is the actual `gap` in the answer.

This gap is not an error to eliminate; it is the measurement of what the coarser description cannot hold (Fig 4 :31). It is also why the outreach packet sends the generator, not the one-sentence version (Fig 4 :25). The one-paragraph version in `07` is a deliberately lossy artefact and is labelled as such.
