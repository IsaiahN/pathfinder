# The Tether's answer on psychological safety, learning and innovation

**Mode: `specified` - not `grounded`.** This is a shape and a route, not a result. Simulated derivation checked against the figures (`05_what_shape_means.md` section 7), not a ground reading. No prospective team measurement pays for any claim here.

## 1. The residual it answers

An answer would explain why a reduction in the felt interpersonal cost of speaking up sometimes shrinks the gap between what a team predicts will happen and what its ground returns, and sometimes grows it by removing the challenge that would have corrected a poor decision. `R = |Γ(b,a) - o′|` is indexed per team and per member, never averaged into an organisation-wide safety score (Fig 1 :19, :22). The question is about **the direction of an effect on contact**, not about the level of safety.

The union must be unbundled before it is searched (Fig 9 :54). It is two conditions, not one main effect:

- **C1:** the team's channel to a ground that still pays it stays reachable.
- **C2:** the team's reach keeps moving outward through contact with a closure that differs from its own.

## 2. The shape

1. **Safety is team-and-habitat, not a member possession.** Fig 11 :39: *"capability is a property of agent-and-habitat, never of the agent alone."* Fig 11 :40: *"An improvement that does not change contact changes nothing, however much it improves."* Training individuals to feel safer while leaving the reporting system and the habitat untouched is an agent-only improvement.
2. **The anchor is the ground, not the survey.** Fig 2 :29: *"A measurement, an experiment, a counter, a market, a patient outcome."* Fig 2 :35: *"Alignment is triangulation, not negotiation. A mirror cannot correct you."* A team that reports high safety and high learning on the same questionnaire has scored itself with quantities it also produces (Fig 1 :27).
3. **Learning increases while the channel is reachable.** Fig 10 :24: *"the ground does not decay, the channel does"*; :26: *"stale percepts, dropped transients, saturated metrics, pooled readings"*; :29: *"→ keep the anchor reachable"*. Fig 7 :37-39 gives the two-factor form: bracket fidelity times ground pressure, and zero either stops the chain.
4. **Innovation requires an import.** Fig 6 :23: *"No frame escapes its own closure. The bound moves only on contact with a frame whose closure differs."* Fig 8 :32: closure is idempotent, so no amount of internal discussion adds an atom. A dissenting member, another function, a customer, a regulator or science is not a distraction from the work; it is the only operator that moves the wall. Safety is the valve that lowers the cost of that crossing, not the atom itself.
5. **Safety can suppress challenge when it becomes an agreement room.** Fig 7 :13: *"agreement compounds, evidence does not"*; :35: *"A room’s agreement is variation, not a verdict. Selection between rooms is where the ground votes."*; :36: *"A chain of mirrors is one long room. The next room must differ from the last, and match the residual it surfaced."* This is the figure-grounded reading of the question's second clause. Cangiano 2026 places it in Edmondson's own terms: *"high psychological safety combined with low accountability produces a comfort zone rather than a learning zone"*.
6. **The valence of challenge flips with the residual and the phase.** Fig 11 :49-51: *"An actor’s valence is relative to the residual, not a property it carries."* Fig 12 :119: a catalyst is a part that survives its own use, and *"nothing records which a term is"*. The same dissent that is a benefactor while a plan is being formed can be a malfactor during execution. The phase schedule itself is single-source, from Cangiano 2026 and the aviation rule; it is not presented as a figure line.
7. **The market, patient or task ground is the anchor, not internal agreement.** Fig 2 :29 names *"a market"* and *"a patient outcome"* as anchors; :37-38 warns that *"instruments can be wrong on axes they do not measure"*. Analyst consensus, engagement scores and safety surveys are instruments.
8. **The break is usually in the middle of the chain.** Fig 3 :24 and :27: the vocabulary and objective link has no instrument, and a link with no instrument is a link nobody has looked at. More safety training lands above the break.
9. **Two teams with the same people can still differ.** Under Fig 12 :117 they are isomers if the bonds differ: *"same atoms, different bond, different substance."* This is why a headcount plus a safety score cannot predict learning.

## 3. What it explicitly does not say

- Not `grounded`. No prospective per-team measurement has paid for this shape.
- Not a main effect. It makes no claim that safety always increases learning, or that it always suppresses challenge.
- Not a number. It does not say what score is "high enough" or where an inverted-U turns. Fig 9 :32: *"you get a kind of thing, never the answer itself."*
- Not a phase schedule. The figures give the valence rule; the deliberation/execution distinction comes from one world source and is marked single-source.
- Not an average. It makes no claim about a correlation coefficient across teams. Such numbers are filters for budget, not witnesses (Fig 9 :60).
- Not that safety, leaders or culture are irrelevant. They are Γ and habitat. The claim is that Γ alone does not settle learning (Fig 11 :40).
- Not a theory of everything about organisations. It stops where the instrument stops.
- Not corroboration by agreement. Edmondson, Frazier and Cangiano share one evidence pool (Fig 2 :31, :40). The low-overlap sources from social learning, animal behaviour and aviation corroborate the mechanism shape, not the team-level fact.

## 4. What would settle it - the ground

A source-discriminating instrument, not a further argument. The source essay's metamerism rule: a reading of green *"cannot comment on the source"*, and *"the answer is unreached until a better instrument exists"*. Here the reading is a safety score; the candidate sources are a reachable reporting channel, an agreement room, a temporary halo and a response bias.

The falsifiers, pre-registered in `01` and repeated in `08`:

1. individual-level felt safety changes error reporting while team-habitat contact measures as unchanged;
2. safety rises while bracket fidelity and ground pressure both measure high and non-declining, and error correction still declines;
3. high safety and high internal agreement coexist with continuing ground-verified new readings and no differing room or import;
4. ground-verified new atoms appear purely inside a team's closure, with no contact with a differing frame;
5. the value of the same voice act is invariant across a documented change of residual and phase.

A prospective study that measures C1 and C2 separately per team, records the bonds as well as the atoms, and reports which condition failed before the decline would turn this from `specified` toward `grounded`.

## 5. The honest stop

**Unreached, not unreachable.** Fig 13 :134: *"Unreachable is a verdict here, not a silence,"* and Fig 8 :39: *"So a search that finds nothing is never a proof of absence."* The shape stops at the limit of the available instruments and records the lineage so the stop is checkable. The missing thing is a discriminator of sources, and it is owed. Naming a winner, a number or a phase rule from a correlation would be the metamerism error the source essay forbids. The nine points in section 2 are a shape; the tenth thing, which specific amount of safety and which specific phase rule would close the question, is not held and is not pretended.

## 6. One-paragraph version for outreach

> The Tether does not give a number for psychological safety and does not claim it is always good. It says the effect is conditional on two things: the team's channel to whatever actually pays it must stay reachable, meaning its readings of errors and decisions are not stale, saturated or pooled into an average, and its reach must keep moving outward through contact with people whose ways of working differ from its own. Those are two conditions with two failure modes, not two degrees of one problem. Quick loss of learning is usually the channel going stale or pooled; sustained innovation is a chain of rooms that each differ from the last and are selected against what the last one surfaced. Safety can make a team less willing to challenge poor performance when it turns into an agreement room where no one brings in a differing view, and the same voice can help in deliberation and hurt in execution because its value depends on the residual. The safety score is an instrument, not the anchor; the anchor is the error corrected, the decision revised and the idea adopted. What is offered is that shape, five falsifiers written in advance, and the instruments that would close it. Which number or intervention occupies the slots stays open until a source-discriminating instrument exists, and the stop is recorded rather than filled.
