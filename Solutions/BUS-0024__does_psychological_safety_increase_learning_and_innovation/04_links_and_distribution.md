## 2. What the wider literature bears on the question

**One evidence pool, one witness.** Edmondson 1999, Frazier et al. 2017 and Cangiano 2026 all draw on the organisational behaviour and psychological safety literature and cite one another. Per Fig 2 :31, *"Independence, between two frames, is how little they draw on the same evidence."* Their agreement is one witness, not three.

- Edmondson 1999 is the source of the team-level construct. The Field Guide summary says the scale *"is what licenses treating it as a property of the team"* and that *"the design is cross-sectional and single-company, so nothing here is causal"*. That corroborates F1's team-level location and refuses any causal reading of the original study.
- Frazier et al. 2017 confirms relationships with learning, performance and engagement across 136 samples. That is the strongest pooled correlation in the field, but a meta-analysis of self-report instruments is still one pool and a budget filter, never the verdict (Fig 9 :60).
- Cangiano 2026 gives the conditional directly: *"moderate levels producing the best outcomes and very high levels generating process losses"*, and reports that Edmondson's own framework puts *"high psychological safety combined with low accountability"* in a comfort zone rather than a learning zone. It corroborates the two-condition shape as a conceptual claim, not as a measurement.
- Eldor, Hodor and Cappelli 2023 is the one empirical nonlinear result read here: *"routine task performance increases with psychological safety only to a point, then declines"*, with collective accountability as a buffer. It corroborates F3 and F6 at the performance stratum, and it is still inside the management pool.

**Low-overlap witnesses**, each from a different field and each read directly:

1. **Human social learning and animal behaviour.** Toyokawa, Whalen and Laland 2019 report that *"Challenging tasks elicit greater conformity among individuals, with rates of copying increasing with group size"*. Collet et al. 2023 show that in animal groups *"the links between individual learning capacities and a collective's performance can be extremely complex"* and that collective learning can arise from members learning about each other or improving complementarity, not only from individual learning. Together they corroborate Fig 7 :12-13 and Fig 11 :40 at a different stratum: agreement is not the same as evidence, and collective capability is not located in one member. They do not corroborate the team-level fact.
2. **Aviation engineering and law.** 14 CFR 121.542(b) names *"engaging in nonessential conversations within the cockpit"* as not required for safe operation during critical phases. Cangiano 2026 describes the industry's response to the Tenerife disaster as *"crew resource management, engineered a calibrated version of psychological safety"*. This is an engineering anchor for the phase-dependence claim in F6: voice is trained at defined moments and restricted at others. It shares no evidence pool with management surveys.
3. **Instrument discrimination.** *The Hard Problem is About Source*, read directly: *"If several paths create the same behavior, then behavior is a projection with lossy information"*, and *"the answer is unreached until a better instrument exists"*. A high safety score is consistent with a genuine reporting channel, a comfortable agreement room, or a survey artifact. The reading does not determine the source. This is framework-internal philosophy, recorded for the canonical reading in `05_what_shape_means.md`, not counted as an independent world witness.

**Framework-internal essays** are used for the canonical reading of "shape" only. Their author wrote the figures, so their agreement with the figures is expected and carries no independent corroboration weight. Overlap is recorded in the ledger.

## 3. Distribution rules

Figure-grounded reasons for how material is offered:

- **Fig 4 :22:** *"Only methods cross up. Only head starts come down."* Send the generator, the residual and the refuter, not the conclusion. A recording carried upward *"looks like knowledge and is a description of one occasion"* (Fig 4 :25).
- **Fig 10 :42:** *"A verifier that can reconstruct the claim cannot verify it."* A recipient who has absorbed the framework before being asked for a verdict has joined the frame being checked.
- **Fig 9 :27-28:** *"describe it before you go looking, or any frame you pick will seem to fit"* if the residual is not described first. Ask what they would expect to see if the shape claim were true before showing the source.
- **Fig 2 :40:** agreement from a shared pool is worthless as a verdict. Later agreement about the framework is never pooled as corroboration.
- **Fig 10 :59:** *"an interest in the outcome is unavoidable, an interest in the reading is disqualifying."* Declare the stake; do not ask for belief.

**A hard practical constraint.** The AOM Publishing page states: *"Artificial Intelligence: Work submitted to AOM must be created by the authors and not the product of artificial intelligence tools unless appropriate to the research question and properly cited."* This whole solution is a simulated derivation produced with an LLM and is labelled as such everywhere. It must **not** be submitted as an authored empirical or theoretical article. The legitimate use is as a private checking instrument: post the residual and the refuter, invite a human to run the check, and let that human author whatever they conclude. Any submission must be rewritten and owned by a human author who can defend the derivation, with the AI contribution disclosed under the applicable policy.

**Order in any reply:** (1) the shape claim and the pre-registered refuter; (2) the question "what would you count as a failure here?"; (3) only after a verdict is recorded, the source links with the note that the figures, not the summary, are the authority; (4) the formula as a reusable method, with the instruction that each user writes their own refuter and states their own mode.

## 4. The two-stage offer, compressed

**Stage 1.** Send `08_expert_verification_packet.md` sections 1 to 3 only: the one-paragraph claim, the three checkable sub-claims with their verbatim figure lines and refuters, and the non-claims. No provenance bundle, no essay links.

**Stage 2, opt-in only.** If the recipient is curious and has already recorded a verdict on stage 1, offer the sources: the figure text, the formula and usage rule, and the essays. Their later agreement is a conversation, not corroboration (Fig 2 :40).

**Stage 3.** Offer the formula as a method, never as an answer (Fig 4 :22). Each user writes a refuter before running it and states the mode of what they get.

**Stop rule.** If stage 1 fails, the theory is not offered. A failure is more useful than an endorsement.

**Hosting.** Nothing here is hosted or sent by this agent. The human operator hosts the packet on an opt-in page and sends one to one. Prefer a download page that lets a recipient decline contact over an attachment pushed to a list.
