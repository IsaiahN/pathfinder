## 1. A shape is a constraint you can state before you can name it

The canonical statement in `FIGURES_README_canonical.md` is that the framework returns *"what a missing thing would have to look like (a shape, not a name)"*, then stops where instruments can no longer distinguish and records the lineage. Fig 9 :32: *"you get a kind of thing, never the answer itself."* Fig 13 :134: *"Unreachable is a verdict here, not a silence,"* and :135: *"It cannot say which primitive is missing, because naming it would mean already holding it."*

From *The Library of Babel*, read directly:

> *"You can cut your search short if the set of books returned is small and descriptive enough that it provides a general shape of the answer that satisfies your question."*

So for BUS-0024 a shape is: **the narrowed form of an answer about when safety changes contact with the ground, statable before any number, programme or phase rule is named.** It constrains. It does not fill.

- Shape: *safety increases learning and innovation while the channel to a paying ground stays reachable and reach is renewed from a closure that differs; safety becomes a suppressor of challenge when the team hands itself the same agreement room, or when voice changes sign with the residual.*
- Name, withheld: *a safety score of 3.9, a quarterly speaking-up workshop, a fixed rule that dissent always helps, or a single optimum on an inverted-U curve.*

The distinction matters here because the field is almost entirely in the naming business. Edmondson names a seven-item scale and a team-level construct; Frazier names antecedents and outcomes; Cangiano names the regulatory loss pathway. Those are bindings from the psychology and management frame onto atoms, not outputs of the loop.

## 2. How the shape was obtained

The essay gives the bootstrapping procedure:

> *"If you don’t know the right terms to search for, you will have to use the existing vocabulary you do know that is closest to the words that you wish to describe and grasp the shape of."*
> *"With each new term gained, you are able to narrow the space by definition and deepen your vocabulary."*

Applied here: the closest available terms were "psychological safety", "team learning behaviour", "voice and silence", "groupthink", "comfort zone", "inverted-U", "crew resource management" and "regulatory loss". None was used as the answer. Each was treated as a witness to be ranged against the residual, per Fig 8 :53: *"Residual first, frame second."* The description in effect terms came first: a belief about the cost of voice, an action of speaking, an outcome at the ground, indexed per team. The frames were selected only after that description was fixed.

The essay also names the bundle stop:

> *"Perhaps your problem was a bundle or conflation of several problems masquerading as one? You will need to search all of those problems individually to find their answers and then combine them in a satisfactory way."*

That is the canonical warrant for treating BUS-0024 as a union of C1 and C2 (Fig 9 :54-57), not as one main effect. Chapter 5 of the exemplar's `05` records that the eight essays can be consulted through the Substack archive; only the three named above were opened this session.

The stop condition:

> *"You will know that you are done because the finish line will also be the starting place, as there is nowhere else to search and nothing else that can describe the answer better."*

In this solution the stop is reached when the two conditions are stated, an instrument for each is named, and the falsifiers are written. Any further search that only renames the conditions is motion, not progress (Fig 7 :32: *"one long room: motion, no progress"*).

## 3. What licenses a name

Only two things, both outside the shape:

1. **The ground settles it.** Formula step 5: *"the ground settles it. Verified only by what pays. A gate is not the ground."* The README: the framework *"stops at the limit of what the available instruments can distinguish, and records the lineage so the stop is honest."*
2. **A provenanced import from a frame whose closure differs** (Fig 6 :23, Fig 8 :50-51), with a source gate and a shadow test. Fig 8 :50: *"Two frames can hold the same atom by convergence, reaching it separately, which is corroboration at maximum independence."*

For BUS-0024 neither has happened. There is no prospective, per-team measurement that pays for "this specific amount of safety caused this specific challenge", and the field's names were adopted without a shadow test. Hence the `gap` row in F11 of `01_figure_grounded_audit.md`.

## 4. The source error is the specific forbidden move here

From *The Hard Problem is About Source*, read directly:

> *"The Problem: You can have several underlying causes that create identical readings but the definition of the output would be read as the same."*
> *"If several paths create the same behavior, then behavior is a projection with lossy information"*
> *"What the Spectrometer Example Reveals: The answer is unreached until a better instrument exists."*

This is the exact error a safety explanation makes when it reads a survey score and names its source. A high safety score is a reading. It is consistent with a genuine reporting channel, with an agreement room where no one challenges, with a temporary halo after a successful project, or with a response bias in the same questionnaire that measures learning. The reading does not determine the source. Fig 2 :37-38: *"instruments can be wrong on axes they do not measure."* What is owed is an instrument that discriminates sources, which this solution does not have. Fig 6 :30: *"outward: does another frame already hold it? The description travels. Fails by being sharpened into terms only we can read. Cast it in effect terms."*

The essay closes: *"An instrument that can’t distinguish these cases demonstrates its own inadequacy, it cannot claim anything substantial about what is being measured or what causes it."* That is why the expert packet asks for a failure condition before a theory.

## 5. Prestige is not action, applied to safety research

From *The Recursive Self-Improvement Trap*, read directly:

> *"Prestige must never be allowed to validate an action directly."*
> *"Prestige can fund an action. It can give an action a budget to be tried. But it cannot settle an action. Only the ground can settle it. The Ground Maintainer must be blind to prestige."*
> *"If prestige could buy actions, the system would collapse into a monoculture. The highest-prestige agent would monopolize compute and repeat its past success, and the system would stop exploring."*

The last line is an instance, in the author's own agent system, of Fig 7 :36 (*"A chain of mirrors is one long room"*) and Fig 10 :24 (*"the ground does not decay, the channel does"*). Because it is the author's own design, it is framework-internal corroboration, not an independent witness; it is recorded here as an illustration of the rule, not as evidence about teams.

For outreach this means: a list of famous safety researchers who agree with the shape would be a monoculture, not a check. Fig 2 :42: *"Buy independence in proportion to the cost of being wrong."* The ask gives the claim a budget to be tried by someone outside; it does not solicit endorsement.

## 6. Correction to my own §3 (an overstatement, retained as evidence)

I initially wrote that naming any particular amount of psychological safety is forbidden by "shape, not a name" in the same way that naming a seventh physical primitive would be. That was too broad and is withdrawn here. The canonical phrase governs a **missing** primitive, one not held in Γ. "Psychological safety", "voice", "error reporting" and "closure" are not missing primitives; they are bindings from the psychology and management frame onto structures the figures already describe (habitat, channel, import, room selection). What remains forbidden is claiming that such a binding moves the wall when it adds no atom (Fig 6 :21: *"MINT composes inside the closure and can never add an atom."*). The binding still needs provenance and a ground test, but it is not a category error in the way naming a seventh term would be. Both the corrected and the withdrawn formulations are kept so the correction is visible rather than silent.

A second narrowing: I initially read "when might it make teams less willing to challenge poor performance" as a claim that safety directly causes unwillingness. Fig 11 :49 says *"An actor’s valence is relative to the residual, not a property it carries."* So the corrected claim is conditional: safety bound to an agreement closure suppresses challenge (F4), and the same voice act can change sign with the residual and phase (F6). The earlier reading is not erased; it is recorded as the thing that was narrowed.

## 7. This derivation is a simulation, not a ground reading

From the same essay, read directly:

> *"the output is only as good as the validator. The agent, or the tool, or the frame, or the theory, cannot validate itself. An external validator, or ground, is always needed to tether the veracity of the output to reality. Anything else is drift."*
> *"LLMs can quickly simulate an approximate answer/output with the formula found in the architecture post. It will often be better than the generic outputs that you will usually get otherwise too (intelligence is essentially arrangement). However, the truly testable output would need to be derived from the real architecture or working the formula yourself."*

So this `05` file, like `01`, is a simulated derivation checked for internal consistency against the figures. It is labelled `specified` wherever it makes a claim about teams. It is not a test of the question and cannot settle it. The point of publishing it is to make the shape and its refuters easy to check by someone whose ground contact is real and whose closure differs.

## 8. The shape, stated once more

For BUS-0024 the canonical shape is: **a two-condition conjunction with two distinct failure modes, a valve that lets a differing closure cross, and a valence that changes with the residual and phase.** It is not a correlation coefficient, not a safety score, not a workshop, and not a ranking of teams. The names that appear in the safety literature are bindings, provenance owed. The instruments that would convert the shape into a grounded answer are not held here, so the answer stops at `specified` and the stop is recorded.
