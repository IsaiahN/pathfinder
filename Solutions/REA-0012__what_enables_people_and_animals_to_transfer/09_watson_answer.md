## VERDICT (from the figures)

The carryable-generator shape survives; the three named candidates do not. Far transfer is a union of conditions with different anchors, read per slot, never pooled into one score. What crosses is a reapplicable method or head start with its typed relation intact, described in effect terms, entering a reproduced habitat and settled by a ground that does not update on the learner's own report. Capability belongs to learner and habitat together. Source and target situations are isomers only over a fixed atom set; where the atom sets differ, composition plus import is owed. The specific cognitive mechanism is a declared `gap`, reported as a verdict and not as silence. Mode `specified` per domain, `general` on the shape, not `grounded`, and simulated rather than a ground reading. The second seat in `11_audit.md` shares this evidence pool, so its agreement is internal consistency, not corroboration.

- **cost if wrong:** bounded and locatable. The packet states its mode, its six refuters and its lineage before its conclusion, so an error is findable rather than invisible. The real cost is spending an outside reader's independence by sending a recording of one result instead of a generator, which Fig 2 :42 prices as "Buy independence in proportion to the cost of being wrong." A second cost is calling a substituted habitat a harder version of the source, which hides the two silent failures until they act. Both costs are avoidable by leading with the shape and the refuter and by enumerating the habitat before scoring.
- **undoable:** yes. The three named candidates are removable without touching the derivation. `07_the_tether_answer.md` and `08_expert_verification_packet.md` already remove them. Nothing has to be withdrawn from the library, and a claim that fails can be superseded by a corrected packet. A refutation adds a term and removes nothing; the reach falls because something was added.
- **reversed by:** a ground reading, not agreement. A source-discriminating observation in a substituted habitat showing whether a novel success came from a method crossing or a recording replayed would close the first slot; a result showing transfer fully predicted by shared attributes with relations held constant would break the bond finding; a single scalar with zero residual on every slot would revive option C; an agent-only improvement that raises transfer with no change of contact would break Fig 11 :40; a zero round-trip gap across habitats would collapse the abstraction finding. A derivation of a target atom from the old closure alone, settled by the ground, would break the import barrier. Until one of these fires, the verdict stands as `candidate`.
