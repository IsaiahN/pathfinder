# Expert verification packet: what enables far transfer?

**What this is:** a claim in a form a specialist can check. Mode, derivation and refuters are stated before the conclusion, so a reader knows what to look for and what would settle it.

**Mode: `general` on the structural shape, `specified` on the per-domain claims, not `grounded`.** No experiment was run here. This is a shape and a route, not a result.

**Standing:** a simulated derivation checked for internal consistency against the figures, not a ground reading. It cannot settle the question it reports on.

## 1. The claim, in one paragraph

Far transfer is a union of conditions with different anchors, not one faculty and not one score. What crosses into a substantially different situation must be a reapplicable method or a head start with its typed relation intact, never a recording of one occasion. The description must be cast in effect terms so a different closure can read it. A substantially different situation is a substituted habitat, with two failures that stay invisible until the goal fails or an unintended actor acts. Capability belongs to learner and habitat together, so improving the learner with no change of contact changes nothing. The residual must be read per slot, and the round-trip gap between an abstract method and its concretisation in the new habitat is the honest measure of what the abstraction cannot hold. The specific cognitive mechanism that occupies the method slot is not named; the slot stays `???`.

## 2. Three checkable sub-claims

### C1: framework-internal, checkable by reading Figures 4 and 12

- **Statement:** a reapplicable method crosses a boundary; a recording of one occasion does not. The carried item is a typed relation, not an ingredient list.
- **Source, verbatim:** Fig 4 :23 - **"upward: a method that can be reapplied. Never the recording of a particular success."** Fig 4 :25 - **"A recording carried upward looks like knowledge and is a description of one occasion."** Fig 12 :5 - **"A set of ingredients is not one. The bond is the rest of the information, and it is the half nothing was recording."**
- **Refuter:** exhibit a verbatim recording of one success, with no re-derivation, that produces a novel success in a genuinely substituted habitat and where the ground pays; or show the typed bond is recoverable from its operands alone.

### C2: world-facing, checkable by a researcher in transfer or comparative cognition

- **Statement:** the residual must be indexed per slot and the question is a union of conditions, so a single far-transfer effect size cannot decide it. Novel, dissimilar transfer stimuli are required before calling a result abstraction rather than stimulus generalization.
- **Source, verbatim:** Fig 1 :19 - **"The residual is indexed by object slot, not aggregated."** Fig 9 :57 - **"What remains is smaller and it is also categorically different: a case in two conditions is not a harder case of either."** World witness, Barnett and Ceci 2002, Psychological Bulletin, abstract read 2026-11-23: *"Estimation of a single effect size for far transfer is misguided in view of this complexity."* Comparative-cognition witness, abstract read via search index 2026-11-23: *"Transfer stimuli must be novel to release behavior from a prior reinforcement history that could confound transfer performance."*
- **Refuter:** produce a single scalar that predicts transfer across every slot and every target situation with zero residual left anywhere, measured against a ground that does not update on the scalar.

### C3: framework-internal derivation with a habitat condition

- **Statement:** a substantially different situation is a substituted habitat, and capability is a property of agent-and-habitat. An improvement that does not change contact changes nothing.
- **Source, verbatim:** Fig 11 :66 - **"Isolation is not removal of the habitat. It is substitution of one habitat for another."** Fig 11 :39 - **"So the answerable question is not what intelligence is, but what makes a system capable. And capability is a property of agent-and-habitat, never of the agent alone."** Fig 11 :40 - **"A system cannot be made more capable by improving the agent in isolation. An improvement that does not change contact changes nothing, however much it improves."**
- **Refuter:** improve an agent in isolation, with no change of contact, and show a durable rise in transfer to substantially different situations. That would break the claim. The source essay records this as refutable, not as settled law.

## 3. What is explicitly not claimed

- Not a cognitive mechanism. The framework returns a shape, not a name, so the slot stays `???`.
- Not that shared elements or surface similarity cause transfer. That is a filter for the budget, not a witness for the verdict (Fig 9 :60).
- Not that one score decides far transfer.
- Not that more training inside the source closure can supply a missing atom (Fig 8 :32).
- Not that a synthetic solve demonstrates capability (Fig 11 :70).
- Not `grounded`. No measurement was taken.
- Not corroborated by agreement. If a reader agrees only after reading the framework interior, their agreement is one evidence pool talking.

## 5. What would settle it

A discriminating observation, not an argument. The framework's own source essay says that several paths can produce the same reading and that a better instrument is what resolves it. Specifically:

1. In a substituted habitat with unchosen actors present, does the learner generate a novel success or only replay a source occasion?
2. Does transfer survive when shared object attributes are held constant and the typed relation is varied?
3. Are the conditions the goal required actually reproduced, and were unintended actors enumerated before scoring?
4. Which slot persists and refuses to trend down under more trials?
5. What is the measured round-trip gap R_T between the abstract method and the concretised behaviour?

## 6. What an expert can check in twenty minutes

1. Read Fig 4 :22-25, Fig 12 :5, Fig 1 :19, Fig 9 :57 and Fig 11 :39-40, six short passages.
2. Check C2 against your own literature: is a single far-transfer effect size defensible given the dimensions your field actually manipulates?
3. Name one instrument that would distinguish a method crossing from a recording replayed in a substituted habitat, or say why none is possible.
4. Send the refutation. A failure is more useful than an endorsement. If you engage after reading the theory, record that order; a later agreement is not pooled as corroboration.
